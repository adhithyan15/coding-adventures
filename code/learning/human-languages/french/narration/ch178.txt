French, chapter 178: To snow, To go with, To add, To announce, To appear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
neiger — to snow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to protect, then the French for to rescue.

You'll want to know: neiger.
neiger — "to snow".

The word, taken apart.
neiger from Vulgar Latin nivicare, from Latin nix, nivis, "snow" — inherited; neige was made from it.
