## You'll want to know: चोंच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C448-CHONCH448-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चोंच** — *coñc* — "a beak".

> तोते की चोंच लाल होती है।
>
> *tote kī coñc lāl hotī hai.*
>
> A parrot's beak is red.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चोंच** (*coñc*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *cañcu*, "a beak" (CDIAL 4569), which lists Hindi *cõc*; CDIAL thinks the Sanskrit word itself came from Dravidian.
