## You'll want to know: analizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C599-INVESTIGA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**analizar** — "to analyse".

> Tenemos que analizar los datos.
>
> *We have to analyse the data.*

Spelling: *analicé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*analizar* is built on *análisis*, a learned borrowing from Greek *analysis* 'a loosening, a breaking up' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
