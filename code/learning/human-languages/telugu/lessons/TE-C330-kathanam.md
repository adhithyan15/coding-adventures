## You'll want to know: కథనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C330-PATRIKA330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కథనం** — *kathanaṁ* — "a news story; an account".

> విలేకరి ఆ సంఘటన మీద కథనం రాశాడు.
>
> *vilēkari ā saṅghaṭana mīda kathanaṁ rāśāḍu.*
>
> The reporter wrote a story on that incident.

A cousin of కథ, a story: the news calls each report a కథనం.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కథనం** (*kathanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kathana*, "narration, telling" (CDIAL 2702), from the root *kath*, "to tell", which also gives కథ, a story.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
