## You'll want to know: dìzhèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C300-DEATH300-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dìzhèn* — "earthquake".

> *zhèr yǐqián yǒu guo dìzhèn* — There was once an earthquake here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìzhèn* is built from 地 'the ground' and *zhèn* 'to shake', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *tsyinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'thunder' and 'to move': the ground shaking.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
