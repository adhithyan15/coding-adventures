# लीपणो (līpṇo) — to plaster (a floor or wall with mud)

## You'll want to know: लीपणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C172-JHADU172-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लीपणो** — *līpṇo* — "to plaster (a floor or wall with mud)".

> मां आंगणो लीपै है।
>
> *mā̃ ā̃gaṇo līpai hai.*
>
> Mother plasters the courtyard.

Past (I …): म्हैं आंगणो लीप्यो, *mhaĩ ā̃gaṇo līpyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लीपणो** (*līpṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lipyate*, "is smeared"; CDIAL lists Hindi *līpnā*, "to smear" (CDIAL 11061). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
