## You'll want to know: पायदळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C330-SENAPATI330-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पायदळ** — *pāydaḷ* — "infantry".

> पायदळ घोडदळाच्या मागे चालत होतं.
>
> *pāydaḷ ghoḍdaḷācyā māge cālat hotaṁ.*
>
> The infantry was marching behind the cavalry.

Neuter.

पाय, a foot, + दळ, a troop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पायदळ** (*pāydaḷ*) is built from *pāy*, 'a foot', and *daḷ*, 'a troop', from Sanskrit *dala* (Monier-Williams): the troop on foot.
