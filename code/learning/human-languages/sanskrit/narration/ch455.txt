Sanskrit, chapter 455: Fondness and Attachment.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
सान्निध्यम् (sānnidhyam) — nearness; presence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for enmity, then the Sanskrit for covetous longing.

You'll want to know: सान्निध्यम्.
सान्निध्यम् — sānnidhyam — "nearness; presence".
मातुः सान्निध्ये शिशुः शान्तः भवति। mātuḥ sānnidhye śiśuḥ śāntaḥ bhavati. In its mother's presence the baby is calm.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
सान्निध्यम् (sānnidhyam) is built on saṃnidhi, "nearness", from sam, ni and the root √dhā, "to put", with the lengthening of its first vowel; MW gives "the being near, nearness, vicinity, presence" (MW).

Warm-up.

वात्सल्यम् — vātsalyam — "tenderness for a child".
मातुः वात्सल्यं शिशुं रक्षति। mātuḥ vātsalyaṃ śiśuṃ rakṣati. A mother's tenderness protects her baby.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
वात्सल्यम् (vātsalyam) is built on vatsala, "fond, tender", from vatsa, "a calf" (वत्सः); MW gives "affection or tenderness, esp. towards offspring" (MW).
