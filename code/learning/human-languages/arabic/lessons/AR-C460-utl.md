## You'll want to know: عطل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C460-ATAL460-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عطل** — *ʿuṭl* — "a breakdown; a fault".

> كان في السيارة عطل صغير.
>
> *kāna fī as-sayyāra ʿuṭl ṣaghīr.*
>
> The car had a small fault.

Plural: أعطال, *aʿṭāl*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عطل** (*ʿuṭl*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṭ-l*, "to be idle, without work" (Lane).
