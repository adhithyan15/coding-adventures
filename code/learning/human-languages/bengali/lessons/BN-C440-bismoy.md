## You'll want to know: বিস্ময়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C440-BISMOY440-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিস্ময়** — *bismôy* — "wonder; amazement".

> শিশুটি বিস্ময়ে চাঁদটা দেখছিল।
>
> *shishuṭi bismôye chā̃dṭā dekhchhilô.*
>
> The child was gazing at the moon in wonder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিস্ময়** (*bismôy*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vismaya*, "wonder, surprise, amazement" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
