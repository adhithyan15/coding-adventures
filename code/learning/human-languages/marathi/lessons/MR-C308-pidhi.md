## You'll want to know: पिढी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C308-PIDHI308-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पिढी** — *piḍhī* — "a generation".

> आमच्या घरात तीन पिढ्या एकत्र राहतात.
>
> *āmacyā gharāt tīn piḍhyā ekatra rāhtāt.*
>
> Three generations live together in our house.

Feminine. Plural: पिढ्या, *piḍhyā*.

Plural पिढ्या; पुढची पिढी, the next generation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पिढी** (*piḍhī*) is the Marathi word for a generation; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
