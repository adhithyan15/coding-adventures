## You'll want to know: مسرت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C441-JOY441-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مسرت** — *masarrat* — "delight; joy".

> سالگرہ پر سارا کی مسرت دیکھ کر سب خوش تھے۔
>
> *sālgirah par sārā kī masarrat dekh kar sab khush the.*
>
> Everyone was happy to see Sara's delight on her birthday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسرت** (*masarrat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *masarra*, "gladness".
