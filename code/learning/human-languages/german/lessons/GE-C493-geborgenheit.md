## You'll want to know: die Geborgenheit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C493-ZUNEIGUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Geborgenheit** — "feeling of safety, security".

> Bei meinen Großeltern spürte ich immer Geborgenheit.
>
> *At my grandparents' I always felt safe and secure.*

No plural. Built on *geborgen* — safe, sheltered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Geborgenheit* is built on *geborgen* "sheltered", the participle of *bergen* "to shelter, to save", inherited from Old High German *bergan*, from Proto-Germanic *berganą* (EtymDB).
