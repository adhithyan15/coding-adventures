# മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ) — a mundu

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C46-ASK-02, ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the word for *cloth*. Then say *new*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C100-MUNDU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**മുണ്ട്** — *muṇṭŭ* — **a mundu**, the length of cloth worn wrapped at the
waist.

> **ഇത് പുതിയ മുണ്ടാണ്.** — *itŭ putiya muṇṭāṇŭ* — "This is a new mundu."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C100-MUNDU-01, ML-LEX-C46-ASK-02, ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-04, ML-LEX-AANU-01] -->

**The book gave you the cloth before it gave you the garment.** **തുണി** is the
stuff; **മുണ്ട്** is the thing made of it and worn.

Two things in that sentence you already know how to do, and neither is new:

- **പുതിയ** sits in front and does not change — that is the whole adjective
  pattern from the chapter that taught it.
- **മുണ്ട്** ends in the breathed half-u, so **ആണ്** lands straight on it:
  **മുണ്ടാണ്**, nothing slid in — the same join **പാട്ട്** takes.

**So the only new thing here is the word itself.**
