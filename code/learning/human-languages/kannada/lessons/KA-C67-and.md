# ಮತ್ತು (mattu) — and

## You'll want to know: ಮತ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C67-LINK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮತ್ತು** (*mattu*) — "and".

It stands between the two things it joins, in the same place English puts *and*:
**ಚಹಾ ಮತ್ತು ಕಾಫಿ** (*cahā mattu kāphi*) — "tea and coffee".

You have had a hundred and thirty nouns and no way to say two of them in one
breath. Every list so far had to be a series of separate sentences. This is the
word that ends that.

A note on how it is actually said. In unhurried speech Kannada will often join
two nouns by simply naming them one after the other, with nothing between; ಮತ್ತು
is what a careful sentence, a notice or a written list reaches for. Both are
ordinary standard-colloquial Kannada, and ಮತ್ತು is the one that never sounds
wrong.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The first join. Two things, one sentence.
