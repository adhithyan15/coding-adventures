## You'll want to know: સખાવત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C461-KRUPAN461-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સખાવત** — *sakhāvat* — "charity; open-handed giving".

> તે વેપારીએ ગામ માટે ઘણી સખાવત કરી.
>
> *te vepārīe gām māṭe ghaṇī sakhāvat karī.*
>
> That merchant gave generously to the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સખાવત** (*sakhāvat*) is usually traced to Arabic *saxāwa*, "generosity", which reached Gujarati through Persian.
