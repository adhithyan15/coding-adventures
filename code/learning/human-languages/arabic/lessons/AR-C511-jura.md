# جرعة (jurʿa) — a dose

## You'll want to know: جرعة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C511-JURA511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جرعة** — *jurʿa* — "a dose".

> شرب المريض جرعة من الدواء.
>
> *shariba al-marīḍ jurʿa min ad-dawāʾ.*
>
> The patient drank a dose of the medicine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جرعة** (*jurʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-ʿ*, "to gulp, to swallow" (Lane). Lane gives jurʿa as a single gulp: a dose is one swallow of medicine.
