## You'll want to know: سربازی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C333-NOWJAVANI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سربازی** (*sarbâzi*) — "military service".

> برادرم دو سال سربازی رفت.
>
> *Barâdaram do sâl sarbâzi raft.*
>
> My brother did two years of military service.

From *sarbâz* "a soldier". *Sarbâzi raftan* is "to do military service".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarbâzi* adds the suffix *-i* to *sarbâz* "a soldier", usually explained as *sar* "head" + *bâz* "one who stakes": one who stakes his head. EtymDB lists no entry.
