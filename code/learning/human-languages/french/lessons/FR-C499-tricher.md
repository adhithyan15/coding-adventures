## You'll want to know: tricher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C499-JEUX-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tricher** — "to cheat".

> Mon petit frère triche toujours aux cartes.
>
> *My little brother always cheats at cards.*

*Un tricheur* — a cheat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tricher* is inherited from Old French *trichier* (EtymDB), usually traced to Vulgar Latin *triccāre*, from Latin *trīcārī* "to make difficulties, to trifle" (Lewis & Short).
