## You'll want to know: آبرو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C506-TIES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**آبرو** (*âberu*) — "reputation, good name".

> این کار آبروی خانواده را برد.
>
> *in kâr âberu-ye khânevâde râ bord.*
>
> This act disgraced the family.

*âberu bordan* — to disgrace; *âberu dâshtan* — to be respected.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âberu* is built on two native Persian words: *âb*, 'water, lustre', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *ru*, 'face': the shine on a face.
