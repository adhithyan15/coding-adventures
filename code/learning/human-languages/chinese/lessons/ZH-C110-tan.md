## You'll want to know: tán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C110-SKILL110-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tán* — "to play an instrument; to pluck".

> *wǒ huì tán gāngqín* — I can play the piano.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tán* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to shoot' and named in a game of flicking pieces. Playing an instrument is not given there, so how the word came to that sense is not settled.
