## You'll want to know: ममता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C459-SNEH459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ममता** — *mamtā* — "a mother's love".

> माँ की ममता की कोई क़ीमत नहीं होती।
>
> *māṁ kī mamtā kī koī qīmat nahīṃ hotī.*
>
> No price can be put on a mother's love.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ममता** (*mamtā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *mamatā*, "the state of 'mine'", built on *mama*, "mine" (Monier-Williams). Hindi turned the sense of ownership into the tender love of a mother.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
