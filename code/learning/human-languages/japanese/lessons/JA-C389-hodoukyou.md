## You'll want to know: ほどうきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C389-OODOORI389-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ほどうきょう** — *hodōkyō* — "a footbridge (over a road)".

> **ほどうきょうで どうろを わたりました。** — *hodōkyō de dōro o watarimashita* — "I crossed the road by the footbridge."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほどうきょう** (*hodōkyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ho*, "walk" (Middle Chinese *buH*), *dō*, "street" (*dawX*) and *kyō*, "bridge" (*gjew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
