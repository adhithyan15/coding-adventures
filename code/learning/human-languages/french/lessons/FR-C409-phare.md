## You'll want to know: le phare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C409-VIRAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le phare** — "the headlight; the lighthouse".

> La nuit, les phares éclairent la route.
>
> *At night the headlights light up the road.*

*Un phare* is also a lighthouse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*phare* comes from Latin *pharus* 'lighthouse', from Greek *pháros*, first the name of an island whose lighthouse was one of the wonders of the ancient world (EtymDB).
