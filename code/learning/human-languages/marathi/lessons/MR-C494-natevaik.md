## You'll want to know: नातेवाईक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C494-NATE494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नातेवाईक** — *nātevāīk* — "a relative".

> सुट्टीत सगळे नातेवाईक आजीच्या घरी जमले.
>
> *suṭṭīt sagḷe nātevāīk ājīcyā gharī jamle.*
>
> In the holidays all the relatives gathered at Grandmother's house.

Masculine.

Plural नातेवाईक. Built on नाते, kinship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नातेवाईक** (*nātevāīk*) is a Marathi derivation of नाते, "kinship" (CDIAL 5279); the origin of the ending *-vāīk* is not settled.
