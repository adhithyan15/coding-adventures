## You'll want to know: láojià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C457-HELP457-01]; assesses=[] -->

*láojià* — "may I trouble you (asking a favour)".

> *láojià qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu* — Excuse me, how do I get to the railway station?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*láojià* is built from *láo* 'to tire' and *jià* 'a carriage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *law* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'weary' and the second 'to ride'. The pair is usually explained as an old polite way of asking someone to trouble their carriage.
