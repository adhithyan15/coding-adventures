Telugu, chapter 412: Research.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పరిశోధన (pariśōdhana) — research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for public opinion, then the Telugu for a percentage.

You'll want to know: పరిశోధన.
పరిశోధన — pariśōdhana — "research".
ఆమె తెలుగు పాటల మీద పరిశోధన చేస్తోంది. āme telugu pāṭala mīda pariśōdhana cēstōndi. She is doing research on Telugu songs.
పరిశోధన (pariśōdhana) చేయు, to do research.

The word, taken apart.
పరిశోధన (pariśōdhana) is borrowed from Sanskrit pariśodhana, "cleaning, purification" (Monier-Williams); in Telugu it is going through a subject thoroughly, research.

Lesson 2 of 5.
పరిశోధించు (pariśōdhiñcu) — to research; to investigate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a percentage, then the Telugu for research.

You'll want to know: పరిశోధించు.
పరిశోధించు — pariśōdhiñcu — "to research; to investigate".
ఆమె తన కుటుంబ చరిత్రను పరిశోధించింది. āme tana kuṭumba caritranu pariśōdhiñcindi. She researched her family history.
Past (I …): పరిశోధించాను, pariśōdhiñcānu.
Also for the police: a case is investigated.

The word, taken apart.
పరిశోధించు (pariśōdhiñcu) is built on Sanskrit pariśodhana, "cleaning, purification" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; the noun is పరిశోధన (pariśōdhana).

Lesson 3 of 5.
పరిశోధకుడు (pariśōdhakuḍu) — a researcher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for research, then the Telugu for to research.

You'll want to know: పరిశోధకుడు.
పరిశోధకుడు — pariśōdhakuḍu — "a researcher".
ఆ పరిశోధకుడు పది సంవత్సరాలు అడవిలో ఉన్నాడు. ā pariśōdhakuḍu padi saṁvatsarālu aḍavilō unnāḍu. That researcher stayed in the forest for ten years.
Plural: పరిశోధకులు, pariśōdhakulu.
A woman researcher is పరిశోధకురాలు.

The word, taken apart.
పరిశోధకుడు (pariśōdhakuḍu) is built on Sanskrit pariśodhana (Monier-Williams), with -అక, "one who", and the Telugu masculine ending -డు.

The word, taken apart.
అన్వేషించు (anvēṣiñcu) is built on Sanskrit anveṣaṇa, "seeking for, searching, investigating" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
