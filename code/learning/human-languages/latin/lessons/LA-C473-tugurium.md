## You'll want to know: tugurium, tuguriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C473-RUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tugurium, tuguriī** — "a hut, a shed".

> Pauper agricola in tuguriō cum familiā vīvit.
>
> *The poor farmer lives in a hut with his family.*

Second declension, neuter: *tugurium, tuguriī n.* A rough hut, poorer than a *casa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tugurium* is usually linked with *tegō* 'to cover' (Lewis & Short), so a shelter with a roof; EtymDB traces it to PIE *(s)teg-* 'to cover with a roof', with Greek *stegō* 'to cover' as a cousin.
