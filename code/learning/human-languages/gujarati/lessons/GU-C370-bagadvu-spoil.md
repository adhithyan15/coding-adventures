# બગાડવું (bagāḍvũ) — to spoil

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: બગાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C370-KHATRO370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બગાડવું** — *bagāḍvũ* — "to spoil".

> જો તમે વધારે મીઠું નાખશો તો દાળ બગાડશો.
>
> *jo tame vadhāre mīṭhũ nākhśo to dāḷ bagāḍśo.*
>
> If you put in too much salt, you will spoil the dal.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આખું કામ બગાડ્યું, *teṇe ākhũ kām bagāḍyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બગાડવું** (*bagāḍvũ*) is the transitive partner of **બગડવું** (*bagaḍvũ*), "to go bad", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *vighaṭatē*, "flies apart" (CDIAL 11673).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
