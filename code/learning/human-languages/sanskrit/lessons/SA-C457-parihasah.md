## You'll want to know: परिहासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C457-HASA457-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परिहासः** — *parihāsaḥ* — "a jest".

> अरुणस्य परिहासेन सर्वे हसन्ति।
>
> *aruṇasya parihāsena sarve hasanti.*
>
> Everyone laughs at Arun's joke.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिहासः** (*parihāsaḥ*) is built from *pari*, "around", and the root √has, "to laugh"; MW gives "jesting, joking" and "a jest, joke" (MW).
