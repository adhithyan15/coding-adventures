## You'll want to know: ठहरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C413-SARAY413-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ठहरना** — *ṭhaharnā* — "to stop over; to stay (somewhere)".

> हम दो रात एक होटल में ठहरे।
>
> *ham do rāt ek hoṭal meṁ ṭhahre.*
>
> We stayed two nights at a hotel.

Past (he …): वह धर्मशाला में ठहरा, *vah dharmśālā meṁ ṭhahrā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठहरना** (*ṭhaharnā*) goes back to *\*stabhira*, "fixed, firm", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 13680), which lists Hindi *ṭhaharnā*, "to stop". To ṭhaharnā is to stand still in one place for a while.
