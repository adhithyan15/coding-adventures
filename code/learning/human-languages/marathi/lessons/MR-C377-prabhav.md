## You'll want to know: प्रभाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C377-PRABHAV377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रभाव** — *prabhāv* — "an influence".

> त्याच्यावर वडिलांचा मोठा प्रभाव आहे.
>
> *tyācyāvar vaḍilāñcā moṭhā prabhāv āhe.*
>
> His father has a great influence on him.

Masculine.

X वर Y चा प्रभाव, Y's influence on X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रभाव** (*prabhāv*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prabhāva*, "power, might" (Monier-Williams).
