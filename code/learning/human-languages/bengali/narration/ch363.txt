Bengali, chapter 363: Problems and Answers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সমস্যা (sômôsyā) — a problem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to sense, then the Bengali for clear.

You'll want to know: সমস্যা.
সমস্যা — sômôsyā — "a problem".
তোমার কোনো সমস্যা হলে আমাকে বলো। tomār kono sômôsyā hôle āmāke bôlo. If you have any problem, tell me.

The word, taken apart.
সমস্যা (sômôsyā) is Sanskrit samasyā, a part of a verse given to someone else to complete, a kind of puzzle (Monier-Williams). Bengali widened the puzzle to any problem; that widening is not in the dictionary.

Lesson 4 of 5.
উত্তর (uttôr) — an answer; a reply.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a means, then the Bengali for an alternative.

You'll want to know: উত্তর.
উত্তর — uttôr — "an answer; a reply".
আমার প্রশ্নের উত্তর দাও। āmār prôshner uttôr dāo. Answer my question.

The word, taken apart.
উত্তর (uttôr) is Sanskrit uttara, the later or the upper one, and so a reply (Monier-Williams). The same word also means north. It is given with দেওয়া.

Lesson 5 of 5.
উতরে যাওয়া (utre jāoyā) — to come through; to get through (a test).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an alternative, then the Bengali for an answer.

You'll want to know: উতরে যাওয়া.
উতরে যাওয়া — utre jāoyā — "to come through; to get through (a test)".
অনেক ঝামেলার পরে আমরা উতরে গেলাম। ônek jhāmelār pôre āmrā utre gelām. After a lot of trouble, we came through.
Past (he/she …): সেই পরিকল্পনাটা উতরে গেল, sei pôrikôlpônāṭā utre gelô.

The word, taken apart.
উতরে যাওয়া (utre jāoyā) is the -এ form of উতরা 'to cross, to get over' (CDIAL 1770 lists Bengali utarā) with যাওয়া: to get across a difficulty to the other side. The sense 'to pass a test' is a Bengali use.
