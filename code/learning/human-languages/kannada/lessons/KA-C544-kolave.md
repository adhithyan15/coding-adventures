## You'll want to know: ಕೊಳವೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C544-VIDYUT544-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಳವೆ** — *koḷave* — "a tube; a pipe".

> ತೋಟಕ್ಕೆ ನೀರು ಕೊಳವೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *tōṭakke nīru koḷaveyalli baruttade.*
>
> Water comes to the garden through a pipe.

ಕೊಳವೆ ಬಾವಿ, a borewell. ಕೊಳಲು, the flute, comes from the same old word for a tube.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಳವೆ** (*koḷave*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1818 lists the Kannada word for "a tube, a blow-pipe" in the same article as the word for a flute, ಕೊಳಲು.
