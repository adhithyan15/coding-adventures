## You'll want to know: じょげん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C340-JOGEN340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**じょげん** — *jogen* — "advice".

> **せんぱいの じょげんを きいた ほうが いいです。** — *senpai no jogen o kiita hō ga ii desu* — "You should listen to your senior's advice."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょげん** (*jogen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jo*, "help" (Middle Chinese *dzrjoH*) and *gen*, "word" (*ngjon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
