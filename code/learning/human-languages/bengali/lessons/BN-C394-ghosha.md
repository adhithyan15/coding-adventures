## You'll want to know: ঘষা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C394-JONTRANGSHO394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘষা** — *ghôshā* — "to rub; to scrub".

> অরুণ সাবান নিয়ে সাইকেলের চাকা ঘষল।
>
> *ôrun sābān niye sāikeler chākā ghôshlô.*
>
> Arun took some soap and scrubbed the bicycle wheel.

Past (he/she …): তিনি চাকাটা ঘষলেন, *tini chākāṭā ghôshlen*.

The second letter is ষ, said sh like শ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘষা** (*ghôshā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ghasā*, "to rub", under Sanskrit *ghárṣati*, "rubs" (CDIAL 4450).
