## You'll want to know: 开关
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C520-APPLIANCE520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**开关** — *kāiguān* — "a light switch; a switch on a machine".

> *wǒ zhǎo bu dào dēng de kāiguān* — I can't find the light switch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开关** (*kāiguān*) is built from 开 'to open' and 关 'to close', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *kwaen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second, after an older dictionary, as the bar laid across a door to hold it shut: the thing that opens and closes.
