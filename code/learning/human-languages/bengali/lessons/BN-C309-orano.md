## You'll want to know: ওড়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C309-GHURI309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ওড়ানো** — *oṛāno* — "to fly a kite; to make fly".

> বিকেলে বাবা আর আমি ছাদে ঘুড়ি ওড়ালাম।
>
> *bikele bābā ār āmi chhāde ghuṛi oṛālām.*
>
> In the late afternoon my father and I flew kites on the roof.

Past (he/she …): সেই ছেলে ঘুড়ি ওড়াল, *sei chhele ghuṛi oṛālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ওড়ানো** (*oṛāno*) is **ওড়া**, to fly — CDIAL 1697, Sanskrit *uḍḍīyatē*, 'flies up' — with the -ānō ending: to make something fly.
