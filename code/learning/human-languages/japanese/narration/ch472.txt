Japanese, chapter 472: Ingredients.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ざいりょう (zairyō) — ingredients; materials.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to sing in a choir, then the Japanese for to compose music.

You'll want to know: ざいりょう.
ざいりょう — zairyō — "ingredients; materials".
ばんごはんの ざいりょうを かいました。 — bangohan no zairyō o kaimashita — "I bought the ingredients for dinner."

The word, taken apart.
ざいりょう (zairyō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are zai, "ingredients" (Middle Chinese dzoj) and ryō, "materials" (lew) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

The word, taken apart.
ねぎ (negi) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
