## You'll want to know: soplar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C772-MONTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**soplar** — "to blow".

> Si sopla mucho viento, volvemos.
>
> *If the wind blows hard, we'll turn back.*

*Soplar las velas* — to blow out the candles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soplar* is inherited from Latin *sufflāre* 'to blow from below, to puff out' (Lewis & Short), built on *sub* and *flāre* 'to blow'.
