## You'll want to know: やく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C172-MEALS172-05]; assesses=[] -->

**やく** — *yaku* — "to grill; to bake, to roast".

> **にわで にくを やきます。** — *niwa de niku o yakimasu* — "We grill meat in the garden."

Polite **やきます**, *yakimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やく** (*yaku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
