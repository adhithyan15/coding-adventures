## You'll want to know: напоследок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C274-POSTEPENNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**напоследок** (*naposlédok*) — "at the very end, last of all".

> Напоследок он улыбнулся и ушёл.
>
> *Naposlédok on ulybnúlsya i ushyól.*
>
> At the very end he smiled and left.

Colloquial: the last thing before parting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*напоследок* is built on *на* and *последок* "the last bit", from *последний* "last", which goes back to *след* "a track" (EtymDB).
