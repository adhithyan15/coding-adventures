## You'll want to know: أشعل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C442-MUNTAJA442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أشعل** — *ashʿala* — "to set alight; to light".

> أشعل أبي النار أمام الخيمة.
>
> *ashʿala abī an-nār amāma al-khayma.*
>
> My father lit the fire in front of the tent.

Present: يشعل, *yushʿil*.

It also means to switch on a lamp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أشعل** (*ashʿala*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-ʿ-l*, "to blaze, to flame" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to make blaze".
