## You'll want to know: दिग्दर्शक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C539-DAD539-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दिग्दर्शक** — *digdarśak* — "a film director; a stage director".

> दिग्दर्शकाने नवीन चित्रपटाची घोषणा केली.
>
> *digdarśakāne navīn citrapṭācī ghoṣṇā kelī.*
>
> The director announced a new film.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिग्दर्शक** (*digdarśak*) is a tatsama coinage from Sanskrit *diś*, "direction", and *darśaka*, "one who shows" (Monier-Williams).
