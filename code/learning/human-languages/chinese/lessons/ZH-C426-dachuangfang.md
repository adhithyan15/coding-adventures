## You'll want to know: dàchuángfáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C426-ROOM426-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dàchuángfáng* — "room with a double bed".

> *wǒmen yào yì jiān dàchuángfáng* — We'd like a double room.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàchuángfáng* is built from 大 'big', *chuáng* 'a bed' and 房 'a room', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *daH*, *dzrjang* and *bjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
