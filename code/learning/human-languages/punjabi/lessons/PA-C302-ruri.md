## You'll want to know: ਰੂੜੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C302-SOW302-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੂੜੀ** — *rūṛī* — "a manure heap".

> ਰੂੜੀ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੇ ਬਾਹਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *rūṛī piṇḍ de bāhar hai.*
>
> The manure heap is outside the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੂੜੀ** (*rūṛī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rūḍhi*, "rise, increase"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *rūṛī*, "heap of manure outside village", under it (CDIAL 10802).
