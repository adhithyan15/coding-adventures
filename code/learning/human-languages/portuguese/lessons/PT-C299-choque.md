## You'll want to know: o choque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C299-ACIDENTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o choque** — "the crash, the impact; the shock".

> O choque entre os dois carros foi forte.
>
> *The crash between the two cars was violent.*

Also an electric shock or an emotional one: *ficar em choque*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*choque* was borrowed from French *choc* "a blow, an impact" (EtymDB).
