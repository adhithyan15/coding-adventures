## You'll want to know: le virement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C392-BANQUE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le virement** — "the bank transfer".

> J'ai fait un virement pour payer le loyer.
>
> *I made a transfer to pay the rent.*

*Un virement bancaire* — a bank transfer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*virement* is built from *virer* "to turn, to transfer" and *-ment* (EtymDB). The origin of *virer* is usually traced to a Latin form linked to *vibrāre* "to shake"; it is not settled.
