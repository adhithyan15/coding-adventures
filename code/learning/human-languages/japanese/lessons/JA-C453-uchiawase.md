## You'll want to know: うちあわせ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C453-PAPERS453-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うちあわせ** — *uchiawase* — "a briefing; a planning meeting".

> **あしたの あさ、うちあわせが あります。** — *ashita no asa, uchiawase ga arimasu* — "There is a briefing tomorrow morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うちあわせ** (*uchiawase*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *uchi*, "striking (utsu)" and *awase*, "fitting together (awaseru)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
