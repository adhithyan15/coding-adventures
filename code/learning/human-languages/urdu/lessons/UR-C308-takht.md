## You'll want to know: تخت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C308-SHAHENSHAH308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تخت** — *takht* — "a throne".

> بادشاہ تخت پر بیٹھا۔
>
> *bādshāh takht par baiṭhā.*
>
> The king sat on the throne.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تخت** (*takht*) is usually traced to Persian *takht*, "a throne, a raised wooden seat". **تخت پر بیٹھنا** is to come to the throne.
