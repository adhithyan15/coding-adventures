## You'll want to know: ಆರನೆಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C74-ORDINAL-02] -->

**ಆರನೆಯ** (*āraneya*) — "**sixth**". **ಆರು** drops its **-ು** and takes
**-ಅನೆಯ**, with nothing else to notice.

**ಆರನೆಯ ದಿನ** (*āraneya dina*), the sixth day.

That "nothing else to notice" is the point of the whole chapter. Six to ten were
a separate lesson from one to five when you learned to count; for ordering they
are not a separate anything.
