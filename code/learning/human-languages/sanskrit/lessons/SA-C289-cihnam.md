## You'll want to know: चिह्नम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C289-SAKSHYA289-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चिह्नम्** — *cihnam* — "a trace; a sign".

> भित्तौ चौरस्य चिह्नम् अस्ति।
>
> *bhittau caurasya cihnam asti.*
>
> There is a mark of the thief on the wall.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिह्नम्** (*cihnam*) MW gives *cihna* as a mark, a sign or a characteristic, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
