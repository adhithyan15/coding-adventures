## You'll want to know: खूँटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C518-TAAR518-04]; assesses=[] -->

**खूँटी** — *khū̃ṭī* — "a peg; a hook (on a wall)".

> दरवाज़े के पीछे एक खूँटी है।
>
> *Darvāze ke pīche ek khū̃ṭī hai.*
>
> There is a peg behind the door.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खूँटी** (*khū̃ṭī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*khuṭṭa*, "a peg, a post", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3893).
