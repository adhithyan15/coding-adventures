Arabic, chapter 300: A crime.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
جريمة (jarīma) — a crime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to mention, then the Arabic for to pass on.

You'll want to know: جريمة.
جريمة — jarīma — "a crime".
وقعت جريمة في الحي أمس. waqaʿat jarīma fī al-ḥayy ams. A crime took place in the neighbourhood yesterday.

The word, taken apart.
جريمة (jarīma) is built on the native Arabic root j-r-m (Lane), first "to cut off (dates from the palm)", then "to commit a crime"; jurm is a wrong done.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Before the new one: say the Arabic for to pass on, then the Arabic for a crime.

You'll want to know: مجرم.
مجرم — mujrim — "a criminal".
هرب المجرم من الشرطة. haraba al-mujrim min ash-shurṭa. The criminal ran away from the police.
Plural مجرمون.

The word, taken apart.
مجرم (mujrim) is built on the native Arabic root j-r-m (Lane), "to commit a crime". Mujrim is the Form IV participle, "one who commits a crime".

Lesson 3 of 6.
لص (liṣṣ) — a thief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a crime, then the Arabic for a criminal.

You'll want to know: لص.
لص — liṣṣ — "a thief".
دخل لص البيت في الليل. dakhala liṣṣ al-bayt fī al-layl. A thief got into the house at night.
Plural: لصوص, luṣūṣ.

The word, taken apart.
لص (liṣṣ) is filed by Lane under the root l-ṣ-ṣ, with the plural luṣūṣ. It was borrowed from Greek lēistēs, a robber (EtymDB); the path it took into Arabic is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
سرق (saraqa) — to steal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a criminal, then the Arabic for a thief.

You'll want to know: سرق.
سرق — saraqa — "to steal".
سرق اللص حقيبة السيدة. saraqa al-liṣṣ ḥaqībat as-sayyida. The thief stole the lady’s bag.
Present: يسرق, yasriq.
The passive سرق (saraqa), suriqa, is common: سرقت سيارتي, my car was stolen.

The word, taken apart.
سرق (saraqa) is built on the native Arabic root s-r-q (Lane), "to take a thing secretly"; sariqa is theft and sāriq a thief.

Lesson 5 of 6.
اعتقل (iʿtaqala) — to arrest, to detain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a thief, then the Arabic for to steal.

You'll want to know: اعتقل.
اعتقل — iʿtaqala — "to arrest, to detain".
اعتقلت الشرطة الرجل في المطار. iʿtaqalat ash-shurṭa ar-rajul fī al-maṭār. The police arrested the man at the airport.
Present: يعتقل, yaʿtaqil.

The word, taken apart.
اعتقل (iʿtaqala) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-q-l (Lane), "to tie up a camel's foreleg", the root of ʿaql, the mind that holds one back. Form VIII iʿtaqala is "to hold a person in".
