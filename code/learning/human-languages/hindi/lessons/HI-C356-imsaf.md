## You'll want to know: इंसाफ़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C356-BARABAR356-04]; assesses=[] -->

**इंसाफ़** — *iṁsāf* — "fairness, justice (everyday)".

> यह इंसाफ़ नहीं है!
>
> *yah iṁsāf nahīṃ hai!*
>
> This isn't fair!

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इंसाफ़** (*iṁsāf*) is usually traced to Arabic *inṣāf*, "equity", which reached Hindi through Persian.
