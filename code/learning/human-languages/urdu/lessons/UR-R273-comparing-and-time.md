# Review — comparing, and how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C272-AMBITION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I want to become a teacher" and "I'm happy". (*maiṅ ustād bannā chāhtā hūṅ*, *maiṅ khush hūṅ*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C273-COMPARISON-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C273-COMPARISON-02, UR-LEX-C273-BEHTAR-01, UR-LEX-C273-SUBAH-01, UR-SCRIPT-C273-SUAD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C273-DURATION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C273-DURATION-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-02, UR-LEX-C202-CHIZ202-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: The bus is cheaper than a taxi. In my view, it is better.]
- [YOU SAY: This is the best tea.]
- [YOU SAY: I've lived in Lahore for two years.]
- [YOU SAY: I work from morning to evening.]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday it rained all day.]
- [YOU WRITE: **صبح** once, and point at ص inside it]

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] "Bigger than this"? (**اس سے بڑا**.) Now the other way round: what does **سب سے** add? (The most.) "Since yesterday"? (**کل سے**.)
