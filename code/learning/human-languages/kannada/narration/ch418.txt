6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಪರ್ಯಾಯ (paryāya) — an alternative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to recover dues, then the Kannada for to submit.

You'll want to know: ಪರ್ಯಾಯ.
ಪರ್ಯಾಯ — paryāya — "an alternative".
ಬಸ್ ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ ಬೇರೆ ಪರ್ಯಾಯ ಇಲ್ಲ. bas illadiddare bēre paryāya illa. If there is no bus, there is no other option.
ಪರ್ಯಾಯವಾಗಿ, as an alternative.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರ್ಯಾಯ (paryāya) is borrowed from Sanskrit paryāya, "going round, succession, turn" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಮಾರ್ಪಡಿಸು (mārpaḍisu) — to modify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for temporary, then the Kannada for permanent.

You'll want to know: ಮಾರ್ಪಡಿಸು.
ಮಾರ್ಪಡಿಸು — mārpaḍisu — "to modify".
ದರ್ಜಿ ನನ್ನ ಅಂಗಿಯನ್ನು ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಮಾರ್ಪಡಿಸಿದನು. darji nanna aṅgiyannu svalpa mārpaḍisidanu. The tailor altered my shirt a little.
Past (I …): ಮಾರ್ಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, mārpaḍiside.
ಮಾರ್ಪಾಡು, a change.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾರ್ಪಡಿಸು (mārpaḍisu) is the causative of mārpaḍu, "to change", and is usually traced to native māṟu, "to be opposite, to take or give one thing for another" (DEDR 4834), the root of ಮಾರು, "to sell"; DEDR does not list the verb itself, so the formation is not settled.

What you've built.
