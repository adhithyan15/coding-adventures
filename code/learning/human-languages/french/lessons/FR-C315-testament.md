## You'll want to know: le testament
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C315-DEUIL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le testament** — "the will (legal)".

> Il a laissé sa maison à ses enfants dans son testament.
>
> *He left his house to his children in his will.*

*Faire son testament* — to make one's will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testament* is a learned borrowing from Latin *testāmentum* "a will", from *testārī* "to bear witness", built on *testis* "witness" (Lewis & Short).
