# رخ (rukh) — the way something faces; a direction

## You'll want to know: رخ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C409-JANIB409-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رخ** — *rukh* — "the way something faces; a direction".

> ہوا کا رخ شمال کی طرف ہے۔
>
> *hawā kā rukh shimāl kī taraf hai.*
>
> The wind is blowing towards the north.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *rukh*, "the face, a cheek": the way a thing faces is its **رخ**.
