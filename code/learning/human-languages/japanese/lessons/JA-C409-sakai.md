## You'll want to know: さかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C409-CHIIKI409-04]; assesses=[] -->

**さかい** — *sakai* — "a boundary".

> **かわが ふたつの まちの さかいです。** — *kawa ga futatsu no machi no sakai desu* — "The river is the boundary between the two towns."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さかい** (*sakai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
