# ಉಯಿಲು (uyilu) — a will (testament)

## You'll want to know: ಉಯಿಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C353-UYILU353-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಯಿಲು** — *uyilu* — "a will (testament)".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಸಾಯುವ ಮುಂಚೆ ಉಯಿಲು ಬರೆದಿದ್ದರು.
>
> *ajja sāyuva muñce uyilu barediddaru.*
>
> Grandfather had written a will before he died.

A will is "written": ಉಯಿಲು ಬರೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಯಿಲು** (*uyilu*) is usually traced to English *will*, the legal paper, which reached the South Indian languages under British courts.
