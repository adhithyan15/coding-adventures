## You'll want to know: 北方
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C436-SOUTH436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**北方** — *běifāng* — "the north of the country; northern China".

> *wǒ zhù zài běifāng dōngtiān hěn lěng* — I live in the north, where the winters are cold.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**北方** (*běifāng*) is built from 北 'north' and 方 'a quarter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pok* and *pjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
