## You'll want to know: la marée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C458-LITTORAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la marée** — "the tide".

> À marée basse, on peut marcher jusqu'à l'île.
>
> *At low tide you can walk to the island.*

*Marée haute* — high tide; *une marée noire* — an oil spill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marée* is built on *mer* 'sea' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *mare*.
