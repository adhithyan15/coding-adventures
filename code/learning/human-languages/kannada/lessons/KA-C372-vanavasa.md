## You'll want to know: ವನವಾಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C372-VANAVASA372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವನವಾಸ** — *vanavāsa* — "exile in the forest".

> ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ರಾಜಕುಮಾರ ಹನ್ನೆರಡು ವರ್ಷ ವನವಾಸ ಮಾಡಿದನು.
>
> *katheyalli rājakumāra hanneraḍu varṣa vanavāsa māḍidanu.*
>
> In the story the prince spent twelve years in exile in the forest.

The epics are full of ವನವಾಸ; today it can mean any long hard time away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವನವಾಸ** (*vanavāsa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vanavāsa*, "dwelling in the forest" (CDIAL 11265a), from *vana* "forest" and *vāsa* "dwelling".
