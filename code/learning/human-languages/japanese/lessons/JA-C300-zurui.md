## You'll want to know: ずるい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C300-KITSUNE300-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ずるい** — *zurui* — "sly; cunning".

> **ずるい きつねは たぬきを だましました。** — *zurui kitsune wa tanuki o damashimashita* — "The sly fox tricked the raccoon dog."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ずるい** (*zurui*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
