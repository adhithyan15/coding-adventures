## Script — the foot of 男, inside the walking piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-BIAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 边

**The inside first, two strokes**, the foot of 男: a stroke across that turns down and hooks, then a left-falling stroke through it.

**The walking piece, three strokes**, written last, as in 这 and 进: a dot, a small zigzag down the left, and a long flat sweep along the foot.

2 + 3 = five.
