Telugu, chapter 540: Literature.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సాహిత్యం (sāhityaṁ) — literary works; literature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a lyric, then the Telugu for folk tradition.

You'll want to know: సాహిత్యం.
సాహిత్యం — sāhityaṁ — "literary works; literature".
తెలుగు సాహిత్యం చాలా పాతది. telugu sāhityaṁ cālā pātadi. Telugu literature is very old.
The everyday word; వాఙ్మయం is more learned.

The word, taken apart.
సాహిత్యం (sāhityaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit sāhitya, "association, union; literary composition" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
రచించు (raciñcu) — to compose; to author.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a short story, then the Telugu for a piece of writing.

You'll want to know: రచించు.
రచించు — raciñcu — "to compose; to author".
ఆ కవి వంద పాటలు రచించారు. ā kavi vanda pāṭalu raciñcāru. That poet composed a hundred songs.
Past (I …): రచించాను, raciñcānu.
Formal, for books, songs and plays.

The word, taken apart.
రచించు (raciñcu) is built on Sanskrit racana, "the act of making, arranging, composing" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
