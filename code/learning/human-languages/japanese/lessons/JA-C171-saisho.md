## You'll want to know: さいしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C171-ORDER171-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さいしょ** — *saisho* — "the beginning; at first".

> **あしたは さいしょに えきに いきます。** — *ashita wa saisho ni eki ni ikimasu* — "Tomorrow I will go to the station first."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さいしょ** (*saisho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sai*, "most, utmost" (Middle Chinese *tswajH*) and *sho*, "first time, beginning" (*tsrhjo*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
