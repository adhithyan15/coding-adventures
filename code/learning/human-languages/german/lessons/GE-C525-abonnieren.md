## You'll want to know: abonnieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C525-STREAMEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**abonnieren** — "to subscribe to".

> Ich habe eine Zeitschrift über Gärten abonniert.
>
> *I've subscribed to a gardening magazine.*

Regular, Perfekt without *ge-*. The noun: *das Abonnement*, in speech *das Abo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abonnieren* was borrowed from French *abonner* "to subscribe" (EtymDB).
