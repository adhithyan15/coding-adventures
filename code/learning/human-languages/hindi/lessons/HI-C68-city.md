# शहर (shahar) — a city, a town

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-01, HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Which of the two words you have is the name of a country, and which
is the word for any country at all?

## You'll want to know: शहर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-03]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C56-ROAD-02] -->

**शहर** (*shahar*) — "a city, a town".

Persian شهر (*shahr*), "city", and behind it Old Persian *xšaça*, "kingdom,
dominion" — a शहर was first the seat of a king rather than a crowd of people.
The same word ends dozens of place names across the region.

Set it against **गाँव** (*gā̃v*), the village you already own. गाँव and शहर are
the pair Hindi uses for the whole of settled life, and a speaker will place
themselves in one or the other within a sentence of meeting you.

A form asks for your शहर on the line under your देश.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Three: देश, भारत, शहर. A country, its name, and the place in it you live.
