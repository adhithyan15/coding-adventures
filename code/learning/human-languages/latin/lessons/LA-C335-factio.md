## You'll want to know: factiō, factiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C335-AURIGA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**factiō, factiōnis** — "a racing team; a party, a faction".

> Puer factiōnem viridem amābat.
>
> *The boy loved the Green team.*

Feminine, from *faciō*. The racing teams wore colours: white, red, green and blue. English *faction*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**factiō** is built on *faciō*, "to make, to do" (EtymDB): a doing together, and so a group that acts together. English *faction* comes from it.
