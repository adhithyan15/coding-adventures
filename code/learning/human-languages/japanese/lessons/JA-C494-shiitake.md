## You'll want to know: しいたけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C494-VEG494-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しいたけ** — *shiitake* — "a shiitake mushroom".

> **しいたけを みそしるに いれます。** — *shiitake o misoshiru ni iremasu* — "I put shiitake in the miso soup."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しいたけ** (*shiitake*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
