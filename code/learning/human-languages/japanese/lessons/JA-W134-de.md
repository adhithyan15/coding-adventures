## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-DE-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-TE-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01] -->

> **て** *te* + **゛** → **で** *de*

Write **て** in full: one continuous stroke, across the top, back, then down
and round to the right. Then add the two short strokes of the voicing mark
at the upper right, the left one first. The base shape does not change.

The mark voices the opening consonant: *t* becomes *d*, and the vowel stays
*e*. It is the change you already made on **た** for **だ** and on **と** for
**ど**.

With **ん** and **わ** after it, you can now write the word from the last
lesson: **でんわ**, *denwa*, a telephone.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-DE-01, JA-SCRIPT-TE-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-DENWA] -->

1. Write **て**, then **で**, and say *te*, *de*.
2. Write **た だ**, **と ど** and **て で** in pairs, and say each one.
3. Write **でんわ** from memory and say *denwa*.
