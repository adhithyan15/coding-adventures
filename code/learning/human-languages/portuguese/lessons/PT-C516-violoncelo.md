## You'll want to know: o violoncelo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C516-ORQUESTRA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o violoncelo** — "cello".

> O violoncelo tem um som grave e bonito.
>
> *The cello has a deep, beautiful sound.*

The player is *o violoncelista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*violoncelo* comes from Italian *violoncello* (EtymDB), a diminutive of *violone*, a large viol.
