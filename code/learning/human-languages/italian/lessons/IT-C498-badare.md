## You'll want to know: badare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C498-BAMBINI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**badare** — "to look after; to mind".

> Chi bada ai bambini stasera?
>
> *Who is looking after the children tonight?*

*Badare a* + person or thing; *non badare a spese* — not to worry about the cost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*badare* is inherited from Medieval Latin *batāre* "to gape, to stare" (EtymDB): first to stand and watch, then to look after.
