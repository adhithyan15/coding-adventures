## You'll want to know: मौक्तिकम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C418-JALA418-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मौक्तिकम्** — *mauktikam* — "a pearl".

> शुक्तौ मौक्तिकं जायते।
>
> *śuktau mauktikaṃ jāyate.*
>
> A pearl forms inside an oyster.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मौक्तिकम्** (*mauktikam*) is built on *muktā*, "a pearl", with the ending *-ika*; MW gives *mauktika* as a pearl (MW).
