## You'll want to know: انعام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C382-INAM382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انعام** — *inām* — "a prize; a reward".

> اگر تم جیتے تو تمہیں انعام ملے گا۔
>
> *agar tum jīte to tumheṅ inām mile gā.*
>
> If you win, you will get a prize.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انعام** (*inām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *inʿām*, "a bestowing of favour".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
