## You'll want to know: ಚಿಂತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C83-FEELINGS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಂತೆ** (*cinte*) — "worry".

**ಚಿಂತೆ ಮಾಡಬೇಡಿ** (*cinte māḍabēḍi*) — "don't worry". The ending **‑ಬೇಡಿ**
is **ಬೇಡ**, "don't want", said politely.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A feeling, and a kind thing to say about it.
