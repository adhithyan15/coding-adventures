## You'll want to know: étirer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C504-GYM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**étirer** — "to stretch; (s'étirer) to stretch (oneself)".

> Après le sport, je m'étire pendant dix minutes.
>
> *After sport I stretch for ten minutes.*

Mostly reflexive for the body: *s'étirer*. *Un étirement* — a stretch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*étirer* is built from *é-* and *tirer* "to pull". The origin of *tirer* is not settled.
