Bengali, chapter 480: Staying Healthy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পুষ্টি (pushṭi) — nutrition; nourishment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to nod off, then the Bengali for to stretch on waking.

You'll want to know: পুষ্টি.
পুষ্টি — pushṭi — "nutrition; nourishment".
শিশুদের পুষ্টির জন্য দুধ আর ডিম দরকার। shishuder pushṭir jônyô dudh ār ḍim dôrkār. Children need milk and eggs for nourishment.

The word, taken apart.
পুষ্টি (pushṭi) is a tatsama, Sanskrit puṣṭi, "a well-nourished condition, growth, thriving" (Monier-Williams).

ধূমপান স্বাস্থ্যের জন্য খারাপ। dhūmpān svāsthyer jônyô khārāp. Smoking is bad for your health.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an addiction, then the Bengali for smoking.

You'll want to know: ঝরানো.
ঝরানো — jhôrāno — "to shed (weight, tears)".
ব্যায়াম করে বাবা পাঁচ কেজি ওজন ঝরালেন। bæyām kôre bābā pā̃ch keji ojôn jhôrālen. By exercising, my father shed five kilos.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দশ কেজি ওজন ঝরালেন, tini dôsh keji ojôn jhôrālen.

The word, taken apart.
ঝরানো (jhôrāno) is the causative of ঝরা, to fall away, which CDIAL lists for Bengali under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 5346): to make something fall away.
