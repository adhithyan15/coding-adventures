Lesson 1 of 5.
नक़्शा (naqśā) — a map.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a journey, then the Hindi for luggage.

You'll want to know: नक़्शा.
नक़्शा — naqśā — "a map".

The word, taken apart.
नक़्शा (naqśā) is a loan from Arabic naqsh, "a painting, an engraving", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is n-q-sh (Lane). Hindi naqśā came through Persian naqsha, "a drawing, a plan".

Lesson 3 of 5.
मोड़ (moṛ) — a turn, a bend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a map, then the Hindi for a way.

You'll want to know: मोड़.
मोड़ — moṛ — "a turn, a bend".

The word, taken apart.
मोड़ (moṛ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from muṭati, "twists" (CDIAL 10186). moṛ, "a turn", is the noun of moṛnā, "to twist, bend".

Lesson 4 of 5.
चौराहा (caurāhā) — a crossroads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a way, then the Hindi for a turn.

You'll want to know: चौराहा.
चौराहा — caurāhā — "a crossroads".

The word, taken apart.
चौराहा (caurāhā) is usually explained as cau-, "four", with rāh, "road", the Persian word that रास्ता (rāstā), "a way", also belongs with. CDIAL does not list it; it lists cauhaṭṭā, "a crossing of market streets", from catur-, "four" (CDIAL 4626).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: जहाज़.
जहाज़ — jahāz — "a ship, an aeroplane".

The word, taken apart.
जहाज़ (jahāz) is a loan from Arabic jahāz, "equipment, gear", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is j-h-z (Lane). In Persian and Hindi it came to mean a ship.
