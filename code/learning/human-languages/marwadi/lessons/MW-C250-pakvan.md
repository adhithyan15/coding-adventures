## You'll want to know: पकवान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C250-UCHHAV250-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पकवान** — *pakvān* — "festive dishes; fried delicacies".

> आज मां पकवान बणावै है।
>
> *āj mā̃ pakvān baṇāvai hai.*
>
> Today my mother is making festive dishes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पकवान** (*pakvān*) goes back to Sanskrit *pakvānna*, "cooked or dressed food" (Monier-Williams), built from *pakva*, "cooked", and *anna*, "food".
