Marathi, chapter 300: Threats and Bribes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धमकावणे (dhamkāvṇe) — to threaten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to confess, then the Marathi for to file.

You'll want to know: धमकावणे.
धमकावणे — dhamkāvṇe — "to threaten".
त्या गुंडाने दुकानदाराला धमकावलं. tyā guṇḍāne dukāndārālā dhamkāvlaṁ. That thug threatened the shopkeeper.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने धमकावलं, tyāne dhamkāvlaṁ.
The person threatened takes -ला; the noun is धमकी, a threat.

The word, taken apart.
धमकावणे (dhamkāvṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi dhamkāvṇe, 'to threaten', under dhammakka, 'a threat' (CDIAL 6736).

Lesson 2 of 5.
लाच (lāc) — a bribe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to file, then the Marathi for to threaten.

You'll want to know: लाच.
लाच — lāc — "a bribe".
लाच घेताना एका कारकुनाला पकडलं. lāc ghetānā ekā kārkunālā pakaḍlaṁ. A clerk was caught taking a bribe.
Feminine.
लाच (lāc) घेणे, to take a bribe; लाच (lāc) देणे, to give one.

The word, taken apart.
लाच (lāc) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit lañcā, 'a present, a bribe' (CDIAL 10914).

Lesson 3 of 5.
शिक्षा (śikṣā) — a punishment; a sentence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to threaten, then the Marathi for a bribe.

You'll want to know: शिक्षा.
शिक्षा — śikṣā — "a punishment; a sentence".
न्यायालयाने त्याला तीन वर्षांची शिक्षा दिली. nyāyālayāne tyālā tīn varṣāñcī śikṣā dilī. The court sentenced him to three years.
Feminine.
शिक्षा (śikṣā) देणे, to sentence; शिक्षा (śikṣā) होणे, to be punished. In Marathi it means punishment, not teaching.

The word, taken apart.
शिक्षा (śikṣā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit śikṣā, 'learning, teaching; also chastisement, punishment' (Monier-Williams); Marathi keeps the last sense.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पळ काढणे (paḷ kāḍhṇe) — to flee; to take to one's heels.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a bribe, then the Marathi for a punishment.

You'll want to know: पळ काढणे.
पळ काढणे — paḷ kāḍhṇe — "to flee; to take to one's heels".
पोलिसांना पाहिल्यावर चोराने पळ काढला. polisānnā pāhilyāvar corāne paḷ kāḍhlā. When he saw the police, the thief fled.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पळ काढला, tyāne paḷ kāḍhlā.
The agent takes -ने and पळ is masculine: चोराने पळ काढला. Plainer: पळून जाणे.

The word, taken apart.
पळ काढणे (paḷ kāḍhṇe) is built from paḷ, 'flight', of the family of Sanskrit palāyate, 'flees' (CDIAL 7955), and kāḍhṇe, 'to take out'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कोंडणे (koṇḍṇe) — to shut in; to lock up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a punishment, then the Marathi for to flee.

You'll want to know: कोंडणे.
कोंडणे — koṇḍṇe — "to shut in; to lock up".
चोरांनी चौकीदाराला खोलीत कोंडलं. corānnī caukīdārālā kholīt koṇḍlaṁ. The thieves locked the watchman in a room.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कोंडलं, tyāne koṇḍlaṁ.
The place takes -त: खोलीत कोंडणे (koṇḍṇe); कोंडून ठेवणे, to keep shut in.

The word, taken apart.
कोंडणे (koṇḍṇe) is a Marathi verb of shutting in; its origin is not settled.
