5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
зубная паста (zubnáya pásta) — toothpaste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for main, then the Russian for ordinary.

You'll want to know: зубная паста.
зубная паста (zubnáya pásta) — "toothpaste".

The word, taken apart.
зубная паста (zubnáya pásta) is "tooth paste": зубная, from зуб, "tooth", with паста, "a paste", which Vasmer thinks more likely from Italian pasta, "dough", than from Greek.

Lesson 4 of 5.
пылесос (pylesós) — a vacuum cleaner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a razor, then the Russian for an iron.

You'll want to know: пылесос.
пылесос (pylesós) — "a vacuum cleaner".

The word, taken apart.
пылесос (pylesós) is пыль, "dust", with -о- and сос, from сосать, "to suck": a dust-sucker. Vasmer traces сосать to a PIE sound-word su-, as in Latin sūgō, "I suck".
