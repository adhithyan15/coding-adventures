Hindi, chapter 362: What the Figures Say.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निष्कर्ष (niṣkarṣ) — a conclusion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to gauge, then the Hindi for a laboratory.

You'll want to know: निष्कर्ष.
निष्कर्ष — niṣkarṣ — "a conclusion".
बहस के बाद हम एक निष्कर्ष पर पहुँचे। bahas ke bād ham ek niṣkarṣ par pahuñce. After the debate we reached a conclusion.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
निष्कर्ष (niṣkarṣ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit niṣkarṣa, "drawing out, extracting" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for the conclusion drawn out of an argument.

उभारना — ubhārnā — "to bring out, to raise (a question)".
इस प्रयोग ने कई नए सवाल उभारे। is prayog ne kaī nae savāl ubhāre. This experiment raised several new questions.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने वही सवाल उभारा, usne vahī savāl ubhārā.

The word, taken apart.
उभारना (ubhārnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit udbharati, "raises up" (CDIAL 2038).
