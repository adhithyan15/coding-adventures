## Chapters 159-161: the past, the future and practical texts

Marathi had realized two of its five A2 spine nodes. Three chapters realize
the other three, so the A2 gate reports no missing spine node. Vocabulary and
verb vocabulary remain.

- Chapter 159 (SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-PAST): *अलीकडे* alīkaḍe, *पूर्वी* pūrvī,
  *आधीच* ādhīc, *भूतकाळ* bhūtkāḷ, *घडणे* ghaḍṇe. The notes introduce the
  past in **-ले / -लो**: *वाचले*, *जेवलो*, *होते*, *घडले*.
- Chapter 160 (SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE): *भविष्य* bhaviṣya, *लवकरच*
  lavkarac, *पुढचा / पुढची / पुढचे* puḍhcā, *यानंतर* yānantar, *आखणे*
  ākhṇe. The notes introduce the future in **-ईन / -ईल**: *जाईन*, *येईन*,
  *चालेन*, *आखीन*.
- Chapter 161 (SPINE-READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS): *वेळापत्रक* veḷāpatrak,
  *सूचना* sūcanā, *भाडे* bhāḍe, *जाहिरात* jāhirāt, *पावती* pāvtī. Each lesson
  reads a short sign, timetable or advert (*खोली भाड्याने देणे आहे*).
- No new forward reference and no unwritten letter. Every headword is screened
  against the letters the track has taught to write and against earlier lesson
  bodies. *काल* and *उद्या* already appear untaught, and *बेत*, *योजना*,
  *ठरवणे* and *नंतर* are already taught, so all six were left out.
- The extensions carry stage A2. Two reviews close the three chapters.
