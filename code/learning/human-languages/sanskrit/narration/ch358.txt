Sanskrit, chapter 358: Good Conduct.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आचारः (ācāraḥ) — conduct; custom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a secretary, then the Sanskrit for directs.

You'll want to know: आचारः.
आचारः — ācāraḥ — "conduct; custom".
मीरायाः आचारः उत्तमः अस्ति। mīrāyāḥ ācāraḥ uttamaḥ asti. Mira's conduct is excellent.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आचारः (ācāraḥ) is built on ā and the root √car, "to move"; MW gives ā-cāra "conduct, behaviour" and "custom, practice" (MW).
