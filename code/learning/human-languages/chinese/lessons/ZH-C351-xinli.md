## You'll want to know: 心理

**心理** — *xīnlǐ* — "state of mind; psychology".

> *wǒ juéde zhè shì xīnlǐ wèntí* — I think it's all in the mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**心理** (*xīnlǐ*) is built from 心 'the heart' and 理 'order', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sim* and *liX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to put in order'.
