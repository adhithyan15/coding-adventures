Lesson 1 of 5.
la cuisine — a kitchen; cooking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for pepper, then the French for a walnut.

You'll want to know: la cuisine.
la cuisine — "a kitchen; cooking". The noun behind cuisiner.

The word, taken apart.
cuisine from Vulgar Latin cocina from Latin coquina, "kitchen", from coquere, "to cook" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la chaise — a chair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a kitchen; cooking, then the French for a window.

You'll want to know: la chaise.
la chaise — "a chair".

The word, taken apart.
chaise from Latin cathedra, "a seat", from Greek kathedra (Lewis & Short) — inherited. Old French said chaiere, which EtymDB 2.1 traces to cathedra; Paris speech turned its r into a z. The older form lives on in the French word for a pulpit.
