## You'll want to know: গাঁথা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C492-KARIGOR492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**গাঁথা** — *gā̃thā* — "to string together; to lay (bricks)".

> মজুর ইট গেঁথে দেওয়াল বানাল।
>
> *Mojur iṭ gẽthe deoyāl bānālô.*
>
> The labourer laid the bricks and built a wall.

Past (he/she …): তিনি ফুল গাঁথলেন, *tini phul gā̃thlen*.

The -এ form raises the vowel: গেঁথে, having laid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গাঁথা** (*gā̃thā*) is a tadbhava from Sanskrit *granthayati*, "ties, strings together"; CDIAL gives Bengali *gā̃thā* for threading and for laying bricks (CDIAL 4353).
