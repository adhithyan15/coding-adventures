## You'll want to know: લટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C470-CAHERO470-04]; assesses=[] -->

**લટ** — *laṭ* — "a lock of hair".

> પવનથી તેના કપાળ પર એક લટ આવી ગઈ.
>
> *pavanthī tenā kapāḷ par ek laṭ āvī gaī.*
>
> The wind blew a lock of hair onto her forehead.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લટ** (*laṭ*) probably goes back to a reconstructed *\*laṭṭa-*, "hanging, tangled", under which CDIAL lists the Gujarati *laṭiyā̃*, "matted locks" (CDIAL 10918).
