Marathi, chapter 121: To be able to, To manage to, Skill, Ability, Right.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शकणे (śakṇe) — to be able to, can.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a country, then the Marathi for a story.
