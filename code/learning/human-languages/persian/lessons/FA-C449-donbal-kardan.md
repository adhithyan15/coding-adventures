## You'll want to know: دنبال کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C449-MASIRYAB-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دنبال کردن** (*donbâl kardan*) — "to follow".

> ما ماشین دوستمان را تا روستا دنبال کردیم.
>
> *Mâ mâshin-e dustemân râ tâ rustâ donbâl kardim.*
>
> We followed our friend's car to the village.

*donbâl* 'behind, the tail' + *kardan*. Also to look for: *donbâl-e kelid migardam*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*donbâl kardan* is built from native *donbâl* 'tail, the back', whose older history is not settled, and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
