## You'll want to know: وفي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C499-AMANA499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**وفي** — *wafī* — "faithful".

> صديقها القديم وفي جدا.
>
> *ṣadīquhā al-qadīm wafī jiddan.*
>
> Her old friend is very faithful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وفي** (*wafī*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-f-y*, "to fulfil, to keep a promise" (Lane). A wafī person keeps their word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
