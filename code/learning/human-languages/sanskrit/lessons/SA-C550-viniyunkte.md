## You'll want to know: विनियुंक्ते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C550-AMSA550-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विनियुंक्ते** — *viniyuṅkte* — "puts to use".

> यदि सः धनं सम्यक् विनियुंक्ते, तर्हि सः धनिकः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi saḥ dhanaṃ samyak viniyuṅkte, tarhi saḥ dhanikaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If he puts his money to good use, he will become rich.

Past (he …): सः धनं सम्यक् विनियुक्तवान्, *saḥ dhanaṃ samyak viniyuktavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विनियुंक्ते** (*viniyuṅkte*) is built on *vi*, *ni* and the root √yuj, "to yoke"; MW gives *viniyuj* as to separate, and to assign, to commit, to appoint to a use (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
