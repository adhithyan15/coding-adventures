## You'll want to know: শহিদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C317-BIR317-03]; assesses=[] -->

**শহিদ** — *shôhid* — "a martyr".

> স্বাধীনতার যুদ্ধে অনেক মানুষ শহিদ হলেন।
>
> *svādhīnôtār juddhe ônek mānush shôhid hôlen.*
>
> Many people gave their lives in the war of independence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শহিদ** (*shôhid*) is usually traced through Persian to Arabic *shahīd*, 'a witness', and so one who bears witness with his life. শহিদ হওয়া, to die for a cause.
