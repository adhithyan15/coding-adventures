## You'll want to know: o tempero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C331-REFOGAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o tempero** — "the seasoning".

> O segredo deste prato é o tempero.
>
> *The secret of this dish is the seasoning.*

Built on *temperar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tempero* is built on *temperar* "to season" (EtymDB), from Latin *temperāre* "to mix in due proportion".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
