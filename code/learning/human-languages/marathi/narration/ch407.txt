Marathi, chapter 407: Distance and Speed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अंतर (antar) — a distance; a gap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a flyover, then the Marathi for to gaze at.

You'll want to know: अंतर.
अंतर — antar — "a distance; a gap".
घर ते शाळा हे अंतर किती आहे? ghar te śāḷā he antar kitī āhe? What is the distance from home to school?
Neuter.
X ते Y, from X to Y: घर ते शाळा.

The word, taken apart.
अंतर (antar) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit antara, "interior; interval, space between" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: वेग.
वेग — veg — "speed".
घाटात बसचा वेग कमी असतो. ghāṭāt bascā veg kamī asto. On the ghat road the bus goes at a low speed.
Masculine.
वेगाने, at speed, fast; वेग (veg) वाढवणे, to speed up.

The word, taken apart.
वेग (veg) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vega, "violent agitation, rush, speed" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पायपीट (pāypīṭ) — a long trudge; a long walk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a brake, then the Marathi for a signpost.

You'll want to know: पायपीट.
पायपीट — pāypīṭ — "a long trudge; a long walk".
बस नव्हती म्हणून आमची खूप पायपीट झाली. bas navhatī mhaṇūn āmcī khūp pāypīṭ jhālī. There was no bus, so we had a long trudge on foot.
Feminine.
पायपीट (pāypīṭ) होणे or पायपीट (pāypīṭ) करणे; it sounds tiring.

The word, taken apart.
पायपीट (pāypīṭ) is built from पाय, "a foot", and pīṭ, a beating, from the family of Sanskrit piṭṭayati, "beats" (CDIAL 8165): beating the road with your feet.
