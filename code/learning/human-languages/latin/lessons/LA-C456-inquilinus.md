## You'll want to know: inquilīnus, inquilīnī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C456-INCOLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**inquilīnus, inquilīnī** — "a tenant, a lodger".

> Inquilīnus dominō mercēdem dat.
>
> *The tenant pays the landlord his rent.*

Second declension, masculine: *inquilīnus, inquilīnī m.* Someone living in a house that is not his own; most people in a big city rented.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inquilīnus* is built on *incolō* 'to live in', from *in-* and *colō* 'to dwell, to till' (Lewis & Short, from an older *incolīnus*). EtymDB lists it without a parent.
