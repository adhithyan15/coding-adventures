Russian, chapter 339: Changing minds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
убеждать (ubezhdát) — to convince, to persuade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for proof, then the Russian for convincing.

You'll want to know: убеждать.
убеждать (ubezhdát) — "to convince, to persuade".
Я убеждал друга поехать с нами, но он не захотел. Ya ubezhdál drúga poyékhat s námi, no on ne zakhotél. I tried to persuade my friend to come with us, but he did not want to.
Imperfective; perfective убедить, which has no form for я in the future. Убеждать кого-то + infinitive, or в + prepositional.

The word, taken apart.
убеждать (ubezhdát) is the imperfective of убедить, on the root of беда "trouble", Proto-Slavic běda (EtymDB). The жд is the Church Slavonic way of softening д; native Russian softens it to ж, as in вожу beside водить.

Lesson 2 of 5.
переубеждать (pereubezhdát) — to change someone’s mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for convincing, then the Russian for to convince.

You'll want to know: переубеждать.
переубеждать (pereubezhdát) — "to change someone’s mind".
Маму невозможно переубедить. Mámu nevozmózhno pereubedít. It is impossible to change Mum’s mind.
Imperfective; perfective переубедить. Пере- adds the sense of over again: to convince someone the other way.

The word, taken apart.
переубеждать (pereubezhdát) is built from пере- "over, again" and убеждать (ubezhdát) "to convince", whose root is that of беда, Proto-Slavic běda (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
убеждение (ubezhdéniye) — a conviction, a firm belief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to convince, then the Russian for to change someone’s mind.

You'll want to know: убеждение.
убеждение (ubezhdéniye) — "a conviction, a firm belief".
Он не ест мясо по убеждению. On ne yest myáso po ubezhdéniyu. He does not eat meat out of conviction.
Neuter. По убеждению — out of conviction. The plural убеждения means one’s beliefs.

The word, taken apart.
убеждение (ubezhdéniye) is built on убедить "to convince" with the noun suffix -ение, keeping the Church Slavonic жд. The root is that of беда, Proto-Slavic běda (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
уверенность (uvérennost) — confidence, certainty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to change someone’s mind, then the Russian for a conviction.

You'll want to know: уверенность.
уверенность (uvérennost) — "confidence, certainty".
Мне не хватает уверенности в себе. Mne ne khvatáyet uvérennosti v sebé. I lack self-confidence.
Feminine. Уверенность в себе — self-confidence. From уверенный, sure.

The word, taken apart.
уверенность (uvérennost) is built on уверенный "sure", from уверить "to assure", and so on вера "faith", inherited from Proto-Slavic věra; EtymDB names Latin vērus "true" as a cousin (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
сомневаться (somnevátsya) — to doubt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a conviction, then the Russian for confidence.

You'll want to know: сомневаться.
сомневаться (somnevátsya) — "to doubt".
Я сомневаюсь, что он придёт вовремя. Ya somneváyus, chto on pridyót vovrémya. I doubt that he will come on time.
Imperfective only. Сомневаться в + prepositional: я сомневаюсь в этом.

The word, taken apart.
сомневаться (somnevátsya) is built on the root of мнить "to think, to suppose" (EtymDB lists mnitĭ "to think"), the root of мнение "opinion", with the prefix со-. EtymDB sets Latin mēns "mind" beside мнить as a cousin.
