## You'll want to know: susurrus, susurrī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C301-SUSURRUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**susurrus, susurrī** — "a whisper, a murmur".

> Per tōtam urbem susurrus erat.
>
> *There was a whisper all through the city.*

Masculine. A sound word: the hum of bees, the rustle of wind, the murmur of gossip. English *susurration*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**susurrus** is a doubled word for a low hum, which Lewis & Short build on a root *sur* for sound. English *susurration* comes from its family.
