Spanish, chapter 579: Críticas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
criticar — to criticise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for in favour, then the Spanish for opposed.

You'll want to know: criticar.
criticar — "to criticise".
No me gusta criticar a la gente. I do not like criticising people.
Spelling: critiqué in the past, critique in the subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
criticar is built on crítico, a learned borrowing from Latin criticus and Greek kritikos 'able to judge', from krinein 'to separate, to decide' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.
contradecir — to contradict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to criticise, then the Spanish for the criticism.

You'll want to know: contradecir.
contradecir — "to contradict".
No me gusta contradecir a mi jefe. I do not like contradicting my boss.
Like decir: contradigo, contradije, contradicho.

The word, taken apart.
contradecir is a learned borrowing from Latin contradicere 'to speak against', built from contra and dicere 'to say', and rebuilt on decir (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
reconocer — to admit; to recognise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the criticism, then the Spanish for to contradict.

You'll want to know: reconocer.
reconocer — "to admit; to recognise".
Reconozco que me equivoqué. I admit that I was wrong.
Like conocer: reconozco. In a debate it means to admit a point.

The word, taken apart.
reconocer is inherited from Latin recognoscere 'to know again, to examine', built on cognoscere 'to get to know', the same verb as conocer (EtymDB).
