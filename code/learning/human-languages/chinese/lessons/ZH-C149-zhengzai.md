## You'll want to know: 正在
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C149-ZHENGZAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**正在** — *zhèngzài* — "in the middle of (doing)".

> **我正在吃饭** *wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn* — I'm eating right now.

> **他正在看书** *tā zhèngzài kàn shū* — He's reading at the moment.

The next lesson puts the first character in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**正在** (*zhèngzài*) is built from 正 'exactly' and 在 'at', the 在 you write. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyengH* and *dzojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'exactly fitting; proper' and 'to dwell; to exist'. The use before a verb, in the middle of doing it, is the modern one and is assumed here, not quoted.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C85-ZAI-01] -->

正在 goes before the verb: 我, 正在, 吃饭.

在 without 正 can do the same job before a verb; that comes two lessons on.
