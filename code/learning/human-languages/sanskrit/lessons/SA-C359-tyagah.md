## You'll want to know: त्यागः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C359-TYAGA359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**त्यागः** — *tyāgaḥ* — "renunciation; giving up".

> सैनिकस्य त्यागः महान् भवति।
>
> *sainikasya tyāgaḥ mahān bhavati.*
>
> A soldier's sacrifice is great.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्यागः** (*tyāgaḥ*) comes from the root √tyaj, "to leave, to abandon"; MW gives *tyāga* "leaving, abandoning" and "giving up, a gift" (MW).
