Kannada, chapter 468: Size and Quality.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಗಾತ್ರ (gātra) — size.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a sale, then the Kannada for to buy for oneself.

You'll want to know: ಗಾತ್ರ.
ಗಾತ್ರ — gātra — "size".
ಈ ಅಂಗಿ ನನ್ನ ಗಾತ್ರಕ್ಕೆ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿಲ್ಲ. ī aṅgi nanna gātrakke sariyāgilla. This shirt is not my size.
In Sanskrit a limb or the body; Kannada uses it for the size of things.

The word, taken apart.
ಗಾತ್ರ (gātra) is borrowed from Sanskrit gātra, "a limb, the body" (CDIAL 4124); in Kannada it came to mean bulk and size.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ಅಸಲಿ (asali) — genuine; real.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to buy for oneself, then the Kannada for size.

You'll want to know: ಅಸಲಿ.
ಅಸಲಿ — asali — "genuine; real".
ಇದು ಅಸಲಿ ರೇಷ್ಮೆ ಅಂತ ಅಂಗಡಿಯವನು ಹೇಳಿದನು. idu asali rēṣme anta aṅgaḍiyavanu hēḷidanu. The shopkeeper said this is genuine silk.
Its opposite is ನಕಲಿ, a fake, from ನಕಲು, a copy.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಸಲಿ (asali) is usually traced to Arabic aṣl, "root, origin", through Hindustani aslī, "original, genuine".

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ (guṇamaṭṭa) — quality; standard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for size, then the Kannada for genuine.

You'll want to know: ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ.
ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ — guṇamaṭṭa — "quality; standard".
ಈ ಅಂಗಡಿಯ ಬಟ್ಟೆಗಳ ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ. ī aṅgaḍiya baṭṭegaḷa guṇamaṭṭa cennāgide. The quality of the clothes in this shop is good.
ಗುಣ, a quality, plus ಮಟ್ಟ, a level.

The word, taken apart.
ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ (guṇamaṭṭa) joins Sanskrit guṇa, "a quality" (CDIAL 4190), and native maṭṭa, "measure, level, limit" (DEDR 4660).

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಖಾತರಿ (khātari) — a guarantee; an assurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for genuine, then the Kannada for quality.

You'll want to know: ಖಾತರಿ.
ಖಾತರಿ — khātari — "a guarantee; an assurance".
ಈ ಫ್ಯಾನಿಗೆ ಎರಡು ವರ್ಷದ ಖಾತರಿ ಇದೆ. ī phyānige eraḍu varṣada khātari ide. This fan has a two-year guarantee.
ಖಾತರಿ (khātari) ಕೊಡು, to give an assurance.

The word, taken apart.
ಖಾತರಿ (khātari) came into Kannada through Hindustani; it is usually traced to Arabic khāṭir, "the mind, regard", but how it came to mean an assurance is not settled.

ಮಾಸು — māsu — "to fade; to grow dull".
ಎರಡು ಸಲ ತೊಳೆದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಂಗಿಯ ಬಣ್ಣ ಮಾಸಿತು. eraḍu sala toḷeda mēle aṅgiya baṇṇa māsitu. After two washes the colour of the shirt faded.
Past (it …): ಮಾಸಿತು, māsitu.
Of colours, cloth and old paint.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾಸು (māsu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4781 lists Kannada māsu, "to grow dim, to be obscured, to fade".
