## You'll want to know: l'hebdomadaire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C278-PRESSE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'hebdomadaire** — "the weekly (paper or magazine)".

> Ma grand-mère lit un hebdomadaire sur la cuisine.
>
> *My grandmother reads a weekly about cooking.*

Often *un hebdo* in speech. As an adjective: *une réunion hebdomadaire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hebdomadaire* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *hebdomadārius* (EtymDB), built on Greek *hebdomás* "a group of seven, a week".
