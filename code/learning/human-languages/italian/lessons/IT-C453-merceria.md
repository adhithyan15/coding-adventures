## You'll want to know: la merceria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C453-NEGOZI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la merceria** — "the haberdashery".

> In merceria vendono bottoni e filo.
>
> *At the haberdasher's they sell buttons and thread.*

Buttons, thread, ribbons and zips.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*merceria* is built on *merce* 'goods', which is inherited from Latin *merx, mercis* 'goods' (Lewis & Short).
