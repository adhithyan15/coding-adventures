## You'll want to know: سد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C469-TALAB-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سد** (*sadd*) — "a dam".

> پشت سد یک دریاچه بزرگ است.
>
> *Posht-e sadd yek daryâche-ye bozorg ast.*
>
> Behind the dam there is a big lake.

Also a barrier: *sadd-e râh* — an obstacle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sadd* was borrowed from Arabic *sadd* 'dam, barrier' (EtymDB).
