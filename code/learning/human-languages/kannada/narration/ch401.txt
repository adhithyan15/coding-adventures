5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ (abhivṛddhi) — development.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to utilise, then the Kannada for to waste.

You'll want to know: ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ.
ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ — abhivṛddhi — "development".
ಹೊಸ ರಸ್ತೆಯಿಂದ ಊರಿನ ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ ಆಯಿತು. hosa rasteyinda ūrina abhivṛddhi āyitu. The new road brought development to the town.
ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ (abhivṛddhi) ಹೊಂದು, to develop.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ (abhivṛddhi) is borrowed from Sanskrit abhivṛddhi, "growth, increase, prosperity" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹಣಕಾಸು (haṇakāsu) — finance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for development, then the Kannada for economic.

You'll want to know: ಹಣಕಾಸು.
ಹಣಕಾಸು — haṇakāsu — "finance".
ಮನೆಯ ಹಣಕಾಸು ಅಮ್ಮನೇ ನೋಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾಳೆ. maneya haṇakāsu ammanē nōḍikoḷḷuttāḷe. Mother herself looks after the household finances.
ಹಣ (money) + ಕಾಸು (coin).

The word, taken apart.
ಹಣಕಾಸು (haṇakāsu) is built from haṇa, "money", paired with native kāsu, "a small coin, money" (DEDR 1431).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for economic, then the Kannada for finance.

You'll want to know: ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸು.
ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸು — uttējisu — "to promote; to boost".
ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಸಣ್ಣ ಉದ್ಯಮಗಳನ್ನು ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸಿತು. sarkāra saṇṇa udyamagaḷannu uttējisitu. The government boosted small businesses.
Past (I …): ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸಿದೆ, uttējiside.
ಉತ್ತೇಜನ, encouragement.

The word, taken apart.
ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸು (uttējisu) is built on Sanskrit uttejana, "incitement, encouragement, stimulation" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹೂಡು (hūḍu) — to invest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for finance, then the Kannada for to promote.

You'll want to know: ಹೂಡು.
ಹೂಡು — hūḍu — "to invest".
ನಾನು ಉಳಿಸಿದ ಹಣವನ್ನು ಒಂದು ಅಂಗಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹೂಡಿದೆ. nānu uḷisida haṇavannu ondu aṅgaḍiyalli hūḍide. I invested the money I had saved in a shop.
Past (I …): ಹೂಡಿದೆ, hūḍide.
ಹೂಡಿಕೆ, an investment. Also to yoke: ಎತ್ತು ಹೂಡು (hūḍu).

The word, taken apart.
ಹೂಡು (hūḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4361 lists Kannada pūḍu, "to join, to tie to, to yoke, to fix, to arrange"; old p- became h-, and "to put money to work" is the modern sense.
