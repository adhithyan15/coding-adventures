Kannada, chapter 125: Idli, Dosa, Vada, Upma, Finger millet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಇಡ್ಲಿ (iḍli) — an idli.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for garlic, then the Kannada for honey.

You'll want to know: ಇಡ್ಲಿ.
ಇಡ್ಲಿ — iḍli — "an idli".

The word, taken apart.
ಇಡ್ಲಿ (iḍli) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil iṭṭali and Malayalam iṭṭali (DEDR 455). DEDR lists iḍḍali, iḍḍalige, a steamed cake of rice flour and black gram. DEDR 455 compares Sanskrit iṇḍarī.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ವಡೆ (vaḍe) — a vada.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an idli, then the Kannada for a dosa.

You'll want to know: ವಡೆ.
ವಡೆ — vaḍe — "a vada".

The word, taken apart.
ವಡೆ (vaḍe) comes from Sanskrit vaṭaka, "round cake of pulse fried in oil", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 11213). Prakrit vaḍaga shows the softened middle.
