Urdu, chapter 380: Getting Ready.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طریقہ (tarīqā) — a method; a way (of doing).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for without a break, then the Urdu for to go over again.

You'll want to know: طریقہ.
طریقہ — tarīqā — "a method; a way (of doing)".
اگر یہ طریقہ نہیں چلا تو ہم دوسرا سوچیں گے۔ agar yih tarīqā nahīṅ chalā to ham dūsrā socheṅ ge. If this method does not work, we will think of another.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
طریقہ (tarīqā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṭarīqa, "a path, a way".

Lesson 4 of 5.
سوجھنا (sūjhnā) — to occur to (someone); to strike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for practice, then the Urdu for nerve.

You'll want to know: سوجھنا.
سوجھنا — sūjhnā — "to occur to (someone); to strike".
اگر مجھے کوئی حل سوجھا تو میں بتاؤں گا۔ agar mujhe koī hal sūjhā to maiṅ batāūṅ gā. If a solution occurs to me, I will tell you.
Past (it …): وہ خیال اچانک سوجھا, voh khayāl achānak sūjhā.

The word, taken apart.
سوجھنا (sūjhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sūjhnā, "to be perceptible, to occur to", under Sanskrit śúdhyati, "becomes clear" (CDIAL 12527); سجھانا, "to suggest", is its partner.

Lesson 5 of 5.
جٹنا (juṭnā) — to get down to (a task); to apply oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for nerve, then the Urdu for to occur to.

You'll want to know: جٹنا.
جٹنا — juṭnā — "to get down to (a task); to apply oneself".
اگر سب جٹ گئے تو کام جلدی ختم ہو گا۔ agar sab juṭ gae to kām jaldī khatm ho gā. If everyone gets down to work, it will soon be done.
Past (he …): وہ کام میں جٹ گیا, voh kām meṅ juṭ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
جٹنا (juṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin juṭnā, "to come together", under yuṭati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10496); the sense "to throw oneself into a task" is a later one our sources do not trace.
