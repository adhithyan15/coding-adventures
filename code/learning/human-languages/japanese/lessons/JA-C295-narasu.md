## You'll want to know: ならす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C295-HATSUMOUDE295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ならす** — *narasu* — "to ring; to sound".

> **てらで かねを ならしました。** — *tera de kane o narashimashita* — "We rang the bell at the temple."

Polite **ならします**, *narashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おとこが かねを ならしました。**, *otoko ga kane o narashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ならす** (*narasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
