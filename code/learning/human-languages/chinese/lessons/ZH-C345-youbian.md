## You'll want to know: yóubiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C345-LOOKBACK345-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yóubiān* — "postcode".

> *nǐ jiā de yóubiān shì duōshao* — What's your postcode?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yóubiān* is built from *yóu* 'the post' and *biān* 'to arrange', a short form of a longer name. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuw* and *pjien* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which calls the first a lodge on the border; the dictionary lists the second under *pen* and *pjien*, both glossed 'to put in order'.
