## You'll want to know: मुद्दा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C343-MUDDA343-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मुद्दा** — *muddā* — "a point at issue; a point in an argument".

> हा मुद्दा फार महत्त्वाचा आहे.
>
> *hā muddā phār mahattvācā āhe.*
>
> This point is very important.

Masculine. Plural: मुद्दे, *mudde*.

मुद्द्यावर येणे, to come to the point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुद्दा** (*muddā*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic *muddaʿā*, "a thing claimed or alleged", from the same Arabic root as *daʿwā*, "a claim"; in Marathi it is the point under discussion.
