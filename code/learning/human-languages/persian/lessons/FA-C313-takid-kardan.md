## You'll want to know: تاکید کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C313-AMAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تاکید کردن** (*ta'kid kardan*) — "to stress, to emphasise".

> پزشک تاکید کرد که بیمار باید استراحت کند.
>
> *Pezeshk ta'kid kard ke bimâr bâyad esterâhat konad.*
>
> The doctor stressed that the patient had to rest.

With *bar* or a *ke* clause: *bar in nokte ta'kid kard*. Careful print adds a hamza sign. Present *ta'kid mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'kid* is borrowed from Arabic *ta'kīd*, "emphasis, confirmation" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
