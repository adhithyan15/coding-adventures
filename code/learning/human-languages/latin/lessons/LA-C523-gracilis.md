## You'll want to know: gracilis, gracile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C523-ROBU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**gracilis, gracile** — "slender, slim".

> Puella gracilis et alta est.
>
> *The girl is slender and tall.*

Two endings. Slim in a graceful way; *macer* is thin from want.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**gracilis** is a native Latin adjective; Lewis & Short compares an old Latin word for "slender", but the origin is not settled. English *gracile* comes from it; *grace* does not.
