## You'll want to know: ঠাসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C311-MELA311-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠাসা** — *ṭhāsā* — "packed; crammed".

> মেলার দিন বাসগুলো লোকে ঠাসা ছিল।
>
> *melār din bāsgulo loke ṭhāsā chhilô.*
>
> On the day of the fair the buses were packed with people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠাসা** (*ṭhāsā*) is filed in CDIAL 5499 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *ṭhass-*, 'press down', which lists Bengali *thāsā*, 'to knead, cram'; ঠাসা is the crammed result.
