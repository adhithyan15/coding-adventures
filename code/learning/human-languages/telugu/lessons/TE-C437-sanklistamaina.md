## You'll want to know: సంక్లిష్టమైన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C437-SANKLISTAM437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సంక్లిష్టమైన** — *saṅkliṣṭamaina* — "complicated; complex".

> ఆ సమస్య చాలా సంక్లిష్టమైనది.
>
> *ā samasya cālā saṅkliṣṭamainadi.*
>
> That problem is very complicated.

సంక్లిష్టత, complexity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంక్లిష్టమైన** (*saṅkliṣṭamaina*) is built on Sanskrit *saṁkliṣṭa*, "pressed together; beset with difficulties" (Monier-Williams), with Telugu -మైన for an adjective.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
