## You'll want to know: 期间
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C342-AUTUMN342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**期间** — *qījiān* — "period (during which); during".

> **中秋期间他给家人买了月饼** *Zhōngqiū qījiān tā gěi jiārén mǎi le yuèbǐng* — Over the moon festival he bought mooncakes for his family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**期间** (*qījiān*) is built from 期 'a set time' and 间 'between', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gi* and *kean* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the second under an older form of the character and glosses it 'a gap'. Middle Chinese *gi* would regularly give qí; the level tone of qī is irregular.
