# పిడుగు (piḍugu) — a thunderbolt; a lightning strike

## You'll want to know: పిడుగు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C479-PIDUGU479-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పిడుగు** — *piḍugu* — "a thunderbolt; a lightning strike".

> పొలంలో తాటిచెట్టు మీద పిడుగు పడింది.
>
> *polaṁlō tāṭiceṭṭu mīda piḍugu paḍindi.*
>
> Lightning struck a palm tree in the field.

పిడుగు పడు, lightning strikes. Bad news that comes out of nowhere is also a పిడుగు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పిడుగు** (*piḍugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4150 lists Telugu *piḍugu*, "a thunderbolt, lightning", with Kannada *piḍugu*.
