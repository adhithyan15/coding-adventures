Telugu, chapter 86: Slower, Please.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నెమ్మదిగా (nemmadigā) — slowly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for anger, then the Telugu for sorrow.

You'll want to know: నెమ్మదిగా.
నెమ్మదిగా (nemmadigā) — "slowly".
నెమ్మదిగా (nemmadigā) మాట్లాడండి — "please speak slowly". You have the verb from your first chapters and the polite ending from raṇḍi.
Notice the -గా at the end: the same ending that made alasaṭagā and caligā.

What you've built.
The request a beginner needs most.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఇంకోసారి (iṅkōsāri) — once more.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for sorrow, then the Telugu for slowly.

You'll want to know: ఇంకోసారి.
ఇంకోసారి (iṅkōsāri) — "once more, one more time".
ఇంకోసారి (iṅkōsāri) చెప్పండి — "please say it once more". సారి is "a time, an occasion", and ఇంకో is "another".

What you've built.
A way to hear it again without saying you did not understand.

Lesson 3 of 5.
గట్టిగా (gaṭṭigā) — loudly; firmly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for slowly, then the Telugu for once more.

You'll want to know: గట్టిగా.
గట్టిగా (gaṭṭigā) — "loudly", and also "firmly, tightly".
కొంచెం గట్టిగా (gaṭṭigā) మాట్లాడండి — "please speak a little louder". కొంచెం softens it, as it softens any request.

What you've built.
Slowly and loudly: the two fixes for a sentence you could not catch.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఒక్క నిమిషం (okka nimiṣaṁ) — one minute, a moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for once more, then the Telugu for loudly.

You'll want to know: ఒక్క నిమిషం.
ఒక్క నిమిషం (okka nimiṣaṁ) — "one minute", said the way English says "a moment".
నిమిషం is "a minute", and ఒక్క is "a single one", a close relative of ఒకటి, the "one" you count with.

What you've built.
A way to buy time while you find the word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఆగండి (āgaṇḍi) — please wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for loudly, then the Telugu for one minute.

You'll want to know: ఆగండి.
ఆగండి (āgaṇḍi) — "please wait", or "please stop".
It is the verb ఆగు with the polite -అండి. ఆగండి (āgaṇḍi), ఒక్క నిమిషం (okka nimiṣaṁ) — "wait, one minute".

What you've built.
Wait, slower, louder, once more: enough to hold a conversation you cannot yet follow.

[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for loudly, then the Telugu for one minute, then say āgaṇḍi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
