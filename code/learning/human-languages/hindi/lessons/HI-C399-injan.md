## You'll want to know: इंजन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C399-LAIN399-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इंजन** — *injan* — "an engine".

> इंजन बहुत पुराना था, पर तेज़ चलता था।
>
> *injan bahut purānā thā, par tez caltā thā.*
>
> The engine was very old, but it ran fast.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इंजन** (*injan*) is English *engine*, taken over with the railways; the English soft g is said with ज.
