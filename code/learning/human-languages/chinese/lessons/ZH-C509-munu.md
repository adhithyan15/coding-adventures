## You'll want to know: 母女
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C509-PARENTS509-03]; assesses=[] -->

**母女** — *mǔnǚ* — "mother and daughter".

> **母女两个人都喜欢去舞会** *mǔnǚ liǎng ge rén dōu xǐhuan qù wǔhuì* — Mother and daughter both love going to dances.

Two third tones meet in this word: mǔ is said with a rising tone before nǚ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**母女** (*mǔnǚ*) is built from 母 'a mother' and 女 'a daughter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *muwX* and *nrjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
