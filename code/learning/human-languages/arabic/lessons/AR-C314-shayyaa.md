## You'll want to know: شيع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C314-JANAZA314-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شيع** — *shayyaʿa* — "to escort a coffin; to see off".

> شيع أهل القرية الرجل إلى المقبرة.
>
> *shayyaʿa ahl al-qarya ar-rajul ilā al-maqbara.*
>
> The villagers walked with the man’s coffin to the cemetery.

Present: يشيع, *yushayyiʿ*.

The noun is تشييع; شيع ضيفه: he saw his guest off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شيع** (*shayyaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-y-ʿ* (Lane), "to follow". It is Form II, "to go along with one who is leaving", and so with the dead.
