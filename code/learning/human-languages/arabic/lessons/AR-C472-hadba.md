## You'll want to know: هضبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C472-QIMMA472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**هضبة** — *haḍba* — "a plateau; a hill".

> تقع القرية على هضبة واسعة.
>
> *taqaʿu al-qarya ʿalā haḍba wāsiʿa.*
>
> The village lies on a wide plateau.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هضبة** (*haḍba*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-ḍ-b*: Lane gives *haḍba* as a hill, or a mountain spreading over the surface of the ground, beside the root's sense of steady rain (Lane). Modern Arabic uses it for a plateau.
