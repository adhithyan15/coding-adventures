Arabic, chapter 428: On the railway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سكة حديد (sikkat ḥadīd) — a railway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to hire, then the Arabic for a backpack.

You'll want to know: سكة حديد.
سكة حديد — sikkat ḥadīd — "a railway".
وصلت سكة الحديد إلى قريتنا قبل قرن. waṣalat sikkat al-ḥadīd ilā qaryatinā qabla qarn. The railway reached our village a century ago.

The word, taken apart.
سكة حديد (sikkat ḥadīd) is built from two native Arabic words: sikka, a straight road or row, on the root s-k-k (Lane), and ḥadīd, "iron", on the root ḥ-d-d (Lane): a road of iron.

جلسنا في العربة الأخيرة من القطار. jalasnā fī al-ʿaraba al-akhīra min al-qiṭār. We sat in the last carriage of the train.

The word, taken apart.
عربة (ʿaraba) names a cart and a railway carriage; the origin is not settled, and some trace it to Turkish araba.

Lesson 3 of 5.
مقصورة (maqṣūra) — a compartment; a cabin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a railway, then the Arabic for a carriage.

You'll want to know: مقصورة.
مقصورة — maqṣūra — "a compartment; a cabin".
كانت المقصورة مزدحمة بالمسافرين. kānat al-maqṣūra muzdaḥima bi-l-musāfirīn. The compartment was crowded with travellers.

The word, taken apart.
مقصورة (maqṣūra) is built on the native Arabic root q-ṣ-r, "to confine, to keep within bounds" (Lane). It is a passive participle: a space closed off, a compartment.

Lesson 5 of 5.
أنزل (anzala) — to unload; to bring down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a compartment, then the Arabic for the metro.

You'll want to know: أنزل.
أنزل — anzala — "to unload; to bring down".
أنزل أبي الأمتعة من القطار. anzala abī al-amtiʿa min al-qiṭār. My father unloaded the luggage from the train.
Present: ينزل, yunzil.
Unvocalized, its present ينزل looks like that of nazala, "to go down" (yanzil).

The word, taken apart.
أنزل (anzala) is built on the native Arabic root n-z-l, "to come down, to alight" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to bring down".
