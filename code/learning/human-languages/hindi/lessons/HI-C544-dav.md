## You'll want to know: दाँव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C544-KISMAT544-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दाँव** — *dā̃v* — "a turn (in a game); a stake".

> अगर इस बार मेरा दाँव लग गया, तो मैं जीत जाऊँगा।
>
> *Agar is bār merā dā̃v lag gayā, to maiṁ jīt jāūṅgā.*
>
> If my luck holds this time, I will win.

Masculine.

दाँव लगना: one's turn or one's gamble comes off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाँव** (*dā̃v*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *dātu*, "a share" (CDIAL 6258). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "a turn": one's share of the play.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
