## You'll want to know: आयुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C490-RUPA490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आयुः** — *āyuḥ* — "length of life; life".

> व्यायामेन आयुः वर्धते।
>
> *vyāyāmena āyuḥ vardhate.*
>
> Exercise lengthens life.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आयुः** (*āyuḥ*) MW gives "life, vital power, health, duration of life, long life", the word at the head of **आयुर्वेदः**; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
