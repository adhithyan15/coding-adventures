## You'll want to know: くぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C482-NAIL482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**くぎ** — *kugi* — "a nail".

> **いたに くぎを うちます。** — *ita ni kugi o uchimasu* — "I drive a nail into the board."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くぎ** (*kugi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
