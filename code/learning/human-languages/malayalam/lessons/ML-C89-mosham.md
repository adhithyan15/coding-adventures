# മോശം (mōśaṁ) — bad

[PAUSE 2s] Say *a good one*. Then notice what you still cannot say about
anything at all.

## You'll want to know: മോശം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C89-EVAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**മോശം** — *mōśaṁ* — bad, poor.

**The book has given you five qualities and not one of them is negative.** A
thing could be good, big, small, new or old, and there was no way to say it was
**bad** — which is half of every opinion anybody has ever offered.

Put it with the *is* word you have had since the second chapter:

**മോശമാണ്** — *mōśamāṇŭ* — it is bad.

Notice the join. **മോശം** ends in the **ം**, and before a word starting with a
vowel that **ം** becomes **മ**. You have seen this book change a word's shape
before something else attaches; this is the same habit in a smaller place.

The partner sentence is built the same way, from the word you learned a moment
ago: **നല്ലതാണ്** — *nallathāṇŭ* — it is good.
