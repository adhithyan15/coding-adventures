Bengali, chapter 48: Woman, Neighbour, To hear, To sit, To walk.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মহিলা (môhilā) — a woman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a child, then the Bengali for a person.
