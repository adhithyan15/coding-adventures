# લટકાવવું (laṭkāvvũ) — to hang (something) from

## You'll want to know: લટકાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C514-DHILU514-05]; assesses=[] -->

**લટકાવવું** — *laṭkāvvũ* — "to hang (something) from".

> મીરાએ થેલી ખીંટી પર લટકાવી.
>
> *Mīrāe thelī khī̃ṭī par laṭkāvī.*
>
> Mira hung the bag on the peg.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે થેલી લટકાવી, *teṇe thelī laṭkāvī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લટકાવવું** (*laṭkāvvũ*) is built from **લટકવું**, "to hang, to dangle", with the causative *-āv-*: to make something hang. CDIAL files the family under *\*laṭṭa*, "hanging" (CDIAL 10918).
