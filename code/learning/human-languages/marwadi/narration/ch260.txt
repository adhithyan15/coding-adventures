Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 260: Married Into the Family.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बहू (bahū) — a daughter-in-law; a young wife of the house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a husband's sister, then the Marwadi for a husband (in his wife's words).

You'll want to know: बहू.
बहू — bahū — "a daughter-in-law; a young wife of the house".
म्हारी बहू घणी हुसियार है। mhārī bahū ghaṇī husiyār hai. My daughter-in-law is very clever.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बहू (bahū) is inherited from Sanskrit vadhū, "a bride, a young wife". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi bahū, "son's wife" (CDIAL 11250).
