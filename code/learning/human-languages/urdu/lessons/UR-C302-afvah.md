## You'll want to know: افواہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C302-AFWAH302-01]; assesses=[] -->

**افواہ** — *afvāh* — "a rumour".

> شہر میں ایک افواہ تھی۔
>
> *shahr meṅ ek afvāh thī.*
>
> There was a rumour going round the city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افواہ** (*afvāh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *afwāh*, "mouths": what passes from mouth to mouth. It is feminine.
