## You'll want to know: うりば
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C183-STAFF183-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うりば** — *uriba* — "sales floor; counter, department".

> **くつの うりばは どこですか。** — *kutsu no uriba wa doko desu ka* — "Where is the shoe department?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うりば** (*uriba*) is native Japanese, built from *uri*, the stem of *uru*, "to sell", and *ba*, "place" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
