Lesson 1 of 5.
दर (dar) — a rate.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: दर.
दर — dar — "a rate".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of दर (dar), "rate", is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it in this sense.

Lesson 3 of 5.
व्यापार (vyāpār) — trade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rate, then the Marathi for income.

You'll want to know: व्यापार.
व्यापार — vyāpār — "trade".

The word, taken apart.
व्यापार (vyāpār) is Sanskrit vyāpāra, "occupation, business", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 12205). CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi form as vāvar, "business, activity".

Lesson 4 of 5.
दुकानदार (dukāndār) — a shopkeeper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for income, then the Marathi for trade.

You'll want to know: दुकानदार.
दुकानदार — dukāndār — "a shopkeeper".

The word, taken apart.
दुकानदार (dukāndār) is dukān, "shop", from Arabic dukkān, "shop", through Persian (Lane, root d-k-n), with Persian -dār, "holder".
