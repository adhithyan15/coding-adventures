Italian, chapter 506: Fotografare.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
scattare — to take (a photo); to spring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for model making, then the Italian for to assemble.

You'll want to know: scattare.
scattare — "to take (a photo); to spring".
Ho scattato cento foto in vacanza. I took a hundred photos on holiday.
Scattare una foto — to take a photo; lo scatto is one shot.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of scattare is not settled; it may be built on an old verb cattare "to catch".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: inquadrare.
inquadrare — "to frame (a shot)".
Inquadra bene il paesaggio prima di fare la foto. Frame the landscape well before you take the photo.
Built on quadro "square, picture"; also to put in context.

The word, taken apart.
inquadrare is built on quadro "a square, a picture", from Latin quadrum "a square" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
il treppiede — tripod.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to take, then the Italian for to frame.

You'll want to know: il treppiede.
il treppiede — "tripod".
Per le foto di notte serve il treppiede. For night photos you need a tripod.
Also spelt treppiedi.

The word, taken apart.
treppiede is built from tre "three" and piede "foot", inherited from Latin pēs, pedis (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
il rullino — roll of film.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to frame, then the Italian for tripod.

You'll want to know: il rullino.
il rullino — "roll of film".
Ho trovato un vecchio rullino nel cassetto. I found an old roll of film in the drawer.
From the days before digital photos.

The word, taken apart.
rullino is a little form of rullo "roller", usually traced to Latin rotulus "a little wheel" (Lewis & Short), from rota "wheel".

Lesson 5 of 5.
sviluppare — to develop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for tripod, then the Italian for roll of film.

You'll want to know: sviluppare.
sviluppare — "to develop".
Ho fatto sviluppare le foto in un negozio. I had the photos developed in a shop.
Also for ideas and skills: sviluppare un progetto.

The word, taken apart.
sviluppare is built from s- "un-" and viluppo "a tangle" (EtymDB lists sviluppo): to untangle, to unroll. The origin of viluppo is not settled.
