## You'll want to know: प्रातः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C45-DAYTIME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रातः** (*prātaḥ*) — "morning".

**प्रातः** is early morning: the stretch before the sun stands high, and the hour the first greeting of the day belongs to.

You have said *pra-* at the front of a word twice already, in **प्रणामः** and in **प्रसन्नः**, where it carries the sense "forward, out in front". **प्रातः** is the front end of the day.

Greek *prōi*, "early", is the same small piece put to the same work, and Latin *prae* is its cousin; English carries it as the *pre-* in front of a thousand words.
