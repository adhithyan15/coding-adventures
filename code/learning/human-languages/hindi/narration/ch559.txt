Hindi, chapter 559: Looking Ahead.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
कल्पना (kalpnā) — imagination; an idea of what could be.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an ambition, then the Hindi for a passion.

You'll want to know: कल्पना.
कल्पना — kalpnā — "imagination; an idea of what could be".
बच्चों की कल्पना बहुत तेज़ होती है। Baccoṁ kī kalpnā bahut tez hotī hai. Children have very lively imaginations.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कल्पना (kalpnā) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit kalpanā, "making; creating in the mind, fiction" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
