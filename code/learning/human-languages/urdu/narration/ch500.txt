Urdu, chapter 500: At the Tailor's.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آستین (āstīn) — a sleeve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to spin (thread), then the Urdu for a knot.

You'll want to know: آستین.
آستین — āstīn — "a sleeve".
اس قمیض کی آستین لمبی ہے۔ is qamīz kī āstīn lambī hai. The sleeves of this shirt are long.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
آستین (āstīn) is usually traced to Persian āstīn, "a sleeve".

Lesson 2 of 5.
سلوانا (silvānā) — to have (clothes) sewn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a knot, then the Urdu for a sleeve.

You'll want to know: سلوانا.
سلوانا — silvānā — "to have (clothes) sewn".
میں نے درزی سے نیا کرتا سلوایا۔ maiṅ ne darzī se nayā kurtā silvāyā. I had a new kurta made by the tailor.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نئی شلوار سلوائی, us ne naī shalvār silvāī.

The word, taken apart.
سلوانا (silvānā) is the second causative of sīnā, "to sew", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit sīvyati, "sews" (CDIAL 13444): to have someone sew.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ادھیڑنا (udheṛnā) — to unpick (stitches).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ادھیڑنا.
ادھیڑنا — udheṛnā — "to unpick (stitches)".
درزی نے پرانی آستین ادھیڑی۔ darzī ne purānī āstīn udheṛī. The tailor unpicked the old sleeve.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پرانا کرتا ادھیڑا, us ne purānā kurtā udheṛā.

The word, taken apart.
ادھیڑنا (udheṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin udheṛnā, "to unravel", under Sanskrit uddharati, "takes out" (CDIAL 2009).

Lesson 4 of 5.
رفو کرنا (rafū karnā) — to darn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (clothes) sewn, then the Urdu for to unpick (stitches).

You'll want to know: رفو کرنا.
رفو کرنا — rafū karnā — "to darn".
درزی نے میری پتلون رفو کی۔ darzī ne merī patlūn rafū kī. The tailor darned my trousers.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پرانا کوٹ رفو کیا, us ne purānā koṭ rafū kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
رفو کرنا (rafū karnā) is built from rafū, "darning", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic rafw, and karnā, "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ادھڑنا (udhaṛnā) — to come unstitched.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to unpick (stitches), then the Urdu for to darn.

You'll want to know: ادھڑنا.
ادھڑنا — udhaṛnā — "to come unstitched".
میری آستین ادھڑ گئی۔ merī āstīn udhaṛ gaī. My sleeve has come unstitched.
Past (it …): وہ جیب ادھڑ گئی, voh jeb udhaṛ gaī.

The word, taken apart.
ادھڑنا (udhaṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin udhaṛnā, "to be taken out, to be unravelled", under Sanskrit uddharati, "takes out" (CDIAL 2009).
