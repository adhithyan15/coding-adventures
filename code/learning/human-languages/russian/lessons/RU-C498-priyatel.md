## You'll want to know: приятель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C498-FRIENDS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**приятель** (*priyátel*) — "a pal, a friend".

> Вчера я встретил старого приятеля.
>
> *Vcherá ya vstrétil stárogo priyátelya.*
>
> Yesterday I ran into an old friend.

Masculine; feminine приятельница. Less close than *друг*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*приятель* is inherited from Old East Slavic *prijatelь*, from Proto-Slavic *prijateľь*, derived from *prijati* 'to please, to favour' (EtymDB).
