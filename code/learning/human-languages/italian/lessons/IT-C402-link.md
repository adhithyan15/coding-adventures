## You'll want to know: il link
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C402-RETE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il link** — "the link".

> Ti mando il link: clicca e guarda.
>
> *I will send you the link: click and have a look.*

Masculine, invariable: *i link*. Also *il collegamento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*link* was borrowed from English *link* "a ring of a chain" (EtymDB), a word usually traced to Old Norse.
