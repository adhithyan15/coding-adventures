## You'll want to know: effondrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C303-SEISME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**effondrer** — "to collapse, to cave in (as s'effondrer)".

> Le vieux pont s'est effondré.
>
> *The old bridge collapsed.*

Mostly reflexive. Also of a person: *elle s'est effondrée en larmes* — she broke down in tears.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*effondrer* comes from Old French *esfondrer* (EtymDB), built on *fond* "bottom", from Latin *fundus* "bottom, ground" (Lewis & Short).
