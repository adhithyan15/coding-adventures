মলম (môlôm) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic marham, "ointment".

Lesson 4 of 5.
নাড়ি (nāṛi) — a pulse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a doctor's surgery, then the Bengali for a patient.

You'll want to know: নাড়ি.
নাড়ি — nāṛi — "a pulse".

The word, taken apart.
নাড়ি (nāṛi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nāḍī, "tubular organ, tube" (CDIAL 7047). CDIAL lists Bengali nāṛi, "vein, navel string".
