## You'll want to know: أكاديمي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C376-ILM376-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أكاديمي** — *akādīmī* — "academic".

> يعمل عمي في مجال أكاديمي.
>
> *yaʿmalu ʿammī fī majāl akādīmī.*
>
> My uncle works in an academic field.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أكاديمي** (*akādīmī*) is a modern loan with the Arabic ending *-ī*. It is usually traced to Greek *akadēmia*, the name of a school of learning, through European languages.
