## You'll want to know: بلد بودن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C444-NESHANI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بلد بودن** (*balad budan*) — "to know (the way, how to do something)".

> راننده تاکسی همه کوچه های شهر را بلد است.
>
> *Rânande-ye tâksi hame-ye kuche-hâ-ye shahr râ balad ast.*
>
> The taxi driver knows every lane in town.

Very common in speech: *râh râ balad nistam* — I don't know the way. *Balad*, a guide, is someone who knows the land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*balad budan* is built from *balad* 'knowing the way; a guide', a loan usually traced to Arabic *balad* 'land, country' (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), and the native verb *budan* 'to be'.
