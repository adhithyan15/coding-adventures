## You'll want to know: refellō, refellere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C362-REFELLO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**refellō, refellere** — "to prove false, to refute".

> Facile erat eius verba refellere.
>
> *It was easy to refute his words.*

*refellō, refellere, refellī*, with no supine. Built on *fallō*, to deceive: to show up a deception.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*refellō* is built from *re-* "back" and *fallō* "to deceive" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to turn a deception back on itself.
