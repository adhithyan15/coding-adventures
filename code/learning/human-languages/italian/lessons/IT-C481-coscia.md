## You'll want to know: la coscia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C481-GAMBA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la coscia** — "thigh".

> Dopo la corsa mi fanno male le cosce.
>
> *After the run my thighs hurt.*

The plural is *le cosce*. In the kitchen: *una coscia di pollo* — a chicken leg.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coscia* is inherited from Latin *coxa* "hip" (Lewis & Short); in Italian the word moved down to the thigh.
