## You'll want to know: o falecimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C314-FALECER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o falecimento** — "the passing, the death (formal)".

> O falecimento do avô juntou a família toda.
>
> *Grandfather’s passing brought the whole family together.*

A respectful word, common in notices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*falecimento* is built on *falecer* "to pass away", with the ending *-mento*; the verb goes back to Vulgar Latin *fallēscere* (EtymDB).
