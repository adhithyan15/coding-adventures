# म (ma) — the letter that ends राम

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-A-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-U-INDEPENDENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write अ and उ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **राम** (*rām*), "Ram".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-C43-MA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**म** — *ma*, the sound *m*.

It ends **राम** (*rām*), the name in the greeting **राम-राम सा**, the first words in
this book. The first chapter built **राम** from **र**, **ा** and **म**, and gave **र**
and **ा** lessons of their own. **म** gets its own lesson now.

## Writing: म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-C43-MA-01] -->

- **1.** start here: descend the left stem, circle clockwise through the loop, and sweep right
- **2.** lift, then descend the right stem
- **3.** lift, then draw the shirorekha left-to-right — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: JackPotte, ‘Devanagari m म.gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 29 March 2009.
