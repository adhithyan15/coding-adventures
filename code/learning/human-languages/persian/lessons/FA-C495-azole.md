## You'll want to know: عضله
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C495-ORGANS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عضله** (*'azole*) — "a muscle".

> بعد از کوهنوردی عضله های پایم درد میکند.
>
> *ba'd az kuhnavardi 'azolehâ-ye pâyam dard mikonad.*
>
> After hiking my leg muscles ache.

Plural *'azolehâ*, or the Arabic *'azolât* in formal Persian.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'azole* was borrowed from Arabic *ʿaḍala*, 'muscle' (EtymDB).
