## You'll want to know: قاحل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C473-WAHA473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**قاحل** — *qāḥil* — "barren".

> كانت الأرض قاحلة بلا ماء.
>
> *kānat al-arḍ qāḥila bi-lā māʾ.*
>
> The land was barren, without water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قاحل** (*qāḥil*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *q-ḥ-l*, "to dry up".
