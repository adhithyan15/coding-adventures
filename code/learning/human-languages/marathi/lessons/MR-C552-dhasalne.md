# ढासळणे (ḍhāsaḷṇe) — to crumble; to cave in

## You'll want to know: ढासळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C552-BIGHADNE552-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ढासळणे** — *ḍhāsaḷṇe* — "to crumble; to cave in".

> जोरदार पावसात जुन्या किल्ल्याची भिंत ढासळली.
>
> *jordār pāvsāt junyā killyācī bhint ḍhāsaḷlī.*
>
> In the heavy rain the old fort's wall crumbled.

Past (he …): तो किल्ला ढासळला, *to killā ḍhāsaḷlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढासळणे** (*ḍhāsaḷṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *dhāsaḷṇẽ*, "to fall", under Sanskrit *dhvaṁsati*, "falls to pieces" (CDIAL 6896).
