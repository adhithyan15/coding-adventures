## You'll want to know: しゅくはく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C397-MINSHUKU397-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅくはく** — *shukuhaku* — "accommodation; lodging".

> **しゅくはくの よやくを しました。** — *shukuhaku no yoyaku o shimashita* — "I booked somewhere to stay."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅくはく** (*shukuhaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shuku*, "lodging" (Middle Chinese *sjuwk*) and *haku*, "overnight stay" (*bak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
