## You'll want to know: પેટ્રોલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C207-TAXI207-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પેટ્રોલ** — *peṭrol* — "petrol".

> **ગાડીમાં પેટ્રોલ ખૂટ્યું.** — *gāḍīmā̃ peṭrol khūṭyũ* — "The car ran out of petrol."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પેટ્રોલ** (*peṭrol*) is a loan from English *petrol*.
