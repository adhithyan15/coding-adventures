# Chapter 4 — The farewells

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-GACCHAMI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. Three ways to part — the ancestors of the modern Indian
goodbyes.

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-GACCHAMI, SA-PATTERN-PUNAR-DARSHANAYA, SA-LEX-SHVAH, SA-LEX-PUNAH] -->

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: taking leave — *gacchāmi* ("I go")]
- [YOU SAY: "until we meet again" — *punar-darśanāya*]
- [YOU SAY: "see you tomorrow" — *śvaḥ punar-darśanam*]

## How to answer: the atoms

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-ETYMON-GAM-TO-GO, SA-ETYMON-PUNAR-AGAIN, SA-GRAMMAR-ONE-WORD-ONE-DAY] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: the verb "to go" and its Ch-1 cousin (*gam*; *svāgatam*)]
- [YOU SAY: "again" and its combining form (*punaḥ* / *punar-*)]
- [YOU SAY: "tomorrow" vs. "yesterday" (Sanskrit keeps *śvaḥ* / *hyaḥ* distinct)]

## Guided Practice — inside the farewells

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-ETYMON-DARSHANA-SEEING, SA-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-OF-PURPOSE, SA-ETYMON-SHVAH-TOMORROW] -->

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: what *punar-darśanāya* literally means, and the case that gives the "for" ("for seeing again"; the dative *-āya*)]
- [YOU SAY: what *darśana* means beyond "seeing" (a beholding — the *darśan* of a deity; the six schools of philosophy)]
- [YOU SAY: the PIE root of *śvaḥ*, and the Hindi word that blurs it with "yesterday" (*\*ḱes-*; *kal*)]

## Script — the chapter's two shapes

> प · र

- [YOU POINT: *ra*, then *pa*]
- [YOU SAY: the sound of each and a word you say with it (**र**, *ra*, in *namaskāraḥ*; **प**, *pa*, in *punaḥ*)]

## What you've built: the roots you now carry

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-HISTORY-LEAVE-TAKING-ROOTS]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *gam* "to go" ← PIE *\*gʷem-* → English **come**
- *punaḥ / punar-* "again" (→ *punarjanma*, "rebirth")
- *darśana* "a seeing / beholding" (the six *darśanas* of philosophy)
- *śvaḥ* "tomorrow," *hyaḥ* "yesterday" — kept separate, unlike Hindi *kal*

## Wrap-up Recall

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-HISTORY-LEAVE-TAKING-ROOTS] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You're taking leave and expect to see someone tomorrow. Give the
graceful goodbye, and specify tomorrow. (*Punar-darśanāya* — or *śvaḥ
punar-darśanam*.)
