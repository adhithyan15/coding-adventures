Russian, chapter 392: Limits and offenders.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ограничивать (ograníchivat) — to limit, to restrict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for of the law, then the Russian for a restriction.

You'll want to know: ограничивать.
ограничивать (ograníchivat) — "to limit, to restrict".
Родители ограничивают время у компьютера. Rodíteli ograníchivayut vrémya u kompyútera. The parents limit time at the computer.
Imperfective; perfective ограничить. Ограничиваться + instrumental — to be limited to.

The word, taken apart.
ограничивать (ograníchivat) is built from о- and граница "a border", which is built on грань "an edge": to limit a thing is to draw a border round it.

Lesson 2 of 6.
лишать (lishát) — to deprive of, to take away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a restriction, then the Russian for to limit.

You'll want to know: лишать.
лишать (lishát) — "to deprive of, to take away".
Боль лишает его сна. Bol lisháyet yegó sna. The pain keeps him from sleeping.
Imperfective; perfective лишить. Лишать кого чего: the thing taken goes in the genitive.

The word, taken apart.
лишать (lishát) is usually linked with лишний "extra, too many" and лишь "only", on an old Slavic root meaning excess: to take away what someone has.

Lesson 3 of 6.
обязывать (obyázyvat) — to oblige, to require.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to limit, then the Russian for to deprive of.

You'll want to know: обязывать.
обязывать (obyázyvat) — "to oblige, to require".
Договор обязывает нас закончить работу в мае. Dogovór obyázyvayet nas zakónchit rabótu v máye. The contract obliges us to finish the work in May.
Imperfective; perfective обязать (обяжу, обяжешь). Обязывать кого + infinitive. Я обязан — I am obliged.

The word, taken apart.
обязывать (obyázyvat) is built from о- and вязать "to tie, to bind", from Proto-Slavic vęzati: to oblige someone is to bind them.

нарушитель (narushítel) — "an offender, a violator".
Полиция остановила нарушителя. Polítsiya ostanovíla narushítelya. The police stopped the offender.
Masculine. Нарушитель порядка — someone disturbing the peace.

The word, taken apart.
нарушитель (narushítel) is built on нарушить "to violate", from на- and рушить "to pull down", with the agent suffix -тель.

Lesson 5 of 6.
невиновный (nevinóvnyy) — innocent, not guilty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to oblige, then the Russian for an offender.

You'll want to know: невиновный.
невиновный (nevinóvnyy) — "innocent, not guilty".
Судья решил, что он невиновен. Sudyá reshíl, chto on nevinóven. The judge decided that he was not guilty.
Short form невиновен. A guilty person is виновный.

The word, taken apart.
невиновный (nevinóvnyy) is built from не- "not" and виновный "guilty", on вина "guilt", which is usually traced to Proto-Slavic vina.
