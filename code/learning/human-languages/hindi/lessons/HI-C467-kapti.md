## You'll want to know: कपटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C467-KAPAT467-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कपटी** — *kapṭī* — "deceitful; two-faced".

> वह आदमी मीठा बोलता है, लेकिन बहुत कपटी है।
>
> *vah ādmī mīṭhā boltā hai, lekin bahut kapṭī hai.*
>
> That man talks sweetly, but he is very two-faced.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कपटी** (*kapṭī*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kapaṭin*, the adjective of *kapaṭa*, "fraud, deceit" (Monier-Williams).
