## You'll want to know: a vigilância
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C293-PATRULHA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a vigilância** — "surveillance, watch".

> O museu tem vigilância dia e noite.
>
> *The museum is watched day and night.*

*Câmaras de vigilância* — security cameras.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vigilância* was borrowed from Latin *vigilantia* "wakefulness, watchfulness" (EtymDB), from *vigilāre* "to keep watch".
