German, chapter 492: Verliebt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
verlieben — to fall in love (sich verlieben).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for spouse, then the German for offspring.

You'll want to know: verlieben.
verlieben — "to fall in love (sich verlieben)".
Im Sommer hat sie sich in ihren Tanzlehrer verliebt. In the summer she fell in love with her dance teacher.
Reflexive: sich verlieben in + accusative. Verliebt sein — to be in love.

The word, taken apart.
verlieben is built on ver- and lieben "to love" (EtymDB lists both), a Germanic verb related to lieb "dear", from Proto-Germanic leubaz.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: küssen.
küssen — "to kiss".
Zum Abschied küsste er sie. He kissed her goodbye.
Sich küssen — to kiss each other.

The word, taken apart.
küssen is inherited from Old High German kussen, from Proto-Germanic kussijaną "to kiss" (EtymDB); English kiss is a cousin.
