## You'll want to know: જુગાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C534-JUGAR534-01]; assesses=[] -->

**જુગાર** — *jugār* — "gambling".

> જો તું જુગાર રમીશ તો બધા પૈસા ગુમાવીશ.
>
> *jo tũ jugār ramīś to badhā paisā gumāvīś.*
>
> If you gamble, you will lose all your money.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જુગાર** (*jugār*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *dyūtakāra*, "a gambler", built on *dyūta*, "gambling" (CDIAL 6609); CDIAL itself lists no Gujarati form there.
