## You'll want to know: نخست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C303-EBTEDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نخست** (*nakhost*) — "first, firstly".

> نخست نامه را خواند و بعد گریه کرد.
>
> *Nakhost nâme râ khând va ba'd gerye kard.*
>
> First she read the letter, and then she cried.

Literary; *nakhost vazir* "prime minister". In speech *avval*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nakhost* is inherited from Middle Persian *naxust*, "first" (EtymDB).
