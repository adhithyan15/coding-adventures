## You'll want to know: o caloiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C376-FACULDADE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o caloiro** — "the first-year student, the fresher".

> Os caloiros chegam em setembro.
>
> *The first-year students arrive in September.*

European Portuguese; in Brazil *o calouro*. Feminine *a caloira*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *caloiro* is not settled; it is often linked with Greek *kalógēros* "good old man, monk", through an old word for a novice monk. EtymDB has no entry for it.
