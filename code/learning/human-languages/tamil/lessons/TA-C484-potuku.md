## You'll want to know: பொடுகு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C484-MUTI484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**பொடுகு** — *poṭuku* — "dandruff".

> குளிர் காலத்தில் எனக்குப் பொடுகு அதிகமாகும்.
>
> *kuḷir kālattil eṉakkup poṭuku atikamākum.*
>
> In winter my dandruff gets worse.

A mass noun: no plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **பொடுகு** (*poṭuku*) is not settled; we could not find it in DEDR.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
