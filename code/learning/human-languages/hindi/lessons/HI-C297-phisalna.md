## You'll want to know: फिसलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C297-PHISAL297-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फिसलना** — *phisalnā* — "to slip".

> बारिश के बाद मीरा सीढ़ी पर फिसल गई।
>
> *bāriś ke bād Mīrā sīṛhī par phisal gaī.*
>
> After the rain Mira slipped on the stairs.

Past (she …): वह फिसल गई, *vah phisal gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फिसलना** (*phisalnā*) is inherited from *\*phiss*, a form CDIAL reconstructs for slipping (CDIAL 9080). A slippery floor is one you **फिसलते हैं** on.
