## You'll want to know: زیور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C501-LOHA501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**زیور** — *zevar* — "a piece of jewellery".

> سنار نے دلہن کے لیے زیور بنائے۔
>
> *sunār ne dulhan ke liye zevar banāe.*
>
> The goldsmith made jewellery for the bride.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زیور** (*zevar*) is usually traced to Persian *zēvar*, "an ornament, jewellery".
