## You'll want to know: बोझ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C458-TANAV458-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बोझ** — *bojh* — "a burden; a weight on the mind".

> सच कहने के बाद अरुण के मन से बोझ उतर गया।
>
> *sac kahne ke bād Aruṇ ke man se bojh utar gayā.*
>
> Once he had told the truth, a weight lifted from Arun's mind.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बोझ** (*bojh*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *bojh*, "load", under Sanskrit *vahyá* (CDIAL 11465). A load on the back and a load on the mind are both *bojh*.
