Urdu, chapter 470: Out of Breath.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پھولنا (phūlnā) — to swell up; to puff up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for phlegm, then the Urdu for catarrh.

You'll want to know: پھولنا.
پھولنا — phūlnā — "to swell up; to puff up".
گرم پانی میں چاول پھول گئے۔ garm pānī meṅ chāval phūl gae. The rice swelled up in the hot water.
Past (it …): وہ روٹی پھول گئی, voh roṭī phūl gaī.

The word, taken apart.
پھولنا (phūlnā) is inherited from Sanskrit phúllati, "expands, opens like a flower" (CDIAL 9093); پھول, "a flower", is from the same family.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پسیجنا (pasījnā) — to break into a sweat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to swell up, then the Urdu for stamina.

You'll want to know: پسیجنا.
پسیجنا — pasījnā — "to break into a sweat".
گرمی سے اس کا ماتھا پسیج گیا۔ garmī se us kā māthā pasīj gayā. His forehead broke into a sweat in the heat.
Past (he …): وہ پسیج گیا, voh pasīj gayā.

The word, taken apart.
پسیجنا (pasījnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin pasījnā, "to sweat", under prasvidyati, "sweats", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8896); پسینہ, "sweat", is from the same family.

Lesson 4 of 5.
غش (ghash) — a faint; a swoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for stamina, then the Urdu for to break into a sweat.

You'll want to know: غش.
غش — ghash — "a faint; a swoon".
تیز دھوپ میں اسے غش آ گیا۔ tez dhūp meṅ use ghash ā gayā. He fainted in the strong sun.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
غش (ghash) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ghashy, "a covering over", and so a swoon.
