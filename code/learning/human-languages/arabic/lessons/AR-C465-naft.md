## You'll want to know: نفط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C465-TAQA465-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نفط** — *nafṭ* — "crude oil; petroleum".

> في هذه المنطقة آبار نفط.
>
> *fī hādhihi al-minṭaqa ābār nafṭ.*
>
> There are oil wells in this area.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نفط** (*nafṭ*) is a very old word for oil; the origin is not settled.
