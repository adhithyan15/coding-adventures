German, chapter 300: Neue Wege.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Scheidung — the divorce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the wedding ceremony, then the German for the registry office.

You'll want to know: die Scheidung.
die Scheidung — "the divorce".
Nach der Scheidung zog er in eine kleine Wohnung. After the divorce he moved into a small flat.
Plural: die Scheidungen. Geschieden — divorced — is already known.

The word, taken apart.
Scheidung is built on scheiden "to separate", from Old High German sceidan, Proto-Germanic skaiþaną (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
scheiden — to divorce, as sich scheiden lassen; to part.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the registry office, then the German for the divorce.

You'll want to know: scheiden.
scheiden — "to divorce, as sich scheiden lassen; to part".
Meine Eltern ließen sich scheiden, als ich zehn war. My parents got divorced when I was ten.
In this sense with lassen: sich scheiden lassen. Strong verb: schied, geschieden.

The word, taken apart.
scheiden is inherited from Old High German sceidan, which EtymDB traces to Proto-Germanic skaiþaną "to separate, to divide".

Lesson 4 of 5.
adoptieren — to adopt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to divorce, then the German for the separation.

You'll want to know: adoptieren.
adoptieren — "to adopt".
Sie adoptierten zwei Kinder. They adopted two children.
Regular, without ge-: hat adoptiert.

The word, taken apart.
adoptieren is a learned borrowing from Latin adoptāre "to adopt" (EtymDB), built on optāre "to choose" (Lewis & Short).
