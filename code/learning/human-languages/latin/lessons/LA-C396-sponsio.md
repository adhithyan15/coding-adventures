## You'll want to know: spōnsiō, spōnsiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C396-POLLICEOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**spōnsiō, spōnsiōnis** — "a solemn promise, a pledge; a wager".

> Spōnsiōnem inter sē fēcērunt.
>
> *They made a formal wager with each other.*

*spōnsiō, spōnsiōnis* f., built on *spondeō*, to pledge: the old spoken contract, *spondēsne?* — *spondeō*. Also a bet. English *sponsor* is from the same verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spōnsiō* is the noun built on *spondeō* "to pledge solemnly" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *sponsor* comes from the same verb.
