## You'll want to know: शासनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C353-SHASANA353-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शासनम्** — *śāsanam* — "government; rule".

> देशस्य शासनं कः करोति?
>
> *deśasya śāsanaṃ kaḥ karoti?*
>
> Who runs the country's government?

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शासनम्** (*śāsanam*) comes from the root √śās, "to rule, to order"; MW gives *śāsana* "government, dominion, rule" (MW).
