## You'll want to know: прогноз
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C464-PROGNOZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**прогноз** (*prognóz*) — "a forecast".

> По прогнозу завтра будет дождь.
>
> *Po prognózu závtra búdet dozhd.*
>
> According to the forecast it will rain tomorrow.

Masculine. *Прогноз погоды* — the weather forecast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прогноз* is usually traced through Latin *prognōsis* to Greek *prógnōsis* 'knowing beforehand' (EtymDB lists both; the Russian entry has no source), from *pro-* 'before' and *gnōsis* 'knowledge'.
