Hindi, chapter 520: Making Rotis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सुलगाना (sulgānā) — to kindle; to light (a fire).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to sweep (with a broom), then the Hindi for to scour (pots).

You'll want to know: सुलगाना.
सुलगाना — sulgānā — "to kindle; to light (a fire)".
दादी ने सुबह चूल्हा सुलगाया। Dādī ne subah cūlhā sulgāyā. Grandmother lit the stove in the morning.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने आग सुलगाई, usne āg sulgāī.

The word, taken apart.
सुलगाना (sulgānā) is the causative of sulagnā, "to catch fire", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit saṁlagna, "attached, joined" (CDIAL 12999).

Lesson 2 of 5.
गूँधना (gū̃dhnā) — to knead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to scour (pots), then the Hindi for to kindle.

You'll want to know: गूँधना.
गूँधना — gū̃dhnā — "to knead".
माँ आटा गूँध रही है। Māṁ āṭā gū̃dh rahī hai. Mother is kneading the dough.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने आटा गूँधा, usne āṭā gū̃dhā.

The word, taken apart.
गूँधना (gū̃dhnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit guphati, "strings together" (CDIAL 4205). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to plait, mix, knead".

चकला (caklā) और बेलन मेज़ पर रखे हैं। Caklā aur belan mez par rakhe haiṁ. The rolling board and the rolling pin are on the table.
Masculine.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बेलना (belnā) — to roll out (dough).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to knead, then the Hindi for a round board for rolling dough.

You'll want to know: बेलना.
बेलना — belnā — "to roll out (dough)".
मीरा ने चकले पर गोल रोटी बेली। Mīrā ne cakle par gol roṭī belī. Mira rolled out a round roti on the board.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पराठा बेला, usne parāṭhā belā.

The word, taken apart.
बेलना (belnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit vēllayati, "causes to shake, kneads" (CDIAL 12121). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to roll out".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: फूलना.
फूलना — phūlnā — "to puff up; to swell; to bloom".
गरम तवे पर रोटी फूल गई। Garam tave par roṭī phūl gaī. The roti puffed up on the hot griddle.
Past (it …): वह रोटी फूल गई, vah roṭī phūl gaī.

The word, taken apart.
फूलना (phūlnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit phullati, "expands, opens (as a flower)" (CDIAL 9093). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to swell, blossom".
