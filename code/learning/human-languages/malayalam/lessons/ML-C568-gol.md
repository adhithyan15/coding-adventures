## You'll want to know: ഗോൾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C568-KAYIKAM568-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗോൾ** — *gōḷ* — "a goal (in a match)".

> അവസാന മിനിറ്റിൽ അവൻ ഒരു ഗോൾ അടിച്ചു.
>
> *avasāna miniṟṟil avan oru gōḷ aṭiccu.*
>
> He scored a goal in the last minute.

ഗോൾ അടിക്കുക is to score a goal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗോൾ** (*gōḷ*) was borrowed from English *goal*.
