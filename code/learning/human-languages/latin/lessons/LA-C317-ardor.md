## You'll want to know: ārdor, ārdōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C317-INCENDIUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ārdor, ārdōris** — "burning heat; passion".

> Ārdor flammārum mūrōs nigrōs fēcit.
>
> *The heat of the flames blackened the walls.*

Masculine, 3rd declension, from *ārdeō*, 'to burn' (taught). Also a burning eagerness. English *ardour*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ārdor** is built on *ārdeō*, "to burn" (EtymDB): burning, in fire and in feeling. English *ardour* comes from it through French.
