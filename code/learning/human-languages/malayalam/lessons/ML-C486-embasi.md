## You'll want to know: എംബസി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C486-OPHIS486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**എംബസി** — *embasi* — "an embassy".

> വിസയ്ക്കായി അവൾ എംബസിയിൽ പോയി.
>
> *visaykkāyi avaḷ embasiyil pōyi.*
>
> She went to the embassy for a visa.

Each country has one embassy in the capital, and a consulate in some other cities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**എംബസി** (*embasi*) was borrowed from English *embassy*, the office of a foreign government in the capital.
