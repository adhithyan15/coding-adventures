Lesson 1 of 6.
हिमपातः (himapātaḥ) — a snowfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sets swaying, then the Sanskrit for a gentle breeze.

You'll want to know: हिमपातः.
हिमपातः — himapātaḥ — "a snowfall".
पर्वते रात्रौ हिमपातः भवति। parvate rātrau himapātaḥ bhavati. There is a snowfall on the mountain at night.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
हिमपातः (himapātaḥ) is built from hima, "snow", and pāta, "a fall", from the root √pat, "to fall", which also gives patati, "falls"; MW gives the compound as a fall of snow (MW).

Lesson 2 of 6.
कन्था (kanthā) — a patched garment; a quilt of rags.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a gentle breeze, then the Sanskrit for a snowfall.

You'll want to know: कन्था.
कन्था — kanthā — "a patched garment; a quilt of rags".
शीते भिक्षुः कन्थां धारयति। śīte bhikṣuḥ kanthāṃ dhārayati. In the cold the monk wears a patched garment.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कन्था (kanthā) is the word MW gives for a rag or a patched garment, especially one that ascetics wear; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it lives on as Hindi kathrī, "a quilt of patches" (MW; CDIAL 2721).

Lesson 3 of 6.
विलीयते (vilīyate) — melts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a snowfall, then the Sanskrit for a patched garment.

You'll want to know: विलीयते.
विलीयते — vilīyate — "melts".
ग्रीष्मे पर्वते हिमं विलीयते। grīṣme parvate himaṃ vilīyate. In summer the snow on the mountain melts.
Past (it …): हिमं विलीनवत्, himaṃ vilīnavat.

The word, taken apart.
विलीयते (vilīyate) is built from vi, "apart", and the root √lī, "to cling, to dissolve"; MW gives vi-lī as to be dissolved, to melt, with the participle vilīna, "melted" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 6.
संकुचति (saṅkucati) — huddles up; shrinks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a patched garment, then the Sanskrit for melts.

You'll want to know: संकुचति.
संकुचति — saṅkucati — "huddles up; shrinks".
शीते बालः कम्बले संकुचति। śīte bālaḥ kambale saṅkucati. In the cold the boy huddles up in a blanket.
Past (he …): सः कम्बले संकुचितवान्, saḥ kambale saṅkucitavān.

The word, taken apart.
संकुचति (saṅkucati) is built from sam, "together", and the root √kuc, "to contract, to bend"; MW gives saṃ-kuc as to contract or shrink, and its participle saṃkucita as crouching, cowering (MW).

प्रावारः — prāvāraḥ — "a cloak".
शीते वृद्धः प्रावारं धारयति। śīte vṛddhaḥ prāvāraṃ dhārayati. In the cold the old man wears a cloak.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रावारः (prāvāraḥ) is built from prā, a form of pra, and the root √vṛ, "to cover"; MW gives prāvāra as an outer garment, a mantle, a cloak (MW).
