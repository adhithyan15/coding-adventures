## You'll want to know: कष्टम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C533-ANTARAYA533-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कष्टम्** — *kaṣṭam* — "trouble; hardship".

> यदि कष्टम् आगच्छति, तर्हि धैर्यं धारयतु।
>
> *yadi kaṣṭam āgacchati, tarhi dhairyaṃ dhārayatu.*
>
> If trouble comes, hold on to your courage.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कष्टम्** (*kaṣṭam*) is the word MW gives as bad, ill, wrong, and as a noun evil, trouble; MW suggests, with a "perhaps", the past participle of the root √kaṣ, "to rub, to scratch", so its origin is uncertain (MW).
