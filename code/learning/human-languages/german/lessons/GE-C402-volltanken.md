## You'll want to know: volltanken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C402-TANKEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**volltanken** — "to fill the tank right up".

> Vor der langen Reise tankt er das Auto voll.
>
> *Before the long trip he fills the car right up.*

Separable: *ich tanke voll*, *ich habe vollgetankt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*volltanken* is built from *voll* 'full', an inherited Germanic word, and *tanken*, from *Tank*, which was borrowed from English *tank* (EtymDB).
