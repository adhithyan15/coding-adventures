## You'll want to know: حقق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C308-TAHQIQ308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حقق** — *ḥaqqaqa* — "to investigate; to achieve".

> حققت الشرطة مع الحارس.
>
> *ḥaqqaqat ash-shurṭa maʿa al-ḥāris.*
>
> The police questioned the guard.

Present: يحقق, *yuḥaqqiq*.

حقق مع someone: to question them. حقق هدفه: he reached his goal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حقق** (*ḥaqqaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-q-q* (Lane), the root of *ḥaqq*, truth. It is Form II, "to make sure of; to make come true".
