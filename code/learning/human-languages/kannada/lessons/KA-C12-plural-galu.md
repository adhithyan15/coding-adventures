# ಹೆಸರುಗಳು, ಬಣ್ಣಗಳು — more than one non-human thing

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-HUMAN-NONHUMAN-04, KA-LEX-C02-HESARU-01, KA-LEX-C11-BANNAGALU-01, KA-LEX-C11-BANNAGALU-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ಹೆಸರು**, “name”, then the four colours. These are non-human
things, so their ordinary plural can use one ending.

## Grammar Lens: add -ಗಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-PLURAL-GALU-07]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-HUMAN-NONHUMAN-04, KA-LEX-C02-HESARU-01, KA-LEX-C11-BANNAGALU-01, KA-LEX-C11-BANNAGALU-02] -->

- **ಹೆಸರು** (*hesaru*, name) → **ಹೆಸರುಗಳು** (*hesarugaḷu*, names)
- **ಬಣ್ಣ** (*banna*, colour) → **ಬಣ್ಣಗಳು** (*bannagaḷu*, colours)

The ending is **-ಗಳು** (*-gaḷu*). Use it here for ordinary non-human nouns.
Do not force it onto the family words: human plurals have their own patterns,
and *avaru* already gives you “they” for people.
