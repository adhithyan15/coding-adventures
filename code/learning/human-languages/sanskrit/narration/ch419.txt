Sanskrit, chapter 419: Reservoirs, Springs and Pools.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वापी (vāpī) — an oblong reservoir; a tank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a pearl, then the Sanskrit for coral.

You'll want to know: वापी.
वापी — vāpī — "an oblong reservoir; a tank".
ग्रामस्य मध्ये गभीरा वापी अस्ति। grāmasya madhye gabhīrā vāpī asti. In the middle of the village there is a deep reservoir.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वापी (vāpī) comes from the root √vap, "to strew, to heap up", which also gives vapati, "sows": MW gives vāpī as a pond made by damming up earth, an oblong reservoir, a tank; it lives on as Hindi bāuṛī, "a well with steps" (MW; CDIAL 11529).

पर्वते शीतलः उत्सः अस्ति। parvate śītalaḥ utsaḥ asti. There is a cool spring on the mountain.

उत्सः (utsaḥ) comes from the root √ud, "to wet", as MW gives it; MW gives utsa as a spring or fountain (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

पुष्करिणी — puṣkariṇī — "a lotus pool".
पुष्करिण्यां पद्मानि सन्ति। puṣkariṇyāṃ padmāni santi. There are lotuses in the lotus pool.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पुष्करिणी (puṣkariṇī) is built on puṣkara, "a blue lotus", with the ending -iṇī; MW gives puṣkariṇī as a lotus pool, any pool or pond (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कुल्या (kulyā) — a water channel; a canal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a spring of water, then the Sanskrit for a lotus pool.

You'll want to know: कुल्या.
कुल्या — kulyā — "a water channel; a canal".
कुल्या क्षेत्रेषु जलं वहति। kulyā kṣetreṣu jalaṃ vahati. The channel carries water to the fields.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कुल्या (kulyā) is the word MW gives for a small river, a canal or a channel for water; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पल्वलम् (palvalam) — a puddle; a small pond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a lotus pool, then the Sanskrit for a water channel.

You'll want to know: पल्वलम्.
पल्वलम् — palvalam — "a puddle; a small pond".
वर्षायाः अनन्तरं मार्गे पल्वलानि सन्ति। varṣāyāḥ anantaraṃ mārge palvalāni santi. After the rain there are puddles on the road.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पल्वलम् (palvalam) is the word MW gives for a pool or small pond; MW compares Greek pēlos, "mud", and Latin palus, "a marsh", with a query, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
