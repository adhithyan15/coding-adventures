## You'll want to know: जीवति

**जीवति** — *jīvati* — "is alive, lives".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jīvati* is the present of the root √jīv, "to live" (MW). The root is inherited from Indo-European: MW compares Latin *vīvō*, "I live". It lives on as Hindi *jīnā*, "to live" (CDIAL 5241).
