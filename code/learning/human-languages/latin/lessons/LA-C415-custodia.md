## You'll want to know: custōdia, custōdiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C415-CASTIGO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**custōdia, custōdiae** — "custody, guard; prison".

> Reus in custōdiā erat.
>
> *The accused was in custody.*

*custōdia, custōdiae* f., built on *custōs*, a guard. *In custōdiam dare*, to put under guard. English *custody*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*custōdia* is built on *custōs, custōdis* "a guard" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *custody* comes from it.
