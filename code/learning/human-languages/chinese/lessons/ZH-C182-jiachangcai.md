## You'll want to know: 家常菜
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C182-EATOUT182-04]; assesses=[] -->

**家常菜** — *jiāchángcài* — "home cooking; everyday dishes".

> **我们去对面吃家常菜** *wǒmen qù duìmiàn chī jiāchángcài* — Let's go across the road for some home cooking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**家常菜** (*jiāchángcài*) is built from 家 'home', 常 'everyday' and 菜 'dish', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, *kae*, *dzyang* and *tshojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the dishes a home makes every day. The everyday sense of the second is not given there.
