# iūs, iūris — law, right; justice

## You'll want to know: iūs, iūris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C308-IUDEX-05]; assesses=[] -->

**iūs, iūris** — "law, right; justice".

> Omnēs cīvēs eadem iūra habēbant.
>
> *All the citizens had the same rights.*

Neuter, 3rd declension. *Iūs* is law and right as a whole; a *lēx* is one law. A look-alike *iūs* means 'broth'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**iūs** is inherited from Proto-Indo-European *\*h₂yéwos*, "law, right" (EtymDB). English *jury*, *justice* and *jurist* come from its family.
