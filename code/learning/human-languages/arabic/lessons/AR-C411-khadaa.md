## You'll want to know: خضع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C411-IJAZA411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خضع** — *khaḍaʿa* — "to be subject to".

> يخضع الجميع للقانون.
>
> *yakhḍaʿu al-jamīʿ li-l-qānūn.*
>
> Everyone is subject to the law.

Present: يخضع, *yakhḍaʿ*.

It takes لـ: خضع لـ …. خضع لعملية, underwent an operation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خضع** (*khaḍaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ḍ-ʿ*, "to be lowly, submissive" (Lane). *Khaḍaʿa* is a Form I verb: to submit, to be subject to.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
