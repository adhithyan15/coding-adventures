## You'll want to know: магистр
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C385-OBRAZOVANIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**магистр** (*magístr*) — "a master’s graduate".

> Она хочет получить степень магистра.
>
> *Oná khóchet poluchít stépen magístra.*
>
> She wants to get a master’s degree.

Masculine. *Магистратура* — a master’s course.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**магистр** is usually traced to Latin *magister* "a master, a teacher, a chief" (Lewis & Short).
