# (reading) — six lines, in the order they happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SKILL-READ-WORDS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson gave you single words. These are whole turns.

[PAUSE 1s] You have said every one of them. None is new.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SKILL-READ-LINES]; assesses=[UR-LEX-SALAM, UR-LEX-NAME-QUESTION] -->

> سلام
> آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟
> آپ کیسے ہیں؟
> میں یہاں ہوں۔
> شکریہ
> نہیں

[PAUSE 3s] Read down once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and this time hear that these are not six separate lines.
They are the opening of a meeting, in the order it happens.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KAISE-KAISI, UR-LEX-YAHAN, UR-LEX-NAHIN, UR-SKILL-READ-LINES] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Two of these are questions and you can see it before you read a
word of them, because the question mark sits at the end where Urdu puts it.

[PAUSE 2s] Spoken, a voice told you which turn was whose. On the page nothing
does. You worked it out from where each line sits, which is most of what
reading a conversation is.

[PAUSE 2s] Six lines, and the order is the whole lesson.
