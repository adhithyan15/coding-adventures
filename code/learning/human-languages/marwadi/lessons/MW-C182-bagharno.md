## You'll want to know: बघारणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C182-JHATKO182-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बघारणो** — *baghārṇo* — "to temper (spices in hot oil)".

> मां दाल बघारै है।
>
> *mā̃ dāl baghārai hai.*
>
> Mother tempers the dal.

Past (I …): म्हैं दाल बघारी, *mhaĩ dāl baghārī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बघारणो** (*baghārṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vyāghārayati*, "sprinkles over" (with ghee); CDIAL lists Hindi *baghārnā*, for frying spices in hot oil (CDIAL 12191). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
