## You'll want to know: પૂરું પાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C375-JARURI375-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂરું પાડવું** — *pūrũ pāḍvũ* — "to provide".

> જો સરકાર પાણી પૂરું પાડશે તો ખેતરો લીલાં થશે.
>
> *jo sarkār pāṇī pūrũ pāḍśe to khetro līlā̃ thaśe.*
>
> If the government supplies water, the fields will turn green.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધું સાધન પૂરું પાડ્યું, *teṇe badhũ sādhan pūrũ pāḍyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂરું પાડવું** (*pūrũ pāḍvũ*) is built from *pūrũ*, "full", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *pūra*, "filling" (CDIAL 8330), and *pāḍvũ*, "to make fall", the causative of **પડવું** (*paḍvũ*), from Sanskrit *patati* (CDIAL 7722): to make a supply full.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
