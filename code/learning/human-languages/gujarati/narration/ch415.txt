Gujarati, chapter 415: Landmarks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચોકડી (cokḍī) — a crossroads (on a highway).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the wrong way round, then the Gujarati for a compass.

You'll want to know: ચોકડી.
ચોકડી — cokḍī — "a crossroads (on a highway)".
ચોકડી પરથી જમણી બાજુ વળો. cokḍī parthī jamṇī bāju vaḷo. Turn right at the crossroads.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચોકડી (cokḍī) is built on ચોક, a square, from Sanskrit catuṣka, "a set of four" (CDIAL 4629): the place where four roads meet.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ખાંચો (khā̃co) — a side lane; a nook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a compass, then the Gujarati for a crossroads.

You'll want to know: ખાંચો.
ખાંચો — khā̃co — "a side lane; a nook".
દુકાન પેલા ખાંચામાં છે. dukān pelā khā̃cāmā̃ che. The shop is in that side lane.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ખાંચો (khā̃co) may belong with a Gujarati word for a corner, which CDIAL lists under khōñcā, "a curve, a bend", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3930); CDIAL gives no form for ખાંચો itself, so the link is hedged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
નિશાની (niśānī) — a landmark; a sign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a crossroads, then the Gujarati for a side lane.

You'll want to know: નિશાની.
નિશાની — niśānī — "a landmark; a sign".
અમારા ઘરની નિશાની એક મોટો લીમડો છે. amārā gharnī niśānī ek moṭo līmḍo che. The landmark for our house is a big neem tree.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નિશાની (niśānī) is usually traced to Persian nišānī, "a mark, a token", built on nišān, "a sign": something you know a place by.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ટાવર (ṭāvar) — a clock tower; a tower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a side lane, then the Gujarati for a landmark.

You'll want to know: ટાવર.
ટાવર — ṭāvar — "a clock tower; a tower".
શહેરના ટાવરની ઘડિયાળ બંધ છે. śahernā ṭāvarnī ghaḍiyāḷ bandh che. The clock on the town's clock tower has stopped.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટાવર (ṭāvar) is borrowed from English tower; in many Gujarati towns the ટાવર is the clock tower in the main square.
