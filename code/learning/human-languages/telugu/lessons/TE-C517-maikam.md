# మైకం (maikaṁ) — dizziness; a daze

## You'll want to know: మైకం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C517-LAKSANAM517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మైకం** — *maikaṁ* — "dizziness; a daze".

> ఎండలో నడిచాక నాకు మైకంగా అనిపించింది.
>
> *eṇḍalō naḍicāka nāku maikaṅgā anipiñcindi.*
>
> After walking in the sun, I felt giddy.

మైకంగా ఉంది, I feel giddy; also the daze of sleep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మైకం** (*maikaṁ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4706 lists Telugu *maikamu*, "intoxication, unconsciousness, drowsiness", in the article of Tamil *mayakku*, "to bewilder, to confuse".
