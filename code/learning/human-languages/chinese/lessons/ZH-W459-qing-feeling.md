## Script — the heart piece, then a top over 月
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QING-FEELING-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 情

**Three pieces: the left first, then the top right, then the bottom right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, three strokes: the heart piece.** A short dot on the left, a short dot on the right falling to the right, then a long vertical down between them from top to foot.

**The top right, four strokes.** A bar from left to right, a second bar under it, a vertical down through both, then a long bar under them, wider than the two above.

**The bottom right, 月, four strokes.** The stroke down the left that curves to the left at the foot, the stroke that runs right, turns down and hooks, then the two short bars inside.

3 + 4 + 4 = eleven.
