## You'll want to know: मन्त्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C321-ABHISHEKA321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मन्त्रः** — *mantraḥ* — "a sacred verse; a mantra".

> पुरोहितः मन्त्रं पठति।
>
> *purohitaḥ mantraṃ paṭhati.*
>
> The priest recites a sacred verse.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मन्त्रः** (*mantraḥ*) comes from the root √man, "to think": MW explains *mantra* as an instrument of thought, and gives it as speech, a sacred text, a prayer or hymn (MW).
