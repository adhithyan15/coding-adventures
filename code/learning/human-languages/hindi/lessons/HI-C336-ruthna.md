## You'll want to know: रूठना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C336-SHAAP336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रूठना** — *rūṭhnā* — "to sulk; to take offence".

> रानी राजा से रूठ गई।
>
> *rānī rājā se rūṭh gaī.*
>
> The queen took offence at the king.

Past (she …): वह रूठ गई, *vah rūṭh gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रूठना** (*rūṭhnā*) goes back to Sanskrit *ruṣṭa*, "angry, offended" (CDIAL 10791). It is a quiet anger that waits to be soothed with **मनाना**.
