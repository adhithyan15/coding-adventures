## You'll want to know: معرفة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C367-IDRAK367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**معرفة** — *maʿrifa* — "knowledge".

> الكتب طريق إلى المعرفة.
>
> *al-kutub ṭarīq ilā al-maʿrifa.*
>
> Books are a road to knowledge.

Plural معارف, *maʿārif*, also acquaintances.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معرفة** (*maʿrifa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-f*, "to know" (Lane). *Maʿrifa* is a verbal noun of *ʿarafa*, "to know": knowledge, knowing.
