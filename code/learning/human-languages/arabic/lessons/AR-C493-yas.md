## You'll want to know: يأس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C493-NADAM493-05]; assesses=[] -->

**يأس** — *yaʾs* — "despair".

> لم يشعر المريض باليأس أبدا.
>
> *lam yashʿur al-marīḍ bi-l-yaʾs abadan.*
>
> The patient never felt despair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**يأس** (*yaʾs*) is built on the native Arabic root *y-ʾ-s*, "to despair, to give up hope" (Lane). Yaʾs is the noun of giving up hope.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
