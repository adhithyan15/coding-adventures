## You'll want to know: o disjuntor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C432-LUZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o disjuntor** — "the circuit breaker".

> O disjuntor desligou quando liguei o forno.
>
> *The circuit breaker tripped when I turned on the oven.*

People also say *o quadro* for the box of breakers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disjuntor* came from French *disjoncteur*, built on Latin *disiungere* 'to unyoke, to separate', on *iungere* 'to join' (Lewis & Short).
