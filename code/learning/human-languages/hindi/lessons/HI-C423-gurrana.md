## You'll want to know: गुर्राना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C423-GARD423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गुर्राना** — *gurrānā* — "to growl".

> रात को गली का कुत्ता गुर्रा रहा था।
>
> *rāt ko galī kā kuttā gurrā rahā thā.*
>
> At night the dog in the lane was growling.

Past (it …): वह शेर गुर्राया, *vah śer gurrāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुर्राना** (*gurrānā*) goes back to *\*guragura*, "growl", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as imitative (CDIAL 4207), which lists Hindi *gurrānā*: the word rumbles like the sound.
