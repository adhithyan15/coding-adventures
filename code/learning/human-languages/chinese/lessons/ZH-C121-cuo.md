## You'll want to know: cuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C121-LEARN121-05]; assesses=[] -->

*cuò* — "wrong; mistaken".

> *zhège hànzì cuò le* — This character is wrong.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cuò* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tshak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'mixed' and 'crossing slantwise'; how it came to mean wrong is not settled.
