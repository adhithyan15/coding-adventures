## You'll want to know: たすけあう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C268-KYUUJO268-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たすけあう** — *tasukeau* — "to help each other".

> **こまった とき、みんなで たすけあいます。** — *komatta toki, minna de tasukeaimasu* — "In hard times we all help one another."

Polite **たすけあいます**, *tasukeaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **みんなが たすけあいました。**, *minna ga tasukeaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たすけあう** (*tasukeau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tasuke*, "helping" (from *tasukeru*), and *au*, "to do together" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
