## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘਾਹ** (*ghāh*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *ghāsa*, "food, pasture grass" (CDIAL 4471).
