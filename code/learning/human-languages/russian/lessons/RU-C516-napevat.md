## You'll want to know: напевать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C516-MELODY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**напевать** (*napevát*) — "to hum".

> На кухне мама напевает любимую песню.
>
> *Na kúkhne máma napeváyet lyubímuyu pésnyu.*
>
> Mum is humming her favourite song in the kitchen.

Imperfective; present напеваю. Perfective напеть.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*напевать* is built on *на-* and *петь* 'to sing'; EtymDB has no entry.
