Malayalam, chapter 169: Clever, Lazy, Calm, Busy, Expensive.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ബുദ്ധിയുള്ള (buddhiyuḷḷa) — clever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for ripe, then the Malayalam for brave.
[pause 1 second]
Say "a cap." (Toppi.)

You'll want to know: ബുദ്ധിയുള്ള.
ബുദ്ധിയുള്ള — buddhiyuḷḷa — "clever".

The word, taken apart.
ബുദ്ധിയുള്ള (buddhiyuḷḷa) joins Sanskrit buddhi, "intelligence" (CDIAL 9277), to native -uḷḷa, "that has" (DEDR 697).

Lesson 5 of 5.
വിലകൂടിയ (vilakūṭiya) — expensive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for calm, then the Malayalam for busy.
[pause 1 second]
Say "marks," what an exam gives back. (Mārkkŭ.)

You'll want to know: വിലകൂടിയ.
വിലകൂടിയ — vilakūṭiya — "expensive".

The word, taken apart.
വിലകൂടിയ (vilakūṭiya) is native Dravidian, "whose price has grown": vila, "price" (DEDR 5421), with kūṭiya, "increased", from the verb "to join, be added" (DEDR 1882, with Tamil kūṭu).
