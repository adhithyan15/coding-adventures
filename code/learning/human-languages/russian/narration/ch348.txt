Russian, chapter 348: Grounds and motives.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
повод (póvod) — an occasion, a reason (for); a pretext.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for so, then the Russian for in other words.

You'll want to know: повод.
повод (póvod) — "an occasion, a reason (for); a pretext".
У нас есть повод для радости: сестра вышла замуж. U nas yest póvod dlya rádosti: sestrá výshla zámuzh. We have a reason to be happy: my sister has got married.
Masculine. По поводу + genitive — about, concerning. Its first sense is a horse’s rein.

The word, taken apart.
повод (póvod) is built from по- and вод-, the root of водить "to lead", from Proto-Slavic voditi "to lead, to conduct" (EtymDB). It was first the rein that leads a horse, then whatever leads to an event.

Lesson 2 of 5.
основание (osnovániye) — grounds, a basis; a founding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for in other words, then the Russian for an occasion.

You'll want to know: основание.
основание (osnovániye) — "grounds, a basis; a founding".
У тебя нет оснований так думать. U tebyá net osnovániy tak dúmat. You have no grounds for thinking so.
Neuter. На основании + genitive — on the basis of. It also means the founding of a city: основание (osnovániye) Москвы.

The word, taken apart.
основание (osnovániye) is built on основать "to found", from основа "a base, a warp", inherited from Proto-Slavic osnova (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
фактор (fáktor) — a factor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an occasion, then the Russian for grounds.

You'll want to know: фактор.
фактор (fáktor) — "a factor".
Погода — важный фактор для туризма. Pogóda — vázhnyy fáktor dlya turízma. The weather is an important factor for tourism.
Masculine. Человеческий фактор (fáktor) — the human factor.

The word, taken apart.
фактор (fáktor) is usually traced, through German, to Latin factor "a maker, a doer" (Lewis & Short), built on facere "to do, to make". A factor is something that does part of the work.

Lesson 4 of 5.
мотив (motív) — a motive; a tune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for grounds, then the Russian for a factor.

You'll want to know: мотив.
мотив (motív) — "a motive; a tune".
Какой у него был мотив? Kakóy u negó byl motív? What was his motive?
Masculine. It also means a melody: знакомый мотив (motív).

The word, taken apart.
мотив (motív) was borrowed from German Motiv, from French motif, from Medieval Latin motivum, from Latin mōtīvus "serving to move" (EtymDB), built on movēre "to move".

Lesson 5 of 5.
происхождение (proiskhozhdéniye) — origin, descent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a factor, then the Russian for a motive.

You'll want to know: происхождение.
происхождение (proiskhozhdéniye) — "origin, descent".
Он русский по происхождению. On rússkiy po proiskhozhdéniyu. He is Russian by origin.
A neuter noun. По происхождению — by origin, by birth. From происходить, to come from; the жд is the Church Slavonic softening of д.

The word, taken apart.
происхождение (proiskhozhdéniye) is built on происходить "to come from", made of про-, из- "out of" and ходить "to go", inherited from Proto-Slavic xoditi. The жд is the Church Slavonic way of softening д.
