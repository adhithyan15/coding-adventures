## You'll want to know: verēcundia, verēcundiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C513-MISE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**verēcundia, verēcundiae** — "shyness, modesty".

> Puella prae verēcundiā tacet.
>
> *The girl stays silent out of shyness.*

Feminine, first declension, from *verēcundus* — shy, modest. A gentle sense of shame.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**verēcundia** is built on *verēcundus* "modest, shy", from *vereor* "to respect, fear" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
