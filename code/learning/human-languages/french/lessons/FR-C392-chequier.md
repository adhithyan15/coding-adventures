## You'll want to know: le chéquier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C392-BANQUE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le chéquier** — "the chequebook".

> La banque m'a envoyé un nouveau chéquier.
>
> *The bank sent me a new chequebook.*

From *chèque*. Fewer and fewer people use one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéquier* is built from *chèque* and the suffix *-ier*. *chèque* is usually traced to English *cheque*, *check*, linked to *check* "a control".
