## You'll want to know: गवाह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C288-CHORI288-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गवाह** — *gavāh* — "a witness".

> गवाह ने पुलिस को सब कुछ बताया।
>
> *gavāh ne pulis ko sab kuch batāyā.*
>
> The witness told the police everything.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गवाह** (*gavāh*) is usually traced to Persian *gavāh*, "a witness", a word that reached Hindi through the courts. It is used of a man or a woman: **वह गवाह है**.
