# (reading) — the first paragraph

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SKILL-READ-LINES] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Six words, then six lines. Now a whole paragraph.

[PAUSE 1s] Thirty-seven words, and every letter inside them is one you can
already write.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING]; assesses=[UR-LEX-NILA, UR-LEX-KALA, UR-LEX-YIH] -->

> یہ میری ماں ہے۔
> یہ ہمارا کمرہ ہے۔
> کمرے میں پانی ہے۔
> آسمان نیلا ہے۔
> وہ کالا ہے۔
> ہم لاہور میں ہیں۔
> لاہور ایک شہر ہے۔
> یہاں کام ہے۔
> کیا یہ پرانا ہے؟
> نہیں۔
> سلام اور شکریہ۔

[PAUSE 3s] Read it once without stopping. Do not translate as you go — let the
sentences arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Now read it again, and notice how little work it took.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-MEIN-POST, UR-LEX-AUR, UR-LEX-PURANA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Almost every line here is the same sentence: something is something.
**ہے** ends nearly all of them.

[PAUSE 2s] That is what makes a first paragraph possible. You know the shape of
each line before you reach it, so your attention goes to the two or three words
in the middle that change.

[PAUSE 2s] Then one line breaks the pattern, and you can see it break before
you read it: the ninth ends in a question mark, and the tenth answers it in one
word.

[PAUSE 2s] This is the first whole paragraph the book has asked you to hold at
once, and it works because every piece was paid for.
