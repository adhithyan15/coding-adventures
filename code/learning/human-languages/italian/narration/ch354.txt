Italian, chapter 354: Gli ostacoli.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
l'ostacolo — the obstacle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the excuse, then the Italian for to justify.

You'll want to know: l'ostacolo.
l'ostacolo — "the obstacle".
La lingua è il primo ostacolo per chi arriva in un paese nuovo. Language is the first obstacle for someone arriving in a new country.
Superare un ostacolo — to overcome an obstacle. In sport, i 110 ostacoli are the hurdles.

The word, taken apart.
ostacolo is a learned borrowing from Latin obstāculum "a hindrance" (Lewis & Short), from obstāre "to stand in the way", built from ob "against" and stāre "to stand".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: impedire.
impedire — "to prevent, to stop (from)".
La nebbia ci ha impedito di partire. The fog prevented us from leaving.
Impedire a qualcuno di fare qualcosa. Conjugated with -isc-: impedisce.

The word, taken apart.
impedire is a learned borrowing from Latin impedīre "to entangle, to hinder" (Lewis & Short), built from in and pēs, pedis "foot": to tie someone's feet.
