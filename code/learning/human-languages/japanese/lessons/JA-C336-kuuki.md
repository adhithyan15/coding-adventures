## You'll want to know: くうき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C336-KANKYOU336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**くうき** — *kūki* — "air; the atmosphere".

> **まちは くうきが きたないので、いなかに すみたいです。** — *machi wa kūki ga kitanai node, inaka ni sumitai desu* — "The air in town is dirty, so I want to live in the country."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くうき** (*kūki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kū*, "empty" (Middle Chinese *khuwng*) and *ki*, "air" (*khj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
