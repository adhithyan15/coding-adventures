## You'll want to know: غصہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C444-ANGER444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غصہ** — *ghussā* — "anger".

> دیر سے آنے پر استاد کو غصہ آیا۔
>
> *der se āne par ustād ko ghussā āyā.*
>
> The teacher got angry at someone coming late.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غصہ** (*ghussā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghuṣṣa*, "a choking, a lump in the throat"; the sense "anger" is the one Urdu gives it.
