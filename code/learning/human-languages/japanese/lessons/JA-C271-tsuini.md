## You'll want to know: ついに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C271-TSUKAMARU271-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ついに** — *tsuini* — "finally; in the end".

> **ついに はんにんが つかまりました。** — *tsuini hannin ga tsukamarimashita* — "At long last the culprit was caught."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ついに** (*tsuini*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
