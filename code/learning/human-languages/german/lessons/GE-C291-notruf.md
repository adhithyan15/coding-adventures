## You'll want to know: der Notruf
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C291-NOTRUF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Notruf** — "the emergency call; the emergency number".

> Sie wählte sofort den Notruf.
>
> *She dialled the emergency number at once.*

Plural: *die Notrufe*. The number 112 reaches the fire brigade and the ambulance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Notruf* joins *Not* "need", a cousin of English *need*, and *Ruf* "call", from *rufen*, Proto-Germanic *hrōpaną* (EtymDB).
