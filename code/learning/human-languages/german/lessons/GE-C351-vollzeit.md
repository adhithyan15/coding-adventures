## You'll want to know: die Vollzeit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C351-ARBEITSZEIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Vollzeit** — "full-time (work)".

> Mein Vater arbeitet Vollzeit, meine Mutter halbtags.
>
> *My father works full-time, my mother half-days.*

*Vollzeit arbeiten* — to work full-time. *Die Vollzeitstelle* — the full-time job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vollzeit* joins *voll* "full", Old High German *foll*, Proto-Germanic *fullaz* (EtymDB), to *Zeit* "time", Proto-Germanic *tīdiz* (EtymDB).
