## You'll want to know: किशोर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C499-KISHOR499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**किशोर** — *kiśor* — "an adolescent; a teenager".

> किशोर उम्र में बच्चे बहुत जल्दी बदलते हैं।
>
> *kiśor umr meṁ bacce bahut jaldī badalte haiṁ.*
>
> In their teens, children change very quickly.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किशोर** (*kiśor*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *kiśora*, first "a colt" and then "a youth, a lad" (Monier-Williams). The feminine is *kiśorī*.
