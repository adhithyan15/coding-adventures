Gujarati, chapter 318: The King and the Queen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રાજા (rājā) — a king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the past, then the Gujarati for to be born.

You'll want to know: રાજા.
રાજા — rājā — "a king".
જૂના વખતમાં અહીં એક દયાળુ રાજા રહેતો હતો. jūnā vakhatmā̃ ahī̃ ek dayāḷu rājā raheto hato. Long ago a kind king lived here.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રાજા (rājā) is inherited from Sanskrit rājan, "a chieftain, a king" (CDIAL 10679). It ends in -ā but does not change like છોકરો: રાજાનું ઘર, the king's house.

રાણી (rāṇī) is inherited from Sanskrit rājñī, "a queen", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 10692). With એ she is the doer: રાણીએ અનાજ આપ્યું.

Lesson 3 of 5.
મહેલ (mahel) — a palace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a king, then the Gujarati for a queen.

You'll want to know: મહેલ.
મહેલ — mahel — "a palace".
રાજા અને રાણી મોટા મહેલમાં રહેતાં હતાં. rājā ane rāṇī moṭā mahelmā̃ rahetā̃ hatā̃. The king and queen lived in a big palace.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મહેલ (mahel) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maḥall, "a place, a residence". A king and a queen together take the plural રહેતાં હતાં, the form for a man and a woman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a queen, then the Gujarati for a palace.

દરબાર — darbār — "a royal court".
રાજાના દરબારમાં એક વેપારી આવ્યો. rājānā darbārmā̃ ek vepārī āvyo. A merchant came to the king's court.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દરબાર (darbār) is usually traced to Persian darbār, "a court, a royal audience": the hall where a king hears his people. દરબારમાં, at court.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ગાદી (gādī) — a throne.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a palace, then the Gujarati for a royal court.

You'll want to know: ગાદી.
ગાદી — gādī — "a throne".
રાજાના મૃત્યુ પછી તેનો દીકરો ગાદી પર બેઠો. rājānā mṛtyu pachī teno dīkro gādī par beṭho. After the king's death his son took the throne.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગાદી (gādī) comes from garda, a form CDIAL reconstructs, which lists Gujarati gādī, "a pad, a raised seat" (CDIAL 4053): the cushioned seat of a ruler. ગાદી પર બેઠો, he came to the throne.
