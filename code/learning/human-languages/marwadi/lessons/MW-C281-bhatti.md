## You'll want to know: भट्टी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C281-CHAK281-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भट्टी** — *bhaṭṭī* — "a kiln".

> भट्टी घणी गरम है।
>
> *bhaṭṭī ghaṇī garam hai.*
>
> The kiln is very hot.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भट्टी** (*bhaṭṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhrāṣṭra*, "a frying pan"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Marathi *bhaṭṭī*, "forge", Sindhi *baṭhī*, "distilling furnace", and Hindi *bhaṭṭhā*, "kiln" (CDIAL 9656).
