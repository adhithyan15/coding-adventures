## You'll want to know: تنیس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C514-BALL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تنیس** (*tenis*) — "tennis".

> او هفته ای دو بار تنیس بازی میکند.
>
> *u hafte-i do bâr tenis bâzi mikonad.*
>
> He plays tennis twice a week.

*tenis-e ru-ye miz* — table tennis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tenis* is a modern loan, usually traced to English *tennis*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
