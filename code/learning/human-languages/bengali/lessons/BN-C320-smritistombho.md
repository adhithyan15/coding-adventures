## You'll want to know: স্মৃতিস্তম্ভ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C320-SMRITI320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**স্মৃতিস্তম্ভ** — *smritistômbhô* — "a memorial; a monument".

> নদীর পাশে শহিদদের একটা স্মৃতিস্তম্ভ আছে।
>
> *nôdīr pāshe shôhidder ekṭā smritistômbhô āchhe.*
>
> Beside the river there is a memorial to the martyrs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্মৃতিস্তম্ভ** (*smritistômbhô*) joins **স্মৃতি**, memory, to Sanskrit *stambha*, 'a post, a pillar' (Monier-Williams): a pillar of memory.
