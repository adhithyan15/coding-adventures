# على الأرجح (ʿalā al-arjaḥ) — most probably

## You'll want to know: على الأرجح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C403-IRTIYAB403-04]; assesses=[] -->

**على الأرجح** — *ʿalā al-arjaḥ* — "most probably".

> سيصل على الأرجح بعد الظهر.
>
> *sa-yaṣilu ʿalā al-arjaḥ baʿda aẓ-ẓuhr.*
>
> He will most probably arrive in the afternoon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**على الأرجح** (*ʿalā al-arjaḥ*) joins *ʿalā*, "on", with *al-arjaḥ*, "the weightier", built on the native Arabic root *r-j-ḥ*, "to outweigh" (Lane): on the heavier side of the scale, most probably.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
