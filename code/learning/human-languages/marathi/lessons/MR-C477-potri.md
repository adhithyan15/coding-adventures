## You'll want to know: पोटरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C477-HAT477-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पोटरी** — *poṭrī* — "the calf (of the leg)".

> डास माझ्या पोटरीला चावला.
>
> *ḍās mājhyā poṭrīlā cāvlā.*
>
> A mosquito bit me on the calf.

Feminine.

Feminine: पोटरीला, on the calf.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोटरी** (*poṭrī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
