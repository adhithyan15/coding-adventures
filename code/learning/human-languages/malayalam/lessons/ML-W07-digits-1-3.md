# ൧ · ൨ · ൩ — three numeral shapes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-CONCEPT-C07-NUMBERS-1-5-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *onnu, randu, moonnu*. You already own the meanings and sounds;
this lesson adds only their three numeral shapes.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-DIGITS-1-3-07]; assesses=[] -->

- **൧** means 1: *onnu*.
- **൨** means 2: *randu*.
- **൩** means 3: *moonnu*.

Point in order, then out of order: **൩ ൧ ൨**.

## Writing — observe and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-DIGITS-1-3-07] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Keep the models visible. Finger-trace **൧**, **൨**, and **൩** once each. Say the
number word only after your finger stops. Do not copy from memory yet.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-CONCEPT-C07-NUMBERS-1-5-01, ML-SCRIPT-DIGITS-1-3-07] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point to **൨** and say it. (***Randu.***) Which shape means three?
(**൩**.) How many new shapes arrived? (**Three.**)
