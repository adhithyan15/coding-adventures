# Marathi A1 mock rubric

Score either A1 form against
[`task-shapes/a1.json`](../../task-shapes/a1.json). Reading, listening, writing,
and speaking are separate 100-point papers. A learner must earn at least
60/100 on **every** paper; there is no compensatory total.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-forms -->
Signs and forms carry 35 points across seven keyed items. Award five points for
each correct selection and zero for an unkeyed selection.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-forms -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->
Short messages carry 35 points across seven keyed items. Award five points for
each correct selection and zero for an unkeyed selection.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-personal-descriptions -->
Personal descriptions carry 30 points across six keyed items. Award five
points for each correct selection and zero for an unkeyed selection.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-personal-descriptions -->

Reading aloud does not earn reading credit. Marking on the learner paper is
allowed; romanization, a dictionary, or a translator is not.

## Listening — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-announcements -->
Announcements carry 35 points across six keyed items. Divide the part equally
and retain one decimal place.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-announcements -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->
Short exchanges carry 35 points across six keyed items. Divide the part equally
and retain one decimal place.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-personal-account -->
The personal account carries 30 points across five keyed items. Award six
points for each correct selection.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-personal-account -->

Use the separate examiner script at 90–110 words per minute and exactly two
plays. The full part stimulus windows are 60–90, 90–120, and 120–180 seconds.
Do not show a transcript, romanization, dictionary, or translator. The script
is an administration boundary, not proof that a qualified recording exists.

## Writing — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
The practical form carries 40 points: requested content 16, task fulfilment 8,
Marathi vocabulary and grammar 8, and orthographic control 8. Score 6–8 fields
only when the learner independently supplies the requested type of content.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->
The 30–40-word named-reader message carries 60 points: relevant content 20,
named reader and purpose 10, organization 10, Marathi vocabulary and grammar
12, and orthographic control 8. A copied, translated, romanized, or
non-Marathi-script response earns no independent-production credit.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->

Accept a documented regional Marathi form and natural first-person gender
choice when used consistently. Do not penalize a variety merely for differing
from an example key; score task fulfilment, comprehensibility, and control.

## Speaking — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
The five-question personal interview carries 30 points. Score the combined
60–120-second response window for relevant answers, interaction, Marathi
control, pronunciation, and fluency.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
The exactly one-minute prepared description carries 30 points. Score task
fulfilment and relevant content 35%, organization 25%, Marathi vocabulary and
grammar 25%, and pronunciation and fluency 15%.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
The 60–120-second transactional role-play carries 40 points. Score task
fulfilment and relevant content 35%, interaction and repair 25%, Marathi
vocabulary and grammar 25%, and pronunciation and fluency 15%.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->

Accept a documented regional Marathi form and internally consistent gender
agreement. Preparation notes may contain keywords, not sentences. A written
answer script, romanization, dictionary, or translator is forbidden.

## Readiness boundary

These papers, keys, scripts, manifests, and this rubric make two project-defined
mocks structurally executable. Their existence does not establish exam readiness,
recording provenance, qualified recordings, independent human validation,
standard setting, learner readiness, or learner attainment. Those remain
separate evidence obligations on #13459 and in `assessment.json`.
