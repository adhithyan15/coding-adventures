## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-HAVE-NOT-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C87-MEIYOU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-BU-MEI-01, ZH-LEX-C87-DENG-01, ZH-LEX-C87-YAOSHI-01, ZH-LEX-C87-BINGXIANG-01, ZH-LEX-C57-HOME57-02, ZH-LEX-C81-CITY81-05] -->

> **A:** *zhè shì nǐ de fángjiān* — This is your room.
>
> **B:** 谢谢
>
> *xièxie* — Thanks.
>
> **B:** *fángjiān lǐ yǒu dēng ma* — Is there a light in the room?
>
> **A:** *yǒu, zài mén pángbiān* — Yes, by the door.
>
> **B:** *nǐ yǒu méiyǒu yàoshi* — Have you got a key?
>
> **A:** 没有
>
> *méiyǒu* — No, I haven't.
>
> **A:** *bīngxiāng lǐ yǒu niúnǎi* — There's milk in the fridge.
>
> **B:** 我不喝
>
> *wǒ bù hē* — I don't drink it.

Two kinds of no in one room: 没有, there isn't any, and 我不喝, I don't drink it. The first reports, the second refuses.

## What you've built — four things, and what is missing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-HAVE-NOT-01, ZH-SCRIPT-MEI-01, ZH-LEX-C87-MEIYOU-01, ZH-LEX-C87-CHUANG-01] -->

A bed, a light, a key and a fridge, and two ways to say a thing is not there:

- *nǐ yǒu méiyǒu yàoshi* — **没有** *méiyǒu*
- *fángjiān lǐ yǒu chuáng ma* — **没有** *méiyǒu*

**Listen.** *Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu shǒujī?*

**Speak.** Answer it with 有, then with 没有, then say what your room has.
