Mandarin Chinese, chapter 84: Doing Things Together.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
一起 (yìqǐ) — together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to run out of time, then the Mandarin for to be late.

You'll want to know: 一起.
一起 — yìqǐ — "together".
三年前我们一起上学 sān nián qián wǒmen yìqǐ shàngxué — Three years ago we were at school together.
yī changes its tone here, falling to yì before the dipping qǐ: yìqǐ.

The word, taken apart.
一起 (yìqǐ) is built from 一 'one' and 起 'to rise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit and khiX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). Rising as one is a likely picture, but how the pair came to mean together is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for everyday answers.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: yìqǐ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: yìqǐ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sān nián qián wǒmen yìqǐ shàngxué — Three years ago we were at school together.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for to run out of time, then the Mandarin for to be late, then say yìqǐ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does 一起 (yìqǐ) mean? ("Together".) Say it once more.

liáotiān — "to chat".

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
liáotiān is built from liáo and 天 'sky', both inherited from Middle Chinese, lew and then (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as a helping word; how the pair came to mean chatting is not settled.
