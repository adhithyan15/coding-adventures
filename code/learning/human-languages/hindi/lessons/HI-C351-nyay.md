## You'll want to know: न्याय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C351-ADHIKAR351-05]; assesses=[] -->

**न्याय** — *nyāy* — "justice".

> ग़रीबों को भी न्याय मिलना चाहिए।
>
> *ġarīboṁ ko bhī nyāy milnā cāhiye.*
>
> The poor should get justice too.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**न्याय** (*nyāy*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nyāya*, "a rule; a judgement" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
