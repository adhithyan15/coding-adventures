## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাক** (*kāk*) is Sanskrit *kāka*, "crow", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 2993). The word inherited from it is *kāuā*, which CDIAL lists.
