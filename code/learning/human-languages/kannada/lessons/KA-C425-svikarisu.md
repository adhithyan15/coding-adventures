## You'll want to know: ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C425-SVIKARISU425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸು** — *svīkarisu* — "to accept; to receive".

> ಅವಳು ಹೊಸ ಕೆಲಸದ ಆಹ್ವಾನವನ್ನು ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸಿದಳು.
>
> *avaḷu hosa kelasada āhvānavannu svīkarisidaḷu.*
>
> She accepted the offer of a new job.

Past (I …): ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ, *svīkariside*.

Formal. ಸ್ವೀಕಾರ, acceptance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸು** (*svīkarisu*) is built on Sanskrit *svīkāra*, "making one's own" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
