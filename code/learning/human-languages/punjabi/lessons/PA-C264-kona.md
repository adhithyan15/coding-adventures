## You'll want to know: ਕੋਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C264-CROSS264-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਨਾ** — *konā* — "a corner".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਘਰ ਗਲੀ ਦੇ ਕੋਨੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā ghar galī de kone utte hai.*
>
> My house is on the corner of the lane.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਨਾ** (*konā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *koṇa*, "corner, angle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *koṇā*, "corner", under it (CDIAL 3504).
