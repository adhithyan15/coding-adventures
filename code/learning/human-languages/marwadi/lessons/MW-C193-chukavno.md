## You'll want to know: चुकावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C193-MONEY193-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चुकावणो** — *chukāvṇo* — "to pay; to settle (a bill)".

> आप पैसा अबार चुकावो।
>
> *āp paisā abār chukāvo.*
>
> Please pay now.

Past (I …): म्हैं पैसा चुकाया, *mhaĩ paisā chukāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुकावणो** (*chukāvṇo*) is the causative of a verb inherited from a reconstructed *\*cukk*, "fall short of, stop": CDIAL lists Marwari *cūkṇo*, "to be completed", and Hindi *cukānā*, "to settle" (CDIAL 4848). To pay a bill is to make it complete.
