## You'll want to know: پیشرفت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C374-BEHBUD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پیشرفت** (*pishraft*) — "progress".

> پیشرفت تو در زبان فارسی عالی است.
>
> *pishraft-e to dar zabân-e fârsi 'âli ast.*
>
> Your progress in Persian is excellent.

*Pish* "forward" + *raft* "went". Plural *pishrafthâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیشرفت** is a native Persian compound built from *pish* "forward, before" and *raft*, the past stem of *raftan* "to go". Progress is going forward.
