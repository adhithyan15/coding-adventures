Kannada, chapter 460: Finding a Room.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಸತಿ (vasati) — lodging; accommodation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tent, then the Kannada for an encampment.

You'll want to know: ವಸತಿ.
ವಸತಿ — vasati — "lodging; accommodation".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ವಸತಿ ಸಿಗುವುದು ಕಷ್ಟ. habbada samayadalli vasati siguvudu kaṣṭa. At festival time lodging is hard to find.
ವಸತಿ (vasati) ಶಾಲೆ, a boarding school; ವಸತಿಗೃಹ, a lodge.

The word, taken apart.
ವಸತಿ (vasati) is borrowed from Sanskrit vasati, "a dwelling, a lodging", from vas, "to stay, to dwell" (CDIAL 11435).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕೊಠಡಿ (koṭhaḍi) — a chamber; a room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for lodging, then the Kannada for a lodge.

You'll want to know: ಕೊಠಡಿ.
ಕೊಠಡಿ — koṭhaḍi — "a chamber; a room".
ನಮ್ಮ ಕೊಠಡಿ ಮೂರನೇ ಮಹಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು. namma koṭhaḍi mūranē mahaḍiyalli ittu. Our room was on the third floor.
A formal word; ಕೋಣೆ is the everyday room.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊಠಡಿ (koṭhaḍi) is usually traced to Sanskrit kōṣṭha, "a storeroom, an inner apartment" (CDIAL 3546), through Prakrit.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ (svāgatakāra) — a receptionist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lodge, then the Kannada for a chamber.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ.
ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ — svāgatakāra — "a receptionist".
ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ ನಮಗೆ ಕೊಠಡಿಯ ಕೀಲಿ ಕೊಟ್ಟನು. svāgatakāra namage koṭhaḍiya kīli koṭṭanu. The receptionist gave us the key to the room.
ಸ್ವಾಗತ, welcome, plus -ಕಾರ, one who does it.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ (svāgatakāra) is built from Sanskrit svāgata, "welcome" (CDIAL 13920), and -kāra, "one who does": the one who welcomes.
