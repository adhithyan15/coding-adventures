## You'll want to know: काउंटर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C433-QATAR433-03]; assesses=[] -->

**काउंटर** — *kāunṭar* — "a counter".

> दूसरे काउंटर पर जाइए।
>
> *dūsre kāunṭar par jāiye.*
>
> Please go to the second counter.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काउंटर** (*kāunṭar*) is borrowed from English *counter*, at a bank, a shop or a station.
