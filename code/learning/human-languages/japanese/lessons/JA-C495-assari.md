## You'll want to know: あっさり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C495-SMELL495-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あっさり** — *assari* — "light (of food); plain and simple".

> **この りょうりは あっさりしています。** — *kono ryōri wa assari shiteimasu* — "This dish is light and simple."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あっさり** (*assari*) is a native mimetic word (gitaigo), imitating the impression it describes rather than borrowed from Chinese; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
