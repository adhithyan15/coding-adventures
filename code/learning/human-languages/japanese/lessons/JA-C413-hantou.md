## You'll want to know: はんとう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C413-HEIYA413-03]; assesses=[] -->

**はんとう** — *hantō* — "a peninsula".

> **この はんとうには みさきが みっつ あります。** — *kono hantō ni wa misaki ga mittsu arimasu* — "This peninsula has three capes."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はんとう** (*hantō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *han*, "half" (Middle Chinese *panH*) and *tō*, "island" (*tawX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
