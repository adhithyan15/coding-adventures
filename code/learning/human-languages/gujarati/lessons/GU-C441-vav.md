## You'll want to know: વાવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C441-LAKE441-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વાવ** — *vāv* — "a stepwell".

> ગામની જૂની વાવમાં હજી પાણી છે.
>
> *gāmnī jūnī vāvmā̃ hajī pāṇī che.*
>
> There is still water in the village's old stepwell.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાવ** (*vāv*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *vāpī*, "an oblong pond"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a large well with steps" (CDIAL 11529): steps that go down to the water.
