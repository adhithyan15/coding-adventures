## You'll want to know: 但愿
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C535-WILLING535-05]; assesses=[] -->

**但愿** — *dànyuàn* — "let us hope that; if only".

> **但愿他明天能来** *dànyuàn tā míngtiān néng lái* — Let's hope he can come tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**但愿** (*dànyuàn*) is built from 但 'only' and 愿 'to wish', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *danX* and *ngjwonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a grammar word and 'merely': I only wish.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
