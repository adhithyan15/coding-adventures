Persian, chapter 82: Now, Never, This, That, These.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اکنون (aknun) — now.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for Wednesday, then the Persian for Thursday.

You'll want to know: اکنون.
اکنون — aknun — "now".

The word, taken apart.
aknun is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian aknūn, "now". EtymDB links it to the Indo-European nu, "now", the word behind English now.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: هرگز.
هرگز — hargez — "never".

The word, taken apart.
hargez is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian hagriz, "ever; never" (EtymDB). Old Persian had hakaram-čiy, "once" (EtymDB).
