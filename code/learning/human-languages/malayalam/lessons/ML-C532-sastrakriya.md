## You'll want to know: ശസ്ത്രക്രിയ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C532-ROGAM532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ശസ്ത്രക്രിയ** — *śastrakriya* — "surgery; an operation".

> അച്ഛന് അടുത്ത ആഴ്ച ഒരു ശസ്ത്രക്രിയ ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *acchanŭ aṭutta āḻca oru śastrakriya uṇṭŭ.*
>
> Father has an operation next week.

The formal word; in speech many say ഓപ്പറേഷൻ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശസ്ത്രക്രിയ** (*śastrakriya*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *śastra*, "an instrument for cutting" (CDIAL 12367), and *kriyā*, "work, an act" (CDIAL 3587): work with the knife.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
