## Added — नाम and the ā sign print filmstrips

The ā sign now has a cited pen path, and a cited place in a word: its stem
comes after its consonant's body and before the headline, as the cited आ
draws the same bar (most native writers' आ do too). Three lessons gain a
stroke-order strip:

- **HI-S06-vowel-sign-aa:** ◌ा by itself: the stem, then the piece of
  headline the printed sign carries.
- **HI-A1F01-name-label** and **HI-W12-schwa-drop:** नाम: the body of न, the
  stem of ◌ा, the body of म, then one headline across the word.
- **Rewritten:** HI-S06's Writing block no longer says the book gives no
  stroke order; it points at the strip. HI-A1F01-name-label told the learner
  to draw each head-line after its letter shape; it now follows the strip
  (one head-line, last, across the word).
