## Script — the right of 作, then 心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 怎

**Two pieces, and you write both already.**

**The right of 作, five strokes**, here on top: a short left-falling stroke; a horizontal from it to the right; a long vertical down from the horizontal; then two short horizontals to its right, the upper one first.

**心, four strokes**, at the foot: the left dot, the curve along the foot that hooks up, the middle dot, the right dot.

5 + 4 = nine.
