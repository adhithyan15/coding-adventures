## You'll want to know: عمده فروش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C458-DASTFORUSH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عمده فروش** (*'omdeforush*) — "a wholesaler".

> عمده فروش برنج را کیسه ای میفروشد.
>
> *'Omdeforush berenj râ kise-i miforushad.*
>
> The wholesaler sells rice by the sack.

*'omde* 'in bulk' + *forush* 'seller'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'omdeforush* is built from *'omde* 'main part, in bulk', which EtymDB takes from Arabic *ʿumda*, and native *forush*, the present stem of *forukhtan* 'to sell'.
