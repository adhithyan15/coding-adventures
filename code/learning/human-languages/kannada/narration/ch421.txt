Kannada, chapter 421: Supply and Demand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬೇಡಿಕೆ (bēḍike) — a demand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for uncertain, then the Kannada for a calamity.

You'll want to know: ಬೇಡಿಕೆ.
ಬೇಡಿಕೆ — bēḍike — "a demand".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೂವಿಗೆ ಬೇಡಿಕೆ ಹೆಚ್ಚು. habbada samayadalli hūvige bēḍike heccu. At festival time flowers are in high demand.
Also a demand made by people: ಕಾರ್ಮಿಕರ ಬೇಡಿಕೆ (bēḍike).

The word, taken apart.
ಬೇಡಿಕೆ (bēḍike) is native Dravidian, a noun from bēḍu, "to ask for", listed with bēḍike in DEDR 5528.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a calamity, then the Kannada for a demand.

You'll want to know: ಪೂರೈಸು.
ಪೂರೈಸು — pūraisu — "to supply".
ಆ ಹಳ್ಳಿ ನಗರಕ್ಕೆ ಹಾಲು ಪೂರೈಸುತ್ತದೆ. ā haḷḷi nagarakke hālu pūraisuttade. That village supplies milk to the city.
Past (I …): ಪೂರೈಸಿದೆ, pūraiside.
Also to complete: ಓದು ಪೂರೈಸು (pūraisu), to finish one's studies.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೂರೈಸು (pūraisu) is built on Sanskrit pūra, "filling" (CDIAL 8330), with a Kannada verb ending: to make full.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a demand, then the Kannada for to supply.

You'll want to know: ಕೊರತೆ.
ಕೊರತೆ — korate — "a shortage; a lack".
ಬೇಸಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನೀರಿನ ಕೊರತೆ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ. bēsigeyalli nīrina korate iruttade. In summer there is a shortage of water.
What is lacking takes -ದ / -ಇನ: ಹಣದ ಕೊರತೆ (korate).

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊರತೆ (korate) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1851, the "short, small" family, lists Kannada koṟate, "a deficiency".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ರಫ್ತು (raphtu) — export.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to supply, then the Kannada for a shortage.

You'll want to know: ರಫ್ತು.
ರಫ್ತು — raphtu — "export".
ನಮ್ಮ ಕಾಫಿ ಬೇರೆ ದೇಶಗಳಿಗೆ ರಫ್ತು ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ. namma kāphi bēre dēśagaḷige raphtu āguttade. Our coffee is exported to other countries.
ರಫ್ತು (raphtu) ಮಾಡು, to export. ಆಮದು, import.

The word, taken apart.
ರಫ್ತು (raphtu) is usually traced to Persian raft, "gone", through Hindustani raftanī, "export".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ (haṇadubbara) — inflation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a shortage, then the Kannada for export.

You'll want to know: ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ.
ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ — haṇadubbara — "inflation".
ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರದಿಂದ ಎಲ್ಲದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಏರಿತು. haṇadubbaradinda elladara bele ēritu. With inflation the price of everything went up.
Word by word: money swelling.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ (haṇadubbara) is a modern Kannada word built from haṇa, "money", and ubbara, "a swelling", from native ubbu, "to swell" (DEDR 666).
