# ಬಂದೂಕು (bandūku) — a gun; a rifle

## You'll want to know: ಬಂದೂಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C344-AYUDHA344-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂದೂಕು** — *bandūku* — "a gun; a rifle".

> ಕಾವಲುಗಾರನ ಕೈಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹಳೆಯ ಬಂದೂಕು ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *kāvalugārana kaiyalli haḷeya bandūku ittu.*
>
> The watchman had an old gun in his hand.

Plural: ಬಂದೂಕುಗಳು, *bandūkugaḷu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂದೂಕು** (*bandūku*) is a loan from Arabic *bunduq*, which came through Persian and Hindustani *bandūq*, "a musket, a gun".
