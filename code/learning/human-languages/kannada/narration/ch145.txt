Kannada, chapter 145: Bitter, Sour, Sweet, Lazy, Clever.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಹಿ (kahi) — bitter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for grey, then the Kannada for spicy.

You'll want to know: ಕಹಿ.
ಕಹಿ — kahi — "bitter".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಹಿ (kahi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kaya and Telugu kasu (DEDR 1249). DEDR lists the older Kannada kayi, kaypu, "bitterness". DEDR 1249 compares Sanskrit kaṣāya.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಿಹಿ (sihi) is native Dravidian, of the root for "sweet", and DEDR 3268 lists sihi, "sweet", itself; the entry also has Tamil tīm. DEDR 3268 compares, with a query, Prakrit tiṇisa.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ಸೋಮಾರಿ.
ಸೋಮಾರಿ — sōmāri — "lazy".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ಸೋಮಾರಿ (sōmāri) is uncertain. DEDR has no Kannada form for it, though DEDR 2882 lists Tamil cōmāṟi, "sluggard", which looks like the same word.
