## You'll want to know: تمييز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C381-MUJTAMA381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تمييز** — *tamyīz* — "discrimination".

> تحدثت الجريدة عن التمييز في العمل.
>
> *taḥaddathat al-jarīda ʿan at-tamyīz fī al-ʿamal.*
>
> The newspaper wrote about discrimination at work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تمييز** (*tamyīz*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-y-z*, "to separate, to set apart" (Lane). *Tamyīz* is the Form II verbal noun, setting apart: telling apart, and treating people apart, discrimination.
