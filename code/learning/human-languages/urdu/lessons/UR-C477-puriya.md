## You'll want to know: پڑیا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C477-MEDICINE477-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پڑیا** — *puṛiyā* — "a paper packet (of powder)".

> حکیم نے دوا کی تین پڑیاں دیں۔
>
> *hakīm ne davā kī tīn puṛiyāṅ dīṅ.*
>
> The physician gave three packets of powder.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پڑیا** (*puṛiyā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *puṛiyā*, "anything wrapped in paper or leaves", under Sanskrit *puṭa*, "a fold, a small receptacle" (CDIAL 8253).
