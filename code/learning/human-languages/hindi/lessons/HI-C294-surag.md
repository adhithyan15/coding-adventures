## You'll want to know: सुराग़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C294-TAFTISH294-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सुराग़** — *surāġ* — "a clue; a trace".

> पुलिस को दरवाज़े पर एक सुराग़ मिला।
>
> *pulis ko darvāze par ek surāġ milā.*
>
> The police found a clue on the door.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *surāġ*, "a trace, a track". It is what you follow: one small **सुराग़** can lead to the thief.
