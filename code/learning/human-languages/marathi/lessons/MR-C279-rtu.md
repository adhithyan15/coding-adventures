# ऋतू (ṛtū) — a season

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C127-TIME127-02, MR-LEX-C126-TIME126-05, MR-LEX-C127-TIME127-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the summer, the monsoon and the winter. (*unhāḷā*, *pāvsāḷā*, *hivāḷā*.) Each of them is one of these.

## You'll want to know: ऋतू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C279-RTU-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-AVADNE, MR-LEX-C246-VASTU246-03] -->

**ऋतू** — *ṛtū* — "a season".

> **मला पावसाळा हा ऋतू खूप आवडतो.**
>
> *malā pāvsāḷā hā ṛtū khūp āvaḍto.* — "I like the monsoon season very much."

Its first letter is the vowel you know as the sign **ृ** in **नृत्य**, standing on its own. Marathi says it *ru*: **ऋतू** is said *rutū*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऋतू** (*ṛtū*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *ṛtu*, "a fixed time, a season" (Monier-Williams). CDIAL has no entry for it. Everyday Marathi names three seasons; the Sanskrit calendar counts six, and **वसंत**, "springtime", is one of them.
