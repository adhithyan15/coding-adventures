# ಪೇಟೆ (pēṭe) — a bazaar; a market street

## You'll want to know: ಪೇಟೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C467-MARUKATTE467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೇಟೆ** — *pēṭe* — "a bazaar; a market street".

> ಹಳೆಯ ಪೇಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಅಗ್ಗವಾಗಿ ಸಿಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *haḷeya pēṭeyalli baṭṭe aggavāgi siguttade.*
>
> Cloth is cheap in the old bazaar.

Also the market quarter of a town: ಪೇಟೆಗೆ ಹೋಗು, to go into town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೇಟೆ** (*pēṭe*) is a Kannada word for a market street whose origin is not settled.
