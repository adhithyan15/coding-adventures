## You'll want to know: rasten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C454-WANDERUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rasten** — "to rest, to stop for a rest (on a walk)".

> Auf halbem Weg rasten wir an einer Hütte.
>
> *Halfway we stop for a rest at a hut.*

*Die Rast* — the rest. *Rasten* sounds a little old-fashioned; people also say *eine Pause machen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rasten* is built on *Rast* 'rest, a break' (EtymDB lists the verb 'to rest'), an old Germanic word; English *rest* is usually counted as a cousin.
