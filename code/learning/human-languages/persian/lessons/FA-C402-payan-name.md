## You'll want to know: پایان نامه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C402-TAHQIQ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پایان نامه** (*pâyân nâme*) — "a thesis, a dissertation".

> خواهرم پایان نامه اش را تمام کرد.
>
> *khâharam pâyân nâme ash râ tamâm kard.*
>
> My sister finished her thesis.

*Pâyân* "end" + *nâme* "letter, book": the book at the end of a degree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پایان نامه** is a native Persian compound built from *pâyân* "end" + *nâme* "letter, book", a word EtymDB lists without a parent and usually traced to Middle Persian *nāmag*. It is the book that ends a degree.
