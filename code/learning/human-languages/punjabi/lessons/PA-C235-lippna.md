## You'll want to know: ਲਿੱਪਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C235-KAM235-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਿੱਪਣਾ** — *lippṇā* — "to plaster (with mud)".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਗਾਰੇ ਨਾਲ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਲਿੱਪਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī gāre nāl chullhā lippdī hai.*
>
> Grandmother plasters the stove with mud.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਲਿੱਪਿਆ, *maiṁ chullhā lippiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਿੱਪਣਾ** (*lippṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lipyatē*, "is smeared"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *lippṇā* among the verbs for plastering a wall (CDIAL 11061).
