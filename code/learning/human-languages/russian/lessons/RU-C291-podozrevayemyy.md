## You'll want to know: подозреваемый
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C291-SLEDOVATEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**подозреваемый** (*podozreváyemyy*) — "a suspect".

> Подозреваемый молчал.
>
> *Podozreváyemyy molchál.*
>
> The suspect said nothing.

A participle that declines like an adjective; a woman is *подозреваемая*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подозреваемый* is the passive participle of *подозревать* "to suspect", built on *под-* and the old root *зр-* "to look", as in *зрение* "sight", Old East Slavic *zĭrěti* "to see" (EtymDB): the one watched from below.
