# Four ways to mean more than you say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-ES-QUE, ES-LEX-SI-CLARO] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Four devices, and the useful skill is telling them apart fast enough
to keep up with a speaker.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-ES-QUE, ES-SEMANTIC-IMPLICATURE, ES-LEX-SI-CLARO, ES-SEMANTIC-IRONY, ES-LEX-TOMAR-EL-PELO, ES-SEMANTIC-IDIOM-OPAQUE, ES-LEX-MIRA-DISCOURSE, ES-SEMANTIC-TONE-SHIFT] -->

[PAUSE 1s] For each of the four, say which strategy the listener has to use.

- [YOU SAY: *es que mañana trabajo* — and then say what the listener must do
  (**infer** — supply the refusal that was not spoken)]
- [YOU SAY: *sí, claro*, flat and slow — and what the listener must do
  (**invert** — take the opposite of the words)]
- [YOU SAY: *me estás tomando el pelo* — and what the listener must do
  (**know it already** — nothing in the words will help)]
- [YOU SAY: *mira, no es así* — and what the listener must do
  (**re-tune** — the marker says how to take what follows, not what it means)]

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: which of the four a written text hides from you, and why (*sí,
  claro* — the irony is in the voice)]
- [YOU SAY: which of the four you could not have guessed without being taught]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-SEMANTIC-IMPLICATURE, ES-SEMANTIC-IRONY, ES-SEMANTIC-IDIOM-OPAQUE, ES-SEMANTIC-TONE-SHIFT] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name the four strategies a listener uses: infer, invert, look it up,
re-tune. Which one is carried in the voice rather than the words? (Irony.) Which
one cannot be worked out at all? (The idiom.)
