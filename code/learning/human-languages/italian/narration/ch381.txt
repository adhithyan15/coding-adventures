Italian, chapter 381: Studiare a fondo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il docente — the teacher, the lecturer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the enrolment, then the Italian for the semester.

You'll want to know: il docente.
il docente — "the teacher, the lecturer".
Il docente risponde alle domande dopo la lezione. The lecturer answers questions after the lesson.
Formal; one form for a man or a woman. In speech, il professore.

The word, taken apart.
docente is a learned borrowing from Latin docēns, present participle of docēre "to teach" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

il relatore — the thesis supervisor; the speaker (at a conference).

You'll want to know: il relatore.
il relatore — "the thesis supervisor; the speaker (at a conference)".
Il mio relatore legge la tesi pagina per pagina. My supervisor reads the thesis page by page.
At a conference, il relatore gives a talk.

The word, taken apart.
relatore is a learned borrowing from Latin relātor "one who reports, a narrator" (Lewis & Short), from relātus, the past participle of referre "to bring back, to report".

Lesson 3 of 5.
la borsa di studio — the scholarship, the grant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the teacher, then the Italian for the thesis supervisor.

You'll want to know: la borsa di studio.
la borsa di studio — "the scholarship, the grant".
Se ho buoni voti, posso avere una borsa di studio. If I get good marks, I can get a scholarship.
Literally a purse for study.

The word, taken apart.
borsa is inherited from Late Latin bursa "a leather bag", from Greek býrsa "a hide"; studio is a learned borrowing from Latin studium "zeal, eagerness, study" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'appello — the exam session; the roll call; the appeal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the scholarship, then the Italian for the doctorate.

You'll want to know: l'appello.
l'appello — "the exam session; the roll call; the appeal".
Se non passo l'esame a giugno, provo all'appello di luglio. If I do not pass the exam in June, I will try at the July session.
At school, fare l'appello is to call the register.

The word, taken apart.
appello is built on appellare (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin appellāre "to address, to call upon, to call by name" (Lewis & Short).
