## You'll want to know: o gesso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C307-FERIDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o gesso** — "the plaster (cast)".

> O meu irmão anda de gesso há um mês.
>
> *My brother has been in a plaster cast for a month.*

Also plaster for walls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gesso* comes from Latin *gypsum* (EtymDB), from Greek *gýpsos* "chalk, gypsum".
