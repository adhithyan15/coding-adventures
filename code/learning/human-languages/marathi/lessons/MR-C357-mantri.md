## You'll want to know: मंत्री
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C357-SHASAN357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मंत्री** — *mantrī* — "a cabinet minister; a minister".

> मंत्र्यांनी नव्या पुलाचं उद्घाटन केलं.
>
> *mantryānnī navyā pulācaṁ udghāṭan kelaṁ.*
>
> The minister opened the new bridge.

Masculine.

Respect takes the plural: मंत्री आले, the minister came.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मंत्री** (*mantrī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mantrin*, "a counsellor, a minister" (Monier-Williams). Marathi drops the final -n.
