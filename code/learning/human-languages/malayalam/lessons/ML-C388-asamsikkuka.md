# ആശംസിക്കുക (āśaṁsikkuka) — to wish someone well

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a gratuity, then the Malayalam for to bless.

## You'll want to know: ആശംസിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C388-ASAMSA388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ആശംസിക്കുക** — *āśaṁsikkuka* — "to wish someone well".

> എല്ലാവരും ദമ്പതികൾക്ക് നല്ല ഭാവി ആശംസിച്ചു.
>
> *ellāvaruṁ dampatikaḷkkŭ nalla bhāvi āśaṁsiccu.*
>
> Everyone wished the couple a good future.

Past: ആശംസിച്ചു, *āśaṁsiccu*.

The person takes -ക്ക് or -ന്. The noun is ആശംസകൾ, good wishes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആശംസിക്കുക** (*āśaṁsikkuka*) is built from the Sanskrit verb *ā-śaṁs*, "to hope for, to wish" (Monier-Williams), whose noun gave ആശംസകൾ (*āśaṁsakaḷ*), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
