## You'll want to know: きたく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C153-OUTING153-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きたく** — *kitaku* — "coming home; getting home".

> **ちちの きたくは ゆうがたです。** — *chichi no kitaku wa yūgata desu* — "My father gets home in the evening."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きたく** (*kitaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "return home" (Middle Chinese *kjw+j*) and *taku*, "house, home" (*draek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
