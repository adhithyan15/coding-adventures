## You'll want to know: miǎnfèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C156-MIANFEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*miǎnfèi* — "free (of charge)".

> *shuǐ miǎnfèi* — The water's free.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miǎnfèi* is built from miǎn 'to be spared' and fèi 'cost'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjenX* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to escape; to remove' and 'to use up; to spend'. Spared the spending: free.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C156-YUAN-01] -->

*miǎnfèi* follows the thing, as a price does: *shuǐ miǎnfèi*, where a price list would say 水 一元.

Its first syllable dips: *miǎn*.
