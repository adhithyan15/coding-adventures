## You'll want to know: ಗ್ರಂಥ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C409-PRABANDHA409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗ್ರಂಥ** — *grantha* — "a treatise; a book".

> ಅಜ್ಜನ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಹಳೆಯ ಗ್ರಂಥಗಳು ಇವೆ.
>
> *ajjana maneyalli tumbā haḷeya granthagaḷu ive.*
>
> Grandfather's house has many old books.

Plural: ಗ್ರಂಥಗಳು, *granthagaḷu*.

A weighty or old book. ಗ್ರಂಥಾಲಯ, a library.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗ್ರಂಥ** (*grantha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *grantha*, "a knot", and later "a book" (CDIAL 4350), from *granth*, "to tie".
