## You'll want to know: encadrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C518-EXPOSITION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**encadrer** — "to put in a frame; to supervise".

> J'ai fait encadrer une vieille photo de mes parents.
>
> *I had an old photo of my parents framed.*

Also to supervise: *encadrer une équipe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*encadrer* is built from *en-* and *cadre* "frame", borrowed from Italian *quadro*, from Latin *quadrum* "a square" (Lewis & Short).
