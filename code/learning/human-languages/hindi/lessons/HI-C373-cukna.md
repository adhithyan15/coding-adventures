## You'll want to know: चूकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C373-MUMKIN373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चूकना** — *cūknā* — "to miss (a chance, a mark)".

> अगर तुम तेज़ नहीं दौड़ोगे तो गेंद पकड़ने से चूक जाओगे।
>
> *agar tum tez nahīṃ dauṛoge to gẽd pakaṛne se cūk jāoge.*
>
> If you don't run fast, you'll fail to catch the ball.

Past (he …): वह चूक गया, *vah cūk gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूकना** (*cūknā*) is inherited from *\*cukk*, "fall short", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4848).
