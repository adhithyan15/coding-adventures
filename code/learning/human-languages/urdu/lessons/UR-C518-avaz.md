## You'll want to know: آواز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C518-RIYAZ518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آواز** — *āvāz* — "a voice; a sound".

> اس گلوکار کی آواز میٹھی ہے۔
>
> *is gulūkār kī āvāz mīṭhī hai.*
>
> This singer has a sweet voice.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آواز** (*āvāz*) is usually traced to Persian *āvāz*, "a voice, a sound, a song".
