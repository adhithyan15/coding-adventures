Punjabi, chapter 225: Waking Up Early.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪਸਾਰਨਾ (pasārnā) — to stretch out (arms or legs).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to take out, then the Punjabi for perhaps.

You'll want to know: ਪਸਾਰਨਾ.
ਪਸਾਰਨਾ — pasārnā — "to stretch out (arms or legs)".
ਸਵੇਰੇ ਮੈਂ ਮੰਜੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਲੱਤਾਂ ਪਸਾਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। savere maiṁ manje utte lattāṁ pasārdā hāṁ. In the morning I stretch out my legs on the cot.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਲੱਤਾਂ ਪਸਾਰੀਆਂ, maiṁ lattāṁ pasārīāṁ.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਸਾਰਨਾ (pasārnā) is inherited from Sanskrit prasārayati, "stretches out, extends"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pasārṇā, "to stretch out" (CDIAL 8838). CDIAL adds that the Punjabi form may have been taken from the central languages.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਜਗਾਉਣਾ (jagāuṇā) — to wake (someone) up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for perhaps, then the Punjabi for to stretch out.

You'll want to know: ਜਗਾਉਣਾ.
ਜਗਾਉਣਾ — jagāuṇā — "to wake (someone) up".
ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਜੇ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਜਗਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ savere chhe vaje bachchiāṁ nū̃ jagāundī hāṁ. I wake the children at six in the morning.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਜਗਾਇਆ, maiṁ Aman nū̃ jagāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਜਗਾਉਣਾ (jagāuṇā) is the causative partner of ਜਾਗਣਾ, built on a verb inherited from Sanskrit jāgrati, "are awake"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jagāuṇā under it (CDIAL 5175).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਵਿਹਲਾ (vihlā) — free; not busy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to stretch out, then the Punjabi for to wake (someone) up.

You'll want to know: ਵਿਹਲਾ.
ਵਿਹਲਾ — vihlā — "free; not busy".
ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਵਿਹਲਾ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। shām nū̃ maiṁ vihlā hundā hāṁ. In the evening I am free.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਿਹਲਾ (vihlā) is inherited from a reconstructed vēlya, "at leisure"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vihlā, "vacant, at leisure" (CDIAL 12118).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਕਾਹਲਾ (kāhlā) — in a hurry; hasty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for not busy, then the Punjabi for to lie down.

You'll want to know: ਕਾਹਲਾ.
ਕਾਹਲਾ — kāhlā — "in a hurry; hasty".
ਸਵੇਰੇ ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਕਾਹਲਾ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। savere maiṁ bahut kāhlā hundā hāṁ. In the mornings I am in a great hurry.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਾਹਲਾ (kāhlā) is inherited: CDIAL sets it under Sanskrit kāhala, "speaking indistinctly, faint-hearted", and lists Punjabi kāhlā, "impatient, hasty" (CDIAL 3142); CDIAL does not give the everyday sense "in a hurry" as such.
