## You'll want to know: बाँध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C305-BAADH305-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बाँध** — *bā̃dh* — "a dam; an embankment".

> पानी के ज़ोर से बाँध टूट गया।
>
> *pānī ke zor se bā̃dh ṭūṭ gayā.*
>
> The force of the water broke the dam.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

comes from Sanskrit *bandha*, "a binding, a fastening" (Monier-Williams), the family of **बाँधना**, to tie: a **बाँध** ties the water in.
