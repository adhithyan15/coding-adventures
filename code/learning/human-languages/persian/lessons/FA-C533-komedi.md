## You'll want to know: کمدی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C533-MEDIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کمدی** (*komedi*) — "a comedy".

> ما یک کمدی خنده دار دیدیم.
>
> *mâ yek komedi-ye khandedâr didim.*
>
> We watched a funny comedy.

Plural *komedihâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*komedi* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *comédie*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
