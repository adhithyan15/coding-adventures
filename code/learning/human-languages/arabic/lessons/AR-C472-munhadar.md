## You'll want to know: منحدر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C472-QIMMA472-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منحدر** — *munḥadar* — "a slope".

> نزلنا المنحدر ببطء.
>
> *nazalnā al-munḥadar bi-buṭʾ.*
>
> We went down the slope slowly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منحدر** (*munḥadar*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-d-r*, "to descend" (Lane). It is the place-noun of Form VII, "the way down".
