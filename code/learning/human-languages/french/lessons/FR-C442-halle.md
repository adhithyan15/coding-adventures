## You'll want to know: la halle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C442-FACADES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la halle** — "the covered market (hall)".

> Le samedi, les paysans vendent leurs légumes sous la halle.
>
> *On Saturdays the farmers sell their vegetables in the covered market.*

*Les halles* — the covered market of a town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*halle* comes from Old French *hale*, from Frankish *halla* 'large room, hall' (EtymDB).
