# కలవరపడు (kalavarapaḍu) — to be agitated; to be upset

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-01, TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for gloom, then the Telugu for to worry.

## You'll want to know: కలవరపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**కలవరపడు** — *kalavarapaḍu* — "to be agitated; to be upset".

> ఆ వార్త విని అమ్మ కలవరపడింది.
>
> *ā vārta vini amma kalavarapaḍindi.*
>
> Hearing that news, mum was upset.

Past (I …): కలవరపడ్డాను, *kalavarapaḍḍānu*.

The noun is కలవరం, confusion of mind. A noun of feeling + -పడు means to feel it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కలవరపడు** (*kalavarapaḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1306 lists Telugu *kalavaramu*, "confusion, anxiety", and *kalavara-paḍu*, "to be confused", beside Tamil *kalavaram*, "perturbation". The second part is the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
