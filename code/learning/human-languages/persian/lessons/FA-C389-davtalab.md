## You'll want to know: داوطلب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C389-DAVTALAB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**داوطلب** (*dâvtalab*) — "a volunteer; a candidate".

> صد داوطلب بعد از زلزله کمک کردند.
>
> *sad dâvtalab ba'd az zelzele komak kardand.*
>
> A hundred volunteers helped after the earthquake.

Also an exam candidate. Plural *dâvtalabân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**داوطلب** joins *dâv* "a turn, a stake", a Persian word whose origin is not settled, to *talab* "seeking", from Arabic *ṭalab*: someone who seeks a turn.
