Arabic, chapter 308: An investigation.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
تحقيق (taḥqīq) — an investigation, an inquiry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a battle, then the Arabic for to wage war.

You'll want to know: تحقيق.
تحقيق — taḥqīq — "an investigation, an inquiry".
بدأت الشرطة تحقيقا في الحادث. badaʾat ash-shurṭa taḥqīqan fī al-ḥādith. The police opened an investigation into the accident.
Plural تحقيقات. In the press, a تحقيق (taḥqīq) is an investigative report.

The word, taken apart.
تحقيق (taḥqīq) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-q-q (Lane), the root of ḥaqq, truth and right. Taḥqīq is the verbal noun of Form II: "making sure of the truth".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: دليل.
دليل — dalīl — "evidence, proof; a guide".
وجد المحقق دليلا في الغرفة. wajada al-muḥaqqiq dalīlan fī al-ghurfa. The investigator found a piece of evidence in the room.
Plural: أدلة, adilla.
Also a guidebook or a guide: دليل (dalīl) سياحي.

The word, taken apart.
دليل (dalīl) is built on the native Arabic root d-l-l (Lane), "to direct, to show the way". A dalīl is what points the way to a place, or to the truth.

Lesson 5 of 6.
بصمة (baṣma) — a fingerprint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an investigator, then the Arabic for evidence.

You'll want to know: بصمة.
بصمة — baṣma — "a fingerprint".
وجدت الشرطة بصمة على الباب. wajadat ash-shurṭa baṣma ʿalā al-bāb. The police found a fingerprint on the door.
Plural بصمات. بصمة (baṣma) الإصبع in full.

The word, taken apart.
بصمة (baṣma) is built on the native Arabic root b-ṣ-m (Lane), where buṣm is the span between the little finger and the third finger spread apart. Baṣma, a fingerprint, is a modern sense.
