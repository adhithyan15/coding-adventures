## You'll want to know: hǎochu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C373-PROSCONS373-02]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎochu* — "benefit; advantage".

> *wǒ juéde měitiān yùndòng yǒu hǎochu* — I think exercising every day does you good.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎochu* is built from 好 'good' and *chù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xawX* and *tsyhoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'good' and the second 'a place'. The second is said light here.
