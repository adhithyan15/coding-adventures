## You'll want to know: きまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C168-DELAY168-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きまる** — *kimaru* — "to be decided; to be settled".

> **らいしゅう よていが きまります。** — *raishū yotei ga kimarimasu* — "The plan will be decided next week."

Polite **きまります**, *kimarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きまる** (*kimaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
