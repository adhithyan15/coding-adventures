## You'll want to know: मात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C545-JUA545-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मात** — *māt* — "defeat; checkmate".

> खेल में मीरा ने अरुण को मात दी।
>
> *Khel meṁ Mīrā ne Aruṇ ko māt dī.*
>
> Mira beat Arun at the game.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मात** (*māt*) is usually traced to Persian *māt*, "checkmated, at a loss".
