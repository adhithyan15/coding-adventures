Marathi, chapter 86: Banana, Potato, Carrot, Sugar, Salt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
केळे (keḷe) — a banana.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fruit, then the Marathi for an apple.

You'll want to know: केळे.
केळे — keḷe — "a banana".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: keḷe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: keḷe, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say keḷe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a fruit, then the Marathi for an apple, then say keḷe again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does केळे (keḷe) mean? ("A banana".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: a hand, a foot, hair, a tooth, the throat]

[your turn — say: the stomach, a shoulder, a finger, the neck, the face]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: blood, a knee, the head, a fever, a clinic]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ill, pain, thirst, hunger, happy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sad, a father, a mother, a son; a boy, a daughter; a girl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a grandfather, a grandmother, an uncle, an aunt, an uncle]

[your turn — say: a husband, a wife, a baby, a person, a woman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a neighbour, a guest, a teacher, to hear, to get up]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 3 seconds]
