## You'll want to know: বিবরণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C301-BIBORON301-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিবরণ** — *bibôrôn* — "an account; a description".

> সাক্ষী পুলিশকে ঘটনার পুরো বিবরণ দিল।
>
> *sākkhī pulishke ghôṭônār puro bibôrôn dilô.*
>
> The witness gave the police a full account of what happened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিবরণ** (*bibôrôn*) is Sanskrit *vivaraṇa*, 'an uncovering, a laying open; an explanation' (Monier-Williams): an account lays the events open.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
