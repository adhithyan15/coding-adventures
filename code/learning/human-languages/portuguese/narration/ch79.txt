Portuguese, chapter 79: Forty to a Thousand, and Zero.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
quarenta — forty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for socks, then the Portuguese for a belt.

You'll want to know: quarenta.
quarenta — "forty".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: quarenta]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: quarenta, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say quarenta]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for socks, then the Portuguese for a belt, then say quarenta again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does quarenta mean? ("Forty".) Say it once more.
