# पुगावणो (pugāvṇo) — to take to a place; to see off

## You'll want to know: पुगावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C218-RAIL218-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पुगावणो** — *pugāvṇo* — "to take to a place; to see off".

> हूं काल दूध सहर पुगासूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl dūdh sahar pugāsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll take the milk to the city.

Past (I …): म्हैं दूध सहर पुगायो, *mhaĩ dūdh sahar pugāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुगावणो** (*pugāvṇo*) is built from **पूगणो**, to reach, with the causative *-āv-*: to make someone or something reach a place. **पूगणो** goes back to Sanskrit *pūryate*, "is filled"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 8342).
