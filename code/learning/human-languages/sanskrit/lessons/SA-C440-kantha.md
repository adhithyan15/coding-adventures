## You'll want to know: कन्था
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C440-HIMA440-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कन्था** — *kanthā* — "a patched garment; a quilt of rags".

> शीते भिक्षुः कन्थां धारयति।
>
> *śīte bhikṣuḥ kanthāṃ dhārayati.*
>
> In the cold the monk wears a patched garment.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कन्था** (*kanthā*) is the word MW gives for a rag or a patched garment, especially one that ascetics wear; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it lives on as Hindi *kathrī*, "a quilt of patches" (MW; CDIAL 2721).
