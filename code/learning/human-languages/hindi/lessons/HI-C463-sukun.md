## You'll want to know: सुकून
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C463-SUKUN463-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सुकून** — *sukūn* — "peace of mind".

> गाँव की शांत शाम में बहुत सुकून है।
>
> *gā̃v kī śānt śām meṁ bahut sukūn hai.*
>
> There is a great peace in a quiet village evening.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुकून** (*sukūn*) is usually traced to Arabic *sukūn*, "stillness, rest", which reached Hindi through Persian.
