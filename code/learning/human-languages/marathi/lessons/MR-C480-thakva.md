## You'll want to know: थकवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C480-TABYET480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**थकवा** — *thakvā* — "fatigue".

> प्रवासानंतर त्याला खूप थकवा आला.
>
> *pravāsānantar tyālā khūp thakvā ālā.*
>
> He felt very tired after the journey.

Masculine.

From थकणे, to get tired: थकवा येणे, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थकवा** (*thakvā*) is the Marathi noun of थकणे, "to get tired", which goes back to the reconstructed *\*sthakk*, "stop, halt" (CDIAL 13737).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
