## You'll want to know: le décollage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C419-DECOLLAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le décollage** — "the take-off".

> Au décollage, les enfants regardent par la fenêtre.
>
> *At take-off the children look out of the window.*

Built on *décoller* with *-age*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*décollage* is built on *décoller* with *-age*. *Décoller* is *dé-* 'un-' plus *coller* 'to stick', from *colle* 'glue', from Greek *kólla* (EtymDB).
