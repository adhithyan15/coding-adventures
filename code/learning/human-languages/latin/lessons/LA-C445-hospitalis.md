## You'll want to know: hospitālis, hospitāle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C445-DEVERTICULUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**hospitālis, hospitāle** — "hospitable; of a guest or host".

> Caupō hospitālis est et viātōribus bonam cēnam dat.
>
> *The innkeeper is hospitable and gives the travellers a good dinner.*

An adjective of two endings, from *hospes* 'host, guest'. English *hospital* and *hospitable* come from the same family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hospitālis* is built on *hospes, hospitis* 'host, guest', from PIE *gʰóspot-* 'master of guests' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *hospital* comes from it through French.
