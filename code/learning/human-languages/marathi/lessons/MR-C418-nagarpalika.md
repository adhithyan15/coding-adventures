## You'll want to know: नगरपालिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C418-NAGAR418-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नगरपालिका** — *nagarpālikā* — "a municipal council".

> नगरपालिकेने नवीन रस्ता बांधला.
>
> *nagarpālikene navīn rastā bāndhlā.*
>
> The municipal council built a new road.

Feminine.

Before an ending -का becomes -के-: नगरपालिकेने.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नगरपालिका** (*nagarpālikā*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *nagara*, "a town", and *pālaka*, "a guardian, a protector" (Monier-Williams), here in its feminine *pālikā*.
