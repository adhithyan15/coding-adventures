## You'll want to know: आविष्करोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C323-STUPA323-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आविष्करोति** — *āviṣkaroti* — "brings to light; discovers".

> वैज्ञानिकः प्राचीनं नगरम् आविष्करोति।
>
> *vaijñānikaḥ prācīnaṃ nagaram āviṣkaroti.*
>
> The scientist brings an ancient city to light.

Past (he …): सः नगरम् आविष्कृतवान्, *saḥ nagaram āviṣkṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आविष्करोति** (*āviṣkaroti*) is built from *āvis*, "openly, before the eyes", and the root √kṛ, "to make"; MW gives *āviṣ-kṛ* as to make apparent, reveal, uncover, and to discover is its modern use (MW).
