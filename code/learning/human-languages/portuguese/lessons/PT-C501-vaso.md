## You'll want to know: o vaso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C501-JARDIM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o vaso** — "flowerpot; vase".

> Tenho um vaso de flores na varanda.
>
> *I have a pot of flowers on the balcony.*

In Portugal mainly a pot for plants; cut flowers go in *uma jarra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vaso* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Latin *vāsum* "a vessel" (EtymDB).
