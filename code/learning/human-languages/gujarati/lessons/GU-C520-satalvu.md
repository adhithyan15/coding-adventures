# સાંતળવું (sā̃taḷvũ) — to sauté

## You'll want to know: સાંતળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C520-VAGHAR520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંતળવું** — *sā̃taḷvũ* — "to sauté".

> મીરાએ તેલમાં ડુંગળી સાંતળી.
>
> *Mīrāe telmā̃ ḍungḷī sā̃tḷī.*
>
> Mira sautéed the onion in oil.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ડુંગળી સાંતળી, *teṇe ḍungḷī sā̃tḷī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંતળવું** (*sā̃taḷvũ*) comes from *\*saṁtalati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to fry in ghee or oil" (CDIAL 12883).
