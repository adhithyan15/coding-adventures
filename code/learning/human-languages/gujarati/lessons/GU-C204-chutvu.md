# છૂટવું (chūṭvũ) — to leave, to get loose

## You'll want to know: છૂટવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C204-TIMES204-05]; assesses=[] -->

**છૂટવું** — *chūṭvũ* — "to leave, to get loose".

> **મારી ટ્રેન છૂટી ગઈ.** — *mārī ṭren chūṭī gaī* — "My train left without me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છૂટવું** (*chūṭvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to get loose", under *\*kṣuṭyatē*, "is released", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 3707).
