## You'll want to know: یخبندان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C476-KULAK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**یخبندان** (*yakhbandân*) — "a frost, freezing weather".

> در یخبندان لوله های آب ترکید.
>
> *Dar yakhbandân lule-hâ-ye âb tarkid.*
>
> In the frost the water pipes burst.

*yakh* 'ice' + *band* 'binding' + *-ân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yakhbandân* is a native compound of *yakh* 'ice', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Iranian *aixa-*, *band* 'binding', from the root of *bastan*, and the ending *-ân*.
