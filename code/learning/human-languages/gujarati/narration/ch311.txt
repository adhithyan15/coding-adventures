Gujarati, chapter 311: The Morning News.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સમાચાર (samācār) — the news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be at a loss, then the Gujarati for to disperse.

You'll want to know: સમાચાર.
સમાચાર — samācār — "the news".
મેં સવારે સમાચાર સાંભળ્યા. mẽ savāre samācār sā̃bhaḷyā. I listened to the news this morning.
A masculine noun, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
સમાચાર (samācār) is a tatsama, Sanskrit samācāra, "procedure, conduct, custom" (Monier-Williams); the sense "news" is the modern language's. The verb with it is plural: સમાચાર સાંભળ્યા.

You'll want to know: છાપું.
છાપું — chāpũ — "a newspaper".
મારા દાદા રોજ સવારે છાપું વાંચે છે. mārā dādā roj savāre chāpũ vā̃ce che. My grandfather reads the newspaper every morning.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
છાપું (chāpũ) is built on છાપવું, to print, from chapp, "press, cover", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4994): the printed sheet. છાપામાં, in the paper.

Lesson 3 of 5.
મથાળું (mathāḷũ) — a headline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the news, then the Gujarati for a newspaper.

You'll want to know: મથાળું.
મથાળું — mathāḷũ — "a headline".
છાપાનું મથાળું હતું: પોલીસે ઠગને ઝડપ્યો. chāpānũ mathāḷũ hatũ: polīse ṭhagne jhaḍapyo. The headline read: the police nab a swindler.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
મથાળું (mathāḷũ) comes from mastatalaka, a form CDIAL reconstructs on Sanskrit mastaka, the head, and CDIAL lists Gujarati mathāḷũ, "top, heading" (CDIAL 9927). In a newspaper it is the line at the head of a story.

Lesson 5 of 5.
જણાવવું (jaṇāvvũ) — to inform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a headline, then the Gujarati for a radio.

You'll want to know: જણાવવું.
જણાવવું — jaṇāvvũ — "to inform".
પોલીસે લોકોને જણાવ્યું કે રસ્તો બંધ છે. polīse lokone jaṇāvyũ ke rasto bandh che. The police told people that the road was closed.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધાને જણાવ્યું, teṇe badhāne jaṇāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
જણાવવું (jaṇāvvũ) belongs with Sanskrit jānāti, "knows", where CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "informs" (CDIAL 5193): to make someone know. It sits beside જાણવું, to know.
