## You'll want to know: il libretto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C415-NOLEGGIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il libretto** — "the logbook (of a car); the booklet".

> Il vigile chiede patente e libretto.
>
> *The warden asks for the licence and the logbook.*

*Il libretto di circolazione* is the car's registration document; at university, *il libretto* records your exams.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*libretto* is a little *libro* 'book', which is inherited from Latin *liber* 'book' (EtymDB).
