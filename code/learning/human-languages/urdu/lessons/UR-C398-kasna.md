## You'll want to know: کسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C398-PARVAZ398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کسنا** — *kasnā* — "to tighten; to fasten".

> پرواز سے پہلے بیلٹ کس لیجیے۔
>
> *parvāz se pehle belṭ kas lījiye.*
>
> Please fasten your seat belt before the flight.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے رسی کسی, *us ne rassī kasī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کسنا** (*kasnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kasnā*, "to tighten", under Sanskrit *kárṣati*, "draws, pulls" (CDIAL 2908).
