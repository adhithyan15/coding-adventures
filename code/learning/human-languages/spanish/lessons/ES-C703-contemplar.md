## You'll want to know: contemplar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C703-ANOCHECER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**contemplar** — "to gaze at, to contemplate".

> Contemplamos el paisaje en silencio.
>
> *We gazed at the landscape in silence.*

Slower and calmer than *mirar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contemplar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *contemplārī* "to observe, to gaze at" (EtymDB), built on *templum*, an open space marked out for watching the sky.
