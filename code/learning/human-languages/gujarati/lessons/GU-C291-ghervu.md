## You'll want to know: ઘેરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C291-GHERVU291-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘેરવું** — *ghervũ* — "to surround".

> પોલીસે આખું મકાન ઘેર્યું.
>
> *polīse ākhũ makān gheryũ.*
>
> The police surrounded the whole building.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મકાન ઘેર્યું, *teṇe makān gheryũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘેરવું** (*ghervũ*) comes from *\*ghēr-*, "to make go round", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to surround" (CDIAL 4474).
