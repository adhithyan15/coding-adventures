## You'll want to know: कसीदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C526-FURSAT526-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कसीदा** — *kasīdā* — "embroidery (a pattern sewn in thread)".

> मेरी नानी दुपट्टे पर सुंदर कसीदा करती हैं।
>
> *Merī nānī dupaṭṭe par sundar kasīdā kartī haiṁ.*
>
> My grandmother embroiders beautiful patterns on dupattas.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसीदा** (*kasīdā*) is usually traced to Persian *kashīda*, "drawn (out)". The threads are drawn through the cloth.
