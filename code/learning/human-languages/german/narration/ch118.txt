German, chapter 118: Candle, Calendar, Jumper, Tie, Gloves.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Kerze — a candle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a present, then the German for a photo.

You'll want to know: die Kerze.
die Kerze — "a candle".

The word, taken apart.
Kerze from Middle High German kerze from Old High German kerza (EtymDB 2.1). Its origin is uncertain; one account derives it from Latin charta, "papyrus", used as a wick.

Krawatte from French cravate, from the Croatian word for "Croat" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed: the neckcloths of Croatian soldiers in French service set the fashion.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a jumper, then the German for a tie.

You'll want to know: die Handschuhe.
die Handschuhe — "gloves".

The word, taken apart.
Handschuhe is the plural of Handschuh from Middle High German hantschuoch: Hand + Schuh (EtymDB 2.1). A glove is a shoe for the hand.
