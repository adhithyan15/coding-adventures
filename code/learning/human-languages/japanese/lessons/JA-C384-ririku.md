## You'll want to know: りりく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C384-HIKOUJOU384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**りりく** — *ririku* — "takeoff".

> **ひこうきは くじに りりくしました。** — *hikōki wa kuji ni ririku shimashita* — "The plane took off at nine."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りりく** (*ririku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ri*, "detach" (Middle Chinese *lje*) and *riku*, "land" (*ljuwk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
