# অলৌকিক (ôloukik) — supernatural; miraculous

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C325-UPODESH325-05, BN-LEX-C326-ASHCHORJO326-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for to be forced, then the Bengali for amazing.

## You'll want to know: অলৌকিক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C326-ASHCHORJO326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অলৌকিক** — *ôloukik* — "supernatural; miraculous".

> লোকে বলে যে মন্দিরের পুকুরের জলে অলৌকিক শক্তি আছে।
>
> *loke bôle je mondirer pukurer jôle ôloukik shôkti āchhe.*
>
> People say the water of the temple pond has supernatural power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অলৌকিক** (*ôloukik*) is Sanskrit *alaukika*, 'uncommon; not of this world, supernatural' (Monier-Williams): *a-*, 'not', and *laukika*, 'worldly'.
