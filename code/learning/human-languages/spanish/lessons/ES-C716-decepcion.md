## You'll want to know: la decepción
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C716-LASTIMA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la decepción** — "disappointment".

> La película fue una decepción.
>
> *The film was a disappointment.*

A false friend: it means disappointment, not deception.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*decepción* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēceptiō* "deceit", from *dēcipere* "to catch out", built on *capere* "to take"; it probably took the sense of disappointment from French *déception*.
