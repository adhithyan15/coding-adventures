Malayalam, chapter 89: What You Think Of It.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
നല്ലത് (nallatŭ) — a good one.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say good. Then say where it has to stand, and what it needs after it.

Grammar Lens: a quality with nothing to lean on.
നല്ല has always needed a noun to sit in front of. You can say a good book and you cannot say the good one — there is nothing for the quality to lean on.
-അത് fixes that. Put it on the end of a quality and the quality becomes a thing:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
നല്ല, good — needs a noun after it.
നല്ലത് (nallatŭ), a good one, a good thing — stands alone.
And look at what that ending is. അത് is the word for that, which you have had since the pointing chapter. നല്ലത് (nallatŭ) is the that-which-is-good — the sense fits the shape exactly.
This works on every quality the book has given you, and that is the reason it is worth a lesson rather than a footnote:
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
വലിയ becomes വലിയത്, a big one.
ചെറിയ becomes ചെറിയത്, a small one.
പുതിയ becomes പുതിയത്, a new one.
പഴയ becomes പഴയത്, an old one.
Five qualities, one ending, and no exceptions among them.

Lesson 2 of 4.
മോശം (mōśaṁ) — bad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say a good one. Then notice what you still cannot say about anything at all.

You'll want to know: മോശം.
മോശം — mōśaṁ — bad, poor.
The book has given you five qualities and not one of them is negative. A thing could be good, big, small, new or old, and there was no way to say it was bad — which is half of every opinion anybody has ever offered.
Put it with the is word you have had since the second chapter:
മോശമാണ് — mōśamāṇŭ — it is bad.
Notice the join. മോശം (mōśaṁ) ends in the ം, and when ആണ് follows it that ം is written മ. You have seen this book change a word's shape before something else attaches; this is the same habit in a smaller place, and here it is only the writing that moves.
The partner sentence is built the same way, from the word you learned a moment ago: നല്ലതാണ് — nallatāṇŭ — it is good.

Lesson 3 of 4.
നല്ലതാണ് എന്ന് ഞാൻ ചിന്തിക്കുന്നു (nallatāṇŭ ennŭ ñān cintikkunnu) — I think it is good.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say it is bad. Then say the marker that turns any sentence into something you can report.

Grammar Lens: nothing new is needed.
An opinion is a sentence with your own judgement wrapped around it, and you now have both halves.
അത് നല്ലതാണ് എന്ന് ഞാൻ ചിന്തിക്കുന്നു. atŭ nallatāṇŭ ennŭ ñān cintikkunnu — I think it is good.
Take it apart and every piece is already yours:
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
piece: അത് നല്ലതാണ്. where it came from: this chapter — it is good.
piece: എന്ന്. where it came from: the quoting marker.
piece: ഞാൻ ചിന്തിക്കുന്നു. where it came from: the thinking verb, already paired with that marker.
The quoting chapter did the hard part. It showed that the marker takes whatever sentence you can build and hands it to a verb of saying, thinking, knowing or asking — so an opinion needed no new grammar at all, only something worth having an opinion about.
Swap the inner sentence and the frame does not move:
അത് മോശമാണ് എന്ന് ഞാൻ ചിന്തിക്കുന്നു — I think it is bad.
This is what a well-built system looks like from the inside. Two vocabulary words arrived, and a whole function — giving an opinion — came with them, because the machinery was already in place and waiting for something to carry.

Lesson 4 of 4.
(what you think of it) (abhipraayam enna ōrmma) — from cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Close the lessons before this one. Say a good one, then say bad.

Grammar Lens: a quality standing on its own.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
നല്ല, good — needs a noun after it.
നല്ലത് (nallatŭ), a good one — stands alone.
-അത് is the word for that, and നല്ലത് (nallatŭ) is the that-which-is-good. The sense fits the shape, and the ending works on every quality the book has given you — valiyatŭ, ceṟiyatŭ, putiyatŭ, paḻayatŭ — with no exceptions among them.
മോശം (mōśaṁ) filled the other hole. Five qualities and not one of them negative meant half of every opinion was unsayable. Before the is word, that ം is written മ: മോശമാണ്.

What you've built.
An opinion, out of parts that were already lying about.
അത് നല്ലതാണ് എന്ന് ഞാൻ ചിന്തിക്കുന്നു (nallatāṇŭ ennŭ ñān cintikkunnu). — I think it is good.
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
piece: അത് നല്ലതാണ്. new?: the two words of this chapter.
piece: എന്ന്. new?: no — the quoting marker.
piece: ഞാൻ ചിന്തിക്കുന്നു. new?: no — already paired with that marker.
The quoting chapter did the hard part when it showed the marker takes any sentence you can build and hands it to a verb of saying, thinking, knowing or asking. So this chapter added two vocabulary words and no grammar, and a whole function arrived with them.
That is worth noticing about how this book is built: machinery is often in place before there is anything worth carrying with it, and the chapter that finally uses it can be very small.
