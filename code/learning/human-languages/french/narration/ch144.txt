French, chapter 144: To explain, To translate, To decide, To try, To continue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
expliquer — to explain.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the French for to smile, then the French for to shout.

You'll want to know: expliquer.
expliquer — "to explain".

The word, taken apart.
expliquer from Latin explicare, "to unfold" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).
