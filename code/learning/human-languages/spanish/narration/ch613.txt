Spanish, chapter 613: Lo que hace falta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
requerir — to require, to call for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for in that case, then the Spanish for in any case.

You'll want to know: requerir.
requerir — "to require, to call for".
El puesto requiere experiencia. The post requires experience.
Like sentir: requiere, requirió. Common in job offers and rules.

The word, taken apart.
requerir is inherited from Latin requirere 'to look for again, to need', built from re and quaerere 'to seek', and it follows querer in its forms (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la incertidumbre — the uncertainty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to require, then the Spanish for the requirement.

You'll want to know: la incertidumbre.
la incertidumbre — "the uncertainty".
Hay mucha incertidumbre sobre el futuro. There is a lot of uncertainty about the future.
The opposite of la certeza.

The word, taken apart.
incertidumbre is usually traced to Late Latin incertitūdō 'uncertainty', a learned word built on incertus 'not sure', from certus 'sure' (Lewis & Short).
