## You'll want to know: saevitia, saevitiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C511-CRUD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**saevitia, saevitiae** — "fury, savagery".

> Saevitia tempestātis nautās terret.
>
> *The fury of the storm frightens the sailors.*

Feminine, first declension, from *saevus* — fierce. Of beasts, storms and harsh masters.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**saevitia** is built on *saevus* "fierce, savage" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB); the origin of *saevus* is not settled.
