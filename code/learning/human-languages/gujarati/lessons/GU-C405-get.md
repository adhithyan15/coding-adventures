## You'll want to know: ગેટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C405-FLIGHT405-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગેટ** — *geṭ* — "an airport gate; a gateway".

> અમારું વિમાન પાંચ નંબરના ગેટ પરથી ઊપડશે.
>
> *amārũ vimān pā̃c nambarnā geṭ parthī ūpaḍśe.*
>
> Our plane will leave from gate number five.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગેટ** (*geṭ*) is borrowed from English *gate*, at an airport the numbered gate where you board.
