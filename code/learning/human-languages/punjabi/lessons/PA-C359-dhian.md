## You'll want to know: ਧਿਆਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C359-STUDY359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧਿਆਨ** — *dhiān* — "attention".

> ਜਮਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਧਿਆਨ ਨਾਲ ਸੁਣੋ।
>
> *jamāt vich dhiān nāl suṇo.*
>
> Listen carefully in class.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧਿਆਨ** (*dhiān*) is taken over from Sanskrit *dhyāna*, "meditation, thought, reflection", a tatsama (Monier-Williams); the everyday sense "attention" is not among them.
