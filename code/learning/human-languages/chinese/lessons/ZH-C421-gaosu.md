## You'll want to know: 高速
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C421-EXPRESS421-02]; assesses=[] -->

**高速** — *gāosù* — "expressway".

> **去高速怎么走** *qù gāosù zěnme zǒu* — How do I get onto the expressway?

The new character, in pieces: 速 — ten strokes in two pieces: the right piece first, seven strokes, a bar, a small 口, a long vertical down through them, a stroke falling to the left and a dot; then the walking piece of 这 under it, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**高速** (*gāosù*) is built from 高 'high' and 速 'speed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaw* and *suwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'swift'; it is a short form of a longer name, the high-speed road.
