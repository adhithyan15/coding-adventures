## You'll want to know: le deuil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C315-DEUIL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le deuil** — "mourning, grief".

> Toute la famille est en deuil.
>
> *The whole family is in mourning.*

*Porter le deuil* — to wear mourning; *faire son deuil* — to come to terms with a loss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*deuil* is usually traced to a Late Latin noun *dolus* "grief", built on *dolēre* "to suffer, to grieve" (Lewis & Short); it is not classical *dolus* "trickery", and EtymDB lists no parent.
