# પાંથી (pā̃thī) — the parting of the hair

## You'll want to know: પાંથી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C470-CAHERO470-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પાંથી** — *pā̃thī* — "the parting of the hair".

> મીરાની પાંથી વચ્ચે છે.
>
> *Mīrānī pā̃thī vacce che.*
>
> Mira parts her hair in the middle.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાંથી** (*pā̃thī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pánthā*, "path"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "parting of the hair" (CDIAL 7785): a little path through the hair.
