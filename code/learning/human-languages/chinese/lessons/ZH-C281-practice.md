## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-REASON-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-YINWEI-SUOYI-01, ZH-LEX-C281-BIRU-01, ZH-LEX-C281-LIZI-01, ZH-LEX-C281-YINCI-01, ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C96-SUOYI-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01, ZH-ORTHO-HANYU-01, ZH-LEX-C38-EAT38-05, ZH-LEX-C101-KITCHEN101-02] -->

> **A:** 你为什么学汉语
>
> *nǐ wèishénme xué Hànyǔ* — Why are you learning Chinese?
>
> **B:** 因为我喜欢中国 所以学汉语
>
> *yīnwèi wǒ xǐhuan Zhōngguó, suǒyǐ xué Hànyǔ* — Because I like China, so I'm learning Chinese.
>
> **A:** 你喜欢中国的什么
>
> *nǐ xǐhuan Zhōngguó de shénme* — What do you like about China?
>
> **B:** 中国菜 中国茶
>
> *Zhōngguó cài, Zhōngguó chá* — Chinese food, Chinese tea.
>
> **A:** *nǐ néng shuō yí ge lìzi ma* — Can you give an example?
>
> **B:** 比如白菜 我很喜欢
>
> *bǐrú báicài, wǒ hěn xǐhuan* — Cabbage, for one. I love it.
>
> **A:** *wǒ yě shì, yīncǐ wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó* — Me too, and so I want to go to China.

因为 … 所以 … gives the reason and the result in one breath; 比如 then backs it up. The line with *yīncǐ* sounds a little careful, as that word does.

## What you've built — a reason and an example
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-REASON-01, ZH-SCRIPT-SUO-01, ZH-SCRIPT-RU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YINWEI-SUOYI-01, ZH-LEX-C281-BIRU-01, ZH-LEX-C281-YINCI-01, ZH-LEX-C281-LIZI-01] -->

A reason, what follows from it, and something to show it:

- **因为下雨了 所以我们没去** *yīnwèi xiàyǔ le, suǒyǐ wǒmen méi qù* — the reason and the result.
- *tiānqì bù hǎo, yīncǐ wǒmen méi qù* — the careful version.
- **中国菜 比如白菜** *Zhōngguó cài, bǐrú báicài* — an example brought in.
- *qǐng shuō yí ge lìzi* — asking for one.

**Listen.** *Nǐ wèishénme xué Hànyǔ?*

**Speak.** Give two reasons for something you do every week, each with 因为 … 所以 ….

[YOU WRITE: 所以 and 比如]
