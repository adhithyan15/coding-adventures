## You'll want to know: 书法
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C503-ART503-01]; assesses=[] -->

**书法** — *shūfǎ* — "calligraphy".

> *wǒ yéye xué le shí nián shūfǎ* — My grandpa studied calligraphy for ten years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**书法** (*shūfǎ*) is built from 书 'writing' and 法 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syo* and *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a rule' and 'a norm'.
