## You'll want to know: incola, incolae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C456-INCOLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**incola, incolae** — "a resident, an inhabitant".

> Incolae vīcī nōs benignē accipiunt.
>
> *The people who live in the village receive us kindly.*

First declension, masculine or feminine: *incola, incolae*. Like *nauta* and *agricola*, a first-declension noun for a person who is often a man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incola* is built on *incolō* 'to live in' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which comes from *in-* and *colō* 'to dwell'.
