## You'll want to know: সৈকত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C398-SROT398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সৈকত** — *soikôt* — "a beach".

> সন্ধ্যায় আমরা সৈকতে হাঁটলাম।
>
> *sôndhyāy āmrā soikôte hā̃ṭlām.*
>
> In the evening we walked on the beach.

The first vowel sign is ৈ, the vowel oi; in talk people also say সমুদ্রের ধার.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সৈকত** (*soikôt*) is Sanskrit *saikata*, "sandy, made of sand" (Monier-Williams), from *sikatā*, sand: a beach is the sandy place.
