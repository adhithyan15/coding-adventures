Russian, chapter 396: Rising prices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
дорожать (dorozhát) — to go up in price.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for cost, then the Russian for a budget.

You'll want to know: дорожать.
дорожать (dorozhát) — "to go up in price".
Хлеб опять дорожает. Khleb opyát dorozháyet. Bread is going up in price again.
Imperfective; perfective подорожать: всё подорожало — everything has gone up.

The word, taken apart.
дорожать (dorozhát) is built on дорогой "dear, expensive", inherited from Proto-Slavic dorgŭ "dear": to become dear.

Lesson 2 of 5.
дешеветь (deshevét) — to get cheaper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a budget, then the Russian for to go up in price.

You'll want to know: дешеветь.
дешеветь (deshevét) — "to get cheaper".
Летом фрукты дешевеют. Létom frúkty deshevéyut. In summer fruit gets cheaper.
Imperfective; perfective подешеветь.

The word, taken apart.
дешеветь (deshevét) is built on дешёвый "cheap": to become cheap. Дешёвый goes back to Old Russian deševŭ, which is usually derived from десить "to find, to come upon": what is cheap is easily come by.

Lesson 3 of 5.
инфляция (inflyátsiya) — inflation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to go up in price, then the Russian for to get cheaper.

You'll want to know: инфляция.
инфляция (inflyátsiya) — "inflation".
В прошлом году инфляция была высокой. V próshlom godú inflyátsiya bylá vysókoy. Last year inflation was high.
Feminine. Уровень инфляции — the rate of inflation.

The word, taken apart.
инфляция (inflyátsiya) was borrowed through western languages from Latin īnflātiō "a blowing up, a swelling" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from īnflāre "to blow into": prices swell.

Lesson 4 of 5.
курс (kurs) — an exchange rate; a course; a year (of study).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get cheaper, then the Russian for inflation.

You'll want to know: курс.
курс (kurs) — "an exchange rate; a course; a year (of study)".
Какой сегодня курс доллара? Kakóy segódnya kurs dóllara? What is the dollar exchange rate today?
Masculine. На третьем курсе — in the third year at university. Курс английского — an English course.

The word, taken apart.
курс (kurs) is usually traced to German Kurs, from Latin cursus "a running, a course" (Lewis & Short), from currere "to run".
