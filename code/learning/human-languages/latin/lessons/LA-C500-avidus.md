## You'll want to know: avidus, avida, avidum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C500-CUPI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**avidus, avida, avidum** — "greedy, eager".

> Canis avidus cibum statim edit.
>
> *The greedy dog eats the food at once.*

With the genitive: *avidus glōriae* — hungry for glory. English *avid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**avidus** is built on *aveō* "to long for" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *avid* comes from it.
