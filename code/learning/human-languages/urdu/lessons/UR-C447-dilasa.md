## You'll want to know: دلاسا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C447-CALM447-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دلاسا** — *dilāsā* — "consolation; comforting words".

> دوست نے مجھے دلاسا دیا۔
>
> *dost ne mujhe dilāsā diyā.*
>
> My friend comforted me.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دلاسا** (*dilāsā*) is usually traced to Persian *dilāsā*, "comfort", a word built on *dil*, "heart" (**دل**).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
