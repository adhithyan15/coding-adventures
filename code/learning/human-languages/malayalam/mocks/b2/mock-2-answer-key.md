## Writing

Task 1 must address Sudha, balance benefit and labour concern, specify service
scope and safeguard, and request two distinct outcome measures in 100–130
independently produced Malayalam words. Task 2 must sustain a developed
220–280-word argument with cross-domain examples, differentiated stakeholders,
counterargument, conditional response, measures, limits, and conclusion.

## Speaking

Allow keyword notes only. Require candidates to compare all options and expose
trade-offs before reaching a defended joint plan. Follow-up questions should
probe affected groups, evidence, revision, and uncertainty without supplying
language. Score the complete recording.
