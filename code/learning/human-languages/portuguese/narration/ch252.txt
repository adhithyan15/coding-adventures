Lesson 1 of 5.
a derrota — a defeat.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: a derrota.
a derrota — "a defeat".

The word, taken apart.
derrota — its origin is uncertain. It is usually traced to Latin dirupta, "broken up": a broken army.

You'll want to know: o campeonato.

The word, taken apart.
campeonato is built from the word for a champion and -ato (EtymDB 2.1). The champion word came from Italian campione (EtymDB 2.1).
