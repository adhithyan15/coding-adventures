## You'll want to know: ਹਰਗਿਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C339-REFUSE339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਰਗਿਜ਼** — *hargiz* — "ever; at all (with ਨਹੀਂ: by no means)".

> ਮੈਂ ਉੱਥੇ ਹਰਗਿਜ਼ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ utthe hargiz nahīṁ jāvāṅgā.*
>
> I will by no means go there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਰਗਿਜ਼** (*hargiz*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *hargiz*, "ever".
