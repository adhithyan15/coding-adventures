## You'll want to know: ਹਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C301-PLOUGH301-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਲ** — *hal* — "a plough".

> ਸਾਡਾ ਹਲ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍā hal purāṇā hai.*
>
> Our plough is old.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਲ** (*hal*) is inherited from Sanskrit *hala*, "plough"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *hal* under it (CDIAL 14000).
