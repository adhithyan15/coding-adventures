Malayalam, chapter 126: About You.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വിലാസം (vilāsaṁ) — address.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for uncle, then the Malayalam for a nephew.

You'll want to know: വിലാസം.
വിലാസം (vilāsaṁ) — "address". നിങ്ങളുടെ വിലാസം എന്താണ്? — "what is your address?"

What you've built.
The name question, asked about a place.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vilāsaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vilāsaṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask niṅṅaḷuṭe vilāsaṁ entāṇŭ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for uncle, then the Malayalam for a nephew, then say vilāsaṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does വിലാസം (vilāsaṁ) mean? ("Address".) Say it once more.
