Hindi, chapter 512: Thread and Loom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कपास (kapās) — cotton (the plant, the raw fibre).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a mould, then the Hindi for to pour into a mould.

You'll want to know: कपास.
कपास — kapās — "cotton (the plant, the raw fibre)".
किसान ने खेत में कपास बोई। Kisān ne khet meṁ kapās boī. The farmer sowed cotton in the field.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कपास (kapās) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit karpāsa, "the cotton plant" (CDIAL 2877).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कातना (kātnā) — to spin (thread).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for cotton (the plant, the raw fibre), then the Hindi for a spinning wheel.

You'll want to know: कातना.
कातना — kātnā — "to spin (thread)".
वह चरखे पर कपास से धागा कात रही है। Vah carkhe par kapās se dhāgā kāt rahī hai. She is spinning thread from cotton on the wheel.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने धागा काता, usne dhāgā kātā.

The word, taken apart.
कातना (kātnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from kartati, "spins", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2855).
