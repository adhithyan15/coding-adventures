## You'll want to know: حوار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C302-HIWAR302-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حوار** — *ḥiwār* — "a dialogue, a conversation".

> نشرت المجلة حوارا مع الكاتب.
>
> *nasharat al-majalla ḥiwāran maʿa al-kātib.*
>
> The magazine published a conversation with the writer.

In the press a حوار is an interview printed as questions and answers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حوار** (*ḥiwār*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-r* (Lane), "to go back, to return". Speech that goes back and forth is a *ḥiwār*, the verbal noun of Form III.
