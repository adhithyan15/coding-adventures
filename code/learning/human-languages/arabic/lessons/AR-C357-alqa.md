## You'll want to know: ألقى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C357-KHITAB357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ألقى** — *alqā* — "to deliver a speech; to throw".

> ألقت الطالبة كلمة في الحفل.
>
> *alqat aṭ-ṭāliba kalima fī al-ḥafl.*
>
> The student gave a speech at the ceremony.

Present: يلقي, *yulqī*.

ألقى خطابا, محاضرة: to give a speech, a lecture. ألقى القبض على: to arrest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ألقى** (*alqā*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-q-y* (Lane), "to meet", the root of *liqāʾ*, a meeting, as in the taught *ilā l-liqāʾ*. Form IV *alqā* is "to cast a thing so that it meets the ground", and words cast to listeners.
