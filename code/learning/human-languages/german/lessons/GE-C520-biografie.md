## You'll want to know: die Biografie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C520-BUECHER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Biografie** — "biography".

> Sie liest eine Biografie über eine berühmte Sängerin.
>
> *She is reading a biography of a famous singer.*

Plural: *die Biografien*. Also spelt *die Biographie*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Biografie* is a learned word built from Greek *bíos* "life" and *gráphein* "to write"; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
