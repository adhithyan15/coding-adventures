# The opening five, again — three jobs

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C01-NAMASKARAM-01, TE-LEX-C01-SARE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Close the book on everything so far. From memory: the word that opens
a conversation, and the word that closes a piece of business.

## You'll want to know: sort them by the job, not by the order they arrived
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C01-NAMASKARAM-01, TE-LEX-C01-DHANYAVADAMULU-01, TE-LEX-C01-AVUNU-01, TE-LEX-C01-LEDU-01, TE-LEX-C01-SARE-01] -->

This far on, the question is no longer *what does each word mean* but
*which one do I reach for*. Three jobs cover all five:

| job | the word |
|---|---|
| open or close the whole exchange | **నమస్కారం** *namaskāram* |
| answer a question | **అవును** *avunu* · **లేదు** *lēdu* |
| close one turn and move on | **సరే** *sarē* · **ధన్యవాదములు** *dhanyavādamulu* |

The second row is the one to feel: **అవును** and **లేదు** are the two ways a
Telugu answer can go.

## What you've built — what each word was carrying
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C01-DHANYAVADAMULU-03, TE-GRAMMAR-C01-AVUNU-03, TE-GRAMMAR-C01-LEDU-03, TE-GRAMMAR-C01-SARE-03, TE-PRAGMATICS-C01-NAMASKARAM-03] -->

Each of the five arrived with a point attached. Recall the point, not the
sentence it came in:

- **నమస్కారం** — hello **and** goodbye in one word, respectful without being stiff.
- **ధన్యవాదములు** — its *-mulu* is an ending glued to a stem, which is how
  Telugu does most of its grammar.
- **అవును** — a small statement of being, "it is so," not a bare particle.
- **లేదు** — one of **two** negatives: *lēdu* denies that a thing is there,
  **కాదు** *kādu* that it is the thing you named.
- **సరే** — one Dravidian word wearing three scripts.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C01-NAMASKARAM-01, TE-PRAGMATICS-C01-NAMASKARAM-03, TE-LEX-C01-DHANYAVADAMULU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C01-DHANYAVADAMULU-03, TE-LEX-C01-AVUNU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C01-AVUNU-03, TE-LEX-C01-LEDU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C01-LEDU-03, TE-LEX-C01-SARE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C01-SARE-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which word does both hello and goodbye? (**నమస్కారం**.) Which two
negatives does Telugu keep apart? (**లేదు**, *not there*; **కాదు**, *not
that*.) What is the *-mulu* an example of? (**An ending glued onto a stem**.)
Which of the five would a Kannada speaker know on sight? (**సరే**.)
