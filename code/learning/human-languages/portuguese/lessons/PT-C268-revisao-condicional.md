# Review — if this, then that would

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-SERIA-01, PT-GRAMMAR-CONDICIONAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "it would be difficult". (*Seria difícil.*) Then "I could".
(*Poderia.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-GRAMMAR-SE-PRESENTE-01, PT-LEX-DEPENDE-01, PT-GRAMMAR-DEPENDE-DE-02, PT-GRAMMAR-CONDICIONAL-01, PT-GRAMMAR-SERIA-BOM-02, PT-LEX-C75-CIDADE3-02] -->

Two chapters, and a whole way of talking about what has not happened yet. A
real branch: *se chove, não saio*. A branch not yet named: *depende*, or
*depende de ti*. And the softened result, in the condicional: *poderia*,
*deveria*, *seria*.

> — *Vamos ao parque amanhã?*
> — *Depende. Seria bom, mas se chover, poderíamos ir ao museu.*

[PAUSE 2s] *Vamos* is "shall we go", from *ir*, and *poderíamos* is "we
could", the *nós* form of *poderia*: a soft suggestion, which is why it can
follow *se chover*. And listen to *se chover*: one occasion
still to come, so the special form you met last chapter.

condicional? (***Poderíamos ir.***) Which form does the *se* half take, and why?
(***Chover*** — one occasion still to come.) What would "we would be" be, from
*seria*? (***Seríamos.***) And which two Latin pieces hide in the *dever* of
*deverias*? (***Dē*** and ***habēre***: to have from someone.)
