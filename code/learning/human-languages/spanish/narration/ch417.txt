Spanish, chapter 417: An Adult Swimming Course.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el curso — a path of learning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall aprender, to learn. A planned path for learning something is un curso, a course or class.

The word, taken apart.
Latin cursus meant a running or a course, from currere, to run. English course keeps the same idea of a route, while current is something running onward. A learning course is a route followed from lesson to lesson.

Grammar Lens: name the subject.
The noun is masculine: el curso, un curso. Use de to name its subject: un curso de natación, a swimming course.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: el curso]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: un curso]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: un curso de natación]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Course? (El curso.) A swimming course? (Un curso de natación.)
Source: DLE: curso.


Lesson 2 of 5.
nadar — move through the water.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall la piscina and un curso. What might a course at a pool teach? Nadar, to swim.

The word, taken apart.
Spanish nadar continues Latin natāre, to swim. English keeps the root in natatorium, a building with a swimming pool. The infinitive ends in -ar, like many familiar action words.

Grammar Lens: learn to swim.
After aprender, Spanish uses a before another verb: aprender a nadar, to learn to swim. The related noun is la natación, swimming, as in curso de natación.

Lesson 3 of 5.
adulto, adulta — fully grown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall un curso and nadar. If this class is for grown-ups, the notice says para adultos, for adults.

The word, taken apart.
Latin adultus meant grown up, from adolescere, to grow. English adult is the same word with its ending shortened. Spanish keeps the ending and matches gender and number: adulto, adulta, adultos, adultas.

Grammar Lens: say who it is for.
Use para for the intended group: un curso de natación para adultos. The plural adultos can describe a mixed group or a group of men.
