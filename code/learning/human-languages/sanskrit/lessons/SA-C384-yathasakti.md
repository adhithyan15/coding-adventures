## You'll want to know: यथाशक्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C384-UDYAMA384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**यथाशक्ति** — *yathāśakti* — "as far as one can".

> यदि भवान् यथाशक्ति यतते, तर्हि अलम्।
>
> *yadi bhavān yathāśakti yatate, tarhi alam.*
>
> If you try as hard as you can, that is enough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यथाशक्ति** (*yathāśakti*) is built from *yathā*, "as", and *śakti*, "power, ability"; MW gives *yathā-śakti* "according to power or ability, to the utmost of one's power" (MW).
