## You'll want to know: வம்சம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C327-MUNNOR327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**வம்சம்** — *vamcam* — "a dynasty; a family line".

> அவர் ஒரு பழைய வம்சத்தைச் சேர்ந்தவர்.
>
> *avar oru paḻaiya vamcattaic cērntavar.*
>
> He belongs to an old family line.

Also a royal line.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வம்சம்** (*vamcam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vaṃśa*, "bamboo", and so "family, lineage" (CDIAL 11175), a line that grows joint by joint.
