Marathi, chapter 347: For and Against.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विरोध करणे (virodh karṇe) — to oppose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to verify, then the Marathi for to reject.

You'll want to know: विरोध करणे.
विरोध करणे — virodh karṇe — "to oppose".
लोकांनी नव्या रस्त्याला विरोध केला. lokānnī navyā rastyālā virodh kelā. People opposed the new road.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने विरोध केला, tyāne virodh kelā.
What is opposed takes -ला: रस्त्याला विरोध करणे (virodh karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
विरोध करणे (virodh karṇe) joins the tatsama विरोध, Sanskrit virodha, "opposition, hindrance" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".

Lesson 2 of 5.
पाठिंबा देणे (pāṭhimbā deṇe) — to support; to back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to reject, then the Marathi for to oppose.

You'll want to know: पाठिंबा देणे.
पाठिंबा देणे — pāṭhimbā deṇe — "to support; to back".
माझ्या मित्रांनी मला पूर्ण पाठिंबा दिला. mājhyā mitrānnī malā pūrṇa pāṭhimbā dilā. My friends gave me full support.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाठिंबा दिला, tyāne pāṭhimbā dilā.
The person backed takes -ला: मला पाठिंबा दिला.

The word, taken apart.
पाठिंबा देणे (pāṭhimbā deṇe) joins पाठिंबा, "backing", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to देणे, "to give".

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: समर्थन करणे.
समर्थन करणे — samarthan karṇe — "to justify; to defend (a view)".
तो नेहमी आपल्या चुकीचं समर्थन करतो. to nehmī āpalyā cukīcaṁ samarthan karto. He always justifies his mistakes.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने समर्थन केलं, tyāne samarthan kelaṁ.
The view defended takes -चं: निर्णयाचं समर्थन करणे (samarthan karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
समर्थन करणे (samarthan karṇe) joins the tatsama समर्थन, Sanskrit samarthana, "establishing, supporting an argument" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".

Lesson 4 of 5.
तटस्थ (taṭastha) — neutral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to support, then the Marathi for to justify.

You'll want to know: तटस्थ.
तटस्थ — taṭastha — "neutral".
या वादात मी तटस्थ राहणार आहे. yā vādāt mī taṭastha rāhṇār āhe. I am going to stay neutral in this dispute.
Invariable: तो तटस्थ (taṭastha) आहे, ती तटस्थ (taṭastha) आहे.

The word, taken apart.
तटस्थ (taṭastha) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit taṭastha, "standing on the bank; standing aloof" (Monier-Williams). One who watches from the bank (taṭa) takes no side.
