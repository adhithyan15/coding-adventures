## You'll want to know: hánlěng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C515-SEASONS515-01]; assesses=[] -->

*hánlěng* — "bitterly cold (of a climate)".

> *zhèr de dōngtiān bǐ Běijīng hánlěng* — Winters here are colder than in Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hánlěng* is built from *hán* and 冷 'cold', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *han* and *lengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'cold, as against heat'; it lists the second three times, *leng*, *laengX* and *lengX*, the last two glossed 'cold'. Two words for cold, put together, make it bitter.
