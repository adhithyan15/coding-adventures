## You'll want to know: بوجھ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C446-WORRY446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بوجھ** — *bojh* — "a burden; a load".

> قرض کا بوجھ اس کے دل پر ہے۔
>
> *qarz kā bojh us ke dil par hai.*
>
> The burden of the debt weighs on his mind.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بوجھ** (*bojh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vahyá*, "something to be carried" (CDIAL 11465).
