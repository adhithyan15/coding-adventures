Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 152: He and That.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
वो (vo) — he, the man you talk about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say how you are: हूं ठीक हूं। (hū̃ ṭhīk hū̃.) Then ask the polite question: आप कैसो हो? (āp kaiso ho?)
So far you talk about yourself, हूं, and to the person in front of you, आप. Now a third person: someone you talk about.

Grammar Lens: वो.
वो राम है। vo rām hai. — He is Ram.
वो (vo) is he: a man you are talking about, not to. He comes first, and है ends the sentence, as it does in म्हारो नाम राम है।.
वो बीमार है। vo bīmār hai. — He is ill.
Old Marwari already had vo, "that": CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit asau, "that" (CDIAL 972). This book keeps वो for a man, and names a woman by a word you know: मां, बहन, छोरी.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: वो छोरो है। — vo chhoro hai. — He is a boy.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: वो बीमार है पण हूं ठीक हूं। — vo bīmār hai paṇ hū̃ ṭhīk hū̃. — He is ill, but I am fine.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं राजी हूं क्यूंकै वो अठै है। — hū̃ rājī hū̃ kyū̃kai vo aṭhai hai. — I am happy because he is here.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Who is वो (vo)? (A man you are talking about.) Which word ends वो (vo) बीमार है।? (है.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say that he is ill, but you are fine.


Lesson 2 of 3.
यो and वो (vo) — this one here, that one there.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say he is a boy. Then the near word you know, यो (yo), "this". And offer the old choice once more: चाय अर पाणी? (chāy ar pāṇī?)

Grammar Lens: वो points away.
वो (vo) also points at a thing away from you: that. यो is the near one, this.
यो म्हारो घर है। yo mhāro ghar hai. — This is my house.
वो दुकान है। vo dukān hai. — That is a shop.
So वो (vo) is he or that: a man, or a thing over there.

Guided Practice.
Offer two things the way you offered tea or water: यो अर वो? (yo ar vo?), this or that?
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: यो पाणी है। — yo pāṇī hai. — This is water.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: वो चाय है। — vo chāy hai. — That is tea.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: यो अर वो? — yo ar vo? — This or that?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: वो। — vo. — That one.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which is near, यो or वो (vo)? (यो.) What else can वो (vo) be? (He.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Offer someone a choice: this or that?


Lesson 3 of 3.
He and that — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say he is ill.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: he is Ram; he is a boy; he is here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: this is my house; that is a shop; this or that?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: he is ill, but I am fine]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I am happy because he is here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tea or water?, and answer with one of them]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
He is here? (वो (vo) अठै है।) That is a shop? (वो (vo) दुकान है।) Tea or water? (चाय अर पाणी?) He is ill, but I am fine? (वो (vo) बीमार है पण हूं ठीक हूं।).
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say: that is a shop, but this is my house.
