Persian, chapter 359: Finishing the dish.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
زعفران (za'farân) — saffron.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be well cooked, then the Persian for a slotted spoon.

You'll want to know: زعفران.
زعفران (za'farân) — "saffron".
زعفران (za'farân) به برنج رنگ زرد میدهد. Za'farân be berenj rang-e zard midahad. Saffron gives rice a yellow colour.
It is brewed first in a little hot water, then poured over the rice.

The word, taken apart.
za'farân is borrowed from Arabic za'farān, "saffron" (EtymDB), and EtymDB links the Arabic word in turn with a Persian zarparān, "with golden leaves".

Lesson 2 of 7.
اضافه کردن (ezâfe kardan) — to add.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a slotted spoon, then the Persian for saffron.

You'll want to know: اضافه کردن.
اضافه کردن (ezâfe kardan) — "to add".
در آخر کمی نمک اضافه کرد. Dar âkhar kami namak ezâfe kard. At the end she added a little salt.
With be: be khoresh ezâfe kard. Ezâfe is also the name of the Persian linking -e.

The word, taken apart.
ezâfe is borrowed from Arabic iḍāfa, "an adding, an adjunct" (EtymDB). kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 7.
صاف کردن (sâf kardan) — to strain, to filter; to smooth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for saffron, then the Persian for to add.

You'll want to know: صاف کردن.
صاف کردن (sâf kardan) — "to strain, to filter; to smooth".
چای را صاف کرد و در لیوان ریخت. Chây râ sâf kard va dar livân rikht. She strained the tea and poured it into a glass.
Also "to smooth, to straighten". Present sâf mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
sâf "clear, smooth" is borrowed from Arabic ṣāfī, "pure" (EtymDB). kardan "to do" is inherited Persian: to make clear.

Lesson 4 of 7.
برشته (bereshte) — toasted, crisp and golden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to add, then the Persian for to strain.

You'll want to know: برشته.
برشته (bereshte) — "toasted, crisp and golden".
نان برشته بوی خوبی داشت. Nân-e bereshte bu-ye khubi dâsht. The toasted bread smelled good.
From bereshtan "to roast". Qahve-ye bereshte "roasted coffee".

The word, taken apart.
bereshte is the participle of the native verb bereshtan "to roast, to parch"; EtymDB lists no entry.

طعم (ta'm) این خورش خیلی خوب است. Ta'm-e in khoresh kheyli khub ast. This stew tastes very good.
