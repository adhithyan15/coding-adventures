Italian, chapter 525: In libreria.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il libraio — bookseller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for twist, then the Italian for ending.

You'll want to know: il libraio.
il libraio — "bookseller".
Il libraio mi ha consigliato un romanzo giallo. The bookseller recommended a crime novel to me.
La libreria is the bookshop; la biblioteca is the library.

The word, taken apart.
libraio is built on libro "book", inherited from Latin liber "the inner bark of a tree, a book" (Lewis & Short).

Ho perso il segnalibro e non so a che pagina ero. I lost my bookmark and do not know what page I was on.
From segnare and libro.

segnalibro is built from segna "marks", from Latin signāre (EtymDB), and libro "book", from Latin liber (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
il tascabile — paperback; pocket-sized.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to leaf through, then the Italian for bookmark.

You'll want to know: il tascabile.
il tascabile — "paperback; pocket-sized".
Compro i tascabili perché costano meno. I buy paperbacks because they cost less.
As an adjective: un'edizione tascabile.

The word, taken apart.
tascabile is built on tasca "pocket", which EtymDB traces to Frankish taska "pouch"; others start from Late Latin tasca, so the origin is not fully settled.
