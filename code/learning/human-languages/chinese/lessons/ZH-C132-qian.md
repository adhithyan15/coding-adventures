## You'll want to know: 千
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C132-BIG132-01]; assesses=[] -->

**千** — *qiān* — "thousand".

> **我们大学有三千个学生** *wǒmen dàxué yǒu sānqiān ge xuésheng* — Our university has three thousand students.

The new character, in pieces: 千 — a short fall over 十. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**千** (*qiān*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *tshen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'ten hundreds'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for counting.
