## You'll want to know: accadere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C275-ACCADERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**accadere** — "to happen".

> È accaduto tutto in pochi secondi.
>
> *It all happened in a few seconds.*

Takes *essere*: *è accaduto*. A little more formal than *succedere*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accadere* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *accadĕre*, a remake of Latin *accidere* "to fall upon, to happen", from *ad* and *cadere* "to fall" (Lewis & Short).
