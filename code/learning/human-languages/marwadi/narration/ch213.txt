Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 213: When Prices Come Down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मोल (mol) — the cost; worth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for double, then the Marwadi for a chhatak.

You'll want to know: मोल.
मोल — mol — "the cost; worth".
मोल पूछो, पछै खरीदो। mol pūchho, pachhai kharīdo. Ask the cost, then buy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मोल (mol) is inherited from Sanskrit maulya, "price"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari mola, "price" (CDIAL 10373).

Lesson 2 of 5.
घटणो (ghaṭṇo) — to decrease; to come down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a chhatak, then the Marwadi for the cost.

You'll want to know: घटणो.
घटणो — ghaṭṇo — "to decrease; to come down".
आजकाल भाव घटै है। ājkāl bhāv ghaṭai hai. Prices are coming down these days.
Past (it …): वो भाव घट्यो, vo bhāv ghaṭyo.

The word, taken apart.
घटणो (ghaṭṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ghaṭṭati, "decreases"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb ghaṭai, "decreases" (CDIAL 4415).

Lesson 3 of 5.
घटावणो (ghaṭāvṇo) — to lower (a price).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the cost, then the Marwadi for to decrease.

You'll want to know: घटावणो.
घटावणो — ghaṭāvṇo — "to lower (a price)".
आप भाव थोड़ु घटावो। āp bhāv thoṛu ghaṭāvo. Please lower the price a little.
Past (I …): म्हैं भाव घटायो, mhaĩ bhāv ghaṭāyo.

The word, taken apart.
घटावणो (ghaṭāvṇo) is the causative of घटणो (ghaṭṇo), "to decrease", inherited from the same reconstructed ghaṭṭati; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ghaṭāvai, which it glosses "belittles" (CDIAL 4415).

Lesson 5 of 5.
बिकणो (bikṇo) — to be sold; to sell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to lower, then the Marwadi for free of charge.

You'll want to know: बिकणो.
बिकणो — bikṇo — "to be sold; to sell".
ये घी जल्दी बिकै है। ye ghī jaldī bikai hai. This ghee sells quickly.
Past (it …): वो घी बिक्यो, vo ghī bikyo.

The word, taken apart.
बिकणो (bikṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vikrīyate, "is sold". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi biknā, "to be sold" (CDIAL 11642).
