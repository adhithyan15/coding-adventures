## You'll want to know: शुल्कस्थानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C426-VIDESHA426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शुल्कस्थानम्** — *śulkasthānam* — "a customs house".

> सीमान्ते शुल्कस्थानम् अस्ति।
>
> *sīmānte śulkasthānam asti.*
>
> There is a customs house at the border.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शुल्कस्थानम्** (*śulkasthānam*) is built from *śulka*, "a toll, a duty", and *sthāna*, "a place"; MW gives the compound as a toll-house, a tax office, a custom house (MW).
