## You'll want to know: નિવેડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C538-SANSAY538-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નિવેડો** — *niveḍo* — "a settlement (of a matter)".

> જો આપણે બેસીને વાત કરીશું તો નિવેડો આવશે.
>
> *jo āpaṇe besīne vāt karīśũ to niveḍo āvśe.*
>
> If we sit down and talk, the matter will be settled.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિવેડો** (*niveḍo*) is how a matter ends once it is decided; a settlement "comes": **નિવેડો આવશે**. CDIAL records the Gujarati noun, "a settlement, a decision", under a reconstructed *\*nirvarati*, "restrains" (CDIAL 7392).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
