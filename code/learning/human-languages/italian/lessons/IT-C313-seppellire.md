## You'll want to know: seppellire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C313-LUTTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**seppellire** — "to bury".

> Lo hanno sepolto nel paese dove era nato.
>
> *They buried him in the village where he was born.*

Past participle *sepolto*, also *seppellito*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seppellire* is inherited from Latin *sepelīre* "to bury" (Lewis & Short).
