## You'll want to know: おがわ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C411-OGAWA411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おがわ** — *ogawa* — "a stream; a brook".

> **おがわで こどもが あそんでいます。** — *ogawa de kodomo ga asonde imasu* — "Children are playing in the stream."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おがわ** (*ogawa*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *o*, "small" and *gawa*, "river" (from *kawa*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
