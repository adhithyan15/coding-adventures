Telugu, chapter 224: Ladle, Rubbish, Floor, Mattress, Flour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
గరిటె (gariṭe) — a ladle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for messy, then the Telugu for rude.

You'll want to know: గరిటె.
గరిటె — gariṭe — "a ladle".

The word, taken apart.
గరిటె (gariṭe) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1267 lists a nasalised gariṭe, "spoon, ladle", with Tamil karaṇṭi and Malayalam karaṇṭi, "spoon".
