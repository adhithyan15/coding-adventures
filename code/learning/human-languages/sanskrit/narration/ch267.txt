Sanskrit, chapter 267: One small school-day plan.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.

Lesson 2 of 6.
Build one bounded school plan — ask when an exam will be, answer, and learn two complete schoolwork frames.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask the familiar market question and give its short rupee answer. (Mūlyaṃ kiyat? Viṃśatiḥ rūpyakāṇi.)

Grammar Lens: four complete turns.
Put four known pieces together:
परीक्षा कदा भविष्यति? Parīkṣā kadā bhaviṣyati? When will the exam be?
Answer from the smallest possible timetable:
श्वः परीक्षा भविष्यति। Śvaḥ parīkṣā bhaviṣyati. The exam will be tomorrow.
This is one useful school-time pattern. It does not teach a new future paradigm or a complete timetable system. Complete the bounded plan with two whole schoolwork frames:
गृहकार्यं करिष्यामि। Gṛhakāryaṃ kariṣyāmi. I will do the homework.
पाठं पठिष्यामि। Pāṭhaṃ paṭhiṣyāmi. I will read or study the lesson.
Own those two lines as complete frames. In particular, पाठं belongs to the whole second frame here; do not use it to infer a masculine accusative rule.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: ask when the exam will be]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: answer that it will be tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the complete homework frame]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the complete study frame]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask and answer the exam-time turn, then say both complete schoolwork frames from memory.


Lesson 3 of 6.
Retrieve your homework and study plan — reuse two complete first-person future forms with familiar schoolwork.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say “a lesson,” then “homework.” (Pāṭhaḥ; gṛhakāryam.)

Grammar Lens: two complete owned frames.
Retrieve the two whole frames introduced in the preceding lesson:
गृहकार्यं करिष्यामि। Gṛhakāryaṃ kariṣyāmi. I will do the homework.
पाठं पठिष्यामि। Pāṭhaṃ paṭhiṣyāmi. I will read or study the lesson.
The school-plan atom owns both complete lines. This lesson does not derive पाठं from पाठः, generalise a masculine accusative rule, or add a new future form.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: “I will do the homework”]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: “I will study the lesson”]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: both plans after the exam-time answer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
State the homework plan and the study plan without looking.


Lesson 4 of 6.
Join the pieces into one school-day plan — join school vocabulary, one timetable turn, and two study plans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say “a fort,” then “I will do.” (Durgam; kariṣyāmi.)

Guided Practice.
First name the setting and its useful records: विद्यालयः, school; कक्षा, classroom; समयसारणी, timetable; अंकः, mark; उत्तरम्, answer. Name the people: छात्रः, student; अध्यापकः, teacher.
Then say the working plan:
परीक्षा कदा भविष्यति? — When will the exam be?
श्वः परीक्षा भविष्यति। — The exam will be tomorrow.
गृहकार्यं करिष्यामि। — I will do the homework.
पाठं पठिष्यामि। — I will study the lesson.

What you've built.
The nouns were already yours. Now a timetable question, answer, and two plans hold them together without adding a new paradigm.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the question, the answer, the homework plan, and the study plan.


Lesson 5 of 6.
Independent school plan: round one — independently retrieve one timetable answer and two schoolwork plans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say “a palace,” then “I will read or study.” (Prāsādaḥ; paṭhiṣyāmi.)

Guided Practice: answer before the check.
No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.
[pause 12 seconds]
You are a student reading a school timetable. Ask when the exam will be. Answer that it will be tomorrow. State that you will do the homework and study the lesson. Then name the teacher, classroom, mark, and answer words that belong to this school setting.

What you've built: check after speaking.
A complete response has four connected turns and the setting words. One check is परीक्षा कदा भविष्यति? श्वः परीक्षा भविष्यति। गृहकार्यं करिष्यामि। पाठं पठिष्यामि। Then recall विद्यालयः, छात्रः, अध्यापकः, समयसारणी, कक्षा, अंकः, उत्तरम्.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What held the old school words together? (One timetable question, its answer, and two first-person plans.)


Lesson 6 of 6.
Independent school plan: round two — independently retrieve a second school-day plan without a visible model.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say “a hut,” then make one familiar near-term first-person plan. (Kuṭīram; use the taught śvaḥ/paraśvaḥ plan frame.)

Guided Practice: a fresh prompt.
No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.
[pause 12 seconds]
Imagine that a teacher points to the timetable in a classroom. Ask when the exam will be and answer that it will be tomorrow. Say what homework you will do and what lesson you will study. Finish by recalling the school, student, teacher, mark, and answer words.

What you've built: boundary.
You can now complete one small A1 school-day plan with familiar language. This is not complete classroom discourse, school-system, grading, educational rights, or future-paradigm coverage, and it is not evidence of exam readiness, human validation, or learner success.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Name the four jobs: read the timetable, ask and answer the exam time, state the homework plan, and state the study plan.
