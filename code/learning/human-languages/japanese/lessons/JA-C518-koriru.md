## You'll want to know: こりる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C518-REGRET518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こりる** — *koriru* — "to learn one's lesson".

> **いちど けがを したら、おとうとも こりるでしょう。** — *ichido kega o shitara, otōto mo koriru deshō* — "Once he gets hurt, my younger brother will learn his lesson too."

Polite **こります**, *korimasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **おとうとが こりました。**, *otōto ga korimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こりる** (*koriru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
