## You'll want to know: proibire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C385-VIETATO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**proibire** — "to ban, to prohibit".

> Il comune ha proibito le macchine in centro.
>
> *The town council has banned cars in the centre.*

Conjugated with *-isc-*: *proibisco*. Stronger and more official than *vietare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*proibire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prohibēre* "to hold back, to forbid" (Lewis & Short), built from *prō* "before" and *habēre* "to hold".
