Lesson 1 of 7.
感觉 (gǎnjué) — to feel; a feeling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

[your turn — recall: say 我的车票不见了 — wǒ de chēpiào bú jiàn le — R1, the chapter you have finished]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say gǎndào, to feel — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 开心 — kāixīn, happy — R3]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 感觉.
感觉 — gǎnjué — "to feel", how something seems to you; "a feeling".
我感觉很好 wǒ gǎnjué hěn hǎo — I feel fine.
wǒ gǎnjué tā bù gāoxìng — I have a feeling he's not happy.
The next lesson puts the first character in your hand. The second is the 觉 of juéde.

The word, taken apart.
感觉 (gǎnjué) is built from 感 (gǎn) and 觉, both inherited from Middle Chinese. 感 (gǎn) is komX, glossed 'to move, to stir'. The Guangyun lists 觉 under two readings: kaewH, glossed as sleep, and kaewk, glossed 'to understand' and 'to wake'; jué is the regular modern sound of kaewk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). 'To feel' for the pair is the word's own sense.

What you've built.
gǎndào and 感觉 both report a feeling; 感觉 is also how something seems to you: 我感觉很好 (wǒ gǎnjué hěn hǎo).
juéde, as in 我觉得, is what you think about it.

Script — a frame with 口 inside, then 心.
感 (gǎn).
Two pieces: a frame with 口 inside it, then 心.
The frame, nine strokes: a long horizontal at the top; a left-falling stroke down the left side; a short horizontal under the top; 口, three strokes, small, under that; then the long slanting hook down to the lower right, as in 我; a short left-falling stroke across it; and last the dot at the top right.
心, four strokes, at the foot: the left dot, the curve along the foot that hooks up, the middle dot, the right dot.
9 + 4 = thirteen.

[pause 3 seconds]

Grammar Lens: 我感觉 and a feeling.
我感觉 and a feeling word says how it felt; the feeling words are ones you know.
那天我感觉很开心 nà tiān wǒ gǎnjué hěn kāixīn — I felt really happy that day. shàngkè de shíhou wǒ gǎnjué hěn jǐnzhāng — I felt nervous in class.
The feeling takes 很, as it does after 我: 我感觉很开心. Any feeling you know fits: gāoxìng, nánguò, jǐnzhāng, jīdòng, shīwàng.
感觉 is for how it felt; 觉得 is for what you think. 我觉得他说得对 is an opinion; 我感觉很好 (wǒ gǎnjué hěn hǎo) is a feeling.

Grammar Lens: the length after the verb.
How long something lasted goes after the verb and its 了; with an object, the object comes after the length.
我学了两年汉语 wǒ xué le liǎng nián Hànyǔ — I studied Chinese for two years. tā zài Zhōngguó zhù le yì nián — He lived in China for a year.
When it happened goes before the verb: 去年. How long it lasted goes after: 两年.
To ask, put 多 before 长, as you put it before 大 in 多大: 你学了多长时间 nǐ xué le duō cháng shíjiān?

You'll want to know: duōjiǔ.
duōjiǔ — "how long", of time.
nǐ xué le duōjiǔ — How long did you study?
nǐ zài Zhōngguó zhù le duōjiǔ — How long did you live in China?
Its second character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
duōjiǔ is built from 多 'much' and jiǔ 'long-lasting', both inherited from Middle Chinese, ta and kjuwX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'many' and the second 'lasting long'.

What you've built.
duōjiǔ is the short form of 多长时间, and it takes the same place, after the verb.
hǎojiǔ answers it: a long time.

You'll want to know: yíhàn.
yíhàn — "a pity; regrettable".
tài yíhàn le — What a pity!
wǒ méi qù, hěn yíhàn — I didn't go, which is a shame.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yíhàn is built from yí 'to leave behind' and hàn 'resentment', both inherited from Middle Chinese. The Guangyun lists the first under two readings, ywij, glossed 'to lose' and 'to leave', and ywijH, glossed 'to give'; the second is homH, glossed 'resentment' (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). 'A pity' for the pair is the modern word's own sense.

What you've built.
hòuhuǐ is regretting what you did yourself; yíhàn is a pity, whoever was to blame.
It takes 很 or 太 … 了, as a feeling does: tài yíhàn le.

The exchange.
A: 你去过中国吗 nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma — Have you been to China? B: 去过 我在中国学了一年汉语 qù guo, wǒ zài Zhōngguó xué le yì nián Hànyǔ — I have. I studied Chinese there for a year. A: nǐ gǎnjué zěnmeyàng — How did it feel? B: 我感觉很好 wǒ gǎnjué hěn hǎo — It felt good. A: nǐ zài Zhōngguó zhù le duōjiǔ — How long did you live there? B: 一年 yì nián — A year. A: 你想再去吗 nǐ xiǎng zài qù ma — Do you want to go again? B: xiǎng, méiyǒu shíjiān, tài yíhàn le — I do, but there's no time. What a pity.
A asks with 过, then with 感觉 (gǎnjué), then with duōjiǔ; B answers each in as few words as will do: 去过, 一年.
