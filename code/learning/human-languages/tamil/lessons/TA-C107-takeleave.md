## You'll want to know: விடைபெறுகிறேன்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**விடைபெறுகிறேன்** (*viṭaipeṟukiṟēṉ*) — "I take my leave", literally "I receive leave".

**விடை** is "leave, permission". The formal goodbye.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A formal way out of a room.
