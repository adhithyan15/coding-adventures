## You'll want to know: fāyán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C240-SICK240-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fāyán* — "to become inflamed".

> *wǒ de sǎngzi fāyán le* — My throat is inflamed.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fāyán* is built from *fā* 'to start' and *yán* 'flame', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjot* and *hjem* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'heat' and explains it, after an older book, as fire rising.
