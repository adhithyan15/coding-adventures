## You'll want to know: fastīdiō, fastīdīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C494-ODI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**fastīdiō, fastīdīre** — "to turn up one's nose at, feel disgust at".

> Puer holera fastīdit.
>
> *The boy turns up his nose at vegetables.*

*fastīdiō, fastīdīre, fastīdīvī, fastīdītum*, with the accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fastīdiō** is built on *fastīdium* "loathing" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
