## You'll want to know: ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C432-HATTIKKU432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕು** — *hattikku* — "to suppress; to stifle".

> ಅವಳು ತನ್ನ ಕೋಪವನ್ನು ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕಿದಳು.
>
> *avaḷu tanna kōpavannu hattikkidaḷu.*
>
> She suppressed her anger.

Past (I …): ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕಿದೆ, *hattikkide*.

Of feelings and of protests.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕು** (*hattikku*) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled: DEDR does not list it.
