## You'll want to know: پیشگیری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C377-BOHRAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پیشگیری** (*pishgiri*) — "prevention".

> پیشگیری بهتر از درمان است.
>
> *pishgiri behtar az darmân ast.*
>
> Prevention is better than cure.

*Pish* "before" + *giri* "taking". *Pishgiri kardan* — to prevent (an illness).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیشگیری** is a native Persian compound built from *pish* "before" + *giri* "taking", built on *gir-*, the present stem of *gereftan* "to take": taking hold of a problem before it comes.
