## You'll want to know: निषेधति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C373-ANUMATI373-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निषेधति** — *niṣedhati* — "forbids".

> यदि वैद्यः निषेधति, तर्हि सः मिष्टान्नं न खादति।
>
> *yadi vaidyaḥ niṣedhati, tarhi saḥ miṣṭānnaṃ na khādati.*
>
> If the doctor forbids it, he does not eat sweets.

Past (he …): सः मिष्टान्नं निषिद्धवान्, *saḥ miṣṭānnaṃ niṣiddhavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निषेधति** (*niṣedhati*) is built on *ni* and the root √sidh, "to keep off"; MW gives *ni-√ṣidh* "to keep off, to prevent, to prohibit" and *ni-ṣiddha* "forbidden" (MW).
