Kannada, chapter 99: The Body Again.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉಸಿರು (usiru) — breath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for alarm, then the Kannada for peace of mind.

You'll want to know: ಉಸಿರು.
ಉಸಿರು (usiru) — "breath".
ಉಸಿರು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ (usiru tegedukoḷḷi) — "take a breath", with ತೆಗೆದುಕೊ, "take", as in ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ.

What you've built.
What the body does all day.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: usiru]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: usiru, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say usiru tegedukoḷḷi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for alarm, then the Kannada for peace of mind, then say usiru again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಉಸಿರು (usiru) mean? ("Breath".) Say it once more.
