# Hello and goodbye, the everyday way — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C91-GREET-01, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-02, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You meet a neighbour you have not seen in a long time, around lunch. Say three things.

## Grammar Lens: the ending -కి, "to"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-03, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-04] -->

| word | built from |
|---|---|
| **ఇంటికి** | house + -కి |
| **ఎక్కడికి?** | where + -కి |

The same **-కి** as **నాకు**: a direction, to a place or to a person.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-01, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-02, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-03, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-04, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-05, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-01, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-02, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-03, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-04, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: ask a friend what's new, then ask where they are going]
- [YOU SAY: answer that you are going home]
- [YOU SAY: at the door, tell your host to take care and to come soon]
- [YOU SAY: take your leave formally, then hurry your group along]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-01, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-02, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-03, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-04, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-05, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-01, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-02, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-03, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-04, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Are you well? (**బాగున్నారా?**) Long time? (**చాలా రోజులైంది**.) What's new?
(**ఏంటి సంగతి?**) Where to? (**ఎక్కడికి?**) Have you eaten? (**భోంచేశారా?**) Take care?
(**జాగ్రత్త**.) Soon? (**త్వరగా**.) Home? (**ఇంటికి**.) Leave? (**సెలవు**.) Let's go? (**పదండి**.)
