Japanese, chapter 206: Traffic, Passengers and Bookings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
のりもの (norimono) — a vehicle; a means of transport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for one way, then the Japanese for a round trip.

You'll want to know: のりもの.
のりもの — norimono — "a vehicle; a means of transport".
でんしゃや ふねは のりものです。 — densha ya fune wa norimono desu — "Trains and boats are vehicles."

The word, taken apart.
のりもの (norimono) is native Japanese (wago), built from nori, "riding", and mono, "thing", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict).

Lesson 2 of 5.
こうつう (kōtsū) — traffic; transport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a round trip, then the Japanese for a vehicle.

You'll want to know: こうつう.
こうつう — kōtsū — "traffic; transport".
この みちは こうつうが おおいです。 — kono michi wa kōtsū ga ōi desu — "This road has a lot of traffic."

The word, taken apart.
こうつう (kōtsū) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kō, "to cross, mix" (Middle Chinese kaew) and tsū, "to pass through" (thuwng) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
