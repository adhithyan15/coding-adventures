## You'll want to know: منقذ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C310-NAJDA310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**منقذ** — *munqidh* — "a rescuer; a lifeguard".

> سبح المنقذ نحو الطفل بسرعة.
>
> *sabaḥa al-munqidh naḥwa aṭ-ṭifl bi-surʿa.*
>
> The lifeguard swam quickly towards the child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منقذ** (*munqidh*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-dh* (Lane), the root of the taught *anqadha*, to rescue. *Munqidh* is its Form IV participle, "one who rescues".
