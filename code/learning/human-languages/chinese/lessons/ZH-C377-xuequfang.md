## You'll want to know: 学区房
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C377-AREA377-05]; assesses=[] -->

**学区房** — *xuéqūfáng* — "a home near a good school".

> **大家都想买学区房因为学校好** *dàjiā dōu xiǎng mǎi xuéqūfáng yīnwèi xuéxiào hǎo* — Everyone wants a flat near a good school, because the school is good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**学区房** (*xuéqūfáng*) is built from 学 'to study', 区 'an area' and 房 'a house', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *haewk*, *khju* and *bjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last 'a room'. A home inside a good school's catchment area is a recent use.
