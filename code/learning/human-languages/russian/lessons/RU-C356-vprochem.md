# впрочем (vpróchem) — anyway, mind you

## You'll want to know: впрочем
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C356-NESMOTRYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**впрочем** (*vpróchem*) — "anyway, mind you".

> Я не люблю мясо, впрочем, сегодня попробую.
>
> *Ya ne lyublyú myáso, vpróchem, segódnya popróbuyu.*
>
> I do not like meat; still, today I will try some.

It softens or partly takes back what was said: *Впрочем, это неважно* — anyway, it does not matter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**впрочем** is fused from *в* "in" and *прочем*, a form of *прочий* "other, the rest": as for the rest.
