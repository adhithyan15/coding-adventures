# 们 — the person piece, then a doorway

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C31-PEOPLE31-03, ZH-SCRIPT-REN-RADICAL-01, ZH-SKILL-READ-CHARACTERS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The word first, then the character inside it.

- [YOU RECALL: say *tāmen* — **R1**, one lesson back, before it decays]

> 他 你

[YOU READ: each one aloud, with nothing said first — *tā*, *nǐ*]

## Script — the person piece, then a doorway
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-MEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 们

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, 亻, two strokes**, exactly as in 他 and 你.

**The right piece, a doorway, three strokes.** A dot at the top left. A vertical down the left side. Then a horizontal across the top that turns down the right side and hooks in at the foot.

2 + 3 = five. The doorway is read mén, and alone it is the word for door; here it gives 们 its sound and loses its tone.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-MEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] How many pieces in 们? (**Two** — 亻, then the doorway.) Which stroke of the doorway comes first? (**The dot**.) Which other character opens with the same two strokes? (**他**, and 你.)
