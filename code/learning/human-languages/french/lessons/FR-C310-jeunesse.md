## You'll want to know: la jeunesse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C310-JEUNESSE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la jeunesse** — "youth, young people".

> Dans sa jeunesse, il jouait au rugby.
>
> *In his youth, he played rugby.*

Also young people as a group: *la jeunesse d'aujourd'hui*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jeunesse* is built on *jeune* "young", which is inherited from Latin *iuvenis* "young" (Lewis & Short).
