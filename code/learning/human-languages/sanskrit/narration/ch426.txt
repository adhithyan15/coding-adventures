Sanskrit, chapter 426: Abroad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विदेशः (videśaḥ) — a foreign country; abroad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a round window, then the Sanskrit for memorable.

You'll want to know: विदेशः.
विदेशः — videśaḥ — "a foreign country; abroad".
मम भ्राता विदेशे वसति। mama bhrātā videśe vasati. My brother lives abroad.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विदेशः (videśaḥ) is built from vi, "apart, away", and deśa, "a country"; MW gives videśa as another country, a foreign land, abroad (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अवतिष्ठते (avatiṣṭhate) — stays on; remains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for crowded, then the Sanskrit for a customs house.

You'll want to know: अवतिष्ठते.
अवतिष्ठते — avatiṣṭhate — "stays on; remains".
सः विदेशे एकं संवत्सरम् अवतिष्ठते। saḥ videśe ekaṃ saṃvatsaram avatiṣṭhate. He stays on abroad for a year.
Past (he …): सः विदेशे अवस्थितवान्, saḥ videśe avasthitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अवतिष्ठते (avatiṣṭhate) is built from ava, "down", and the root √sthā, "to stand", which also gives tiṣṭhati, "stands"; MW gives ava-sthā as to remain or stay, with the participle avasthita, and Pāṇini (1.3.22) gives √sthā the middle endings after ava (MW).
