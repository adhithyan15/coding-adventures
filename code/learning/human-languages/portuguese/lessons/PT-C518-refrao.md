## You'll want to know: o refrão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C518-CORO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o refrão** — "chorus, refrain".

> Toda a gente canta o refrão.
>
> *Everybody sings the chorus.*

The part of a song that comes back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*refrão* came through Spanish *refrán* from Old French *refrain* (EtymDB), built on a verb from Latin *frangere* "to break": the song breaks off and starts again.
