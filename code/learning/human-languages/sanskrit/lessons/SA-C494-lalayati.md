## You'll want to know: लालयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C494-LALANA494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लालयति** — *lālayati* — "pampers".

> पितामही पौत्रं लालयति।
>
> *pitāmahī pautraṃ lālayati.*
>
> Grandmother pampers her grandson.

Past (she …): सा पौत्रं लालितवती, *sā pautraṃ lālitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लालयति** (*lālayati*) is built on the causative of the root √lal, "to play, to frolic"; MW's ppp *lālita* is "caressed, fondled, indulged" (MW).
