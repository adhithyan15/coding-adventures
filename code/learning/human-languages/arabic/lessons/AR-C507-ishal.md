## You'll want to know: إسهال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C507-MARAD507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**إسهال** — *ishāl* — "diarrhoea".

> أعطى الطبيب الطفل دواء للإسهال.
>
> *aʿṭā aṭ-ṭabīb aṭ-ṭifl dawāʾ li-l-ishāl.*
>
> The doctor gave the child medicine for diarrhoea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إسهال** (*ishāl*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-h-l*, "to be smooth, easy" (Lane). Lane gives Form IV ashala, of a medicine, as to loosen the bowels; sahl, easy, is the same root.
