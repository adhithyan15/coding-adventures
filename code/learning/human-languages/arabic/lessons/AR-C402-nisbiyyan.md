## You'll want to know: نسبيا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C402-HATMAN402-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نسبيا** — *nisbiyyan* — "relatively".

> الشقة رخيصة نسبيا.
>
> *ash-shaqqa rakhīṣa nisbiyyan.*
>
> The flat is relatively cheap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نسبيا** (*nisbiyyan*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-s-b*, "to trace lineage, to relate" (Lane). *Nisbiyyan* is the adverb of *nisbī*, relative, from a noun meaning a relation.
