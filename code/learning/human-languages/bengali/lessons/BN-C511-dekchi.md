## You'll want to know: ডেকচি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C511-BASON511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ডেকচি** — *ḍekchi* — "a wide cooking pot".

> দিদিমা ডেকচিতে খিচুড়ি রান্না করলেন।
>
> *Didimā ḍekchite khichuṛi rānnā kôrlen.*
>
> My grandmother cooked khichuri in a big pot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ডেকচি** (*ḍekchi*) is usually traced to Persian *degcha*, "a small cauldron", a smaller form of *deg*, "a cauldron".
