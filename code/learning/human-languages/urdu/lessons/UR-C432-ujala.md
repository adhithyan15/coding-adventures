## You'll want to know: اجالا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C432-TULU432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اجالا** — *ujālā* — "daylight; the first light".

> صبح چھ بجے اجالا ہوتا ہے۔
>
> *subah chhe baje ujālā hotā hai.*
>
> It gets light at six in the morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجالا** (*ujālā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ujālā*, "bright", under *ujjvālaka*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1673).
