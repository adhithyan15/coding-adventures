## You'll want to know: buhen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C510-TRIBUENE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**buhen** — "to boo".

> Nach dem schlechten Spiel buhten die Zuschauer.
>
> *After the poor game the spectators booed.*

Regular: *hat gebuht*. From the cry *buh!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*buhen* is built on the cry *buh!*; EtymDB lists the verb without a source, and it is usually explained as imitative.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
