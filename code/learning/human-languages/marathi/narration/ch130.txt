Marathi, chapter 130: Courtyard, Door, Pillow, Bedsheet, Mattress.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
अंगण (aṅgaṇ) — a courtyard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a building, then the Marathi for a bathroom.

You'll want to know: अंगण.
अंगण — aṅgaṇ — "a courtyard".

Lesson 6 of 7.
Can, want and time — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a bedsheet and a mattress.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: to be able to, to manage to, a skill, ability, a right]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a means, an owner, strength, wanted, a wish]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a need, hope, a dream, to ask for, to decide]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a plan, dawn, midnight, the day after tomorrow, Monday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To be able to? (शकणे, śakṇe.) To manage to? (जमणे, jamṇe.) A skill? (कौशल्य, kauśalya.) Ability? (क्षमता, kṣamatā.) A right? (अधिकार, adhikār.) A means? (साधन, sādhan.) An owner? (मालक, mālak.) Strength? (ताकद, tākad.) Wanted? (हवा, havā.) A wish? (इच्छा, icchā.) A need? (गरज, garaj.) Hope? (आशा, āśā.) A dream? (स्वप्न, svapna.) To ask for? (मागणे, māgṇe.) To decide? (ठरवणे, ṭharavṇe.) A plan? (योजना, yojanā.) Dawn? (पहाट, pahāṭ.) Midnight? (मध्यरात्र, madhyarātra.) The day after tomorrow? (परवा, parvā.) Monday? (सोमवार, somvār.) Tuesday? (मंगळवार, maṅgaḷvār.) Wednesday? (बुधवार, budhvār.) Thursday? (गुरुवार, guruvār.) Friday? (शुक्रवार, śukravār.) Saturday? (शनिवार, śanivār.)
