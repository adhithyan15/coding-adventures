## You'll want to know: ആതിഥേയൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C386-ATHITHEYAN386-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ആതിഥേയൻ** — *ātithēyan* — "a host".

> ആതിഥേയൻ എല്ലാ അതിഥികളെയും വാതിൽക്കൽ സ്വാഗതം ചെയ്തു.
>
> *ātithēyan ellā atithikaḷeyuṁ vātilkkal svāgataṁ ceytu.*
>
> The host welcomed all the guests at the door.

From അതിഥി, a guest: the one who receives guests.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആതിഥേയൻ** (*ātithēyan*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ātitheya*, "proper for a guest, hospitable" (Monier-Williams), from *atithi*, "a guest", the source of അതിഥി (*atithi*).
