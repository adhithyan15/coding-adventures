## You'll want to know: 事先
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C317-LOOKBACK317-05]; assesses=[] -->

**事先** — *shìxiān* — "beforehand; in advance".

> **他又没有事先说我们都生气了** *tā yòu méiyǒu shìxiān shuō, wǒmen dōu shēngqì le* — Once again he didn't say so beforehand, and we were all cross.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**事先** (*shìxiān*) is built from 事 'a matter' and 先 'before', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzriH* and *sen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'first and after': before the matter.
