## You'll want to know: huānyíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C74-VISIT74-04]; assesses=[] -->

*huānyíng* — "welcome".

> *huānyíng nǐ lái wǒ jiā* — Welcome to my home!

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huānyíng* is built from huān 'glad' and yíng 'to go to meet', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwan* and *ngjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): glad to meet you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
