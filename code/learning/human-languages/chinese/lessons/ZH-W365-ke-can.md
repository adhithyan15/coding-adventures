## Script — a bar, a mouth and a hook
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-KE-CAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 可

**One piece, five strokes.** Count it in three parts.

**A long horizontal** across the top.

**口, three strokes**, small, under the bar on the left: a vertical, a horizontal that turns down, and the bottom horizontal.

**A long vertical down the right side**, from the bar to the foot, hooking up to the left.

1 + 3 + 1 = five.
