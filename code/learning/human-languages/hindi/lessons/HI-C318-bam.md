## You'll want to know: बम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C318-DHAMAKA318-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बम** — *bam* — "a bomb".

> पुलिस को स्टेशन के पास एक बम मिला।
>
> *pulis ko sṭeśan ke pās ek bam milā.*
>
> The police found a bomb near the station.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बम** (*bam*) is borrowed from English *bomb*, shortened to one Hindi syllable and masculine.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
