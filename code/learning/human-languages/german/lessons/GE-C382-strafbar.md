## You'll want to know: strafbar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C382-VERSTOSS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**strafbar** — "punishable, a criminal offence".

> Fahren ohne Führerschein ist strafbar.
>
> *Driving without a licence is a criminal offence.*

*Sich strafbar machen* — to commit an offence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*strafbar* is built with *-bar* "able to be" on *Strafe* "punishment", inherited from Middle High German *strāfe* (EtymDB).
