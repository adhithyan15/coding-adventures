# Review — comparing, and how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C314-AUSADHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, KA-LEX-C310-SVACCHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read **ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ** and **ಔಷಧ** aloud. (*svaccha*, *auṣadha*.) Name the letter each one taught you. (**ಛ**; **ಔ**.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C316-KINTA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C316-MOST-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C316-SPAN-INDA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C316-FROM-UNTIL-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-FEELING-DATIVE-01, KA-LEX-C10-VAARA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C314-BUY-TICKET-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: My town is smaller than Mysuru.]
- [YOU SAY: I like the train more than the bus. One ticket to Mysuru, please.]
- [YOU SAY: This is the biggest shop in our town.]
- [YOU SAY: I have been learning Kannada for two months, and I am glad.]
- [YOU SAY: I was in Mysuru from Monday until Friday.]
