## You'll want to know: kěyǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C90-KEYI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*kěyǐ* — "may; can (with permission)".

> *wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma* — May I sit here?

Two dips in a row: spoken, the first one rises, *kéyǐ*. It is written *kěyǐ*.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kěyǐ* is built from kě 'may' and yǐ 'to use', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaX* and *yiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to permit' and 'to use'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C90-HUI-01, ZH-LEX-C90-NENG-01] -->

会 learned, 能 able or allowed, *kěyǐ* permitted. Asking whether you may, *kěyǐ* is the polite one; the answer is *kěyǐ*, or *bù kěyǐ*.
