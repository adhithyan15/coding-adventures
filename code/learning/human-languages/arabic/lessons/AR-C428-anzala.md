## You'll want to know: أنزل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C428-SIKKA428-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أنزل** — *anzala* — "to unload; to bring down".

> أنزل أبي الأمتعة من القطار.
>
> *anzala abī al-amtiʿa min al-qiṭār.*
>
> My father unloaded the luggage from the train.

Present: ينزل, *yunzil*.

Unvocalized, its present ينزل looks like that of *nazala*, "to go down" (*yanzil*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أنزل** (*anzala*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-z-l*, "to come down, to alight" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to bring down".
