## You'll want to know: آهنگساز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C520-MUSICIANS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**آهنگساز** (*âhangsâz*) — "a composer".

> این آهنگساز موسیقی فیلم میسازد.
>
> *in âhangsâz musiqi-ye film misâzad.*
>
> This composer writes film music.

*âhang* 'tune' + *sâz* 'maker' (stem of *sâkhtan*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âhangsâz* is built on native Persian *âhang*, which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian word for 'pull, draught; melody' (EtymDB), and *sâz*, the present stem of *sâkhtan*, 'to make'.
