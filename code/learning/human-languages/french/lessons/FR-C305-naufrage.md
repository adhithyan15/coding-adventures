## You'll want to know: le naufrage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C305-NAUFRAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le naufrage** — "the shipwreck".

> Le naufrage a eu lieu près de la côte.
>
> *The shipwreck happened near the coast.*

*Faire naufrage* — to be shipwrecked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naufrage* is a learned borrowing from Latin *naufragium* "a shipwreck" (EtymDB), from *nāvis* "ship" and *frangere* "to break".
