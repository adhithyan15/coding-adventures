# आठ — eight on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-AABHAAR-01, MW-SCRIPT-SAAT-01] -->

[PAUSE 16s] Write formal thanks, then write seven.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-AATH-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-AA-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, MW-LEX-AATH-01] -->

> **आ** + **ठ** → **आठ** — *āṭh* — eight

Two letters, both of them years old in this book's own timeline: **आ** from
chapter 2 and **ठ** from chapter 17. Nothing is joined, nothing is marked.

Compare **सात** and **आठ** side by side. Seven takes the vowel as a mark on a
consonant; eight takes the same vowel as a letter, because there is no
consonant for it to sit on.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-AATH-01, MW-SCRIPT-SAAT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **आठ**, cover it, wait five seconds, write it. Then write **सात** above
it and circle the long *ā* in each.
