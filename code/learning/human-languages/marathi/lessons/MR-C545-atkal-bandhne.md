## You'll want to know: अटकळ बांधणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C545-BHAVISHYA545-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अटकळ बांधणे** — *aṭkaḷ bāndhṇe* — "to conjecture; to guess".

> ढग पाहून आजोबांनी पावसाची अटकळ बांधली.
>
> *ḍhag pāhūn ājobānnī pāvsācī aṭkaḷ bāndhlī.*
>
> Seeing the clouds, Grandfather guessed it would rain.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अटकळ बांधली, *tyāne aṭkaḷ bāndhlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अटकळ बांधणे** (*aṭkaḷ bāndhṇe*) is अटकळ, inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *aṭkaḷ*, "conjecture", under *\*aṭṭakkalā*, "guess" (CDIAL 183), with बांधणे, "to tie".
