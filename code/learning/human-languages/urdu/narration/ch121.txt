Urdu, chapter 121: Loose trousers, Scarf, Jumper, Jacket, Pyjamas.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
شلوار (shalvār) — loose trousers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a kebab, then the Urdu for biryani.

You'll want to know: شلوار.
شلوار — shalvār — "loose trousers".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: shalvār]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: shalvār, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say shalvār]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for a kebab, then the Urdu for biryani, then say shalvār again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does شلوار (shalvār) mean? ("Loose trousers".) Say it once more.
