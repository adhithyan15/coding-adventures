## You'll want to know: જકાત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C406-BORDER406-03]; assesses=[] -->

**જકાત** — *jakāt* — "a customs duty; a toll on goods".

> અમારે સીમા પર જકાત ભરવી પડી.
>
> *amāre sīmā par jakāt bharvī paḍī.*
>
> We had to pay customs duty at the border.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જકાત** (*jakāt*) is usually traced to Arabic *zakāt*, "alms, a religious levy", which came through Persian; in Gujarati it became a duty on goods carried across a border or into a town.
