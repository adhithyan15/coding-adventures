# すえっこ (suekko) — the youngest child

## You'll want to know: すえっこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C477-PARENT477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**すえっこ** — *suekko* — "the youngest child".

> **すえっこは まだ ちいさいです。** — *suekko wa mada chiisai desu* — "The youngest child is still small."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すえっこ** (*suekko*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *sue*, "end" and *ko*, "child" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
