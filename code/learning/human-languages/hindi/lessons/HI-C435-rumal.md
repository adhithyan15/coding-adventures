## You'll want to know: रुमाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C435-REZGARI435-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रुमाल** — *rumāl* — "a handkerchief".

> मैंने बाज़ार से दो रुमाल ख़रीदे।
>
> *maiṁne bāzār se do rumāl kharīde.*
>
> I bought two handkerchiefs at the market.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुमाल** (*rumāl*) is usually traced to Persian *rūmāl*, from *rū*, "face", and a word for wiping: a cloth for the face.
