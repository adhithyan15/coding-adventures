## You'll want to know: વહેંચવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C163-MEAL163-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વહેંચવું** — *vahẽcvũ* — "to share out, to divide".

> **બાળકો રોટલી વહેંચે છે.** — *bāḷko roṭlī vahẽce che* — "The children share the rotli."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વહેંચવું** (*vahẽcvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *vibhakta*, "divided, distributed", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "divides, distributes" (CDIAL 11808).
