## You'll want to know: yǒngyuǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C344-MEMORY344-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒngyuǎn* — "forever".

> *wǒ huì yǒngyuǎn jìde nàge Zhōngqiū* — I'll always remember that moon festival.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: yǒng is said with a rising tone before yuǎn, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒngyuǎn* is built from *yǒng* and *yuǎn* 'far', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjwaengX* and *hjwonX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'long' and the second 'distant'.
