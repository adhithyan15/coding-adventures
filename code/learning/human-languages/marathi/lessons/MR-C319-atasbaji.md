## You'll want to know: आतषबाजी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C319-ROSHNAI319-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आतषबाजी** — *ātaṣbājī* — "fireworks".

> जत्रेच्या शेवटी आकाशात आतषबाजी झाली.
>
> *jatrecyā śevṭī ākāśāt ātaṣbājī jhālī.*
>
> At the end of the fair there were fireworks in the sky.

Feminine.

आतषबाजी करणे, to put on fireworks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आतषबाजी** (*ātaṣbājī*) is a loan from Persian *ātaš-bāzī*, built from *ātaš*, 'fire', and *bāzī*, 'play'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
