## You'll want to know: lo sciatore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C515-NEVE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lo sciatore** — "skier".

> Ci sono molti sciatori sulla pista.
>
> *There are lots of skiers on the slope.*

A woman is *la sciatrice*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sciatore* is built on *sci* "ski", which was borrowed from Norwegian *ski*, from Old Norse *skíð* "a stick of wood, a snowshoe" (EtymDB, under French *ski*).
