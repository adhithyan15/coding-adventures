## You'll want to know: ધર્મશાળા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C212-REST212-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ધર્મશાળા** — *dharmaśāḷā* — "rest house, pilgrims' lodge".

> **અમે રાત્રે ધર્મશાળામાં રહ્યા.** — *ame rātre dharmaśāḷāmā̃ rahyā* — "We spent the night at a rest house."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધર્મશાળા** (*dharmaśāḷā*) is Sanskrit *dharmaśālā*, "charitable asylum", built from *dhárma*, "duty, charity", and *śā́lā*, "hall", taken over with Gujarati *-ḷ-*, a semi-tatsama (CDIAL 6760a).
