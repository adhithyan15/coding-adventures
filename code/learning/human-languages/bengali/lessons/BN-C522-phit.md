## You'll want to know: ফিট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C522-BYAYAM522-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ফিট** — *phiṭ* — "fit (in good shape)".

> রোজ হাঁটলে তুমি ফিট থাকবে।
>
> *Roj hā̃ṭle tumi phiṭ thākbe.*
>
> If you walk every day, you'll stay fit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফিট** (*phiṭ*) is borrowed from English *fit*, used of health.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
