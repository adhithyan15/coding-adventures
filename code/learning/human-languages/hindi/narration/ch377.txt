Hindi, chapter 377: As Things Stand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्थिति (sthiti) — a situation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for bound to happen, then the Hindi for to emerge.

You'll want to know: स्थिति.
स्थिति — sthiti — "a situation".
अगर स्थिति बिगड़ेगी तो हम शहर छोड़ देंगे। agar sthiti bigṛegī to ham śahar choṛ deṅge. If the situation gets worse, we'll leave the city.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
स्थिति (sthiti) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sthiti, "standing, a state" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 5.
फँसाना (phãsānā) — to trap, to land (someone) in trouble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a need, then the Hindi for a hardship.

You'll want to know: फँसाना.
फँसाना — phãsānā — "to trap, to land (someone) in trouble".
अगर तुम उस आदमी की बात मानोगे तो वह तुम्हें फँसा देगा। agar tum us ādmī kī bāt mānoge to vah tumhẽ phãsā degā. If you listen to that man, he'll land you in trouble.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे फँसाया, usne mujhe phãsāyā.

The word, taken apart.
फँसाना (phãsānā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit spāśayati, "causes to be bound" (CDIAL 13814).
