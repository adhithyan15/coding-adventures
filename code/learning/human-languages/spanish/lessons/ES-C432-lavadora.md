# la lavadora — the thing that washes

## You'll want to know: la lavadora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-01]; assesses=[ES-LEX-LAVAR] -->

**la lavadora** — the washing machine.

If you guessed it, that is the point of the last chapter arriving before this
one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-01, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-04] -->

**lavar** plus **-dora**. The same ending as **el proveedor**, in its feminine
form, and it does the same job — except that here the doer is a machine rather
than a person.

| verb | the doer |
|---|---|
| lavar | la lavadora |
| secar | la secadora |
| calcular | la calculadora |

Spanish names most household machines this way, so **-dora** is worth more to
you than any single appliance. A machine that does something is usually that
something plus this ending, and you can often reach the word without having met
it.
