## You'll want to know: l'adversaire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C345-PARTISAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'adversaire** — "the opponent".

> Son adversaire a gagné le débat.
>
> *His opponent won the debate.*

Masculine or feminine: *un* or *une adversaire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adversaire* comes from Latin *adversārius* "an opponent" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *adversus* "turned toward, facing", built on *vertere* "to turn".
