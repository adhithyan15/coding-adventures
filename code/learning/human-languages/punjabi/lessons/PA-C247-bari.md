## You'll want to know: ਬਾਰੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C247-GHR247-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਾਰੀ** — *bārī* — "a small window".

> ਰਸੋਈ ਦੀ ਬਾਰੀ ਛੋਟੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *rasoī dī bārī chhoṭī hai.*
>
> The kitchen window is small.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਾਰੀ** (*bārī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dvāra*, "door"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bārī*, "window" (CDIAL 6663).
