# കുടുംബാംഗം (kuṭumbāṁgaṁ) — a family member

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C554-MATAPITA554-03, ML-LEX-C554-MATAPITA554-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a brother-in-law, then the Malayalam for a sister-in-law.

## You'll want to know: കുടുംബാംഗം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C554-MATAPITA554-05]; assesses=[] -->

**കുടുംബാംഗം** — *kuṭumbāṁgaṁ* — "a family member".

> കല്യാണത്തിന് എല്ലാ കുടുംബാംഗങ്ങളും എത്തി.
>
> *kalyāṇattinŭ ellā kuṭumbāṁgaṅṅaḷuṁ etti.*
>
> All the family members came to the wedding.

Plural: കുടുംബാംഗങ്ങൾ, *kuṭumbāṁgaṅṅaḷ*.

കുടുംബം family + അംഗം a limb, a member.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുടുംബാംഗം** (*kuṭumbāṁgaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *kuṭumba*, "a household" (CDIAL 3233), and *aṅga*, "a limb" (CDIAL 114).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
