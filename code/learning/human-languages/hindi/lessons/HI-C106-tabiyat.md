# तबीयत (tabīyat) — health, how one is feeling

## You'll want to know: तबीयत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C106-UNWELL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तबीयत** (*tabīyat*) — "how one is feeling", one's health on the day.

**आपकी तबीयत कैसी है?** — "how are you feeling?", the question to ask someone who has been ill.
**तबीयत ठीक नहीं है** — "I am not well".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The question after how are you, when you know someone has been unwell.
