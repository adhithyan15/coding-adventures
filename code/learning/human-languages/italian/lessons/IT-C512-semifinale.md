## You'll want to know: la semifinale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C512-TORNEO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la semifinale** — "semi-final".

> Abbiamo perso in semifinale.
>
> *We lost in the semi-final.*

The plural is *le semifinali*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*semifinale* is built from Latin *sēmi-* "half" and *finale*, from *fīnis* "end" (Lewis & Short).
