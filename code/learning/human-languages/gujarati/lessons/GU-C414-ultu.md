# ઊલટું (ūlṭũ) — opposite; the wrong way round

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C414-EAST414-02, GU-LEX-C414-EAST414-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the west, then the Gujarati for the north.

## You'll want to know: ઊલટું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C414-EAST414-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊલટું** — *ūlṭũ* — "opposite; the wrong way round".

> તમે ઊલટી દિશામાં જાઓ છો.
>
> *tame ūlṭī diśāmā̃ jāo cho.*
>
> You are going in the opposite direction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊલટું** (*ūlṭũ*) belongs with the verb CDIAL lists for Gujarati, "to be the reverse of", from *\*ullaṭati*, "turns over", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2368).
