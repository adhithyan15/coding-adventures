## You'll want to know: कोंडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C300-LACH300-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कोंडणे** — *koṇḍṇe* — "to shut in; to lock up".

> चोरांनी चौकीदाराला खोलीत कोंडलं.
>
> *corānnī caukīdārālā kholīt koṇḍlaṁ.*
>
> The thieves locked the watchman in a room.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कोंडलं, *tyāne koṇḍlaṁ*.

The place takes -त: खोलीत कोंडणे; कोंडून ठेवणे, to keep shut in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोंडणे** (*koṇḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb of shutting in; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
