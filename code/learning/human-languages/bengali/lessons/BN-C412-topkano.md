## You'll want to know: টপকানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C412-PANCHIL412-05]; assesses=[] -->

**টপকানো** — *ṭôpkāno* — "to climb over; to jump over".

> চোর পাঁচিল টপকে পালাল।
>
> *chor pā̃chil ṭôpke pālālô.*
>
> The thief climbed over the wall and ran away.

Past (he/she …): তিনি পাঁচিল টপকালেন, *tini pā̃chil ṭôpkālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টপকানো** (*ṭôpkāno*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ṭapkāna*, "to leap over", under the reconstructed *tarpati*, "jumps" (CDIAL 5727).
