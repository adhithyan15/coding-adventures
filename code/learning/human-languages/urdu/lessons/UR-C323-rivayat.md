## You'll want to know: روایت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C323-RIVAJ323-01]; assesses=[] -->

**روایت** — *rivāyat* — "a tradition".

> یہ ہمارے گاؤں کی پرانی روایت ہے۔
>
> *yih hamāre gāoṅ kī purānī rivāyat hai.*
>
> This is an old tradition of our village.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**روایت** (*rivāyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *riwāya*, "a handing on of a report": what is passed down.
