## You'll want to know: నాగరికత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C368-SASANAM368-04]; assesses=[] -->

**నాగరికత** — *nāgarikata* — "a civilisation".

> ఆ నది ఒడ్డున ఒక పురాతన నాగరికత ఉండేది.
>
> *ā nadi oḍḍuna oka purātana nāgarikata uṇḍēdi.*
>
> An ancient civilisation once stood on the bank of that river.

Built on నగరం, a city: the life of cities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నాగరికత** (*nāgarikata*) is built on Sanskrit *nāgarika*, "born or living in a town, civic" (Monier-Williams), from *nagara*, "a city", with the noun ending *-ta*.
