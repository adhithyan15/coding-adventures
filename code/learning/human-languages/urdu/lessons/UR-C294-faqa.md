## You'll want to know: فاقہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C294-QAHAT294-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فاقہ** — *fāqā* — "going without food".

> لوگ فاقے سے کمزور ہو گئے۔
>
> *log fāqe se kamzor ho gae.*
>
> People grew weak from hunger.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fāqa*, "want, need". A **فاقہ** is a day with nothing to eat.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
