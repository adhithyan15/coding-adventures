## You'll want to know: しょうぼうしゃ

**しょうぼうしゃ** — *shōbōsha* — "a fire engine".

> **しょうぼうしゃが すぐ きました。** — *shōbōsha ga sugu kimashita* — "A fire engine arrived at once."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうぼうしゃ** (*shōbōsha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "extinguish" (Middle Chinese *sjew*), *bō*, "ward off" (*bjang*) and *sha*, "car" (*tsyhae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
