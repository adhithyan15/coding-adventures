## You'll want to know: o conto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C324-CONTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o conto** — "the short story, the tale".

> O avô contava-nos um conto antes de dormir.
>
> *Grandfather used to tell us a story before bed.*

Built on *contar*, ‘to tell’: *contar um conto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conto* is built on *contar* "to count, to tell", which is inherited from Latin *computāre* "to reckon" (Lewis & Short): to tell events one by one, like counting.
