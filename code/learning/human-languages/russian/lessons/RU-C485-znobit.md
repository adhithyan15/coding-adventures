## You'll want to know: знобить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C485-NAUSEA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**знобить** (*znobít*) — "to have the shivers (impersonal)".

> У меня температура, и меня знобит.
>
> *U menyá temperatúra, i menyá znobít.*
>
> I've got a temperature and I've got the shivers.

Impersonal: *меня знобит*. Imperfective only.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*знобить* shares its root with *озноб* 'a chill'; EtymDB has no entry for either, and the origin is not settled.
