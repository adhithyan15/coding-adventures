## You'll want to know: दवात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C284-BASTO284-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दवात** — *davāt* — "an inkpot".

> दवात मेज माथै है।
>
> *davāt mej māthai hai.*
>
> The inkpot is on the table.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दवात** (*davāt*) is usually traced, through Persian *davāt*, to Arabic *dawāt*, "an inkstand".
