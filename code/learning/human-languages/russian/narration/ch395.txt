Russian, chapter 395: Savings and budgets.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сбережения (sberezhéniya) — savings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to give back, then the Russian for a per cent.

You'll want to know: сбережения.
сбережения (sberezhéniya) — "savings".
Все сбережения он потратил на учёбу. Vse sberezhéniya on potrátil na uchyóbu. He spent all his savings on his studies.
Plural. From сберегать, to save; беречь has the same root.

The word, taken apart.
сбережения (sberezhéniya) is built on сберечь "to save, to keep", from беречь "to protect", inherited from Proto-Slavic berťi (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
вкладывать (vkládyvat) — to invest, to put in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a per cent, then the Russian for savings.

You'll want to know: вкладывать.
вкладывать (vkládyvat) — "to invest, to put in".
Он вкладывает деньги в недвижимость. On vkládyvayet déngi v nedvízhimost. He invests money in property.
Imperfective; perfective вложить. Вкладывать в + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
вкладывать (vkládyvat) is built from в- "in" and the root of класть "to lay", inherited from Proto-Slavic klasti: to lay money into something.

Lesson 3 of 5.
вклад (vklad) — a deposit; a contribution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for savings, then the Russian for to invest.

You'll want to know: вклад.
вклад (vklad) — "a deposit; a contribution".
Я открыл вклад в банке. Ya otkrýl vklad v bánke. I opened a deposit account at the bank.
Masculine. Вклад в + accusative — a contribution to: большой вклад (vklad) в науку.

The word, taken apart.
вклад (vklad) is formed from вкладывать (vkládyvat) "to put in", from в- and класть "to lay", Proto-Slavic klasti.

Lesson 4 of 5.
стоимость (stóimost) — cost, value.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to invest, then the Russian for a deposit.

You'll want to know: стоимость.
стоимость (stóimost) — "cost, value".
Стоимость билета — тысяча рублей. Stóimost biléta — týsyacha rubléy. The ticket costs a thousand roubles.
Feminine. From стоить, to cost.

The word, taken apart.
стоимость (stóimost) is built on стоить "to cost", which perhaps came through Polish and copies the sense of Latin cōnstāre "to stand firm; to cost", by a usual account; it goes with стоять "to stand".

Lesson 5 of 5.
бюджет (byudzhét) — a budget.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a deposit, then the Russian for cost.

You'll want to know: бюджет.
бюджет (byudzhét) — "a budget".
У нас маленький семейный бюджет. U nas málenkiy seméynyy byudzhét. We have a small family budget.
Masculine. Государственный бюджет (byudzhét) — the state budget.

The word, taken apart.
бюджет (byudzhét) is usually traced to English budget, from Old French bougette "a little bag", from Latin bulga "a leather bag" (Lewis & Short): first the bag the money was kept in.
