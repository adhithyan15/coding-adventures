## You'll want to know: la timidité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C466-TIMIDITE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la timidité** — "shyness".

> Avec le temps, il a perdu sa timidité.
>
> *With time, he lost his shyness.*

The adjective *timide* "shy" is the everyday word; *la timidité* names the quality.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la timidité* is a learned borrowing from Latin *timiditās* "fearfulness" (EtymDB), built on *timidus* "fearful", from *timēre* "to fear" (Lewis & Short).
