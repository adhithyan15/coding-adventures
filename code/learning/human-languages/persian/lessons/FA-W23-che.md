# چ (che) — the letter that opens چیست

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-JIM-01, FA-SCRIPT-C23-KHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ج and خ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **چیست** (*chist*), "what is?".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: چ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-CHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چ** — *che*, the sound *ch*.

It opens **چیست** (*chist*), "what is?", one of your first questions. It is one of
the four letters Persian **added** to the Arabic alphabet: the same skeleton as
**ح**, with **three dots below**.

## Writing: چ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-CHE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: draw the short upper head from left to right
- **2.** continue down and around the deep bowl without lifting
- **3.** lift, then place the lower-left dot
- **4.** lift again and place the lower-right dot
- **5.** lift again and place the lower-center dot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 3.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, چ demonstration at 00:35–00:41 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).
