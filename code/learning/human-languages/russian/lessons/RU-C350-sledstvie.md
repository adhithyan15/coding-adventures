## You'll want to know: следствие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C350-POSLEDSTVIYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**следствие** (*slédstviye*) — "a result, an outcome; an investigation".

> Его болезнь — следствие стресса.
>
> *Yegó bolézn — slédstviye stréssa.*
>
> His illness is the result of stress.

Neuter, stressed on the first syllable. In law, *следствие* is a criminal investigation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**следствие** is built on *след* "a track, a trail, a mark" (EtymDB) with the suffix *-ствие*, a Church Slavonic pattern: a result follows its cause like a track.
