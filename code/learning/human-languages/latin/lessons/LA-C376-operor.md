## You'll want to know: operor, operārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C376-OPUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**operor, operārī** — "to work, to be busy (at)".

> Fabrī in officīnā operābantur.
>
> *The smiths were at work in the workshop.*

Deponent: *operor, operārī, operātus sum*, often with the dative of the task. English *operate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*operor* is built on *opus, operis* "work" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *operate* comes from it.
