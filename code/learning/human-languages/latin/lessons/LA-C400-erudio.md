## You'll want to know: ērudiō, ērudīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C400-DOCEO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ērudiō, ērudīre** — "to educate, to polish".

> Pater fīlium dīligenter ērudīvit.
>
> *The father educated his son carefully.*

*ērudiō, ērudīre, ērudīvī*; its participle follows in a later lesson. *Ē* + *rudis*, rough: to take the roughness out. English *erudite*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ērudiō* is built from *ē* "out of" and *rudis* "rough, untaught" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to bring out of the rough state. English *erudite* comes from its participle.
