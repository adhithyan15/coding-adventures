## You'll want to know: il ruscello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C460-BOSCO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il ruscello** — "the brook, the stream".

> Beviamo l'acqua fresca del ruscello.
>
> *We drink the cool water of the brook.*

A small, quiet stream; *il fiume* is a river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruscello* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *rīvuscellus* (EtymDB), a little form of Latin *rīvus* 'stream'.
