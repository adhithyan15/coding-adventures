## You'll want to know: ふる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふる** — *furu* — "to fall (of rain or snow)".

> **あした あめが ふります。** — *ashita ame ga furimasu* — "It will rain tomorrow."

Polite **ふります**, *furimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふる** (*furu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
