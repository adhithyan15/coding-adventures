Bengali, chapter 472: Medicine and Treatment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ওষুধ (oshudh) — a medicine; medicine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to faint, then the Bengali for bedridden.

You'll want to know: ওষুধ.
ওষুধ — oshudh — "a medicine; medicine".
সকালে আর রাতে এই ওষুধটা খেতে হবে। sôkāle ār rāte ei oshudhṭā khete hôbe. This medicine has to be taken morning and night.
Bengali eats its medicine: ওষুধ (oshudh) খাওয়া, to take medicine.

The word, taken apart.
ওষুধ (oshudh) comes from Sanskrit auṣadha, "consisting of herbs", and so "herbs, a remedy" (CDIAL 2571), worn down in Bengali speech.

জ্বরের পরে দাদুর পথ্য ছিল ভাত আর মাছ। jbôrer pôre dādur pôthyô chhilô bhāt ār māchh. After the fever, my grandfather's diet was rice and fish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for medical treatment, then the Bengali for a diet for the sick.

You'll want to know: টিকা.
টিকা — ṭikā — "a vaccine; a vaccination".
নার্সটি শিশুদের টিকা দিলেন। nārsṭi shishuder ṭikā dilen. The nurse vaccinated the children.
টিকা (ṭikā) দেওয়া, to vaccinate; টিকা (ṭikā) নেওয়া, to be vaccinated.

The word, taken apart.
টিকা (ṭikā) is ṭikā, "a mark", which CDIAL lists for Bengali under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 5458); the vaccination sense probably comes from the mark it leaves on the skin.

Lesson 5 of 5.
সেরে ওঠা (sere oṭhā) — to recover; to get well.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a diet for the sick, then the Bengali for a vaccine.

You'll want to know: সেরে ওঠা.
সেরে ওঠা — sere oṭhā — "to recover; to get well".
দুই সপ্তাহ পরে অরুণ জ্বর থেকে সেরে উঠল। dui sôptāhô pôre ôrun jbôr theke sere uṭhlô. Two weeks later Arun recovered from the fever.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শিগগির সেরে উঠলেন, tini shiggir sere uṭhlen.

The word, taken apart.
সেরে ওঠা (sere oṭhā) is sere, the -এ form of sārā, "to get well", the plain partner of সারানো (taught), to repair, with oṭhā, "to rise" (CDIAL 1793).
