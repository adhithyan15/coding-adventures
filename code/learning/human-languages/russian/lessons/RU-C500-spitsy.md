## You'll want to know: спицы
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C500-KNITTING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**спицы** (*spítsy*) — "knitting needles".

> Для этого свитера нужны толстые спицы.
>
> *Dlya étogo svítera nuzhný tólstyye spítsy.*
>
> You need thick needles for this sweater.

Plural of *спица*, which also means a spoke of a wheel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*спицы* is the plural of *спица* 'a spoke, a knitting needle', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic *stъpica*; EtymDB has no entry for the plural.
