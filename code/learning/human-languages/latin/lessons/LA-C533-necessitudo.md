## You'll want to know: necessitūdō, necessitūdinis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C533-FAMI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**necessitūdō, necessitūdinis** — "a close bond, tie".

> Magna necessitūdō mihi cum frātre tuō est.
>
> *I have a close bond with your brother.*

Feminine, third declension, from *necesse*. Also need, but in letters it often means a tie of family or friendship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**necessitūdō** is built on *necesse* "necessary" (Lewis & Short): first need, then the ties that bind people.
