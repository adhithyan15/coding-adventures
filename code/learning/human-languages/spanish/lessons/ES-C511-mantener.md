## You'll want to know: mantener
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C511-HACER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**mantener** — "to keep, to maintain".

> Mantenga la distancia.
>
> *Keep your distance.*

Conjugated like *tener*: *mantengo*, *mantiene*.
