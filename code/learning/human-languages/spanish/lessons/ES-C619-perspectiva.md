## You'll want to know: la perspectiva
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C619-QUIZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la perspectiva** — "the prospect; the perspective".

> Las perspectivas de empleo son buenas.
>
> *The job prospects are good.*

In the plural it often means future chances.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perspectiva* is a learned word usually traced to Medieval Latin *perspectiva* 'the science of optics'; it is built on Latin *perspicere* 'to look through' (Lewis & Short), from *specere* 'to look'.
