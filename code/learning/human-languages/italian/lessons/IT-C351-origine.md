## You'll want to know: l'origine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C351-ORIGINE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'origine** — "the origin".

> Qual è l'origine di questa tradizione?
>
> *What is the origin of this tradition?*

*Avere origine da* — to come from. *Di origine italiana* — of Italian origin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*origine* is a learned borrowing from Latin *orīgō, orīginis* "beginning, source, birth" (Lewis & Short), from *orīrī* "to rise", as the sun rises.
