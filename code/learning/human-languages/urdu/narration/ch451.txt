Urdu, chapter 451: Hesitation and Regret.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ہچکچانا (hichkichānā) — to hesitate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to whimper, then the Urdu for out of sight.

You'll want to know: ہچکچانا.
ہچکچانا — hichkichānā — "to hesitate".
وہ سوال پوچھنے سے ہچکچاتی ہے۔ voh savāl pūchhne se hichkichātī hai. She hesitates to ask questions.
Past (he …): وہ ہچکچایا, voh hichkichāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ہچکچانا (hichkichānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi hicaknā, "to draw back", under hicc, "draw back", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 14083); the longer Urdu verb probably repeats it.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Urdu for out of sight, then the Urdu for to hesitate.

You'll want to know: لجانا.
لجانا — lajānā — "to blush; to be bashful".
دولہا کو دیکھ کر دلہن لجا گئی۔ dūlhā ko dekh kar dulhan lajā gaī. The bride blushed when she saw the bridegroom.
Past (she …): وہ لجا گئی, voh lajā gaī.

The word, taken apart.
لجانا (lajānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lajānā, "to be ashamed", under Sanskrit lajjátē, "is ashamed" (CDIAL 10909).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ندامت (nadāmat) — remorse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to hesitate, then the Urdu for to blush.

You'll want to know: ندامت.
ندامت — nadāmat — "remorse".
جھوٹ بول کر اسے ندامت ہوئی۔ jhūṭ bol kar use nadāmat huī. After telling the lie he felt remorse.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ندامت (nadāmat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic nadāma, "regret".
