## You'll want to know: avoir lieu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C274-SURVENIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**avoir lieu** — "to take place, to be held".

> La réunion a eu lieu hier.
>
> *The meeting took place yesterday.*

For planned events: *la fête aura lieu samedi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lieu* is inherited from Latin *locus* "place", and *avoir* from Latin *habēre* "to have, to hold" (Lewis & Short): to have a place.
