## You'll want to know: ٹرافی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C522-PHURTI522-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹرافی** — *ṭrāfī* — "a trophy".

> کپتان نے ٹرافی اٹھائی۔
>
> *kaptān ne ṭrāfī uṭhāī.*
>
> The captain lifted the trophy.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹرافی** (*ṭrāfī*) is borrowed from English *trophy*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
