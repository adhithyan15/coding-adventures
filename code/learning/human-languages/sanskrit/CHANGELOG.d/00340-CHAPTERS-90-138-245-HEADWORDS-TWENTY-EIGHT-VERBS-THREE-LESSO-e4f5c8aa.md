## Chapters 90-138: 245 headwords, twenty-eight verbs, three lessons split, and Sanskrit attains A1

Sanskrit had three kinds of A1 gap:

- it was short on headwords and verbs;
- three chapter-6 lessons introduced more than three atoms each;
- nineteen atoms were revisited fewer than twice.

**The words.** Forty-nine chapters of five, closed by ten review lessons:

- **time:** **अहः**, **प्रदोषः**, **मध्यरात्रिः**, **प्रभातम्**, the seasons,
  **अधुना**, **सर्वदा** (TIME-OF-DAY)
- **place:** **अन्तः**, **बहिः**, **उपरि**, **अधः**, **मध्ये**, **सर्वत्र**,
  **पुस्तकालयः** and more (ASK-LOCATION)
- **why:** **अतः**, **तस्मात्**, **हेतुः**, **तदर्थम्**, **यस्मात्** (SAY-WHY)
- **things:** 135 of them, covering tools, the kitchen, clothes, machines,
  food, nature, animals and the body (NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS)
- **qualities:** forty describing words, three of them verbs (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)
- **verbs:** twenty-eight in all, twenty-five of them on NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS

**Checks.** Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword,
romanization and lesson text. Every word uses only letters the book has already
taught the reader to write. Ten first-draft headwords used ङ. The track teaches
that letter, but `data/scripts/devanagari.json` does not cover it yet. As with
मंगलवासरः, the ten now use the anusvara spelling: कंकणम्, अंगुलिः, संगणकम्,
तरंगः, and six more. The review lessons that name them use it too. The mistake
is recorded in `lessons.d/`.

**The splits.** Each over-budget lesson now ends where its first idea ends. A
`-more` continuation carries the rest, together with its practice and wrap-up:

- **numbers 1-5** keeps the five and the dual. Numeral gender and the neuter
  forms the daughter languages inherit move to *numbers-1-5-more*.
- **number cognates** keeps the table. The *\*kʷ* outcomes, Grimm's law and the
  *four*/*five* analogy move to *number-cognates-more*.
- **pancha travels** keeps Punjab, *panj* and *pente*. The qualified *punch*
  story moves to *pancha-travels-more*.

The splits shifted later lessons by one or two positions. Three atoms lost a
reinforcement window as a result, and each got a warm-up retrieval back:
the goodbye roots, *na cintā*, and the letter अ.

**The revisits.** Thirty-one thin atoms, nineteen of them blocking, each get
warm-up retrievals. An atom with no revisit gets two, in two lessons. The
retrievals include:

- the time words that keep one shape
- the *k-* questions
- the number family and *four*'s borrowed *f-*
- sandhi in *sāhāyyaṁ karoti*
- the roots *vad*, *vas* and *kṛ*
- the *-aya-* stem
- the locative *nagare*
- the chapter-64 reading skills

Two lessons ran past five minutes with every line added. Their extra lines
moved to later lessons instead.

      sanskrit lessons     487  ->  745
      level gate           pre-A1 -> A1
