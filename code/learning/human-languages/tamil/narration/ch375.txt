Tamil, chapter 375: Parties and Power.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கட்சி (kaṭci) — a political party; a side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a constituency, then the Tamil for a campaign.

You'll want to know: கட்சி.
கட்சி — kaṭci — "a political party; a side".
இந்தத் தேர்தலில் பல கட்சிகள் போட்டியிடுகின்றன. intat tērtalil pala kaṭcikaḷ pōṭṭiyiṭukiṉṟaṉa. Many parties are contesting this election.
Plural: கட்சிகள், kaṭcikaḷ.
Also a side in an argument: அவர் என் கட்சி (kaṭci), he is on my side.

The word, taken apart.
கட்சி (kaṭci) is a word whose origin is not settled; it is often compared with Sanskrit pakṣa, "a wing, a side" (Monier-Williams), which also names a side in a debate.

Lesson 2 of 5.
கூட்டணி (kūṭṭaṇi) — an alliance; a coalition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a campaign, then the Tamil for a political party.

You'll want to know: கூட்டணி.
கூட்டணி — kūṭṭaṇi — "an alliance; a coalition".
இரண்டு கட்சிகளும் கூட்டணி அமைத்தன. iraṇṭu kaṭcikaḷum kūṭṭaṇi amaittaṉa. The two parties formed an alliance.
கூட்டணி (kūṭṭaṇi) அமை, to form an alliance.

The word, taken apart.
கூட்டணி (kūṭṭaṇi) is native: kūṭṭu, "union, joining" (DEDR 1882), and aṇi, "array of an army, row" (DEDR 117): ranks drawn up together.

Lesson 3 of 5.
அதிகாரம் (atikāram) — authority; power.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a political party, then the Tamil for an alliance.

You'll want to know: அதிகாரம்.
அதிகாரம் — atikāram — "authority; power".
இந்த முடிவை எடுக்க அவருக்கு அதிகாரம் இல்லை. inta muṭivai eṭukka avarukku atikāram illai. He has no authority to make this decision.
அதிகாரி, an official, is the person who holds it.

The word, taken apart.
அதிகாரம் (atikāram) is borrowed from Sanskrit adhikāra, "authority; government, rule, office" (Monier-Williams), the source of அதிகாரி.

Lesson 4 of 5.
செல்வாக்கு (celvākku) — influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an alliance, then the Tamil for authority.

You'll want to know: செல்வாக்கு.
செல்வாக்கு — celvākku — "influence".
அவருக்கு இந்த ஊரில் நிறையச் செல்வாக்கு இருக்கிறது. avarukku inta ūril niṟaiyac celvākku irukkiṟatu. He has a lot of influence in this town.
X-இன் மீது செல்வாக்கு (celvākku), influence over X.

The word, taken apart.
செல்வாக்கு (celvākku) is usually read as native cel, "to go, pass, be current" (DEDR 2781), and vākku, "word": a word that carries. The analysis is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
கவர் (kavar) — to attract; to win over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for authority, then the Tamil for influence.

You'll want to know: கவர்.
கவர் — kavar — "to attract; to win over".
அவருடைய பேச்சு பல மாணவர்களைக் கவர்ந்தது. avaruṭaiya pēccu pala māṇavarkaḷaik kavarntatu. His speech won over many students.
Past (I …): கவர்ந்தேன், kavarntēṉ.
Who is attracted takes -ஐ. கவர்ச்சி, attraction.

The word, taken apart.
கவர் (kavar) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1326 lists Tamil kavar, "to seize, grasp, get control of, desire": to attract is to take hold of someone's mind.
