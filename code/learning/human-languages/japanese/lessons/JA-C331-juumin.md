## You'll want to know: じゅうみん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C331-SHAKAI331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうみん** — *jūmin* — "a resident; the local people".

> **じゅうみんが はんたいしたので、ちゅうしに なりました。** — *jūmin ga hantai shita node, chūshi ni narimashita* — "The residents were against it, so it was called off."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうみん** (*jūmin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jū*, "dwell" (Middle Chinese *drjuH*) and *min*, "people" (*mjin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
