## You'll want to know: 亲自
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C511-KIN511-05]; assesses=[] -->

**亲自** — *qīnzì* — "personally (doing it oneself)".

> **妈妈亲自来学校看我** *māma qīnzì lái xuéxiào kàn wǒ* — Mum came to school to see me herself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**亲自** (*qīnzì*) is built from 亲 'close' and 自 'self', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshin* and *dzijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'from'; the sense 'self' is not given there.
