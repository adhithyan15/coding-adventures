## You'll want to know: तमासो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C223-FAIR223-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तमासो** — *tamāso* — "a show; a spectacle".

> हूं काल तमासो देखसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl tamāso dekhsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll watch the show.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तमासो** (*tamāso*) is usually traced to Persian *tamāšā*, "a spectacle", which Persian took from Arabic; Marwadi ends it in *-o*.
