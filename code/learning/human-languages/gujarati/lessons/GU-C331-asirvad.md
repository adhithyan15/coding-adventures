# આશીર્વાદ (āśīrvād) — a blessing

## You'll want to know: આશીર્વાદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C331-UPVAS331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આશીર્વાદ** — *āśīrvād* — "a blessing".

> મારા દાદાએ મને આશીર્વાદ આપ્યા.
>
> *mārā dādāe mane āśīrvād āpyā.*
>
> My grandfather gave me his blessing.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આશીર્વાદ** (*āśīrvād*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *āśīrvāda*, "a benediction, a spoken blessing" (Monier-Williams). Elders give blessings in the plural: **આશીર્વાદ આપ્યા**.
