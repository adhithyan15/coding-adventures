# બેભાન થવું (bebhān thavũ) — to faint, to pass out

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-03, GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for tear, then the Gujarati for to suffer.

## You'll want to know: બેભાન થવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-05]; assesses=[] -->

**બેભાન થવું** — *bebhān thavũ* — "to faint, to pass out".

> **શું તે બેભાન થયો?** — *śũ te bebhān thayo?* — "Did he faint?"

Word for word, to become senseless: **બેભાન**, *bebhān*, and **થવું**, *thavũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બેભાન થવું** (*bebhān thavũ*) is built from *bebhān*, "senseless", and *thavũ*, "to become". *Bebhān* is usually explained as the Persian prefix *be-*, "without", joined to *bhān*, "awareness", usually traced to Sanskrit *bhāna*, "appearance" (CDIAL 9451); CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of the whole.
