## You'll want to know: जेबकतरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C289-BHAGA289-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जेबकतरा** — *jebkatrā* — "a pickpocket".

> बाज़ार में एक जेबकतरा घूम रहा था।
>
> *bāzār meṁ ek jebkatrā ghūm rahā thā.*
>
> A pickpocket was wandering through the market.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जेबकतरा** (*jebkatrā*) is **जेब**, a pocket, and *katrā*, one who snips, from *katarnā*, to clip, which CDIAL 2858 lists for Hindi under Sanskrit *kartarī*, "scissors": the one who cuts into pockets.
