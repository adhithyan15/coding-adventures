## You'll want to know: a no ser que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C612-SINO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a no ser que** — "except if; unless".

> Salimos a las ocho, a no ser que llueva.
>
> *We leave at eight, unless it rains.*

Means the same as *a menos que*, with the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a no ser que* is built on *ser*, usually explained as a merger of Latin *esse* 'to be' and *sedere* 'to sit'; EtymDB takes it back to Old Spanish *seer*. The phrase means 'unless it be that'.
