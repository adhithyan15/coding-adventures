## You'll want to know: ఆలోచన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C382-ALOCANA382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆలోచన** — *ālōcana* — "an idea; a thought".

> నాకు ఒక మంచి ఆలోచన వచ్చింది.
>
> *nāku oka mañci ālōcana vaccindi.*
>
> I had a good idea.

Plural: ఆలోచనలు, *ālōcanalu*.

An idea comes to you: నాకు ఆలోచన వచ్చింది, literally an idea came to me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆలోచన** (*ālōcana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ālocana*, "seeing", and also "considering, reflection" (Monier-Williams).
