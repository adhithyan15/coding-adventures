# ਪੌਣੇ (pauṇe) — less a quarter; a quarter to

## You'll want to know: ਪੌਣੇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C260-TIME260-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੌਣੇ** — *pauṇe* — "less a quarter; a quarter to".

> ਰੇਲ ਪੌਣੇ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *rel pauṇe das vaje jāndī hai.*
>
> The train goes at a quarter to ten.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੌਣੇ** (*pauṇe*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*padūna*, "less by a quarter"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pauṇe*, "three quarters", under it (CDIAL 7765).
