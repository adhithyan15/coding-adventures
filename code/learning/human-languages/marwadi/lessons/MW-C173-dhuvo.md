## You'll want to know: धूंवो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C173-CHULHO173-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धूंवो** — *dhū̃vo* — "smoke".

> रसोई मांय घणो धूंवो है।
>
> *rasoī mā̃y ghaṇo dhū̃vo hai.*
>
> There's a lot of smoke in the kitchen.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूंवो** (*dhū̃vo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhūma*, "smoke, vapour"; CDIAL lists Hindi *dhūā̃* (CDIAL 6849). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
