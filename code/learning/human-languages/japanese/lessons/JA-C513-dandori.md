## You'll want to know: だんどり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C513-PLAN513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**だんどり** — *dandori* — "arrangements; the order of the steps".

> **もし だんどりが よかったら、しごとは はやく おわるでしょう。** — *moshi dandori ga yokattara, shigoto wa hayaku owaru deshō* — "If the arrangements are good, the work will probably finish early."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だんどり** (*dandori*) mixes the two layers, native and Sino-Japanese: Sino-Japanese *dan*, "steps" (Middle Chinese *dwanH*), borrowed from Chinese, and native *dori*, "taking", the voiced form of *tori*, from *toru*, "to take" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
