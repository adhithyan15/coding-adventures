Arabic, chapter 376: Fields of study.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فلسفة (falsafa) — philosophy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for likewise, then the Arabic for moreover.

You'll want to know: فلسفة.
فلسفة — falsafa — "philosophy".
يدرس ابن عمي الفلسفة في الجامعة. yadrusu ibn ʿammī al-falsafa fī al-jāmiʿa. My cousin studies philosophy at university.

The word, taken apart.
فلسفة (falsafa) was borrowed from Greek philosophia, "love of wisdom" (EtymDB). Arabic fitted the loan to its own four-consonant pattern.

Lesson 2 of 5.
لغويات (lughawiyyāt) — linguistics.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for moreover, then the Arabic for philosophy.

You'll want to know: لغويات.
لغويات — lughawiyyāt — "linguistics".
تخصصت أختي في اللغويات. takhaṣṣaṣat ukhtī fī al-lughawiyyāt. My sister specialized in linguistics.

The word, taken apart.
لغويات (lughawiyyāt) is a modern plural built on lugha, "a language", which Lane files under the native Arabic root l-gh-w (Lane). It names the study of language.

قرأت كتابا لفيلسوف قديم. qaraʾtu kitāban li-faylasūf qadīm. I read a book by an ancient philosopher.

Lesson 4 of 5.
خبير (khabīr) — an expert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for linguistics, then the Arabic for a philosopher.

You'll want to know: خبير.
خبير — khabīr — "an expert".
سأل الصحفي خبيرا في التاريخ. saʾala aṣ-ṣaḥafī khabīran fī at-tārīkh. The journalist asked an expert on history.
Plural: خبراء, khubarāʾ.

The word, taken apart.
خبير (khabīr) is built on the native Arabic root kh-b-r, "to know thoroughly, to test" (Lane). A khabīr knows a thing from the inside: an expert.

Lesson 5 of 5.
أكاديمي (akādīmī) — academic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a philosopher, then the Arabic for an expert.

You'll want to know: أكاديمي.
أكاديمي — akādīmī — "academic".
يعمل عمي في مجال أكاديمي. yaʿmalu ʿammī fī majāl akādīmī. My uncle works in an academic field.

The word, taken apart.
أكاديمي (akādīmī) is a modern loan with the Arabic ending -ī. It is usually traced to Greek akadēmia, the name of a school of learning, through European languages.
