## You'll want to know: સાળો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C491-JAMAI491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સાળો** — *sāḷo* — "a wife's brother".

> અરુણનો સાળો શહેરમાં નોકરી કરે છે.
>
> *Aruṇno sāḷo śahermā̃ nokrī kare che.*
>
> Arun's wife's brother works in the city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાળો** (*sāḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *syāla*, "wife's brother"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 13871).
