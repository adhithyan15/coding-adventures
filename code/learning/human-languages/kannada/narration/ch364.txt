Kannada, chapter 364: Writing Lives.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ದಿನಚರಿ (dinacari) — a diary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to emerge, then the Kannada for to become visible.

You'll want to know: ದಿನಚರಿ.
ದಿನಚರಿ — dinacari — "a diary".
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ರಾತ್ರಿ ದಿನಚರಿ ಬರೆಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು. ajji pratidina rātri dinacari bareyuttiddaru. Grandmother used to write her diary every night.
Plural: ದಿನಚರಿಗಳು, dinacarigaḷu.
It also means a daily routine: ನನ್ನ ದಿನಚರಿ (dinacari) ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಶುರು.

The word, taken apart.
ದಿನಚರಿ (dinacari) is borrowed from Sanskrit dinacaryā, "daily work" (Monier-Williams), from dina "a day" and caryā "conduct" (CDIAL 4710).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಆತ್ಮಕಥೆ (ātmakathe) — an autobiography.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to become visible, then the Kannada for a diary.

You'll want to know: ಆತ್ಮಕಥೆ.
ಆತ್ಮಕಥೆ — ātmakathe — "an autobiography".
ಆ ಆಟಗಾರನ ಆತ್ಮಕಥೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಜನಪ್ರಿಯವಾಯಿತು. ā āṭagārana ātmakathe tumbā janapriyavāyitu. That player's autobiography became very popular.
Plural: ಆತ್ಮಕಥೆಗಳು, ātmakathegaḷu.
ಆತ್ಮ, self, and ಕಥೆ, story: the story of oneself.

The word, taken apart.
ಆತ್ಮಕಥೆ (ātmakathe) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of ātman, "breath, self" (CDIAL 1135), and kathā, "a story" (CDIAL 2704).

ನಾನು ಆ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಿಯ ಜೀವನಚರಿತ್ರೆ ಓದಿದೆ. nānu ā vijñāniya jīvanacaritre ōdide. I read that scientist's biography.

The word, taken apart.
ಜೀವನಚರಿತ್ರೆ (jīvanacaritre) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of jīvana, "life" (CDIAL 5243), and caritra, "going, behaviour, deeds" (CDIAL 4691).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪೀಠಿಕೆ (pīṭhike) — a preface; an introduction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an autobiography, then the Kannada for a biography.

You'll want to know: ಪೀಠಿಕೆ.
ಪೀಠಿಕೆ — pīṭhike — "a preface; an introduction".
ಪುಸ್ತಕದ ಪೀಠಿಕೆಯನ್ನು ಲೇಖಕರ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ ಬರೆದನು. pustakada pīṭhikeyannu lēkhakara snēhita baredanu. The book's preface was written by the author's friend.
The opening part that comes before the story; also the background to a talk.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೀಠಿಕೆ (pīṭhike) is borrowed from Sanskrit pīṭhikā, "a stool, a base, a pedestal" (Monier-Williams): the base a book stands on.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತಿರುಳು (tiruḷu) — the gist; the core.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a biography, then the Kannada for a preface.

You'll want to know: ತಿರುಳು.
ತಿರುಳು — tiruḷu — "the gist; the core".
ಅವನ ಮಾತಿನ ತಿರುಳು ಇಷ್ಟೇ, ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಸೇರಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡಬೇಕು. avana mātina tiruḷu iṣṭē, ellarū sēri kelasa māḍabēku. The gist of his talk was this: everyone must work together.
Also the kernel of a fruit or a nut.

The word, taken apart.
ತಿರುಳು (tiruḷu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3252 lists Kannada tiruḷ, tiruḷe, "the essential part, strength, value".
