## You'll want to know: aterrizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C654-AVION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aterrizar** — "to land".

> Vamos a aterrizar en diez minutos.
>
> *We are going to land in ten minutes.*

The noun is *el aterrizaje*. The z becomes c before e: *aterricé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aterrizar* is built on *tierra* "land", which is inherited from Latin *terra* (EtymDB), perhaps after French *atterrir* "to land".
