## You'll want to know: बदतमीज़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C471-VINAMRA471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बदतमीज़** — *badtamīz* — "rude; ill-mannered".

> वह लड़का बहुत बदतमीज़ है, बड़ों का आदर नहीं करता।
>
> *vah laṛkā bahut badtamīz hai, baṛoṁ kā ādar nahīṃ kartā.*
>
> That boy is very rude; he doesn't respect his elders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बदतमीज़** (*badtamīz*) is usually traced to Persian *bad*, "bad", joined to Arabic *tamīz*, "discernment, good manners", which came through Persian: having bad manners.
