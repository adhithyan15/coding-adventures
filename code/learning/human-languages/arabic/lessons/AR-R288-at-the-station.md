# Review — at the station, when things go wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C287-TARIQ287-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-IMPERATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-DIRECTIONS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask the way to the airport. (*Min faḍlik, kayfa aṣilu ilā
al-maṭār?*) Then give the answer yourself: "go straight on, then cross the
bridge". (*Idhhab mustaqīman, thumma uʿbur al-jisr*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C288-COUNTER-QUESTIONS-01, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-02, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-03, AR-GRAMMAR-C288-PROBLEM-PAST-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C288-ASK-HELP-01] -->

Five lessons, and a whole trip through a station. At the counter: *tadhkirat
dhahāb wa-iyāb*, *bi-kam?*, *min ayyi raṣīf?* When it goes wrong: *fātanī
al-qiṭār*, *ḍāʿat ḥaqībatī*, *taʾakhkharat aṭ-ṭāʾira*, and *mādhā yajibu an
aʿmala?* And if nobody can help, *al-masʾūl*.

[PAUSE 2s] The hamza now has three of its seats in your hand: **أ** in **سأل**,
**ؤ** in **سؤال** and **مسؤول**, **ئ** in **طائرة**. One sound, three ways
to write it.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C288-HAMZA-YAA-01, AR-SCRIPT-C285-HAMZA-WAW-01, AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-02, AR-LEX-C281-AMAL281-02, AR-LEX-C280-AMAL280-04, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-AS-SABAB-HUWA-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-LAN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-FUTURE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "A return ticket, please. From which platform?"]
- [YOU SAY: "Excuse me, I missed the plane. What should I do?" — *ʿafwan, fātatnī aṭ-ṭāʾira. mādhā yajibu an aʿmala?*]
- [YOU SAY: "My luggage is lost. Who is in charge here?"]
- [YOU SAY: "I won't travel today; the reason is that the train is late" — *lan usāfira al-yawm; as-sababu huwa anna al-qiṭāra mutaʾakhkhir*]
- [YOU SAY: "I'll take the bus" — *sa-ākhudhu al-ḥāfila*]
- [YOU SAY: "The pilot was on a phone call" — *kāna aṭ-ṭayyār fī mukālama*]
- [YOU WRITE: **سؤال**, **مسؤول** and **طائرة**, and circle each seat]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-01, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-03, AR-GRAMMAR-C288-PROBLEM-PAST-01, AR-SCRIPT-C288-HAMZA-YAA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "One way only"? (***Tadhkirat dhahāb faqaṭ.***) "My bag is
lost"? (***Ḍāʿat ḥaqībatī.***) "The person in charge"? (***Al-masʾūl.***)
Which seat does the hamza take in **طائرة**? (**ئ**, the dotless tooth.)
