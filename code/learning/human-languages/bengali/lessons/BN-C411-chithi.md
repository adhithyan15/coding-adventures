## You'll want to know: চিঠি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C411-DAKGHOR411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**চিঠি** — *chiṭhi* — "a letter (sent by post)".

> মামা বিদেশ থেকে একটা চিঠি লিখলেন।
>
> *māmā bidesh theke ekṭā chiṭhi likhlen.*
>
> My uncle wrote a letter from abroad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চিঠি** (*chiṭhi*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ciṭhi*, "a letter", under the reconstructed *ciṣṭa* (CDIAL 4832).
