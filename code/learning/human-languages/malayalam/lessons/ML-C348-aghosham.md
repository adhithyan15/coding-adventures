# ആഘോഷം (āghōṣaṁ) — a celebration

## You'll want to know: ആഘോഷം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C348-AGHOSHAM-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C141-FESTIVALS-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-ETTAVUM-01, ML-LEX-C126-ABOUTYOU-02, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-UNDAYIRUNNU-01] -->

**ആഘോഷം** — *āghōṣaṁ* — "a celebration, festivities".

> **ഓണം കേരളത്തിലെ ഏറ്റവും വലിയ ആഘോഷമാണ്.**
>
> *ōṇaṁ kēraḷattile ēṟṟavuṁ valiya āghōṣamāṇŭ.* — "Onam is the biggest celebration in Kerala."

> **കല്യാണത്തിന് വലിയ ആഘോഷം ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു.**
>
> *kalyāṇattinŭ valiya āghōṣaṁ uṇṭāyirunnu.* — "There was a big celebration at the wedding."

**ഉത്സവം** is the festival itself, often at a temple; **ആഘോഷം** is the celebrating, at a festival,
a wedding or a birthday. The verb is **ആഘോഷിക്കുക**, "to celebrate": **ഞങ്ങൾ ഓണം ആഘോഷിച്ചു**.

Its second consonant is new. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആഘോഷം** (*āghōṣaṁ*) is a Sanskrit word, *āghoṣa*, which Monier-Williams glosses as "a calling out,
a proclaiming". In Malayalam it has come to mean the noise and gathering of a celebration; how the
sense moved is not recorded there.
