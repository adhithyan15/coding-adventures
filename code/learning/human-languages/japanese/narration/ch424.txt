Japanese, chapter 424: Lost and Found.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
おとしもの (otoshimono) — a lost item; something dropped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to treat someone, then the Japanese for a full stomach.

You'll want to know: おとしもの.
おとしもの — otoshimono — "a lost item; something dropped".
こうばんに おとしものを とどけました。 — kōban ni otoshimono o todokemashita — "I took something I had found to the police box."

The word, taken apart.
おとしもの (otoshimono) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from otoshi, "dropping" (from otosu) and mono, "thing"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
