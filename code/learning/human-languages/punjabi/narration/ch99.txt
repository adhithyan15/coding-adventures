Punjabi, chapter 99: Midnight, Daybreak, Day after tomorrow, Summer, Winter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਅੱਧੀ ਰਾਤ (adhī rāt) — midnight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a way, a route, then the Punjabi for a country.
[pause 1 second]
Say "I live in Amritsar." (Maiṁ Amritsar rahindā hāṁ: the place-name needs no "in," and the verb comes last.)

You'll want to know: ਅੱਧੀ ਰਾਤ.
ਅੱਧੀ ਰਾਤ — adhī rāt — "midnight".

What you've built.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
