## Script — the right piece first, then the walking piece under it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-TONG-THROUGH-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 通

**Two pieces, and the walking piece comes last.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The right piece, seven strokes.** A short bar that turns down to the left, and a dot under its end. A vertical down the left. A bar across the top that turns down the right side and hooks to the left at the foot. Two short bars inside. Then a vertical down through the middle.

**The walking piece, three strokes**, as in 这: a dot, a stroke that zigzags down, then a long sweep along the foot under the whole character.

7 + 3 = ten.
