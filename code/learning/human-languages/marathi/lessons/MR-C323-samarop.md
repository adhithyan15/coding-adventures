## You'll want to know: समारोप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C323-NIROP323-05]; assesses=[] -->

**समारोप** — *samārop* — "a closing; a conclusion".

> संमेलनाचा समारोप एका गाण्याने झाला.
>
> *sammelanācā samārop ekā gāṇyāne jhālā.*
>
> The gathering closed with a song.

Masculine.

समारोप करणे, to close an event.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समारोप** (*samārop*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *samāropa*, 'a placing upon' (Monier-Williams); the Marathi sense, the close of an event, is its own.
