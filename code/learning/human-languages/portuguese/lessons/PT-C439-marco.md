## You'll want to know: o marco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C439-CARDEAIS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o marco** — "the landmark; the milestone; the boundary stone".

> A torre é um marco da cidade.
>
> *The tower is a landmark of the town.*

*Um marco do correio* — a pillar box. *Um marco histórico* — a milestone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marco* comes from Medieval Latin *marcus*, from Germanic *markō* 'boundary, border' (EtymDB): first a stone that marked the edge of a field.
