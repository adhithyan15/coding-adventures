## You'll want to know: ਉਧਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C258-LOAN258-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਧਾਰ** — *udhār* — "credit; money lent".

> ਮੈਂ ਉਧਾਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਲੈਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ udhār nahīṁ laindā hāṁ.*
>
> I do not take things on credit.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਧਾਰ** (*udhār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *uddhāra*, "raising; a loan"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *udhār*, "loan", under it (CDIAL 2018).
