## You'll want to know: orīgō, orīginis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C344-MAIORES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**orīgō, orīginis** — "an origin, a beginning; ancestry".

> Dē orīgine urbis multae fābulae nārrantur.
>
> *Many stories are told about the origin of the city.*

Feminine, 3rd declension, from *orior*, 'to rise'. English *origin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**orīgō** is built on *orior*, "to rise, to arise" (Lewis & Short): the rising, the source. English *origin* comes from it.
