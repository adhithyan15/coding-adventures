## You'll want to know: सवेरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C441-SAVERA441-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सवेरा** — *saverā* — "early morning, daybreak".

> हम सवेरे पाँच बजे निकले।
>
> *ham savere pā̃c baje nikle.*
>
> We set off at five in the early morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सवेरा** (*saverā*) goes back to *\*savēla*, "in time, early", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 13291), which lists Hindi *saverā*, "early morning".
