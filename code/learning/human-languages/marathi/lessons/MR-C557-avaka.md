## You'll want to know: आवाका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C557-KUVAT557-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आवाका** — *āvākā* — "scope; reach".

> हे काम माझ्या आवाक्याबाहेर आहे.
>
> *he kām mājhyā āvākyābāher āhe.*
>
> This job is beyond my reach.

Masculine.

The oblique is आवाक्या-: आवाक्याबाहेर, beyond reach.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आवाका** (*āvākā*) is a Marathi word for the reach of one's ability; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
