# स्त — two known consonants, now joined

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ASTI, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-05, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-107, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-108] -->

Read **अस्ति** and say its meaning: *asti*, “is.” Point to **स**, the virama
**◌्**, and **त**. Every sign and the word itself are already known; this lesson
only makes their join visible.

## Writing — observe and trace the conjunct
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-05, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-107, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-108] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Watch the pieces close up: **स + ◌् + त → स्त**. The virama removes the *a*
from **स**, so the consonants touch: *s* followed by *ta*.

Keep **स्त** visible. Trace the printed join once with a fingertip, then once
with a pencil on tracing paper. Preserve the headline and the smaller first
consonant; do not add an *a* between the two sounds.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ASTI] -->

What does the join inside **अस्ति** say? (**s + ta**, with no vowel after the
first consonant.)
