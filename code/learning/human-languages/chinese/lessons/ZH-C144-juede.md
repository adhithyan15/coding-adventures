## You'll want to know: juéde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C144-MATTER144-05]; assesses=[] -->

*juéde* — "to find (that); to feel; to think (that)".

> *wǒ juéde hànyǔ hěn yǒuyìsi* — I find Chinese interesting.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juéde* is built from *jué*, glossed 'to understand' and 'to wake', and *de*, from a word glossed 'to get', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewk* and *tok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
