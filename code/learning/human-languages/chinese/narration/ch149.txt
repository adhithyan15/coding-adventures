Lesson 1 of 6.

You'll want to know: 正在.
正在 — zhèngzài — "in the middle of (doing)".
我正在吃饭 wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn — I'm eating right now.
他正在看书 tā zhèngzài kàn shū — He's reading at the moment.
The next lesson puts the first character in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
正在 (zhèngzài) is built from 正 (zhèng) 'exactly' and 在 'at', the 在 you write. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, tsyengH and dzojX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'exactly fitting; proper' and 'to dwell; to exist'. The use before a verb, in the middle of doing it, is the modern one and is assumed here, not quoted.

What you've built.
正在 (zhèngzài) goes before the verb: 我, 正在 (zhèngzài), 吃饭.
在 without 正 (zhèng) can do the same job before a verb; that comes two lessons on.

Script — 一 on top, then the middle of 上 on a long bar.
正 (zhèng).
一 on top, then four strokes, two of them shaped as in 上.
一, a bar across the top.
A vertical down the middle, from under the bar to the foot.
A short bar to its right, halfway down: with the vertical, the upper part of 上.
A short vertical on the left, from the height of that bar down to the foot.
The long bar along the foot, last, under everything.
1 + 4 = five.

Lesson 3 of 6.
你在做什么 (nǐ zài zuò shénme) — 在 before a verb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
在 again, and this time it comes before a verb.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 正在, aloud — zhèngzài]
[your turn — recall: say 你呢 — nǐ ne, and you?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Grammar Lens: 在 before a place, 在 before a verb.
在 before a place says where. 在 before a verb says what is going on right now.
他在家 tā zài jiā — He's at home. 他在看书 tā zài kàn shū — He's reading.
正在 says it more firmly, in the very middle of it: 我正在吃饭 (wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn). 呢 at the end can round off either one:
他在看书呢 tā zài kàn shū ne — He's busy reading.
The negative is 没在, since 没 reports what is not going on:
我没在看书 wǒ méi zài kàn shū — I'm not reading.
你在做什么 nǐ zài zuò shénme — What are you doing?

Lesson 4 of 6.
正好 (zhènghǎo) — exactly right; as it happens.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: read the last lesson's question, then the word for good.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 你在做什么, aloud — nǐ zài zuò shénme]
[your turn — recall: say 好 — hǎo, good — R4, recognition at distance]

You'll want to know: 正好.
正好 — zhènghǎo — "exactly right; as it happens".
正好 我也要去 zhènghǎo, wǒ yě yào qù — As it happens, I'm going too.
这个正好 zhège zhènghǎo — This one is exactly right.

The word, taken apart.
正好 (zhènghǎo) is built from 正 (zhèng) 'exactly' and 好 'good': exactly right. 好 is inherited from Middle Chinese xawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'good; fine'.

What you've built.
正好 (zhènghǎo) can open a sentence, a happy coincidence: 正好 (zhènghǎo), 我也要去.
Or it can follow a thing, which then fits: 这个正好.

Lesson 5 of 6.
一边 (yìbiān) — at the same time.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: 一边.
一边 — yìbiān — "at the same time", said twice, before two verbs; also "one side".
他一边吃饭一边看书 tā yìbiān chī fàn yìbiān kàn shū — He reads while he eats.
一 falls before the level tone of biān: yìbiān, as in yì tiān.

The word, taken apart.
一边 (yìbiān) is built from 一 'one' and 边 'side', the 边 you write. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit and pen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), pen glossed 'an edge; a border'.

What you've built.
Said twice, each straight before its own verb, 一边 (yìbiān) … 一边 (yìbiān) … puts two actions side by side: 一边吃饭, 一边看书.
The person is said once, at the front: 他一边吃饭一边看书.

The exchange.
A: 你在做什么 nǐ zài zuò shénme — What are you doing? B: 我正在吃饭 你呢 wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn, nǐ ne — I'm eating. And you? A: 我在看书 wǒ zài kàn shū — I'm reading. B: 你妈妈呢 nǐ māma ne — And your mum? A: 妈妈在做饭 māma zài zuò fàn — She's cooking. B: nǐ bàba ne — And your dad? A: 他没在家 他在开车 tā méi zài jiā, tā zài kāichē — He isn't home. He's driving. B: 我一边吃饭一边打电话 wǒ yìbiān chī fàn yìbiān dǎ diànhuà — I'm eating and on the phone at once. A: 正好 我也在吃饭 zhènghǎo, wǒ yě zài chī fàn — As it happens, I'm eating too.
Every answer keeps 在 before its verb. 他没在家 is 在 before a place, at home; 他在开车 is 在 before a verb, driving.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]

What is A's mum doing?

What you've built — now, and two things at once.
在 or 正在 (zhèngzài) before the verb says what is going on now:
我正在吃饭 wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn — I'm eating right now.
他在开车 tā zài kāichē — he's driving.
白天我在看书 báitiān wǒ zài kàn shū — in the daytime I'm reading.
bànyè tā zhèngzài shuìjiào — in the middle of the night he's asleep.
Listen. Nǐ zài zuò shénme ne?
