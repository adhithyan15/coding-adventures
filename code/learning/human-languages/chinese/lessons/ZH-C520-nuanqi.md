## You'll want to know: nuǎnqì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C520-APPLIANCE520-04]; assesses=[] -->

*nuǎnqì* — "central heating".

> *yǒu nuǎnqì de fángjiān bǐ wàibian nuǎnhuo* — A room with heating is warmer than outside.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nuǎnqì* is built from *nuǎn* 'warm' and 气 'air', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nwanX* and *khj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary gives the first as the same word as another spelling glossed 'the warmth of a fire', and the second 'breath'.
