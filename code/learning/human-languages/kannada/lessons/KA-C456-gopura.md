# ಗೋಪುರ (gōpura) — a tower; a temple tower

## You'll want to know: ಗೋಪುರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C456-SMARAKA456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೋಪುರ** — *gōpura* — "a tower; a temple tower".

> ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನದ ಗೋಪುರ ದೂರದಿಂದಲೇ ಕಾಣುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *dēvasthānada gōpura dūradindalē kāṇuttade.*
>
> The temple tower can be seen from far away.

ಗಡಿಯಾರ ಗೋಪುರ, a clock tower.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೋಪುರ** (*gōpura*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gōpura*, "a town gate" (Monier-Williams); in South India it names the tall tower over a temple gate.
