# Put the new went form after a familiar pronoun

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-PRON-OH-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Retrieve the familiar pronoun for “they,” then the new masculine
plural form for “went.”

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-PRON-OH-01] -->

> **ਉਹ ਗਏ** — *oh gae* — they went

For this one frame, put familiar **ਉਹ** before the new whole form **ਗਏ**. In a
different context the same masculine plural form can respectfully refer to one
masculine person. Nothing else in the perfective or agreement system is added.
