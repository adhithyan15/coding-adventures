## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-CAMBIAR]; assesses=[] -->

**cambiar** — "to change" or "to exchange."

It comes from Latin **cambiāre**, which the Academy identifies as a word of
Gaulish origin. Its exchange sense makes the core visible: leave one thing and
take another. On a journey that may mean another train, route, or destination.

- **cambiar de tren** — change trains;
- **cambiar de dirección** — change direction;
- **cambiar el destino** — change the destination.

English **change** reached its present form by a different Romance route, but
it belongs to this same old family of exchange.
