## You'll want to know: la connessione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C402-RETE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la connessione** — "the connection".

> Se la connessione è lenta, il video si ferma.
>
> *If the connection is slow, the video stops.*

Also a tie between ideas.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*connessione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnexiō, cōnexiōnis* "a binding together" (EtymDB), from *cōnectere* "to tie together", built on *nectere* "to tie".
