Kannada, chapter 146: Bad, Terrible, Brave, Golden, Silver.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕೆಟ್ಟ (keṭṭa) — bad, spoiled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for lazy, then the Kannada for clever.

You'll want to know: ಕೆಟ್ಟ.
ಕೆಟ್ಟ — keṭṭa — "bad, spoiled".

The word, taken apart.
ಕೆಟ್ಟ (keṭṭa) is native Dravidian, from keḍu, "to be spoiled, become bad", which DEDR 1942 lists; keṭṭa is its past form, "spoiled"; the entry also has Tamil keṭu and Malayalam keṭu.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಚಿನ್ನದ.
ಚಿನ್ನದ — cinnada — "golden".

The word, taken apart.
ಚಿನ್ನದ (cinnada) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil ciṉṉam (DEDR 2596). Cinnada is "of gold", from cinna, "gold", which DEDR lists with Tamil ciṉṉam, "gold coin".
