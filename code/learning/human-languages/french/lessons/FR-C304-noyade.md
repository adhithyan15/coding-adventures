## You'll want to know: la noyade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C304-NOYADE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la noyade** — "drowning, a drowning".

> Il y a beaucoup de noyades en été.
>
> *There are a lot of drownings in summer.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*noyade* is built on *noyer* "to drown" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *necāre* "to kill".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
