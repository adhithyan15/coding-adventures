## You'll want to know: प्रभञ्जनः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C439-PAVANA439-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रभञ्जनः** — *prabhañjanaḥ* — "a tempest".

> रात्रौ प्रभञ्जनेन वृक्षाः पतन्ति।
>
> *rātrau prabhañjanena vṛkṣāḥ patanti.*
>
> At night trees fall in the tempest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रभञ्जनः** (*prabhañjanaḥ*) is built from *pra* and the root √bhañj, "to break": the breaker; MW gives *prabhañjana* as wind, storm, tempest, hurricane (MW).
