## You'll want to know: సామరస్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C409-AIKYATA409-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సామరస్యం** — *sāmarasyaṁ* — "harmony; concord".

> మా వీధిలో అందరూ సామరస్యంగా ఉంటారు.
>
> *mā vīdhilō andarū sāmarasyaṅgā uṇṭāru.*
>
> Everyone in our street lives in harmony.

సామరస్యంగా, in harmony, peaceably.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సామరస్యం** (*sāmarasyaṁ*) is built on Sanskrit *samarasa*, "having equal feelings" (Monier-Williams), from *sama*, "same", and *rasa*, "feeling".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
