# دبکنا (dubaknā) — to crouch out of sight

## You'll want to know: دبکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C445-FEAR445-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دبکنا** — *dubaknā* — "to crouch out of sight".

> بلی ڈر کر کرسی کے نیچے دبک گئی۔
>
> *billī ḍar kar kursī ke nīche dubak gaī.*
>
> The cat took fright and crouched under the chair.

Past (he …): وہ دبک گیا, *voh dubak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دبکنا** (*dubaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *dabaknā*, "to be hidden", under *dabb*, "press", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6173).
