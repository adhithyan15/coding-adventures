# ಘರ್ಜಿಸು (gharjisu) — to roar

## You'll want to know: ಘರ್ಜಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C477-PRANI477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘರ್ಜಿಸು** — *gharjisu* — "to roar".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ಕಾಡಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಸಿಂಹ ಘರ್ಜಿಸಿತು.
>
> *rātri kāḍinalli siṃha gharjisitu.*
>
> At night a lion roared in the forest.

Past (it …): ಘರ್ಜಿಸಿತು, *gharjisitu*.

Of lions and tigers, and of thunder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘರ್ಜಿಸು** (*gharjisu*) is built from Sanskrit *garj*, "to thunder, to roar" (CDIAL 4046, *garjati*), with the verb ending -*isu*; Kannada writes it with the aspirate ಘ.
