## You'll want to know: લાયક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C543-APVAD543-03]; assesses=[] -->

**લાયક** — *lāyak* — "deserving".

> જો તમે લાયક હશો તો તમને બઢતી મળશે.
>
> *jo tame lāyak haśo to tamne baḍhtī maḷśe.*
>
> If you deserve it, you will get a promotion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાયક** (*lāyak*) is usually traced to Arabic *lāyiq*, "worthy, fitting", through Persian. The quality it names, **લાયકાત**, is a qualification.
