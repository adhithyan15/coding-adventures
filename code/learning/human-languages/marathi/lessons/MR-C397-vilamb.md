## You'll want to know: विलंब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C397-VILAMB397-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विलंब** — *vilamb* — "a delay".

> पावसामुळे गाडीला विलंब झाला.
>
> *pāvsāmuḷe gāḍīlā vilamb jhālā.*
>
> The train was delayed because of the rain.

Masculine.

विलंब होणे, to be delayed: what is late takes -ला, गाडीला विलंब झाला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विलंब** (*vilamb*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vilamba*, "hanging down; slowness, delay" (Monier-Williams).
