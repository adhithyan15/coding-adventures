## You'll want to know: बुजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C456-SANKOC456-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बुजणे** — *bujṇe* — "to be bashful".

> पाहुणे आले की आमचा मुलगा बुजतो.
>
> *pāhuṇe āle kī āmcā mulgā bujto.*
>
> Our son goes shy when guests come.

Past (he …): तो बुजला, *to bujlā*.

Of a shy child, and of a horse that shies; बुजरा, bashful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुजणे** (*bujṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
