Arabic, chapter 202: Usually, Immediately, Later, Date, Moment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غالبا (ghāliban) — usually, mostly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a burn, then the Arabic for a stomach cramp.

You'll want to know: غالبا.
غالبا — ghāliban — "usually, mostly".

The word, taken apart.
غالبا (ghāliban) is built on the native Arabic root gh-l-b, "to overcome" (Lane). Ghāliban is the active participle, "prevailing", with the adverb ending -an.

You'll want to know: فورا.
فورا — fawran — "immediately".

The word, taken apart.
فورا (fawran) is built on the native Arabic root f-w-r, "to boil, to gush" (Lane). Fawran is fawr, "the first moment of boiling", with the adverb ending -an: "at once".

Lesson 4 of 5.
تاريخ (tārīkh) — a date; history.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for immediately, then the Arabic for later.

You'll want to know: تاريخ.
تاريخ — tārīkh — "a date; history".

The word, taken apart.
تاريخ (tārīkh) is disputed in Lane's sources: some call it arabicized, borrowed from the people of the Bible, and Lane compares Hebrew yeraḥ, "month"; others give it a native root ʾ-r-kh, "to date". The origin is not settled.
