## You'll want to know: el crimen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C520-DELITO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el crimen** — "the crime (serious)".

> Nadie vio el crimen.
>
> *Nobody saw the crime.*

The plural moves the accent: *los crímenes*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crimen* is a learned borrowing from Latin *crīmen* 'a verdict, a charge, a crime' (EtymDB).
