## You'll want to know: घुँघरू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C531-DHOLAK531-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घुँघरू** — *ghũghrū* — "ankle bells".

> उसके पैरों के घुँघरू बज रहे थे।
>
> *Uske pairoṁ ke ghũghrū baj rahe the.*
>
> The bells on her feet were ringing.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घुँघरू** (*ghũghrū*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*ghuṅghura*, "a string of bells", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4477).
