## Script — two signs, neither new
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-OH-01]; assesses=[PA-PRON-OH-01] -->

> **ਉ** — the *u* carrier · **ਹ** — *ha*

Both taught long ago. **ਉ** is the carrier you met when the book first showed a
vowel standing on its own, and **ਹ** you have written since **ਨਹੀਂ** at the top
of this run.

## Writing — guided copy, model in view
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-OH-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **ਉਹ** once with the model in view. Compare its first letter against the
**ਅ** of **ਅਤੇ** — two different carriers, and the difference matters.
