## You'll want to know: facoltativo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C384-NORMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**facoltativo** — "optional".

> La lezione del sabato è facoltativa.
>
> *The Saturday lesson is optional.*

Built on *facoltà* in the sense of free choice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*facoltativo* is built on *facoltà* "power, option", a learned borrowing from Latin *facultās* "ability, means" (Lewis & Short): left to your own choice.
