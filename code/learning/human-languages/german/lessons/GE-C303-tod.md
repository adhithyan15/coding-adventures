## You'll want to know: der Tod
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C303-BEERDIGUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Tod** — "the death".

> Nach dem Tod seiner Frau lebte er allein.
>
> *After the death of his wife he lived alone.*

The plural is rare. The adjective is *tot*, already known.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tod* is inherited from Old High German *tōd*, from Proto-Germanic *dauþuz* "death" (EtymDB), the same source as English *death*.
