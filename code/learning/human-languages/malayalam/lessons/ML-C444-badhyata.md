## You'll want to know: ബാധ്യത
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C444-KATAMA444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ബാധ്യത** — *bādhyata* — "a liability; an obligation".

> കടം വീട്ടാനുള്ള ബാധ്യത മകനാണ്.
>
> *kaṭaṁ vīṭṭānuḷḷa bādhyata makanāṇŭ.*
>
> The obligation to repay the debt is the son's.

A debt is a ബാധ്യത in the sense of a liability.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ബാധ്യത** (*bādhyata*) is built from Sanskrit *bādhya*, "to be pressed hard, to be harassed" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -ത: what presses on a person.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
