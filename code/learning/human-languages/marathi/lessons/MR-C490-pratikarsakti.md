## You'll want to know: प्रतिकारशक्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C490-PATHYA490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिकारशक्ती** — *pratikārśaktī* — "immunity; resistance".

> चांगल्या आहारामुळे प्रतिकारशक्ती वाढते.
>
> *cāṅglyā āhārāmuḷe pratikārśaktī vāḍhte.*
>
> A good diet builds up immunity.

Feminine.

प्रतिकार, resistance, + शक्ती, power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिकारशक्ती** (*pratikārśaktī*) is a modern learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, *pratikāra*, "requital, counteraction", and *śakti*, "power" (Monier-Williams).
