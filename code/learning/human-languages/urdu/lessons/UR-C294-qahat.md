## You'll want to know: قحط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C294-QAHAT294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قحط** — *qahat* — "a famine".

> اس سال گاؤں میں قحط پڑا۔
>
> *is sāl gāoṅ meṅ qahat paṛā.*
>
> This year there was a famine in the village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قحط** (*qahat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qaḥṭ*, "drought, a failure of rain". A famine is said to fall: **قحط پڑا**.
