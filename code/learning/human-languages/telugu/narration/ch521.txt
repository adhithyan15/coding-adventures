Telugu, chapter 521: Losing Strength.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
బలహీనత (balahīnata) — frailty; weakness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to blow one's nose, then the Telugu for to pick up.

You'll want to know: బలహీనత.
బలహీనత — balahīnata — "frailty; weakness".
జ్వరం తరువాత కొన్ని రోజులు బలహీనత ఉంటుంది. jvaraṁ taruvāta konni rōjulu balahīnata uṇṭundi. After a fever there is weakness for a few days.
బలహీనంగా, weak; బలం, strength, + హీన, lacking.

The word, taken apart.
బలహీనత (balahīnata) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit bala, "strength" (CDIAL 9161), and hīna, "lacking" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త.

జ్వరంతో పాప బాగా నీరసించింది. jvarantō pāpa bāgā nīrasiñcindi. With the fever the little girl grew very weak.
Past (I …): నీరసించాను, nīrasiñcānu.
The verb of నీరసం, weakness.

The word, taken apart.
నీరసించు (nīrasiñcu) is built on Sanskrit nīrasa, "sapless, tasteless" (CDIAL 7556), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు: to lose one's sap.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: చిక్కిపోవు.
చిక్కిపోవు — cikkipōvu — "to grow thin".
జబ్బు తరువాత అతను బాగా చిక్కిపోయాడు. jabbu taruvāta atanu bāgā cikkipōyāḍu. After his illness he grew very thin.
Past (I …): చిక్కిపోయాను, cikkipōyānu.
చిక్కు, to grow lean, + పోవు.

The word, taken apart.
చిక్కిపోవు (cikkipōvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2503 lists Telugu cikku, "to become lean or thin", with Tamil ciṅku, "to wane"; the verb ends in pōvu, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

లావెక్కు — lāvekku — "to put on weight".
పెళ్ళి తరువాత అన్నయ్య లావెక్కాడు. peḷḷi taruvāta annayya lāvekkāḍu. After his wedding my brother put on weight.
Past (I …): లావెక్కాను, lāvekkānu.
లావు, stout, + ఎక్కు, to rise.

The word, taken apart.
లావెక్కు (lāvekku) is a native compound: lāvu, "bigness, fatness" (DEDR 291), and ekku, "to increase, to rise" (DEDR 768).
