You'll want to know: экзамен.
экзамен (ekzámen) — "an exam".

The word, taken apart.
экзамен (ekzámen) — from Peter the Great's time; it came through Polish egzamen from Latin exāmen, "a weighing, a test" (Vasmer).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

[once you have stopped driving — read: the two signs aloud, as one run — лифт направо, метро налево]

You'll want to know: доска.
доска (doská) — "a board".

The word, taken apart.
доска (doská) from Proto-Slavic dŭska, "a board, a table" — borrowed through Germanic (compare Old High German tisc, "table", Old English disc, "dish") from Latin discus, Greek dískos, "a disc" (Vasmer). German Tisch, "table", is the same word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
домашнее задание (domáshneye zadániye) — homework.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a board, then the Russian for a rubber.

You'll want to know: домашнее задание.
домашнее задание (domáshneye zadániye) — "homework".

The word, taken apart.
домашнее задание (domáshneye zadániye) is "a home task": домашнее, from дом, "house", with задание, "a set task", from задать, "to set", on the root of дать, "to give".
