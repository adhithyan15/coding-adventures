Russian, chapter 334: Stuffing and straining.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
фарш (farsh) — mince, minced meat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a seasoning, then the Russian for to marinate.

You'll want to know: фарш.
фарш (farsh) — "mince, minced meat".
Для котлет нужен фарш. Dlya kotlét núzhen farsh. You need mince for meat patties.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
фарш (farsh) was borrowed from French farce "stuffing" (EtymDB), from Latin farcīre "to cram, to stuff".

Lesson 2 of 6.
фаршировать (farshirovát) — to stuff.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to marinate, then the Russian for mince.

You'll want to know: фаршировать.
фаршировать (farshirovát) — "to stuff".
Мама фарширует перец рисом и мясом. Máma farshirúyet pérets rísom i myásom. Mum stuffs peppers with rice and meat.
Imperfective; perfective нафаршировать (nafarshirovát). The filling is in the instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
фаршировать (farshirovát) is built on фарш (farsh) "stuffing", from French farce, Latin farcīre "to stuff" (EtymDB), with the verb ending -ировать.

Lesson 3 of 6.
процеживать (protsézhivat) — to strain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for mince, then the Russian for to stuff.

You'll want to know: процеживать.
процеживать (protsézhivat) — "to strain".
Он процедил бульон. On protsedíl bulón. He strained the broth.
Imperfective; perfective процедить (protsedít), процежу.

The word, taken apart.
процеживать (protsézhivat) is built on про- "through" and цедить "to strain", inherited from Proto-Slavic cěditi "to strain, to filter" (EtymDB).
