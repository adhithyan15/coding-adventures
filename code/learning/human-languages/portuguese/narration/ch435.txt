Portuguese, chapter 435: Orientar-se.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
orientar — to guide, to orient; (orientar-se) to find your bearings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to regret, then the Portuguese for the compensation.

You'll want to know: orientar.
orientar — "to guide, to orient; (orientar-se) to find your bearings".
Sem mapa, oriento-me pelo sol. Without a map, I find my bearings by the sun.
Orientar-se — to find your way. A teacher also orienta a student's work.

The word, taken apart.
orientar is built on oriente 'east', which is inherited from Latin oriēns 'rising', of the sun (EtymDB), from orīrī 'to rise': first to face the east.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: a bússola.
a bússola — "the compass".
Na montanha, levamos sempre uma bússola. In the mountains, we always take a compass.
The needle points north.

The word, taken apart.
bússola was borrowed from Italian bussola, from Late Latin buxula 'little box' (EtymDB), on buxus 'boxwood': the needle sat in a small wooden box.

You'll want to know: desorientado.
desorientado — "lost, disoriented, confused".
Saí do metro completamente desorientado. I came out of the metro completely disoriented.
Also confused about what to do: Estou desorientado com tantas opções.

The word, taken apart.
desorientado is built from des- 'un-' and orientado 'oriented' (EtymDB), on orientar, from Latin oriēns 'rising (sun)'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
o roteiro — the travel plan, the route guide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the compass, then the Portuguese for lost.

You'll want to know: o roteiro.
o roteiro — "the travel plan, the route guide".
Fizemos um roteiro de três dias pela costa. We planned a three-day route along the coast.
In Brazil o roteiro is also a film script.

The word, taken apart.
roteiro is built on rota 'route, course' with -eiro (EtymDB); rota is usually traced to Latin rupta (via) 'broken (road)', from rumpere 'to break' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
localizar — to locate, to find.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for lost, then the Portuguese for the travel plan.

You'll want to know: localizar.
localizar — "to locate, to find".
Não consigo localizar o hotel no mapa. I cannot find the hotel on the map.
Estar localizado — to be located: A casa está bem localizada.

The word, taken apart.
localizar is built on local 'place', a learned borrowing from Late Latin locālis (EtymDB), on locus 'place'.
