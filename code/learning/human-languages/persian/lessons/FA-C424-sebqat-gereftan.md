## You'll want to know: سبقت گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C424-SORAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سبقت گرفتن** (*sebqat gereftan*) — "to overtake (US: to pass)".

> در پیچ هیچ وقت از اتوبوسها سبقت نمیگیرم.
>
> *Dar pich hich vaqt az otobus-hâ sebqat nemigiram.*
>
> I never overtake buses on a bend.

*sebqat* 'going ahead' + *gereftan*; the one overtaken takes *az*: *az u sebqat gereft*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sebqat gereftan* is built from *sebqat* 'getting ahead', a loan usually traced to Arabic *sabq* 'going ahead, a race', and the native verb *gereftan* 'to take'. EtymDB lists *sebqat* without a source.
