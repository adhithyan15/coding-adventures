Kannada, chapter 453: By Sea.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಡಗು (haḍagu) — a ship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to transport, then the Kannada for a courier.

You'll want to know: ಹಡಗು.
ಹಡಗು — haḍagu — "a ship".
ಬಂದರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ದೊಡ್ಡ ಹಡಗು ನಿಂತಿದೆ. bandarinalli ondu doḍḍa haḍagu nintide. A big ship is standing in the harbour.
Plural: ಹಡಗುಗಳು, haḍagugaḷu.
A ದೋಣಿ is a boat; a ಹಡಗು (haḍagu) crosses the sea.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಡಗು (haḍagu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3838 lists Kannada paḍagu, haḍagu, the old p- turned into h-.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ನಾವಿಕ (nāvika) — a sailor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a courier, then the Kannada for a ship.

You'll want to know: ನಾವಿಕ.
ನಾವಿಕ — nāvika — "a sailor".
ನನ್ನ ಅಜ್ಜ ಹಡಗಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಾವಿಕರಾಗಿದ್ದರು. nanna ajja haḍaginalli nāvikarāgiddaru. My grandfather was a sailor on a ship.
Plural: ನಾವಿಕರು, nāvikaru.
Built on Sanskrit nau, a boat, with -ಇಕ, one who works with it: the one who works the boat.

The word, taken apart.
ನಾವಿಕ (nāvika) is borrowed from Sanskrit nāvika, "a sailor, a helmsman" (CDIAL 7082), from nau, "a boat".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಲಂಗರು (laṅgaru) — an anchor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ship, then the Kannada for a sailor.

You'll want to know: ಲಂಗರು.
ಲಂಗರು — laṅgaru — "an anchor".
ಹಡಗು ಬಂದರಿನ ಹೊರಗೆ ಲಂಗರು ಹಾಕಿತು. haḍagu bandarina horage laṅgaru hākitu. The ship dropped anchor outside the harbour.
ಲಂಗರು (laṅgaru) ಹಾಕು, to drop anchor.

The word, taken apart.
ಲಂಗರು (laṅgaru) is usually traced to Persian langar, "an anchor", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿ.
ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿ — diksūci — "a compass".
ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿಯ ಮುಳ್ಳು ಉತ್ತರಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗುತ್ತದೆ. diksūciya muḷḷu uttarakke tiruguttade. The needle of a compass turns to the north.
ದಿಕ್ಕು, a direction, plus ಸೂಚಿ, a pointer.

The word, taken apart.
ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿ (diksūci) is built from two Sanskrit words: diś, "a direction" (CDIAL 6339), and sūci, "a needle" (CDIAL 13551).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

ಹಡಗು ಸಾಗರವನ್ನು ಹತ್ತು ದಿನದಲ್ಲಿ ದಾಟಿತು. haḍagu sāgaravannu hattu dinadalli dāṭitu. The ship crossed the ocean in ten days.
ಸಮುದ್ರ is the sea; ಸಾಗರ (sāgara) is the wider, grander word.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಗರ (sāgara) is borrowed from Sanskrit sāgara, "the ocean" (CDIAL 13325).
