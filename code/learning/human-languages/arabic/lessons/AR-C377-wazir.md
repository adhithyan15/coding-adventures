## You'll want to know: وزير
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C377-HUKUMA377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**وزير** — *wazīr* — "a minister".

> زار الوزير المدرسة أمس.
>
> *zāra al-wazīr al-madrasa ams.*
>
> The minister visited the school yesterday.

Plural: وزراء, *wuzarāʾ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وزير** (*wazīr*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-z-r*, "to bear a heavy load" (Lane): the one who carries the ruler's burden. EtymDB lists a Middle Persian cognate, and some trace the word to Persian, so its origin is disputed.
