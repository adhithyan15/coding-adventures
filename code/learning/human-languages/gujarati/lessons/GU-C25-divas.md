<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-C25-divas-reading","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-DIVAS-01","GU-LEX-DIVAS-01"],"prompt":"Read this word aloud and give its meaning: દિવસ.","answer":"divas — day","accepted":["divas day"],"feedback":{"correct":"You read divas from signs you already knew.","incorrect":"Name the four pieces one at a time, then read the word again."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Uncover **દિવસ** once. Watch the second piece. The **િ** sign is written to the LEFT of **દ** but is read AFTER it, exactly as the forms before your name taught; **વ** and **સ** then follow in order. Name the four pieces aloud in order, then
read the whole word once.
