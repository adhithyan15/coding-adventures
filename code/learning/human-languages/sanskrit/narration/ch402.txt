Sanskrit, chapter 402: Rest Houses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धर्मशाला (dharmaśālā) — a pilgrims' rest house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a lay follower, then the Sanskrit for a memorial shrine.

You'll want to know: धर्मशाला.
धर्मशाला — dharmaśālā — "a pilgrims' rest house".
यात्रिकाः धर्मशालायां रात्रिं वसन्ति। yātrikāḥ dharmaśālāyāṃ rātriṃ vasanti. The pilgrims stay the night in the rest house.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
धर्मशाला (dharmaśālā) is built from dharma, "right conduct, charity", and śālā, "a hall, a house"; MW gives the compound as a charitable asylum, especially a religious one (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
निषीदति (niṣīdati) — sits down to rest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a pilgrims' rest house, then the Sanskrit for a hut of leaves.

You'll want to know: निषीदति.
निषीदति — niṣīdati — "sits down to rest".
श्रान्तः पथिकः वृक्षस्य छायायां निषीदति। śrāntaḥ pathikaḥ vṛkṣasya chāyāyāṃ niṣīdati. The tired wayfarer sits down to rest in the shade of a tree.
Past (he …): सः छायायां निषण्णवान्, saḥ chāyāyāṃ niṣaṇṇavān.

The word, taken apart.
निषीदति (niṣīdati) is built from ni, "down", and the root √sad, "to sit"; MW gives ni-ṣad as to sit down, rest or settle, with the participle niṣaṇṇa, "seated, resting" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
संविशति (saṃviśati) — lies down to sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a hut of leaves, then the Sanskrit for sits down to rest.

You'll want to know: संविशति.
संविशति — saṃviśati — "lies down to sleep".
रात्रौ यात्रिकाः धर्मशालायां संविशन्ति। rātrau yātrikāḥ dharmaśālāyāṃ saṃviśanti. At night the pilgrims lie down to sleep in the rest house.
Past (he …): सः धर्मशालायां संविष्टवान्, saḥ dharmaśālāyāṃ saṃviṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
संविशति (saṃviśati) is built from sam, "together", and the root √viś, "to enter, to settle"; MW gives saṃ-viś as to lie down or go to rest, and its participle saṃviṣṭa as one who has lain down, sleeping (MW).

स्वामी अतिथये पाद्यं ददाति। svāmī atithaye pādyaṃ dadāti. The host gives the guest water to wash his feet.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पाद्यम् (pādyam) is built on pāda, "a foot", with the ending -ya; MW gives pādya as belonging to the foot and, as a noun, water for washing the feet, offered to a guest (MW).
