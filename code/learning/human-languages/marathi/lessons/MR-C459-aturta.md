## You'll want to know: आतुरता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C459-ODH459-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आतुरता** — *āturtā* — "eagerness".

> निकालाची सर्वांना आतुरता होती.
>
> *nikālācī sarvānnā āturtā hotī.*
>
> Everyone was eager for the result.

Feminine.

आतुर, eager, + -ता; आतुरतेने वाट पाहणे, to wait eagerly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आतुरता** (*āturtā*) is built on the tatsama आतुर, Sanskrit *ātura*, glossed "suffering, sick" (Monier-Williams); Marathi आतुर has moved to "eager", and *-tā* makes the noun.
