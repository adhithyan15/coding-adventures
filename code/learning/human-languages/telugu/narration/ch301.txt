Telugu, chapter 301: Dictionary, Cart, Auto-rickshaw, Taxi, Petrol.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నిఘంటువు (nighaṇṭuvu) — a dictionary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a button, then the Telugu for a handkerchief.

You'll want to know: నిఘంటువు.
నిఘంటువు — nighaṇṭuvu — "a dictionary".

The word, taken apart.
నిఘంటువు (nighaṇṭuvu) is Sanskrit nighaṇṭu, "glossary, word list", with the Telugu ending -vu.
