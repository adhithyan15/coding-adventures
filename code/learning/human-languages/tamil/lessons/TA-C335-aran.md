## You'll want to know: அரண்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C335-KOTTAI335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**அரண்** — *araṇ* — "a fortification; a defence".

> அந்த மலை நகரத்துக்கு ஒரு இயற்கை அரணாக இருந்தது.
>
> *anta malai nakarattukku oru iyaṟkai araṇāka iruntatu.*
>
> That hill was a natural defence for the city.

-ஆக, as: அரணாக இருந்தது, it served as a defence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அரண்** (*araṇ*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *śaraṇa*, "protecting, shelter" (CDIAL 12326), as DEDR 201 notes.
