Spanish, chapter 414: Sign Up at Reception.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
apuntarse — put your name on the list.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall el punto, a point or item. Spanish builds apuntar from that family, and apuntarse can mean to put your own name down: to sign up.

The word, taken apart.
The base punto goes back to Latin punctum, a mark made by a prick. To apuntar is to note or point something; with se, apuntarse often means to add oneself to a list. The root is already anchored in punto, so this lesson extends that family.

Grammar Lens: who signs up?
The pronoun changes with the person: me apunto for “I sign up” and apuntarse after another verb, as in hay que apuntarse, “one must sign up.” Use a for the activity: apuntarse a una clase.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: apuntarse]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: me apunto a la clase]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hay que apuntarse antes]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To sign up? (Apuntarse.) One must sign up beforehand? (Hay que apuntarse antes.)
Source: DLE: apuntar.


Lesson 2 of 4.
la recepción — the place that receives you.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall apuntarse, to put your name on a list. At a sports centre, the list may be kept at la recepción, the reception desk.

The word, taken apart.
Latin receptio meant a receiving. Spanish recepción and English reception belong to the same family. The place receives visitors, questions and sign-up requests.

Grammar Lens: place after en.
Use en la recepción for “at the reception desk.” The written accent marks the final stress: re-cep-CIÓN. A useful notice says Hay que apuntarse en la recepción.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: la recepción]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: en la recepción]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hay que apuntarse en la recepción]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Reception desk? (La recepción.) At reception? (En la recepción.)
Source: DLE: recepción.
