## You'll want to know: ईर्ष्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C454-IRSYA454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ईर्ष्यति** — *īrṣyati* — "envies".

> सः धनिकाय मित्राय ईर्ष्यति।
>
> *saḥ dhanikāya mitrāya īrṣyati.*
>
> He envies his rich friend.

Past (he …): सः मित्राय ईर्ष्यितवान्, *saḥ mitrāya īrṣyitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ईर्ष्यति** (*īrṣyati*) comes from the root √īrṣy, "to envy, to feel impatient at another's prosperity", which takes the dative; MW gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
