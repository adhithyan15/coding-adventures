## You'll want to know: કવચ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C372-VIMO372-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કવચ** — *kavac* — "a protective cover; armour".

> જો ઘર પર વીમાનું કવચ હશે તો આગ પછી મરામતની ચિંતા નહીં રહે.
>
> *jo ghar par vīmānũ kavac haśe to āg pachī marāmatnī cintā nahī̃ rahe.*
>
> If the house has insurance cover, there will be no worry about repairs after a fire.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કવચ** (*kavac*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *kavaca*, "armour, a coat of mail; any covering" (Monier-Williams): what covers and guards.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
