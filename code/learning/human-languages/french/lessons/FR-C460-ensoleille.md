## You'll want to know: ensoleillé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C460-METEO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ensoleillé** — "sunny".

> La terrasse est ensoleillée l'après-midi.
>
> *The terrace is sunny in the afternoon.*

*Une journée ensoleillée* — a sunny day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ensoleillé* is built on *soleil* 'sun', which goes back to Vulgar Latin *sōliculus*, a small *sōl* 'sun' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
