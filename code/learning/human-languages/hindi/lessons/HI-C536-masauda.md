## You'll want to know: मसौदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C536-YOJNA536-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मसौदा** — *masaudā* — "a draft (of a text)".

> दफ़्तर ने नई योजना का मसौदा भेजा।
>
> *Daftar ne naī yojnā kā masaudā bhejā.*
>
> The office sent a draft of the new scheme.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मसौदा** (*masaudā*) is usually traced to Arabic *musawwada*, "a rough copy", through Persian.
