German, chapter 99: Dress, Trousers, Shoe, Cap, Sock.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Kleid — a dress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for clothes, then the German for a shirt.

You'll want to know: das Kleid.
das Kleid — "a dress".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: das Kleid]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: das Kleid, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say das Kleid]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for clothes, then the German for a shirt, then say das Kleid again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does das Kleid mean? ("A dress".) Say it once more.
