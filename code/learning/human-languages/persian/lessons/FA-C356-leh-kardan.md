## You'll want to know: له کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C356-RIZKARDAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**له کردن** (*leh kardan*) — "to mash, to crush".

> سیب زمینی ها را با چنگال له کرد.
>
> *Sibzamini-hâ râ bâ changâl leh kard.*
>
> She mashed the potatoes with a fork.

*Leh* "crushed, squashed". *Leh shodan* "to get squashed".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*leh* "crushed" is a Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
