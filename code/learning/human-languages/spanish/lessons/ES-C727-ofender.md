## You'll want to know: ofender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C727-SENSIBLE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ofender** — "to offend".

> No quería ofenderte.
>
> *I did not want to offend you.*

*Ofenderse* — to take offence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ofender* comes from Latin *offendere* "to strike against" (EtymDB), built on *ob* "against".
