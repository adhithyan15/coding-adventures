## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-BE-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-HE-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01] -->

> **へ** *he* + **゛** → **べ** *be*

Write **へ** in full: one stroke, up to the low peak and down to the right.
Then add the two short strokes of the voicing mark at the upper right, above
the long way down, the left one first.

As with **は** and **ば**, the *h* sign voices to *b*: *he* becomes *be*.

With **た** in front and **る** after it, you can now write the word from the
last lesson: **たべる**, *taberu*, to eat.
