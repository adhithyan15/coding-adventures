## You'll want to know: 重伤
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C478-HURT478-03]; assesses=[] -->

**重伤** — *zhòngshāng* — "serious injury; badly hurt".

> **那个事故里有两个人重伤** *nàge shìgù lǐ yǒu liǎng ge rén zhòngshāng* — Two people were badly hurt in that accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**重伤** (*zhòngshāng*) is built from 重 'heavy' and 伤 'to hurt', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjowngX* and *syang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'thick' and the second 'to harm'.
