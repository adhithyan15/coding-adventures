## You'll want to know: رخصة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C434-RUKHSA434-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رخصة** — *rukhṣa* — "a licence; a permit".

> عند أختي رخصة قيادة جديدة.
>
> *ʿinda ukhtī rukhṣat qiyāda jadīda.*
>
> My sister has a new driving licence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رخصة** (*rukhṣa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-kh-ṣ*, "to be soft, easy, cheap" (Lane). A *rukhṣa* is an easing: a permission. It shares its root with *rakhīṣ*, "cheap".
