## You'll want to know: よごす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C160-DIRT160-04]; assesses=[] -->

**よごす** — *yogosu* — "to make dirty; to soil".

> **いぬが ゆかを よごします。** — *inu ga yuka o yogoshimasu* — "The dog makes the floor dirty."

Polite **よごします**, *yogoshimasu*; a godan verb. Its intransitive partner is **よごれる**, *yogoreru*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よごす** (*yogosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as *yogoreru*, "to get dirty"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
