## You'll want to know: 人山人海
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C433-OVERSEAS433-03]; assesses=[] -->

**人山人海** — *rénshānrénhǎi* — "huge crowds; a sea of people".

> *zhōumò gōngyuán lǐ rénshānrénhǎi* — At the weekend the park is packed with people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**人山人海** (*rénshānrénhǎi*) is built from 人 'people', 山 'a mountain' and 海 'the sea', said twice with 人: people like mountains, people like seas. The parts are inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyin*, *srean* and *xojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
