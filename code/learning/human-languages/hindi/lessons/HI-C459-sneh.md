## You'll want to know: स्नेह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C459-SNEH459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्नेह** — *sneh* — "affection; fondness".

> दादी सब बच्चों से बहुत स्नेह करती हैं।
>
> *dādī sab baccoṁ se bahut sneh kartī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother is very fond of all the children.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्नेह** (*sneh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sneha*, first "oiliness" and then "tenderness, love, fondness" (Monier-Williams).
