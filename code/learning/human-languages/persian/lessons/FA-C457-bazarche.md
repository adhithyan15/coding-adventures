## You'll want to know: بازارچه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C457-RASTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بازارچه** (*bâzârche*) — "a small bazaar, a local market".

> نزدیک خانه ما یک بازارچه قدیمی هست.
>
> *Nazdik-e khâne-ye mâ yek bâzârche-ye qadimi hast.*
>
> There is an old little market near our house.

*bâzâr* + *-che* 'little'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâzârche* is built from *bâzâr* 'market', inherited from Middle Persian *wāzār* (EtymDB), and the native ending *-che* 'little'.
