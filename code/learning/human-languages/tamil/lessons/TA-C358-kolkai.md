# கொள்கை (koḷkai) — a policy; a principle

## You'll want to know: கொள்கை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C358-KOTPATU358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கொள்கை** — *koḷkai* — "a policy; a principle".

> அரசின் புதிய கொள்கையை மக்கள் வரவேற்றார்கள்.
>
> *araciṉ putiya koḷkaiyai makkaḷ varavēṟṟārkaḷ.*
>
> People welcomed the new government policy.

Also a personal principle: அது என் கொள்கை, that is my principle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கொள்கை** (*koḷkai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2151 lists Tamil *koḷ*, "to take, receive, regard", and *koḷkai*, "taking, acceptance", the noun that now means "a principle one holds".
