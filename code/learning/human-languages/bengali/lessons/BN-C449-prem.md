## You'll want to know: প্রেম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C449-SNEHO449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রেম** — *prem* — "love; romantic love".

> কলেজে পড়ার সময় অরুণ আর মীরার প্রেম হল।
>
> *kôleje pôṛār sômôy ôrun ār mīrār prem hôlô.*
>
> Arun and Mira fell in love while they were at college.

প্রেমে পড়া, to fall in love.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রেম** (*prem*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *preman*, "love, affection, kindness" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
