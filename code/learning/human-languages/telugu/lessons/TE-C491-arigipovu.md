# అరిగిపోవు (arigipōvu) — to wear down; to wear out

## You'll want to know: అరిగిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C491-TUPPU491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అరిగిపోవు** — *arigipōvu* — "to wear down; to wear out".

> ఎక్కువ నడవడంతో నా చెప్పులు అరిగిపోయాయి.
>
> *ekkuva naḍavaḍantō nā ceppulu arigipōyāyi.*
>
> With so much walking, my sandals have worn down.

Past (it …): అరిగిపోయింది, *arigipōyindi*.

Said of soles, tyres and steps worn thin by use. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: అరిగిపోయింది, it wore down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అరిగిపోవు** (*arigipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 316 lists Telugu *aṟugu*, "to wear away, to waste away; to be digested", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572), which marks a change that is complete.
