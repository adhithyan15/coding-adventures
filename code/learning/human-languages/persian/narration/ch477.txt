Persian, chapter 477: Heat and humidity.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شرجی (sharji) — muggy, humid (and hot).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to freeze, then the Persian for to melt.

You'll want to know: شرجی.
شرجی (sharji) — "muggy, humid (and hot)".
تابستان در بندر هوا شرجی است. Tâbestân dar bandar havâ sharji ast. In summer the air in the port is muggy.
Said of the Gulf coast in summer.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of sharji is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.

رطوبت (rotubat) هوا امروز خیلی بالاست. Rotubat-e havâ emruz kheyli bâlâst. The humidity is very high today.
Martub — humid.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 3 of 5.
گرمازدگی (garmâzadegi) — heatstroke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for muggy, then the Persian for humidity.

You'll want to know: گرمازدگی.
گرمازدگی (garmâzadegi) — "heatstroke".
کارگر در آفتاب گرمازدگی گرفت. Kârgar dar âftâb garmâzadegi gereft. The worker got heatstroke in the sun.
garmâ 'heat' + zade 'struck' + -gi.

The word, taken apart.
garmâzadegi is a native compound of garmâ 'heat', from garm 'warm', zade 'struck', from zadan 'to strike', and the noun ending -gi: being struck by the heat.

هوای بهار در این شهر معتدل است. Havâ-ye bahâr dar in shahr mo'tadel ast. The spring weather in this town is mild.

Lesson 5 of 5.
آفتاب گرفتن (âftâb gereftan) — to sunbathe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for heatstroke, then the Persian for mild.

You'll want to know: آفتاب گرفتن.
آفتاب گرفتن (âftâb gereftan) — "to sunbathe".
گردشگرها کنار دریا آفتاب میگرفتند. Gardeshgar-hâ kenâr-e daryâ âftâb migereftand. The tourists were sunbathing by the sea.
âftâb 'sun' + gereftan 'to take'.

The word, taken apart.
âftâb gereftan is built from âftâb 'sunshine', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian, and the native verb gereftan 'to take'.
