Lesson 1 of 6.
সন্তান (sôntān) — a child (as offspring); offspring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a husband's younger brother, then the Bengali for a husband's elder brother.

You'll want to know: সন্তান.
সন্তান — sôntān — "a child (as offspring); offspring".
আমার কাকার দুই সন্তান। āmār kākār dui sôntān. My uncle has two children.

The word, taken apart.
সন্তান (sôntān) is a tatsama, Sanskrit saṃtāna, "continued succession", and so "offspring" (Monier-Williams): what carries the family on.

বংশ — bôngshô — "a family line; lineage".
আমাদের বংশ এই গ্রামে একশো বছর ধরে আছে। āmāder bôngshô ei grāme eksho bôchhôr dhôre āchhe. Our family line has been in this village for a hundred years.

The word, taken apart.
বংশ (bôngshô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vaṃśa, both "a bamboo cane" and "a lineage" (Monier-Williams); বাঁশ (taught), bamboo, is the same word worn down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: প্রজন্ম.
প্রজন্ম — prôjônmô — "a generation".
নতুন প্রজন্ম ফোনে গান শোনে। nôtun prôjônmô phone gān shone. The new generation listens to music on their phones.

The word, taken apart.
প্রজন্ম (prôjônmô) is a modern formation from Sanskrit pra-, "forth", and janman, "birth" (CDIAL 5113), the জন্ম of জন্মদিন (taught), a birthday.

পূর্বপুরুষ — pūrbôpurush — "an ancestor; the forefathers".
আমাদের পূর্বপুরুষরা নদীর ধারে থাকতেন। āmāder pūrbôpurushrā nôdīr dhāre thākten. Our ancestors lived on the riverbank.

পূর্বপুরুষ (pūrbôpurush) is a tatsama, Sanskrit pūrvapuruṣa, "a forefather" (Monier-Williams): pūrva "earlier" with puruṣa "man".
