Kannada, chapter 515: Blindness, Deafness and Pain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕುರುಡ (kuruḍa) — a blind man; blind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to get tired, then the Kannada for lifespan.

You'll want to know: ಕುರುಡ.
ಕುರುಡ — kuruḍa — "a blind man; blind".
ಆ ಕುರುಡ ಸಂಗೀತಗಾರ ಸುಂದರವಾಗಿ ಹಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ. ā kuruḍa saṅgītagāra sundaravāgi hāḍuttāne. That blind musician sings beautifully.
Plural: ಕುರುಡರು, kuruḍaru.
A blind woman is ಕುರುಡಿ. Politer: ದೃಷ್ಟಿ ಇಲ್ಲದವರು, those without sight.

The word, taken apart.
ಕುರುಡ (kuruḍa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1787 lists Kannada kuruḍu, "blindness", and kuruḍa, "a blind man".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮೂಕ (mūka) — mute; unable to speak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a blind man, then the Kannada for deaf.

You'll want to know: ಮೂಕ.
ಮೂಕ — mūka — "mute; unable to speak".
ಆ ಮೂಕ ಹುಡುಗ ಕೈಸನ್ನೆಯಿಂದ ಮಾತಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ. ā mūka huḍuga kaisanneyinda mātāḍuttāne. That mute boy talks with hand signs.
ಮೂಕ (mūka) ಪ್ರಾಣಿ, a dumb creature, an animal that cannot complain.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೂಕ (mūka) is borrowed from Sanskrit mūka, "tied, tongue-tied, dumb, silent" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ನಲುಗು (nalugu) — to droop; to be worn down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for mute, then the Kannada for to groan.

You'll want to know: ನಲುಗು.
ನಲುಗು — nalugu — "to droop; to be worn down".
ಬಿಸಿಲಿಗೆ ತೋಟದ ಹೂವುಗಳು ನಲುಗಿದವು. bisilige tōṭada hūvugaḷu nalugidavu. The garden flowers drooped in the sun.
Past (I …): ನಲುಗಿದೆ, nalugide.
Of people too: ಕಷ್ಟದಿಂದ ನಲುಗು (nalugu), to be worn down by hardship.

The word, taken apart.
ನಲುಗು (nalugu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3611 lists Kannada nalagu, nalugu, "to become rumpled, to droop, to grow weak or faint from sickness, trouble or grief".
