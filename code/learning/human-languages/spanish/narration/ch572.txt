Spanish, chapter 572: Todo cambió.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
convertirse — to become, to turn into.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the origin, then the Spanish for the development.

You'll want to know: convertirse.
convertirse — "to become, to turn into".
El pueblo se convirtió en una ciudad. The village turned into a city.
Convertirse en + noun. Stem change: se convierte.

The word, taken apart.
convertirse comes from Latin convertere 'to turn around, to change' (Lewis & Short), built on con- and vertere 'to turn'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
desarrollar — to develop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to transform, then the Spanish for to evolve.

You'll want to know: desarrollar.
desarrollar — "to develop".
La empresa desarrolló un coche nuevo. The company developed a new car.
Desarrollarse is to take place or to develop: La historia se desarrolla en Lima.

The word, taken apart.
desarrollar is built on arrollar 'to roll up' with des- 'un-'. Arrollar comes from rollo, usually traced to Latin rotulus 'a little wheel', built on rota 'wheel'. To develop is to unroll.
