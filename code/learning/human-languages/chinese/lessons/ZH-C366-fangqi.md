## You'll want to know: fàngqì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C366-ADVICE366-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngqì* — "to give up".

> *nǐ bù yīnggāi fàngqì* — You shouldn't give up.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngqì* is built from *fàng* and *qì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjangH* and *khjijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to drive out' and the second, quoting an older dictionary, 'to throw away'.
