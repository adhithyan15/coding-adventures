## You'll want to know: xìngyùn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C529-LUCK529-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xìngyùn* — "lucky; fortunate".

> *wǒ hěn xìngyùn bùrán wǒ jiù chídào le* — I was lucky, or I'd have been late.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìngyùn* is built from *xìng* and 运 'luck', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *heangX* and *hjunH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first, after an older book, as having good fortune and escaping harm.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
