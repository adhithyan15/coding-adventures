## You'll want to know: तरजीह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C549-VIKALP549-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तरजीह** — *tarjīh* — "preference; priority".

> दुकानदार ने पुराने ग्राहकों को तरजीह दी।
>
> *Dukāndār ne purāne grāhkoṁ ko tarjīh dī.*
>
> The shopkeeper gave priority to his old customers.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तरजीह** (*tarjīh*) is usually traced to Arabic *tarjīḥ*, "giving more weight, preferring", through Persian.
