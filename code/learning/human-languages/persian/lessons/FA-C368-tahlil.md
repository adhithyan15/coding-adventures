## You'll want to know: تحلیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C368-TAHLIL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تحلیل** (*tahlil*) — "an analysis".

> تحلیل او از بازی خیلی خوب بود.
>
> *tahlil-e u az bâzi kheyli khub bud.*
>
> His analysis of the game was very good.

Plural *tahlilhâ*. *Tahlilgar* — an analyst.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحلیل** is usually traced to Arabic *taḥlīl* "analysis", a verbal noun usually explained as untying or breaking something into its parts.
