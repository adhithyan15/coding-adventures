Urdu, chapter 349: Research.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تحقیق (tahqīq) — research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to threaten, then the Urdu for to coax.

You'll want to know: تحقیق.
تحقیق — tahqīq — "research".
وہ پانی پر تحقیق کر رہی تھی۔ voh pānī par tahqīq kar rahī thī. She was doing research on water.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تحقیق (tahqīq) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taḥqīq, "verification, finding out what is true".

Lesson 5 of 5.
کھوجنا (khojnā) — to search out; to investigate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a researcher, then the Urdu for a discovery.

You'll want to know: کھوجنا.
کھوجنا — khojnā — "to search out; to investigate".
وہ اس بیماری کی وجہ کھوج رہے تھے۔ voh is bīmārī kī vajah khoj rahe the. They were searching out the cause of this illness.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے راز کھوجا, us ne rāz khojā.

The word, taken apart.
کھوجنا (khojnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin khojnā, "to seek, to enquire", under khōjja, "a footprint", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3929). To follow a trail of footprints.
