## You'll want to know: شجيرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C468-NABAT468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شجيرة** — *shujayra* — "a shrub; a bush".

> زرع جدي شجيرة ورد في الحديقة.
>
> *zaraʿa jaddī shujayrat ward fī al-ḥadīqa.*
>
> My grandfather planted a rose bush in the garden.

The pattern *fuʿayla* makes a small one: *shujayra* is a little *shajara*, "a tree".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شجيرة** (*shujayra*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-j-r*, "trees" (Lane).
