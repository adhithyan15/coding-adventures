# పట్టాభిషేకం (paṭṭābhiṣēkaṁ) — a coronation

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a crown, then the Telugu for a throne.

## You'll want to know: పట్టాభిషేకం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C335-KIRITAM335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టాభిషేకం** — *paṭṭābhiṣēkaṁ* — "a coronation".

> కొత్త రాజు పట్టాభిషేకం నిన్న జరిగింది.
>
> *kotta rāju paṭṭābhiṣēkaṁ ninna jarigindi.*
>
> The new king's coronation took place yesterday.

పట్టం, the royal band, and అభిషేకం, an anointing with water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టాభిషేకం** (*paṭṭābhiṣēkaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *paṭṭa*, "a band, a diadem" (CDIAL 7700), and *abhiṣeka*, "anointing; the inauguration of a king" (Monier-Williams).
