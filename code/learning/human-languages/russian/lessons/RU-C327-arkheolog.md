## You'll want to know: археолог
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C327-KLAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**археолог** (*arkheológ*) — "an archaeologist".

> Археолог работал в пустыне два года.
>
> *Arkheológ rabótal v pustýne dva góda.*
>
> The archaeologist worked in the desert for two years.

*Археология* — archaeology.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*археолог* is built on Greek *arkhaîos* "ancient", from *arkhḗ* "beginning", and *lógos* "word, account" (EtymDB): one who studies ancient things.
