## You'll want to know: завещание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C393-DOKUMENTY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**завещание** (*zaveshchániye*) — "a will (a testament)".

> Дед написал завещание.
>
> *Ded napisál zaveshchániye.*
>
> Grandfather made a will.

Neuter. *Оставить завещание* — to leave a will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**завещание** is built on *завещать* "to bequeath", on *вещать* "to proclaim", the Church Slavonic form of the root of *ответ*, Old Russian *větŭ* "counsel": a will is a last word spoken for the future.
