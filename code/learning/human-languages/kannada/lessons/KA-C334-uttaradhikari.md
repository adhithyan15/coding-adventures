## You'll want to know: ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C334-JANANA334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ** — *uttarādhikāri* — "a successor".

> ಆ ಮಗು ರಾಜನ ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *ā magu rājana uttarādhikāri āyitu.*
>
> That child became the king's successor.

Plural: ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳು, *uttarādhikārigaḷu*.

The one who takes over a throne, a post or property after someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ** (*uttarādhikāri*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *uttarādhikārin*, "an heir or claimant after the death of the owner" (Monier-Williams).
