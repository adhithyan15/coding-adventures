## You'll want to know: वक्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C370-VYAKHYAN370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वक्ता** — *vaktā* — "a speaker".

> आजचे वक्ते पुण्याहून आले आहेत.
>
> *ājce vakte puṇyāhūn āle āhet.*
>
> Today's speaker has come from Pune.

Masculine. Plural: वक्ते, *vakte*.

Plural वक्ते; respect takes the plural; a listener is श्रोता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वक्ता** (*vaktā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vaktṛ*, "a speaker" (Monier-Williams). The Sanskrit nominative is *vaktā*.
