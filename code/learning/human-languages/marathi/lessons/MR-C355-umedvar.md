## You'll want to know: उमेदवार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C355-MATDAN355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उमेदवार** — *umedvār* — "a candidate".

> या जागेसाठी चार उमेदवार उभे आहेत.
>
> *yā jāgesāṭhī cār umedvār ubhe āhet.*
>
> Four candidates are standing for this seat.

Masculine.

उभा राहणे, to stand for election; also a job applicant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उमेदवार** (*umedvār*) is a loan from Persian *ummedvār*, "hopeful, a candidate", built on *ummed*, "hope".
