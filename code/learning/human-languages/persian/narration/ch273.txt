Persian, chapter 273: Boring, Useful, Traditional, Suitable, Possible.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کسل کننده (kesel-konande) — boring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for pink, then the Persian for dark.

You'll want to know: کسل کننده.
کسل کننده — kesel-konande — "boring".

The word, taken apart.
kesel-konande is "making weary": kesel, "weary, bored", is borrowed from Arabic kasal, "sluggishness" (root k-s-l, Lane), and konande, "making", is from kardan.
