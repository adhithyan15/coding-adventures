Persian, chapter 362: Making an argument.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
استدلال (estedlâl) — an argument, a line of reasoning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to believe in, then the Persian for to doubt.

You'll want to know: استدلال.
استدلال (estedlâl) — "an argument, a line of reasoning".
استدلال او خیلی قوی بود. estedlâl-e u kheyli qavi bud. His argument was very strong.
Formal. Plural estedlâlhâ. It is an argument as reasoning, not a quarrel.

The word, taken apart.
استدلال (estedlâl) comes from Arabic istidlāl "reasoning, inference" (EtymDB), a verbal noun from the same Arabic family as dalīl "proof, guide", which gave Persian dalil.

Lesson 2 of 5.
استدلال کردن (estedlâl kardan) — to argue, to reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to doubt, then the Persian for an argument.

You'll want to know: استدلال کردن.
استدلال کردن (estedlâl kardan) — "to argue, to reason".
وکیل با دقت استدلال کرد. vakil bâ deqqat estedlâl kard. The lawyer argued carefully.
Estedlâl "reasoning" + kardan (stem kon-). To argue that …: estedlâl mikonad ke ….

The word, taken apart.
استدلال کردن (estedlâl kardan) joins estedlâl, from Arabic istidlāl "reasoning" (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian verb kardan "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.
منطق (manteq) — logic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an argument, then the Persian for to argue.

You'll want to know: منطق.
منطق (manteq) — "logic".
حرف تو منطق ندارد. harf-e to manteq nadârad. What you say has no logic.
Also the subject of logic at university.

The word, taken apart.
منطق (manteq) was borrowed from Arabic manṭiq (EtymDB glosses it "speech"); in Arabic and Persian it came to mean logic, the art of correct speech and reasoning.

Lesson 4 of 5.
منطقی (manteqi) — logical, reasonable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to argue, then the Persian for logic.

You'll want to know: منطقی.
منطقی (manteqi) — "logical, reasonable".
این یک تصمیم منطقی است. in yek tasmim-e manteqi ast. This is a logical decision.
Manteq + the adjective suffix -i. The opposite is gheyr-e manteqi.

The word, taken apart.
منطقی (manteqi) comes from Arabic manṭiqī "logical" (EtymDB), the adjective of manṭiq "logic"; Persian hears it as manteq + -i.

Lesson 5 of 5.
قانع کردن (qâne' kardan) — to convince, to persuade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for logic, then the Persian for logical.

You'll want to know: قانع کردن.
قانع کردن (qâne' kardan) — "to convince, to persuade".
بالاخره دوستم را قانع کردم. bâlâkhare dustam râ qâne' kardam. In the end I convinced my friend.
Qâne' "satisfied, content" + kardan. Qâne' shodan — to be convinced. The person takes râ.

The word, taken apart.
قانع is usually traced to Arabic qāni' "content, satisfied"; with the Persian verb kardan "to do", to convince someone is to make them content with your view.
