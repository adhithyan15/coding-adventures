## Added — B1 vocabulary: chapters 290-541; Chinese attains B1

- Nine tranches of 28 chapters, five words each: 1,260 words in 252 chapters.
  Each chapter has at least two examples in its spine node's grammar.
  - **i, 290-317** (`SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS`): news and events, accidents,
    births, schools and weddings, police and crime, moving.
  - **j, 318-345** (`SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS`): letters and the phone, history
    and memory, legends and folk tales, festivals and customs.
  - **k, 346-373** (`SPINE-GIVE-REASONS`): opinions, agreeing and refusing,
    rules and manners, advice, explaining.
  - **l, 374-401** (`SPINE-GIVE-REASONS`, then `SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION`):
    society, the neighbourhood and the environment; plans, appointments and
    changes.
  - **m, 402-429** (`SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL`): stations, buses and ferries, the
    cabin and the airport, roads, hotels.
  - **n, 430-457** (`SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL`): sightseeing, regions and the
    countryside, rivers, islands and old towns, markets, luggage and asking
    for help.
  - **o, 458-485** (`SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE`): feelings and moods,
    character, the body, injuries and health.
  - **p, 486-513** (`SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE`): work, study and exams,
    sport, art, music, family and ambitions.
  - **q, 514-541** (`SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE`, then
    `SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION`): weather, nature, the household, buying and
    selling; chance and luck, hopes and wishes, cause and consequence.

- Every tranche was drafted, then reviewed on its own. The reviews:
  - made examples natural;
  - swapped words that duplicated taught ones (for example 教书 -> 教育, 照看
    -> 懂事, 好学 -> 体贴, 停车 -> 车道);
  - removed forward references;
  - hedged every Guangyun claim the dictionary does not make;
  - named irregular sounds.
- Origins come from the Guangyun in Baxter's transcription. Distinct words keep
  distinct slugs, for example 獎 *tsjangX* apart from 搶 *tshjangX*, and 才
  'talent' (*dzoj-talent*) apart from 才 'only then'.
- Cues avoid the phrases the modality reader takes as "look at the page". The
  first opening lesson of ch334 was not drivable by ear because 守岁's cue
  read "to see the new year in". It now reads "to keep the New Year vigil".
- B1 headwords 1,329 -> 2,589 and verbs 410 -> 866. The gate's 2,500
  headwords are met, and Chinese attains B1. Next comes B2: four spine nodes
  and 4,000 headwords.
