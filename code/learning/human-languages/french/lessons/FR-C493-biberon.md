## You'll want to know: le biberon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C493-BEBE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le biberon** — "baby's bottle".

> Le père donne le biberon au bébé.
>
> *The father gives the baby its bottle.*

*Donner le biberon* — to bottle-feed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le biberon* is built on Latin *bibere* "to drink" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-on*.
