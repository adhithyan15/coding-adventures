## You'll want to know: shēnyè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C169-EARLY169-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shēnyè* — "late at night; the dead of night".

> *shēnyè wàibian hěn ānjìng* — Late at night it's quiet outside.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shēnyè* is built from *shēn* 'deep' and *yè* 'night', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syim* and *yaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): deep in the night. The dictionary gives 'deep' under the falling-tone reading *syimH*; today's level tone goes with *syim*.
