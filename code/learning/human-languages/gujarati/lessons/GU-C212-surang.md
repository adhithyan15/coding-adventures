## You'll want to know: સુરંગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C212-REST212-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સુરંગ** — *surang* — "tunnel".

> **ટ્રેન લાંબી સુરંગમાંથી ગઈ.** — *ṭren lā̃bī surangmā̃thī gaī* — "The train went through a long tunnel."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સુરંગ** (*surang*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "underground passage, mine for blasting", under Sanskrit *suruṅgā*, "subterranean passage, mine" (CDIAL 13506).
