## You'll want to know: participar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C606-SOLIDAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**participar** — "to take part, to participate".

> Quiero participar en el proyecto.
>
> *I want to take part in the project.*

*Participar en* something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*participar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *participare* 'to share in', built on *particeps*, from *pars* 'part' and *capere* 'to take' (EtymDB).
