# The first farewell line

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-AGAIN]; assesses=[MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-THANKS] -->

The known phrase **पुन्हा भेटू** promises another meeting. Keep it on a new
line after thanks.

## Writing — delayed copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-THANKS, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-AGAIN] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Study **पुन्हा भेटू.**, cover it, wait ten seconds, and write it. Compare the
space between the two words before comparing the final mark.
