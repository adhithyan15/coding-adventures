# શહેર — hear city first

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-SHAHAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

Listen twice: **shahar**. It means **city**. Hold only the sound and meaning;
the Gujarati spelling waits for the next small step.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-SHAHAR-01] -->

Point to the city picture when you hear *shahar*, then say *shahar* once from
the English cue.
