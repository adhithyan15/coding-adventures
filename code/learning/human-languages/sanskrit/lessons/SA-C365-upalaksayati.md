## You'll want to know: उपलक्षयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C365-SIDDHANTA365-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उपलक्षयति** — *upalakṣayati* — "notices; observes".

> वैज्ञानिकः नक्षत्राणि उपलक्षयति।
>
> *vaijñānikaḥ nakṣatrāṇi upalakṣayati.*
>
> The scientist observes the stars.

Past (he …): सः दोषम् उपलक्षितवान्, *saḥ doṣam upalakṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपलक्षयति** (*upalakṣayati*) is built on *upa*, "towards", and the verb *lakṣaya*, "to mark"; MW gives *upa-√lakṣ* "to observe, to perceive" and "to pay attention to" (MW).
