## You'll want to know: el material
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C475-OFICIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el material** — "the material, the equipment".

> La empresa pone el material.
>
> *The company provides the equipment.*

*El material escolar* — school supplies.
