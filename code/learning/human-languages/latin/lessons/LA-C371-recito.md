## You'll want to know: recitō, recitāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C371-ROSTRA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**recitō, recitāre** — "to read aloud, to recite".

> Poēta carmina sua amīcīs recitāvit.
>
> *The poet read his poems aloud to his friends.*

*recitō, recitāre, recitāvī, recitātum*. At Rome authors read new work aloud to invited listeners. English *recite*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recitō* is built from *re-* and *citō* "to call, to summon" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to call words out again. English *recite* comes from it.
