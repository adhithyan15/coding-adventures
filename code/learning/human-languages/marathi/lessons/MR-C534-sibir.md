## You'll want to know: शिबिर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C534-BAHER534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिबिर** — *śibir* — "a camp".

> सुट्टीत मुलांसाठी जंगलात शिबिर होतं.
>
> *suṭṭīt mulāṁsāṭhī jaṅglāt śibir hotaṁ.*
>
> In the holidays there was a camp in the forest for children.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिबिर** (*śibir*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śibira*, "a camp, a tent" (Monier-Williams).
