## You'll want to know: आखर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C285-AKHAR285-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आखर** — *ākhar* — "a letter of the alphabet".

> यो आखर नुवो है।
>
> *yo ākhar nuvo hai.*
>
> This letter is new.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आखर** (*ākhar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *akṣara*, "a syllable, a letter"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *ākhar*, "letter" (CDIAL 38).
