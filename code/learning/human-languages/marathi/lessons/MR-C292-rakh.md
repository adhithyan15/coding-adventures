## You'll want to know: राख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C292-AAG292-04]; assesses=[] -->

**राख** — *rākh* — "ash; ashes".

> आग विझल्यावर तिथे फक्त राख उरली.
>
> *āg vijhalyāvar tithe phakta rākh urlī.*
>
> When the fire went out, only ash was left there.

Feminine.

राख होणे, to burn to ashes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राख** (*rākh*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *rakṣā*, 'ashes' (CDIAL 10552).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
