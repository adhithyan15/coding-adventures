## You'll want to know: صدق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C366-IDDIA366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صدق** — *ṣaddaqa* — "to believe someone".

> لم يصدق أحد قصته.
>
> *lam yuṣaddiq aḥad qiṣṣatah.*
>
> Nobody believed his story.

Present: يصدق, *yuṣaddiq*.

Form I صدق, *ṣadaqa*, with the same letters, means to tell the truth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صدق** (*ṣaddaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-d-q*, "to be truthful" (Lane). It is Form II, to hold to be truthful: to believe someone.
