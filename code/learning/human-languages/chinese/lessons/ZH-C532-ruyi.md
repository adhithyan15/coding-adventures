## You'll want to know: 如意
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C532-HOPE532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**如意** — *rúyì* — "as one would wish".

> *rúguǒ gōngzuò bú tài rúyì jiù huàn yí ge* — If your job isn't quite what you hoped, change it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**如意** (*rúyì*) is built from 如 'like' and 意 'what one means', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyo* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'intent': as one intends.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
