## You'll want to know: વહુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C265-LOVE265-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વહુ** — *vahu* — "daughter-in-law, bride".

> **નવી વહુ ઘરમાં નથી.** — *navī vahu gharmā̃ nathī* — "The new bride is not in the house."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વહુ** (*vahu*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *vadhū́*, "bride, young wife"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "son's wife" (CDIAL 11250).
