## You'll want to know: سلامت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C296-BACHAO296-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سلامت** — *salāmat* — "safe and sound".

> صبح تک سب سلامت تھے۔
>
> *subah tak sab salāmat the.*
>
> By morning everyone was safe and sound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سلامت** (*salāmat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *salāma*, "safety, soundness", a cousin of **سلام**.
