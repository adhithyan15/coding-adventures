## You'll want to know: zìpāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C430-SIGHTS430-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zìpāi* — "to take a selfie".

> *tā zài jiǔdiàn de dàtáng zìpāi* — She's taking a selfie in the hotel lobby.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zìpāi* is built from *zì* 'oneself' and *pāi* 'to snap a picture', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzijH* and *phaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'from' and the second 'to strike'; the sense 'to take a photo' is modern.
