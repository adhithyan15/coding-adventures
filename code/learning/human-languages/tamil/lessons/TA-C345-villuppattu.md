## You'll want to know: வில்லுப்பாட்டு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C345-KATHAICOLLI345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**வில்லுப்பாட்டு** — *villuppāṭṭu* — "a bow-song".

> திருவிழாவில் வில்லுப்பாட்டு நடந்தது.
>
> *tiruviḻāvil villuppāṭṭu naṭantatu.*
>
> There was a bow-song performance at the festival.

A sung story, told with a large bow as the main instrument.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வில்லுப்பாட்டு** (*villuppāṭṭu*) is a Tamil compound built from வில், "bow" (DEDR 5422), and பாட்டு, "song" (DEDR 4065).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
