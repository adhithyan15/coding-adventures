# सेल (sel) — a sale (at reduced prices)

## You'll want to know: सेल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C431-SAUDA431-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सेल** — *sel* — "a sale (at reduced prices)".

> कपड़ों की दुकान में सेल लगी है।
>
> *kapṛoṁ kī dukān meṁ sel lagī hai.*
>
> There is a sale on at the clothes shop.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेल** (*sel*) is borrowed from English *sale*, a time of cut prices; it is feminine: सेल लगी है.
