# Review — the past three ways

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-PLAN-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-NAHI-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-NOT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I am going to go" and "I won't go". (*mī jāṇār āhe*, *mī jāṇār nāhī*.) Then "he was not at home" and "she was reading". (*to gharī navhtā*, *tī vācat hotī*.)

## Guided Practice

- [YOU SAY: At nine last night I was reading a book.]
- [YOU SAY: While I was reading, Meera came.]
- [YOU SAY: When I came home, Mother was making tea.]
- [YOU SAY: When I was small, I used to go to the river every day.]
- [YOU SAY: one thing you were doing yesterday evening, and one thing that cut into it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-SCENE-EVENT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ASTANA-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-WHEN-PAST-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-HABITUAL-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which past is the event? (**The plain past**: **आली**.) Which is the scene? (**-त होतो**, or **असताना**.) Which is the habit? (**-ायचो**.) "When I came home"? (**मी घरी आलो, तेव्हा …**)
