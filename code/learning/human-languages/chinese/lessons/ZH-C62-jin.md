## You'll want to know: jìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C62-FAST62-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jìn* — "near; close".

> *wǒ jiā hěn jìn* — My home is close by.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *gj+nX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'pressing close' and 'almost'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
