Telugu, chapter 303: Thorn, Creeper, Bamboo, Sugarcane, Worship.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ముల్లు (mullu) — a thorn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a wolf, then the Telugu for a crocodile.

You'll want to know: ముల్లు.
ముల్లు — mullu — "a thorn".

The word, taken apart.
ముల్లు (mullu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil muḷ and Kannada muḷḷu (DEDR 4995). DEDR lists Telugu mullu, "thorn".

పూజ — pūja — "worship, a puja".

The word, taken apart.
పూజ (pūja) is Sanskrit pūjā, "honour, worship", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the final -ā shortened (CDIAL 8317).
