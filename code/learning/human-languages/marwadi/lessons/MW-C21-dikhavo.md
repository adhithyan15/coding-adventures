# दिखावो — the asking word on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-DIKHAVO-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DIKHAVO-01, MW-SCRIPT-KHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-HAWA-01] -->

[PAUSE 18s] Say *show it*. [YOU WRITE: **ख**, then the word for wind]

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-DA-01, MW-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-VA-01, MW-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01] -->

> **दि** + **खा** + **वो** → **दिखावो** — *dikhāvo* — show it

Three groups, one for each beat you already hear. **ि** is written before
**द** and read after it, the order you first met in **मंदिर**. **वो** is the
**व** of **हवा** with the **ो** of **घोड़ो**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिखावो** (*dikhāvo*), "show", is the causing form of the verb "to see". CDIAL lists Marwari *dekhṇo*, "to see", and Old Marwari *dikhāṛaï* under *\*dṛkṣati*, "sees", a present stem it reconstructs from Sanskrit (CDIAL 6507).
