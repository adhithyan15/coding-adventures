## You'll want to know: ふと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C510-CHANCE510-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふと** — *futo* — "out of the blue; suddenly".

> **もし ふと めが さめたら、みずを のみます。** — *moshi futo me ga sametara, mizu o nomimasu* — "If I happen to wake up, I will drink some water."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふと** (*futo*) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: JMdict marks it as usually written in kana and its rare kanji spellings as ateji, chosen for their sound, and no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).

One more word for saying what happens if.
