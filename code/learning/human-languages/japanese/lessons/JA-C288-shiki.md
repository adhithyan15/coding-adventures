## You'll want to know: しき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C288-NYUUGAKU288-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しき** — *shiki* — "a ceremony".

> **がっこうで しきが ありました。** — *gakkō de shiki ga arimashita* — "There was a ceremony at the school."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しき** (*shiki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *shiki*, "ceremony" (Middle Chinese *syik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
