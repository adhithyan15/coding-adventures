# ঘুম পাড়ানো (ghum pāṛāno) — to put to sleep (a child)

## You'll want to know: ঘুম পাড়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C486-LALON486-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুম পাড়ানো** — *ghum pāṛāno* — "to put to sleep (a child)".

> বাবা গান গেয়ে মেয়েকে ঘুম পাড়ালেন।
>
> *bābā gān geye meyeke ghum pāṛālen.*
>
> Father sang his daughter to sleep.

Past (he/she …): তিনি শিশুকে ঘুম পাড়ালেন, *tini shishuke ghum pāṛālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুম পাড়ানো** (*ghum pāṛāno*) is ঘুম, sleep, with *pāṛāno*, "to lay down", from the verb CDIAL lists for Bengali as felling and laying down, under Sanskrit *pātayati* (CDIAL 8053).
