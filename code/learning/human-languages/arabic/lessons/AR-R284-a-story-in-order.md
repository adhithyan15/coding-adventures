# Review — a story, in order

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C284-STORY-STEPS-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C284-FI-AN-NIHAYA-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C284-INDAMA-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C284-BADA-AN-01, AR-LEX-C284-QISSA284-01] -->

Four lessons, and a story has its order. Joiners at the head of each step:
*awwalan*, *thumma*, *baʿda dhālika*, *fī an-nihāya*. Clauses that name the
moment: *ʿindamā* with the past, and *baʿda an* with the past. And *ḥakā*,
to hand the whole thing on.

[PAUSE 2s] One small story, in four steps:

> **أولا ذهبت إلى المكتب، ثم كتبت رسالة. عندما رجعت، حكيت القصة لصديقي.** —
> *awwalan dhahabtu ilā al-maktab, thumma katabtu risāla. ʿindamā rajaʿtu,
> ḥakaytu al-qiṣṣa li-ṣadīqī* — First I went to the office, then I wrote a letter.
> When I came back, I told my friend the story.
