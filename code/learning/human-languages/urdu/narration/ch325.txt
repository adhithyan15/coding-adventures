Lesson 2 of 5.
پری (parī) — a fairy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a proverb, then the Urdu for a giant in tales.

You'll want to know: پری.
پری — parī — "a fairy".
پری نے بچے کو ایک تحفہ دیا۔ parī ne bachche ko ek tohfā diyā. The fairy gave the child a gift.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
پری (parī) is usually traced to Persian parī, "a fairy". Urdu calls a beautiful face پری جیسا.

جادو (jādū) is usually traced to Persian jādū, "magic, a sorcerer". جادوگر is a magician.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بھوت (bhūt) — a ghost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fairy, then the Urdu for magic.

بچوں کو بھوت سے ڈر لگتا ہے۔ bachchoṅ ko bhūt se ḍar lagtā hai. Children are afraid of ghosts.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
بھوت (bhūt) is inherited from Sanskrit bhūta, "a being, a spirit" (Monier-Williams): what has been, and haunts.
