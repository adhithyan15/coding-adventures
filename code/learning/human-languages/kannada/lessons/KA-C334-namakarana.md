# ನಾಮಕರಣ (nāmakaraṇa) — a naming ceremony

## You'll want to know: ನಾಮಕರಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C334-JANANA334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಮಕರಣ** — *nāmakaraṇa* — "a naming ceremony".

> ಮಗುವಿನ ನಾಮಕರಣ ಹನ್ನೊಂದನೆಯ ದಿನ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *maguvina nāmakaraṇa hannondaneya dina naḍeyitu.*
>
> The baby's naming ceremony took place on the eleventh day.

The day a baby gets its name, often with the baby in a cradle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಮಕರಣ** (*nāmakaraṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nāmakaraṇa*, "the ceremony of naming after birth" (CDIAL 7066), from *nāman* "a name" and *karaṇa* "making".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
