## You'll want to know: o arbusto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C456-BOSQUE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o arbusto** — "the bush, the shrub".

> Um coelho escondeu-se atrás de um arbusto.
>
> *A rabbit hid behind a bush.*

Smaller than a tree, with many stems.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arbusto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *arbustum* 'place planted with trees' (EtymDB), built on *arbor* 'tree'.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
