Lesson 1 of 5.
promettre — to promise.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the French for to take off, then the French for to hold.

You'll want to know: promettre.
promettre — "to promise".

The word, taken apart.
promettre from Old French prometre from Latin promittere, "to send forward, to promise" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited: mettre with pro-.
