## You'll want to know: pescare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C501-PESCA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**pescare** — "to fish; to draw (a card)".

> Ogni domenica mio zio va a pescare al lago.
>
> *Every Sunday my uncle goes fishing at the lake.*

In card games: *pescare una carta* — to draw a card.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pescare* is inherited from Latin *piscārī* "to fish" (EtymDB), from *piscis* "a fish".
