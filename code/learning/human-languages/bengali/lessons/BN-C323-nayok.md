# নায়ক (nāyôk) — the hero of a story; a protagonist

## You'll want to know: নায়ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C323-NAYOK323-01]; assesses=[] -->

**নায়ক** — *nāyôk* — "the hero of a story; a protagonist".

> গল্পের নায়ক এক গরিব জেলে।
>
> *gôlper nāyôk ek gôrib jele.*
>
> The hero of the story is a poor fisherman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নায়ক** (*nāyôk*) is Sanskrit *nāyaka*, 'a guide, a leader, a chief' (Monier-Williams); in plays and stories it is the leading man.
