## You'll want to know: लवणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C35-HOUSE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लवणम्** (*lavaṇam*) — "salt".

**लवणम्** is salt, and salty as a taste.

Here Sanskrit went its own way: Latin *sal* and English *salt* come from a different old word altogether, and **लवणम्** has no cousin among them. It stayed at home instead — *lavaṇa* still names the salty one among the six tastes of Indian cooking, while everyday Hindi took *namak* from Persian for the thing in the jar. Neuter **-अम्**.
