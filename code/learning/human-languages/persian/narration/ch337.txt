Persian, chapter 337: Death.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مرگ (marg) — death.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an orphan, then the Persian for custody.

You'll want to know: مرگ.
مرگ (marg) — "death".
بعد از مرگ پدرش به شهر برگشت. Ba'd az marg-e pedarash be shahr bargasht. After his father's death he went back to the city.
Plain and strong; in polite speech people say dargozasht or fowt.

The word, taken apart.
marg is a native Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian marg, "death"; EtymDB lists it without a parent.

Lesson 2 of 5.
درگذشتن (dargozashtan) — to pass away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for custody, then the Persian for death.

You'll want to know: درگذشتن.
درگذشتن (dargozashtan) — "to pass away".
مادربزرگم سال گذشته درگذشت. Mâdarbozorgam sâl-e gozashte dargozasht. My grandmother passed away last year.
Respectful. Present stem gozar-: dar migozarad. The noun is dargozasht "a passing".

The word, taken apart.
dargozashtan is the native verb gozashtan "to pass" with the prefix dar: to pass on. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
فوت کردن (fowt kardan) — to die (formal), to pass on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for death, then the Persian for to pass away.

You'll want to know: فوت کردن.
فوت کردن (fowt kardan) — "to die (formal), to pass on".
همسایه ما دیروز فوت کرد. Hamsâye-ye mâ diruz fowt kard. Our neighbour died yesterday.
Neutral and polite, common in notices. Present fowt mikonad; the noun fowt is "a death". Spelled the same, fut kardan "to blow" is a different word.

The word, taken apart.
fowt "death, a passing" is usually traced to Arabic fawt, "a passing, a loss"; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دفن کردن (dafn kardan) — to bury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pass away, then the Persian for to die.

You'll want to know: دفن کردن.
دفن کردن (dafn kardan) — "to bury".
او را در روستای خودش دفن کردند. U râ dar rustâ-ye khodash dafn kardand. They buried him in his own village.
Present dafn mikonad; the passive is dafn shod.

The word, taken apart.
dafn is borrowed from Arabic dafn, "burial" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word). kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

You'll want to know: قبر.
قبر (qabr) — "a grave".
هر پنجشنبه سر قبر پدرش میرفت. Har panjshanbe sar-e qabr-e pedarash miraft. Every Thursday he used to go to his father's grave.
Broken plural qobur. Thursday is the usual day for visiting graves.

The word, taken apart.
