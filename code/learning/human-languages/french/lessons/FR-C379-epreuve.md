## You'll want to know: l'épreuve
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C379-DIPLOME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'épreuve** — "the test, the exam paper; the ordeal".

> La première épreuve de l'examen dure trois heures.
>
> *The first paper of the exam lasts three hours.*

*Une épreuve écrite*, *une épreuve orale*. In sport, an event. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*épreuve* is formed on *éprouver* "to test, to feel" (EtymDB), built from *é-* and *prouver*, which is inherited from Latin *probāre* "to test, to examine" (Lewis & Short).
