## You'll want to know: ചെലവ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C494-BILL494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ചെലവ്** — *celavŭ* — "an expense; a cost".

> യാത്രയുടെ ചെലവ് ഞങ്ങൾ പങ്കുവച്ചു.
>
> *yātrayuṭe celavŭ ñaṅṅaḷ paṅkuvaccu.*
>
> We shared the cost of the trip.

You met the verb ചെലവഴിക്കുക, to spend: literally to let the cost go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചെലവ്** (*celavŭ*) is native Dravidian, from the root ചെൽ, to go or pass: money that goes out (DEDR 2781).
