Lesson 1 of 5.
बाळगणे (bāḷagṇe) — to entertain (a hope); to keep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a summary, then the Marathi for in short.

You'll want to know: बाळगणे.
बाळगणे — bāḷagṇe — "to entertain (a hope); to keep".
त्याने निकालाबद्दल मोठी आशा बाळगली होती. tyāne nikālābaddal moṭhī āśā bāḷaglī hotī. He had cherished high hopes about the result.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आशा बाळगली, tyāne āśā bāḷaglī.
आशा बाळगणे (bāḷagṇe), to cherish a hope; राग बाळगणे (bāḷagṇe), to bear a grudge.

The word, taken apart.
बाळगणे (bāḷagṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
विशद करणे (viśad karṇe) — to elucidate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for in short, then the Marathi for to entertain.

You'll want to know: विशद करणे.
विशद करणे — viśad karṇe — "to elucidate".
शिक्षकांनी उदाहरण देऊन नियम विशद केला. śikṣakānnī udāhraṇ deūn niyam viśad kelā. The teacher explained the rule in detail with an example.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने विशद केलं, tyāne viśad kelaṁ.
Formal; in speech समजावून सांगणे.

The word, taken apart.
विशद करणे (viśad karṇe) joins the tatsama विशद, Sanskrit viśada, "clear, bright" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to make": to make clear.

Lesson 3 of 5.
खात्री (khātrī) — certainty; assurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to entertain, then the Marathi for to elucidate.

You'll want to know: खात्री.
खात्री — khātrī — "certainty; assurance".
तो येईल याची मला खात्री आहे. to yeīl yācī malā khātrī āhe. I am sure that he will come.
Feminine.
X ची खात्री (khātrī) असणे, to be sure of X; खात्री (khātrī) करणे, to make sure.

The word, taken apart.
खात्री (khātrī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian khāṭir, "mind", itself from Arabic: a mind set at rest.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पडताळणे (paḍtāḷṇe) — to verify; to cross-check.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to elucidate, then the Marathi for certainty.

You'll want to know: पडताळणे.
पडताळणे — paḍtāḷṇe — "to verify; to cross-check".
पत्रकाराने बातमी छापण्याआधी पडताळली. patrakārāne bātmī chāpaṇyāādhī paḍtāḷlī. The journalist checked the story before printing it.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पडताळलं, tyāne paḍtāḷlaṁ.
Transitive: the past agrees with the thing checked, बातमी पडताळली; पडताळा, a cross-check.

The word, taken apart.
पडताळणे (paḍtāḷṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled; the noun पडताळा, a cross-check, goes with it.
