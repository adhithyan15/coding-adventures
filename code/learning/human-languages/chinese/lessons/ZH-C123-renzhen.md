## You'll want to know: rènzhēn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C123-PEOPLE123-02]; assesses=[] -->

*rènzhēn* — "conscientious; serious".

> *tā gōngzuò hěn rènzhēn* — He takes his work seriously.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rènzhēn* is built from *rèn* 'to recognise' and *zhēn* 'true', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyinH* and *tsyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). How the pair came to mean conscientious is not given there, so it is not settled.
