# ಚದುರು (caduru) — to scatter; to disperse

## You'll want to know: ಚದುರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C339-GALABHE339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚದುರು** — *caduru* — "to scatter; to disperse".

> ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಬಂದ ಕೂಡಲೇ ಜನ ಚದುರಿದರು.
>
> *polīsaru banda kūḍalē jana caduridaru.*
>
> As soon as the police came, the people scattered.

Past (it …): ಚದುರಿತು, *caduritu*.

A crowd or clouds disperse: ಮೋಡ ಚದುರಿತು. To scatter them is ಚದುರಿಸು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚದುರು** (*caduru*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1546 lists Kannada *cadaṟu*, "to be scattered about, disperse", beside Tamil *citar*, "to scatter".
