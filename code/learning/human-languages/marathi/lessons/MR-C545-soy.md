## You'll want to know: सोय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C545-BHAVISHYA545-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सोय** — *soy* — "a convenience; an arrangement".

> गावात राहण्याची चांगली सोय आहे.
>
> *gāvāt rāhṇyācī cāṅglī soy āhe.*
>
> There are good arrangements for staying in the village.

Feminine.

सोय करणे, to make arrangements.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सोय** (*soy*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *soy*, "comfortableness", under Sanskrit *saukhya*, "comfort, welfare" (CDIAL 13612).
