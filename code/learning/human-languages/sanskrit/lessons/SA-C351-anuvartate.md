## You'll want to know: अनुवर्तते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C351-VIVEKA351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुवर्तते** — *anuvartate* — "conforms to; follows (a custom)".

> पुत्रः धर्मस्य मार्गम् अनुवर्तते।
>
> *putraḥ dharmasya mārgam anuvartate.*
>
> The son follows the path of dharma.

Past (he …): सः धर्मम् अनुवृत्तवान्, *saḥ dharmam anuvṛttavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुवर्तते** (*anuvartate*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √vṛt, "to turn, to be"; MW gives *anu-√vṛt* "to follow" and "to obey, to respect, to imitate" (MW).
