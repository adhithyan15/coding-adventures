## You'll want to know: કાણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C515-POPDO515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કાણું** — *kāṇũ* — "a hole".

> બાલદીમાં એક કાણું છે.
>
> *bāldīmā̃ ek kāṇũ che.*
>
> There is a hole in the bucket.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાણું** (*kāṇũ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *kāṇá*, "one-eyed", with the Gujarati word glossed "full of holes; a hole" (CDIAL 3019): perhaps first an empty eye-hole, as CDIAL suggests.
