## You'll want to know: つかまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C271-TSUKAMARU271-01]; assesses=[] -->

**つかまる** — *tsukamaru* — "to be caught; to be arrested".

> **はんにんは えきで つかまりました。** — *hannin wa eki de tsukamarimashita* — "The culprit was arrested at the station."

Polite **つかまります**, *tsukamarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **はんにんが つかまりました。**, *hannin ga tsukamarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つかまる** (*tsukamaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
