## You'll want to know: स्यालः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C500-DEVARA500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्यालः** — *syālaḥ* — "a wife's brother".

> मम स्यालः अद्य आगच्छति।
>
> *mama syālaḥ adya āgacchati.*
>
> My wife's brother is coming today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्यालः** (*syālaḥ*) MW calls it of doubtful derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it gives "a wife's brother" and notes the spelling *śyāla* (MW).
