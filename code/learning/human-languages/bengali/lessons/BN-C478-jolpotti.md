## You'll want to know: জলপট্টি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C478-JOTNO478-04]; assesses=[] -->

**জলপট্টি** — *jôlpôṭṭi* — "a cold compress (a wet cloth on the forehead)".

> জ্বর কমাতে দিদিমা নাতির কপালে জলপট্টি দিলেন।
>
> *jbôr kômāte didimā nātir kôpāle jôlpôṭṭi dilen.*
>
> To bring the fever down, the grandmother laid a cold compress on her grandson's forehead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জলপট্টি** (*jôlpôṭṭi*) puts জল (taught), water, with *pôṭṭi*, "a strip of cloth", probably from Sanskrit *paṭṭa*, "a slab" (CDIAL 7699), which Monier-Williams also gives for a bandage or strip of cloth.
