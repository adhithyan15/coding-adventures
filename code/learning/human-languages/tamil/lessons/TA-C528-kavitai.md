## You'll want to know: கவிதை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C528-VASIPPU528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**கவிதை** — *kavitai* — "a poem; poetry".

> அவள் தன் அம்மாவைப் பற்றி ஒரு கவிதை எழுதினாள்.
>
> *avaḷ taṉ ammāvaip paṟṟi oru kavitai eḻutiṉāḷ.*
>
> She wrote a poem about her mother.

The plural is கவிதைகள். A poet is a கவிஞர்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கவிதை** (*kavitai*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kavitā*, "poetry; a poem" (Monier-Williams), with the long final vowel written -ஐ.
