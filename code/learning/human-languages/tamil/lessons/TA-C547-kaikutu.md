# கைகூடு (kaikūṭu) — to work out; to come true

## You'll want to know: கைகூடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C547-KAIKOODU547-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கைகூடு** — *kaikūṭu* — "to work out; to come true".

> எல்லாம் சரியாக நடந்தால் எங்கள் கனவு கைகூடும்.
>
> *ellām cariyāka naṭantāl eṅkaḷ kaṉavu kaikūṭum.*
>
> If everything goes right, our dream will come true.

Past (it …): கைகூடியது, *kaikūṭiyatu*.

The plan or wish is the subject, so the past is shown with அது: முயற்சி கைகூடியது, the effort succeeded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கைகூடு** (*kaikūṭu*) is a native compound: கை (*kai*), "a hand", and கூடு (*kūṭu*), "to come together, to be achieved" (DEDR 1882): to come into one's hand.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
