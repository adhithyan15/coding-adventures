## You'll want to know: लंघयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C526-ABHYASYATI526-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लंघयति** — *laṅghayati* — "leaps over".

> धावकः रज्जुं लंघयति।
>
> *dhāvakaḥ rajjuṃ laṅghayati.*
>
> The runner leaps over the rope.

Past (he …): सः रज्जुं लंघितवान्, *saḥ rajjuṃ laṅghitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लंघयति** (*laṅghayati*) comes from the root √laṅgh, "to leap over, to go beyond"; its causative *laṅghayati* has the same sense, and the past participle is *laṅghita* (MW).
