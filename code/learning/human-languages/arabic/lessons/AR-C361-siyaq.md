## You'll want to know: سياق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C361-QADIYYA361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سياق** — *siyāq* — "a context".

> فهمت معنى الكلمة من السياق.
>
> *fahimtu maʿnā al-kalima min as-siyāq.*
>
> I understood the meaning of the word from the context.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سياق** (*siyāq*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-w-q*, "to drive along" (Lane). A *siyāq* is a driving along in order; in modern use, the run of words around a word: its context.
