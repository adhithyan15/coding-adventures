Arabic, chapter 548: In the pool and the ring.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سباحة (sibāḥa) — swimming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a medal, then the Arabic for to qualify.

You'll want to know: سباحة.
سباحة — sibāḥa — "swimming".
السباحة رياضة صحية. as-sibāḥa riyāḍa ṣiḥḥiyya. Swimming is a healthy sport.

The word, taken apart.
سباحة (sibāḥa) is built on the native Arabic root s-b-ḥ, "to swim, to float" (Lane). Sabaḥa, to swim, which you know, is from the same root.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ملاكمة (mulākama) — boxing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to qualify, then the Arabic for swimming.

You'll want to know: ملاكمة.
ملاكمة — mulākama — "boxing".
يشاهد أبي الملاكمة على التلفاز. yushāhidu abī al-mulākama ʿalā at-tilfāz. My father watches boxing on television.

The word, taken apart.
ملاكمة (mulākama) is built on the native Arabic root l-k-m, "to strike with the fist" (Lane). Mulākama is the verbal noun of Form III: hitting one another with the fist.

صارع (ṣāraʿa) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-r-ʿ, "to throw down" (Lane). Form III ṣāraʿa is to try to throw one another down.

نافس (nāfasa) is built on the native Arabic root n-f-s, "a soul; to be precious" (Lane). Form III nāfasa is to vie with someone for a precious thing.

Lesson 5 of 5.
لكم (lakama) — to punch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to wrestle, then the Arabic for to compete with.

You'll want to know: لكم.
لكم — lakama — "to punch".
لكم الملاكم خصمه. lakama al-mulākim khaṣmahu. The boxer punched his opponent.
Present: يلكم, yalkum.

The word, taken apart.
لكم (lakama) is built on the native Arabic root l-k-m, "to strike with the fist" (Lane). Lane gives lakma as a blow with the fist; mulākama, boxing, is from the same root.
