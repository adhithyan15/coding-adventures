Marathi, chapter 348: Links and Likenesses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संबंध (sambandh) — a connection; a relationship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for neutral, then the Marathi for against.

You'll want to know: संबंध.
संबंध — sambandh — "a connection; a relationship".
या दोन घटनांचा एकमेकांशी काही संबंध नाही. yā don ghaṭnāñcā ekmekāṁśī kāhī sambandh nāhī. These two events have no connection with each other.
Masculine.
X चा Y शी संबंध (sambandh) असणे, X is connected with Y.

The word, taken apart.
संबंध (sambandh) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sambandha, "binding together, connection" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
निगडित (nigḍit) — linked (with).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a similarity, then the Marathi for a context.

You'll want to know: निगडित.
निगडित — nigḍit — "linked (with)".
आरोग्य आणि आहार एकमेकांशी निगडित आहेत. ārogya āṇi āhār ekmekāṁśī nigḍit āhet. Health and diet are linked with each other.
Takes -शी: X शी निगडित (nigḍit). Invariable.

The word, taken apart.
निगडित (nigḍit) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nigaḍita, "chained, fettered" (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for things bound up with each other.

Lesson 5 of 5.
याउलट (yāulaṭ) — on the contrary; by contrast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a context, then the Marathi for linked.

You'll want to know: याउलट.
याउलट — yāulaṭ — "on the contrary; by contrast".
भाऊ शांत आहे, याउलट बहीण खूप बोलकी आहे. bhāū śānt āhe, yāulaṭ bahīṇ khūp bolkī āhe. The brother is quiet; by contrast, the sister is very talkative.
या + उलट, this + the reverse.

The word, taken apart.
याउलट (yāulaṭ) is built from या, "this" (oblique), and उलट, "the reverse": the reverse of this. उलट goes with the verb उलटणे; CDIAL lists Marathi ulaṭṇẽ, "to be upset, to turn over", under ullaṭati, "turns over" (CDIAL 2368).
