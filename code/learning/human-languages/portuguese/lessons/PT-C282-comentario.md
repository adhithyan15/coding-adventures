## You'll want to know: o comentário
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C282-AFIRMAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o comentário** — "the comment, the remark".

> O presidente não fez nenhum comentário.
>
> *The president made no comment.*

*Sem comentários* — no comment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comentário* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commentārium* "notes, a commentary" (Lewis & Short), related to *comminīscī* "to devise".
