# તમે શું કરવાના છો? — asking about plans

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C150-VAANO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POLAR-SHUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I'm going tomorrow*, as a plan already made. Then ask *do you
speak Gujarati?* with **શું** in front.

## Grammar Lens: the question
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C150-VAANO-Q-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-SHUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-PRESENT-O-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01] -->

> **તમે આવતીકાલે શું કરવાના છો?** — *tame āvtīkāle śũ karvānā cho?* — What are you going to do tomorrow?

**તમે** takes **-વાના** and **છો**: respect takes the **-ા** forms here as it does
with **મારા પિતા**. Put **શું** at the front instead of inside, and the plan
becomes a yes-or-no question:

> **શું તમે આવવાના છો?** — *śũ tame āvvānā cho?* — Are you going to come?

## You'll want to know: the plan is off
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C150-VAANO-NEG-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NEGATE-01, GU-NEG-NATHI-01, GU-CONCEPT-C01-HAANAA-01] -->

**નથી** takes the copula's place, as in every present negative:

> **ના, હું નથી આવવાનો.** — *nā, hũ nathī āvvāno.* — No, I'm not going to come.
