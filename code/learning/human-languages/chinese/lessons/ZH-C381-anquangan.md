## You'll want to know: 安全感
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C381-FAMILY381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**安全感** — *ānquángǎn* — "a sense of security".

> **在这个小区里我很有安全感** *zài zhège xiǎoqū lǐ wǒ hěn yǒu ānquángǎn* — In this estate I feel really safe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**安全感** (*ānquángǎn*) is built from 安全 'safe' and 感 'to feel', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *'an*, *dzjwen* and *komX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last 'to move'.
