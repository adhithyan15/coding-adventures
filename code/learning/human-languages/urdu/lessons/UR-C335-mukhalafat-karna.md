# مخالفت کرنا (mukhālafat karnā) — to oppose

## You'll want to know: مخالفت کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C335-HIMAYAT335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مخالفت کرنا** — *mukhālafat karnā* — "to oppose".

> کسان نئے ٹیکس کی مخالفت کرتے ہیں۔
>
> *kisān naye ṭaiks kī mukhālafat karte haiṅ.*
>
> The farmers oppose the new tax.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے قانون کی مخالفت کی, *us ne qānūn kī mukhālafat kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخالفت کرنا** (*mukhālafat karnā*) is built from *mukhālafat*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mukhālafa*, "opposing", and **کرنا**, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
