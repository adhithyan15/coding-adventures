## You'll want to know: прощать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C471-HURT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**прощать** (*proshchát*) — "to forgive".

> Мама всегда прощает нас.
>
> *Máma vsegdá proshcháyet nas.*
>
> Mum always forgives us.

Imperfective; perfective простить (прощу, простишь), the verb behind *простите*. *Прощать кому что*: прощать другу ошибку.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прощать* is the imperfective partner of *простить* 'to forgive'; EtymDB links it to Old East Slavic *prostiti*, and both verbs are usually traced to *простой* 'simple, free', so to forgive is to set someone free.
