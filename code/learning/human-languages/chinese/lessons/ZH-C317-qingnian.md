## You'll want to know: qīngnián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C317-LOOKBACK317-01]; assesses=[] -->

*qīngnián* — "young people; youth".

> *tā shì yí ge hěn yǒu nénglì de qīngnián* — He's a very capable young man.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qīngnián* is built from *qīng* 'green' and 年 'years', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsheng* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the colour of the east: green years.
