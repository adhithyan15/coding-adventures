## You'll want to know: stertō, stertere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C448-STERTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**stertō, stertere** — "to snore".

> Hospes ēbrius tōtam noctem stertit.
>
> *The drunken guest snores all night.*

*stertō, stertere, stertuī*. English *stertorous* 'heavy and snoring' comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *stertō* is not settled: Lewis & Short compare Greek *darthanō* 'to sleep', but EtymDB gives no parent. English *stertor* and *stertorous* come from it.
