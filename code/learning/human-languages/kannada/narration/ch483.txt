Kannada, chapter 483: Dams and Groundwater.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಜಲಾಶಯ (jalāśaya) — a reservoir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to spurt, then the Kannada for to trickle.

You'll want to know: ಜಲಾಶಯ.
ಜಲಾಶಯ — jalāśaya — "a reservoir".
ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಜಲಾಶಯ ಪೂರ್ತಿ ತುಂಬಿದೆ. ī varṣa jalāśaya pūrti tumbide. This year the reservoir is completely full.
ಜಲ, water, plus ಆಶಯ, a receptacle.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಲಾಶಯ (jalāśaya) is built from two Sanskrit words: jala, "water" (CDIAL 5155), and āśaya, "a resting place, a receptacle" (CDIAL 1453).

ಅಣೆಕಟ್ಟಿನ ಹಿನ್ನೀರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಹಳೆಯ ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳು ಮುಳುಗಿದವು. aṇekaṭṭina hinnīrinalli haḷeya haḷḷigaḷu muḷugidavu. Old villages went under the backwater of the dam.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಅಂತರ್ಜಲ (antarjala) — groundwater.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a dam, then the Kannada for backwater.

You'll want to know: ಅಂತರ್ಜಲ.
ಅಂತರ್ಜಲ — antarjala — "groundwater".
ಬರಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಂತರ್ಜಲ ಕಡಿಮೆ ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ. baragāladalli antarjala kaḍime āguttade. In a drought the groundwater goes down.
ಅಂತರ್, within, plus ಜಲ, water: the water under the ground.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂತರ್ಜಲ (antarjala) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: antar, "within, inside" (Monier-Williams), and jala, "water" (CDIAL 5155).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಆವಿಯಾಗು (āviyāgu) — to evaporate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for backwater, then the Kannada for groundwater.

You'll want to know: ಆವಿಯಾಗು.
ಆವಿಯಾಗು — āviyāgu — "to evaporate".
ಬಿಸಿಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆರೆಯ ನೀರು ಬೇಗ ಆವಿಯಾಯಿತು. bisilinalli kereya nīru bēga āviyāyitu. In the sun the water of the lake soon evaporated.
Past (it …): ಆವಿಯಾಯಿತು, āviyāyitu.
ಆವಿ is steam or vapour; ಆಗು, to become.

The word, taken apart.
ಆವಿಯಾಗು (āviyāgu) is native: DEDR 393 lists Kannada āvi, āviri, "steam, vapour, heat", here joined with ಆಗು, to become.
