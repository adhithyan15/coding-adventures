Persian, chapter 279: Cypress, Plane tree, Palm tree, Tulip, Jasmine.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
سرو (sarv) — a cypress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a prize, then the Persian for respect.

You'll want to know: سرو.
سرو — sarv — "a cypress".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: sarv]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

[pause 3 seconds]

Pink? (صورتی, surati.) Dark? (تاریک, târik.) Boring? (کسل کننده, kesel-konande.) Useful? (مفید, mofid.) Traditional? (سنتی, sonnati.) Suitable? (مناسب, monâseb.) Possible? (ممکن, momken.) A heater? (بخاری, bokhâri.) A shelf? (قفسه, qafase.) A price? (قیمت, qeymat.) A discount? (تخفیف, takhfif.) A banknote? (اسکناس, eskenâs.)
