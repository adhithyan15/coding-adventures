## You'll want to know: apparecchiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C533-TAVOLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**apparecchiare** — "to lay (a table)".

> Apparecchio la tavola per sei persone.
>
> *I lay the dinner table for six people.*

*Apparecchiare la tavola*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apparecchiare* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *appariculāre* "to prepare", from Latin *apparāre*, built on *parāre* "to make ready" (Lewis & Short).
