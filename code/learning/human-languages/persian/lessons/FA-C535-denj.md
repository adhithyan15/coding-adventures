## You'll want to know: دنج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C535-COSY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دنج** (*denj*) — "cosy, quiet (of a place)".

> این کافه یک جای دنج است.
>
> *in kâfe yek jâ-ye denj ast.*
>
> This café is a cosy spot.

Of a quiet, tucked-away place, not of a person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*denj* is an everyday Persian word for a quiet, tucked-away place, but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled.
