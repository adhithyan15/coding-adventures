## You'll want to know: o turno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C358-GREVE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o turno** — "the shift; the turn".

> Esta semana trabalho no turno da noite.
>
> *This week I am on the night shift.*

*Por turnos* — in shifts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*turno* was borrowed from Spanish *turno* (EtymDB), built on *turnar* "to take turns", which goes back to French *tourner*, from Latin *tornāre* "to turn on a lathe" (Lewis & Short).
