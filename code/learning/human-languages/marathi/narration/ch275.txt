Marathi, chapter 275: Puzzle, Play, Film, Dance, Stage.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
कोडे (koḍe) — a puzzle, a riddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for worship, then the Marathi for an officer.

You'll want to know: कोडे.
कोडे — koḍe — "a puzzle, a riddle".

The word, taken apart.
कोडे (koḍe) matches Marathi kohḍẽ, "puzzle, riddle", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit kuha, "cheat" (CDIAL 3385).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an officer, then the Marathi for a puzzle.

You'll want to know: नाटक.
नाटक — nāṭak — "a play, a drama".

The word, taken apart.
नाटक (nāṭak) is Sanskrit nāṭaka, "dancer, actor", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 7045). It is built on naṭa, "actor". The sense "a play" is the later Sanskrit one.
