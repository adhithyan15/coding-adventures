## What you've built in two chapters, in three numbers
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-KUN-IN-THE-COUNT, JA-SCRIPT-RO-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YU-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-FIVE-COMPONENT-01, JA-LEX-ICHIDO] -->

**Ten numbers. Two new signs. Ninety-nine numbers you can now say.**

*Ichi* and *go* were yours before chapter fourteen opened. Only **ろ** and **ゅ**
had to be bought, one word each.

At **four** and **seven** the native word stands beside the borrowed reading; at
**nine** both readings are borrowed, and **eight** has no second name at all.

**五** is still the only one of the ten you can write as Japanese writes it. The
other nine kanji are the next thing this book owes you.
