## You'll want to know: درآمد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C378-DASTMOZD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**درآمد** (*darâmad*) — "an income".

> درآمد او از کار تازه بیشتر است.
>
> *darâmad-e u az kâr-e tâze bishtar ast.*
>
> His income from the new job is higher.

*Dar* "in" + *âmad* "came": what comes in. Plural *darâmadhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**درآمد** is native Persian, built from *dar* "in" + *âmad*, the past stem of *âmadan* "to come", which EtymDB traces to PIE *gʷem-* "to step". Income is what comes in.
