## You'll want to know: o namoro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C491-NAMORO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o namoro** — "relationship (between a couple who are dating)".

> O namoro deles durou cinco anos.
>
> *Their relationship lasted five years.*

The boyfriend is *o namorado*, the girlfriend *a namorada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*namoro* comes from *namorar* "to date", built on *amor* "love", from Latin *amor* (Lewis & Short).
