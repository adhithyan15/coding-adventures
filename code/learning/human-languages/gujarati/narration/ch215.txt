Gujarati, chapter 215: A cold foggy morning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઠરવું (ṭharvũ) — to freeze, to cool down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to drip, then the Gujarati for to get soaked.

You'll want to know: ઠરવું.
ઠરવું — ṭharvũ — "to freeze, to cool down".
રાત્રે પાણી ઠરશે. — rātre pāṇī ṭharśe — "The water will freeze at night."

The word, taken apart.
ઠરવું (ṭharvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from stharati, "is firm", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to become fixed, be settled, freeze, congeal" (CDIAL 13742).

Lesson 2 of 5.
હિમ (him) — frost, snow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get soaked, then the Gujarati for to freeze.

You'll want to know: હિમ.
હિમ — him — "frost, snow".
આવતીકાલે સવારે હિમ પડશે. — āvtīkāle savāre him paḍśe — "There will be frost tomorrow morning."
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
હિમ (him) is Sanskrit hima, "cold, frost, snow", taken over almost whole, a tatsama. CDIAL lists Gujarati him, "hoarfrost, dew" (CDIAL 14096).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઓસ (os) — dew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to freeze, then the Gujarati for frost.

You'll want to know: ઓસ.
ઓસ — os — "dew".
સવારે ઓસ પડશે. — savāre os paḍśe — "Dew will fall in the morning."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઓસ (os) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from avaśyā, "hoarfrost, dew" (CDIAL 855).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ગાઢ (gāḍh) — thick, dense.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for frost, then the Gujarati for dew.

You'll want to know: ગાઢ.
ગાઢ — gāḍh — "thick, dense".
કાલે સવારે ગાઢ ધુમ્મસ હશે. — kāle savāre gāḍh dhummas haśe — "There will be thick fog tomorrow morning."
It does not change for gender.

The word, taken apart.
ગાઢ (gāḍh) comes from Sanskrit gāḍha, "pressed together, thick, firm". CDIAL lists Gujarati gāḍh, "thick, sound (of sleep)" (CDIAL 4118).

Lesson 5 of 5.
હૂંફાળું (hū̃phāḷũ) — lukewarm, pleasantly warm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for dew, then the Gujarati for thick.

You'll want to know: હૂંફાળું.
હૂંફાળું — hū̃phāḷũ — "lukewarm, pleasantly warm".
હું હૂંફાળું પાણી પીશ. — hũ hū̃phāḷũ pāṇī pīś — "I will drink warm water."

The word, taken apart.
હૂંફાળું (hū̃phāḷũ) is built on hū̃ph, "warmth", a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Gujarati hũph, "genial warmth", under Sanskrit ūṣman, "heat, steam" (CDIAL 2441). The next entry, ūṣmāyate, "emits heat", holds the close Gujarati ũphāylũ, "lukewarm, tepid" (CDIAL 2442).
