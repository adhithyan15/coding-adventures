## You'll want to know: 出海
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C433-OVERSEAS433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**出海** — *chūhǎi* — "to go out to sea".

> **今天风很大我们不能出海** *jīntiān fēng hěn dà wǒmen bù néng chūhǎi* — It's very windy today, so we can't go out to sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**出海** (*chūhǎi*) is built from 出 'to go out' and 海 'the sea', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *xojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
