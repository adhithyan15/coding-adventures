# ಗಿರಿಧಾಮ (giridhāma) — a hill station

## You'll want to know: ಗಿರಿಧಾಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C458-YATRE458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಿರಿಧಾಮ** — *giridhāma* — "a hill station".

> ಬೇಸಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಗಿರಿಧಾಮದಲ್ಲಿ ತಣ್ಣಗೆ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *bēsigeyalli giridhāmadalli taṇṇage iruttade.*
>
> In summer it is cool at a hill station.

ಗಿರಿ, a hill, plus ಧಾಮ, a dwelling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಿರಿಧಾಮ** (*giridhāma*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: *giri*, "a rock, a mountain" (CDIAL 4161), and *dhāman*, "a dwelling place" (Monier-Williams).
