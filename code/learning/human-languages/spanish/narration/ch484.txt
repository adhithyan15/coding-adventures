Spanish, chapter 484: Sacar partido.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
utilizar — to use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the improvement, then the Spanish for to go.

You'll want to know: utilizar.
utilizar — "to use".
Utilice la otra puerta. Use the other door.
A formal twin of usar.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: utilizar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: utilizar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with utilizar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the improvement, then the Spanish for to go, then say utilizar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does utilizar mean? ("To use".) Say it once more.
