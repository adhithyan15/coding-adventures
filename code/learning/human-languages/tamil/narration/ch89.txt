Tamil, chapter 89: Sad, Worried, Calm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சோகம் (cōkam) — sadness, grief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for health, then the Tamil for sweat.

You'll want to know: சோகம்.
சோகம் (cōkam) — "sadness, grief".
சோகமாக இருக்கிறேன் — "I am sad". The -ஆக ending turns a feeling into how you are.

What you've built.
The first feeling.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: cōkam]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cōkam, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say cōkamāka irukkiṟēṉ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for health, then the Tamil for sweat, then say cōkam again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does சோகம் (cōkam) mean? ("Sadness, grief".) Say it once more.
