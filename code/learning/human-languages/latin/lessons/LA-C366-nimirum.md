## You'll want to know: nīmīrum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C366-PROINDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**nīmīrum** — "without doubt, surely".

> Nīmīrum vērum dīcis.
>
> *No doubt you are telling the truth.*

*Nīmīrum* = *nī mīrum*, no wonder. It can carry a smile of irony.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nīmīrum* is built from *nī* "not" and *mīrum* "a wonder" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): it is no wonder, so surely.
