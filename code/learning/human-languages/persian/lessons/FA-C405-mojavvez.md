## You'll want to know: مجوز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C405-QANUNI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مجوز** (*mojavvez*) — "a permit, a licence".

> برای ساختن خانه به مجوز نیاز داریم.
>
> *barâye sâkhtan-e khâne be mojavvez niyâz dârim.*
>
> We need a permit to build a house.

Plural *mojavvezhâ*. *Mojavvez gereftan* — to get a permit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجوز** is usually traced to Arabic *mujawwiz* "what permits, what allows".
