## You'll want to know: അംഗം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C422-SABHA422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**അംഗം** — *aṁgaṁ* — "a member".

> ഞാൻ ആ ക്ലബ്ബിലെ അംഗമാണ്.
>
> *ñān ā klabbile aṁgamāṇŭ.*
>
> I am a member of that club.

Plural: അംഗങ്ങൾ, *aṁgaṅṅaḷ*.

Plural: അംഗങ്ങൾ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അംഗം** (*aṁgaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *aṅga*, "a limb; the body" (CDIAL 114). A member is a limb of a body of people, as in English.
