## You'll want to know: огнетушитель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C295-POZHAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**огнетушитель** (*ognetushítel*) — "a fire extinguisher".

> Огнетушитель висит на стене.
>
> *Ognetushítel visít na stené.*
>
> The fire extinguisher hangs on the wall.

Masculine; genitive *огнетушителя*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*огнетушитель* joins *огонь* "fire", inherited through Old East Slavic *ognĭ* "fire" (EtymDB), and *тушить* "to put out": a fire-quencher.
