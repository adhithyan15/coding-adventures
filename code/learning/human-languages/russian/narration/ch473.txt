Russian, chapter 473: Disappointment and Relief.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
разочарование (razocharovániye) — disappointment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for fury, then the Russian for quick-tempered.

You'll want to know: разочарование.
разочарование (razocharovániye) — "disappointment".
Фильм стал для нас большим разочарованием. Film stal dlya nas bolshím razocharovániyem. The film was a big disappointment for us.
Neuter. The verbs are разочаровываться / разочароваться — to be disappointed.

The word, taken apart.
разочарование (razocharovániye) is built on разочаровать 'to break the spell', from очаровать 'to enchant' and чары 'spells'; EtymDB traces чары to Proto-Slavic čarъ 'magic, sorcery'.

Past participle of разочаровать, used as an adjective. Short form: разочарован в ком — disappointed in someone.

Lesson 3 of 5.
облегчение (oblegchéniye) — relief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for disappointment, then the Russian for disappointed.

You'll want to know: облегчение.
облегчение (oblegchéniye) — "relief".
Когда врач сказал, что всё в порядке, я почувствовал облегчение. Kogdá vrach skazál, chto vsyo v poryádke, ya pochúvstvoval oblegchéniye. When the doctor said everything was fine, I felt relief.
Neuter. С облегчением — with relief.

The word, taken apart.
облегчение (oblegchéniye) is built on облегчить 'to lighten', from лёгкий 'light, easy'; EtymDB lists облегчить without a source, and лёгкий is inherited from Proto-Slavic lьgъkъ (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
отчаяние (otcháyaniye) — despair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for disappointed, then the Russian for relief.

You'll want to know: отчаяние.
отчаяние (otcháyaniye) — "despair".
В отчаянии она позвонила сестре. V otcháyanii oná pozvoníla sestré. In despair she phoned her sister.
Neuter. The verbs are отчаиваться / отчаяться — to despair.

The word, taken apart.
отчаяние (otcháyaniye) is built on отчаяться 'to give up hope', from от- and чаять 'to hope', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic čajati 'to expect, to wait' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
вздыхать (vzdykhát) — to sigh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for relief, then the Russian for despair.

You'll want to know: вздыхать.
вздыхать (vzdykhát) — "to sigh".
Бабушка вздыхает и смотрит в окно. Bábushka vzdykháyet i smótrit v oknó. Grandma sighs and gazes out of the window.
Imperfective; perfective вздохнуть. The noun is вздох — a sigh.

The word, taken apart.
вздыхать (vzdykhát) is built on вз- 'up' and the root of дышать 'to breathe' and дух 'breath', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic duxъ 'breath, spirit'.
