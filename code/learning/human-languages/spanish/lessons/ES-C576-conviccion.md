## You'll want to know: la convicción
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C576-OPINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la convicción** — "the conviction, the firm belief".

> Lo digo con convicción.
>
> *I say it with conviction.*

*Tener la convicción de que* — to be firmly convinced that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convicción* is a learned borrowing from Latin *convictio* 'proof', built on *convincere* 'to overcome, to prove', from *vincere* 'to win' (EtymDB).
