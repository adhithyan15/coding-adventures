## You'll want to know: राजकुमार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C339-RAJKUMAR339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**राजकुमार** — *rājkumār* — "a prince".

> राजकुमार घोड़े पर जंगल में गया।
>
> *rājkumār ghoṛe par jaṅgal meṁ gayā.*
>
> The prince rode into the forest.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राजकुमार** (*rājkumār*) is Sanskrit *rājakumāra* (Monier-Williams), **राजा**, a king, and *kumāra*, a boy: a king's son.
