## You'll want to know: राखून ठेवणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C398-TIKITGHAR398-05]; assesses=[] -->

**राखून ठेवणे** — *rākhūn ṭhevṇe* — "to set aside; to keep back".

> मी तुझ्यासाठी एक जागा राखून ठेवली.
>
> *mī tujhyāsāṭhī ek jāgā rākhūn ṭhevlī.*
>
> I kept a seat aside for you.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने राखून ठेवलं, *tyāne rākhūn ṭhevlaṁ*.

राखणे is to guard or keep; with ठेवणे it means to hold something back for later. The verb agrees with the thing kept: जागा राखून ठेवली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राखून ठेवणे** (*rākhūn ṭhevṇe*) joins **राखून**, the -ऊन form of **राखणे**, "to keep, to guard", inherited from Sanskrit *rakṣati*, "guards" (CDIAL 10547), to **ठेवणे**, "to put, to keep".
