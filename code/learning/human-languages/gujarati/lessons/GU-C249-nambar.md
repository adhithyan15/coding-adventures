## You'll want to know: નંબર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C249-PHONE249-02]; assesses=[] -->

**નંબર** — *nambar* — "number".

> **તમારો ફોન નંબર શું છે?** — *tamāro phon nambar śũ che?* — "What is your phone number?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નંબર** (*nambar*) is a loan from English *number*, used for phone, house and bus numbers.
