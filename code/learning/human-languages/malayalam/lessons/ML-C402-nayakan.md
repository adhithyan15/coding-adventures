## You'll want to know: നായകൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C402-PURANAM402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**നായകൻ** — *nāyakan* — "a hero; a leader".

> കഥയിലെ നായകൻ ഒരു പാവം കർഷകൻ ആണ്.
>
> *kathayile nāyakan oru pāvaṁ karṣakan āṇŭ.*
>
> The hero of the story is a poor farmer.

A heroine is നായിക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നായകൻ** (*nāyakan*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nāyaka*, "a guide, a leader, a chief" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ.
