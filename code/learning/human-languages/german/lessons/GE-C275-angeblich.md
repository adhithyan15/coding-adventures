## You'll want to know: angeblich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C275-ANGEBLICH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**angeblich** — "supposedly, allegedly".

> Angeblich war der Dieb ein junger Mann.
>
> *Supposedly the thief was a young man.*

The speaker does not promise that it is true.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*angeblich* is built on *angeben* "to state", from *geben* "to give", Old High German *geban* (EtymDB): as it is stated.
