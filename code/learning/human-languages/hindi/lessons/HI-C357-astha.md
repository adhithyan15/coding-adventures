## You'll want to know: आस्था
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C357-MULYA357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आस्था** — *āsthā* — "belief, steady faith (in something)".

> मेरी दादी की पूजा में गहरी आस्था है।
>
> *merī dādī kī pūjā meṁ gahrī āsthā hai.*
>
> My grandmother has deep faith in worship.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आस्था** (*āsthā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āsthā*, "regard, care for" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for steady faith in something.
