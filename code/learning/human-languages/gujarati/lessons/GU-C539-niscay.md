# નિશ્ચય (niścay) — resolve (a firm decision)

## You'll want to know: નિશ્ચય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C539-MAKKAM539-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નિશ્ચય** — *niścay* — "resolve (a firm decision)".

> જો તમારો નિશ્ચય મક્કમ હશે તો તમે આ કામ પતાવી દેશો.
>
> *jo tamāro niścay makkam haśe to tame ā kām patāvī deśo.*
>
> If your resolve is firm, you will finish this work.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિશ્ચય** (*niścay*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *niścaya*, "certainty; resolve, a fixed intention" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for saying what happens if.
