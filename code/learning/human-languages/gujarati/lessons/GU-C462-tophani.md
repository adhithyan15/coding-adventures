# તોફાની (tophānī) — naughty

## You'll want to know: તોફાની
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C462-CANCAL462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તોફાની** — *tophānī* — "naughty".

> તોફાની છોકરાએ મારું રમકડું તોડ્યું.
>
> *tophānī chokrāe mārũ ramakḍũ toḍyũ.*
>
> The naughty boy broke my toy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તોફાની** (*tophānī*) is built on **તોફાન** (*tophān*), "a storm", traced to Arabic *ṭūfān*, "a flood", through Persian; with *-ī* it names a child who stirs up a storm.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
