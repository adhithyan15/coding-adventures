## You'll want to know: ожидать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C374-OZHIDANIYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ожидать** (*ozhidát*) — "to expect, to await".

> Мы не ожидали такого результата.
>
> *My ne ozhidáli takógo rezultáta.*
>
> We did not expect such a result.

Imperfective. It takes the genitive or the accusative: *ожидать ответа*. More formal than *ждать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ожидать** is built from *о-* and *-жидать*, the long shape of the root of *ждать* "to wait" (as *-бирать* stands beside *брать*); *ждать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*žĭdati* "to wait, to await" (EtymDB).
