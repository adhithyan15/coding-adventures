# ¿cómo estás? — how are you? (and a verb of *standing*)

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Your first real question, and your first verb. Two words, and each
opens a big English family — one about *mode*, one about *standing*.

## You'll want to know first

- [ES-C01-L01 (hola)](./ES-C01-L01-hola.md) — *¿cómo estás?* is the informal
  "how are you," the natural follow-on to an informal *hola*.

## Sounds you'll need

- `inverted-marks` — a question opens with an upside-down **¿** so you see
  the question coming: *¿cómo estás?*
- `accent-mark` — both words carry accents that place the stress: *CÓ-mo*,
  *es-TÁS*.

## The word, taken apart

**cómo** — how. Root: Latin **quomodo** — literally *quo modo*, "in what
**manner**." That *modo* ("manner, measure") is a word you own many times
over in English:

- **mode**, **mod**e of transport; **method**, **model**, **mod**erate;
- **modo** even survives whole in the music/logic term "modus."

So *cómo* is asking "in what mode/manner?" — "how?"

**estás** — you are. This is your first conjugated verb, from the infinitive
**estar** ("to be"). Root: Latin **stare** — and *stare* did not mean "to be."
It meant **"to stand."** That single root is one of the richest in all of
English:

- **st**and, **st**ate, **st**atus, **st**able, **st**atue, **st**ation,
  **st**ance;
- con**st**ant ("standing with"), circum**st**ance ("standing around"),
  sub**st**ance ("standing under"), ob**st**acle ("standing in the way").

Spanish took "to stand" and made it one of its two verbs for "to be" — the
one for **states and conditions**: how you *are* right now. Asking *¿cómo
estás?* is really asking "how are you *standing*?" — how do things stand with
you today.

## Why it's said this way

*¿cómo estás?* uses *estar*, the temporary/condition "to be" — because your
mood and health are passing states, not permanent identity. (Spanish has a
second "to be," *ser*, for identity — a distinction English doesn't make and
a lesson for later.) This is also the **informal** "how are you," matching
*tú* ("you," casual). The formal version swaps one letter: *¿cómo está?*
(with *usted*, the polite "you"). For now, *estás* is the friend-to-friend
form.
