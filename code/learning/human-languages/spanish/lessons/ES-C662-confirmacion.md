## You'll want to know: la confirmación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C662-RESERVA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la confirmación** — "the confirmation".

> Recibí la confirmación del vuelo.
>
> *I got the flight confirmation.*

It is also a church ceremony.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*confirmación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnfirmātiō* (EtymDB), from *cōnfirmāre* "to make firm", built on *firmus* "firm".
