# جھلانا (jhulānā) — to rock; to swing (someone)

## You'll want to know: جھلانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C484-RAISE484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جھلانا** — *jhulānā* — "to rock; to swing (someone)".

> دادی بچے کو جھلا رہی تھیں۔
>
> *dādī bachche ko jhulā rahī thīṅ.*
>
> My grandmother was rocking the baby.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو جھلایا, *us ne bachche ko jhulāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھلانا** (*jhulānā*) is the causative of *jhūlnā*, "to swing", which CDIAL lists under *jhulyati*, "swings", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5406).
