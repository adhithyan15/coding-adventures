## You'll want to know: обои
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C532-DIY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**обои** (*obói*) — "wallpaper".

> Мы выбрали светлые обои для спальни.
>
> *My výbrali svétlyye obói dlya spálni.*
>
> We chose light wallpaper for the bedroom.

Plural only; genitive обоев.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обои* is usually traced to *обить* 'to cover, to upholster', from *о-* and *бить* 'to beat'; EtymDB lists *обои* without a source.
