## You'll want to know: قرض گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C411-QARZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قرض گرفتن** (*qarz gereftan*) — "to borrow".

> از دوستم کمی پول قرض گرفتم.
>
> *az dustam kami pul qarz gereftam.*
>
> I borrowed a little money from my friend.

*Qarz* "loan" + *gereftan* "to take" (stem *gir-*). *Az kasi qarz gereftan* — to borrow from someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قرض گرفتن** is literally "to take a loan": *qarz* was borrowed from Arabic *qarḍ* "a loan" (EtymDB), and *gereftan* "to take" is inherited Persian.
