## You'll want to know: પહોંચાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C384-SAFAL384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પહોંચાડવું** — *pahõcāḍvũ* — "to deliver".

> જો ટપાલી સમયસર આવશે તો તે પત્ર આજે પહોંચાડશે.
>
> *jo ṭapālī samaysar āvśe to te patra āje pahõcāḍśe.*
>
> If the postman comes on time, he will deliver the letter today.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પાર્સલ ઘરે પહોંચાડ્યું, *teṇe pārsal ghare pahõcāḍyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પહોંચાડવું** (*pahõcāḍvũ*) is the causative of **પહોંચવું** (*pahõcvũ*), "to arrive", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *prábhūta*, "much, great" (CDIAL 8716): to make a thing arrive.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
