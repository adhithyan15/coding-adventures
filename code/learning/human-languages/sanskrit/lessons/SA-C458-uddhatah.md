## You'll want to know: उद्धतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C458-DARPA458-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्धतः** — *uddhataḥ* — "arrogant".

> उद्धतः छात्रः अध्यापकं न नमति।
>
> *uddhataḥ chātraḥ adhyāpakaṃ na namati.*
>
> The arrogant student does not bow to the teacher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्धतः** (*uddhataḥ*) is built from *ud*, "up", and the root √han, "to strike": raised up; MW gives "lifted up" and then "puffed up, haughty, arrogant, rude" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
