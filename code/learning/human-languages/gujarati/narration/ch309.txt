Lesson 3 of 5.
ઇનામ (inām) — a prize; a reward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an assembly, then the Gujarati for an honour.

You'll want to know: ઇનામ.
ઇનામ — inām — "a prize; a reward".
ગામના લોકોએ અરુણને ઇનામ આપ્યું. gāmnā lokoe Aruṇne inām āpyũ. The villagers gave Arun a reward.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઇનામ (inām) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic inʿām, "something bestowed". It is neuter: ઇનામ આપ્યું, gave a prize; ઇનામ જીત્યું, won one.

Lesson 4 of 5.
બિરદાવવું (birdāvvũ) — to applaud; to praise publicly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an honour, then the Gujarati for a prize.

You'll want to know: બિરદાવવું.
બિરદાવવું — birdāvvũ — "to applaud; to praise publicly".
બધાએ અરુણના સાહસને બિરદાવ્યું. badhāe Aruṇnā sāhasne birdāvyũ. Everyone applauded Arun's courage.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે તેને બિરદાવ્યો, teṇe tene birdāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
is built on birad, praise, from Sanskrit viruda, "a laudatory poem", which Monier-Williams also gives as birada. વખાણવું is to praise; બિરદાવવું (birdāvvũ) is to praise in public, before others.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ગર્વ (garva) — pride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a prize, then the Gujarati for to applaud.

You'll want to know: ગર્વ.
ગર્વ — garva — "pride".
માતાને દીકરા પર ખૂબ ગર્વ હતો. mātāne dīkrā par khūb garva hato. The mother was very proud of her son.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગર્વ (garva) is a tatsama, Sanskrit garva, "pride, arrogance" (CDIAL 4064). In Gujarati it is often good pride: દીકરા પર ગર્વ, proud of a son.
