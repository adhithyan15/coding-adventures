## You'll want to know: la reina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C568-REY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la reina** — "the queen".

> La reina inauguró el hospital.
>
> *The queen opened the hospital.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reina* is inherited from Latin *rēgīna* 'queen' (EtymDB), through old Spanish *reyna*. *Rēgīna* is built on *rēx* 'king'.
