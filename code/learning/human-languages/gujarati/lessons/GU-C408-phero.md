## You'll want to know: ફેરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C408-ROUNDS408-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેરો** — *phero* — "a round trip; a round".

> આ બસ દિવસમાં ચાર ફેરા કરે છે.
>
> *ā bas divasmā̃ cār pherā kare che.*
>
> This bus does four round trips a day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેરો** (*phero*) goes back to *\*phēra*, "a turn, a turning", a form CDIAL reconstructs beside *\*phirati*, "moves, turns"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9078). ફરવું, to go around, is its relative.
