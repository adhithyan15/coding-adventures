## You'll want to know: ਵਧਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C257-BANK257-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਧਣਾ** — *vadhṇā* — "to go up; to grow".

> ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਪੈਸੇ ਹੌਲੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਵਧਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *baiṅk vich paise haulī haulī vadhde han.*
>
> Money slowly grows in the bank.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਰੁੱਖ ਬਹੁਤ ਵਧਿਆ, *oh rukkh bahut vadhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਧਣਾ** (*vadhṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vardhate*, "grows, increases"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vadhṇā*, "to increase, grow, advance", under it (CDIAL 11376).
