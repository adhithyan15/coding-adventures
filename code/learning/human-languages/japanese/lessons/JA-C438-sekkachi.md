## You'll want to know: せっかち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C438-STUBBORN438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**せっかち** — *sekkachi* — "impatient; hasty".

> **ちちは せっかちです。** — *chichi wa sekkachi desu* — "My father is impatient."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せっかち** (*sekkachi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; JMdict writes it in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
