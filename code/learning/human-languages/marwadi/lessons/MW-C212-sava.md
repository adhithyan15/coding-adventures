## You'll want to know: सवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C212-AMOUNT212-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सवा** — *savā* — "plus a quarter".

> सवा सेर आटो दो।
>
> *savā ser āṭo do.*
>
> Give me a seer and a quarter of flour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सवा** (*savā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sapāda*, "increased by a fourth"; CDIAL lists Marwari *savā*, "plus a quarter" (CDIAL 13134).
