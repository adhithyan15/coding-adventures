Punjabi, chapter 163: Return to Making, Sewing, Filling, and Pulling.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
An old word for making — to make, recalled after a long gap.

This is one familiar action, heard again after a long gap. Say the word before the model if you can. There is no new Punjabi or scored A1 mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to make.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਬਣਾਉਣਾ (baṇāuṇā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਬਣਾਉਣਾ (baṇāuṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to make. Now hear to make once more.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਬਣਾਉਣਾ (baṇāuṇā) before the model. One word is enough; no sentence is needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
One last cue: to make.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਬਣਾਉਣਾ (baṇāuṇā). Listen and retry once if the word does not come back.


Lesson 2 of 4.
An old word for sewing — to sew, recalled after a long gap.

Retrieve one action you met earlier. Hear the meaning first; there is no need to sew anything. This short return is not scored A1 mock work.

Warm-up.
Hear to sew.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਸਿਉਣਾ (siuṇā) before the model.

Guided Practice.
Hear ਸਿਉਣਾ (siuṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall its meaning, to sew. Then hear to sew again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਸਿਉਣਾ (siuṇā). One familiar word is the whole answer.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to sew once more.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਸਿਉਣਾ (siuṇā). If needed, listen to the model and try again once.


Lesson 3 of 4.
An old word for filling — to fill, recalled after a long gap.

Return to one familiar action. Listen, pause, and say the old word; no new words, sentences, or scored A1 mock are added here.

Warm-up.
Hear to fill.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਭਰਨਾ (bharnā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਭਰਨਾ (bharnā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to fill. Now hear to fill.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਭਰਨਾ (bharnā) before the model. Only this old meaning and word are needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to fill again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਭਰਨਾ (bharnā). Listen and retry once if the word feels distant.


Lesson 4 of 4.
An old word for pulling — to pull, then a return to four familiar actions.

First retrieve to pull by itself. Then hear four already-taught actions in a small shuffled mix. Pause before each model. This is not scored A1 writing or a complete mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to pull.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਖਿੱਚਣਾ (khiccṇā).

Guided Practice.
Pause after each familiar meaning, then hear its model:
to fill —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਭਰਨਾ (bharnā).
to make —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਬਣਾਉਣਾ (baṇāuṇā).
to pull —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਖਿੱਚਣਾ (khiccṇā).
to sew —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਸਿਉਣਾ (siuṇā).
Only the order of these four old words has changed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to sew, to pull, to make, then to fill. Pause after each cue and recall its Punjabi word before the model: ਸਿਉਣਾ (siuṇā), ਖਿੱਚਣਾ (khiccṇā), ਬਣਾਉਣਾ (baṇāuṇā), ਭਰਨਾ (bharnā). Revisit only the old word you missed.
