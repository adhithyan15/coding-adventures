## You'll want to know: புனை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C343-PAZHANKATHAI343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**புனை** — *puṉai* — "to make up; to compose".

> அவன் ஒரு பொய்யான காரணத்தைப் புனைந்தான்.
>
> *avaṉ oru poyyāṉa kāraṇattaip puṉaintāṉ.*
>
> He made up a false reason.

Past (I …): புனைந்தேன், *puṉaintēṉ*.

Also to compose a poem; புனைபெயர், a pen name.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**புனை** (*puṉai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4340): to dress, adorn, make, form.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
