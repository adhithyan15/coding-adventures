## You'll want to know: যন্ত্রণা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C477-JBOLUNI477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**যন্ত্রণা** — *jôntrônā* — "agony; torment; severe pain".

> দাঁতের যন্ত্রণায় দাদু সারারাত জেগে ছিলেন।
>
> *dā̃ter jôntrônāy dādu sārārāt jege chhilen.*
>
> Grandfather lay awake all night in agony from his tooth.

ব্যথা (taught) is any pain; যন্ত্রণা is pain that torments, of the body or of the mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**যন্ত্রণা** (*jôntrônā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *yantraṇā*, which Monier-Williams gives first for restraint and constraint and then for pain and anguish; Bengali keeps the pain.
