## You'll want to know: కబురు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C68-SOUND-05]; assesses=[] -->

**కబురు** (*kaburu*) — "a message, news".

కబురు is a message: word sent, or news arriving.

It came into Telugu from Arabic by way of Persian, and it was worn in so long ago that it stopped feeling borrowed. It is the word for what an అరుపు is for — getting something to somebody who is not there.

Sending కబురు is a fixed pair of words in the countryside, and the greeting at the head of a message is part of the message. Names travel this way too: a కబురు usually opens by saying whose it is.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Five: శబ్దం, గొంతు, పాట, అరుపు, కబురు. Sound, and the whole way it gets from one person to another.
