## You'll want to know: आश्वसिति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C451-ANUNAYA451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्वसिति** — *āśvasiti* — "takes heart".

> वैद्यः आगच्छति, माता आश्वसिति च।
>
> *vaidyaḥ āgacchati, mātā āśvasiti ca.*
>
> The physician comes, and the mother takes heart.

Past (she …): सा आश्वस्तवती, *sā āśvastavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्वसिति** (*āśvasiti*) is built from *ā*, "towards", and the root √śvas, "to breathe": to breathe again; MW gives "to take or recover breath" and "take heart" (MW).
