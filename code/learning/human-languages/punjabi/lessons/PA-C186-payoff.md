# A gentle third-person future payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186, PA-FORM-HOVEGI-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Without looking, say one future form ending in **ਾ** and one ending
in **ੀ** for each verb.

## Guided Practice: four skills, bounded to the two pairs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186, PA-FORM-HOVEGI-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186, PA-LEX-KAMM-05, PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01, PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-TRAVEL-RAIL-01] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *kamm hovegaa, yaatraa hovegii, Aman aavegaa, rel aavegii*; YOU SAY: **ਕੰਮ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ, ਯਾਤਰਾ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ, ਅਮਨ ਆਵੇਗਾ, ਰੇਲ ਆਵੇਗੀ**]
- [YOU READ: **ਕੰਮ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ। ਯਾਤਰਾ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ। ਅਮਨ ਆਵੇਗਾ। ਰੇਲ ਆਵੇਗੀ।**]
- [YOU WRITE: cover the model and write **ਹੋਵੇਗਾ, ਹੋਵੇਗੀ, ਆਵੇਗਾ, ਆਵੇਗੀ**]

Now underline the two masculine endings and circle the two feminine endings.
