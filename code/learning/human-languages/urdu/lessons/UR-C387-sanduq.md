## You'll want to know: صندوق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C387-QULI387-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صندوق** — *sandūq* — "a trunk; a chest".

> قلی نے میرا صندوق اٹھایا۔
>
> *qulī ne merā sandūq uṭhāyā.*
>
> The porter picked up my trunk.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صندوق** (*sandūq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣundūq*, "a chest, a box".
