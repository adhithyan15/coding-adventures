## You'll want to know: favoriser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C352-FREIN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**favoriser** — "to favour, to encourage".

> Le sport favorise un bon sommeil.
>
> *Exercise encourages good sleep.*

To help something happen; also to treat someone better than others.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*favoriser* is built on *faveur* "favour", from Latin *favor* "goodwill, favour" (Lewis & Short), from *favēre* "to be well disposed".
