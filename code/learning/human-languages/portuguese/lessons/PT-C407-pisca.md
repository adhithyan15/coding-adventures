## You'll want to know: o pisca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C407-CAPO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o pisca** — "the indicator, the turn signal".

> Ele nunca liga o pisca antes de virar.
>
> *He never puts the indicator on before turning.*

Short for *o pisca-pisca*. *Piscar o olho* — to wink.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pisca* is built on *piscar* 'to blink'. The origin of *piscar* is not settled; EtymDB has no entry for it.
