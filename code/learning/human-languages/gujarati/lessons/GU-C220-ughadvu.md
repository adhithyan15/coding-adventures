# ઊઘડવું (ūghaḍvũ) — to clear up, to open

## You'll want to know: ઊઘડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C220-RIVER220-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊઘડવું** — *ūghaḍvũ* — "to clear up, to open".

> **વરસાદ પછી આકાશ ઊઘડશે.** — *varsād pachī ākāś ūghaḍśe* — "The sky will clear after the rain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊઘડવું** (*ūghaḍvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*udghaṭati*, "is open", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to open, bloom, be free from rain" (CDIAL 1968).
