## You'll want to know: غطى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C356-JUMHUR356-05]; assesses=[] -->

**غطى** — *ghaṭṭā* — "to cover; to report on".

> غطى المراسل المباراة من الملعب.
>
> *ghaṭṭā al-murāsil al-mubārāh min al-malʿab.*
>
> The correspondent covered the match from the stadium.

Present: يغطي, *yughaṭṭī*.

Of a blanket too: غطت الأم الطفل.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غطى** (*ghaṭṭā*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-ṭ-w* (Lane), the root of the taught *ghiṭāʾ*, a cover. It is Form II, "to put a cover over"; the press sense matches English cover and is usually taken as modelled on it.
