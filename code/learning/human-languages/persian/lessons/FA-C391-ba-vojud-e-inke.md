# با وجود اینکه (bâ vojud-e inke) — even though, despite the fact that

## You'll want to know: با وجود اینکه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C391-FARZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**با وجود اینکه** (*bâ vojud-e inke*) — "even though, despite the fact that".

> با وجود اینکه باران میآمد، بیرون رفتیم.
>
> *bâ vojud-e inke bârân miâmad, birun raftim.*
>
> Even though it was raining, we went out.

It takes an indicative clause, like *agarche*. With a noun, *bâ vojud-e* alone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**با وجود اینکه** is literally "with the existence of the fact that": *vojud* comes from Arabic *wujūd* "existence" (EtymDB), and *bâ* "with" and *inke* "that" are Persian.
