## You'll want to know: ликовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C508-FANS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ликовать** (*likovát*) — "to rejoice".

> После победы весь стадион ликовал.
>
> *Pósle pobédy ves stadión likovál.*
>
> After the victory the whole stadium rejoiced.

Imperfective; present ликую, ликуешь. Rather bookish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ликовать* is usually traced to Church Slavonic *ликъ* 'a chorus, a round dance', so rejoicing was first dancing; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
