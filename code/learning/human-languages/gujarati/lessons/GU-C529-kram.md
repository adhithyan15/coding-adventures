## You'll want to know: ક્રમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C529-TABAKKO529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ક્રમ** — *kram* — "a sequence".

> જો તમે આ ક્રમ બદલશો તો બધું બગડી જશે.
>
> *jo tame ā kram badalśo to badhũ bagḍī jaśe.*
>
> If you change this order, everything will go wrong.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ક્રમ** (*kram*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *krama*, "a step; going, a course" (Monier-Williams): one step after another.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
