## You'll want to know: دستمزد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C378-DASTMOZD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دستمزد** (*dastmozd*) — "a wage, pay".

> دستمزد کارگرها کم است.
>
> *dastmozd-e kârgarhâ kam ast.*
>
> The workers' wages are low.

*Dast* "hand" + *mozd* "wage". For daily or hourly pay; a monthly salary is *hoquq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دستمزد** is a native Persian compound built from *dast* "hand", which EtymDB traces to PIE *ǵʰes-* "hand", and *mozd* "wage", usually traced to Middle Persian *mizd*: pay for the work of your hands.
