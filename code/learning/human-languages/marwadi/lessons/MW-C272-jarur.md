## You'll want to know: जरूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C272-PHAGAT272-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जरूर** — *jarūr* — "certainly".

> हूं जरूर आसूं।
>
> *hū̃ jarūr āsū̃.*
>
> I'll certainly come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जरूर** (*jarūr*) is usually traced, through Persian *zarūr*, "necessary; certainly", to Arabic *ḍarūra*, "necessity". **जरूरी**, necessary, and **जरूरत**, a need, come from the same word.
