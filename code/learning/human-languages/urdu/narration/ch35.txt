Now say جی ہاں (jī hā̃), "yes". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ج.
ج — jīm, the sound j.
It opens جی (jī) in جی ہاں, "yes", from the first chapter. A hooked head, a deep bowl, and one dot below.

Writing: ج.
1. start here: place the dot below.
2. lift, then sweep left through the pointed hooked head.
3. without lifting, continue down and around the bowl — and only now lift.
Pen lifts: 1.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Zer o Zabar, Te, mim, jim, che, and more diacritics, independent ج (jīm) handwriting animation and flat-head insight (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

Script you'll notice: ح.
ح — baṛī he, "big he", the sound h.
It opens حافظ (hāfiz), the second half of خدا حافظ. Urdu already has an h, ہ, which you write; this one came in with Arabic words and sounds the same. It is the hook-and-bowl skeleton of ج (jīm), with no dots.

Writing: ح.
1. start here: sweep left through the pointed hooked head.
2. continue down and around the deep bowl without lifting — and only now lift.
Pen lifts: 0. The pen never leaves the paper.

Now say سوچنا (sochnā), "to think". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: چ.
چ — che, the sound ch.
It is inside سوچنا (sochnā), "to think". The same skeleton once more, with three dots below: one letter, four sounds, and the dots decide which.

Writing: چ.
1. start here: sweep left through the pointed hooked head.
2. continue down and around the bowl without lifting.
3. after one lift, place the lower-left dot.
4. after another lift, place the lower-right dot.
5. after a third lift, place the lower-center dot — and only now lift.
Pen lifts: 3.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Zer o Zabar, Te, mīm, jīm, che, and more diacritics, independent چ calligraphic and handwriting animations and Che instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).
