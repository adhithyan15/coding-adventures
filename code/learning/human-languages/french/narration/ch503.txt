French, chapter 503: Le jardinage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le jardinage — gardening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to frame, then the French for tripod.

You'll want to know: le jardinage.
le jardinage — "gardening".
Le jardinage me détend après le travail. Gardening relaxes me after work.
Faire du jardinage — to do some gardening.

The word, taken apart.
le jardinage is built on jardin "garden" with -age; jardin goes back to Frankish gardo "an enclosure", a Germanic word.

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.
arroser — to water (plants).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to garden, then the French for to sow.

You'll want to know: arroser.
arroser — "to water (plants)".
Pendant les vacances, le voisin arrose nos plantes. During the holidays the neighbour waters our plants.
Informally, to celebrate with a drink: on va arroser ça.

The word, taken apart.
arroser comes from Late Latin arrōsāre "to bedew", built on Latin rōs "dew" (Lewis & Short).
