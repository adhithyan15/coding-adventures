## You'll want to know: चिकाटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C464-HIMMAT464-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चिकाटी** — *cikāṭī* — "perseverance".

> चिकाटीने सराव केला की यश मिळतं.
>
> *cikāṭīne sarāv kelā kī yaś miḷtaṁ.*
>
> If you practise with perseverance, success comes.

Feminine.

Feminine; चिकाटी सोडणे, to give up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिकाटी** (*cikāṭī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled; it is often linked with चिकट, "sticky".
