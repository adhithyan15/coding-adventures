## You'll want to know: নেশা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C480-PUSHTI480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**নেশা** — *neshā* — "an addiction; intoxication".

> ফোন দেখা অরুণের একটা নেশা।
>
> *phon dækhā ôruner ekṭā neshā.*
>
> Looking at his phone is an addiction for Arun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নেশা** (*neshā*) is usually traced to Persian *nasha*, "intoxication".
