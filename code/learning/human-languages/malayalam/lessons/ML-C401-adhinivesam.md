# അധിനിവേശം (adhinivēśaṁ) — an invasion; occupation

## You'll want to know: അധിനിവേശം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C401-VIPLAVAM401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**അധിനിവേശം** — *adhinivēśaṁ* — "an invasion; occupation".

> വിദേശ അധിനിവേശം നൂറ് വർഷം നീണ്ടു.
>
> *vidēśa adhinivēśaṁ nūṟŭ varṣaṁ nīṇṭu.*
>
> The foreign occupation lasted a hundred years.

A learned word of history books and the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അധിനിവേശം** (*adhinivēśaṁ*) is a learned word usually traced to Sanskrit *adhi*, "over, upon", and *niveśa*, "settling, a camp": to settle on top of someone else's land.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
