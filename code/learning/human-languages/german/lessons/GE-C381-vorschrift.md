## You'll want to know: die Vorschrift
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C381-VORSCHRIFT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Vorschrift** — "the regulation".

> Nach der Vorschrift muss man im Labor eine Schutzbrille tragen.
>
> *According to the regulations you have to wear goggles in the lab.*

Plural: *die Vorschriften*. *Dienst nach Vorschrift* — work-to-rule.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vorschrift* goes with *vorschreiben* "to lay down", built on *Schrift* "writing", from Old High German *scrift* and Latin *scrīptum* (EtymDB): a rule written out in front of you.
