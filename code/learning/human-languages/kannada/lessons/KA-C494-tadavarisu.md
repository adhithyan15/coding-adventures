# ತಡವರಿಸು (taḍavarisu) — to fumble; to falter

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C493-KOPA493-05, KA-LEX-C494-BERAGU494-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for agitation, then the Kannada for to shrink back.

## You'll want to know: ತಡವರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C494-BERAGU494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಡವರಿಸು** — *taḍavarisu* — "to fumble; to falter".

> ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ ಕೇಳಿದಾಗ ಅವನು ಉತ್ತರಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ತಡವರಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *praśne kēḷidāga avanu uttarakkāgi taḍavarisidanu.*
>
> When he was asked, he fumbled for an answer.

Past (I …): ತಡವರಿಸಿದೆ, *taḍavariside*.

For words: ಮಾತಿಗೆ ತಡವರಿಸು, to grope for words. In the dark it is feeling your way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಡವರಿಸು** (*taḍavarisu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3025 gives Kannada *taḍavu*, "to touch gently, grope", and *taḍa-varisu*, "to feel one's way with the hands or feet".
