Bengali, chapter 388: Trams, the Metro and Autos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ট্রাম (ṭrām) — a tram.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to commute, then the Bengali for return (of a journey).

You'll want to know: ট্রাম.
ট্রাম — ṭrām — "a tram".
ট্রামটা ধীরে চলে, কিন্তু ভাড়া সস্তা। ṭrāmṭā dhīre chôle, kintu bhāṛā sôstā. The tram is slow, but the fare is cheap.

The word, taken apart.
ট্রাম (ṭrām) is borrowed from English tram, short for tramway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a tram, then the Bengali for the metro.

You'll want to know: অটো.
অটো — ôṭo — "an autorickshaw".
স্টেশন থেকে আমরা অটোতে বাড়ি ফিরলাম। sṭeshôn theke āmrā ôṭote bāṛi phirlām. We came home from the station by auto.
The three-wheeler with a motor; a রিকশা is pulled or pedalled.

The word, taken apart.
অটো (ôṭo) is borrowed from English auto, clipped from autorickshaw; the first letter is অ, said as an open ô.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ভ্যান (bhæn) — a van; a cycle van.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the metro, then the Bengali for an autorickshaw.

You'll want to know: ভ্যান.
ভ্যান — bhæn — "a van; a cycle van".
একটা ভ্যান আমাদের মালপত্র স্টেশনে নিয়ে গেল। ekṭā bhæn āmāder mālpôtrô sṭeshône niye gelô. A van took our luggage to the station.
In a small town a ভ্যান (bhæn) is often a pedalled tricycle with a flat wooden bed.

The word, taken apart.
ভ্যান (bhæn) is borrowed from English van; Bengali has no v, so the word begins with ভ, and the y-phala gives the English vowel æ.

Lesson 5 of 5.
দৌড়ানো (douṛāno) — to race; to run fast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an autorickshaw, then the Bengali for a van.

You'll want to know: দৌড়ানো.
দৌড়ানো — douṛāno — "to race; to run fast".
অরুণ দৌড়ে বাসে উঠল। ôrun douṛe bāse uṭhlô. Arun ran and got on the bus.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দৌড়ালেন, tini douṛālen.
The -এ form দৌড়ে is the one you hear most: দৌড়ে এল, came running.
The first vowel sign is ৌ, the vowel ou.

The word, taken apart.
দৌড়ানো (douṛāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali dauṛā and dauṛāna, "to run", under Sanskrit drávati, "runs" (CDIAL 6624), and notes that the word probably came east from the languages to the west.
