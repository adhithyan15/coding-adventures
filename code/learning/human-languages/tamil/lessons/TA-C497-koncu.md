# கொஞ்சு (koñcu) — to coo over; to fondle

## You'll want to know: கொஞ்சு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C497-VALARPPU497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கொஞ்சு** — *koñcu* — "to coo over; to fondle".

> தாத்தா பேத்தியைக் கொஞ்சினார்.
>
> *tāttā pēttiyaik koñciṉār.*
>
> Grandfather cooed over his granddaughter.

Past (I …): கொஞ்சினேன், *koñciṉēṉ*.

Also of a child’s sweet prattle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கொஞ்சு** (*koñcu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2043), for loving baby talk and fondling.
