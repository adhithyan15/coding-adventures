Marathi, chapter 490: Staying Healthy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पथ्य (pathya) — a diet regimen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a stitch, then the Marathi for to prick.

You'll want to know: पथ्य.
पथ्य — pathya — "a diet regimen".
आजारपणात आजीने कडक पथ्य पाळलं. ājārpaṇāt ājīne kaḍak pathya pāḷlaṁ. During her illness Grandmother kept to a strict diet.
Neuter.
पथ्य (pathya) पाळणे, to keep to a prescribed diet; its opposite is कुपथ्य.

The word, taken apart.
पथ्य (pathya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pathya, "suitable" (CDIAL 7746): what suits the patient.

Lesson 2 of 5.
निरोगी (nirogī) — healthy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to prick, then the Marathi for a diet regimen.

You'll want to know: निरोगी.
निरोगी — nirogī — "healthy".
रोज चालल्याने आपण निरोगी राहतो. roj cāllyāne āpaṇ nirogī rāhto. Walking every day keeps us healthy.
नि-, without, + रोग, disease, + -ई. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
निरोगी (nirogī) is a Marathi form built on Sanskrit nīroga, "free from sickness, healthy" (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ī.

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रतिकारशक्ती (pratikārśaktī) — immunity; resistance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a diet regimen, then the Marathi for healthy.

You'll want to know: प्रतिकारशक्ती.
प्रतिकारशक्ती — pratikārśaktī — "immunity; resistance".
चांगल्या आहारामुळे प्रतिकारशक्ती वाढते. cāṅglyā āhārāmuḷe pratikārśaktī vāḍhte. A good diet builds up immunity.
Feminine.
प्रतिकार, resistance, + शक्ती, power.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिकारशक्ती (pratikārśaktī) is a modern learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, pratikāra, "requital, counteraction", and śakti, "power" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for healthy, then the Marathi for immunity.

You'll want to know: शेकणे.
शेकणे — śekṇe — "to apply heat; to warm".
आजीने गरम पाण्याच्या पिशवीने पाठ शेकली. ājīne garam pāṇyācyā piśvīne pāṭh śeklī. Grandmother warmed her back with a hot-water bag.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाठ शेकली, tyāne pāṭh śeklī.
Gentle heat on a sore spot; शेक, a warm compress.

The word, taken apart.
शेकणे (śekṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi śekṇẽ, "to warm oneself before a fire, foment", under the reconstructed sēkk (CDIAL 13581).

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: चोळणे.
चोळणे — coḷṇe — "to rub".
थंडीत त्याने हात चोळले. thaṇḍīt tyāne hāt coḷle. He rubbed his hands in the cold.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने हात चोळले, tyāne hāt coḷle.
Of oil or a sore spot: तेल चोळणे (coḷṇe), to rub in oil.

The word, taken apart.
चोळणे (coḷṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
