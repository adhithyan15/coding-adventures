## You'll want to know: 家教
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C493-TUTOR493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**家教** — *jiājiào* — "private tutor".

> **大学的时候我当过家教** *dàxué de shíhou wǒ dāng guo jiājiào* — At university I worked as a private tutor.

The new character, in pieces: 教 — eleven strokes in two pieces: on the left the top of 老 *lǎo* with 子 *zǐ* tucked under it, seven strokes; on the right four strokes, two falling to the left, a short bar and a long stroke falling to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**家教** (*jiājiào*) is built from 家 'a home' and 教 'to teach', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kae* and *kaewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to instruct'. Here 教 keeps the falling tone, jiào.
