## You'll want to know: しゃしょう

**しゃしょう** — *shashō* — "a train conductor".

> **しゃしょうに きっぷを わたしました。** — *shashō ni kippu o watashimashita* — "I handed my ticket to the conductor."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃしょう** (*shashō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sha*, "car" (Middle Chinese *tsyhae*) and *shō*, "administer" (*tsyangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
