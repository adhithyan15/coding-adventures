## You'll want to know: अफ़वाह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C314-AFVAH314-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अफ़वाह** — *afvāh* — "a rumour".

> बाज़ार में एक अफ़वाह फैली।
>
> *bāzār meṁ ek afvāh phailī.*
>
> A rumour spread through the market.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अफ़वाह** (*afvāh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *afwāh*, "mouths": what goes from mouth to mouth. It is feminine: **अफ़वाह फैली**.
