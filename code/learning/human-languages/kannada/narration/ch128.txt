Kannada, chapter 128: Lightning, Storm, Wave, Waterfall, Island.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮಿಂಚು (miṁcu) — lightning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a stone, then the Kannada for thunder.

You'll want to know: ಮಿಂಚು.
ಮಿಂಚು — miṁcu — "lightning".

The word, taken apart.
ಮಿಂಚು (miṁcu) is native Dravidian, from the root for "to glitter", and DEDR 4876 lists miñcu, "to shine, flash", itself; the entry also has Tamil miṉ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಅಲೆ (ale) — a wave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for lightning, then the Kannada for a storm.

You'll want to know: ಅಲೆ.
ಅಲೆ — ale — "a wave".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಲೆ (ale) is native Dravidian, from ale, "to move to and fro, shake", which DEDR 240 lists itself; the entry also has Telugu ala and Malayalam ala. DEDR 240 compares, with a query, Sanskrit alaka.
