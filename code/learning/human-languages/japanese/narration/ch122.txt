Lesson 1 of 5.
ふかい (fukai) — deep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for thin, then the Japanese for shallow.

You'll want to know: ふかい.
ふかい — fukai — "deep".

The word, taken apart.
ふかい (fukai) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

たいせつ — taisetsu — "precious".

The word, taken apart.
たいせつ (taisetsu) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are tai, "big, great" (Middle Chinese dajH) and setsu, "cut, sharp" (tshet) (Unihan, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese after Baxter–Sagart).
