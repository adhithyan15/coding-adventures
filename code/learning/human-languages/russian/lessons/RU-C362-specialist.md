## You'll want to know: специалист
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C362-POVYSHENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**специалист** (*spetsialíst*) — "a specialist, an expert".

> Он хороший специалист по компьютерам.
>
> *On khoróshiy spetsialíst po kompyúteram.*
>
> He is a good computer specialist.

Masculine, also of a woman. *Специалист по* + dative — an expert in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**специалист** is usually traced to German *Spezialist*, built on Latin *speciālis* "particular", from *speciēs* "a look, a kind" (Lewis & Short).
