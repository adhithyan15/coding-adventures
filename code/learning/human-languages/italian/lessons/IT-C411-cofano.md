## You'll want to know: il cofano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C411-COFANO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il cofano** — "the bonnet (of a car); the chest".

> Il meccanico apre il cofano.
>
> *The mechanic opens the bonnet.*

In American English, the hood. Its old sense is a chest or coffer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cofano* is inherited from Latin *cophinus* 'basket' (EtymDB), from Greek *kóphinos*; it first meant a chest or coffer.
