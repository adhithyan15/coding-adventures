## You'll want to know: dìnghūn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C305-ENGAGE305-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dìnghūn* — "to get engaged".

> *tāmen shàng ge yuè dìnghūn le* — They got engaged last month.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìnghūn* is built from *dìng* 'to agree on' and *hūn* 'marriage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dengX* and *xwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to discuss and settle' and the second 'marriage'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
