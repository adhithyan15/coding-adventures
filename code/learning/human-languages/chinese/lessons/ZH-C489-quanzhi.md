## You'll want to know: 全职
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C489-STAFF489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**全职** — *quánzhí* — "full-time (of a job)".

> *quánzhí de gōngzuò bǐ dǎgōng lèi* — A full-time job is more tiring than casual work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**全职** (*quánzhí*) is built from 全 'whole' and 职 'a post', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjwen* and *tsyik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'complete'.
