Kannada, chapter 334: A Baby in the Family.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬಾಣಂತಿ (bāṇanti) — a new mother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to set up, then the Kannada for an invitation card.

You'll want to know: ಬಾಣಂತಿ.
ಬಾಣಂತಿ — bāṇanti — "a new mother".
ಬಾಣಂತಿ ಮೂರು ತಿಂಗಳು ಅಮ್ಮನ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದಳು. bāṇanti mūru tiṅgaḷu ammana maneyalli iddaḷu. The new mother stayed three months at her mother's house.
A woman in the weeks after giving birth, looked after with rest, warm food and the home-made ಔಷಧ, medicine, that the elders prepare.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಾಣಂತಿ (bāṇanti) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5347 lists Kannada bāṇanti, "a woman recently delivered", beside Malayalam vaṇṇa.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಜನನ (janana) — a birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an invitation card, then the Kannada for a new mother.

You'll want to know: ಜನನ.
ಜನನ — janana — "a birth".
ಮಗುವಿನ ಜನನ ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆಯಿತು. maguvina janana āspatreyalli āyitu. The baby was born in the hospital.
A formal word: ಜನನ (janana) ಪ್ರಮಾಣಪತ್ರ, a birth certificate. At home people say ಹುಟ್ಟು.

The word, taken apart.
ಜನನ (janana) is borrowed from Sanskrit janana, "birth" (CDIAL 5099), from the root jan, "to be born".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ನಾಮಕರಣ (nāmakaraṇa) — a naming ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a new mother, then the Kannada for a birth.

You'll want to know: ನಾಮಕರಣ.
ನಾಮಕರಣ — nāmakaraṇa — "a naming ceremony".
ಮಗುವಿನ ನಾಮಕರಣ ಹನ್ನೊಂದನೆಯ ದಿನ ನಡೆಯಿತು. maguvina nāmakaraṇa hannondaneya dina naḍeyitu. The baby's naming ceremony took place on the eleventh day.
The day a baby gets its name, often with the baby in a cradle.

The word, taken apart.
ನಾಮಕರಣ (nāmakaraṇa) is borrowed from Sanskrit nāmakaraṇa, "the ceremony of naming after birth" (CDIAL 7066), from nāman "a name" and karaṇa "making".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸೀಮಂತ (sīmanta) — a baby shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a birth, then the Kannada for a naming ceremony.

You'll want to know: ಸೀಮಂತ.
ಸೀಮಂತ — sīmanta — "a baby shower".
ಅಕ್ಕನ ಸೀಮಂತಕ್ಕೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಹೆಂಗಸರು ಬಂದರು. akkana sīmantakke tumbā heṅgasaru bandaru. Many women came to my elder sister's baby shower.
A ceremony for a woman expecting her first child, with bangles, flowers and sweets.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೀಮಂತ (sīmanta) is borrowed from Sanskrit sīmanta, "the parting of the hair" (CDIAL 13436): the old rite for a pregnant woman began with parting her hair.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ (uttarādhikāri) — a successor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a naming ceremony, then the Kannada for a baby shower.

You'll want to know: ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ.
ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ — uttarādhikāri — "a successor".
ಆ ಮಗು ರಾಜನ ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ ಆಯಿತು. ā magu rājana uttarādhikāri āyitu. That child became the king's successor.
Plural: ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳು, uttarādhikārigaḷu.
The one who takes over a throne, a post or property after someone.

The word, taken apart.
ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರಿ (uttarādhikāri) is borrowed from Sanskrit uttarādhikārin, "an heir or claimant after the death of the owner" (Monier-Williams).
