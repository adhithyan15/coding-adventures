## You'll want to know: वसाहत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C419-VASTI419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वसाहत** — *vasāhat* — "a housing colony".

> आमची वसाहत शहराच्या बाहेर आहे.
>
> *āmcī vasāhat śahrācyā bāher āhe.*
>
> Our housing colony is outside the city.

Feminine.

Also a colony in history: a land settled and ruled from far away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वसाहत** (*vasāhat*) is a Marathi word for a planned settlement; its origin is not settled.
