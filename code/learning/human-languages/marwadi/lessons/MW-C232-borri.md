## You'll want to know: बोरड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C232-EARTH232-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बोरड़ी** — *borṛī* — "a jujube bush".

> खेत कनै बोरड़ी है।
>
> *khet kanai borṛī hai.*
>
> There's a jujube bush near the field.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बोरड़ी** (*borṛī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *badara*, "the fruit of the jujube tree"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *borṛī*, the jujube tree. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 9125).
