## You'll want to know: تعليق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C355-AJIL355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تعليق** — *taʿlīq* — "a comment; a commentary".

> كتب القراء تعليقات كثيرة على المقال.
>
> *kataba al-qurrāʾ taʿlīqāt kathīra ʿalā al-maqāl.*
>
> Readers wrote a lot of comments on the article.

A sports commentator is معلق.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعليق** (*taʿlīq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-l-q* (Lane), "to hang", the root of the taught *ʿallaqa*. A comment hangs on what was said.
