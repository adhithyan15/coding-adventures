## You'll want to know: مزایا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C378-DASTMOZD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مزایا** (*mazâyâ*) — "benefits, perks".

> این شغل حقوق خوب و مزایای زیادی دارد.
>
> *in shoghl hoquq-e khub va mazâyâ-ye ziyâdi dârad.*
>
> This job has a good salary and a lot of benefits.

The Arabic broken plural of *maziyat* "advantage"; at work, insurance, bonuses and leave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مزایا** is usually traced to Arabic *mazāyā*, the broken plural of *maziyya* "merit, advantage", the word behind *maziyat*.
