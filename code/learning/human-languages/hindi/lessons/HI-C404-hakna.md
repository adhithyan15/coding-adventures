## You'll want to know: हाँकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C404-TRAIFIK404-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हाँकना** — *hā̃knā* — "to drive (animals); to drive (a cart)".

> एक किसान सड़क पर भैंसें हाँक रहा था।
>
> *ek kisān saṛak par bhaĩsẽ hā̃k rahā thā.*
>
> A farmer was driving buffaloes along the road.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने गायें हाँकीं, *usne gāyẽ hā̃kīṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हाँकना** (*hā̃knā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *hakkayati*, "calls to" (CDIAL 13939), which lists Hindi *hā̃knā*, "to cry out, drive off": cattle are driven by shouting at them.
