## You'll want to know: порезаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C302-ZADYKHATSYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**порезаться** (*porézatsya*) — "to cut oneself".

> Он порезался ножом.
>
> *On porézalsya nozhóm.*
>
> He cut himself with a knife.

Perfective. The tool is in the instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*порезаться* is built on *по-* and *резать* "to cut", inherited from Proto-Slavic *rězati* (EtymDB), with the reflexive *-ся*.
