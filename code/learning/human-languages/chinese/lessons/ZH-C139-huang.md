## You'll want to know: huáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C139-COLOUR139-02]; assesses=[] -->

*huáng* — "yellow".

> *zhège píngguǒ shì huáng de* — This apple is a yellow one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huáng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the colour of the centre'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
