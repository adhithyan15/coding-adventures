## You'll want to know: dānrénjiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C200-HOTEL200-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dānrénjiān* — "single room".

> *wǒ yào yí ge dānrénjiān* — I'd like a single room.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dānrénjiān* is built from *dān* 'single', 人 'person' and 间 'a room'. The first two are inherited from Middle Chinese, *tan* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
