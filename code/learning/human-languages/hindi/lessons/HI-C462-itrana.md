## You'll want to know: इतराना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C462-ABHIMAN462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इतराना** — *itrānā* — "to put on airs".

> नए कपड़े पहनकर मीरा की बहन इतरा रही थी।
>
> *nae kapṛe pahankar Mīrā kī bahan itrā rahī thī.*
>
> Wearing her new clothes, Mira's sister was putting on airs.

Past (she …): वह इतराई, *vah itrāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इतराना** (*itrānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *itrānā*, "to strut", under Sanskrit *itvará*, "going, walking" (CDIAL 1566).
