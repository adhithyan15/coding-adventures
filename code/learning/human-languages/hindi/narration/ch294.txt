Hindi, chapter 294: The Investigation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तफ़्तीश (taftīś) — an investigation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a crook, then the Hindi for fake.

You'll want to know: तफ़्तीश.
तफ़्तीश — taftīś — "an investigation".
अगर तफ़्तीश ठीक से हुई तो सच सामने आएगा। agar taftīś ṭhīk se huī to sac sāmne āegā. If the investigation is done properly, the truth will come out.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तफ़्तीश (taftīś) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taftīsh, "a searching, an inspection". It is the word of police reports: पुलिस तफ़्तीश कर रही है, the police are investigating.

Lesson 5 of 5.
खोजना (khojnā) — to search for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a detective, then the Hindi for evidence.

You'll want to know: खोजना.
खोजना — khojnā — "to search for".
पुलिस ने पूरे गाँव में चोर को खोजा। pulis ne pūre gā̃v meṁ cor ko khojā. The police searched the whole village for the thief.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चाबी खोजी, usne cābī khojī.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited from khōjja, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3929), with the sense of following a track. It is close to ढूँढना, to look for; खोजना (khojnā) is the slower, more thorough search.
