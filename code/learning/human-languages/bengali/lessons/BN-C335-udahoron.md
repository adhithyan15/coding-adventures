## You'll want to know: উদাহরণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C335-PROSONGO335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**উদাহরণ** — *udāhôrôn* — "an example".

> একটা উদাহরণ দাও।
>
> *ekṭā udāhôrôn dāo.*
>
> Give an example.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উদাহরণ** (*udāhôrôn*) is Sanskrit *udāharaṇa*, an example or illustration (Monier-Williams). You give one with দেওয়া: উদাহরণ দাও.
