## You'll want to know: घट्टः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C398-GHATTA398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घट्टः** — *ghaṭṭaḥ* — "a bathing ghat; river steps".

> प्रातः घट्टे यात्रिकाः स्नान्ति।
>
> *prātaḥ ghaṭṭe yātrikāḥ snānti.*
>
> In the morning pilgrims bathe at the bathing ghat.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घट्टः** (*ghaṭṭaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a landing-place, a bathing place or steps by a river, with no derivation; CDIAL notes that a Dravidian origin has been suggested (T. Burrow), so its origin is uncertain; it lives on as Hindi *ghāṭ*, "a bathing or landing place" (MW; CDIAL 4414).
