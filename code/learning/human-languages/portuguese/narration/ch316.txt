Portuguese, chapter 316: A herança.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
a herança — the inheritance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the cemetery, then the Portuguese for the tomb.

You'll want to know: a herança.
a herança — "the inheritance".
Com a herança, comprou uma casa. With the inheritance he bought a house.
Also what we receive from the past: a herança cultural.

The word, taken apart.
herança comes down from Old Portuguese erança (EtymDB), from Latin haerentia "things that cling", from haerēre "to cling".

What you've built.

o herdeiro — "the heir".
O filho mais velho é o único herdeiro. The eldest son is the only heir.

herdeiro comes down from Old Portuguese erdeiro (EtymDB) and goes back to Latin hērēditārius, built on hērēs "an heir" (Lewis & Short).

a pensão — "the pension; the guesthouse".
A minha avó vive da pensão. My grandmother lives on her pension.
Also a small, cheap guesthouse: ficámos numa pensão.

The word, taken apart.
pensão was borrowed from Latin pēnsiō "a payment, a rent" (EtymDB), from pendere "to weigh, to pay".
