# Hear *roṭī* — flatbread

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-PERFORMANCE-FOOD-THREE-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-LEX-HAATH-01, MW-LEX-DAAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Recall the three-word food payoff, then say hand and lentils.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-ROTI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *roṭī* — **flatbread**

The middle consonant is made with the tongue tip curled back, further back
than the *d* of *dāl*. Hear it twice and say it once. The spelling waits for
the next lesson, because one sign in it has not been taught yet.
