## You'll want to know: اختلاف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C530-MARZI530-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اختلاف** — *ikhtilāf* — "a disagreement; a difference of opinion".

> اگر کسی کو اختلاف ہے تو ابھی بتائیے۔
>
> *agar kisī ko ikhtilāf hai to abhī batāiye.*
>
> If anyone disagrees, say so now.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اختلاف** (*ikhtilāf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ikhtilāf*, "differing, disagreement".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
