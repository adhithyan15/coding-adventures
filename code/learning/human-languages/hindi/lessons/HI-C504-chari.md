## You'll want to know: छड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C504-BUDHAPA504-02]; assesses=[] -->

**छड़ी** — *chaṛī* — "a walking stick; a cane".

> दादा छड़ी के सहारे चलते हैं।
>
> *dādā chaṛī ke sahāre calte haiṁ.*
>
> Grandfather walks with the help of a stick.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छड़ी** (*chaṛī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *chaṛī*, "stick, rod, whip", under *\*chaṭa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4966).
