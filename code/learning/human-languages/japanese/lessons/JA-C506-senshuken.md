## You'll want to know: せんしゅけん

**せんしゅけん** — *senshuken* — "a championship".

> **あには すいえいの せんしゅけんで およぎます。** — *ani wa suiei no senshuken de oyogimasu* — "My elder brother swims in the swimming championship."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんしゅけん** (*senshuken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "elect" (Middle Chinese *sjwenX*), *shu*, "hand" (*syuwX*) and *ken*, "authority" (*gjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
