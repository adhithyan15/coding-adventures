# One heard greeting — write what you know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-01, HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-02] -->

Cover the answer lower on this page. You will hear one familiar greeting. No
new Hindi and no new Devanagari shape is hiding in this lesson.

## Writing — short dictation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-01, HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-02] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

[YOU HEAR: *namaste* — "a bow to you"]

Write the Devanagari word from sound alone. Do not use a visible model,
romanized answer, or tracing guide. Now uncover and compare: **नमस्ते**. Check
**न**, **म**, **स्**, and **ते** in that order. Repair one place if needed.
