# объявление (obyavléniye) — an announcement; a notice

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-OPOZDAL-NA-POEZD-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-PERFECTIVE-01, RU-LEX-TUDA-I-OBRATNO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BILET-DO-NA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I've missed the train". (*Ya opozdál na póyezd.*) Then
ask for a return ticket to Moscow. (*Bilét do Moskvý, tudá i obrátno.*)
At a station, a voice from above often tells you first that something has
gone wrong.

## You'll want to know: объявление
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-OBYAVLENIYE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-02, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-02, RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-02, RU-GRAMMAR-DO-GENITIVE] -->

**объявление** — *obyavléniye* — "an announcement". At the station it is
the voice over the platform:

> **На вокзале объявление: поезд до Москвы опаздывает.** — *na vokzále
> obyavléniye: póyezd do Moskvý opázdyvayet* — An announcement at the
> station: the train to Moscow is running late.

Say it in five beats, *ob-yav-lé-ni-ye*, with the weight on **-lé-**. The
*b* stops before the *y*: *ob-yav*, and not *o-byav*. Russian marks that
break with a letter of its own, and the next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C48-HOME1-05, RU-LEX-C138-FUT138-02, RU-LEX-OPOZDAL-NA-POEZD-01] -->

> **— Что в объявлении? — Поезд до Москвы опаздывает.**
> **— Ничего. Есть поезд через час.**

*Shto v obyavlénii?* — "What's in the announcement?" After **в**, the
word ends in **-нии**: *v obyavlénii*. The noun is neuter. A notice on a door is an **объявление** too: *na dverí
obyavléniye*, "there's a notice on the door".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-OBYAVLENIYE-01] -->

**объявление** is the noun of the verb *объявить*, "to announce", made
with the ending *-ение* that turns an action into a thing. *Объявить* is
*об-* with **явить**, "to show, to make appear", the verb behind *явный*,
"plain, obvious". EtymDB 2.1 does not list *объявление* or *объявить*,
but it takes *явить* through Old East Slavic *javiti* back to Proto-Slavic
*\*aviti*, often written *\*javiti*, "to make visible". EtymDB stops there,
and gives no older parent. An announcement shows everyone the same thing
at once.
