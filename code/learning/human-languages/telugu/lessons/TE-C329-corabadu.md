# చొరబడు (corabaḍu) — to break in; to intrude

## You'll want to know: చొరబడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C329-DONGATANAM329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**చొరబడు** — *corabaḍu* — "to break in; to intrude".

> దొంగలు కిటికీ నుంచి ఇంట్లోకి చొరబడ్డారు.
>
> *doṅgalu kiṭikī nuñci iṇṭlōki corabaḍḍāru.*
>
> The thieves broke into the house through the window.

Past (I …): చొరబడ్డాను, *corabaḍḍānu*.

The place you enter takes -లోకి, into: ఇంట్లోకి చొరబడు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చొరబడు** (*corabaḍu*) is a native compound: the stem *cor-* of *coccu*, "to enter, penetrate" (DEDR 2778), with *paḍu*, the helping verb of things that befall.
