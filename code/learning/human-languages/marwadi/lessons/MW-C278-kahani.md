## You'll want to know: कहाणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C278-KAHANI278-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कहाणी** — *kahāṇī* — "a story".

> दादी रोज कहाणी सुणावै है।
>
> *dādī roj kahāṇī suṇāvai hai.*
>
> Grandmother tells a story every day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कहाणी** (*kahāṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kathānaka*, "a little tale"; CDIAL lists Marwari *kā̃ṇi*, "tale, proverb", and Gujarati *kahāṇī* (CDIAL 2705).
