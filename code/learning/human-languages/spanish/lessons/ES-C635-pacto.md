## You'll want to know: el pacto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C635-PROMESA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el pacto** — "the pact, the deal".

> Hicimos un pacto: yo cocino y tú limpias.
>
> *We made a deal: I cook and you clean.*

*Pactar* — to agree a deal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pacto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pactum* 'an agreement' (EtymDB), from *pacisci* 'to make a bargain'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
