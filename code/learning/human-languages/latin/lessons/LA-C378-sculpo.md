## You'll want to know: sculpō, sculpere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C378-TEXO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sculpō, sculpere** — "to carve, to engrave".

> Statuārius nōmen in saxō sculpsit.
>
> *The sculptor carved the name in stone.*

*sculpō, sculpere, sculpsī, sculptum*. English *sculpture* and *sculptor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sculpō* is a form beside *scalpō* "to scratch, to scrape" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *sculpture* and *sculptor* come from it.
