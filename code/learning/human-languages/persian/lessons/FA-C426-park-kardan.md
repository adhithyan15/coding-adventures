## You'll want to know: پارک کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C426-RAHBANDAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پارک کردن** (*pârk kardan*) — "to park".

> جلوی بیمارستان نمیشود پارک کرد.
>
> *Jolow-ye bimârestân nemishavad pârk kard.*
>
> You cannot park in front of the hospital.

*pârk* + *kardan*. Present stem *kon-*: *pârk mikonam*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pârk kardan* is built from *pârk*, a loan usually traced to French *parc* 'park', and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
