## You'll want to know: yěxǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C256-THINK256-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yěxǔ* — "it may be that; perhaps".

> *yěxǔ tā wàng le* — Maybe he forgot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: yě is said with a rising tone before xǔ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yěxǔ* is built from 也 'also' and *xǔ* 'to allow', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yaeX* and *xjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a particle that closes a sentence and the second 'to permit'. How the pair came to mean perhaps is not settled.
