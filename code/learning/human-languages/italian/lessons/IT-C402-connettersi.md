## You'll want to know: connettersi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C402-RETE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**connettersi** — "to connect, to go online".

> Se ti connetti al wifi dell'albergo, non paghi.
>
> *If you connect to the hotel wifi, you do not pay.*

*Connettersi a* — to connect to. Past participle *connesso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*connettersi* comes from Latin *cōnectere* "to tie together" (Lewis & Short), built from *con-* "together" and *nectere* "to tie".
