5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தேர்வு (tērvu) — an exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a glove, then the Tamil for a class.
[pause 2 seconds]
Name a Tamil month, சித்திரை or தை, then give a தேதி, with the date ending -ām.

You'll want to know: தேர்வு.
தேர்வு — tērvu — "an exam".

The word, taken apart.
தேர்வு (tērvu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3419). It is the noun of tēr, "to examine, to inquire into", which DEDR files with teri, "to become clear".

புளி — puḷi — "tamarind".

The word, taken apart.
புளி (puḷi) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4322 lists Kannada puḷy, "sour" and Telugu puli, "sour". The tree is named for its sourness.
