Russian, chapter 319: Legends and heroes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
легенда (legénda) — a legend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a ball, then the Russian for festivities.

You'll want to know: легенда.
легенда (legénda) — "a legend".
Об этом озере есть красивая легенда. Ob étom ózere yest krasívaya legénda. There is a beautiful legend about this lake.
Feminine. По легенде — according to legend.

The word, taken apart.
легенда (legénda) goes back to Medieval Latin legenda "things to be read", from legere "to gather, to read" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent for the Russian word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
рассказчик (rasskázchik) — a storyteller, a narrator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a myth, then the Russian for a hero.

You'll want to know: рассказчик.
рассказчик (rasskázchik) — "a storyteller, a narrator".
Дедушка был отличным рассказчиком. Dédushka byl otlíchnym rasskázchikom. Grandpa was an excellent storyteller.
A woman is рассказчица.

The word, taken apart.
рассказчик (rasskázchik) is built on рассказать "to tell", from рас- and казать "to show, to tell", inherited from Proto-Slavic kazati (EtymDB), with the ending -чик for a person.

Lesson 5 of 5.
сочинять (sochinyát) — to make up, to compose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a hero, then the Russian for a storyteller.

You'll want to know: сочинять.
сочинять (sochinyát) — "to make up, to compose".
Мама сочиняла сказки для нас. Máma sochinyála skázki dlya nas. Mum made up fairy tales for us.
Imperfective; perfective сочинить (sochinít). Also of music or verse; in speech also "to tell tales": не сочиняй!

The word, taken apart.
сочинять (sochinyát) is built on со- "together" and чин "order, rank", inherited from Proto-Slavic činŭ (EtymDB): to put together in order.
