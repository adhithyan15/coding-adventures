## You'll want to know: aufziehen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C462-UNWETTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aufziehen** — "to come up, to gather (of a storm); to bring up (a child)".

> Am Abend zieht ein Gewitter auf.
>
> *In the evening a thunderstorm comes up.*

Separable; Perfekt with *sein* for weather. *Kinder aufziehen* — to bring up children (with *haben*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aufziehen* is built from *auf* 'up' and *ziehen* 'to pull, to move', inherited from Proto-Germanic *teuhaną* (EtymDB).
