## You'll want to know: zhǔnbèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C84-TOGETHER84-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔnbèi* — "to get ready; to prepare".

> *wǒ zhǔnbèi chànggē* — I'm getting ready to sing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔnbèi* is built from zhǔn 'level, a standard' and bèi 'to have ready', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsywinX* and *bijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

One more word for everyday answers.
