## You'll want to know: gānzào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C218-WIND218-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gānzào* — "dry air; dry (of weather or skin)".

> *dōngtiān zhèr hěn gānzào* — Winters here are very dry.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gānzào* is built from *gān* 'dry' and *zào* 'parched'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *kan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second, *sawX* there, with this very word, 'dry'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
