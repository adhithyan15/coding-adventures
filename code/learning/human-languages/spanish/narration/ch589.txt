Spanish, chapter 589: En realidad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
de hecho — in fact.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for above all, then the Spanish for in general.

You'll want to know: de hecho.
de hecho — "in fact".
De hecho, ya lo sabía. In fact, I already knew it.
It adds a stronger point or a correction.

The word, taken apart.
de hecho is built on hecho, inherited from Latin factus 'done, made', the participle of facere (EtymDB).

en absoluto — "not at all".
