Kannada, chapter 508: Inside the Body.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮೆದುಳು (meduḷu) — the brain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for hiccups, then the Kannada for asthma.

You'll want to know: ಮೆದುಳು.
ಮೆದುಳು — meduḷu — "the brain".
ಮೆದುಳು ದೇಹದ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಕೆಲಸಗಳನ್ನು ನಡೆಸುತ್ತದೆ. meduḷu dēhada ellā kelasagaḷannu naḍesuttade. The brain runs all the work of the body.
Also written ಮಿದುಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೆದುಳು (meduḷu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5062 lists Kannada miduḷ and meduḷ, "brain, marrow", beside Telugu medaḍu.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಶ್ವಾಸಕೋಶ (śvāsakōśa) — a lung.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for asthma, then the Kannada for the brain.

You'll want to know: ಶ್ವಾಸಕೋಶ.
ಶ್ವಾಸಕೋಶ — śvāsakōśa — "a lung".
ಹೊಗೆಯಿಂದ ಶ್ವಾಸಕೋಶಕ್ಕೆ ಹಾನಿ ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ. hogeyinda śvāsakōśakke hāni āguttade. Smoke harms the lungs.
Plural: ಶ್ವಾಸಕೋಶಗಳು, śvāsakōśagaḷu.
ಶ್ವಾಸ, breath + ಕೋಶ, a case: the case that holds the breath.

The word, taken apart.
ಶ್ವಾಸಕೋಶ (śvāsakōśa) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit śvāsa, "breath" (CDIAL 12769), and kośa, "a case, a sheath" (CDIAL 3539).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lung, then the Kannada for a kidney.

You'll want to know: ಕರುಳು.
ಕರುಳು — karuḷu — "the intestine; the gut".
ಹಸಿವಾದಾಗ ಕರುಳು ಸದ್ದು ಮಾಡುತ್ತದೆ. hasivādāga karuḷu saddu māḍuttade. When you are hungry, the gut rumbles.
Also the seat of tender feeling: ಕರುಳಿನ ಸಂಬಂಧ, a blood tie.

The word, taken apart.
ಕರುಳು (karuḷu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1274 lists Kannada karuḷ, "an entrail, the bowels; love", beside Malayalam karaḷ.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ನಾಯು (snāyu) is borrowed whole from Sanskrit snāyu, "tendon, muscle, sinew" (CDIAL 13793).
