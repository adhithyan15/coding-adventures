## You'll want to know: ಹವಳ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C481-KARAVALI481-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹವಳ** — *havaḷa* — "coral".

> ಸಮುದ್ರದ ತಳದಲ್ಲಿ ಬಣ್ಣಬಣ್ಣದ ಹವಳ ಇದೆ.
>
> *samudrada taḷadalli baṇṇabaṇṇada havaḷa ide.*
>
> There is colourful coral on the sea bed.

ಹವಳದ ಸರ, a coral necklace.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹವಳ** (*havaḷa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3998 lists Kannada *pavaḷa*, "coral", with the old *p-* that modern Kannada turns into *h-*. Sanskrit *pravāla*, "coral", was itself taken from Dravidian, as Turner notes (CDIAL 8794).
