## You'll want to know: にちようび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C164-WEEK164-05]; assesses=[] -->

**にちようび** — *nichiyōbi* — "Sunday".

> **らいしゅうの にちようびは うちで やすみます。** — *raishū no nichiyōbi wa uchi de yasumimasu* — "Next Sunday I will rest at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にちようび** (*nichiyōbi*) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese *nichi*, "sun, day" (Middle Chinese *nyit*), and *yō*, "planet, weekday" (*yewH*), borrowed from Chinese, with native *hi*, "day", voiced to *bi* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
