## You'll want to know: validar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C414-BILHETEIRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**validar** — "to validate (a ticket); to approve".

> Validamos o bilhete à entrada do metro.
>
> *We validate the ticket at the metro entrance.*

Machines at the gates *validam* the card.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*validar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *validāre* 'to make strong', from *validus* 'strong' (EtymDB), on *valēre* 'to be strong'.
