## You'll want to know: سخنرانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C402-TAHQIQ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سخنرانی** (*sokhanrâni*) — "a lecture, a talk".

> سخنرانی استاد یک ساعت طول کشید.
>
> *sokhanrâni-ye ostâd yek sâ'at tul keshid.*
>
> The professor's lecture lasted an hour.

*Sokhan* "speech" + *rân* "driving", from *rândan*, + *-i*. *Sokhanrâni kardan* — to give a talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سخنرانی** is native Persian, built from *sokhan* "speech, word" + *rân-*, the present stem of *rândan* "to drive", + *-i*. A lecturer drives the speech forward.
