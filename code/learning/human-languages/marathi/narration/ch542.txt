Marathi, chapter 542: Rules and Defiance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नियमावली (niyamāvalī) — a rulebook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for alert, then the Marathi for to issue a warning.

You'll want to know: नियमावली.
नियमावली — niyamāvalī — "a rulebook".
शाळेने सगळ्या पालकांना नियमावली दिली. śāḷene sagḷyā pālkānnā niyamāvalī dilī. The school gave all the parents a rulebook.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
नियमावली (niyamāvalī) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit niyama, "rule", and āvali, "a row" (Monier-Williams): a row of rules.

उशिरा आल्यामुळे व्यवस्थापकाने त्याला ताकीद दिली. uśirā ālyāmuḷe vyavasthāpakāne tyālā tākīd dilī. Because he came late, the manager gave him a stern warning.
Feminine.

Lesson 4 of 5.
जुमानणे (jumānṇe) — to heed; to care about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for control, then the Marathi for a stern warning.

You'll want to know: जुमानणे.
जुमानणे — jumānṇe — "to heed; to care about".
तो कोणाचाच सल्ला जुमानत नाही. to koṇācāc sallā jumānat nāhī. He heeds nobody's advice.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आदेश जुमानला, tyāne ādeś jumānlā.
Mostly with नाही: तो कोणालाच जुमानत नाही, he heeds no one.

The word, taken apart.
जुमानणे (jumānṇe) is a Marathi verb heard mostly with a negative; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a stern warning, then the Marathi for to heed.

You'll want to know: झुगारणे.
झुगारणे — jhugārṇe — "to defy; to throw off".
काही लोकांनी नियम झुगारले आणि दंड भरला. kāhī lokānnī niyam jhugārle āṇi daṇḍ bharlā. Some people defied the rules and paid a fine.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नियम झुगारले, tyāne niyam jhugārle.

The word, taken apart.
झुगारणे (jhugārṇe) is a Marathi verb for throwing off a rule or a burden; its origin is not settled.
