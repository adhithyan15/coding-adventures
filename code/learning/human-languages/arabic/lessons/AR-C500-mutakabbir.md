## You'll want to know: متكبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C500-GHURUR500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**متكبر** — *mutakabbir* — "arrogant".

> كان المدير متكبرا مع الموظفين.
>
> *kāna al-mudīr mutakabbiran maʿa al-muwaẓẓafīn.*
>
> The manager was arrogant with the staff.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متكبر** (*mutakabbir*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-b-r*, "to be big, great" (Lane). Kabīr, big, is from the same root; Form V mutakabbir is one who makes himself big.
