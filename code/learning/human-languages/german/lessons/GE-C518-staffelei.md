## You'll want to know: die Staffelei
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C518-MALEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Staffelei** — "easel".

> Die Staffelei steht am Fenster, wo das Licht gut ist.
>
> *The easel stands by the window, where the light is good.*

Plural: *die Staffeleien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Staffelei* is built on *Staffel* "step, rung", which EtymDB traces to Proto-Germanic *stapulaz* "post"; the ending *-ei* makes a noun.
