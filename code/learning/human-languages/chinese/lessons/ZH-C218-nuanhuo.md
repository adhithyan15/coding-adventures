## You'll want to know: nuǎnhuo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C218-WIND218-02]; assesses=[] -->

*nuǎnhuo* — "pleasantly warm; mild".

> *jīntiān hěn nuǎnhuo* — It's lovely and warm today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nuǎnhuo* is built from *nuǎn* 'warm' and *huo* 'mild', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nwanX* and *hwa* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first beside another character glossed 'warm', and glosses the second 'in harmony' and 'neither hard nor soft'; in the word the second loses its tone and its vowel changes.
