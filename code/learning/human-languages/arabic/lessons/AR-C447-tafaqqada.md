## You'll want to know: تفقد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C447-DUSH447-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تفقد** — *tafaqqada* — "to check on; to inspect".

> تفقد العامل الحمام بعد العطل.
>
> *tafaqqada al-ʿāmil al-ḥammām baʿda al-ʿuṭl.*
>
> The worker checked on the bathroom after the breakdown.

Present: يتفقد, *yatafaqqad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفقد** (*tafaqqada*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-q-d*, "to lose, to miss" (Lane). It is Form V, "to look for what is missing", and so to check on.
