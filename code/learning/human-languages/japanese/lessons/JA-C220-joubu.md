## You'll want to know: じょうぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C220-WEIGHT220-02]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうぶ** — *jōbu* — "robust; sturdy".

> **ちちは からだが じょうぶです。** — *chichi wa karada ga jōbu desu* — "My father has a strong body."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうぶ** (*jōbu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jō*, "ten feet, a full height" (Middle Chinese *drjangX*) and *bu*, "man" (*pju*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
