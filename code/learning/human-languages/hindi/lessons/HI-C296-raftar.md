## You'll want to know: रफ़्तार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C296-TAKKAR296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रफ़्तार** — *raftār* — "speed".

> ट्रक की रफ़्तार बस से ज़्यादा थी।
>
> *ṭrak kī raftār bas se zyādā thī.*
>
> The lorry was going faster than the bus.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रफ़्तार** (*raftār*) is usually traced to Persian *raftār*, "a going, a gait", from the Persian verb to go. It is feminine: **तेज़ रफ़्तार**, high speed.
