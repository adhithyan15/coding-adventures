## You'll want to know: yuányīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C145-LATE145-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yuányīn* — "reason; cause".

> *wǒ bù zhīdào shì shénme yuányīn* — I don't know what the reason is.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuányīn* is built from *yuán* and 因 'because', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjwon* and *'jin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'a broad plain' and the second 'to follow from'; how the first came to mean origin is not settled.
