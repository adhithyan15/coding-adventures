## You'll want to know: かようび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C164-WEEK164-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かようび** — *kayōbi* — "Tuesday".

> **らいしゅうの かようびに ともだちに あいます。** — *raishū no kayōbi ni tomodachi ni aimasu* — "I will meet a friend next Tuesday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かようび** (*kayōbi*) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese *ka*, "fire" (Middle Chinese *xwaX*), and *yō*, "planet, weekday" (*yewH*), borrowed from Chinese, with native *hi*, "day", voiced to *bi* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
