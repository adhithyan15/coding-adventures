## You'll want to know: patricius, patricia, patricium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C382-SENATOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**patricius, patricia, patricium** — "patrician, of the old noble families".

> Familia eius patricia et antīqua est.
>
> *His family is patrician and ancient.*

*patricius, patricia, patricium*, built on *patrēs*, the fathers, an old name of the senators. English *patrician*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patricius* is built on *patrēs* "the fathers" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), the plural of *pater* used of the first senators. English *patrician* comes from it.
