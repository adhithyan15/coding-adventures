## You'll want to know: a norma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C381-NORMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a norma** — "the rule, the standard, the norm".

> A escola tem normas muito claras.
>
> *The school has very clear rules.*

*Por norma* — as a rule, usually.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*norma* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nōrma* "a carpenter's square, a rule, a pattern" (Lewis & Short).
