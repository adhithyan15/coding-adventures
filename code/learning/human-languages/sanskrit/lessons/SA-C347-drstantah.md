## You'll want to know: दृष्टान्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C347-UDAHARANA347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दृष्टान्तः** — *dṛṣṭāntaḥ* — "an illustration (in an argument)".

> गुरुः दृष्टान्तेन विचारं स्पष्टं करोति।
>
> *guruḥ dṛṣṭāntena vicāraṃ spaṣṭaṃ karoti.*
>
> The teacher makes the idea clear with an illustration.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दृष्टान्तः** (*dṛṣṭāntaḥ*) is built from *dṛṣṭa*, "seen", and *anta*, "an end", which MW explains as the end or aim of what is seen; MW gives the senses "example, standard" and "exemplification" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
