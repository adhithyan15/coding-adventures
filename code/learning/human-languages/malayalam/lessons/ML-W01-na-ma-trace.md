# ന · മ — two sounds you already heard

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-NAMASKARAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *na-ma* — the first two beats of *namaskāram*. Your hand meets
only those two familiar sounds today.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-NA-01, ML-SCRIPT-MA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Malayalam is an abugida: a bare consonant already carries a short *a*.

- **ന** is *na*.
- **മ** is *ma*.

Put them together: **നമ**, *na-ma*. Keep both models visible.

## Writing — observe and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-NA-01, ML-SCRIPT-MA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ന** once, then **മ** once. Trace **നമ** once from left to right.
Say *na-ma* after your finger stops. This is observation with a visible model,
not a memory test.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-NAMASKARAM-01, ML-SCRIPT-NA-01, ML-SCRIPT-MA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point to *na*. (**ന**.) Point to *ma*. (**മ**.) What vowel did you
write after either consonant? (**None** — the short *a* is inherent.)
