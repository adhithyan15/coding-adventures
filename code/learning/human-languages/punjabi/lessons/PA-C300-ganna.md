## You'll want to know: ਗੰਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C300-CROP300-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੰਨਾ** — *gannā* — "sugar cane".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਗੰਨੇ ਤੋਂ ਗੁੜ ਬਣਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *asīṁ ganne ton guṛ baṇāunde hāṁ.*
>
> We make jaggery from sugar cane.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੰਨਾ** (*gannā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gaṇḍa*, "joint of plant"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gannā*, "sugarcane", under it (CDIAL 3998).
