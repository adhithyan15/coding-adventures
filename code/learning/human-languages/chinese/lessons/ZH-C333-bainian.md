## You'll want to know: bàinián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C333-EVE333-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bàinián* — "to pay a New Year call".

> *dì'èr tiān wǒmen qù yéye jiā bàinián* — The next day we went to my grandpa's to wish him a happy new year.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàinián* is built from *bài* 'to bow' and 年 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *peajH* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists the first among the forms of a bow.
