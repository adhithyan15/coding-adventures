# … উচিত / -তে হবে (uchit / -te hôbe) — ought to, have to

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C269-UCHIT-01, BN-SCRIPT-C269-U-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "You ought to go to the doctor". (*tomār ḍāktārer kāchhe jāoyā uchit*.)

## You'll want to know: ought to, ought not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C269-UCHIT-SHOULD-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C269-UCHIT-01, BN-LEX-ANCHOR-EKA, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-01] -->

**উচিত** gives advice. **না** after it gives a warning:

> **রাতে তোমার একা যাওয়া উচিত না।**
>
> *rāte tomār ekā jāoyā uchit nā.* — "You shouldn't go alone at night."

For yourself, **আমার**: **আমার বিশ্রাম নেওয়া উচিত** (*āmār bishrām neoyā uchit*), "I ought to rest".

## You'll want to know: have to
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C82-PLACE82-05, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-LEX-C48-FAMILY48-05] -->

What you must do, not only ought to, is **-তে হবে**: the one who must in the **-কে** form, the verb in its **-তে** form, then **হবে**.

> **কাল আমাকে স্টেশনে যেতে হবে।**
>
> *kāl āmāke sṭeshône jete hôbe.* — "Tomorrow I have to go to the station."

The **-তে** form: **যাওয়া** → **যেতে** (*jete*), **খাওয়া** → **খেতে** (*khete*), **করা** → **করতে** (*kôrte*), **আসা** → **আসতে** (*āste*). In the past, **হবে** becomes **হল**: **আমাকে হেঁটে যেতে হল** (*āmāke hẽṭe jete hôlô*), "I had to walk".
