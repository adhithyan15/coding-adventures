Bengali, chapter 67: Sand, Moon, Sea, Fire, Ice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বালি (bāli) — sand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pond, then the Bengali for a stone.

You'll want to know: বালি.
বালি — bāli — "sand".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 3 seconds]
