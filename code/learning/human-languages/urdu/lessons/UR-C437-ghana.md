## You'll want to know: گھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C437-JHARI437-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گھنا** — *ghanā* — "dense; thick".

> یہ جنگل گھنا ہے۔
>
> *yih jangal ghanā hai.*
>
> This forest is dense.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گھنا** (*ghanā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ghanā*, "dense", under Sanskrit *ghaná*, "compact, dense" (CDIAL 4424).
