## You'll want to know: партия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C366-VYBORY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**партия** (*pártiya*) — "a party (in politics); a game".

> Он член этой партии.
>
> *On chlen étoy pártii.*
>
> He is a member of this party.

Feminine. Also one game of chess: *партия в шахматы*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**партия** was borrowed from Polish *partia*, from French *partie* "a part", from *partir* "to divide", from Latin *partīre*, built on *pars* "a part, a piece" (EtymDB).
