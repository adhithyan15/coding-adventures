## You'll want to know: अभिनय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C529-NATAK529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनय** — *abhinay* — "acting; a performance".

> लोगों को मीरा का अभिनय बहुत अच्छा लगा।
>
> *Logoṁ ko Mīrā kā abhinay bahut acchā lagā.*
>
> People liked Mira's acting very much.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनय** (*abhinay*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *abhinaya*, "acting, dramatic action" (Monier-Williams).
