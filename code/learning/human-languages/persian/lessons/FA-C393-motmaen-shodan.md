# مطمئن شدن (motma'en shodan) — to make sure, to become sure

## You'll want to know: مطمئن شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C393-ETMINAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مطمئن شدن** (*motma'en shodan*) — "to make sure, to become sure".

> قبل از رفتن مطمئن شدم که در قفل است.
>
> *qabl az raftan motma'en shodam ke dar qofl ast.*
>
> Before leaving I made sure that the door was locked.

*Motma'en* "sure" + *shodan*. *Motma'en budan* — to be sure. It is spelled with ئ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مطمئن** comes from Arabic *muṭma'inn* "calm, sure" (EtymDB); with the inherited Persian verb *shodan* "to become", it means to become sure.
