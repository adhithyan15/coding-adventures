## You'll want to know: तुटवडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C435-TANCHAI435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तुटवडा** — *tuṭvaḍā* — "a scarcity".

> बाजारात कांद्याचा तुटवडा आहे.
>
> *bājārāt kāndyācā tuṭvaḍā āhe.*
>
> There is a scarcity of onions in the market.

Masculine.

Built on तुट-, the stem of तुटणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तुटवडा** (*tuṭvaḍā*) is built on the stem of **तुटणे**, "to break, to fall short" (CDIAL 6065), with a noun ending.
