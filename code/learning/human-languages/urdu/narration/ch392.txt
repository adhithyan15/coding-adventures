Urdu, chapter 392: Stands and Stops.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اسٹینڈ (isṭainḍ) — a stand (for taxis or rickshaws).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a rush (of people), then the Urdu for to push in.

You'll want to know: اسٹینڈ.
اسٹینڈ — isṭainḍ — "a stand (for taxis or rickshaws)".
رکشہ اسٹینڈ بازار کے سامنے ہے۔ rikshā isṭainḍ bāzār ke sāmne hai. The rickshaw stand is opposite the market.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اسٹینڈ (isṭainḍ) is borrowed from English stand.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 5.
راہ (rāh) — a path; a way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a stand (for taxis or rickshaws), then the Urdu for a bus stop.

You'll want to know: راہ.
راہ — rāh — "a path; a way".
یہ راہ سیدھی دریا تک جاتی ہے۔ yih rāh sīdhī daryā tak jātī hai. This path runs straight to the river.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
راہ (rāh) is usually traced to Persian rāh, "a road, a way"; it is the first half of راہگیر, "a passer-by".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ٹہلنا (ṭahalnā) — to stroll; to walk up and down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a bus stop, then the Urdu for a path.

You'll want to know: ٹہلنا.
ٹہلنا — ṭahalnā — "to stroll; to walk up and down".
شام کو ہم دریا کے کنارے ٹہلتے ہیں۔ shām ko ham daryā ke kināre ṭahalte haiṅ. In the evening we stroll along the riverbank.
Past (he …): وہ پارک میں ٹہلا, voh pārk meṅ ṭahlā.

The word, taken apart.
ٹہلنا (ṭahalnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭahalnā, "to take a walk", under ṭahall, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5453).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ہانپنا (hāṅpnā) — to pant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a path, then the Urdu for to stroll.

You'll want to know: ہانپنا.
ہانپنا — hāṅpnā — "to pant".
سارا دوڑ کر اسٹاپ پر پہنچی اور ہانپ رہی تھی۔ sārā dauṛ kar isṭāp par pahuṅchī aur hāṅp rahī thī. Sara ran to the stop and was out of breath.
Past (he …): وہ سیڑھی پر ہانپا, voh sīṛhī par hāṅpā.

The word, taken apart.
ہانپنا (hāṅpnā) is inherited from hamph, an imitative form CDIAL reconstructs for panting (CDIAL 13973).
