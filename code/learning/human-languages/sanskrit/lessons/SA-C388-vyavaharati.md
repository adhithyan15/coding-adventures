## You'll want to know: व्यवहरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C388-VYAVAHARA388-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवहरति** — *vyavaharati* — "deals with people; behaves".

> यदि सः विनयेन व्यवहरति, तर्हि जनाः तं सम्मानयन्ति।
>
> *yadi saḥ vinayena vyavaharati, tarhi janāḥ taṃ sammānayanti.*
>
> If he deals with people politely, they honour him.

Past (he …): सः विनयेन व्यवहृतवान्, *saḥ vinayena vyavahṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवहरति** (*vyavaharati*) is built on *vi*, *ava* and the root √hṛ, "to take"; MW gives *vy-ava-√hṛ* "to act, to behave towards or deal with" (MW).
