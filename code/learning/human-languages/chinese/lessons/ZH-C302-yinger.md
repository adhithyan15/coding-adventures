## You'll want to know: yīng'ér
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C302-BIRTH302-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yīng'ér* — "newborn; infant".

> *zhège yīng'ér hěn kě'ài* — This baby is very sweet.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yīng'ér* is built from *yīng* and 儿 'a child', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jieng* and *nye* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes an older word list for the pair: a baby girl is *'jieng*, a baby boy *nye*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
