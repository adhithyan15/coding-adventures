Hindi, chapter 411: Weights and Delays.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वज़न (vazan) — weight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for customs, then the Hindi for a fee.

You'll want to know: वज़न.
वज़न — vazan — "weight".
मेरे सामान का वज़न बीस किलो है। mere sāmān kā vazan bīs kilo hai. My luggage weighs twenty kilos.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
वज़न (vazan) is usually traced to Arabic wazn, "weight, weighing", through Persian; Hindi breaks up the final zn with a vowel.

Lesson 2 of 5.
विलंब (vilamb) — a delay (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a fee, then the Hindi for weight.

You'll want to know: विलंब.
विलंब — vilamb — "a delay (formal)".
ट्रेन दो घंटे के विलंब से आई। ṭren do ghaṇṭe ke vilamb se āī. The train came two hours late.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विलंब (vilamb) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vilamba, "hanging down" and so "slowness, delay" (Monier-Williams); announcements use it where talk says देर.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पेटी (peṭī) — a belt (a seat belt).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for weight, then the Hindi for a delay (formal).

You'll want to know: पेटी.
पेटी — peṭī — "a belt (a seat belt)".
विमान उतरने तक अपनी पेटी मत खोलिए। vimān utarne tak apnī peṭī mat kholiye. Do not undo your seat belt until the plane has landed.
Feminine.
पेटी (peṭī) is also a small box or chest.

The word, taken apart.
पेटी (peṭī) goes back to pēṭṭa, "belly", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 8376), which lists Hindi peṭ, the belly, and peṭī, "a girth", the band round a horse's belly; the seat belt is the newest band of all. The box called पेटी may be a different word: CDIAL notes look-alike words for a basket.

Lesson 4 of 5.
रद्द (radd) — cancelled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a delay (formal), then the Hindi for a belt.

You'll want to know: रद्द.
रद्द — radd — "cancelled".
आँधी की वजह से उड़ान रद्द हो गई। ā̃dhī kī vajah se uṛān radd ho gaī. The flight was cancelled because of the storm.
रद्द (radd) होना, to be cancelled; रद्द (radd) करना, to cancel.

The word, taken apart.
रद्द (radd) is usually traced to Arabic radd, "a turning back, a rejection", through Persian. It does not change: उड़ान रद्द हुई, ट्रेन रद्द हुई.
