## You'll want to know: ambulātiō, ambulātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C542-STRI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ambulātiō, ambulātiōnis** — "a walk, stroll".

> Post cēnam ambulātiōnem in hortō facimus.
>
> *After dinner we take a walk in the garden.*

Feminine, third declension, from *ambulō*. Also a covered walk in a villa.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ambulātiō** is the action noun of *ambulō* "to walk" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *ambulance* and *perambulate* come from the same verb.
