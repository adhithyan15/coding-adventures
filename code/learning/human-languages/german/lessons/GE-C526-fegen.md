## You'll want to know: fegen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C526-PUTZEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**fegen** — "to sweep".

> Mein Vater fegt jeden Morgen die Terrasse.
>
> *My father sweeps the terrace every morning.*

In southern Germany and Austria also *kehren*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fegen* goes back to Middle High German *vegen* "to clean, to polish"; EtymDB lists the word, glossed "to cleanse, to sweep", without a source.
