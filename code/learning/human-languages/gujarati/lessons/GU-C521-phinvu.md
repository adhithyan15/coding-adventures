# ફીણવું (phīṇvũ) — to whisk (to a froth)

## You'll want to know: ફીણવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C521-VALONU521-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફીણવું** — *phīṇvũ* — "to whisk (to a froth)".

> મીરાએ ચમચીથી છાશ ફીણી.
>
> *Mīrāe camcīthī chāś phīṇī.*
>
> Mira whisked the buttermilk with a spoon.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે છાશ ફીણી, *teṇe chāś phīṇī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફીણવું** (*phīṇvũ*) is built on *phīṇ*, "foam", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *phḗna*, "foam" (CDIAL 9108): to beat a liquid until it froths.
