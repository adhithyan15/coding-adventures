Bengali, chapter 481: Mother and Elder Siblings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মা (mā) — a mother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for smoking, then the Bengali for to shed.

You'll want to know: মা.
মা — mā — "a mother".
আমার মা রোজ সকালে চা বানান। āmār mā roj sôkāle chā bānān. My mother makes tea every morning.

The word, taken apart.
মা (mā) comes from Sanskrit mātṛ, "mother" (CDIAL 10016), which CDIAL lists in this Bengali form; মেয়ে (taught), a girl, is filed under the same entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
দাদা (dādā) — an elder brother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to shed, then the Bengali for a mother.

You'll want to know: দাদা.
দাদা — dādā — "an elder brother".
আমার দাদা শহরে চাকরি করেন। āmār dādā shôhôre chākri kôren. My elder brother has a job in the city.
ভাই (taught) is a brother in general, often the younger; দাদা (dādā) is the elder, and the word you call him by.

The word, taken apart.
দাদা (dādā) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as both a grandfather and an elder brother, under a reconstructed nursery word (CDIAL 6261).

Lesson 4 of 5.
বউদি (boudi) — an elder brother's wife; a sister-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an elder brother, then the Bengali for an elder sister.

You'll want to know: বউদি.
বউদি — boudi — "an elder brother's wife; a sister-in-law".
বউদি আমাদের জন্য পায়েস রান্না করলেন। boudi āmāder jônyô pāyes rānnā kôrlen. My sister-in-law made rice pudding for us.

The word, taken apart.
বউদি (boudi) puts bou, "wife, bride", from Sanskrit vadhū (CDIAL 11250), with দিদি (didi), elder sister: the elder brother's wife, called by a sister's name.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ঠাকুমা (ṭhākumā) — a paternal grandmother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an elder sister, then the Bengali for an elder brother's wife.

You'll want to know: ঠাকুমা.
ঠাকুমা — ṭhākumā — "a paternal grandmother".
ঠাকুমা আমাদের রাজার গল্প বলতেন। ṭhākumā āmāder rājār gôlpo bôlten. Grandmother used to tell us stories about kings.
দিদিমা (taught) is the mother's mother; ঠাকুমা (ṭhākumā) is the father's.

The word, taken apart.
ঠাকুমা (ṭhākumā) puts ṭhākur, a title of respect, from Sanskrit ṭhakkura, "a deity; a title" (CDIAL 5488), with মা (mā), mother.
