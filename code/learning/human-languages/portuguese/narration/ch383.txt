Portuguese, chapter 383: Fora das regras.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a exceção — the exception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to break, then the Portuguese for the violation.

You'll want to know: a exceção.
a exceção — "the exception".
Todos pagam, sem exceção. Everyone pays, without exception.
AO90 exceção; older excepção. Abrir uma exceção — to make an exception.

The word, taken apart.
exceção comes from Latin exceptiō "an exception, a restriction" (EtymDB), on excipere "to take out", built on capere "to take" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: excluir.
excluir — "to exclude, to leave out; to rule out".
Não excluo a hipótese de mudar de casa. I do not rule out the possibility of moving house.
Conjugated like concluir: excluo, excluis, exclui.

The word, taken apart.
excluir is a learned borrowing from Latin exclūdere "to shut out, to exclude", built from ex and claudere "to shut" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
a infração — the offence, the infringement (of a rule).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the requirement, then the Portuguese for to exclude.

You'll want to know: a infração.
a infração — "the offence, the infringement (of a rule)".
Estacionar ali é uma infração. Parking there is an offence.
AO90 infração; older infracção. Infração de trânsito — traffic offence.

The word, taken apart.
infração is a learned borrowing from Latin īnfrāctiō "a breaking" (Lewis & Short), from īnfringere "to break, to infringe", built on frangere "to break".

transgredir is a learned borrowing from Latin trānsgredī "to step across, to pass over", built from trāns "across" and gradī "to step" (Lewis & Short).
