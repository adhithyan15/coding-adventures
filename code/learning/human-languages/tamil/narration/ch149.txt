Tamil, chapter 149: Famous, Dangerous, Safe, True, False.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பிரபலமான (pirapalamāṉa) — famous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for dry, then the Tamil for wet.

You'll want to know: பிரபலமான.
பிரபலமான — pirapalamāṉa — "famous".

The word, taken apart.
பிரபலமான (pirapalamāṉa) is a loan from Sanskrit prabala, "strong" (CDIAL 8696). Tamil shifted it from "strong" to "widely known", and -āṉa makes it a describing word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
பாதுகாப்பான (pātukāppāṉa) — safe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for famous, then the Tamil for dangerous.

You'll want to know: பாதுகாப்பான.
பாதுகாப்பான — pātukāppāṉa — "safe".

The word, taken apart.
பாதுகாப்பான (pātukāppāṉa) ends in native kāppu, "protection", from kā, "to guard" (DEDR 1416). The first part, pātu, is not settled, so the origin of the whole word is uncertain.
