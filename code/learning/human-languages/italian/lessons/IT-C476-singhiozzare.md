## You'll want to know: singhiozzare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C476-DELUSIONE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**singhiozzare** — "to sob; to have hiccups".

> Il bambino singhiozzava e non riusciva a parlare.
>
> *The child was sobbing and could not speak.*

*Il singhiozzo* is a sob, and also the hiccups: *ho il singhiozzo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*singhiozzare* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *singlutiāre* (EtymDB), from Latin *singultus* "a sob, a hiccup" (Lewis & Short).
