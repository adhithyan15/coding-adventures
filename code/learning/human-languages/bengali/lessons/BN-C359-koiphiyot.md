# কৈফিয়ত (koiphiyôt) — a demanded explanation; an account of oneself

## You'll want to know: কৈফিয়ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C359-OJUHAT359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**কৈফিয়ত** — *koiphiyôt* — "a demanded explanation; an account of oneself".

> ম্যানেজার দেরির জন্য কৈফিয়ত চাইলেন।
>
> *mænejār derir jônyô koiphiyôt chāilen.*
>
> The manager asked for an explanation for the delay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কৈফিয়ত** (*koiphiyôt*) is Persian *kaifiyat*, how things stand, an account, usually traced to Arabic *kayfiyya*. In Bengali it is the account you are asked to give of yourself.
