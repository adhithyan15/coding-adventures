## You'll want to know: ほこら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C303-RYUU303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ほこら** — *hokora* — "a small wayside shrine".

> **ぬまの ちかくに ちいさい ほこらが あります。** — *numa no chikaku ni chiisai hokora ga arimasu* — "There is a little shrine near the marsh."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほこら** (*hokora*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
