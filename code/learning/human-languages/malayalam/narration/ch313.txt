എക്കിൾ (ekkiḷ) — a hiccup.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an injection, then the Malayalam for an ambulance.

You'll want to know: എക്കിൾ.
എക്കിൾ — ekkiḷ — "a hiccup".

The word, taken apart.
എക്കിൾ (ekkiḷ) is native Dravidian, one word with Kannada ekku and Telugu ekkili (DEDR 772). The word imitates the sound.

You'll want to know: കോട്ടുവാ.
കോട്ടുവാ — kōṭṭuvā — "a yawn".

The word, taken apart.
കോട്ടുവാ (kōṭṭuvā) is a Dravidian word. DEDR 2172 lists it as kōṭṭāvi, "yawn, gaping", with Tamil koṭṭāvi, "yawn", and compares them, with a query, to Kui and Kuwi words for drowsiness and dozing.

ലേപനം (lēpanaṁ) from Sanskrit lēpana, "the act of smearing", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 11111).
