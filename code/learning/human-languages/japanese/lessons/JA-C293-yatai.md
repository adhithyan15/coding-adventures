## You'll want to know: やたい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C293-HANABI293-03]; assesses=[] -->

**やたい** — *yatai* — "a food stall; a street stall".

> **えきの まえに やたいが ならんでいます。** — *eki no mae ni yatai ga narande imasu* — "Food stalls stand in a row in front of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やたい** (*yatai*) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: *ya*, "house", is native Japanese, and *tai*, "a stand", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *doj*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
