## You'll want to know: краснеть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C470-SHAME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**краснеть** (*krasnét*) — "to blush".

> Она краснеет, когда говорит с ним.
>
> *Oná krasnéyet, kogdá govorít s nim.*
>
> She blushes when she talks to him.

Imperfective; present краснею, краснеешь. Perfective покраснеть. Also to turn red.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*краснеть* is built on *красный* 'red', whose older sense 'beautiful' lives on in *красивый*; EtymDB lists the verb without a source, and the root is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *krasa* 'beauty'.
