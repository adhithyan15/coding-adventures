# (reading) — six words off a signboard

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-GHAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have written every one of these. Not one is new.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that nobody is going to say them first.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SKILL-READ-WORDS]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BAJAR-01, GU-SCRIPT-SHAALAA-01] -->

> ઘર
> બજાર
> શાળા
> દુકાન
> પાણી
> પુસ્તક

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once. Do not build each one sign by sign — look
at the whole word and let it arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Again, and notice which came at once and which you had to assemble.
Both are fine. The difference is what practice removes.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DUKAAN-01, GU-LEX-PAANI, GU-LEX-PUSTAK-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Writing gave you the signs in order and all the time you wanted. A
signboard gives you one look.

[PAUSE 2s] These six are the ones you would actually meet painted on something
— over a shop, on a school gate, beside a tap.

[PAUSE 2s] That is why six is enough for one sitting.
