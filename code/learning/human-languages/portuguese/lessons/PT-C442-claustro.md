## You'll want to know: o claustro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C442-HISTORICO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o claustro** — "the cloister".

> O claustro do mosteiro é muito silencioso.
>
> *The monastery cloister is very quiet.*

A square garden with covered walks round it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*claustro* is a learned borrowing from Latin *claustrum* 'bar, closed place' (EtymDB), from *claudere* 'to close'.
