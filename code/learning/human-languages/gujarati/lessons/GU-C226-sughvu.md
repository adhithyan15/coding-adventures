# સૂંઘવું (sū̃ghvũ) — to sniff, to smell

## You'll want to know: સૂંઘવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C226-BREATH226-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂંઘવું** — *sū̃ghvũ* — "to sniff, to smell".

> **શું કૂતરો ફૂલ સૂંઘે છે?** — *śũ kūtro phūl sū̃ghe che?* — "Is the dog sniffing the flower?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂંઘવું** (*sū̃ghvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "smells", under Sanskrit *śiṅghati*, "smells", and its variant *\*śṛṅkhati* (CDIAL 12579).
