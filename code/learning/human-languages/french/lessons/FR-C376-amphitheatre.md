## You'll want to know: l'amphithéâtre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C376-FAC-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'amphithéâtre** — "the lecture hall".

> Le cours a lieu dans le grand amphithéâtre.
>
> *The lecture takes place in the big lecture hall.*

Shortened to *l'amphi*. Also an ancient arena. Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amphithéâtre* comes from Latin *amphitheātrum* (EtymDB), from Greek *amphithéatron*, built from *amphí* "around" and *théatron* "a place for seeing".
