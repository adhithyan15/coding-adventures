German, chapter 291: Der Notruf.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Notruf — the emergency call; the emergency number.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to choke, then the German for to drown.

You'll want to know: der Notruf.
der Notruf — "the emergency call; the emergency number".
Sie wählte sofort den Notruf. She dialled the emergency number at once.
Plural: die Notrufe. The number 112 reaches the fire brigade and the ambulance.

The word, taken apart.
Notruf joins Not "need", a cousin of English need, and Ruf "call", from rufen, Proto-Germanic hrōpaną (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: der Hubschrauber.
der Hubschrauber — "the helicopter".
Ein Hubschrauber brachte die Frau ins Krankenhaus. A helicopter took the woman to hospital.
Plural: die Hubschrauber. Built from Hub 'lift' and Schraube 'screw'.

The word, taken apart.
Hubschrauber is a modern compound of Hub "a lift", from heben "to lift", and Schrauber, from Schraube "screw"; EtymDB lists no parent: a screw that lifts.

Plural: die Notaufnahmen. Die Aufnahme — admission.

The word, taken apart.
Notaufnahme joins Not "need" and Aufnahme "admission", from aufnehmen "to take in", built on nehmen, Proto-Germanic nemaną (EtymDB).
