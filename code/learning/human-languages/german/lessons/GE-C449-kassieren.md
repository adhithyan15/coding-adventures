## You'll want to know: kassieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C449-KASSE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**kassieren** — "to take the money, to collect (payment)".

> Im Restaurant kassiert der Kellner an jedem Tisch.
>
> *In the restaurant the waiter takes the money at each table.*

Past participle without *ge-*: *er hat kassiert*. Colloquial: *eine Strafe kassieren* — to get a penalty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kassieren* is built on *Kasse* 'till, cash box' (EtymDB lists the verb), which came from Italian *cassa*, from Latin *capsa* 'box' (Lewis & Short).
