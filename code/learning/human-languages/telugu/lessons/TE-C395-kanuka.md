# కనుక (kanuka) — so; hence

## You'll want to know: కనుక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C395-KANUKA395-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కనుక** — *kanuka* — "so; hence".

> వర్షం పడింది, కనుక మేము బయటకు వెళ్ళలేదు.
>
> *varṣaṁ paḍindi, kanuka mēmu bayaṭaku veḷḷalēdu.*
>
> It rained, so we did not go out.

It opens the result, after the reason, as అందుకే does.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కనుక** (*kanuka*) is a Telugu connective of reason whose origin is uncertain; DEDR gives it no article of its own.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
