## You'll want to know: अफ़सर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C507-DAFTAR507-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अफ़सर** — *afsar* — "an officer; an official".

> अफ़सर ने हर मज़दूर से एक सवाल पूछा।
>
> *Afsar ne har mazdūr se ek savāl pūchā.*
>
> The officer asked every labourer a question.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अफ़सर** (*afsar*) is usually traced to English *officer*, reshaped to fit Hindi sounds.
