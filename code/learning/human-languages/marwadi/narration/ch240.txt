Lesson 1 of 7.
पाकणो (pākṇo) — to ripen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to grow, then the Marwadi for a new shoot.

You'll want to know: पाकणो.
पाकणो — pākṇo — "to ripen".
काती मांय बाजरो पाकै है। kātī mā̃y bājro pākai hai. The millet ripens in autumn.
Past (it …): वो बाजरो पाक्यो, vo bājro pākyo.

The word, taken apart.
पाकणो (pākṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit pakva, "cooked, ripe"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb pākaï, "becomes ripe" (CDIAL 7621). पक्को, settled, comes from the same word.

You'll want to know: लूणणो.
लूणणो — lūṇṇo — "to reap".
हूं काल बाजरो लूणसूं। hū̃ kāl bājro lūṇsū̃. Tomorrow I'll reap the millet.
Past (I …): म्हैं बाजरो लूण्यो, mhaĩ bājro lūṇyo.

The word, taken apart.
लूणणो (lūṇṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit lunāti, "cuts, reaps"; CDIAL lists Hindi lunnā, "to reap". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11082).

Lesson 4 of 7.
बाली (bālī) — an ear of grain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to reap, then the Marwadi for a threshing floor.

You'll want to know: बाली.
बाली — bālī — "an ear of grain".
बाली पीळी है। bālī pīḷī hai. The ear of grain is yellow.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बाली (bālī) is inherited from Sanskrit valla, "a kind of wheat"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bālī, "the standing crop" (CDIAL 11425). In Marwadi it names the ear at the top of the stalk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

ढेर — ḍher — "a heap; a pile".
खेत मांय मोटो ढेर है। khet mā̃y moṭo ḍher hai. There's a big heap in the field.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ढेर (ḍher) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍhēra, "a heap"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati ḍher. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5599).
