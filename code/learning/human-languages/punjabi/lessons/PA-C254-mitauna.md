# ਮਿਟਾਉਣਾ (miṭāuṇā) — to rub out; to erase

## You'll want to know: ਮਿਟਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C254-FORM254-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਿਟਾਉਣਾ** — *miṭāuṇā* — "to rub out; to erase".

> ਮੈਂ ਗ਼ਲਤ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਮਿਟਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ ghalat shabad miṭāundā hāṁ.*
>
> I rub out the wrong word.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਗ਼ਲਤ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਮਿਟਾਇਆ, *maiṁ ghalat shabad miṭāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਿਟਾਉਣਾ** (*miṭāuṇā*) is the causative partner of *miṭnā*, "is wiped out", which goes back to Sanskrit *mṛṣṭa*, "rubbed, washed"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *miṭnā* under it (CDIAL 10299).
