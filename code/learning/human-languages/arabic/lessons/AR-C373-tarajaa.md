## You'll want to know: تراجع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C373-MAWQIF373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تراجع** — *tarājaʿa* — "to back down".

> تراجع أبي عن قراره بعد نقاش.
>
> *tarājaʿa abī ʿan qarārihi baʿda niqāsh.*
>
> My father backed down from his decision after a discussion.

Present: يتراجع, *yatarājaʿ*.

It takes عن: تراجع عن ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تراجع** (*tarājaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-j-ʿ*, "to return, to go back" (Lane). It is Form VI, to go back step by step: to back down, to decline.
