## You'll want to know: धजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C546-NISHCHAY546-05]; assesses=[] -->

**धजणे** — *dhajṇe* — "to dare".

> अंधारात एकट्याने जायला तो धजला नाही.
>
> *andhārāt ekṭyāne jāylā to dhajlā nāhī.*
>
> He did not dare to go alone in the dark.

Past (he …): तो धजला, *to dhajlā*.

It takes the -ायला form: बोलायला धजणे, to dare to speak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धजणे** (*dhajṇe*) is a Marathi verb heard mostly with a negative; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
