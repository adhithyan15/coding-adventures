## You'll want to know: yǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C229-PET229-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎng* — "to keep an animal; to raise".

> *wǒ xiǎng yǎng yì zhī māo* — I'd like to have a cat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *yangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to rear' and 'to delight'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what I do.
