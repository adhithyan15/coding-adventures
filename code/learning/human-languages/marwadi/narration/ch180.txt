Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 180: Putting Things Away.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धरणो (dharṇo) — to set down; to place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to peel, then the Marwadi for to winnow.

You'll want to know: धरणो.
धरणो — dharṇo — "to set down; to place".
हूं घड़ो भींत कनै धरूं हूं। hū̃ ghaṛo bhī̃t kanai dharū̃ hū̃. I set the water pot down by the wall.
Past (I …): म्हैं घड़ो भींत कनै धर्यो, mhaĩ ghaṛo bhī̃t kanai dharyo.

The word, taken apart.
धरणो (dharṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit dharati, "holds, keeps"; CDIAL lists Marwari dharṇo, "to keep, place" (CDIAL 6747).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: घालणो.
घालणो — ghālṇo — "to put in".
मां दाल मांय लूण घालै है। mā̃ dāl mā̃y lūṇ ghālai hai. Mother puts salt in the dal.
Past (I …): म्हैं दाल मांय लूण घाल्यो, mhaĩ dāl mā̃y lūṇ ghālyo.

The word, taken apart.
घालणो (ghālṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ghalyati, "pours"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ghālaï, "puts, places, gives" (CDIAL 4457).

Lesson 3 of 5.
नाखणो (nākhṇo) — to throw (away).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to set down, then the Marwadi for to put in.

You'll want to know: नाखणो.
नाखणो — nākhṇo — "to throw (away)".
हूं रोज कचरो नाखूं हूं। hū̃ roj kachro nākhū̃ hū̃. I throw out the rubbish every day.
Past (I …): म्हैं कचरो नाख्यो, mhaĩ kachro nākhyo.

The word, taken apart.
नाखणो (nākhṇo) is inherited, by CDIAL's account, from Sanskrit naṅkṣyati, "will perish", a sense far from today's; CDIAL lists Old Marwari nākhaï, "puts, drops, strews" (CDIAL 6930).

Lesson 4 of 5.
छींको (chhī̃ko) — a hanging net for pots.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to put in, then the Marwadi for to throw.

You'll want to know: छींको.
छींको — chhī̃ko — "a hanging net for pots".
छींको नुवो है। chhī̃ko nuvo hai. The hanging net is new.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
छींको (chhī̃ko) is inherited from Sanskrit śikya, "a rope sling for carrying things"; CDIAL lists Hindi chī̃kā and Sindhi chiko, a net or shelf that hangs (CDIAL 12427). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

छाबड़ी मांय रोटी है। chhābṛī mā̃y roṭī hai. There are rotis in the flat basket.

The word, taken apart.
छाबड़ी (chhābṛī) is inherited, by CDIAL's account, from Sanskrit chadman, "a roof"; CDIAL lists Hindi chabṛī, "a strainer, a bamboo basket" (CDIAL 4981). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
