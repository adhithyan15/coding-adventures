Lesson 1 of 5.
l'employé — an employee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a concert, then the French for a show.

You'll want to know: l'employé.
l'employé — "an employee".

The word, taken apart.
employé is the past participle of the verb for "to employ", from Latin implicare, "to involve" (Lewis & Short), used as a noun: one employed.
