## You'll want to know: попутчик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C411-POPUTCHIK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**попутчик** (*popútchik*) — "a fellow traveller".

> В поезде у меня был весёлый попутчик.
>
> *V póyezde u menyá byl vesyólyy popútchik.*
>
> I had a cheerful fellow traveller on the train.

Feminine *попутчица*. Built on *по пути* — on the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*попутчик* is built on *попутный* 'going the same way', from *по пути* 'on the way'. *Путь* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *pǫtĭ* 'a path, a road' (EtymDB).
