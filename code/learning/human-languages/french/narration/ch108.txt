Lesson 1 of 5.
le bureau — an office; a desk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a swimming pool, then the French for a grocer's shop.

You'll want to know: le bureau.
le bureau — "an office; a desk".

The word, taken apart.
bureau from Old French burel, "coarse woollen cloth", from Old French bure, from Latin burra, "shaggy cloth" (EtymDB 2.1): the cloth on a desk, then the desk, then the room.

Lesson 5 of 5.
le feu rouge — a red light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a pavement, then the French for a crossroads.

You'll want to know: le feu rouge.
le feu rouge — "a red light". le feu is a fire; at a crossing it is the traffic light.

The phrase, taken apart.
feu rouge — "red fire": feu, "fire", from Latin focus, and rouge, "red", from Latin rubeus. (EtymDB 2.1).
