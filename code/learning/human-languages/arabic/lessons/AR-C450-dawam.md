## You'll want to know: دوام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C450-BALADIYYA450-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دوام** — *dawām* — "working hours; office hours".

> ينتهي الدوام بعد الظهر.
>
> *yantahī ad-dawām baʿda aẓ-ẓuhr.*
>
> Office hours end in the afternoon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دوام** (*dawām*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-w-m*, "to last, to continue" (Lane). Working hours are the time one keeps at a job.
