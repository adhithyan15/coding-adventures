## You'll want to know: ઉત્તમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C387-BAHETAR387-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્તમ** — *uttam* — "excellent".

> જો તમે તાજું શાક વાપરશો તો દાળ ઉત્તમ બનશે.
>
> *jo tame tājũ śāk vāparśo to dāḷ uttam banśe.*
>
> If you use fresh vegetables, the dal will turn out excellent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્તમ** (*uttam*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *uttama*, "uppermost, highest, best" (Monier-Williams).
