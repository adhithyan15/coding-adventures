# دور شدن (dur shodan) — to move away, to get further away

## You'll want to know: دور شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C446-JAHAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دور شدن** (*dur shodan*) — "to move away, to get further away".

> قایق کم کم از ساحل دور شد.
>
> *Qâyeq kam-kam az sâhel dur shod.*
>
> The boat slowly moved away from the shore.

*dur* 'far' + *shodan*. The opposite is *nazdik shodan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dur shodan* is built from native *dur* 'far', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Iranian *duHráh* 'far' and PIE *duh₂-ró-s* 'long', and the native verb *shodan* 'to become'.
