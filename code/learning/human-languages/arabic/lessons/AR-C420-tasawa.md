## You'll want to know: تساوى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C420-ISTIJABA420-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تساوى** — *tasāwā* — "to be equal".

> تساوى الفريقان في النقاط.
>
> *tasāwā al-farīqān fī an-niqāṭ.*
>
> The two teams were level on points.

Present: يتساوى, *yatasāwā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تساوى** (*tasāwā*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-w-y*, "to be equal, level" (Lane). It is Form VI, to be level with one another: to be equal.
