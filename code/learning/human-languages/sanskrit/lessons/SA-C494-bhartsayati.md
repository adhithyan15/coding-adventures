## You'll want to know: भर्त्सयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C494-LALANA494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भर्त्सयति** — *bhartsayati* — "scolds".

> पिता अलसं पुत्रं भर्त्सयति।
>
> *pitā alasaṃ putraṃ bhartsayati.*
>
> Father scolds his lazy son.

Past (he …): सः पुत्रं भर्त्सितवान्, *saḥ putraṃ bhartsitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भर्त्सयति** (*bhartsayati*) comes from the root √bharts, "to menace, to threaten, to abuse"; MW's ppp is *bhartsita* (MW).
