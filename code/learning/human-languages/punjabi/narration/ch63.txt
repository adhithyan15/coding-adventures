Punjabi, chapter 63: To find, To throw, Meat, Fish, Rice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਲੱਭਣਾ (labbhṇā) — to find, to look for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to leave, then the Punjabi for to wait for.

You'll want to know: ਲੱਭਣਾ.
ਲੱਭਣਾ — labbhṇā — "to find, to look for".

The word, taken apart.
ਲੱਭਣਾ (labbhṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from labhyate, "is taken" (CDIAL 10950).

The word, taken apart.
Where ਸੁੱਟਣਾ (suṭṭṇā) comes from is not settled. CDIAL does not list it, though it gives the close Punjabi forms siṭṭṇā and saṭṭṇā, "to throw", under Sanskrit sṛṣṭa, "released, thrown" (CDIAL 13578).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਚੌਲ (chaul) — rice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for meat, then the Punjabi for a fish.

You'll want to know: ਚੌਲ.
ਚੌਲ — chaul — "rice".

The word, taken apart.
ਚੌਲ (chaul) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cāvala, "husked rice", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4749). CDIAL thinks it possibly shares a non-Aryan origin with Sanskrit taṇḍula, "rice".
