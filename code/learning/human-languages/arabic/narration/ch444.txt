Arabic, chapter 444: Power and light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
كهرباء (kahrabāʾ) — electricity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to overlook, then the Arabic for a strongbox.

You'll want to know: كهرباء.
كهرباء — kahrabāʾ — "electricity".
تعمل الثلاجة بالكهرباء. taʿmalu ath-thallāja bi-l-kahrabāʾ. The fridge runs on electricity.

The word, taken apart.
كهرباء (kahrabāʾ) goes back to Persian kahrubā, "amber" (EtymDB), a name usually read as "straw-robber", since rubbed amber draws straw to it; the old name passed to electricity.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سخن (sakhkhana) — to heat up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a strongbox, then the Arabic for electricity.

You'll want to know: سخن.
سخن — sakhkhana — "to heat up".
سخنت أمي الحساء. sakhkhanat ummī al-ḥasāʾ. My mother heated up the soup.
Present: يسخن, yusakhkhin.

The word, taken apart.
سخن (sakhkhana) is built on the native Arabic root s-kh-n, "to be hot" (Lane). It is Form II, "to make hot", beside sākhin, "hot".
