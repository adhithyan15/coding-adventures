# તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો (tamne maḷīne ānand thayo) — "pleased to meet you"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-MAARUNNAAMCHHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The warm close of an introduction — a word that means bliss itself.

## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-ANAND-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-MAARUNNAAMCHHE-01] -->

The key word is **આનંદ** (*ānand*): **આ** + **ન** + **ં** (the nasal) + **દ**
(*da*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-ANAND-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-MAARUNNAAMCHHE-01] -->

**તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો** = **તમને** (*tamne*, "to you") + **મળીને** (*maḷīne*,
"having met") + **આનંદ થયો** (*ānand thayo*, "joy happened") — literally "**having
met you, joy happened**." The key word **આનંદ** (*ānand*, "joy, bliss") is
**Sanskrit** — *ā-* ("fully") + *nand* ("to rejoice") — the *ānanda* of
*sat-cit-ānanda* ("being-consciousness-bliss"), and a common Gujarati name. So a
simple greeting reaches, as Sanskrit did, for the word for the deepest happiness.
