Gujarati, chapter 58: In the Pantry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચોખા (cokhā) — rice (uncooked).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for rupees, then the Gujarati for a coin.

You'll want to know: ચોખા.
ચોખા (cokhā) — "rice (uncooked)". Cooked, it is ભાત.

What you've built.
One more word for in the pantry.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: cokhā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cokhā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say cokhā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for rupees, then the Gujarati for a coin, then say cokhā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ચોખા (cokhā) mean? ("Rice (uncooked)".) Say it once more.
