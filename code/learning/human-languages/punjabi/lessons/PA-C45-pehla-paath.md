# (reading) — the same six facts, as sentences

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-READ-FORM] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Six labels, then six lines of a form. Now the same person, in
sentences.

[PAUSE 1s] Forty-three words, and not one of them is new.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-SOCHDA-KI-01, PA-LEX-CHANGA] -->

> ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਂ ਅਮਨ ਹੈ।
> ਮੇਰਾ ਕੰਮ ਨੌਕਰੀ ਹੈ।
> ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
> ਮੈਂ ਸੋਚਦਾ ਹਾਂ ਕਿ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਚੰਗਾ ਹੈ।
> ਮਨਨ ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਹੈ।
> ਮਨਨ ਦਾ ਕੰਮ ਖੇਤੀ ਹੈ।
> ਮਨਨ ਠੀਕ-ਠਾਕ ਹੈ।
> ਪਰ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਹੀਂ ਪਤਾ ਕਿ ਉਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ।
> ਅਸੀਂ ਜਲਦੀ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ।
> ਧੰਨਵਾਦ।

[PAUSE 3s] Read it once without stopping. Do not translate as you go — let the
sentences arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Now read it again, and notice how little work it took.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-PAR-01, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-Q-KITTHE-01, PA-LEX-THIK-THAK] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The form said the same things in six lines with no verb anywhere.
Here **ਹੈ** has to be said out loud, and it comes last every time.

[PAUSE 2s] **ਕਿ** hangs a whole sentence off another one. **ਪਰ** turns the
paragraph. And after **ਮਨਨ** is named twice, he becomes **ਉਹ** — you followed
that without being asked to.

[PAUSE 2s] This is the first whole paragraph the book has asked you to hold at
once, and it works because every piece was paid for.
