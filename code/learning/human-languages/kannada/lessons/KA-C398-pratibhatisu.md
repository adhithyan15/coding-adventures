# ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸು (pratibhaṭisu) — to protest

## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C398-PRATIBHATISU398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸು** — *pratibhaṭisu* — "to protest".

> ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳು ಶುಲ್ಕ ಏರಿಕೆಯನ್ನು ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *vidyārthigaḷu śulka ērikeyannu pratibhaṭisidaru.*
>
> The students protested the rise in fees.

Past (I …): ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸಿದೆ, *pratibhaṭiside*.

ಪ್ರತಿಭಟನೆ, a protest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸು** (*pratibhaṭisu*) is built on Sanskrit *pratibhaṭa*, "a match for, rivalling" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*: to stand up against.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
