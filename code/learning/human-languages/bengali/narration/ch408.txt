Bengali, chapter 408: Rented Rooms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বাসা (bāsā) — a rented home; lodgings; a nest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a tip (for service), then the Bengali for a laundry.

You'll want to know: বাসা.
বাসা — bāsā — "a rented home; lodgings; a nest".
শহরে মীরা একটা ছোট বাসা ভাড়া নিল। shôhôre mīrā ekṭā chhoṭo bāsā bhāṛā nilô. In the city Mira rented a small flat.
Also a bird's nest: পাখির বাসা (bāsā). বাড়ি is the family home; বাসা (bāsā) is where you live now.

The word, taken apart.
বাসা (bāsā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali bāsā, "a lodging-house, a nest, a lair", under Sanskrit vāsá, "a dwelling" (CDIAL 11591).

You'll want to know: ভাড়াটে.
ভাড়াটে — bhāṛāṭe — "a tenant".
আমাদের বাড়িতে নতুন ভাড়াটে এল। āmāder bāṛite nôtun bhāṛāṭe elô. A new tenant moved into our house.

The word, taken apart.
ভাড়াটে (bhāṛāṭe) is built on ভাড়া, "rent", which CDIAL lists as Bengali bhāṛā, "hire, rent", under the reconstructed bhārta (CDIAL 9468), with the describing ending -টে: of rent, hired; as a noun it settled on the one who pays the rent.

বাসার জন্য দুই মাসের ভাড়া অগ্রিম দিতে হবে। bāsār jônyô dui māser bhāṛā ôgrim dite hôbe. For the flat you have to pay two months' rent in advance.

You'll want to know: জামানত.
জামানত — jāmānôt — "a security deposit".
ঘর ছাড়ার সময় মালিক জামানতের টাকা ফিরিয়ে দিলেন। ghôr chhāṛār sômôy mālik jāmānôter ṭākā phiriye dilen. When we left the room, the owner gave back the deposit.

The word, taken apart.
জামানত (jāmānôt) is usually traced to Persian zamānat, "surety, a guarantee", from Arabic ḍamāna.

Lesson 5 of 5.
বসবাস করা (bôsbās kôrā) — to live (in a place); to reside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an advance (payment), then the Bengali for a security deposit.

You'll want to know: বসবাস করা.
বসবাস করা — bôsbās kôrā — "to live (in a place); to reside".
এই পাড়ায় অনেক পরিবার বসবাস করে। ei pāṛāy ônek pôribār bôsbās kôre. Many families live in this neighbourhood.
Past (he/she …): তিনি গ্রামে বসবাস করতেন, tini grāme bôsbās kôrten.
A careful word for settled living; থাকা is the everyday one.

The word, taken apart.
বসবাস করা (bôsbās kôrā) joins বস-, the stem of বসা, to sit, and বাস, "a dwelling", Sanskrit vāsa (Monier-Williams), to করা, to do: to sit down and dwell.
