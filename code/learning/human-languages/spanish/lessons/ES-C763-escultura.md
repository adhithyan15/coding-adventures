## You'll want to know: la escultura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C763-ARTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la escultura** — "the sculpture".

> En el museo hay una escultura enorme.
>
> *There is a huge sculpture in the museum.*

*El escultor* — the sculptor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escultura* comes from Latin *sculptūra* "sculpture" (EtymDB), from *sculpere* "to carve in stone".
