## You'll want to know: die Ebbe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C457-KUESTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ebbe** — "the low tide, the ebb".

> Bei Ebbe kann man weit ins Watt laufen.
>
> *At low tide you can walk a long way out onto the mudflats.*

Plural: *die Ebben*, rare. Figurative: *In meinem Portemonnaie ist Ebbe* — my purse is empty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ebbe* came into the standard language from Middle Low German *ebbe* (EtymDB), a cousin of English *ebb*.
