## You'll want to know: बेल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C447-JHADI447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बेल** — *bel* — "a creeper, a vine".

> दीवार पर हरी बेल चढ़ रही है।
>
> *dīvār par harī bel caṛh rahī hai.*
>
> A green creeper is climbing the wall.

Feminine.

A different बेल, masculine, is the wood-apple tree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेल** (*bel*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *velli*, "a creeping plant" (CDIAL 12123), which lists Hindi *bel*, "creeper".
