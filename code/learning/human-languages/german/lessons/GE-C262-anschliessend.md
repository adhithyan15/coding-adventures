## You'll want to know: anschließend
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C262-ZUNAECHST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**anschließend** — "afterwards, subsequently".

> Wir waren im Kino, und anschließend haben wir gegessen.
>
> *We were at the cinema, and afterwards we ate.*

At the start of a clause the verb comes second: *Anschließend gingen wir nach Hause*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anschließend* is the present participle of *anschließen* "to join on", built on *schließen* "to close", which is inherited from Old High German *sliozan* "to close, to lock" (EtymDB): what is joined on next.
