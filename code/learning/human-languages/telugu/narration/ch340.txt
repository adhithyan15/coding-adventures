Telugu, chapter 340: Out of the Blue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తక్షణం (takṣaṇaṁ) — instantly; at once.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a march, then the Telugu for to march against.

You'll want to know: తక్షణం.
తక్షణం — takṣaṇaṁ — "instantly; at once".
సాక్షి తక్షణం పోలీసులకు ఫోను చేశాడు. sākṣi takṣaṇaṁ pōlīsulaku phōnu cēśāḍu. The witness phoned the police at once.
Literally that very moment: తత్ and క్షణం. తక్షణమే is stronger still. Where ఈలోగా, meanwhile, leaves a little time, తక్షణం (takṣaṇaṁ) leaves none.

The word, taken apart.
తక్షణం (takṣaṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit tatkṣaṇam, "at that very moment", from tad, "that", and kṣaṇa, "a moment" (CDIAL 5640).

Lesson 2 of 5.
అనుకోకుండా (anukōkuṇḍā) — unexpectedly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to march against, then the Telugu for instantly.

You'll want to know: అనుకోకుండా.
అనుకోకుండా — anukōkuṇḍā — "unexpectedly".
బజారులో అనుకోకుండా మా మాస్టారు కనిపించారు. bajārulō anukōkuṇḍā mā māsṭāru kanipiñcāru. I unexpectedly ran into our teacher in the market.
The negative of అనుకో, to think: without thinking of it.

The word, taken apart.
అనుకోకుండా (anukōkuṇḍā) is native Telugu: anukonu, "to think" (DEDR 868), with -kuṇḍā, "without -ing": without having thought of it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఉన్నట్టుండి (unnaṭṭuṇḍi) — out of the blue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for instantly, then the Telugu for unexpectedly.

You'll want to know: ఉన్నట్టుండి.
ఉన్నట్టుండి — unnaṭṭuṇḍi — "out of the blue".
ఉన్నట్టుండి వర్షం మొదలైంది. unnaṭṭuṇḍi varṣaṁ modalaindi. Out of the blue it started to rain.
Literally while being as it was: all was calm, and then it was not.

The word, taken apart.
ఉన్నట్టుండి (unnaṭṭuṇḍi) is native Telugu, built twice on uṇḍu, "to be, exist" (DEDR 697): while things were as they were.

Lesson 4 of 5.
తీరా (tīrā) — in the end; when it came to it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for unexpectedly, then the Telugu for out of the blue.

You'll want to know: తీరా.
తీరా — tīrā — "in the end; when it came to it".
చాలా రోజులు వెతికాము, తీరా అది ఇంట్లోనే ఉంది. cālā rōjulu vetikāmu, tīrā adi iṇṭlōnē undi. We searched for days, and in the end it was right at home.
A spoken word that brings a surprise ending, often an ironic one.

The word, taken apart.
తీరా (tīrā) is native Dravidian, built on tīru, "to be finished, concluded" (DEDR 3278): when everything was done.

Lesson 5 of 5.
మేల్కొను (mēlkonu) — to wake up; to awaken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for out of the blue, then the Telugu for in the end.

You'll want to know: మేల్కొను.
మేల్కొను — mēlkonu — "to wake up; to awaken".
నేను పొద్దున్నే ఆరు గంటలకు మేల్కొన్నాను. nēnu poddunnē āru gaṇṭalaku mēlkonnānu. I woke up at six in the morning.
Past (I …): మేల్కొన్నాను, mēlkonnānu.
A slightly bookish partner of నిద్ర లేచు.

The word, taken apart.
మేల్కొను (mēlkonu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5086 lists Telugu mēlu-konu, "to wake up, get up", under the word for "above, high".
