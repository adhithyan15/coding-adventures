Urdu, chapter 129: Back, Forehead, Cheek, Chin, Wrist.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پیٹھ (pīṭh) — the back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a bear, then the Urdu for the waist.

You'll want to know: پیٹھ.
پیٹھ — pīṭh — "the back".

The word, taken apart.
پیٹھ (pīṭh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pṛṣṭi, "vertebrae; back" (CDIAL 8370).
