## You'll want to know: ಒಂಬತ್ತನೆಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-04]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C07-OMBATTU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C74-ORDINAL-02] -->

**ಒಂಬತ್ತನೆಯ** (*ombattaneya*) — "**ninth**".

**ಒಂಬತ್ತು** is the longest cardinal in the set: an anusvara, a doubled **ತ್ತ**,
and four syllables. None of that matters to the ending, which does what it did to
the two-syllable **ಆರು** — takes the final **-ು** off and goes on after
everything else.

**ಒಂಬತ್ತನೆಯ ದಿನ** (*ombattaneya dina*), the ninth day.
