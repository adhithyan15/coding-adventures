# پر منحصر ہے (par munhasir hai) — it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C275-MUNHASIR-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C274-REAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "it depends on the weather". (*yih mausam par munhasir hai*.) Then "if it rains". (*agar bārish huī*.) A question often gets this answer.

## You'll want to know: it depends on …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C62-SCHOOL62-02, UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

> **یہ منحصر ہے۔**
>
> *yih munhasir hai.* — "It depends."

> **کیا آپ کل آئیں گے؟**
>
> *kyā āp kal āeṅ ge?* — "Will you come tomorrow?"

> **یہ کام پر منحصر ہے۔**
>
> *yih kām par munhasir hai.* — "It depends on work."

**یہ** (*yih*), "this", is the plan in question; what decides it takes **پر**:

> **یہ پیسوں پر منحصر ہے۔**
>
> *yih paisoṅ par munhasir hai.* — "It depends on the money."

## You'll want to know: it depends on whether …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-02]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-KI-COMPLEMENTISER, UR-LEX-YA, UR-LEX-C93-TIME93-05, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-02] -->

When a whole question decides it, **اس پر** (*is par*) stands for it, and **کہ** (*ki*) brings the question:

> **یہ اس پر منحصر ہے،**
>
> *yih is par munhasir hai,*
>
> **کہ بارش ہو گی یا نہیں۔**
>
> *ki bārish ho gī yā nahīṅ.* — "It depends on whether it will rain or not."

> **یہ اس پر منحصر ہے،**
>
> *yih is par munhasir hai,*
>
> **کہ گاڑی کب جائے گی۔**
>
> *ki gāṛī kab jāe gī.* — "It depends on when the train leaves."
