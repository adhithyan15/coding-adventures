## You'll want to know: శ్వాస
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C515-SARIRAM515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**శ్వాస** — *śvāsa* — "breath; breathing".

> లోతుగా శ్వాస తీసుకుంటే మనసు ప్రశాంతంగా ఉంటుంది.
>
> *lōtugā śvāsa tīsukuṇṭē manasu praśāntaṅgā uṇṭundi.*
>
> If you breathe deeply, the mind is calm.

శ్వాస తీసుకో, to take a breath.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శ్వాస** (*śvāsa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śvāsa*, "breath, breathing" (CDIAL 12769).
