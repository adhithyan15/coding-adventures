## You'll want to know: भनक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C547-CHAUKANNA547-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भनक** — *bhanak* — "an inkling; a whisper (of news)".

> मुझे उसकी योजना की भनक लग गई।
>
> *Mujhe uskī yojnā kī bhanak lag gaī.*
>
> I got wind of his plan.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भनक** (*bhanak*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*bhan*, "hum, buzz", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9382). CDIAL gives the Hindi word only as "hum, buzz"; the sense "an inkling", news that reaches you as a faint buzz, is a later Hindi use CDIAL does not give.
