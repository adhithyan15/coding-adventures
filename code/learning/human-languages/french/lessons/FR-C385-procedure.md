## You'll want to know: la procédure
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C385-DEMARCHE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la procédure** — "the procedure".

> La procédure est longue, mais simple.
>
> *The procedure is long, but simple.*

In law, a court case: *une procédure judiciaire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*procédure* is built on *procéder* "to proceed", from Latin *prōcēdere* "to go forward" (Lewis & Short), built on *cēdere* "to go".
