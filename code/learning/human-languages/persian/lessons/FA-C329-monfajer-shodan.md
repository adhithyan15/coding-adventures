## You'll want to know: منفجر شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C329-ENFEJAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منفجر شدن** (*monfajer shodan*) — "to explode, to blow up".

> کپسول گاز در آشپزخانه منفجر شد.
>
> *Kapsul-e gâz dar âshpazkhâne monfajer shod.*
>
> The gas cylinder exploded in the kitchen.

Present *monfajer mishavad*; *monfajer kardan* is "to blow something up".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*monfajer* is usually traced to Arabic *munfajir*, "bursting", of the same root as *enfejâr*; EtymDB lists no entry. *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian.
