## You'll want to know: شعله
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C328-ATASHSUZI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شعله** (*sho'le*) — "a flame".

> شعله ها تا سقف رسید.
>
> *Sho'le-hâ tâ saqf resid.*
>
> The flames reached the ceiling.

Plural *sho'le-hâ*. On a cooker, *sho'le-ye gâz* is the burner flame.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sho'le* is borrowed from Arabic *shu'la*, "flame" (EtymDB).
