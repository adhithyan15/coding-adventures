## You'll want to know: খাঁড়ি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C431-UPOSAGOR431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**খাঁড়ি** — *khā̃ṛi* — "a creek; an inlet".

> জোয়ারের সময় খাঁড়িতে জল ঢোকে।
>
> *joyārer sômôy khā̃ṛite jôl ḍhoke.*
>
> At high tide the water comes into the creek.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খাঁড়ি** (*khā̃ṛi*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *khāṛī*, "a creek, an inlet", under the reconstructed *khāḍa* (CDIAL 3860), and again under *khaḍḍa* (CDIAL 3790).
