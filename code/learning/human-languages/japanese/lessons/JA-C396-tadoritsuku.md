## You'll want to know: たどりつく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C396-YUKISAKI396-03]; assesses=[] -->

**たどりつく** — *tadoritsuku* — "to finally reach".

> **ばんに やどに たどりつきました。** — *ban ni yado ni tadoritsukimashita* — "We finally reached the inn in the evening."

Polite **たどりつきます**, *tadoritsukimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ふねが しまに たどりつきました。**, *fune ga shima ni tadoritsukimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たどりつく** (*tadoritsuku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tadori*, "following a path" (from *tadoru*) and *tsuku*, "to arrive"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
