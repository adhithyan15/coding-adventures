## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**oceano** ← Latin *oceanus*, "the ocean" (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing from Latin. Latin took it from Greek *ōkeanos*, the great river round the world.
