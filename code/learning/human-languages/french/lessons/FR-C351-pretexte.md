## You'll want to know: le prétexte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C351-REACTION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le prétexte** — "the pretext".

> Il a trouvé un prétexte pour partir plus tôt.
>
> *He found a pretext to leave earlier.*

*Sous prétexte que* — on the pretext that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prétexte* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praetextus* "an outward show, a pretext" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *praetexere* "to weave in front, to cover", built on *texere* "to weave".
