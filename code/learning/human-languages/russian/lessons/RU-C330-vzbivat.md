## You'll want to know: взбивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C330-SMESHIVAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**взбивать** (*vzbivát*) — "to whisk, to beat".

> Папа взбивает яйца.
>
> *Pápa vzbiváyet yáytsa.*
>
> Dad is beating the eggs.

Imperfective; the perfective is *взбить* (*vzbit*), *взобью*. Also *взбить подушку* — to plump up a pillow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*взбивать* is built on *вз-* "up" and *бить* "to beat", inherited from Proto-Slavic *biti*, PIE *bʰeyH-* (EtymDB).
