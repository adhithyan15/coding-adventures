# (en el mostrador) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-03, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el carné** and **el ticket**. The notice below asks for both,
at different moments.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-01, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-03, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-05] -->

A notice above the counter at a leisure centre. Read it, then answer.

> **CAMBIOS Y DEVOLUCIONES**

> 1. Haga **cola** en la ventanilla 2. No se atiende sin turno.

> 2. **Muestre** el **carné** de socio y el **ticket** de compra.

> 3. Si no tiene el ticket, **apúntese** en la lista y le llamamos.

> 4. **Revisamos** cada caso en 48 horas.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: the two things you must have with you]
- [YOU SAY: what to do if you have lost the receipt]
- [YOU SAY: how long the decision takes]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-02, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-04, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-06] -->

The notice asks for two pieces of plastic and the words are not
interchangeable. The **carné de socio** says who you are; the **ticket** says
you paid. Lose the first and they cannot identify you; lose the second and
they cannot see what you bought. So step three deals only with the missing
ticket, and identity is still required.

Then look at the verbs. **Muestre**, **apúntese**, **haga** are all *usted*
commands — the register of any counter in Spain. **Apúntese** carries its
pronoun on the end and takes a written accent to hold the stress where it was
in *apunte*; without the mark you would read *apuntese* with the stress in the
wrong place.

**Revisamos** breaks the pattern deliberately. The first three lines tell you
what to do; the fourth says what *we* do. Moving to *nosotros* at the end is
how a notice stops giving orders and starts making a promise.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-01, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-02, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-03, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-04, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-05, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-06] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four numbered lines
for a counter notice: tell people to queue, to show a card, to keep the
receipt, and say that you check each case within two days.

Then check yourself: did you use the *usted* command for the instructions and
switch to *nosotros* for what the office does?
