Malayalam, chapter 206: Naughty, Obedient, Selfish, Permanent, Single.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കുസൃതിയുള്ള (kusṛtiyuḷḷa) — naughty, mischievous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for strange, then the Malayalam for modern.

You'll want to know: കുസൃതിയുള്ള.
കുസൃതിയുള്ള — kusṛtiyuḷḷa — "naughty, mischievous".

The word, taken apart.
കുസൃതിയുള്ള (kusṛtiyuḷḷa) joins Sanskrit kusṛti, "trickery, a crooked way", to native -uḷḷa, "that has" (DEDR 697).

Lesson 3 of 5.
സ്വാർത്ഥമായ (svārtthamāya) — selfish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for naughty, then the Malayalam for obedient.

You'll want to know: സ്വാർത്ഥമായ.
സ്വാർത്ഥമായ — svārtthamāya — "selfish".

The word, taken apart.
സ്വാർത്ഥമായ (svārtthamāya) is Sanskrit svārtha, "one's own aim": sva, "own", and artha, "aim" (CDIAL 638), with native āya, "having become" (DEDR 333). DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.
