## You'll want to know: निमज्जति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C302-JALAUGHA302-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निमज्जति** — *nimajjati* — "sinks; goes under".

> जले नौका शनैः निमज्जति।
>
> *jale naukā śanaiḥ nimajjati.*
>
> The boat slowly goes under in the water.

Past (it …): नौका जले निमग्नवती, *naukā jale nimagnavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निमज्जति** (*nimajjati*) is built from *ni*, "down", and the root √majj, "to sink, dive", as in *majjati*; MW gives *ni-majj* as to sink down, plunge into or disappear (MW).
