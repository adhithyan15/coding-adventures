## You'll want to know: exploser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C302-INCENDIE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**exploser** — "to explode, to blow up".

> Une bouteille de gaz a explosé.
>
> *A gas bottle exploded.*

Informal: *exploser de rire* — to burst out laughing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exploser* is built on *explosion*, a learned borrowing from Latin *explōsiō*, from *explōdere* (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
