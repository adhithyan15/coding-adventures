## You'll want to know: つまり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C367-HAKKIRI367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**つまり** — *tsumari* — "in other words; that is".

> **つまり、あめが ふったら、ちゅうしです。** — *tsumari, ame ga futtara, chūshi desu* — "In other words, if it rains, it is called off."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つまり** (*tsumari*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
