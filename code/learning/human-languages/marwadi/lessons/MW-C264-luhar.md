## You'll want to know: लुहार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C264-LUHAR264-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लुहार** — *luhār* — "a blacksmith".

> लुहार कुल्हाड़ी बणावै है।
>
> *luhār kulhāṛī baṇāvai hai.*
>
> The blacksmith makes an axe.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लुहार** (*luhār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lohakāra*, "blacksmith". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *luhār* (CDIAL 11159).
