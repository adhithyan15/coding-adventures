## You'll want to know: ઉલેચવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C410-BATH410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉલેચવું** — *ulecvũ* — "to bail out (water)".

> વરસાદ પછી અમે ઓરડામાંથી પાણી ઉલેચ્યું.
>
> *varsād pachī ame orḍāmā̃thī pāṇī ulecyũ.*
>
> After the rain we bailed the water out of the room.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પાણી ઉલેચ્યું, *teṇe pāṇī ulecyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉલેચવું** (*ulecvũ*) goes back to *\*udriñcati*, "empties", a form CDIAL lists beside Sanskrit *údricyatē*; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to draw out water" (CDIAL 2061).
