# നാശനഷ്ടം (nāśanaṣṭaṁ) — damage; losses

## You'll want to know: നാശനഷ്ടം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C363-JVALA363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**നാശനഷ്ടം** — *nāśanaṣṭaṁ* — "damage; losses".

> വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കത്തിൽ ഗ്രാമത്തിന് വലിയ നാശനഷ്ടം ഉണ്ടായി.
>
> *veḷḷappokkattil grāmattinŭ valiya nāśanaṣṭaṁ uṇṭāyi.*
>
> The village suffered great damage in the flood.

Two words that say almost the same, destruction and loss: the whole cost of a disaster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നാശനഷ്ടം** (*nāśanaṣṭaṁ*) is built from two Sanskrit words: *nāśa*, "destruction, ruin" (CDIAL 7084), and *naṣṭa*, "lost, destroyed" (Monier-Williams), the source of നഷ്ടം (*naṣṭaṁ*), "a loss".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
