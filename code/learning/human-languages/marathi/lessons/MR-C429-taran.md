## You'll want to know: तारण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C429-LILAV429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तारण** — *tāraṇ* — "collateral; security".

> त्याने कर्जासाठी शेत तारण ठेवलं.
>
> *tyāne karjāsāṭhī śet tāraṇ ṭhevlaṁ.*
>
> He put up his field as security for the loan.

Neuter.

तारण ठेवणे, to give as security for a loan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तारण** (*tāraṇ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *tāraṇ*, "deposit made against a debt", under Sanskrit *tāraṇa*, "crossing, safe passage" (CDIAL 5795).
