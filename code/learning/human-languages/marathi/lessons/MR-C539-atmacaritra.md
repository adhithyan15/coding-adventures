## You'll want to know: आत्मचरित्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C539-DAD539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आत्मचरित्र** — *ātmacaritra* — "an autobiography".

> आजोबांनी त्यांचं आत्मचरित्र लिहिलं.
>
> *ājobānnī tyāñcaṁ ātmacaritra lihilaṁ.*
>
> Grandfather wrote his autobiography.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आत्मचरित्र** (*ātmacaritra*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *ātman*, "self", and *caritra*, "conduct, a life story" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
