## You'll want to know: కుట్ర
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C354-KUTRA354-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కుట్ర** — *kuṭra* — "a conspiracy; a plot".

> రాజు మీద ఒక కుట్ర జరిగింది.
>
> *rāju mīda oka kuṭra jarigindi.*
>
> There was a plot against the king.

కుట్ర పన్ను, to hatch a plot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కుట్ర** (*kuṭra*) is a Telugu word for a secret plot; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
