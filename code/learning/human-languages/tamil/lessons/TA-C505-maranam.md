# மரணம் (maraṇam) — death

## You'll want to know: மரணம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C505-MARANAM505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**மரணம்** — *maraṇam* — "death".

> அவருடைய மரணம் எல்லோருக்கும் அதிர்ச்சியாக இருந்தது.
>
> *avaruṭaiya maraṇam ellōrukkum atircciyāka iruntatu.*
>
> His death was a shock to everyone.

Formal and written; in speech சாவு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மரணம்** (*maraṇam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *maraṇa*, "dying" (CDIAL 9869).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
