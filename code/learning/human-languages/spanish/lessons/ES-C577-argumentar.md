## You'll want to know: argumentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C577-DEFIENDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**argumentar** — "to argue, to give reasons".

> Tienes que argumentar tu respuesta.
>
> *You have to give reasons for your answer.*

To argue a point with reasons, not to quarrel: a quarrel is *discutir* or *pelearse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*argumentar* is built on *argumento*, a learned borrowing from Latin *argumentum* 'proof', from *arguere* 'to make clear, to prove' (Lewis & Short).
