## You'll want to know: ઘુવડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C237-WINGS237-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘુવડ** — *ghuvaḍ* — "owl".

> **રાત્રે ઘુવડ બોલ્યું.** — *rātre ghuvaḍ bolyũ* — "The owl called at night."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘુવડ** (*ghuvaḍ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ghūka*, "owl", which CDIAL takes from Dravidian, as in Tamil *kūkai*, after T. Burrow (CDIAL 4494).
