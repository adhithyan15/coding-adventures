## You'll want to know: جلوگیری کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C377-BOHRAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جلوگیری کردن** (*jolowgiri kardan*) — "to prevent".

> پلیس از تصادف جلوگیری کرد.
>
> *polis az tasâdof jolowgiri kard.*
>
> The police prevented an accident.

Takes *az*: *az chizi jolowgiri kardan*. *Jolow* "front" + *giri* "taking".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جلوگیری کردن** is native Persian, built from *jolow* "front" + *giri* "taking", from *gir-*, the present stem of *gereftan* "to take", + *kardan*. To prevent is to take the front, to block the way.
