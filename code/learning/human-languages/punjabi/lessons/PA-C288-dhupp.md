## You'll want to know: ਧੁੱਪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C288-HEAT288-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੁੱਪ** — *dhupp* — "sunshine".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਤੇਜ਼ ਧੁੱਪ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ।
>
> *kall tez dhupp hovegī.*
>
> Tomorrow there will be strong sunshine.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੁੱਪ** (*dhupp*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhuppā*, "sunshine"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dhupp* under it (CDIAL 6825).
