Russian, chapter 97: Broom, Washing machine, Sofa, Wardrobe, Saucepan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
веник (vénik) — a broom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a comb, then the Russian for rubbish.

You'll want to know: веник.
веник (vénik) — "a broom".

The word, taken apart.
веник (vénik) — Old Russian venikŭ, Czech věník, "a bundle". Vasmer links it with венок, "a wreath", and вить, "to twist": a broom is a bound bundle of twigs.

Lesson 2 of 5.
стиральная машина (stirálnaya mashína) — a washing machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for rubbish, then the Russian for a broom.

You'll want to know: стиральная машина.
стиральная машина (stirálnaya mashína) — "a washing machine".

The word, taken apart.
стиральная машина (stirálnaya mashína) is "washing machine": стиральная, from стирать, "to wash", from тереть, "to rub" (Latin terō, "I rub", is a cousin), with машина, "machine", from German Maschine (Vasmer).

The word, taken apart.
диван (diván) — Vasmer thinks it more likely came from the West, French divan, "a couch", than straight from its Turkish and Persian source divan, "a council, a council chamber" with its cushioned benches.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a washing machine, then the Russian for a sofa.

You'll want to know: шкаф.
шкаф (shkaf) — "a wardrobe, a cupboard".

The word, taken apart.
шкаф (shkaf) — most likely reshaped from older шкап under the influence of German forms such as Schaff, Middle High German schaf, "a cupboard" (Vasmer).
