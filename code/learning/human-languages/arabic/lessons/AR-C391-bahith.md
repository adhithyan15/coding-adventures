## You'll want to know: باحث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C391-BAHTH391-01]; assesses=[] -->

**باحث** — *bāḥith* — "a researcher".

> يعمل الباحث في الجامعة منذ سنوات.
>
> *yaʿmalu al-bāḥith fī al-jāmiʿa mundhu sanawāt.*
>
> The researcher has worked at the university for years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**باحث** (*bāḥith*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-ḥ-th*, "to dig, to search" (Lane). *Bāḥith* is the active participle of *baḥatha*, to search: a researcher.
