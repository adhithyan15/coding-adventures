## You'll want to know: поначалу
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C274-POSTEPENNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**поначалу** (*ponachálu*) — "at first".

> Поначалу мне было трудно.
>
> *Ponachálu mne býlo trúdno.*
>
> At first it was hard for me.

Colloquial, and it hints that things changed later. The neutral word is *сначала* (*snachála*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поначалу* is built on *по* and *начало* "beginning", inherited through Old East Slavic *načęlo* from Proto-Slavic *načędlo* (EtymDB), the root of *начать* "to begin".
