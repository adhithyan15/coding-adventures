Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 234: The Herd.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
एवाळियो (evāḷiyo) — a shepherd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to tighten, then the Marwadi for a small bell.

You'll want to know: एवाळियो.
एवाळियो — evāḷiyo — "a shepherd".
एवाळियो जंगळ मांय जावै है। evāḷiyo jaṅgaḷ mā̃y jāvai hai. The shepherd goes out into the scrubland.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
एवाळियो (evāḷiyo) is inherited from Sanskrit avipāla, "a shepherd", built from avi, "sheep", and pāla, "a keeper"; CDIAL lists Marwari evāḷiyo (CDIAL 893).

Lesson 4 of 6.
हांकणो (hā̃kṇo) — to drive (animals or a cart).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a long stick, then the Marwadi for to graze.

You'll want to know: हांकणो.
हांकणो — hā̃kṇo — "to drive (animals or a cart)".
हूं काल ऊंट हांकसूं। hū̃ kāl ū̃ṭ hā̃ksū̃. Tomorrow I'll drive the camel.
Past (I …): म्हैं ऊंट हांक्यो, mhaĩ ū̃ṭ hā̃kyo.

The word, taken apart.
हांकणो (hā̃kṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit hakkayati, "calls to"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb, "drives", under it (CDIAL 13939): a herder drives the animals by calling to them.
