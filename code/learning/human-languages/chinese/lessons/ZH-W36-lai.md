## Script — one piece, top to bottom
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-LAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 来

**One piece, three layers, top to bottom.**

**The top layer, three strokes.** A short horizontal. Under it, a dot on the left and a short left-falling stroke on the right, leaning in toward each other.

**The middle, two strokes.** A long horizontal, then the vertical through the middle of everything, from the very top, through both bars, to the foot.

**The foot, two strokes.** A left-falling stroke and a right-falling stroke, spreading out from the vertical like the legs of 大.

3 + 2 + 2 = seven.
