## You'll want to know: el pronóstico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C696-CLIMA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el pronóstico** — "the forecast; the prognosis".

> El pronóstico dice que va a llover.
>
> *The forecast says it is going to rain.*

*El pronóstico del tiempo* — the weather forecast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pronóstico* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *prognōsticus*, from Greek *prognōstikós* "knowing beforehand".
