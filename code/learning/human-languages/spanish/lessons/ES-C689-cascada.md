## You'll want to know: la cascada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C689-AGUA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la cascada** — "the waterfall".

> Caminamos hasta la cascada.
>
> *We walked to the waterfall.*

A very big one is *una catarata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cascada* was borrowed from Italian *cascata* "a fall" (EtymDB), from *cascare* "to fall", which goes back to Latin *cadere* "to fall".

## What you've built
