## You'll want to know: zhìxiàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C512-DREAMS512-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhìxiàng* — "ambition in life; the goal one sets oneself".

> *wǒ zuì dà de zhìxiàng shì dāng yīshēng* — My greatest ambition is to be a doctor.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhìxiàng* is built from *zhì* 'a longing' and *xiàng* 'to face', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyiH* and *xjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'what the mind longs for' and the second 'to face'.
