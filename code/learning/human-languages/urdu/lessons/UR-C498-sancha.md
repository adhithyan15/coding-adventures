## You'll want to know: سانچا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C498-AVA498-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سانچا** — *sāṅchā* — "a mould".

> اس سانچے سے مٹی کے کھلونے بنتے ہیں۔
>
> *is sāṅche se miṭṭī ke khilaune bante haiṅ.*
>
> Clay toys are made in this mould.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سانچا** (*sāṅchā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sā̃cā*, "a mould", under Sanskrit *sañcaka*, "a mould, a figure" (CDIAL 13096).
