Tamil, chapter 261: Diabetes, Exercise, Yoga, Itch, Dizziness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சர்க்கரை நோய் (carkkarai nōy) — diabetes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to float, then the Tamil for to squeeze.

You'll want to know: சர்க்கரை நோய்.
சர்க்கரை நோய் — carkkarai nōy — "diabetes".

The word, taken apart.
சர்க்கரை நோய் (carkkarai nōy) is carkkarai, "sugar", from Sanskrit śarkarā, "candied sugar" (CDIAL 12338), and native nōy, "illness, pain" (DEDR 3793): "sugar illness".
