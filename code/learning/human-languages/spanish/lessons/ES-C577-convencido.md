## You'll want to know: convencido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C577-DEFIENDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**convencido** — "convinced".

> Estoy convencido de que es verdad.
>
> *I am convinced that it is true.*

With *estar* and *de que*: *estoy convencida de que...*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convencido* is built on *convencer*, inherited from Latin *convincere* (EtymDB) 'to overcome, to prove wrong', *con* + *vincere* 'to conquer' (Lewis & Short).
