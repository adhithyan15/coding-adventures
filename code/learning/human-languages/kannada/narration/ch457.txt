Kannada, chapter 457: Wandering About.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಓಣಿ (ōṇi) — a lane; an alley.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a landmark, then the Kannada for to view.

You'll want to know: ಓಣಿ.
ಓಣಿ — ōṇi — "a lane; an alley".
ಹಳೆಯ ಊರಿನ ಓಣಿಗಳು ತುಂಬಾ ಕಿರಿದು. haḷeya ūrina ōṇigaḷu tumbā kiridu. The lanes of the old town are very narrow.
Plural: ಓಣಿಗಳು, ōṇigaḷu.
A narrow way between houses; ಬೀದಿ is a proper street.

The word, taken apart.
ಓಣಿ (ōṇi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1046 lists Kannada ōṇi, "a lane, an alley, a street".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಹಾದುಹೋಗು (hāduhōgu) — to pass through; to pass by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to view, then the Kannada for a lane.

You'll want to know: ಹಾದುಹೋಗು.
ಹಾದುಹೋಗು — hāduhōgu — "to pass through; to pass by".
ನಮ್ಮ ರೈಲು ಸುರಂಗದ ಮೂಲಕ ಹಾದುಹೋಯಿತು. namma railu suraṅgada mūlaka hāduhōyitu. Our train passed through a tunnel.
Past (I …): ಹಾದುಹೋದೆ, hāduhōde.
ಮೂಲಕ ಹಾದುಹೋಗು (hāduhōgu), to pass through; ಪಕ್ಕದಿಂದ ಹಾದುಹೋಗು (hāduhōgu), to pass by.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಾದುಹೋಗು (hāduhōgu) is native Dravidian: hādu, the participle of hāy, "to cross, to step over" (DEDR 4087), with ಹೋಗು, to go.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಅನ್ವೇಷಿಸು (anvēṣisu) — to explore; to search out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lane, then the Kannada for to pass through.

You'll want to know: ಅನ್ವೇಷಿಸು.
ಅನ್ವೇಷಿಸು — anvēṣisu — "to explore; to search out".
ನಾವು ಕಾಡಿನ ಹೊಸ ದಾರಿಗಳನ್ನು ಅನ್ವೇಷಿಸಿದೆವು. nāvu kāḍina hosa dārigaḷannu anvēṣisidevu. We explored new paths in the forest.
Past (I …): ಅನ್ವೇಷಿಸಿದೆ, anvēṣiside.
What is explored takes -ಅನ್ನು; ಅನ್ವೇಷಣೆ is exploration.

The word, taken apart.
ಅನ್ವೇಷಿಸು (anvēṣisu) is built from Sanskrit anvēṣaṇa, "seeking, searching for" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತಾಣ (tāṇa) — a spot; a haunt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to pass through, then the Kannada for to explore.

You'll want to know: ತಾಣ.
ತಾಣ — tāṇa — "a spot; a haunt".
ಈ ಜಲಪಾತ ಪ್ರವಾಸಿಗರ ಅಚ್ಚುಮೆಚ್ಚಿನ ತಾಣ. ī jalapāta pravāsigara accumeccina tāṇa. This waterfall is a favourite spot for tourists.
ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ ತಾಣ (tāṇa), a tourist spot.

The word, taken apart.
ತಾಣ (tāṇa) is usually traced to Sanskrit sthāna, "a place", through a Prakrit form ṭhāṇa (CDIAL 13753).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಂಗೊಳಿಸು (kaṅgoḷisu) — to look splendid; to be a fine sight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to explore, then the Kannada for a spot.

You'll want to know: ಕಂಗೊಳಿಸು.
ಕಂಗೊಳಿಸು — kaṅgoḷisu — "to look splendid; to be a fine sight".
ಮಳೆಯ ನಂತರ ಬೆಟ್ಟಗಳು ಹಸಿರಾಗಿ ಕಂಗೊಳಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು. maḷeya nantara beṭṭagaḷu hasirāgi kaṅgoḷisuttiddavu. After the rain the hills looked splendid in green.
Past (it …): ಕಂಗೊಳಿಸಿತು, kaṅgoḷisitu.
Said of landscapes, lights and festivals: ಊರು ದೀಪಗಳಿಂದ ಕಂಗೊಳಿಸಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಂಗೊಳಿಸು (kaṅgoḷisu) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled; DEDR shows no clear source for it.
