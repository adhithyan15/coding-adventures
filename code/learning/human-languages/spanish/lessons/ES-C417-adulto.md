# adulto, adulta — fully grown

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-CURSO, ES-LEX-NADAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **un curso** and **nadar**. If this class is for grown-ups,
the notice says **para adultos**, for adults.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-ADULTO]; assesses=[] -->

Latin **adultus** meant grown up, from **adolescere**, to grow. English
**adult** is the same word with its ending shortened. Spanish keeps the ending
and matches gender and number: **adulto**, **adulta**, **adultos**, **adultas**.

## Grammar Lens: say who it is for
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-ADULTO, ES-LEX-CURSO, ES-LEX-NADAR] -->

Use **para** for the intended group: **un curso de natación para adultos**.
The plural **adultos** can describe a mixed group or a group of men.
