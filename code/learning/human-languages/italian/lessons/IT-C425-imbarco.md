## You'll want to know: l'imbarco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C425-AEROPORTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'imbarco** — "the boarding".

> L'imbarco chiude venti minuti prima.
>
> *Boarding closes twenty minutes before.*

*La carta d'imbarco* — the boarding pass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imbarco* is built on *imbarcare* 'to take on board', from *in-* and *barca* 'boat', which is inherited from Late Latin *barca*.
