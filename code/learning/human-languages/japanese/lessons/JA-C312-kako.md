## You'll want to know: かこ

**かこ** — *kako* — "the past".

> **かこの じけんを しらべました。** — *kako no jiken o shirabemashita* — "I looked into incidents from the past."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かこ** (*kako*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ka*, "go beyond" (Middle Chinese *kwaH*) and *ko*, "past" (*khjoH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
