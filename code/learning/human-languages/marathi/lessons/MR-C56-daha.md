## You'll want to know: दहा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-DAHA]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **दहा** — *dahā* — **10**

Sanskrit **दश** (*daśa*) — Latin *decem*, English **ten**. This is the one to
hold hardest, because the eleven-to-eighteen you are about to meet are all
*something-and-ten* with the ten worn down to a **-रा** on the end.
