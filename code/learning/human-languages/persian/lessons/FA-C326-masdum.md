## You'll want to know: مصدوم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C326-MASDUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مصدوم** (*masdum*) — "injured; an injured person".

> مصدوم را با آمبولانس به بیمارستان بردند.
>
> *Masdum râ bâ âmbulâns be bimârestân bordand.*
>
> They took the injured man to hospital by ambulance.

In news reports; plural *masdumân* or *masdumin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*masdum* is usually traced to Arabic *maṣdūm*, "struck", a participle of the same root as *ṣadma* "a blow"; EtymDB lists no entry.
