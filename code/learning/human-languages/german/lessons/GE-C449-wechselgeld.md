## You'll want to know: das Wechselgeld
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C449-KASSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Wechselgeld** — "the change (money given back)".

> Sie zählt das Wechselgeld und lächelt.
>
> *She counts her change and smiles.*

No plural. Small coins in a pocket are *das Kleingeld*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wechselgeld* is built from *Wechsel* 'change, exchange', inherited from Old High German *wehsal* (EtymDB), and *Geld* 'money'.
