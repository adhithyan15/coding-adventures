## You'll want to know: কৃপণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C457-UDAR457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কৃপণ** — *kripôn* — "miserly; a miser".

> লোকটার অনেক টাকা আছে, কিন্তু সে খুব কৃপণ।
>
> *lokṭār ônek ṭākā āchhe, kintu she khub kripôn.*
>
> The man has plenty of money, but he is a real miser.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কৃপণ** (*kripôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kṛpaṇa*, "miserable, pitiable" (CDIAL 3436), which Monier-Williams also gives as "niggardly"; that is the sense Bengali kept.
