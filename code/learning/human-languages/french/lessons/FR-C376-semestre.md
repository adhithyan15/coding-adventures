## You'll want to know: le semestre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C376-FAC-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le semestre** — "the semester, the half-year".

> Le premier semestre commence en septembre.
>
> *The first semester starts in September.*

Six months; three months is *un trimestre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*semestre* comes from Latin *sēmēstris* "of six months" (EtymDB), built from *sex* "six" and *mēnsis* "month" (Lewis & Short).
