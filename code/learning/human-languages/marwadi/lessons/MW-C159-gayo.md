# गयो, गई, गया — went

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-01, MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04, MW-LEX-C49-FAMILY49-03, MW-LEX-C49-FAMILY49-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I go to the market every day*. Then *yesterday*, **काल**, and *a
boy*, *a girl*.

## Grammar Lens: one word for the past
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GAYO-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-02, MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04, MW-SCRIPT-BAJAR-01] -->

**गयो** is the past of **जाणो**, all on its own: no **हूं** and no **है** after it.

> **हूं काल बाजार गयो।**
>
> *hū̃ kāl bājār gayo.* — I went to the market yesterday.

It ends like **छोरो**, and changes like it, to agree with whoever went: **गयो** for
a man, **गई** for a woman, **गया** for more than one.

> **मां घरै गई।**
>
> *mā̃ gharai gaī.* — Mother went home.

> **सगळा घरै गया।**
>
> *sagḷā gharai gayā.* — Everyone went home.

With a past, **काल** is *yesterday*. CDIAL lists Old Marwari *gayaü*, "gone"
(CDIAL 4008).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GAYO-01, MW-LEX-C102-DEF102-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GHARAI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-VERB-01] -->

A woman says **हूं काल बाजार गई।** (*hū̃ kāl bājār gaī.*)

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: **राम काल बाजार गयो।** — *rām kāl bājār gayo.* — Ram went to the market yesterday.]
- [YOU SAY: **मां घरै गई।** — *mā̃ gharai gaī.* — Mother went home.]
- [YOU SAY: **वो कोनी गयो।** — *vo konī gayo.* — He didn't go.]
