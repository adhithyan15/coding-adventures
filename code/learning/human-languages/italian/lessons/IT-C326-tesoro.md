## You'll want to know: il tesoro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C326-MAGIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il tesoro** — "the treasure; darling".

> Hanno trovato il tesoro sotto un albero.
>
> *They found the treasure under a tree.*

*Tesoro!* — darling!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tesoro* is inherited from Latin *thēsaurus* "a treasure, a store" (EtymDB), from Greek *thēsaurós*.
