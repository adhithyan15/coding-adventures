## You'll want to know: o detido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C295-SUSPEITO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o detido** — "the person arrested, the detainee".

> O detido vai ser ouvido amanhã.
>
> *The man arrested will be questioned tomorrow.*

The past participle of *deter* used as a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*detido* is the past participle of *deter* "to arrest" used as a noun; the verb comes from Latin *dētinēre* "to hold back" (EtymDB).
