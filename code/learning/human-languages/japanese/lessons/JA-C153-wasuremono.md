## You'll want to know: わすれもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C153-OUTING153-03]; assesses=[] -->

**わすれもの** — *wasuremono* — "something left behind".

> **この かさは ちちの わすれものです。** — *kono kasa wa chichi no wasuremono desu* — "This umbrella is something my father left behind."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わすれもの** (*wasuremono*) is native Japanese, built from *wasure*, "forgetting", the stem of *wasureru*, and *mono*, "thing", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
