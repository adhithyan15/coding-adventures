## You'll want to know: perterreō, perterrēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C496-PAV-03]; assesses=[] -->

**perterreō, perterrēre** — "to terrify thoroughly".

> Tonitrus puerōs perterret.
>
> *The thunder terrifies the children.*

*perterreō, perterrēre, perterruī, perterritum*: *per-* 'thoroughly' + *terreō* 'to frighten'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**perterreō** is built on *per-* "thoroughly" and *terreō* "to frighten" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *terrify* and *terror* belong to the same family.
