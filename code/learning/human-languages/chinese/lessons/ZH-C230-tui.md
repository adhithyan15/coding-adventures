## You'll want to know: tuǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C230-BODY230-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tuǐ* — "leg".

> *wǒ shēnshang téng tuǐ yě téng* — I ache all over, and my legs too.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuǐ* is inherited from Middle Chinese *thwojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the thigh'; the dictionary lists the character as the everyday form of another.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
