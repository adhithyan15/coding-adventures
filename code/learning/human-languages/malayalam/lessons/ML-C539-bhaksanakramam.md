## You'll want to know: ഭക്ഷണക്രമം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C539-AHARAM539-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭക്ഷണക്രമം** — *bhakṣaṇakramaṁ* — "a diet; eating habits".

> വൈദ്യൻ പറഞ്ഞതുപോലെ ഞാൻ ഭക്ഷണക്രമം മാറ്റി.
>
> *vaidyan paṟaññatupōle ñān bhakṣaṇakramaṁ māṟṟi.*
>
> I changed my diet as the doctor said.

ഭക്ഷണം food + ക്രമം order, an arrangement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭക്ഷണക്രമം** (*bhakṣaṇakramaṁ*) is a learned compound built from ഭക്ഷണം, from Sanskrit *bhakṣaṇa*, "eating" (Monier-Williams), and *krama*, "a step, a series, an order" (CDIAL 3577).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
