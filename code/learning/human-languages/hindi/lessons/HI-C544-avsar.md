## You'll want to know: अवसर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C544-KISMAT544-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अवसर** — *avsar* — "an opportunity (formal)".

> ऐसा अवसर रोज़ नहीं आता।
>
> *Aisā avsar roz nahīṃ ātā.*
>
> An opportunity like this does not come every day.

Masculine.

A formal word; everyday speech says मौक़ा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवसर** (*avsar*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *avasara*, "an occasion, a favourable opportunity" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
