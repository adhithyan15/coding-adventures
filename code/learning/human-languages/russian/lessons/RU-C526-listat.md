## You'll want to know: листать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C526-BOOKS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**листать** (*listát*) — "to leaf through".

> В очереди я листал журнал.
>
> *V ócheredi ya listál zhurnál.*
>
> While I was in the queue I leafed through a magazine.

Imperfective; perfective пролистать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*листать* is built on *лист* 'a leaf, a sheet of paper'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
