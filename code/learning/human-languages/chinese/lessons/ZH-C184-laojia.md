## You'll want to know: lǎojiā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C184-HOLIDAY184-04]; assesses=[] -->

*lǎojiā* — "home town; family home".

> *Chūnjié wǒ yào huí lǎojiā* — I'm going back to my home town for the new year holiday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǎojiā* is built from *lǎo* 'old' and 家 'home', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lawX* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the old home.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
