# नाना (nānā) — grandfather, the mother's father

## You'll want to know: नाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C116-GRAND-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नाना** (*nānā*) — "grandfather", your MOTHER's father.

So **दादा** and **नाना** are both "grandfather" in English, and never the same man in Hindi.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A grandfather, on your mother's side.
