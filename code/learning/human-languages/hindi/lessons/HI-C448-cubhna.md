## You'll want to know: चुभना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C448-CHONCH448-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चुभना** — *cubhnā* — "to prick; to stick into".

> एक काँटा मेरे पैर में चुभ गया।
>
> *ek kā̃ṭā mere pair meṁ cubh gayā.*
>
> A thorn pricked my foot.

Past (it …): वह काँटा उँगली में चुभा, *vah kā̃ṭā uṅglī meṁ cubhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुभना** (*cubhnā*) goes back to *\*cubhyate*, "is pierced", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4867), which lists Hindi *cubhnā*. A harsh word चुभता too: it stings.
