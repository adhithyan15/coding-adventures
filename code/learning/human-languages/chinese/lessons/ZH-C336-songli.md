## You'll want to know: sònglǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C336-SEEOFF336-04]; assesses=[] -->

*sònglǐ* — "to give a present".

> *Chūnjié de shíhou Zhōngguó rén chángcháng sònglǐ* — At the lunar new year Chinese people often give presents.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sònglǐ* is built from 送 'to send' and *lǐ* 'a courtesy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *suwngH* and *lejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says of the second, quoting an older dictionary, that it is how one serves the spirits and brings blessing.

## What you've built
