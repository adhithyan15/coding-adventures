## You'll want to know: богатство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C367-RAVENSTVO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**богатство** (*bogátstvo*) — "wealth, riches".

> Здоровье — главное богатство.
>
> *Zdoróvye — glávnoye bogátstvo.*
>
> Health is the greatest wealth.

Neuter. *Природные богатства* — natural resources.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**богатство** is built on *богатый* "rich", which is usually traced to *бог* in its old sense "a share, good fortune".
