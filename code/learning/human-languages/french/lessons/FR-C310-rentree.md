## You'll want to know: la rentrée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C310-JEUNESSE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la rentrée** — "the start of the school year; the return".

> La rentrée a lieu en septembre.
>
> *The school year starts in September.*

A very French notion: after the summer, everyone goes back to school and to work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rentrée* is built on *rentrer* "to go back in", from *re-* and *entrer*, which is inherited from Latin *intrāre* "to go in" (Lewis & Short).
