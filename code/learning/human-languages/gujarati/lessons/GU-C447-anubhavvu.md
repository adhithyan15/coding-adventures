# અનુભવવું (anubhavvũ) — to feel; to experience

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C447-HARAKH447-02, GU-LEX-C447-HARAKH447-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to rejoice, then the Gujarati for warmth of feeling.

## You'll want to know: અનુભવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C447-HARAKH447-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અનુભવવું** — *anubhavvũ* — "to feel; to experience".

> લગ્નમાં મેં ઘણી ખુશી અનુભવી.
>
> *lagnamā̃ mẽ ghaṇī khuśī anubhavī.*
>
> I felt great happiness at the wedding.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ખુશી અનુભવી, *teṇe khuśī anubhavī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અનુભવવું** (*anubhavvũ*) is built on **અનુભવ** (*anubhav*), "experience", a tatsama of Sanskrit *anubhava*, "perception, fruition" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending *-vũ*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
