## You'll want to know: наезд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C298-NAEZD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**наезд** (*nayézd*) — "a hit (by a vehicle)".

> Вчера на углу был наезд на мальчика.
>
> *Vcherá na uglú byl nayézd na málchika.*
>
> Yesterday a boy was hit by a car on the corner.

*Наезд на* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наезд* is built on *наехать* "to run into", from *на-* and *ехать* "to go by vehicle", inherited from Proto-Slavic *ěxati* "to travel" (EtymDB).
