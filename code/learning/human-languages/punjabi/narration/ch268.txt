Punjabi, chapter 268: At the Bus Stand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਅੱਡਾ (aḍḍā) — a bus stand; a stand for vehicles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a court of law, then the Punjabi for whereabouts.

You'll want to know: ਅੱਡਾ.
ਅੱਡਾ — aḍḍā — "a bus stand; a stand for vehicles".
ਬੱਸ ਅੱਡਾ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦੇ ਬਾਹਰ ਹੈ। bass aḍḍā shahir de bāhar hai. The bus stand is outside the town.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਅੱਡਾ (aḍḍā) is inherited from a reconstructed aḍḍ, "obstruct, stop"; CDIAL lists Punjabi aḍḍā, "perch for birds", under it (CDIAL 188). The sense "a stand" is not given there.
