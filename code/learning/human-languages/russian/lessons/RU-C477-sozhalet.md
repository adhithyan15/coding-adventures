## You'll want to know: сожалеть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C477-LONGING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сожалеть** (*sozhalét*) — "to regret".

> Я сожалею, что не поехал с вами.
>
> *Ya sozhaléyu, chto ne poyékhal s vámi.*
>
> I regret not going with you.

Imperfective; present сожалею. *Сожалеть о* + prepositional. More formal than *жалеть*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сожалеть* is built on *со-* and *жалеть* 'to pity', from *жаль* 'pity'; EtymDB lists these words without a source, and they are usually traced to Proto-Slavic *žalь* 'grief'.
