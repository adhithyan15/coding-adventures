## You'll want to know: وصول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C425-RAKIB425-05]; assesses=[] -->

**وصول** — *wuṣūl* — "arrival".

> اتصلت بأمي بعد الوصول.
>
> *ittaṣaltu bi-ummī baʿda al-wuṣūl.*
>
> I phoned my mother after arrival.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وصول** (*wuṣūl*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ṣ-l*, "to arrive, to reach" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of *waṣala*, "to arrive".
