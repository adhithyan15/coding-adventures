## You'll want to know: die Datei
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C393-DATEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Datei** — "the (computer) file".

> Die Datei ist zu groß für eine E-Mail.
>
> *The file is too big for an email.*

Plural: *die Dateien*. *Eine Datei öffnen* — to open a file.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Datei* is usually explained as a 20th-century German blend of *Daten* "data", from Latin *datum* "something given" (EtymDB), and *Kartei* "card index". EtymDB has no entry for *Datei* itself.
