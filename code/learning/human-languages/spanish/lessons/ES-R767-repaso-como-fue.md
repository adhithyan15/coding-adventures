## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-FELIZ, ES-GRAMMAR-ADJECTIVE-NO-GENDER-Z, ES-LEX-MAS-QUE, ES-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE, ES-LEX-ESPERAR-HOPE, ES-SEMANTIC-HOPE-AND-WAIT, ES-LEX-DURANTE, ES-SEMANTIC-DURATION, ES-GRAMMAR-NARRATIVE-10, ES-GRAMMAR-SCENE-VS-EVENT-06] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In your story, which tense set the scene? (The imperfect: *llovía*.) Which told the events? (The preterite: *llegamos*, *comimos*.) Why does *feliz* not change for *ella*? (It ends in a consonant.) What two things can *esperar* mean? (To wait and to hope.)
