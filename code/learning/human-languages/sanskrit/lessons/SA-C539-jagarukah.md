## You'll want to know: जागरूकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C539-AVAHITA539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जागरूकः** — *jāgarūkaḥ* — "watchful".

> यदि रक्षकः जागरूकः अस्ति, तर्हि चौरः न आगच्छति।
>
> *yadi rakṣakaḥ jāgarūkaḥ asti, tarhi cauraḥ na āgacchati.*
>
> If the guard is watchful, the thief does not come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जागरूकः** (*jāgarūkaḥ*) comes from the root √jāgṛ, "to be awake", with the ending *-ūka*; MW gives *jāgarūka* as wakeful, watchful (MW).
