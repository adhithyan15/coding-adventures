## You'll want to know: очевидный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C344-LOGIKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**очевидный** (*ochevídnyy*) — "obvious".

> Ответ был очевиден для всех.
>
> *Otvét byl ochevíden dlya vsekh.*
>
> The answer was obvious to everyone.

Short forms *очевиден, очевидна*. *Очевидно* — evidently, clearly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**очевидный** is built from *очи*, the old plural of *око* "eye", and *вид-*, the root of *видеть* "to see", from Proto-Slavic *\*viděti* (EtymDB). What is obvious is seen with your own eyes.
