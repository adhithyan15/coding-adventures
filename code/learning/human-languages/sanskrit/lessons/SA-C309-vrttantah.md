## You'll want to know: वृत्तान्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C309-VRITTANTA309-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वृत्तान्तः** — *vṛttāntaḥ* — "an account; a report".

> पत्रिकायां जलौघस्य वृत्तान्तः अस्ति।
>
> *patrikāyāṃ jalaughasya vṛttāntaḥ asti.*
>
> There is an account of the flood in the newspaper.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वृत्तान्तः** (*vṛttāntaḥ*) is built from *vṛtta*, "what has happened", and *anta*, "an end"; MW gives *vṛttānta* as an occurrence or event and also as tidings, a report, a tale (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
