## You'll want to know: o afeto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C496-CARINHO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o afeto** — "affection".

> Os animais também precisam de afeto.
>
> *Animals need affection too.*

Before AO90 spelled *afecto* in Portugal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*afeto* comes from Latin *affectus* "a state of mind, a feeling" (EtymDB), from *afficere* "to act on".
