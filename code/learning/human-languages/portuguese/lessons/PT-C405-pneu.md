## You'll want to know: o pneu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C405-PNEU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o pneu** — "the tyre".

> O pneu da frente está vazio.
>
> *The front tyre is flat.*

*Um pneu furado* — a flat tyre, a puncture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pneu* is short for *pneumático*, a learned borrowing from Latin *pneumaticus*, from Greek *pneumatikós* 'of wind or air' (EtymDB): a tyre is full of air.
