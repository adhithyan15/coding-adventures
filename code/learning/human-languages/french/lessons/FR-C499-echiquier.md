## You'll want to know: l'échiquier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C499-JEUX-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'échiquier** — "chessboard".

> Ils ont posé l'échiquier sur une petite table.
>
> *They put the chessboard on a little table.*

Masculine. Chess is *les échecs*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'échiquier* comes from Old French *eschequier*, built on *eschec* "check", which goes back through Arabic to Persian *šāh* "king".
