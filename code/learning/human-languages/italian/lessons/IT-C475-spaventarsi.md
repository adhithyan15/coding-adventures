## You'll want to know: spaventarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C475-SPAVENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**spaventarsi** — "to get frightened".

> Il cane si spaventa quando sente i tuoni.
>
> *The dog gets frightened when it hears thunder.*

Without *si*, *spaventare* is to frighten someone: *mi hai spaventato!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spaventarsi* is the reflexive of *spaventare* "to frighten", usually traced to Vulgar Latin *expaventāre*, from Latin *pavēre* "to be afraid" (Lewis & Short).
