## You'll want to know: દંભ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C468-JUTHU468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દંભ** — *dambh* — "hypocrisy; pretence".

> તેનું સ્મિત ફક્ત દંભ હતું.
>
> *tenũ smit phakt dambh hatũ.*
>
> His smile was nothing but pretence.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દંભ** (*dambh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dambha*, "deceit, feigning, hypocrisy", as Monier-Williams records it.
