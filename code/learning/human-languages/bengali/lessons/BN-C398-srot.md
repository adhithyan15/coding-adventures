## You'll want to know: স্রোত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C398-SROT398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**স্রোত** — *srot* — "a current; a stream".

> নদীর স্রোতে নৌকাটা দ্রুত চলল।
>
> *nôdīr srote noukāṭā drutô chôllô.*
>
> The boat moved fast on the river's current.

Also a flow of people or things: মানুষের স্রোত, a stream of people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্রোত** (*srot*) is Sanskrit *srotas*, "the current or bed of a river, a stream" (Monier-Williams), from *sru*, to flow.
