## You'll want to know: うらやましい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C433-LONG433-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うらやましい** — *urayamashii* — "envious".

> **あねの くるまが うらやましいです。** — *ane no kuruma ga urayamashii desu* — "I envy my elder sister her car."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うらやましい** (*urayamashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
