## You'll want to know: くみたてる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C468-WORKS468-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くみたてる** — *kumitateru* — "to assemble; to put together".

> **ちちは たなを くみたてています。** — *chichi wa tana o kumitatete imasu* — "My father is putting a shelf together."

Polite **くみたてます**, *kumitatemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **だいくが いすを くみたてました。**, *daiku ga isu o kumitatemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くみたてる** (*kumitateru*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *kumi*, "putting together (kumu)" and *tateru*, "to set up" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
