## You'll want to know: pēnsum, pēnsī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C405-CALCULUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**pēnsum, pēnsī** — "a task, a day's work".

> Ancillae pēnsum suum perfēcērunt.
>
> *The maids finished their day's work.*

*pēnsum, pēnsī* n., the participle of *pendō*, to weigh: the wool weighed out to a spinner each day, then any set task.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pēnsum* is the participle of *pendō* "to weigh out" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the wool weighed out for a day's spinning.
