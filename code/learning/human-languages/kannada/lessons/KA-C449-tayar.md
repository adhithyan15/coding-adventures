## You'll want to know: ಟಯರ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C449-CAKRA449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟಯರ್** — *ṭayar* — "a tyre".

> ಕಾರಿನ ಟಯರ್ ಹಳೆಯದಾಗಿತ್ತು.
>
> *kārina ṭayar haḷeyadāgittu.*
>
> The car tyre was old.

The rubber round the ಚಕ್ರ.

## The word, taken apart
