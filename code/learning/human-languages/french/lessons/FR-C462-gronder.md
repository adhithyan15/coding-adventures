## You'll want to know: gronder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C462-ORAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gronder** — "to rumble; to scold".

> Le tonnerre gronde au loin.
>
> *Thunder is rumbling in the distance.*

*Gronder un enfant* — to tell a child off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gronder* is inherited from Latin *grundīre* 'to grunt' (EtymDB).
