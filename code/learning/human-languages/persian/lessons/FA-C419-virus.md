## You'll want to know: ویروس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C419-HARIM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ویروس** (*virus*) — "a virus".

> کامپیوترم ویروس گرفته است.
>
> *kâmpyuteram virus gerefte ast.*
>
> My computer has caught a virus.

For computers and for illness alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ویروس** came into Persian from European languages, usually traced through French *virus* to Latin *vīrus* "slime, poison" (Lewis & Short).
