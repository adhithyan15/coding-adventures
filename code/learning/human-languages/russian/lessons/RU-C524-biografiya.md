## You'll want to know: биография
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C524-GENRES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**биография** (*biográfiya*) — "a biography".

> Я прочитал биографию известного учёного.
>
> *Ya prochitál biográfiyu izvéstnogo uchyónogo.*
>
> I've read the biography of a famous scientist.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*биография* is a learned word built from Greek *bíos* 'life' and *graphḗ* 'writing'; EtymDB lists the Russian word without a source.
