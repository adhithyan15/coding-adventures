## You'll want to know: घोषणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C313-AFVA313-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घोषणा** — *ghoṣṇā* — "an announcement; a slogan".

> स्टेशनवर गाडी उशिरा येण्याची घोषणा झाली.
>
> *sṭeśanvar gāḍī uśirā yeṇyācī ghoṣṇā jhālī.*
>
> At the station it was announced that the train would be late.

Feminine.

घोषणा करणे, to announce.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोषणा** (*ghoṣṇā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ghoṣaṇā*, 'a proclaiming aloud' (Monier-Williams).
