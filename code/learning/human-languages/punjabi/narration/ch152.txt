Punjabi, chapter 152: To run, To wander, To stop, To believe, To change.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਭੱਜਣਾ (bhajjṇā) — to run.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to save, then the Punjabi for to sow.

You'll want to know: ਭੱਜਣਾ.
ਭੱਜਣਾ — bhajjṇā — "to run".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bhajjṇā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bhajjṇā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bhajjṇā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for to save, then the Punjabi for to sow, then say bhajjṇā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਭੱਜਣਾ (bhajjṇā) mean? ("To run".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to believe and to change.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: soft, hard, difficult, clever, lazy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: brave, calm, hungry, thirsty, fresh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: stale, ripe, raw, true, false]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: rich, poor, young, strong, weak]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Soft? (ਨਰਮ, naram.) Hard? (ਸਖ਼ਤ, sakht.) Difficult? (ਮੁਸ਼ਕਲ, mushkal.) Clever? (ਹੁਸ਼ਿਆਰ, hushiār.) Lazy? (ਆਲਸੀ, ālsī.) Brave? (ਬਹਾਦਰ, bahādar.) Calm? (ਸ਼ਾਂਤ, shā̃t.) Hungry? (ਭੁੱਖਾ, bhukkhā.) Thirsty? (ਪਿਆਸਾ, piāsā.) Fresh? (ਤਾਜ਼ਾ, tāzā.) Stale? (ਬੇਹਾ, behā.) Ripe? (ਪੱਕਾ, pakkā.) Raw? (ਕੱਚਾ, kaccā.) True? (ਸੱਚਾ, saccā.) False? (ਝੂਠਾ, jhūṭhā.) Rich? (ਅਮੀਰ, amīr.) Poor? (ਗ਼ਰੀਬ, garīb.) Young? (ਜਵਾਨ, javān.) Strong? (ਤਕੜਾ, takṛā.) Weak? (ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰ, kamzor.)

(ਬੁਲਾਉਣਾ, bulāuṇā.) To use? (ਵਰਤਣਾ, vartṇā.) To make? (ਬਣਾਉਣਾ, baṇāuṇā.) To sew? (ਸਿਉਣਾ, siuṇā.) To fill? (ਭਰਨਾ, bharnā.) To pull? (ਖਿੱਚਣਾ, khiccṇā.) To wake up? (ਜਾਗਣਾ, jāgṇā.) To drive? (ਚਲਾਉਣਾ, calāuṇā.) To say? (ਕਹਿਣਾ, kahiṇā.) To live? (ਜੀਉਣਾ, jīuṇā.) To spend? (ਖ਼ਰਚਣਾ, kharcṇā.) To save? (ਬਚਾਉਣਾ, bacāuṇā.) To sow? (ਬੀਜਣਾ, bījṇā.) To run? (ਭੱਜਣਾ, bhajjṇā.) To wander?
