Arabic, chapter 304: Long ago.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
زمن (zaman) — an age, a span of time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a villain, then the Arabic for to imagine.

You'll want to know: زمن.
زمن — zaman — "an age, a span of time".
كان ذلك في زمن جدي. kāna dhālika fī zaman jaddī. That was in my grandfather’s time.
Plural: أزمنة, azmina.
وقت is a time for something; زمن (zaman) is time as a stretch, an age.

The word, taken apart.
زمن (zaman) is built on the native Arabic root z-m-n (Lane), where zaman and zamān name time, a short span or a long one.

Lesson 2 of 5.
منذ زمن طويل (mundhu zaman ṭawīl) — long ago; for a long time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to imagine, then the Arabic for an age.

You'll want to know: منذ زمن طويل.
منذ زمن طويل — mundhu zaman ṭawīl — "long ago; for a long time".
منذ زمن طويل كانت هنا غابة كبيرة. mundhu zaman ṭawīl kānat hunā ghāba kabīra. Long ago there was a big forest here.
It opens a story set in the past, or says how long a thing has lasted.

The word, taken apart.
منذ زمن طويل (mundhu zaman ṭawīl) is "since a long time": the taught mundhu, "since", with zaman, built on the native Arabic root z-m-n (Lane), and ṭawīl, long.

كانت تلك حقبة صعبة في تاريخ البلد. kānat tilka ḥiqba ṣaʿba fī tārīkh al-balad. That was a hard era in the country’s history.

حقبة (ḥiqba) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-q-b (Lane), where ḥiqba and ḥuqb name a long span of years, and ḥaqība, a bag tied behind the saddle, is the same root.

The word, taken apart.
آنذاك (ānadhāk) is usually traced to ān, "a time", the same word as in the taught الآن, now, joined to dhāka, "that": "at that time".

Lesson 5 of 5.
عهد (ʿahd) — a reign, an era; a pledge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an era, then the Arabic for at that time.

You'll want to know: عهد.
عهد — ʿahd — "a reign, an era; a pledge".
بني الجسر في عهد الملك الأول. buniya al-jisr fī ʿahd al-malik al-awwal. The bridge was built in the reign of the first king.
Plural: عهود, ʿuhūd.
في عهد (ʿahd): in the days of, under. Also a promise: وفى بالعهد, he kept the pledge.

The word, taken apart.
عهد (ʿahd) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-h-d (Lane), "to enjoin, to bind by a compact". A ruler's pledge marks his time, so ʿahd is also a reign.
