## You'll want to know: गिर्यारोहण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C534-BAHER534-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गिर्यारोहण** — *giryārohaṇ* — "mountaineering".

> माझ्या भावाला गिर्यारोहणाची आवड आहे.
>
> *mājhyā bhāvālā giryārohaṇācī āvaḍ āhe.*
>
> My brother loves mountaineering.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गिर्यारोहण** (*giryārohaṇ*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *giri*, "mountain", and *ārohaṇa*, "climbing" (Monier-Williams).
