## You'll want to know: عہدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C343-MAHKAMA343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عہدہ** — *uhdā* — "a post; a rank (in an office)".

> وہ ایک بڑے عہدے پر ہے۔
>
> *voh ek baṛe uhde par hai.*
>
> She holds a senior post.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عہدہ** (*uhdā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿuhda*, "a charge, a responsibility": the duty a post carries.
