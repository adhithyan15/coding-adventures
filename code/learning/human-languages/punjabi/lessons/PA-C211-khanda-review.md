# Hear and say eats and drinks again

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211] -->

[YOU HEAR: *khāndā; pīndā; jāndā*] Say **ਖਾਂਦਾ · ਪੀਂਦਾ · ਜਾਂਦਾ**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-LEX-CHA, PA-LEX-DUDH] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I drink tea every day, as a man*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *she eats roti every day*] Say **ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਖਾਂਦੀ**] Say *khāndī*.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the model and write **ਪੀਂਦੀ** once]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say what you drink every day, with the ending that fits you.
