## You'll want to know: la adolescencia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C545-NINEZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la adolescencia** — "adolescence, the teenage years".

> Pasé la adolescencia en Barcelona.
>
> *I spent my teenage years in Barcelona.*

*En la adolescencia* — in your teens.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adolescencia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adolēscentia* 'youth' (EtymDB), built on *adolēscere* 'to grow up'.
