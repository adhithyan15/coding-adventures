Lesson 1 of 5.
o ambiente — the environment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a vase, a jug, then the Portuguese for a plant.

You'll want to know: o ambiente.
o ambiente — "the environment".

The word, taken apart.
ambiente from Latin ambiens, ambientis, "going around" (EtymDB 2.1), from ambire, "to go around" — a learned borrowing from Latin.
