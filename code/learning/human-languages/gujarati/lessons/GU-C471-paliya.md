## You'll want to know: પળિયાં
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C471-VAN471-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પળિયાં** — *paḷiyā̃* — "grey hairs".

> પિતાના વાળમાં હવે પળિયાં આવ્યાં છે.
>
> *pitānā vāḷmā̃ have paḷiyā̃ āvyā̃ che.*
>
> Father's hair has started to turn grey.

A neuter noun, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પળિયાં** (*paḷiyā̃*) is inherited from Sanskrit *palita*, "grey, hoary, old"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "grey hair, old age" (CDIAL 7962).
