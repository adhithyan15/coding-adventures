Persian, chapter 361: Agreeing and disagreeing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
موافقت کردن (movâfeqat kardan) — to agree, to consent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to believe, then the Persian for a theory.

You'll want to know: موافقت کردن.
موافقت کردن (movâfeqat kardan) — "to agree, to consent".
مدیر با پیشنهاد ما موافقت کرد. modir bâ pishnahâd-e mâ movâfeqat kard. The manager agreed to our proposal.
Takes bâ: bâ chizi movâfeqat kardan. Movâfeqat "agreement" + kardan "to do" (present stem kon-). Compare movâfeq budan, to agree in opinion.

The word, taken apart.
موافقت is usually traced to Arabic muwāfaqa "agreement, consent", from the same Arabic family as movâfeq "agreeing". Kardan "to do" is the inherited Persian verb.

Lesson 2 of 5.
مخالفت کردن (mokhâlefat kardan) — to oppose, to object to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a theory, then the Persian for to agree.

You'll want to know: مخالفت کردن.
مخالفت کردن (mokhâlefat kardan) — "to oppose, to object to".
پدرم با سفر من مخالفت کرد. pedaram bâ safar-e man mokhâlefat kard. My father opposed my trip.
Takes bâ: bâ chizi mokhâlefat kardan. Mokhâlefat "opposition" + kardan (stem kon-).

The word, taken apart.
مخالفت is derived from mokhâlef "opposed, contrary" (EtymDB), a word of Arabic origin, usually traced to Arabic mukhālafa "opposition". Kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
مخالف (mokhâlef) — opposed, against; an opponent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to agree, then the Persian for to oppose.

You'll want to know: مخالف.
مخالف (mokhâlef) — "opposed, against; an opponent".
بیشتر مردم با این طرح مخالف هستند. bishtar-e mardom bâ in tarh mokhâlef hastand. Most people are against this plan.
Bâ chizi mokhâlef budan — to be against something. The opposite is movâfeq. As a noun, an opponent: plural mokhâlefân.

The word, taken apart.
مخالف (mokhâlef) "contrary, opposed" is in EtymDB; it is usually traced to Arabic mukhālif "opposing, going against".

Lesson 4 of 5.
باور داشتن (bâvar dâshtan) — to believe in, to have faith in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to oppose, then the Persian for opposed.

You'll want to know: باور داشتن.
باور داشتن (bâvar dâshtan) — "to believe in, to have faith in".
او به خودش باور دارد. u be khodash bâvar dârad. She believes in herself.
Bâvar "belief" + dâshtan "to have" (present stem dâr-). Takes be: be chizi bâvar dâshtan. Compare bâvar kardan, to believe a statement.

The word, taken apart.
باور داشتن (bâvar dâshtan) is native Persian, built from bâvar "belief", usually traced to Middle Persian wābar, and dâshtan "to have", the inherited verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
شک داشتن (shak dâshtan) — to doubt, to be unsure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for opposed, then the Persian for to believe in.

You'll want to know: شک داشتن.
شک داشتن (shak dâshtan) — "to doubt, to be unsure".
من در درستی این خبر شک دارم. man dar dorosti-ye in khabar shak dâram. I doubt that this news is true.
Shak "doubt" + dâshtan (stem dâr-). Takes dar or be: dar chizi shak dâshtan.

The word, taken apart.
شک was borrowed from Arabic shakk "doubt" (EtymDB); dâshtan "to have" is the inherited Persian verb, so the phrase is literally to have a doubt.
