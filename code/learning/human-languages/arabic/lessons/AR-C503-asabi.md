## You'll want to know: عصبي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C503-TABA503-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عصبي** — *ʿaṣabī* — "irritable, edgy".

> يصبح المدير عصبيا قبل الاجتماع.
>
> *yuṣbiḥu al-mudīr ʿaṣabiyyan qabla al-ijtimāʿ.*
>
> The manager gets edgy before the meeting.

It is built on ʿaṣab, a nerve: a nervy person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عصبي** (*ʿaṣabī*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṣ-b*, "to bind, to tie tight" (Lane). ʿAṣabī is the adjective of ʿaṣab, a nerve.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
