## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-JOIN-TETHI-01]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-KEMKE-01] -->

**કેમકે** puts the result first and the reason second. **તેથી** does the
opposite — reason first, then the conclusion drawn from it:

> *(reason)*. **તેથી** *(result)*. — "…, **therefore** …"

The word says so itself. **તે** is *that*, and the **-થી** ending means *from* —
so **તેથી** is literally **from that**, which is what a conclusion is. You have
seen that **-થી** before, on the end of **નથી**, where it was the worn-down *is*;
here it is the separate postposition meaning *from*. Same two letters, two
different histories, and it is worth knowing they are not the same thing.
