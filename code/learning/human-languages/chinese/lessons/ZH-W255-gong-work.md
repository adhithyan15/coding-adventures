[YOU READ: each one aloud, with nothing said first — *zài*, *dǎ*]

## Script — two bars and a post
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-GONG-WORK-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 工

**One piece, three strokes, top to bottom.**

**A short bar** across the top.

**A vertical** down from the middle of the bar. It starts at the bar and does not cross it.

**A long bar** across the foot, longer than the top one, closing the vertical.

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] How many strokes in 工? (**Three**.) Which bar is longer? (**The one at the foot**.) Does the vertical cross the top bar? (**No, it starts there**.)
