Marathi, chapter 483: An Upset Stomach.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अपचन (apcan) — indigestion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to groan, then the Marathi for to moan in pain.

You'll want to know: अपचन.
अपचन — apcan — "indigestion".
तेलकट खाल्ल्याने मला अपचन झालं. telkaṭ khāllyāne malā apcan jhālaṁ. I got indigestion from eating oily food.
Neuter.
अ-, not, + पचन, digestion. अपचन (apcan) होणे, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
अपचन (apcan) is built from Sanskrit a-, "not", and pacana, "cooking, maturing" (Monier-Williams): a learned word for food not digested.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ओकणे (okṇe) — to vomit; to throw up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for acidity, then the Marathi for diarrhoea.

You'll want to know: ओकणे.
ओकणे — okṇe — "to vomit; to throw up".
बसमध्ये बाळाने दूध ओकलं. basmadhye bāḷāne dūdh oklaṁ. The baby threw up its milk on the bus.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ओकलं, tyāne oklaṁ.
Blunter than उलटी होणे; what comes up is the object.

The word, taken apart.
ओकणे (okṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi okṇẽ, "vomit", under the expressive ōkk (CDIAL 2538).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पचणे (pacṇe) — to be digested.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for diarrhoea, then the Marathi for to vomit.

You'll want to know: पचणे.
पचणे — pacṇe — "to be digested".
रात्रीचं जड जेवण लवकर पचत नाही. rātrīcaṁ jaḍ jevaṇ lavkar pacat nāhī. A heavy dinner does not digest quickly.
Past (to him …): त्याला पचलं, tyālā paclaṁ.
The eater takes the dative: मला पचलं. पचवणे, to digest.

The word, taken apart.
पचणे (pacṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit pacyate; CDIAL lists Marathi pacṇẽ, "to be digested" (CDIAL 7654).
