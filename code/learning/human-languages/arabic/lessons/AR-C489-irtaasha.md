# ارتعش (irtaʿasha) — to shiver

## You'll want to know: ارتعش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C489-KHAWF489-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتعش** — *irtaʿasha* — "to shiver".

> ارتعش الولد من الخوف.
>
> *irtaʿasha al-walad min al-khawf.*
>
> The boy shivered with fear.

Present: يرتعش, *yartaʿish*.

It is said of cold as well as fear: ارتعش من البرد.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتعش** (*irtaʿasha*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ʿ-sh*, "to tremble, to quiver" (Lane). Form VIII irtaʿasha is to shake all over.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
