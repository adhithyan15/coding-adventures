## You'll want to know: ぶつだん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C408-SENKOU408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶつだん** — *butsudan* — "a household altar (for the Buddha)".

> **そぼの うちには ぶつだんが あります。** — *sobo no uchi ni wa butsudan ga arimasu* — "There is a family altar in my grandmother's house."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶつだん** (*butsudan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *butsu*, "Buddha" (Middle Chinese *bjut*) and *dan*, "podium" (*dan*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
