# ಸಾರಿಗೆ (sārige) — transport; public transport

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C451-VAHANA451-04, KA-LEX-C451-VAHANA451-05] -->

## You'll want to know: ಸಾರಿಗೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C452-SARIGE452-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾರಿಗೆ** — *sārige* — "transport; public transport".

> ಈ ಊರಿಗೆ ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ಸಾರಿಗೆ ಬಸ್ ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ಎರಡು ಸಲ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *ī ūrige sarkāri sārige bas dinakke eraḍu sala baruttade.*
>
> A government transport bus comes to this town twice a day.

ಸಾರಿಗೆ ಇಲಾಖೆ, the transport department.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾರಿಗೆ** (*sārige*) is a Kannada word for transport whose origin is not settled.
