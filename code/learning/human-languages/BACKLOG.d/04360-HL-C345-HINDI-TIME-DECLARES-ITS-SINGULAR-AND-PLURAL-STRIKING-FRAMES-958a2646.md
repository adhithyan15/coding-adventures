## HL-C345 — Hindi time declares its singular and plural striking frames

After the longing-noun repair, **रहे** is the highest-ranked modern Hindi form
in the glossed-but-never-taught queue, with **रहा** close behind. Chapter 18
already explains, models, practises, and contrasts **बज रहा है** for one o'clock
with **बज रहे हैं** for plural hours, but its headword declares only **घंटा /
बजना**. Chapter 7 also uses **रहे** inside an unexplained coming question long
before this time lesson.

Declare both complete striking frames in Chapter 18 and replace Chapter 7's
premature progressive example with a question built from already-known words.
This makes the taught agreement visible to reports and downstream cards while
removing the earlier forward reference and preserving the gentle-ramp baseline.
