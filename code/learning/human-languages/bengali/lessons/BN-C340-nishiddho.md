## You'll want to know: নিষিদ্ধ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C340-BOIDHO340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**নিষিদ্ধ** — *nishiddhô* — "forbidden; banned".

> এখানে পার্কিং নিষিদ্ধ।
>
> *ekhāne pārking nishiddhô.*
>
> Parking is forbidden here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নিষিদ্ধ** (*nishiddhô*) is Sanskrit *niṣiddha*, kept back or forbidden (Monier-Williams). It is the word on notices: ধূমপান নিষিদ্ধ, no smoking.
