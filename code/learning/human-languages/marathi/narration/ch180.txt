Lesson 1 of 5.
लेखक / लेखिका (lekhak / lekhikā) — a writer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an election, then the Marathi for politics.

You'll want to know: लेखक / लेखिका.
लेखक / लेखिका — lekhak / lekhikā — "a writer".

The word, taken apart.
लेखक (lekhak) is Sanskrit lekhaka, "scribe", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 11102). It is built on likh, "to write".

चित्रकार (citrakār) is Sanskrit citrakāra, "painter", a tatsama: citra, "picture", and kāra, "maker" (CDIAL 4807). CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi forms as citārī, "painter".
