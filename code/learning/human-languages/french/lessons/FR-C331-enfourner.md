## You'll want to know: enfourner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C331-FOUR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**enfourner** — "to put in the oven".

> Il enfourne le gâteau pour quarante minutes.
>
> *He puts the cake in the oven for forty minutes.*

Informal: to gobble up food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enfourner* is built on *en-* and *four* "oven", which is inherited from Latin *furnus* "an oven" (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
