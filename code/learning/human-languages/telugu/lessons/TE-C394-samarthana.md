## You'll want to know: సమర్థన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C394-VIVARANA394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సమర్థన** — *samarthana* — "a justification; a defence".

> తన తప్పుకు అతని దగ్గర ఏ సమర్థన లేదు.
>
> *tana tappuku atani daggara ē samarthana lēdu.*
>
> He has no justification for his mistake.

The reasons that back an act or a view.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సమర్థన** (*samarthana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samarthana*, "reflection, deliberation" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for the case made for an act or a view.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
