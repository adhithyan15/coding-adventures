# वह गया (vah gayā) — the past, by who did it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C275-THG275-04, HI-LEX-C275-THG275-05, HI-LEX-C123-HAAL-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say turning over and a stretch. (*karvaṭ*, *aṅgṛāī*.) Then the host's question **खाना खाया?**, "have you eaten?" Its **खाया** is a past, and this chapter builds every past like it.

## You'll want to know: the past, by who did it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C03-MAIN-01, HI-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-02, HI-LEX-C86-PRON-01, HI-LEX-C86-PRON-02, HI-LEX-C69-GOING-05, HI-LEX-C56-ROAD-03, HI-CONCEPT-C04-KALMILTEHAIN-01, HI-LEX-C41-ADJ-03] -->

> **कल मैं बाज़ार गया।**
>
> *kal maiṁ bāzār gayā.* — "Yesterday I went to the market."

With a past, **कल** is yesterday. **गया** is **जाना** in the past, with no **है** after it. Its ending follows the one who went, as **अच्छा**, **अच्छी**, **अच्छे** follow a noun:

| who went | the past |
|---|---|
| a man: **मैं**, **वह** | **गया** (*gayā*) |
| a woman | **गई** (*gaī*) |
| men, or **आप**, **तुम**, **हम** | **गए** (*gae*) |
| women | **गईं** (*gaīṁ*) |

## You'll want to know: how a stem takes the ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-STEMS-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-SIT, HI-LEX-C164-ACT164-04, HI-LEX-COME, HI-LEX-C159-QUAL159-02, HI-LEX-STAND, HI-LEX-C122-LATE-02] -->

Most verbs put the ending on the stem: **बैठना** → **वह बैठा**, **वह बैठी** (*vah baiṭhā*, *vah baiṭhī*). A stem ending in a vowel adds **-या** for a man: **आना** → **आया**, **आई** (*āyā*, *āī*). Two change the stem: **जाना** → **गया**, and **होना** → **हुआ**, **हुई** (*huā*, *huī*): **क्या हुआ?**, "what happened?"

"Did not": **मैं नहीं गया** (*maiṁ nahīṁ gayā*). A bare stem with **गया** says the change is complete, as in **देर हो गई** and **वह सो गया** (*vah so gayā*), "he fell asleep".
