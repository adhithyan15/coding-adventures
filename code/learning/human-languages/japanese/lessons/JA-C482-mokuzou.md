## You'll want to know: もくぞう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C482-NAIL482-04]; assesses=[] -->

**もくぞう** — *mokuzō* — "made of wood; wooden".

> **この うちは もくぞうです。** — *kono uchi wa mokuzō desu* — "This house is made of wood."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もくぞう** (*mokuzō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *moku*, "tree" (Middle Chinese *muwk*) and *zō*, "create" (*dzawX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
