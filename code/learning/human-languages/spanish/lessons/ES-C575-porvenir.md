## You'll want to know: el porvenir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C575-PASADO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el porvenir** — "the future (what lies ahead)".

> Nadie sabe qué nos traerá el porvenir.
>
> *Nobody knows what the future will bring us.*

A little literary; it often means someone's prospects: *un joven con porvenir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*porvenir* is built on *por* and *venir* 'to come', which is inherited from Latin *venīre* 'to come' (EtymDB). The future is what is still to come.
