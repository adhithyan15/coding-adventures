## You'll want to know: os danos
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C300-CAPOTAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**os danos** — "the damage".

> Os danos no carro foram grandes.
>
> *The damage to the car was serious.*

Usually plural. *Danos materiais* — damage to property.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dano* is inherited from Latin *damnum* "loss, damage" (EtymDB).
