## You'll want to know: సంతానం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C531-SANTANAM531-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సంతానం** — *santānaṁ* — "offspring; children".

> ఆ దంపతులకు ముగ్గురు సంతానం.
>
> *ā dampatulaku mugguru santānaṁ.*
>
> That couple has three children.

All of one's children taken together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంతానం** (*santānaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁtāna*, "continued succession, an unbroken line" (Monier-Williams): the line a family continues in.
