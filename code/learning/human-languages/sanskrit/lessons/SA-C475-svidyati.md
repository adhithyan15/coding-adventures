## You'll want to know: स्विद्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C475-SVEDA475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्विद्यति** — *svidyati* — "sweats".

> ग्रीष्मे कृषकः बहु स्विद्यति।
>
> *grīṣme kṛṣakaḥ bahu svidyati.*
>
> In summer the farmer sweats a lot.

Past (he …): सः बहु स्विन्नवान्, *saḥ bahu svinnavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्विद्यति** (*svidyati*) comes from the root √svid, "to sweat, to perspire"; MW's ppp *svinna* is "sweating" (MW).
