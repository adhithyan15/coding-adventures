## You'll want to know: il campeggio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C429-DORMIRE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il campeggio** — "the campsite; camping".

> Il campeggio ha una piscina.
>
> *The campsite has a pool.*

*Andare in campeggio* — to go camping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*campeggio* is built on *campo* 'field', which is inherited from Latin *campus* (EtymDB), shaped on the model of English *camping*.
