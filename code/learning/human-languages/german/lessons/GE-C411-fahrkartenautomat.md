## You'll want to know: der Fahrkartenautomat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C411-FAHRSCHEIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Fahrkartenautomat** — "the ticket machine".

> Der Fahrkartenautomat nimmt auch Kreditkarten.
>
> *The ticket machine also takes credit cards.*

Plural: *die Fahrkartenautomaten*; weak noun: *den Fahrkartenautomaten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fahrkartenautomat* is built from *Fahrkarte* 'ticket', from *fahren* and *Karte* 'card' (from Latin *charta*, Lewis & Short), and *Automat* 'machine', which goes back to Greek *autómatos* 'acting by itself' (EtymDB).
