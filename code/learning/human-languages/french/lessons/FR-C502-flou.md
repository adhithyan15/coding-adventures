## You'll want to know: flou
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C502-PHOTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**flou** — "blurred, out of focus; vague".

> Toutes mes photos de la fête sont floues.
>
> *All my photos from the party are blurred.*

Feminine *floue*. Also vague: *des idées floues*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flou* goes back to Old French *flou* "faded, weak, soft" (EtymDB). Its earlier origin is not settled.
