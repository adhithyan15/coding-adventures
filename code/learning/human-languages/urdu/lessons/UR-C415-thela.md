## You'll want to know: ٹھیلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C415-DHABA415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھیلا** — *ṭhelā* — "a handcart".

> ٹھیلے پر ایک آدمی پھل بیچتا ہے۔
>
> *ṭhele par ek ādmī phal bechtā hai.*
>
> A man sells fruit from a handcart.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھیلا** (*ṭhelā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭhelā*, "a push-cart", under *ṭhēḍḍ*, "push", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5512).
