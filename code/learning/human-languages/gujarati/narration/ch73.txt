Gujarati, chapter 73: Light, New, Old, Hot, Cheap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હલકું (halkũ) — light (not heavy).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for short, then the Gujarati for heavy.

You'll want to know: હલકું.
હલકું (halkũ) — "light (not heavy)". The opposite of ભારે.

The word, taken apart.
હલકું (halkũ) goes back to Sanskrit laghu, "light", with its first sounds swapped (CDIAL 10896). CDIAL marks this form as taken from Hindi; Gujarati's own descendants include haḷvũ, "light, soft".

What you've built.
One more word for light, new, old, hot, cheap.

નવું (navũ) — "new".

The word, taken apart.
નવું (navũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nava, "new, young" (CDIAL 6983).
