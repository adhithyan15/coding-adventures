## You'll want to know: la prioridad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C617-DECIDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la prioridad** — "the priority".

> Mi familia es mi prioridad.
>
> *My family is my priority.*

*Dar prioridad a* — to put first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prioridad* is a learned word usually traced to Medieval Latin *prioritas*, built on Latin *prior* 'earlier, former' (Lewis & Short).
