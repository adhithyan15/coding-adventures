## You'll want to know: ವರದಿಗಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C320-VARADI320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವರದಿಗಾರ** — *varadigāra* — "a reporter".

> ಆ ವರದಿಗಾರ ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದಿದ್ದರೆ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಗೊತ್ತಾಗುತ್ತಿತ್ತು.
>
> *ā varadigāra alli iddiddare ellarigū suddi gottāguttittu.*
>
> If that reporter had been there, everyone would have known the news.

Plural: ವರದಿಗಾರರು, *varadigāraru*.

ವರದಿ is a report; -ಗಾರ makes "one who does it", as in ಕೆಲಸಗಾರ, a worker.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವರದಿಗಾರ** (*varadigāra*) is a Kannada word built from ವರದಿ, "a report", and the ending -*gāra*, "one who does", as in ಕೆಲಸಗಾರ. The origin of ವರದಿ itself is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
