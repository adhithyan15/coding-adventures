## You'll want to know: れんらく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C231-PHONE231-03]; assesses=[] -->

**れんらく** — *renraku* — "contact; getting in touch".

> **せんぱいは いつも でんわで れんらくを します。** — *senpai wa itsumo denwa de renraku o shimasu* — "My senior always gets in touch by phone."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**れんらく** (*renraku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ren*, "link" (Middle Chinese *ljen*) and *raku*, "entwine" (*lak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
