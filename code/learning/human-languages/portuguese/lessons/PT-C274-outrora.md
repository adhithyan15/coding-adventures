## You'll want to know: outrora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C274-OUTRORA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**outrora** — "formerly, in the old days".

> Outrora, esta aldeia tinha muitas crianças.
>
> *In the old days this village had many children.*

A literary word; *antigamente* is the everyday choice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*outrora* fuses *outra hora* "another hour, another time" (EtymDB lists *hora*), from Latin *alter* "other" and *hōra* "hour" (Lewis & Short).
