## You'll want to know: संदेह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C556-SANDEH556-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संदेह** — *sandeh* — "doubt (formal)".

> उसकी बात पर मुझे कोई संदेह नहीं है।
>
> *Uskī bāt par mujhe koī sandeh nahīṃ hai.*
>
> I have no doubt at all about what he says.

Masculine.

A formal word; everyday speech says शक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संदेह** (*sandeh*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *saṃdeha*, "doubt, uncertainty" (Monier-Williams).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
