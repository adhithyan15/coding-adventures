## You'll want to know: la protección
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C614-RIESGO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la protección** — "the protection".

> Usa protección solar.
>
> *Use sun protection.*

*Proteger* — to protect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protección* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *protectio* 'a covering over', built on *protegere*, from *tegere* 'to cover' (EtymDB).
