Kannada, chapter 409: Writing an Essay.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪ್ರಬಂಧ (prabandha) — an essay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a syllabus, then the Kannada for revision.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಬಂಧ.
ಪ್ರಬಂಧ — prabandha — "an essay".
ನಾನು ನಮ್ಮ ಊರಿನ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಬಂಧ ಬರೆದೆ. nānu namma ūrina bagge ondu prabandha barede. I wrote an essay about our town.
Also a thesis or a paper.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಬಂಧ (prabandha) is borrowed from Sanskrit prabandha, "a connection, a continuous composition" (CDIAL 8695).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸಂಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸು (saṅkṣēpisu) — to summarise; to abridge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for revision, then the Kannada for an essay.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸು.
ಸಂಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸು — saṅkṣēpisu — "to summarise; to abridge".
ನಾನು ಹತ್ತು ಪುಟದ ಲೇಖನವನ್ನು ಒಂದು ಪುಟಕ್ಕೆ ಸಂಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸಿದೆ. nānu hattu puṭada lēkhanavannu ondu puṭakke saṅkṣēpiside. I cut a ten-page article down to one page.
Past (I …): ಸಂಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸಿದೆ, saṅkṣēpiside.
ಸಂಕ್ಷಿಪ್ತ, brief; ಸಂಕ್ಷಿಪ್ತವಾಗಿ, in brief.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸು (saṅkṣēpisu) is built on Sanskrit saṃkṣepa, "compression, abridgment" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಗ್ರಂಥ (grantha) — a treatise; a book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an essay, then the Kannada for to summarise.

You'll want to know: ಗ್ರಂಥ.
ಗ್ರಂಥ — grantha — "a treatise; a book".
ಅಜ್ಜನ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಹಳೆಯ ಗ್ರಂಥಗಳು ಇವೆ. ajjana maneyalli tumbā haḷeya granthagaḷu ive. Grandfather's house has many old books.
Plural: ಗ್ರಂಥಗಳು, granthagaḷu.
A weighty or old book. ಗ್ರಂಥಾಲಯ, a library.

The word, taken apart.
ಗ್ರಂಥ (grantha) is borrowed from Sanskrit grantha, "a knot", and later "a book" (CDIAL 4350), from granth, "to tie".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಂಡಿಸು (maṇḍisu) — to present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to summarise, then the Kannada for a treatise.

You'll want to know: ಮಂಡಿಸು.
ಮಂಡಿಸು — maṇḍisu — "to present".
ನಾನು ಸಮ್ಮೇಳನದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಬಂಧ ಮಂಡಿಸಿದೆ. nānu sammēḷanadalli ondu prabandha maṇḍiside. I presented a paper at the conference.
Past (I …): ಮಂಡಿಸಿದೆ, maṇḍiside.
For a paper, a case or a bill. ಮಂಡನೆ, a presentation.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಂಡಿಸು (maṇḍisu) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled: neither DEDR nor CDIAL lists it.

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ.

ಉಪನ್ಯಾಸದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಂಡೆ. upanyāsada samayadalli nānu ṭippaṇi māḍikoṇḍe. I took notes during the lecture.
ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ (ṭippaṇi) ಮಾಡಿಕೊ, to take notes for oneself.

The word, taken apart.
ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ (ṭippaṇi) is borrowed from Sanskrit ṭippaṇī, "a gloss, a commentary" (Monier-Williams).
