## You'll want to know: o preso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C296-ALGEMAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o preso** — "the prisoner".

> O preso escreve cartas à mãe.
>
> *The prisoner writes letters to his mother.*

As an adjective: *estar preso* — to be in jail, or to be stuck.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*preso* comes from Latin *prehēnsus* "seized" (EtymDB), the past participle of *prehendere*.
