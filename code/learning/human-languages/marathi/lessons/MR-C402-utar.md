## You'll want to know: उतार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C402-GHAT402-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उतार** — *utār* — "a downhill slope".

> उतारावरून सायकल लवकर खाली येते.
>
> *utārāvarūn sāykal lavkar khālī yete.*
>
> On a downhill slope a bicycle comes down quickly.

Masculine.

The opposite is चढ, the way up; उतारावर, on the slope.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उतार** (*utār*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *utār*, "ford, ferry fare", under Sanskrit *uttāra*, "crossing over, landing" (CDIAL 1791); in Marathi today it is mostly a slope going down.
