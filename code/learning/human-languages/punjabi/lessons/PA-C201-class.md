## Grammar Lens: direct owner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-CLASS-201]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਕਲਾਸ** — *kalās* — school class; grade

Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole school noun
rather than inventing smaller Punjabi pieces. This lesson owns the word alone;
the complete identity frame comes only after a separate retrieval.

## The word, taken apart — a documented source
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-CLASS-201] -->

Punjabi University's Punjabipedia explicitly records English **Class** as the
source of modern written **ਕਲਾਸ**. It also lists several meanings; this lesson
keeps only the bounded school class-or-grade meaning.
The Punjabi school noun comes from English **Class**.
Source: [Punjabi University Punjabipedia](https://punjabipedia.org/topic.aspx?txt=%E0%A8%95%E0%A8%B2%E0%A8%BE%E0%A8%B8).

The Punjab School Education Board's current circular page uses the word in
school scheduling prose. That current school use supports the narrow meaning
here. The deeper history and other meanings stay outside this lesson.
Source: [Punjab School Education Board](https://pseb.ac.in/public/pressnotescirculars).
