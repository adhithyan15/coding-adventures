## You'll want to know: accélérer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C401-CONDUITE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**accélérer** — "to speed up, to accelerate".

> Sur l'autoroute, mon père accélère un peu trop.
>
> *On the motorway my father speeds up a little too much.*

*L'accélérateur* is the accelerator pedal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accélérer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *accelerāre* 'to hasten', built on *celer* 'swift' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
