## You'll want to know: خطوبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C306-URS306-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خطوبة** — *khuṭūba* — "an engagement to marry".

> أعلنت أختي خطوبتها أمس.
>
> *aʿlanat ukhtī khuṭūbatahā ams.*
>
> My sister announced her engagement yesterday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خطوبة** (*khuṭūba*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-b* (Lane), "to address; to ask in marriage". *Khuṭūba* is the time between the asking and the wedding.
