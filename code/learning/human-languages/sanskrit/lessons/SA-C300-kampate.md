## You'll want to know: कम्पते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C300-BHUKAMPA300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कम्पते** — *kampate* — "shakes; trembles".

> भूमिः कम्पते, जनाः पलायन्ते।
>
> *bhūmiḥ kampate, janāḥ palāyante.*
>
> The ground shakes and people run away.

Past (it …): भूमिः कम्पितवती, *bhūmiḥ kampitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कम्पते** (*kampate*) comes from the root √kamp, "to tremble, shake"; MW compares Greek *kamptō* (MW).
