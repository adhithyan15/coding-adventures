Telugu, chapter 294: Advice, Moreover, By the way, Almost, All in all.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సలహా (salahā) — advice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the village council, then the Telugu for a loft.

You'll want to know: సలహా.
సలహా — salahā — "advice".

The word, taken apart.
సలహా (salahā) is a loan from Arabic ṣalāḥ, "counsel, good advice", which came through Persian and Hindustani.

Lesson 2 of 5.
పైగా (paigā) — moreover, besides.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a loft, then the Telugu for advice.

You'll want to know: పైగా.
పైగా — paigā — "moreover, besides".

The word, taken apart.
పైగా (paigā) is native Dravidian: pai, "upon, above, extra", one word with Kannada pai (DEDR 4451), with the adverb ending -gā: "over and above".
