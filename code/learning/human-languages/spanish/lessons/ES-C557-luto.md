## You'll want to know: el luto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C557-LUTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el luto** — "mourning".

> Toda la familia iba de luto.
>
> *The whole family was in mourning.*

*Ir de luto* — to wear black for mourning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*luto* is inherited from Latin *lūctus* 'grief, mourning' (EtymDB), built on *lūgēre* 'to mourn'.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
