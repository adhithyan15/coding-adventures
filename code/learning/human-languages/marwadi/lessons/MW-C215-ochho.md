## You'll want to know: ओछो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C215-WRAP215-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ओछो** — *ochho* — "too small; short".

> म्हारी टोपी ओछी है।
>
> *mhārī ṭopī ochhī hai.*
>
> My cap is too small.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओछो** (*ochho*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ōccha*, "small, thin, mean". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *ochā*, "empty, small", and Gujarati *ochũ*, "deficient" (CDIAL 2540).
