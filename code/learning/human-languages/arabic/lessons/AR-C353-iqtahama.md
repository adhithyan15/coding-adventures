## You'll want to know: اقتحم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C353-MUZAHARA353-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتحم** — *iqtaḥama* — "to break into, to storm".

> اقتحمت الشرطة البيت في الصباح.
>
> *iqtaḥamat ash-shurṭa al-bayt fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> The police stormed the house in the morning.

Present: يقتحم, *yaqtaḥim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتحم** (*iqtaḥama*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ḥ-m* (Lane), "to rush into a thing without thought". It is Form VIII, "to force one's way in".
