German, chapter 130: Pink, Orange, Pretty, Round, Wide.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
rosa — pink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a concert, then the German for a celebration.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count eins, then say ein, the same word with its counting -s dropped. Then say sieben, with the s that buzzes.

You'll want to know: rosa.
rosa — "pink".

The word, taken apart.
rosa is Latin rosa, "rose": the colour of the flower — borrowed. EtymDB 2.1 has the German word but not its Latin source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
orange — orange (the colour).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: orange.
orange — "orange (the colour)".

The word, taken apart.
orange from French orange, the fruit (EtymDB 2.1), whose name came west from Persian nārang through Arabic — borrowed; the colour is named after the fruit.

Lesson 3 of 5.
hübsch — pretty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for pink, then the German for orange.

You'll want to know: hübsch.
hübsch — "pretty".

The word, taken apart.
hübsch from Middle High German hübesch, "courtly" (EtymDB 2.1), built on the word for "court", itself copying French courtois. Pretty was what suited the court.
