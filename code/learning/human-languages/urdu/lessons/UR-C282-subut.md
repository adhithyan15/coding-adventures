## You'll want to know: ثبوت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C282-TAFTISH282-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ثبوت** — *subūt* — "proof; evidence".

> پولیس کے پاس کوئی ثبوت نہیں تھا۔
>
> *polīs ke pās koī subūt nahīṅ thā.*
>
> The police had no proof.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ثبوت** (*subūt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *thubūt*, "firmness, a being established": what makes a claim stand. Urdu says its first letter as *s*.
