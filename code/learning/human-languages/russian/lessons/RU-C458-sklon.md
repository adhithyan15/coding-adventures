## You'll want to know: склон
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C458-DOLINA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**склон** (*sklon*) — "a slope".

> Зимой на этом склоне катаются на лыжах.
>
> *Zimóy na étom sklóne katáyutsya na lýzhakh.*
>
> In winter people ski on this slope.

Masculine. *На склоне лет* — in old age.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*склон* is built on *склонить* 'to bend, to incline', from *клонить* 'to lean'. EtymDB lists *склон* without a deeper source.
