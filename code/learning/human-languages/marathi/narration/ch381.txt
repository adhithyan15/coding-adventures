Marathi, chapter 381: As Long As.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
जोपर्यंत (joparyant) — as long as; until.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to impose, then the Marathi for a provision.

You'll want to know: जोपर्यंत.
जोपर्यंत — joparyant — "as long as; until".
जोपर्यंत तू येत नाहीस, मी जेवणार नाही. joparyant tū yet nāhīs, mī jevṇār nāhī. I won't eat until you come.
With a negative it means until: जोपर्यंत (joparyant) … नाही; a तो- clause often answers it.

The word, taken apart.
जोपर्यंत (joparyant) is built from जो, "which", and -पर्यंत, "up to", from Sanskrit paryanta, "limit, edge" (Monier-Williams): up to the point which.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

तोपर्यंत — toparyant — "until then; by then".
बस सात वाजता येईल, तोपर्यंत आपण चहा घेऊ. bas sāt vājtā yeīl, toparyant āpaṇ cahā gheū. The bus will come at seven; until then, let us have tea.
It answers जोपर्यंत (joparyant): जोपर्यंत (joparyant) … तोपर्यंत (toparyant) ….

The word, taken apart.
तोपर्यंत (toparyant) is built from तो, "that", and -पर्यंत, "up to", from Sanskrit paryanta, "limit, edge" (Monier-Williams): up to that point.

Lesson 3 of 6.
बशर्ते (baśarte) — provided that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for as long as, then the Marathi for until then.

You'll want to know: बशर्ते.
बशर्ते — baśarte — "provided that".
मी मदत करीन, बशर्ते तुम्ही वेळेवर याल. mī madat karīn, baśarte tumhī veḷevar yāl. I will help, provided that you come on time.
Formal; it stands before the condition.

The word, taken apart.
बशर्ते (baśarte) is a loan from Persian ba-sharṭ, "on condition", usually traced to Arabic sharṭ, "condition".

Lesson 4 of 6.
जेणेकरून (jeṇekarūn) — so that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for until then, then the Marathi for provided that.

You'll want to know: जेणेकरून.
जेणेकरून — jeṇekarūn — "so that".
मी लवकर निघालो, जेणेकरून गाडी चुकू नये. mī lavkar nighālo, jeṇekarūn gāḍī cukū naye. I left early so that I wouldn't miss the train.
Followed by a should-form or a future: जेणेकरून (jeṇekarūn) … होईल.

The word, taken apart.
जेणेकरून (jeṇekarūn) is built from जेणे, "by which", an old instrumental form of the relative जो, "which", and करून, "having done": done in such a way that. Sanskrit had the same pair, the relative ya- and its instrumental yena (CDIAL 10391).

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 6.
जणू (jaṇū) — as if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for provided that, then the Marathi for so that.

You'll want to know: जणू.
जणू — jaṇū — "as if".
तो असा वागला, जणू काही घडलंच नाही. to asā vāglā, jaṇū kāhī ghaḍlañc nāhī. He behaved as if nothing had happened.
जणू (jaṇū) काही, as though; often after असा: असा … जणू (jaṇū).

The word, taken apart.
जणू (jaṇū) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled.
