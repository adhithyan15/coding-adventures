# ನನಗೆ ಸಂತೋಷವಾಯಿತು (nanage santōṣavāyitu) — how you felt

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C315-KHUSI-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C06-DATIVE-SUBJECT-02, KA-LEX-C06-DATIVE-SUBJECT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was very glad to see you". (*nimmannu nōḍi nanage tumbā khuṣi āyitu*.) Then "I know Kannada". (*nanage kannaḍa gottu*.)

## You'll want to know: a feeling that comes to you
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C315-FEELING-DATIVE-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C02-SANTOSHA-01, KA-LEX-C83-FEELINGS-03, KA-LEX-C98-MOODS-01, KA-LEX-C83-FEELINGS-01, KA-LEX-C83-FEELINGS-04, KA-LEX-C284-KALA284-03, KA-LEX-C73-ABLE-01, KA-ETYMON-C24-RATRI-01] -->

> **ನಿನ್ನೆ ನನಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಸಂತೋಷವಾಯಿತು.**
>
> *ninne nanage tumbā santōṣavāyitu.* — "Yesterday I was very happy."

Kannada builds most feelings the way it builds **ನನಗೆ ಕನ್ನಡ ಗೊತ್ತು**: the person takes **ನನಗೆ**, "to me", and the feeling happens to them. **ಸಂತೋಷ** + **ಆಯಿತು** → **ಸಂತೋಷವಾಯಿತು**, with the **-ವ-** you met in **ಸ್ವಚ್ಛವಾಗಿದೆ**. Any feeling word fits the frame:

- **ನನಗೆ ಭಯವಾಯಿತು** (*nanage bhayavāyitu*) — I got scared
- **ನನಗೆ ಬೇಸರವಾಯಿತು** (*nanage bēsaravāyitu*) — I felt low; I got bored
- **ನನಗೆ ದುಃಖವಾಯಿತು** (*nanage duḥkhavāyitu*) — I felt sad
- **ನನಗೆ ಕೋಪ ಬಂತು** (*nanage kōpa bantu*) — I got angry: anger "came" to me

For what happens time after time, **ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ**:

> **ರಾತ್ರಿ ನನಗೆ ಭಯವಾಗುತ್ತದೆ.**
>
> *rātri nanage bhayavāguttade.* — "I get scared at night."

## You'll want to know: a feeling you are in
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C315-FEELING-STATE-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C315-KHUSI-01, KA-LEX-C50-LEAVE-01, KA-LEX-C03-CENNAAGI-01] -->

> **ಅವಳು ಈಗ ಖುಷಿಯಾಗಿದ್ದಾಳೆ.**
>
> *avaḷu īga khuṣiyāgiddāḷe.* — "She is happy now."

**ಖುಷಿಯಾಗಿದ್ದಾಳೆ** is **ಖುಷಿ** + **ಆಗಿ**, "having become glad", + **ಇದ್ದಾಳೆ** (*iddāḷe*, "she is"). For "I": **ನಾನು ಖುಷಿಯಾಗಿದ್ದೇನೆ** (*nānu khuṣiyāgiddēne*), "I am happy", with the **ಇದ್ದೇನೆ** of **ನಾನು ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದ್ದೇನೆ**.

Here the person is the subject, in **ನಾನು** or **ಅವಳು**. The **ನನಗೆ** frame says what came over you; the **-ಆಗಿದ್ದೇನೆ** frame says the state you are in. And when you cannot name the feeling, **ನನಗೆ ಹಾಗೆ ಅನಿಸಿತು** (*nanage hāge anisitu*, "that's how I felt").
