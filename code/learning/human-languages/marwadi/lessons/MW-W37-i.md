# इ — the fourth standing vowel

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-PAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 14s] Say the line that closes a bargain — *paisā le lo* — and write the
**ि** mark on any consonant you like.

## Script — the mark, standing on its own
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-I-INDEPENDENT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **इ** — *i* — the vowel with no consonant under it

Since chapter 7 the hand has made **ि**, the hook that leans in *front* of the
consonant it is read *after*. A vowel that opens a word has nothing to lean on,
so Devanagari gives it a letter that stands on the line by itself.

**आ** was isolated in chapter 2, **ई** in chapter 6, **ए** in chapter 32 for the
first number. **इ** is the fourth, and eleven is what needs it.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-I-INDEPENDENT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **इ** three times. Write **ि** beside it and say which of the two can begin
a word on its own. Then look at **ई** and mark the one stroke that separates the
two standing letters.
