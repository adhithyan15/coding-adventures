# कुढ़ना (kuṛhnā) — to fret; to grieve inwardly

## You'll want to know: कुढ़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C455-GAM455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुढ़ना** — *kuṛhnā* — "to fret; to grieve inwardly".

> अरुण अपनी हार पर बहुत कुढ़ा।
>
> *Aruṇ apnī hār par bahut kuṛhā.*
>
> Arun fretted bitterly over his defeat.

Past (he …): वह बहुत कुढ़ा, *vah bahut kuṛhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुढ़ना** (*kuṛhnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *kuṛhnā*, "to be annoyed, to grieve", under Sanskrit *kruddhá*, "angry" (CDIAL 3598). It is the anger or sorrow one swallows instead of showing.
