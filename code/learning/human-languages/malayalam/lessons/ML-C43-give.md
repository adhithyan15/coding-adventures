## You'll want to know: തരൂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-GIVE]; assesses=[] -->

**തരൂ** (*tarū*) — "give".

'Give — to me.' Malayalam distinguishes giving toward the speaker from giving away; this is the toward-me one.
