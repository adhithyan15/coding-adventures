## You'll want to know: la lumaca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C465-PICCOLI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la lumaca** — "the snail, the slug".

> Dopo la pioggia escono le lumache.
>
> *After the rain the snails come out.*

*Lento come una lumaca* — as slow as a snail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lumaca* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *limāca*, from Latin *līmāx* 'slug, snail' (EtymDB).
