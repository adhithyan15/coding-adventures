## You'll want to know: تقلب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C486-TAQALLUB486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تقلب** — *taqallaba* — "to keep changing; to fluctuate".

> يتقلب الطقس كثيرا في الربيع.
>
> *yataqallabu aṭ-ṭaqs kathīran fī ar-rabīʿ.*
>
> The weather changes a lot in spring.

Present: يتقلب, *yataqallab*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تقلب** (*taqallaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-l-b*, "to turn over" (Lane). It is Form V, "to turn this way and that".
