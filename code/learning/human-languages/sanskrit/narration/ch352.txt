Sanskrit, chapter 352: Harm and No Harm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हिंसा (hiṃsā) — violence; harm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for abides by, then the Sanskrit for the bounds of propriety.

You'll want to know: हिंसा.
हिंसा — hiṃsā — "violence; harm".
हिंसा कदापि उचिता न भवति। hiṃsā kadāpi ucitā na bhavati. Violence is not right at any time.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
हिंसा (hiṃsā) comes from the root √hiṃs, "to injure"; MW gives hiṃsā "injury, harm, hurt, mischief" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अपकरोति (apakaroti) — does harm to; wrongs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for violence, then the Sanskrit for tolerant.

You'll want to know: अपकरोति.
अपकरोति — apakaroti — "does harm to; wrongs".
सः कस्य अपि न अपकरोति। saḥ kasya api na apakaroti. He does no one any harm.
Past (he …): सः मम अपकृतवान्, saḥ mama apakṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
अपकरोति (apakaroti) is built on apa, "away, off", and the root √kṛ, "to do"; MW gives apa-kṛta "done wrongly or maliciously" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनुगृह्णाति (anugṛhṇāti) — shows favour to; obliges.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for tolerant, then the Sanskrit for does harm to.

You'll want to know: अनुगृह्णाति.
अनुगृह्णाति — anugṛhṇāti — "shows favour to; obliges".
गुरुः शिष्यम् अनुगृह्णाति। guruḥ śiṣyam anugṛhṇāti. The teacher shows his pupil favour.
Past (he …): सः माम् अनुगृहीतवान्, saḥ mām anugṛhītavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुगृह्णाति (anugṛhṇāti) is built on anu, "after", and the root √grah, "to seize, to take"; MW gives anu-√grah "to support" and "to treat with kindness, to favour, to oblige" (MW).
