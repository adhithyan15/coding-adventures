## You'll want to know: hàipà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C236-ODD236-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hàipà* — "to be scared".

> *wǎnshang yí ge rén zài jiā wǒ hěn hàipà* — Home alone at night, I was really scared.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hàipà* is built from *hài* 'harm' and *pà* 'to fear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hajH* and *phaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to injure'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
