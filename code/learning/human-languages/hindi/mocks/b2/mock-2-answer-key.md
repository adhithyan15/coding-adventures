## Writing

Task 1 must explain why passenger totals alone are insufficient, identify two
additional outcomes or affected groups, request a workable non-digital payment
or access route, and propose transparent publication. Task 2 must engage with
the claim, use device rules and another educational example, weigh benefits and
unintended effects, define proportionate exceptions, and reach a balanced
recommendation. Apply the rubric rather than matching fixed wording.
Scorer-only reference openings:

- **आदरणीय परिवहन दल, केवल यात्रियों की कुल संख्या से यह नहीं पता चलेगा कि…**
- **अस्थायी रोक शांत अभ्यास का समय बना सकती है, पर निर्णय-कौशल सिखाने का…**

Do not show these stems to the learner.

## Speaking

The presentation must sustain four minutes and develop continuity, change,
credit/payment, an ethical risk, and a responsible recommendation. The
collaborative task must compare the named phone-policy options, negotiate
exceptions and first responses, incorporate student voice, and agree on
six-month measures. The defended discussion requires supported positions,
responses, clarification, and credible conditions for revision. Score the
learner's own development, interaction, negotiation, Hindi control,
organisation, register, and intelligibility; no fixed wording is required.
