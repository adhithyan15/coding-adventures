## You'll want to know: नज़ारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C451-SAIR451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नज़ारा** — *nazārā* — "a view, a sight".

> पहाड़ से शहर का नज़ारा बहुत सुंदर था।
>
> *pahāṛ se śahar kā nazārā bahut sundar thā.*
>
> The view of the city from the mountain was beautiful.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नज़ारा** (*nazārā*) is usually traced to Arabic *naẓāra*, "a looking, a sight", through Persian; the root is the same as in नज़र, a glance.
