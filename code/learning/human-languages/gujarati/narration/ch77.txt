Gujarati, chapter 77: Always, Sometimes, Now, Later.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.

You'll want to know: હંમેશા.

The word, taken apart.
હંમેશા (hammeśā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian hamēsha, "always".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ક્યારેક (kyārek) — sometimes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for again, then the Gujarati for always.

You'll want to know: ક્યારેક.
ક્યારેક (kyārek) — "sometimes". Built on ક્યારે, when.

The word, taken apart.
ક્યારેક (kyārek) is kyāre, "when", with ek, "one". CDIAL has no entry for kyāre, so beyond its question stem its history is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for always, then the Gujarati for sometimes.

You'll want to know: હવે.
હવે (have) — "now, from now on".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists Old Gujarati hava, "now", the older form of હવે (have), under Sanskrit evam eva, "exactly so", crossed with the pronoun stem a- (CDIAL 2528).

Lesson 4 of 5.
પછી (pachī) — later, after.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for sometimes, then the Gujarati for now.

You'll want to know: પછી.
પછી (pachī) — "later, after".

The word, taken apart.
પછી (pachī) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under paśca, "hinder part", a form drawn from Sanskrit paścāt, "behind, afterward" (CDIAL 7990).

Lesson 5 of 5.

The word, taken apart.
પહેલાં (pahelā̃) (pahelāṁ) is a form of pahelũ, "first", which CDIAL lists under prathilla, a variant of a reconstructed prathila, "first", built on Sanskrit prathama (CDIAL 8652).
