Marathi, chapter 382: Likely or Certain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संभाव्य (sambhāvya) — likely; potential.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for so that, then the Marathi for as if.

You'll want to know: संभाव्य.
संभाव्य — sambhāvya — "likely; potential".
संभाव्य धोका लक्षात घेऊन शाळा बंद ठेवल्या. sambhāvya dhokā lakṣāt gheūn śāḷā band ṭhevalyā. Keeping the possible danger in mind, the schools were kept closed.
Invariable; संभाव्यता, probability.

The word, taken apart.
संभाव्य (sambhāvya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sambhāvya, "possible, probable" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बहुधा (bahudhā) — probably; most likely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for as if, then the Marathi for likely.

You'll want to know: बहुधा.
बहुधा — bahudhā — "probably; most likely".
बहुधा उद्या पाऊस पडेल. bahudhā udyā pāūs paḍel. It will probably rain tomorrow.
Often first in the sentence, before the guess: बहुधा (bahudhā) तो येईल.

The word, taken apart.
बहुधा (bahudhā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit bahudhā, "in many ways" (CDIAL 9191); Marathi uses it for what is most likely.

Lesson 3 of 5.
साहजिकच (sāhjikac) — naturally; of course.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for likely, then the Marathi for probably.

You'll want to know: साहजिकच.
साहजिकच — sāhjikac — "naturally; of course".
तो खूप थकला होता, साहजिकच लगेच झोपला. to khūp thaklā hotā, sāhjikac lagec jhoplā. He was very tired, so naturally he fell asleep at once.
साहजिक, natural, + -च.

The word, taken apart.
साहजिकच (sāhjikac) is built from साहजिक, "natural", made on Sanskrit sahaja, "born together, innate" (Monier-Williams), with the stressing ending -च.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for probably, then the Marathi for naturally.

You'll want to know: निश्चित.
निश्चित — niścit — "certain; fixed".
लग्नाची तारीख अजून निश्चित नाही. lagnācī tārīkh ajūn niścit nāhī. The wedding date is not fixed yet.
निश्चित (niścit) करणे, to fix; निश्चितपणे, certainly.

निश्चित (niścit) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit niścita, "ascertained, settled" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
भासणे (bhāsṇe) — to seem; to appear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for naturally, then the Marathi for certain.

You'll want to know: भासणे.
भासणे — bhāsṇe — "to seem; to appear".
दुरून ती टेकडी खूप जवळ भासते. durūn tī ṭekḍī khūp javaḷ bhāste. From afar that hill seems very close.
Past (to him …): त्याला भासलं, tyālā bhāslaṁ.
Formal; in speech वाटणे. Also with the dative: मला तसं भासलं.

The word, taken apart.
भासणे (bhāsṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi bhāsṇẽ, "to appear, seem, seem probable", under Sanskrit bhāsati, "is bright, appears" (CDIAL 9481).
