# كما لو (kamā law) — as if

## You'll want to know: كما لو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C404-SHART404-04]; assesses=[] -->

**كما لو** — *kamā law* — "as if".

> تكلم كما لو كان المدير.
>
> *takallama kamā law kāna al-mudīr.*
>
> He spoke as if he were the manager.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كما لو** (*kamā law*) joins the native Arabic *kamā*, "as", and *law*, "if": as if. The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
