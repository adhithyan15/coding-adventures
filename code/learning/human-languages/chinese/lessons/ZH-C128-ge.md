## You'll want to know: gē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C128-MUSIC128-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gē* — "song".

> *wǒ xǐhuan Zhōngguó gē* — I love Chinese songs.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gē* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ka* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary quotes an older book: the voice of a person is called song.
