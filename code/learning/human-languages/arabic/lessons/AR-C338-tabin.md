# تأبين (taʾbīn) — a eulogy, a memorial tribute

## You'll want to know: تأبين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C338-MAWTA338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تأبين** — *taʾbīn* — "a eulogy, a memorial tribute".

> حضر الكتاب حفل تأبين الشاعر.
>
> *ḥaḍara al-kuttāb ḥafl taʾbīn ash-shāʿir.*
>
> The writers attended the poet’s memorial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تأبين** (*taʾbīn*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-b-n* (Lane), where Form II *abbana* is "to praise a person after his death"; *taʾbīn* is its verbal noun.
