# HL87 — Punjabi A2 plural past-copula prerequisite

Issue: #17977

Punjabi A2 mock drafting exposed `ਸਨ` six times: four times in the learner
paper and twice in the confidential examiner master. The authored course
already owns singular past `ਸੀ`, but it does not yet directly own the
plural/polite past copula or its use after a familiar plural habitual
predicate. Chapter 187 closes only that prerequisite and does not edit the
mock.

The ramp first retrieves `ਸੀ` with familiar singular descriptions. It then
introduces `ਸਨ` in a transparent plural description, contrasts the two forms,
and moves `ਸਨ` into familiar “they used to …” language. The target has one
direct owner and at least two later retrieval lessons before a four-skill
payoff. All other words and inflections in learner-facing Punjabi must already
occur in earlier lessons.

This remains a prerequisite tranche, not mock-readiness evidence. The separate
perfective family (`ਕੀਤਾ`, `ਮਿਲਿਆ`, `ਗਏ`, `ਦੱਸਿਆ`, and `ਬਦਲਿਆ`) and
first-person future forms remain out of scope. All authored and generated
ownership stays sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case; no
corpus-wide aggregate is introduced.
