## You'll want to know: 王后
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C330-KING330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**王后** — *wánghòu* — "queen (a king's wife)".

> **国王和王后有一个女儿** *guówáng hé wánghòu yǒu yí ge nǚ'ér* — The king and queen had a daughter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**王后** (*wánghòu*) is built from 王 'a king' and *hòu* 'a queen', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjwang* and *huwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists the second under both *huwX* and *huwH*, 'a sovereign', and under *huwH* also 'an empress'. This *hòu* is a different word from the 后 of 后来, whose older form the dictionary lists under *huwX*, 'before and after'.
