## You'll want to know: समृद्धिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C386-SIDDHI386-05]; assesses=[] -->

**समृद्धिः** — *samṛddhiḥ* — "prosperity".

> यदि व्यापारः वर्धते, तर्हि नगरे समृद्धिः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi vyāpāraḥ vardhate, tarhi nagare samṛddhiḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If trade grows, there will be prosperity in the city.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समृद्धिः** (*samṛddhiḥ*) is built on *sam* and the root √ṛdh, "to thrive"; MW gives *sam-ṛddhi* "great prosperity or success, growth, welfare" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
