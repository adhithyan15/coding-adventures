# સીંચવું (sī̃cvũ) — to irrigate, to water

## You'll want to know: સીંચવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C240-GARDEN240-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સીંચવું** — *sī̃cvũ* — "to irrigate, to water".

> **મેં સાંજે છોડ સીંચ્યો.** — *mẽ sā̃je choḍ sī̃cyo* — "I watered the plant in the evening."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સીંચવું** (*sī̃cvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *siñcati*, "pours out, sprinkles", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to irrigate" (CDIAL 13394).
