# ಕೊರಗು (koragu) — to pine; to brood

## You'll want to know: ಕೊರಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C491-DUHKHA491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊರಗು** — *koragu* — "to pine; to brood".

> ಕೆಲಸ ಹೋದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವನು ದಿನವೂ ಕೊರಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *kelasa hōda mēle avanu dinavū koraguttiddanu.*
>
> After he lost his job, he used to brood every day.

Past (I …): ಕೊರಗಿದೆ, *koragide*.

A quiet, long sorrow kept inside: ಮನಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲೇ ಕೊರಗು, to grieve in silence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊರಗು** (*koragu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1778 gives Kannada *koragu*, "to lose its sap, shrivel, waste away", and also "to sorrow". A grief that dries a person up is the picture behind it.
