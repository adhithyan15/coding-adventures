## You'll want to know: жульничать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C504-BOARDGAMES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**жульничать** (*zhúlnichat*) — "to cheat (at a game)".

> С ним нельзя играть в карты: он жульничает.
>
> *S nim nelzyá igrát v kárty: on zhúlnichayet.*
>
> You can't play cards with him: he cheats.

Imperfective, colloquial; present жульничаю. Built on *жулик* — a crook.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*жульничать* is built on *жулик* 'a crook'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source, and the origin of *жулик* is not settled.
