Gujarati, chapter 537: Choosing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પસંદગી (pasandgī) — a choice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a birth chart, then the Gujarati for the future.

You'll want to know: પસંદગી.
પસંદગી — pasandgī — "a choice".
જો પસંદગી મારી હોત તો હું પર્વત પર જાત. jo pasandgī mārī hot to hũ parvat par jāt. If the choice were mine, I would go to the mountains.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પસંદગી (pasandgī) is usually traced to Persian pasand, "approved, liked", with the ending -gī; પસંદ કરવું is to choose.

Lesson 2 of 5.
બદલે (badle) — instead of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the future, then the Gujarati for a choice.

You'll want to know: બદલે.
બદલે — badle — "instead of".
જો ચા નહીં હોય તો હું ચાને બદલે દૂધ પીશ. jo cā nahī̃ hoy to hũ cāne badle dūdh pīś. If there's no tea, I'll drink milk instead.

The word, taken apart.
બદલે (badle) is usually traced to Arabic badal, "a substitute", through Persian, in the form that follows ને: ચાને બદલે, instead of tea. The verb બદલવું, to change, belongs with it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
મનપસંદ (manapsand) — favourite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a choice, then the Gujarati for instead of.

You'll want to know: મનપસંદ.
મનપસંદ — manapsand — "favourite".
જો દુકાનમાં મારો મનપસંદ રંગ હશે તો હું તે સાડી ખરીદીશ. jo dukānmā̃ māro manapsand rang haśe to hũ te sāḍī kharīdīś. If the shop has my favourite colour, I will buy that sari.

The word, taken apart.
મનપસંદ (manapsand) is built from man, "the mind", from Sanskrit manas (Monier-Williams), and pasand, "liked", usually traced to Persian pasand: liked by one's own mind.

Lesson 4 of 5.
વીણવું (vīṇvũ) — to pick out (one by one).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for instead of, then the Gujarati for favourite.

You'll want to know: વીણવું.
વીણવું — vīṇvũ — "to pick out (one by one)".
જો તમે સારી કેરીઓ વીણશો તો હું તે ખરીદીશ. jo tame sārī kerīo vīṇśo to hũ te kharīdīś. If you pick out the good mangoes, I'll buy them.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કેરીઓ વીણી, teṇe kerīo vīṇī.

The word, taken apart.
વીણવું (vīṇvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit vícinōti, "picks out, sorts", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to pick, gather" (CDIAL 11686).

Lesson 5 of 5.
રુચવું (rucvũ) — to appeal (to someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for favourite, then the Gujarati for to pick out.

You'll want to know: રુચવું.
રુચવું — rucvũ — "to appeal (to someone)".
જો આ યોજના તમને રુચશે તો આપણે કાલે શરૂ કરીશું. jo ā yojnā tamne rucśe to āpaṇe kāle śarū karīśũ. If this plan appeals to you, we will start tomorrow.
Past (it …): તે વાત તેને રુચી, te vāt tene rucī.

The word, taken apart.
રુચવું (rucvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit rucyatē, "is pleasing", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be liked" (CDIAL 10765). The person it appeals to takes ને.
