# ഇടപെടുക (iṭapeṭuka) — to interfere; to intervene

## You'll want to know: ഇടപെടുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C451-SAMMARDDAM451-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇടപെടുക** — *iṭapeṭuka* — "to interfere; to intervene".

> അവരുടെ കാര്യത്തിൽ ഞാൻ ഇടപെട്ടില്ല.
>
> *avaruṭe kāryattil ñān iṭapeṭṭilla.*
>
> I did not interfere in their affairs.

Past: ഇടപെട്ടു, *iṭapeṭṭu*.

In a matter: -ഇൽ ഇടപെടുക. The noun is ഇടപെടൽ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇടപെടുക** (*iṭapeṭuka*) is a native Malayalam compound: ഇട, "space, place" (DEDR 434), and പെടുക, "to be caught, to be in" (DEDR 3853): to step into the space between.
