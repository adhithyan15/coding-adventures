## You'll want to know: ਇਕੱਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C327-UPSET327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਕੱਲਾ** — *ikallā* — "alone".

> ਛੁੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਇਕੱਲਾ ਸੀ।
>
> *chhuṭṭī vich maiṁ ghar vich ikallā sī.*
>
> During the holiday I was alone at home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਕੱਲਾ** (*ikallā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ekkala*, "alone"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ikallā*, "alone", under it (CDIAL 2506).
