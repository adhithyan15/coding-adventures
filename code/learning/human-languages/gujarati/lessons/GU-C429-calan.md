## You'll want to know: ચલણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C429-SAFE429-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચલણ** — *calaṇ* — "a currency".

> પરદેશમાં ત્યાંનું ચલણ વાપરવું પડે છે.
>
> *pardeśmā̃ tyā̃nũ calaṇ vāparvũ paḍe che.*
>
> Abroad you have to use the local currency.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચલણ** (*calaṇ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *calana*, "moving, going"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "conduct; currency" (CDIAL 4716): money that goes round.
