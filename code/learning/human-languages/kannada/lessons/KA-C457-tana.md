## You'll want to know: ತಾಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C457-VIHARA457-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾಣ** — *tāṇa* — "a spot; a haunt".

> ಈ ಜಲಪಾತ ಪ್ರವಾಸಿಗರ ಅಚ್ಚುಮೆಚ್ಚಿನ ತಾಣ.
>
> *ī jalapāta pravāsigara accumeccina tāṇa.*
>
> This waterfall is a favourite spot for tourists.

ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ ತಾಣ, a tourist spot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾಣ** (*tāṇa*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *sthāna*, "a place", through a Prakrit form *ṭhāṇa* (CDIAL 13753).
