## You'll want to know: o herói
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C325-HEROI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o herói** — "the hero".

> No fim, o herói salva a cidade.
>
> *In the end the hero saves the city.*

A woman is *a heroína*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*herói* is a learned borrowing from Latin *hērōs* (EtymDB), from Greek *hḗrōs* "a demigod, a hero".
