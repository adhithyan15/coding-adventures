## You'll want to know: મતભેદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C342-VIVAD342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મતભેદ** — *matbhed* — "a disagreement".

> આ યોજના પર અરુણ અને મીરા વચ્ચે મતભેદ છે.
>
> *ā yojnā par Aruṇ ane Mīrā vacce matbhed che.*
>
> Arun and Mira disagree about this plan.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મતભેદ** (*matbhed*) is built from **મત** (*mat*), "an opinion", and Sanskrit *bheda*, "a split, a difference" (Monier-Williams): a split of opinion.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
