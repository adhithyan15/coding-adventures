## You'll want to know: बेड़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C308-BACHAV308-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बेड़ा** — *beṛā* — "a raft; a fleet".

> लोगों ने लकड़ी का एक बेड़ा बनाया और बच्चों को पानी से निकाला।
>
> *logoṁ ne lakṛī kā ek beṛā banāyā aur baccoṁ ko pānī se nikālā.*
>
> People built a wooden raft and got the children out of the water.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

goes back to Sanskrit *bēḍā*, "a boat" (CDIAL 9308), where CDIAL gives Hindi *beṛā*, a boat or a raft. In a flood a **बेड़ा** is anything that floats and carries people; with ships, it is a fleet.
