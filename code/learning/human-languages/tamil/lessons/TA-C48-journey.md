## You'll want to know: பயணம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C48-LEAVE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**பயணம்** (*payaṇam*) — "a journey".

Beside it Tamil also keeps *pirayāṇam*, straight from Sanskrit *prayāṇa*, and the pair divides the work the way *nalam* and *sugam* did: the shorter word is the everyday one, the longer is what goes into writing.

Somebody travelling is a *payaṇi*, with the same *-i* ending that made the farmer out of his field.

## What you've built
