## You'll want to know: la coherencia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C635-PROMESA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la coherencia** — "the consistency, the coherence".

> Sus ideas tienen coherencia.
>
> *Her ideas are consistent.*

*Coherente* — consistent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coherencia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cohaerentia* (EtymDB), built on *cohaerere* 'to stick together', from *haerere* 'to stick'.
