## You'll want to know: किमान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C379-MARYADA379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**किमान** — *kimān* — "minimum; at least".

> या कामासाठी किमान दोन दिवस लागतील.
>
> *yā kāmāsāṭhī kimān don divas lāgtīl.*
>
> This work will take at least two days.

Before a number, at least: किमान दोन, at least two.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किमान** (*kimān*) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
