Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 176: Covering, Tying and Hanging.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ढांकणो (ḍhā̃kṇo) — to cover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a small water pot, then the Marwadi for a spouted water pot.

You'll want to know: ढांकणो.
ढांकणो — ḍhā̃kṇo — "to cover".
मां दूध ढांकै है। mā̃ dūdh ḍhā̃kai hai. Mother covers the milk.
Past (I …): म्हैं दूध ढांक्यो, mhaĩ dūdh ḍhā̃kyo.

The word, taken apart.
ढांकणो (ḍhā̃kṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍhakk, "cover"; CDIAL lists Marwari ḍhā̃kṇo under it (CDIAL 5574).

Lesson 3 of 5.
गांठ (gā̃ṭh) — a knot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to cover, then the Marwadi for to tie.

You'll want to know: गांठ.
गांठ — gā̃ṭh — "a knot".
गांठ घणी कड़क है। gā̃ṭh ghaṇī kaṛak hai. The knot is very tight.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गांठ (gā̃ṭh) is inherited from Sanskrit granthi, "a knot"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati gā̃ṭh (CDIAL 4354). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सांकळ (sā̃kaḷ) — a door chain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to tie, then the Marwadi for a knot.

You'll want to know: सांकळ.
सांकळ — sā̃kaḷ — "a door chain".
किवाड़ माथै सांकळ है। kivāṛ māthai sā̃kaḷ hai. There's a chain on the door.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सांकळ (sā̃kaḷ) is inherited from Sanskrit śṛṅkhala, "a chain"; CDIAL lists Gujarati sā̃kaḷ and Hindi sā̃kal (CDIAL 12580). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Warm-up.

टांगणो — ṭā̃gṇo — "to hang up".

Past (I …): म्हैं कमीज टांगी, mhaĩ kamīj ṭā̃gī.

टांगणो (ṭā̃gṇo) comes from a reconstructed ṭaṅg, "hang up"; CDIAL lists Hindi ṭā̃gnā and Gujarati ṭā̃gvũ (CDIAL 5436). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
