Urdu, chapter 385: Choosing What to Study.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
وظیفہ (vazīfā) — a scholarship; a stipend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for pampering, then the Urdu for to learn by rote.

You'll want to know: وظیفہ.
وظیفہ — vazīfā — "a scholarship; a stipend".
اگر مجھے وظیفہ ملا تو میں یونیورسٹی جاؤں گا۔ agar mujhe vazīfā milā to maiṅ yūnīvarsiṭī jāūṅ gā. If I get a scholarship, I will go to university.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
وظیفہ (vazīfā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic waẓīfa, "a duty; an allowance".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 7.
ترجیح دینا (tarjīh denā) — to prefer; to give priority to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a topic, then the Urdu for admission.

You'll want to know: ترجیح دینا.
ترجیح دینا — tarjīh denā — "to prefer; to give priority to".
اگر انتخاب میرا ہوتا تو میں تاریخ کو ترجیح دیتا۔ agar intikhāb merā hotā to maiṅ tārīkh ko tarjīh detā. If the choice were mine, I would pick history.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے ترجیح دی, us ne tarjīh dī.

The word, taken apart.
ترجیح دینا (tarjīh denā) is built from tarjīh, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tarjīḥ, "giving more weight (to one side)", and دینا, "to give".
