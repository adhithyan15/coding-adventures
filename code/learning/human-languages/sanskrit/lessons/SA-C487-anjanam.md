## You'll want to know: अञ्जनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C487-PRASADHANA487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अञ्जनम्** — *añjanam* — "kohl".

> नारी नेत्रे अञ्जनं स्थापयति।
>
> *nārī netre añjanaṃ sthāpayati.*
>
> The woman puts kohl on her eyes.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अञ्जनम्** (*añjanam*) comes from the root √añj, "to anoint"; MW gives "black pigment or collyrium applied to the eyelashes" (MW).
