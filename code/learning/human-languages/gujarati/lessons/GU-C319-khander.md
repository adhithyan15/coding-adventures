## You'll want to know: ખંડેર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C319-KILLO319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ખંડેર** — *khanḍer* — "ruins".

> આજે ત્યાં ફક્ત ખંડેર છે.
>
> *āje tyā̃ phakt khanḍer che.*
>
> Today only ruins remain there.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખંડેર** (*khanḍer*) probably goes back to *\*khaṇḍitaghara*, "a broken house", a form CDIAL reconstructs, where it lists Gujarati *khãḍiyer*, "ruins" (CDIAL 3798). It is what is left when walls and roofs have fallen.
