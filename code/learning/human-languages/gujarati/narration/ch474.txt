Gujarati, chapter 474: Eating Slowly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
લાળ (lāḷ) — saliva; drool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the calf of the leg, then the Gujarati for an armpit.

You'll want to know: લાળ.
લાળ — lāḷ — "saliva; drool".
ઊંઘમાં બાળકના મોઢામાંથી લાળ પડે છે. ū̃ghmā̃ bāḷaknā moḍhāmā̃thī lāḷ paḍe che. The baby drools in its sleep.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
લાળ (lāḷ) is inherited from Sanskrit lālā, "spittle, saliva"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 11027).

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to chew, then the Gujarati for to suck.

You'll want to know: થૂંકવું.
થૂંકવું — thū̃kvũ — "to spit".
રસ્તા પર થૂંકવું ખરાબ ટેવ છે. rastā par thū̃kvũ kharāb ṭev che. Spitting in the street is a bad habit.
Past (he …): તે રસ્તા પર થૂંક્યો, te rastā par thū̃kyo.

The word, taken apart.
થૂંકવું (thū̃kvũ) goes back to a reconstructed thukk-, "spit", an imitative word; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 6097).
