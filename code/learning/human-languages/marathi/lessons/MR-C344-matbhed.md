## You'll want to know: मतभेद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C344-MATBHED344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मतभेद** — *matbhed* — "a disagreement".

> भावांमध्ये पैशांवरून मतभेद झाले.
>
> *bhāvāmmadhye paiśāṁvarūn matbhed jhāle.*
>
> The brothers disagreed over money.

Masculine.

Mostly plural: मतभेद होणे, to disagree; मत + भेद, opinion + split.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मतभेद** (*matbhed*) is built from two tatsamas, Sanskrit *mata*, "thought, opinion", and *bheda*, "splitting, difference" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
