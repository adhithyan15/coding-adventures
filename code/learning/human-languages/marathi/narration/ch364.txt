Marathi, chapter 364: Organizations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संस्था (saṁsthā) — an institution; an organization.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for self-interest, then the Marathi for to incite.

You'll want to know: संस्था.
संस्था — saṁsthā — "an institution; an organization".
ही संस्था गरीब मुलांना शिकवते. hī saṁsthā garīb mulānnā śikavte. This organization teaches poor children.
Feminine.
Feminine: ही संस्था (saṁsthā); शिक्षणसंस्था, an educational institution.

The word, taken apart.
संस्था (saṁsthā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit saṁsthā, "standing together, an establishment" (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a committee, then the Marathi for an assembly.

You'll want to know: प्रवक्ता.
प्रवक्ता — pravaktā — "a spokesperson".
संस्थेच्या प्रवक्त्याने प्रश्नांची उत्तरं दिली. saṁsthecyā pravaktyāne praśnāñcī uttaraṁ dilī. The organization's spokesperson answered the questions.
Masculine. Plural: प्रवक्ते, pravakte.
Plural प्रवक्ते; oblique प्रवक्त्या-.

The word, taken apart.
प्रवक्ता (pravaktā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pravaktṛ, "one who announces, a speaker" (Monier-Williams). The Sanskrit nominative is pravaktā.
