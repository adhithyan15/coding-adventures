## You'll want to know: りょうほう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C368-ZENTAI368-03]; assesses=[] -->

**りょうほう** — *ryōhō* — "both".

> **りょうほう かったら、たかいでしょう。** — *ryōhō kattara, takai deshō* — "If you buy both, it will probably be expensive."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りょうほう** (*ryōhō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ryō*, "both" (Middle Chinese *ljangX*) and *hō*, "direction" (*pjang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
