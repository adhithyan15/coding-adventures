Kannada, chapter 521: Hard Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ದುಡಿ (duḍi) — to toil; to labour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for supervision, then the Kannada for punctuality.

You'll want to know: ದುಡಿ.
ದುಡಿ — duḍi — "to toil; to labour".
ಕುಟುಂಬಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಅಪ್ಪ ಹಗಲು ರಾತ್ರಿ ದುಡಿದರು. kuṭumbakkāgi appa hagalu rātri duḍidaru. Father toiled day and night for the family.
Past (I …): ದುಡಿದೆ, duḍide.
ದುಡಿಮೆ is the work and what it earns.

The word, taken apart.
ದುಡಿ (duḍi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3295 lists Kannada duḍi, "to labour, acquire by one's labour", and duḍime, "gain".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಒತ್ತಾಯಿಸು (ottāyisu) — to insist; to urge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to toil, then the Kannada for oversight.

You'll want to know: ಒತ್ತಾಯಿಸು.
ಒತ್ತಾಯಿಸು — ottāyisu — "to insist; to urge".
ಊಟ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಂಡು ಹೋಗು ಅಂತ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಒತ್ತಾಯಿಸಿದರು. ūṭa māḍikoṇḍu hōgu anta ajji ottāyisidaru. Grandmother insisted that I eat before going.
Past (I …): ಒತ್ತಾಯಿಸಿದೆ, ottāyiside.
The one urged takes -ಅನ್ನು; ಒತ್ತಾಯ is pressure, insistence.

The word, taken apart.
ಒತ್ತಾಯಿಸು (ottāyisu) is usually traced to the native verb ottu, "to press, push" (DEDR 1021): to put pressure on someone. The middle part is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪರಿಶ್ರಮ (pariśrama) — hard work; toil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for oversight, then the Kannada for to insist.

You'll want to know: ಪರಿಶ್ರಮ.
ಪರಿಶ್ರಮ — pariśrama — "hard work; toil".
ಅವಳ ಯಶಸ್ಸಿಗೆ ಅವಳ ಪರಿಶ್ರಮವೇ ಕಾರಣ. avaḷa yaśassige avaḷa pariśramavē kāraṇa. Her hard work is the reason for her success.
ಪರಿ-, all round + ಶ್ರಮ, labour.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಿಶ್ರಮ (pariśrama) is borrowed from Sanskrit pariśrama, "fatigue, pain, exertion" (CDIAL 7892).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪೈಪೋಟಿ (paipōṭi) — rivalry; stiff competition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to insist, then the Kannada for hard work.

You'll want to know: ಪೈಪೋಟಿ.
ಪೈಪೋಟಿ — paipōṭi — "rivalry; stiff competition".
ಎರಡು ಅಂಗಡಿಗಳ ನಡುವೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಪೈಪೋಟಿ ಇದೆ. eraḍu aṅgaḍigaḷa naḍuve tumbā paipōṭi ide. There is fierce rivalry between the two shops.
Closer and fiercer than ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೈಪೋಟಿ (paipōṭi) is built on the native pōṭi, "competition, rivalry", which DEDR 4583 lists for Kannada beside Tamil pōṭṭi; the origin of the first part, pai-, is not settled.
