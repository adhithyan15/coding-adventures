## You'll want to know: పద్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C539-PADYAM539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పద్యం** — *padyaṁ* — "a metrical verse; a poem".

> తాతయ్య చాలా పద్యాలు నోటికి చెబుతారు.
>
> *tātayya cālā padyālu nōṭiki cebutāru.*
>
> Grandpa can recite many verses by heart.

Plural: పద్యాలు, *padyālu*.

Classical Telugu verse in a fixed metre; పాట is a song.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పద్యం** (*padyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *padya*, "relating to a foot; made of the parts of verses" (Monier-Williams).
