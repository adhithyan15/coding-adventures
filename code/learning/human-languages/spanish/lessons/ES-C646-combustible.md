## You'll want to know: el combustible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C646-GASOLINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el combustible** — "the fuel".

> El combustible está muy caro.
>
> *Fuel is very expensive.*

As an adjective it means something that can burn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*combustible* is a learned borrowing built on Latin *combūrere* "to burn up, to consume" (Lewis & Short), through its past form *combustus*.
