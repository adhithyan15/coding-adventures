## You'll want to know: फँसाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C377-HALAT377-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फँसाना** — *phãsānā* — "to trap, to land (someone) in trouble".

> अगर तुम उस आदमी की बात मानोगे तो वह तुम्हें फँसा देगा।
>
> *agar tum us ādmī kī bāt mānoge to vah tumhẽ phãsā degā.*
>
> If you listen to that man, he'll land you in trouble.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे फँसाया, *usne mujhe phãsāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फँसाना** (*phãsānā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *spāśayati*, "causes to be bound" (CDIAL 13814).
