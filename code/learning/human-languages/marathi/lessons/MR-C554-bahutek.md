## You'll want to know: बहुतेक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C554-VEL554-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बहुतेक** — *bahutek* — "mostly; most".

> बहुतेक लोक रविवारी घरी असतात.
>
> *bahutek lok ravivārī gharī astāt.*
>
> Most people are at home on Sundays.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहुतेक** (*bahutek*) is a Marathi compound built from बहुत, "much", and एक, "one". CDIAL lists Marathi *bahut*, "much, many", under Sanskrit *bahutva*, "abundance" (CDIAL 9190).
