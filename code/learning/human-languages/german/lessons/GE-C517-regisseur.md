## You'll want to know: der Regisseur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C517-THEATER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Regisseur** — "director (of a film or play)".

> Der Regisseur arbeitet seit Jahren mit denselben Schauspielern.
>
> *The director has worked with the same actors for years.*

Plural: *die Regisseure*. Said the French way: *re-shi-SÖR*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Regisseur* was borrowed from French *régisseur* "manager" (EtymDB), built on *régir* "to manage", from Latin *regere* "to rule".
