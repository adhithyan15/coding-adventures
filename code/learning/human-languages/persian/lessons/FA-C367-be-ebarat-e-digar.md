## You'll want to know: به عبارت دیگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C367-AMMA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**به عبارت دیگر** (*be 'ebârat-e digar*) — "in other words".

> او کار را تمام نکرد. به عبارت دیگر، فردا باید دوباره بیاید.
>
> *u kâr râ tamâm nakard. be 'ebârat-e digar, fardâ bâyad dobâre biyâyad.*
>
> He did not finish the job. In other words, he has to come again tomorrow.

*Be* "with" + *'ebârat* "an expression" + *digar* "other". It restates the same idea in plainer words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به عبارت دیگر** joins *'ebârat*, from Arabic *'ibāra* "an expression" (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian words *be* and *digar* "other": in another expression.
