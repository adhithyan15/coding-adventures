## You'll want to know: die Presse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C272-SENDUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Presse** — "the press; a squeezer".

> Die Presse schrieb viel über den Fall.
>
> *The press wrote a lot about the case.*

Also a kitchen press: *eine Knoblauchpresse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Presse* is built on *pressen* "to press", Old High German *pressōn* (EtymDB), which came from Latin *pressāre*, built on *premere* "to press" (Lewis & Short): first the printing press.
