Urdu, chapter 526: Before the Deadline.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
میعاد (mīād) — a time limit; a term.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for still standing, then the Urdu for to keep (something) pending.

You'll want to know: میعاد.
میعاد — mīād — "a time limit; a term".
اگر میعاد ختم ہوئی تو ٹکٹ بیکار ہو گا۔ agar mīād khatm huī to ṭikaṭ bekār ho gā. If the time limit runs out, the ticket will be useless.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
میعاد (mīād) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mīʿād, "an appointed time".
