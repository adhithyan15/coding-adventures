## You'll want to know: సేనాధిపతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C339-SAINYAM339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సేనాధిపతి** — *sēnādhipati* — "a commander; a general".

> సేనాధిపతి సైనికులతో మాట్లాడాడు.
>
> *sēnādhipati sainikulatō māṭlāḍāḍu.*
>
> The commander spoke with the soldiers.

సేన, an army, and అధిపతి, a lord or chief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సేనాధిపతి** (*sēnādhipati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *senā*, "an army", and *adhipati*, "a lord, master" (CDIAL 255).
