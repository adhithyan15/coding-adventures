## You'll want to know: पक्षपातः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C348-AUCITYA348-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्षपातः** — *pakṣapātaḥ* — "partiality; bias".

> न्यायाधीशः पक्षपातं त्यजेत्।
>
> *nyāyādhīśaḥ pakṣapātaṃ tyajet.*
>
> A judge should put aside partiality.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्षपातः** (*pakṣapātaḥ*) is built from *pakṣa*, "a side", and *pāta*, "a falling"; MW gives *pakṣa-pāta* "adopting a side, siding with, partiality" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
