# वफ़ादार (vafādār) — loyal, faithful

## You'll want to know: वफ़ादार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C372-DAYA372-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वफ़ादार** — *vafādār* — "loyal, faithful".

> कुत्ता अपने मालिक का वफ़ादार होता है।
>
> *kuttā apne mālik kā vafādār hotā hai.*
>
> A dog is loyal to its master.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वफ़ादार** (*vafādār*) is usually traced to Persian *vafādār*, "keeping faith". Its first part is Arabic *wafāʾ*, "faithfulness".
