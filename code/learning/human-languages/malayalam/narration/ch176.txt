Malayalam, chapter 176: Get down, Swim, Jump, Fall, Live.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ഇറങ്ങുക (iṟaṅṅuka) — to get down, to get off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to throw, then the Malayalam for to climb.
[pause 1 second]
Say the sound of ṭha. (ṭa with a breath after it.)

You'll want to know: ഇറങ്ങുക.
ഇറങ്ങുക — iṟaṅṅuka — "to get down, to get off".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: iṟaṅṅuka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: iṟaṅṅuka, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say iṟaṅṅuka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for to throw, then the Malayalam for to climb, then say iṟaṅṅuka again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ഇറങ്ങുക (iṟaṅṅuka) mean? ("To get down, to get off".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words and verbs — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to fall and to live.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: clever, lazy, calm, busy, expensive]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cheap, wrong, complete, to run, to walk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to get up, to sleep, to wake up, to bathe, to wash]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to cook, to give, to bring, to call, to cry]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Clever? (ബുദ്ധിയുള്ള, buddhiyuḷḷa.) Lazy? (മടിയുള്ള, maṭiyuḷḷa.) Calm? (ശാന്തമായ, śāntamāya.) Busy? (തിരക്കുള്ള, tirakkuḷḷa.) Expensive? (വിലകൂടിയ, vilakūṭiya.) Cheap? (വിലകുറഞ്ഞ, vilakuṟañña.) Wrong? (തെറ്റായ, teṟṟāya.) Complete? (പൂർണ്ണമായ, pūrṇamāya.) To run? (ഓടുക, ōṭuka.) To walk? (നടക്കുക, naṭakkuka.) To get up? (എഴുന്നേൽക്കുക, eḻunnēlkkuka.) To sleep? (ഉറങ്ങുക, uṟaṅṅuka.) To wake up? (ഉണരുക, uṇaruka.) To bathe? (കുളിക്കുക, kuḷikkuka.) To wash? (കഴുകുക, kaḻukuka.) To cook? (വേവിക്കുക, vēvikkuka.) To give? (കൊടുക്കുക, koṭukkuka.) To bring? (കൊണ്ടുവരുക, koṇṭuvaruka.) To call? (വിളിക്കുക, viḷikkuka.) To cry? (കരയുക, karayuka.)
