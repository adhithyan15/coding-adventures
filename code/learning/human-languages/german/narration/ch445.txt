German, chapter 445: Schuhe und Schlüssel.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
der Schuster — the cobbler, the shoemaker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the flower shop, then the German for to stick.

You'll want to know: der Schuster.
der Schuster — "the cobbler, the shoemaker".
Der Schuster repariert meine alten Stiefel. The cobbler repairs my old boots.
Same form in the plural. Also der Schuhmacher.

The word, taken apart.
Schuster comes from Middle High German schuochsūtære 'shoe-stitcher' (EtymDB), built from Schuh 'shoe' and sūtære, from Latin sūtor 'cobbler', from suere 'to sew' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 6.
besohlen — to resole.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to stick, then the German for the cobbler.

You'll want to know: besohlen.
besohlen — "to resole".
Der Schuster besohlt die Stiefel bis Freitag. The cobbler will resole the boots by Friday.
Inseparable: er besohlt, er hat besohlt.

The word, taken apart.
besohlen is built from be- and Sohle 'sole', which goes back to Old High German sola (EtymDB) and is usually traced to Latin solea 'sandal' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 6.

Before the new one: say the German for the cobbler, then the German for to resole.

You'll want to know: polieren.
polieren — "to polish".
Vor der Hochzeit poliert er seine Schuhe. Before the wedding he polishes his shoes.
Past participle without ge-: ich habe poliert.

The word, taken apart.
polieren is inherited from Middle High German polieren (EtymDB), a borrowing through Old French polir from Latin polīre 'to make smooth, to polish' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 6.
der Schlüsseldienst — the locksmith (service).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to resole, then the German for to polish.

You'll want to know: der Schlüsseldienst.
der Schlüsseldienst — "the locksmith (service)".
Sie hat ihren Schlüssel verloren und ruft den Schlüsseldienst. She has lost her key and calls the locksmith.
Plural: die Schlüsseldienste. The craftsman is der Schlosser.

The word, taken apart.
Schlüsseldienst is built from Schlüssel 'key', inherited from Old High German sluzzil, from schließen 'to lock' (EtymDB), and Dienst 'service', from Middle High German dienst (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 6.
aufschließen — to unlock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to polish, then the German for the locksmith.

You'll want to know: aufschließen.
aufschließen — "to unlock".
Der Mann schließt die Tür in einer Minute auf. The man unlocks the door in one minute.
Separable: ich schließe auf, ich habe aufgeschlossen.

The word, taken apart.
aufschließen is built from auf 'open' and schließen 'to lock, to close', inherited from Old High German sliozan (EtymDB).
