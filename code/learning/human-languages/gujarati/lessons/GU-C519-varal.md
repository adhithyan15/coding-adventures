## You'll want to know: વરાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C519-SAGDI519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વરાળ** — *varāḷ* — "steam".

> તપેલીમાંથી ગરમ વરાળ નીકળે છે.
>
> *tapelīmā̃thī garam varāḷ nīkḷe che.*
>
> Hot steam is coming out of the pot.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વરાળ** (*varāḷ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
