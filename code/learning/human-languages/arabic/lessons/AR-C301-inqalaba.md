# انقلب (inqalaba) — to overturn, to turn over

## You'll want to know: انقلب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C301-TARIQ301-02]; assesses=[] -->

**انقلب** — *inqalaba* — "to overturn, to turn over".

> انقلبت الشاحنة على جانب الطريق.
>
> *inqalabat ash-shāḥina ʿalā jānib aṭ-ṭarīq.*
>
> The lorry overturned at the side of the road.

Present: ينقلب, *yanqalib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انقلب** (*inqalaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-l-b* (Lane), "to turn a thing over", the root of *qalb*, the heart, which turns. It is Form VII, "to be turned over".
