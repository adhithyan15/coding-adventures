## You'll want to know: dēdō, dēdere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C343-TRIUMPHUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dēdō, dēdere** — "to give up, to surrender".

> Cīvēs sē et urbem imperātōrī dēdidērunt.
>
> *The citizens surrendered themselves and the city to the general.*

*dēdō, dēdere, dēdidī, dēditum*: *dē* + *dō*. *Sē dēdere*: to surrender.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēdō** is built on *dō*, "to give", with *dē*, "away" (EtymDB): to give away from oneself.
