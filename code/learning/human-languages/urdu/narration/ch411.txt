Urdu, chapter 411: Slopes, Verandas and Cellars.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ڈھلان (ḍhalān) — a slope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a pole, then the Urdu for to show.

You'll want to know: ڈھلان.
ڈھلان — ḍhalān — "a slope".
پہاڑی کی ڈھلان پر گھاس ہے۔ pahāṛī kī ḍhalān par ghās hai. There is grass on the slope of the hill.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ڈھلان (ḍhalān) is built on ḍhalnā, "to topple, to slope away", which CDIAL lists under ḍhalati, "bends over, falls", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5581).

Lesson 2 of 5.
لڑھکنا (luṛhaknā) — to roll (down).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to show, then the Urdu for a slope.

You'll want to know: لڑھکنا.
لڑھکنا — luṛhaknā — "to roll (down)".
ایک پتھر ڈھلان سے نیچے لڑھکا۔ ek patthar ḍhalān se nīche luṛhkā. A stone rolled down the slope.
Past (it …): وہ گیند نیچے لڑھکی, voh gend nīche luṛhkī.

The word, taken apart.
لڑھکنا (luṛhaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin luṛhakṇā, "to roll, to slip", under Sanskrit luṭhati, "rolls" (CDIAL 11079).

Lesson 3 of 5.
برآمدہ (barāmadā) — a veranda.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a slope, then the Urdu for to roll (down).

You'll want to know: برآمدہ.
برآمدہ — barāmadā — "a veranda".
ہم برآمدے میں بیٹھ کر چائے پیتے ہیں۔ ham barāmade meṅ baiṭh kar chāe pīte haiṅ. We sit on the veranda and drink tea.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
برآمدہ (barāmadā) is usually traced to Persian barāmada, "come out, projecting": the part of a house that juts out.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تہہ خانہ (tah khānā) — a basement; a cellar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to roll (down), then the Urdu for a veranda.

You'll want to know: تہہ خانہ.
تہہ خانہ — tah khānā — "a basement; a cellar".
پرانی عمارت میں ایک تہہ خانہ ہے۔ purānī imārat meṅ ek tah khānā hai. The old building has a basement.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تہہ خانہ (tah khānā) is built from Persian tah, "the bottom", and khāna, "a room": it is usually traced to Persian tahkhāna.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بغل (baghal) — beside; the side (of someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a veranda, then the Urdu for a basement.

You'll want to know: بغل.
بغل — baghal — "beside; the side (of someone)".
ہوٹل کی بغل میں ایک دکان ہے۔ hoṭal kī baghal meṅ ek dukān hai. There's a shop next to the hotel.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بغل (baghal) is usually traced to Persian baghal, "the armpit, the side"; کی بغل میں is "right beside".
