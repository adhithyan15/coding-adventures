## You'll want to know: resmungar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C434-RECLAMACAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**resmungar** — "to grumble, to mutter".

> O meu avô resmunga quando o café está frio.
>
> *My grandfather grumbles when the coffee is cold.*

Said under your breath.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *resmungar* is not settled; EtymDB lists Spanish *remusgar* as a cousin.
