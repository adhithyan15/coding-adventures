## You'll want to know: چڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C444-ANGER444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**چڑنا** — *chiṛnā* — "to get irritated".

> وہ ہر چھوٹی بات پر چڑتا ہے۔
>
> *voh har chhoṭī bāt par chiṛtā hai.*
>
> He gets irritated over every little thing.

Past (she …): وہ بچوں سے چڑی, *voh bachchoṅ se chiṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چڑنا** (*chiṛnā*) is inherited from *ciḍ*, "be angry", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4794).
