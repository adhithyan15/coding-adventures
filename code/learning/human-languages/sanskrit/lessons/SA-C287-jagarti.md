## You'll want to know: जागर्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C287-ANUDHAVANA287-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जागर्ति** — *jāgarti* — "stays awake; keeps watch".

> यामिकः सर्वां रात्रिं जागर्ति।
>
> *yāmikaḥ sarvāṃ rātriṃ jāgarti.*
>
> The watchman stays awake all night.

Past (he …): सः रात्रौ जागरितवान्, *saḥ rātrau jāgaritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जागर्ति** (*jāgarti*) comes from the root √jāgṛ, "to be awake, watch"; MW compares Greek *egeirō* and Latin *vigilo* (MW).
