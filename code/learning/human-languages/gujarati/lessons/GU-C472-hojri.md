## You'll want to know: હોજરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C472-NAS472-05]; assesses=[] -->

**હોજરી** — *hojrī* — "the stomach (the organ)".

> વધારે મરચાંથી હોજરીમાં બળે છે.
>
> *vadhāre marcā̃thī hojrīmā̃ baḷe che.*
>
> Too many chillies make your stomach burn.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હોજરી** (*hojrī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "stomach", under Sanskrit *ūbadhya*, "undigested grass in the stomach of an animal" (CDIAL 2417).
