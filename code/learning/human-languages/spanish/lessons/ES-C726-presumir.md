## You'll want to know: presumir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C726-HUMILDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**presumir** — "to show off, to boast".

> Le gusta presumir de su coche.
>
> *He likes to show off his car.*

*Presumir de algo*. Also to presume: *se presume inocente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*presumir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praesūmere* "to take beforehand" (EtymDB), built on *sūmere* "to take".
