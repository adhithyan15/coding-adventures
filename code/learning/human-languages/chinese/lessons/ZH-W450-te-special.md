# 特 — the cow piece, then two pieces stacked

## Script — the cow piece, then two pieces stacked
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-TE-SPECIAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 特

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, the cow piece, four strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left. A bar. A long vertical down through the bar. Then a stroke that rises to the right.

**The right piece, six strokes, in two parts.** On top, the earth piece of 地 with its last bar long: a short bar, a vertical, and a long bar under them. Under it, the right piece of 对: a bar, a vertical with a hook, and a dot.

4 + 6 = ten.
