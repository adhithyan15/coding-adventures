## You'll want to know: تطريز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C538-IBRA538-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تطريز** — *taṭrīz* — "embroidery".

> تعلمت جدتي التطريز في طفولتها.
>
> *taʿallamat jaddatī at-taṭrīz fī ṭufūlatihā.*
>
> My grandmother learned embroidery in her childhood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تطريز** (*taṭrīz*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-r-z*, "to embroider, to figure a cloth" (Lane). Taṭrīz is the verbal noun of Form II.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
