Russian, chapter 243: Young, Elderly, Tall, Low, Ugly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
молодой (molodóy) — young.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a zoo, then the Russian for a circus.

You'll want to know: молодой.
молодой (molodóy) — "young".

The word, taken apart.
молодой (molodóy) from Proto-Slavic moldŭ — inherited; Church Slavonic has младой, "young". Vasmer relates it to Old Prussian maldai, "youths", Sanskrit mṛdú-, "soft", and Latin mollis, "soft".

высокий (vysókiy) — "tall, high".

The word, taken apart.
высокий (vysókiy) from Proto-Slavic vysokŭ — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Greek hypsēlós, "high", Old Irish uasal, "high", and Gaulish Uxello-, "high", in place names.
