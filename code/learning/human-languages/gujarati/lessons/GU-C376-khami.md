## You'll want to know: ખામી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C376-BHUL376-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ખામી** — *khāmī* — "a defect".

> જો નવા ફોનમાં કોઈ ખામી હશે તો દુકાનદાર તે બદલી આપશે.
>
> *jo navā phonmā̃ koī khāmī haśe to dukāndār te badlī āpśe.*
>
> If there is a defect in the new phone, the shopkeeper will replace it.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખામી** (*khāmī*) is usually traced to Persian *khāmī*, "rawness, a shortcoming".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
