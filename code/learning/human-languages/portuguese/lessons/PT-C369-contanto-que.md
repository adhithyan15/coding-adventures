## You'll want to know: contanto que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C369-CONDICAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**contanto que** — "as long as, on condition that".

> Empresto-te o carro, contanto que tenhas cuidado.
>
> *I will lend you the car, as long as you are careful.*

Takes the subjunctive; a little more formal than *desde que*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contanto que* is built from *com* "with", *tanto* "so much", inherited from Latin *tantum* "so much" (Lewis & Short), and *que*: "with so much as".
