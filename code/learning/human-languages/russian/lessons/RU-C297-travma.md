## You'll want to know: травма
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C297-TRAVMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**травма** (*trávma*) — "an injury".

> Футболист получил травму.
>
> *Futbolíst poluchíl trávmu.*
>
> The footballer got injured.

Feminine. *Травмпункт* — an emergency clinic for injuries.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*травма* goes back to Greek *traûma* "a wound"; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.
