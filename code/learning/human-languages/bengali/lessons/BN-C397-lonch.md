## You'll want to know: লঞ্চ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C397-GHAT397-02]; assesses=[] -->

**লঞ্চ** — *lônch* — "a launch; a motor ferry".

> লঞ্চটা ভোরে ঘাট ছাড়ল।
>
> *lônchṭā bhore ghāṭ chhāṛlô.*
>
> The launch left the ghat at dawn.

The motor boat that carries passengers on the big rivers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লঞ্চ** (*lônch*) is borrowed from English *launch*, a motor boat; ঞ্চ is the n-ch joined letter.
