## You'll want to know: تھاپنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C507-ROTI507-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تھاپنا** — *thāpnā* — "to pat (into shape)".

> دادی ہاتھوں سے مکئی کی روٹی تھاپتی ہیں۔
>
> *dādī hāthoṅ se makaī kī roṭī thāptī haiṅ.*
>
> Grandmother pats maize rotis into shape by hand.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے روٹی تھاپی, *us ne roṭī thāpī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تھاپنا** (*thāpnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *thāpnā*, "to pat", under *\*thapp*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6091).
