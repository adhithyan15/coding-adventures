## You'll want to know: le commissariat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C440-QUARTIERS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le commissariat** — "the police station".

> Après le vol de son sac, elle est allée au commissariat.
>
> *After her bag was stolen, she went to the police station.*

In full: *le commissariat de police*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*commissariat* is built on *commissaire* 'commissioner', from Medieval Latin *commissārius*, from *committere* 'to entrust', built on *mittere* 'to send' (Lewis & Short).
