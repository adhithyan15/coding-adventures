## You'll want to know: かくじつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C520-WORRY520-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かくじつ** — *kakujitsu* — "sure; reliable".

> **もし でんしゃで いったら、くるまより かくじつです。** — *moshi densha de ittara, kuruma yori kakujitsu desu* — "If you go by train, it is more reliable than by car."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かくじつ** (*kakujitsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kaku*, "assurance" (Middle Chinese *khaewk*) and *jitsu*, "reality" (*zyit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
