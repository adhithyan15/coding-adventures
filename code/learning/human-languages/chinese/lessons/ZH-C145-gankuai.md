## You'll want to know: gǎnkuài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C145-LATE145-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnkuài* — "quickly; hurry".

> *bā diǎn shàngkè wǒmen gǎnkuài zǒu* — Class is at eight, so let's hurry.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnkuài* is built from *gǎn* 'to hurry' and 快 'fast'. 快 is inherited from Middle Chinese *khwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.
