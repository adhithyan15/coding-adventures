# ತಗುಲು (tagulu) — to catch (an illness); to touch

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C510-NOVU510-04, KA-LEX-C510-NOVU510-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for the gums, then the Kannada for the jaw.

## You'll want to know: ತಗುಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C511-SONKU511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಗುಲು** — *tagulu* — "to catch (an illness); to touch".

> ಮಳೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನೆನೆದಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ಮಗುವಿಗೆ ಶೀತ ತಗುಲಿತು.
>
> *maḷeyalli nenediddarinda maguvige śīta tagulitu.*
>
> Because the child got soaked in the rain, it caught a cold.

Past (it …): ತಗುಲಿತು, *tagulitu*.

The one who falls ill takes -ಗೆ: ಅವನಿಗೆ ಸೋಂಕು ತಗುಲಿತು. Costs too: ಖರ್ಚು ತಗುಲುತ್ತದೆ, it will cost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಗುಲು** (*tagulu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3004 lists Kannada *tagalu*, *tagilu* and *tagulu*, "to come in contact with, touch, hit".
