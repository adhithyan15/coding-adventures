## You'll want to know: تورم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C413-EQTESAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تورم** (*tavarrom*) — "inflation".

> تورم امسال بیشتر از پارسال است.
>
> *tavarrom emsâl bishtar az pârsâl ast.*
>
> Inflation this year is higher than last year.

Literally swelling, and also a medical word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تورم** comes from Arabic *tawarrum* "swelling" (EtymDB): inflation is prices swelling.
