## You'll want to know: अधमर्णः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C434-RNA434-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अधमर्णः** — *adhamarṇaḥ* — "a debtor".

> अधमर्णः शनैः ऋणं शोधयति।
>
> *adhamarṇaḥ śanaiḥ ṛṇaṃ śodhayati.*
>
> The debtor slowly clears the debt.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधमर्णः** (*adhamarṇaḥ*) is built from *adhama*, "lowest", and *ṛṇa*, "a debt"; MW gives the compound as a debtor (MW).
