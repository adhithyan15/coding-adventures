## You'll want to know: संघर्ष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C325-ANDOLAN325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संघर्ष** — *saṅgharṣ* — "a struggle".

> आज़ादी के लिए लोगों ने लंबा संघर्ष किया।
>
> *āzādī ke liye logoṁ ne lambā saṅgharṣ kiyā.*
>
> People waged a long struggle for freedom.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संघर्ष** (*saṅgharṣ*) is Sanskrit *saṁgharṣa* (Monier-Williams), a rubbing together and so a rivalry: in Hindi, a long fight against hardship or against a ruler.
