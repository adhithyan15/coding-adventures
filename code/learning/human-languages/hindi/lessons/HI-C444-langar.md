## You'll want to know: लंगर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C444-BANDARGAH444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लंगर** — *laṅgar* — "an anchor; a free kitchen".

> जहाज़ का लंगर बहुत भारी था।
>
> *jahāz kā laṅgar bahut bhārī thā.*
>
> The ship's anchor was very heavy.

Masculine.

At a gurdwara, the लंगर is the free kitchen where everyone eats together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लंगर** (*laṅgar*) is usually traced to Persian *langar*, "an anchor", and also a place where food is given out; both senses live in Hindi.
