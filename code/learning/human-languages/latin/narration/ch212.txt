Latin, chapter 212: Inquisitive, Envious, Anxious, Peaceful, Silent.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
cūriōsus, cūriōsa, cūriōsum — inquisitive, painstaking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for in vain, then the Latin for back again.

You'll want to know: cūriōsus, cūriōsa, cūriōsum.
cūriōsus, cūriōsa, cūriōsum — "inquisitive, painstaking".

The word, taken apart.
cūriōsus is built on cūra, "care" (Lewis & Short), with -ōsus, "full of": full of care, so careful or prying. English curious comes from it.
