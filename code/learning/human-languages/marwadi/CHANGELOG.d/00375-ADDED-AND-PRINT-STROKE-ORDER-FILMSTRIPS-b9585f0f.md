## Added — ी and ो print stroke-order filmstrips

ी and ो now have a cited pen path, from native writers' pen traces in HP Labs
India's LipiTk Devanagari recognizer (counts and shares only). Each strip
draws the sign alone.

- **New filmstrips (2 lessons, 41 -> 43):** MW-W04-ii-matra (ी: one run from
  the hook's lower tip, over the top and down the stem) and MW-W05-o-matra
  (ो: the stem down, a lift, then the flag from its upper-left tip).
- **The grey bar.** The printed sign carries a short piece of headline that
  the strip leaves grey: it belongs to the word's headline, not to the sign.
- Lesson prose, narration and durations do not change; the strip is printed
  in the book only.
- **Not yet:** MW-W07-i-matra (ि: native writers split three ways),
  MW-W05-ai-matra (ै) and MW-W12-au-matra (ौ: the traces split).
