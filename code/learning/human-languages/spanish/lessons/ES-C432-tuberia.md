# la tubería — all the pipes together

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-03, ES-LEX-AGUA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *la estantería*. Say *el agua*. A **tubo** is a pipe. Say what a
*tubería* must be before reading on.

## You'll want to know: la tubería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-04]; assesses=[ES-LEX-AGUA-01] -->

**la tubería** — the pipework, the plumbing, the run of pipes.

**el tubo** is one pipe. The ending collects them, exactly as it collected the
shelves.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-04, ES-LEX-C432-CASA-03] -->

Third time for an ending in two chapters, and the second time for this one:

| one | the collection |
|---|---|
| el estante | la estantería |
| el tubo | la tubería |

This is the word in the notice that says the water is being cut off: *reparar
una tubería*. The repair is to the **run of pipes**, not to one length of tube,
and the word chooses itself accordingly.

If you are keeping count, you have now reached four words in this chapter and
three of them you could have guessed from a piece you already had.
