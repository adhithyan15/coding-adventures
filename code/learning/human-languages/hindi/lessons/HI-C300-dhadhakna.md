# धधकना (dhadhaknā) — to blaze

## You'll want to know: धधकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C300-DAHAK300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धधकना** — *dhadhaknā* — "to blaze".

> पूरा घर धधक रहा था।
>
> *pūrā ghar dhadhak rahā thā.*
>
> The whole house was ablaze.

Past (it …): वह आग धधकी, *vah āg dhadhkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धधकना** (*dhadhaknā*) is inherited from *\*dhagg*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6704); the doubled *dha-dha* gives the roar of a big fire.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
