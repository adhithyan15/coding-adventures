## You'll want to know: طلاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C336-TALAQ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**طلاق** (*talâq*) — "a divorce".

> طلاق آنها برای بچه ها سخت بود.
>
> *Talâq-e ânhâ barâ-ye bachche-hâ sakht bud.*
>
> Their divorce was hard on the children.

Traditionally *talâq dâdan* is said of the husband.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*talâq* is borrowed from Arabic *ṭalāq*, "divorce" (EtymDB).
