Telugu, chapter 270: Saucepan, Flowerpot, Griddle, Mortar, Pestle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తపేలా (tapēlā) — a saucepan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for wood, then the Telugu for brass.

You'll want to know: తపేలా.
తపేలా — tapēlā — "a saucepan".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of తపేలా (tapēlā) is uncertain. DEDR and CDIAL list no word of this shape meaning "saucepan".

Lesson 4 of 5.
రోలు (rōlu) — a mortar (for pounding).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a flowerpot, then the Telugu for a griddle.

You'll want to know: రోలు.
రోలు — rōlu — "a mortar (for pounding)".

The word, taken apart.
రోలు (rōlu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil ural and Kannada oral (DEDR 651). DEDR lists Telugu rōlu, "mortar".

The word, taken apart.
DEDR 665 lists a nasalised రోకలి (rōkali), "a large wooden pestle", with a query, under the word for "to rub", one word with Tamil urai.
