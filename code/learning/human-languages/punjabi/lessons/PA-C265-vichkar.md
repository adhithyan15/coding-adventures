## You'll want to know: ਵਿਚਕਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C265-SIDE265-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਚਕਾਰ** — *vichkār* — "in the middle; between".

> ਬੈਂਕ ਸਕੂਲ ਅਤੇ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਦੇ ਵਿਚਕਾਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *baiṅk sakūl ate daftar de vichkār hai.*
>
> The bank is between the school and the office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਚਕਾਰ** (*vichkār*) is built on *vicc*, "middle", from a reconstructed *\*vīcya*, "middle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vicc* under it (CDIAL 12042). How the ending *-kār* joined it is not settled.
