## You'll want to know: たまたま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C523-ANY523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たまたま** — *tamatama* — "as it happens; by accident".

> **もし たまたま じかんが あったら、あそびに きて ください。** — *moshi tamatama jikan ga attara, asobi ni kite kudasai* — "If you happen to have time, come and see us."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たまたま** (*tamatama*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: *tama*, the native reading KANJIDIC2 gives the kanji for "accidentally", said twice (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
