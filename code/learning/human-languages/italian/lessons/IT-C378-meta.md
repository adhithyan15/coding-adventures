## You'll want to know: la meta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C378-SCOPO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la meta** — "the goal; the destination".

> La nostra meta è la cima della montagna.
>
> *Our goal is the top of the mountain.*

Stress on the first syllable: *mèta*. Not to be mixed up with *metà* "half".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*meta* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mēta* "a cone-shaped turning post in a racecourse; a limit, a goal" (Lewis & Short).
