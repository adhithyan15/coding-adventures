# Three lines from two old cards

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-A1-FORM-MIXED-CUES-01]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SIX-CUE-01] -->

The old fictional A · ਕ and B · ਖ cards are familiar. A form can choose a
different old card for each line. The mark tells you which known value to
retrieve; it is not a new word or a new person to memorise.

## Writing — supported mixed selection
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-FORM-MIXED-CUES-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Reopen the two old value banks for this rehearsal only. Read one label and its
mark, select the taught value, then cover that line before moving to the next.
There is no clock and no independent writing credit in this supported step.

> ਨਾਂ (A · ਕ): ______
>
> ਭਾਸ਼ਾ (B · ਖ): ______
>
> ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ (A · ਕ): ______

## Guided Practice — one check
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-FORM-MIXED-CUES-01] -->

Check only card selection first. Then check spelling and the space after each
colon in a separate pass. The next lesson will close the banks and use all six
familiar labels.
