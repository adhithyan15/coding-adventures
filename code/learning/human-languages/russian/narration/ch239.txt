Russian, chapter 239: Weight, Height, Eyebrow, Eyelash, Forehead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
вес (ves) — weight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a clinic, then the Russian for an ambulance.

You'll want to know: вес.
вес (ves) — "weight".
