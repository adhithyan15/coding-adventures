## You'll want to know: すっぱい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C177-TASTE177-02]; assesses=[] -->

**すっぱい** — *suppai* — "sour".

> **この みかんは すっぱいです。** — *kono mikan wa suppai desu* — "This mandarin is sour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すっぱい** (*suppai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
