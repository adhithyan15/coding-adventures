Kannada, chapter 134: Rat, Buffalo, Sheep, Face, Tongue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಇಲಿ (ili) — a rat, a mouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a donkey, then the Kannada for a camel.

You'll want to know: ಇಲಿ.
ಇಲಿ — ili — "a rat, a mouse".

The word, taken apart.
ಇಲಿ (ili) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil eli and Telugu eluka (DEDR 833). DEDR lists eli, ili, "rat, mouse".
