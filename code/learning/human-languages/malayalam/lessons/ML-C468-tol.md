## You'll want to know: ടോൾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C468-PATA468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ടോൾ** — *ṭōḷ* — "a toll".

> പാലം കടക്കാൻ ലോറികൾ ടോൾ കൊടുക്കണം.
>
> *pālaṁ kaṭakkān lōṟikaḷ ṭōḷ koṭukkaṇaṁ.*
>
> Lorries must pay a toll to cross the bridge.

The place where it is paid is a ടോൾ പ്ലാസ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ടോൾ** (*ṭōḷ*) was borrowed from English *toll*, a charge for using a road or a bridge.
