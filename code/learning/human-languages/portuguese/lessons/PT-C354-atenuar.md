## You'll want to know: atenuar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C354-INFLUENCIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**atenuar** — "to ease, to soften, to lessen".

> Este comprimido atenua a dor.
>
> *This pill eases the pain.*

Formal; in speech *aliviar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atenuar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *attenuāre* "to make thin or weak" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *tenuis* "thin".
