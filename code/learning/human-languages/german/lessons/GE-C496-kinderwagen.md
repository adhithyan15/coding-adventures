## You'll want to know: der Kinderwagen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C496-BABY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kinderwagen** — "pram, baby buggy".

> Mit dem Kinderwagen gehen wir jeden Tag in den Park.
>
> *We go to the park with the pram every day.*

Plural: *die Kinderwagen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kinderwagen* is built on *Kind* "child" and *Wagen* "cart", inherited from Old High German *wagan*, from Proto-Germanic *wagnaz* (EtymDB).
