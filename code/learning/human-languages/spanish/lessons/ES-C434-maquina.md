# la máquina — the word above the others

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-01, ES-LEX-ORDENADOR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *la lavadora*. Say *el ordenador*. You can name particular
machines. Name the **kind**.

## You'll want to know: la máquina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C434-MAQ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la máquina** — the machine.

Stressed on the first syllable, *MÁ-qui-na*, and the written accent marks it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C434-MAQ-02, ES-LEX-ORDENADOR] -->

Notice what **el ordenador** is: *ordenar* plus the doer-ending, the thing that
orders. Spanish named the computer after what it **does**, where English named
it after **counting**.

| language | the computer is |
|---|---|
| Spanish | the orderer |
| English | the counter |

**la máquina** sits above all of these as the general term, and it is the word
you need for the ones with no special name: *la máquina de hacer pan*, *la
máquina del billete*. When you do not know an appliance's name, *la máquina de*
plus what it does will be understood every time.
