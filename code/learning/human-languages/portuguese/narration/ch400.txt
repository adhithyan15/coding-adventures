Lesson 1 of 7.
publicar — to post, to publish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the search engine, then the Portuguese for the web link.

You'll want to know: publicar.
publicar — "to post, to publish".
Ela publicou as fotos da viagem. She posted the photos of the trip.
Publicar um livro — to publish a book.

The word, taken apart.
publicar is a learned borrowing from Latin pūblicāre "to make public" (EtymDB), on pūblicus "of the people, public" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 7.
reencaminhar — to forward (a message).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to comment, then the Portuguese for the follower.

You'll want to know: reencaminhar.
reencaminhar — "to forward (a message)".
Reencaminhei a mensagem para a minha irmã. I forwarded the message to my sister.
European Portuguese; in Brazil encaminhar.

The word, taken apart.
reencaminhar is built from re-, en- and caminho "way", which is inherited from Late Latin cammīnus "way" (EtymDB), usually traced to a Celtic word.

Lesson 5 of 7.
bloquear — to block; to freeze (a screen).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the follower, then the Portuguese for to forward.

You'll want to know: bloquear.
bloquear — "to block; to freeze (a screen)".
Bloqueei um utilizador que me insultava. I blocked a user who was insulting me.
O ecrã bloqueou — the screen froze.

The word, taken apart.
bloquear is usually traced to French bloquer "to block", the source EtymDB gives for its Spanish cousin bloquear; bloquer is built on bloc, which came through Old French bloc "log, block" from Middle Dutch blok "a tree trunk" (EtymDB).
