## You'll want to know: défectueux
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C432-GERANT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**défectueux** — "faulty, defective".

> La télévision est défectueuse depuis notre arrivée.
>
> *The television has been faulty since we arrived.*

Feminine *défectueuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*défectueux* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *dēfectuōsus*, from *dēfectus* 'lack', from *dēficere* 'to fail', built on *facere* 'to do' (Lewis & Short).
