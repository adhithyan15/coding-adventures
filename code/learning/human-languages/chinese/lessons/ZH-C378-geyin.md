## You'll want to know: géyīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C378-NOISE378-03]; assesses=[] -->

*géyīn* — "soundproofing; soundproof".

> *zhège fángzi de géyīn bù hǎo suǒyǐ hěn chǎo* — This house isn't soundproofed well, so it's noisy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*géyīn* is built from *gé* 'to block' and 音 'a sound', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keak* and *'im* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stop up'.
