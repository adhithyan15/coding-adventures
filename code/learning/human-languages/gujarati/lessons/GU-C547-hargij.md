## You'll want to know: હરગિજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C547-CHATA547-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હરગિજ** — *hargij* — "by no means (with a negative)".

> જો વરસાદ પડશે તો હું હરગિજ બહાર નહીં જાઉં.
>
> *jo varsād paḍśe to hũ hargij bahār nahī̃ jāũ.*
>
> If it rains, I will by no means go out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હરગિજ** (*hargij*) is usually traced to Persian *hargiz*, "ever", which with a negative means "never"; in Gujarati it comes with **નહીં**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
