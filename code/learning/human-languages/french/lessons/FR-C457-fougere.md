## You'll want to know: la fougère
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C457-BOIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la fougère** — "the fern, the bracken".

> Les fougères poussent sous les arbres.
>
> *The ferns grow under the trees.*

Often in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fougère* comes from Old French *fulgiere*, from Vulgar Latin *filicāria* 'place full of ferns', from Latin *filix* 'fern' (EtymDB).
