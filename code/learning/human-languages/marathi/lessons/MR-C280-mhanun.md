# म्हणून (mhaṇūn) — so, and because of that

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-JOIN-KAARAN, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "because", the word that answers **का**. (*kāraṇ*.) Then "it was raining". (*pāūs paḍat hotā*.)

## You'll want to know: म्हणून, 'so'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C280-MHANUN-01]; assesses=[MR-JOIN-KAARAN, MR-LEX-RAHNE, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01] -->

> **पाऊस पडत होता, म्हणून मी घरी राहिलो.**
>
> *pāūs paḍat hotā, mhaṇūn mī gharī rāhilo.* — "It was raining, so I stayed at home."

**कारण** gives the reason **after** the event; **म्हणून** gives the result **after** the reason. Same two facts, opposite order: **मी घरी राहिलो, कारण पाऊस पडत होता** (*mī gharī rāhilo, kāraṇ pāūs paḍat hotā*).

**म्हणून** is built like **करून**: the **-ऊन** form of **म्हणणे**, "to say".

## You'll want to know: त्यामुळे, 'because of that'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C280-TYAMULE-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C99-NATURE99-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-NEGATE-NAHI] -->

> **काल खूप पाऊस होता, त्यामुळे आम्ही बाहेर गेलो नाही.**
>
> *kāl khūp pāūs hotā, tyāmuḷe āmhī bāher gelo nāhī.* — "Yesterday there was a lot of rain, so we didn't go out."

**त्यामुळे** (*tyāmuḷe*) is **त्या**, "that", and **-मुळे**, "because of". It leans on the cause: "because of that". Where **म्हणून** often marks a choice someone made, **त्यामुळे** marks what came of something.
