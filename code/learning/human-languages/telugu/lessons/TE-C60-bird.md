## You'll want to know: పక్షి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C60-ANIMAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పక్షి** (*pakṣi*) — "a bird".

పక్షి is the general one: any bird at all, the కోడి included. It came in from Sanskrit, and beside it Telugu keeps its own పిట్ట for the small ones about a garden.

That is a split this book has met before — an inherited word doing the everyday work and a borrowed one carrying the wider idea, the same shape as నిప్పు beside అగ్ని. Once you start expecting it, half the vocabulary sorts itself.

Five animals now, from the గేదె at the gate to the చీమ under it, and a నమస్కారం for whoever keeps them.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Five: గేదె, మేక, కోడి, చీమ, పక్షి. Enough to greet a house and name what lives in it.
