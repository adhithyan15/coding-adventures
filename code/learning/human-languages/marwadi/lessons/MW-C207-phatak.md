## You'll want to know: फाटक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C207-GARDEN207-04]; assesses=[] -->

**फाटक** — *phāṭak* — "a big gate; a railway crossing".

> फाटक बंद है, धीरे चालो।
>
> *phāṭak band hai, dhīre chālo.*
>
> The gate is shut, go slowly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फाटक** (*phāṭak*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*phāṭṭakka*, "gate, door". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *phāṭak*, "gate" (CDIAL 9069).
