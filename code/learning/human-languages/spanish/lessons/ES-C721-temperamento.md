## You'll want to know: el temperamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C721-CARACTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el temperamento** — "temperament".

> Es un niño de temperamento tranquilo.
>
> *He is a child with a calm temperament.*

*Tener temperamento* — to be fiery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*temperamento* is a learned borrowing from Latin *temperāmentum* "a due mixture" (EtymDB), from *temperāre* "to mix in proportion"; old medicine saw character as a mix of body fluids.
