## You'll want to know: मकान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C422-KOTHI422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मकान** — *makān* — "a house (the building)".

> हमने किराये पर एक मकान लिया।
>
> *hamne kirāye par ek makān liyā.*
>
> We rented a house.

Masculine.

घर is home; मकान is the building, the thing you rent, buy or build.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मकान** (*makān*) is usually traced to Arabic *makān*, "a place", which came through Persian and narrowed in Hindi to a house as a building.
