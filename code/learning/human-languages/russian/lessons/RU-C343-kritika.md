## You'll want to know: критика
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C343-KRITIKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**критика** (*krítika*) — "criticism".

> Он спокойно слушает критику.
>
> *On spokóyno slúshayet krítiku.*
>
> He listens calmly to criticism.

Feminine. *Конструктивная критика* — constructive criticism. It is also reviewing as a field: *литературная критика*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**критика** is usually traced, through French and German, to Greek *kritikḗ* "the art of judging", built on *krínein* "to separate, to decide".
