## You'll want to know: yúshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C294-RESULT294-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yúshì* — "so; and then (as a result)".

> *yǔ hěn dà, yúshì wǒmen huí jiā le* — It was raining hard, so we went home.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yúshì* is built from *yú* 'at' and 是, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jo* and *dzyeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which calls the first a particle, without the sense 'at', and glosses the second 'right, not wrong'; how the pair came to mean 'and so' is not settled. The rising tone of yú is irregular for *'jo*.
