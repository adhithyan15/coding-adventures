Lesson 1 of 6.
意见 (yìjiàn) — a suggestion; an objection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

[your turn — recall: say 他突然来了 — tā tūrán lái le — R1, the chapter you have finished]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say kànfǎ, a view — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say yǒuyìsi, interesting — R3]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 意见.
意见 — yìjiàn — "a suggestion; an objection".
你有什么意见 nǐ yǒu shénme yìjiàn — What do you suggest? Or: what's your objection?
我没有意见 wǒ méiyǒu yìjiàn — I've no objection; fine by me.
The next lesson puts the first character in your hand. It is the yì of yǒuyìsi.

The word, taken apart.
意见 (yìjiàn) is built from 意 (yì) 'intention' and 见 'to see', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'iH and kenH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'will, intention' and the second 'to look'; 'a suggestion' or 'an objection' for the pair is the modern word's own sense.

What you've built.
kànfǎ is your view of a matter; 意见 (yìjiàn) is what you put forward about it, and often it is a point against.
It is a noun: you have one, 有意见, or you have none, 没有意见.

Script — the left of 站 on top, then 日 and 心.
意 (yì).
Three pieces, and you write every one of them already.
The left of 站, five strokes, here on top and wider: the dot, the bar, a dot on the left, a left-falling stroke on the right, and a long horizontal. In 站 that last stroke rises; here it lies flat and runs the whole width.
日, four strokes, in the middle.
心, four strokes, at the foot: the left dot, the curve along the foot that hooks up, the middle dot, the right dot.
5 + 4 + 4 = thirteen.

[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 意 (yì)? (Thirteen.) Which character you write has the same top? (站, where it stands on the left.) What is at the foot? (心.)

Lesson 3 of 6.
我觉得… (wǒ juéde …) — I think.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
A word you have said for a long time, printed for the first time.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 意见, aloud — yìjiàn]
[your turn — recall: say juéde, to find; to think — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say rènwéi, to hold a view — R3]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Grammar Lens: 我觉得 and a whole sentence.
我觉得 and then a whole sentence gives your opinion: the sentence after it is what you think.
我觉得他说得对 wǒ juéde tā shuō de duì — I think he's right. 我觉得今天太冷了 wǒ juéde jīntiān tài lěng le — I find it too cold today.
Both characters of juéde, 觉 and 得, are ones you write, so 我觉得 prints in characters now.
To ask, put 你觉得 in front: nǐ juéde zěnmeyàng?, what do you think?
rènwéi says the same more formally, a view you hold and would defend; 我觉得 is the everyday one.

hǎoxiàng — as if; to look like.

[pause 2 seconds]

[once you have stopped driving — read: 我觉得今天太冷了, aloud — wǒ juéde jīntiān tài lěng le]
[your turn — recall: say xiàng, to be like — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 看来 — kànlai, it seems — R3]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: hǎoxiàng.
hǎoxiàng — "as if; to look like", a guess.
tā hǎoxiàng bìng le — He looks as if he's ill.
jīntiān hǎoxiàng yào xiàyǔ — It looks like rain today.
Its second character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
hǎoxiàng is built from 好 and xiàng 'to be like', both inherited from Middle Chinese, xawX and zjangX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'good, fine' and the second 'to be like'; 'as if' for the pair is the word's own sense.

What you've built.
我觉得 is your opinion. 看来 draws a conclusion from what you see. hǎoxiàng is softer still: a guess about how things look.
It goes before the verb, after the person: tā hǎoxiàng bìng le.

Lesson 5 of 6.
这么 (zhème) — so; like this.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: last lesson's guess, and a word that points the other way.
[your turn — recall: say hǎoxiàng, as if — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: 那么, aloud — nàme]

You'll want to know: 这么.
这么 — zhème — "so; this much; like this".
今天这么冷 jīntiān zhème lěng — It's so cold today!
今天天气这么好 jīntiān tiānqì zhème hǎo — The weather's so good today!
Both characters are ones you write.

The word, taken apart.
这么 (zhème) is built from 这 'this' and the ending 么 of 什么. No dictionary we use records an older form of 这 in this sense, or of the ending, so its history is not settled.

What you've built.
那么 points at something over there, as in 他没有你那么高. 这么 (zhème) points at what is here and now: 今天这么冷.
Both go before an adjective: 这么冷, 这么好.

Lesson 6 of 6.
我觉得这个意见很好 (wǒ juéde zhège yìjiàn hěn hǎo) — two speakers weigh a plan: a suggestion, an objection, a guess and an opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The chapter closes here, with a plan and two people deciding on it.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 今天这么冷, aloud — jīntiān zhème lěng]
[your turn — recall: say hǎoxiàng, as if — R2]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 我没有意见 — wǒ méiyǒu yìjiàn — R2]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

The exchange.
A: 明天我们去山上 好不好 míngtiān wǒmen qù shān shàng, hǎo bu hǎo — Shall we go up the mountain tomorrow? B: 我觉得这个意见很好 wǒ juéde zhège yìjiàn hěn hǎo — I think that's a good suggestion. A: 你们有意见吗 nǐmen yǒu yìjiàn ma — Any objections? B: wǒ méiyǒu yìjiàn, tā hǎoxiàng bù xiǎng qù — None from me. He looks as if he doesn't want to go. A: 为什么 wèishénme — Why? B: wǒ juéde tā tài lèi le — I think he's too tired. A: 今天这么冷 明天会冷吗 jīntiān zhème lěng, míngtiān huì lěng ma — It's so cold today. Will it be cold tomorrow? B: 我觉得不会 wǒ juéde bú huì — I don't think so.
我觉得 answers on its own too: 我觉得不会, I don't think it will. The 不 goes after 我觉得, with the verb it denies.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Why does B think he doesn't want to go?

What you've built — giving an opinion.
Four ways to say how you see it, from sure to unsure:
我觉得他说得对 wǒ juéde tā shuō de duì — your opinion.
我没有意见 wǒ méiyǒu yìjiàn — no objection.
tā hǎoxiàng bìng le — a guess.
今天这么冷 jīntiān zhème lěng — so cold, here and now.
Listen. Nǐ juéde míngtiān huì xiàyǔ ma?
Speak. Give your opinion of a plan for the weekend, and one objection.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 意见 (yìjiàn) — the left of 站 on top, then 日, then 心]
