Russian, chapter 532: Wallpaper and Drills.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
обои (obói) — wallpaper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to fold, then the Russian for to throw away.

You'll want to know: обои.
обои (obói) — "wallpaper".
Мы выбрали светлые обои для спальни. My výbrali svétlyye obói dlya spálni. We chose light wallpaper for the bedroom.
Plural only; genitive обоев.

The word, taken apart.
обои (obói) is usually traced to обить 'to cover, to upholster', from о- and бить 'to beat'; EtymDB lists обои (obói) without a source.

В выходные мы клеили обои. V vykhodnýye my kléili obói. At the weekend we were hanging wallpaper.
Imperfective; present клею, клеишь. Perfective склеить or поклеить.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 3 of 6.
сверлить (sverlít) — to drill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for wallpaper, then the Russian for to glue.

You'll want to know: сверлить.
сверлить (sverlít) — "to drill".
Сосед с утра сверлит стену. Soséd s utrá sverlít sténu. The neighbour has been drilling the wall since the morning.
Imperfective; perfective просверлить. The drill bit is a сверло.

The word, taken apart.
сверлить (sverlít) is built on сверло 'a drill bit', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic svьrdьlo (EtymDB); EtymDB has no entry for the verb.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: отвёртка.
отвёртка (otvyórtka) — "a screwdriver".
Папа ищет отвёртку в ящике. Pápa íshchet otvyórtku v yáshchike. Dad is looking for the screwdriver in the drawer.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
отвёртка (otvyórtka) is built on отвертеть, отвернуть 'to unscrew', from вертеть 'to turn'; EtymDB has no entry.
