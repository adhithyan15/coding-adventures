## You'll want to know: गूँधना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C520-AATA520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गूँधना** — *gū̃dhnā* — "to knead".

> माँ आटा गूँध रही है।
>
> *Māṁ āṭā gū̃dh rahī hai.*
>
> Mother is kneading the dough.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने आटा गूँधा, *usne āṭā gū̃dhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गूँधना** (*gū̃dhnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *guphati*, "strings together" (CDIAL 4205). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to plait, mix, knead".
