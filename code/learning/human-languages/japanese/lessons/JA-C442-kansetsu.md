## You'll want to know: かんせつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C442-INSIDE442-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かんせつ** — *kansetsu* — "a joint (of the body)".

> **さむい あさは ひざの かんせつが いたみます。** — *samui asa wa hiza no kansetsu ga itamimasu* — "On cold mornings my knee joints ache."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんせつ** (*kansetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "connection" (Middle Chinese *kwaen*) and *setsu*, "joint" (*tset*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
