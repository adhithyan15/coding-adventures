Marathi, chapter 97: Lake, Stone, Sand, Field, Star.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तलाव (talāv) — a lake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a forest, then the Marathi for a river.

You'll want to know: तलाव.
तलाव — talāv — "a lake".

The word, taken apart.
तलाव (talāv) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from taḍāga, "pool" (CDIAL 5635).
