# ಕಳೆದುಹೋಯಿತು (kaḷeduhōyitu) — when something goes wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C238-KRIYE238-02, KA-LEX-C202-KRIYE202-01, KA-LEX-C314-AUSADHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-175] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to get lost" (*kaḷeduhōgu*) and "to complain" (*dūru koḍu*). Then "a medicine". (*auṣadha*.) Which letter opens it? (**ಔ**, *au*.)

## You'll want to know: the bus got away
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C314-MISSED-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C87-REPLIES-03, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-03, KA-LEX-C102-THISWAY-02, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01] -->

> **ಅಯ್ಯೋ, ನನ್ನ ಬಸ್ ತಪ್ಪಿಹೋಯಿತು!**
>
> *ayyō, nanna bas tappihōyitu!* — "Oh no, I've missed my bus!"

**ತಪ್ಪಿಹೋಯಿತು** is two pieces: **ತಪ್ಪಿ**, the **-ಇ** form of the verb **ತಪ್ಪು**, "to slip, to go astray", the verb behind the noun **ತಪ್ಪು**, "a mistake"; and **ಹೋಯಿತು**, "it went". Word for word: "my bus slipped away". Kannada says the bus got away, not that you missed it.

## You'll want to know: lost, and reported
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C314-LOST-REPORT-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C238-KRIYE238-02, KA-LEX-C118-THINGS118-03, KA-LEX-C106-SOON-04, KA-LEX-C104-JOBS-03, KA-LEX-C202-KRIYE202-01, KA-LEX-C89-CARE-01, KA-LEX-C314-AUSADHA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C72-WANT-04] -->

> **ನನ್ನ ಚೀಲ ಕಳೆದುಹೋಯಿತು. ನನ್ನ ಫೋನ್ ಚೀಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು.**
>
> *nanna cīla kaḷeduhōyitu. nanna phōn cīladalli ittu.* — "My bag is lost. My phone was in the bag."

**ಕಳೆದುಹೋಯಿತು** is the "it" past of **ಕಳೆದುಹೋಗು**; **ಇತ್ತು** says where the phone was. **ಚೀಲದಲ್ಲಿ** (*cīladalli*, "in the bag"). Then the report:

> **ನಾನು ಪೊಲೀಸರಿಗೆ ದೂರು ಕೊಟ್ಟೆ.**
>
> *nānu polīsarige dūru koṭṭe.* — "I made a complaint to the police."

**ಪೊಲೀಸರಿಗೆ** (*polīsarige*, "to the police"): **ಪೊಲೀಸ್** with the respectful **-ರು** and the "to" ending.

And when the trouble is your health:

> **ನನಗೆ ಹುಷಾರಿಲ್ಲ. ನನಗೆ ಔಷಧ ಬೇಕು.**
>
> *nanage huṣārilla. nanage auṣadha bēku.* — "I'm not well. I need medicine."

**ಹುಷಾರಿಲ್ಲ** is **ಹುಷಾರು**, "careful, well", with **ಇಲ್ಲ**: "(I am) not well".
