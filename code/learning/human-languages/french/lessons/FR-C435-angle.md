## You'll want to know: l'angle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C435-PANNEAUX-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'angle** — "the corner; the angle".

> La pharmacie est à l'angle de la rue.
>
> *The chemist's is on the corner of the street.*

A masculine noun. *À l'angle de* — on the corner of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*angle* came through Old French from Latin *angulus* 'corner, angle' (EtymDB).
