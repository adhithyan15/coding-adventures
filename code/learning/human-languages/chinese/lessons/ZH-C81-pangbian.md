## You'll want to know: pángbiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C81-CITY81-05]; assesses=[] -->

*pángbiān* — "side; beside".

> *wǒ jiā pángbiān yǒu yí ge shāngdiàn* — There's a shop next to my home.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pángbiān* is built from páng 'side' and biān 'edge', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bang* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
