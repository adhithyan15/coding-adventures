## You'll want to know: равенство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C367-RAVENSTVO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**равенство** (*rávenstvo*) — "equality".

> Мы верим в равенство всех людей.
>
> *My vérim v rávenstvo vsekh lyudéy.*
>
> We believe in the equality of all people.

Neuter. *Равенство мужчин и женщин* — equality of men and women.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**равенство** is built on *равный* "equal", the Church Slavonic twin of native *ровный* "even, level"; both go back to Proto-Slavic *\*orvĭnŭ*. The *ра-* is the Church Slavonic shape, *ро-* the native one.
