## You'll want to know: die Trennung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C300-SCHEIDUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Trennung** — "the separation, the break-up".

> Die Trennung war für beide schwer.
>
> *The separation was hard for both.*

Plural: *die Trennungen*. Built on *trennen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Trennung* is built on *trennen* "to separate", from Old High German *trennen*, Proto-Germanic *trannijaną* (EtymDB).
