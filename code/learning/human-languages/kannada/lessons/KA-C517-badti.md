## You'll want to know: ಬಡ್ತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C517-PRAGATI517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಡ್ತಿ** — *baḍti* — "a promotion".

> ಈ ವರ್ಷ ನನ್ನ ಅಕ್ಕನಿಗೆ ಬಡ್ತಿ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು.
>
> *ī varṣa nanna akkanige baḍti sikkitu.*
>
> This year my elder sister got a promotion.

ಬಡ್ತಿ ಕೊಡು, to promote; ಬಡ್ತಿ ಪಡೆ, to be promoted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಡ್ತಿ** (*baḍti*) is usually traced to Hindustani *baṛhtī*, "growth, increase"; the path into Kannada is not settled.
