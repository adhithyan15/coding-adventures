## You'll want to know: aufhängen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C309-SCHMUECKEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aufhängen** — "to hang up".

> Die Kinder hängten Bilder an der Wand auf.
>
> *The children hung pictures on the wall.*

Separable: *er hängt auf*. On the phone, to hang up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aufhängen* joins *auf* "up" and *hängen* "to hang", an inherited Germanic verb and a cousin of English *hang*; EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
