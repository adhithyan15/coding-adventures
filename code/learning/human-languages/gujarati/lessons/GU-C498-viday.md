## You'll want to know: વિદાય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C498-VIDAY498-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વિદાય** — *vidāy* — "a farewell; the bride's send-off".

> દીકરીની વિદાય વખતે માતા રડી પડી.
>
> *dīkrīnī vidāy vakhte mātā raḍī paḍī.*
>
> At her daughter's send-off the mother burst into tears.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિદાય** (*vidāy*) is recorded by Monier-Williams as Sanskrit *vidāya*, "permission to go away, dismissal with good wishes", with a note that in this sense it is perhaps not a Sanskrit word at all and a comparison with Arabic *wadāʿ*, "farewell"; its history is not settled.
