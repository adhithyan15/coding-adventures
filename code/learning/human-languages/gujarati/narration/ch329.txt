Gujarati, chapter 329: Lights and Colours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રંગોળી (rangoḷī) — a rangoli (a coloured floor pattern).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to light, then the Gujarati for incense.

You'll want to know: રંગોળી.
રંગોળી — rangoḷī — "a rangoli (a coloured floor pattern)".
દિવાળીમાં મીરાએ આંગણામાં રંગોળી બનાવી. divāḷīmā̃ Mīrāe ā̃gṇāmā̃ rangoḷī banāvī. At Diwali Mira made a rangoli in the courtyard.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રંગોળી (rangoḷī) has a history that is not settled: Monier-Williams records a Sanskrit raṅgavallī, built from raṅga, colour, and vallī, a creeper, but only as the name of a plant used at sacrifices. It is a pattern of coloured powder laid on the floor at a doorway for a festival; it is feminine: રંગોળી બનાવી.

Lesson 2 of 5.
શણગારવું (śaṇgārvũ) — to adorn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for incense, then the Gujarati for a rangoli.

You'll want to know: શણગારવું.
શણગારવું — śaṇgārvũ — "to adorn".
લોકોએ આખું ગામ શણગાર્યું. lokoe ākhũ gām śaṇgāryũ. People decorated the whole village.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘર શણગાર્યું, teṇe ghar śaṇgāryũ.

The word, taken apart.
comes from śṛṅgārayati, "adorns", a form CDIAL reconstructs on śṛṅgāra, finery (CDIAL 12593). સજાવવું is to decorate; શણગારવું (śaṇgārvũ) is to dress up richly, a bride or a whole town.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઝગમગવું (jhagmagvũ) — to sparkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a rangoli, then the Gujarati for to adorn.

You'll want to know: ઝગમગવું.
ઝગમગવું — jhagmagvũ — "to sparkle".
દિવાળીની રાત્રે આખું શહેર દીવાથી ઝગમગતું હતું. divāḷīnī rātre ākhũ śaher dīvāthī jhagmagtũ hatũ. On Diwali night the whole city sparkled with lamps.
Past (it …): તે શહેર ઝગમગ્યું, te śaher jhagmagyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઝગમગવું (jhagmagvũ) is an imitative word: CDIAL sets the Hindi cousin jagmag, "glitter", under jag, "glitter", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5076), but does not list the Gujarati verb. The doubled sound jhag-mag imitates light that flickers and shines.

મીરાએ ફૂલની માળા બનાવી. Mīrāe phūlnī māḷā banāvī. Mira made a garland of flowers.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
માળા (māḷā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit mālā, "a wreath, a garland" (CDIAL 10092). It is feminine: ફૂલની માળા.

Lesson 5 of 5.
શોભાયાત્રા (śobhāyātrā) — a procession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sparkle, then the Gujarati for a garland.

You'll want to know: શોભાયાત્રા.
શોભાયાત્રા — śobhāyātrā — "a procession".
સાંજે ગામમાં મોટી શોભાયાત્રા નીકળી. sā̃je gāmmā̃ moṭī śobhāyātrā nīkḷī. In the evening a big procession went through the village.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શોભાયાત્રા (śobhāyātrā) is built from Sanskrit śobhā, "splendour", and yātrā, "a going, a journey" (Monier-Williams), the word behind યાત્રા: a splendid going through the streets. A procession નીકળે છે, sets out.
