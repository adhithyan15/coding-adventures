## You'll want to know: guìhuā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C342-AUTUMN342-04]; assesses=[] -->

*guìhuā* — "osmanthus flowers".

> *qiūtiān de shíhou guìhuā kāi le* — In the autumn the osmanthus came into flower.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guìhuā* is built from *guì* and 花 'a flower', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwejH* and *xwae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which describes the first as a tree that grows in groves and stays green in winter and summer.
