## You'll want to know: der Köder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C502-ANGELN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Köder** — "bait".

> Als Köder nimmt er Würmer.
>
> *He uses worms as bait.*

Plural: *die Köder*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Köder* is inherited from Middle High German *querder* and Old High German *querdar*, from Proto-Germanic *kwerþraz* "bait" (EtymDB).
