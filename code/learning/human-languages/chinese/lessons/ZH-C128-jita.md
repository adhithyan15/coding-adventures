## You'll want to know: jítā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C128-MUSIC128-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jítā* — "guitar".

> *wǒ xiǎng xué jítā* — I'd like to learn the guitar.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jítā* is usually traced to English *guitar*; its two characters were chosen for their sound, the first a word for lucky and the second the 他 of 'he'.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for what I like.
