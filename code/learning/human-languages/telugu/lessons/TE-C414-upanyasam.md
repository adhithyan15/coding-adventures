## You'll want to know: ఉపన్యాసం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C414-UPANYASAM414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపన్యాసం** — *upanyāsaṁ* — "a lecture; a speech".

> ప్రొఫెసరు ఉపన్యాసం రెండు గంటలు సాగింది.
>
> *prophesaru upanyāsaṁ reṇḍu gaṇṭalu sāgindi.*
>
> The professor’s lecture went on for two hours.

Plural: ఉపన్యాసాలు, *upanyāsālu*.

ఉపన్యాసం ఇచ్చు, to give a lecture or a speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపన్యాసం** (*upanyāsaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *upanyāsa*, "placing near; bringing forward, speaking of, mention" (Monier-Williams).
