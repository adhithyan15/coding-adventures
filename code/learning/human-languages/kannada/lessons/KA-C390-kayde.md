# ಕಾಯ್ದೆ (kāyde) — an act of law

## You'll want to know: ಕಾಯ್ದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C390-KANUNU390-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾಯ್ದೆ** — *kāyde* — "an act of law".

> ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಹೊಸ ಕಾಯ್ದೆ ತಂದಿತು.
>
> *sarkāra hosa kāyde tanditu.*
>
> The government brought in a new act.

A law passed under a name: ಕಾಯ್ದೆ. The law in general: ಕಾನೂನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾಯ್ದೆ** (*kāyde*) is a loan from Arabic *qāʿida*, "a rule, a base", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.
