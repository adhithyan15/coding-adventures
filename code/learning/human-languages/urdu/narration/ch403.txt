Urdu, chapter 403: Inns and Lodgings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سرائے (sarāe) — an inn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to put (someone) to sleep, then the Urdu for to wake (someone) up.

You'll want to know: سرائے.
سرائے — sarāe — "an inn".
پرانے شہر میں ایک سرائے ہے۔ purāne shahr meṅ ek sarāe hai. There is an inn in the old city.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
سرائے (sarāe) is usually traced to Persian sarāy, "a house, a palace"; in Urdu it is an inn where travellers stop for the night.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قیام (qiyām) — a stay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to wake (someone) up, then the Urdu for an inn.

You'll want to know: قیام.
قیام — qiyām — "a stay".
سرائے میں ہمارا قیام تین دن کا تھا۔ sarāe meṅ hamārā qiyām tīn din kā thā. We stayed at the inn for three days.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
قیام (qiyām) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qiyām, "standing, remaining"; in Urdu it is a stay somewhere.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ٹھکانا (ṭhikānā) — a place to stay; whereabouts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an inn, then the Urdu for a stay.

You'll want to know: ٹھکانا.
ٹھکانا — ṭhikānā — "a place to stay; whereabouts".
رات کے لیے ہمیں ٹھکانا ملا۔ rāt ke liye hameṅ ṭhikānā milā. We found somewhere to stay for the night.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ٹھکانا (ṭhikānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭhikānā, "a station", under ṭhīkka, "firm, right", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5503); ٹھیک comes from the same source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بچھانا (bichhānā) — to spread out (a sheet); to make up (a bed).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a stay, then the Urdu for a place to stay.

You'll want to know: بچھانا.
بچھانا — bichhānā — "to spread out (a sheet); to make up (a bed)".
سرائے والے نے بستر پر نئی چادر بچھائی۔ sarāe vāle ne bistar par naī chādar bichhāī. The innkeeper spread a fresh sheet on the bed.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چٹائی بچھائی, us ne chaṭāī bichhāī.

The word, taken apart.
بچھانا (bichhānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bichānā, "to spread", under vicchādayati, "spreads out", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11692).
