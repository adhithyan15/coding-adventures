## You'll want to know: دم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C470-BREATH470-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دم** — *dam* — "breath; stamina".

> سیڑھی چڑھ کر میرا دم پھول گیا۔
>
> *sīṛhī chaṛh kar merā dam phūl gayā.*
>
> I was out of breath after climbing the stairs.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دم** (*dam*) is usually traced to Persian *dam*, "breath".
