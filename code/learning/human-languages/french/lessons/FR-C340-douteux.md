## You'll want to know: douteux
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C340-FIABLE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**douteux** — "doubtful, dubious".

> Ce restaurant a l'air douteux : il n'y a personne.
>
> *This restaurant looks dubious: there is nobody there.*

Feminine *douteuse*. *Il est douteux que* takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*douteux* is built from *doute* "doubt" and the suffix *-eux* (EtymDB). *doute* comes from *douter*, inherited from Latin *dubitāre* "to waver, to doubt" (Lewis & Short).
