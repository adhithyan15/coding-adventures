## You'll want to know: ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C391-SAMANATE391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡು** — *tāratamya māḍu* — "to discriminate".

> ಆ ಅಂಗಡಿಯವನು ಗ್ರಾಹಕರಲ್ಲಿ ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *ā aṅgaḍiyavanu grāhakaralli tāratamya māḍuvudilla.*
>
> That shopkeeper does not discriminate among customers.

Past (I …): ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *tāratamya māḍide*.

ತಾರತಮ್ಯ alone is discrimination.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡು** (*tāratamya māḍu*) is built from Sanskrit *tāratamya*, "gradation, difference" (Monier-Williams), with *māḍu*, "to do": to rank people as more and less.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
