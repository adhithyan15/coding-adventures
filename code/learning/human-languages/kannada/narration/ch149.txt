Kannada, chapter 149: To get down, To join, To wear, To call, To count.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಇಳಿ (iḷi) — to get down, to get off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to teach, then the Kannada for to jump.

You'll want to know: ಇಳಿ.
ಇಳಿ — iḷi — "to get down, to get off".

The word, taken apart.
ಇಳಿ (iḷi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil iḻi, Telugu ḍigu and Malayalam iḻika (DEDR 502). DEDR gives the older Kannada iḻi, "to come down, descend": the ḻ has become ḷ.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: iḷi, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say iḷi]

[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for to teach, then the Kannada for to jump, then say iḷi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಇಳಿ (iḷi) mean? ("To get down, to get off".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಎಣಿಸು (eṇisu) — to count.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to wear, then the Kannada for to call.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the ordinals past eight: ಒಂಬತ್ತನೆಯ, ಹತ್ತನೆಯ, ಹನ್ನೊಂದನೆಯ. Then the spoken ಮೊದಲನೇ.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the sign form ೧ನೇ]

You'll want to know: ಎಣಿಸು.
ಎಣಿಸು — eṇisu — "to count".

The word, taken apart.
ಎಣಿಸು (eṇisu) is native Dravidian, of the root for "to count, think", and DEDR 793 lists eṇisu, "to count", itself; the entry also has Tamil eṇ, Telugu ennu and Malayalam eṇ.
