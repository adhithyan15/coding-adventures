## You'll want to know: sulcus, sulcī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C474-ARO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sulcus, sulcī** — "a furrow; a trench".

> Agricola sulcōs rēctōs in agrō facit.
>
> *The farmer makes straight furrows in the field.*

Second declension, masculine: *sulcus, sulcī m.* Also the wake of a ship. English keeps *sulcus* in anatomy for a groove.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sulcus* is inherited from PIE *solk-o-* 'furrow' (EtymDB); Lewis & Short compare Greek *holkos* 'furrow'.
