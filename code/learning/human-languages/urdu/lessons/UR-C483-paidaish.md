## You'll want to know: پیدائش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C483-BABY483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پیدائش** — *paidāish* — "birth".

> بیٹی کی پیدائش پر سب خوش تھے۔
>
> *beṭī kī paidāish par sab khush the.*
>
> Everyone was happy when the daughter was born.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیدائش** (*paidāish*) is usually traced to Persian *paidāish*, "birth", the noun of *paidā*, "born".
