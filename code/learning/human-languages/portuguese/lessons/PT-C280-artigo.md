## You'll want to know: o artigo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C280-PUBLICAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o artigo** — "the article".

> Li um artigo interessante sobre a saúde.
>
> *I read an interesting article about health.*

In grammar too: *o* and *a* are *artigos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*artigo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *articulus* "a small joint, a part", a little *artus* "a joint" (Lewis & Short).
