## You'll want to know: pugillārēs, pugillārium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C551-CAPS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pugillārēs, pugillārium** — "writing tablets".

> In pugillāribus pauca verba scrībit.
>
> *He writes a few words on his writing tablets.*

Masculine, third declension, plural. Small wax tablets that fitted in the hand, from *pugillus* — a little fist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pugillārēs** is built on *pugillus* "a little fist, handful", from *pugnus* "fist" (Lewis & Short): tablets held in the hand.
