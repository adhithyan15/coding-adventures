## You'll want to know: ہلانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C401-ISTIQBAL401-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ہلانا** — *hilānā* — "to wave; to shake".

> دوستوں نے ہمیں دیکھ کر ہاتھ ہلائے۔
>
> *dostoṅ ne hameṅ dekh kar hāth hilāe.*
>
> Our friends saw us and waved.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے ہاتھ ہلایا, *us ne hāth hilāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہلانا** (*hilānā*) is built on *hilnā*, "to shake", which CDIAL lists under *hillati*, "moves, shakes", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 14120): this is the form for shaking something.
