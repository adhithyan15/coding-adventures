## You'll want to know: داخلہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C385-DAKHILA385-04]; assesses=[] -->

**داخلہ** — *dākhilā* — "admission (to a school or college)".

> اگر نمبر اچھے ہوئے تو کالج میں داخلہ ملے گا۔
>
> *agar nambar achchhe hue to kālij meṅ dākhilā mile gā.*
>
> If the marks are good, there will be a place at college.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**داخلہ** (*dākhilā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dākhil*, "entering, inside": a way in.
