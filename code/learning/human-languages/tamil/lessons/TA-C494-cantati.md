## You'll want to know: சந்ததி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C494-MARAPU494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்ததி** — *cantati* — "offspring; descendants".

> நம் சந்ததிக்காக இயற்கையைக் காக்க வேண்டும்.
>
> *nam cantatikkāka iyaṟkaiyaik kākka vēṇṭum.*
>
> We must protect nature for our descendants.

A collective noun for all who come after.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்ததி** (*cantati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃtati*, "continuity, a line of descent" (Monier-Williams).
