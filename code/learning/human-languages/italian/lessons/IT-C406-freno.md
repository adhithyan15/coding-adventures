## You'll want to know: il freno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C406-GUIDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il freno** — "the brake".

> Il freno della bici non funziona.
>
> *The brake on the bike does not work.*

*Il freno a mano* is the handbrake. Figuratively, *senza freni* means without restraint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*freno* is inherited from Latin *frēnum* 'bridle, bit' (EtymDB): first the thing that held back a horse.
