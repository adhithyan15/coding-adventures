## You'll want to know: ausrutschen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C289-STURZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ausrutschen** — "to slip".

> Sie rutschte auf dem nassen Boden aus.
>
> *She slipped on the wet floor.*

Separable, Perfekt with *sein*: *Ich bin ausgerutscht*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ausrutschen* joins *aus* and *rutschen* "to slide", which is usually traced to Middle High German *rutschen*; EtymDB lists no parent.
