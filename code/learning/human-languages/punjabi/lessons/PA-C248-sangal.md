## You'll want to know: ਸੰਗਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C248-GHR248-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੰਗਲ** — *saṅgal* — "a chain".

> ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਭਾਰੀ ਸੰਗਲ ਹੈ।
>
> *darvāze utte ikk bhārī saṅgal hai.*
>
> There is a heavy chain on the door.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੰਗਲ** (*saṅgal*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śṛṅkhala*, "chain"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *saṅgal*, "chain" (CDIAL 12580).
