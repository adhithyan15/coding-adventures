Lesson 4 of 5.
परवा (parvā) — the day after tomorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for dawn, then the Marathi for midnight.

You'll want to know: परवा.
परवा — parvā — "the day after tomorrow".

The word, taken apart.
परवा (parvā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from paraśvas, "the day after tomorrow" (CDIAL 7799j). It is para, "beyond", and śvas, "tomorrow". CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "the day after tomorrow or before yesterday".

Lesson 5 of 5.
सोमवार (somvār) — Monday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for midnight, then the Marathi for the day after tomorrow.

You'll want to know: सोमवार.
सोमवार — somvār — "Monday".

The word, taken apart.
सोमवार (somvār) is a Sanskrit compound, a tatsama: soma, "the moon", and vāra, "day of the week" (CDIAL 11547). Monday is the moon's day, as in English.
