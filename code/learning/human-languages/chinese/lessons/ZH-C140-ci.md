## You'll want to know: cì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C140-ORDER140-03]; assesses=[] -->

*cì* — "time (one occurrence)".

> *zhè shì wǒ dìyī cì lái Běijīng* — This is my first time in Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cì* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tshijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'order, sequence' and 'a halt on a journey'.
