## You'll want to know: exspīrō, exspīrāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C329-MORS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**exspīrō, exspīrāre** — "to breathe one's last".

> Senex in lectō exspīrāvit.
>
> *The old man breathed his last in bed.*

*exspīrō, exspīrāre, exspīrāvī, exspīrātum*: *ex* + *spīrō* (taught), to breathe out. English *expire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**exspīrō** is built on *spīrō*, "to breathe", with *ex-*, "out" (EtymDB): to breathe out the last breath. English *expire* comes from it.
