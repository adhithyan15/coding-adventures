## You'll want to know: आनंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C453-ANAND453-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आनंद** — *ānand* — "joy; bliss".

> बच्चों के साथ खेलकर दादा को बहुत आनंद आया।
>
> *baccoṁ ke sāth khelkar dādā ko bahut ānand āyā.*
>
> Grandfather had a wonderful time playing with the children.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आनंद** (*ānand*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ānanda*, "happiness, joy" (Monier-Williams). *ānand ānā*, "joy comes", is how Hindi says someone enjoyed a thing.
