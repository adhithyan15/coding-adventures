Punjabi, chapter 288: The Hot Season Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਧੁੱਪ (dhupp) — sunshine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to drip, then the Punjabi for mud.

You'll want to know: ਧੁੱਪ.
ਧੁੱਪ — dhupp — "sunshine".
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਤੇਜ਼ ਧੁੱਪ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ। kall tez dhupp hovegī. Tomorrow there will be strong sunshine.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਧੁੱਪ (dhupp) is inherited from a reconstructed dhuppā, "sunshine"; CDIAL lists Punjabi dhupp under it (CDIAL 6825).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਲੂ (lū) — a hot wind; the summer heatwave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for mud, then the Punjabi for sunshine.

You'll want to know: ਲੂ.
ਲੂ — lū — "a hot wind; the summer heatwave".
ਲੂ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਬਾਹਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ। lū vich asīṁ bāhar nahīṁ jānde hāṁ. We do not go out in the hot wind.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੂ (lū) goes back to a reconstructed lūṣā, "burning"; CDIAL lists Punjabi only lūh, "burnt, scalded", under it; the sense "hot wind" is the one it gives for Lahnda lō (CDIAL 11099).

ਤਪਣਾ — tapṇā — "to blaze with heat; to become very hot".
ਜੂਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਧਰਤੀ ਤਪੇਗੀ। jūn vich dhartī tapegī. In June the earth will blaze with heat.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੱਥਰ ਤਪਿਆ, oh patthar tapiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਪਣਾ (tapṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit tapyati, "is hot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi tapṇā, "to be heated", under it (CDIAL 5684).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਸੋਕਾ (sokā) — a drought.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to blaze with heat, then the Punjabi for the hot season.

You'll want to know: ਸੋਕਾ.
ਸੋਕਾ — sokā — "a drought".
ਜੇ ਮੀਂਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਵੇਗਾ ਤਾਂ ਸੋਕਾ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ। je mīṁh nahīṁ āvegā tāṁ sokā hovegā. If the rain does not come, there will be a drought.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੋਕਾ (sokā) is inherited from a reconstructed śauṣkya, "dryness"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sok and sokkā, "dryness, drought", under it (CDIAL 12652).
