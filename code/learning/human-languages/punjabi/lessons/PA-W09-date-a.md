# Fictional date A — one box at a time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->

Trace the three empty boxes with a finger: day / month / year. The slash marks
stay in place.

## Reading — assemble one safe example
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-DATE-A-01]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-MONTH-MAP-01] -->

Practice card A says “the fifteenth of January, two thousand twenty-five.”
It is fictional. The day is **੧੫**, January is **੦੧**, and the four year digits
are **੨੦੨੫**. Put the pieces together: **੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫**. Touch and read only one
piece at a time; no real birthday is requested.

## Guided Practice — copy in three passes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-A-01, PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy the day **੧੫**, add a slash, copy month **੦੧**, add a slash, then copy the
year **੨੦੨੫**. Check that the two slash marks divide two, two, and four digits.
