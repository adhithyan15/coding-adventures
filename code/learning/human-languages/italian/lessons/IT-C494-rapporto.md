## You'll want to know: il rapporto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C494-SEPARARSI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il rapporto** — "relationship; report".

> Ho un buon rapporto con i miei genitori.
>
> *I have a good relationship with my parents.*

At work, *un rapporto* can also be a report.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rapporto* is built on *rapportare* "to bring back, to report", from *ri-*, *a-* and *portare* "to carry", inherited from Latin *portāre* (Lewis & Short).
