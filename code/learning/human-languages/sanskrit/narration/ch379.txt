Sanskrit, chapter 379: More Than Enough.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
अधिकः (adhikaḥ) — more; additional.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for falling short, then the Sanskrit for dwindles.

You'll want to know: अधिकः.
अधिकः — adhikaḥ — "more; additional".
यदि अधिकं धनम् अस्ति, तर्हि दरिद्राय ददातु। yadi adhikaṃ dhanam asti, tarhi daridrāya dadātu. If there is extra money, give it to a poor man.

The word, taken apart.
अधिकः (adhikaḥ) MW gives adhika "additional" and "surpassing in number or quantity, more numerous"; MW gives no derivation here, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

पर्याप्तः — paryāptaḥ — "sufficient".
यदि अन्नं पर्याप्तम्, तर्हि अतिथिः अपि खादिष्यति। yadi annaṃ paryāptam, tarhi atithiḥ api khādiṣyati. If there is enough food, the guest will eat too.

The word, taken apart.
पर्याप्तः (paryāptaḥ) is built on pari, "around", and the root √āp, "to reach, to obtain"; MW gives pary-āpta "sufficient for" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 6.
विपुलः (vipulaḥ) — ample; abundant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sufficient, then the Sanskrit for plentiful.

You'll want to know: विपुलः.
विपुलः — vipulaḥ — "ample; abundant".
यदि धनं विपुलम्, तर्हि ग्रामः सुखी भविष्यति। yadi dhanaṃ vipulam, tarhi grāmaḥ sukhī bhaviṣyati. If there is ample money, the village will be happy.

The word, taken apart.
विपुलः (vipulaḥ) is built on vi and pula, which MW takes to be probably the same as pura, "full"; MW gives vi-pula "large, extensive, abundant" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
वर्धयति (vardhayati) — increases (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for plentiful, then the Sanskrit for ample.

You'll want to know: वर्धयति.
वर्धयति — vardhayati — "increases (something)".
यदि वणिक् मूल्यं वर्धयति, तर्हि जनाः अल्पं क्रीणन्ति। yadi vaṇik mūlyaṃ vardhayati, tarhi janāḥ alpaṃ krīṇanti. If the merchant raises the price, people buy less.
Past (he …): सः मूल्यं वर्धितवान्, saḥ mūlyaṃ vardhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
वर्धयति (vardhayati) comes from the root √vṛdh, "to grow", in its causative form; MW gives vardhita "increased, grown, augmented" (MW).
