## You'll want to know: shuāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C187-BADDAY187-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shuāi* — "to have a fall; to drop (and break)".

> *tā shuāi le shǒu hěn téng* — He had a fall, and his hand really hurts.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuāi* No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
