# दुखाना (dukhānā) — to hurt someone's feelings

## You'll want to know: दुखाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C455-GAM455-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दुखाना** — *dukhānā* — "to hurt someone's feelings".

> मीरा ने कड़वी बात कहकर अपनी माँ का दिल दुखाया।
>
> *Mīrā ne kaṛvī bāt kahkar apnī māṁ kā dil dukhāyā.*
>
> Mira hurt her mother's feelings with a harsh remark.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दिल दुखाया, *usne dil dukhāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुखाना** (*dukhānā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *dukhānā*, the causative, beside *dukhnā*, "to ache", under Sanskrit *duḥkhati*, "pains" (CDIAL 6376). *dil dukhānā*, "to make the heart ache", is the usual phrase.
