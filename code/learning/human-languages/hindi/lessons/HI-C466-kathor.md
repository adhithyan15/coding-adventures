## You'll want to know: कठोर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C466-DAYA466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कठोर** — *kaṭhor* — "harsh; stern".

> पिता की बात कठोर थी, लेकिन सही थी।
>
> *pitā kī bāt kaṭhor thī, lekin sahī thī.*
>
> Father's words were harsh, but they were right.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कठोर** (*kaṭhor*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *kaṭhora*, "hard, solid, stiff" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for hard words and hard hearts.
