# मी वाचत होतो (mī vācat hoto) — what was going on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-NAHI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I won't come tomorrow". (*mī udyā yeṇār nāhī*.) Then "I was at home" and "I read a book". (*mī gharī hoto*, *mī pustak vāclaṁ*.)

[PAUSE 1s] The last is an event: it happened and it was over. A story also needs what was going on at the time.

## You'll want to know: -त होतो, 'was doing'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-VACHNE, MR-LEX-RATRA, MR-LEX-NAU, MR-LEX-VAJTA, MR-GRAMMAR-NEGATE-NAHI] -->

> **काल रात्री नऊ वाजता मी पुस्तक वाचत होतो.**
>
> *kāl rātrī naū vājtā mī pustak vācat hoto.* — "At nine last night I was reading a book."

Two pieces. **वाचत** is the bare **-त** shape you know from **आवडत नाही**; **होतो**, "I was", carries the person and the gender:

- **ती** **वाचत होती** (*vācat hotī*) — she was reading
- **तो** **काम करत होता** (*kām karat hotā*) — he was working
- **आम्ही** **बोलत होतो** (*bolat hoto*) — we were talking
- **पाऊस पडत होता** (*pāūs paḍat hotā*) — it was raining

This past follows the doer even with an object: **ती पुस्तक वाचत होती**. No **-ने** here.

## Grammar Lens: the scene and the event
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C278-SCENE-EVENT-01]; assesses=[MR-JOIN-JEVHA, MR-LEX-MITRA, MR-LEX-YENE] -->

> **मी वाचत होतो. तेव्हा मीरा आली.**
>
> *mī vācat hoto. tevhā mīrā ālī.* — "I was reading. At that moment, Meera came."

The first sentence is the **scene**: it was going on. **आली** is the **event**: it happened once and cut into the scene. **तेव्हा**, the "then" of **जेव्हा … तेव्हा**, hooks one onto the other. A told story moves on this rhythm.
