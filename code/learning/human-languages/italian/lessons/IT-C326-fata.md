## You'll want to know: la fata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C326-MAGIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la fata** — "the fairy".

> La fata ha aiutato la ragazza.
>
> *The fairy helped the girl.*

*Una fata madrina* — a fairy godmother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fata* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *Fāta* "goddess of fate" (EtymDB), built on Latin *fātum* "destiny".
