## You'll want to know: جاع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C517-ADAT517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جاع** — *jāʿa* — "to get hungry".

> جاع الأطفال في الطريق.
>
> *jāʿa al-aṭfāl fī aṭ-ṭarīq.*
>
> The children got hungry on the way.

Present: يجوع, *yajūʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جاع** (*jāʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-w-ʿ*, "hunger" (Lane). Jūʿ, hunger, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
