# Return to the small marker

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-AGENT-NE-210, PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-FORM-KITA-190] -->

[YOU HEAR: *Aman ne kamm kītā*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ**, "Aman did the work".

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-AGENT-NE-210, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-05] -->

After a name, the doer of a past like **ਕੀਤਾ** takes the small marker **ਨੇ**.
After **ਮੈਂ** it takes nothing: **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ**. Hold one more verb ready:
**ਖ਼ਰੀਦਣਾ**, "to buy".

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-AGENT-NE-210, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-05] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *Aman did the work*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *I drank tea*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *to buy*] Say **ਖ਼ਰੀਦਣਾ**.
