## You'll want to know: interrogare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C296-MOVENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**interrogare** — "to question; to test orally (at school)".

> La polizia ha interrogato il sospettato per ore.
>
> *The police questioned the suspect for hours.*

At school, *interrogare* is to test a pupil out loud.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*interrogare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *interrogāre* "to ask, to question" (Lewis & Short), from *inter* and *rogāre* "to ask".
