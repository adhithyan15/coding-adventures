# ग — the other way round

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-137] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: **ढ** — how many pen lifts did it take?

[PAUSE 2s] That one came before any word that used it. This one comes **long
after**.

## Script you'll notice: ग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-138]; assesses=[] -->

**ग** — *ga*.

What it is made of:

- a counterclockwise left loop joined to an ascending stem
- a top-to-bottom right stem
- the top shirorekhā

## Script: you have been reading it for twenty-two chapters
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-138] -->

Every one of these was yours before this page:

- **स्वागत** — the *welcome* you were greeted with early on
- **मिलेंगे** — *we will meet*
- **मंगलवार** — Tuesday

So this is the opposite arrangement from the last two letters. They arrived
before their words; **this one is late, and there is no honest way to dress
that up**.

The book taught its opening by **copying whole words** — a real sentence in two
chapters, which is worth a great deal — and the cost is that the letters inside
those words went unnamed. You have been recognising **ग** by its company.
Now you get the letter itself.

## Writing: ग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-138] -->

- **1.** start at the loop's upper-right junction and sweep left over the top, down around the left side, back to the lower-right junction, and continue up the joined stem to the headline
- **2.** lift and draw the right stem top-to-bottom
- **3.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 2.** The pen comes up 2 times and no more.

> Verified three-stroke teaching form; everyday handwriting may join or simplify the body differently.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Opiaterein, ‘Deva-ग-order.gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 9 May 2009.
