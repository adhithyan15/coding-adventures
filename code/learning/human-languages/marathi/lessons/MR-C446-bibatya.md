# बिबट्या (bibaṭyā) — a leopard

## You'll want to know: बिबट्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C446-ABHAYARANYA446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बिबट्या** — *bibaṭyā* — "a leopard".

> रात्री गावाजवळ बिबट्या दिसला.
>
> *rātrī gāvājavaḷ bibaṭyā dislā.*
>
> A leopard was seen near the village at night.

Masculine.

Before an ending बिबट्या stays as it is: बिबट्याला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिबट्या** (*bibaṭyā*) is the Marathi word for the leopard; its origin is not settled.
