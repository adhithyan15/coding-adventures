Portuguese, chapter 375: Na escola.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a escola secundária — the secondary school.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for available, then the Portuguese for the commitment.

You'll want to know: a escola secundária.
a escola secundária — "the secondary school".
A minha irmã anda na escola secundária. My sister goes to secondary school.
In Portugal, years 10 to 12; in speech a secundária.

The word, taken apart.
escola is inherited from Latin schola "leisure given to learning, a school" (Lewis & Short), from Greek skholḗ, and secundária comes from Latin secundārius "of the second rank", on secundus "following".

Ele frequenta aulas de inglês à noite. He attends English classes in the evening.

frequentar is a learned borrowing from Latin frequentāre "to visit or resort to frequently" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), on frequēns "crowded, frequent".

Lesson 3 of 5.
a falta — the absence (from class); the lack; the foul.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the secondary school, then the Portuguese for to attend.

You'll want to know: a falta.
a falta — "the absence (from class); the lack; the foul".
Tive uma falta porque cheguei atrasado. I was marked absent because I arrived late.
Fazer falta — to be missed or needed; sem falta — without fail. In football, a foul.

The word, taken apart.
falta is built on faltar "to be missing", usually traced to Vulgar Latin fallita, from Latin fallere "to deceive, to disappoint" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
a pauta — the list of marks; the musical stave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to attend, then the Portuguese for the absence.

You'll want to know: a pauta.
a pauta — "the list of marks; the musical stave".
As notas já estão na pauta. The marks are already on the list.
A European school word: the posted list of final marks. Also a stave in music.

The word, taken apart.
pauta goes back to Latin pacta "agreements" (EtymDB), the plural of pactum "an agreement" (Lewis & Short): first a written list of terms, then any ruled list.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a disciplina — the school subject; discipline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the absence, then the Portuguese for the list of marks.

You'll want to know: a disciplina.
a disciplina — "the school subject; discipline".
A minha disciplina preferida é a história. My favourite subject is history.
In Portugal uma disciplina is a school subject; also discipline, order.

The word, taken apart.
disciplina is a learned borrowing from Latin disciplīna "instruction, teaching" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on discipulus "pupil", from discere "to learn".
