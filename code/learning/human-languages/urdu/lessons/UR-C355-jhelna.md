# جھیلنا (jhelnā) — to endure; to put up with

## You'll want to know: جھیلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C355-TARIF355-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جھیلنا** — *jhelnā* — "to endure; to put up with".

> کسانوں نے سخت سردی جھیلی۔
>
> *kisānoṅ ne sakht sardī jhelī.*
>
> The farmers endured a hard winter.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سردی جھیلی, *us ne sardī jhelī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھیلنا** (*jhelnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhelnā* under *\*jhēlati*, "undergoes", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5413).
