Persian, chapter 307: Early, mid and late.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
اوایل (avâyel) — early in, the early part.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for during, then the Persian for again.

You'll want to know: اوایل.
اوایل (avâyel) — "early in, the early part".
اوایل (avâyel) بهار به خانه جدید رفتیم. Avâyel-e bahâr be khâne-ye jadid raftim. Early in the spring we moved to the new house.
An Arabic broken plural of avval "first". With the ezafe: avâyel-e mâh "early in the month".

The word, taken apart.
avâyel is usually traced to Arabic awā'il, the broken plural of awwal "first", which Persian also has as avval. EtymDB lists no entry for the plural.

Lesson 2 of 6.
اواسط (avâset) — the middle of, mid-.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for again, then the Persian for early in.

You'll want to know: اواسط.
اواسط (avâset) — "the middle of, mid-".
اواسط (avâset) تابستان هوا خیلی گرم بود. Avâset-e tâbestân havâ kheyli garm bud. In the middle of summer the weather was very hot.
Broken plural of vasat "middle": avâset-e hafte "midweek".

The word, taken apart.
avâset is usually traced to Arabic awāsiṭ, the broken plural of wasaṭ "middle", which Persian also has as vasat. EtymDB lists no entry for the plural.

Lesson 3 of 6.
اواخر (avâkher) — late in, the last part.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for early in, then the Persian for the middle of.

You'll want to know: اواخر.
اواخر (avâkher) — "late in, the last part".
اواخر (avâkher) پاییز اولین برف آمد. Avâkher-e pâyiz avvalin barf âmad. Late in the autumn the first snow fell.
Broken plural of âkhar "last": avâkher-e shab "late at night".

The word, taken apart.
avâkher is the Arabic broken plural awākhir of ākhir "last" (EtymDB lists Arabic ākhir); Persian also has the singular as âkhar.

Lesson 4 of 6.
آستانه (âstâne) — the eve, the threshold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the middle of, then the Persian for late in.

You'll want to know: آستانه.
آستانه (âstâne) — "the eve, the threshold".
در آستانه سال نو همه خرید میکردند. Dar âstâne-ye sâl-e now hame kharid mikardand. On the eve of the new year everyone was out shopping.
Dar âstâne-ye... "on the eve of, on the brink of". Its first meaning is a doorstep.

The word, taken apart.
âstâne is inherited from Middle Persian āstānag (EtymDB); its first sense is "threshold, doorstep".

Lesson 5 of 6.
حدودا (hodudan) — roughly, approximately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for late in, then the Persian for the eve.

You'll want to know: حدودا.
حدودا (hodudan) — "roughly, approximately".
حدودا (hodudan) صد نفر در جشن بودند. Hodudan sad nafar dar jashn budand. Roughly a hundred people were at the celebration.
Also dar hodud-e "around": dar hodud-e sâ'at-e dah "around ten o'clock".

The word, taken apart.
hodudan is built on hodud "limits", the Arabic plural of ḥadd "limit, edge", with the Arabic adverb ending -an. EtymDB lists Arabic ḥudūd, "boundaries", though not the adverb.
