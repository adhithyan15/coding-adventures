## You'll want to know: सियार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C449-SIYAR449-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सियार** — *siyār* — "a jackal".

> रात को खेतों में सियार घूमते हैं।
>
> *rāt ko khetoṁ meṁ siyār ghūmte haiṁ.*
>
> Jackals roam the fields at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सियार** (*siyār*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *śṛgāla*, "a jackal" (CDIAL 12578): the g between vowels has dropped and the vowels have run together.
