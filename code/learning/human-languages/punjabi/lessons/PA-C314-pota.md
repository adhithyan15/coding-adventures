## You'll want to know: ਪੋਤਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C314-BABY314-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੋਤਾ** — *potā* — "a son's son".

> ਪਿਛਲੇ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਦਾਦਾ ਜੀ ਦਾ ਪਹਿਲਾ ਪੋਤਾ ਜੰਮਿਆ।
>
> *pichhle mahīne dādā jī dā pahilā potā jammiā.*
>
> Last month my grandfather's first grandson was born.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੋਤਾ** (*potā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pautra*, "a son's son"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pottā*, "son's son", under it (CDIAL 8416).
