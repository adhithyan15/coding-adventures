## You'll want to know: le bricolage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le bricolage** — "DIY, odd jobs".

> Le week-end, mon oncle fait du bricolage.
>
> *At the weekend my uncle does DIY.*

*Un magasin de bricolage* — a DIY shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le bricolage* is built on *bricoler*, from Old French *bricole* (EtymDB), once a catapult and later a small job. The earlier origin is not settled.
