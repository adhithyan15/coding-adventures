## Added the Punjabi A2 core: chapters 211-224

- Add fourteen hand-authored chapters (83 lessons, sequences 11500-12320,
  sessions 1142-1224) that give the A2 grammar a home on its own spine nodes:
  211 the vowel-stem present (**ਜਾਂਦਾ**, **ਖਾਂਦਾ**, **ਪੀਂਦਾ** and their -ਦੀ / -ਦੇ
  forms); 212-215 `SPINE-NEGATE-AND-ASK` (**ਨਹੀਂ** before a present verb with the
  helper kept, **ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ**, the polite request in -ੋ and **ਨਾ** before it, **ਕੀ**
  yes-or-no questions and their answers, a four-line exchange); 216-220
  `SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-PAST` (**ਗਿਆ · ਗਈ · ਗਏ**, **ਆਇਆ · ਆਈ · ਆਏ**, -ਿਆ / -ੀ on
  consonant stems, **ਨਹੀਂ** before a past form, **ਪੀਤਾ / ਪੀਤੀ** and **ਖਾਧਾ / ਖਾਧੀ**
  agreeing with the thing after **ਮੈਂ**, **ਅਸੀਂ**, **ਤੁਸੀਂ**, a name or **ਉਸ** with
  **ਨੇ**, **ਲਿਆ** and **ਦਿੱਤਾ**); 221-223 `SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE` (**ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ**,
  **ਜਾਵੇਗਾ**, **ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ**, **ਕਰਾਂਗਾ**, **ਕਰੇਗਾ**, **ਕਰੋਗੇ**, **ਕਰਨਗੇ**, the future
  with **ਕੀ** and **ਨਹੀਂ**, a spoken plan); 224 a short message read for what
  happened, what did not, and what will happen when, then the core review in
  two passes.
- Each chapter opens on a review drivable by ear and closes on a four-skill
  payoff; the canonical tags `VERB-NEGATE`, `QUESTION-POLAR`, `VERB-PAST` and
  `VERB-FUTURE` sit on one lesson each, so the three spine omits are now empty.
- Teach five words as word lessons: **ਅਜੇ** (CDIAL 245), **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ** (CDIAL 3104),
  **ਔਰਤ** and **ਸ਼ਾਇਦ** (Persian loans, hedged; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form) and the
  verb **ਕੱਢਣਾ** (CDIAL 2660).
- Add **ਔ** (from **ਔਰਤ**) and **ਢ** (from **ਕੱਢਣਾ**) as copy-only letter lessons and
  inventory rows; no stroke order is claimed for either.
- Practise `PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-YA-01` (through **ਯਾਤਰੀ**) and
  `PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-E-206` (through **ਗਏ**) twice more each, so no atom at or
  below A2 is revisited fewer than twice.
- The polite request is taught on consonant stems only (**ਬੈਠੋ**, **ਉੱਠੋ**, **ਕਰੋ**):
  a vowel stem's request (ਜਾ + ਓ) needs the standing vowel ਓ, which the track does
  not teach yet.
