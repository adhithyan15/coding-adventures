Sanskrit, chapter 388: Dealing with People.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
व्यवहरति (vyavaharati) — deals with people; behaves.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for refines, then the Sanskrit for growth.

You'll want to know: व्यवहरति.
व्यवहरति — vyavaharati — "deals with people; behaves".
यदि सः विनयेन व्यवहरति, तर्हि जनाः तं सम्मानयन्ति। yadi saḥ vinayena vyavaharati, tarhi janāḥ taṃ sammānayanti. If he deals with people politely, they honour him.
Past (he …): सः विनयेन व्यवहृतवान्, saḥ vinayena vyavahṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
व्यवहरति (vyavaharati) is built on vi, ava and the root √hṛ, "to take"; MW gives vy-ava-√hṛ "to act, to behave towards or deal with" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: उपेक्षते.
उपेक्षते — upekṣate — "neglects; overlooks".
यदि सः स्वास्थ्यम् उपेक्षते, तर्हि रुग्णः भविष्यति। yadi saḥ svāsthyam upekṣate, tarhi rugṇaḥ bhaviṣyati. If he neglects his health, he will fall ill.
Past (he …): सः कार्यम् उपेक्षितवान्, saḥ kāryam upekṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपेक्षते (upekṣate) is built on upa and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives upekṣ "to overlook, to disregard, to neglect" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सौम्यः (saumyaḥ) — gentle; mild-mannered.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for deals with people, then the Sanskrit for neglects.

You'll want to know: सौम्यः.
सौम्यः — saumyaḥ — "gentle; mild-mannered".
यदि नायकः सौम्यः, तर्हि जनाः तम् अनुगच्छन्ति। yadi nāyakaḥ saumyaḥ, tarhi janāḥ tam anugacchanti. If a leader is gentle, people follow him.

The word, taken apart.
सौम्यः (saumyaḥ) is built on soma, the Soma drink and the moon, with the ending -ya; MW gives saumya "relating to Soma", then "resembling the moon", and so "placid, gentle, mild" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for neglects, then the Sanskrit for mild-mannered.

You'll want to know: वृथा.
वृथा — vṛthā — "in vain".
यदि भवान् न शृणोति, तर्हि मम उपदेशः वृथा। yadi bhavān na śṛṇoti, tarhi mama upadeśaḥ vṛthā. If you do not listen, my advice is in vain.

The word, taken apart.
वृथा (vṛthā) MW gives vṛthā "at will" and "in vain, uselessly, fruitlessly"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: आदर्शः.
आदर्शः — ādarśaḥ — "an ideal; a model".
यदि पिता सत्यवादी, तर्हि सः पुत्रस्य आदर्शः भवति। yadi pitā satyavādī, tarhi saḥ putrasya ādarśaḥ bhavati. If a father is truthful, he is a model for his son.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आदर्शः (ādarśaḥ) is built on ā and the root √dṛś, "to see"; MW gives ā-darśa "a mirror", "a copy" and "ideal perfection" (MW).
