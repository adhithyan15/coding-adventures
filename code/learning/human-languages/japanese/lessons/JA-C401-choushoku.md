## You'll want to know: ちょうしょく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C401-CHOUSHOKU401-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうしょく** — *chōshoku* — "breakfast (a formal word)".

> **ちょうしょくは なんじからですか。** — *chōshoku wa nanji kara desu ka* — "What time is breakfast served from?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうしょく** (*chōshoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "morning" (Middle Chinese *trjew*) and *shoku*, "eat" (*zyik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
