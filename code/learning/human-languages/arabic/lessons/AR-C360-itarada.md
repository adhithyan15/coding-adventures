## You'll want to know: اعترض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C360-JADAL360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اعترض** — *iʿtaraḍa* — "to object".

> اعترض جارنا على الموسيقى العالية.
>
> *iʿtaraḍa jārunā ʿalā al-mūsīqā al-ʿāliya.*
>
> Our neighbour objected to the loud music.

Present: يعترض, *yaʿtariḍ*.

It takes على: اعترض على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعترض** (*iʿtaraḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-ḍ*, "to be broad; to lie across" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to put oneself across the way: to object.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
