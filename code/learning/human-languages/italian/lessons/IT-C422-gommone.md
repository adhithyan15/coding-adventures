## You'll want to know: il gommone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C422-BARCA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il gommone** — "the rubber dinghy, the inflatable boat".

> Con il gommone andiamo alle grotte.
>
> *With the dinghy we go to the caves.*

A big thing made of *gomma*, rubber.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gommone* is built on *gomma* 'rubber', which is inherited from Late Latin *gumma*, from Latin *gummi* 'gum' (EtymDB).
