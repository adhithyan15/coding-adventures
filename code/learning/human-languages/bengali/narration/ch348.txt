Bengali, chapter 348: Public Money.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
রাজস্ব (rājôsvô) — public revenue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to appoint, then the Bengali for to issue an order.

You'll want to know: রাজস্ব.
রাজস্ব — rājôsvô — "public revenue".
রাজস্বের টাকায় নতুন হাসপাতাল হবে। rājôsver ṭākāy nôtun hāspātāl hôbe. A new hospital will be built with revenue money.

The word, taken apart.
রাজস্ব (rājôsvô) is Sanskrit rājasva, a king's own property (Monier-Williams). The sense 'public revenue' is the modern one, the state having taken the king's place.

Lesson 3 of 5.
কোষাগার (koshāgār) — a treasury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for public revenue, then the Bengali for a budget.

You'll want to know: কোষাগার.
কোষাগার — koshāgār — "a treasury".
সরকারের কোষাগার এখন খালি। sôrkārer koshāgār ekhôn khāli. The government's treasury is empty now.

The word, taken apart.
কোষাগার (koshāgār) is Sanskrit kośāgāra, kośa 'a store of wealth' with āgāra 'a house': a treasure-house (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
আদায় করা (ādāy kôrā) — to collect dues; to get (money) out of someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a budget, then the Bengali for a treasury.

You'll want to know: আদায় করা.
আদায় করা — ādāy kôrā — "to collect dues; to get (money) out of someone".
পৌরসভা দোকানদারদের থেকে ভাড়া আদায় করে। pourôsôbhā dokāndārder theke bhāṛā ādāy kôre. The council collects rent from the shopkeepers.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সব ধার আদায় করলেন, tini sôb dhār ādāy kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
আদায় করা (ādāy kôrā) is usually traced to Arabic adāʾ, payment or discharge, through Persian, with করা: to make sure what is owed gets paid.

Lesson 5 of 5.
মঞ্জুর করা (mônjur kôrā) — to grant; to sanction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a treasury, then the Bengali for to collect dues.

You'll want to know: মঞ্জুর করা.
মঞ্জুর করা — mônjur kôrā — "to grant; to sanction".
সরকার স্কুলের জন্য টাকা মঞ্জুর করল। sôrkār skuler jônyô ṭākā mônjur kôrlô. The government granted money for the school.
Past (he/she …): তিনি আমার ছুটি মঞ্জুর করলেন, tini āmār chhuṭi mônjur kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
মঞ্জুর করা (mônjur kôrā) is usually traced to Arabic manẓūr, 'looked at' and so approved, through Persian, with করা: to approve a grant, a leave or a request.
