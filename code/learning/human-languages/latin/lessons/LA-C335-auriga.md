## You'll want to know: aurīga, aurīgae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C335-AURIGA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**aurīga, aurīgae** — "a charioteer".

> Aurīga equōs celeriter agēbat.
>
> *The charioteer was driving the horses fast.*

Masculine, 1st declension, though it ends in *-a*, like *nauta*. Famous charioteers grew very rich.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**aurīga** is explained by Lewis & Short as built on an old word for the reins and *agō*, "to drive": he who handles the reins. The details are not settled.
