## You'll want to know: der Zugang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C395-PASSWORT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zugang** — "the access; the way in".

> Im Hotel gibt es kostenlosen Zugang zum Internet.
>
> *The hotel has free internet access.*

Plural: *die Zugänge*. *Zugang zu* + dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zugang* joins *zu* "to" to *Gang* "going, path", inherited from Old High German *gang* and Proto-Germanic *gangaz* (EtymDB): the way to something.
