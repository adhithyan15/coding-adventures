Telugu, chapter 334: A Baby Is Born.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
జననం (jananaṁ) — a birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a trace, then the Telugu for a fingerprint.

You'll want to know: జననం.
జననం — jananaṁ — "a birth".
ఆ పాప జననం ఆసుపత్రిలో జరిగింది. ā pāpa jananaṁ āsupatrilō jarigindi. The baby's birth took place in the hospital.
A formal word for birth: జనన తేదీ is the date of birth on a form.

The word, taken apart.
జననం (jananaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit janana, "birth" (CDIAL 5099), from the root jan, "to be born".

Lesson 2 of 5.
పసికందు (pasikandu) — a newborn baby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a fingerprint, then the Telugu for a birth.

You'll want to know: పసికందు.
పసికందు — pasikandu — "a newborn baby".
పసికందు రాత్రంతా ఏడ్చింది. pasikandu rātrantā ēḍcindi. The newborn cried all night.
Plural: పసికందులు, pasikandulu.
A tender word for a baby only days or weeks old.

The word, taken apart.
పసికందు (pasikandu) is a Telugu compound whose second part, kandu, "an infant", is native Dravidian (DEDR 1411, with Tamil kaṉṟu, "a calf, a young one").

Lesson 3 of 5.
నామకరణం (nāmakaraṇaṁ) — a naming ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a birth, then the Telugu for a newborn baby.

You'll want to know: నామకరణం.
నామకరణం — nāmakaraṇaṁ — "a naming ceremony".
పాపకు ఆదివారం నామకరణం చేశారు. pāpaku ādivāraṁ nāmakaraṇaṁ cēśāru. They held the baby's naming ceremony on Sunday.
నామం, a name, and కరణం, a making: నామకరణం (nāmakaraṇaṁ) చేయు, to name a child.

The word, taken apart.
నామకరణం (nāmakaraṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit nāmakaraṇa, "the ceremony of naming after birth" (CDIAL 7066), from nāman, "a name", and karaṇa, "a making".

Lesson 4 of 5.
జన్మించు (janmiñcu) — to take birth; to be born.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a newborn baby, then the Telugu for a naming ceremony.

You'll want to know: జన్మించు.
జన్మించు — janmiñcu — "to take birth; to be born".
నేను ఒక చిన్న పల్లెలో జన్మించాను. nēnu oka cinna pallelō janmiñcānu. I was born in a small village.
Past (I …): జన్మించాను, janmiñcānu.
A formal partner of పుట్టు: life stories and the news use జన్మించు (janmiñcu).

The word, taken apart.
జన్మించు (janmiñcu) is built on Sanskrit janman, "birth" (CDIAL 5113), with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu.

You'll want to know: దీవించు.
దీవించు — dīviñcu — "to bless".
అమ్మమ్మ పసికందును దీవించింది. ammamma pasikandunu dīviñcindi. Grandmother blessed the newborn.
Past (I …): దీవించాను, dīviñcānu.
Its noun is దీవెన, a blessing: elders bless, the young bow.

The word, taken apart.
దీవించు (dīviñcu) is a Telugu verb whose origin is not settled; DEDR has no entry for it.
