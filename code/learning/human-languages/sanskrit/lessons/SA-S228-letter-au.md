# औ — the vowel of ौ, standing alone

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C281-AUSADHAM-01, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-115, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-03] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: say **औषधम्** (*auṣadham*), "a medicine". Then name the sign **ौ** and the letter **अ**. (*au*, *a*.)

[PAUSE 2s] The sign **ौ** has a standing form, and **औषधम्** opens with it.

## Script you'll notice: औ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-228]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-115, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-03] -->

**औ** — *au*.

It is an **independent vowel**: the full-size letter *au* wears when it opens a word. On a consonant the same vowel is the sign **ौ**, as in **द्वौ** (*dvau*).

What it is made of:

- the joined upper and lower bowls of अ
- a middle shoulder running into the inner stem
- the inner stem
- the trailing stem
- two separate upper arcs
- the top shirorekhā

You have met it here:

- **औषधम्** *auṣadham* — a medicine

## Writing: औ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-228] -->

- **1.** curve right around the upper bowl and continue down and around the lower bowl without lifting
- **2.** lift and sweep the middle shoulder right
- **3.** without lifting, climb up the inner stem to the headline
- **4.** descend the inner stem
- **5.** lift and descend the trailing stem
- **6.** lift at the trailing stem's headline junction and sweep the lower upper arc upward and left
- **7.** lift at the same junction and sweep the taller upper arc upward and left
- **8.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 5.** The pen comes up 5 times and no more.

> The source shows seven movements; native writers draw औ as six pen-down strokes (60% of HP Labs India's native-writer samples, 49 of 82). This ductus follows the seven movements in order and direction, lifting five times: the middle shoulder runs on, climbing the inner stem and descending it, and every other movement starts with a lift.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari औ stroke order.svg’, panels 1–7, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.
