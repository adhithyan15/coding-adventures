## You'll want to know: 可怕
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C463-FEAR463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**可怕** — *kěpà* — "frightening; awful".

> *zhège diànyǐng hěn kěpà* — This film is really frightening.

The new character, in pieces: 怕 — eight strokes in two pieces: on the left the heart piece, a dot, a second dot and a long vertical; on the right 白 *bái*, five strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**可怕** (*kěpà*) is built from 可 'can' and 怕 'to fear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaX* and *phaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to permit' and the second 'to fear'. The dictionary lists 怕 under a second reading, *phaek*, glossed 'still and quiet', which is not this word.
