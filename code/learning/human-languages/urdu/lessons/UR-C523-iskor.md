## You'll want to know: اسکور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C523-STADIUM523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اسکور** — *iskor* — "a score (in a game)".

> میچ کا اسکور کیا ہے؟
>
> *maich kā iskor kyā hai?*
>
> What's the score in the match?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اسکور** (*iskor*) is borrowed from English *score*.
