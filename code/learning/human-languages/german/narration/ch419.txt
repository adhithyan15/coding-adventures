German, chapter 419: Warten am Flughafen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Zwischenlandung — the stopover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to take off, then the German for the landing.

You'll want to know: die Zwischenlandung.
die Zwischenlandung — "the stopover".
Der Flug hat eine Zwischenlandung von drei Stunden. The flight has a three-hour stopover.
Plural: die Zwischenlandungen.

The word, taken apart.
Zwischenlandung is built from zwischen 'between', inherited from Old High German in zwiskēn 'between two' (EtymDB), and Landung 'landing', from Land.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Zeitverschiebung — the time difference; jet lag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the landing, then the German for the stopover.

You'll want to know: die Zeitverschiebung.
die Zeitverschiebung — "the time difference; jet lag".
Wegen der Zeitverschiebung ist er um vier Uhr morgens wach. Because of the time difference he is awake at four in the morning.
Plural: die Zeitverschiebungen. People also say der Jetlag.

The word, taken apart.
Zeitverschiebung is built from Zeit 'time', inherited from Proto-Germanic tīdiz (EtymDB), and Verschiebung 'shift', from schieben 'to push', inherited from Old High German skioban (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.

verspäten — "to be late, to run late (mostly reflexive: sich verspäten)".
Der Flug verspätet sich um zwei Stunden. The flight is two hours late.
Mostly reflexive: ich verspäte mich. Die Verspätung — the delay.

The word, taken apart.
verspäten is built from ver- and spät 'late', an inherited West Germanic word (EtymDB lists spät).

Lesson 4 of 5.
festsitzen — to be stuck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the time difference, then the German for to be late.

You'll want to know: festsitzen.
festsitzen — "to be stuck".
Wegen des Schnees sitzen wir am Flughafen fest. Because of the snow we are stuck at the airport.
Separable: wir sitzen fest. Perfekt with haben, in the south also sein: wir haben festgesessen.

The word, taken apart.
festsitzen is built from fest 'firm, fixed' and sitzen 'to sit', inherited from Proto-Germanic sitjaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
notlanden — to make an emergency landing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to be late, then the German for to be stuck.

You'll want to know: notlanden.
notlanden — "to make an emergency landing".
Das kleine Flugzeug musste auf einem Feld notlanden. The small plane had to make an emergency landing in a field.
Perfekt with sein: das Flugzeug ist notgelandet. Die Notlandung — the emergency landing.

The word, taken apart.
notlanden is built from Not 'need, distress', an inherited word that is a cousin of English need (EtymDB), and landen 'to land', from Land.
