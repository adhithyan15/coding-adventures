# डबके (ḍabke) — a puddle

## You'll want to know: डबके
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C444-DABKE444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**डबके** — *ḍabke* — "a puddle".

> रस्त्यावर पावसाचं डबकं साचलं.
>
> *rastyāvar pāvsācaṁ ḍabkaṁ sāclaṁ.*
>
> A puddle of rain collected on the road.

Neuter. Plural: डबकी, *ḍabkī*.

Written डबके, said and often written डबकं; the plural is डबकी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डबके** (*ḍabke*) is a Marathi word for a small pool of standing water; its origin is not settled.
