Kannada, chapter 288: Moreover, Suddenly, Except, Without, Anyway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಲ್ಲದೆ (allade) — moreover, besides.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the world, then the Kannada for a wall.

You'll want to know: ಅಲ್ಲದೆ.
ಅಲ್ಲದೆ — allade — "moreover, besides".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಲ್ಲದೆ (allade) is native Dravidian, from alla, "is not", which DEDR 234 lists: "not being (only that)" came to mean "besides"; the entry also has Tamil al- and Malayalam alla.

ಇದ್ದಕ್ಕಿದ್ದಂತೆ — iddakkiddante — "suddenly".

The word, taken apart.
ಇದ್ದಕ್ಕಿದ್ದಂತೆ (iddakkiddante) is native Dravidian, built twice on iddu, "having been", the past form of iru, "to be", which DEDR 480 lists, with -ante, "as, like".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹೊರತು (horatu) — except.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for moreover, then the Kannada for suddenly.

You'll want to know: ಹೊರತು.
ಹೊರತು — horatu — "except".

The word, taken apart.
ಹೊರತು (horatu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil puṟam, Telugu peṟa and Malayalam puṟam (DEDR 4333). DEDR lists older hoṟatu, "that is outside, excepted; except, without".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹೇಗೂ (hēgū) — anyway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for except, then the Kannada for without.

You'll want to know: ಹೇಗೂ.
ಹೇಗೂ — hēgū — "anyway".

The word, taken apart.
ಹೇಗೂ (hēgū) is native Dravidian: hēge, "how", which DEDR 5151 lists among the question words, with -ū, "even": "however it is".
