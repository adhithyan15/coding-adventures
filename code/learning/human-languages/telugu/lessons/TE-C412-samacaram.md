## You'll want to know: సమాచారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C412-PARISODHANA412-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సమాచారం** — *samācāraṁ* — "information".

> ఆ విషయం మీద నాకు ఎక్కువ సమాచారం కావాలి.
>
> *ā viṣayaṁ mīda nāku ekkuva samācāraṁ kāvāli.*
>
> I need more information on that subject.

సమాచారం ఇచ్చు, to inform.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సమాచారం** (*samācāraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samācāra*, "conduct, practice, custom" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for news and information.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
