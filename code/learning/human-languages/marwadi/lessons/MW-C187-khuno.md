## You'll want to know: खूणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C187-KOTHO187-04]; assesses=[] -->

**खूणो** — *khūṇo* — "a corner".

> खूणो खाली है।
>
> *khūṇo khālī hai.*
>
> The corner is empty.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खूणो** (*khūṇo*) comes from a reconstructed *\*khuṇṭa*, "a corner"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *khūṇo* and Hindi *khū̃ṭ* (CDIAL 3898). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
