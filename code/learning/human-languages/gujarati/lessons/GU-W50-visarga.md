# ઃ (visarga) — the two dots in દુઃખ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C281-DUKH-01, GU-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say **દુઃખ** (*duḥkh*), "sorrow". Then write **ં**, the one dot above a syllable.

## Script you'll notice: ઃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-KHAAVU] -->

**ઃ** — the visarga, *ḥ*: a pair of dots to the right of the syllable, marking a voiceless breath after the vowel.

In **દુઃખ** it stands after **દુ** and before **ખ**:

| | |
|---|---|
| **દુ** | *du* |
| **દુઃ** | *duḥ*: the two dots after it |
| **દુઃખ** | *duḥkh* |

It is written after its consonant and its vowel sign: the consonant first, then the visarga to its right, the upper dot, then the lower dot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Follow the numbered strip:

1. start at the top of the upper dot and curve down its left side
2. without lifting, round its bottom and up its right side to close it
3. lift, then start at the top of the lower dot and curve down its left side
4. without lifting, round its bottom and up its right side to close it — and only now lift

Two strokes — four movements, one pen lift; cited to KanoAI's hand-made Gujarati barakhadi templates. Finger-trace **ઃ** once.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **દુઃ** once: **દુ** first, then the two dots. Compare only two things: do the dots sit to the right, one above the other? Repair one if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id": "GU-W50-visarga-recall", "kind": "text", "assesses": ["GU-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01"], "prompt": "Hide the model and write the visarga from memory.", "answer": "ઃ", "accepted": [], "feedback": {"correct": "ઃ — the upper dot, then the lower one.", "incorrect": "Look once at ઃ, hide it, and repair only the dot that differs."}, "response_seconds": 20} -->

Hide the model and write the visarga once. Uncover **ઃ**, compare, and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01, GU-LEX-C281-DUKH-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **દુઃખ** aloud. Which dot comes first? (**The upper one.**) Is it written before or after **દુ**? (After.)
