# વટાવવું (vaṭāvvũ) — to cash a cheque, to exchange

## You'll want to know: વટાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C190-BANK190-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વટાવવું** — *vaṭāvvũ* — "to cash a cheque, to exchange".

> **તે બેંકમાં ચેક વટાવે છે.** — *te bẽkmā̃ cek vaṭāve che* — "She cashes a cheque at the bank."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વટાવવું** (*vaṭāvvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *vartáyati*, "causes to turn", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to exchange, cash" (CDIAL 11356).
