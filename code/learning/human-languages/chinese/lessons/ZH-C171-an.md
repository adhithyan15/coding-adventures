## You'll want to know: àn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C171-SWITCH171-04]; assesses=[] -->

*àn* — "to press; to push down".

> *qǐng àn zhège* — Please press this one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*àn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *'anH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to press down' and 'to stop'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what I do.
