## You'll want to know: मना करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C378-ZARURAT378-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मना करना** — *manā karnā* — "to forbid; to say no".

> अगर डाक्टर मना करेगा तो मैं मिठाई नहीं खाऊँगा।
>
> *agar ḍākṭar manā karegā to maiṁ miṭhāī nahīṃ khāūṅgā.*
>
> If the doctor forbids it, I won't eat sweets.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मना किया, *usne manā kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मना करना** (*manā karnā*) joins मना to करना, "to do". मना is usually traced to Arabic *manʿ*, "prevention, holding back", which reached Hindi through Persian.
