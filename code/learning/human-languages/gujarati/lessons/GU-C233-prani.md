# પ્રાણી (prāṇī) — animal, living creature

## You'll want to know: પ્રાણી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C233-WORK233-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રાણી** — *prāṇī* — "animal, living creature".

> **મેં જંગલમાં ઘણાં પ્રાણી જોયાં.** — *mẽ jangalmā̃ ghaṇā̃ prāṇī joyā̃* — "I saw many animals in the forest."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રાણી** (*prāṇī*) is Sanskrit *prāṇin*, "living, a living creature", built on *prāṇa*, "breath of life", and taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 8932).
