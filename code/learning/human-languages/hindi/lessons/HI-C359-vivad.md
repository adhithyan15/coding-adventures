## You'll want to know: विवाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C359-MUDDA359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विवाद** — *vivād* — "a dispute".

> दो परिवारों में पानी पर विवाद है।
>
> *do parivāroṁ meṁ pānī par vivād hai.*
>
> Two families have a dispute over water.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विवाद** (*vivād*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vivāda*, "a dispute, a contest" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
