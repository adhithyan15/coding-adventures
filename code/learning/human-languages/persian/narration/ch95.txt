Persian, chapter 95: Towel, Cup, Pot, Frying pan, Stove.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حوله (hule) — a towel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for thread, then the Persian for soap.

You'll want to know: حوله.
حوله — hule — "a towel".

The word, taken apart.
hule is the everyday word for a towel; it is sometimes linked with Turkish havlu, "towel", but its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
فنجان (fenjân) — a cup.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for soap, then the Persian for a towel.

You'll want to know: فنجان.
فنجان — fenjân — "a cup".

The word, taken apart.
fenjân is the Arabic form of the Persian pengân, "cup, bowl" (EtymDB): Arabic, with no p, took the word as finjān, and Persian took that form back. EtymDB traces pengân to Greek pinax, "board, plate".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ماهیتابه (mâhitâbe) — a frying pan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cup, then the Persian for a pot.

You'll want to know: ماهیتابه.
ماهیتابه — mâhitâbe — "a frying pan".

The word, taken apart.
mâhitâbe is mâhi, "fish", with tâbe, "frying pan" (EtymDB): the pan for frying fish. Both parts are native; mâhi continues Middle Persian māhīg (EtymDB).
