## You'll want to know: عامل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C369-ELLAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عامل** (*'âmel*) — "a factor; an agent".

> پول تنها عامل خوشبختی نیست.
>
> *pul tanhâ 'âmel-e khoshbakhti nist.*
>
> Money is not the only factor in happiness.

Plural *'avâmel*, an Arabic broken plural. Also the person who does a deed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عامل** comes from Arabic *'āmil* "worker, doer" (EtymDB): a factor is whatever does the work behind an effect.
