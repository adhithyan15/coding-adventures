Hindi, chapter 374: Perhaps, Probably.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
अटकल (aṭkal) — a guess, speculation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to miss, then the Hindi for useful.

You'll want to know: अटकल.
अटकल — aṭkal — "a guess, speculation".
लोगों की अटकल है कि अगर वह चुनाव जीत गया तो मंत्री बनेगा। logoṁ kī aṭkal hai ki agar vah cunāv jīt gayā to mantrī banegā. People speculate that if he wins the election, he'll become a minister.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
अटकल (aṭkal) is inherited from aṭṭakkalā, "a guess", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 183).

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: अनुमान.
अनुमान — anumān — "an estimate, a forecast".
अनुमान के अनुसार अगर ऐसी गर्मी रहेगी तो पानी कम पड़ेगा। anumān ke anusār agar aisī garmī rahegī to pānī kam paṛegā. According to the forecast, if the heat goes on like this, water will run short.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अनुमान (anumān) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit anumāna, "inferring, a conclusion" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
बूझना (būjhnā) — to make out, to guess (a riddle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for certainty, then the Hindi for usually.

You'll want to know: बूझना.
बूझना — būjhnā — "to make out, to guess (a riddle)".
अगर तुमने मेरी पहेली बूझ ली तो मैं हार मान लूँगा। agar tumne merī pahelī būjh lī to maiṁ hār mān lūṅgā. If you guess my riddle, I'll admit defeat.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पहेली बूझ ली, usne pahelī būjh lī.

The word, taken apart.
बूझना (būjhnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit budhyate, "wakes; understands" (CDIAL 9279).
