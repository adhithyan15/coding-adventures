## You'll want to know: il borgo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C446-BORGO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il borgo** — "the village, the old quarter".

> Il borgo ha solo trecento abitanti.
>
> *The village has only three hundred inhabitants.*

A small old town, often with walls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*borgo* comes from Late Latin *burgus* 'small fort' (EtymDB), which was borrowed from a Germanic word for a fort.
