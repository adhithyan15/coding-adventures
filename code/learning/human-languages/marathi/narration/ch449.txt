Lesson 1 of 7.
निसर्ग (nisarga) — nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rot, then the Marathi for to decay.

You'll want to know: निसर्ग.
निसर्ग — nisarga — "nature".
मला निसर्गात फिरायला आवडतं. malā nisargāt phirāylā āvaḍtaṁ. I like walking out in nature.
Masculine.
निसर्गरम्य, beautiful with nature, is a common word for scenery.

The word, taken apart.
निसर्ग (nisarga) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nisarga, "giving forth; the natural state" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 7.
जंगलतोड (jaṅgaltoḍ) — deforestation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for conservation, then the Marathi for tree planting.

You'll want to know: जंगलतोड.
जंगलतोड — jaṅgaltoḍ — "deforestation".
जंगलतोडीमुळे पाऊस कमी झाला. jaṅgaltoḍīmuḷe pāūs kamī jhālā. The rain has decreased because of deforestation.
Feminine.
Before an ending जंगलतोडी-: जंगलतोडीमुळे.

The word, taken apart.
जंगलतोड (jaṅgaltoḍ) is built from जंगल, "a forest", from the family of Sanskrit jāṅgala, "arid land", where Hindi has jaṅgal, "forest" (CDIAL 5177), and toḍ, "cutting", from तोडणे.

Lesson 5 of 7.
नामशेष होणे (nāmśeṣ hoṇe) — to die out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for tree planting, then the Marathi for deforestation.

You'll want to know: नामशेष होणे.
नामशेष होणे — nāmśeṣ hoṇe — "to die out".
हा पक्षी आता नामशेष झाला आहे. hā pakṣī ātā nāmśeṣ jhālā āhe. This bird has now died out.
Past (he …): तो नामशेष झाला, to nāmśeṣ jhālā.
नाम, the name, and शेष, what is left: only the name remains.

The word, taken apart.
नामशेष होणे (nāmśeṣ hoṇe) joins nāmaśeṣa, "of whom only the name is left", built from Sanskrit nāman, "name", and śeṣa, "remainder" (Monier-Williams), to होणे.
