## You'll want to know: īnsānus, īnsāna, īnsānum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C510-INSA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**īnsānus, īnsāna, īnsānum** — "mad, insane".

> Vir īnsānus in viā clāmat.
>
> *A madman is shouting in the street.*

*in-* 'not' + *sānus* 'healthy'. Also of wild, crazy plans. English *insane*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**īnsānus** is built on *in-* "not" and *sānus* "sound, healthy" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *insane* comes from it.
