## A2 core

- **Ch. 211-224 — the A2 core** (`PA-C211-*` … `PA-C224-*`, `PA-R224-*`): the
  vowel-stem present (*jāndā*, *khāndā*, *pīndā*); saying no with **ਨਹੀਂ** and
  asking someone not to with **ਨਾ**; **ਕੀ** yes-or-no questions; the past of going,
  coming, getting up and sitting, agreeing with the one who did it; the past of
  drinking, eating, buying, taking and giving, agreeing with the thing, after
  **ਮੈਂ / ਅਸੀਂ / ਤੁਸੀਂ** or a name or **ਉਸ** with **ਨੇ**; the -ਗਾ future; and a
  short message read for yesterday and tomorrow. Words: *aje*, *kall*, *aurat*,
  *kaḍḍhṇā*, *shāid*; letters **ਔ** and **ਢ**, copy-only. **Authored.** The A2
  vocabulary (chapters 225 onward) builds on it.
