## You'll want to know: першить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C486-FEVER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**першить** (*pershít*) — "to tickle (in the throat)".

> У меня в горле першит.
>
> *U menyá v górle pershít.*
>
> I've got a tickle in my throat.

Impersonal: *в горле першит*. Imperfective only.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*першить* is usually linked to *перхать* 'to clear one's throat'; EtymDB has no entry, so the origin is not settled.
