## You'll want to know: réanimer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C300-SECOURS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**réanimer** — "to resuscitate, to revive".

> Les pompiers ont réanimé l'homme.
>
> *The firefighters revived the man.*

*Le service de réanimation* — intensive care.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réanimer* is built on *animer*, a learned borrowing from Latin *animāre* "to give life", from *anima* "breath, life" (Lewis & Short).
