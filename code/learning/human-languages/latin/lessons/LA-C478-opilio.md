## You'll want to know: ōpiliō, ōpiliōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C478-OPILIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ōpiliō, ōpiliōnis** — "a shepherd (of sheep)".

> Ōpiliō ovēs in colle servat.
>
> *The shepherd watches over the sheep on the hill.*

Third declension, masculine: *ōpiliō, ōpiliōnis m.*, a word of comedy and the farm writers. *Pāstor* is the general word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ōpiliō* is usually traced to *ovis* 'sheep' (Lewis & Short, from an older *ovilio*), so a sheep-keeper; the second part is not settled. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
