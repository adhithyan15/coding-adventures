## You'll want to know: scettico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C343-DUBBIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**scettico** — "sceptical".

> Sono un po' scettico su questa dieta.
>
> *I am a bit sceptical about this diet.*

Plural *scettici*, feminine *scettica*. *Essere scettico su qualcosa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scettico* is a learned borrowing, through Latin *scepticus*, from Greek *skeptikós* "inquiring, reflective", from *sképtesthai* "to look about carefully"; a related Greek word passed into Latin as *scopos* "a mark to aim at" (Lewis & Short).
