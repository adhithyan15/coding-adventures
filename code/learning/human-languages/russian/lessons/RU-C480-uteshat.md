## You'll want to know: утешать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C480-LAUGHTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**утешать** (*uteshát*) — "to comfort".

> Мама утешает плачущего ребёнка.
>
> *Máma utesháyet pláchushchego rebyónka.*
>
> Mum is comforting the crying child.

Imperfective; perfective утешить.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*утешать* is built on *у-* and *тешить* 'to amuse, to please'; EtymDB lists *тешить* without a source, and it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *těšiti*.
