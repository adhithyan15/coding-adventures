# ઠરાવવું (ṭharāvvũ) — to settle on (a decision)

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a settlement, then the Gujarati for resolute.

## You'll want to know: ઠરાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C539-MAKKAM539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠરાવવું** — *ṭharāvvũ* — "to settle on (a decision)".

> અમે ઠરાવ્યું છે કે જો વરસાદ પડશે તો અમે નહીં જઈએ.
>
> *ame ṭharāvyũ che ke jo varsād paḍśe to ame nahī̃ jaīe.*
>
> We have decided that if it rains we won't go.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઠરાવ્યું, *teṇe ṭharāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠરાવવું** (*ṭharāvvũ*) is the causative of **ઠરવું**, "to settle, to become fixed", which CDIAL lists under *\*stharati*, "is firm" (CDIAL 13742): to make a decision firm.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
