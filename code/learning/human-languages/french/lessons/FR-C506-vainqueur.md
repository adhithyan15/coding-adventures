## You'll want to know: le vainqueur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C506-RESULTATS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le vainqueur** — "winner, victor".

> Le vainqueur du match jouera la finale.
>
> *The winner of the match will play in the final.*

Traditionally the same form for a woman: *elle est sortie vainqueur*; *la vainqueure* is also found today, especially in Québec.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le vainqueur* is built on *vaincre* "to defeat", which is inherited from Latin *vincere* "to conquer" (Lewis & Short).
