## You'll want to know: el padrino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C546-BAUTIZO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el padrino** — "the godfather; the best man".

> Mi tío es mi padrino.
>
> *My uncle is my godfather.*

At a wedding, *el padrino* walks with the bride or the groom. *Los padrinos* — the godparents.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*padrino* is inherited from spoken Latin *\*patrīnus* 'a godfather' (EtymDB), built on *pater* 'father'.
