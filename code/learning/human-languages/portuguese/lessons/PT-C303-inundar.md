## You'll want to know: inundar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C303-CHEIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**inundar** — "to flood".

> A chuva inundou a cave.
>
> *The rain flooded the cellar.*

Also of feelings or crowds: *os turistas inundam a praça*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inundar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inundāre* "to overflow", built on *unda* "a wave" (Lewis & Short).
