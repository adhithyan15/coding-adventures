## You'll want to know: qiǎngjié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C309-THIEF309-01]; assesses=[] -->

*qiǎngjié* — "to rob by force".

> *yǒu rén qiǎngjié le zhè jiā yínháng* — Someone robbed this bank.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiǎngjié* is built from *qiǎng* 'to snatch' and *jié* 'to plunder', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjangX* and *kjaep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to rob by force'; the sense 'to snatch' for the first is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
