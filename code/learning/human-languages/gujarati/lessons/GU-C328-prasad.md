## You'll want to know: પ્રસાદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C328-PUJA328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રસાદ** — *prasād* — "a blessed offering (of food)".

> આરતી પછી દાદીએ બધાને પ્રસાદ આપ્યો.
>
> *ārtī pachī dādīe badhāne prasād āpyo.*
>
> After the aarti my grandmother gave everyone some of the blessed food.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રસાદ** (*prasād*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *prasāda*, "clearness, kindness, favour" (Monier-Williams): the sweet or the fruit offered to a god and then shared out as the god's favour.
