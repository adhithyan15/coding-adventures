## You'll want to know: करवत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C521-DURUSTI521-04]; assesses=[] -->

**करवत** — *karvat* — "a saw".

> सुताराने करवतीने लाकूड कापलं.
>
> *sutārāne karavtīne lākūḍ kāplaṁ.*
>
> The carpenter cut the wood with a saw.

Feminine.

The oblique is करवती-: करवतीने, with a saw.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**करवत** (*karvat*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *karapattra*, "saw" (CDIAL 2795).
