Hindi, chapter 352: In Court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
क़ैद (qaid) — imprisonment, a prison sentence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for proper, then the Hindi for justice.

You'll want to know: क़ैद.
क़ैद — qaid — "imprisonment, a prison sentence".
अदालत ने चोर को दो साल की क़ैद सुनाई। adālat ne cor ko do sāl kī qaid sunāī. The court sentenced the thief to two years in prison.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
क़ैद (qaid) is usually traced to Arabic qayd, "a fetter, a bond", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.

दोषी — doṣī — "guilty".
अदालत ने उसे दोषी माना। adālat ne use doṣī mānā. The court found him guilty.

The word, taken apart.
दोषी (doṣī) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit doṣin, "guilty of an offence" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a verdict, then the Hindi for guilty.

You'll want to know: ज़मानत.
ज़मानत — zamānat — "bail".
जज ने उसे ज़मानत दी। jaj ne use zamānat dī. The judge granted him bail.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ज़मानत (zamānat) is usually traced to Arabic ḍamāna, "a guarantee", which reached Hindi through Persian. Persian and Hindi say zamānat.
