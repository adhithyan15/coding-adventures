Script — meet one form.
ઠ — ṭha.
Keep ઠ (ṭha) visible. Start high, a little to the right of where the ring will sit. Curl left across the top and down the outside of a small hook. From the foot of the hook, sweep down and to the right until you reach the widest point on the right. Now carry the ring round clockwise — down the right side, along the bottom, up the left — and close it exactly where your diagonal crossed on the way down. One stroke, no lift.
ટ turns the other way. Its bowl runs anticlockwise and finishes still open on the right; ઠ (ṭha) runs clockwise and finishes closed. If the ring will not close, you have made a ટ.

Writing — copy with the model.
Copy visible ઠ (ṭha) once. Compare only two things: does the ring close, and does it close where the diagonal came in? Repair one landmark if needed.

Writing — recall after hiding.
