German, chapter 196: A harbour, A bank, A beach, A meadow, A valley.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Hafen — a harbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a tower, then the German for a castle.

You'll want to know: der Hafen.
der Hafen — "a harbour".
