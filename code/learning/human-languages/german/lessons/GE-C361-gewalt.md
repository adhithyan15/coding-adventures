## You'll want to know: die Gewalt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C361-EINWANDERUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Gewalt** — "violence; power, force".

> Gewalt ist keine Lösung.
>
> *Violence is no solution.*

No plural in this sense. Also power: *die Gewalt über das Auto verlieren* — to lose control of the car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gewalt* is inherited from Middle High German *gewalt* "strength, power" and Old High German *giwalt* (EtymDB), from Proto-Germanic *waldą*, built on *waldaną* "to rule".
