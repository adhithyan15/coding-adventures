## You'll want to know: audītus, audītūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C519-SENS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**audītus, audītūs** — "hearing".

> Canis audītum ācrem habet.
>
> *The dog has sharp hearing.*

Masculine, fourth declension, from *audiō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**audītus** is the action noun of *audiō* "to hear" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *audition* comes from the related *audītiō*.
