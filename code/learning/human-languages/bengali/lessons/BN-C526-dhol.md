## You'll want to know: ঢোল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C526-BAJNA526-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢোল** — *ḍhol* — "a drum; a dhol (a two-headed drum)".

> মেলায় একজন ঢোল বাজাচ্ছিল।
>
> *Melāy ekjôn ḍhol bājāchchhilô.*
>
> Someone was playing a drum at the fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢোল** (*ḍhol*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *ḍhol*, "drum", under *ḍhōla* (CDIAL 5608).
