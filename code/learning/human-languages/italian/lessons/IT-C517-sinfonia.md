## You'll want to know: la sinfonia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C517-ORCHESTRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la sinfonia** — "symphony".

> Stasera l'orchestra suona una sinfonia famosa.
>
> *Tonight the orchestra is playing a famous symphony.*

*Sinfonico* — symphonic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sinfonia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *symphōnia* (EtymDB), from Greek *symphōnía* "sounding together".
