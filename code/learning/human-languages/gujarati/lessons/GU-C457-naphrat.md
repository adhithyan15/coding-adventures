## You'll want to know: નફરત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C457-NAFRAT457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નફરત** — *naphrat* — "hatred".

> મને ખોટા વચનથી નફરત છે.
>
> *mane khoṭā vacanthī naphrat che.*
>
> I hate empty promises.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નફરત** (*naphrat*) is usually traced to Arabic *nafra*, "aversion, running away", which reached Gujarati through Persian *nafrat*.
