## You'll want to know: 个性
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C467-PERSON467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**个性** — *gèxìng* — "individuality; a strong personality".

> *tā hěn yǒu gèxìng* — She has a lot of personality.

The new character, in pieces: 性 — eight strokes in two pieces: on the left the heart piece, a dot, a second dot and a long vertical; on the right 生 *shēng*, five strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**个性** (*gèxìng*) is built from 个 'single' and 性 'nature', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaH* and *sjengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'one-sided' and the second 'nature and conduct'.
