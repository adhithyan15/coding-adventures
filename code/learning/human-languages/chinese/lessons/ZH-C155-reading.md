## Reading — the pinyin, after you have tried
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SKILL-READ-TIMETABLE-01, ZH-SKILL-READ-WORDS, ZH-GRAMMAR-CONG-DAO-01, ZH-LEX-C155-KAIMEN-01] -->

> *cóng zhèr dào dàxué de huǒchē*
> *zǎoshang bā diǎn kāi, shí diǎn bàn dào*
> *xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn kāi, sì diǎn bàn dào*
> *wǎnshang qī diǎn kāi, jiǔ diǎn dào*

> *shū*
> *xīngqīyī dào xīngqīwǔ*
> *zǎoshang jiǔ diǎn dào wǎnshang bā diǎn kāimén*
> *xīngqīliù*
> *shàngwǔ shí diǎn dào xiàwǔ wǔ diǎn kāimén*
> *xīngqītiān bù kāimén*

A timetable leaves out every word it can. 开 is the train leaving and 到 is it arriving; the shop's name is one character, 书, and its hours are two times with 到 between them and 开门 after.

- You must be at the university by eleven: which train? (**The eight o'clock: 早上八点开 十点半到**.)
- Can you buy a book on Sunday? (**No: 星期天不开门**.)
- What time does the bookshop open on Saturday? (**Ten in the morning: 上午十点**.)

- [YOU SAY: the timetable aloud, once, without stopping]
