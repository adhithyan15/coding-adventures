## You'll want to know: der Sabbat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-SABBAT-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-DER-DIE-DAS-02] -->

> **der Sabbat** — "the Sabbath," the day of rest.

Hebrew *shabbāt* means "to rest." Greek-speaking Christians took it as
*sábbaton* and carried it north, and German has it as *Sabbat*.

Hold the word on its own for a moment before the next lesson does anything with
it. Its road is the point: **Hebrew to Greek to German**, with no Germanic
ancestor and no Latin one either. It is the only word in the German week that
came from outside Europe.
