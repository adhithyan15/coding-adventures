## You'll want to know: a ternura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C496-CARINHO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a ternura** — "tenderness".

> A avó olhava para o neto com ternura.
>
> *Grandma was looking at her grandson with tenderness.*

Built on *terno* "tender".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ternura* is built on *terno* "tender", which is inherited from Latin *tener* "soft, tender" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
