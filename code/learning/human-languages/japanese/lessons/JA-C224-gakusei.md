## You'll want to know: がくせい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C224-SCHOOL224-01]; assesses=[] -->

**がくせい** — *gakusei* — "a student; a university student".

> **がくせいは まいにち がっこうに いきます。** — *gakusei wa mainichi gakkō ni ikimasu* — "The students go to school every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がくせい** (*gakusei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gaku*, "learning" (Middle Chinese *haewk*) and *sei*, "life, birth" (*srjaeng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
