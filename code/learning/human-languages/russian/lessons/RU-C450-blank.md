## You'll want to know: бланк
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C450-SPRAVKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**бланк** (*blank*) — "a form (to fill in)".

> В банке мне дали бланк.
>
> *V bánke mne dáli blank.*
>
> At the bank they gave me a form.

Masculine. *Заполнить бланк* — to fill in a form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бланк* is usually traced, probably through German, to French *blanc* 'white, blank' (EtymDB lists the French word): a sheet left blank to be filled in.
