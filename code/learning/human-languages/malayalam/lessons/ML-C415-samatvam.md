## You'll want to know: സമത്വം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C415-SAMUHAM415-04]; assesses=[] -->

**സമത്വം** — *samatvaṁ* — "equality".

> സ്ത്രീക്കും പുരുഷനും സമത്വം വേണം.
>
> *strīkkuṁ puruṣanuṁ samatvaṁ vēṇaṁ.*
>
> Women and men need equality.

The -ത്വം ending makes an abstract noun, like English -ness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സമത്വം** (*samatvaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samatva*, "equality" (Monier-Williams), *sama*, "same, equal", with the abstract ending *-tva*.
