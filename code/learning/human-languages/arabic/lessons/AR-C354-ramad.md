## You'll want to know: رماد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C354-HUTAM354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رماد** — *ramād* — "ash, ashes".

> بقي الرماد في المدفأة حتى الصباح.
>
> *baqiya ar-ramād fī al-midfaʾa ḥattā aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> The ash stayed in the stove until morning.

The taught رمادي, grey, is the colour of ash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رماد** (*ramād*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-m-d* (Lane), where *ramād* is ashes; the taught *ramādī*, grey, is "ash-coloured".
