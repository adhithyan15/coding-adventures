## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-SOLA-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-CHHA-SIX-01, MW-SCRIPT-LLA-01] -->

> *soḷā* — **sixteen**

Six is *chha*. Sixteen is *soḷā*, and the two share nothing you can hear — the
teens have worn their small numbers down further here than anywhere else in the
set.

What they do share is the retroflex **ḷ** you have just written. Curl the tongue
back for it: *so-ḷā*, not *so-lā*. This is where the new letter earns its lesson.
