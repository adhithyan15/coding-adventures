Sanskrit, chapter 361: Faith and Loyalty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
श्रद्धा (śraddhā) — faith; trust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an insult, then the Sanskrit for misleads.

You'll want to know: श्रद्धा.
श्रद्धा — śraddhā — "faith; trust".
मम वैद्ये श्रद्धा अस्ति। mama vaidye śraddhā asti. I have faith in my doctor.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
श्रद्धा (śraddhā) is built from śrat, "trust, truth", and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives śrad-dhā "faith, trust, confidence, belief" (MW).

कृतघ्नः जनः उपकारं विस्मरति। kṛtaghnaḥ janaḥ upakāraṃ vismarati. An ungrateful person forgets a kindness.

The word, taken apart.
कृतघ्नः (kṛtaghnaḥ) is built from kṛta, "what has been done", and the root √han, "to strike, to destroy"; MW explains it as "destroying past services", that is "ungrateful" (MW).
