## You'll want to know: भाप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C436-BUND436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भाप** — *bhāp* — "steam; vapour".

> चाय से भाप उठ रही थी।
>
> *cāy se bhāp uṭh rahī thī.*
>
> Steam was rising from the tea.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाप** (*bhāp*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *bāṣpa*, "tears" and also "vapour" (CDIAL 9223), which lists Hindi *bhāp*, "steam".
