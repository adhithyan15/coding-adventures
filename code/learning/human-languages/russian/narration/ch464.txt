Russian, chapter 464: The weather forecast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
прогноз (prognóz) — a forecast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a seashell, then the Russian for to dive.

You'll want to know: прогноз.
прогноз (prognóz) — "a forecast".
По прогнозу завтра будет дождь. Po prognózu závtra búdet dozhd. According to the forecast it will rain tomorrow.
Masculine. Прогноз погоды — the weather forecast.

The word, taken apart.
прогноз (prognóz) is usually traced through Latin prognōsis to Greek prógnōsis 'knowing beforehand' (EtymDB lists both; the Russian entry has no source), from pro- 'before' and gnōsis 'knowledge'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
синоптик (sinóptik) — a weather forecaster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to dive, then the Russian for a forecast.

You'll want to know: синоптик.
синоптик (sinóptik) — "a weather forecaster".
Синоптики обещают тёплую неделю. Sinóptiki obeshcháyut tyópluyu nedélyu. The forecasters promise a warm week.
Masculine, for a woman too.

The word, taken apart.
синоптик (sinóptik) is usually traced to Greek synoptikós 'seeing the whole together at a glance' (EtymDB lists the Greek word but not the Russian): a forecaster reads the whole weather map at once.

Lesson 3 of 5.
осадки (osádki) — precipitation (rain or snow).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a forecast, then the Russian for a weather forecaster.

You'll want to know: осадки.
осадки (osádki) — "precipitation (rain or snow)".
Сегодня без осадков. Sevódnya bez osádkov. Today there will be no rain or snow.
Plural; a weather word. In the singular, осадок is a sediment.

The word, taken apart.
осадки (osádki) is the plural of осадок 'a sediment, what settles', from о- and садить 'to set down'. EtymDB lists осадки (osádki) without a deeper source.

You'll want to know: пасмурный.
пасмурный (pásmurnyy) — "overcast, cloudy, gloomy".
Был пасмурный и холодный день. Byl pásmurnyy i kholódnyy den. It was an overcast, cold day.
Пасмурно — it is overcast.

The word, taken apart.
пасмурный (pásmurnyy) is usually linked with the old word смурый 'dark grey, gloomy'. EtymDB does not list it, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 5 of 5.
проясняться (proyasnyátsya) — to clear up (of the weather).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for precipitation, then the Russian for overcast.

You'll want to know: проясняться.
проясняться (proyasnyátsya) — "to clear up (of the weather)".
К вечеру небо проясняется. K vécheru nébo proyasnyáyetsya. Towards evening the sky clears up.
Imperfective; perfective проясниться. Built on ясный — clear.

The word, taken apart.
проясняться (proyasnyátsya) is built from про-, ясный 'clear, bright' and -ся. EtymDB does not list the verb.
