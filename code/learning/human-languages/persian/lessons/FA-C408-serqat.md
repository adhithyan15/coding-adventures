## You'll want to know: سرقت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C408-KOLAHBARDARI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سرقت** (*serqat*) — "theft, robbery".

> دیشب در محله ما یک سرقت اتفاق افتاد.
>
> *dishab dar mahalle-ye mâ yek serqat ettefâq oftâd.*
>
> Last night there was a robbery in our neighbourhood.

The formal word; everyday speech says *dozdi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرقت** is usually traced to Arabic *sariqa* "theft".
