## You'll want to know: टीका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C364-VISHESHAJNA364-03]; assesses=[] -->

**टीका** — *ṭīkā* — "a commentary".

> पण्डितः ग्रन्थस्य टीकां लिखति।
>
> *paṇḍitaḥ granthasya ṭīkāṃ likhati.*
>
> The scholar writes a commentary on the book.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टीका** (*ṭīkā*) MW gives *ṭīkā* "a commentary", especially one on another commentary, and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
