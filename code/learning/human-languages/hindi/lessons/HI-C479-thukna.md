## You'll want to know: थूकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C479-MUNH479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**थूकना** — *thūknā* — "to spit".

> सड़क पर थूकना बुरी आदत है।
>
> *saṛak par thūknā burī ādat hai.*
>
> Spitting in the street is a bad habit.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सड़क पर थूका, *usne saṛak par thūkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थूकना** (*thūknā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *thūknā*, "spits", under *\*thukk*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6097); the word copies the sound it names.
