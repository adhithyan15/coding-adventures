## You'll want to know: ફેરિયો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C422-STALL422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેરિયો** — *pheriyo* — "a hawker; a street seller".

> ફેરિયો ઘરે ઘરે જઈને રમકડાં વેચે છે.
>
> *pheriyo ghare ghare jaīne ramakḍā̃ vece che.*
>
> The hawker goes from house to house selling toys.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેરિયો** (*pheriyo*) is built on ફેરો, a round, which goes back to *\*phēra*, "a turn", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9078): the seller who goes on rounds.
