## You'll want to know: মুষলধারে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C425-MEGH425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মুষলধারে** — *mushôldhāre* — "in torrents (of rain)".

> সারারাত মুষলধারে বৃষ্টি হল।
>
> *sārārāt mushôldhāre brishṭi hôlô.*
>
> It poured all night.

Always with rain: মুষলধারে বৃষ্টি, rain in torrents.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মুষলধারে** (*mushôldhāre*) joins Sanskrit *musala*, "a pestle", and *dhārā*, "a stream, a jet" (Monier-Williams for both), with the locative ending: rain falling in streams as thick as pestles.
