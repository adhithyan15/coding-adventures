## You'll want to know: وابل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C481-MATAR481-03]; assesses=[] -->

**وابل** — *wābil* — "a downpour".

> نزل وابل من المطر في المساء.
>
> *nazala wābil min al-maṭar fī al-masāʾ.*
>
> A downpour came in the evening.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وابل** (*wābil*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-b-l*, "heavy rain of large drops" (Lane).
