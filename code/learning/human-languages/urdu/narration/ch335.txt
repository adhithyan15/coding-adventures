Urdu, chapter 335: Taking Sides.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خلاف (khilāf) — against (کے خلاف).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an altercation, then the Urdu for logic.

You'll want to know: خلاف.
خلاف — khilāf — "against (کے خلاف)".
شہری اس نئے قانون کے خلاف ہیں۔ shahrī is naye qānūn ke khilāf haiṅ. The citizens are against this new law.

The word, taken apart.
خلاف (khilāf) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khilāf, "opposition, the contrary".

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Lesson 2 of 5.
حمایت کرنا (himāyat karnā) — to back; to support (an idea, a person).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for logic, then the Urdu for against.

You'll want to know: حمایت کرنا.
حمایت کرنا — himāyat karnā — "to back; to support (an idea, a person)".
میں آپ کی رائے کی حمایت کرتا ہوں۔ maiṅ āp kī rāe kī himāyat kartā hūṅ. I support your view.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سارا کی حمایت کی, us ne sārā kī himāyat kī.

The word, taken apart.
حمایت کرنا (himāyat karnā) is built from himāyat, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥimāya, "protection", and کرنا, "to do".
