## You'll want to know: brucare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C464-SELVATICI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**brucare** — "to graze, to nibble (grass or leaves)".

> Le capre brucano l'erba.
>
> *The goats graze on the grass.*

Of animals eating leaves and grass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *brucare* is not settled; it may be linked to an old word for a shoot or a twig.
