## You'll want to know: scrīnium, scrīniī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C551-CAPS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**scrīnium, scrīniī** — "a writing case, chest for papers".

> Epistulās in scrīniō servō.
>
> *I keep the letters in a writing case.*

Neuter, second declension. A round box for book-rolls and letters.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**scrīnium** is a native Latin noun; Lewis & Short links it with *scrībō* "to write", but EtymDB traces it to a root meaning "to cut", so the origin is not settled. English *shrine* comes from it.
