## You'll want to know: ಗೀಸರ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C463-SEVE463-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೀಸರ್** — *gīsar* — "a water heater".

> ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಗೀಸರ್ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *beḷagge gīsar kelasa māḍalilla.*
>
> In the morning the water heater did not work.

In Indian English a geyser heats bath water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೀಸರ್** (*gīsar*) is a loan from English *geyser*, which in Indian English names a bathroom water heater.
