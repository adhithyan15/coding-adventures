# నిర్బంధించు (nirbandhiñcu) — to detain; to confine

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to punish, then the Telugu for a captive.

## You'll want to know: నిర్బంధించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C351-BANDI351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నిర్బంధించు** — *nirbandhiñcu* — "to detain; to confine".

> పోలీసులు అనుమానితులను నిర్బంధించారు.
>
> *pōlīsulu anumānitulanu nirbandhiñcāru.*
>
> The police detained the suspects.

Past (I …): నిర్బంధించాను, *nirbandhiñcānu*.

An official word: police and governments detain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిర్బంధించు** (*nirbandhiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *nirbandha*, "perseverance, insistence" (CDIAL 7355), from *bandh*, "to bind", with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
