## You'll want to know: लाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C472-TALU472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लाला** — *lālā* — "saliva".

> शुनकस्य मुखात् लाला पतति।
>
> *śunakasya mukhāt lālā patati.*
>
> Saliva drips from the dog's mouth.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाला** (*lālā*) MW gives "saliva, spittle, slobber"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
