## You'll want to know: el gobierno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C601-GOBIERNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el gobierno** — "the government".

> El gobierno subió los impuestos.
>
> *The government raised taxes.*

The verb is *gobernar* (e→ie).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gobierno* is built on *gobernar*, inherited from Latin *gubernare* 'to steer' (EtymDB), a verb Latin took from Greek *kybernan* (Lewis & Short).
