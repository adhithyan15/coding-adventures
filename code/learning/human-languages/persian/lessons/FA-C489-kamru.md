## You'll want to know: کمرو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C489-MANNERS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کمرو** (*kamru*) — "shy, timid (in company)".

> پسرشان کمرو است و در مهمانی حرف نمیزند.
>
> *pesarashân kamru ast va dar mehmâni harf nemizanad.*
>
> Their son is shy and doesn't talk at parties.

*kam* 'little' + *ru* 'face': little face, little boldness. The opposite is *porru* 'cheeky'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kamru* is built on two native Persian words, *kam*, 'little', and *ru*, 'face'. It is the mirror of *porru*, 'cheeky', built on *por*, 'full'.
