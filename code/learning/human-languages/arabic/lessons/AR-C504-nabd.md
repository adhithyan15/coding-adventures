## You'll want to know: نبض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C504-BATN504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نبض** — *nabḍ* — "the pulse".

> فحص الطبيب نبض المريض.
>
> *faḥaṣa aṭ-ṭabīb nabḍ al-marīḍ.*
>
> The doctor checked the patient's pulse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نبض** (*nabḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-b-ḍ*, "to beat, to throb" (Lane). Nabḍ is each beat of the blood.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
