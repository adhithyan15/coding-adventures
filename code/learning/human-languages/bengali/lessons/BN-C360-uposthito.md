## You'll want to know: উপস্থিত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C360-BOKTRITA360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**উপস্থিত** — *upôsthitô* — "present; in attendance".

> সভায় একশো লোক উপস্থিত ছিল।
>
> *sôbhāy eksho lok upôsthitô chhilô.*
>
> A hundred people were at the meeting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপস্থিত** (*upôsthitô*) is Sanskrit *upasthita*, standing near, present (Monier-Williams). It is the word for being present at a meeting or in class.
