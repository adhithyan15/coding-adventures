Italian, chapter 303: Un boato.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'esplosione — the explosion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the burn, then the Italian for to get burned.

You'll want to know: l'esplosione.
l'esplosione — "the explosion".
L'esplosione si è sentita in tutto il paese. The explosion was heard all over the village.
Un'esplosione di gas — a gas explosion.

The word, taken apart.
esplosione is a learned borrowing from Latin explōsiō (EtymDB), from explōdere "to drive off by clapping".

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
scoppiare — to burst; to break out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the explosion, then the Italian for to explode.

You'll want to know: scoppiare.
scoppiare — "to burst; to break out".
È scoppiata una gomma. A tyre burst.
Scoppiare a ridere — to burst out laughing; è scoppiata la guerra — war broke out.

The word, taken apart.
scoppiare is built on scoppio "a bang", from Late Latin scloppus "a slapping sound" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
il crollo — the collapse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to burst, then the Italian for the bang.

You'll want to know: il crollo.
il crollo — "the collapse".
Il crollo del ponte ha bloccato la città. The collapse of the bridge brought the city to a halt.
Also for prices: il crollo della borsa.

The word, taken apart.
crollo is built on crollare "to collapse", which is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin corrotulāre "to roll down" (Lewis & Short gives rotula "a small wheel").
