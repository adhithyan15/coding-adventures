## You'll want to know: o orgulho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C467-ORGULHO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o orgulho** — "pride".

> O meu pai fala do meu trabalho com muito orgulho.
>
> *My father talks about my work with great pride.*

*Ter orgulho em alguém* (or *de alguém*) — to be proud of someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orgulho* came into Old Portuguese from Catalan *orgull* "pride" (EtymDB), which was borrowed from a Frankish word *urgol* of the same meaning.
