## You'll want to know: डर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C108-FEEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**डर** (*ḍar*) — "fear".

**मुझे डर लग रहा है** — "I am scared", literally "fear is striking me". Feelings take the
**मुझे** frame.
