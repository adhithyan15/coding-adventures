# ઉત્તર (uttar) — the north

## You'll want to know: ઉત્તર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C414-EAST414-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્તર** — *uttar* — "the north".

> અમારું ગામ શહેરની ઉત્તરમાં છે.
>
> *amārũ gām śahernī uttarmā̃ che.*
>
> Our village is to the north of the city.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્તર** (*uttar*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *uttara*, "upper, higher", and so "northern" (Monier-Williams). The same word also means an answer.
