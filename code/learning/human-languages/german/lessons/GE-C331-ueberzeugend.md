## You'll want to know: überzeugend
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C331-UEBERZEUGEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**überzeugend** — "convincing".

> Seine Antwort war nicht sehr überzeugend.
>
> *His answer was not very convincing.*

The present participle of *überzeugen*, here an adjective: *ein überzeugendes Beispiel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*überzeugend* is the present participle of *überzeugen*, with the ending *-end*; the verb is built on *zeugen* "to testify", from Middle High German *ziugen* (EtymDB).
