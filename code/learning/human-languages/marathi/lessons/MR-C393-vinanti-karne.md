# विनंती करणे (vinantī karṇe) — to request

## You'll want to know: विनंती करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C393-SABAB393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विनंती करणे** — *vinantī karṇe* — "to request".

> आम्ही त्यांना थोडं थांबण्याची विनंती केली.
>
> *āmhī tyānnā thoḍaṁ thāmbaṇyācī vinantī kelī.*
>
> We asked them to wait a little.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने विनंती केली, *tyāne vinantī kelī*.

The person asked takes -ला, the thing asked for -ची.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विनंती करणे** (*vinantī karṇe*) joins विनंती, "a request", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to करणे, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
