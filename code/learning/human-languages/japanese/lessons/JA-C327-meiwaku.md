## You'll want to know: めいわく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C327-REIGI327-04]; assesses=[] -->

**めいわく** — *meiwaku* — "a nuisance; trouble for others".

> **ばんに うたったら、めいわくです。** — *ban ni utattara, meiwaku desu* — "Singing in the evening is a nuisance."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めいわく** (*meiwaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mei*, "astray" (Middle Chinese *mej*) and *waku*, "delusion" (*hwok*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
