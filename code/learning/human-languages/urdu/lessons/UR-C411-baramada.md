## You'll want to know: برآمدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C411-DHALAN411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**برآمدہ** — *barāmadā* — "a veranda".

> ہم برآمدے میں بیٹھ کر چائے پیتے ہیں۔
>
> *ham barāmade meṅ baiṭh kar chāe pīte haiṅ.*
>
> We sit on the veranda and drink tea.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برآمدہ** (*barāmadā*) is usually traced to Persian *barāmada*, "come out, projecting": the part of a house that juts out.
