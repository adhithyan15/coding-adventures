## You'll want to know: 言う
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C28-VERBS28-03]; assesses=[] -->

**言う** (いう) — *iu* — "to say".

Its first sign is **言**, the speech side of **語** that you have written since
chapter five; the **う** after it is hiragana.
