## You'll want to know: કોશિશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C383-PRAYATNA383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કોશિશ** — *kośiś* — "an effort".

> જો તમે કોશિશ કરશો તો હું પણ મદદ કરીશ.
>
> *jo tame kośiś karśo to hũ paṇ madad karīś.*
>
> If you make the effort, I will help too.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કોશિશ** (*kośiś*) is usually traced to Persian *kōshish*, "effort, striving".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
