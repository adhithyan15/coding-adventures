## You'll want to know: ಸುತ್ತುವರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C342-MUTTIGE342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುತ್ತುವರಿ** — *suttuvari* — "to surround".

> ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಆ ಮನೆಯನ್ನು ಸುತ್ತುವರಿದರು.
>
> *polīsaru ā maneyannu suttuvaridaru.*
>
> The police surrounded that house.

Past (I …): ಸುತ್ತುವರಿದೆ, *suttuvaride*.

What is surrounded takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುತ್ತುವರಿ** (*suttuvari*) is built on native *suttu*, "to surround, go round" (DEDR 2715), as in ಸುತ್ತ, "around"; the origin of the second part is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
