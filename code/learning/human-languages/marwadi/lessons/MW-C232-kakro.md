## You'll want to know: कांकरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C232-EARTH232-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कांकरो** — *kā̃kro* — "a pebble".

> जूती मांय कांकरो है।
>
> *jūtī mā̃y kā̃kro hai.*
>
> There's a pebble in my shoe.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कांकरो** (*kā̃kro*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karkara*, "a stone"; CDIAL lists Hindi *kā̃kar*, a nodule of limestone, and a Gujarati word for a pebble. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2820).
