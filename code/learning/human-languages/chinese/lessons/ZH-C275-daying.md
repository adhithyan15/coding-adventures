## You'll want to know: dāying
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C275-SORRY275-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dāying* — "to agree (to a request); to say yes".

> *tā dāying le* — He said yes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dāying* is built from *dā* 'to answer' and *yìng* 'to respond', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *top* and *'ingH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to match' and the second 'things answering each other'. In the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
