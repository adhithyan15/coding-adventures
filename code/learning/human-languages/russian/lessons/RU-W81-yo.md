# ё (yo) — the yo inside ребёнок

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-C28-YA-SMALL-01, RU-SCRIPT-YU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write я and ю once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ребёнок** (*rebyónok*), "a child".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ё
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SCRIPT-C81-YO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ё** — *yo*.

It is inside **ребёнок** (*rebyónok*), "a child", and in **мёд**, **чёрный** and
**тёплый**. It is **е** with **two dots** above it, and the syllable that carries it
takes the stress. Many printed texts leave the dots off; this book keeps
them, so you can see how the word is said.

## Writing: ё
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-C81-YO-01] -->

Put your pen on ё and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
