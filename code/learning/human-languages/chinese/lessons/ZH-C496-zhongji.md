## You'll want to know: 中级
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C496-LEVELS496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**中级** — *zhōngjí* — "intermediate (in level)".

> **我在公司学中级汉语** *wǒ zài gōngsī xué zhōngjí Hànyǔ* — I'm taking intermediate Chinese at work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**中级** (*zhōngjí*) is built from 中 'the middle' and 级 'a grade', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trjuwng* and *kip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
