Gujarati, chapter 414: East and West.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પૂર્વ (pūrva) — the east.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to lead, then the Gujarati for to point out.

You'll want to know: પૂર્વ.
પૂર્વ — pūrva — "the east".
સૂરજ પૂર્વમાં ઊગે છે. sūraj pūrvamā̃ ūge che. The sun rises in the east.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પૂર્વ (pūrva) is a tatsama, Sanskrit pūrva, "in front, before", and so "eastern" (Monier-Williams): facing the sunrise, the east is in front of you.

ઊલટું (ūlṭũ) belongs with the verb CDIAL lists for Gujarati, "to be the reverse of", from ullaṭati, "turns over", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2368).

Lesson 5 of 5.
હોકાયંત્ર (hokāyantra) — a compass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the north, then the Gujarati for the wrong way round.

You'll want to know: હોકાયંત્ર.
હોકાયંત્ર — hokāyantra — "a compass".
હોકાયંત્રની સોય ઉત્તર બતાવે છે. hokāyantranī soy uttar batāve che. The compass needle shows north.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
હોકાયંત્ર (hokāyantra) is built on Sanskrit yantra, "an instrument, a device" (Monier-Williams); where its first part comes from is not settled.
