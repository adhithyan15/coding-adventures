# ಬಂಡೆ (baṇḍe) — a rock; a boulder

## You'll want to know: ಬಂಡೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C480-BAYALU480-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂಡೆ** — *baṇḍe* — "a rock; a boulder".

> ನದಿಯ ನಡುವೆ ಒಂದು ದೊಡ್ಡ ಬಂಡೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *nadiya naḍuve ondu doḍḍa baṇḍe ide.*
>
> There is a big rock in the middle of the river.

Plural: ಬಂಡೆಗಳು, *baṇḍegaḷu*.

ಕಲ್ಲು is a stone; ಬಂಡೆ is a big rock or a slab.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂಡೆ** (*baṇḍe*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3903 lists Kannada *baṇḍe*, "a rock, a block or slab of stone".
