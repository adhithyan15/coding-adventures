## Chapters 145-147: the past, the future and practical texts

Bengali had realized two of its five A2 spine nodes. Three chapters realize the
other three, so the A2 gate no longer reports a missing spine node. Bengali is
still 557 headwords and 36 verbs short of A2.

- **Chapter 145 (TALK-ABOUT-PAST):** *সম্প্রতি*, *আগেই*, *গত*, *একসময়*,
  *কাটানো*. The notes introduce the simple past in **-লাম** (*পড়লাম*,
  *কাটালাম*) and **ছিল**, the past of **আছে**.
- **Chapter 146 (TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE):** *আগামী*, *এরপর*, *পরিকল্পনা*,
  *স্থির করা*, *আশা করা*. The notes introduce the future in **-ব** (*যাব*,
  *হাঁটব*).
- **Chapter 147 (READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS):** *টাইমটেবিল*, *নোটিশ*, *ভাড়া*,
  *বিজ্ঞাপন*, *রসিদ*: a timetable, a shop notice, the rent, a house to let and
  a receipt.
- Every headword and every Bengali example uses only letters the course has
  taught (the common *অতীত*, *ঘটা* and *ভবিষ্যৎ* need অ, ঘ and ষ, which have
  no letter lesson yet), so script closure gains no violation. *শিগগির* was a
  candidate but is already taught.
