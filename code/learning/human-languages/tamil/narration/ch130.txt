Tamil, chapter 130: Fox, Snake, Rat, Frog, Tortoise.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
நரி (nari) — a fox.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a rabbit, then the Tamil for a deer.

You'll want to know: நரி.
நரி — nari — "a fox".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nari]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nari, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nari]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for a rabbit, then the Tamil for a deer, then say nari again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does நரி (nari) mean? ("A fox".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
The house, things you carry and school — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a frog and a tortoise.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: rasam, butter, an egg, a fish, meat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vegetables, a banana, a mango, a coconut, an onion]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a tomato, a chilli, ginger, garlic, honey]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an idli, a dosa, a vadai, coffee, the sea]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Rasam? (ரசம், rasam.) Butter? (வெண்ணெய், veṇṇey.) An egg? (முட்டை, muṭṭai.) A fish? (மீன், mīṉ.) Meat? (இறைச்சி, iṟaicci.) Vegetables? (காய்கறி, kāykaṟi.) A banana? (வாழைப்பழம், vāḻaippaḻam.) A mango? (மாம்பழம், māmpaḻam.) A coconut? (தேங்காய், tēṅkāy.) An onion? (வெங்காயம், veṅkāyam.) A tomato? (தக்காளி, takkāḷi.) A chilli? (மிளகாய், milakāy.) Ginger? (இஞ்சி, iñci.) Garlic? (பூண்டு, pūṇṭu.) Honey? (தேன், tēṉ.) An idli? (இட்லி, iṭli.) A dosa? (தோசை, tōcai.) A vadai? (வடை, vaṭai.) Coffee? (காபி, kāpi.) The sea? (கடல், kaṭal.)
