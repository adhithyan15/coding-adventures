## You'll want to know: गुप्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C336-LENI336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गुप्त** — *gupta* — "secret; hidden".

> त्याने गुप्त जागी पैसे ठेवले.
>
> *tyāne gupta jāgī paise ṭhevle.*
>
> He kept the money in a secret place.

गुप्त keeps one form: गुप्त मार्ग, गुप्त गोष्ट.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुप्त** (*gupta*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *gupta*, 'guarded, protected, hidden' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
