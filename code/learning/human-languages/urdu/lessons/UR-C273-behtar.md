# بہتر (behtar) — better

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C273-COMPARISON-01, UR-LEX-C70-ADJ70-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "the bus is cheaper than a taxi". (*bas ṭaiksī se sastī hai*.) "Good" has a word of its own for "better".

## You'll want to know: بہتر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C273-BEHTAR-01]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C273-COMPARISON-01, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-02] -->

**بہتر** — *behtar* — "better".

> **یہ بہتر ہے۔**
>
> *yih behtar hai.* — "This is better."

> **بس ٹیکسی سے بہتر ہے۔**
>
> *bas ṭaiksī se behtar hai.* — "The bus is better than a taxi."

> **آج میں بہتر ہوں۔**
>
> *āj maiṅ behtar hūṅ.* — "I'm better today."

It does not change for a man or a woman, and it takes **سے** like any comparison.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بہتر** (*behtar*) is usually traced to Persian *behtar*, "better", built from *beh*, "good", and the Persian ending *-tar*, "more". Urdu took it whole.
