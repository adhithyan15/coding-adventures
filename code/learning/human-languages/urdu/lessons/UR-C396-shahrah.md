## You'll want to know: شاہراہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C396-SHAHRAH396-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شاہراہ** — *shāhrāh* — "a highway; a main road".

> شاہراہ پر ٹرک تیز چلتے ہیں۔
>
> *shāhrāh par ṭrak tez chalte haiṅ.*
>
> Lorries drive fast on the highway.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شاہراہ** (*shāhrāh*) is built from Persian *shāh*, "a king", and *rāh*, "a road" (**راہ**): it is usually traced to Persian *shāhrāh*, "the king's road".
