# लागू करणे (lāgū karṇe) — to enforce; to bring into force

## You'll want to know: लागू करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C358-SANVIDHAN358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लागू करणे** — *lāgū karṇe* — "to enforce; to bring into force".

> सरकारने नवा नियम लागू केला.
>
> *sarkārne navā niyam lāgū kelā.*
>
> The government brought a new rule into force.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नियम लागू केला, *tyāne niyam lāgū kelā*.

लागू होणे, to apply: हा नियम सगळ्यांना लागू होतो.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लागू करणे** (*lāgū karṇe*) joins लागू, "in force, applying", built on लागणे, "to attach", which is inherited from Sanskrit *lagyati*, "adheres to" (CDIAL 10895), to करणे.
