Russian, chapter 478: Stubborn and Fussy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
упрямый (upryámyy) — stubborn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for lonely, then the Russian for to regret.

You'll want to know: упрямый.
упрямый (upryámyy) — "stubborn".
Упрямый ребёнок не хотел есть суп. Upryámyy rebyónok ne khotél yest sup. The stubborn child wouldn't eat the soup.
Short forms: упрям, упряма.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists упрямый (upryámyy) without a source; it is usually linked to упереться 'to dig one's heels in', but the origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
упрямство (upryámstvo) — stubbornness.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: упрямство.
упрямство (upryámstvo) — "stubbornness".
Его упрямство нас удивило. Yegó upryámstvo nas udivílo. His stubbornness surprised us.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
упрямство (upryámstvo) is built on упрямый (upryámyy) 'stubborn' with -ство; EtymDB has no entry for the noun, and the origin of упрямый (upryámyy) is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
капризный (kapríznyy) — fussy, capricious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for stubborn, then the Russian for stubbornness.

You'll want to know: капризный.
капризный (kapríznyy) — "fussy, capricious".
Капризный ребёнок плачет из-за каждой мелочи. Kapríznyy rebyónok pláchet iz-za kázhdoy mélochi. The fussy child cries over every little thing.
Built on каприз — a whim.

The word, taken apart.
капризный (kapríznyy) is built on каприз 'whim', which was borrowed from French caprice, itself from Italian capriccio (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ворчать (vorchát) — to grumble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for fussy, then the Russian for to play up.

You'll want to know: ворчать.
ворчать (vorchát) — "to grumble".
Дед ворчит, что молодёжь сидит в телефонах. Ded vorchít, chto molodyózh sidít v telefónakh. Grandad grumbles that young people are glued to their phones.
Imperfective; present ворчу, ворчишь. Perfective проворчать.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists ворчать (vorchát), glossed 'grumble', without a source; it is usually taken as imitative, like урчать 'to rumble'.
