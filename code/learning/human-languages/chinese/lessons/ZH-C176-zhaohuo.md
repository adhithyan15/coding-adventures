## You'll want to know: zháohuǒ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C176-KNOCK176-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zháohuǒ* — "to catch fire".

> *qiántiān línjū jiā zháohuǒ le* — The neighbours' house caught fire the day before yesterday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zháohuǒ* is built from *zháo* 'to attach to' and 火 'fire', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjak* and *xwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): fire takes hold.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
