## You'll want to know: منافع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C419-MUNAFA419-01]; assesses=[] -->

**منافع** — *munāfā* — "a profit".

> اس سال دکان کو اچھا منافع ہوا۔
>
> *is sāl dukān ko achchhā munāfā huā.*
>
> The shop made a good profit this year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منافع** (*munāfā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *manāfiʿ*, "benefits, gains".
