## You'll want to know: el teletrabajo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C591-DESPIDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el teletrabajo** — "remote work, working from home".

> Hago teletrabajo dos días a la semana.
>
> *I work from home two days a week.*

The verb is *teletrabajar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*teletrabajo* is built on Greek *tele* 'far' and *trabajo*. *Trabajo* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *tripālium*, a device for torture (EtymDB), so the word for work first meant suffering.
