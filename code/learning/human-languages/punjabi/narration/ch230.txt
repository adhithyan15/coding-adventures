Punjabi, chapter 230: Getting Dressed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮੋਕਲਾ (moklā) — roomy; loose-fitting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a cord, then the Punjabi for a knotted cloth button.

You'll want to know: ਮੋਕਲਾ.
ਮੋਕਲਾ — moklā — "roomy; loose-fitting".
ਇਹ ਕੁੜਤਾ ਮੋਕਲਾ ਹੈ ਪਰ ਉਹ ਕਮੀਜ਼ ਭੀੜੀ ਹੈ। eh kuṛtā moklā hai par oh kamīz bhīṛī hai. This kurta is roomy, but that shirt is tight.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੋਕਲਾ (moklā) is inherited from a reconstructed mukna, "loosed"; CDIAL lists Punjabi moklā, "loose" (CDIAL 10157).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਲਾਹੁਣਾ (lāhuṇā) — to take off (clothes or shoes).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a knotted cloth button, then the Punjabi for roomy.

You'll want to know: ਲਾਹੁਣਾ.
ਲਾਹੁਣਾ — lāhuṇā — "to take off (clothes or shoes)".
ਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੁੱਤੀ ਲਾਹੁੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ ghar vich juttī lāhundā hāṁ. I take off my shoes in the house.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕੋਟ ਲਾਹਿਆ, maiṁ koṭ lāhiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਲਾਹੁਣਾ (lāhuṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed lāsayati, "makes slip, makes descend"; CDIAL lists Punjabi lāhṇā, "to bring down, unload" (CDIAL 11042), but not the everyday sense "take off (clothes)".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਝੁਮਕਾ (jhumkā) — a hanging earring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to wrap, then the Punjabi for loose trousers.

You'll want to know: ਝੁਮਕਾ.
ਝੁਮਕਾ — jhumkā — "a hanging earring".
ਭੈਣ ਦੇ ਝੁਮਕੇ ਲਾਲ ਹਨ। bhaiṇ de jhumke lāl han. My sister's earrings are red.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਝੁਮਕਾ (jhumkā) is inherited from a reconstructed jhumma, a form of jhuppa, "crowd, cluster"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jhumkā, "pendent earring" (CDIAL 5404).
