## You'll want to know: پوشاک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C427-POSHAK427-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پوشاک** — *poshāk* — "an outfit; a dress".

> شادی کے لیے سارا نے نئی پوشاک خریدی۔
>
> *shādī ke liye sārā ne naī poshāk kharīdī.*
>
> Sara bought a new outfit for the wedding.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پوشاک** (*poshāk*) is usually traced to Persian *poshāk*, "clothing, a dress".
