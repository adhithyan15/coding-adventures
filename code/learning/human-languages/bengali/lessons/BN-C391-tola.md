## You'll want to know: টলা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C391-SONGHORSHO391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**টলা** — *ṭôlā* — "to totter; to sway".

> ভিড়ের ধাক্কায় বুড়ো লোকটা টলে উঠল।
>
> *bhiṛer dhākkāy buṛo lokṭā ṭôle uṭhlô.*
>
> Jostled by the crowd, the old man tottered.

Past (he/she …): তিনি টললেন, *tini ṭôllen*.

Not তোলা, to lift: ট and ত are different letters.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টলা** (*ṭôlā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ṭalan*, "staggering, slipping", under Sanskrit *ṭálati*, "moves aside, is disturbed" (CDIAL 5450).
