Kannada, chapter 107: Right, Left, Straight, Far, Above.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬಲ (bala) — right (not left).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for please phone, then the Kannada for I'll see you.

You'll want to know: ಬಲ.
ಬಲ — bala — "right (not left)".

Wrap-up Recall.
