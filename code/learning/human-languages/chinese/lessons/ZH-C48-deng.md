## You'll want to know: děng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C48-CLOCK48-05]; assesses=[] -->

*děng* — "to wait; to wait for".

> *wǒ děng nǐ* — I'll wait for you.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*děng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tongX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'equal, of a kind'. The sense 'to wait' is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.
