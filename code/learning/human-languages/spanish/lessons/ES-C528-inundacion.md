## You'll want to know: la inundación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C528-DESASTRE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la inundación** — "the flood".

> Después de la lluvia hubo inundaciones.
>
> *After the rain there were floods.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inundación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inundātiō* 'an overflowing' (EtymDB), built on *unda* 'a wave'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
