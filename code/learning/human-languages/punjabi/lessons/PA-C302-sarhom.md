## You'll want to know: ਸਰ੍ਹੋਂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C302-SOW302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਰ੍ਹੋਂ** — *sarhoṁ* — "mustard (the crop)".

> ਮਾਘ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਰ੍ਹੋਂ ਦੇ ਫੁੱਲ ਪੀਲੇ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *māgh vich sarhoṁ de phull pīle hunde han.*
>
> In Magh the mustard flowers are yellow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਰ੍ਹੋਂ** (*sarhoṁ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sarṣapa*, "mustard seed"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sarhõ*, "rape seed", under it (CDIAL 13281).
