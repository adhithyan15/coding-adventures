## You'll want to know: suāvis, suāve
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C536-BLAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**suāvis, suāve** — "sweet, pleasant".

> Odor rosārum suāvis est.
>
> *The scent of the roses is sweet.*

Two endings. Sweet to every sense, and in manner. English *suave*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**suāvis** is inherited through Proto-Italic from a Proto-Indo-European word for "sweet" (EtymDB), and English *sweet* is a cousin. English *suave* comes from it.
