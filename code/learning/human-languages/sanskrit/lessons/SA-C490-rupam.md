## You'll want to know: रूपम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C490-RUPA490-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रूपम्** — *rūpam* — "appearance; beauty".

> मीरायाः रूपं मातुः इव अस्ति।
>
> *mīrāyāḥ rūpaṃ mātuḥ iva asti.*
>
> Mira looks like her mother.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रूपम्** (*rūpam*) MW suggests, with a "perhaps", a link with *varpa*, *varpas*, "form"; it gives "form, shape, figure" and "loveliness, beauty", and its origin is not settled (MW).
