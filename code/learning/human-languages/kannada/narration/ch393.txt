Kannada, chapter 393: Running a Country.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಂಸತ್ತು (saṃsattu) — a parliament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to campaign, then the Kannada for to address a crowd.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಸತ್ತು.
ಸಂಸತ್ತು — saṃsattu — "a parliament".
ಹೊಸ ನಿಯಮದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಸಂಸತ್ತಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಚರ್ಚೆ ನಡೆಯಿತು. hosa niyamada bagge saṃsattinalli carce naḍeyitu. There was a debate in parliament on the new rule.
ಸಂಸದ, a member of parliament.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಸತ್ತು (saṃsattu) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃsad, "a sitting together, an assembly" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada -u after the final consonant.

ಮಂತ್ರಿ ನಮ್ಮ ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಭೇಟಿ ಕೊಟ್ಟರು. mantri namma śālege bhēṭi koṭṭaru. The minister visited our school.
Plural: ಮಂತ್ರಿಗಳು, mantrigaḷu.
Respectful plural verb: ಮಂತ್ರಿ (mantri) ಬಂದರು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಂತ್ರಿ (mantri) is borrowed from Sanskrit mantrin, "one who gives counsel", a king's adviser (CDIAL 9838).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ (prajāprabhutva) — democracy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a minister, then the Kannada for a constitution.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ.
ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ — prajāprabhutva — "democracy".
ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವದಲ್ಲಿ ಜನರೇ ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಆರಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ. prajāprabhutvadalli janarē sarkāra ārisuttāre. In a democracy the people choose the government.
ಪ್ರಜೆ (citizen) + ಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ (rule).

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ (prajāprabhutva) is a modern word built from Sanskrit prajā, "people, subjects", and prabhutva, "lordship, rule" (Monier-Williams): rule by the people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿನಿಧಿಸು.
ಪ್ರತಿನಿಧಿಸು — pratinidhisu — "to represent".
ಅವಳು ರಾಜ್ಯವನ್ನು ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಪ್ರತಿನಿಧಿಸಿದಳು. avaḷu rājyavannu spardheyalli pratinidhisidaḷu. She represented the state in the competition.
Past (I …): ಪ್ರತಿನಿಧಿಸಿದೆ, pratinidhiside.
ಪ್ರತಿನಿಧಿ, a representative.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರತಿನಿಧಿಸು (pratinidhisu) is built on Sanskrit pratinidhi, "a substitute, a representative" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
