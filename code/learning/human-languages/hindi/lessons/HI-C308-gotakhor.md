## You'll want to know: गोताख़ोर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C308-BACHAV308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गोताख़ोर** — *gotākhor* — "a diver".

> गोताख़ोर कितने बजे पहुँचेंगे?
>
> *gotākhor kitne baje pahũceṅge?*
>
> What time will the divers get here?

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोताख़ोर** (*gotākhor*) is usually traced to Persian *ġōṭa-xōr*, built from *ġōṭa*, a plunge, and *-xōr*, one who takes: someone who dives under the water.
