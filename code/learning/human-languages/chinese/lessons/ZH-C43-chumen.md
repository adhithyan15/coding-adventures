## You'll want to know: chūmén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chūmén* — "to go out".

> *māma chūmén le* — Mum's gone out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chūmén* is built from chū 'to come forth' and mén 'door', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *mwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
