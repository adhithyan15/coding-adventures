# आप कहाँ थे? — the paper's own sentence, one step into the past

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-POST-01, HI-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-06] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read this and say what it means before reading on.

> मैं घर पर हूँ।

## Grammar Lens: the sentence a paper prints
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-POST-01, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-01] -->

That line is reading item 14 of the first practice paper, word for word. Until
this chapter it had one unreadable piece in it, and the piece was **पर**.

Now take one step:

| the paper's line | one step back |
|---|---|
| मैं घर पर हूँ। | मैं घर पर था। |

Everything that made the first line readable makes the second line readable too.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-PAST-03, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-02] -->

> — आप कहाँ थे?
> — मैं घर पर था।

Three things are doing their job at once here. **कहाँ** asks for a place.
**पर** attaches the answer to one. **थे** is the past word आप takes, and **था**
is the one the answer needs, because the answer is about one man speaking for
himself.

A woman answering the same question says **मैं घर पर थी**, and the question
itself does not change: the asker does not know yet who will answer.

## How to answer — three ways
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-PAST-04, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-03, HI-LEX-C87-POST-01] -->

Answer the question three ways, out loud:

- you were at home
- you were at the station
- you were not at home

[PAUSE 4s]

Then ask it of somebody you address as आप, and listen for which of था, थी or थे
comes back.
