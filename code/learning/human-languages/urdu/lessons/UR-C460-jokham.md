## You'll want to know: جوکھم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C460-BRAVE460-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جوکھم** — *jokham* — "a risk".

> پہاڑ پر چڑھنا جوکھم کا کام ہے۔
>
> *pahāṛ par chaṛhnā jokham kā kām hai.*
>
> Climbing a mountain is risky work.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جوکھم** (*jokham*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jokham*, "risk, danger", under Sanskrit *yōgakṣēmá*, "the keeping of one's property" (CDIAL 10527): the sense probably moved through the charge paid against loss to the risk itself.
