## You'll want to know: distribuer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C500-CARTES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**distribuer** — "to deal (cards); to hand out".

> C'est à ton tour de distribuer les cartes.
>
> *It is your turn to deal the cards.*

Also to hand out: *distribuer des tracts*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*distribuer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *distribuere* "to divide out", built on *tribuere* "to assign" (Lewis & Short).
