## You'll want to know: dēbitor, dēbitōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C418-AESALIENUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dēbitor, dēbitōris** — "a debtor".

> Dēbitor pecūniam reddere nōn potuit.
>
> *The debtor could not pay the money back.*

*dēbitor, dēbitōris* m., built on *dēbeō*, to owe. English *debtor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēbitor* is built on *dēbitum* "a debt" (EtymDB), from *dēbeō* "to owe" (Lewis & Short). English *debtor* comes from it through French.
