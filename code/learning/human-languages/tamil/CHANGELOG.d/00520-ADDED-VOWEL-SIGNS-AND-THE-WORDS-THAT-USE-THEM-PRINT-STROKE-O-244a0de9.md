## Added — vowel signs and the words that use them print stroke-order filmstrips

ா, ி, ீ, ெ, ே and ை now have a cited pen path, and the book prints a
stroke-order filmstrip for the lessons that teach them and for words that
use them. Each sign is one continuous stroke. Its start, direction and pen
lifts come from native Tamil writers' pen traces stored in HP Labs India's
LipiTk Tamil handwriting recognizer, and the path is fitted to the book's
Noto Sans Tamil letters.

- **Signs written before the consonant come first.** In a word, ெ, ே and ை
  are drawn before the consonant they follow when typed, and ொ and ோ are
  drawn in two halves around it, the way they are written by hand: மேசை is
  ே, ம, ை, ச. The strip calls its groups parts.
- **New filmstrips (14 lessons):** TA-S09 (ி), TA-S102 (ோ), TA-S114 (ா),
  TA-S123 (ே), TA-S128 (ை), TA-S132 (ீ) and TA-S133 (ெ); and the words
  சரி (TA-W07-write-sari), சொ (TA-W08-short-o-observe), போ (TA-W15),
  மேலே (TA-W22), கடை (TA-W24), மேசை (TA-W26) and சரியா (TA-W30).
- **Not yet:** the pulli ், ு and ூ have no cited pen path, so TA-S08,
  TA-S121, TA-S135 and every word that carries one of them (வணக்கம்,
  பேசு, சொல் and others) still print no filmstrip.
- Lesson prose, narration and durations do not change: filmstrips are
  printed in the book only.
