## You'll want to know: شادی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C342-TAZYIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شادی** (*shâdi*) — "joy, happiness".

> مردم از شادی فریاد میزدند.
>
> *Mardom az shâdi faryâd mizadand.*
>
> People were shouting for joy.

From *shâd* "glad". *Shâdi kardan* is "to rejoice".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shâdi* adds the noun suffix *-i* to *shâd* "glad", a native Persian word. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
