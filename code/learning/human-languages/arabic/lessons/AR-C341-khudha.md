## You'll want to know: خوذة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C341-FARIS341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خوذة** — *khūdha* — "a helmet".

> لبس العامل خوذة صفراء.
>
> *labisa al-ʿāmil khūdha ṣafrāʾ.*
>
> The worker put on a yellow helmet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خوذة** (*khūdha*) is usually traced to Persian *khōd*, "a helmet", an old borrowing into Arabic.
