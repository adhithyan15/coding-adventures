Urdu, chapter 364: Clearly So.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ظاہر (zāhir) — evident; plain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a nagging fear, then the Urdu for confidence.

You'll want to know: ظاہر.
ظاہر — zāhir — "evident; plain".
اگر وہ نہیں آیا تو ظاہر ہے وہ ناراض ہے۔ agar voh nahīṅ āyā to zāhir hai voh nārāz hai. If he has not come, he is clearly cross.

The word, taken apart.
ظاہر (zāhir) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ẓāhir, "outward, apparent".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ابھرنا (ubharnā) — to emerge; to come to the surface.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for really, then the Urdu for prominent.

You'll want to know: ابھرنا.
ابھرنا — ubharnā — "to emerge; to come to the surface".
اگر کوئی نیا سوال ابھرا تو ہم بات کریں گے۔ agar koī nayā savāl ubharā to ham bāt kareṅ ge. If a new question comes up, we will talk.
Past (it …): وہ سوال پھر ابھرا, voh savāl phir ubharā.

The word, taken apart.
ابھرنا (ubharnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ubharnā, "to rise, to swell", under Sanskrit údbharati, "raises" (CDIAL 2038); ابھارنا, "to bring out", is its causative.
