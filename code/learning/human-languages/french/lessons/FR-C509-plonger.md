## You'll want to know: plonger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C509-EAU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**plonger** — "to dive".

> Les enfants plongent du haut du rocher.
>
> *The children dive off the top of the rock.*

Spelling: *nous plongeons*. *La plongée* — scuba diving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*plonger* is inherited from Old French *plongier* (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *plumbicāre* "to sink like lead", built on *plumbum* "lead" (Lewis & Short).
