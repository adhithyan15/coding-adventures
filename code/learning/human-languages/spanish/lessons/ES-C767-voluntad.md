## You'll want to know: la voluntad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C767-PORVENIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la voluntad** — "will, willpower".

> Con fuerza de voluntad, todo es posible.
>
> *With willpower, anything is possible.*

*Fuerza de voluntad* — willpower. *Buena voluntad* — goodwill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*voluntad* is usually traced to Latin *voluntās*, *voluntātem* "will", built on *velle* "to want".
