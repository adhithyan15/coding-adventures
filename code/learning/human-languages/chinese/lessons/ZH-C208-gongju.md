## You'll want to know: gōngjù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C208-FIX208-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngjù* — "tool".

> *shīfu dàile gōngjù* — The repairman brought his tools.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngjù* is built from *gōng* 'work' and *jù* 'an implement', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *gjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to prepare'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
