## You'll want to know: नमति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C73-VOICE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नमति** (*namati*) — "(he, she) bows". It is the verb inside **नमस्ते** and
**प्रणामः**.
