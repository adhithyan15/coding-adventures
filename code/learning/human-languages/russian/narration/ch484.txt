Russian, chapter 484: In Plaster.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
гипс (gips) — a plaster cast; plaster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a graze, then the Russian for to heal.

You'll want to know: гипс.
гипс (gips) — "a plaster cast; plaster".
Шесть недель он ходил с рукой в гипсе. Shest nedél on khodíl s rukóy v gípse. He went about with his arm in plaster for six weeks.
Masculine. В гипсе — in plaster.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists гипс (gips) without a source; it is usually traced through German Gips to Greek gýpsos 'chalk, gypsum'.

Lesson 2 of 6.

You'll want to know: костыль.
костыль (kostýl) — "a crutch".
После операции бабушка ходила на костылях. Pósle operátsii bábushka khodíla na kostylyákh. After the operation Grandma walked on crutches.
Masculine; genitive костыля. Ходить на костылях — to walk on crutches.

The word, taken apart.
костыль (kostýl) is derived from кость 'bone' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Proto-Slavic kostь.

Lesson 3 of 6.
вывих (vývikh) — a dislocation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a plaster cast, then the Russian for a crutch.

You'll want to know: вывих.
вывих (vývikh) — "a dislocation".
Врач сказал, что у меня вывих плеча. Vrach skazál, chto u menyá vývikh plechá. The doctor said I had a dislocated shoulder.
Masculine. The verb is вывихнуть — to dislocate, to twist.

The word, taken apart.
вывих (vývikh) is built on вы- 'out' and the stem of вывихнуть 'to dislocate'; EtymDB has no entry, and the origin of the stem is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
растяжение (rastyazhéniye) — a strain (of a muscle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a crutch, then the Russian for a dislocation.

You'll want to know: растяжение.
растяжение (rastyazhéniye) — "a strain (of a muscle)".
У футболиста растяжение мышцы. U futbolísta rastyazhéniye mýshtsy. The footballer has a pulled muscle.
Neuter. Растяжение связок — a sprain.

The word, taken apart.
растяжение (rastyazhéniye) is built on растянуть 'to stretch', from тянуть 'to pull', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic tęgnǫti (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 6.
хромать (khromát) — to limp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dislocation, then the Russian for a strain.

You'll want to know: хромать.
хромать (khromát) — "to limp".
Собака хромает на переднюю лапу. Sobáka khromáyet na perédnyuyu lápu. The dog is limping on its front paw.
Imperfective; present хромаю. The adjective is хромой — lame.

The word, taken apart.
хромать (khromát) is built on хромой 'lame'; EtymDB lists both without a source, and хромой is usually traced to Proto-Slavic xromъ.
