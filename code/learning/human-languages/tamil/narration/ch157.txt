Tamil, chapter 157: To hide, To save, To blow, To burn, To put out.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
மறை (maṟai) — to hide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to win, then the Tamil for to find.

You'll want to know: மறை.
மறை — maṟai — "to hide".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: maṟai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: maṟai, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say maṟai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for to win, then the Tamil for to find, then say maṟai again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does மறை (maṟai) mean? ("To hide".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to burn and to put out; to hug.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: famous, dangerous, safe, true, false]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: purple, grey; ash, to sing, to cook, to cut]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to break, to put, to send, to look for, to teach]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to forget, to jump, to swim, to fly, to fall]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Famous? (பிரபலமான, pirapalamāṉa.) Dangerous? (ஆபத்தான, āpattāṉa.) Safe? (பாதுகாப்பான, pātukāppāṉa.) True? (உண்மையான, uṇmaiyāṉa.) False? (பொய்யான, poyyāṉa.) Purple? (ஊதா, ūtā.) Grey; ash? (சாம்பல், cāmpal.) To sing? (பாடு, pāṭu.) To cook? (சமை, camai.) To cut? (வெட்டு, veṭṭu.) To break? (உடை, uṭai.) To put? (வை, vai.) To send? (அனுப்பு, anuppu.) To look for? (தேடு, tēṭu.) To teach? (கற்பி, kaṟpi.) To forget? (மற, maṟa.) To jump? (குதி, kuti.) To swim? (நீந்து, nīntu.) To fly? (பற, paṟa.) To fall? (விழு, viḻu.)
