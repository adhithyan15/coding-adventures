## You'll want to know: सबूत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C294-TAFTISH294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सबूत** — *sabūt* — "evidence; proof".

> पुलिस को सबूत चाहिए, नहीं तो चोर छूट जाएगा।
>
> *pulis ko sabūt cāhiye, nahīṃ to cor chūṭ jāegā.*
>
> The police need evidence, or the thief will go free.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सबूत** (*sabūt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *thubūt*, "firmness, being established": what makes a claim stand. Hindi also spells it **सुबूत**.
