# અમાસ (amās) — new moon day

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C174-MONTH174-05, GU-LEX-C175-MOON175-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for beginning, then the Gujarati for full moon day.

## You'll want to know: અમાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C175-MOON175-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અમાસ** — *amās* — "new moon day".

> **અમાસની રાત્રે અંધારું હશે.** — *amāsnī rātre andhārũ haśe* — "It will be dark on the night of the new moon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અમાસ** (*amās*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *amāvāsyā*, "the night of the new moon", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati *amās* with that sense (CDIAL 565).
