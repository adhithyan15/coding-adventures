### Spanish A1 shopping tranche — five exam-derived words (chapter 402)

Chapter 402 adds **costar**, **la camisa**, **la camiseta**, **la talla**, and
**el gorro** as a clothes-shop sequence. The chapter uses separate A1 action and
thing paths, contrasts *costar/contar* and *gorro/gordo*, and refuses an origin
story for *gorro* where the dictionaries do not support one.

Two delayed recall lessons turn the vocabulary into a compact exchange: name a
garment, ask its size, and ask what it costs. The slice ships with authored
capability, required extension, and generated book, narration, modality,
progress, and gentle-ramp evidence.
