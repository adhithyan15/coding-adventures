## You'll want to know: حكة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C508-JILD508-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حكة** — *ḥikka* — "an itch, itching".

> الحكة تزعجني في الليل.
>
> *al-ḥikka tuzʿijunī fī al-layl.*
>
> The itching bothers me at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حكة** (*ḥikka*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-k-k*, "to rub, to scratch" (Lane). Ḥikka is the itch that makes you scratch.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
