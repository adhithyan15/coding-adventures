## You'll want to know: il torneo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C512-TORNEO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il torneo** — "tournament".

> A giugno c'è il torneo di tennis della scuola.
>
> *In June there is the school tennis tournament.*

*Vincere un torneo* — to win a tournament.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*torneo* comes from Old French *tornei* "a tournament", built on *torner* "to turn", from Latin *tornāre* "to turn on a lathe" (Lewis & Short).
