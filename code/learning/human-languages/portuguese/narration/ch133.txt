Portuguese, chapter 133: To go down, To rent, To repair, To tell, To walk.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
descer — to go down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to fall, then the Portuguese for to go up.

You'll want to know: descer.
descer — "to go down".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: descer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: descer, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say descer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for to fall, then the Portuguese for to go up, then say descer again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does descer mean? ("To go down".) Say it once more.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ready, safe; sure, dangerous, wet, dry]

[your turn — say: cool; fresh, soft, hard, round, wide]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Nice? (simpático.) Kind? (gentil.) Serious? (sério.) Right? (certo.) Wrong? (errado.) Ready? (pronto.) Safe; sure? (seguro.) Dangerous? (perigoso.) Wet? (molhado.) Dry? (seco.) Cool; fresh? (fresco.) Soft? (macio.) Hard? (duro.) Round? (redondo.) Wide? (largo.) Narrow? (estreito.) Angry? (zangado.) Bitter? (amargo.) Salty? (salgado.) Light? (leve.) Heavy? (pesado.) Thin? (magro.) Pleased? (contente.) The weekend? (o fim de semana.) A date? (a data.)
