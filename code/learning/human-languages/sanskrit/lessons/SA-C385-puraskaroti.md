## You'll want to know: पुरस्करोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C385-SPARDHA385-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरस्करोति** — *puraskaroti* — "rewards (with a prize)".

> यदि छात्रः प्रथमः, तर्हि विद्यालयः तं पुरस्करोति।
>
> *yadi chātraḥ prathamaḥ, tarhi vidyālayaḥ taṃ puraskaroti.*
>
> If the student comes first, the school rewards him.

Past (he …): सः छात्रं पुरस्कृतवान्, *saḥ chātraṃ puraskṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरस्करोति** (*puraskaroti*) is built from *puras*, "in front", and the root √kṛ, "to make"; MW gives *puras-√kṛ* "to place before" and "to respect, to honour", and "rewards with a prize" is its modern sense (MW).
