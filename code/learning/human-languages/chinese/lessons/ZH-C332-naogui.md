## You'll want to know: nàoguǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C332-GHOST332-03]; assesses=[] -->

*nàoguǐ* — "to be haunted".

> *dàjiā dōu shuō nàge fángzi nàoguǐ* — Everyone says that house is haunted.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nàoguǐ* is built from *nào* 'to make a din' and 鬼 'a ghost', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nraewH* and *kjw+jX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'not quiet' and 'to disturb'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
