Kannada, chapter 198: To scrub, To bring, To carry away, To hold, To let go.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉಜ್ಜು (ujju) — to scrub, to rub, to brush (teeth).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a headmaster, then the Kannada for a secretary.

You'll want to know: ಉಜ್ಜು.
ಉಜ್ಜು — ujju — "to scrub, to rub, to brush (teeth)".

The word, taken apart.
ಉಜ್ಜು (ujju) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil urai and Telugu ora (DEDR 665). DEDR lists urdu, uddu, ujju, "to rub". DEDR 665 compares, with a query, Sanskrit uñch.

You'll want to know: ಹಿಡಿ.
ಹಿಡಿ — hiḍi — "to hold, to catch".

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿಡಿ (hiḍi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil piṭi (DEDR 4148). DEDR gives the older Kannada piḍi, "to seize, hold": the p- has become h-.
