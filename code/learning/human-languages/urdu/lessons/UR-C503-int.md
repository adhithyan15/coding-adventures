## You'll want to know: اینٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C503-SILAN503-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اینٹ** — *īṅṭ* — "a brick".

> مزدور نے دیوار کے لیے اینٹیں اٹھائیں۔
>
> *mazdūr ne dīvār ke liye īṅṭeṅ uṭhāīṅ.*
>
> The labourer carried bricks for the wall.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اینٹ** (*īṅṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *iṣṭakā*, "a brick" (CDIAL 1600).
