## You'll want to know: guānzhòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C292-RADIO292-02]; assesses=[] -->

*guānzhòng* — "viewers; spectators; the people watching a show".

> *guānzhòng dōu hěn xǐhuan zhège jiémù* — The viewers all love this show.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guānzhòng* is built from *guān* 'to watch' and *zhòng* 'a crowd', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwan* and *tsyuwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to view' and the second 'many', adding that three people make a crowd.
