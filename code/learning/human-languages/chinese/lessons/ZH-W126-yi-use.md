## Script — four strokes in two pairs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YI-USE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 以

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, two strokes.** A vertical that runs down and flicks up to the right at its foot, then a short dot to the right of it, falling to the lower right.

**The right piece, two strokes.** A long left-falling stroke from the top right, sweeping down toward the foot of the first stroke, then a dot at the lower right, falling to the lower right.

2 + 2 = four.
