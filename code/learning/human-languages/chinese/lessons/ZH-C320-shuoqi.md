## You'll want to know: 说起
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C320-PHONE320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**说起** — *shuōqǐ* — "to mention; to bring up a subject".

> **他在信里说起了你** *tā zài xìn lǐ shuōqǐ le nǐ* — He mentioned you in his letter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**说起** (*shuōqǐ*) is built from 说 'to say' and 起 'to rise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sywet* and *khiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tell' and the second 'to rise' and 'to stand up'.
