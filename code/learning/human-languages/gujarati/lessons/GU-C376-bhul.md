## You'll want to know: ભૂલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C376-BHUL376-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ભૂલ** — *bhūl* — "a mistake".

> જો હિસાબમાં એક પણ ભૂલ હશે તો આખું પરિણામ ખોટું આવશે.
>
> *jo hisābmā̃ ek paṇ bhūl haśe to ākhũ pariṇām khoṭũ āvśe.*
>
> If there is even one mistake in the accounts, the whole result will come out wrong.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભૂલ** (*bhūl*) shares its source with **ભૂલવું** (*bhūlvũ*), "to forget": CDIAL lists both under a reconstructed *bhull*, "err, forget" (CDIAL 9538).
