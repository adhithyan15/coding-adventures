# Review: Greetings & Introductions

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Yesterday you learned four greetings and how to say your name.
Before you look at anything: say "hello" and "my name is..." in Spanish, out
loud, right now.

## New Material

No new vocabulary — this unit resurfaces Unit 1's phrases in a fresh
combination instead of repeating the same lines.

Imagine you're getting into someone's car in the morning:

- "**¡Hola! Buenos días.**" — Hi! Good morning.
- "**Me llamo [their name]. ¿Cómo estás?**" — My name is ___. How are you?

And the same exchange later in the day:

- "**¡Hola! Buenas tardes.**" — Hi! Good afternoon.
- "**Buenas noches**" — used when *leaving* in the evening, not just arriving
  — a small usage note Unit 1 didn't cover: *buenas noches* doubles as both
  "good evening" and "goodnight."

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: greet someone in the morning, using *buenos días*]
- [YOU SAY: greet someone in the afternoon, using *buenas tardes*]
- [YOU SAY: introduce yourself using *me llamo*, then ask *¿cómo estás?*]

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Which greeting would you use to say goodnight to someone leaving
your car at 9pm? ... *Buenas noches* — same phrase, different direction.
