## You'll want to know: പക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C523-PAKA523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**പക** — *paka* — "a grudge; vengefulness".

> പഴയ വഴക്കിന്റെ പക അവൻ മറന്നില്ല.
>
> *paḻaya vaḻakkinṟe paka avan maṟannilla.*
>
> He did not forget the grudge from the old quarrel.

പക വീട്ടുക is to take revenge, literally to pay off a grudge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പക** (*paka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3808 lists it beside verbs for dividing and sharing, with the sense of separation and enmity (DEDR 3808).
