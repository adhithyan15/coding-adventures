# ಪಳಗು (paḷagu) — to get practised; to become skilled

## You'll want to know: ಪಳಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C520-TANDA520-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಳಗು** — *paḷagu* — "to get practised; to become skilled".

> ಎರಡು ವರ್ಷದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನು ಈ ಕೆಲಸದಲ್ಲಿ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ಪಳಗಿದನು.
>
> *eraḍu varṣadalli avanu ī kelasadalli cennāgi paḷagidanu.*
>
> In two years he became well practised in this work.

Past (I …): ಪಳಗಿದೆ, *paḷagide*.

In what: -ಅಲ್ಲಿ. ಪಳಗಿಸು, to tame or train an animal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಳಗು** (*paḷagu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4000 lists older Kannada *paḻagu*, "to become used to, to be trained", and *paḻaka*, "use, practice, habit", beside Tamil *paḻaku*.
