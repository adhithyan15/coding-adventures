## You'll want to know: سدھارنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C356-ISLAH356-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سدھارنا** — *sudhārnā* — "to improve; to put right".

> اس نے اپنا کام سدھارا۔
>
> *us ne apnā kām sudhārā.*
>
> She improved her work.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گھر سدھارا, *us ne ghar sudhārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سدھارنا** (*sudhārnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sudhārnā*, "to mend", under *\*śuddhakāra*, "making clean", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12521).
