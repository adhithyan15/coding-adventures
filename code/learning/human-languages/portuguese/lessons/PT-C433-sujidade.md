## You'll want to know: a sujidade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C433-BARULHO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a sujidade** — "dirt, dirtiness".

> Havia sujidade debaixo da cama.
>
> *There was dirt under the bed.*

In Brazil: *a sujeira*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sujidade* is built on *sujo* 'dirty', which is inherited from Latin *sūcidus* 'juicy, greasy' (EtymDB).
