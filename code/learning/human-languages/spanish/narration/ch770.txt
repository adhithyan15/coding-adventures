Spanish, chapter 770: Las tiendas del barrio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la tintorería — the dry cleaner's.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the plumber, then the Spanish for the electrician.

You'll want to know: la tintorería.
la tintorería — "the dry cleaner's".
Llevo el traje a la tintorería porque no se puede lavar. I take the suit to the dry cleaner's because it can't be washed.

The word, taken apart.
tintorería is built on tintorero 'a dyer', from tinto 'dyed', which comes from Latin tīnctus, the participle of tingere 'to wet, to dye' (Lewis & Short). Dry cleaners were first dyers.

carnicería is built on carnicero 'a butcher' with the ending -ería (EtymDB). The root is carne, which comes from Latin carō, carnem 'flesh'.
