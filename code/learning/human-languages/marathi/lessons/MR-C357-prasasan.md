## You'll want to know: प्रशासन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C357-SHASAN357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रशासन** — *praśāsan* — "administration".

> पुरानंतर प्रशासनाने लोकांना मदत केली.
>
> *purānantar praśāsnāne lokānnā madat kelī.*
>
> After the flood the administration helped people.

Neuter.

Neuter; प्रशासकीय, administrative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रशासन** (*praśāsan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *praśāsana*, "instruction, government" (Monier-Williams).
