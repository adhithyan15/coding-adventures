## You'll want to know: cognōmen, cognōminis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C325-BULLA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**cognōmen, cognōminis** — "an extra family name, a surname".

> Ob magnam victōriam cognōmen novum accēpit.
>
> *Because of a great victory he received a new surname.*

Neuter, 3rd declension. The third name, which often told something about a person or a branch of the family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cognōmen** joins *co-*, "together with", and *nōmen*, "name" (EtymDB): the name added to the family name.
