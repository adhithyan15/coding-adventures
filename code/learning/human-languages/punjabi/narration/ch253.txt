Punjabi, chapter 253: Rules at the Gate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਨਿਯਮ (niyam) — a rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to take out, then the Punjabi for perhaps.

You'll want to know: ਨਿਯਮ.
ਨਿਯਮ — niyam — "a rule".
ਬੈਂਕ ਦਾ ਨਿਯਮ ਸਖ਼ਤ ਹੈ। baiṅk dā niyam sakht hai. The bank's rule is strict.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਿਯਮ (niyam) is taken over from Sanskrit niyama, "any fixed rule or law", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).

[pause 2 seconds]

ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੰਮੀ ਕਤਾਰ ਹੈ। baiṅk vich lammī katār hai. There is a long queue in the bank.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਹਟਣਾ (haṭṇā) — to move out of the way; to step back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a notice, then the Punjabi for a queue.

You'll want to know: ਹਟਣਾ.
ਹਟਣਾ — haṭṇā — "to move out of the way; to step back".
ਲੋਕ ਬੱਸ ਲਈ ਸੜਕ ਤੋਂ ਹਟਦੇ ਹਨ। lok bass laī saṛak ton haṭde han. People move off the road for the bus.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸੜਕ ਤੋਂ ਹਟਿਆ, maiṁ saṛak ton haṭiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਹਟਣਾ (haṭṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed haṭṭ, "move"; CDIAL lists Punjabi haṭṇā, "to move back or out of the way", under it (CDIAL 13943).
