## You'll want to know: દિલાસો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C487-CAKRI487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**દિલાસો** — *dilāso* — "comfort; consoling words".

> મિત્રે મને દિલાસો આપ્યો.
>
> *mitre mane dilāso āpyo.*
>
> My friend comforted me.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દિલાસો** (*dilāso*) is usually traced to Persian *dilāsā*, "consolation", built on *dil*, "heart".

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
