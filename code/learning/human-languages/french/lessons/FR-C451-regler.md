## You'll want to know: régler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C451-BANQUE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**régler** — "to settle (a bill), to pay; to adjust".

> Nous avons réglé la note de l'hôtel par carte.
>
> *We paid the hotel bill by card.*

*Régler un problème* — to sort out a problem; *régler la radio* — to tune the radio.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*régler* is built on *règle* 'rule, ruler', which goes back to Latin *rēgula* (EtymDB).
