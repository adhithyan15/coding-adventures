Marathi, chapter 98: Sun, Moon, Sea, Fire, Snow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सूर्य (sūrya) — the sun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a field, then the Marathi for a star.

You'll want to know: सूर्य.
सूर्य — sūrya — "the sun".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sūrya]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sūrya, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sūrya]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a field, then the Marathi for a star, then say sūrya again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सूर्य (sūrya) mean? ("The sun".) Say it once more.
