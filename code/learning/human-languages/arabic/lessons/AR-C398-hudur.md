## You'll want to know: حضور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C398-TALIM398-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حضور** — *ḥuḍūr* — "attendance".

> الحضور إلزامي في هذا الصف.
>
> *al-ḥuḍūr ilzāmī fī hādhā aṣ-ṣaff.*
>
> Attendance is compulsory in this class.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حضور** (*ḥuḍūr*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ḍ-r*, "to be present" (Lane). *Ḥuḍūr* is a verbal noun of *ḥaḍara*, to be present: attendance.
