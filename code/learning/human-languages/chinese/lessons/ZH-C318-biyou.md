## You'll want to know: bǐyǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C318-LETTER318-01]; assesses=[] -->

*bǐyǒu* — "pen pal".

> *wǒ xiǎoshíhou yǒu yí ge bǐyǒu* — When I was little I had a pen pal.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: bǐ is said with a rising tone before yǒu, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǐyǒu* is built from *bǐ* 'a pen' and *yǒu* 'a friend', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pit* and *hjuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which names the first as the writing brush and glosses the second as those who share one mind.
