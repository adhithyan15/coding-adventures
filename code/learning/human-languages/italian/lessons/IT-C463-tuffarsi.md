## You'll want to know: tuffarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C463-SCOGLI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tuffarsi** — "to dive, to plunge".

> Ci tuffiamo dallo scoglio più alto.
>
> *We dive off the highest rock.*

*Tuffarsi in un libro* — to lose yourself in a book.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuffarsi* is the reflexive of *tuffare* 'to plunge', which EtymDB traces to Lombardic *taufan* 'to dip, to immerse', a Germanic word borrowed into Italian.
