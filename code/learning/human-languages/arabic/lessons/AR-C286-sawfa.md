# سوف (sawfa) — will, for a plan

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-BI-SABAB-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-AS-SABAB-HUWA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "because of the rain". (*Bi-sabab al-maṭar*.) Then "the
reason is that". (*As-sababu huwa anna*.)

[PAUSE 1s] You have heard *sa-usāfiru al-usbūʿa al-qādim*, "I will travel
next week". Reasons are often reasons for a plan, and a plan needs a future.

## Grammar Lens: sa- and sawfa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-FUTURE-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C157-MUSTAQBAL157-01, AR-LEX-C159-ACT159-04, AR-LEX-C87-TIME87-02] -->

Arabic builds the future on the present. Put **سـ** — *sa-* — onto its front,
with no space, or put **سوف** — *sawfa* — before it as a word of its own:

> **سأسافر غدا.** — *sa-usāfiru ghadan* — I will travel tomorrow.

> **سوف نسافر في العطلة القادمة.** — *sawfa nusāfiru fī al-ʿuṭla al-qādima* —
> We will travel in the next holiday.

The two say the same "will". *Sawfa* is the fuller one, and it is often
heard for something further off or said with weight.

## Grammar Lens: lan, will not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-LAN-01]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-SUBJUNCTIVE-01, AR-LEX-C98-WORLD98-04] -->

For "will not", the *sa-* goes and **لن** — *lan* — takes its place. After
*lan* the verb is in the subjunctive, as after *an*:

> **لن أسافر في العطلة.** — *lan usāfira fī al-ʿuṭla* — I won't travel in the
> holiday.

*Lan adhhaba*, I won't go; *lan nadhhaba*, we won't go.

- [YOU SAY: "I will travel tomorrow" — *sa-usāfiru ghadan*]

- [YOU SAY: "I won't go" — *lan adhhaba*]
- [YOU SAY: a plan with its reason — *sa-usāfiru li-anna ʿindī waqtan*]
- [YOU SAY: the opposite — *lan usāfira li-anna ʿindī ʿamalan*]
