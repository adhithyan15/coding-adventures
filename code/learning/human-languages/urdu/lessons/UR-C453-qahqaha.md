# قہقہہ (qahqahā) — a burst of laughter

## You'll want to know: قہقہہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C453-LAUGH453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قہقہہ** — *qahqahā* — "a burst of laughter".

> مجھے اس کا قہقہہ بہت اچھا لگتا ہے۔
>
> *mujhe us kā qahqahā bahut achchhā lagtā hai.*
>
> I really like her loud laugh.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قہقہہ** (*qahqahā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qahqaha*, "loud laughter", a word that imitates the sound.
