## You'll want to know: যৌবন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C488-SHOISHOB488-03]; assesses=[] -->

**যৌবন** — *joubôn* — "youth; young adulthood".

> যৌবনে দাদু সেনাবাহিনীতে ছিলেন।
>
> *joubône dādu senābāhinīte chhilen.*
>
> In his youth my grandfather was in the army.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**যৌবন** (*joubôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *yauvana*, "youth, youthfulness" (Monier-Williams).
