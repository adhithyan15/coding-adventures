## You'll want to know: значок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C503-COLLECTING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**значок** (*znachók*) — "a badge".

> У него на рюкзаке много значков.
>
> *U negó na ryukzaké mnógo znachkóv.*
>
> He has a lot of badges on his backpack.

Masculine; genitive значка. A diminutive of *знак* — a sign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*значок* is a diminutive of *знак* 'a sign', built on the root of *знать* 'to know'; EtymDB has no entry for *значок*.
