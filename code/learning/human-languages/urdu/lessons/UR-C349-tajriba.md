## You'll want to know: تجربہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C349-TAHQIQ349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تجربہ** — *tajribā* — "an experiment; experience".

> سائنسدان نے ایک نیا تجربہ کیا۔
>
> *sāinsdān ne ek nayā tajribā kiyā.*
>
> The scientist did a new experiment.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تجربہ** (*tajribā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tajriba*, "a trial, a test".
