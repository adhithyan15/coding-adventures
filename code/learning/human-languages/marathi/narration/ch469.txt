Marathi, chapter 469: Harsh Natures.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कठोर (kaṭhor) — harsh; stern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for sensitive, then the Marathi for helpful to others.

You'll want to know: कठोर.
कठोर — kaṭhor — "harsh; stern".
ते शिक्षक खूप कठोर आहेत. te śikṣak khūp kaṭhor āhet. That teacher is very stern.
Unchanging: कठोर (kaṭhor) शब्द, harsh words; कठोर (kaṭhor) नियम, strict rules.

The word, taken apart.
कठोर (kaṭhor) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kaṭhora, "hard, stiff; cruel" (CDIAL 2651).

Lesson 2 of 5.
निर्दयी (nirdayī) — merciless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for helpful to others, then the Marathi for harsh.

You'll want to know: निर्दयी.
निर्दयी — nirdayī — "merciless".
कुत्र्याला मारणारा माणूस निर्दयी असतो. kutryālā mārṇārā māṇūs nirdayī asto. A man who beats a dog is merciless.
निर्, without, + दया, pity, + the ending -ई.

The word, taken apart.
निर्दयी (nirdayī) is built on the tatsama nirdaya, Sanskrit for "pitiless, cruel" (Monier-Williams), with the Marathi adjective ending -ī.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कपटी (kapṭī) — deceitful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: कपटी.

From कपट, deceit. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
कपटी (kapṭī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kapaṭin, "deceitful" (Monier-Williams), built on kapaṭa, "fraud".
