## You'll want to know: la acusación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C626-JUICIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la acusación** — "the accusation, the charge".

> La acusación es muy grave.
>
> *The accusation is very serious.*

In court *la acusación* is also the prosecution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acusación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *accusatio* 'an accusation', built on *accusare*, from *ad* and *causa* 'case at law' (EtymDB).
