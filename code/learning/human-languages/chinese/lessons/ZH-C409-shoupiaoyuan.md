## You'll want to know: shòupiàoyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C409-TRAFFIC409-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shòupiàoyuán* — "ticket seller; bus conductor".

> *shòupiàoyuán zuò zài ménkǒu* — The conductor sits by the door.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shòupiàoyuán* is built from *shòu* 'to sell', 票 'a ticket' and *yuán* 'a member of staff'. The first and the last are inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyuwH* and *hjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of 票, so its history is not settled.
