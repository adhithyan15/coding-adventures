## You'll want to know: el candidato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C602-VOTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el candidato** — "the candidate".

> Hay tres candidatos para alcalde.
>
> *There are three candidates for mayor.*

A woman is *la candidata*. Also used for a job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*candidato* is a learned borrowing from Latin *candidatus* 'clothed in white', built on *candidus* 'white': Roman candidates wore white togas (Lewis & Short).
