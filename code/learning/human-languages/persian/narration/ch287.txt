Lesson 1 of 1.
My own line — the first Persian writing with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice — check the dots first.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Everything you have written so far had something to reach for: a letter to trace, a word to copy, a voice to take down.
[pause 1 second]
This does not. You choose the words, from a supply that is closed: only what this book has taught you.

Writing — controlled composition.
[pause 2 seconds]
Write one line about yourself in Persian script, right to left: your name, and how you are today. Every dot and every join is now yours to place.
There is no Persian model, word bank, or romanization on this page. Choose the pieces yourself and put them in Persian order. If your name needs a letter you have not yet written, write the rest and check only the letters you own. This first composition is untimed: finish the line before you look back at any earlier lesson.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice — check the dots first.
[pause 2 seconds]
Copying hid a decision from you, because the model always had the right dots. In Persian script a dot is part of the letter, not a mark on top of it. ب and پ share one body: one dot below, or three. Move a dot or drop one and you have written a different letter, and often a different word.
[pause 2 seconds]
And ی changes its coat: two dots below at the start or in the middle of a word, and no dots when it ends one. Your line may end a word on it.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read your line back, letter by letter, and count the dots on each one before you check anything else. Repair the first wrong dot, then write the whole line once more from meaning.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
What separates ب from پ? (Only the dots: one below, or three below.)
[pause 2 seconds]
How many dots does ی carry at the end of a word? (None.)
One chosen line closes this writing rung. It does not prove A1 attainment, readiness, human validation, or completion of the timed writing task that comes next.
