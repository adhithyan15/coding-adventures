Persian, chapter 269: Only, Even, Completely, Exactly, Fortunately.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فقط (faqat) — only.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a street gutter, then the Persian for a pavement.

You'll want to know: فقط.
فقط — faqat — "only".

The word, taken apart.
faqat is borrowed from Arabic faqaṭ, "only". Its Arabic root is q-ṭ-ṭ (Lane). It is fa-, "so", with qaṭ, "only, enough".

Lesson 5 of 5.
خوشبختانه (khoshbakhtâne) — fortunately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for completely, then the Persian for exactly.

You'll want to know: خوشبختانه.
خوشبختانه — khoshbakhtâne — "fortunately".

The word, taken apart.
khoshbakhtâne is khosh, "good", with bakht, "luck", and the ending -âne: by good luck. All are native; bakht is usually traced to Middle Persian baxt.
