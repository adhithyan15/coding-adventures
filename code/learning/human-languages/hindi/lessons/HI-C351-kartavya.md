## You'll want to know: कर्तव्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C351-ADHIKAR351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्तव्य** — *kartavya* — "a duty".

> नियम मानना हमारा कर्तव्य है।
>
> *niyam mānnā hamārā kartavya hai.*
>
> Following the rules is our duty.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्तव्य** (*kartavya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *kartavya*, "what ought to be done" (Monier-Williams).
