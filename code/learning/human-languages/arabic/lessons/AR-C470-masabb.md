## You'll want to know: مصب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C470-YANBU470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مصب** — *maṣabb* — "a river mouth".

> عند المصب يلتقي النهر بالبحر.
>
> *ʿinda al-maṣabb yaltaqī an-nahr bi-l-baḥr.*
>
> At the mouth the river meets the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصب** (*maṣabb*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-b-b*, "to pour" (Lane). It is the place where a river pours out; *ṣabba*, "to pour", shares the root.
