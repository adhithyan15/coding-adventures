Lesson 1 of 5.
হৃৎপিণ্ড (hritpinḍô) — the heart (the organ).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the voice, then the Bengali for to knit one's brows.

You'll want to know: হৃৎপিণ্ড.
হৃৎপিণ্ড — hritpinḍô — "the heart (the organ)".
ব্যায়াম করলে হৃৎপিণ্ড ভালো থাকে। bæyām kôrle hritpinḍô bhālo thāke. Exercise keeps the heart healthy.
হৃদয় (taught) is the heart of feeling; হৃৎপিণ্ড (hritpinḍô) is the pump the doctor listens to.

The word, taken apart.
হৃৎপিণ্ড (hritpinḍô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit hṛtpiṇḍa, "heart-mass", the heart (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: ধুকপুক করা.
ধুকপুক করা — dhukpuk kôrā — "to thump; to throb (of the heart)".
ফলাফলের আগে মীরার বুক ধুকপুক করছিল। phôlāphôler āge mīrār buk dhukpuk kôrchhilô. Before the results Mira's heart was thumping.
Past (he/she …): সেই ছাত্রীর বুক ধুকপুক করল, sei chhātrīr buk dhukpuk kôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
ধুকপুক করা (dhukpuk kôrā) is dhukpuk, an imitative word for the beat of the heart, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
