## You'll want to know: خشونت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C389-DAVTALAB-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خشونت** (*khoshunat*) — "violence".

> خشونت هیچ مشکلی را حل نمیکند.
>
> *khoshunat hich moshkeli râ hal nemikonad.*
>
> Violence does not solve any problem.

*Khoshunat-âmiz* — violent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خشونت** was borrowed from Arabic *khushūna* "roughness, harshness" (EtymDB); in Persian it means violence.
