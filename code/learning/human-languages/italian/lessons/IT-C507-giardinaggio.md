## You'll want to know: il giardinaggio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C507-ORTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il giardinaggio** — "gardening".

> Mia madre fa giardinaggio tutti i fine settimana.
>
> *My mother does gardening every weekend.*

*Fare giardinaggio* — to garden.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*giardinaggio* comes from French *jardinage*, built on *jardin* "garden", from Medieval Latin *jardīnus* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to a Frankish word for an enclosure.
