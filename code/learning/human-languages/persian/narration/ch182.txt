Persian, chapter 182: To book, To hire, To repeat, To translate, To talk.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رزرو کردن (rezerv kardan) — to book, to reserve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to cross, then the Persian for to stroll.

You'll want to know: رزرو کردن.
رزرو کردن — rezerv kardan — "to book, to reserve".

The word, taken apart.
rezerv kardan pairs rezerv, borrowed from French réserve, "reserve", with the Persian verb kardan, "to do". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
