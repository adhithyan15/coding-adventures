## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-DIKHAVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *dikhāvo* — **show it**

Three beats: *di-khā-vo*, the middle one long. The middle beat carries a breath
after the *k* that the *k* of *kapṛā* does not have. Hear both, then say both:
*ka* — *khā*.

The source prints this form twice, once for a menu and once for another colour.
It is the asking form used to one person across a counter, not a command shouted
at anyone, and this lesson teaches the printed form rather than the whole
system of asking forms behind it.
