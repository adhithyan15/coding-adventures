# ज — a letter you have been reading without being taught

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-131] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: **ए** — does it sit on a consonant or stand
alone?

[PAUSE 2s] This next one is not new to your eye. It is new to your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-132]; assesses=[] -->

**ज** — *ja*.

What it is made of:

- an open left hook curling clockwise around the lower bowl and continuing through the middle bar
- a top-to-bottom right stem
- the top shirorekhā

## Script: where it has been hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-132] -->

You have been reading this letter for a long time without anyone stopping to
name it:

- **जी** — the respect-marker in **जी हाँ**
- **जनवरी** — January, and **जून** and **जुलाई** after it

That is worth saying out loud rather than passing over. A letter can appear on
every other page and still never be **taught**, because the words that carry it
were introduced for their meanings and the shapes inside them were left to
look after themselves.

**Seeing a shape often is not the same as being shown it.** The first gives you
a guess; the second gives you the letter.

## Writing: ज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-132] -->

- **1.** start at the open upper-left tip, sweep down and clockwise around the lower bowl, then rise through the inner shoulder and continue right along the middle bar to the right-stem junction without lifting
- **2.** lift and draw the right stem top-to-bottom
- **3.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 2.** The pen comes up 2 times and no more.

> Verified three-stroke teaching form fitted to the bundled printed outline; everyday handwriting may divide the hook and bar differently.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Opiaterein, ‘Deva-ज-order.gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 10 May 2009.
