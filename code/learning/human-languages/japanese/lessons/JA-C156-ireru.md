## You'll want to know: いれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C156-CLOSET156-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いれる** — *ireru* — "to put in".

> **かばんに ほんを いれます。** — *kaban ni hon o iremasu* — "I put a book in my bag."

Polite **いれます**, *iremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いれる** (*ireru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as *iru*, "to go in"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
