## You'll want to know: ಆಶಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C429-ASHISU429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಶಿಸು** — *āśisu* — "to hope for".

> ಮಗ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಪಾಸಾಗುತ್ತಾನೆ ಅಂತ ತಾಯಿ ಆಶಿಸಿದಳು.
>
> *maga parīkṣeyalli pāsāguttāne anta tāyi āśisidaḷu.*
>
> The mother hoped her son would pass the exam.

Past (I …): ಆಶಿಸಿದೆ, *āśiside*.

What is hoped comes before ಅಂತ or ಎಂದು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಶಿಸು** (*āśisu*) is built on Sanskrit *āśā*, "wish, desire, hope" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
