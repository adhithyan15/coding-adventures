German, chapter 171: Only, First, Afterwards, Before, Later.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
erst — only (so far), not until.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for finally, then the German for still.

You'll want to know: erst.
erst — "only (so far), not until".

The word, taken apart.
erst from Old High German ērist, "first" from Proto-Germanic airistaz, "earliest" — inherited, the same word as old English erst, "formerly". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
danach — afterwards.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for only, then the German for first.

You'll want to know: danach.
danach — "afterwards".

The word, taken apart.
danach is the word for "there, that" + the word for "after": after that. EtymDB 2.1 has the compound but not its parts; both are inherited.
