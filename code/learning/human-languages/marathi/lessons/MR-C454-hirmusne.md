## You'll want to know: हिरमुसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C454-RUSVA454-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हिरमुसणे** — *hirmusṇe* — "to be crestfallen".

> परीक्षेत कमी गुण मिळाले म्हणून ती हिरमुसली.
>
> *parīkṣet kamī guṇ miḷāle mhaṇūn tī hirmuslī.*
>
> She was crestfallen because she got low marks in the exam.

Past (he …): तो हिरमुसला, *to hirmuslā*.

Of a disappointed child or player: हिरमुसलेला चेहरा, a crestfallen face.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिरमुसणे** (*hirmusṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
