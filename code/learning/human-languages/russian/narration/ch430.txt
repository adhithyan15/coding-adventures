Russian, chapter 430: Making the bed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
постель (postél) — bedding; a bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a deposit, then the Russian for to extend.

You'll want to know: постель.
постель (postél) — "bedding; a bed".
Постель в номере была чистая и мягкая. Postél v nómere bylá chístaya i myágkaya. The bed in the room was clean and soft.
Feminine. Постельное бельё — bed linen.

The word, taken apart.
постель (postél) is built on the root of стлать, стелить (stelít) 'to spread': what is spread out to sleep on. EtymDB lists постель (postél) with the senses 'bed; bedding'.

В шкафу лежат чистые простыни. V shkafú lezhát chístyye próstyni. There are clean sheets in the wardrobe.
Feminine; plural простыни, genitive plural простынь.

The word, taken apart.
простыня (prostynyá) is usually linked with простой 'plain, simple': a plain piece of cloth. EtymDB does not list the word, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
наволочка (návolochka) — a pillowcase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for bedding, then the Russian for a sheet.

You'll want to know: наволочка.
наволочка (návolochka) — "a pillowcase".
На подушке не было наволочки. Na podúshke né bylo návolochki. There was no pillowcase on the pillow.
Feminine; the stress is on the first syllable.

The word, taken apart.
наволочка (návolochka) is built from на- 'onto' and the root of волочь 'to drag, to pull', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic velťi 'to draw': a cover pulled on.

Lesson 4 of 5.
горничная (górnichnaya) — a chambermaid, a maid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sheet, then the Russian for a pillowcase.

You'll want to know: горничная.
горничная (górnichnaya) — "a chambermaid, a maid".
Каждое утро горничная убирает номер. Kázhdoye útro górnichnaya ubiráyet nómer. Every morning the chambermaid cleans the room.
An adjective used as a noun; it declines like an adjective.

The word, taken apart.
горничная (górnichnaya) is built on горница 'a room', first an upper room. EtymDB traces горница to Old East Slavic gorĭnĭ, a word that goes with гора 'a mountain'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
стелить (stelít) — to make up (a bed); to spread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pillowcase, then the Russian for a chambermaid.

You'll want to know: стелить.
стелить (stelít) — "to make up (a bed); to spread".
Бабушка стелит нам постель на диване. Bábushka stélit nam postél na diváne. Grandma makes up a bed for us on the sofa.
Imperfective; perfective постелить. Present стелю, стелешь. Also стлать.

The word, taken apart.
стелить (stelít) is a form of стлать 'to spread', the root of постель (postél) 'bedding'. EtymDB lists стелить (stelít) without a deeper source.
