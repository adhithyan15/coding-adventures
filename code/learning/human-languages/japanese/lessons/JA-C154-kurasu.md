## You'll want to know: くらす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C154-HABIT154-04]; assesses=[] -->

**くらす** — *kurasu* — "to get by; to live one's days".

> **いなかで くらします。** — *inaka de kurashimasu* — "I live in the countryside."

Polite **くらします**, *kurashimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くらす** (*kurasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
