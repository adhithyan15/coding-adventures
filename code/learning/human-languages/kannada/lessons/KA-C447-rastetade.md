# ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ (rastetaḍe) — a roadblock

## You'll want to know: ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C447-TADE447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ** — *rastetaḍe* — "a roadblock".

> ಮುಷ್ಕರದ ಕಾರಣ ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *muṣkarada kāraṇa heddāriyalli rastetaḍe ittu.*
>
> Because of the strike there was a roadblock on the highway.

ರಸ್ತೆ, road, plus ತಡೆ, a stop: a road held shut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ** (*rastetaḍe*) is a Kannada compound of ರಸ್ತೆ, "a road", usually traced to Persian *rāsta*, and native ತಡೆ, "to stop, to block" (DEDR 3031).
