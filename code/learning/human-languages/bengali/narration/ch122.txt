Bengali, chapter 122: Fly, Mosquito, Earthworm, Gecko, Turtle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মাছি (māchhi) — a fly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a sparrow, then the Bengali for a heron.

You'll want to know: মাছি.
মাছি — māchhi — "a fly".

The word, taken apart.
মাছি (māchhi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from makṣikā, "fly, bee" (CDIAL 9696).

টিকটিকি — ṭikṭiki — "a gecko".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of টিকটিকি (ṭikṭiki) is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it; the only Bengali word for a house lizard it gives is jeṭhī, under Sanskrit jyaiṣṭhī (CDIAL 5295), a different word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
কচ্ছপ (kôchchhôp) — a turtle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an earthworm, then the Bengali for a gecko.

You'll want to know: কচ্ছপ.
কচ্ছপ — kôchchhôp — "a turtle".

The word, taken apart.
কচ্ছপ (kôchchhôp) is Sanskrit kacchapa, "turtle, tortoise", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 2619). The word inherited from it is kāchim, which CDIAL lists.
