# محار (maḥār) — oysters; shellfish

## You'll want to know: محار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C477-QIRSH477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**محار** — *maḥār* — "oysters; shellfish".

> أكلنا المحار في مطعم على البحر.
>
> *akalnā al-maḥār fī maṭʿam ʿalā al-baḥr.*
>
> We ate oysters at a restaurant by the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محار** (*maḥār*) names oysters and other shellfish; the origin is not settled.
