# ಎಚ್ಚೆತ್ತುಕೊ (eccettuko) — to wake up to

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C419-VIPHALA419-05, KA-LEX-C420-ALUKU420-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for unavoidable, then the Kannada for to waver.

## You'll want to know: ಎಚ್ಚೆತ್ತುಕೊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C420-ALUKU420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಎಚ್ಚೆತ್ತುಕೊ** — *eccettuko* — "to wake up to".

> ಅಪಾಯದ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಕೇಳಿ ಜನರು ಎಚ್ಚೆತ್ತುಕೊಂಡರು.
>
> *apāyada suddi kēḷi janaru eccettukoṇḍaru.*
>
> Hearing the news of danger, people woke up to it.

Past (I …): ಎಚ್ಚೆತ್ತುಕೊಂಡೆ, *eccettukoṇḍe*.

Built with -ಕೊ, for oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಎಚ್ಚೆತ್ತುಕೊ** (*eccettuko*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 851 lists Kannada *eccatt-*, "to wake up", here with -*ko*, "for oneself".
