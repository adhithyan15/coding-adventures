## You'll want to know: હવા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C217-WIND217-01]; assesses=[] -->

**હવા** — *havā* — "air".

> **સવારે હવા ઠંડી હશે.** — *savāre havā ṭhanḍī haśe* — "The air will be cool in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હવા** (*havā*) is a loan from Arabic *hawā'*, "air", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *h-w-y* (Lane).
