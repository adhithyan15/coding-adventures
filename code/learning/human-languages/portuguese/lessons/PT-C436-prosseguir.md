## You'll want to know: prosseguir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C436-INDICACOES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prosseguir** — "to carry on, to continue".

> Depois da ponte, prosseguimos em frente.
>
> *After the bridge, we carried on straight ahead.*

A little formal; people also say *seguir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prosseguir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōsequī* 'to follow on' (EtymDB), reshaped on *seguir* 'to follow'.
