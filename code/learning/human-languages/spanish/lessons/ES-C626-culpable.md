## You'll want to know: culpable
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C626-JUICIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**culpable** — "guilty; the culprit".

> El juez lo declaró culpable.
>
> *The judge found him guilty.*

The opposite is *inocente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*culpable* is a learned borrowing from Latin *culpabilis* 'blameworthy', built on *culpa* 'fault' (EtymDB).
