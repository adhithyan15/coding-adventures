## You'll want to know: धनादेश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C426-ROKAD426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धनादेश** — *dhanādeś* — "a cheque".

> त्याने भाड्यासाठी धनादेश दिला.
>
> *tyāne bhāḍyāsāṭhī dhanādeś dilā.*
>
> He gave a cheque for the rent.

Masculine.

A formal word; in speech many people say चेक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धनादेश** (*dhanādeś*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *dhana*, "wealth", and *ādeśa*, "an order" (Monier-Williams).
