# గందరగోళం (gandaragōḷaṁ) — confusion; a muddle

## You'll want to know: గందరగోళం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C492-PORABADU492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**గందరగోళం** — *gandaragōḷaṁ* — "confusion; a muddle".

> టిక్కెట్లు తారుమారు కావడంతో స్టేషన్లో గందరగోళం మొదలైంది.
>
> *ṭikkeṭlu tārumāru kāvaḍantō sṭēṣanlō gandaragōḷaṁ modalaindi.*
>
> When the tickets got mixed up, confusion broke out at the station.

గందరగోళంగా ఉంది, it is confusing; గందరగోళం చేయు, to make a mess of things.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గందరగోళం** (*gandaragōḷaṁ*) is an everyday Telugu word for confusion and uproar whose origin is not settled.
