## You'll want to know: потомок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C326-PREDOK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**потомок** (*potómok*) — "a descendant".

> Он потомок известного художника.
>
> *On potómok izvéstnovo khudózhnika.*
>
> He is a descendant of a famous painter.

Genitive *потомка*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*потомок* is usually traced to *потом* "afterwards", from *по* and *том*, a form of *тот* "that": the one who comes after. EtymDB lists no parent.
