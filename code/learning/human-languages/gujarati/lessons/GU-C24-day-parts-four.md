# The day in four parts, by ear

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-SAVAAR-01, GU-LEX-RAAT-01] -->

[PAUSE 5s] Nothing new arrives here. Say the first part of the day, then the
last, before any list is read to you.

Keep two scores. **Listening** goes from sound to meaning; **speaking** goes from
meaning to sound. A strong score in one cannot cover a miss in the other.
