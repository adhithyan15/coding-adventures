## You'll want to know: le nourrisson
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C493-BEBE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le nourrisson** — "infant, small baby".

> Le nourrisson dort presque toute la journée.
>
> *The infant sleeps almost all day.*

Formal: a baby of a few months.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le nourrisson* comes from Late Latin *nūtrītiō* "feeding, nursing", built on *nūtrīre* "to feed" (Lewis & Short), the source of *nourrir*.
