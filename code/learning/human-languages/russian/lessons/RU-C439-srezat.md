## You'll want to know: срезать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C439-RAZVILKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**срезать** (*srézat*) — "to take a short cut; to cut off".

> Мы срезали дорогу через парк.
>
> *My srézali dorógu chérez park.*
>
> We took a short cut through the park.

Perfective; the imperfective *срезать* (*srezát*) has the stress on the а. *Срезать путь* — to take a short cut. Future *срежу, срежешь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*срезать* is built from *с-* 'off' and *резать* 'to cut', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *rězati* (EtymDB).
