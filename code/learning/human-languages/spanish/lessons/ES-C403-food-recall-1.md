# food recall one — rebuild the meal

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-POLLO, ES-LEX-VERDURA, ES-LEX-PAELLA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Give the three new food words. (*El pollo, la verdura, la paella*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-POLLO, ES-LEX-VERDURA, ES-LEX-PAELLA] -->

You can name chicken and vegetables, distinguish **pollo** from **polvo**, and
name the dish whose pan supplied its name.

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *pollo, no polvo*]
- [YOU SAY: *fruta y verdura*]
- [YOU SAY: *la paella*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-POLLO, ES-LEX-VERDURA, ES-LEX-PAELLA] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name a dish with chicken and vegetables. (*Paella con pollo y
verdura*.)
