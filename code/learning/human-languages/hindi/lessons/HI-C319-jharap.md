# झड़प (jhaṛap) — a scuffle; a skirmish

## You'll want to know: झड़प
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C319-MARPEET319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**झड़प** — *jhaṛap* — "a scuffle; a skirmish".

> बाज़ार में दो दुकानदारों के बीच झड़प हुई।
>
> *bāzār meṁ do dukāndāroṁ ke bīc jhaṛap huī.*
>
> A scuffle broke out between two shopkeepers in the market.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is set by CDIAL under *\*jhaṭ*, a form it reconstructs for quick, sudden movements (CDIAL 5327), where it gives Hindi *jhaṛap*, a fight: the same family as **झटका**, a jolt. A **झड़प** is short and sudden, between two people or two small groups.
