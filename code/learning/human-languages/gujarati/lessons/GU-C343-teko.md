## You'll want to know: ટેકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C343-TEKO343-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ટેકો** — *ṭeko* — "backing; support for a person or a plan".

> આખા ગામે આ યોજનાને ટેકો આપ્યો.
>
> *ākhā gāme ā yojnāne ṭeko āpyo.*
>
> The whole village backed this plan.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટેકો** (*ṭeko*) CDIAL lists the related Gujarati *ṭek*, "support", under a reconstructed *ṭakk*, "remain, stop" (CDIAL 5420): what keeps a thing standing.
