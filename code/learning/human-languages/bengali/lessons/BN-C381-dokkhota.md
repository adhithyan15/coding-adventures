## You'll want to know: দক্ষতা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C381-BYOBOSTHA381-04]; assesses=[] -->

**দক্ষতা** — *dôkkhôtā* — "skill; competence".

> দক্ষতা থাকলে চাকরি পাওয়া সহজ।
>
> *dôkkhôtā thākle chākri pāoyā sôhôj.*
>
> If you have the skills, it's easy to get a job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দক্ষতা** (*dôkkhôtā*) is Sanskrit *dakṣatā*, dexterity or ability (Monier-Williams), from *dakṣa* 'able'.
