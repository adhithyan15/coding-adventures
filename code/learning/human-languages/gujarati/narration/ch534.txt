Gujarati, chapter 534: Bets and Lotteries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
જુગાર (jugār) — gambling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a round (of a game), then the Gujarati for to play a game of chance.

You'll want to know: જુગાર.
જુગાર — jugār — "gambling".
જો તું જુગાર રમીશ તો બધા પૈસા ગુમાવીશ. jo tũ jugār ramīś to badhā paisā gumāvīś. If you gamble, you will lose all your money.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
જુગાર (jugār) is usually traced to Sanskrit dyūtakāra, "a gambler", built on dyūta, "gambling" (CDIAL 6609); CDIAL itself lists no Gujarati form there.

Lesson 2 of 5.
સટ્ટો (saṭṭo) — a speculative bet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to play a game of chance, then the Gujarati for gambling.

You'll want to know: સટ્ટો.
સટ્ટો — saṭṭo — "a speculative bet".
જો તમે સટ્ટામાં પૈસા નાખશો તો કદાચ બધું જશે. jo tame saṭṭāmā̃ paisā nākhśo to kadāc badhũ jaśe. If you put money into speculation, you may lose it all.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સટ્ટો (saṭṭo) is a bet on prices or results. CDIAL lists Gujarati sāṭũ, "a bargain", and Panjabi saṭṭī, "an exchange market", under a reconstructed saṭṭa, "exchange" (CDIAL 13101); it does not list this word, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
હોડ (hoḍ) — a wager.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for gambling, then the Gujarati for a speculative bet.

You'll want to know: હોડ.
હોડ — hoḍ — "a wager".
જો આજે વરસાદ પડશે તો હું હોડ હારી જઈશ. jo āje varsād paḍśe to hũ hoḍ hārī jaīś. If it rains today, I'll lose the bet.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હોડ (hoḍ) comes from hōḍḍa, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a wager" (CDIAL 14175).

Lesson 4 of 5.
લોટરી (loṭrī) — a lottery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a speculative bet, then the Gujarati for a wager.

You'll want to know: લોટરી.
લોટરી — loṭrī — "a lottery".
જો મને લોટરી લાગશે તો હું મારી માતા માટે નવું ઘર ખરીદીશ. jo mane loṭrī lāgśe to hũ mārī mātā māṭe navũ ghar kharīdīś. If I win the lottery, I'll buy a new house for my mother.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
લોટરી (loṭrī) is borrowed from English lottery. A lottery "strikes" the winner: મને લોટરી લાગી.

Lesson 5 of 5.
જોગાનુજોગ (jogānujog) — a chance meeting; a coincidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a wager, then the Gujarati for a lottery.

You'll want to know: જોગાનુજોગ.
જોગાનુજોગ — jogānujog — "a chance meeting; a coincidence".
જો હું મોડો આવત તો આ જોગાનુજોગ ન બનત. jo hũ moḍo āvat to ā jogānujog na banat. If I had come late, this coincidence would never have happened.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
જોગાનુજોગ (jogānujog) is built from jog, "a meeting, a joining", from Sanskrit yoga, "joining" (Monier-Williams), with anu, "after": one meeting falling after another.
