## You'll want to know: اقلیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C387-MOHAJERAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اقلیت** (*aqalliyat*) — "a minority".

> آنها در این کشور یک اقلیت کوچک هستند.
>
> *ânhâ dar in keshvar yek aqalliyat-e kuchek hastand.*
>
> They are a small minority in this country.

Plural *aqalliyathâ*. The opposite is *aksariyat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقلیت** is usually traced to Arabic *aqalliyya* "minority", built on *aqall* "fewer, least".
