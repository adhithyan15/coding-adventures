## You'll want to know: o triângulo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C405-PNEU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o triângulo** — "the warning triangle; the triangle".

> Ele pôs o triângulo atrás do carro parado.
>
> *He put the warning triangle behind the stopped car.*

In Portugal a car must carry *o triângulo* and a reflective vest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*triângulo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *triangulus* 'three-cornered', from *trēs* 'three' and *angulus* 'corner' (EtymDB).
