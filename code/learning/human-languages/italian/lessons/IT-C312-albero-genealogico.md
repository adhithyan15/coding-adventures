## You'll want to know: l'albero genealogico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C312-ANTENATI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'albero genealogico** — "the family tree".

> Mio padre ha disegnato l'albero genealogico della famiglia.
>
> *My father drew the family tree.*

*Genealogico* — of the family line.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*albero* is inherited from Latin *arbor* "tree" (EtymDB); *genealogico* comes from Greek *genealogía*, an account of a family line, which Latin took over as *geneālogia* (Lewis & Short).
