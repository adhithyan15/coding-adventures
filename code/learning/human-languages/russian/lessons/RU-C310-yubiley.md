## You'll want to know: юбилей
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C310-YUBILEY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**юбилей** (*yubiléy*) — "a jubilee, a big round birthday or anniversary".

> У дедушки скоро юбилей, семьдесят лет.
>
> *U dédushki skóro yubiléy, sémdesyat let.*
>
> Grandpa soon has a big birthday: seventy.

A round number: fifty, sixty, seventy years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*юбилей* was borrowed from German *Jubiläum* (EtymDB), which comes from Late Latin *iūbilaeus* "the jubilee year", which is usually traced to a Hebrew word for the ram's horn that announced it.
