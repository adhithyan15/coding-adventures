# ખાળવું (khāḷvũ) — to keep in check

## You'll want to know: ખાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C374-AVRODH374-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાળવું** — *khāḷvũ* — "to keep in check".

> જો આપણે નદીનું પાણી ખાળીશું તો ગામ બચી જશે.
>
> *jo āpaṇe nadīnũ pāṇī khāḷīśũ to gām bacī jaśe.*
>
> If we hold back the river water, the village will be saved.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આંસુ ખાળ્યાં, *teṇe ā̃su khāḷyā̃*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાળવું** (*khāḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *skhālayati*, "causes to falter, stops"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to hinder, to stop" (CDIAL 13664).
