# Two old month positions

Only the two previously taught months return. This is a tiny closed bank,
not a new month list or a scored form task.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-MONTH-MAP-01] -->

Look at **੦੧** for January (**ਜਨਵਰੀ**) and **੦੨** for February
(**ਫ਼ਰਵਰੀ**). The first place is zero in both; the second changes.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-MONTH-MAP-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

With both codes visible, copy **੦੧** under January and **੦੨** under
February. Say the month, cover the code, and try its two digits once.
Uncover and repair only the differing digit.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-MONTH-MAP-01] -->

From the old English cue **February**, write its month position **੦੨**.
Pause before looking. These are month positions, not new Punjabi words.
