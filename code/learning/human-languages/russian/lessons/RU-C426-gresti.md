## You'll want to know: грести
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C426-VESLO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**грести** (*grestí*) — "to row; to rake".

> Отец гребёт, а мы сидим и отдыхаем.
>
> *Otéts grebyót, a my sidím i otdykháyem.*
>
> Dad rows, and we sit and rest.

Imperfective. Present *гребу, гребёшь*; past *грёб, гребла*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*грести* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *greti* 'to dig, to scrape, to rake' (EtymDB): an oar pulls through water as a rake pulls through soil.
