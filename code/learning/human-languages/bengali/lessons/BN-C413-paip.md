## You'll want to know: পাইপ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C413-NORDOMA413-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পাইপ** — *pāip* — "a pipe".

> মিস্ত্রি নর্দমার পুরনো পাইপ বদলালেন।
>
> *mistri nôrdômār purôno pāip bôdlālen.*
>
> The plumber replaced the old drainpipe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পাইপ** (*pāip*) is borrowed from English *pipe*.
