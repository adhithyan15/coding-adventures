## You'll want to know: rùjìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C417-CUSTOMS417-02]; assesses=[] -->

*rùjìng* — "to enter a country".

> *rùjìng yào yòng hùzhào* — You need a passport to enter the country.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rùjìng* is built from 入 'to go in' and *jìng* 'a border', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyip* and *kjaengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a boundary'.
