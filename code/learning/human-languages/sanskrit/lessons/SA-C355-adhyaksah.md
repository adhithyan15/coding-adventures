## You'll want to know: अध्यक्षः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C355-SANSAD355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अध्यक्षः** — *adhyakṣaḥ* — "a president; a chairperson".

> अध्यक्षः सभाम् आरभते।
>
> *adhyakṣaḥ sabhām ārabhate.*
>
> The president opens the meeting.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अध्यक्षः** (*adhyakṣaḥ*) is built from *adhi*, "over", and *akṣa*, "the eye"; MW gives *adhy-akṣa* "an inspector, a superintendent", and "a president" is its modern sense (MW).
