Mandarin Chinese, chapter 76: Parting.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
一会儿 (yíhuìr) — a little while; in a moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for present, then the Mandarin for to give as a present.

You'll want to know: 一会儿.
一会儿 — yíhuìr — "a little while; in a moment".
我一会儿打电话 wǒ yíhuìr dǎ diànhuà — I'll ring in a little while.
The new character, in pieces: 会 (huì) — 人 spread as a roof, two bars, and a bend with a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.
yī changes its tone here, rising to yí before the falling huì: yíhuìr.

The word, taken apart.
一会儿 (yíhuìr) is built from 一 'one', huì and the ending r, inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit, hwajH and nye (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses huì as 'to come together'; how the phrase came to mean a short while is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for times and partings.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for present, then the Mandarin for to give as a present, then say yíhuìr again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 2 of 6.
会 (huì) — 人 over two bars and a bend.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — 人 over two bars and a bend and Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The word first, then the character inside it.
[your turn — recall: say yíhuìr — R1, one lesson back, before it decays]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
人 今 去.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — rén, jīn, qù]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — 人 over two bars and a bend.
会 (huì).
Three parts, top to bottom.
人, two strokes, spread wide as a roof, as on 今.
Two horizontals under it, the lower one longer.
A stroke that falls left and turns right along the bottom, then a dot: the same two strokes that end 去.
2 + 2 + 2 = six.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 会 (huì), all six, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 会 (huì)? (Six.) Which character you know is on top? (人.) What comes last? (A dot.)
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 会 (huì), pinned stroke paths and medians.

明天见 — míngtiān jiàn — "see you tomorrow".
下雨我们明天见 xiàyǔ wǒmen míngtiān jiàn — It's raining: let's meet tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
明天见 (míngtiān jiàn) is built from 明天 'tomorrow' and 见 'to see', the 见 of 再见.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: 再说.
再说 — zàishuō — "to leave it till later".
对不起一会儿再说 duìbuqǐ yíhuìr zàishuō — Sorry: let's talk about it in a bit.

The word, taken apart.
再说 (zàishuō) is built from 再 'again' and 说 'to say', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsojH and sywet (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): say it again, another time.
