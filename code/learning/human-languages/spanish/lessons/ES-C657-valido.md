## You'll want to know: válido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C657-PAPELES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**válido** — "valid".

> Este billete es válido por un mes.
>
> *This ticket is valid for a month.*

*Válido hasta* — valid until.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*válido* is a learned borrowing from Latin *validus* "strong, able, powerful" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from *valēre* "to be strong".
