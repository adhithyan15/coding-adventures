Bengali, chapter 462: Lazy and Industrious.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
অলস (ôlôs) — lazy; idle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for whimsical, then the Bengali for to dig one's heels in.

You'll want to know: অলস.
অলস — ôlôs — "lazy; idle".
ছুটির দিনে আমি অলস হয়ে বিছানায় শুয়ে থাকি। chhuṭir dine āmi ôlôs hôye bichhānāy shuye thāki. On holidays I lie lazily in bed.

The word, taken apart.
অলস (ôlôs) is a tatsama, Sanskrit alasa, "inactive, lazy" (CDIAL 708).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ফাঁকি দেওয়া (phā̃ki deoyā) — to shirk; to dodge (work).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for industrious, then the Bengali for in disorder.

You'll want to know: ফাঁকি দেওয়া.
ফাঁকি দেওয়া — phā̃ki deoyā — "to shirk; to dodge (work)".
অলস কর্মচারীটা কাজে ফাঁকি দিত। ôlôs kôrmôchārīṭā kāje phā̃ki ditô. The lazy employee used to shirk his work.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কাজে ফাঁকি দিলেন, tini kāje phā̃ki dilen.

The word, taken apart.
ফাঁকি দেওয়া (phā̃ki deoyā) is phā̃ki, "a trick, a dodge", which CDIAL lists for Bengali (CDIAL 9035), with deoyā, "to give" (CDIAL 6141).

বাড়ির কাজ করতে ছেলেটা গড়িমসি করছিল। bāṛir kāj kôrte chheleṭā gôṛimôsi kôrchhilô. The boy was dawdling over his homework.

গড়িমসি করা (gôṛimôsi kôrā) is gôṛimôsi, "dawdling", whose origin is not settled, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
