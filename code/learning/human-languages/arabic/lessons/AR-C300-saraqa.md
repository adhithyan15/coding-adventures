## You'll want to know: سرق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C300-JARIMA300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سرق** — *saraqa* — "to steal".

> سرق اللص حقيبة السيدة.
>
> *saraqa al-liṣṣ ḥaqībat as-sayyida.*
>
> The thief stole the lady’s bag.

Present: يسرق, *yasriq*.

The passive سرق, *suriqa*, is common: سرقت سيارتي, my car was stolen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرق** (*saraqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-r-q* (Lane), "to take a thing secretly"; *sariqa* is theft and *sāriq* a thief.
