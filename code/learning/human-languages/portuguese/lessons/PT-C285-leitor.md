## You'll want to know: o leitor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C285-OUVINTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o leitor** — "the reader".

> Um leitor escreveu uma carta ao jornal.
>
> *A reader wrote a letter to the paper.*

A woman is *a leitora*. Also a device: *um leitor de CD*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*leitor* comes from Latin *lēctor* "a reader" (EtymDB), from *legere* "to read".
