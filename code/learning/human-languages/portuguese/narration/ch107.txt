Portuguese, chapter 107: Sandwich, Sauce, Vinegar, Dessert, Meal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a sandes — a sandwich.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: a sandes.
a sandes — "a sandwich". In Brazil, o sanduíche.
