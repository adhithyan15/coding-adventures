Gujarati, chapter 361: Criticism and Pleading.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નિંદા (nindā) — speaking ill of someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a betrayal, then the Gujarati for to entrap.

You'll want to know: નિંદા.
નિંદા — nindā — "speaking ill of someone".
બીજાની નિંદા કરવી સારી વાત નથી. bījānī nindā karvī sārī vāt nathī. It isn't good to speak ill of others.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નિંદા (nindā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit nindā, "blame, censure, reproach" (Monier-Williams).

જનતાએ સરકારની નવી નીતિની ટીકા કરી. jantāe sarkārnī navī nītinī ṭīkā karī. The public criticised the government's new policy.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટીકા (ṭīkā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit ṭīkā, "a commentary" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati has moved it from comment to criticism.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ઠપકો.
ઠપકો — ṭhapko — "a telling-off".
હું મોડો આવ્યો એટલે ગુરુએ મને ઠપકો આપ્યો. hũ moḍo āvyo eṭle gurue mane ṭhapko āpyo. I came late, so the teacher told me off.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઠપકો (ṭhapko) CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "a rebuke", under a reconstructed ṭhapp, "strike, press" (CDIAL 5495).

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: વગોવવું.
વગોવવું — vagovvũ — "to run down (to defame)".
તે બધાની સામે પોતાના ભાઈને વગોવે છે. te badhānī sāme potānā bhāīne vagove che. He runs his brother down in front of everyone.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પોતાના પડોશીને વગોવ્યો, teṇe potānā paḍośīne vagovyo.

The word, taken apart.
વગોવવું (vagovvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to revile", under a reconstructed vigrōcati, "robs" (CDIAL 11671): to rob someone of a good name.

Lesson 5 of 5.

વીનવવું — vīnavvũ — "to entreat".
મીરા દાક્તરને વહેલા આવવા વીનવે છે. Mīrā dāktarne vahelā āvvā vīnve che. Mira is pleading with the doctor to come early.

The word, taken apart.
વીનવવું (vīnavvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit víjñapayati, "makes known, begs"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to entreat" (CDIAL 11705).
