## Writing

<!-- mock-part:a2-writing-functional-response -->
The 25–35-word message must address **अरुण**, explain the delay, give an
arrival time, and ask how to complete the missed work. Score relevance,
reader-purpose completion, range, accuracy, and clarity with the rubric.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-writing-functional-response -->
<!-- mock-part:a2-writing-connected-message-or-description -->
The 70–90-word account must identify the familiar event, place, time and
companion, sequence what happened, and give a reaction. Accept internally
consistent learner details and documented regional Marathi forms.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-writing-connected-message-or-description -->

## Speaking

<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-personal-interview -->
Score six relevant answers that add a reason or detail where prompted. Do not
reward a memorized answer that does not address the question.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-personal-interview -->
<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-prepared-account -->
Score a 90–120-second organized account of a familiar visited place, supported
by keyword notes rather than a written answer script.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-prepared-account -->
<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-information-exchange-role-play -->
The learner must state the destination and day, ask both times and the fare,
clarify one incomplete detail, and close appropriately.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-information-exchange-role-play -->
