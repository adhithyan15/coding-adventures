Lesson 1 of 5.
वाक्यम् (vākyam) — a sentence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a nest, then the Sanskrit for an egg.

You'll want to know: वाक्यम्.
वाक्यम् — vākyam — "a sentence".

The word, taken apart.
vākya is built on the root √vac, "to speak" (MW): a sentence is something said (CDIAL 11468). The root is inherited from Indo-European, and MW compares Latin vōx, "voice".

भाषा — bhāṣā — "a language".

The word, taken apart.
bhāṣā is built on the root √bhāṣ, "to speak" (MW). MW notes it meant everyday spoken language, as opposed to the language of the Veda; CDIAL 9479 lists it, and Hindi and Telugu still use it for "language".

Lesson 3 of 5.
व्याकरणम् (vyākaraṇam) — grammar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a sentence, then the Sanskrit for a language.

You'll want to know: व्याकरणम्.
व्याकरणम् — vyākaraṇam — "grammar".

The word, taken apart.
vyākaraṇa is built on vi-ā- with the root √kṛ, "to do, make" (MW). The verb means "to separate, explain", so grammar is the taking apart of language (CDIAL 12182).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गणितम् (gaṇitam) — mathematics.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a language, then the Sanskrit for grammar.

You'll want to know: गणितम्.
गणितम् — gaṇitam — "mathematics".

The word, taken apart.
gaṇita is the past participle of gaṇayati, "counts", built on the root √gaṇ (MW; CDIAL 3996). "What is counted" became "reckoning", and then mathematics.

Lesson 5 of 5.
विज्ञानम् (vijñānam) — science.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for grammar, then the Sanskrit for mathematics.

You'll want to know: विज्ञानम्.
विज्ञानम् — vijñānam — "science".

The word, taken apart.
vijñāna is built on vi- and the root √jñā, "to know" (MW), the root of jānāti, "knows". It first meant "the act of discerning" (CDIAL 11707), and later "science".
