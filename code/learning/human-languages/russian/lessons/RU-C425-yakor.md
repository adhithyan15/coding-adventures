## You'll want to know: якорь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C425-KACHKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**якорь** (*yákor*) — "an anchor".

> Корабль бросил якорь недалеко от берега.
>
> *Korábl brósil yákor nedalekó ot bérega.*
>
> The ship dropped anchor not far from the shore.

Masculine; plural *якоря*. *Бросить якорь* — to drop anchor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*якорь* comes from Old East Slavic *ękorĭ* (EtymDB), an old loan that is usually traced, perhaps through Norse, to Greek *ánkyra* 'an anchor'.
