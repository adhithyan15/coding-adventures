## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-SKILL-READ-NARRATIVE-01]; assesses=[LA-LEX-C108-VESTIS-02, LA-LEX-C117-LOC117-05, LA-LEX-C152-QUAL152-05, LA-LEX-C158-ACT158-04] -->

> Herī māne caelum obscūrum erat, et pluēbat. Mārcus in vīllā sedēbat et
> librum legēbat. Subitō amīcus ad portam vēnit et clāmāvit: "Venī ad
> forum!" Prīmum Mārcus nōn volēbat, quia fessus erat. Deinde tamen vēnit,
> et ad forum ambulāvērunt.
>
> Dum ambulant, amīcam vīdērunt, et amīca quoque vēnit. In forō mercātōrēs
> pānem et vīnum vendēbant et multum clāmābant. Tum amīcī in caupōnam
> intrāvērunt. In caupōnā calor erat. Ibi cēnāvērunt et vīnum bibērunt, et
> amīca fābulam nārrāvit. Postrēmō domum ambulāvērunt. Iam nōn pluēbat,
> caelum serēnum erat, et Mārcus laetus erat.

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-GRAMMAR-C281-PERFECTUM-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C281-PERFECTUM-AVI-02, LA-GRAMMAR-C281-ERAM-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C281-IMPERFECTUM-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C282-ORDO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The events pile is the perfects: *vēnit*, *clāmāvit*, *vēnit*,
*ambulāvērunt*, *vīdērunt*, *vēnit*, *intrāvērunt*, *cēnāvērunt*,
*bibērunt*, *nārrāvit*, *ambulāvērunt*. Eleven things happened, and
*prīmum*, *deinde*, *tum* and *postrēmō* keep them in order.

[PAUSE 2s] Everything else is scene: the dark sky, the rain, Marcus reading,
the merchants shouting, the warmth of the inn. A story is mostly
background.

[PAUSE 2s] And *dum ambulant* is a present inside a past story, the
stretch of time the meeting happened in.
