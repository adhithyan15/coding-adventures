## You'll want to know: la caparra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C431-CAPARRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la caparra** — "the deposit".

> Per la camera chiedono una caparra del trenta per cento.
>
> *For the room they ask for a thirty per cent deposit.*

If you cancel, you may lose *la caparra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caparra* is built from *capo* 'head, chief', inherited from Latin *caput*, and *arra* 'pledge', from Latin *arrha*, which came into Latin from Greek (Lewis & Short).
