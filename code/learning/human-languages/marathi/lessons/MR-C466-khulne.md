## You'll want to know: खुलणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C466-SAMADHAN466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खुलणे** — *khulṇe* — "to brighten up".

> आजोबांना पाहून बाळाचा चेहरा खुलला.
>
> *ājobānnā pāhūn bāḷācā cehrā khullā.*
>
> The baby's face brightened at the sight of Grandfather.

Past (he …): तो खुलला, *to khullā*.

Of a face, a mood or a colour: रंग खुलणे, a colour sets something off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खुलणे** (*khulṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *khulṇẽ*, "to be open", under the reconstructed *\*khull-* (CDIAL 3945); a face that opens up brightens.
