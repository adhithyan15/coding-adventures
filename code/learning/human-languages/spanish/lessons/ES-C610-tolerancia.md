## You'll want to know: la tolerancia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C610-CONVIVIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la tolerancia** — "the tolerance".

> La tolerancia es importante en una sociedad.
>
> *Tolerance is important in a society.*

*Tolerante* — tolerant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tolerancia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tolerantia* 'endurance', built on *tolerare* 'to bear' (EtymDB).
