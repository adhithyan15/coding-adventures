## You'll want to know: いなずま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C278-INAZUMA278-01]; assesses=[] -->

**いなずま** — *inazuma* — "a flash of lightning".

> **ゆうべ、そらに いなずまが ひかりました。** — *yūbe, sora ni inazuma ga hikarimashita* — "Last night lightning flashed across the sky."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いなずま** (*inazuma*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
