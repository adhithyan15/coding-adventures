## You'll want to know: ਵਿਆਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C313-WED313-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਆਹ** — *viāh* — "a wedding".

> ਪਿਛਲੇ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਭਰਾ ਦਾ ਵਿਆਹ ਸੀ।
>
> *pichhle mahīne mere bharā dā viāh sī.*
>
> My brother's wedding was last month.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਆਹ** (*viāh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vivāha*, "marriage"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *viāh*, "marriage", under it (CDIAL 11920).
