## You'll want to know: जलौघः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C302-JALAUGHA302-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जलौघः** — *jalaughaḥ* — "a flood".

> वर्षायाः अनन्तरं ग्रामे जलौघः आगच्छति।
>
> *varṣāyāḥ anantaraṃ grāme jalaughaḥ āgacchati.*
>
> After the rain a flood comes to the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जलौघः** (*jalaughaḥ*) is built from *jala*, "water", and *ogha*, "a flood, a rapid flow of water"; MW gives *jalaugha* as a mass of water (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
