## You'll want to know: हफ्तो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C219-GUEST219-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हफ्तो** — *haphto* — "a week".

> हूं एक हफ्तो गांव मांय रैसूं।
>
> *hū̃ ek haphto gā̃v mā̃y raisū̃.*
>
> I'll stay a week in the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हफ्तो** (*haphto*) is usually traced to Persian *hafta*, "a week", from *haft*, "seven"; Marwadi ends it in *-o* like its own nouns.
