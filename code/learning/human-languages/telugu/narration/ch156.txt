Telugu, chapter 156: A Timetable, a Notice, the Rent, an Advert, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
కాలపట్టిక (kālapaṭṭika) — a timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to hope, then the Telugu for to look forward to.

You'll want to know: కాలపట్టిక.
కాలపట్టిక — kālapaṭṭika — "a timetable". Read the board: బస్సు: ఉదయం ఎనిమిది గంటలు — bassu: udayaṁ enimidi gaṇṭalu — "Bus: 8 a.m." The bus leaves at eight, so be there before eight.

Lesson 5 of 7.
రసీదు (rasīdu) — a receipt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for rent, then the Telugu for an advertisement.

You'll want to know: రసీదు.
రసీదు — rasīdu — "a receipt". Ask for it when you pay: రసీదు ఇస్తారా? — rasīdu istārā? — "Could you give me the receipt?" Read two words again: ఛాయ (chāya, shade) is written with ఛ, and ఝరి (jhari, a stream) with ఝ.
