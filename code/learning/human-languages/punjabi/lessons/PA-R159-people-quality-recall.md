# Three old words for describing people

The writing return is finished. This is a separate, untimed recall of three
words learned earlier. The English cues add no Punjabi words to memorise.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-01] -->

Hear the cue **rich**. [PAUSE 4s] Say the old Punjabi word before the
spoken model: **ਅਮੀਰ** (*amīr*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-01, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-02, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-03] -->

Say only the old describing word for each familiar meaning:

- **poor** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਗ਼ਰੀਬ** (*garīb*).
- **young** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਜਵਾਨ** (*javān*).
- **rich** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਅਮੀਰ** (*amīr*).

These are word meanings, not labels for a real person in the room.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-01, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-02, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-03] -->

Hear **young**, **rich**, then **poor**. Pause after each cue and say its
Punjabi word before the model: **ਜਵਾਨ**, **ਅਮੀਰ**, **ਗ਼ਰੀਬ**.
