## You'll want to know: তাঁবু
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C399-BHROMON399-03]; assesses=[] -->

**তাঁবু** — *tā̃bu* — "a tent".

> পাহাড়ে আমরা তাঁবুতে রাত কাটালাম।
>
> *pāhāṛe āmrā tā̃bute rāt kāṭālām.*
>
> In the hills we spent the night in a tent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তাঁবু** (*tā̃bu*) is usually traced to Persian *tanbū*, "a tent", with the n become the nasal ঁ.
