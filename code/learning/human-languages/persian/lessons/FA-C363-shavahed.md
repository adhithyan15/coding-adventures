## You'll want to know: شواهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C363-EDDEA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شواهد** (*shavâhed*) — "evidence".

> شواهد زیادی وجود دارد.
>
> *shavâhed-e ziyâdi vojud dârad.*
>
> There is a lot of evidence.

An Arabic broken plural of *shâhed* "a witness; a piece of evidence".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شواهد** is usually traced to Arabic *shawāhid*, a broken plural of *shāhid* "a witness" (EtymDB lists *shāhid* as the source of Persian *shâhed*). Evidence is what bears witness.
