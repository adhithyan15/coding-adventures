## You'll want to know: تلوار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C310-DHAL310-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تلوار** — *talvār* — "a sword".

> سپاہی کے پاس تلوار تھی۔
>
> *sipāhī ke pās talvār thī.*
>
> The soldier had a sword.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تلوار** (*talvār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *taravāri*, "a sword" (CDIAL 5706), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *talwār*. It is feminine.
