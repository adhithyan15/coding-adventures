## You'll want to know: きんじょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C196-ADDR196-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きんじょ** — *kinjo* — "neighbourhood; vicinity".

> **きんじょに いい みせが あります。** — *kinjo ni ii mise ga arimasu* — "There is a good shop in my neighbourhood."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きんじょ** (*kinjo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kin*, "near" (Middle Chinese *gj+nX*) and *jo*, "place" (*srjoX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
