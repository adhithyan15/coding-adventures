## You'll want to know: جفن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C506-WAJH506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جفن** — *jafn* — "an eyelid".

> فتح المريض جفنيه ببطء.
>
> *fataḥa al-marīḍ jafnayhi bi-buṭʾ.*
>
> The patient slowly opened his eyelids.

Plural: جفون, *jufūn*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جفن** (*jafn*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-f-n*, "a sheath, a covering" (Lane). The eyelid is the eye's cover.
