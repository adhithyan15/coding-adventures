## You'll want to know: promover
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C360-PROMOCAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**promover** — "to promote".

> Promoveram o meu irmão a chefe de equipa.
>
> *They promoted my brother to team leader.*

*Ser promovido* — to be promoted. Also to promote an event.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*promover* comes from Latin *prōmovēre* "to move forward, to advance" (EtymDB), built from *prō* and *movēre* "to move" (Lewis & Short).
