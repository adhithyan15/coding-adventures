## You'll want to know: رنگین کمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C479-RANGINKAMAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رنگین کمان** (*ranginkamân*) — "a rainbow".

> بعد از باران یک رنگین کمان در آسمان دیدیم.
>
> *Ba'd az bârân yek ranginkamân dar âsmân didim.*
>
> After the rain we saw a rainbow in the sky.

Literally 'coloured bow': *rangin* 'coloured' + *kamân* 'bow'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ranginkamân* is a native compound of *rangin* 'coloured', from *rang* 'colour', and *kamân* 'bow', inherited from Middle Persian *kamān* (EtymDB).
