## You'll want to know: भविष्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भविष्यति** — *bhaviṣyati* — "will be". The future of **भवति**, "is, becomes": **श्वः रविवासरः भविष्यति।** — "Tomorrow will be Sunday."
