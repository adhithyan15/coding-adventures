## You'll want to know: વફાદાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C360-IMAN360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વફાદાર** — *vaphādār* — "loyal".

> કૂતરો પોતાના માલિકને વફાદાર રહે છે.
>
> *kūtro potānā mālikne vaphādār rahe che.*
>
> A dog stays loyal to its owner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વફાદાર** (*vaphādār*) is usually traced to Persian *vafādār*, "faithful", built from Arabic *wafā*, "faithfulness", and the Persian *-dār*, "holding".
