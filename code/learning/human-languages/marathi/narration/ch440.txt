Marathi, chapter 440: Droughts and Cloudbursts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दुष्काळ (duṣkāḷ) — a drought; a famine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to howl, then the Marathi for to crackle.

You'll want to know: दुष्काळ.
दुष्काळ — duṣkāḷ — "a drought; a famine".
त्या वर्षी मोठा दुष्काळ पडला. tyā varṣī moṭhā duṣkāḷ paḍlā. That year there was a great drought.
Masculine.
दुष्काळ (duṣkāḷ) पडणे, a drought strikes.

The word, taken apart.
दुष्काळ (duṣkāḷ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit duṣkāla, "a bad time, a famine" (CDIAL 6470), where CDIAL also lists the inherited Marathi dukāḷ.

डोंगरावर ढगफुटी झाली. ḍoṅgarāvar ḍhagphuṭī jhālī. There was a cloudburst on the mountain.

The word, taken apart.
ढगफुटी (ḍhagphuṭī) is built from ढग, "a cloud", and the stem of फुटणे, "to burst" (CDIAL 13845).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कडाका (kaḍākā) — a severe spell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a drought, then the Marathi for a cloudburst.

You'll want to know: कडाका.
कडाका — kaḍākā — "a severe spell".
यंदा थंडीचा कडाका जास्त आहे. yandā thaṇḍīcā kaḍākā jāsta āhe. This year the cold is especially severe.
Masculine.
Of cold or heat: थंडीचा कडाका (kaḍākā), उन्हाचा कडाका (kaḍākā).

The word, taken apart.
कडाका (kaḍākā) is inherited, from the family of Sanskrit kaṭakaṭā, where CDIAL lists Marathi kaḍakṇẽ, "to crackle" (CDIAL 2632): cold sharp enough to crack.

Lesson 4 of 5.
झोडपणे (jhoḍapṇe) — to lash; to batter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cloudburst, then the Marathi for a severe spell.

You'll want to know: झोडपणे.
झोडपणे — jhoḍapṇe — "to lash; to batter".
पावसाने गावाला झोडपलं. pāvsāne gāvālā jhoḍaplaṁ. The rain lashed the village.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने झोडपलं, tyāne jhoḍaplaṁ.
The rain is the agent and takes -ने: पावसाने झोडपलं.

The word, taken apart.
झोडपणे (jhoḍapṇe) is a Marathi verb for heavy rain or blows beating down; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ओसरणे (osarṇe) — to recede; to subside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a severe spell, then the Marathi for to lash.

You'll want to know: ओसरणे.
ओसरणे — osarṇe — "to recede; to subside".
दोन दिवसांनी पूर ओसरला. don divasānnī pūr osarlā. After two days the flood went down.
Past (he …): तो ओसरला, to osarlā.
Floods, crowds and fevers ओसरतात; not the same word as ओसरी, a veranda.

The word, taken apart.
ओसरणे (osarṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi osarṇẽ, "to flow away, decrease, subside", under Sanskrit apasarati, "slips off, goes away" (CDIAL 465).
