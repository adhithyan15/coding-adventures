## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-OR-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C154-HAISHI-01, ZH-LEX-C154-HAI-01, ZH-LEX-C154-HUOZHE-01, ZH-LEX-C154-BIE-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WANG-01, ZH-LEX-C147-HOUTIAN-01] -->

> **A:** 你要水还是要饭
>
> *nǐ yào shuǐ háishi yào fàn* — Do you want water or food?
>
> **B:** 我要水
>
> *wǒ yào shuǐ* — Water, please.
>
> **A:** 你吃饭了吗
>
> *nǐ chī fàn le ma* — Have you eaten?
>
> **B:** 还没
>
> *hái méi* — Not yet.
>
> **A:** 我们去吃饭还是在家吃
>
> *wǒmen qù chī fàn háishi zài jiā chī* — Shall we go out to eat, or eat at home?
>
> **B:** 在家吃 妈妈还在做饭
>
> *zài jiā chī, māma hái zài zuò fàn* — At home. Mum's still cooking.
>
> **A:** 我们明天去还是后天去
>
> *wǒmen míngtiān qù háishi hòutiān qù* — Are we going tomorrow or the day after?
>
> **B:** *míngtiān huòzhě hòutiān dōu kěyǐ* — Tomorrow or the day after: either is fine.
>
> **A:** *hǎo, bié wàng le* — Fine. Don't forget.

还是 asks, and the answer picks one; *huòzhě* refuses to pick. 还没 is a whole answer: not yet. *bié wàng le*: don't forget.

## What you've built — or, still, not yet, don't
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-OR-01, ZH-SCRIPT-HAI-STILL-01, ZH-LEX-C154-HAISHI-01, ZH-LEX-C154-HUOZHE-01, ZH-LEX-C154-HAI-01, ZH-LEX-C154-BIE-01] -->

Four small words that steer a conversation:

- **你要水还是要饭** *nǐ yào shuǐ háishi yào fàn* — water or food?
- *míngtiān huòzhě hòutiān dōu kěyǐ* — tomorrow or the day after, either is fine.
- **我还没吃饭** *wǒ hái méi chī fàn* — I haven't eaten yet.
- *bié qù* — don't go.

**Listen.** *Nǐ hē chá háishi hē shuǐ?*

**Speak.** Offer a friend two choices, then tell them not to forget.
