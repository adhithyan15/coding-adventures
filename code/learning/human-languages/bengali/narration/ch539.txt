Bengali, chapter 539: Facing Obstacles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বিঘ্ন (bighnô) — an obstacle; a hindrance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for imminent, then the Bengali for for emergencies.

You'll want to know: বিঘ্ন.
বিঘ্ন — bighnô — "an obstacle; a hindrance".
বৃষ্টি হলে আমাদের যাত্রায় বিঘ্ন ঘটবে। Brishṭi hôle āmāder jātrāy bighnô ghôṭbe. If it rains, our journey will be held up.
With ঘটা: বিঘ্ন (bighnô) ঘটা, to be disrupted; বিঘ্ন (bighnô) ঘটানো, to disrupt.

The word, taken apart.
বিঘ্ন (bighnô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vighna, "a breaker", and so "an obstacle" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
নাছোড়বান্দা (nāchhoṛbāndā) — persistent; never giving up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for for emergencies, then the Bengali for a hindrance.

You'll want to know: নাছোড়বান্দা.
নাছোড়বান্দা — nāchhoṛbāndā — "persistent; never giving up".
ছেলেটা নাছোড়বান্দা, শেষে মা রাজি হলেন। Chheleṭā nāchhoṛbāndā, sheshe mā rāji hôlen. The boy wouldn't give up, and in the end his mother agreed.

The word, taken apart.
নাছোড়বান্দা (nāchhoṛbāndā) is an expressive compound: না, "not", ছোড়, "letting go" (from the family of ছাড়া, CDIAL 4998), and বান্দা, usually traced to Persian banda, "a servant": one who will not let go.

বন্যার জল বাড়ছিল, তাই লোকজন মরিয়া হল। Bônyār jôl bāṛchhilô, tāi lokjôn môriyā hôlô. The flood water was rising, so people grew desperate.

মরিয়া (môriyā) is probably built on মরা, "to die" (CDIAL 9871): ready to face death itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
হাল ছাড়া (hāl chhāṛā) — to give up hope; to stop trying.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for persistent, then the Bengali for desperate.

You'll want to know: হাল ছাড়া.
হাল ছাড়া — hāl chhāṛā — "to give up hope; to stop trying".
হাল না ছাড়লে তোমার দল জিতবে। Hāl nā chhāṛle tomār dôl jitbe. If your team doesn't give up, it will win.
Past (he/she …): তিনি হাল ছাড়লেন না, tini hāl chhāṛlen nā.

The word, taken apart.
হাল ছাড়া (hāl chhāṛā) joins হাল, "the helm, the rudder", to ছাড়া, "to let go": to let go of the helm. Where হাল in this sense comes from is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
জড়ানো (jôṛāno) — to get tangled up; to wrap round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for desperate, then the Bengali for to give up hope.

You'll want to know: জড়ানো.
জড়ানো — jôṛāno — "to get tangled up; to wrap round".
ঘুড়ির সুতো গাছের ডালে জড়াল। Ghuṛir suto gāchher ḍāle jôṛālô. The kite string got tangled in the branches of the tree.
Past (he/she …): সেই দড়ি চাকায় জড়াল, sei dôṛi chākāy jôṛālô.

The word, taken apart.
জড়ানো (jôṛāno) comes from a reconstructed jaḍati, "joins, sets", which Turner took from Dravidian; CDIAL lists Bengali jaṛāna, "to wrap round, to entangle" (CDIAL 5091).
