Tamil, chapter 135: Electric fan, Scissors, Hammer, Nail, Ladder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
மின்விசிறி (miṉviciṟi) — an electric fan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a mobile phone, then the Tamil for a computer.

You'll want to know: மின்விசிறி.
மின்விசிறி — miṉviciṟi — "an electric fan".

The word, taken apart.
மின்விசிறி (miṉviciṟi) is a modern coinage from native parts: miṉ, "lightning, electricity" (DEDR 4876), and viciṟi, "fan" (DEDR 5450, which compares Sanskrit vīj-, "to fan").

Lesson 4 of 5.
ஆணி (āṇi) — a nail (to hammer).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for scissors, then the Tamil for a hammer.

You'll want to know: ஆணி.
ஆணி — āṇi — "a nail (to hammer)".

The word, taken apart.
ஆணி (āṇi) is filed in DEDR's appendix of Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 10), beside Malayalam āṇi, "peg", and Kannada āṇi, "nail". The source is Sanskrit āṇi, "linch-pin".

The origin of ஏணி (ēṇi) is uncertain. DEDR has an ēṇi, "boundary, edge" (DEDR 886), but not this word for a ladder.
