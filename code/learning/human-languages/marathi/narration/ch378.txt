Marathi, chapter 378: Conditions and Exceptions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अट (aṭ) — a condition (stipulation).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for inspiration, then the Marathi for to bother.

You'll want to know: अट.
अट — aṭ — "a condition (stipulation)".
करारात एक महत्त्वाची अट होती. karārāt ek mahattvācī aṭ hotī. There was an important condition in the contract.
Feminine. Plural: अटी, aṭī.
अटीवर, on condition: एका अटीवर हो म्हणणे, to say yes on one condition.

The word, taken apart.
अट (aṭ) is usually traced to the root aṭṭ-, "obstruct, stop", under which CDIAL lists Marathi aṭ, "obstruction" (CDIAL 179): a condition holds a thing back.

Lesson 2 of 5.
अपवाद (apvād) — an exception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to bother, then the Marathi for a condition.

You'll want to know: अपवाद.
अपवाद — apvād — "an exception".
हा नियम सगळ्यांना लागू आहे, कोणीही अपवाद नाही. hā niyam sagaḷyānnā lāgū āhe, koṇīhī apvād nāhī. This rule applies to everyone; nobody is an exception.
Masculine.
अपवादाने, as an exception; X चा अपवाद (apvād), the exception to X.

The word, taken apart.
अपवाद (apvād) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit apavāda, "evil speaking", which grammarians also used for a special rule that sets a general one aside, an exception (Monier-Williams). The same head gave the inherited Marathi avāī, "rumour" (CDIAL 456).

पुरामुळे प्रवासावर निर्बंध घातले आहेत. purāmuḷe pravāsāvar nirbandh ghātle āhet. Restrictions have been placed on travel because of the flood.

The word, taken apart.
निर्बंध (nirbandh) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nirbandha, "perseverance, insistence" (CDIAL 7355); Marathi uses it for a binding limit.

निकष (nikaṣ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nikaṣa, "roller; touchstone" (CDIAL 7139): the stone gold is tested on.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पूर्तता करणे (pūrtatā karṇe) — to fulfil (a condition).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a curb, then the Marathi for a criterion.

You'll want to know: पूर्तता करणे.
पूर्तता करणे — pūrtatā karṇe — "to fulfil (a condition)".
सगळ्या अटींची पूर्तता केल्यास प्रवेश मिळेल. sagaḷyā aṭīñcī pūrtatā kelyās praveś miḷel. If all the conditions are fulfilled, you will get admission.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पूर्तता केली, tyāne pūrtatā kelī.
What is fulfilled takes -ची: अटींची पूर्तता.

The word, taken apart.
पूर्तता करणे (pūrtatā karṇe) joins पूर्तता, a learned noun built on Sanskrit pūrti, "filling, completion" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".
