German, chapter 119: Scarf, Boots, Skirt, Coat, Shoes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Schal — a scarf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a tie, then the German for gloves.

You'll want to know: der Schal.
der Schal — "a scarf".

The word, taken apart.
Schal from English shawl from Persian šāl, "shawl, scarf" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed.

Stiefel from Old High German stival, from Old French estivel (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed, probably from Latin aestīvāle, a light summer shoe.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Rock — a skirt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a scarf, then the German for boots.

You'll want to know: der Rock.
der Rock — "a skirt".

The word, taken apart.
Rock from Old High German roc, "tunic" from Proto-Germanic hrukkaz, "robe, tunic" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) It first named a coat and later a skirt.

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Schuhe — shoes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a skirt, then the German for a coat.

You'll want to know: die Schuhe.
die Schuhe — "shoes".

The word, taken apart.
Schuhe is the plural of Schuh from Old High German scuoh from Proto-Germanic skōhaz, the same word as English shoe. (EtymDB 2.1).
