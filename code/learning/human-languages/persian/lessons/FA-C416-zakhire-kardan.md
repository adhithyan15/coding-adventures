## You'll want to know: ذخیره کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C416-KARBAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ذخیره کردن** (*zakhire kardan*) — "to save, to store".

> عکسها را در کامپیوتر ذخیره کردم.
>
> *'akshâ râ dar kâmpyuter zakhire kardam.*
>
> I saved the photos on the computer.

*Zakhire* "store, reserve" + *kardan*. Also to store food or water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذخیره** was borrowed from Arabic *dhakhīra* "store, reserve" (EtymDB); Persian says the Arabic *dh* as *z*.
