## You'll want to know: কাস্টমস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C395-BIMAN395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**কাস্টমস** — *kāsṭôms* — "customs (at a border or airport)".

> কাস্টমসে আমাদের ব্যাগ খুলতে হল।
>
> *kāsṭômse āmāder byāg khulte hôlô.*
>
> At customs we had to open our bags.

-তে হল is the past of -তে হবে: খুলতে হল, we had to open.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাস্টমস** (*kāsṭôms*) is borrowed from English *customs*, the office at a border that checks goods and collects duty.
