## You'll want to know: l'exposition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C518-EXPOSITION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'exposition** — "exhibition".

> Nous avons vu une exposition de photos.
>
> *We saw an exhibition of photos.*

Feminine. Informally *une expo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'exposition* is a learned borrowing from Latin *expositiō* "a setting out" (EtymDB), from *expōnere* "to put out", built on *pōnere* "to put" (Lewis & Short).
