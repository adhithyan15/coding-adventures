Russian, chapter 268: Better Than, and Years Ago.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
быстрее, чем; лучше, хуже — comparing two things.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "fast", "expensive" and "cheap". (Býstryy. Dorogóy. Deshyóvyy.) Then "you'd better take a taxi". (Tebé lúchshe vzyat taksí.) That лучше was already a comparison: "better".

Grammar Lens: -ее, and чем.
Most adjectives compare by swapping their ending for -ее: быстрый, быстрее (bystréye); тёплый, теплее (tepléye); интересный, интереснее (interésneye). The form does not change for gender or number.
"Than" is чем, with a comma in front:
Поезд быстрее, чем автобус. — póyezd bystréye, chem avtóbus — The train is faster than the bus.
In a short adjective the stress usually moves onto -ее: bystréye. In a longer one it usually stays where it was: interésneye.

Grammar Lens: the ones that change shape.
A few common ones are learned whole: большой, больше (bólshe), bigger, more; маленький, меньше (ménshe), smaller, less; хороший, лучше (lúchshe), better; плохой, хуже (khúzhe), worse; дорогой, дороже (dorózhe); дешёвый, дешевле (deshévle).
Самолёт быстрее, но поезд дешевле. — samolyót bystréye, no póyezd deshévle — The plane is faster, but the train is cheaper. По-моему, поезд лучше. — po-móyemu, póyezd lúchshe — In my view the train is better.

The word, taken apart.
чем is a form of что, the "by what" form, on the question-root PIE kʷo- behind что, кто and English what. EtymDB 2.1 lists чем without taking it apart, so the link to что is the plain reading of the forms.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: Póyezd bystréye, chem avtóbus.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Samolyót bystréye, no póyezd deshévle.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bólshe, ménshe, lúchshe, khúzhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: Shto lúchshe: póyezd íli samolyót? "Which is better?" Give one reason with potomú shto]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Warmer"? (Теплее.) "Than"? (Чем, with a comma.) "Worse"? (Хуже.) "Cheaper"? (Дешевле.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
уже два года; два года назад (uzhé dva góda; dva góda nazád) — how long, and how long ago.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "already", "back" and "a year". (Uzhé. Nazád. God.) Then "more" and "less". (Bólshe. Ménshe.) Comparing things leads to comparing times: how long, and how long ago.

Grammar Lens: how long, in the present.
Я уже два года учу русский. — ya uzhé dva góda uchú rússkiy — I've been learning Russian for two years.
English says "I have been learning". Russian keeps the present, because the learning is still going on; уже, "already", marks how far it has come. In the same way: Я давно живу здесь. — ya davnó zhivú zdes — I've lived here for a long time.

Grammar Lens: how long ago, in the past.
Назад, "back", follows a stretch of time to mean "ago":
Два года назад я был в Москве. — dva góda nazád ya byl v Moskvé — Two years ago I was in Moscow. Час назад позвонила мама. — chas nazád pozvoníla máma — Mum rang an hour ago.

Grammar Lens: год, года, лет.
After a number, the word for a year changes: один год; два, три, четыре года; from пять to двадцать, лет. Ask someone's age with it: Сколько тебе лет? — skólko tebé let? And answer: Мне двадцать лет. — mne dvádtsat let — I'm twenty.

The word, taken apart.
лет is the counting form of лето, "summer". EtymDB 2.1 traces лето to Proto-Slavic lěto, which already meant "year", so a long count of years is a count of summers. год goes back through Old East Slavic godŭ, "time, year", to Proto-Slavic godŭ, "the suitable time" (EtymDB).

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Dva góda nazád ya byl v Moskvé.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: odín god, dva góda, pyat let]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I've been learning Russian for two years": which tense? (The present, with уже.) "Two years ago"? (Два года назад.) "Five years"? (Пять лет.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — better than, and years ago.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "the train is faster than the bus". (Póyezd bystréye, chem avtóbus.)

What you've built.
Two lessons, and an experience can be measured. -ее and чем for most comparisons, and six to learn whole: больше, меньше, лучше, хуже, дороже, дешевле. The present with уже for what is still going on, the past with назад for when it happened, and год, года, лет to count.
[pause 2 seconds]
A trip, measured:
Два года назад я был в Москве. Поезд был дешевле, чем самолёт, и мне было интересно. Я уже два года мечтаю вернуться.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: Dva góda nazád ya byl v Moskvé.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Póyezd byl deshévle, chem samolyót.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Ya uzhé dva góda mechtáyu vernútsya.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: Shto lúchshe, po-tvóyemu: móre íli góry? Po-tvóyemu is "in your view". Answer with po-móyemu and one comparison]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
