## You'll want to know: हिक्का
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C475-SVEDA475-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हिक्का** — *hikkā* — "a hiccup".

> सः शीघ्रं खादति, अतः तस्य हिक्का भवति।
>
> *saḥ śīghraṃ khādati, ataḥ tasya hikkā bhavati.*
>
> He eats too fast, so he gets hiccups.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिक्का** (*hikkā*) MW gives "hiccup, sob, a spasmodic sound in the throat" and compares the form *hekkā*; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
