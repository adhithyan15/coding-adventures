## You'll want to know: l'arresto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C297-MANETTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'arresto** — "the arrest; the stop".

> L'arresto è avvenuto alle tre di notte.
>
> *The arrest took place at three in the morning.*

*Gli arresti domiciliari* — house arrest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arresto* is built on *arrestare* "to arrest", from Vulgar Latin *arrestāre*, built on *restāre* "to stay behind" (Lewis & Short).
