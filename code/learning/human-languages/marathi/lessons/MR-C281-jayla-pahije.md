# मला जायला पाहिजे (malā jāylā pāhije) — must, and must not

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C196-ACT196-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C280-MHANUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to go for a walk": *phirāylā jāṇe*. **फिरायला** is "for walking", and it is the form this lesson needs.

## You'll want to know: -ायला पाहिजे, 'must, should'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C281-AYLA-PAHIJE-01]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-LIKING, MR-LEX-C110-TIME110-03, MR-LEX-GHAR, MR-LEX-C122-CAN122-04, MR-LEX-VACHNE] -->

> **मला आता घरी जायला पाहिजे.**
>
> *malā ātā gharī jāylā pāhije.* — "I must go home now."

Take **-णे** off and put **-ायला** on: **जाणे** → **जायला** (*jāylā*), **करणे** → **करायला** (*karāylā*), **येणे** → **यायला** (*yāylā*), **वाचणे** → **वाचायला**. Then **पाहिजे** (*pāhije*), which does not change. The one who must stands in the dative, as with **आवडणे**: **मला**, **तुला** (*tulā*, "to you, a friend"), **त्याला** (*tyālā*, "to him"), **तिला** (*tilā*, "to her").

**हवं**, the **हवा** you know, does the same work: **मला जायला हवं** (*malā jāylā havaṁ*).

## You'll want to know: -ऊ नये, 'should not'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C281-U-NAYE-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NAKO, MR-LEX-RATRA] -->

> **तू रात्री जाऊ नयेस.**
>
> *tū rātrī jāū nayes.* — "You shouldn't go at night."

The **-ऊ** of **आम्ही जाऊ** with **नये**, "should not", which takes **-स** after **तू**. Said to no one in particular: **असं करू नये** (*asaṁ karū naye*), "one shouldn't do that". A flat "don't" uses the **नको** you know: **जाऊ नकोस** (*jāū nakos*), "don't go".
