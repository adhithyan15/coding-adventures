# ਬ · ਲ · ੋ — three pieces met inside a word you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-SA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-KA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-NANNA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-BIHARI-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DULAVA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TAINKA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TTHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-GA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-SIHARI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The stem of this chapter's first verb is three pieces, and they are today's three.

First, say the last things you learned once more, out loud and from
memory:

- [YOU SAY: *tusī̃ kivēṁ ho — maiṁ ṭhīk hāṁ — koī gall nahīṁ* · *rabb rākhā*]

## Script you'll notice: ਬ · ਲ · ੋ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-BA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-LA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-HORA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ਬ

**babbā** — *ba*. A consonant, the voiced *b* of *bat*, with no breath after it.

> ਲ

**lallā** — *la*. A consonant, a clear *l* made at the teeth — never the dark *l* at the end of English *full*.

> ੋ

**horā** — *-o*. A mātrā for *o*, written as a single stroke **above** the head-line.

Now put them where you have already heard them:

> **ਬ** + **ੋ** → **ਬੋ** (*bo*);  **ਬੋ** + **ਲ** → **ਬੋਲ** (*bol*)

> **ਬੋਲ** — *bol* — **speak — the bare stem of this chapter's first verb**

You are not being asked to write any of this. You are being asked to
**recognise** it the next time it goes past.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-BA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-LA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-HORA-01] -->

Keep ਬ · ਲ · ੋ in front of you and do not cover any of them. Follow each shape once with a
finger, then copy it once with a pencil, larger than it is printed. Say its
sound as your hand stops.

> This is **observe-and-trace with the model visible**. Nothing in these
> chapters asks you to write a Punjabi word from memory; that ladder starts
> later, and it starts from these same shapes.
