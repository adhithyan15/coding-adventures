## You'll want to know: विरासत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C504-BUDHAPA504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विरासत** — *virāsat* — "an inheritance; a legacy".

> दादा की किताबें मुझे विरासत में मिलीं।
>
> *dādā kī kitābeṁ mujhe virāsat meṁ milīṃ.*
>
> I inherited my grandfather's books.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विरासत** (*virāsat*) is usually traced to Arabic *wirātha*, "inheritance", which reached Hindi through Persian. *virāsat meṁ milnā* is to inherit.
