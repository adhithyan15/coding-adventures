## You'll want to know: lōrum, lōrī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C431-HABENA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lōrum, lōrī** — "a leather strap, a thong; (plural) reins".

> Servus sarcinās lōrīs ad mūlum ligat.
>
> *The slave ties the bags to the mule with straps.*

Second declension, neuter: *lōrum, lōrī n.* A strap of leather for tying, for reins, or for whipping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lōrum* is inherited. Lewis & Short compare Greek *eulēra* 'reins' and suggest an older form *vlōrum*; EtymDB gives no parent, so the link is hedged.
