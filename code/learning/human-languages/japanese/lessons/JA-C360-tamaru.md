## You'll want to know: たまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C360-SHUUNYUU360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たまる** — *tamaru* — "to be saved up; to build up".

> **おかねが たまったら、りょこうに いきたいです。** — *okane ga tamattara, ryokō ni ikitai desu* — "When I have saved enough money, I want to go travelling."

Polite **たまります**, *tamarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ごみが たまりました。**, *gomi ga tamarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たまる** (*tamaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
