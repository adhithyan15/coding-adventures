## You'll want to know: خلیج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C435-KHALIJ435-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خلیج** — *khalīj* — "a bay; a gulf".

> خلیج کا پانی گہرا اور نیلا ہے۔
>
> *khalīj kā pānī gahrā aur nīlā hai.*
>
> The water in the bay is deep and blue.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خلیج** (*khalīj*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khalīj*, "a bay, a gulf".
