## You'll want to know: ब्रह्ममुहूर्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C486-UPAVASA486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्रह्ममुहूर्तः** — *brahmamuhūrtaḥ* — "the hour before dawn".

> पितामहः ब्रह्ममुहूर्ते उत्तिष्ठति।
>
> *pitāmahaḥ brahmamuhūrte uttiṣṭhati.*
>
> Grandfather gets up before dawn.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्रह्ममुहूर्तः** (*brahmamuhūrtaḥ*) is built from *brahman* and **मुहूर्तः**, "an hour"; MW gives only "a particular hour of the day"; "the hour before dawn" is the sense it has in modern use (MW).
