## You'll want to know: متابع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C356-JUMHUR356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**متابع** — *mutābiʿ* — "a follower; an observer".

> للمغنية مليون متابع.
>
> *li-l-mughanniya milyūn mutābiʿ.*
>
> The singer has a million followers.

Also someone who follows the news closely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متابع** (*mutābiʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *t-b-ʿ* (Lane), "to follow". *Mutābiʿ* is the participle of the taught *tābaʿa*, "one who keeps following".
