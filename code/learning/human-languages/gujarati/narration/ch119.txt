Lesson 1 of 5.
કિનારો (kināro) — a shore, a bank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for grass, then the Gujarati for an island.

You'll want to know: કિનારો.
કિનારો (kināro) — "a shore, a bank".

The word, taken apart.
કિનારો (kināro) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kinār, "edge, shore".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ગુફા (guphā) — a cave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a shore, then the Gujarati for a waterfall.

You'll want to know: ગુફા.
ગુફા (guphā) — "a cave".

The word, taken apart.
ગુફા (guphā) comes from gupphā, "cave", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4204). It suggests a crossing of Sanskrit guhā, "cave", with words for hiding, and calls that very doubtful.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ટેકરી (ṭekrī) — a hill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a cave, then the Gujarati for a valley.

You'll want to know: ટેકરી.
ટેકરી (ṭekrī) — "a hill".

The word, taken apart.
ટેકરી (ṭekrī) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under ṭekka, one of many reconstructed hill words it groups under ṭakka, "hill" (CDIAL 5423). CDIAL thinks the variety of forms points to a non-Aryan, perhaps Munda, origin.
