# しあげる (shiageru) — to finish off; to complete (a piece of work)

## You'll want to know: しあげる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C480-MAKE480-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しあげる** — *shiageru* — "to finish off; to complete (a piece of work)".

> **あしたまでに いすを しあげます。** — *ashita made ni isu o shiagemasu* — "I will finish the chair by tomorrow."

Polite **しあげます**, *shiagemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **だいくが いすを しあげました。**, *daiku ga isu o shiagemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しあげる** (*shiageru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
