## You'll want to know: স্তর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C372-BARA372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**স্তর** — *stôr* — "a level; a layer".

> বৃষ্টির পরে নদীর জলের স্তর অনেক বাড়ল।
>
> *brishṭir pôre nôdīr jôler stôr ônek bāṛlô.*
>
> After the rain the water level in the river rose a lot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্তর** (*stôr*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *stara*, 'a layer' (CDIAL 13685). In modern use a layer of something has also become its level: জলের স্তর, the water level.
