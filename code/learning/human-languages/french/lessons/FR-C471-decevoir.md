## You'll want to know: décevoir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C471-DECEPTION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**décevoir** — "to disappoint".

> Le concert m'a beaucoup déçu.
>
> *The concert disappointed me a lot.*

Like *recevoir*: *je déçois*, *j'ai été déçu*. It does not mean to deceive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*décevoir* is inherited, through Old French *deceveir*, from Latin *dēcipere* "to deceive" (EtymDB), built on *capere* "to take" (Lewis & Short).
