# Hear *dāl* — lentils

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-PERFORMANCE-TRANSPORT-FIVE-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-LEX-GHODO-01, MW-LEX-BAJAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Recall the five-word transport payoff, then say horse and market.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-DAAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *dāl* — **lentils**

Two beats, and the second one is long. Hear it twice, point to a bowl of
cooked lentils, and say it once. The spelling stays covered until the sound
and the meaning are comfortable.
