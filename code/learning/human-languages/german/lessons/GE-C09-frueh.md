# früh — "early"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-JAHRESZEIT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] One small word first, because the first season is built out of it.

## You'll want to know: früh
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-FRUEH-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **früh** — "early."

Not a noun and not capitalised: an adjective, and one you will meet constantly —
*früh aufstehen*, to get up early.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-FRUEH-01, GE-SOUND-H-LENGTHEN-01] -->

- `umlaut-ue` — *ü* is a sound English does not have. Say **ee**. Hold it. Now
  round your lips as if for **oo**, and do not let your tongue move. That is *ü*.
- `h-silent-lengthening` — and the *h* is silent again, stretching the vowel, as
  it did in *Jahr*.

*fr-ü-h*: one syllable, the middle held long.
