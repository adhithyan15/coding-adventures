Hindi, chapter 203: A zoo, A museum, A tunnel, A bicycle, A scooter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चिड़ियाघर (ciṛiyāghar) — a zoo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a tower, then the Hindi for a garden.

You'll want to know: चिड़ियाघर.
चिड़ियाघर — ciṛiyāghar — "a zoo".

The word, taken apart.
चिड़ियाघर (ciṛiyāghar) is "bird-house": ciṛiyā, "bird", from Sanskrit caṭaka, "sparrow", a word imitating the call (CDIAL 4571), and ghar, "house", from ghara (CDIAL 4428).
