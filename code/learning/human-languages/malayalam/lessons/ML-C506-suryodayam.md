# സൂര്യോദയം (sūryōdayaṁ) — sunrise

## You'll want to know: സൂര്യോദയം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C506-AKASAM506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**സൂര്യോദയം** — *sūryōdayaṁ* — "sunrise".

> സൂര്യോദയം കാണാൻ ഞങ്ങൾ പുലർച്ചെ എഴുന്നേറ്റു.
>
> *sūryōdayaṁ kāṇān ñaṅṅaḷ pularcce eḻunnēṟṟu.*
>
> We got up at dawn to watch the sunrise.

സൂര്യൻ, the sun, plus ഉദയം, rising: the vowels join as -ോ-.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സൂര്യോദയം** (*sūryōdayaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sūryodaya*, sunrise, from *sūrya*, "the sun", and *udaya*, "rising" (Monier-Williams).
