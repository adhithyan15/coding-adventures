## You'll want to know: घासणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C524-SAPHAI524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**घासणे** — *ghāsṇe* — "to scrub; to rub".

> जेवणानंतर मी सगळी भांडी घासली.
>
> *jevṇānantar mī sagḷī bhāṇḍī ghāslī.*
>
> After the meal I scrubbed all the dishes.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भांडी घासली, *tyāne bhāṇḍī ghāslī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घासणे** (*ghāsṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *gharṣati*, "rubs" (CDIAL 4450).
