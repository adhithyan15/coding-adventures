## You'll want to know: influenciar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C354-INFLUENCIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**influenciar** — "to influence".

> Os amigos influenciam muito os jovens.
>
> *Friends influence young people a lot.*

*Influenciar alguém* takes a direct object, with no preposition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*influenciar* is built on *influência* "influence", from Medieval Latin *īnfluentia* (EtymDB), on Latin *īnfluere* "to flow in" (Lewis & Short).
