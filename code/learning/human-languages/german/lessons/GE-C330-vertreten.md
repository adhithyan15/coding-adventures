## You'll want to know: vertreten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C330-STANDPUNKT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vertreten** — "to advocate (a view), to represent; to stand in for".

> Sie vertritt die Meinung, dass Kinder weniger fernsehen sollten.
>
> *She holds the view that children should watch less television.*

Inseparable and strong: *sie vertritt, sie hat vertreten*. At work, *jemanden vertreten* is to stand in for someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vertreten* is built from *ver-* and *treten* "to step, to tread", inherited from Old High German *tretan* and Proto-Germanic *tredaną* (EtymDB): you step into someone's place, or step forward for a view.
