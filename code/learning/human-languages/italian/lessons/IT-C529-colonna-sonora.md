## You'll want to know: la colonna sonora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C529-CINEMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la colonna sonora** — "soundtrack".

> La colonna sonora del film è bellissima.
>
> *The film's soundtrack is beautiful.*

Literally "the sound strip": on old film, the strip that carried the sound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*colonna sonora* is built from *colonna*, inherited from Latin *columna* "a pillar, a post" (EtymDB), and *sonoro* "of sound", from Latin *sonōrus* (Lewis & Short).
