## You'll want to know: पत्नी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C51-KIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पत्नी** (*patnī*) — "a wife".

**पत्नी** is a wife, the mistress of the household.

It is पतिः with a feminine ending laid on it, and the two have travelled together since before Sanskrit was Sanskrit: Greek *potnia*, "lady", is the very same formation made in the very same way.

So the second word costs almost nothing once the first is yours. Feminine, ending in *-ī*.
