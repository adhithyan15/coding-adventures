## You'll want to know: കള്ളൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C364-KALLAN364-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കള്ളൻ** — *kaḷḷan* — "a thief".

> ഇന്നലെ രാത്രി ഒരു കള്ളൻ ഞങ്ങളുടെ വീട്ടിൽ കയറി.
>
> *innale rātri oru kaḷḷan ñaṅṅaḷuṭe vīṭṭil kayaṟi.*
>
> Last night a thief got into our house.

Plural: കള്ളന്മാർ, *kaḷḷanmār*.

A woman thief is കള്ളി. Also said, half in fun, of a cheeky child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കള്ളൻ** (*kaḷḷan*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1372 groups Malayalam *kaḷḷan*, "thief, liar, rogue", with Tamil *kaḷ*, "to steal".
