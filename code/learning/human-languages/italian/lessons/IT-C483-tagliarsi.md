## You'll want to know: tagliarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C483-FERITE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tagliarsi** — "to cut oneself".

> Mi sono tagliato con il coltello.
>
> *I cut myself with the knife.*

*Tagliarsi un dito* — to cut a finger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tagliarsi* is the reflexive of *tagliare* "to cut", inherited from Late Latin *tāleāre*, from *tālea* "a rod, a cutting" (Lewis & Short).
