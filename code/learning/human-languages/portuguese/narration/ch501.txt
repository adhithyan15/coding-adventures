Portuguese, chapter 501: Mãos na terra.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a jardinagem — gardening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to knit, then the Portuguese for to embroider.

You'll want to know: a jardinagem.
a jardinagem — "gardening".
A jardinagem é o passatempo preferido do meu pai. Gardening is my father's favourite hobby.
Built on o jardim.

The word, taken apart.
jardinagem comes from French jardinage "gardening" (EtymDB), built on jardin "garden", a word of Germanic origin.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: semear.
semear — "to sow".
Em março semeamos tomates. In March we sow tomatoes.
Present: semeio, semeias, semeia.

The word, taken apart.
semear is inherited, through Old Portuguese (EtymDB), from Latin sēmināre "to sow", built on sēmen "seed" (Lewis & Short).
