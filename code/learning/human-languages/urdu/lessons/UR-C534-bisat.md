## You'll want to know: بساط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C534-BISAT534-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بساط** — *bisāt* — "a game board".

> اگر بساط ہے تو ہم شطرنج کھیلیں گے۔
>
> *agar bisāt hai to ham shatranj kheleṅ ge.*
>
> If you have a board, we'll play chess.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بساط** (*bisāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bisāṭ*, "a carpet, something spread out".
