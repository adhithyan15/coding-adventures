## You'll want to know: la maternité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C309-BAPTEME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la maternité** — "the maternity hospital; motherhood".

> Le bébé est né à la maternité de la ville.
>
> *The baby was born at the town's maternity hospital.*

*Le congé de maternité* — maternity leave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maternité* is a learned borrowing from Latin *māternitās* "motherhood" (EtymDB), built on *māter* "mother".
