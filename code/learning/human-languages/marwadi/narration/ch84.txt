Lesson 1 of 5.
गांव (gā̃v) — a village.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a boat, then the Marwadi for a bicycle.

You'll want to know: गांव.
गांव — gā̃v — "a village".

The word, taken apart.
गांव (gā̃v) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from grāma, "troop, village". CDIAL lists Marwari gā̃v under it (CDIAL 4368).

पजामो — pajāmo — "pyjama trousers".

The word, taken apart.
पजामो (pajāmo) is a loan, usually traced to Persian pāy-jāma, "leg garment". It is built on Persian pāy, "leg", and jāma, "garment". English pyjamas is the same word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
जूती (jūtī) — a shoe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a kurta, then the Marwadi for pyjama trousers.

You'll want to know: जूती.
जूती — jūtī — "a shoe".

The word, taken apart.
जूती (jūtī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from yukta, "joined, yoked". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 10479); the match is through Hindi jūtī and Gujarati jūtī. The sense moved from "fastened on" to "shoe".
