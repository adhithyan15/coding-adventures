Italian, chapter 438: Il reclamo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il reclamo — the complaint (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the mould, then the Italian for to creak.

You'll want to know: il reclamo.
il reclamo — "the complaint (formal)".
Ho scritto un reclamo alla direzione. I wrote a complaint to the management.
Fare reclamo — to lodge a complaint.

The word, taken apart.
reclamo is built on reclamare, a learned borrowing from Latin reclāmāre 'to cry out against' (EtymDB), from clāmāre 'to shout'.

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la lamentela — the grumble, the complaint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the complaint, then the Italian for to complain.

You'll want to know: la lamentela.
la lamentela — "the grumble, the complaint".
Ricevono molte lamentele per la colazione. They get a lot of complaints about breakfast.
Less formal than il reclamo; lamentarsi — to complain.

The word, taken apart.
lamentela is built on lamentare 'to lament', which is inherited from Latin lāmentārī (EtymDB), from lāmentum 'wailing' (Lewis & Short).
