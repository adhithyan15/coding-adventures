## You'll want to know: reclamar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C604-PROTESTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**reclamar** — "to claim, to demand; to complain".

> Reclamo mi dinero.
>
> *I am claiming my money back.*

*Reclamar a la empresa* — to complain to the company.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reclamar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *reclamare* 'to cry out against', built on *clamare* 'to shout' (EtymDB).
