## You'll want to know: മാൾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C491-KATA491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**മാൾ** — *māḷ* — "a shopping mall".

> ഞായറാഴ്ച മാളിൽ വലിയ തിരക്കായിരുന്നു.
>
> *ñāyaṟāḻca māḷil valiya tirakkāyirunnu.*
>
> The mall was very crowded on Sunday.

A loan ending in a chillu: മാൾ, മാളിൽ, in the mall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മാൾ** (*māḷ*) was borrowed from English *mall*, a large building full of shops.
