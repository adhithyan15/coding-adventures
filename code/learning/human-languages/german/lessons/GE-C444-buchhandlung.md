## You'll want to know: die Buchhandlung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C444-BAUMARKT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Buchhandlung** — "the bookshop".

> In der Buchhandlung findet sie einen Reiseführer.
>
> *In the bookshop she finds a travel guide.*

Plural: *die Buchhandlungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Buchhandlung* is built from *Buch* 'book', inherited from Old High German *buoh* (EtymDB), and *Handlung* in the old sense 'trade, shop', from *handeln* 'to trade'.
