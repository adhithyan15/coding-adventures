# ఒక టిక్కెట్టు ఇవ్వండి (oka ṭikkeṭṭu ivvaṇḍi) — at the ticket counter

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C130-THINGS130-04, TE-LEX-GIVE, TE-LEX-C321-SITU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a ticket" (*ṭikkeṭṭu*) and "give", politely (*ivvaṇḍi*). Then "is this seat free?"
(*ī sīṭu khāḷīgā undā?*)

## You'll want to know: buying a ticket
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C321-BUY-TICKET-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C130-THINGS130-04, TE-LEX-C72-MARKET-03, TE-LEX-C246-KRIYA246-02] -->

> **విజయవాడకి రెండు టిక్కెట్లు ఇవ్వండి.**
>
> *vijayavāḍaki reṇḍu ṭikkeṭlu ivvaṇḍi.* — "Two tickets to Vijayawada, please."

The place takes **-కి**, "to"; the number comes before the ticket; **ఇవ్వండి**, "give", closes the request.

> **టిక్కెట్టు ధర ఎంత?**
>
> *ṭikkeṭṭu dhara enta?* — "How much is the ticket?"

**ఎంత** (*enta*, "how much"). For the way back:

> **నేను రేపు తిరిగి వస్తాను.**
>
> *nēnu rēpu tirigi vastānu.* — "I will come back tomorrow."

**తిరిగి**, "back", is the one in **తిరిగి ఇవ్వు**, "to give back".

## You'll want to know: when does it leave?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C321-WHEN-LEAVES-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C133-THINGS133-05, TE-LEX-C50-LEAVE-04, TE-LEX-C146-ACT146-01, TE-LEX-C18-GANTA-01, TE-LEX-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-01] -->

> **రైలు ఎన్ని గంటలకు బయలుదేరుతుంది?**
>
> *railu enni gaṇṭalaku bayaludērutundi?* — "At what time does the train leave?"

> **ఆరు గంటలకు బయలుదేరుతుంది.**
>
> *āru gaṇṭalaku bayaludērutundi.* — "It leaves at six."

**ఎన్ని గంటలకు** (*enni gaṇṭalaku*, "at how many o'clock"): times of day take **-కు**, "at": **ఆరు గంటలకు**, "at six".
