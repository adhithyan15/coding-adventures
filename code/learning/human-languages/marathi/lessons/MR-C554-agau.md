## You'll want to know: आगाऊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C554-VEL554-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आगाऊ** — *āgāū* — "in advance".

> सहलीचे पैसे आम्ही आगाऊ भरले.
>
> *sahlīce paise āmhī āgāū bharle.*
>
> We paid for the trip in advance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आगाऊ** (*āgāū*), "ahead of time", is perhaps kin to Marathi *agāv*, "in front", which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit *agra*, "top, front" (CDIAL 68). CDIAL lists no आगाऊ itself, so the link is not settled.
