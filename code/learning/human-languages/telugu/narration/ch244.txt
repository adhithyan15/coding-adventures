Telugu, chapter 244: To blow, To shine, To freeze, To wilt, To bark.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వీచు (vīcu) — to blow (of the wind).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to inform, then the Telugu for to transfer.

You'll want to know: వీచు.
వీచు — vīcu — "to blow (of the wind)".

The word, taken apart.
వీచు (vīcu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5450 lists vīcu, "to blow as the wind", with Tamil vīcu and Kannada bīsu, "to blow, fan". DEDR compares Sanskrit vyajana, "fanning; fan" (CDIAL 12043).
