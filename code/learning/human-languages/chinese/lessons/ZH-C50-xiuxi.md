## You'll want to know: xiūxi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C50-SLEEP50-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xiūxi* — "to rest".

> *xīngqītiān wǒ xiūxi* — On Sunday I take it easy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiūxi* is built from xiū 'to rest' and xi 'to stop, to breathe', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xjuw* and *sik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
