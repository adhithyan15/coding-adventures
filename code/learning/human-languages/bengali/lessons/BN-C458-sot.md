## You'll want to know: সৎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C458-SOT458-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সৎ** — *sôt* — "honest; upright".

> সৎ লোকটা মানিব্যাগটা পুলিশকে দিল।
>
> *sôt lokṭā mānibyāgṭā pulishke dilô.*
>
> The honest man handed the wallet to the police.

সৎ also marks a step-relation: সৎ ভাই, a half-brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সৎ** (*sôt*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sat*, "being, existing" and "true, good, honest" (Monier-Williams); it ends in the new letter ৎ.
