## You'll want to know: قصه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C345-QESSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قصه** (*qesse*) — "a tale, a story".

> مادربزرگم هر شب برای ما قصه میگفت.
>
> *Mâdarbozorgam har shab barâ-ye mâ qesse migoft.*
>
> Every night my grandmother used to tell us a story.

Close to *dâstân*, but often a folk tale told aloud. *Qesse goftan* "to tell a tale".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qesse* is borrowed from Arabic *qiṣṣa*, "story" (EtymDB).
