# ലോറി (lōṟi) — a lorry

## You'll want to know: ലോറി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C467-CARAKKU467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ലോറി** — *lōṟi* — "a lorry".

> ഒരു വലിയ ലോറി പാലത്തിന് മുകളിലൂടെ പോയി.
>
> *oru valiya lōṟi pālattinŭ mukaḷilūṭe pōyi.*
>
> A big lorry went over the bridge.

Malayalam keeps the British word; a truck is a ലോറി too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ലോറി** (*lōṟi*) was borrowed from English *lorry*, a large goods vehicle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
