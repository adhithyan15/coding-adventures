## You'll want to know: ふくらむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C473-SIMMER473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ふくらむ** — *fukuramu* — "to swell; to puff up".

> **もちが おおきく ふくらみました。** — *mochi ga ōkiku fukuramimashita* — "The rice cake puffed up big."

Polite **ふくらみます**, *fukuramimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **もちが ふくらみました。**, *mochi ga fukuramimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふくらむ** (*fukuramu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
