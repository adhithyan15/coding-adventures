## You'll want to know: अनेकदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C306-KRAMASH306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अनेकदा** — *anekdā* — "many times".

> मी हा किल्ला अनेकदा पाहिला आहे.
>
> *mī hā killā anekdā pāhilā āhe.*
>
> I have seen this fort many times.

Built from अनेक, many, and -दा, times.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनेकदा** (*anekdā*) is built from Sanskrit *aneka*, 'not one, many' (Monier-Williams), and the ending *-dā*, 'times'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
