## You'll want to know: पुष्टः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C476-SAKTI476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पुष्टः** — *puṣṭaḥ* — "well-nourished".

> पुष्टः वत्सः क्षेत्रे क्रीडति।
>
> *puṣṭaḥ vatsaḥ kṣetre krīḍati.*
>
> The well-fed calf plays in the field.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुष्टः** (*puṣṭaḥ*) is built on the root √puṣ, "to thrive"; MW gives "nourished, well-fed, thriving, strong" (MW).
