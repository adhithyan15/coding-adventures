## You'll want to know: منصوبہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C378-HADAF378-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منصوبہ** — *mansūbā* — "a scheme; a plan (worked out)".

> اگر منصوبہ اچھا ہے تو حکومت پیسے دے گی۔
>
> *agar mansūbā achchhā hai to hukūmat paise de gī.*
>
> If the scheme is good, the government will fund it.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منصوبہ** (*mansūbā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *manṣūba*, "something set up, arranged"; the sense "a plan" is the one Urdu uses.
