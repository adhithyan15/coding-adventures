## You'll want to know: اسکی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C511-OUTDOORS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اسکی** (*eski*) — "skiing; a ski".

> زمستانها برای اسکی به کوه میرویم.
>
> *zemestânhâ barâ-ye eski be kuh miravim.*
>
> In winter we go to the mountains to ski.

*eski kardan* — to ski. Persian puts *e-* before the *sk-* cluster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eski* was borrowed from French *ski*, which EtymDB traces to Norwegian *ski* and Old Norse *skíð*, 'stick of wood, snowshoe' (EtymDB).
