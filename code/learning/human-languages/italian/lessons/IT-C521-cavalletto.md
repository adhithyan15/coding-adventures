## You'll want to know: il cavalletto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C521-PITTURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il cavalletto** — "easel; stand".

> Metto la tela sul cavalletto.
>
> *I put the canvas on the easel.*

A little *cavallo* "horse"; any frame on legs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cavalletto* is a little *cavallo* "horse", inherited from Latin *caballus* "a nag" (Lewis & Short): a frame on legs.
