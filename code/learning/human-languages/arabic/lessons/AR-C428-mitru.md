## You'll want to know: مترو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C428-SIKKA428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مترو** — *mitrū* — "the metro; the underground".

> يذهب أبي إلى العمل بالمترو.
>
> *yadhhabu abī ilā al-ʿamal bi-l-mitrū.*
>
> My father goes to work by metro.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مترو** (*mitrū*) is usually traced to French *métro*, short for *métropolitain*.
