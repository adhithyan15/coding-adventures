German, chapter 89: Small, Short, Fast, Slow, Light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
klein — small.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to fetch, then the German for to wish.

You'll want to know: klein.
klein — "small".

The word, taken apart.
klein from Old High German kleini, "fine, delicate" from Proto-Germanic klainiz, "shining, fine" — inherited, the same word as English clean. (EtymDB 2.1) German moved from "fine" to "small".
