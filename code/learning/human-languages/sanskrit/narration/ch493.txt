Sanskrit, chapter 493: Rites of Childhood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संस्कारः (saṃskāraḥ) — a rite of passage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for newborn, then the Sanskrit for a swing.

You'll want to know: संस्कारः.
संस्कारः — saṃskāraḥ — "a rite of passage".
विवाहः अपि एकः संस्कारः अस्ति। vivāhaḥ api ekaḥ saṃskāraḥ asti. A wedding too is a rite of passage.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
संस्कारः (saṃskāraḥ) is built from sam and the root √kṛ, "to make": a making perfect; MW gives "a sacred or sanctifying ceremony", twelve of them in a life (MW).

The word, taken apart.
उपनयनम् (upanayanam) is built from upa, "near", and the root √nī, "to lead", the root of नयति: the teacher leads the boy to himself (MW).
