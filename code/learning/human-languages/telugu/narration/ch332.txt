Telugu, chapter 332: Already, Meanwhile, Suddenly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అప్పటికే (appaṭikē) — already by then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to rescue, then the Telugu for to run for it.

You'll want to know: అప్పటికే.
అప్పటికే — appaṭikē — "already by then".
పోలీసులు వచ్చారు, కానీ దొంగ అప్పటికే పారిపోయాడు. pōlīsulu vaccāru, kānī doṅga appaṭikē pāripōyāḍu. The police came, but the thief had already run away.
అప్పటికి, by then, with -ఏ, the ending that stresses. Compare ఇప్పటికే, already by now.

The word, taken apart.
అప్పటికే (appaṭikē) is native Telugu: appaṭiki, "by then", from appuḍu, "at that time" (DEDR 4559), with the stressing ending -ē.

Lesson 2 of 5.
అంతలో (antalō) — in the meantime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to run for it, then the Telugu for already by then.

You'll want to know: అంతలో.
అంతలో — antalō — "in the meantime".
నేను ఫోను చేశాను, అంతలో అతనే వచ్చాడు. nēnu phōnu cēśānu, antalō atanē vaccāḍu. I phoned, and in the meantime he came himself.
అంత, that much, with -లో, in: within that span. It joins two happenings that overlap.

The word, taken apart.
అంతలో (antalō) is native Telugu: anta, "that much", from the far demonstrative a- (DEDR 1), with the locative ending -lō, "in".

Lesson 3 of 5.
హఠాత్తుగా (haṭhāttugā) — all of a sudden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for already by then, then the Telugu for in the meantime.

You'll want to know: హఠాత్తుగా.
హఠాత్తుగా — haṭhāttugā — "all of a sudden".
హఠాత్తుగా కరెంటు పోయింది. haṭhāttugā kareṇṭu pōyindi. All of a sudden the power went.
Like అకస్మాత్తుగా, it ends in -గా, the ending that makes an adverb.

The word, taken apart.
హఠాత్తుగా (haṭhāttugā) is built on Sanskrit haṭhāt, "by force, forcibly", a form of haṭha, "force" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu adverb ending -gā.

Lesson 4 of 5.
క్షణం (kṣaṇaṁ) — a moment; an instant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for in the meantime, then the Telugu for all of a sudden.

You'll want to know: క్షణం.
క్షణం — kṣaṇaṁ — "a moment; an instant".
ఒక్క క్షణంలో అంతా మారిపోయింది. okka kṣaṇaṁlō antā māripōyindi. In a single moment everything changed.
Plural: క్షణాలు, kṣaṇālu.
ఒక్క క్షణం (kṣaṇaṁ), one moment, also asks someone to wait.

The word, taken apart.
క్షణం (kṣaṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit kṣaṇa, "the twinkling of an eye, a moment" (CDIAL 3642).

Lesson 5 of 5.
అరుచు (arucu) — to yell; to shout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for all of a sudden, then the Telugu for a moment.

You'll want to know: అరుచు.
అరుచు — arucu — "to yell; to shout".
పామును చూసి పిల్లవాడు గట్టిగా అరిచాడు. pāmunu cūsi pillavāḍu gaṭṭigā aricāḍu. Seeing a snake, the boy yelled loudly.
Past (I …): అరిచాను, aricānu.
You know its noun, అరుపు, a shout.

The word, taken apart.
అరుచు (arucu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 367 lists Telugu ārcu, ārucu, "to cry aloud, shout", with Tamil ār, "to shout, roar".
