Portuguese, chapter 335: Na minha opinião.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a opinião — the opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a jungle, then the Portuguese for a slipper.

You'll want to know: a opinião.
a opinião — "the opinion".
Cada pessoa tem a sua opinião. Every person has their own opinion.
Na minha opinião opens a view; mudar de opinião is to change your mind.

The word, taken apart.
opinião comes from Latin opīniō, opīniōnis "opinion, supposition, belief" (Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese opynion (EtymDB); it is built on the verb opīnārī "to suppose".

Lesson 2 of 5.
convencido — convinced, sure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a slipper, then the Portuguese for the opinion.

You'll want to know: convencido.
convencido — "convinced, sure".
Estou convencido de que é a melhor solução. I am convinced that it is the best solution.
Estar convencido de que… — to be convinced that… A woman says convencida. It is the past participle of convencer.

The word, taken apart.
convencido is the past participle of convencer, which comes from Latin convincere "to overcome, to convict of error" (Lewis & Short), built on vincere "to conquer".

Lesson 3 of 5.
o ponto de vista — the point of view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the opinion, then the Portuguese for convinced.

You'll want to know: o ponto de vista.
o ponto de vista — "the point of view".
Do meu ponto de vista, a ideia é boa. From my point of view, the idea is good.
The plural is os pontos de vista. Do ponto de vista de… — from the point of view of…

The word, taken apart.
ponto is inherited from Latin punctum "a prick, a point" (Lewis & Short), and vista is built on ver "to see", inherited from Latin vidēre: the spot you see things from.

Lesson 4 of 5.
opinar — to give an opinion, to express a view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for convinced, then the Portuguese for the point of view.

You'll want to know: opinar.
opinar — "to give an opinion, to express a view".
Toda a gente quis opinar sobre o novo jardim. Everyone wanted to give an opinion on the new garden.
Opinar sobre — to give a view on. A little formal; in speech people say dar a opinião.

The word, taken apart.
opinar is a learned borrowing from Latin opīnārī "to be of opinion, to suppose, to believe" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
sustentar — to maintain, to claim; to support (a family, a weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the point of view, then the Portuguese for to give an opinion.

You'll want to know: sustentar.
sustentar — "to maintain, to claim; to support (a family, a weight)".
Ele sustenta que o comboio é mais rápido do que o carro. He maintains that the train is faster than the car.
Also to support a family: sustentar a família.

The word, taken apart.
sustentar is a learned borrowing from Latin sustentāre "to hold up, to support, to sustain" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), a frequentative of sustinēre, built from sub "under" and tenēre "to hold".
