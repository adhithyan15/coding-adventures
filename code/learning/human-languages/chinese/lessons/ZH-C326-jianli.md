## You'll want to know: jiànlì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C326-WORLD326-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jiànlì* — "to found; to set up".

> *tāmen yìbǎi nián qián jiànlì le zhège xuéxiào* — They founded this school a hundred years ago.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiànlì* is built from *jiàn* and *lì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjonH* and *lip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to set up' and 'to plant', and the second 'to stand' and 'to complete'.
