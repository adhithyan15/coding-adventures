## You'll want to know: económico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C630-ECONOMIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**económico** — "economic; cheap".

> Es un hotel económico.
>
> *It is a cheap hotel.*

It means cheap as well as economic: *una opción económica*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*económico* is a learned borrowing from Latin *oeconomicus*, taken from Greek *oikonomikos* 'skilled in household management', from *oikos* 'house' (EtymDB).
