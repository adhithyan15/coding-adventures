## You'll want to know: आकुलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C548-SANTVANA548-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आकुलः** — *ākulaḥ* — "flustered; agitated".

> यदि भवान् आकुलः अस्ति, तर्हि माम् आह्वयतु।
>
> *yadi bhavān ākulaḥ asti, tarhi mām āhvayatu.*
>
> If you are flustered, please call me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकुलः** (*ākulaḥ*) is a word MW traces, with a query, to *ā* and the root √kṝ, "to scatter"; MW gives *ākula* as confounded, confused, agitated, flurried, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
