## You'll want to know: vadear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C456-BOSQUE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vadear** — "to wade across, to ford".

> Vadeámos a ribeira com as botas na mão.
>
> *We waded across the stream with our boots in our hands.*

Built on *o vau*, the ford.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vadear* goes back to Latin *vadum* 'shallow place, ford', the source of *vau* 'ford' (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
