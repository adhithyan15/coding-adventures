## You'll want to know: zhuī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C311-POLICE311-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuī* — "to chase; to run after".

> *jǐngchá zài zhuī xiǎotōu* — The police are chasing the thief.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuī* is inherited from Middle Chinese *trwij* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'to chase' and 'to follow'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
