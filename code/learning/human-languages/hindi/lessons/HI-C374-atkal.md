## You'll want to know: अटकल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C374-ANDAZA374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अटकल** — *aṭkal* — "a guess, speculation".

> लोगों की अटकल है कि अगर वह चुनाव जीत गया तो मंत्री बनेगा।
>
> *logoṁ kī aṭkal hai ki agar vah cunāv jīt gayā to mantrī banegā.*
>
> People speculate that if he wins the election, he'll become a minister.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अटकल** (*aṭkal*) is inherited from *\*aṭṭakkalā*, "a guess", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 183).
