## You'll want to know: ぜんりょく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C516-AHEAD516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんりょく** — *zenryoku* — "all one's strength; one's utmost".

> **もし ぜんりょくで はしったら、かれは まにあうでしょう。** — *moshi zenryoku de hashittara, kare wa maniau deshō* — "If he runs with all his strength, he will make it."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんりょく** (*zenryoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zen*, "whole" (Middle Chinese *dzjwen*) and *ryoku*, "power" (*lik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
