## You'll want to know: गजर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C547-ANIBANI547-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गजर** — *gajar* — "an alarm".

> पहाटे पाच वाजता घड्याळाचा गजर वाजला.
>
> *pahāṭe pāc vājtā ghaḍyāḷācā gajar vājlā.*
>
> The alarm clock rang at five in the morning.

Masculine.

गजर वाजणे, an alarm rings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गजर** (*gajar*) is the word for an alarm or a loud call; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
