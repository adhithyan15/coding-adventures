## You'll want to know: affettare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C332-TAGLIERE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**affettare** — "to slice".

> Ho affettato il pane e il prosciutto.
>
> *I sliced the bread and the ham.*

Built on *fetta*, ‘slice’. *Gli affettati* are cold cuts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*affettare* is built on *fetta* "slice", whose origin is not settled (EtymDB has no entry).
