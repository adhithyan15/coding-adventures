## You'll want to know: 如果
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C289-RUGUO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**如果** — *rúguǒ* — "if; in case".

> **如果你有时间 就来我家** *rúguǒ nǐ yǒu shíjiān, jiù lái wǒ jiā* — If you have time, come round.

如果 means what 要是 means, and it fits anywhere, in speech or in writing; 要是 sounds more casual. The next lesson puts the second character in your hand; the first is the 如 of 比如, and the second is the guǒ of *jiéguǒ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**如果** (*rúguǒ*) is built from 如 and 果, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyo* and *kwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first with several words, among them 'to resemble', and the second 'resolute' and 'the fruit of a tree'. Whether 'if' was already among the first's senses the gloss does not make clear; 'if' for the pair is the modern word's sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C288-YAOSHI-01, ZH-LEX-C288-JIU-01, ZH-LEX-C281-BIRU-01] -->

如果 opens the condition as 要是 does, and 就 opens the result.

要是 belongs to easy talk; 如果 fits a chat, a note or a talk alike.
