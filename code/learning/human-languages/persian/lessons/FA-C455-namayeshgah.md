## You'll want to know: نمایشگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C455-SHAHRBAZI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نمایشگاه** (*namâyeshgâh*) — "an exhibition; a trade fair".

> هفته پیش به نمایشگاه کتاب رفتیم.
>
> *Hafte-ye pish be namâyeshgâh-e ketâb raftim.*
>
> Last week we went to the book fair.

*namâyesh* 'show' + *gâh* 'place'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*namâyeshgâh* is a native compound of *namâyesh* 'a showing', built on *nemudan* 'to show', and *gâh* 'place'.
