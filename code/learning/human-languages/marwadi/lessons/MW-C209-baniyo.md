## You'll want to know: बाणियो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C209-SHOP209-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बाणियो** — *bāṇiyo* — "a trader; a shopkeeper".

> बाणियो दिनूगै दुकान खोलै है।
>
> *bāṇiyo dinūgai dukān kholai hai.*
>
> The trader opens the shop early in the morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाणियो** (*bāṇiyo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vāṇija*, "trader"; CDIAL lists Marwari *bā̃ṇyõ*, "trader" (CDIAL 11484).
