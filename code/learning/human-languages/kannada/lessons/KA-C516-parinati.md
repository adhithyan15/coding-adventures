# ಪರಿಣತಿ (pariṇati) — expertise; mastery

## You'll want to know: ಪರಿಣತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C516-VRITTI516-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಣತಿ** — *pariṇati* — "expertise; mastery".

> ಅವರಿಗೆ ಕಂಪ್ಯೂಟರ್ ಕೆಲಸದಲ್ಲಿ ಪರಿಣತಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *avarige kampyūṭar kelasadalli pariṇati ide.*
>
> She has expertise in computer work.

ಪರಿಣತ, an expert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಣತಿ** (*pariṇati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pariṇati*, from *pari-ṇam*, "to change, ripen" (Monier-Williams): skill that has ripened.
