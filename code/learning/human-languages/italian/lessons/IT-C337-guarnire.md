## You'll want to know: guarnire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C337-CONDIRE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**guarnire** — "to garnish".

> Guarnisci il piatto con una fetta di limone.
>
> *Garnish the dish with a slice of lemon.*

Present like *capire*: *guarnisco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guarnire* is usually traced to a Germanic verb *warnjan*; EtymDB lists it beside Old French *guarnir* "to equip, to protect" and French *garnir* "to furnish, to garnish".
