Kannada, chapter 135: Chin, Cheek, Knee, Elbow, Wrist.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ಗಲ್ಲ (galla) — the chin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the face, then the Kannada for the tongue.

You'll want to know: ಗಲ್ಲ.
ಗಲ್ಲ — galla — "the chin".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: galla, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say galla]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for the face, then the Kannada for the tongue, then say galla again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಗಲ್ಲ (galla) mean? ("The chin".) Say it once more.
