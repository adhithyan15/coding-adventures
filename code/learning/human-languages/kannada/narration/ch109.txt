Kannada, chapter 109: Bridge, Park, Temple, Library, Bank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸೇತುವೆ (sētuve) — a bridge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for in the middle, then the Kannada for a road.

You'll want to know: ಸೇತುವೆ.
ಸೇತುವೆ — sētuve — "a bridge".

The word, taken apart.
ಸೇತುವೆ (sētuve) comes from Sanskrit setu, "dam, bridge", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 13585). Kannada added -ve to the end.
