## You'll want to know: das Stipendium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C380-ZULASSUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Stipendium** — "the scholarship, the grant".

> Mit einem Stipendium kann sie im Ausland studieren.
>
> *With a scholarship she can study abroad.*

Plural: *die Stipendien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Stipendium* was taken from Latin *stīpendium* "a tax, a soldier's pay" (Lewis & Short), from *stips* "a small coin" and *pendere* "to weigh out, to pay".
