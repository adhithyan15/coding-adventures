## You'll want to know: পরিবেশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C434-PROKRITI434-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিবেশ** — *pôribesh* — "the environment; surroundings".

> কারখানার ধোঁয়া পরিবেশের ক্ষতি করে।
>
> *kārkhānār dhõyā pôribesher khôti kôre.*
>
> Factory smoke harms the environment.

Also the surroundings of a place: শান্ত পরিবেশ, a calm setting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিবেশ** (*pôribesh*) is Sanskrit *pariveṣa*, "a surrounding, a circle, a halo" (Monier-Williams); the sense "the environment" is a modern use.
