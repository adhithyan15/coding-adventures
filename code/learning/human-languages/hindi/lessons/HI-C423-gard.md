## You'll want to know: गार्ड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C423-GARD423-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गार्ड** — *gārḍ* — "a guard".

> बैंक के दरवाज़े पर एक गार्ड खड़ा था।
>
> *baiṅk ke darvāze par ek gārḍ khaṛā thā.*
>
> A guard was standing at the door of the bank.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गार्ड** (*gārḍ*) is borrowed from English *guard*, the security guard at a bank, a shop or a block of flats; on the railway it is also the guard at the back of the train.
