French, chapter 86: Bear, Fox, Snake, Hen, Duck.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'ours — a bear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a mouse, then the French for a wolf.

You'll want to know: l'ours.
l'ours — "a bear".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: l'ours]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: l'ours, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say l'ours]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the French for a mouse, then the French for a wolf, then say l'ours again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does l'ours mean? ("A bear".) Say it once more.
