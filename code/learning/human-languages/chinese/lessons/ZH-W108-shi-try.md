## Script — the speech piece, then a slanting hook
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHI-TRY-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 试

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The speech piece, two strokes**, as in 说: a dot, then a stroke across that turns down and rises.

**The right piece, six strokes**: a horizontal across the top; then, low on the left, a short horizontal, a vertical and a stroke that rises to the right; then one long stroke that slants down to the right and hooks up; and last a dot at the top right.

2 + 6 = eight.
