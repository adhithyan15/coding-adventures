German, chapter 198: An animal, A hare, A fly, A butterfly, A plant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Tier — an animal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for cold, then the German for lightning.

You'll want to know: das Tier.
das Tier — "an animal".

The word, taken apart.
Tier from Old High German tior from Proto-Germanic deuzą, "animal" — inherited, the same word as English deer, which narrowed to one kind of animal. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Schmetterling — a butterfly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a hare, then the German for a fly.

You'll want to know: der Schmetterling.
der Schmetterling — "a butterfly".

The word, taken apart.
Schmetterling is built on an East Central German word for "cream", taken from Czech smetana (EtymDB 2.1), + -ling: butterflies were said to be drawn to milk and cream.

Pflanze from Old High German pflanza from Latin planta, "shoot, cutting" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed so early that its p shifted to pf. English plant is the same Latin word.
