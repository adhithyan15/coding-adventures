ಜಗಲಿ (jagali) — a raised veranda, a front porch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for cement, then the Kannada for bamboo.

You'll want to know: ಜಗಲಿ.
ಜಗಲಿ — jagali — "a raised veranda, a front porch".

The word, taken apart.
ಜಗಲಿ (jagali) is native Dravidian, one word with Telugu jagati (DEDR 2279). DEDR lists jagali, "a raised place, a seat of mud or stone".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಅಟ್ಟ (aṭṭa) — a loft, an attic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for bamboo, then the Kannada for a raised veranda.

You'll want to know: ಅಟ್ಟ.
ಅಟ್ಟ — aṭṭa — "a loft, an attic".

The word, taken apart.
DEDR 93 lists ಅಟ್ಟ (aṭṭa) beside Tamil aṭṭam, "terraced roof", but marks the Kannada word as one of the tatsamas, and notes that it is uncertain whether the word is Indo-Aryan or Dravidian in origin. Sanskrit has aṭṭa, "tower" (CDIAL 180), which CDIAL calls non-Aryan.

ಹಿಡಿಕೆ — hiḍike — "a handle".

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿಡಿಕೆ (hiḍike) is native Dravidian, from hiḍi, "to grasp", and DEDR 4148 lists piḍi, "a hold, a handle, a hilt", with the older p-; the entry also has Tamil piṭi, Telugu piḍi and Malayalam piṭi.
