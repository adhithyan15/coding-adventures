## You'll want to know: a condenação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C388-PENA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a condenação** — "the conviction; the condemnation".

> A condenação foi notícia em todos os jornais.
>
> *The conviction was news in every paper.*

*Condenar* — to convict, to condemn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condenação* is a learned borrowing from Latin *condemnātiō* "a condemning" (EtymDB), on *condemnāre* "to condemn", built on *damnum* "loss, damage" (Lewis & Short).
