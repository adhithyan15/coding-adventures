## You'll want to know: विस्फोटः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C315-VISPHOTA315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विस्फोटः** — *visphoṭaḥ* — "an explosion".

> प्रातः नगरे विस्फोटः अभवत्।
>
> *prātaḥ nagare visphoṭaḥ abhavat.*
>
> There was an explosion in the city in the morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विस्फोटः** (*visphoṭaḥ*) is built from *vi* and the root √sphuṭ, "to burst", as in *sphuṭati*; MW gives *visphoṭa* as cracking, crashing, and an explosion is its modern use (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
