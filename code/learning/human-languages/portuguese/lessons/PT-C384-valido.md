## You'll want to know: válido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C384-VALIDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**válido** — "valid".

> O meu passaporte ainda é válido.
>
> *My passport is still valid.*

*Válido até* — valid until.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*válido* is a learned borrowing from Latin *validus* "strong, healthy, in good order" (EtymDB), from *valēre* "to be strong" (Lewis & Short).
