## You'll want to know: चाटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C480-SPARSH480-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चाटना** — *cāṭnā* — "to lick".

> बच्चे ने उँगली से शहद चाटा।
>
> *bacce ne uṅglī se śahad cāṭā.*
>
> The child licked honey off his finger.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने शहद चाटा, *usne śahad cāṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चाटना** (*cāṭnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *cāṭnā*, "to lick, taste, eat", under *\*caṭṭ*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4573).
