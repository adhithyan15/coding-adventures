# ಪೋಷಿಸು (pōṣisu) — to nourish; to support

## You'll want to know: ಪೋಷಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C527-POSHANE527-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೋಷಿಸು** — *pōṣisu* — "to nourish; to support".

> ಅವನು ತನ್ನ ದುಡಿಮೆಯಿಂದ ಇಡೀ ಕುಟುಂಬವನ್ನು ಪೋಷಿಸುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *avanu tanna duḍimeyinda iḍī kuṭumbavannu pōṣisuttāne.*
>
> He supports the whole family with his earnings.

Past (I …): ಪೋಷಿಸಿದೆ, *pōṣiside*.

ಪೋಷಕರು, parents or guardians.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೋಷಿಸು** (*pōṣisu*) is built on Sanskrit *poṣaṇa*, "nourishing, cherishing" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
