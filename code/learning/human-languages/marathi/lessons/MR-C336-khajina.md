## You'll want to know: खजिना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C336-LENI336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खजिना** — *khajinā* — "a treasure".

> भुयारात राजाचा खजिना लपवला होता.
>
> *bhuyārāt rājācā khajinā lapavlā hotā.*
>
> The king's treasure had been hidden in the passage.

Masculine.

Also a treasury.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खजिना** (*khajinā*) is a loan from Persian *xazīna*, usually traced to Arabic *khazīna*, 'a treasury'.
