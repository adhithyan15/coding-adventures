# సామాజిక (sāmājika) — social

## You'll want to know: సామాజిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C408-SAMAJAM408-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సామాజిక** — *sāmājika* — "social".

> పేదరికం ఒక పెద్ద సామాజిక సమస్య.
>
> *pēdarikaṁ oka pedda sāmājika samasya.*
>
> Poverty is a big social problem.

It stands before a noun: సామాజిక సమస్య, a social problem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సామాజిక** (*sāmājika*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sāmājika*, "relating to an assembly" (Monier-Williams), from *samāja*, the word Telugu has as సమాజం.
