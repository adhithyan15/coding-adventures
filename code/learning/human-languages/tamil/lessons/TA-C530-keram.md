## You'll want to know: கேரம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C530-KUZHANTHAI530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கேரம்** — *kēram* — "carrom".

> மழைக்காலத்தில் நாங்கள் வீட்டில் கேரம் விளையாடுவோம்.
>
> *maḻaikkālattil nāṅkaḷ vīṭṭil kēram viḷaiyāṭuvōm.*
>
> In the rainy season we play carrom at home.

A board game: players flick a striker to knock pieces into the four corner pockets.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **கேரம்** (*kēram*) is not settled: it is the same word as English *carrom*, but which way the name travelled is unclear.
