## You'll want to know: సత్రం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C457-VASATI457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సత్రం** — *satraṁ* — "an inn; a rest house".

> పాత రోజుల్లో ప్రయాణికులు సత్రంలో ఉండేవారు.
>
> *pāta rōjullō prayāṇikulu satraṁlō uṇḍēvāru.*
>
> In the old days travellers used to stay at an inn.

A free or cheap rest house for travellers and pilgrims, often run by a trust.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సత్రం** (*satraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sattra*, first a long sacrificial session and later a house where food and shelter were given (Monier-Williams).
