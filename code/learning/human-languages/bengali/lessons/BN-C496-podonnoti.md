## You'll want to know: পদোন্নতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C496-PODONNOTI496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পদোন্নতি** — *pôdonnôti* — "a promotion".

> দুই বছর পরে আমার পদোন্নতি হল।
>
> *Dui bôchhôr pôre āmār pôdonnôti hôlô.*
>
> After two years I got a promotion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পদোন্নতি** (*pôdonnôti*) joins Sanskrit *pada*, "rank", and *unnati*, "rising, advancement" (Monier-Williams): a rise in rank.
