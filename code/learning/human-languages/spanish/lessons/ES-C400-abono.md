## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-ABONO]; assesses=[] -->

**el abono** — "the pass," "the season ticket," or "the subscription."

The dictionary keeps two nouns spelled **abono** apart. One belongs to
*abonar* in the sense of paying or improving soil, so it can mean a payment or
fertilizer. The other belongs to **abonarse**, "to subscribe." It names the
right a subscriber acquires and the document or set of tickets that proves
that right.

Travel uses the second word:

- **un abono de transporte** — a public-transport pass;
- **un abono mensual** — a monthly pass.

Context is doing real work. At a metro ticket machine, *abono* is not compost;
in a garden shop, it probably is. One spelling can hold two histories, and the
noun beside it tells you which one you have.
