## You'll want to know: atrasar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C416-ATRASOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**atrasar** — "to delay, to make late; to put back (a clock)".

> A neve atrasou todos os voos.
>
> *The snow delayed all the flights.*

*Atrasar-se* — to be late. In autumn people *atrasam o relógio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atrasar* is built on *atrás* 'behind' (from *a* and *trás*) with *-ar* (EtymDB); *trás* goes back to Latin *trāns* 'across, beyond'.
