Kannada, chapter 158: To boil, To cook, To buy, To pay, To bathe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬೇಯಿಸು (bēyisu) — to boil, to cook (in water or steam).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an advertisement, then the Kannada for a receipt.

You'll want to know: ಬೇಯಿಸು.
ಬೇಯಿಸು — bēyisu — "to boil, to cook (in water or steam)".

The word, taken apart.
ಬೇಯಿಸು (bēyisu) is native Dravidian, from bē, "to be cooked, be burnt", with -isu, "to make"; DEDR 5517 lists bēyisu itself; the entry also has Tamil vē.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಅಡುಗೆ ಮಾಡು (aḍuge māḍu) — to cook.

You'll want to know: ಅಡುಗೆ ಮಾಡು.
ಅಡುಗೆ ಮಾಡು — aḍuge māḍu — "to cook".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಡುಗೆ ಮಾಡು (aḍuge māḍu) is native Dravidian: aḍuge, "cooking", which DEDR 76 lists under aḍu, "to cook" (Tamil aṭu), and māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797). DEDR 76 compares Sanskrit aṭṭa.
