## You'll want to know: o granizo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C463-TROVOADA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o granizo** — "hail".

> O granizo partiu o vidro da estufa.
>
> *The hail broke the greenhouse glass.*

*Cair granizo* — to hail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*granizo* was borrowed from Spanish *granizo* (EtymDB), built on *grano* 'grain', from Latin *grānum*: hail falls like grains.
