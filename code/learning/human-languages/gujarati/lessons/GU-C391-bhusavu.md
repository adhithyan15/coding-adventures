# ભૂંસાવું (bhū̃sāvũ) — to get wiped out

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C391-GADBAD391-03, GU-LEX-C391-GADBAD391-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to shatter, then the Gujarati for to get scattered.

## You'll want to know: ભૂંસાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C391-GADBAD391-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભૂંસાવું** — *bhū̃sāvũ* — "to get wiped out".

> જો વીજળી જશે તો સ્ક્રીન પરનું બધું કામ ભૂંસાઈ જશે.
>
> *jo vījḷī jaśe to skrīn parnũ badhũ kām bhū̃sāī jaśe.*
>
> If the power goes, all the work on the screen will be wiped out.

Past (it …): તે પાટિયા પરનું નામ ભૂંસાયું, *te pāṭiyā parnũ nām bhū̃sāyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભૂંસાવું** (*bhū̃sāvũ*) is the *-āvũ* partner of **ભૂંસવું** (*bhū̃svũ*), "to rub out", whose own history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
