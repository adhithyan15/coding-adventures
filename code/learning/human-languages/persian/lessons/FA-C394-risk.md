## You'll want to know: ریسک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C394-RISK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ریسک** (*risk*) — "a risk".

> این کار ریسک زیادی دارد.
>
> *in kâr risk-e ziyâdi dârad.*
>
> This is very risky.

A European loan; *risk kardan* — to take a risk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ریسک** is a modern loan, usually traced to French *risque* "risk".
