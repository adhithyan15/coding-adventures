# പരിഭവം (paribhavaṁ) — a hurt feeling; a mild grievance

## You'll want to know: പരിഭവം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C525-VILAPAM525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിഭവം** — *paribhavaṁ* — "a hurt feeling; a mild grievance".

> പിറന്നാളിന് വിളിക്കാത്തതിൽ സുഹൃത്തിന് പരിഭവം ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *piṟannāḷinŭ viḷikkāttatil suhṛttinŭ paribhavaṁ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> My friend is hurt that I didn't call on his birthday.

A soft, hurt complaint between people close to each other, milder than ദേഷ്യം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിഭവം** (*paribhavaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paribhava*, "an insult, a humiliation" (Monier-Williams). In Malayalam it softened into the hurt one feels at being slighted.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
