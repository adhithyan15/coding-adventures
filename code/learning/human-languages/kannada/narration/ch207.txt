Kannada, chapter 207: To sniff, To hang up, To pound, To stack, To collect.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮೂಸು (mūsu) — to sniff.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to escape, then the Kannada for to hurry.

You'll want to know: ಮೂಸು.
ಮೂಸು — mūsu — "to sniff".

The word, taken apart.
ಮೂಸು (mūsu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil muka (DEDR 4886). DEDR lists mūsu itself, "to smell".

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ನೇತುಹಾಕು.
ನೇತುಹಾಕು — nētuhāku — "to hang up".

The word, taken apart.
ನೇತುಹಾಕು (nētuhāku) is nētu, "hanging", from the native nēl, "to be suspended, hang" (DEDR 2912), with hāku, "to put", whose history neither DEDR nor CDIAL gives: "to put hanging".

You'll want to know: ಕುಟ್ಟು.
ಕುಟ್ಟು — kuṭṭu — "to pound (in a mortar)".

The word, taken apart.
ಕುಟ್ಟು (kuṭṭu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kuṭṭu and Malayalam kuṭṭuka (DEDR 1671). DEDR lists kuṭṭu itself, "to beat, pound, bruise". DEDR 1671 compares Sanskrit kuṭṭayati.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪೇರಿಸು (pērisu) — to stack, to pile up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಪೇರಿಸು.
ಪೇರಿಸು — pērisu — "to stack, to pile up".

The word, taken apart.
ಪೇರಿಸು (pērisu) is native Dravidian, from pēru, "to load, pile up", which DEDR 4446 lists as Kannada pēṟu, with -isu; the entry also has Telugu pērcu and Malayalam pēṟu.
