Urdu, chapter 64: Leaf, Mountain, Forest, River, Lake.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پتا (pattā) — a leaf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a tree, then the Urdu for a flower.

You'll want to know: پتا.
پتا — pattā — "a leaf".
