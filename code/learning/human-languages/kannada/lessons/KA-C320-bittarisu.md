# ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸು (bittarisu) — to broadcast; to air

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C320-VARADI320-02, KA-LEX-C320-VARADI320-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to occur, then the Kannada for a broadcast.

## You'll want to know: ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C320-VARADI320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸು** — *bittarisu* — "to broadcast; to air".

> ಟೀವಿ ಆ ಸಂದರ್ಶನವನ್ನು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸಿತು.
>
> *ṭīvi ā sandarśanavannu rātri bittarisitu.*
>
> The television aired that interview at night.

Past (I …): ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸಿದೆ, *bittariside*.

What is aired takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಪ್ರಸಾರ ಮಾಡು says the same thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸು** (*bittarisu*) is built on ಬಿತ್ತರ, the Kannada form of Sanskrit *vistara*, "spreading out, extent" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*. Sanskrit *v* often turns into Kannada *b*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
