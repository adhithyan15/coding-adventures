## You'll want to know: अनुस्मरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C324-PURA324-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुस्मरति** — *anusmarati* — "calls to mind; recollects".

> जनाः वीरस्य शौर्यम् अनुस्मरन्ति।
>
> *janāḥ vīrasya śauryam anusmaranti.*
>
> People call to mind the hero's valour.

Past (he …): सः पितामहम् अनुस्मृतवान्, *saḥ pitāmaham anusmṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुस्मरति** (*anusmarati*) is built from *anu*, "after", and the root √smṛ, "to remember", as in *smarati*; MW gives *anu-smṛ* as to remember, recollect (MW).
