## You'll want to know: il perdono
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C480-RANCORE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il perdono** — "forgiveness".

> Ti chiedo perdono.
>
> *I ask your forgiveness.*

The noun for *perdonare* "to forgive".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perdono* comes from *perdonare* "to forgive", usually traced to Late Latin *perdōnāre*, built on Latin *dōnāre* "to give" (Lewis & Short).
