Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮਹਿਮਾਨ (mahimān) — a guest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a girl, then the Punjabi for a man.

You'll want to know: ਮਹਿਮਾਨ.
ਮਹਿਮਾਨ — mahimān — "a guest".

The word, taken apart.
ਮਹਿਮਾਨ (mahimān) is a loan, usually traced to Persian mihmān, "guest".

The word, taken apart.
ਸੁਣਨਾ (suṇnā) is inherited. CDIAL lists no Punjabi form, but gives the matching Lahnda suṇaṇ and Hindi sunnā under Sanskrit śṛṇoti, "hears" (CDIAL 12598).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਬੈਠਣਾ (baiṭhṇā) — to sit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to drink, then the Punjabi for to hear.

You'll want to know: ਬੈਠਣਾ.
ਬੈਠਣਾ — baiṭhṇā — "to sit".

The word, taken apart.
ਬੈਠਣਾ (baiṭhṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from upaviśati, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245). The -ṭh- goes back to its past form upaviṣṭa, "seated".
