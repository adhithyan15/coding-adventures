## You'll want to know: कर्फ़्यू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C316-UPADRAV316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्फ़्यू** — *karfyū* — "a curfew".

> मेरी राय में कर्फ़्यू ज़रूरी था।
>
> *merī rāy meṁ karfyū zarūrī thā.*
>
> In my opinion the curfew was necessary.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्फ़्यू** (*karfyū*) is borrowed from English *curfew*, taken into Hindi as a masculine noun. A curfew **लगता है**, is imposed, and **हटता है**, is lifted.
