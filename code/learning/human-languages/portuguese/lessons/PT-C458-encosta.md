## You'll want to know: a encosta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C458-SERRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a encosta** — "the hillside, the slope".

> Há vinhas na encosta do rio.
>
> *There are vineyards on the slope above the river.*

*Encosta abaixo* — downhill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*encosta* is built on *encostar* 'to lean against' (EtymDB), on *costa* 'side, rib', from Latin *costa* 'rib' (Lewis & Short).
