# बारगळणे (bārgaḷṇe) — to fizzle out

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C551-YADI551-05, MR-LEX-C552-BIGHADNE552-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Marathi for unforeseen, then the Marathi for to fall apart.

## You'll want to know: बारगळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C552-BIGHADNE552-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बारगळणे** — *bārgaḷṇe* — "to fizzle out".

> पैसे नसल्यामुळे नवीन दुकानाची योजना बारगळली.
>
> *paise naslyāmuḷe navīn dukānācī yojnā bārgaḷlī.*
>
> For lack of money the plan for a new shop fizzled out.

Past (he …): तो प्रयत्न बारगळला, *to prayatna bārgaḷlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बारगळणे** (*bārgaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb for a plan that comes to nothing; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
