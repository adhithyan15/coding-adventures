## You'll want to know: ਸਲਾਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C281-PLAN281-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਲਾਹ** — *salāh* — "advice; counsel".

> ਪਿਤਾ ਜੀ ਚੰਗੀ ਸਲਾਹ ਦਿੰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *pitā jī chaṅgī salāh dinde han.*
>
> Father gives good advice.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਲਾਹ** (*salāh*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *salāh*, "counsel", itself from Arabic.
