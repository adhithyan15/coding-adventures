# વણસવું (vaṇasvũ) — to worsen

## You'll want to know: વણસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C377-DASHA377-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વણસવું** — *vaṇasvũ* — "to worsen".

> જો સારવાર મોડી થશે તો દર્દીની તબિયત વણસશે.
>
> *jo sārvār moḍī thaśe to dardīnī tabiyat vaṇasśe.*
>
> If treatment is delayed, the patient's health will worsen.

Past (it …): તે દર્દીની તબિયત વણસી, *te dardīnī tabiyat vaṇsī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વણસવું** (*vaṇasvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vínaśyati*, "perishes"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be spoilt" (CDIAL 11770).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
