## You'll want to know: रवाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C400-AARAKSHAN400-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रवाना** — *ravānā* — "setting off; on its way".

> ट्रेन सुबह छह बजे रवाना हुई।
>
> *ṭren subah chah baje ravānā huī.*
>
> The train set off at six in the morning.

रवाना होना, to set off; रवाना करना, to send off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रवाना** (*ravānā*) is usually traced to Persian *ravāna*, "going, flowing, setting out". It does not change for gender: ट्रेन रवाना हुई, the train left.
