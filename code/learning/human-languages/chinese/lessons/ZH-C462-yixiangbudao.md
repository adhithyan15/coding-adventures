## You'll want to know: 意想不到
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C462-SURPRISE462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**意想不到** — *yìxiǎngbúdào* — "unexpected; beyond what anyone imagined".

> **这真是一个意想不到的好方法** *zhè zhēn shì yí ge yìxiǎngbúdào de hǎo fāngfǎ* — That really is an unexpectedly good way of doing it.

不 is said with a rising tone before the falling tone of dào: bú dào.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**意想不到** (*yìxiǎngbúdào*) is built from 意 'the mind', 想 'to think', 不 'not' and 到 'to reach', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *'iH*, *sjangX*, *pjut* and *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): what the mind's thinking does not reach.
