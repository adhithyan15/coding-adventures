## You'll want to know: il molo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C421-PORTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il molo** — "the pier, the jetty (of stone)".

> Le barche sono legate al molo.
>
> *The boats are tied to the pier.*

*Il molo* is built of stone and shelters the harbour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*molo* is inherited from Latin *mōlēs* 'mass, heap, rock' (EtymDB), which could also mean a massive stone structure such as a dam or a pier (Lewis & Short).
