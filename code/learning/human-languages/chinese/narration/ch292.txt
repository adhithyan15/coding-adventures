Mandarin Chinese, chapter 292: On the Radio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
guǎngbō — to broadcast; a broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to break out suddenly, then the Mandarin for to announce officially.

You'll want to know: guǎngbō.
guǎngbō — "to broadcast; a broadcast".
diàntái měitiān zǎoshang guǎngbō xīnwén — The station broadcasts the news every morning.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
guǎngbō is built from guǎng 'wide' and bō 'to scatter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwangX and paH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'big' and 'broad' and the second 'to spread' and 'to sow': to spread far and wide. The level tone of bō is irregular for paH.

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
fābiǎo — to publish an article; to make public.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: fābiǎo.
fābiǎo — "to publish an article; to make public".
tā fābiǎo le yì piān xiǎoshuō — He published a short story.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
fābiǎo is built from 发 'to send out' and biǎo 'the outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjot and pjewX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to make clear' and 'a letter to the throne': to send out where all can see.
