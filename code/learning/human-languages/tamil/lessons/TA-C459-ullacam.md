# உல்லாசம் (ullācam) — merriment; carefree fun

## You'll want to know: உல்லாசம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C459-MAKIZHCCI459-05]; assesses=[] -->

**உல்லாசம்** — *ullācam* — "merriment; carefree fun".

> கடற்கரையில் நாங்கள் உல்லாசமாக நேரம் கழித்தோம்.
>
> *kaṭaṟkaraiyil nāṅkaḷ ullācamāka nēram kaḻittōm.*
>
> We spent a carefree time at the beach.

உல்லாசப் பயணம், a pleasure trip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உல்லாசம்** (*ullācam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ullāsa*, "brightness; coming forth" (Monier-Williams); Tamil uses it for carefree enjoyment.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
