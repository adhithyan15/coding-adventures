## You'll want to know: منعطف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C434-RUKHSA434-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منعطف** — *munʿaṭaf* — "a bend; a turning".

> كان المنعطف على طريق الجبل خطيرا.
>
> *kāna al-munʿaṭaf ʿalā ṭarīq al-jabal khaṭīran.*
>
> The bend on the mountain road was dangerous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منعطف** (*munʿaṭaf*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṭ-f*, "to bend, to turn" (Lane). It is the place-noun of *inʿaṭafa*, "to turn".
