## You'll want to know: der Fensterplatz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C418-BORD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Fensterplatz** — "the window seat".

> Mein Sohn möchte immer einen Fensterplatz.
>
> *My son always wants a window seat.*

Plural: *die Fensterplätze*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fensterplatz* is built from *Fenster* 'window', an early borrowing from Latin *fenestra* (Lewis & Short), and *Platz* 'place', which came through Old French *place* from Latin *platea* 'broad street' (EtymDB).
