# હાલ (hāl) — at present

## You'll want to know: હાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C316-MUJAB316-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હાલ** — *hāl* — "at present".

> હાલ તે દવાખાનામાં છે.
>
> *hāl te davākhānāmā̃ che.*
>
> At present he is in hospital.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હાલ** (*hāl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥāl*, "a state, the present", the word behind **હાલત**. It is the news word for "now": **હાલ તે સલામત છે**.
