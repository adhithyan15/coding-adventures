## You'll want to know: জালিয়াতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C341-ONYAY341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**জালিয়াতি** — *jāliyāti* — "forgery; fraud".

> পুলিশ একটা বড় জালিয়াতি ধরল।
>
> *pulish ekṭā bôṛo jāliyāti dhôrlô.*
>
> The police uncovered a big fraud.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জালিয়াতি** (*jāliyāti*) is built on জাল, 'counterfeit', usually traced to Arabic *jaʿl*, fabrication, through Persian; it is a different word from জাল 'a net'. The -ইয়াতি ending makes the act.
