## You'll want to know: પોટલું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C394-PORTER394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પોટલું** — *poṭlũ* — "a bundle (tied in cloth)".

> તેણે કપડાંનું પોટલું માથા પર મૂક્યું.
>
> *teṇe kapḍā̃nũ poṭlũ māthā par mūkyũ.*
>
> He put the bundle of clothes on his head.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પોટલું** (*poṭlũ*) goes back to *\*pōṭṭa*, "a bundle", a form CDIAL reconstructs beside Sanskrit *pōṭṭala*; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8396).
