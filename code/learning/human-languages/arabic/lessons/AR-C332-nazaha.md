## You'll want to know: نزح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C332-HIJRA332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نزح** — *nazaḥa* — "to be displaced, to flee one’s home".

> نزح كثير من الناس بعد الفيضان.
>
> *nazaḥa kathīr min an-nās baʿda al-fayaḍān.*
>
> Many people left their homes after the flood.

Present: ينزح, *yanzaḥ*.

A displaced person is نازح.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نزح** (*nazaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-z-ḥ* (Lane), "to become far away", and "to draw all the water from a well". People who must go far from home are *nāziḥūn*.
