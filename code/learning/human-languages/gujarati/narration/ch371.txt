Gujarati, chapter 371: Gains and Losses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ફાયદો (phāydo) — a benefit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to spoil, then the Gujarati for to put up with.

You'll want to know: ફાયદો.
ફાયદો — phāydo — "a benefit".
જો તમે વહેલા ટિકિટ ખરીદશો તો ફાયદો થશે. jo tame vahelā ṭikiṭ kharīdśo to phāydo thaśe. If you buy the tickets early, you will save.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફાયદો (phāydo) is usually traced to Arabic fāʾida, "a benefit, a profit", through Persian.

You'll want to know: કરજ.
કરજ — karaj — "a debt".
જો તમે વધારે કરજ લેશો તો વ્યાજ પણ વધારે ભરવું પડશે. jo tame vadhāre karaj leśo to vyāj paṇ vadhāre bharvũ paḍśe. If you take on more debt, you will have to pay more interest too.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખોવું (khovũ) — to lose for good.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for use, then the Gujarati for a debt.

You'll want to know: ખોવું.
ખોવું — khovũ — "to lose for good".
જો તમે આ તક ખોશો તો ફરી તક નહીં આવે. jo tame ā tak khośo to pharī tak nahī̃ āve. If you lose this chance, it will not come again.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પોતાનું પાકીટ ખોયું, teṇe potānũ pākīṭ khoyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ખોવું (khovũ) is inherited from Sanskrit kṣapayati, "destroys"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to lose, to waste" (CDIAL 3651). ખોવાવું (khovāvũ), "to get lost", is its partner.
