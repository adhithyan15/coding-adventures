## You'll want to know: kāichú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C490-PRESSURE490-05]; assesses=[] -->

*kāichú* — "to fire; to dismiss (from a job or school)".

> *tā chángcháng chídào suǒyǐ lǎobǎn kāichú le tā* — He was often late, so the boss fired him.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kāichú* is built from 开 'to open' and *chú* 'to remove', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *drjo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to release' and the second 'to take away'.
