## You'll want to know: трибуна
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C508-FANS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**трибуна** (*tribúna*) — "a stand (in a stadium)".

> Все трибуны были полны.
>
> *Vse tribúny býli polný.*
>
> All the stands were full.

Feminine. Also a speaker's platform.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*трибуна* goes back to Latin *tribūnus* 'a tribune' (EtymDB), usually through Italian *tribuna*, a raised platform.
