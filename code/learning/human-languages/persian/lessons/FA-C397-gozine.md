## You'll want to know: گزینه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C397-EHTIYAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گزینه** (*gozine*) — "an option, a choice".

> ما دو گزینه داریم.
>
> *mâ do gozine dârim.*
>
> We have two options.

From *gozin-*, the present stem of *gozidan* "to choose".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گزینه** is a modern native word, built on *gozin-*, the present stem of *gozidan* "to choose": what you can choose.
