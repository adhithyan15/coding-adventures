## You'll want to know: enojado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C707-ENFADO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**enojado** — "cross, angry".

> ¿Por qué estás enojada?
>
> *Why are you cross?*

The everyday word in Latin America; in Spain people mostly say *enfadado*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enojado* is built on *enojar*, which is usually traced to spoken Latin *inodiāre*, from the phrase *in odiō* "in hatred"; *odio* comes from the same Latin word.
