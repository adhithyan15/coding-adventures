# 女 — female, woman

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-REN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-NU-01, ZH-LEX-HAO] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **person**, then write the three-stroke left half of 好.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-NU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **女** — *nǚ* — **female; woman**

Say it low: *nǚ*. The rounded vowel is the same written **ü** you met in the
character lesson. On its own, 女 labels female gender; it is not a polite way to
call out to a woman. Here you are learning the word for reading and building.
