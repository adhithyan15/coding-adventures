## You'll want to know: زیستگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C472-TABIAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**زیستگاه** (*zistgâh*) — "a habitat".

> این تالاب زیستگاه پرنده های زیادی است.
>
> *In tâlâb zistgâh-e parande-hâ-ye ziyâdi ast.*
>
> This wetland is the habitat of many birds.

*zist* 'living' + *gâh* 'place'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zistgâh* is a native compound of *zist*, the past stem of *zistan* 'to live', an inherited verb usually traced to Middle Persian *zīstan*, and *gâh* 'place'.
