## You'll want to know: ていあんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C317-SANSEI317-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ていあんする** — *teian suru* — "to propose; to suggest a plan".

> **やすいので、この ほうほうを ていあんします。** — *yasui node, kono hōhō o teian shimasu* — "It is cheap, so I propose this method."

Polite **ていあんします**, *teianshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **じょうしが ていあんしました。**, *jōshi ga teian shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ていあんする** (*teian suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *teian*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *tei*, "propose" and *an*, "plan" (Middle Chinese *'anH*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
