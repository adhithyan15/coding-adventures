Arabic, chapter 381: Social problems.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
فقر (faqr) — poverty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for fair, then the Arabic for to deserve.

You'll want to know: فقر.
فقر — faqr — "poverty".
يعيش كثير من الناس في فقر. yaʿīshu kathīr min an-nās fī faqr. Many people live in poverty.

The word, taken apart.
فقر (faqr) is built on the native Arabic root f-q-r, "to be needy, poor" (Lane). Faqr is the verbal noun: poverty. فقير, poor, is from the same root.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

تحدثت الجريدة عن التمييز في العمل. taḥaddathat al-jarīda ʿan at-tamyīz fī al-ʿamal. The newspaper wrote about discrimination at work.

تمييز (tamyīz) is built on the native Arabic root m-y-z, "to separate, to set apart" (Lane). Tamyīz is the Form II verbal noun, setting apart: telling apart, and treating people apart, discrimination.

Lesson 5 of 6.
عانى (ʿānā) — to suffer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for discrimination, then the Arabic for violence.

You'll want to know: عانى.
عانى — ʿānā — "to suffer".
يعاني جدي من ألم في الظهر. yuʿānī jaddī min alam fī aẓ-ẓahr. My grandfather suffers from back pain.
Present: يعاني, yuʿānī.
It takes من: عانى (ʿānā) من ….

The word, taken apart.
عانى (ʿānā) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-n-y, "to concern; to toil" (Lane). It is Form III, to wrestle with a hardship: to suffer.
