## You'll want to know: غیرقانونی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C405-QANUNI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**غیرقانونی** (*gheyr-e qânuni*) — "illegal".

> فروش این دارو غیرقانونی است.
>
> *forush-e in dâru gheyr-e qânuni ast.*
>
> Selling this medicine is illegal.

*Gheyr* "non-, other than" + *qânuni*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غیرقانونی** joins *gheyr*, from Arabic *ghayr* "other than" (EtymDB), to *qânuni* "legal", from *qânun* "law" (Arabic *qānūn*, from Greek *kanōn*).
