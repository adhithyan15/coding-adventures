## You'll want to know: بالخصوص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C542-KHAIR542-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بالخصوص** — *bilkhusūs* — "especially; in particular".

> اگر سفر لمبا ہے تو بالخصوص پانی ساتھ رکھیے۔
>
> *agar safar lambā hai to bilkhusūs pānī sāth rakhiye.*
>
> If it's a long journey, be sure to take water with you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بالخصوص** (*bilkhusūs*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bi-l-khuṣūṣ*, "in particular".
