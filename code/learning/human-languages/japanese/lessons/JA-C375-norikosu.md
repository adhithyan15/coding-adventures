## You'll want to know: のりこす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C375-SHUUDEN375-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のりこす** — *norikosu* — "to ride past one's stop".

> **でんしゃで しんぶんを よんで、えきを のりこしました。** — *densha de shinbun o yonde, eki o norikoshimashita* — "I read the newspaper on the train and rode past my station."

Polite **のりこします**, *norikoshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが えきを のりこしました。**, *ani ga eki o norikoshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のりこす** (*norikosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *nori*, "riding" (from *noru*) and *kosu*, "to cross over"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
