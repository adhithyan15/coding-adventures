Tamil, chapter 95: Exactly, Really, Nothing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சரியாக (cariyāka) — exactly, correctly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for one more time, then the Tamil for what did you say.

You'll want to know: சரியாக.
சரியாக (cariyāka) — "exactly, correctly".
It is the சரி of "okay", with -ஆக. சரியாகச் சொன்னீர்கள் — "you said it exactly".

What you've built.
Okay, made into exactly.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: cariyāka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cariyāka, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say cariyāka!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for one more time, then the Tamil for what did you say, then say cariyāka again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does சரியாக (cariyāka) mean? ("Exactly, correctly".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
உண்மையா? (uṇmaiyā?) — really? is it true?

You'll want to know: உண்மையா?
உண்மையா? (uṇmaiyā?) — "really?", literally "is it the truth?".
உண்மை is "truth", and -ஆ makes the question.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

Lesson 5 of 5.
அதே (atē) — the same, that very one.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for nothing, then the Tamil for not yet.

You'll want to know: அதே.
அதே (atē) — "the same", "that very one".
It is அது, "that", with -ஏ, "exactly". When someone names what you meant: அதே (atē)!

What you've built.
