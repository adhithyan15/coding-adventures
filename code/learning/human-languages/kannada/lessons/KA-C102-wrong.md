# ತಪ್ಪು (tappu) — wrong; a mistake

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C101-OPEN-05, KA-LEX-C102-THISWAY-01] -->

## You'll want to know: ತಪ್ಪು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C102-THISWAY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಪ್ಪು** (*tappu*) — "wrong", and also "a mistake".

**ತಪ್ಪು ಆಯ್ತು** (*tappu āytu*) — "I made a mistake", literally "a mistake happened".
It is the honest partner of **ಸರಿ** (*sari*), "right".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The opposite of sari.
