# ধড়ফড় করা (dhôṛphôṛ kôrā) — to pound; to flutter (of the heart)

## You'll want to know: ধড়ফড় করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C441-ATONKO441-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ধড়ফড় করা** — *dhôṛphôṛ kôrā* — "to pound; to flutter (of the heart)".

> ভয়ে আমার বুক ধড়ফড় করছিল।
>
> *bhôye āmār buk dhôṛphôṛ kôrchhilô.*
>
> My heart was pounding with fear.

Past (he/she …): সেই রোগীর বুক ধড়ফড় করল, *sei rogīr buk dhôṛphôṛ kôrlô*.

The heart is বুক (taught), the chest: বুক ধড়ফড় করা.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধড়ফড় করা** (*dhôṛphôṛ kôrā*) is *dhôṛphôṛ*, an imitative word for a quick, fluttering beat, with *kôrā*, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
