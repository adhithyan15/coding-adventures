Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಾಮಾನು (sāmānu) — luggage, belongings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a handkerchief, then the Kannada for a necklace.

You'll want to know: ಸಾಮಾನು.
ಸಾಮಾನು — sāmānu — "luggage, belongings".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಮಾನು (sāmānu) is Persian sāmān, "goods, equipment", which came into Kannada through Hindustani, with a final -u added.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕೀಲಿ (kīli) — a key.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a necklace, then the Kannada for luggage.

You'll want to know: ಕೀಲಿ.
ಕೀಲಿ — kīli — "a key".

The word, taken apart.
ಕೀಲಿ (kīli) is probably the same word as kīlu, "a pin, a bolt", which DEDR lists among Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 26): it goes back to Sanskrit kīla, "a stake, a bolt" (CDIAL 3202). CDIAL 3202 adds that the Sanskrit word itself may be of non-Aryan origin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ (patrike) — a newspaper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for luggage, then the Kannada for a key.

You'll want to know: ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ.
ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ — patrike — "a newspaper".

The word, taken apart.
ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ (patrike) is Sanskrit patrikā, "a leaf, a letter", which CDIAL 7733 spells pattrikā, with the Kannada -e for final -ā: a newspaper is "a sheet".
