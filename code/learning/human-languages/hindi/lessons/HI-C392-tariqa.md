## You'll want to know: तरीक़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C392-TAIYARI392-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तरीक़ा** — *tarīqā* — "a method, a way (of doing)".

> अगर यह तरीक़ा काम नहीं करेगा तो हम कोई दूसरा तरीक़ा आज़माएँगे।
>
> *agar yah tarīqā kām nahīṃ karegā to ham koī dūsrā tarīqā āzmāeṅge.*
>
> If this method doesn't work, we'll try another one.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तरीक़ा** (*tarīqā*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṭarīqa*, "a way, a path", which reached Hindi through Persian.
