# ಬಂದ ಕಾರಣ (banda kāraṇa) — giving the reason

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C115-WHY115-01, KA-LEX-C115-WHY115-04, KA-GRAMMAR-C312-OBLIGATIVE-01, KA-LEX-C48-ROLE-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a reason" (*kāraṇa*) and "because" (*yākendare*). Then "You should see a doctor." (*nīvu vaidyarannu nōḍabēku*.)

## You'll want to know: the reason first, with ಕಾರಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C312-KARANA-CLAUSE-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C72-WANT-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-AFTER-DOING-01, KA-LEX-C63-HEALTH-03, KA-LEX-C103-HOME-01] -->

> **ಮಳೆ ಬಂದ ಕಾರಣ ನಾನು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ ಇದ್ದೆ.**
>
> *maḷe banda kāraṇa nānu maneyallē idde.* — "Because it rained, I stayed at home."

**ಬಂದ** is the past without its ending, the piece inside **ಬಂದಾಗ** and **ಮಾಡಿದ ನಂತರ**. With **ಕಾರಣ**, "reason", after it, it reads "(for) the reason (that) it came". Any verb fits:

> **ಅವನಿಗೆ ಜ್ವರ ಇದ್ದ ಕಾರಣ ಅವನು ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ.**
>
> *avanige jvara idda kāraṇa avanu baralilla.* — "Because he had a fever, he did not come."

**ಅವನಿಗೆ** (*avanige*, "to him") is **ಅವನು** in the "to" shape, as **ನನಗೆ** is **ನಾನು**.

## You'll want to know: the reason after, with ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C312-REASON-ORDER-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C115-WHY115-04, KA-LEX-C70-TELL-04, KA-LEX-C106-SOON-02, KA-LEX-C69-ASK-05, KA-LEX-C284-KALA284-03] -->

> **ನಾನು ಇವತ್ತು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ ಇರುತ್ತೇನೆ. ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ ನನಗೆ ಜ್ವರ ಇದೆ.**
>
> *nānu ivattu maneyallē iruttēne. yākendare nanage jvara ide.* — "I'll stay at home today, because I have a fever."

**ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ** opens a second sentence, and that sentence is the reason. In writing you will meet its twin, **ಏಕೆಂದರೆ**. So there are two orders, and both are good Kannada: the reason first, ending in **-ಅ ಕಾರಣ**, then the result; or the result first, then **ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ** and the reason.

When someone asks you why, answer in either order:

> **ನೀನು ನಿನ್ನೆ ಯಾಕೆ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ?**
>
> *nīnu ninne yāke baralilla?* — "Why didn't you come yesterday?"
