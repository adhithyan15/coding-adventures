# Practice — the table, and asking for some of it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Three nouns and one rule. Run the nouns first, then put the rule on
each of them in turn.

## Guided Practice: the three, with their articles
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-LEX-EAU-03, FR-LEX-VIN-05] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "le pain, l'eau, le vin"]
- [YOU SAY: the odd one — "l'eau elides, because it opens with a vowel"]
- [YOU SAY: the near-pair — "le pain, le vin"; the same nasal, one consonant apart]

[REPEAT x2] "le pain, l'eau, le vin"

## Guided Practice: now ask for some
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-DU-PARTITIF-07, FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-LEX-EAU-03, FR-LEX-VIN-05] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "du pain, du vin, de l'eau"]
- [YOU SAY: each noun three ways — "le pain, un pain, du pain"]
- [YOU SAY: the same three ways for wine — "le vin, un vin, du vin"]

## Guided Practice: two words that wore differently
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-ETYMON-AQUA-EAU-04, FR-ETYMON-VINUM-VIN-06, FR-ETYMON-PANIS-COMPAGNON-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the erosion — "aqua, ewe, eaue, eau"]
- [YOU SAY: the survival — "vīnum: wine, vine, vinegar"]
- [YOU SAY: the sum — "com plus pānis: companion"]

## What you've built this chapter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-DU-PARTITIF-07, FR-ETYMON-PANIS-COMPAGNON-02] -->

- **three nouns** you can put on a table, each with the right article.
- **one rule for "some"** — *de* plus the article, contracting to **du** where it
  can. It is the first time French has asked you to say something English simply
  leaves out.
- **two opposite fates for one kind of word.** *aqua* dissolved into *eau*;
  *vīnum* is still legible in four English words. Everyday things wear down,
  traded things stay legible — which is a rule you can use on words you have not
  met yet.
- **a root that paid twice** — *pānis* gave French its bread and English its
  **companions**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-LEX-EAU-03, FR-LEX-VIN-05, FR-GRAM-DU-PARTITIF-07, FR-ETYMON-AQUA-EAU-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Give all three with their articles. (**le pain, l'eau, le vin**.) Why
does only one of them elide? (**eau** begins with a **vowel sound**.) Ask for
some of each. (**du pain, du vin, de l'eau**.) What two words are inside **du**?
(**de** + **le**.) Which Latin word wore down to a single vowel, and which one
barely moved? (**aqua** → *eau*; **vīnum** → *vin*.)
