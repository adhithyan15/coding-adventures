## You'll want to know: انهار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C316-KARITHA316-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انهار** — *inhāra* — "to collapse".

> انهار البيت القديم بعد المطر.
>
> *inhāra al-bayt al-qadīm baʿda al-maṭar.*
>
> The old house collapsed after the rain.

Present: ينهار, *yanhār*.

Of a person: انهار بعد الخبر, he broke down at the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انهار** (*inhāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-w-r* (Lane), said of a building or a bank of earth that falls down. It is Form VII, "to tumble down".
