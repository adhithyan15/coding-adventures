## You'll want to know: venditor, venditōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C420-AUCTIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**venditor, venditōris** — "a seller".

> Venditor equum laudābat.
>
> *The seller was praising the horse.*

*venditor, venditōris* m., built on *vendō*, to sell. English *vendor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*venditor* is built on *vendō* "to sell" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from its supine *venditum*. English *vendor* comes from the same verb.
