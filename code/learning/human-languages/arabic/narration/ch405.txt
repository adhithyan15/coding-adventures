Arabic, chapter 405: Exceptions.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
استثناء (istithnāʾ) — an exception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for as if, then the Arabic for to ensure.

You'll want to know: استثناء.
استثناء — istithnāʾ — "an exception".
لكل قاعدة استثناء. li-kulli qāʿida istithnāʾ. Every rule has an exception.

The word, taken apart.
استثناء (istithnāʾ) is built on the native Arabic root th-n-y, "to fold, to bend back" (Lane). Istithnāʾ is the Form X verbal noun, bending a thing back out of the rest: an exception.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 6.
تعلق (taʿallaqa) — to concern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to include, then the Arabic for to contain.

You'll want to know: تعلق.
تعلق — taʿallaqa — "to concern".
يتعلق الأمر بصحة أبي. yataʿallaqu al-amr bi-ṣiḥḥat abī. The matter concerns my father's health.
Present: يتعلق, yataʿallaq.
It takes بـ: يتعلق بـ …, it is about ….

The word, taken apart.
تعلق (taʿallaqa) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-l-q, "to hang, to cling" (Lane). It is Form V, to hang on a thing: to be attached to it, to concern it. علق, to hang, is from the same root.
