# कलंडणे (kalaṇḍṇe) — to tip over

## You'll want to know: कलंडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C403-KHADDA403-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कलंडणे** — *kalaṇḍṇe* — "to tip over".

> वळणावर भाजीची गाडी कलंडली.
>
> *vaḷaṇāvar bhājīcī gāḍī kalaṇḍlī.*
>
> The vegetable cart tipped over at the bend.

Past (he …): तो कलंडला, *to kalaṇḍlā*.

It is something heavy leaning until it falls on its side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कलंडणे** (*kalaṇḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb for a load or vehicle tipping onto its side; its origin is not settled.
