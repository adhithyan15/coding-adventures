## You'll want to know: 还价
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C524-PRICE524-04]; assesses=[] -->

**还价** — *huánjià* — "to offer a lower price back; to make a counter-offer".

> **在这儿买东西最好还价** *zài zhèr mǎi dōngxi zuìhǎo huánjià* — When you buy things here, it's best to offer less.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**还价** (*huánjià*) is built from 还 huán 'to give back' and 价 'a price', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwaen* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to return': a price handed back.
