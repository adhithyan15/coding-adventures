Persian, chapter 515: Moves in Sport.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شوت کردن (shut kardan) — to shoot (the ball).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a racket, then the Persian for a goalkeeper.

You'll want to know: شوت کردن.
شوت کردن (shut kardan) — "to shoot (the ball)".
او از وسط زمین شوت کرد. u az vasat-e zamin shut kard. He shot from the middle of the pitch.
shut 'a shot' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
shut was borrowed from English shoot (EtymDB). kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پرتاب کردن (partâb kardan) — to hurl, to throw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to shoot, then the Persian for to pass.

You'll want to know: پرتاب کردن.
پرتاب کردن (partâb kardan) — "to hurl, to throw".
او توپ را تا آن طرف زمین پرتاب کرد. u tup râ tâ ân taraf-e zamin partâb kard. He threw the ball to the other end of the pitch.
partâb 'a throw' + kardan. Stronger than andâkhtan.

The word, taken apart.
partâb is usually taken as a native Persian word for a throw, but EtymDB lists it without a source, and its origin is not settled. kardan is inherited.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pass, then the Persian for to hurl.

You'll want to know: کشتی گرفتن.
کشتی گرفتن (koshti gereftan) — "to wrestle".
پسرها در باشگاه کشتی میگیرند. pesarhâ dar bâshgâh koshti migirand. The boys wrestle at the club.
koshti 'wrestling' + gereftan; present stem gir-. Spelled like kashti 'ship'.

The word, taken apart.
koshti, 'wrestling', is spelled like kashti, 'ship', but is a different word, and no source used here gives its origin, which is not settled. gereftan is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
وزنه برداری (vazne bardâri) — weightlifting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to hurl, then the Persian for to wrestle.

You'll want to know: وزنه برداری.
وزنه برداری (vazne bardâri) — "weightlifting".
او هر روز وزنه برداری تمرین میکند. u har ruz vazne bardâri tamrin mikonad. He practises weightlifting every day.
vazne 'a weight' + bardâri 'lifting' (from bardâshtan).

The word, taken apart.
vazne bardâri is built in Persian on vazne, 'a weight', from vazn, a loan from Arabic wazn, 'weight', and bardâri, from native bardâshtan, 'to lift'.
