## You'll want to know: féng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C521-CHORES521-02]; assesses=[] -->

*féng* — "to sew; to stitch".

> *māma bāng wǒ féng kùzi féng le bàntiān* — Mum sewed up my trousers for me, and it took her ages.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*féng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *bjowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to stitch'; its departing-tone reading, *bjowngH*, is glossed 'a seam'.
