## You'll want to know: il cenone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C321-PRESEPE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il cenone** — "the big festive dinner".

> Il cenone di fine anno è a casa nostra.
>
> *The big New Year's Eve dinner is at our house.*

The ending *-one* makes things big: *cena*, *cenone*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cenone* is *cena* "dinner" with *-one* "big"; *cena* is inherited from Latin *cēna* (Lewis & Short).
