## You'll want to know: सांकळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C176-GANTH176-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सांकळ** — *sā̃kaḷ* — "a door chain".

> किवाड़ माथै सांकळ है।
>
> *kivāṛ māthai sā̃kaḷ hai.*
>
> There's a chain on the door.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सांकळ** (*sā̃kaḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śṛṅkhala*, "a chain"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *sā̃kaḷ* and Hindi *sā̃kal* (CDIAL 12580). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
