## You'll want to know: yùxí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C249-STUDY249-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yùxí* — "to prepare a lesson (before class)".

> *míngtiān de kè nǐ yùxí le ma* — Have you prepared tomorrow's lesson?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùxí* is built from *yù* 'beforehand' and *xí* 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yoH* and *zip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, 'before', and the second 'to learn'.
