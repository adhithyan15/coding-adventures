## You'll want to know: けっさく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C468-WORKS468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**けっさく** — *kessaku* — "a masterpiece".

> **これは かれの けっさくです。** — *kore wa kare no kessaku desu* — "This is his masterpiece."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けっさく** (*kessaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ke*, "excellence" (Middle Chinese *gjet*) and *saku*, "make" (*tsak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
