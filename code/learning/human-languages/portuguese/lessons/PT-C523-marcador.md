## You'll want to know: o marcador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C523-LIVROS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o marcador** — "bookmark; marker pen".

> Perdi o marcador e não sei em que página ia.
>
> *I lost my bookmark and do not know what page I was on.*

Also a felt-tip marker, and in football a goalscorer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marcador* is built on *marcar* "to mark", which is usually traced to a Germanic word for a boundary sign.
