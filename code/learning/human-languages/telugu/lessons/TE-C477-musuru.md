## You'll want to know: ముసురు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C477-RUTUPAVANAM477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ముసురు** — *musuru* — "a spell of drizzle".

> మూడు రోజులుగా ముసురు పట్టింది.
>
> *mūḍu rōjulugā musuru paṭṭindi.*
>
> For three days there has been a steady drizzle.

Grey days of light rain that will not stop: ముసురు పట్టింది.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ముసురు** (*musuru*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4914 lists Telugu *musuru*, "a constant or continued rain".
