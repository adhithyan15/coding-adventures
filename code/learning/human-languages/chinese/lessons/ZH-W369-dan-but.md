## Script — the person piece, then the sun over a line
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-DAN-BUT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 但

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, 亻, two strokes**, as in 你: a left-falling stroke, then a vertical.

**The right piece, five strokes**: 日, four strokes, a vertical, a horizontal that turns down, a short horizontal inside and the bottom one; then 一, a long horizontal under it, with a little space between.

2 + 5 = seven.
