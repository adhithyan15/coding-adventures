## You'll want to know: diagnosticar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C738-CONSULTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**diagnosticar** — "to diagnose".

> Le diagnosticaron diabetes.
>
> *He was diagnosed with diabetes.*

Spelling: *diagnostiqué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diagnosticar* is built on *diagnóstico*, a learned word from Greek *diagnōstikós* "able to distinguish", built on *gignṓskein* "to know".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
