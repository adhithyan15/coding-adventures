German, chapter 344: Einfluss haben.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
beeinflussen — to influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for so that, then the German for therefore.

You'll want to know: beeinflussen.
beeinflussen — "to influence".
Das Wetter beeinflusst meine Laune. The weather influences my mood.
Inseparable: es beeinflusst, es hat beeinflusst. It takes a direct object.

The word, taken apart.
beeinflussen is built on Einfluss "influence", literally an "inflow", from ein "in" and Fluss "flow, river". Einfluss is usually explained as a rendering of Latin influxus "a flowing in"; EtymDB has no history for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Einfluss — the influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for therefore, then the German for to influence.

You'll want to know: der Einfluss.
der Einfluss — "the influence".
Meine Großmutter hatte einen großen Einfluss auf mich. My grandmother had a great influence on me.
Plural: die Einflüsse. Einfluss auf + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
Einfluss joins ein "in" to Fluss "flow, river". It is usually explained as a rendering of Latin influxus "a flowing in", which late Latin also used of the influence of the stars (Lewis & Short); EtymDB has no history for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Auswirkung — the impact, the effect (on something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to influence, then the German for the influence.

You'll want to know: die Auswirkung.
die Auswirkung — "the impact, the effect (on something)".
Die neue Straße hat Auswirkungen auf das ganze Dorf. The new road has an impact on the whole village.
Mostly plural: die Auswirkungen auf + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
Auswirkung is the noun of sich auswirken, built on wirken "to work", inherited from Old High German wirken and Proto-Germanic wirkijaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
auswirken — to affect (reflexive, sich auswirken auf).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the influence, then the German for the impact.

You'll want to know: auswirken.
auswirken — "to affect (reflexive, sich auswirken auf)".
Der Stress wirkt sich auf meinen Schlaf aus. The stress affects my sleep.
Reflexive and separable: Das wirkt sich auf … aus, das hat sich ausgewirkt.

The word, taken apart.
auswirken is built from aus "out" and wirken "to work", inherited from Old High German wirken and Proto-Germanic wirkijaną "to work, to make" (EtymDB): an effect works its way out.

Lesson 5 of 5.
betreffen — to concern, to affect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the impact, then the German for to affect.

You'll want to know: betreffen.
betreffen — "to concern, to affect".
Die neue Regel betrifft alle Schüler. The new rule concerns all pupils.
Inseparable and strong: es betrifft, es hat betroffen. Was mich betrifft, … — as far as I am concerned, …

The word, taken apart.
betreffen is built with be- on treffen "to hit, to meet", inherited from Old High German treffan "to hit" and Proto-Germanic drepaną (EtymDB): a rule hits the people it is about.
