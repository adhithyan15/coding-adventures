## You'll want to know: くるしむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C223-SAFETY223-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くるしむ** — *kurushimu* — "to suffer".

> **かんじゃは もう くるしみません。** — *kanja wa mō kurushimimasen* — "The patient is no longer suffering."

Polite **くるしみます**, *kurushimimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くるしむ** (*kurushimu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
