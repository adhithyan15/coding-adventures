Kannada, chapter 488: Heat and Dry Spells.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿ (bisigāḷi) — a heatwave; a hot wind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a landslide, then the Kannada for a tsunami.

You'll want to know: ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿ.
ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿ — bisigāḷi — "a heatwave; a hot wind".
ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿಯ ಕಾರಣ ಮಧ್ಯಾಹ್ನ ಹೊರಗೆ ಹೋಗಬಾರದು. bisigāḷiya kāraṇa madhyāhna horage hōgabāradu. Because of the heatwave nobody should go out at midday.
ಬಿಸಿ, hot, plus ಗಾಳಿ, wind.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿ (bisigāḷi) is a native Kannada compound of bisi, "heat, hot" (DEDR 5517), and ಗಾಳಿ, "wind" (DEDR 1499).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲು (uribisilu) — scorching sun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tsunami, then the Kannada for a heatwave.

You'll want to know: ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲು.
ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲು — uribisilu — "scorching sun".
ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕೂಲಿಗಳು ರಸ್ತೆ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು. uribisilinalli kūligaḷu raste kelasa māḍuttiddaru. Labourers were working on the road in the scorching sun.
Burning sunshine: the hottest hours of a summer day.

The word, taken apart.
ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲು (uribisilu) is a native Kannada compound of uri, "to burn, to blaze" (DEDR 656), and ಬಿಸಿಲು, "the heat and glare of the sun" (DEDR 5517).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬತ್ತು (battu) — to run dry; to dry up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a heatwave, then the Kannada for scorching sun.

You'll want to know: ಬತ್ತು.
ಬತ್ತು — battu — "to run dry; to dry up".
ಬರದಿಂದ ಊರಿನ ಕೆರೆ ಬತ್ತಿತು. baradinda ūrina kere battitu. Because of the drought the town lake ran dry.
Past (it …): ಬತ್ತಿತು, battitu.
Of wells, lakes, rivers and springs.

The word, taken apart.
ಬತ್ತು (battu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5320 lists Kannada battu, "to grow dry as a stream, to dry up, to evaporate", in the family of ಬರ, a drought.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಬಿರುಕು.
ಬಿರುಕು — biruku — "a crack; a fissure".
ಬರಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೊಲದ ಮಣ್ಣಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಬಿರುಕುಗಳು ಬಂದವು. baragāladalli holada maṇṇinalli birukugaḷu bandavu. In the drought cracks opened in the soil of the fields.
Plural: ಬಿರುಕುಗಳು, birukugaḷu.
ಬಿರುಕು (biruku) ಬಿಡು, to crack.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಿರುಕು (biruku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5411 lists Kannada biriku, biruku, "a cleft, a fissure", beside biri, "to burst open".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತಗ್ಗು (taggu) — to subside; to go down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to run dry, then the Kannada for a crack.

You'll want to know: ತಗ್ಗು.
ತಗ್ಗು — taggu — "to subside; to go down".
ಸಂಜೆ ಮಳೆ ಬಂದ ಮೇಲೆ ಸೆಕೆ ತಗ್ಗಿತು. sañje maḷe banda mēle seke taggitu. After it rained in the evening the heat died down.
Past (it …): ತಗ್ಗಿತು, taggitu.
As a noun, a low spot or a hollow: ರಸ್ತೆಯ ತಗ್ಗು (taggu).

The word, taken apart.
ತಗ್ಗು (taggu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3178 lists Kannada targu, "to become depressed, low or less, to decline".
