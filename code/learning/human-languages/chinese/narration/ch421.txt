You'll want to know: 公路.
公路 — gōnglù — "highway; main road".
zhè tiáo gōnglù hěn cháng — This main road is very long.

The word, taken apart.
公路 (gōnglù) is built from 公 'public' and 路 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kuwng and luH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 6.
高速 (gāosù) — expressway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to break the traffic rules, then the Mandarin for a main road between towns.

You'll want to know: 高速.
高速 — gāosù — "expressway".
去高速怎么走 qù gāosù zěnme zǒu — How do I get onto the expressway?
The new character, in pieces: 速 (sù) — ten strokes in two pieces: the right piece first, seven strokes, a bar, a small 口, a long vertical down through them, a stroke falling to the left and a dot; then the walking piece of 这 under it, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
高速 (gāosù) is built from 高 'high' and 速 (sù) 'speed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kaw and suwk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'swift'; it is a short form of a longer name, the high-speed road.

Script — a bundle, then the walking piece.
速 (sù).
Two pieces, and the walking piece comes last. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The upper piece, seven strokes. A bar across the top. A small 口 under it, three strokes. A long vertical down through the bar and 口. Then a stroke falling to the left from the vertical and a dot to the right, as at the foot of the tree piece.
The walking piece, three strokes, as in 这: a dot, a stroke that zigzags down, then a long sweep along the foot.
7 + 3 = ten.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

shōufèizhàn — "toll gate".
wǎng qián kāi jiù shì shōufèizhàn — Drive straight on and you come to the toll gate.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shōufèizhàn is built from shōu 'to collect', fèi 'a fee' and 站 'a station', all inherited from Middle Chinese, syuw, phj+jH and treamH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to gather in'.
