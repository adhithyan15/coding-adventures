## You'll want to know: regatear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C453-CAIXA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**regatear** — "to haggle".

> Na feira, as pessoas regateiam o preço.
>
> *At the market, people haggle over the price.*

In shops with fixed prices nobody *regateia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *regatear* is not settled; it is linked with *regatão* 'street trader', and EtymDB has no entry for either word.
