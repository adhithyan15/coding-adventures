## You'll want to know: 少量
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C526-WEIGHT526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**少量** — *shǎoliàng* — "a small amount of".

> **吃了少量的米饭以后我感觉很好** *chī le shǎoliàng de mǐfàn yǐhòu wǒ gǎnjué hěn hǎo* — After a little rice I felt fine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**少量** (*shǎoliàng*) is built from 少 'few' and 量 'an amount', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syewX* and *ljangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'not many'.
