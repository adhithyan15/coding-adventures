Hindi, chapter 298: The Injured.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हादसा (hādsā) — a mishap; an accident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be grazed, then the Hindi for covered in blood.

You'll want to know: हादसा.
हादसा — hādsā — "a mishap; an accident".
हादसे की वजह यह थी कि ड्राइवर बहुत तेज़ गाड़ी चला रहा था। hādse kī vajah yah thī ki ḍrāivar bahut tez gāṛī calā rahā thā. The cause of the accident was that the driver was going too fast.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
हादसा (hādsā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥāditha, "something that happens". It is the everyday word beside the formal दुर्घटना.

Lesson 2 of 5.
घायल (ghāyal) — injured; wounded.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for covered in blood, then the Hindi for a mishap.

You'll want to know: घायल.
घायल — ghāyal — "injured; wounded".
हादसे में दस लोग घायल हुए। hādse meṁ das log ghāyal hue. Ten people were injured in the accident.

The word, taken apart.
घायल (ghāyal) is built on ghāy, a wound, which CDIAL 4460 lists for Hindi under Sanskrit ghāta, "a blow". घायल होना is to be injured: दस लोग घायल हुए.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for injured, then the Hindi for unconscious.

You'll want to know: कुचलना.
कुचलना — kucalnā — "to crush; to run over".
एक ट्रक ने उसकी साइकिल कुचल दी। ek ṭrak ne uskī sāikil kucal dī. A lorry ran over his bicycle.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने साँप को कुचला, usne sā̃p ko kuclā.

The word, taken apart.
कुचलना (kucalnā) is listed by CDIAL under kucyatē, "is contracted, is bent" (CDIAL 3221), with Hindi kucalnā, to trample on. A wheel or a foot that कुचलता है leaves the thing flattened.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सँभलना (sãbhalnā) — to steady oneself; to recover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for unconscious, then the Hindi for to crush.

You'll want to know: सँभलना.
सँभलना — sãbhalnā — "to steady oneself; to recover".
थोड़ी देर बाद वह सँभला और उठा। thoṛī der bād vah sãbhlā aur uṭhā. After a little while he steadied himself and got up.
Past (he …): वह सँभल गया, vah sãbhal gayā.

The word, taken apart.
सँभलना (sãbhalnā) is the intransitive partner of सँभालना, to look after, and CDIAL 12961 lists both under Sanskrit saṁbharati, "brings together". You सँभलते हैं when you pull yourself together after a fall or a shock.
