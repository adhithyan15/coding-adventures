Kannada, chapter 242: To temper, To soak, To wring, To dip, To tear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಒಗ್ಗರಣೆ ಹಾಕು (oggaraṇe hāku) — to temper (fry spices in hot oil).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to find out, then the Kannada for to borrow.

You'll want to know: ಒಗ್ಗರಣೆ ಹಾಕು.
ಒಗ್ಗರಣೆ ಹಾಕು — oggaraṇe hāku — "to temper (fry spices in hot oil)".

The word, taken apart.
ಒಗ್ಗರಣೆ ಹಾಕು (oggaraṇe hāku) is oggaraṇe, "seasoning", which DEDR lists among Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 17), and hāku, "to put", whose history neither DEDR nor CDIAL gives.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ನೆನೆಸು (nenesu) — to soak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to borrow, then the Kannada for to temper.

You'll want to know: ನೆನೆಸು.
ನೆನೆಸು — nenesu — "to soak".

The word, taken apart.
ನೆನೆಸು (nenesu) is native Dravidian, from nene, "to become wet", and DEDR 3630 lists nenasu, "to make wet, moisten", itself; the entry also has Tamil naṉai, Telugu nānu and Malayalam nana.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹಿಂಡು (hiṇḍu) — to wring, to squeeze out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to temper, then the Kannada for to soak.

You'll want to know: ಹಿಂಡು.
ಹಿಂಡು — hiṇḍu — "to wring, to squeeze out".

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿಂಡು (hiṇḍu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil piḻi (DEDR 4183). DEDR lists piṇḍu, hiṇḍu, "to squeeze out, wring". DEDR 4183 compares Sanskrit piṇyāka.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹರಿ (hari) — to tear, to rip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to wring, then the Kannada for to dip.

You'll want to know: ಹರಿ.
ಹರಿ — hari — "to tear, to rip".

The word, taken apart.
ಹರಿ (hari) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil pari and Malayalam pari (DEDR 3963). DEDR lists pari, hari, "to run, flow, go away": the p- has become h-.
