# నొప్పి (noppi) — a pain, an ache

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C63-HEALTH-05, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for health, then the Telugu for a fever.

## You'll want to know: నొప్పి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నొప్పి** (*noppi*) — "a pain, an ache".

Put a body part in front of it and you have the complaint: **తల నొప్పి** (*tala noppi*), a
headache, and **కడుపు నొప్పి** (*kaḍupu noppi*), a stomach ache. Both body words are ones you
already own.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One word that turns every body part you know into something you can complain about.
