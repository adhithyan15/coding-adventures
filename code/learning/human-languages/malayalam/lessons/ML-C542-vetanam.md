## You'll want to know: വേതനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C542-TOZHIL542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**വേതനം** — *vētanaṁ* — "pay; wages".

> തൊഴിലാളികൾ കൂടുതൽ വേതനം ചോദിച്ചു.
>
> *toḻilāḷikaḷ kūṭutal vētanaṁ cōdiccu.*
>
> The workers asked for more pay.

Formal, in contracts and the news; ശമ്പളം and കൂലി are the everyday words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വേതനം** (*vētanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vetana*, "wages, hire, salary" (Monier-Williams).
