## You'll want to know: собираться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C377-PLANY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**собираться** (*sobirátsya*) — "to be going to, to intend; to gather".

> Летом мы собираемся на море.
>
> *Létom my sobiráyemsya na móre.*
>
> In the summer we are planning to go to the seaside.

Imperfective; perfective *собраться*. With an infinitive, to intend: *я собираюсь уехать*. It also means to gather: *гости собираются*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**собираться** is the reflexive of *собирать* "to gather", from *со-* "together" and *бирать*, from *брать* "to take": to gather yourself up for a journey or a plan.
