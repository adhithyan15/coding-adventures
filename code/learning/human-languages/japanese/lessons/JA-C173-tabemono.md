## You'll want to know: たべもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C173-FOOD173-01]; assesses=[] -->

**たべもの** — *tabemono* — "food".

> **にほんの たべものは おいしいです。** — *nihon no tabemono wa oishii desu* — "Japanese food is tasty."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たべもの** (*tabemono*) is native Japanese, built from *tabe*, the stem of *taberu*, "to eat", and *mono*, "thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
