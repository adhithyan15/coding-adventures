## You'll want to know: उच्छलति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C547-UTTHANA547-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उच्छलति** — *ucchalati* — "springs up".

> यदि जले शिला पतति, तर्हि जलम् उच्छलति।
>
> *yadi jale śilā patati, tarhi jalam ucchalati.*
>
> If a stone falls into the water, the water splashes up.

Past (it …): जलम् उच्छलितवत्, *jalam ucchalitavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उच्छलति** (*ucchalati*) is built on *ud*, "up", and the root √śal, "to move", which after *ud* is written *chal*; MW gives *ucchal* as to fly upwards, to spring up (MW).
