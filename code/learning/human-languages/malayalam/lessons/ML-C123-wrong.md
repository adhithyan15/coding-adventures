## You'll want to know: തെറ്റ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C123-MAYBE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**തെറ്റ്** (*teṟṟŭ*) — "wrong", and also "a mistake". It is the honest partner of
**ശരി**, "right".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The opposite of śari.
