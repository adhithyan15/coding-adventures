## You'll want to know: téng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C130-ILL130-04]; assesses=[] -->

*téng* — "sore; painful".

> *wǒ de tóu hěn téng* — My head really hurts.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*téng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *downg* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'pain'. The regular sound change would give *tóng*, not *téng*; why the vowel differs is not settled.
