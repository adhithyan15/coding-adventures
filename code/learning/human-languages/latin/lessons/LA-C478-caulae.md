## You'll want to know: caulae, caulārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C478-OPILIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**caulae, caulārum** — "a sheepfold, a pen; an opening".

> Lupus prope caulās vagātur.
>
> *A wolf prowls near the sheepfold.*

First declension, feminine plural: *caulae, caulārum f.* First a hole or passage, then a fenced pen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caulae* is usually traced to *cavus* 'hollow', through an older *cavile* (Lewis & Short, after an ancient grammarian); EtymDB gives no parent, so the line is hedged.
