Punjabi, chapter 323: Inside the Body.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਢਿੱਡ (ḍhiḍḍ) — the belly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a bandage, then the Punjabi for an ointment.

You'll want to know: ਢਿੱਡ.
ਢਿੱਡ — ḍhiḍḍ — "the belly".
ਮੇਰੇ ਢਿੱਡ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਰਦ ਹੈ। mere ḍhiḍḍ vich darad hai. I have a pain in my belly.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਢਿੱਡ (ḍhiḍḍ) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍhiḍḍha, "belly"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍhiḍḍh, "belly", under it (CDIAL 5589).
