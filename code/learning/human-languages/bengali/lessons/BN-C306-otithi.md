## You'll want to know: অতিথি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C306-OTITHI306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অতিথি** — *ôtithi* — "a guest".

> পুজোর দিন বাড়িতে অনেক অতিথি এলেন।
>
> *pujor din bāṛite ônek ôtithi elen.*
>
> Many guests came to the house on the day of the puja.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অতিথি** (*ôtithi*) is Sanskrit *atithi*, 'a guest, one owed hospitality' (Monier-Williams), which records an old explanation: *a-tithi*, one with no fixed day for coming.
