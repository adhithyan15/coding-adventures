## You'll want to know: طرز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C538-IBRA538-02]; assesses=[] -->

**طرز** — *ṭarraza* — "to embroider".

> طرزت أمي وردة على الوسادة.
>
> *ṭarrazat ummī warda ʿalā al-wisāda.*
>
> My mother embroidered a rose on the pillow.

Present: يطرز, *yuṭarriz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طرز** (*ṭarraza*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-r-z*, "to embroider, to figure a cloth" (Lane). Form II ṭarraza is the usual verb.
