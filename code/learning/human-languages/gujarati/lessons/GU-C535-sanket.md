## You'll want to know: સંકેત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C535-SHUKAN535-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સંકેત** — *sanket* — "a hint".

> જો આકાશમાં કાળાં વાદળ હશે તો તે વરસાદનો સંકેત છે.
>
> *jo ākāśmā̃ kāḷā̃ vādaḷ haśe to te varsādno sanket che.*
>
> If there are black clouds in the sky, that is a sign of rain.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંકેત** (*sanket*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṃketa*, "an agreement; a sign, a signal" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for saying what happens if.
