## You'll want to know: شانه خاکی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C425-OTOBAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شانه خاکی** (*shâne-khâki*) — "a hard shoulder (of a road)".

> ماشین خراب را در شانه خاکی دیدیم.
>
> *Mâshin-e kharâb râ dar shâne-khâki didim.*
>
> We saw the broken-down car on the hard shoulder.

Literally 'earthy shoulder': *shâne* 'shoulder' + *khâki* 'of earth'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shâne-khâki* is a native compound: *shâne* 'shoulder' is inherited from Middle Persian *šānag* (EtymDB), and *khâki* 'earthy' is built on *khâk* 'earth', from Middle Persian *xāk* (EtymDB).
