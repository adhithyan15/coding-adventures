5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अर्थव्यवस्था (arthavyavasthā) — the economy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tip; a prize, then the Marathi for a stop.

You'll want to know: अर्थव्यवस्था.
अर्थव्यवस्था — arthavyavasthā — "the economy".

The word, taken apart.
अर्थव्यवस्था (arthavyavasthā) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit artha, "aim, wealth" (CDIAL 638), and vyavasthā, "arrangement".
