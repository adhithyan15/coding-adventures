## You'll want to know: чаевые
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C435-OBSLUZHIVANIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**чаевые** (*chayevýye*) — "a tip, tips (for service)".

> В ресторане мы оставили чаевые.
>
> *V restoráne my ostávili chayevýye.*
>
> We left a tip in the restaurant.

Plural only; an adjective used as a noun: money *на чай*, for tea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*чаевые* is built on *чай* 'tea' (EtymDB lists *чай*): money once given *на чай*, for a glass of tea.
