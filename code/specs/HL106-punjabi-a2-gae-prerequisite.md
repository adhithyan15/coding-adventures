# HL106 — Punjabi A2 `ਗਏ` prerequisite

Issue: #18329

After PR #18292 merged, an aggregate-only audit of the preserved confidential
Punjabi A2 mock drafts against exact merged main
`e0e91828f4d0d829fd1b3377a5f061dd5b7cbe9c` finds **ਗਏ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs twice in the confidential learner material and
once in the confidential examiner material. Neither confidential body nor the
remaining candidate set belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 206 closes that one-form gap through one deliberately narrow frame:
**ਉਹ ਗਏ** means “they went” here, with the same masculine plural form also
available for a respectful masculine singular referent. The voice-first opener
retrieves only already-owned **ਉਹ** and **ਜਾਣਾ**. One lesson directly owns exact
**ਗਏ**, a short model-visible script lesson isolates **ਏ** from that now-known
word without claiming unverified stroke order, an immediate lesson retrieves
the word's sound, meaning, and script, a bounded frame lesson places it after
familiar **ਉਹ**, and a four-skill payoff retrieves the complete frame again.
The target has one direct owner and four later
retrievals. It must not leak into authored, generated-book, or narration copy
before its owner.

The new atoms are `PA-GRAM-GAE-206` and `PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-E-206`. The direct
owner presents **ਗਏ** as the
irregular masculine plural perfective form of already-owned **ਜਾਣਾ**, then uses
only the bounded “went” reading needed for **ਉਹ ਗਏ**. It does not teach the
whole perfective or agreement system, open ergative alignment, add oblique use,
or introduce the neighbouring forms **ਗਿਆ**, **ਗਈ**, and **ਗਈਆਂ**. The script
owner follows the known word, keeps the printed model visible, and cites the
Unicode Gurmukhi chart for the modern character identity; it explicitly defers
stroke order because the inventory has no verified stroke-order source for
**ਏ**.

Punjabi University Patiala's lexical statistics identifies **ਗਏ** as the
masculine plural perfect form of **ਜਾ**, “go / went”:
<https://www.learnpunjabi.org/statistics.html>. Its verb reference places
**ਗਏ** in the IA/perfective forms of **ਜਾ**:
<https://www.learnpunjabi.org/Verb.html>. Its reference grammar independently
pairs irregular **ਜਾ** with **ਗਿਆ / ਗਏ** and identifies **ਗਏ** as masculine
plural:
<https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/assets/a%20reference%20grammar_final.pdf>.

Those sources support the modern inflectional relationship. They do not justify
inventing a new etymology for the inflected form: the course reuses the history
already attached to **ਜਾਣਾ**, keeps **ਗਏ** whole as an irregular form, and
makes no broader historical claim.

This is the twenty-ninth prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
