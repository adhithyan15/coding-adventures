## You'll want to know: alertar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C303-CHEIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**alertar** — "to warn, to alert".

> Os vizinhos alertaram os bombeiros.
>
> *The neighbours alerted the fire brigade.*

*Alertar para um perigo* — to warn of a danger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alertar* is built on *alerta*, from Italian *all’erta* "on the lookout" (EtymDB).
