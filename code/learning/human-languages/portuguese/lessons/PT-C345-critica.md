## You'll want to know: a crítica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C345-VANTAGEM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a crítica** — "the criticism; the review (of a film or a book)".

> Ele não aceita bem as críticas.
>
> *He does not take criticism well.*

*Fazer uma crítica* — to criticise; *o crítico* — the critic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crítica* is a learned word from Greek *kritikḗ* "the art of judging", built on *krínein* "to separate, to judge"; Latin borrowed the adjective as *criticus* "fit for deciding" (Lewis & Short).
