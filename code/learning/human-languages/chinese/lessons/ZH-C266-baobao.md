## You'll want to know: bǎobao
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C266-AGE266-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎobao* — "baby; darling (said to a small child)".

> *wǒ jiějie de bǎobao hěn kě'ài* — My sister's baby is really cute.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎobao* is built from *bǎo* 'a treasure', said twice, the second time with no tone. It is inherited from Middle Chinese *pawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a treasure'; its use for a baby is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for what I have and can do.
