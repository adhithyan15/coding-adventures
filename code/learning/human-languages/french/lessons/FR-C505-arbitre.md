## You'll want to know: l'arbitre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C505-TERRAIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'arbitre** — "referee, umpire".

> L'arbitre a donné un carton rouge.
>
> *The referee gave a red card.*

The same form for a man and a woman: *un arbitre*, *une arbitre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'arbitre* is a learned borrowing from Latin *arbiter* "a witness, a judge" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
