## You'll want to know: भाव करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C423-KHAREDI423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भाव करणे** — *bhāv karṇe* — "to bargain".

> आजीने भाजीवाल्याशी भाव केला.
>
> *ājīne bhājīvālyāśī bhāv kelā.*
>
> Grandmother bargained with the vegetable seller.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भाव केला, *tyāne bhāv kelā*.

The seller takes -शी: दुकानदाराशी भाव करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाव करणे** (*bhāv karṇe*) joins **भाव**, "the going rate", inherited from Sanskrit *bhāva*, where CDIAL lists Marathi *bhāv*, "market rate" (CDIAL 9475), to **करणे**.
