## You'll want to know: متدرب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C519-WAZIFA519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**متدرب** — *mutadarrib* — "a trainee, an intern".

> يعمل في المكتب ثلاثة متدربين.
>
> *yaʿmalu fī al-maktab thalāthat mutadarribīn.*
>
> Three trainees work in the office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متدرب** (*mutadarrib*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-r-b*, "to be used to, to be practised in" (Lane). Mutadarrib is the participle of Form V: one being trained.
