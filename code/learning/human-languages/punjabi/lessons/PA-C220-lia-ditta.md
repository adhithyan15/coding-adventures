# ਲਿਆ, ਦਿੱਤਾ — took, gave

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-LAINA, PA-LEX-C58-VERBS58-02, PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *to take* and *to give*: **ਲੈਣਾ**, **ਦੇਣਾ**. Then *Aman bought a
book*.

## Grammar Lens: two short pasts
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-LIA-DITTA-220]; assesses=[PA-LEX-LAINA, PA-LEX-C58-VERBS58-02, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAM-NU-DATIVE, PA-LEX-PAANI, PA-LEX-C72-SCHOOL72-02] -->

**ਲੈਣਾ** and **ਦੇਣਾ** have short pasts of their own: **ਲਿਆ**, **ਦਿੱਤਾ**. They
agree with the thing, like every past you have met:

> **ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਲਿਆ।** — *maiṁ pāṇī liā.* — I took water.

> **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਪੈਸਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ।** — *Aman ne paisā dittā.* — Aman gave money.

> **ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਦਿੱਤੀ।** — *maiṁ Aman nūṁ kitāb dittī.* — I gave Aman a book.

The one who receives takes **ਨੂੰ**. CDIAL lists Punjabi *dittā*, "given" (CDIAL
6140).
