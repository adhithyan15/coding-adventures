Spanish, chapter 676: Turismo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el turismo — tourism; a private car (Spain, on forms).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the arcade, then the Spanish for the paving stone.

You'll want to know: el turismo.
el turismo — "tourism; a private car (Spain, on forms)".
El turismo es importante aquí. Tourism is important here.
Hacer turismo — to go sightseeing.

The word, taken apart.
turismo was borrowed from English tourism (EtymDB), built on tour, from French tour "a turn, a round trip".

You'll want to know: el folleto.
el folleto — "the leaflet, the brochure".
Coge un folleto en la oficina de turismo. Take a leaflet at the tourist office.
Hotels and museums give them out for free.

The word, taken apart.
folleto came from Italian foglietto "a little sheet" (EtymDB), built on foglio, from Latin folium "leaf".

Lesson 5 of 5.
regatear — to haggle, to bargain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for tourist, then the Spanish for the leaflet.

You'll want to know: regatear.
regatear — "to haggle, to bargain".
En el mercado puedes regatear un poco. At the market you can haggle a little.
Shops have fixed prices; at a market stall you can try.

The word, taken apart.
regatear is usually traced, through older recatear (EtymDB), to Vulgar Latin recaptāre, built on Latin captāre "to try to seize".
