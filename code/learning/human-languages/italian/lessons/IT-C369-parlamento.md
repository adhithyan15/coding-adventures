## You'll want to know: il parlamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C369-PARTITO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il parlamento** — "the parliament".

> Il parlamento discute la nuova legge.
>
> *Parliament is debating the new law.*

Stress: *parlaménto*. Its members are elected by the citizens.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parlamento* is built on *parlare* "to speak", inherited from Late Latin *parabolāre* (EtymDB), after Old French *parlement* "a speaking, a meeting": the place where people talk.
