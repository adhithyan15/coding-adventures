Punjabi, chapter 227: Soap and Water.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਔਖਾ (aukhā) — hard to do; difficult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to shut a door, then the Punjabi for a walk.

You'll want to know: ਔਖਾ.
ਔਖਾ — aukhā — "hard to do; difficult".
ਸਰਦੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਨਹਾਉਣਾ ਔਖਾ ਹੈ। sardī vich savere nahāuṇā aukhā hai. Bathing early on a winter morning is hard.

The word, taken apart.
ਔਖਾ (aukhā) is inherited: CDIAL sets it under Sanskrit apakṣa, "without wings, without followers", and lists Punjabi aukhā, "difficult, distressed" (CDIAL 414).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a walk, then the Punjabi for hard to do.

You'll want to know: ਮਲਣਾ.
ਮਲਣਾ — malṇā — "to rub".
ਮੈਂ ਹੱਥਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ ਸਾਬਣ ਮਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ hatthāṁ utte sābaṇ maldā hāṁ. I rub soap on my hands.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਾਬਣ ਮਲਿਆ, maiṁ sābaṇ maliā.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਲਣਾ (malṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed marati, "crushes, rubs"; CDIAL lists Punjabi maḷnā, "to rub" (CDIAL 9870).
