## You'll want to know: инспектор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C408-SKOROST-03]; assesses=[] -->

**инспектор** (*inspéktor*) — "an inspector; a traffic police officer".

> Инспектор попросил водителя выйти из машины.
>
> *Inspéktor poprosíl vodítelya výyti iz mashíny.*
>
> The inspector asked the driver to get out of the car.

Masculine; plural *инспекторы*, in speech also *инспектора*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*инспектор* is usually traced to Latin *inspector* 'an examiner', from *inspicere* 'to look into', built on *specere* 'to look' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists the Russian word without a source, so the route is hedged.
