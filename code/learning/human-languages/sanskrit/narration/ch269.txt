Lesson 2 of 6.
आकाशवाणी (ākāśavāṇī) — radio.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall the four familiar words from the previous lesson.

You'll want to know: आकाशवाणी.
आकाशवाणी — ākāśavāṇī — “radio.” The Kosha Sanskrit Today radio entry explicitly attests this modern use. This tranche teaches only the modern radio meaning. It does not ask you to generalise from the word's older “voice from the sky” sense.

The word, taken apart.
The cited entry attests the whole modern term but does not document who coined it or when, so its origin remains unknown here. For memory only, the written form visibly contains आकाश, “sky,” and वाणी, “voice.” The one meaning to retrieve is “radio.”

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: आकाशवाणी (ākāśavāṇī) — radio]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — contrast: आकाशवाणी (ākāśavāṇī) — radio; दूरभाषः — telephone]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What is the sourced modern radio word in this lesson?


Lesson 3 of 6.
अन्तर्जालम् (antarjālam) — the internet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the new radio word once from memory.

You'll want to know: अन्तर्जालम्.
अन्तर्जालम् — antarjālam — “the internet.” The modern computing term is explicitly attested in the Kosha Sanskrit Today internet entry and its Computer — Internet glossary. This lesson owns only that sourced modern meaning.

The word, taken apart.
The cited entries attest the whole modern term but do not document who coined it or when, so its origin remains unknown here. For memory only, the written form visibly contains अन्तर्, “between” or “inter-,” and जाल, “net.” That “inter-net” shape is not a lesson in using the internet.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — contrast: आकाशवाणी (ākāśavāṇī) — radio; अन्तर्जालम् (antarjālam) — internet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 4 of 6.
Six words, one small field — distinguish and retrieve the six meanings in one guided round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
Say “radio,” then “internet.”

Guided Practice.
[pause 3 seconds after each]
Retrieve each word before checking:
press: पत्रिका — patrikā.
telephone: दूरभाषः — dūrabhāṣaḥ.
radio: आकाशवाणी — ākāśavāṇī.
television: दूरदर्शनम् — dūradarśanam.
internet: अन्तर्जालम् — antarjālam.
letter or article: लेखः — lekhaḥ.

What you've built.
You can now distinguish six meanings in one compact lexical field. This is word retrieval, not evidence that you can use devices, follow news, write correspondence, broadcast, or navigate online.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which two words were new in this chapter?
