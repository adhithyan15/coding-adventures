# ことわる (kotowaru) — to refuse; to turn down

## You'll want to know: ことわる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C232-TALK232-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ことわる** — *kotowaru* — "to refuse; to turn down".

> **あには よく しごとを ことわります。** — *ani wa yoku shigoto o kotowarimasu* — "My elder brother often turns down work."

Polite **ことわります**, *kotowarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ことわる** (*kotowaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
