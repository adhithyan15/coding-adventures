## You'll want to know: rétorquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C336-DESACCORD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rétorquer** — "to retort".

> Il a rétorqué que ce n'était pas vrai.
>
> *He retorted that it was not true.*

Literally to twist back: you turn the words back on the speaker.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rétorquer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *retorquēre* "to twist or bend back" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *torquēre* "to twist".
