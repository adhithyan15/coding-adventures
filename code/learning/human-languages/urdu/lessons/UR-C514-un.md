## You'll want to know: اون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C514-KARHAI514-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اون** — *ūn* — "wool".

> سردیوں میں ہم اون کے سویٹر پہنتے ہیں۔
>
> *sardiyoṅ meṅ ham ūn ke sveṭar pahante haiṅ.*
>
> In winter we wear woollen jumpers.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اون** (*ūn*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ūrṇā*, "wool" (CDIAL 2424).
