## You'll want to know: die Flamme
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C293-BRENNEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Flamme** — "the flame".

> Die Flammen waren schon hoch über dem Dach.
>
> *The flames were already high above the roof.*

Plural: *die Flammen*. *In Flammen stehen* — to be on fire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Flamme* was borrowed from Latin *flamma* "a flame, a blaze" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB records the loan already in Old High German *flamma*.
