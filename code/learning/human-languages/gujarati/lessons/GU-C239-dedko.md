## You'll want to know: દેડકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C239-POND239-01]; assesses=[] -->

**દેડકો** — *deḍko* — "frog".

> **રાત્રે તળાવમાં દેડકો બોલ્યો.** — *rātre taḷāvmā̃ deḍko bolyo* — "A frog croaked in the pond at night."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દેડકો** (*deḍko*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *dardura*, "frog" (CDIAL 6198).
