## You'll want to know: le manette
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C297-MANETTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le manette** — "the handcuffs".

> Il poliziotto gli ha messo le manette.
>
> *The policeman put the handcuffs on him.*

Plural; literally ‘little hands’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manette* is the plural of *manetta*, a little *mano* "hand", from Latin *manus* (Lewis & Short).
