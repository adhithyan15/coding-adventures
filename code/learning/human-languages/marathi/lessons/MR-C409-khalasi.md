## You'll want to know: खलाशी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C409-KHALASHI409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खलाशी** — *khalāśī* — "a sailor".

> जहाजावर वीस खलाशी काम करतात.
>
> *jahājāvar vīs khalāśī kām kartāt.*
>
> Twenty sailors work on the ship.

Masculine.

The plural is the same in the direct case: एक खलाशी, वीस खलाशी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खलाशी** (*khalāśī*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to *khalāṣī*, a word for a ship's hand.
