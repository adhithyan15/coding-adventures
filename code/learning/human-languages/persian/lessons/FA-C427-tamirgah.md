## You'll want to know: تعمیرگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C427-TAMIRGAH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تعمیرگاه** (*ta'mirgâh*) — "a garage, a repair shop".

> ماشین را به تعمیرگاه بردیم.
>
> *Mâshin râ be ta'mirgâh bordim.*
>
> We took the car to the garage.

*ta'mir* 'repair' + *gâh* 'place'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'mirgâh* is built from *ta'mir* 'repair', a loan usually traced to Arabic *taʿmīr* 'making habitable, restoring', and the native *gâh* 'place'. EtymDB does not list it.
