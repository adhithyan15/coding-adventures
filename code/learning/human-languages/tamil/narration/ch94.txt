Tamil, chapter 94: When You Are Lost.
4 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 4.
புரியவில்லை (puriyavillai) — I don't understand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for please show me, then the Tamil for I did not hear it.

You'll want to know: புரியவில்லை.
புரியவில்லை (puriyavillai) — "I don't understand", literally "it did not become clear".
The most useful sentence a learner owns. It also ends in இல்லை.

What you've built.
The honest sentence every learner needs.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: puriyavillai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: puriyavillai, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say puriyavillai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for please show me, then the Tamil for I did not hear it, then say puriyavillai again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does புரியவில்லை (puriyavillai) mean? ("I don't understand".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 4.
எழுதுங்கள் (eḻutuṅkaḷ) — please write it.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for I did not hear it, then the Tamil for I don't understand.

You'll want to know: எழுதுங்கள்.
எழுதுங்கள் (eḻutuṅkaḷ) — "please write (it)".
It shares its root with எழுத்து, "a letter of the script". இங்கே எழுதுங்கள் (eḻutuṅkaḷ) — "please write it here".

What you've built.
A request to see the word.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: eḻutuṅkaḷ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say iṅkē eḻutuṅkaḷ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for I did not hear it, then the Tamil for I don't understand, then say eḻutuṅkaḷ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does எழுதுங்கள் (eḻutuṅkaḷ) mean? ("Please write it".) Say it once more.


Lesson 3 of 4.
இன்னொரு முறை (iṉṉoru muṟai) — one more time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for I don't understand, then the Tamil for please write it.

You'll want to know: இன்னொரு முறை.
இன்னொரு முறை (iṉṉoru muṟai) — "one more time".
இன்னொரு is "another", and முறை is "a time, a turn".

What you've built.
Once more, politely.

Lesson 4 of 4.
என்ன சொன்னீர்கள்? (eṉṉa coṉṉīrkaḷ?) — what did you say?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for please write it, then the Tamil for one more time.

You'll want to know: என்ன சொன்னீர்கள்?
என்ன சொன்னீர்கள்? (eṉṉa coṉṉīrkaḷ?) — "what did you say?".
என்ன is "what"; சொன்னீர்கள் is "you said", respectfully.

What you've built.
Don't understand, write it, one more time, what did you say.
