## You'll want to know: grato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C499-GRATIDAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**grato** — "grateful".

> Estou muito grato pela vossa ajuda.
>
> *I am very grateful for your help.*

More formal than *obrigado*: *estar grato por*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grato* is a learned borrowing from Latin *grātus* "pleasing, thankful" (EtymDB).
