## You'll want to know: چپکانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C506-SAFAI506-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چپکانا** — *chipkānā* — "to stick (something) on; to glue".

> میں نے لفافے پر ٹکٹ چپکایا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne lifāfe par ṭikaṭ chipkāyā.*
>
> I stuck a stamp on the envelope.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دیوار پر اشتہار چپکایا, *us ne dīvār par ishtihār chipkāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چپکانا** (*chipkānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *cep*, "gummy matter", and *cepnā*, "to stick on", under *\*cippa*, "gummy matter" (CDIAL 4819).
