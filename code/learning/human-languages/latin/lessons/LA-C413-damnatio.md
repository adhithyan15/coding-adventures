## You'll want to know: damnātiō, damnātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C413-CONDEMNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**damnātiō, damnātiōnis** — "a conviction, a condemnation".

> Post damnātiōnem in exsilium iit.
>
> *After his conviction he went into exile.*

*damnātiō, damnātiōnis* f., the noun of *damnō*. English *damnation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*damnātiō* is the noun built on *damnō* "to condemn" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *damnum* "a loss". English *damnation* comes from it.
