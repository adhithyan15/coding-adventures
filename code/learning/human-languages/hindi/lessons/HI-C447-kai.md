## You'll want to know: काई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C447-JHADI447-03]; assesses=[] -->

**काई** — *kāī* — "moss; green scum (on water)".

> पत्थरों पर काई थी, इसलिए मैं गिर गया।
>
> *pattharoṁ par kāī thī, isliye maiṁ gir gayā.*
>
> There was moss on the stones, so I fell over.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काई** (*kāī*) goes back to *\*kāvikā*, "scum", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3109), which lists Hindi *kāī*, "green scum on stagnant water".
