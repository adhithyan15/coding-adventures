## You'll want to know: लेख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C311-LEKH311-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लेख** — *lekh* — "an article".

> तिचा लेख आजच्या वृत्तपत्रात आला.
>
> *ticā lekh ājacyā vṛttapatrāt ālā.*
>
> Her article appeared in today's newspaper.

Masculine.

लेख लिहिणे, to write an article.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लेख** (*lekh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *lekha*, 'a line, a writing' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
