## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Two tickets, please. How much is the fare?]
- [YOU SAY: When is the next train? Which platform does it leave from?]
- [YOU SAY: The fast train is quick, but it's expensive.]
- [YOU SAY: My bag is lost. My wallet is lost too.]
- [YOU SAY: I missed my train. Will you help me? I'll have to make a complaint to the police.]
