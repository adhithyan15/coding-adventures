## You'll want to know: જોડણી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C245-SPELL245-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જોડણી** — *joḍṇī* — "spelling".

> **આ શબ્દની જોડણી અઘરી છે.** — *ā śabdanī joḍṇī agharī che* — "The spelling of this word is hard."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જોડણી** (*joḍṇī*), the joining of letters, is built on the inherited verb *joḍvũ*, "to join", with the suffix *-aṇī*. CDIAL lists *joḍvũ* under *\*yōṭayati*, "joins", a reconstructed form in its entry *\*yuṭati*, "is joined", and calls the history of the group obscure (CDIAL 10496).
