## You'll want to know: 用心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C273-CHAR273-03]; assesses=[] -->

**用心** — *yòngxīn* — "attentive; putting your heart into it".

> **我老公工作很用心** *wǒ lǎogōng gōngzuò hěn yòngxīn* — My husband puts his heart into his work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**用心** (*yòngxīn*) is built from 用 'to use' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yowngH* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
