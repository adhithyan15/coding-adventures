## You'll want to know: रहदारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C404-VALSA404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रहदारी** — *rahdārī* — "road traffic; movement".

> सकाळी या चौकात खूप रहदारी असते.
>
> *sakāḷī yā caukāt khūp rahdārī aste.*
>
> In the morning there is a lot of traffic at this square.

Feminine.

A feminine noun; वाहतूक is the same idea in a more official word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रहदारी** (*rahdārī*) is a loan usually traced to Persian *rāhdārī*, built on *rāh*, "road": the coming and going on a road.
