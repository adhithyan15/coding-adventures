## You'll want to know: гарантировать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C375-NEOBKHODIMO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**гарантировать** (*garantírovat*) — "to guarantee".

> Я не могу гарантировать, что всё получится.
>
> *Ya ne mogú garantírovat, chto vsyo polúchitsya.*
>
> I cannot guarantee that everything will work out.

The same form serves as both imperfective and perfective; present *гарантирую, гарантируешь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**гарантировать** is usually traced, through German, to French *garantir* "to guarantee" (EtymDB), built on *garant* "a guarantor"; EtymDB sets *garant* beside Old High German *werento* "a guarantor" (EtymDB).
