## You'll want to know: отставать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C512-RACE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**отставать** (*otstavát*) — "to fall behind".

> Я часто отстаю от группы.
>
> *Ya chásto otstayú ot grúppy.*
>
> I often fall behind the group.

Imperfective; present отстаю, отстаёшь (-ава- becomes -а-). Perfective отстать. Of a clock, to be slow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отставать* is built on *от-* 'away' and *ставать*, the stem of *стать* 'to stand, to become'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
