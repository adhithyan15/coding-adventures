## You'll want to know: جرقه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C328-ATASHSUZI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جرقه** (*jaraqe*) — "a spark".

> از سیم برق جرقه پرید.
>
> *Az sim-e barq jaraqe parid.*
>
> A spark flew from the electric wire.

Also figurative: *jaraqe-ye fekr* "the spark of an idea".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *jaraqe* "a spark" is not settled, and EtymDB lists no entry.
