## You'll want to know: भाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C322-HATHIYAR322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भाला** — *bhālā* — "a spear".

> उस सिपाही के हाथ में एक लंबा भाला था।
>
> *us sipāhī ke hāth meṁ ek lambā bhālā thā.*
>
> That soldier had a long spear in his hand.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाला** (*bhālā*) goes back to Sanskrit *bhalla* (CDIAL 9409), where CDIAL records Hindi *bhālā*, a large spear.
