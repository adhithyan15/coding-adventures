## You'll want to know: संगोपन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C500-SANGOPAN500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संगोपन** — *saṅgopan* — "upbringing".

> आजीने माझं संगोपन केलं.
>
> *ājīne mājhaṁ saṅgopan kelaṁ.*
>
> My grandmother brought me up.

Neuter.

संगोपन करणे, to bring up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संगोपन** (*saṅgopan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṃgopana*, "hiding or guarding well" (Monier-Williams); in Marathi it is the guarding of a child as it grows.
