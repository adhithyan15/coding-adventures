## You'll want to know: zaubern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C314-ZAUBERN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**zaubern** — "to conjure, to do magic".

> Der Zauberer zauberte eine Blume aus dem Hut.
>
> *The magician conjured a flower out of the hat.*

Regular: *zauberte*, *hat gezaubert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zaubern* is inherited from Middle High German *zoubern* (EtymDB), built on *Zauber* "magic", Proto-Germanic *taubrą*.
