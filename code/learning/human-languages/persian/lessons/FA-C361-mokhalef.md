## You'll want to know: مخالف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C361-MOVAFEQAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مخالف** (*mokhâlef*) — "opposed, against; an opponent".

> بیشتر مردم با این طرح مخالف هستند.
>
> *bishtar-e mardom bâ in tarh mokhâlef hastand.*
>
> Most people are against this plan.

*Bâ chizi mokhâlef budan* — to be against something. The opposite is *movâfeq*. As a noun, an opponent: plural *mokhâlefân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخالف** "contrary, opposed" is in EtymDB; it is usually traced to Arabic *mukhālif* "opposing, going against".
