## You'll want to know: diū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C116-CARRY116-05]; assesses=[] -->

*diū* — "to lose (a thing)".

> *wǒ de yàoshi diū le* — I've lost my keys.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diū* is the everyday verb for losing a thing. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for what I have and can do.
