## You'll want to know: فرز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C464-TALAWWUTH464-05]; assesses=[] -->

**فرز** — *faraza* — "to sort".

> فرزت أمي القمامة في المطبخ.
>
> *farazat ummī al-qumāma fī al-maṭbakh.*
>
> My mother sorted the rubbish in the kitchen.

Present: يفرز, *yafriz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فرز** (*faraza*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-z*, "to separate, to set apart" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
