## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಕ್ರಿಯೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C422-MANADANDA422-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಕ್ರಿಯೆ** — *prakriye* — "a process".

> ಮನೆ ಕೊಳ್ಳುವ ಪ್ರಕ್ರಿಯೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಉದ್ದ.
>
> *mane koḷḷuva prakriye tumbā udda.*
>
> The process of buying a house is very long.

ಪ್ರಕ್ರಿಯೆ ಮುಗಿಸು, to complete a process.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಕ್ರಿಯೆ** (*prakriye*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prakriyā*, "production; procedure, way, manner" (Monier-Williams).
