## You'll want to know: うけいれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C322-SHOUCHI322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うけいれる** — *ukeireru* — "to accept; to take in".

> **みんなが さんせいしたので、かいしゃは ていあんを うけいれました。** — *minna ga sansei shita node, kaisha wa teian o ukeiremashita* — "Everyone agreed, so the company accepted the proposal."

Polite **うけいれます**, *ukeiremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **かいしゃが うけいれました。**, *kaisha ga ukeiremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うけいれる** (*ukeireru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *ukeru*, "to receive" and *ireru*, "to put in", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
