Punjabi, chapter 221: I Will Go.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
Return to three familiar futures — retrieve the familiar will be, will come and we will meet by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: hovegā; āvegā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਹੋਵੇਗਾ, ਆਵੇਗਾ, "will be; will come". Then the farewell ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ, "we will meet again".

Grammar Lens.
All three end in -ਗਾ or -ਗੇ, the future. ਹੋਵੇਗੀ and ਆਵੇਗੀ have -ਗੀ for a feminine subject. ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ is "we will meet". The next lesson gives you I will go.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: Aman will come]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਆਵੇਗਾ.
[your turn — hear: the train will come]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਰੇਲ ਆਵੇਗੀ.
[your turn — hear: we will meet tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say will come, then we will meet.


Lesson 2 of 6.
ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ, ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ — I will go.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say Aman will come. Then we will meet tomorrow.

Grammar Lens: ਮੈਂ in the future.
After ਜਾ comes ਵ, as in ਆਵੇਗਾ. With ਮੈਂ the ending is -ਾਂਗਾ for a man and -ਾਂਗੀ for a woman:
ਜਾ + ਵ + ਾਂਗਾ becomes ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ.
ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ। — maiṁ kallh sakūl jāvāṅgā. — I will go to school tomorrow (a man speaking).
ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ। — maiṁ kallh sakūl jāvāṅgī. — I will go to school tomorrow (a woman speaking).
With a future form, ਕੱਲ੍ਹ means tomorrow.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: I will go home tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: I will go to the market]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ। or ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ]
Point to ਵ, then to ਗਾ.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I will go, as a man? (ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ.) As a woman? (ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ.)


Lesson 3 of 6.
Hear and say I will go again — retrieve I will go.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: jāvāṅgā; jāvāṅgī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ, ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: I will go to school tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: I went to school yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਗਿਆ। or ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਗਈ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: cover the model and write ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Same ਕੱਲ੍ਹ, two days: which verb makes it tomorrow? (The future.)


Lesson 4 of 6.
ਜਾਵੇਗਾ, ਜਾਵੇਗੀ — will go.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I will go tomorrow. Then Aman will come and the train will come.

Grammar Lens: like ਆਵੇਗਾ.
For one other person the ending is the one ਆਵੇਗਾ has: -ੇਗਾ for a man, -ੇਗੀ for a woman.
ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ। — Aman kallh ghar jāvegā. — Aman will go home tomorrow.
ਕੁੜੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ। — kuṛī kallh sakūl jāvegī. — The girl will go to school tomorrow.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: he will go to the market]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ।.
[your turn — hear: the woman will go home tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਔਰਤ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਜਾਵੇਗਾ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
He will go? (ਜਾਵੇਗਾ.) She will go? (ਜਾਵੇਗੀ.)


Lesson 5 of 6.
ਅਸੀਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ (asīṁ jāvāṅge) — we will go.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say Aman will go home tomorrow. Then we will meet again.

Grammar Lens: the ending of ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ.
ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ has been "we will meet" since the early lessons. Its ending, -ਾਂਗੇ, is the one for ਅਸੀਂ:
ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ। — asīṁ kallh bāzār jāvāṅge. — We will go to the market tomorrow.
ਅਸੀਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ। — asīṁ savere jāvāṅge. — We will go in the morning.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: we will go home tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।.
[your turn — hear: I will go, and Aman will go]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ ਅਤੇ ਅਮਨ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅਸੀਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
We will go? (ਅਸੀਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ (asīṁ jāvāṅge).) We will meet? (ਅਸੀਂ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ.)


Lesson 6 of 6.
Tomorrow: a four-skill payoff — say who will go where tomorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I will go, she will go, we will go.

Guided Practice: four skills.
[your turn — hear: I will go to the market tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: she went yesterday; he will go tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਗਈ। ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।]
Give its meaning: we will go to school tomorrow.
[once you have stopped driving — write: cover the line and write ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ।]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say where you will go tomorrow, and who will go with you.
