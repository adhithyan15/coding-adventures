## You'll want to know: درد کشیدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C491-ACHES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**درد کشیدن** (*dard keshidan*) — "to suffer pain".

> او سالها درد کشید.
>
> *u sâlhâ dard keshid.*
>
> She suffered pain for years.

*dard* 'pain' + *keshidan* 'to pull, to bear'; present stem *kesh-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dard keshidan* is built on two inherited Persian words, *dard*, 'pain', and *keshidan*, 'to pull, to bear': to bear pain.
