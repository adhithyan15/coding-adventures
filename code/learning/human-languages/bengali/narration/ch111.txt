Bengali, chapter 111: Rope, Nail, Hammer, Axe, Umbrella.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
দড়ি (dôṛi) — a rope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for matches, then the Bengali for coal.

You'll want to know: দড়ি.
দড়ি — dôṛi — "a rope".

The word, taken apart.
দড়ি (dôṛi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dṛti, "skin, leather bag" (CDIAL 6511). CDIAL glosses Bengali daṛi "sinew, tendon, cord, string".
