## You'll want to know: स्वेच्छा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C543-HATHA543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वेच्छा** — *svecchā* — "free will".

> यदि सः स्वेच्छया आगच्छति, तर्हि वयं प्रसन्नाः स्मः।
>
> *yadi saḥ svecchayā āgacchati, tarhi vayaṃ prasannāḥ smaḥ.*
>
> If he comes of his own free will, we are glad.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वेच्छा** (*svecchā*) is built from *sva*, "own", and *icchā*, "a wish", from the root √iṣ, "to wish", which also gives *icchati*; MW gives *svecchā* as one's own wish, free will (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
