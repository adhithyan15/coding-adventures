## You'll want to know: aufdrehen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C427-KLIMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**aufdrehen** — "to turn up, to turn on (a tap, the heating)".

> Ihm ist kalt, deshalb dreht er die Heizung auf.
>
> *He is cold, so he turns the heating up.*

Separable: *ich drehe auf*, *ich habe aufgedreht*. *Das Radio aufdrehen* — to turn the radio up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aufdrehen* is built from *auf* 'up, open' and *drehen* 'to turn', an inherited Germanic verb (EtymDB lists Dutch *draaien* as a cousin).
