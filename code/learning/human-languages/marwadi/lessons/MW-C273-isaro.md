## You'll want to know: इसारो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C273-CHUP273-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इसारो** — *isāro* — "a gesture".

> मास्टर इसारो करै है अर छोरा चुप है।
>
> *māsṭar isāro karai hai ar chhorā chup hai.*
>
> The teacher makes a sign and the boys are quiet.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इसारो** (*isāro*) is usually traced, through Persian *ishāra*, to Arabic *ishāra*, "a sign"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.
