## You'll want to know: a perda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C395-LUCRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a perda** — "the loss".

> A empresa teve grandes perdas este ano.
>
> *The company had big losses this year.*

*Perda de tempo* — waste of time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perda* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *perdita* "a thing lost" (EtymDB), from *perdere* "to destroy, to lose" (Lewis & Short).
