## You'll want to know: جوش آمدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C358-QABLAME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جوش آمدن** (*jush âmadan*) — "to come to the boil".

> وقتی آب جوش آمد، برنج را در آن ریخت.
>
> *Vaqti âb jush âmad, berenj râ dar ân rikht.*
>
> When the water came to the boil, she put the rice in.

In speech also "to lose one's temper". Present *jush miâyad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jush* "boiling, a simmer" is a Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; *âmadan* "to come" is inherited Persian.
