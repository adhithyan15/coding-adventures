# बहाना (bahānā) — to wash away; to make (water) flow

## You'll want to know: बहाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C407-NAKA407-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बहाना** — *bahānā* — "to wash away; to make (water) flow".

> बाढ़ ने पुराना पुल बहा दिया।
>
> *bāṛh ne purānā pul bahā diyā.*
>
> The flood washed away the old bridge.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नाली में पानी बहाया, *usne nālī meṁ pānī bahāyā*.

A different word, the noun बहाना, an excuse, is spelled the same way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहाना** (*bahānā*) is the causative partner of बहना, "to flow", from Sanskrit *vahati*, "carries along" (CDIAL 11453): to make water flow, or to let it carry a thing away.
