## You'll want to know: shuōbudìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C244-STORM244-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shuōbudìng* — "perhaps; who knows".

> *shuōbudìng míngtiān huì xiàyǔ* — Who knows, it might rain tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Tone: 不 is light here, bu, with no tone of its own.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuōbudìng* is built from 说 'to say', 不 'not' and *dìng* 'fixed', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sywet*, *pjut* and *dengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last 'settled'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
