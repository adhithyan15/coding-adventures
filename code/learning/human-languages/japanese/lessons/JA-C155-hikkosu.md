## You'll want to know: ひっこす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C155-MOVE155-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ひっこす** — *hikkosu* — "to move house".

> **おじさんは よく ひっこします。** — *ojisan wa yoku hikkoshimasu* — "My uncle often moves house."

Polite **ひっこします**, *hikkoshimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひっこす** (*hikkosu*) is native Japanese, built from *hiki*, "pulling", the stem of *hiku*, and *kosu*, "to cross over", joined with a held *k*; both parts are inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
