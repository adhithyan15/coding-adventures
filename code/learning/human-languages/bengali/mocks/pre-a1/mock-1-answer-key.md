## Writing

1. After the card has been removed, the recalled word is **নমস্কার**.
2. After the card has been removed, the recalled word is **নাম**.
3. The dictated word is **আমি**.
4. The dictated word is **পানি**.
5. The dictated word is **বাংলা**.
6. The dictated word is **ধন্যবাদ**.
7. Accept a chosen identity independently written in recognizable Bengali
   script. Do not require the learner to choose **রবি** or another model name.
8. Accept an appropriate independently written greeting, for example
   **নমস্কার**. A romanized or visibly copied answer is not independent writing.

Apply the script-specific matra-line, vowel-sign-side, and hasanta criteria in
the rubric rather than scoring handwriting by its resemblance to a typeface.

## Speaking

1. Accept a relevant greeting such as **নমস্কার**.
2. Accept a chosen identity in a comprehensible frame such as **আমার নাম ...**.
3. Accept a relevant state such as **আমি ভালো আছি**.
4. Accept a comprehensible place response; do not require a particular city.
5. Accept a relevant yes/no response; a short phrase is enough at pre-A1.
6. Accept a comprehensible polite request for **পানি**.

Examples guide raters; the learner need not reproduce a single fixed sentence.
