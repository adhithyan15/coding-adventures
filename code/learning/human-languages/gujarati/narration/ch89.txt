Gujarati, chapter 89: Questions and Answers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વિદ્યાર્થી (vidyārthī) — a student.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a letter, then the Gujarati for a gift.

You'll want to know: વિદ્યાર્થી.
વિદ્યાર્થી (vidyārthī) — "a student". Built on વિદ્યા, learning.

The word, taken apart.
વિદ્યાર્થી (vidyārthī) is a Sanskrit compound, taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama: vidyā, "knowledge", and arthin, "seeking". A student is one who seeks knowledge.

What you've built.
One more word for questions and answers.

The word, taken apart.
જવાબ (javāb) is a loan from Arabic jawāb, "an answer", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is j-w-b (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ભણવું (bhaṇvũ) — to study.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ભણવું.
ભણવું (bhaṇvũ) — "to study".

The word, taken apart.
ભણવું (bhaṇvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit bhaṇati, "calls aloud, speaks" (CDIAL 9383). CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb with the senses "to say, repeat, study", and notes that a Munda origin has also been proposed for the verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
શીખવવું (śīkhavvũ) — to teach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an answer, then the Gujarati for to study.

You'll want to know: શીખવવું.
શીખવવું (śīkhavvũ) — "to teach". The causative of શીખવું, to learn.

The word, taken apart.
શીખવવું (śīkhavvũ) is the causing form of śīkhvũ, "to learn": to teach is to make someone learn. CDIAL lists both under Sanskrit śikṣate, "learns" (CDIAL 12430).
