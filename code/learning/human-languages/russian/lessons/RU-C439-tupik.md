## You'll want to know: тупик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C439-RAZVILKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**тупик** (*tupík*) — "a dead end, a cul-de-sac; an impasse".

> Эта улица — тупик.
>
> *Éta úlitsa — tupík.*
>
> This street is a dead end.

Masculine; the stress moves: *тупика* (*tupiká*). Figurative: *в тупике* — at a dead end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тупик* is built from *тупой* 'blunt' and the ending *-ик* (EtymDB): a street with a blunt end.
