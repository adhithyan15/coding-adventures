## You'll want to know: ರಚಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C537-KAVYA537-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಚಿಸು** — *racisu* — "to compose; to create".

> ಈ ಹಾಡನ್ನು ಒಬ್ಬ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಕವಿ ರಚಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *ī hāḍannu obba haḷḷiya kavi racisidaru.*
>
> A village poet composed this song.

Past (I …): ರಚಿಸಿದೆ, *raciside*.

ರಚನೆ, a composition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಚಿಸು** (*racisu*) is built on the Sanskrit root *rac*, "to arrange, to compose", seen in *racanā* (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*.
