# Recording word bands must intersect every allowed pace with the duration window

The first Sanskrit A2 test used the widest mathematically possible word band:
minimum duration at the slowest pace through maximum duration at the fastest
pace. That admits a script which falls outside the duration window at another
permitted pace. Use the stricter intersection instead: minimum duration times
the fastest pace, and maximum duration times the slowest pace. For 110–130 wpm,
that makes 90–150 seconds equal 195–275 words and 120–180 seconds equal
260–330 words.
