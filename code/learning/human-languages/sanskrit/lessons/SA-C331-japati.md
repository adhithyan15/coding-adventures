## You'll want to know: जपति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C331-NADITIRA331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जपति** — *japati* — "chants softly; recites a prayer".

> वृद्धः नदीतीरे मन्त्रं जपति।
>
> *vṛddhaḥ nadītīre mantraṃ japati.*
>
> The old man softly chants a mantra on the riverbank.

Past (he …): सः मन्त्रं जपितवान्, *saḥ mantraṃ japitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जपति** (*japati*) comes from the root √jap, "to utter in a low voice, mutter prayers" (MW).
