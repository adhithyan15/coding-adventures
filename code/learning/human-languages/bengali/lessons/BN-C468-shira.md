## You'll want to know: শিরা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C468-HRITPINDO468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**শিরা** — *shirā* — "a vein".

> নার্সটি হাতের শিরায় ইনজেকশন দিলেন।
>
> *nārsṭi hāter shirāy injekshôn dilen.*
>
> The nurse gave the injection into a vein in the arm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শিরা** (*shirā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sirā* (also written *śirā*), "a vessel of the body, a vein" (Monier-Williams).
