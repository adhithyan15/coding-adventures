## You'll want to know: ਖੂੰਜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C247-GHR247-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੂੰਜਾ** — *khūnjā* — "a corner of a room".

> ਝਾੜੂ ਕਮਰੇ ਦੇ ਖੂੰਜੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *jhāṛū kamre de khūnje vich hai.*
>
> The broom is in the corner of the room.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੂੰਜਾ** (*khūnjā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khōñcā*, "curve, bend"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khū̃jā*, "corner" (CDIAL 3930).
