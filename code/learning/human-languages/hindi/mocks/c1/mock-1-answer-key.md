## Writing

The synthesis must accurately distinguish the observation report's increased
use, shade pattern, litter finding, and measurement limits from the coalition's
access and displacement concerns. It should identify agreement on more shade
and continued review, contrast aggregate observation with participatory and
qualitative evidence, and propose a combined evaluation without inventing
facts. The commentary must develop and qualify the uncertainty claim, distinguish
urgent action from later detail, address false certainty and paralysis, use two
public-decision contexts, and propose traceable revision. Score source fidelity,
selection, integration, argument, organisation, register, Hindi control, and
orthography; no fixed wording is required.

## Speaking

The presentation must distinguish packet evidence from its recommendation and
address priorities, fragile and contested cases, public accountability, and
education without assuming continued visibility. Collaborative synthesis must
compare the four launch choices and explicitly agree on scope, minimal fallback,
community checking, and a third-month stop/expand condition. Challenge and
defence requires supported positions, meaningful qualification or
reformulation, precise interaction, and a credible condition for revision.
Score only the learner's own contribution; no fixed position is required.
