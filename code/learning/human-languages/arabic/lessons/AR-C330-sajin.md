## You'll want to know: سجين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C330-SIJN330-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سجين** — *sajīn* — "a prisoner".

> هرب سجين من السجن ليلا.
>
> *haraba sajīn min as-sijn laylan.*
>
> A prisoner escaped from the prison at night.

Plural: سجناء, *sujanāʾ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سجين** (*sajīn*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-j-n* (Lane), "to confine". The *faʿīl* pattern gives the one confined.
