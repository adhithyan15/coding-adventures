## You'll want to know: جرثومة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C509-ADWA509-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جرثومة** — *jurthūma* — "a germ".

> يقتل الصابون الجراثيم.
>
> *yaqtulu aṣ-ṣābūn al-jarāthīm.*
>
> Soap kills germs.

Plural: جراثيم, *jarāthīm*.

It is mostly met in the plural جراثيم.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جرثومة** (*jurthūma*) is built on a native four-letter root, *j-r-th-m*, where it named the root or origin of a thing; modern Arabic gave it the sense of a germ. The origin of the root itself is not settled.
