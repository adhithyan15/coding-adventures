## You'll want to know: o equívoco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C343-ESCLARECER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o equívoco** — "the misunderstanding, the mistake".

> Houve um equívoco: eu disse terça, não quinta.
>
> *There was a misunderstanding: I said Tuesday, not Thursday.*

*Por equívoco* — by mistake. As an adjective, ambiguous: *uma frase equívoca*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*equívoco* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *aequivocus* "of like significations, ambiguous" (Lewis & Short), built on *aequus* "equal" and *vocāre* "to call": one name that calls two things.
