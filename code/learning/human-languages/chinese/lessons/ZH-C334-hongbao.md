## You'll want to know: 红包
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C334-REDENV334-02]; assesses=[] -->

**红包** — *hóngbāo* — "red envelope".

> **过年的时候外公给了我一个红包** *guònián de shíhou wàigōng gěi le wǒ yí ge hóngbāo* — At the new year my grandpa gave me a red envelope.

The new character, in pieces: 红 — six strokes: on the left the silk piece of 给, three strokes; on the right 工. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**红包** (*hóngbāo*) is built from 红 'red' and 包 'a packet', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwng* and *paew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a colour' and the second 'to wrap'.
