French, chapter 507: Le tournoi.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le tournoi — tournament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for (se qualifier) to qualify, then the French for winner.

You'll want to know: le tournoi.
le tournoi — "tournament".
Notre école organise un tournoi de basket. Our school is organising a basketball tournament.
In the Middle Ages, a contest between knights.

The word, taken apart.
le tournoi is built on tournoyer "to wheel about", from tourner "to turn", which comes from Latin tornāre "to turn on a lathe" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: la médaille.
la médaille — "medal".
Elle a gagné une médaille d'argent. She won a silver medal.
Le revers de la médaille — the other side of the coin.

The word, taken apart.
la médaille was borrowed from Italian medaglia (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin metallum "metal, a mine", from Greek métallon.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Before the new one: say the French for medal, then the French for podium.

You'll want to know: le champion.
le champion — "champion".
Il est champion de natation de sa région. He is the swimming champion of his region.
A woman is la championne.

The word, taken apart.
le champion is inherited from Medieval Latin campiō "a fighter in a duel" (EtymDB), a Germanic word built on the word for a battlefield, related to Latin campus "field".

You'll want to know: affronter.
affronter — "to face, to take on".
Demain, notre équipe affronte les champions. Tomorrow our team takes on the champions.
S'affronter — to face each other.

The word, taken apart.
affronter comes from Old French afronter "to face" (EtymDB), built on front "forehead", from Latin frōns (Lewis & Short): to stand forehead to forehead.
