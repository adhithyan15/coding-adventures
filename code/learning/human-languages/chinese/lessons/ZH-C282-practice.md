## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-SHOULD-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-YINGGAI-01, ZH-LEX-C282-YINGGAI-01, ZH-LEX-C282-GAI-01, ZH-LEX-C282-ZUIHAO-01, ZH-LEX-C282-DEI-01, ZH-LEX-C280-HAOXIANG-01, ZH-LEX-C50-SLEEP50-05, ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-04, ZH-LEX-C79-SCHOOL79-01] -->

> **A:** *wǒ hǎoxiàng bìng le* — I think I'm ill.
>
> **B:** 你应该去看医生
>
> *nǐ yīnggāi qù kàn yīshēng* — You should see a doctor.
>
> **A:** 我得去上课
>
> *wǒ děi qù shàngkè* — I have to go to class.
>
> **B:** 你病了 不应该去上课
>
> *nǐ bìng le, bù yīnggāi qù shàngkè* — You're ill. You shouldn't go to class.
>
> **A:** 那我该做什么
>
> *nà wǒ gāi zuò shénme* — Then what should I do?
>
> **B:** 你最好在家
>
> *nǐ zuìhǎo zài jiā* — You'd better stay at home.
>
> **A:** 好 我该回家了
>
> *hǎo, wǒ gāi huí jiā le* — All right. Time I went home.

Four words of advice and duty, in rising order of force: 最好 is wise, 应该 is right, 该 says the time has come, *děi* allows no choice.

## What you've built — advice and duty
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-SHOULD-01, ZH-SCRIPT-YING-01, ZH-SCRIPT-GAI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YINGGAI-01, ZH-LEX-C282-ZUIHAO-01, ZH-LEX-C282-DEI-01, ZH-LEX-C282-GAI-01] -->

Four words before the verb, each with its own force:

- **你应该去看医生** *nǐ yīnggāi qù kàn yīshēng* — what is right.
- **你最好在家** *nǐ zuìhǎo zài jiā* — what is wise.
- **我该回家了** *wǒ gāi huí jiā le* — the time has come.
- **我得去上课** *wǒ děi qù shàngkè* — no choice.

**Listen.** *Nǐ juéde wǒ yīnggāi qù ma?*

**Speak.** Give a friend two pieces of advice about the weekend, one with 应该 and one with 最好.

[YOU WRITE: 应该 — the roof of 店 first, then the right of 孩 after the left of 说]
