## You'll want to know: spazzare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C532-PULIZIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**spazzare** — "to sweep".

> Dopo cena spazzo la cucina.
>
> *After dinner I sweep the kitchen.*

With *la scopa* "the broom".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spazzare* is usually traced to Late Latin *spatiāre* (EtymDB), from *spatium* "space": to clear a space.
