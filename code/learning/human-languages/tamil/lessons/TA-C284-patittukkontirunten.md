# படித்துக்கொண்டிருந்தேன் (paṭittukkoṇṭiruntēṉ) — what was going on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-PERSON-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-STEMS-01, TA-LEX-C44-ASK-05, TA-LEX-PADI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I read a book". (*nāṉ oru puttakam paṭittēṉ*.) Then "I was", on its own. (*iruntēṉ*.)

[PAUSE 1s] The first is an event: it happened and it was over. A story also needs what was going on at the time.

## You'll want to know: -கொண்டிருந்தேன், 'I was doing'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C284-ONGOING-PAST-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-02, TA-LEX-IRAVU-01, TA-LEX-NUMBERS-6-10-01, TA-ETYMON-MANI-01, TA-LEX-IRU-01] -->

> **நேற்று இரவு ஒன்பது மணிக்கு நான் ஒரு புத்தகம் படித்துக்கொண்டிருந்தேன்.**
>
> *nēṟṟu iravu oṉpatu maṇikku nāṉ oru puttakam paṭittukkoṇṭiruntēṉ.* — "At nine last night I was reading a book."

It is three short words in one: **படித்து**, "having read", **கொண்டு**, "holding", and **இருந்தேன்**, "I was". The person ending is the ending of **இருந்தேன்**, so it changes the same way. "He was reading": **அவன்** **படித்துக்கொண்டிருந்தான்**. "She was talking": **அவள்** **பேசிக்கொண்டிருந்தாள்** (*pēcikkoṇṭiruntāḷ*).

For "it": **மழை பெய்துகொண்டிருந்தது** (*maḻai peytukoṇṭiruntatu*), "it was raining". **மழை** (*maḻai*, "rain") **பெய்** (*pey*, "falls").

The first piece ends in **-உ** or **-இ**: **படித்து**, **பேசி**. Take each one whole for now; the story lessons that follow take it apart.

## Grammar Lens: the scene and the event
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C79-WHEN-02, TA-LEX-KUDUMBAM-02, TA-LEX-VAA-01] -->

> **நான் படித்துக்கொண்டிருந்தேன். அப்போது அம்மா வந்தார்.**
>
> *nāṉ paṭittukkoṇṭiruntēṉ. appōtu ammā vantār.* — "I was reading. At that moment, Mother came."

The first sentence is the **scene**: it was going on. **வந்தார்** is the **event**: it happened once and cut into the scene. **அப்போது**, "at that time", hooks one onto the other. A told story moves on this rhythm.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C284-ONGOING-PAST-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I was reading"? (**படித்துக்கொண்டிருந்தேன்**.) "It was raining"? (**மழை பெய்துகொண்டிருந்தது**.) Which three words make the long one? (**படித்து**, **கொண்டு**, **இருந்தேன்**.) Which past is the scene? (**-கொண்டிருந்தேன்**.) Which is the event? (**The plain past**: **வந்தார்**.)
