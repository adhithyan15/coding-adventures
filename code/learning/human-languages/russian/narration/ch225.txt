Russian, chapter 225: Crocodile, Giraffe, Zebra, Camel, Deer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
крокодил (krokodíl) — a crocodile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bush, then the Russian for a sheep.

You'll want to know: крокодил.
крокодил (krokodíl) — "a crocodile".

The word, taken apart.
крокодил (krokodíl) is a western loan, perhaps through German Krokodil, from Latin crocodīlus, Greek krokódeilos; Old Russian korkodilŭ came straight from Greek (Vasmer).

The word, taken apart.
жираф (zhiráf) came through French girafe, and the older жирафа through German or Italian, from Arabic zurāfa, "giraffe" (Vasmer).

Lesson 4 of 5.
верблюд (verblyúd) — a camel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a giraffe, then the Russian for a zebra.

You'll want to know: верблюд.
верблюд (verblyúd) — "a camel".

The word, taken apart.
верблюд (verblyúd) — Old Russian velĭbludŭ; an old loan from Gothic ulbandus, reshaped after велий, "great" (Vasmer). The Gothic word goes back to Greek eléphas, "elephant": the name passed from one large beast to another.

Lesson 5 of 5.
олень (olén) — a deer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a zebra, then the Russian for a camel.

You'll want to know: олень.
олень (olén) — "a deer".

The word, taken apart.
олень (olén) from Proto-Slavic olenĭ — inherited. Vasmer lists Lithuanian élnis, "deer, elk", Greek élaphos, "deer", and Welsh elain, "hind".
