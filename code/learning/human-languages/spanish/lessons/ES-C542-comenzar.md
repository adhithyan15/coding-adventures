## You'll want to know: comenzar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C542-COMIENZO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**comenzar** — "to begin, to start".

> La película comenzó a las nueve.
>
> *The film began at nine.*

Stem change: *comienza*, *comienzan*. A little more formal than *empezar*. *Comenzar a* + infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comenzar* is inherited from spoken Latin *\*cominitiāre* (EtymDB), built on *com-* and *initiāre* 'to begin', from *initium* 'a beginning'.
