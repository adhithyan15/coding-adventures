## You'll want to know: a integração

**a integração** — "the integration".

> A língua ajuda muito na integração.
>
> *The language helps a lot with integration.*

*Integrar-se* — to fit in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*integração* is a learned borrowing from Latin *integrātiō* "a renewing, a restoring" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *integrāre* "to make whole", on *integer* "whole, untouched".
