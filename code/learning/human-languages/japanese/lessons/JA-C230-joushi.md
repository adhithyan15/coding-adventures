## You'll want to know: じょうし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C230-BOSS230-02]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうし** — *jōshi* — "a boss; one's superior".

> **じょうしは いつも ていねいに おしえます。** — *jōshi wa itsumo teinei ni oshiemasu* — "My boss always explains things carefully."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうし** (*jōshi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jō*, "above" (Middle Chinese *dzyangH*) and *shi*, "official" (*si*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
