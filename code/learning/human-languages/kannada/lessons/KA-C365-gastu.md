## You'll want to know: ಗಸ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C365-HODEDATA365-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಸ್ತು** — *gastu* — "a patrol; a beat".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಬೀದಿಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಗಸ್ತು ತಿರುಗುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *rātri polīsaru bīdigaḷalli gastu tiruguttāre.*
>
> At night the police patrol the streets.

Police on their round are ಗಸ್ತಿನಲ್ಲಿ, on patrol.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಸ್ತು** (*gastu*) is a loan from Persian *gašt*, "a walk, a round, a patrol", which came through Hindustani.
