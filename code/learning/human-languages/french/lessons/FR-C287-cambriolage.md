## You'll want to know: le cambriolage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C287-CAMBRIOLAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le cambriolage** — "the burglary, the break-in".

> Le cambriolage a eu lieu pendant la nuit.
>
> *The burglary took place during the night.*

Said of a home, a shop or an office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cambriolage* is built on *cambrioler* "to burgle" with the noun suffix *-age* (EtymDB).
