# रसोई मांय, मेज माथै — in and on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C81-PLACE81-03, MW-LEX-C96-LOC96-05, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSS-AGREE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *inside* and *on top*: **मांय**, **माथै**. Then *my cow is here*.

## Grammar Lens: a noun in front
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-LOC-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C81-PLACE81-03, MW-LEX-C96-LOC96-05, MW-LEX-C65-HOME65-02, MW-LEX-C62-HOME62-03, MW-LEX-C65-HOME65-04, MW-LEX-C155-FOOD155-01] -->

Put a noun in front of **मांय** and **माथै**, and they say *in* and *on*:

> **दूध रसोई मांय है।**
>
> *dūdh rasoī mā̃y hai.* — The milk is in the kitchen.

> **किताब मेज माथै है।**
>
> *kitāb mej māthai hai.* — The book is on the table.

**कनै**, *near*, works the same: **घर कनै**, near the house. Old Marwari already
had *kanai*, "near", and *māhī*, "in, into" (CDIAL 2830, 9804).

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: **दुकान बाजार मांय है।** — *dukān bājār mā̃y hai.* — The shop is in the market.]
- [YOU SAY: **चाय मेज माथै है।** — *chāy mej māthai hai.* — The tea is on the table.]
- [YOU SAY: **वो घर कनै है।** — *vo ghar kanai hai.* — He is near the house.]
