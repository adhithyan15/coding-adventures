# بڑبڑانا (baṛbaṛānā) — to mutter; to grumble

## You'll want to know: بڑبڑانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C463-TALK463-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بڑبڑانا** — *baṛbaṛānā* — "to mutter; to grumble".

> دادا اکیلے بیٹھ کر بڑبڑاتے ہیں۔
>
> *dādā akele baiṭh kar baṛbaṛāte haiṅ.*
>
> My grandfather sits on his own and mutters.

Past (he …): وہ کچھ بڑبڑایا, *voh kuchh baṛbaṛāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بڑبڑانا** (*baṛbaṛānā*) is inherited from *baḍabaḍa*, "grumble, mutter", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as an imitation of the sound (CDIAL 9122).
