## You'll want to know: loben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C497-TROTZIG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**loben** — "to praise".

> Die Lehrerin lobt den Jungen für seine Arbeit.
>
> *The teacher praises the boy for his work.*

The noun: *das Lob* — praise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*loben* is inherited from Middle High German *loben*, from Proto-Germanic *lubōną* "to praise" (EtymDB).
