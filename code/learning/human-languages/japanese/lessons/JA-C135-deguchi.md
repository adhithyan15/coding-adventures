## You'll want to know: でぐち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C135-DEGUCHI]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-CHI-01] -->

**でぐち** — *deguchi* — "an exit, the way out".

The new sign is spent at once, beside one from the last chapter: **で**, then
**ぐ** and **ち**, three beats, *de–gu–chi*. The *guchi* is the same voiced
"mouth" as in **いりぐち**. On a sign, the two words face each other: the way
in, and the way out. You write every sign in both now.
