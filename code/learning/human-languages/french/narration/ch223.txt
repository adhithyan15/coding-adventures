5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la batterie — a battery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a court, then the French for a judge.

You'll want to know: la batterie.
la batterie — "a battery".

The word, taken apart.
batterie — built from the verb for "to beat", from Latin battuere, with -erie (EtymDB 2.1): guns firing together, then cells working together.
