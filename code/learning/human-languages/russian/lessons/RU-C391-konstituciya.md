## You'll want to know: конституция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C391-ZAKONODATELSTVO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**конституция** (*konstitútsiya*) — "a constitution".

> Конституция — главный закон страны.
>
> *Konstitútsiya — glávnyy zakón straný.*
>
> The constitution is the main law of the country.

Feminine. It also means a person’s build.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**конституция** is usually traced, through western languages, to Latin *cōnstitūtiō* "an arrangement, a decree" (Lewis & Short), from *cōnstituere* "to set up, to establish".
