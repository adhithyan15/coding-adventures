## You'll want to know: zhāodài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C361-GUEST361-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāodài* — "to look after your guests; to entertain".

> *tāmen zhāodài kèrén hěn rèqíng* — They're very warm hosts.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāodài* is built from *zhāo* and *dài*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyew* and *dojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to beckon' and the second 'to wait'.
