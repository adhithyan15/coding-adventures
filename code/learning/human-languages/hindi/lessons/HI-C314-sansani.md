## You'll want to know: सनसनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C314-AFVAH314-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सनसनी** — *sansanī* — "a sensation; a thrill".

> उस ख़बर से पूरे देश में सनसनी फैल गई।
>
> *us khabar se pūre deś meṁ sansanī phail gaī.*
>
> That news caused a sensation across the whole country.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सनसनी** (*sansanī*) belongs with *sansanānā*, to hum or tingle, which CDIAL 13901 sets under Sanskrit *svana*, "a noise", noting that many of these words are imitative: a stir that runs through people like a buzz.
