## You'll want to know: zhùyì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C115-CAREFUL115-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhùyì* — "to pay attention; to take care".

> *qǐng zhùyì nǐ de bāo* — Please keep an eye on your bag.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhùyì* is built from zhù 'to pour into' and yì 'mind', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyuH* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): to pour the mind into something.
