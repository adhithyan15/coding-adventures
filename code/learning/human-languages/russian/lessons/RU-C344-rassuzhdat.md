## You'll want to know: рассуждать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C344-LOGIKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**рассуждать** (*rassuzhdát*) — "to reason, to think aloud".

> Он любит рассуждать о жизни.
>
> *On lyúbit rassuzhdát o zhízni.*
>
> He likes to reason about life.

Imperfective only. *Рассуждать о* + prepositional.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**рассуждать** is built from *рас-* "apart" and *судить* "to judge", on *суд*, inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*sǫdŭ* (EtymDB). To reason is to take a question apart and judge it.
