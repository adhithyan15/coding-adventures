## You'll want to know: ubbidire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C498-BAMBINI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ubbidire** — "to obey".

> Il cane ubbidisce solo a mio padre.
>
> *The dog only obeys my father.*

An *-isc-* verb with *a*: *ubbidire ai genitori*. Also *obbedire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ubbidire* is inherited from Latin *oboedīre* "to listen to, to obey" (EtymDB), built on *audīre* "to hear" (Lewis & Short).
