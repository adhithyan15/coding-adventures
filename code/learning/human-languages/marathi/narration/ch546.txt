Marathi, chapter 546: Firm Intentions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संकल्प (saṅkalpa) — a resolution; a vow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to conjecture, then the Marathi for to forecast.

You'll want to know: संकल्प.
संकल्प — saṅkalpa — "a resolution; a vow".
नवीन वर्षी मी रोज चालण्याचा संकल्प केला. navīn varṣī mī roj cālṇyācā saṅkalpa kelā. In the new year I resolved to walk every day.
Masculine.
संकल्प (saṅkalpa) करणे, to make a resolution.

The word, taken apart.
संकल्प (saṅkalpa) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit saṁkalpa, "resolve, intention" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for determination, then the Marathi for an intention.

You'll want to know: शपथ घेणे.
शपथ घेणे — śapath gheṇe — "to take an oath".
नव्या महापौरांनी सभेत शपथ घेतली. navyā mahāpaurānnī sabhet śapath ghetlī. The new mayor took the oath at the meeting.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने शपथ घेतली, tyāne śapath ghetlī.

The word, taken apart.
शपथ घेणे (śapath gheṇe) is the tatsama शपथ, from Sanskrit śapatha, "a curse, an oath" (Monier-Williams), with घेणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
धजणे (dhajṇe) — to dare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an intention, then the Marathi for to take an oath.

You'll want to know: धजणे.
धजणे — dhajṇe — "to dare".
अंधारात एकट्याने जायला तो धजला नाही. andhārāt ekṭyāne jāylā to dhajlā nāhī. He did not dare to go alone in the dark.
Past (he …): तो धजला, to dhajlā.
It takes the -ायला form: बोलायला धजणे (dhajṇe), to dare to speak.

The word, taken apart.
धजणे (dhajṇe) is a Marathi verb heard mostly with a negative; its origin is not settled.
