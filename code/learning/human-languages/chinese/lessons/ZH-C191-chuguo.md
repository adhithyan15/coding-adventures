## You'll want to know: 出国
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C191-BACK191-04]; assesses=[] -->

**出国** — *chūguó* — "to go abroad".

> **我明年要出国** *wǒ míngnián yào chūguó* — I'm going abroad next year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**出国** (*chūguó*) is built from 出 'to go out' and 国 'country', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *kwok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
