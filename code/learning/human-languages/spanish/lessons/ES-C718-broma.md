## You'll want to know: la broma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C718-SONRISA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la broma** — "the joke, the prank".

> Era una broma, no te enfades.
>
> *It was a joke, do not be angry.*

*Gastar una broma* — to play a joke. *En broma* — jokingly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*broma* comes from Greek *brôma* "food" (EtymDB), through Latin. It first named the shipworm that eats ship timber, then any nuisance, and at last a joke.
