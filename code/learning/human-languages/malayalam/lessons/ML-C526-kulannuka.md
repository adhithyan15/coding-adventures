# കുഴങ്ങുക (kuḻaṅṅuka) — to be stumped; to be at a loss

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to burst out laughing, then the Malayalam for confusion.

## You'll want to know: കുഴങ്ങുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C526-RASAM526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കുഴങ്ങുക** — *kuḻaṅṅuka* — "to be stumped; to be at a loss".

> ചോദ്യം കേട്ട് കുട്ടി കുഴങ്ങി.
>
> *cōdyaṁ kēṭṭŭ kuṭṭi kuḻaṅṅi.*
>
> The child was stumped by the question.

Past: കുഴങ്ങി, *kuḻaṅṅi*.

Often കുഴങ്ങിപ്പോയി, was left at a loss. ആശയക്കുഴപ്പം shares its root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുഴങ്ങുക** (*kuḻaṅṅuka*) is native Dravidian, the verb beside കുഴപ്പം, "intricacy, trouble" (DEDR 1817).
