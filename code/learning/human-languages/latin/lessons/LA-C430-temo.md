## You'll want to know: tēmō, tēmōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C430-TEMO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**tēmō, tēmōnis** — "a cart pole, the shaft of a wagon or plough".

> Agricola bovēs ad tēmōnem plaustrī iungit.
>
> *The farmer harnesses the oxen to the pole of the wagon.*

Third declension, masculine: *tēmō, tēmōnis m.* The long beam that runs forward from the cart to the yoke.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tēmō* is inherited. Lewis & Short connect it with the root *tek-* of *texō* 'to weave, to build', and EtymDB lists *texō* as a cousin; the exact path is not settled.
