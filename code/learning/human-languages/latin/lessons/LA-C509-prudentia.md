## You'll want to know: prūdentia, prūdentiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C509-FORT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prūdentia, prūdentiae** — "good sense, prudence".

> Prūdentiā patris familia servātur.
>
> *The family is saved by the father's good sense.*

Feminine, first declension, from *prūdēns*, a short form of *prōvidēns* — foreseeing. English *prudence*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**prūdentia** is built on *prūdēns* "wise", a shortened *prōvidēns* "foreseeing" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB links it with *prōvidentia*). English *prudence* comes from it.
