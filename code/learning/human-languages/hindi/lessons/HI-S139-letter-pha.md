# फ — the fourth breath pair

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-138] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: **ग** — what sound does it carry?

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **प**, then say it again with a puff of air behind it.

## Script you'll notice: फ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-139]; assesses=[] -->

**फ** — *pha*.

It is the **breathy partner of प**, the fourth such pair you have met.

What it is made of:

- a left stem joined through the lower bowl to a rising and retraced central stem
- a separate clockwise right arch
- the top shirorekhā

## Script: four pairs, one idea
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-139, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-138] -->

| plain | breathy |
|---|---|
| त | थ |
| च | छ |
| ज | झ |
| **प** | **फ** |

Four pairs now. **The distinction was learned once and each pair since has cost
only a shape.**

Like **ग**, this one is late: **फिर** — *again*, in *see you again* — has been
yours since the fourth chapter, and the letter opening it went unnamed until
now.

## Writing: फ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-139] -->

- **1.** descend the left stem, curve right around the lower bowl, rise along the central side to the headline, then retrace down the central stem without lifting
- **2.** lift at the central junction and sweep clockwise over and down through the open right arch
- **3.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 2.** The pen comes up 2 times and no more.

> Verified three-stroke teaching form; another learner source stages the components differently.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: JackPotte, ‘Devanagari pʰ फ.gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 29 March 2009.
