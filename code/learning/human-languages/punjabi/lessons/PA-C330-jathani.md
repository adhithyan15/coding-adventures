## You'll want to know: ਜਠਾਣੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C330-SPOUSE330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਠਾਣੀ** — *jaṭhāṇī* — "a husband's elder brother's wife".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਜਠਾਣੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਸਿਆਣੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī jaṭhāṇī bahut siāṇī hai.*
>
> My husband's elder brother's wife is very wise.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਠਾਣੀ** (*jaṭhāṇī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jyeṣṭhajāni*, "a senior wife; an elder brother's wife"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jaṭhāṇī*, "husband's elder brother's wife", under it (CDIAL 5287).
