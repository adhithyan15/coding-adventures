## You'll want to know: పీట
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C546-INTLO546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పీట** — *pīṭa* — "a low wooden stool".

> అమ్మమ్మ పీట మీద కూర్చుని కూరగాయలు కోసింది.
>
> *ammamma pīṭa mīda kūrcuni kūragāyalu kōsindi.*
>
> Grandma sat on a low stool and cut the vegetables.

The low plank seat for eating or working on the floor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పీట** (*pīṭa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pīṭha*, "a stool, a bench" (CDIAL 8222).
