## You'll want to know: despedir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C356-DESPEDIMENTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**despedir** — "to fire, to dismiss; (despedir-se) to say goodbye".

> A fábrica despediu cinquenta trabalhadores.
>
> *The factory fired fifty workers.*

*Despedir-se de alguém* — to say goodbye to someone. Conjugated *despeço, despedes, despede*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*despedir* is formed from Latin *dē* and *expetere* "to seek after, to ask for" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *petere* "to seek"; it first meant "to ask leave to go", and then "to send away".
