## You'll want to know: തണൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C495-MALA495-05]; assesses=[] -->

**തണൽ** — *taṇal* — "shade".

> ഉച്ചയ്ക്ക് ഞങ്ങൾ ഒരു വലിയ മരത്തിന്റെ തണലിൽ ഇരുന്നു.
>
> *uccaykkŭ ñaṅṅaḷ oru valiya marattinṟe taṇalil irunnu.*
>
> At noon we sat in the shade of a big tree.

Shade is a kind of coolness: the word comes from the same root as തണുപ്പ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തണൽ** (*taṇal*) is native Dravidian, from the root of തണുപ്പ്, cold; DEDR lists it as shade, a shady spot (DEDR 3045).
