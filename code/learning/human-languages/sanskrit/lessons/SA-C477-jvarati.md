## You'll want to know: ज्वरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C477-BHRAMA477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वरति** — *jvarati* — "runs a fever".

> शीतेन बालः रात्रौ ज्वरति।
>
> *śītena bālaḥ rātrau jvarati.*
>
> After the cold the child runs a fever at night.

Past (he …): सः रात्रौ ज्वरितवान्, *saḥ rātrau jvaritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वरति** (*jvarati*) comes from the root √jvar, "to be hot, to be feverish", the root of **ज्वरः** (MW).
