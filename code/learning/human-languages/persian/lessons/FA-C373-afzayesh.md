## You'll want to know: افزایش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C373-AFZAYESH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**افزایش** (*afzâyesh*) — "an increase, a rise".

> افزایش قیمت نان همه را نگران کرد.
>
> *afzâyesh-e qeymat-e nân hame râ negarân kard.*
>
> The rise in the price of bread worried everyone.

From *afzâ-*, the present stem of *afzudan* "to add, to increase". The opposite is *kâhesh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افزایش** is native Persian, built on *afzâ-*, the present stem of *afzudan* "to add, to increase", with the noun suffix *-yesh*.
