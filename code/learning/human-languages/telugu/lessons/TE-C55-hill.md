## You'll want to know: గుట్ట
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C55-ROAD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గుట్ట** (*guṭṭa*) — "a low hill".

గుట్ట is inherited, and it means the small rocky rise that the Deccan is covered in — a heap of boulders rather than a peak. It also means a heap of anything: a గుట్ట of grain, a గుట్ట of clothes.

That double sense is honest about the landscape. These hills look piled rather than pushed up, and a speaker naming one is naming a pile.

The borrowed పర్వతం is the big mountain, the one in a poem. Nobody calls the rise behind their village a పర్వతం.
