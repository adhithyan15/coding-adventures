Keep two scores. **Listening** goes from sound to meaning; **speaking** goes from
meaning to sound. A strong score in one cannot cover a miss in the other. The
third row is not new material — it is the eight time words of the day-and-its-times chapter, returning about thirty lessons on at their third spacing window.
