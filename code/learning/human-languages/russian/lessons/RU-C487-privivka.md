## You'll want to know: прививка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C487-RASH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**прививка** (*privívka*) — "a vaccination, a jab".

> Перед зимой мы сделали прививку от гриппа.
>
> *Péred zimóy my sdélali privívku ot gríppa.*
>
> Before the winter we had a flu jab.

Feminine. *Сделать прививку от* + genitive — to be vaccinated against.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прививка* is built on *прививать* 'to graft, to inoculate', from *вить* 'to twist, to weave', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *viti* (EtymDB).
