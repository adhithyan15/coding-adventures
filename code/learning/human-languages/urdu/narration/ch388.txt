Urdu, chapter 388: Signals and Crossings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سگنل (signal) — a signal (for trains or traffic).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to haul, then the Urdu for to seat (someone).

You'll want to know: سگنل.
سگنل — signal — "a signal (for trains or traffic)".
سگنل لال تھا، اس لیے گاڑی رک گئی۔ signal lāl thā, is liye gāṛī ruk gaī. The signal was red, so the train stopped.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
سگنل (signal) is borrowed from English signal.

گاڑی آ رہی تھی، اس لیے پھاٹک بند تھا۔ gāṛī ā rahī thī, is liye phāṭak band thā. A train was coming, so the level crossing was shut.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تاکنا (tāknā) — to gaze (at); to look out (at).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a gate, then the Urdu for a berth (on a train).

You'll want to know: تاکنا.
تاکنا — tāknā — "to gaze (at); to look out (at)".
سارا کھڑکی سے کھیتوں کو تاکتی ہے۔ sārā khiṛkī se khetoṅ ko tāktī hai. Sara gazes out of the window at the fields.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کھڑکی سے باہر تاکا, us ne khiṛkī se bāhar tākā.

The word, taken apart.
تاکنا (tāknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin tāknā, "to look for", under Sanskrit tarkayati, "guesses, thinks" (CDIAL 5716); the sense "to gaze at" probably grew from looking out for something.
