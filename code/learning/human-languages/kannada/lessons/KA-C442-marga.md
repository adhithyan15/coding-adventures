## You'll want to know: ಮಾರ್ಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C442-HEDDARI442-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾರ್ಗ** — *mārga* — "a route; a way".

> ಈ ಬಸ್ಸಿನ ಮಾರ್ಗ ಬದಲಾಯಿತು.
>
> *ī bassina mārga badalāyitu.*
>
> The route of this bus has changed.

ಮಾರ್ಗ is the formal word; ದಾರಿ is the everyday way or path.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾರ್ಗ** (*mārga*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mārga*, "a track, a road" (CDIAL 10071), first the trail of a wild animal.
