## You'll want to know: ನಿಧಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C348-NIDHI348-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿಧಿ** — *nidhi* — "a treasure".

> ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹುಡುಗರಿಗೆ ಗುಹೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಧಿ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು.
>
> *katheyalli huḍugarige guheyalli nidhi sikkitu.*
>
> In the story the boys found a treasure in a cave.

Plural: ನಿಧಿಗಳು, *nidhigaḷu*.

Also a fund: ಕಲ್ಯಾಣ ನಿಧಿ, a welfare fund.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿಧಿ** (*nidhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nidhi*, "setting down, a hoard" (CDIAL 7207), from *ni-dhā*, "to put down".
