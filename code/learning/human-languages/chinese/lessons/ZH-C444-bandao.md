## You'll want to know: 半岛
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C444-ISLAND444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**半岛** — *bàndǎo* — "peninsula".

> **这个半岛有很多酒店** *zhège bàndǎo yǒu hěn duō jiǔdiàn* — There are lots of hotels on this peninsula.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**半岛** (*bàndǎo*) is built from 半 'half' and 岛 'an island', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *panH* and *tawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
