## You'll want to know: نظر آنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C279-GAVAHI279-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نظر آنا** — *nazar ānā* — "to be seen; to come into view".

> مجھے کھڑکی میں ایک آدمی نظر آیا۔
>
> *mujhe khiṛkī meṅ ek ādmī nazar āyā.*
>
> I saw a man at the window.

Past (he …): وہ نظر آیا, *voh nazar āyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نظر آنا** (*nazar ānā*) is built from *nazar*, a look, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *naẓar*, "a looking", and **آنا**: a thing comes into your look. The one who sees takes **مجھے**, and the verb follows the thing seen: **آدمی نظر آیا**.
