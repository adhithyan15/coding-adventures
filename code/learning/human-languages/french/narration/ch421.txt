French, chapter 421: Vol annulé.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'annulation — the cancellation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the gap, then the French for to delay.

You'll want to know: l'annulation.
l'annulation — "the cancellation".
Après l'annulation, la compagnie nous a donné une chambre. After the cancellation the airline gave us a room.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
annulation is built on annuler 'to cancel', a learned borrowing from Late Latin annullāre 'to make nothing', from nullus 'none' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
le remboursement — the refund.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to delay, then the French for the cancellation.

You'll want to know: le remboursement.
le remboursement — "the refund".
Elle attend le remboursement de son billet. She is waiting for the refund on her ticket.
Built on rembourser.

The word, taken apart.
remboursement is built on rembourser 'to pay back', from bourse 'purse', which comes from Old French borse (EtymDB) and is usually traced to Late Latin bursa, from Greek býrsa 'hide'. EtymDB does not list the noun.

Lesson 4 of 5.
décommander — to cancel (a booking, an order).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the refund, then the French for to confirm.

You'll want to know: décommander.
décommander — "to cancel (a booking, an order)".
Après l'annulation du vol, nous avons décommandé le taxi. After the flight was cancelled, we cancelled the taxi.
Se décommander — to cry off, to cancel at the last minute.

The word, taken apart.
décommander is built from dé- and commander 'to order', which goes back through Vulgar Latin to Latin commendāre 'to entrust', built on mandāre (EtymDB lists commander).

surréservation is built from sur- 'over' and réservation, from réserver 'to keep aside', a learned borrowing from Latin reservāre (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
