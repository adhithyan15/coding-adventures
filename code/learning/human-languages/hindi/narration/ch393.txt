Hindi, chapter 393: Success.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सफलता (saphaltā) — success.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for experience, then the Hindi for to occur to.

You'll want to know: सफलता.
सफलता — saphaltā — "success".
अगर तुम हार नहीं मानोगे तो सफलता ज़रूर मिलेगी। agar tum hār nahīṃ mānoge to saphaltā zarūr milegī. If you don't give up, success will surely come.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सफलता (saphaltā) is Sanskrit saphala, "bearing fruit; having good results" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -tā.

Lesson 3 of 5.
हासिल करना (hāsil karnā) — to attain, to achieve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for success, then the Hindi for an achievement.

You'll want to know: हासिल करना.
हासिल करना — hāsil karnā — "to attain, to achieve".
अगर तुम रोज़ अभ्यास करोगे तो अपना लक्ष्य हासिल करोगे। agar tum roz abhyās karoge to apnā lakṣya hāsil karoge. If you practise every day, you'll reach your goal.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना लक्ष्य हासिल किया, usne apnā lakṣya hāsil kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
हासिल करना (hāsil karnā) joins हासिल to करना, "to do". हासिल is usually traced to Arabic ḥāṣil, "resulting, obtained", which reached Hindi through Persian.
