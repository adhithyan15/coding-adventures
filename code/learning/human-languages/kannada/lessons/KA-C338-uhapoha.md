## You'll want to know: ಊಹಾಪೋಹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C338-VADANTI338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಊಹಾಪೋಹ** — *ūhāpōha* — "speculation".

> ಆ ಘಟನೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಊಹಾಪೋಹ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *ā ghaṭaneya bagge tumbā ūhāpōha ittu.*
>
> There was a lot of speculation about that incident.

ಊಹೆ, a guess, and ಅಪೋಹ, a denial: guesses for and against.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಊಹಾಪೋಹ** (*ūhāpōha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *ūha*, "consideration" (CDIAL 2443), and *apoha*, "reasoning, denying" (Monier-Williams).
