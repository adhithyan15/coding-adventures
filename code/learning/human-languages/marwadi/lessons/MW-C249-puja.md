## You'll want to know: पूजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C249-HOLI249-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पूजा** — *pūjā* — "worship; a prayer ceremony".

> मां रोज पूजा करै है।
>
> *mā̃ roj pūjā karai hai.*
>
> Mother does the worship every day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूजा** (*pūjā*) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit word *pūjā*, "honour, worship", taken over unchanged (Monier-Williams).
