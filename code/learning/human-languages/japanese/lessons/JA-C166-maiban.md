## You'll want to know: まいばん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C166-TIMEOFDAY166-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まいばん** — *maiban* — "every night".

> **らいしゅうは まいばん はたらきます。** — *raishū wa maiban hatarakimasu* — "Next week I will work every night."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まいばん** (*maiban*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mai*, "every" (Middle Chinese *mwojX*) and *ban*, "nightfall, evening" (*mjonX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
