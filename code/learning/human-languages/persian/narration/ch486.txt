Persian, chapter 486: Joy and Love.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
هیجان زده (hayajânzade) — excited.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for exasperated, then the Persian for to sigh.

You'll want to know: هیجان زده.
هیجان زده (hayajânzade) — "excited".
بچه ها برای سفر هیجان زده هستند. bachchehâ barâ-ye safar hayajânzade hastand. The children are excited about the trip.
hayajân 'excitement' + zade 'struck' (from zadan): struck by excitement.

The word, taken apart.
hayajânzade is built in Persian on hayajân, 'excitement', a loan usually traced to Arabic hayajān, 'agitation', and zade, 'struck', from the inherited verb zadan. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ذوق کردن (zowq kardan) — to be thrilled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to sigh, then the Persian for excited.

You'll want to know: ذوق کردن.
ذوق کردن (zowq kardan) — "to be thrilled".
او با دیدن هدیه ذوق کرد. u bâ didan-e hediye zowq kard. He was thrilled when he saw the present.
zowq 'delight; taste, flair' + kardan. Warm and colloquial.

The word, taken apart.
zowq is a loan, usually traced to Arabic ḏawq, 'taste, relish'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for excited, then the Persian for to be thrilled.

You'll want to know: سرحال.
سرحال (sarhâl) — "in good spirits; lively".
امروز صبح خیلی سرحال هستم. emruz sobh kheyli sarhâl hastam. This morning I feel full of beans.
sar 'head' + hâl 'state'. The opposite is bihâl.

The word, taken apart.
sarhâl is built in Persian on sar, 'head', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and hâl, 'state', a loan from Arabic ḥāl.

Lesson 4 of 5.
عشق ('eshq) — love (passionate).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be thrilled, then the Persian for in good spirits.

You'll want to know: عشق.
عشق ('eshq) — "love (passionate)".
عشق او به موسیقی از کودکی شروع شد. 'eshq-e u be musiqi az kudaki shoru' shod. His love of music began in childhood.
Strong love, for a person or a passion. For liking, Persian says dust dâshtan.

The word, taken apart.
'eshq was borrowed from Arabic ʿišq, 'love, passion' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
متنفر بودن (motenaffer budan) — to hate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for in good spirits, then the Persian for love.

You'll want to know: متنفر بودن.
متنفر بودن (motenaffer budan) — "to hate".
او از سرما متنفر است. u az sarmâ motenaffer ast. She hates the cold.
motenaffer 'averse' + budan 'to be', used as az chizi motenaffer budan. Motenaffer-am means 'I hate it'.

The word, taken apart.
motenaffer is a loan, usually traced to Arabic mutanaffir, 'averse, recoiling'. EtymDB has no entry for it. budan, 'to be', is inherited Persian.
