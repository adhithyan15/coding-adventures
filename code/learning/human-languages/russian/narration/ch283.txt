Russian, chapter 283: Claiming and checking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
утверждать (utverzhdát) — to claim, to assert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a detail, then the Russian for a fact.

You'll want to know: утверждать.
утверждать (utverzhdát) — "to claim, to assert".
Он утверждает, что видел медведя. On utverzhdáyet, shto vídel medvédya. He claims that he saw a bear.
In this sense imperfective. The perfective утвердить (utverdít) means "to approve": утвердить план.

The word, taken apart.
утверждать (utverzhdát) is built on у- and the root of твёрдый "firm", Proto-Slavic tvĭrdŭ, in its Church Slavonic shape -твержд-; EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.

Lesson 2 of 5.
упоминать (upominát) — to mention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fact, then the Russian for to claim.

You'll want to know: упоминать.
упоминать (upominát) — "to mention".
В письме она упомянула твоё имя. V pismé oná upomyanúla tvoyó ímya. In the letter she mentioned your name.
Imperfective; perfective упомянуть (upomyanút). It takes the accusative or о + prepositional.

The word, taken apart.
упоминать (upominát) is built on у- and the Slavic root of помнить "to remember" and память "memory": to bring to mind. EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
уточнять (utochnyát) — to clarify, to check (details).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to claim, then the Russian for to mention.

You'll want to know: уточнять.
уточнять (utochnyát) — "to clarify, to check (details)".
Я позвонил, чтобы уточнить время. Ya pozvoníl, shtóby utochnít vrémya. I phoned to check the time.
Imperfective; perfective уточнить (utochnít).

The word, taken apart.
уточнять (utochnyát) is built on у- and точный "exact", which EtymDB derives from Church Slavonic tŭčĭnŭ; it is usually traced further to the root of ткнуть "to poke", so "hitting the spot".

Мы выяснили, кто взял ключ. My výyasnili, kto vzyal klyuch. We found out who had taken the key.
Imperfective; perfective выяснить (výyasnit), stressed on вы-.

The word, taken apart.
выяснять (vyyasnyát) is built on вы- "out" and ясный "clear, bright", inherited from Proto-Slavic ěsnŭ "clear" (EtymDB): to bring out into the light.

Lesson 5 of 5.
предупреждать (preduprezhdát) — to warn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to clarify, then the Russian for to find out.

You'll want to know: предупреждать.
предупреждать (preduprezhdát) — "to warn".
Мать предупредила детей о грозе. Mat predupredíla detéy o grozé. Mother warned the children about the storm.
Imperfective; perfective предупредить (predupredít), я предупрежу. The person is in the accusative, the danger with о.

The word, taken apart.
предупреждать (preduprezhdát) is built on пред- "before" and упредить "to get ahead of", in the Church Slavonic shape of the root seen in native перед: to tell before it happens. EtymDB lists no parent.
