Spanish, chapter 585: Propuestas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
proponer — to propose, to suggest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for positive, then the Spanish for negative.

You'll want to know: proponer.
proponer — "to propose, to suggest".
Propongo salir más temprano. I propose leaving earlier.
Like poner: propongo, propuse, propuesto.

The word, taken apart.
proponer is a learned borrowing from Latin proponere 'to put forward', pro + ponere 'to put', and rebuilt on poner (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la propuesta — the proposal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for negative, then the Spanish for to propose.

You'll want to know: la propuesta.
la propuesta — "the proposal".
Me gusta tu propuesta. I like your proposal.
Hacer una propuesta — to make a proposal.

The word, taken apart.
la propuesta is built on propuesto, the participle of proponer, a learned borrowing from Latin proponere, whose participle was propositus 'put forward' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
advertir — to warn; to notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the proposal, then the Spanish for the suggestion.

You'll want to know: advertir.
advertir — "to warn; to notice".
Te advierto que es difícil. I am warning you that it is difficult.
Like sentir: advierto, advirtió.

The word, taken apart.
advertir is inherited from Latin advertere 'to turn towards, to give attention', built from ad and vertere 'to turn' (EtymDB).
