# చాలా రోజులైంది (cālā rōjulaindi) — it has been a long time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-05, TE-LEX-C91-GREET-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for let's go, then the Telugu for are you well.

## You'll want to know: చాలా రోజులైంది
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C91-GREET-02]; assesses=[] -->

**చాలా రోజులైంది** (*cālā rōjulaindi*) — "it has been a long time", literally "many days have
become".

**చాలా** and **రోజు** are both yours already; **-లు** makes the days plural.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The greeting for someone you have not seen in a while.
