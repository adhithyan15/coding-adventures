## You'll want to know: きのこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C494-VEG494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きのこ** — *kinoko* — "a mushroom".

> **あきの やまで きのこを みつけました。** — *aki no yama de kinoko o mitsukemashita* — "I found mushrooms on the mountain in autumn."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きのこ** (*kinoko*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
