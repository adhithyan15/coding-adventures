## You'll want to know: रोजगार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C290-SARU290-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रोजगार** — *rojgār* — "employment".

> सहर मांय रोजगार है।
>
> *sahar mā̃y rojgār hai.*
>
> There is work to be had in the city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोजगार** (*rojgār*) is usually traced to Persian *rōzgār*, "time; livelihood".
