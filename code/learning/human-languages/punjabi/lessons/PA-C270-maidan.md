## You'll want to know: ਮੈਦਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C270-SEARCH270-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੈਦਾਨ** — *maidān* — "open ground; a playing field".

> ਸਕੂਲ ਦੇ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਇੱਕ ਵੱਡਾ ਮੈਦਾਨ ਹੈ।
>
> *sakūl de pichchhe ikk vaḍḍā maidān hai.*
>
> There is a big playing field behind the school.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੈਦਾਨ** (*maidān*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *maidān*, "open ground".
