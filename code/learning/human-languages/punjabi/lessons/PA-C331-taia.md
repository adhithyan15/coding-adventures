## You'll want to know: ਤਾਇਆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C331-UNCLE331-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਾਇਆ** — *tāiā* — "a father's elder brother".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਤਾਇਆ ਸਾਡੇ ਨਾਲ ਰਹਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā tāiā sāḍe nāl rahindā hai.*
>
> My father's elder brother lives with us.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਾਇਆ** (*tāiā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tātya*, "fatherly"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tāiā*, "father's elder brother", under it (CDIAL 5759).
