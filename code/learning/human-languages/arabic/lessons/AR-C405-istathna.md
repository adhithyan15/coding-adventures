# استثنى (istathnā) — to exclude

## You'll want to know: استثنى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C405-ISTITHNA405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**استثنى** — *istathnā* — "to exclude".

> استثنى المدير الموظفين الجدد من الاجتماع.
>
> *istathnā al-mudīr al-muwaẓẓafīn al-judud min al-ijtimāʿ.*
>
> The manager excluded the new employees from the meeting.

Present: يستثني, *yastathnī*.

It takes من: استثنى … من ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استثنى** (*istathnā*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-n-y*, "to fold, to bend back" (Lane). It is Form X, to bend a thing back out of a group: to exclude, to except.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
