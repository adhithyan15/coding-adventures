## You'll want to know: સડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C480-KHAS480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સડવું** — *saḍvũ* — "to rot; to decay".

> મીઠાઈથી દાંત સડે છે.
>
> *mīṭhāīthī dā̃t saḍe che.*
>
> Sweets make your teeth decay.

Past (it …): તે દાંત સડી ગયો, *te dā̃t saḍī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સડવું** (*saḍvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śaṭati*, "disintegrates"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to rot" (CDIAL 12268).
