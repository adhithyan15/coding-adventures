## You'll want to know: ಸತ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C338-VADANTI338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸತ್ಯ** — *satya* — "the truth; true".

> ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಸತ್ಯ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಗೊತ್ತಾಯಿತು.
>
> *konege satya ellarigū gottāyitu.*
>
> In the end everyone came to know the truth.

ನಿಜ is the everyday word for "true"; ಸತ್ಯ is weightier: ಸತ್ಯ ಹೇಳು, to tell the truth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸತ್ಯ** (*satya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *satya*, "true", and as a noun "truth" (CDIAL 13112).
