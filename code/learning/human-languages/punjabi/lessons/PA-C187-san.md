# ਸਨ — were

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SI-187, PA-LEX-C73-SCHOOL73-04, PA-LEX-C84-ADJ84-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਸੀ**, then **ਫੁੱਲ** and **ਲਾਲ**.

## Grammar Lens: the plural and polite past form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-SAN-187]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SI-187, PA-LEX-HONA] -->

> **ਸਨ** — *san* — were

Use this past copula with a plural subject here. It also serves respectful
singular reference, matching Punjabi’s familiar plural-for-respect pattern.
