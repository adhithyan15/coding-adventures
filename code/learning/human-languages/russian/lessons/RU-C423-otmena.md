## You'll want to know: отмена
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C423-ZADERZHKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**отмена** (*otména*) — "a cancellation".

> После отмены рейса мы вернулись домой.
>
> *Pósle otmény réysa my vernúlis domóy.*
>
> After the flight was cancelled we went back home.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отмена* is built from *от-* 'away' and *мена* 'an exchange', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *měna* 'change' (EtymDB).
