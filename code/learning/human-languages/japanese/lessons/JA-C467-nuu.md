## You'll want to know: ぬう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C467-CRAFT467-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬう** — *nuu* — "to sew".

> **ははは ゆかたを ぬっています。** — *haha wa yukata o nutte imasu* — "My mother is sewing a cotton kimono."

Polite **ぬいます**, *nuimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **そぼが きものを ぬいました。**, *sobo ga kimono o nuimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬう** (*nuu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
