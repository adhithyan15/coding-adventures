Marathi, chapter 320: Festival Days.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सजवणे (sajavṇe) — to decorate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a firecracker, then the Marathi for fireworks.

You'll want to know: सजवणे.
सजवणे — sajavṇe — "to decorate".
आम्ही सणासाठी घर फुलांनी सजवलं. āmhī saṇāsāṭhī ghar phulānnī sajavlaṁ. We decorated the house with flowers for the festival.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सजवलं, tyāne sajavlaṁ.
Transitive; what is decorated agrees: घर सजवलं, खोली सजवली.

The word, taken apart.
सजवणे (sajavṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi sajavṇe, 'to equip', under Sanskrit sajjayati, 'equips, prepares' (CDIAL 13091).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for fireworks, then the Marathi for to decorate.

You'll want to know: उत्सव.
उत्सव — utsav — "a festivity; a festival".
गावात दहा दिवस मोठा उत्सव असतो. gāvāt dahā divas moṭhā utsav asto. There is a big festival in the village for ten days.
Masculine.
सण is a feast day; उत्सव (utsav), the celebration around it.

The word, taken apart.
उत्सव (utsav) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit utsava, 'a festival, a joyful occasion' (Monier-Williams).
