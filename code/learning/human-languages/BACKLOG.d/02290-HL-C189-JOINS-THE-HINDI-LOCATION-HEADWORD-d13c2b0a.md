## HL-C189 — में joins the Hindi location headword

After **सोच**, the ranked Hindi queue still begins with script and morphology
fragments, previously dispositioned forms, and etymological ancestors. **में**
is the next unclassified learner-facing form with direct practice and recall
evidence.

Chapter 5 defines **में** as “in,” teaches that the Hindi postposition follows
its noun, practises the placement in **मैं दिल्ली में रहता हूँ**, and asks the
learner to recall both the full sentence and what differs from English word
order. The old headword declared only **रहना**, leaving that assessed grammar
word invisible to reports and downstream cards.

The headword is now **रहना / में**. This repairs metadata for content already
taught by the lesson without introducing a new form or changing the learning
sequence. Rerun the ranked report next and continue the evidence-first audit
after the explicitly classified script, morphology, and historical-source
rows.
