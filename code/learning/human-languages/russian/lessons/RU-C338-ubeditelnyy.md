## You'll want to know: убедительный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C338-DOVOD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**убедительный** (*ubedítelnyy*) — "convincing".

> Его ответ был не очень убедительным.
>
> *Yegó otvét byl ne óchen ubedítelnym.*
>
> His answer was not very convincing.

From *убедить*, to convince. *Убедительная просьба* — an urgent request, a fixed formula on notices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**убедительный** is built on *убедить* "to convince". The root is that of *беда* "trouble", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*běda*, which EtymDB sets beside Greek *peíthō* "I persuade" (EtymDB). To convince someone was first to press them hard.
