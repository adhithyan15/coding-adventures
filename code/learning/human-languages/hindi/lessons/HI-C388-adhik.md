## You'll want to know: अधिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C388-PARYAPT388-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिक** — *adhik* — "more (formal), excessive".

> अगर पानी अधिक होगा तो चावल गीला हो जाएगा।
>
> *agar pānī adhik hogā to cāval gīlā ho jāegā.*
>
> If there is too much water, the rice will turn soggy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिक** (*adhik*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *adhika*, "surpassing, more" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
