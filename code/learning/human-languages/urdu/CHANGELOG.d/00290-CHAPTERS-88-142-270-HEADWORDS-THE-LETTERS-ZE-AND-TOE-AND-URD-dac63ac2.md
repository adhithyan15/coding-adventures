## Chapters 88-142: 270 headwords, the letters ze and toe, and Urdu attains A1

Urdu had three A1 gaps. It was 261 headwords short. Five A1 spine nodes had no
segment: TIME-OF-DAY, DEFINITE-REFERENCE, DESCRIBE-QUALITIES,
SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO and SAY-WHAT-I-WANT. Six atoms were revisited fewer
than twice.

**The words.** Fifty-four chapters of five:

- **this and that:** **یہی**, **وہی**, **ایسا**, **سب**, **ہر**, **کوئی**, **کچھ** (DEFINITE-REFERENCE)
- **time:** **دوپہر**, **آدھی رات**, **بجے**, **ساڑھے**, **سوا**, **پونے**, the days, the seasons (TIME-OF-DAY)
- **places and directions:** **دائیں**, **بائیں**, **سیدھا**, **سامنے**, **طرف**, **بازار**, and the town
- **can and want:** **سکنا**, **پانا**, **پاس**, **ہنر**, **قابل** (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO);
  **چاہیے**, **خواہش**, **ضرورت**, **شوق**, **ارادہ** (SAY-WHAT-I-WANT)
- **things:** 128 of them, covering the house, things you carry, school,
  food, clothes, nature, animals and the body
- **qualities:** thirty-four describing words (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)
- **verbs:** twenty-six, plus **سکنا** and **پانا** above

Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword, romanization and
lesson text. Forward references stay at 4.

**Only letters the reader can write.** Every new word uses letters the book
has already taught the reader to write, plus two. **ز** is in **روز** (*roz*),
**بازار** and **میز**. **ط** is in **طرف** (*taraf*) and **طوطا**. Chapter 99
writes both, each from the word it was first read in: **ز** from **روز** and
**ط** from **طرف**. The first run of words (chapters 88-98) carries no reviews
of its own, so no review prints either letter before chapter 99. Its chain
continues into chapter 100, so every word still gets its two revisits. Words
with letters the book has not taught yet (ع, غ, ص, ڈ, ذ, ث) were left for a
later tranche.

**The six revisits.** Each one is a new warm-up line:

- the ordinals with **تاریخ**
- **لاہوری** with **بازار**
- writing **جانا** with **لوٹنا**
- the chapter-33 reading skills in the school chapters: a read-aloud line of
  place names, and two short sentences, **یہ میز ہے۔ وہ دروازہ ہے۔**
