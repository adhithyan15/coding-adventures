## You'll want to know: সংসদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C345-SONGSOD345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সংসদ** — *sôngsôd* — "a parliament".

> সংসদে আজ নতুন আইন নিয়ে আলোচনা হল।
>
> *sôngsôde āj nôtun āin niye ālochônā hôlô.*
>
> Parliament discussed the new law today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংসদ** (*sôngsôd*) is Sanskrit *saṃsad*, 'sitting together', an assembly (Monier-Williams). Modern Bengali uses it for a national parliament.
