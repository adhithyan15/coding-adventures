## You'll want to know: सन्दधाति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C508-NIRMANA508-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्दधाति** — *sandadhāti* — "mends; puts together".

> सः जीर्णं कटं सन्दधाति।
>
> *saḥ jīrṇaṃ kaṭaṃ sandadhāti.*
>
> He mends the old mat.

Past (he …): सः जीर्णं कटं संहितवान्, *saḥ jīrṇaṃ kaṭaṃ saṃhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्दधाति** (*sandadhāti*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives *saṃdhā* as to put together, to join, and also to mend, to restore; its past participle is *saṃhita* (MW).
