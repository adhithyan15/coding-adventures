# கணக்கெடுப்பு (kaṇakkeṭuppu) — a census; a survey

## You'll want to know: கணக்கெடுப்பு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C393-ARIKKAI393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**கணக்கெடுப்பு** — *kaṇakkeṭuppu* — "a census; a survey".

> பத்து ஆண்டுகளுக்கு ஒருமுறை மக்கள்தொகைக் கணக்கெடுப்பு நடக்கிறது.
>
> *pattu āṇṭukaḷukku orumuṟai makkaḷtokaik kaṇakkeṭuppu naṭakkiṟatu.*
>
> A population census is held once every ten years.

Literally a taking of the count: எடு, to take, is inside it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கணக்கெடுப்பு** (*kaṇakkeṭuppu*) is built from *kaṇakku*, "an account, a count", and native *eṭuppu*, "a taking up", from *eṭu*, "to take" (DEDR 851).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
