## You'll want to know: أطروحة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C396-MUSTALAH396-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أطروحة** — *uṭrūḥa* — "a thesis".

> كتب الباحث أطروحة عن الهجرة.
>
> *kataba al-bāḥith uṭrūḥa ʿan al-hijra.*
>
> The researcher wrote a thesis on migration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أطروحة** (*uṭrūḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-r-ḥ*, "to throw, to cast" (Lane). An *uṭrūḥa* is a thing put forward, thrown out for discussion: a thesis.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
