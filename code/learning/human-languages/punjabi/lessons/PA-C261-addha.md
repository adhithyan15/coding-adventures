# ਅੱਧਾ (addhā) — half

## You'll want to know: ਅੱਧਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C261-TRAIN261-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅੱਧਾ** — *addhā* — "half".

> ਮੈਂ ਅੱਧਾ ਘੰਟਾ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਉਡੀਕਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ addhā ghaṇṭā saṭeshan utte uḍīkdā hāṁ.*
>
> I wait half an hour at the station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅੱਧਾ** (*addhā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ardha*, "half"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *addhā*, "half", under it (CDIAL 644).
