## You'll want to know: cotillear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C724-MENTIRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cotillear** — "to gossip".

> Mis vecinas cotillean en la escalera.
>
> *My neighbours gossip on the stairs.*

Spain; a gossip is *un cotilla*. In Latin America people say *chismear*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cotillear* is built on *cotilla* "a gossip" with the ending *-ear*. For *cotilla* in this sense the origin is not settled; an older *cotilla* was a corset.
