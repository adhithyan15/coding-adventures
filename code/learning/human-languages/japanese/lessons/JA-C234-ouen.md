## You'll want to know: おうえん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C234-MATCH234-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おうえん** — *ōen* — "cheering; support".

> **きのう みんなで せんしゅの おうえんを しました。** — *kinō minna de senshu no ōen o shimashita* — "Yesterday we all cheered for the players."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おうえん** (*ōen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ō*, "respond" (Middle Chinese *'ingH*) and *en*, "help" (*hjwenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
