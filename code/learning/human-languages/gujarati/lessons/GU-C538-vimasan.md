# વિમાસણ (vimāsaṇ) — a quandary

## You'll want to know: વિમાસણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C538-SANSAY538-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વિમાસણ** — *vimāsaṇ* — "a quandary".

> જો તમે વિમાસણમાં પડશો તો મને ફોન કરો.
>
> *jo tame vimāsaṇmā̃ paḍśo to mane phon karo.*
>
> If you find yourself in a quandary, phone me.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિમાસણ** (*vimāsaṇ*) is being caught between choices and uneasy about what will come; one "falls" into it: **વિમાસણમાં પડવું**. CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, with the sense "fearful anticipation", under Sanskrit *vimarśana*, "investigation" (CDIAL 11820).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
