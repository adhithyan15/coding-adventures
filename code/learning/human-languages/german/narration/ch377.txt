German, chapter 377: An der Hochschule.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
die Hochschule — the university, the college.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for concentration, then the German for the revision.

You'll want to know: die Hochschule.
die Hochschule — "the university, the college".
Meine Schwester studiert an einer Hochschule für Musik. My sister studies at a college of music.
Plural: die Hochschulen. A general word for universities and colleges; die Fachhochschule is a university of applied sciences.

The word, taken apart.
Hochschule joins hoch "high", Old High German hōh (EtymDB), to Schule "school", from Old High German scuola, Latin schola and Greek scholḗ "leisure, learned talk" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 6.
die Vorlesung — the lecture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the revision, then the German for the university.

You'll want to know: die Vorlesung.
die Vorlesung — "the lecture".
Die Vorlesung beginnt um neun Uhr im großen Saal. The lecture starts at nine in the big hall.
Plural: die Vorlesungen. From vorlesen — to read aloud: lecturers once read from a book.

The word, taken apart.
Vorlesung is the noun of vorlesen "to read aloud", built on lesen "to read", inherited from Old High German lesan and Proto-Germanic lesaną "to gather" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 6.
der Dozent — the lecturer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the university, then the German for the lecture.

You'll want to know: der Dozent.
der Dozent — "the lecturer".
Der Dozent erklärt das Thema sehr gut. The lecturer explains the topic very well.
A weak noun: den Dozenten, plural die Dozenten. A woman is die Dozentin.

The word, taken apart.
Dozent is a learned borrowing from Latin docēns "teaching" (EtymDB), the present participle of docēre "to teach, to instruct" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 6.
das Praktikum — the internship, the work placement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the lecturer, then the German for the degree programme.

You'll want to know: das Praktikum.
das Praktikum — "the internship, the work placement".
Im Sommer mache ich ein Praktikum bei einer Zeitung. In the summer I am doing an internship at a newspaper.
Plural: die Praktika. Der Praktikant, die Praktikantin — the intern.

The word, taken apart.
Praktikum is a learned formation on Medieval Latin practicus "active, practical", from Greek praktikós "fit for action", from prássein "to do". EtymDB has no entry for the German word.
