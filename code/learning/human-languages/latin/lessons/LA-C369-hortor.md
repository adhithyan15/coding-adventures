## You'll want to know: hortor, hortārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C369-HORTOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**hortor, hortārī** — "to urge, to encourage".

> Mīlitēs hortātus est ut fortiter pugnārent.
>
> *He urged the soldiers to fight bravely.*

Deponent: *hortor, hortārī, hortātus sum* + accusative and *ut* (or *nē*) with the subjunctive. English *exhort*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hortor* comes from an old verb *horior* "to urge on" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), traced to a root meaning "to desire" that Greek *khairō* "to rejoice" shares. English *exhort* comes from the compound *exhortor*.
