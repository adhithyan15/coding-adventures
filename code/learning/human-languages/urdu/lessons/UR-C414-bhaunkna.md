## You'll want to know: بھونکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C414-GARD414-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بھونکنا** — *bhauṅknā* — "to bark".

> رات کو کتے بھونکتے ہیں۔
>
> *rāt ko kutte bhauṅkte haiṅ.*
>
> At night the dogs bark.

Past (it …): وہ کتا دیر تک بھونکا, *voh kuttā der tak bhauṅkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھونکنا** (*bhauṅknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *bhaũknā* and *bhū̃knā*, "to bark", under Sanskrit *búkkati*, "barks" (CDIAL 9265).
