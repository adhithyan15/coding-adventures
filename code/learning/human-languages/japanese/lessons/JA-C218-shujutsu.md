## You'll want to know: しゅじゅつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C218-HOSPITAL218-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅじゅつ** — *shujutsu* — "surgery; an operation".

> **しゅじゅつは ひつようですか。** — *shujutsu wa hitsuyō desu ka* — "Is surgery needed?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅじゅつ** (*shujutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shu*, "hand" (Middle Chinese *syuwX*) and *jutsu*, "skill" (*zywit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
