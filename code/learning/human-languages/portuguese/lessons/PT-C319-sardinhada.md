## You'll want to know: a sardinhada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C319-SANTOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a sardinhada** — "the sardine feast".

> Fizemos uma sardinhada no quintal.
>
> *We had a sardine barbecue in the back yard.*

Grilled sardines on bread, outdoors, typical of summer festivals.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sardinhada* is built on *sardinha*, from Latin *sardīna* "a sardine" (Lewis & Short), with *-ada* for a meal of something.
