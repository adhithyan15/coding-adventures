## You'll want to know: назначать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C377-PLANY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**назначать** (*naznachát*) — "to set, to fix (a time); to appoint".

> Мы назначили встречу на пять часов.
>
> *My naznáchili vstréchu na pyat chasóv.*
>
> We set the meeting for five o’clock.

Imperfective; perfective *назначить*. It also means to appoint someone to a post, or to prescribe a medicine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**назначать** is built from *на-* and *знак* "a sign, a mark", on the root of *знать* "to know", Proto-Slavic *\*znati*: to mark a time out.
