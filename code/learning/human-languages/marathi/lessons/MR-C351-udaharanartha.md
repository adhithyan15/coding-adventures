## You'll want to know: उदाहरणार्थ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C351-JODNE351-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उदाहरणार्थ** — *udāharaṇārtha* — "for example".

> मला फळं आवडतात, उदाहरणार्थ आंबा आणि केळं.
>
> *malā phaḷaṁ āvaḍtāt, udāharaṇārtha āmbā āṇi keḷaṁ.*
>
> I like fruit, for example mangoes and bananas.

उदाहरण + अर्थ: for the sake of an example.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उदाहरणार्थ** (*udāharaṇārtha*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *udāharaṇa*, "example", and *artha*, "aim, sake" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
