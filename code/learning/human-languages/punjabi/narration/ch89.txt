Punjabi, chapter 89: In front, Behind, Here, There, Shop.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਾਹਮਣੇ (sāhmaṇe) — in front.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for on, then the Punjabi for below.

You'll want to know: ਸਾਹਮਣੇ.
ਸਾਹਮਣੇ — sāhmaṇe — "in front".

The word, taken apart.
ਸਾਹਮਣੇ (sāhmaṇe) goes with Hindi sāmhnā, "front part", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit saṁmukha, "facing, present", beside Punjabi saũhe, "in front" (CDIAL 12982).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਪਿੱਛੇ (pichchhe) — behind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for below, then the Punjabi for in front.

You'll want to know: ਪਿੱਛੇ.
ਪਿੱਛੇ — pichchhe — "behind".

The word, taken apart.
ਪਿੱਛੇ (pichchhe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from paśca, "hinder part", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7990). CDIAL says the form was drawn from Sanskrit paścāt, "behind".

The word, taken apart.
ਉੱਥੇ (utthe) is inherited. CDIAL lists it beside itthe, "here", under Sanskrit ittham, "thus", with its u- taken from the stem of asau, "that" (CDIAL 1564).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਦੁਕਾਨ (dukān) — a shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for here, then the Punjabi for there.

You'll want to know: ਦੁਕਾਨ.
ਦੁਕਾਨ — dukān — "a shop".

The word, taken apart.
ਦੁਕਾਨ (dukān) is a loan from Arabic dukkān, "shop", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is d-k-n (Lane).
