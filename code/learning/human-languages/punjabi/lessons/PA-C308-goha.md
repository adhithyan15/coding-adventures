## You'll want to know: ਗੋਹਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C308-FEED308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੋਹਾ** — *gohā* — "cow dung".

> ਗੋਹਾ ਰੂੜੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *gohā rūṛī utte jāndā hai.*
>
> The cow dung goes onto the manure heap.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੋਹਾ** (*gohā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gōśakṛt*, "cowdung"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gohā*, "cowdung", under it (CDIAL 4333).
