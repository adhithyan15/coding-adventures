## You'll want to know: il prelievo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C459-BANCA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il prelievo** — "the withdrawal; the sample (of blood)".

> Il prelievo massimo è di cinquecento euro.
>
> *The maximum withdrawal is five hundred euros.*

At the doctor's, *il prelievo* is a blood test.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prelievo* is built on *prelevare* 'to withdraw', a learned borrowing from Late Latin *praelevāre* 'to lift first', from *levāre* 'to lift'.
