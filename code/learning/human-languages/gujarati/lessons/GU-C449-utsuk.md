# ઉત્સુક (utsuk) — keen; curious to know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C448-HASYA448-05, GU-LEX-C449-ATUR449-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to tease, then the Gujarati for eager.

## You'll want to know: ઉત્સુક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C449-ATUR449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્સુક** — *utsuk* — "keen; curious to know".

> મીરા પરિણામ જાણવા માટે ઉત્સુક હતી.
>
> *Mīrā pariṇām jāṇvā māṭe utsuk hatī.*
>
> Mira was keen to know the result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્સુક** (*utsuk*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *utsuka*, "anxiously desirous, striving for", as Monier-Williams records it.
