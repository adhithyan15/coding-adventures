## You'll want to know: हितम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C382-HITA382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हितम्** — *hitam* — "benefit; welfare".

> यदि एतत् मम हितम्, तर्हि अहम् अवश्यं करिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi etat mama hitam, tarhi aham avaśyaṃ kariṣyāmi.*
>
> If this is for my good, I will do it by all means.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हितम्** (*hitam*) comes from the root √dhā, "to put", whose participle MW gives as *hita*, "put, placed"; MW gives *hita* the senses "beneficial, advantageous, salutary" (MW).
