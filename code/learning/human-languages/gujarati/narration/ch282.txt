Gujarati, chapter 282: Comparing, and how long.
8 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
સૌથી (sauthī) — most; of all.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "I want to become a doctor". (māre dāktar banvũ che.) Then "small", "more" and "to like". (નાનું, વધારે, ગમવું.)

You'll want to know: સૌથી.
સૌથી — sauthī — "most; of all". Put it before a describing word and it becomes the most of all:
મીરા સૌથી નાની છે. Mīrā sauthī nānī che. — "Mira is the youngest."
નાની follows Mira; સૌથી (sauthī) stays as it is. With વધારે it is "most of all":
મને ચોમાસું સૌથી વધારે ગમે છે. mane comāsũ sauthī vadhāre game che. — "I like the monsoon most of all."

The word, taken apart.
સૌથી (sauthī) is built from sau, "all, everyone", and -થી, "from": "from all", so "more than all". CDIAL lists Gujarati sau, "wholly", under Sanskrit sarva, "all, whole" (CDIAL 13276).

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Script you'll notice: ૌ.
ૌ — the au sign: the ા bar on the right plus two stacked strokes above. It sits on સ in સૌથી (sauthī).
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
one stroke above: ે e. two strokes above: ૈ ai, as in પૈસા.
one stroke above: ો o: ા with ે above. two strokes above: ૌ (au sign) au: ા with ૈ above.
It is written after its consonant: સ first, then the bar to its right, then the lower flag, then the upper flag.

Writing — notice and trace.
Follow the numbered strip:
start at the top of the bar and draw it straight down.
without lifting, turn into the short foot to the right.
lift, then start at the lower flag's upper-left tip and arc it to the right.
without lifting, curve it down to the right.
lift, then start at the upper flag's upper-left tip and arc it to the right.
without lifting, curve it down to the right — and only now lift.
Three strokes — six movements, two pen lifts; cited to KanoAI's hand-made Gujarati barakhadi templates. Finger-trace ૌ (au sign) once.

Writing — copy with the model.
Copy સૌથી (sauthī) once: સ, the bar, the two flags, then થી. Compare only two things: are there two flags, and does the lower one come first? Repair one if needed.

Writing — recall after hiding.
Hide the model and write the au sign once. Uncover ૌ, compare, and stop after one repair.

Lesson 3 of 8.
… કરતાં મોટું (… kartā̃ moṭũ) — bigger than …

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "Mira is the youngest". (Mīrā sauthī nānī che.) Then "big" and "a city". (મોટું, શહેર.)

Grammar Lens: કરતાં, than.
શહેર ગામ કરતાં મોટું છે. śaher gām kartā̃ moṭũ che. — "The city is bigger than the village."
The thing being described, then the one it is measured against with કરતાં (kartā̃), "than", then the describing word, which follows the first: મીરા અરુણ કરતાં નાની છે (Mīrā Aruṇ kartā̃ nānī che), "Mira is younger than Arun".
A word with no ending of its own takes વધારે, "more", or ઓછું, "less": ટ્રેન બસ કરતાં વધારે ઝડપી છે (ṭren bas kartā̃ vadhāre jhaḍpī che).

You'll want to know: the most of all.
ઝડપી ટ્રેન સૌથી મોંઘી છે. jhaḍpī ṭren sauthī moṅghī che. — "The fast train is the most expensive."
Two things take કરતાં; the top of a whole group takes સૌથી (sauthī):
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
the bus is cheaper than the train. say: બસ ટ્રેન કરતાં સસ્તી છે.
the bus is the cheapest. say: બસ સૌથી (sauthī) સસ્તી છે.

Lesson 4 of 8.
સુધી (sudhī) — until; as far as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "the city is bigger than the village". (śaher gām kartā̃ moṭũ che.) Then "evening" and "a river". (સાંજ, નદી.)

You'll want to know: સુધી.
સુધી — sudhī — "until; as far as". It comes after its word, as કરતાં does: an end in time, or in space.
હું સાંજ સુધી ઘરે છું. hũ sānj sudhī ghare chũ. — "I'm at home until evening."
અમે નદી સુધી ચાલ્યા. ame nadī sudhī cālyā. — "We walked as far as the river."

The word, taken apart.
સુધી (sudhī) is a small word with no older form in any dictionary we use, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 8.
બે વરસથી (be varasthī) — for how long, since when.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "until evening". (sānj sudhī.) Then "a year" and "morning". (વરસ, સવાર.)

Grammar Lens: -થી and the present.
હું બે વરસથી અહીં રહું છું. hũ be varasthī ahī̃ rahũ chũ. — "I have lived here for two years."
-થી, "from", on a length of time or a starting point says how long something has gone on, and the verb stays in the present: it is still going on. English says "have lived"; Gujarati says "I live, from two years". સવારથી વરસાદ પડે છે (savārthī varsād paḍe che): "it has been raining since morning". To ask: તમે કેટલા વખતથી અહીં રહો છો? (tame keṭlā vakhatthī ahī̃ raho cho?), "how long have you lived here?"

You'll want to know: from … until, and all day.
દુકાન સવારથી સાંજ સુધી ખુલ્લી છે. dukān savārthī sānj sudhī khullī che. — "The shop is open from morning until evening."
-થી marks the start and સુધી (sudhī) the end. A span with no start or end named takes આખું, "whole", and neither -થી nor સુધી (sudhī): મેં આખો દિવસ કામ કર્યું (mẽ ākho divas kām karyũ), "I worked all day".

Lesson 6 of 8.
ઋતુ (ṛtu) — a season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "summer", "winter" and "the monsoon". (ઉનાળો, શિયાળો, ચોમાસું.) Then મોસમ: "a season; the weather".

You'll want to know: ઋતુ.
ઋતુ — ṛtu — "a season". It is feminine. મોસમ is also the weather; ઋતુ is one part of the year.
અહીં ત્રણ ઋતુ છે: ઉનાળો, ચોમાસું અને શિયાળો. ahī̃ traṇ ṛtu che: unāḷo, comāsũ ane śiyāḷo. — "There are three seasons here: summer, the monsoon and winter."
તમને કઈ ઋતુ સૌથી વધારે ગમે છે? tamne kaī ṛtu sauthī vadhāre game che? — "Which season do you like most?"
કઈ is કયું, "which", following ઋતુ (ṛtu).

The word, taken apart.
ઋતુ (ṛtu) is a tatsama: Sanskrit ṛtu, "a fixed time; a season" (Monier-Williams), taken into Gujarati as it was, with the vowel letter ઋ (ṛ) that the next lesson writes.

ઋતુ (ṛtu) opens on the same vowel ṛ, with no consonant to carry it.

You'll want to know: ૃ, standing alone.
[a table of 1 row, read aloud]
ṛ. after a consonant: ૃ, as in હૃદય. standing alone: ?
The missing letter is ઋ (ṛ). Its print has three parts: a bent left body, a separate central stem with a foot, and a right loop flowing into a descending tail.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — meet one form.
ઋ — ṛ.
Keep ઋ (ṛ) visible. Follow the numbered strip:
sweep right along the upper left body and turn back down-left.
lift and descend the central stem into its foot.
lift again, circle the right loop, and finish through the descending tail.
Three strokes, two pen lifts: a verified three-stroke teaching-app variant fitted to the bundled printed outline (t30apps.com, ‘Gujarati Alphabet Writing Practice’, version 1.0, ઋ (ṛ) animation, first through third SVG paths).

Copy visible ઋ (ṛ) once. Compare only two things: are there three parts, and does the loop come last? Repair one landmark if needed.
