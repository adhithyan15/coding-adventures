## You'll want to know: 喝
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C39-DRINK39-01]; assesses=[] -->

**喝** — *hē* — "to drink".

> **你喝什么** *nǐ hē shénme* — What would you like to drink?

The new character, in pieces: 喝 — 口 on the left; on the right 日 on top of a wrap with a small shape inside it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**喝** (*hē*) is an old character: the Guangyun records it as *xat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to shout at'. The sense 'to drink' is not given there, so how it came to mean drink is not settled.
