## Script: six shapes, two ideas
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-161, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-162, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-163, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-164, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-165, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-166] -->

**One — a vowel can be long, or it can move.** Three signs, and none of them can
stand on its own:

| sign | sound | met in |
|---|---|---|
| ◌ೀ | *ī* | ನೀವು — you |
| ◌ೈ | *ai* | ಕೈ — a hand |
| ◌ೌ | *au* | ಹೌದು — yes |

**Two — the tongue can curl back**, and Kannada writes that position where
English marks nothing:

| flat | curled |
|---|---|
| ನ *na* | ಣ *ṇa* |
| ಸ *sa* | ಷ *ṣa* |

**ಶ** *śa* is the third s — flat against the front of the roof. One set of three
where English has a single letter, and the word **ಸಂತೋಷ** uses two of them, one
at each end.
