## You'll want to know: उत्सव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C320-UTSAV320-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सव** — *utsav* — "a festivity; a festival".

> गावात दहा दिवस मोठा उत्सव असतो.
>
> *gāvāt dahā divas moṭhā utsav asto.*
>
> There is a big festival in the village for ten days.

Masculine.

सण is a feast day; उत्सव, the celebration around it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सव** (*utsav*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *utsava*, 'a festival, a joyful occasion' (Monier-Williams).
