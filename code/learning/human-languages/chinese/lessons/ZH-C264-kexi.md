## You'll want to know: kěxī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C264-REFUSE264-02]; assesses=[] -->

*kěxī* — "a pity; it's a shame".

> *nǐ bù néng lái tài kěxī le* — It's a real shame you can't come.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kěxī* is built from *kě* and *xī*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaX* and *sjek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to permit' and the second 'to grudge' and, after an older dictionary, 'to grieve'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
