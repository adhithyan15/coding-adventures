## You'll want to know: সাধ্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C545-SADHYO545-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সাধ্য** — *sādhyô* — "one's power; what one can manage".

> আমার সাধ্যমতো আমি তোমাকে সাহায্য করব।
>
> *Āmār sādhyômôto āmi tomāke sāhājyô kôrbô.*
>
> I'll help you as much as I can.

With -মতো: সাধ্যমতো, as far as one can.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাধ্য** (*sādhyô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sādhya*, "to be accomplished, attainable" (Monier-Williams).
