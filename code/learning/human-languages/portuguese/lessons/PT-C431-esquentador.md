## You'll want to know: o esquentador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C431-AQUECIMENTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o esquentador** — "the water heater (gas)".

> O esquentador avariou e não há água quente.
>
> *The water heater broke down and there is no hot water.*

In Brazil *o aquecedor* covers both. *Esquentar* — to heat up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esquentador* is built on *esquentar* 'to heat', from *es-* and *quente* 'hot' (EtymDB); *quente* is inherited from Latin *calēns* 'warm'.
