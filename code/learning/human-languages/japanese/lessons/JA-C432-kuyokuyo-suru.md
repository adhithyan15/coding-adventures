## You'll want to know: くよくよする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C432-WORRY432-02]; assesses=[] -->

**くよくよする** — *kuyokuyo suru* — "to brood; to fret".

> **ちいさい ことで くよくよしないで ください。** — *chiisai koto de kuyokuyo shinaide kudasai* — "Please do not brood over small things."

Polite **くよくよします**, *kuyokuyoshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かれが くよくよしました。**, *kare ga kuyokuyo shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くよくよする** (*kuyokuyo suru*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, *kuyokuyo*, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, made a verb with *suru*, "to do"; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
