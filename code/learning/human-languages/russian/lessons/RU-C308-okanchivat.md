## You'll want to know: оканчивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C308-VYPUSKNIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**оканчивать** (*okánchivat*) — "to finish, to graduate from".

> Он окончил школу в прошлом году.
>
> *On okónchil shkólu v próshlom godú.*
>
> He finished school last year.

Imperfective; perfective *окончить* (*okónchit*). It takes the accusative: *окончить университет*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*оканчивать* is built on *о-* and *конец* "end", inherited through Old East Slavic *konĭcĭ* from Proto-Slavic *konĭcĭ* "end" (EtymDB).
