## You'll want to know: l'insonnia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C490-SONNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'insonnia** — "insomnia, sleeplessness".

> Quando sono stressato soffro d'insonnia.
>
> *When I am stressed I suffer from insomnia.*

*Soffrire d'insonnia* — to suffer from insomnia.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*insonnia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *insomnia* "sleeplessness" (EtymDB), from *in-* "not" and *somnus* "sleep".
