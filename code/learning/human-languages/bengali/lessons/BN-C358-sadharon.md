## You'll want to know: সাধারণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C358-MIL358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সাধারণ** — *sādhārôn* — "ordinary; common".

> আমি একজন সাধারণ মানুষ।
>
> *āmi ekjôn sādhārôn mānush.*
>
> I'm an ordinary person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাধারণ** (*sādhārôn*) is Sanskrit *sādhāraṇa*, common to all (Monier-Williams). In Bengali it is both 'general' and 'ordinary, nothing special'.
