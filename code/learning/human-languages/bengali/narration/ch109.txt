Bengali, chapter 109: Comb, Soap, Thin towel, Needle, Thread.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
চিরুনি (chiruni) — a comb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a quilt, then the Bengali for a mat.

You'll want to know: চিরুনি.
চিরুনি — chiruni — "a comb".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: chiruni]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: chiruni, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say chiruni]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for a quilt, then the Bengali for a mat, then say chiruni again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does চিরুনি (chiruni) mean? ("A comb".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.

Warm-up.

[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: courage, talent, a machine, equipment, a method]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cooking, to join, to fill, to burn, sewing]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to weave, to attach, to move aside, to wake someone, to save]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to get down, swimming, to jump, to hit, a share]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to measure, to stand, to lie down, closed, to wipe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

To fill? (ভরানো, bhôrāno.) To burn? (পোড়ানো, poṛāno.) Sewing? (সেলাই, selāi.) To weave? (বোনা, bonā.) To attach? (লাগানো, lāgāno.) To move aside? (সরানো, sôrāno.) To wake someone? (জাগানো, jāgāno.) To save? (বাঁচানো, bā̃chāno.) To get down? (নামা, nāmā.) Swimming? (সাঁতার, sā̃tār.) To jump? (লাফানো, lāphāno.) To hit? (মারা, mārā.) A share? (ভাগ, bhāg.) To measure? (মাপা, māpā.) To stand? (দাঁড়ানো, dā̃ṛāno.) To lie down? (শোয়া, shoyā.) Closed? (বন্ধ, bôndhô.) To wipe? (মোছা, mochhā.)
