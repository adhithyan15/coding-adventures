## You'll want to know: ਟਪਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C287-WET287-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟਪਕਣਾ** — *ṭapakṇā* — "to drip; to leak".

> ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਪੁਰਾਣੀ ਛੱਤ ਟਪਕਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *mīṁh vich purāṇī chhatt ṭapakdī hai.*
>
> The old roof drips in the rain.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਛੱਤ ਟਪਕੀ, *oh chhatt ṭapkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟਪਕਣਾ** (*ṭapakṇā*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ṭapp*, "drip, drop", an imitative root; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists Hindi *ṭapaknā*, "to drip" (CDIAL 5444).
