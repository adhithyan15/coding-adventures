Lesson 1 of 5.
ഊർജ്ജം (ūrjjaṁ) — energy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to be ruined, then the Malayalam for to reuse.

You'll want to know: ഊർജ്ജം.
ഊർജ്ജം — ūrjjaṁ — "energy".
സൂര്യനിൽ നിന്ന് നമുക്ക് ഊർജ്ജം കിട്ടും. sūryanil ninnŭ namukkŭ ūrjjaṁ kiṭṭuṁ. We can get energy from the sun.
Written with a doubled ജ്ജ after ർ, the way ഓർമ്മ doubles its മ.

The word, taken apart.
ഊർജ്ജം (ūrjjaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit ūrja, "strong, powerful" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ഭൂഗർഭജലം (bhūgarbhajalaṁ) — groundwater.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for energy, then the Malayalam for a shortage.

You'll want to know: ഭൂഗർഭജലം.
ഭൂഗർഭജലം — bhūgarbhajalaṁ — "groundwater".
വേനലിൽ ഭൂഗർഭജലം താഴുന്നു. vēnalil bhūgarbhajalaṁ tāḻunnu. In the dry season the groundwater level falls.
ഭൂ-, the earth, plus ഗർഭം, the womb, plus ജലം, water: water in the belly of the earth.

The word, taken apart.
ഭൂഗർഭജലം (bhūgarbhajalaṁ) is a Malayalam compound built from three Sanskrit words, bhū, "the earth", garbha, "the womb, the inside", and jala, "water" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ആഗോളതാപനം (āgōḷatāpanaṁ) — global warming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a shortage, then the Malayalam for groundwater.

You'll want to know: ആഗോളതാപനം.
ആഗോളതാപനം — āgōḷatāpanaṁ — "global warming".
ആഗോളതാപനം കാരണം കടലിലെ വെള്ളം ഉയരുന്നു. āgōḷatāpanaṁ kāraṇaṁ kaṭalile veḷḷaṁ uyarunnu. Because of global warming the sea is rising.
ആഗോള, global, from ഗോളം, a globe, plus താപനം, heating.

The word, taken apart.
ആഗോളതാപനം (āgōḷatāpanaṁ) is a modern Malayalam compound built from Sanskrit gola, "a ball, a globe", and tāpana, "burning" (Monier-Williams), which Malayalam uses for heating.

Lesson 5 of 5.
മഴക്കുഴി (maḻakkuḻi) — a rain pit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for groundwater, then the Malayalam for global warming.

You'll want to know: മഴക്കുഴി.
മഴക്കുഴി — maḻakkuḻi — "a rain pit".
വീടിന്റെ പിന്നിൽ ഞങ്ങൾ ഒരു മഴക്കുഴി കുഴിച്ചു. vīṭinṟe pinnil ñaṅṅaḷ oru maḻakkuḻi kuḻiccu. We dug a rain pit behind the house.
A pit dug to catch rainwater so that it sinks into the ground and fills the wells.

The word, taken apart.
മഴക്കുഴി (maḻakkuḻi) is a native Dravidian compound of മഴ, rain (DEDR 4753), and കുഴി, a pit (DEDR 1818).
