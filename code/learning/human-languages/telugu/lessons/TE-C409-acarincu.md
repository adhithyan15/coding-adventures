# ఆచరించు (ācariñcu) — to put into practice; to observe

## You'll want to know: ఆచరించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C409-AIKYATA409-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆచరించు** — *ācariñcu* — "to put into practice; to observe".

> అతను చెప్పిన మాటను తానే ఆచరించాడు.
>
> *atanu ceppina māṭanu tānē ācariñcāḍu.*
>
> He practised what he preached.

Past (I …): ఆచరించాను, *ācariñcānu*.

For customs, rules and advice: ఆచారం, a custom, which you know, is the same family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆచరించు** (*ācariñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ācaraṇa*, "undertaking, practising, performing; conduct" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
