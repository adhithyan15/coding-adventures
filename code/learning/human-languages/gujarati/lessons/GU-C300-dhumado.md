## You'll want to know: ધુમાડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C300-AAG300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ધુમાડો** — *dhumāḍo* — "smoke".

> રસોડામાંથી કાળો ધુમાડો નીકળતો હતો.
>
> *rasoḍāmā̃thī kāḷo dhumāḍo nīkaḷto hato.*
>
> Black smoke was coming out of the kitchen.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધુમાડો** (*dhumāḍo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhūmá*, "smoke", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 6849). It is masculine: **કાળો ધુમાડો**, black smoke.
