## You'll want to know: তৎক্ষণাৎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C287-MUHURTO287-04]; assesses=[] -->

**তৎক্ষণাৎ** — *tôtkkhônāt* — "at once; immediately".

> আওয়াজ শুনে দারোগা তৎক্ষণাৎ বাইরে এলেন।
>
> *āoyāj shune dārogā tôtkkhônāt bāire elen.*
>
> Hearing the noise, the inspector came out at once.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তৎক্ষণাৎ** (*tôtkkhônāt*) is Sanskrit *tatkṣaṇāt*, 'at that very moment' (Monier-Williams): *tat*, 'that', and *kṣaṇa*, 'an instant'. It belongs to the news and to careful speech.
