## You'll want to know: ಸಮಾಧಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C361-SMARAKA361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಾಧಿ** — *samādhi* — "a tomb; a grave".

> ಹಳೆಯ ರಾಜನ ಸಮಾಧಿ ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಕೆಳಗೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *haḷeya rājana samādhi beṭṭada keḷage ide.*
>
> The old king's tomb is below the hill.

Plural: ಸಮಾಧಿಗಳು, *samādhigaḷu*.

In religion ಸಮಾಧಿ is also deep meditation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಾಧಿ** (*samādhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samādhi*, "putting together, joining", with later senses that include deep meditation and a tomb (Monier-Williams).
