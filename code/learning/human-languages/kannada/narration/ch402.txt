5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರ (bhraṣṭācāra) — corruption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to promote, then the Kannada for to invest.

You'll want to know: ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರ.
ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರ — bhraṣṭācāra — "corruption".
ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರದ ವಿರುದ್ಧ ಜನರು ಬೀದಿಗೆ ಇಳಿದರು. bhraṣṭācārada viruddha janaru bīdige iḷidaru. The people took to the streets against corruption.
ಭ್ರಷ್ಟ (fallen) + ಆಚಾರ (conduct).

The word, taken apart.
ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರ (bhraṣṭācāra) is built from Sanskrit bhraṣṭa, "fallen" (CDIAL 9655), and ācāra, "conduct" (CDIAL 1071): fallen conduct.

Before the new one: say the Kannada for corruption, then the Kannada for a bribe.

You'll want to know: ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕ.
ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕ — pāradarśaka — "transparent".
ಆಡಳಿತ ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕವಾಗಿ ಇರಬೇಕು. āḍaḷita pāradarśakavāgi irabēku. Administration should be transparent.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮರೆಮಾಚು (maremācu) — to conceal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a bribe, then the Kannada for transparent.

You'll want to know: ಮರೆಮಾಚು.
ಮರೆಮಾಚು — maremācu — "to conceal".
ಅವನು ತನ್ನ ತಪ್ಪನ್ನು ಮರೆಮಾಚಿದನು. avanu tanna tappannu maremācidanu. He concealed his mistake.
Past (I …): ಮರೆಮಾಚಿದೆ, maremācide.
What you hide takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮರೆಮಾಚು (maremācu) is native Kannada, a compound whose first part is maṟe, "concealment, a cover" (DEDR 4760); the second part, mācu, is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು (śiphārasu māḍu) — to recommend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for transparent, then the Kannada for to conceal.

You'll want to know: ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು.
ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು — śiphārasu māḍu — "to recommend".
ಮ್ಯಾನೇಜರ್ ಲಂಚ ಪಡೆದು ಅವನನ್ನು ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡಿದನು. myānējar lañca paḍedu avanannu kelasakke śiphārasu māḍidanu. The manager took a bribe and recommended him for the job.
Past (I …): ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡಿದೆ, śiphārasu māḍide.
For what: -ಗೆ. ಶಿಫಾರಸು alone is a recommendation.

The word, taken apart.
ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು (śiphārasu māḍu) is built from śiphārasu, a loan from Hindustani sifāriś, "a recommendation", which comes from Persian, with māḍu, "to do".
