## You'll want to know: انعام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C443-ANAM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**انعام** (*an'âm*) — "a tip, a gratuity".

> به گارسون انعام دادیم.
>
> *Be gârson an'âm dâdim.*
>
> We gave the waiter a tip.

*An'âm dâdan* — to tip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*an'âm* was borrowed from Arabic *inʿām* 'that which is bestowed or granted' (EtymDB); in Persian it means a reward, and today a tip.
