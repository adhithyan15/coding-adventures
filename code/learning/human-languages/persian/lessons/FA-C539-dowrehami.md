## You'll want to know: دورهمی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C539-GARDEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دورهمی** (*dowrehami*) — "a get-together (of family or friends)".

> آخر هفته ها در خانه مادربزرگ دورهمی داریم.
>
> *âkhar-e haftehâ dar khâne-ye mâdarbozorg dowrehami dârim.*
>
> At weekends we have a get-together at grandmother's house.

Colloquial: *dowr-e ham* 'around each other' + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dowrehami* is built in Persian on *dowr*, 'circle, round', a loan usually traced to Arabic *dawr*, and native *ham*, 'together', with *-i*: sitting round together. EtymDB has no entry for it.
