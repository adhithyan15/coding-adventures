## You'll want to know: lapicīda, lapicīdae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C471-TORNUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**lapicīda, lapicīdae** — "a stonecutter, a quarryman".

> Lapicīda saxum magnum in forō caedit.
>
> *The stonecutter cuts a large stone in the forum.*

First declension, masculine: *lapicīda, lapicīdae m.* Like *agricola*, a masculine noun in *-a*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lapicīda* is built from *lapis, lapidis* 'stone' and *caedō* 'to cut' (Lewis & Short; an older form is *lapidicīda*). EtymDB lists it without a parent.
