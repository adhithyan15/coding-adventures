Hindi, chapter 526: Free Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फ़ुरसत (fursat) — free time; leisure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to plant (a sapling), then the Hindi for to pull up.

You'll want to know: फ़ुरसत.
फ़ुरसत — fursat — "free time; leisure".
फ़ुरसत (fursat) में मैं कहानियाँ पढ़ता हूँ। Fursat meṁ maiṁ kahāniyā̃ paṛhtā hūṁ. In my free time I read stories.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
फ़ुरसत (fursat) is usually traced to Arabic furṣa, "an occasion, a chance", which reached Hindi through Persian; in Hindi it is time with nothing that must be done.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for embroidery (a pattern sewn in thread), then the Hindi for an ace (in cards).

You'll want to know: शतरंज.
शतरंज — śatrañj — "chess".
दादा जी हर शाम अपने दोस्त के साथ शतरंज खेलते हैं। Dādā jī har śām apne dost ke sāth śatrañj khelte haiṁ. My grandfather plays chess with his friend every evening.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
शतरंज (śatrañj) is usually traced to Persian shatranj, which goes back to Sanskrit caturaṅga, "having four limbs", the four arms of an army, and already "a kind of chess" (CDIAL 4596).
