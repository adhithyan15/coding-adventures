## You'll want to know: નિરાંત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C369-BHAY369-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નિરાંત** — *nirā̃t* — "peace of mind; relief".

> જો પરિણામ સારું આવશે તો મને નિરાંત થશે.
>
> *jo pariṇām sārũ āvśe to mane nirā̃t thaśe.*
>
> If the result is good, I will be relieved.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિરાંત** (*nirā̃t*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "peace, tranquillity", under a reconstructed *nirānti* (CDIAL 7273).
