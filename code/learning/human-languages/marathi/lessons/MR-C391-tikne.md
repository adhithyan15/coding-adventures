## You'll want to know: टिकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C391-GHATNE391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टिकणे** — *ṭikṇe* — "to hold up; to last; to survive".

> ही चप्पल दोन वर्षं टिकली.
>
> *hī cappal don varṣaṁ ṭiklī.*
>
> These sandals lasted two years.

Past (he …): तो टिकला, *to ṭiklā*.

टिकाऊ, durable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टिकणे** (*ṭikṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ṭikṇẽ*, "to stay", under *\*ṭakk-* / *\*ṭikk-*, "remain, stop" (CDIAL 5420).
