## You'll want to know: прилавок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C452-BULOCHNAYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**прилавок** (*prilávok*) — "a counter (in a shop)".

> Продавец стоит за прилавком.
>
> *Prodavéts stoít za prilávkom.*
>
> The shop assistant stands behind the counter.

Masculine; the о drops out: *прилавка*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прилавок* is built from *при-* 'beside' and *лавка* 'a bench; a shop', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *lavŭka* 'a bench, a shop' (EtymDB).
