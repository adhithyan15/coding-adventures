Latin, chapter 201: To talk together, To hand over, To straighten, To celebrate, To pursue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
colloquor, colloquī — to talk together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to refuse, then the Latin for to fly about.

You'll want to know: colloquor, colloquī.
colloquor, colloquī — "to talk together".

The word, taken apart.
colloquor is con-, "together", with loquor, "to speak" (EtymDB 2.1). English colloquial comes from it.
