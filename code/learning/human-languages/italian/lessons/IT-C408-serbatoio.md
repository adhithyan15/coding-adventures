## You'll want to know: il serbatoio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C408-BENZINAIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il serbatoio** — "the tank (for fuel); the reservoir".

> Il serbatoio è quasi vuoto.
>
> *The tank is almost empty.*

*Fare il pieno* — to fill the tank.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*serbatoio* is built on *serbare* 'to keep, to store', which is inherited from Latin *servāre* 'to keep safe' (EtymDB).
