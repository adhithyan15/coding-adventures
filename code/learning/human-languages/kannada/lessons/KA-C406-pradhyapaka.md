## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಾಧ್ಯಾಪಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C406-VISHVAVIDYALAYA406-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಾಧ್ಯಾಪಕ** — *prādhyāpaka* — "a professor".

> ಆ ಪ್ರಾಧ್ಯಾಪಕರು ಗಣಿತ ಕಲಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *ā prādhyāpakaru gaṇita kalisuttāre.*
>
> That professor teaches mathematics.

Plural: ಪ್ರಾಧ್ಯಾಪಕರು, *prādhyāpakaru*.

Respectful plural verb: ಪ್ರಾಧ್ಯಾಪಕರು ಬಂದರು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಾಧ್ಯಾಪಕ** (*prādhyāpaka*) is a modern word built on Sanskrit *adhyāpaka*, "a teacher" (CDIAL 278), with *pra-*, "forward, chief".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
