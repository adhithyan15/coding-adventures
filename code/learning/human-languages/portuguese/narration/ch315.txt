Portuguese, chapter 315: O funeral.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o funeral — the funeral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for mourning, then the Portuguese for the wake.

You'll want to know: o funeral.
o funeral — "the funeral".
O funeral foi na sexta-feira de manhã. The funeral was on Friday morning.
Ir ao funeral — to go to the funeral.

The word, taken apart.
funeral was borrowed from Late Latin fūnerālis "of a funeral" (EtymDB), from fūnus "a funeral, death".

What you've built.

You'll want to know: enterrar.
enterrar — "to bury".

For people and for things.

The word, taken apart.
enterrar is inherited from Vulgar Latin interrāre "to put in the earth" (EtymDB), built on terra "earth".
