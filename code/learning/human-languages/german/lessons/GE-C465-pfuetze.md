## You'll want to know: die Pfütze
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C465-STURM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Pfütze** — "the puddle".

> Das Kind springt mit den Stiefeln in jede Pfütze.
>
> *The child jumps into every puddle in his boots.*

Plural: *die Pfützen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Pfütze* is an early borrowing, Old High German *pfuzzi*, from Latin *puteus* 'well, pit' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short); English *pit* goes back to the same Latin word.
