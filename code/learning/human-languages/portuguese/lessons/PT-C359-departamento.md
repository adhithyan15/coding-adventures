## You'll want to know: o departamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C359-DEPARTAMENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o departamento** — "the department".

> Ela trabalha no departamento de vendas.
>
> *She works in the sales department.*

*O departamento de recursos humanos* — the human resources department.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*departamento* was borrowed from French *département* (EtymDB), built on Old French *departir* "to divide", which goes back to Latin *partīre* "to divide", on *pars* "part" (Lewis & Short).
