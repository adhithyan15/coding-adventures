Persian, chapter 519: Singing.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
آواز خواندن (âvâz khândan) — to sing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to play, then the Persian for to tune.

You'll want to know: آواز خواندن.
آواز خواندن (âvâz khândan) — "to sing".
مادرم در آشپزخانه آواز میخواند. mâdaram dar âshpazkhâne âvâz mikhânad. My mother sings in the kitchen.
âvâz 'song, voice' + khândan 'to read, to sing'; present stem khân-.

The word, taken apart.
âvâz is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Middle Persian word for 'voice, sound, tune' and lists Latin vōx, 'voice', as a cousin (EtymDB). khândan is inherited too.

Lesson 2 of 6.
زمزمه کردن (zemzeme kardan) — to hum, to murmur.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to tune, then the Persian for to sing.

You'll want to know: زمزمه کردن.
زمزمه کردن (zemzeme kardan) — "to hum, to murmur".
او در حمام آهنگ زمزمه میکند. u dar hammâm âhang zemzeme mikonad. He hums tunes in the bath.
zemzeme 'a murmur' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
zemzeme is a loan, usually traced to Arabic zamzama, 'a low murmuring'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ترانه (tarâne) — a song (popular).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to sing, then the Persian for to hum.

You'll want to know: ترانه.
ترانه (tarâne) — "a song (popular)".
این ترانه را از رادیو شنیدم. in tarâne râ az râdiyo shenidam. I heard this song on the radio.
A popular song; âhang is the tune, tarâne the song with words.

The word, taken apart.
tarâne is usually taken as a native Persian word for a song, but EtymDB lists it without a source, and its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
نت (not) — a musical note; sheet music.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a song, then the Persian for rhythm.

You'll want to know: نت.
نت (not) — "a musical note; sheet music".
او بدون نت پیانو میزند. u bedun-e not piyâno mizanad. He plays the piano without sheet music.
not khândan — to read music.

The word, taken apart.
not is a loan, usually traced to French note, 'note'. EtymDB links the spelling only to English net, a different word.
