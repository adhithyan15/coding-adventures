## You'll want to know: बजावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C276-BANSLI276-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बजावणो** — *bajāvṇo* — "to play an instrument".

> भाई बांसळी बजावै है।
>
> *bhāī bā̃sḷī bajāvai hai.*
>
> My brother plays the flute.

Past (I …): म्हैं बांसळी बजाई, *mhaĩ bā̃sḷī bajāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बजावणो** (*bajāvṇo*) is built on the verb CDIAL lists as Old Marwari *bājaï*, "to sound", under Sanskrit *vādyate*, "is made to sound" (CDIAL 11513); as a causative it means "to make sound".
