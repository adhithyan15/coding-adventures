## You'll want to know: der Chor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C515-CHOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Chor** — "choir".

> Meine Mutter singt in einem Chor.
>
> *My mother sings in a choir.*

Plural: *die Chöre*. *Ch* sounds like *k*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Chor* is a learned borrowing from Latin *chorus*, from Greek *chorós* "a dance, a band of dancers and singers" (Lewis & Short).
