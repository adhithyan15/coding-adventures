## You'll want to know: تکرار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C334-BAHAS334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تکرار** — *takrār* — "an altercation; a row".

> دکان پر گاہک اور دکاندار میں تکرار ہوئی۔
>
> *dukān par gāhak aur dukāndār meṅ takrār huī.*
>
> A customer and the shopkeeper had a row in the shop.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تکرار** (*takrār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *takrār*, "repetition"; the sense "a row", words thrown back and forth, is Urdu's own, as far as our sources show.
