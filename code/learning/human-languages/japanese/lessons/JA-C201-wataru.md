# わたる (wataru) — to cross; to go across

## You'll want to know: わたる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C201-ROAD201-01]; assesses=[] -->

**わたる** — *wataru* — "to cross; to go across".

> **しんごうで どうろを わたります。** — *shingō de dōro o watarimasu* — "I cross the road at the traffic lights."

Polite **わたります**, *watarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わたる** (*wataru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
