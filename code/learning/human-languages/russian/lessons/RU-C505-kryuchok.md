## You'll want to know: крючок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C505-FISHING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**крючок** (*kryuchók*) — "a hook".

> На крючок попалась маленькая рыбка.
>
> *Na kryuchók popalás málenkaya rýbka.*
>
> A little fish got caught on the hook.

Masculine; genitive крючка. Also a coat hook.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*крючок* is a diminutive of *крюк* 'a hook'; EtymDB has no entry.
