## You'll want to know: उदाहरणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C347-UDAHARANA347-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उदाहरणम्** — *udāharaṇam* — "an example".

> कृपया एकम् उदाहरणं ददातु।
>
> *kṛpayā ekam udāharaṇaṃ dadātu.*
>
> Please give one example.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उदाहरणम्** (*udāharaṇam*) is built on *ud*, *ā* and the root √hṛ, "to take, to carry"; MW gives *ud-āharaṇa* "declaration" and "an example, an illustration" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
