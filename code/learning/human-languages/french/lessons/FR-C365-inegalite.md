## You'll want to know: l'inégalité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C365-PAUVRETE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'inégalité** — "the inequality".

> Les inégalités entre riches et pauvres augmentent.
>
> *Inequalities between rich and poor are growing.*

Often plural: *les inégalités*. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inégalité* is built from *in-* "not" and *égalité* "equality", which rests on *égal*, from Latin *aequālis* "equal" (Lewis & Short).
