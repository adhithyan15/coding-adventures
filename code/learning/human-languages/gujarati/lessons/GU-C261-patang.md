## You'll want to know: પતંગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C261-KITES261-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પતંગ** — *patang* — "kite".

> **મારો પતંગ દૂર ગયો.** — *māro patang dūr gayo* — "My kite went far."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પતંગ** (*patang*) comes from Sanskrit *pataṅga*, "bird; flying insect", taken over in a learned shape and used for the paper kite (CDIAL 7721). CDIAL lists no Gujarati form.
