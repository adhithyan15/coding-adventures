## You'll want to know: えいぎょうちゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C142-EIGYOUCHUU]; assesses=[] -->

**えいぎょうちゅう** — *eigyōchū* — "open", "open for business", on a
small sign hung on a shop or restaurant door. **ちゅう** at the end means
"in the middle of": the shop is in the middle of doing business. Eight
signs make six beats, *e–i–gyo–o–chu–u*: each small sign joins the one
before it, and each **う** holds the sound before it long.
On a real sign this word is usually printed in kanji, which this book has
not reached yet; the reading is the same.

Read it and act on it: the door says **えいぎょうちゅう**, so you can go in.
