Kannada, chapter 380: Winning People Over.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮನವೊಲಿಸು (manavolisu) — to persuade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to oppose, then the Kannada for mutual.

You'll want to know: ಮನವೊಲಿಸು.
ಮನವೊಲಿಸು — manavolisu — "to persuade".
ಅಮ್ಮನನ್ನು ಮನವೊಲಿಸಲು ನನಗೆ ಒಂದು ವಾರ ಬೇಕಾಯಿತು. ammanannu manavolisalu nanage ondu vāra bēkāyitu. It took me a week to persuade Mother.
Past (I …): ಮನವೊಲಿಸಿದೆ, manavoliside.
The person persuaded takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮನವೊಲಿಸು (manavolisu) is built from two parts: mana, "mind" (Sanskrit manas, CDIAL 9822), and olisu, "to win over", from native ol / oli, "to be pleased, to favour" (DEDR 1006).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪ್ರೇರೇಪಿಸು (prērēpisu) — to motivate; to urge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for mutual, then the Kannada for to persuade.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರೇರೇಪಿಸು.
ಪ್ರೇರೇಪಿಸು — prērēpisu — "to motivate; to urge".
ಶಿಕ್ಷಕಿ ನನ್ನನ್ನು ಓದಲು ಪ್ರೇರೇಪಿಸಿದರು. śikṣaki nannannu ōdalu prērēpisidaru. The teacher motivated me to study.
Past (I …): ಪ್ರೇರೇಪಿಸಿದೆ, prērēpiside.
The person urged takes -ಅನ್ನು, the aim the -ಅಲು form: ಓದಲು, to study.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರೇರೇಪಿಸು (prērēpisu) is usually traced to Sanskrit preraṇa, "setting in motion, urging, inciting" (Monier-Williams), from pra-īr, "to urge on", with the Kannada verb ending -isu; how the shape -ēpisu arose is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮನದಟ್ಟು ಮಾಡು (manadaṭṭu māḍu) — to drive home; to make clear.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಮನದಟ್ಟು ಮಾಡು.
ಮನದಟ್ಟು ಮಾಡು — manadaṭṭu māḍu — "to drive home; to make clear".
ನಿಯಮದ ಉದ್ದೇಶವನ್ನು ಶಿಕ್ಷಕರು ಮಕ್ಕಳಿಗೆ ಮನದಟ್ಟು ಮಾಡಿದರು. niyamada uddēśavannu śikṣakaru makkaḷige manadaṭṭu māḍidaru. The teacher made the purpose of the rule clear to the children.
Past (I …): ಮನದಟ್ಟು ಮಾಡಿದೆ, manadaṭṭu māḍide.
The listener takes -ಗೆ. ಮನದಟ್ಟಾಗು, to sink in.

The word, taken apart.
ಮನದಟ್ಟು ಮಾಡು (manadaṭṭu māḍu) is built from mana, "mind" (Sanskrit manas, CDIAL 9822), joined to daṭṭu, whose history is not settled, with māḍu, "to do".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to motivate, then the Kannada for to drive home.

You'll want to know: ಆಮಿಷ.
ಆಮಿಷ — āmiṣa — "an inducement; a lure".
ಹಣದ ಆಮಿಷಕ್ಕೆ ಅವನು ಒಪ್ಪಲಿಲ್ಲ. haṇada āmiṣakke avanu oppalilla. He did not give in to the lure of money.
ಆಮಿಷ (āmiṣa) ಒಡ್ಡು, to dangle an inducement.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಮಿಷ (āmiṣa) is borrowed from Sanskrit āmiṣa, "flesh, prey", and so "a bait" (CDIAL 1256).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to drive home, then the Kannada for an inducement.

You'll want to know: ಆಕರ್ಷಿಸು.
ಆಕರ್ಷಿಸು — ākarṣisu — "to attract".
ಹೊಸ ಉದ್ಯಾನ ಅನೇಕ ಜನರನ್ನು ಆಕರ್ಷಿಸಿತು. hosa udyāna anēka janarannu ākarṣisitu. The new park attracted many people.

ಆಕರ್ಷಕ, attractive; ಆಕರ್ಷಣೆ, attraction.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಕರ್ಷಿಸು (ākarṣisu) is built on Sanskrit ākarṣaṇa, "pulling, drawing near, attracting" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
