## You'll want to know: طعم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C359-ZAFARAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طعم** (*ta'm*) — "a taste, a flavour".

> طعم این خورش خیلی خوب است.
>
> *Ta'm-e in khoresh kheyli khub ast.*
>
> This stew tastes very good.

*Khoshta'm* "tasty". Close to *maze*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'm* is borrowed from Arabic *ṭa'm*, "taste" (EtymDB).
