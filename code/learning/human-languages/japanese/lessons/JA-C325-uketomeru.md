## You'll want to know: うけとめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C325-SHITAGAU325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うけとめる** — *uketomeru* — "to take to heart; to catch".

> **いけんが ただしいと おもったので、ちちは うけとめました。** — *iken ga tadashii to omotta node, chichi wa uketomemashita* — "My father thought the point was fair, so he took it to heart."

Polite **うけとめます**, *uketomemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが うけとめました。**, *chichi ga uketomemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うけとめる** (*uketomeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *ukeru*, "to receive" and *tomeru*, "to stop", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
