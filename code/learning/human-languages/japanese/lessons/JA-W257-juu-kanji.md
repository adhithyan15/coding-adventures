# 中 — the middle, four strokes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C257-NIHONJUU-01, JA-LEX-KUCHI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *nihonjū*, all over Japan. Then call up **口**, a mouth: three strokes, a box.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-JUU-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-JIN-01, JA-LEX-KUCHI] -->

> **中** — *jū*

Its parts:

- 口 (box)
- a long centre stem through it

Four strokes, three lifts:

1. draw the left side of the box from the top down
2. lift, draw the top from left to right, then without lifting turn down the right side
3. lift, close the base of the box from left to right
4. lift, draw the long centre stem from above the box straight down through it

The box is **口**, wider than it is tall, and the stem comes last, running through it from above to below. Here it is read **じゅう** (*jū*), as in **日本中**; this lesson asks only for that reading.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-JUU-01, JA-LEX-C257-NIHONJUU-01] -->

1. Find **中** in **日本中** and say *nihonjū*.
2. Copy **中** three times, saying *jū* at each one.
3. Find it once more, in **日本中に ともだちが います** (*nihonjū ni tomodachi ga imasu*), and say the line.
