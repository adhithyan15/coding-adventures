German, chapter 330: Mein Standpunkt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Standpunkt — the standpoint, the point of view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for at least, then the German for during.

You'll want to know: der Standpunkt.
der Standpunkt — "the standpoint, the point of view".
Von meinem Standpunkt aus ist das zu teuer. From my point of view, that is too expensive.
Plural: die Standpunkte. Von meinem Standpunkt aus — from where I stand.

The word, taken apart.
Standpunkt is built from Stand "standing, position", inherited from Old High German stand and Proto-Germanic standaz (EtymDB), of the family of stehen "to stand", and Punkt, borrowed from Latin punctum "a prick, a point" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the spot you stand on.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Ansicht — the view, the opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for during, then the German for the standpoint.

You'll want to know: die Ansicht.
die Ansicht — "the view, the opinion".
Meiner Ansicht nach ist der Film zu lang. In my view, the film is too long.
Meiner Ansicht nach works like meiner Meinung nach. Plural: die Ansichten; die Ansichtskarte is a picture postcard.

The word, taken apart.
Ansicht is formed on ansehen "to view, to regard", from an and sehen "to see", which is inherited from Old High German sehan and Proto-Germanic sehwaną (EtymDB): the way a thing looks to you.

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Auffassung — the understanding, the conception (of a question).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the standpoint, then the German for the view.

You'll want to know: die Auffassung.
die Auffassung — "the understanding, the conception (of a question)".
Nach meiner Auffassung muss die Stadt mehr Busse kaufen. As I understand it, the town has to buy more buses.
More formal than die Meinung. Ich bin der Auffassung, dass … — I take the view that …

The word, taken apart.
Auffassung is the noun of auffassen "to take in, to grasp", built on fassen "to seize, to hold", inherited from Old High German fazzōn and Proto-Germanic fatōną (EtymDB): the way you take hold of an idea.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Gesichtspunkt — the aspect, the angle (of a question).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the view, then the German for the understanding.

You'll want to know: der Gesichtspunkt.
der Gesichtspunkt — "the aspect, the angle (of a question)".
Unter diesem Gesichtspunkt ist die Idee gar nicht so schlecht. From this angle, the idea is not so bad at all.
Plural: die Gesichtspunkte. Gesicht here keeps its old sense, sight.

The word, taken apart.
Gesichtspunkt joins Gesicht, here in its older sense "sight", from the family of sehen "to see", to Punkt "point", from Latin punctum (EtymDB). It is usually explained as a loan translation of the Latin term of optics punctum visus, "the point of sight".

Lesson 5 of 5.
vertreten — to advocate (a view), to represent; to stand in for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the understanding, then the German for the aspect.

You'll want to know: vertreten.
vertreten — "to advocate (a view), to represent; to stand in for".
Sie vertritt die Meinung, dass Kinder weniger fernsehen sollten. She holds the view that children should watch less television.
Inseparable and strong: sie vertritt, sie hat vertreten. At work, jemanden vertreten is to stand in for someone.

The word, taken apart.
vertreten is built from ver- and treten "to step, to tread", inherited from Old High German tretan and Proto-Germanic tredaną (EtymDB): you step into someone's place, or step forward for a view.
