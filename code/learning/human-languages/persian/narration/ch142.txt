Persian, chapter 142: To wait, To suggest, To choose, To catch a cold, To get hurt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صبر کردن (sabr kardan) — to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to settle the bill, then the Persian for to order.

You'll want to know: صبر کردن.
صبر کردن — sabr kardan — "to wait".

The word, taken apart.
sabr kardan pairs sabr, borrowed from Arabic ṣabr, "patience, endurance" (root ṣ-b-r, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: پیشنهاد کردن.
پیشنهاد کردن — pishnahâd kardan — "to suggest".

The word, taken apart.
pishnahâd kardan pairs pishnahâd, "suggestion", with kardan. pishnahâd is pish, "forward", with nahâd, "placed" (from nahâdan, "to put"): something put forward. All the pieces are native Persian. kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
سرما خوردن (sarmâ khordan) — to catch a cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to suggest, then the Persian for to choose.

You'll want to know: سرما خوردن.
سرما خوردن — sarmâ khordan — "to catch a cold".

The word, taken apart.
sarmâ khordan pairs sarmâ, "the cold" (from sard, "cold", inherited, EtymDB), with khordan, "to eat": to "eat" the cold, the way Persian also "eats" a blow or a fall. khordan, "to eat", is an inherited verb; EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian xwar-, "to eat".

زخمی شدن (zakhmi shodan) — to get hurt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to choose, then the Persian for to catch a cold.

You'll want to know: زخمی شدن.
زخمی شدن — zakhmi shodan — "to get hurt".

The word, taken apart.
zakhmi shodan pairs zakhmi, "wounded" (zakhm, "wound", with -i), with shodan, "to become". zakhm is native, usually traced to Middle Persian zaxm. shodan, "to become", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian šudan, "to go".
