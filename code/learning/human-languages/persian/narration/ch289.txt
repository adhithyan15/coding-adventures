Persian, chapter 289: Writing --- Four Lines, And The Gap Inside A Word.
1 lesson. It needs your eyes or your hands, so save it for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 1.
Four lines, and the gap that sits inside a word — four Persian lines to one reader, joined with but and because, and the gap that sits inside a word.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Wrap-up Recall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The last lesson asked for writing finished on time. This one asks for writing that holds together.
[pause 1 second]
Pick one person who will read it: a friend, by name.

You'll want to know — two joiners, one letter that will not join.
[pause 2 seconds]
Connected writing is not longer writing. Four short lines can be connected; one long line can fail to be. What makes it connected is that a sentence stops standing on its own.
[pause 2 seconds]
You hold two joiners. ولی sets one thing against another. چون gives the reason.
[pause 2 seconds]
And both of them carry و, the letter that takes the hand of the letter on its right and offers nothing to the left. So each word breaks open after it: و then لی, and چو then ن. A Persian word can have a gap inside it.
[pause 2 seconds]
That makes your spacing part of the spelling. The gap between words has to be clearly wider than the break inside one. Make them equal and a reader cannot tell whether ولی is one word or two.

Writing — connected composition.
[pause 2 seconds]
Write four lines to the person you picked, right to left.
At least one line must use ولی. At least one must use چون to give a reason.
[pause 3 seconds]
Use only words you already write. This lesson adds no vocabulary; the whole exercise is the joining, and a simpler familiar line is better than an invented one. This attempt is untimed.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Cover your first line and read the second alone. Does it still make complete sense without the first? If it does, you wrote a list, like the form one lesson ago.
[pause 2 seconds]
Then find every و you wrote. Is the break after it smaller than the space between your words? It should be.
Four untimed lines close this writing rung. They are not the 30 to 40 word message of the full A1 paper, and they do not prove A1 attainment, readiness, or human validation.
