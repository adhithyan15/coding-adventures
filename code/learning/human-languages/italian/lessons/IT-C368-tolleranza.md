## You'll want to know: la tolleranza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C368-CONVIVENZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la tolleranza** — "the tolerance".

> Ci vuole un po' di tolleranza con i bambini.
>
> *You need a little tolerance with children.*

*Tolleranza zero* — zero tolerance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tolleranza* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tolerantia* "endurance, bearing" (Lewis & Short), from *tolerāre* "to bear, to put up with".
