# Copy one small body block

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

Read the name sentence and the language sentence in the order chosen last
time. Their meanings are already familiar.

## Writing — supported two-sentence block
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy just this block, not the whole message:

> ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਂ ਅਮਨ ਹੈ। ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।

Check **ਹੈ** at the end of the name sentence and **ਹਾਂ** at the end of the
speaker sentence. Keep one space after the first sentence mark.

## Guided Practice — one contrast
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

Point to **ਹੈ**, then **ਹਾਂ**. Aman is named in the first sentence; the
speaker says “I” in the second. Do not repair a different dimension yet.
