## You'll want to know: la cabine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C422-TRAVERSEE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la cabine** — "the cabin".

> Pour la nuit, ils ont pris une cabine avec deux lits.
>
> *For the night they took a cabin with two beds.*

*Une cabine d'essayage* — a fitting room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *cabine* is not settled; it is usually linked to a northern dialect word and to English *cabin*. EtymDB lists it without a source.
