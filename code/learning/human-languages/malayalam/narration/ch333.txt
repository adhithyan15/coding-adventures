Malayalam, chapter 333: Cashew nut, Sugarcane, Sickle, Bindi, Helmet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കശുവണ്ടി (kaśuvaṇṭi) — a cashew nut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for ash, then the Malayalam for a sapling.

You'll want to know: കശുവണ്ടി.
കശുവണ്ടി — kaśuvaṇṭi — "a cashew nut".

The word, taken apart.
കശുവണ്ടി (kaśuvaṇṭi) joins kaśu, from Portuguese caju, "cashew", a word from the Tupi of Brazil, to native aṇṭi, "kernel, nut" (DEDR 126, with Tamil aṇṭi-kkoṭṭai).
