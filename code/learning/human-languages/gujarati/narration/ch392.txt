Gujarati, chapter 392: Ready for Anything.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
સજ્જ (sajj) — fully equipped; ready.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get scattered, then the Gujarati for to get wiped out.

You'll want to know: સજ્જ.
સજ્જ — sajj — "fully equipped; ready".
જો તોફાન આવશે તો પણ અમે સજ્જ છીએ. jo tophān āvśe to paṇ ame sajj chīe. Even if a storm comes, we are ready.

The word, taken apart.
સજ્જ (sajj) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sajja, "fixed, prepared, equipped" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 7.
આત્મવિશ્વાસ (ātmaviśvās) — self-confidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for watchful, then the Gujarati for a device.

You'll want to know: આત્મવિશ્વાસ.
આત્મવિશ્વાસ — ātmaviśvās — "self-confidence".
જો તમારામાં આત્મવિશ્વાસ હશે તો તમે કોઈ પણ મુશ્કેલીને પહોંચી વળશો. jo tamārāmā̃ ātmaviśvās haśe to tame koī paṇ muśkelīne pahõcī vaḷśo. If you believe in yourself, you will cope with any difficulty.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આત્મવિશ્વાસ (ātmaviśvās) is built from Sanskrit ātman, "self", and viśvāsa, "trust" (Monier-Williams): trust in oneself. વિશ્વાસ (viśvās) is the same second word.

ગોઠવાવું — goṭhvāvũ — "to fall into place".
જો બધા મદદ કરશે તો બધું જલદી ગોઠવાઈ જશે. jo badhā madad karśe to badhũ jaldī goṭhvāī jaśe. If everyone helps, everything will soon fall into place.
Past (he …): તે નવા ઘરમાં ગોઠવાઈ ગયો, te navā gharmā̃ goṭhvāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
ગોઠવાવું (goṭhvāvũ) is the -āvũ partner of ગોઠવવું (goṭhavvũ), "to arrange", whose own history is not settled.
