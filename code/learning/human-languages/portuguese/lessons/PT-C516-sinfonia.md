## You'll want to know: a sinfonia

**a sinfonia** — "symphony".

> A sinfonia tem quatro partes.
>
> *The symphony has four parts.*

*Orquestra sinfónica* — symphony orchestra.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sinfonia* comes from Latin *symphōnia* (EtymDB), from Greek *symphōnía* "a sounding together", built on *sýn* "with" and *phōnḗ* "sound".
