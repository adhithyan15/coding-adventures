## You'll want to know: der Klempner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C428-ABFLUSS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Klempner** — "the plumber".

> Der Klempner kommt erst am Montag.
>
> *The plumber is not coming until Monday.*

Same form in the plural. In Austria: *der Installateur*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Klempner* first meant a tinsmith, someone who hammers sheet metal. It is usually traced to *klempern* 'to bang on metal'; EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the origin is hedged.
