Arabic, chapter 344: In the fairy tale.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حطاب (ḥaṭṭāb) — a woodcutter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a coincidence, then the Arabic for to take a risk.

You'll want to know: حطاب.
حطاب — ḥaṭṭāb — "a woodcutter".
كان في الغابة حطاب فقير. kāna fī al-ghāba ḥaṭṭāb faqīr. There was a poor woodcutter in the forest.

The word, taken apart.
حطاب (ḥaṭṭāb) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-ṭ-b (Lane), where ḥaṭab is firewood. The faʿʿāl pattern names the trade: "the firewood man".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to take a risk, then the Arabic for a woodcutter.

You'll want to know: سحر.
سحر — saḥara — "to enchant, to bewitch".
سحرت الموسيقى الجمهور. saḥarat al-mūsīqā al-jumhūr. The music enchanted the audience.
Present: يسحر, yasḥar.
The noun سحر (saḥara), siḥr, magic, is spelled the same.

The word, taken apart.
سحر (saḥara) is built on the native Arabic root s-ḥ-r (Lane), "to enchant"; siḥr is magic, and saḥar, the time before dawn, is spelled the same.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a woodcutter, then the Arabic for to enchant.

You'll want to know: تحول.
تحول — taḥawwala — "to turn into, to change".
تحول الضفدع إلى أمير. taḥawwala aḍ-ḍifdaʿ ilā amīr. The frog turned into a prince.
Present: يتحول, yataḥawwal.
It takes إلى: تحول (taḥawwala) إلى.

The word, taken apart.
تحول (taḥawwala) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-w-l (Lane), "to change, to shift", the root of ḥāl, a state. It is Form V, "to change oneself".
