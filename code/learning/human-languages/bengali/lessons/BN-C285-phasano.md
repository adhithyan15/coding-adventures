## You'll want to know: ফাঁসানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C285-HUMKI285-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ফাঁসানো** — *phā̃sāno* — "to frame; to trap (someone)".

> আসল চোর আমার ভাইকে ফাঁসাতে চাইল।
>
> *āsôl chor āmār bhāike phā̃sāte chāilô.*
>
> The real thief tried to frame my brother.

Past (he/she …): সেই চোর তাকে ফাঁসাল, *sei chor tāke phā̃sālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফাঁসানো** (*phā̃sāno*) comes down from Sanskrit *spāśayati*, 'causes to be bound' (CDIAL 13814, which lists Bengali *phā̃sā*, 'to be ensnared'). A ফাঁস is a noose: to frame someone is to slip a noose on them.
