## You'll want to know: کیمپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C297-RAHAT297-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کیمپ** — *kaimp* — "a relief camp; a camp".

> شہر کے باہر ایک کیمپ تھا۔
>
> *shahr ke bāhar ek kaimp thā.*
>
> There was a camp outside the city.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is borrowed from English *camp*. After a flood or an earthquake, people live in a **کیمپ** for a time.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
