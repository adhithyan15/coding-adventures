# Retrieve the regular-group frame

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-MILDE-188] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Hear “we meet regularly” and prepare the complete frame.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-MILNA-188, PA-FORM-MILDE-188] -->

Do not replace the regular-group form with the infinitive. Read the subject,
the meeting form, and the copula as one measured frame.
