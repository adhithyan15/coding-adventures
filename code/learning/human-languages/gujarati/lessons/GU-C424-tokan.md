## You'll want to know: ટોકન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C424-COUNTER424-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ટોકન** — *ṭokan* — "a token (a numbered slip for your turn)".

> બેંકમાં મને પાંચ નંબરનું ટોકન મળ્યું.
>
> *beṅkmā̃ mane pā̃c nambarnũ ṭokan maḷyũ.*
>
> At the bank I got token number five.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટોકન** (*ṭokan*) is borrowed from English *token*: at a bank or a clinic, the numbered slip that keeps your place in the queue.
