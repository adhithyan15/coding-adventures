French, chapter 292: Les enquêteurs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'enquêteur — the investigator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the patrol, then the French for the squad.

You'll want to know: l'enquêteur.
l'enquêteur — "the investigator".
Les enquêteurs ont parlé avec les voisins. The investigators talked to the neighbours.
Feminine: l'enquêtrice.

The word, taken apart.
enquêteur is built on the noun for "an inquiry", which is inherited from Latin inquīsīta "things sought", from inquīrere "to seek into", built on quaerere "to seek" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
le détective — the private detective.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the investigator, then the French for the inspector.

You'll want to know: le détective.
le détective — "the private detective".
Il lit des romans de détectives. He reads detective novels.
Un détective privé works for clients; police officers are inspecteurs or enquêteurs.

The word, taken apart.
détective is usually traced to English detective (EtymDB lists no parent), which is built on Latin dētēctus, from dētegere "to uncover" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'empreinte — the print, the fingerprint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the private detective, then the French for the clue.

You'll want to know: l'empreinte.
l'empreinte — "the print, the fingerprint".
Il y avait des empreintes sur le verre. There were prints on the glass.
Les empreintes digitales — fingerprints; l'empreinte carbone — the carbon footprint.

The word, taken apart.
empreinte is the past participle of Old French empreindre "to press in", which is inherited from Latin imprimere "to press into" (Lewis & Short).
