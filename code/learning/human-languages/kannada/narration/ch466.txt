Kannada, chapter 466: Living in the Suburbs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉಪನಗರ (upanagara) — a suburb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a place, then the Kannada for a centre.

You'll want to know: ಉಪನಗರ.
ಉಪನಗರ — upanagara — "a suburb".
ನಾವು ನಗರದ ಹೊರಗಿನ ಒಂದು ಉಪನಗರದಲ್ಲಿ ಮನೆ ಹುಡುಕಿದೆವು. nāvu nagarada horagina ondu upanagaradalli mane huḍukidevu. We looked for a house in a suburb outside the city.
ಉಪ-, near or secondary, plus ನಗರ, a city.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಪನಗರ (upanagara) is borrowed from Sanskrit upanagara, "a suburb" (Monier-Williams), from upa-, "near", and nagara, "a city".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವಾಸಿಸು (vāsisu) — to reside; to live.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a centre, then the Kannada for a suburb.

You'll want to know: ವಾಸಿಸು.
ವಾಸಿಸು — vāsisu — "to reside; to live".
ನನ್ನ ಅಕ್ಕ ಉಪನಗರದಲ್ಲಿ ವಾಸಿಸುತ್ತಾಳೆ. nanna akka upanagaradalli vāsisuttāḷe. My elder sister lives in a suburb.
Past (I …): ವಾಸಿಸಿದೆ, vāsiside.
A formal word for ಇರು; ವಾಸ is a stay, a residence.

The word, taken apart.
ವಾಸಿಸು (vāsisu) is built from Sanskrit vāsa, "an abode, staying" (CDIAL 11591), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪುರಸಭೆ (purasabhe) — a town council; a municipality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a suburb, then the Kannada for to reside.

You'll want to know: ಪುರಸಭೆ.
ಪುರಸಭೆ — purasabhe — "a town council; a municipality".
ಪುರಸಭೆ ಬೀದಿಗಳಿಂದ ಕಸ ಸಂಗ್ರಹಿಸುತ್ತದೆ. purasabhe bīdigaḷinda kasa saṅgrahisuttade. The town council collects rubbish from the streets.
ಪುರ, a town, plus ಸಭೆ, a meeting or council.

The word, taken apart.
ಪುರಸಭೆ (purasabhe) is built from two Sanskrit words: pura, "a fortress, a town" (CDIAL 8278), and sabhā, "an assembly, a council" (CDIAL 13167).

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಬೀದಿದೀಪ.
ಬೀದಿದೀಪ — bīdidīpa — "a streetlight".
ನಮ್ಮ ಬೀದಿಯ ಬೀದಿದೀಪ ಒಂದು ವಾರದಿಂದ ಉರಿಯುತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ. namma bīdiya bīdidīpa ondu vāradinda uriyuttilla. The streetlight on our street has not been on for a week.
ಬೀದಿ, a street, plus ದೀಪ, a lamp.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೀದಿದೀಪ (bīdidīpa) is a Kannada compound built from ಬೀದಿ, "a street", from Sanskrit vīthi, "a row, a road" (Monier-Williams), and ದೀಪ, "a lamp" (CDIAL 6348).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Kannada for a town council, then the Kannada for a streetlight.

You'll want to know: ಚರಂಡಿ.
ಚರಂಡಿ — caraṇḍi — "a drain".
ಮಳೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಚರಂಡಿ ತುಂಬಿ ನೀರು ರಸ್ತೆಗೆ ಬಂದಿತು. maḷeyalli caraṇḍi tumbi nīru rastege banditu. In the rain the drain filled up and water came onto the road.
The open drain beside a street; ಒಳಚರಂಡಿ is an underground sewer.

The word, taken apart.
ಚರಂಡಿ (caraṇḍi) is a Kannada word for a drain whose origin is not settled.
