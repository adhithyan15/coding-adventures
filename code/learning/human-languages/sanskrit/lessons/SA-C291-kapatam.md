## You'll want to know: कपटम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C291-VANCANA291-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कपटम्** — *kapaṭam* — "fraud; a trick".

> एतत् सर्वं कपटम् अस्ति।
>
> *etat sarvaṃ kapaṭam asti.*
>
> All this is a fraud.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कपटम्** (*kapaṭam*) MW gives *kapaṭa* as fraud, deceit and cheating, and reports a commentator's derivation from √kamp, "to tremble", so its origin is uncertain (MW).
