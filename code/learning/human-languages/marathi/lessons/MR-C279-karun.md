# करून (karūn) — having done it, and then

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-PHRASE-BHETUN-ANAND-JHALA, MR-GRAMMAR-LYAAVAR, MR-GRAMMAR-C279-FIRST-THEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "pleased to meet you". (*bheṭūn ānand jhālā*.) **भेटून** is "having met": the form this lesson teaches has been in your mouth since the first meeting.

## You'll want to know: -ऊन, 'having done'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C279-CONJUNCTIVE-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-JANE, MR-LEX-JHOPNE, MR-LEX-GHENE, MR-LEX-YENE, MR-LEX-PAHNE, MR-LEX-BASNE] -->

> **मी घरी जाऊन झोपलो.**
>
> *mī gharī jāūn jhoplo.* — "I went home and slept."

Take **-णे** off and put **-ऊन** on: **करणे** → **करून** (*karūn*), **बसणे** → **बसून** (*basūn*), **पाहणे** → **पाहून** (*pāhūn*). A stem ending in a vowel keeps it: **जाणे** → **जाऊन** (*jāūn*), **येणे** → **येऊन** (*yeūn*), **घेणे** → **घेऊन** (*gheūn*).

The tense comes once, at the end: **जाऊन झोपलो** is "went and slept". **तो चहा घेऊन आला** (*to cahā gheūn ālā*), "having taken tea he came", is how Marathi says "he brought tea".

## Grammar Lens: -ऊन or -ल्यावर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C279-SAME-DOER-01]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-LYAAVAR, MR-LEX-C153-QUAL153-05, MR-LEX-C74-HEALTH74-03] -->

> **घरी पोहोचल्यावर मी झोपलो.**
>
> *gharī pohocalyāvar mī jhoplo.* — "After I reached home, I slept."

**-ऊन** joins two actions of **one** doer, one after the other. **-ल्यावर** says "after", and the doer can change: **मी घरी पोहोचल्यावर आई आली** (*mī gharī pohocalyāvar āī ālī*), "after I reached home, Mother came".
