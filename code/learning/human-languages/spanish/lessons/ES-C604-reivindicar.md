## You'll want to know: reivindicar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C604-PROTESTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**reivindicar** — "to demand, to claim (a right)".

> Los trabajadores reivindican mejores sueldos.
>
> *The workers are demanding better pay.*

Spelling: *reivindiqué*. *La reivindicación* — the demand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reivindicar* is a learned word built from Latin *rei*, a form of *res* 'thing, property', and *vindicare* 'to claim' (Lewis & Short).
