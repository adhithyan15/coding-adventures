## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कावळा** (*kāvḷā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kāka*, "crow", with an added *-la-* (CDIAL 2993). CDIAL notes that the word imitates the bird's call.
