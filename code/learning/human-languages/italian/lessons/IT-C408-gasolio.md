## You'll want to know: il gasolio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C408-BENZINAIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il gasolio** — "the diesel (fuel)".

> La mia macchina va a gasolio.
>
> *My car runs on diesel.*

Also *il diesel*. *Andare a gasolio* — to run on diesel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gasolio* is built from *gas*, a word coined in Dutch from Greek *kháos* 'empty space', and *olio* 'oil', which is inherited from Latin *oleum* (EtymDB).
