Gujarati, chapter 325: The Fight for Freedom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
આઝાદી (ājhādī) — freedom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for control, then the Gujarati for to overthrow.

You'll want to know: આઝાદી.
આઝાદી — ājhādī — "freedom".
લોકો આઝાદી માટે લડ્યા. loko ājhādī māṭe laḍyā. People fought for freedom.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આઝાદી (ājhādī) is usually traced to Persian āzādī, "freedom", the noun of āzād, free. It is feminine: આઝાદી માટે, for freedom.

અંગ્રેજ (angrej) is usually traced, through Persian angrez, to Portuguese inglês, "English". In the plural it is the English rulers of colonial times: અંગ્રેજો.

Lesson 4 of 5.
આંદોલન (ā̃dolan) — a movement; a campaign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a slave, then the Gujarati for an Englishman.

You'll want to know: આંદોલન.
આંદોલન — ā̃dolan — "a movement; a campaign".
ગામના લોકોએ મોટું આંદોલન કર્યું. gāmnā lokoe moṭũ ā̃dolan karyũ. The villagers started a big campaign.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
આંદોલન (ā̃dolan) is a tatsama, Sanskrit āndolana, "swinging, a swing" (Monier-Williams); the sense of a stirring of people is the modern language's. It is neuter: મોટું આંદોલન.

Lesson 5 of 5.
બળવો (baḷvo) — a revolt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an Englishman, then the Gujarati for a campaign.

You'll want to know: બળવો.
બળવો — baḷvo — "a revolt".
એક રાત્રે સૈનિકોનો બળવો શરૂ થયો. ek rātre sainikono baḷvo śarū thayo. One night the soldiers' revolt began.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બળવો (baḷvo) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. It is masculine: બળવો થયો, there was a revolt; બળવો કર્યો, they rose up.
