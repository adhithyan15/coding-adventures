## You'll want to know: सूजना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C486-DUKHNA486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सूजना** — *sūjnā* — "to swell (of the body)".

> मोच से अरुण का पैर सूज गया।
>
> *moc se Aruṇ kā pair sūj gayā.*
>
> Arun's foot swelled up from the sprain.

Past (it …): वह पैर सूज गया, *vah pair sūj gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूजना** (*sūjnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *sūjnā*, "to swell", under Sanskrit *śūyatē*, "swells" (CDIAL 12568). *sūjan*, "a swelling", is its noun.
