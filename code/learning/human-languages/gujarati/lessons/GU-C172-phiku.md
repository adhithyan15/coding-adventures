## You'll want to know: ફીકું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C172-STALE172-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ફીકું** — *phīkũ* — "bland, pale".

> **આ શાક ફીકું છે.** — *ā śāk phīkũ che* — "This curry is bland."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફીકું** (*phīkũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*phikka*, "defective", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "pale, tasteless" (CDIAL 9037).
