## You'll want to know: pēnicillus, pēnicillī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C468-TINGO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**pēnicillus, pēnicillī** — "a paintbrush, a fine brush".

> Pictor pēnicillō tenuī flōrēs pingit.
>
> *The painter paints flowers with a fine brush.*

Second declension, masculine: *pēnicillus, pēnicillī m.* (also *pēnicillum n.*). English *pencil* comes from it: the first pencils were fine brushes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pēnicillus* is a diminutive of *pēniculus* 'brush' (Lewis & Short), which first meant 'a little tail', since brushes were made of ox-tails and horse-tails. English *pencil* comes from it through French.
