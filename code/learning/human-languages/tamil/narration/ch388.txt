Tamil, chapter 388: Approval and Appointments.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ஒப்புதல் (opputal) — approval; consent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a pledge, then the Tamil for to fulfil.

You'll want to know: ஒப்புதல்.
ஒப்புதல் — opputal — "approval; consent".
இந்தத் திட்டத்துக்கு அரசின் ஒப்புதல் கிடைத்தது. intat tiṭṭattukku araciṉ opputal kiṭaittatu. This plan got the approval of the government.
ஒப்புதல் (opputal) கொடு, to approve.

The word, taken apart.
ஒப்புதல் (opputal) is native: the verbal noun in -தல் of oppu, "to agree, accept", from o, "to be suited, acceptable" (DEDR 924).

Lesson 2 of 5.
அங்கீகரி (aṅkīkari) — to recognise officially; to accredit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to fulfil, then the Tamil for approval.

You'll want to know: அங்கீகரி.
அங்கீகரி — aṅkīkari — "to recognise officially; to accredit".
அரசு இந்தப் பள்ளியை அங்கீகரித்தது. aracu intap paḷḷiyai aṅkīkarittatu. The government recognised this school.
Past (I …): அங்கீகரித்தேன், aṅkīkarittēṉ.
What is recognised takes -ஐ. The noun is அங்கீகாரம், recognition.

The word, taken apart.
அங்கீகரி (aṅkīkari) is a verb built on Sanskrit aṅgīkāra, "agreement, promise" (Monier-Williams), from aṅgī-kṛ, "to make one's own, to accept".

Lesson 3 of 5.
நியமி (niyami) — to appoint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for approval, then the Tamil for to recognise officially.

You'll want to know: நியமி.
நியமி — niyami — "to appoint".
நிறுவனம் ஒரு புதிய மேலாளரை நியமித்தது. niṟuvaṉam oru putiya mēlāḷarai niyamittatu. The company appointed a new manager.
Past (I …): நியமித்தேன், niyamittēṉ.
Who is appointed takes -ஐ; as what: மேலாளராக நியமி (niyami).

The word, taken apart.
நியமி (niyami) is a verb built on Sanskrit niyama, "restraint; fixed law" (CDIAL 7250), with the loan-verb ending -இ: to fix someone in a post.

Lesson 4 of 5.
தெரிவி (terivi) — to inform; to convey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to recognise officially, then the Tamil for to appoint.

You'll want to know: தெரிவி.
தெரிவி — terivi — "to inform; to convey".
அவர் தன் முடிவை எங்களுக்குத் தெரிவித்தார். avar taṉ muṭivai eṅkaḷukkut terivittār. He informed us of his decision.
Past (I …): தெரிவித்தேன், terivittēṉ.
The person told takes -க்கு: கருத்துத் தெரிவி (terivi), to give one's view.

The word, taken apart.
தெரிவி (terivi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3419 lists Tamil teri, "to be seen, understood", and its causative terivi, "to make known".

Lesson 5 of 5.
அமர்வு (amarvu) — a session; a sitting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to appoint, then the Tamil for to inform.

You'll want to know: அமர்வு.
அமர்வு — amarvu — "a session; a sitting".
நாடாளுமன்றத்தின் அடுத்த அமர்வு திங்கள்கிழமை தொடங்கும். nāṭāḷumaṉṟattiṉ aṭutta amarvu tiṅkaḷkiḻamai toṭaṅkum. The next session of parliament begins on Monday.
From அமர், to sit.

The word, taken apart.
அமர்வு (amarvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 161 lists Tamil amar, "to abide, remain, rest", and amarvu, "abode"; a session is a sitting together.
