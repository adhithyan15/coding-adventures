# આઘાત (āghāt) — a shock (of bad news)

## You'll want to know: આઘાત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C298-MRUTYU298-03]; assesses=[] -->

**આઘાત** — *āghāt* — "a shock (of bad news)".

> દીકરાના મૃત્યુથી માતાને મોટો આઘાત લાગ્યો.
>
> *dīkrānā mṛtyuthī mātāne moṭo āghāt lāgyo.*
>
> Her son's death came as a terrible shock to the mother.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આઘાત** (*āghāt*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *āghāta*, "striking, a blow" (Monier-Williams): bad news that hits like a blow. It **લાગે છે**, strikes you: **મોટો આઘાત લાગ્યો**.
