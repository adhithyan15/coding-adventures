## You'll want to know: शुरुआत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C559-KALPANA559-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शुरुआत** — *śuruāt* — "a beginning; a start".

> नए साल की शुरुआत अच्छी हुई।
>
> *Nae sāl kī śuruāt acchī huī.*
>
> The new year got off to a good start.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शुरुआत** (*śuruāt*) is usually traced to Arabic *shurūʿ*, "a beginning", through Persian. Hindi adds -āt to make a noun of it.
