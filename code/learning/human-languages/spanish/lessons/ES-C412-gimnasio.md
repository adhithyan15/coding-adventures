# el gimnasio — the long form behind gym

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] English shortens **gymnasium** to **gym**. Spanish keeps more of the
old word: **el gimnasio**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-GIMNASIO]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish **gimnasio** came through Latin **gymnasium** from Greek
**gymnásion**, a place for exercise and training. English **gymnasium** is the
same travelled word, and **gym** is simply its clipped form. Spanish spelling
turns the learned **gy-** into **gi-**, pronounced with the strong Spanish
**g** sound heard before **i**.

## Grammar Lens: naming the place
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-GIMNASIO] -->

The noun is masculine: **el gimnasio**, **un gimnasio**. Opening-hours signs
often place the name first: **Gimnasio Atlas — abierto de 7 a 23 h**.
