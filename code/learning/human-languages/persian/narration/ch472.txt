Persian, chapter 472: Protecting nature.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طبیعت (tabi'at) — nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a blossom, then the Persian for to bloom.

You'll want to know: طبیعت.
طبیعت (tabi'at) — "nature".
ما جمعه ها به دل طبیعت میرویم. Mâ jom'e-hâ be del-e tabi'at miravim. On Fridays we go out into the countryside.
Del-e tabi'at — the heart of nature, out in the wild.

The word, taken apart.
tabi'at was borrowed from Arabic ṭabīʿa 'nature, natural disposition' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
حفاظت کردن (hefâzat kardan) — to protect, to conserve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to bloom, then the Persian for nature.

You'll want to know: حفاظت کردن.
حفاظت کردن (hefâzat kardan) — "to protect, to conserve".
مردم روستا از جنگل حفاظت میکنند. Mardom-e rustâ az jangal hefâzat mikonand. The villagers protect the forest.
The thing protected takes az. Mantaqe-ye hefâzat-shode — a protected area.

The word, taken apart.
hefâzat kardan is built from hefâzat 'protection', borrowed from Arabic ḥifāẓa, which EtymDB derives from ḥafiẓa 'to guard, to know by heart', the source of hefz kardan 'to keep, to memorize', and kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
گردشگر (gardeshgar) — a tourist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for nature, then the Persian for to protect.

You'll want to know: گردشگر.
گردشگر (gardeshgar) — "a tourist".
در تابستان این جزیره پر از گردشگر است. Dar tâbestân in jazire por az gardeshgar ast. In summer this island is full of tourists.
gardesh 'outing' + -gar 'doer'. Tourism is gardeshgari.

The word, taken apart.
gardeshgar is a native compound of gardesh 'an outing', built on gashtan 'to turn, to wander', and the ending -gar 'doer'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
آتشفشان (âtashfeshân) — a volcano.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to protect, then the Persian for a tourist.

You'll want to know: آتشفشان.
آتشفشان (âtashfeshân) — "a volcano".
این کوه یک آتشفشان خاموش است. In kuh yek âtashfeshân-e khâmush ast. This mountain is an extinct volcano.
Literally 'fire-scatterer'.

The word, taken apart.
âtashfeshân is a native compound of âtash 'fire', inherited from Middle Persian ātaxš (EtymDB), and feshân, the stem of feshândan 'to scatter'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
زیستگاه (zistgâh) — a habitat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a tourist, then the Persian for a volcano.

You'll want to know: زیستگاه.
زیستگاه (zistgâh) — "a habitat".
این تالاب زیستگاه پرنده های زیادی است. In tâlâb zistgâh-e parande-hâ-ye ziyâdi ast. This wetland is the habitat of many birds.
zist 'living' + gâh 'place'.

The word, taken apart.
zistgâh is a native compound of zist, the past stem of zistan 'to live', an inherited verb usually traced to Middle Persian zīstan, and gâh 'place'.
