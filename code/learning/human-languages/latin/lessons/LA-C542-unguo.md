## You'll want to know: unguō, unguere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C542-STRI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**unguō, unguere** — "to anoint, rub with oil".

> Post balneum corpus oleō unguimus.
>
> *After the bath we rub the body with oil.*

*unguō, unguere, ūnxī, ūnctum*; also spelt *ungō*. *Unguentum*, already known, comes from it. English *unction*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**unguō** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to anoint, salve" (EtymDB). *unguentum* "ointment" comes from it, and English *unction* and *ointment* from its family.
