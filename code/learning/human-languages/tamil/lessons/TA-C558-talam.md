## You'll want to know: தாளம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C558-MELAM558-04]; assesses=[] -->

**தாளம்** — *tāḷam* — "rhythm; a beat".

> தாளம் தவறினால் பாடல் கெட்டுவிடும்.
>
> *tāḷam tavaṟiṉāl pāṭal keṭṭuviṭum.*
>
> If the beat goes wrong, the song is spoiled.

One keeps time with the hand: தாளம் போடு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தாளம்** (*tāḷam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tāla*, "a cymbal" (CDIAL 5801), and in music "a time measure".
