## You'll want to know: फूँकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C428-KAMANA428-02]; assesses=[] -->

**फूँकना** — *phū̃knā* — "to blow (on); to squander".

> दादी ने फूँककर दीया बुझाया।
>
> *dādī ne phū̃kkar dīyā bujhāyā.*
>
> Grandmother blew out the lamp.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना पैसा फूँक दिया, *usne apnā paisā phū̃k diyā*.

पैसा फूँकना, to blow your money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फूँकना** (*phū̃knā*) goes back to *\*phūtka*, "blowing", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9102), which lists Hindi *phū̃knā*, "to blow". Money that is blown on is gone like smoke.
