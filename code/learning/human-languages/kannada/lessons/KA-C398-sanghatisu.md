# ಸಂಘಟಿಸು (saṅghaṭisu) — to mobilise; to organise

## You'll want to know: ಸಂಘಟಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C398-PRATIBHATISU398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಘಟಿಸು** — *saṅghaṭisu* — "to mobilise; to organise".

> ಅವಳು ಕೆಲಸಗಾರರನ್ನು ಸಂಘಟಿಸಿದಳು.
>
> *avaḷu kelasagārarannu saṅghaṭisidaḷu.*
>
> She organised the workers.

Past (I …): ಸಂಘಟಿಸಿದೆ, *saṅghaṭiside*.

ಸಂಘಟನೆ, an organisation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಘಟಿಸು** (*saṅghaṭisu*) is built on Sanskrit *saṃghaṭana*, "union, junction" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
