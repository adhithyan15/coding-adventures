## You'll want to know: dōngbian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C117-COMPASS117-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dōngbian* — "the east; on the east side".

> *chēzhàn zài dōngbian* — The station is on the east side.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dōngbian* is built from dōng 'east' and 边 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tuwng* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary calls the east the direction of spring, and repeats an old reading of the character as the sun inside a tree.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
