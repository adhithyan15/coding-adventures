# идите прямо, поверните направо — following the answer

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KAK-PROYTI-01, RU-GRAMMAR-K-DATIVE-01, RU-LEX-C87-LOC87-01, RU-LEX-C86-LOC86-04, RU-LEX-C86-LOC86-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask the way to the museum. (*Izvinítye, kak proytí k muzéyu?*)
Then say "straight on", "to the right", "to the left". (*Pryámo. Naprávo.
Nalévo.*)

## Grammar Lens: идите and поверните
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-IDITE-POVERNITE-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-IMPERATIVE-ITE-01, RU-LEX-C146-ACT146-02, RU-LEX-IDTI] -->

The answer comes as instructions, with the same **-ите** as *извините*
and *повторите*:

> **Идите прямо.** — *idítye pryámo* — Go straight on.
> **Поверните направо.** — *povernítye naprávo* — Turn right.

**Идите** is *идти* as an instruction. **Поверните** comes from
*повернуть*, the perfective partner of *поворачивать*: one turn, at one
corner. To a friend, the *-ите* drops to **иди**, **поверни**.

## Grammar Lens: as far as, and at
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-DO-NA-ROUTE-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-DO-GENITIVE, RU-LEX-C89-LOC89-05, RU-LEX-C89-LOC89-04, RU-LEX-C74-WHEN-05] -->

Two small words carry a route. **до** is "as far as", and the noun after it
changes as in *до свидания*: *светофор*, **до светофора**. **на** is "at" a
crossroads: *перекрёсток*, **на перекрёстке**.

> **Идите прямо до светофора, потом поверните налево.** — *idítye pryámo
> do svetofóra, potóm povernítye nalévo* — Go straight on to the lights,
> then turn left.
> **На перекрёстке поверните направо.** — *na perekryóstke povernítye
> naprávo* — At the crossroads, turn right.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-IDITE-POVERNITE-01, RU-LEX-C73-WHERE2-03, RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-03] -->

> **— Как пройти к музею? — Идите прямо, потом направо. Музей справа.**

*Muzéy správa*: "the museum is on the right". **Справа** is where a thing
stands; **направо** is where you go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C86-LOC86-04, RU-LEX-C146-ACT146-02] -->

**направо** is *на*, "onto", with the root of *правый*, "right": onto
the right hand. EtymDB 2.1 traces *правый* to Proto-Slavic *\*pravŭ*,
"right, correct", so the right hand and being right share one root. **поверните** is on the root of
*вертеть*, "to turn", which EtymDB takes back through Proto-Slavic
*\*vĭrtěti* to Proto-Indo-European *\*wert-*: the root of Latin *vertere*
and English *-ward*.
