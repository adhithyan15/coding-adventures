Italian, chapter 432: Pernottare.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
alloggiare — to lodge, to stay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the cancellation fee, then the Italian for to bring forward.

You'll want to know: alloggiare.
alloggiare — "to lodge, to stay".
Alloggiamo in un piccolo albergo in centro. We are staying in a small hotel in the centre.
L'alloggio — lodging, accommodation.

The word, taken apart.
alloggiare is built on loggia (EtymDB), which was borrowed from Old French loge 'shelter, arbour', from Frankish laubja 'arbour'.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: pernottare.
pernottare — "to stay the night, to spend the night".
Pernottiamo a metà strada e ripartiamo all'alba. We spend the night halfway and set off again at dawn.
Formal; in speech, passare la notte.

The word, taken apart.
pernottare is a learned borrowing from Latin pernoctāre 'to stay all night', built from per and nox, noctis 'night' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
soggiornare — to stay (for some time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to stay the night, then the Italian for the overnight stay.

You'll want to know: soggiornare.
soggiornare — "to stay (for some time)".
Abbiamo soggiornato una settimana al lago. We stayed a week at the lake.
Il soggiorno is a stay, and also the living room.

The word, taken apart.
soggiornare is usually traced through Old French sojorner to Vulgar Latin subdiurnāre 'to spend the day', built on diurnus 'of the day', from diēs 'day' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
il portiere — the porter, the doorman; the goalkeeper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the overnight stay, then the Italian for to stay.

You'll want to know: il portiere.
il portiere — "the porter, the doorman; the goalkeeper".
Il portiere ci dà la chiave della camera. The porter gives us the room key.
In football, il portiere is the goalkeeper.

The word, taken apart.
portiere is built on porta 'door', which is inherited from Latin porta 'gate' (EtymDB).
