Arabic, chapter 57: Teacher, To drink, To hear, To sit, To sleep.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
معلم (muʿallim) — a teacher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a woman, then the Arabic for a guest.

You'll want to know: معلم.
معلم — muʿallim — "a teacher".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: muʿallim]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: muʿallim, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say muʿallim]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a woman, then the Arabic for a guest, then say muʿallim again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does معلم (muʿallim) mean? ("A teacher".) Say it once more.
