## You'll want to know: lamentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C716-LASTIMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lamentar** — "to regret, to be sorry about".

> Lamento llegar tarde.
>
> *I am sorry I am late.*

Formal: *lamentamos las molestias* — we apologise for the inconvenience.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lamentar* comes from Latin *lāmentārī*, built on *lāmentum* "wail, wailing" (EtymDB).
