## You'll want to know: ثرثار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C502-THARTHAR502-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ثرثار** — *tharthār* — "talkative; a chatterbox".

> جارنا ثرثار ويتكلم ساعات.
>
> *jārunā tharthār wa-yatakallamu sāʿāt.*
>
> Our neighbour is talkative and talks for hours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ثرثار** (*tharthār*) goes with the verb *tharthara*, "to chatter"; its doubled shape, *thar-thar*, is usually said to echo endless talk, but the origin is not settled.
