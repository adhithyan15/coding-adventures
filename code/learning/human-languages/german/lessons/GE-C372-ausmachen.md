## You'll want to know: ausmachen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C372-VEREINBARUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ausmachen** — "to arrange (informal); to switch off; to matter".

> Wir haben ausgemacht, dass wir uns um acht treffen.
>
> *We arranged to meet at eight.*

Separable: *wir machen aus*, *wir haben ausgemacht*. Also to switch off: *das Licht ausmachen*; and to matter: *Das macht nichts aus* — that does not matter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ausmachen* is built from *aus* "out" and *machen* "to make" (EtymDB): you make a plan out between you.
