# आक्षेपः (ākṣepaḥ) — an objection

## You'll want to know: आक्षेपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C346-AKSHEPA346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आक्षेपः** — *ākṣepaḥ* — "an objection".

> कस्य आक्षेपः अस्ति?
>
> *kasya ākṣepaḥ asti?*
>
> Who has an objection?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आक्षेपः** (*ākṣepaḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √kṣip, "to throw"; MW gives *ā-kṣepa* "drawing together" and, among its later senses, "objection" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
