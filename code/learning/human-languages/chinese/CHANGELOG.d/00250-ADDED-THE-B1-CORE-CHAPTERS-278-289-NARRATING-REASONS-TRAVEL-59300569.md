## Added — the B1 core: chapters 278-289, narrating, reasons, travel, experience and conditions

- Twelve hand-authored chapters, 91 lessons (sequences 21000-21900), stage B1, two or
  three on each B1 spine node, so all five are realized:
  - **Narrating events** (`SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS`, chapters 278-279): 最后, *shǒuxiān*,
    *jiēzhe*; 先 … 然后 … 最后 (NARRATIVE-SEQUENCE); 了 … 以后 for after doing; 第二天;
    …的时候 with 正在 for what was going on (ASPECT-ONGOING-PAST); 突然, 一下子; a
    thirteen-line story about a day on a mountain, 132 characters, every one written.
  - **Giving reasons** (`SPINE-GIVE-REASONS`, chapters 280-282): 意见, 我觉得 and a
    whole sentence (OPINION-I-THINK), *hǎoxiàng*, 这么; 比如, 因为 … 所以 … as one
    sentence (EXPLANATION-GIVE), *yīncǐ*, *lìzi*; 该, 应该 and 不应该 (MODAL-SHOULD),
    最好, *děi*.
  - **Handling travel** (`SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL`, chapters 283-285): 怎么, 去 … 怎么走
    (DIRECTION-ASK), *wǎng*, 再 'again', 路过; *zhàntái*, 坐 … 去 with tickets counted
    by *zhāng* (TRANSPORT-TICKET), *dānchéng*, *zuòwèi*, *tuìpiào*, 在 after a verb
    (你坐在这儿), 家 counting shops (一家书店); 找回, …不见了 (PROBLEM-REPORT),
    *zhèngjiàn*, *guàshī*, *liánxì*, 找不到 (the potential complement, with 到, 懂 and 完
    only).
  - **Describing experience** (`SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE`, chapters 286-287): 感觉,
    我感觉 and a feeling (ADJECTIVE-FEELING), the length of time after the verb
    (TIME-DURATION), *duōjiǔ*, *yíhàn*; 当, 以后我想 … (AMBITION-EXPRESS),
    *mèngxiǎng*, *mùbiāo*, 最 before 喜欢 or an adjective.
  - **Expressing conditions** (`SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION`, chapters 288-289): 就,
    要是, 要是 … 就 … (CONDITIONAL-REAL), 的话, 看 + a noun for 'it depends on'
    (CONDITION-DEPENDS); 如果, 如果 … 就 … (CONNECTIVE-IF), 如果 … 就会 … (MODAL-WOULD),
    不然.
- 39 new headwords (22 in characters, 17 by ear with pinyin headwords), 7 verbs; 20
  grammar lessons, one point each. 就 and 要是 take character headwords, because *jiù*
  'old' and *yàoshi* 'key' are already taught; *děi* is said to be written 得, the
  character of 说得好, as a different word.
- Sixteen characters written, at most two a chapter, each directly after the lesson whose
  headword first shows it: 最 然 第 突 意 如 所 该 应 怎 走 坐 感 当 就 果, 227 in all, each taught
  as pieces the reader already writes. 然, 所, 走 and 坐 are anchored by the grammar
  lesson whose pattern first prints them (先…然后…最后, 因为…所以…, 去…怎么走, 坐火车去).
- Three compositions: ZH-W281-one-reason (one controlled line answering why, with
  因为 … 所以 …), ZH-W285-a-note (four connected lines to a hotel desk about a lost
  thing) and ZH-W289-six-lines (six connected lines to a friend: a trip told in order,
  a reason and advice). ZH-R289-review-final-289 is chapter 289's payoff.
- Spine: the omits of NARRATE-EVENTS, GIVE-REASONS, HANDLE-TRAVEL, DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE
  and EXPRESS-CONDITION are empty; no new concept aliases.
- Origins from the Guangyun in Baxter's transcription, every reading listed before one
  is chosen (突 *thwot* and *dwot*, 应 *'ing* and *'ingH*, 当 *tang* and *tangH*, 觉
  *kaewH* and *kaewk*), every sense the gloss does not give hedged, and "not settled"
  where there is no reading (怎, 找, 联, the 么 of 这么 and 怎么). Corpus slugs are reused
  for their own etymon (坐 *dzwaX* as in *zuò*, 突 *thwot* as in Japanese 突然, 当
  *tang* as in *dāngshí*); new ones: *ing* (应 'ought to', apart from *ingh* 'to
  respond'), *koj*, *nyo*, *tshjex*, *ljejh*, *homh*, and the sense-tagged *kejh-tie*
  and *kaewk-awake*, whose bare slugs are 计 and 角.
- B1 headwords 1,290 -> 1,329 and verbs 403 -> 410; all five B1 spine nodes realized.
  B1 is not attained: 2,500 headwords are needed. Attained stays A2.
