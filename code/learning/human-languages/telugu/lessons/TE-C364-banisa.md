# బానిస (bānisa) — a slave

## You'll want to know: బానిస
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C364-VIMUKTI364-01]; assesses=[] -->

**బానిస** — *bānisa* — "a slave".

> పూర్వం బానిసలను అమ్మేవారు.
>
> *pūrvaṁ bānisalanu ammēvāru.*
>
> In the past, slaves were sold.

Plural: బానిసలు, *bānisalu*.

బానిసత్వం is slavery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బానిస** (*bānisa*) is the Telugu word for a slave; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
