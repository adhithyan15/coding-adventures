## You'll want to know: رشد کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C373-AFZAYESH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رشد کردن** (*roshd kardan*) — "to grow".

> این شهر در ده سال گذشته خیلی رشد کرده است.
>
> *in shahr dar dah sâl-e gozashte kheyli roshd karde ast.*
>
> This city has grown a lot in the last ten years.

*Roshd* "growth" + *kardan*. For people, plants, cities and economies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رشد** was borrowed from Arabic *rushd* (EtymDB), a word usually glossed as maturity or right guidance; in Persian it means growth.
