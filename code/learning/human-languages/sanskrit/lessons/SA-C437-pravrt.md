## You'll want to know: प्रावृट्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C437-PRAVRSH437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रावृट्** — *prāvṛṭ* — "the rainy season".

> प्रावृट् आगच्छति, नद्यः पूर्णाः भवन्ति।
>
> *prāvṛṭ āgacchati, nadyaḥ pūrṇāḥ bhavanti.*
>
> The rainy season comes, and the rivers fill up.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रावृट्** (*prāvṛṭ*) is built from *pra*, "forth", and the root √vṛṣ, "to rain"; MW gives *prāvṛṣ*, whose nominative is *prāvṛṭ*, as the rainy season, the rains (MW).
