Urdu, chapter 86: Seventy, Eighty, Ninety, World, Colour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ستر (sattar) — seventy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fifty, then the Urdu for sixty.

You'll want to know: ستر.
ستر — sattar — "seventy".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sattar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sattar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sattar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for fifty, then the Urdu for sixty, then say sattar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ستر (sattar) mean? ("Seventy".) Say it once more.
