# ترقى (taraqqā) — to be promoted

## You'll want to know: ترقى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C524-TAAWUN524-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ترقى** — *taraqqā* — "to be promoted".

> ترقى خالي في الشركة بسرعة.
>
> *taraqqā khālī fī ash-sharika bi-surʿa.*
>
> My uncle was promoted quickly in the company.

Present: يترقى, *yataraqqā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترقى** (*taraqqā*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-q-y*, "to go up, to climb" (Lane). Form V taraqqā is to rise step by step.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
