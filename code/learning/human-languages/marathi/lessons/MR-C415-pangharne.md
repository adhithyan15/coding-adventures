## You'll want to know: पांघरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C415-BICHANA415-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पांघरणे** — *pāṅgharṇe* — "to cover oneself".

> रात्री मी जाड चादर पांघरली.
>
> *rātrī mī jāḍ cādar pāṅgharlī.*
>
> At night I covered myself with a thick sheet.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पांघरलं, *tyāne pāṅgharlaṁ*.

The cover is the object, and the verb agrees with it: चादर पांघरली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पांघरणे** (*pāṅgharṇe*) is a Marathi verb for pulling a cover over yourself; its origin is not settled; the noun **पांघरूण**, a covering, belongs with it.
