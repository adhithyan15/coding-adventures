## You'll want to know: વર્ણવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C312-PATRAKAR312-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વર્ણવવું** — *varṇavvũ* — "to describe".

> મીરાએ પત્રકારને બધું વર્ણવ્યું.
>
> *Mīrāe patrakārne badhũ varṇavyũ.*
>
> Mira described everything to the journalist.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધું વર્ણવ્યું, *teṇe badhũ varṇavyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વર્ણવવું** (*varṇavvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *varṇana*, "description" (CDIAL 11340), made into a Gujarati verb: to tell what something was like.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
