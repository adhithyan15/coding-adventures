Punjabi, chapter 28: Supported Forms, Then Head and Na at Distance.
2 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 2.
Two supported lines, then three, at R3 — rebuild the two supported form shapes at R3 while every answer bank remains visible.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Keep these old banks visible:
ਭਾਸ਼ਾ: ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, ਹਿੰਦੀ ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: ਪਿੰਡ, ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਕੰਮ: ਖੇਤੀ, ਨੌਕਰੀ.
Point to ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, ਸ਼ਹਿਰ, and ਖੇਤੀ. Nothing here is new.

Guided Practice — two supported lines.
Copy only the two requested lines. Check the labels before adding anything.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
With the banks still visible, copy the language value Punjabi and the residence value city into two labelled lines.

Guided Practice — add the third supported line.
Check selection, spelling, and placement one pass at a time.
[question — say your answer, then pause 50 seconds]
Keep the same two lines and add the visible work value farming as the third labelled line.

Wrap-up Recall.
The models and answers stayed visible, so this is supported guided-copy practice. It does not award independent Punjabi writing evidence.


Lesson 2 of 2.
Head and na return separately at R4 — retrieve head, its horn history, and the already-taught na shape at R4.

Warm-up.
[pause 7 seconds]
Say the Punjabi word for head from memory. Then read ਸਿਰ once.

Guided Practice — meaning and history.
Keep the two claims together: sir means head, and horn is its surprising English cousin. No new etymology follows.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
Say head in Punjabi. Is its real English root-cousin head or horn?

Guided Practice — recognize na.
Read ਨ once and say na. Do not copy it in this lesson.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Which already-taught Gurmukhi consonant is na: ਨ or ਸ?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say sir — head — horn, then point to ਨ. This lesson measures listening, speech, and recognition. It does not award independent Gurmukhi writing evidence.
