French, chapter 460: Le bulletin météo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la météo — the weather forecast; the weather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the stag, then the French for the doe.

You'll want to know: la météo.
la météo — "the weather forecast; the weather".
Selon la météo, il va faire beau demain. According to the forecast it is going to be nice tomorrow.
Short for la météorologie.

The word, taken apart.
météo is short for météorologie, a learned borrowing from Greek meteōrología, from metéōros 'high in the air' (EtymDB lists the short form).

Lesson 2 of 5.
les prévisions — the forecast, the outlook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the doe, then the French for the weather forecast.

You'll want to know: les prévisions.
les prévisions — "the forecast, the outlook".
Les prévisions pour le week-end ne sont pas bonnes. The forecast for the weekend is not good.
Mostly plural: les prévisions météo.

The word, taken apart.
prévision is built on prévoir 'to foresee', from Latin praevidēre, built on vidēre 'to see' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list the noun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
nuageux — cloudy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the weather forecast, then the French for the forecast.

You'll want to know: nuageux.
nuageux — "cloudy".
Le ciel est nuageux, mais il ne pleut pas. The sky is cloudy, but it is not raining.
Feminine nuageuse.

The word, taken apart.
nuageux is built on nuage 'cloud' (EtymDB lists it), from the old word nue 'cloud', which goes back to Latin nūbēs.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ensoleillé — sunny.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the forecast, then the French for cloudy.

You'll want to know: ensoleillé.
ensoleillé — "sunny".
La terrasse est ensoleillée l'après-midi. The terrace is sunny in the afternoon.
Une journée ensoleillée — a sunny day.

The word, taken apart.
ensoleillé is built on soleil 'sun', which goes back to Vulgar Latin sōliculus, a small sōl 'sun' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
