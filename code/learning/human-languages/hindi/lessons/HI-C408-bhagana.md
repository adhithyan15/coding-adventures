# भगाना (bhagānā) — to chase off; to drive (a vehicle) fast

## You'll want to know: भगाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C408-MIL408-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भगाना** — *bhagānā* — "to chase off; to drive (a vehicle) fast".

> ड्राइवर ने गाड़ी बहुत तेज़ भगाई।
>
> *ḍrāivar ne gāṛī bahut tez bhagāī.*
>
> The driver drove the car very fast.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कुत्ते को भगाया, *usne kutte ko bhagāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भगाना** (*bhagānā*) is the causative partner of भागना, "to run away" (CDIAL 9361): to make a dog run off, or to make a car run fast.
