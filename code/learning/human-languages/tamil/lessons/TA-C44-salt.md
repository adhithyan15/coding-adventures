## You'll want to know: உப்பு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C44-ASK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**உப்பு** (*uppu*) — "salt".

Proto-Dravidian **\*upp-**, and none of the four sisters did much to it: Malayalam ഉപ്പ്, Telugu ఉప్పు, Kannada ಉಪ್ಪು. Set them beside உப்பு and you are looking at one word in four hands.

A word that stable is usually a word nobody could do without. Salt is also how Tamil talks about savour in general — food with no *uppu* in it is food with nothing in it at all.
