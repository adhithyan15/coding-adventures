## You'll want to know: кисть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C521-PAINTING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**кисть** (*kist*) — "a paintbrush; the hand".

> Художник взял тонкую кисть.
>
> *Khudózhnik vzyal tónkuyu kist.*
>
> The artist picked up a fine brush.

Feminine. Also the hand from the wrist down, and a bunch of grapes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *кисть*, glossed 'cluster, bunch', without a source, and the origin is not settled.
