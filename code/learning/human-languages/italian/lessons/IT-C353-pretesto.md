## You'll want to know: il pretesto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C353-REAZIONE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il pretesto** — "the pretext".

> Ha usato il mal di testa come pretesto per non uscire.
>
> *He used the headache as a pretext for not going out.*

*Con il pretesto di* — on the pretext of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pretesto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praetextus* "an outward show, a pretext" (Lewis & Short), from *praetexere* "to weave in front, to cover", built on *texere* "to weave".
