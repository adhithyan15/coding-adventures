## You'll want to know: अवगाहते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C398-GHATTA398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अवगाहते** — *avagāhate* — "plunges into; bathes in".

> यात्रिकः नद्याम् अवगाहते।
>
> *yātrikaḥ nadyām avagāhate.*
>
> The pilgrim plunges into the river.

Past (he …): सः नद्याम् अवगाढवान्, *saḥ nadyām avagāḍhavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवगाहते** (*avagāhate*) is built from *ava*, "down", and the root √gāh, "to plunge, to dive into"; MW gives *ava-gāh* as to plunge into or bathe in, with the participle *avagāḍha* (MW).
