## You'll want to know: páiliàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C505-DANCE505-05]; assesses=[] -->

*páiliàn* — "to rehearse".

> *wǒmen páiliàn le yí ge yuè* — We rehearsed for a month.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*páiliàn* is built from *pái* 'to line up' and *liàn* 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *beaj* and *lenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to push' and the second 'white silk'; the senses 'to line up' and 'to practise' are not given there.
