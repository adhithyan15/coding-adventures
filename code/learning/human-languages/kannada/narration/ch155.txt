Kannada, chapter 155: Recently, Already, Last, Once, To Spend (Time).
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಇತ್ತೀಚೆಗೆ (ittīcege) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for anyone, then the Kannada for anything.

You'll want to know: ಇತ್ತೀಚೆಗೆ.
ಇತ್ತೀಚೆಗೆ — ittīcege — "recently". Kannada's past for "I" ends in -ಎ (-e) after the past stem: ನಾನು ಇತ್ತೀಚೆಗೆ ಒಂದು ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಓದಿದೆ — nānu ittīcege ondu pustaka ōdide — "I recently read a book."
