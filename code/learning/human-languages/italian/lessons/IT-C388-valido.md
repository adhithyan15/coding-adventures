## You'll want to know: valido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C388-VALIDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**valido** — "valid; worthwhile, sound".

> Il biglietto è valido per una settimana.
>
> *The ticket is valid for a week.*

Of an idea or a person: *un valido motivo* — a sound reason.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*valido* is a learned borrowing from Latin *validus* "strong, effective" (Lewis & Short), from *valēre* "to be strong, to be worth".
