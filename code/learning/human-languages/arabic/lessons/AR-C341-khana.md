## You'll want to know: خان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C341-FARIS341-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خان** — *khāna* — "to betray".

> خان الوزير الملك في القصة.
>
> *khāna al-wazīr al-malik fī al-qiṣṣa.*
>
> In the story the minister betrayed the king.

Present: يخون, *yakhūn*.

Not the noun خان, an inn, which has another source.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خان** (*khāna*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-w-n* (Lane), "to be unfaithful to a trust"; *khiyāna* is treachery.
