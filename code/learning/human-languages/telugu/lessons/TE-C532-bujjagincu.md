## You'll want to know: బుజ్జగించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C532-PENPAKAM532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**బుజ్జగించు** — *bujjagiñcu* — "to coax; to cajole".

> మందు తాగమని అమ్మ పాపను బుజ్జగించింది.
>
> *mandu tāgamani amma pāpanu bujjagiñcindi.*
>
> Mum coaxed the little girl to take her medicine.

Past (I …): బుజ్జగించాను, *bujjagiñcānu*.

Gentle persuasion, usually of a child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బుజ్జగించు** (*bujjagiñcu*) is a Telugu verb for coaxing a child; its origin is not settled.
