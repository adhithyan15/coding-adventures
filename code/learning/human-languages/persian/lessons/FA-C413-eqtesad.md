## You'll want to know: اقتصاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C413-EQTESAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتصاد** (*eqtesâd*) — "the economy; economics".

> اقتصاد کشور در حال رشد است.
>
> *eqtesâd-e keshvar dar hâl-e roshd ast.*
>
> The country's economy is growing.

*Eqtesâdi* — economic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتصاد** was borrowed from Arabic *iqtiṣād* "economy; economics" (EtymDB).
