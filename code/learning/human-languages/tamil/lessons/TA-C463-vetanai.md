## You'll want to know: வேதனை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C463-TUKKAM463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**வேதனை** — *vētaṉai* — "torment; anguish".

> காயத்தின் வலி அவருக்கு வேதனை தந்தது.
>
> *kāyattiṉ vali avarukku vētaṉai tantatu.*
>
> The pain of the wound tormented him.

Pain of body or of mind: மன வேதனை, anguish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வேதனை** (*vētaṉai*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vēdanā*, "feeling; pain" (CDIAL 12106).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
