## You'll want to know: शृंखला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C293-NYAYALAYA293-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शृंखला** — *śṛṅkhalā* — "a chain".

> चौरस्य पादे शृंखला अस्ति।
>
> *caurasya pāde śṛṅkhalā asti.*
>
> There is a chain on the thief's foot.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शृंखला** (*śṛṅkhalā*) MW gives *śṛṅkhalā* as a chain or fetter, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
