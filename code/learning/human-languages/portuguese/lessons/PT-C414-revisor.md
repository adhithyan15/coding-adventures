## You'll want to know: o revisor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C414-BILHETEIRA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o revisor** — "the ticket inspector".

> O revisor pediu os bilhetes a toda a gente.
>
> *The inspector asked everyone for their tickets.*

Also a proofreader of texts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revisor* is a learned formation on Latin *revīsere* 'to come back to see, to look again at', built on *vidēre* 'to see' (Lewis & Short): the person who checks the tickets again.
