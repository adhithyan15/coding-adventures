## You'll want to know: constater
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C411-ACCIDENT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**constater** — "to note, to record (what happened)".

> Les deux conducteurs ont constaté les dégâts ensemble.
>
> *The two drivers noted the damage together.*

*Le constat amiable* is the form drivers fill in together after an accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*constater* is built on Latin *constat* 'it is certain', from *constāre* 'to stand firm', from *con-* and *stāre* 'to stand' (EtymDB).
