## You'll want to know: व्यग्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C452-UDVEGA452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यग्रः** — *vyagraḥ* — "agitated; distracted".

> विलम्बेन यात्री व्यग्रः भवति।
>
> *vilambena yātrī vyagraḥ bhavati.*
>
> The passenger grows agitated at the delay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यग्रः** (*vyagraḥ*) is built from *vi*, "apart", and *agra*, "a point": a mind with no single point; MW gives "distracted" and "bewildered, agitated, alarmed" (MW).
