# बुआ (buā) — aunt, the father's sister

## You'll want to know: बुआ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C117-AUNT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बुआ** (*buā*) — "aunt", your father's sister.

**मौसी** and **बुआ** are both "aunt" in English; in Hindi the side of the family decides.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Both aunts, and the side each belongs to.
