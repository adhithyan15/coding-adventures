Arabic, chapter 458: Guarantees and swaps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ضمان (ḍamān) — a guarantee; a warranty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to get cheaper, then the Arabic for to spend money.

You'll want to know: ضمان.
ضمان — ḍamān — "a guarantee; a warranty".
للجهاز ضمان لمدة سنة. li-l-jihāz ḍamān li-muddat sana. The device has a one-year guarantee.

The word, taken apart.
ضمان (ḍamān) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-m-n, "to be answerable for" (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
سعر (siʿr) — a rate; a price.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to spend money, then the Arabic for a guarantee.

You'll want to know: سعر.
سعر — siʿr — "a rate; a price".
سعر الصرف في المطار مرتفع. siʿr aṣ-ṣarf fī al-maṭār murtafiʿ. The exchange rate at the airport is high.
Plural: أسعار, asʿār.

The word, taken apart.
سعر (siʿr) is built on the native Arabic root s-ʿ-r, "to kindle a fire" (Lane). Lane gives siʿr, "a price", on the same root.

Lesson 4 of 5.
أرجع (arjaʿa) — to take back; to return (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a rate, then the Arabic for a commodity.

You'll want to know: أرجع.
أرجع — arjaʿa — "to take back; to return (something)".
أرجعت الحذاء إلى المتجر. arjaʿtu al-ḥidhāʾ ilā al-matjar. I took the shoes back to the shop.
Present: يرجع, yurjiʿ.

The word, taken apart.
أرجع (arjaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root r-j-ʿ, "to return" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to make go back".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a commodity, then the Arabic for to take back.

قايض المزارع القمح بالملح. qāyaḍa al-muzāriʿ al-qamḥ bi-l-milḥ. The farmer bartered the wheat for salt.

The word, taken apart.
قايض (qāyaḍa) is usually traced to the native Arabic root q-y-ḍ, "to give in exchange". It is Form III.
