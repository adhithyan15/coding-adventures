## You'll want to know: la duda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C493-OPINION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la duda** — "the doubt; the question".

> Si tienes dudas, pregunta.
>
> *If you have questions, ask.*

*Sin duda* — without a doubt.
