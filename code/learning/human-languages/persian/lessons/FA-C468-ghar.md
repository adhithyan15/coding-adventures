## You'll want to know: غار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C468-GHAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غار** (*ghâr*) — "a cave".

> در کوه یک غار تاریک پیدا کردیم.
>
> *Dar kuh yek ghâr-e târik peydâ kardim.*
>
> We found a dark cave in the mountains.

Note the letter غ, said *gh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ghâr* was borrowed from Arabic *ġār* 'grotto, cave' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
