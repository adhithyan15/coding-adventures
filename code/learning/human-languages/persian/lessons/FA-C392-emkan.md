## You'll want to know: امکان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C392-EHTEMAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**امکان** (*emkân*) — "a possibility; a facility".

> امکان دارد که فردا برف بیاید.
>
> *emkân dârad ke fardâ barf biyâyad.*
>
> It may snow tomorrow.

*Emkân dârad ke* + subjunctive — it is possible that. Plural *emkânât*, facilities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امکان** is usually traced to Arabic *imkān* "possibility", from the same Arabic family as *mumkin*, the source of Persian *momken* "possible".
