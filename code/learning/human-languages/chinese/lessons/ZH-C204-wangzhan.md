## You'll want to know: 网站
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C204-ONLINE204-01]; assesses=[] -->

**网站** — *wǎngzhàn* — "website".

> **这个网站关了** *zhège wǎngzhàn guān le* — This website has closed down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**网站** (*wǎngzhàn*) is built from 网 'net' and 站 'a station', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjangX* and *treamH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
