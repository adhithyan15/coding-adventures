# ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸು (bahiraṅgapaḍisu) — to reveal; to expose

## You'll want to know: ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C357-VANCHANE357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸು** — *bahiraṅgapaḍisu* — "to reveal; to expose".

> ಒಬ್ಬ ಪತ್ರಕರ್ತ ಆ ಮೋಸವನ್ನು ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *obba patrakarta ā mōsavannu bahiraṅgapaḍisidanu.*
>
> A journalist exposed that fraud.

Past (I …): ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, *bahiraṅgapaḍiside*.

ಬಹಿರಂಗ, open, public, with -ಪಡಿಸು, to make: to make public.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸು** (*bahiraṅgapaḍisu*) is built from Sanskrit *bahiraṅga*, "relating to the exterior, external" (Monier-Williams), and the Kannada verb-maker -*paḍisu*, from native *paḍu* (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
