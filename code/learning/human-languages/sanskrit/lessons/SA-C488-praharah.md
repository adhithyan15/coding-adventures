## You'll want to know: प्रहरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C488-PRATIBODHA488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रहरः** — *praharaḥ* — "a three-hour watch".

> रात्रेः प्रथमे प्रहरे सः स्वपिति।
>
> *rātreḥ prathame prahare saḥ svapiti.*
>
> He sleeps in the first watch of the night.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रहरः** (*praharaḥ*) is built from *pra* and the root √hṛ, "to take, to strike"; MW gives "the eighth part of a day, a watch" (MW).
