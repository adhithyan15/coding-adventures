## You'll want to know: પસ્તી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C518-PASTI518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પસ્તી** — *pastī* — "waste paper (sold by weight)".

> અમે મહિને એક વાર પસ્તી વેચીએ છીએ.
>
> *ame mahine ek vār pastī vecīe chīe.*
>
> Once a month we sell our old newspapers.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પસ્તી** (*pastī*) is old newspaper and paper, sold to a dealer by weight. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
