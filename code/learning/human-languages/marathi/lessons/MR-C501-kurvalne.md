## You'll want to know: कुरवाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C501-MITHI501-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कुरवाळणे** — *kurvāḷṇe* — "to caress".

> आजीने प्रेमाने मला कुरवाळलं.
>
> *ājīne premāne malā kurvāḷlaṁ.*
>
> Grandmother caressed me lovingly.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कुत्र्याला कुरवाळलं, *tyāne kutryālā kurvāḷlaṁ*.

A loving stroke of the head or face; the one caressed takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुरवाळणे** (*kurvāḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
