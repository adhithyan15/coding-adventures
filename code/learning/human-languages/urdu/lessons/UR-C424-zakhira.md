# ذخیرہ (zakhīrā) — a stockpile; a store (of goods)

## You'll want to know: ذخیرہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C424-MAAL424-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ذخیرہ** — *zakhīrā* — "a stockpile; a store (of goods)".

> دکاندار کے پاس چینی کا بڑا ذخیرہ ہے۔
>
> *dukāndār ke pās chīnī kā baṛā zakhīrā hai.*
>
> The shopkeeper has a big stock of sugar.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذخیرہ** (*zakhīrā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dhakhīra*, "a store, provisions"; Urdu says its **ذ** as a *z*.
