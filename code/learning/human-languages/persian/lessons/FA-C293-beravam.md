# بروم (beravam) — a going that is wanted, not stated

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-MI-01, FA-GRAMMAR-NEGATIVE-NE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'm going", then "I'm not going". (*Miravam*, *nemiravam*.)

[PAUSE 1s] Both are statements. Advice, plans and wishes talk about a going
that has not happened, and Persian gives that its own shape.

## Grammar Lens: be- in place of mi-
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-SUBJUNCTIVE-BE-01]; assesses=[FA-STEM-RAV, FA-STEM-KHAN, FA-STEM-NEVIS, FA-STEM-GIR, FA-LEX-C36-VERBS36-04, FA-LEX-KOMAK-KARDAN, FA-LEX-BUDAN] -->

Swap *mi-* for **be-** and keep the present stem and the endings:

> be- + rav + -am → **بروم** — *beravam* — that I go, for me to go

So *beravi*, *beravad*, *beravim*, *beravid*, *beravand*. The same swap works
on any stored stem: *bekhânam*, *benevisam*, *begiram*, and *bekharam* from
*kharidan*. This is the **subjunctive**: the verb shape for something
wanted, needed or possible rather than reported.

Three habits to know. A compound verb mostly leaves *be-* off: *komak
konam*, *fekr konam*. *Budan* has a stem of its own here: *bâsham*, *bâshi*,
*bâshad*. And "not" is *na-* in place of *be-*: *naravam*.

## Grammar Lens: mikhâham, and the verb after it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-MIKHAHAM-SUBJ-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C38-VERBS38-02, FA-LEX-KETAB] -->

*Khâstan*, "to want", from chapter thirty-eight, has the present stem
**khâh-**: *mikhâham*, I want; *mikhâhi*, you want. Whatever you want to do
comes after it in the subjunctive:

> **میخواهم بروم.** — *mikhâham beravam* — I want to go.

> **میخواهم کتاب بخوانم.** — *mikhâham ketâb bekhânam* — I want to read a book.

> **میخواهی بروی؟** — *mikhâhi beravi?* — Do you want to go?

English says "want to go", with the plain verb. Persian gives the wanted
verb its own person: "I want that I go".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khâstan* goes back, in EtymDB 2.1, to a Middle Persian verb written *hwʾstn'*
in Pahlavi letters, *xwāstan*. EtymDB has no entry for the prefix *be-*, so
no history is claimed for it here.
