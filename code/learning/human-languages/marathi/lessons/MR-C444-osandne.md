# ओसंडणे (osaṇḍṇe) — to overflow

## You'll want to know: ओसंडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C444-DABKE444-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ओसंडणे** — *osaṇḍṇe* — "to overflow".

> धरण भरून ओसंडलं.
>
> *dharaṇ bharūn osaṇḍlaṁ.*
>
> The dam filled and overflowed.

Past (he …): तो ओसंडला, *to osaṇḍlā*.

Often with भरून: भरून ओसंडणे, to be full to overflowing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओसंडणे** (*osaṇḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb for water spilling over the top; its origin is not settled.
