## You'll want to know: ملامت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C355-TARIF355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ملامت** — *malāmat* — "reproach; blame".

> اسے ہر طرف سے ملامت ملی۔
>
> *use har taraf se malāmat milī.*
>
> He was blamed from every side.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملامت** (*malāmat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *malāma*, "blame".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
