Italian, chapter 535: Fai da te.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il trapano — drill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for sink, then the Italian for to polish.

You'll want to know: il trapano.
il trapano — "drill".
Mi presti il trapano? Devo fare un buco nel muro. Can you lend me the drill? I have to make a hole in the wall.
Il trapano elettrico — the power drill.

The word, taken apart.
trapano comes from Latin trepanum "a drill", from Greek trýpanon "an auger, a borer" (EtymDB, under English trepan).

You'll want to know: la vite.
la vite — "screw; vine".
Questa vite è troppo lunga. This screw is too long.
Also the grapevine: la vite gives grapes.

The word, taken apart.
vite is inherited from Latin vītis "a vine" (EtymDB): the screw is probably named for the spiral of the vine's tendrils.

Lesson 3 of 5.
il cacciavite — screwdriver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for drill, then the Italian for screw.

You'll want to know: il cacciavite.
il cacciavite — "screwdriver".
Mi serve un cacciavite piccolo. I need a small screwdriver.
Literally "drive-screw"; the same form in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
cacciavite is built from caccia "drives", from cacciare, and vite "screw", inherited from Latin vītis "a vine" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
aggiustare — to fix, to mend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for screw, then the Italian for screwdriver.

You'll want to know: aggiustare.
aggiustare — "to fix, to mend".
Mio padre aggiusta tutto in casa. My father fixes everything in the house.
Close to riparare; also to adjust.

The word, taken apart.
aggiustare is built on giusto "right, exact", inherited from Latin iūstus "right, lawful" (Lewis & Short): to make right.

Lesson 5 of 5.
imbiancare — to paint (walls), to whitewash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for screwdriver, then the Italian for to fix.

You'll want to know: imbiancare.
imbiancare — "to paint (walls), to whitewash".
Questo fine settimana imbianchiamo la camera. This weekend we are painting the bedroom.
Built on bianco "white", even if the paint is another colour.

The word, taken apart.
imbiancare is built on bianco "white", which comes from Proto-Germanic blankaz "bright, shining, white" (EtymDB, under Spanish blanco).
