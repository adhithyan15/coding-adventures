## You'll want to know: സമീപനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C452-SAMADHANAM452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**സമീപനം** — *samīpanaṁ* — "an approach; an attitude to".

> പ്രശ്നത്തോട് അവരുടെ സമീപനം വളരെ നല്ലതായിരുന്നു.
>
> *praśnattōṭŭ avaruṭe samīpanaṁ vaḷare nallatāyirunnu.*
>
> Their approach to the problem was very good.

Towards something: -ഓട് സമീപനം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സമീപനം** (*samīpanaṁ*) is built on Sanskrit *samīpa*, "near" (CDIAL 13226), with the noun ending -അനം: an approach is a way of coming near a thing.
