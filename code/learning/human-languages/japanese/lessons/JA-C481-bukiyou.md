## You'll want to know: ぶきよう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C481-SKILL481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶきよう** — *bukiyō* — "clumsy; awkward (with one's hands)".

> **あには てが ぶきようです。** — *ani wa te ga bukiyō desu* — "My elder brother is clumsy with his hands."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶきよう** (*bukiyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bu*, "negative", *ki*, "utensil" (Middle Chinese *khijH*) and *yō*, "utilize" (*yowngH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
