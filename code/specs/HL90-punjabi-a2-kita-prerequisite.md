# HL90 — Punjabi A2 `ਕੀਤਾ` prerequisite

Issue: #18012

The restored Punjabi A2 mock uses `ਕੀਤਾ` five times across the learner paper
and confidential examiner master. The infinitive `ਕਰਨਾ` already has a direct
owner, and chapter 188 provides a bounded completed-action model with `ਮਿਲਿਆ`,
but the irregular perfective form `ਕੀਤਾ` has no instructional owner. The two
completed forms are not assigned the same agreement controller: in the new
transitive frame, `ਕੀਤਾ` agrees with the unmarked masculine-singular object
`ਕੰਮ`, not with the speaker.

Chapter 190 closes that narrow dependency gap without editing either mock. The
ramp retrieves familiar `ਕਰਨਾ` and the completed-action frame from chapter 188,
directly teaches `ਕੀਤਾ`, and gives the form an immediate retrieval step. It
then builds and retrieves `ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ`, explicitly binds its agreement to
`ਕੰਮ`, contrasts the infinitive with the completed-action form, and finishes
with a four-skill payoff.

`ਕੀਤਾ` has one direct owner and at least two later retrieval lessons. All
supporting Punjabi must occur before the chapter, and the target must not leak
into authored or generated material before its owner. Other perfective forms
and unrelated high-frequency mock words remain out of scope. This is a
prerequisite tranche, not mock-readiness or attainment evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
