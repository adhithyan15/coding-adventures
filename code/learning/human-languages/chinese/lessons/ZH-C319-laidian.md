## You'll want to know: 来电
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C319-CALL319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**来电** — *láidiàn* — "incoming call".

> **开会的时候我有三个来电** *kāihuì de shíhou wǒ yǒu sān ge láidiàn* — During the meeting three calls came in for me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**来电** (*láidiàn*) is built from 来 'to come' and 电 'electricity', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *loj* and *denH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to arrive' and the second 'lightning'.
