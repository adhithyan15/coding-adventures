## You'll want to know: la escarcha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C700-NIEVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la escarcha** — "the hoarfrost, the white frost".

> Por la mañana hay escarcha en los coches.
>
> *In the morning there is frost on the cars.*

The thin white ice on grass and glass.

## The word, taken apart
