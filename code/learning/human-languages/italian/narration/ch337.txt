Italian, chapter 337: Il tocco finale.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
condire — to dress, to season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the baking tray, then the Italian for the cooking.

You'll want to know: condire.
condire — "to dress, to season".
Condisco l'insalata con olio e limone. I dress the salad with oil and lemon.
Present like capire: condisco.

The word, taken apart.
condire is inherited from Latin condīre "to pickle, to season" (EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
guarnire is usually traced to a Germanic verb warnjan; EtymDB lists it beside Old French guarnir "to equip, to protect" and French garnir "to furnish, to garnish".
