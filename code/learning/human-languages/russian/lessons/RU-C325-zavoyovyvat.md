## You'll want to know: завоёвывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C325-IMPERIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**завоёвывать** (*zavoyóvyvat*) — "to conquer".

> Они завоевали много земель.
>
> *Oní zavoyeváli mnógo zemél.*
>
> They conquered many lands.

Imperfective; the perfective is *завоевать* (*zavoyevát*), *завоюю*. Also *завоевать сердце* — to win a heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*завоёвывать* is built on *за-* and *воевать* "to wage war", from *воин* "a warrior", Proto-Slavic *vojinŭ* (EtymDB).
