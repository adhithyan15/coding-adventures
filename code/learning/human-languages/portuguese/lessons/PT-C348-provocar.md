## You'll want to know: provocar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C348-EFEITO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**provocar** — "to bring about, to cause; to provoke".

> A chuva forte provocou muitos acidentes.
>
> *The heavy rain caused many accidents.*

Also to provoke or tease a person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*provocar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōvocāre* "to call forth, to call out, to challenge" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *prō* and *vocāre* "to call".
