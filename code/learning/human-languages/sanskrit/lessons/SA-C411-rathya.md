## You'll want to know: रथ्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C411-RATHYA411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रथ्या** — *rathyā* — "a carriage road; a street".

> रथ्यायां वाहनानि चलन्ति।
>
> *rathyāyāṃ vāhanāni calanti.*
>
> Vehicles move along the carriage road.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रथ्या** (*rathyā*) is built on *ratha*, "a chariot", with the ending *-ya*; MW gives *rathyā* as a carriage road, a highway, a street (MW).
