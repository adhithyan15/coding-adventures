# The second pair, from memory

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C268-SERVICE-01, SA-LEX-C238-PPL238-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “financial bank” and “policeman.”

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C268-SERVICE-01, SA-LEX-C238-PPL238-04, SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-03, SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-04, SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-06, SA-LEX-ASTI, SA-PATTERN-C268-SERVICE-LOCATION-02] -->

[PAUSE 4s each]
- [YOU ASK AND ANSWER: Where is the financial bank? The financial bank is there.]
- [YOU ASK AND ANSWER: Where is the policeman? The policeman is here.]

Check only after speaking: **वित्तकोषः कुत्र अस्ति? वित्तकोषः तत्र अस्ति।
आरक्षकः कुत्र अस्ति? आरक्षकः अत्र अस्ति।**

## What you've built

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C268-SERVICE-LOCATION-02] -->

You have now retrieved both halves of the four-service exchange. Nothing here
adds directions, a police station, or a service transaction.
