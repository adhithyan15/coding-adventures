Arabic, chapter 62: To stop, To look for, To find, To wear, To leave.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
وقف (waqafa) — to stop (he stopped).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to send, then the Arabic for to cut.

You'll want to know: وقف.
وقف — waqafa — "to stop (he stopped)".

Wrap-up Recall.
