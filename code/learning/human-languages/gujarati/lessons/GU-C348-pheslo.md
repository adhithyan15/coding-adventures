## You'll want to know: ફેંસલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C348-KES348-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેંસલો** — *phẽslo* — "a ruling".

> ન્યાયાધીશે આજે કેસનો ફેંસલો આપ્યો.
>
> *nyāyādhīśe āje kesno phẽslo āpyo.*
>
> The judge gave the ruling in the case today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેંસલો** (*phẽslo*) is usually traced to Arabic *faiṣala*, "a decision", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
