## You'll want to know: سلعة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C458-DAMAN458-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سلعة** — *silʿa* — "a commodity; an item for sale".

> هذه السلعة رخيصة في السوق.
>
> *hādhihi as-silʿa rakhīṣa fī as-sūq.*
>
> This item is cheap at the market.

Plural: سلع, *silaʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سلعة** (*silʿa*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *s-l-ʿ*, "goods, merchandise".
