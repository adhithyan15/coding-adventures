## You'll want to know: bù hǎoyìsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bù hǎoyìsi* — "excuse me; sorry to trouble you".

> *bù hǎoyìsi wǒ xiǎng wèn nǐ* — Excuse me, could I ask you something?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

bù keeps its own falling tone here, because hǎo after it is a third tone, not a fourth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bù hǎoyìsi* is built from bù 'not', hǎo 'good' and yìsi 'meaning', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjut*, *xawX*, *'iH* and *si* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the last two as 'intention' and 'to think of'. 'Not a good feeling' is the literal sense; how it became an apology is not settled.
