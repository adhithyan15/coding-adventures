## You'll want to know: سرمقاله
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C315-KHABARFOWRI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سرمقاله** (*sarmaqâle*) — "an editorial, a leading article".

> سرمقاله امروز درباره آموزش بود.
>
> *Sarmaqâle-ye emruz darbâre-ye âmuzesh bud.*
>
> Today's editorial was about education.

*sar* "head" + *maqâle* "article": the head article of the paper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarmaqâle* joins native Persian *sar* "head" and *maqâle* "an article", which is borrowed from Arabic *maqāla*, "article, essay" (EtymDB).
