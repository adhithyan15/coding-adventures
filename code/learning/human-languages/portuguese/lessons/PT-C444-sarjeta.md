## You'll want to know: a sarjeta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C444-MOBILIARIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a sarjeta** — "the gutter (drain at the kerb)".

> A chuva corre pela sarjeta.
>
> *The rain runs along the gutter.*

Figurative: *cair na sarjeta* — to hit rock bottom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *sarjeta* is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
