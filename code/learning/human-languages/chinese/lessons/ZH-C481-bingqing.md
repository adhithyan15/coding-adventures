## You'll want to know: 病情
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C481-SERIOUS481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**病情** — *bìngqíng* — "a patient's condition; how an illness is going".

> **他的病情不要紧你不要伤心** *tā de bìngqíng bú yàojǐn nǐ bú yào shāngxīn* — His condition isn't serious, so don't be upset.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**病情** (*bìngqíng*) is built from 病 'illness' and 情 'the state of things', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjaengH* and *dzjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as an illness growing worse.
