## You'll want to know: بیاہنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C488-INLAWS488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بیاہنا** — *byāhnā* — "to marry off; to wed".

> انہوں نے بیٹی کو لاہور میں بیاہا۔
>
> *unhoṅ ne beṭī ko lāhaur meṅ byāhā.*
>
> They married their daughter into a family in Lahore.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بیٹے کو بیاہا, *us ne beṭe ko byāhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بیاہنا** (*byāhnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vivāhayati*, "leads away (a bride), marries" (CDIAL 11923).
