Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 206: The Third House, Upstairs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तीजो (tījo) — third.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to get stuck, then the Marwadi for a bus conductor.

You'll want to know: तीजो.
तीजो — tījo — "third".
तीजो घर म्हारो है। tījo ghar mhāro hai. The third house is mine.

The word, taken apart.
तीजो (tījo) is inherited from Sanskrit tṛtīya, "third". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi tījā, "third" (CDIAL 5912).

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: ऊपर.
ऊपर — ūpar — "up; upstairs".
दफ्तर ऊपर है। daphtar ūpar hai. The office is upstairs.

The word, taken apart.
ऊपर (ūpar) is inherited from a reconstructed uppari, "above"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ūpara, "on" (CDIAL 2333).
