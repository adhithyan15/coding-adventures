## You'll want to know: политик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C365-VLAST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**политик** (*polítik*) — "a politician".

> Этот политик часто выступает по телевизору.
>
> *Étot polítik chásto vystupáyet po televízoru.*
>
> This politician often speaks on television.

Masculine, also of a woman. Not to be confused with *политика*, politics.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**политик** is usually traced, through German, to Greek *politikós* "of the citizens, of the state", built on *pólis* "a city, a state".
