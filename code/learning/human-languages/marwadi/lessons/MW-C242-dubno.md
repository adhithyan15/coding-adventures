# डूबणो (ḍūbṇo) — to sink; to drown

## You'll want to know: डूबणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C242-FIRE242-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डूबणो** — *ḍūbṇo* — "to sink; to drown".

> नदी मांय नाव डूबी।
>
> *nadī mā̃y nāv ḍūbī.*
>
> The boat sank in the river.

Past (it …): वो भाटो डूब्यो, *vo bhāṭo ḍūbyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डूबणो** (*ḍūbṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍubb*, "sink". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *ḍūbnā*, "to sink" (CDIAL 5561).
