## You'll want to know: とびこむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C266-OBORE266-02]; assesses=[] -->

**とびこむ** — *tobikomu* — "to jump in; to dive in".

> **こどもが おぼれたら、すぐ とびこみます。** — *kodomo ga oboretara, sugu tobikomimasu* — "If a child is drowning, I jump in at once."

Polite **とびこみます**, *tobikomimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが かわに とびこみました。**, *chichi ga kawa ni tobikomimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とびこむ** (*tobikomu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tobi*, "leaping" (from *tobu*), and *komu*, "to go into" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
