## You'll want to know: दक्षः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C503-SAHAKARI503-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दक्षः** — *dakṣaḥ* — "adroit; capable".

> सः दक्षः तक्षकः अस्ति।
>
> *saḥ dakṣaḥ takṣakaḥ asti.*
>
> He is a capable carpenter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दक्षः** (*dakṣaḥ*) comes from the root √dakṣ, "to be able, to be strong", under which MW gives the adjective *dakṣa*, "able, fit, adroit, clever" (MW).
