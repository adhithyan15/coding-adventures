## You'll want to know: ٹہلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C392-STAND392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹہلنا** — *ṭahalnā* — "to stroll; to walk up and down".

> شام کو ہم دریا کے کنارے ٹہلتے ہیں۔
>
> *shām ko ham daryā ke kināre ṭahalte haiṅ.*
>
> In the evening we stroll along the riverbank.

Past (he …): وہ پارک میں ٹہلا, *voh pārk meṅ ṭahlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹہلنا** (*ṭahalnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭahalnā*, "to take a walk", under *ṭahall*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5453).
