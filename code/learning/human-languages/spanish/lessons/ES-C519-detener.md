## You'll want to know: detener
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C519-POLICIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**detener** — "to detain, to arrest; to stop".

> La policía detuvo al ladrón en la estación.
>
> *The police arrested the thief at the station.*

It changes like *tener*: *detuve*, *detuvo*. *Detenerse* is to stop: *El coche se detuvo.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*detener* is inherited from Latin *dētinēre* 'to hold back' (EtymDB), built on *dē-* and *tenēre* 'to hold'. English *detain* is the same Latin verb through French.
