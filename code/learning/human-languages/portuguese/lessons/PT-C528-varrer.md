## You'll want to know: varrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C528-LIMPEZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**varrer** — "to sweep".

> Varri a cozinha depois do jantar.
>
> *I swept the kitchen after dinner.*

*Varrer o chão* — to sweep the floor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*varrer* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Latin *verrere* "to sweep" (EtymDB).
