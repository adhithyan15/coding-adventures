## You'll want to know: आहेर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C317-AHER317-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आहेर** — *āher* — "a wedding gift".

> मामांनी नवरीला साडीचा आहेर दिला.
>
> *māmānnī navrīlā sāḍīcā āher dilā.*
>
> Uncle gave the bride a sari as a wedding gift.

Masculine.

आहेर करणे or देणे, to give the customary gift.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आहेर** (*āher*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *āher*, 'presents to a family celebrating a marriage', under Sanskrit *abhihāra*, 'a bringing near' (CDIAL 534).
