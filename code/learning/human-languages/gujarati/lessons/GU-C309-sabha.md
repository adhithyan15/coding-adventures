## You'll want to know: સભા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C309-INAM309-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સભા** — *sabhā* — "a gathering; an assembly".

> સાંજે ગામમાં મોટી સભા થઈ.
>
> *sā̃je gāmmā̃ moṭī sabhā thaī.*
>
> In the evening there was a big gathering in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sabhā*, "an assembly, a meeting place" (Monier-Williams). A **સભા** is people gathered to hear and to decide; **બેઠક** is a smaller meeting.
