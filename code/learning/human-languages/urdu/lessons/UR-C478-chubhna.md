## You'll want to know: چبھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C478-CUT478-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چبھنا** — *chubhnā* — "to prick; to stick in".

> میری انگلی میں کانٹا چبھ گیا۔
>
> *merī unglī meṅ kā̃ṭā chubh gayā.*
>
> A thorn stuck in my finger.

Past (it …): وہ کانٹا چبھا, *voh kā̃ṭā chubhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چبھنا** (*chubhnā*) is inherited from *cubhyatē*, "is pierced", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4867).
