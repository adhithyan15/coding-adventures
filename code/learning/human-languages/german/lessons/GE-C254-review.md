# Review — what I think, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-DASS-VERB-ENDE-01, GE-LEX-C67-VERBEN4-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I believe that the train is late". (*Ich glaube, dass der
Zug Verspätung hat.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-MEINEN-02, GE-LEX-MEINER-MEINUNG-NACH-01, GE-GRAMMAR-MEINUNG-ERSTE-STELLE-02, GE-LEX-DARUM-01] -->

Three lessons, and an argument has all its joints. An opinion: *ich finde*,
*ich glaube* or *ich meine*, then **dass** and the verb at the end. Or
**meiner Meinung nach** in the first place, with the verb right after it.
A reason with **weil**, verb last. A consequence with **darum**, verb
second.

[PAUSE 2s] Two sentences, and both word orders in them:

> *Meiner Meinung nach* **kommen** *wir spät, weil der Zug Verspätung*
> **hat**.
> *Der Zug hat Verspätung, darum* **nehme** *ich ein Taxi.*

Verb second after the opinion phrase, verb last after *weil*, and verb
second again after *darum*.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-DASS-VERB-ENDE-01, GE-GRAMMAR-MEINUNG-ERSTE-STELLE-02, GE-GRAMMAR-WEIL-DARUM-02] -->

After *darum* and *meiner Meinung nach*? (**Second**, straight after
