## Script — five strokes over 日
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YIN-SOUND-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 音

**Two pieces, top to bottom.**

**The top piece, five strokes.** A dot at the top, a short horizontal under it, a short stroke on the left falling to the lower right, a short stroke on the right falling to the lower left, then a long horizontal across the whole width.

**日, four strokes**, the four your hand makes in 日: the left side, the top that turns down the right side, the middle bar, and the bar that closes the bottom.

5 + 4 = nine.
