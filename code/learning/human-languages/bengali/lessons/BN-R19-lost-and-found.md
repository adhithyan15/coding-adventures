## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Four chapters ago this book could not deny a sentence, join two
nouns, give a reason or ask for a repeat. Say the sentence that agrees and then
objects. (**আমি একমত, কিন্তু …**) Say the one that reports a thought.
(**আমি ভাবি যে …**) And say the two sentences a lost learner needs.
(***āmi bujhi nā*** and **আবার বলবেন**.)
