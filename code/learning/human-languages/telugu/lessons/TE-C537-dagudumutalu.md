# దాగుడుమూతలు (dāguḍumūtalu) — hide-and-seek

## You'll want to know: దాగుడుమూతలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C537-ATALU537-03]; assesses=[] -->

**దాగుడుమూతలు** — *dāguḍumūtalu* — "hide-and-seek".

> సాయంత్రం పిల్లలు దాగుడుమూతలు ఆడారు.
>
> *sāyantraṁ pillalu dāguḍumūtalu āḍāru.*
>
> In the evening the children played hide-and-seek.

దాగు, to hide, is at its heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దాగుడుమూతలు** (*dāguḍumūtalu*) is built on native *dāgu*, "to lie hidden" (DEDR 63), and *mūta*, "a closing", for the shut eyes of the seeker.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
