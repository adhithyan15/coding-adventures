Portuguese, chapter 307: Feridos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o ferido — the injured person, the wounded.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to survive, then the Portuguese for the survivor.

You'll want to know: o ferido.
o ferido — "the injured person, the wounded".
O ferido foi levado para o hospital. The injured man was taken to hospital.
Feridos ligeiros — minor injuries; feridos graves — serious ones.

The word, taken apart.
ferido comes from Latin ferītus "struck" (EtymDB), from ferīre "to strike".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.

a fratura — "the fracture, the break".
Ela tem uma fratura no braço. She has a fractured arm.

The word, taken apart.
fratura is a learned borrowing from Latin frāctūra "a breaking", from frangere "to break" (Lewis & Short).
