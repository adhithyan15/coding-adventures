## You'll want to know: la curva
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C414-STATALE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la curva** — "the bend, the curve".

> Rallenta, c'è una curva pericolosa.
>
> *Slow down, there is a dangerous bend.*

In a stadium, *la curva* is the end stand where the keenest fans sit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*curva* is inherited from Latin *curva* 'curve' (EtymDB), from *curvus* 'bent'.
