## You'll want to know: обманывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C290-MOSHENNIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**обманывать** (*obmányvat*) — "to deceive, to cheat".

> Он никогда не обманывает друзей.
>
> *On nikogdá ne obmányvayet druzéy.*
>
> He never deceives his friends.

Imperfective; perfective *обмануть* (*obmanút*). *Обман* — a deceit, a trick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обманывать* is built on *об-* and *манить* "to lure, to beckon", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *maniti* "to deceive" (EtymDB).
