## You'll want to know: o antepassado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C329-BATALHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o antepassado** — "the ancestor".

> Um antepassado meu viveu nesta casa.
>
> *An ancestor of mine lived in this house.*

Usually plural: *os nossos antepassados*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*antepassado* joins *ante* "before", from Latin *ante* (Lewis & Short), and *passado* "past": one who lived before us.
