## You'll want to know: ಹಾಗಲ್ಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C95-MAYBE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾಗಲ್ಲ** (*hāgalla*) — "it's not like that", "no, that's not it". It is **ಹಾಗೆ**
(*hāge*), "like that", with **ಅಲ್ಲ** (*alla*), "is not".
