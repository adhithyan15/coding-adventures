## You'll want to know: el huésped
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C661-RECEPCION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el huésped** — "the guest (at a hotel or a house)".

> Los huéspedes desayunan a las ocho.
>
> *The guests have breakfast at eight.*

*Una casa de huéspedes* — a guesthouse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huésped* is inherited from Latin *hospitem*, a form of *hospes* "guest; host" (EtymDB).
