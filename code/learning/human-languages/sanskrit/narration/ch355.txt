Sanskrit, chapter 355: The Assembly.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
संसद् (saṃsad) — a parliament; an assembly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for independent, then the Sanskrit for organizes.

You'll want to know: संसद्.
संसद् — saṃsad — "a parliament; an assembly".
संसत् नूतनं नियमं करोति। saṃsat nūtanaṃ niyamaṃ karoti. Parliament makes a new law.
A feminine noun.
The stem ends in -d; said on its own, as here, the word ends in -t: संसत्, saṃsat.

The word, taken apart.
संसद् (saṃsad) is built on sam, "together", and the root √sad, "to sit"; MW gives saṃ-sad "an assembly, a meeting, a court", and "a parliament" is its modern sense (MW).
