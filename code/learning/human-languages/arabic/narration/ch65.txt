Arabic, chapter 65: To speak, To carry, To win, To go down, Fish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تكلم (takallama) — to speak (he spoke).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to wait, then the Arabic for to come back.

You'll want to know: تكلم.
تكلم — takallama — "to speak (he spoke)".

The word, taken apart.
تكلم (takallama) is built on the native Arabic root k-l-m, "to speak to; speech" (Lane). Takallama is Form V, "to speak"; the same root gives kalima, "a word", and kalām, "speech".
