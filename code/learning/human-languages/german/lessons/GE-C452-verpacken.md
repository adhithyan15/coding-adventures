## You'll want to know: verpacken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C452-LIEFERUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**verpacken** — "to wrap up, to pack".

> Im Laden verpackt die Verkäuferin das Buch als Geschenk.
>
> *In the shop the assistant wraps the book as a present.*

*Verpacken* — to put in packaging; *einpacken* — to pack, also to gift-wrap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verpacken* is built from *ver-* and *packen* 'to pack', which was borrowed from Middle Low German *packen* (EtymDB).
