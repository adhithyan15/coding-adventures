Lesson 1 of 1.
One old date-form repair — repair only the mistaken mark in an already-taught fictional date field.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Stop before looking at or marking the printed form. This is a short return to the same two fictional cards, not a real personal date or a scored A1 mock.

Warm-up.
The old bank is still here: ਕ chooses A ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫, and ਖ chooses B ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫. The label is ਤਾਰੀਖ਼. Check one dimension at a time: selector, day, month, year, slash marks, then label spelling and spacing.

Guided Practice.
Keep the bank visible. For ਕ, the printed value ੧੫/੦੧੨੦੨੫ lacks only one slash. Copy it with that slash after ੦੧: ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫. Do not change any digit. For ਖ, the value in ਤਾਰੀਖ: ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫ is already right; copy only the repaired label ਤਾਰੀਖ਼ with its final dot beneath ਖ਼. Leave the date untouched and keep the space after the colon.

Wrap-up Recall.
Cover the two repairs for a few seconds. Point to the one missing mark in each, then uncover the bank and compare. If one mark differs, repair that mark alone. The model stays available; this is not independent timed writing.
