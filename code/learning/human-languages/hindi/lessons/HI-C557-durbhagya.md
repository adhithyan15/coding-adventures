## You'll want to know: दुर्भाग्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C557-ANDESHA557-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्भाग्य** — *durbhāgya* — "misfortune; bad luck".

> दुर्भाग्य से हमारी ट्रेन छूट गई।
>
> *Durbhāgya se hamārī ṭren chūṭ gaī.*
>
> Unfortunately we missed our train.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्भाग्य** (*durbhāgya*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *durbhāgya*, "ill luck" (Monier-Williams).
