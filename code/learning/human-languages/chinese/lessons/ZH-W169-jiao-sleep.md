## Script — three strokes, a cover, then 见
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JIAO-SLEEP-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 觉

**Three pieces, top to bottom.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**Three short strokes on top:** a dot on the left, a dot in the middle, and a short stroke on the right falling to the left.

**Then the cover piece of 学, two strokes:** a short stroke falling down on the left, then a horizontal that turns down and hooks.

**Then 见, four strokes,** as you write it, under the cover.

3 + 2 + 4 = nine.
