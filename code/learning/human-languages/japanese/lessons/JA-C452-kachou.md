## You'll want to know: かちょう

**かちょう** — *kachō* — "a section chief".

> **かちょうは まいあさ はやく きます。** — *kachō wa maiasa hayaku kimasu* — "The section chief comes in early every morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かちょう** (*kachō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ka*, "section" (Middle Chinese *khwaH*) and *chō*, "leader" (*trjangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
