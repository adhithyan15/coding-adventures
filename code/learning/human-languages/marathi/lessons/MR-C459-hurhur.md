## You'll want to know: हुरहूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C459-ODH459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हुरहूर** — *hurhūr* — "a wistful unease".

> शाळेचा शेवटचा दिवस होता, मनात हुरहूर होती.
>
> *śāḷecā śevaṭcā divas hotā, manāt hurhūr hotī.*
>
> It was the last day of school, and there was an ache in the heart.

Feminine.

The ache at an ending or a parting: हुरहूर लागणे, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हुरहूर** (*hurhūr*) is a Marathi noun built on a doubled syllable; its origin is not settled.
