Italian, chapter 468: Il gelo.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
il gelo — the frost, the icy cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the hail, then the Italian for to hail.

You'll want to know: il gelo.
il gelo — "the frost, the icy cold".
Con questo gelo resto a casa. In this icy cold I am staying at home.
Also a chill between people: è sceso il gelo.

The word, taken apart.
gelo is inherited from Latin gelū 'frost, chill' (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 6.
la rugiada — the dew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to freeze, then the Italian for the white frost.

You'll want to know: la rugiada.
la rugiada — "the dew".
L'erba è bagnata di rugiada. The grass is wet with dew.
In the morning, after a clear night.

The word, taken apart.
rugiada goes back to Latin rōs, rōris 'dew' (EtymDB); its shape points to a Gallo-Romance go-between like Old French rosée, though that path is not settled.
