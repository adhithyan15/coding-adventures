## You'll want to know: शिट्टी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C533-TANDURUSTI533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिट्टी** — *śiṭṭī* — "a whistle".

> प्रशिक्षकाने शिट्टी वाजवली आणि खेळ सुरू झाला.
>
> *praśikṣakāne śiṭṭī vājavlī āṇi kheḷ surū jhālā.*
>
> The coach blew the whistle and the game began.

Feminine.

शिट्टी वाजवणे, to blow a whistle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिट्टी** (*śiṭṭī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *śiṭī*, "whistle", under the sound word *\*sīṭṭa* (CDIAL 13427).
