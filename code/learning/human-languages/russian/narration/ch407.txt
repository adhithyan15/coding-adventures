Russian, chapter 407: Overtaking and turning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
обгонять (obgonyát) — to overtake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a seat, then the Russian for a windscreen wiper.

You'll want to know: обгонять.
обгонять (obgonyát) — "to overtake".
На этой дороге опасно обгонять. Na étoy doróge opásno obgonyát. It is dangerous to overtake on this road.
Imperfective; perfective обогнать (обгоню, обгонишь). Takes the accusative: обгонять (obgonyát) грузовик.

The word, taken apart.
обгонять (obgonyát) is built from об- 'round, past' and гонять 'to drive'. Гонять is inherited from Proto-Slavic ganjati (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
перестраиваться (perestráivatsya) — to change lanes; to adapt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a windscreen wiper, then the Russian for to overtake.

You'll want to know: перестраиваться.
перестраиваться (perestráivatsya) — "to change lanes; to adapt".
Перед перекрёстком она перестраивается в левый ряд. Péred perekryóstkom oná perestráivayetsya v lévyy ryad. Before the crossroads she moves into the left lane.
Imperfective; perfective перестроиться. Перестраиваться в ряд — to move into a lane.

The word, taken apart.
перестраиваться (perestráivatsya) is built from пере- 'across, again', строить 'to build, to set in rows' and -ся. EtymDB does not list the verb; its parts are ordinary Russian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
сворачивать (svoráchivat) — to turn (off a road), to turn (into a street).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to overtake, then the Russian for to change lanes.

You'll want to know: сворачивать.
сворачивать (svoráchivat) — "to turn (off a road), to turn (into a street)".
После моста мы всегда сворачиваем направо. Pósle mósta my vsegdá svoráchivayem naprávo. After the bridge we always turn right.
Imperfective; perfective свернуть. Свернуть налево — to turn left.

The word, taken apart.
сворачивать (svoráchivat) is built from с- and -ворачивать, on the root of вертеть 'to turn'. EtymDB traces вертеть to Proto-Slavic vĭrtěti and the PIE root wert- 'to turn'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
обочина (obóchina) — a hard shoulder, a roadside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to turn, then the Russian for to turn round.

You'll want to know: обочина.
обочина (obóchina) — "a hard shoulder, a roadside".
Сломанная машина стояла на обочине. Slómannaya mashína stoyála na obóchine. A broken-down car was standing on the hard shoulder.
Feminine. Съехать на обочину — to pull over onto the shoulder.

The word, taken apart.
обочина (obóchina) is usually traced to the old adverb обочь 'alongside', built on бок 'a side': the strip at the side of the road. EtymDB does not list the word, so the line is hedged.
