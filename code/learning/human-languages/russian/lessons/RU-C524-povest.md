## You'll want to know: повесть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C524-GENRES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**повесть** (*póvest*) — "a novella".

> Эту повесть мы читали в школе.
>
> *Étu póvest my chitáli v shkóle.*
>
> We read this novella at school.

Feminine. Longer than *рассказ*, shorter than *роман*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*повесть* is built on *по-* and *весть* 'news, tidings', so a story is something told; EtymDB lists *повесть* without a source.
