## You'll want to know: तंग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C211-SIZE211-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तंग** — *taṅg* — "tight".

> ये पजामो तंग है।
>
> *ye pajāmo taṅg hai.*
>
> These trousers are tight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तंग** (*taṅg*) is usually traced to Persian *tang*, "narrow, tight".
