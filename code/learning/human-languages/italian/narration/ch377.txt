Italian, chapter 377: Se ho tempo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
disponibile — available; willing, helpful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for unlikely, then the Italian for the probability.

You'll want to know: disponibile.
disponibile — "available; willing, helpful".
Se sei disponibile sabato, ci vediamo. If you are free on Saturday, let us meet.
Of a person, also willing to help: è sempre molto disponibile.

The word, taken apart.
disponibile is built on disporre "to arrange, to have at one's disposal", from Latin dispōnere "to set in order" (Lewis & Short), built on pōnere "to put".

You'll want to know: impegnato.
impegnato — "busy, tied up; committed".
Domani sono impegnato tutto il giorno. Tomorrow I am busy all day.

The word, taken apart.
impegnato is the past participle of impegnare "to pledge, to engage", built from in- and pegno "a pledge", inherited from Latin pignus "a pledge" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
flessibile — flexible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the commitment, then the Italian for to commit yourself.

You'll want to know: flessibile.
flessibile — "flexible".
Ho un orario flessibile: posso cominciare alle dieci. I have flexible hours: I can start at ten.
Of things too: un tubo flessibile — a hose.

The word, taken apart.
flessibile is a learned borrowing from Latin flexibilis "pliant, easily bent" (Lewis & Short), from flectere "to bend".
