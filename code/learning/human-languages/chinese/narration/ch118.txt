Mandarin Chinese, chapter 118: Sending Things.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bāoguǒ — parcel; package.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the north, then the Mandarin for direction.

You'll want to know: bāoguǒ.
bāoguǒ — "parcel; package".
zhège bāoguǒ shì nǐ de ma — Is this parcel yours?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
bāoguǒ is built from bāo and guǒ, both inherited from Middle Chinese, paew and kwaX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second 'to wrap, to bind', and gives the pair itself as the gloss of the first.

What you've built.
One more word for everyday actions.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bāoguǒ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bāoguǒ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: zhège bāoguǒ shì nǐ de ma — Is this parcel yours?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for the north, then the Mandarin for direction, then say bāoguǒ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does bāoguǒ mean? ("Parcel; package".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.
xìn — letter (in the post).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for parcel, then the Mandarin for to post.

You'll want to know: xìn.
xìn — "letter (in the post)".
wǒ xiǎng gěi péngyou xiě xìn — I'd like to write to a friend.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xìn is inherited from Middle Chinese sinH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'trust' and 'proof'; how it came to mean a letter is not settled.

duǎnxìn is built from duǎn 'short' and xìn 'letter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, twanX and sinH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a short letter.
