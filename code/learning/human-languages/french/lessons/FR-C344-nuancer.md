## You'll want to know: nuancer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C344-RAISONNEMENT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**nuancer** — "to qualify, to tone down (a view)".

> Je voudrais nuancer ce que j'ai dit hier.
>
> *I would like to qualify what I said yesterday.*

*Nous nuançons*, with *ç* before *o*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nuancer* is built on *nuance* "shade", whose origin is usually traced to Old French *nue* "cloud", inherited from Latin *nūbēs* "cloud" (Lewis & Short).
