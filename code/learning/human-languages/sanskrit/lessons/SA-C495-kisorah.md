## You'll want to know: किशोरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C495-YAUVANA495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**किशोरः** — *kiśoraḥ* — "a lad".

> किशोरः क्रीडांगणे कन्दुकेन क्रीडति।
>
> *kiśoraḥ krīḍāṅgaṇe kandukena krīḍati.*
>
> The lad plays ball in the playground.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किशोरः** (*kiśoraḥ*) MW's first sense is "a colt", then "a youth, a lad"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
