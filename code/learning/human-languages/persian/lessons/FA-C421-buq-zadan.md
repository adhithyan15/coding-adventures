## You'll want to know: بوق زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C421-BUGH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بوق زدن** (*buq zadan*) — "to honk, to sound the horn".

> راننده تاکسی جلوی خانه ما دو بار بوق زد.
>
> *Rânande-ye tâksi jolow-ye khâne-ye mâ do bâr buq zad.*
>
> The taxi driver honked twice in front of our house.

*buq* + *zadan* (to strike). Present stem *zan-*: *buq mizanad* — he honks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*buq zadan* is built from *buq* 'horn', a loan usually traced to Arabic *būq* 'a trumpet', and the native verb *zadan* 'to strike'.
