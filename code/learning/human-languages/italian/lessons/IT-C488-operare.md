## You'll want to know: operare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C488-OSPEDALE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**operare** — "to operate (on)".

> Mi hanno operato al ginocchio l'anno scorso.
>
> *I had an operation on my knee last year.*

*Farsi operare* — to have an operation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*operare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *operārī* "to work, to be busy" (EtymDB), from *opus, operis* "work".
