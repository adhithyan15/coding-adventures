# ਠੰਢਾ (ṭhaṇḍhā) — cold; cool

## You'll want to know: ਠੰਢਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C291-WARM291-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠੰਢਾ** — *ṭhaṇḍhā* — "cold; cool".

> ਘੜੇ ਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਠੰਢਾ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *ghaṛe dā pāṇī ṭhaṇḍhā hundā hai.*
>
> Water from a clay pot is cool.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠੰਢਾ** (*ṭhaṇḍhā*) goes back to Sanskrit *stabdha*, "stiff, solidified (as water)"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭhāḍhā* under it only as "upright", a word it marks as taken from Hindi; the sense "cold" is the one it gives for Lahnda *ṭhaḍḍhā* and Hindi *ṭhaṇḍhā* (CDIAL 13676).
