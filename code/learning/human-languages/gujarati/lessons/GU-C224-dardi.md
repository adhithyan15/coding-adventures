# દર્દી (dardī) — patient

## You'll want to know: દર્દી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C224-DOCTOR224-02]; assesses=[] -->

**દર્દી** — *dardī* — "patient".

> **શું દર્દી ઘરે છે?** — *śũ dardī ghare che?* — "Is the patient at home?"

A man or a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દર્દી** (*dardī*) is built on *dard*, "pain", a loan usually traced to Persian *dard*, with the suffix *-ī*, "one who has". CDIAL has no entry for it.
