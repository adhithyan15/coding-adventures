## You'll want to know: प्रतिपक्षः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C344-PAKSHA344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिपक्षः** — *pratipakṣaḥ* — "the opposing side".

> प्रतिपक्षस्य मतम् अन्यत् अस्ति।
>
> *pratipakṣasya matam anyat asti.*
>
> The other side's view is different.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिपक्षः** (*pratipakṣaḥ*) is built from *prati*, "against", and *pakṣa*, "a wing, a side"; MW gives *prati-pakṣa* "the opposite side, a hostile party" (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
