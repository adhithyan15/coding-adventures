## You'll want to know: आठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-AATH]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **आठ** — *āṭh* — **8**

Sanskrit **अष्ट** (*aṣṭa*) — Latin *octo*, English **eight**. Hear what the
**ष्ट** became: a single retroflex **ठ**. That is the regular outcome, and it is
why so many Marathi words carry a retroflex where Sanskrit had a cluster.
