Gujarati, chapter 399: Delays and Announcements.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વિલંબ (vilamb) — a delay (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a jolt, then the Gujarati for to rattle.

You'll want to know: વિલંબ.
વિલંબ — vilamb — "a delay (formal)".
ધુમ્મસને લીધે ટ્રેનમાં બે કલાકનો વિલંબ છે. dhummasne līdhe ṭrenmā̃ be kalākno vilamb che. Because of the fog the train is two hours late.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વિલંબ (vilamb) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vilamba, "hanging down", then "slowness, delay" (Monier-Williams); station announcements use it where speech says મોડું.

Lesson 2 of 5.
સંભળાવું (sambhḷāvũ) — to be heard; to be audible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to rattle, then the Gujarati for a delay.

You'll want to know: સંભળાવું.
સંભળાવું — sambhḷāvũ — "to be heard; to be audible".
દૂરથી ટ્રેનની સીટી સંભળાય છે. dūrthī ṭrennī sīṭī sambhḷāy che. The train's whistle can be heard from far away.
Past (it …): તે અવાજ સંભળાયો, te avāj sambhḷāyo.

The word, taken apart.
સંભળાવું (sambhḷāvũ) is the intransitive partner of સાંભળવું, "to listen, to hear", which goes back to saṁbhalati, "attends to", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 12962): what you hear, સાંભળવું; what can be heard, સંભળાવું.

Lesson 3 of 5.
એલાન (elān) — a public announcement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a delay, then the Gujarati for to be heard.

You'll want to know: એલાન.
એલાન — elān — "a public announcement".
સ્ટેશન પર એલાન સંભળાયું કે ટ્રેન મોડી છે. sṭeśan par elān sambhḷāyũ ke ṭren moḍī che. An announcement at the station said the train was late.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
એલાન (elān) is usually traced to Arabic iʿlān, "a declaration, a making known", which reached Gujarati through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ટળવું (ṭaḷvũ) — to be averted; to be put off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be heard, then the Gujarati for a public announcement.

You'll want to know: ટળવું.
ટળવું — ṭaḷvũ — "to be averted; to be put off".
પવનની દિશા ફરી ગઈ, એટલે મોટું તોફાન ટળી ગયું. pavannī diśā pharī gaī, eṭle moṭũ tophān ṭaḷī gayũ. The wind changed direction, so the big storm was averted.
Past (it …): તે તોફાન ટળી ગયું, te tophān ṭaḷī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
ટળવું (ṭaḷvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit ṭálati, "moves aside" (CDIAL 5450); ટાળવું, to avoid, is its causative partner: what you put off, ટાળવું; what passes off by itself, ટળવું.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ડોકિયું.
ડોકિયું — ḍokiyũ — "a peep; a quick look".
મેં બારીમાંથી બહાર ડોકિયું કર્યું. mẽ bārīmā̃thī bahār ḍokiyũ karyũ. I took a peep out of the window.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ડોકિયું (ḍokiyũ) is built on ડોક, the neck, which CDIAL lists under ḍōka, "head" (CDIAL 5566): you stretch your neck out to look.
