## You'll want to know: সাইজ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C423-THONGA423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সাইজ** — *sāij* — "a size (of clothes or shoes)".

> এই জুতোটা আমার সাইজের নয়।
>
> *ei jutoṭā āmār sāijer nôy.*
>
> These shoes are not my size.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাইজ** (*sāij*) is borrowed from English *size*; the English z is said with জ.
