# ഇരുട്ട് (iruṭṭŭ) — darkness

## You'll want to know: ഇരുട്ട്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C381-IRUTTU381-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇരുട്ട്** — *iruṭṭŭ* — "darkness".

> കറന്റ് പോയി, വീട് മുഴുവൻ ഇരുട്ടിലായി.
>
> *kaṟanṟŭ pōyi, vīṭŭ muḻuvan iruṭṭilāyi.*
>
> The power went and the whole house was in darkness.

In the dark is ഇരുട്ടിൽ. The adjective ഇരുണ്ട, dark, has the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇരുട്ട്** (*iruṭṭŭ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2552 lists Malayalam *iruṭṭu*, "darkness", with *iruḷ* and the verb for growing dark.
