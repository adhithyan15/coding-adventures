## You'll want to know: le locataire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C312-DEMENAGEMENT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le locataire** — "the tenant".

> Le nouveau locataire est arrivé lundi.
>
> *The new tenant arrived on Monday.*

The owner is *le propriétaire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*locataire* is a learned word built on Latin *locātus*, from *locāre* "to place, to let out" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists Italian *locatario* beside it.
