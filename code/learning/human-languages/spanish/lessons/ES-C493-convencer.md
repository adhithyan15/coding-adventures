## You'll want to know: convencer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C493-OPINION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**convencer** — "to convince".

> No me convence la idea.
>
> *I am not convinced by the idea.*

It takes a *z* in the first person present: *convenzo*.
