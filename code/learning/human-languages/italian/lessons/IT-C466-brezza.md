## You'll want to know: la brezza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C466-AFA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la brezza** — "the breeze".

> La sera arriva una brezza dal mare.
>
> *In the evening a breeze comes in from the sea.*

A gentle wind; *la brezza marina* — the sea breeze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *brezza* is not settled; it may be linked to French *brise*.
