## You'll want to know: herdar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C316-HERANCA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**herdar** — "to inherit".

> Herdei os olhos azuis da minha mãe.
>
> *I inherited my blue eyes from my mother.*

*Herdar de alguém* — to inherit from someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*herdar* comes down from Old Portuguese *erdar* (EtymDB), from Latin *hērēditāre* "to inherit", built on *hērēs* "an heir".
