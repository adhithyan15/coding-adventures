## You'll want to know: प्रधान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C326-SAMRAT326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रधान** — *pradhān* — "a minister".

> राजाने हुशार प्रधानाचा सल्ला घेतला.
>
> *rājāne huśār pradhānācā sallā ghetlā.*
>
> The king took the advice of his clever minister.

Masculine.

Today पंतप्रधान is the prime minister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रधान** (*pradhān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pradhāna*, 'chief, foremost; a chief minister' (Monier-Williams).
