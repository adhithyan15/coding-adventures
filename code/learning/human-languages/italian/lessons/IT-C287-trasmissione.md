## You'll want to know: la trasmissione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C287-DIRETTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la trasmissione** — "the programme, the broadcast; transmission".

> È una trasmissione molto seguita.
>
> *It is a very popular programme.*

On TV or radio: *una trasmissione di cucina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trasmissione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *trānsmissiō* "a sending across" (EtymDB), from *trānsmittere*.
