## You'll want to know: remendar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C451-ARRANJOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**remendar** — "to patch, to mend".

> A minha avó remenda as meias à noite.
>
> *My grandmother patches socks in the evening.*

*Um remendo* — a patch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remendar* is built from *re-* and *emendar* 'to correct' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *ēmendāre* 'to free from faults'.
