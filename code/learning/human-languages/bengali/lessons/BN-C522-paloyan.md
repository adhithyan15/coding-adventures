## You'll want to know: পালোয়ান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C522-BYAYAM522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পালোয়ান** — *pāloyān* — "a strongman; a wrestler (in the old style)".

> গ্রামের পালোয়ান একাই একটা ভারী পাথর তুলল।
>
> *Grāmer pāloyān ekāi ekṭā bhārī pāthôr tullô.*
>
> The village strongman lifted a heavy stone all by himself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পালোয়ান** (*pāloyān*) is usually traced to Persian *pahlavān*, "a champion, a hero", worn down in everyday speech.
