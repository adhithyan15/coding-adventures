Italian, chapter 136: To tell, To walk, To clean, To find, To look for.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
raccontare — to tell (a story).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to rent, then the Italian for to repair.

You'll want to know: raccontare.
raccontare — "to tell (a story)".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: serious, right, wrong, ready, safe; sure]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hard, round, wide, narrow; tight, angry]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the weekend, a date, a century, often, sometimes]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Serious? (serio.) Right? (giusto.) Wrong? (sbagliato.) Ready? (pronto.) Safe; sure? (sicuro.) Dangerous? (pericoloso.) Wet? (bagnato.) Dry? (asciutto.) Cool; fresh? (fresco.) Soft? (morbido.) Hard? (duro.) Round? (rotondo.) Wide? (largo.) Narrow; tight? (stretto.) Angry? (arrabbiato.) Bitter? (amaro.) Salty? (salato.) Light? (leggero.) Heavy? (pesante.) Thin? (magro.) The weekend? (il fine settimana.) A date? (la data.) A century? (il secolo.) Often? (spesso.) Sometimes? (qualche volta.)
