## You'll want to know: mogeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C501-BRETTSPIEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**mogeln** — "to cheat (in a game)".

> Beim Kartenspielen mogelt mein kleiner Bruder immer.
>
> *My little brother always cheats at cards.*

Informal; also *schummeln*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *mogeln* is not settled; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
