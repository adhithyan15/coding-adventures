## You'll want to know: оркестр
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C517-ORCHESTRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**оркестр** (*orkéstr*) — "an orchestra".

> Оркестр играл два часа без перерыва.
>
> *Orkéstr igrál dva chasá bez pererýva.*
>
> The orchestra played for two hours without a break.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*оркестр* comes from Greek *orkhḗstra*, the dancing floor of a theatre, built on *orkhéomai* 'to dance' (EtymDB).
