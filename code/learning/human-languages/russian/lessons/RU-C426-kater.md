## You'll want to know: катер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C426-VESLO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**катер** (*káter*) — "a motor boat, a launch".

> На катере мы быстро добрались до острова.
>
> *Na kátere my býstro dobrális do óstrova.*
>
> We quickly got to the island by motor boat.

Masculine; plural *катера*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*катер* was borrowed from English *cutter* (EtymDB), a small fast boat, built on *cut*.
