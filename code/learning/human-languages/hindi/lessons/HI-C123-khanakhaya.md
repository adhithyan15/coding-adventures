## You'll want to know: खाना खाया?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C123-HAAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खाना खाया?** (*khānā khāyā?*) — "have you eaten?".

**खाना** is both "food" and "to eat": you *ate the eating*. Around a mealtime it is a greeting
and a kindness.
