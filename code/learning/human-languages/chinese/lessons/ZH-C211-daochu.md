## You'll want to know: dàochù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C211-LOCAL211-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dàochù* — "everywhere".

> *shìzhōngxīn dàochù dōu shì rén* — There are people everywhere in the city centre.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàochù* is built from 到 'to reach' and *chù* 'a place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tawH* and *tsyhoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a place'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
