Hindi, chapter 340: A Novel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उपन्यास (upanyās) — a novel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for trickery, then the Hindi for to lure.

You'll want to know: उपन्यास.
उपन्यास — upanyās — "a novel".
मीरा ने छुट्टी में एक लंबा उपन्यास पढ़ा। Mīrā ne chuṭṭī meṁ ek lambā upanyās paṛhā. Mira read a long novel during the holidays.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
उपन्यास (upanyās) is Sanskrit upanyāsa, "a placing near; a statement" (Monier-Williams). Modern Hindi uses it for a novel; the dictionary gives only the older senses.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a novel, then the Hindi for a hero (of a story).

You'll want to know: पात्र.
पात्र — pātra — "a character (in a story)".
इस कहानी में सिर्फ़ तीन पात्र हैं। is kahānī meṁ sirf tīn pātra haiṁ. There are only three characters in this story.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पात्र (pātra) is Sanskrit pātra (Monier-Williams), first a vessel and then a worthy person and an actor in a play; in Hindi it is any character in a story.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अंजाम (añjām) — an outcome; the end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a character (in a story), then the Hindi for a chapter of a book.

You'll want to know: अंजाम.
अंजाम — añjām — "an outcome; the end".
उपन्यास का अंजाम मुझे पसंद नहीं आया। upanyās kā añjām mujhe pasand nahīṃ āyā. I didn't like the way the novel ended.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अंजाम (añjām) is usually traced to Persian anjām, "an end, a completion". It is how things turn out: इसका अंजाम बुरा होगा, this will end badly.
