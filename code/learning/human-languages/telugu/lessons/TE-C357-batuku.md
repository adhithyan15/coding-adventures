# బతుకు (batuku) — to survive; to live

## You'll want to know: బతుకు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C357-VAMSAM357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**బతుకు** — *batuku* — "to survive; to live".

> ఆ ప్రమాదంలో అతను బతికాడు.
>
> *ā pramādaṁlō atanu batikāḍu.*
>
> He survived that accident.

Past (I …): బతికాను, *batikānu*.

Also a noun: బతుకు, a life, a living.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బతుకు** (*batuku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5372 lists Telugu *batuku*, *braduku*, "to live, subsist, survive".
