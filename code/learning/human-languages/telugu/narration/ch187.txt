Telugu, chapter 187: Bee, Lizard, Insect, Sparrow, Koel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తేనెటీగ (tēneṭīga) — a bee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for twins, then the Telugu for a baby.

You'll want to know: తేనెటీగ.
తేనెటీగ — tēneṭīga — "a bee".

The word, taken apart.
తేనెటీగ (tēneṭīga) is native Dravidian: tēne, "honey", one word with Tamil tēṉ (DEDR 3268), and īga, "fly", one word with Tamil ī, "fly, bee" (DEDR 533). For the first, DEDR compares, with a query, Prakrit tiṇisa, "honeycomb".

Lesson 3 of 5.
పురుగు (purugu) — an insect, a worm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a bee, then the Telugu for a lizard.

You'll want to know: పురుగు.
పురుగు — purugu — "an insect, a worm".

The word, taken apart.
పురుగు (purugu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4312 lists purugu, "worm, any insect", with Tamil puḻu, "worm", and Kannada puḻu, "worm, insect". DEDR compares several Sanskrit words for "worm", among them pīlu, "worm" (CDIAL 8240), which CDIAL takes from Dravidian.
