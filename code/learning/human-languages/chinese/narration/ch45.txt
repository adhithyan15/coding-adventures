Mandarin Chinese, chapter 45: Today and Tomorrow.
7 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
天 (tiān) — day; sky.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to sit, then the Mandarin for taxi.

You'll want to know: 天.
天 — tiān — "day; sky".
那天我们去大学了 nà tiān wǒmen qù dàxué le — That day we went to the university.
The new character, in pieces: 天 (tiān) — 一 above 大: one more bar over the big piece. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
天 (tiān) is inherited from Middle Chinese then (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the highest, with nothing above it', the sky. Its use for a day is not given there.

What you've built.
One more word for times and partings.

一 大.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — yī, dà]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — one more bar over 大.
天 (tiān).
Two pieces your hand knows, top to bottom: 一, then 大.
一, one stroke, a short horizontal at the top.
大, three strokes: a longer horizontal, a left-falling stroke from above it, and a right-falling stroke.
1 + 3 = four. Keep the top bar shorter than the one under it.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 3 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for taxi, then the Mandarin for day.

You'll want to know: 今天.
今天 — jīntiān — "today".
你们今天去吗 nǐmen jīntiān qù ma — Are you going today?
The new character, in pieces: 今 (jīn) — 人 as a roof, a dot under it, and a stroke that runs across and turns down. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
今天 (jīntiān) is built from 今 (jīn) 'now' and 天 (tiān) 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kim and then (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses kim as the opposite of olden times.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. [once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — rén, gè]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — a roof, a dot and a turn.
今 (jīn).
Top to bottom.
The roof, 人, two strokes, spread wide: a left-falling stroke, then a right-falling stroke, as in 个.
A dot in the middle, under the roof.
A stroke that runs across and turns down to the left, under the dot.
2 + 1 + 1 = four.

Guided Practice.

[pause 3 seconds]

You'll want to know: zuótiān.
zuótiān — "yesterday".
zuótiān wǒ hěn lèi — I was really tired yesterday.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zuótiān is built from zuó 'the day before' and 天 (tiān) 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzak and then (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses dzak as the day one night back.
