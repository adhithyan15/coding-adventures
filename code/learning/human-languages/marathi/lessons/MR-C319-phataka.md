## You'll want to know: फटाका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C319-ROSHNAI319-04]; assesses=[] -->

**फटाका** — *phaṭākā* — "a firecracker".

> रात्री मुलांनी खूप फटाके फोडले.
>
> *rātrī mulānnī khūp phaṭāke phoḍle.*
>
> At night the children set off lots of firecrackers.

Masculine. Plural: फटाके, *phaṭāke*.

Plural फटाके; फटाके फोडणे, to set off crackers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फटाका** (*phaṭākā*) is a Marathi word that seems built on the sound *phaṭ*, a bang; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
