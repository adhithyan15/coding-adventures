# क़सूर (qasūr) — fault, blame

## You'll want to know: क़सूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C367-TARIF367-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क़सूर** — *qasūr* — "fault, blame".

> यह तुम्हारा क़सूर नहीं है।
>
> *yah tumhārā qasūr nahīṃ hai.*
>
> It isn't your fault.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़सूर** (*qasūr*) is usually traced to Arabic *quṣūr*, "a shortcoming", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
