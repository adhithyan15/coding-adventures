## You'll want to know: xǐliǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C99-WASH99-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐliǎn* — "to wash one's face".

> *nǐ xǐliǎn le ma* — Have you washed your face?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones in a row: xǐ is said with a rising tone before liǎn, though both are written with the third tone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐliǎn* is built from 洗 'to wash' and liǎn 'face'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *sejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary has the second only as *leamX*, the name of a kind of stew, so how it came to mean face is not settled.
