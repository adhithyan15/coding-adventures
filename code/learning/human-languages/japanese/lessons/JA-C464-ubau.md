## You'll want to know: うばう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C464-CROWD464-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うばう** — *ubau* — "to snatch; to take away".

> **あめが かんきゃくの たのしみを うばいました。** — *ame ga kankyaku no tanoshimi o ubaimashita* — "The rain took away the spectators' fun."

Polite **うばいます**, *ubaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せんしゅが まりを うばいました。**, *senshu ga mari o ubaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うばう** (*ubau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
