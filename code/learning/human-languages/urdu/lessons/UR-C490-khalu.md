# خالو (khālū) — a mother's sister's husband

## You'll want to know: خالو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C490-STEP490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خالو** — *khālū* — "a mother's sister's husband".

> خالہ اور خالو کل آئیں گے۔
>
> *khālā aur khālū kal āeṅ ge.*
>
> My aunt and uncle will come tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خالو** (*khālū*) is built on **خالہ**, "a mother's sister", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khāla*: the aunt's husband.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
