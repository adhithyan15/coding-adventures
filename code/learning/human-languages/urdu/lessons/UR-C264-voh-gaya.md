# وہ گیا (voh gayā) — the past, by the one who went

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-NICE-TO-MEET, UR-LEX-JANA, UR-LEX-ANA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "pleased to meet you". (*āp se mil kar khushī huī*.) Its last word, **ہوئی** (*huī*), "happened", is a past. This chapter writes the past in Urdu script.

## You'll want to know: the past follows the one who went
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-VOH, UR-LEX-C57-FOOD57-05, UR-LEX-C100-LOC100-04, UR-LEX-HUM] -->

> **وہ گیا۔**
>
> *voh gayā.* — "He went."

> **وہ گئی۔**
>
> *voh gaī.* — "She went."

**گیا** (*gayā*) is **جانا** (*jānā*) in the past, with no **ہے** (*hai*) after it. Its ending follows the one who went, as **اچھا** (*achchhā*) follows its noun:

> **وہ گھر گیا۔**
>
> *voh ghar gayā.* — "He went home."

> **سارا گھر گئی۔**
>
> *Sārā ghar gaī.* — "Sara went home."

> **ہم بازار گئے۔**
>
> *ham bāzār gae.* — "We went to the market."

| who went | the past |
|---|---|
| a man | **گیا** (*gayā*) |
| a woman | **گئی** (*gaī*) |
| men; **ہم**; **آپ** | **گئے** (*gae*) |
| women | **گئیں** (*gaīṅ*) |

## You'll want to know: how a verb takes the ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-ANA, UR-LEX-C47-VERBS47-01, UR-LEX-C47-VERBS47-02, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-03] -->

Most verbs put the ending on the stem: **بیٹھنا** (*baiṭhnā*) gives **بیٹھا** (*baiṭhā*) and **بیٹھی** (*baiṭhī*).

> **وہ بیٹھی۔**
>
> *voh baiṭhī.* — "She sat down."

A stem that ends in a vowel adds **-یا** for a man and **-ئی** for a woman:

> **وہ کل آیا۔**
>
> *voh kal āyā.* — "He came yesterday."

> **سارا کل آئی۔**
>
> *Sārā kal āī.* — "Sara came yesterday."

**سونا** (*sonā*) gives **سویا** (*soyā*), and **اٹھنا** (*uṭhnā*) gives **اٹھا** (*uṭhā*). **ہونا** (*honā*) gives **ہوا** (*huā*) and **ہوئی** (*huī*): **کیا ہوا؟** (*kyā huā?*), "what happened?" A bare stem with **گیا** says a change is complete: **وہ سو گیا** (*voh so gayā*), "he fell asleep". "Did not" puts **نہیں** (*nahīṅ*) before the verb:

> **میں نہیں گیا۔**
>
> *maiṅ nahīṅ gayā.* — "I didn't go."
