## You'll want to know: a média
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C379-CHUMBAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a média** — "the average (mark)".

> Ela acabou o curso com média de dezassete.
>
> *She finished the degree with an average of seventeen.*

In Portugal marks go from 0 to 20; *a média* is your average. *Em média* — on average.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*média* comes from Latin *media*, the feminine of *medius* "middle" (Lewis & Short): the middle value.
