## You'll want to know: शंका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C368-SHANKA368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शंका** — *śaṅkā* — "a misgiving; a fear".

> यदि शंका अस्ति, तर्हि प्रश्नं पृच्छतु।
>
> *yadi śaṅkā asti, tarhi praśnaṃ pṛcchatu.*
>
> If you have a misgiving, ask a question.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शंका** (*śaṅkā*) comes from the root √śaṅk, "to doubt, to fear"; MW gives *śaṅkā* "apprehension, fear, distrust" and "doubt, hesitation" (MW).
