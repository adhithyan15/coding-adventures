## You'll want to know: audītor, audītōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C549-POEM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**audītor, audītōris** — "a listener, hearer".

> Audītōrēs poētae plaudunt.
>
> *The listeners applaud the poet.*

Masculine, third declension, from *audiō*; also a pupil. English *auditor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**audītor** is built on *audiō* "to hear" with *-tor* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *auditor* and *auditorium* come from the family.
