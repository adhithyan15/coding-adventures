## You'll want to know: der Abfluss
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C428-ABFLUSS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Abfluss** — "the drain, the plughole".

> Im Abfluss der Dusche sind viele Haare.
>
> *There is a lot of hair in the shower drain.*

Plural: *die Abflüsse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Abfluss* is built from *ab* 'away' and *Fluss* 'flow, river', inherited from Old High German *fluz* (EtymDB), from the verb for flowing.
