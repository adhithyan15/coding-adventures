Lesson 2 of 5.
साडी (sāḍī) — a sari.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for clothes, then the Marathi for a shirt.

You'll want to know: साडी.
साडी — sāḍī — "a sari".

The word, taken apart.
साडी (sāḍī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śāṭī, "strip of cloth, garment" (CDIAL 12381). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "the sari given to the bride at a wedding".

Lesson 5 of 5.
अंगठी (aṅgṭhī) — a ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sock, then the Marathi for a pocket.

You'll want to know: अंगठी.
अंगठी — aṅgṭhī — "a ring".

The word, taken apart.
अंगठी (aṅgṭhī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aṅguṣṭhiya, "of the thumb", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 138). It is built on aṅguṣṭha, "thumb".
