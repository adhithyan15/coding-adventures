## You'll want to know: ভূমিকম্প
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C293-BHUMIKOMPO293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ভূমিকম্প** — *bhūmikômpô* — "an earthquake".

> ভূমিকম্পে শহরের অনেক পুরনো বাড়ি ভেঙে পড়ল।
>
> *bhūmikômpe shôhôrer ônek purôno bāṛi bhenge pôṛlô.*
>
> Many old buildings in the city collapsed in the earthquake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভূমিকম্প** (*bhūmikômpô*) is Sanskrit *bhūmikampa*, 'an earthquake' (Monier-Williams): *bhūmi*, 'the earth', and *kampa*, 'trembling'. In the example, ভেঙে carries ঙ.
