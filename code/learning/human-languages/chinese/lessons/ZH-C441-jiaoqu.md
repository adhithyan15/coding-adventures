## You'll want to know: jiāoqū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C441-CAMP441-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāoqū* — "suburbs; outskirts".

> *wǒmen zhù zài jiāoqū zuò dìtiě qù shìzhōngxīn* — We live in the suburbs and take the underground into the centre.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāoqū* is built from *jiāo* 'the land beyond the walls' and 区 'an area', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaew* and *khju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the land outside a walled town.
