## You'll want to know: کرم کتاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C529-READERS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کرم کتاب** (*kerm-e ketâb*) — "a bookworm".

> خواهرم کرم کتاب است و هر هفته یک رمان میخواند.
>
> *khâharam kerm-e ketâb ast va har hafte yek român mikhânad.*
>
> My sister is a bookworm and reads a novel every week.

*kerm* 'worm' + *ketâb* 'book', as in English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kerm-e ketâb* joins inherited *kerm*, 'worm', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Iranian and sets beside Lithuanian *kirmis*, 'worm' (EtymDB), and *ketâb*, 'book', from Arabic.
