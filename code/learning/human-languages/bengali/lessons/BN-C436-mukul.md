## You'll want to know: মুকুল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C436-PHOTA436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**মুকুল** — *mukul* — "a mango blossom; a bud".

> বসন্তে আমগাছে মুকুল আসে।
>
> *bôsônte āmgāchhe mukul āse.*
>
> In spring the mango trees come into blossom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মুকুল** (*mukul*) is Sanskrit *mukula*, "a bud" (Monier-Williams); in Bengal it is above all the mango's spring blossom.
