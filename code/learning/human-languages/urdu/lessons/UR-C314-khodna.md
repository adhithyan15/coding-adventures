## You'll want to know: کھودنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C314-KHANDAR314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کھودنا** — *khodnā* — "to dig".

> مزدوروں نے کھنڈر میں زمین کھودی۔
>
> *mazdūroṅ ne khanḍar meṅ zamīn khodī.*
>
> The labourers dug the ground in the ruins.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے زمین کھودی, *us ne zamīn khodī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھودنا** (*khodnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khodnā*, "to dig, to carve", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3934).
