# വാങ്ങുക (vāṅṅuka) — to buy

[PAUSE 2s] Ask a price. Then say the verb *to take*.

## You'll want to know: വാങ്ങുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C91-BUY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**വാങ്ങുക** — *vāṅṅuka* — to buy.

It is built the way your verbs are built, on the **-ഉക** ending you have had
since your first ones, so there is nothing new in its shape — only its meaning.

**ഞാൻ പുസ്തകം വാങ്ങുന്നു** — *ñān pustakaṁ vāṅṅunnu* — I buy the book.

Look at the object. **പുസ്തകം** is bare — **no ending on it** — because it is a
thing and not a person, which is the rule the object chapter gave you. The verb
changed and the rule did not.

With this word the chapter closes a circle. **You can name the place, ask the
price, hear a number back, and say what you are doing there** — and of those
four, only the place, the price and this verb were new.
