## You'll want to know: преступник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C287-PRESTUPLENIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**преступник** (*prestúpnik*) — "a criminal".

> Преступник убежал через окно.
>
> *Prestúpnik ubezhál chérez oknó.*
>
> The criminal ran away through the window.

A woman is *преступница*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*преступник* is built on *преступить* "to step over", Church Slavonic *пре-* with *ступить* "to step" (EtymDB lists *ступень*, Proto-Slavic *stǫpenĭ*), and the ending *-ник* for a person.
