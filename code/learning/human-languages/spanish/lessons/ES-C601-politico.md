## You'll want to know: político
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C601-GOBIERNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**político** — "political; the politician".

> Es un tema político.
>
> *It is a political issue.*

As a noun: *el político, la política* — the politician.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*político* is a learned borrowing from Latin *politicus*, taken from Greek *politikos* 'of citizens, of the state', built on *polis* 'city' (EtymDB).
