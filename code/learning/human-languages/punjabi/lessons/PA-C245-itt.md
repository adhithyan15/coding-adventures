## You'll want to know: ਇੱਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C245-GHR245-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇੱਟ** — *iṭṭ* — "a brick".

> ਕੰਧ ਲਾਲ ਇੱਟਾਂ ਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *kandh lāl iṭṭāṁ dī hai.*
>
> The wall is made of red bricks.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇੱਟ** (*iṭṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *iṣṭakā*, "brick"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *iṭṭ*, "brick" (CDIAL 1600).
