Arabic, chapter 127: Curtain, Lock, Handkerchief, Bracelet, Earring.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ستارة (sitāra) — a curtain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a toy, then the Arabic for a fridge.

You'll want to know: ستارة.
ستارة — sitāra — "a curtain".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sitāra]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sitāra, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sitāra]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a toy, then the Arabic for a fridge, then say sitāra again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ستارة (sitāra) mean? ("A curtain".) Say it once more.
