Lesson 1 of 5.
মাটি (māṭi) — soil, earth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for fire, then the Bengali for ice.

You'll want to know: মাটি.
মাটি — māṭi — "soil, earth".

The word, taken apart.
মাটি (māṭi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mṛttikā, "earth, clay" (CDIAL 10286).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for ice, then the Bengali for soil.

You'll want to know: বাতাস.
বাতাস — bātās — "wind".

The word, taken apart.
বাতাস (bātās) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vātatrāsa, "wind-storm", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 11493). It is a compound of vāta, "wind", and trāsa, "fear".

কুকুর (kukur) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kukkura, "dog" (CDIAL 3329). CDIAL calls the word imitative of the animal's sound.
