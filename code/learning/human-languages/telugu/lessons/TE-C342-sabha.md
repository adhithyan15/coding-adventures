## You'll want to know: సభ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C342-SABHA342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సభ** — *sabha* — "a public meeting; an assembly".

> సాయంత్రం గుడి దగ్గర సభ జరిగింది.
>
> *sāyantraṁ guḍi daggara sabha jarigindi.*
>
> In the evening a meeting was held near the temple.

Plural: సభలు, *sabhalu*.

సభ జరిగింది, a meeting took place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సభ** (*sabha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sabhā*, "an assembly, a hall" (CDIAL 13167).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
