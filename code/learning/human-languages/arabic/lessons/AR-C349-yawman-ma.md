## You'll want to know: يوما ما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C349-MARRA349-04]; assesses=[] -->

**يوما ما** — *yawman mā* — "someday, one day".

> سأزور تلك المدينة يوما ما.
>
> *sa-azūru tilka al-madīna yawman mā.*
>
> I will visit that city someday.

Of the future; ذات يوم opens a story in the past.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**يوما ما** (*yawman mā*) is "on some day": *yawm* is usually traced to an inherited Semitic word for day, and *mā* after it means "some, any".
