## You'll want to know: शोषणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C348-AUCITYA348-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शोषणम्** — *śoṣaṇam* — "exploitation".

> धनिकः कर्मकरस्य शोषणं करोति।
>
> *dhanikaḥ karmakarasya śoṣaṇaṃ karoti.*
>
> The rich man exploits the worker.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शोषणम्** (*śoṣaṇam*) comes from the root √śuṣ, "to dry", in its causative form, "to make dry"; MW gives *śoṣaṇa* "drying up, draining, suction", and "exploitation" is the sense modern Sanskrit gives it (MW).
