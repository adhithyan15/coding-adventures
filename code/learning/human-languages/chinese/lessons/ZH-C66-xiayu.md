## You'll want to know: xiàyǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàyǔ* — "to rain".

> *xiàyǔ wǒmen bù dǎqiú* — It's raining, so we're not playing ball.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàyǔ* is built from xià 'to come down' and 雨 'rain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *hjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): rain comes down.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
