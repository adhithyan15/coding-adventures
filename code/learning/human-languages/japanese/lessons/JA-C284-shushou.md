## You'll want to know: しゅしょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C284-SENKYO284-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅしょう** — *shushō* — "the prime minister".

> **けさ しゅしょうが はっぴょうしました。** — *kesa shushō ga happyō shimashita* — "The prime minister made an announcement this morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅしょう** (*shushō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shu*, "neck" (Middle Chinese *syuwX*) and *shō*, "minister of state" (*sjangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
