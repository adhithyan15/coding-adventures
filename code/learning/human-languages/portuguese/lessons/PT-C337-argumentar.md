## You'll want to know: argumentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C337-ARGUMENTAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**argumentar** — "to argue (with reasons), to make a case".

> Ela argumentou que o bairro precisa de mais árvores.
>
> *She argued that the neighbourhood needs more trees.*

*Argumentar que…* — to argue that… It is about giving reasons, not about quarrelling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*argumentar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *argūmentārī* "to adduce proof of a thing" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), formed on *argūmentum* "proof".
