## You'll want to know: 音乐
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C128-MUSIC128-01]; assesses=[] -->

**音乐** — *yīnyuè* — "music".

> **我喜欢音乐** *wǒ xǐhuan yīnyuè* — I love music.

The new character, in pieces: 音 — on top a five-stroke piece — a dot, a short bar, two short strokes leaning toward each other, and a long bar — and under it 日. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**音乐** (*yīnyuè*) is built from 音 'sound' and 乐, here said *yuè*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'im* and *ngaewk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second with the whole word, 'music'.
