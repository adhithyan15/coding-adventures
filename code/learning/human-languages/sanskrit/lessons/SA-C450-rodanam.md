## You'll want to know: रोदनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C450-VISADA450-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रोदनम्** — *rodanam* — "weeping; crying".

> शिशोः रोदनेन माता रात्रौ जागर्ति।
>
> *śiśoḥ rodanena mātā rātrau jāgarti.*
>
> The baby's crying keeps its mother awake at night.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोदनम्** (*rodanam*) comes from the root √rud, "to weep", the root of **रोदिति**; MW gives "weeping, crying" and also "tears" (MW).
