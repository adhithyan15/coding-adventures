## You'll want to know: ڈھلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C373-BIKHARNA373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھلنا** — *ḍhalnā* — "to set (of the sun); to wane".

> اگر سورج ڈھل گیا تو ہم گھر نہیں پہنچیں گے۔
>
> *agar sūraj ḍhal gayā to ham ghar nahīṅ pahuncheṅ ge.*
>
> If the sun sets, we will not reach home.

Past (it …): وہ سورج ڈھل گیا, *voh sūraj ḍhal gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھلنا** (*ḍhalnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍhalnā*, "to topple over", under *\*ḍhalati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5581).
