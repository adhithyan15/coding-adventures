## You'll want to know: a menos que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C611-CONDIC-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a menos que** — "unless".

> Voy a la playa, a menos que llueva.
>
> *I am going to the beach, unless it rains.*

Always followed by the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a menos que* is built on *menos*, inherited from Latin *minus* 'less' (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
