## You'll want to know: সামর্থ্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C528-UPAY528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সামর্থ্য** — *sāmôrthyô* — "ability; the capacity (to do something)".

> এই কঠিন কাজ করার সামর্থ্য তার আছে।
>
> *Ei kôṭhin kāj kôrār sāmôrthyô tār āchhe.*
>
> She has the ability to do this hard work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সামর্থ্য** (*sāmôrthyô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sāmarthya*, which Monier-Williams gives with a range of senses up to "ability, power".
