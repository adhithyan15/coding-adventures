## You'll want to know: callejear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C674-PASEO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**callejear** — "to wander the streets".

> Me encanta callejear por el barrio viejo.
>
> *I love wandering the streets of the old quarter.*

Built on *la calle*: walking with no plan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*callejear* is built on *calle* "street", which is inherited from Latin *callis* "a footpath".
