## You'll want to know: 愿望
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C535-WILLING535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**愿望** — *yuànwàng* — "a wish one has; a desire".

> **如果可以有一个愿望你想要什么** *rúguǒ kěyǐ yǒu yí ge yuànwàng nǐ xiǎng yào shénme* — If you could have one wish, what would you want?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**愿望** (*yuànwàng*) is built from 愿 'to desire' and 望 'a hope', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjwonH* and *mjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
