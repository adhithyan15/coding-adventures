## You'll want to know: こうじょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C197-CITY197-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こうじょう** — *kōjō* — "factory; plant, workshop".

> **ちちは こうじょうで はたらきます。** — *chichi wa kōjō de hatarakimasu* — "My father works at a factory."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうじょう** (*kōjō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "craft, work" (Middle Chinese *kuwng*) and *jō*, "place" (*drjang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
