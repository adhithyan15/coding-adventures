## You'll want to know: निराकरोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C312-NIRAKARANA312-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निराकरोति** — *nirākaroti* — "denies; rejects".

> न्यायाधीशः आरोपं दृढं निराकरोति।
>
> *nyāyādhīśaḥ āropaṃ dṛḍhaṃ nirākaroti.*
>
> The judge firmly denies the allegation.

Past (he …): सः आरोपं निराकृतवान्, *saḥ āropaṃ nirākṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निराकरोति** (*nirākaroti*) is built from *nir*, *ā* and the root √kṛ, "to do"; MW gives *nir-ā-kṛ* as to drive away, repudiate, reject and contradict (MW).
