## You'll want to know: દાબવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C487-CAKRI487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**દાબવું** — *dābvũ* — "to massage by pressing; to press (the legs) to ease pain".

> રાતે મીરા દાદીના પગ દાબે છે.
>
> *rāte Mīrā dādīnā pag dābe che.*
>
> At night Mira presses her grandmother's legs to ease them.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દાદીના પગ દાબ્યા, *teṇe dādīnā pag dābyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાબવું** (*dābvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*dabb-*, "press", which some trace to Dravidian; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to press" (CDIAL 6173).
