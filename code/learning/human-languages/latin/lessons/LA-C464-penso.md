## You'll want to know: pēnsō, pēnsāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C464-STATERA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**pēnsō, pēnsāre** — "to weigh carefully, to weigh out; to ponder".

> Mercātor prūdēns mercem pēnsat.
>
> *A careful merchant weighs the goods.*

*pēnsō, pēnsāre, pēnsāvī, pēnsātum*, a frequentative of *pendō*. English *pensive* comes from it through French *penser* 'to think'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pēnsō* is a frequentative of *pendō* 'to weigh', made from its participle *pēnsus* (Lewis & Short). English *pensive* comes from it through French *penser* 'to think, to weigh in the mind'.
