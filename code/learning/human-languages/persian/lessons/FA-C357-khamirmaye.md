## You'll want to know: خمیرمایه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C357-KHAMIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خمیرمایه** (*khamirmâye*) — "yeast, leaven".

> او کمی خمیرمایه در آب گرم حل کرد.
>
> *U kami khamirmâye dar âb-e garm hall kard.*
>
> She dissolved a little yeast in warm water.

*khamir* "dough" + *mâye* "substance, starter".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khamirmâye* joins *khamir* "dough", borrowed from Arabic *khamīr* (EtymDB), and the native Persian *mâye* "substance, starter".
