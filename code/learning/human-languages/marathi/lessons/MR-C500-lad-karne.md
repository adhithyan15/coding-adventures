## You'll want to know: लाड करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C500-SANGOPAN500-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लाड करणे** — *lāḍ karṇe* — "to pamper".

> आजोबा नातवाचे खूप लाड करतात.
>
> *ājobā nātvāce khūp lāḍ kartāt.*
>
> Grandfather pampers his grandson a lot.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने लाड केले, *tyāne lāḍ kele*.

लाड is plural here; the one pampered takes the genitive: मुलाचे लाड करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाड करणे** (*lāḍ karṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on लाड, "fondling", which CDIAL lists under the reconstructed *\*lāḍya* (CDIAL 11013), with करणे.
