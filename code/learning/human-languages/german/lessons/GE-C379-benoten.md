## You'll want to know: benoten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C379-BEWERTUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**benoten** — "to grade (with a mark)".

> Die Klausuren werden erst nächste Woche benotet.
>
> *The exams will not be graded until next week.*

Inseparable: *er benotet, er hat benotet*. From *die Note* — the mark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*benoten* is built with *be-* on *Note* "mark, grade", from Latin *nota* "a mark, a sign, a note" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
