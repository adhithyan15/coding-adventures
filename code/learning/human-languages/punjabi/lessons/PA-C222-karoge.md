# ਕਰੋਗੇ, ਕਰਨਗੇ — you will do, they will do

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-LEX-YOU-REGISTER-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I will work tomorrow* and *we will work*. Then **ਤੁਸੀਂ**.

## Grammar Lens: two more cells
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-LEX-YOU-REGISTER-02, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-LEX-KAMM-05] -->

With **ਤੁਸੀਂ** the future of **ਕਰਨਾ** is **ਕਰੋਗੇ**; with **ਉਹ**, for a group, it is
**ਕਰਨਗੇ**:

> **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੀ ਕਰੋਗੇ?** — *tusīṁ kallh kī karoge?* — What will you do tomorrow?

> **ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਨਗੇ।** — *uh kallh kamm karange.* — They will work tomorrow.

Here **ਕੀ** is "what": it stands where the answer goes.
