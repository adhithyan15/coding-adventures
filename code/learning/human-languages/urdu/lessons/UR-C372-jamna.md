## You'll want to know: جمنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C372-JAMNA372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جمنا** — *jamnā* — "to freeze; to set".

> اگر رات کو سردی ہوئی تو پانی جم جائے گا۔
>
> *agar rāt ko sardī huī to pānī jam jāe gā.*
>
> If it is cold tonight, the water will freeze.

Past (it …): وہ دودھ جم گیا, *voh dūdh jam gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جمنا** (*jamnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jamnā*, "to coagulate, to freeze", under Sanskrit *yamyátē*, "is held fast" (CDIAL 10428).
