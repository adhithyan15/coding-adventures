## You'll want to know: expédier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C449-BURALISTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**expédier** — "to send off, to ship".

> Nous avons expédié le colis mardi.
>
> *We sent the parcel off on Tuesday.*

*L'expéditeur* — the sender.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*expédier* is built on *expédient*, from Latin *expedīre* 'to free the feet, to get things ready', from *pēs* 'foot' (Lewis & Short).
