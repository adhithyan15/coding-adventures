# द — one sign for both beats

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DADA-01, MW-LEX-BHAI-01, MW-SCRIPT-BHAI-01, MW-LEX-BAHAN-01, MW-ANSWER-WELLBEING-01, MW-SCRIPT-HOON-01, MW-SCRIPT-THIK-01] -->

[PAUSE 22s] Write brother, then the known wellbeing answer. Say paternal
grandfather and sister; keep *dādā* oral until the new sign appears.

## Script — one sign
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-DA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **द** — *da*

Keep the model visible. Trace the curved lower body, return to the upright, and
add the headline. Say *da* after the pen stops.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-DA-01, MW-LEX-DADA-01] -->

Copy **द** once. Cover it, wait five seconds, and write it twice with a small
space: **द द**. Say *dā-dā* without assembling the word yet.
