## You'll want to know: خلاصه کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C313-AMAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خلاصه کردن** (*kholâse kardan*) — "to summarise, to sum up".

> معلم داستان را در سه جمله خلاصه کرد.
>
> *Mo'allem dâstân râ dar se jomle kholâse kard.*
>
> The teacher summed up the story in three sentences.

Present *kholâse mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kholâse kardan* joins *kholâse* "a summary", borrowed from Arabic *khulāṣa* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), with *kardan* "to do", inherited Persian.
