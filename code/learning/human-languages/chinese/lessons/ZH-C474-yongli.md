## You'll want to know: 用力
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C474-STRENGTH474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**用力** — *yònglì* — "to push hard; to put one's strength into it".

> **牙医说吃东西的时候不要太用力** *yáyī shuō chī dōngxi de shíhou bú yào tài yònglì* — The dentist says not to bite down too hard when I eat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**用力** (*yònglì*) is built from 用 'to use' and 力 'strength', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yowngH* and *lik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
