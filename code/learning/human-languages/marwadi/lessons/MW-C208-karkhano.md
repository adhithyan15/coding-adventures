## You'll want to know: कारखानो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C208-OFFICES208-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कारखानो** — *kārkhāno* — "a factory".

> कारखानो सहर सूं दूर है।
>
> *kārkhāno sahar sū̃ dūr hai.*
>
> The factory is far from the city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कारखानो** (*kārkhāno*) is usually traced to Persian *kārkhāna*, "workshop".
