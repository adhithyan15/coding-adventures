## You'll want to know: ڈھونڈنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C282-TAFTISH282-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھونڈنا** — *ḍhūnḍnā* — "to look for".

> پولیس نے ہر جگہ چور کو ڈھونڈا۔
>
> *polīs ne har jagah chor ko ḍhūnḍā.*
>
> The police looked for the thief everywhere.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چابی ڈھونڈی, *us ne chābī ḍhūnḍī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍhū̃ḍhnā*, "to seek", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6839). Urdu writes the second *ḍ* without the breath: **ڈھونڈنا**.
