## You'll want to know: chēwèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C420-PARK420-03]; assesses=[] -->

*chēwèi* — "parking space".

> *wǒ zài tíngchēchǎng zhǎo bu dào chēwèi* — I can't find a space in the car park.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chēwèi* is built from 车 'vehicle' and *wèi* 'a place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhae* and *hwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
