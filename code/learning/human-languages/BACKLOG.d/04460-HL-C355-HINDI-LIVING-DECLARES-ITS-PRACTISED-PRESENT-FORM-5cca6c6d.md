## HL-C355 — Hindi living declares its practised present form

Chapter 5 builds **रहना** through the same stem-plus-ending machine as the
preceding speaking lesson, then teaches and practises **मैं दिल्ली में रहता
हूँ**. Its declaration exposes the infinitive and postposition but not
**रहता**, leaving the lesson's central written present form looking untaught to
the corpus report.

Declare **रहता** beside **रहना** and **में**. Keep the feminine form out of the
declaration for now: the lesson prompts it in romanization but never presents
its Devanagari spelling, so declaring it would outrun the written material.
