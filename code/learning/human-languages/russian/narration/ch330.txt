Russian, chapter 330: Mixing and stirring.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
смешивать (sméshivat) — to mix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a lid, then the Russian for to cover.

You'll want to know: смешивать.
смешивать (sméshivat) — "to mix".
Она смешала муку с сахаром. Oná smeshála múku s sákharom. She mixed the flour with the sugar.
Imperfective; perfective смешать (smeshát). It takes с + instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
смешивать (sméshivat) is built on с- and мешать "to stir, to mix", a cousin of месить "to knead", which EtymDB traces to PIE meyḱ- "to mix".

Lesson 2 of 5.
взбивать (vzbivát) — to whisk, to beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to cover, then the Russian for to mix.

You'll want to know: взбивать.
взбивать (vzbivát) — "to whisk, to beat".
Папа взбивает яйца. Pápa vzbiváyet yáytsa. Dad is beating the eggs.
Imperfective; the perfective is взбить (vzbit), взобью. Also взбить подушку — to plump up a pillow.

The word, taken apart.
взбивать (vzbivát) is built on вз- "up" and бить "to beat", inherited from Proto-Slavic biti, PIE bʰeyH- (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
венчик (vénchik) — a whisk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to mix, then the Russian for to whisk.

You'll want to know: венчик.
венчик (vénchik) — "a whisk".
Венчик лежит в ящике. Vénchik lezhít v yáshchike. The whisk is in the drawer.
Diminutive of венец "a crown, a wreath"; also a halo or a corolla.

The word, taken apart.
венчик (vénchik) is a diminutive of венец "a crown, a wreath" (EtymDB lists венец with no parent): a whisk is a little ring of wire.
