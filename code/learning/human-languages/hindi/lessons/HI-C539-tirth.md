## You'll want to know: तीर्थ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C539-DUA539-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तीर्थ** — *tīrth* — "a place of pilgrimage".

> दादी हर साल तीर्थ पर जाती हैं।
>
> *Dādī har sāl tīrth par jātī haiṁ.*
>
> Every year my grandmother goes on a pilgrimage.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तीर्थ** (*tīrth*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *tīrtha*, "a ford; a place of pilgrimage" (Monier-Williams): a holy place where one crosses over.
