## You'll want to know: el entierro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C556-ENTIERRO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el entierro** — "the burial, the funeral".

> Fuimos al entierro de su abuelo.
>
> *We went to his grandfather's funeral.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entierro* is built on *enterrar* 'to bury', which comes from spoken Latin *\*interrāre* 'to put in the earth' (EtymDB), from *terra* 'earth'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
