## You'll want to know: il detersivo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C532-PULIZIE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il detersivo** — "detergent, washing-up liquid".

> È finito il detersivo per i piatti.
>
> *We have run out of washing-up liquid.*

*Il detersivo per la lavatrice* — washing powder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*detersivo* is a learned word built on Latin *dētersus*, from *dētergēre* "to wipe off" (Lewis & Short).
