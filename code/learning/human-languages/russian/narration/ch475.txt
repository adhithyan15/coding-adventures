Russian, chapter 475: Delight and Pride.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
восторг (vostórg) — delight, rapture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for worry, then the Russian for to calm down.

You'll want to know: восторг.
восторг (vostórg) — "delight, rapture".
Дети были в восторге от цирка. Déti býli v vostórge ot tsírka. The children were delighted with the circus.
Masculine. Быть в восторге от + genitive — to be thrilled with.

The word, taken apart.
восторг (vostórg) is built on вос- 'up' and a root usually linked to the one in исторгнуть 'to tear out', so rapture is being carried away; EtymDB has no entry, so the link is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
восхищаться (voskhishchátsya) — to admire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to calm down, then the Russian for delight.

You'll want to know: восхищаться.
восхищаться (voskhishchátsya) — "to admire".
Я восхищаюсь её терпением. Ya voskhishcháyus yeyó terpéniyem. I admire her patience.
Imperfective; perfective восхититься (восхищусь). Takes the instrumental: восхищаться (voskhishchátsya) кем, чем.

The word, taken apart.
восхищаться (voskhishchátsya) is built on восхитить, which was borrowed from Church Slavonic vъsxytiti 'to snatch away, to fascinate' (EtymDB): admiration carries you away.

Lesson 3 of 5.
гордиться (gordítsya) — to be proud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for delight, then the Russian for to admire.

You'll want to know: гордиться.
гордиться (gordítsya) — "to be proud".
Отец гордится своим сыном. Otéts gordítsya svoím sýnom. The father is proud of his son.
Imperfective; present горжусь, гордишься (д becomes ж). Takes the instrumental: гордиться (gordítsya) сыном.

The word, taken apart.
гордиться (gordítsya) is built on гордый 'proud', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic gъrdъ 'proud' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
хвастаться (khvástatsya) — to boast, to show off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be proud, then the Russian for pride.

You'll want to know: хвастаться.
хвастаться (khvástatsya) — "to boast, to show off".
Он любит хвастаться своей машиной. On lyúbit khvástatsya svoyéy mashínoy. He likes to show off his car.
Imperfective; the perfective is похвастаться. Takes the instrumental: хвастаться (khvástatsya) машиной.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of хвастаться (khvástatsya) is not settled: EtymDB lists the word without a source, and it is often compared with хвалить 'to praise'.
