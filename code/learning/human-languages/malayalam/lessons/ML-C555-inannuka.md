# ഇണങ്ങുക (iṇaṅṅuka) — to be reconciled; to make up

## You'll want to know: ഇണങ്ങുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C555-TALAMURA555-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇണങ്ങുക** — *iṇaṅṅuka* — "to be reconciled; to make up".

> വഴക്കിന് ശേഷം കുട്ടികൾ വേഗം ഇണങ്ങി.
>
> *vaḻakkinŭ śēṣaṁ kuṭṭikaḷ vēgaṁ iṇaṅṅi.*
>
> After the quarrel the children soon made up.

Past: ഇണങ്ങി, *iṇaṅṅi*.

The opposite of പിണങ്ങുക, to sulk. It also means to get used to a place or a person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇണങ്ങുക** (*iṇaṅṅuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 457 lists it with ഇണ, "a pair, a mate", and verbs for suiting and uniting (DEDR 457).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
