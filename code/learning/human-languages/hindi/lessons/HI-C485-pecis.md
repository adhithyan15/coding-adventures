## You'll want to know: पेचिश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C485-CHAKKAR485-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पेचिश** — *peciś* — "dysentery".

> गंदा पानी पीकर अरुण को पेचिश हो गई।
>
> *gandā pānī pīkar Aruṇ ko peciś ho gaī.*
>
> Arun got dysentery from drinking dirty water.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेचिश** (*peciś*) is usually traced to Persian *pēchish*, "twisting, gripes", from *pēch*, "a twist": the illness is named for the cramps in the belly.
