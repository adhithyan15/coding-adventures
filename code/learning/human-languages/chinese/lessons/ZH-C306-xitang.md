## You'll want to know: xǐtáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C306-WEDDING306-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐtáng* — "wedding sweets".

> *wǒmen chī le tāmen de xǐtáng* — We had sweets at their wedding.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐtáng* is built from 喜 'joy' and *táng* 'sugar', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xiX* and *dang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a sweet syrup'.
