## You'll want to know: attenuare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C355-RIMEDIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**attenuare** — "to soften, to lessen, to ease".

> Questa crema attenua il dolore.
>
> *This cream eases the pain.*

For pain, light, noise or blame: *attenuare le colpe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*attenuare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *attenuāre* "to make thin, to weaken" (EtymDB), built from *ad* and *tenuis* "thin".
