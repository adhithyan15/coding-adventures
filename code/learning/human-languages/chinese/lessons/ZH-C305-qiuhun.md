## You'll want to know: qiúhūn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C305-ENGAGE305-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qiúhūn* — "to propose marriage".

> *tā zuótiān wǎnshang qiúhūn le* — He proposed last night.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiúhūn* is built from *qiú* 'to seek' and *hūn* 'marriage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjuw* and *xwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to search for' and the second 'marriage'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
