# ئ (hamze) — the catch inside مسئول

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C291-EYN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ی and ع once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **مسئول** (*mas'ul*), "the person in charge". You met it a
moment ago. This lesson takes its catch out and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ئ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C295-HAMZE-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C295-MASUL-01] -->

**ئ** — *hamze*, a glottal catch, written *'* in this book's romanization.

It is the third letter of **مسئول**, between **س** and **و**. It is two
pieces. Below sits a small tooth like the tooth of **ی** in the middle of a
word, with no dots: that is the seat. On top sits a little mark like a small
hook, the *hamze* itself. The seat takes the hand of **س** on its right and
joins on to **و**.

In Persian the *hamze* and **ع** sound alike, a light stop in the throat, so
*mas'ul* and *ta'rif* share their catch. Spelling keeps them apart, the way it
keeps four letters for *z*. Most words with *hamze* on this seat came into Persian
from Arabic.

## Writing: ئ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C295-HAMZE-01] -->

Put your pen on ئ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
