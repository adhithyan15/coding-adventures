## You'll want to know: nātūra, nātūrae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C503-INDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**nātūra, nātūrae** — "nature, character".

> Nātūra frātris meī placida est.
>
> *My brother's nature is calm.*

Feminine, first declension, from *nāscor* — to be born: what you are born with. English *nature*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**nātūra** is built on *nātus*, the participle of *nāscor* "to be born" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *nature* comes from it.
