## You'll want to know: गति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C405-GATI405-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गति** — *gati* — "speed (formal); motion".

> इस सड़क पर गति सीमा चालीस है।
>
> *is saṛak par gati sīmā cālīs hai.*
>
> The speed limit on this road is forty.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गति** (*gati*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *gati*, "going, moving, motion" (Monier-Williams), kept unchanged; road signs and the news use it: गति सीमा, the speed limit.
