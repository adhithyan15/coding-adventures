## You'll want to know: कथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C303-KATHA303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कथा** — *kathā* — "a short story; a story".

> आजी रोज रात्री मुलांना एक कथा सांगते.
>
> *ājī roj rātrī mulānnā ek kathā sāṅgte.*
>
> Every night Grandma tells the children a story.

Feminine.

Plural कथा. गोष्ट is the everyday word; कथा is the written, literary one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कथा** (*kathā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kathā*, 'a talk, a tale' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
