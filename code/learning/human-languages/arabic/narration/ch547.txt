Arabic, chapter 547: Winning and prizes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فوز (fawz) — a triumph; a win.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to pass the ball, then the Arabic for to compete.

You'll want to know: فوز.
فوز — fawz — "a triumph; a win".
احتفل اللاعبون بالفوز. iḥtafala al-lāʿibūn bi-l-fawz. The players celebrated the win.

The word, taken apart.
فوز (fawz) is built on the native Arabic root f-w-z, "to win, to escape safely" (Lane). Fāza, to win, which you know, is from the same root.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بطولة (buṭūla) — a championship; heroism.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to compete, then the Arabic for a triumph.

You'll want to know: بطولة.
بطولة — buṭūla — "a championship; heroism".
فاز فريق المدرسة بالبطولة. fāza farīq al-madrasa bi-l-buṭūla. The school team won the championship.

The word, taken apart.
بطولة (buṭūla) is built on the native Arabic root b-ṭ-l, "to be void, to be idle; to be brave" (Lane). Lane gives the root both senses: baṭāla is idleness, and baṭal a brave man.

رفع الفريق الكأس بعد المباراة. rafaʿa al-farīq al-kaʾs baʿda al-mubārā. The team lifted the trophy after the match.
It is also a drinking glass.

The word, taken apart.
كأس (kaʾs) is built on the native Arabic root k-ʾ-s, "a cup, a drinking vessel" (Lane). Lane gives kaʾs as a cup with drink in it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ميدالية (mīdāliya) — a medal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ميدالية.
ميدالية — mīdāliya — "a medal".
فاز أخي بميدالية ذهبية. fāza akhī bi-mīdāliya dhahabiyya. My brother won a gold medal.

The word, taken apart.
ميدالية (mīdāliya) is a modern loan, usually traced to Italian medaglia or French médaille, which go back to Latin metallum, "metal".

Lesson 5 of 5.
تأهل (taʾahhala) — to qualify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a trophy, then the Arabic for a medal.

You'll want to know: تأهل.
تأهل — taʾahhala — "to qualify".
تأهل فريقنا إلى البطولة. taʾahhala farīqunā ilā al-buṭūla. Our team qualified for the championship.
Present: يتأهل, yataʾahhal.
It takes إلى or لـ.

The word, taken apart.
تأهل (taʾahhala) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-h-l, "a family; to be fit for" (Lane). Form V taʾahhala is to become fit for a thing.
