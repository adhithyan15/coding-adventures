## You'll want to know: ようじんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C350-YOUJIN350-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ようじんする** — *yōjin suru* — "to take precautions; to be on guard".

> **ばんに でかけたら、ようじんして ください。** — *ban ni dekaketara, yōjin shite kudasai* — "If you go out in the evening, be on your guard."

Polite **ようじんします**, *yōjinshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ちちは ようじんしました。**, *chichi wa yōjin shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ようじんする** (*yōjin suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *yōjin*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *yō*, "utilize" (Middle Chinese *yowngH*) and *jin*, "heart" (*sim*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
