## You'll want to know: आख्यानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C332-AKHYANA332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आख्यानम्** — *ākhyānam* — "a legend; a tale".

> पितामही प्राचीनम् आख्यानं कथयति।
>
> *pitāmahī prācīnam ākhyānaṃ kathayati.*
>
> Grandmother tells an old legend.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आख्यानम्** (*ākhyānam*) is built from *ā* and the root √khyā, "to tell, make known"; MW gives *ākhyāna* as telling, communication, and a tale, story, legend (MW).
