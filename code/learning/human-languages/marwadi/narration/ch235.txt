Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 235: The Cattle Yard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गुवाड़ो (guvāṛo) — a cattle yard; a cowshed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to drive (animals or a cart), then the Marwadi for to look after.

You'll want to know: गुवाड़ो.
गुवाड़ो — guvāṛo — "a cattle yard; a cowshed".
गुवाड़ो घर कनै है। guvāṛo ghar kanai hai. The cattle yard is near the house.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गुवाड़ो (guvāṛo) is inherited from Sanskrit govāṭa, "a fold for cattle", built from go, "cow", and vāṭa, "an enclosure"; CDIAL lists Marwari guvāṛo, "a cowshed" (CDIAL 4327).

Lesson 2 of 5.
चारो (chāro) — fodder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to look after, then the Marwadi for a cattle yard.

You'll want to know: चारो.
चारो — chāro — "fodder".
भैंस चारो खावै है। bhaĩs chāro khāvai hai. The buffalo eats fodder.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चारो (chāro) is inherited from Sanskrit cāra, "motion", which CDIAL also takes in the sense of grazing and fodder; CDIAL lists a Gujarati word for fodder under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4755).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पाळणो (pāḷṇo) — to rear (animals); to keep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for fodder, then the Marwadi for cow dung.

You'll want to know: पाळणो.
पाळणो — pāḷṇo — "to rear (animals); to keep".
हूं बकरी पाळूं हूं। hū̃ bakrī pāḷū̃ hū̃. I keep goats.
Past (I …): म्हैं बकरी पाळी, mhaĩ bakrī pāḷī.

The word, taken apart.
पाळणो (pāḷṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit pālayati, "guards"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb pālaï, "to rear, to keep" (CDIAL 8129).
