Bengali, chapter 279: Fire!.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
জ্বলা (jvôlā) — to burn; to be alight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to overturn, then the Bengali for injured.

You'll want to know: জ্বলা.
জ্বলা — jvôlā — "to burn; to be alight".
যদি রাতে বাড়িতে আলো জ্বলে, তাহলে বুঝব যে কেউ আছে। jôdi rāte bāṛite ālo jvôle, tāhôle bujhbô je keu āchhe. If there's a light on in the house at night, I'll know someone is there.
Past (he/she …): সেই বাতি জ্বলল, sei bāti jvôllô.

The word, taken apart.
জ্বলা (jvôlā) comes down from Sanskrit jvalati, 'burns brightly' (CDIAL 5306, which lists Bengali jalā, 'to burn'). It is the partner of জ্বালানো: you light a lamp, and it burns.

শিখা (shikhā) is Sanskrit śikhā, a tuft of hair or a crest (Monier-Williams); the pointed top of a fire is its crest, its flame.

স্ফুলিঙ্গ (sphulinggô) — a spark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to be alight, then the Bengali for a flame.

You'll want to know: স্ফুলিঙ্গ.
স্ফুলিঙ্গ — sphulinggô — "a spark".
একটা ছোট স্ফুলিঙ্গ থেকে বাজারে আগুন লাগল। ekṭā chhoṭo sphulinggô theke bājāre āgun lāglô. The market caught fire from one small spark.

The word, taken apart.
স্ফুলিঙ্গ (sphulinggô) is Sanskrit sphuliṅga, 'a spark of fire' (Monier-Williams), taken over whole. It is a written word; in talk a spark is often ফুলকি.

Lesson 4 of 5.
পুড়ে যাওয়া (puṛe jāoyā) — to burn down; to get burnt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a flame, then the Bengali for a spark.

You'll want to know: পুড়ে যাওয়া.
পুড়ে যাওয়া — puṛe jāoyā — "to burn down; to get burnt".
আগুনে দোকানটা পুড়ে গেল। āgune dokānṭā puṛe gelô. The shop burned down in the fire.
Past (he/she …): সেই দোকান পুড়ে গেল, sei dokān puṛe gelô.

The word, taken apart.
পুড়ে যাওয়া (puṛe jāoyā) is the -e form of পোড়া, to be burnt — CDIAL 8397 lists Bengali poṛā and puṛā, 'to burn, be burnt' — before যাওয়া, which finishes the burning off. পোড়ানো is to burn something.

Lesson 5 of 5.
বিস্ফোরণ (bisphorôn) — an explosion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a spark, then the Bengali for to burn down.

You'll want to know: বিস্ফোরণ.
বিস্ফোরণ — bisphorôn — "an explosion".
রাতে কারখানায় একটা বিস্ফোরণ হল। rāte kārkhānāy ekṭā bisphorôn hôlô. There was an explosion at the factory last night.

The word, taken apart.
বিস্ফোরণ (bisphorôn) is a learned word built from Sanskrit vi-, 'apart', and sphoraṇa, 'throbbing, quivering' (Monier-Williams); Sanskrit itself has the near word visphoṭa, 'a crash'. বিস্ফোরণ হওয়া, an explosion happens.
