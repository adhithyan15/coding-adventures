French, chapter 524: En version originale.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la version originale — original-language version.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for (streaming) platform, then the French for thrilling.

You'll want to know: la version originale.
la version originale — "original-language version".
Je préfère voir les films en version originale. I prefer to watch films in the original language.
Often la VO; la VOST has subtitles; la VF is the French dub.

The word, taken apart.
la version originale joins version, from Medieval Latin versiō "a turning", from vertere "to turn", and originale, from Latin orīgō "beginning", from orīrī "to rise" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
projeter — to screen (a film); to plan; to project.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for thrilling, then the French for original-language version.

You'll want to know: projeter.
projeter — "to screen (a film); to plan; to project".
Le cinéma du quartier projette des films en version originale. The local cinema screens films in the original language.
Spelling: je projette, nous projetons. Also to plan: projeter un voyage.

The word, taken apart.
projeter comes from Old French porjeter "to throw forward", built on jeter "to throw", which goes back to Latin iactāre, from iacere "to throw" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: le doublage.
le doublage — "dubbing".
Le doublage de ce dessin animé est très réussi. The dubbing of this cartoon is very good.
Built on doubler "to dub".

The word, taken apart.
le doublage is built on doubler with -age; doubler comes from double, from Latin duplus "twofold", built on duo "two" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: le réalisateur.
le réalisateur — "(film) director".
Le réalisateur a présenté son film au public. The director presented his film to the audience.
A woman is la réalisatrice. In a theatre, le metteur en scène.

The word, taken apart.
le réalisateur is built on réaliser "to make real", from réel, from Late Latin reālis, built on rēs "a thing" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
le tournage — shooting, filming (of a film).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for dubbing, then the French for (film) director.

You'll want to know: le tournage.
le tournage — "shooting, filming (of a film)".
Le tournage a duré trois mois. The filming lasted three months.
Tourner un film — to shoot a film.

The word, taken apart.
le tournage is built on tourner with -age (EtymDB); tourner comes from Latin tornāre "to turn on a lathe" (Lewis & Short), and early cameras were cranked by hand.
