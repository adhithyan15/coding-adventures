## You'll want to know: صلح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C311-FATEH311-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صلح** — *sulah* — "a peace settlement; a reconciliation".

> آخر دونوں بادشاہوں میں صلح ہوئی۔
>
> *ākhir donoṅ bādshāhoṅ meṅ sulah huī.*
>
> In the end the two kings made peace.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣulḥ*, "peace, a settlement". **امن** is peace as a state; **صلح** is the peace two sides make.
