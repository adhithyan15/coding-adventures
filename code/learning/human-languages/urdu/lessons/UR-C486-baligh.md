## You'll want to know: بالغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C486-GROWUP486-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بالغ** — *bāligh* — "of age; adult".

> اٹھارہ سال کی عمر میں انسان بالغ ہوتا ہے۔
>
> *aṭhārah sāl kī umr meṅ insān bāligh hotā hai.*
>
> A person comes of age at eighteen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بالغ** (*bāligh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bāligh*, "reaching maturity".
