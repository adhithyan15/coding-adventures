## You'll want to know: tiáojiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C395-RESULT395-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tiáojiàn* — "condition (that must be met)".

> *wǒ kěyǐ qù kěshì yǒu yí ge tiáojiàn* — I can go, but there's one condition.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tiáojiàn* is built from *tiáo* 'a twig' and *jiàn* 'a portion', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dew* and *gjenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a small branch' and the second 'to divide into items'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
