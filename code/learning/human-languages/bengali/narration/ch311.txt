Bengali, chapter 311: At the Village Fair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নাগরদোলা (nāgôrdolā) — a Ferris wheel; a big wheel at a fair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a goddess, then the Bengali for holy.

You'll want to know: নাগরদোলা.
নাগরদোলা — nāgôrdolā — "a Ferris wheel; a big wheel at a fair".
মেলায় শিশুরা নাগরদোলায় চড়ল। melāy shishurā nāgôrdolāy chôṛlô. At the fair the children rode the big wheel.

The word, taken apart.
নাগরদোলা (nāgôrdolā) is usually explained as নাগর, 'of the town' — Sanskrit nāgara, 'town-born' (Monier-Williams) — with দোলা, a swing, the word behind দোলানো (CDIAL 6453): the great swing of the fair.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ঘোরা (ghorā) — to go round; to wander about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a puppet show, then the Bengali for a balloon.

You'll want to know: ঘোরা.
ঘোরা — ghorā — "to go round; to wander about".
আমরা বিকেলে মেলায় অনেক ঘুরলাম। āmrā bikele melāy ônek ghurlām. We spent the afternoon wandering round the fair.
Past (he/she …): সেই চাকা ঘুরল, sei chākā ghurlô.
The stem vowel rises in the past: ঘুরল, turned; ঘুরলাম, we wandered.

The word, taken apart.
ঘোরা (ghorā) is filed in CDIAL 4488 under a Middle Indo-Aryan ghurati, 'revolves', which lists Bengali ghurā, 'to revolve'. A wheel ঘোরে; so does a visitor at a fair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a balloon, then the Bengali for to go round.

You'll want to know: ঠাসা.
ঠাসা — ṭhāsā — "packed; crammed".
মেলার দিন বাসগুলো লোকে ঠাসা ছিল। melār din bāsgulo loke ṭhāsā chhilô. On the day of the fair the buses were packed with people.

The word, taken apart.
ঠাসা (ṭhāsā) is filed in CDIAL 5499 under a Middle Indo-Aryan ṭhass-, 'press down', which lists Bengali thāsā, 'to knead, cram'; ঠাসা is the crammed result.
