## You'll want to know: えいが
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-01]; assesses=[] -->

**えいが** — *eiga* — "a film; a movie".

> **せんしゅう かぞくと えいがに いきました。** — *senshū kazoku to eiga ni ikimashita* — "Last week I went to a film with my family."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えいが** (*eiga*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ei*, "reflect, project" (Middle Chinese *'jaengH*) and *ga*, "picture" (*hweaH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
