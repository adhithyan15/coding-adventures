## You'll want to know: 点心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C120-TAKEAWAY120-05]; assesses=[] -->

**点心** — *diǎnxin* — "dim sum; light snacks and pastries".

> **我们去外边吃点心** *wǒmen qù wàibian chī diǎnxin* — We're going out for dim sum.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**点心** (*diǎnxin*) is built from 点 'a dot, a little' and 心 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *temX* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a dot made with the brush. How the pair came to mean a light snack is not settled.
