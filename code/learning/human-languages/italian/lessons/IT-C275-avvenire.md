## You'll want to know: avvenire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C275-ACCADERE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**avvenire** — "to take place, to come about".

> Il fatto è avvenuto di notte.
>
> *The incident took place at night.*

Conjugated like *venire*: *è avvenuto*. As a noun, *l'avvenire* is the future.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avvenire* is inherited from Latin *advenīre* "to come to, to arrive" (EtymDB), from *ad* and *venīre* "to come".
