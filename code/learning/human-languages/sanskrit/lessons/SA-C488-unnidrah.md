## You'll want to know: उन्निद्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C488-PRATIBODHA488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्निद्रः** — *unnidraḥ* — "sleepless".

> उद्वेगेन पिता रात्रौ उन्निद्रः भवति।
>
> *udvegena pitā rātrau unnidraḥ bhavati.*
>
> Father lies sleepless at night with worry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्निद्रः** (*unnidraḥ*) is built from *ud*, "up, out", and **निद्रा**, "sleep"; MW gives "sleepless, awake" (MW).
