## You'll want to know: सारथिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C325-GADA325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सारथिः** — *sārathiḥ* — "a charioteer".

> सारथिः रथं वेगेन चालयति।
>
> *sārathiḥ rathaṃ vegena cālayati.*
>
> The charioteer drives the chariot fast.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सारथिः** (*sārathiḥ*) is built on *sa-ratha*, "with a chariot", from *ratha*, "a chariot"; MW gives *sārathi* as a charioteer, a driver (MW).
