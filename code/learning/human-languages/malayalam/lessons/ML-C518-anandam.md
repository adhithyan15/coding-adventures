## You'll want to know: ആനന്ദം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C518-BHAYAM518-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ആനന്ദം** — *ānandaṁ* — "bliss; great joy".

> കുഞ്ഞിനെ കണ്ടപ്പോൾ മുത്തച്ഛന്റെ മുഖത്ത് ആനന്ദം നിറഞ്ഞു.
>
> *kuññine kaṇṭappōḷ muttacchanṟe mukhattŭ ānandaṁ niṟaññu.*
>
> When he saw the baby, joy filled grandfather's face.

Stronger than സന്തോഷം; a word of songs and of writing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആനന്ദം** (*ānandaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ānanda*, "happiness, bliss" (CDIAL 1172).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
