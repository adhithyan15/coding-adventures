## You'll want to know: ખડક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C439-HILL439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ખડક** — *khaḍak* — "a crag; a big rock".

> દરિયાના કિનારે મોટા ખડક છે.
>
> *dariyānā kināre moṭā khaḍak che.*
>
> There are big rocks on the sea shore.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખડક** (*khaḍak*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
