## You'll want to know: विवेकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C351-VIVEKA351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विवेकः** — *vivekaḥ* — "discernment; good judgement".

> विवेकः सत्यं मिथ्या च दर्शयति।
>
> *vivekaḥ satyaṃ mithyā ca darśayati.*
>
> Discernment shows what is true and what is false.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विवेकः** (*vivekaḥ*) is built on *vi* and the root √vic, "to sift, to separate"; MW gives *vi-veka* "discrimination, distinction" and "right judgement" (MW).
