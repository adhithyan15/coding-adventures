## You'll want to know: étóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C472-FACE472-05]; assesses=[] -->

*étóu* — "forehead".

> *wǒ de étóu hěn rè kěnéng fāshāo le* — My forehead's hot. I might have a temperature.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*étóu* is built from *é* 'the forehead' and 头 'head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngaek* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as the brow.
