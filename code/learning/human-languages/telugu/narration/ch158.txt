Telugu, chapter 158: To hurry, To brush, To change, To take off, To measure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తొందరపడు (tondarapaḍu) — to hurry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to mix, then the Telugu for to grind.

You'll want to know: తొందరపడు.
తొందరపడు — tondarapaḍu — "to hurry".

The word, taken apart.
తొందరపడు (tondarapaḍu) is native Dravidian: tondara, "haste, trouble", one word with Kannada tondare, "trouble" (DEDR 3506), and paḍu, "to feel, undergo" (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 5 of 5.
కొలుచు (kolucu) — to measure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to change, then the Telugu for to take off.

You'll want to know: కొలుచు.
కొలుచు — kolucu — "to measure".

The word, taken apart.
కొలుచు (kolucu) is Dravidian. DEDR 1827 lists kolucu, "to measure", with kola, "measurement", but doubts whether this group belongs with Tamil kuḷakam, "a grain measure". DEDR compares Sanskrit kuḍava, "a grain measure" (CDIAL 3246), which CDIAL takes from Dravidian.
