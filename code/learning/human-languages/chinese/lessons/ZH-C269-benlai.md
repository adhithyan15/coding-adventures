## You'll want to know: 本来
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C269-YOUNG269-04]; assesses=[] -->

**本来** — *běnlái* — "originally; at first".

> **我本来想先吃饭** *wǒ běnlái xiǎng xiān chīfàn* — I'd meant to eat first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**本来** (*běnlái*) is built from 本 'the root' and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pwonX* and *loj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the root' and 'the beginning'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
