# പരിശോധന (pariśōdhana) — an inspection; a check

## You'll want to know: പരിശോധന
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C464-ATIRTTI464-04]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിശോധന** — *pariśōdhana* — "an inspection; a check".

> വിമാനത്താവളത്തിൽ സുരക്ഷാ പരിശോധന ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു.
>
> *vimānattāvaḷattil surakṣā pariśōdhana uṇṭāyirunnu.*
>
> There was a security check at the airport.

The noun of പരിശോധിക്കുക, to check. A medical check-up is also a പരിശോധന.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിശോധന** (*pariśōdhana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pariśodhana*, "cleaning, purification" (Monier-Williams). In Malayalam it means a close check or an inspection.
