5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
هدية (hadiyya) — a present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a letter, then the Arabic for an envelope.

You'll want to know: هدية.
هدية — hadiyya — "a present".

The word, taken apart.
هدية (hadiyya) is built on the native Arabic root h-d-y, "to guide; to send a gift" (Lane). Hadiyya is a gift sent.

You'll want to know: مظلة.
مظلة — miẓalla — "an umbrella".

The word, taken apart.
مظلة (miẓalla) is built on the native Arabic root ẓ-l-l, "shade, shadow" (Lane). Lane's miẓalla is a large tent or shelter; he also records a shade held over kings as they ride.
