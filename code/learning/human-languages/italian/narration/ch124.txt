Italian, chapter 124: Busy, Free, Open, Closed, Quiet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
occupato — busy; taken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for orange, then the Italian for pretty.

You'll want to know: occupato.
occupato — "busy; taken".

The word, taken apart.
occupato is the past participle of occupare, "to occupy", taken from Latin occupare, "to seize" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).
