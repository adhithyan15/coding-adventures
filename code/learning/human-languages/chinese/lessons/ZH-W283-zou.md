## Script — 土, then four strokes along the foot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZOU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 走

**Two pieces, and you write the first already.**

**土, three strokes**, on top, as at the lower right of 在: a short horizontal, a vertical down through it, and a longer horizontal.

**The lower piece, four strokes**: a short vertical; a short horizontal to its right, the way the lower part of 正 begins; a left-falling stroke to the lower left; and last the long sweep along the foot to the right.

3 + 4 = seven.
