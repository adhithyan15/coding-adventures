## You'll want to know: a madrinha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C310-BATIZADO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a madrinha** — "the godmother".

> A madrinha deu-lhe um presente.
>
> *Her godmother gave her a present.*

*O afilhado*, *a afilhada* — the godchild.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*madrinha* comes down from Old Portuguese *madrỹa* (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *matrīna*, built on Latin *māter* "mother".
