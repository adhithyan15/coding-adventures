## You'll want to know: ತಗ್ಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C488-BISIGALI488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಗ್ಗು** — *taggu* — "to subside; to go down".

> ಸಂಜೆ ಮಳೆ ಬಂದ ಮೇಲೆ ಸೆಕೆ ತಗ್ಗಿತು.
>
> *sañje maḷe banda mēle seke taggitu.*
>
> After it rained in the evening the heat died down.

Past (it …): ತಗ್ಗಿತು, *taggitu*.

As a noun, a low spot or a hollow: ರಸ್ತೆಯ ತಗ್ಗು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಗ್ಗು** (*taggu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3178 lists Kannada *targu*, "to become depressed, low or less, to decline".
