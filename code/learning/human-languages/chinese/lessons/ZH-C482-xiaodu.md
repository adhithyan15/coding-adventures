## You'll want to know: xiāodú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C482-TREAT482-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xiāodú* — "to disinfect".

> *hùshi xiān gěi shāngkǒu xiāodú* — The nurse disinfects the wound first.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiāodú* is built from *xiāo* 'to wipe out' and *dú* 'poison', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjew* and *dowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to destroy' and 'to use up' and the second 'harm'.
