Lesson 1 of 5.
kaputt — broken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for helpful, then the German for intelligent.

You'll want to know: kaputt.
kaputt — "broken".

The word, taken apart.
kaputt from French capot, said of a card player who has won no trick (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed: the loser was done for. English kaput came from German.

Lesson 3 of 5.
kostenlos — free of charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for broken, then the German for clever.

You'll want to know: kostenlos.
kostenlos — "free of charge".

The word, taken apart.
kostenlos is the word for "costs", from kosten, + -los, "without", the same ending as English -less: without cost. EtymDB 2.1 lists English costless beside it.

You'll want to know: kräftig.
kräftig — "strong, hearty".

The word, taken apart.
kräftig is the word for "strength", Old High German kraft from Proto-Germanic kraftaz (EtymDB 2.1), + -ig. English craft, first "strength, skill", is the same word.
