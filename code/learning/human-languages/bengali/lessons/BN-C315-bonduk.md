## You'll want to know: বন্দুক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C315-OSTRO315-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বন্দুক** — *bônduk* — "a gun".

> পুলিশের হাতে বন্দুক ছিল।
>
> *pulisher hāte bônduk chhilô.*
>
> The police were carrying guns.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বন্দুক** (*bônduk*) is usually traced through Persian to Arabic *bunduq*, a hazelnut and then a pellet; the name passed from the shot to the gun.
