## You'll want to know: नामकरणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C493-SAMSKARA493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नामकरणम्** — *nāmakaraṇam* — "the naming ceremony".

> दशमे दिने शिशोः नामकरणं भवति।
>
> *daśame dine śiśoḥ nāmakaraṇaṃ bhavati.*
>
> On the tenth day the baby is given a name.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नामकरणम्** (*nāmakaraṇam*) is built from **नाम**, "a name", and *karaṇa*, "making", from the root √kṛ; MW gives "the ceremony of naming a child after birth" (MW).
