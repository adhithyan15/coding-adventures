## Added — the A2 core, chapters 152-165

- Added 61 hand-authored lessons in fourteen chapters (26 grammar, 12 word, 6
  letter, 2 reading and 15 review lessons), the track's first grammar lessons.
  They realize the four A2 spine nodes Marwadi lacked: negation and yes-or-no
  questions (157-158), the past (159-161), the future (162-163) and practical
  texts (164-165), and lay the present underneath them on SAY-WHAT-I-DO
  (152-156): **वो** "he; that", **हूं / है / हो** as *am / is / are*, the present
  habitual (**बोलूं हूं, बोलै है, बोलो हो**; **जाऊं हूं, जावै है**), **म्हारो /
  म्हारी** agreement, a noun + **मांय / माथै / कनै**, and **सूं** "from".
- Negation is **कोनी** before the verb or in place of **है**, and **मत** before a
  request; a yes-or-no question is the statement said rising, answered **हां सा
  / कोनी सा**. The past is **गयो / गई / गया**, **आयो / आई / आया**, **-्यो** on
  consonant stems, and **म्हैं** with a transitive past that agrees with the thing
  (**म्हैं रोटी खाई।**, **म्हैं दूध लियो।**). The future is the first-person
  **-सूं** (**हूं काल जासूं।**), its negative, and a dated plan.
- Six letters, each right after the word that first shows it: **ऊ** (ऊंट),
  **ध** (दूध), **झ** (समझणो), **ओ** (ओळखणो), **ढ** (काढणो), **फ** (फेर), copied
  from their sourced `devanagari.json` rows. The unread inventory falls from 11
  to 5 (ऋ ऐ औ ञ ष) and six filmstrips are added (43 -> 49).
- Two readings: a 51-word dairy notice (when to buy milk) and a 54-word
  neighbour's note about a repair visit (when to be at home).
- Only forms a CDIAL Marwari or Old Marwari row or the track itself prints are
  taught. Left out, for want of such a source: **वा, वै, थे, तूं, आपां, म्हे**,
  **उण, वां**, **हां** "we are", the past copula **हो / ही / हा**, **दियो**, **रो /
  री / रा** after a noun, **नै**, **में**, the oblique **-ा**, the **-सी / -सां /
  -सो** futures, and **कांई** at the head of a yes-or-no question.
- Book: quote blocks are set ragged right (HL-C88). No readiness, mock or
  attainment claim: the vocabulary still stands at 640 headwords against the
  A2 gate's 1,200.
