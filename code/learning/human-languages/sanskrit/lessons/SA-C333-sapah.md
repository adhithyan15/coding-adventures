## You'll want to know: शापः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C333-SHAPA333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शापः** — *śāpaḥ* — "a curse".

> ऋषेः शापेन राजा दुःखी भवति।
>
> *ṛṣeḥ śāpena rājā duḥkhī bhavati.*
>
> The seer's curse makes the king unhappy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शापः** (*śāpaḥ*) comes from the root √śap, "to curse", as in *śapati*; MW gives *śāpa* as a curse, malediction, oath (MW).
