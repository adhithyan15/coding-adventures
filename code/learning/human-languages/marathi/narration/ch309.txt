Marathi, chapter 309: Losses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गमावणे (gamāvṇe) — to lose forever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a lineage, then the Marathi for to preserve.

You'll want to know: गमावणे.
गमावणे — gamāvṇe — "to lose forever".
त्या पुरात अनेकांनी आपली घरं गमावली. tyā purāt anekānnī āplī gharaṁ gamāvlī. Many people lost their homes in that flood.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने गमावलं, tyāne gamāvlaṁ.
For what does not come back: a life, a chance, a home. हरवणे is for mislaying.

The word, taken apart.
गमावणे (gamāvṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi gamāvṇe, 'to lose', under Sanskrit gamyate, 'one goes' (CDIAL 4033): to let go away.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to preserve, then the Marathi for to lose forever.

You'll want to know: सोसणे.
सोसणे — sosṇe — "to endure; to bear".
त्या कुटुंबाने खूप दुःख सोसलं. tyā kuṭumbāne khūp duḥkh soslaṁ. That family endured a lot of sorrow.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सोसलं, tyāne soslaṁ.
Transitive: दुःख सोसणे (sosṇe), to bear grief; सहन करणे is the formal cousin.

The word, taken apart.
सोसणे (sosṇe) is a Marathi verb of bearing pain; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मरण पावणे (maraṇ pāvṇe) — to pass away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to endure, then the Marathi for death.

You'll want to know: मरण पावणे.
मरण पावणे — maraṇ pāvṇe — "to pass away".
ते महान लेखक गतवर्षी मरण पावले. te mahān lekhak gatvarṣī maraṇ pāvle. That great writer passed away last year.
Past (he …): तो मरण पावला, to maraṇ pāvlā.
Formal, for news: X मरण पावला. मरणे is the plain verb.

The word, taken apart.
मरण पावणे (maraṇ pāvṇe) is built from maraṇ, 'death', from Sanskrit maraṇa, 'dying' (CDIAL 9869), and pāvṇe, 'to reach, obtain': to reach death.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उद्भवणे (udbhavṇe) — to arise; to crop up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for death, then the Marathi for to pass away.

You'll want to know: उद्भवणे.
उद्भवणे — udbhavṇe — "to arise; to crop up".
पावसामुळे पाण्याचा नवा प्रश्न उद्भवला. pāvsāmuḷe pāṇyācā navā praśna udbhavlā. A new water problem arose because of the rain.
Past (he …): तो उद्भवला, to udbhavlā.
Intransitive and formal: प्रश्न उद्भवणे (udbhavṇe), a problem arises.

The word, taken apart.
उद्भवणे (udbhavṇe) is built on Sanskrit udbhava, 'arising, origin' (Monier-Williams), with the Marathi verb ending -ṇe.
