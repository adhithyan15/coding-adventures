## You'll want to know: le fleuriste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C449-BURALISTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le fleuriste** — "the florist".

> Le fleuriste a fait un joli bouquet pour ma mère.
>
> *The florist made a pretty bunch of flowers for my mother.*

Same form for a woman: *la fleuriste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fleuriste* is built on *fleur* 'flower' with *-iste*; *fleur* is inherited from Latin *flōs, flōris* (EtymDB).
