Telugu, chapter 487: Spending and Saving.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వెచ్చించు (vecciñcu) — to spend money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for value, then the Telugu for to weigh.

You'll want to know: వెచ్చించు.
వెచ్చించు — vecciñcu — "to spend money".
ఇల్లు కట్టడానికి చాలా డబ్బు వెచ్చించాం. illu kaṭṭaḍāniki cālā ḍabbu vecciñcāṁ. We spent a lot of money building the house.
Past (I …): వెచ్చించాను, vecciñcānu.
A little more formal than ఖర్చు చేయు. Time can be spent this way too: సమయం వెచ్చించు (vecciñcu).

The word, taken apart.
వెచ్చించు (vecciñcu) is a Telugu verb in -ఇంచు whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఆదా చేయు (ādā cēyu) — to save money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to weigh, then the Telugu for to spend money.

You'll want to know: ఆదా చేయు.
ఆదా చేయు — ādā cēyu — "to save money".
బస్సులో వెళ్ళి కిరాయి ఆదా చేశాం. bassulō veḷḷi kirāyi ādā cēśāṁ. We went by bus and saved on the fare.
Past (I …): ఆదా చేశాను, ādā cēśānu.
Said of money, time or fuel saved: సమయం ఆదా చేయు (ādā cēyu).

The word, taken apart.
ఆదా చేయు (ādā cēyu) is a Telugu phrase: ఆదా, "saving", whose origin is not settled, and the native చేయు, "to do" (DEDR 1957).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to spend money, then the Telugu for to save money.

You'll want to know: గిరాకీ.
గిరాకీ — girākī — "demand".
వేసవిలో మామిడి పండ్లకు గిరాకీ ఎక్కువ. vēsavilō māmiḍi paṇḍlaku girākī ekkuva. In summer there is high demand for mangoes.
Demand for goods in a market; also customers for a shop: గిరాకీ (girākī) లేదు, there are no buyers.

The word, taken apart.
గిరాకీ (girākī) is a market word for demand whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
లభించు (labhiñcu) — to be available; to be obtained.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to save money, then the Telugu for demand.

You'll want to know: లభించు.
లభించు — labhiñcu — "to be available; to be obtained".
ఆ పుస్తకం మా పట్టణంలో లభించదు. ā pustakaṁ mā paṭṭaṇaṁlō labhiñcadu. That book is not available in our town.
Past (it …): లభించింది, labhiñcindi.
The person who gets it takes -కి: నాకు ఉద్యోగం లభించింది, I got a job. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: లభించింది, it was got.

The word, taken apart.
లభించు (labhiñcu) is built from Sanskrit labh, "to take, to obtain" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; లాభం, a profit, is the same root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
అమ్ముడుపోవు (ammuḍupōvu) — to sell out; to be sold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for demand, then the Telugu for to be available.

You'll want to know: అమ్ముడుపోవు.
అమ్ముడుపోవు — ammuḍupōvu — "to sell out; to be sold".
పండుగ ముందు టిక్కెట్లన్నీ అమ్ముడుపోయాయి. paṇḍuga mundu ṭikkeṭlannī ammuḍupōyāyi. Before the festival all the tickets sold out.
Past (it …): అమ్ముడుపోయింది, ammuḍupōyindi.
అమ్ముడు, selling, + పోవు. Said of goods; said of a person, it means to be bought off. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: అమ్ముడుపోయింది, it sold out.

The word, taken apart.
అమ్ముడుపోవు (ammuḍupōvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 186 lists Telugu ammuḍu, "sale, selling", beside ammu, "to sell", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
