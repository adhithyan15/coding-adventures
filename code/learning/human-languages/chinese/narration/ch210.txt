Mandarin Chinese, chapter 210: Colours and Styles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
zǐsè — purple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to disturb, then the Mandarin for to permit.

You'll want to know: zǐsè.
zǐsè — "purple".
wǒ xǐhuan zǐsè — I like purple.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zǐsè is built from zǐ 'purple' and sè 'colour', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsjeX and srik (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a colour between the pure ones.

What you've built.
One more word for asking for what I want.

Lesson 3 of 5.
kuǎnshì — style; design.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for purple, then the Mandarin for brown.

You'll want to know: kuǎnshì.
kuǎnshì — "style; design".
zhège kuǎnshì hěn hǎokàn — This style looks nice.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kuǎnshì is built from kuǎn and shì 'a model', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khwanX and syik (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'sincere' and the second 'a rule'; how the pair came to mean a style is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
试衣间 (shìyījiān) — fitting room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for brown, then the Mandarin for style of clothes.

You'll want to know: 试衣间.
试衣间 — shìyījiān — "fitting room".
我在找试衣间 wǒ zài zhǎo shìyījiān — I'm looking for the fitting room.

The word, taken apart.
试衣间 (shìyījiān) is built from 试 'to try', 衣 'clothes' and 间 'a room'. The first two are inherited from Middle Chinese, syiH and 'j+j (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
