Bengali, chapter 302: Telling It Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
কাহিনি (kāhini) — a tale; a narrative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for detailed, then the Bengali for to verify.

You'll want to know: কাহিনি.
কাহিনি — kāhini — "a tale; a narrative".
দাদু রাতে আমাদের একটা পুরনো কাহিনি বললেন। dādu rāte āmāder ekṭā purôno kāhini bôllen. At night my grandfather told us an old tale.

The word, taken apart.
কাহিনি (kāhini) comes down from Sanskrit kathana, 'narration' (CDIAL 2702, which lists Bengali kahinī, 'a story'); it is a cousin of কথা, talk.

Lesson 2 of 5.
শোনানো (shonāno) — to tell a story to; to recite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to verify, then the Bengali for a tale.

You'll want to know: শোনানো.
শোনানো — shonāno — "to tell a story to; to recite".
দিদিমা আমাদের রাজা আর রানির গল্প শোনালেন। didimā āmāder rājā ār rānir gôlpo shonālen. Grandmother told us the story of a king and a queen.
Past (he/she …): তিনি একটা গান শোনালেন, tini ekṭā gān shonālen.

The word, taken apart.
শোনানো (shonāno) is শোনা, to hear — CDIAL 12598, Sanskrit śṛṇōti, 'hears' — with the -ānō ending that makes someone else hear: to tell a story to someone.

Lesson 3 of 5.
বর্ণনা করা (bôrnônā kôrā) — to describe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a tale, then the Bengali for to tell a story.

You'll want to know: বর্ণনা করা.
বর্ণনা করা — bôrnônā kôrā — "to describe".
সাক্ষী চোরের চেহারা বর্ণনা করল। sākkhī chorer chehārā bôrnônā kôrlô. The witness described what the thief looked like.
Past (he/she …): তিনি জায়গাটা বর্ণনা করলেন, tini jāygāṭā bôrnônā kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
বর্ণনা করা (bôrnônā kôrā) puts Sanskrit varṇanā, which Monier-Williams gives as 'praise, commendation', before করা; it is built on varṇa, 'colour', and to describe is to give a thing its colours.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ঘটানো.
ঘটানো — ghôṭāno — "to bring about; to cause".
একটা ছোট ভুল বড় দুর্ঘটনা ঘটাল। ekṭā chhoṭo bhul bôṛo durghôṭônā ghôṭālô. One small mistake caused a big accident.
Past (he/she …): সেই ভুল দুর্ঘটনা ঘটাল, sei bhul durghôṭônā ghôṭālô.

The word, taken apart.
ঘটানো (ghôṭāno) is ঘটা, to happen — Sanskrit ghaṭatē, 'is busy with; happens' (CDIAL 4407) — with the -ānō ending: to make something happen.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ক্রমশ (krômôsh) — gradually; little by little.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to describe, then the Bengali for to bring about.

You'll want to know: ক্রমশ.
ক্রমশ — krômôsh — "gradually; little by little".
ঝড়টা ক্রমশ দুর্বল হল। jhôṛṭā krômôsh durbôl hôlô. The storm gradually grew weaker.

The word, taken apart.
ক্রমশ (krômôsh) is Sanskrit kramaśas, 'gradually, by degrees' (Monier-Williams), built on krama, 'a step', the word in ভাগ্যক্রমে.
