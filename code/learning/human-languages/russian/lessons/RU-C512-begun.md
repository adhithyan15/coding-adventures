## You'll want to know: бегун
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C512-RACE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**бегун** (*begún*) — "a runner".

> Бегуны ждут сигнала на старте.
>
> *Beguný zhdut signála na stárte.*
>
> The runners are waiting for the signal at the start.

Masculine; genitive бегуна.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
