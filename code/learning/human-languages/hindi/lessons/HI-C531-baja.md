## You'll want to know: बाजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C531-DHOLAK531-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बाजा** — *bājā* — "a musical instrument; a band".

> मेले में बाजा बज रहा था।
>
> *Mele meṁ bājā baj rahā thā.*
>
> A band was playing at the fair.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाजा** (*bājā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *vādya*, "to be played; music" (CDIAL 11511).
