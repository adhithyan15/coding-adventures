## You'll want to know: shǎndiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C514-WEATHER514-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shǎndiàn* — "lightning".

> *wǒ zuì pà dǎléi hé shǎndiàn* — Thunder and lightning are what I'm most afraid of.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shǎndiàn* is built from *shǎn* and *diàn* 'lightning', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syemX* and *denH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the look of something coming out of a door, and explains the second as the flash when yin and yang clash; the sense 'to flash' is not given for the first.
