Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 255: Better or Worse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तबियत (tabiyat) — one's health; how one feels.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for rest, then the Marwadi for sluggishness.

You'll want to know: तबियत.
तबियत — tabiyat — "one's health; how one feels".
आज म्हारी तबियत ठीक है। āj mhārī tabiyat ṭhīk hai. I am feeling well today.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
तबियत (tabiyat) is usually traced to Persian tabīat, "nature, temperament"; in Marwari it is how one feels in body.

You'll want to know: बिगड़णो.
बिगड़णो — bigaṛṇo — "to get worse; to go wrong".
काल म्हारी तबियत बिगड़ी। kāl mhārī tabiyat bigaṛī. Yesterday I felt worse.
Past (it …): वो दूध बिगड़्यो, vo dūdh bigaṛyo.

The word, taken apart.
बिगड़णो (bigaṛṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vighaṭate, "is marred". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi bigaṛnā, "to be spoilt" (CDIAL 11673).

सुधरणो — sudharṇo — "to get better; to improve".
आज तबियत सुधरी। āj tabiyat sudharī. Today I am feeling better.
Past (it …): वो मौसम सुधर्यो, vo mausam sudharyo.

सुधरणो (sudharṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed śuddhakāra, "making clean". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi sudharnā, "to be mended" (CDIAL 12521).
