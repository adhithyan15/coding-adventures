Gujarati, chapter 69: Things That Shine, Fly and Swim.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
તારો (tāro) — a star.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a monkey, then the Gujarati for a snake.

You'll want to know: તારો.
તારો (tāro) — "a star". Out at રાત.

What you've built.
One more word for things that shine, fly and swim.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tāro]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tāro, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say tāro]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a monkey, then the Gujarati for a snake, then say tāro again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does તારો (tāro) mean? ("A star".) Say it once more.
