## The pattern, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-JOIN-JO-OH-01]; assesses=[PA-PRON-JO-01, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-JOIN-JE-TAN-01] -->

> **ਜੋ** *(description)*, **ਉਹ** *(the one meant)*

This is the same shape as **ਜੇ … ਤਾਂ …**, and seeing that is most of the work.
Punjabi marks **both** ends: the clause that describes, and the thing described.
English collapses them — *the one who came is my friend* — and Punjabi keeps them
visibly paired.

Now the whole system is visible:

> **ਜ-** opens the clause · **ਉ-** / **ਤ-** points at what it refers to

**ਜੇ … ਤਾਂ**, **ਜੋ … ਉਹ**. One habit, and you have met it twice.
