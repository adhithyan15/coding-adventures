## You'll want to know: ярмарка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C316-KARNAVAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ярмарка** (*yármarka*) — "a fair".

> На ярмарке мы купили мёд.
>
> *Na yármarke my kupíli myod.*
>
> At the fair we bought honey.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ярмарка* was borrowed through Polish *jarmark* from German *Jahrmarkt* "a yearly market" (EtymDB), with *Jahr* "year".
