## You'll want to know: उचकी लागणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C492-UCAKI492-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उचकी लागणे** — *uckī lāgṇe* — "to have hiccups".

> घाईत जेवल्याने त्याला उचकी लागली.
>
> *ghāīt jevlyāne tyālā uckī lāglī.*
>
> Eating in a hurry gave him hiccups.

Past (to him …): त्याला उचकी लागली, *tyālā uckī lāglī*.

Dative: मला उचकी लागली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उचकी लागणे** (*uckī lāgṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on उचकी, "a hiccup", whose origin is not settled, with लागणे.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
