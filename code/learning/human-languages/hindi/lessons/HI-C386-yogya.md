## You'll want to know: योग्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C386-SAHARA386-05]; assesses=[] -->

**योग्य** — *yogya* — "eligible, qualified".

> अगर तुम्हारे अच्छे अंक आएँगे तो तुम नौकरी के योग्य होगे।
>
> *agar tumhāre acche aṅk āeṅge to tum naukrī ke yogya hoge.*
>
> If you get good marks, you'll be eligible for the job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**योग्य** (*yogya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *yogya*, "fit, suitable" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
