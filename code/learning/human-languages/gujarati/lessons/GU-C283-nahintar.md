# નહીંતર (nahī̃tar) — otherwise; or else

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C283-IF-FUTURE-01, GU-LEX-C76-HOWMUCH-04, GU-LEX-C87-CLOTHES-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "if it rains, we'll stay at home". (*jo varsād paḍśe, to ame ghare rahīśũ*.) Then "quickly" and "an umbrella". (**જલદી**, **છત્રી**.)

## You'll want to know: નહીંતર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C283-NAHINTAR-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C54-MOVE-02, GU-LEX-C214-RAIN214-05, GU-LEX-LEVU] -->

**નહીંતર** — *nahī̃tar* — "otherwise; or else". First what to do, then **નહીંતર**, then what will happen if it is not done:

> **જલદી ચાલો, નહીંતર ટ્રેન છૂટી જશે.**
>
> *jaldī cālo, nahī̃tar ṭren chūṭī jaśe.* — "Walk quickly, or else we'll miss the train."

> **છત્રી લો, નહીંતર તમે પલળી જશો.**
>
> *chatrī lo, nahī̃tar tame palḷī jaśo.* — "Take an umbrella, otherwise you'll get soaked."

It says in one word what **જો … નહીં …, તો …** says in a whole clause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નહીંતર** (*nahī̃tar*) is built from **નહીં**, "not", and *-tar*: "if not, then". CDIAL lists Gujarati *nahitar*, "otherwise", under Sanskrit *tarhi*, "then" (CDIAL 5730).
