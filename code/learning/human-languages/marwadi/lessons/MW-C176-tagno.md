## You'll want to know: टांगणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C176-GANTH176-05]; assesses=[] -->

**टांगणो** — *ṭā̃gṇo* — "to hang up".

> हूं कमीज खूंटी माथै टांगूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ kamīj khū̃ṭī māthai ṭā̃gū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I hang my shirt on the peg.

Past (I …): म्हैं कमीज टांगी, *mhaĩ kamīj ṭā̃gī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टांगणो** (*ṭā̃gṇo*) comes from a reconstructed *\*ṭaṅg*, "hang up"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ṭā̃gnā* and Gujarati *ṭā̃gvũ* (CDIAL 5436). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
