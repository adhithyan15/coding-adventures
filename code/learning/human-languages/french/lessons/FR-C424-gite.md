## You'll want to know: le gîte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C424-HEBERGEMENT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le gîte** — "the holiday cottage; the shelter".

> Cet été, ils louent un gîte à la campagne.
>
> *This summer they are renting a holiday cottage in the country.*

*Un gîte rural* — a holiday cottage in the country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gîte* comes from Old French *giste* (EtymDB), built on *gésir* 'to lie down', which is inherited from Latin *iacēre* 'to lie' (Lewis & Short).
