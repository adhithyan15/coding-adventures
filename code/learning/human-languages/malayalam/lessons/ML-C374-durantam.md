## You'll want to know: ദുരന്തം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C374-DURANTHAM374-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ദുരന്തം** — *durantaṁ* — "a disaster; a tragedy".

> ആ ദുരന്തത്തിൽ ഒരുപാട് വീടുകൾ തകർന്നു.
>
> *ā durantattil orupāṭŭ vīṭukaḷ takarnnu.*
>
> Many houses collapsed in that disaster.

Also the tragic end of a story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ദുരന്തം** (*durantaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *duranta*, "having a bad end; miserable" (Monier-Williams), made of *dur-*, "bad", and *anta*, "end".
