## You'll want to know: ਹੁਨਾਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C288-HEAT288-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੁਨਾਲ** — *hunāl* — "the hot season".

> ਹੁਨਾਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਛਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਬੈਠਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *hunāl vich asīṁ chhāṁ vich baiṭhde hāṁ.*
>
> In the hot season we sit in the shade.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੁਨਾਲ** (*hunāl*) is inherited from Sanskrit *uṣṇakāla*, "hot season"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *hunālā* under it (CDIAL 2391).
