Arabic, chapter 403: Sure and unsure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تيقن (tayaqqana) — to make certain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for not at all, then the Arabic for certainty.

You'll want to know: تيقن.
تيقن — tayaqqana — "to make certain".
تيقن الطبيب من سلامة الطفل. tayaqqana aṭ-ṭabīb min salāmat aṭ-ṭifl. The doctor made certain that the child was safe.
Present: يتيقن, yatayaqqan.
It takes من: تيقن (tayaqqana) من ….

The word, taken apart.
تيقن (tayaqqana) is built on the native Arabic root y-q-n, usually traced to the sense "to be sure". It is Form V, to make oneself sure: to make certain.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ارتاب.
ارتاب — irtāba — "to be suspicious".
ارتاب الحارس في الرجل. irtāba al-ḥāris fī ar-rajul. The guard grew suspicious of the man.
Present: يرتاب, yartāb.
It takes في: ارتاب (irtāba) في ….

The word, taken apart.
ارتاب (irtāba) is built on the native Arabic root r-y-b, "to disquiet, to raise doubt" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to be filled with disquiet: to doubt, to be suspicious.

Lesson 4 of 5.
على الأرجح (ʿalā al-arjaḥ) — most probably.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to be suspicious, then the Arabic for impossible.

You'll want to know: على الأرجح.
على الأرجح — ʿalā al-arjaḥ — "most probably".
سيصل على الأرجح بعد الظهر. sa-yaṣilu ʿalā al-arjaḥ baʿda aẓ-ẓuhr. He will most probably arrive in the afternoon.

The word, taken apart.
على الأرجح (ʿalā al-arjaḥ) joins ʿalā, "on", with al-arjaḥ, "the weightier", built on the native Arabic root r-j-ḥ, "to outweigh" (Lane): on the heavier side of the scale, most probably.

Warm-up.

الطقس عموما جميل هنا. aṭ-ṭaqs ʿumūman jamīl hunā. The weather here is generally pleasant.

عموما (ʿumūman) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-m-m, "to be general, to include all" (Lane). ʿUmūman is the verbal noun ʿumūm, generality, with the adverb ending -an: in general.
