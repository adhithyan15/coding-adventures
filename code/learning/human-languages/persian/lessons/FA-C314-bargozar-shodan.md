## You'll want to know: برگزار شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C314-RUYDAD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**برگزار شدن** (*bargozâr shodan*) — "to be held".

> جلسه در سالن بزرگ مدرسه برگزار شد.
>
> *Jalase dar sâlon-e bozorg-e madrese bargozâr shod.*
>
> The meeting was held in the big hall of the school.

For organised events. Present *bargozâr mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bargozâr* "carried out" joins *bar* "up, on" and the native stem *gozâr-* "to set out"; *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.
