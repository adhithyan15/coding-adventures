## You'll want to know: ధోరణి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C431-PRAMANAM431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ధోరణి** — *dhōraṇi* — "a trend; a tendency".

> యువతలో ఒక కొత్త ధోరణి కనిపిస్తోంది.
>
> *yuvatalō oka kotta dhōraṇi kanipistōndi.*
>
> A new trend can be seen among young people.

Also a person’s manner: అతని ధోరణి నాకు నచ్చలేదు, I did not like his attitude.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ధోరణి** (*dhōraṇi*) may go back to Sanskrit *dhoraṇī*, "an uninterrupted series" (Monier-Williams), but the link is not settled.
