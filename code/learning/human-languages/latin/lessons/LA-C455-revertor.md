## You'll want to know: revertor, revertī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C455-CONSPICOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**revertor, revertī** — "to turn back, to return".

> Via clausa est; itaque viātōrēs domum revertuntur.
>
> *The road is closed; so the travellers turn back home.*

Deponent in the present: *revertor, revertī*, but the perfect is active: *revertī*, *reversus* as a participle. English *revert* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revertor* is built from *re-* 'back' and *vertō* 'to turn' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short, under *revertō*). English *revert* comes from it.
