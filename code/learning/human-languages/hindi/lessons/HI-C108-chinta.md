## You'll want to know: चिंता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C108-FEEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चिंता** (*cintā*) — "worry". It is feminine.

**चिंता मत कीजिए** — "don't worry", with the **मत** that forbids.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Fear, anger, sadness, trouble, and a way to calm them.
