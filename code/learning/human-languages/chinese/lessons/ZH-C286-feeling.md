## Grammar Lens: 我感觉 and a feeling
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-FEELING-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C252-FEEL252-01, ZH-LEX-C125-FEEL125-05, ZH-LEX-C265-SHY265-05, ZH-LEX-C252-FEEL252-04, ZH-LEX-C51-FEEL51-01, ZH-LEX-C144-MATTER144-05, ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-SHIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C79-SCHOOL79-01] -->

> **我感觉 and a feeling word says how it felt; the feeling words are ones you know.**

> **那天我感觉很开心** *nà tiān wǒ gǎnjué hěn kāixīn* — I felt really happy that day.
> *shàngkè de shíhou wǒ gǎnjué hěn jǐnzhāng* — I felt nervous in class.

The feeling takes 很, as it does after 我: 我感觉很开心. Any feeling you know fits: *gāoxìng*, *nánguò*, *jǐnzhāng*, *jīdòng*, *shīwàng*.

感觉 is for how it felt; 觉得 is for what you think. 我觉得他说得对 is an opinion; 我感觉很好 is a feeling.
