## You'll want to know: વતન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C407-ABROAD407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વતન** — *vatan* — "a homeland; one's home town".

> દિવાળીમાં બધા વતન પાછા આવે છે.
>
> *divāḷīmā̃ badhā vatan pāchā āve che.*
>
> At Diwali everyone comes back to their home town.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વતન** (*vatan*) is usually traced to Arabic *waṭan*, "a homeland, a dwelling place", which came through Persian: the place you belong to, wherever you live now.
