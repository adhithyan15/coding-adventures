Gujarati, chapter 27: Village and Shop Complete Ten.
8 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
ગામ (gām) — hear village first.

Warm-up.
Recall shahar, city. Now listen twice: gām. It means village.

Guided Practice.
Choose city or village as you hear shahar or gām, then say gām once.

Wrap-up Recall.

Say the three meaning cues without reading a Gujarati model. Record speaking separately.
Reading — a cold shuffled row.
Read once before checking. Record the reading score on its own.
