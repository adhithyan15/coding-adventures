## You'll want to know: परिषद्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C357-SAMITI357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**परिषद्** — *pariṣad* — "a council".

> ग्रामस्य परिषत् जलस्य व्यवस्थां करोति।
>
> *grāmasya pariṣat jalasya vyavasthāṃ karoti.*
>
> The village council arranges the water supply.

A feminine noun.

The stem ends in *-d*; said on its own, as here, the word ends in *-t*: परिषत्, *pariṣat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिषद्** (*pariṣad*) is built on *pari*, "around", and the root √sad, "to sit"; MW gives *pari-ṣad* "an assembly, a meeting, an audience, a council" (MW).
