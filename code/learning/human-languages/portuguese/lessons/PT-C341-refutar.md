## You'll want to know: refutar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C341-PROVA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**refutar** — "to refute, to disprove".

> A cientista refutou a teoria com dados novos.
>
> *The scientist refuted the theory with new data.*

Formal. *Refutar um argumento* — to refute an argument.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*refutar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *refūtāre* "to check, to drive back, to repress", and so "to disprove" (Lewis & Short).
