## You'll want to know: tī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C135-SPORT135-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tī* — "to kick".

> *wǒmen qù tī zúqiú* — We're off to play football.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tī* The character is in the Guangyun as *thek* (in Baxter's transcription), but only in the name of a beast; the verb is not given there. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
