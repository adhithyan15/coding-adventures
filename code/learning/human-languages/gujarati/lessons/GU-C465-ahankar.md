## You'll want to know: અહંકાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C465-SAKHAT465-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અહંકાર** — *ahankār* — "egotism; conceit".

> પૈસાનો અહંકાર સારો નથી.
>
> *paisāno ahankār sāro nathī.*
>
> Pride in money is not good.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અહંકાર** (*ahankār*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ahaṃkāra*, the making of an "I", egotism, as Monier-Williams records it.
