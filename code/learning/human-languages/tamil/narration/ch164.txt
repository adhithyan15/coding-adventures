Tamil, chapter 164: To describe, To hand over, To practise, To pass, To pay attention.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
விவரி (vivari) — to describe, to explain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to bargain, then the Tamil for to check.

You'll want to know: விவரி.
விவரி — vivari — "to describe, to explain".

The word, taken apart.
விவரி (vivari) is a verb made from Sanskrit vivaraṇa, "explanation, an opening up", a well-established loan.

The word, taken apart.
ஒப்படை (oppaṭai) is native: oppu, "agreement, consent", from o, "to be suited, to agree" (DEDR 924), and aṭai, "to deliver, to entrust" (DEDR 79).

Lesson 5 of 5.
கவனி (kavaṉi) — to pay attention, to notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to practise, then the Tamil for to pass.

You'll want to know: கவனி.
கவனி — kavaṉi — "to pay attention, to notice".

The word, taken apart.
கவனி (kavaṉi) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1328 lists Malayalam kavanam, "care" and Kannada gavana, "attention". It is a verb made from kavaṉam, "care, attention".
