Punjabi, chapter 88: Near, Inside, Outside, On, Below.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਨੇੜੇ (neṛe) — near.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for before, then the Punjabi for far.

You'll want to know: ਨੇੜੇ.
ਨੇੜੇ — neṛe — "near".

The word, taken apart.
ਨੇੜੇ (neṛe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nikaṭam, "near" (CDIAL 7136). CDIAL calls a Munda origin unlikely.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਅੰਦਰ (andar) — inside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for far, then the Punjabi for near.

You'll want to know: ਅੰਦਰ.
ਅੰਦਰ — andar — "inside".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists ਅੰਦਰ (andar), "the inside", under Sanskrit antara, "interior" (CDIAL 357). Hindi andar is usually traced instead to Persian andar, "inside", a cousin of the Sanskrit word, so which one Punjabi has is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਬਾਹਰ (bāhar) — outside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for near, then the Punjabi for inside.

You'll want to know: ਬਾਹਰ.
ਬਾਹਰ — bāhar — "outside".

The word, taken apart.
ਬਾਹਰ (bāhar) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bāhira, "external", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9226). It is built on Sanskrit bahis, "outside".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਉੱਤੇ (utte) — on, above.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for inside, then the Punjabi for outside.

You'll want to know: ਉੱਤੇ.
ਉੱਤੇ — utte — "on, above".

The word, taken apart.
ਉੱਤੇ (utte) is inherited. CDIAL lists Old Punjabi utte, "on", as coming from uttame, the locative of Sanskrit uttama, "highest" (CDIAL 1765).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਹੇਠਾਂ.

The word, taken apart.
ਹੇਠਾਂ (heṭhāṁ) goes with heṭh, "below", which CDIAL lists under adhiṣṭāt, a form it sets beside Sanskrit adhastāt, "underneath" (CDIAL 248).
