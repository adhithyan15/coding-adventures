## You'll want to know: jīngshen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C485-MIND485-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jīngshen* — "vigour; get-up-and-go".

> *hē le kāfēi yǐhòu wǒ gǎnjué hěn yǒu jīngshen* — After a coffee, I feel full of energy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jīngshen* is built from *jīng* 'refined' and *shén* 'spirit', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsjeng* and *zyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'bright' and 'pure' and the second 'divine'. In this sense the second is said light.
