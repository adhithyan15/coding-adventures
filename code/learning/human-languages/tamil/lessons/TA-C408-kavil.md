## You'll want to know: கவிழ்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C408-VALAIVU408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கவிழ்** — *kaviḻ* — "to overturn; to capsize".

> வளைவில் ஒரு லாரி கவிழ்ந்தது.
>
> *vaḷaivil oru lāri kaviḻntatu.*
>
> A lorry overturned on the bend.

Past (it …): கவிழ்ந்தது, *kaviḻntatu*.

Of a boat, to capsize: படகு கவிழ்ந்தது.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கவிழ்** (*kaviḻ*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1335), "to be turned bottom upwards, to capsize".
