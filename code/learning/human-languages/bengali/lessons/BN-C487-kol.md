## You'll want to know: কোল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C487-SOMPORKO487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কোল** — *kol* — "a lap".

> শিশুটা দিদিমার কোলে বসে আছে।
>
> *shishuṭā didimār kole bôse āchhe.*
>
> The child is sitting on its grandmother's lap.

কোলে নেওয়া, to take onto your lap, to pick up a child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কোল** (*kol*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under Sanskrit *krōḍa*, "breast, bosom", with its side form *kōla*, "lap" (CDIAL 3607).
