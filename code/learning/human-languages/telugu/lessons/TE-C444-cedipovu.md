# చెడిపోవు (ceḍipōvu) — to break down; to go bad

## You'll want to know: చెడిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C444-INDHANAM444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**చెడిపోవు** — *ceḍipōvu* — "to break down; to go bad".

> దారిలో మా బస్సు చెడిపోయింది.
>
> *dārilō mā bassu ceḍipōyindi.*
>
> Our bus broke down on the way.

Past (it …): చెడిపోయింది, *ceḍipōyindi*.

చెడు, bad, + పోవు, to go: gone bad. A machine breaks down this way, and food goes off this way too. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: చెడిపోయింది, it broke down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చెడిపోవు** (*ceḍipōvu*) is native Dravidian: చెడు, "to rot, to be spoiled" (DEDR 2760), with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572), which marks a change that has happened.
