## You'll want to know: صفحه نمایش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C532-WATCHING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صفحه نمایش** (*safhe-ye namâyesh*) — "a screen (display)".

> صفحه نمایش موبایلم شکست.
>
> *safhe-ye namâyesh-e mobâylam shekast.*
>
> My phone screen broke.

*safhe* 'page, surface' + *namâyesh* 'showing'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*safhe-ye namâyesh* is built in Persian on *safhe*, 'page, surface', from Arabic *ṣafḥa* (EtymDB), and native *namâyesh*, 'showing', from *namudan*.
