## You'll want to know: localizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C435-ORIENTAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**localizar** — "to locate, to find".

> Não consigo localizar o hotel no mapa.
>
> *I cannot find the hotel on the map.*

*Estar localizado* — to be located: *A casa está bem localizada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*localizar* is built on *local* 'place', a learned borrowing from Late Latin *locālis* (EtymDB), on *locus* 'place'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
