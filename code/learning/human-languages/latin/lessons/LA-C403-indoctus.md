## You'll want to know: indoctus, indocta, indoctum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C403-DOCTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**indoctus, indocta, indoctum** — "unlearned, untaught".

> Indoctus est, sed nōn stultus.
>
> *He is unlearned, but not foolish.*

*indoctus, indocta, indoctum*: *in-* not + *doctus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indoctus* is built from *in-* "not" and *doctus* "taught" (Lewis & Short), the participle of *doceō*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
