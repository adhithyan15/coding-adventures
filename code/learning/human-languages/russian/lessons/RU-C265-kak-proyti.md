# как пройти к … (kak proytí k …) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-IZVINITE-01, RU-LEX-C86-LOC86-01, RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-02, RU-GRAMMAR-DOLZHEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "excuse me", "where" and "the station". (*Izvinítye. Gde.
Vokzál.*) Then "I must": a man and a woman. (*Ya dólzhen. Ya dolzhná.*)

[PAUSE 1s] Asking *где вокзал* works. Russians also ask for the way itself.

## You'll want to know: как пройти
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-KAK-PROYTI-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-02, RU-LEX-IDTI, RU-GRAMMAR-KOGDA-EVENT-01] -->

> **Извините, как пройти к вокзалу?** — *izvinítye, kak proytí k vokzálu?*
> — Excuse me, how do I get to the station?

**Пройти** is the perfective of a going-verb: to get somewhere on foot,
through the streets, and arrive. It stays in the infinitive, with no
"I" in the question: literally "how to get through to the station".

## Grammar Lens: к and the "to" form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-K-DATIVE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-03, RU-LEX-C87-LOC87-04, RU-LEX-C87-LOC87-05, RU-LEX-C91-LOC91-02] -->

**к** — *k* — "towards, to". The place after it takes the same "to" form
as *маме* and *брату*:

> **к вокзалу**, **к музею** — *k vokzálu, k muzéyu* — a masculine noun
> ending in a consonant takes **-у**, and one in *-й* takes **-ю**.
> **к остановке** — *k ostanóvke* — most nouns in *-а* take **-е**.
> **к площади** — *k plóshchadi* — *площадь*, in *-ь*, takes **-и**.
> **к метро** — *k metró* — *метро* stays as it is.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KAK-PROYTI-01, RU-LEX-C72-WHERE1-03, RU-LEX-C72-WHERE1-04, RU-LEX-C87-LOC87-02] -->

> **— Извините, как пройти к музею? Это далеко?**
> **— Нет, это близко. Музей рядом.**

*Éto dalekó?* — "Is it far?" *Nyet, éto blízko. Muzéy ryádom* — "No,
it's near. The museum is close by."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-ETYMON-IDTI-GO-WENT] -->

**пройти** is *про-*, "through", with **идти**, "to go", which EtymDB 2.1
traces through Old East Slavic *iti* and Proto-Slavic *\*jĭti* to
Proto-Indo-European *\*h₁éyti*, "to go", the root of Latin *īre*. **к**
goes back to Proto-Slavic *\*kŭ*, which EtymDB links to a
Proto-Indo-European *\*kom*, "next to, with".

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KAK-PROYTI-01, RU-GRAMMAR-K-DATIVE-01, RU-LEX-IZVINITE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *Izvinítye, kak proytí k vokzálu? Éto dalekó?*]
- [YOU SAY: the same to the museum, the stop and the square: *k muzéyu*, *k ostanóvke*, *k plóshchadi*]
- [YOU ANSWER: *Kak proytí k metró?* *Éto blízko* or *Éto dalekó*]
