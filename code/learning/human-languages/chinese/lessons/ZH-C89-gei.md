## You'll want to know: 给
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C89-GEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**给** — *gěi* — "to give; to, for".

> **妈妈给我一个手机** *māma gěi wǒ yí ge shǒujī* — Mum gives me a phone.

> **给我** *gěi wǒ* — Give it to me.

The next lesson puts the new character in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**给** (*gěi*) is in the Guangyun as *kip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to supply'. That reading regularly gives jǐ, still heard in a few written words; how the everyday gěi came about is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C75-PARTY75-05] -->

The person comes first, then the thing: 给, 我, 一个手机. On its own, 给我 is a request: give me. *sòng* gives a present; 给 gives anything at all.
