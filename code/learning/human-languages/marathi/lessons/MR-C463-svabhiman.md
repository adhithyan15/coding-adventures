## You'll want to know: स्वाभिमान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C463-GARVA463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वाभिमान** — *svābhimān* — "self-respect".

> गरीब असला तरी त्याने स्वाभिमान सोडला नाही.
>
> *garīb aslā tarī tyāne svābhimān soḍlā nāhī.*
>
> Though he was poor, he never gave up his self-respect.

Masculine.

स्व, own, + अभिमान, pride; स्वाभिमानी, self-respecting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वाभिमान** (*svābhimān*) is a learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, *sva*, "own", and *abhimāna*, "pride" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
