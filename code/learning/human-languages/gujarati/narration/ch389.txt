Gujarati, chapter 389: Making a Choice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નિર્ણય (nirṇay) — a decision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to commence, then the Gujarati for to undertake.

You'll want to know: નિર્ણય.
નિર્ણય — nirṇay — "a decision".
જો તમે ઉતાવળમાં નિર્ણય લેશો તો પછી પસ્તાશો. jo tame utāvaḷmā̃ nirṇay leśo to pachī pastāśo. If you decide in a hurry, you will regret it later.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નિર્ણય (nirṇay) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit nirṇaya, "taking off, settling, a decision" (Monier-Williams).

જો તમારી પાસે કોઈ પ્રશ્ન હશે તો પૂછવામાં ન ખચકાઓ. jo tamārī pāse koī praśna haśe to pūchvāmā̃ na khackāo. If you have a question, do not hesitate to ask.
Past (he …): તે થોડો ખચકાયો, te thoḍo khackāyo.

The word, taken apart.
ખચકાવું (khackāvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to shrink back", under a reconstructed khacc, "sink down or back" (CDIAL 3764).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ડગવું (ḍagvũ) — to waver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ડગવું.
ડગવું — ḍagvũ — "to waver".
જો તમે તમારા નિર્ણયથી ડગશો તો બીજા તમારો વિશ્વાસ નહીં કરે. jo tame tamārā nirṇaythī ḍagśo to bījā tamāro viśvās nahī̃ kare. If you waver from your decision, others will not trust you.
Past (he …): તે એક પળ માટે ડગ્યો, te ek paḷ māṭe ḍagyo.

The word, taken apart.
ડગવું (ḍagvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to tremble", under a reconstructed ḍag (CDIAL 5522); "to waver", a shaking of the mind, is a sense CDIAL does not give.
