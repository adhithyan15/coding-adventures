## You'll want to know: ぶっか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C362-HIYOU362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶっか** — *bukka* — "prices in general; the cost of living".

> **ぶっかが あがったら、こまるでしょう。** — *bukka ga agattara, komaru deshō* — "If prices go up, people will have a hard time."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶっか** (*bukka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *butsu*, "thing" (Middle Chinese *mjut*) and *ka*, "value" (*kaeH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as *buk-ka*.
