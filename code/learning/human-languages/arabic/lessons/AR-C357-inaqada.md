## You'll want to know: انعقد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C357-KHITAB357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**انعقد** — *inʿaqada* — "to convene, to be held".

> انعقد الاجتماع في الفندق.
>
> *inʿaqada al-ijtimāʿ fī al-funduq.*
>
> The meeting was held at the hotel.

Present: ينعقد, *yanʿaqid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انعقد** (*inʿaqada*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-d* (Lane), "to tie a knot", the root of the taught *ʿaqd*, a contract. It is Form VII, "to be tied": a meeting is tied together.
