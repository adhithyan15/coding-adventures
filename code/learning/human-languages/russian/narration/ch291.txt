Russian, chapter 291: The investigation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
следователь (slédovatel) — an investigator, a detective.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to kidnap, then the Russian for to hide.

You'll want to know: следователь.
следователь (slédovatel) — "an investigator, a detective".
Следователь долго говорил со свидетелем. Slédovatel dólgo govoríl so svidételem. The investigator talked with the witness for a long time.
Genitive следователя.

The word, taken apart.
следователь (slédovatel) is built on следовать "to follow", from след "a track, a trail" (EtymDB): one who follows the footsteps.

Lesson 4 of 5.
подозреваемый (podozreváyemyy) — a suspect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an investigation, then the Russian for to investigate.

You'll want to know: подозреваемый.
подозреваемый (podozreváyemyy) — "a suspect".
Подозреваемый молчал. Podozreváyemyy molchál. The suspect said nothing.
A participle that declines like an adjective; a woman is подозреваемая.

The word, taken apart.
подозреваемый (podozreváyemyy) is the passive participle of подозревать (podozrevát) "to suspect", built on под- and the old root зр- "to look", as in зрение "sight", Old East Slavic zĭrěti "to see" (EtymDB): the one watched from below.

Lesson 5 of 5.
подозревать (podozrevát) — to suspect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to investigate, then the Russian for a suspect.

You'll want to know: подозревать.
подозревать (podozrevát) — "to suspect".
Я подозреваю, что он нас обманул. Ya podozreváyu, shto on nas obmanúl. I suspect that he deceived us.
An imperfective verb. Подозревать кого-то в + prepositional: его подозревают в краже.

The word, taken apart.
подозревать (podozrevát) is built on под- "under" and the old root зр- "to look", as in зрение "sight", inherited through Old East Slavic zĭrěti "to see" (EtymDB): to watch from below. EtymDB lists the verb with no parent.
