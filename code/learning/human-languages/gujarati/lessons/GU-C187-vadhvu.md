## You'll want to know: વધવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C187-PRICE187-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વધવું** — *vadhvũ* — "to go up, to grow".

> **દૂધનો ભાવ વધે છે.** — *dūdhno bhāv vadhe che* — "The price of milk is going up."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વધવું** (*vadhvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to advance", under Sanskrit *várdhatē*, "grows, increases" (CDIAL 11376).
