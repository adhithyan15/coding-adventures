## You'll want to know: накачивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C405-SHINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**накачивать** (*nakáchivat*) — "to pump up, to inflate".

> Каждую неделю он накачивает шины на велосипеде.
>
> *Kázhduyu nedélyu on nakáchivayet shíny na velosipéde.*
>
> Every week he pumps up the tyres on his bicycle.

Imperfective; perfective *накачать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*накачивать* is built from *на-* and *качать* 'to swing, to pump'. EtymDB lists *качать* without a source, so its deeper origin is left open here.
