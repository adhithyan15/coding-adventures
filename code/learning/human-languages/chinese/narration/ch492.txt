Mandarin Chinese, chapter 492: School Subjects.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
语文 (yǔwén) — Chinese (as a school subject).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the world of work, then the Mandarin for someone under one at work.

You'll want to know: 语文.
语文 — yǔwén — "Chinese (as a school subject)".
我最喜欢语文课 wǒ zuì xǐhuan yǔwén kè — Chinese is my favourite lesson.

The word, taken apart.
语文 (yǔwén) is built from 语 'speech' and 文 'writing', both inherited from Middle Chinese, ngjoX and mjun (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to discuss' and the second 'a written piece'. The name of the school subject is modern and not given there.

One more word for saying how it was.

Lesson 2 of 5.
shùxué — maths; mathematics.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for someone under one at work, then the Mandarin for Chinese (as a school subject).

You'll want to know: shùxué.
shùxué — "maths; mathematics".
wǒ de shùxué bǐ yǔwén hǎo — I'm better at maths than at Chinese.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shùxué is built from shù 'a number' and 学 'to learn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, srjuH and haewk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'reckoning' and names the nine arts of reckoning of an old book of rites.

The word, taken apart.
