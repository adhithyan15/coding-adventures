## You'll want to know: సవరించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C428-LOPAM428-05]; assesses=[] -->

**సవరించు** — *savariñcu* — "to amend; to correct".

> ఉపాధ్యాయుడు నా తప్పులను సవరించాడు.
>
> *upādhyāyuḍu nā tappulanu savariñcāḍu.*
>
> The teacher corrected my mistakes.

Past (I …): సవరించాను, *savariñcānu*.

The noun is సవరణ, an amendment, a correction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సవరించు** (*savariñcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2342 lists Telugu *savarincu*, "to adjust, to trim, to put in order", and *savaraṇa*, "correction, setting right".
