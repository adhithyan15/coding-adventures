Lesson 1 of 6.
ઉચાટ (ucāṭ) — anxious unease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for torment, then the Gujarati for apprehension.

You'll want to know: ઉચાટ.
ઉચાટ — ucāṭ — "anxious unease".
દીકરો મોડે સુધી ઘરે ન આવ્યો, તેથી માતાને ઉચાટ થયો. dīkro moḍe sudhī ghare na āvyo, tethī mātāne ucāṭ thayo. The son had not come home till late, so his mother grew anxious.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉચાટ (ucāṭ) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "impatience, anxiety", under Sanskrit uccaṭati, "goes away" (CDIAL 1635): a mind that will not stay put.

તમે મારી ફિકર ન કરો. tame mārī phikar na karo. Don't worry about me.

The word, taken apart.
ફિકર (phikar) is usually traced to Arabic fikr, "thought, reflection", which reached Gujarati through Persian with the sense "worry".
