## Writing

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
### Part 1 — practical form

Score seven relevant independent entries. Personal or fictional values are
valid; they need not match any reading or listening profile. Require Gurmukhi
where the value is lexical, and accept Gurmukhi digits in numeric fields.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->
### Part 2 — named-reader message

Score a 30–40 word independent message to **ਅਮਨ** for its greeting, identity,
residence, language, preference, market meeting plan, and polite close. No
single wording is required. Do not show a scorer example to the learner or
award independent-writing credit to copied text.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->

## Speaking

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
### Part 1 — personal interview

Score five relevant personal responses: name, residence, language, preferred
activity, and location of one familiar object. Accept internally consistent
taught gender choices and genuine repair.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
### Part 2 — prepared description

Score one minute about a familiar person for identity, residence, one language,
and one preference or activity. Keywords used in preparation are not a written
script.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
### Part 3 — transactional role-play

Score a greeting, a comprehensible book or pen request, handling the stated
price, and a polite close. The learner may repair an utterance; do not require
one memorized sentence.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->

Productive examples and criteria guide scoring; they are not scripts the
learner must reproduce. A qualified Punjabi reviewer must still validate the
paper, mastered recordings, responses, and scoring decisions.
