## You'll want to know: ಬೇಕರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C469-VARANTI469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೇಕರಿ** — *bēkari* — "a bakery".

> ಸಂಜೆ ಬೇಕರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಿಸಿ ಬ್ರೆಡ್ ಸಿಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *sañje bēkariyalli bisi breḍ siguttade.*
>
> In the evening you get hot bread at the bakery.

Bread, biscuits, cakes and tea are sold there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೇಕರಿ** (*bēkari*) is a loan from English *bakery*.
