## You'll want to know: أشار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C417-WUDUH417-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أشار** — *ashāra* — "to refer".

> أشار المعلم إلى خطأ في الجملة.
>
> *ashāra al-muʿallim ilā khaṭaʾ fī al-jumla.*
>
> The teacher drew attention to a mistake in the sentence.

Present: يشير, *yushīr*.

It takes إلى: أشار إلى ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أشار** (*ashāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-w-r*, "to take honey; to give a sign" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make a sign to someone: to refer, to indicate.
