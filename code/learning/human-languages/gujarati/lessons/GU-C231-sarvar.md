# સારવાર (sārvār) — treatment, medical care

## You'll want to know: સારવાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C231-MEDICINE231-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સારવાર** — *sārvār* — "treatment, medical care".

> **શું અહીં સારવાર મોંઘી છે?** — *śũ ahī̃ sārvār mõghī che?* — "Is treatment expensive here?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **સારવાર** (*sārvār*) is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form for it, and its make-up is not clear.
