## You'll want to know: इत्तफ़ाक़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C546-TAQDIR546-05]; assesses=[] -->

**इत्तफ़ाक़** — *ittafāq* — "chance; a chance happening".

> इत्तफ़ाक़ से हम दोनों एक ही ट्रेन में थे।
>
> *Ittafāq se ham donõ ek hī ṭren meṁ the.*
>
> By chance we were both on the same train.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इत्तफ़ाक़** (*ittafāq*) is usually traced to Arabic *ittifāq*, "a coming together, agreement", through Persian.
