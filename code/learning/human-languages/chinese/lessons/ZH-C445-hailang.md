## You'll want to know: hǎilàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C445-SEA445-01]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎilàng* — "sea wave; a wave of the sea".

> *hǎilàng tài dà le chuán kāi bu dào nàge dǎo* — The waves are too big: the boat can't get to the island.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎilàng* is built from 海 'the sea' and *làng* 'a wave', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xojX* and *langH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'waves'.
