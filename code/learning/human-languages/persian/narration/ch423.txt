Persian, chapter 423: Filling up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پمپ بنزین (pomp-e benzin) — a petrol station (US: gas station).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to start, then the Persian for to stall.

You'll want to know: پمپ بنزین.
پمپ بنزین (pomp-e benzin) — "a petrol station (US: gas station)".
در این جاده پمپ بنزین کم است. Dar in jâdde pomp-e benzin kam ast. There are few petrol stations on this road.
Literally 'petrol pump'. In speech also pomp benzin.

The word, taken apart.
pomp-e benzin is built from pomp 'pump', a European loan usually traced to French pompe, and benzin 'petrol', usually traced to French benzine. EtymDB does not list the compound.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بنزین زدن (benzin zadan) — to fill up (with petrol), to get petrol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to stall, then the Persian for a petrol station.

You'll want to know: بنزین زدن.
بنزین زدن (benzin zadan) — "to fill up (with petrol), to get petrol".
قبل از سفر بنزین زدیم. Qabl az safar benzin zadim. We filled up before the trip.
benzin + zadan. Present stem zan-: benzin mizanim — we fill up.

The word, taken apart.
benzin zadan is built from benzin 'petrol', usually traced to French benzine, and the native verb zadan 'to strike'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
باک (bâk) — a fuel tank (of a car).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a petrol station, then the Persian for to fill up.

You'll want to know: باک.
باک (bâk) — "a fuel tank (of a car)".
باک (bâk) ماشین تقریبا خالی است. Bâk-e mâshin taqriban khâli ast. The car's tank is almost empty.
Bâk râ por kardan — to fill the tank. A different, older bâk means fear.

The word, taken apart.
bâk 'fuel tank' was borrowed from Russian and is usually traced to Russian bak 'a tank'. EtymDB lists Russian bak only with a different, nautical sense, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تمام شدن (tamâm shodan) — to run out; to be finished, to end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to fill up, then the Persian for a fuel tank.

You'll want to know: تمام شدن.
تمام شدن (tamâm shodan) — "to run out; to be finished, to end".
وسط جاده بنزین ما تمام شد. Vasat-e jâdde benzin-e mâ tamâm shod. Our petrol ran out in the middle of the road.
tamâm 'complete, finished' + shodan. Tamâm kardan is to finish something.

The word, taken apart.
tamâm shodan is built from tamâm 'complete, finished', borrowed from Arabic tamām 'complete' (EtymDB), and the native verb shodan 'to become'.
