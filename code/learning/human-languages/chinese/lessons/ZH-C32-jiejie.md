## You'll want to know: jiějie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C32-FAMILY32-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jiějie* — "elder sister".

> *wǒ jiějie shì dàxuésheng* — My older sister is at university.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiějie* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsjaeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), where it is glossed as the word one people used for mother. How it came to mean elder sister is not settled.
