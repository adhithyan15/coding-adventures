# অভিশাপ (ôbhishāp) — a curse

## You'll want to know: অভিশাপ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C322-RAKKHOSH322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিশাপ** — *ôbhishāp* — "a curse".

> ডাইনির অভিশাপে রাজকুমার পাথর হলেন।
>
> *ḍāinir ôbhishāpe rājkumār pāthôr hôlen.*
>
> By the witch's curse the prince was turned to stone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিশাপ** (*ôbhishāp*) is Sanskrit *abhiśāpa*, 'a curse; a charge' (Monier-Williams). অভিশাপ দেওয়া, to put a curse on someone.
