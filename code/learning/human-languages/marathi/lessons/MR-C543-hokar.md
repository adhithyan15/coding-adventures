## You'll want to know: होकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C543-HOKAR543-03]; assesses=[] -->

**होकार** — *hokār* — "assent; a yes".

> मीराने लग्नाला होकार दिला.
>
> *mīrāne lagnālā hokār dilā.*
>
> Meera said yes to the marriage.

Masculine.

होकार देणे, to say yes; its opposite is नकार.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**होकार** (*hokār*) is a Marathi derivation built on हो, "yes", with -कार, "a saying", from Sanskrit *kāra* (Monier-Williams), formed like नकार, "a no".
