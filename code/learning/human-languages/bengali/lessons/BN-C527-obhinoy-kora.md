# অভিনয় করা (ôbhinôy kôrā) — to act on stage; to act in a film

## You'll want to know: অভিনয় করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C527-MONCHO527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিনয় করা** — *ôbhinôy kôrā* — "to act on stage; to act in a film".

> আমার বোন স্কুলের নাটকে অভিনয় করল।
>
> *Āmār bon skuler nāṭôke ôbhinôy kôrlô.*
>
> My sister acted in the school play.

Past (he/she …): তিনি সিনেমায় অভিনয় করলেন, *tini sinemāy ôbhinôy kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিনয় করা** (*ôbhinôy kôrā*) joins অভিনয়, Sanskrit *abhinaya*, the art of showing feeling by look and gesture (Monier-Williams), to করা, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
