## You'll want to know: der Streik
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C277-STREIK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Streik** — "the strike (at work)".

> Wegen des Streiks fuhren keine Züge.
>
> *Because of the strike no trains ran.*

Plural: *die Streiks*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Streik* was borrowed from English *strike* (EtymDB) and given German spelling.
