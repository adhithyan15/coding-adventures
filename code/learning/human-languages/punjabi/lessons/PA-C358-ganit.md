## You'll want to know: ਗਣਿਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C358-SUBJ358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਣਿਤ** — *gaṇit* — "mathematics".

> ਗਣਿਤ ਮੇਰੇ ਲਈ ਸੌਖਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *gaṇit mere laī saukhā hai.*
>
> Mathematics is easy for me.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਣਿਤ** (*gaṇit*) is taken over from Sanskrit *gaṇita*, "reckoning, calculating", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
