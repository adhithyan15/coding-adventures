# ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ (dṛṣṭikōna) — a point of view

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C377-TARKA377-04, KA-LEX-C377-TARKA377-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to object, then the Kannada for reasonable.

## You'll want to know: ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C378-NILUVU378-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ** — *dṛṣṭikōna* — "a point of view".

> ಪ್ರತಿಯೊಬ್ಬರಿಗೂ ಬೇರೆ ಬೇರೆ ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *pratiyobbarigū bēre bēre dṛṣṭikōna iruttade.*
>
> Everyone has a different point of view.

Word by word it is sight + angle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ** (*dṛṣṭikōna*) is built from Sanskrit *dṛṣṭi*, "sight, view", and *koṇa*, "a corner, an angle" (Monier-Williams): the angle you look from.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
