French, chapter 140: Already, Recently, the Day before Yesterday, Formerly, To Happen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
déjà — already.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for which one, then the French for anything at all.

You'll want to know: déjà.
déjà — "already". It sits between avoir and the participle: J'ai déjà parlé. — "I have already spoken."

récemment — "recently". Récemment, j'ai beaucoup lu. — "Recently I have read a lot." The ending -ment makes an adverb, the way English uses -ly.

You'll want to know: autrefois.
autrefois — "formerly, in the old days". It points to a time long past rather than a single day: Autrefois, le magasin était ici. — "In the old days the shop was here."
