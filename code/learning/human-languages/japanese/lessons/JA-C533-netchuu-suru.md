## You'll want to know: ねっちゅうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C533-WORTH533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ねっちゅうする** — *netchū suru* — "to be absorbed (in)".

> **もし しゅみに ねっちゅうしたら、あには じかんを わすれるでしょう。** — *moshi shumi ni netchū shitara, ani wa jikan o wasureru deshō* — "If my elder brother gets absorbed in a hobby, he will forget the time."

Polite **ねっちゅうします**, *netchūshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **おとうとが しょうぎに ねっちゅうしました。**, *otōto ga shōgi ni netchūshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねっちゅうする** (*netchū suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *netchū* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ne*, "heat" (Middle Chinese *nyet*) and *chū*, "in" (*trjuwng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
