## You'll want to know: أنقاض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C316-KARITHA316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أنقاض** — *anqāḍ* — "ruins, debris".

> بني بيت جديد فوق أنقاض البيت القديم.
>
> *buniya bayt jadīd fawqa anqāḍ al-bayt al-qadīm.*
>
> A new house was built on the ruins of the old one.

A plural word, as English ruins.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أنقاض** (*anqāḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-ḍ* (Lane), "to undo, to pull down a building". *Anqāḍ* is the plural of *niqḍ*, "what has been pulled down".
