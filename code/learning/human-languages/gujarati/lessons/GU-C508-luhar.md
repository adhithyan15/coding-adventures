## You'll want to know: લુહાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C508-LUHAR508-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લુહાર** — *luhār* — "a blacksmith".

> લુહાર લોખંડમાંથી ઓજારો બનાવે છે.
>
> *luhār lokhanḍmā̃thī ojāro banāve che.*
>
> The blacksmith makes tools out of iron.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લુહાર** (*luhār*) goes back to Sanskrit *lōhakāra*, "an iron-worker", built from *lōha*, "iron", and *kāra*, "maker"; CDIAL gives the Gujarati form as *lavār* (CDIAL 11159).
