Malayalam, chapter 247: Double, Elderly, Bald, Curly, Orderly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഇരട്ട (iraṭṭa) — double, twin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for firm, then the Malayalam for broken.

You'll want to know: ഇരട്ട.
ഇരട്ട — iraṭṭa — "double, twin".

The word, taken apart.
ഇരട്ട (iraṭṭa) is native Dravidian, from the root for "two": DEDR 474 lists it with Tamil iraṭṭai, "pair, twins".

Lesson 2 of 5.
പ്രായമായ (prāyamāya) — elderly, aged.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for broken, then the Malayalam for double.

You'll want to know: പ്രായമായ.
പ്രായമായ — prāyamāya — "elderly, aged".

The word, taken apart.
പ്രായമായ (prāyamāya) joins prāyaṁ, "age", a word of Sanskrit shape whose exact source is uncertain, to native āya, "having become" (DEDR 333): "having become of age". DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

കഷണ്ടിയുള്ള (kaṣaṇṭiyuḷḷa) is "having baldness": kaṣaṇṭi, a word of uncertain origin, with the native -uḷḷa, "that has" (DEDR 697).
