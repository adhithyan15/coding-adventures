## You'll want to know: ತಜ್ಞ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C516-VRITTI516-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಜ್ಞ** — *tajña* — "an expert; a specialist".

> ಅಪ್ಪನನ್ನು ಹೃದಯ ತಜ್ಞರ ಬಳಿ ಕರೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಹೋದೆವು.
>
> *appanannu hṛdaya tajñara baḷi karedukoṇḍu hōdevu.*
>
> We took father to a heart specialist.

Plural: ತಜ್ಞರು, *tajñaru*.

Often after a field: ಹೃದಯ ತಜ್ಞ, a heart specialist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಜ್ಞ** (*tajña*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tajjña*, "knowing that; a man who knows" (Monier-Williams).
