# ಸಚಿವಾಲಯ (sacivālaya) — a ministry

## You'll want to know: ಸಚಿವಾಲಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C395-ADALITA395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಚಿವಾಲಯ** — *sacivālaya* — "a ministry".

> ಅವನ ಅಪ್ಪ ಸಚಿವಾಲಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *avana appa sacivālayadalli kelasa māḍuttāre.*
>
> His father works in the ministry.

ಸಚಿವ (minister) + ಆಲಯ (house).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಚಿವಾಲಯ** (*sacivālaya*) is built from Sanskrit *saciva*, "a companion, a king's minister" (Monier-Williams), and *ālaya*, "a house" (CDIAL 1366).
