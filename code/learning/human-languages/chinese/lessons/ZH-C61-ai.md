## You'll want to know: ǎi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C61-LONG61-04]; assesses=[] -->

*ǎi* — "short in height; low".

> *wǒ mèimei hěn ǎi* — My little sister is short.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ǎi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *'eaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'short to look at'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
