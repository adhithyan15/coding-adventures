## You'll want to know: じょゆう

**じょゆう** — *joyū* — "an actress".

> **じょゆうは えいがで ないていました。** — *joyū wa eiga de naiteimashita* — "The actress was crying in the film."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょゆう** (*joyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jo*, "woman" (Middle Chinese *nrjoX*) and *yū*, "actor" (*'juw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
