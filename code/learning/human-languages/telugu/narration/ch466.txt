Telugu, chapter 466: Around the Square.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
చౌరస్తా (caurastā) — a town square; a crossroads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a local person, then the Telugu for a stranger.

You'll want to know: చౌరస్తా.
చౌరస్తా — caurastā — "a town square; a crossroads".
చౌరస్తాలో పెద్ద గడియారం ఉంది. caurastālō pedda gaḍiyāraṁ undi. There is a big clock in the town square.
Where four roads meet; the busy centre of a small town.

The word, taken apart.
చౌరస్తా (caurastā) is usually traced to Persian chahār, "four", and rāsta, "a road", which came into Telugu through Hindustani: the place where four roads meet.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఉద్యానవనం (udyānavanaṁ) — a public garden; a park.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a stranger, then the Telugu for a town square.

You'll want to know: ఉద్యానవనం.
ఉద్యానవనం — udyānavanaṁ — "a public garden; a park".
సాయంత్రం ఉద్యానవనంలో చాలా మంది నడుస్తారు. sāyantraṁ udyānavanaṁlō cālā mandi naḍustāru. In the evening a lot of people walk in the park.
ఉద్యానం, a garden, + వనం, a grove. పార్కు is the everyday loan.

The word, taken apart.
ఉద్యానవనం (udyānavanaṁ) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: udyāna, "a park, a garden" (CDIAL 2052), and vana, "a wood, a grove".

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Telugu for a town square, then the Telugu for a public garden.

You'll want to know: పచ్చిక.
పచ్చిక — paccika — "a lawn; grassland".
పిల్లలు పచ్చిక మీద ఆడుకుంటున్నారు. pillalu paccika mīda āḍukuṇṭunnāru. The children are playing on the lawn.
Fresh green grass for sitting or grazing. పచ్చ, green, is in the word.

The word, taken apart.
పచ్చిక (paccika) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3821 lists Telugu paccika, "grass", beside pacca, "green".

Lesson 4 of 5.
వీధిదీపం (vīdhidīpaṁ) — a street lamp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a public garden, then the Telugu for a lawn.

You'll want to know: వీధిదీపం.
వీధిదీపం — vīdhidīpaṁ — "a street lamp".
మా వీధిలో వీధిదీపాలు వెలగడం లేదు. mā vīdhilō vīdhidīpālu velagaḍaṁ lēdu. The street lamps in our street are not working.
Plural: వీధిదీపాలు, vīdhidīpālu.
వీధి, a street, + దీపం, a lamp. Plural వీధిదీపాలు.

The word, taken apart.
వీధిదీపం (vīdhidīpaṁ) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: vīthi, "a road, a street" (CDIAL 12050), and dīpa, "a lamp" (CDIAL 6348).

Lesson 5 of 5.
పరిసరాలు (parisarālu) — surroundings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a lawn, then the Telugu for a street lamp.

You'll want to know: పరిసరాలు.
పరిసరాలు — parisarālu — "surroundings".
మన పరిసరాలను శుభ్రంగా ఉంచాలి. mana parisarālanu śubhraṅgā uñcāli. We should keep our surroundings clean.
Mostly plural. The area around a house, a school or a town.

The word, taken apart.
పరిసరాలు (parisarālu) is borrowed from Sanskrit parisara, "the surroundings, the neighbourhood" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu plural -లు.
