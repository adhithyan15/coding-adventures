Italian, chapter 401: File e cartelle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il file — the computer file.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the device, then the Italian for the software.

You'll want to know: il file.
il file — "the computer file".
Se mi mandi il file, lo stampo. If you send me the file, I will print it.
Masculine, invariable: i file. Pronounced as in English.

The word, taken apart.
file was borrowed from English file, which came from French fil "thread", from Latin fīlum (Lewis & Short): papers were once kept threaded on a string.

Lesson 2 of 5.
cancellare — to delete, to rub out; to cancel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the software, then the Italian for the computer file.

You'll want to know: cancellare.
cancellare — "to delete, to rub out; to cancel".
Se cancelli la foto, non la trovi più. If you delete the photo, you will not find it again.
Also to cancel: il volo è stato cancellato.

The word, taken apart.
cancellare comes from Latin cancellāre "to make like a lattice, to cross out" (Lewis & Short), from cancellī "a lattice, bars": lines drawn across a text. The word cancello "gate" shares it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
copiare — to copy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the computer file, then the Italian for to delete.

You'll want to know: copiare.
copiare — "to copy".
Ho copiato il testo e te l'ho mandato. I copied the text and sent it to you.
At school, copiare is to cheat in a test.

The word, taken apart.
copiare is built on copia "copy", from Medieval Latin cōpia, which in Latin meant "plenty, abundance" (Lewis & Short): making many from one.

Lesson 4 of 5.
incollare — to paste; to glue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to delete, then the Italian for to copy.

You'll want to know: incollare.
incollare — "to paste; to glue".
Ho copiato l'indirizzo e l'ho incollato nel messaggio. I copied the address and pasted it into the message.
Copia e incolla — copy and paste.

The word, taken apart.
incollare is built from in- and colla "glue", which is inherited from Vulgar Latin colla (EtymDB), from Greek kólla "glue".

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: il backup.
il backup — "the backup (copy)".
Se fai un backup ogni settimana, non perdi niente. If you make a backup every week, you will not lose anything.
Masculine, invariable. Also la copia di sicurezza.

The word, taken apart.
backup was borrowed from English backup (EtymDB), built from back and up: something kept in support.
