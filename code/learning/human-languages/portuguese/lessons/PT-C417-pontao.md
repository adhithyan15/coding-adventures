## You'll want to know: o pontão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C417-PORTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o pontão** — "the pontoon, the jetty".

> O barco pequeno está preso ao pontão.
>
> *The small boat is tied to the pontoon.*

A floating platform where small boats stop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pontão* is inherited through Old Portuguese *ponton* from Latin *pontō* 'ferryboat, pontoon', built on *pōns, pontis* 'bridge' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). EtymDB also reads it as *ponte* with *-ão*.
