## You'll want to know: el suplemento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C663-TEMPORADA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el suplemento** — "the extra charge, the supplement".

> La cama extra tiene un suplemento.
>
> *There is an extra charge for the extra bed.*

A newspaper *suplemento* is a magazine inside it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suplemento* is a learned borrowing from Latin *supplēmentum* "that which makes a thing full, a supply" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from *supplēre* "to fill up".
