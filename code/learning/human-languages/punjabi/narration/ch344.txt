Punjabi, chapter 344: We Don't Agree.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਦਲੀਲ (dalīl) — an argument; a reason given in support.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a matter, then the Punjabi for proof.

You'll want to know: ਦਲੀਲ.
ਦਲੀਲ — dalīl — "an argument; a reason given in support".
ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਦਲੀਲ ਠੀਕ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ। tuhāḍī dalīl ṭhīk nahīṁ hai. Your argument is not right.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਦਲੀਲ (dalīl) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic dalīl, "guide".

What you've built.
One more word for asking and answering.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dalīl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dalīl, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tuhāḍī dalīl ṭhīk nahīṁ hai. — Your argument is not right.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for a matter, then the Punjabi for proof, then say dalīl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਦਲੀਲ (dalīl) mean? ("An argument; a reason given in support".) Say it once more.
