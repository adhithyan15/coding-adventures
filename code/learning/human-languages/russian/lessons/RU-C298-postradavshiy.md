## You'll want to know: пострадавший
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C298-NAEZD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**пострадавший** (*postradávshiy*) — "an injured person, a casualty".

> Пострадавшего отвезли в больницу.
>
> *Postradávshevo otvezlí v bolnítsu.*
>
> The injured man was taken to hospital.

A participle that declines like an adjective; a woman is *пострадавшая*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пострадавший* is the past participle of *пострадать* "to be hurt", from *по-* and *страдать* "to suffer"; EtymDB lists no parent.
