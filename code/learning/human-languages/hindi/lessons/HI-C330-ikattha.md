## You'll want to know: इकट्ठा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C330-MELA330-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इकट्ठा** — *ikaṭṭhā* — "gathered together".

> मेले में पूरा गाँव इकट्ठा हुआ।
>
> *mele meṁ pūrā gā̃v ikaṭṭhā huā.*
>
> The whole village gathered at the fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इकट्ठा** (*ikaṭṭhā*) goes back to Sanskrit *ekastha*, "standing together" (CDIAL 2480): all in one spot. **इकट्ठा होना** is to gather; **इकट्ठा करना**, to collect.
