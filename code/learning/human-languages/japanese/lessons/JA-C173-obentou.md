## You'll want to know: おべんとう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C173-FOOD173-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おべんとう** — *obentō* — "boxed lunch; bento".

> **こうえんで おべんとうを たべます。** — *kōen de obentō o tabemasu* — "I eat a boxed lunch in the park."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おべんとう** (*obentō*) is built from polite *o-* and Sino-Japanese *bentō*, two characters read with their on'yomi, *ben* and *tō*, readings borrowed from Chinese; the older history of the compound is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
