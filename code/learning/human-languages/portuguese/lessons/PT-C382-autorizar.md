## You'll want to know: autorizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C382-PROIBIDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**autorizar** — "to authorise, to allow (officially)".

> O diretor autorizou a visita de estudo.
>
> *The head teacher authorised the school trip.*

*A autorização* — the permit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*autorizar* comes from Medieval Latin *auctōrizāre* (EtymDB), built on *auctor* "author, guarantor", from *augēre* "to increase" (Lewis & Short).
