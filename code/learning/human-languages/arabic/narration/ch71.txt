Arabic, chapter 71: Mirror, Lamp, Key, Plate, Knife.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مرآة (mirʾāh) — a mirror.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bed, then the Arabic for the floor; the earth.

مرآة — mirʾāh — "a mirror".
