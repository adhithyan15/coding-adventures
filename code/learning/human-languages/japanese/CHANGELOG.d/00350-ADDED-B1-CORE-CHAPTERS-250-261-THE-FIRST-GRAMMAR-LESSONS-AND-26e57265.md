## Added — B1 core: chapters 250–261, the first grammar lessons and six kanji

- **Twelve chapters (250–261), 62 lessons, are the track's first grammar lessons.** Until now grammar rode inside word lessons; here each new form gets a lesson of its own, one at a time, in the polite です / ます frame the track uses:
  - 250–251 bridge the two A2 nodes the track had left open (their spine `omits` are cleared): the plain past -た, first for the verbs that drop る (みた, たべた) and する / くる, then the godan verbs by their last kana, one group a step (かった, のんだ, かいた, およいだ, はなした, いった); the future as the non-past with a time word (Japanese has no future form); the plain negative -ない / -なかった; and the verb なる.
  - 252–253 (NARRATE-EVENTS): the -て form as the plain past with え in place of あ, -てから, それから, そして, -ています / -ていました, and とき for the scene.
  - 254–255 (GIVE-REASONS): a plain clause + と おもいます, どう おもいますか, から / ので, -た ほうが いいです / -ない ほうが いいです (advice only), だから, ですから, それで.
  - 256–257 (HANDLE-TRAVEL): asking the way, directions with -て ください, tickets with the counter まい, なんじに でますか, を なくしました / が こわれました, たすけて ください, のりおくれる.
  - 258–259 (DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE): the i-adjective past -かった / -くなかった, 〜に なりたいです, ゆめは …ことです, -た ことが あります, より, どちら, いちばん, 〜じかんぐらい, 〜から 〜まで.
  - 260–261 (EXPRESS-CONDITION): -たら as the plain past + ら, もし … -たら, 〜に よります, ばあい, and "would" as -たら … でしょう / と おもいます.
  All five B1 spine nodes are now realized. Each chapter ends on its review. Every lesson carries one typed activity, and every grammar lesson ends on three spoken lines that change one word.
- **A gentle ramp.** Every Japanese line has at most four bunsetsu in 250–253 and six after, at most one new form, its romanization and its English. Every chapter opens on a lesson that can be followed by ear.
- **A reading and a writing task.** Chapter 253 has a 181-sign story of a day at the river, in lines of at most 20 signs. Chapter 261 has a six-line writing task about a trip, one frame a line, with no model to copy.
- **Six kanji, copy-only.** 月 (in 月日), 年 (年じゅう), 出 (言い出す), 人 (日本人), 中 (日本中) and 今 (今まで). Each comes right after the word that holds it, with its strokes in words from KanjiVG and the one reading that word uses. Five new inventory rows list KanjiVG's order and cite its file in the stroke-order note. They carry no pen-lift count or structured source, because script-ductus holds both to a fitted ductus, and none exists yet. The 今 row is corrected to KanjiVG's four strokes; it had listed three, with no source. The Noto Sans JP subset is rebuilt (918 ideographs) so every new kanji renders.
- **Sources.** JMdict and KANJIDIC2 for readings, spellings, verb classes and kanji meanings, and Middle Chinese from the Guangyun in Baxter's transcription (tshet-uinh). Each verb form printed is the regular conjugation of a verb whose class JMdict gives. Not taught, and still untaught: the potential, passive, causative, volitional, imperative, -ば, -ながら, -たり, -なくて, -なければ, -ては / -ても, and -て + an auxiliary other than いる.
