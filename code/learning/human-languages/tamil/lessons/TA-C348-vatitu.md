# வாதிடு (vātiṭu) — to argue

## You'll want to know: வாதிடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C348-ETIR348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**வாதிடு** — *vātiṭu* — "to argue".

> அவள் தன் உரிமைக்காக வாதிட்டாள்.
>
> *avaḷ taṉ urimaikkāka vātiṭṭāḷ.*
>
> She argued for her right.

Past (I …): வாதிட்டேன், *vātiṭṭēṉ*.

With -உடன் for the other side: அவருடன் வாதிட்டேன், I argued with him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வாதிடு** (*vātiṭu*) is a compound: *vātam*, "argument", is borrowed from Sanskrit *vāda*, "speech" (CDIAL 11507), and native *iṭu*, "to put" (DEDR 442), makes it a verb, as in முத்தமிடு.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
