## You'll want to know: にんぎょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C237-PHOTO237-05]; assesses=[] -->

**にんぎょう** — *ningyō* — "a doll; a puppet".

> **いもうとは きょねん にんぎょうを あつめました。** — *imōto wa kyonen ningyō o atsumemashita* — "Last year my younger sister collected dolls."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にんぎょう** (*ningyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nin*, "person" (Middle Chinese *nyin*) and *gyō*, "shape" (*heng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
