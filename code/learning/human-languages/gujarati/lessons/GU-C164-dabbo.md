## You'll want to know: ડબ્બો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C164-SPICE164-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ડબ્બો** — *ḍabbo* — "tin, box".

> **મસાલાનો ડબ્બો રસોડામાં છે.** — *masālāno ḍabbo rasoḍāmā̃ che* — "The spice tin is in the kitchen."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ડબ્બો** (*ḍabbo*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
