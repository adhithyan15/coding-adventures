## You'll want to know: विस्तारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C380-MATRA380-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विस्तारयति** — *vistārayati* — "expands; extends".

> यदि वणिक् व्यापारं विस्तारयति, तर्हि लाभः वर्धते।
>
> *yadi vaṇik vyāpāraṃ vistārayati, tarhi lābhaḥ vardhate.*
>
> If the merchant expands his trade, his profit grows.

Past (he …): सः व्यापारं विस्तारितवान्, *saḥ vyāpāraṃ vistāritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विस्तारयति** (*vistārayati*) is built on *vi* and the root √stṛ, "to spread", in its causative form; MW gives *vi-stārita* "spread, extended" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
