## You'll want to know: бакалавр
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C385-OBRAZOVANIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**бакалавр** (*bakalávr*) — "a bachelor (degree holder)".

> После четырёх лет учёбы он стал бакалавром.
>
> *Pósle chetyryókh let uchyóby on stal bakalávrom.*
>
> After four years of study he became a bachelor.

Masculine. *Степень бакалавра* — a bachelor’s degree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**бакалавр** is usually traced to Medieval Latin *baccalaureus*, a reshaping of *baccalārius* "a young man, a junior"; the further origin is not settled.
