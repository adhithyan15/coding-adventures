# किस्सा (kissā) — an anecdote

## You'll want to know: किस्सा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C303-KATHA303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**किस्सा** — *kissā* — "an anecdote".

> बाबांनी त्यांच्या लहानपणीचा एक मजेदार किस्सा सांगितला.
>
> *bābānnī tyāñcyā lahānapṇīcā ek majedār kissā sāṅgitlā.*
>
> Dad told a funny anecdote from his childhood.

Masculine. Plural: किस्से, *kisse*.

Plural किस्से.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किस्सा** (*kissā*) is a loan from Persian *qissa*, usually traced to Arabic *qiṣṣa*, 'a story, a narrative'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
