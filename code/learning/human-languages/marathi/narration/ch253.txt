Marathi, chapter 253: To turn, To enjoy, To imagine, To inform, To complain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वळणे (vaḷṇe) — to turn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to smoke, then the Marathi for to go with.

You'll want to know: वळणे.
वळणे — vaḷṇe — "to turn".

The word, taken apart.
वळणे (vaḷṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from valati, "turns" (CDIAL 11405). CDIAL glosses the Marathi verb "to turn, turn round, bend".

कळवणे — kaḷavṇe — "to inform, to let know".

The word, taken apart.
कळवणे (kaḷavṇe) is the "make" form of kaḷṇẽ, "to be clear to, be understood", which comes from Sanskrit kalayati, "counts, thinks" (CDIAL 2918). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit verb from Dravidian.

तक्रार करणे — takrār karṇe — "to complain".

The word, taken apart.
तक्रार, "a complaint", is usually traced through Persian to Arabic takrār, "repetition", whose root is k-r-r (Lane). करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).
