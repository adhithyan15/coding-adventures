## You'll want to know: निर्गमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C423-PRAVESHA423-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्गमः** — *nirgamaḥ* — "a way out; an exit".

> निर्गमः दक्षिणतः अस्ति।
>
> *nirgamaḥ dakṣiṇataḥ asti.*
>
> The exit is on the right.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्गमः** (*nirgamaḥ*) is built from *nir*, "out", and the root √gam, "to go", as *nirgacchati*, "goes out", is; MW gives *nirgama* as going forth, departure, and the way out of a building is that sense in modern use (MW).
