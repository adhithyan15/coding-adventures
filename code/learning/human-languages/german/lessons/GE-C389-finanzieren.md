## You'll want to know: finanzieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C389-LEISTEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**finanzieren** — "to finance, to pay for".

> Sie finanziert ihr Studium mit einem Nebenjob.
>
> *She pays for her studies with a part-time job.*

Perfekt: *hat finanziert*. *Die Finanzen* — finances.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*finanzieren* was borrowed from French *financer* (EtymDB), from *finance* "payment", built on Old French *finer* "to settle a debt", from *fin* "end", Latin *fīnis* (Lewis & Short): you bring a debt to an end.

## What you've built
