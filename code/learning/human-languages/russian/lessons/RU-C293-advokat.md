## You'll want to know: адвокат
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C293-SUD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**адвокат** (*advokát*) — "a lawyer (for the defence)".

> Адвокат говорил спокойно и уверенно.
>
> *Advokát govoríl spokóyno i uvérenno.*
>
> The lawyer spoke calmly and confidently.

Masculine, also of a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*адвокат* goes back to Latin *advocātus* (EtymDB), "one called in to help", the participle of *advocāre* "to call to" (Lewis & Short).
