## You'll want to know: fīliola, fīliolae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C529-NOVE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fīliola, fīliolae** — "a little daughter".

> Fīliola mea hodiē quīnque annōs nāta est.
>
> *My little daughter is five years old today.*

Feminine, a diminutive of *fīlia*: a word of affection.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fīliola** is a diminutive of *fīlia* "daughter" (Lewis & Short).
