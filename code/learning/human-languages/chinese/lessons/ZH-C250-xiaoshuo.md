## You'll want to know: 小说
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C250-HOBBY250-03]; assesses=[] -->

**小说** — *xiǎoshuō* — "novel; story (in a book)".

> **我喜欢读小说** *wǒ xǐhuan dú xiǎoshuō* — I like reading novels.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**小说** (*xiǎoshuō*) is built from 小 'small' and 说 'to tell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX* and *sywet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to tell'. How 'small tellings' came to mean a novel is not settled.
