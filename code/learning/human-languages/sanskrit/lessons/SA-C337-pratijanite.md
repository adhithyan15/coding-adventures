## You'll want to know: प्रतिजानीते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C337-VANAVASA337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिजानीते** — *pratijānīte* — "promises".

> सः पित्रे प्रतिजानीते, अहं वनं गमिष्यामि इति।
>
> *saḥ pitre pratijānīte, ahaṃ vanaṃ gamiṣyāmi iti.*
>
> He promises his father that he will go to the forest.

Past (he …): सः पित्रे प्रतिज्ञातवान्, *saḥ pitre pratijñātavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिजानीते** (*pratijānīte*) is built from *prati* and the root √jñā, "to know"; MW gives *prati-jñā* as to admit, acknowledge, and to promise (MW).
