## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-FUTURE-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-HUI-FUTURE-01, ZH-LEX-C147-HOUTIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C147-KENENG-01, ZH-LEX-C147-YIDING-01, ZH-LEX-C147-TIANQIYUBAO-01, ZH-LEX-NE-01] -->

> **A:** 明天会下雨吗
>
> *míngtiān huì xiàyǔ ma* — Will it rain tomorrow?
>
> **B:** *huì, tiānqì yùbào shuō huì xiàyǔ* — It will. The forecast says so.
>
> **A:** 后天呢
>
> *hòutiān ne* — And the day after?
>
> **B:** 后天不会
>
> *hòutiān bú huì* — Not the day after.
>
> **A:** 我们后天去
>
> *wǒmen hòutiān qù* — Then we'll go the day after tomorrow.
>
> **B:** 他会来吗
>
> *tā huì lái ma* — Will he come?
>
> **A:** *tā kěnéng bù lái* — He may not.
>
> **B:** *wǒ yídìng lái* — I'll come, for sure.

会 answers a question about 会 on its own: *míngtiān huì xiàyǔ ma?* — *huì*. The answer drops everything else, as 是 and 有 do.

## What you've built — a forecast, a chance, a certainty
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-FUTURE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-HOU-AFTER-01, ZH-LEX-C147-HOUTIAN-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-HUI-FUTURE-01, ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-03] -->

Three ways to talk about what is coming, all before the verb:

- **明天会下雨** *míngtiān huì xiàyǔ* — it will rain tomorrow.
- *míngtiān kěnéng huì xiàyǔ* — it might.
- *tā yídìng huì lái* — he certainly will come.

**Listen.** *Hòutiān huì xiàyǔ ma?*

**Speak.** Say what the weather will do tomorrow, then what you will do the day after.

[YOU WRITE: 后天 — the frame first, then 口, then 天]
