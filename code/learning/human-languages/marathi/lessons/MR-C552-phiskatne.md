# फिसकटणे (phiskaṭṇe) — to fall apart; to fall through

## You'll want to know: फिसकटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C552-BIGHADNE552-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फिसकटणे** — *phiskaṭṇe* — "to fall apart; to fall through".

> पावसामुळे आमचा सहलीचा बेत फिसकटला.
>
> *pāvsāmuḷe āmcā sahlīcā bet phiskaṭlā.*
>
> Because of the rain our plan for a trip fell through.

Past (he …): तो सौदा फिसकटला, *to saudā phiskaṭlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फिसकटणे** (*phiskaṭṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *phiskaṭṇẽ*, "to fall away", under *\*phiss*, "slip" (CDIAL 9080).
