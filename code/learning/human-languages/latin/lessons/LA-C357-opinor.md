## You'll want to know: opīnor, opīnārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C357-SENTENTIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**opīnor, opīnārī** — "to suppose, to imagine".

> Ille, opīnor, nōn veniet.
>
> *He will not come, I suppose.*

Deponent: *opīnor, opīnārī, opīnātus sum*. Dropped into a sentence, *opīnor* softens it, like English *I suppose*. English *opine* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*opīnor* comes from an old adjective *opīnus* "expecting" (EtymDB), which survives in *necopīnus* "unexpected" (Lewis & Short). English *opine* and *opinion* come from it.
