अद्भुतः (adbhutaḥ) — marvellous; wonderful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a view, then the Sanskrit for strolls.

You'll want to know: अद्भुतः.
अद्भुतः — adbhutaḥ — "marvellous; wonderful".
मम यात्रायाः अनुभवः अद्भुतः अस्ति। mama yātrāyāḥ anubhavaḥ adbhutaḥ asti. The experience of my journey is marvellous.

The word, taken apart.
अद्भुतः (adbhutaḥ) is the word MW gives for wonderful, marvellous; MW gives no sure derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 2 of 6.
स्मारकम् (smārakam) — a memorial.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for strolls, then the Sanskrit for marvellous.

You'll want to know: स्मारकम्.
स्मारकम् — smārakam — "a memorial".
नगरस्य मध्ये एकं स्मारकम् अस्ति। nagarasya madhye ekaṃ smārakam asti. In the middle of the city there is a memorial.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
स्मारकम् (smārakam) is built on the causative of the root √smṛ, "to remember", which also gives smarati, "remembers": MW gives smāraka as recalling, reminding of, and a memorial is its use as a noun in modern Sanskrit (MW).

Lesson 3 of 6.
प्रेक्षकः (prekṣakaḥ) — an onlooker; a spectator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for marvellous, then the Sanskrit for a memorial.

You'll want to know: प्रेक्षकः.
प्रेक्षकः — prekṣakaḥ — "an onlooker; a spectator".
स्मारकस्य समीपे प्रेक्षकाः सन्ति। smārakasya samīpe prekṣakāḥ santi. There are onlookers near the memorial.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रेक्षकः (prekṣakaḥ) is built from pra, "forth", and the root √īkṣ, "to see", with the agent ending -aka; MW gives prekṣaka as a spectator, a member of an audience (MW).

Lesson 4 of 6.
गवाक्षः (gavākṣaḥ) — a round window.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a memorial, then the Sanskrit for an onlooker.

You'll want to know: गवाक्षः.
गवाक्षः — gavākṣaḥ — "a round window".
राज्ञी गवाक्षात् नगरं पश्यति। rājñī gavākṣāt nagaraṃ paśyati. The queen looks out at the city from the round window.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गवाक्षः (gavākṣaḥ) is built from go, "a bull", and akṣa, "an eye": a bull's eye, as MW glosses it; MW gives gavākṣa as an air-hole, a round window (MW).
