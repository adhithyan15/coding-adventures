## You'll want to know: બંગલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C418-SOCIETY418-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બંગલો** — *banglo* — "a bungalow; a large house".

> નદી કિનારે એક સુંદર બંગલો છે.
>
> *nadī kināre ek sundar banglo che.*
>
> There is a beautiful bungalow on the river bank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બંગલો** (*banglo*) is borrowed from English *bungalow*, a word English itself took from India: it is usually traced to Hindi *baṅglā*, "in the Bengal style".
