# कोनी — not

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-KONI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *no*, the way you turn down a price: **कोनी।** Then say *I drink
tea*: **हूं चाय पीऊं हूं।**

## Grammar Lens: कोनी before the verb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-VERB-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-KONI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01, MW-LEX-C58-VERBS58-03] -->

The word you refuse with also turns a sentence round. Put **कोनी** in front of the
verb, and let the **हूं** or **है** at the end go:

> **हूं चाय कोनी पीऊं।**
>
> *hū̃ chāy konī pīū̃.* — I don't drink tea.

> **वो मांस कोनी खावै।**
>
> *vo mā̃s konī khāvai.* — He doesn't eat meat.

## Grammar Lens: कोनी in place of है
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-ISNOT-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-03, MW-LEX-C82-PLACE82-01, MW-LEX-RAAM-01] -->

With no other verb, **कोनी** stands where **है** stood:

> **राम आज अठै कोनी।**
>
> *rām āj aṭhai konī.* — Ram isn't here today.
