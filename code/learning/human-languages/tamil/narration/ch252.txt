Tamil, chapter 252: Mark, University, Education, Mathematics, Science.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
மதிப்பெண் (matippeṇ) — a mark, a score.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a sale, then the Tamil for an employer.

You'll want to know: மதிப்பெண்.
மதிப்பெண் — matippeṇ — "a mark, a score".

The word, taken apart.
மதிப்பெண் (matippeṇ) is matippu, "value, worth", and native eṇ, "number" (DEDR 793): "the value number". The origin of matippu is uncertain.
