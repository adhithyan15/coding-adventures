## You'll want to know: गुर्दा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C478-PHEPHDA478-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गुर्दा** — *gurdā* — "a kidney".

> हर आदमी के दो गुर्दे होते हैं।
>
> *har ādmī ke do gurde hote haiṁ.*
>
> Every person has two kidneys.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुर्दा** (*gurdā*) is usually traced to Persian *gurda*, "kidney".
