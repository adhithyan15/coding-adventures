Malayalam, chapter 97: Beautiful, and Not.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) — beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say this is easy. You built that sentence one chapter ago.

You'll want to know.
സുന്ദരം — sundaraṁ — beautiful.
ഇത് സുന്ദരമാണ്. — itŭ sundaramāṇŭ — "This is beautiful."

What you've built.
Read what you did there. The word ends in ം, so it welds onto ആണ് the way the last chapter's two words did, and you did it without being told.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
ഇത് എളുപ്പമാണ്, this is easy.
ഇത് സുന്ദരമാണ്, this is beautiful.
That is what a frame is for. The chapter before this one cost you a rule; this one cost you a word, and the weld came free.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What is beautiful? (സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ).) Did you need a new rule to say this is beautiful? (No — it ends in ം, like the last two.)


Lesson 2 of 4.
ഭംഗി (bhaṅgi) — beauty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say this is beautiful. Then say child — you have had that word a long time.

You'll want to know.
ഭംഗി — bhaṅgi — beauty, the thing itself rather than the quality.
ഇത് ഭംഗിയാണ്. — itŭ bhaṅgiyāṇŭ — "This is beauty."

The word, taken apart.
This one does not end in ം, and it shows. Read the two welds side by side:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) becomes സുന്ദരമാണ്, a മ appears.
ഭംഗി (bhaṅgi) becomes ഭംഗിയാണ്, a യ appears.
You have seen the second one before, on a word you learned long ago: ഇത് എന്റെ കുട്ടിയാണ് — this is my child.
Neither word loses anything. A letter arrives to carry the vowel of ആണ്, and which letter arrives depends on how the word ended. That is the whole difference.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What is beauty? (ഭംഗി (bhaṅgi).) Which letter appears when ആണ് follows it? (യ.) And after a word ending in ം? (മ.)


Lesson 3 of 4.
ഇത് സുന്ദരം അല്ല (itŭ sundaraṁ alla) — this is not beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say this is beautiful. Then say the word that denies being what you were called.

Grammar Lens: the denial stands apart.
ഇത് സുന്ദരം അല്ല. — itŭ sundaraṁ alla — "This is not beautiful."
Look at what did not happen. അല്ല did not weld onto the word in front of it, the way ആണ് did. It stands on its own, after the thing being denied — exactly as you first met it:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
ഞാൻ അധ്യാപകൻ അല്ല, I am not a teacher.
ഇത് സുന്ദരം അല്ല (itŭ sundaraṁ alla), this is not beautiful.
Same shape, different subject. The word being denied sits in the middle, unchanged and unwelded, and സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) keeps its ം because nothing attached to it.

What you've built.
Both ends of the same line, from one new word and one you already had.
This is how the far end of most qualities will arrive for a while: not by a second word, but by denying the first one. It is what a learner can actually use early, and it is what a Malayalam speaker often says anyway.

How do you say this is not beautiful? (ഇത് സുന്ദരം അല്ല (itŭ sundaraṁ alla).) Does അല്ല weld onto the word before it? (No — it stands apart.) So what happens to the ം? (Nothing.)


Lesson 4 of 4.
(beautiful, and not) (sundaravuṁ allāthatuṁ enna ōrmma) — from cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Close the lessons before this one. Say this is beautiful, then deny it.

Grammar Lens: which letter arrives.
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
word: എളുപ്പം. joined to ആണ്: എളുപ്പമാണ്, മ arrives.
word: സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ). joined to ആണ്: സുന്ദരമാണ്, മ arrives.
word: കുട്ടി. joined to ആണ്: കുട്ടിയാണ്, യ arrives.
word: ഭംഗി (bhaṅgi). joined to ആണ്: ഭംഗിയാണ്, യ arrives.
Nothing is lost in any of the four. A letter arrives to carry the vowel of ആണ്, and which one depends on how the word ended — nothing else.

What you've built.
ഇത് സുന്ദരമാണ്. — this is beautiful. ഇത് സുന്ദരം അല്ല (itŭ sundaraṁ alla). — this is not beautiful.
The second is not a second word. അല്ല stands apart, the way it has since you first met it, and സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) keeps its ം because nothing attached.
So one new quality gave you both ends of a line. That is how the far end of most qualities will arrive for a while — by denying the near one, which is both what you can build today and what a speaker often says anyway.

Wrap-up Recall.

Beautiful, and beauty? (സുന്ദരം, ഭംഗി (bhaṅgi).) Which letter arrives on each before ആണ്? (മ and യ.) And how do you deny it? (ഇത് സുന്ദരം അല്ല (itŭ sundaraṁ alla) — അല്ല standing apart.)
