## Script — the fifth standing vowel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-U-INDEPENDENT-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-UU-MATRA-01] -->

> **उ** — *u* — the vowel that hangs under a consonant, standing up

Since chapter 13 the hand has hooked **ु** beneath a letter, and since chapter 6
the longer **ू**. Neither can begin a word.

**उ** is the fifth vowel this book has had to stand up — after **आ**, **ई**,
**ए** and **अ** — and it is the last one the numbers ask for. Nineteen needs it,
and nineteen is the last of the teens.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-U-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **उ** three times. Write **ु** under a consonant beside it and say which of
the two can open a word. Then write the five standing vowels in a row — **अ आ इ
ई** and **उ** — and say each aloud.
