## You'll want to know: 成家
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C399-ONLYIF399-05]; assesses=[] -->

**成家** — *chéngjiā* — "to marry and settle down".

> **只要找到好工作他就想成家** *zhǐyào zhǎodào hǎo gōngzuò tā jiù xiǎng chéngjiā* — As long as he finds a good job, he wants to marry and settle down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**成家** (*chéngjiā*) is built from 成 'to complete' and 家 'a home', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a dwelling'.
