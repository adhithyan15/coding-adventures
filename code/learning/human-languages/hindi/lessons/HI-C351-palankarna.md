## You'll want to know: पालन करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C351-ADHIKAR351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पालन करना** — *pālan karnā* — "to abide by, to follow (a rule)".

> हमें नियमों का पालन करना चाहिए।
>
> *hamẽ niyamoṁ kā pālan karnā cāhiye.*
>
> We should follow the rules.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नियम का पालन किया, *usne niyam kā pālan kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पालन करना** (*pālan karnā*) joins पालन to करना, "to do". पालन is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pālana*, "guarding, protecting" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for keeping to a rule.
