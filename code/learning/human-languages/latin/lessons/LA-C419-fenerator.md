## You'll want to know: fēnerātor, fēnerātōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C419-PERSOLVO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**fēnerātor, fēnerātōris** — "a moneylender".

> Fēnerātor ā pauperibus pecūniam repetit.
>
> *The moneylender demands his money back from the poor.*

*fēnerātor, fēnerātōris* m., also spelled *faenerātor*, built on *fēnus*: a man who lends at interest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēnerātor* is built on *fēneror* "to lend at interest" (Lewis & Short), which comes from *fēnus* "interest".
