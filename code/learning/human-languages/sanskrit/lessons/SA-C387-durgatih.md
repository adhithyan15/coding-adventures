## You'll want to know: दुर्गतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C387-UNNATI387-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्गतिः** — *durgatiḥ* — "hard times; misfortune".

> यदि कार्यं न अस्ति, तर्हि ग्रामस्य दुर्गतिः भवति।
>
> *yadi kāryaṃ na asti, tarhi grāmasya durgatiḥ bhavati.*
>
> If there is no work, the village falls on hard times.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्गतिः** (*durgatiḥ*) is built from *dur-*, "bad, hard", and *gati*, "a going, a way", from the root √gam, "to go"; MW gives *dur-gati* "misfortune, distress, poverty" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
