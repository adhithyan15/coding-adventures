# っ — one small held beat

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-YUKKURI, JA-LEX-ITTE-KUDASAI] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say *yukkuri* and *itte kudasai*. Hold the silence before each
second consonant; do not add a vowel.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **っ** — small *tsu* — one beat of consonant closure

It has the same family shape as full-sized **つ**, but it is smaller and sits
lower. Copy only the small form today. In **ゆっくり**, it holds before *k*;
in **いって**, it holds before *t*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01, JA-LEX-YUKKURI, JA-LEX-ITTE-KUDASAI] -->

1. Trace **っ** once and copy it once.
2. Hide it; write the small sign after ten seconds.
3. Read **ゆっくり** and **いって** with one silent beat at **っ**.
