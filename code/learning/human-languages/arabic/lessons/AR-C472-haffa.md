## You'll want to know: حافة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C472-QIMMA472-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حافة** — *ḥāffa* — "an edge; a rim".

> وقفنا على حافة الوادي.
>
> *waqafnā ʿalā ḥāffat al-wādī.*
>
> We stood on the edge of the valley.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حافة** (*ḥāffa*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ḥ-f-f*, "to surround, to border".
