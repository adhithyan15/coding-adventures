## You'll want to know: استثمر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C383-IQTISAD383-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استثمر** — *istathmara* — "to invest".

> استثمر عمي ماله في شركة صغيرة.
>
> *istathmara ʿammī mālahu fī sharika ṣaghīra.*
>
> My uncle invested his money in a small company.

Present: يستثمر, *yastathmir*.

The noun استثمار, *istithmār*, an investment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استثمر** (*istathmara*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-m-r*, "to bear fruit" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek fruit from a thing: to invest.
