## You'll want to know: liúxuè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C232-HURT232-03]; assesses=[] -->

*liúxuè* — "to bleed".

> *nǐ de shǒu liúxuè le* — Your hand's bleeding.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liúxuè* is built from *liú* 'to flow' and *xuè* 'blood', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljuw* and *xwet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as water moving.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
