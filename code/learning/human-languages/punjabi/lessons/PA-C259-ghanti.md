## You'll want to know: ਘੰਟੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C259-PHONE259-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੰਟੀ** — *ghaṇṭī* — "a bell; a ring".

> ਫ਼ੋਨ ਦੀ ਘੰਟੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਉੱਚੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *fon dī ghaṇṭī bahut uchchī hai.*
>
> The phone's ring is very loud.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੰਟੀ** (*ghaṇṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ghaṇṭikā*, "a small bell", beside *ghaṇṭā*, "bell"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ghaṇḍā*, "a bell used to attract deer", under it (CDIAL 4421).
