## You'll want to know: દાહ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C483-VEDNA483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**દાહ** — *dāh* — "a burning feeling".

> તાવમાં આખા શરીરમાં દાહ થાય છે.
>
> *tāvmā̃ ākhā śarīrmā̃ dāh thāy che.*
>
> With a fever the whole body feels as if it is burning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાહ** (*dāh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dāha*, "burning, heat", as Monier-Williams records it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
