Malayalam, chapter 514: Drought and Power Cuts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വരൾച്ച (varaḷcca) — a drought.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to be uprooted, then the Malayalam for to be in distress.

You'll want to know: വരൾച്ച.
വരൾച്ച — varaḷcca — "a drought".
വരൾച്ച കാരണം കർഷകർ ബുദ്ധിമുട്ടി. varaḷcca kāraṇaṁ karṣakar buddhimuṭṭi. The farmers suffered because of the drought.
The noun of വരളുക, to dry up.

The word, taken apart.
വരൾച്ച (varaḷcca) is native Dravidian, the noun of വരളുക, to grow dry (DEDR 5320).

Lesson 2 of 5.
വിണ്ടുകീറുക (viṇṭukīṟuka) — to crack open.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: വിണ്ടുകീറുക.
വിണ്ടുകീറുക — viṇṭukīṟuka — "to crack open".
വരൾച്ചയിൽ വയലുകൾ വിണ്ടുകീറി. varaḷccayil vayalukaḷ viṇṭukīṟi. In the drought the fields cracked open.
Past: വിണ്ടുകീറി, viṇṭukīṟi.
വിണ്ട്, cracking, plus കീറുക, to tear.

The word, taken apart.
വിണ്ടുകീറുക (viṇṭukīṟuka) is a native Dravidian compound built on വിള്ളുക, to burst open or crack (DEDR 5432), and കീറുക, to tear.

Lesson 3 of 5.
കരിയുക (kariyuka) — to get scorched; to wither.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a drought, then the Malayalam for to crack open.

You'll want to know: കരിയുക.
കരിയുക — kariyuka — "to get scorched; to wither".
കടുത്ത വെയിലിൽ ചെടികൾ കരിഞ്ഞു. kaṭutta veyilil ceṭikaḷ kariññu. In the fierce sun the plants were scorched.
Past: കരിഞ്ഞു, kariññu.
For plants and food: ചോറ് കരിഞ്ഞു, the rice got burnt.

The word, taken apart.
കരിയുക (kariyuka) is native Dravidian, "to be scorched, to be singed", from the same root as കരി, charcoal (DEDR 1278).

Lesson 4 of 5.
മുറിഞ്ഞുപോകുക (muṟiññupōkuka) — to be disconnected; to break off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to crack open, then the Malayalam for to get scorched.

You'll want to know: മുറിഞ്ഞുപോകുക.
മുറിഞ്ഞുപോകുക — muṟiññupōkuka — "to be disconnected; to break off".
സംസാരിക്കുന്നതിനിടെ ഫോൺ മുറിഞ്ഞുപോയി. saṁsārikkunnatiniṭe phōṇ muṟiññupōyi. In the middle of talking, the phone call was cut off.
Past: മുറിഞ്ഞുപോയി, muṟiññupōyi.
മുറിഞ്ഞ്, being cut, plus പോകുക: for calls, power and roads.

The word, taken apart.
മുറിഞ്ഞുപോകുക (muṟiññupōkuka) is a native Dravidian compound of മുറിയുക, to be cut or broken (DEDR 5008), and പോകുക, to go.

Lesson 5 of 5.
നിലയ്ക്കുക (nilaykkuka) — to stop working; to cease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to get scorched, then the Malayalam for to be disconnected.

You'll want to know: നിലയ്ക്കുക.
നിലയ്ക്കുക — nilaykkuka — "to stop working; to cease".
വൈദ്യുതി പോയപ്പോൾ ഫാൻ നിലച്ചു. vaidyuti pōyappōḷ phān nilaccu. When the power went, the fan stopped.
Past: നിലച്ചു, nilaccu.
For machines, sounds and breath: ശബ്ദം നിലച്ചു, the sound stopped.

The word, taken apart.
നിലയ്ക്കുക (nilaykkuka) is native Dravidian, from the root of നിൽക്കുക, to stand; DEDR gives it as to come to a stand, to cease (DEDR 3675).
