## You'll want to know: हमखास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C553-DHYEY553-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हमखास** — *hamkhās* — "without fail; surely".

> तो दर रविवारी हमखास आईला फोन करतो.
>
> *to dar ravivārī hamkhās āīlā phon karto.*
>
> He calls his mother every Sunday without fail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हमखास** (*hamkhās*) is a loan from Persian, built from *ham*, "also", and Arabic *khāṣṣ*, "special".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
