## You'll want to know: mola, molae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C352-SUBIGO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**mola, molae** — "a millstone; a mill; salted meal".

> Asinus molam in pistrīnō vertēbat.
>
> *A donkey was turning the millstone in the bakery.*

Feminine; the plural *molae* is a mill. *Mola salsa*, salted meal, was sprinkled on victims. English *molar*, the grinding tooth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mola** is inherited from the Proto-Indo-European root *\*melh₂-*, "to grind" (EtymDB). English *molar* comes from it, and English *meal*, "flour", is a cousin.
