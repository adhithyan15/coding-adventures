## You'll want to know: a comemoração
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C318-FESTEJAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a comemoração** — "the commemoration, the celebration".

> A comemoração terminou com um concerto.
>
> *The celebration ended with a concert.*

Built on *comemorar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comemoração* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commemorātiō* "a calling to mind" (EtymDB), from *commemorāre*.
