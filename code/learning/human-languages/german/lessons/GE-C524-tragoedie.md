## You'll want to know: die Tragödie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C524-FILM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Tragödie** — "tragedy".

> Das Theater spielt eine Tragödie aus der Antike.
>
> *The theatre is staging a tragedy from ancient times.*

Plural: *die Tragödien*. Also a real disaster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tragödie* comes from Latin *tragoedia*, from Greek *tragōidía*, built on *trágos* "male goat" and *ōidḗ* "song" (EtymDB).
