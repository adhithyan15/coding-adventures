## You'll want to know: பள்ளி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C105-WORK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**பள்ளி** (*paḷḷi*) — "a school".

**பள்ளிக்கூடம்** is the fuller word; **பள்ளி** alone is what people say.
