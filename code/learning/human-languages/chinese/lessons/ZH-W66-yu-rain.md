## Script — a lid, a frame and four drops
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YU-RAIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 雨

**One piece, built from the top down.**

**A horizontal** across the top, the lid.

**A frame, two strokes**: a short vertical down the left, then a horizontal that turns down the right side and hooks to the left.

**A vertical** down the middle from the lid.

**Four dots**, two on the left of the middle line, then two on the right.

1 + 2 + 1 + 4 = eight.
