## You'll want to know: cancellare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C401-FILE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cancellare** — "to delete, to rub out; to cancel".

> Se cancelli la foto, non la trovi più.
>
> *If you delete the photo, you will not find it again.*

Also to cancel: *il volo è stato cancellato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cancellare* comes from Latin *cancellāre* "to make like a lattice, to cross out" (Lewis & Short), from *cancellī* "a lattice, bars": lines drawn across a text. The word *cancello* "gate" shares it.
