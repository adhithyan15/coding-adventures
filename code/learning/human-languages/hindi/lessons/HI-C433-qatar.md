## You'll want to know: क़तार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C433-QATAR433-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क़तार** — *qatār* — "a queue; a row".

> टिकट घर के सामने लंबी क़तार थी।
>
> *ṭikaṭ ghar ke sāmne lambī qatār thī.*
>
> There was a long queue in front of the ticket office.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़तार** (*qatār*) is usually traced to Arabic *qiṭār*, "a line of camels tied one behind another", through Persian: people waiting in line, one behind another.
