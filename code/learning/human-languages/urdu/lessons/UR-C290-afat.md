## You'll want to know: آفت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C290-AILAN290-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آفت** — *āfat* — "a disaster; a calamity".

> یہ ایک بڑی آفت تھی۔
>
> *yih ek baṛī āfat thī.*
>
> This was a great disaster.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آفت** (*āfat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *āfa*, "a blight, a misfortune". It is feminine: **بڑی آفت**.
