## You'll want to know: वाहिनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C312-VAHINI312-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहिनी** — *vāhinī* — "a channel".

> ही वाहिनी दिवसभर फक्त बातम्या दाखवते.
>
> *hī vāhinī divasbhar phakta bātamyā dākhavte.*
>
> This channel shows only news all day.

Feminine.

वृत्तवाहिनी, a news channel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहिनी** (*vāhinī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vāhinī*, 'an army, a host' (Monier-Williams), a word built on *vah*, 'to carry'; the sense 'a channel' is modern.
