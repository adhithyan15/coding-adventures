## You'll want to know: पर्याप्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C388-PARYAPT388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्याप्त** — *paryāpt* — "sufficient".

> अगर खाना पर्याप्त नहीं होगा तो और बनाना पड़ेगा।
>
> *agar khānā paryāpt nahīṃ hogā to aur banānā paṛegā.*
>
> If there isn't enough food, we'll have to make more.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्याप्त** (*paryāpt*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *paryāpta*, "complete; sufficient" (Monier-Williams).
