## You'll want to know: a gabardina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C466-CHUVA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a gabardina** — "the raincoat".

> Saí de gabardina porque estava a chover.
>
> *I went out in my raincoat because it was raining.*

In Brazil: *a capa de chuva*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gabardina* is usually traced to Spanish *gabardina* 'long cloak'; its earlier origin is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
