## You'll want to know: o vilão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C325-HEROI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o vilão** — "the villain".

> O vilão do filme é muito engraçado.
>
> *The villain in the film is very funny.*

A woman is *a vilã*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vilão* comes from Late Latin *vīllānus* "a farm worker" (EtymDB), from *vīlla* "a farm": a peasant, later a scoundrel.
