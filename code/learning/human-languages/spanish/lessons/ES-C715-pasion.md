## You'll want to know: la pasión
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C715-AMOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la pasión** — "passion".

> Baila con pasión.
>
> *She dances with passion.*

*Tener pasión por algo* — to be passionate about something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pasión* is a learned borrowing from Latin *passiō* "suffering" (EtymDB), built on *patī* "to suffer"; strong feeling was something you suffered.
