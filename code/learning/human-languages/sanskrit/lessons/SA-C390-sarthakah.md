## You'll want to know: सार्थकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C390-LAKSHANA390-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सार्थकः** — *sārthakaḥ* — "worthwhile; meaningful".

> यदि प्रयत्नः फलं ददाति, तर्हि सः सार्थकः।
>
> *yadi prayatnaḥ phalaṃ dadāti, tarhi saḥ sārthakaḥ.*
>
> If an effort bears fruit, it is worthwhile.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सार्थकः** (*sārthakaḥ*) is built from *sa*, "with", and *artha*, "meaning, use"; MW gives *sārthaka* "having meaning, significant" and "serviceable, useful" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
