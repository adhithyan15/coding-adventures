# నెమరువేయు (nemaruvēyu) — to go over again; to chew the cud

## You'll want to know: నెమరువేయు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C511-GNAPAKAM511-04]; assesses=[] -->

**నెమరువేయు** — *nemaruvēyu* — "to go over again; to chew the cud".

> ఆ సంతోషకరమైన రోజును నేను మళ్ళీ మళ్ళీ నెమరువేశాను.
>
> *ā santōṣakaramaina rōjunu nēnu maḷḷī maḷḷī nemaruvēśānu.*
>
> I went over that happy day again and again.

Past (I …): నెమరువేశాను, *nemaruvēśānu*.

First said of a cow chewing the cud, then of turning a memory over and over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నెమరువేయు** (*nemaruvēyu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3595 lists Telugu *nemaru*, "rumination, chewing the cud", beside *namalu*, "to chew"; the verb *vēyu*, "to put", makes it an action.

## What you've built
