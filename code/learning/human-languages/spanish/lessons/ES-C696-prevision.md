## You'll want to know: la previsión
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C696-CLIMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la previsión** — "the outlook, the forecast; foresight".

> La previsión para mañana es buena.
>
> *The forecast for tomorrow is good.*

The verb is *prever*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*previsión* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *praevīsiō* "foresight" (EtymDB), from *praevidēre* "to foresee", built on *vidēre* "to see".
