## You'll want to know: সহানুভূতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C352-BOISHOMMO352-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সহানুভূতি** — *sôhānubhūti* — "sympathy".

> সবাই মীরাকে সহানুভূতি জানাল।
>
> *sôbāi mīrāke sôhānubhūti jānālô.*
>
> Everyone offered Mira their sympathy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সহানুভূতি** (*sôhānubhūti*) is a later compound of Sanskrit *saha*, with, and *anubhūti*, perception or feeling (Monier-Williams): feeling along with someone.
