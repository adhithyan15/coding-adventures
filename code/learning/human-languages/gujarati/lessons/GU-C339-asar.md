## You'll want to know: અસર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C339-ASAR339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અસર** — *asar* — "an effect".

> દવાની અસર બે કલાક પછી થઈ.
>
> *davānī asar be kalāk pachī thaī.*
>
> The medicine took effect two hours later.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અસર** (*asar*) is usually traced to Arabic *athar*, "a trace, a mark", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Gujarati uses it for what one thing does to another.
