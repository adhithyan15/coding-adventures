## You'll want to know: تردد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C369-NASIHA369-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تردد** — *taraddada* — "to hesitate".

> تردد الولد قبل أن يقفز في الماء.
>
> *taraddada al-walad qabla an yaqfiza fī al-māʾ.*
>
> The boy hesitated before jumping into the water.

Present: يتردد, *yataraddad*.

Also to go often: تردد على السوق, went to the market often.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تردد** (*taraddada*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-d-d*, "to send back" (Lane). It is Form V, to turn back and forth: to waver, to hesitate.
