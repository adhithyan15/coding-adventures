## You'll want to know: fēijī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-03]; assesses=[] -->

*fēijī* — "aeroplane".

> *wǒmen zuò fēijī qù Běijīng* — We're flying to Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēijī* is built from fēi 'to fly' and 机 'machine', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pj+j* and *kj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a flying machine.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
