## You'll want to know: tolerar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C382-PROIBIDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tolerar** — "to tolerate, to put up with".

> Não tolero barulho depois da meia-noite.
>
> *I do not put up with noise after midnight.*

*A tolerância* — tolerance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tolerar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tolerāre* "to bear, to endure, to sustain" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).
