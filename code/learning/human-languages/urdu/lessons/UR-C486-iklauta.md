## You'll want to know: اکلوتا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C486-GROWUP486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اکلوتا** — *iklautā* — "only (of a child)".

> وہ اپنے ماں باپ کا اکلوتا بیٹا ہے۔
>
> *voh apne māṅ bāp kā iklautā beṭā hai.*
>
> He is his parents' only son.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اکلوتا** (*iklautā*) is inherited from *ēkkalaputra*, "an only son", a form CDIAL reconstructs from the words for "alone" and "son" (CDIAL 2507).
