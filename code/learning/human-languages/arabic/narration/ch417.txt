Arabic, chapter 417: Clear or vague.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غامض (ghāmiḍ) — vague.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for insurance, then the Arabic for apart from.

You'll want to know: غامض.
غامض — ghāmiḍ — "vague".
كان جوابه غامضا. kāna jawābuhu ghāmiḍan. His answer was vague.

The word, taken apart.
غامض (ghāmiḍ) is built on the native Arabic root gh-m-ḍ, "to be hidden, unapparent" (Lane). Ghāmiḍ is the active participle: what is hidden, obscure, vague.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for frank, then the Arabic for specific.

You'll want to know: اتضح.
اتضح — ittaḍaḥa — "to become evident".
اتضح أن الطريق مغلق. ittaḍaḥa anna aṭ-ṭarīq mughlaq. It became evident that the road was closed.
Present: يتضح, yattaḍiḥ.

The word, taken apart.
اتضح (ittaḍaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root w-ḍ-ḥ, "to be clear, plain" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to make itself plain: to become evident. واضح, clear, is from the same root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
أشار (ashāra) — to refer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for specific, then the Arabic for to become evident.

You'll want to know: أشار.
أشار — ashāra — "to refer".
أشار المعلم إلى خطأ في الجملة. ashāra al-muʿallim ilā khaṭaʾ fī al-jumla. The teacher drew attention to a mistake in the sentence.
Present: يشير, yushīr.
It takes إلى: أشار (ashāra) إلى ….

The word, taken apart.
أشار (ashāra) is built on the native Arabic root sh-w-r, "to take honey; to give a sign" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make a sign to someone: to refer, to indicate.
