## You'll want to know: तलवार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C322-HATHIYAR322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तलवार** — *talvār* — "a sword".

> राजा ने तलवार उठाई।
>
> *rājā ne talvār uṭhāī.*
>
> The king raised his sword.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तलवार** (*talvār*) goes back to Sanskrit *taravāri*, "a sword" (CDIAL 5706), which CDIAL gives with a Hindi *talwār*. It is feminine: **तलवार उठाई**.
