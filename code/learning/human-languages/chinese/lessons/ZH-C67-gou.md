## You'll want to know: gǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C67-ANIMALS67-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gǒu* — "dog".

> *nǐ xǐhuan gǒu ma* — Do you like dogs?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǒu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'dog'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
