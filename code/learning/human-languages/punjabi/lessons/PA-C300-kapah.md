## You'll want to know: ਕਪਾਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C300-CROP300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਪਾਹ** — *kapāh* — "raw cotton; the cotton plant".

> ਸਾਡੇ ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਪਾਹ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍe khet vich kapāh hai.*
>
> There is cotton in our field.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਪਾਹ** (*kapāh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karpāsa*, "the cotton plant"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kapāh*, "cotton plant, raw cotton", under it (CDIAL 2877).
