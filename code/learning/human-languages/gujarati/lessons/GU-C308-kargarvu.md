## You'll want to know: કરગરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C308-SAHAS308-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કરગરવું** — *kargarvũ* — "to plead; to beg".

> છોકરાની માતા લોકોને મદદ માટે કરગરતી હતી.
>
> *chokrānī mātā lokone madad māṭe kargartī hatī.*
>
> The boy's mother was pleading with people for help.

Past (she …): તે માતા કરગરી, *te mātā karagrī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કરગરવું** (*kargarvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. The person you plead with takes **ને**: **લોકોને કરગરતી હતી**.
