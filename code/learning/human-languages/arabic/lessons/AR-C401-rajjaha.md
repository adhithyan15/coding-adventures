## You'll want to know: رجح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C401-IHTIMAL401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رجح** — *rajjaḥa* — "to consider likely".

> يرجح الأطباء أن السبب هو التوتر.
>
> *yurajjiḥu al-aṭibbāʾ anna as-sabab huwa at-tawattur.*
>
> Doctors consider it likely that the cause is stress.

Present: يرجح, *yurajjiḥ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رجح** (*rajjaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-j-ḥ*, "to outweigh (of a scale)" (Lane). It is Form II, to make one pan of the scale outweigh the other: to judge a thing more likely.

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
