## You'll want to know: весло
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C426-VESLO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**весло** (*vesló*) — "an oar, a paddle".

> У лодки было только одно весло.
>
> *U lódki býlo tólko odnó vesló.*
>
> The boat had only one oar.

Neuter; plural *вёсла*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*весло* is inherited through Old East Slavic *veslo* from Proto-Slavic *veslo* 'an oar' (EtymDB).
