## You'll want to know: एकान्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C456-SAMA456-05]; assesses=[] -->

**एकान्तः** — *ekāntaḥ* — "a secluded place; solitude".

> एकान्ते मनः शान्तं भवति।
>
> *ekānte manaḥ śāntaṃ bhavati.*
>
> In a quiet place the mind grows calm.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एकान्तः** (*ekāntaḥ*) is built from *eka*, "one", and *anta*, "an end, a side": a place set to one side; MW gives "a lonely or retired or secret place" (MW).
