Italian, chapter 495: Famiglie nuove.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
adottare — to adopt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to break up, then the Italian for to remarry.

You'll want to know: adottare.
adottare — "to adopt".
Hanno adottato una bambina di due anni. They adopted a two-year-old girl.
Also for ideas: adottare un metodo.

The word, taken apart.
adottare is a learned borrowing from Latin adoptāre "to choose, to adopt" (Lewis & Short), from ad- and optāre "to choose".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la matrigna — stepmother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for adoption, then the Italian for stepfather.

You'll want to know: la matrigna.
la matrigna — "stepmother".
Con la mia matrigna parlo di tutto. I talk about everything with my stepmother.
Fairy tales gave the word a bad name; in real life many say la compagna di mio padre.

The word, taken apart.
matrigna is usually traced to Late Latin mātrīnia, built on māter "mother" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB links it to madre.

Lesson 5 of 5.
il fratellastro — half-brother, stepbrother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for stepfather, then the Italian for stepmother.

You'll want to know: il fratellastro.
il fratellastro — "half-brother, stepbrother".
Ho un fratellastro più piccolo di dieci anni. I have a half-brother ten years younger.
La sorellastra — half-sister. In other words -astro can sound negative; here it is neutral.

The word, taken apart.
fratellastro is built on fratello "brother", usually traced to Late Latin frātellus, a little form of frāter "brother" (Lewis & Short), with the ending -astro.
