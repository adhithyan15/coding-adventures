## You'll want to know: ਅੰਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C360-EXAM360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅੰਕ** — *aṅk* — "a mark in a test; a figure".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਗਣਿਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੰਗੇ ਅੰਕ ਮਿਲੇ।
>
> *mainū̃ gaṇit vich chaṅge aṅk mile.*
>
> I got good marks in mathematics.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅੰਕ** (*aṅk*) is taken over from Sanskrit *aṅka*, "mark", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
