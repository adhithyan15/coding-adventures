## You'll want to know: उपस्थित राहणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C321-PANGAT321-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उपस्थित राहणे** — *upasthit rāhṇe* — "to attend; to be present".

> कार्यक्रमाला गावातले सगळे लोक उपस्थित राहिले.
>
> *kāryakramālā gāvātle sagḷe lok upasthit rāhile.*
>
> All the people of the village attended the event.

Past (he …): तो उपस्थित राहिला, *to upasthit rāhilā*.

The event takes -ला: सभेला उपस्थित राहणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपस्थित राहणे** (*upasthit rāhṇe*) joins *upasthit*, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit *upasthita*, 'present, near at hand' (Monier-Williams), to *rāhṇe*, 'to stay, to be'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
