## You'll want to know: أزهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C468-NABAT468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أزهر** — *azhara* — "to bloom; to flower".

> أزهرت الأشجار في الربيع.
>
> *azharat al-ashjār fī ar-rabīʿ.*
>
> The trees blossomed in spring.

Present: يزهر, *yuzhir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أزهر** (*azhara*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-h-r*, "flowers; to shine" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to put out flowers"; *zahra*, "a flower", shares the root.
