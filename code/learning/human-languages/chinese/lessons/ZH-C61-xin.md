## You'll want to know: xīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C61-LONG61-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xīn* — "new".

> *zhè shì wǒ de xīn shǒujī* — This is my new phone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'new, as against old'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
