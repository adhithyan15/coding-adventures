## You'll want to know: мачеха
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C492-STEPFAMILY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**мачеха** (*máchekha*) — "a stepmother".

> В этой сказке мачеха была очень злой.
>
> *V étoy skázke máchekha bylá óchen zloy.*
>
> In this fairy tale the stepmother was very wicked.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мачеха* is usually explained as *мать* 'mother' with the ending *-еха*; EtymDB has no entry for *мачеха*, and *мать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *mati* (EtymDB).
