## You'll want to know: かいしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C229-OFFICE229-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かいしゃ** — *kaisha* — "a company; a workplace".

> **ちちは まいにち かいしゃに いきます。** — *chichi wa mainichi kaisha ni ikimasu* — "My father goes to the company every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいしゃ** (*kaisha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "meet" (Middle Chinese *hwajH*) and *sha*, "company, association" (*dzyaeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
