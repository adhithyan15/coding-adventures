# অজুহাত (ôjuhāt) — an excuse; a pretext

## You'll want to know: অজুহাত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C359-OJUHAT359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অজুহাত** — *ôjuhāt* — "an excuse; a pretext".

> দেরির অজুহাত দিও না।
>
> *derir ôjuhāt dio nā.*
>
> Don't make excuses for being late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অজুহাত** (*ôjuhāt*) is usually traced to Arabic *wujūhāt*, a plural meaning reasons or aspects, through Persian: a reason offered to get out of something.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
