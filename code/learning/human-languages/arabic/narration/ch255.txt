Arabic, chapter 255: To be late, To visit, To phone, To get better, To examine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تأخر (taʾakhkhara) — to be late.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to mix, then the Arabic for to peel.

You'll want to know: تأخر.
تأخر — taʾakhkhara — "to be late".

The word, taken apart.
تأخر (taʾakhkhara) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-kh-r, "to come after, to be late" (Lane). Taʾakhkhara is Form V, "to lag behind".
