## You'll want to know: এটিএম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C417-NOT417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**এটিএম** — *eṭiem* — "a cash machine; an ATM".

> স্টেশনের পাশের এটিএম থেকে টাকা তুললাম।
>
> *sṭeshôner pāsher eṭiem theke ṭākā tullām.*
>
> I took money out of the cash machine beside the station.

টাকা তোলা, to take money out; টাকা জমা দেওয়া, to put it in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**এটিএম** (*eṭiem*) is borrowed from English *ATM*, the letters of *automated teller machine*, said as a word.
