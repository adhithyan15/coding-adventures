Persian, chapter 305: Dawn, morning and the day after.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سپیده دم (sepide dam) — dawn, daybreak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for from then on, then the Persian for recently.

You'll want to know: سپیده دم.
سپیده دم (sepide dam) — "dawn, daybreak".
ما سپیده دم راه افتادیم. Mâ sepide dam râh oftâdim. We set off at dawn.
Literary; in speech sobh-e zud. sepide "whiteness" + dam "breath, moment".

The word, taken apart.
sepide "whiteness, the dawn" (EtymDB) is built on sepid "white", and dam here is the Persian word for "breath, moment": the moment of whiteness. EtymDB gives neither a parent.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بامداد (bâmdâd) — early morning, the small hours.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for recently, then the Persian for dawn.

You'll want to know: بامداد.
بامداد (bâmdâd) — "early morning, the small hours".
زلزله ساعت چهار بامداد اتفاق افتاد. Zelzele sâ'at-e chahâr-e bâmdâd ettefâq oftâd. The earthquake happened at four in the morning.
The news gives night-time hours with bâmdâd: sâ'at-e se-ye bâmdâd "3 a.m.".

The word, taken apart.
bâmdâd is inherited from Middle Persian bāmdād, "dawn, morning" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
فردای آن روز (fardâ-ye ân ruz) — the next day, the following day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for dawn, then the Persian for early morning.

You'll want to know: فردای آن روز.
فردای آن روز (fardâ-ye ân ruz) — "the next day, the following day".
فردای آن روز (fardâ-ye ân ruz) دوباره به بیمارستان رفتیم. Fardâ-ye ân ruz dobâre be bimârestân raftim. The next day we went to the hospital again.
In a story set in the past, fardâ alone would mean "tomorrow"; this phrase, or ruz-e ba'd, gives "the day after".

The word, taken apart.
fardâ "tomorrow", ân "that" and ruz "day" are inherited Persian; EtymDB takes ruz back through Middle Persian to Old Persian rauča-, "day".

Lesson 4 of 5.
هنگام (hengâm) — the time, the season (formal); at the time of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for early morning, then the Persian for the next day.

You'll want to know: هنگام.
هنگام (hengâm) — "the time, the season (formal); at the time of".
هنگام (hengâm) غروب به ساحل رسیدیم. Hengâm-e ghorub be sâhel residim. We reached the beach at sunset.
Formal, with the ezafe: hengâm-e shâm "at dinner time". Hengâmi ke "when" opens a clause.

The word, taken apart.
hengâm is a native Persian word. EtymDB lists it without a parent, and its deeper origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
شبانه روز (shabâne ruz) — round the clock, day and night; a full day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the next day, then the Persian for the time.

You'll want to know: شبانه روز.
شبانه روز (shabâne ruz) — "round the clock, day and night; a full day".
او در بیمارستان شبانه روز کار میکرد. U dar bimârestân shabâne ruz kâr mikard. She worked round the clock at the hospital.
Also a noun, a full twenty-four hours: do shabâne ruz "two days and nights". Built from shab "night" with -âne and ruz "day".

The word, taken apart.
shabâne ruz joins shabâne "nightly", built on shab "night", with ruz "day"; both are inherited Persian. EtymDB takes ruz back through Middle Persian to Old Persian rauča-, and sets shab beside Sanskrit kṣap- "night".
