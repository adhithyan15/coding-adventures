## You'll want to know: पास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C398-TIKITGHAR398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पास** — *pās* — "a travel pass; a season ticket".

> अरुणकडे बसचा महिन्याचा पास आहे.
>
> *aruṇkaḍe bascā mahinyācā pās āhe.*
>
> Arun has a monthly bus pass.

Masculine.

A pass lasts a week, a month or a year: आठवड्याचा पास, a weekly pass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पास** (*pās*) is a loan from English *pass*, the card that lets you travel again and again for one price.
