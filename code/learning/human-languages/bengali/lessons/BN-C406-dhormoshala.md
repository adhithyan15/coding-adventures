## You'll want to know: ধর্মশালা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C406-THAI406-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ধর্মশালা** — *dhôrmôshālā* — "a rest house for pilgrims".

> মন্দিরের পাশের ধর্মশালায় আমরা রাত কাটালাম।
>
> *mondirer pāsher dhôrmôshālāy āmrā rāt kāṭālām.*
>
> We spent the night in the rest house beside the temple.

Often free or nearly so, kept by a temple or a charitable trust.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধর্মশালা** (*dhôrmôshālā*) is Sanskrit *dharmaśālā*, "a charitable asylum" (Monier-Williams), from *dharma*, duty and merit, and *śālā*, a hall: a house kept as an act of merit.
