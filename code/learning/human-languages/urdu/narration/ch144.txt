Urdu, chapter 144: Recently, Already, the Past, Last, To Happen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حال ہی میں (hāl hī meṅ) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be saved, to avoid, then the Urdu for to grow, to increase.

You'll want to know: حال ہی میں.
حال ہی میں — hāl hī meṅ — "recently". With a verb like پڑھنا, Urdu's past puts نے after the doer: میں نے حال ہی میں ایک کتاب پڑھی۔ — maiṅ ne hāl hī meṅ ek kitāb paṛhī — "I read a book recently." پڑھی agrees with کتاب.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to grow, to increase, then the Urdu for recently.

You'll want to know: پہلے سے.
پہلے سے — pehle se — "already, beforehand". It is پہلے, "before", with سے: میں پہلے سے جانتا ہوں۔ — maiṅ pehle se jāntā hūṅ — "I already know." A woman says جانتی.

Lesson 4 of 5.
گزشتہ (guzashtā) — last, previous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for already, then the Urdu for the past.

You'll want to know: گزشتہ.
گزشتہ — guzashtā — "last, previous". گزشتہ سال — guzashtā sāl — "last year". It is the formal word; everyday speech also says پچھلا.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پیش آنا (pesh ānā) — to happen, to occur.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the past, then the Urdu for last.

You'll want to know: پیش آنا.
پیش آنا — pesh ānā — "to happen, to occur". Said of events: کل ایک مسئلہ پیش آیا۔ — kal ek mas'alā pesh āyā — "A problem came up yesterday." Its past is پیش آیا, or پیش آئی with a feminine subject.
