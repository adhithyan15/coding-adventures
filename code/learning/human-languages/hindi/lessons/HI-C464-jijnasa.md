## You'll want to know: जिज्ञासा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C464-UBNA464-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जिज्ञासा** — *jijñāsā* — "curiosity; the wish to know".

> छोटे बच्चों में बहुत जिज्ञासा होती है।
>
> *choṭe baccoṁ meṁ bahut jijñāsā hotī hai.*
>
> Small children are very curious.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जिज्ञासा** (*jijñāsā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *jijñāsā*, "the desire to know", the desiderative of *jñā*, "to know" (Monier-Williams).
