## You'll want to know: મૂલ્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C357-MULYA357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂલ્ય** — *mūlya* — "a moral value; worth".

> અમારા કુટુંબમાં સાચું બોલવું એ મોટું મૂલ્ય છે.
>
> *amārā kuṭumbmā̃ sācũ bolvũ e moṭũ mūlya che.*
>
> In our family, telling the truth matters a great deal.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂલ્ય** (*mūlya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *mūlya*, "price, worth" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati also uses it for what people hold dear.
