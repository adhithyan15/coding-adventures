## You'll want to know: निबंध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C370-VYAKHYAN370-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निबंध** — *nibandh* — "an essay".

> मी पावसाळ्यावर निबंध लिहिला.
>
> *mī pāvsāḷyāvar nibandh lihilā.*
>
> I wrote an essay on the rainy season.

Masculine.

X वर निबंध, an essay on X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निबंध** (*nibandh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nibandha*, "fastening; a literary composition" (CDIAL 7222).
