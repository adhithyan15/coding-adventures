## You'll want to know: राजकन्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C326-SAMRAT326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**राजकन्या** — *rājkanyā* — "a princess".

> राजकन्येला घोड्यावर बसायला आवडायचं.
>
> *rājkanyelā ghoḍyāvar basāylā āvḍāycaṁ.*
>
> The princess used to love riding horses.

Feminine.

राज-, royal, + कन्या, a daughter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राजकन्या** (*rājkanyā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *rājakanyā*, 'a king's daughter' (Monier-Williams).
