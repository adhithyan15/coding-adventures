## You'll want to know: dàiyù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C486-JOBHUNT486-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dàiyù* — "pay and conditions (of a job)".

> *zhè jiā gōngsī de dàiyù bǐ nà jiā hǎo* — This company pays better than that one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàiyù* is built from *dài* 'to treat' and *yù* 'to meet', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dojX* and *ngjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to wait for' and the second 'to meet without arranging it'; the sense 'how one is treated at work' is not given there.
