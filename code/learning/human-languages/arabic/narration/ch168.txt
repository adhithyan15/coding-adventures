Arabic, chapter 168: Arm, Body, Ankle, Throat, Headache.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ذراع (dhirāʿ) — an arm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to escape, to run away, then the Arabic for to disappear.

You'll want to know: ذراع.
ذراع — dhirāʿ — "an arm".

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
