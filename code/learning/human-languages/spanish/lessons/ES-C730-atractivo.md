## You'll want to know: atractivo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C730-ENCANTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**atractivo** — "attractive".

> Es una oferta muy atractiva.
>
> *It is a very attractive offer.*

Also a noun: *el atractivo de la ciudad* — the city's appeal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atractivo* comes from Latin *attractīvus* (EtymDB), built on *attrahere* "to draw towards", from *trahere* "to pull".
