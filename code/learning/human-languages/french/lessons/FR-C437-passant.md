## You'll want to know: le passant
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C437-RUELLES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le passant** — "the passer-by".

> J'ai demandé mon chemin à un passant.
>
> *I asked a passer-by for directions.*

Feminine *la passante*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*passant* is the present participle of *passer* 'to pass', used as a noun (EtymDB); *passer* goes back to Vulgar Latin *passāre*, from *passus* 'a step'.
