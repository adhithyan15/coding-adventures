## You'll want to know: modificar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C662-RESERVA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**modificar** — "to change, to modify".

> ¿Puedo modificar la fecha de la reserva?
>
> *Can I change the date of the booking?*

The c becomes qu before e: *modifiqué*. Formal, common on booking sites.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modificar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *modificāre* "to set limits to, to regulate" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built from *modus* "measure" and *facere* "to make".
