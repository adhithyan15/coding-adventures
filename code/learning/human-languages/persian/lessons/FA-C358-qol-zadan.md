## You'll want to know: قل زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C358-QABLAME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قل زدن** (*qol zadan*) — "to bubble, to simmer".

> خورش آرام قل میزد.
>
> *Khoresh ârâm qol mizad.*
>
> The stew was simmering gently.

*Qol* imitates the sound of bubbling. Present *qol mizanad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qol* is a sound-word that imitates bubbling; *zadan* "to strike" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.
