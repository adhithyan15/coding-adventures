## You'll want to know: شمال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C408-SIMT408-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شمال** — *shimāl* — "the north".

> پہاڑ شہر کے شمال میں ہیں۔
>
> *pahāṛ shahr ke shimāl meṅ haiṅ.*
>
> The mountains lie to the north of the city.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شمال** (*shimāl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shamāl*, "the north".
