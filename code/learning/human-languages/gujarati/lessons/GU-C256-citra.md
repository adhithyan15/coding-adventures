## You'll want to know: ચિત્ર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C256-PAINT256-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચિત્ર** — *citra* — "picture, painting".

> **બાળકે સુંદર ચિત્ર બનાવ્યું.** — *bāḷake sundar citra banāvyũ* — "The child made a beautiful picture."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચિત્ર** (*citra*) is Sanskrit *citrá*, "conspicuous, bright, variegated", taken over as a tatsama for a picture (CDIAL 4803).
