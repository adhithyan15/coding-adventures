Persian, chapter 83: Those, This very, That very, Such, Every.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آنها (ânhâ) — those, they.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for that, then the Persian for these.

You'll want to know: آنها.
آنها — ânhâ — "those, they".

The word, taken apart.
ânhâ is ân, "that", with the plural ending -hâ: "those". Both pieces are native Persian; ân is usually traced to Middle Persian ān.
