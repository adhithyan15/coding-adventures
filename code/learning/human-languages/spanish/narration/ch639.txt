8 lessons. All 8 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 8.
obtener — to obtain, to get.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to control, then the Spanish for except.

You'll want to know: obtener.
obtener — "to obtain, to get".
Obtuvo el mejor resultado. She got the best result.
Like tener: obtengo, obtuve. More formal than conseguir.

The word, taken apart.
obtener is a learned borrowing from Latin obtinere 'to hold, to get', built from ob and tenere 'to hold', rebuilt on tener (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: abandonar.
abandonar — "to abandon, to give up; to leave".
Abandonó los estudios a los dieciocho años. He gave up his studies at eighteen.
Abandonar un proyecto — to drop a project.

The word, taken apart.
abandonar was borrowed from French abandonner, from the phrase à bandon 'under someone's power', built on a Germanic word for an order, bannan (EtymDB).

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word points back at a cause, por or para? (Por.) Which form says "if I had": tenía or tuviera? (Tuviera.) What does depende leave unsaid? (The condition; add de to name it.) Say one reason chain of your own with creo que, porque and así que.
