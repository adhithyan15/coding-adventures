## You'll want to know: いぜん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C312-REKISHI312-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いぜん** — *izen* — "formerly; in the past".

> **いぜん、ここに がっこうが ありました。** — *izen, koko ni gakkō ga arimashita* — "There used to be a school here."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いぜん** (*izen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *i*, "by means of" (Middle Chinese *yiX*) and *zen*, "before" (*dzen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
