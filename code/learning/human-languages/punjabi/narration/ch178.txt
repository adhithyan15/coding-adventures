Punjabi, chapter 178: From Eight Until Ten, Then Home.
5 lessons. You can do the first 4 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje) — one familiar number becomes a clock time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the familiar phrase ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ once. Then say the familiar number ਅੱਠ. This lesson keeps that number and adds one time word after it.

Grammar Lens: ਵਜੇ follows the whole hour.
ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje) — aṭṭh vaje — at eight o'clock.
Keep the order number + ਵਜੇ. Here ਵਜੇ marks the whole-hour time. This first step does not add half hours, minutes, or a general clock paradigm.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — hear: aṭṭh vaje; YOU SAY: ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the whole phrase once more — ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say at eight o'clock. (ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje).) Which piece follows the number? (ਵਜੇ.)
Source: Ranbir Johal, Speaking and Writing Punjabi (2018), pp. 69–70, whole-hour vocabulary and examples.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਣਾ (ghar ton sakul jana) — name where a route starts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
First retrieve ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ once. Then recall ਘਰ, ਸਕੂਲ, and ਜਾਣਾ. Keep the route in that meaning order: home, then school, then go.

Grammar Lens: ਤੋਂ follows the source.
ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਣਾ (ghar ton sakul jana) — ghar tõ sakūl jāṇā — to go from home to school.
Punjabi uses a postposition: ਤੋਂ comes after its noun. In this bounded route, ਘਰ is the source, so the pair is ਘਰ ਤੋਂ. Do not move ਤੋਂ before ਘਰ as English moves from.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — hear: ਘਰ ਤੋਂ; YOU SAY: the route starts at home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਣਾ (ghar ton sakul jana)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਣਾ (ghar ton sakul jana)]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Build to go from home to school. (ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਣਾ (ghar ton sakul jana).) Which word comes after the source noun? (ਤੋਂ.)
Source: Ranbir Johal, Speaking and Writing Punjabi (2018), pp. 67–68, from-until schedule frame.


Lesson 3 of 5.
ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੱਕ (atth vaje ton das vaje tak) — give both ends of one time span.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje), then ਦਸ ਵਜੇ. Put familiar ਤੋਂ after the starting time: ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje) ਤੋਂ.

Grammar Lens: ਤੱਕ follows the end limit.
ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੱਕ (atth vaje ton das vaje tak) aṭṭh vaje tõ das vaje takk from eight o'clock until ten o'clock.
The start is followed by ਤੋਂ; the end limit is followed by ਤੱਕ. Both are postpositions and each comes after the words it relates to. This step owns only the bounded from-until time frame.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (atth vaje) ਤੋਂ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — add: ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੱਕ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੱਕ (atth vaje ton das vaje tak)]
[once you have stopped driving — check: start + ਤੋਂ; end + ਤੱਕ]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Give the whole span from eight until ten. (ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੱਕ (atth vaje ton das vaje tak).) Which word follows the limit? (ਤੱਕ.)
Source: Ranbir Johal, Speaking and Writing Punjabi (2018), pp. 67–70, ਤੋਂ … ਤੱਕ and whole-hour schedule examples.


Lesson 4 of 5.
ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋ (Aman naal kamm karo) — name one companion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall the practice name ਅਮਨ, the familiar work word ਕੰਮ, and polite ਕਰੋ. Keep all three unchanged.

Grammar Lens: ਨਾਲ follows the companion noun.
ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋ (Aman naal kamm karo)। Aman nāl kamm karo. Work with Aman.
As with the last two forms, the postposition comes after its noun. The companion is ਅਮਨ, so the pair is ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ. This lesson teaches only the companion meaning of ਨਾਲ in this polite instruction.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — hear: ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ; YOU SAY: with Aman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋ (Aman naal kamm karo)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋ (Aman naal kamm karo)]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask politely for work with Aman. (ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋ (Aman naal kamm karo).) Which word follows the companion? (ਨਾਲ.)
Source: Ranbir Johal, Speaking and Writing Punjabi (2018), pp. 76 and 84, postpositions and ਨਾਲ.


Lesson 5 of 5.
ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ — place one step after a time.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Guided Practice: one two-sentence schedule and Wrap-up Recall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the schedule span ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੱਕ (atth vaje ton das vaje tak). Then recall the companion pair ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ. Nothing in those pieces changes.

Grammar Lens: ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ marks the later step.
ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ — das vaje tõ bāad — after ten o'clock.
Keep ਤੋਂ before ਬਾਅਦ. The time comes first, then the two-word sequence. This lesson owns only this bounded time use; it does not claim every temporal or clausal use of ਬਾਅਦ.
