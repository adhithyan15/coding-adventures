## You'll want to know: duǎnxìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C118-SEND118-04]; assesses=[] -->

*duǎnxìn* — "text message".

> *wǒ gěi māma xiě duǎnxìn* — I'm writing a text to my mum.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duǎnxìn* is built from duǎn 'short' and xìn 'letter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *twanX* and *sinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a short letter.
