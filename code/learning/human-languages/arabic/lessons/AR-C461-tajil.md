## You'll want to know: تأجيل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C461-ILGHA461-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تأجيل** — *taʾjīl* — "a postponement".

> أخبرنا المدير بتأجيل الاجتماع.
>
> *akhbaranā al-mudīr bi-taʾjīl al-ijtimāʿ.*
>
> The manager told us the meeting was postponed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تأجيل** (*taʾjīl*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-j-l*, "an appointed term" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form II, "to set a later term".
