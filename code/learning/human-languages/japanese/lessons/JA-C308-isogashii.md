## You'll want to know: いそがしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C308-MACHIGAI308-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いそがしい** — *isogashii* — "busy".

> **ちちは いつも いそがしいです。** — *chichi wa itsumo isogashii desu* — "My father is always busy."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いそがしい** (*isogashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
