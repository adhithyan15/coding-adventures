# なまける (namakeru) — to slack off; to be idle

## You'll want to know: なまける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C228-LEARN228-03]; assesses=[] -->

**なまける** — *namakeru* — "to slack off; to be idle".

> **おとうとは よく しゅくだいを なまけます。** — *otōto wa yoku shukudai o namakemasu* — "My younger brother often neglects his homework."

Polite **なまけます**, *namakemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なまける** (*namakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
