## You'll want to know: આવરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C372-VIMO372-05]; assesses=[] -->

**આવરવું** — *āvarvũ* — "to include".

> જો આ વીમો આગ અને પૂર બંનેને આવરશે તો હું તે લઈશ.
>
> *jo ā vīmo āg ane pūr bannene āvarśe to hũ te laīś.*
>
> If this policy covers both fire and flood, I will take it.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધા પ્રશ્નો આવર્યા, *teṇe badhā praśno āvaryā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આવરવું** (*āvarvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to cover, to spread over", under a reconstructed *āvarati*, "covers" (CDIAL 1414).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
