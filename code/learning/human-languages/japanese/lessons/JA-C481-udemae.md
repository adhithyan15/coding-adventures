## You'll want to know: うでまえ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C481-SKILL481-01]; assesses=[] -->

**うでまえ** — *udemae* — "skill; ability".

> **あの だいくは うでまえが いいです。** — *ano daiku wa udemae ga ii desu* — "That carpenter is very skilled."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うでまえ** (*udemae*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *ude*, "arm" and *mae*, "front" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
