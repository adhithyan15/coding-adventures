## You'll want to know: कवी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C337-DANTKATHA337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कवी** — *kavī* — "a poet".

> एका कवीने आमच्या गावावर सुंदर कविता लिहिली.
>
> *ekā kavīne āmacyā gāvāvar sundar kavitā lihilī.*
>
> A poet wrote a beautiful poem about our village.

Masculine.

Plural कवी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कवी** (*kavī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kavi*, 'a wise man, a poet' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
