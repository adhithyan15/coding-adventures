## You'll want to know: سعر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C458-DAMAN458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سعر** — *siʿr* — "a rate; a price".

> سعر الصرف في المطار مرتفع.
>
> *siʿr aṣ-ṣarf fī al-maṭār murtafiʿ.*
>
> The exchange rate at the airport is high.

Plural: أسعار, *asʿār*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سعر** (*siʿr*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-ʿ-r*, "to kindle a fire" (Lane). Lane gives *siʿr*, "a price", on the same root.
