## You'll want to know: اعتماد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C363-ANDESHA363-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتماد** — *etimād* — "confidence; trust".

> اگر اعتماد ہے تو آپ ضرور جیتیں گے۔
>
> *agar etimād hai to āp zarūr jīteṅ ge.*
>
> If you have confidence, you will certainly win.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتماد** (*etimād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iʿtimād*, "reliance, leaning on".
