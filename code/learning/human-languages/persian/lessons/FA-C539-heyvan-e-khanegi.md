## You'll want to know: حیوان خانگی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C539-GARDEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حیوان خانگی** (*heyvân-e khânegi*) — "a pet".

> ما یک گربه به عنوان حیوان خانگی داریم.
>
> *mâ yek gorbe be 'onvân-e heyvân-e khânegi dârim.*
>
> We have a cat as a pet.

*heyvân* 'animal' + *khânegi* 'of the home'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*heyvân-e khânegi* is built in Persian on *heyvân*, from Arabic *ḥayawān*, 'animal' (EtymDB), and *khânegi*, 'of the home', from native *khâne*, 'house'.
