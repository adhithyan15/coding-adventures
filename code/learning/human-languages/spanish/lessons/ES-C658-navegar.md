## You'll want to know: navegar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C658-BARCO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**navegar** — "to sail; to browse (the internet)".

> Navegamos dos días hasta la costa.
>
> *We sailed for two days to the coast.*

*Navegar por internet* — to browse the web.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navegar* comes from Latin *nāvigāre* "to sail" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built from *nāvis* "ship" and *agere* "to drive".
