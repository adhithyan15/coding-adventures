## You'll want to know: दादी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C116-GRAND-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दादी** (*dādī*) — "grandmother", your father's mother.

It pairs with **दादा**: the **-ई** ending marks the woman, as in **बच्ची** beside **बच्चा**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The father's side, both grandparents.
