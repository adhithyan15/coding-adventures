## You'll want to know: ڈاکہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C280-DAKU280-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈاکہ** — *ḍākā* — "an armed robbery".

> کل بینک میں ڈاکہ پڑا۔
>
> *kal baink meṅ ḍākā paṛā.*
>
> Yesterday the bank was robbed.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈاکہ** (*ḍākā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍākā*, "robbery, a band of robbers", under *\*ḍākka* (CDIAL 5543), the entry that also holds **ڈاکو**. A robbery is said to fall: **ڈاکہ پڑا**.
