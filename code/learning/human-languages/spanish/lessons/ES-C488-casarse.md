## You'll want to know: casarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C488-GENTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**casarse** — "to get married".

> Se casan en junio.
>
> *They are getting married in June.*

It goes with *con*: *casarse con alguien*.
