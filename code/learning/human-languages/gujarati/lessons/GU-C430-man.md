## You'll want to know: મણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C430-SCALES430-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મણ** — *maṇ* — "a maund (an old measure of weight)".

> વેપારીએ બજારમાં દસ મણ ઘઉં વેચ્યા.
>
> *vepārīe bajārmā̃ das maṇ ghaũ vecyā.*
>
> The trader sold ten maunds of wheat in the market.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મણ** (*maṇ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *maṇa*, "a measure of grain"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a maund" (CDIAL 9730). Farmers still sell grain by the maund.
