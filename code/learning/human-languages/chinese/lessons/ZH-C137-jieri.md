## You'll want to know: 节日
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C137-HOLIDAY137-01]; assesses=[] -->

**节日** — *jiérì* — "festival".

> **今天是什么节日** *jīntiān shì shénme jiérì* — What festival is it today?

The new character, in pieces: 节 — the grass piece on top, a bar crossed by two short verticals, and under it a bar that turns down into a hook, with a long vertical through it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**节日** (*jiérì*) is built from 节 'a joint' and 日 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tset* and *nyit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary quotes an older book for the first: the joint of a bamboo. A festival as a joint in the year is the likely image, though it is not given there.
