Malayalam, chapter 270: To oppose, To guess, To compare, To argue, To pay attention.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
എതിർക്കുക (etirkkuka) — to oppose, to object.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to be born, then the Malayalam for to die.

You'll want to know: എതിർക്കുക.
എതിർക്കുക — etirkkuka — "to oppose, to object".

The word, taken apart.
എതിർക്കുക (etirkkuka) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil etir, Kannada idir and Telugu eduru (DEDR 795). It is the verb of etire, "opposite".
