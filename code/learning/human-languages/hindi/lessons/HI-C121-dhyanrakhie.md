# ध्यान रखिए (dhyān rakhie) — take care

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Hindi for a person, then the Hindi for nice to meet you.

## You'll want to know: ध्यान रखिए
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C121-CARE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ध्यान रखिए** (*dhyān rakhie*) — "take care", literally "keep attention".

**अपना ध्यान रखिए** — "take care of yourself", the kindest goodbye.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The kindest thing to say at the door.
