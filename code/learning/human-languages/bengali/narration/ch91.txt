Bengali, chapter 91: Tomorrow, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আগামীকাল (āgāmikāl) — tomorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the day after tomorrow, then the Bengali for yesterday.

You'll want to know: আগামীকাল.
আগামীকাল — āgāmikāl — "tomorrow".

The word, taken apart.
আগামীকাল (āgāmikāl) is āgāmi, "coming", Sanskrit āgāmin taken over whole, + kāl, "yesterday, tomorrow", from Sanskrit kalya, "dawn" (CDIAL 3104).

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for the day after tomorrow, then the Bengali for yesterday, then say āgāmikāl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does আগামীকাল (āgāmikāl) mean? ("Tomorrow".) Say it once more.
