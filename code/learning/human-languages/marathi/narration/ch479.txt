Marathi, chapter 479: Pulse and Breath.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नाडी (nāḍī) — the pulse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the gut, then the Marathi for a joint.

You'll want to know: नाडी.
नाडी — nāḍī — "the pulse".
दवाखान्यात त्यांनी माझी नाडी तपासली. davākhānyāt tyānnī mājhī nāḍī tapāslī. At the clinic they took my pulse.
Feminine.
नाडी (nāḍī) तपासणे, to take the pulse; also a cord.

The word, taken apart.
नाडी (nāḍī) is a tatsama, Sanskrit nāḍī, "tube, vein"; CDIAL lists Marathi nāḍī, "pulse" (CDIAL 7047).

Lesson 2 of 5.
नस (nas) — a vein; a nerve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a joint, then the Marathi for the pulse.

You'll want to know: नस.
नस — nas — "a vein; a nerve".
रक्त नसांमधून वाहतं. rakta nasāmmadhūn vāhtaṁ. Blood flows through the veins.
Feminine. Plural: नसा, nasā.
Plural नसा. नस (nas) दबणे, a nerve gets pinched.

The word, taken apart.
नस (nas) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi nas, "vein, nerve, tendon", under Sanskrit snasā (CDIAL 13784).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पेशी (peśī) — a cell (of the body).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a vein, then the Marathi for the gullet.

You'll want to know: पेशी.
पेशी — peśī — "a cell (of the body)".
प्रत्येक जीव पेशींनी बनलेला असतो. pratyek jīv peśīnnī banlelā asto. Every living thing is made of cells.
Feminine.
Feminine; plural पेशी (peśī).

The word, taken apart.
पेशी (peśī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit peśī, "piece of flesh, muscle" (CDIAL 8384); modern Marathi gave it the sense "cell".

Lesson 5 of 5.
काळीज (kāḷīj) — the innermost heart; the liver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the gullet, then the Marathi for a cell.

You'll want to know: काळीज.
काळीज — kāḷīj — "the innermost heart; the liver".
ती बातमी ऐकून माझं काळीज तुटलं. tī bātmī aikūn mājhaṁ kāḷīj tuṭlaṁ. My heart broke when I heard that news.
Neuter.
Neuter: माझं काळीज (kāḷīj). काळजाचा तुकडा, a darling. काळजी, care, is often linked with it.

The word, taken apart.
काळीज (kāḷīj) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi kāḷīj, "liver, heart", under Sanskrit kāleyaka (CDIAL 3103). काळजी, care, is often linked with it, but CDIAL does not list it there.
