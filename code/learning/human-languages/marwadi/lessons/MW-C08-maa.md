# मां — a new word from known signs

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-MAA-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-MAA-01, MW-SCRIPT-MA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01, MW-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01, MW-LEX-KAIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 16s] Say *mā̃* from its meaning. Write **म**, **ा**, and **ं**; then
write **ि** and say the known question word *kāĩ*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-MAA-01, MW-SCRIPT-MA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01] -->

> **म** + **ा** + **ं** → **मां** — *mā̃* — mother

Nothing on the page is new. Join three earned units and read the complete word.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-MAA-01, MW-SCRIPT-MAA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **मां** for five seconds. Cover it, wait five seconds, then write the
word once. Uncover and compare the long-vowel sign and nasal mark separately.
