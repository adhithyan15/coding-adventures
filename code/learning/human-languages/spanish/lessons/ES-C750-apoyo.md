## You'll want to know: el apoyo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C750-CONVIVIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el apoyo** — "support".

> Gracias por tu apoyo.
>
> *Thank you for your support.*

*Apoyar* — to support.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apoyo* is built on *apoyar* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Italian *appoggiare* "to lean on", from Latin *podium* "a raised base".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
