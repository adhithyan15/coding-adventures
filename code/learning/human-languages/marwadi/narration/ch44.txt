Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 44: Foot, Head, Hair, Tooth, Throat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पग (pag) — a foot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for nineteen, then the Marwadi for no.

You'll want to know: पग.
पग — pag — "a foot".

The word, taken apart.
पग (pag) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from padga, "afoot; footsoldier". CDIAL lists Old Marwari paga under it (CDIAL 7766). It is built on pad, "foot", the same ancient word as English foot.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बाळ (bāḷ) — hair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a foot, then the Marwadi for the head.

You'll want to know: बाळ.
बाळ — bāḷ — "hair".

The word, taken apart.
बाळ (bāḷ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vāla, "hair of tail, tail, hair". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11572); the match is through Hindi bāl and Gujarati vāḷ. Marwadi, like Gujarati, says the l curled back.
