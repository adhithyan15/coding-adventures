## You'll want to know: schriftlich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C375-KLAUSUR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**schriftlich** — "written, in writing".

> Die Kündigung muss schriftlich sein.
>
> *The notice must be in writing.*

The opposite of *mündlich*. *Schriftlich und mündlich* — written and spoken.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schriftlich* is built with *-lich* on *Schrift* "writing", from Old High German *scrift*, which goes back to Latin *scrīptum* "something written" (EtymDB).
