## You'll want to know: 用意
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C372-ANYWAY372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**用意** — *yòngyì* — "intention; the purpose behind it".

> **虽然他没有说但是我明白他的用意** *suīrán tā méiyǒu shuō dànshì wǒ míngbai tā de yòngyì* — He didn't say so, but I understand what he meant by it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**用意** (*yòngyì*) is built from 用 'to use' and 意 'intent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yowngH* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to use' and the second 'will'.
