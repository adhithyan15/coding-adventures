# ഞരമ്പ് (ñarampŭ) — a vein; a nerve

## You'll want to know: ഞരമ്പ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C536-RAKTAM536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഞരമ്പ്** — *ñarampŭ* — "a vein; a nerve".

> നഴ്സ് കയ്യിലെ ഞരമ്പിൽ നിന്ന് രക്തം എടുത്തു.
>
> *naḻsŭ kayyile ñarampil ninnŭ raktaṁ eṭuttu.*
>
> The nurse took blood from a vein in my arm.

One word for a vein, a nerve and a sinew.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഞരമ്പ്** (*ñarampŭ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2903 gives it as a sinew, a tendon, a nerve and the pulse (DEDR 2903).
