## Writing

1. Award form credit for six or seven independently completed Bengali-script
   fields with internally consistent invented details. The learner may choose
   any safe name, city, language, date, meeting time, and drink; do not require
   a copied model or real phone number. A phone-like digit sequence may use
   Bengali digits, and a declared accessibility accommodation may permit
   other digits without replacing the Bengali labels and written responses.
2. The message must address অনিকা as a new classmate, introduce the writer,
   state a city, and propose a place and time for study in 30–40 Bengali
   words. Accept a natural equivalent, including a different safe meeting
   place or time. Score independent expression, not agreement with one sample.

## Speaking

1. Accept relevant short answers to all five personal questions; a chosen
   identity and safe invented details are valid.
2. The one-minute description must identify a familiar place, locate it,
   mention a companion, and say one activity. Do not require memorized wording.
3. The shop exchange must include a greeting, a drink request, a price question,
   and a polite closing. A short repair or repetition is acceptable at A1.

The learner is not required to reproduce a particular city, name, or sentence.
