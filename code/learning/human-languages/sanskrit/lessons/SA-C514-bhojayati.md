## You'll want to know: भोजयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C514-PARIVESAYATI514-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भोजयति** — *bhojayati* — "feeds".

> सा शिशुं भोजयति।
>
> *sā śiśuṃ bhojayati.*
>
> She feeds the baby.

Past (she …): सा बालं भोजितवती, *sā bālaṃ bhojitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भोजयति** (*bhojayati*) comes from the causative of the root √bhuj, "to enjoy, to eat": to make someone eat, to feed (MW).
