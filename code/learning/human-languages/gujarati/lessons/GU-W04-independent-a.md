# અ — short a standing alone

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-THA-01] -->

Write *tha* once from memory. Then say the vowel already carried by a bare
Gujarati consonant: short *a*.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **અ** visible. It is independent short *a*, used when the vowel begins a
syllable without a consonant. Sweep clockwise around the open left curve,
continue through the lower body, rise into the middle shoulder, and finish the
small right arch. Lift once; descend the separate right stem into its foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **અ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice open left curve, middle
shoulder, small arch, separate right stem; one pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **અ** once. Compare only the open curve and separate stem; repair
one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W04-independent-a-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write independent short a from memory.","answer":"અ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"અ — short a is standing as its own letter.","incorrect":"Look once at અ, hide it, and repair only the open curve or separate stem."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write independent short *a* once. Uncover **અ**, compare, and
stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] When does short *a* need **અ** instead of arriving free on a
consonant? (**When it stands without a consonant.**)
