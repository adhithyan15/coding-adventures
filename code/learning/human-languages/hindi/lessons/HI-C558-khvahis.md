## You'll want to know: ख़्वाहिश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C558-KHVAHISH558-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़्वाहिश** — *khvāhiś* — "a wish, a longing (for something)".

> दादी ने मेरी हर ख़्वाहिश पूरी की।
>
> *Dādī ne merī har khvāhiś pūrī kī.*
>
> Grandmother granted my every wish.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़्वाहिश** (*khvāhiś*) is usually traced to Persian *khwāhish*, "a wish".
