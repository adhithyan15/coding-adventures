Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 192: Danger on the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खतरो (khatro) — danger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for careful, then the Marwadi for to obey.

You'll want to know: खतरो.
खतरो — khatro — "danger".
पुल माथै खतरो है। pul māthai khatro hai. The bridge is dangerous.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
खतरो (khatro) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khaṭar, "danger".

Lesson 2 of 5.
धीरे (dhīre) — slowly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to obey, then the Marwadi for danger.

You'll want to know: धीरे.
धीरे — dhīre — "slowly".
गळी मांय धीरे चालो। gaḷī mā̃y dhīre chālo. Go slowly in the lane.

The word, taken apart.
धीरे (dhīre) is inherited from Sanskrit dhīra, "steady, resolute". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Sindhi dhīre, "quietly", and Gujarati dhīrũ, "slow" (CDIAL 6817).

Lesson 4 of 5.
छापणो (chhāpṇo) — to print.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for slowly, then the Marwadi for a pit.

You'll want to know: छापणो.
छापणो — chhāpṇo — "to print".
अखबार रोज सूचना छापै है। akhbār roj sūchnā chhāpai hai. The newspaper prints notices every day.
Past (I …): म्हैं सूचना छापी, mhaĩ sūchnā chhāpī.

The word, taken apart.
छापणो (chhāpṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed chapp, "press, cover, hide"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari chipāvaï, "hides", and Hindi chāpnā, "to print", under it (CDIAL 4994). To print is to press letters onto paper.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सुधारणो (sudhārṇo) — to mend; to correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a pit, then the Marwadi for to print.

You'll want to know: सुधारणो.
सुधारणो — sudhārṇo — "to mend; to correct".
मिस्त्री सड़क सुधारै है। mistrī saṛak sudhārai hai. The mason is mending the road.
Past (I …): म्हैं साइकल सुधारी, mhaĩ sāikal sudhārī.

The word, taken apart.
सुधारणो (sudhārṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed śuddhakāra, "making clean". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi sudhārnā, "to mend" (CDIAL 12521).
