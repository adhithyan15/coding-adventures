## You'll want to know: عرش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C309-QASR309-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عرش** — *ʿarsh* — "a throne".

> جلس الملك على العرش.
>
> *jalasa al-malik ʿalā al-ʿarsh.*
>
> The king sat on the throne.

Plural: عروش, *ʿurūsh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عرش** (*ʿarsh*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-sh* (Lane), where *ʿarasha* is "to build a roof or a trellis". A raised seat under a canopy became the throne.
