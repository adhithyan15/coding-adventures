Persian, chapter 338: Mourning and what is left.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ختم (khatm) — a memorial gathering.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to bury, then the Persian for a grave.

You'll want to know: ختم.
ختم (khatm) — "a memorial gathering".
مراسم ختم در مسجد برگزار شد. Marâsem-e khatm dar masjed bargozâr shod. The memorial service was held at the mosque.
A gathering soon after a death, often at a mosque. khatm first means "a completion".

The word, taken apart.
khatm is usually traced to Arabic khatm, "a sealing, a completion", as of a reading of the Quran for the dead. EtymDB lists the Persian word with no parent.

'Azâdâr "a mourner"; black clothes are worn in 'azâ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تسلیت گفتن (tasliyat goftan) — to offer condolences.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a memorial gathering, then the Persian for mourning.

You'll want to know: تسلیت گفتن.
تسلیت گفتن (tasliyat goftan) — "to offer condolences".
همه همکاران به او تسلیت گفتند. Hame-ye hamkârân be u tasliyat goftand. All his colleagues offered him their condolences.
The person takes be. Present stem gu-: tasliyat miguyam, in speech tasliyat migam "my condolences".

The word, taken apart.
tasliyat is usually traced to Arabic tasliya, "consolation"; EtymDB lists no entry. goftan "to say" is native Persian.

وصیت (vasiyat) — "a will, a last wish".
در وصیت خود خانه را به دخترش داد. Dar vasiyat-e khod khâne râ be dokhtarash dâd. In his will he left the house to his daughter.
Vasiyatnâme is a written will.

The word, taken apart.
vasiyat is borrowed from Arabic waṣiyya (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a bequest, a last instruction".
