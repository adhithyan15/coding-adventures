Hindi, chapter 432: Goods and Stock.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
माल (māl) — goods; stock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a sale, then the Hindi for good value.

You'll want to know: माल.
माल — māl — "goods; stock".
दुकान में नया माल आया है। dukān meṁ nayā māl āyā hai. New stock has come into the shop.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
माल (māl) is usually traced to Arabic māl, "property, wealth", through Persian; in Hindi it is what a shop has to sell. मालगाड़ी is a goods train.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for good value, then the Hindi for goods.

You'll want to know: भंडार.
भंडार — bhaṇḍār — "a store; a stock".
गाँव के भंडार में बहुत अनाज है। gā̃v ke bhaṇḍār meṁ bahut anāj hai. There is plenty of grain in the village store.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
भंडार (bhaṇḍār) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit bhāṇḍāgāra, "a treasury", literally a house for goods (CDIAL 9442), which lists Hindi bhā̃ḍār, "a storehouse".

Lesson 5 of 5.
लपेटना (lapeṭnā) — to wrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a packet, then the Hindi for a label.

You'll want to know: लपेटना.
लपेटना — lapeṭnā — "to wrap".
दुकानदार ने किताब काग़ज़ में लपेटी। dukāndār ne kitāb kāġaz meṁ lapeṭī. The shopkeeper wrapped the book in paper.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने तोहफ़ा काग़ज़ में लपेटा, usne tohfā kāġaz meṁ lapeṭā.

The word, taken apart.
लपेटना (lapeṭnā) goes back to lappēṭṭ, "wrap", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10942), which lists Hindi lapeṭnā, "to wrap, fold, wind".
