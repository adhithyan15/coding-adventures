## You'll want to know: উত্তাপ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C427-GRISHMO427-05]; assesses=[] -->

**উত্তাপ** — *uttāp* — "heat; warmth".

> আগুনের উত্তাপে ঘর গরম হল।
>
> *āguner uttāpe ghôr gôrôm hôlô.*
>
> The heat of the fire warmed the room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উত্তাপ** (*uttāp*) is Sanskrit *ut-tāpa*, "great heat, glow" (Monier-Williams), from *ut*, up, and *tāpa*, heat.
