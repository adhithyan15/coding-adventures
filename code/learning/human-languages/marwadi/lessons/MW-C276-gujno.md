## You'll want to know: गूंजणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C276-BANSLI276-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गूंजणो** — *gū̃jṇo* — "to hum; to ring out".

> मंदिर मांय गीत गूंजै है।
>
> *mandir mā̃y gīt gū̃jai hai.*
>
> A song rings out in the temple.

Past (it …): वो गीत गूंज्यो, *vo gīt gū̃jyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गूंजणो** (*gū̃jṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *guñjati*, "hums, buzzes"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *gū̃jnā*, "to hum" (CDIAL 4175). A sound that hums on fills the room.
