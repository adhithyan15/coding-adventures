## You'll want to know: گمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C538-TAZABZUB538-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گمان** — *gumān* — "a hunch; a notion".

> اگر میرا گمان ٹھیک ہے تو وہ کل آئے گا۔
>
> *agar merā gumān ṭhīk hai to voh kal āe gā.*
>
> If my hunch is right, he will come tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گمان** (*gumān*) is usually traced to Persian *gumān*, "a supposition, a doubt".

## What you've built
