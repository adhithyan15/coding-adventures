## You'll want to know: el estrés
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C710-NERVIOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el estrés** — "stress".

> El trabajo me causa estrés.
>
> *Work causes me stress.*

*Estar estresado* — to be stressed. Spanish adds an *e-* before *s* + consonant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estrés* was borrowed from English *stress*, usually dated to the twentieth century; Spanish put an *e-* in front, as it does with *esnob* and *escáner*.
