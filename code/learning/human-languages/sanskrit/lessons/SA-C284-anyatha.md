# अन्यथा (anyathā) — otherwise

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C284-CONDITIONAL-REAL-01, SA-LEX-C277-SIGHRAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "if there is time, I will come". (*yadi samayaḥ bhaviṣyati, tarhi aham āgamiṣyāmi*.) Then say "quickly". (*śīghram*.)

## You'll want to know: अन्यथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C284-ANYATHA-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C123-THINGS123-05, SA-LEX-AGACCHATI-COME, SA-LEX-C29-COURTESY-01] -->

**अन्यथा** — *anyathā* — "otherwise": if not, then this.

> **शीघ्रं गच्छतु,**
>
> *śīghraṃ gacchatu,*
>
> **अन्यथा धूमयानं गमिष्यति।**
>
> *anyathā dhūmayānaṃ gamiṣyati.* — "Go quickly, otherwise the train will leave."

> **कृपया शीघ्रम् आगच्छतु,**
>
> *kṛpayā śīghram āgacchatu,*
>
> **अन्यथा अहं गमिष्यामि।**
>
> *anyathā ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi.* — "Please come quickly, otherwise I will leave."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्यथा** (*anyathā*) is built on **अन्य** (*anya*), "other", with *-thā*, "in the way of": "in another way". Monier-Williams gives it as "otherwise, in a different manner", and also "in the contrary case", the sense here.
