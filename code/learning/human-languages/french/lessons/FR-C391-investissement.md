## You'll want to know: l'investissement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C391-EPARGNE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'investissement** — "the investment".

> Une bonne formation est un bon investissement.
>
> *Good training is a good investment.*

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investissement* is built from *investir* and *-ment*. *investir* comes from Latin *investīre* "to clothe", built on *vestis* "clothing" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
