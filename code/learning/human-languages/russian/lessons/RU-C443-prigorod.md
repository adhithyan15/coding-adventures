## You'll want to know: пригород
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C443-BRODIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**пригород** (*prígorod*) — "a suburb".

> Мы живём в пригороде и ездим в город на поезде.
>
> *My zhivyóm v prígorode i yézdim v górod na póyezde.*
>
> We live in a suburb and go into town by train.

Masculine; the stress is on *при-* (*prí-*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пригород* is built from *при-* 'near' and *город* 'a town', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *gordŭ* 'an enclosure' (EtymDB).
