Japanese, chapter 344: Making Plans.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
けいかく (keikaku) — a scheme; a detailed plan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be found out, then the Japanese for to jump to conclusions.

You'll want to know: けいかく.
けいかく — keikaku — "a scheme; a detailed plan".
もし けいかくが かわったら、れんらくして ください。 — moshi keikaku ga kawattara, renraku shite kudasai — "If the plan changes, please let me know."

The word, taken apart.
けいかく (keikaku) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kei, "plan" (Middle Chinese kejH) and kaku, "brush-stroke" (hweak) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
よういする (yōi suru) — to get things ready; to prepare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to jump to conclusions, then the Japanese for a detailed plan.

You'll want to know: よういする.
よういする — yōi suru — "to get things ready; to prepare".
あめが ふったら、かさを よういして ください。 — ame ga futtara, kasa o yōi shite kudasai — "If it rains, please have an umbrella ready."
Polite よういします, yōishimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): ははが おべんとうを よういしました。, haha ga obentō o yōi shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
よういする (yōi suru) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb suru, "to do". The noun, yōi, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: yō, "utilize" (Middle Chinese yowngH) and i, "mind" ('iH) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
