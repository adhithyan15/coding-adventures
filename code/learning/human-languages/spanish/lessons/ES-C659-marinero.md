## You'll want to know: el marinero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C659-BARCA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el marinero** — "the sailor".

> Mi abuelo era marinero.
>
> *My grandfather was a sailor.*

As an adjective it means seafaring: *un pueblo marinero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marinero* is built on *marina* (EtymDB), and through it on *mar* "sea", inherited from Latin *mare*.
