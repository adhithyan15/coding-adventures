## You'll want to know: o diagnóstico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C487-CONSULTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o diagnóstico** — "diagnosis".

> O diagnóstico foi uma simples constipação.
>
> *The diagnosis was a simple cold.*

*Fazer o diagnóstico* — to make the diagnosis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diagnóstico* is a learned word, usually traced to Greek *diagnōstikós* "able to tell apart", built on *gignṓskein* "to know".
