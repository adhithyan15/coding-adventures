## You'll want to know: fugō, fugāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C342-CLADES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**fugō, fugāre** — "to put to flight, to rout".

> Equitēs nostrī hostēs fugāvērunt.
>
> *Our cavalry put the enemy to flight.*

*fugō, fugāre, fugāvī, fugātum*: to make others run.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fugō** is built on *fuga*, "flight" (Lewis & Short): to cause to flee.
