Urdu, chapter 492: Growing Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بڑھاپا (buṛhāpā) — old age.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a divorce, then the Urdu for to forgive.

You'll want to know: بڑھاپا.
بڑھاپا — buṛhāpā — "old age".
بڑھاپے میں نیند کم ہو جاتی ہے۔ buṛhāpe meṅ nīṅd kam ho jātī hai. In old age people sleep less.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
بڑھاپا (buṛhāpā) is inherited: it is built on بوڑھا, "old", from buḍḍha, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9271), with the abstract ending -āpā.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
