## You'll want to know: ਕਤਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C253-RULE253-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਤਾਰ** — *katār* — "a queue; a line of people".

> ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੰਮੀ ਕਤਾਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *baiṅk vich lammī katār hai.*
>
> There is a long queue in the bank.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਤਾਰ** (*katār*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *qitār*, "a line of camels".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
