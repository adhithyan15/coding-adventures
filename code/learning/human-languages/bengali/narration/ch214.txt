Lesson 1 of 5.
ছবি তোলা (chhôbi tolā) — to take a photo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to set off, then the Bengali for to ride.

You'll want to know: ছবি তোলা.
ছবি তোলা — chhôbi tolā — "to take a photo".

The phrase, taken apart.
ছবি তোলা (chhôbi tolā) is chhôbi, "a picture", Sanskrit chavi, "skin, colour, beauty", taken over whole, + tolā, "to raise", from Sanskrit tōlayati, "lifts, weighs" (CDIAL 5979).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to pass, then the Bengali for to fail.

You'll want to know: ঝাঁকানো.
ঝাঁকানো — jhā̃kāno — "to shake".

The word, taken apart.
ঝাঁকানো (jhā̃kāno) (jhāṁkāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jhakk, "sudden movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5316). CDIAL lists Bengali jhāṁkan, "jerking".

You'll want to know: নাড়ানো.
নাড়ানো — nāṛāno — "to stir, to wave".

The word, taken apart.
নাড়ানো (nāṛāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from naṭati, "trembles, totters", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6934). CDIAL lists Bengali nāṛā, "to shake, agitate", and takes the group as probably Dravidian.
