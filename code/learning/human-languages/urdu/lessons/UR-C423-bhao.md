## You'll want to know: بھاؤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C423-SAUDA423-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بھاؤ** — *bhāo* — "the going rate; a price per unit".

> آج آلو کا بھاؤ کیا ہے؟
>
> *āj ālū kā bhāo kyā hai?*
>
> What are potatoes going for today?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھاؤ** (*bhāo*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bhāu*, "a price", under Sanskrit *bhāvá*, "being, state" (CDIAL 9475).
