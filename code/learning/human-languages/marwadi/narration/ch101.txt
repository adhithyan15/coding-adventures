Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 101: This, Like this, Like that, Which, Who.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
यो (yo) — this.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a fair, then the Marwadi for a weekly market.

You'll want to know: यो.
यो — yo — "this".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists Hindi yah, "this", under Sanskrit eṣa, "this" (CDIAL 2530). It gives no Marwari form, so the exact path to यो (yo) is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
इसो (iso) — like this, such.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a weekly market, then the Marwadi for this.

You'll want to know: इसो.
इसो — iso — "like this, such".

The word, taken apart.
इसो (iso) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from īdṛśa, "such, like this". CDIAL lists Old Marwari iso under it (CDIAL 1611).

Lesson 5 of 5.
कुण (kuṇ) — who.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for like that, then the Marwadi for which.

You'll want to know: कुण.
कुण — kuṇ — "who".

The word, taken apart.
कुण (kuṇ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kaḥ punar, "who". CDIAL lists Old Marwari kuṇa under it (CDIAL 2575). It is built on kaḥ, "who", and punar, "again".
