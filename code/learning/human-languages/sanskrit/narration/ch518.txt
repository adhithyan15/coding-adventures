Sanskrit, chapter 518: Colours on the Wall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तूलिका (tūlikā) — a paintbrush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a marble, then the Sanskrit for a doll.

You'll want to know: तूलिका.
तूलिका — tūlikā — "a paintbrush".
चित्रकारः नूतनां तूलिकां क्रीणाति। citrakāraḥ nūtanāṃ tūlikāṃ krīṇāti. The painter buys a new brush.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
तूलिका (tūlikā) is the word MW gives as the same as tūli, a painter's brush; MW gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
रंगः (raṅgaḥ) — paint; a dye.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a doll, then the Sanskrit for a paintbrush.

You'll want to know: रंगः.
रंगः — raṅgaḥ — "paint; a dye".
चित्रकारः रंगं जलेन संयोजयति। citrakāraḥ raṅgaṃ jalena saṃyojayati. The painter mixes the paint with water.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
रंगः (raṅgaḥ) comes from the root √rañj, "to be coloured"; MW gives raṅga as colour, paint, dye, and also as a theatre, a stage (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
रेखा (rekhā) — a line.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a paintbrush, then the Sanskrit for paint.

You'll want to know: रेखा.
रेखा — rekhā — "a line".
बालः ऋजुं रेखां लिखति। bālaḥ ṛjuṃ rekhāṃ likhati. The child draws a straight line.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
रेखा (rekhā) probably comes from the root √likh (older rikh), "to scratch", beside which MW sets it; MW gives rekhā as a scratch, a streak, a line, a row (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आलिखति (ālikhati) — paints.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for paint, then the Sanskrit for a line.

You'll want to know: आलिखति.
आलिखति — ālikhati — "paints".
मीरा पुष्पाणि आलिखति। mīrā puṣpāṇi ālikhati. Mira paints flowers.
Past (she …): सा मयूरम् आलिखितवती, sā mayūram ālikhitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
आलिखति (ālikhati) is built on ā and the root √likh, "to scratch, to write", which also gives likhati; MW gives ālikh as to mark, to draw, to delineate, to paint (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रतिकृतिः (pratikṛtiḥ) — a portrait; a likeness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a line, then the Sanskrit for paints.

You'll want to know: प्रतिकृतिः.
प्रतिकृतिः — pratikṛtiḥ — "a portrait; a likeness".
चित्रकारः मम मातुः प्रतिकृतिम् आलिखति। citrakāraḥ mama mātuḥ pratikṛtim ālikhati. The painter paints my mother's portrait.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिकृतिः (pratikṛtiḥ) is built on prati, "in return, against", and the root √kṛ, "to make": a thing made to answer another; MW gives pratikṛti as an image, a likeness, a model (MW).
