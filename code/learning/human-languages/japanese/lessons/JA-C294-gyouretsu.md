## You'll want to know: ぎょうれつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C294-MIKOSHI294-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょうれつ** — *gyōretsu* — "a procession; a queue".

> **ぎょうれつは ゆっくり すすみました。** — *gyōretsu wa yukkuri susumimashita* — "The procession moved slowly along."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょうれつ** (*gyōretsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gyō*, "line" (Middle Chinese *hang*) and *retsu*, "row" (*ljet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
