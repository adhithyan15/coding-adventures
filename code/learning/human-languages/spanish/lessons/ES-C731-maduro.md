## You'll want to know: maduro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C731-MADUREZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**maduro** — "mature; ripe".

> Es muy maduro para su edad.
>
> *He is very mature for his age.*

With *estar* it is ripe fruit: *el plátano está maduro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maduro* is inherited from Latin *mātūrus* "ripe, mature" (EtymDB); the Latin *-t-* softened to *-d-*.
