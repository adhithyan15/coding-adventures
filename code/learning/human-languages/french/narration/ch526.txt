French, chapter 526: Laver le linge.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la machine à laver — washing machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for documentary, then the French for to repeat.

You'll want to know: la machine à laver.
la machine à laver — "washing machine".
La machine à laver tourne depuis une heure. The washing machine has been running for an hour.
Also le lave-linge. Faire une machine — to do a load of washing.

The word, taken apart.
la machine à laver joins machine, a learned borrowing from Latin māchina "a device", and laver "to wash", inherited from Latin lavāre (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
essorer — to spin (dry), to wring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to repeat, then the French for washing machine.

You'll want to know: essorer.
essorer — "to spin (dry), to wring".
La machine essore le linge à la fin du programme. The machine spins the washing at the end of the cycle.
L'essorage — the spin cycle.

The word, taken apart.
essorer comes from Old French essorer (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin exaurāre, built on Latin aura "a breeze" (Lewis & Short): to dry washing was first to put it out in the air.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la pince à linge — clothes peg.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to spin, then the French for to hang out.

You'll want to know: la pince à linge.
la pince à linge — "clothes peg".
Il me faut encore des pinces à linge. I still need some clothes pegs.
Une pince alone is pliers or tongs.

The word, taken apart.
la pince à linge joins pince, built on pincer "to pinch", an expressive word, and linge, from Latin līneus "made of linen", from līnum "flax" (Lewis & Short).
