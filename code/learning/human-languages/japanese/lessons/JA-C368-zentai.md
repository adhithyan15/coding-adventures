## You'll want to know: ぜんたい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C368-ZENTAI368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんたい** — *zentai* — "the whole".

> **ぜんたいを よんだら、いけんを 言って ください。** — *zentai o yondara, iken o itte kudasai* — "When you have read the whole thing, please tell me what you think."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんたい** (*zentai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zen*, "all" (Middle Chinese *dzjwen*) and *tai*, "body" (*thejX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
