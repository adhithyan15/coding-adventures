## You'll want to know: جھاڑی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C437-JHARI437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جھاڑی** — *jhāṛī* — "a bush".

> سڑک کے کنارے جھاڑیاں ہیں۔
>
> *saṛak ke kināre jhāṛiyāṅ haiṅ.*
>
> There are bushes along the road.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھاڑی** (*jhāṛī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *jhāṛ* and *jhāṛī*, "a bush", under *jhāṭa*, "a forest, an arbour" (CDIAL 5362).
