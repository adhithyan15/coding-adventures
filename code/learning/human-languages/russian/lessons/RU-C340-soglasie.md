## You'll want to know: согласие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C340-SOGLASIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**согласие** (*soglásiye*) — "agreement, consent".

> Родители дали согласие на свадьбу.
>
> *Rodíteli dáli soglásiye na svádbu.*
>
> The parents gave their consent to the wedding.

Neuter. *Согласие на* + accusative — consent to. *В согласии с* — in agreement with.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**согласие** is built from *со-* "together" and *глас* "voice", which Russian took from Church Slavonic; the native form of the same Proto-Slavic word, *\*golsŭ*, is *голос* (EtymDB).
