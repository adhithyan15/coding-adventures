## You'll want to know: l'agresseur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C289-AGRESSION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'agresseur** — "the attacker, the assailant".

> L'agresseur est parti en courant.
>
> *The attacker ran off.*

In news reports the attacker is often unnamed: *un agresseur inconnu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*agresseur* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aggressor* "an attacker", from *aggredī* "to attack" (Lewis & Short).
