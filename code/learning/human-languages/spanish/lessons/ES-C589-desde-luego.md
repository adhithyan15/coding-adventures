## You'll want to know: desde luego
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C589-MATIZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**desde luego** — "certainly, of course".

> Desde luego, te ayudo.
>
> *Of course I will help you.*

*¡Desde luego!* can also show annoyance: honestly!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desde luego* is built on *luego*, inherited from Latin *loco* 'on the spot', a form of *locus* 'place' (EtymDB).
