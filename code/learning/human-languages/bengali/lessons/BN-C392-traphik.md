## You'll want to know: ট্রাফিক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C392-TRAFFIC392-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ট্রাফিক** — *ṭrāphik* — "traffic (on the roads)".

> ট্রাফিক পুলিশ হাত তুলে গাড়ি থামালেন।
>
> *ṭrāphik pulish hāt tule gāṛi thāmālen.*
>
> The traffic policeman raised his hand and stopped the cars.

যানজট is a jam; ট্রাফিক is the traffic itself, and ট্রাফিক পুলিশ the officer at the crossing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ট্রাফিক** (*ṭrāphik*) is borrowed from English *traffic*; Bengali has no f, so the English f is said with ফ.
