## You'll want to know: dànjì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C432-QUIET432-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dànjì* — "off season; quiet season".

> *dànjì de shíhou yóukè hěn shǎo* — In the off season there are very few visitors.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dànjì* is built from *dàn* 'thin, slack' and *jì* 'a season', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *damX* and *kjwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a thin taste and the second as the youngest of brothers; the sense 'a season' is not given there.
