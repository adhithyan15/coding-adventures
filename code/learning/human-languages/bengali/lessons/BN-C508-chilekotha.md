## You'll want to know: চিলেকোঠা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C508-GHOR508-02]; assesses=[] -->

**চিলেকোঠা** — *chilekoṭhā* — "a small room on the roof; an attic".

> পুরনো খেলনাগুলো চিলেকোঠায় আছে।
>
> *Purôno khelnāgulo chilekoṭhāy āchhe.*
>
> The old toys are in the room on the roof.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চিলেকোঠা** (*chilekoṭhā*) is the small room at the top of the stairs, on the roof. The second part is কোঠা, "a room", which CDIAL lists as Bengali *koṭhā*, "a brick-built house", under Sanskrit *kōṣṭha*, "a storeroom, an inner apartment" (CDIAL 3546); where the first part comes from is not settled.
