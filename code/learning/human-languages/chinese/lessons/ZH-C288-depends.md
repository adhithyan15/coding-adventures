## Grammar Lens: 看 and a noun, it depends on
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-KAN-DEPENDS-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-KAN-01, ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-01] -->

> **看 and a noun can say 'it depends on': what happens turns on that.**

> **去不去 要看天气** *qù bu qù, yào kàn tiānqì* — Whether we go depends on the weather.
> **那要看你** *nà yào kàn nǐ* — That's up to you.

去不去, go or not, is the open question; 要看 and the noun says what settles it.
