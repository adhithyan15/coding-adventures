## You'll want to know: логичный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C344-LOGIKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**логичный** (*logíchnyy*) — "logical".

> Твой план вполне логичен.
>
> *Tvoy plan vpolné logíchen.*
>
> Your plan is quite logical.

Short forms *логичен, логична*. Of people too: *логичный человек*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**логичный** is built on *логика* with the Russian adjective suffix *-ичный*; *логика* is usually traced to Greek *logikḗ*, on *lógos* "word, reason".
