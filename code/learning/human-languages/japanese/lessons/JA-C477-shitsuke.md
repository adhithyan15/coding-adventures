## You'll want to know: しつけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C477-PARENT477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しつけ** — *shitsuke* — "upbringing; discipline".

> **そふは しつけに きびしいです。** — *sofu wa shitsuke ni kibishii desu* — "My grandfather is strict about upbringing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しつけ** (*shitsuke*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
