## You'll want to know: جال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C435-KHALIJ435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جال** — *jāl* — "a net".

> مچھیرے نے دریا میں جال پھینکا۔
>
> *machhere ne daryā meṅ jāl pheṅkā.*
>
> The fisherman cast his net into the river.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جال** (*jāl*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jāl*, "a net for catching birds or fish", under Sanskrit *jāla* (CDIAL 5213).
