## You'll want to know: बदतर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C389-BEHTAR389-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बदतर** — *badtar* — "worse".

> अगर इलाज नहीं हुआ तो हालत बदतर हो जाएगी।
>
> *agar ilāj nahīṃ huā to hālat badtar ho jāegī.*
>
> If there's no treatment, the condition will get worse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बदतर** (*badtar*) is usually traced to Persian *badtar*, "worse".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
