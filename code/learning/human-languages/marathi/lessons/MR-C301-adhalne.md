## You'll want to know: आढळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C301-DISNE301-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आढळणे** — *āḍhaḷṇe* — "to come across".

> जंगलात अभ्यासकांना एक दुर्मीळ पक्षी आढळला.
>
> *jaṅglāt abhyāskānnā ek durmīḷ pakṣī āḍhaḷlā.*
>
> The researchers came across a rare bird in the forest.

Past (to him …): त्याला आढळलं, *tyālā āḍhaḷlaṁ*.

A dative subject, more formal than सापडणे: लोकांना आढळलं, people came across it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आढळणे** (*āḍhaḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *āḍhaḷṇe*, 'to fall in the way of, to meet', under *\*āḍḍhalati*, 'falls towards' (CDIAL 1105).
