Persian, chapter 427: At the garage.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
تعمیرگاه (ta'mirgâh) — a garage, a repair shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for one-way, then the Persian for a driving licence.

You'll want to know: تعمیرگاه.
تعمیرگاه (ta'mirgâh) — "a garage, a repair shop".
ماشین را به تعمیرگاه بردیم. Mâshin râ be ta'mirgâh bordim. We took the car to the garage.
ta'mir 'repair' + gâh 'place'.

The word, taken apart.
ta'mirgâh is built from ta'mir 'repair', a loan usually traced to Arabic taʿmīr 'making habitable, restoring', and the native gâh 'place'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 3 of 6.
جوش آوردن (jush âvardan) — to overheat (of an engine); to boil over; to lose your temper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a garage, then the Persian for to repair.

You'll want to know: جوش آوردن.
جوش آوردن (jush âvardan) — "to overheat (of an engine); to boil over; to lose your temper".
در گرمای تابستان ماشین ما جوش آورد. Dar garmâ-ye tâbestân mâshin-e mâ jush âvard. In the summer heat our car overheated.
jush 'boiling' + âvardan 'to bring'. Said of people too: jush âvard — he lost his temper.

The word, taken apart.
jush âvardan is built from jush 'boiling, simmering', a native word that EtymDB lists, and âvardan 'to bring', usually traced to Middle Persian āwurdan.

Lesson 4 of 6.
یدک کش (yadakkesh) — a tow truck, a breakdown lorry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to repair, then the Persian for to overheat.

You'll want to know: یدک کش.
یدک کش (yadakkesh) — "a tow truck, a breakdown lorry".
یدک کش (yadakkesh) ماشین ما را به تعمیرگاه برد. Yadakkesh mâshin-e mâ râ be ta'mirgâh bord. The tow truck took our car to the garage.
yadak 'spare, in tow' + kesh 'puller', from keshidan.

The word, taken apart.
yadakkesh is built from yadak 'a led horse, a spare', usually traced to a Turkic word for a horse led on a rope, and kesh 'puller', from the native verb keshidan 'to pull'. EtymDB lists neither part.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.

بکسل کردن (boksel kardan) — "to tow".
برادرم ماشین خراب مرا با طناب بکسل کرد. Barâdaram mâshin-e kharâb-e marâ bâ tanâb boksel kard. My brother towed my broken-down car with a rope.
boksel (a tow rope) + kardan. Present stem kon-: boksel mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
boksel kardan is built from boksel 'a tow, a tow rope', which EtymDB takes from Russian buksír 'towing', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
