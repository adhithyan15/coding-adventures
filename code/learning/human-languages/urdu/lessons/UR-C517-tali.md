## You'll want to know: تالی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C517-STAGE517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تالی** — *tālī* — "a clap (of the hands)".

> ناٹک کے بعد سب نے تالیاں بجائیں۔
>
> *nāṭak ke bād sab ne tāliyāṅ bajāīṅ.*
>
> After the play everyone clapped.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تالی** (*tālī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tāla*, "slapping the hands together; musical time" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
