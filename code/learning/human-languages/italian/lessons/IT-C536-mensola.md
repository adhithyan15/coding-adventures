## You'll want to know: la mensola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C536-CASA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la mensola** — "shelf".

> Metto i libri sulla mensola.
>
> *I put the books on the shelf.*

A single shelf on the wall; a bookcase is *la libreria*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mensola* comes from Latin *mēnsula* "a little table" (Lewis & Short), from *mēnsa* "table".
