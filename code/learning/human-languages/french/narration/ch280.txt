French, chapter 280: Le journal télévisé.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le journal télévisé — the television news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to come out, then the French for the publication.

You'll want to know: le journal télévisé.
le journal télévisé — "the television news".
Nous regardons le journal télévisé à vingt heures. We watch the television news at eight in the evening.
Often le JT in speech, or le journal de vingt heures.

The word, taken apart.
journal is inherited from Latin diurnālis "daily" (EtymDB); télévisé is built on télévision, from Greek tēle "far" and Latin vīsiō "sight", from vidēre "to see".

Lesson 5 of 5.
le micro — the microphone, the mic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the viewer, then the French for the listener.

You'll want to know: le micro.
le micro — "the microphone, the mic".
Le chanteur a pris le micro. The singer took the mic.
Short for le microphone. Au micro — speaking on air.

The word, taken apart.
micro is short for microphone, a learned word built from Greek mīkrós "small" and phōnḗ "voice, sound" (EtymDB lists the short form without a parent).
