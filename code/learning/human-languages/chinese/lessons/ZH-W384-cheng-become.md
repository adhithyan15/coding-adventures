## Script — a bar, a fall, a small turn, then the slanting hook of 我
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-CHENG-BECOME-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 成

**One shape, six strokes in a fixed order.** The order is the thing to hold.

**A bar,** left to right, from the left edge to past the middle.

**A long stroke falling to the left** from the left end of the bar down to the foot.

**A small turning hook** under the bar: a short bar that turns down and hooks to the left.

**The long slanting hook,** as in 我: from the top, a long stroke down to the lower right that flicks up at the end. Then **a short stroke falling to the left** across it, and **a dot** at the upper right.

Six strokes in all.
