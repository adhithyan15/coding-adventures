## You'll want to know: schalten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C399-LENKRAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**schalten** — "to change gear; to switch".

> Am Berg schaltet sie in den zweiten Gang.
>
> *On the hill she changes into second gear.*

*Schalten* also means to switch: *das Licht einschalten* — to switch the light on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schalten* is inherited through Middle High German *schalten* from Old High German *scaltan* (EtymDB), usually glossed 'to push, to shove'; it is said to have first meant pushing a boat along with a pole, later a lever pushed into place.
