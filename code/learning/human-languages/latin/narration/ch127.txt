Latin, chapter 127: Bean, Pea, Cucumber, Chicken, Pepper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
faba, fabae — a bean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for an onion, then the Latin for garlic.
