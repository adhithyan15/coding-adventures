Telugu, chapter 284: To let go, To dig, To happen, To sweep, To knead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వదులు (vadulu) — to let go, to leave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to oppose, then the Telugu for to argue.

You'll want to know: వదులు.
వదులు — vadulu — "to let go, to leave".

The word, taken apart.
వదులు (vadulu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5243 lists vadalu, "to leave, let loose", with Kurux odoxnā, "to loosen, get rid of".
