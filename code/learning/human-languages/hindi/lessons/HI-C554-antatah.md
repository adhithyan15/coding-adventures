## You'll want to know: अंततः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C554-NATIJATAN554-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अंततः** — *antataḥ* — "in the end, ultimately (formal)".

> लंबी बहस के बाद अंततः सब लोग सहमत हो गए।
>
> *Lambī bahas ke bād antataḥ sab log sahmat ho gae.*
>
> After a long debate everyone finally agreed.

A formal word; speech says आख़िर में.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंततः** (*antataḥ*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *antatas*, "from the end; lastly, finally" (Monier-Williams).
