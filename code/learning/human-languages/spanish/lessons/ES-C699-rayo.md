## You'll want to know: el rayo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C699-TORMENTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el rayo** — "the lightning bolt; the ray".

> Un rayo cayó en el árbol.
>
> *Lightning struck the tree.*

*Los rayos del sol* — the sun’s rays.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rayo* is inherited from Latin *radius* "a staff, a spoke, a ray".
