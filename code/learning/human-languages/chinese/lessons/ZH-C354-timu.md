## You'll want to know: tímù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C354-TOPIC354-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tímù* — "title; question on a test".

> *wǒ kàn bu dǒng zhège tímù* — I can't make sense of this question.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tímù* is built from 题 'a heading' and *mù* 'the eye', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dej* and *mjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the title of a book' and, quoting an older dictionary, the second 'the human eye'.
