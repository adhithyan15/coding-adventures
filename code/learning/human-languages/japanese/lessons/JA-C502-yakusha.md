## You'll want to know: やくしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C502-STAGE502-03]; assesses=[] -->

**やくしゃ** — *yakusha* — "a stage actor; a player".

> **あの やくしゃは こえが いいです。** — *ano yakusha wa koe ga ii desu* — "That actor has a good voice."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やくしゃ** (*yakusha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yaku*, "duty" (Middle Chinese *ywek*) and *sha*, "someone" (*tsyaeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
