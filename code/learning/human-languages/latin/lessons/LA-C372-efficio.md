## You'll want to know: efficiō, efficere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C372-AUCTOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**efficiō, efficere** — "to bring about, to cause; to make".

> Pluvia effēcit ut domī manērēmus.
>
> *The rain made us stay at home.*

*efficiō, efficere, effēcī, effectum*. *Efficiō ut* + subjunctive: I bring it about that. Built on *faciō*. English *efficient*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*efficiō* is built from *ex* "out" and *faciō* "to make" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to make a thing until it is done. English *efficient* comes from its present participle.
