## You'll want to know: iocor, iocārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C502-IOCU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**iocor, iocārī** — "to joke, jest".

> Servī in culīnā iocantur.
>
> *The slaves joke in the kitchen.*

Deponent: *iocor, iocārī, iocātus sum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**iocor** is built on *iocus* "a joke" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
