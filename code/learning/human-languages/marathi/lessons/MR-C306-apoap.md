## You'll want to know: आपोआप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C306-KRAMASH306-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आपोआप** — *āpoāp* — "by itself; automatically".

> वारा आला आणि दार आपोआप बंद झालं.
>
> *vārā ālā āṇi dār āpoāp band jhālaṁ.*
>
> The wind blew and the door shut by itself.

A doubling of आप, self.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपोआप** (*āpoāp*) is built from *āp*, 'self', said twice; CDIAL lists Marathi *āplā*, 'own', under Sanskrit *ātman*, 'self' (CDIAL 1135).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
