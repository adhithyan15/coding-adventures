## You'll want to know: たいさく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C350-YOUJIN350-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たいさく** — *taisaku* — "a countermeasure".

> **あめが ふったら、どんな たいさくが ありますか。** — *ame ga futtara, donna taisaku ga arimasu ka* — "If it rains, what can we do about it?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいさく** (*taisaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "opposite" (Middle Chinese *twojH*) and *saku*, "scheme" (*tsrheak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
