# সমবেদনা (sômôbedônā) — sympathy; condolences

## You'll want to know: সমবেদনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C491-SHRADDHO491-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সমবেদনা** — *sômôbedônā* — "sympathy; condolences".

> প্রতিবেশীরা এসে আমাদের সমবেদনা জানাল।
>
> *prôtibeshīrā ese āmāder sômôbedônā jānālô.*
>
> The neighbours came to offer us their condolences.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সমবেদনা** (*sômôbedônā*) puts Sanskrit *sama*, "same" (Monier-Williams), with *vedanā*, "feeling, pain" (CDIAL 12106): feeling the same pain as someone else.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
