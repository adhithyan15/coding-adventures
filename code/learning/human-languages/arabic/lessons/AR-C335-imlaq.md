## You'll want to know: عملاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C335-SIHR335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عملاق** — *ʿimlāq* — "a giant".

> في القصة عملاق يعيش في الغابة.
>
> *fī al-qiṣṣa ʿimlāq yaʿīshu fī al-ghāba.*
>
> In the story a giant lives in the forest.

Plural: عمالقة, *ʿamāliqa*.

Of a company or a team: عملاق, a giant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عملاق** (*ʿimlāq*) is built on the four-letter root *ʿ-m-l-q* (Lane), where it is "very tall", and the *ʿamāliqa* are a people of old remembered as giants. Its origin before that is not settled.
