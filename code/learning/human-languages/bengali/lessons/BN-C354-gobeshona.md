## You'll want to know: গবেষণা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C354-GOBESHONA354-01]; assesses=[] -->

**গবেষণা** — *gôbeshônā* — "research".

> মীরা বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়ে গবেষণা করে।
>
> *mīrā bishvôbidyālôye gôbeshônā kôre.*
>
> Mira does research at the university.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গবেষণা** (*gôbeshônā*) is Sanskrit *gaveṣaṇā*, beside *gaveṣaṇa*, seeking or searching (Monier-Williams), which files the verb *gaveṣ*, to seek, among the compounds of *go*, a cow. Bengali uses it for scholarly research.
