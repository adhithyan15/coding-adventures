## You'll want to know: ਹਲਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C303-WATER303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਲਟ** — *halaṭ* — "a Persian wheel".

> ਹਲਟ ਖੂਹ ਤੋਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਕੱਢਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *halaṭ khūh ton pāṇī kaḍḍhdā hai.*
>
> The Persian wheel draws water from the well.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਲਟ** (*halaṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *araghaṭṭa*, "a wheel for raising water"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *halhaṭ*, "Persian wheel", under it (CDIAL 596).
