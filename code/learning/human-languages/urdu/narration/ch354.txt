Urdu, chapter 354: Reasonable or Not.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
معقول (māqūl) — reasonable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to hold up, then the Urdu for to win acceptance for.

You'll want to know: معقول.
معقول — māqūl — "reasonable".
یہ قیمت معقول ہے۔ yih qīmat māqūl hai. This price is reasonable.

The word, taken apart.
معقول (māqūl) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maʿqūl, "understood; rational".

Warm-up.

بے جا — be-jā — "unwarranted; out of place".
آپ کی شکایت بے جا ہے۔ āp kī shikāyat be-jā hai. Your complaint is unwarranted.

The word, taken apart.
بے جا (be-jā) is usually traced to Persian bē-jā, "out of place": bē, "without", and jā, "a place".
