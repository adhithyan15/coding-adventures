## You'll want to know: বৈধ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C340-BOIDHO340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বৈধ** — *boidhô* — "lawful; valid".

> তোমার ভিসা কি বৈধ?
>
> *tomār bhisā ki boidhô?*
>
> Is your visa valid?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বৈধ** (*boidhô*) is Sanskrit *vaidha*, 'according to rule', legal (Monier-Williams), from *vidhi* 'a rule'. A paper or an act can be বৈধ.
