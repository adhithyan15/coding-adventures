Hindi, chapter 120: Forms and Introductions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हस्ताक्षर (hastākṣar) — a signature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for married, then the Hindi for a birthday.

You'll want to know: हस्ताक्षर.
हस्ताक्षर (hastākṣar) — "a signature", literally "hand-letters".
हाथ and this word's हस्त- are the same old word for hand. यहाँ हस्ताक्षर (hastākṣar) कीजिए — "please sign here".

What you've built.
A signature, built on the word for hand.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 5 of 5.
मिलकर अच्छा लगा (milkar acchā lagā) — nice to meet you.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an introduction, then the Hindi for a person.

You'll want to know: मिलकर अच्छा लगा.
मिलकर अच्छा लगा (milkar acchā lagā) — "nice to meet you", literally "having met, it felt good".
It is the everyday cousin of आपसे मिलकर ख़ुशी हुई, built on the same मिलना, "to meet".

What you've built.
A signature, an identity, an introduction, a person, and the goodbye after meeting.
