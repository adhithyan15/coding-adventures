## You'll want to know: la morsure
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C306-PIQURES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la morsure** — "the bite (a wound)".

> La morsure du chien n'était pas grave.
>
> *The dog bite was not serious.*

For insects, French uses the verb *piquer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*morsure* is built on *mors*, the old past participle of *mordre* "to bite", from Latin *morsus* "a bite" (Lewis & Short).
