Marathi, chapter 425: Weights and Measures.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तराजू (tarājū) — a pair of scales.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a broker, then the Marathi for a bill.

You'll want to know: तराजू.
तराजू — tarājū — "a pair of scales".
भाजीवाला तराजूने कांद्यांचं वजन करतो. bhājīvālā tarājūne kāndyāñcaṁ vajan karto. The vegetable seller weighs the onions on the scales.
Masculine.
A masculine noun; the plural is the same.

The word, taken apart.
तराजू (tarājū) is a loan usually traced to Persian tarāzū, "a balance, a pair of scales".

Lesson 2 of 5.
मापणे (māpṇe) — to measure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a bill, then the Marathi for a pair of scales.

You'll want to know: मापणे.
मापणे — māpṇe — "to measure".
मी खोलीची लांबी मापली. mī kholīcī lāmbī māplī. I measured the length of the room.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मापलं, tyāne māplaṁ.
The thing measured is the object: लांबी मापली. The noun is माप: माप घेणे, to take measurements.

The word, taken apart.
मापणे (māpṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi māpṇẽ, "to measure", under māpyate, "is measured" (CDIAL 10054).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अस्सल (assal) — genuine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pair of scales, then the Marathi for to measure.

You'll want to know: अस्सल.
अस्सल — assal — "genuine".
हे अस्सल सोनं आहे. he assal sonaṁ āhe. This is genuine gold.
It does not change: अस्सल (assal) रेशीम, अस्सल (assal) पुणेरी.

The word, taken apart.
अस्सल (assal) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic aṣl, "root, origin": something true to its origin.

Lesson 5 of 5.
नक्कल (nakkal) — a copy; an imitation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for genuine, then the Marathi for fake.

You'll want to know: नक्कल.

हा फोटो मूळ चित्राची नक्कल आहे. hā phoṭo mūḷ citrācī nakkal āhe. This photo is a copy of the original painting.
Feminine.
नक्कल (nakkal) करणे, to copy or to mimic someone.

The word, taken apart.
नक्कल (nakkal) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic naql, "a transfer, a copy".
