## You'll want to know: a comunidade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C367-COMUNIDADE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a comunidade** — "the community".

> A comunidade do bairro organizou uma festa.
>
> *The neighbourhood community organised a party.*

*A comunidade portuguesa* — the Portuguese community abroad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comunidade* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commūnitās* "community, fellowship" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *commūnis* "common".
