## You'll want to know: ઇલાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C484-ILAJ484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇલાજ** — *ilāj* — "a cure".

> આ રોગનો ઇલાજ છે, ચિંતા ન કરો.
>
> *ā rogno ilāj che, cintā na karo.*
>
> There is a cure for this illness; don't worry.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇલાજ** (*ilāj*) is usually traced to Arabic *ʿilāj*, "treatment, cure", which reached Gujarati through Persian.
