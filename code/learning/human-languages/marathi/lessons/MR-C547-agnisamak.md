## You'll want to know: अग्निशामक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C547-ANIBANI547-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्निशामक** — *agniśāmak* — "a fire extinguisher".

> प्रत्येक मजल्यावर एक अग्निशामक ठेवला आहे.
>
> *pratyek majlyāvar ek agniśāmak ṭhevlā āhe.*
>
> A fire extinguisher is kept on every floor.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्निशामक** (*agniśāmak*) is a tatsama coinage from Sanskrit *agni*, "fire" (Monier-Williams), and a noun of *śam-*, "to quench".
