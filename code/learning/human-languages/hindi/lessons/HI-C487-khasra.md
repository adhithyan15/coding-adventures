## You'll want to know: खसरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C487-SANKRAMAN487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खसरा** — *khasrā* — "measles".

> मेरे भाई को बचपन में खसरा हुआ था।
>
> *mere bhāī ko bacpan meṁ khasrā huā thā.*
>
> My brother had measles as a child.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खसरा** (*khasrā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *khasrā*, "scab, measles", under Sanskrit *khasa* (CDIAL 3854).
