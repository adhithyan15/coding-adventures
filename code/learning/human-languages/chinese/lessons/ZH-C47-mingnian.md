## You'll want to know: 明年
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C47-DATE47-03]; assesses=[] -->

**明年** — *míngnián* — "next year".

> **明年我买车** *míngnián wǒ mǎi chē* — I'm buying a car next year.

The new character, in pieces: 明 — 日 on the left and, on the right, the moon piece, the word yuè from the lesson before. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**明年** (*míngnián*) is built from 明 'bright' and 年 'year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjaeng* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses *mjaeng* 'light'.
