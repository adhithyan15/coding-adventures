Marathi, chapter 301: Strange Sights.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दिसणे (disṇe) — to be seen; to appear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to flee, then the Marathi for to shut in.

You'll want to know: दिसणे.
दिसणे — disṇe — "to be seen; to appear".
खिडकीतून मला एक विचित्र प्रकाश दिसला. khiḍkītūn malā ek vicitra prakāś dislā. I saw a strange light through the window.
Past (to him …): त्याला दिसलं, tyālā dislaṁ.
A dative-subject verb: मला दिसलं, I saw; the thing seen agrees: मला घर दिसलं, मला गाडी दिसली.

The word, taken apart.
दिसणे (disṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi disṇe, 'to appear, seem', under Sanskrit dṛśyate, 'is seen, appears' (CDIAL 6516).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सापडणे (sāpaḍṇe) — to be found; to turn up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to shut in, then the Marathi for to be seen.

You'll want to know: सापडणे.
सापडणे — sāpaḍṇe — "to be found; to turn up".
बागेत मला एक सोन्याची अंगठी सापडली. bāget malā ek sonyācī aṅgṭhī sāpaḍlī. I found a gold ring in the garden.
Past (to him …): त्याला सापडलं, tyālā sāpaḍlaṁ.
A dative-subject verb like दिसणे (disṇe): मला किल्ली सापडली, I found the key; चोर सापडला, the thief was caught.

The word, taken apart.
सापडणे (sāpaḍṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi sāpaḍṇe, 'to fall into, be got hold of', under Sanskrit saṁpatati, 'flies together, reaches, happens' (CDIAL 12930).

Lesson 3 of 5.
आढळणे (āḍhaḷṇe) — to come across.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be seen, then the Marathi for to be found.

You'll want to know: आढळणे.
आढळणे — āḍhaḷṇe — "to come across".
जंगलात अभ्यासकांना एक दुर्मीळ पक्षी आढळला. jaṅglāt abhyāskānnā ek durmīḷ pakṣī āḍhaḷlā. The researchers came across a rare bird in the forest.
Past (to him …): त्याला आढळलं, tyālā āḍhaḷlaṁ.
A dative subject, more formal than सापडणे (sāpaḍṇe): लोकांना आढळलं, people came across it.

The word, taken apart.
आढळणे (āḍhaḷṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi āḍhaḷṇe, 'to fall in the way of, to meet', under āḍḍhalati, 'falls towards' (CDIAL 1105).

Lesson 4 of 5.
डोकावणे (ḍokāvṇe) — to peep; to peek.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be found, then the Marathi for to come across.

You'll want to know: डोकावणे.
डोकावणे — ḍokāvṇe — "to peep; to peek".
मुलगा दारातून आत डोकावला. mulgā dārātūn āt ḍokāvlā. The boy peeped in through the doorway.
Past (he …): तो डोकावला, to ḍokāvlā.
Built on डोके, the head: to put one's head in for a look.

The word, taken apart.
डोकावणे (ḍokāvṇe) is built on ḍoke, 'the head', which goes back to ḍōka, 'head' (CDIAL 5566): to push the head in for a look.

Lesson 5 of 5.
निरखणे (nirakhṇe) — to observe closely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to come across, then the Marathi for to peep.

You'll want to know: निरखणे.
निरखणे — nirakhṇe — "to observe closely".
आजोबांनी नाणं उलटं करून नीट निरखलं. ājobānnī nāṇaṁ ulṭaṁ karūn nīṭ nirakhlaṁ. Grandfather turned the coin over and studied it closely.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने निरखलं, tyāne nirakhlaṁ.
Transitive: the thing studied is the object. निरीक्षण करणे is its formal cousin.

The word, taken apart.
निरखणे (nirakhṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi nirakhṇe, of close looking, under Sanskrit nirīkṣate, 'looks at, regards' (CDIAL 7280).
