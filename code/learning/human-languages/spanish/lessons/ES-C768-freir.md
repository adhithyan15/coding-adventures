## You'll want to know: freír
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C768-COCINAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**freír** — "to fry".

> Frió los huevos con un poco de aceite.
>
> *He fried the eggs in a little oil.*

Stem change: *frío*, *frió*. Participle: *frito* — *patatas fritas*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*freír* is inherited from Latin *frīgere* 'to roast, to fry' (EtymDB). The *g* between vowels was lost. It is not the *frigidus* 'cold' behind *frío*.
