## You'll want to know: निसटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C291-SUTKA291-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निसटणे** — *nisaṭṇe* — "to slip away; to escape".

> दार उघडं असतं तर मांजर बाहेर निसटलं असतं.
>
> *dār ughḍaṁ astaṁ tar māñjar bāher nisaṭlaṁ astaṁ.*
>
> If the door had been open, the cat would have slipped out.

Past (he …): तो निसटला, *to nisaṭlā*.

The place left takes -तून: खोलीतून निसटणे, to slip out of a room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निसटणे** (*nisaṭṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *nisaṭṇe*, 'to slip away', under *\*niḥsṛṣṭa*, 'sent forth' (CDIAL 7132).
