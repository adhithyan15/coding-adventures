Persian, chapter 380: Applying for work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رزومه (rezume) — a CV, a résumé.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for unemployment, then the Persian for retirement.

You'll want to know: رزومه.
رزومه (rezume) — "a CV, a résumé".
رزومه خودم را برای سه شرکت فرستادم. rezume-ye khodam râ barâye se sherkat ferestâdam. I sent my CV to three companies.
A loan from French. A more Persian phrase is sâbeqe-ye kâri, a work record.

The word, taken apart.
رزومه (rezume) was borrowed from French résumé "a summary", the participle of résumer, from Latin resumere "to take up again" (Lewis & Short): a CV sums up your working life.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قرارداد (qarârdâd) — a contract.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for retirement, then the Persian for a CV.

You'll want to know: قرارداد.
قرارداد (qarârdâd) — "a contract".
او یک قرارداد یک ساله امضا کرد. u yek qarârdâd-e yek sâle emzâ kard. He signed a one-year contract.
Qarâr "agreement" + dâd "gave". Qarârdâd bastan — to sign a contract.

The word, taken apart.
قرارداد (qarârdâd) joins qarâr, from Arabic qarār "a settled decision", to dâd, the past stem of dâdan "to give", which EtymDB traces to PIE deh₃-: an agreement that has been given.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ترفیع (tarfi') — a promotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a CV, then the Persian for a contract.

You'll want to know: ترفیع.
ترفیع (tarfi') — "a promotion".
خواهرم امسال ترفیع گرفت. khâharam emsâl tarfi' gereft. My sister got a promotion this year.
Tarfi' gereftan — to be promoted.

The word, taken apart.
ترفیع (tarfi') is usually traced to Arabic tarfī' "raising, lifting up": a promotion lifts you to a higher post.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کارآموز (kârâmuz) — a trainee, an intern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a contract, then the Persian for a promotion.

You'll want to know: کارآموز.
کارآموز (kârâmuz) — "a trainee, an intern".
سه کارآموز در دفتر ما کار میکنند. se kârâmuz dar daftar-e mâ kâr mikonand. Three trainees work in our office.
Kâr "work" + âmuz, the present stem of âmukhtan "to learn". Plural kârâmuzân.

The word, taken apart.
کارآموز (kârâmuz) is a native Persian compound built from kâr "work" + âmuz-, the present stem of âmukhtan "to learn, to teach" (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian). A trainee is a work-learner.

Lesson 5 of 5.
کارآموزی (kârâmuzi) — an internship, training.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a promotion, then the Persian for a trainee.

You'll want to know: کارآموزی.
کارآموزی (kârâmuzi) — "an internship, training".
او تابستان دوره کارآموزی را در یک بیمارستان گذراند. u tâbestân dowre-ye kârâmuzi râ dar yek bimârestân gozarând. In the summer he did his internship in a hospital.
Kârâmuz + the noun suffix -i. Dowre-ye kârâmuzi — a training period.

The word, taken apart.
کارآموزی (kârâmuzi) is native Persian, built from kârâmuz "trainee", from kâr "work" and âmuz- "learn", with the noun suffix -i.
