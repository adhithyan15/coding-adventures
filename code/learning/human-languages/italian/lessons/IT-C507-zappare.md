## You'll want to know: zappare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C507-ORTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**zappare** — "to hoe, to dig".

> Il nonno zappa l'orto ogni mattina.
>
> *Grandpa hoes the vegetable garden every morning.*

The tool is *la zappa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zappare* is built on *zappa* "hoe" (EtymDB), usually traced to Late Latin *sappa*; the deeper origin is not settled.
