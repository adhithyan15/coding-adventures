## You'll want to know: લાડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C450-VAHAL450-05]; assesses=[] -->

**લાડ** — *lāḍ* — "pampering; fond indulgence".

> દાદાના લાડથી બાળક જિદ્દી થઈ ગયું.
>
> *dādānā lāḍthī bāḷak jiddī thaī gayũ.*
>
> Grandfather's pampering has made the child stubborn.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાડ** (*lāḍ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*lāḍya-*, "fondling"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "caress" (CDIAL 11013).
