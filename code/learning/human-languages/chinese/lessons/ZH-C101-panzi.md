## You'll want to know: pánzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C101-KITCHEN101-04]; assesses=[] -->

*pánzi* — "plate; dish".

> *zhège pánzi hěn gānjìng* — This plate is nice and clean.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pánzi* is built from pán and the noun ending zi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ban* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as the name of a vessel.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
