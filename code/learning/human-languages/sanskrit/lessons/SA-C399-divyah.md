## You'll want to know: दिव्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C399-HRADA399-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दिव्यः** — *divyaḥ* — "divine; heavenly".

> मन्दिरे दिव्यः प्रकाशः अस्ति।
>
> *mandire divyaḥ prakāśaḥ asti.*
>
> There is a heavenly light in the temple.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिव्यः** (*divyaḥ*) is built on *div*, "the sky, heaven", with the ending *-ya*; MW gives *divya* as divine, heavenly, celestial (MW).
