## You'll want to know: cantus, cantūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C543-CANT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cantus, cantūs** — "singing, song; music".

> Cantum avium māne audīmus.
>
> *In the morning we hear the song of the birds.*

Masculine, fourth declension, from *canō*. Singing, and playing too. English *chant* comes from the verb *cantō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cantus** is the action noun of *canō* "to sing", inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to sing" (EtymDB). English *chant* belongs to the same family.
