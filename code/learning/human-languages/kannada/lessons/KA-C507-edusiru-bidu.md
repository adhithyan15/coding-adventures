# ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಡು (ēdusiru biḍu) — to pant; to gasp

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C507-SADDU507-01, KA-LEX-C507-SADDU507-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to belch, then the Kannada for to blink.

## You'll want to know: ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C507-SADDU507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಡು** — *ēdusiru biḍu* — "to pant; to gasp".

> ಮೆಟ್ಟಿಲು ಹತ್ತಿ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಟ್ಟರು.
>
> *meṭṭilu hatti ajji ēdusiru biṭṭaru.*
>
> Grandmother panted after climbing the stairs.

Past (I …): ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಟ್ಟೆ, *ēdusiru biṭṭe*.

ಏದುಸಿರು is panting breath, built on ಉಸಿರು, breath; ಬಿಡು, to let out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಡು** (*ēdusiru biḍu*) is a native Kannada phrase: *usiru*, "breath" (DEDR 645), and *biḍu*, "to let go" (DEDR 5393). The origin of the first part, ēd-, is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
