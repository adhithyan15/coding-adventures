## You'll want to know: el probador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C770-COMERCIOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el probador** — "the fitting room".

> Prefiero esta tienda porque los probadores son grandes.
>
> *I prefer this shop because the fitting rooms are big.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*probador* is built on *probar* 'to try on', inherited from Latin *probāre* 'to test, to prove' (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-dor*.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
