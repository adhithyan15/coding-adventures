## You'll want to know: बधाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C245-TEDNO245-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बधाई** — *badhāī* — "congratulations; good wishes".

> पाड़ोसी बधाई देवै है।
>
> *pāṛosī badhāī devai hai.*
>
> The neighbour offers congratulations.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बधाई** (*badhāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vardhāpayati*, "gladdens, congratulates"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *badhāī*, "a present", and Hindi *badhāī*, "congratulations", under it (CDIAL 11383).
