# उखाड़ना (ukhāṛnā) — to pull up; to uproot

## You'll want to know: उखाड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C525-PAUDHA525-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उखाड़ना** — *ukhāṛnā* — "to pull up; to uproot".

> माली ने बगीचे से पुराना पेड़ उखाड़ा।
>
> *Mālī ne bagīce se purānā peṛ ukhāṛā.*
>
> The gardener pulled up the old tree from the garden.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने घास उखाड़ी, *usne ghās ukhāṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उखाड़ना** (*ukhāṛnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*utskarati*, "digs out", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 1895). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to uproot".
