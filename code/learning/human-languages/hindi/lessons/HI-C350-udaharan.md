## You'll want to know: उदाहरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C350-MISAAL350-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उदाहरण** — *udāharaṇ* — "an example".

> शिक्षक ने एक उदाहरण दिया।
>
> *śikṣak ne ek udāharaṇ diyā.*
>
> The teacher gave an example.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उदाहरण** (*udāharaṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *udāharaṇa*, "an example, an illustration" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
