## You'll want to know: மரபு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C321-SADANGU321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**மரபு** — *marapu* — "a convention; a tradition".

> இது எங்கள் குடும்பத்தின் பழைய மரபு.
>
> *itu eṅkaḷ kuṭumpattiṉ paḻaiya marapu.*
>
> This is an old convention in our family.

Also lineage, the line of a family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மரபு** (*marapu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4715): lineage and established usage, what comes down a family line.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
