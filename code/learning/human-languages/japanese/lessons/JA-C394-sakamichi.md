## You'll want to know: さかみち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C394-SAKAMICHI394-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さかみち** — *sakamichi* — "a hill road; a sloping street".

> **さかみちを ゆっくり のぼりました。** — *sakamichi o yukkuri noborimashita* — "I slowly climbed the hill road."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さかみち** (*sakamichi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *saka*, "slope" and *michi*, "road"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
