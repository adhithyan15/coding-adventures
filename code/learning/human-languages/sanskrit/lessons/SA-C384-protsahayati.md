## You'll want to know: प्रोत्साहयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C384-UDYAMA384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रोत्साहयति** — *protsāhayati* — "encourages".

> यदि माता प्रोत्साहयति, तर्हि बालः उत्साहेन पठति।
>
> *yadi mātā protsāhayati, tarhi bālaḥ utsāhena paṭhati.*
>
> If his mother encourages him, the child studies with zeal.

Past (he …): सः छात्रान् प्रोत्साहितवान्, *saḥ chātrān protsāhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रोत्साहयति** (*protsāhayati*) is built on *pra*, *ut* and the root √sah, "to be able, to endure", in its causative form; MW gives *prot-sāhita* "incited, stimulated, encouraged" (MW).
