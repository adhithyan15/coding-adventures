Marathi, chapter 144: Fox, Donkey, Ox, Mosquito, Forehead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कोल्हा (kolhā) — a fox.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rabbit, then the Marathi for a wolf.

You'll want to know: कोल्हा.
कोल्हा — kolhā — "a fox".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kolhā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kolhā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kolhā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a rabbit, then the Marathi for a wolf, then say kolhā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does कोल्हा (kolhā) mean? ("A fox".) Say it once more.
