## You'll want to know: der Gastgeber
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C307-GASTGEBER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Gastgeber** — "the host".

> Der Gastgeber begrüßte jeden Gast an der Tür.
>
> *The host greeted every guest at the door.*

Plural: *die Gastgeber*. Feminine *die Gastgeberin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gastgeber* joins *Gast* "guest" and *Geber* "giver", from *geben* "to give", Proto-Germanic *gebaną* (EtymDB).
