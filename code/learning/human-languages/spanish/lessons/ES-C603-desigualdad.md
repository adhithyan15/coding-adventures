## You'll want to know: la desigualdad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C603-DERECHO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la desigualdad** — "the inequality".

> La desigualdad crece en las ciudades.
>
> *Inequality is growing in the cities.*

The opposite of *la igualdad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desigualdad* is built on *igualdad* with the prefix *des-*; *igualdad* is inherited from Latin *aequalitas*, from *aequus* 'equal' (EtymDB).
