## You'll want to know: salūbris, salūbre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C523-ROBU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**salūbris, salūbre** — "wholesome, healthy".

> Aqua huius fontis salūbris est.
>
> *The water of this spring is wholesome.*

Two endings; an older masculine *salūber* also appears. Of places, food and air. English *salubrious*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**salūbris** is built on *salūs* "health, safety" (Lewis & Short). English *salubrious* comes from it.
