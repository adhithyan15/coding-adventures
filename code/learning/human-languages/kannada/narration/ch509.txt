Kannada, chapter 509: The Frame of the Body.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನರ (nara) — a nerve; a sinew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the intestine, then the Kannada for a muscle.

You'll want to know: ನರ.
ನರ — nara — "a nerve; a sinew".
ವೈದ್ಯರು ಅವನ ಕೈಯ ನರವನ್ನು ಪರೀಕ್ಷಿಸಿದರು. vaidyaru avana kaiya naravannu parīkṣisidaru. The doctor examined the nerve in his arm.
Plural: ನರಗಳು, naragaḷu.
In everyday talk also a vein; ನರಗಳು, the nerves.

The word, taken apart.
ನರ (nara) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2903 lists Kannada nara, naravu, "sinew, tendon, nerve, vein".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪಕ್ಕೆಲುಬು (pakkelubu) — a rib.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a muscle, then the Kannada for a nerve.

You'll want to know: ಪಕ್ಕೆಲುಬು.
ಪಕ್ಕೆಲುಬು — pakkelubu — "a rib".
ಬಿದ್ದಾಗ ಅವನ ಪಕ್ಕೆಲುಬು ಮುರಿಯಿತು. biddāga avana pakkelubu muriyitu. When he fell, he broke a rib.
ಪಕ್ಕ, the side + ಎಲುಬು, a bone: a bone of the side.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಕ್ಕೆಲುಬು (pakkelubu) is a Kannada compound: pakka, "the side", which goes back to Sanskrit pakṣa, "wing, side", through Prakrit pakkha (CDIAL 7627), and elubu, "a bone", which DEDR 839 lists among the native words for bone.

ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ಕುಳಿತರೆ ಬೆನ್ನುಮೂಳೆಗೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದು. sariyāgi kuḷitare bennumūḷege oḷḷeyadu. Sitting properly is good for the spine.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೆನ್ನುಮೂಳೆ (bennumūḷe) is a compound of two native Dravidian words: bennu, "the back" (DEDR 5488), and mūḷe, "a bone" (DEDR 5050).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತೋಳು (tōḷu) — an arm; the upper arm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a rib, then the Kannada for the spine.

You'll want to know: ತೋಳು.
ತೋಳು — tōḷu — "an arm; the upper arm".
ಅಪ್ಪ ಮಗುವನ್ನು ತೋಳಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಎತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡರು. appa maguvannu tōḷinalli ettikoṇḍaru. Father picked the baby up in his arms.
Plural: ತೋಳುಗಳು, tōḷugaḷu.
ಕೈ is the hand or the whole arm; ತೋಳು (tōḷu) is from shoulder to elbow, and also a sleeve.

The word, taken apart.
ತೋಳು (tōḷu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3564 lists Kannada tōḷ(u), "the arm", beside Tamil tōḷ, "shoulder, arm".

ಕುಂಟು (kuṇṭu) — to limp.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ಕುಂಟು.
ಕುಂಟು — kuṇṭu — "to limp".
ಕಾಲಿಗೆ ಗಾಯ ಆಗಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ಅವನು ಕುಂಟುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. kālige gāya āgiddarinda avanu kuṇṭuttiddanu. Because his leg was hurt, he was limping.
Past (I …): ಕುಂಟಿದೆ, kuṇṭide.
ಕುಂಟ is a lame man; ಕುಂಟಾಟ is hopscotch.

The word, taken apart.
ಕುಂಟು (kuṇṭu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1688 lists Kannada kuṇṭu, "to limp, halt, hop", and kuṇṭa, "a lame man".
