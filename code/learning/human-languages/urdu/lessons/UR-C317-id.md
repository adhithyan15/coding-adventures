## You'll want to know: عید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C317-EID317-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عید** — *īd* — "the festival of Eid".

> کل عید ہے۔
>
> *kal īd hai.*
>
> Tomorrow is Eid.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عید** (*īd*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿīd*, "a festival, a day that comes round again". It is feminine: **عید آئی**.
