## You'll want to know: بھالا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C310-DHAL310-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بھالا** — *bhālā* — "a spear".

> اس کے ہاتھ میں لمبا بھالا تھا۔
>
> *us ke hāth meṅ lambā bhālā thā.*
>
> He had a long spear in his hand.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھالا** (*bhālā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bhālā*, "a large spear", under Sanskrit *bhalla* (CDIAL 9409).
