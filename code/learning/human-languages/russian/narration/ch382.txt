Russian, chapter 382: Failing and retaking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
провалить (provalít) — to fail (an exam); to ruin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for lecture notes, then the Russian for a point.

You'll want to know: провалить.
провалить (provalít) — "to fail (an exam); to ruin".
Он провалил экзамен по математике. On provalíl ekzámen po matemátike. He failed his maths exam.
Perfective; imperfective проваливать. Colloquial for exams; the neutral phrase is не сдать экзамен.

The word, taken apart.
провалить (provalít) is built from про- "through" and валить "to topple, to knock down": to make a thing fall through. Провал is a failure, and literally a hole in the ground.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: пересдавать.
пересдавать (peresdavát) — "to retake (an exam)".
Мне придётся пересдавать химию. Mne pridyótsya peresdavát khímiyu. I will have to retake chemistry.
Imperfective; present пересдаю, пересдаёшь; perfective пересдать.

The word, taken apart.
пересдавать (peresdavát) is built from пере- "again" and сдавать "to sit an exam, to hand in", from давать "to give", the ongoing partner of дать.

Lesson 5 of 5.
готовиться (gotóvitsya) — to prepare oneself, to study for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a resit, then the Russian for preparation.

You'll want to know: готовиться.
готовиться (gotóvitsya) — "to prepare oneself, to study for".
Я готовлюсь к экзамену. Ya gotóvlyus k ekzámenu. I am preparing for the exam.
Imperfective, with the present готовлюсь, готовишься; perfective подготовиться. Готовиться к + dative. Without -ся, готовить is to cook or to prepare something.

The word, taken apart.
готовиться (gotóvitsya) is the reflexive of готовить, from готовый "ready", inherited from Proto-Slavic gotovŭ: to make yourself ready.
