## You'll want to know: l'historien
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C325-PASSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'historien** — "the historian".

> Les historiens ne sont pas d'accord sur cette date.
>
> *Historians do not agree on this date.*

Feminine: *l'historienne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*historien* is built on *histoire*, from Latin *historia*, a borrowing from Greek *historía* "inquiry, knowledge" (Lewis & Short).
