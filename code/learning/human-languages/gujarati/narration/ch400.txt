Lesson 1 of 6.
સ્કૂટર (skūṭar) — a scooter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be averted, then the Gujarati for a peep.

You'll want to know: સ્કૂટર.
સ્કૂટર — skūṭar — "a scooter".
મારા ભાઈ પાસે લાલ સ્કૂટર છે. mārā bhāī pāse lāl skūṭar che. My brother has a red scooter.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સ્કૂટર (skūṭar) is borrowed from English scooter.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ઉછાળવું (uchāḷvũ) — to toss (up); to throw up in the air.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a vehicle horn, then the Gujarati for a handlebar.

You'll want to know: ઉછાળવું.
ઉછાળવું — uchāḷvũ — "to toss (up); to throw up in the air".
બાળક દડો ઉપર ઉછાળે છે. bāḷak daḍo upar uchāḷe che. The child tosses the ball up.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દડો ઉછાળ્યો, teṇe daḍo uchāḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઉછાળવું (uchāḷvũ) goes back to ut-śālayati, "throws up", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 1848). ઊછળવું, to bounce, is its intransitive partner.

One more word for getting around.
