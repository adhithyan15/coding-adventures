Hindi, chapter 167: Rent, a Date, a Number, a Floor, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
किराया (kirāyā) — rent, a fare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an advertisement, then the Hindi for a repair.

You'll want to know: किराया.
किराया — kirāyā — "rent, a fare".
[once you have stopped driving — read: the advert — कमरा ख़ाली है। किराया: पाँच सौ रुपये। (kamrā khālī hai. kirāyā: pā̃c sau rupaye.)]
The rent is five hundred rupees.
