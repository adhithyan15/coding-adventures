## You'll want to know: مشغلہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C513-FURSAT513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مشغلہ** — *mashghalā* — "a pastime".

> شطرنج میرے دادا کا مشغلہ ہے۔
>
> *shatranj mere dādā kā mashghalā hai.*
>
> Chess is my grandfather's pastime.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشغلہ** (*mashghalā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mashghala*, "something that keeps one busy".
