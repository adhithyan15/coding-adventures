# ઊગવું (ūgvũ) — to rise, to sprout

## You'll want to know: ઊગવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C218-SUN218-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊગવું** — *ūgvũ* — "to rise, to sprout".

> **કાલે સૂરજ વહેલો ઊગશે.** — *kāle sūraj vahelo ūgśe* — "The sun will rise early tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊગવું** (*ūgvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*udgāti*, "rises, comes forth", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to rise, come out, grow" (CDIAL 1954).
