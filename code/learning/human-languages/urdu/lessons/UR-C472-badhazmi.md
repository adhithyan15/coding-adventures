## You'll want to know: بدہضمی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C472-STOMACH472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بدہضمی** — *badhazmī* — "indigestion".

> زیادہ کھانے سے بدہضمی ہو گئی۔
>
> *zyādā khāne se badhazmī ho gaī.*
>
> Eating too much gave me indigestion.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بدہضمی** (*badhazmī*) is usually traced to Persian *badhazmī*, built from *bad*, "bad", and **ہضم**, "digestion", from Arabic *haḍm*, with the ending *-ī*.
