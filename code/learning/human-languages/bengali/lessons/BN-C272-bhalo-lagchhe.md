# আমার ভালো লাগছে (āmār bhālo lāgchhe) — how I feel

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C272-OBHIGYOTA-01, BN-LEX-C09-BHALOLAGA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-I-THINK-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I had a new experience in the village". (*grāme āmār ekṭā nôtun ôbhigyôtā hôlô*.) Then **ভালো লাগা**, "to like": "good sticks to me".

## You'll want to know: feelings now
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-GENITIVE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C09-BHALOLAGA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-NOW-01, BN-LEX-C169-JINIS169-03, BN-LEX-C169-JINIS169-04, BN-LEX-C45-HEALTH45-01, BN-LEX-C137-QUAL137-04] -->

> **আমার এখানে ভালো লাগছে।**
>
> *āmār ekhāne bhālo lāgchhe.* — "I'm enjoying it here."

Many feelings come to a person, as a thought does: the one who feels is in the **-র** form. Going on now, they take **-ছে**: **আমার ভালো লাগছে**, "I'm enjoying it"; **আমার ভয় করছে** (*āmār bhôy kôrchhe*) or **আমার ভয় লাগছে** (*āmār bhôy lāgchhe*), "I'm scared", with the same **লাগা** as **ভালো লাগা**; **আমার রাগ হচ্ছে** (*āmār rāg hôchchhe*), "I'm getting angry". Words that describe a person take **আমি**, as before: **আমি খুশি**, "I'm happy"; **আমি ক্লান্ত**.

## You'll want to know: feelings then
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-PAST-01]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-LEX-C45-HEALTH45-01, BN-LEX-C07-HOWA-01] -->

In the past, a whole feeling takes the plain past, and a feeling that lasted takes **-ছিল**:

> **গ্রামটা আমার ভালো লাগল, কিন্তু ঝড়ে আমার ভয় করছিল।**
>
> *grāmṭā āmār bhālo lāglô, kintu jhôṛe āmār bhôy kôrchhilô.* — "I liked the village, but I was scared in the storm."

With **হওয়া**, a describing word becomes a change: **সে খুশি হল** (*she khushi hôlô*), "she was pleased".
