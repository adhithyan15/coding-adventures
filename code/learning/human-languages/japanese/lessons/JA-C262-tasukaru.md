## You'll want to know: たすかる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C262-JIKO262-02]; assesses=[] -->

**たすかる** — *tasukaru* — "to be saved; to survive".

> **くるまの じこで ちちは たすかりました。** — *kuruma no jiko de chichi wa tasukarimashita* — "My father came through the car crash alive."

Polite **たすかります**, *tasukarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもは たすかりました。**, *kodomo wa tasukarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たすかる** (*tasukaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the intransitive partner of *tasukeru*, "to help".
