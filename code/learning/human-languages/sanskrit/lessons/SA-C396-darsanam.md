## You'll want to know: दर्शनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C396-DARSHANA396-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दर्शनम्** — *darśanam* — "a sight of the deity; seeing".

> यात्रिकाः प्रातः मन्दिरे दर्शनं लभन्ते।
>
> *yātrikāḥ prātaḥ mandire darśanaṃ labhante.*
>
> In the morning the pilgrims are granted a sight of the deity in the temple.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दर्शनम्** (*darśanam*) is built on the root √dṛś, "to see", with the ending *-ana*; MW gives *darśana* as seeing, sight and an audience, and the sight of a deity at a shrine is that sense in pilgrims' use (MW).
