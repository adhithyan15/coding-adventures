## You'll want to know: غرض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C333-MAQSAD333-03]; assesses=[] -->

**غرض** — *gharaz* — "a motive; self-interest".

> ہر کام کے پیچھے کوئی غرض ہوتی ہے۔
>
> *har kām ke pīchhe koī gharaz hotī hai.*
>
> There is a motive behind every action.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غرض** (*gharaz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *gharaḍ*, "an aim, a purpose"; Urdu often uses it for an aim of one's own.
