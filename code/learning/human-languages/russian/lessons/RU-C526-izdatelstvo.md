## You'll want to know: издательство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C526-BOOKS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**издательство** (*izdátelstvo*) — "a publishing house".

> Его роман вышел в большом издательстве.
>
> *Yegó román výshel v bolshóm izdátelstve.*
>
> His novel came out with a big publishing house.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*издательство* is built on *издатель* 'a publisher', from *издать* 'to publish', which is built on *из-* and *дать* 'to give'; EtymDB lists it, glossed 'publishing house', without a source.
