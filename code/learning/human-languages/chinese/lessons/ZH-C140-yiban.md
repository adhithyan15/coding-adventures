## You'll want to know: 一半
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C140-ORDER140-02]; assesses=[] -->

**一半** — *yíbàn* — "one half; half (of something)".

> **两千的一半是一千** *liǎngqiān de yíbàn shì yìqiān* — Half of two thousand is one thousand.

The new character, in pieces: 半 — two short dots on top, two bars, and a long vertical down through them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The tone of 一 changes: yī is said yí before the fourth tone of bàn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**一半** (*yíbàn*) is built from 一 'one' and 半, inherited from Middle Chinese *'jit* and *panH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second 'a thing divided in the middle', the *bàn* of half past.
