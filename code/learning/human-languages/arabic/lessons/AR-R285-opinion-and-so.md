# Review — an opinion, its reason and what came of it

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Retell the trip to the sea in three steps, with *awwalan*,
*thumma* and *fī an-nihāya*. Then say how it ended, with *ʿindamā*:
*ʿindamā waṣaltu, ḥakaytu al-qiṣṣa*. And one step with *baʿda an*: *baʿda an

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C285-OPINION-ANNA-01, AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-02, AR-GRAMMAR-C285-FA-SO-01, AR-LEX-C40-LIANNA-01] -->

Four lessons, and an opinion has its parts. A question to open it: **عندي
سؤال**, *ʿindī suʾāl*. Three ways to start it: *fī raʾyī*, *aʿtaqidu anna*,
*aẓunnu anna*. A reason after the claim, with *li-anna*; a result after the
reason, with *li-dhālika* or *fa-*.

[PAUSE 2s] All of it in one breath:

> **في رأيي القصة جميلة، لذلك أحكيها كثيرا.** — *fī raʾyī al-qiṣṣa jamīla,
> li-dhālika aḥkīhā kathīran* — In my opinion the story is lovely, so I tell
> it often.
