## You'll want to know: fěnhóng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C139-COLOUR139-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fěnhóng* — "pink".

> *tā de màozi shì fěnhóng de* — Her hat is a pink one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fěnhóng* is built from *fěn* 'powder' and *hóng* 'red', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjunX* and *huwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): red as pale as face powder. The dictionary glosses the first with a powder made by burning lead.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
