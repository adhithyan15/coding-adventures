## You'll want to know: влиять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C349-VLIYANIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**влиять** (*vliyát*) — "to influence, to affect".

> Погода влияет на моё настроение.
>
> *Pogóda vliyáyet na moyó nastroyéniye.*
>
> The weather affects my mood.

Imperfective; perfective *повлиять*. *Влиять на* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**влиять** was taken from Church Slavonic *vŭlĭjati* (EtymDB) "to pour in", built from *в-* "into" and the root of *лить* "to pour". To influence someone was to pour something into them.
