## You'll want to know: a desculpa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C352-REACAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a desculpa** — "the excuse; the apology".

> Ele chegou tarde e deu uma desculpa estranha.
>
> *He arrived late and gave a strange excuse.*

*Pedir desculpa* — to apologise; *desculpa!* — sorry!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desculpa* is built on the verb *desculpar* "to excuse", from *des-* and *culpa* "fault, blame", from Latin *culpa* "fault" (Lewis & Short).
