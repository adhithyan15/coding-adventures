## You'll want to know: پھانکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C477-MEDICINE477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پھانکنا** — *phāṅknā* — "to toss into the mouth (a dry powder)".

> اس نے پڑیا کی دوا پھانکی۔
>
> *us ne puṛiyā kī davā phāṅkī.*
>
> He tossed the powder from the packet into his mouth.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چنے پھانکے, *us ne chane phāṅke*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھانکنا** (*phāṅknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *phā̃knā* under *phakk*, "toss food into the mouth", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9034).
