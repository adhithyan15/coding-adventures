## You'll want to know: certāmen, certāminis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C337-PALMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**certāmen, certāminis** — "a contest, a struggle".

> Certāmen longum et difficile erat.
>
> *The contest was long and hard.*

Neuter, 3rd declension, from *certō*. Any contest, friendly or hostile.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**certāmen** is built on *certō*, "to contend" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-men*.
