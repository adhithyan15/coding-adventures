## You'll want to know: முரசு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C334-THER334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**முரசு** — *muracu* — "a war drum".

> போர் தொடங்கியபோது முரசு ஒலித்தது.
>
> *pōr toṭaṅkiyapōtu muracu olittatu.*
>
> When the war began, the war drum sounded.

முரசு அறை, to beat the drum, once a way to make news known.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**முரசு** (*muracu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4973): a drum, a war drum, from a family of words for making a sound.
