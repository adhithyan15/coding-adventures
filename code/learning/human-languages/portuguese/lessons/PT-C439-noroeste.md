## You'll want to know: o noroeste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C439-CARDEAIS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o noroeste** — "the north-west".

> O vento vem de noroeste.
>
> *The wind is coming from the north-west.*

*Vento de noroeste* — a north-westerly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*noroeste* is built from *norte* and *oeste* on the model of French *nord-ouest*; like *nordeste* (EtymDB), it rests on Germanic words for 'north' and 'west'.
