## You'll want to know: ادائیگی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C418-HISAB418-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ادائیگی** — *adāigī* — "a payment".

> قرض کی ادائیگی اگلے سال تک ہے۔
>
> *qarz kī adāigī agle sāl tak hai.*
>
> The loan has to be paid back by next year.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ادائیگی** (*adāigī*) is built on *adā*, "a payment, a carrying out", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *adāʾ*, "discharging (a debt)", with the Persian ending *-gī*.
