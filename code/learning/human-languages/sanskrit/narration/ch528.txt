Sanskrit, chapter 528: Making a Plan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आयोजयति (āyojayati) — organises; arranges.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for cheers on, then the Sanskrit for a bowstring.

You'll want to know: आयोजयति.
आयोजयति — āyojayati — "organises; arranges".
यदि भवान् इच्छति, तर्हि अहं सभाम् आयोजयामि। yadi bhavān icchati, tarhi ahaṃ sabhām āyojayāmi. If you wish, I will organise a meeting.
Past (he …): सः उत्सवम् आयोजितवान्, saḥ utsavam āyojitavān.
Future: आयोजयिष्यति, āyojayiṣyati.

The word, taken apart.
आयोजयति (āyojayati) is built on ā and the causative of the root √yuj, "to join", which also gives yojayati; MW gives āyojayati as to join together, to form; "to organise" is its modern use (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bowstring, then the Sanskrit for organises.

सामग्री — sāmagrī — "materials".
यदि सामग्री सज्जा अस्ति, तर्हि अहं पायसं पचामि। yadi sāmagrī sajjā asti, tarhi ahaṃ pāyasaṃ pacāmi. If the materials are ready, I'll make rice pudding.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सामग्री (sāmagrī) comes from samagra, "whole, complete", as MW derives it: entirety, and so a complete set of implements or materials (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for organises, then the Sanskrit for materials.

You'll want to know: सम्भारः.
सम्भारः — sambhāraḥ — "supplies; provisions".
यदि अतिथयः आगच्छन्ति, तर्हि सम्भारः आवश्यकः अस्ति। yadi atithayaḥ āgacchanti, tarhi sambhāraḥ āvaśyakaḥ asti. If guests are coming, we need supplies.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सम्भारः (sambhāraḥ) is built on sam, "together", and the root √bhṛ, "to bear, to bring", which also gives bharati; MW gives sambhāra as preparation, provision, the materials needed (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
उपकल्पयति (upakalpayati) — makes ready; prepares.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for materials, then the Sanskrit for supplies.

You'll want to know: उपकल्पयति.
उपकल्पयति — upakalpayati — "makes ready; prepares".
यदि श्वः यात्रा अस्ति, तर्हि अद्य स्यूतम् उपकल्पयतु। yadi śvaḥ yātrā asti, tarhi adya syūtam upakalpayatu. If the trip is tomorrow, get your bag ready today.
Past (he …): सः स्यूतम् उपकल्पितवान्, saḥ syūtam upakalpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपकल्पयति (upakalpayati) is built on upa, "near", and the causative of the root √kḷp, "to be fit, to be in order"; MW gives upakalpayati as to prepare, to make ready, to equip (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पञ्चांगम् (pañcāṅgam) — an almanac.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for supplies, then the Sanskrit for makes ready.

You'll want to know: पञ्चांगम्.
पञ्चांगम् — pañcāṅgam — "an almanac".
यदि पञ्चांगं शुभं दिनं वदति, तर्हि विवाहः भविष्यति। yadi pañcāṅgaṃ śubhaṃ dinaṃ vadati, tarhi vivāhaḥ bhaviṣyati. If the almanac gives a lucky day, the wedding will take place.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पञ्चांगम् (pañcāṅgam) is built from pañca, "five", and aṅga, "a part"; MW gives pañcāṅga as a calendar or almanac, which treats of five things, among them the days and the lunar days (MW).
