## You'll want to know: la conchiglia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C463-SCOGLI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la conchiglia** — "the seashell".

> I bambini raccolgono conchiglie sulla spiaggia.
>
> *The children pick up shells on the beach.*

*Le conchiglie* are also a pasta shape.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conchiglia* is inherited from Latin *conchylium* 'shellfish' (Lewis & Short), from Greek *konkhúlion*, a little *kónkhē* 'mussel'.
