## Script — a hand on top, a cover, and 又 under them
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-AI-LOVE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 爱

**Three pieces, top to bottom.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The top, four strokes.** A short stroke from the right falling to the left, a dot, a second dot, then a short stroke falling to the left on the right: a hand reaching down.

**The cover, two strokes.** A short stroke down on the left, then a bar that runs right, turns down and hooks.

**The bottom, four strokes.** A bar across, a long stroke falling to the left from above the bar, then 又: a bar that turns into a stroke falling to the left, and a long stroke falling to the right.

4 + 2 + 4 = ten.
