## You'll want to know: мрамор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C522-SCULPTURE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**мрамор** (*mrámor*) — "marble".

> Лестница во дворце сделана из мрамора.
>
> *Léstnitsa vo dvortsé sdélana iz mrámora.*
>
> The staircase in the palace is made of marble.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мрамор* was borrowed from Church Slavonic *mramorъ*, the *-ра-* shape of Proto-Slavic *mormorъ*, which came from Latin *marmor* and Greek *mármaros* (EtymDB).
