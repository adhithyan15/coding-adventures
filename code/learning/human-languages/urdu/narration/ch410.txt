Urdu, chapter 410: Landmarks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عمارت (imārat) — a building.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a nook, then the Urdu for to peep.

You'll want to know: عمارت.
عمارت — imārat — "a building".
بینک ایک اونچی عمارت میں ہے۔ baink ek ū̃chī imārat meṅ hai. The bank is in a tall building.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
عمارت (imārat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿimāra, "a building, a thing built".

Lesson 5 of 5.
دکھانا (dikhānā) — to show.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a signboard, then the Urdu for a pole.

You'll want to know: دکھانا.
دکھانا — dikhānā — "to show".
ایک راہگیر نے ہمیں راستہ دکھایا۔ ek rāhgīr ne hameṅ rāstā dikhāyā. A passer-by showed us the way.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نقشہ دکھایا, us ne naqshā dikhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
دکھانا (dikhānā) is built on دیکھنا, "to see", which comes from dekṣati, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6507): it is the form for making someone see.
