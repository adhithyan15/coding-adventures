## You'll want to know: la flora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C695-FAUNA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la flora** — "the flora, the plant life".

> Aquí la flora es muy variada.
>
> *The plant life here is very varied.*

Often with *la fauna*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flora* is a learned borrowing from Latin *Flōra*, the Roman goddess of flowers (Lewis & Short), from *flōs* "flower".
