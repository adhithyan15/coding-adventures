## You'll want to know: ਸਬਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C359-STUDY359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਬਕ** — *sabak* — "a lesson to learn".

> ਅੱਜ ਦਾ ਸਬਕ ਛੋਟਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *ajj dā sabak chhoṭā hai.*
>
> Today's lesson is short.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਬਕ** (*sabak*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *sabaq*, "lesson".
