## You'll want to know: રજાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C411-QUILT411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**રજાઈ** — *rajāī* — "a quilt".

> શિયાળામાં રાત્રે હું ભારે રજાઈ ઓઢું છું.
>
> *śiyāḷāmā̃ rātre hũ bhāre rajāī oḍhũ chũ.*
>
> On winter nights I wrap myself in a heavy quilt.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રજાઈ** (*rajāī*) is usually traced to Persian *razāī*, "a quilt", a cover stuffed with cotton.
