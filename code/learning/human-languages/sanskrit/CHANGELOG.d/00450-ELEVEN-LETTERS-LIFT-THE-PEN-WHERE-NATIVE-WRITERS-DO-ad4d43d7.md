## Eleven letters lift the pen where native writers do

- The writing steps for क (SA-S111), य (SA-S204), र (SA-S110), प (SA-S113), ध (SA-S212),
  ल (SA-S213), द (SA-S200), ठ (SA-S117) and ष (SA-S118) now lift the pen only where most native
  writers do (HP Labs India's native-writer samples): once, before the headline,
  for the first eight, and twice for ष (before the diagonal and the
  headline). The cited
  animations stage each letter one part at a time; their parts are still
  taught in the same order and direction, joined by short climbs up (or
  back along) ink the pen draws anyway.
- Each lesson's "Pen lifts" line, step list and source note changed, and
  the filmstrips were redrawn to match.
