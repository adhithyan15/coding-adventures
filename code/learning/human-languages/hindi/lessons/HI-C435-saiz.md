## You'll want to know: साइज़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C435-REZGARI435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**साइज़** — *sāiz* — "a size".

> इस कमीज़ का साइज़ क्या है?
>
> *is kamīz kā sāiz kyā hai?*
>
> What size is this shirt?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साइज़** (*sāiz*) is borrowed from English *size*.
