# മൊഴി നൽകുക (moḻi nalkuka) — to testify; to give a statement

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C370-MOZHI370-02, ML-LEX-C370-MOZHI370-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an eyewitness, then the Malayalam for a lie.

## You'll want to know: മൊഴി നൽകുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C370-MOZHI370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**മൊഴി നൽകുക** — *moḻi nalkuka* — "to testify; to give a statement".

> ദൃക്സാക്ഷി പോലീസിന് മൊഴി നൽകി.
>
> *dṛksākṣi pōlīsinŭ moḻi nalki.*
>
> The eyewitness gave a statement to the police.

Past: മൊഴി നൽകി, *moḻi nalki*.

Who receives it takes -ക്ക് or -ന്: പോലീസിന് മൊഴി നൽകി.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മൊഴി നൽകുക** (*moḻi nalkuka*) joins മൊഴി (*moḻi*), "a statement", a native Dravidian word (DEDR 4989), and നൽകുക, "to give".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
