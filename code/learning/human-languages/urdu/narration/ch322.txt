Urdu, chapter 322: The Month of Fasting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
روزہ (rozā) — a fast (from dawn to dusk).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for gathered together, then the Urdu for a spectacle.

You'll want to know: روزہ.
روزہ — rozā — "a fast (from dawn to dusk)".
اس نے آج روزہ رکھا۔ us ne āj rozā rakhā. He fasted today.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
روزہ (rozā) is usually traced to Persian rōza, "a fast", built on rōz, "a day", the word inside روز. A fast is kept: روزہ رکھا.

افطاری — iftārī — "the food that breaks a fast".

Feminine.

افطاری (iftārī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ifṭār, "a breaking of the fast", with the ending -ī: the dates, fruit and fried food set out at sunset.

The word, taken apart.
خیرات (khairāt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khayrāt, "good deeds, good things", a plural; in Urdu it is what is given to the poor.
