## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**يسار** (*yasār*) is built on the native Arabic root *y-s-r*, "ease; the left hand" (Lane). Lane gives *yasār* as the left hand and also as ease, wealth.
