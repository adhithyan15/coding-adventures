## You'll want to know: ратуша
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C444-RATUSHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ратуша** (*rátusha*) — "a town hall (in an old town)".

> На площади стоит старая ратуша.
>
> *Na plóshchadi stoít stáraya rátusha.*
>
> The old town hall stands on the square.

Feminine. Said of old town halls in central Europe and the Baltic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ратуша* came through Old East Slavic *ratuša* from Polish *ratusz* (EtymDB), a cousin of German *Rathaus* 'a council house'.
