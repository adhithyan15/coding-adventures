## You'll want to know: причём
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C347-ODNAKO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**причём** (*prichyóm*) — "and what is more, moreover".

> Он опоздал, причём уже в третий раз.
>
> *On opozdál, prichyóm uzhé v trétiy raz.*
>
> He was late, and what is more, for the third time.

It adds a detail that sharpens what came before. Stress on the second syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**причём** is fused from *при* "at, alongside" and *чём*, the prepositional case of *что*: "alongside which".
