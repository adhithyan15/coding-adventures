Japanese, chapter 409: Regions and Borders.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ちいき (chiiki) — an area; a district.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for religion, then the Japanese for to enshrine.

You'll want to know: ちいき.
ちいき — chiiki — "an area; a district".
この ちいきは ゆきが おおいです。 — kono chiiki wa yuki ga ōi desu — "This region gets a lot of snow."

The word, taken apart.
ちいき (chiiki) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are chi, "ground" (Middle Chinese dijH) and iki, "region" (hwik) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.

さかい — sakai — "a boundary".

The word, taken apart.
さかい (sakai) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
