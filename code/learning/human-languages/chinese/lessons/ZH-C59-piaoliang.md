## You'll want to know: piàoliang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C59-WEAR59-05]; assesses=[] -->

*piàoliang* — "pretty; good-looking".

> *tā de yǎnjing hěn piàoliang* — She has beautiful eyes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piàoliang* is built from piào and liang, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *phjiewH* and *ljangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as beating silk floss in water and the second as 'bright'; how the pair came to mean pretty is not settled.
