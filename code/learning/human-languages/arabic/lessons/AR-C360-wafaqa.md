## You'll want to know: وافق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C360-JADAL360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**وافق** — *wāfaqa* — "to approve; to agree to".

> وافق المدير على الخطة الجديدة.
>
> *wāfaqa al-mudīr ʿalā al-khiṭṭa al-jadīda.*
>
> The manager approved the new plan.

Present: يوافق, *yuwāfiq*.

It takes على: وافق على …. اتفق is two sides agreeing with each other.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وافق** (*wāfaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-f-q*, "to fit, to suit" (Lane). It is Form III, to be in accord with: to agree to a thing.
