## You'll want to know: મુકાદમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C511-MAJUR511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મુકાદમ** — *mukādam* — "a foreman".

> મુકાદમે મજૂરોને કામ સમજાવ્યું.
>
> *mukādme majūrone kām samjāvyũ.*
>
> The foreman explained the work to the labourers.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુકાદમ** (*mukādam*) is usually traced to Arabic *muqaddam*, "one placed in front, a headman", which came to Gujarati through Persian.
