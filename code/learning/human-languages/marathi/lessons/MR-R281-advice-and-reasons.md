# Review — advice and reasons

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C280-VATTE-KI-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C280-MHANUN-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think it will rain tomorrow, so I will stay at home". (*malā vāṭtaṁ kī udyā pāūs paḍel, mhaṇūn mī gharī rāhīn*.) Then say **ऋतू**, and **ज्ञान** with its *dny*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C281-AYLA-PAHIJE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C281-U-NAYE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C281-MULE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C281-REASON-ORDER-01, MR-LEX-C281-JNAN-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C280-MAJHYA-MATE-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-LEX-C279-RTU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I must go home now, because it is late.]
- [YOU SAY: You shouldn't go at night.]
- [YOU SAY: Because of the rain, the train came late.]
- [YOU SAY: Why didn't you come? I didn't come, because I was ill.]
- [YOU SAY: In my opinion knowledge grows because of books.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C281-AYLA-PAHIJE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C281-U-NAYE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C281-MULE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C281-REASON-ORDER-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "Must"? (**-ायला पाहिजे**.) "Should not"? (**-ऊ नये**.) "Because of"? (**-मुळे**.) Which letter hides in **ज्ञ**? (**ञ**.) Which starts **ऋतू**? (**ऋ**.)
