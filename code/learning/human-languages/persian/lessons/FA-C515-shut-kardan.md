## You'll want to know: شوت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C515-MOVES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شوت کردن** (*shut kardan*) — "to shoot (the ball)".

> او از وسط زمین شوت کرد.
>
> *u az vasat-e zamin shut kard.*
>
> He shot from the middle of the pitch.

*shut* 'a shot' + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shut* was borrowed from English *shoot* (EtymDB). *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
