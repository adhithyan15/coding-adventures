Portuguese, chapter 228: Jar, Slice, Piece, Dozen, Half.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o frasco — a jar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a shopping centre, then the Portuguese for a shop window.

You'll want to know: o frasco.
