## You'll want to know: دستاویز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C336-SABIT336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دستاویز** — *dastāvez* — "a document".

> یہ دستاویز ہمارے دفتر میں ہے۔
>
> *yih dastāvez hamāre daftar meṅ hai.*
>
> This document is in our office.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دستاویز** (*dastāvez*) is usually traced to Persian *dastāwēz*, "a document, a deed".
