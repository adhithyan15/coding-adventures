Malayalam, chapter 446: Clear and Reliable.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വ്യക്തമായ (vyaktamāya) — evident; clear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for serious, then the Malayalam for complex.

You'll want to know: വ്യക്തമായ.
വ്യക്തമായ — vyaktamāya — "evident; clear".
അവൻ വ്യക്തമായ ഒരു ഉത്തരം നൽകി. avan vyaktamāya oru uttaraṁ nalki. He gave a clear answer.
The adverb is വ്യക്തമായി, clearly.

The word, taken apart.
വ്യക്തമായ (vyaktamāya) is built from Sanskrit vyakta, "adorned; manifest, evident" (CDIAL 12156; Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ആയ.

What you've built.

ആ വാർത്ത വിശ്വസനീയമായ ഒരു ഉറവിടത്തിൽ നിന്നാണ്. ā vārtta viśvasanīyamāya oru uṟaviṭattil ninnāṇŭ. That news is from a reliable source.
