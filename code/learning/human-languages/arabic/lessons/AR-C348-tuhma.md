## You'll want to know: تهمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C348-TUHMA348-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تهمة** — *tuhma* — "an accusation, a charge".

> كانت التهمة سرقة سيارة.
>
> *kānat at-tuhma sariqat sayyāra.*
>
> The charge was stealing a car.

Plural: تهم, *tuham*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تهمة** (*tuhma*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-h-m* (Lane), "to fancy, to suspect". In *tuhma* the *w* of the root shows as *t*, as in *ittahama*.
