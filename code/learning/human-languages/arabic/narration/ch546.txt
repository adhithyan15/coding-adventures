Arabic, chapter 546: Match day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شوط (shawṭ) — a half (of a match).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to create, then the Arabic for to direct films.

You'll want to know: شوط.
شوط — shawṭ — "a half (of a match)".
سجل الفريق هدفا في الشوط الأول. sajjala al-farīq hadafan fī ash-shawṭ al-awwal. The team scored a goal in the first half.
Plural: أشواط, ashwāṭ.

The word, taken apart.
شوط (shawṭ) is built on the native Arabic root sh-w-ṭ, "a run, a single course" (Lane). Lane gives shawṭ as one run of a course: one stretch of a race.
