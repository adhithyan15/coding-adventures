# Review — what you should do, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-DU-SOLLTEST-01, GE-GRAMMAR-VERBKLAMMER-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "you should read the timetable". (*Du solltest den
Fahrplan lesen.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-MAN-SOLLTE-02, GE-GRAMMAR-ERKLAEREN-WARUM-01, GE-GRAMMAR-ERKLAERUNG-DREI-02, GE-GRAMMAR-MEINUNG-ERSTE-STELLE-02] -->

Two chapters, and the whole of giving reasons is in place. An opinion,
with *dass* or *meiner Meinung nach*. A reason with *weil*, a consequence
with *darum*. Advice with *du solltest* or *man sollte*. And a question
folded into a sentence, with its verb at the end: *erklären, warum …*

[PAUSE 2s] Here is a friend's problem, and an answer that uses three of
those pieces:

> *Der Zug hat Verspätung. Was soll ich machen?*
> *Meiner Meinung nach solltest du ein Taxi nehmen, weil es regnet.*

Listen to the answer: after *meiner Meinung nach*, the modal comes
straight next, *solltest du*, and the infinitive *nehmen* waits at the end
of its clause.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-DU-SOLLTEST-01, GE-GRAMMAR-ERKLAERUNG-DREI-02, GE-GRAMMAR-ERKLAEREN-WARUM-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "One should"? (***Man sollte.***) After *meiner Meinung nach*,
which comes next? (**The verb**, *solltest du*.) Inside *erklären, warum*,
where is the verb? (**At the end.**)
