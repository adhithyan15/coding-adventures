Tamil, chapter 400: Platforms and Tracks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
நடைமேடை (naṭaimēṭai) — a platform; a raised walkway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a complaint, then the Tamil for an athlete.

You'll want to know: நடைமேடை.
நடைமேடை — naṭaimēṭai — "a platform; a raised walkway".
ரயில் மூன்றாவது நடைமேடைக்கு வருகிறது. rayil mūṉṟāvatu naṭaimēṭaikku varukiṟatu. The train is coming to the third platform.
Before -க்கு the final -ஐ stays as it is: நடைமேடைக்கு, to the platform.

The word, taken apart.
நடைமேடை (naṭaimēṭai) is built from naṭai, "walking, gait" (DEDR 3582, the family of நட, to walk), and mēṭai, "a raised floor, a stage", whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
தண்டவாளம் (taṇṭavāḷam) — a railway track; a rail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an athlete, then the Tamil for a platform.

You'll want to know: தண்டவாளம்.
தண்டவாளம் — taṇṭavāḷam — "a railway track; a rail".
குழந்தைகள் தண்டவாளத்தில் நடக்கக் கூடாது. kuḻantaikaḷ taṇṭavāḷattil naṭakkak kūṭātu. Children must not walk on the railway track.
A -அம் noun takes -அத்து- before a case ending: தண்டவாளத்தில், on the rails.

The word, taken apart.
தண்டவாளம் (taṇṭavāḷam) is listed in DEDR 3050 with the senses "cast iron, an iron rail, a girder"; where the word comes from before that is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
வருகை (varukai) — arrival; a visit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a platform, then the Tamil for a railway track.

You'll want to know: வருகை.
வருகை — varukai — "arrival; a visit".
விமானத்தின் வருகை ஒரு மணி நேரம் தாமதமானது. vimāṉattiṉ varukai oru maṇi nēram tāmatamāṉatu. The plane's arrival was an hour late.
வருகை (varukai) is a noun made from வா, to come: its stem வரு- takes the ending -கை.

The word, taken apart.
வருகை (varukai) is built on varu-, the stem of வா, "to come", with the noun ending -kai; DEDR 5270 gathers the family, including வரவு, "coming, income".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ஒத்திவை (ottivai) — to postpone; to put off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a railway track, then the Tamil for arrival.

You'll want to know: ஒத்திவை.
ஒத்திவை — ottivai — "to postpone; to put off".
மழை பெய்ததால் நாங்கள் பயணத்தை ஒத்திவைத்தோம். maḻai peytatāl nāṅkaḷ payaṇattai ottivaittōm. Because it rained, we put off the trip.
Past (I …): ஒத்திவைத்தேன், ottivaittēṉ.
What is put off takes -ஐ: பயணத்தை ஒத்திவை (ottivai), to postpone a journey.

The word, taken apart.
ஒத்திவை (ottivai) is built from otti, the joining form of ஒத்து, "to move aside, make room" (DEDR 973), and வை, "to put": to put a thing aside for later.

Lesson 5 of 5.
மாறு (māṟu) — to switch; to change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for arrival, then the Tamil for to postpone.

You'll want to know: மாறு.
மாறு — māṟu — "to switch; to change".
அடுத்த நிலையத்தில் நாங்கள் ரயில் மாற வேண்டும். aṭutta nilaiyattil nāṅkaḷ rayil māṟa vēṇṭum. We have to change trains at the next station.
Past (I …): மாறினேன், māṟiṉēṉ.
மாறு (māṟu) is to change or become different oneself; மாற்று is to change something.

The word, taken apart.
மாறு (māṟu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4834); the same article holds the transitive மாற்று, "to change something".
