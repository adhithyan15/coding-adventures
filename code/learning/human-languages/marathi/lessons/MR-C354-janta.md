## You'll want to know: जनता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C354-NAGARIK354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जनता** — *jantā* — "the general public; the people".

> नव्या पुलाचा जनतेला खूप फायदा झाला.
>
> *navyā pulācā jantelā khūp phāydā jhālā.*
>
> The public benefited greatly from the new bridge.

Feminine.

Feminine and singular, though it means many people: जनता जमली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जनता** (*jantā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *janatā*, "a number of people, the people" (Monier-Williams).
