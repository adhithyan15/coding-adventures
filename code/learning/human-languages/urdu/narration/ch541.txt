Urdu, chapter 541: In That Case.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
زاویہ (zāviyā) — an angle; a point of view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for damages (to be paid), then the Urdu for to be robbed.

You'll want to know: زاویہ.
زاویہ — zāviyā — "an angle; a point of view".
اگر آپ اس زاویے سے سوچتے تو بات سمجھ جاتے۔ agar āp is zāviye se sochte to bāt samajh jāte. If you looked at it from this angle, you would understand.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
زاویہ (zāviyā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic zāwiya, "a corner, an angle".

Lesson 2 of 5.
حالات (hālāt) — circumstances.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be robbed, then the Urdu for an angle.

You'll want to know: حالات.
حالات — hālāt — "circumstances".
اگر حالات بدلے تو ہم اپنا پروگرام بدلیں گے۔ agar hālāt badle to ham apnā progrām badleṅ ge. If circumstances change, we will change our plans.
Masculine, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
حالات (hālāt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥālāt, the plural of ḥāl, "a state, a condition".

Lesson 5 of 5.
بالفرض (bilfarz) — supposing (for argument's sake).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for conditional, then the Urdu for a point (that things come to).

You'll want to know: بالفرض.
بالفرض — bilfarz — "supposing (for argument's sake)".
بالفرض اگر بارش ہوئی تو ہم کیا کریں گے؟ bilfarz agar bārish huī to ham kyā kareṅ ge? Supposing it rains, what will we do?

The word, taken apart.
بالفرض (bilfarz) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic bi-l-farḍ, "by way of supposing", built from bi, "by", al, "the", and farḍ, the word Urdu also has as فرض (farz), "a duty".
