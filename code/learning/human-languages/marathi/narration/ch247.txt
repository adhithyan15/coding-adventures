Marathi, chapter 247: Job, Degree, Homework, Education, Feeling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नोकरी (nokrī) — a job.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a planet, then the Marathi for freedom.

You'll want to know: नोकरी.
नोकरी — nokrī — "a job".

The word, taken apart.
नोकरी (nokrī) is nokar, "servant", with the ending -ī. Nokar is usually traced to Persian naukar, which Persian took from Mongolic nökör, "companion".
