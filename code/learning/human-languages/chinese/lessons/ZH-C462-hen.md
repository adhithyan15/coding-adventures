## You'll want to know: hèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C462-SURPRISE462-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hèn* — "to hate deeply; to resent".

> *nǐ hái hèn tā ma* — Do you still hate him?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hèn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *honH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'resentment'.
