## You'll want to know: ਮਿਹਨਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C359-STUDY359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਿਹਨਤ** — *mihnat* — "hard work; effort".

> ਉਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਿਹਨਤ ਕਰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *oh bahut mihnat kardī hai.*
>
> She works very hard.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਿਹਨਤ** (*mihnat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *miḥna*, "trial".
