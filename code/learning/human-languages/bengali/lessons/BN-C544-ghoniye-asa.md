## You'll want to know: ঘনিয়ে আসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C544-SOMOY544-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘনিয়ে আসা** — *ghôniye āsā* — "to draw near (of a date or danger)".

> বিয়ের দিন ঘনিয়ে এল।
>
> *Biyer din ghôniye elô.*
>
> The wedding day drew near.

Past (he/she …): সেই দিন ঘনিয়ে এল, *sei din ghôniye elô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘনিয়ে আসা** (*ghôniye āsā*) joins ঘনিয়ে, the -এ form of a verb built on ঘন, "dense, close", to আসা, "to come": coming closer and closer. CDIAL lists Bengali *ghana*, "thick, dense", under Sanskrit *ghana*, and notes a Bengali verb from it meaning to come close (CDIAL 4424).
