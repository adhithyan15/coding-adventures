## You'll want to know: शीकरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C437-PRAVRSH437-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शीकरः** — *śīkaraḥ* — "drizzle; spray".

> प्रातः शीकराः पतन्ति।
>
> *prātaḥ śīkarāḥ patanti.*
>
> In the morning a drizzle falls.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शीकरः** (*śīkaraḥ*) is the word MW gives, mostly in the plural, for fine or drizzling rain and spray; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
