## You'll want to know: सन्नाटा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C307-PHANSA307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्नाटा** — *sannāṭā* — "a dead silence".

> रात को गाँव में सन्नाटा था।
>
> *rāt ko gā̃v meṁ sannāṭā thā.*
>
> At night a dead silence lay over the village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्नाटा** (*sannāṭā*) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit *saṁnaṣṭa* (CDIAL 12925). It is not a calm quiet but a silence that frightens: **सन्नाटा छा गया**, a hush fell.
