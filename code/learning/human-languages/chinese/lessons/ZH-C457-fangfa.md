## You'll want to know: 方法
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C457-HELP457-05]; assesses=[] -->

**方法** — *fāngfǎ* — "a way to solve a problem; means".

> **找不到路的时候你有什么好方法** *zhǎo bu dào lù de shíhou nǐ yǒu shénme hǎo fāngfǎ* — When you can't find the way, what do you do?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**方法** (*fāngfǎ*) is built from 方 'a technique' and 法 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjang* and *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, as an art or technique and the second 'a rule'.
