## You'll want to know: أدان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C334-MUHAKAMA334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أدان** — *adāna* — "to convict; to condemn".

> أدانت المحكمة الرجل بالسرقة.
>
> *adānat al-maḥkama ar-rajul bi-s-sariqa.*
>
> The court convicted the man of theft.

Present: يدين, *yudīn*.

Of words: أدان الهجوم, he condemned the attack.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أدان** (*adāna*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-y-n* (Lane), the root of *dayn*, a debt, and of a judgment that is owed. Modern Arabic gives Form IV *adāna* the sense "to find guilty".
