## You'll want to know: пеленать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C496-BABY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**пеленать** (*pelenát*) — "to swaddle".

> Медсестра показала нам, как пеленать ребёнка.
>
> *Medsestrá pokazála nam, kak pelenát rebyónka.*
>
> The nurse showed us how to swaddle the baby.

Imperfective; perfective запеленать. The cloth is a *пелёнка*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пеленать* is built on *пелена* 'a cloth, a veil', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *pelena* 'thin skin, thin fabric' (EtymDB); EtymDB has no entry for the verb.
