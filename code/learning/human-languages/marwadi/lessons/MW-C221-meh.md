## You'll want to know: मेह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C221-RAIN221-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मेह** — *meh* — "the rains; rain".

> सावण मांय मेह आवै है।
>
> *sāvaṇ mā̃y meh āvai hai.*
>
> The rains come in the monsoon month.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेह** (*meh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *megha*, "a cloud, rain"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *meha*, "rain" (CDIAL 10302).
