## You'll want to know: el arcén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C642-CARRETERA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el arcén** — "the hard shoulder (of a road)".

> Paramos en el arcén con las luces encendidas.
>
> *We stopped on the hard shoulder with the lights on.*

A Spain word; Mexico says *el acotamiento* and Argentina *la banquina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *arcén* is not settled.
