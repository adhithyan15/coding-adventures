Lesson 2 of 5.
ભગવાન (bhagvān) — god; the Lord.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to offer (to a god), then the Gujarati for a legend.

You'll want to know: ભગવાન.
ભગવાન — bhagvān — "god; the Lord".
લોકોએ ભગવાનને પ્રાર્થના કરી. lokoe bhagvānne prārthanā karī. The people prayed to god.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભગવાન (bhagvān) is Sanskrit bhagavān, the nominative of bhagavat, "fortunate, blessed", and so "the Lord" (Monier-Williams). Gujarati keeps the nominative ending as the whole word.

દૈત્ય (daitya) is a tatsama, Sanskrit daitya, "a son of Diti, a demon" (Monier-Williams): in the old stories, the enemies of the gods.

વરદાન (vardān) is Sanskrit varadāna, "the granting of a boon" (Monier-Williams), built from vara, a wish chosen, and dāna, a giving. A god વરદાન આપે છે.
