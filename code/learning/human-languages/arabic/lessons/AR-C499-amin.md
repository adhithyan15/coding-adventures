## You'll want to know: أمين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C499-AMANA499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أمين** — *amīn* — "honest, trustworthy".

> المحاسب رجل أمين.
>
> *al-muḥāsib rajul amīn.*
>
> The accountant is an honest man.

It also names a keeper of something: أمين المكتبة, the librarian.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أمين** (*amīn*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-m-n*, "to be safe, to trust" (Lane). Āmin, safe, which you know, comes from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
