Hindi, chapter 388: More and Less.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अधिक (adhik) — more (formal), excessive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for plenty, then the Hindi for tremendous.

You'll want to know: अधिक.
अधिक — adhik — "more (formal), excessive".
अगर पानी अधिक होगा तो चावल गीला हो जाएगा। agar pānī adhik hogā to cāval gīlā ho jāegā. If there is too much water, the rice will turn soggy.

The word, taken apart.
अधिक (adhik) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit adhika, "surpassing, more" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for tremendous, then the Hindi for more (formal).

You'll want to know: तादाद.
तादाद — tādād — "a number, a quantity (of people or things)".
अगर मेहमानों की तादाद बढ़ेगी तो खाना कम पड़ेगा। agar mehmānoṁ kī tādād baṛhegī to khānā kam paṛegā. If the number of guests goes up, the food will run short.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तादाद (tādād) is usually traced to Arabic taʿdād, "counting, a number", which reached Hindi through Persian.
