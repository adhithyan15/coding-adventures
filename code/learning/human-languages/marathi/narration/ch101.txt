Marathi, chapter 101: Mouse, Lion, Tiger, Elephant, Monkey.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उंदीर (undīr) — a mouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sheep, then the Marathi for a bird.

You'll want to know: उंदीर.
उंदीर — undīr — "a mouse".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: undīr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: undīr, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say undīr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a sheep, then the Marathi for a bird, then say undīr again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does उंदीर (undīr) mean? ("A mouse".) Say it once more.
