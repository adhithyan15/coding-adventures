## You'll want to know: 不必
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C360-NEED360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**不必** — *búbì* — "need not; there's no need to".

> **你不必说对不起你没有错** *nǐ búbì shuō duìbuqǐ nǐ méiyǒu cuò* — You needn't apologize; you did nothing wrong.

The tone of bù changes here: before a falling tone it is said bú, and the spelling writes the change.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**不必** (*búbì*) is built from 不 'not' and 必 'surely', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjut* and *pjit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'certain' and 'so'.
