## You'll want to know: きんし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C326-KISOKU326-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きんし** — *kinshi* — "a ban; a prohibition".

> **かわは あぶないから、きんしに なりました。** — *kawa wa abunai kara, kinshi ni narimashita* — "The river is dangerous, so it has been put off limits."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きんし** (*kinshi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kin*, "prohibition" (Middle Chinese *kimH*) and *shi*, "stop" (*tsyiX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
