Marathi, chapter 106: Clean, Sweet, Thick, Thin, Light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्वच्छ (svachchha) — clean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for poor, then the Marathi for dirt; dirty.

You'll want to know: स्वच्छ.
स्वच्छ — svachchha — "clean".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: svachchha]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: svachchha, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say svachchha]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for poor, then the Marathi for dirt; dirty, then say svachchha again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does स्वच्छ (svachchha) mean? ("Clean".) Say it once more.
