## You'll want to know: أبدع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C545-SHIR545-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أبدع** — *abdaʿa* — "to create; to excel".

> أبدعت الفرقة في الحفلة.
>
> *abdaʿat al-firqa fī al-ḥafla.*
>
> The band was brilliant at the party.

Present: يبدع, *yubdiʿ*.

أبدع في … is to do brilliantly at a thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أبدع** (*abdaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-d-ʿ*, "to originate, to make a thing new" (Lane). Mubdiʿ, creative, is its participle.

## What you've built
