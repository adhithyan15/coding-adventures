## You'll want to know: подгорать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C332-ZAPEKAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**подгорать** (*podgorát*) — "to get burnt (of food), to catch".

> Пирог немного подгорел.
>
> *Piróg nemnógo podgorél.*
>
> The pie got a little burnt.

Imperfective; perfective *подгореть* (*podgorét*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подгорать* is built on *под-* "underneath" and *гореть* "to burn", inherited from Proto-Slavic *gorěti* (EtymDB): to burn at the bottom.
