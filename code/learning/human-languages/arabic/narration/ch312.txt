Arabic, chapter 312: Discoveries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
كشف (kashafa) — to uncover, to reveal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to have a child, then the Arabic for a naming.

You'll want to know: كشف.
كشف — kashafa — "to uncover, to reveal".
كشف التحقيق سبب الحريق. kashafa at-taḥqīq sabab al-ḥarīq. The investigation revealed the cause of the fire.
Present: يكشف, yakshif.
كشف (kashafa) عن: to disclose. A medical check-up is كشف (kashafa) طبي.

The word, taken apart.
كشف (kashafa) is built on the native Arabic root k-sh-f (Lane), "to take away a covering from a thing".

Lesson 3 of 5.
عثر (ʿathara) — to come across, to find.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to uncover, then the Arabic for to discover.

You'll want to know: عثر.
عثر — ʿathara — "to come across, to find".
عثر الولد على مفتاح في الحديقة. ʿathara al-walad ʿalā miftāḥ fī al-ḥadīqa. The boy came across a key in the garden.
Present: يعثر, yaʿthur.
It takes على: عثر (ʿathara) على.

The word, taken apart.
عثر (ʿathara) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-th-r (Lane), "to stumble", the root of the taught taʿaththara. To stumble upon a thing is to find it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
كنز (kanz) — a treasure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to come across, then the Arabic for an explorer.

You'll want to know: كنز.
كنز — kanz — "a treasure".
وجد الصياد كنزا تحت الرمل. wajada aṣ-ṣayyād kanzan taḥta ar-raml. The fisherman found a treasure under the sand.
Plural: كنوز, kunūz.

The word, taken apart.
كنز (kanz) is built on the root k-n-z, "to store up, to bury wealth" (Lane). A link with Persian ganj, treasure, is often proposed, but the origin is not settled.
