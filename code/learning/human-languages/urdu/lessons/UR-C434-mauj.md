## You'll want to know: موج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C434-MAUJ434-01]; assesses=[] -->

**موج** — *mauj* — "a wave; a billow".

> سمندر میں اونچی موجیں ہیں۔
>
> *samandar meṅ ū̃chī maujeṅ haiṅ.*
>
> There are high waves on the sea.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**موج** (*mauj*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mawj*, "waves, surging".
