# शिष्टः (śiṣṭaḥ) — well-bred; cultured

## You'll want to know: शिष्टः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C467-SISTA467-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शिष्टः** — *śiṣṭaḥ* — "well-bred; cultured".

> शिष्टः बालः अतिथिं नमति।
>
> *śiṣṭaḥ bālaḥ atithiṃ namati.*
>
> The well-bred boy bows to the guest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिष्टः** (*śiṣṭaḥ*) is built on the root √śās, "to instruct, to train": one who has been taught; MW gives "disciplined, cultured, educated, wise" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
