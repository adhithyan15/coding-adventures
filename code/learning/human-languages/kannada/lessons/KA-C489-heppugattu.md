# ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟು (heppugaṭṭu) — to freeze; to set

## You'll want to know: ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C489-IBBANI489-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟು** — *heppugaṭṭu* — "to freeze; to set".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ಚಳಿಗೆ ಬಕೆಟ್ನ ನೀರು ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟಿತು.
>
> *rātri caḷige bakeṭna nīru heppugaṭṭitu.*
>
> In the cold of the night the water in the bucket froze.

Past (it …): ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟಿತು, *heppugaṭṭitu*.

In the kitchen it is milk setting into curd: ಹೆಪ್ಪು is the starter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟು** (*heppugaṭṭu*) is a native Kannada compound of *heppu*, "a curdling agent" (DEDR 4421), and ಕಟ್ಟು, "to gather, to harden" (DEDR 1148).
