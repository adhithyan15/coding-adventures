## You'll want to know: झूंपड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C188-PHUL188-04]; assesses=[] -->

**झूंपड़ी** — *jhū̃pṛī* — "a hut".

> खेत मांय एक छोटी झूंपड़ी है।
>
> *khet mā̃y ek chhoṭī jhū̃pṛī hai.*
>
> There's a small hut in the field.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झूंपड़ी** (*jhū̃pṛī*) comes from a reconstructed *\*jhuppa*, "a hut"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *jhũpṛī* and Hindi *jhopṛī* (CDIAL 5403). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
