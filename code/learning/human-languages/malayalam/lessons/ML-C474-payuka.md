# പായുക (pāyuka) — to speed along; to rush

## You'll want to know: പായുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C474-TIVANDI474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പായുക** — *pāyuka* — "to speed along; to rush".

> ആംബുലൻസ് ആശുപത്രിയിലേക്ക് പാഞ്ഞു.
>
> *āmbulansŭ āśupatriyilēkkŭ pāññu.*
>
> The ambulance sped to the hospital.

Past: പാഞ്ഞു, *pāññu*.

The past is പാഞ്ഞു: a verb in -ആയുക makes its past in -ആഞ്ഞു.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പായുക** (*pāyuka*) is native Dravidian, "to leap, to rush out" (DEDR 4087).
