Russian, chapter 375: Without fail.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
гарантировать (garantírovat) — to guarantee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to succeed, then the Russian for to work out.

You'll want to know: гарантировать.
гарантировать (garantírovat) — "to guarantee".
Я не могу гарантировать, что всё получится. Ya ne mogú garantírovat, chto vsyo polúchitsya. I cannot guarantee that everything will work out.
The same form serves as both imperfective and perfective; present гарантирую, гарантируешь.

The word, taken apart.
гарантировать (garantírovat) is usually traced, through German, to French garantir "to guarantee" (EtymDB), built on garant "a guarantor"; EtymDB sets garant beside Old High German werento "a guarantor" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
обязательно (obyazátelno) — definitely, without fail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to work out, then the Russian for to guarantee.

You'll want to know: обязательно.
обязательно (obyazátelno) — "definitely, without fail".
Я обязательно позвоню тебе завтра. Ya obyazátelno pozvonyú tebé závtra. I will definitely call you tomorrow.
It also means necessarily: не обязательно (obyazátelno) — not necessarily.

The word, taken apart.
обязательно (obyazátelno) is built on обязать "to oblige", from о- and вязать "to tie, to bind", Proto-Slavic vęzati: bound to happen.

Lesson 3 of 5.
необходимый (neobkhodímyy) — necessary, essential.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to guarantee, then the Russian for definitely.

You'll want to know: необходимый.
необходимый (neobkhodímyy) — "necessary, essential".
У нас есть всё необходимое. U nas yest vsyo neobkhodímoye. We have everything we need.
Необходимо + infinitive — it is necessary to. More formal than нужный.

The word, taken apart.
необходимый (neobkhodímyy) is built from не- "not" and обходить "to go round", from ходить "to go", Proto-Slavic xoditi: what cannot be got round.

Lesson 4 of 5.
необходимость (neobkhodímost) — necessity, need.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: необходимость.
необходимость (neobkhodímost) — "necessity, need".
В этом нет необходимости. V étom net neobkhodímosti. There is no need for that.
Feminine. В случае необходимости — if need be.

The word, taken apart.
необходимость (neobkhodímost) is built on необходимый (neobkhodímyy) "necessary" with the abstract suffix -ость; the root is ходить "to go".

Lesson 5 of 5.
достаточно (dostátochno) — enough; fairly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for necessary, then the Russian for necessity.

You'll want to know: достаточно.
достаточно (dostátochno) — "enough; fairly".
У нас достаточно времени. U nas dostátochno vrémeni. We have enough time.
With the genitive, enough: достаточно (dostátochno) денег. Before an adjective, fairly: достаточно (dostátochno) трудно.

The word, taken apart.
достаточно (dostátochno) is built on достаток "sufficiency", from достать "to reach, to be enough", on стать, the partner of стоять, Proto-Slavic stojati (EtymDB).
