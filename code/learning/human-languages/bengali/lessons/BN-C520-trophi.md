## You'll want to know: ট্রফি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C520-PODOK520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ট্রফি** — *ṭrôphi* — "a trophy".

> আমাদের স্কুল ক্রিকেটের ট্রফি জিতল।
>
> *Āmāder skul krikeṭer ṭrôphi jitlô.*
>
> Our school won the cricket trophy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ট্রফি** (*ṭrôphi*) is borrowed from English *trophy*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
