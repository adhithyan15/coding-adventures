## You'll want to know: la scusa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C353-REAZIONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la scusa** — "the excuse; the apology".

> Non cercare scuse: hai sbagliato tu.
>
> *Do not look for excuses: you got it wrong.*

*Chiedere scusa* — to apologise. *Scusa!* — sorry! (to a friend); *scusi!* to a stranger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scusa* is built on *scusare* "to excuse", inherited from Latin *excūsāre* "to free from a charge" (Lewis & Short), built from *ex* "out of" and *causa* "a case, a charge".
