## You'll want to know: ضجر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C495-INZIAJ495-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ضجر** — *ḍajira* — "to be fed up".

> ضجر المسافرون من التأخير الطويل.
>
> *ḍajira al-musāfirūn min at-taʾkhīr aṭ-ṭawīl.*
>
> The travellers were fed up with the long delay.

Present: يضجر, *yaḍjar*.

It takes من, as مل does.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضجر** (*ḍajira*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-j-r*, "to be vexed, disquieted" (Lane). Lane gives yaḍjaru as its present.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
