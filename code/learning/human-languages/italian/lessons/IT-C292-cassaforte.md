## You'll want to know: la cassaforte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C292-RAPINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la cassaforte** — "the safe".

> I soldi erano nella cassaforte.
>
> *The money was in the safe.*

Plural *le casseforti*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cassaforte* joins *cassa* "box", from Latin *capsa* "a case", and *forte* "strong", from Latin *fortis* (Lewis & Short).
