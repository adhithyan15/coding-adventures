## You'll want to know: مضيف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C425-RAKIB425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مضيف** — *muḍīf* — "a flight attendant; a host".

> أحضر المضيف كوب ماء لأمي.
>
> *aḥḍara al-muḍīf kūb māʾ li-ummī.*
>
> The flight attendant brought my mother a cup of water.

A woman is *muḍīfa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مضيف** (*muḍīf*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-y-f*, "to be a guest" (Lane). It is the Form IV participle, "one who takes in guests": a host, and on a plane a flight attendant.
