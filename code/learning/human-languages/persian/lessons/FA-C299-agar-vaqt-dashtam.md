# اگر وقت داشتم، میرفتم (agar vaqt dâshtam, miraftam) — if I had time, I would go

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-AGAR-SUBJ-01, FA-GRAMMAR-REAL-CONDITION-01, FA-LEX-BASTEGI-DARAD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if the weather is good, we'll go". (*Agar havâ khub bâshad,
miravim.*) Then "it depends". (*Bastegi dârad.*)

[PAUSE 1s] That condition may come true. The last turn in this run is a
condition that is not true at all, and what would follow if it were.

## Grammar Lens: a past after agar, the mi- past after it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-UNREAL-CONDITION-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-DASHTAN, FA-LEX-C136-NAFY136-05, FA-LEX-C78-WORLD78-01, FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01] -->

> **اگر وقت داشتم، به سفر میرفتم.** — *agar vaqt dâshtam, be safar miraftam* —
> If I had time, I would go on a trip.

I have no time, and I am not going. The *agar* half says so by moving into
the plain past, *dâshtam*, the way English moves to "had". The result goes
into the *mi-* past, *miraftam*.

That plain past belongs mostly to *dâshtan* and *budan*. Most other verbs
take the *mi-* past in the *agar* half too: *agar bârân nemiâmad, miraftim*,
if it weren't raining, we would go.

Set it beside the real one: *agar havâ khub bâshad, miravim*, it may be
fine, and then we'll go; *agar havâ khub bud, miraftim*, it is not fine, so
we are not going.

## Grammar Lens: the mi- past as "would"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-WOULD-MI-PAST-01]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-PAST-CONTINUOUS-01, FA-LEX-C49-SCHOOL49-05, FA-LEX-C70-TOWN70-03, FA-LEX-C193-MAKAN193-04, FA-LEX-C36-VERBS36-04, FA-LEX-C36-VERBS36-01, FA-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-TAR-01] -->

The shape from chapter two hundred and ninety, *miraftam*, "I was going",
does a third job here: "I would go".

> **اگر پول داشتم، یک ماشین میخریدم.** — *agar pul dâshtam, yek mâshin
> mikharidam* — If I had the money, I'd buy a car.

> **اگر جای تو بودم، زودتر میخوابیدم.** — *agar jâ-ye to budam, zudtar
> mikhâbidam* — If I were you, I'd go to bed earlier.

*Jâ-ye to* is "your place", so the advice is "if I were in your place".
*Budam* and *dâshtam* keep their plain past here too, with no *mi-*.

The same pair can also look back, "if I had had time, I would have gone":
*agar vaqt dâshtam, miraftam* covers both. The situation tells you which.

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Which past goes after *agar* when the condition is not true? (A **past**: the
plain *dâshtam* or *budam*, and for most other verbs the *mi-* past.) Which says "would"? (The ***mi-*** past:
