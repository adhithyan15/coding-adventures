## You'll want to know: つもり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C141-TSUMORI]; assesses=[] -->

**つもり** — *tsumori* — "an intention", "a plan": what you mean to do.
It follows a verb in its dictionary form, and **です** closes the sentence.

> **らいねん うみに いく つもりです。** — *rainen umi ni iku tsumori desu*
> — "I plan to go to the sea next year."

Here the dictionary form matters: *iku*, not *ikimasu*, comes before
**つもり**. **ます** goes at the very end of a polite sentence, and
**です** does that job here.
