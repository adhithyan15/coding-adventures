# fùzé — to be in charge (of)

## You'll want to know: fùzé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C263-LEAVE263-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fùzé* — "to be in charge (of)".

> *zhège gōngzuò wǒ fùzé* — I'm in charge of this job.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fùzé* is built from *fù* 'to carry' and *zé* 'to demand', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjuwX* and *tsreak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to bear on the back' and the second 'to seek'.
