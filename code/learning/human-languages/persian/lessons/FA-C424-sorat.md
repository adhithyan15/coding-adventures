## You'll want to know: سرعت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C424-SORAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سرعت** (*sor'at*) — "speed".

> سرعت این قطار خیلی زیاد است.
>
> *Sor'at-e in qatâr kheyli ziyâd ast.*
>
> This train's speed is very high.

*Bâ sor'at* — quickly, at speed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sor'at* was borrowed from Arabic *surʿa* 'speed, haste' (EtymDB).
