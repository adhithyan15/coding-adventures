# ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು (sābītupaḍisu) — to prove

## You'll want to know: ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C376-PURAVE376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು** — *sābītupaḍisu* — "to prove".

> ನನ್ನ ಮಾತು ನಿಜ ಅಂತ ನಾನು ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *nanna mātu nija anta nānu sābītupaḍiside.*
>
> I proved that my words were true.

Past (I …): ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, *sābītupaḍiside*.

ಸಾಬೀತು means proved; -ಪಡಿಸು makes a verb of it. ಸಾಬೀತಾಯಿತು, it was proved.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು** (*sābītupaḍisu*) is a loan from Hindustani *sābit*, "proved, established", which comes from Arabic *thābit*, "firm", with the Kannada ending -*paḍisu* that makes a verb.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
