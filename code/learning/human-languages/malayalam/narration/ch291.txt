Malayalam, chapter 291: Creeper, Animal, Deer, Bull, Wolf.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വള്ളി (vaḷḷi) — a creeper, a vine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a chenda drum, then the Malayalam for a pooram.

You'll want to know: വള്ളി.
വള്ളി — vaḷḷi — "a creeper, a vine".

The word, taken apart.
വള്ളി (vaḷḷi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil vaḷḷi, Kannada baḷḷi and Telugu valli (DEDR 5316). DEDR 5316 compares Sanskrit vallī.
