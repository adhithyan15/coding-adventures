## You'll want to know: l'orchestre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C513-ORCHESTRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'orchestre** — "orchestra; stalls (in a theatre)".

> L'orchestre joue ce soir au théâtre.
>
> *The orchestra is playing at the theatre tonight.*

Masculine. In a theatre, *l'orchestre* is also the stalls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'orchestre* comes from Latin *orchēstra*, a borrowing from Greek *orkhḗstra* "the space where the chorus danced" (EtymDB), built on *orkheîsthai* "to dance".
