# കൊഴിയുക (koḻiyuka) — to fall off; to drop

## You'll want to know: കൊഴിയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C502-TOTTAM502-04]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊഴിയുക** — *koḻiyuka* — "to fall off; to drop".

> വേനലിൽ മരങ്ങളുടെ ഇലകൾ കൊഴിഞ്ഞു.
>
> *vēnalil maraṅṅaḷuṭe ilakaḷ koḻiññu.*
>
> In the dry season the leaves fell from the trees.

Past: കൊഴിഞ്ഞു, *koḻiññu*.

For leaves, fruit, petals and hair; a person falls with വീഴുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊഴിയുക** (*koḻiyuka*) is native Dravidian, "to fall out, to drop, as fruit, leaves or hair" (DEDR 1825).
