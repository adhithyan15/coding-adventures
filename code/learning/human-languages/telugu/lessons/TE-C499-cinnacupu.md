# చిన్నచూపు (cinnacūpu) — contempt; looking down on

## You'll want to know: చిన్నచూపు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C499-ASUYA499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**చిన్నచూపు** — *cinnacūpu* — "contempt; looking down on".

> పేదవాళ్ళను చిన్నచూపు చూడకూడదు.
>
> *pēdavāḷḷanu cinnacūpu cūḍakūḍadu.*
>
> One must not look down on the poor.

చిన్నచూపు చూడు, to look down on someone: literally a small look.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చిన్నచూపు** (*cinnacūpu*) is native Dravidian: చిన్న, "small, petty" (DEDR 2594), and *cūpu*, "sight, a look", the noun of *cūcu*, "to see" (DEDR 2735): a look that makes someone small.
