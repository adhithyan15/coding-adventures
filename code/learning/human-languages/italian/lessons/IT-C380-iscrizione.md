## You'll want to know: l'iscrizione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C380-UNIVERSITA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'iscrizione** — "the enrolment, the registration; the inscription".

> L'iscrizione costa cento euro.
>
> *Registration costs a hundred euros.*

On a stone or a monument, it is an inscription.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*iscrizione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnscrīptiō* "a writing on, an inscription" (Lewis & Short), from *īnscrībere*, built on *scrībere* "to write".
