Lesson 1 of 6.
खड्डा (khaḍḍā) — a pothole; a pit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for winding, then the Marathi for a rock pinnacle.

You'll want to know: खड्डा.
खड्डा — khaḍḍā — "a pothole; a pit".
पावसाळ्यात रस्त्यावर खूप खड्डे पडतात. pāvsāḷyāt rastyāvar khūp khaḍḍe paḍtāt. In the rainy season lots of potholes form on the road.
Masculine. Plural: खड्डे, khaḍḍe.
खड्डा (khaḍḍā) पडणे, a pothole forms; the plural is खड्डे.

The word, taken apart.
खड्डा (khaḍḍā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi khaḍḍā, "rough hole, pit", under khaḍḍa, "hole, pit" (CDIAL 3790).

Lesson 2 of 6.
गतिरोधक (gatirodhak) — a speed breaker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rock pinnacle, then the Marathi for a pothole.

You'll want to know: गतिरोधक.
गतिरोधक — gatirodhak — "a speed breaker".
शाळेजवळ दोन गतिरोधक आहेत. śāḷejavaḷ don gatirodhak āhet. There are two speed breakers near the school.
Masculine.
The plural is the same: दोन गतिरोधक (gatirodhak).

The word, taken apart.
गतिरोधक (gatirodhak) is a modern word built from Sanskrit gati, "motion", and rodhaka, "blocking, checking" (Monier-Williams): a thing that holds speed back.

Lesson 3 of 6.
खडबडीत (khaḍbaḍīt) — bumpy; rough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pothole, then the Marathi for a speed breaker.

You'll want to know: खडबडीत.
खडबडीत — khaḍbaḍīt — "bumpy; rough".
हा रस्ता फार खडबडीत आहे. hā rastā phār khaḍbaḍīt āhe. This road is very bumpy.
The opposite is गुळगुळीत, smooth; both end in -ईत and do not change for gender.

The word, taken apart.
खडबडीत (khaḍbaḍīt) is built on the echo word khaḍbaḍ, a rough, knocking noise, with the adjective ending -ईत; its deeper origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
कलंडणे (kalaṇḍṇe) — to tip over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a speed breaker, then the Marathi for bumpy.

You'll want to know: कलंडणे.
कलंडणे — kalaṇḍṇe — "to tip over".
वळणावर भाजीची गाडी कलंडली. vaḷaṇāvar bhājīcī gāḍī kalaṇḍlī. The vegetable cart tipped over at the bend.
Past (he …): तो कलंडला, to kalaṇḍlā.
It is something heavy leaning until it falls on its side.

The word, taken apart.
कलंडणे (kalaṇḍṇe) is a Marathi verb for a load or vehicle tipping onto its side; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
आपटणे (āpaṭṇe) — to bang against.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for bumpy, then the Marathi for to tip over.

You'll want to know: आपटणे.
आपटणे — āpaṭṇe — "to bang against".
खड्ड्यामुळे माझं डोकं खिडकीला आपटलं. khaḍḍyāmuḷe mājhaṁ ḍokaṁ khiḍkīlā āpaṭlaṁ. Because of the pothole my head banged against the window.
Past (he …): तो आपटला, to āpaṭlā.
What gets hit takes -ला or -वर: दाराला आपटणे (āpaṭṇe), to bang against the door.

The word, taken apart.
आपटणे (āpaṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi apaṭṇẽ, "to strike against", under Sanskrit āpāṭyate (CDIAL 1203).
