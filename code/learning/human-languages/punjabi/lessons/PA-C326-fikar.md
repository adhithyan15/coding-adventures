## You'll want to know: ਫ਼ਿਕਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C326-FEAR326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਿਕਰ** — *fikar* — "worry".

> ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਨੂੰ ਮੇਰਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਫ਼ਿਕਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *mātā jī nū̃ merā bahut fikar hai.*
>
> Mother worries about me a lot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਿਕਰ** (*fikar*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *fikr*, "thought".
