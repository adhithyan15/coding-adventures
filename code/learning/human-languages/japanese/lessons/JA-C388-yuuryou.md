## You'll want to know: ゆうりょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C388-RYOHI388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうりょう** — *yūryō* — "requiring a fee; not free".

> **えきの ちゅうしゃじょうは ゆうりょうです。** — *eki no chūshajō wa yūryō desu* — "The station car park charges a fee."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうりょう** (*yūryō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yū*, "have" (Middle Chinese *hjuwX*) and *ryō*, "fee" (*lew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
