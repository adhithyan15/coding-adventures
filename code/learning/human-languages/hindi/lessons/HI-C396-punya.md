## You'll want to know: पुण्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C396-SANSKAR396-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पुण्य** — *puṇya* — "a good deed, merit".

> अगर तुम भूखे को खाना दोगे तो पुण्य मिलेगा।
>
> *agar tum bhūkhe ko khānā doge to puṇya milegā.*
>
> If you give food to the hungry, you will earn merit.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुण्य** (*puṇya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *puṇya*, "good, auspicious; merit" (Monier-Williams).
