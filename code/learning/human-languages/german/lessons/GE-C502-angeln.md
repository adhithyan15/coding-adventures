## You'll want to know: angeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C502-ANGELN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**angeln** — "to fish (with a rod)".

> Am Samstag angelt mein Vater am See.
>
> *On Saturday my father goes fishing at the lake.*

*Angeln gehen* — to go fishing. *Die Angel* — fishing rod.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*angeln* is built on *Angel* "fishing rod", inherited from Middle High German *angel* and Old High German *angul* "fish hook", from Proto-Germanic *angulō* (EtymDB).
