## You'll want to know: आपद्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C375-VIPATTI375-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आपद्** — *āpad* — "a calamity".

> यदि आपत् आगच्छति, तर्हि धैर्यं धारयतु।
>
> *yadi āpat āgacchati, tarhi dhairyaṃ dhārayatu.*
>
> If a calamity comes, keep your courage.

A feminine noun.

The stem ends in *-d*; said on its own, as here, the word ends in *-t*: आपत्, *āpat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपद्** (*āpad*) is built on *ā* and the root √pad, "to fall"; MW gives *āpad* "misfortune, calamity, distress" (MW).
