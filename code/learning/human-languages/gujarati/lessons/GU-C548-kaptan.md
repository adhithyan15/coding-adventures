## You'll want to know: કપ્તાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C548-TEAM548-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કપ્તાન** — *kaptān* — "a captain".

> જો કપ્તાન સારું રમશે તો ટીમ જીતશે.
>
> *jo kaptān sārũ ramśe to ṭīm jītśe.*
>
> If the captain plays well, the team will win.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કપ્તાન** (*kaptān*) is usually traced to Portuguese *capitão*, "a captain", the word of the ships that reached the Indian coast.
