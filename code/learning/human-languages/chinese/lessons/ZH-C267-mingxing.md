## You'll want to know: 明星
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C267-FILM267-03]; assesses=[] -->

**明星** — *míngxīng* — "star; celebrity".

> **他是一个大明星** *tā shì yí ge dà míngxīng* — He's a big star.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**明星** (*míngxīng*) is built from 明 'bright' and 星 'a star', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjaeng* and *seng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'light' and the second 'a star in the sky'.
