## You'll want to know: shāngdiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C42-SHOP42-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shāngdiàn* — "shop".

> *zhège shāngdiàn hěn xiǎo* — This shop is tiny.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shāngdiàn* is built from shāng, usually explained as 'trade', and diàn 'shop', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syang* and *temH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary explains the second as a place where goods are kept and sold.
