## You'll want to know: das Unwetter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C462-UNWETTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Unwetter** — "the severe weather, the violent storm".

> Nach dem Unwetter liegen Äste auf der Straße.
>
> *After the storm there are branches lying in the road.*

Same form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Unwetter* is inherited from Middle High German *unweter* 'bad weather' (EtymDB), built from *un-* 'bad, not' and *Wetter* 'weather'.
