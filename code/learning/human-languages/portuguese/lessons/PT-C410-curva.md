## You'll want to know: a curva
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C410-CURVAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a curva** — "the bend, the curve".

> Depois da curva, a estrada desce até ao rio.
>
> *After the bend, the road goes down to the river.*

*Uma curva perigosa* — a dangerous bend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*curva* comes from Latin *curvus* 'bent, crooked' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB has no entry for the Portuguese word.
