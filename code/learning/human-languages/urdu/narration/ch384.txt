Urdu, chapter 384: Values Passed On.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تربیت (tarbiyat) — upbringing; training.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to rake in, then the Urdu for to fail an exam.

You'll want to know: تربیت.
تربیت — tarbiyat — "upbringing; training".
اگر تربیت اچھی ہوئی تو بچے اچھے بنیں گے۔ agar tarbiyat achchhī huī to bachche achchhe baneṅ ge. If they are brought up well, children will turn out well.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تربیت (tarbiyat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tarbiya, "bringing up, rearing".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

اگر وہ فراخ دل ہوتا تو مدد کرتا۔ agar voh farākh-dil hotā to madad kartā. If he were generous, he would help.

The word, taken apart.
فراخ دل (farākh-dil) is built from Persian farākh, "wide", and دل, "heart", usually traced to Persian dil: a wide heart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
رٹنا (raṭnā) — to learn by rote; to cram.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for manners, then the Urdu for pampering.

You'll want to know: رٹنا.
رٹنا — raṭnā — "to learn by rote; to cram".
اگر تم صرف رٹو گے تو کچھ نہیں سمجھو گے۔ agar tum sirf raṭo ge to kuchh nahīṅ samjho ge. If you only learn by rote, you will understand nothing.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سبق رٹا, us ne sabaq raṭā.

The word, taken apart.
رٹنا (raṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin raṭnā, "to cry out, to repeat", under Sanskrit ráṭati, "cries out" (CDIAL 10590): saying a thing over and over.
