Mandarin Chinese, chapter 261: Have You Ever?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
jīngyàn — experience; know-how.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for technique, then the Mandarin for to make progress.

You'll want to know: jīngyàn.
jīngyàn — "experience; know-how".
tā hěn yǒu jīngyàn — He's very experienced.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jīngyàn is built from jīng and yàn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, keng and ngjemH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'constant' and 'the warp of cloth', and the second 'to verify'. How the pair came to mean experience is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for talking about the past.

Lesson 4 of 5.
国外 (guówài) — abroad; overseas.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for at one time, then the Mandarin for to study abroad.

You'll want to know: 国外.
国外 — guówài — "abroad; overseas".
你去过国外吗 nǐ qù guo guówài ma — Have you ever been abroad?

The word, taken apart.
国外 (guówài) is built from 国 'a country' and 外 'outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwok and ngwajH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'outside' and 'far'.
