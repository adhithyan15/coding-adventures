Marathi, chapter 286: If, and Otherwise.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
जर … तर … (jar … tar …) — if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "it will rain" and "it rained". (pāūs paḍel, pāūs paḍlā.) Then "it rained all day". (divasbhar pāūs paḍat hotā.)

You'll want to know: जर … तर.
जर उद्या पाऊस पडला, तर मी घरी राहीन. jar udyā pāūs paḍlā, tar mī gharī rāhīn. — "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home."
जर (jar) opens the condition, तर (tar) opens the result. The condition takes the past form, पडला, though it looks ahead; the result is in the future, राहीन. जर can go: पाऊस पडला तर मी घरी राहीन (pāūs paḍlā tar mī gharī rāhīn). तर stays.

You'll want to know: if it is.
जर तुम्हाला वेळ असेल, तर या. jar tumhālā veḷ asel, tar yā. — "If you have time, come."
For a state, the condition takes the future of "to be", असेल; the result can be a request. या, "come", is the polite form of येणे.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home" — jar udyā pāūs paḍlā, tar mī gharī rāhīn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If Meera comes, we will go" — mīrā ālī tar āmhī jāū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If you have time, come" — jar tumhālā veḷ asel, tar yā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that if it rains tomorrow you will stay at home.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"If"? (जर.) "Then"? (तर.) Which form does the condition take? (The past: पडला.) "If you have time"? (जर तुम्हाला वेळ असेल.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
पाऊस पडल्यास (pāūs paḍalyās) — should it happen, and otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "if it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home". (jar udyā pāūs paḍlā, tar mī gharī rāhīn.) Then "after reading". (vācalyāvar.)

You'll want to know: -ल्यास, 'should it'.
पाऊस पडल्यास आम्ही घरी राहू. pāūs paḍalyās āmhī gharī rāhū. — "Should it rain, we will stay at home."
The -ल्- of -ल्यावर, with -ल्यास: पडल्यास says जर … पडला तर in one word. It is the careful, written way, and you will read it on notices more than you will hear it: उशीर झाल्यास (uśīr jhālyās), "in case of delay".

You'll want to know: नाहीतर, 'otherwise'.
लवकर निघा, नाहीतर गाडी चुकेल. lavkar nighā, nāhītar gāḍī cukel. — "Leave early, otherwise you'll miss the train."
नाहीतर (nāhītar) is नाही and तर: "if not, then". निघा is the polite "set out"; चुकेल is the future of चुकणे, with the train as its subject, as in माझी गाडी चुकली.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Should it rain, we will stay at home" — pāūs paḍalyās āmhī gharī rāhū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Leave early, otherwise you'll miss the train" — lavkar nighā, nāhītar gāḍī cukel]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Come tomorrow, otherwise I'll get angry" — udyā yā, nāhītar malā rāg yeīl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Say with -lyās that should it rain, we will stay at home; then tell someone to leave early, otherwise they will miss the train.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Should it rain"? (पाऊस पडल्यास (pāūs paḍalyās).) Is it said or written more? (Written, on notices.) "Otherwise"? (नाहीतर.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — if and otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "the train is faster than the bus" and "I have lived in Pune for three years". (gāḍī baspekṣā jalad āhe, mī tīn varṣāmpāsūn puṇyāt rāhto.) Then "the biggest" and "from morning until evening". (sarvāt moṭhā, sakāḷpāsūn sandhyākāḷparyant.) Last, ऋतू and ज्ञान, and their letters ऋ and ञ.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: If you have time, come.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Leave early, otherwise you'll miss the train.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: If it rains, you shouldn't go out.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: one plan of yours for next week, with जर … तर]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"If … then"? (जर … तर.) Which form does the condition take? (The past: पडला.) "Should it rain"? (पाऊस पडल्यास (pāūs paḍalyās).) "Otherwise"? (नाहीतर.)
