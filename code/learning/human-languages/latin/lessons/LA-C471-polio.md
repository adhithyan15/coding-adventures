## You'll want to know: poliō, polīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C471-TORNUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**poliō, polīre** — "to polish, to smooth; to refine".

> Servus vāsa argentea polit.
>
> *The slave polishes the silver dishes.*

*poliō, polīre, polīvī, polītum*. *Polītus* is polished, also refined in manner. English *polish* and *polite* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*poliō* is usually traced to PIE *pelh₂-* (EtymDB), though Lewis & Short suggest another root and the origin is not settled. English *polish* comes from it through French, and *polite* from its participle *polītus*.
