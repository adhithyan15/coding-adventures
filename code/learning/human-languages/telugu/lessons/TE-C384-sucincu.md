# సూచించు (sūciñcu) — to suggest; to indicate

## You'll want to know: సూచించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C384-SUCANA384-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సూచించు** — *sūciñcu* — "to suggest; to indicate".

> మా ఉపాధ్యాయుడు ఒక కొత్త పుస్తకం సూచించాడు.
>
> *mā upādhyāyuḍu oka kotta pustakaṁ sūciñcāḍu.*
>
> Our teacher suggested a new book.

Past (I …): సూచించాను, *sūciñcānu*.

The verb of సూచన, a suggestion, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సూచించు** (*sūciñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *sūcana*, "pointing out, indicating; an indication" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
