## You'll want to know: высказываться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C342-OBSUZHDAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**высказываться** (*vyskázyvatsya*) — "to speak one’s mind, to give one’s opinion".

> На собрании каждый мог высказаться.
>
> *Na sobránii kázhdyy mog výskazatsya.*
>
> At the meeting everyone could give their opinion.

Imperfective; perfective *высказаться* (stressed on the first syllable). *Высказываться за / против* — to speak for or against.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**высказываться** is built from *вы-* "out" and *сказать* "to say"; the root is *казать* "to show", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*kazati* (EtymDB). To speak your mind is to say it out.
