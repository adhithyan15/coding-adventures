Spanish, chapter 680: Tiendas del barrio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la pastelería — the cake shop, the pastry shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the stamp, then the Spanish for the toilet.

You'll want to know: la pastelería.
la pastelería — "the cake shop, the pastry shop".
Compramos un pastel en la pastelería. We bought a cake at the cake shop.
The baker of cakes is el pastelero.

The word, taken apart.
pastelería is built on pastel "cake" with -ería (EtymDB); pastel is usually traced to Late Latin pasta "dough".

En el barrio hay una lavandería. There is a launderette in the neighbourhood.
