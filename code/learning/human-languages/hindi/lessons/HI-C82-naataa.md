# नाटा — the gap that a word you already knew could not fill

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C82-BODY-01, HI-LEX-C41-ADJ-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have **लंबा** for tall. Reach for its opposite using a word you
already know, and then read on to find out why that reach does not work.

## You'll want to know: नाटा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C82-BODY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नाटा** (*nāṭā*) — "short, of a person's height."

The obvious guess is *choṭā*, and the obvious guess is wrong — not because it is
rude, but because it means something else.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C82-BODY-04, HI-LEX-C41-ADJ-02] -->

This is the clearest case in the chapter of a word existing because another word
is busy:

| word | about a thing | about a person |
|---|---|---|
| *choṭā* | small | **younger** |
| **नाटा** | — | **short in height** |

*Choṭā bhāī* is a younger brother regardless of how tall he is. Hindi took the
size word for a person's age and left the height meaning without an owner, and
**नाटा** is what filled the hole.

Sanskrit **नत** (*nata*) is behind it, meaning **bent, bowed, stooping** — the
same root as *namaste*, where the bowing is a greeting. A *nāṭā* person is
pictured as one closer to the ground.

A note on using it. **नाटा** is plain rather than polite, and about a person it
can land as blunt. When you want neutral, Hindi says *qad meṅ choṭā*, "short in
height" — which spells out the *height* part that *choṭā* on its own leaves
ambiguous. Recognise **नाटा**; reach for the longer phrase.

## Grammar Lens: the fourth of four, and the rule has not moved
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C82-BODY-04, HI-LEX-C82-BODY-01, HI-LEX-C82-BODY-03, HI-GRAMMAR-C41-ADJ-SYSTEM] -->

| adjective | masculine | feminine |
|---|---|---|
| लंबा | लंबा | लंबी |
| पतला | पतला | पतली |
| मोटा | मोटा | मोटी |
| नाटा | नाटा | नाटी |

Four words for the shape of a person, all four ending in **-आ**, all four
bending. You can now answer a question about anyone's build without once
needing to remember which words agree.
