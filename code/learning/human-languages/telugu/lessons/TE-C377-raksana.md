## You'll want to know: రక్షణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C377-VIPATTU377-03]; assesses=[] -->

**రక్షణ** — *rakṣaṇa* — "protection; safety".

> ప్రభుత్వం ప్రజల రక్షణ కోసం పోలీసులను పంపింది.
>
> *prabhutvaṁ prajala rakṣaṇa kōsaṁ pōlīsulanu pampindi.*
>
> The government sent police for the people's protection.

Also defence, as in the name of a ministry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రక్షణ** (*rakṣaṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rakṣaṇa*, "guarding, protecting" (CDIAL 10546).
