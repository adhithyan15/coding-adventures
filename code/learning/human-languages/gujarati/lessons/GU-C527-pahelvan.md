## You'll want to know: પહેલવાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C527-AKHADO527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પહેલવાન** — *pahelvān* — "a wrestler".

> આ પહેલવાન ઘણો મજબૂત છે.
>
> *ā pahelvān ghaṇo majbūt che.*
>
> This wrestler is very strong.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પહેલવાન** (*pahelvān*) is usually traced to Persian *pahlavān*, "a champion, a wrestler".
