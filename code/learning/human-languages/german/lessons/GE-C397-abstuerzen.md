## You'll want to know: abstürzen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C397-ABSTURZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**abstürzen** — "to crash; to fall (from a height)".

> Mein Computer ist schon wieder abgestürzt.
>
> *My computer has crashed yet again.*

Separable, Perfekt with *sein*: *er stürzt ab*, *er ist abgestürzt*. Also of planes and climbers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abstürzen* is built from *ab* "down, off" and *stürzen* "to plunge", inherited from Middle High German *stürzen*, Old High German *sturzen* and Proto-Germanic *sturtijaną* (EtymDB).
