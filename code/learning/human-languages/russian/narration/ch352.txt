Russian, chapter 352: Whose fault is it?.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
вина (viná) — guilt, fault, blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for owing to, then the Russian for therefore.

You'll want to know: вина.
вина (viná) — "guilt, fault, blame".
Это не твоя вина. Éto ne tvoyá viná. It is not your fault.
Feminine, stressed on the end. По моей вине — through my fault. Not to be confused with вино, wine.

The word, taken apart.
вина (viná) is usually traced to Proto-Slavic vina "guilt, cause"; it is the root of извиняться "to apologise", which asks to be taken out of guilt.

Lesson 2 of 6.
виноватый (vinovátyy) — guilty, to blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for therefore, then the Russian for guilt.

You'll want to know: виноватый.
виноватый (vinovátyy) — "guilty, to blame".
Никто не виноват, так получилось. Niktó ne vinovát, tak poluchílos. Nobody is to blame; that is how it turned out.
The short form виноват is the usual one: я виноват — it is my fault. Виноват! is also a quick apology.

The word, taken apart.
виноватый (vinovátyy) is built on вина (viná) "guilt" with the suffix -оватый; вина (viná) is usually traced to Proto-Slavic vina.

Lesson 3 of 6.
винить (vinít) — to blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for guilt, then the Russian for guilty.

You'll want to know: винить.
винить (vinít) — "to blame".
Она винит себя в этой аварии. Oná vinít sebyá v étoy avárii. She blames herself for this accident.
Imperfective. Винить кого в чём: the fault goes after в + prepositional.

The word, taken apart.
винить (vinít) is the verb made from вина (viná) "guilt", which is usually traced to Proto-Slavic vina: to put the guilt on someone.

Lesson 4 of 6.
оправдание (opravdániye) — an excuse, a justification.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for guilty, then the Russian for to blame.

You'll want to know: оправдание.
оправдание (opravdániye) — "an excuse, a justification".
Для такого поведения нет оправдания. Dlya takógo povedéniya net opravdániya. There is no excuse for such behaviour.
Neuter. In court it means an acquittal.

The word, taken apart.
оправдание (opravdániye) is built on оправдать "to justify", from правда "truth", inherited from Proto-Slavic pravĭda, built on pravŭ "right, correct" (EtymDB). To justify is to show that someone was in the right.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ответственный (otvétstvennyy) — responsible, in charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to blame, then the Russian for an excuse.

You'll want to know: ответственный.
ответственный (otvétstvennyy) — "responsible, in charge".
Кто ответственный за этот проект? Kto otvétstvennyy za étot proyékt? Who is responsible for this project?
Ответственный за + accusative. Short form ответствен. Of a moment: ответственный (otvétstvennyy) момент, a crucial moment.

The word, taken apart.
ответственный (otvétstvennyy) is built on ответ "an answer", from от- "back" and the old word for counsel and speech, Old Russian větŭ. The person responsible is the one who must answer for it.
