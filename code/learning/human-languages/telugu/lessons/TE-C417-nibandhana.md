## You'll want to know: నిబంధన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C417-NIBANDHANA417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**నిబంధన** — *nibandhana* — "a stipulation; a clause".

> ఒప్పందంలో ఒక కొత్త నిబంధన చేర్చారు.
>
> *oppandaṁlō oka kotta nibandhana cērcāru.*
>
> They added a new clause to the agreement.

Plural: నిబంధనలు, *nibandhanalu*.

Plural నిబంధనలు, terms and conditions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిబంధన** (*nibandhana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nibandhana*, "binding, fastening; a band, a support" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
