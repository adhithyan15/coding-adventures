## You'll want to know: ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C261-TRAIN261-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ** — *paleṭfāram* — "a railway platform".

> ਰੇਲ ਦੂਜੇ ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਉੱਤੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ।
>
> *rel dūje paleṭfāram utte āvegī.*
>
> The train will come in at the second platform.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ** (*paleṭfāram*) is borrowed from English *platform*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
