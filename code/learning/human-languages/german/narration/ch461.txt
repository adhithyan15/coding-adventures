Lesson 1 of 6.
der Nieselregen — the drizzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for overcast, then the German for to change suddenly.

You'll want to know: der Nieselregen.
der Nieselregen — "the drizzle".
Bei Nieselregen gehen wir trotzdem spazieren. We go for a walk in the drizzle anyway.
No plural.

The word, taken apart.
Nieselregen is built from nieseln 'to drizzle' and Regen 'rain', an inherited word and a cousin of English rain (EtymDB).

The origin of nieseln is not settled; EtymDB lists it without a source. It is sometimes linked to niesen 'to sneeze', for the fine spray.

Lesson 3 of 6.
der Schauer — the shower (of rain); a shiver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the drizzle, then the German for to drizzle.

You'll want to know: der Schauer.
der Schauer — "the shower (of rain); a shiver".
Am Nachmittag gibt es immer wieder kurze Schauer. In the afternoon there are short showers again and again.
Same form in the plural. Ein Schauer is also a shiver.

The word, taken apart.
Schauer is inherited through Middle High German schure from Old High German scūr 'storm, shower' (EtymDB); English shower is a cousin.

Lesson 4 of 6.
die Aufheiterung — the sunny spell, the bright interval.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: die Aufheiterung.
die Aufheiterung — "the sunny spell, the bright interval".
Für den Abend meldet der Wetterbericht Aufheiterungen. The weather report forecasts sunny spells for the evening.
Plural: die Aufheiterungen. A weather report word.

The word, taken apart.
Aufheiterung is built on aufheitern 'to brighten up', from heiter 'bright, clear', an inherited Germanic adjective. EtymDB does not list the noun.

Lesson 5 of 6.
aufklaren — to clear up (of the sky).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the shower, then the German for the sunny spell.

You'll want to know: aufklaren.
aufklaren — "to clear up (of the sky)".
Nach dem Gewitter klart der Himmel schnell auf. After the thunderstorm the sky quickly clears.
Separable: es klart auf, es hat aufgeklart. Not the same as aufklären 'to explain'.

The word, taken apart.
aufklaren is built from auf and klar 'clear', which reached Middle High German as klār through Middle Dutch from Latin clārus 'bright, clear' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). Aufklaren is usually described as a sailors' word for the sky.
