German, chapter 141: To pay, To rent, To pack, To repair, To start.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
zahlen — to pay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to feel, then the German for to fall.

You'll want to know: zahlen.
zahlen — "to pay".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: zahlen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: zahlen, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say zahlen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for to feel, then the German for to fall, then say zahlen again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does zahlen mean? ("To pay".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Wanting, time and numbers — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to repair and to start.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: angry; bad, funny, serious, famous, interesting]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: boring, difficult, finished, ready, safe; sure]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dangerous, wet, dry, cool, soft]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to want, a wish, to hope, to order, rather]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Angry; bad? (böse.) Funny? (lustig.) Serious? (ernst.) Famous? (berühmt.) Interesting? (interessant.) Boring? (langweilig.) Difficult? (schwierig.) Finished? (fertig.) Ready? (bereit.) Safe; sure? (sicher.) Dangerous? (gefährlich.) Wet? (nass.) Dry? (trocken.) Cool? (kühl.) Soft? (weich.) To want? (wollen.) A wish? (der Wunsch.) To hope? (hoffen.) To order? (bestellen.) Rather? (lieber.)

(die Million.) To begin? (beginnen.) To visit? (besuchen.) To answer? (antworten.) To lay? (legen.) To put? (stellen.) To lie? (liegen.) To get dressed? (sich anziehen.) To tell? (erzählen.) To feel? (fühlen.) To fall? (fallen.) To pay? (zahlen.) To rent? (mieten.) To pack? (packen.) To repair? (reparieren.) To start? (anfangen.)
