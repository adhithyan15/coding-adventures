## You'll want to know: confidarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C499-LITIGI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**confidarsi** — "to confide (in someone)".

> Mi confido solo con mia sorella.
>
> *I only confide in my sister.*

*Confidarsi con qualcuno*. A secret you tell is *una confidenza*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*confidarsi* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *cōnfīdāre*, from Latin *cōnfīdere* "to trust fully" (Lewis & Short), built on *fīdere* "to trust".
