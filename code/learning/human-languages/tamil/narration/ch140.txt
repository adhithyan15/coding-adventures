Tamil, chapter 140: Grapes, Pomegranate, Pickle, Waterfall, Hill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
திராட்சை (tirāṭcai) — grapes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a guava, then the Tamil for a lemon.

You'll want to know: திராட்சை.
திராட்சை — tirāṭcai — "grapes".

The word, taken apart.
திராட்சை (tirāṭcai) is a loan from Sanskrit drākṣā, "vine, grape" (CDIAL 6628).

You'll want to know: ஊறுகாய்.
ஊறுகாய் — ūṟukāy — "a pickle".

The word, taken apart.
ஊறுகாய் (ūṟukāy) is native: ūṟu, "to soak, to be steeped" (DEDR 761), and kāy, "unripe fruit" (DEDR 1459): fruit left to steep.

Lesson 4 of 5.
அருவி (aruvi) — a waterfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a pomegranate, then the Tamil for a pickle.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say வானிலை, then மழை பெய்கிறது. Name the rainy season, மழைக் காலம், the one that shapes the year here.

You'll want to know: அருவி.
அருவி — aruvi — "a waterfall".

The word, taken apart.
அருவி (aruvi) is native Dravidian (DEDR 225). The entry glosses aruvi "river's mouth".
