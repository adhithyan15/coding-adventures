# लुबाडणे (lubāḍṇe) — to fleece; to swindle

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C297-CHOR297-03, MR-LEX-C297-CHOR297-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Marathi for to snatch, then the Marathi for to loot.

## You'll want to know: लुबाडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C297-CHOR297-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लुबाडणे** — *lubāḍṇe* — "to fleece; to swindle".

> त्या माणसाने गोड बोलून आजीला लुबाडलं.
>
> *tyā māṇsāne goḍ bolūn ājīlā lubāḍlaṁ.*
>
> That man swindled Grandma with sweet talk.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने लुबाडलं, *tyāne lubāḍlaṁ*.

Like लुटणे, but by trickery; the victim takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लुबाडणे** (*lubāḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb of fleecing by trickery; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
