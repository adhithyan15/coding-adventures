## You'll want to know: риск
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C372-VEROYATNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**риск** (*risk*) — "a risk".

> Это большой риск.
>
> *Éto bolshóy risk.*
>
> That is a big risk.

Masculine. *На свой риск* — at one’s own risk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**риск** is usually traced, through French *risque*, to Italian *risco*; the further origin of the Italian word is not settled.
