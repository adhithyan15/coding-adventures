## You'll want to know: o guião
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C527-FILMAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o guião** — "script, screenplay".

> O guião do filme é baseado num romance.
>
> *The film's script is based on a novel.*

In Brazil: *o roteiro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guião* is linked in EtymDB with *guia* "a guide": the text that guides the film. The verb *guiar* is usually traced to a Germanic word.
