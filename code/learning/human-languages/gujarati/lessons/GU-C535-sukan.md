## You'll want to know: શુકન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C535-SHUKAN535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શુકન** — *śukan* — "an omen".

> જો સવારે ગાય સામે આવશે તો દાદી તેને સારું શુકન માનશે.
>
> *jo savāre gāy sāme āvśe to dādī tene sārũ śukan mānśe.*
>
> If a cow comes towards her in the morning, my grandmother will take it as a good omen.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શુકન** (*śukan*) goes back to Sanskrit *śakuna*, "a bird", especially a bird of good or bad omen (Monier-Williams).
