# يعتمد على (yaʿtamidu ʿalā) — it depends on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C292-SHART292-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-FA-RESULT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I relied on my friend". (*Iʿtamadtu ʿalā ṣadīqī*.) Then "if
it rains, we won't go out". (*Idhā amṭarat, fa-lan nakhruja*.)

[PAUSE 1s] Someone asks about your plan, and you do not know yet. That is
an answer too.

## Grammar Lens: it depends on
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C292-DEPENDS-01]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-FUTURE-01, AR-CONCEPT-C18-AL-TAQS-01] -->

> — **هل ستسافر غدا؟** — *hal sa-tusāfiru ghadan?* — Are you travelling
> tomorrow?
> — **يعتمد على الطقس.** — *yaʿtamidu ʿalā aṭ-ṭaqs* — It depends on the
> weather.

"It" is not said: the *ya-* of *yaʿtamidu* carries it. Put the thing the plan
hangs on after *ʿalā*: *yaʿtamidu ʿalā al-waqt*, it depends on the time;
*yaʿtamidu ʿalayka*, it's up to you.

It answers the same need as a condition with *idhā*, but leaves the
condition open: *idhā kāna aṭ-ṭaqs jamīlan, fa-sa-usāfiru* says which way
it goes; *yaʿtamidu ʿalā aṭ-ṭaqs* only says what decides.

## Grammar Lens: ḥasaba, according to
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C292-HASABA-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C268-SABAB268-02, AR-LEX-C158-QIRAA158-01, AR-LEX-C226-ACT226-01, AR-LEX-C97-NUMBERS97-01, AR-LEX-C86-ADJ86-05] -->

*Ḥasaba*, from chapter two hundred and sixty-eight, puts the deciding thing
in front of a plain statement:

> **حسب الجدول، ينطلق القطار بعد عشر دقائق.** — *ḥasaba al-jadwal,
> yanṭaliqu al-qiṭār baʿda ʿashr daqāʾiq* — According to the timetable, the
> train leaves in ten minutes.

*Ḥasaba aṭ-ṭaqs* is "depending on the weather": *sa-nusāfiru bi-l-qiṭār aw
bi-l-ḥāfila, ḥasaba aṭ-ṭaqs*.
