## You'll want to know: ఋతుపవనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C477-RUTUPAVANAM477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఋతుపవనం** — *ṛtupavanaṁ* — "the monsoon".

> వేసవి తరువాత ఋతుపవనాలు వస్తాయి.
>
> *vēsavi taruvāta ṛtupavanālu vastāyi.*
>
> After the summer the monsoon arrives.

Mostly plural: ఋతుపవనాలు, the monsoon winds. ఋతువు, a season, + పవనం, a wind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఋతుపవనం** (*ṛtupavanaṁ*) is a modern compound built from two Sanskrit words: *ṛtu*, "a season", and *pavana*, "wind, breeze" (Monier-Williams): the wind of the season.
