## You'll want to know: bubulcus, bubulcī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C478-OPILIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**bubulcus, bubulcī** — "an ox-driver, a herdsman".

> Bubulcus cum bōbus ad agrum it.
>
> *The ox-driver goes to the field with the oxen.*

Second declension, masculine: *bubulcus, bubulcī m.* The man who drives the plough oxen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bubulcus* is built on *būbulus* 'of cattle', from *bōs, bovis* 'ox' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from PIE *gʷṓws* 'cow'. English *cow* is a Germanic cousin.
