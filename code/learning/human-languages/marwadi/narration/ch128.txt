Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 128: Turban, Long skirt, Woman's wrap, Bodice, Long coat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पगड़ी (pagṛī) — a turban.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a plough, then the Marwadi for a bullock cart.

You'll want to know: पगड़ी.
पगड़ी — pagṛī — "a turban".

The word, taken apart.
पगड़ी (pagṛī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from paggā, "headdress", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 7644); the match is through Hindi pagṛī. CDIAL thinks a source in Sanskrit pragraha scarcely likely, and explains the ṛ as an added suffix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: लूगड़ो.
लूगड़ो — lūgṛo — "a woman's wrap".

The word, taken apart.
लूगड़ो (lūgṛo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from lugga, "defective", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari lovaṛī under it (CDIAL 11072). The Old Marwari row means "a woman's waist garment"; in the same large group of "defective" words, Hindi lugṛā means "rag" and Gujarati lugṛũ "clothes". How the senses connect is not settled.
