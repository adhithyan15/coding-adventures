## You'll want to know: تمدد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C516-HARAKA516-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تمدد** — *tamaddada* — "to stretch out".

> تمدد القط في الشمس.
>
> *tamaddada al-qiṭṭ fī ash-shams.*
>
> The cat stretched out in the sun.

Present: يتمدد, *yatamaddad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تمدد** (*tamaddada*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-d-d*, "to stretch, to extend" (Lane). Mudda, a period, which you know, is from the same root: a stretch of time.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
