## You'll want to know: поход
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C459-POKHOD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поход** (*pokhód*) — "a hike, a trek; a campaign".

> Летом мы ходили в поход в горы.
>
> *Létom my khodíli v pokhód v góry.*
>
> In summer we went on a hike in the mountains.

Masculine. *Идти в поход* — to go hiking or camping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поход* is built from *по-* and *ход* 'a going', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *xodŭ* 'motion' (EtymDB).
