## You'll want to know: किश्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C445-KISHTI445-01]; assesses=[] -->

**किश्ती** — *kiśtī* — "a small boat, a skiff".

> झील में एक छोटी किश्ती तैर रही थी।
>
> *jhīl meṁ ek choṭī kiśtī tair rahī thī.*
>
> A little boat was floating on the lake.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किश्ती** (*kiśtī*) is usually traced to Persian *kištī*, "a boat, a ship"; songs and poems love it.
