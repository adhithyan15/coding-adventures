## You'll want to know: cervīx, cervīcis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C516-GUTT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**cervīx, cervīcis** — "neck, nape".

> Servus sarcinam in cervīcibus portat.
>
> *The slave carries the pack on his neck.*

Feminine, third declension; often plural *cervīcēs*. English *cervical*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cervīx** is inherited; EtymDB traces it to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "horn, head". English *cervical* comes from it.
