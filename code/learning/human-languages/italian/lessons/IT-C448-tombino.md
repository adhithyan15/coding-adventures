## You'll want to know: il tombino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C448-ARREDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il tombino** — "the drain cover, the manhole".

> Con la pioggia i tombini sono pieni.
>
> *With the rain the drains are full.*

A little *tomba*: a covered hole in the street.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tombino* is a little *tomba* 'tomb', which is inherited from Late Latin *tumba*, from Greek *týmbos* 'burial mound' (EtymDB).
