## You'll want to know: सुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C276-BANSLI276-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सुर** — *sur* — "a tune".

> यो सुर मीठो है।
>
> *yo sur mīṭho hai.*
>
> This tune is sweet.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुर** (*sur*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sura*, "sound", a variant of *svara*; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *sur*, "sound, words, tune" (CDIAL 13498).
