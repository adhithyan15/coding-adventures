Persian, chapter 137: Recently, Already, Last Year, Once, To Spend (Time).
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
به تازگی (be-tâzegi) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for surely... not?, then the Persian for when.

You'll want to know: به تازگی.
به تازگی — be-tâzegi — "recently". Persian's past for "I" ends in -am: به تازگی یک کتاب خواندم — be-tâzegi yek ketâb khândam — "I recently read a book."
