# ಅಂತ ನನಗೆ ಅನಿಸುತ್ತದೆ (anta nanage anisuttade) — what I think

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C277-KRIYE277-02, KA-LEX-C70-TELL-02, KA-LEX-C310-SVACCHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to seem, to feel (that)" (*anisu*) and the word that closes a quotation (*anta*). Then "clean" (*svaccha*). Which letter sits small inside it? (**ಛ**, *cha*.)

## You'll want to know: … ಅಂತ ನನಗೆ ಅನಿಸುತ್ತದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C311-THINK-THAT-01]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C70-TELL-03, KA-LEX-C03-CENNAAGI-01, KA-LEX-C46-ASK-05, KA-GRAMMAR-C06-DATIVE-SUBJECT-02, KA-LEX-C02-ENU-01, KA-LEX-C290-GUNA290-03] -->

> **ಈ ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ ಅಂತ ನನಗೆ ಅನಿಸುತ್ತದೆ.**
>
> *ī pustaka cennāgide anta nanage anisuttade.* — "I think this book is good."

The thought comes first, whole: **ಈ ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ** (*ī pustaka cennāgide*, "this book is good"). **ಅಂತ** closes it like a pair of quotation marks. Then **ನನಗೆ ಅನಿಸುತ್ತದೆ**: "it seems to me". Kannada does not say "I think" here. The thought comes **to** you, so "me" takes the **ನನಗೆ** of **ನನಗೆ ಕನ್ನಡ ಗೊತ್ತು**.

To ask for someone's view:

> **ನಿಮಗೆ ಏನು ಅನಿಸುತ್ತದೆ?**
>
> *nimage ēnu anisuttade?* — "What do you think?"

For what you thought then, the "it" past: **ಅನಿಸಿತು**, "it seemed".

> **ಅದು ಕಷ್ಟ ಅಂತ ನನಗೆ ಅನಿಸಿತು.**
>
> *adu kaṣṭa anta nanage anisitu.* — "I felt that it was difficult."

## Grammar Lens: ನನ್ನ ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯದಲ್ಲಿ, in my opinion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C311-IN-MY-OPINION-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C228-VASTU228-04, KA-LEX-C02-NANNA-01, KA-LEX-C111-LOC111-01, KA-LEX-C42-ADJ-03, KA-LEX-C215-KARANA215-02, KA-LEX-C33-YOCISU-01] -->

> **ನನ್ನ ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಈ ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದು.**
>
> *nanna abhiprāyadalli ī hōṭel oḷḷeyadu.* — "In my opinion this hotel is good."

**ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯದಲ್ಲಿ** is **ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ**, "an opinion", with **-ದಲ್ಲಿ**, "in". **ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದು** (*oḷḷeyadu*, "a good one; (it is) good"): **ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ** goes in front of a noun, and at the end of a sentence it takes **-ದು**.

In talk you will also hear **ನನ್ನ ಪ್ರಕಾರ** (*nanna prakāra*, "according to me"). **ಯೋಚಿಸು** stays the verb for thinking something over; for a view given in passing, **ಅನಿಸುತ್ತದೆ** is the everyday one.
