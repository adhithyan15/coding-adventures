## You'll want to know: o anzol
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C502-PESCA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o anzol** — "fish hook".

> O peixe ficou preso no anzol.
>
> *The fish was caught on the hook.*

Plural *anzóis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anzol* is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin diminutive built on Latin *hāmus* "a hook" (Lewis & Short); the exact form is not settled.
