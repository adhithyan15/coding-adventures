## You'll want to know: सम्राट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C320-SAMRAT320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्राट** — *samrāṭ* — "an emperor".

> सम्राट के पास बहुत बड़ी सेना थी।
>
> *samrāṭ ke pās bahut baṛī senā thī.*
>
> The emperor had a very large army.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्राट** (*samrāṭ*) is Sanskrit *samrāj*, "a universal ruler" (Monier-Williams), whose nominative is *samrāṭ*: a king above kings.
