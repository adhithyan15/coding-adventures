## You'll want to know: qiúmí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C274-FREE274-05]; assesses=[] -->

*qiúmí* — "sports fan".

> *wǒ bàba shì yí ge qiúmí* — My dad is a big sports fan.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiúmí* is built from *qiú* 'a ball' and *mí*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjuw* and *mej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to be led astray'.
