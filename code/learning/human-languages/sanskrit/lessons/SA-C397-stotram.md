## You'll want to know: स्तोत्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C397-DIPA397-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्तोत्रम्** — *stotram* — "a hymn of praise".

> बालाः सायं स्तोत्रं गायन्ति।
>
> *bālāḥ sāyaṃ stotraṃ gāyanti.*
>
> The children sing a hymn in the evening.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्तोत्रम्** (*stotram*) is built on the root √stu, "to praise", with the ending *-tra*; MW gives *stotra* as praise, a hymn of praise (MW).
