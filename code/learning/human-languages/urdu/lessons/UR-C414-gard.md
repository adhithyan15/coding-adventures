## You'll want to know: گارڈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C414-GARD414-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گارڈ** — *gārḍ* — "a guard".

> عمارت کے دروازے پر ایک گارڈ ہے۔
>
> *imārat ke darvāze par ek gārḍ hai.*
>
> There's a guard at the door of the building.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گارڈ** (*gārḍ*) is borrowed from English *guard*.
