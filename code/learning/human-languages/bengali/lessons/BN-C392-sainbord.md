## You'll want to know: সাইনবোর্ড
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C392-TRAFFIC392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সাইনবোর্ড** — *sāinborḍ* — "a signboard".

> সাইনবোর্ডটা দেখে আমরা ডানদিকে গেলাম।
>
> *sāinborḍṭā dekhe āmrā ḍāndike gelām.*
>
> We saw the signboard and turned right.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাইনবোর্ড** (*sāinborḍ*) is borrowed from English *signboard*, the painted board over a shop or at a turning.
