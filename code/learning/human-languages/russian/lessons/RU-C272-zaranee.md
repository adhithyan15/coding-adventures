## You'll want to know: заранее
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C272-ZARANEE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**заранее** (*zaráneye*) — "in advance, beforehand".

> Мы купили билеты заранее.
>
> *My kupíli biléty zaráneye.*
>
> We bought the tickets in advance.

*Заранее спасибо* — thanks in advance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*заранее* is built on *за-* and *ранний* "early", the adjective of *рано*, which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *ranŭ* "early" (EtymDB).
