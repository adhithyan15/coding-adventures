## You'll want to know: বিচক্ষণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C465-BIBEK465-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিচক্ষণ** — *bichôkkhôn* — "shrewd; judicious".

> বিচক্ষণ ডাক্তার রোগটা প্রথমেই বুঝলেন।
>
> *bichôkkhôn ḍāktār rogṭā prôthômei bujhlen.*
>
> The shrewd doctor understood the illness straight away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিচক্ষণ** (*bichôkkhôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vicakṣaṇa*, "conspicuous, clear" and "clear-sighted, wise" (Monier-Williams).
