## You'll want to know: ભીતિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C453-BIK453-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભીતિ** — *bhīti* — "apprehension; fear of what may come".

> ભૂકંપ પછી ગામમાં ઘણી ભીતિ હતી.
>
> *bhūkamp pachī gāmmā̃ ghaṇī bhīti hatī.*
>
> After the earthquake there was great apprehension in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભીતિ** (*bhīti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *bhīti*, "fear, alarm, dread", as Monier-Williams records it; it shares its root with **બીવું** (*bīvũ*), from the start of the chapter.
