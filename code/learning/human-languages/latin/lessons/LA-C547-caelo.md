## You'll want to know: caelō, caelāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C547-IMAG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**caelō, caelāre** — "to engrave, carve in relief".

> Faber pōculum argenteum caelat.
>
> *The smith engraves a silver cup.*

*caelō, caelāre, caelāvī, caelātum*: raised work on silver, gold or ivory. Not to be confused with *cēlō* — to hide.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**caelō** is built on *caelum* "an engraver’s chisel", a different word from *caelum* "sky" (Lewis & Short).
