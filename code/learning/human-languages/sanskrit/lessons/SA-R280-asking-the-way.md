# Review — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SO-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "you should read every day", to a friend. (*tvaṃ pratidinaṃ paṭheḥ*.) Then "therefore she is staying at home". (*tasmāt sā gṛhe tiṣṭhati*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-02, SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-03, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-04, SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Please, where is the shop?]
- [YOU SAY: How do I get to the school?]
- [YOU SAY: How far is the station? It is quite near.]
- [YOU SAY: Go straight on, then turn left.]
- [YOU SAY: The station is on the right. Please wait here.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **वामतः** or **दक्षिणतः**, for "on the right"? (**दक्षिणतः**.) Which ending makes a request polite? (**-तु**: **गच्छतु**, **तिष्ठतु**.) Now the other way round: what does **कथं गच्छामि?** ask? ("How do I get there?")
