## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-SEGUIR]; assesses=[] -->

**seguir** — "to follow" or "to continue."

It comes through Vulgar Latin **sequīre** from Latin **sequī**, "to follow,"
reshaped with the ending of **īre**, "to go." English **sequence** is a set in
which one thing follows another; a **consequence** follows from a cause. Those
words keep the same old motion.

In directions, **seguir** tells you to keep to the route:

- **seguir la avenida** — follow the avenue;
- **seguir** — continue.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

You can tell someone to follow a named road or continue moving.
