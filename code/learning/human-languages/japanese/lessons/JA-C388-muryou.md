## You'll want to know: むりょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C388-RYOHI388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**むりょう** — *muryō* — "free of charge".

> **こどもの うんちんは むりょうです。** — *kodomo no unchin wa muryō desu* — "Children travel free."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むりょう** (*muryō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mu*, "nothing" (Middle Chinese *mju*) and *ryō*, "fee" (*lew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
