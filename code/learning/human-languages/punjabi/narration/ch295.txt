Punjabi, chapter 295: Cracks and Hollows.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਤਰੇੜ (tareṛ) — a crack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a clod, then the Punjabi for dust.

You'll want to know: ਤਰੇੜ.
ਤਰੇੜ — tareṛ — "a crack".
ਕੰਧ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਲੰਮੀ ਤਰੇੜ ਹੈ। kandh vich ikk lammī tareṛ hai. There is a long crack in the wall.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਰੇੜ (tareṛ) is inherited from a reconstructed tṛdi, "crack, fissure"; CDIAL lists Punjabi tareṛ, "crack, crevice", under it (CDIAL 5926).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਫਟਣਾ (phaṭṇā) — to split open; to crack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for dust, then the Punjabi for a crack.

You'll want to know: ਫਟਣਾ.
ਫਟਣਾ — phaṭṇā — "to split open; to crack".
ਸੋਕੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਜ਼ਮੀਨ ਫਟਦੀ ਹੈ। soke vich zamīn phaṭdī hai. In a drought the ground splits open.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਘੜਾ ਫਟਿਆ, oh ghaṛā phaṭiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਫਟਣਾ (phaṭṇā) goes back to Sanskrit sphāṭayati, "causes to split", as a word taken from Hindi: CDIAL lists Punjabi phāṭṇā, "to be split", under it, and derives the short-vowel phaṭṇā from Hindi phaṭnā (CDIAL 13825).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਗਲਣਾ (galṇā) — to rot; to decay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for hollow, then the Punjabi for a ravine.

You'll want to know: ਗਲਣਾ.
ਗਲਣਾ — galṇā — "to rot; to decay".
ਗਿੱਲੇ ਫਲ ਜਲਦੀ ਗਲਦੇ ਹਨ। gille phal jaldī galde han. Wet fruit rots quickly.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਸੇਬ ਗਲਿਆ, oh seb galiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਗਲਣਾ (galṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit galati, "drips, oozes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi galṇā, "to melt, sink", under it; the sense "to rot" is not among those it gives (CDIAL 4074).
