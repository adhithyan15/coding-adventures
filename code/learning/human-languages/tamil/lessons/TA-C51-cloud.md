## You'll want to know: மேகம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C51-SKY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**மேகம்** (*mēkam*) — "a cloud".

Sanskrit *megha*, taken in whole. Tamil has inherited cloud-words too — *mukil* and *kār*, both still alive in names and in verse — but மேகம் is the one doing the daily work.

Telugu did not borrow here at all: its cloud is *mabbu*, its own. Which words a language takes and which it keeps is rarely tidy. On the sky the two of them borrowed together; on the cloud they part.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

That is the whole sky: the space, the sun, the moon, the small lights, and the thing that hides them.
