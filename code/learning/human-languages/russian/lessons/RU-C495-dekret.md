## You'll want to know: декрет
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C495-EXPECTING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**декрет** (*dekrét*) — "maternity leave; a decree".

> После рождения сына она год была в декрете.
>
> *Pósle rozhdéniya sýna oná god bylá v dekréte.*
>
> After her son was born she was on maternity leave for a year.

Masculine. Colloquial for *декретный отпуск*. Its older sense is a decree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*декрет* was borrowed from Latin *decretum* 'a decree' (EtymDB), from *dēcernere* 'to decide' (Lewis & Short).
