## You'll want to know: مرح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C503-TABA503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مرح** — *mariḥ* — "cheerful, fun-loving".

> خالي رجل مرح ويضحك كثيرا.
>
> *khālī rajul mariḥ wa-yaḍḥaku kathīran.*
>
> My uncle is a cheerful man and laughs a lot.

The noun مرح, read maraḥ, is merriment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرح** (*mariḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-r-ḥ*, "to be lively, merry" (Lane). Mariḥ is the adjective; maraḥ, with the same letters, is the noun.
