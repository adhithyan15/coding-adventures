## You'll want to know: حماة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C528-ZAWAJ528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حماة** — *ḥamāh* — "a mother-in-law".

> تزور حماتي بيتنا كل جمعة.
>
> *tazūru ḥamātī baytanā kull jumʿa.*
>
> My mother-in-law visits our house every Friday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حماة** (*ḥamāh*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-m-w*, "a husband's or wife's kin" (Lane). Lane gives ḥam as the husband's father and ḥamāh as his mother.

## What you've built
