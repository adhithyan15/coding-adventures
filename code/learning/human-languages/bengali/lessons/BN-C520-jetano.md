## You'll want to know: জেতানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C520-PODOK520-05]; assesses=[] -->

**জেতানো** — *jetāno* — "to lead to victory; to make win".

> অধিনায়ক একা দলকে জেতাল।
>
> *Ôdhināyôk ekā dôlke jetālô.*
>
> The captain won the match for the team on her own.

Past (he/she …): তিনি দলকে জেতালেন, *tini dôlke jetālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জেতানো** (*jetāno*) is built on জেতা, "to win", as its causative partner: to make someone win.
