## You'll want to know: भाचा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C495-NATU495-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भाचा** — *bhācā* — "a sister's son".

> सुट्टीत माझा भाचा आमच्याकडे राहायला आला.
>
> *suṭṭīt mājhā bhācā āmcyākaḍe rāhāylā ālā.*
>
> My nephew came to stay with us in the holidays.

Masculine.

A sister's son; भाची, a sister's daughter. To the child, the mother's brother is मामा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाचा** (*bhācā*) is filed by CDIAL under *bhrātrya*, "pertaining to a brother", together with Prakrit *bhacca*, "sister's son" (CDIAL 9673); how a brother's word came to name a sister's son is not settled.
