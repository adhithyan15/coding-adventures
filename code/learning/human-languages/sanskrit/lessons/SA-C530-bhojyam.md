## You'll want to know: भोज्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C530-BHOJYA530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भोज्यम्** — *bhojyam* — "eatables; food".

> यदि अतिथयः आगच्छन्ति, तर्हि बहु भोज्यं पचामः।
>
> *yadi atithayaḥ āgacchanti, tarhi bahu bhojyaṃ pacāmaḥ.*
>
> If the guests come, we will cook a lot of food.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भोज्यम्** (*bhojyam*) comes from the root √bhuj, "to enjoy, to eat", with the ending *-ya*; MW gives *bhojya* as eatable, what is eaten (MW).
