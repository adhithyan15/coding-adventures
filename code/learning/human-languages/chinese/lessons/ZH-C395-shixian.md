## You'll want to know: shíxiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C395-RESULT395-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shíxiàn* — "to come true; to make real".

> *yàoshi yǒu qián wǒ de mèngxiǎng jiù néng shíxiàn* — If I had the money, my dream could come true.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shíxiàn* is built from *shí* 'real' and 现 'to appear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zyit* and *henH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'full' and 'true' and the second 'to show'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
