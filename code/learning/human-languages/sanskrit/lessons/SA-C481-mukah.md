## You'll want to know: मूकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C481-ANDHA481-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मूकः** — *mūkaḥ* — "mute".

> मूकः बालः न वदति, किन्तु सर्वं जानाति।
>
> *mūkaḥ bālaḥ na vadati, kintu sarvaṃ jānāti.*
>
> The mute boy does not speak, but he understands everything.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूकः** (*mūkaḥ*) MW explains it as "tied or bound", that is, tongue-tied, and gives "dumb, speechless, mute"; it names no root, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
