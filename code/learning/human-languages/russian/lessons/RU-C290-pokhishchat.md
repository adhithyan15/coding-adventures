## You'll want to know: похищать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C290-MOSHENNIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**похищать** (*pokhishchát*) — "to kidnap, to abduct; to steal".

> В фильме похитили дочь богатого человека.
>
> *V fílme pokhítili doch bogátovo chelovéka.*
>
> In the film the daughter of a rich man was kidnapped.

Imperfective; perfective *похитить* (*pokhítit*), *я похищу*. Bookish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*похищать* is built on *по-* and an old verb *хитить* "to seize", the root of *хищник* "a predator"; EtymDB lists no parent.
