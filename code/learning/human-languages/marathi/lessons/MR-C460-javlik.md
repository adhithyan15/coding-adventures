## You'll want to know: जवळीक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C460-MAYA460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जवळीक** — *javḷīk* — "closeness".

> एकत्र प्रवास केल्यावर दोघींमध्ये जवळीक वाढली.
>
> *ekatra pravās kelyāvar doghīmmadhye javḷīk vāḍhlī.*
>
> After travelling together the two women grew close.

Feminine.

Built on जवळ, near: closeness between people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जवळीक** (*javḷīk*) is usually traced to जवळ, "near", with the noun ending *-īk*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
