## You'll want to know: ਪੀੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C325-BETTER325-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੀੜ** — *pīṛ* — "an ache".

> ਦਵਾਈ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਪੀੜ ਘੱਟ ਹੈ।
>
> *davāī ton bāad pīṛ ghaṭṭ hai.*
>
> After the medicine the ache is less.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੀੜ** (*pīṛ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pīḍā*, "pain, damage"; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists Lahnda *pīṛ*, "pain" (CDIAL 8227).
