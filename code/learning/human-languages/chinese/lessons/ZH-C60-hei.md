## You'll want to know: hēi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C60-COLOR60-04]; assesses=[] -->

*hēi* — "black; dark".

> *nàge fángjiān hěn hēi* — That room is dark.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hēi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *xok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the colour of the north', which the old scheme of five colours paired with black.
