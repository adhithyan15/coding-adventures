## You'll want to know: qǐchuáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C50-SLEEP50-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qǐchuáng* — "to get up".

> *wǒ qī diǎn qǐchuáng* — I get up at seven.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qǐchuáng* is built from qǐ 'to rise' and chuáng 'bed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khiX* and *dzrjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
