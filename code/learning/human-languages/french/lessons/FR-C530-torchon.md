## You'll want to know: le torchon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C530-VAISSELLE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le torchon** — "tea towel, dishcloth".

> Le torchon est accroché près de l'évier.
>
> *The tea towel is hanging by the sink.*

In Belgium *l'essuie*. *Le torchon brûle* — there is trouble between them.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le torchon* is built on *torche* "a twist of straw", which goes back to Vulgar Latin *torca*, from Latin *torquēre* "to twist" (Lewis & Short).
