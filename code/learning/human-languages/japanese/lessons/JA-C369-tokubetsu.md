## You'll want to know: とくべつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C369-JUUYOU369-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とくべつ** — *tokubetsu* — "special".

> **たんじょうびに なったら、しょくじは とくべつです。** — *tanjōbi ni nattara, shokuji wa tokubetsu desu* — "On my birthday, the meal is special."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とくべつ** (*tokubetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *toku*, "special" (Middle Chinese *dok*) and *betsu*, "separate" (*bjet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
