## You'll want to know: nel frattempo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C274-FRATTEMPO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**nel frattempo** — "in the meantime, meanwhile".

> Lui è andato a prendere il pane; nel frattempo io ho fatto il caffè.
>
> *He went to get the bread; in the meantime I made the coffee.*

Close to *intanto*, a little more formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frattempo* joins *fra* "between" and *tempo* "time", which is inherited from Latin *tempus* "time" (Lewis & Short): the time in between.
