Italian, chapter 512: Il torneo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il torneo — tournament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for league table, then the Italian for to knock out.

You'll want to know: il torneo.
il torneo — "tournament".
A giugno c'è il torneo di tennis della scuola. In June there is the school tennis tournament.
Vincere un torneo — to win a tournament.

The word, taken apart.
torneo comes from Old French tornei "a tournament", built on torner "to turn", from Latin tornāre "to turn on a lathe" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: qualificarsi.
qualificarsi — "to qualify".
Se vinciamo domani, ci qualifichiamo per la finale. If we win tomorrow, we qualify for the final.

The word, taken apart.
qualificarsi comes from Medieval Latin quālificāre "to give a quality", built on Latin quālis "of what kind" and facere "to make" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: il podio.
il podio — "podium".
Sono salita sul podio per la prima volta. I stood on the podium for the first time.
Salire sul podio — to finish in the top three.

The word, taken apart.
podio is a learned borrowing from Latin podium "a raised platform" (EtymDB), from Greek pódion "a little foot, a base".
