## You'll want to know: il cestino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C448-ARREDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il cestino** — "the litter bin; the small basket".

> Butta la carta nel cestino.
>
> *Throw the paper in the bin.*

*Il cestino della spesa* — a shopping basket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cestino* is a little *cesta* 'basket', which is inherited from Latin *cista*, from Greek *kístē* 'box, basket' (EtymDB).
