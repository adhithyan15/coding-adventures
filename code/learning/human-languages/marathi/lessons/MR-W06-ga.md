# ग — the same place, with the voice on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Put two fingers on your throat and say *ka*, then *ga*. The place in your mouth does not move. Something else switches on.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-GA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-KHA-01, MR-SCRIPT-A-INDEPENDENT-01] -->

> ग

**ग** is *ga*: the same back-of-the-mouth closure as **क**, made with the voice
running. That is the only difference, and your fingers felt it a moment ago.

The shape is a single curve that swings down and left from the headline and
finishes with a small tail on the right. It is the first letter of *gāv*,
"village", and it sits inside *sāṅgṇe*, "to tell" — a verb this book uses
long before it shows you this letter.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-GA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **ग** twice with the model visible. Then write **ख** once from memory
beside it, and check the two are not the same shape.
