## You'll want to know: অফার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C424-DAM424-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অফার** — *ôphār* — "an offer; a special deal".

> দোকানে এই সপ্তাহে চায়ের ওপর অফার আছে।
>
> *dokāne ei sôptāhe chāyer opôr ôphār āchhe.*
>
> This week the shop has an offer on tea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অফার** (*ôphār*) is borrowed from English *offer*.
