## Fixed — no lesson announced as drivable asks a driver to read, write or gesture

A lesson whose core is `voice` is announced as "you can do this one in the car", and
only its detachable sections (writing, the letters in a word, script) wait behind the
stop guard. The modality rule now reads the sections a driver hears plainly for a step
that needs eyes or hands (reason `eyes-or-hands-step`, human-language-data); before it,
such steps in the core of a lesson with a letters or writing section were read out at
the wheel.

- **Rewritten, still drivable (11).** KA-C01-haudu, KA-C01-illa, KA-C01-namaskara and
  KA-C01-sari open their wrap-up with "[YOU READ: **…**]". KA-C01-namaskara's warm-up no
  longer asks for a bow or a reading ("Say **namaskāra**: “hello” or “greetings.” Its letters
  come next, one small piece at a time."; the custom stays in "Why it's said this way"), and
  its practice cue is "[YOU SAY: "namaskāra" — you are offering respect]". Recalls split into
  RECALL and READ in KA-C02-enu, KA-C03-hege, KA-C03-naanu, KA-C05-iru, KA-C05-maatanaadu and
  KA-C39-kaapi. KA-R80-the-breath's "[YOU SAY: ದ then ಧ, and ಬ then ಭ, with a hand in front of
  your mouth]" becomes the SAY and "[YOU FEEL: the breath on the second of each pair, with a
  hand in front of your mouth]", which the narration defers.

- **Now not drivable at the core (1):** KA-R81-ten-consonants ("Read **ಕ**, then **ಗ**, and
  say what your throat does").
