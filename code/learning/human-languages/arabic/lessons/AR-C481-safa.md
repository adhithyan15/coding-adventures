## You'll want to know: صفا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C481-MATAR481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صفا** — *ṣafā* — "to clear up; to become clear".

> صفت السماء بعد المطر.
>
> *ṣafat as-samāʾ baʿda al-maṭar.*
>
> The sky cleared after the rain.

Present: يصفو, *yaṣfū*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صفا** (*ṣafā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-f-w*, "to be clear, pure" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
