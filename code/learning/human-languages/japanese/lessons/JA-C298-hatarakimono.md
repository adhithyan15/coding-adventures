## You'll want to know: はたらきもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C298-MUKASHI298-05]; assesses=[] -->

**はたらきもの** — *hatarakimono* — "a hard worker".

> **むらの わかい おとこは はたらきものでした。** — *mura no wakai otoko wa hatarakimono deshita* — "The young man of the village was a hard worker."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はたらきもの** (*hatarakimono*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hataraki*, "working" (from *hataraku*), and *mono*, "person" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
