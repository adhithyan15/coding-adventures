## You'll want to know: মিল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C358-MIL358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মিল** — *mil* — "a likeness; a match".

> দুই ভাইয়ের চেহারায় অনেক মিল আছে।
>
> *dui bhāiyer chehārāy ônek mil āchhe.*
>
> The two brothers look very alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মিল** (*mil*) is of the same family as মেলানো, from Sanskrit *milana*, meeting (CDIAL 10134 lists Bengali milan): the place where two things meet and agree.
