## You'll want to know: 生人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C378-NOISE378-04]; assesses=[] -->

**生人** — *shēngrén* — "stranger".

> **晚上有生人来你最好不要开门** *wǎnshang yǒu shēngrén lái nǐ zuìhǎo bú yào kāimén* — If a stranger comes at night, you'd better not open the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**生人** (*shēngrén*) is built from 生 'raw, new' and 人 'a person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjaeng* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to grow'. The sense 'unfamiliar' is not given there.
