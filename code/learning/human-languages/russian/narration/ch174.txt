Russian, chapter 174: To advise, To describe, To repeat, To translate, To remember.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
советовать (sovétovat) — to advise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to fry, then the Russian for to pour.

You'll want to know: советовать.
советовать (sovétovat) — "to advise".

The word, taken apart.
советовать (sovétovat) comes from совет, "advice", со-, "together", with old вет, "counsel, word" (Vasmer), the root also of ответ, "answer".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to repeat, then the Russian for to translate.

You'll want to know: помнить.
помнить (pómnit) — "to remember".

The word, taken apart.
помнить (pómnit) is по- with мнить, "to think, to suppose". Vasmer relates мнить to Lithuanian minėti, "to recall", Sanskrit mányate, "thinks", and Latin mēns, "mind".
