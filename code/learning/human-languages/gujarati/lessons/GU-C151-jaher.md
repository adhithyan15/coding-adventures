# જાહેર (jāher) — public

## You'll want to know: જાહેર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C151-READ151-04]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C80-TIME-03, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01] -->

**જાહેર** (*jāher*) — "public, open to all".

It marks what belongs to everyone, and on a notice it most often comes before
**રજા**, *a holiday*:

> **આવતીકાલે જાહેર રજા છે.** — *āvtīkāle jāher rajā che.* — Tomorrow is a public holiday.

A **જાહેર રજા** closes offices, banks and many shops for everybody at once.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાહેર** (*jāher*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ẓāhir*,
"apparent, manifest", from the root *ẓ-h-r*, "to come out into the open, to
become evident" (Lane). What is out in the open is public.
