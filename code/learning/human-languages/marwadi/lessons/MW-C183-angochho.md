## You'll want to know: अंगोछो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C183-HATH183-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगोछो** — *aṅgochho* — "a shoulder cloth".

> म्हारो अंगोछो गीलो है।
>
> *mhāro aṅgochho gīlo hai.*
>
> My shoulder cloth is wet.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगोछो** (*aṅgochho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aṅgoñcha*, "a towel"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ãgochā* and Sindhi *aṅgocho* (CDIAL 139). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
