## You'll want to know: چھپوانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C524-FEHRIST524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چھپوانا** — *chhapvānā* — "to have (something) printed".

> اگر کتاب تیار ہوئی تو ہم اسے چھپوائیں گے۔
>
> *agar kitāb tayyār huī to ham use chhapvāeṅ ge.*
>
> If the book is ready, we'll have it printed.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اپنی کتاب چھپوائی, *us ne apnī kitāb chhapvāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چھپوانا** (*chhapvānā*) is the causative of *chhāpnā*, "to print", which CDIAL lists under *\*chapp*, "press", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4994).
