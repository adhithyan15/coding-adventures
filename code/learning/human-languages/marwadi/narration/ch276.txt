Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 276: Flute and Clapping.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बांसळी (bā̃sḷī) — a flute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for credit for a deed, then the Marwadi for an opinion.

You'll want to know: बांसळी.
बांसळी — bā̃sḷī — "a flute".
म्हारी बांसळी लांबी है। mhārī bā̃sḷī lā̃bī hai. My flute is long.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बांसळी (bā̃sḷī) is inherited from Sanskrit vamśī, "a flute, a pipe", itself from vamśa, "bamboo"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Gujarati vā̃sḷī, "pipe, flute", and Hindi bãsī, "flute" (CDIAL 11180).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बजावणो (bajāvṇo) — to play an instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an opinion, then the Marwadi for a flute.

You'll want to know: बजावणो.
बजावणो — bajāvṇo — "to play an instrument".
भाई बांसळी बजावै है। bhāī bā̃sḷī bajāvai hai. My brother plays the flute.
Past (I …): म्हैं बांसळी बजाई, mhaĩ bā̃sḷī bajāī.

The word, taken apart.
बजावणो (bajāvṇo) is built on the verb CDIAL lists as Old Marwari bājaï, "to sound", under Sanskrit vādyate, "is made to sound" (CDIAL 11513); as a causative it means "to make sound".

Lesson 3 of 5.
सुर (sur) — a tune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a flute, then the Marwadi for to play an instrument.

You'll want to know: सुर.
सुर — sur — "a tune".
यो सुर मीठो है। yo sur mīṭho hai. This tune is sweet.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सुर (sur) is inherited from Sanskrit sura, "sound", a variant of svara; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi sur, "sound, words, tune" (CDIAL 13498).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ताळी (tāḷī) — a clap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to play an instrument, then the Marwadi for a tune.

You'll want to know: ताळी.
ताळी — tāḷī — "a clap".
सगळा ताळी बजावै है। sagḷā tāḷī bajāvai hai. Everyone claps.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ताळी (tāḷī) is inherited from Sanskrit tāla, "a clap, rhythm"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Gujarati tāḷī, "clapping" (CDIAL 5748). You clap with बजावणो (bajāvṇo): ताळी बजावो.

Lesson 5 of 5.
गूंजणो (gū̃jṇo) — to hum; to ring out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a tune, then the Marwadi for a clap.

You'll want to know: गूंजणो.
गूंजणो — gū̃jṇo — "to hum; to ring out".
मंदिर मांय गीत गूंजै है। mandir mā̃y gīt gū̃jai hai. A song rings out in the temple.
Past (it …): वो गीत गूंज्यो, vo gīt gū̃jyo.

The word, taken apart.
गूंजणो (gū̃jṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit guñjati, "hums, buzzes"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi gū̃jnā, "to hum" (CDIAL 4175). A sound that hums on fills the room.
