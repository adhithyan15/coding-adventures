# ಹತ್ತು — ten, and the p that turned into h

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C07-OMBATTU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Nine was *ombattu*. One number left, and it carries the change that
most makes Kannada sound like Kannada.

## You'll want to know: ಹತ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-ETYMON-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹತ್ತು** (*hattu*) — "**ten**." Tamil says *pattu*.

Kannada softened an old word-initial **p-** into **h-**, and did it right across
the language: *pāl* became *hālu* ("milk"), *pal* became *hallu* ("tooth").

Once you know the rule it runs backwards for you. Meet a Kannada word starting
**h-**, take the *h* off, put a *p* on, and you often have its Tamil cousin.

Every letter in ಹತ್ತು is one you have learned: **ಹ**, **ತ**, the virama **್**,
and **ು**.
