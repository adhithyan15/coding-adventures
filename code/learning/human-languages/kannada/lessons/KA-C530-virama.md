## You'll want to know: ವಿರಾಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C530-BIDUVU530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿರಾಮ** — *virāma* — "a pause; a break".

> ಎರಡು ಗಂಟೆ ಓದಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ನಾನು ವಿರಾಮ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡೆ.
>
> *eraḍu gaṇṭe ōdida mēle nānu virāma tegedukoṇḍe.*
>
> After studying for two hours I took a break.

ವಿರಾಮದ ವೇಳೆ, leisure time. In grammar ಪೂರ್ಣವಿರಾಮ, a full pause, is the full stop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿರಾಮ** (*virāma*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *virāma*, "cessation, termination" (Monier-Williams), from *vi-ram*, "to stop, to rest".
