## You'll want to know: desde entonces
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C537-ENTONCES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**desde entonces** — "since then".

> Desde entonces no lo he visto.
>
> *I haven't seen him since then.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desde entonces* is built on *desde* 'since' and *entonces*, which comes from Latin *in tunc* 'at that time' (EtymDB), with an *-s* added on the end.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
