## You'll want to know: percuter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C411-ACCIDENT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**percuter** — "to crash into, to hit".

> Une moto a percuté l'arrière du bus.
>
> *A motorbike crashed into the back of the bus.*

A hard, violent hit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*percuter* is a learned borrowing from Latin *percutere* 'to strike hard' (EtymDB), from *per-* and *quatere* 'to shake'.
