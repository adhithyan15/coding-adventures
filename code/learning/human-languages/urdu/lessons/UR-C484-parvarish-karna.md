## You'll want to know: پرورش کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C484-RAISE484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پرورش کرنا** — *parvarish karnā* — "to raise a child; to bring up".

> نانی نے میری پرورش کی۔
>
> *nānī ne merī parvarish kī.*
>
> My grandmother brought me up.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بیٹی کی پرورش کی, *us ne beṭī kī parvarish kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پرورش کرنا** (*parvarish karnā*) is built from **پرورش**, usually traced to Persian *parvarish*, "nurture", and **کرنا**, "to do".
