## You'll want to know: el invento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C571-DESCUBRIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el invento** — "the invention".

> El teléfono fue un gran invento.
>
> *The telephone was a great invention.*

*Inventar* is to invent, and also to make something up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invento* comes from Latin *inventum* 'a device, an invention' (EtymDB), from *invenīre* 'to come upon, to find'.
