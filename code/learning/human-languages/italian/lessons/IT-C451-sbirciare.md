## You'll want to know: sbirciare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C451-PASSEGGIATA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sbirciare** — "to peek, to sneak a look".

> Ho sbirciato dalla finestra.
>
> *I peeked out of the window.*

Quick and secret.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sbirciare* is usually linked to an old word *bircio* 'short-sighted', but the origin is not settled.
