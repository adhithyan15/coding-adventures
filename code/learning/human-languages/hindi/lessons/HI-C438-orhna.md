## You'll want to know: ओढ़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C438-JADA438-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढ़ना** — *oṛhnā* — "to wrap oneself in (a shawl or a blanket)".

> दादी ने कंबल ओढ़ा।
>
> *dādī ne kambal oṛhā.*
>
> Grandmother wrapped herself in a blanket.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चादर ओढ़ी, *usne cādar oṛhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढ़ना** (*oṛhnā*) goes back to *\*ōḍḍh*, "put on, wear, cover", a form CDIAL reconstructs and thinks may come from Dravidian (CDIAL 2547); it lists Hindi *oṛhnā*, "to cover".
