## You'll want to know: しばらく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C264-KOROBU264-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しばらく** — *shibaraku* — "for a while; for some time".

> **しばらく びょういんに いました。** — *shibaraku byōin ni imashita* — "I was in hospital for some time."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しばらく** (*shibaraku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
