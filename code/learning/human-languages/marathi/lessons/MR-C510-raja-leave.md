## You'll want to know: रजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C510-SODNE510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रजा** — *rajā* — "time off work; leave".

> आजारी असल्यामुळे मी दोन दिवस रजा घेतली.
>
> *ājārī aslyāmuḷe mī don divas rajā ghetlī.*
>
> Because I was ill I took two days off.

Feminine.

रजा घेणे, to take leave; रजेवर असणे, to be on leave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रजा** (*rajā*) is a loan from Persian *razā*, "leave, permission", usually traced to Arabic *riḍā*, "consent".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
