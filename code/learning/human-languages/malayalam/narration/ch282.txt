Lesson 1 of 5.
പ്രാധാന്യം (prādhānyaṁ) — importance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for that is, then the Malayalam for especially.

You'll want to know: പ്രാധാന്യം.
പ്രാധാന്യം — prādhānyaṁ — "importance".

The word, taken apart.
പ്രാധാന്യം (prādhānyaṁ) from Sanskrit prādhānya, "pre-eminence", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ. It is built on pradhāna, "chief".
