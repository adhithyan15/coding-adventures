Arabic, chapter 316: When buildings fall.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
انهار (inhāra) — to collapse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to defeat, then the Arabic for to surrender.

You'll want to know: انهار.
انهار — inhāra — "to collapse".
انهار البيت القديم بعد المطر. inhāra al-bayt al-qadīm baʿda al-maṭar. The old house collapsed after the rain.
Present: ينهار, yanhār.
Of a person: انهار (inhāra) بعد الخبر, he broke down at the news.

The word, taken apart.
انهار (inhāra) is built on the native Arabic root h-w-r (Lane), said of a building or a bank of earth that falls down. It is Form VII, "to tumble down".

Lesson 3 of 6.
اندلع (indalaʿa) — to break out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to collapse, then the Arabic for to overflow.

You'll want to know: اندلع.
اندلع — indalaʿa — "to break out".
اندلع حريق في السوق. indalaʿa ḥarīq fī as-sūq. A fire broke out in the market.
Present: يندلع, yandaliʿ.
Of fires, wars and quarrels.

The word, taken apart.
اندلع (indalaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root d-l-ʿ (Lane), said of a tongue that lolls out and of a sword that slips from its sheath. It is Form VII; a fire or a war that breaks out comes from that sense.

Lesson 5 of 6.
أنقاض (anqāḍ) — ruins, debris.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to break out, then the Arabic for rubble.

You'll want to know: أنقاض.
أنقاض — anqāḍ — "ruins, debris".
بني بيت جديد فوق أنقاض البيت القديم. buniya bayt jadīd fawqa anqāḍ al-bayt al-qadīm. A new house was built on the ruins of the old one.
A plural word, as English ruins.

The word, taken apart.
أنقاض (anqāḍ) is built on the native Arabic root n-q-ḍ (Lane), "to undo, to pull down a building". Anqāḍ is the plural of niqḍ, "what has been pulled down".
