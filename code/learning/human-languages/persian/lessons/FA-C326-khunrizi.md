## You'll want to know: خونریزی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C326-MASDUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خونریزی** (*khunrizi*) — "bleeding; bloodshed".

> پرستار خونریزی را متوقف کرد.
>
> *Parastâr khunrizi râ motevaqqef kard.*
>
> The nurse stopped the bleeding.

*khun* "blood" + *rizi* "pouring", from *riz-*, the present stem of *rikhtan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khunrizi* joins *khun* "blood" and *rizi* "pouring", from *rikhtan* "to pour", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian. All of it is native Persian.
