> **むっつ** — *mu-t-tsu* — **six things**

**む** from last lesson, then the small **っ** and **つ**.

Last chapter said of **ろく**: *six has one everyday reading; no native word
stands beside it.* That was true of the borrowed count and it is worth putting
straight now. The native word for six exists — it is **む**, and you are holding
it. What it does not do is stand inside the borrowed count the way **よん**
does.
