## You'll want to know: a viela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C437-RUAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a viela** — "the narrow lane, the alleyway".

> No bairro antigo, as vielas são estreitas e escuras.
>
> *In the old quarter, the alleyways are narrow and dark.*

Typical of the old quarters of hill towns.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viela* is usually explained as a small form of *via* 'way' with *-ela*, from Latin *via* (Lewis & Short); EtymDB has no Portuguese entry.
