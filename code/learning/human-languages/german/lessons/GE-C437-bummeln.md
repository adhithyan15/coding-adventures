## You'll want to know: bummeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C437-BUMMELN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**bummeln** — "to stroll, to wander around; to dawdle".

> Am Samstag bummeln wir durch die Fußgängerzone.
>
> *On Saturday we stroll through the pedestrian zone.*

Perfekt with *sein* for moving: *wir sind durch die Stadt gebummelt*. *Der Stadtbummel* — a stroll round town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *bummeln* is not settled. It is often explained as a sound word for something swinging slowly to and fro, like a bell; EtymDB lists the sense 'to loaf'.
