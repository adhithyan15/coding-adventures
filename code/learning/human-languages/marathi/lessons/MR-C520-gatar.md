## You'll want to know: गटार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C520-NAL520-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गटार** — *gaṭār* — "a drain; a gutter".

> पावसाळ्यात रस्त्यावरचं गटार भरून वाहतं.
>
> *pāvsāḷyāt rastyāvarcaṁ gaṭār bharūn vāhtaṁ.*
>
> In the rainy season the drain on the road overflows.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गटार** (*gaṭār*) is the word for an open drain; its origin is not settled.
