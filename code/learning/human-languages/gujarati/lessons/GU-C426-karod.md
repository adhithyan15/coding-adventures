## You'll want to know: કરોડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C426-LAKH426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કરોડ** — *karoḍ* — "a crore (ten million)".

> આ શહેરમાં એક કરોડ માણસો રહે છે.
>
> *ā śahermā̃ ek karoḍ māṇso rahe che.*
>
> Ten million people live in this city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કરોડ** (*karoḍ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kōṭi*, "ten million"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 3498). A crore is a hundred lakhs.
