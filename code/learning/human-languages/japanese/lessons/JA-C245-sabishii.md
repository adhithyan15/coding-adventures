## You'll want to know: さびしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C245-JOY245-02]; assesses=[] -->

**さびしい** — *sabishii* — "lonely".

> **ひとりで さびしいですか。** — *hitori de sabishii desu ka* — "Are you lonely on your own?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さびしい** (*sabishii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
