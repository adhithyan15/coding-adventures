## You'll want to know: स्वभावः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C460-SVABHAVA460-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वभावः** — *svabhāvaḥ* — "temperament".

> मीरायाः स्वभावः शान्तः अस्ति।
>
> *mīrāyāḥ svabhāvaḥ śāntaḥ asti.*
>
> Mira has a calm temperament.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वभावः** (*svabhāvaḥ*) is built from *sva*, "own", and *bhāva*, "being", from the root √bhū: one's own way of being; MW gives "natural state, innate disposition, nature" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
