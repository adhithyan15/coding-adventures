## You'll want to know: मस्तिष्कः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C471-MASTISKA471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मस्तिष्कः** — *mastiṣkaḥ* — "the brain".

> मस्तिष्केण नरः चिन्तयति।
>
> *mastiṣkeṇa naraḥ cintayati.*
>
> A person thinks with the brain.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मस्तिष्कः** (*mastiṣkaḥ*) MW gives "the brain" from the Rigveda on; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
