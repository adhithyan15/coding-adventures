## You'll want to know: بادکنک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C341-MEHMANI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بادکنک** (*bâdkonak*) — "a balloon".

> بچه ها با بادکنک بازی میکردند.
>
> *Bachche-hâ bâ bâdkonak bâzi mikardand.*
>
> The children were playing with balloons.

*bâd* "wind, air" + *kon-* "make" + *-ak*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâdkonak* is built from Persian *bâd* "wind, air", *kon-*, the present stem of *kardan* "to make", and the suffix *-ak*. All of it is native.

## What you've built
