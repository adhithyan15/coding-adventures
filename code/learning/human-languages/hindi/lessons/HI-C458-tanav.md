## You'll want to know: तनाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C458-TANAV458-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तनाव** — *tanāv* — "stress; tension".

> नौकरी के तनाव से अरुण की नींद चली गई।
>
> *naukrī ke tanāv se Aruṇ kī nīnd calī gaī.*
>
> The stress of his job has cost Arun his sleep.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तनाव** (*tanāv*) is probably a noun built on the root of *tānnā*, "to stretch, pull, tighten", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *tānáyati* (CDIAL 5762): a rope pulled tight, and then a mind. CDIAL gives no row for *tanāv* itself.
