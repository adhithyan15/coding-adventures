## You'll want to know: danzare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C322-FUOCHI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**danzare** — "to dance (formal)".

> Abbiamo danzato intorno al fuoco.
>
> *We danced around the fire.*

A literary word for *ballare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*danzare* was borrowed from French *danser* (EtymDB), whose deeper origin is not settled.
