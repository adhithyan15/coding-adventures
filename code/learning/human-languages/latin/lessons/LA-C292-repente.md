## You'll want to know: repente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C292-REPENTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**repente** — "suddenly, unexpectedly".

> Repente magna pluvia cecidit, et omnēs fūgērunt.
>
> *Suddenly heavy rain fell, and everyone ran away.*

The ablative of the old adjective *repēns*, 'sudden'. Close to *subitō* (taught), and common in Livy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**repente** is the ablative of *repēns*, "sudden" (Lewis & Short), used as an adverb. Where *repēns* itself comes from is not settled.
