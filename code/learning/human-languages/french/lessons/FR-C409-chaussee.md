## You'll want to know: la chaussée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C409-VIRAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la chaussée** — "the road surface, the carriageway".

> La chaussée est glissante après la pluie.
>
> *The road surface is slippery after the rain.*

The part of the street for cars, not the pavement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chaussée* is inherited from Late Latin *calciāta* 'paved with lime', from *calx* 'lime' (EtymDB). English *causeway* is a cousin.
