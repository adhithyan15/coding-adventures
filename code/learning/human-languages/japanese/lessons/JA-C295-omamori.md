## You'll want to know: おまもり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C295-HATSUMOUDE295-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おまもり** — *omamori* — "a charm; an amulet".

> **ははが じんじゃで おまもりを かいました。** — *haha ga jinja de omamori o kaimashita* — "My mother bought a charm at the shrine."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おまもり** (*omamori*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *o-*, "the polite prefix", and *mamori*, "protection" (from *mamoru*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
