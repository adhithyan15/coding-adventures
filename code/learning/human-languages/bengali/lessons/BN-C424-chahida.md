## You'll want to know: চাহিদা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C424-DAM424-04]; assesses=[] -->

**চাহিদা** — *chāhidā* — "demand (for goods)".

> শীতে কম্বলের চাহিদা বেশি।
>
> *shīte kômbôler chāhidā beshi.*
>
> In winter there is more demand for blankets.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চাহিদা** (*chāhidā*) is built on চাহ-, the old stem of **চাওয়া**, "to want", from the reconstructed *cāh*, "see, desire" (CDIAL 4775): what people want to buy.
