## You'll want to know: тариф
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C413-PROYEZD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**тариф** (*taríf*) — "a rate, a tariff; a fare".

> С января в автобусе новый тариф.
>
> *S yanvaryá v avtóbuse nóvyy taríf.*
>
> From January there is a new fare on the bus.

Masculine. *Тариф* is the official rate; *цена* the everyday word for a price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тариф* was borrowed from Italian *tariffa* 'a tariff', which EtymDB traces to Arabic *taʿrīfa* 'a rate'.
