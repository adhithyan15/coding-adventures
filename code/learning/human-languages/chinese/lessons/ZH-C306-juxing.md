## You'll want to know: jǔxíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C306-WEDDING306-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jǔxíng* — "to hold a ceremony; to hold an event".

> *hūnlǐ zài zhōngwǔ jǔxíng* — The wedding is at noon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jǔxíng* is built from *jǔ* 'to raise' and *xíng* 'to go', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjoX* and *haeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to lift' and 'to set moving' and the second 'to walk': to set going.
