## You'll want to know: কৃতী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C543-SIDDHI543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**কৃতী** — *kritī* — "accomplished; high-achieving".

> আমাদের গ্রামের এক কৃতী ছাত্রী বৃত্তি পেল।
>
> *Āmāder grāmer ek kritī chhātrī britti pelô.*
>
> An accomplished student from our village won a scholarship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কৃতী** (*kritī*) is Sanskrit *kṛtin*, "one who acts", and so "skilful, successful" (Monier-Williams).
