## You'll want to know: हप्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C540-VIMA540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हप्ता** — *haptā* — "an instalment; a premium".

> विम्याचा हप्ता दर महिन्याला भरावा लागतो.
>
> *vimyācā haptā dar mahinyālā bharāvā lāgto.*
>
> The insurance premium has to be paid every month.

Masculine.

हप्ता भरणे, to pay an instalment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हप्ता** (*haptā*) is a loan from Persian *hafta*, "week": a payment made at a set interval.
