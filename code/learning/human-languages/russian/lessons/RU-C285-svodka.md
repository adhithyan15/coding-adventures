## You'll want to know: сводка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C285-SOBYTIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**сводка** (*svódka*) — "a summary, a bulletin".

> По радио передавали сводку погоды.
>
> *Po rádio peredaváli svódku pogódy.*
>
> The radio gave the weather report.

*Сводка погоды* — the weather report. Genitive plural *сводок*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сводка* is built on *сводить* "to bring together", from *водить* "to lead", inherited from Proto-Slavic *voditi* (EtymDB): facts brought together.
