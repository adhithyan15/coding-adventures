## You'll want to know: دوڑانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C522-PHURTI522-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دوڑانا** — *dauṛānā* — "to make (someone) run".

> استاد نے لڑکوں کو میدان میں دوڑایا۔
>
> *ustād ne laṛkoṅ ko maidān meṅ dauṛāyā.*
>
> The teacher made the boys run round the field.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گھوڑا دوڑایا, *us ne ghoṛā dauṛāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دوڑانا** (*dauṛānā*) is the causative of *dauṛnā*, "to run", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *dravati*, "runs" (CDIAL 6624).
