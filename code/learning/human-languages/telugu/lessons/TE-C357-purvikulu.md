# పూర్వీకులు (pūrvīkulu) — ancestors; forefathers

## You'll want to know: పూర్వీకులు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C357-VAMSAM357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పూర్వీకులు** — *pūrvīkulu* — "ancestors; forefathers".

> మా పూర్వీకులు రైతులు.
>
> *mā pūrvīkulu raitulu.*
>
> Our ancestors were farmers.

Built on పూర్వం, formerly: those who came before.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పూర్వీకులు** (*pūrvīkulu*) is built on Sanskrit *pūrvika*, "former, ancient" (Monier-Williams), from *pūrva*, "former" (CDIAL 8343).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
