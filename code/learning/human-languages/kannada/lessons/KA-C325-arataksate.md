## You'll want to know: ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C325-VIVAHA325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ** — *āratakṣate* — "a wedding reception".

> ಮದುವೆಯ ಹಿಂದಿನ ಸಂಜೆ ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *maduveya hindina sañje āratakṣate ittu.*
>
> The wedding reception was on the evening before the wedding.

The evening when guests meet the couple and bless them with rice grains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ** (*āratakṣate*) is usually traced to ಆರತಿ, the waving of a lamp, and ಅಕ್ಷತೆ, grains of unbroken rice, from Sanskrit *akṣata*, "unbroken" (Monier-Williams).
