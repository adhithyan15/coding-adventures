## You'll want to know: नोंदवणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C315-ANK315-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नोंदवणे** — *nondavṇe* — "to register; to put on record".

> हवालदाराने माझी तक्रार नोंदवली.
>
> *havāldārāne mājhī takrār nondavlī.*
>
> The constable registered my complaint.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नोंदवलं, *tyāne nondavlaṁ*.

तक्रार नोंदवणे, to lodge a complaint; मत नोंदवणे, to put an opinion on record.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नोंदवणे** (*nondavṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *nõdṇẽ*, 'to copy and enter a document', under *\*nibandhati*, 'binds on; writes down' (CDIAL 7223).
