## You'll want to know: спустя
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C272-ZARANEE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**спустя** (*spustyá*) — "later, after (a stretch of time)".

> Спустя два года они снова встретились.
>
> *Spustyá dva góda oní snóva vstrétilis.*
>
> Two years later they met again.

With the accusative of time, before or after it: *спустя неделю* or *неделю спустя* — a week later.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*спустя* is an old adverbial participle of *спустить* "to let down", built on *пустить* "to let go", inherited from Proto-Slavic *pustiti* (EtymDB): time let go by.
