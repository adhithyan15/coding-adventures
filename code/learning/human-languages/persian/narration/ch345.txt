Persian, chapter 345: Once upon a time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قصه (qesse) — a tale, a story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for saffron rice pudding, then the Persian for rosewater.

You'll want to know: قصه.
قصه (qesse) — "a tale, a story".
مادربزرگم هر شب برای ما قصه میگفت. Mâdarbozorgam har shab barâ-ye mâ qesse migoft. Every night my grandmother used to tell us a story.
Close to dâstân, but often a folk tale told aloud. Qesse goftan "to tell a tale".

The word, taken apart.
qesse is borrowed from Arabic qiṣṣa, "story" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
روزی روزگاری (ruzi ruzegâri) — once upon a time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for rosewater, then the Persian for a tale.

You'll want to know: روزی روزگاری.
روزی روزگاری (ruzi ruzegâri) — "once upon a time".
روزی روزگاری (ruzi ruzegâri) در یک روستای کوچک پیرزنی زندگی میکرد. Ruzi ruzegâri dar yek rustâ-ye kuchak pirzani zendegi mikard. Once upon a time an old woman lived in a little village.
Literally "one day, in one time". ruzegâr "time, age". The past continuous sets the scene.

The word, taken apart.
ruzi ruzegâri repeats ruz "day" in ruzi "one day" and ruzegâri "in one age", from ruzegâr "time, age". EtymDB takes ruz back through Middle Persian to Old Persian rauča-.

Lesson 3 of 5.
یکی بود یکی نبود (yeki bud yeki nabud) — there was one and there was none (the opening of a folk tale).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a tale, then the Persian for once upon a time.

You'll want to know: یکی بود یکی نبود.
یکی بود یکی نبود (yeki bud yeki nabud) — "there was one and there was none (the opening of a folk tale)".
قصه (qesse) با یکی بود یکی نبود شروع شد. Qesse bâ yeki bud yeki nabud shoru' shod. The tale began with There was one, there was none.
The traditional opening of a Persian folk tale, often followed by gheyr az khodâ hich-kas nabud "and apart from God there was nobody".

The word, taken apart.
yeki bud yeki nabud is built from native Persian words only: yeki "one", bud "was" and nabud "was not".

Lesson 4 of 5.
قصه گو (qesse gu) — a storyteller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for once upon a time, then the Persian for there was one and there was none.

You'll want to know: قصه گو.
قصه گو (qesse gu) — "a storyteller".
قصه گو (qesse gu) داستان را با صدای بلند تعریف کرد. Qesse gu dâstân râ bâ sedâ-ye boland ta'rif kard. The storyteller told the story in a loud voice.
qesse "tale" + gu, the present stem of goftan "to say".

The word, taken apart.
qesse "tale" is borrowed from Arabic qiṣṣa (EtymDB), and gu- is the present stem of the native verb goftan "to say": a tale-sayer.

Lesson 5 of 5.
راوی (râvi) — a narrator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for there was one and there was none, then the Persian for a storyteller.

You'll want to know: راوی.
راوی (râvi) — "a narrator".
در این رمان راوی یک بچه است. Dar in român râvi yek bachche ast. In this novel the narrator is a child.
Plural râviyân. It also names someone who passes on a traditional report.

The word, taken apart.
râvi is usually traced to Arabic rāwī, "narrator, one who passes on a report"; EtymDB lists no entry.
