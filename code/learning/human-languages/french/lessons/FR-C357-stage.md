## You'll want to know: le stage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C357-STAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le stage** — "the internship, the work placement; a training course".

> Cet été, je fais un stage dans un hôtel.
>
> *This summer, I am doing an internship in a hotel.*

A false friend: not a theatre stage. *Un stage de cuisine* — a cooking course.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stage* comes from Medieval Latin *stagium* "a period of residence" (EtymDB), usually linked to Latin *stāre* "to stand" (Lewis & Short).
