## You'll want to know: la faillite
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C394-FAILLITE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la faillite** — "the bankruptcy".

> Le restaurant a fait faillite après deux ans.
>
> *The restaurant went bankrupt after two years.*

*Faire faillite* — to go bankrupt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*faillite* was borrowed from Italian *fallita* "failure" (EtymDB), from Latin *fallere* "to deceive, to fail" (Lewis & Short).
