## You'll want to know: મનપસંદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C537-PASANDGI537-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મનપસંદ** — *manapsand* — "favourite".

> જો દુકાનમાં મારો મનપસંદ રંગ હશે તો હું તે સાડી ખરીદીશ.
>
> *jo dukānmā̃ māro manapsand rang haśe to hũ te sāḍī kharīdīś.*
>
> If the shop has my favourite colour, I will buy that sari.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મનપસંદ** (*manapsand*) is built from *man*, "the mind", from Sanskrit *manas* (Monier-Williams), and *pasand*, "liked", usually traced to Persian *pasand*: liked by one's own mind.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
