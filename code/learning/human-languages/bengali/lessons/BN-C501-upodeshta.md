## You'll want to know: উপদেষ্টা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C501-BISHESHOGGO501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**উপদেষ্টা** — *upôdeshṭā* — "an adviser".

> নতুন ব্যবসার জন্য আমি একজন উপদেষ্টার পরামর্শ নিলাম।
>
> *Nôtun bæbsār jônyô āmi ekjôn upôdeshṭār pôrāmôrshô nilām.*
>
> I took an adviser's advice for the new business.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপদেষ্টা** (*upôdeshṭā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *upadeṣṭṛ*, "one who teaches, an adviser" (Monier-Williams).
