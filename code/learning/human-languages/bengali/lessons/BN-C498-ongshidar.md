# অংশীদার (ôngshīdār) — a business partner

## You'll want to know: অংশীদার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C498-UDYOG498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**অংশীদার** — *ôngshīdār* — "a business partner".

> আমার বন্ধু এই ব্যবসায় আমার অংশীদার।
>
> *Āmār bôndhu ei bæbsāy āmār ôngshīdār.*
>
> My friend is my partner in this business.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অংশীদার** (*ôngshīdār*) joins Sanskrit *aṃśa*, "a share" (Monier-Williams), to the Persian ending *-dār*, "one who holds": a holder of a share.
