## You'll want to know: کلاچ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C420-FARMAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کلاچ** (*kelâch*) — "a clutch (pedal)".

> کلاچ ماشین برادرم خیلی سفت است.
>
> *Kelâch-e mâshin-e barâdaram kheyli seft ast.*
>
> The clutch in my brother's car is very stiff.

*Kelâch gereftan* — to press the clutch. Automatic cars have no *kelâch*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kelâch* was borrowed from a European language and is usually traced to English *clutch*, the pedal that grips and frees the engine. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
