## You'll want to know: ささげる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C406-TORII406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ささげる** — *sasageru* — "to offer up; to hold up".

> **じんじゃに はなを ささげました。** — *jinja ni hana o sasagemashita* — "We offered flowers at the shrine."

Polite **ささげます**, *sasagemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **かんぬしが はなを ささげました。**, *kannushi ga hana o sasagemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ささげる** (*sasageru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
