## You'll want to know: मर्ज़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C550-CHAYAN550-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मर्ज़ी** — *marzī* — "one's own wish; one's choice".

> छुट्टी के दिन मैं अपनी मर्ज़ी से उठता हूँ।
>
> *Chuṭṭī ke din maiṁ apnī marzī se uṭhtā hūṁ.*
>
> On a day off I get up when I like.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मर्ज़ी** (*marzī*) is usually traced to Arabic *marḍī*, "pleasing, approved", through Persian.
