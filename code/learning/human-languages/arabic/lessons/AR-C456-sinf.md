## You'll want to know: صنف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C456-HAJM456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صنف** — *ṣinf* — "a variety; a kind".

> اشتريت صنفا جديدا من الشاي.
>
> *ishtaraytu ṣinfan jadīdan min ash-shāy.*
>
> I bought a new variety of tea.

Plural: أصناف, *aṣnāf*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صنف** (*ṣinf*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-n-f*, "a sort, a kind" (Lane).
