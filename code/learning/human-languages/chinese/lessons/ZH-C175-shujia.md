## You'll want to know: shūjià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C175-FURN175-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shūjià* — "bookshelf".

> *zhège shūjià hěn hǎokàn* — This bookshelf looks nice.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shūjià* is built from 书 'book' and *jià* 'frame'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *syo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the second is listed there as *kaeH*, glossed as the frame of a house: a frame for books.

## What you've built

One more word for everyday things.
