Kannada, chapter 367: Heroes and Villains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಖಳನಾಯಕ (khaḷanāyaka) — a villain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an oath, then the Kannada for to wail.

You'll want to know: ಖಳನಾಯಕ.
ಖಳನಾಯಕ — khaḷanāyaka — "a villain".
ಸಿನಿಮಾದ ಕೊನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಖಳನಾಯಕ ಸೋತನು. sinimāda koneyalli khaḷanāyaka sōtanu. At the end of the film the villain lost.
Plural: ಖಳನಾಯಕರು, khaḷanāyakaru.
ಖಳ is wicked: the wicked lead of a story or a film.

The word, taken apart.
ಖಳನಾಯಕ (khaḷanāyaka) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of khala, "a mischievous person" (CDIAL 3835), and nāyaka, "a guide, a leader" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವೀರ (vīra) — a hero; a brave man.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to wail, then the Kannada for a villain.

You'll want to know: ವೀರ.
ವೀರ — vīra — "a hero; a brave man".
ಆ ವೀರ ಒಬ್ಬನೇ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯನ್ನು ಕಾಪಾಡಿದನು. ā vīra obbanē haḷḷiyannu kāpāḍidanu. That hero saved the village on his own.
Plural: ವೀರರು, vīraru.
As an adjective: ವೀರ (vīra) ಯೋಧ, a brave warrior. A heroine is a ವೀರೆ or ವೀರ (vīra) ಮಹಿಳೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ವೀರ (vīra) is borrowed from Sanskrit vīra, "a man, a hero" (CDIAL 12056).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಶತ್ರು (śatru) — an enemy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a villain, then the Kannada for a hero.

You'll want to know: ಶತ್ರು.
ಶತ್ರು — śatru — "an enemy".
ವೀರ ತನ್ನ ಶತ್ರುವನ್ನು ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿದನು. vīra tanna śatruvannu kṣamisidanu. The hero forgave his enemy.
Plural: ಶತ್ರುಗಳು, śatrugaḷu.
A friend is a ಮಿತ್ರ; ಶತ್ರು (śatru) is the opposite.

The word, taken apart.
ಶತ್ರು (śatru) is borrowed from Sanskrit śatru, "an enemy" (CDIAL 12286).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನಾಶಮಾಡು (nāśamāḍu) — to destroy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a hero, then the Kannada for an enemy.

You'll want to know: ನಾಶಮಾಡು.
ನಾಶಮಾಡು — nāśamāḍu — "to destroy".
ಖಳನಾಯಕ ಇಡೀ ಊರನ್ನು ನಾಶಮಾಡಲು ಬಯಸಿದನು. khaḷanāyaka iḍī ūrannu nāśamāḍalu bayasidanu. The villain wanted to destroy the whole town.
Past (I …): ನಾಶಮಾಡಿದೆ, nāśamāḍide.
ನಾಶ, ruin, with ಮಾಡು. Something destroyed ನಾಶವಾಯಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ನಾಶಮಾಡು (nāśamāḍu) is built from Sanskrit nāśa, "destruction, ruin" (CDIAL 7084), and native māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು (kaṇmareyāgu) — to disappear; to vanish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an enemy, then the Kannada for to destroy.

You'll want to know: ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು.
ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು — kaṇmareyāgu — "to disappear; to vanish".
ಮಾಂತ್ರಿಕ ಹೊಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾದನು. māntrika hogeyalli kaṇmareyādanu. The sorcerer vanished in the smoke.
Past (it …): ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಯಿತು, kaṇmareyāyitu.
ಕಣ್, eye, ಮರೆ, hidden, and ಆಗು: to become hidden from the eye.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು (kaṇmareyāgu) is a native Dravidian compound: kaṇ, "eye" (DEDR 1159), mare, "hidden, screened" (DEDR 4760), and āgu, "to become" (DEDR 333).
