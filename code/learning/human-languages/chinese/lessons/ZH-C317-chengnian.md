## You'll want to know: chéngnián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C317-LOOKBACK317-02]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngnián* — "to come of age; fully grown".

> *háizi chéngnián yǐhòu, jiù líkāi le jiā* — Once the children were grown, they left home.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngnián* is built from *chéng* 'to complete' and 年 'years', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to finish' and 'to make'.
