## You'll want to know: ਸਾਮਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C277-CHOOSE277-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਮਾਨ** — *sāmān* — "goods; luggage".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਮਾਨ ਬੱਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā sāmān bass vich hai.*
>
> My luggage is on the bus.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਮਾਨ** (*sāmān*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *sāmān*, "goods, things".
