## You'll want to know: દાખલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C346-DAKHLO346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**દાખલો** — *dākhlo* — "an example".

> હું તમને એક દાખલો આપીશ.
>
> *hũ tamne ek dākhlo āpīś.*
>
> Let me give you an example.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાખલો** (*dākhlo*) is usually traced to Persian *dākhila*, "an entry, an admission", a word of Arabic origin; "an example" is a sense Gujarati gave it.
