# اگرچہ (agarche) — even though

## You'll want to know: اگرچہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C359-BAVUJUD359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اگرچہ** — *agarche* — "even though".

> اگرچہ وہ بیمار تھا، پھر بھی کام پر آیا۔
>
> *agarche voh bīmār thā, phir bhī kām par āyā.*
>
> Even though he was ill, he still came to work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اگرچہ** (*agarche*) is usually traced to Persian *agarchi*, "even if", built on **اگر**, "if".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
