## You'll want to know: la testimonianza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C284-INCHIESTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la testimonianza** — "the testimony, the eyewitness account".

> La sua testimonianza è molto importante.
>
> *Her testimony is very important.*

*Rendere testimonianza* — to bear witness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testimonianza* is built on *testimoniare* "to bear witness" with the ending *-anza*; the root is Latin *testimōnium* "evidence", from *testis* "a witness" (Lewis & Short).
