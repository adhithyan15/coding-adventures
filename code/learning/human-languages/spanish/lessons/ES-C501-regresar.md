## You'll want to know: regresar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C501-CAMINO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**regresar** — "to come back, to return".

> Regresamos el domingo.
>
> *We are coming back on Sunday.*

A close twin of *volver*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**regresar** is built on *regreso*, "a return", from Latin *regressus*, "a going back" (EtymDB 2.1), from *regredi*, "to step back".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.
