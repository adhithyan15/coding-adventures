Gujarati, chapter 55: Cook, Wash, Give, Bring, Drink.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રાંધવું (rā̃dhvũ) — to cook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sleep, then the Gujarati for to play.

You'll want to know: રાંધવું.
રાંધવું (rā̃dhvũ) — "to cook".

The word, taken apart.
રાંધવું (rā̃dhvũ) (rāṁdhvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from randhayati, "subjects, torments; prepares or cooks food" (CDIAL 10616).

What you've built.
One more word for cook, wash, give, bring, drink.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: લાવવું.
લાવવું (lāvvũ) — "to bring".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of લાવવું (lāvvũ) is not settled. CDIAL lists a Gujarati lāvvũ only in the sense "to apply" (CDIAL 11004). For Hindi lānā, "to bring", it gives Sanskrit labhate, "takes", crossed with dadāti, "gives" (CDIAL 10948).
