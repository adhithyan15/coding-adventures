## You'll want to know: झेपावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C533-TANDURUSTI533-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झेपावणे** — *jhepāvṇe* — "to leap; to spring".

> चेंडू पकडण्यासाठी तो पुढे झेपावला.
>
> *ceṇḍū pakaḍṇyāsāṭhī to puḍhe jhepāvlā.*
>
> He sprang forward to catch the ball.

Past (he …): तो झेपावला, *to jhepāvlā*.

Built on झेप, a leap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झेपावणे** (*jhepāvṇe*) is built on झेप, "a leap"; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
