## You'll want to know: одинокий
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C477-LONGING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**одинокий** (*odinókiy*) — "lonely".

> Наш сосед — одинокий старик.
>
> *Nash soséd — odinókiy starík.*
>
> Our neighbour is a lonely old man.

Also single, living alone. Short forms: одинок, одинока.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*одинокий* is built on *один* 'one', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *edinъ*, from PIE *óynos 'one' (EtymDB).
