## You'll want to know: えんそく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C499-PARK499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**えんそく** — *ensoku* — "a school outing; an excursion".

> **らいしゅう がっこうの えんそくが あります。** — *raishū gakkō no ensoku ga arimasu* — "Next week there is a school outing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんそく** (*ensoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *en*, "distant" (Middle Chinese *hjwonX*) and *soku*, "leg" (*tsjowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
