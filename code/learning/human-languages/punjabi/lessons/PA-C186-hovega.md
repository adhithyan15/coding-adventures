# ਹੋਵੇਗਾ — it will be or happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HONA, PA-LEX-KAMM-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਹੋਣਾ**, then say **ਕੰਮ**.

## Grammar Lens: the masculine singular future
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HONA] -->

> **ਹੋਵੇਗਾ** — *hovegaa* — it will be or it will happen

This is the masculine singular future form. First keep it whole; the feminine
contrast comes in the next small step.
