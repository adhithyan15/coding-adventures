## You'll want to know: بیکاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C379-ESTEFA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بیکاری** (*bikâri*) — "unemployment".

> بیکاری بین جوانها زیاد است.
>
> *bikâri beyn-e javânhâ ziyâd ast.*
>
> Unemployment is high among young people.

From *bikâr* "unemployed": *bi-* "without" + *kâr* "work" + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بیکاری** is native Persian, built from *bi-* "without" + *kâr* "work" (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian) + the noun suffix *-i*.
