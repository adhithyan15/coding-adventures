## You'll want to know: せいこうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C460-GOAL460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**せいこうする** — *seikō suru* — "to succeed".

> **まいにち れんしゅうして、せいこうしました。** — *mainichi renshū shite, seikō shimashita* — "I practised every day and succeeded."

Polite **せいこうします**, *seikōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **あにが せいこうしました。**, *ani ga seikō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいこうする** (*seikō suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *seikō* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "become" (Middle Chinese *dzyeng*) and *kō*, "success" (*kuwng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
