# Return to a message and a time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SUNEHA-198, PA-TIME-VAJE-01, PA-LEX-C95-NUMBERS95-03] -->

[YOU HEAR: *sunehā*] Say **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ**, "a message". Then *at nine*: **ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ**.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

A message from a friend often says three things: what happened, what did not,
and what will happen when. You can say all three now. Listen for **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ**: the
verb tells you whether it is yesterday or tomorrow.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SUNEHA-198, PA-TIME-VAJE-01, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *a message*] Say **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *at eight*] Say **ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *I went to the market yesterday*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *we will go tomorrow*] Say **ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SUNEHA-198, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ** with **ਗਿਆ**? (Yesterday.) With **ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ**? (Tomorrow.)
