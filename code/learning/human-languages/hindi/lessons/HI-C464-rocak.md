## You'll want to know: रोचक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C464-UBNA464-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रोचक** — *rocak* — "interesting; engaging".

> दादा की कहानियाँ बहुत रोचक होती हैं।
>
> *dādā kī kahāniyā̃ bahut rocak hotī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandfather's stories are always very interesting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोचक** (*rocak*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *rocaka*, "giving an appetite" and "pleasing, agreeable" (Monier-Williams): what gives the mind an appetite.
