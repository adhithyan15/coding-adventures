# ખવડાવવું (khavḍāvvũ) — to feed

## You'll want to know: ખવડાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C488-NAVDAV488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ખવડાવવું** — *khavḍāvvũ* — "to feed".

> દાદી બાળકને ચમચીથી ખીચડી ખવડાવે છે.
>
> *dādī bāḷakne camcīthī khīcḍī khavḍāve che.*
>
> Grandmother feeds the baby khichdi with a spoon.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને ખીચડી ખવડાવી, *teṇe bāḷakne khīcḍī khavḍāvī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખવડાવવું** (*khavḍāvvũ*) is the causative of **ખાવું** (*khāvũ*), "to eat", which goes back to Sanskrit *khādati*, "chews, eats" (CDIAL 3865).
