# ذرا (zarā) — a little

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C270-KITNA-01, UR-LEX-C71-ADJ71-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "how much is the fare?" (*kirāyā kitnā hai?*) Then "expensive". (*mahãgā*.)

## You'll want to know: ذرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C270-ZARA-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C71-ADJ71-04, UR-LEX-ARAM-SE] -->

**ذرا** — *zarā* — "a little".

> **یہ ذرا مہنگا ہے۔**
>
> *yih zarā mahãgā hai.* — "This is a little expensive."

> **ذرا آرام سے۔**
>
> *zarā ārām se.* — "A little more slowly, please."

Before a request it softens it, as "please" does. The next lessons put it to work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذرا** (*zarā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dharra*, "a speck, an atom". Its first letter is new to your hand; the next lesson takes it out.
