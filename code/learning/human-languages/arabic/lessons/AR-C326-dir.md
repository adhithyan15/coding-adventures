## You'll want to know: درع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C326-SILAH326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**درع** — *dirʿ* — "armour; a shield".

> لبس الجندي درعا من الحديد.
>
> *labisa al-jundī dirʿan min al-ḥadīd.*
>
> The soldier wore armour of iron.

Plural: دروع, *durūʿ*.

Today a درع is also a shield-shaped trophy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**درع** (*dirʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-r-ʿ* (Lane), where *dirʿ* is a coat of mail, rings linked over the body.
