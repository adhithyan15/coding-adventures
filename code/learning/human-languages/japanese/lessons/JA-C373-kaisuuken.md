## You'll want to know: かいすうけん

**かいすうけん** — *kaisūken* — "a book of tickets".

> **かいすうけんは ふつうの きっぷより やすいです。** — *kaisūken wa futsū no kippu yori yasui desu* — "A book of tickets is cheaper than ordinary tickets."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいすうけん** (*kaisūken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "round" (Middle Chinese *hwoj*), *sū*, "number" (*srjuH*) and *ken*, "ticket" (*khjwonH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
