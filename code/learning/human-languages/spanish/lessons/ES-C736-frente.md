## You'll want to know: la frente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C736-CARA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la frente** — "the forehead".

> Tienes la frente caliente.
>
> *Your forehead is hot.*

*La frente* is the forehead; *el frente* is the front.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frente* is inherited from Latin *frōns*, *frontem* "forehead, front" (EtymDB), through Old Spanish *fruente*.
