# とれる (toreru) — to come off; to come loose

## You'll want to know: とれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C162-REPAIR162-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とれる** — *toreru* — "to come off; to come loose".

> **この はこの ふたは よく とれます。** — *kono hako no futa wa yoku toremasu* — "The lid of this box often comes off."

Polite **とれます**, *toremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とれる** (*toreru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as *toru*, "to take"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
