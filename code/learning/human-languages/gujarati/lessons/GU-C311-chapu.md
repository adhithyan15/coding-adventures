## You'll want to know: છાપું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C311-SAMACHAR311-02]; assesses=[] -->

**છાપું** — *chāpũ* — "a newspaper".

> મારા દાદા રોજ સવારે છાપું વાંચે છે.
>
> *mārā dādā roj savāre chāpũ vā̃ce che.*
>
> My grandfather reads the newspaper every morning.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છાપું** (*chāpũ*) is built on **છાપવું**, to print, from *\*chapp*, "press, cover", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4994): the printed sheet. **છાપામાં**, in the paper.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
