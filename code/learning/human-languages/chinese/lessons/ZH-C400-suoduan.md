## You'll want to know: suōduǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C400-ADJUST400-03]; assesses=[] -->

*suōduǎn* — "to shorten".

> *zuò fēijī kěyǐ suōduǎn yíbàn de shíjiān* — Flying can cut the journey time by half.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suōduǎn* is built from *suō* 'to draw in' and *duǎn* 'short', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjuwk* and *twanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to shrink' and the second 'not long'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
