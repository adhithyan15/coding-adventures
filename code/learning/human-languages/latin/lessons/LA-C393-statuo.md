## You'll want to know: statuō, statuere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C393-STATUO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**statuō, statuere** — "to set up; to decide".

> Statuī domī manēre.
>
> *I decided to stay at home.*

*statuō, statuere, statuī, statūtum*. With an infinitive, to decide to; with the accusative and infinitive, to judge that. English *statute*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*statuō* is built on *status* "a standing, a position" (Lewis & Short), from the root of *stō* "to stand": to make a thing stand. English *statute* comes from it.
