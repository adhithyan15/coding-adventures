## You'll want to know: rauben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C282-UEBERFALL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rauben** — "to rob, to steal by force".

> Der Mann raubte einer Frau die Tasche.
>
> *The man robbed a woman of her bag.*

*Jemandem etwas rauben*: the person goes in the dative. *Der Raub* — the robbery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rauben* is inherited from Old High German *roubōn*, which EtymDB traces to Proto-Germanic *raubōną* and further to a root meaning "to snatch".
