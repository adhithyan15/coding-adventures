## You'll want to know: il segnalibro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C525-LIBRERIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il segnalibro** — "bookmark".

> Ho perso il segnalibro e non so a che pagina ero.
>
> *I lost my bookmark and do not know what page I was on.*

From *segnare* and *libro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*segnalibro* is built from *segna* "marks", from Latin *signāre* (EtymDB), and *libro* "book", from Latin *liber* (Lewis & Short).
