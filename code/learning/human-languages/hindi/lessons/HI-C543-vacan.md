## You'll want to know: वचन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C543-VACHAN543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वचन** — *vacan* — "a word given; a pledge".

> राजा ने रानी को एक वचन दिया।
>
> *Rājā ne rānī ko ek vacan diyā.*
>
> The king gave the queen his word.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वचन** (*vacan*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *vacana*, "speaking, an utterance, a statement" (Monier-Williams); Hindi uses it for the word one gives.
