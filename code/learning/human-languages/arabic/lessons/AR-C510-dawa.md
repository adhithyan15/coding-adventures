## You'll want to know: داوى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C510-TASHKHIS510-05]; assesses=[] -->

**داوى** — *dāwā* — "to cure".

> كانت جدتي تداوي الناس بالأعشاب.
>
> *kānat jaddatī tudāwī an-nās bi-l-aʿshāb.*
>
> My grandmother used to cure people with herbs.

Present: يداوي, *yudāwī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**داوى** (*dāwā*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-w-y*, "sickness; a cure" (Lane). Dawāʾ, medicine, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
