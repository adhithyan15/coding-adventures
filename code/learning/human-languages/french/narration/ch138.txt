French, chapter 138: To know (a person, a place), To prefer, To hope, To drive, To get dressed.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
connaître — to know (a person, a place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to put; to put on, then the French for to believe.

You'll want to know: connaître.
connaître — "to know (a person, a place)". savoir knows a fact; connaître knows a person or a place.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — recall: say the French for to put; to put on, then the French for to believe, then say connaître again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
