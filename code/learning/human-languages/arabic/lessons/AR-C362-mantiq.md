## You'll want to know: منطق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C362-MANTIQ362-01]; assesses=[] -->

**منطق** — *manṭiq* — "logic".

> لا أفهم منطق هذا القرار.
>
> *lā afhamu manṭiq hādhā al-qarār.*
>
> I do not understand the logic of this decision.

The adjective is منطقي, *manṭiqī*, logical.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منطق** (*manṭiq*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ṭ-q*, "to speak" (Lane). Lane gives *manṭiq* as speech, the putting of ideas into words; later it became the word for logic.
