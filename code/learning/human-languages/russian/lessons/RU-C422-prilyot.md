## You'll want to know: прилёт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C422-VYLET-03]; assesses=[] -->

**прилёт** (*prilyót*) — "an arrival (of a flight)".

> После прилёта мы сразу поехали в гостиницу.
>
> *Pósle prilyóta my srázu poyékhali v gostínitsu.*
>
> After we landed we went straight to the hotel.

Masculine. *Зал прилёта* — the arrivals hall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прилёт* is built from *при-* 'to, towards' and *лёт* 'flight', the root of *летать* 'to fly'. EtymDB lists *взлёт* from the same root, Proto-Slavic *vŭzletŭ*.
