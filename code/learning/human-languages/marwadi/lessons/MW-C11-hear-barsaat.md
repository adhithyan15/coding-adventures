## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barsāt* is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *varṣārātri*, "the rainy season". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11398); the match is through Hindi *barsāt*. CDIAL also allows a blend with *varṣartu*, "rainy season". It is built on *varṣā*, "rain", and *rātri*, "night".
