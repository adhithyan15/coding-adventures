## You'll want to know: خزنة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C443-MULAA443-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خزنة** — *khazna* — "a strongbox; a safe".

> وضعت جوازي في خزنة الغرفة.
>
> *waḍaʿtu jawāzī fī khaznat al-ghurfa.*
>
> I put my passport in the room safe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خزنة** (*khazna*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-z-n*, "to store up" (Lane). It shares its root with *khizāna*, "a cupboard".
