Gujarati, chapter 459: Gentle and Harsh.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કોમળ (komaḷ) — gentle; tender.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to crave, then the Gujarati for to come to mind.

You'll want to know: કોમળ.
કોમળ — komaḷ — "gentle; tender".
નર્સનો અવાજ ઘણો કોમળ હતો. narsano avāj ghaṇo komaḷ hato. The nurse's voice was very gentle.

The word, taken apart.
કોમળ (komaḷ) goes back to Sanskrit kōmala, "tender, soft"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "soft" (CDIAL 3523).

બાળકનાં આંસુ જોઈને પિતાનું હૃદય પીગળી ગયું. bāḷaknā̃ ā̃su joīne pitānũ hṛday pīgḷī gayũ. Seeing the child's tears, the father's heart melted.
Past (it …): તે બરફ પીગળી ગયો, te baraph pīgḷī gayo.

પીગળવું (pīgaḷvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit pragalati, "drips"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to melt, be dissolved" (CDIAL 8468).
