## You'll want to know: договариваться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C378-DOGOVORILIS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**договариваться** (*dogovárivatsya*) — "to arrange, to come to an agreement".

> Мы договорились встретиться у метро.
>
> *My dogovorílis vstrétitsya u metró.*
>
> We agreed to meet by the metro.

Imperfective; perfective *договориться*. *Договариваться с кем о чём*, or + infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**договариваться** is built from *до-* "through to the end" and *говорить* "to speak", on the old Slavic noun *\*govorŭ* "talk" (EtymDB): to talk a matter through.
