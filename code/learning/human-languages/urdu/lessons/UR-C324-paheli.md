## You'll want to know: پہیلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C324-DASTAN324-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پہیلی** — *pahelī* — "a riddle".

> کوئی پہیلی کا جواب نہیں جانتا تھا۔
>
> *koī pahelī kā javāb nahīṅ jāntā thā.*
>
> Nobody knew the answer to the riddle.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پہیلی** (*pahelī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prahēlikā*, "a riddle" (CDIAL 8911).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
