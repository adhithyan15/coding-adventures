## You'll want to know: सँवारना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C394-INAAM394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सँवारना** — *sãvārnā* — "to put in order, to make (something) look its best".

> अगर तुम अपना कमरा सँवारोगे तो मेहमान ख़ुश होंगे।
>
> *agar tum apnā kamrā sãvāroge to mehmān khuś hoṅge.*
>
> If you tidy up your room, the guests will be pleased.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना घर सँवारा, *usne apnā ghar sãvārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सँवारना** (*sãvārnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *saṃvārayati*, "keeps off; puts in order" (CDIAL 13021).
