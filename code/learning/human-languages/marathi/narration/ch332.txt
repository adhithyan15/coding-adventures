Marathi, chapter 332: Courage and Sacrifice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वीर (vīr) — a hero; a warrior.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rampart, then the Marathi for a bastion.

You'll want to know: वीर.
वीर — vīr — "a hero; a warrior".
गावात त्या वीराचं स्मारक आहे. gāvāt tyā vīrācaṁ smārak āhe. There is a memorial to that hero in the village.
Masculine.
As an adjective, brave: वीर (vīr) सैनिक.

The word, taken apart.
वीर (vīr) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vīra, 'a man, a hero' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
शरण जाणे (śaraṇ jāṇe) — to surrender.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for bravery, then the Marathi for a sacrifice.

You'll want to know: शरण जाणे.
शरण जाणे — śaraṇ jāṇe — "to surrender".
शेवटी शत्रू राजाला शरण गेला. śevṭī śatrū rājālā śaraṇ gelā. In the end the enemy surrendered to the king.
Past (he …): तो शरण गेला, to śaraṇ gelā.
The one surrendered to takes -ला: राजाला शरण जाणे (śaraṇ jāṇe).

The word, taken apart.
शरण जाणे (śaraṇ jāṇe) joins śaraṇ, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit śaraṇa, 'shelter, refuge' (Monier-Williams), to jāṇe, 'to go': to go for refuge.
