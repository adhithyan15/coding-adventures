Urdu, chapter 294: Famine and Sickness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قحط (qahat) — a famine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a wave, then the Urdu for a whirlpool.

You'll want to know: قحط.
قحط — qahat — "a famine".
اس سال گاؤں میں قحط پڑا۔ is sāl gāoṅ meṅ qahat paṛā. This year there was a famine in the village.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
قحط (qahat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qaḥṭ, "drought, a failure of rain". A famine is said to fall: قحط پڑا.
