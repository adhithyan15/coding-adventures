## You'll want to know: вредить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C353-USHCHERB-03]; assesses=[] -->

**вредить** (*vredít*) — "to harm, to do damage to".

> Сладкое вредит зубам.
>
> *Sládkoye vredít zubám.*
>
> Sweet things harm your teeth.

Imperfective; present *врежу, вредишь*; perfective *навредить*. It takes the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**вредить** is the verb made from *вред* "harm", which Russian took from Church Slavonic *vrědŭ*, from Proto-Slavic *\*verdŭ* (EtymDB).
