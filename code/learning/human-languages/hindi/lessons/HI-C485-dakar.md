## You'll want to know: डकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C485-CHAKKAR485-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डकार** — *ḍakār* — "a burp; a belch".

> खाना खाकर दादा ने ज़ोर से डकार ली।
>
> *khānā khākar dādā ne zor se ḍakār lī.*
>
> After eating, my grandfather gave a loud burp.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डकार** (*ḍakār*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *ḍakār*, "belch", under *\*ḍakkāra*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5521). *ḍakār lenā* is to burp.
