## You'll want to know: il chiarimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C346-MALINTESO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il chiarimento** — "the clarification, the explanation".

> Ho chiesto un chiarimento al professore.
>
> *I asked the teacher for a clarification.*

*Chiedere un chiarimento* — to ask for a clarification. Between friends it can also be a frank talk that sorts things out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chiarimento* is built on *chiarire* "to clarify" with the noun ending *-mento*; the verb comes from *chiaro*, inherited from Latin *clārus* "bright, clear" (Lewis & Short).
