Telugu, chapter 429: Progress and Setbacks.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
పురోగతి (purōgati) — progress; headway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a flaw, then the Telugu for to amend.

You'll want to know: పురోగతి.
పురోగతి — purōgati — "progress; headway".
ఆ పనిలో మంచి పురోగతి ఉంది. ā panilō mañci purōgati undi. There is good progress in that work.
Formal; పురోగమించు, to advance.

The word, taken apart.
పురోగతి (purōgati) is built from Sanskrit puras, "in front, forward", and gati, "going, motion" (Monier-Williams; CDIAL 4009).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ముందడుగు (mundaḍugu) — a step forward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to amend, then the Telugu for progress.

You'll want to know: ముందడుగు.
ముందడుగు — mundaḍugu — "a step forward".
ఆ చట్టం సమానత్వం వైపు ఒక ముందడుగు. ā caṭṭaṁ samānatvaṁ vaipu oka mundaḍugu. That law is a step forward towards equality.
ముందు, forward, + అడుగు, a step. ముందడుగు (mundaḍugu) వేయు, to take a step forward.

The word, taken apart.
ముందడుగు (mundaḍugu) is native Dravidian, a compound of mundu, "front, before" (DEDR 5020), and aḍugu, "a foot, a step" (DEDR 72).

You'll want to know: వెనుకబాటు.
వెనుకబాటు — venukabāṭu — "backwardness; a setback".
ఆ గ్రామం వెనుకబాటుకు కారణం విద్య లేకపోవడం. ā grāmaṁ venukabāṭuku kāraṇaṁ vidya lēkapōvaḍaṁ. The reason the village has fallen behind is the lack of education.
వెనుకబడిన, backward: వెనుకబడిన ప్రాంతం, a backward region.

The word, taken apart.
వెనుకబాటు (venukabāṭu) is native Dravidian, built on venuka, "the back, the rear" (DEDR 5488); the ending -బాటు, which makes a noun, is of uncertain origin.

Lesson 4 of 6.
మెరుగుపరచు (meruguparacu) — to improve; to better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a step forward, then the Telugu for backwardness.

You'll want to know: మెరుగుపరచు.
మెరుగుపరచు — meruguparacu — "to improve; to better".
ఆమె తన తెలుగును మెరుగుపరిచింది. āme tana telugunu meruguparicindi. She improved her Telugu.
Past (I …): మెరుగుపరిచాను, meruguparicānu.
మెరుగు, polish, betterment, + -పరచు. మెరుగైన, better.

The word, taken apart.
మెరుగుపరచు (meruguparacu) is native Dravidian: merugu, "lustre, polish" (DEDR 5074, with Kannada merugu, "shine", and Telugu meṟacu, "to glitter"), with -పరచు, a native ending that makes a verb (DEDR 3853 lists paṟacu, "to cause to feel", beside paḍu).

Lesson 5 of 6.
పరిణమించు (pariṇamiñcu) — to turn into; to develop into.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for backwardness, then the Telugu for to improve.

You'll want to know: పరిణమించు.
పరిణమించు — pariṇamiñcu — "to turn into; to develop into".
చిన్న గొడవ పెద్ద సమస్యగా పరిణమించింది. cinna goḍava pedda samasyagā pariṇamiñcindi. A small quarrel turned into a big problem.
Past (it …): పరిణమించింది, pariṇamiñcindi.
What it becomes takes -గా; situations do this, so its past is shown as పరిణమించింది, it turned into. The noun పరిణామం, a consequence, you know.

The word, taken apart.
పరిణమించు (pariṇamiñcu) is built on Sanskrit pariṇāma, "change, alteration, transformation" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
