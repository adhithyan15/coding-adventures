Persian, chapter 445: Junctions and roundabouts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تقاطع (taqâto') — a crossroads, an intersection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get lost, then the Persian for a landmark.

You'll want to know: تقاطع.
تقاطع (taqâto') — "a crossroads, an intersection".
تاکسی در تقاطع دوم به راست پیچید. Tâksi dar taqâto'-e dovvom be râst pichid. The taxi turned right at the second crossroads.
More formal than chahârrâh.

The word, taken apart.
taqâto' was borrowed from Arabic taqāṭuʿ, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'cutting across each other'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
فلکه (falake) — a roundabout (US: a traffic circle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a landmark, then the Persian for a crossroads.

You'll want to know: فلکه.
فلکه (falake) — "a roundabout (US: a traffic circle)".
هتل ما کنار فلکه اول است. Hotel-e mâ kenâr-e falake-ye avval ast. Our hotel is next to the first roundabout.
Many big roundabouts are also called meydân.

The word, taken apart.
falake is usually traced to Arabic falak 'the turning sphere of the sky, a circle', with the Persian ending -e. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سه راه (serâh) — a T-junction, a three-way junction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a crossroads, then the Persian for a roundabout.

You'll want to know: سه راه.
سه راه (serâh) — "a T-junction, a three-way junction".
به سه راه رسیدیم و نمیدانستیم کدام طرف برویم. Be serâh residim va nemidânestim kodâm taraf beravim. We reached the junction and didn't know which way to go.
se 'three' + râh 'road'. A crossroads is chahârrâh.

The word, taken apart.
serâh is a native compound of se 'three', usually traced to PIE tréyes, and râh 'road', from Middle Persian rāh (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
دوراهی (dorâhi) — a fork (in the road); a dilemma.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a roundabout, then the Persian for a T-junction.

You'll want to know: دوراهی.
دوراهی (dorâhi) — "a fork (in the road); a dilemma".
در دوراهی راه سمت چپ را گرفتیم. Dar dorâhi râh-e samt-e chap râ gereftim. At the fork we took the road on the left.
Also figurative: sar-e dorâhi budan — to be at a crossroads in life.

The word, taken apart.
dorâhi is a native compound of do 'two', which EtymDB traces to PIE dwóh₁, râh 'road', from Middle Persian rāh (EtymDB), and the ending -i.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عبور کردن ('obur kardan) — to cross, to go across.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a T-junction, then the Persian for a fork.

You'll want to know: عبور کردن.
عبور کردن ('obur kardan) — "to cross, to go across".
بچه ها با دقت از خیابان عبور کردند. Bachche-hâ bâ deqqat az khiyâbân 'obur kardand. The children crossed the street carefully.
More formal than rad shodan. 'Obur mamnu' — no crossing.

The word, taken apart.
'obur kardan is built from 'obur 'crossing', a loan usually traced to Arabic ʿubūr, and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
