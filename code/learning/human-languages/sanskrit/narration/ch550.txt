Sanskrit, chapter 550: Sharing Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वण्टयति (vaṇṭayati) — divides up; shares out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a wish to see, then the Sanskrit for strives for.

You'll want to know: वण्टयति.
वण्टयति — vaṇṭayati — "divides up; shares out".
यदि माता मोदकान् वण्टयति, तर्हि सर्वे एकं लभन्ते। yadi mātā modakān vaṇṭayati, tarhi sarve ekaṃ labhante. If my mother shares out the sweets, everyone gets one.
Past (she …): सा मोदकान् वण्टितवती, sā modakān vaṇṭitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
वण्टयति (vaṇṭayati) comes from the root √vaṇṭ, "to partition, to share, to divide"; its past participle is vaṇṭita (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for strives for, then the Sanskrit for divides up.

You'll want to know: प्रत्येकम्.
प्रत्येकम् — pratyekam — "each one; one by one".
यदि दश फलानि सन्ति, तर्हि प्रत्येकं बालः एकं लभते। yadi daśa phalāni santi, tarhi pratyekaṃ bālaḥ ekaṃ labhate. If there are ten fruits, each child gets one.

The word, taken apart.
प्रत्येकम् (pratyekam) is built from prati, "towards, for each", and eka, "one"; MW gives pratyekam as one by one, singly, for every single one (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
दायः (dāyaḥ) — an inheritance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for divides up, then the Sanskrit for each one.

You'll want to know: दायः.
दायः — dāyaḥ — "an inheritance".
यदि पितुः दायः अस्ति, तर्हि भ्रातरः तं वण्टयन्ति। yadi pituḥ dāyaḥ asti, tarhi bhrātaraḥ taṃ vaṇṭayanti. If there is an inheritance from their father, the brothers share it.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दायः (dāyaḥ) comes from the root √dā, "to give", which also gives dadāti; MW gives dāya as a share, a portion, an inheritance (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
विनियुंक्ते (viniyuṅkte) — puts to use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for each one, then the Sanskrit for an inheritance.

You'll want to know: विनियुंक्ते.
विनियुंक्ते — viniyuṅkte — "puts to use".
यदि सः धनं सम्यक् विनियुंक्ते, तर्हि सः धनिकः भविष्यति। yadi saḥ dhanaṃ samyak viniyuṅkte, tarhi saḥ dhanikaḥ bhaviṣyati. If he puts his money to good use, he will become rich.
Past (he …): सः धनं सम्यक् विनियुक्तवान्, saḥ dhanaṃ samyak viniyuktavān.

The word, taken apart.
विनियुंक्ते (viniyuṅkte) is built on vi, ni and the root √yuj, "to yoke"; MW gives viniyuj as to separate, and to assign, to commit, to appoint to a use (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
वितरणम् (vitaraṇam) — a handing out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an inheritance, then the Sanskrit for puts to use.

You'll want to know: वितरणम्.
वितरणम् — vitaraṇam — "a handing out".
यदि उत्सवे वितरणं भवति, तर्हि बालाः प्रसन्नाः सन्ति। yadi utsave vitaraṇaṃ bhavati, tarhi bālāḥ prasannāḥ santi. If things are handed out at the festival, the children are happy.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
वितरणम् (vitaraṇam) is built on vi and the root √tṝ, "to cross", which also gives vitarati, "distributes"; MW gives vitaraṇa as leading over, and granting, bestowal, a gift (MW).
