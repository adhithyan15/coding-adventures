## You'll want to know: 看到
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C151-KANDAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**看到** — *kàndào* — "to see; to catch sight of".

> **我看到他了** *wǒ kàndào tā le* — I saw him.

> **我没看到** *wǒ méi kàndào* — I didn't spot it.

The next lesson puts the second character in your hand. It is the *dào* you already say for arriving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**看到** (*kàndào*) is built from 看 'to look' and 到 'to arrive; to reach'. 看 is inherited from Middle Chinese *khanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the Guangyun lists beside a level-tone reading glossed 'to look'; 到 is inherited from *tawH*, glossed 'to arrive'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-KANJIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-05] -->

看 is the looking; 到 says the looking reached something.

看见, which you learned long ago as one word, is made the same way: 看, then 见.
