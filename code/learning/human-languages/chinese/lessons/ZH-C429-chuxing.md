## You'll want to know: chūxíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C429-HOME429-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chūxíng* — "to get about; to go out on a journey".

> *xiàyǔ de shíhou chūxíng hěn bù fāngbiàn* — When it rains, getting about is a real nuisance.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chūxíng* is built from 出 'to go out' and *xíng* 'to go', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *haeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
