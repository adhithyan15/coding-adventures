Telugu, chapter 382: Ideas and Points of View.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఆలోచన (ālōcana) — an idea; a thought.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a watchman, then the Telugu for a milkman.

You'll want to know: ఆలోచన.
ఆలోచన — ālōcana — "an idea; a thought".
నాకు ఒక మంచి ఆలోచన వచ్చింది. nāku oka mañci ālōcana vaccindi. I had a good idea.
Plural: ఆలోచనలు, ālōcanalu.
An idea comes to you: నాకు ఆలోచన (ālōcana) వచ్చింది, literally an idea came to me.

The word, taken apart.
ఆలోచన (ālōcana) is borrowed from Sanskrit ālocana, "seeing", and also "considering, reflection" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
దృక్పథం (dṛkpathaṁ) — an outlook; a perspective.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a milkman, then the Telugu for an idea.

You'll want to know: దృక్పథం.
దృక్పథం — dṛkpathaṁ — "an outlook; a perspective".
ప్రతి మనిషికి ఒక దృక్పథం ఉంటుంది. prati maniṣiki oka dṛkpathaṁ uṇṭundi. Every person has an outlook.
A formal word for the way someone looks at life or at a question.

The word, taken apart.
దృక్పథం (dṛkpathaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit dṛkpatha, "the range of sight" (Monier-Williams), a compound of dṛk-, from dṛś "to see", and patha, "a path" (CDIAL 7743).

Lesson 3 of 5.
అభిప్రాయభేదం (abhiprāyabhēdaṁ) — difference of opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an idea, then the Telugu for an outlook.

You'll want to know: అభిప్రాయభేదం.
అభిప్రాయభేదం — abhiprāyabhēdaṁ — "difference of opinion".
మా ఇద్దరి మధ్య చిన్న అభిప్రాయభేదం వచ్చింది. mā iddari madhya cinna abhiprāyabhēdaṁ vaccindi. A small difference of opinion came up between the two of us.
Two words you can take apart: అభిప్రాయం, an opinion, which you know, and భేదం, a difference.

The word, taken apart.
అభిప్రాయభేదం (abhiprāyabhēdaṁ) is built from two Sanskrit words: abhiprāya, "aim, intention", which Telugu uses for an opinion, and bheda, "a splitting, a breaking apart" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
భావించు (bhāviñcu) — to regard as; to consider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an outlook, then the Telugu for difference of opinion.

You'll want to know: భావించు.
భావించు — bhāviñcu — "to regard as; to consider".
నేను ఆ పనిని నా బాధ్యతగా భావించాను. nēnu ā panini nā bādhyatagā bhāviñcānu. I regarded that work as my responsibility.
Past (I …): భావించాను, bhāviñcānu.
The thing takes -ని and what you take it to be takes -గా: దాన్ని బాధ్యతగా భావించు (bhāviñcu).

The word, taken apart.
భావించు (bhāviñcu) is built on Sanskrit bhāvana, "causing to be", and as a noun "imagination" (CDIAL 9476), from the root bhū, "to be", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 5 of 5.
కోణం (kōṇaṁ) — an angle; a point of view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for difference of opinion, then the Telugu for to regard as.

You'll want to know: కోణం.
కోణం — kōṇaṁ — "an angle; a point of view".
మనం ఆ సమస్యను వేరే కోణం నుంచి చూడాలి. manaṁ ā samasyanu vērē kōṇaṁ nuñci cūḍāli. We should view that problem from a different angle.
Plural: కోణాలు, kōṇālu.
In geometry a corner or an angle; in talk, the side from which you see a question.

The word, taken apart.
కోణం (kōṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit koṇa, "a corner, an angle" (Monier-Williams). Like many Sanskrit nouns in -a, it ends in -ం in Telugu.
