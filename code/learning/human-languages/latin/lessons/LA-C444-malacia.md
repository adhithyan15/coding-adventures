## You'll want to know: malacia, malaciae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C444-MALACIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**malacia, malaciae** — "a dead calm (at sea)".

> Malacia est; nāvēs in marī stant et nautae ventum exspectant.
>
> *There is a dead calm; the ships stand still on the sea and the sailors wait for wind.*

First declension, feminine: *malacia, malaciae f.* No wind at all, the sailor's other trouble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*malacia* was borrowed from Greek *malakiā* 'softness, slackness' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *malakos* 'soft': a sea gone slack.
