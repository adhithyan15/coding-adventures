## You'll want to know: وثیقه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C322-MAHKUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وثیقه** (*vasiqe*) — "bail, a security".

> او با وثیقه آزاد شد.
>
> *U bâ vasiqe âzâd shod.*
>
> He was released on bail.

Also a guarantee for a loan. Written with ث.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vasiqe* is usually traced to Arabic *wathīqa*, "a document, a pledge"; EtymDB lists no entry.
