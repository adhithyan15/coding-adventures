## You'll want to know: o ferimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C307-FERIDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o ferimento** — "the injury, the wound".

> O ferimento na cabeça não é grave.
>
> *The head injury is not serious.*

Built on *ferir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferimento* is built on *ferir* "to injure", from Latin *ferīre* "to strike" (EtymDB), with the noun ending *-mento*.
