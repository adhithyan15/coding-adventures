## You'll want to know: die Klausur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C375-KLAUSUR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Klausur** — "the written exam".

> Morgen schreiben wir eine Klausur in Mathe.
>
> *Tomorrow we are sitting a written exam in maths.*

Plural: *die Klausuren*. At school in Austria the written test is *die Schularbeit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Klausur* is a learned borrowing from Latin *clausūra* "a lock, a bar" (Lewis & Short), from *claudere* "to close": you sit the exam shut away from help.
