## You'll want to know: einst
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C264-DAMALS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**einst** — "once, long ago; one day".

> Hier stand einst eine Burg.
>
> *Long ago a castle stood here.*

Literary. It can also look ahead: *einst wirst du es verstehen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*einst* is inherited from Middle High German *einest*, from Old High German *einēst* "once", built on *ein* "one" (EtymDB).
