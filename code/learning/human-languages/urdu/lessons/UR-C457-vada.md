## You'll want to know: وعدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C457-TRUST457-05]; assesses=[] -->

**وعدہ** — *vādā* — "a promise".

> اس نے آنے کا وعدہ کیا۔
>
> *us ne āne kā vādā kiyā.*
>
> She promised to come.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وعدہ** (*vādā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *waʿda*, "a promise".
