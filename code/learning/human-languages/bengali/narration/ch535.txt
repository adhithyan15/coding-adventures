Bengali, chapter 535: Staying Safe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সিটবেল্ট (siṭbelṭ) — a seat belt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for auspicious, then the Bengali for to come true.

You'll want to know: সিটবেল্ট.
সিটবেল্ট — siṭbelṭ — "a seat belt".
গাড়িতে উঠে আমি সিটবেল্ট বাঁধলাম। Gāṛite uṭhe āmi siṭbelṭ bā̃dhlām. I got into the car and fastened my seat belt.

The word, taken apart.
সিটবেল্ট (siṭbelṭ) is borrowed from English seat belt, written as one word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
বিমা (bimā) — insurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to come true, then the Bengali for a seat belt.

You'll want to know: বিমা.
বিমা — bimā — "insurance".
গাড়ির বিমা থাকলে দুর্ঘটনার পরে কোম্পানি খরচ দেবে। Gāṛir bimā thākle durghôṭônār pôre kompāni khôrôch debe. If the car is insured, the company will pay the costs after an accident.

The word, taken apart.
বিমা (bimā) is usually traced to Persian bīma, built on bīm, "fear": cover against what you fear.

You'll want to know: সুরক্ষিত.
সুরক্ষিত — surôkkhit — "well protected; safe".
আমাদের টাকা ব্যাংকে সুরক্ষিত। Āmāder ṭākā bængke surôkkhit. Our money is safe in the bank.

The word, taken apart.
সুরক্ষিত (surôkkhit) is a tatsama, Sanskrit surakṣita, "well protected, carefully guarded" (Monier-Williams).

আগুন লাগলে আমরা অগ্নিনির্বাপক দিয়ে আগুন নেভাব। Āgun lāgle āmrā ôgninirbāpôk diye āgun nebhābô. If a fire breaks out, we'll put it out with the extinguisher.

অগ্নিনির্বাপক (ôgninirbāpôk) joins Sanskrit agni, "fire", and nirvāpaka, "putting out" (Monier-Williams).
