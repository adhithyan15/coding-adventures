## You'll want to know: संशोधयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C529-PARIVARTANA529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संशोधयति** — *saṃśodhayati* — "revises; corrects".

> यदि योजना कठिना अस्ति, तर्हि वयं तां संशोधयामः।
>
> *yadi yojanā kaṭhinā asti, tarhi vayaṃ tāṃ saṃśodhayāmaḥ.*
>
> If the plan is hard, we revise it.

Past (he …): सः योजनां संशोधितवान्, *saḥ yojanāṃ saṃśodhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संशोधयति** (*saṃśodhayati*) is built on *sam*, "thoroughly", and the causative of the root √śudh, "to be pure", which also gives *śodhayati*; MW gives *saṃśodhayati* as to cleanse thoroughly, to clear; "to revise" is its modern use (MW).
