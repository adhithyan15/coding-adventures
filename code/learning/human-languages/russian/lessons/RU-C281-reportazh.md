## You'll want to know: репортаж
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C281-EFIR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**репортаж** (*reportázh*) — "a report (on TV or radio), a news story".

> Вечером мы смотрели репортаж о футболе.
>
> *Vécherom my smotréli reportázh o futbóle.*
>
> In the evening we watched a report about football.

Masculine. *Вести репортаж* — to report live.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*репортаж* was borrowed from French *reportage* (EtymDB), built on *reporter* "to bring back, to report" and the ending *-age*; the verb goes back to Latin *reportāre*, from *portāre* "to carry".
