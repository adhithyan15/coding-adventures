## You'll want to know: پیامد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C370-PAYAMAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پیامد** (*payâmad*) — "a consequence, an outcome".

> این تصمیم پیامدهای مهمی داشت.
>
> *in tasmim payâmadhâ-ye mohemmi dâsht.*
>
> This decision had important consequences.

Native and formal: *pay* "trace, footstep" + *âmad* "came". Plural *payâmadhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیامد** is a native Persian compound built from *pay* "footstep, trace" and *âmad*, the past stem of *âmadan* "to come", which EtymDB traces to PIE *gʷem-* "to step": what comes in the footsteps of an act.
