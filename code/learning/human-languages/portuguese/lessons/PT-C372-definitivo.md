## You'll want to know: definitivo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C372-PLANOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**definitivo** — "final, definitive".

> A data do casamento ainda não é definitiva.
>
> *The wedding date is not final yet.*

*Em definitivo* — for good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*definitivo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēfīnītīvus* "definitive, explanatory" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *dēfīnīre* "to set bounds", on *fīnis* "boundary, end".
