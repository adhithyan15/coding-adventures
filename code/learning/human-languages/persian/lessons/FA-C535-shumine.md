## You'll want to know: شومینه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C535-COSY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شومینه** (*shumine*) — "a fireplace".

> شبهای زمستان کنار شومینه مینشینیم.
>
> *shabhâ-ye zemestân kenâr-e shumine mineshinim.*
>
> On winter nights we sit by the fireplace.

An open fireplace; a stove is *bokhâri*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shumine* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *cheminée*, 'fireplace'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
