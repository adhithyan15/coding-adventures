## You'll want to know: संकटम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C299-GHOSHANA299-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संकटम्** — *saṅkaṭam* — "danger".

> नद्याः समीपे संकटम् अस्ति।
>
> *nadyāḥ samīpe saṅkaṭam asti.*
>
> There is danger near the river.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकटम्** (*saṅkaṭam*) MW thinks it is probably a Prakrit form of *saṃkṛta*, "brought together", built from *sam* and the root √kṛ; its senses run from narrow to dangerous (MW).
