# విచారం (vicāraṁ) — regret, sorrow

## You'll want to know: విచారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C97-MOOD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**విచారం** (*vicāraṁ*) — "regret, sorrow".

**బాధ** is pain; **విచారం** is the sorrow of looking back. **విచారంగా ఉంది** — "I am sorry about
it", "I regret it".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A sorrow about something that has happened.
