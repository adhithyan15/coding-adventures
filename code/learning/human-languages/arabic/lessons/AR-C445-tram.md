## You'll want to know: ترام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C445-TRAM445-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ترام** — *trām* — "a tram".

> ركبنا الترام إلى وسط المدينة.
>
> *rakibnā at-trām ilā wasaṭ al-madīna.*
>
> We took the tram to the city centre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترام** (*trām*) is usually traced to English *tram*.
