## You'll want to know: きょうだい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C243-KIN243-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうだい** — *kyōdai* — "siblings; brothers and sisters".

> **きょうだいが いますか。** — *kyōdai ga imasu ka* — "Do you have any brothers or sisters?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうだい** (*kyōdai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyō*, "elder brother" (Middle Chinese *xjwaeng*) and *dai*, "younger brother" (*dejX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
