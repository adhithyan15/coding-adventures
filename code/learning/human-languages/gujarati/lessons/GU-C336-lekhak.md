## You'll want to know: લેખક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C336-LEKHAK336-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લેખક** — *lekhak* — "an author".

> આ પુસ્તકનો લેખક અમારા ગામનો છે.
>
> *ā pustakno lekhak amārā gāmno che.*
>
> The author of this book is from our village.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લેખક** (*lekhak*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *lekhaka*, "a writer, a scribe" (CDIAL 11102), from the same root as **લખવું**, to write.
