# ছাড়ানো (chhāṛāno) — to overtake; to get past; to peel

## You'll want to know: ছাড়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C391-SONGHORSHO391-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ছাড়ানো** — *chhāṛāno* — "to overtake; to get past; to peel".

> বাসটা একটা ট্রাককে ছাড়িয়ে গেল।
>
> *bāsṭā ekṭā ṭrākke chhāṛiye gelô.*
>
> The bus overtook a lorry.

Past (he/she …): সেই বাস ট্রাকটাকে ছাড়াল, *sei bās ṭrākṭāke chhāṛālô*.

Mostly in the -এ form with যাওয়া: ছাড়িয়ে যাওয়া, to go past. Also to peel: কমলা ছাড়ানো, to peel an orange.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছাড়ানো** (*chhāṛāno*) is the causing form of **ছাড়া**, "to leave, to let go", from Sanskrit *chardayati* (CDIAL 4998): to leave a vehicle behind, or a peel.
