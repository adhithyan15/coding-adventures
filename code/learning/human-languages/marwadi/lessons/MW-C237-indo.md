## You'll want to know: इंडो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C237-FIELD237-04]; assesses=[] -->

**इंडो** — *iṇḍo* — "an egg".

> चिड़ी इंडो देवै है।
>
> *chiṛī iṇḍo devai hai.*
>
> The sparrow lays an egg.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इंडो** (*iṇḍo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aṇḍa*, "an egg"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *ĩḍũ*, "an egg". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 1111).
