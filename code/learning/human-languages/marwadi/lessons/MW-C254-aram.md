## You'll want to know: आराम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C254-VAID254-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आराम** — *ārām* — "rest".

> आज आराम करो।
>
> *āj ārām karo.*
>
> Rest today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आराम** (*ārām*) is usually traced to Persian *ārām*, "rest, ease".
