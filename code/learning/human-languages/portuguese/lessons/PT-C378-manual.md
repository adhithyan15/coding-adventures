## You'll want to know: o manual
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C378-REVER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o manual** — "the textbook; the manual".

> O manual de matemática pesa muito.
>
> *The maths textbook is very heavy.*

In Portugal, *o manual escolar* is the school textbook. As an adjective, by hand: *trabalho manual*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manual* is a learned borrowing from Latin *manuālis* "of the hand, that fills the hand" (EtymDB), built on *manus* "hand": a book you hold.
