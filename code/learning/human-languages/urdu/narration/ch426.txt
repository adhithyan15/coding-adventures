Urdu, chapter 426: Pure and Genuine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خالص (khālis) — unmixed; pure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for loose change, then the Urdu for to hand (to someone).

You'll want to know: خالص.
خالص — khālis — "unmixed; pure".
یہ دودھ خالص ہے۔ yih dūdh khālis hai. This milk is pure.

The word, taken apart.
خالص (khālis) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khāliṣ, "pure, unmixed".

Lesson 2 of 5.
اصلی (aslī) — authentic; real.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to hand (to someone), then the Urdu for unmixed.

You'll want to know: اصلی.
اصلی — aslī — "authentic; real".
یہ اصلی سونا ہے، نقلی نہیں۔ yih aslī sonā hai, naqlī nahīṅ. This is real gold, not fake.

The word, taken apart.
اصلی (aslī) is built on asl, "the origin, the real thing", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic aṣl, "a root", with the ending -ī.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ملاوٹ (milāvaṭ) — adulteration; mixing in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for unmixed, then the Urdu for authentic.

You'll want to know: ملاوٹ.
ملاوٹ — milāvaṭ — "adulteration; mixing in".
اس دودھ میں پانی کی ملاوٹ ہے۔ is dūdh meṅ pānī kī milāvaṭ hai. This milk has been watered down.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ملاوٹ (milāvaṭ) is built on ملانا, "to mix", from Sanskrit milati, "meets" (CDIAL 10133), with the noun ending -āvaṭ: in a shop it means something cheaper mixed in.

Lesson 4 of 5.
رگڑنا (ragaṛnā) — to rub; to scrub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for authentic, then the Urdu for adulteration.

You'll want to know: رگڑنا.
رگڑنا — ragaṛnā — "to rub; to scrub".
سنار نے سونا پتھر پر رگڑا۔ sunār ne sonā patthar par ragaṛā. The goldsmith rubbed the gold on a stone.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے برتن رگڑے, us ne bartan ragaṛe.

The word, taken apart.
رگڑنا (ragaṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ragaṛnā, "to rub", under ragg, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10558).

Lesson 5 of 5.
چمکانا (chamkānā) — to polish; to make (something) shine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for adulteration, then the Urdu for to rub.

You'll want to know: چمکانا.
چمکانا — chamkānā — "to polish; to make (something) shine".
اس نے کپڑے سے انگوٹھی چمکائی۔ us ne kapṛe se angūṭhī chamkāī. She polished the ring with a cloth.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے جوتے چمکائے, us ne jūte chamkāe.

The word, taken apart.
چمکانا (chamkānā) is built on chamaknā, "to shine", which CDIAL lists under camakka, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4676): this is the form for making something shine.
