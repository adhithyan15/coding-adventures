## You'll want to know: मुखपृष्ठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C314-VACHAK314-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मुखपृष्ठ** — *mukhpṛṣṭha* — "a front page; a cover".

> पुस्तकाच्या मुखपृष्ठावर एक जुना किल्ला आहे.
>
> *pustakācyā mukhpṛṣṭhāvar ek junā killā āhe.*
>
> There is an old fort on the book's cover.

Neuter.

Built from मुख, face, and पृष्ठ, a page.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुखपृष्ठ** (*mukhpṛṣṭha*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit *mukha*, 'the face', and *pṛṣṭha*, 'the back, a surface, a page' (Monier-Williams).
