## You'll want to know: شق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C367-QAIDA367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شق** — *shiq* — "a clause (in an agreement)".

> اگر آپ اس شق سے متفق نہیں تو بتائیے۔
>
> *agar āp is shiq se muttafiq nahīṅ to batāiye.*
>
> If you do not agree with this clause, say so.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شق** (*shiq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shiqq*, "a split; one of two halves"; Urdu uses it for a clause of a written agreement or a law, a sense our sources do not trace.
