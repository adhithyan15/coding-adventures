## You'll want to know: testemunhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C390-CULPA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**testemunhar** — "to testify, to bear witness; to witness".

> Ela testemunhou a favor do arguido.
>
> *She testified for the defendant.*

Also to witness an event: *testemunhar um acidente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testemunhar* is built on *testemunha* "witness", from Latin *testimōnium* "evidence, testimony", on *testis* "witness" (Lewis & Short).
