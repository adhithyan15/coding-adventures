## You'll want to know: sēditiō, sēditiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C387-SEDITIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sēditiō, sēditiōnis** — "a rising, a mutiny, sedition".

> In castrīs sēditiō orta est.
>
> *A mutiny broke out in the camp.*

*sēditiō, sēditiōnis* f., *sēd-* apart + *itiō*, a going: a going apart. English *sedition*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sēditiō* is built from *sēd-* "apart" and *itiō* "a going", from *eō* "to go" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): people going off on their own. English *sedition* comes from it.
