## Script — 门 with 口 inside
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-WEN-ASK-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 问

**Two pieces: the door first, then the mouth inside it.**

**门, three strokes**: the dot, the left post, the frame that turns down and hooks.

**口, three strokes**, small and inside: the left side, the top that turns down, the bottom that closes it.

3 + 3 = six, and both pieces are already yours.
