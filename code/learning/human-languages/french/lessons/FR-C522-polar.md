## You'll want to know: le polar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C522-GENRES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le polar** — "crime novel, detective story (informal)".

> En été, je lis surtout des polars.
>
> *In summer I mostly read crime novels.*

Informal for *le roman policier*. Also a crime film.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le polar* is a slang shortening of *(roman) policier* "detective (novel)" with the familiar ending *-ar* (EtymDB).
