Bengali, chapter 532: What Lies Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নিয়তি (niyôti) — destiny; fate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an outline, then the Bengali for to leave out.

You'll want to know: নিয়তি.
নিয়তি — niyôti — "destiny; fate".
দাদু বলেন, নিয়তি কেউ বদলাতে পারে না। Dādu bôlen, niyôti keu bôdlāte pāre nā. My grandfather says no one can change destiny.

The word, taken apart.
নিয়তি (niyôti) is a tatsama, Sanskrit niyati, "the fixed order of things, destiny" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
আঁচ করা (ā̃ch kôrā) — to sense; to guess (what is coming).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for destiny, then the Bengali for what is bound to happen.

You'll want to know: আঁচ করা.
আঁচ করা — ā̃ch kôrā — "to sense; to guess (what is coming)".
আকাশ দেখে আমি ঝড়ের আঁচ করলাম। Ākāsh dekhe āmi jhôṛer ā̃ch kôrlām. Looking at the sky, I sensed a storm coming.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বিপদটা আঁচ করলেন, tini bipôdṭā ā̃ch kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
আঁচ করা (ā̃ch kôrā) joins আঁচ, "an inkling, a rough guess", to করা, "to do"; CDIAL lists Bengali ā̃cā, "to estimate", under Sanskrit añcati, "bends" (CDIAL 166).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for what is bound to happen, then the Bengali for to sense trouble coming.

You'll want to know: পূর্বাভাস.
পূর্বাভাস — pūrbābhās — "a forecast".
পূর্বাভাস (pūrbābhās) ভালো হলে আমরা কাল নৌকায় নদী পার হব। Pūrbābhās bhālo hôle āmrā kāl noukāy nôdī pār hôbô. If the forecast is good, we'll cross the river by boat tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
পূর্বাভাস (pūrbābhās) joins Sanskrit pūrva, "before, earlier", and ābhāsa, "light, appearance" (Monier-Williams): a first glimpse of what is coming.
