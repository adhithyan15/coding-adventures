## You'll want to know: सबब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C393-SABAB393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सबब** — *sabab* — "an excuse".

> उशिरा येण्याची त्याची सबब कोणालाच पटली नाही.
>
> *uśirā yeṇyācī tyācī sabab koṇālāc paṭlī nāhī.*
>
> Nobody believed his excuse for coming late.

Feminine.

Feminine: सबब सांगणे, to make an excuse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सबब** (*sabab*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic *sabab*, "cause, reason".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
