# ಮಂಡಿಸು (maṇḍisu) — to present

## You'll want to know: ಮಂಡಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C409-PRABANDHA409-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಂಡಿಸು** — *maṇḍisu* — "to present".

> ನಾನು ಸಮ್ಮೇಳನದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಬಂಧ ಮಂಡಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *nānu sammēḷanadalli ondu prabandha maṇḍiside.*
>
> I presented a paper at the conference.

Past (I …): ಮಂಡಿಸಿದೆ, *maṇḍiside*.

For a paper, a case or a bill. ಮಂಡನೆ, a presentation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಂಡಿಸು** (*maṇḍisu*) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled: neither DEDR nor CDIAL lists it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
