Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to repeat, then the Malayalam for to stroke.

You'll want to know: ഒതുക്കുക.
ഒതുക്കുക — otukkuka — "to tidy away, to put in order".

The word, taken apart.
ഒതുക്കുക (otukkuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 973 lists it, "to subdue, settle", with Tamil otukku, "to put on one side, settle".

You'll want to know: പൊളിക്കുക.

The word, taken apart.
പൊളിക്കുക (poḷikkuka) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil poḷi and Kannada poḷḷu (DEDR 4560). Its first sense is "to break open, unroof".

Lesson 4 of 5.
കായ്ക്കുക (kāykkuka) — to bear fruit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to tidy away, then the Malayalam for to pull down.

You'll want to know: കായ്ക്കുക.
കായ്ക്കുക — kāykkuka — "to bear fruit".

The word, taken apart.
കായ്ക്കുക (kāykkuka) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kāy, Kannada kāy and Telugu kācu (DEDR 1459). It is the verb of kāy, "unripe fruit". DEDR 1459 compares Indo-Aryan kacca, "raw, unripe" (CDIAL 2613).
