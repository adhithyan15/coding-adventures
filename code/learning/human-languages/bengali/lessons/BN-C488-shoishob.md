## You'll want to know: শৈশব
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C488-SHOISHOB488-01]; assesses=[] -->

**শৈশব** — *shoishôb* — "childhood".

> দাদুর শৈশব গ্রামে কাটল।
>
> *dādur shoishôb grāme kāṭlô.*
>
> Grandfather spent his childhood in the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শৈশব** (*shoishôb*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śaiśava*, "childhood" (Monier-Williams), from *śiśu*, the শিশু (taught) of Bengali.
