# आटोक्यात आणणे (āṭokyāt āṇṇe) — to bring under control

## You'll want to know: आटोक्यात आणणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C547-ANIBANI547-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आटोक्यात आणणे** — *āṭokyāt āṇṇe* — "to bring under control".

> सुरक्षारक्षकाने अग्निशामक वापरून आग आटोक्यात आणली.
>
> *surakṣārakṣakāne agniśāmak vāprūn āg āṭokyāt āṇlī.*
>
> The security guard used the extinguisher and brought the fire under control.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आग आटोक्यात आणली, *tyāne āg āṭokyāt āṇlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आटोक्यात आणणे** (*āṭokyāt āṇṇe*) is आटोका, "reach, control", in the locative, with आणणे, "to bring"; the origin of आटोका is not settled.
