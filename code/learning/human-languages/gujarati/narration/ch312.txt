Gujarati, chapter 312: The Reporter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પત્રકાર (patrakār) — a journalist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a radio, then the Gujarati for to inform.

You'll want to know: પત્રકાર.
પત્રકાર — patrakār — "a journalist".
એક પત્રકારે મીરાને ઘણા પ્રશ્ન પૂછ્યા. ek patrakāre Mīrāne ghaṇā praśna pūchyā. A journalist asked Mira a lot of questions.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
પત્રકાર (patrakār) is built from પત્ર, a letter or a printed sheet, from Sanskrit patra, "a leaf", and -kār, "a maker" (Monier-Williams): the one who makes the paper. It is used of a man or a woman.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઘટના (ghaṭnā) — an incident; an event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to inform, then the Gujarati for a journalist.

You'll want to know: ઘટના.
ઘટના — ghaṭnā — "an incident; an event".
પત્રકારે આખી ઘટના લખી. patrakāre ākhī ghaṭnā lakhī. The journalist wrote down the whole incident.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઘટના (ghaṭnā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit ghaṭanā, "taking effect, a coming together" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is something that happens, the word of reports: આખી ઘટના, the whole incident.

Lesson 3 of 5.
કિસ્સો (kisso) — a case (to tell); an incident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a journalist, then the Gujarati for an incident.

You'll want to know: કિસ્સો.
કિસ્સો — kisso — "a case (to tell); an incident".
પત્રકારે છાપામાં આ કિસ્સો લખ્યો. patrakāre chāpāmā̃ ā kisso lakhyo. The journalist wrote this story up in the paper.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કિસ્સો (kisso) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qiṣṣa, "a story". It is an incident told as a story: એક કિસ્સો, a case, something that happened to someone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
અહેવાલ (ahevāl) — a report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an incident, then the Gujarati for a case (to tell).

You'll want to know: અહેવાલ.
અહેવાલ — ahevāl — "a report".
બીજા દિવસે છાપામાં તેનો અહેવાલ આવ્યો. bījā divse chāpāmā̃ teno ahevāl āvyo. The next day his report appeared in the paper.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અહેવાલ (ahevāl) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic aḥwāl, "states, conditions", the plural of the word behind હાલત: an account of how things stand.

Lesson 5 of 5.
વર્ણવવું (varṇavvũ) — to describe.

You'll want to know: વર્ણવવું.
વર્ણવવું — varṇavvũ — "to describe".
મીરાએ પત્રકારને બધું વર્ણવ્યું. Mīrāe patrakārne badhũ varṇavyũ. Mira described everything to the journalist.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધું વર્ણવ્યું, teṇe badhũ varṇavyũ.

The word, taken apart.
વર્ણવવું (varṇavvũ) is built on Sanskrit varṇana, "description" (CDIAL 11340), made into a Gujarati verb: to tell what something was like.
