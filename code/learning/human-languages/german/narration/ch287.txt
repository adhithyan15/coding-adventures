German, chapter 287: Das Gefängnis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
gestehen — to confess, to admit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for guilty, then the German for innocent.

You'll want to know: gestehen.
gestehen — "to confess, to admit".
Nach drei Stunden gestand er alles. After three hours he confessed everything.
Strong verb: gestand, hat gestanden.

The word, taken apart.
gestehen is built on stehen "to stand", from Old High German stān, Proto-Germanic stāną (EtymDB), with ge-: to stand by what one did.

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Gefängnis — the prison.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to confess, then the German for the confession.

You'll want to know: das Gefängnis.
das Gefängnis — "the prison".
Er musste zwei Jahre ins Gefängnis. He had to go to prison for two years.
Plural: die Gefängnisse. Im Gefängnis sitzen — to be in prison.

The word, taken apart.
Gefängnis is built on fangen "to catch", from Old High German fāhan, Proto-Germanic fanhaną (EtymDB): the place where the caught are kept.

Lesson 4 of 5.
verschwinden — to disappear, to vanish.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: verschwinden.
verschwinden — "to disappear, to vanish".
Der Dieb verschwand im Wald. The thief disappeared into the forest.
Strong, with sein: verschwand, ist verschwunden.

The word, taken apart.
verschwinden is inherited from Middle High German verswinden, Old High German farswindan (EtymDB), built on an old verb swindan "to fade".

Lesson 5 of 5.
fliehen — to flee, to escape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the prison, then the German for to disappear.

You'll want to know: fliehen.
fliehen — "to flee, to escape".
Der Täter floh mit dem Auto. The offender fled by car.
Strong, with sein: floh, ist geflohen. Vor etwas fliehen — to flee from something.

The word, taken apart.
fliehen is an inherited Germanic verb, usually traced to Proto-Germanic fleuhaną; EtymDB lists English flee and Dutch vlieden as its cousins.
