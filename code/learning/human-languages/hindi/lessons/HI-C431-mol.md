## You'll want to know: मोल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C431-SAUDA431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मोल** — *mol* — "worth; a price (old-fashioned)".

> माँ के प्यार का कोई मोल नहीं होता।
>
> *māṁ ke pyār kā koī mol nahīṃ hotā.*
>
> A mother's love is beyond price.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोल** (*mol*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *maulya*, "a price" (CDIAL 10373), which lists Hindi *mol*. It survives in sayings and in मोलभाव, bargaining.
