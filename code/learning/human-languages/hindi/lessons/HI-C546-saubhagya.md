## You'll want to know: सौभाग्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C546-TAQDIR546-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सौभाग्य** — *saubhāgya* — "good fortune".

> आपसे मिलना मेरा सौभाग्य है।
>
> *Āpse milnā merā saubhāgya hai.*
>
> It is my good fortune to meet you.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौभाग्य** (*saubhāgya*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *saubhāgya*, "welfare, good fortune" (Monier-Williams).
