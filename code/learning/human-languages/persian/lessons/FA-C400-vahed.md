## You'll want to know: واحد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C400-DANESHKADE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**واحد** (*vâhed*) — "a course credit; a unit".

> این ترم بیست واحد گرفتم.
>
> *in term bist vâhed gereftam.*
>
> This term I took twenty credits.

Also a unit of measure, and a flat in a building. Its first meaning is "one".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**واحد** is usually traced to Arabic *wāḥid* "one": a unit is one of something.
