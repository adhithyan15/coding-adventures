# ఆధారపడు (ādhārapaḍu) — to depend, to rely

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C219-KARANAM219-02, TE-LEX-C158-KRIYA158-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-REAL-CONDITION-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "a support, a basis" (*ādhāraṁ*) and "to hurry" (*tondarapaḍu*). Then "if
it rains tomorrow, I will not come". (*rēpu varṣaṁ paḍitē nēnu rānu*.)

## You'll want to know: ఆధారపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C325-ADHARAPADU-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-GRANDMOTHER, TE-LEX-C12-KUTUMBAM-01, TE-LEX-C320-URU-01] -->

**ఆధారపడు** — *ādhārapaḍu* — "to depend (on), to rely (on)".

> **అమ్మమ్మ నాన్న మీద ఆధారపడుతుంది.**
>
> *ammamma nānna mīda ādhārapaḍutundi.* — "Grandmother depends on Father."

> **మా ఊరు వర్షం మీద ఆధారపడుతుంది.**
>
> *mā ūru varṣaṁ mīda ādhārapaḍutundi.* — "Our town depends on the rain."

What you depend on takes **మీద** (*mīda*, "on"). The past, "I depended", is **ఆధారపడ్డాను** (*ādhārapaḍḍānu*): **-పడు** makes its past
**పడ్డాను**, as **తొందరపడు** makes **తొందరపడ్డాను**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆధారపడు** (*ādhārapaḍu*) is two pieces. **ఆధారం**, "a support, a basis", which you know, is borrowed from Sanskrit
*ādhāra* (CDIAL 1165). **-పడు** is the native verb that DEDR 3853 lists as "to feel, to suffer", and that makes
verbs of nouns, as in **తొందరపడు** and **సిగ్గుపడు**. So: "to come to rest on a support".
