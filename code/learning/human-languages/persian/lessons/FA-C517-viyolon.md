## You'll want to know: ویولن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C517-INSTRUMENTS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ویولن** (*viyolon*) — "a violin".

> او در کنسرت ویولن زد.
>
> *u dar konsert viyolon zad.*
>
> She played the violin at the concert.

*viyolon zadan* — to play the violin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viyolon* was borrowed from French *violon*, 'violin' (EtymDB).
