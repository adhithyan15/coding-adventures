# Return to three familiar futures

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04] -->

[YOU HEAR: *hovegā; āvegā*] Say **ਹੋਵੇਗਾ · ਆਵੇਗਾ**, "will be; will come". Then the
farewell **ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ**, "we will meet again".

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-AANGE-04, PA-FORM-HOVEGI-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186] -->

All three end in **-ਗਾ** or **-ਗੇ**, the future. **ਹੋਵੇਗੀ** and **ਆਵੇਗੀ** have
**-ਗੀ** for a feminine subject. **ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ** is "we will meet". The next lesson
gives you *I will go*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186, PA-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-AANGE-04] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *Aman will come*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਆਵੇਗਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *the train will come*] Say **ਰੇਲ ਆਵੇਗੀ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *we will meet tomorrow*] Say **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-AANGE-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *will come*, then *we will meet*.
