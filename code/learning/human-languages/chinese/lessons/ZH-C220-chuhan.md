## You'll want to know: chūhàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C220-FIT220-03]; assesses=[] -->

*chūhàn* — "to sweat".

> *tiān tài rè le wǒ chūhàn le* — It's so hot that I'm sweating.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chūhàn* is built from 出 'to come out' and *hàn* 'sweat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *hanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'hot sweat'.
