Punjabi, chapter 248: At the Door.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਡਿਉਢੀ (ḍiuḍhī) — a covered entrance; a threshold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a low stool, then the Punjabi for a trunk.

You'll want to know: ਡਿਉਢੀ.
ਡਿਉਢੀ — ḍiuḍhī — "a covered entrance; a threshold".
ਅਸੀਂ ਜੁੱਤੀਆਂ ਡਿਉਢੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੱਖਦੇ ਹਾਂ। asīṁ juttīāṁ ḍiuḍhī vich rakkhde hāṁ. We keep our shoes in the covered entrance.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਡਿਉਢੀ (ḍiuḍhī) is inherited from a reconstructed dēhuḍī, "mound, threshold"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍeuḍhī, "threshold" (CDIAL 6565).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a covered entrance, then the Punjabi for a door frame.

You'll want to know: ਅਰਲ.
ਅਰਲ — aral — "a door bolt".
ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ੇ ਦੀ ਅਰਲ ਬੰਦ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ। rāt nū̃ darvāze dī aral band hundī hai. At night the door bolt is shut.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਖੂੰਟੀ (khūṇṭī) — a wall peg.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a door bolt, then the Punjabi for a chain.

You'll want to know: ਖੂੰਟੀ.
ਖੂੰਟੀ — khūṇṭī — "a wall peg".
ਮੇਰਾ ਬਸਤਾ ਖੂੰਟੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ। merā bastā khūṇṭī utte hai. My school bag is on the peg.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੂੰਟੀ (khūṇṭī) goes back to a reconstructed khuṭṭa, "peg, post", as a word taken from Hindi: CDIAL lists Punjabi khū̃ṭī, "stake, peg", as borrowed from Hindi khū̃ṭī, "small peg" (CDIAL 3893).
