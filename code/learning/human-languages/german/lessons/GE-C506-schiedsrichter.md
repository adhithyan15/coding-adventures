## You'll want to know: der Schiedsrichter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C506-FUSSBALL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schiedsrichter** — "referee".

> Der Schiedsrichter zeigte dem Spieler die gelbe Karte.
>
> *The referee gave the player a yellow card.*

Plural: *die Schiedsrichter*. In speech also *der Schiri*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schiedsrichter* is built on *Schied* "decision", from *scheiden* "to separate, to decide", and *Richter* "judge" (EtymDB lists the German words).
