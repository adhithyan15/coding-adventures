## You'll want to know: гарантия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C456-OBMENNIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**гарантия** (*garántiya*) — "a warranty, a guarantee".

> На этот телефон гарантия два года.
>
> *Na étot telefón garántiya dva góda.*
>
> This phone has a two-year warranty.

Feminine. *По гарантии* — under warranty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гарантия* is usually traced to French *garantie* 'a guarantee' (EtymDB lists the French word), from Old French *garantir* 'to vouch for', a word of Germanic origin.
