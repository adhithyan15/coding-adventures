## You'll want to know: কয়েদি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C284-THANA284-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কয়েদি** — *kôyedi* — "a prisoner".

> গতকাল রাতে জেল থেকে কয়েদিরা পালাল।
>
> *gôtôkāl rāte jel theke kôyedirā pālālô.*
>
> Last night prisoners escaped from the jail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কয়েদি** (*kôyedi*) is usually traced through Persian to Arabic *qaidī*, one held in fetters, from *qaid*, a fetter.
