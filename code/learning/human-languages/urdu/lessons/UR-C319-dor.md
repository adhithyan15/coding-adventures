## You'll want to know: ڈور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C319-BASANT319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈور** — *ḍor* — "a kite string; a thread".

> میرے پاس لمبی ڈور تھی۔
>
> *mere pās lambī ḍor thī.*
>
> I had a long kite string.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈور** (*ḍor*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍor*, "thread, string", under Sanskrit *davara* (CDIAL 6225). It is feminine.
