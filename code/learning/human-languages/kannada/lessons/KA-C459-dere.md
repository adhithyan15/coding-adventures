## You'll want to know: ಡೇರೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C459-CARANA459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಡೇರೆ** — *ḍēre* — "a tent".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ನಾವು ಶಿಖರದ ಕೆಳಗೆ ಡೇರೆ ಹಾಕಿದೆವು.
>
> *rātri nāvu śikharada keḷage ḍēre hākidevu.*
>
> At night we pitched a tent below the summit.

ಡೇರೆ ಹಾಕು, to pitch a tent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಡೇರೆ** (*ḍēre*) is usually traced to Persian *dēra*, "a tent, a camp", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.
