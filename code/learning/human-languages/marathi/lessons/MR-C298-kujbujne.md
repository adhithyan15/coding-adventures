# कुजबुजणे (kujbujṇe) — to whisper

## You'll want to know: कुजबुजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C298-GONDHAL298-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कुजबुजणे** — *kujbujṇe* — "to whisper".

> वर्गात दोन मुली हळू आवाजात कुजबुजत होत्या.
>
> *vargāt don mulī haḷū āvājāt kujbujat hotyā.*
>
> Two girls were whispering softly in class.

Past (he …): तो कुजबुजला, *to kujbujlā*.

The listener takes -शी: मित्राशी कुजबुजणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुजबुजणे** (*kujbujṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled sound, *kuj-buj*; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
