Bengali, chapter 391: Collisions and Close Calls.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
সংঘর্ষ (sôngghôrshô) — a collision; a clash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for tar, then the Bengali for to roll.

You'll want to know: সংঘর্ষ.
সংঘর্ষ — sôngghôrshô — "a collision; a clash".
মোড়ে একটা বাস আর একটা ট্রাকের সংঘর্ষ হল। moṛe ekṭā bās ār ekṭā ṭrāker sôngghôrshô hôlô. A bus and a lorry collided at the crossroads.
A word of the news: the paper reports a সংঘর্ষ (sôngghôrshô) where a witness says the two vehicles hit each other.

The word, taken apart.
সংঘর্ষ (sôngghôrshô) is Sanskrit saṃgharṣa, "rubbing together, friction, rivalry" (Monier-Williams); the sense of two vehicles meeting is the modern use.

Lesson 2 of 6.
টলা (ṭôlā) — to totter; to sway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to roll, then the Bengali for a collision.

You'll want to know: টলা.
টলা — ṭôlā — "to totter; to sway".
ভিড়ের ধাক্কায় বুড়ো লোকটা টলে উঠল। bhiṛer dhākkāy buṛo lokṭā ṭôle uṭhlô. Jostled by the crowd, the old man tottered.
Past (he/she …): তিনি টললেন, tini ṭôllen.
Not তোলা, to lift: ট and ত are different letters.

The word, taken apart.
টলা (ṭôlā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali ṭalan, "staggering, slipping", under Sanskrit ṭálati, "moves aside, is disturbed" (CDIAL 5450).

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a collision, then the Bengali for to totter.

You'll want to know: নড়া.
নড়া — nôṛā — "to budge; to stir".
যানজটে বাসটা এক ঘণ্টা নড়ল না। jānjôṭe bāsṭā ek ghônṭā nôṛlô nā. In the traffic jam the bus did not budge for an hour.
Past (he/she …): সেই গাড়ি নড়ল, sei gāṛi nôṛlô.
নাড়ানো, to stir or to wave, is the causing partner.

The word, taken apart.
নড়া (nôṛā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali naṛā, "to shake, to flinch, to move", under the reconstructed naṭati (CDIAL 6934).

Lesson 4 of 6.
ছাড়ানো (chhāṛāno) — to overtake; to get past; to peel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to totter, then the Bengali for to budge.

You'll want to know: ছাড়ানো.
ছাড়ানো — chhāṛāno — "to overtake; to get past; to peel".
বাসটা একটা ট্রাককে ছাড়িয়ে গেল। bāsṭā ekṭā ṭrākke chhāṛiye gelô. The bus overtook a lorry.
Past (he/she …): সেই বাস ট্রাকটাকে ছাড়াল, sei bās ṭrākṭāke chhāṛālô.
Mostly in the -এ form with যাওয়া: ছাড়িয়ে যাওয়া, to go past. Also to peel: কমলা ছাড়ানো (chhāṛāno), to peel an orange.

The word, taken apart.
ছাড়ানো (chhāṛāno) is the causing form of ছাড়া, "to leave, to let go", from Sanskrit chardayati (CDIAL 4998): to leave a vehicle behind, or a peel.

Lesson 5 of 6.
কষা (kôshā) — to apply (the brakes); to tighten; to work out (a sum).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to budge, then the Bengali for to overtake.

You'll want to know: কষা.
কষা — kôshā — "to apply (the brakes); to tighten; to work out (a sum)".
রাস্তায় গরু দেখে চালক হঠাৎ ব্রেক কষলেন। rāstāy gôru dekhe chālôk hôṭhāt brek kôshlen. Seeing a cow on the road, the driver suddenly slammed on the brakes.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ব্রেক কষলেন, tini brek kôshlen.
Also to pull a knot tight or to work out a sum on paper: a verb of pressing and rubbing hard.

The word, taken apart.
কষা (kôshā) probably goes back to Sanskrit kaṣ, "to rub, to scratch, to scrape" (Monier-Williams): brakes, knots and sums are all worked by rubbing hard.
