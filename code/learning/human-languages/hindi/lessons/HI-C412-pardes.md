## You'll want to know: परदेस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C412-VIDESH412-02]; assesses=[] -->

**परदेस** — *pardes* — "a land far from home".

> परदेस में अपने घर की बहुत याद आती है।
>
> *pardes meṁ apne ghar kī bahut yād ātī hai.*
>
> Far from home, you miss your own house a lot.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परदेस** (*pardes*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *paradeśa*, "another or a foreign country" (Monier-Williams). It may be another state of India: anywhere that is not home.
