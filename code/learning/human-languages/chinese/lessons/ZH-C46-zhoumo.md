## You'll want to know: zhōumò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C46-WEEK46-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōumò* — "weekend".

> *zhōumò wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū* — At the weekend I like to read.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōumò* is built from zhōu 'week' and mò 'end'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *mat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the tip of a tree' and 'end'. The first character is recorded there as *tsyuw*, 'a full circuit'; the sense week is not given, so how it came to name the week is not settled.
