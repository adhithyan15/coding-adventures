## You'll want to know: negar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C340-DUVIDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**negar** — "to deny; to refuse".

> Ele nega tudo, mas toda a gente viu.
>
> *He denies everything, but everyone saw.*

*Negar que* takes the subjunctive. Also to refuse: *negar ajuda*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*negar* is inherited from Latin *negāre* "to say no, to deny" (Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese *negar* (EtymDB).
