## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-NOT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-NOW-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-SCENE-EVENT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-TOKHON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-SAME-DOER-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FIRST-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FINALLY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] New forms: **দিদিমার**, "Grandmother's", with the **-র** of **আমার**; **শুলাম** (*shulām*), **শোয়া** in the past, "I lay down to sleep"; **দিনটা আমার ভালো লাগল**, "the day felt good to me".

[PAUSE 2s] The scenes take **-ছিল-**; the events take the plain past, joined with *-e* forms and kept in order by **প্রথমে**, **তারপর** and **শেষে**. **হঠাৎ** turns the story; **ফিরব** is the plan.
