Lesson 1 of 5.
ઉદ્ઘાટન (udghāṭan) — an inauguration; a formal opening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a speech, then the Gujarati for to resound.

You'll want to know: ઉદ્ઘાટન.
ઉદ્ઘાટન — udghāṭan — "an inauguration; a formal opening".
આજે નવા પુલનું ઉદ્ઘાટન થયું. āje navā pulnũ udghāṭan thayũ. The new bridge was opened today.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉદ્ઘાટન (udghāṭan) is a tatsama, Sanskrit udghāṭana, "opening, unlocking" (Monier-Williams): the formal opening of a bridge, a road or a school.

Lesson 2 of 5.
પ્રસંગ (prasang) — an occasion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to resound, then the Gujarati for an inauguration.

You'll want to know: પ્રસંગ.
પ્રસંગ — prasang — "an occasion".
આ પ્રસંગે ગામના બધા લોકો ભેગા થયા. ā prasange gāmnā badhā loko bhegā thayā. Everyone in the village gathered for the occasion.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પ્રસંગ (prasang) is a tatsama, Sanskrit prasaṅga, "connection", and also "an event, a case" (Monier-Williams). In Gujarati it is a special occasion: આ પ્રસંગે, on this occasion.

Lesson 3 of 5.
સ્થળ (sthaḷ) — a place; a site.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an inauguration, then the Gujarati for an occasion.

You'll want to know: સ્થળ.
સ્થળ — sthaḷ — "a place; a site".
પત્રકારો અકસ્માતના સ્થળે પહોંચ્યા. patrakāro akasmātnā sthaḷe pahõcyā. Journalists reached the scene of the accident.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સ્થળ (sthaḷ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sthala, "ground, place, spot" (Monier-Williams). It is the formal word for the place where something happened: અકસ્માતના સ્થળે, at the scene.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઉલ્લેખ (ullekh) — a mention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an occasion, then the Gujarati for a site.

You'll want to know: ઉલ્લેખ.
ઉલ્લેખ — ullekh — "a mention".
છાપામાં અમારા ગામનો ઉલ્લેખ હતો. chāpāmā̃ amārā gāmno ullekh hato. Our village got a mention in the newspaper.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉલ્લેખ (ullekh) is a tatsama, Sanskrit ullekha, "mentioning, speaking of" (Monier-Williams). Something is mentioned with કરવું: ઉલ્લેખ કર્યો.

Lesson 5 of 5.
નિહાળવું (nihāḷvũ) — to watch; to behold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a site, then the Gujarati for a mention.

You'll want to know: નિહાળવું.
નિહાળવું — nihāḷvũ — "to watch; to behold".
ઘણા લોકોએ ઉદ્ઘાટન નિહાળ્યું. ghaṇā lokoe udghāṭan nihāḷyũ. Many people watched the opening.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આકાશ નિહાળ્યું, teṇe ākāś nihāḷyũ.

The word, taken apart.
નિહાળવું (nihāḷvũ) goes back to Sanskrit nibhālayati, "perceives, sees", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to see" (CDIAL 7228). It is to watch with attention, a fuller word than જોવું.
