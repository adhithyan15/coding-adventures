## You'll want to know: स्पर्श
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C501-MITHI501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्श** — *sparśa* — "a touch".

> आईच्या स्पर्शाने बाळ शांत झालं.
>
> *āīcyā sparśāne bāḷ śānt jhālaṁ.*
>
> The baby calmed down at its mother's touch.

Masculine.

स्पर्श करणे, to touch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्श** (*sparśa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sparśa*, "touch" (CDIAL 13809).
