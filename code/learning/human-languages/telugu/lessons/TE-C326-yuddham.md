# యుద్ధం (yuddhaṁ) — a war; a battle

## You'll want to know: యుద్ధం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C326-SANGHATANA326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**యుద్ధం** — *yuddhaṁ* — "a war; a battle".

> ఆ యుద్ధం చాలా రోజులు జరిగింది.
>
> *ā yuddhaṁ cālā rōjulu jarigindi.*
>
> That war went on for many days.

With చేయు it makes a verb: యుద్ధం చేయు, to fight a war.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**యుద్ధం** (*yuddhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *yuddha*, "fought", and as a noun "a battle", the past participle of the root *yudh*, "to fight" (CDIAL 10499). Like many Sanskrit nouns in -a, it ends in -ం in Telugu.
