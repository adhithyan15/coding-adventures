Gujarati, chapter 155: Getting ready in the morning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઉઠાડવું (uṭhāḍvũ) — to wake someone, to get someone up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to take off, then the Gujarati for to wrap oneself in.

You'll want to know: ઉઠાડવું.
ઉઠાડવું — uṭhāḍvũ — "to wake someone, to get someone up".
માતા મને સવારે ઉઠાડે છે. — mātā mane savāre uṭhāḍe che — "Mother wakes me in the morning."
Built on ઉઠવું, uṭhvũ, to get up: this verb is to get someone else up.

The word, taken apart.
ઉઠાડવું (uṭhāḍvũ) is built on the inherited uṭhvũ, "to get up", with the causative -āḍ-. CDIAL traces uṭhvũ to utsthāti, "stands up", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 1900); it lists no Gujarati form of the causative itself.

You'll want to know: તૈયાર થવું.
તૈયાર થવું — taiyār thavũ — "to get ready".
હું સવારે જલદી તૈયાર થાઉં છું. — hũ savāre jaldī taiyār thāũ chũ — "I get ready quickly in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
તૈયાર થવું (taiyār thavũ) is built from taiyār, "ready", a loan from Persian tayyār (EtymDB lists the Persian word), and the inherited verb thavũ, "to become".

તે રોજ વાળ ઓળે છે. — te roj vāḷ oḷe che — "She combs her hair every day."

Lesson 4 of 5.
અત્તર (attar) — perfume, attar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get ready, then the Gujarati for to comb one's hair.

You'll want to know: અત્તર.
અત્તર — attar — "perfume, attar".
આ અત્તર મોંઘું છે. — ā attar moṅghũ che — "This perfume is expensive."

The word, taken apart.
અત્તર (attar) is a loan from Arabic ʿiṭr, "perfume", which reached Gujarati through Persian; EtymDB lists the Persian word, glossed "scent". Its Arabic root is ʿ-ṭ-r (Lane).

તે વાળમાં તેલ લગાડે છે. — te vāḷmā̃ tel lagāḍe che — "She puts oil in her hair."

લગાડવું (lagāḍvũ) is built on the inherited lāgvũ, "to touch, to come into contact", with the causative -āḍ-. CDIAL traces lāgvũ to Sanskrit lagyati, "adheres to" (CDIAL 10895).
