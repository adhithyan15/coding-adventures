Portuguese, chapter 525: Na biblioteca.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o bibliotecário — librarian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for rhyme, then the Portuguese for to recite.

You'll want to know: o bibliotecário.
o bibliotecário — "librarian".
O bibliotecário ajudou-me a encontrar o livro. The librarian helped me find the book.
A woman is a bibliotecária.

The word, taken apart.
bibliotecário is built on biblioteca, a learned borrowing through Latin bibliothēca "a library" (Lewis & Short) from Greek bibliothḗkē, from biblíon "book" and thḗkē "a store".

Lesson 2 of 5.
requisitar — to borrow (from a library); to request.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to recite, then the Portuguese for librarian.

You'll want to know: requisitar.
requisitar — "to borrow (from a library); to request".
Requisitei três livros na biblioteca. I borrowed three books from the library.
In Portugal the usual verb for library loans.

The word, taken apart.
requisitar is built on Latin requīsītus "sought, asked for" (Lewis & Short), from requīrere, built on quaerere "to seek".

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: a editora.
a editora — "publisher, publishing house".
A editora vai publicar o livro em maio. The publisher is going to bring out the book in May.
Built on editar "to publish".

The word, taken apart.
editora is built on editar "to publish", which came through French éditer (EtymDB) from Latin ēdere "to give out, to put forth" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
a tradução — translation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to borrow, then the Portuguese for publisher.

You'll want to know: a tradução.
a tradução — "translation".
Li o romance numa boa tradução. I read the novel in a good translation.
Tradução simultânea — simultaneous interpreting.

The word, taken apart.
tradução comes from Latin trāductiō "a leading across" (EtymDB), from trādūcere, built on trāns "across" and dūcere "to lead".
