# മൂന്ന് വർഷമായി (mūnnŭ varṣamāyi) — for three years now

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C139-TIME-05, ML-LEX-C139-TIME-03, ML-LEX-C179-BHAVI179-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a year", "an hour", and "from now on". (*varṣaṁ*, *maṇikkūr*, *ini mutal*.)

## Grammar Lens: a span with -ആയി, still running
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C346-SPAN-AAYI-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C139-TIME-05, ML-GRAMMAR-C104-ONE-PRESENT-01, ML-LEX-C173-ACT173-03, ML-LEX-C126-ABOUTYOU-03, ML-LEX-TAAMASIKKUKA-01] -->

To say how long something has been going on, put **-ആയി**, "has become", on the span, and keep the
verb in the present:

> **മൂന്ന് വർഷമായി ഞാൻ മലയാളം പഠിക്കുന്നു.**
>
> *mūnnŭ varṣamāyi ñān malayāḷaṁ paṭhikkunnu.* — "I have been learning Malayalam for three years."

The question puts **എത്ര** in front: **എത്ര വർഷമായി?** (*etra varṣamāyi?*), "for how many years?"

## Grammar Lens: since, and a finished span
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C346-SINCE-MUTAL-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C179-BHAVI179-02, ML-LEX-C94-YESTERDAY-01, ML-LEX-C63-FEEL-05, ML-LEX-C139-TIME-03, ML-LEX-C174-ACT174-02] -->

**മുതൽ**, which you know from **ഇനി മുതൽ**, says "since, from":

> **ഇന്നലെ മുതൽ എനിക്ക് പനിയാണ്.**
>
> *innale mutal enikkŭ paniyāṇŭ.* — "I have had a fever since yesterday."

A span that is over takes no ending at all: the plain past says it lasted that long and stopped.

> **ഞാൻ രണ്ട് മണിക്കൂർ കാത്തിരുന്നു.**
>
> *ñān raṇṭŭ maṇikkūr kāttirunnu.* — "I waited for two hours."
