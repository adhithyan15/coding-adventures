# ਸੁਕਾਉਣਾ (sukāuṇā) — to dry something

## You'll want to know: ਸੁਕਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C228-DIN228-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁਕਾਉਣਾ** — *sukāuṇā* — "to dry something".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਛੱਤ ਉੱਤੇ ਸੁਕਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *asīṁ kappṛe chhatt utte sukāunde hāṁ.*
>
> We dry the clothes on the roof.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਸੁਕਾਏ, *maiṁ kappṛe sukāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁਕਾਉਣਾ** (*sukāuṇā*) is the causative of a verb inherited from a reconstructed *\*śuṣkati*, "becomes dry"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sukkṇā*, "dries up", and its causative *sukhāuṇā* (CDIAL 12552).
