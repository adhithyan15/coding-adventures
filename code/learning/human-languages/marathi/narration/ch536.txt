Marathi, chapter 536: Music Making.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गायन (gāyan) — singing as an art.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a paintbrush, then the Marathi for a shade.

You'll want to know: गायन.
गायन — gāyan — "singing as an art".
संध्याकाळी शाळेत गायनाचा कार्यक्रम आहे. sandhyākāḷī śāḷet gāyanācā kāryakram āhe. There is a singing programme at school this evening.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
गायन (gāyan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit gāyana, "a singer, singing" (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for singing as an art, then the Marathi for an instrumentalist.

You'll want to know: सूर.
सूर — sūr — "a musical note; a tune".
गायिकेचा पहिलाच सूर खूप गोड होता. gāyikecā pahilāc sūr khūp goḍ hotā. The singer's very first note was very sweet.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
सूर (sūr) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi sūr, "musical note", under Sanskrit sura, "sound", a variant of svara (CDIAL 13498).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गुणगुणणे (guṇguṇṇe) — to hum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an instrumentalist, then the Marathi for a musical note.

You'll want to know: गुणगुणणे.
गुणगुणणे — guṇguṇṇe — "to hum".
स्वयंपाक करताना आई एक जुनं गाणं गुणगुणत होती. svayampāk kartānā āī ek junaṁ gāṇaṁ guṇguṇat hotī. While cooking, Mother was humming an old song.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने गाणं गुणगुणलं, tyāne gāṇaṁ guṇguṇlaṁ.

The word, taken apart.
गुणगुणणे (guṇguṇṇe) is an inherited echo word: CDIAL sets Hindi gungunānā and the Marathi murmuring words under ganagana, gunaguna, "murmur" (CDIAL 4013).

Lesson 5 of 5.
रियाज करणे (riyāj karṇe) — to practise music.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a musical note, then the Marathi for to hum.

You'll want to know: रियाज करणे.
रियाज करणे — riyāj karṇe — "to practise music".
गायक रोज पहाटे रियाज करतो. gāyak roj pahāṭe riyāj karto. The singer practises every day at dawn.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने रियाज केला, tyāne riyāj kelā.

The word, taken apart.
रियाज करणे (riyāj karṇe) is रियाज, a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic riyāḍa, "training", with करणे.
