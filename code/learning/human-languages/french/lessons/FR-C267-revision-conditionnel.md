# Review — if this, then that would

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-CE-SERAIT-01, FR-GRAM-CONDITIONNEL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "that would be good". (*Ce serait bien.*) Then "I could".
(*Je pourrais.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SI-PRESENT-01, FR-LEX-CA-DEPEND-01, FR-GRAM-DEPEND-DE-02, FR-GRAM-SI-IMPARFAIT-02, FR-LEX-C100-VILLE52-03] -->

Two chapters, and a whole way of talking about what has not happened yet. A
real branch: *s'il pleut, je reste*. A branch not yet named: *ça dépend*, or
*ça dépend de toi*. The softened result, in the conditionnel: *je pourrais*,
*j'aimerais*, *je devrais*, *ce serait*. And the condition that is not true
now: *si j'avais le temps, je partirais*.

> — *On va au parc demain ?*
> — *Ça dépend. Ce serait bien, mais s'il pleut, on pourrait aller au musée.*

[PAUSE 2s] *On pourrait* is "we could", the *on* form of *je pourrais*: a
soft suggestion, which is why it fits after *s'il pleut*. The *si* half
stays in the present, because rain tomorrow is a real possibility.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-CONDITIONNEL-01, FR-LEX-CE-SERAIT-01, FR-GRAM-SI-IMPARFAIT-02, FR-LEX-DEVOIR-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In *s'il pleut, on pourrait aller au musée*, which half is the
conditionnel? (***On pourrait aller.***) Which tense does the *si* half take
when the condition is not true now? (The **imparfait**.) What would "we
would be" be, from *serait*? (***Nous serions.***) And which Latin verb hides
in the *devoir* of *je devrais*? (***Dēbēre***, to owe.)
