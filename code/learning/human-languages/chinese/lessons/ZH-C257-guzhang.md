## You'll want to know: gǔzhǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C257-SHOW257-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔzhǎng* — "to clap; to applaud".

> *dàjiā dōu gǔzhǎng le* — Everyone clapped.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: gǔ is said with a rising tone before zhǎng, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔzhǎng* is built from *gǔ* 'a drum' and *zhǎng* 'the palm of the hand', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *tsyangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes an older dictionary for the first, 'to beat a drum', and glosses the second 'the palm'.
