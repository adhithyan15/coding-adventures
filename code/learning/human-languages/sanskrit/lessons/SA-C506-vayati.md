## You'll want to know: वयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C506-VAYATI506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वयति** — *vayati* — "weaves".

> तन्तुवायः कार्पासस्य वस्त्रं वयति।
>
> *tantuvāyaḥ kārpāsasya vastraṃ vayati.*
>
> The weaver weaves cotton cloth.

Past (he …): सः वस्त्रम् उतवान्, *saḥ vastram utavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वयति** (*vayati*) comes from the root √ve, "to weave", whose present is *vayati*; its past participle is *uta*, and KRM lists the past *utavān* (MW).
