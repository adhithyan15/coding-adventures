## You'll want to know: साक्षी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C286-CAURYA286-04]; assesses=[] -->

**साक्षी** — *sākṣī* — "a witness".

> साक्षी चौरं न दृष्टवान्।
>
> *sākṣī cauraṃ na dṛṣṭavān.*
>
> The witness did not see the thief.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साक्षी** (*sākṣī*) is the nominative of *sākṣin*, which MW gives as one who sees with his own eyes, an eye-witness, and a witness in law; it sits beside MW's *sākṣāt*, "with one's own eyes", but MW gives no derivation in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
