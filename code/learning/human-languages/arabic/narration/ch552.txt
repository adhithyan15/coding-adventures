Arabic, chapter 552: Doing up the house.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
طلاء (ṭilāʾ) — a coat of paint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a mop, then the Arabic for a couch.

You'll want to know: طلاء.
طلاء — ṭilāʾ — "a coat of paint".
جف الطلاء الأزرق على الباب. jaffa aṭ-ṭilāʾ al-azraq ʿalā al-bāb. The blue paint dried on the door.

The word, taken apart.
طلاء (ṭilāʾ) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-l-y, "to smear, to coat" (Lane). Ṭilāʾ is what is smeared on.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: رمم.
رمم — rammama — "to renovate, to restore".
رمم أبي البيت القديم. rammama abī al-bayt al-qadīm. My father renovated the old house.
Present: يرمم, yurammim.

The word, taken apart.
رمم (rammama) is built on the native Arabic root r-m-m, "to repair, to mend" (Lane). Form II rammama is to put an old thing right.
