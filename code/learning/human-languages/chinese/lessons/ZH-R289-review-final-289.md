> **A:** 那天你做什么了
>
> *nà tiān nǐ zuò shénme le* — What did you do that day?
>
> **B:** 我先去了书店 然后坐火车回家
>
> *wǒ xiān qù le shūdiàn, ránhòu zuò huǒchē huí jiā* — First the bookshop, then home by train.
>
> **B:** 在火车上 我的手机突然不见了
>
> *zài huǒchē shàng, wǒ de shǒujī tūrán bú jiàn le* — On the train my phone suddenly went missing.
>
> **A:** 你找回了吗
>
> *nǐ zhǎohuí le ma* — Did you get it back?
>
> **B:** 最后找回了 我感觉很好
>
> *zuìhòu zhǎohuí le, wǒ gǎnjué hěn hǎo* — In the end I did. I feel fine.
>
> **B:** *wǒ de zhèngjiàn yě bú jiàn le, wǒ děi mǎshàng guàshī* — My papers went missing too; I have to report them lost at once.
>
> **A:** *wǒ juéde nǐ yīnggāi xiān liánxì wǒ* — I think you should have got in touch with me first.
>
> **B:** *yīnwèi wǒ hěn lèi, suǒyǐ wàng le* — I was tired, so I forgot.
>
> **A:** 以后你想当什么
>
> *yǐhòu nǐ xiǎng dāng shénme* — What do you want to be later on?
>
> **B:** *wǒ de mèngxiǎng shì dāng yīshēng, wǒ de mùbiāo shì shuō hǎo Hànyǔ* — My dream is to be a doctor; my goal is to speak good Chinese.
>
> **A:** *nǐ xué le duōjiǔ* — How long have you studied?
>
> **B:** *liǎng nián, wǒ méi qù guo Zhōngguó, tài yíhàn le* — Two years. I've never been to China, sadly.
>
> **A:** 明天去山上吗 去山上怎么走
>
> *míngtiān qù shān shàng ma, qù shān shàng zěnme zǒu* — Going up the mountain tomorrow? How do you get there?
>
> **B:** 要看天气 要是下雨的话 我就不去了
>
> *yào kàn tiānqì, yàoshi xiàyǔ dehuà, wǒ jiù bú qù le* — It depends on the weather. If it rains, I won't go.
>
> **A:** 如果不下雨 我们就坐火车去
>
> *rúguǒ bú xiàyǔ, wǒmen jiù zuò huǒchē qù* — If it doesn't rain, we'll go by train.
>
> **A:** 快点儿 不然我们就晚了
>
> *kuài diǎnr, bùrán wǒmen jiù wǎn le* — Hurry, or we'll be late.
