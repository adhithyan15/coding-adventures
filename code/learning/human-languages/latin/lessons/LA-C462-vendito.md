## You'll want to know: vēnditō, vēnditāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C462-NUNDINOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vēnditō, vēnditāre** — "to offer for sale, to try to sell".

> Mercātor vīnum suum omnibus vēnditat.
>
> *The merchant tries to sell his wine to everyone.*

*vēnditō, vēnditāre, vēnditāvī, vēnditātum*, a frequentative of *vendō*: offering again and again, praising your goods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vēnditō* is built on *vendō* 'to sell' with the frequentative ending *-itō* (Lewis & Short). English *vend* and *vendor* come from *vendō*.
