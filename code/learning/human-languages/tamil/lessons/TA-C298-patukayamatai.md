# படுகாயமடை (paṭukāyamaṭai) — to be badly hurt

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for to accuse, then the Tamil for an accident.

## You'll want to know: படுகாயமடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C298-VIBATHU298-02]; assesses=[] -->

**படுகாயமடை** — *paṭukāyamaṭai* — "to be badly hurt".

> விபத்தில் ஓட்டுநர் படுகாயமடைந்தார்.
>
> *vipattil ōṭṭunar paṭukāyamaṭaintār.*
>
> The driver was badly hurt in the accident.

Past (I …): படுகாயமடைந்தேன், *paṭukāyamaṭaintēṉ*.

படு- before a noun makes it severe: படுகாயம், a severe wound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**படுகாயமடை** (*paṭukāyamaṭai*) is a Tamil compound built from படு, "severe", காயம், "a wound", and அடை, "to reach, get" (DEDR 79).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
