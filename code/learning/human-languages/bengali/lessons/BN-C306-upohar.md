## You'll want to know: উপহার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C306-OTITHI306-04]; assesses=[] -->

**উপহার** — *upôhār* — "a gift; a present".

> জন্মদিনে বন্ধুরা আমাকে একটা বই উপহার দিল।
>
> *jônmôdine bôndhurā āmāke ekṭā bôi upôhār dilô.*
>
> My friends gave me a book as a birthday present.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপহার** (*upôhār*) is Sanskrit *upahāra*, an offering to a god or a present to a superior (Monier-Williams); it has nothing to do with **হার**, a necklace. উপহার দেওয়া, to give a present.
