## You'll want to know: lēctor, lēctōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C550-LIBR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lēctor, lēctōris** — "a reader".

> Lēctor dominō librum legit.
>
> *The reader reads a book to his master.*

Masculine, third declension, from *legō*. Rich houses kept a slave to read aloud.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lēctor** is built on *legō* "to read" with *-tor* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *lecture* comes from the related *lēctūra*.
