## You'll want to know: లాక్కో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C348-DOPIDI348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**లాక్కో** — *lākkō* — "to snatch; to grab".

> దొంగ ఆమె సంచి లాక్కుని పారిపోయాడు.
>
> *doṅga āme sañci lākkuni pāripōyāḍu.*
>
> The thief snatched her bag and ran away.

Past (I …): లాక్కున్నాను, *lākkunnānu*.

లాగు, to pull, with -కో, for oneself: to pull something away to yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లాక్కో** (*lākkō*) is *lāgu*, "to pull", with the self-ending *-kō*; the origin of *lāgu* is not settled.
