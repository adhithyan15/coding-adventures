Sanskrit, chapter 522: A Song to Dance To.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पदन्यासः (padanyāsaḥ) — a dance step.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a tambourine, then the Sanskrit for a resonant sound.

You'll want to know: पदन्यासः.
पदन्यासः — padanyāsaḥ — "a dance step".
नर्तकस्य पदन्यासः सुन्दरः अस्ति। nartakasya padanyāsaḥ sundaraḥ asti. The dancer's footwork is beautiful.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पदन्यासः (padanyāsaḥ) is built from pada, "a foot, a step", and nyāsa, "a putting down"; MW gives padanyāsa as putting down the feet, a step (MW).

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a resonant sound, then the Sanskrit for a dance step.

You'll want to know: आवर्तते.
आवर्तते — āvartate — "spins round; turns back".
नर्तकः शीघ्रम् आवर्तते। nartakaḥ śīghram āvartate. The dancer turns round quickly.
Past (he …): सः शीघ्रम् आवृत्तवान्, saḥ śīghram āvṛttavān.

The word, taken apart.
आवर्तते (āvartate) is built on ā and the root √vṛt, "to turn", which also gives vartate; MW gives āvṛt as to turn or draw round, to turn back (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for spins round, then the Sanskrit for a small bell.

You'll want to know: अनुकरोति.
अनुकरोति — anukaroti — "imitates".
शिष्यः गुरोः पदन्यासम् अनुकरोति। śiṣyaḥ guroḥ padanyāsam anukaroti. The pupil imitates the teacher's steps.
Past (he …): सः गुरुम् अनुकृतवान्, saḥ gurum anukṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुकरोति (anukaroti) is built on anu, "after", and the root √kṛ, "to do", which also gives karoti: to do after another; MW gives anukṛ as to imitate, to copy (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
करतालिका (karatālikā) — clapping; applause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a small bell, then the Sanskrit for imitates.

You'll want to know: करतालिका.
करतालिका — karatālikā — "clapping; applause".
नृत्यं दृष्ट्वा पिता करतालिकां करोति। nṛtyaṃ dṛṣṭvā pitā karatālikāṃ karoti. After the dance, father claps.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
करतालिका (karatālikā) is built from kara, "the hand", and tālikā, "clapping", a word of the same family as tāla, "beating time"; MW gives karatālikā as clapping the hands and beating time by clapping (MW).
