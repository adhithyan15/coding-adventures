## You'll want to know: فسر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C363-NAZARIYYA363-05]; assesses=[] -->

**فسر** — *fassara* — "to interpret".

> فسر المعلم القصيدة للطلاب.
>
> *fassara al-muʿallim al-qaṣīda li-ṭ-ṭullāb.*
>
> The teacher interpreted the poem for the students.

Present: يفسر, *yufassir*.

The noun تفسير, *tafsīr*, an interpretation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فسر** (*fassara*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-s-r*, "to uncover, to make plain" (Lane). It is Form II, to make plain: to explain what a thing means.
