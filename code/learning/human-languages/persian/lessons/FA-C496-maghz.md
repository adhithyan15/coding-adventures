## You'll want to know: مغز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C496-NERVES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مغز** (*maghz*) — "the brain".

> مغز در خواب هم کار میکند.
>
> *maghz dar khâb ham kâr mikonad.*
>
> The brain works even during sleep.

Also the kernel of a nut: *maghz-e gerdu* 'a walnut kernel'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maghz* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian, where the word meant 'brain, marrow' (EtymDB).
