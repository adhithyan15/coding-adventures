## You'll want to know: صادق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C483-HONESTY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صادق** (*sâdeq*) — "honest, sincere".

> دوست من همیشه با من صادق است.
>
> *dust-e man hamishe bâ man sâdeq ast.*
>
> My friend is always honest with me.

*bâ kasi sâdeq budan* — to be honest with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sâdeq* was borrowed from Arabic *ṣādiq*, 'true, sincere; reliable' (EtymDB).
