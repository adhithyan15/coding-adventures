## You'll want to know: કડિયો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C511-MAJUR511-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કડિયો** — *kaḍiyo* — "a mason".

> કડિયાએ ઈંટોથી નવી દીવાલ બનાવી.
>
> *kaḍiyāe ī̃ṭothī navī dīvāl banāvī.*
>
> The mason built a new wall of bricks.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કડિયો** (*kaḍiyo*) builds with brick, stone and mortar. As the doer of a past action it becomes **કડિયાએ**. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
