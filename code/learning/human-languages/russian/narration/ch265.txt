Russian, chapter 265: Asking the Way.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
как пройти к … (kak proytí k …) — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "excuse me", "where" and "the station". (Izvinítye. Gde. Vokzál.) Then "I must": a man and a woman. (Ya dólzhen. Ya dolzhná.)
[pause 1 second]
Asking где вокзал works. Russians also ask for the way itself.

You'll want to know: как пройти.
Извините, как пройти к вокзалу? — izvinítye, kak proytí k vokzálu? — Excuse me, how do I get to the station?
Пройти is the perfective of a going-verb: to get somewhere on foot, through the streets, and arrive. It stays in the infinitive, with no "I" in the question: literally "how to get through to the station".

Grammar Lens: к and the "to" form.
к — k — "towards, to". The place after it takes the same "to" form as маме and брату:
к вокзалу, к музею — k vokzálu, k muzéyu — a masculine noun ending in a consonant takes -у, and one in -й takes -ю. к остановке — k ostanóvke — most nouns in -а take -е. к площади — k plóshchadi — площадь, in -ь, takes -и. к метро — k metró — метро stays as it is.

The exchange.
— Извините, как пройти к музею? Это далеко? — Нет, это близко. Музей рядом.
Éto dalekó? — "Is it far?" Nyet, éto blízko. Muzéy ryádom — "No, it's near. The museum is close by."

The word, taken apart.
пройти is про-, "through", with идти, "to go", which EtymDB 2.1 traces through Old East Slavic iti and Proto-Slavic jĭti to Proto-Indo-European h₁éyti, "to go", the root of Latin īre. к goes back to Proto-Slavic kŭ, which EtymDB links to a Proto-Indo-European kom, "next to, with".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: Izvinítye, kak proytí k vokzálu? Éto dalekó?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the same to the museum, the stop and the square: k muzéyu, k ostanóvke, k plóshchadi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: Kak proytí k metró? Éto blízko or Éto dalekó]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"How do I get to the station?" (Как пройти к вокзалу?) What does к do to остановка? (К остановке.) And to метро? (Nothing: к метро.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
идите прямо, поверните направо (idítye pryámo, povernítye naprávo) — following the answer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask the way to the museum. (Izvinítye, kak proytí k muzéyu?) Then say "straight on", "to the right", "to the left". (Pryámo. Naprávo. Nalévo.)

Grammar Lens: идите and поверните.
The answer comes as instructions, with the same -ите as извините and повторите:
Идите прямо. — idítye pryámo — Go straight on. Поверните направо. — povernítye naprávo — Turn right.
Идите is идти as an instruction. Поверните comes from повернуть, the perfective partner of поворачивать: one turn, at one corner. To a friend, the -ите drops to иди, поверни.

Grammar Lens: as far as, and at.
Two small words carry a route. до is "as far as", and the noun after it changes as in до свидания: светофор, до светофора. на is "at" a crossroads: перекрёсток, на перекрёстке.
Идите прямо до светофора, потом поверните налево. — idítye pryámo do svetofóra, potóm povernítye nalévo — Go straight on to the lights, then turn left. На перекрёстке поверните направо. — na perekryóstke povernítye naprávo — At the crossroads, turn right.

The exchange.
— Как пройти к музею? — Идите прямо, потом направо. Музей справа.
Muzéy správa: "the museum is on the right". Справа is where a thing stands; направо is where you go.

The word, taken apart.
направо is на, "onto", with the root of правый, "right": onto the right hand. EtymDB 2.1 traces правый to Proto-Slavic pravŭ, "right, correct", so the right hand and being right share one root. поверните is on the root of вертеть, "to turn", which EtymDB takes back through Proto-Slavic vĭrtěti to Proto-Indo-European wert-: the root of Latin vertere and English -ward.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: Idítye pryámo do svetofóra, potóm povernítye nalévo.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Na perekryóstke povernítye naprávo.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the same to a friend: Idí pryámo, potóm poverní naprávo.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: Kak proytí k metró? Give two steps, and end with správa or sléva]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Turn left", politely? (Поверните налево.) "As far as the lights"? (До светофора.) Where you go, or where it stands: направо or справа? (Направо is where you go.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — asking the way, and giving it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask the way to the park, then to the theatre. (Kak proytí k párku? Kak proytí k teátru?)

What you've built.
Two lessons, and a stranger's street is no longer a wall. The question: Извините, как пройти к …? (kak proytí k …?), with the place in its "to" form. The answer: идите прямо, поверните направо (idítye pryámo, povernítye naprávo) or налево, до something, на a corner.

The exchange.
— Извините, как пройти к театру? — Это далеко. Вам лучше взять такси.
Vam lúchshe vzyat taksí — "You'd better take a taxi." Advice from the last chapter, now with the polite вам.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: Izvinítye, kak proytí k párku?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Idítye pryámo do perekryóstka, potóm povernítye naprávo.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Éto dalekó. Vam lúchshe vzyat taksí.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: a stranger asks Kak proytí k vokzálu? Give the way from where you live, in two or three steps]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"To the park"? (К парку.) "Go straight on"? (Идите прямо.) And to a friend? (Иди прямо.)
