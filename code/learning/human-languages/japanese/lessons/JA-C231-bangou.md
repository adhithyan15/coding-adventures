## You'll want to know: ばんごう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C231-PHONE231-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ばんごう** — *bangō* — "a number; a phone number".

> **ちちは でんわの ばんごうを よく まちがえます。** — *chichi wa denwa no bangō o yoku machigaemasu* — "My father often gets phone numbers wrong."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばんごう** (*bangō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ban*, "turn, number" (Middle Chinese *phjon*) and *gō*, "number, name" (*hawH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
