## You'll want to know: ഗ്രന്ഥകാരൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C439-GRANTHAM439-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗ്രന്ഥകാരൻ** — *granthakāran* — "an author".

> ഗ്രന്ഥകാരൻ പുസ്തകത്തിൽ ഒപ്പിട്ടു തന്നു.
>
> *granthakāran pustakattil oppiṭṭu tannu.*
>
> The author signed the book for me.

A woman author is ഗ്രന്ഥകാരി.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗ്രന്ഥകാരൻ** (*granthakāran*) is built from Sanskrit *grantha*, "a knot; a book" (CDIAL 4350), and *kāra*, "making, doing" (CDIAL 3053), with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ: the maker of a book.
