## You'll want to know: めぐすり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C450-CLINIC450-04]; assesses=[] -->

**めぐすり** — *megusuri* — "eye drops".

> **めが かゆいので、めぐすりを つかいました。** — *me ga kayui node, megusuri o tsukaimashita* — "My eyes itched, so I used eye drops."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めぐすり** (*megusuri*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *me*, "eye" and *kusuri*, "medicine" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
