Gujarati, chapter 380: Enough or Not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પૂરતું (pūrtũ) — enough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for absolutely, then the Gujarati for solely.

You'll want to know: પૂરતું.
પૂરતું — pūrtũ — "enough".
જો પૂરતો વરસાદ પડશે તો પાણીની ચિંતા નહીં રહે. jo pūrto varsād paḍśe to pāṇīnī cintā nahī̃ rahe. If there is enough rain, there will be no worry about water.

The word, taken apart.
પૂરતું (pūrtũ) is built on pūrũ, "full", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit pūra, "filling" (CDIAL 8330): as much as fills the need.

Lesson 2 of 5.
તંગી (tangī) — a shortage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for solely, then the Gujarati for enough.

You'll want to know: તંગી.
તંગી — tangī — "a shortage".
જો ઉનાળામાં વરસાદ મોડો આવશે તો પાણીની તંગી પડશે. jo unāḷāmā̃ varsād moḍo āvśe to pāṇīnī tangī paḍśe. If the rain comes late this summer, there will be a water shortage.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
તંગી (tangī) is usually traced to Persian tangī, "narrowness, want".

Lesson 3 of 5.
થોડું (thoḍũ) — a little.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for enough, then the Gujarati for a shortage.

You'll want to know: થોડું.
થોડું — thoḍũ — "a little".
જો તમે થોડી રાહ જોશો તો બસ આવશે. jo tame thoḍī rāh jośo to bas āvśe. If you wait a little, the bus will come.

The word, taken apart.
થોડું (thoḍũ) is inherited from Sanskrit stōka, "a drop", and as an adverb "a little"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a little, few" (CDIAL 13720).

Lesson 4 of 5.
સહેજ (sahej) — slightly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a shortage, then the Gujarati for a little.

You'll want to know: સહેજ.
સહેજ — sahej — "slightly".
જો તમે સહેજ ખસશો તો અમે પણ બેસી શકીશું. jo tame sahej khasśo to ame paṇ besī śakīśũ. If you move up slightly, we will be able to sit too.

The word, taken apart.
સહેજ (sahej) is usually traced to Sanskrit sahaja, "born together, innate, natural" (Monier-Williams); "slightly, easily" is a Gujarati sense the source does not give.

Lesson 5 of 5.
પહોંચી વળવું (pahõcī vaḷvũ) — to cope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a little, then the Gujarati for slightly.

You'll want to know: પહોંચી વળવું.
પહોંચી વળવું — pahõcī vaḷvũ — "to cope".
જો બધા મદદ કરશે તો આપણે આ કામને પહોંચી વળીશું. jo badhā madad karśe to āpaṇe ā kāmne pahõcī vaḷīśũ. If everyone helps, we will cope with this work.
Past (he …): તે બધી મુશ્કેલીઓને પહોંચી વળ્યો, te badhī muśkelīone pahõcī vaḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
પહોંચી વળવું (pahõcī vaḷvũ) is built from પહોંચવું (pahõcvũ), "to reach" (CDIAL 8716), and વળવું (vaḷvũ), "to turn" (CDIAL 11405): to reach a task and turn it round.
