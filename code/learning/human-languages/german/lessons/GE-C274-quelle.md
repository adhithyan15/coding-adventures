## You'll want to know: die Quelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C274-GERUECHT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Quelle** — "the source; the spring".

> Der Journalist nannte seine Quelle nicht.
>
> *The journalist did not name his source.*

Plural: *die Quellen*. The first sense is a spring of water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Quelle* is inherited from Old High German *quella*, which EtymDB traces to Proto-Germanic *kwellōn* "well, spring": where water, and later news, comes from.
