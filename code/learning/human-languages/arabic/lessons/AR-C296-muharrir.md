## You'll want to know: محرر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C296-SAHAFA296-02]; assesses=[] -->

**محرر** — *muḥarrir* — "an editor".

> يعمل خالي محررا في مجلة.
>
> *yaʿmalu khālī muḥarriran fī majalla.*
>
> My uncle works as an editor at a magazine.

The verbal noun تحرير is editing: the editorial office is قسم التحرير.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محرر** (*muḥarrir*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-r-r* (Lane), "to be free", the root of *ḥurr*, free. Form II *ḥarrara* means "to set free" and "to write a text out clean"; *muḥarrir* is its participle.
