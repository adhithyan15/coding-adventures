Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 68: Forest, River, Stone, Sand, Field.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जंगळ (jaṅgaḷ) — a forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a leaf, then the Marwadi for a hill.

You'll want to know: जंगळ.
जंगळ — jaṅgaḷ — "a forest".

The word, taken apart.
जंगळ (jaṅgaḷ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jāṅgala, "arid, sparingly grown with trees". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5177); the match is through Hindi jaṅgal. CDIAL notes evidence for a non-Aryan origin. The sense moved from "dry, open country" to "forest".

Lesson 4 of 5.
रेत (ret) — sand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a river, then the Marwadi for a stone.

You'll want to know: रेत.
रेत — ret — "sand".

The word, taken apart.
रेत (ret) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from retra, "perfumed powder". CDIAL lists Old Marwari reta under it (CDIAL 10816). CDIAL thinks a Munda source for the Sanskrit word scarcely likely.
