# ઢળવું (ḍhaḷvũ) — to lean towards; to slip down

## You'll want to know: ઢળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C343-TEKO343-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢળવું** — *ḍhaḷvũ* — "to lean towards; to slip down".

> આખી ચર્ચા પછી મારો મત તમારી બાજુ ઢળે છે.
>
> *ākhī carcā pachī māro mat tamārī bāju ḍhaḷe che.*
>
> After the whole discussion my view leans your way.

Past (it …): તે દીવાલ એક બાજુ ઢળી ગઈ, *te dīvāl ek bāju ḍhaḷī gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢળવું** (*ḍhaḷvũ*) is inherited from *ḍhalati*, "bends over, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to slip, to be poured out" (CDIAL 5581). Leaning towards an opinion is a later sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
