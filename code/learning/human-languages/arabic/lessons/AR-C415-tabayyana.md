## You'll want to know: تبين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C415-INJAZ415-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تبين** — *tabayyana* — "to become clear".

> تبين أن الخبر غير صحيح.
>
> *tabayyana anna al-khabar ghayr ṣaḥīḥ.*
>
> It became clear that the news was not true.

Present: يتبين, *yatabayyan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تبين** (*tabayyana*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-y-n*, "to be apart; to become clear" (Lane). It is Form V, to become plain: to become clear, to turn out.
