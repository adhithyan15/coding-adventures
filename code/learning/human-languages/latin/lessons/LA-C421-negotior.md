## You'll want to know: negōtior, negōtiārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C421-COMMERCIUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**negōtior, negōtiārī** — "to do business, to trade".

> In prōvinciā negōtiābātur.
>
> *He was doing business in the province.*

Deponent: *negōtior, negōtiārī, negōtiātus sum*, built on *negōtium*, business. English *negotiate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*negōtior* is built on *negōtium* "business" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which is *nec* "not" and *ōtium* "leisure". English *negotiate* comes from it.
