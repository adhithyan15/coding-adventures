Mandarin Chinese, chapter 123: What People Are Like.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
cōngming — clever; bright.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for timetable, then the Mandarin for to give a discount.

You'll want to know: cōngming.
cōngming — "clever; bright".
wǒ mèimei hěn cōngming — My little sister is very bright.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
cōngming is built from cōng and míng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, tshuwng and mjaeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to hear' and 'to discern', and the second 'bright'.

What you've built.
One more word for describing things.

Lesson 5 of 5.
guānxīn — to care about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for lovable, then the Mandarin for warm-hearted.

You'll want to know: guānxīn.
guānxīn — "to care about".
māma hěn guānxīn wǒ — Mum really cares about me.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
guānxīn is built from guān, the word for shutting, and xīn 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwaen and sim (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the bar that shuts a door; how the pair came to mean caring is not settled.
