## You'll want to know: অভাব
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C419-ABHAB419-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অভাব** — *ôbhāb* — "want; lack; need".

> গরমে গ্রামে জলের অভাব হয়।
>
> *gôrôme grāme jôler ôbhāb hôy.*
>
> In summer the village runs short of water.

Also hardship itself: অভাবের দিন, hard times; টাকার অভাব, a shortage of money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অভাব** (*ôbhāb*) is Sanskrit *abhāva*, "non-existence, absence" (Monier-Williams), from *a-*, not, and *bhāva*, being; Bengali uses it for the want of what one needs.
