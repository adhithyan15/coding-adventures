## You'll want to know: l'étang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C456-RUISSEAU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'étang** — "the pond".

> Des canards nagent sur l'étang.
>
> *Some ducks are swimming on the pond.*

A masculine noun. Bigger than a *mare*, smaller than a *lac*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*étang* comes from Old French *estanc* (EtymDB lists it), built on *estanchier* 'to stop the flow of water', which is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin verb; the details are not settled.
