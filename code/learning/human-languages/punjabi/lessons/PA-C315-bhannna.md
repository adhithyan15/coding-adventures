## You'll want to know: ਭੰਨਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C315-BANDIT315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭੰਨਣਾ** — *bhannṇā* — "to smash".

> ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਖਿੜਕੀ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ਾ ਭੰਨਿਆ।
>
> *Aman ne khiṛkī dā shīshā bhanniā.*
>
> Aman smashed the windowpane.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਘੜਾ ਭੰਨਿਆ, *maiṁ purāṇā ghaṛā bhanniā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭੰਨਣਾ** (*bhannṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhajyate*, "is broken"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bhanṇā*, "to break", under it (CDIAL 9361).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
