## You'll want to know: die Schwangerschaft
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C296-GEBURT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Schwangerschaft** — "the pregnancy".

> Während der Schwangerschaft arbeitete sie weiter.
>
> *During the pregnancy she went on working.*

Plural: *die Schwangerschaften*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schwangerschaft* is built on *schwanger* "pregnant", Old High German *swangar* (EtymDB), with the noun ending *-schaft*.
