## You'll want to know: angiportum, angiportī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C458-ANGIPORTUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**angiportum, angiportī** — "an alley, a narrow lane".

> Nocte angiportum obscūrum et perīculōsum est.
>
> *At night the alley is dark and dangerous.*

Second declension, neuter: *angiportum, angiportī n.*; also *angiportus, angiportūs m.* A narrow side street between houses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*angiportum* is built, as Lewis & Short explain under *angiportus*, on the root of *angustus* 'narrow' and on *portus* in its old sense 'passage'. EtymDB does not list it.
