## You'll want to know: वादकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C521-VADAKA521-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वादकः** — *vādakaḥ* — "a musician".

> वादकः मृदंगं वादयति।
>
> *vādakaḥ mṛdaṅgaṃ vādayati.*
>
> The musician plays the mridanga.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वादकः** (*vādakaḥ*) comes from the causative of the root √vad, "to make sound", with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *vādaka* as a musician (MW).
