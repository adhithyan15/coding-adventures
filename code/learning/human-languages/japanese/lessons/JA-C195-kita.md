## You'll want to know: きた
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C195-CORNER195-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きた** — *kita* — "north".

> **えきの きたに こうえんが あります。** — *eki no kita ni kōen ga arimasu* — "There is a park north of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きた** (*kita*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
