# Yes or no? — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C158-POLAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Ask *do you drink tea?*, to **आप**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C158-POLAR-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C158-ANSWER-01, MW-LEX-C158-ASK158-01, MW-LEX-C158-VERB158-01, MW-SCRIPT-C158-O-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-VERB-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *is Ram here?*; *no, Ram isn't here*]
- [YOU SAY: *do you eat meat?*; *no, I don't eat meat*]
- [YOU SAY: *when do you come?*; *when does he go?*]
- [YOU SAY: *do you recognise this song?*; *yes, I recognise it*]
- [YOU READ: **ओळखणो**, and name its first letter]
