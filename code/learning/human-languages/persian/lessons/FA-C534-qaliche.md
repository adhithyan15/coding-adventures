## You'll want to know: قالیچه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C534-FURNISH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قالیچه** (*qâliche*) — "a rug, a small carpet".

> جلوی در یک قالیچه قرمز است.
>
> *jelo-ye dar yek qâliche-ye qermez ast.*
>
> There's a red rug in front of the door.

*qâli* 'carpet' + the diminutive *-che*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qâliche* is built on *qâli*, 'carpet', with the native diminutive *-che*. EtymDB lists *qâli* without a source, and its origin is not settled.
