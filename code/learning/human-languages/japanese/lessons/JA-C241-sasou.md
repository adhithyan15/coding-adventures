## You'll want to know: さそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C241-OUTING241-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さそう** — *sasou* — "to invite; to ask out".

> **せんしゅう ともだちを えいがに さそいました。** — *senshū tomodachi o eiga ni sasoimashita* — "Last week I invited a friend to a film."

Polite **さそいます**, *sasoimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さそう** (*sasou*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
