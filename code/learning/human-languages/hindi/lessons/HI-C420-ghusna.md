## You'll want to know: घुसना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C420-DHALAN420-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घुसना** — *ghusnā* — "to push in; to get in".

> भीड़ में घुसकर हमने बस पकड़ी।
>
> *bhīṛ meṁ ghuskar hamne bas pakṛī.*
>
> We pushed through the crowd and caught the bus.

Past (he …): वह कमरे में घुसा, *vah kamre meṁ ghusā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घुसना** (*ghusnā*) goes back to *\*ghuss*, "thrust in, pierce", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4492), which lists Hindi *ghusnā*, "to be thrust in".
