## You'll want to know: bìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C50-SLEEP50-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bìng* — "to fall ill; illness".

> *wǒ māma bìng le* — My mum is ill.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bìng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *bjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'worry, pain' and 'an illness grown worse'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
