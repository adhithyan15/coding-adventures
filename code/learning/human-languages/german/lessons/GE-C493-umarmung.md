## You'll want to know: die Umarmung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C493-ZUNEIGUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Umarmung** — "hug".

> Zur Begrüßung gab es eine feste Umarmung.
>
> *There was a tight hug by way of hello.*

Plural: *die Umarmungen*. Built on *umarmen* — to hug.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Umarmung* is built on *umarmen* "to put your arms around", from *um* "around" and *Arm*, which is inherited from Old High German *arm*, from Proto-Germanic *armaz* (EtymDB).
