## You'll want to know: ಯುದ್ಧ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C319-YUDDHA319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಯುದ್ಧ** — *yuddha* — "a war; a battle".

> ಯುದ್ಧದ ಫಲಿತಾಂಶ ಹವಾಮಾನದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿತ್ತು.
>
> *yuddhada phalitāṃśa havāmānada mēle avalambisittu.*
>
> The outcome of the war depended on the weather.

With ಮಾಡು it makes a verb: ಯುದ್ಧ ಮಾಡು, to fight a war.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಯುದ್ಧ** (*yuddha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *yuddha*, "fought", and as a noun "a battle", the past participle of the root *yudh*, "to fight" (CDIAL 10499). Kannada keeps the Sanskrit form whole, as it does many learned nouns.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
