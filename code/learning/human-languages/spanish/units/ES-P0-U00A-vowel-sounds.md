# Unit 0A: The Five Vowel Sounds

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before a single word of vocabulary, one fact that will save you
more confusion than anything else in this whole curriculum: Spanish has
exactly **five** vowel sounds, and each letter only ever makes *one* of
them. English has around 12-20 depending on dialect, and the letter "a"
alone can sound like the "a" in *cat*, *father*, *cake*, or *about* — four
different sounds, one letter. Spanish doesn't do that.

## New Material

**a** — like the "a" in *father*, always. Never like *cake*, never like
*cat*.

**e** — like the "e" in *bed*, always. Never like the long "e" in *see*.

**i** — like the "ee" in *see*, always. (This is the one that trips people
up: Spanish *i* sounds like English *ee*, not English *i*.)

**o** — like the "o" in *note*, but shorter and cleaner — no glide into a
"w" sound at the end the way English *note* trails off.

**u** — like the "oo" in *food*, always.

> There's no etymology note for this unit — these are sounds, not words
> with a history. The etymology threads pick back up at Unit 1.

The practical payoff: once you know these five sounds cold, you can
correctly pronounce almost any Spanish word you've never seen before, just
by reading it. That's not true of English at all — you can't reliably
pronounce an unfamiliar English word from its spelling alone. Spanish
spelling is far more consistent, and it starts here.

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "a" — like in *father*]
- [YOU SAY: "e" — like in *bed*]
- [YOU SAY: "i" — like *ee* in *see*]
- [YOU SAY: "o" — clean, no glide]
- [YOU SAY: "u" — like *oo* in *food*]

[REPEAT x2] Say all five in order: *a, e, i, o, u.*

Now say the word *"casa"* (house) sounding out each vowel purely: *ka-sa* —
notice there's no diphthong-glide the way English vowels often have.

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Which Spanish vowel sounds like English *ee*, not English *i*?
... *i*. That's the single most common vowel-sound mistake English speakers
make in Spanish — worth over-correcting for now.
