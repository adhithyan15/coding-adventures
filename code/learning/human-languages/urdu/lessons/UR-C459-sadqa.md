## You'll want to know: صدقہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C459-GIVE459-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صدقہ** — *sadqā* — "alms (given for a blessing)".

> دادی ہر ہفتے صدقہ دیتی ہیں۔
>
> *dādī har hafte sadqā detī haiṅ.*
>
> My grandmother gives alms every week.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صدقہ** (*sadqā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣadaqa*, "alms".
