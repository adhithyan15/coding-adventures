Lesson 1 of 5.
ಶುಲ್ಕ (śulka) — a fee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for housework, then the Kannada for a boss.

You'll want to know: ಶುಲ್ಕ.
ಶುಲ್ಕ — śulka — "a fee".

The word, taken apart.
ಶುಲ್ಕ (śulka) is Sanskrit śulka, "price, value; tax, toll", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 12543).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬಡ್ಡಿ (baḍḍi) — interest (on money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a fee, then the Kannada for an account.

You'll want to know: ಬಡ್ಡಿ.
ಬಡ್ಡಿ — baḍḍi — "interest (on money)".

The word, taken apart.
ಬಡ್ಡಿ (baḍḍi) comes from Sanskrit vṛddhi, "increase, prosperity", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 12076). Prakrit vaḍḍhi shows the step between: interest is the increase on money.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತೆರಿಗೆ (terige) — tax.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ತೆರಿಗೆ.
ತೆರಿಗೆ — terige — "tax".

The word, taken apart.
ತೆರಿಗೆ (terige) is native Dravidian, from teṟu, "to pay", and DEDR 3441 lists teṟige, "tribute, tax", itself; the entry also has Telugu teṟe and Malayalam tiṟa.
