## You'll want to know: बजट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C428-KAMANA428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बजट** — *bajaṭ* — "a budget".

> हमारा बजट कम है, इसलिए हम सस्ते होटल में ठहरेंगे।
>
> *hamārā bajaṭ kam hai, isliye ham saste hoṭal meṁ ṭhahreṅge.*
>
> Our budget is small, so we will stay in a cheap hotel.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बजट** (*bajaṭ*) is borrowed from English *budget*, a household's or a government's.
