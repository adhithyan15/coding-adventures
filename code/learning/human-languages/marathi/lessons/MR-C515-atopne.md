# आटोपणे (āṭopṇe) — to wrap up; to finish

## You'll want to know: आटोपणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C515-VISHESH515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आटोपणे** — *āṭopṇe* — "to wrap up; to finish".

> लेखापालाने संध्याकाळी काम आटोपलं आणि तो घरी गेला.
>
> *lekhāpālāne sandhyākāḷī kām āṭoplaṁ āṇi to gharī gelā.*
>
> The accountant wrapped up his work in the evening and went home.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने काम आटोपलं, *tyāne kām āṭoplaṁ*.

Close to उरकणे: both say a job was finished.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आटोपणे** (*āṭopṇe*) is a Marathi verb for bringing a task to a close; its origin is not settled.
