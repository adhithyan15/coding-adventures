## You'll want to know: کریدنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C478-CUT478-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کریدنا** — *kurednā* — "to pick at; to scrape at".

> زخم کو نہیں کریدنا چاہیے۔
>
> *zakhm ko nahīṅ kurednā chāhiye.*
>
> You should not pick at a wound.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے زخم کو کریدا, *us ne zakhm ko kuredā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کریدنا** (*kurednā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kurednā*, "to scrape", under *kura*, "scraping", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3319).
