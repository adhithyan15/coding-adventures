## You'll want to know: وارڈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C479-WARD479-02]; assesses=[] -->

**وارڈ** — *vārḍ* — "a hospital ward".

> مریض اوپر والے وارڈ میں ہے۔
>
> *marīz ūpar vāle vārḍ meṅ hai.*
>
> The patient is in the ward upstairs.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وارڈ** (*vārḍ*) is borrowed from English *ward*.
