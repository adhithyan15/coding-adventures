## You'll want to know: హారను
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C443-VAHANAM443-05]; assesses=[] -->

**హారను** — *hāranu* — "a horn".

> వెనక ఉన్న కారు హారను కొట్టింది.
>
> *venaka unna kāru hāranu koṭṭindi.*
>
> The car behind sounded its horn.

హారను కొట్టు, to sound the horn: the same కొట్టు as in to hit or to beat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**హారను** (*hāranu*) is a loan from English *horn*, the warning horn of a vehicle.
