## You'll want to know: plōrō, plōrāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C491-LACR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**plōrō, plōrāre** — "to wail, cry aloud".

> Puella plōrat, quia pila eius in flūmen cecidit.
>
> *The girl is wailing because her ball fell into the river.*

*plōrō, plōrāre, plōrāvī, plōrātum*. Louder than *fleō*: crying out loud. English *deplore* and *implore* come from its compounds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**plōrō** is a native Latin verb whose origin is not settled; Lewis & Short calls it doubtful. English *deplore* and *implore* come from its compounds *dēplōrō* and *implōrō*.
