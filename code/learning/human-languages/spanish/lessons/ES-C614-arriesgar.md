## You'll want to know: arriesgar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C614-RIESGO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**arriesgar** — "to risk".

> No quiero arriesgar mi dinero.
>
> *I do not want to risk my money.*

*Arriesgarse* — to take a chance. Spelling: *arriesgué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arriesgar* is built on *riesgo* 'risk', which comes from Italian *rischio* (EtymDB); the older origin is not settled.
