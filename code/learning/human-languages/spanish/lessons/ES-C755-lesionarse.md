## You'll want to know: lesionarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C755-ENTRENAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lesionarse** — "to get injured".

> Se lesionó la rodilla en el partido.
>
> *He injured his knee in the match.*

*Una lesión* — an injury.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lesionarse* is built on *lesión* "injury", a learned borrowing from Latin *laesiō* "injury" (EtymDB), from *laedere* "to hurt".
