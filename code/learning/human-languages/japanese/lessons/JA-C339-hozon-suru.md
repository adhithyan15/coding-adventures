## You'll want to know: ほぞんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C339-BUNBETSU339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ほぞんする** — *hozon suru* — "to keep; to preserve".

> **あついので、たべものは ひやして ほぞんして ください。** — *atsui node, tabemono wa hiyashite hozon shite kudasai* — "It is hot, so keep food cool when you store it."

Polite **ほぞんします**, *hozonshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **みせが たべものを ほぞんしました。**, *mise ga tabemono o hozon shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほぞんする** (*hozon suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *hozon*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *ho*, "preserve" (Middle Chinese *pawX*) and *zon*, "exist" (*dzwon*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
