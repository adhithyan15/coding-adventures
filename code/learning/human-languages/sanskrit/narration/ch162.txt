Lesson 1 of 5.
वेतनम् (vetanam) — wages, salary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an anklet, then the Sanskrit for an earring.

You'll want to know: वेतनम्.
वेतनम् — vetanam — "wages, salary".

The word, taken apart.
vetana "wages, hire, salary" appears from Manu on. The grammarians derive it from √vī, while MW thinks it rather belongs with √vṛt "turn" and vartana "livelihood", so its origin is uncertain.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ऋणम् (ṛṇam) — a debt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an earring, then the Sanskrit for wages.

You'll want to know: ऋणम्.
ऋणम् — ṛṇam — "a debt".

The word, taken apart.
ṛṇa "a debt" is old, in the Rigveda, and is inherited from Indo-Iranian, with an Avestan cousin (EtymDB); MW compares Latin reus "liable". It lives on as Hindi and Punjabi rin "debt" (CDIAL 2451).

Lesson 3 of 5.
लाभः (lābhaḥ) — profit, gain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for wages, then the Sanskrit for a debt.

You'll want to know: लाभः.
लाभः — lābhaḥ — "profit, gain".

The word, taken apart.
lābha comes from the root √labh "take, obtain" (CDIAL 11018): "finding, getting", and so "profit". MW compares √labh with Greek lambanō "take", and labhate "gets" is its present.

Lesson 4 of 5.

हानिः — hāniḥ — "loss".

The word, taken apart.
hāni "abandonment, decrease, loss" is filed by MW under the root √hā "leave, abandon", noting that some derive it from √han "strike", so the root is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
व्यापारः (vyāpāraḥ) — trade, business.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for profit, then the Sanskrit for loss.

You'll want to know: व्यापारः.
व्यापारः — vyāpāraḥ — "trade, business".

The word, taken apart.
vyāpāra comes from vi-ā-√pṛ "be busy, be occupied", the root √pṛ with vi and ā (MW; CDIAL 12205): "occupation, business", and so "trade". It lives on as Marathi vāvar "business, activity" (CDIAL 12205).
