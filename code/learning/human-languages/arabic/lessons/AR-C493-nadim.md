## You'll want to know: نادم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C493-NADAM493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نادم** — *nādim* — "regretful".

> كان اللاعب نادما بعد المباراة.
>
> *kāna al-lāʿib nādiman baʿda al-mubārā.*
>
> The player was full of regret after the match.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نادم** (*nādim*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-d-m*, "to repent, to regret" (Lane). Nādim is the active participle: one who regrets.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
