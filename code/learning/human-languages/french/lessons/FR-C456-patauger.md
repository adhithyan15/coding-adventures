## You'll want to know: patauger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C456-RUISSEAU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**patauger** — "to splash about, to wade; to flounder".

> Les enfants pataugent dans le ruisseau.
>
> *The children are splashing about in the stream.*

Figurative: *je patauge* — I am getting nowhere.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patauger* is usually linked to *patte* 'paw, foot', but the origin is not settled. EtymDB lists it without a source.
