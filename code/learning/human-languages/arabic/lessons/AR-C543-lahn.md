## You'll want to know: لحن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C543-MUSIQA543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**لحن** — *laḥn* — "a melody, a tune".

> غنت البنت لحنا جميلا.
>
> *ghannat al-bint laḥnan jamīlan.*
>
> The girl sang a beautiful tune.

Plural: ألحان, *alḥān*.

In grammar لحن also means a mistake in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لحن** (*laḥn*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-ḥ-n*, "to speak with a tone; to make a mistake in speech" (Lane). Lane gives laḥn both as a tone of voice and as a fault in speaking.
