## You'll want to know: कतरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C513-DARZI513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कतरना** — *katarnā* — "to snip; to trim (with scissors)".

> उसने कैंची से कपड़े के कोने कतरे।
>
> *Usne kaĩcī se kapṛe ke kone katre.*
>
> She snipped the corners of the cloth with scissors.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने धागा कतरा, *usne dhāgā katrā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कतरना** (*katarnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *kartarī*, "scissors" (CDIAL 2858).
