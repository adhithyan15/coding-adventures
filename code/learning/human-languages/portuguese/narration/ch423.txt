Portuguese, chapter 423: No controlo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
revistar — to search (a person or a bag).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the padlock, then the Portuguese for to lose.

You'll want to know: revistar.
revistar — "to search (a person or a bag)".
No aeroporto, revistaram a minha mochila. At the airport, they searched my backpack.
A revista is the search, and also a magazine.

The word, taken apart.
revistar is built on revista 'inspection, review', from rever 'to look over again', which goes back to Latin vidēre 'to see' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: apreender.
apreender — "to confiscate, to seize; to grasp (an idea)".
A polícia apreendeu as garrafas de água. The police confiscated the bottles of water.
Do not confuse with aprender, to learn: same Latin root.

The word, taken apart.
apreender is a learned borrowing from Latin apprehendere 'to take hold of' (EtymDB), the same verb that gave the inherited aprender 'to learn'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
o detetor — the detector.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to declare, then the Portuguese for to confiscate.

You'll want to know: o detetor.
o detetor — "the detector".
O detetor fez barulho por causa do meu cinto. The detector beeped because of my belt.
Spelled detetor since the 1990 agreement; Brazil writes detector.

The word, taken apart.
detetor is a learned formation on Latin dētegere, dētectum 'to uncover', from tegere 'to cover' (Lewis & Short).
