## You'll want to know: russare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C490-SONNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**russare** — "to snore".

> Mio marito russa e io non dormo.
>
> *My husband snores and I cannot sleep.*

An *-are* verb: *russo*, *russi*, *russa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *russare* is not settled; it is usually taken to imitate the sound of snoring.
