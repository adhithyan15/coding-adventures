# ووٹ (voṭ) — a vote

## You'll want to know: ووٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C342-VOT342-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ووٹ** — *voṭ* — "a vote".

> میں نے صبح اپنا ووٹ دیا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne subah apnā voṭ diyā.*
>
> I cast my vote in the morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ووٹ** (*voṭ*) is borrowed from English *vote*.
