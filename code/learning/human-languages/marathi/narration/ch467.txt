Marathi, chapter 467: Bearing It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
यातना (yātnā) — torment; agony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rest a while, then the Marathi for to brighten up.

You'll want to know: यातना.
यातना — yātnā — "torment; agony".
आजारपणात आजोबांनी खूप यातना सहन केल्या. ājārpaṇāt ājobānnī khūp yātnā sahan kelyā. Grandfather bore great agony during his illness.
Feminine.
Often plural: यातना (yātnā) सहन करणे, to bear agony.

The word, taken apart.
यातना (yātnā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit yātanā, "torment" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
तडफडणे (taḍphaḍṇe) — to writhe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to brighten up, then the Marathi for torment.

You'll want to know: तडफडणे.
तडफडणे — taḍphaḍṇe — "to writhe".
पाण्याबाहेर काढलेला मासा तडफडत होता. pāṇyābāher kāḍhlelā māsā taḍphaḍat hotā. The fish taken out of the water was writhing.
Past (he …): तो तडफडला, to taḍphaḍlā.
Of pain, or of fierce impatience: भेटायला तडफडणे (taḍphaḍṇe).

The word, taken apart.
तडफडणे (taḍphaḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi taḍphaḍṇẽ, "to toss about", under the expressive taḍapphaḍ (CDIAL 5634).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कळवळणे (kaḷvaḷṇe) — to ache with pity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for torment, then the Marathi for to writhe.

You'll want to know: कळवळणे.
कळवळणे — kaḷvaḷṇe — "to ache with pity".
भुकेल्या मुलाला पाहून आईचा जीव कळवळला. bhukelyā mulālā pāhūn āīcā jīv kaḷvaḷlā. Seeing the hungry child, the mother's heart was wrung.
Past (he …): तो कळवळला, to kaḷvaḷlā.
Usually of the जीव or the मन: pity that hurts.

The word, taken apart.
कळवळणे (kaḷvaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled syllable; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
जगणे (jagṇe) — to stay alive; to live.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to writhe, then the Marathi for to ache with pity.

You'll want to know: जगणे.
जगणे — jagṇe — "to stay alive; to live".
पाण्याशिवाय कोणीही जगू शकत नाही. pāṇyāśivāy koṇīhī jagū śakat nāhī. No one can live without water.
Past (he …): तो जगला, to jaglā.
Staying alive, where राहणे is living somewhere.

The word, taken apart.
जगणे (jagṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled; CDIAL lists the older Marathi verb of living, jiṇẽ, under Sanskrit jīvati (CDIAL 5241).

Lesson 5 of 5.
डगमगणे (ḍagmagṇe) — to wobble; to waver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to ache with pity, then the Marathi for to stay alive.

You'll want to know: डगमगणे.
डगमगणे — ḍagmagṇe — "to wobble; to waver".
संकटातही तिचा धीर डगमगला नाही. saṅkaṭātahī ticā dhīr ḍagmaglā nāhī. Even in the crisis her courage did not waver.
Past (he …): तो डगमगला, to ḍagmaglā.
Of a boat, a step or resolve: पाय डगमगणे (ḍagmagṇe), to stagger.

The word, taken apart.
डगमगणे (ḍagmagṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ḍagmagṇẽ, "to tremble", under the expressive ḍag (CDIAL 5522).
