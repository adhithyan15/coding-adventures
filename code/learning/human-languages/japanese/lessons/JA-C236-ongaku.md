## You'll want to know: おんがく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おんがく** — *ongaku* — "music".

> **きのう ともだちと おんがくを ききました。** — *kinō tomodachi to ongaku o kikimashita* — "Yesterday I listened to music with a friend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おんがく** (*ongaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *on*, "sound" (Middle Chinese *'im*) and *gaku*, "music" (*ngaewk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
