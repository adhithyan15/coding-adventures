Japanese, chapter 314: Everything Has Changed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
すっかり (sukkari) — completely; quite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the other day, then the Japanese for ruins.

You'll want to know: すっかり.
すっかり — sukkari — "completely; quite".
まちは すっかり かわりました。 — machi wa sukkari kawarimashita — "The town has changed completely."

The word, taken apart.
すっかり (sukkari) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 4 of 5.
へる (heru) — to decrease; to go down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for before one knows it, then the Japanese for before long.

You'll want to know: へる.
へる — heru — "to decrease; to go down".
いなかの ひとが へっています。 — inaka no hito ga hette imasu — "The number of people in the countryside is falling."
Polite へります, herimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): むらの ひとが へりました。, mura no hito ga herimashita.

The word, taken apart.
へる (heru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 5 of 5.
おもいつく (omoitsuku) — to think of; to hit upon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for before long, then the Japanese for to decrease.

You'll want to know: おもいつく.
おもいつく — omoitsuku — "to think of; to hit upon".
いい ほうほうを おもいつきました。 — ii hōhō o omoitsukimashita — "I have had a good idea how to do it."
Polite おもいつきます, omoitsukimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): ちちが いい ほうほうを おもいつきました。, chichi ga ii hōhō o omoitsukimashita.

The word, taken apart.
おもいつく (omoitsuku) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from omoi, "thinking" (from omou), and tsuku, "to arrive at" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
