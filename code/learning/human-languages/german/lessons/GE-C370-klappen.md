## You'll want to know: klappen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C370-KLAPPEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**klappen** — "to work out (informal); to fold".

> Mit der neuen Wohnung hat es endlich geklappt.
>
> *It finally worked out with the new flat.*

Informal and very common: *Das klappt nicht* — that does not work. Literally to fold or flap: *den Sitz nach vorne klappen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*klappen* imitates a sharp sound; EtymDB lists English *clap* and Dutch *klappen* as cousins. It is usually said to have come into the standard language from Low German. The sense "to work out" is usually explained as things snapping neatly into place.
