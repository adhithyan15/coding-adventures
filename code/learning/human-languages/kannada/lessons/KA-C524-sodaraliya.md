# ಸೋದರಳಿಯ (sōdaraḷiya) — a nephew

## You'll want to know: ಸೋದರಳಿಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೋದರಳಿಯ** — *sōdaraḷiya* — "a nephew".

> ನನ್ನ ಸೋದರಳಿಯ ಕಾಲೇಜಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಓದುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *nanna sōdaraḷiya kālējinalli ōduttāne.*
>
> My nephew studies at college.

A sister's son: ಸೋದರ, sibling + ಅಳಿಯ. A niece is ಸೋದರ ಸೊಸೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೋದರಳಿಯ** (*sōdaraḷiya*) joins Sanskrit *sodara*, "born of the same womb" (Monier-Williams), and the native *aḷiya*, "son-in-law; sister's son" (DEDR 2410).
