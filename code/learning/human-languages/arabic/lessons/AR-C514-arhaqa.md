## You'll want to know: أرهق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C514-TAAB514-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أرهق** — *arhaqa* — "to wear out".

> أرهقني العمل الطويل.
>
> *arhaqanī al-ʿamal aṭ-ṭawīl.*
>
> The long work wore me out.

Present: يرهق, *yurhiq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أرهق** (*arhaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-h-q*, "to come upon, to overburden" (Lane). It is Form IV: to put too heavy a load on someone.
