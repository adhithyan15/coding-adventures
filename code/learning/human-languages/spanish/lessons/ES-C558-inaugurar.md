## You'll want to know: inaugurar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C558-HOMENAJE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**inaugurar** — "to open, to inaugurate".

> El alcalde inauguró la nueva biblioteca.
>
> *The mayor opened the new library.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inaugurar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inaugurāre* 'to take omens from the flight of birds' (Lewis & Short), built on *augur*, the priest who read the birds before anything new began.
