Portuguese, chapter 120: Ankle, Wrist, Cheek, Lips, Chest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o tornozelo — an ankle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a dolphin, then the Portuguese for an elbow.

You'll want to know: o tornozelo.
o tornozelo — "an ankle".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o tornozelo mean? ("An ankle".) Say it once more.
