## You'll want to know: jiàoxun
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C356-MISS356-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàoxun* — "hard lesson learned".

> *wǒ juéde zhè shì yí ge hěn hǎo de jiàoxun* — I think that's a good lesson to learn.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàoxun* is built from *jiào* and *xùn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewH* and *xjunH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to teach' and the second 'to admonish'. The second is said light here.
