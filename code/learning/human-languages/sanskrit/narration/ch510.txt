Sanskrit, chapter 510: Clearing the Clutter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अवकरः (avakaraḥ) — rubbish; sweepings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for trickles, then the Sanskrit for a repair.

You'll want to know: अवकरः.
अवकरः — avakaraḥ — "rubbish; sweepings".
प्रकोष्ठे बहु अवकरः अस्ति। prakoṣṭhe bahu avakaraḥ asti. There is a lot of rubbish in the room.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अवकरः (avakaraḥ) is built on ava, "down", and the root √kṝ, "to scatter"; MW gives avakara as dust or sweepings (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उत्सारयति (utsārayati) — clears away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a repair, then the Sanskrit for rubbish.

You'll want to know: उत्सारयति.
उत्सारयति — utsārayati — "clears away".
माता अवकरं सम्मार्जन्या उत्सारयति। mātā avakaraṃ sammārjanyā utsārayati. Mother clears away the rubbish with a broom.
Past (she …): सा अवकरम् उत्सारितवती, sā avakaram utsāritavatī.

The word, taken apart.
उत्सारयति (utsārayati) is built on ud, "out", and the causative of the root √sṛ, "to run, to flow"; MW gives utsārayati as to drive away, to put or throw away (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
व्यवस्थापयति (vyavasthāpayati) — puts in order.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rubbish, then the Sanskrit for clears away.

You'll want to know: व्यवस्थापयति.
व्यवस्थापयति — vyavasthāpayati — "puts in order".
सः पुस्तकानि व्यवस्थापयति। saḥ pustakāni vyavasthāpayati. He puts the books in order.
Past (he …): सः पुस्तकानि व्यवस्थापितवान्, saḥ pustakāni vyavasthāpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
व्यवस्थापयति (vyavasthāpayati) is built on vi, "apart", ava, "down", and the causative of the root √sthā, "to stand": to set each thing down in its place; MW gives the causative vyavasthāpayati as to put down, to place, to fix (MW).

गृहस्य बहिः अवकरस्य पुञ्जः अस्ति। gṛhasya bahiḥ avakarasya puñjaḥ asti. There is a heap of rubbish outside the house.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पुञ्जः (puñjaḥ) is the word MW records for a heap, a mass, a multitude; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सम्मार्जनम् (sammārjanam) — a thorough cleaning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for puts in order, then the Sanskrit for a pile.

You'll want to know: सम्मार्जनम्.
सम्मार्जनम् — sammārjanam — "a thorough cleaning".
उत्सवात् पूर्वं गृहस्य सम्मार्जनं भवति। utsavāt pūrvaṃ gṛhasya sammārjanaṃ bhavati. Before the festival the house gets a thorough cleaning.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
सम्मार्जनम् (sammārjanam) is built on sam, "thoroughly", and the root √mṛj, "to wipe, to clean", which also gives mārjayati; MW gives sammārjana as sweeping or cleansing thoroughly (MW).
