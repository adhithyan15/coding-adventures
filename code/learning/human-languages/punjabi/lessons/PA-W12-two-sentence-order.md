# Name first, language second

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

The old connected reading gave you these two facts about Aman. This lesson
changes only their order for a reader meeting Aman on the page.

## Reading — two familiar chunks
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

> ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਂ ਅਮਨ ਹੈ।

Both sentences are already taught. Point to the name sentence, then to the
language sentence. The reader needs to know who **ਮੈਂ** is.

## Writing — order before composing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਂ ਅਮਨ ਹੈ।** first and **ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** second. Do not
join them or change their words. One sentence boundary is the whole task.
