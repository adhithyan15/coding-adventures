## HL-C328 — the Hindi vowel-sign lesson declares मात्रा

After the child-form repair, **मात्रा** is the next independent modern Hindi
term in the ranked glossed-but-never-taught queue. The Chapter 2 writing lesson
already defines it as the name for a Devanagari vowel sign and connects its
literal “measure” meaning to the way a sign measures a syllable.

The lesson asks learners to say **मात्रा** in guided practice and assesses its
meaning and etymological family again in wrap-up recall. Its old headword
declared only the example signs **ा** and **े**, leaving their explicitly taught
category name invisible to reports and downstream cards.

Declare **ा, े / मात्रा** as the lesson headword, align the romanization with
the term, and regenerate the derived Hindi artifacts. This is a metadata repair
for existing teaching, not a new vocabulary claim.
