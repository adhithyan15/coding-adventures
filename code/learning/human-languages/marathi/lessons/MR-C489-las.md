## You'll want to know: लस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C489-LAS489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लस** — *las* — "a vaccine".

> लहान बाळांना वेळेवर लस द्यायला हवी.
>
> *lahān bāḷānnā veḷevar las dyāylā havī.*
>
> Small babies should be vaccinated on time.

Feminine.

लस देणे, to vaccinate; लस घेणे, to get vaccinated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लस** (*las*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *las*, "serum, pus", under the reconstructed *\*lasa*, "stickiness" (CDIAL 10992). The sense "vaccine" is modern, usually explained by the serum vaccines were made from.
