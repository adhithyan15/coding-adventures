## You'll want to know: वार्ताहर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C311-LEKH311-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्ताहर** — *vārtāhar* — "a reporter; a correspondent".

> वार्ताहराने अपघाताची माहिती फोनवर दिली.
>
> *vārtāharāne apghātācī māhitī phonvar dilī.*
>
> The reporter gave the details of the accident over the phone.

Masculine.

Built from वार्ता, news, and -हर, carrier.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्ताहर** (*vārtāhar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vārttāhara*, 'a carrier of tidings, a messenger' (Monier-Williams).
