## You'll want to know: めいれい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C455-TEAM455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**めいれい** — *meirei* — "a command; an order".

> **それは しゃちょうの めいれいです。** — *sore wa shachō no meirei desu* — "That is the company president's order."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めいれい** (*meirei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mei*, "command" (Middle Chinese *mjaengH*) and *rei*, "orders" (*ljengH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
