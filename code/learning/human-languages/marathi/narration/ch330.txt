Marathi, chapter 330: The Army in Camp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सेनापती (senāpatī) — a commander.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cannon, then the Marathi for cavalry.

You'll want to know: सेनापती.
सेनापती — senāpatī — "a commander".
सेनापतीने सैन्याला थांबायला सांगितलं. senāpatīne sainyālā thāmbāylā sāṅgitlaṁ. The commander told the army to halt.
Masculine.
सेना, an army, + पती, a lord.

The word, taken apart.
सेनापती (senāpatī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit senāpati, 'the lord of an army, a general' (Monier-Williams).

पायदळ — pāydaḷ — "infantry".
पायदळ घोडदळाच्या मागे चालत होतं. pāydaḷ ghoḍdaḷācyā māge cālat hotaṁ. The infantry was marching behind the cavalry.

The word, taken apart.
पायदळ (pāydaḷ) is built from pāy, 'a foot', and daḷ, 'a troop', from Sanskrit dala (Monier-Williams): the troop on foot.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पहारा देणे (pahārā deṇe) — to stand guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for infantry, then the Marathi for an army camp.

You'll want to know: पहारा देणे.
पहारा देणे — pahārā deṇe — "to stand guard".
दोन सैनिकांनी रात्रभर दारावर पहारा दिला. don sainikānnī rātrabhar dārāvar pahārā dilā. Two soldiers stood guard at the gate all night.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पहारा दिला, tyāne pahārā dilā.
The place guarded takes -वर; पहारा is masculine, so the past is पहारा दिला.

The word, taken apart.
पहारा देणे (pahārā deṇe) is built from pahārā, 'a watch, a guard', of the family of Sanskrit prahara, 'a watch of about three hours' (CDIAL 8900), and deṇe, 'to give'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
झुंजणे (jhuñjṇe) — to battle; to fight hard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an army camp, then the Marathi for to stand guard.

You'll want to know: झुंजणे.
झुंजणे — jhuñjṇe — "to battle; to fight hard".
मूठभर सैनिक मोठ्या सैन्याशी झुंजले. mūṭhbhar sainik moṭhyā sainyāśī jhuñjle. A handful of soldiers battled a large army.
Past (he …): तो झुंजला, to jhuñjlā.
The opponent takes -शी: शत्रूशी झुंजणे (jhuñjṇe).

The word, taken apart.
झुंजणे (jhuñjṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi jhuñjṇe, 'to fight', under Sanskrit yudhyate, 'fights against' (CDIAL 10502).
