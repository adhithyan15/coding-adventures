Malayalam, chapter 411: Because and Therefore.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
അതിനാൽ (atināl) — therefore; so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for relevant, then the Malayalam for irrelevant.

You'll want to know: അതിനാൽ.
അതിനാൽ — atināl — "therefore; so".
മഴ പെയ്തു, അതിനാൽ കളി നിർത്തി. maḻa peytu, atināl kaḷi nirtti. It rained, so the game was stopped.
A written, formal word beside the everyday അതുകൊണ്ട്.

The word, taken apart.
അതിനാൽ (atināl) is a native Malayalam formation: അത്, "that" (DEDR 1), with -ഇനാൽ, the instrumental ending: "by that".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ആയതിനാൽ (āyatināl) — hence; therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for irrelevant, then the Malayalam for therefore.

You'll want to know: ആയതിനാൽ.
ആയതിനാൽ — āyatināl — "hence; therefore".
അവന് പനിയാണ്, ആയതിനാൽ ഇന്ന് വരില്ല. avanŭ paniyāṇŭ, āyatināl innŭ varilla. He has a fever; hence he will not come today.
Formal, common in letters and notices.

The word, taken apart.
ആയതിനാൽ (āyatināl) is a native Malayalam formation: ആയത്, "that which became", from ആകുക, "to become" (DEDR 333), with the instrumental ending -ഇനാൽ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
മൂലം (mūlaṁ) — because of; due to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for therefore, then the Malayalam for hence.

You'll want to know: മൂലം.
മൂലം — mūlaṁ — "because of; due to".
മഴ മൂലം ബസ് വൈകി. maḻa mūlaṁ basŭ vaiki. The bus was late because of the rain.
It follows the noun directly: മഴ മൂലം (mūlaṁ), because of the rain.

The word, taken apart.
മൂലം (mūlaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit mūla, "a root" (CDIAL 10250). What a thing grows from is its cause.

Lesson 4 of 5.
യഥാർത്ഥത്തിൽ (yathārtthattil) — actually; in fact.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for hence, then the Malayalam for because of.

You'll want to know: യഥാർത്ഥത്തിൽ.
യഥാർത്ഥത്തിൽ — yathārtthattil — "actually; in fact".
യഥാർത്ഥത്തിൽ അവൻ ആ പരീക്ഷ എഴുതിയില്ല. yathārtthattil avan ā parīkṣa eḻutiyilla. Actually, he did not sit that exam.
The locative -ത്തിൽ on യഥാർത്ഥം, the real thing: in the real thing.

The word, taken apart.
യഥാർത്ഥത്തിൽ (yathārtthattil) is built from Sanskrit yathārtha, "in keeping with reality" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam locative ending -ത്തിൽ: "in reality".

Lesson 5 of 5.
വാസ്തവത്തിൽ (vāstavattil) — in reality; truly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for because of, then the Malayalam for actually.

You'll want to know: വാസ്തവത്തിൽ.
വാസ്തവത്തിൽ — vāstavattil — "in reality; truly".
വാസ്തവത്തിൽ അവൾക്ക് ആ ജോലി ഇഷ്ടമായിരുന്നില്ല. vāstavattil avaḷkkŭ ā jōli iṣṭamāyirunnilla. In reality she did not like that job.
A close partner of യഥാർത്ഥത്തിൽ (yathārtthattil); both open a sentence that corrects a belief.

The word, taken apart.
വാസ്തവത്തിൽ (vāstavattil) is built from Sanskrit vāstava, "real, true, genuine" (Monier-Williams), made from vastu, "a thing", with the Malayalam locative ending -ത്തിൽ.
