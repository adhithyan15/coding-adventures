## You'll want to know: das Programm
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C396-COMPUTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Programm** — "the program; the programme".

> Mit diesem Programm kann man Fotos bearbeiten.
>
> *You can edit photos with this program.*

Plural: *die Programme*. Also a TV channel or a schedule: *das Fernsehprogramm*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Programm* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *programma* "a proclamation, an edict" (Lewis & Short), from Greek *prógramma* "a public notice", built from *pro* "before" and *gráphein* (EtymDB).
