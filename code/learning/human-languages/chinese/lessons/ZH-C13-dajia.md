# 大家 — everyone on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-DA-01, ZH-SCRIPT-JIA-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Write **大**, then **家** with a small gap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-DAJIA-01] -->

> **大家** — *dàjiā* — **everyone**

Close the gap. Read the two characters as one word and keep both tone shapes.
