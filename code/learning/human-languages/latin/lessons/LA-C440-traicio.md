## You'll want to know: trāiciō, trāicere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C440-FLUCTUO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**trāiciō, trāicere** — "to take across, to cross over; to throw across".

> Mīlitēs equōs nāvibus trāns flūmen trāiciunt.
>
> *The soldiers take their horses across the river in boats.*

*trāiciō, trāicere, trāiēcī, trāiectum*. *Mare trāicere*, to cross the sea. English *trajectory* comes from its participle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trāiciō* is built from *trāns* 'across' and *iaciō* 'to throw' (Lewis & Short). English *trajectory* comes from its participle *trāiectus*.
