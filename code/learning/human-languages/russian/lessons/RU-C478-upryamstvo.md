## You'll want to know: упрямство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C478-STUBBORN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**упрямство** (*upryámstvo*) — "stubbornness".

> Его упрямство нас удивило.
>
> *Yegó upryámstvo nas udivílo.*
>
> His stubbornness surprised us.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*упрямство* is built on *упрямый* 'stubborn' with *-ство*; EtymDB has no entry for the noun, and the origin of *упрямый* is not settled.
