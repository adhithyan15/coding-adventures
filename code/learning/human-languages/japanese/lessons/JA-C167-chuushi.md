## You'll want to know: ちゅうし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C167-DONE167-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうし** — *chūshi* — "calling off; suspension".

> **あしたの まつりは ちゅうしでしょう。** — *ashita no matsuri wa chūshi deshō* — "Tomorrow's festival will probably be called off."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうし** (*chūshi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chū*, "middle" (Middle Chinese *trjuwng*) and *shi*, "stop" (*tsyiX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
