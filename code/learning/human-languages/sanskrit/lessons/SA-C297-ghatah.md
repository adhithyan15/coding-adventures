## You'll want to know: घटः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C297-NIRVAPANA297-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घटः** — *ghaṭaḥ* — "a water jar".

> जनाः घटैः जलम् आनयन्ति।
>
> *janāḥ ghaṭaiḥ jalam ānayanti.*
>
> People bring water in jars.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घटः** (*ghaṭaḥ*) MW gives *ghaṭa* as a jar or pitcher, a large earthen water-jar, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
