## You'll want to know: درگذشتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C337-MARG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**درگذشتن** (*dargozashtan*) — "to pass away".

> مادربزرگم سال گذشته درگذشت.
>
> *Mâdarbozorgam sâl-e gozashte dargozasht.*
>
> My grandmother passed away last year.

Respectful. Present stem *gozar-*: *dar migozarad*. The noun is *dargozasht* "a passing".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dargozashtan* is the native verb *gozashtan* "to pass" with the prefix *dar*: to pass on. EtymDB lists no entry.
