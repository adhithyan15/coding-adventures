## You'll want to know: لمس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C468-SMELL468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**لمس** — *lams* — "the sense of touch".

> ماں کے لمس سے بچہ سو گیا۔
>
> *māṅ ke lams se bachchā so gayā.*
>
> At his mother's touch the baby fell asleep.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لمس** (*lams*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *lams*, "touching".
