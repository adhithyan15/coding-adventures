## You'll want to know: تعافى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C512-SHIFA512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تعافى** — *taʿāfā* — "to recover".

> تعافى اللاعب وعاد إلى الفريق.
>
> *taʿāfā al-lāʿib wa-ʿāda ilā al-farīq.*
>
> The player recovered and went back to the team.

Present: يتعافى, *yataʿāfā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعافى** (*taʿāfā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-f-w*, "to efface; to pardon; to give health" (Lane). ʿAfwan, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
