## You'll want to know: આરંભવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C388-UPAKRAM388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આરંભવું** — *ārambhvũ* — "to commence".

> જો બધા સમયસર આવશે તો આપણે કાર્યક્રમ આરંભીશું.
>
> *jo badhā samaysar āvśe to āpaṇe kāryakram ārambhīśũ.*
>
> If everyone arrives on time, we will begin the programme.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નવું કામ આરંભ્યું, *teṇe navũ kām ārambhyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આરંભવું** (*ārambhvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *ārambha*, "undertaking, beginning" (Monier-Williams), with the Gujarati verb ending.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
