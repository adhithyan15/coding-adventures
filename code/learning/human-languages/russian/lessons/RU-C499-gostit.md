## You'll want to know: гостить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C499-VISITS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**гостить** (*gostít*) — "to stay as a guest".

> Летом я гостила у тёти в деревне.
>
> *Létom ya gostíla u tyóti v derévne.*
>
> In the summer I stayed with my aunt in the village.

Imperfective; present гощу, гостишь (ст becomes щ). *Гостить у кого*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гостить* is built on *гость* 'guest'; EtymDB lists it, glossed 'to be on a visit, to stay', without a source.
