Sanskrit, chapter 360: Honour and Dishonour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
यशः (yaśaḥ) — fame; glory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for craving, then the Sanskrit for dedicates.

You'll want to know: यशः.
यशः — yaśaḥ — "fame; glory".
लेखकस्य यशः सर्वत्र अस्ति। lekhakasya yaśaḥ sarvatra asti. The writer's fame is everywhere.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
यशः (yaśaḥ) MW gives yaśas "honour, glory, fame, renown" and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for standing, then the Sanskrit for arrogance.

You'll want to know: अपमानः.
अपमानः — apamānaḥ — "an insult; dishonour".
सः अपमानं सहते, किन्तु न विस्मरति। saḥ apamānaṃ sahate, kintu na vismarati. He bears an insult but does not forget it.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अपमानः (apamānaḥ) is built on apa, "away", and māna, "honour", from the root √man; MW gives apa-māna "disrespect, contempt, disgrace" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रतारयति (pratārayati) — misleads; deceives.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for arrogance, then the Sanskrit for an insult.

You'll want to know: प्रतारयति.
प्रतारयति — pratārayati — "misleads; deceives".
धूर्तः जनान् प्रतारयति। dhūrtaḥ janān pratārayati. The rogue misleads people.
Past (he …): सः मित्रं प्रतारितवान्, saḥ mitraṃ pratāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतारयति (pratārayati) is built on pra and the root √tṝ, "to cross", in its causative form; MW gives pra-√tṝ "to mislead, to take in, to deceive" and pra-tārita "misled, deceived" (MW).
