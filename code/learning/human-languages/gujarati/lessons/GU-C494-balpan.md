## You'll want to know: બાળપણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C494-BALPAN494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બાળપણ** — *bāḷpaṇ* — "childhood".

> દાદા પોતાના બાળપણની વાર્તા કહે છે.
>
> *dādā potānā bāḷpaṇnī vārtā kahe che.*
>
> Grandfather tells stories of his childhood.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બાળપણ** (*bāḷpaṇ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*bālatvana-*, "childhood", built on Sanskrit *bāla*, "a child"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9218).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
