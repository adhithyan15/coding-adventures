## You'll want to know: पौष्टिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C495-KASRAT495-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पौष्टिक** — *pauṣṭik* — "nourishing".

> दाल और सब्ज़ी पौष्टिक खाना है।
>
> *dāl aur sabzī pauṣṭik khānā hai.*
>
> Lentils and vegetables are nourishing food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पौष्टिक** (*pauṣṭik*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pauṣṭika*, "nourishing, invigorating", from *puṣṭi*, "growth" (Monier-Williams).
