Marathi, chapter 164: To announce, To clap, To cross, To increase, To reduce.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जाहीर करणे (jāhīr karṇe) — to announce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to check, then the Marathi for to sign.

You'll want to know: जाहीर करणे.
जाहीर करणे — jāhīr karṇe — "to announce".

The word, taken apart.
जाहीर is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic ẓāhir, "evident, manifest", whose root is ẓ-h-r (Lane). करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 2 of 5.
टाळ्या वाजवणे (ṭāḷyā vājavṇe) — to clap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to sign, then the Marathi for to announce.

You'll want to know: टाळ्या वाजवणे.
टाळ्या वाजवणे — ṭāḷyā vājavṇe — "to clap".

The word, taken apart.
टाळ्या is the plural of टाळी, "a clap", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit tāḍa, "blow", whose form tāla meant "clap, rhythm" (CDIAL 5748). वाजवणे is the "make" form of वाजणे, "to sound", from Sanskrit vādyate, "is made to sound" (CDIAL 11513).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ओलांडणे (olāṇḍṇe) — to cross.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to announce, then the Marathi for to clap.

You'll want to know: ओलांडणे.
ओलांडणे — olāṇḍṇe — "to cross".

The word, taken apart.
ओलांडणे (olāṇḍṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from avalaṇḍayati, "crosses", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 825).

वाढवणे (vāḍhavṇe) is the "make" form of वाढणे, "to grow", from Sanskrit vardhate, "grows, increases" (CDIAL 11376); Sanskrit had its own "make" form, vardhayati, "makes grow" (CDIAL 11382).
