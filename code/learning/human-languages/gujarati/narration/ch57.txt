Lesson 2 of 5.
વેચવું (vecvũ) — to sell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to learn, then the Gujarati for to buy.

You'll want to know: વેચવું.
વેચવું (vecvũ) — "to sell".

The word, taken apart.
વેચવું (vecvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vetyayati, "makes a price", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12100).

Lesson 4 of 5.
રૂપિયા (rūpiyā) — rupees.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sell, then the Gujarati for a price.

You'll want to know: રૂપિયા.
રૂપિયા (rūpiyā) — "rupees". The પૈસા of India.

The word, taken apart.
રૂપિયા (rūpiyā) is usually traced to Sanskrit rūpya, "bearing a stamp; silver", built on rūpa, "form" (CDIAL 10805). CDIAL reconstructs a variant rūpiya beside it, and lists Gujarati rūpũ, "silver".
