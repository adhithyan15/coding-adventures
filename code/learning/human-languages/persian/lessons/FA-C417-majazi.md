## You'll want to know: مجازی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C417-SHABAKE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مجازی** (*majâzi*) — "virtual, online".

> این ترم کلاسها مجازی است.
>
> *in term kelâshâ majâzi ast.*
>
> This term the classes are online.

*Fazâ-ye majâzi* — cyberspace, the internet. It also means figurative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجازی** is built on *majâz* "figure of speech, a non-literal sense", usually traced to Arabic *majāz*, with the suffix *-i*: not real in the literal sense.
