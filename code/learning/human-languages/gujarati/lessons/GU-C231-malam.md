## You'll want to know: મલમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C231-MEDICINE231-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મલમ** — *malam* — "ointment, balm".

> **શું આ મલમ સારો છે?** — *śũ ā malam sāro che?* — "Is this ointment good?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મલમ** (*malam*) is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *marham*, "ointment", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *r-h-m* (Lane).
