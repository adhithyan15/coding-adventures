## You'll want to know: жертва
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C287-PRESTUPLENIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**жертва** (*zhértva*) — "a victim; a sacrifice".

> Жертва ничего не помнила.
>
> *Zhértva nichevó ne pómnila.*
>
> The victim remembered nothing.

Feminine, even of a man: *он стал жертвой обмана* — he fell victim to a fraud.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*жертва* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *žĭrtva* "a sacrifice", built on *žĭrti* "to offer, to sacrifice", which EtymDB traces to PIE *gʷerH-*.
