## You'll want to know: موكب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C328-HAFL328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**موكب** — *mawkib* — "a procession".

> مر موكب العرس في الشارع.
>
> *marra mawkib al-ʿurs fī ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> The wedding procession went down the street.

Plural: مواكب, *mawākib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**موكب** (*mawkib*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-k-b* (Lane), "to walk along in a grave, leisurely way". A *mawkib* is a company moving at that pace.
