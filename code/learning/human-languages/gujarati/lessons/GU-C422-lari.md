## You'll want to know: લારી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C422-STALL422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લારી** — *lārī* — "a handcart (a street seller's)".

> શાકની લારી રોજ અમારી શેરીમાં આવે છે.
>
> *śāknī lārī roj amārī śerīmā̃ āve che.*
>
> The vegetable cart comes into our street every day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લારી** (*lārī*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
