Mandarin Chinese, chapter 204: Online.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
网站 (wǎngzhàn) — website.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a deposit paid in advance, then the Mandarin for contract.

You'll want to know: 网站.
网站 — wǎngzhàn — "website".
这个网站关了 zhège wǎngzhàn guān le — This website has closed down.

The word, taken apart.
网站 (wǎngzhàn) is built from 网 'net' and 站 'a station', both inherited from Middle Chinese, mjangX and treamH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
dēnglù — to log in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for contract, then the Mandarin for website.

You'll want to know: dēnglù.
dēnglù — "to log in".
nǐ dēnglù le ma — Have you logged in?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dēnglù is built from dēng 'to go up' and lù 'to record', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tong and ljowk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to climb' and the second 'to take down'. Logging in is a modern sense, and how the pair came to it is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
shìpín — video.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to log in, then the Mandarin for to click.

You'll want to know: shìpín.
shìpín — "video".
wǒ xǐhuan kàn shìpín — I like watching videos.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shìpín is built from shì 'to look at' and pín 'frequent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyijH and bjin (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The word is modern, and how the pair came to mean a video is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
打字 (dǎzì) — to type.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to click, then the Mandarin for video.

You'll want to know: 打字.
打字 — dǎzì — "to type".
他打字很快 tā dǎzì hěn kuài — He types fast.

The word, taken apart.
打字 (dǎzì) is built from 打 'to strike' and 字 'a character', both inherited from Middle Chinese, taengX and dziH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): striking out characters. The modern dǎ is not the regular outcome of taengX, and how it came is not settled.
