Italian, chapter 465: Piccoli animali.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la lucertola — the lizard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the falcon, then the Italian for to graze.

You'll want to know: la lucertola.
la lucertola — "the lizard".
Una lucertola prende il sole sul muro. A lizard is sunbathing on the wall.
Very common on old walls in summer.

The word, taken apart.
lucertola is inherited from Latin lacertula, a little lacerta 'lizard' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
strisciare — to slither, to crawl; to swipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the toad, then the Italian for the snail.

You'll want to know: strisciare.
strisciare — "to slither, to crawl; to swipe".
Il serpente striscia tra i sassi. The snake slithers among the stones.
Strisciare la carta — to swipe a card.

The word, taken apart.
strisciare is built on striscia 'stripe, trail', which EtymDB traces to Latin stria 'furrow'; it is inherited.
