# في الوقت نفسه (fī al-waqt nafsih) — at the same time

## You'll want to know: في الوقت نفسه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C327-LAYLA327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**في الوقت نفسه** — *fī al-waqt nafsih* — "at the same time".

> تطبخ أمي وتتكلم في الهاتف في الوقت نفسه.
>
> *taṭbukhu ummī wa-tatakallamu fī al-hātif fī al-waqt nafsih.*
>
> My mother cooks and talks on the phone at the same time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**في الوقت نفسه** (*fī al-waqt nafsih*) is "in the time itself": *waqt* is built on the native Arabic root *w-q-t* (Lane), and *nafs*, "self, soul", on *n-f-s*.
