# ಎರಡು — two, and the softening that runs through Kannada

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C07-ONDU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] One was *ondu*. Say it before you meet the next one.

## You'll want to know: ಎರಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C07-ERADU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಎರಡು** (*eraḍu*) — "**two**."

Tamil says *iraṇṭu*. Hold the two beside each other and one difference does all
the work: where Tamil keeps a hard cluster **ṇṭ**, Kannada wears it down to a
single soft **ḍ**.

That softening is not a one-off for "two". It is a habit of the language, and
you will hear it again at *aidu* — "five" — in three lessons.
