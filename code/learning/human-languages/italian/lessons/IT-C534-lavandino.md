## You'll want to know: il lavandino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C534-PIATTI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il lavandino** — "sink, washbasin".

> Il lavandino della cucina è pieno di piatti.
>
> *The kitchen sink is full of dishes.*

Also *il lavello* in the kitchen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lavandino* is built on *lavanda* "washing", from Latin *lavanda* "things to be washed", from *lavāre* "to wash" (Lewis & Short).
