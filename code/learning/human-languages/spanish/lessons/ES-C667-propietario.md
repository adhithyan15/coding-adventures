## You'll want to know: el propietario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C667-APARTAMENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el propietario** — "the proprietor, the owner; the landlord".

> El propietario nos da las llaves.
>
> *The owner gives us the keys.*

A woman is *la propietaria*. In speech *el dueño* is common.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*propietario* is a learned borrowing from Latin *proprietārius* "an owner, a proprietor" (Lewis & Short), built on *proprius* "own".
