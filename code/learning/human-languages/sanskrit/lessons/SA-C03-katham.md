# कथम् (katham) — "how," another of the ka- questions

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-KIM] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You met *kim* ("what") in Chapter 2. Here is its sibling in
the question-family.

## Sounds you'll need

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-KATHAM]; assesses=[] -->

*ka · tham*. The *th* is one sound — a *t* with breath after it, never
the *th* of English *thin*.

## The word, taken apart

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-ETYMON-KATHAM-INTERROGATIVE]; assesses=[] -->

*katham* ("how, in what way") is from the interrogative stem *ka-*, PIE **\*kʷo-** — the same root that gave *kim* and, in the west, English
**wh-** and Latin *qu-*. It heads the same *ka-* question-family: *kim* (what),
*kaḥ* (who), *kadā* (when), *kutra* (where), *katham* (how). From *katham* comes
the abstract noun *kathā* ("a telling, a story" — "the how of a thing"), and,
worn down, Hindi's *kaise*.
