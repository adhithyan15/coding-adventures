## You'll want to know: perdiscō, perdiscere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C402-EDISCO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**perdiscō, perdiscere** — "to learn thoroughly".

> Artem medicīnae perdidicit.
>
> *He learned the art of medicine thoroughly.*

*perdiscō, perdiscere, perdidicī*: *per-* through + *discō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perdiscō* is built from *per-* "through, thoroughly" and *discō* "to learn" (Lewis & Short).
