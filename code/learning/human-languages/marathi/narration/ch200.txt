Lesson 1 of 5.
स्नायू (snāyū) — a muscle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to knit, then the Marathi for to harvest.

You'll want to know: स्नायू.
स्नायू — snāyū — "a muscle".

The word, taken apart.
स्नायू (snāyū) is Sanskrit snāyu, "tendon, muscle, sinew", a tatsama. CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi form as sāv, "muscle, sinew, catgut" (CDIAL 13793).

Lesson 5 of 5.
चिठ्ठी (ciṭṭhī) — a note; a prescription.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a scar, then the Marathi for a head cold.

You'll want to know: चिठ्ठी.
चिठ्ठी — ciṭṭhī — "a note; a prescription".

The word, taken apart.
चिठ्ठी (ciṭṭhī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ciṣṭa, "message", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4832). CDIAL compares Avestan činasti, "teaches, makes known". It glosses the Marathi word "letter".
