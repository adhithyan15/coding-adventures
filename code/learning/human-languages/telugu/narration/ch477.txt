Telugu, chapter 477: The Monsoon.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ఋతుపవనం (ṛtupavanaṁ) — the monsoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for muggy heat, then the Telugu for to heat up.

You'll want to know: ఋతుపవనం.
ఋతుపవనం — ṛtupavanaṁ — "the monsoon".
వేసవి తరువాత ఋతుపవనాలు వస్తాయి. vēsavi taruvāta ṛtupavanālu vastāyi. After the summer the monsoon arrives.
Mostly plural: ఋతుపవనాలు, the monsoon winds. ఋతువు, a season, + పవనం, a wind.

The word, taken apart.
ఋతుపవనం (ṛtupavanaṁ) is a modern compound built from two Sanskrit words: ṛtu, "a season", and pavana, "wind, breeze" (Monier-Williams): the wind of the season.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: వర్షపాతం.
వర్షపాతం — varṣapātaṁ — "rainfall".
ఆ సంవత్సరం వర్షపాతం చాలా తక్కువ. ā saṁvatsaraṁ varṣapātaṁ cālā takkuva. Rainfall that year was very low.
వర్ష, rain, + పాతం, a fall: the amount of rain that falls.

The word, taken apart.
వర్షపాతం (varṣapātaṁ) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: varṣa, "rain" (CDIAL 11392), and pāta, "a fall, a falling" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 6.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ముసురు.
ముసురు — musuru — "a spell of drizzle".
మూడు రోజులుగా ముసురు పట్టింది. mūḍu rōjulugā musuru paṭṭindi. For three days there has been a steady drizzle.
Grey days of light rain that will not stop: ముసురు (musuru) పట్టింది.

The word, taken apart.
ముసురు (musuru) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4914 lists Telugu musuru, "a constant or continued rain".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for rainfall, then the Telugu for a spell of drizzle.

You'll want to know: జల్లు.
జల్లు — jallu — "a shower of rain".
సాయంత్రం ఒక చిన్న జల్లు పడింది. sāyantraṁ oka cinna jallu paḍindi. In the evening there was a short shower.
జల్లు (jallu) పడు, a shower falls; చిరుజల్లు is a light one.

The word, taken apart.
జల్లు (jallu) is native Dravidian, of the family of Telugu callu, "to sprinkle, to scatter" (DEDR 2384).

Lesson 5 of 6.
తడిసిపోవు (taḍisipōvu) — to get soaked.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a spell of drizzle, then the Telugu for a shower of rain.

You'll want to know: తడిసిపోవు.
తడిసిపోవు — taḍisipōvu — "to get soaked".
గొడుగు లేక వర్షంలో తడిసిపోయాను. goḍugu lēka varṣaṁlō taḍisipōyānu. I had no umbrella and got soaked in the rain.
Past (I …): తడిసిపోయాను, taḍisipōyānu.
తడి, wet, + పోవు: soaked through. తడిసి ముద్దయ్యాను, I got drenched.

The word, taken apart.
తడిసిపోవు (taḍisipōvu) is native Dravidian: తడియు, "to get wet", the verb of తడి, "wet" (DEDR 3045), with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
