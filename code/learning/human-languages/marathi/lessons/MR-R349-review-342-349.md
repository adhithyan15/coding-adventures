(**निर्माण होणे**, *nirmāṇ hoṇe*.) A point at issue? (**मुद्दा**, *muddā*.) Logic? (**तर्क**, *tarka*.) A reasoned argument? (**युक्तिवाद**, *yuktivād*.) To put forward? (**मांडणे**, *māṇḍṇe*.) Proof? (**पुरावा**, *purāvā*.) A disagreement? (**मतभेद**, *matbhed*.) To agree? (**सहमत होणे**, *sahmat hoṇe*.) To object? (**आक्षेप घेणे**, *ākṣep gheṇe*.) An inconsistency? (**विसंगती**, *visaṅgtī*.) A standpoint? (**दृष्टिकोन**, *dṛṣṭikon*.) A conclusion? (**निष्कर्ष**, *niṣkarṣa*.) To infer? (**अनुमान काढणे**, *anumān kāḍhṇe*.) A basis? (**आधार**, *ādhār*.) A summary? (**सारांश**, *sārāṁś*.) In short? (**थोडक्यात**, *thoḍkyāt*.) To entertain? (**बाळगणे**, *bāḷagṇe*.) To elucidate? (**विशद करणे**, *viśad karṇe*.) Certainty? (**खात्री**, *khātrī*.) To verify? (**पडताळणे**, *paḍtāḷṇe*.) To reject? (**फेटाळणे**, *pheṭāḷṇe*.) To oppose? (**विरोध करणे**, *virodh karṇe*.) To support? (**पाठिंबा देणे**, *pāṭhimbā deṇe*.) To justify? (**समर्थन करणे**, *samarthan karṇe*.) Neutral? (**तटस्थ**, *taṭastha*.) Against? (**विरुद्ध**, *viruddha*.) A connection? (**संबंध**, *sambandh*.) A similarity? (**साम्य**, *sāmya*.) A context? (**संदर्भ**, *sandarbha*.) Linked? (**निगडित**, *nigḍit*.) On the contrary? (**याउलट**, *yāulaṭ*.) A problem? (**समस्या**, *samasyā*.) A difficulty? (**अडचण**, *aḍcaṇ*.) A remedy? (**उपाय**, *upāy*.) An alternative? (**पर्याय**, *paryāy*.) To unravel? (**उकलणे**, *ukalṇe*.)
