# राज़ी (rāzī) — willing; agreed

## You'll want to know: राज़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C543-VACHAN543-03]; assesses=[] -->

**राज़ी** — *rāzī* — "willing; agreed".

> पिता जी मेरी बात पर राज़ी हो गए।
>
> *Pitā jī merī bāt par rāzī ho gae.*
>
> Father agreed to what I said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राज़ी** (*rāzī*) is usually traced to Persian *rāzī*, "content, consenting".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
