Hindi, chapter 350: For Example.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
उदाहरण (udāharaṇ) — an example.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to admit, then the Hindi for a misunderstanding.

You'll want to know: उदाहरण.
उदाहरण — udāharaṇ — "an example".
शिक्षक ने एक उदाहरण दिया। śikṣak ne ek udāharaṇ diyā. The teacher gave an example.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
उदाहरण (udāharaṇ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit udāharaṇa, "an example, an illustration" (Monier-Williams).

मुझे फल पसंद हैं, मसलन आम और केला। mujhe phal pasand haiṁ, maslan ām aur kelā. I like fruit, for instance mangoes and bananas.

Lesson 5 of 6.
गिनाना (ginānā) — to list, to enumerate (reasons, faults).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for that is to say, then the Hindi for explicit.

You'll want to know: गिनाना.
गिनाना — ginānā — "to list, to enumerate (reasons, faults)".
मीरा ने तीन कारण गिनाए। Mīrā ne tīn kāraṇ gināe. Mira listed three reasons.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कई कारण गिनाए, usne kaī kāraṇ gināe.

The word, taken apart.
गिनाना (ginānā) is the causative of गिनना, "to count", which is inherited from Sanskrit gaṇayati, "counts" (CDIAL 3993): to make the items count out, one by one.
