# ಪುರಾತತ್ವ (purātatva) — archaeology

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: ಪುರಾತತ್ವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C355-UTKHANANA355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪುರಾತತ್ವ** — *purātatva* — "archaeology".

> ಅವಳು ಕಾಲೇಜಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಪುರಾತತ್ವ ಓದುತ್ತಿದ್ದಾಳೆ.
>
> *avaḷu kālējinalli purātatva ōduttiddāḷe.*
>
> She is studying archaeology at college.

ಪುರಾತತ್ವ ಇಲಾಖೆ is the department that looks after old monuments.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪುರಾತತ್ವ** (*purātatva*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of *purā*, "formerly, of old" (Monier-Williams), and *tattva*, "the real state, truth" (CDIAL 5642).
