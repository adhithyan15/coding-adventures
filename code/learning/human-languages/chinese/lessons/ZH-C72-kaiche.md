## You'll want to know: 开车
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-02]; assesses=[] -->

**开车** — *kāichē* — "to drive".

> **明天我们开车去看山** *míngtiān wǒmen kāichē qù kàn shān* — Tomorrow we're driving out to see the mountains.

The new character, in pieces: 开 — two bars, with two legs down through them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开车** (*kāichē*) is built from 开 'to open' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). Driving is not among the senses of the first there; opening up, setting going, is the likely step, but it is not settled.
