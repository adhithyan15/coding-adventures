## You'll want to know: મોજીલું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C462-CANCAL462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મોજીલું** — *mojīlũ* — "fun-loving".

> મારો મોજીલો ભાઈ હંમેશા ગીતો ગાય છે.
>
> *māro mojīlo bhāī hammeśā gīto gāy che.*
>
> My fun-loving brother is always singing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મોજીલું** (*mojīlũ*) is built on *moj*, "fun, high spirits", which is usually traced to Arabic *mawj*, "a wave", with the adjective ending *-īlũ*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
