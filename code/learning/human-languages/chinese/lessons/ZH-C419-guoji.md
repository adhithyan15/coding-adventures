## You'll want to know: guójì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C419-ARRIVE419-04]; assesses=[] -->

*guójì* — "international".

> *qù guójì hángzhànlóu zěnme zǒu* — How do I get to the international terminal?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guójì* is built from 国 'a country' and *jì* 'an edge, a meeting', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwok* and *tsjejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a border' and 'to meet'.
