Telugu, chapter 433: Effort and Strategy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కృషి (kṛṣi) — endeavour; hard work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for credibility, then the Telugu for awareness.

You'll want to know: కృషి.
కృషి — kṛṣi — "endeavour; hard work".
ఆమె కృషి వల్లే ఆ పాఠశాల బాగుపడింది. āme kṛṣi vallē ā pāṭhaśāla bāgupaḍindi. That school got better because of her hard work.
కృషి (kṛṣi) చేయు, to strive.

The word, taken apart.
కృషి (kṛṣi) is borrowed from Sanskrit kṛṣi, "ploughing, cultivation of the soil" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for patient, hard effort.

Lesson 2 of 5.
సవాలు (savālu) — a challenge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for awareness, then the Telugu for endeavour.

You'll want to know: సవాలు.
సవాలు — savālu — "a challenge".
కొత్త ఉద్యోగం నాకు ఒక పెద్ద సవాలు. kotta udyōgaṁ nāku oka pedda savālu. The new job is a big challenge for me.
Plural: సవాళ్ళు, savāḷḷu.
సవాలు (savālu) చేయు, to challenge someone.

The word, taken apart.
సవాలు (savālu) is usually traced to Hindustani savāl, "a question, a demand", a word of Arabic origin; in Telugu it is a challenge.

ఆ ఆటలో వాళ్ళ ఆధిపత్యం కొనసాగింది. ā āṭalō vāḷḷa ādhipatyaṁ konasāgindi. Their dominance in that game continued.

Lesson 5 of 5.
కోల్పోవు (kōlpōvu) — to be deprived of; to lose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a strategy, then the Telugu for dominance.

You'll want to know: కోల్పోవు.
కోల్పోవు — kōlpōvu — "to be deprived of; to lose".
వరదల్లో వాళ్ళు తమ ఇల్లు కోల్పోయారు. varadallō vāḷḷu tama illu kōlpōyāru. They lost their home in the floods.
Past (I …): కోల్పోయాను, kōlpōyānu.
For losing what you had: a chance, a right, a home, a person.

The word, taken apart.
కోల్పోవు (kōlpōvu) is native Telugu, ending in pōvu, "to go, to be lost" (DEDR 4572); the origin of the first part is uncertain.
