## You'll want to know: die Himmelsrichtung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C433-KOMPASS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Himmelsrichtung** — "the point of the compass, the direction".

> Norden, Süden, Osten und Westen sind die vier Himmelsrichtungen.
>
> *North, south, east and west are the four points of the compass.*

Plural: *die Himmelsrichtungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Himmelsrichtung* is built from *Himmel* 'sky, heaven' and *Richtung* 'direction', from *richten* 'to direct'. Both are inherited, and *Richtung* shares its root with *recht* 'right', Proto-Germanic *rehtaz*.
