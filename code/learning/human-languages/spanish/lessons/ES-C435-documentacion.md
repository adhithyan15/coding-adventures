# la documentación — the papers, as one thing

## You'll want to know: la documentación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la documentación** — the paperwork, the documents, your papers.

Singular in form and plural in meaning: one word for **the whole set**. *Traiga
la documentación* asks for all of it, not one sheet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-02] -->

**documentar** plus **-ción**, the ending that turns an action into a thing:

| verb | the thing |
|---|---|
| documentar | la documentación |
| reparar | la reparación |
| informar | la información |

Fourth ending in five chapters, and the most productive of the lot. Almost any
Spanish verb in **-ar** can take it, and English usually has the twin in
**-tion** — so **-ción** words are the cheapest reading you will ever get.

One thing to watch: they are **always feminine**, whatever the verb, and the
plural drops the accent — *la reparación*, *las reparaciones*.
