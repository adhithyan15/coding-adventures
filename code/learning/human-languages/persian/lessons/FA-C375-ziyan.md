## You'll want to know: زیان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C375-MAZIYYAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**زیان** (*ziyân*) — "harm, a loss".

> سیگار برای بدن زیان دارد.
>
> *sigâr barâye badan ziyân dârad.*
>
> Smoking is harmful to the body.

*Ziyân dâshtan* — to be harmful; *ziyân didan* — to suffer a loss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زیان** "damage, injury" is listed in EtymDB without a parent; it is an inherited Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian.
