# లొంగిపోవు (loṅgipōvu) — to surrender; to give in

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C352-VEMBADI352-01, TE-LEX-C352-VEMBADI352-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to pursue, then the Telugu for to hide oneself.

## You'll want to know: లొంగిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C352-VEMBADI352-03]; assesses=[] -->

**లొంగిపోవు** — *loṅgipōvu* — "to surrender; to give in".

> చివరికి దొంగ పోలీసులకు లొంగిపోయాడు.
>
> *civariki doṅga pōlīsulaku loṅgipōyāḍu.*
>
> In the end the thief surrendered to the police.

Past (I …): లొంగిపోయాను, *loṅgipōyānu*.

To whom takes -కు: పోలీసులకు లొంగిపోవు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లొంగిపోవు** (*loṅgipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 698 lists Telugu *loṅgu*, "to yield, submit", with a query, and *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572), completes the action.
