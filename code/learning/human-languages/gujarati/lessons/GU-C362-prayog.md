## You'll want to know: પ્રયોગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C362-SANSHODHAN362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રયોગ** — *prayog* — "an experiment".

> વિદ્યાર્થીઓએ વર્ગમાં પાણી પર એક પ્રયોગ કર્યો.
>
> *vidyārthīoe vargamā̃ pāṇī par ek prayog karyo.*
>
> The students did an experiment with water in class.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રયોગ** (*prayog*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *prayoga*, "use, application" (Monier-Williams): an idea put to use.
