Italian, chapter 462: In montagna.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la vetta — the summit, the peak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to prune, then the Italian for the bark.

You'll want to know: la vetta.
la vetta — "the summit, the peak".
Dalla vetta si vedono due laghi. From the summit you can see two lakes.
Like la cima, a little more literary.

The word, taken apart.
vetta is traced in EtymDB to Latin vitta 'headband, ribbon'; it is inherited, and first meant the tip of a branch.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: la caverna.
la caverna — "the cave, the cavern".
Nella caverna fa freddo anche d'estate. In the cave it is cold even in summer.
Bigger and deeper than una grotta.

The word, taken apart.
caverna is a learned borrowing from Latin caverna 'cave, hollow' (EtymDB), from cavus 'hollow'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
arrampicarsi — to climb (using hands and feet).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the ravine, then the Italian for the cave.

You'll want to know: arrampicarsi.
arrampicarsi — "to climb (using hands and feet)".
I ragazzi si arrampicano sulle rocce. The boys climb on the rocks.
L'arrampicata — rock climbing.

The word, taken apart.
arrampicarsi is built on rampa 'claw, paw', which is usually traced to a Germanic word that came into Italian as a borrowing: climbing with your claws.
