## You'll want to know: きゅうこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C199-DEPART199-05]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうこう** — *kyūkō* — "an express (train)".

> **きゅうこうは この えきに とまりません。** — *kyūkō wa kono eki ni tomarimasen* — "The express does not stop at this station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうこう** (*kyūkō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyū*, "urgent" (Middle Chinese *kip*) and *kō*, "to go" (*haeng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
