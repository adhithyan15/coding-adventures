## You'll want to know: आयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C433-AYA433-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आयः** — *āyaḥ* — "income".

> कृषकस्य आयः अल्पः अस्ति।
>
> *kṛṣakasya āyaḥ alpaḥ asti.*
>
> The farmer's income is small.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आयः** (*āyaḥ*) is built from *ā*, "towards", and the root √i, "to go": what comes in; MW gives *āya* as arrival and as income, revenue (MW).
