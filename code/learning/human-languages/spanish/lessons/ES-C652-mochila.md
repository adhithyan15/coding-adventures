## You'll want to know: la mochila
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C652-AEROPUERTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la mochila** — "the backpack, the rucksack".

> Solo llevo una mochila.
>
> *I am only taking a backpack.*

*Un mochilero* is a backpacker.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mochila* comes from *mochil* "an errand boy", a word borrowed from Basque *motxil* (EtymDB); the bag was what such a boy carried.
