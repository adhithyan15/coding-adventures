Gujarati, chapter 109: Button, Bucket, Broom, Rubbish, Basket.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
બટન (baṭan) — a button.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a needle, then the Gujarati for a thread.

You'll want to know: બટન.
બટન (baṭan) — "a button".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: baṭan]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: baṭan, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say baṭan]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a needle, then the Gujarati for a thread, then say baṭan again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does બટન (baṭan) mean? ("A button".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Owning and being able — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for rubbish and a basket.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: some, this much, that much, here, there]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to be able to, with, strength, power, a right]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: authority, a chance, property, wealth, an owner]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: experience, a habit, a habit, a qualification, an art]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Some? (અમુક.) This much? (આટલું.) That much? (એટલું.) Here? (અહીં.) There? (ત્યાં.) To be able to? (શકવું.) With? (પાસે.) Strength? (તાકાત.) Power? (શક્તિ.) A right? (હક.) Authority? (અધિકાર.) A chance? (તક.) Property? (મિલકત.) Wealth? (સંપત્તિ.) An owner? (માલિક.) Experience? (અનુભવ.) A habit? (ટેવ.) A habit? (આદત.) A qualification? (લાયકાત.) An art? (કળા.)

(ગાદલું.) A bedsheet? (ચાદર.) A blanket? (ધાબળો.) A step? (પગથિયું.) A stove? (ચૂલો.) A vessel? (વાસણ.) A knife? (છરી.) Scissors? (કાતર.) A needle? (સોય.) A thread? (દોરો.) A button? (બટન (baṭan).) A bucket? (બાલદી (bāldī).) A broom? (સાવરણી (sāvarṇī).) Rubbish? (કચરો (kacro).) A basket? (ટોપલી (ṭoplī).)
