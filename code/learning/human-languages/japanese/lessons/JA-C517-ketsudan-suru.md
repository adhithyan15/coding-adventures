## You'll want to know: けつだんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C517-DECIDE517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**けつだんする** — *ketsudan suru* — "to make a firm decision".

> **もし はやく けつだんしたら、きっぷが やすいでしょう。** — *moshi hayaku ketsudanshitara, kippu ga yasui deshō* — "If you decide early, the tickets will probably be cheaper."

Polite **けつだんします**, *ketsudanshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **しゃちょうが けつだんしました。**, *shachō ga ketsudanshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けつだんする** (*ketsudan suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *ketsudan* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ketsu*, "decide" (Middle Chinese *kwet*) and *dan*, "severance" (*dwanX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
