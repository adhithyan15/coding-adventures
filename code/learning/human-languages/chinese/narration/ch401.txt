Mandarin Chinese, chapter 401: Plans for the Future.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
qiántú — prospects; future (of a person or job).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for originally planned, then the Mandarin for settled and secure.

You'll want to know: qiántú.
qiántú — "prospects; future (of a person or job)".
wǒ juéde zhège gōngzuò hěn yǒu qiántú — I think this job has good prospects.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qiántú is built from 前 'ahead' and tú 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzen and du (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a way': the road ahead.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what would happen.

Lesson 2 of 7.
gǎiháng — to change careers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for settled and secure, then the Mandarin for future prospects.

You'll want to know: gǎiháng.
gǎiháng — "to change careers".
yàoshi bù xǐhuan zhège gōngzuò wǒ jiù gǎiháng — If I don't like this job, I'll change careers.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gǎiháng is built from 改 'to alter' and háng 'a line', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kojX and hang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a rank' and 'a row'. A line of work is a later sense.

Lesson 5 of 7.
yíqiè — everything; all.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to mature (as a person), then the Mandarin for to get older.

You'll want to know: yíqiè.
yíqiè — "everything; all".
zhǐyào nǐ zài yíqiè dōu hǎo — As long as you're here, everything's fine.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
Tone: 一 is said yí here, rising, because qiè after it is a falling tone.

The word, taken apart.
yíqiè is built from 一 'one' and qiè, both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit and tshejH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second, in this tone, 'the many': all of them as one.

What you've built.
