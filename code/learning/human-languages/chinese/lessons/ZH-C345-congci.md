## You'll want to know: cóngcǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C345-LOOKBACK345-02]; assesses=[] -->

*cóngcǐ* — "from then on".

> *cóngcǐ wángzǐ hé gōngzhǔ zhù zài yìqǐ* — From then on the prince and the princess lived together.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cóngcǐ* is built from 从 'from' and *cǐ* 'this', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjowng* and *tshjeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to follow' and the second with a word for 'to stop'; the sense 'this' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
