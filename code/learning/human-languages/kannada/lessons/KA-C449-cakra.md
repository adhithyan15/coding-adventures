## You'll want to know: ಚಕ್ರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C449-CAKRA449-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಕ್ರ** — *cakra* — "a wheel".

> ಸೈಕಲ್ನ ಹಿಂದಿನ ಚಕ್ರ ಡೊಂಕಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *saikalna hindina cakra ḍoṅkāgide.*
>
> The back wheel of the bicycle is bent.

Plural: ಚಕ್ರಗಳು, *cakragaḷu*.

ದ್ವಿಚಕ್ರ ವಾಹನ, a two-wheeler.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಕ್ರ** (*cakra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *cakra*, "a wheel, a potter's wheel, a circle" (CDIAL 4538).
