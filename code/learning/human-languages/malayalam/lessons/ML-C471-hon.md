## You'll want to know: ഹോൺ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C471-VANDI471-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഹോൺ** — *hōṇ* — "a horn".

> പിന്നിലെ കാർ ഉറക്കെ ഹോൺ അടിച്ചു.
>
> *pinnile kār uṟakke hōṇ aṭiccu.*
>
> The car behind honked loudly.

To sound a horn is ഹോൺ അടിക്കുക, to hit the horn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഹോൺ** (*hōṇ*) was borrowed from English *horn*, the device a vehicle sounds as a warning.
