## You'll want to know: घोषणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C315-DAVA315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घोषणा** — *ghoṣṇā* — "a declaration; an official announcement".

> सरकार ने नई सड़क की घोषणा की।
>
> *sarkār ne naī saṛak kī ghoṣṇā kī.*
>
> The government announced a new road.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोषणा** (*ghoṣṇā*) is Sanskrit *ghoṣaṇā*, "a proclaiming aloud" (Monier-Williams), from *ghoṣa*, a loud noise. It is the formal word beside **ऐलान**.
