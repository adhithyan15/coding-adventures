# ಅವಲೋಕಿಸು (avalōkisu) — to observe; to review

## You'll want to know: ಅವಲೋಕಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C424-AVALOKISU424-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅವಲೋಕಿಸು** — *avalōkisu* — "to observe; to review".

> ವರ್ಷದ ಕೊನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ನನ್ನ ಖರ್ಚನ್ನು ಅವಲೋಕಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *varṣada koneyalli nānu nanna kharcannu avalōkiside.*
>
> At the end of the year I reviewed my spending.

Past (I …): ಅವಲೋಕಿಸಿದೆ, *avalōkiside*.

ಅವಲೋಕನ, a review, an overview.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅವಲೋಕಿಸು** (*avalōkisu*) is built on Sanskrit *avalokana*, "seeing, viewing, observing" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
