Marathi, chapter 379: Limits.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मर्यादा (maryādā) — a limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a criterion, then the Marathi for to fulfil.

You'll want to know: मर्यादा.
मर्यादा — maryādā — "a limit".
प्रत्येक गोष्टीला एक मर्यादा असते. pratyek goṣṭīlā ek maryādā aste. Everything has a limit.
Feminine.
मर्यादा (maryādā) घालणे, to set a limit; मर्यादा (maryādā) ओलांडणे, to overstep it.

The word, taken apart.
मर्यादा (maryādā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit maryādā, "region, boundary" (CDIAL 9895).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कमाल (kamāl) — maximum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a limit, then the Marathi for minimum.

You'll want to know: कमाल.
कमाल — kamāl — "maximum".
आज कमाल तापमान चाळीस अंश होतं. āj kamāl tāpmān cāḷīs aṁś hotaṁ. Today's maximum temperature was forty degrees.
In speech also amazing: कमाल (kamāl) आहे!

The word, taken apart.
कमाल (kamāl) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic kamāl, "perfection, completeness": the fullest amount.

Lesson 4 of 5.
शिथिल करणे (śithil karṇe) — to relax (a rule).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for minimum, then the Marathi for maximum.

You'll want to know: शिथिल करणे.
शिथिल करणे — śithil karṇe — "to relax (a rule)".
पाऊस थांबल्यावर प्रशासनाने निर्बंध शिथिल केले. pāūs thāmbalyāvar praśāsnāne nirbandh śithil kele. When the rain stopped, the administration relaxed the restrictions.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नियम शिथिल केला, tyāne niyam śithil kelā.
शिथिल, slack, loose; शिथिल होणे, to slacken.

The word, taken apart.
शिथिल करणे (śithil karṇe) joins the tatsama शिथिल, Sanskrit śithila, "loose, slack" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to make".

Lesson 5 of 5.
झेपणे (jhepṇe) — to be manageable (for someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for maximum, then the Marathi for to relax.

You'll want to know: झेपणे.
झेपणे — jhepṇe — "to be manageable (for someone)".
एवढं काम मला एकट्याला झेपणार नाही. evḍhaṁ kām malā ekaṭyālā jhepṇār nāhī. I won't be able to manage so much work alone.
Past (to him …): त्याला झेपलं, tyālā jheplaṁ.
Dative: मला झेपतं, I can cope; mostly with नाही.

The word, taken apart.
झेपणे (jhepṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
