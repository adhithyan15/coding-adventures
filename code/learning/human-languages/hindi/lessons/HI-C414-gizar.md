## You'll want to know: गीज़र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C414-GUSALKHANA414-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गीज़र** — *gīzar* — "a water heater, a geyser".

> गीज़र ठीक से नहीं चल रहा था।
>
> *gīzar ṭhīk se nahīṃ cal rahā thā.*
>
> The water heater was not working properly.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गीज़र** (*gīzar*) is borrowed from English *geyser*, which in India names the electric water heater in a bathroom.
