Urdu, chapter 285: A Slip and a Fall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ٹھوکر (ṭhokar) — a stumble; a knock of the foot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to overturn, then the Urdu for a ride.

You'll want to know: ٹھوکر.
ٹھوکر — ṭhokar — "a stumble; a knock of the foot".
مجھے پتھر سے ٹھوکر لگی۔ mujhe patthar se ṭhokar lagī. I tripped over a stone.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ٹھوکر (ṭhokar) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭhokar, "a blow", under ṭhōkk, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5513). The one who trips takes مجھے: مجھے ٹھوکر لگی.

Lesson 2 of 5.
لڑکھڑانا (laṛkhaṛānā) — to stagger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a ride, then the Urdu for a stumble.

You'll want to know: لڑکھڑانا.
لڑکھڑانا — laṛkhaṛānā — "to stagger".
وہ لڑکھڑا کر گر گیا۔ voh laṛkhaṛā kar gir gayā. He staggered and fell.
Past (he …): وہ لڑکھڑایا, voh laṛkhaṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
لڑکھڑانا (laṛkhaṛānā) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit laṭati, "shakes" (CDIAL 10916), where the Hindi cousin appears as larkharānā, "to stumble". The repeated -khaṛ- gives the unsteady steps.

Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
پاؤں (pāoṅ) is inherited from Sanskrit pāda, "foot" (CDIAL 8056), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin pā̃u. Urdu writes it with ؤ and the nasal ں.

The word, taken apart.
belongs with chīlnā, "to peel", which CDIAL lists for Hindi under chilla, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5051); چھلنا (chhilnā) is the skin coming off by itself, in a fall.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خراش (kharāsh) — a scratch; a graze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a foot, then the Urdu for to be grazed.

You'll want to know: خراش.
خراش — kharāsh — "a scratch; a graze".
ہاتھ پر ایک خراش تھی۔ hāth par ek kharāsh thī. There was a scratch on the hand.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
خراش (kharāsh) is usually traced to Persian kharāsh, "a scratching, a scraping". It is feminine: ایک خراش تھی.
