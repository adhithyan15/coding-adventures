## You'll want to know: zōngsè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C210-STYLE210-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zōngsè* — "brown".

> *wǒ yào zōngsè de* — I'll have the brown one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zōngsè* is built from *zōng* and *sè* 'colour'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *srik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
