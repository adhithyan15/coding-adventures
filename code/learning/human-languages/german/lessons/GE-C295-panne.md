## You'll want to know: die Panne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C295-PANNE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Panne** — "the breakdown; a mishap".

> Auf der Autobahn hatten wir eine Panne.
>
> *We had a breakdown on the motorway.*

Plural: *die Pannen*. Also a small mishap: *eine kleine Panne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Panne* was borrowed from French *panne* "breakdown" (EtymDB).
