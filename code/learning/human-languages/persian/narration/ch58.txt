Persian, chapter 58: Wolf, Big, Small, Fresh, Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گرگ (gorg) — a wolf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a butterfly, then the Persian for an ant.

You'll want to know: گرگ.
گرگ — gorg — "a wolf".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does گرگ (gorg) mean? ("A wolf".) Say it once more.
