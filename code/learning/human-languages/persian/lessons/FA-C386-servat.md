## You'll want to know: ثروت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C386-BARABARI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ثروت** (*servat*) — "wealth".

> ثروت او از پدرش به او رسیده است.
>
> *servat-e u az pedarash be u reside ast.*
>
> His wealth came to him from his father.

*Servatmand* — wealthy. ث sounds like s.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ثروت** was borrowed from Arabic *tharwa* "wealth" (EtymDB); Persian says the Arabic *th* as *s*.
