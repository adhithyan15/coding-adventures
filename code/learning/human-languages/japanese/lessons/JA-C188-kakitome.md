## You'll want to know: かきとめ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C188-POST188-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かきとめ** — *kakitome* — "registered mail; registered post".

> **ゆうびんきょくで かきとめを おくります。** — *yūbinkyoku de kakitome o okurimasu* — "I send registered mail at the post office."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かきとめ** (*kakitome*) is native Japanese, built from *kaki*, the stem of *kaku*, "to write", and *tome*, the stem of *tomeru*, "to fasten, to keep in place", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
