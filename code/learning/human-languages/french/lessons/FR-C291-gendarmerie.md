## You'll want to know: la gendarmerie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C291-GENDARMES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la gendarmerie** — "the gendarmerie; its offices".

> Elle a perdu son sac et elle a appelé la gendarmerie.
>
> *She lost her bag and called the gendarmerie.*

Both the force and the building.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gendarmerie* is built on *gendarme* (EtymDB), from *gens d'armes* "men of arms".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
