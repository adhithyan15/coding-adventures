# Review — a feeling, and a hope

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-FROH-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ICH-WAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was glad". (*Ich war froh.*) And "I was tired, but
happy". (*Ich war müde, aber glücklich.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-GEFUEHL-DASS-02, GE-GRAMMAR-HOFFEN-DASS-01, GE-GRAMMAR-TRAUM-ZU-02] -->

Two lessons, and an experience can now have an inside. *Ich war* and a
feeling, then the reason with *dass* or *weil*. And a hope, with *ich
hoffe, dass …* or *ich hoffe, … zu …*, or a dream framed by *mein Traum ist
es*.

[PAUSE 2s] The end of a story can carry both:

> *Schließlich war es Abend. Ich war froh, dass ich in Berlin war. Ich
> hoffe, meine Großmutter bald wieder zu besuchen.*

- [YOU SAY: *Ich war froh, dass der Zug endlich kam.*]
- [YOU SAY: *Ich war traurig, weil es regnete.*]
- [YOU SAY: *Ich hoffe, dass du bald kommst.*]
- [YOU SAY: *Mein Traum ist es, nach Japan zu reisen.*]
- [YOU SAY: the end of the journey from chapter two hundred and fifty-three, with one feeling and one hope]
- [YOU SAY: a problem first, then the feeling: *Ich habe den Zug verpasst. Was kann ich machen? Ich war sehr nervös.*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-FROH-01, GE-GRAMMAR-HOFFEN-DASS-01, GE-GRAMMAR-TRAUM-ZU-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I was glad that …"? (***Ich war froh, dass …***) "I hope to
visit you"? (***Ich hoffe, dich zu besuchen.***) Where does *zu* stand?
(**Right before the infinitive, at the end.**)
