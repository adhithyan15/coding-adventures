## You'll want to know: la torre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C678-VISITA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la torre** — "the tower".

> Subimos a la torre de la iglesia.
>
> *We went up the church tower.*

In Spain *una torre* is also a tall block of flats.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*torre* is inherited, through Old Spanish *torre*, from Latin *turris* "a tower" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
