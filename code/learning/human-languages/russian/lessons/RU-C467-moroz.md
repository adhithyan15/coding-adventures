## You'll want to know: мороз
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C467-MOROZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**мороз** (*moróz*) — "frost, freezing cold".

> Утром был мороз, и окна были белые.
>
> *Útrom byl moróz, i ókna býli bélyye.*
>
> In the morning there was a frost, and the windows were white.

Masculine. *Минус двадцать — сильный мороз* — minus twenty is a hard frost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мороз* comes from Old East Slavic *morozŭ* (EtymDB), with the native *-оро-* shape; the same root gives *мёрзнуть* 'to freeze'.
