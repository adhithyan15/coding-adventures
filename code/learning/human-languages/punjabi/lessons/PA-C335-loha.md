## You'll want to know: ਲੋਹਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C335-METAL335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੋਹਾ** — *lohā* — "iron".

> ਇਹ ਪੁਲ ਲੋਹੇ ਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh pul lohe dā hai.*
>
> This bridge is made of iron.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੋਹਾ** (*lohā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *loha*, "red; copper; iron"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *lohā*, "iron", under it (CDIAL 11158).
