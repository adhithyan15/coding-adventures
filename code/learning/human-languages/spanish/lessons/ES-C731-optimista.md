## You'll want to know: optimista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C731-MADUREZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**optimista** — "optimistic; the optimist".

> Soy optimista sobre el futuro.
>
> *I am optimistic about the future.*

Same form for men and women.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*optimista* is usually traced to French *optimiste*, built on Latin *optimus* "best".
