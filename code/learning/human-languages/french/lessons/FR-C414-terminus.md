## You'll want to know: le terminus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C414-COUCHETTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le terminus** — "the end of the line, the terminus".

> Le bus s'arrête au terminus et tout le monde descend.
>
> *The bus stops at the end of the line and everyone gets off.*

The final *s* is pronounced.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terminus* was borrowed from English *terminus*, a learned word from Latin *terminus* 'boundary, end' (EtymDB).
