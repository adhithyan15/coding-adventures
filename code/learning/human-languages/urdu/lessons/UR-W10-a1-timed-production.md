# A short Urdu A1 writing checkpoint

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-MERA-NAAM-HAI, UR-LEX-LAHORE, UR-LEX-BOLNA, UR-LEX-C50-VERBS50-04, UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-PASAND, UR-LEX-PANI, UR-LEX-CHAI, UR-LEX-KAHAN] -->

[PAUSE 2s] This is a short independent attempt, not a model. You will write
the answers, and this page will not show you any copyable Urdu answer.

Put away Roman Urdu, Devanagari, dictionaries, translators, and spell-checkers.
Set a **three-minute timer**. Write in Urdu script; either approved Nastaliq or
accessibility Naskh is fine. Ordinary legible handwriting is enough: imitation
Nastaliq calligraphy is not scored.

## Writing — timed assessment production
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-MERA-NAAM-HAI, UR-LEX-LAHORE, UR-LEX-BOLNA, UR-LEX-C50-VERBS50-04, UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-PASAND, UR-LEX-PANI, UR-LEX-CHAI, UR-LEX-KAHAN] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: timed-assessment-production -->

**One practical form — three minutes**

Complete **all six fields in Urdu script** for a new neighbourhood activity
group. Write one short answer in every field. Start the timer when you begin
the first field; leave any unfinished field as evidence when it rings.

1. Your name
2. Your city or village
3. A language you speak
4. One place you like
5. One drink or food you like
6. One everyday action or kind of work you do

When the three-minute timer rings, **stop**. Record the number of completed
fields. An honest short attempt is more useful than a perfect untimed one.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-MERA-NAAM-HAI, UR-LEX-LAHORE, UR-LEX-BOLNA, UR-LEX-C50-VERBS50-04, UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-PASAND, UR-LEX-PANI, UR-LEX-CHAI, UR-LEX-KAHAN] -->

Spend at most one minute looking at this first attempt. Note one field you
completed clearly and one field to practise next; do not rewrite the timed
answer. Keep the page as evidence.

This checkpoint is **not** the complete A1 writing paper. On another day,
close this lesson and open the separate
[A1 writing-only learner paper](../mocks/a1/writing-paper-1.md) for the
authentic form and named-reader message under one 20-minute assessment clock.
That paper and its later rubric review are a separate assessment session, not
work inside this under-five-minute lesson. It is writing practice only; it does
not establish full A1 exam readiness.
