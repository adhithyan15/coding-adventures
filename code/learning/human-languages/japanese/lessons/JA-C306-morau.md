## You'll want to know: もらう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C306-AISATSU306-05]; assesses=[] -->

**もらう** — *morau* — "to receive; to get".

> **そふから ねんがじょうを もらいました。** — *sofu kara nengajō o moraimashita* — "I got a greeting card from my grandfather."

Polite **もらいます**, *moraimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **いもうとが はがきを もらいました。**, *imōto ga hagaki o moraimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もらう** (*morau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
