## You'll want to know: हाट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C433-QATAR433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हाट** — *hāṭ* — "a weekly market; a village market".

> हर मंगलवार को गाँव में हाट लगती है।
>
> *har maṅgalvār ko gā̃v meṁ hāṭ lagtī hai.*
>
> Every Tuesday there is a market in the village.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हाट** (*hāṭ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *haṭṭa*, "a market, a fair" (CDIAL 13944), which lists Hindi *hāṭ*, "market, shop"; हड़ताल, a strike, closing the market, is in the same entry.
