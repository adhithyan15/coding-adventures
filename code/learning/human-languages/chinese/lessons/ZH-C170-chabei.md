## You'll want to know: 茶杯
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C170-POT170-02]; assesses=[] -->

**茶杯** — *chábēi* — "teacup".

> **我觉得这个茶杯很好看** *wǒ juéde zhège chábēi hěn hǎokàn* — I think this teacup is lovely.

The new character, in pieces: 杯 — eight strokes in two pieces: the tree piece on the left of 机, four strokes, and 不 on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**茶杯** (*chábēi*) is built from 茶 'tea' and 杯 'cup', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drae* and *pwoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
