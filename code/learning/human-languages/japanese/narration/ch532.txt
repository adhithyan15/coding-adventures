Japanese, chapter 532: Limits and Advantages.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
かぎる (kagiru) — to limit; to restrict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for desirable, then the Japanese for somehow.

You'll want to know: かぎる.
かぎる — kagiru — "to limit; to restrict".
もし じかんを いちじかんに かぎったら、みんな いそぐでしょう。 — moshi jikan o ichijikan ni kagittara, minna isogu deshō — "If we limit the time to one hour, everyone will hurry."
Polite かぎります, kagirimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): みせが きゃくを じゅうにんに かぎりました。, mise ga kyaku o jūnin ni kagirimashita.

The word, taken apart.
かぎる (kagiru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 2 of 5.
せいげん (seigen) — a limit; a restriction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for somehow, then the Japanese for to limit.

You'll want to know: せいげん.
せいげん — seigen — "a limit; a restriction".
もし じかんの せいげんが あったら、おしえて ください。 — moshi jikan no seigen ga attara, oshiete kudasai — "If there is a time limit, please tell me."

The word, taken apart.
せいげん (seigen) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are sei, "rule" (Middle Chinese tsyejH) and gen, "limit" (heanX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
