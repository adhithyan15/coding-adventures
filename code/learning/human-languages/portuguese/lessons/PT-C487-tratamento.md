## You'll want to know: o tratamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C487-CONSULTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o tratamento** — "treatment".

> O tratamento dura duas semanas.
>
> *The treatment lasts two weeks.*

Built on *tratar* — to treat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tratamento* is built on *tratar* "to treat", from Latin *tractāre* "to handle" (Lewis & Short), from *trahere* "to draw".
