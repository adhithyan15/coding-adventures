# অবস্থা (ôbôsthā) — a state; a condition (of something)

## You'll want to know: অবস্থা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C368-SHORTO368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অবস্থা** — *ôbôsthā* — "a state; a condition (of something)".

> রাস্তার অবস্থা খারাপ হলে আমরা বাসে যাব না।
>
> *rāstār ôbôsthā khārāp hôle āmrā bāse jābô nā.*
>
> If the roads are in a bad state, we won't go by bus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অবস্থা** (*ôbôsthā*) is Sanskrit *avasthā*, a state or condition (Monier-Williams): how a thing or a person is at present.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
