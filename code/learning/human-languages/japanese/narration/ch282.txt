Japanese, chapter 282: News from Abroad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
せかい (sekai) — the world.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a topic of conversation, then the Japanese for an uproar.

You'll want to know: せかい.
せかい — sekai — "the world".
せかいで いちばん たかい やまは どこですか。 — sekai de ichiban takai yama wa doko desu ka — "Which is the highest mountain in the world?"

The word, taken apart.
せかい (sekai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are se, "world" (Middle Chinese syejH) and kai, "boundary" (keajH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.
たたかう (tatakau) — to fight; to battle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a war, then the Japanese for peace.

You'll want to know: たたかう.
たたかう — tatakau — "to fight; to battle".
ふたつの くにが たたかいました。 — futatsu no kuni ga tatakaimashita — "The two countries fought each other."
Polite たたかいます, tatakaimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): その くにが たたかいました。, sono kuni ga tatakaimashita.

The word, taken apart.
たたかう (tatakau) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
