## You'll want to know: accudire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C498-BAMBINI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**accudire** — "to care for, to look after".

> Mia zia accudisce la nonna malata.
>
> *My aunt looks after my sick grandmother.*

An *-isc-* verb: *accudisco*, *accudisci*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accudire* was borrowed, according to EtymDB, from Spanish *acudir* "to come to help"; the deeper origin is not settled.
