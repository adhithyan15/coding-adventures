## You'll want to know: نگہداشت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C480-CARE480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نگہداشت** — *nigahdāsht* — "care; looking after".

> بوڑھوں کو اچھی نگہداشت کی ضرورت ہے۔
>
> *būṛhoṅ ko achchhī nigahdāsht kī zarūrat hai.*
>
> Old people need good care.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نگہداشت** (*nigahdāsht*) is usually traced to Persian *nigahdāsht*, "keeping, guarding".
