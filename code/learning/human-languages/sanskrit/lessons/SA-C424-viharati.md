## You'll want to know: विहरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C424-PARYATANA424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विहरति** — *viharati* — "strolls; walks for pleasure".

> सायं वयम् उद्याने विहरामः।
>
> *sāyaṃ vayam udyāne viharāmaḥ.*
>
> In the evening we stroll in the garden.

Past (he …): सः उद्याने विहृतवान्, *saḥ udyāne vihṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विहरति** (*viharati*) is built from *vi*, "apart", and the root √hṛ, "to carry", which also gives *harati*, "takes away"; MW gives *vi-hṛ* as to walk for pleasure, to roam, with the participle *vihṛta* (MW).
