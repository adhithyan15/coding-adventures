## You'll want to know: sincero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C473-SINCERITA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**sincero** — "sincere, honest".

> Sii sincero: ti piace il mio vestito?
>
> *Be honest: do you like my dress?*

The noun is *la sincerità*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sincero* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sincērus* "pure, clean, whole" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
