Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 108: To wake someone, To save, To get down, To swim, To jump.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जगावणो (jagāvṇo) — to wake someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to remove, then the Marwadi for to spread out.

You'll want to know: जगावणो.
जगावणो — jagāvṇo — "to wake someone".

The word, taken apart.
जगावणो (jagāvṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jāgrati, "are awake". CDIAL lists Marwari jāgṇo under it (CDIAL 5175). It is the causing form, "make awake".

You'll want to know: बचावणो.
बचावणो — bachāvṇo — "to save, to rescue".

The word, taken apart.
बचावणो (bachāvṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vañcati, "moves crookedly; escapes from". CDIAL lists Old Marwari vacaï under it (CDIAL 11208). It is the causing form of "to escape": "make escape", so "save"; the Old Marwari row means "is saved".

The word, taken apart.
उतरणो (utarṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from uttarati, "passes over; descends". CDIAL lists Old Marwari utaraï under it (CDIAL 1770). It is built on ud, "up, out", and tar, "to cross".

कूदणो (kūdṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kūrdati, "leaps, jumps". CDIAL lists Old Marwari kūdaï under it (CDIAL 3412). CDIAL thinks the Sanskrit verb itself probably came from Dravidian.
