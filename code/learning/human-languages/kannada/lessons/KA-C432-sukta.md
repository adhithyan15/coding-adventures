## You'll want to know: ಸೂಕ್ತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C432-HATTIKKU432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೂಕ್ತ** — *sūkta* — "suitable; appropriate".

> ಈ ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಅವನೇ ಸೂಕ್ತ ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿ.
>
> *ī kelasakke avanē sūkta vyakti.*
>
> He is the right person for this job.

ಸೂಕ್ತವಾದ, fitting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೂಕ್ತ** (*sūkta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sūkta*, "well said, fitting" (CDIAL 13545).
