5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಂತ (hanta) — a stage; a phase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a method, then the Kannada for a process.

You'll want to know: ಹಂತ.
ಹಂತ — hanta — "a stage; a phase".
ಕೆಲಸದ ಮೊದಲ ಹಂತ ಮುಗಿಯಿತು. kelasada modala hanta mugiyitu. The first stage of the work is over.
ಹಂತಹಂತವಾಗಿ, step by step.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಂತ (hanta) is a Kannada word whose origin is not settled: neither DEDR nor CDIAL lists it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ವಿಭಜಿಸು.
ವಿಭಜಿಸು — vibhajisu — "to divide; to split".
ಶಿಕ್ಷಕರು ತರಗತಿಯನ್ನು ನಾಲ್ಕು ಗುಂಪುಗಳಾಗಿ ವಿಭಜಿಸಿದರು. śikṣakaru taragatiyannu nālku gumpugaḷāgi vibhajisidaru. The teacher divided the class into four groups.
Past (I …): ವಿಭಜಿಸಿದೆ, vibhajiside.
Into what: -ಆಗಿ, as in ಗುಂಪುಗಳಾಗಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಭಜಿಸು (vibhajisu) is built on Sanskrit vibhajana, "separation, distinction" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
