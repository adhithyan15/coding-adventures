## You'll want to know: هدفون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C532-WATCHING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**هدفون** (*hedfon*) — "headphones".

> او با هدفون موسیقی گوش میدهد.
>
> *u bâ hedfon musiqi gush midahad.*
>
> She listens to music on headphones.

One word for the pair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hedfon* is a modern loan, usually traced to English *headphone*. EtymDB has no entry for it.
