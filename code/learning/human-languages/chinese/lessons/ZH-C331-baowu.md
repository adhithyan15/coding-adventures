## You'll want to know: bǎowù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C331-MAGIC331-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎowù* — "treasure".

> *tā zài shān lǐ zhǎodào le yí ge bǎowù* — He found a treasure in the mountains.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎowù* is built from *bǎo* 'precious' and *wù* 'a thing', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX* and *mjut* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a treasure' and the second 'all things'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
