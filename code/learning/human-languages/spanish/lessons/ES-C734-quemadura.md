## You'll want to know: la quemadura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C734-HERIDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la quemadura** — "the burn".

> Tengo una quemadura en el brazo.
>
> *I have a burn on my arm.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quemadura* is built on *quemar* "to burn" with the ending *-dura*; *quemar* is inherited from Latin *cremāre* (EtymDB).
