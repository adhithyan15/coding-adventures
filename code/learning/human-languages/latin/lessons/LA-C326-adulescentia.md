## You'll want to know: adulēscentia, adulēscentiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C326-SENECTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**adulēscentia, adulēscentiae** — "young adulthood, youth".

> In adulēscentiā multa et stulta fēcī.
>
> *In my youth I did many foolish things.*

Feminine, from *adulēscēns* (taught): roughly from fifteen to thirty. English *adolescence*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**adulēscentia** is built on *adulēscēns*, the participle of *adulēscō*, "to grow up" (Lewis & Short). English *adolescence* comes from it.
