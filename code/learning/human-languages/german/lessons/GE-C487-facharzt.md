## You'll want to know: der Facharzt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C487-HAUSARZT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Facharzt** — "specialist (doctor)".

> Der Hausarzt schickt mich zu einem Facharzt.
>
> *The GP is sending me to a specialist.*

Plural: *die Fachärzte*. *Das Fach* — subject, field.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Facharzt* is built on *Fach* "compartment, field", inherited from Old High German *fah*, and *Arzt* "doctor", from Late Latin *archiāter* (EtymDB).
