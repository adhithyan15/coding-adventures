Hindi, chapter 474: Sense and Folly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अक़्ल (aql) — sense; wits.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to grumble, then the Hindi for silence.

You'll want to know: अक़्ल.
अक़्ल — aql — "sense; wits".
अरुण छोटा है, लेकिन उसकी अक़्ल बहुत तेज़ है। Aruṇ choṭā hai, lekin uskī aql bahut tez hai. Arun is young, but he's very sharp-witted.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
अक़्ल (aql) is usually traced to Arabic ʿaql, "reason, intelligence", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
बेवकूफ़ (bevkūf) — a fool; foolish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for silence, then the Hindi for sense.

You'll want to know: बेवकूफ़.
बेवकूफ़ — bevkūf — "a fool; foolish".
मैं कितना बेवकूफ़ हूँ, छाता घर पर भूल गया! maiṁ kitnā bevkūf hūṁ, chātā ghar par bhūl gayā! What a fool I am — I left my umbrella at home!
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
बेवकूफ़ (bevkūf) is usually traced to Persian bēvuqūf, "ignorant", which joins Persian bē-, "without", to Arabic vuqūf, "knowledge, understanding".

Lesson 3 of 5.
नादान (nādān) — naive; innocent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for sense, then the Hindi for a fool.

You'll want to know: नादान.
नादान — nādān — "naive; innocent".
वह बच्चा अभी नादान है, ख़तरा नहीं समझता। vah baccā abhī nādān hai, khatrā nahīṃ samajhtā. That child is still naive; he doesn't understand danger.

The word, taken apart.
नादान (nādān) is usually traced to Persian nādān, "not knowing", built on nā-, "not", and dān, "knowing". Hindi uses it kindly, for someone too young to know.

Lesson 5 of 5.
बहकना (bahaknā) — to go astray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for naive, then the Hindi for mad.

You'll want to know: बहकना.
बहकना — bahaknā — "to go astray".
बुरे दोस्तों के साथ रहकर अरुण का भाई बहक गया। bure dostoṁ ke sāth rahkar Aruṇ kā bhāī bahak gayā. Mixing with bad friends, Arun's brother went astray.
Past (he …): वह बहक गया, vah bahak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
बहकना (bahaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi bahaknā, "to be misled", under Sanskrit vyáthatē, "wavers, is disturbed" (CDIAL 12164).
