# Review — telling the trip back

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C291-TARIF-KARDAN-01, FA-GRAMMAR-PAST-CONTINUOUS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "she was telling a story". (*U dâstân ta'rif mikard.*) Then
"I was listening". (*Gush midâdam.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-STORY-ORDER-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BAD-THEN-01, FA-GRAMMAR-DAR-AKHAR-01, FA-GRAMMAR-VAGHTI-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BA-WITH-01, FA-SKILL-READ-NARRATIVE-01] -->

Two chapters, and a whole story fits in your mouth. The time first, with
*gozashte*. The scene in *bud* and the *mi-* past. The events in the plain
past, strung on *avval*, *ba'd* and *vaqti*. The close with *dar âkhar*. *Bâ*
tells you who came along and how you travelled, and *ta'rif kardan* is what
you are doing while you say it.

[PAUSE 2s] Now tell the winter trip back, not word for word, and in the
first person plural, *mâ*. The skeleton:

> *sâl-e gozashte be Tehrân raftim... havâ sard bud... vaqti residim...
> avval... ba'd... dar âkhar bâ qatâr bargashtim.*

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the skeleton, filled in, in at least five sentences]
- [YOU SAY: one thing they did not do — *be sinemâ naraftim*, they didn't go to the cinema]
- [YOU SAY: "When we arrived, it was cold" — *vaqti residim, havâ sard bud*]
- [YOU SAY: "Last week I told the story" — *hafte-ye gozashte dâstân râ ta'rif kardam*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-STORY-ORDER-01, FA-LEX-C291-TARIF-KARDAN-01, FA-SCRIPT-C291-EYN-01, FA-SCRIPT-C290-ZAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What are the three parts of a told story, in order? (**The time
and scene, the events, the close.**) Which joint ties an event to its
moment? (***Vaqti.***) Which letter in **تعریف** is the catch? (**ع**,
*'eyn*.) And which one in **گذشته** says *z*? (**ذ**, *zâl*.)
