# பகிரங்கமாக (pakiraṅkamāka) — openly; publicly

## You'll want to know: பகிரங்கமாக
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C308-PARAPARAPPU308-05]; assesses=[] -->

**பகிரங்கமாக** — *pakiraṅkamāka* — "openly; publicly".

> அவர் தன் தவறைப் பகிரங்கமாக ஒப்புக்கொண்டார்.
>
> *avar taṉ tavaṟaip pakiraṅkamāka oppukkoṇṭār.*
>
> He openly admitted his mistake.

-ஆக makes the adverb; பகிரங்கம், public.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பகிரங்கமாக** (*pakiraṅkamāka*) is built on பகிரங்கம், "public", borrowed from Sanskrit *bahiraṅga*, "outer, external" (Monier-Williams), with the adverb ending -ஆக.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
