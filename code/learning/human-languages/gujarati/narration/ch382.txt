Gujarati, chapter 382: Setting a Goal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ધ્યેય (dhyey) — a goal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to long for someone, then the Gujarati for an opening.

You'll want to know: ધ્યેય.
ધ્યેય — dhyey — "a goal".
જો ધ્યેય નક્કી હશે તો મહેનત કરવી સહેલી લાગશે. jo dhyey nakkī haśe to mahenat karvī sahelī lāgśe. If the goal is clear, the hard work will feel easier.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધ્યેય (dhyey) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit dhyeya, "to be meditated on, to be pondered" (Monier-Williams): what the mind is fixed on.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a resolution, then the Gujarati for an achievement.

You'll want to know: આયોજન.
આયોજન — āyojan — "planning".
જો આયોજન સારું હશે તો કાર્યક્રમ સમયસર પતશે. jo āyojan sārũ haśe to kāryakram samaysar patśe. If the planning is good, the programme will finish on time.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
આયોજન (āyojan) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit āyojana, "junction, combination" (Monier-Williams); "planning", putting the parts together, is the Gujarati sense.

Lesson 5 of 5.
મથવું (mathvũ) — to strive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an achievement, then the Gujarati for planning.

You'll want to know: મથવું.
મથવું — mathvũ — "to strive".
જો તમે સતત મથશો તો ધ્યેય સુધી પહોંચશો. jo tame satat mathśo to dhyey sudhī pahõcśo. If you keep striving, you will reach your goal.
Past (he …): તે આખું વરસ મથ્યો, te ākhũ varas mathyo.

The word, taken apart.
મથવું (mathvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit mathnāti, "whirls, churns"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to churn" (CDIAL 9771). "To strive", to toil at a thing like churning milk, is a sense CDIAL does not give.
