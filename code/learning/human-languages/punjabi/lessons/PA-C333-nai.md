## You'll want to know: ਨਾਈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C333-BARBER333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਾਈ** — *nāī* — "a barber".

> ਨਾਈ ਹਰ ਸਵੇਰ ਆਪਣੀ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਖੋਲ੍ਹਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *nāī har saver āpṇī dukān kholhdā hai.*
>
> The barber opens his shop every morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਾਈ** (*nāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nāpita*, "a barber"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nāī*, "barber", under it (CDIAL 13792).
