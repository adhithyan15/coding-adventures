### Added — Malayalam chapter 97, beautiful and not, written from an enumeration rather than a guess

- `ML-A1-LEX-52` closes. Malayalam A1 coverage 203/243 -> **204/243 (84%)**, 39
  points unmapped.
- **The method changed, and it changed the chapter.** Chapter 96's explanatory
  paragraph was wrong four times across five review rounds because the tidy
  version got written first and checked afterwards. Here the corpus was swept
  **before** drafting.
- The sweep found **three** attested welds with **ആണ്**, not one: **-ം → മാണ്**
  (സുഖമാണ്, മോശമാണ്, എളുപ്പമാണ്), **-ി → യാണ്** (കുട്ടിയാണ്), and **-അത് →
  താണ്** (നല്ലതാണ്, from `ML-C89-nallathu`).
- **The sweep was not enough, and the review caught what it missed.** The first
  draft still unified two different processes: *"a letter arrives to carry ആണ്'s
  vowel."* That is **false for the anusvara** — the ം already **is** the **m**,
  so nothing arrives and only the writing moves, exactly as chapters 89 and 96
  say — and **true only for ഭംഗി/കുട്ടി**, where a **യ** is genuinely added
  because the word ends in a vowel and two vowels will not sit together.
- The unification was tidy and wrong, and it was reached for **because** it made
  the two rows look parallel. Same failure mode as chapter 96, one chapter later,
  in a chapter whose own note claimed to have avoided it. The lesson now splits
  the two and says which is which.
- `ML-C97-sundaram` is deliberately cheap: **സുന്ദരം** drops into the frame
  chapter 96 built, and the lesson says so — *"the chapter before this one cost
  you a rule; this one costs you a word."*
- `ML-C97-bhangi` earns its place by ending differently. **ഭംഗി → ഭംഗിയാണ്**
  against **സുന്ദരം → സുന്ദരമാണ്**, with **കുട്ടിയാണ്** as the precedent the
  learner already owns.
- **അല്ല does weld, and the draft taught that it does not.** That doctrine was
  read out of `ML-C77`'s single example — ഞാൻ അധ്യാപകൻ അല്ല — whose host ends in
  a **chillu**, a consonant with no vowel to pass on. It is the one host in the
  corpus that **cannot** show the rule either way, and the claim was repeated in
  five places.
- The natural form is **ഇത് സുന്ദരമല്ല**. അല്ല is the negative of ആണ് and welds
  the same way, **same മ, same reason** — and the lesson now explains why the
  chapter-77 example looked separate.
- Three smaller corrections: the recall's *"depends on how the word ended —
  nothing else"* was falsified by its own title romanization **sundaravuṁ**,
  where the same ending gives **വ** because what attached was **ഉം**; **ഇത്
  ഭംഗിയാണ്** means *this is beautiful*, not *this is beauty*, and is the commoner
  way to say it; and `bhaṅgi` used the letter this track reserves for **ങ** —
  **കുംഭം → `kuṁbhaṁ`** is the exact parallel, so it is **`bhaṁgi`**, which also
  stops the romanization erasing an anusvara in the one chapter about which words
  have one.
- **No dedicated word for "ugly" is taught, deliberately.** വൃത്തികെട്ട would
  need an attributive form and a register judgement that could not be defended
  under review — the same call made about ബുദ്ധിമുട്ട് in chapter 96. One new
  quality gives both ends of the line, which is the honest A1 shape.
- All four candidates grepped as tokens first (zero occurrences each); glyph
  pre-check run with its control validated before the clean result was trusted.
