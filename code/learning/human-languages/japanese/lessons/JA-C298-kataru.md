## You'll want to know: かたる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C298-MUKASHI298-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かたる** — *kataru* — "to tell (a story); to relate".

> **そふは ときどき むかしばなしを かたります。** — *sofu wa tokidoki mukashibanashi o katarimasu* — "Grandfather sometimes tells old stories."

Polite **かたります**, *katarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **そぼが ゆめを かたりました。**, *sobo ga yume o katarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かたる** (*kataru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
