## You'll want to know: رمح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C326-SILAH326-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رمح** — *rumḥ* — "a spear, a lance".

> حمل الصياد رمحا طويلا.
>
> *ḥamala aṣ-ṣayyād rumḥan ṭawīlan.*
>
> The hunter carried a long spear.

Plural: رماح, *rimāḥ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رمح** (*rumḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-m-ḥ* (Lane), where *ramaḥa* is "to pierce with a spear" and is said of a horse that kicks.
