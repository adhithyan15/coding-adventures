## You'll want to know: ور آمدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C357-KHAMIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ور آمدن** (*var âmadan*) — "to rise (of dough)".

> خمیر در جای گرم ور آمد.
>
> *Khamir dar jâ-ye garm var âmad.*
>
> The dough rose in a warm place.

Present *var miâyad*. *Var* is an old word for "up".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*var* "up, on" is an old native Persian word, and *âmadan* "to come" is inherited Persian: to come up.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
