## You'll want to know: আঁচানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C515-PORIBESHON515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**আঁচানো** — *ā̃chāno* — "to rinse after a meal (the mouth and hands)".

> খাওয়ার পরে সবাই উঠে আঁচাল।
>
> *Khāoyār pôre sôbāi uṭhe ā̃chālô.*
>
> After the meal everyone got up and rinsed their mouths and hands.

Past (he/she …): তিনি খাওয়ার পরে আঁচালেন, *tini khāoyār pôre ā̃chālen*.

Rice is often eaten by hand, so mouth and hand are rinsed when the meal is over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আঁচানো** (*ā̃chāno*) comes down from Sanskrit *ācāmati*, taking a sip of water from the hollow of the hand (CDIAL 1069, which lists Bengali *ā̃cāna*). The everyday sense, rinsing mouth and hand at the end of a meal, probably grew out of that sip.
