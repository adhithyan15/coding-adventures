## You'll want to know: മൂന്നാം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C67-ORDINAL-05]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C67-ORDINAL-02, ML-CONCEPT-C07-NUMBERS-1-5-01] -->

**മൂന്നാം** (*mūnnāṁ*) — "**third**".

Three was the number the Dravidian family never quarrelled over: Tamil *mūṉṟu*,
Malayalam *mūnnŭ*, Kannada *mūru*, Telugu *mūḍu*, all keeping the long **ū** at
the front. The ordinal keeps it too, because the ending only ever touches the end
of the word.

| cardinal | ordinal |
|---|---|
| **മൂന്ന്** (*mūnnŭ*) | **മൂന്നാം** (*mūnnāṁ*) |

**മൂന്നാം കസേര** (*mūnnāṁ kasēra*), the third chair.
