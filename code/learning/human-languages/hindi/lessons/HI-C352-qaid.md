## You'll want to know: क़ैद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C352-ADALAT352-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क़ैद** — *qaid* — "imprisonment, a prison sentence".

> अदालत ने चोर को दो साल की क़ैद सुनाई।
>
> *adālat ne cor ko do sāl kī qaid sunāī.*
>
> The court sentenced the thief to two years in prison.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़ैद** (*qaid*) is usually traced to Arabic *qayd*, "a fetter, a bond", which reached Hindi through Persian.
