## You'll want to know: दरबार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C325-RAJA325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दरबार** — *darbār* — "a royal court".

> राजाच्या दरबारात लोक तक्रारी घेऊन यायचे.
>
> *rājācyā darbārāt lok takrārī gheūn yāyce.*
>
> People used to come to the king's court with complaints.

Masculine.

दरबारात, at court.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरबार** (*darbār*) is a loan from Persian *darbār*, 'a royal court, an audience hall'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
