Bengali, chapter 145: Recently, Already, Last, Once, To Spend (Time).
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সম্প্রতি (sômprôti) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for smooth, then the Bengali for rough.

You'll want to know: সম্প্রতি.
সম্প্রতি — sômprôti — "recently". Bengali's simple past adds -লাম for "I": আমি সম্প্রতি একটা বই পড়লাম — āmi sômprôti ekṭā bôi pôṛlām — "I recently read a book."

Lesson 4 of 5.
একসময় (æksômôy) — once, at one time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for already, then the Bengali for last.

You'll want to know: একসময়.
একসময় — æksômôy — "once, at one time". It points far back: একসময় এখানে একটা পুকুর ছিল — æksômôy ekhāne ekṭā pukur chhilô — "There was once a pond here." ছিল is the past of আছে.
