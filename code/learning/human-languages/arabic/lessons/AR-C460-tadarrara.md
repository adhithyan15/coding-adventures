# تضرر (taḍarrara) — to suffer damage

## You'll want to know: تضرر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C460-ATAL460-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تضرر** — *taḍarrara* — "to suffer damage".

> تضرر سقف المنزل بسبب العاصفة.
>
> *taḍarrara saqf al-manzil bi-sabab al-ʿāṣifa.*
>
> The roof of the house was damaged by the storm.

Present: يتضرر, *yataḍarrar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تضرر** (*taḍarrara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-r-r*, "to harm" (Lane). It is Form V; *ḍarar*, "harm", shares the root.
