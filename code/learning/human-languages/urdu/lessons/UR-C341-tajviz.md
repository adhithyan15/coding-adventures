## You'll want to know: تجویز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C341-HUKMRAN341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تجویز** — *tajvīz* — "a proposal; a suggestion".

> وزیر نے ایک نئی تجویز دی۔
>
> *vazīr ne ek naī tajvīz dī.*
>
> The minister put forward a new proposal.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تجویز** (*tajvīz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tajwīz*, "allowing, approving"; Urdu uses it for a proposal put forward for others to approve, a sense our sources do not trace.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
