## You'll want to know: विकास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C388-UDDISHTA388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विकास** — *vikās* — "development".

> गावाच्या विकासासाठी नवा रस्ता बांधला.
>
> *gāvācyā vikāsāsāṭhī navā rastā bāndhlā.*
>
> A new road was built for the development of the village.

Masculine.

X चा विकास, the development of X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विकास** (*vikās*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vikāsa*, "expanding, flowering, growth" (CDIAL 11630).
