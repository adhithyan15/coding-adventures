Urdu, chapter 140: To win, To lose, To teach, To explain, To ask for.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جیتنا (jītnā) — to win.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to run away, then the Urdu for to call.

You'll want to know: جیتنا.
جیتنا — jītnā — "to win".

The word, taken apart.
جیتنا (jītnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jita, "won, conquered" (CDIAL 5224).

You'll want to know: سکھانا.
سکھانا — sikhānā — "to teach".

The word, taken apart.
سکھانا (sikhānā) is the causing form of sīkhnā, "to learn": to teach is to make someone learn. CDIAL lists both under Sanskrit śikṣate, "learns" (CDIAL 12430).
