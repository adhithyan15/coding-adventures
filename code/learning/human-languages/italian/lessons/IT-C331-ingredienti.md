## You'll want to know: gli ingredienti
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C331-RICETTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**gli ingredienti** — "the ingredients".

> Gli ingredienti sono pochi: farina, uova e latte.
>
> *There are only a few ingredients: flour, eggs and milk.*

Singular *l'ingrediente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingrediente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ingrediēns*, the present participle of *ingredī* "to go in" (Lewis & Short): what goes into the dish.
