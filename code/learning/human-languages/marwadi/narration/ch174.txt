Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 174: Getting Dressed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ओढणो (oḍhṇo) — to cover oneself (with a cloth).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to put out, then the Marwadi for to toast.

You'll want to know: ओढणो.
ओढणो — oḍhṇo — "to cover oneself (with a cloth)".
रात मांय हूं रजाई ओढूं हूं। rāt mā̃y hū̃ rajāī oḍhū̃ hū̃. At night I pull the quilt over me.
Past (I …): म्हैं रजाई ओढी, mhaĩ rajāī oḍhī.

The word, taken apart.
ओढणो (oḍhṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ōḍḍh, "put on, wear, cover"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari auḍhaï, "covers" (CDIAL 2547).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ओढणी (oḍhṇī) — a head-cloth worn by women.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to toast, then the Marwadi for to cover oneself.

You'll want to know: ओढणी.
ओढणी — oḍhṇī — "a head-cloth worn by women".
म्हारी ओढणी लाल है। mhārī oḍhṇī lāl hai. My head-cloth is red.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ओढणी (oḍhṇī) is inherited from a reconstructed ōḍḍh, "put on, wear, cover", which CDIAL takes from Dravidian; it lists the Old Marwari verb auḍhaï, "covers", under it (CDIAL 2547). It is the cloth you cover yourself with: ओढणो (oḍhṇo).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

साड़ी — sāṛī — "a sari".
मां नुवी साड़ी पैरै है। mā̃ nuvī sāṛī pairai hai. Mother wears a new sari.
A feminine noun.

साड़ी (sāṛī) is inherited from Sanskrit śāṭī, "a strip of cloth"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari sāḍī, "a sari", feminine (CDIAL 12381).

Lesson 4 of 5.
उतारणो (utārṇo) — to take off (clothes).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a head-cloth, then the Marwadi for a sari.

You'll want to know: उतारणो.
उतारणो — utārṇo — "to take off (clothes)".
हूं घरै जूती उतारूं हूं। hū̃ gharai jūtī utārū̃ hū̃. At home I take off my shoes.
Past (I …): म्हैं जूती उतारी, mhaĩ jūtī utārī.

The word, taken apart.
उतारणो (utārṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit uttārayati, the causative of uttarati, "comes down"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari utaraï, "descends" (CDIAL 1770). Its partner उतरणो, to get down, is already yours.

Lesson 5 of 5.
चोटी (choṭī) — a plait of hair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a sari, then the Marwadi for to take off.

You'll want to know: चोटी.
चोटी — choṭī — "a plait of hair".
म्हारी चोटी लांबी है। mhārī choṭī lā̃bī hai. My plait is long.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चोटी (choṭī) is inherited from Sanskrit cūḍa, "a topknot"; CDIAL lists Gujarati coṭī, "a tuft of hair" (CDIAL 4883). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
