## You'll want to know: بکنگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C391-QATAR391-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بکنگ** — *buking* — "a booking".

> میں نے کل ٹکٹ کی بکنگ کی۔
>
> *maiṅ ne kal ṭikaṭ kī buking kī.*
>
> I booked the ticket yesterday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بکنگ** (*buking*) is borrowed from English *booking*.
