## You'll want to know: જંકશન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C393-ENGINE393-03]; assesses=[] -->

**જંકશન** — *jankśan* — "a junction (of railway lines)".

> આ જંકશન પર ત્રણ લાઈન મળે છે.
>
> *ā jankśan par traṇ lāīn maḷe che.*
>
> Three lines meet at this junction.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જંકશન** (*jankśan*) is borrowed from English *junction*, a station where railway lines meet.
