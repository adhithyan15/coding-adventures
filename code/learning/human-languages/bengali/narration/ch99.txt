Bengali, chapter 99: Property, Income, Job, Intelligence, Knowledge.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
সম্পত্তি (sômpôtti) — property.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an owner, then the Bengali for land.

You'll want to know: সম্পত্তি.
সম্পত্তি — sômpôtti — "property".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sômpôtti]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sômpôtti, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sômpôtti]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for an owner, then the Bengali for land, then say sômpôtti again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does সম্পত্তি (sômpôtti) mean? ("Property".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Times of day and the week — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for intelligence and knowledge.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: the late afternoon, dawn, midnight, the day after tomorrow, yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tomorrow, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Saturday, Sunday, every day, daily, winter; cold]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: spring, late autumn, a second, the beginning, nowadays]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: these days, night-time, often, ever, soon]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
The late afternoon? (বিকেল, bikel.) Dawn? (ভোর, bhor.) Midnight? (মাঝরাত, mājhrāt.) The day after tomorrow? (পরশু, pôrshu.) Yesterday? (গতকাল, gôtôkāl.) Tomorrow? (আগামীকাল, āgāmikāl.) Monday? (সোমবার, sombār.) Wednesday? (বুধবার, budhbār.) Thursday? (বৃহস্পতিবার, brihôspôtibār.) Friday? (শুক্রবার, shukrôbār.) Saturday? (শনিবার, shônibār.) Sunday? (রবিবার, rôbibār.) Every day? (প্রতিদিন, prôtidin.) Daily? (রোজ, roj.) Winter; cold? (শীত, shīt.) Spring? (বসন্ত, bôsôntô.) Late autumn? (হেমন্ত, hemôntô.) A second? (সেকেন্ড, sekend.) The beginning? (শুরু, shuru.) Nowadays? (আজকাল, ājkāl.) These days? (দিনকাল, dinkāl.) Night-time? (রাত্তির, rāttir.) Often? (প্রায়ই, prāyi.) Ever? (কখনো, kôkhôno.) Soon? (শিগগির, shiggir.)
