## You'll want to know: 小孩
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C253-GRAND253-03]; assesses=[] -->

**小孩** — *xiǎohái* — "child; kid".

> **那个小孩八岁** *nàge xiǎohái bā suì* — That kid is eight.

The new character, in pieces: 孩 — nine strokes: 子 on the left, its last stroke rising; on the right a dot, a bar, a stroke that falls to the left and turns up, two strokes falling to the left, and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**小孩** (*xiǎohái*) is built from 小 'small' and 孩 'a child', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX* and *hoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a newborn child'.
