## You'll want to know: ગુનો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C286-GUNO286-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુનો** — *guno* — "a crime".

> ચોરી એક ગુનો છે.
>
> *corī ek guno che.*
>
> Theft is a crime.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુનો** (*guno*) is usually traced to Persian *gunāh*, "a sin, a fault"; in Gujarati it is an offence against the law. It is masculine: **મોટો ગુનો**, a serious crime.
