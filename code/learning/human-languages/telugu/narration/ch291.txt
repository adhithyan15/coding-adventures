Telugu, chapter 291: South, Place, Border, Opposite, Around.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
దక్షిణం (dakṣiṇaṁ) — south.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a century, then the Telugu for childhood.

You'll want to know: దక్షిణం.
దక్షిణం — dakṣiṇaṁ — "south".

The word, taken apart.
దక్షిణం (dakṣiṇaṁ) is Sanskrit dakṣiṇa, "right (hand); southern", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 6119).

Lesson 3 of 5.
సరిహద్దు (sarihaddu) — a border, a boundary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for south, then the Telugu for a place.

You'll want to know: సరిహద్దు.
సరిహద్దు — sarihaddu — "a border, a boundary".

The word, taken apart.
సరిహద్దు (sarihaddu) is sari, "even, right", whose origin is uncertain, and haddu, "limit", a loan from Arabic ḥadd, "limit, boundary", through Persian and Hindustani.

ఎదురుగా (edurugā) is native Dravidian: eduru, "the front, opposite", one word with Tamil etir (DEDR 795), with the adverb ending -gā.
