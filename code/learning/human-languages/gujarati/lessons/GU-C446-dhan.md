## You'll want to know: ધણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C446-HERD446-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ધણ** — *dhaṇ* — "a herd (of cattle)".

> સાંજે ગાયોનું ધણ ગામમાં પાછું આવે છે.
>
> *sā̃je gāyonũ dhaṇ gāmmā̃ pāchũ āve che.*
>
> In the evening the herd of cows comes back to the village.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધણ** (*dhaṇ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *dhana*, "wealth"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a herd of cattle" (CDIAL 6717): cattle were a family's wealth.
