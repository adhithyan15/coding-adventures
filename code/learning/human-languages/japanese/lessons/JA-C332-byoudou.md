## You'll want to know: びょうどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C332-KENRI332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**びょうどう** — *byōdō* — "equal; equality".

> **おとこと おんなは びょうどうです。** — *otoko to onna wa byōdō desu* — "Men and women are equal."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**びょうどう** (*byōdō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *byō*, "even" (Middle Chinese *bjaeng*) and *dō*, "equal" (*tongX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
