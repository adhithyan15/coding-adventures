## You'll want to know: تجاهل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C420-ISTIJABA420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تجاهل** — *tajāhala* — "to ignore".

> تجاهل الولد كلام أمه.
>
> *tajāhala al-walad kalām ummih.*
>
> The boy ignored his mother's words.

Present: يتجاهل, *yatajāhal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تجاهل** (*tajāhala*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-h-l*, usually traced to the sense "not to know, to be ignorant". It is Form VI, to act as if one did not know: to ignore.
