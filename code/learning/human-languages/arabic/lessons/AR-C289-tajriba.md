# تجربة (tajriba) — an experience

## Warm-up

*shaʿartu bi-l-khawf*.)

[PAUSE 1s] You have a verb for trying on a coat, *jarraba*. Its noun covers

## You'll want to know: تجربة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C289-TAJRIBA289-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C82-ADJ82-01, AR-LEX-C98-WORLD98-03, AR-LEX-C149-QUAL149-05] -->

**تجربة** — *tajriba* — "an experience"; also "a try, an experiment".

> **كانت الرحلة تجربة جديدة.** — *kānat ar-riḥla tajribatan jadīda* — The
> trip was a new experience.

It is *kāna*'s *-an* again: *tajribatan*, though the **ة** hides it on the
page. *Tajriba mumtiʿa* is an enjoyable experience; *tajriba mumilla*, a
boring one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C193-ACT193-02] -->

**تجربة** (*tajriba*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-b*, "to try,
to test" (Lane). Lane gives *jarraba* as making trial of a thing, time after
time, and *tajriba* as its noun: a trial. An experience, in Arabic, is
something you have tried.
