Telugu, chapter 431: Standards and Levels.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ప్రమాణం (pramāṇaṁ) — a standard; a norm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to control, then the Telugu for a limit.

You'll want to know: ప్రమాణం.
ప్రమాణం — pramāṇaṁ — "a standard; a norm".
ఆ కళాశాలలో విద్యా ప్రమాణాలు ఎక్కువ. ā kaḷāśālalō vidyā pramāṇālu ekkuva. Academic standards are high at that college.
Plural: ప్రమాణాలు, pramāṇālu.
Also an oath: ప్రమాణం (pramāṇaṁ) చేయు, to swear.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రమాణం (pramāṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit pramāṇa, "measure; a standard, an authority; proof" (CDIAL 8723).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ధోరణి (dhōraṇi) — a trend; a tendency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a standard, then the Telugu for a level.

You'll want to know: ధోరణి.
ధోరణి — dhōraṇi — "a trend; a tendency".
యువతలో ఒక కొత్త ధోరణి కనిపిస్తోంది. yuvatalō oka kotta dhōraṇi kanipistōndi. A new trend can be seen among young people.
Also a person’s manner: అతని ధోరణి (dhōraṇi) నాకు నచ్చలేదు, I did not like his attitude.

The word, taken apart.
ధోరణి (dhōraṇi) may go back to Sanskrit dhoraṇī, "an uninterrupted series" (Monier-Williams), but the link is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
సమర్థవంతమైన (samarthavantamaina) — effective; efficient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a level, then the Telugu for a trend.

You'll want to know: సమర్థవంతమైన.
సమర్థవంతమైన — samarthavantamaina — "effective; efficient".
ఆమె సమర్థవంతమైన అధికారి. āme samarthavantamaina adhikāri. She is an efficient officer.
సమర్థ, capable, + -వంత, having, + -మైన. సమర్థవంతంగా, effectively.

The word, taken apart.
సమర్థవంతమైన (samarthavantamaina) is built on Sanskrit samartha, "appropriate; capable" (CDIAL 13189), with -vant, "having", and Telugu -మైన for an adjective.

Lesson 5 of 5.
పరీక్షించు (parīkṣiñcu) — to test out; to examine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a trend, then the Telugu for effective.

You'll want to know: పరీక్షించు.
పరీక్షించు — parīkṣiñcu — "to test out; to examine".
వైద్యుడు నా కళ్ళను పరీక్షించాడు. vaidyuḍu nā kaḷḷanu parīkṣiñcāḍu. The doctor examined my eyes.
Past (I …): పరీక్షించాను, parīkṣiñcānu.
The verb of పరీక్ష, an exam, a test, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
పరీక్షించు (parīkṣiñcu) is built on Sanskrit parīkṣā, "inspection, investigation, examination, test" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; పరీక్ష is the same word.
