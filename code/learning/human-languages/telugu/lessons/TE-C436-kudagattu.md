## You'll want to know: కూడగట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C436-PALGONU436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**కూడగట్టు** — *kūḍagaṭṭu* — "to rally; to gather (support)".

> ఆమె గ్రామస్తుల మద్దతు కూడగట్టింది.
>
> *āme grāmastula maddatu kūḍagaṭṭindi.*
>
> She rallied the villagers’ support.

Past (I …): కూడగట్టాను, *kūḍagaṭṭānu*.

కూడ, together, + కట్టు, to tie, sounded గట్టు after it: to tie people together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కూడగట్టు** (*kūḍagaṭṭu*) is native Dravidian, a compound of *kūḍu*, "to join, to gather, to collect" (DEDR 1882), and *kaṭṭu*, "to tie, to bind" (DEDR 1147), whose k sounds as g after the first word.
