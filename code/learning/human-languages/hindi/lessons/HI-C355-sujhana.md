# सुझाना (sujhānā) — to suggest, to put (an idea) to someone

## You'll want to know: सुझाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C355-VIBHAG355-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सुझाना** — *sujhānā* — "to suggest, to put (an idea) to someone".

> अधिकारी ने हमें एक नया रास्ता सुझाया।
>
> *adhikārī ne hamẽ ek nayā rāstā sujhāyā.*
>
> The official suggested a new way to us.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने एक उपाय सुझाया, *usne ek upāy sujhāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुझाना** (*sujhānā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *śudhyati*, "becomes clear" (CDIAL 12527). CDIAL glosses the Hindi *sujhānā* "to explain"; the sense of suggesting is not among its glosses, though it is close: making an idea clear to someone.
