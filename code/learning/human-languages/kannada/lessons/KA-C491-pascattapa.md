# ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ (paścāttāpa) — remorse; repentance

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C491-DUHKHA491-03, KA-LEX-C491-DUHKHA491-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for anguish, then the Kannada for regret.

## You'll want to know: ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C491-DUHKHA491-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ** — *paścāttāpa* — "remorse; repentance".

> ಸುಳ್ಳು ಹೇಳಿದ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *suḷḷu hēḷiddakke avanige paścāttāpa āyitu.*
>
> He felt remorse for having lied.

For what: -ಕ್ಕೆ. ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ ಪಡು, to repent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ** (*paścāttāpa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paścāttāpa*, literally "after-pain": sorrow, regret, repentance (Monier-Williams). It is built from *paścāt*, "afterwards", and *tāpa*, "heat, pain".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
