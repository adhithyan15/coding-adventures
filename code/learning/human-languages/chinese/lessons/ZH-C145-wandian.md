## You'll want to know: 晚点
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C145-LATE145-02]; assesses=[] -->

**晚点** — *wǎndiǎn* — "to run behind schedule; to be late (of a train or plane)".

> **火车晚点了半个小时** *huǒchē wǎndiǎn le bàn ge xiǎoshí* — The train was half an hour late.

Two third tones: wǎndiǎn is said wándiǎn, the first rising.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**晚点** (*wǎndiǎn*) is built from 晚 and 点, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjonX* and *temX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'dusk' and 'a dot'. Late against the hour is the sense of the pair, which the dictionary does not give.
