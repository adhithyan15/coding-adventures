# ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹ (atithigṛha) — a guest house

## You'll want to know: ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C461-ATITHI461-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹ** — *atithigṛha* — "a guest house".

> ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೊಠಡಿಗಳು ಅಗ್ಗ.
>
> *sarkāri atithigṛhadalli koṭhaḍigaḷu agga.*
>
> Rooms in the government guest house are cheap.

ಅತಿಥಿ, a guest, plus ಗೃಹ, a house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹ** (*atithigṛha*) is built from two Sanskrit words: *atithi*, "a guest", and *gṛha*, "a house" (Monier-Williams).
