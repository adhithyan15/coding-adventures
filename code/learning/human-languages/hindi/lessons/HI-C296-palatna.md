## You'll want to know: पलटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C296-TAKKAR296-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पलटना** — *palaṭnā* — "to overturn".

> मोड़ पर बस पलट गई।
>
> *moṛ par bas palaṭ gaī.*
>
> The bus overturned on the bend.

Past (it …): वह गाड़ी पलट गई, *vah gāṛī palaṭ gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from *\*pallaṭṭ*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 7968). It is a whole turning over: a bus **पलट गई**, and a page or a decision can **पलटना** too.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
