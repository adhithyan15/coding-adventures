## You'll want to know: ほうこくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C283-HAPPYOU283-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうこくする** — *hōkoku suru* — "to report; to give an account".

> **おまわりさんが じこを ほうこくしました。** — *omawarisan ga jiko o hōkoku shimashita* — "The officer reported the accident."

Polite **ほうこくします**, *hōkokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **きしゃが ほうこくしました。**, *kisha ga hōkoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうこくする** (*hōkoku suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hō*, "report" (Middle Chinese *pawH*) and *koku*, "inform" (*kowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The native verb *suru*, "to do", makes it a verb.
