# Close the numeral slice

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-PERFORMANCE-ORDINALS, UR-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-FIVE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the first four ordinals, then count one to five.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-PERFORMANCE-DIGITS-READ, UR-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-ALOUD, UR-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-TEN, UR-LEX-EK, UR-LEX-DAS, UR-LEX-BEES, UR-LEX-SAU, UR-LEX-PEHLA, UR-MORPH-ORDINAL-VAN, UR-GRAMMAR-TEENS-IRREGULAR, UR-SCRIPT-DIGITS-LTR, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-ZERO, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-NINE, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FOUR, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FIVE, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SIX, UR-REGISTER-TWO-ROADS-ONE-ROOT, UR-ETYMON-PANCH-FIVE-PIE, UR-LEX-PANCH, UR-LEX-CHAR, UR-LEX-TEEN, UR-LEX-DO] -->

Everything this slice added, with nothing in front of you.

1. Count aloud one to ten, then say **bīs** and **sau**.
2. Write the ten figures shuffled, checking **۴**, **۵** and **۶** against the
   models rather than against memory.
3. Say the first four ordinals, then build **fifth**, **tenth** and
   **twentieth**.
4. Say the two things this slice deliberately did NOT give you, and why: the
   nine irregular teens, and the ordinals built on them.

Then say what the whole slice cost the hand: **no new letter at all.** Every
sign in every one of the twelve number words was already on the page; only the
ten figures were new. And say which road all twelve came by.
