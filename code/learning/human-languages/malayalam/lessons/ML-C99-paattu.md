# പാട്ട് (pāṭṭŭ) — a song

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C98-CINEMA-01, ML-LEX-AANU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *this is a good film*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C99-SONG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**പാട്ട്** — *pāṭṭŭ* — **a song.**

> **ഇത് നല്ല പാട്ടാണ്.** — *itŭ nalla pāṭṭāṇŭ* — "This is a good song."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C99-SONG-01, ML-LEX-AANU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C89-NOMINAL-01] -->

**Listen to the join and you will hear nothing arrive.** **പാട്ട്** ends in the
breathed half-u, and the chapter before this one told you what those do when an
ending comes: they step aside and let it land.

| word | joined to **ആണ്** |
|---|---|
| **നല്ലത്** | **നല്ലതാണ്** (*nallatāṇŭ*) |
| **പാട്ട്** | **പാട്ടാണ്** (*pāṭṭāṇŭ*) |

**You have done this before without a new word to do it on.** **നല്ലതാണ്** has
been in the book since the opinion chapter. **പാട്ടാണ്** is the same event on a
word you met a moment ago — no **യ**, no **വ**, nothing inserted.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C99-SONG-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C89-NOMINAL-01] -->

vowel**.)
