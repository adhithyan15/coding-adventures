German, chapter 103: Song, Game, Colour, Journey, Holiday.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
das Lied — a song.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for work, then the German for music.

You'll want to know: das Lied.
das Lied — "a song".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 3 seconds]
