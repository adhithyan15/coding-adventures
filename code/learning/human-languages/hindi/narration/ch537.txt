Lesson 1 of 6.
कार्यक्रम (kāryakram) — a programme; a schedule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an outline, then the Hindi for a draft (of a text).

You'll want to know: कार्यक्रम.
कार्यक्रम — kāryakram — "a programme; a schedule".
स्कूल का कार्यक्रम शाम छह बजे है। Skūl kā kāryakram śām chah baje hai. The school programme is at six in the evening.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
कार्यक्रम (kāryakram) is a modern compound of two Sanskrit words, kārya, "to be done", and krama, "a step, a course" (Monier-Williams): the things to be done, one after another.

Lesson 3 of 6.
हड़बड़ी (haṛbaṛī) — a flustered hurry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a programme, then the Hindi for extra time.

You'll want to know: हड़बड़ी.
हड़बड़ी — haṛbaṛī — "a flustered hurry".
हड़बड़ी (haṛbaṛī) में मैं अपना बटुआ घर पर भूल गया। Haṛbaṛī meṁ maiṁ apnā baṭuā ghar par bhūl gayā. In my hurry I forgot my wallet at home.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
हड़बड़ी (haṛbaṛī) is a tadbhava, inherited from haḍabaḍa, "confusion", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13949). Today it is the confusion of someone short of time.

Lesson 4 of 6.
मुहूर्त (muhūrt) — an auspicious moment (to begin something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for extra time, then the Hindi for a flustered hurry.

You'll want to know: मुहूर्त.
मुहूर्त — muhūrt — "an auspicious moment (to begin something)".
पुजारी ने शादी का मुहूर्त निकाला। Pujārī ne śādī kā muhūrt nikālā. The priest worked out the auspicious time for the wedding.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मुहूर्त (muhūrt) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit muhūrta, "a moment, a short space of time" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for the moment the stars favour.

Lesson 5 of 6.
खिसकाना (khiskānā) — to shift; to push back (a date).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a flustered hurry, then the Hindi for an auspicious moment (to begin something).

You'll want to know: खिसकाना.
खिसकाना — khiskānā — "to shift; to push back (a date)".
हमने बैठक अगले हफ़्ते तक खिसका दी। Hamne baiṭhak agle hafte tak khiskā dī. We pushed the meeting back to next week.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मेज़ खिसकाई, usne mez khiskāī.

The word, taken apart.
खिसकाना (khiskānā) is the causative of khisaknā, "to slip away", which CDIAL files under khis, a reconstructed form (CDIAL 3888).
