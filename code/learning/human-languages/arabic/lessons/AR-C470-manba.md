## You'll want to know: منبع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C470-YANBU470-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منبع** — *manbaʿ* — "a river source; a source".

> يقع منبع النهر في الجبال.
>
> *yaqaʿu manbaʿ an-nahr fī al-jibāl.*
>
> The source of the river lies in the mountains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منبع** (*manbaʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-b-ʿ*, "to well up, to gush" (Lane). The pattern *mafʿal* makes it the place where water wells up.
