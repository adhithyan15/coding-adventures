## You'll want to know: ಮದುವೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C91-ABOUTYOU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮದುವೆ** (*maduve*) — "wedding, marriage".

**ಮದುವೆಗೆ ಆಹ್ವಾನ** (*maduvege āhvāna*) — "an invitation to a wedding", with
**ಆಹ್ವಾನ**, "invitation", from the last tranche.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The biggest invitation of all.
