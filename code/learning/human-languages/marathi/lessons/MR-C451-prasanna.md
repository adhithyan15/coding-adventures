## You'll want to know: प्रसन्न
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C451-MOOD451-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसन्न** — *prasanna* — "cheerful; bright and clear".

> सकाळी बागेत फिरल्यावर मन प्रसन्न होतं.
>
> *sakāḷī bāget phirlyāvar man prasanna hotaṁ.*
>
> After a morning walk in the garden, the mind feels bright.

Unchanging for gender: प्रसन्न चेहरा, a cheerful face; प्रसन्न सकाळ, a bright morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसन्न** (*prasanna*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prasanna*, "pleased; clear, bright" (CDIAL 8823).
