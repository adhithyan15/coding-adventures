# ద్వేషించు (dvēṣiñcu) — to hate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-05, TE-LEX-C499-ASUYA499-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to hang one's head, then the Telugu for to be jealous.

## You'll want to know: ద్వేషించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C499-ASUYA499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ద్వేషించు** — *dvēṣiñcu* — "to hate".

> అబద్ధాలను నేను ద్వేషిస్తాను.
>
> *abaddhālanu nēnu dvēṣistānu.*
>
> I hate lies.

Past (I …): ద్వేషించాను, *dvēṣiñcānu*.

The noun ద్వేషం is hatred; its opposite is ప్రేమ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ద్వేషించు** (*dvēṣiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *dvēṣa*, "hatred" (CDIAL 6696), from the root *dviṣ*, with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
