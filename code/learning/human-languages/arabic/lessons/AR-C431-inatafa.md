# انعطف (inʿaṭafa) — to turn off; to turn (left or right)

## You'll want to know: انعطف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C431-MURUR431-05]; assesses=[] -->

**انعطف** — *inʿaṭafa* — "to turn off; to turn (left or right)".

> انعطفنا يسارا بعد الجسر.
>
> *inʿaṭafnā yasāran baʿda al-jisr.*
>
> We turned left after the bridge.

Present: ينعطف, *yanʿaṭif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انعطف** (*inʿaṭafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṭ-f*, "to bend, to turn" (Lane). It is Form VII.
