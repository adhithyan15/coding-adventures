Persian, chapter 302: Beginnings and endings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آغاز (âghâz) — a beginning, a start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to take place, then the Persian for to last.

You'll want to know: آغاز.
آغاز (âghâz) — "a beginning, a start".
آغاز (âghâz) کلاس ساعت هشت است. Âghâz-e kelâs sâ'at-e hasht ast. The start of the class is at eight o'clock.
More formal than shoru'. Dar âghâz "at the beginning".

The word, taken apart.
âghâz is a native Persian word. EtymDB lists it without a parent, and its deeper origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
آغاز شدن (âghâz shodan) — to begin, to start (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to last, then the Persian for a beginning.

You'll want to know: آغاز شدن.
آغاز شدن (âghâz shodan) — "to begin, to start (formal)".
جشن ساعت هفت شب آغاز شد. Jashn sâ'at-e haft-e shab âghâz shod. The celebration began at seven in the evening.
The thing itself begins: âghâz mishavad. With a doer the verb is âghâz kardan, "to begin something".

The word, taken apart.
âghâz shodan joins âghâz "a beginning", a native word whose deeper origin is not settled, with shodan "to become", inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پایان (pâyân) — an end, an ending.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a beginning, then the Persian for to begin.

You'll want to know: پایان.
پایان (pâyân) — "an end, an ending".
پایان (pâyân) فیلم را دوست نداشتم. Pâyân-e film râ dust nadâshtam. I did not like the end of the film.
Dar pâyân "in the end"; be pâyân residan "to come to an end".

The word, taken apart.
pâyân is a native Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian pāyān, "end"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پایان یافتن (pâyân yâftan) — to come to an end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to begin, then the Persian for an end.

You'll want to know: پایان یافتن.
پایان یافتن (pâyân yâftan) — "to come to an end".
جنگ بعد از هشت سال پایان یافت. Jang ba'd az hasht sâl pâyân yâft. The war came to an end after eight years.
Formal; in speech tamâm shod. Present stem yâb-: pâyân miyâbad.

The word, taken apart.
pâyân yâftan joins pâyân "end" and yâftan "to find, to attain", both native Persian words: literally "to find an end". EtymDB lists neither.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سرانجام (saranjâm) — finally, at last.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an end, then the Persian for to come to an end.

You'll want to know: سرانجام.
سرانجام (saranjâm) — "finally, at last".
سرانجام (saranjâm) بعد از سه ساعت به خانه رسیدیم. Saranjâm ba'd az se sâ'at be khâne residim. Finally, after three hours, we reached home.
Written and storytelling Persian; in speech bel-akhare. Also a noun, "outcome": saranjâm-e kâr "the outcome of the affair".

The word, taken apart.
saranjâm joins sar "head" and anjâm "end, completion", both native Persian words. EtymDB lists the compound with no parent.
