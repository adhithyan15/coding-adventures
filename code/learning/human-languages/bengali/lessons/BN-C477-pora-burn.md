## You'll want to know: পোড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C477-JBOLUNI477-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পোড়া** — *poṛā* — "to get burnt; to burn (by itself)".

> গরম তেলে মীরার হাত পুড়ল।
>
> *gôrôm tele mīrār hāt puṛlô.*
>
> Mira's hand got burnt by hot oil.

Past (he/she …): সেই রুটি পুড়ল, *sei ruṭi puṛlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পোড়া** (*poṛā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as burning and being burnt, under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 8397); পোড়ানো (taught), to burn something, is its causative.
