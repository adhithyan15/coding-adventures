## You'll want to know: వేడి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**వేడి** (*vēḍi*) — "heat", and before a noun, "hot".

**ఇవాళ చాలా వేడిగా ఉంది** — "it is very hot today". **వేడి నీళ్ళు** — "hot water".

It is the pair of చలి, and it slots into the same frame.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Cold and hot, as a pair, in one frame.
