## You'll want to know: استعاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C313-FAWR313-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استعاد** — *istaʿāda* — "to regain, to get back".

> استعاد الرجل حقيبته من الشرطة.
>
> *istaʿāda ar-rajul ḥaqībatahu min ash-shurṭa.*
>
> The man got his bag back from the police.

Present: يستعيد, *yastaʿīd*.

استعاد ذكرياته: he went back over his memories.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استعاد** (*istaʿāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-w-d* (Lane), "to return", the root of *ʿāda*, he came back. It is Form X, "to seek the return of".
