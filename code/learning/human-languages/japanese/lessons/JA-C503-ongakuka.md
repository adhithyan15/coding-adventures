## You'll want to know: おんがくか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C503-SONG503-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おんがくか** — *ongakuka* — "a musician".

> **おじさんは おんがくかです。** — *ojisan wa ongakuka desu* — "My uncle is a musician."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おんがくか** (*ongakuka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *on*, "sound" (Middle Chinese *'im*), *gaku*, "music" (*ngaewk*) and *ka*, "expert" (*kae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
