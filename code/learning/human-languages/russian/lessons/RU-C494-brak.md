## You'll want to know: брак
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C494-MARRIAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**брак** (*brak*) — "marriage; a defect".

> Их брак длится уже тридцать лет.
>
> *Ikh brak dlítsya uzhé trídtsat let.*
>
> Their marriage has lasted thirty years.

Masculine. A second word *брак* means faulty goods: *бракованный* — defective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*брак* 'marriage' was borrowed from Church Slavonic *brakъ*, the shape with *-ра-* beside Proto-Slavic *borkъ* (EtymDB).
