## You'll want to know: discutibile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C343-DUBBIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**discutibile** — "questionable, debatable".

> La scelta dell'allenatore è discutibile.
>
> *The coach's choice is questionable.*

It politely suggests something is wrong: *una scelta discutibile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discutibile* is built on *discutere* "to discuss", from Latin *discutere* "to strike apart, to break up, to scatter" (Lewis & Short), built from *dis-* and *quatere* "to shake": a matter that can be shaken.
