## You'll want to know: पाथेयम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C394-TIRTHA394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पाथेयम्** — *pātheyam* — "food for the journey".

> माता मह्यं पाथेयं ददाति।
>
> *mātā mahyaṃ pātheyaṃ dadāti.*
>
> Mother gives me food for the journey.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाथेयम्** (*pātheyam*) is built on *pathin*, "a road", with the ending *-eya*, "belonging to"; MW gives *pātheya* as provisions for a journey (MW).
