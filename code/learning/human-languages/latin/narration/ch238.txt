Latin, chapter 238: Beyond, On this side, At the house of, Face to face, By which way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ultrā — beyond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for every day, then the Latin for the day before.

You'll want to know: ultrā.
ultrā — "beyond".

The word, taken apart.
ultrā is an old case form of ulter, "lying beyond" (Lewis & Short), built on uls, "beyond", with parte, "side", understood: on the far side. English ultra- comes from it.

apud — "at the house of, among".

The word, taken apart.
apud has no sure origin; old inscriptions also write aput (Lewis & Short). A link with Greek apo, "from", is often suggested.
