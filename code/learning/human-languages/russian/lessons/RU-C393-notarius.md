## You'll want to know: нотариус
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C393-DOKUMENTY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**нотариус** (*notárius*) — "a notary".

> Мы ходили к нотариусу с документами.
>
> *My khodíli k notáriusu s dokuméntami.*
>
> We went to the notary with our documents.

Masculine, also of a woman. In Russia a notary certifies documents, wills and powers of attorney.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**нотариус** is usually traced to Latin *notārius* "a shorthand clerk, a secretary" (Lewis & Short), built on *nota* "a mark, a sign".
