Spanish, chapter 652: En el aeropuerto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la terminal — the terminal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the berth, then the Spanish for to get off.

You'll want to know: la terminal.
la terminal — "the terminal".
Los vuelos internacionales salen de la terminal dos. International flights leave from terminal two.
At an airport it is feminine: la terminal; el terminal is a device.

The word, taken apart.
terminal is a learned borrowing from Latin terminālis "of a boundary" (EtymDB), from terminus "a boundary, an end" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
extraviarse — to go missing, to get lost (of things).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to get off, then the Spanish for the terminal.

You'll want to know: extraviarse.
extraviarse — "to go missing, to get lost (of things)".
Mi maleta se extravió en el viaje. My suitcase went missing on the trip.
Formal; airports talk about equipaje extraviado.

The word, taken apart.
extraviarse is built from extra "outside" and vía "way" (EtymDB), from Latin via: to go off the road.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la aerolínea — the airline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the terminal, then the Spanish for to go missing.

You'll want to know: la aerolínea.
la aerolínea — "the airline".
La aerolínea nos paga el hotel por el retraso. The airline is paying for our hotel because of the delay.
Also la compañía aérea.

The word, taken apart.
aerolínea is built from aero-, from Greek aḗr "air", and línea "line", a learned borrowing from Latin līnea (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la etiqueta — the label, the tag; etiquette.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to go missing, then the Spanish for the airline.

You'll want to know: la etiqueta.
la etiqueta — "the label, the tag; etiquette".
Pon una etiqueta con tu nombre en la maleta. Put a label with your name on the suitcase.
De etiqueta — formal dress.

The word, taken apart.
etiqueta was borrowed from French étiquette "a label, a ticket", from Old French estiquier "to attach, to stick" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
la mochila — the backpack, the rucksack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the airline, then the Spanish for the label.

You'll want to know: la mochila.
la mochila — "the backpack, the rucksack".
Solo llevo una mochila. I am only taking a backpack.
Un mochilero is a backpacker.

The word, taken apart.
mochila comes from mochil "an errand boy", a word borrowed from Basque motxil (EtymDB); the bag was what such a boy carried.
