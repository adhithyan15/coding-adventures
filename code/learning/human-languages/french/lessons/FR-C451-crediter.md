## You'll want to know: créditer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C451-BANQUE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**créditer** — "to credit, to pay into".

> La banque a crédité mon compte ce matin.
>
> *The bank paid the money into my account this morning.*

The opposite of *débiter*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*créditer* is built on *crédit*, from Latin *crēditum* 'a loan', from *crēdere* 'to trust' (Lewis & Short).
