## You'll want to know: અંગારો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C300-AAG300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અંગારો** — *angāro* — "an ember".

> ચૂલામાં હજી અંગારા સળગતા હતા.
>
> *cūlāmā̃ hajī angārā saḷagtā hatā.*
>
> Embers were still glowing in the stove.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અંગારો** (*angāro*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aṅgāra*, "charcoal", and CDIAL lists Gujarati *ãgāro*, "glowing charcoal" (CDIAL 125). Embers stay in a stove after the flames have gone: **અંગારા**.
