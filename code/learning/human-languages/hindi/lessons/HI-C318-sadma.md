## You'll want to know: सदमा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C318-DHAMAKA318-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सदमा** — *sadmā* — "a shock (of grief or fright)".

> धमाके के बाद मीरा को गहरा सदमा लगा।
>
> *dhamāke ke bād Mīrā ko gahrā sadmā lagā.*
>
> After the blast Mira was in deep shock.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सदमा** (*sadmā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣadma*, "a blow, a collision": a blow to the mind. A shock **लगता है**: **मीरा को सदमा लगा**.
