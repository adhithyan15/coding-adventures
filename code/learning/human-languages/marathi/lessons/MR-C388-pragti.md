## You'll want to know: प्रगती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C388-UDDISHTA388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रगती** — *pragtī* — "progress".

> या वर्षी अरुणने अभ्यासात चांगली प्रगती केली.
>
> *yā varṣī aruṇne abhyāsāt cāṅglī pragtī kelī.*
>
> Arun made good progress in his studies this year.

Feminine.

प्रगती करणे, to make progress.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रगती** (*pragtī*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit *pra-*, "forward", and *gati*, "going" (Monier-Williams).
