## You'll want to know: मल्लः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C525-MALLA525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मल्लः** — *mallaḥ* — "a wrestler".

> ग्रामस्य मल्लः बलवान् अस्ति।
>
> *grāmasya mallaḥ balavān asti.*
>
> The village wrestler is strong.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मल्लः** (*mallaḥ*) is the word MW records for a wrestler, a boxer; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
