Gujarati, chapter 435: Thunder and Cyclones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કડાકો (kaḍāko) — a crash of thunder; a loud crack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to gather (of clouds), then the Gujarati for to get wet through.

You'll want to know: કડાકો.
કડાકો — kaḍāko — "a crash of thunder; a loud crack".
રાત્રે વીજળીનો મોટો કડાકો થયો. rātre vījḷīno moṭo kaḍāko thayo. At night there was a great crash of thunder.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કડાકો (kaḍāko) goes back to Sanskrit kaṭakaṭā, an imitative word for a grating or crackling sound; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a loud crack" (CDIAL 2632).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ચક્રવાત (cakravāt) — a whirlwind; a cyclone (in weather reports).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get wet through, then the Gujarati for a crash of thunder.

You'll want to know: ચક્રવાત.
ચક્રવાત — cakravāt — "a whirlwind; a cyclone (in weather reports)".
દરિયામાં ચક્રવાત આવ્યો, એટલે હોડીઓ કિનારે રહી. dariyāmā̃ cakravāt āvyo, eṭle hoḍīo kināre rahī. A cyclone came over the sea, so the boats stayed on the shore.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચક્રવાત (cakravāt) is a tatsama, Sanskrit cakravāta, "a whirlwind" (Monier-Williams), made of cakra, "a wheel", and vāta, "wind": the wind that turns like a wheel.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a crash of thunder, then the Gujarati for a whirlwind.

You'll want to know: સુસવાટો.
સુસવાટો — susvāṭo — "a howl of wind; a whistling gust".
રાત્રે પવનના સુસવાટા સંભળાતા હતા. rātre pavannā susvāṭā sambhḷātā hatā. At night you could hear the wind howling.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સુસવાટો (susvāṭo) goes back to śūṣa, "blowing", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a blast of wind" (CDIAL 12577).

Lesson 4 of 5.
આગાહી (āgāhī) — a forecast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a whirlwind, then the Gujarati for a howl of wind.

You'll want to know: આગાહી.
આગાહી — āgāhī — "a forecast".
હવામાન ખાતાએ ભારે વરસાદની આગાહી કરી. havāmān khātāe bhāre varsādnī āgāhī karī. The weather office forecast heavy rain.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આગાહી (āgāhī) is usually traced to Persian āgāhī, "awareness, being told in advance", built on āgāh, "aware".

Past (it …): તે વીજળી ઝબકી, te vījḷī jhabkī.

ઝબકવું (jhabakvũ) goes back to jhamm, "a flash", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to flash" (CDIAL 5341).
