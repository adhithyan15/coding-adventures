Marathi, chapter 540: Insurance and Risk.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विमा (vimā) — insurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a film director, then the Marathi for to applaud.

You'll want to know: विमा.
विमा — vimā — "insurance".
जर घराचा विमा असेल तर नुकसान भरून मिळतं. jar gharācā vimā asel tar nuksān bharūn miḷtaṁ. If the house is insured, the damage is made good.
Masculine.
विमा (vimā) उतरवणे, to take out insurance.

The word, taken apart.
विमा (vimā) is a loan from Persian bīma, "insurance, fear".

Lesson 2 of 5.
जोखीम (jokhīm) — a risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to applaud, then the Marathi for insurance.

You'll want to know: जोखीम.
जोखीम — jokhīm — "a risk".
कोणताही व्यवसाय सुरू करताना थोडी जोखीम असतेच. koṇtāhī vyavsāy surū kartānā thoḍī jokhīm astec. Starting any business always carries some risk.
Feminine.
जोखीम (jokhīm) घेणे, to take a risk.

The word, taken apart.
जोखीम (jokhīm) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi jokhīm, "risk, venture", under Sanskrit yōgakṣēma, "the safe keeping of one's property" (CDIAL 10527).

Lesson 5 of 5.
परवडणे (parvaḍṇe) — to be affordable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a guarantee, then the Marathi for an instalment.

You'll want to know: परवडणे.
परवडणे — parvaḍṇe — "to be affordable".
इतकी महाग गाडी आम्हाला परवडणार नाही. itkī mahāg gāḍī āmhālā parvaḍṇār nāhī. We cannot afford such an expensive car.
Past (to him …): त्याला परवडलं, tyālā parvaḍlaṁ.
The person takes -ला: मला परवडतं, I can afford it.

The word, taken apart.
परवडणे (parvaḍṇe) is a Marathi verb with the one who pays in the dative; its origin is not settled.
