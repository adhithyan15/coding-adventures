## You'll want to know: a associação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C367-COMUNIDADE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a associação** — "the association, the club".

> Ela é sócia de uma associação de moradores.
>
> *She is a member of a residents' association.*

*Associação de estudantes* — students' union.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*associação* is built on *associar* "to join together", a learned borrowing from Latin *associāre* "to join to, to unite with" (Lewis & Short), on *socius* "companion, partner".
