## You'll want to know: magistrātus, magistrātūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C383-MAGISTRATUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**magistrātus, magistrātūs** — "a public office; a magistrate".

> Magistrātūs quotannīs ā populō creantur.
>
> *Magistrates are elected by the people every year.*

*magistrātus, magistrātūs* m., built on *magister*: both the office and the man who holds it. Roman offices lasted a year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*magistrātus* is built on *magister* "a master" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the office of one set over others. English *magistrate* comes from it.
