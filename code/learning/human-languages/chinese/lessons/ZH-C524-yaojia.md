## You'll want to know: 要价
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C524-PRICE524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**要价** — *yàojià* — "to name one's price; to ask a price".

> *tā yàojià bǐ nà jiā shāngdiàn gāo* — He's asking more than that shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**要价** (*yàojià*) is built from 要 'to want' and 价 'a price', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jiewH* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to bind by agreement' and the second as the count of a price.
