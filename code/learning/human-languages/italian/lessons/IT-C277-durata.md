## You'll want to know: la durata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C277-DURATA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la durata** — "the length of time, the duration".

> La durata del viaggio è di due ore.
>
> *The journey lasts two hours.*

Built on *durare*, ‘to last’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*durata* is built on *durare* "to last", from Latin *dūrāre* (EtymDB).
