Arabic, chapter 254: To fry, To grill, To boil, To mix, To peel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قلى (qalā) — to fry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for chalk, then the Arabic for a stapler.

You'll want to know: قلى.
قلى — qalā — "to fry".

The word, taken apart.
قلى (qalā) is built on the native Arabic root q-l-y, "to fry" (Lane). Qalā is the plain verb, "he fried".
