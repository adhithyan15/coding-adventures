## You'll want to know: થૂંકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C474-CAVVU474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**થૂંકવું** — *thū̃kvũ* — "to spit".

> રસ્તા પર થૂંકવું ખરાબ ટેવ છે.
>
> *rastā par thū̃kvũ kharāb ṭev che.*
>
> Spitting in the street is a bad habit.

Past (he …): તે રસ્તા પર થૂંક્યો, *te rastā par thū̃kyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થૂંકવું** (*thū̃kvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*thukk-*, "spit", an imitative word; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 6097).
