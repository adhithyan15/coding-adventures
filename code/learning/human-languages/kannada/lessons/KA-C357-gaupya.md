## You'll want to know: ಗೌಪ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C357-VANCHANE357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೌಪ್ಯ** — *gaupya* — "confidential; secret".

> ಈ ಪತ್ರ ಗೌಪ್ಯ, ಅದನ್ನು ಬೇರೆ ಯಾರೂ ಓದಬಾರದು.
>
> *ī patra gaupya, adannu bēre yārū ōdabāradu.*
>
> This letter is confidential; nobody else may read it.

ಗೌಪ್ಯವಾಗಿ ಇಡು, to keep confidential. ಗೌಪ್ಯತೆ is privacy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೌಪ್ಯ** (*gaupya*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *gopya*, "to be kept secret or hidden" (Monier-Williams), from the root *gup*, "to guard, to hide"; the dictionary has no form with *au*.
