## You'll want to know: इंतज़ाम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C395-MANZURI395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इंतज़ाम** — *intazām* — "an arrangement".

> अगर तुम खाने का इंतज़ाम करोगे तो मैं मेहमानों को बुलाऊँगा।
>
> *agar tum khāne kā intazām karoge to maiṁ mehmānoṁ ko bulāūṅgā.*
>
> If you arrange the food, I'll invite the guests.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इंतज़ाम** (*intazām*) is usually traced to Arabic *intiẓām*, "putting in order", which reached Hindi through Persian.
