## You'll want to know: die Rente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C302-RUHESTAND-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Rente** — "the pension".

> Mit fünfundsechzig ging mein Vater in Rente.
>
> *At sixty-five my father retired.*

*In Rente gehen* — to retire. In Austria: *die Pension*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Rente* was borrowed from French *rente* "income", which is usually traced to Latin *reddere* "to give back" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent.
