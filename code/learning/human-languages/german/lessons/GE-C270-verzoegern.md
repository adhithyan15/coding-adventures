## You'll want to know: verzögern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C270-ABWARTEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**verzögern** — "to delay; mostly reflexive: sich verzögern, to be delayed".

> Der Flug verzögerte sich um eine Stunde.
>
> *The flight was delayed by an hour.*

Often reflexive: *sich verzögern* — to be delayed. *Die Verzögerung* — the delay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verzögern* is built on *zögern* "to hesitate", usually traced to Middle High German *zogen* "to drag", with the prefix *ver-*; EtymDB lists no parent.
