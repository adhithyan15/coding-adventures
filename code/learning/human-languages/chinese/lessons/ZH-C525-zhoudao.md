## You'll want to know: zhōudào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C525-SERVICE525-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōudào* — "attentive and thorough (of service)".

> *zhè jiā fàndiàn de fúwùyuán bǐ nà jiā zhōudào* — The waiters in this restaurant are more attentive than in that one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōudào* is built from *zhōu* and 到 'to reach', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyuw* and *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'all round', 'complete' and 'close' and the second 'to arrive': reaching all round.
