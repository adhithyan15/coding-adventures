## You'll want to know: দেওর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C483-JAMAI483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**দেওর** — *deor* — "a husband's younger brother".

> দেওর বউদির সঙ্গে ঠাট্টা করে।
>
> *deor boudir sôngge ṭhāṭṭā kôre.*
>
> A husband's younger brother jokes with his elder brother's wife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দেওর** (*deor*) comes from Sanskrit *devṛ*, "a husband's brother" (CDIAL 6546), which CDIAL lists in this Bengali form.
