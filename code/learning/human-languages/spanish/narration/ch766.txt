Spanish, chapter 766: Retos y logros.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el reto — the challenge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for imagination, then the Spanish for to aspire.

You'll want to know: el reto.
el reto — "the challenge".
Aprender chino es un gran reto. Learning Chinese is a big challenge.
Desafío is a synonym.

The word, taken apart.
reto is built on retar (EtymDB), which comes from Old French reter "to accuse", from Latin reputāre "to reckon".

Lesson 2 of 5.
la ambición — ambition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to aspire, then the Spanish for the challenge.

You'll want to know: la ambición.
la ambición — "ambition".
Su ambición es abrir un restaurante. Her ambition is to open a restaurant.
It can be positive or negative.

The word, taken apart.
ambición comes from Latin ambitiō "a going round, canvassing" (EtymDB), from ambīre "to go around": a Roman candidate went round asking for votes.

Lesson 4 of 5.
el logro — the achievement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for ambition, then the Spanish for creative.

You'll want to know: el logro.
el logro — "the achievement".
Terminar la carrera fue un gran logro. Finishing my degree was a great achievement.
The verb is lograr.

The word, taken apart.
logro comes from Latin lucrum "gain, profit" (EtymDB), through lograr, from lucrārī "to gain"; in old Spanish logro also meant interest on a loan.
