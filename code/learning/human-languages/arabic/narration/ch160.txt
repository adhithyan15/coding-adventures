Arabic, chapter 160: To taste, To pour, To switch off, To fill, To bathe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ذاق (dhāqa) — to taste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to travel, then the Arabic for to cross.

You'll want to know: ذاق.
ذاق — dhāqa — "to taste".

The word, taken apart.
ذاق (dhāqa) is built on the native Arabic root dh-w-q, "to taste" (Lane). Dhāqa is the plain verb, "he tasted".
