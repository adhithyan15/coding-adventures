# Who you are and how you are — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-01, TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-02, TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Give your surname first, then your nickname, then ask someone their age.

## Grammar Lens: a name in two parts
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-01, TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-02, TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-04, TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-05] -->

| word | literally |
|---|---|
| **ఇంటిపేరు** | house name |
| **ముద్దుపేరు** | affection name |
| **పుట్టినరోజు** | born day |

Each is built from pieces you know, and the house name goes first.
