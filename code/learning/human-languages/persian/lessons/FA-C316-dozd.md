## You'll want to know: دزد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C316-DOZD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دزد** (*dozd*) — "a thief".

> دزد از پنجره وارد خانه شد.
>
> *Dozd az panjere vâred-e khâne shod.*
>
> The thief came into the house through the window.

Plural *dozd-hâ*. The verb *dozdidan* "to steal" is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dozd* is a native Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian *duzd*, "thief"; EtymDB lists it without a parent.
