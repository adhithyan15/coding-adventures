Gujarati, chapter 79: The Weekend, Tomorrow and Yesterday.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
શનિવાર (śanivār) — Saturday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for Thursday, then the Gujarati for Friday.

You'll want to know: શનિવાર.
શનિવાર (śanivār) — "Saturday".
