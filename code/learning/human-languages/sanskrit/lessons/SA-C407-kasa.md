## You'll want to know: कशा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C407-YANTA407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कशा** — *kaśā* — "a whip".

> यन्ता कशया अश्वं न ताडयति।
>
> *yantā kaśayā aśvaṃ na tāḍayati.*
>
> The driver does not strike the horse with the whip.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कशा** (*kaśā*) is the word MW gives for a whip, from the Rigveda on; MW connects it with a root √kaś but gives no meaning for it, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
