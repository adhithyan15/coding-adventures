## You'll want to know: समर्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C376-YOGYA376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**समर्थः** — *samarthaḥ* — "capable; able".

> यदि सः समर्थः, तर्हि कार्यं करिष्यति।
>
> *yadi saḥ samarthaḥ, tarhi kāryaṃ kariṣyati.*
>
> If he is capable, he will do the work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समर्थः** (*samarthaḥ*) is built from *sam*, "together", and *artha*, "aim"; MW gives *sam-artha* "having a suitable aim" and "competent, capable, able" (MW).
