## You'll want to know: अपि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C36-REPLY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अपि** (*api*) — "also, too".

**अपि** adds what you have just said to what came before. Put it after a word and you have piled that word on top: *dugdham api*, "milk as well".

Greek *epi-* is the same small word, and it fronts *epidemic*, *epilogue*, *epicentre* and *epitaph*. Latin *ob* is a cousin too. It is short, it never changes shape, and it does a great deal of work.

## What you've built
