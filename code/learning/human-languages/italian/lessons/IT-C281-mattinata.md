## You'll want to know: la mattinata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C281-VIGILIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la mattinata** — "the whole morning, the morning as a span".

> Ho passato la mattinata in biblioteca.
>
> *I spent the morning in the library.*

The ending *-ata* stresses the span of time, as in *la giornata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mattinata* is *mattina* "morning" with *-ata*, the ending for a stretch of time; *mattina* goes back to Latin *mātūtīnus* "of the early morning" (Lewis & Short).
