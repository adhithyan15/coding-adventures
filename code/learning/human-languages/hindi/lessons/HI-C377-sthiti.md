## You'll want to know: स्थिति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C377-HALAT377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थिति** — *sthiti* — "a situation".

> अगर स्थिति बिगड़ेगी तो हम शहर छोड़ देंगे।
>
> *agar sthiti bigṛegī to ham śahar choṛ deṅge.*
>
> If the situation gets worse, we'll leave the city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थिति** (*sthiti*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sthiti*, "standing, a state" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
