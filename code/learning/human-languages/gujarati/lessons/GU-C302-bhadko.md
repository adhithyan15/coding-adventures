## You'll want to know: ભડકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C302-BAMBO302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભડકો** — *bhaḍko* — "a blaze; a sudden flare of flame".

> તપેલીમાં તેલ ખૂબ ગરમ થયું અને મોટો ભડકો થયો.
>
> *tapelīmā̃ tel khūb garam thayũ ane moṭo bhaḍko thayo.*
>
> The oil in the pan got too hot and flared up in a big blaze.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

comes from *\*bhaṭ*, "a sudden movement or noise", a form CDIAL reconstructs, under which it lists Gujarati *bhaṛkũ*, "a blaze" (CDIAL 9365). A **ભડકો** is flame that leaps up all at once.

One more word for telling what happened.
