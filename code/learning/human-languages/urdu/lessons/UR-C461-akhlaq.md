## You'll want to know: اخلاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C461-MANNERS461-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اخلاق** — *akhlāq* — "manners; moral conduct".

> اس کے اخلاق بڑے اچھے ہیں۔
>
> *us ke akhlāq baṛe achchhe haiṅ.*
>
> He has very good manners.

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اخلاق** (*akhlāq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *akhlāq*, "traits of character".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
