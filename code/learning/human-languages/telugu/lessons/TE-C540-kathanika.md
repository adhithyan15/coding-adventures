# కథానిక (kathānika) — a short story

## You'll want to know: కథానిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C540-SAHITYAM540-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కథానిక** — *kathānika* — "a short story".

> ఆ పత్రికలో ఆమె కథానిక వచ్చింది.
>
> *ā patrikalō āme kathānika vaccindi.*
>
> Her short story appeared in that magazine.

Shorter than a కథ: the modern short-story form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కథానిక** (*kathānika*) is built on Sanskrit *kathā*, "conversation; story" (CDIAL 2704), with a diminutive ending.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
