## You'll want to know: la política
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C601-GOBIERNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la política** — "the politics; the policy".

> No me interesa la política.
>
> *I am not interested in politics.*

Also a policy: *la política de la empresa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la política* is a learned borrowing from Greek *politikē* 'the affairs of the city', built on *polis* 'city', through Latin *politicus* (EtymDB).
