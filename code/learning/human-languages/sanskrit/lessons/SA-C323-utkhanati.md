## You'll want to know: उत्खनति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C323-STUPA323-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्खनति** — *utkhanati* — "digs up; excavates".

> वैज्ञानिकाः भूमिम् उत्खनन्ति।
>
> *vaijñānikāḥ bhūmim utkhananti.*
>
> The scientists dig up the ground.

Past (he …): सः भूमिम् उत्खातवान्, *saḥ bhūmim utkhātavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्खनति** (*utkhanati*) is built from *ud*, "up, out", and the root √khan, "to dig", as in *khanati*; MW gives *ut-khan* as to dig up or out, excavate (MW).
