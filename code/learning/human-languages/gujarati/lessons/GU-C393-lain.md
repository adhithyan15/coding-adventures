## You'll want to know: લાઈન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C393-ENGINE393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લાઈન** — *lāīn* — "a railway line; a line".

> અમારા ગામ પાસે રેલવેની લાઈન છે.
>
> *amārā gām pāse relvenī lāīn che.*
>
> There is a railway line near our village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાઈન** (*lāīn*) is borrowed from English *line*, here the line of track a train runs on.
