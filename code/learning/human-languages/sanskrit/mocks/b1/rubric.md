# Sanskrit B1 mock rubric

Score either B1 mock against
[`task-shapes/b1.json`](../../task-shapes/b1.json). Reading, listening, writing,
and speaking are independent 100-point papers. A learner passes only by earning
at least **60/100 on every paper** in one administration; a 240-point total
cannot compensate for a failed skill.

## Reading — 100 points

Correspondence, public information, narrative, and informational-article parts
are worth 25 points each across seven keyed items. Divide each part evenly and
retain one decimal place. Score the keyed purpose, explicit detail, sequence,
inference, motive, paragraph function, or writer stance. B1 sources use
external sandhi; successful decoding remains part of the reading construct.

## Listening — 100 points

Transactions, interview, narrative, and short-talk parts are worth 25 points
each. Play parts 1 and 2 twice. Play parts 3 and 4 once after one unscored
orienting preview. Use only the confidential mastered files, delivered at
130–150 words per minute inside the task-shape duration bands. Score selected
answers only. Do not provide a transcript, romanization, dictionary,
translator, or learner-controlled replay.

## Writing — 100 points

The 50–70 word functional text is worth 35 points: task fulfilment and required
content 12, reader/purpose/register 7, organisation 4, Sanskrit vocabulary and
grammar 6, external sandhi 3, and orthographic control 3. The 130–170 word connected text
is worth 65 points: task fulfilment and development 18,
organisation and cohesion 13, audience-aware register 9, Sanskrit vocabulary
and grammar 15, external sandhi 5, and orthographic control 5. A response
outside its band loses only content that cannot be evidenced; there is no
automatic length penalty.

Orthographic control includes recognisable ordered letter bodies, vowel signs
and conjuncts, continuous śirorekhā where the word requires it, word-final
visarga or anusvara, and clear word boundaries. Romanized, traced, translated,
copied, non-Devanagari, or answer-model-dependent work earns no
independent-writing credit. Vedic accent and morphology remain outside this
assessment.

## Speaking — 100 points

The five-question interview is worth 20 points, the 150–180 second prepared
account 30, collaborative planning 30, and four-question follow-up discussion
20. Within each part award 30% for task fulfilment and relevant development,
25% for interaction and repair, 25% for Sanskrit vocabulary and grammar, 10%
for organisation, and 10% for pronunciation and fluency. Permit paper keyword
notes made during ten minutes of preparation, never a written answer script.
Accept a consistent documented pronunciation tradition. Score paired
candidates only on their own contributions; a partner's performance cannot
compensate.

## Evidence boundary

These source papers, confidential masters, keys, and rubric are not qualified
Sanskrit recordings, independent human review, standard setting, or book-only
validation. They make no readiness or validated-pass claim. Those artifacts
remain separate prerequisites. `assessment.json` is active as a source-artifact
contract; its presence does not satisfy them.
