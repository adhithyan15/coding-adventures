## You'll want to know: उत्साह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C453-ANAND453-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्साह** — *utsāh* — "zeal; enthusiasm".

> नई नौकरी के लिए मीरा में बहुत उत्साह है।
>
> *naī naukrī ke liye Mīrā meṁ bahut utsāh hai.*
>
> Mira is full of enthusiasm for her new job.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्साह** (*utsāh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *utsāha*, which Monier-Williams glosses as strength of will, effort and energy (Monier-Williams).
