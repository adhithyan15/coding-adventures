Urdu, chapter 93: Quarter to, Half, Now, Then, When?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پونے (paune) — a quarter to (less a quarter).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for half past, then the Urdu for a quarter past.

You'll want to know: پونے.
پونے — paune — "a quarter to (less a quarter)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: paune]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: paune, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say paune]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for half past, then the Urdu for a quarter past, then say paune again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does پونے (paune) mean? ("A quarter to (less a quarter)".) Say it once more.
