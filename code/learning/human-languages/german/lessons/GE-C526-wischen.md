## You'll want to know: wischen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C526-PUTZEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**wischen** — "to wipe; to mop".

> Am Samstag wische ich den Boden in der Küche.
>
> *On Saturday I mop the kitchen floor.*

*Staub wischen* — to dust.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wischen* is inherited from Middle High German *wischen* and Old High German *wisken*, from Proto-Germanic *wiskijaną* (EtymDB).
