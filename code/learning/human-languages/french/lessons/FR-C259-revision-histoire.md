# Review — the journey, retold

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-DABORD-01, FR-LEX-RACONTER-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "first". (*D'abord.*) Say "I tell a story". (*Je raconte une
histoire.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-RECIT-ORDRE-02, FR-GRAM-RECIT-SQUELETTE-02, FR-SKILL-READ-NARRATIVE-01, FR-GRAM-DECOR-EVENEMENT-01] -->

Two chapters, and a whole story. A scene in the imparfait: *il faisait
froid*, *il y avait beaucoup de monde*, *j'étais inquiet*. Events in the
passé composé, held in line by *d'abord*, *ensuite*, *puis* and *enfin*. A
turn with *mais*. And the joint where a scene meets an event: *quand*.

[PAUSE 2s] Now the journey from the reading, told by you. Here is its
skeleton, scene first, then the events:

> *C'était samedi. Il faisait froid et il pleuvait.*
> *D'abord, j'ai attendu le train. Ensuite, je suis monté.*
> *Quand je suis arrivé, mon ami m'attendait.*
> *Enfin, je suis rentré à la maison. J'étais content.*

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-RECIT-SQUELETTE-02, FR-GRAM-DECOR-EVENEMENT-01, FR-LEX-RACONTER-01, FR-SKILL-READ-NARRATIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which word puts the first event in place? (***D'abord.***) Is
*j'étais content* scene or event? (**Scene** — a feeling has no edge.) Which
word marks the turn in a story? (***Mais.***) And which Latin verb is inside
*raconter*? (***Computāre***, to count it out.)
