## You'll want to know: el inquilino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C667-APARTAMENTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el inquilino** — "the tenant".

> El inquilino paga el alquiler cada mes.
>
> *The tenant pays the rent every month.*

A woman is *la inquilina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inquilino* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inquilīnus* "a tenant, a lodger" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from *incolere* "to dwell in".
