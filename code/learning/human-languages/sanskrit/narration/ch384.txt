Sanskrit, chapter 384: Effort.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उद्यमः (udyamaḥ) — perseverance; exertion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a heartfelt wish, then the Sanskrit for more and more.

You'll want to know: उद्यमः.
उद्यमः — udyamaḥ — "perseverance; exertion".
यदि उद्यमः अस्ति, तर्हि सफलता अपि भविष्यति। yadi udyamaḥ asti, tarhi saphalatā api bhaviṣyati. If there is perseverance, success will follow.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उद्यमः (udyamaḥ) is built on ud, "up", and the root √yam, "to hold"; MW gives ud-yama "undertaking" and "exertion, strenuous and continued effort, perseverance" (MW).

यदि भवान् यथाशक्ति यतते, तर्हि अलम्। yadi bhavān yathāśakti yatate, tarhi alam. If you try as hard as you can, that is enough.

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनुतिष्ठति (anutiṣṭhati) — carries out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for constancy, then the Sanskrit for as far as one can.

You'll want to know: अनुतिष्ठति.
अनुतिष्ठति — anutiṣṭhati — "carries out".
यदि सः योजनाम् अनुतिष्ठति, तर्हि लाभः भविष्यति। yadi saḥ yojanām anutiṣṭhati, tarhi lābhaḥ bhaviṣyati. If he carries out the plan, there will be a gain.
Past (he …): सः योजनाम् अनुष्ठितवान्, saḥ yojanām anuṣṭhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुतिष्ठति (anutiṣṭhati) is built on anu, "after", and the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives anu-ṣṭhā "to carry out, to perform, to practise" and anu-ṣṭhita "done, practised" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: प्रोत्साहयति.
प्रोत्साहयति — protsāhayati — "encourages".
यदि माता प्रोत्साहयति, तर्हि बालः उत्साहेन पठति। yadi mātā protsāhayati, tarhi bālaḥ utsāhena paṭhati. If his mother encourages him, the child studies with zeal.
Past (he …): सः छात्रान् प्रोत्साहितवान्, saḥ chātrān protsāhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रोत्साहयति (protsāhayati) is built on pra, ut and the root √sah, "to be able, to endure", in its causative form; MW gives prot-sāhita "incited, stimulated, encouraged" (MW).
