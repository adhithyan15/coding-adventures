## You'll want to know: ずらす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C345-HENKOU345-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ずらす** — *zurasu* — "to shift; to move (a time) a little".

> **かいぎが ながく なったら、ひるごはんを ずらします。** — *kaigi ga nagaku nattara, hirugohan o zurashimasu* — "If the meeting runs long, we will move lunch back a little."

Polite **ずらします**, *zurashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが じかんを ずらしました。**, *sensei ga jikan o zurashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ずらす** (*zurasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled; JMdict gives it no kanji spelling (JMdict).
