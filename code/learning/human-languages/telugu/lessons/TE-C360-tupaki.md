# తుపాకి (tupāki) — a gun

## You'll want to know: తుపాకి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C360-AYUDHAM360-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తుపాకి** — *tupāki* — "a gun".

> పోలీసు దగ్గర తుపాకి ఉంది.
>
> *pōlīsu daggara tupāki undi.*
>
> The policeman has a gun.

Plural: తుపాకులు, *tupākulu*.

తుపాకి పేల్చు, to fire a gun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తుపాకి** (*tupāki*) is the Telugu word for a gun; its origin is not settled.
