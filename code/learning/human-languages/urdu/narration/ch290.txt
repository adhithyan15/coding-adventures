Urdu, chapter 290: A Warning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آفت (āfat) — a disaster; a calamity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to melt, then the Urdu for to rush in a panic.

You'll want to know: آفت.
آفت — āfat — "a disaster; a calamity".
یہ ایک بڑی آفت تھی۔ yih ek baṛī āfat thī. This was a great disaster.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
آفت (āfat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic āfa, "a blight, a misfortune". It is feminine: بڑی آفت.

You'll want to know: ٹلنا.
ٹلنا — ṭalnā — "to be averted; to pass off".

Past (it …): وہ خطرہ ٹل گیا, voh khatrā ṭal gayā.

The word, taken apart.
ٹلنا (ṭalnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭalnā, "to pass by, to be displaced", under Sanskrit ṭálati (CDIAL 5450). A danger that ٹل گیا moved off without striking.
