## You'll want to know: सिलसिला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C553-KRAM553-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सिलसिला** — *silsilā* — "a series; a run (of events)".

> बारिश का सिलसिला तीन दिन से चल रहा है।
>
> *Bāriś kā silsilā tīn din se cal rahā hai.*
>
> The rain has gone on for three days in a row.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिलसिला** (*silsilā*) is usually traced to Arabic *silsila*, "a chain", through Persian.
