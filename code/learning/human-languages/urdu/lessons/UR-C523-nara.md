## You'll want to know: نعرہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C523-STADIUM523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نعرہ** — *nārā* — "a cheer; a slogan".

> اسٹیڈیم میں ہر طرف نعروں کی آواز تھی۔
>
> *isṭeḍiyam meṅ har taraf nāroṅ kī āvāz thī.*
>
> The sound of cheering filled the stadium.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نعرہ** (*nārā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *naʿra*, "a shout, a cry".
