## You'll want to know: tā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C31-PEOPLE31-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tā* — "she; her".

> *tā shì wǒ de tóngxué* — She's a classmate of mine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tā* sounds exactly like the word for he. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
