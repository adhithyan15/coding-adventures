Japanese, chapter 504: Books and Art.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ぶんがく (bungaku) — literature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a musician, then the Japanese for to flutter.

You'll want to know: ぶんがく.
ぶんがく — bungaku — "literature".
あには にほんの ぶんがくを べんきょうしています。 — ani wa nihon no bungaku o benkyō shiteimasu — "My elder brother studies Japanese literature."

The word, taken apart.
ぶんがく (bungaku) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are bun, "sentence" (Middle Chinese mjun) and gaku, "study" (haewk) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
