## Changed — அ, ஆ, எ, ங and ஷ are written without lifting the pen

Native Tamil writers draw these five letters in one stroke (HP Labs India's
LipiTk Tamil recognizer: 91%, 97%, 92%, 85% and 93% of its stored prototypes;
counts and shares only). Their strips and steps now do too. அ and ஆ (TA-S119,
TA-S120) climb the right upright from the end of the horizontal and draw it
down, with no lift before it. எ (TA-S112) starts inside its bowl and draws the
right upright down last. ங (TA-S130) goes down its left upright and back up
first and draws the right upright upward last, instead of starting with it.
ஷ (TA-S140) draws Narale's four numbered parts in one stroke instead of four.
Every "Pen lifts" line is now 0. The words ஆம், என், நீங்கள் and ஆனால் redraw
their strips with the new letters.
