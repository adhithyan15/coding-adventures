# A message about tomorrow

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SUNEHA-198, PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *a message*. Then *Aman*.

[PAUSE 1s] Aman sends you a message. Read it for one thing first: what time you
have to be at the station.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-READ-MESSAGE-224]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SUNEHA-198, PA-LEX-SAT-SRI-AKAL-01, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-05, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218, PA-LEX-KALL-216, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-TIME-VAJE-01, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-REQUEST-NA-213, PA-LEX-SHAID-223, PA-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-AANGE-04] -->

> **ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ ਜੀ!**
>
> **ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਹਾਂ।**
> **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਗਿਆ।**
> **ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ, ਪਰ ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਤੀ।**
> **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਰੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।**
> **ਰੇਲ ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ।**
> **ਮੈਂ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ।**
> **ਟਿਕਟ ਨਾ ਭੁੱਲੋ!**
> **ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਾ ਆਵੇਗਾ।**
> **ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ।**

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: the message once, straight through, without translating]

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: it a second time, and find every line with a clock time]

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C90-TOWN90-04, PA-TRAVEL-TICKET-01, PA-LEX-C60-VERBS60-02, PA-LEX-BHARA, PA-LEX-MERA-02, PA-JOIN-PAR-01, PA-GRAMMAR-REQUEST-O-213] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ** comes twice: with **ਗਿਆ** it is yesterday, with
**ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ** tomorrow. **ਟਿਕਟ ਨਾ ਭੁੱਲੋ!** is a request built like **ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ**,
on **ਭੁੱਲਣਾ**, "to forget". The last line but one is the only one that is not
sure.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-READ-MESSAGE-224, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186, PA-LEX-SHAID-223] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU ANSWER: what did Aman buy yesterday? (A book: **ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ।**)]
- [YOU ANSWER: what did he not do? (He didn't drink tea.)]
- [YOU ANSWER: when will the train come? (At nine: **ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ**.)]
- [YOU ANSWER: who will perhaps come? (His brother.)]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-READ-MESSAGE-224, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221] -->

[PAUSE 3s] When will Aman be at the station? (At eight.) What must you not
forget? (The ticket.)
