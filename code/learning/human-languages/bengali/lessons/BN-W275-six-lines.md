# Six lines about a trip

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SKILL-C267-READ-STORY-01, BN-LEX-C275-SONGGE-01, BN-LEX-C271-NOUKA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The A2 message asked for a practical note to a named reader. This task asks for six lines, and they tell a story: a trip, by train, bus or boat, real or invented.

[PAUSE 1s] Pick one reader: Arun, **অরুণ**, or Mira, **মীরা**. Say **সঙ্গে** and **নৌকা** aloud; both may go in.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-SCENE-EVENT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FIRST-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FINALLY-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-KARON-BECAUSE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TAI-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-GENITIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-PAST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C274-LE-FORM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A told story is not a list of events. It has a **scene**, in **-ছিল-**, or with **যখন … তখন**. It has **events** in the plain past, kept in order with **প্রথমে**, **তারপর** and **শেষে**, and joined with the *-e* form. It has a **reason**, with **কারণ**, **তাই** or **-এর জন্য**, and a **feeling**, with **আমার … লাগল** or **ভয় করছিল**.

[PAUSE 2s] A good one ends by looking at what might have been: **যদি …, তাহলে … যেতাম**.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-SCENE-EVENT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FIRST-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FINALLY-01, BN-LEX-C267-HOTHAT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-KARON-BECAUSE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-JONNO-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TAI-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-ASK-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-LOST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-MISSED-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-GENITIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-PAST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, BN-LEX-C275-SONGGE-01, BN-LEX-C271-NOUKA-01, BN-LEX-C270-DUR-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C273-THEKE-PORJONTO-01, BN-LEX-C264-GHOTA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C265-MURDHANYA-SHA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C266-TTHA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C267-KHANDA-TA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C268-MURDHANYA-NA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C269-U-01, BN-SCRIPT-C270-UU-KAR-01, BN-SCRIPT-C271-AU-KAR-01, BN-SCRIPT-C272-O-INDEP-01, BN-SCRIPT-C273-OI-KAR-01, BN-SCRIPT-C274-DDHA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C275-UMA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] On paper, in Bengali script, write **six lines** to your reader about the trip, one line for each of these:

1. the scene: when it was, and what you were doing as it began, with **-ছিলাম** or **যখন … তখন**;
2. the first thing that happened, with **প্রথমে**, and who you went with, with **সঙ্গে**;
3. what happened next, with **তারপর** or an *-e* form, and one thing that went wrong: **হারিয়ে গেছে** or **ছেড়ে গেছে**, or **হঠাৎ**;
4. why, with **কারণ**, **তাই** or **-এর জন্য**;
5. how it ended and how you felt, with **শেষে** and **আমার … লাগল** or **ভয় করছিল**;
6. what might have been, with **যদি …, তাহলে … -তাম**, and how far you went, with **দূর** or **থেকে … পর্যন্ত**.

[PAUSE 3s] There is no model in this lesson. Use only words you already write. The work is choosing the right past for each line, and joining them.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, BN-SCRIPT-C271-AU-KAR-01, BN-SCRIPT-C275-UMA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C267-KHANDA-TA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Now read your lines as you read Mira's Saturday. Mark every verb **s** for scene, **e** for event. Line one should be mostly **s**; lines two and three mostly **e**.

[PAUSE 2s] Then three checks. A past ending is **-ল**, said *-lô*: **গেল**, **এল**. **নৌকা** has **ৌ** around its **ন**, and **সঙ্গে** has **ঙ** joined to **গ**. **হঠাৎ** ends in **ৎ**.

[PAUSE 2s] Last, read line six alone. Does the if half take **-ত-** or **-লে** (**থাকত**, **থাকলে**), and the other half **-ত-** (**যেতাম**)?
