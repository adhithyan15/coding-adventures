## You'll want to know: વસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C319-KILLO319-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વસવું** — *vasvũ* — "to settle (in a place)".

> ઘણાં વરસ પહેલાં લોકો આ નદી કિનારે વસ્યા.
>
> *ghaṇā̃ varas pahelā̃ loko ā nadī kināre vasyā.*
>
> Many years ago people settled on the bank of this river.

Past (he …): તે અહીં વસ્યો, *te ahī̃ vasyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વસવું** (*vasvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vásati*, "dwells", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "settles" (CDIAL 11435). People **વસે છે** where they make their homes; **રહેવું** is everyday living.
