## You'll want to know: yūndǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C299-RESCUE299-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yūndǎo* — "to faint; to pass out".

> *tā tūrán yūndǎo le* — She suddenly fainted.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yūndǎo* is built from *yūn* 'dizzy' and *dǎo* 'to fall over', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjunH* and *tawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a halo round the sun or moon' and the second 'to fall flat'. The sense 'dizzy' is not given there, and the level tone of yūn is irregular for *hjunH*.
