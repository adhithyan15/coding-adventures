## You'll want to know: भुंक्ते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C338-SVARGA338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भुंक्ते** — *bhuṅkte* — "reaps; enjoys".

> जनः पुण्यस्य फलं भुंक्ते।
>
> *janaḥ puṇyasya phalaṃ bhuṅkte.*
>
> A person reaps the fruit of merit.

Past (he …): सः फलं भुक्तवान्, *saḥ phalaṃ bhuktavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भुंक्ते** (*bhuṅkte*) comes from the root √bhuj, "to enjoy, use, eat", the root of *bhojana*, "a meal"; MW gives it as to enjoy, use, possess, consume (MW).
