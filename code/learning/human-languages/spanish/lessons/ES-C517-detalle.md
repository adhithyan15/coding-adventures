## You'll want to know: el detalle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C517-TESTIGO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el detalle** — "the detail".

> Recuerdo cada detalle de aquel día.
>
> *I remember every detail of that day.*

*¡Qué detalle!* — how thoughtful! — said about a small kind gesture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*detalle* was borrowed from French *détail*, which is usually traced to *tailler* 'to cut', from Late Latin *taliāre*. A detail is a small piece cut off from the whole.
