## You'll want to know: चमत्कार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C338-PARI338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चमत्कार** — *camatkār* — "a miracle; a wonder".

> बच्चे का बचना एक चमत्कार था।
>
> *bacce kā bacnā ek camatkār thā.*
>
> The child's survival was a miracle.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चमत्कार** (*camatkār*) is Sanskrit *camatkāra*, "astonishment, a marvel" (Monier-Williams): something that cannot be explained.
