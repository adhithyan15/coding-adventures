## You'll want to know: шашки
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C504-BOARDGAMES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**шашки** (*sháshki*) — "draughts, checkers".

> По вечерам дед играет с соседом в шашки.
>
> *Po vecherám ded igráyet s sosédom v sháshki.*
>
> In the evenings Grandad plays draughts with the neighbour.

Plural. *Играть в шашки* — to play draughts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *шашки* without a source; it is usually traced to *шах* 'check', from Persian *shāh* 'king', like *шахматы* 'chess'.
