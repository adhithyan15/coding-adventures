Lesson 1 of 5.
предсказывать (predskázyvat) — to predict, to forecast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to make up one’s mind, then the Russian for depending on.

You'll want to know: предсказывать.
предсказывать (predskázyvat) — "to predict, to forecast".
Трудно предсказывать будущее. Trúdno predskázyvat búdushcheye. It is hard to predict the future.
Imperfective; perfective предсказать (предскажу, предскажешь).

The word, taken apart.
предсказывать (predskázyvat) is built from пред- "before" and сказать "to say", on казать, Proto-Slavic kazati (EtymDB): to say a thing before it happens.

Lesson 2 of 5.
угадывать (ugádyvat) — to guess (right).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for depending on, then the Russian for to predict.

You'll want to know: угадывать.
угадывать (ugádyvat) — "to guess (right)".
Он всегда угадывает, что я хочу. On vsegdá ugádyvayet, chto ya khochú. He always guesses what I want.
Imperfective; perfective угадать. It means guessing right; to guess at something is догадываться.

The word, taken apart.
угадывать (ugádyvat) is built from у- and гадать "to guess, to tell fortunes", an inherited Slavic verb: to hit on the answer.

Masculine. Colloquial, from office talk; in formal Russian, крайний срок.

Lesson 5 of 5.
временный (vrémennyy) — temporary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a deadline, then the Russian for permanent.

You'll want to know: временный.
временный (vrémennyy) — "temporary".
Это временная работа на лето. Éto vrémennaya rabóta na léto. It is a temporary job for the summer.
The opposite of постоянный (postoyánnyy). Временно — for the time being.

The word, taken apart.
временный (vrémennyy) is built on время "time", inherited from Proto-Slavic vermę; the -ре- is the Church Slavonic shape, beside native веремя in old texts.
