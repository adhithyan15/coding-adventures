Arabic, chapter 485: Sky and light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صحو (ṣaḥw) — clear weather; cloudless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for drought, then the Arabic for to evaporate.

You'll want to know: صحو.
صحو — ṣaḥw — "clear weather; cloudless".
الطقس صحو اليوم. aṭ-ṭaqs ṣaḥw al-yawm. The weather is clear today.

The word, taken apart.
صحو (ṣaḥw) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-ḥ-w, "to be cloudless" (Lane). Lane gives it of a day when the clouds have gone.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قوس قزح (qaws quzaḥ) — a rainbow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to evaporate, then the Arabic for clear weather.

You'll want to know: قوس قزح.
قوس قزح — qaws quzaḥ — "a rainbow".
ظهر قوس قزح بعد المطر. ẓahara qaws quzaḥ baʿda al-maṭar. A rainbow appeared after the rain.

The word, taken apart.
قوس قزح (qaws quzaḥ) is "the bow of quzaḥ": qaws, "a bow", is built on the native root q-w-s (Lane), but the origin of quzaḥ is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
أشرق (ashraqa) — to shine; to rise (of the sun).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a rainbow, then the Arabic for dew.

You'll want to know: أشرق.
أشرق — ashraqa — "to shine; to rise (of the sun)".
أشرقت الشمس بعد الضباب. ashraqat ash-shams baʿda aḍ-ḍabāb. The sun came out after the fog.
Present: يشرق, yushriq.

The word, taken apart.
أشرق (ashraqa) is built on the native Arabic root sh-r-q, "to rise (of the sun)" (Lane). It is Form IV; sharq, "east", and shurūq, "sunrise", share the root.
