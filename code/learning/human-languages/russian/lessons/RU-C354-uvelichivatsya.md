## You'll want to know: увеличиваться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C354-BOLSHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**увеличиваться** (*uvelíchivatsya*) — "to increase, to grow".

> Цены увеличиваются каждый год.
>
> *Tsény uvelíchivayutsya kázhdyy god.*
>
> Prices increase every year.

Imperfective; the perfective is *увеличиться*. The transitive verb is *увеличивать*, to increase something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**увеличиваться** is built on *великий* "great", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*velikŭ*, from *\*velĭjĭ* "great" (EtymDB): to become greater.
