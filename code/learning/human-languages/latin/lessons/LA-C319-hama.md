## You'll want to know: hama, hamae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C319-SIPHO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**hama, hamae** — "a fire bucket".

> Vigilēs aquam hamīs portābant.
>
> *The watchmen were carrying water in fire buckets.*

Feminine, short *a*. The watchmen of the fire service kept them ready.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**hama** was borrowed from Greek *amē*, "a bucket" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).
