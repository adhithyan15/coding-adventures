## You'll want to know: der Heizkörper
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C427-KLIMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Heizkörper** — "the radiator".

> Sie hängt die nassen Socken über den Heizkörper.
>
> *She hangs the wet socks over the radiator.*

Same form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Heizkörper* is built from *heizen* 'to heat', an inherited Germanic verb (EtymDB), and *Körper* 'body', which goes back to Latin *corpus* (Lewis & Short).
