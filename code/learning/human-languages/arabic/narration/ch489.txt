Arabic, chapter 489: Panic, terror and calm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ذعر (dhuʿr) — panic, alarm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to grieve, then the Arabic for to sigh.

You'll want to know: ذعر.
ذعر — dhuʿr — "panic, alarm".
أصاب الناس ذعر بعد الزلزال. aṣāba an-nās dhuʿr baʿda az-zilzāl. Panic seized the people after the earthquake.

The word, taken apart.
ذعر (dhuʿr) is built on the native Arabic root dh-ʿ-r, "to frighten, to alarm" (Lane). Dhuʿr is a sudden fright that runs through a crowd.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 2 of 5.
رعب (ruʿb) — terror.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to sigh, then the Arabic for panic.

You'll want to know: رعب.
رعب — ruʿb — "terror".
شاهد أخي فيلم رعب أمس. shāhada akhī fīlm ruʿb ams. My brother watched a horror film yesterday.
فيلم رعب (ruʿb) is a horror film.

The word, taken apart.
رعب (ruʿb) is built on the native Arabic root r-ʿ-b, "to fill with fear" (Lane). Ruʿb is fear at its strongest.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: هدوء.
هدوء — hudūʾ — "calm, quiet".
أحب هدوء القرية في المساء. uḥibbu hudūʾ al-qarya fī al-masāʾ. I love the quiet of the village in the evening.

The word, taken apart.
هدوء (hudūʾ) is built on the native Arabic root h-d-ʾ, "to become still, to be at rest" (Lane). Hudūʾ is the stillness itself: of a place, the sea or a person.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ارتعش (irtaʿasha) — to shiver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for terror, then the Arabic for calm.

You'll want to know: ارتعش.
ارتعش — irtaʿasha — "to shiver".
ارتعش الولد من الخوف. irtaʿasha al-walad min al-khawf. The boy shivered with fear.
Present: يرتعش, yartaʿish.
It is said of cold as well as fear: ارتعش (irtaʿasha) من البرد.

The word, taken apart.
ارتعش (irtaʿasha) is built on the native Arabic root r-ʿ-sh, "to tremble, to quiver" (Lane). Form VIII irtaʿasha is to shake all over.

Lesson 5 of 5.
أخاف (akhāfa) — to frighten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for calm, then the Arabic for to shiver.

You'll want to know: أخاف.
أخاف — akhāfa — "to frighten".
أخافني صوت الرعد في الليل. akhāfanī ṣawt ar-raʿd fī al-layl. The sound of the thunder frightened me in the night.
Present: يخيف, yukhīf.
Do not confuse it with أخاف (akhāfa) read akhāfu, I am afraid, the present of خاف.

The word, taken apart.
أخاف (akhāfa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-w-f, "fear" (Lane). It is Form IV of khāfa, to be afraid: to make someone afraid. Mukhīf, frightening, is its participle.
