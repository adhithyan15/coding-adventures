मदद — madad — "help".

The word, taken apart.
मदद (madad) is a loan from Arabic madad, "help, reinforcement", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is m-d-d (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अख़बार (akhbār) — a newspaper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for help, then the Hindi for news.

You'll want to know: अख़बार.
अख़बार — akhbār — "a newspaper".

The word, taken apart.
अख़बार (akhbār) is a loan from Arabic akhbār, "reports, news", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is kh-b-r (Lane). It is the plural of khabar, "news"; Hindi uses it for the newspaper that carries them.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रंग (raṅg) — a colour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a newspaper, then the Hindi for a thing.

You'll want to know: रंग.
रंग — raṅg — "a colour".

The word, taken apart.
रंग (raṅg) has two possible sources: Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour", whose inherited Hindi form CDIAL gives as rāṁg (CDIAL 10560), and Persian rang, "colour". The two may have merged.
