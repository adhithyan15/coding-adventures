## You'll want to know: rigeō, rigēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C486-PRUINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rigeō, rigēre** — "to be stiff with cold, to be numb; to be stiff".

> Manūs viātōrum frīgore rigent.
>
> *The travellers' hands are stiff with cold.*

*rigeō, rigēre, riguī*, second conjugation, no supine. English *rigid* comes from *rigidus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *rigeō* is not settled: Lewis & Short suggest a link with Greek *rhigeō* 'to shiver' and *frīgeō*, and EtymDB gives no parent. English *rigid* comes from *rigidus*, built on it.
