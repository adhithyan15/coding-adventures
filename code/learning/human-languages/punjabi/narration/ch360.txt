Punjabi, chapter 360: Exam Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ (imtihān) — an examination; a test.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for attention, then the Punjabi for to put right.

You'll want to know: ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ.
ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ — imtihān — "an examination; a test".
ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਸਾਡਾ ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ ਹੈ। agle hafte sāḍā imtihān hai. We have an exam next week.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ (imtihān) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic imtiḥān, "examination".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਅੰਕ (aṅk) — a mark in a test; a figure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an examination, then the Punjabi for an exam paper.

You'll want to know: ਅੰਕ.
ਅੰਕ — aṅk — "a mark in a test; a figure".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਗਣਿਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੰਗੇ ਅੰਕ ਮਿਲੇ। mainū̃ gaṇit vich chaṅge aṅk mile. I got good marks in mathematics.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਅੰਕ (aṅk) is taken over from Sanskrit aṅka, "mark", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
