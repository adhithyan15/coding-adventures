# Hide the bank, then write

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-CUE-MAP-01, PA-FORM-DATE-B-01] -->

Study B **੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫** for five seconds. Touch day, month, and year, then
cover this line and the earlier date lessons.

## Writing — delayed entry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-DATE-01, PA-FORM-DATE-B-01, PA-FORM-DATE-CUE-MAP-01, PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

After the short delay, the fictional card shows **ਖ**. Fill its **ਤਾਰੀਖ਼**
line from memory. Do not uncover the bank before you finish. Write the day,
the month, and the year in the printed order, with two slash marks.

## Guided Practice — uncover and repair
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-MONTH-MAP-01, PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->

Uncover B only after the attempt. Compare one box at a time. If the month is
wrong, correct only the middle two digits; keep the other boxes unchanged.
