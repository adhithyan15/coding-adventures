# తప్పుకో (tappukō) — to step aside; to withdraw

## You'll want to know: తప్పుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C352-VEMBADI352-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తప్పుకో** — *tappukō* — "to step aside; to withdraw".

> పోలీసు జీపు రాగానే జనం తప్పుకున్నారు.
>
> *pōlīsu jīpu rāgānē janaṁ tappukunnāru.*
>
> As soon as the police jeep came, people stepped aside.

Past (I …): తప్పుకున్నాను, *tappukunnānu*.

Also to pull out: పోటీ నుంచి తప్పుకో, to withdraw from a contest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తప్పుకో** (*tappukō*) is native Dravidian: *tappu*, "to miss, slip, escape" (DEDR 3071), with the self-ending *-kō*: to slip out of the way.
