## You'll want to know: riservato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C471-TIMIDEZZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**riservato** — "reserved, private (of a person); booked".

> Mio figlio è un ragazzo riservato e parla poco di sé.
>
> *My son is a reserved boy and says little about himself.*

Of a table or a seat it means booked: *un tavolo riservato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*riservato* is the past participle of *riservare* "to keep back", a learned borrowing from Latin *reservāre* (Lewis & Short), built on *servāre* "to keep, to watch over".
