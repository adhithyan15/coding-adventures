## You'll want to know: чёлка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C449-STRIZHKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**чёлка** (*chyólka*) — "a fringe (of hair), bangs".

> У девочки длинная чёлка.
>
> *U dévochki dlínnaya chyólka.*
>
> The girl has a long fringe.

Feminine. In American English, bangs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*чёлка* is usually derived from *чело* 'a brow', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *čelo* 'a forehead' (EtymDB): the hair that falls on the brow. EtymDB does not list *чёлка* itself.
