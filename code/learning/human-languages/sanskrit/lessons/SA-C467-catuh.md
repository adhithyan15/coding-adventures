## You'll want to know: चाटुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C467-SISTA467-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चाटुः** — *cāṭuḥ* — "flattery; sweet talk".

> धूर्तः मधुरं चाटुं वदति।
>
> *dhūrtaḥ madhuraṃ cāṭuṃ vadati.*
>
> The rogue speaks sweet flattery.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चाटुः** (*cāṭuḥ*) MW gives "pleasing or graceful words or discourse, flattery"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
