## You'll want to know: लालसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C460-BICHHAD460-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लालसा** — *lālsā* — "a longing; a craving".

> मीरा को अच्छी नौकरी की लालसा है।
>
> *Mīrā ko acchī naukrī kī lālsā hai.*
>
> Mira longs for a good job.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लालसा** (*lālsā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *lālasā*, "longing or ardent desire" (Monier-Williams).
