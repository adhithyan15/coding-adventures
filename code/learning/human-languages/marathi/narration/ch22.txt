Marathi, chapter 22: The Doorway Signs at Long Distance.
9 lessons. All 9 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 9.
The doorway word, much later — distant doorway warm-up — meaning before signs.

Warm-up.
Close every earlier model and listen before picking up the pencil.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear dhanyavād. Say its meaning, read धन्यवाद, then cover the model and write it once. This reconnects meaning and script without adding anything new.

Lesson 6 of 9.
The six marks (sahā khuṇā), a whole book later — distant retrieval of the six marks, by sound and place, with no page.

Warm-up.
You have not been asked for these since the counting chapter. Say them anyway, and notice which one comes slowest.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Short i — before, above, or below? (Before.)
[pause 2 seconds]
Short u? (Below.)
[pause 2 seconds]
Long ū? (Below, deeper.)
[pause 2 seconds]
The vocalic r? (Below, opening the other way.)
[pause 2 seconds]
The candrabindu? (Above, in a crescent.)
[pause 2 seconds]
And short a standing alone? (Its own letter.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 48 seconds]
From their sounds alone, name all six marks (sahā khuṇā) and say for each whether it is written before, above, below, or on its own.

Lesson 8 of 9.
The curled row (vaḷlelī olī), a whole book later — distant retrieval of the curled row, by sound and place, with no page.

Warm-up.
These five are the reason the romanizations in this book carry dots under some letters and not others.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
The four curled letters. (ṭa, ṭha, ḍa, ṇa.)
[pause 2 seconds]
What is the dot under ṭ telling you? (Curl the tongue back.)
[pause 2 seconds]
Which letter ends bolṇe and khāṇe? (ṇa.)
[pause 2 seconds]
And ba with the voice switched off? (pa.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 44 seconds]
Name the four curled letters, say what the dot under a romanized letter means, and name the lip letter that is ba with the voice off.
