Persian, chapter 388: Pollution and recycling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آلودگی (âludegi) — pollution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a majority, then the Persian for discrimination.

You'll want to know: آلودگی.
آلودگی (âludegi) — "pollution".
آلودگی هوا در شهرهای بزرگ زیاد است. âludegi-ye havâ dar shahrhâ-ye bozorg ziyâd ast. Air pollution is high in big cities.
From âlude "dirty, polluted", the participle of âludan "to stain", + -gi.

The word, taken apart.
آلودگی (âludegi) is native Persian, built on âlude "stained, polluted", the participle of âludan "to stain", with the noun suffix -gi.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بازیافت (bâzyâft) — recycling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for discrimination, then the Persian for pollution.

You'll want to know: بازیافت.
بازیافت (bâzyâft) — "recycling".
بازیافت کاغذ درختها را نجات میدهد. bâzyâft-e kâghaz derakhthâ râ nejât midahad. Recycling paper saves trees.
Bâz "again, back" + yâft "found", from yâftan "to find".

The word, taken apart.
بازیافت (bâzyâft) is a modern native coinage built from bâz "back, again" + yâft, the past stem of yâftan "to find". Recycling finds a material again.

Lesson 3 of 5.
بازیافت کردن (bâzyâft kardan) — to recycle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for pollution, then the Persian for recycling.

You'll want to know: بازیافت کردن.
بازیافت کردن (bâzyâft kardan) — "to recycle".
ما بطریهای پلاستیکی را بازیافت میکنیم. mâ bottrihâ-ye plâstiki râ bâzyâft mikonim. We recycle plastic bottles.
Bâzyâft + kardan (stem kon-). The object takes râ.

The word, taken apart.
بازیافت کردن (bâzyâft kardan) is native Persian, built from bâzyâft "recycling", from bâz "again" and yâft "found", with kardan "to do".

Lesson 4 of 5.
مصرف (masraf) — consumption, use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for recycling, then the Persian for to recycle.

You'll want to know: مصرف.
مصرف (masraf) — "consumption, use".
مصرف برق در تابستان بالا میرود. masraf-e barq dar tâbestân bâlâ miravad. Electricity use goes up in summer.
Also the dosage of a medicine: tarz-e masraf.

The word, taken apart.
مصرف (masraf) was borrowed from Arabic maṣraf (EtymDB), a word usually glossed as a place or way of spending; in Persian it is consumption.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: مصرف کردن.
مصرف کردن (masraf kardan) — "to consume, to use up".
این ماشین بنزین زیادی مصرف میکند. in mâshin benzin-e ziyâdi masraf mikonad. This car uses a lot of petrol.
Masraf + kardan. For food, fuel, medicine and electricity.

The word, taken apart.
مصرف کردن (masraf kardan) joins masraf, from Arabic maṣraf (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian verb kardan "to do".
