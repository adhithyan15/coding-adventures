Lesson 1 of 6.
la tasa — the tax, the fee; the rate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the penalty, then the Spanish for the cancellation.

You'll want to know: la tasa.
la tasa — "the tax, the fee; the rate".
El hotel cobra una tasa turística. The hotel charges a tourist tax.
La tasa turística — the tourist tax. La tasa de paro — the unemployment rate.

The word, taken apart.
tasa is built on tasar "to value, to set a price on", which is inherited from Latin taxāre "to assess, to rate" (EtymDB).

What you've built.

You'll want to know: el suplemento.

La cama extra tiene un suplemento. There is an extra charge for the extra bed.
A newspaper suplemento is a magazine inside it.

The word, taken apart.
suplemento is a learned borrowing from Latin supplēmentum "that which makes a thing full, a supply" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from supplēre "to fill up".

You'll want to know: la fianza.
la fianza — "the deposit, the security; bail".
Hay que dejar una fianza de cien euros. You have to leave a hundred-euro deposit.
For a rented flat, the deposit is la fianza.

The word, taken apart.
fianza is built on fiar "to vouch for" (EtymDB), which comes from Vulgar Latin fīdāre, Latin fīdere "to trust".

The word, taken apart.
reembolso is built on reembolsar, from re- and embolsar "to put in the purse"; bolsa comes from Late Latin bursa, from Greek býrsa "hide".
