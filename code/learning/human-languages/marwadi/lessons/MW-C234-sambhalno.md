# संभाळणो (sambhāḷṇo) — to look after; to take care of

## You'll want to know: संभाळणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C234-HERD234-05]; assesses=[] -->

**संभाळणो** — *sambhāḷṇo* — "to look after; to take care of".

> हूं गाय संभाळूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ gāy sambhāḷū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I look after the cow.

Past (I …): म्हैं गाय संभाळी, *mhaĩ gāy sambhāḷī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संभाळणो** (*sambhāḷṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sambharati*, "brings together"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *samālaï*, "takes care of" (CDIAL 12961).
