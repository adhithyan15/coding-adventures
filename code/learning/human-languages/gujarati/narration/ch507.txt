Gujarati, chapter 507: Needle and Thread.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પરોવવું (parovvũ) — to thread (a needle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to stretch tight, then the Gujarati for to wind round.

You'll want to know: પરોવવું.
પરોવવું — parovvũ — "to thread (a needle)".
દાદીએ ચશ્માં પહેરીને સોયમાં દોરો પરોવ્યો. dādīe caśmā̃ paherīne soymā̃ doro parovyo. Grandmother put on her glasses and threaded the needle.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દોરો પરોવ્યો, teṇe doro parovyo.

The word, taken apart.
પરોવવું (parovvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit právayati, "weaves on", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to thread a needle, string pearls" (CDIAL 8785). What goes through the needle is the object: દોરો પરોવ્યો.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to wind round, then the Gujarati for to thread (a needle).

You'll want to know: મોતી.
મોતી — motī — "a pearl".
તેણે દોરામાં મોતી પરોવ્યાં. teṇe dorāmā̃ motī parovyā̃. She strung pearls on a thread.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
મોતી (motī) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit mauktika, "a pearl", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 10365). It is neuter, and the plural looks the same: મોતી પરોવ્યાં.

થીંગડું (thī̃gḍũ) comes from thigga, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "a patch, a rag" (CDIAL 6096).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to mend (a tear), then the Gujarati for a patch (on cloth).

You'll want to know: ભરતકામ.
ભરતકામ — bharatkām — "embroidery".
મારી માસી સુંદર ભરતકામ કરે છે. mārī māsī sundar bharatkām kare che. My aunt does beautiful embroidery.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભરતકામ (bharatkām) is built from bharat, "filling in", from ભરવું, "to fill" (CDIAL 9397), and કામ, "work": thread fills in the pattern.
