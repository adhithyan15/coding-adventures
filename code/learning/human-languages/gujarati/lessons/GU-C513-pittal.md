## You'll want to know: પિત્તળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C513-KARKHANU513-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પિત્તળ** — *pittaḷ* — "brass".

> આ દીવો પિત્તળનો છે.
>
> *ā dīvo pittaḷno che.*
>
> This lamp is made of brass.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પિત્તળ** (*pittaḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pittala*, "brass", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8184).
