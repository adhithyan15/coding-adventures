## You'll want to know: дружба
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C498-FRIENDS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**дружба** (*drúzhba*) — "friendship".

> Наша дружба началась в школе.
>
> *Násha drúzhba nachalás v shkóle.*
>
> Our friendship began at school.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дружба* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *družьba*, built on *drugъ* 'friend', which EtymDB traces to PIE *dʰrewgʰ-.
