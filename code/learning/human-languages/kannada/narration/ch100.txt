Kannada, chapter 100: Help Me Find It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಕ್ಷೆ (nakṣe) — a map.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a stomach ache, then the Kannada for weight.

You'll want to know: ನಕ್ಷೆ.
ನಕ್ಷೆ (nakṣe) — "a map". The ಕ್ಷ is k and ṣ joined, the same pair as in ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ.
ನಕ್ಷೆ ತೋರಿಸಿ (nakṣe tōrisi) — "please show me the map".

What you've built.
Something to point at when words run out.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nakṣe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nakṣe, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nakṣe tōrisi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for a stomach ache, then the Kannada for weight, then say nakṣe again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ನಕ್ಷೆ (nakṣe) mean? ("A map".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ಟಿಕೆಟ್ (ṭikeṭ) — a ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for weight, then the Kannada for a map.

You'll want to know: ಟಿಕೆಟ್.
ಟಿಕೆಟ್ (ṭikeṭ) — "a ticket", borrowed from English. Kannada hears the English t as the curled ಟ.
ಒಂದು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ (ondu ṭikeṭ koḍi) — "one ticket, please".

What you've built.
An English word, heard with Kannada ears.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (nildāṇa) — a station, a stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ticket, then the Kannada for a bus.

You'll want to know: ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ.
ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (nildāṇa) — "a station, a stop", from ನಿಲ್ಲು (nillu), "to stop, to stand", which you know.
ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (bas nildāṇa elli?) — "where is the bus stop?"
