## You'll want to know: घोकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C371-PARIKSHA371-04]; assesses=[] -->

**घोकणे** — *ghokṇe* — "to cram; to learn by rote".

> न समजता उत्तरं घोकून काही उपयोग नाही.
>
> *na samajtā uttaraṁ ghokūn kāhī upyog nāhī.*
>
> There is no use cramming answers without understanding them.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने घोकलं, *tyāne ghoklaṁ*.

घोकंपट्टी, rote learning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोकणे** (*ghokṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
