# हूं काल कोनी जासूं — I won't go

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-VERB-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I will come tomorrow*. Then *I don't drink tea*.

## Grammar Lens: कोनी before the future
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-NEG-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-VERB-01, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-05] -->

**कोनी** goes in front of the future, as it does in front of the present:

> **हूं काल कोनी जासूं।**
>
> *hū̃ kāl konī jāsū̃.* — I won't go tomorrow.

> **हूं परसूं कोनी आसूं।**
>
> *hū̃ parsū̃ konī āsū̃.* — I won't come the day after tomorrow.
