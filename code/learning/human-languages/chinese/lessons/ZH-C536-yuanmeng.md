## You'll want to know: yuánmèng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C536-DREAM536-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yuánmèng* — "to fulfil a dream".

> *rúguǒ míngnián néng qù Zhōngguó wǒ jiù yuánmèng le* — If I can go to China next year, my dream will have come true.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuánmèng* is built from *yuán* 'round, whole' and *mèng* 'a dream', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjwen* and *mjuwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second, after an older dictionary, as waking within sleep: a dream made whole.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
