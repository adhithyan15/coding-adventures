## You'll want to know: o poste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C444-MOBILIARIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o poste** — "the post, the pole".

> O carro bateu num poste de luz.
>
> *The car hit a lamp post.*

*O poste de luz* — the lamp post.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*poste* goes back to Latin *postis* 'doorpost' (Lewis & Short), probably through French *poste*.
