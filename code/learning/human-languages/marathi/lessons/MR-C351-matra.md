## You'll want to know: मात्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C351-JODNE351-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मात्र** — *mātra* — "however; though".

> घर लहान आहे, मात्र खूप स्वच्छ आहे.
>
> *ghar lahān āhe, mātra khūp svaccha āhe.*
>
> The house is small; it is very clean, though.

Often after the first word: मी मात्र घरीच राहिलो, I, however, stayed home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मात्र** (*mātra*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mātra*, "measure", which at the end of a compound meant "nothing but, only" (Monier-Williams); Marathi uses it on its own for "only" and "however".
