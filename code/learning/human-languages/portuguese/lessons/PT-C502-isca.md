## You'll want to know: a isca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C502-PESCA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a isca** — "bait".

> O peixe comeu a isca e fugiu.
>
> *The fish ate the bait and got away.*

Also figurative: *morder a isca* — to take the bait.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*isca* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *ysca*, from Latin *ēsca* "food" (EtymDB), built on *edere* "to eat".
