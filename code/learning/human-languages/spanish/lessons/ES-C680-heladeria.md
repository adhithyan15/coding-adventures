## You'll want to know: la heladería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C680-TIENDAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la heladería** — "the ice-cream shop".

> Después de cenar vamos a la heladería.
>
> *After dinner we go to the ice-cream shop.*

Built on *el helado*, ice cream.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*heladería* is built on *helado* "ice cream", the past form of a verb inherited from Latin *gelāre* "to freeze".
