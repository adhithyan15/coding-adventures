## You'll want to know: अभ्युदयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C386-SIDDHI386-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अभ्युदयः** — *abhyudayaḥ* — "a rise in fortune; prosperity".

> यदि शान्तिः अस्ति, तर्हि देशस्य अभ्युदयः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi śāntiḥ asti, tarhi deśasya abhyudayaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If there is peace, the country will prosper.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभ्युदयः** (*abhyudayaḥ*) is built on *abhi*, *ud* and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives *abhy-udaya* "sunrise" and "elevation, increase, prosperity" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
