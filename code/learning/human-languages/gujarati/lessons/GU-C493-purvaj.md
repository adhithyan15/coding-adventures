## You'll want to know: પૂર્વજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C493-PEDHI493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂર્વજ** — *pūrvaj* — "an ancestor".

> અમારા પૂર્વજો ઘણા મહેનતુ હતા.
>
> *amārā pūrvajo ghaṇā mahenatu hatā.*
>
> Our ancestors were very hard-working.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂર્વજ** (*pūrvaj*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pūrvaja*, "born before, former", as Monier-Williams records it.
