## You'll want to know: దళం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C361-YUDDHABHUMI361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**దళం** — *daḷaṁ* — "a troop; a squad".

> ఒక చిన్న దళం కోటను కాపాడింది.
>
> *oka cinna daḷaṁ kōṭanu kāpāḍindi.*
>
> A small troop defended the fort.

Plural: దళాలు, *daḷālu*.

Also a petal or a leaf: a part of a whole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దళం** (*daḷaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dala*, "a party, a band" (CDIAL 6215); CDIAL notes that this sense may come from Dravidian.
