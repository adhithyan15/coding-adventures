## You'll want to know: behandeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C487-HAUSARZT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**behandeln** — "to treat".

> Ein junger Arzt hat meinen Vater gut behandelt.
>
> *A young doctor treated my father well.*

Also of people in general: *Er behandelt seine Kollegen fair.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*behandeln* is built on *be-* and *handeln* "to act, to handle", from *Hand*; EtymDB sets it beside Dutch *behandelen* "to handle, to treat".
