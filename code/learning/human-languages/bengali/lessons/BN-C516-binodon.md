## You'll want to know: বিনোদন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C516-BINODON516-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিনোদন** — *binodôn* — "entertainment".

> আমার বিনোদন বই পড়া।
>
> *Āmār binodôn bôi pôṛā.*
>
> Reading books is my entertainment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিনোদন** (*binodôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vinodana*, "diversion, pastime" (Monier-Williams).
