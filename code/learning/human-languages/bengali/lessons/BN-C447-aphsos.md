## You'll want to know: আফসোস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C447-APHSOS447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**আফসোস** — *āphsos* — "regret; a pity".

> ছুটিতে গ্রামে যেতে পারলাম না, এটাই আমার আফসোস।
>
> *chhuṭite grāme jete pārlām nā, eṭāi āmār āphsos.*
>
> I couldn't go to the village for the holidays, and that is my one regret.

আফসোস করা, to say you regret something; আফসোস হওয়া, to feel regret.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আফসোস** (*āphsos*) is usually traced to Persian *afsōs*, a cry of "alas" that also means regret.
