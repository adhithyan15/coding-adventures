## You'll want to know: excluir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C383-EXCECAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**excluir** — "to exclude, to leave out; to rule out".

> Não excluo a hipótese de mudar de casa.
>
> *I do not rule out the possibility of moving house.*

Conjugated like *concluir*: *excluo, excluis, exclui*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*excluir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exclūdere* "to shut out, to exclude", built from *ex* and *claudere* "to shut" (Lewis & Short).
