# તફાવત (taphāvat) — a distinction

## You'll want to know: તફાવત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C544-TAPHAVAT544-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તફાવત** — *taphāvat* — "a distinction".

> જો બંનેના ભાવમાં મોટો તફાવત હશે તો હું સસ્તું ખરીદીશ.
>
> *jo bannenā bhāvmā̃ moṭo taphāvat haśe to hũ sastũ kharīdīś.*
>
> If there's a big difference between the two prices, I'll buy the cheaper one.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તફાવત** (*taphāvat*) is usually traced to Arabic *tafāwut*, "a difference, a disparity", through Persian.
