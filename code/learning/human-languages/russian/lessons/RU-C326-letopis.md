## You'll want to know: летопись
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C326-PREDOK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**летопись** (*létopis*) — "a chronicle, annals".

> Город впервые упоминается в летописи.
>
> *Górod vpervýye upomináyetsya v létopisi.*
>
> The town is first mentioned in a chronicle.

Feminine. The old chronicles of Rus were written year by year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*летопись* joins *лето* "summer, year", Proto-Slavic *lěto* "year", and *писать* "to write", Proto-Slavic *pisati* (EtymDB): a record kept year by year.
