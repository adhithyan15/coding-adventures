# கள்ளநோட்டு (kaḷḷanōṭṭu) — a counterfeit note

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for to smuggle, then the Tamil for to flee.

## You'll want to know: கள்ளநோட்டு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C297-KADATHAL297-03]; assesses=[] -->

**கள்ளநோட்டு** — *kaḷḷanōṭṭu* — "a counterfeit note".

> கடைக்காரர் ஒரு கள்ளநோட்டைக் கண்டுபிடித்தார்.
>
> *kaṭaikkārar oru kaḷḷanōṭṭaik kaṇṭupiṭittār.*
>
> The shopkeeper spotted a counterfeit note.

கள்ள- means false or stolen before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கள்ளநோட்டு** (*kaḷḷanōṭṭu*) joins native கள்ளம், "deceit, theft" (DEDR 1372), with நோட்டு, a loan from English *note*, "a banknote".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
