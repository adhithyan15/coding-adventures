Malayalam, chapter 123: Maybe, and Not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പറ്റില്ല (paṟṟilla) — it's not possible, I can't.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for the same, then the Malayalam for oh, I see.

You'll want to know: പറ്റില്ല.
പറ്റില്ല (paṟṟilla) — "it's not possible, (I) can't". A firm, everyday no.

The word, taken apart.
പറ്റില്ല (paṟṟilla) is native Dravidian: the verb "to catch, stick to, suit" (DEDR 4034, with Tamil paṟṟu), with illa, "not" (DEDR 2559): "it does not catch".

What you've built.
The polite but firm no.

Lesson 2 of 5.
അങ്ങനെയല്ല (aṅṅaneyalla) — it's not like that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for oh, I see, then the Malayalam for not possible.

You'll want to know: അങ്ങനെയല്ല.
അങ്ങനെയല്ല (aṅṅaneyalla) — "it's not like that": അങ്ങനെ (aṅṅane), "like that", with അല്ല (alla), "is not".

The word, taken apart.
അങ്ങനെയല്ല (aṅṅaneyalla) is native Dravidian: aṅṅane, "that way" (DEDR 1), with alla, "is not" (DEDR 234).

Two known pieces, one correction.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ആയിരിക്കാം (āyirikkāṁ) — maybe, it may be.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for not possible, then the Malayalam for not like that.

You'll want to know: ആയിരിക്കാം.
ആയിരിക്കാം (āyirikkāṁ) — "maybe, it could be". ഒരുപക്ഷേ (orupakṣē), "perhaps", often comes before it.

The word, taken apart.
ആയിരിക്കാം (āyirikkāṁ) is native Dravidian: āyi, "having become" (DEDR 333, with Tamil āku), the verb "to be, to sit" (DEDR 480, with Tamil iru), and the ending -āṁ, "may". DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

What you've built.
A guess, softly put.

Lesson 4 of 5.
വേറെ (vēṟe) — other, different.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for not like that, then the Malayalam for maybe.

You'll want to know: വേറെ.
വേറെ (vēṟe) — "other, different". വേറെ വേണം (vēṟe vēṇaṁ) — "I want a different one".

The word, taken apart.
വേറെ (vēṟe) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil vēṟu (DEDR 5548).

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
തെറ്റ് (teṟṟŭ) — wrong; a mistake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for maybe, then the Malayalam for other.

You'll want to know: തെറ്റ്.
തെറ്റ് (teṟṟŭ) — "wrong", and also "a mistake". It is the honest partner of ശരി, "right".

The word, taken apart.
തെറ്റ് (teṟṟŭ) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil teṟṟu (DEDR 3445). Its first sense is a slip or stumble.

What you've built.
The opposite of śari.
