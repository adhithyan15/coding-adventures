## You'll want to know: la salle d'attente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C484-URGENCES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la salle d'attente** — "waiting room".

> Nous avons passé deux heures dans la salle d'attente.
>
> *We spent two hours in the waiting room.*

At a doctor's, a station or a hospital.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la salle d'attente* joins *salle* "room", from Frankish *sal* "a hall", and *attente* "waiting", built on *attendre*, inherited from Latin *attendere* "to stretch towards", from *tendere* (Lewis & Short).
