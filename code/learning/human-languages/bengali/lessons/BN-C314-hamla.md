## You'll want to know: হামলা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C314-SHOTRU314-04]; assesses=[] -->

**হামলা** — *hāmlā* — "a raid; an assault".

> রাতে ডাকাতরা গ্রামে হামলা করল।
>
> *rāte ḍākātrā grāme hāmlā kôrlô.*
>
> At night the robbers raided the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হামলা** (*hāmlā*) is usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ḥamla*, 'an attack, a charge'. হামলা করা, to raid.
