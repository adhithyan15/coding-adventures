## You'll want to know: la penalización
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C662-RESERVA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la penalización** — "the penalty, the charge (for cancelling)".

> Si cancela hoy, no hay penalización.
>
> *If you cancel today, there is no charge.*

Booking terms often say *sin penalización* — free of charge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*penalización* is built on *penalizar* "to penalise", from *penal*, a learned borrowing from Latin *poenālis*, built on *poena* "penalty, punishment".
