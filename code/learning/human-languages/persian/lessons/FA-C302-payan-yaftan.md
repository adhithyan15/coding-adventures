## You'll want to know: پایان یافتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C302-AGHAZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پایان یافتن** (*pâyân yâftan*) — "to come to an end".

> جنگ بعد از هشت سال پایان یافت.
>
> *Jang ba'd az hasht sâl pâyân yâft.*
>
> The war came to an end after eight years.

Formal; in speech *tamâm shod*. Present stem *yâb-*: *pâyân miyâbad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pâyân yâftan* joins *pâyân* "end" and *yâftan* "to find, to attain", both native Persian words: literally "to find an end". EtymDB lists neither.
