## You'll want to know: সুখ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C439-SUKH439-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সুখ** — *sukh* — "happiness; comfort".

> আমার দাদু গ্রামে সুখে আছেন।
>
> *āmār dādu grāme sukhe āchhen.*
>
> My grandfather lives happily in the village.

সুখে থাকা, to live happily.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সুখ** (*sukh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sukha*, "pleasant, easy" (CDIAL 13451), and so comfort and happiness; সুখী (taught), contented, is the adjective made from it.
