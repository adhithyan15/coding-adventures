## You'll want to know: loger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C424-HEBERGEMENT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**loger** — "to lodge, to stay; to put up".

> Pendant le festival, nous logeons chez des amis.
>
> *During the festival we are staying with friends.*

*Le logement* — housing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*loger* comes from Old French *logier* (EtymDB), built on *loge* 'hut', which is usually traced to a Frankish word for a shelter of leaves.
