## You'll want to know: नक्तम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C443-ASTA443-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नक्तम्** — *naktam* — "by night".

> उलूकः नक्तं चरति।
>
> *ulūkaḥ naktaṃ carati.*
>
> The owl moves about by night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नक्तम्** (*naktam*) is the word MW gives for by night, often set against *divā*, "by day"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
