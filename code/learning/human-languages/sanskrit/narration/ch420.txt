Sanskrit, chapter 420: Water for the Traveller.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रपा (prapā) — a roadside water shed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a water channel, then the Sanskrit for a puddle.

You'll want to know: प्रपा.
प्रपा — prapā — "a roadside water shed".
ग्रीष्मे मार्गे प्रपा अस्ति। grīṣme mārge prapā asti. In summer there is a water shed by the road.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रपा (prapā) is built from pra and the root √pā, "to drink", which also gives pibati, "drinks"; MW gives prapā as a place for watering cattle and a shed by the road where water is given to travellers (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
पाययति (pāyayati) — gives a drink to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a puddle, then the Sanskrit for a roadside water shed.

You'll want to know: पाययति.
पाययति — pāyayati — "gives a drink to".
नारी प्रपायां पथिकान् जलं पाययति। nārī prapāyāṃ pathikān jalaṃ pāyayati. At the water shed a woman gives the wayfarers water to drink.
Past (she …): सा पथिकान् जलं पायितवती, sā pathikān jalaṃ pāyitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
पाययति (pāyayati) is built on the root √pā, "to drink", which gives pibati, "drinks"; its causative, "makes drink", is to give a drink to, and KRM gives the causative participle pāyita (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
मृगतृष्णा (mṛgatṛṣṇā) — a mirage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a roadside water shed, then the Sanskrit for gives a drink to.

You'll want to know: मृगतृष्णा.
मृगतृष्णा — mṛgatṛṣṇā — "a mirage".
मृगतृष्णा जलं न अस्ति। mṛgatṛṣṇā jalaṃ na asti. A mirage is not water.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मृगतृष्णा (mṛgatṛṣṇā) is built from mṛga, "a deer, a wild animal", and tṛṣṇā, "thirst": the water a thirsty animal runs after; MW gives the compound as a mirage (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आसादयति (āsādayati) — comes upon; reaches.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gives a drink to, then the Sanskrit for a mirage.

You'll want to know: आसादयति.
आसादयति — āsādayati — "comes upon; reaches".
सायं पथिकः प्रपाम् आसादयति। sāyaṃ pathikaḥ prapām āsādayati. In the evening the wayfarer comes upon a water shed.
Past (he …): सः प्रपाम् आसादितवान्, saḥ prapām āsāditavān.

The word, taken apart.
आसादयति (āsādayati) is built from ā and the causative of the root √sad, "to sit"; MW gives its participle āsādita as reached (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: तर्पयति.
तर्पयति — tarpayati — "refreshes; satisfies".
शीतलं जलं श्रान्तं पथिकं तर्पयति। śītalaṃ jalaṃ śrāntaṃ pathikaṃ tarpayati. Cool water refreshes the tired wayfarer.
Past (it …): जलं पथिकं तर्पितवत्, jalaṃ pathikaṃ tarpitavat.

The word, taken apart.
तर्पयति (tarpayati) is built on the root √tṛp, "to be satisfied", in its causative, "to satisfy, to refresh"; MW gives the participle tarpita, "satisfied" (MW).
