## You'll want to know: фарш
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C334-FARSH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**фарш** (*farsh*) — "mince, minced meat".

> Для котлет нужен фарш.
>
> *Dlya kotlét núzhen farsh.*
>
> You need mince for meat patties.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*фарш* was borrowed from French *farce* "stuffing" (EtymDB), from Latin *farcīre* "to cram, to stuff".
