Russian, chapter 363: Negotiations and contracts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
переговоры (peregovóry) — negotiations, talks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a specialist, then the Russian for to dismiss.

You'll want to know: переговоры.
переговоры (peregovóry) — "negotiations, talks".
Переговоры шли всю ночь. Peregovóry shli vsyu noch. The talks went on all night.
Plural only. Вести переговоры (peregovóry) — to hold talks.

The word, taken apart.
переговоры (peregovóry) is built from пере- "across" and говор "speech", the root of говорить: talk that goes back and forth across a table.

Lesson 2 of 5.
контракт (kontrákt) — a contract.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to dismiss, then the Russian for negotiations.

You'll want to know: контракт.
контракт (kontrákt) — "a contract".
Он подписал контракт на три года. On podpisál kontrákt na tri góda. He signed a three-year contract.
Masculine. Работать по контракту — to work on a contract. Close to договор.

The word, taken apart.
контракт (kontrákt) was borrowed from German Kontrakt, from Latin contractus (EtymDB) "a drawing together, an agreement", from contrahere "to draw together" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
заказчик (zakázchik) — a client (who orders work).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: заказчик.
заказчик (zakázchik) — "a client (who orders work)".
Заказчик недоволен нашей работой. Zakázchik nedovólen náshey rabótoy. The client is unhappy with our work.
Masculine. The one who orders a job or a product, from заказывать.

The word, taken apart.
заказчик (zakázchik) "buyer, client" (EtymDB) is built on заказывать "to order", whose root is казать "to show, to say", Proto-Slavic kazati (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
конкурент (konkurént) — a competitor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a contract, then the Russian for a client.

You'll want to know: конкурент.
конкурент (konkurént) — "a competitor".
Наш главный конкурент снизил цены. Nash glávnyy konkurént snízil tsény. Our main competitor has cut its prices.
Masculine. Конкуренция — competition.

The word, taken apart.
конкурент (konkurént) is usually traced, through German, to Latin concurrēns, the participle of concurrere "to run together, to clash" (Lewis & Short), built on currere "to run".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: сотрудничать.
сотрудничать (sotrúdnichat) — "to cooperate, to work together".
Наша фирма сотрудничает с немецкой компанией. Násha fírma sotrúdnichayet s nemétskoy kompániyey. Our firm works together with a German company.
Imperfective only. Сотрудничать с + instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
сотрудничать (sotrúdnichat) is built on сотрудник, from со- "together" and труд "work": to do work together.
