## You'll want to know: معاهدة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C341-FARIS341-04]; assesses=[] -->

**معاهدة** — *muʿāhada* — "a treaty".

> وقع البلدان معاهدة سلام.
>
> *waqqaʿa al-baladān muʿāhadat salām.*
>
> The two countries signed a peace treaty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معاهدة** (*muʿāhada*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-h-d* (Lane), where *ʿahd* is a pledge. *Muʿāhada* is the verbal noun of Form III, "pledging to one another".
