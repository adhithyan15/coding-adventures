## You'll want to know: खतरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C192-BOARD192-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खतरो** — *khatro* — "danger".

> पुल माथै खतरो है।
>
> *pul māthai khatro hai.*
>
> The bridge is dangerous.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खतरो** (*khatro*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khaṭar*, "danger".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
