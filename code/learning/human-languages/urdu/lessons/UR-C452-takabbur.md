## You'll want to know: تکبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C452-PRIDE452-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تکبر** — *takabbur* — "haughtiness".

> تکبر انسان کو چھوٹا بنا دیتا ہے۔
>
> *takabbur insān ko chhoṭā banā detā hai.*
>
> Haughtiness makes a person small.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تکبر** (*takabbur*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *takabbur*, "making oneself out to be great".
