## You'll want to know: పరాజయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C361-YUDDHABHUMI361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పరాజయం** — *parājayaṁ* — "a defeat".

> ఆ యుద్ధంలో రాజుకు పరాజయం ఎదురైంది.
>
> *ā yuddhaṁlō rājuku parājayaṁ eduraindi.*
>
> The king met with defeat in that war.

The opposite of విజయం, a victory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పరాజయం** (*parājayaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *parājaya*, "conquest, defeat" (CDIAL 7799p).
