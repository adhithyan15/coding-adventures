## You'll want to know: فطرت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C454-CHARACTER454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فطرت** — *fitrat* — "a person's nature; disposition".

> اس کی فطرت بڑی نرم ہے۔
>
> *us kī fitrat baṛī narm hai.*
>
> She has a very gentle nature.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فطرت** (*fitrat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fiṭra*, "the nature a person is born with".
