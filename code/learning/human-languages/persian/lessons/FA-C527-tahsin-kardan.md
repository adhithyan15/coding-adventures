# تحسین کردن (tahsin kardan) — to admire, to praise

## You'll want to know: تحسین کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C527-TALENT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تحسین کردن** (*tahsin kardan*) — "to admire, to praise".

> همه کار او را تحسین کردند.
>
> *hame kâr-e u râ tahsin kardand.*
>
> Everyone admired her work.

*tahsin* 'admiration, praise' + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tahsin* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *taḥsīn*, 'finding something beautiful, praising'. EtymDB has no entry for it. *kardan* is inherited Persian.
