## You'll want to know: ज़मानत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C352-ADALAT352-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़मानत** — *zamānat* — "bail".

> जज ने उसे ज़मानत दी।
>
> *jaj ne use zamānat dī.*
>
> The judge granted him bail.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़मानत** (*zamānat*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḍamāna*, "a guarantee", which reached Hindi through Persian. Persian and Hindi say *zamānat*.
