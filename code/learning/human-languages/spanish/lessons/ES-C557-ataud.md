## You'll want to know: el ataúd
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C557-LUTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el ataúd** — "the coffin".

> Seis amigos llevaron el ataúd.
>
> *Six friends carried the coffin.*

In Latin America *el féretro* is also used.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ataúd* was borrowed from Arabic *tābūt* 'a box, a coffin' (EtymDB), with the Arabic article joined to the front.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
