## You'll want to know: 打开
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C171-SWITCH171-01]; assesses=[] -->

**打开** — *dǎkāi* — "to open; to switch on".

> **你去打开书房的门** *nǐ qù dǎkāi shūfáng de mén* — Go and open the study door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**打开** (*dǎkāi*) is built from 打 'to strike' and 开 'to open', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *taengX* and *khoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), though dǎ is not the sound *taengX* would regularly give.
