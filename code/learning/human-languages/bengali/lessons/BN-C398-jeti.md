## You'll want to know: জেটি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C398-SROT398-03]; assesses=[] -->

**জেটি** — *jeṭi* — "a jetty".

> জেটিতে দাঁড়িয়ে আমরা জাহাজ দেখলাম।
>
> *jeṭite dā̃ṛiye āmrā jāhāj dekhlām.*
>
> We stood on the jetty and watched the ships.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জেটি** (*jeṭi*) is borrowed from English *jetty*, a landing stage built out into the water.
