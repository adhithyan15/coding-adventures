## You'll want to know: la brûlure
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C479-BLESSURES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la brûlure** — "burn".

> Elle s'est fait une brûlure en sortant le gâteau du four.
>
> *She burnt herself taking the cake out of the oven.*

Built on *brûler* "to burn".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la brûlure* is built on *brûler* "to burn" with *-ure*. The origin of *brûler* is not settled: Latin *ustulāre* "to scorch" and a Germanic verb for burning have both been proposed.
