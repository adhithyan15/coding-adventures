## You'll want to know: కాలం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C323-KALAM-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C64-JOIN-04, TE-LEX-C320-URU-01, TE-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-03, TE-LEX-C14-RUTUVULU-01, TE-LEX-C170-SAMAYAM170-01] -->

**కాలం** — *kālaṁ* — "a span of time; a period, an age".

> **చాలా కాలం తరువాత నేను మా ఊరికి వెళ్ళాను.**
>
> *cālā kālaṁ taruvāta nēnu mā ūriki veḷḷānu.* — "After a long time I went to my home town."

> **మీరు ఇక్కడ ఎంత కాలం ఉంటారు?**
>
> *mīru ikkaḍa enta kālaṁ uṇṭāru?* — "How long will you stay here?"

**సమయం** is time on the clock: is there time, what time is it. **కాలం** is time as a stretch: how long, a long
while, a season. You have said it already without noticing it: **వానాకాలం** is "the rains' time", the rainy
season, and **శీతాకాలం** is "the cold time", winter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కాలం** (*kālaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kāla*, "time" (CDIAL 3084), with Telugu's noun ending **-ం**. The same
*kāla* is the first half of **కాలపట్టిక**, "a timetable".
