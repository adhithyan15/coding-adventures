Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 218: Bus and Train.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रेल (rel) — a train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to load, then the Marwadi for to halt.

You'll want to know: रेल.
रेल — rel — "a train".
हूं काल रेल मांय बैठसूं। hū̃ kāl rel mā̃y baiṭhsū̃. Tomorrow I'll take the train.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
रेल (rel) is borrowed from English rail; Marwadi uses it for the train itself.

Lesson 2 of 5.
सवारी (savārī) — a passenger; a ride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to halt, then the Marwadi for a train.

You'll want to know: सवारी.
सवारी — savārī — "a passenger; a ride".
सवारी बस मांय बैठै है। savārī bas mā̃y baiṭhai hai. The passenger sits in the bus.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सवारी (savārī) is usually traced to Persian savārī, "riding, a mount"; in Marwadi it has come to mean the one who rides, a passenger.

Lesson 4 of 5.
छूटणो (chhūṭṇo) — to depart (of a bus or a train); to get loose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a passenger, then the Marwadi for a driver.

You'll want to know: छूटणो.
छूटणो — chhūṭṇo — "to depart (of a bus or a train); to get loose".
बस आथण छूटै है। bas āthaṇ chhūṭai hai. The bus leaves in the evening.
Past (it …): वो बस छूटी, vo bas chhūṭī.

The word, taken apart.
छूटणो (chhūṭṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed kṣuṭyate, "is released"; CDIAL lists the Marwari verb, "to get loose", under it (CDIAL 3707).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पुगावणो (pugāvṇo) — to take to a place; to see off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a driver, then the Marwadi for to depart.

You'll want to know: पुगावणो.
पुगावणो — pugāvṇo — "to take to a place; to see off".
हूं काल दूध सहर पुगासूं। hū̃ kāl dūdh sahar pugāsū̃. Tomorrow I'll take the milk to the city.
Past (I …): म्हैं दूध सहर पुगायो, mhaĩ dūdh sahar pugāyo.

The word, taken apart.
पुगावणो (pugāvṇo) is built from पूगणो, to reach, with the causative -āv-: to make someone or something reach a place. पूगणो goes back to Sanskrit pūryate, "is filled"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 8342).
