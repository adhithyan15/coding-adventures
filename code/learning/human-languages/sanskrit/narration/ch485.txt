Sanskrit, chapter 485: Nursing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शुश्रूषा (śuśrūṣā) — nursing; dutiful service.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for phlegm, then the Sanskrit for a wholesome diet.

You'll want to know: शुश्रूषा.
शुश्रूषा — śuśrūṣā — "nursing; dutiful service".
परिचारिकायाः शुश्रूषया आतुरः स्वस्थः भवति। paricārikāyāḥ śuśrūṣayā āturaḥ svasthaḥ bhavati. With the nurse's care the patient gets well.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
शुश्रूषा (śuśrūṣā) is built on the desiderative of the root √śru, "to hear", the root of शृणोति: MW's first sense is "desire to hear", then "reverence, obedience, service"; "nursing the sick" is the modern sense (MW).

The word, taken apart.
उपचरति (upacarati) is built from upa, "near", and the root √car, "to move"; MW gives "to wait upon, serve, attend" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for nursing, then the Sanskrit for attends on.

You'll want to know: आप्याययति.
आप्याययति — āpyāyayati — "revives; refreshes".
उष्णः सूपः आतुरम् आप्याययति। uṣṇaḥ sūpaḥ āturam āpyāyayati. Warm soup refreshes the patient.
Past (it …): सूपः आतुरम् आप्यायितवान्, sūpaḥ āturam āpyāyitavān.

The word, taken apart.
आप्याययति (āpyāyayati) is built from ā and the root √pyai, "to swell, to grow full": to fill up again; MW's ppp āpyāyita is "satisfied, improved, pleased" (MW).

The word, taken apart.
रसायनम् (rasāyanam) is built from रसः, "sap, essence", and ayana, "a way"; MW gives "a medicine supposed to prevent old age and prolong life, an elixir" (MW).
