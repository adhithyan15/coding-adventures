## You'll want to know: मांडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C477-HAT477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मांडी** — *māṇḍī* — "the thigh; the lap".

> बाळ आईच्या मांडीवर झोपलं.
>
> *bāḷ āīcyā māṇḍīvar jhoplaṁ.*
>
> The baby fell asleep on its mother's lap.

Feminine.

मांडीवर, on the lap; मांडी घालून बसणे, to sit cross-legged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मांडी** (*māṇḍī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
