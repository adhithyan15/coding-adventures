## You'll want to know: বজ্রপাত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C425-MEGH425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বজ্রপাত** — *bôjrôpāt* — "a lightning strike; a thunderbolt".

> ঝড়ের রাতে গ্রামের একটা উঁচু গাছে বজ্রপাত হল।
>
> *jhôṛer rāte grāmer ekṭā ũchu gāchhe bôjrôpāt hôlô.*
>
> On the stormy night lightning struck a tall tree in the village.

বজ্র is thunder itself; বজ্রপাত is the bolt that falls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বজ্রপাত** (*bôjrôpāt*) is Sanskrit *vajra-pāta*, "the fall of a thunderbolt, a stroke of lightning" (Monier-Williams), from **বজ্র**, thunder, and *pāta*, a fall.
