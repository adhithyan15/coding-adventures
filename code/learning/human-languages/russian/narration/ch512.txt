Russian, chapter 512: The Marathon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
марафон (marafón) — a marathon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a medal, then the Russian for to award.

You'll want to know: марафон.
марафон (marafón) — "a marathon".
Мой брат пробежал свой первый марафон. Moy brat probezhál svoy pérvyy marafón. My brother has run his first marathon.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
марафон (marafón) was borrowed from Greek Marathṓn (EtymDB), a place name built on márathon 'fennel'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
отставать (otstavát) — to fall behind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a runner, then the Russian for the finish.

You'll want to know: отставать.
отставать (otstavát) — "to fall behind".
Я часто отстаю от группы. Ya chásto otstayú ot grúppy. I often fall behind the group.
Imperfective; present отстаю, отстаёшь (-ава- becomes -а-). Perfective отстать. Of a clock, to be slow.

The word, taken apart.
отставать (otstavát) is built on от- 'away' and ставать, the stem of стать 'to stand, to become'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
