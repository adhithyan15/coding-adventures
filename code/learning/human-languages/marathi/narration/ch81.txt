Marathi, chapter 81: To stop, To look for, To lose, To put on, To leave.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
थांबणे (thāmbṇe) — to stop, to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to catch, then the Marathi for to cut.

You'll want to know: थांबणे.
थांबणे — thāmbṇe — "to stop, to wait".

The word, taken apart.
थांबणे (thāmbṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from stambhate, "supports, arrests" (CDIAL 13683). CDIAL glosses the Marathi verb "to stop".
