## You'll want to know: ઇન્જેક્શન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C484-ILAJ484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇન્જેક્શન** — *injekśan* — "an injection".

> નર્સે મને ઇન્જેક્શન આપ્યું.
>
> *narse mane injekśan āpyũ.*
>
> The nurse gave me an injection.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇન્જેક્શન** (*injekśan*) is borrowed from English *injection*.
