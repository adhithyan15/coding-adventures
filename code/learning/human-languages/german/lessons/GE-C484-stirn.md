## You'll want to know: die Stirn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C484-GESICHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Stirn** — "forehead".

> Das Kind hat Fieber, die Stirn ist ganz heiß.
>
> *The child has a fever; its forehead is very hot.*

Plural: *die Stirnen*. *Die Stirn runzeln* — to frown.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Stirn* is inherited from Middle High German *stirne* and Old High German *stirna*, from Proto-Germanic *stirnijō* "forehead" (EtymDB).
