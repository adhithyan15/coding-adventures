French, chapter 352: Freins et atouts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
empêcher — to prevent, to stop (from).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to justify, then the French for the justification.

You'll want to know: empêcher.
empêcher — "to prevent, to stop (from)".
Le bruit m'empêche de dormir. The noise stops me from sleeping.
Empêcher quelqu'un de faire quelque chose. Il n'empêche que — all the same.

The word, taken apart.
empêcher is inherited through Old French empeechier from Late Latin impedicāre "to fetter, to hinder" (EtymDB), built on pedica "a fetter for the foot" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
entraver — to hinder, to hamper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the justification, then the French for to prevent.

You'll want to know: entraver.
entraver — "to hinder, to hamper".
La neige entrave le travail des pompiers. The snow is hampering the firefighters.
Formal. Une entrave was a chain on an animal's legs.

The word, taken apart.
entraver is usually explained as built from en- and Old French tref "beam", from Latin trabs "a beam" (Lewis & Short): a piece of wood tied to the legs. The details are not settled.

favoriser is built on faveur "favour", from Latin favor "goodwill, favour" (Lewis & Short), from favēre "to be well disposed".

Lesson 4 of 5.
l'atout — the asset, the strong point; the trump card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to hinder, then the French for to favour.

You'll want to know: l'atout.
l'atout — "the asset, the strong point; the trump card".
Parler trois langues est un vrai atout. Speaking three languages is a real asset.
In card games, l'atout is the trump suit. Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
atout is built from à and tout "all": a card that beats all the others. tout is inherited from Latin tōtus "whole" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
le frein — the brake; the obstacle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to favour, then the French for the asset.

You'll want to know: le frein.
le frein — "the brake; the obstacle".
Le prix est un frein pour beaucoup de familles. The price is an obstacle for many families.
On a car or a bike, les freins are the brakes; the verb is freiner.

The word, taken apart.
frein is inherited from Latin frēnum "a bridle, a bit" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): what holds a horse back.
