## You'll want to know: सिंहासनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C316-SAMRAT316-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सिंहासनम्** — *siṃhāsanam* — "a throne".

> सम्राट् सिंहासने उपविशति।
>
> *samrāṭ siṃhāsane upaviśati.*
>
> The emperor sits on the throne.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिंहासनम्** (*siṃhāsanam*) is built from *siṃha*, "a lion", and *āsana*, "a seat"; MW gives it as the lion's seat, the king's seat, a throne (MW).
