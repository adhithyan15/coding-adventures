## You'll want to know: ਜਾਦੂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C354-STORY354-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਾਦੂ** — *jādū* — "magic".

> ਉਸ ਕਹਾਣੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਜਾਦੂ ਹੈ।
>
> *us kahāṇī vich bahut jādū hai.*
>
> There is a lot of magic in that story.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਾਦੂ** (*jādū*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *jādū*, "magic".
