## You'll want to know: o estrago
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C300-CAPOTAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o estrago** — "the harm, the mess; the ruin".

> O estrago na cozinha foi enorme.
>
> *The mess in the kitchen was huge.*

Built on *estragar*, ‘to spoil’. *Fazer estragos* — to do damage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estrago* is built on *estragar* "to spoil", which is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *stragāre*, from Latin *strāgēs* "destruction" (Lewis & Short).
