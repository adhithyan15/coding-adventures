## You'll want to know: sgomberare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C449-LAVORI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sgomberare** — "to clear (out), to evacuate".

> Hanno sgomberato la strada dopo la frana.
>
> *They cleared the road after the landslide.*

Also *sgombrare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sgomberare* is built from *s-* and the root of *ingombrare* 'to clutter': to take the clutter away. The origin of that root is not settled.
