## You'll want to know: 故意
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C309-THIEF309-05]; assesses=[] -->

**故意** — *gùyì* — "on purpose; deliberately".

> **他故意不说话** *tā gùyì bù shuōhuà* — He's keeping quiet on purpose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**故意** (*gùyì*) is built from 故 and 意 'an intention', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuH* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'old' and 'a matter' and the second 'will'; how the pair came to mean 'on purpose' is not settled.
