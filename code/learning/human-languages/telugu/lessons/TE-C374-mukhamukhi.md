# ముఖాముఖి (mukhāmukhi) — a face-to-face interview

## You'll want to know: ముఖాముఖి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C374-PRASARAM374-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ముఖాముఖి** — *mukhāmukhi* — "a face-to-face interview".

> టీవీలో ఆ నటుడితో ముఖాముఖి వచ్చింది.
>
> *ṭīvīlō ā naṭuḍitō mukhāmukhi vaccindi.*
>
> A face-to-face interview with that actor was on TV.

Literally face to face; ఇంటర్వ్యూ says the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ముఖాముఖి** (*mukhāmukhi*) is built on Sanskrit *mukha*, "the mouth, the face" (CDIAL 10158), said twice: face to face.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
