## You'll want to know: jiànmiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C84-TOGETHER84-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jiànmiàn* — "to meet up; to see each other".

> *wǒmen xīngqītiān jiànmiàn* — We're meeting on Sunday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiànmiàn* is built from 见 'to see' and miàn 'face', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kenH* and *mjienH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): to see each other's face.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
