## You'll want to know: ഭരണാധികാരി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C398-SIMHASANAM398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭരണാധികാരി** — *bharaṇādhikāri* — "a sovereign; a ruler".

> അക്കാലത്ത് ആ രാജ്യത്തിന്റെ ഭരണാധികാരി ഒരു രാജ്ഞി ആയിരുന്നു.
>
> *akkālattŭ ā rājyattinṟe bharaṇādhikāri oru rājñi āyirunnu.*
>
> In those days the ruler of that country was a queen.

One who holds the authority to rule a land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭരണാധികാരി** (*bharaṇādhikāri*) is built from Sanskrit *bharaṇa*, "bearing, maintaining", and *adhikārin*, "possessing authority; a governor" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
