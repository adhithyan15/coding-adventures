## You'll want to know: effectus, effectūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C373-EFFECTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**effectus, effectūs** — "a result, an effect; performance".

> Effectus cōnsiliī bonus fuit.
>
> *The result of the plan was good.*

*effectus, effectūs* m., built on *efficiō*. English *effect*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*effectus* is the noun built on *efficiō* "to bring about" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *effect* comes from it.
