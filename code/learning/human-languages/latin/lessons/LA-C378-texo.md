## You'll want to know: texō, texere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C378-TEXO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**texō, texere** — "to weave; to build".

> Fēmina vestem ē lānā texit.
>
> *The woman weaves a garment from wool.*

*texō, texere, texuī, textum*. A speech too can be woven. English *text*, *textile* and *texture*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*texō* is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*teksō* (EtymDB), from a root meaning "to fashion, to build" that Greek *tektōn* "a builder" shares. English *text* and *textile* come from it.
