# Session Map — Italian Chapter 1 (Greetings)

How the lessons compose into commute sessions. Mechanics in
[`HL00`](../../../specs/HL00-human-language-curriculum-framework.md); same
spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ciao | ciao | "hi/bye" ← *sclavus* ("slave"; English *slave*, *Slav*) |
| 2 | buono | buono / buon | "good" ← *bonus*; adjective agreement |
| 3 | il-la-lo | il / la / lo | grammatical **gender** ← *ille/illa*; two masc. articles |
| 4 | giorno | giorno | first noun (masc.); *diurnum* → *journal/journey* |
| 5 | buongiorno | buongiorno | assembled; the respectful daytime hello |
| 6 | sera | sera / buonasera | evening (fem.); *serus*; feminine agreement |
| 7 | notte | notte / buonanotte | night (fem.); Latin *-ct-* → Italian *-tt-* |
| 8 | grazie | grazie | "thanks" ← *gratia* ("grace"); reply *prego* |
| 9 | practice | (recap) | the greetings together |
