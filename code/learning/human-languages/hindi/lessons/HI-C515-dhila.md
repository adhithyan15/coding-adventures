## You'll want to know: ढीला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C515-PENCH515-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ढीला** — *ḍhīlā* — "loose; slack".

> यह कमीज़ मुझे बहुत ढीली है।
>
> *Yah kamīz mujhe bahut ḍhīlī hai.*
>
> This shirt is very loose on me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढीला** (*ḍhīlā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*ḍhila*, "slack", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5590).
