## You'll want to know: pōtiō, pōtiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C526-SANO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**pōtiō, pōtiōnis** — "a draught, drink; a potion".

> Medicus aegrō pōtiōnem amāram dat.
>
> *The doctor gives the patient a bitter draught.*

Feminine, third declension, from the root of *pōtō* — to drink. English *potion*, and *poison* too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pōtiō** is built on the root of *pōtō* "to drink" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *potion* comes from it, and *poison* too, through Old French.
