## You'll want to know: jǐnzhāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C265-SHY265-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jǐnzhāng* — "nervous; tense".

> *míngtiān kǎoshì wǒ hěn jǐnzhāng* — I have an exam tomorrow, and I'm nervous.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jǐnzhāng* is built from *jǐn* 'tight' and *zhāng* 'to stretch', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjinX* and *trjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'tightly bound' and the second 'to stretch out'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
