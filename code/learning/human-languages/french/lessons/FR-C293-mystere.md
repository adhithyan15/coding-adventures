## You'll want to know: le mystère
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C293-MYSTERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le mystère** — "the mystery".

> Personne ne sait où est le tableau: c'est un mystère.
>
> *Nobody knows where the painting is: it is a mystery.*

The adjective is *mystérieux*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mystère* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mystērium*, from Greek *mustḗrion* "a secret rite" (Lewis & Short).
