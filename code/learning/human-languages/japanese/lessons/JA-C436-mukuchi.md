## You'll want to know: むくち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C436-BRIGHT436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**むくち** — *mukuchi* — "taciturn; of few words".

> **ちちは あさは むくちです。** — *chichi wa asa wa mukuchi desu* — "My father says little in the mornings."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むくち** (*mukuchi*) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese *mu*, "nothing" (Middle Chinese *mju*), borrowed from Chinese, joined to native *kuchi*, "mouth", the kun'yomi of its second kanji (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
