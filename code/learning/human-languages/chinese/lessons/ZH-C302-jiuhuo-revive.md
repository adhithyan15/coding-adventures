## You'll want to know: 救活
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C302-BIRTH302-04]; assesses=[] -->

**救活** — *jiùhuó* — "to bring back to life".

> **医生救活了他** *yīshēng jiùhuó le tā* — The doctor brought him back to life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**救活** (*jiùhuó*) is built from 救 'to save' and 活 'alive', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjuwH* and *hwat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): to save and make alive.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
