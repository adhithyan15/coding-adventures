# más tarde — later

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C535-FINAL-03, ES-LEX-C535-FINAL-04] -->

## You'll want to know: más tarde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C535-FINAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**más tarde** — "later".

> Más tarde fuimos al hospital.
>
> *Later we went to the hospital.*

*Hasta más tarde* — see you later.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*más tarde* is built on *tarde*, which is inherited from Latin *tardē* 'slowly, late', from *tardus* 'slow' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
