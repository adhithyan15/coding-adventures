## You'll want to know: తగాదా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C534-GODAVA534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**తగాదా** — *tagādā* — "a dispute; a row".

> పొలం గురించి రెండు కుటుంబాల మధ్య తగాదా వచ్చింది.
>
> *polaṁ guriñci reṇḍu kuṭumbāla madhya tagādā vaccindi.*
>
> A dispute arose between two families over a field.

Often about land or money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తగాదా** (*tagādā*) is usually traced to Arabic *taqāḍā*, "a demand, a claim", which came through Hindustani.
