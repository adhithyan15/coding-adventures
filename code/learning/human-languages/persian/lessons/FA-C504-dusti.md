## You'll want to know: دوستی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C504-FRIENDS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دوستی** (*dusti*) — "friendship".

> دوستی ما از دبیرستان شروع شد.
>
> *dusti-ye mâ az dabirestân shoru' shod.*
>
> Our friendship began in high school.

*dust* 'friend' + *-i*. As a modifier, 'friendly': *mosâbeqe-ye dusti* 'a friendly match'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dusti* is built on native *dust*, 'friend', which is usually traced to Old Persian *dauštā*, 'beloved', with the noun ending *-i*. EtymDB lists *dust* without a source.
