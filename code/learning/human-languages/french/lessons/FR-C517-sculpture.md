## You'll want to know: la sculpture
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C517-SCULPTURE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la sculpture** — "sculpture".

> Le musée présente des sculptures modernes.
>
> *The museum is showing modern sculptures.*

The *p* is silent: *scul-ture*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la sculpture* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sculptūra* (EtymDB), from *sculpere* "to carve in stone" (Lewis & Short).
