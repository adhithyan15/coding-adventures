## You'll want to know: മാനസികാവസ്ഥ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C523-PAKA523-01]; assesses=[] -->

**മാനസികാവസ്ഥ** — *mānasikāvastha* — "a state of mind; a mood".

> പരീക്ഷയ്ക്ക് മുൻപ് അവന്റെ മാനസികാവസ്ഥ മോശമായിരുന്നു.
>
> *parīkṣaykkŭ munpŭ avanṟe mānasikāvastha mōśamāyirunnu.*
>
> Before the exam he was in a bad state of mind.

മാനസിക of the mind + അവസ്ഥ a state: the two vowels join into one long ആ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മാനസികാവസ്ഥ** (*mānasikāvastha*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *mānasika*, "of the mind" (Monier-Williams), and *avasthā*, "a state, a condition" (CDIAL 872).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
