## You'll want to know: あまやかす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C478-GROW478-02]; assesses=[] -->

**あまやかす** — *amayakasu* — "to spoil a child".

> **そぼは いもうとを あまやかしています。** — *sobo wa imōto o amayakashite imasu* — "My grandmother spoils my younger sister."

Polite **あまやかします**, *amayakashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが むすめを あまやかしました。**, *chichi ga musume o amayakashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あまやかす** (*amayakasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
