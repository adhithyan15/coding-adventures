7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.

A packet? (പാക്കറ്റ്, pākkaṟṟŭ.) A salary? (ശമ്പളം, śambaḷaṁ.) Wages? (കൂലി, kūli.) A worker? (തൊഴിലാളി, toḻilāḷi.)
