## You'll want to know: 这些
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C127-THESE127-01]; assesses=[] -->

**这些** — *zhèxiē* — "these".

> **这些是我以前的书** *zhèxiē shì wǒ yǐqián de shū* — These are my old books.

The new character, in pieces: 些 — on top a four-stroke piece and a two-stroke piece side by side, and under them the two bars of 二. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**这些** (*zhèxiē*) is built from 这 'this' and 些 'a few'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *sjae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a little'.
