# अनाथः (anāthaḥ) — orphaned; without a protector

## You'll want to know: अनाथः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C307-VIYOGA307-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनाथः** — *anāthaḥ* — "orphaned; without a protector".

> अनाथः बालः शिबिरे वसति।
>
> *anāthaḥ bālaḥ śibire vasati.*
>
> The orphaned boy lives in the camp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनाथः** (*anāthaḥ*) is built from *a*, "not", and *nātha*, "a protector, lord"; MW gives *anātha* as having no master or protector (MW).
