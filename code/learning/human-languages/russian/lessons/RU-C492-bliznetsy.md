## You'll want to know: близнецы
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C492-STEPFAMILY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**близнецы** (*bliznetsý*) — "twins".

> Близнецы так похожи, что их путают даже друзья.
>
> *Bliznetsý tak pokhózhi, chto ikh pútayut dázhe druzyá.*
>
> The twins are so alike that even their friends mix them up.

Plural of *близнец*; genitive близнецов.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*близнецы* is the plural of *близнец*, which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *bliznьcь* (EtymDB) and shares its root with *близкий* 'near, close', from Proto-Slavic *blizъ* 'near' (EtymDB).
