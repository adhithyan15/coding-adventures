## You'll want to know: der Frieden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C318-KRIEG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Frieden** — "the peace".

> Nach dem Krieg kam endlich Frieden.
>
> *After the war peace came at last.*

Also *der Friede*, older and more formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Frieden* goes back to Proto-Germanic *friþuz* "peace" (EtymDB), with the old English cousin *frith* "peace".
