# ふっています / ふっていました (futte imasu / futte imashita) — what is going on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-02, JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-02, JA-LEX-C44-NATURE44-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "It will probably rain tomorrow". (*ashita ame ga furu deshō*.) Then the **-て** form of **ふる**. (*futte*.)

## You'll want to know: -ています
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C253-ONGOING-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C54-TIME54-03, JA-LEX-C27-VERBS27-04, JA-LEX-C25-FAMILY25-01] -->

> **あめが ふっています。**
>
> *ame ga futte imasu.* — "It is raining."

The **-て** form with **います** (*imasu*), "is there", says what is going on: the rain has started and has not stopped.

> **いま あめが ふっています。**
>
> *ima ame ga futte imasu.* — "It is raining now."

> **ともだちを まっています。**
>
> *tomodachi o matte imasu.* — "I am waiting for a friend."

## You'll want to know: -ていました
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C253-ONGOING-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-HAHA, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-SHINBUN, JA-LEX-C27-VERBS27-01, JA-LEX-C199-DEPART199-04] -->

With **いました** (*imashita*), it was going on:

> **きのう あめが ふっていました。**
>
> *kinō ame ga futte imashita.* — "It was raining yesterday."

> **ははは しんぶんを よんでいました。**
>
> *haha wa shinbun o yonde imashita.* — "My mother was reading the newspaper."

A story needs both kinds of past. **-ていました** is the scene, going on; **-ました** is the event, once, inside it:

> **あめが ふっていました。**
>
> *ame ga futte imashita.* — "It was raining."

> **でんしゃが とまりました。**
>
> *densha ga tomarimashita.* — "The train stopped."
