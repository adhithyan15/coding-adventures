Gujarati, chapter 517: Wires and Switches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વાયર (vāyar) — an electric wire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to colour, then the Gujarati for to plaster.

You'll want to know: વાયર.
વાયર — vāyar — "an electric wire".
પંખાનો વાયર જૂનો થઈ ગયો છે. pankhāno vāyar jūno thaī gayo che. The fan's wire has got old.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વાયર (vāyar) is borrowed from English wire.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખોસવું (khosvũ) — to push in (firmly).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a switch, then the Gujarati for a light bulb.

You'll want to know: ખોસવું.
ખોસવું — khosvũ — "to push in (firmly)".
અરુણે પ્લગ દીવાલમાં ખોસ્યો. Aruṇe plag dīvālmā̃ khosyo. Arun pushed the plug into the wall.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પ્લગ ખોસ્યો, teṇe plag khosyo.

The word, taken apart.
ખોસવું (khosvũ) comes from skōṣati, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "to force in" (CDIAL 13661).
