## Chapters 225-253 — 145 more A2 headwords

The third of four Tamil A2 vocabulary tranches: twenty-nine chapters of five
words, in four runs, each closed by two review lessons; thirty-five are
verbs. Christmas and cabbage were swapped for வாசனை (a fragrance) and
ஆண்டுதோறும் (every year), because their spellings need ஸ, which the script
inventory does not pin. Level gate: 101 headwords short of A2.
