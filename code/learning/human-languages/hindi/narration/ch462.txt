Hindi, chapter 462: Showing Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अभिमान (abhimān) — self-esteem; pride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to blush, then the Hindi for brazen.

You'll want to know: अभिमान.
अभिमान — abhimān — "self-esteem; pride".
अरुण को अपने काम पर अभिमान है। Aruṇ ko apne kām par abhimān hai. Arun takes pride in his work.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अभिमान (abhimān) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit abhimāna, "a high opinion of oneself, pride" (Monier-Williams). It can be honest pride or vanity: the sentence decides.

Lesson 3 of 5.
शेख़ी (śekhī) — a boast; bragging.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for self-esteem, then the Hindi for to put on airs.

You'll want to know: शेख़ी.
शेख़ी — śekhī — "a boast; bragging".
अरुण का दोस्त हमेशा अपनी गाड़ी की शेख़ी मारता है। Aruṇ kā dost hameśā apnī gāṛī kī śekhī mārtā hai. Arun's friend is always boasting about his car.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
शेख़ी (śekhī) is usually traced to Persian shēkhī, "the airs of a sheikh", hence boasting. śekhī mārnā is "to brag".

Lesson 4 of 5.
अकड़ना (akaṛnā) — to strut; to stiffen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to put on airs, then the Hindi for a boast.

You'll want to know: अकड़ना.
अकड़ना — akaṛnā — "to strut; to stiffen".
जीत के बाद खिलाड़ी बहुत अकड़ रहा था। jīt ke bād khilāṛī bahut akaṛ rahā thā. After the win, the player was strutting about.
Past (he …): वह अकड़ गया, vah akaṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
अकड़ना (akaṛnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi akaṛnā, "to be numbed", under ākkaḍa, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1013). Stiff limbs and a stiff, haughty back are both akaṛnā.
