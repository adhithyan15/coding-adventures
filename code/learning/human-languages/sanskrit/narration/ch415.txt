Sanskrit, chapter 415: River Currents.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्रोतः (srotaḥ) — a flowing current; a stream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for unfamiliar, then the Sanskrit for a newcomer.

You'll want to know: स्रोतः.
स्रोतः — srotaḥ — "a flowing current; a stream".
वर्षायां नद्याः स्रोतः वर्धते। varṣāyāṃ nadyāḥ srotaḥ vardhate. In the rains the river's current grows.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
स्रोतः (srotaḥ) comes from the root √sru, "to flow"; MW gives srotas as the current of a river, a stream (MW).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a newcomer, then the Sanskrit for a flowing current.

आवर्तः — āvartaḥ — "a whirlpool".
नद्याः मध्ये आवर्तः अस्ति। nadyāḥ madhye āvartaḥ asti. There is a whirlpool in the middle of the river.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आवर्तः (āvartaḥ) is built from ā and the root √vṛt, "to turn": a turning round; MW gives āvarta as a whirlpool (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सन्तरति (santarati) — swims across; crosses over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a flowing current, then the Sanskrit for a whirlpool.

You'll want to know: सन्तरति.
सन्तरति — santarati — "swims across; crosses over".
बालः नदीं सन्तरति। bālaḥ nadīṃ santarati. The boy swims across the river.
Past (he …): सः नदीं सन्तीर्णवान्, saḥ nadīṃ santīrṇavān.

The word, taken apart.
सन्तरति (santarati) is built from sam and the root √tṝ, "to cross", which also gives tarati, "swims, crosses"; MW gives saṃ-tṝ as to cross or pass through, with the participle saṃtīrṇa (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पारम् (pāram) — the far bank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a whirlpool, then the Sanskrit for swims across.

You'll want to know: पारम्.
पारम् — pāram — "the far bank".
नौका नद्याः पारं गच्छति। naukā nadyāḥ pāraṃ gacchati. The boat goes to the far bank of the river.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पारम् (pāram) comes from the root √pṛ, "to bring across"; MW gives pāra as the further bank or shore, the opposite side; it lives on as Hindi pār, "the further bank" (MW; CDIAL 8100).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सेतुबन्धः (setubandhaḥ) — a causeway; a dam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for swims across, then the Sanskrit for the far bank.

You'll want to know: सेतुबन्धः.
सेतुबन्धः — setubandhaḥ — "a causeway; a dam".
ग्रामस्य समीपे नद्यां सेतुबन्धः अस्ति। grāmasya samīpe nadyāṃ setubandhaḥ asti. Near the village there is a causeway across the river.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सेतुबन्धः (setubandhaḥ) is built from setu, "a bridge, a dam", and bandha, "a binding", from the root √bandh, "to bind"; MW gives the compound as the making of a causeway, a dam or a bridge (MW).
