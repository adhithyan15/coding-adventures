## You'll want to know: décoller
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C419-DECOLLAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**décoller** — "to take off; to unstick".

> L'avion a décollé avec une heure de retard.
>
> *The plane took off an hour late.*

Also *décoller une étiquette* — to peel off a label.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*décoller* is built from *dé-* 'un-' and *coller* 'to stick', from *colle* 'glue', which goes back to Greek *kólla* (EtymDB): the plane comes unstuck from the ground.
