# ई — long *ī* standing alone

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-KA-01, MW-SCRIPT-PA-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Write **प**, then **क**, once each. Point to attached **ी** in **पाणी** and say
its long vowel.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-II-INDEPENDENT-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->

> ई

Read **ई** as long *ī*. Use this independent form when the vowel stands on
its own. Attached **ी** still belongs after a consonant.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-II-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **ई** visible and copy it once. Then point: **ई** stands alone; **ी**
attaches.
