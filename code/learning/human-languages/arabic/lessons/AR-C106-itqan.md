**إتقان** — *itqān* — "mastery".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إتقان** (*itqān*) is built on the native Arabic root *t-q-n* (Lane). *Itqān* is the verbal noun of Form IV *atqana*, "to make firm, to do skilfully".
