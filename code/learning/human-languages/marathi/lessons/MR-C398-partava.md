## You'll want to know: परतावा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C398-TIKITGHAR398-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परतावा** — *partāvā* — "a refund".

> मला तिकिटाचा पूर्ण परतावा मिळाला.
>
> *malā tikiṭācā pūrṇa partāvā miḷālā.*
>
> I got a full refund for the ticket.

Masculine.

Built on परत, back: a refund is money that comes back to you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परतावा** (*partāvā*) is a Marathi derivation built on **परत**, "back, again", from the family of Sanskrit *parivartate*, "turns round", where CDIAL lists Marathi *paratṇẽ*, "to return" (CDIAL 7872).
