## You'll want to know: tíqián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C388-DATE388-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tíqián* — "to do ahead of time; to bring forward (a date)".

> *yàoshi nǐ bù néng lái zuìhǎo tíqián gàosu wǒ* — If you can't come, you'd better tell me in good time.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tíqián* is built from *tí* 'to carry by the handle' and 前 'before', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dej* and *dzen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'first': lifting a time forward.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
