# (reading) — a notice and a message

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C157-HUIFU-01, ZH-LEX-C157-TONGZHI-01, ZH-LEX-DAJIA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A notice pinned up at work, then a message from a friend.

- [YOU RECALL: say *huífù*, to reply — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *tōngzhī*, a notice — **R2**]

> 大家

[YOU READ: it aloud, with nothing said first — *dàjiā*]

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SKILL-READ-NOTICE-01]; assesses=[ZH-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING, ZH-LEX-C157-KAIHUI-01, ZH-LEX-C157-TONGZHI-01, ZH-LEX-C157-XIAOXI-01, ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C147-HOUTIAN-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YAO-FUTURE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-BING-ILL-01, ZH-LEX-C63-PHONE63-02] -->

> 明天下午三点开会
> 大家都要来
> 请不要来晚了

> 我明天不能去了
> 因为我妈妈病了
> 后天下午你在家吗
> 我想两点来看你
> 给我打电话

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: the notice and the message through once without stopping; do not translate as you go]

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: the message again, and find the question your friend asks you]

## Reading — the pinyin, after you have tried
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SKILL-READ-NOTICE-01, ZH-SKILL-READ-WORDS, ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C157-KAIHUI-01] -->

> *míngtiān xiàwǔ sān diǎn kāihuì*
> *dàjiā dōu yào lái*
> *qǐng bú yào lái wǎn le*

> *wǒ míngtiān bù néng qù le*
> *yīnwèi wǒ māma bìng le*
> *hòutiān xiàwǔ nǐ zài jiā ma*
> *wǒ xiǎng liǎng diǎn lái kàn nǐ*
> *gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà*

The notice speaks to everyone, 大家, and asks with 请. The message runs in order: what has changed, why, a question, an offer, a request. 因为 ties the reason to the line before it.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SKILL-READ-NOTICE-01, ZH-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING, ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C147-HOUTIAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]

- When is the meeting? (**明天下午三点**: three tomorrow afternoon.)
- Why isn't your friend coming tomorrow? (**因为我妈妈病了**: the mother is ill.)
- What does your friend ask you? (**后天下午你在家吗**: whether you are home the afternoon after tomorrow.)
- What does your friend want you to do? (**给我打电话**: phone back.)

- [YOU SAY: the message aloud, once, as your friend would say it]
