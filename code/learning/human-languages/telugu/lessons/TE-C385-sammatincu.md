# సమ్మతించు (sammatiñcu) — to consent

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for consent, then the Telugu for to agree with.

## You'll want to know: సమ్మతించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C385-ANGIKARAM385-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సమ్మతించు** — *sammatiñcu* — "to consent".

> మా నాన్న ఆ ప్రయాణానికి సమ్మతించాడు.
>
> *mā nānna ā prayāṇāniki sammatiñcāḍu.*
>
> My father consented to that trip.

Past (I …): సమ్మతించాను, *sammatiñcānu*.

What you consent to takes -కి: దానికి సమ్మతించు. The noun is సమ్మతి, consent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సమ్మతించు** (*sammatiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *sammati*, "sameness of opinion, agreement, approval" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
