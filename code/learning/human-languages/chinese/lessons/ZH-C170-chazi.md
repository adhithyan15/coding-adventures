## You'll want to know: chāzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C170-POT170-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chāzi* — "fork".

> *nǐ yòng kuàizi háishi yòng chāzi* — Chopsticks or a fork?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chāzi* is built from *chā* and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrhae* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'hands crossed', and under a second reading 'two branches': a forked thing. In the word the second loses its tone.
