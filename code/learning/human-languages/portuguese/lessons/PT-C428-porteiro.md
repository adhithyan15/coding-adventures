## You'll want to know: o porteiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C428-PERNOITAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o porteiro** — "the doorman, the porter".

> O porteiro do hotel chamou um táxi.
>
> *The hotel doorman called a taxi.*

In a block of flats, *o porteiro* looks after the entrance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*porteiro* is inherited (EtymDB) from Latin *portārius* 'doorkeeper', built on *porta* 'door' (Lewis & Short).
