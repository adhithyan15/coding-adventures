# ઝડપી (jhaḍpī) — fast; quick

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C279-WAY-ASK-01, GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-02, GU-LEX-C74-WHERE1-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: ask how to get to the station. (*sṭeśan kevī rīte javũ?*) Then say "a train" and "expensive". (**ટ્રેન**, **મોંઘું**.)

## You'll want to know: ઝડપી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C280-JHADPI-01]; assesses=[GU-Q-KYAARE-01, GU-JOIN-PAN-01, GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-01] -->

**ઝડપી** — *jhaḍpī* — "fast; quick". It ends in **-ી** whatever it describes: **ઝડપી ટ્રેન**, **ઝડપી બસ**, **ઝડપી વિમાન**.

> **ઝડપી ટ્રેન ક્યારે છે?**
>
> *jhaḍpī ṭren kyāre che?* — "When is the fast train?"

> **તે ઝડપી છે, પણ મોંઘી છે.**
>
> *te jhaḍpī che, paṇ moṅghī che.* — "It's fast, but it's expensive."

Said of the train, **મોંઘી** follows **ટ્રેન**; **ઝડપી** stays as it is.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝડપી** (*jhaḍpī*) is built from **ઝડપ**, "speed", and the **-ી** that makes a describing word of it. CDIAL lists Gujarati *jhaṛap* under a reconstructed root *jhaṭ*, "sudden movement" (CDIAL 5327). It glosses that form "scuffling", so the sense of speed is probably the later one.
