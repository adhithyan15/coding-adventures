## You'll want to know: गळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C520-NAL520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गळणे** — *gaḷṇe* — "to drip; to leak".

> तो नळ रात्रभर गळत होता.
>
> *to naḷ rātrabhar gaḷat hotā.*
>
> That tap was dripping all night.

Past (he …): तो नळ गळला, *to naḷ gaḷlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गळणे** (*gaḷṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *galati*, "drips, trickles" (CDIAL 4074).
