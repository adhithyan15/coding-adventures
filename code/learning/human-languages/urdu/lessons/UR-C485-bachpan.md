## You'll want to know: بچپن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C485-CHILDHOOD485-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بچپن** — *bachpan* — "childhood".

> بچپن میں میں روز پارک جاتا تھا۔
>
> *bachpan meṅ maiṅ roz pārk jātā thā.*
>
> As a child I went to the park every day.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بچپن** (*bachpan*) is built on **بچہ**, "a child", usually traced to Persian *bachcha*, with the Indo-Aryan ending *-pan*, "-hood".
