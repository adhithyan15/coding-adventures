## You'll want to know: le caniveau
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C443-HIVER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le caniveau** — "the gutter (in the street)".

> La neige fond et coule dans le caniveau.
>
> *The snow melts and runs into the gutter.*

At the edge of the road, beside the pavement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
