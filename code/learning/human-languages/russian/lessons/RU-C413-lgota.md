## You'll want to know: льгота
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C413-PROYEZD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**льгота** (*lgóta*) — "a concession, a reduced fare; a benefit".

> У пенсионеров есть льгота на проезд.
>
> *U pensionérov yest lgóta na proyézd.*
>
> Pensioners have a reduced fare on public transport.

Feminine. *Льготный билет* — a concession ticket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*льгота* is usually linked with an old root meaning 'light, easy', the one in *лёгкий*: a concession makes things lighter. EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the line is hedged.
