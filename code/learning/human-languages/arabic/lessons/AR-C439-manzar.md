## You'll want to know: منظر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C439-MANZAR439-01]; assesses=[] -->

**منظر** — *manẓar* — "a view; scenery".

> كان المنظر من الجبل جميلا.
>
> *kāna al-manẓar min al-jabal jamīlan.*
>
> The view from the mountain was beautiful.

Plural: مناظر, *manāẓir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منظر** (*manẓar*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ẓ-r*, "to look" (Lane). The pattern *mafʿal* makes it the thing looked at.
