## You'll want to know: সমাপ্তি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C329-SHESHPATA329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সমাপ্তি** — *sômāpti* — "a close; a conclusion".

> গল্পের সমাপ্তিতে নায়ক গ্রামে ফিরল।
>
> *gôlper sômāptite nāyôk grāme phirlô.*
>
> At the close of the story the hero went back to his village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সমাপ্তি** (*sômāpti*) is Sanskrit *samāpti*, 'accomplishment, completion, conclusion' (Monier-Williams). It is the formal partner of **শেষ**.
