Marathi, chapter 463: Pride and Shame.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गर्व (garva) — pride; conceit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for temptation, then the Marathi for greed.

You'll want to know: गर्व.
गर्व — garva — "pride; conceit".
बक्षीस मिळाल्याचा त्याला गर्व झाला. bakṣīs miḷālyācā tyālā garva jhālā. Winning the prize made him conceited.
Masculine.
Often a fault: गर्व (garva) होणे, to grow conceited; गर्विष्ठ, arrogant.

The word, taken apart.
गर्व (garva) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit garva, "pride, arrogance" (CDIAL 4064).

गरीब असला तरी त्याने स्वाभिमान सोडला नाही. garīb aslā tarī tyāne svābhimān soḍlā nāhī. Though he was poor, he never gave up his self-respect.
Masculine.
स्व, own, + अभिमान, pride; स्वाभिमानी, self-respecting.

The word, taken apart.
स्वाभिमान (svābhimān) is a learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, sva, "own", and abhimāna, "pride" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
शरमणे (śaramṇe) — to feel ashamed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for ego, then the Marathi for self-respect.

You'll want to know: शरमणे.
शरमणे — śaramṇe — "to feel ashamed".
आपली चूक लक्षात आल्यावर तो शरमला. āplī cūk lakṣāt ālyāvar to śaramlā. He felt ashamed when he realised his mistake.
Past (he …): तो शरमला, to śaramlā.
From शरम, shame, a Persian word beside the Marathi लाज.

The word, taken apart.
शरमणे (śaramṇe) is a Marathi verb built on शरम, "shame", a loan from Persian sharm.

Past (he …): तो ओशाळला, to ośāḷlā.

The word, taken apart.
ओशाळणे (ośāḷṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
