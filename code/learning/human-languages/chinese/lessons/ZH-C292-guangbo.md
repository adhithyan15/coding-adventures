## You'll want to know: guǎngbō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C292-RADIO292-01]; assesses=[] -->

*guǎngbō* — "to broadcast; a broadcast".

> *diàntái měitiān zǎoshang guǎngbō xīnwén* — The station broadcasts the news every morning.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guǎngbō* is built from *guǎng* 'wide' and *bō* 'to scatter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwangX* and *paH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'big' and 'broad' and the second 'to spread' and 'to sow': to spread far and wide. The level tone of bō is irregular for *paH*.
