## You'll want to know: निर्माण होणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C342-HETU342-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्माण होणे** — *nirmāṇ hoṇe* — "to come about; to arise".

> या निर्णयामुळे नवा प्रश्न निर्माण झाला.
>
> *yā nirṇayāmuḷe navā praśna nirmāṇ jhālā.*
>
> A new question arose because of this decision.

Past (he …): तो निर्माण झाला, *to nirmāṇ jhālā*.

Intransitive: what arises is the subject. निर्माण करणे is to create.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्माण होणे** (*nirmāṇ hoṇe*) joins the tatsama निर्माण, Sanskrit *nirmāṇa*, "measuring, making" (CDIAL 7368), to होणे, "to become": to come into being.
