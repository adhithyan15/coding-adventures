## You'll want to know: a cronologia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C273-ETAPA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a cronologia** — "the chronology, the order of events".

> O professor escreveu a cronologia no quadro.
>
> *The teacher wrote the chronology on the board.*

*Por ordem cronológica* — in order of time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cronologia* is a learned word built from Greek *khrónos* "time" and *-logía* "an account, a reckoning" (EtymDB traces *crónica* to the same *khrónos*).
