German, chapter 250: Besides, Therefore, Nevertheless, In any case, Actually.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
außerdem — besides, moreover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to produce, then the German for to demand.

You'll want to know: außerdem.
außerdem — "besides, moreover".

The word, taken apart.
außerdem is the word for "besides, outside", built on the word for "out", + dem, a form of der: "besides that".

Lesson 2 of 5.
deshalb — therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to demand, then the German for besides.

You'll want to know: deshalb.
deshalb — "therefore".

The word, taken apart.
deshalb is des, "of that", a form of der, + halb, an old word for "side, behalf" (as in English behalf): on that account. Halb from Proto-Germanic halbaz (EtymDB 2.1).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for nevertheless, then the German for in any case.

You'll want to know: eigentlich.
eigentlich — "actually".

The word, taken apart.
eigentlich from Middle High German eigenlīche (EtymDB 2.1): the word for "own", Old High German eigan, English own, + -lich: in its own nature, actually.
