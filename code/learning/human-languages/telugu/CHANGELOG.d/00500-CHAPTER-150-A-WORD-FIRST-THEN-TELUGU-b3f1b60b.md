## Chapter 150: a word first, then Telugu ఙ

The writing ramp no longer asks the rare consonant ఙ to appear from nowhere.
Chapter 150 first teaches **వాఙ్మయం** (*vāṅmayaṁ*, literature or the wider body
of expression in words), then takes ఙ from that known word. The writing lesson
adds a five-movement, three-run filmstrip backed by the packaged tracing guide.
