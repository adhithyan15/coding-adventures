## You'll want to know: जोपासणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C500-SANGOPAN500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जोपासणे** — *jopāsṇe* — "to nurture".

> त्याने अनेक वर्षं ही बाग जोपासली.
>
> *tyāne anek varṣaṁ hī bāg jopāslī.*
>
> He nurtured this garden for many years.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बाग जोपासली, *tyāne bāg jopāslī*.

Of a plant, a talent or a friendship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जोपासणे** (*jopāsṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
