## You'll want to know: むら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C298-MUKASHI298-04]; assesses=[] -->

**むら** — *mura* — "a village".

> **その むらは やまの なかに あります。** — *sono mura wa yama no naka ni arimasu* — "The village lies in the mountains."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むら** (*mura*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
