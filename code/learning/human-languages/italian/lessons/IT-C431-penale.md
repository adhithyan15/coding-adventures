## You'll want to know: la penale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C431-CAPARRA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la penale** — "the cancellation fee, the penalty".

> Se disdici tardi, paghi una penale.
>
> *If you cancel late, you pay a fee.*

As an adjective, *penale* means criminal: *il codice penale*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*penale* is a learned borrowing from Latin *poenālis* 'of punishment', from *poena* 'penalty' (Lewis & Short).
