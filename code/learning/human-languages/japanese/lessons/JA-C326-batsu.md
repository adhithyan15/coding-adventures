## You'll want to know: ばつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C326-KISOKU326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ばつ** — *batsu* — "a punishment; a penalty".

> **きそくを やぶったので、ばつが ありました。** — *kisoku o yabutta node, batsu ga arimashita* — "He broke the rules, so there was a punishment."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばつ** (*batsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *batsu*, "punishment" (Middle Chinese *bjot*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
