## Added — B1 vocabulary: 1,120 words in chapters 349–572; Malayalam attains B1

- **224 new chapters (349–572) teach 1,120 new headwords, one word per lesson.** 400 of them are verbs, each given with its past (*sambhavikkuka*, past *sambhaviccu*). Each lesson gives:
  - the word in Malayalam script with its romanization, which follows the track's A2 scheme exactly (chillus, ṟ / ṟṟ, ḻ, ṁ, final ŭ);
  - a short example, its romanization word for word, and its translation;
  - a note on the past, the plural, the case a verb takes, or a set phrase;
  - a "The word, taken apart" origin.

  Each word is practised again in the next two lessons, and a review closes every eight chapters. Every chapter sits on one B1 spine node:
  - 349–404, SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS (280 words): news and the press, incidents and emergencies, the police and the courts, life events, history and stories.
  - 405–460, SPINE-GIVE-REASONS and SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION (280 words): opinion and argument, society, politics and the law, education, then conditions and likelihood.
  - 461–516, SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL (280 words): roads, buses, trains and planes, lodging, the town and its offices, shopping, nature and the monsoon.
  - 517–572, SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE (280 words): feelings and character, health, work, family, the arts and free time, sport and the home.
- **Malayalam now teaches 2,569 headwords at or below B1**, past the level gate's target of 2,500. Together with the B1 core (chapters 339–348), this means Malayalam attains B1.
- **Sources:**
  - native Dravidian words cite DEDR;
  - Sanskrit words cite CDIAL or Monier-Williams;
  - English, Portuguese and Hindustani loans are named as such;
  - anything the sources do not settle is hedged.
- **Independent review:** each tranche was reviewed on its own. The reviews fixed errors in 145 of the 1,120 words (12.9%), mostly etymology citations that did not match DEDR or CDIAL, and root slugs that split from the track's own (*dravidian-tala-kai*, *sanskrit-manas-mind*, *dravidian-pidi-catch*). They also fixed three spellings: the doubled ണ്ണ after ർ (നിർണ്ണായകമായ), സംപ്രേഷണം and നിശ്ശബ്ദത.
- **The new letter ഘ is revisited.** Two chapter-349 notes use ആഘോഷം, so the ഘ letter lesson at the end of the core is practised twice before tranche b.
