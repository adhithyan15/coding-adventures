Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 46: Back, Tongue, Eye, Nose, Heart.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पीठ (pīṭh) — the back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the face, then the Marwadi for a knee.

You'll want to know: पीठ.
पीठ — pīṭh — "the back".

The word, taken apart.
पीठ (pīṭh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pṛṣṭi, "vertebrae; back". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 8370); the match is through Hindi pīṭh and Gujarati pīṭh.

The word, taken apart.
आंख (ā̃kh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from akṣi, "eye". CDIAL lists Old Marwari ākhi under it (CDIAL 43). English eye and Latin oculus are the same ancient word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
नाक (nāk) — the nose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the tongue, then the Marwadi for an eye.

You'll want to know: नाक.
नाक — nāk — "the nose".

The word, taken apart.
नाक (nāk) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nakka, "nose", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6909); the match is through Hindi nāk and Gujarati nāk. CDIAL thinks it possibly goes back to the old word nas-, "nose", the same ancient word as English nose.
