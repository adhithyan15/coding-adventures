## You'll want to know: ಇಂಗಾಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C473-PARISARA473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇಂಗಾಲ** — *iṅgāla* — "carbon".

> ಮರಗಳು ಗಾಳಿಯಿಂದ ಇಂಗಾಲವನ್ನು ಹೀರುತ್ತವೆ.
>
> *maragaḷu gāḷiyinda iṅgālavannu hīruttave.*
>
> Trees take in carbon from the air.

In everyday Kannada a burnt-out coal; in science, carbon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇಂಗಾಲ** (*iṅgāla*) comes from Sanskrit *aṅgāra*, "glowing charcoal", through the variant *iṅgāla* (CDIAL 125).
