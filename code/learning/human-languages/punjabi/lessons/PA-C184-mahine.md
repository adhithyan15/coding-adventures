## Grammar Lens: make the same ending change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-MAHINE-184]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਮਹੀਨੇ** — *mahiine* — month inside a larger time phrase

Again the final *-aa* becomes *-e*. The phrase **ਹਰ ਮਹੀਨੇ** means “every month.”
