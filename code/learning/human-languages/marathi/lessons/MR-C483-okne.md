## You'll want to know: ओकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C483-PACAN483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ओकणे** — *okṇe* — "to vomit; to throw up".

> बसमध्ये बाळाने दूध ओकलं.
>
> *basmadhye bāḷāne dūdh oklaṁ.*
>
> The baby threw up its milk on the bus.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ओकलं, *tyāne oklaṁ*.

Blunter than उलटी होणे; what comes up is the object.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओकणे** (*okṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *okṇẽ*, "vomit", under the expressive *\*ōkk* (CDIAL 2538).
