Persian, chapter 494: Caring for the Sick.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عیادت کردن ('iyâdat kardan) — to visit (a sick person).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get vaccinated, then the Persian for a check-up.

You'll want to know: عیادت کردن.
عیادت کردن ('iyâdat kardan) — "to visit (a sick person)".
همکارانش در بیمارستان از او عیادت کردند. hamkârânash dar bimârestân az u 'iyâdat kardand. His colleagues visited him in hospital.
az kasi 'iyâdat kardan: said only of visiting the sick. 'iyâdat + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
'iyâdat is a loan, usually traced to Arabic ʿiyāda, 'visiting the sick'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پرستاری کردن (parastâri kardan) — to nurse, to care for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to visit, then the Persian for to look after.

You'll want to know: پرستاری کردن.
پرستاری کردن (parastâri kardan) — "to nurse, to care for".
وقتی بیمار بودم، خواهرم از من پرستاری کرد. vaqti bimâr budam, khâharam az man parastâri kard. When I was ill, my sister nursed me.
parastâr 'nurse' + -i + kardan. The person nursed takes az.

The word, taken apart.
parastâri is built on parastâr, 'nurse', which is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Middle Persian word for a maidservant (EtymDB). The ending -i and kardan are native too.

Lesson 4 of 5.
سلامتی (salâmati) — health.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to look after, then the Persian for to nurse.

You'll want to know: سلامتی.
سلامتی (salâmati) — "health".
ورزش برای سلامتی خوب است. varzesh barâ-ye salâmati khub ast. Exercise is good for your health.
Also a toast and a wish: be salâmati 'cheers, to your health'.

The word, taken apart.
salâmati is built in Persian on salâmat, 'soundness', a loan usually traced to Arabic salāma, from the same root s-l-m as salâm, with the native ending -i.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: خوب شدن.
خوب شدن (khub shodan) — "to get better, to recover".
او بعد از یک هفته خوب شد. u ba'd az yek hafte khub shod. He got better after a week.
khub 'good, well' + shodan 'to become'. It also means 'to turn out well'.

The word, taken apart.
khub shodan is built on two inherited words: khub, 'good', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian xūb (EtymDB), and shodan, 'to become'.
