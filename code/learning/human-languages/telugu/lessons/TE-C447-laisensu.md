# లైసెన్సు (laisensu) — a licence

## You'll want to know: లైసెన్సు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C447-SIGNALU447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**లైసెన్సు** — *laisensu* — "a licence".

> లైసెన్సు లేకుండా బండి నడపకూడదు.
>
> *laisensu lēkuṇḍā baṇḍi naḍapakūḍadu.*
>
> You must not drive without a licence.

A driving licence is డ్రైవింగ్ లైసెన్సు; a shop needs a trade లైసెన్సు too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లైసెన్సు** (*laisensu*) is a loan from English *licence*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
