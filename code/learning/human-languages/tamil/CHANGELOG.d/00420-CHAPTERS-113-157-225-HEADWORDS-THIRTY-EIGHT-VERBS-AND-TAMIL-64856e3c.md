## Chapters 113-157: 225 headwords, thirty-eight verbs, and Tamil attains A1

Tamil had four A1 gaps. It was 219 headwords and 17 verbs short. Four A1 spine
nodes had no segment: ASK-LOCATION, SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO,
NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS and DESCRIBE-QUALITIES. Thirty-nine atoms were revisited
fewer than twice.

**The words.** Forty-five chapters of five:

- **places and directions:** **நேராக**, **அருகில்**, **தூரம்**, **பின்னால்**,
  **முன்னால்**, **நடுவில்**, the street, the park, the temple, the library, the
  market and more (ASK-LOCATION)
- **can:** **முடியும்**, **திறமை**, **வல்லமை**, **உரிமை**, **வாய்ப்பு**, **கருவி**,
  **உடைமை** (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO)
- **things:** the house, school, food from **இட்லி** to **மாம்பழம்**, nature,
  animals and the body (NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS)
- **qualities:** thirty-six describing words (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)
- **verbs:** thirty-eight, as the stems the track already uses

Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword, romanization and
lesson text, with Tamil letter boundaries. Every word uses only letters the
book has already taught the reader to write, so none brings in ஈ, ஜ, ஹ or ஃ.
Forward references stay at 8, and letter anchoring is unchanged.

**The revisits.** Forty thin atoms each get a warm-up retrieval in the new
chapters. Where an atom had no revisit at all, its line appears twice. The
lines cover:

- one slot that changes: **இங்கே**, **அங்கே**, **எங்கே**
- adjectives before the noun
- telling the time and the afternoon's three names
- the week and **கிழமை**
- the day and night words
- the Tamil greetings
- the colours
- counting with its sounds
- the Tamil months and dates
- **வானிலை** and the rainy season
- **பிடிக்கும்** and **விரும்பு**
- the chapter-84 reading skills
- **ழ** in **தமிழ்**
- **வா** and **வருகிறேன்**
- **நாய்** and **பூனை**
