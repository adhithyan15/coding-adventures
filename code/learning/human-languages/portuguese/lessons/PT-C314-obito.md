## You'll want to know: o óbito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C314-FALECER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o óbito** — "the death (in official language)".

> O médico confirmou o óbito às seis horas.
>
> *The doctor confirmed the death at six o’clock.*

An official word: *certidão de óbito* — death certificate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*óbito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *obitus* "a going down, death", from *obīre* "to go towards, to die", built on *īre* "to go" (Lewis & Short).
