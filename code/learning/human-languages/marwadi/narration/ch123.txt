Lesson 1 of 5.
तकियो (takiyo) — a pillow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a quilt, then the Marwadi for a mattress.

You'll want to know: तकियो.
तकियो — takiyo — "a pillow".

The word, taken apart.
तकियो (takiyo) is a loan, usually traced to Persian takya, "pillow, support". Persian took it from Arabic, from a root meaning "to lean" (Lane, root w-k-ʾ).

Lesson 3 of 5.
दरी (darī) — a cotton rug.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a pillow, then the Marwadi for a sheet.

You'll want to know: दरी.
दरी — darī — "a cotton rug".

The word, taken apart.
दरी (darī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from darikā, "split cane, mat", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6194); the match is through Hindi darī. It is built on the root dṛ, "to split".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a cotton rug, then the Marwadi for a curtain.

You'll want to know: आरसी.
आरसी — ārsī — "a mirror".

The word, taken apart.
आरसी (ārsī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ādarśa, "mirror". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 1143); the match is through Hindi ārsī and Gujarati ārsī. It is built on the root dṛś, "to see".
