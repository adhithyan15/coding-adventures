## You'll want to know: વ્યવહાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C365-SIDDHANT365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યવહાર** — *vyavhār* — "everyday practice; dealings".

> પણ વ્યવહારમાં આ સિદ્ધાંત ચાલતો નથી.
>
> *paṇ vyavhārmā̃ ā siddhā̃t cālto nathī.*
>
> But in practice this theory does not work.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યવહાર** (*vyavhār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vyavahāra*, "doing, practice, conduct" (Monier-Williams).
