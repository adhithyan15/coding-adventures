## You'll want to know: حراج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C462-HARAJ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حراج** (*harâj*) — "a sale (with reduced prices); an auction".

> در حراج زمستانی یک کاپشن ارزان خریدم.
>
> *Dar harâj-e zemestâni yek kâpshan-e arzân kharidam.*
>
> I bought a cheap jacket in the winter sale.

*Harâj!* written in a shop window means a sale is on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*harâj* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *ḥarāj* 'an auction'. EtymDB does not list it.
