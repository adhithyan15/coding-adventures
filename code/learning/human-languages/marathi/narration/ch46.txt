Marathi, chapter 46: Where You Came From.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
-हून (-hūn) — from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Chapter twenty-three taught शहर as a line on a form. A form wants the name of a city. An interviewer wants something else.
[your turn — recall: say -ne, then -śī, and name which one takes a person]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: -हून.
-हून — -hūn — from.
मी पुण्याहून येतो. — mī puṇyāhūn yeto. — I come from Pune.
That sentence is the second thing said in almost every spoken A1 interview, and until this lesson the book could write शहर: पुणे on a form and not say it out loud. A form field is a label; -हून (-hūn) is an answer.
The stem is the one you already bend: पुणे becomes पुण्या-, then -हून (-hūn), no space. And because इथे and तिथे are place words too, they take it and give you two more:
इथून — ithūn — from here तिथून — tithūn — from there.
Notice what happened to the shape: इथे ends in -ए and the ending replaced it, exactly as it does on पुणे. The rule you learned in chapter forty-one is doing all the work.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mī puṇyāhūn yeto — I come from Pune]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ithūn, then tithūn — from here, from there]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gharāhūn — from the house]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say that you come from Pune, then from here.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What can you now say that a form field could not? (I come from Pune — an answer, not a label.) What does इथे become with it? (इथून.)
Sources: Wiktionary: हून.


Lesson 2 of 4.
पासून (pāsūn) — from, since.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
-हून (-hūn) says where a journey began. It does not say when one did.

You'll want to know: पासून.
पासून — pāsūn — from, since.
उद्यापासून — udyāpāsūn — from tomorrow onwards.
पासून (pāsūn) is written joined, like -हून (-hūn), and takes the same oblique stem. What makes it worth its own lesson is that it works on time as well as place — Marathi uses one word where English splits from and since, and a Marathi speaker feels no difference between the start of a road and the start of a week.
Against -हून (-hūn) the contrast is direction of attention. -हून (-hūn) looks back at the place you LEFT; पासून (pāsūn) looks forward from a starting point that is still running:
पुण्याहून — I came from Pune, and I am elsewhere now. उद्यापासून — starting tomorrow, and continuing.
That is why a notice about opening hours uses पासून (pāsūn) and a traveller uses -हून (-hūn).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: udyāpāsūn — from tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gharāpāsūn — from the house onwards]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the pair — puṇyāhūn, udyāpāsūn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say 'from tomorrow' and 'from Pune', and name which one is about time.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which of the two also does TIME? (पासून (pāsūn).) Which one looks back at where you left? (-हून (-hūn).)
Sources: Wiktionary: पासून (pāsūn).


Lesson 3 of 4.
कुठून (kuṭhūn) — from where.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
You can answer I come from Pune. Nobody has asked you yet.

You'll want to know: कुठून.
कुठून — kuṭhūn — from where.
तुम्ही कुठून येता? — tumhī kuṭhūn yetā? — where are you from?
Nothing here is new except the answer to a question you can now finally ask. कुठून (kuṭhūn) is कुठे with -हून (-hūn) on it, and the -ए dropped for -ऊ in exactly the way इथे became इथून two lessons ago. A place word, an ablative, and the same rule.
Its position obeys chapter thirty-eight: an asking word stands in the answer's own slot, so कुठून (kuṭhūn) sits precisely where पुण्याहून would sit and nothing moves to the front.
तुम्ही पुण्याहून येता. becomes तुम्ही कुठून (kuṭhūn) येता?
Now put the whole family beside each other, because they are one shape with three stems and you have owned two of them since chapter forty:
कुठून (kuṭhūn) from where, इथून from here, तिथून from there.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kuṭhūn — from where]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tumhī kuṭhūn yetā? — and answer it about yourself]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the three together — kuṭhūn, ithūn, tithūn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Ask where somebody is from, then answer with Pune.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What two pieces is कुठून (kuṭhūn) made of? (कुठे plus -हून (-hūn).) Where in the sentence does it stand? (Where the answer would stand.)
