# تخلیه کردن (takhliye kardan) — to evacuate, to empty

## You'll want to know: تخلیه کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C331-VIRAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تخلیه کردن** (*takhliye kardan*) — "to evacuate, to empty".

> پلیس ساختمان را تخلیه کرد.
>
> *Polis sâkhtemân râ takhliye kard.*
>
> The police evacuated the building.

Also "to empty, to unload". Present *takhliye mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*takhliye* is borrowed from Arabic *takhliya* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "an emptying, a clearing". *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
