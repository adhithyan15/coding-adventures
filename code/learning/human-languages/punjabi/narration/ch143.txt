Punjabi, chapter 143: Dry, Dirty, Sour, Bitter, Sharp.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਸੁੱਕਾ (sukkā) — dry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for narrow, then the Punjabi for wet.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਮਨਨ ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਹੈ। ਉਹ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹੈ। without stopping]

You'll want to know: ਸੁੱਕਾ.
ਸੁੱਕਾ — sukkā — "dry".

The word, taken apart.
ਸੁੱਕਾ (sukkā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śuṣka, "dried" (CDIAL 12548).

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sukkā]

[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for narrow, then the Punjabi for wet, then say sukkā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਸੁੱਕਾ (sukkā) mean? ("Dry".) Say it once more.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੱਟਾ (khaṭṭā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khaṭṭa, "sour" (CDIAL 3777). CDIAL asks whether it is linked with kaṭu, "pungent", and says Hindi khaṭṭā was taken from Punjabi.
