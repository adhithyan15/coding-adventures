Kannada, chapter 528: Friendship.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸ್ನೇಹ (snēha) — friendship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for crawling on all fours, then the Kannada for a stepmother.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ನೇಹ.
ಸ್ನೇಹ — snēha — "friendship".
ನಮ್ಮ ಸ್ನೇಹ ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಶುರುವಾಯಿತು. namma snēha śāleyalli śuruvāyitu. Our friendship began at school.
ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ, a friend, is built on it.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ನೇಹ (snēha) is borrowed from Sanskrit sneha, "oiliness, smoothness", and from there "tenderness, friendship" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

ಬಾಂಧವ್ಯ — bāndhavya — "a bond; close ties".
ಎರಡು ಕುಟುಂಬಗಳ ನಡುವೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಬಾಂಧವ್ಯ ಇದೆ. eraḍu kuṭumbagaḷa naḍuve oḷḷeya bāndhavya ide. There is a good bond between the two families.
Related to ಬಂಧು, kin.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಾಂಧವ್ಯ (bāndhavya) is a learned abstract noun built on Sanskrit bāndhava, "kinsman" (CDIAL 9208): the state of being bound as kin.

ಅಜ್ಜನ ಒಡನಾಟದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ತುಂಬಾ ಕಲಿತೆ. ajjana oḍanāṭadalli nānu tumbā kalite. In grandfather's company I learned a great deal.

The word, taken apart.
ಒಡನಾಟ (oḍanāṭa) is native Dravidian, built on Kannada oḍan, "union, together, with" (DEDR 945).

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ರಾಜಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊ (rāji māḍiko) — to make up; to reconcile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for companionship, then the Kannada for dear.

You'll want to know: ರಾಜಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊ.
ರಾಜಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊ — rāji māḍiko — "to make up; to reconcile".
ಜಗಳದ ನಂತರ ಇಬ್ಬರು ಸ್ನೇಹಿತರು ರಾಜಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಂಡರು. jagaḷada nantara ibbaru snēhitaru rāji māḍikoṇḍaru. After the quarrel the two friends made up.
Past (I …): ರಾಜಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಂಡೆ, rāji māḍikoṇḍe.
With whom: -ಜೊತೆ. ರಾಜಿ is a settlement.

The word, taken apart.
ರಾಜಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊ (rāji māḍiko) joins rāji, usually traced to Hindustani rāzī, "content, agreeing", and ಮಾಡಿಕೊ, "to do for oneself".
