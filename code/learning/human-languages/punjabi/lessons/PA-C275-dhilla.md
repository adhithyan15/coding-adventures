## You'll want to know: ਢਿੱਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C275-FIT275-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਿੱਲਾ** — *ḍhillā* — "loose; slack".

> ਇਹ ਕੁੜਤਾ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਢਿੱਲਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh kuṛtā mainū̃ ḍhillā hai.*
>
> This kurta is loose on me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਿੱਲਾ** (*ḍhillā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhila*, "loose, slack"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍhillā*, "slack, slow, late", under it (CDIAL 5590).
