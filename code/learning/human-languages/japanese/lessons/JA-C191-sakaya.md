## You'll want to know: さかや
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C191-SHUT191-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さかや** — *sakaya* — "liquor store; sake shop".

> **さかやで おさけを かいます。** — *sakaya de osake o kaimasu* — "I buy sake at the liquor store."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さかや** (*sakaya*) is native Japanese, built from *saka-*, the form *sake*, "rice wine", takes in compounds, and *ya*, "house, shop" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
