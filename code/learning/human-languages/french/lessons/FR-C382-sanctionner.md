## You'll want to know: sanctionner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C382-INFRACTION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sanctionner** — "to penalise, to sanction".

> L'école a sanctionné les élèves qui fumaient dans les toilettes.
>
> *The school penalised the pupils who were smoking in the toilets.*

*Être sanctionné* — to be penalised.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sanctionner* is built on *sanction*, from Latin *sānctiō* "a decree, a penalty clause", from *sancīre* "to make sacred, to decree" (Lewis & Short).
