Marathi, chapter 492: Hiccups and Gasps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उचकी लागणे (uckī lāgṇe) — to have hiccups.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to take a nap, then the Marathi for to lie down.

You'll want to know: उचकी लागणे.
उचकी लागणे — uckī lāgṇe — "to have hiccups".
घाईत जेवल्याने त्याला उचकी लागली. ghāīt jevlyāne tyālā uckī lāglī. Eating in a hurry gave him hiccups.
Past (to him …): त्याला उचकी लागली, tyālā uckī lāglī.
Dative: मला उचकी लागली.

The word, taken apart.
उचकी लागणे (uckī lāgṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on उचकी, "a hiccup", whose origin is not settled, with लागणे.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ढेकर देणे (ḍhekar deṇe) — to burp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to lie down, then the Marathi for to have hiccups.

You'll want to know: ढेकर देणे.
ढेकर देणे — ḍhekar deṇe — "to burp".
पोटभर जेवल्यावर आजोबांनी ढेकर दिली. poṭbhar jevlyāvar ājobānnī ḍhekar dilī. After a full meal Grandfather burped.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ढेकर दिली, tyāne ḍhekar dilī.
ढेकर, a burp, is feminine in everyday use (ढेकर आली, ढेकर दिली), though CDIAL marks it masculine. ढेकर येणे, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
ढेकर देणे (ḍhekar deṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on ढेकर, "a burp", which is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ḍhẽkar, "belch", under ḍakkāra (CDIAL 5521); देणे is the light verb.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: थुंकणे.
थुंकणे — thuṅkṇe — "to spit".
रस्त्यावर थुंकणं चुकीचं आहे. rastyāvar thuṅkṇaṁ cukīcaṁ āhe. Spitting in the street is wrong.
Past (he …): तो थुंकला, to thuṅklā.
थुंकी, spittle.

The word, taken apart.
थुंकणे (thuṅkṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi thũkṇẽ, "spits", under the expressive thukk (CDIAL 6097).

जिना चढल्यावर आजीला धाप लागली. jinā caḍhlyāvar ājīlā dhāp lāglī. Grandmother was out of breath after climbing the stairs.

धाप लागणे (dhāp lāgṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on धाप, "panting", which CDIAL lists under the expressive dhapp (CDIAL 6729), with लागणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ठसका लागणे (ṭhaskā lāgṇe) — to choke on food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to spit, then the Marathi for to get breathless.

You'll want to know: ठसका लागणे.
ठसका लागणे — ṭhaskā lāgṇe — "to choke on food".
घाईत पाणी प्यायल्याने त्याला ठसका लागला. ghāīt pāṇī pyāylyāne tyālā ṭhaskā lāglā. He choked from drinking water too fast.
Past (to him …): त्याला ठसका लागला, tyālā ṭhaskā lāglā.
A coughing fit when food or water goes down the wrong way.

The word, taken apart.
ठसका लागणे (ṭhaskā lāgṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on ठसका, "a choking cough", whose origin is not settled, with लागणे.
