## You'll want to know: رسیدگی کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C441-DARKHAST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رسیدگی کردن** (*residegi kardan*) — "to look into, to deal with (a problem)".

> مدیر قول داد به مشکل ما رسیدگی کند.
>
> *Modir qol dâd be moshkel-e mâ residegi konad.*
>
> The manager promised to look into our problem.

*residegi* 'attention, follow-up', from *residan*. The matter takes *be*: *be in kâr residegi mikonim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*residegi kardan* is built from *residegi* 'attending to', formed on *residan* 'to arrive, to reach', usually traced to Middle Persian *rasīdan*, and *kardan* 'to do'.
