## You'll want to know: महिषः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C113-THINGS113-05]; assesses=[] -->

**महिषः** — *mahiṣaḥ* — "a buffalo".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mahiṣa* first meant "great, mighty", from the root of *mah-* "great", which MW compares with Latin *magnus* and English *much*. CDIAL 9964 explains "buffalo" from *mahiṣo mṛgāṇām*, "the great one of the beasts". It lives on as Hindi *bhaĩs* and Marathi *mhais* "buffalo" (CDIAL 9964).
