## You'll want to know: వివరణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C394-VIVARANA394-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వివరణ** — *vivaraṇa* — "an explanation; a clarification".

> అతని వివరణ నాకు అర్థం కాలేదు.
>
> *atani vivaraṇa nāku arthaṁ kālēdu.*
>
> I did not understand his explanation.

The noun of వివరించు, to explain, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వివరణ** (*vivaraṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vivaraṇa*, "an uncovering, an opening", and so "explanation, exposition" (Monier-Williams).
