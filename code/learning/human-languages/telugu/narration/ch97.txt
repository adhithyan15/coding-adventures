Telugu, chapter 97: Laughing, Crying, Worrying.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నవ్వు (navvu) — a laugh, a smile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a birthday, then the Telugu for a signature.

You'll want to know: నవ్వు.
నవ్వు (navvu) — "a laugh", and "a smile": Telugu uses one word for both.
నవ్వండి — "smile!", with the polite ending, what a photographer says.

What you've built.
A laugh and a smile, in one word.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: navvu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: navvu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say navvaṇḍi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for a birthday, then the Telugu for a signature, then say navvu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does నవ్వు (navvu) mean? ("A laugh, a smile".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ఏడుపు (ēḍupu) — crying, tears.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a signature, then the Telugu for a laugh.

You'll want to know: ఏడుపు.
ఏడుపు (ēḍupu) — "crying".
నాకు ఏడుపు (ēḍupu) వచ్చింది — "I felt like crying", literally "crying came to me", on the pattern of కోపం వచ్చింది.

What you've built.
Crying, which comes to you like anger does.

Lesson 3 of 5.
విచారం (vicāraṁ) — regret, sorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a laugh, then the Telugu for crying.

You'll want to know: విచారం.
విచారం (vicāraṁ) — "regret, sorrow".
బాధ is pain; విచారం (vicāraṁ) is the sorrow of looking back. విచారంగా ఉంది — "I am sorry about it", "I regret it".

What you've built.
A sorrow about something that has happened.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: ఆందోళన.

ఆందోళన (āndōḷana) పడకండి — "don't worry". Telugu falls into worry: పడు is "to fall".
