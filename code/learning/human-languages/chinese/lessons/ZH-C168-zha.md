## You'll want to know: zhá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C168-COOK168-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhá* — "to deep-fry".

> *jīntiān wǎnshang wǒmen zhá yú* — We're deep-frying fish tonight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhá* is probably the word the Guangyun writes another way as *dzreap*, glossed 'to scald in hot water' (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which would regularly give zhá; but no dictionary we use joins that word to this character, so its history is not settled.
