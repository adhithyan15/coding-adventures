## You'll want to know: تجنب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C407-TAJANNUB407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تجنب** — *tajannaba* — "to avoid".

> تجنب الرجل الطريق المزدحم.
>
> *tajannaba ar-rajul aṭ-ṭarīq al-muzdaḥim.*
>
> The man avoided the crowded road.

Present: يتجنب, *yatajannab*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تجنب** (*tajannaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-n-b*, "to put aside, to turn away" (Lane). It is Form V, to put oneself to one side of a thing: to avoid it. بجانب, beside, shares the root.
