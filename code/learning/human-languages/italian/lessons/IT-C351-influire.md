## You'll want to know: influire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C351-ORIGINE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**influire** — "to influence, to have an effect (on)".

> Il tempo influisce sulle nostre scelte.
>
> *The weather has an effect on our choices.*

*Influire su* qualcosa. Conjugated with *-isc-*: *influisce*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*influire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *influere* "to flow in" (Lewis & Short), built from *in* and *fluere* "to flow"; *influenza* comes from the same verb.
