# மணமேடை (maṇamēṭai) — a wedding dais

## You'll want to know: மணமேடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C322-MANAM322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**மணமேடை** — *maṇamēṭai* — "a wedding dais".

> மணமக்கள் மணமேடையில் அமர்ந்தார்கள்.
>
> *maṇamakkaḷ maṇamēṭaiyil amarntārkaḷ.*
>
> The bride and groom sat on the wedding dais.

மணம், marriage, as in மணமகன், the groom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மணமேடை** (*maṇamēṭai*) is a Tamil compound built from மணம், "marriage" (DEDR 4667), and மேடை, "a raised platform", whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
