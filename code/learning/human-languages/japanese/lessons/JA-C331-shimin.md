## You'll want to know: しみん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C331-SHAKAI331-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しみん** — *shimin* — "a citizen".

> **しみんの いけんを きいた ほうが いいです。** — *shimin no iken o kiita hō ga ii desu* — "They ought to listen to what the citizens think."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しみん** (*shimin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "city" (Middle Chinese *dzyiX*) and *min*, "people" (*mjin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
