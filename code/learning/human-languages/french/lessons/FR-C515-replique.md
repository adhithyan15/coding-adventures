## You'll want to know: la réplique
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C515-SCENE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la réplique** — "line (in a play); reply".

> L'acteur a oublié sa réplique.
>
> *The actor forgot his line.*

Also a copy: *une réplique de la statue*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la réplique* is built on *répliquer* "to reply", from Latin *replicāre* "to fold back, to reply", built on *plicāre* "to fold" (Lewis & Short).
