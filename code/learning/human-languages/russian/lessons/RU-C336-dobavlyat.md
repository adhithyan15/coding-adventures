## You'll want to know: добавлять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C336-GOTOVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**добавлять** (*dobavlyát*) — "to add".

> В суп я добавил немного соли.
>
> *V sup ya dobávil nemnógo sóli.*
>
> I added a little salt to the soup.

Imperfective; perfective *добавить* (*dobávit*), *добавлю*. It takes the accusative, or the genitive for "some": *добавить сахару*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*добавлять* is built on *до-* and an old verb *бавить* "to make, to keep busy", as in *забава* "amusement"; the link is usually traced, and EtymDB lists no parent.
