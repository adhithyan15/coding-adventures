## You'll want to know: سنسنی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C300-KHABAR300-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سنسنی** — *sansanī* — "a sensation; excitement".

> اس خبر سے شہر میں سنسنی تھی۔
>
> *is khabar se shahr meṅ sansanī thī.*
>
> The news caused a sensation in the city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سنسنی** (*sansanī*) belongs with *sansanānā*, "to whizz, to be excited", which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit *svaná*, "a noise" (CDIAL 13901): the hum that runs through a crowd.
