## You'll want to know: ਤਸਵੀਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C356-PHOTO356-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਸਵੀਰ** — *tasvīr* — "a picture".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਇਹ ਤਸਵੀਰ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ eh tasvīr bahut pasand hai.*
>
> I like this picture very much.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਸਵੀਰ** (*tasvīr*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *taṣwīr*, "picturing".
