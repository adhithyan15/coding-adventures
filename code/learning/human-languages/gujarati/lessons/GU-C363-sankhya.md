## You'll want to know: સંખ્યા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C363-ANKDO363-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સંખ્યા** — *sankhyā* — "a count; a number of people or things".

> આ વરસે વિદ્યાર્થીઓની સંખ્યા વધી.
>
> *ā varse vidyārthīonī sankhyā vadhī.*
>
> The number of students went up this year.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંખ્યા** (*sankhyā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃkhyā*, "reckoning, number" (Monier-Williams).
