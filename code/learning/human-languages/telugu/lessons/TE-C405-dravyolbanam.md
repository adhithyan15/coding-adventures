# ద్రవ్యోల్బణం (dravyōlbaṇaṁ) — inflation (of prices)

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for unemployment, then the Telugu for economic.

## You'll want to know: ద్రవ్యోల్బణం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C405-ARTHIKA405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ద్రవ్యోల్బణం** — *dravyōlbaṇaṁ* — "inflation (of prices)".

> ద్రవ్యోల్బణం వల్ల ధరలు పెరుగుతున్నాయి.
>
> *dravyōlbaṇaṁ valla dharalu perugutunnāyi.*
>
> Prices are rising because of inflation.

A newspaper word; in talk, ధరలు పెరగడం, prices going up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ద్రవ్యోల్బణం** (*dravyōlbaṇaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *dravya*, "substance, property" (CDIAL 6625), and *ulbaṇa*, "laid over in addition" (Monier-Williams); the compound is a modern one.
