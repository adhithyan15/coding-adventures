## You'll want to know: onus, oneris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C389-SERVITUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**onus, oneris** — "a load, a burden".

> Asinus onus grave portat.
>
> *The donkey carries a heavy load.*

*onus, oneris* n. A load on a back, and a burden of duty or tax. English *onus* and *onerous*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*onus* is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European noun meaning "a burden" (EtymDB). English took it over as *onus*, and *onerous* comes from its adjective.
