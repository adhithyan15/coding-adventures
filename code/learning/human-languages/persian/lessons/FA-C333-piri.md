## You'll want to know: پیری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C333-NOWJAVANI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پیری** (*piri*) — "old age".

> پدربزرگم در پیری هم هر روز کتاب میخواند.
>
> *Pedarbozorgam dar piri ham har ruz ketâb mikhând.*
>
> Even in old age my grandfather read every day.

*pir* "old (of people)" + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piri* is built from Persian *pir* "old", a native word for elderly people, with the noun suffix *-i*.
