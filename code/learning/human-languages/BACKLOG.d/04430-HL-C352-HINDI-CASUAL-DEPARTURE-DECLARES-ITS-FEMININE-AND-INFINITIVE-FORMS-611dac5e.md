## HL-C352 — Hindi casual departure declares its feminine and infinitive forms

Chapter 4 teaches casual **चलता हूँ** as a deliberately gendered phrase, then
contrasts a woman's **चलती हूँ**, names infinitive **चलना**, and asks the learner
to produce all three. Its declaration exposes only the masculine phrase, leaving
the other modern forms looking untaught to reports and downstream study
artifacts.

Declare the feminine phrase and infinitive beside the masculine phrase. Keep
Sanskrit **चल्** in the etymology only: it explains the history but is not a
modern Hindi form the lesson asks the learner to own.
