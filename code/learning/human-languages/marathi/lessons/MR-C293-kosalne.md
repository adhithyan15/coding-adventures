## You'll want to know: कोसळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C293-BHUKAMP293-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कोसळणे** — *kosaḷṇe* — "to collapse; to come crashing down".

> जुनी भिंत पावसात कोसळली.
>
> *junī bhint pāvsāt kosaḷlī.*
>
> The old wall collapsed in the rain.

Past (he …): तो कोसळला, *to kosaḷlā*.

Also of rain: पाऊस कोसळतो, the rain pours down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोसळणे** (*kosaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb of falling in a heap; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
