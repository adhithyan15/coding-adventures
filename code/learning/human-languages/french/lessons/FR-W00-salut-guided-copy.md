# salut — copy one greeting with help

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-PHONO-SALUT-01, FR-PRAGMATIC-SALUT-03] -->

Point to **salut** and say it once: *sa-LU*. The model stays on the page.

## Writing — one guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-PHONO-SALUT-01, FR-ETYMON-SALUT-02, FR-PRAGMATIC-SALUT-03] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **salut** once on the line below it. Write all five letters, including the
final **t**. Look back at the model whenever you want.

Point to your **t**, keep it silent, and say *sa-LU*. The letter belongs in the
written word even though your voice does not pronounce it.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-PHONO-SALUT-01, FR-PRAGMATIC-SALUT-03] -->

Compare your copy with the model. If one letter differs, repair only that letter.
No hiding, no spelling from memory, and no second word today.
