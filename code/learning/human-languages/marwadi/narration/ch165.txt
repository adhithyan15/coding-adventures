Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 165: A Note About a Repair.
4 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 4.
मिस्त्री (mistrī) — a mechanic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say a neighbour, a fan and spoiled. (पाड़ोसी, पंखो, खराब.)

You'll want to know: मिस्त्री.
मिस्त्री — mistrī — "a mechanic; a mason": the person who comes to mend things.
मिस्त्री पंखो ठीक करै है। mistrī paṅkho ṭhīk karai hai. — The mechanic mends the fan.
मिस्त्री काल आयो। mistrī kāl āyo. — The mechanic came yesterday.

The word, taken apart.
मिस्त्री (mistrī) is a loan, usually traced to Portuguese mestre, "a master (of a trade)".

What you've built.
ठीक करणो, to make right, is how Marwari says to mend: ठीक करै है, and in the past with म्हैं, ठीक कियो.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mistrī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: पंखो खराब है। — paṅkho kharāb hai. — The fan is broken.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: मिस्त्री पंखो ठीक करै है। — mistrī paṅkho ṭhīk karai hai. — The mechanic mends the fan.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does मिस्त्री (mistrī) mean? ("A mechanic; a mason".) Say it once more.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Marwadi for 'a mechanic'.


Lesson 2 of 4.
(reading) (hū̃ kāl dinūgai mistrī lāvsū̃.) — a note about a repair.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, to read the printed text yourself. The questions are about what you read, so save the whole lesson for when you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say a mechanic and a neighbour.
[pause 1 second]
This one is written to you, by someone you know. Read it for one thing: when you have to be at home.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Reading.
राम-राम सा! हूं पाड़ोसी राम हूं। काल सूं अठै पाणी कोनी। आज मिस्त्री (mistrī) कोनी आयो। हूं काल दिनूगै मिस्त्री (mistrī) लावसूं। मिस्त्री (mistrī) चोखो है। आप काल दिनूगै ९ सूं ११ तांई घरै रैवो। दरवाजो खुलो राखो। दोपार हूं घरै कोनी। कोई सवाल? आज आथण ७ सूं ९ तांई हूं घरै हूं। आभार। राम.
[pause 3 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — read: the note once, straight through, without translating]
[pause 3 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — read: it again, and find the two lines with hours in them]

You'll want to know.
[pause 2 seconds]
रैवो and राखो are requests, like लावो: please stay, please keep. लावसूं is Ram's own future: I will bring. काल सूं means since yesterday: सूं marks where the time starts.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — answer: what is wrong? (There has been no water since yesterday.)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: did the mechanic come today? (No: आज मिस्त्री (mistrī) कोनी आयो।)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: if you have a question, when is Ram at home? (This evening, seven until nine.)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
When must you be at home? (Tomorrow morning, ९ सूं ११ तांई.) What should you leave open? (The gate: दरवाजो खुलो राखो।).
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
From the note: when must you be at home tomorrow?
