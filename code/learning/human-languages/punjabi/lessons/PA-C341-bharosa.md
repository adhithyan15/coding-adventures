## You'll want to know: ਭਰੋਸਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C341-DOUBT341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਰੋਸਾ** — *bharosā* — "trust; reliance".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਮੇਰੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਭਰੋਸਾ ਕਰਦੇ ਹੋ?
>
> *kī tusī̃ mere utte bharosā karde ho?*
>
> Do you trust me?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਰੋਸਾ** (*bharosā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*bharavaśya*, "dependence on support"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bharossā*, "confidence", under it (CDIAL 9398).
