## You'll want to know: huáiniàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C322-BACKTHEN322-05]; assesses=[] -->

*huáiniàn* — "to cherish the memory of; to look back fondly on".

> *wǒ hěn huáiniàn xiǎoshíhou de shēnghuó* — I really miss my childhood days.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huáiniàn* is built from *huái* and *niàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hweaj* and *nemH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hold to the breast' and 'to think of', and the second 'to think of'.
