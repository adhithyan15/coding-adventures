# ತೀರ್ಪು (tīrpu) — a verdict; a judgement

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a judge, then the Kannada for to accuse.

## You'll want to know: ತೀರ್ಪು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C321-NYAYA321-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೀರ್ಪು** — *tīrpu* — "a verdict; a judgement".

> ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು ಸೋಮವಾರ ತೀರ್ಪು ಕೊಟ್ಟರು.
>
> *nyāyādhīśaru sōmavāra tīrpu koṭṭaru.*
>
> The judge gave the verdict on Monday.

A verdict is given: ತೀರ್ಪು ಕೊಡು or, more formally, ತೀರ್ಪು ನೀಡು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೀರ್ಪು** (*tīrpu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3278 lists Kannada *tīrpu*, "finishing, concluding", under *tīr*, "to end, be settled, be decided". A verdict settles a case.
