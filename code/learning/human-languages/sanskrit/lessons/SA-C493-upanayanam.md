## You'll want to know: उपनयनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C493-SAMSKARA493-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उपनयनम्** — *upanayanam* — "the sacred-thread ceremony".

> उपनयनात् अनन्तरं बालः शिष्यः भवति।
>
> *upanayanāt anantaraṃ bālaḥ śiṣyaḥ bhavati.*
>
> After the sacred-thread ceremony the boy becomes a pupil.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपनयनम्** (*upanayanam*) is built from *upa*, "near", and the root √nī, "to lead", the root of **नयति**: the teacher leads the boy to himself (MW).
