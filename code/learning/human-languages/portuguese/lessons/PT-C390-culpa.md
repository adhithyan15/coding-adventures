## You'll want to know: a culpa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C390-CULPA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a culpa** — "the blame, the fault; the guilt".

> A culpa não foi tua.
>
> *It was not your fault.*

*Ter a culpa* — to be to blame; *por culpa de* — because of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*culpa* comes straight from Latin *culpa* "fault, blame" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), with no change of form.
