# பாகுபாடு (pākupāṭu) — discrimination

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C370-VARUMAI370-05, TA-LEX-C371-CAMATTUVAM371-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for to exploit, then the Tamil for equality.

## You'll want to know: பாகுபாடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C371-CAMATTUVAM371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**பாகுபாடு** — *pākupāṭu* — "discrimination".

> வேலையில் யாரிடமும் பாகுபாடு காட்டக் கூடாது.
>
> *vēlaiyil yāriṭamum pākupāṭu kāṭṭak kūṭātu.*
>
> Nobody should be discriminated against at work.

பாகுபாடு காட்டு, to discriminate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பாகுபாடு** (*pākupāṭu*) is built from *pāku*, "a portion, a division", usually traced to Sanskrit *bhāga*, "portion" (CDIAL 9430), and *pāṭu*, the noun of *paṭu*: a dividing of people into parts.
