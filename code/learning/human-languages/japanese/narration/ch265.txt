You'll want to know: けむり.
けむり — kemuri — "smoke".
けむりで そらが くらく なりました。 — kemuri de sora ga kuraku narimashita — "The sky went dark with smoke."

The word, taken apart.
けむり (kemuri) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ひろがる (hirogaru) — to spread; to widen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for for a while, then the Japanese for smoke.

You'll want to know: ひろがる.
ひろがる — hirogaru — "to spread; to widen".
かぜが あったら、ひが ひろがるでしょう。 — kaze ga attara, hi ga hirogaru deshō — "If there were a wind, the fire would spread."
Polite ひろがります, hirogarimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): ひが ひろがりました。, hi ga hirogarimashita.

The word, taken apart.
ひろがる (hirogaru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the intransitive partner of hirogeru, "to spread out".

Lesson 3 of 5.
しょうぼうしゃ (shōbōsha) — a fire engine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for smoke, then the Japanese for to spread.

You'll want to know: しょうぼうしゃ.
しょうぼうしゃ — shōbōsha — "a fire engine".
しょうぼうしゃが すぐ きました。 — shōbōsha ga sugu kimashita — "A fire engine arrived at once."

The word, taken apart.
しょうぼうしゃ (shōbōsha) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shō, "extinguish" (Middle Chinese sjew), bō, "ward off" (bjang) and sha, "car" (tsyhae) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to spread, then the Japanese for a fire engine.

You'll want to know: にげみち.
にげみち — nigemichi — "an escape route; a way out".
にげみちは かいだんです。 — nigemichi wa kaidan desu — "The way out is by the stairs."

The word, taken apart.
にげみち (nigemichi) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from nige, "fleeing" (from nigeru), and michi, "road" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
