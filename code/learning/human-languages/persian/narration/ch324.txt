Persian, chapter 324: Accidents.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حادثه (hâdese) — a mishap, an accident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to quarrel, then the Persian for to beat.

You'll want to know: حادثه.
حادثه (hâdese) — "a mishap, an accident".
حادثه (hâdese) در جاده شمال اتفاق افتاد. Hâdese dar jâdde-ye shomâl ettefâq oftâd. The accident happened on the northern road.
Broken plural havâdes. Wider than tasâdof "a crash": any sudden harmful event. Written with ث.

The word, taken apart.
hâdese is borrowed from Arabic ḥāditha (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "an event, an incident".

Lesson 2 of 5.
سانحه (sânehe) — a disaster, a serious accident (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to beat, then the Persian for a mishap.

You'll want to know: سانحه.
سانحه (sânehe) — "a disaster, a serious accident (formal)".
در این سانحه دو نفر زخمی شدند. Dar in sânehe do nafar zakhmi shodand. Two people were injured in this accident.
Formal, in the news, often for air or road accidents: sânehe-ye havâyi "an air crash".

The word, taken apart.
sânehe is usually traced to Arabic sāniḥa, "something that occurs, an incident"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تصادف کردن (tasâdof kardan) — to crash, to have a collision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a mishap, then the Persian for a disaster.

You'll want to know: تصادف کردن.
تصادف کردن (tasâdof kardan) — "to crash, to have a collision".
ماشین ما با یک تاکسی تصادف کرد. Mâshin-e mâ bâ yek tâksi tasâdof kard. Our car crashed into a taxi.
The other vehicle takes bâ. Present tasâdof mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
tasâdof is borrowed from Arabic taṣāduf (EtymDB), usually glossed "a collision, a chance meeting". kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
برخورد کردن (barkhord kardan) — to collide, to hit; to treat (someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a disaster, then the Persian for to crash.

You'll want to know: برخورد کردن.
برخورد کردن (barkhord kardan) — "to collide, to hit; to treat (someone)".
دوچرخه به یک درخت برخورد کرد. Docharkhe be yek derakht barkhord kard. The bicycle hit a tree.
What is hit takes be. With bâ it means "to treat": bâ mâ khub barkhord kard "he treated us well".

The word, taken apart.
barkhord joins bar "on" and khord, the past stem of khordan "to eat, to strike"; kardan "to do" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
واژگون شدن (vâzhgun shodan) — to overturn, to flip over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to crash, then the Persian for to collide.

You'll want to know: واژگون شدن.
واژگون شدن (vâzhgun shodan) — "to overturn, to flip over".
اتوبوس در برف واژگون شد. Otobus dar barf vâzhgun shod. The bus overturned in the snow.
Written Persian; in speech chap kardan. Present vâzhgun mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
vâzhgun "upside down" is a native Persian word written with ژ; EtymDB lists no entry, and its deeper origin is not settled. shodan "to become" is inherited Persian.
