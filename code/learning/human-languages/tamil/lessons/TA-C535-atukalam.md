# ஆடுகளம் (āṭukaḷam) — a pitch; a court

## You'll want to know: ஆடுகளம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C535-AADUKALAM535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆடுகளம்** — *āṭukaḷam* — "a pitch; a court".

> மழையால் ஆடுகளம் நனைந்திருந்தது.
>
> *maḻaiyāl āṭukaḷam naṉaintiruntatu.*
>
> Because of the rain the pitch was soaked.

Word for word "playing ground": ஆடு "to play" + களம் (*kaḷam*) "a field, a floor".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆடுகளம்** (*āṭukaḷam*) is a native compound: ஆடு (*āṭu*), "to move, to dance, to play" (DEDR 347), and களம் (*kaḷam*), "a field, a floor".
