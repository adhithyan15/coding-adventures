Lesson 1 of 5.
l'oreiller — a pillow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a shelf, then the French for a curtain.

You'll want to know: l'oreiller.
l'oreiller — "a pillow".

The word, taken apart.
oreiller from Old French orillier (EtymDB 2.1), built from the word for "ear", from Latin auricula, "a little ear", with -ier: the thing for the ear.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la brosse à dents — a toothbrush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a towel; a napkin, then the French for soap.

You'll want to know: la brosse à dents.
la brosse à dents — "a toothbrush".

The phrase, taken apart.
brosse à dents — "brush for teeth": brosse, "brush", first "brushwood" in Old French (EtymDB 2.1), of uncertain origin; à, "for"; and dents, "teeth", from Latin dens, dentis.
