# Session Map — Chapter 1 (Hola and Buenos Días)

How Chapter 1's five lessons compose into car-ride sessions, with the
spaced-repetition schedule. Mechanics in
[`HL00`](../../../specs/HL00-human-language-curriculum-framework.md).

Chapter 1 is built **atom-first**: teach the pieces, then assemble them.
*hola*, then *bien* (and its adjective *bueno/buena*), then *día* (and the
plural rule), then *buenos días* assembled from *bueno + días* with
agreement, then a practice lesson. Grammar arrives exactly where a word
needs it — plurals with *día*, agreement with *buenos días* — never
front-loaded.

Reviews are scheduled in **session-counts, not calendar days**: a word from
session *N* is resurfaced at *N+1*, *N+3*, *N+7*, *N+15*. Each lesson is a
few minutes, so a short commute can hold one or two. (Lessons are named by
**slug**, not number — see `HL00`; this map is the authoritative pedagogical
order.)

## Session-by-session

| Session | New lesson(s) | Introduces |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | hola | the casual greeting |
| 2 | bien | "well"; adjective sibling bueno/buena |
| 3 | el / la | grammatical **gender** (from Latin) + the two "the"s |
| 4 | día | first noun (applies gender); the plural rule |
| 5 | buenos días | assembled; adjective **agreement** |
| 6 | practice — hola & buenos días | — |

Review lessons (fresh-combination resurfacings, not verbatim repeats) are
generated as each session's due words come up, per the interval schedule.

## Chapter 2 — the rest of the greetings

Atom-first again: *tarde* → *buenas tardes* (introduces **feminine**
agreement), *noche* → *buenas noches*, practice. Reviews of Chapter 1 words
fold in per the interval schedule.

| New lesson(s) | Introduces |
|---|---|
| tarde · buenas tardes | feminine noun (*la tarde*); feminine agreement (*buenas*) |
| noche · buenas noches | -ct-→-ch- sound pattern; greeting-and-farewell |
| practice — four greetings | — |

## Chapter 3 — introducing yourself

The introduction exchange, atom by atom, including the *tú*/*usted* pronoun
pair.

| New lesson(s) | Introduces |
|---|---|
| me · llamar | first pronoun; cl-→ll- pattern |
| me llamo | reflexive verbs |
| tú / usted | informal vs formal "you"; roots (thou; *vuestra merced*) |
| cómo · se llama | question words + accent; reflexive *se* |
| ¿cómo se llama usted? | assembling the question + answering |
| mucho · gusto (mucho gusto) · practice | -lt-→-ch-; the full exchange |

## What comes next (recursive)

Chapter 4 answers what Chapter 3 ends on: their reply to **"¿cómo está
usted?"** — how are you? — opening the responses cycle (*bien*, *gracias*,
*regular*, *más o menos*). Then farewells (*hasta luego / mañana / pronto*,
*adiós*). Each new noun keeps carrying its gender + plural, each new pronoun
its root, per the pattern *día* and *tú/usted* established.
