## You'll want to know: ವಿನಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C497-ABHIMANA497-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿನಯ** — *vinaya* — "modesty; humility".

> ದೊಡ್ಡ ಬಹುಮಾನ ಗೆದ್ದರೂ ಅವಳಲ್ಲಿ ವಿನಯ ಇದೆ.
>
> *doḍḍa bahumāna geddarū avaḷalli vinaya ide.*
>
> Though she won a big prize, she is modest.

ವಿನಯದಿಂದ, politely, humbly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿನಯ** (*vinaya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vinaya*, "leading away; guidance, good breeding" (CDIAL 11767).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
