## You'll want to know: はらいもどす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C420-RYOUSHUUSHO420-05]; assesses=[] -->

**はらいもどす** — *haraimodosu* — "to refund".

> **うんきゅうに なったので、えきが うんちんを はらいもどします。** — *unkyū ni natta node, eki ga unchin o haraimodoshimasu* — "The service was cancelled, so the station is refunding the fare."

Polite **はらいもどします**, *haraimodoshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **えきが うんちんを はらいもどしました。**, *eki ga unchin o haraimodoshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はらいもどす** (*haraimodosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *harai*, "paying" (from *harau*) and *modosu*, "to give back"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
