## You'll want to know: iūdex, iūdicis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C308-IUDEX-01]; assesses=[] -->

**iūdex, iūdicis** — "a judge; a juror".

> Iūdex testēs diū audīvit.
>
> *The judge listened to the witnesses for a long time.*

Masculine (also feminine), 3rd declension. In big trials many *iūdicēs* sat together as a jury.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**iūdex** joins *iūs*, "law, right", and the root of *dīcō*, "to say" (EtymDB): the one who speaks the law. English *judge* comes from it through French.
