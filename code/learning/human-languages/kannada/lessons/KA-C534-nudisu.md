## You'll want to know: ನುಡಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C534-SANGITA534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ನುಡಿಸು** — *nuḍisu* — "to play an instrument".

> ನನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣ ಗಿಟಾರ್ ನುಡಿಸುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *nanna aṇṇa giṭār nuḍisuttāne.*
>
> My elder brother plays the guitar.

Past (I …): ನುಡಿಸಿದೆ, *nuḍiside*.

Literally to make it speak: ನುಡಿ, to speak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನುಡಿಸು** (*nuḍisu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3784 lists Kannada *nuḍi*, "to sound, utter, speak", and *nuḍisu*, "to cause to utter": you make the instrument speak.
