German, chapter 140: To lie, To get dressed, To tell, To feel, To fall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
liegen — to lie (be lying).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to lay, then the German for to put.

You'll want to know: liegen.
liegen — "to lie (be lying)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: liegen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: liegen, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say liegen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for to lay, then the German for to put, then say liegen again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does liegen mean? ("To lie (be lying)".) Say it once more.
