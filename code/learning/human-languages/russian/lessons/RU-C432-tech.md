## You'll want to know: течь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C432-PROTEKAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**течь** (*tech*) — "to flow; to leak".

> Под раковиной течёт вода.
>
> *Pod rákovinoy techyót vodá.*
>
> Water is leaking under the sink.

Imperfective. Present *теку, течёшь, текут*; past *тёк, текла*. As a feminine noun, *течь* is a leak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*течь* 'to flow' is usually counted an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists it with that sense but without a source. *Восток* 'east' is often linked with the same root.
