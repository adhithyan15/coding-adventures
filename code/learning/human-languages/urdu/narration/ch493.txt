Urdu, chapter 493: Mourning.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
انتقال کرنا (intiqāl karnā) — to pass away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a last will, then the Urdu for an inheritance.

You'll want to know: انتقال کرنا.
انتقال کرنا — intiqāl karnā — "to pass away".
دادا پچھلے سال انتقال کر گئے۔ dādā pichhle sāl intiqāl kar gae. My grandfather passed away last year.
Past (she …): وہ انتقال کر گئی, voh intiqāl kar gaī.

The word, taken apart.
انتقال کرنا (intiqāl karnā) is built from انتقال, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic intiqāl, "moving from one place to another", and کرنا, "to do".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

انہوں نے دادا کو قبرستان میں دفنایا۔ unhoṅ ne dādā ko qabristān meṅ dafnāyā. They buried my grandfather in the graveyard.
