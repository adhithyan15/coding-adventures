## You'll want to know: るす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C163-HOME163-04]; assesses=[] -->

**るす** — *rusu* — "being out; being away from home".

> **どようびは いつも るすです。** — *doyōbi wa itsumo rusu desu* — "On Saturdays I am always out."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**るす** (*rusu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ru*, "detain, stop" (Middle Chinese *ljuw*) and *su*, "guard" (*syuwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
