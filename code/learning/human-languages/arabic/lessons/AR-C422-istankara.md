## You'll want to know: استنكر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C422-MAWDUI422-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استنكر** — *istankara* — "to condemn".

> استنكر الناس العنف في الشارع.
>
> *istankara an-nās al-ʿunf fī ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> People condemned the violence in the street.

Present: يستنكر, *yastankir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استنكر** (*istankara*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-k-r*, "not to know, to deny" (Lane). It is Form X, to hold a thing to be wrong: to condemn.
