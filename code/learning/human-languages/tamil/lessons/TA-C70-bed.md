# கட்டில் (kaṭṭil) — a bed

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C70-ROOM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: what did *mēsai* mean?

## You'll want to know: கட்டில்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C70-ROOM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**கட்டில்** (*kaṭṭil*) — "a bed".

A bed — and in Tamil it is named as a **made thing**: a frame with cords or webbing tied
across it.

You already have தூக்கம் (*tūkkam*), sleep. The pair is easy to hold: the தூக்கம் happens on
the கட்டில்.

Its middle is the doubled **ட்ட** you took apart in சட்டை, and it closes on **ல்** — a letter
with the vowel taken off, exactly like the ண் in வண்டி, except that here there is nothing
after it to lean on.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two, and you can now name where you sleep.
