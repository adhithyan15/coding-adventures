Marathi, chapter 297: Thieves in the Night.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घुसणे (ghusṇe) — to barge in; to force one's way in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to recover, then the Marathi for to be missed.

You'll want to know: घुसणे.
घुसणे — ghusṇe — "to barge in; to force one's way in".
रात्री दोन चोर खिडकीतून घरात घुसले. rātrī don cor khiḍkītūn gharāt ghusle. At night two thieves forced their way into the house through the window.
Past (he …): तो घुसला, to ghuslā.
The place entered takes -त: घरात घुसणे (ghusṇe).

The word, taken apart.
घुसणे (ghusṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ghusṇe, 'to enter forcibly', under ghuss, 'thrust in, pierce' (CDIAL 4492).

Lesson 2 of 5.
फोडणे (phoḍṇe) — to break open; to smash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be missed, then the Marathi for to barge in.

You'll want to know: फोडणे.
फोडणे — phoḍṇe — "to break open; to smash".
चोरांनी कपाटाचं कुलूप फोडलं. corānnī kapāṭācaṁ kulūp phoḍlaṁ. The thieves broke the lock of the cupboard.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने फोडलं, tyāne phoḍlaṁ.
Transitive: the thing broken agrees with the past: कुलूप फोडलं, खिडकी फोडली.

The word, taken apart.
फोडणे (phoḍṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi phoḍṇe, 'to break', under Sanskrit sphoṭayati, 'bursts, splits, breaks' (CDIAL 13857).

Lesson 3 of 5.
हिसकावणे (hiskāvṇe) — to snatch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to barge in, then the Marathi for to break open.

You'll want to know: हिसकावणे.
हिसकावणे — hiskāvṇe — "to snatch".
एका माणसाने तिच्या हातातली पिशवी हिसकावली. ekā māṇsāne ticyā hātātlī piśvī hiskāvlī. A man snatched the bag from her hand.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने हिसकावलं, tyāne hiskāvlaṁ.
The thing snatched is the object and agrees with the past: पिशवी हिसकावली.

The word, taken apart.
हिसकावणे (hiskāvṇe) is inherited: it belongs to the family CDIAL lists under hicc (CDIAL 14083), where Marathi hisakṇe is 'to jerk'. To snatch is to jerk away.

Lesson 4 of 5.
लुटणे (luṭṇe) — to loot; to plunder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to break open, then the Marathi for to snatch.

You'll want to know: लुटणे.
लुटणे — luṭṇe — "to loot; to plunder".
रस्त्यात चार माणसांनी प्रवाशांना लुटलं. rastyāt cār māṇsānnī pravāśānnā luṭlaṁ. On the road four men robbed the travellers.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने लुटलं, tyāne luṭlaṁ.
The victims take -ला: प्रवाशांना लुटणे (luṭṇe); goods are the object: दुकान लुटणे (luṭṇe).

The word, taken apart.
लुटणे (luṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi luṭṇe, 'plunders', under luṭṭati, 'plunders' (CDIAL 11078).

Lesson 5 of 5.
लुबाडणे (lubāḍṇe) — to fleece; to swindle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to snatch, then the Marathi for to loot.

You'll want to know: लुबाडणे.
लुबाडणे — lubāḍṇe — "to fleece; to swindle".
त्या माणसाने गोड बोलून आजीला लुबाडलं. tyā māṇsāne goḍ bolūn ājīlā lubāḍlaṁ. That man swindled Grandma with sweet talk.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने लुबाडलं, tyāne lubāḍlaṁ.
Like लुटणे (luṭṇe), but by trickery; the victim takes -ला.

The word, taken apart.
लुबाडणे (lubāḍṇe) is a Marathi verb of fleecing by trickery; its origin is not settled.
