## You'll want to know: допрос
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C292-DOPROS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**допрос** (*doprós*) — "questioning, an interrogation".

> Допрос длился три часа.
>
> *Doprós dlílsya tri chasá.*
>
> The questioning lasted three hours.

Masculine. *На допросе* — under questioning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*допрос* is built on *до-* and the root of *просить* "to ask", Proto-Slavic *prositi*, from PIE *preḱ-* (EtymDB).
