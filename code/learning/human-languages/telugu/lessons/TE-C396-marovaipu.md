# మరోవైపు (marōvaipu) — on the other hand

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C395-KANUKA395-05, TE-LEX-C396-VIRUDDHAM396-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a footing, then the Telugu for nevertheless.

## You'll want to know: మరోవైపు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C396-VIRUDDHAM396-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మరోవైపు** — *marōvaipu* — "on the other hand".

> ఒక వైపు డబ్బు కావాలి, మరోవైపు సమయం లేదు.
>
> *oka vaipu ḍabbu kāvāli, marōvaipu samayaṁ lēdu.*
>
> On one side we need money; on the other hand there is no time.

ఒక వైపు ... మరోవైపు ..., on one hand ... on the other.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మరోవైపు** (*marōvaipu*) is a Telugu phrase of *marō*, "another", and *vaipu*, "a side, a direction"; the origin of *vaipu* is uncertain.
