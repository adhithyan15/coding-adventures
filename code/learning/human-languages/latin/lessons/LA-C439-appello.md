## You'll want to know: appellō, appellere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C439-EGREDIOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**appellō, appellere** — "to put in (to land), to bring a ship to shore".

> Nautae nāvem ad lītus appellunt.
>
> *The sailors bring the ship in to the shore.*

*appellō, appellere, appulī, appulsum*, third conjugation. Do not mix it up with *appellō, appellāre* 'to call', a different verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*appellō, appellere* is built from *ad-* 'to' and *pellō* 'to drive, to push' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to drive a ship to land.
