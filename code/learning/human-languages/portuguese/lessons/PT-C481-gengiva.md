## You'll want to know: a gengiva
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C481-ROSTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a gengiva** — "gum (in the mouth)".

> Escovo os dentes com cuidado para não magoar as gengivas.
>
> *I brush my teeth carefully so as not to hurt my gums.*

Chewing gum is a different word: *a pastilha elástica*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gengiva* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Latin *gingīva* "the gums" (EtymDB).
