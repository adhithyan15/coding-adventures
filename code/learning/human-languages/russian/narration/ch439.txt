Russian, chapter 439: Turns and dead ends.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
поворот (povorót) — a turn, a bend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a landmark, then the Russian for a route map.

You'll want to know: поворот.
поворот (povorót) — "a turn, a bend".
После поворота будет мост. Pósle povoróta búdet most. After the bend there will be a bridge.
Masculine. Поворот направо — a right turn.

The word, taken apart.
поворот (povorót) is built from по- and ворот 'a turning', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic vortŭ, on the root of вертеть 'to turn'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
развилка (razvílka) — a fork (in a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a route map, then the Russian for a turn.

You'll want to know: развилка.
развилка (razvílka) — "a fork (in a road)".
На развилке мы повернули налево. Na razvílke my povernúli nalévo. At the fork we turned left.
Feminine. Built on вилка — a fork.

The word, taken apart.
развилка (razvílka) is built from раз- 'apart' and вилка 'a fork', from вилы 'a pitchfork'. EtymDB lists вилка without a deeper source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
тупик (tupík) — a dead end, a cul-de-sac; an impasse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a turn, then the Russian for a fork.

You'll want to know: тупик.
тупик (tupík) — "a dead end, a cul-de-sac; an impasse".
Эта улица — тупик. Éta úlitsa — tupík. This street is a dead end.
Masculine; the stress moves: тупика (tupiká). Figurative: в тупике — at a dead end.

The word, taken apart.
тупик (tupík) is built from тупой 'blunt' and the ending -ик (EtymDB): a street with a blunt end.

Lesson 4 of 5.
срезать (srézat) — to take a short cut; to cut off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fork, then the Russian for a dead end.

You'll want to know: срезать.
срезать (srézat) — "to take a short cut; to cut off".
Мы срезали дорогу через парк. My srézali dorógu chérez park. We took a short cut through the park.
Perfective; the imperfective срезать (srézat) (srezát) has the stress on the а. Срезать путь — to take a short cut. Future срежу, срежешь.

The word, taken apart.
срезать (srézat) is built from с- 'off' and резать 'to cut', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic rězati (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
огибать (ogibát) — to go round, to skirt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dead end, then the Russian for to take a short cut.

You'll want to know: огибать.
огибать (ogibát) — "to go round, to skirt".
Дорога огибает озеро. Doróga ogibáyet ózero. The road goes round the lake.
Imperfective; perfective обогнуть (обогну, обогнёшь).

The word, taken apart.
огибать (ogibát) is built from о- 'round' and -гибать, on the root of гнуть 'to bend', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic gŭnǫti (EtymDB).
