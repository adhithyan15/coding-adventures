## You'll want to know: īnsānia, īnsāniae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C510-INSA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**īnsānia, īnsāniae** — "madness, folly".

> Haec īnsānia est, nōn cōnsilium.
>
> *This is madness, not a plan.*

Feminine, first declension, from *īnsānus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**īnsānia** is built on *īnsānus* "mad" (Lewis & Short). English *insanity* comes from the related *īnsānitās*.
