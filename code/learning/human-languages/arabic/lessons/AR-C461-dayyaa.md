## You'll want to know: ضيع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C461-ILGHA461-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ضيع** — *ḍayyaʿa* — "to waste time; to lose".

> ضيعنا ساعة في الازدحام.
>
> *ḍayyaʿnā sāʿa fī al-izdiḥām.*
>
> We wasted an hour in the traffic jam.

Present: يضيع, *yuḍayyiʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضيع** (*ḍayyaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-y-ʿ*, "to be lost, to perish" (Lane). It is Form II, "to let be lost".
