# ಗೂಢಚಾರ (gūḍhacāra) — a spy

## You'll want to know: ಗೂಢಚಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C345-ADAGU345-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೂಢಚಾರ** — *gūḍhacāra* — "a spy".

> ರಾಜನ ಗೂಢಚಾರ ಪಕ್ಕದ ರಾಜ್ಯಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋದನು.
>
> *rājana gūḍhacāra pakkada rājyakke hōdanu.*
>
> The king's spy went to the neighbouring kingdom.

Plural: ಗೂಢಚಾರರು, *gūḍhacāraru*.

ಗೂಢ, hidden, and ಚಾರ, a goer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೂಢಚಾರ** (*gūḍhacāra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gūḍhacāra*, "a spy" (Monier-Williams), from *gūḍha*, "hidden", and *cāra*, "going, a goer".
