## You'll want to know: گھونسا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C307-JHAGRA307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گھونسا** — *ghūnsā* — "a punch".

> گھونسا اس کے منہ پر لگا۔
>
> *ghūnsā us ke muṅh par lagā.*
>
> The punch landed on his mouth.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گھونسا** (*ghūnsā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ghūsā*, "a fist", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4498).
