Kannada, chapter 527: Raising a Child.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ದತ್ತು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊ (dattu tegeduko) — to adopt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a great-grandson, then the Kannada for younger.

You'll want to know: ದತ್ತು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊ.
ದತ್ತು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊ — dattu tegeduko — "to adopt".
ಆ ದಂಪತಿ ಒಂದು ಹೆಣ್ಣು ಮಗುವನ್ನು ದತ್ತು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡರು. ā dampati ondu heṇṇu maguvannu dattu tegedukoṇḍaru. That couple adopted a baby girl.
Past (I …): ದತ್ತು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡೆ, dattu tegedukoṇḍe.
ದತ್ತು is adoption, from Sanskrit ದತ್ತ, given; ದತ್ತು ಕೊಡು, to give a child in adoption.

The word, taken apart.
ದತ್ತು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊ (dattu tegeduko) joins dattu, "adoption", from Sanskrit datta, "given" (CDIAL 6140), and the native ತೆಗೆದುಕೊ, "to take": to take a child given to you.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸಲಹು (salahu) — to bring up; to protect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for younger, then the Kannada for to adopt.

You'll want to know: ಸಲಹು.
ಸಲಹು — salahu — "to bring up; to protect".
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ತಾಯಿ ಇಲ್ಲದ ಮಗುವನ್ನು ಸಲಹಿದರು. ajji tāyi illada maguvannu salahidaru. Grandmother brought up the motherless child.
Past (I …): ಸಲಹಿದೆ, salahide.
An old, warm word: to shelter and raise.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಲಹು (salahu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2781 lists Kannada salapu, salavu, salahu, "to forward, foster, tend, preserve, bring up".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪೋಷಿಸು (pōṣisu) — to nourish; to support.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to adopt, then the Kannada for to bring up.

You'll want to know: ಪೋಷಿಸು.
ಪೋಷಿಸು — pōṣisu — "to nourish; to support".
ಅವನು ತನ್ನ ದುಡಿಮೆಯಿಂದ ಇಡೀ ಕುಟುಂಬವನ್ನು ಪೋಷಿಸುತ್ತಾನೆ. avanu tanna duḍimeyinda iḍī kuṭumbavannu pōṣisuttāne. He supports the whole family with his earnings.
Past (I …): ಪೋಷಿಸಿದೆ, pōṣiside.
ಪೋಷಕರು, parents or guardians.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೋಷಿಸು (pōṣisu) is built on Sanskrit poṣaṇa, "nourishing, cherishing" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.

ಮಗು ಅಂಬೆಗಾಲು ಇಡುತ್ತಾ ಅಡುಗೆಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದಿತು. magu ambegālu iḍuttā aḍugemanege banditu. The baby crawled into the kitchen.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮಲತಾಯಿ (malatāyi) — a stepmother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to nourish, then the Kannada for crawling on all fours.

You'll want to know: ಮಲತಾಯಿ.
ಮಲತಾಯಿ — malatāyi — "a stepmother".
ಆ ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮಲತಾಯಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯವಳು. ā katheyalli malatāyi tumbā oḷḷeyavaḷu. In that story the stepmother is very kind.
ಮಲ- marks a step-relation: ಮಲತಮ್ಮ, a younger half-brother.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಲತಾಯಿ (malatāyi) is a Kannada compound: mala-, "step-", whose origin is not settled, and tāyi, "mother" (DEDR 364).
