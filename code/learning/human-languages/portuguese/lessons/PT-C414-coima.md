## You'll want to know: a coima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C414-BILHETEIRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a coima** — "the fine (official)".

> Quem viaja sem bilhete paga uma coima.
>
> *Anyone travelling without a ticket pays a fine.*

*A coima* is the official word on notices; people often say *a multa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *coima* is not settled. It is an old Portuguese word for a penalty, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
