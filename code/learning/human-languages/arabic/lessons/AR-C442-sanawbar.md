## You'll want to know: صنوبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C442-MUNTAJA442-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صنوبر** — *ṣanawbar* — "pine trees; a pine".

> نصبنا الخيمة تحت أشجار الصنوبر.
>
> *naṣabnā al-khayma taḥta ashjār aṣ-ṣanawbar.*
>
> We pitched the tent under the pine trees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صنوبر** (*ṣanawbar*) names the pine; the origin is not settled.
