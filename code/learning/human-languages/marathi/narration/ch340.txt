Marathi, chapter 340: Mysteries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रहस्य (rahasya) — a mystery; a secret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a holy man, then the Marathi for a moneylender.

You'll want to know: रहस्य.
रहस्य — rahasya — "a mystery; a secret".
त्या जुन्या वाड्याचं रहस्य कोणालाच माहीत नाही. tyā junyā vāḍyācaṁ rahasya koṇālāc māhīt nāhī. Nobody knows the secret of that old mansion.
Neuter.
रहस्यकथा, a mystery story.

The word, taken apart.
रहस्य (rahasya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit rahasya, 'secret, private; a mystery' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
थरारक (tharārak) — thrilling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the moral, then the Marathi for extraordinary.

You'll want to know: थरारक.
थरारक — tharārak — "thrilling".
त्या चित्रपटाचा शेवट फार थरारक होता. tyā citrapaṭācā śevaṭ phār tharārak hotā. The ending of that film was very thrilling.
थरारक (tharārak) keeps one form; थरार, a thrill.

The word, taken apart.
थरारक (tharārak) is built on tharār, 'a thrill, a shiver'; CDIAL lists Marathi tharārṇe, 'to tremble', under thar, 'tremble' (CDIAL 6092).

Lesson 5 of 5.
झगडणे (jhagaḍṇe) — to wrestle with; to struggle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for extraordinary, then the Marathi for thrilling.

You'll want to know: झगडणे.
झगडणे — jhagaḍṇe — "to wrestle with; to struggle".
नायक शेवटपर्यंत संकटांशी झगडला. nāyak śevaṭparyant saṅkaṭāṁśī jhagaḍlā. The hero wrestled with troubles to the very end.
Past (he …): तो झगडला, to jhagaḍlā.
The opponent takes -शी: संकटांशी झगडणे (jhagaḍṇe); also to quarrel.

The word, taken apart.
झगडणे (jhagaḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi jhagaḍṇe, 'to quarrel', under jhagaḍ, 'quarrel' (CDIAL 5321).
