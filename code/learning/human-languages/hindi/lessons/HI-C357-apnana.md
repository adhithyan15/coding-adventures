## You'll want to know: अपनाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C357-MULYA357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अपनाना** — *apnānā* — "to adopt, to take up as one's own".

> मीरा ने गाँव की भाषा अपना ली।
>
> *Mīrā ne gā̃v kī bhāṣā apnā lī.*
>
> Mira took the village's language as her own.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने यह आदत अपनाई, *usne yah ādat apnāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपनाना** (*apnānā*) is a verb made on अपना, "one's own", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed *\*ātmanaka*, "own" (CDIAL 1135): to make something one's own.
