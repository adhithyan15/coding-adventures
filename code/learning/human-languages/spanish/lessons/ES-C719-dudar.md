## You'll want to know: dudar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C719-CONFIANZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**dudar** — "to doubt".

> Dudo que venga hoy.
>
> *I doubt he will come today.*

*Dudar que* takes the subjunctive. *La duda* is a doubt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dudar* is inherited from Latin *dubitāre* "to waver, to doubt" (EtymDB); the middle *-bit-* wore away.
