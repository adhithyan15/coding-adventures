Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 241: A Fall in the Lane.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चोट (choṭ) — an injury; a hurt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for brave, then the Marwadi for crazy.

You'll want to know: चोट.
चोट — choṭ — "an injury; a hurt".
म्हारी चोट घणी माड़ी कोनी। mhārī choṭ ghaṇī māṛī konī. My injury is not very bad.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चोट (choṭ) is inherited from Sanskrit cuṭati, "cuts off, strikes". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi coṭ, "wound" (CDIAL 4857).
