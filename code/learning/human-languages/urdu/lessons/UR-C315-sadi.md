## You'll want to know: صدی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C315-AJDAD315-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صدی** — *sadī* — "a century".

> یہ گھر دو صدیوں سے یہاں ہے۔
>
> *yih ghar do sadiyoṅ se yahāṅ hai.*
>
> This house has stood here for two centuries.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صدی** (*sadī*) is usually traced to Persian *sadī*, "a century", built on *sad*, "a hundred".
