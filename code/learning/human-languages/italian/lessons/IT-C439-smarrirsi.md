## You'll want to know: smarrirsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C439-ORIENTARSI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**smarrirsi** — "to get lost, to go astray".

> Senza la mappa ci siamo smarriti.
>
> *Without the map we got lost.*

A little more literary than *perdersi*; *oggetti smarriti* — lost property.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*smarrirsi* was borrowed from Old French *esmarrir* 'to trouble, to dismay' (EtymDB), which came from a Germanic word.
