## You'll want to know: лить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C465-LIVEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**лить** (*lit*) — "to pour".

> Дождь льёт с утра.
>
> *Dozhd lyot s utrá.*
>
> It has been pouring since the morning.

Imperfective. Present *лью, льёшь, льют*; past *лил, лила*. *Льёт как из ведра* — it is pouring with rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*лить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *liti* 'to pour' (EtymDB).
