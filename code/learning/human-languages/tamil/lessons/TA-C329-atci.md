## You'll want to know: ஆட்சி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C329-ARASAVAI329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆட்சி** — *āṭci* — "a reign; rule".

> அவருடைய ஆட்சியில் நாடு அமைதியாக இருந்தது.
>
> *avaruṭaiya āṭciyil nāṭu amaitiyāka iruntatu.*
>
> Under his reign the country was peaceful.

Also government today: ஆட்சிக்கு வா, to come to power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆட்சி** (*āṭci*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5157 gives it under ஆள், "to rule, reign over".
