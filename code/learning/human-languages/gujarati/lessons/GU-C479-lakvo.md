## You'll want to know: લકવો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C479-MANDU479-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લકવો** — *lakvo* — "paralysis".

> ગયા વરસે દાદાને લકવો થયો.
>
> *gayā varse dādāne lakvo thayo.*
>
> Last year my grandfather was paralysed by a stroke.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લકવો** (*lakvo*) is usually traced to Arabic *laqwa*, "facial palsy", through Persian.
