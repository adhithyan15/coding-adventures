## You'll want to know: めったに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C169-OFTEN169-03]; assesses=[] -->

**めったに** — *mettani* — "rarely (with a negative verb)".

> **ちちは めったに やすみません。** — *chichi wa mettani yasumimasen* — "My father rarely takes a day off."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めったに** (*mettani*) is built from *metta* and the native particle *ni*. *Metta* is written with two characters read with their on'yomi, *metsu*, "destroy" (Middle Chinese *mjiet*), and *ta*, "many" (*ta*); whether it was borrowed from Chinese or the characters were chosen for their sound is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
