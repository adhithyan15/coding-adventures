# ഗുണമേന്മ (guṇamēnma) — quality

## You'll want to know: ഗുണമേന്മ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C492-GUNAM492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗുണമേന്മ** — *guṇamēnma* — "quality".

> ആ കടയിലെ സാധനങ്ങൾക്ക് നല്ല ഗുണമേന്മയുണ്ട്.
>
> *ā kaṭayile sādhanaṅṅaḷkkŭ nalla guṇamēnmayuṇṭŭ.*
>
> The goods in that shop are of good quality.

ഗുണം, a good quality, plus മേന്മ, excellence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗുണമേന്മ** (*guṇamēnma*) is a Malayalam compound built from ഗുണം, quality, from Sanskrit *guṇa* (CDIAL 4190), and the native word മേന്മ, excellence (DEDR 5086).
