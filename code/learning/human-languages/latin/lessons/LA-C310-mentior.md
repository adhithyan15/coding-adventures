## You'll want to know: mentior, mentīrī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C310-IURO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mentior, mentīrī** — "to lie, to tell a lie".

> Servus mentītus est; nam pōculum ipse frēgerat.
>
> *The slave lied, for he had broken the cup himself.*

*mentior, mentīrī, mentītus sum*, a deponent. Its noun *mendācium*, 'a lie', is taught.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mentior** is probably built, says Lewis & Short, on the root of *mēns*, "mind", with a first sense "to invent, to make up"; this is not certain.
