## You'll want to know: der Gegner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C508-SIEGER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Gegner** — "opponent".

> Der Gegner war stärker als wir.
>
> *The opponent was stronger than us.*

Plural: *die Gegner*. Also an opponent of an idea: *ein Gegner der Reform*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gegner* comes from Middle Low German *gēgenēre* (EtymDB), built on *gegen* "against".
