## You'll want to know: sfornare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C336-FORNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sfornare** — "to take out of the oven; to turn out (lots of something)".

> Ho appena sfornato il pane.
>
> *I have taken the bread out of the oven this minute.*

Also figurative: *sforna un libro all'anno*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sfornare* is *s-* "out of" with *forno* "oven", from Latin *furnus* (Lewis & Short).
