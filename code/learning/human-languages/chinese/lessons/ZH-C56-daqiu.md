## You'll want to know: dǎqiú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C56-PLAY56-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎqiú* — "to play ball".

> *xīngqītiān wǒmen dǎqiú* — We play ball on Sunday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎqiú* is built from dǎ 'to strike' and qiú 'ball', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *taengX* and *gjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), though dǎ is not the sound *taengX* would regularly give. The dictionary glosses *gjuw* as 'fine jade', and under another way of writing it as a ball of fur that is struck.
