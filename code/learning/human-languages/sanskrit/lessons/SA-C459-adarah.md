## You'll want to know: आदरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C459-ADARA459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आदरः** — *ādaraḥ* — "regard".

> शिष्यः गुरुं प्रति आदरं दर्शयति।
>
> *śiṣyaḥ guruṃ prati ādaraṃ darśayati.*
>
> The disciple shows regard for his teacher.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आदरः** (*ādaraḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √dṛ, "to heed"; MW gives "respect, regard, notice" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
