## You'll want to know: વિજય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C323-SHAHID323-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વિજય** — *vijay* — "a victory".

> છેવટે રાજાની સેનાનો વિજય થયો.
>
> *chevṭe rājānī senāno vijay thayo.*
>
> In the end the king's army won.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિજય** (*vijay*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vijaya*, "victory, conquest" (Monier-Williams). It happens with **થવું**: **સેનાનો વિજય થયો**, the army won.
