## You'll want to know: sinken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C276-UMFRAGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sinken** — "to sink, to fall".

> Die Preise sanken im Sommer.
>
> *Prices fell in the summer.*

Strong verb with *sein*: *sank*, *ist gesunken*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sinken* is an inherited Germanic verb, from Old High German *sinkan* (EtymDB), and EtymDB lists Low German *sinken* and English *sink* among its cousins.
