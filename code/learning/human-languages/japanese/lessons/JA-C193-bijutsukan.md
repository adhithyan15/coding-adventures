**びじゅつかん** — *bijutsukan* — "art museum; art gallery".

> **しゅうまつ びじゅつかんに いきます。** — *shūmatsu bijutsukan ni ikimasu* — "I go to the art museum at the weekend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**びじゅつかん** (*bijutsukan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bi*, "beauty" (Middle Chinese *mijX*), *jutsu*, "art, skill" (*zywit*) and *kan*, "building" (*kwanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
