## You'll want to know: આત્મવિશ્વાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C392-SAJJ392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આત્મવિશ્વાસ** — *ātmaviśvās* — "self-confidence".

> જો તમારામાં આત્મવિશ્વાસ હશે તો તમે કોઈ પણ મુશ્કેલીને પહોંચી વળશો.
>
> *jo tamārāmā̃ ātmaviśvās haśe to tame koī paṇ muśkelīne pahõcī vaḷśo.*
>
> If you believe in yourself, you will cope with any difficulty.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આત્મવિશ્વાસ** (*ātmaviśvās*) is built from Sanskrit *ātman*, "self", and *viśvāsa*, "trust" (Monier-Williams): trust in oneself. **વિશ્વાસ** (*viśvās*) is the same second word.
