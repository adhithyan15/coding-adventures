Portuguese, chapter 368: Suponhamos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
supor — to suppose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the voluntary work, then the Portuguese for the charity.

You'll want to know: supor.
supor — "to suppose".
Suponho que ele já saiu de casa. I suppose he has already left home.
Conjugated like pôr: suponho, supões, supõe. Suponhamos que… — let us suppose that…

The word, taken apart.
supor is inherited from Latin suppōnere "to put or set under, to substitute" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from sub and pōnere "to put", and reshaped on pôr.

Lesson 3 of 5.
a hipótese — the hypothesis; the chance, the possibility.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to suppose, then the Portuguese for the supposition.

You'll want to know: a hipótese.
a hipótese — "the hypothesis; the chance, the possibility".
Há uma hipótese de o voo chegar mais cedo. There is a chance that the flight will arrive earlier.
In Portugal, hipótese also means chance: não tenho hipótese — I have no chance. Na hipótese de — in the event of.

The word, taken apart.
hipótese is a learned borrowing from Late Latin hypothesis (EtymDB), from Greek hupóthesis "base, basis of an argument, supposition", built on hupó "under" and thésis "a placing".

Lesson 5 of 5.
plausível — plausible, believable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the hypothesis, then the Portuguese for to presume.

You'll want to know: plausível.
plausível — "plausible, believable".
A explicação dele é plausível. His explanation is plausible.
Pouco plausível — hardly believable.

The word, taken apart.
plausível is a learned borrowing from Latin plausibilis "deserving applause, acceptable" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on plaudere "to clap": first praiseworthy, then believable.
