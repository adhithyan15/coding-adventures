## You'll want to know: ਮਾਮਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C343-MIXUP343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਮਲਾ** — *māmlā* — "a matter; an affair".

> ਇਹ ਮਾਮਲਾ ਸੌਖਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh māmlā saukhā nahīṁ hai.*
>
> This matter is not simple.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਮਲਾ** (*māmlā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *muʿāmala*, "dealing".
