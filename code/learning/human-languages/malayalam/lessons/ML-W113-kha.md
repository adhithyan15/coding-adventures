# ഖ (kha) — the letter inside സുഖം

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-NGA-01, ML-SCRIPT-C113-LLA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ങ and ള once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **സുഖം** (*sukham*), "well-being".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ഖ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-KHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഖ** — *kha*, a *k* with a puff of breath.

It is inside **സുഖമാണോ?** (*sukhamāṇō?*), "how are you?", built on **സുഖം**
(*sukham*), "well-being".

## Writing: ഖ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-KHA-01] -->

Put your pen on ഖ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
