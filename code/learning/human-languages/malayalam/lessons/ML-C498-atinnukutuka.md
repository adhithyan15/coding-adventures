## You'll want to know: അടിഞ്ഞുകൂടുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C498-KANTAL498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**അടിഞ്ഞുകൂടുക** — *aṭiññukūṭuka* — "to accumulate; to settle (as silt)".

> പുഴയുടെ തീരത്ത് ചെളി അടിഞ്ഞുകൂടി.
>
> *puḻayuṭe tīrattŭ ceḷi aṭiññukūṭi.*
>
> Mud settled along the bank of the river.

Past: അടിഞ്ഞുകൂടി, *aṭiññukūṭi*.

അടിയുക, to settle at the bottom, plus കൂടുക, to gather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അടിഞ്ഞുകൂടുക** (*aṭiññukūṭuka*) is a native Dravidian compound built on അടി, the bottom (DEDR 72), and കൂടുക, to come together (DEDR 1882).
