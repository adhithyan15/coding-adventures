## You'll want to know: بھنانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C420-AAMDANI420-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بھنانا** — *bhunānā* — "to cash (a cheque); to change (a note)".

> سارا نے بینک میں چیک بھنایا۔
>
> *sārā ne baink meṅ chek bhunāyā.*
>
> Sara cashed a cheque at the bank.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نوٹ بھنایا, *us ne noṭ bhunāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھنانا** (*bhunānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *bhunnā*, "to be changed (of money)", and *bhunānā* under Sanskrit *bhajyate*, "is broken" (CDIAL 9361): a big note is broken into small ones.
