## You'll want to know: le conjoint
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C491-RUPTURE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le conjoint** — "spouse, partner".

> Les conjoints sont invités à la fête de l'entreprise.
>
> *Spouses are invited to the company party.*

A woman is *la conjointe*. Formal: married or living together. In Québec, *le conjoint* is very common in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le conjoint* is the participle of *conjoindre* "to join together", from Latin *coniungere*, so *coniūnctus* "joined", built on *iungere* "to join" (Lewis & Short).
