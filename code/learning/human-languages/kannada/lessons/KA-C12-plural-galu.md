# ಹೆಸರುಗಳು, ಕೆಲಸಗಳು — more than one non-human thing

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-HUMAN-NONHUMAN-04, KA-LEX-C02-HESARU-01, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ಹೆಸರು**, “name”, then **ಕೆಲಸ**, “work”. These are non-human
nouns, so their ordinary plural can use one ending.

## Grammar Lens: add -ಗಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-PLURAL-GALU-07]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-HUMAN-NONHUMAN-04, KA-LEX-C02-HESARU-01, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01] -->

- **ಹೆಸರು** (*hesaru*, name) → **ಹೆಸರುಗಳು** (*hesarugaḷu*, names)
- **ಕೆಲಸ** (*kelasa*, work) → **ಕೆಲಸಗಳು** (*kelasagaḷu*, kinds of work or tasks)

The ending is **-ಗಳು** (*-gaḷu*). Use it here for ordinary non-human nouns.
Do not force it onto the family words: human plurals have their own patterns,
and *avaru* already gives you “they” for people.
