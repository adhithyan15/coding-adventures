## You'll want to know: はんたいする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C317-SANSEI317-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はんたいする** — *hantai suru* — "to be against; to oppose".

> **あぶないから、ははは はんたいしました。** — *abunai kara, haha wa hantai shimashita* — "My mother was against it because it is dangerous."

Polite **はんたいします**, *hantaishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **あには はんたいしました。**, *ani wa hantai shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はんたいする** (*hantai suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *hantai*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *han*, "anti-" (Middle Chinese *pjonX*) and *tai*, "opposite" (*twojH*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
