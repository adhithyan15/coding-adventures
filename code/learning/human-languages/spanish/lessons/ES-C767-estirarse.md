## You'll want to know: estirarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C767-PORVENIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**estirarse** — "to stretch".

> Me estiro antes de correr.
>
> *I stretch before running.*

*Estirar las piernas* — to stretch your legs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estirarse* is built on *tirar* "to pull" with the prefix *es-*. For *tirar* the origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
