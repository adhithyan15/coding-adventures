## You'll want to know: la turbulence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C420-ESCALE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la turbulence** — "the turbulence".

> Pendant les turbulences, tout le monde reste assis.
>
> *During the turbulence everyone stays seated.*

Often plural: *des turbulences*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*turbulence* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *turbulentia*, from *turba* 'disorder, crowd' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
