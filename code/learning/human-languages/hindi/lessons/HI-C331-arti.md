## You'll want to know: आरती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C331-VRAT331-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आरती** — *ārtī* — "an arati, worship with a lamp".

> शाम को मंदिर में आरती हुई।
>
> *śām ko mandir meṁ ārtī huī.*
>
> In the evening there was an arati at the temple.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आरती** (*ārtī*) goes back to Sanskrit *ārātrika* (CDIAL 1315): a lamp waved in circles before a god while a song is sung.
