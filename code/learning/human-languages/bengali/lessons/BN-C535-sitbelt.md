# সিটবেল্ট (siṭbelṭ) — a seat belt

## You'll want to know: সিটবেল্ট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C535-SURAKSHA535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সিটবেল্ট** — *siṭbelṭ* — "a seat belt".

> গাড়িতে উঠে আমি সিটবেল্ট বাঁধলাম।
>
> *Gāṛite uṭhe āmi siṭbelṭ bā̃dhlām.*
>
> I got into the car and fastened my seat belt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সিটবেল্ট** (*siṭbelṭ*) is borrowed from English *seat belt*, written as one word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
