## You'll want to know: सींग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C236-BEAST236-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सींग** — *sī̃g* — "a horn".

> हिरण माथै सींग है।
>
> *hiraṇ māthai sī̃g hai.*
>
> The deer has horns on its head.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सींग** (*sī̃g*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śṛṅga*, "a horn"; CDIAL lists Hindi *sī̃g*, "a horn", and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 12583).
