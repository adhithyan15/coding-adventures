# வேறுபடு (vēṟupaṭu) — to differ; to vary

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C357-ENINUM357-03, TA-LEX-C357-ENINUM357-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for except, then the Tamil for likewise.

## You'll want to know: வேறுபடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C357-ENINUM357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**வேறுபடு** — *vēṟupaṭu* — "to differ; to vary".

> இந்த இரண்டு கருத்துகளும் பல வழிகளில் வேறுபடுகின்றன.
>
> *inta iraṇṭu karuttukaḷum pala vaḻikaḷil vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉa.*
>
> These two views differ in many ways.

Past (I …): வேறுபட்டேன், *vēṟupaṭṭēṉ*.

From what: X-இலிருந்து வேறுபடு. The noun is வேறுபாடு, a difference.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வேறுபடு** (*vēṟupaṭu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5548 lists Tamil *vēṟu*, "other, different", and *vēṟu-paṭu*, "to become different", with *paṭu*, "to undergo".
