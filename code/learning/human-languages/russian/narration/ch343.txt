Russian, chapter 343: Criticising and approving.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
критиковать (kritikovát) — to criticise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to speak one’s mind, then the Russian for to share.

You'll want to know: критиковать.
критиковать (kritikovát) — "to criticise".
Мне не нравится, когда меня критикуют при всех. Mne ne nrávitsya, kogdá menyá kritikúyut pri vsekh. I do not like being criticised in front of everyone.
Imperfective, with the present критикую, критикуешь; perfective раскритиковать, to tear apart. It takes the accusative.

The word, taken apart.
критиковать (kritikovát) is built on критика (krítika), which is usually traced, through western languages, to Greek kritikḗ "the art of judging", from krínein "to separate, to judge".

Lesson 2 of 5.
критика (krítika) — criticism.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to share, then the Russian for to criticise.

You'll want to know: критика.
критика (krítika) — "criticism".
Он спокойно слушает критику. On spokóyno slúshayet krítiku. He listens calmly to criticism.
Feminine. Конструктивная критика (krítika) — constructive criticism. It is also reviewing as a field: литературная критика (krítika).

The word, taken apart.
критика (krítika) is usually traced, through French and German, to Greek kritikḗ "the art of judging", built on krínein "to separate, to decide".

Lesson 3 of 5.
одобрять (odobryát) — to approve of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to criticise, then the Russian for criticism.

You'll want to know: одобрять.
одобрять (odobryát) — "to approve of".
Отец не одобряет мой выбор. Otéts ne odobryáyet moy výbor. My father does not approve of my choice.
Imperfective; perfective одобрить. It takes the accusative.

The word, taken apart.
одобрять (odobryát) is built from о- and добрый "good", so to approve is to call a thing good. Добрый is inherited from Proto-Slavic dobrŭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
осуждать (osuzhdát) — to condemn, to disapprove of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for criticism, then the Russian for to approve of.

You'll want to know: осуждать.
осуждать (osuzhdát) — "to condemn, to disapprove of".
Я не осуждаю его: он очень устал. Ya ne osuzhdáyu yegó: on óchen ustál. I do not judge him harshly: he is very tired.
Imperfective; perfective осудить. In court it also means to convict.

The word, taken apart.
осуждать (osuzhdát) is built from о- and судить "to judge", on суд, inherited from Proto-Slavic sǫdŭ (EtymDB). The жд is the Church Slavonic softening of д.

Lesson 5 of 5.
упрекать (uprekát) — to reproach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to approve of, then the Russian for to condemn.

You'll want to know: упрекать.
упрекать (uprekát) — "to reproach".
Жена упрекает мужа в том, что он мало бывает дома. Zhená uprekáyet múzha v tom, chto on málo byváyet dóma. The wife reproaches her husband for being at home so little.
Imperfective; perfective упрекнуть. Упрекать кого в чём. The noun is упрёк, a reproach.

The word, taken apart.
упрекать (uprekát) is built on the noun упрёк "a reproach". The origin of the root is not settled.
