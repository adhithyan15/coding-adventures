Arabic, chapter 270: Boring, Booked, Available, International, Local.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ممل (mumill) — boring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a fault, then the Arabic for an advantage.

You'll want to know: ممل.
ممل — mumill — "boring".

The word, taken apart.
ممل (mumill) is built on the native Arabic root m-l-l, "to be weary of" (Lane). Mumill is the Form IV participle, "wearying".
