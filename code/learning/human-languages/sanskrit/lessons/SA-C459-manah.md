## You'll want to know: मानः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C459-ADARA459-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मानः** — *mānaḥ* — "self-respect; honour".

> मानः धनात् महत्तरः अस्ति।
>
> *mānaḥ dhanāt mahattaraḥ asti.*
>
> Honour is greater than wealth.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मानः** (*mānaḥ*) comes from the root √man, "to think": what one is thought to be worth; MW gives "regard, respect, honour" and also "pride" (MW).
