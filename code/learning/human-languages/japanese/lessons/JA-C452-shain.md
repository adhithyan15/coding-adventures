## You'll want to know: しゃいん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C452-BOSS452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃいん** — *shain* — "a company employee".

> **しゃいんは みんな まじめです。** — *shain wa minna majime desu* — "The employees are all hard-working."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃいん** (*shain*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sha*, "company" (Middle Chinese *dzyaeX*) and *in*, "member" (*hjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
