## You'll want to know: wéizhāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C420-PARK420-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wéizhāng* — "to break the traffic rules".

> *zhèr bù néng tíngchē tíng le jiù wéizhāng* — You can't park here: if you do, you're breaking the rules.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wéizhāng* is built from *wéi* 'to go against' and *zhāng* 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjw+j* and *tsyang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn your back on' and the second as a section of writing; the sense 'a rule' is not given there.
