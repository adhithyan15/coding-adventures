## You'll want to know: ひょうしき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C393-MEJIRUSHI393-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひょうしき** — *hyōshiki* — "a road sign; a marker".

> **ひょうしきに ちゅういして ください。** — *hyōshiki ni chūi shite kudasai* — "Please pay attention to the road signs."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひょうしき** (*hyōshiki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hyō*, "signpost" (Middle Chinese *pjiew*) and *shiki*, "know" (*syik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
