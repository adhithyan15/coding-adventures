## You'll want to know: a patrulha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C293-PATRULHA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a patrulha** — "the patrol".

> Uma patrulha passa pela rua todas as noites.
>
> *A patrol goes down the street every night.*

*Um carro de patrulha* — a patrol car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patrulha* was borrowed from French *patrouille* "a patrol" (EtymDB).
