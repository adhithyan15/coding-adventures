# かならず (kanarazu) — without fail; certainly

## You'll want to know: かならず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C321-ZETTAI321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かならず** — *kanarazu* — "without fail; certainly".

> **やくそくを したから、かならず いきます。** — *yakusoku o shita kara, kanarazu ikimasu* — "I promised, so I will go without fail."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かならず** (*kanarazu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
