Russian, chapter 126: Last, Famous, Important, Interesting, Boring.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
последний (poslédniy) — last.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for quiet, then the Russian for noisy.

You'll want to know: последний.
последний (poslédniy) — "last".

The word, taken apart.
последний (poslédniy) comes from Old Russian последь, "afterwards", which goes with след, "a track" (Vasmer): the last is the one at the back of the track.

Lesson 2 of 5.
известный (izvéstnyy) — famous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for noisy, then the Russian for last.

You'll want to know: известный.
известный (izvéstnyy) — "famous".

The word, taken apart.
известный (izvéstnyy) is built on известие, "news", из- with весть, "a message", from ведать, "to know" (Vasmer): "known". Vasmer compares Sanskrit vitti-, "knowledge".

Lesson 5 of 5.
скучный (skúchnyy) — boring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for important, then the Russian for interesting.

You'll want to know: скучный.
скучный (skúchnyy) — "boring".

The word, taken apart.
скучный (skúchnyy) is скука, "boredom", with -ный. Vasmer will not separate скука from докучать, "to pester", but rejects the proposed outside links, so its origin is open.
