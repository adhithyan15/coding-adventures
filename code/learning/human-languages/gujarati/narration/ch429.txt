Gujarati, chapter 429: Safes and Currency.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
તિજોરી (tijorī) — a safe; a strongbox.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a moneylender, then the Gujarati for to swindle.

You'll want to know: તિજોરી.
તિજોરી — tijorī — "a safe; a strongbox".
દુકાનદાર રોકડ તિજોરીમાં રાખે છે. dukāndār rokaḍ tijorīmā̃ rākhe che. The shopkeeper keeps the cash in a safe.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
તિજોરી (tijorī) is usually traced to Portuguese tesouro, "a treasure, a treasury": the place where the money is locked away.

Lesson 2 of 5.
નાણાં (nāṇā̃) — money; funds.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to swindle, then the Gujarati for a safe.

You'll want to know: નાણાં.
નાણાં — nāṇā̃ — "money; funds".
દાદાએ નવી શાળા માટે નાણાં આપ્યાં. dādāe navī śāḷā māṭe nāṇā̃ āpyā̃. Grandfather gave funds for a new school.
A neuter noun, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
નાણાં (nāṇā̃) goes back to Sanskrit nāṇaka, "a coin"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a coin, money" (CDIAL 7050). The word is plural.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ચલણ (calaṇ) — a currency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a safe, then the Gujarati for funds.

You'll want to know: ચલણ.
ચલણ — calaṇ — "a currency".
પરદેશમાં ત્યાંનું ચલણ વાપરવું પડે છે. pardeśmā̃ tyā̃nũ calaṇ vāparvũ paḍe che. Abroad you have to use the local currency.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચલણ (calaṇ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit calana, "moving, going"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "conduct; currency" (CDIAL 4716): money that goes round.

Lesson 4 of 5.
લૂંટાવું (lū̃ṭāvũ) — to be robbed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for funds, then the Gujarati for a currency.

You'll want to know: લૂંટાવું.
લૂંટાવું — lū̃ṭāvũ — "to be robbed".
રાત્રે રસ્તામાં વેપારી લૂંટાયો. rātre rastāmā̃ vepārī lū̃ṭāyo. The merchant was robbed on the road at night.
Past (he …): તે વેપારી લૂંટાયો, te vepārī lū̃ṭāyo.

The word, taken apart.
લૂંટાવું (lū̃ṭāvũ) is the intransitive partner of લૂંટવું, "to rob", which goes back to luṭṭati, "plunders", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 11078): who robs, લૂંટવું; who is robbed, લૂંટાવું.

Lesson 5 of 5.
પરવડવું (parvaḍvũ) — to be affordable; to be within one's means.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a currency, then the Gujarati for to be robbed.

You'll want to know: પરવડવું.
પરવડવું — parvaḍvũ — "to be affordable; to be within one's means".
આટલી મોંઘી ગાડી મને નહીં પરવડે. āṭlī mõghī gāḍī mane nahī̃ paravḍe. I will not be able to afford such an expensive car.
Past (it …): તે ભાડું તેને પરવડ્યું, te bhāḍũ tene parvaḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
પરવડવું (parvaḍvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit paripatati, "runs about"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be suitable" (CDIAL 7841): what suits your purse.
