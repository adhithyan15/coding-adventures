## You'll want to know: संकेतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C411-RATHYA411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संकेतः** — *saṅketaḥ* — "a signal; a sign".

> आरक्षकः हस्तेन संकेतं ददाति।
>
> *ārakṣakaḥ hastena saṅketaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The policeman gives a signal with his hand.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकेतः** (*saṅketaḥ*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √cit, "to perceive"; MW gives *saṃketa* as an agreement or an appointment, and a sign or signal agreed on is that sense (MW).
