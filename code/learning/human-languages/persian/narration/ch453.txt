Persian, chapter 453: Police and lost things.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پاسگاه (pâsgâh) — a police post; a guard post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a payment slip, then the Persian for tax.

You'll want to know: پاسگاه.
پاسگاه (pâsgâh) — "a police post; a guard post".
در جاده یک پاسگاه پلیس بود. Dar jâdde yek pâsgâh-e polis bud. There was a police post on the road.
pâs 'watch, guard' + gâh 'place'.

The word, taken apart.
pâsgâh is a native compound of pâs 'watch, guard', the word behind pâsbân 'guard', and gâh 'place'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
گمشده (gomshode) — lost, missing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for tax, then the Persian for a police post.

You'll want to know: گمشده.
گمشده (gomshode) — "lost, missing".
کیف گمشده من را در تاکسی پیدا کردند. Kif-e gomshode-ye man râ dar tâksi peydâ kardand. They found my lost bag in the taxi.
Ashyâ-ye gomshode — lost property.

The word, taken apart.
gomshode is built from native gom 'lost', which EtymDB lists, and shode, the past participle of shodan 'to become'.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a police post, then the Persian for lost.

You'll want to know: کیف پول.
کیف پول (kif-e pul) — "a wallet, a purse".
کیف پولم را در اتوبوس گم کردم. Kif-e pulam râ dar otobus gom kardam. I lost my wallet on the bus.
kif 'bag' + pul 'money'.

The word, taken apart.
kif-e pul is built from kif 'bag', whose origin is not settled, and pul 'money', which EtymDB glosses 'change, small money'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پلیس راه (polis-e râh) — the traffic police, the highway police.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for lost, then the Persian for a wallet.

You'll want to know: پلیس راه.
پلیس راه (polis-e râh) — "the traffic police, the highway police".
پلیس راه (polis-e râh) ماشین ما را نگه داشت. Polis-e râh mâshin-e mâ râ negah dâsht. The highway police stopped our car.
Inside towns the traffic police is polis-e râhnamâyi va rânandegi.

The word, taken apart.
polis-e râh is built from polis, a loan usually traced to French police, and native râh 'road', from Middle Persian rāh (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
شماره گرفتن (shomâre gereftan) — to ring a number, to dial.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a wallet, then the Persian for the traffic police.

You'll want to know: شماره گرفتن.
شماره گرفتن (shomâre gereftan) — "to ring a number, to dial".
شماره پلیس را گرفتم. Shomâre-ye polis râ gereftam. I dialled the police number.
shomâre 'number' + gereftan 'to take'.

The word, taken apart.
shomâre gereftan is built from shomâre 'number', inherited from Middle Persian ōšmārag 'reckoning' (EtymDB), and the native verb gereftan 'to take'.
