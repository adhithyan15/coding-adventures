## You'll want to know: भूल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C489-LAS489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भूल** — *bhūl* — "anaesthesia".

> शस्त्रक्रियेआधी त्याला भूल दिली.
>
> *śastrakriyeādhī tyālā bhūl dilī.*
>
> He was given anaesthesia before the operation.

Feminine.

भूल देणे, to anaesthetise. The same word means a slip of memory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भूल** (*bhūl*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *bhulṇẽ*, "errs, forgets", under the reconstructed *\*bhull* (CDIAL 9538); भूल is the noun, and modern Marathi gave it the sense "anaesthesia".
