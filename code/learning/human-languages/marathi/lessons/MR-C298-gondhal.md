## You'll want to know: गोंधळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C298-GONDHAL298-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गोंधळ** — *gondhaḷ* — "commotion; confusion".

> वीज गेल्यावर सभागृहात एकच गोंधळ झाला.
>
> *vīj gelyāvar sabhāgṛhāt ekac gondhaḷ jhālā.*
>
> When the power went out, the hall was in total commotion.

Masculine.

गोंधळ होणे, a commotion breaks out; गोंधळात पडणे, to be confused.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोंधळ** (*gondhaḷ*) is the Marathi word for an uproar or a muddle; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
