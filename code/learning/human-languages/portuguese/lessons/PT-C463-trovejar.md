## You'll want to know: trovejar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C463-TROVOADA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**trovejar** — "to thunder".

> Começou a trovejar quando saímos de casa.
>
> *It started to thunder when we left home.*

Like *chover*, used without a subject: *troveja*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trovejar* is built on *trovão* 'thunder', which is usually traced to Latin *tonitrus* 'thunder' (Lewis & Short).
