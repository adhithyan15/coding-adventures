Lesson 1 of 5.
ഗവേഷണം (gavēṣaṇaṁ) — research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to make headway, then the Malayalam for a system.

You'll want to know: ഗവേഷണം.
ഗവേഷണം — gavēṣaṇaṁ — "research".
അവൾ കടലിലെ മീനുകളെക്കുറിച്ച് ഗവേഷണം നടത്തുന്നു. avaḷ kaṭalile mīnukaḷekkuṟiccŭ gavēṣaṇaṁ naṭattunnu. She is doing research on the fish of the sea.
To do research is ഗവേഷണം (gavēṣaṇaṁ) നടത്തുക.

The word, taken apart.
ഗവേഷണം (gavēṣaṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit gaveṣaṇa, "desiring ardently", the noun of gaveṣ, "to seek, to search for" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams files the verb under go, "a cow", which is often read as a search for cattle; the research sense is modern.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ഗവേഷകൻ (gavēṣakan) — a researcher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a system, then the Malayalam for research.

You'll want to know: ഗവേഷകൻ.
ഗവേഷകൻ — gavēṣakan — "a researcher".
ആ ഗവേഷകൻ പത്ത് വർഷം കാട്ടിൽ ജീവിച്ചു. ā gavēṣakan pattŭ varṣaṁ kāṭṭil jīviccu. That researcher lived in the forest for ten years.
Plural: ഗവേഷകർ, gavēṣakar.
Plural: ഗവേഷകർ.

The word, taken apart.
ഗവേഷകൻ (gavēṣakan) is built on the same Sanskrit word as ഗവേഷണം (gavēṣaṇaṁ), gaveṣaṇa, "searching" (Monier-Williams), with the agent ending -കൻ, "one who".

Lesson 3 of 5.
പ്രബന്ധം (prabandhaṁ) — a thesis; a paper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for research, then the Malayalam for a researcher.

You'll want to know: പ്രബന്ധം.
പ്രബന്ധം — prabandhaṁ — "a thesis; a paper".
അവൻ തന്റെ പ്രബന്ധം സർവകലാശാലയിൽ സമർപ്പിച്ചു. avan tanṟe prabandhaṁ sarvakalāśālayil samarppiccu. He submitted his thesis to the university.
A research paper read at a meeting is also a പ്രബന്ധം (prabandhaṁ).

The word, taken apart.
പ്രബന്ധം (prabandhaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit prabandha, "a band, a connexion" (CDIAL 8695), and in later Sanskrit a connected composition (Monier-Williams).
