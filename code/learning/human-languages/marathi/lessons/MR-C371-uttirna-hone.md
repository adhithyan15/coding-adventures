## You'll want to know: उत्तीर्ण होणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C371-PARIKSHA371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तीर्ण होणे** — *uttīrṇa hoṇe* — "to pass (an exam)".

> अरुण पहिल्या प्रयत्नात परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण झाला.
>
> *aruṇ pahilyā prayatnāt parīkṣā uttīrṇa jhālā.*
>
> Arun passed the exam at the first attempt.

Past (he …): तो उत्तीर्ण झाला, *to uttīrṇa jhālā*.

Formal; what is passed is the object, with no ending: परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण होणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तीर्ण होणे** (*uttīrṇa hoṇe*) joins the tatsama उत्तीर्ण, Sanskrit *uttīrṇa*, "crossed over, landed" (Monier-Williams), to होणे, "to become": to get across.
