Marathi, chapter 398: At the Ticket Office.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तिकीटघर (tikīṭghar) — a ticket office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be stalled, then the Marathi for to set off.

You'll want to know: तिकीटघर.
तिकीटघर — tikīṭghar — "a ticket office".
तिकीटघरासमोर मोठी रांग होती. tikīṭgharāsamor moṭhī rāṅg hotī. There was a long queue in front of the ticket office.
Neuter.
घर at the end of a compound is a room or building for one job: तिकीटघर (tikīṭghar), a ticket house.

The word, taken apart.
तिकीटघर (tikīṭghar) is a Marathi compound of तिकीट, a loan from English ticket, and घर, "house", inherited from Sanskrit ghara (CDIAL 4428).

Lesson 2 of 5.
पास (pās) — a travel pass; a season ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: पास.
पास — pās — "a travel pass; a season ticket".
अरुणकडे बसचा महिन्याचा पास आहे. aruṇkaḍe bascā mahinyācā pās āhe. Arun has a monthly bus pass.
Masculine.
A pass lasts a week, a month or a year: आठवड्याचा पास (pās), a weekly pass.

The word, taken apart.
पास (pās) is a loan from English pass, the card that lets you travel again and again for one price.

Lesson 3 of 5.
परतावा (partāvā) — a refund.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a ticket office, then the Marathi for a travel pass.

You'll want to know: परतावा.
परतावा — partāvā — "a refund".
मला तिकिटाचा पूर्ण परतावा मिळाला. malā tikiṭācā pūrṇa partāvā miḷālā. I got a full refund for the ticket.
Masculine.
Built on परत, back: a refund is money that comes back to you.

The word, taken apart.
परतावा (partāvā) is a Marathi derivation built on परत, "back, again", from the family of Sanskrit parivartate, "turns round", where CDIAL lists Marathi paratṇẽ, "to return" (CDIAL 7872).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गंतव्य (gantavya) — a destination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a travel pass, then the Marathi for a refund.

You'll want to know: गंतव्य.
गंतव्य — gantavya — "a destination".
आमचं गंतव्य अजून खूप दूर आहे. āmcaṁ gantavya ajūn khūp dūr āhe. Our destination is still very far away.
Neuter.
A formal word; in speech people say जिथे जायचं आहे ते ठिकाण.

The word, taken apart.
गंतव्य (gantavya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit gantavya, "to be gone to", a form of the verb gam, "to go" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
राखून ठेवणे (rākhūn ṭhevṇe) — to set aside; to keep back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a refund, then the Marathi for a destination.

You'll want to know: राखून ठेवणे.
राखून ठेवणे — rākhūn ṭhevṇe — "to set aside; to keep back".
मी तुझ्यासाठी एक जागा राखून ठेवली. mī tujhyāsāṭhī ek jāgā rākhūn ṭhevlī. I kept a seat aside for you.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने राखून ठेवलं, tyāne rākhūn ṭhevlaṁ.
राखणे is to guard or keep; with ठेवणे it means to hold something back for later. The verb agrees with the thing kept: जागा राखून ठेवली.

The word, taken apart.
राखून ठेवणे (rākhūn ṭhevṇe) joins राखून, the -ऊन form of राखणे, "to keep, to guard", inherited from Sanskrit rakṣati, "guards" (CDIAL 10547), to ठेवणे, "to put, to keep".
