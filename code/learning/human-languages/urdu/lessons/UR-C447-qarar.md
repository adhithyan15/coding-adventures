## You'll want to know: قرار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C447-CALM447-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قرار** — *qarār* — "composure; rest (of the mind)".

> بیٹے کی خبر سن کر ماں کو قرار آیا۔
>
> *beṭe kī khabar sun kar māṅ ko qarār āyā.*
>
> Hearing news of her son, the mother was at peace.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قرار** (*qarār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qarār*, "settling, rest".
