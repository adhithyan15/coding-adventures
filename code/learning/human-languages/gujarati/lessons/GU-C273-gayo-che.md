# ગયો છે, ગયો હતો (gayo che, gayo hato) — has gone, had gone

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-GAYO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-AAVYO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATU-HATA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "he went" and "she came". (*te gayo*, *te āvī*.) Then "he was". (*te hato*.) Put a past and a copula side by side, and a new past appears.

## Grammar Lens: has gone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C273-PERFECT-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-BAJAR-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01, GU-LEX-C94-TIME94-05, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-YUN-01] -->

> **અરુણ બજાર ગયો છે.**
>
> *Aruṇ bajār gayo che.* — "Arun has gone to the market."

**ગયો** says he went. **ગયો છે** says he went and is still away: the going matters now. The **છે** follows the person, as in the present: **હું હમણાં આવ્યો છું** (*hũ hamṇā̃ āvyo chũ*), "I have come this moment"; **અમે આવ્યા છીએ** (*ame āvyā chīe*), "we have come".

A verb with an object keeps its doer form and follows the thing: **મેં કામ કર્યું છે** (*mẽ kām karyũ che*), "I have done the work". There the copula is **છે**, whoever the doer.

## You'll want to know: had gone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C273-PLUPERFECT-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-02, GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-03, GU-LEX-C206-ARRIVE206-01, GU-LEX-C204-TIMES204-04] -->

> **હું સ્ટેશન પહોંચ્યો, પણ ટ્રેન ઊપડી ગઈ હતી.**
>
> *hũ sṭeśan pahõcyo, paṇ ṭren ūpaḍī gaī hatī.* — "I reached the station, but the train had left."

**હતી** puts the leaving further back, before another past. It follows the way **હતો**, **હતી**, **હતું**, **હતા** do:

| | has (now) | had (before) |
|---|---|---|
| he went | **તે ગયો છે** | **તે ગયો હતો** |
| she went | **તે ગઈ છે** | **તે ગઈ હતી** |
| I did the work | **મેં કામ કર્યું છે** | **મેં કામ કર્યું હતું** |
| Mira drank tea | **મીરાએ ચા પીધી છે** | **મીરાએ ચા પીધી હતી** |
