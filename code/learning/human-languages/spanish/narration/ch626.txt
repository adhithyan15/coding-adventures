Spanish, chapter 626: En el tribunal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la sentencia — the court ruling, the judgment; the sentence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to report, then the Spanish for the report.

You'll want to know: la sentencia.
la sentencia — "the court ruling, the judgment; the sentence".
La sentencia sale mañana. The ruling comes out tomorrow.
A court decision; dictar sentencia — to pass judgment. A sentence in grammar is la oración or la frase.

The word, taken apart.
sentencia is a learned borrowing from Latin sententia 'way of thinking, opinion, verdict', built on sentire 'to feel, to think' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
