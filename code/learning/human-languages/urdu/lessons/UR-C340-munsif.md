## You'll want to know: منصف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C340-FAISLA340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منصف** — *munsif* — "an arbiter; a fair judge".

> اچھا منصف دونوں فریقوں کی بات سنتا ہے۔
>
> *achchhā munsif donoṅ farīqoṅ kī bāt suntā hai.*
>
> A good arbiter listens to both sides.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منصف** (*munsif*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *munṣif*, "one who is fair", of the same family as **انصاف**, "justice".
