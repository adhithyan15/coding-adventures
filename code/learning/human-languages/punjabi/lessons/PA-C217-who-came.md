# ਕੌਣ ਆਇਆ? — who came?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-Q-KAUN-01, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217, PA-LEX-AURAT-217] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *who?*, **ਕੌਣ**. Then *the woman came yesterday*.

## Grammar Lens: the one who came decides
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AGREE-PERSON-217]; assesses=[PA-Q-KAUN-01, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-05, PA-LEX-AURAT-217, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AU-217] -->

Asking, you do not know who came, so you use the plain **ਆਇਆ**. The answer
fits the person:

> **ਕੌਣ ਆਇਆ?** — *kauṇ āiā?* — Who came?

> **ਆਦਮੀ ਆਇਆ।** — *ādmī āiā.* — A man came.

> **ਔਰਤ ਆਈ।** — *aurat āī.* — A woman came.

The went forms work the same way: **ਆਦਮੀ ਗਿਆ**, **ਔਰਤ ਗਈ**.
