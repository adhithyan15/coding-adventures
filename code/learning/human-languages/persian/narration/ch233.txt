Persian, chapter 233: Maybe, Definitely, Of course, Probably, Very.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شاید (shâyad) — maybe, perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a landing, then the Persian for a railing.

You'll want to know: شاید.
شاید — shâyad — "maybe, perhaps".

The word, taken apart.
shâyad is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian šāyēd, "one can, it is possible" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
خیلی (kheyli) — very, a lot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for of course, then the Persian for probably.

You'll want to know: خیلی.
خیلی — kheyli — "very, a lot".

The word, taken apart.
kheyli is usually traced to Arabic ḫayl, "horses, a troop" (root ḫ-y-l, Lane), with the ending -i: "a troop of", so "very many". That account is the usual one, but it is not settled.
