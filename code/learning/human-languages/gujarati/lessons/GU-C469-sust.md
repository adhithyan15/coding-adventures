## You'll want to know: સુસ્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C469-TARANGI469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સુસ્ત** — *sust* — "sluggish".

> ગરમીમાં બધા સુસ્ત થઈ જાય છે.
>
> *garmīmā̃ badhā sust thaī jāy che.*
>
> In the heat everyone becomes sluggish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સુસ્ત** (*sust*) is usually traced to Persian *sust*, "slack, weak".
