## You'll want to know: ड्राइवर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C218-RAIL218-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ड्राइवर** — *ḍrāivar* — "a driver".

> ड्राइवर दिनूगै आवै है।
>
> *ḍrāivar dinūgai āvai hai.*
>
> The driver comes early in the morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ड्राइवर** (*ḍrāivar*) is borrowed from English *driver*.
