## You'll want to know: vomitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C484-ENXAQUECA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vomitar** — "to vomit, to be sick".

> O miúdo vomitou no carro.
>
> *The kid was sick in the car.*

Everyday and neutral.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vomitar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vomitāre* "to vomit" (EtymDB), from *vomere*.
