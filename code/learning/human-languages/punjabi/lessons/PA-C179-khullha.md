# ਬਾਗ਼ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ — one open status

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-TIME-SAVERE-DAYPART-01, PA-LEX-C138-THINGS138-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read **ਬਾਗ਼**. Then say **ਸਵੇਰੇ**.

## Grammar Lens: one open status
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-STATUS-KHULLHA-OPEN-01]; assesses=[PA-TIME-SAVERE-DAYPART-01, PA-LEX-C138-THINGS138-02] -->

> **ਬਾਗ਼ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ** — *bāġ savere khullhā hai* — the garden is open in the morning

Here **ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ** gives the open status. Keep the whole model together.
