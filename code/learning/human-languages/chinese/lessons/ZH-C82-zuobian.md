## You'll want to know: zuǒbian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C82-SIDES82-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zuǒbian* — "the left; on the left".

> *zuǒbian shì wǒ de fángjiān* — My room is on the left.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuǒbian* is built from zuǒ 'left' and bian 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsaX* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
