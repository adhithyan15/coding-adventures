## You'll want to know: مستشار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C518-MIHNA518-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مستشار** — *mustashār* — "an adviser, a consultant".

> يعمل خالي مستشارا في شركة كبيرة.
>
> *yaʿmalu khālī mustashāran fī sharika kabīra.*
>
> My uncle works as an adviser in a big company.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستشار** (*mustashār*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-w-r*, "to point out; to take counsel" (Lane). Mustashār is the one whose counsel is sought (Form X).
