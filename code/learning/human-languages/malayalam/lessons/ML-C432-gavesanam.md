# ഗവേഷണം (gavēṣaṇaṁ) — research

## You'll want to know: ഗവേഷണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C432-GAVESANAM432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗവേഷണം** — *gavēṣaṇaṁ* — "research".

> അവൾ കടലിലെ മീനുകളെക്കുറിച്ച് ഗവേഷണം നടത്തുന്നു.
>
> *avaḷ kaṭalile mīnukaḷekkuṟiccŭ gavēṣaṇaṁ naṭattunnu.*
>
> She is doing research on the fish of the sea.

To do research is ഗവേഷണം നടത്തുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗവേഷണം** (*gavēṣaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gaveṣaṇa*, "desiring ardently", the noun of *gaveṣ*, "to seek, to search for" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams files the verb under *go*, "a cow", which is often read as a search for cattle; the research sense is modern.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
