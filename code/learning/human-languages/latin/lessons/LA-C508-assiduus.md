## You'll want to know: assiduus, assidua, assiduum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C508-IGNA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**assiduus, assidua, assiduum** — "diligent, unremitting".

> Discipulus assiduus semper in scholā est.
>
> *The diligent pupil is always at school.*

From *assideō* — to sit beside: someone who stays at the task. English *assiduous*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**assiduus** is built on *assideō* "to sit beside", from *ad-* and *sedeō* "to sit" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *assiduous* comes from it.
