## You'll want to know: गृहस्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C501-SAMBANDHA501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गृहस्थः** — *gṛhasthaḥ* — "a householder".

> गृहस्थः कुटुम्बं पालयति।
>
> *gṛhasthaḥ kuṭumbaṃ pālayati.*
>
> The householder takes care of the family.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गृहस्थः** (*gṛhasthaḥ*) is built from **गृहम्**, "a house", and *stha*, "staying", from the root √sthā of **तिष्ठति**; MW gives "a householder", a Brāhman in the second stage of his life (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
