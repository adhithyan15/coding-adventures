# ముప్పై (muppai) — thirty (and the tens above it)

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: what did *Bhāratadēśaṁ* mean?

## You'll want to know: ముప్పై
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C77-FROM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ముప్పై** (*muppai*) — "thirty".

Your counting stopped at ఇరవై, twenty. The tens above it are built the same way, from the units you already know:

ముప్పై 30, **నలభై** 40, **యాభై** 50, **అరవై** 60, **డెబ్బై** 70, **ఎనభై** 80, **తొంభై** 90, **వంద** 100. Every one but వంద ends in the same **-ై** twenty does.

## Grammar Lens: an age, at last
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C77-FROM-05]; assesses=[] -->

You have long been able to ask **నీ వయసెంత?** and unable to answer it, because the numbers stopped at twenty and no word for a year existed. With the tens and with సంవత్సరం, the answer is now sayable: **నాకు ముప్పై సంవత్సరాలు** — "I am thirty", literally "to me, thirty years". Note the dative నాకు: an age is something that has arrived at you, like every other Telugu experience.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Numbers past twenty: an age, a price, a year.
