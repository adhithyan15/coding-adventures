## You'll want to know: derivar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C350-ORIGEM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**derivar** — "to derive, to come from".

> Muitas palavras portuguesas derivam do latim.
>
> *Many Portuguese words come from Latin.*

*Derivar de* — to come from.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*derivar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dērīvāre* "to lead or draw off a liquid" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *dē* and *rīvus* "a stream".
