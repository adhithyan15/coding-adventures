## HL-C142 — Japanese tsu closes the full-size tsu gap

After hiragana **め** landed, full-sized hiragana **つ** led the measured queue
at **1 affected realization**.

Sirgazil's CC0 Wikimedia Commons animation contains 24 frames over 2.4
seconds. It writes the character in one uninterrupted run: begin at the upper
left, sweep right across the high arch, curve down around the outer right side,
and return left along the broad lower curve. The learner path is fitted to the
bundled Noto Sans JP subset and keeps full-sized **つ** distinct from small
**っ**.

This closes the final **つ** realization. The reranked measured queue now moves
to Kannada independent vowel **ಐ**, also at **1**.
