## You'll want to know: переезд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C307-RAZVOD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**переезд** (*pereyézd*) — "a move (to a new home)".

> Переезд занял целую неделю.
>
> *Pereyézd zányal tséluyu nedélyu.*
>
> The move took a whole week.

Also a level crossing. The verb is *переезжать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*переезд* is built on *переехать* "to move", from *пере-* "across" and *ехать* "to go by vehicle", inherited from Proto-Slavic *ěxati* "to travel" (EtymDB).
