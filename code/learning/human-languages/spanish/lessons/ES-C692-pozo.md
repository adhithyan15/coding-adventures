## You'll want to know: el pozo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C692-SEMBRAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el pozo** — "the well".

> Sacan agua del pozo.
>
> *They draw water from the well.*

*Un pozo de petróleo* — an oil well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pozo* is inherited from Latin *puteus* "a well, a pit" (EtymDB).
