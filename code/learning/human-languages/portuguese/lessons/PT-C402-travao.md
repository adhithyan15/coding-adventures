## You'll want to know: o travão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C402-VOLANTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o travão** — "the brake".

> O travão do meu carro faz um barulho estranho.
>
> *The brake on my car makes a strange noise.*

In Brazil: *o freio*. The verb is *travar*, to brake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*travão* is built on *travar* 'to hold back, to brake', which is usually traced to *trave* 'beam'; *trave* is inherited from Latin *trabs* 'beam' (EtymDB): a bar that holds something still.
