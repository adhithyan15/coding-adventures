## You'll want to know: скорость
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C408-SKOROST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**скорость** (*skórost*) — "speed".

> В городе можно ехать со скоростью до шестидесяти километров в час.
>
> *V górode mózhno yékhat so skórostyu do shestidesyatí kilométrov v chas.*
>
> In town you can drive at a speed of up to sixty kilometres an hour.

Feminine. *На большой скорости* — at high speed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*скорость* is built on *скорый* 'fast' with the ending *-ость*. *Скорый* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *skorŭ* 'fast' (EtymDB).
