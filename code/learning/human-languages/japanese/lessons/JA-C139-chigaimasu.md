## You'll want to know: ちがいます
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C139-CHIGAIMASU]; assesses=[] -->

**ちがいます** — *chigaimasu* — "it is different", and so, as an answer,
"no, that is not right". It is the polite form of the verb **ちがう**,
*chigau*, "to differ". When someone asks whether a thing is so and it is
not, this is the everyday answer.

> **これは おちゃですか。** — *kore wa ocha desu ka* — "Is this tea?"
> **いいえ、ちがいます。みずです。** — *iie, chigaimasu. mizu desu* — "No,
> it isn't. It is water."
