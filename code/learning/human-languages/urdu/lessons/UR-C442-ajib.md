## You'll want to know: عجیب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C442-SURPRISE442-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عجیب** — *ajīb* — "strange; odd".

> یہ بات بڑی عجیب ہے۔
>
> *yih bāt baṛī ajīb hai.*
>
> This is a very strange thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عجیب** (*ajīb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿajīb*, "wonderful, strange".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
