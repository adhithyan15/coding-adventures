Telugu, chapter 535: Teasing and Telling Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎగతాళి (egatāḷi) — mockery; ridicule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a compromise, then the Telugu for a rift.

You'll want to know: ఎగతాళి.
ఎగతాళి — egatāḷi — "mockery; ridicule".
కొత్త అబ్బాయిని తరగతిలో ఎగతాళి చేశారు. kotta abbāyini taragatilō egatāḷi cēśāru. They made fun of the new boy in class.
ఎగతాళి (egatāḷi) చేయు, to make fun of.

The word, taken apart.
ఎగతాళి (egatāḷi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 767 lists Telugu egatāḷi, "a joke, mockery", and marks Tamil ekattāḷi as taken from Telugu.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: వెక్కిరించు.
వెక్కిరించు — vekkiriñcu — "to mock; to make faces at".
తమ్ముడు నాలుక చూపించి నన్ను వెక్కిరించాడు. tammuḍu nāluka cūpiñci nannu vekkiriñcāḍu. My little brother stuck out his tongue and mocked me.
Past (I …): వెక్కిరించాను, vekkiriñcānu.
Mocking with faces or by copying someone's voice.

The word, taken apart.
వెక్కిరించు (vekkiriñcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 767 lists Telugu ekkirincu, "to make faces at, to mock", in the same article as ఎగతాళి (egatāḷi).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Telugu for mockery, then the Telugu for to mock.

You'll want to know: మందలించు.
మందలించు — mandaliñcu — "to reprimand; to tell off".
ఆలస్యంగా వచ్చినందుకు మేనేజరు అతన్ని మందలించారు. ālasyaṅgā vaccinanduku mēnējaru atanni mandaliñcāru. The manager told him off for coming late.
Past (I …): మందలించాను, mandaliñcānu.
Milder than తిట్టు: a firm word of correction.

The word, taken apart.
మందలించు (mandaliñcu) is a Telugu verb for a firm rebuke; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
నిలదీయు (niladīyu) — to call to account; to confront.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to mock, then the Telugu for to reprimand.

You'll want to know: నిలదీయు.
నిలదీయు — niladīyu — "to call to account; to confront".
డబ్బు గురించి గ్రామస్థులు అధికారిని నిలదీశారు. ḍabbu guriñci grāmasthulu adhikārini niladīśāru. The villagers confronted the official about the money.
Past (I …): నిలదీశాను, niladīśānu.
To stop someone and demand an answer.

The word, taken apart.
నిలదీయు (niladīyu) is a native compound: nilucu, "to stand, to stop" (DEDR 3675), and tīyu, "to draw, to pull" (DEDR 3407): to pull someone up short.

Lesson 5 of 5.
మోసగించు (mōsagiñcu) — to deceive; to cheat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to reprimand, then the Telugu for to call to account.

You'll want to know: మోసగించు.
మోసగించు — mōsagiñcu — "to deceive; to cheat".
ఆ దుకాణదారుడు ముసలివాళ్ళను మోసగించాడు. ā dukāṇadāruḍu musalivāḷḷanu mōsagiñcāḍu. That shopkeeper cheated old people.
Past (I …): మోసగించాను, mōsagiñcānu.
The noun is మోసం, deceit.

The word, taken apart.
మోసగించు (mōsagiñcu) is built on మోసం, "deceit", from Sanskrit moṣa, "robbery, theft" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -గించు.
