## You'll want to know: mensal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C276-QUINZENA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mensal** — "monthly".

> Pago uma renda mensal de quinhentos euros.
>
> *I pay a monthly rent of five hundred euros.*

Built on *mês*. *Semanal* — weekly; *anual* — yearly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mensal* is a learned word built on Latin *mēnsis* "a month" (Lewis & Short), with the adjective ending *-al*.
