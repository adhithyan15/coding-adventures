## You'll want to know: vaniteux
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C473-ORGUEIL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vaniteux** — "vain, conceited".

> Il est vaniteux et parle toujours de lui.
>
> *He is vain and always talks about himself.*

Feminine *vaniteuse*. The noun is *la vanité*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vaniteux* is built on *vanité*, a learned borrowing from Latin *vānitās* "emptiness, vanity", from *vānus* "empty" (Lewis & Short).
