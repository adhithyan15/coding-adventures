## You'll want to know: સિદ્ધિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C382-DHYEY382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સિદ્ધિ** — *siddhi* — "an achievement".

> જો તે આ હરીફાઈ જીતશે તો તે તેની સૌથી મોટી સિદ્ધિ હશે.
>
> *jo te ā harīphāī jītśe to te tenī sauthī moṭī siddhi haśe.*
>
> If she wins this contest, it will be her greatest achievement.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સિદ્ધિ** (*siddhi*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *siddhi*, "accomplishment, success" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
