## You'll want to know: نرخ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C413-EQTESAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نرخ** (*nerkh*) — "a rate".

> نرخ بیکاری کاهش یافت.
>
> *nerkh-e bikâri kâhesh yâft.*
>
> The unemployment rate fell.

*Nerkh-e bahre* — the interest rate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نرخ** "rate, price" is a Persian word whose origin is not settled; EtymDB does not list it.
