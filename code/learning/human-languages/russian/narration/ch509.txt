Russian, chapter 509: Goals and Goalkeepers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ворота (voróta) — gates; the goal (in football).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to cheer on, then the Russian for to rejoice.

You'll want to know: ворота.
ворота (voróta) — "gates; the goal (in football)".
Мяч пролетел мимо ворот. Myach proletél mímo vorót. The ball flew past the goal.
Plural only; genitive ворот.

The word, taken apart.
ворота (voróta) is inherited from Proto-Slavic vorta (EtymDB), with the native East Slavic -оро-.

Lesson 2 of 5.
вратарь (vratár) — a goalkeeper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to rejoice, then the Russian for gates.

You'll want to know: вратарь.
вратарь (vratár) — "a goalkeeper".
Вратарь поймал мяч. Vratár poymál myach. The goalkeeper caught the ball.
Masculine; genitive вратаря.

The word, taken apart.
вратарь (vratár) is built on врата with the ending -арь (EtymDB); врата was borrowed from Church Slavonic, the -ра- shape of Proto-Slavic vorta, which gave native ворота (voróta) 'gates' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
нападающий (napadáyushchiy) — a forward, a striker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for gates, then the Russian for a goalkeeper.

You'll want to know: нападающий.
нападающий (napadáyushchiy) — "a forward, a striker".
Наш нападающий — самый быстрый в команде. Nash napadáyushchiy — sámyy býstryy v kománde. Our striker is the fastest in the team.
Declined like an adjective. The present participle of нападать — to attack.

The word, taken apart.
нападающий (napadáyushchiy) is the present participle of нападать 'to attack', built on на- and падать 'to fall'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
забивать (zabivát) — to score (a goal); to hammer in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a forward, then the Russian for a goal.

You'll want to know: забивать.
забивать (zabivát) — "to score (a goal); to hammer in".
Он забивает почти в каждом матче. On zabiváyet pochtí v kázhdom mátche. He scores in almost every match.
Imperfective; perfective забить (забью). Also to hammer in a nail.

The word, taken apart.
забивать (zabivát) is built on за- and the root of бить 'to strike'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
