Italian, chapter 327: La battaglia.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la battaglia — the battle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the wizard, then the Italian for the treasure.

You'll want to know: la battaglia.
la battaglia — "the battle".
La battaglia è durata tre giorni. The battle lasted three days.
Also a struggle: una battaglia contro la malattia.

The word, taken apart.
battaglia is inherited from Late Latin battālia (EtymDB), built on battuere "to beat".

You'll want to know: il cavaliere.
il cavaliere — "the knight; the rider".
Il cavaliere ha salvato la principessa. The knight saved the princess.
Also a gentleman, and a woman's dance partner.

The word, taken apart.
cavaliere came through Provençal cavalier (EtymDB) from Late Latin caballārius "horseman", built on caballus "horse".

Lesson 5 of 5.
conquistare — to conquer; to win over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the sword, then the Italian for the shield.

You'll want to know: conquistare.
conquistare — "to conquer; to win over".
Hanno conquistato la città in una notte. They conquered the city in one night.
Also in love: mi ha conquistato.

The word, taken apart.
conquistare is usually traced to Vulgar Latin conquīsītāre, from Latin conquīrere "to seek out", built on quaerere "to seek" (Lewis & Short).
