## You'll want to know: अनुमोदते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C356-VIJNAPANA356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमोदते** — *anumodate* — "approves of".

> अध्यक्षः योजनाम् अनुमोदते।
>
> *adhyakṣaḥ yojanām anumodate.*
>
> The president approves the plan.

Past (he …): सः योजनाम् अनुमोदितवान्, *saḥ yojanām anumoditavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमोदते** (*anumodate*) is built on *anu*, "after, along", and the root √mud, "to rejoice"; MW gives *anu-√mud* "to join in rejoicing" and "to express approval, to applaud" (MW).
