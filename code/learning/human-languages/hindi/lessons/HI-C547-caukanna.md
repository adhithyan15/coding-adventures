## You'll want to know: चौकन्ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C547-CHAUKANNA547-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चौकन्ना** — *caukannā* — "alert, watchful".

> जंगल में हिरन हमेशा चौकन्ना रहता है।
>
> *Jaṅgal meṁ hiran hameśā caukannā rahtā hai.*
>
> In the forest a deer is always on the alert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चौकन्ना** (*caukannā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *catuṣkarṇa*, "heard by four ears" (CDIAL 4630). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "vigilant, circumspect": all ears.
