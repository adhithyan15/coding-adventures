Portuguese, chapter 390: Culpado ou inocente.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
o julgamento — the trial; the judgement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the sanction, then the Portuguese for to fine.

You'll want to know: o julgamento.
o julgamento — "the trial; the judgement".
O julgamento começa na segunda-feira. The trial starts on Monday.
Ir a julgamento — to go on trial.

The word, taken apart.
julgamento is built on julgar "to judge" with -mento; julgar is inherited from Latin iūdicāre "to judge" (EtymDB), on iūdex "judge" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the blame, then the Portuguese for guilty.

You'll want to know: inocente.
inocente — "innocent".
Ele diz que está inocente. He says he is innocent.
Also naive: uma pergunta inocente.

The word, taken apart.
inocente is a learned borrowing from Latin innocēns "harmless, blameless", built from in- "not" and nocēre "to harm" (Lewis & Short).
