Malayalam, chapter 495: In the Hills.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കൊടുമുടി (koṭumuṭi) — a peak; a summit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to count and check, then the Malayalam for to sell off.

You'll want to know: കൊടുമുടി.
കൊടുമുടി — koṭumuṭi — "a peak; a summit".
മഞ്ഞുകാലത്ത് ആ കൊടുമുടിയിൽ മഞ്ഞ് വീഴും. maññukālattŭ ā koṭumuṭiyil maññŭ vīḻuṁ. In winter snow falls on that peak.
Also the peak of anything: the top of fame or of a career.

The word, taken apart.
കൊടുമുടി (koṭumuṭi) is a Malayalam compound whose second part is മുടി, the hair or crown of the head (DEDR 4921); the origin of the first part is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
മലഞ്ചെരിവ് (malañcerivŭ) — a hillside; a slope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to sell off, then the Malayalam for a peak.

You'll want to know: മലഞ്ചെരിവ്.
മലഞ്ചെരിവ് — malañcerivŭ — "a hillside; a slope".
മലഞ്ചെരിവിൽ ധാരാളം തേയിലച്ചെടികൾ ഉണ്ട്. malañcerivil dhārāḷaṁ tēyilacceṭikaḷ uṇṭŭ. There are many tea bushes on the hillside.
മല, a hill, plus ചെരിവ്, a slope; ചരിവ് and ചെരിവ് are two spellings of one word.

The word, taken apart.
മലഞ്ചെരിവ് (malañcerivŭ) is a native Dravidian compound of മല, a hill (DEDR 4742), and ചെരിവ്, a slope (DEDR 2360).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ചുരം (curaṁ) — a mountain pass; a ghat road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a peak, then the Malayalam for a hillside.

You'll want to know: ചുരം.
ചുരം — curaṁ — "a mountain pass; a ghat road".
ബസ് പതുക്കെ ചുരം കയറി. basŭ patukke curaṁ kayaṟi. The bus slowly climbed the ghat road.
The road that bends up through the hills; its hairpin bends are ഹെയർപിൻ വളവുകൾ.

The word, taken apart.
ചുരം (curaṁ) is native Dravidian, "a narrow and difficult path, a pass" (DEDR 2683).

Lesson 4 of 5.
പുൽമേട് (pulmēṭŭ) — a meadow; a grassland.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a hillside, then the Malayalam for a mountain pass.

You'll want to know: പുൽമേട്.
പുൽമേട് — pulmēṭŭ — "a meadow; a grassland".
മലമുകളിലെ പുൽമേട്ടിൽ പശുക്കൾ മേഞ്ഞു. malamukaḷile pulmēṭṭil paśukkaḷ mēññu. Cows grazed on the meadow on the hilltop.
പുല്ല്, grass, plus മേട്, high ground.

The word, taken apart.
പുൽമേട് (pulmēṭŭ) is a native Dravidian compound of പുല്ല്, grass (DEDR 4300), and മേട്, a rise or mound of land (DEDR 5058).

Lesson 5 of 5.
തണൽ (taṇal) — shade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a mountain pass, then the Malayalam for a meadow.

You'll want to know: തണൽ.
തണൽ — taṇal — "shade".
ഉച്ചയ്ക്ക് ഞങ്ങൾ ഒരു വലിയ മരത്തിന്റെ തണലിൽ ഇരുന്നു. uccaykkŭ ñaṅṅaḷ oru valiya marattinṟe taṇalil irunnu. At noon we sat in the shade of a big tree.
Shade is a kind of coolness: the word comes from the same root as തണുപ്പ്.

The word, taken apart.
തണൽ (taṇal) is native Dravidian, from the root of തണുപ്പ്, cold; DEDR lists it as shade, a shady spot (DEDR 3045).
