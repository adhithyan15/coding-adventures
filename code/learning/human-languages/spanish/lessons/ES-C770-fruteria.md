## You'll want to know: la frutería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C770-COMERCIOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la frutería** — "the greengrocer's, the fruit shop".

> Voy a la frutería de la esquina porque tiene fruta de temporada.
>
> *I go to the fruit shop on the corner because it has fruit in season.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frutería* is built on *fruta* with the ending *-ería* (EtymDB). *Fruta* comes from Late Latin *frūcta*, from *frūctus* 'produce, enjoyment'.
