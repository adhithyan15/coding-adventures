## You'll want to know: उमेद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C464-HIMMAT464-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उमेद** — *umed* — "zest; hope".

> आजोबा म्हातारे झाले तरी त्यांची उमेद कायम आहे.
>
> *ājobā mhātāre jhāle tarī tyāñcī umed kāyam āhe.*
>
> Grandfather has grown old, but his zest is still there.

Feminine.

उमेद हरणे, to lose heart; उमेदीचे दिवस, the years of youthful drive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उमेद** (*umed*) is a loan from Persian *umed*, "hope".
