Lesson 1 of 5.
abbonarsi — to subscribe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for soundtrack, then the Italian for trailer.

You'll want to know: abbonarsi.
abbonarsi — "to subscribe".
Mi sono abbonato a una rivista di cinema. I subscribed to a film magazine.
Abbonarsi a qualcosa; it takes essere.

The word, taken apart.
abbonarsi comes from French abonner "to subscribe", usually linked to borne "a limit"; the origin is not fully settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
il videogioco — video game.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: il videogioco.
il videogioco — "video game".
Mio figlio passa ore con i videogiochi. My son spends hours on video games.
The plural is i videogiochi.

The word, taken apart.
videogioco is built from Latin videō "I see" (Lewis & Short) and gioco "game", from Latin iocus (EtymDB).
