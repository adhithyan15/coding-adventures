## You'll want to know: إيجابي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C419-IJABI419-01]; assesses=[] -->

**إيجابي** — *ījābī* — "positive".

> كان للقرار أثر إيجابي.
>
> *kāna li-l-qarār athar ījābī.*
>
> The decision had a positive effect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إيجابي** (*ījābī*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-j-b*, "to be due, binding" (Lane). *Ījābī* is a modern adjective from *ījāb*, the Form IV verbal noun, affirming: positive.
