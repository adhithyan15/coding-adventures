## You'll want to know: みつける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C191-SHUT191-05]; assesses=[] -->

**みつける** — *mitsukeru* — "to find; to discover".

> **いちばで やすい やさいを みつけます。** — *ichiba de yasui yasai o mitsukemasu* — "I find cheap vegetables at the market."

Polite **みつけます**, *mitsukemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みつける** (*mitsukeru*) is native Japanese, built from *mi*, "seeing", and *tsukeru*, "to attach", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
