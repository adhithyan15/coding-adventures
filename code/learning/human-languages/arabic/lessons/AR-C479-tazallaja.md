## You'll want to know: تزلج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C479-TAZALLUJ479-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تزلج** — *tazallaja* — "to ski; to skate".

> تزلجنا على الثلج في الشتاء.
>
> *tazallajnā ʿalā ath-thalj fī ash-shitāʾ.*
>
> We skied on the snow in winter.

Present: يتزلج, *yatazallaj*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تزلج** (*tazallaja*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *z-l-j*, "to slip, to slide". It is Form V.
