# Chinese pre-A1 project mock — form 1 (learner paper)

This is an original, project-defined HSK precursor, **not an official HSK paper**.
The directions use English as the declared pre-A1 support language. Standard
Mandarin and simplified characters are assessed. Complete reading (5 minutes),
listening (5), and writing (10) continuously; speaking takes 5 minutes after
1 minute of preparation. A full mock is not a lesson: book lessons remain at
most five minutes. Each skill is worth 25 points; **15/25 in each skill and
60/100 overall** are required in one sitting. Attempt every section.

No dictionary, translator, pinyin input aid, character model, or answer bank.
The examiner holds the recording and delayed-recall card, not the learner.

## Reading — 5 minutes, 25 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->
Circle one meaning for each taught character or word. Each item is 5 points.
Pinyin appears only in item 3, where recognizing pinyin is the task; no
pinyin is printed above the assessed characters.

1. **你**: (a) you · (b) I · (c) good
2. **好**: (a) please · (b) good · (c) not
3. **nǐ hǎo**: (a) goodbye · (b) thank you · (c) hello
4. **再见**: (a) goodbye · (b) good morning · (c) name
5. **请**: (a) what · (b) please / after you · (c) I
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->

## Listening — 5 minutes, 25 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->
Listen to each short recording **twice**. There is a pause between plays and
items. Circle one answer; each item is 5 points. The recording script is held
only in the answer key.

6. Which taught syllable did you hear? (a) mā · (b) má · (c) mǎ
7. Which meaning did you hear? (a) hello · (b) goodbye · (c) thank you
8. You hear a farewell. Which reply fits? (a) 再见 · (b) 什么 · (c) 请
9. Which picture matches? (a) a person pointing to themself · (b) a person
   waving goodbye · (c) a person writing
10. You hear a courtesy word at a doorway. Which reply fits?
    (a) 谢谢 · (b) 什么 · (c) 我
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->

## Speaking — 5 minutes, 25 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->
The examiner speaks each prompt once; you may ask for **one slow repeat**.
Answer with one short turn (no longer than 15 seconds). Each item is 5 points.
You may not read a written answer script.

16. The examiner greets you. Greet back.
17. The examiner thanks you. Give the taught courtesy reply.
18. The examiner says in English, “Please say who you are.” Say **I am** and
    your own name in Mandarin; your name may remain in its own language.
19. The examiner shows a picture of someone leaving. Say the farewell.
20. The examiner says a taught third-tone syllable. Repeat its tone clearly.
