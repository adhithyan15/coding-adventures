## You'll want to know: विलम्बते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C371-VIKALPA371-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विलम्बते** — *vilambate* — "is late; delays".

> यदि भवान् विलम्बते, तर्हि अहं गमिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi bhavān vilambate, tarhi ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi.*
>
> If you are late, I will go.

Past (he …): सः विलम्बितवान्, *saḥ vilambitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विलम्बते** (*vilambate*) is built on *vi* and the root √lamb, "to hang"; MW gives *vi-√lamb* "to hang down" and "to linger, to delay, to tarry" (MW).
