## You'll want to know: రుణం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C405-ARTHIKA405-05]; assesses=[] -->

**రుణం** — *ruṇaṁ* — "a loan; a debt".

> రైతులు బ్యాంకు నుంచి రుణం తీసుకున్నారు.
>
> *raitulu byāṅku nuñci ruṇaṁ tīsukunnāru.*
>
> The farmers took a loan from the bank.

Plural: రుణాలు, *ruṇālu*.

Formal beside అప్పు: banks and governments give రుణాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రుణం** (*ruṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ṛṇa*, "anything due, a debt, money owed" (Monier-Williams).
