## You'll want to know: sitiō, sitīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C525-TUSS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sitiō, sitīre** — "to be thirsty".

> Post cursum valdē sitiō.
>
> *After running I am very thirsty.*

*sitiō, sitīre, sitīvī*, from *sitis* — thirst, already known.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sitiō** is built on *sitis* "thirst" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
