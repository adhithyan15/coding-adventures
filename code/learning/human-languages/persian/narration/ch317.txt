Persian, chapter 317: Chase and escape.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعقیب کردن (ta'qib kardan) — to chase, to pursue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pickpocket, then the Persian for a bag snatcher.

You'll want to know: تعقیب کردن.
تعقیب کردن (ta'qib kardan) — "to chase, to pursue".
پلیس ماشین دزد را تعقیب کرد. Polis mâshin-e dozd râ ta'qib kard. The police chased the thief's car.
Present ta'qib mikonad. In legal Persian also "to prosecute".

The word, taken apart.
ta'qib is borrowed from Arabic ta'qīb (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a following after". kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قایم شدن (qâyem shodan) — to hide (oneself).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a bag snatcher, then the Persian for to chase.

You'll want to know: قایم شدن.
قایم شدن (qâyem shodan) — "to hide (oneself)".
بچه ها پشت درخت قایم شدند. Bachche-hâ posht-e derakht qâyem shodand. The children hid behind the tree.
Conversational. qâyem kardan is "to hide something". Present qâyem mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
qâyem is usually traced to Arabic qā'im, "standing, firm" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word); in Persian it came to mean "tucked away". shodan "to become" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
گریختن (gorikhtan) — to flee, to escape (literary).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to chase, then the Persian for to hide.

You'll want to know: گریختن.
گریختن (gorikhtan) — "to flee, to escape (literary)".
دزد از پنجره گریخت. Dozd az panjere gorikht. The thief escaped through the window.
Literary; in speech farâr kardan. Present stem goriz-: migorizad.

The word, taken apart.
gorikhtan is a native Persian verb, with the present stem goriz-. EtymDB lists it without a parent.

ناپدید شدن (nâpadid shodan) — "to disappear, to vanish".
گربه ما دو روز ناپدید شد. Gorbe-ye mâ do ruz nâpadid shod. Our cat disappeared for two days.
nâ- "not" + padid "visible". Present nâpadid mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
nâpadid joins the native prefix nâ- "not" and padid "visible"; shodan "to become" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
لو رفتن (low raftan) — to be found out, to leak out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to flee, then the Persian for to disappear.

You'll want to know: لو رفتن.
لو رفتن (low raftan) — "to be found out, to leak out".
نقشه آنها زود لو رفت. Naqshe-ye ânhâ zud low raft. Their plan was soon found out.
Colloquial; the secret itself is the subject. Present low miravad.

The word, taken apart.
raftan "to go" is inherited Persian. The origin of low "revealed, exposed" is not settled; EtymDB lists no entry.
