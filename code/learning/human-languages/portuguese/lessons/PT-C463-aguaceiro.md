## You'll want to know: o aguaceiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C463-TROVOADA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o aguaceiro** — "the downpour, the heavy shower".

> Ao meio-dia caiu um aguaceiro.
>
> *At midday there was a downpour.*

Weather forecasts say *aguaceiros* for showers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aguaceiro* is built on *aguaça* 'lots of water' with *-eiro* (EtymDB); *água* is inherited from Latin *aqua*.
