## You'll want to know: interdiū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C295-NOCTU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**interdiū** — "by day, in the daytime".

> Interdiū in agrīs labōrābant, noctū dormiēbant.
>
> *By day they worked in the fields, at night they slept.*

*inter* + an old form of *diēs*: within the day. It pairs with *noctū*. Caesar and Livy use it; Cicero does not.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**interdiū** joins *inter*, "between, within", and an old form of *diēs*, "day" (EtymDB): within the daylight hours.
