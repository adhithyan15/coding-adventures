## You'll want to know: निवेशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C427-VASATI427-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निवेशः** — *niveśaḥ* — "an encampment; a halting place".

> सार्थस्य निवेशः नद्याः तीरे अस्ति।
>
> *sārthasya niveśaḥ nadyāḥ tīre asti.*
>
> The caravan's encampment is on the riverbank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निवेशः** (*niveśaḥ*) is built from *ni*, "down, in", and the root √viś, "to enter, to settle"; MW gives *niveśa* as settling in a place, encamping, halting, and a dwelling or camp (MW).
