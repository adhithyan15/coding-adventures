## You'll want to know: नीम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C188-PHUL188-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नीम** — *nīm* — "a neem tree".

> म्हारो नीम घणो बड़ो है।
>
> *mhāro nīm ghaṇo baṛo hai.*
>
> My neem tree is very big.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नीम** (*nīm*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nimba*, "the neem tree"; CDIAL lists Hindi *nīm* (CDIAL 7245). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
