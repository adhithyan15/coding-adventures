## You'll want to know: ಠಾಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C323-APARADHA323-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಠಾಣೆ** — *ṭhāṇe* — "a police station".

> ನಾನು ಠಾಣೆಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ದೂರು ಕೊಟ್ಟೆ.
>
> *nānu ṭhāṇege hōgi dūru koṭṭe.*
>
> I went to the police station and made a complaint.

In full it is ಪೊಲೀಸ್ ಠಾಣೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಠಾಣೆ** (*ṭhāṇe*) came through Hindustani *thānā*, "a post, a police station", which goes back to Sanskrit *sthāna*, "a place, an abode" (CDIAL 13753).
