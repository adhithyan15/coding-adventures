# ふったら (futtara) — if it rains

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C250-TA-SOUND-01, JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-02, JA-LEX-C44-NATURE44-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say, plainly, "it rained". (*ame ga futta*.) Then "I won't go", politely. (*ikimasen*.)

## You'll want to know: -たら, if
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C260-TARA-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C41-SCHOOL41-02, JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-01, JA-LEX-C53-ADJ53-04, JA-GRAMMAR-C258-FEELING-01] -->

> **あめが ふったら、いきません。**
>
> *ame ga futtara, ikimasen.* — "If it rains, I won't go."

Take the plain past and add **ら**: **ふった** becomes **ふったら** (*futtara*), "if it rains". The condition comes first; what follows it comes second, and that last verb says when.

> **じかんが あったら、えいがを みます。**
>
> *jikan ga attara, eiga o mimasu.* — "If I have time, I will see a film."

An i-adjective does the same from its **-かった**: **さむかった** gives **さむかったら** (*samukattara*).

> **さむかったら、うちに います。**
>
> *samukattara, uchi ni imasu.* — "If it is cold, I will stay home."

## You'll want to know: -たら, once
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C260-TARA-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-EKI, JA-LEX-C198-RIDE198-05, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KIPPU, JA-GRAMMAR-C251-FUTURE-01] -->

When the first thing is sure to happen, **-たら** is "once", "when":

> **えきに ついたら、きっぷを かいます。**
>
> *eki ni tsuitara, kippu o kaimasu.* — "Once I get to the station, I will buy a ticket."

> **うちに かえったら、ねます。**
>
> *uchi ni kaettara, nemasu.* — "When I get home, I will go to bed."
