Punjabi, chapter 213: Please Don't Get Up.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
Return to a familiar request — retrieve the familiar request ending in ਕਰੋ and ਨਾ by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: māf karo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰੋ, "excuse me". Then work with Aman: ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋ.

Grammar Lens.
Both requests end in ਕਰੋ, "do", said to someone politely. You also know ਨਾ, the short "no; don't", and two verbs for the body: ਬੈਠਣਾ, "to sit", and ਉੱਠਣਾ, "to get up".

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਨਾ.
[your turn — hear: to sit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਬੈਠਣਾ.
[your turn — hear: to get up]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉੱਠਣਾ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say excuse me, then don't. Keep both ready.


Lesson 2 of 6.
ਬੈਠੋ, ਉੱਠੋ — please sit, please get up.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰੋ. Then to sit and to get up.

Grammar Lens: stem + ੋ.
ਕਰੋ is the stem ਕਰ with the sign ੋ. Other stems that end in a consonant work the same way:
ਬੈਠ + ੋ becomes ਬੈਠੋ.
ਇੱਥੇ ਬੈਠੋ। — itthe baiṭho. — Please sit here.
ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜਲਦੀ ਉੱਠੋ। — savere jaldī uṭṭho. — Get up early in the morning.
ਕਿਤਾਬ ਪੜ੍ਹੋ। — kitāb paṛho. — Read the book.
It is the polite form, the one you say to ਤੁਸੀਂ.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: please sit there]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉੱਥੇ ਬੈਠੋ।.
[your turn — hear: please get up]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉੱਠੋ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਇੱਥੇ ਬੈਠੋ।]
Point to ੋ at the end of the request.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਬੈਠੋ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Please sit? (ਬੈਠੋ.) Please get up? (ਉੱਠੋ.)

Lesson 4 of 6.
ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ (nā uṭṭho) — please don't get up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say please get up. Then don't, ਨਾ. Then I don't drink tea.

Grammar Lens: ਨਾ for a request, ਨਹੀਂ for a statement.
A request takes ਨਾ, directly before it:
ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ। — nā uṭṭho. — Please don't get up.
ਉੱਥੇ ਨਾ ਬੈਠੋ। — utthe nā baiṭho. — Please don't sit there.
ਹੁਣ ਕੰਮ ਨਾ ਕਰੋ। — huṇ kamm nā karo. — Don't work now.
A statement keeps ਨਹੀਂ: ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ. So the short ਨਾ asks, and the long ਨਹੀਂ tells.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: please don't sit there]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉੱਥੇ ਨਾ ਬੈਠੋ।.
[your turn — hear: don't work now]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਹੁਣ ਕੰਮ ਨਾ ਕਰੋ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ (nā uṭṭho)।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ (nā uṭṭho) once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Don't get up? (ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ (nā uṭṭho).) I don't get up? (ਮੈਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਉੱਠਦਾ ਹਾਂ.)


Lesson 5 of 6.
ਅਜੇ (aje) — not yet; still.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say please don't get up, then now.

You'll want to know: ਅਜੇ.
ਅਜੇ — aje — "not yet; still".
ਮੈਂ ਅਜੇ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਾਂ। — maiṁ aje ghar vichch hāṁ. — I am still at home.
ਅਜੇ ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ। — aje nā uṭṭho. — Don't get up yet.

The word, taken apart.
ਅਜੇ (aje) is inherited from Sanskrit adyaiva, "even today": CDIAL lists Punjabi aje, "hitherto" (CDIAL 245). "Up to today" is where both of its senses come from: still, and with a no, not yet.

What you've built.
One more word for saying no and asking.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: aje]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — hear: I am still at home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਅਜੇ (aje) ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਾਂ।.
[your turn — hear: don't get up yet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਜੇ ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ। (aje nā uṭṭho.)
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਅਜੇ (aje) once from the model]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਅਜੇ (aje) mean? ("Not yet; still".) Say it once more.
