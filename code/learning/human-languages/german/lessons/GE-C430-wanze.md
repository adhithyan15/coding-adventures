## You'll want to know: die Wanze
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C430-MUECKE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Wanze** — "the bedbug; a hidden microphone".

> Im Bett waren Wanzen, und er hat schlecht geschlafen.
>
> *There were bedbugs in the bed, and he slept badly.*

Plural: *die Wanzen*. A hidden microphone is also *eine Wanze*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wanze* goes back to Old High German *wantlūs* 'wall louse' (EtymDB), from *Wand* 'wall' and *Laus* 'louse'.
