## You'll want to know: ਛਾਣਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C243-KAM243-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਾਣਨਾ** — *chhāṇnā* — "to sift".

> ਮੈਂ ਆਟਾ ਛਾਣਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ āṭā chhāṇdī hāṁ.*
>
> I sift the flour.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਟਾ ਛਾਣਿਆ, *maiṁ āṭā chhāṇiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਾਣਨਾ** (*chhāṇnā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kṣaṇati*, "is sifted, is strained"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chāṇnā*, "to sift, strain, choose" (CDIAL 3643).
