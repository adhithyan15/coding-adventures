## You'll want to know: bāsiō, bāsiāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C534-OSCU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**bāsiō, bāsiāre** — "to kiss".

> Avia nepōtēs bāsiat.
>
> *Grandmother kisses her grandchildren.*

*bāsiō, bāsiāre, bāsiāvī, bāsiātum*, from *bāsium*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**bāsiō** is built on *bāsium* "a kiss" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). French *baiser* comes from it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
