## You'll want to know: jiātíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C317-LOOKBACK317-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jiātíng* — "household; a family unit".

> *wǒmen shì yí ge kuàilè de jiātíng* — We're a happy family.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiātíng* is built from 家 'home' and *tíng* 'a courtyard', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kae* and *deng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a gate yard': the home and its yard.
