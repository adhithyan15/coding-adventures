French, chapter 105: Socks, Thirty, Forty, Fifty, Hundred.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
les chaussettes — socks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a coat, then the French for a skirt.

You'll want to know: les chaussettes.
les chaussettes — "socks".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
