## You'll want to know: el comercio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C630-ECONOMIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el comercio** — "the trade, the commerce; the shop".

> El comercio con Europa crece.
>
> *Trade with Europe is growing.*

*Un comercio* is also a shop: *los comercios del barrio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comercio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commercium* 'trade, dealings', built from *con* and *merx* 'goods' (EtymDB).
