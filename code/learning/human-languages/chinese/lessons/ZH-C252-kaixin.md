## You'll want to know: 开心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C252-FEEL252-01]; assesses=[] -->

**开心** — *kāixīn* — "happy; in high spirits".

> **今天我很开心** *jīntiān wǒ hěn kāixīn* — I'm in a really good mood today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开心** (*kāixīn*) is built from 开 'to open' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): an open heart.
