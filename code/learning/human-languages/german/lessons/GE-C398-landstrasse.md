## You'll want to know: die Landstraße
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C398-LANDSTRASSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Landstraße** — "the country road, the main road (outside towns)".

> Wir fahren lieber auf der Landstraße als auf der Autobahn.
>
> *We prefer to drive on the country road rather than on the motorway.*

Plural: *die Landstraßen*. Slower than the *Autobahn*, often with villages on the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Landstraße* is a compound of *Land* 'land, open country', inherited from Proto-Germanic *landą* (EtymDB), and *Straße* 'street', an early borrowing from Latin *(via) strāta* 'paved road' (Lewis & Short) that reached Middle High German as *strāze* (EtymDB).
