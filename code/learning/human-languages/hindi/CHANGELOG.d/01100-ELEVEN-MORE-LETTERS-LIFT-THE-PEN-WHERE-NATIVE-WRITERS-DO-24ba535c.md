## Eleven more letters lift the pen where native writers do

- The writing steps for अ (HI-S03), आ (HI-S04), ओ (HI-S129), झ (HI-S133),
  च (HI-S117), थ (HI-S126) and व (HI-S146) now lift the pen once less, the count most
  native writers use (HP Labs India's native-writer samples): twice for अ, three
  times for आ, four for ओ, twice for झ and once for च, थ and व. The
  run that ends at the stem climbs it and comes back down, instead of
  lifting before it. The cited diagrams' parts are still taught in the same
  order and direction.
- Each lesson's "Pen lifts" line, step list and source note changed, and
  the filmstrips were redrawn to match. झ now takes as many lifts as ज (two), and व one
  fewer than श. औ (HI-S135) is now one lift more than ओ, still the most in
  the book. HI-S133's recall answer was shortened to stay under the 300 s
  duration budget (293 s).
- Every Devanagari filmstrip caption now fits on two lines; the headline
  caption reads "lift, then draw the shirorekha rightward".
