Bengali, chapter 49: To play, To buy, To sell, To put, To open.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
খেলা (khælā) — to play.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to sit, then the Bengali for to walk.

You'll want to know: খেলা.
খেলা — khælā — "to play".

The word, taken apart.
খেলা (khælā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khell, "play", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3918). CDIAL adds that if Sanskrit krīḍati, "plays", is of non-Aryan origin, these forms may share that source.
