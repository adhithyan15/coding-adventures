## You'll want to know: chéngkè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C402-STATION402-01]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngkè* — "passenger".

> *huǒchēzhàn lǐ yǒu yìxiē chéngkè* — There are a few passengers in the station.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngkè* is built from *chéng* 'to ride' and *kè* 'a guest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zying* and *khaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to drive', 'to mount' and 'to prevail'.
