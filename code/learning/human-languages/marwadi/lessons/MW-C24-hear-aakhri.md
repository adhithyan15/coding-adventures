## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-AAKHRI-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DIKHAVO-01] -->

> *ākhrī* — **final; last**

Three beats. The second carries the breathed *kh* you first met inside
*dikhāvo*; say both words in a row and check that the breath falls in the same
place.

A counter-offer can be answered with another counter-offer forever. *ākhrī* is
the word that says this one is the end of it, and it is why the question in the
next lesson gets a different kind of answer from the first price question.
