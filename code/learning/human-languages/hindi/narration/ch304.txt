Hindi, chapter 304: The Storm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अँधेरा (ãdherā) — darkness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to cave in, then the Hindi for to be pressed down.

You'll want to know: अँधेरा.
अँधेरा — ãdherā — "darkness".
दोपहर में ही अँधेरा हो गया। dopahar meṁ hī ãdherā ho gayā. It grew dark in the middle of the afternoon.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अँधेरा (ãdherā) goes back to Sanskrit andhakāra, "darkness" (CDIAL 386), built on andha, blind. अँधेरा होना is for it to grow dark; its opposite is उजाला.

Lesson 2 of 5.
गरजना (garajnā) — to thunder; to roar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be pressed down, then the Hindi for darkness.

You'll want to know: गरजना.
गरजना — garajnā — "to thunder; to roar".
बादल ज़ोर से गरजे। bādal zor se garje. The clouds thundered loudly.
Past (it …): वह शेर गरजा, vah śer garjā.

The word, taken apart.
गरजना (garajnā) comes from the Sanskrit verb garj, garjati, "roars, thunders" (Monier-Williams). Clouds गरजते हैं, and so do lions and angry people.

Lesson 3 of 5.
चमकना (camaknā) — to flash; to gleam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for darkness, then the Hindi for to thunder.

You'll want to know: चमकना.
चमकना — camaknā — "to flash; to gleam".
आसमान में बिजली चमकी। āsmān meṁ bijlī camkī. Lightning flashed in the sky.
Past (it …): वह बिजली चमकी, vah bijlī camkī.

The word, taken apart.
चमकना (camaknā) is inherited from camakka, a sudden movement, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4676). चमकदार, shiny, is built on it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मूसलाधार (mūslādhār) — torrential (of rain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to thunder, then the Hindi for to flash.

You'll want to know: मूसलाधार.
मूसलाधार — mūslādhār — "torrential (of rain)".
शाम को मूसलाधार बारिश हुई। śām ko mūslādhār bāriś huī. In the evening there was torrential rain.

The word, taken apart.
मूसलाधार (mūslādhār) joins Sanskrit musala, "a pestle", and dhārā, "a stream" (Monier-Williams): rain falling in streams as thick as a pestle. It is used of rain and of nothing else.

Lesson 5 of 5.
तबाही (tabāhī) — destruction; havoc.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to flash, then the Hindi for torrential (of rain).

You'll want to know: तबाही.
तबाही — tabāhī — "destruction; havoc".
तूफ़ान ने पूरे शहर में तबाही की। tūfān ne pūre śahar meṁ tabāhī kī. The storm wreaked havoc across the whole city.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तबाही (tabāhī) is usually traced to Persian tabāhī, "ruin", from tabāh, ruined. A storm or a war तबाही करता है, or तबाही मचाता है.
