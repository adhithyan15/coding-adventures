## You'll want to know: सवारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C218-RAIL218-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सवारी** — *savārī* — "a passenger; a ride".

> सवारी बस मांय बैठै है।
>
> *savārī bas mā̃y baiṭhai hai.*
>
> The passenger sits in the bus.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सवारी** (*savārī*) is usually traced to Persian *savārī*, "riding, a mount"; in Marwadi it has come to mean the one who rides, a passenger.
