Arabic, chapter 434: Licences and rules.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رخصة (rukhṣa) — a licence; a permit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a pump, then the Arabic for to tow.

You'll want to know: رخصة.
رخصة — rukhṣa — "a licence; a permit".
عند أختي رخصة (rukhṣa) قيادة جديدة. ʿinda ukhtī rukhṣat qiyāda jadīda. My sister has a new driving licence.

The word, taken apart.
رخصة (rukhṣa) is built on the native Arabic root r-kh-ṣ, "to be soft, easy, cheap" (Lane). A rukhṣa is an easing: a permission. It shares its root with rakhīṣ, "cheap".

منعطف — munʿaṭaf — "a bend; a turning".
كان المنعطف على طريق الجبل خطيرا. kāna al-munʿaṭaf ʿalā ṭarīq al-jabal khaṭīran. The bend on the mountain road was dangerous.

منعطف (munʿaṭaf) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-ṭ-f, "to bend, to turn" (Lane). It is the place-noun of inʿaṭafa, "to turn".

جدد أبي رخصته هذا الشهر. jaddada abī rukhṣatahu hādhā ash-shahr. My father renewed his licence this month.

The word, taken apart.
جدد (jaddada) is built on the native Arabic root j-d-d, "to be new" (Lane). It is Form II, "to make new", beside jadīd, "new".
