## You'll want to know: ころがす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C417-TENIMOTSU417-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ころがす** — *korogasu* — "to roll; to wheel".

> **かばんを ころがして、えきまで あるきました。** — *kaban o korogashite, eki made arukimashita* — "I wheeled my case to the station."

Polite **ころがします**, *korogashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あねが かばんを ころがしました。**, *ane ga kaban o korogashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ころがす** (*korogasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
