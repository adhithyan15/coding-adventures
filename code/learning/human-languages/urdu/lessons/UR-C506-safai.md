## You'll want to know: صفائی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C506-SAFAI506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صفائی** — *safāī* — "cleaning; cleanliness".

> ہم ہر اتوار کو گھر کی صفائی کرتے ہیں۔
>
> *ham har itvār ko ghar kī safāī karte haiṅ.*
>
> We clean the house every Sunday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صفائی** (*safāī*) is built on *sāf*, "clean", which is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣāfī*, "pure, clear", with the noun ending *-āī*.
