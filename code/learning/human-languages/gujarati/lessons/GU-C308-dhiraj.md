## You'll want to know: ધીરજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C308-SAHAS308-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ધીરજ** — *dhīraj* — "patience".

> મીરાએ ધીરજથી રાહ જોઈ.
>
> *Mīrāe dhīrajthī rāh joī.*
>
> Mira waited patiently.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધીરજ** (*dhīraj*) probably goes back to Sanskrit *dhairya*, "firmness, calmness, patience" (Monier-Williams), the noun of *dhīra*, "steady"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *dhīr*, "patience", under *dhīra* but not this longer form (CDIAL 6817). It is kept: **ધીરજ રાખો**, be patient.
