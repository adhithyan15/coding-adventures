# சோர்வு (cōrvu) — weariness, fatigue

## You'll want to know: சோர்வு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C87-UNWELL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**சோர்வு** (*cōrvu*) — "weariness", the drained feeling after an illness.

**களைப்பு** is the tiredness of a long day; **சோர்வு** lasts longer. **சோர்வாக இருக்கிறது** —
"I feel worn out".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two kinds of tired.
