## You'll want to know: લૂંટાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C429-SAFE429-04]; assesses=[] -->

**લૂંટાવું** — *lū̃ṭāvũ* — "to be robbed".

> રાત્રે રસ્તામાં વેપારી લૂંટાયો.
>
> *rātre rastāmā̃ vepārī lū̃ṭāyo.*
>
> The merchant was robbed on the road at night.

Past (he …): તે વેપારી લૂંટાયો, *te vepārī lū̃ṭāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લૂંટાવું** (*lū̃ṭāvũ*) is the intransitive partner of લૂંટવું, "to rob", which goes back to *\*luṭṭati*, "plunders", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 11078): who robs, લૂંટવું; who is robbed, લૂંટાવું.
