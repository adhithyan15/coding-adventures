Marathi, chapter 32: Saying It Is Not So.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
… नाही (… nāhī) — saying that a whole sentence is not so.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Retrieve the two things the last chapter did with ना: the doubled front pair, and the tag on the end. Then say the full word नाही on its own, the way you have said it since the courtesy chapter — as an answer.

Grammar Lens: नाही at the end of a sentence, not instead of one.
मला दूध आवडत नाही. — malā dūdh āvaḍat nāhī. — I don't like milk.
Until now नाही was a whole reply. Here it is a piece of a sentence, and the rule is short:
the verb drops back to its bare -अत shape — आवडते becomes आवडत.
नाही follows it, at the very end, carrying the whole denial.
Marathi does not slip a not in front of the verb the way English does. It finishes the sentence and then cancels it. That order is worth saying out loud a few times, because it is the opposite of the English habit and it is what a listening paper's distractors are made of: āvaḍtaṁ and āvaḍat nāhī differ only in what comes after.

You'll want to know: मला माहीत नाही.
मला माहीत नाही. — malā māhīt nāhī. — I don't know.
You have had मला माहीत आहे for a long time. Swap आहे for नाही and you have the single most useful sentence a beginner owns: the honest admission that lets a conversation continue instead of stopping.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: malā chahā āvaḍto then malā dūdh āvaḍat nāhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: malā māhīt āhe then malā māhīt nāhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: where the denial sits in Marathi — at the end, after the verb]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say that you do not like milk, then that you do not know.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Where does नाही go when it denies a whole sentence? (At the end, after the verb.) What happens to आवडते in front of it? (It drops back to आवडत.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
नको (nako) — "no, thank you".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
You can now accept with चालेल and deny a fact with नाही. Between them sits a gap: how do you turn down something you are being offered?

You'll want to know: नको.
नको — nako — no thank you; I don't want it.
चहा? — नको, धन्यवाद. — chahā? — nako, dhanyavād. — Tea? — No thank you.
नाही says a thing is not so. नको (nako) says you do not want it. Answer an offer with नाही and you have said something slightly odd — that the tea does not exist. Marathi keeps the two apart, and so should you.
The word carries the same ancient na- as नाही, with an old be wanted verb worn into its second syllable. That history is exactly its meaning: नको (nako) is let it not be wanted, which is why it can refuse an offer and can never answer a question of fact.
Its polite partner is the one you already have. चालेल accepts, नको (nako) declines, and धन्यवाद goes on the end of either.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: chahā? — chālel]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dūdh? — nako, dhanyavād]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: which of nāhī and nako answers an OFFER — nako]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Accept an offer of tea, then decline an offer of milk.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word declines an offer? (नको (nako).) Which one says a thing is not so? (नाही.)

Lesson 3 of 3.
Four ways of saying no — the two refusals kept apart, and the negated sentence run against the affirmative one.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
You now hold four different noes: the answer नाही, the doubled ना … ना, the sentence-final नाही, and नको (nako). Only one of them can refuse a cup of tea.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: malā chahā āvaḍto paṇ dūdh āvaḍat nāhī — the joining word and the denial in one sentence]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: malā māhīt nāhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nako, dhanyavād]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 35 seconds]
Say you do not like milk, that you do not know, and decline an offered cup of tea.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which no goes at the END of a sentence and cancels it? (नाही.) Which one answers an offer? (नको (nako).) Then stop.
