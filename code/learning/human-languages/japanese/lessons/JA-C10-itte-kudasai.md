# いってください — please say it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-YUKKURI, JA-LEX-ONEGAI-SHIMASU, JA-SCRIPT-I-01, JA-SCRIPT-KU-01, JA-SCRIPT-SA-01] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Say *yukkuri*. Retrieve *onegaishimasu*, then write **い, く, さ**.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ITTE-KUDASAI]; assesses=[] -->

> **いってください。** — *itte kudasai* — please say it

The first part is the request-linking form of *iu*, “to say.” *Kudasai* makes
the requested action polite. Learn the whole spoken frame now. Its small **っ**,
**て**, and voiced **だ** receive separate writing steps later.
