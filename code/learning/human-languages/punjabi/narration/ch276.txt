Punjabi, chapter 276: What Is the Price?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮੁੱਲ (mull) — a price.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a dozen, then the Punjabi for a bag.

You'll want to know: ਮੁੱਲ.
ਮੁੱਲ — mull — "a price".
ਕਮੀਜ਼ ਦਾ ਮੁੱਲ ਕੀ ਹੈ? kamīz dā mull kī hai? What is the price of the shirt?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੁੱਲ (mull) is inherited from Sanskrit mūlya, "original value, price"; CDIAL lists Punjabi mull, "price", under it (CDIAL 10257).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਘਟਾਉਣਾ (ghaṭāuṇā) — to lower; to reduce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a price, then the Punjabi for a rate.

You'll want to know: ਘਟਾਉਣਾ.
ਘਟਾਉਣਾ — ghaṭāuṇā — "to lower; to reduce".
ਇਹ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਮੁੱਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਘਟਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। eh dukān mull nahīṁ ghaṭāundī hai. This shop does not lower its prices.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੁੱਲ ਘਟਾਇਆ, maiṁ mull ghaṭāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਘਟਾਉਣਾ (ghaṭāuṇā) is the causative partner of ghaṭṇā, "to decrease"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ghaṭṇā under a reconstructed ghaṭṭati, "decreases, is wanting" (CDIAL 4415).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਸੌਦਾ.
ਸੌਦਾ — saudā — "a purchase; a deal".
ਇਹ ਚੰਗਾ ਸੌਦਾ ਹੈ। eh changā saudā hai. This is a good deal.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੌਦਾ (saudā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian saudā, "trade, a bargain".
