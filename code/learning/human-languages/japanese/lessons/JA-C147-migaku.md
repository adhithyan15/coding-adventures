## You'll want to know: みがく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C147-WASH147-02]; assesses=[] -->

**みがく** — *migaku* — "to polish; to brush (teeth)".

> **まいにち はを みがきます。** — *mainichi ha o migakimasu* — "I brush my teeth every day."

Polite **みがきます**, *migakimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みがく** (*migaku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
