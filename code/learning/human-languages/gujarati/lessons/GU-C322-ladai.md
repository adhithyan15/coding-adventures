## You'll want to know: લડાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C322-HUMLO322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લડાઈ** — *laḍāī* — "a battle; a fight".

> આ લડાઈ આખો દિવસ ચાલી.
>
> *ā laḍāī ākho divas cālī.*
>
> This battle went on all day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લડાઈ** (*laḍāī*) is built on **લડવું**, to fight, from *\*laḍ*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10920), with the noun ending *-āī*: the fighting itself, from a quarrel to a battle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
