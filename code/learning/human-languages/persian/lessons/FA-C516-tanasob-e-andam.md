## You'll want to know: تناسب اندام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C516-FITNESS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تناسب اندام** (*tanâsob-e andâm*) — "fitness, being in shape".

> او برای تناسب اندام شنا میکند.
>
> *u barâ-ye tanâsob-e andâm shenâ mikonad.*
>
> She swims to keep fit.

*tanâsob* 'proportion' (Arabic) + *andâm* 'body, figure' (Persian).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tanâsob-e andâm* is built in Persian on *tanâsob*, 'proportion', a loan usually traced to Arabic *tanāsub*, and native *andâm*, 'body, figure'.
