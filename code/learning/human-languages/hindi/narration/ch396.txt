Hindi, chapter 396: Values Passed On.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संस्कार (saṁskār) — upbringing, values instilled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to fail, then the Hindi for in the least.

You'll want to know: संस्कार.
संस्कार — saṁskār — "upbringing, values instilled".
अगर बच्चों को अच्छे संस्कार मिलेंगे तो वे अच्छे इंसान बनेंगे। agar baccoṁ ko acche saṁskār mileṅge to ve acche insān baneṅge. If children are brought up well, they will become good people.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
संस्कार (saṁskār) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit saṃskāra, "refining; an impression" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
रटना (raṭnā) — to learn by rote, to cram.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for generous, then the Hindi for a good deed.

You'll want to know: रटना.
रटना — raṭnā — "to learn by rote, to cram".
अगर तुम केवल रटोगे तो कुछ नहीं समझोगे। agar tum keval raṭoge to kuch nahīṃ samjhoge. If you only learn by rote, you won't understand anything.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पूरा पाठ रटा, usne pūrā pāṭh raṭā.

The word, taken apart.
रटना (raṭnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit raṭati, "cries, yells" (CDIAL 10590). Hindi uses the sense of saying over and over.
