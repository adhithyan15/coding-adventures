# ஊரடங்கு (ūraṭaṅku) — a curfew

## You'll want to know: ஊரடங்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C300-AVASARAM300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊரடங்கு** — *ūraṭaṅku* — "a curfew".

> நகரத்தில் மூன்று நாள் ஊரடங்கு இருந்தது.
>
> *nakarattil mūṉṟu nāḷ ūraṭaṅku iruntatu.*
>
> There was a three-day curfew in the city.

ஊர், town + அடங்கு, to settle down: the town goes quiet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊரடங்கு** (*ūraṭaṅku*) is a Tamil compound built from ஊர், "village, town" (DEDR 752), and அடங்கு, "to submit, settle down, cease" (DEDR 63).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
