## You'll want to know: ಬದುಕುಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C332-VIPATTU332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬದುಕುಳಿ** — *badukuḷi* — "to survive".

> ಆ ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಬ್ಬನೇ ಬದುಕುಳಿದನು.
>
> *ā apaghātadalli obbanē badukuḷidanu.*
>
> Only one man survived that accident.

Past (I …): ಬದುಕುಳಿದೆ, *badukuḷide*.

To be left alive after danger; ಉಳಿ is to remain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬದುಕುಳಿ** (*badukuḷi*) is a native Dravidian compound: *baduku*, "to live" (DEDR 5372), and *uḷi*, "to remain" (DEDR 1009).
