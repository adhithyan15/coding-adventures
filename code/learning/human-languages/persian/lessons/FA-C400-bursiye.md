## You'll want to know: بورسیه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C400-DANESHKADE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بورسیه** (*bursiye*) — "a scholarship".

> او با بورسیه به خارج رفت.
>
> *u bâ bursiye be khârej raft.*
>
> She went abroad on a scholarship.

*Bursiye gereftan* — to win a scholarship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بورسیه** is usually traced to French *bourse* "purse; grant", from Late Latin *bursa* "purse", with the ending *-iye*: money put in a student's purse.
