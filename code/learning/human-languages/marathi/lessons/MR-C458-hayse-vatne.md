# हायसे वाटणे (hāyse vāṭṇe) — to feel relieved

## You'll want to know: हायसे वाटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C458-DILASA458-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हायसे वाटणे** — *hāyse vāṭṇe* — "to feel relieved".

> मुलगा सुखरूप घरी आला तेव्हा आईला हायसं वाटलं.
>
> *mulgā sukhrūp gharī ālā tevhā āīlā hāysaṁ vāṭlaṁ.*
>
> Mother felt relieved when her son came home safe.

Past (to him …): त्याला हायसं वाटलं, *tyālā hāysaṁ vāṭlaṁ*.

Dative, like वाटणे: मला हायसं वाटलं. Sentences write the neuter as हायसं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हायसे वाटणे** (*hāyse vāṭṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on हायसे, "eased", whose origin is not settled, with the verb वाटणे, "to feel".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
