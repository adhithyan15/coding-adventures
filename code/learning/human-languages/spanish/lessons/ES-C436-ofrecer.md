# ofrecer — to carry it toward somebody

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-AYUDAR-05, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *ayudar*. Say *cobrar*. The restaurant has no table on Friday.
Say what it does next instead of refusing outright.

## You'll want to know: ofrecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ofrecer** — to offer.

*Le puedo ofrecer el jueves* — I can offer you Thursday. It is the polite move
between *no* and nothing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-03] -->

**ob-** toward, plus **ferre**, to carry. To carry a thing toward somebody.

That *ferre* is one of the most productive roots in the language, and once you
see it you will read words you have never met:

| word | what is carried |
|---|---|
| ofrecer | toward you |
| transferir | across |
| referir | back |

**Watch the spelling in the yo form.** *Ofrecer* takes a **z** before the *o*:
*ofrezco*, not *ofreco*. Every verb ending **-cer** after a vowel does the same
— *conocer / conozco*, *parecer / parezco* — so learning this one buys the
pattern for a large family.
