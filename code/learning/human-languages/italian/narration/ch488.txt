Italian, chapter 488: Al pronto soccorso.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il pronto soccorso — emergency room, A&E.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to vomit, then the Italian for dizziness.

You'll want to know: il pronto soccorso.
il pronto soccorso — "emergency room, A&E".
Di notte siamo andati al pronto soccorso. At night we went to A&E.
Literally "ready help"; pronto is also what you say on the phone.

The word, taken apart.
pronto soccorso is built from pronto "ready, quick", inherited from Latin prōmptus (EtymDB), and soccorso "help", from Vulgar Latin succursum (EtymDB), built on currere "to run".

il chirurgo — "surgeon".
Il chirurgo dice che l'operazione è andata bene. The surgeon says the operation went well.

The word, taken apart.
chirurgo is a learned borrowing from Latin chīrurgus (EtymDB), from Greek kheirourgós "working with the hands", from kheír "hand" and érgon "work".
