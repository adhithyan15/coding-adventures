## You'll want to know: વીમો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C372-VIMO372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વીમો** — *vīmo* — "insurance".

> જો ગાડીનો વીમો હશે તો મરામતના પૈસા બચશે.
>
> *jo gāḍīno vīmo haśe to marāmatnā paisā bacśe.*
>
> If the car is insured, you will save on repairs.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વીમો** (*vīmo*) is usually traced to Persian *bīma*, "fear; insurance".
