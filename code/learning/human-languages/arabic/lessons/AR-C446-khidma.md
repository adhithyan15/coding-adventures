## You'll want to know: خدمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C446-KHIDMA446-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خدمة** — *khidma* — "a service".

> كانت الخدمة في المطعم ممتازة.
>
> *kānat al-khidma fī al-maṭʿam mumtāza.*
>
> The service in the restaurant was excellent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خدمة** (*khidma*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-d-m*, "to serve" (Lane).
