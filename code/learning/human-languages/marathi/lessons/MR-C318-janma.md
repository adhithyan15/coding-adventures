## You'll want to know: जन्म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C318-BARSE318-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जन्म** — *janma* — "birth".

> माझ्या भावाचा जन्म पुण्यात झाला.
>
> *mājhyā bhāvācā janma puṇyāt jhālā.*
>
> My brother was born in Pune.

Masculine.

X-चा जन्म होणे, X is born.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जन्म** (*janma*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *janman*, 'birth' (Monier-Williams).
