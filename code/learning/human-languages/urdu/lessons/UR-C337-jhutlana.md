# جھٹلانا (jhuṭlānā) — to deny; to give the lie to

## You'll want to know: جھٹلانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C337-TASDIQ337-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جھٹلانا** — *jhuṭlānā* — "to deny; to give the lie to".

> وہ ہر سچی بات کو جھٹلاتا ہے۔
>
> *voh har sachchī bāt ko jhuṭlātā hai.*
>
> He denies everything that is true.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے خبر کو جھٹلایا, *us ne khabar ko jhuṭlāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھٹلانا** (*jhuṭlānā*) is built on **جھوٹ**, "a lie", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhūṭhā*, "lying, false", under *\*jhūṭṭha*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5407). To call a thing a lie.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
