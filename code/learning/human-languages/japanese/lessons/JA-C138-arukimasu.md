## You'll want to know: あるきます
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C138-ARUKIMASU]; assesses=[] -->

**あるきます** — *arukimasu* — "I walk", "I go on foot".

The dictionary form is **あるく**, *aruku*, and it works like **はたらく**:
the last **く** becomes **き**, and **ます** follows. *aruk-u* becomes
*aruk-i-masu*.

> **えきまで あるきます。** — *eki made arukimasu* — "I walk to the
> station." **まで** means "as far as".
