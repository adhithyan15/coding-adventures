## You'll want to know: పైలటు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C454-PAILATU454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పైలటు** — *pailaṭu* — "a pilot".

> విమానం దిగే ముందు పైలటు మాతో మాట్లాడాడు.
>
> *vimānaṁ digē mundu pailaṭu mātō māṭlāḍāḍu.*
>
> Before the plane landed, the pilot spoke to us.

Like other English loans it takes -ఉ: పైలటు. A woman pilot is also పైలటు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పైలటు** (*pailaṭu*) is a loan from English *pilot*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
