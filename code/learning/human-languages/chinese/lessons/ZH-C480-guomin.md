## You'll want to know: guòmǐn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C480-SYMPTOM480-04]; assesses=[] -->

*guòmǐn* — "to be allergic".

> *wǒ duì huā guòmǐn* — I'm allergic to flowers.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guòmǐn* is built from 过 'beyond' and *mǐn* 'quick', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaH* and *minX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go beyond' and the second 'quick' and 'clever': too quick to react. The medical sense is modern.
