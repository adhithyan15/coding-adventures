## You'll want to know: いちおう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C353-TENKEN353-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いちおう** — *ichiō* — "tentatively; for the time being".

> **あめが ふったら こまるので、いちおう かさを もちます。** — *ame ga futtara komaru node, ichiō kasa o mochimasu* — "Rain would be a nuisance, so I'll take an umbrella anyway."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いちおう** (*ichiō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ichi*, "one" (Middle Chinese *'jit*) and *ō*, "answer" (*'ingH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
