## You'll want to know: el relato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C563-RELATO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el relato** — "the account; the short story".

> Escribió un relato sobre la guerra.
>
> *She wrote a short story about the war.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*relato* comes from Latin *relātus* 'brought back, reported' (EtymDB), the participle of *referre* 'to carry back', built on *ferre* 'to carry'.
