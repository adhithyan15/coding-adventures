## You'll want to know: रोधः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C411-RATHYA411-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रोधः** — *rodhaḥ* — "a blockage; an obstruction".

> मार्गे महान् रोधः अस्ति।
>
> *mārge mahān rodhaḥ asti.*
>
> There is a big blockage on the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोधः** (*rodhaḥ*) comes from the root √rudh, "to obstruct, to hold back"; MW gives *rodha* as stopping, checking, obstructing and blockading (MW).
