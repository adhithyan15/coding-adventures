## You'll want to know: しんこん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C290-UTSURU290-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しんこん** — *shinkon* — "newly married".

> **ふたりは しんこんです。** — *futari wa shinkon desu* — "The two are newly married."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんこん** (*shinkon*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "new" (Middle Chinese *sin*) and *kon*, "marriage" (*xwon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
