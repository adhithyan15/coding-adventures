## You'll want to know: داروغہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C278-THANA278-01]; assesses=[] -->

**داروغہ** — *dāroghā* — "a police inspector".

> داروغہ تھانے میں نہیں تھا۔
>
> *dāroghā thāne meṅ nahīṅ thā.*
>
> The inspector was not at the police station.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *dārogha*, "an overseer", a word Persian is thought to have taken from Mongolian. In a town the **داروغہ** runs the **تھانہ**.
