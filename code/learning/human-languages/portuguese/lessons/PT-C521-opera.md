## You'll want to know: a ópera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C521-GENEROS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a ópera** — "opera".

> Ela canta ópera desde criança.
>
> *She has sung opera since she was a child.*

The opera house is *o teatro de ópera*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ópera* was borrowed from Italian *opera* "a work" (EtymDB), from Latin *opera* "work, effort", related to *opus*.
