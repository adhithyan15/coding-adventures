Urdu, chapter 446: Worry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فکر کرنا (fikr karnā) — to worry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to crouch out of sight, then the Urdu for to huddle.

You'll want to know: فکر کرنا.
فکر کرنا — fikr karnā — "to worry".
امی ہر بات کی فکر کرتی ہیں۔ ammī har bāt kī fikr kartī haiṅ. Mum worries about everything.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچوں کی فکر کی, us ne bachchoṅ kī fikr kī.

The word, taken apart.
فکر کرنا (fikr karnā) is built from فکر, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic fikr, "thought", and کرنا, "to do": in Urdu the thought has become a worry.

دھڑکنا — dhaṛaknā — "to throb; to pound (of the heart)".
امتحان سے پہلے میرا دل دھڑک رہا تھا۔ imtihān se pehle merā dil dhaṛak rahā thā. Before the exam my heart was pounding.
Past (it …): وہ دل دھڑکا, voh dil dhaṛkā.

The word, taken apart.
دھڑکنا (dhaṛaknā) is inherited from dhaḍ, "throb, beat", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6711); دھڑکن, "a heartbeat", is its noun.
