## You'll want to know: das Zeugnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C375-KLAUSUR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Zeugnis** — "the school report; the certificate, the reference".

> Am Ende des Schuljahres bekommen die Kinder ihr Zeugnis.
>
> *At the end of the school year the children get their reports.*

Plural: *die Zeugnisse*. From an employer: *das Arbeitszeugnis* — a written reference.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zeugnis* is formed with *-nis* on *zeugen* "to testify", from Middle High German *ziugen* (EtymDB): a paper that bears witness to what you did.
