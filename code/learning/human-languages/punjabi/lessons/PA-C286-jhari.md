# ਝੜੀ (jhaṛī) — a spell of steady rain

## You'll want to know: ਝੜੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C286-STORM286-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝੜੀ** — *jhaṛī* — "a spell of steady rain".

> ਬਰਸਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੰਮੀ ਝੜੀ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *barsāt vich lammī jhaṛī hundī hai.*
>
> In the rainy season there is a long spell of steady rain.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝੜੀ** (*jhaṛī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhaḍī*, "rainstorm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jhaṛī*, "continued rain", under it (CDIAL 5329).
