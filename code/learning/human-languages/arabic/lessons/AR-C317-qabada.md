## You'll want to know: قبض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C317-TAWQIF317-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قبض** — *qabaḍa* — "to seize, to grasp".

> قبضت الشرطة على اللص في السوق.
>
> *qabaḍat ash-shurṭa ʿalā al-liṣṣ fī as-sūq.*
>
> The police caught the thief in the market.

Present: يقبض, *yaqbiḍ*.

قبض على: to arrest. قبض راتبه: he drew his pay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قبض** (*qabaḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-ḍ* (Lane), "to take a thing in the closed hand"; *qabḍa* is a fist.

## What you've built
