Urdu, chapter 521: The Big Match.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
میچ (maich) — a match (a game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to pit against each other, then the Urdu for a loincloth.

You'll want to know: میچ.
میچ — maich — "a match (a game)".
کل ہماری ٹیم کا میچ ہے۔ kal hamārī ṭīm kā maich hai. Our team has a match tomorrow.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
میچ (maich) is borrowed from English match.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
شکست (shikast) is usually traced to Persian shikast, "a breaking; a defeat", from shikastan, "to break".
