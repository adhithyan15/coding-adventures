## You'll want to know: gōnggòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C360-NEED360-01]; assesses=[] -->

*gōnggòng* — "public; shared by everyone".

> *zhèr yǒu gōnggòng cèsuǒ ma* — Is there a public toilet here?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōnggòng* is built from 公 and *gòng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *gjowngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'shared' and 'upright', and the second 'together' and 'all'.
