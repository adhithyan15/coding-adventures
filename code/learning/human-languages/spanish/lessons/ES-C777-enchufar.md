## You'll want to know: enchufar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C777-ENCHUFAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**enchufar** — "to plug in".

> Si no carga, enchufa el cable otra vez.
>
> *If it isn't charging, plug the cable in again.*

*El enchufe* is the plug or socket; in Spain it also means connections, knowing the right people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *enchufar* is not settled. It is usually treated as imitative, built on a sound like *chuf*, the noise of one pipe pushed into another.
