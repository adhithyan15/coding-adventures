Italian, chapter 244: Exactly, Almost, About, Only, Together.

Lesson 1 of 5.
proprio — exactly, really.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: proprio.
proprio — "exactly, really".

The word, taken apart.
proprio from Latin proprius, "one's own" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).

The word, taken apart.
soltanto is solo, "alone, only", plus tanto, "so much": only so much. solo from Latin solus, "alone"; tanto from Latin tantus. (EtymDB 2.1).
