## You'll want to know: céngjīng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C261-EVER261-02]; assesses=[] -->

*céngjīng* — "once (in the past); at one time".

> *wǒ céngjīng qù guo Běijīng* — I've been to Beijing once.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*céngjīng* is built from *céng* and *jīng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzong* and *keng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with the second, 'to pass through'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
