## You'll want to know: راگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C518-RIYAZ518-03]; assesses=[] -->

**راگ** — *rāg* — "a raga (a melodic mode)".

> استاد نے مجھے شام کا ایک راگ سکھایا۔
>
> *ustād ne mujhe shām kā ek rāg sikhāyā.*
>
> My teacher taught me an evening raga.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**راگ** (*rāg*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rāga*, "colouring; passion; a musical mode" (Monier-Williams).
