## You'll want to know: noctū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C295-NOCTU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**noctū** — "by night, at night".

> Fūrēs noctū in vīllam intrāvērunt.
>
> *Thieves got into the villa at night.*

An old case form of *nox* (taught): in the night-time, a word for when thieves and lovers go out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**noctū** is an old case form of *nox*, "night" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Proto-Indo-European *\*nókʷts*, "night"; English *night* is a cousin.
