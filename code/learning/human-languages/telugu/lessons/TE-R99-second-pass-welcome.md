# A warm welcome and short answers — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C99-WELCOME-01, TE-LEX-C99-WELCOME-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] A friend arrives at your door. Greet them with palms together, and say you are delighted.

## Grammar Lens: the ending -ఏ, "exactly this"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C98-REPLY-04, TE-LEX-C98-REPLY-05] -->

| word | built from |
|---|---|
| **నిజమే** | truth + -ఏ |
| **అదే** | that + -ఏ |

The **-ఏ** points: *this truth and no other*, *that one exactly*.

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: hand over a gift; congratulate your friend]
- [YOU SAY: say the wedding invitation came]
- [YOU SAY: someone asks what happened: it's nothing. Have you eaten? Not yet]
- [YOU SAY: agree that it's true; say that will do; and "that's the one!"]
