## You'll want to know: ひろまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C281-UWASA281-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひろまる** — *hiromaru* — "to spread widely; to get about".

> **その うわさは がっこうで ひろまっています。** — *sono uwasa wa gakkō de hiromatte imasu* — "The rumour is going round the school."

Polite **ひろまります**, *hiromarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **うわさが ひろまりました。**, *uwasa ga hiromarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひろまる** (*hiromaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
