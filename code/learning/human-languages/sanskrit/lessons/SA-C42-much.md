## You'll want to know: बहु
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C42-REPLY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बहु** (*bahu*) — "much, many".

**बहु** is "much" of one thing and "many" of several — the far end of the answer **किञ्चित्** starts.

Greek *pakhus*, "thick, stout", is its cousin, and thickness is the sense underneath: plenty as bulk rather than as a count.

Hindi *bahut*, "very, a lot", is **बहु** with an ending on it, and it is one of the commonest words anyone says today.
