## You'll want to know: ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C267-OFFICE267-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ** — *ḍākkhānā* — "a post office".

> ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ ਥਾਣੇ ਦੇ ਸਾਹਮਣੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *ḍākkhānā thāṇe de sāhmaṇe hai.*
>
> The post office is opposite the police station.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ** (*ḍākkhānā*) is built from *ḍāk*, "the post", for which CDIAL gives no Punjabi form (CDIAL 5519), and *khānā*, "house", usually traced to Persian *khāna*.
