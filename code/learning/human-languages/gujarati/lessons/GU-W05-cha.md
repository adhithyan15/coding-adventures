# ચ — the consonant cha

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHHA-01] -->

Point to **છ** and say aspirated *chha* with its puff. The new **ચ** is the
shorter, unaspirated *cha*: no extra puff.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-CHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ચ** visible. It is *cha*. Circle the upper bowl, turn through the small
middle loop, and continue around the broad lower body. Lift once; descend the
separate right spine and turn through its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ચ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice upper bowl, small middle
loop, broad lower body, separate spine; one pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ચ** once. Compare only the upper bowl and middle loop; repair one
landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W05-cha-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-CHA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write unaspirated cha from memory.","answer":"ચ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ચ — the upper bowl and middle loop are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ચ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write unaspirated *cha* once. Uncover **ચ**, compare, and stop
after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-CHHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **ચ છ** aloud, keeping the second sound's puff.
