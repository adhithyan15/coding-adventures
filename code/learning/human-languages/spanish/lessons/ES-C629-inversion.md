## You'll want to know: la inversión
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C629-GANANCIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la inversión** — "the investment".

> La casa es una buena inversión.
>
> *The house is a good investment.*

Built on *invertir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inversión* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inversio* 'a turning about', built on *invertere*, from *vertere* 'to turn' (Lewis & Short).
