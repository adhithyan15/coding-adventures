Arabic, chapter 383: The economy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اقتصاد (iqtiṣād) — the economy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to protest, then the Arabic for a strike.

You'll want to know: اقتصاد.
اقتصاد — iqtiṣād — "the economy".
يتحسن الاقتصاد ببطء. yataḥassanu al-iqtiṣād bi-buṭʾ. The economy is improving slowly.

The word, taken apart.
اقتصاد (iqtiṣād) is built on the native Arabic root q-ṣ-d, "to aim at; to keep to the middle way" (Lane). Iqtiṣād is the Form VIII verbal noun, keeping to the middle way: thrift, and so the economy.

استثمر (istathmara) is built on the native Arabic root th-m-r, "to bear fruit" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek fruit from a thing: to invest.
