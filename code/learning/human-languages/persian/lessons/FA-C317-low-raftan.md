## You'll want to know: لو رفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C317-TAQIB-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لو رفتن** (*low raftan*) — "to be found out, to leak out".

> نقشه آنها زود لو رفت.
>
> *Naqshe-ye ânhâ zud low raft.*
>
> Their plan was soon found out.

Colloquial; the secret itself is the subject. Present *low miravad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raftan* "to go" is inherited Persian. The origin of *low* "revealed, exposed" is not settled; EtymDB lists no entry.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
