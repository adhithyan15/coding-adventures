## You'll want to know: みゃく

**みゃく** — *myaku* — "a pulse".

> **かんごしが みゃくを はかりました。** — *kangoshi ga myaku o hakarimashita* — "The nurse took my pulse."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みゃく** (*myaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *myaku*, "pulse" (Middle Chinese *meak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
