## You'll want to know: おもちゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C500-TOY500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おもちゃ** — *omocha* — "a toy".

> **こどもは おもちゃで あそびます。** — *kodomo wa omocha de asobimasu* — "Children play with toys."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おもちゃ** (*omocha*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
