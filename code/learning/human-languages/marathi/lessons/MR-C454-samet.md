## You'll want to know: समेट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C454-RUSVA454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**समेट** — *sameṭ* — "a reconciliation".

> दोन मित्रांमध्ये शेवटी समेट झाला.
>
> *don mitrāmmadhye śevṭī sameṭ jhālā.*
>
> In the end the two friends made up.

Masculine.

समेट होणे, to be reconciled; समेट घडवणे, to bring two sides together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समेट** (*sameṭ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Marathi verb *sameṭṇẽ*, "to roll up", under Sanskrit *saṁveṣṭayati*, "wraps up" (CDIAL 13026); the noun, a quarrel wound up and settled, is usually connected with that verb.

## What you've built
