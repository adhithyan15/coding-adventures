## You'll want to know: జాలి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C505-KARUNA505-01]; assesses=[] -->

**జాలి** — *jāli* — "pity; sympathy".

> ఆకలితో ఉన్న కుక్కపిల్లను చూసి నాకు జాలి వేసింది.
>
> *ākalitō unna kukkapillanu cūsi nāku jāli vēsindi.*
>
> Seeing the hungry puppy, I felt pity.

జాలి వేయు or జాలి కలుగు, to feel pity; -మీద జాలిపడు, to pity someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జాలి** (*jāli*) is a Telugu word for the pity you feel for someone weaker; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
