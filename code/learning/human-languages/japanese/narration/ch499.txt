Lesson 1 of 5.
ゆうえんち (yūenchi) — an amusement park.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for nodding off, then the Japanese for paid leave.

You'll want to know: ゆうえんち.
ゆうえんち — yūenchi — "an amusement park".
にちようびに ゆうえんちへ いきました。 — nichiyōbi ni yūenchi e ikimashita — "On Sunday we went to the amusement park."

The word, taken apart.
ゆうえんち (yūenchi) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are yū, "play", en, "park" (Middle Chinese hjwon) and chi, "ground" (dijH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
