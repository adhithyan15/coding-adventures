German, chapter 137: Weekend, Date, Century, Never, Often.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Wochenende — the weekend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to order, then the German for rather.
[pause 2 seconds]
The weekend starts on Saturday: say Sonnabend, the northern word for it.

You'll want to know: das Wochenende.
das Wochenende — "the weekend".
