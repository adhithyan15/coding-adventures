Spanish, chapter 696: Tiempo y temperatura.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el termómetro — the thermometer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to fish, then the Spanish for the deer.

You'll want to know: el termómetro.
el termómetro — "the thermometer".
El termómetro marca treinta grados. The thermometer shows thirty degrees.
Treinta grados a la sombra — thirty degrees in the shade.

The word, taken apart.
termómetro came from French thermomètre, a word made from Greek thermós "hot" and métron "measure".

Lesson 2 of 5.
el pronóstico — the forecast; the prognosis.

You'll want to know: el pronóstico.
el pronóstico — "the forecast; the prognosis".
El pronóstico dice que va a llover. The forecast says it is going to rain.
El pronóstico del tiempo — the weather forecast.

The word, taken apart.
pronóstico is a learned borrowing from Late Latin prognōsticus, from Greek prognōstikós "knowing beforehand".

Lesson 3 of 5.
la previsión — the outlook, the forecast; foresight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the thermometer, then the Spanish for the forecast.

You'll want to know: la previsión.
la previsión — "the outlook, the forecast; foresight".
La previsión para mañana es buena. The forecast for tomorrow is good.
The verb is prever.

The word, taken apart.
previsión is a learned borrowing from Late Latin praevīsiō "foresight" (EtymDB), from praevidēre "to foresee", built on vidēre "to see".

Lesson 4 of 5.
húmedo — humid, damp.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Spanish for the forecast, then the Spanish for the outlook.

You'll want to know: húmedo.
húmedo — "humid, damp".
El aire es muy húmedo en la costa. The air is very humid on the coast.
Ropa húmeda — damp clothes.

The word, taken apart.
húmedo is a learned borrowing from Latin hūmidus "moist, damp" (EtymDB).
