## You'll want to know: pàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C94-PANG-01]; assesses=[] -->

*pàng* — "fat; plump".

> *māo tài pàng le* — The cat's too fat.

Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The character for *pàng* is in the Guangyun as *phanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'half of a sacrificial animal'. That reading would give pàn, not pàng, and the sense is not 'fat', so the history of *pàng* is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-TAI-01] -->

*pàng* is a word for people and animals, and said of a person it is blunt: a cat can hear *tài pàng le* without offence.
