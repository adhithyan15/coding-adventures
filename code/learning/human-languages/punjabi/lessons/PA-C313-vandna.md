## You'll want to know: ਵੰਡਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C313-WED313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੰਡਣਾ** — *vaṇḍṇā* — "to hand out".

> ਵਿਆਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਸਭ ਨੂੰ ਖੀਰ ਵੰਡੀ।
>
> *viāh vich asīṁ sabh nū̃ khīr vaṇḍī.*
>
> At the wedding we handed out rice pudding to everyone.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੋਸਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਕੇਲੇ ਵੰਡੇ, *maiṁ dostāṁ nū̃ kele vaṇḍe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੰਡਣਾ** (*vaṇḍṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vaṇṭati*, "shares"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vaṇḍṇā*, "shares", under it (CDIAL 11238).
