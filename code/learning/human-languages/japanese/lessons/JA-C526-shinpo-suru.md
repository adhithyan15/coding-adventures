## You'll want to know: しんぽする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C526-PROGRESS526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しんぽする** — *shinpo suru* — "to progress; to advance".

> **もし ぎじゅつが しんぽしたら、くるまは はやく なるでしょう。** — *moshi gijutsu ga shinpo shitara, kuruma wa hayaku naru deshō* — "If technology advances, cars will get faster."

Polite **しんぽします**, *shinposhimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ぎじゅつが しんぽしました。**, *gijutsu ga shinposhimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんぽする** (*shinpo suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *shinpo* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "advance" (Middle Chinese *tsinH*) and *po*, "walk" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
