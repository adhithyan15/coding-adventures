## You'll want to know: बिचकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C456-SANKOC456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बिचकणे** — *bicakṇe* — "to shrink back".

> कुत्रा जवळ आला तेव्हा मुलगी बिचकली.
>
> *kutrā javaḷ ālā tevhā mulgī bicaklī.*
>
> The girl shrank back when the dog came near.

Past (he …): तो बिचकला, *to bicaklā*.

A small start of fear or doubt; बिचकत बिचकत, hesitantly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिचकणे** (*bicakṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
