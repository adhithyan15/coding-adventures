# ಲ — one character, met inside words you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-136] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: ◌ೂ — what does it do to the consonant it sits on?

[PAUSE 2s] One character this time — and it is the one standing in the middle of
both of Kannada's negatives.

## Script you'll notice: ಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-137]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲ** — *la*.

It is a **consonant**, and in this script a consonant is never bare: it comes with an *a* already in it. So it is not *l*, it is **la**. Kannada has a second l as well — the thicker ಳ you already trace — and the two are different letters for different sounds, not a matter of handwriting.

You already say these, and every one of them has ಲ somewhere inside it:

- **ಅಲ್ಲ** *alla* — is not (that)
- **ಇಲ್ಲ** *illa* — there is not
- **ಹಲ್ಲು** *hallu* — a tooth
- **ಕಾಲು** *kālu* — a leg

## Writing: ಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-137] -->

- **1.** start at the left of the small loop and close it counterclockwise
- **2.** without lifting, sweep down the outer left side and round the base
- **3.** without lifting, climb the right side and curl in to its tip

**Pen lifts: 0.** The whole letter is one run, from the small loop round the
outside to the tip of the right side. ಲ has no curled bar on top, so there
is nothing to lift for.
