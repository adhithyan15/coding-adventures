# परसूं हूं बाजार जासूं — a plan

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I will go to the market tomorrow*. Then *the day after tomorrow*:
**परसूं**.

## Grammar Lens: the time first
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C163-FUT-PLAN-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-05, MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-03, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-02] -->

A plan opens on its time, then says what you will do:

> **परसूं हूं बाजार जासूं।**
>
> *parsū̃ hū̃ bājār jāsū̃.* — The day after tomorrow I'll go to the market.

> **आज आथण हूं घरै आसूं।**
>
> *āj āthaṇ hū̃ gharai āsū̃.* — This evening I'll come home.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C163-FUT-PLAN-01, MW-CONJ-AR-01, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-03, MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GHARAI-01] -->

Two plans join with **अर**:

> **काल हूं दिनूगै उठसूं अर बाजार जासूं।**
>
> *kāl hū̃ dinūgai uṭhsū̃ ar bājār jāsū̃.* — Tomorrow I'll get up early and go to the market.
