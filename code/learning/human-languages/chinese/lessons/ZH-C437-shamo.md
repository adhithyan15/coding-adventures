## You'll want to know: shāmò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C437-LAND437-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shāmò* — "desert".

> *míngtiān wǒmen zuò chē qù shāmò* — Tomorrow we're driving out to the desert.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shāmò* is built from *shā* 'sand' and *mò* 'a waste', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srae* and *mak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as stones scattered by water and gives the pair itself under the second.
