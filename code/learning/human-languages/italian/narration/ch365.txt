Italian, chapter 365: Giusto e ingiusto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la giustizia — the justice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to demonstrate, then the Italian for the banner.

You'll want to know: la giustizia.
la giustizia — "the justice".
Le vittime chiedono giustizia. The victims are asking for justice.
Fare giustizia — to see justice done.

The word, taken apart.
giustizia is a learned borrowing from Latin iūstitia "justice, fairness" (Lewis & Short), from iūstus "fair, right".

Lesson 4 of 5.
il pregiudizio — the prejudice.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: il pregiudizio.
il pregiudizio — "the prejudice".
Bisogna conoscere le persone senza pregiudizi. You have to get to know people without prejudice.
Literally a judgement made before.

The word, taken apart.
pregiudizio is a learned borrowing from Latin praeiūdicium "a judgement made beforehand" (Lewis & Short), built from prae "before" and iūdicium "judgement", on iūdex "judge".

Lesson 5 of 5.
lo stereotipo — the stereotype.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the injustice, then the Italian for the prejudice.

You'll want to know: lo stereotipo.
lo stereotipo — "the stereotype".
Gli stereotipi sugli italiani sono tanti: pizza, mandolino... There are many stereotypes about Italians: pizza, mandolin...
Stress: stereòtipo.

The word, taken apart.
stereotipo is usually traced to French stéréotype, a printing word built from Greek stereós "solid" and týpos "a blow, an impression", the word behind Latin typus "a figure, an image" (Lewis & Short): a fixed plate that prints the same image again and again.
