## You'll want to know: अवक्रयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C429-UDGHATANA429-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अवक्रयः** — *avakrayaḥ* — "rent".

> प्रकोष्ठस्य अवक्रयः कियत् अस्ति?
>
> *prakoṣṭhasya avakrayaḥ kiyat asti?*
>
> How much is the rent for the room?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवक्रयः** (*avakrayaḥ*) is built from *ava* and the root √krī, "to buy", which also gives *krīṇāti*, "buys"; MW gives *avakraya* as letting out to hire and as rent (MW).
