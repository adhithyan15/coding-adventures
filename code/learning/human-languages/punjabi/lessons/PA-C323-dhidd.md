## You'll want to know: ਢਿੱਡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C323-BODY323-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਿੱਡ** — *ḍhiḍḍ* — "the belly".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਢਿੱਡ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere ḍhiḍḍ vich darad hai.*
>
> I have a pain in my belly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਿੱਡ** (*ḍhiḍḍ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhiḍḍha*, "belly"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍhiḍḍh*, "belly", under it (CDIAL 5589).
