Spanish, chapter 700: Nieve y hielo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
nevar — to snow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to hail, then the Spanish for to thunder.

You'll want to know: nevar.
nevar — "to snow".
En la sierra nieva en enero. It snows in the mountains in January.
The e becomes ie: nieva.

The word, taken apart.
nevar is inherited from Vulgar Latin nivāre "to snow" (EtymDB), from Latin nix, nivis "snow".

helar is inherited from Latin gelāre "to freeze" (EtymDB), from gelū "frost"; Latin g before e became Spanish h here.
