## You'll want to know: el esqueleto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C735-HUESO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el esqueleto** — "the skeleton".

> El esqueleto humano tiene 206 huesos.
>
> *The human skeleton has 206 bones.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esqueleto* comes from Greek *skeletós* "dried up, a dried body" (EtymDB), from *skéllein* "to dry up".
