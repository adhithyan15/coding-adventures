## You'll want to know: вывод
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C344-LOGIKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**вывод** (*vývod*) — "a conclusion".

> Какой вывод можно сделать?
>
> *Kakóy vývod mózhno sdélat?*
>
> What conclusion can we draw?

Masculine. *Сделать вывод* — to draw a conclusion. It also means withdrawal: *вывод войск*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**вывод** is built from *вы-* "out" and *вод-*, the root of *водить* "to lead", from Proto-Slavic *\*voditi* "to lead, to conduct" (EtymDB). A conclusion is what you lead out of the facts.
