# खा, पी, आ — three more vowel stems

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-01, MW-LEX-C50-FAMILY50-05, MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-01, MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I go to the market every day*. Then *to eat*, *to drink*, *to
come*: **खाणो**, **पीणो**, **आवणो**.

## Grammar Lens: the same three endings
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C50-FAMILY50-05, MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-01, MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-03, MW-SCRIPT-C153-UU-01] -->

**खा**, **पी** and **आ** end in vowels, and go exactly like **जा**:

> **हूं रोज दिनूगै चाय पीऊं हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ roj dinūgai chāy pīū̃ hū̃.* — I drink tea early every morning.

> **राम रोटी खावै है।**
>
> *rām roṭī khāvai hai.* — Ram eats roti.

> **आप रोज आवो हो।**
>
> *āp roj āvo ho.* — You come every day.

**आवणो** leaves **आव**, but its present follows **जा**: **आऊं**, **आवै**, **आवो**.
Old Marwari had *āvaï*, "comes" (CDIAL 1200).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01, MW-SCRIPT-ROTI-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-03] -->

Every cell of the present, now that each one is taught:

| who | बोल- | जा- |
|---|---|---|
| **हूं** | **बोलूं हूं** | **जाऊं हूं** |
| **वो** | **बोलै है** | **जावै है** |
| **आप** | **बोलो हो** | **जावो हो** |

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: **हूं रोटी खाऊं हूं।** — *hū̃ roṭī khāū̃ hū̃.* — I eat roti.]
- [YOU SAY: **वो चाय पीवै है।** — *vo chāy pīvai hai.* — He drinks tea.]
- [YOU SAY: **वो रोज आवै है।** — *vo roj āvai hai.* — He comes every day.]
