# Hear *shì ma* — is that so?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-SHI, ZH-LEX-MA-01, ZH-LEX-NIHAOMA-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **to be**, then say **how are you**.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-SHIMA-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-SHI, ZH-GRAMMAR-MA-QUESTION-01] -->

> *shì ma* — **is that so? really?**

Two syllables: the verb **to be**, falling hard, then the light question
syllable. Nothing else.

This is the move you make when somebody tells you something and you want to show
you heard it and to invite them to go on. English spends a whole phrase on it —
*is that so*, *really*, *oh yes?* — and Mandarin spends two syllables, one of
which you learned yesterday.

Say it with the voice going **down** on *shì* and staying light on *ma*. Said
flat it checks a fact; said with the fall exaggerated it registers surprise.
