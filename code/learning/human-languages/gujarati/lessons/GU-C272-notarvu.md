## You'll want to know: નોતરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C272-PROMISE272-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નોતરવું** — *notarvũ* — "to invite".

> **શું તમે પડોશીને લગ્નમાં નોતર્યા?** — *śũ tame paḍośīne lagnamā̃ notaryā?* — "Did you invite the neighbours to the wedding?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નોતરવું** (*notarvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *nimantrayatē*, "invites" (CDIAL 7235).
