# ಪಟ್ಟಣ (paṭṭaṇa) — a small town; a town

## You'll want to know: ಪಟ್ಟಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C465-PATTANA465-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಟ್ಟಣ** — *paṭṭaṇa* — "a small town; a town".

> ನನ್ನ ಊರು ಒಂದು ಚಿಕ್ಕ ಪಟ್ಟಣ.
>
> *nanna ūru ondu cikka paṭṭaṇa.*
>
> My home town is a small town.

Plural: ಪಟ್ಟಣಗಳು, *paṭṭaṇagaḷu*.

Bigger than a ಹಳ್ಳಿ, smaller than a ನಗರ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಟ್ಟಣ** (*paṭṭaṇa*) comes from Sanskrit *paṭṭana*, "a town" (CDIAL 7705); Turner notes that the Sanskrit word itself was probably taken from a Dravidian source.
