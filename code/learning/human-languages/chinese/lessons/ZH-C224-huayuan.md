## You'll want to know: huāyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C224-BLOOM224-03]; assesses=[] -->

*huāyuán* — "garden".

> *wǒ jiā hòubian yǒu yí ge huāyuán* — There's a garden behind our house.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huāyuán* is built from 花 'flower' and *yuán* 'a plot', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwae* and *hjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a garden plot'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
