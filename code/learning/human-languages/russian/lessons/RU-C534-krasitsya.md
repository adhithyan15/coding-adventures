## You'll want to know: краситься
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C534-MORNING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**краситься** (*krásitsya*) — "to put on make-up".

> Она не красится, когда сидит дома.
>
> *Oná ne krásitsya, kogdá sidít dóma.*
>
> She doesn't wear make-up when she's at home.

Imperfective; perfective накраситься. Also to dye one's hair. The reflexive of *красить* — to paint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*краситься* is the reflexive of *красить* 'to paint', built on *краска* 'paint', from the root of *краса* 'beauty'; EtymDB has no entry for the reflexive.
