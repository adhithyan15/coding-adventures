## You'll want to know: ಅಸಾಧ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C383-SANDEHA383-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಸಾಧ್ಯ** — *asādhya* — "impossible".

> ಒಂದೇ ದಿನದಲ್ಲಿ ಈ ಕೆಲಸ ಮುಗಿಸುವುದು ಅಸಾಧ್ಯ.
>
> *ondē dinadalli ī kelasa mugisuvudu asādhya.*
>
> Finishing this job in a single day is impossible.

ಅ- (not) + ಸಾಧ್ಯ (possible).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಸಾಧ್ಯ** (*asādhya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *asādhya*, "not to be accomplished", from *a-*, "not", and *sādhya*, "to be done" (Monier-Williams).
