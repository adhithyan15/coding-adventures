<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-C25-mahino-reading","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-MAHINO-01","GU-LEX-MAHINO-01"],"prompt":"Read this word aloud and give its meaning: મહિનો.","answer":"mahino — month","accepted":["mahino month"],"feedback":{"correct":"You read mahino from signs you already knew.","incorrect":"Name the five pieces one at a time, then read the word again."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Uncover **મહિનો** once. **મ**, **હ** and **ન** all come out of the greeting, the **િ** you used yesterday in *divas*, and the **ો** sign from *bapor*. Name the five pieces aloud in order, then
read the whole word once.
