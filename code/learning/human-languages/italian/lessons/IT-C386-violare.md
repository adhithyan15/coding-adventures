## You'll want to know: violare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C386-VIOLAZIONE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**violare** — "to violate, to break (a rule)".

> Chi viola le regole paga una multa.
>
> *Anyone who breaks the rules pays a fine.*

Stress: *vìolo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*violare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *violāre* "to treat with violence, to break" (Lewis & Short), related to *vīs* "force".
