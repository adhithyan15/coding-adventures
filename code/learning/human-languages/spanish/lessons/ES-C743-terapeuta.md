## You'll want to know: el terapeuta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C743-MENTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el terapeuta** — "the therapist".

> Mi terapeuta me escucha mucho.
>
> *My therapist really listens to me.*

Same form for men and women.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terapeuta* comes from Greek *therapeutḗs* "attendant" (EtymDB), from *therapeúein* "to serve, to treat".
