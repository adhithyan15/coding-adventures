## You'll want to know: बीणणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C178-UNGH178-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बीणणो** — *bīṇṇo* — "to pick over (grain)".

> दादी बाजरो बीणै है।
>
> *dādī bājro bīṇai hai.*
>
> Grandmother picks over the millet.

Past (I …): म्हैं बाजरो बीण्यो, *mhaĩ bājro bīṇyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बीणणो** (*bīṇṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vicinoti*, "picks out, chooses"; CDIAL lists Hindi *bīnnā*, "to pick up, choose" (CDIAL 11686). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
