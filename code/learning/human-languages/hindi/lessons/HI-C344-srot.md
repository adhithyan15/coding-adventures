## You'll want to know: स्रोत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C344-PARINAM344-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्रोत** — *srot* — "a source".

> ख़बर का स्रोत क्या है?
>
> *khabar kā srot kyā hai?*
>
> What is the source of the news?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्रोत** (*srot*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *srotas*, "a stream, a current" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for the source of anything, news included.
