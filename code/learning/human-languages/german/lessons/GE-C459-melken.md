## You'll want to know: melken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C459-BAUERNHOF-05]; assesses=[] -->

**melken** — "to milk".

> Der Bauer melkt die Kühe um fünf Uhr morgens.
>
> *The farmer milks the cows at five in the morning.*

Past: *melkte*, *hat gemolken*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*melken* is inherited from Old High German *melchan* and Proto-Germanic *melkaną* (EtymDB); English *milk* is a cousin.
