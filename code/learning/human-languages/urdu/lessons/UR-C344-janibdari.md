## You'll want to know: جانبداری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C344-BARABAR344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جانبداری** — *jānibdārī* — "bias; favouritism".

> اس فیصلے میں جانبداری تھی۔
>
> *is faisle meṅ jānibdārī thī.*
>
> There was bias in this decision.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جانبداری** (*jānibdārī*) is usually traced to Persian *jānibdārī*, "taking a side": Arabic *jānib*, "a side", with Persian *-dārī*, "holding".
