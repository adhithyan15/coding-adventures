## You'll want to know: l'abbonamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C418-BIGLIETTERIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'abbonamento** — "the season ticket, the pass; the subscription".

> Ho l'abbonamento mensile per il tram.
>
> *I have a monthly pass for the tram.*

For a newspaper or a gym too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abbonamento* is built on *abbonare* 'to subscribe', which was borrowed from French *abonner* (EtymDB).
