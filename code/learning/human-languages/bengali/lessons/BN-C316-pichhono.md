# পিছোনো (pichhono) — to fall back; to retreat

## You'll want to know: পিছোনো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C316-GHERA316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**পিছোনো** — *pichhono* — "to fall back; to retreat".

> খাবার শেষ হলে শত্রুরা পিছোল।
>
> *khābār shesh hôle shôtrurā pichholô.*
>
> When their food ran out, the enemy fell back.

Past (he/she …): সেই সেনাবাহিনী পিছোল, *sei senābāhinī pichholô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পিছোনো** (*pichhono*) is built on পিছে, behind — CDIAL 7990, a Middle Indo-Aryan *paśca*, 'the hinder part', which lists Bengali *pichā*, 'the rear' — as **পিছনে** is: to move to the back.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
