# The same writing, with a clock

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-ESSERE-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You wrote your own line with as long as you liked.

[PAUSE 1s] A task you can only do untimed is not yet a task you can do.

## Writing — timed production
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-MI-CHIAMO-04, IT-GRAMMAR-IL-MIO-NOME-02, IT-LEX-ETA-02, IT-LEX-ESSERE-02, IT-GRAMMAR-ESSERE-03, IT-LEX-NONO-02, IT-LEX-C44-FAMIGLIA1-03] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: timed-assessment-production -->

**Whose numbers these are.** This book's A1 writing paper is its own: a
project-defined step towards CILS A1, not a copy of it. Its numbers are in
`task-shapes/a1.json`. The paper runs **20 minutes** and holds **two** parts:
a practical form of **six to eight** short fields, then a message of **30 to
40 words** to a named reader, such as **il nonno**, for a stated purpose. The
message has a floor and a ceiling: under thirty words it is not done, and past
forty you spend time the task did not ask for. Spelling is marked in both
parts, and a held double consonant changes the word: **nono** is not
**nonno**.

[PAUSE 2s] Today you rehearse the first part only, against a short clock.
Set a **three-minute timer**, then fill six fields, in Italian:

1. your name;
2. your surname;
3. your age with **ho … anni** (in figures if it is past twenty: this book
   has spelled numbers only that far);
4. one person in your family;
5. one fact about you with **è**;
6. one more fact you can already write.

[PAUSE 2s] When the three-minute timer rings, **stop**, even mid-word.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-NONO-02, IT-LEX-C44-FAMIGLIA1-03, IT-LEX-ESSERE-02, IT-GRAMMAR-ESSERE-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Spend at most one minute checking, and do not rewrite the timed
answer. Make it one pass for spelling, which the paper marks in both parts.
Italian spelling asks two things above all. A **doubled consonant** is held,
and it changes the word: **nono** is *ninth*, **nonno** is *grandfather*. And a
**final stressed vowel** carries its accent: **è**.

[PAUSE 2s] Now the rule worth carrying out of this lesson. **CILS A1**, the real
examination this book points at, from the Università per Stranieri di Siena,
has **five** parts: listening, reading, a part on the structures of
communication, writing and speaking.

[PAUSE 2s] Each part is scored out of **12**, and you need **7 in every one**.
A strong reading part does not carry a weak writing part. So the part that
decides your result is **the one you are worst at**, not the average.

This book does not repeat how many minutes or words CILS sets for its own
writing part. Check the current guide from Siena before you book.
