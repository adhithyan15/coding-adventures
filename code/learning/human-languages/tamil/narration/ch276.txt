Tamil, chapter 276: Darkness, Shade, Machine, Sofa, Screwdriver.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
இருட்டு (iruṭṭu) — darkness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a plant, then the Tamil for a creeper.

You'll want to know: இருட்டு.
இருட்டு — iruṭṭu — "darkness".

The word, taken apart.
இருட்டு (iruṭṭu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 2552 lists Malayalam iruṭṭu, "darkness" and Telugu irulu. The same entry holds iravu, "night".
