## You'll want to know: ほうしん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C369-JUUYOU369-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうしん** — *hōshin* — "a policy; a course of action".

> **ほうしんは、しゃちょうの いけんに よります。** — *hōshin wa, shachō no iken ni yorimasu* — "The policy depends on what the company president thinks."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうしん** (*hōshin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hō*, "direction" (Middle Chinese *pjang*) and *shin*, "needle" (*tsyim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
