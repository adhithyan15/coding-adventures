## You'll want to know: o javali
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C460-SELVAGENS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o javali** — "the wild boar".

> À noite, os javalis descem até às hortas.
>
> *At night, the wild boar come down to the vegetable gardens.*

Feminine: *a javalina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*javali* comes from Andalusian Arabic *jabalī* 'wild boar' (EtymDB), literally 'of the mountain', short for 'mountain pig'.
