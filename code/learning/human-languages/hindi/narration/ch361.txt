Hindi, chapter 361: Research.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शोध (śodh) — research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to threaten, then the Hindi for to coax.

You'll want to know: शोध.
शोध — śodh — "research".
मीरा पानी पर शोध कर रही है। Mīrā pānī par śodh kar rahī hai. Mira is doing research on water.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
शोध (śodh) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit śodha, "purification, cleansing" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams does not give the sense of research, which is usually taken as a modern Hindi one.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: वैज्ञानिक.
वैज्ञानिक — vaijñānik — "a scientist".
वैज्ञानिक नई दवा पर काम कर रहे हैं। vaijñānik naī davā par kām kar rahe haiṁ. Scientists are working on a new medicine.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
वैज्ञानिक (vaijñānik) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit vaijñānika, "proficient, learned" (Monier-Williams). The sense of a scientist is a modern one that Monier-Williams does not give.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मापना (māpnā) — to gauge, to measure (precisely).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an experiment, then the Hindi for a scientist.

You'll want to know: मापना.
मापना — māpnā — "to gauge, to measure (precisely)".
दर्ज़ी ने मेरी कमर मापी। darzī ne merī kamar māpī. The tailor measured my waist.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कमर मापी, usne kamar māpī.

The word, taken apart.
मापना (māpnā) is inherited from māpyate, "is measured", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10054).

The word, taken apart.
प्रयोगशाला (prayogśālā) is built from prayoga, "use, application", and śālā, "a hall, a house" (Monier-Williams): a hall for experiments.
