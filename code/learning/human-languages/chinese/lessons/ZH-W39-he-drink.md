## Script — a mouth, a sun and a wrap
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HE-DRINK-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 喝

**Three pieces: 口 on the left, then 日, then the wrap under it.** Do not count to twelve; count three.

**口, three strokes**, small, as in 吃.

**日, four strokes**, short and wide, at the top right.

**The wrap, five strokes.** A short left-falling stroke. A horizontal that turns down and hooks, making a roof. Inside it, a small left-falling stroke and a dot. Then a corner stroke, down and along, closing them in.

3 + 4 + 5 = twelve, and the first seven are two shapes you know.
