## You'll want to know: رشح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C420-ISTIJABA420-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رشح** — *rashshaḥa* — "to nominate".

> رشح المدير أختي لوظيفة جديدة.
>
> *rashshaḥa al-mudīr ukhtī li-waẓīfa jadīda.*
>
> The manager nominated my sister for a new job.

Present: يرشح, *yurashshiḥ*.

It takes لـ: رشحه لـ ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رشح** (*rashshaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-sh-ḥ*, "to sweat, to ooze" (Lane). It is Form II; Lane gives it for training young ones until they are strong: to put someone forward, to nominate.

## What you've built
