# ઘાયલ (ghāyal) — injured

## You'll want to know: ઘાયલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C293-AKASMAT293-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘાયલ** — *ghāyal* — "injured".

> આ અકસ્માતમાં ત્રણ લોકો ઘાયલ થયા.
>
> *ā akasmātmā̃ traṇ loko ghāyal thayā.*
>
> Three people were hurt in this accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘાયલ** (*ghāyal*) is built on *ghāy*, a wound, from Sanskrit *ghāta*, "a blow"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *ghāyo*, a wounded person, under that word (CDIAL 4460). A person **ઘાયલ થાય છે**, gets hurt.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
