## You'll want to know: amalgamare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C338-FRUSTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**amalgamare** — "to blend, to mix until smooth".

> Amalgama bene tutti gli ingredienti.
>
> *Blend all the ingredients well.*

A recipe word: until the mixture is even.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amalgamare* is built on *amalgama*, from Medieval Latin *amalgama*, a word of the alchemists; its deeper origin is not settled (EtymDB has no entry).
