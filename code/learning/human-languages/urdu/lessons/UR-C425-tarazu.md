## You'll want to know: ترازو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C425-TARAZU425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ترازو** — *tarāzū* — "a pair of scales; a balance".

> دکاندار ترازو پر سیب تولتا ہے۔
>
> *dukāndār tarāzū par seb toltā hai.*
>
> The shopkeeper weighs apples on the scales.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترازو** (*tarāzū*) is usually traced to Persian *tarāzū*, "a balance, scales".
