## You'll want to know: অফিস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C411-DAKGHOR411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অফিস** — *ôphis* — "an office".

> মীরার অফিস স্টেশনের কাছে।
>
> *mīrār ôphis sṭeshôner kāchhe.*
>
> Mira's office is near the station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অফিস** (*ôphis*) is borrowed from English *office*; the first letter is অ, said as an open ô.
