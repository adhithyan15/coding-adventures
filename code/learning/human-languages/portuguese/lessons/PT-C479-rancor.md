## You'll want to know: o rancor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C479-PERDAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o rancor** — "grudge, resentment".

> Não guardo rancor a ninguém.
>
> *I do not hold a grudge against anyone.*

*Guardar rancor a alguém* — to bear someone a grudge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rancor* comes from Late Latin *rancor* "rancidness, a grudge" (EtymDB), built on *rancēre* "to be rotten".
