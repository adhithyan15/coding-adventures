## You'll want to know: जन्मदिन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C119-ABOUT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जन्मदिन** (*janmdin*) — "a birthday", literally "birth-day".

**दिन** is the day you know. **जन्मदिन की शुभकामनाएँ** — "happy birthday", with the good wishes
you have.
