## You'll want to know: schaukeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C422-KREUZFAHRT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**schaukeln** — "to rock, to swing".

> Bei Wind schaukelt das Schiff hin und her.
>
> *In the wind the ship rocks to and fro.*

*Die Schaukel* — the swing in a playground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schaukeln* is built on *Schaukel* 'swing'. EtymDB lists the verb without a source, and the older history of the word is not settled.
