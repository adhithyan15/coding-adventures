## You'll want to know: персонал
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C435-OBSLUZHIVANIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**персонал** (*personál*) — "staff, personnel".

> Персонал гостиницы был очень вежливый.
>
> *Personál gostínitsy byl óchen vézhlivyy.*
>
> The hotel staff were very polite.

Masculine and singular in Russian: *персонал был*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*персонал* is usually traced to German *Personal* 'staff', from Medieval Latin *persōnālis* 'of a person', on *persōna* 'a mask, a person' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
