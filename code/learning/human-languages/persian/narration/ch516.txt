Persian, chapter 516: Keeping Fit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نرمش کردن (narmesh kardan) — to stretch, to do light exercises.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to wrestle, then the Persian for weightlifting.

You'll want to know: نرمش کردن.
نرمش کردن (narmesh kardan) — "to stretch, to do light exercises".
قبل از دویدن نرمش میکنم. qabl az davidan narmesh mikonam. I stretch before running.
narmesh 'suppleness', from narm 'soft', + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
narmesh is built on native Persian narm, 'soft', with the noun ending -esh: softening the body. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited.

Before the new one: say the Persian for weightlifting, then the Persian for to stretch.

You'll want to know: یوگا.
یوگا (yogâ) — "yoga".
مادرم هفته ای سه بار یوگا میکند. mâdaram hafte-i se bâr yogâ mikonad. My mother does yoga three times a week.
yogâ kardan — to do yoga.

The word, taken apart.
yogâ is a modern loan, usually traced through English to Sanskrit yoga, 'yoking, joining'. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.

تناسب اندام (tanâsob-e andâm) — "fitness, being in shape".
او برای تناسب اندام شنا میکند. u barâ-ye tanâsob-e andâm shenâ mikonad. She swims to keep fit.
tanâsob 'proportion' (Arabic) + andâm 'body, figure' (Persian).

The word, taken apart.
tanâsob-e andâm is built in Persian on tanâsob, 'proportion', a loan usually traced to Arabic tanāsub, and native andâm, 'body, figure'.

پرانرژی (porenerzhi) — "energetic, full of energy".
بچه ها صبحها پرانرژی هستند. bachchehâ sobhhâ porenerzhi hastand. The children are full of energy in the mornings.
por 'full' + enerzhi 'energy'.

The word, taken apart.
porenerzhi is built in Persian on native por, 'full', and enerzhi, 'energy', which was borrowed from French énergie (EtymDB).
