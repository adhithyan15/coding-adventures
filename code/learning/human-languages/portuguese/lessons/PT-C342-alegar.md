## You'll want to know: alegar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C342-AFIRMAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**alegar** — "to claim, to allege".

> Ele alegou que estava doente.
>
> *He claimed that he was ill.*

Often of an excuse that may not be true.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alegar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *allēgāre* "to send with a commission, to bring forward, to cite" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *ad* and *lēgāre* "to send, to depute".
