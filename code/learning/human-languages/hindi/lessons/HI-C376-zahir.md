# ज़ाहिर (zāhir) — evident, plain

## You'll want to know: ज़ाहिर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C376-ZAHIR376-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़ाहिर** — *zāhir* — "evident, plain".

> ज़ाहिर है कि अगर तुम सोओगे नहीं तो थक जाओगे।
>
> *zāhir hai ki agar tum sooge nahīṃ to thak jāoge.*
>
> It's plain that if you don't sleep, you'll get tired.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़ाहिर** (*zāhir*) is usually traced to Arabic *ẓāhir*, "outward, apparent", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
