## You'll want to know: 早年
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C322-BACKTHEN322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**早年** — *zǎonián* — "one's early years; long ago".

> **他早年在外国工作过** *tā zǎonián zài wàiguó gōngzuò guo* — In his early years he worked abroad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**早年** (*zǎonián*) is built from 早 'early' and 年 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsawX* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'morning'.
