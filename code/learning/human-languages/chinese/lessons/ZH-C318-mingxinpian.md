## You'll want to know: míngxìnpiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C318-LETTER318-03]; assesses=[] -->

*míngxìnpiàn* — "postcard".

> *tā cóng Zhōngguó gěi wǒ jì le yì zhāng míngxìnpiàn* — He sent me a postcard from China.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*míngxìnpiàn* is built from 明 'bright, open', *xìn* 'a letter' and *piàn* 'a flat piece', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjaeng*, *sinH* and *phenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'light' and the last 'half' and 'a split piece of wood'.
