Urdu, chapter 418: Accounts and Instalments.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حساب (hisāb) — an account; a reckoning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a cheque, then the Urdu for to withdraw (money).

You'll want to know: حساب.
حساب — hisāb — "an account; a reckoning".
دکاندار ہر شام اپنا حساب لکھتا ہے۔ dukāndār har shām apnā hisāb likhtā hai. Every evening the shopkeeper writes up his accounts.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
حساب (hisāb) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥisāb, "counting, reckoning".

Lesson 2 of 5.
سود (sūd) — interest (on money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to withdraw (money), then the Urdu for a reckoning.

You'll want to know: سود.
سود — sūd — "interest (on money)".
بینک قرض پر سود لیتا ہے۔ baink qarz par sūd letā hai. The bank charges interest on a loan.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
سود (sūd) is usually traced to Persian sūd, "profit, gain"; in Urdu it is the interest on a loan or on savings.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ادائیگی (adāigī) — a payment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for interest (on money), then the Urdu for an instalment.

You'll want to know: ادائیگی.
ادائیگی — adāigī — "a payment".
قرض کی ادائیگی اگلے سال تک ہے۔ qarz kī adāigī agle sāl tak hai. The loan has to be paid back by next year.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ادائیگی (adāigī) is built on adā, "a payment, a carrying out", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic adāʾ, "discharging (a debt)", with the Persian ending -gī.

Lesson 5 of 5.
گھٹانا (ghaṭānā) — to bring down (a price); to reduce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an instalment, then the Urdu for a payment.

You'll want to know: گھٹانا.
گھٹانا — ghaṭānā — "to bring down (a price); to reduce".
بینک نے سود گھٹایا۔ baink ne sūd ghaṭāyā. The bank lowered its interest rate.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے قیمت گھٹائی, us ne qīmat ghaṭāī.

The word, taken apart.
گھٹانا (ghaṭānā) is built on ghaṭnā, "to decrease", which CDIAL lists under ghaṭṭati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4415): this is the form for making something smaller.
