Marathi, chapter 261: Ugly, Tired, Hungry, Thirsty, Short-tempered.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कुरूप (kurūp) — ugly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for unmarried, then the Marathi for married.

You'll want to know: कुरूप.
कुरूप — kurūp — "ugly".

The word, taken apart.
कुरूप (kurūp) is Sanskrit kurūpa, "ill-formed, ugly", a tatsama: ku-, "bad", and rūpa, "form, beauty" (CDIAL 10803).
