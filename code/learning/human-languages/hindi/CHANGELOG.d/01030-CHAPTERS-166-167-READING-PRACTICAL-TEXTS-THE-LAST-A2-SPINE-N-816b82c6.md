## Chapters 166-167: reading practical texts, the last A2 spine node

- Hindi had realized four of the five A2 spine nodes. The fifth,
  `SPINE-READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS` ("find what I need in everyday written
  material ... and act on what it asks of me"), had no segment. Two chapters
  now stand on it.
- **Chapter 166**, *a notice, a message, opening times, an advert, a repair*:
  सूचना, संदेश, समय, विज्ञापन, मरम्मत.
- **Chapter 167**, *rent, a date, a number, a floor, a receipt*: किराया,
  तारीख़, नंबर, मंज़िल, रसीद.
- Each lesson reads one short practical text in Devanagari and says what to
  do about it (**आज दुकान बंद है।**: come back tomorrow). The texts use only
  words the book has already taught plus the lesson's own headword, and only
  taught letters.
- Two reviews close chapter 167, so every new atom is revisited twice.
- Level gate at A2: spine nodes 1 -> 0. Vocabulary (540 short) and verb
  vocabulary (49 short) remain.
