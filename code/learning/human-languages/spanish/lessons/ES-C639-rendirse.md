## You'll want to know: rendirse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C639-FINAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rendirse** — "to give up, to surrender".

> No te rindas.
>
> *Do not give up.*

Like *pedir*: *me rindo, se rindió*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rendirse* is built on *rendir*, inherited from spoken Latin *rendere*, Latin *reddere* 'to give back, to yield' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
