Persian, chapter 444: Asking the way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نشانی (neshâni) — an address (formal word).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a tip, then the Persian for to thank.

You'll want to know: نشانی.
نشانی (neshâni) — "an address (formal word)".
نشانی (neshâni) هتل را به راننده دادم. Neshâni-ye hotel râ be rânande dâdam. I gave the driver the hotel's address.
The formal word for âdres.

The word, taken apart.
neshâni is built from native neshân 'sign, mark', usually traced to Middle Persian nišān, with the ending -i. EtymDB lists neshâni without older forms.

Lesson 2 of 5.
پرس و جو کردن (pors-o ju kardan) — to ask around, to make inquiries.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to thank, then the Persian for an address.

You'll want to know: پرس و جو کردن.
پرس و جو کردن (pors-o ju kardan) — "to ask around, to make inquiries".
در محله پرس و جو کردیم و خانه را پیدا کردیم. Dar mahalle pors-o ju kardim va khâne râ peydâ kardim. We asked around the neighbourhood and found the house.
pors 'ask' (from porsidan) + o 'and' + ju 'seek' (from jostan) + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
pors-o ju kardan is built from two native present stems, pors of porsidan 'to ask', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root preḱ- 'to ask', and ju of jostan 'to seek', with kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
بلد بودن (balad budan) — to know (the way, how to do something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an address, then the Persian for to ask around.

You'll want to know: بلد بودن.
بلد بودن (balad budan) — "to know (the way, how to do something)".
راننده تاکسی همه کوچه های شهر را بلد است. Rânande-ye tâksi hame-ye kuche-hâ-ye shahr râ balad ast. The taxi driver knows every lane in town.
Very common in speech: râh râ balad nistam — I don't know the way. Balad, a guide, is someone who knows the land.

The word, taken apart.
balad budan is built from balad 'knowing the way; a guide', a loan usually traced to Arabic balad 'land, country' (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), and the native verb budan 'to be'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
گم شدن (gom shodan) — to get lost; to go missing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to ask around, then the Persian for to know.

You'll want to know: گم شدن.
گم شدن (gom shodan) — "to get lost; to go missing".
در بازار بزرگ گم شدیم. Dar bâzâr-e bozorg gom shodim. We got lost in the big bazaar.
gom 'lost' + shodan. Gom kardan is to lose something.

The word, taken apart.
gom shodan is built from native gom 'lost, missing', which EtymDB lists, and the native verb shodan 'to become'.
