## You'll want to know: ફૂગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C518-PASTI518-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂગ** — *phūg* — "mould (a fungus)".

> ભીની દીવાલ પર ફૂગ આવી ગઈ છે.
>
> *bhīnī dīvāl par phūg āvī gaī che.*
>
> Mould has appeared on the damp wall.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂગ** (*phūg*) grows where it is damp; it "comes" on a thing: **ફૂગ આવી ગઈ**. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
