# はつもうで (hatsumōde) — the New Year shrine visit; the first shrine visit of the year

## You'll want to know: はつもうで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C295-HATSUMOUDE295-01]; assesses=[] -->

**はつもうで** — *hatsumōde* — "the New Year shrine visit; the first shrine visit of the year".

> **しょうがつに かぞくで はつもうでに いきました。** — *shōgatsu ni kazoku de hatsumōde ni ikimashita* — "At the new year the family made its first shrine visit."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はつもうで** (*hatsumōde*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hatsu*, "first", and *mōde*, "a visit to a shrine" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
