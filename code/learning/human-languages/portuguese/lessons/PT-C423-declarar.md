## You'll want to know: declarar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C423-CONTROLO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**declarar** — "to declare".

> Na alfândega, declaramos o que comprámos.
>
> *At customs, we declare what we bought.*

*Nada a declarar* — nothing to declare.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*declarar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēclārāre* 'to make clear' (EtymDB), built on *clārus* 'clear'.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
