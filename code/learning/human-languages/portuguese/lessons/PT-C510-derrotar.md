## You'll want to know: derrotar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C510-RESULTADO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**derrotar** — "to defeat, to beat".

> A equipa da casa derrotou o campeão.
>
> *The home team beat the champions.*

The noun *a derrota* means defeat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*derrotar* is linked in EtymDB with French *dérouter* "to throw off course, to rout", built on *route* "a road".
