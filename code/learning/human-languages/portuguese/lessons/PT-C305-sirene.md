## You'll want to know: a sirene
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C305-SOCORRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a sirene** — "the siren".

> Ouvi a sirene de uma ambulância.
>
> *I heard an ambulance siren.*

Also *a sirena*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sirene* was borrowed through French *sirène* from Latin *sīrēn*, from Greek *seirḗn*, the singing creature of old stories (Lewis & Short).
