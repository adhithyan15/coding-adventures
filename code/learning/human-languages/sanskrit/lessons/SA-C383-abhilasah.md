## You'll want to know: अभिलाषः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C383-SANKALPA383-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिलाषः** — *abhilāṣaḥ* — "a heartfelt wish".

> यदि तव अभिलाषः शुद्धः, तर्हि सः पूर्णः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi tava abhilāṣaḥ śuddhaḥ, tarhi saḥ pūrṇaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If your wish is pure, it will be fulfilled.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिलाषः** (*abhilāṣaḥ*) is built on *abhi*, "towards", and the root √laṣ, "to desire"; MW gives *abhi-lāṣa* "desire, wish, affection" (MW).

## What you've built
