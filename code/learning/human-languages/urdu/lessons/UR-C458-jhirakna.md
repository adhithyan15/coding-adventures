## You'll want to know: جھڑکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C458-PATIENCE458-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جھڑکنا** — *jhiṛaknā* — "to snap at; to rebuke".

> ابو نے بھائی کو جھڑکا۔
>
> *abbū ne bhāī ko jhiṛkā.*
>
> Dad snapped at my brother.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو جھڑکا, *us ne bachche ko jhiṛkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھڑکنا** (*jhiṛaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhiṛaknā*, "to shake, to scold", under *jhaṭ*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5327).
