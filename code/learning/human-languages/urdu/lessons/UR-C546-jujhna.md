## You'll want to know: جوجھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C546-MASHAQQAT546-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جوجھنا** — *jūjhnā* — "to grapple with (a hard task)".

> اگر ہم ساتھ جوجھے تو یہ مشکل ختم ہو گی۔
>
> *agar ham sāth jūjhe to yih mushkil khatm ho gī.*
>
> If we grapple with it together, this trouble will end.

Past (he …): وہ بیماری سے جوجھا, *voh bīmārī se jūjhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جوجھنا** (*jūjhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jūjhnā*, "to fight", under Sanskrit *yudhyatē*, "fights" (CDIAL 10502).
