Urdu, chapter 399: Security Checks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حفاظت (hifāzat) — protection; safety.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to tighten, then the Urdu for postponed.

You'll want to know: حفاظت.
حفاظت — hifāzat — "protection; safety".
مسافروں کی حفاظت ضروری ہے۔ musāfiroṅ kī hifāzat zarūrī hai. Keeping passengers safe matters.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
حفاظت (hifāzat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥifāẓa, "guarding, keeping safe"; محفوظ, "safe", belongs to the same family.

is built on talāsh, "a search", usually traced to Persian talāsh and perhaps further to Turkic, with the ending -ī: تلاش کرنا is to look for something, تلاشی (talāshī) the search of a bag or a person.

Lesson 5 of 5.
چڑھانا (chaṛhānā) — to put on board; to put up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for customs (at a border), then the Urdu for forbidden.

You'll want to know: چڑھانا.
چڑھانا — chaṛhānā — "to put on board; to put up".
مزدوروں نے صندوق ٹرک پر چڑھائے۔ mazdūroṅ ne sandūq ṭrak par chaṛhāe. The labourers put the trunks up onto the lorry.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سامان اوپر چڑھایا, us ne sāmān ūpar chaṛhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
چڑھانا (chaṛhānā) is built on چڑھنا, "to climb", which comes from caḍhati, "rises", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4578): this is the form for raising something else.
