German, chapter 451: Gute Angebote.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
reduzieren — to reduce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to redeem, then the German for to pay out.

You'll want to know: reduzieren.
reduzieren — "to reduce".
Im Januar reduziert das Kaufhaus alle Preise. In January the department store reduces all its prices.
Past participle without ge-: reduziert — reduced, on price tags.

The word, taken apart.
reduzieren is a learned borrowing from Latin redūcere 'to lead back, to bring back' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the German word.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
das Sonderangebot — the special offer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: das Sonderangebot.
das Sonderangebot — "the special offer".
Die Erdbeeren sind diese Woche im Sonderangebot. Strawberries are on special offer this week.
Plural: die Sonderangebote.

The word, taken apart.
Sonderangebot is built from sonder- 'special, apart', inherited from Proto-Germanic sundraz (a cousin of English asunder), and Angebot 'offer', from anbieten 'to offer'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Schnäppchen — the bargain.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: das Schnäppchen.
das Schnäppchen — "the bargain".
Die Jacke war ein Schnäppchen: nur zwanzig Euro. The jacket was a bargain: only twenty euros.
Same form in the plural. Ein Schnäppchen machen — to get a bargain.

The word, taken apart.
Schnäppchen is built on schnappen 'to snap up, to grab' (EtymDB lists English snap as a cousin) with -chen 'little': something quickly snapped up.

Lesson 4 of 5.
feilschen — to haggle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the special offer, then the German for the bargain.

You'll want to know: feilschen.
feilschen — "to haggle".
Auf dem Markt feilscht er um jeden Euro. At the market he haggles over every euro.
With um: um den Preis feilschen.

The word, taken apart.
feilschen is usually linked to the old adjective feil 'for sale', but the details are not settled, and EtymDB does not list the word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Flohmarkt — the flea market.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: der Flohmarkt.
der Flohmarkt — "the flea market".
Am Sonntag gibt es einen Flohmarkt am Fluss. On Sunday there is a flea market by the river.
Plural: die Flohmärkte.

The word, taken apart.
Flohmarkt is built from Floh 'flea', inherited from Proto-Germanic flauhaz and a cousin of English flea, and Markt 'market', from Latin mercātus (EtymDB).
