## You'll want to know: yīngxióng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C327-HERO327-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yīngxióng* — "hero".

> *wǒ bàba shì wǒ de yīngxióng* — My dad is my hero.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yīngxióng* is built from *yīng* and *xióng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jaeng* and *hjuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a flower' and 'outstanding', and the second 'male' as against female.

## What you've built
