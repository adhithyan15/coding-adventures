## You'll want to know: लुब्धः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C537-PASAKA537-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लुब्धः** — *lubdhaḥ* — "covetous; greedy for gain".

> यदि त्वं लुब्धः असि, तर्हि त्वं सर्वं धनं हारयसि।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ lubdhaḥ asi, tarhi tvaṃ sarvaṃ dhanaṃ hārayasi.*
>
> If you are greedy, you lose all your money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लुब्धः** (*lubdhaḥ*) comes from the root √lubh, "to go astray, to long for eagerly", as its past participle; MW gives *lubdha* as bewildered, and as greedy, covetous (MW).
