Tamil, chapter 344: Magic and Monsters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அரக்கன் (arakkaṉ) — a monster; a demon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to make up, then the Tamil for a proverb.

You'll want to know: அரக்கன்.
அரக்கன் — arakkaṉ — "a monster; a demon".
கதையில் ஒரு அரக்கன் காட்டில் வாழ்ந்தான். kataiyil oru arakkaṉ kāṭṭil vāḻntāṉ. In the story a monster lived in the forest.
The female is அரக்கி.

The word, taken apart.
அரக்கன் (arakkaṉ) is usually traced to Sanskrit rākṣasa, "demon" (CDIAL 10672), with the Tamil அ- before a loan in r-.

மந்திரவாதி இளவரசனை ஒரு தவளையாக மாற்றினான். mantiravāti iḷavaracaṉai oru tavaḷaiyāka māṟṟiṉāṉ. The sorcerer turned the prince into a frog.

Lesson 4 of 5.
சபி (capi) — to curse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a fairy, then the Tamil for a sorcerer.

You'll want to know: சபி.
சபி — capi — "to curse".
கோபமான முனிவர் மன்னரைச் சபித்தார். kōpamāṉa muṉivar maṉṉaraic capittār. The angry sage cursed the king.
Past (I …): சபித்தேன், capittēṉ.
The noun is சாபம், a curse.

The word, taken apart.
சபி (capi) is a Tamil verb built on Sanskrit śapati, "curses, swears" (CDIAL 12289).

Lesson 5 of 5.
உருமாறு (urumāṟu) — to change shape; to transform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a sorcerer, then the Tamil for to curse.

You'll want to know: உருமாறு.
உருமாறு — urumāṟu — "to change shape; to transform".
அந்தத் தவளை ஒரு இளவரசனாக உருமாறியது. antat tavaḷai oru iḷavaracaṉāka urumāṟiyatu. That frog changed into a prince.
Past (I …): உருமாறினேன், urumāṟiṉēṉ.
What it becomes takes -ஆக.

The word, taken apart.
உருமாறு (urumāṟu) is a Tamil compound built from உரு, "form, shape" (DEDR 657, which asks whether it comes from Sanskrit rūpa), and மாறு, "to become changed" (DEDR 4834).
