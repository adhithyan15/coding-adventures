## You'll want to know: تضخم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C383-IQTISAD383-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تضخم** — *taḍakhkhum* — "inflation".

> يخاف الناس من التضخم.
>
> *yakhāfu an-nās min at-taḍakhkhum.*
>
> People are afraid of inflation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تضخم** (*taḍakhkhum*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-kh-m*, "to be large, bulky" (Lane). *Taḍakhkhum* is the Form V verbal noun, swelling: inflation.
