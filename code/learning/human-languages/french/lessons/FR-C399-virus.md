## You'll want to know: le virus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C399-PANNE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le virus** — "the virus".

> Si tu ouvres ce fichier, tu risques d'attraper un virus.
>
> *If you open that file, you risk getting a virus.*

For computers and for illness. The *s* is said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*virus* was taken whole from Latin *vīrus* "slime, poison" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short); the computer sense came from English.
