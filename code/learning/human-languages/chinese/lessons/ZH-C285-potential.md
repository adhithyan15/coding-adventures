## Grammar Lens: 找不到 and 找得到, can't and can
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-POTENTIAL-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C197-WAY197-02, ZH-LEX-C151-TINGDONG-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-RESULT-COMPLEMENT-01, ZH-LEX-C151-KANDAO-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-COMPLEMENT-01, ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-01, ZH-LEX-C151-ZUOWAN-01] -->

> **不 between a verb and its result says you can't manage it; 得 there says you can.**

> **我找不到我的手机** *wǒ zhǎo bu dào wǒ de shǒujī* — I can't find my phone.
> *wǒ tīng bu dǒng* — I can't understand what's being said.

找到, 看到, *tīngdǒng*: you know verbs with a result after them. Put 不 in the middle, said light, and the result is out of reach: 找不到. Put 得 there, also light, and it is within reach: **你找得到吗** *nǐ zhǎo de dào ma*?

Keep to the results *dào*, *dǒng* and *wán* for now: *zuò bu wán*, more than you can finish.
