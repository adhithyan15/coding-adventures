## You'll want to know: かいいん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C335-DANTAI335-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かいいん** — *kaiin* — "a club member; a member".

> **かいいんに なったら、りょうきんが やすく なります。** — *kaiin ni nattara, ryōkin ga yasuku narimasu* — "If you become a member, the fee goes down."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいいん** (*kaiin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "meeting" (Middle Chinese *hwajH*) and *in*, "member" (*hjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
