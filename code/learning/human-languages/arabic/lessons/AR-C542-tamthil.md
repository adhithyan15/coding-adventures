## You'll want to know: تمثيل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C542-MASRAH542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تمثيل** — *tamthīl* — "acting".

> درست أختي التمثيل في الجامعة.
>
> *darasat ukhtī at-tamthīl fī al-jāmiʿa.*
>
> My sister studied acting at university.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تمثيل** (*tamthīl*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-th-l*, "to be like, to resemble" (Lane). Mumaththil, an actor, which you know, is from the same root: acting is making a likeness.
