## You'll want to know: ਦੁਖਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C323-BODY323-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੁਖਣਾ** — *dukhṇā* — "to ache".

> ਅੱਜ ਮੇਰੀ ਪਿੱਠ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੁਖਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *ajj merī piṭṭh bahut dukhdī hai.*
>
> My back aches a lot today.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਦੰਦ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਦੁਖਿਆ, *oh dand rāt nū̃ dukhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੁਖਣਾ** (*dukhṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *duḥkhati*, "pains"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dukhṇā*, "to hurt", under it (CDIAL 6376).
