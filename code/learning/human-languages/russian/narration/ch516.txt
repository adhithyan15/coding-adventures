Russian, chapter 516: Tune and Rhythm.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
мелодия (melódiya) — a melody, a tune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a string, then the Russian for to tune.

You'll want to know: мелодия.
мелодия (melódiya) — "a melody, a tune".
Эта мелодия мне очень знакома. Éta melódiya mne óchen znakóma. This tune is very familiar to me.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
мелодия (melódiya) comes from Greek melōidía 'singing, chanting', built on mélos 'song' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ритм (ritm) — rhythm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to tune, then the Russian for a melody.

You'll want to know: ритм.
ритм (ritm) — "rhythm".
Танцоры двигались в ритме музыки. Tantsóry dvígalis v rítme múzyki. The dancers moved to the rhythm of the music.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ритм (ritm) was borrowed from Latin rhythmus, from Greek rhythmós 'measured flow', built on rhéō 'to flow' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 6.

You'll want to know: припев.
припев (pripév) — "a chorus, a refrain".
Все в зале пели припев вместе с певцом. Vse v zále péli pripév vméste s pevtsóm. Everyone in the hall sang the chorus with the singer.
Masculine. The verse is куплет.

The word, taken apart.
припев (pripév) is built on при- and the root of петь 'to sing'; EtymDB has no entry.

Imperfective; present напеваю. Perfective напеть.

Lesson 5 of 6.
фальшивить (falshívit) — to sing out of tune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a chorus, then the Russian for to hum.

You'll want to know: фальшивить.
фальшивить (falshívit) — "to sing out of tune".
Он громко поёт, но немного фальшивит. On grómko poyót, no nemnógo falshívit. He sings loudly but a little out of tune.
Imperfective; present фальшивлю, фальшивишь (в becomes вл).

The word, taken apart.
фальшивить (falshívit) is derived from фальшь 'falseness' (EtymDB), which was borrowed from German falsch 'false'.
