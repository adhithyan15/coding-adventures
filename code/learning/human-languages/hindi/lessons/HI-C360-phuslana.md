## You'll want to know: फुसलाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C360-APATTI360-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फुसलाना** — *phuslānā* — "to coax, to wheedle".

> बच्चा दवा नहीं पी रहा था, इसलिए माँ ने उसे फुसलाया।
>
> *baccā davā nahīṃ pī rahā thā, isliye māṁ ne use phuslāyā.*
>
> The child wouldn't take the medicine, so his mother coaxed him.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को फुसलाया, *usne bacce ko phuslāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फुसलाना** (*phuslānā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *spṛśati*, "touches" (CDIAL 13815). CDIAL lists the Hindi verb, "to cajole", under this entry; the step from touching to coaxing is not spelled out there.
