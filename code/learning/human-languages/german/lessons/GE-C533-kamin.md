## You'll want to know: der Kamin
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C533-GEMUETLICH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kamin** — "fireplace; chimney".

> Im Winter sitzen wir abends vor dem Kamin.
>
> *In winter we sit in front of the fire in the evenings.*

Plural: *die Kamine*. In southern Germany and Austria also the chimney.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kamin* is inherited from Middle High German *kamīn* and Old High German *kemīn*, an early borrowing from Latin *camīnus* "furnace", from Greek *káminos* (EtymDB).
