Punjabi, chapter 229: Knots and Strings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਗੰਢ (gaṇḍh) — a knot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a rope, then the Punjabi for to wipe.

You'll want to know: ਗੰਢ.
ਗੰਢ — gaṇḍh — "a knot".
ਰੱਸੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਗੰਢ ਹੈ। rassī vich ikk gaṇḍh hai. There is a knot in the rope.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੰਢ (gaṇḍh) is inherited from Sanskrit granthi, "knot, bunch"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gaṇḍh, "knot, joint" (CDIAL 4354). CDIAL marks the Punjabi row masculine and the Lahnda row feminine; here ਗੰਢ is taught as feminine, as the Lahnda row has it.
