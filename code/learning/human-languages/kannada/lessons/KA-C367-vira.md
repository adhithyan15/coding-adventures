## You'll want to know: ವೀರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C367-KHALA367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವೀರ** — *vīra* — "a hero; a brave man".

> ಆ ವೀರ ಒಬ್ಬನೇ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯನ್ನು ಕಾಪಾಡಿದನು.
>
> *ā vīra obbanē haḷḷiyannu kāpāḍidanu.*
>
> That hero saved the village on his own.

Plural: ವೀರರು, *vīraru*.

As an adjective: ವೀರ ಯೋಧ, a brave warrior. A heroine is a ವೀರೆ or ವೀರ ಮಹಿಳೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವೀರ** (*vīra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vīra*, "a man, a hero" (CDIAL 12056).
