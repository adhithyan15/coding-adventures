## You'll want to know: plätschern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C455-WASSERFALL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**plätschern** — "to splash, to babble (of water)".

> Im Garten plätschert ein kleiner Brunnen.
>
> *A little fountain splashes in the garden.*

A gentle, pleasant sound of water: *das Plätschern des Wassers*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*plätschern* is a sound word that imitates water splashing; EtymDB does not list it.
