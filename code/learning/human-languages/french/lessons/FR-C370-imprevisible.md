## You'll want to know: imprévisible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C370-IMPREVU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**imprévisible** — "unpredictable".

> Le temps à la montagne est imprévisible.
>
> *The weather in the mountains is unpredictable.*

With *in-*, the opposite of *prévisible*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imprévisible* is built from *in-* "not" and *prévisible*, which rests on *prévoir*, from Latin *praevidēre* "to foresee" (Lewis & Short).
