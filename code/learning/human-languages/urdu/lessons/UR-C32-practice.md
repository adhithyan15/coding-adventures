# Payoff — which one

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-ALOUD, UR-PERFORMANCE-DIGITS-READ, UR-SCRIPT-DIGITS-LTR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Count aloud to ten, read **۷**, **۲۰** and **۱۰۰**, and say which way
the digits ran.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-PERFORMANCE-ORDINALS]; assesses=[UR-LEX-PEHLA, UR-LEX-DUSRA, UR-LEX-TISRA, UR-LEX-CHAUTHA, UR-MORPH-ORDINAL-VAN, UR-MORPH-ORDINAL-RA, UR-ETYMON-PEHLA-FIRST, UR-LEX-BEES, UR-LEX-SAU, UR-LEX-DAS, UR-LEX-PANCH, UR-LEX-CHAR, UR-LEX-TEEN, UR-GRAMMAR-TEENS-IRREGULAR, UR-ETYMON-CHAR-FOUR-PIE, UR-ETYMON-SAU-HUNDRED-PIE, UR-ETYMON-AATH-EIGHT-PIE, UR-ETYMON-NAU-NINE-PIE, UR-ETYMON-DAS-TEN-PIE, UR-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-TEN, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-ZERO, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-ONE, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-TWO, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-THREE, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FOUR, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FIVE, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SIX, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SEVEN, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-EIGHT, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-NINE] -->

1. Say the first four in order, then out of order.
2. **Build**, do not recall: fifth, seventh, tenth, twentieth, hundredth.
3. Say which of the series is not built from its number at all, and say what
   English does in the same place.
4. Use one: name the floor a room is on, and the day of a month a notice gives.
5. Say what the ordinals still cannot reach, and why — the same nine irregular
   teens that the cardinals could not reach either.
