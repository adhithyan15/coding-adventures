## You'll want to know: میانسالی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C333-NOWJAVANI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**میانسالی** (*miyânsâli*) — "middle age".

> پدرم در میانسالی ورزش را شروع کرد.
>
> *Pedaram dar miyânsâli varzesh râ shoru' kard.*
>
> My father took up exercise in middle age.

*miyân* "middle" + *sâl* "year" + *-i*. *Miyânsâl* is "middle-aged".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miyânsâli* is built from Persian *miyân* "middle" and *sâl* "year", with the noun suffix *-i*. All of it is native.
