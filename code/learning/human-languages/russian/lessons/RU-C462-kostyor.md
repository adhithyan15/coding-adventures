## You'll want to know: костёр
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C462-KOSTYOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**костёр** (*kostyór*) — "a campfire, a bonfire".

> Вечером мы пели песни у костра.
>
> *Vécherom my péli pésni u kostrá.*
>
> In the evening we sang songs by the campfire.

Masculine; the ё drops out: *костра*, *костры*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *костёр* is not settled; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
