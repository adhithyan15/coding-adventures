## You'll want to know: транслировать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C286-OSVESHCHAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**транслировать** (*translírovat*) — "to broadcast".

> Матч транслировали по телевизору.
>
> *Match translírovali po televízoru.*
>
> The match was broadcast on television.

One form for both aspects. *Трансляция* — a broadcast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*транслировать* is usually traced to Latin *trānslātus* "carried across", the participle of *trānsferre* "to carry over" (Lewis & Short), with the Russian ending *-ировать*; EtymDB lists no parent.
