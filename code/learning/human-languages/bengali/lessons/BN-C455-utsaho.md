## You'll want to know: উৎসাহ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C455-DHOIRJO455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**উৎসাহ** — *utsāhô* — "enthusiasm; encouragement".

> বাবার উৎসাহে মীরা গান শিখল।
>
> *bābār utsāhe mīrā gān shikhlô.*
>
> With her father's encouragement Mira learned to sing.

উৎসাহ দেওয়া, to encourage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উৎসাহ** (*utsāhô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *utsāha*, "power; strength of will, resolution" (Monier-Williams).
