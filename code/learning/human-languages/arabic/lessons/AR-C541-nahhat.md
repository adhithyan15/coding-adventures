## You'll want to know: نحات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C541-FANN541-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نحات** — *naḥḥāt* — "a sculptor".

> صنع النحات التمثال من الرخام.
>
> *ṣanaʿa an-naḥḥāt at-timthāl min ar-rukhām.*
>
> The sculptor made the statue out of marble.

The pattern faʿʿāl names a trade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نحات** (*naḥḥāt*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ḥ-t*, "to hew, to carve" (Lane). Naḥḥāt is the craftsman who carves.
