## You'll want to know: জেদি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C461-JEDI461-01]; assesses=[] -->

**জেদি** — *jedi* — "headstrong; stubborn".

> আমার জেদি বোন খেলনাটা ছাড়ল না।
>
> *āmār jedi bon khelnāṭā chhāṛlô nā.*
>
> My headstrong sister would not let go of the toy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জেদি** (*jedi*) is *jed*, "stubbornness", usually traced to Arabic *ḍidd*, "opposition", through Persian, with the ending *-i*.
