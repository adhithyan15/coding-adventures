## You'll want to know: पीढ़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C503-PIDHI503-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पीढ़ी** — *pīṛhī* — "a generation".

> हमारे घर में तीन पीढ़ियाँ एक साथ रहती हैं।
>
> *hamāre ghar meṁ tīn pīṛhiyā̃ ek sāth rahtī haiṁ.*
>
> Three generations live together in our house.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पीढ़ी** (*pīṛhī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *pīṛhī* both as "small square stool" and as "generation" under Sanskrit *pīṭha*, "seat" (CDIAL 8222).
