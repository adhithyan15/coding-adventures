## You'll want to know: net
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C502-PHOTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**net** — "sharp, in focus; clean, clear".

> Sur cette photo, le visage est bien net.
>
> *In this photo the face is nice and sharp.*

Feminine *nette*. Also net (of money): *un salaire net*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*net* is inherited from Latin *nitidus* "shining, clean" (EtymDB), from *nitēre* "to shine" (Lewis & Short).
