## You'll want to know: विशेष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C390-EKUN390-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विशेष** — *viśeṣ* — "particular; special".

> आज जेवणात काही विशेष आहे का?
>
> *āj jevṇāt kāhī viśeṣ āhe kā?*
>
> Is there anything special for dinner today?

विशेष करून, especially.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विशेष** (*viśeṣ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *viśeṣa*, "distinction, peculiarity" (Monier-Williams).
