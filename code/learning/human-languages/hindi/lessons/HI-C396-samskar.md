## You'll want to know: संस्कार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C396-SANSKAR396-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्कार** — *saṁskār* — "upbringing, values instilled".

> अगर बच्चों को अच्छे संस्कार मिलेंगे तो वे अच्छे इंसान बनेंगे।
>
> *agar baccoṁ ko acche saṁskār mileṅge to ve acche insān baneṅge.*
>
> If children are brought up well, they will become good people.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्कार** (*saṁskār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃskāra*, "refining; an impression" (Monier-Williams).
