## You'll want to know: fángzū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C203-RENT203-03]; assesses=[] -->

*fángzū* — "rent for a home".

> *zhèr de fángzū tài guì le* — The rent here is too high.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fángzū* is built from 房 'house' and *zū* 'rent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjang* and *tsu* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a tax'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
