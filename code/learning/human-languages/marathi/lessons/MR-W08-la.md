# ल — the plain *l*, beside the curled one you know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

You learned the harder *l* first. Here is the ordinary one.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-LA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-PA-01, MR-SCRIPT-NNA-01] -->

> ल

**ल** is plain *la*, the tongue at the teeth — the sound English writes with *l*.
You already write **ळ**, the retroflex *ḷ* Marathi keeps alongside it, and which
Hindi does not have at all.

Meeting the rare one first was not an accident: **ळ** sits in *kāḷjī* and
*ḍoḷā*, words this book needed early. Now the common one arrives, and with it
*bolṇe*, "to speak", becomes readable end to end.

## Writing — observe and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-LA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **ल** twice, then trace **ळ** once beside it. Say *la* and *ḷa*, moving the
tongue back only for the second.
