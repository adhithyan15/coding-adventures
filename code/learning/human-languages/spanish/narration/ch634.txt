Spanish, chapter 634: Llegar a un acuerdo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
negociar — to negotiate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the luxury, then the Spanish for free of charge.

You'll want to know: negociar.
negociar — "to negotiate".
Vamos a negociar el precio. We are going to negotiate the price.
Regular -ar verb.

The word, taken apart.
negociar is a learned borrowing from Latin negotiari 'to do business', built on negotium 'business', from nec 'not' and otium 'leisure' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: acordar.
acordar — "to agree on".
Acordamos una fecha. We agreed on a date.
Acordar algo — to agree on it. Stem change o becomes ue.

The word, taken apart.
acordar is inherited from spoken Latin accordare, built on cor 'heart' (EtymDB): to agree is to be of one heart.
