Malayalam, chapter 136: By the Water.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കടൽ (kaṭal) — the sea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a bridge, then the Malayalam for a village.

You'll want to know: കടൽ.
കടൽ (kaṭal) — "the sea". Kerala's whole west side is on it.

The word, taken apart.
കടൽ (kaṭal) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kaṭal, Kannada kaḍal and Telugu kaḍali (DEDR 1118).

What you've built.
Where the fish come from.
