## HL-C120 — Japanese hiragana mi closes the looped-bridge gap

After hiragana **ね** landed, Japanese hiragana **み** led the measured queue at
**3 affected realizations**.

Sirgazil's CC0 29-frame Wikimedia Commons animation supplies the two-run order:
carry the top bar left to right, descend diagonally into the lower-left loop,
sweep out through the middle, then lift once and restart high on the right for
the descending curve and upward finish. The learner filmstrip fits that order
to the bundled Noto Sans JP outline without presenting print geometry as an
independent handwriting source.

This closes all 3 affected realizations for **み** and reranks Japanese hiragana
**せ** first at **3**.
