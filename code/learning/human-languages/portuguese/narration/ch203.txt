Portuguese, chapter 203: To get divorced, To rest, To wake up, To comb one's hair, To get bored.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
divorciar-se — to get divorced.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to empty, then the Portuguese for to measure.

You'll want to know: divorciar-se.
divorciar-se — "to get divorced".

The word, taken apart.
divorciar-se is built from the word for a divorce, from Latin divortium, "a parting of the ways" (Lewis & Short).
