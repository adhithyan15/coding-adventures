## You'll want to know: ক্রমশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C302-KAHINI302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্রমশ** — *krômôsh* — "gradually; little by little".

> ঝড়টা ক্রমশ দুর্বল হল।
>
> *jhôṛṭā krômôsh durbôl hôlô.*
>
> The storm gradually grew weaker.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্রমশ** (*krômôsh*) is Sanskrit *kramaśas*, 'gradually, by degrees' (Monier-Williams), built on *krama*, 'a step', the word in **ভাগ্যক্রমে**.
