# ಬಾಲ್ಕನಿ (bālkani) — a balcony

## You'll want to know: ಬಾಲ್ಕನಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C462-KOTHADI462-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಾಲ್ಕನಿ** — *bālkani* — "a balcony".

> ಕೊಠಡಿಯ ಬಾಲ್ಕನಿಯಿಂದ ಸಮುದ್ರ ಕಾಣುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *koṭhaḍiya bālkaniyinda samudra kāṇuttade.*
>
> The sea can be seen from the balcony of the room.

Older houses have a ಜಗಲಿ in front instead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಾಲ್ಕನಿ** (*bālkani*) is a loan from English *balcony*.
