Kannada, chapter 482: Streams and Waterfalls.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ತೊರೆ (tore) — a brook; a stream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a shell, then the Kannada for an estuary.

You'll want to know: ತೊರೆ.
ತೊರೆ — tore — "a brook; a stream".
ಕಾಡಿನ ನಡುವೆ ಒಂದು ಚಿಕ್ಕ ತೊರೆ ಹರಿಯುತ್ತದೆ. kāḍina naḍuve ondu cikka tore hariyuttade. A small stream flows through the forest.
Smaller than a ನದಿ, like a ಝರಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ತೊರೆ (tore) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3370 lists Kannada toṟe, "a stream, a river".

Lesson 2 of 6.
ಧುಮುಕು (dhumuku) — to plunge; to leap down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಧುಮುಕು.
ಧುಮುಕು — dhumuku — "to plunge; to leap down".
ಜಲಪಾತದ ನೀರು ಬಂಡೆಯಿಂದ ಕೆಳಗೆ ಧುಮುಕುತ್ತದೆ. jalapātada nīru baṇḍeyinda keḷage dhumukuttade. The water of the falls plunges down from the rock.
Past (I …): ಧುಮುಕಿದೆ, dhumukide.
Of water falling and of a diver: ನೀರಿಗೆ ಧುಮುಕು (dhumuku), to dive into water.

The word, taken apart.
ಧುಮುಕು (dhumuku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3326 lists Kannada dumuku, "to leap or jump down from above"; the modern spelling writes it with the aspirate ಧ.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಮೊರೆ (more) — to rumble; to hum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a brook, then the Kannada for to plunge.

You'll want to know: ಮೊರೆ.
ಮೊರೆ — more — "to rumble; to hum".
ಮಳೆಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಜಲಪಾತ ಜೋರಾಗಿ ಮೊರೆಯುತ್ತದೆ. maḷegāladalli jalapāta jōrāgi moreyuttade. In the rainy season the waterfall roars loudly.
Past (it …): ಮೊರೆಯಿತು, moreyitu.
Of rushing water, bees and engines; a steady low sound.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೊರೆ (more) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4973 lists Kannada more, "to hum, to buzz, to murmur, to gurgle", beside moraḷ, "to growl".

You'll want to know: ಚಿಮ್ಮು.
ಚಿಮ್ಮು — cimmu — "to spurt; to splash up".
ಬಂಡೆಗೆ ಬಡಿದ ನೀರು ಮೇಲಕ್ಕೆ ಚಿಮ್ಮಿತು. baṇḍege baḍida nīru mēlakke cimmitu. Striking the rock, the water splashed up.
Past (it …): ಚಿಮ್ಮಿತು, cimmitu.
Of water, blood or sparks that shoot out.

The word, taken apart.
ಚಿಮ್ಮು (cimmu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2548 lists Kannada cimmu, "to flick, to squirt, to burst forth, to gush out".

ಬಂಡೆಯ ಮೇಲಿನಿಂದ ನೀರು ಜಿನುಗುತ್ತಿತ್ತು. baṇḍeya mēlininda nīru jinuguttittu. Water was trickling down from the top of the rock.
