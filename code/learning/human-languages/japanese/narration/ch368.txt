Japanese, chapter 368: All or Part.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ぜんたい (zentai) — the whole.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a degree, then the Japanese for a proportion.

You'll want to know: ぜんたい.
ぜんたい — zentai — "the whole".
ぜんたいを よんだら、いけんを 言って ください。 — zentai o yondara, iken o itte kudasai — "When you have read the whole thing, please tell me what you think."

The word, taken apart.
ぜんたい (zentai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are zen, "all" (Middle Chinese dzjwen) and tai, "body" (thejX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.
かたほう (katahō) — one of a pair; one side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a section, then the Japanese for both.

You'll want to know: かたほう.
かたほう — katahō — "one of a pair; one side".
てぶくろを かたほう なくしたら、こまるでしょう。 — tebukuro o katahō nakushitara, komaru deshō — "If you lose one glove, you will be stuck."

The word, taken apart.
かたほう (katahō) is built from the native kata, "one of a pair", a kun'yomi, and the Sino-Japanese hō, "direction", an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese pjang) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
かたよる (katayoru) — to be one-sided; to lean to one side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for both, then the Japanese for one of a pair.

You'll want to know: かたよる.
かたよる — katayoru — "to be one-sided; to lean to one side".
たべものが かたよったら、びょうきに なるでしょう。 — tabemono ga katayottara, byōki ni naru deshō — "If your diet is unbalanced, you will probably fall ill."
Polite かたよります, katayorimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): いけんが かたよりました。, iken ga katayorimashita.

The word, taken apart.
かたよる (katayoru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
