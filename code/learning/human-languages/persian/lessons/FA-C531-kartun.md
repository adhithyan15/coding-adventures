## You'll want to know: کارتون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C531-SCREEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کارتون** (*kârtun*) — "a cartoon (animated film)".

> بچه ها صبحها کارتون تماشا میکنند.
>
> *bachchehâ sobhhâ kârtun tamâshâ mikonand.*
>
> The children watch cartoons in the mornings.

Not *kârton* 'cardboard box', which is spelled کارتن.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kârtun* was borrowed from English *cartoon* (EtymDB).
