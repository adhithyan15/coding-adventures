Punjabi, chapter 356: Pictures and Photos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੈਮਰਾ (kaimrā) — a camera.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to make someone laugh, then the Punjabi for to tease.

You'll want to know: ਕੈਮਰਾ.
ਕੈਮਰਾ — kaimrā — "a camera".
ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਇੱਕ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਕੈਮਰਾ ਹੈ। mere kol ikk purāṇā kaimrā hai. I have an old camera.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੈਮਰਾ (kaimrā) is borrowed from English camera.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਤਸਵੀਰ (tasvīr) — a picture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to tease, then the Punjabi for a camera.

You'll want to know: ਤਸਵੀਰ.
ਤਸਵੀਰ — tasvīr — "a picture".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਇਹ ਤਸਵੀਰ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ। mainū̃ eh tasvīr bahut pasand hai. I like this picture very much.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਸਵੀਰ (tasvīr) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic taṣwīr, "picturing".
