## You'll want to know: la lampe de poche
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C498-PLEIN-AIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la lampe de poche** — "torch, flashlight".

> Il cherche ses clés avec une lampe de poche.
>
> *He is looking for his keys with a torch.*

Also *une lampe torche*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la lampe de poche* is "a pocket lamp": *lampe* comes from Latin *lampas* "a torch", a borrowing from Greek, and *poche* "pocket" is of Frankish origin.
