## You'll want to know: доверять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C479-TRUST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**доверять** (*doveryát*) — "to trust".

> Я доверяю своему врачу.
>
> *Ya doveryáyu svoyemú vrachú.*
>
> I trust my doctor.

Imperfective; perfective доверить. Takes the dative: *доверять врачу*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*доверять* is built on *до-* and the root of *вера* 'faith', inherited from Proto-Slavic *věra* (EtymDB).
