## You'll want to know: اندهش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C490-DAHSHA490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اندهش** — *indahasha* — "to be amazed".

> اندهشت المعلمة من جواب الطالب.
>
> *indahashat al-muʿallima min jawāb aṭ-ṭālib.*
>
> The teacher was amazed by the student's answer.

Present: يندهش, *yandahish*.

It takes من for the cause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اندهش** (*indahasha*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-h-sh*, "to be amazed, to be bewildered" (Lane). It is Form VII, a pattern that often says a thing happened to you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
