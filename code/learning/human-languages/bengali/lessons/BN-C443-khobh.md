## You'll want to know: ক্ষোভ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C443-KHOBH443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্ষোভ** — *khobh* — "resentment; bitterness".

> নতুন নিয়ম নিয়ে কর্মীরা ক্ষোভ জানাল।
>
> *nôtun niyôm niye kôrmīrā khobh jānālô.*
>
> The workers voiced their resentment over the new rule.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্ষোভ** (*khobh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kṣobha*, "shaking, agitation, emotion" (Monier-Williams); in Bengali it is the stirred-up bitterness of someone who feels wronged.
