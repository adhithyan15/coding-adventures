## You'll want to know: धीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C458-DILASA458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धीर** — *dhīr* — "fortitude; heart".

> आईने धीर दिला म्हणून मी पुन्हा प्रयत्न केला.
>
> *āīne dhīr dilā mhaṇūn mī punhā prayatna kelā.*
>
> Mother gave me heart, so I tried again.

Masculine.

धीर देणे, to give someone heart; धीर धरणे, to be patient.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धीर** (*dhīr*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dhīra*, "wise; steady, resolute"; CDIAL lists Marathi *dhīr*, "patience" (CDIAL 6817).
