# Five parts, and three different kinds of ancestry

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-KARNAH-EAR, SA-LEX-NASIKA-NOSE, SA-LEX-HRIDAYAM-HEART] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Ear, nose, heart. Say all three before you read on.

## Across the family
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-AKSHI-EYE, SA-LEX-KARNAH-EAR, SA-LEX-MUKHAM-MOUTH, SA-LEX-NASIKA-NOSE, SA-LEX-HRIDAYAM-HEART] -->

Five words, and they do not sort into one story. Sorted by how much anyone can
actually claim about where they came from:

| word | part | how secure the ancestry is |
|---|---|---|
| **हृदयम्** *hṛdayam* | heart | **the most secure** the book has shown |
| **अक्षि** *akṣi* | eye | unbroken, and visible in English *eye* |
| **नासिका** *nāsikā* | nose | kin to two modern words, **parent of neither** |
| **मुखम्** *mukham* | mouth, face | **two families claim it**, unsettled |
| **कर्णः** *karṇaḥ* | ear | **never settled**, even by Sanskrit itself |

The list runs from a word whose descent is traceable step by step to a word
nobody has been able to place at all. Both ends of it are ordinary vocabulary a
learner has to know; the difference between them is entirely a matter of what
the evidence supports.

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-NASIKA-NOSE, SA-LEX-KARNAH-EAR, SA-GRAMMAR-NOUN-GENDER-THREE] -->

**Kin is not descent.** *Nāsikā* has relatives in living languages and is the
ancestor of none of them — they and it come from the same older word. A family
tree is not a chain, and *cousin* is the commonest relation in it.

The endings sort the five a second way, cutting across the first. Three are
neuter; *karṇaḥ* takes the masculine visarga; *nāsikā* ends in a long *-ā*, the
shape most feminine nouns in this book take. Gender here is a property of the
word and not of the thing — a heart is neuter, an ear is masculine, and no fact
about bodies explains it.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-HRIDAYAM-HEART, SA-LEX-NASIKA-NOSE, SA-LEX-KARNAH-EAR] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which of the five has the securest ancestry, and which has none
anyone agrees on? (**हृदयम्**; **कर्णः**.) Is *nāsikā* the parent of its modern
look-alikes, or their cousin? (**Cousin.**)
