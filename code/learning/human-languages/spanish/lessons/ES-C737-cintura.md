## You'll want to know: la cintura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C737-CUERPO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la cintura** — "the waist".

> El pantalón me queda grande de cintura.
>
> *The trousers are too big at the waist.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cintura* comes from Latin *cinctūra* "a girdle, a belt" (EtymDB), from *cingere* "to gird".
