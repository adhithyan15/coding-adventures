## You'll want to know: o roteiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C435-ORIENTAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o roteiro** — "the travel plan, the route guide".

> Fizemos um roteiro de três dias pela costa.
>
> *We planned a three-day route along the coast.*

In Brazil *o roteiro* is also a film script.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*roteiro* is built on *rota* 'route, course' with *-eiro* (EtymDB); *rota* is usually traced to Latin *rupta (via)* 'broken (road)', from *rumpere* 'to break' (Lewis & Short).
