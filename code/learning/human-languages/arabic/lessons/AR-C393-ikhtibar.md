## You'll want to know: اختبار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C393-TAQYIM393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اختبار** — *ikhtibār* — "a test".

> نجحت في اختبار القيادة.
>
> *najaḥtu fī ikhtibār al-qiyāda.*
>
> I passed the driving test.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اختبار** (*ikhtibār*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-b-r*, "to know thoroughly, to test" (Lane). *Ikhtibār* is the Form VIII verbal noun of *ikhtabara*, to test: a test.
