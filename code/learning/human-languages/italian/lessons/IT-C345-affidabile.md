## You'll want to know: affidabile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C345-AFFERMARE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**affidabile** — "reliable, dependable".

> Mio fratello è una persona affidabile: puoi lasciargli le chiavi.
>
> *My brother is a reliable person: you can leave him the keys.*

*Una macchina affidabile* — a reliable car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*affidabile* is built on *affidare* "to entrust", which comes from Vulgar Latin *fīdāre*, a form of Latin *fīdere* "to trust" (Lewis & Short): someone you can trust things to.
