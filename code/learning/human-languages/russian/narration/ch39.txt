Russian, chapter 39: Wash, Cook, Buy, Sell, Swim.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
мыть (myt) — to wash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to laugh, then the Russian for to cry.

You'll want to know: мыть.
мыть (myt) — "to wash".

What you've built.
One more word for wash, cook, buy, sell, swim.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: myt]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: myt, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say myt]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for to laugh, then the Russian for to cry, then say myt again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does мыть (myt) mean? ("To wash".) Say it once more.
