Punjabi, chapter 133: Rooster, Hen, Donkey, Owl, Crow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੁੱਕੜ (kukkaṛ) — a rooster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a spider, then the Punjabi for a squirrel.

You'll want to know: ਕੁੱਕੜ.
ਕੁੱਕੜ — kukkaṛ — "a rooster".

The word, taken apart.
ਕੁੱਕੜ (kukkaṛ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kukkuṭa, "cock" (CDIAL 3208). CDIAL calls the word imitative.

What you've built.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਕੁੱਕੜ (kukkaṛ) mean? ("A rooster".) Say it once more.

You'll want to know: ਮੁਰਗੀ.
ਮੁਰਗੀ — murgī — "a hen".

The word, taken apart.
ਮੁਰਗੀ (murgī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian murgh, "bird, fowl". Punjabi adds the ending -ī.
