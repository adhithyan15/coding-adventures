## You'll want to know: der Zusammenhalt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C493-ZUNEIGUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zusammenhalt** — "solidarity, sticking together".

> In unserer Familie ist der Zusammenhalt sehr stark.
>
> *In our family we really stick together.*

No plural. Built on *zusammenhalten* — to stick together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zusammenhalt* is built on *zusammen* "together" and *Halt* "hold", from *halten* "to hold" (EtymDB), a Germanic verb from Proto-Germanic *haldaną*.
