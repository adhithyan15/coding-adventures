Urdu, chapter 409: Which Way?.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
جانب (jānib) — towards; a side (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the east, then the Urdu for the west.

You'll want to know: جانب.
جانب — jānib — "towards; a side (formal)".
دریا کی جانب ایک راستہ جاتا ہے۔ daryā kī jānib ek rāstā jātā hai. A path leads towards the river.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
جانب (jānib) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic jānib, "a side"; کی جانب means "towards", a more formal کی طرف.

Lesson 5 of 6.
جھانکنا (jhāṅknā) — to peep; to peer (in).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for close by, then the Urdu for a nook.

You'll want to know: جھانکنا.
جھانکنا — jhāṅknā — "to peep; to peer (in)".
سارا نے دروازے سے اندر جھانکا۔ sārā ne darvāze se andar jhāṅkā. Sara peeped in round the door.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کھڑکی سے جھانکا, us ne khiṛkī se jhāṅkā.

The word, taken apart.
جھانکنا (jhāṅknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jhā̃khnā, "to peep at", under jhaṅkh, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5325).
