[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਡਿੱਗਣਾ.
ਡਿੱਗਣਾ — ḍiggṇā — "to fall".

The word, taken apart.
ਡਿੱਗਣਾ (ḍiggṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from giḍḍ, "fall", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4157). Punjabi has swapped the first and last sounds.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਿਖਾਉਣਾ (sikhāuṇā) is the causing form of sikkhṇā, "to learn": teaching is "making learn". CDIAL lists both, the second as sakhāuṇā, under Sanskrit śikṣate, "learns" (CDIAL 12430).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਚੁੱਕਣਾ (cukkṇā) — to lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to fall, then the Punjabi for to teach.

You'll want to know: ਚੁੱਕਣਾ.
ਚੁੱਕਣਾ — cukkṇā — "to lift".

The word, taken apart.
Where ਚੁੱਕਣਾ (cukkṇā), "to lift", comes from is not settled. CDIAL lists a Punjabi cukkṇā only in the senses "to be finished, miss, err; to finish, undertake", under a reconstructed cukk, "fall short of, stop" (CDIAL 4848). It gives the close Punjabi cakkṇā, "to lift, take up, carry", in its entry for Sanskrit ucca, "tall, lofty" (CDIAL 1634).
