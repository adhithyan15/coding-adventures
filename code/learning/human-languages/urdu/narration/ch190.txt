Lesson 1 of 5.
وادی (vādī) — a valley.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for right away, then the Urdu for late.

You'll want to know: وادی.
وادی — vādī — "a valley".

The word, taken apart.
وادی (vādī) is a loan from Arabic wādī, "a valley, a river bed", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is w-d-y (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
چڑیا گھر (chiṛyā ghar) — a zoo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for late, then the Urdu for a valley.

You'll want to know: چڑیا گھر.
چڑیا گھر — chiṛyā ghar — "a zoo".

The phrase, taken apart.
چڑیا گھر (chiṛyā ghar) is "bird house". chiṛyā, "bird", comes from Sanskrit caṭaka, "sparrow", through a variant ciṭaka (CDIAL 4571). ghar, "house", is inherited; it is usually taken as a popular form of Sanskrit gṛha, a link CDIAL calls phonetically difficult (CDIAL 4428).

مکان — makān — "a dwelling, a house".

The word, taken apart.
مکان (makān) is a loan from Arabic makān, "a place", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is k-w-n (Lane). Urdu uses it for a house.
