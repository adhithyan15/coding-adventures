## You'll want to know: وقع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C294-FAJATAN294-02]; assesses=[] -->

**وقع** — *waqaʿa* — "to take place; to fall".

> وقع الحادث قرب المدرسة.
>
> *waqaʿa al-ḥādith qurba al-madrasa.*
>
> The accident happened near the school.

Present: يقع, *yaqaʿ*.

Of events: وقع حادث, an accident happened. Of places, يقع means it lies or is located.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وقع** (*waqaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-q-ʿ* (Lane), "to fall, to drop down". A thing that falls upon people is a thing that happens, and *wāqiʿ* is "what has fallen out": reality.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
