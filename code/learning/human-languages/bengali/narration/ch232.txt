শসা (shôsā) — a cucumber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for fine flour, then the Bengali for semolina.

You'll want to know: শসা.
শসা — shôsā — "a cucumber".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of শসা (shôsā) is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it in this sense, and a search by the gloss "cucumber" finds only words of other shapes, such as Bengali khirā (CDIAL 3697).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ভুট্টা (bhuṭṭā) — maize, a corn cob.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cucumber, then the Bengali for a mushroom.

You'll want to know: ভুট্টা.
ভুট্টা — bhuṭṭā — "maize, a corn cob".

The word, taken apart.
ভুট্টা (bhuṭṭā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhṛṣṭa, "fried, roasted" (CDIAL 9594). CDIAL lists Bengali bhuṭā, "maize cob".
