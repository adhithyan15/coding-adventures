## You'll want to know: verdächtig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C284-VERDACHT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**verdächtig** — "suspicious".

> Ein verdächtiges Auto stand vor dem Haus.
>
> *A suspicious car stood in front of the house.*

*Der Verdächtige* — the suspect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verdächtig* is built on *Verdacht* "suspicion", which is usually traced to *verdenken*, from *denken* "to think"; EtymDB lists no parent.
