## You'll want to know: устанавливать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C401-PRILOZHENIYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**устанавливать** (*ustanávlivat*) — "to install; to establish".

> Он устанавливает программу на компьютер.
>
> *On ustanávlivayet prográmmu na kompyúter.*
>
> He is installing a program on the computer.

Imperfective; perfective *установить* (*установлю, установишь*). It also means to set up or establish: *установить рекорд*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**устанавливать** is built from *у-* and *ставить* "to put, to stand up", on the root of *стоять*, Proto-Slavic *\*stojati* (EtymDB): to set a thing firmly in place.
