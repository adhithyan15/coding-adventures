Lesson 1 of 5.
امکان پذیر (emkân pazir) — possible, feasible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to come up, then the Persian for an option.

You'll want to know: امکان پذیر.
امکان پذیر (emkân pazir) — "possible, feasible".
این طرح با این پول امکان پذیر نیست. in tarh bâ in pul emkân pazir nist. This plan is not feasible with this money.
Emkân "possibility" + pazir "accepting", from paziroftan.

The word, taken apart.
امکان پذیر (emkân pazir) joins emkân "possibility", usually traced to Arabic imkān, to pazir "accepting", the present stem of the inherited verb paziroftan (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian): open to being possible.

The word, taken apart.
عملی ('amali) is built on 'amal "deed, action", usually traced to Arabic 'amal, with the adjective suffix -i: something that can be done.

سنجیدن (sanjidan) — "to weigh up, to measure".
قبل از تصمیم همه گزینه ها را سنجید. qabl az tasmim hame-ye gozine hâ râ sanjid. Before deciding he weighed up all the options.

The word, taken apart.
سنجیدن (sanjidan) is an inherited Persian verb "to weigh, to measure", usually traced to Middle Persian; EtymDB lists it without a parent.
