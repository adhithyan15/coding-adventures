## You'll want to know: گھرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C431-ABR431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گھرنا** — *ghirnā* — "to be surrounded; to close in (of clouds)".

> پہاڑوں پر بادل گھر آئے۔
>
> *pahāṛoṅ par bādal ghir āe.*
>
> Clouds closed in over the mountains.

Past (it …): وہ آسمان بادلوں سے گھر گیا, *voh āsmān bādaloṅ se ghir gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گھرنا** (*ghirnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ghirnā*, "to be surrounded", under *ghir*, "go round", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4474).
