Urdu, chapter 544: A Good Start.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آغاز (āghāz) — a beginning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to put a value on, then the Urdu for correct.

You'll want to know: آغاز.
آغاز — āghāz — "a beginning".
اگر آغاز اچھا ہوا تو آخر بھی اچھا ہو گا۔ agar āghāz achchhā huā to ākhir bhī achchhā ho gā. If it starts well, it will end well too.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
آغاز (āghāz) is usually traced to Persian āghāz, "a beginning".
