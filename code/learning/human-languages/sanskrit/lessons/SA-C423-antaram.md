## You'll want to know: अन्तरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C423-PRAVESHA423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्तरम्** — *antaram* — "a gap; the distance between".

> गृहस्य मार्गस्य च अन्तरम् अल्पम् अस्ति।
>
> *gṛhasya mārgasya ca antaram alpam asti.*
>
> The gap between the house and the road is small.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्तरम्** (*antaram*) is built on *antar*, "within, between"; MW gives *antara* as interior and, as a noun, an interval, a distance, a gap (MW).
