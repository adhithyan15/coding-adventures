Lesson 3 of 5.
مشکل (mushkil) — difficult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for hot, then the Urdu for slowly.

You'll want to know: مشکل.
مشکل — mushkil — "difficult".

The word, taken apart.
مشکل (mushkil) is a loan from Arabic mushkil, "difficult, doubtful", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is sh-k-l (Lane). The same root gives shakl, "shape".

Lesson 4 of 5.
مہنگا (mahãgā) — expensive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for slowly, then the Urdu for difficult.

You'll want to know: مہنگا.
مہنگا — mahãgā — "expensive".

The word, taken apart.
مہنگا (mahãgā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mahārgha, "high-priced" (CDIAL 9954). It is built from mahā, "great", and argha, "price".
