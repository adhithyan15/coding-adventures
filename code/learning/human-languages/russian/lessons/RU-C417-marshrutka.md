## You'll want to know: маршрутка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C417-MARSHRUTKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**маршрутка** (*marshrútka*) — "a fixed-route minibus".

> До работы я езжу на маршрутке.
>
> *Do rabóty ya yézzhu na marshrútke.*
>
> I go to work by minibus.

Colloquial for *маршрутное такси*: a minibus that runs on a set route and stops on request.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*маршрутка* is a colloquial short form of *маршрутное такси* 'a route taxi', built on *маршрут* 'a route' (EtymDB lists *маршрутка*). *Маршрут* came from German *Marschroute*.
