## You'll want to know: conmemorar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C558-HOMENAJE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**conmemorar** — "to commemorate".

> El pueblo conmemora aquel día cada año.
>
> *The town commemorates that day every year.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conmemorar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commemorāre* 'to call to mind' (EtymDB), built on *con-* and *memorāre* 'to recall', from *memor* 'mindful'.
