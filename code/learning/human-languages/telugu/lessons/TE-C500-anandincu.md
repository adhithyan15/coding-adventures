# ఆనందించు (ānandiñcu) — to enjoy; to rejoice

## You'll want to know: ఆనందించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C500-ANANDAM500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆనందించు** — *ānandiñcu* — "to enjoy; to rejoice".

> సెలవుల్లో పిల్లలు సముద్రం దగ్గర ఆనందించారు.
>
> *selavullō pillalu samudraṁ daggara ānandiñcāru.*
>
> In the holidays the children enjoyed themselves by the sea.

Past (I …): ఆనందించాను, *ānandiñcānu*.

The noun is ఆనందం, joy; ఆనందంగా, happily.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆనందించు** (*ānandiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ānanda*, "happiness" (CDIAL 1172), from the root *nand*, with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
