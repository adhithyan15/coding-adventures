Kannada, chapter 326: A Child Goes Missing.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಕಾಣೆಯಾಗು (kāṇeyāgu) — to go missing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to bless, then the Kannada for a wedding party.

You'll want to know: ಕಾಣೆಯಾಗು.
ಕಾಣೆಯಾಗು — kāṇeyāgu — "to go missing".
ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ ಮಗು ಕಾಣೆಯಾದರೆ ಅಪ್ಪ ಅಮ್ಮ ತಕ್ಷಣ ದೂರು ಕೊಡುತ್ತಾರೆ. ondu vēḷe magu kāṇeyādare appa amma takṣaṇa dūru koḍuttāre. If a child goes missing, the parents report it at once.
Past (it …): ಕಾಣೆಯಾಯಿತು, kāṇeyāyitu.
ಕಾಣೆ, not to be seen, and ಆಗು, to become.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಣೆಯಾಗು (kāṇeyāgu) is a native Dravidian compound: ಕಾಣೆ, "not seen", from kāṇ "to see" (DEDR 1443), and āgu "to become" (DEDR 333).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚು (patte haccu) — to track down; to detect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a wedding party, then the Kannada for to go missing.

You'll want to know: ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚು.
ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚು — patte haccu — "to track down; to detect".
ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಎರಡು ದಿನದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಗುವನ್ನು ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚಿದರು. polīsaru eraḍu dinadalli maguvannu patte haccidaru. The police tracked down the child in two days.
Past (I …): ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚಿದೆ, patte haccide.
ಪತ್ತೆ is a trace; ಪತ್ತೆಯಾಗು means to be found.

The word, taken apart.
ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚು (patte haccu) is a Kannada phrase verb built from ಪತ್ತೆ, "a trace, a whereabouts", and ಹಚ್ಚು, "to apply, to light". The origin of ಪತ್ತೆ is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಕುರುಹು (kuruhu) — a trace; a clue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to go missing, then the Kannada for to track down.

You'll want to know: ಕುರುಹು.
ಕುರುಹು — kuruhu — "a trace; a clue".
ಕಳ್ಳ ಯಾವ ಕುರುಹೂ ಬಿಡಲಿಲ್ಲ. kaḷḷa yāva kuruhū biḍalilla. The thief left no trace.
ಕುರುಹು (kuruhu) ಬಿಡು, to leave a trace; ಕುರುಹು (kuruhu) ಸಿಗು, to find a clue.

The word, taken apart.
ಕುರುಹು (kuruhu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1847 lists Kannada kuṟuhu, "a mark, a sign, a token", beside kuṟi, "a mark, an aim". The verb ಗುರುತಿಸು belongs to the same root.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಅಪಹರಿಸು (apaharisu) — to kidnap; to abduct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to track down, then the Kannada for a trace.

You'll want to know: ಅಪಹರಿಸು.
ಅಪಹರಿಸು — apaharisu — "to kidnap; to abduct".
ಇಬ್ಬರು ಆ ಹುಡುಗನನ್ನು ಅಪಹರಿಸಿದರು. ibbaru ā huḍuganannu apaharisidaru. Two people kidnapped that boy.
Past (I …): ಅಪಹರಿಸಿದೆ, apahariside.
The person taken takes -ಅನ್ನು. The noun is ಅಪಹರಣ, a kidnapping.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಪಹರಿಸು (apaharisu) is built on Sanskrit apaharaṇa, "taking away, carrying off, stealing" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳು (otteyāḷu) — a hostage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a trace, then the Kannada for to kidnap.

You'll want to know: ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳು.
ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳು — otteyāḷu — "a hostage".
ಕಳ್ಳರು ಅಂಗಡಿಯವನನ್ನು ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳಾಗಿ ಇಟ್ಟುಕೊಂಡರು. kaḷḷaru aṅgaḍiyavanannu otteyāḷāgi iṭṭukoṇḍaru. The robbers kept the shopkeeper as a hostage.
Plural: ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳುಗಳು, otteyāḷugaḷu.
ಒತ್ತೆ, a pledge, and ಆಳು, a person: a person held as a pledge.

The word, taken apart.
ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳು (otteyāḷu) is a native Dravidian compound: otte, "a pledge, a pawn" (DEDR 1020), and āḷ, "a person, a servant" (DEDR 399).
