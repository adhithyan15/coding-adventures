## You'll want to know: لٹیرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C280-DAKU280-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لٹیرا** — *luṭerā* — "a robber; a looter".

> اگر لٹیرے آئے تو ہم بھاگیں گے۔
>
> *agar luṭere āe to ham bhāgeṅ ge.*
>
> If the robbers come, we will run.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لٹیرا** (*luṭerā*) is built on *lūṭnā*, "to plunder", which CDIAL lists for Hindi under *\*luṭṭati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11078), with the ending *-erā* for the one who does it.
