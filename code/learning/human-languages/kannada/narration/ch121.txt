Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Kannada for a dhoti, then the Kannada for sandals.

You'll want to know: ಟೋಪಿ.
ಟೋಪಿ — ṭōpi — "a cap, a hat".

The word, taken apart.
ಟೋಪಿ (ṭōpi) came from Hindustani ṭōpī, "cap". Its deeper origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬಳೆ (baḷe) — a bangle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cap, then the Kannada for a ring.

You'll want to know: ಬಳೆ.
ಬಳೆ — baḷe — "a bangle".

The word, taken apart.
ಬಳೆ (baḷe) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil vaḷai and Telugu balayu (DEDR 5313). DEDR lists baḷe, "ring, armlet, bracelet", under the root for "to go round". DEDR 5313 compares Sanskrit val.

ಸಾರು (sāru) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil cāṟu (DEDR 2484). DEDR lists Kannada sāṟu, "juice, broth, pepper water".
