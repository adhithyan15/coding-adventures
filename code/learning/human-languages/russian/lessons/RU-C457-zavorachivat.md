## You'll want to know: заворачивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C457-DOSTAVKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**заворачивать** (*zavoráchivat*) — "to wrap (up); to turn (into)".

> Продавщица заворачивает рыбу в бумагу.
>
> *Prodavshchítsa zavoráchivayet rýbu v bumágu.*
>
> The shop assistant wraps the fish in paper.

Imperfective; perfective *завернуть*. Also to turn: *завернуть за угол* — to turn the corner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*заворачивать* is built from *за-* and *-ворачивать*, on the root of *вертеть* 'to turn': to turn something round in paper. EtymDB traces *вертеть* to Proto-Slavic *vĭrtěti*.
