## Grammar Lens: change the final vowel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-HAFTE-184]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਹਫ਼ਤੇ** — *hafte* — weeks, or week inside a larger time phrase

The ending changes from *-aa* to *-e*. In the small phrase **ਦੋ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ**, it means “two weeks.”
