## You'll want to know: ਅਰਜ਼ੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C254-FORM254-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਰਜ਼ੀ** — *arzī* — "an application".

> ਅਮਨ ਨੌਕਰੀ ਲਈ ਅਰਜ਼ੀ ਭੇਜੇਗਾ।
>
> *Aman naukrī laī arzī bhejegā.*
>
> Aman will send an application for the job.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਰਜ਼ੀ** (*arzī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *arzī*, "a petition", itself from Arabic.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
