Bengali, chapter 425: Clouds and Monsoon Rain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মেঘ (megh) — a cloud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for demand (for goods), then the Bengali for to fluctuate.

You'll want to know: মেঘ.
মেঘ — megh — "a cloud".
আকাশে কালো মেঘ দেখে আমরা ছাতা নিলাম। ākāshe kālo megh dekhe āmrā chhātā nilām. Seeing dark clouds in the sky, we took umbrellas.
Thunder is said to be the clouds calling: মেঘ (megh) ডাকে.

The word, taken apart.
মেঘ (megh) is Sanskrit megha, "a cloud" (Monier-Williams), a word Monier-Williams links with mih, to sprinkle: the cloud is the sprinkler.

Lesson 2 of 5.
বর্ষা (bôrshā) — the monsoon; the rainy season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to fluctuate, then the Bengali for a cloud.

You'll want to know: বর্ষা.
বর্ষা — bôrshā — "the monsoon; the rainy season".
বর্ষায় গ্রামের রাস্তা কাদায় ভরে যায়। bôrshāy grāmer rāstā kādāy bhôre jāy. In the monsoon the village roads fill up with mud.
বর্ষায়, in the rains; বর্ষাকাল, the rainy season.

The word, taken apart.
বর্ষা (bôrshā) is Sanskrit varṣā, "the rains, the rainy season" (Monier-Williams), from vṛṣ, to rain, the same root as বৃষ্টি, rain.

Lesson 3 of 5.
মুষলধারে (mushôldhāre) — in torrents (of rain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cloud, then the Bengali for the monsoon.

You'll want to know: মুষলধারে.
মুষলধারে — mushôldhāre — "in torrents (of rain)".
সারারাত মুষলধারে বৃষ্টি হল। sārārāt mushôldhāre brishṭi hôlô. It poured all night.
Always with rain: মুষলধারে (mushôldhāre) বৃষ্টি, rain in torrents.

The word, taken apart.
মুষলধারে (mushôldhāre) joins Sanskrit musala, "a pestle", and dhārā, "a stream, a jet" (Monier-Williams for both), with the locative ending: rain falling in streams as thick as pestles.

Lesson 4 of 5.
বজ্রপাত (bôjrôpāt) — a lightning strike; a thunderbolt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the monsoon, then the Bengali for in torrents.

You'll want to know: বজ্রপাত.
বজ্রপাত — bôjrôpāt — "a lightning strike; a thunderbolt".
ঝড়ের রাতে গ্রামের একটা উঁচু গাছে বজ্রপাত হল। jhôṛer rāte grāmer ekṭā ũchu gāchhe bôjrôpāt hôlô. On the stormy night lightning struck a tall tree in the village.
বজ্র is thunder itself; বজ্রপাত (bôjrôpāt) is the bolt that falls.

The word, taken apart.
বজ্রপাত (bôjrôpāt) is Sanskrit vajra-pāta, "the fall of a thunderbolt, a stroke of lightning" (Monier-Williams), from বজ্র, thunder, and pāta, a fall.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ছাওয়া (chhāoyā) — to spread over; to cover over (of clouds); to thatch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for in torrents, then the Bengali for a lightning strike.

You'll want to know: ছাওয়া.
ছাওয়া — chhāoyā — "to spread over; to cover over (of clouds); to thatch".
দুপুরে আকাশ মেঘে ছেয়ে গেল। dupure ākāsh meghe chheye gelô. At midday clouds spread across the sky.
Past (he/she …): সেই আকাশ মেঘে ছেয়ে গেল, sei ākāsh meghe chheye gelô.
Mostly in the -এ form with যাওয়া: ছেয়ে যাওয়া, to be covered all over. Also to thatch a roof.

The word, taken apart.
ছাওয়া (chhāoyā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali chāuyā, "to cover, to thatch", under Sanskrit chādáyati, "covers, veils" (CDIAL 5018), the root of ছই, a boat's hood, as well.
