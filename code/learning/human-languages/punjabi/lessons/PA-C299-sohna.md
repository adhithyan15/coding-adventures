## You'll want to know: ਸੋਹਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C299-WATER299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੋਹਣਾ** — *sohṇā* — "beautiful; lovely".

> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਝੀਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਸੋਹਣੀ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *shām nū̃ jhīl bahut sohṇī hundī hai.*
>
> In the evening the lake is very beautiful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੋਹਣਾ** (*sohṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śōbhana*, "brilliant, beautiful"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sohaṇā*, "beautiful", under it (CDIAL 12637).
