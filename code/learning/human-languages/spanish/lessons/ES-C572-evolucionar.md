## You'll want to know: evolucionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C572-CAMBIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**evolucionar** — "to evolve, to progress".

> El paciente evoluciona bien.
>
> *The patient is progressing well.*

Used for patients, situations and species.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*evolucionar* is built on *evolución*, from Latin *ēvolūtiō* 'an unrolling' (Lewis & Short), from *ēvolvere*, built on *volvere* 'to roll'.

## What you've built
