## You'll want to know: راوی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C345-QESSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**راوی** (*râvi*) — "a narrator".

> در این رمان راوی یک بچه است.
>
> *Dar in român râvi yek bachche ast.*
>
> In this novel the narrator is a child.

Plural *râviyân*. It also names someone who passes on a traditional report.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*râvi* is usually traced to Arabic *rāwī*, "narrator, one who passes on a report"; EtymDB lists no entry.
