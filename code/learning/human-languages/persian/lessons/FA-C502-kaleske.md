## You'll want to know: کالسکه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C502-BABIES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کالسکه** (*kâleske*) — "a pram, a pushchair".

> او بچه را با کالسکه به پارک برد.
>
> *u bachche râ bâ kâleske be pârk bord.*
>
> She took the baby to the park in the pram.

Also an old horse-drawn carriage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kâleske* was borrowed from Russian *koljaska*, 'carriage' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
