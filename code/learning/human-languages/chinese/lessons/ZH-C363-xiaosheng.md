## You'll want to know: xiǎoshēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C363-PUBLIC363-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xiǎoshēng* — "in a low voice; quietly".

> *zài yīyuàn lǐ yīnggāi xiǎoshēng shuōhuà* — You should speak quietly in a hospital.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiǎoshēng* is built from 小 'small' and *shēng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX* and *syeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'tiny' and the second 'a sound'.
