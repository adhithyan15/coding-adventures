## You'll want to know: قضاوت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C365-QEZAVAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قضاوت کردن** (*qezâvat kardan*) — "to judge".

> او زود درباره دیگران قضاوت میکند.
>
> *u zud darbâre-ye digarân qezâvat mikonad.*
>
> She is quick to judge other people.

*Qezâvat* "judgement" + *kardan* (stem *kon-*). *Darbâre-ye kasi qezâvat kardan* — to judge someone. It also means to referee a match.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قضاوت** was borrowed from Arabic *qaḍāwa* "judging" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as *qāḍī* "a judge"; *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
