Arabic, chapter 389: Power and influence.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
نفوذ (nufūdh) — influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to violate, then the Arabic for to exploit.

You'll want to know: نفوذ.
نفوذ — nufūdh — "influence".
لهذا الرجل نفوذ كبير في المدينة. li-hādhā ar-rajul nufūdh kabīr fī al-madīna. This man has great influence in the city.

The word, taken apart.
نفوذ (nufūdh) is built on the native Arabic root n-f-dh, "to pass through, to pierce" (Lane). Nufūdh is passing through: the power to make things happen, influence.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ساد (sāda) — to prevail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to exploit, then the Arabic for influence.

You'll want to know: ساد.
ساد — sāda — "to prevail".
ساد الخوف في المدينة. sāda al-khawf fī al-madīna. Fear prevailed in the city.
Present: يسود, yasūd.

The word, taken apart.
ساد (sāda) is built on the native Arabic root s-w-d, "to be lord, to be chief" (Lane). Sāda is a Form I verb, to be master: to rule, to prevail. سيد, a lord, is from the same root.

أجبر — ajbara — "to force".
أجبر المطر الناس على البقاء في البيت. ajbara al-maṭar an-nās ʿalā al-baqāʾ fī al-bayt. The rain forced people to stay at home.

The word, taken apart.
أجبر (ajbara) is built on the native Arabic root j-b-r, "to set a broken bone" (Lane). It is Form IV; Lane gives it for compelling someone: to force.
