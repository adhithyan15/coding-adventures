Gujarati, chapter 162: Housework and the doorbell.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઘરકામ (gharkām) — housework.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to break, then the Gujarati for to gather.

You'll want to know: ઘરકામ.
ઘરકામ — gharkām — "housework".
અમે સાથે ઘરકામ કરીએ છીએ. — ame sāthe gharkām karīe chīe — "We do the housework together."

The word, taken apart.
ઘરકામ (gharkām) is built from two inherited words already taught, ghar, "house", and kām, "work".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઘંટડી (ghanṭaḍī) — bell, doorbell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to gather, then the Gujarati for housework.

You'll want to know: ઘંટડી.
ઘંટડી — ghanṭaḍī — "bell, doorbell".
બારણા પાસે ઘંટડી છે. — bārṇā pāse ghanṭaḍī che — "There is a bell by the door."

The word, taken apart.
ઘંટડી (ghanṭaḍī) is a small form built on the word for a bell, which comes from Sanskrit ghaṇṭā, "bell". CDIAL lists Old Gujarati ghāṁṭa, "bell", but not the Gujarati diminutive itself (CDIAL 4421).

Lesson 3 of 5.
સમેટવું (sameṭvũ) — to tidy up, to fold away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for housework, then the Gujarati for bell.

You'll want to know: સમેટવું.
સમેટવું — sameṭvũ — "to tidy up, to fold away".
સવારે હું પથારી સમેટું છું. — savāre hũ pathārī sameṭũ chũ — "In the morning I fold away my bedding."

The word, taken apart.
સમેટવું (sameṭvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit saṁvēṣṭayati, "wraps up, winds round", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to wind up, gather up" (CDIAL 13026).

Lesson 4 of 5.
માટલું (māṭlũ) — clay pot, water pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for bell, then the Gujarati for to tidy up.

You'll want to know: માટલું.
માટલું — māṭlũ — "clay pot, water pot".
માટલામાં પાણી છે. — māṭlāmā̃ pāṇī che — "There is water in the clay pot."

The word, taken apart.
માટલું (māṭlũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from mārtta, "earthen", beside Sanskrit mārttika, "earthen pot", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "large earthen pot" (CDIAL 10085).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખૂણો (khūṇo) — corner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to tidy up, then the Gujarati for clay pot.

You'll want to know: ખૂણો.
ખૂણો — khūṇo — "corner".
માટલું રસોડાના ખૂણામાં છે. — māṭlũ rasoḍānā khūṇāmā̃ che — "The clay pot is in the corner of the kitchen."

The word, taken apart.
ખૂણો (khūṇo) is a tadbhava. It comes from khuṇṭa, "corner", a reconstructed form, crossed with Sanskrit koṇa, "corner"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati khūṇo, "corner" (CDIAL 3898).
