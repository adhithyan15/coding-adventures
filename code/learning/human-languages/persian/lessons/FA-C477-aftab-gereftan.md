## You'll want to know: آفتاب گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C477-SHARJI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**آفتاب گرفتن** (*âftâb gereftan*) — "to sunbathe".

> گردشگرها کنار دریا آفتاب میگرفتند.
>
> *Gardeshgar-hâ kenâr-e daryâ âftâb migereftand.*
>
> The tourists were sunbathing by the sea.

*âftâb* 'sun' + *gereftan* 'to take'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âftâb gereftan* is built from *âftâb* 'sunshine', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian, and the native verb *gereftan* 'to take'.
