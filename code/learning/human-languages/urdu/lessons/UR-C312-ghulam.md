## You'll want to know: غلام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C312-BAGHAVAT312-03]; assesses=[] -->

**غلام** — *ghulām* — "a slave".

> پہلے یہاں غلام کام کرتے تھے۔
>
> *pehle yahāṅ ghulām kām karte the.*
>
> Slaves used to work here long ago.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غلام** (*ghulām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghulām*, "a youth, a servant"; in Urdu it is one who is owned. **غلامی** is slavery.
