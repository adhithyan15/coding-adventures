## You'll want to know: માલિશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C487-CAKRI487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**માલિશ** — *māliś* — "a massage".

> દાદી બાળકને રોજ તેલથી માલિશ કરે છે.
>
> *dādī bāḷakne roj telthī māliś kare che.*
>
> Grandmother massages the baby with oil every day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માલિશ** (*māliś*) is usually traced to Persian *māliš*, "rubbing".
