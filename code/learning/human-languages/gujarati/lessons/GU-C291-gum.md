## You'll want to know: ગુમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C291-GHERVU291-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુમ** — *gum* — "missing".

> છોકરો ત્રણ દિવસથી ગુમ હતો.
>
> *chokro traṇ divasthī gum hato.*
>
> The boy had been missing for three days.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુમ** (*gum*) is usually traced to Persian *gum*, "lost". It describes a person or a thing nobody can find: **છોકરો ગુમ છે**; **ગુમ થયો**, went missing.
