## You'll want to know: おいだす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C297-ONI297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おいだす** — *oidasu* — "to drive out; to chase away".

> **みんなで おにを おいだします。** — *minna de oni o oidashimasu* — "We all drive the ogre out."

Polite **おいだします**, *oidashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが おにを おいだしました。**, *kodomo ga oni o oidashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おいだす** (*oidasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *oi*, "chasing" (from *ou*), and *dasu*, "to put out" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
