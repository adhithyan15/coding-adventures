## You'll want to know: il poeta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C527-GENERI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il poeta** — "poet".

> Il poeta legge le sue poesie in piazza.
>
> *The poet reads his poems in the square.*

Masculine but ending in *-a*: *il poeta*, *i poeti*; a woman is *la poetessa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*poeta* is a learned borrowing from Latin *poēta* (EtymDB), from Greek *poiētḗs* "a maker", from *poieîn* "to make".
