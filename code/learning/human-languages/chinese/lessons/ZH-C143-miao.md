## You'll want to know: miǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C143-CLOCK143-01]; assesses=[] -->

*miǎo* — "second (of time)".

> *qǐng děng wǔ miǎo* — Please wait five seconds.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miǎo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *mjiewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the beard of grain'. The tiny unit of time is not given there; the step from something very fine is the likely one, but it is not settled.
