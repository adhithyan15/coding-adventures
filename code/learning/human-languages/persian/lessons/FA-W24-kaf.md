# ک (kâf) — the letter inside فکر کردن

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write پ and ی once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **فکر کردن** (*fekr kardan*), "to think".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-KAF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ک** — *kâf*, the sound *k*.

It is in both halves of **فکر کردن** (*fekr kardan*), "to think". A tall stem, a
shallow bowl, and **one slash** across the top.

## Writing: ک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-KAF-01] -->

- **1.** start here: draw the independent stem downward
- **2.** continue left through the shallow bowl and final hook without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then draw the long slash down from the upper right toward the stem — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, ک demonstration at 02:19–02:23 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-KAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **فکر کردن** and point at ک inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ک three times, saying *k* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ی, then ک, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **فکر کردن** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-KAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ک? (**kâf**, *k*.) Which word did it
come from? (**فکر کردن**, *fekr kardan*, "to think".)
