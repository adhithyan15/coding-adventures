## You'll want to know: મશીન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C513-KARKHANU513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મશીન** — *maśīn* — "a machine".

> કારખાનાનું એક મશીન બગડી ગયું.
>
> *kārkhānānũ ek maśīn bagḍī gayũ.*
>
> One of the factory's machines broke down.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મશીન** (*maśīn*) is borrowed from English *machine*.
