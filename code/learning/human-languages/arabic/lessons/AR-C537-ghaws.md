## You'll want to know: غوص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C537-HIWAYA537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**غوص** — *ghawṣ* — "diving".

> تعلمت الغوص في الصيف الماضي.
>
> *taʿallamtu al-ghawṣ fī aṣ-ṣayf al-māḍī.*
>
> I learned diving last summer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غوص** (*ghawṣ*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-w-ṣ*, "to dive, to plunge" (Lane). Ghawṣ is the plunge under water.
