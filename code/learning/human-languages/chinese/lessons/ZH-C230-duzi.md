## You'll want to know: dùzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C230-BODY230-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dùzi* — "belly; tummy".

> *wǒ dùzi téng* — My tummy hurts.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dùzi* is built from *dù* 'belly' and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duX* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the belly'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
