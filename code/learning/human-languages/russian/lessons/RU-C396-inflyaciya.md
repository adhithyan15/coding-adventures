## You'll want to know: инфляция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C396-TSENY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**инфляция** (*inflyátsiya*) — "inflation".

> В прошлом году инфляция была высокой.
>
> *V próshlom godú inflyátsiya bylá vysókoy.*
>
> Last year inflation was high.

Feminine. *Уровень инфляции* — the rate of inflation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**инфляция** was borrowed through western languages from Latin *īnflātiō* "a blowing up, a swelling" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *īnflāre* "to blow into": prices swell.
