Marathi, chapter 541: Taking Care.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सावधगिरी (sāvadhgirī) — caution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an instalment, then the Marathi for to be affordable.

You'll want to know: सावधगिरी.
सावधगिरी — sāvadhgirī — "caution".
पावसात गाडी चालवताना सावधगिरी हवी. pāvsāt gāḍī cālavtānā sāvadhgirī havī. You need caution when driving in the rain.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सावधगिरी (sāvadhgirī) is built on सावध, "careful", from Sanskrit sāvadhāna, "attentive" (Monier-Williams), with the Persian ending -girī.

आपण खबरदारी घेतली तर अपघात टळतात. āpaṇ khabardārī ghetlī tar apghāt ṭaḷtāt. If we take care, accidents are avoided.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सतर्क (satark) — alert; watchful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a precaution, then the Marathi for vigilance.

You'll want to know: सतर्क.
सतर्क — satark — "alert; watchful".
जंगलात फिरताना सतर्क राहिलं पाहिजे. jaṅglāt phirtānā satark rāhilaṁ pāhije. One must stay alert when walking in the forest.

The word, taken apart.
सतर्क (satark) is a tatsama, from Sanskrit satarka, built on tarka, "reasoning"; it is glossed both as skilled in reasoning and as cautious (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
इशारा देणे (iśārā deṇe) — to issue a warning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for vigilance, then the Marathi for alert.

You'll want to know: इशारा देणे.
इशारा देणे — iśārā deṇe — "to issue a warning".
हवामान विभागाने मोठ्या पावसाचा इशारा दिला. havāmān vibhāgāne moṭhyā pāvsācā iśārā dilā. The weather department warned of heavy rain.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने इशारा दिला, tyāne iśārā dilā.
The danger takes -चा: पावसाचा इशारा देणे (iśārā deṇe).

The word, taken apart.
इशारा देणे (iśārā deṇe) is इशारा, a loan from Persian išāra, "a sign, a hint", usually traced to Arabic, with देणे.
