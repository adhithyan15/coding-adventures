## You'll want to know: अतीतम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C323-STUPA323-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अतीतम्** — *atītam* — "the past".

> इतिहासः अतीतं वर्णयति।
>
> *itihāsaḥ atītaṃ varṇayati.*
>
> History describes the past.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अतीतम्** (*atītam*) is built from *ati*, "beyond", and *ita*, "gone", from the root √i, "to go"; MW gives *atīta* as gone by, past, and as a noun the past (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
