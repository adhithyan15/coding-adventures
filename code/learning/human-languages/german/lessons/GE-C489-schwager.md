## You'll want to know: der Schwager
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C489-VERWANDTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schwager** — "brother-in-law".

> Mein Schwager hilft uns beim Umzug.
>
> *My brother-in-law is helping us move.*

Plural: *die Schwager*. A sister-in-law is *die Schwägerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schwager* is inherited from Middle High German *swāger* and Old High German *suāgur*, from Proto-Germanic *swēgraz* "husband's brother" (EtymDB).
