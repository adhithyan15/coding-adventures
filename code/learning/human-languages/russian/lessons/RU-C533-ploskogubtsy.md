## You'll want to know: плоскогубцы
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C533-REPAIRS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**плоскогубцы** (*ploskogúbtsy*) — "pliers".

> Плоскогубцы лежат в ящике с инструментами.
>
> *Ploskogúbtsy lezhát v yáshchike s instruméntami.*
>
> The pliers are in the toolbox.

Plural only.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*плоскогубцы* is a compound of *плоский* 'flat' and *губа* 'lip': pliers with flat jaws; EtymDB has no entry.
