# Review — it depends, and what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C274-REAL-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C273-DURATION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, we'll stay at home" and "or else". (*agar bārish huī, to ham ghar par raheṅ ge*, *varnā*.) Then "since yesterday" and "in my view". (*kal se*, *mere khayāl meṅ*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C275-MUNHASIR-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-02, UR-LEX-C94-TIME94-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Will you come tomorrow? It depends on the weather.]
- [YOU SAY: It depends on whether it will rain or not.]
- [YOU SAY: If I had time, I would come.]
- [YOU SAY: If it hadn't rained, we would have gone.]
- [YOU SAY: If I had got up early, I wouldn't have missed the train.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C275-MUNHASIR-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "It depends on you"? (**یہ آپ پر منحصر ہے**.) Now the other way round: in **اگر وقت ہوتا، تو میں آتا**, did I come? (No.) Which form do both halves take? (**-تا**, with nothing after it.)
