## You'll want to know: вылет
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C422-VYLET-01]; assesses=[] -->

**вылет** (*výlet*) — "a departure (of a flight)".

> Вылет задержали на час.
>
> *Výlet zaderzháli na chas.*
>
> The departure was delayed by an hour.

Masculine; the stress is on *вы-* (*vý-*). *Время вылета* — departure time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вылет* is built from *вы-* 'out' and *лёт* 'flight', the root of *летать* 'to fly'. EtymDB lists *взлёт* from the same root, Proto-Slavic *vŭzletŭ*.
