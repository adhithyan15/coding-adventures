## You'll want to know: اكتشاف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C421-FURSA421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اكتشاف** — *iktishāf* — "a discovery".

> كان اكتشاف الدواء مهما للعالم.
>
> *kāna iktishāf ad-dawāʾ muhimman li-l-ʿālam.*
>
> The discovery of the medicine was important for the world.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اكتشاف** (*iktishāf*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-sh-f*, "to uncover" (Lane). *Iktishāf* is the Form VIII verbal noun of *iktashafa*, to discover: a discovery.
