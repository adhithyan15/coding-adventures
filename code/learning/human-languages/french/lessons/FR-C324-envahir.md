## You'll want to know: envahir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C324-CONQUETE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**envahir** — "to invade; to overrun".

> L'armée a envahi le pays voisin.
>
> *The army invaded the neighbouring country.*

Figurative: *les touristes envahissent la plage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*envahir* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *invādīre*, from Latin *invādere* "to go into, to attack" (EtymDB).
