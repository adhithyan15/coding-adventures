## You'll want to know: der Faden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C499-HANDARBEIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Faden** — "thread".

> Der Faden ist zu kurz für den Knopf.
>
> *The thread is too short for the button.*

Plural: *die Fäden*. *Den Faden verlieren* — to lose the thread.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Faden* is usually traced to Old High German *fadum* "the span of outstretched arms", a length of thread; English *fathom* is a cousin. EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
