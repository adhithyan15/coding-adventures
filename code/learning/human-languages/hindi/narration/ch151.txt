Hindi, chapter 151: Chin, Tongue, Neck, Chest, Waist.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ठोड़ी (ṭhoṛī) — the chin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a fingernail, then the Hindi for a cheek.

You'll want to know: ठोड़ी.
ठोड़ी — ṭhoṛī — "the chin".

The word, taken apart.
ठोड़ी (ṭhoṛī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from tuṇḍa, "beak, snout" (CDIAL 5853). CDIAL calls the word certainly non-Aryan and probably Munda.
