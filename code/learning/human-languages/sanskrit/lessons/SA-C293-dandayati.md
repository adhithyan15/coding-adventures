## You'll want to know: दण्डयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C293-NYAYALAYA293-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दण्डयति** — *daṇḍayati* — "punishes".

> यदि दोषः अस्ति, तर्हि न्यायाधीशः दण्डयति।
>
> *yadi doṣaḥ asti, tarhi nyāyādhīśaḥ daṇḍayati.*
>
> If there is guilt, then the judge punishes.

Past (he …): सः चौरं दण्डितवान्, *saḥ cauraṃ daṇḍitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दण्डयति** (*daṇḍayati*) is built on *daṇḍa*, "a stick", and so "punishment"; MW gives *daṇḍayati* as a verb made from it, to chastise or punish (MW).
