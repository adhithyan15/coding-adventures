Kannada, chapter 97: Grateful and Sorry.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ಕ್ಷಮೆ (kṣame) — pardon, forgiveness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for hello, then the Kannada for are you well.

You'll want to know: ಕ್ಷಮೆ.
ಕ್ಷಮೆ (kṣame) — "pardon, forgiveness". You know its verb from ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ (kṣamisi), "excuse me".
ಕ್ಷಮೆ ಇರಲಿ (kṣame irali) — "please forgive me".

What you've built.
The noun behind excuse me.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kṣame]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kṣame, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kṣame irali]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for hello, then the Kannada for are you well, then say kṣame again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಕ್ಷಮೆ (kṣame) mean? ("Pardon, forgiveness".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 7.

Before the new one: say the Kannada for pardon, then the Kannada for an introduction.

You'll want to know: ಆಭಾರಿ.
ಆಭಾರಿ (ābhāri) — "grateful, obliged".
ನಾನು ನಿಮಗೆ ಆಭಾರಿ (nānu nimage ābhāri) — "I am grateful to you", a deeper thanks than ಧನ್ಯವಾದ.

Lesson 5 of 7.
ದಯೆ (daye) — kindness, compassion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for grateful, then the Kannada for don't forget.

You'll want to know: ದಯೆ.
ದಯೆ (daye) — "kindness, compassion". It is the daya inside ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು, "please", the word the book took apart early on.

What you've built.
The kindness hidden inside please.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Who you are — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask a friend not to forget, and name the kindness inside please.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: name a grandson, a granddaughter, a relative, the neighbours and an uncle]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask someone's address and home town; name a birthday, a wedding and a language]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: name a school, a college and an office; ask someone's job; say you know English]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Grandson, granddaughter? (ಮೊಮ್ಮಗ, ಮೊಮ್ಮಗಳು.) Relative? (ಸಂಬಂಧಿಕ.) Neighbours? (ನೆರೆಯವರು.) Uncle? (ಮಾವ.) Address? (ವಿಳಾಸ.) Birthday? (ಹುಟ್ಟುಹಬ್ಬ.) Wedding? (ಮದುವೆ.) Home town? (ಊರು.) Language? (ಭಾಷೆ.) School, college, office? (ಶಾಲೆ, ಕಾಲೇಜು, ಕಚೇರಿ.) Job? (ಉದ್ಯೋಗ.) English? (ಇಂಗ್ಲಿಷ್.)


Lesson 7 of 7.
