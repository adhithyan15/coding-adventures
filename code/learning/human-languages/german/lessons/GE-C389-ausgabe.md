## You'll want to know: die Ausgabe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C389-LEISTEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ausgabe** — "the expense; the edition".

> Unsere größte Ausgabe ist die Miete.
>
> *Our biggest expense is the rent.*

Plural: *die Ausgaben*. Also an edition of a newspaper or a book: *die neue Ausgabe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ausgabe* is inherited from Middle High German *ūzgābe* (EtymDB), of the family of *ausgeben* "to spend, to give out", built on *geben* "to give", Proto-Germanic *gebaną*.
