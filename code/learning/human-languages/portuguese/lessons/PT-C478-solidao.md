## You'll want to know: a solidão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C478-SOLIDAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a solidão** — "loneliness, solitude".

> Muitos idosos vivem em solidão.
>
> *Many elderly people live in loneliness.*

*Sentir solidão* — to feel lonely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*solidão* is inherited from Latin *sōlitūdō* "loneliness, a lonely place" (EtymDB), built on *sōlus* "alone".
