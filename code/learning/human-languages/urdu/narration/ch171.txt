Lesson 1 of 5.
شہری (shahrī) — a citizen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an uncle, then the Urdu for an aunt.

You'll want to know: شہری.
شہری — shahrī — "a citizen".

The word, taken apart.
شہری (shahrī) is shahr, "city", usually traced to Persian shahr, with the ending -ī, "of".

Lesson 5 of 5.
مطلب (matlab) — a meaning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a sentence, then the Urdu for a character.

You'll want to know: مطلب.
مطلب — matlab — "a meaning".

The word, taken apart.
مطلب (matlab) is a loan from Arabic maṭlab, "something sought, an aim", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṭ-l-b (Lane). Urdu uses it for what a word aims at: its meaning.
