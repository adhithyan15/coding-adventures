Gujarati, chapter 322: The Attack at Dawn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હુમલો (humlo) — an attack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a cannon, then the Gujarati for to pierce.

You'll want to know: હુમલો.
હુમલો — humlo — "an attack".
પરોઢે દુશ્મને કિલ્લા પર હુમલો કર્યો. paroḍhe duśmane killā par humlo karyo. At dawn the enemy attacked the fort.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હુમલો (humlo) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥamla, "a charge, an attack". The place attacked takes પર: કિલ્લા પર હુમલો.

Lesson 2 of 5.
લડાઈ (laḍāī) — a battle; a fight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pierce, then the Gujarati for an attack.

You'll want to know: લડાઈ.
લડાઈ — laḍāī — "a battle; a fight".
આ લડાઈ આખો દિવસ ચાલી. ā laḍāī ākho divas cālī. This battle went on all day.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
લડાઈ (laḍāī) is built on લડવું, to fight, from laḍ, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10920), with the noun ending -āī: the fighting itself, from a quarrel to a battle.

Lesson 4 of 5.
હણવું (haṇvũ) — to slay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a battle, then the Gujarati for a warrior.

You'll want to know: હણવું.
હણવું — haṇvũ — "to slay".
યોદ્ધાએ એક વાઘને હણ્યો. yoddhāe ek vāghne haṇyo. The warrior slew a tiger.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દુશ્મનને હણ્યો, teṇe duśmanne haṇyo.

The word, taken apart.
હણવું (haṇvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit hánati, "strikes, kills", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to strike, kill" (CDIAL 13963). It is a word of old stories; મારવું is the everyday verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઝઝૂમવું (jhajhūmvũ) — to fight on; to struggle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a warrior, then the Gujarati for to slay.

You'll want to know: ઝઝૂમવું.
ઝઝૂમવું — jhajhūmvũ — "to fight on; to struggle".
સૈનિકો છેલ્લી પળ સુધી ઝઝૂમ્યા. sainiko chellī paḷ sudhī jhajhūmyā. The soldiers fought on to the very last moment.
Past (he …): તે ખૂબ ઝઝૂમ્યો, te khūb jhajhūmyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઝઝૂમવું (jhajhūmvũ) probably belongs with jhujhvũ, to fight, which CDIAL lists for Gujarati under Sanskrit yúdhyatē, "fights against" (CDIAL 10502); CDIAL does not list this longer form, so the link is not settled. It is to keep fighting against long odds: છેલ્લી પળ સુધી ઝઝૂમ્યા.
