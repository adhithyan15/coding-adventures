## You'll want to know: der Durchfall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C480-UEBELKEIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Durchfall** — "diarrhoea".

> Nach dem Essen im Urlaub hatte er Durchfall.
>
> *After the food on holiday he had diarrhoea.*

No article in *Durchfall haben*. No plural in everyday use.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Durchfall* is built on *durch* "through" and *Fall* "fall", which is inherited from Middle High German *val* and Old High German *val* (EtymDB).
