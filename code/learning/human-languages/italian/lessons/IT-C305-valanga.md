## You'll want to know: la valanga
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C305-ALLUVIONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la valanga** — "the avalanche".

> Una valanga è caduta sulla strada.
>
> *An avalanche came down on the road.*

Also a flood of things: *una valanga di lettere*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*valanga* is usually traced to northern Italian dialects; the deeper origin is not settled (EtymDB has no entry).
