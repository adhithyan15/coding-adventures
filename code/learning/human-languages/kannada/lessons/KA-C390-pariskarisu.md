# ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು (pariṣkarisu) — to revise

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a law, then the Kannada for an act of law.

## You'll want to know: ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C390-KANUNU390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು** — *pariṣkarisu* — "to revise".

> ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಹಳೆಯ ನಿಯಮಗಳನ್ನು ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸಿತು.
>
> *sarkāra haḷeya niyamagaḷannu pariṣkarisitu.*
>
> The government revised the old rules.

Past (I …): ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸಿದೆ, *pariṣkariside*.

ಪರಿಷ್ಕೃತ, revised.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು** (*pariṣkarisu*) is built on Sanskrit *pariṣkāra*, "adorning, preparing, refining" (CDIAL 7894), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
