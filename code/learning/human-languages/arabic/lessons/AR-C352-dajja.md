## You'll want to know: ضجة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C352-MUKHALAFA352-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ضجة** — *ḍajja* — "a commotion, an uproar".

> أحدث الخبر ضجة كبيرة.
>
> *aḥdatha al-khabar ḍajja kabīra.*
>
> The news caused a big stir.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضجة** (*ḍajja*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-j-j* (Lane), "to cry out, to clamour"; *ḍajīj* is noise.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
