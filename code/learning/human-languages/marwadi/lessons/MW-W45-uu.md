# ऊ (ū) — the letter that opens ऊंट

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-U-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-UU-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **उ** once, and name it. Then say **हूं**
(*hū̃*): its long *ū* is the sign **ू**.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ऊंट** (*ū̃ṭ*), "a camel". It opens on the long vowel standing
alone, and this lesson puts that letter in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ऊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-C153-UU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊ** — *ū*, the long *u*.

It opens **ऊंट**. It is the letter **उ** with one more part: a separate loop on its
right. **उ** and **ऊ** pair the way the signs **ु** and **ू** do, short and long.

## Writing: ऊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-C153-UU-01] -->

- **1.** curve down and left around the upper bowl, then sweep back through the waist and around the lower loop without lifting
- **2.** lift at the waist and sweep the right-hand loop up, around, and down-left
- **3.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> verified modern printed teaching form; everyday handwriting may simplify the curves

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari ऊ stroke order.svg’, panels 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.
