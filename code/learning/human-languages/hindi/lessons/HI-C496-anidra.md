## You'll want to know: अनिद्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C496-NEEND496-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अनिद्रा** — *anidrā* — "insomnia; sleeplessness".

> तनाव से अरुण को अनिद्रा की शिकायत है।
>
> *tanāv se Aruṇ ko anidrā kī śikāyat hai.*
>
> Because of stress, Arun suffers from insomnia.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनिद्रा** (*anidrā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *a-nidrā*, "sleeplessness", built on *a-*, "not", and *nidrā*, "sleep" (Monier-Williams).
