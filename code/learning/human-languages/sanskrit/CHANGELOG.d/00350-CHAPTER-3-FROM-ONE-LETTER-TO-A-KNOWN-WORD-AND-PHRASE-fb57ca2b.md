## Chapter 3 — from one letter to a known word and phrase

Sanskrit's writing-stage evidence stopped at one isolated **न**. Six short
lessons now widen the runway without adding vocabulary or an untaught shape.

- **मम**, already known as “my,” is copied from its visible two-**म** model,
  recalled after the model is covered, and written from sound and meaning alone.
- **मम नाम**, already known as “my name,” repeats those three steps while
  making the word boundary and the long-ā sign in **नाम** explicit.

Every lesson is at most three minutes. The no-model rungs hide both Devanagari
and romanization until the attempt is complete, and the focused corpus test pins
the prerequisite chain, duration budget, stage evidence, and exact withdrawal
of support. The session map records the two new milestones. Full introductions,
visarga, vocalic ṛ, conjuncts, connected text, and independent recall remain
future rungs of #13413.
