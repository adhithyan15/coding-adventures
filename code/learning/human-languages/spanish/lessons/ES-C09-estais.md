# estáis — and nothing surprises you

## Grammar Lens: predictable, and that is the news
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-GRAMMAR-ESTAR-PRESENT-2PL]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-AR-PRESENT-2PL, ES-LEX-VOSOTROS, ES-CULTURE-VOSOTROS-USTEDES-SPLIT] -->

> **vosotros estáis** — *you all are*, in Spain.

If you guessed that before reading it, you have understood the chapter. The
ending is *-áis*, the plain *-ar* one from *habláis*, and the accent is there
for the reason it is always there with this verb: the stress sits on the ending.

That is now three forms in a row where knowing *estar* meant knowing nothing
new. In the Americas, *ustedes están* does this job.
