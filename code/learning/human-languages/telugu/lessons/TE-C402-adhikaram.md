# అధికారం (adhikāraṁ) — power; authority

## You'll want to know: అధికారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C402-MANTRI402-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అధికారం** — *adhikāraṁ* — "power; authority".

> ఆ పని చేసే అధికారం అతనికి లేదు.
>
> *ā pani cēsē adhikāraṁ ataniki lēdu.*
>
> He has no authority to do that.

అధికారి, an officer, which you know, is the one who holds it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అధికారం** (*adhikāraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *adhikāra*, "authority; government, rule, administration" (Monier-Williams); అధికారి, an officer, is the same word.
