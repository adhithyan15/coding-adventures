## You'll want to know: すき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C240-LIKES240-03]; assesses=[] -->

**すき** — *suki* — "liked; fond of".

> **きょねん あねは まんがが すきでした。** — *kyonen ane wa manga ga suki deshita* — "Last year my elder sister liked comics."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すき** (*suki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it comes from *suku*, "to like", and its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
