# లభించు (labhiñcu) — to be available; to be obtained

## You'll want to know: లభించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C487-KHARCU487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**లభించు** — *labhiñcu* — "to be available; to be obtained".

> ఆ పుస్తకం మా పట్టణంలో లభించదు.
>
> *ā pustakaṁ mā paṭṭaṇaṁlō labhiñcadu.*
>
> That book is not available in our town.

Past (it …): లభించింది, *labhiñcindi*.

The person who gets it takes -కి: నాకు ఉద్యోగం లభించింది, I got a job. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: లభించింది, it was got.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లభించు** (*labhiñcu*) is built from Sanskrit *labh*, "to take, to obtain" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; లాభం, a profit, is the same root.
