Punjabi, chapter 277: Choosing the Best.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਗਾਹਕ (gāhak) — a customer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a purchase, then the Punjabi for free of charge.

You'll want to know: ਗਾਹਕ.
ਗਾਹਕ — gāhak — "a customer".
ਸਵੇਰੇ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਗਾਹਕ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ। savere dukān vich bahut gāhak hunde han. In the morning there are many customers in the shop.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ਗਾਹਕ (gāhak) goes back to Sanskrit grāhaka, "one who takes; a purchaser" (Monier-Williams); CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਚੁਣਨਾ (chuṇnā) — to choose; to pick out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for free of charge, then the Punjabi for a customer.

You'll want to know: ਚੁਣਨਾ.
ਚੁਣਨਾ — chuṇnā — "to choose; to pick out".
ਮੈਂ ਤਾਜ਼ੇ ਸੇਬ ਚੁਣਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ tāze seb chuṇdā hāṁ. I pick out the fresh apples.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਲਾਲ ਕਮੀਜ਼ ਚੁਣੀ, maiṁ lāl kamīz chuṇī.

The word, taken apart.
ਚੁਣਨਾ (chuṇnā) is inherited from Sanskrit cinoti, "heaps up, gathers"; CDIAL lists Punjabi cuṇnā, "to build up in layers, plait", under it, beside Lahnda cuṇaṇ, "to pick, gather, choose" (CDIAL 4814).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਪਰਖਣਾ (parakhṇā) — to test; to check.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a customer, then the Punjabi for to choose.

You'll want to know: ਪਰਖਣਾ.
ਪਰਖਣਾ — parakhṇā — "to test; to check".
ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਤਾ ਹਰ ਸੇਬ ਪਰਖਦੀ ਹੈ। merī mātā har seb parakhdī hai. My mother checks every apple.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਨਵਾਂ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਪਰਖਿਆ, maiṁ navāṁ fon parkhiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਰਖਣਾ (parakhṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit parīkṣate, "observes, examines"; CDIAL lists Punjabi parakhṇā, "to test", under it (CDIAL 7904).
