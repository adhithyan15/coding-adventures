Telugu, chapter 471: Caring for the Environment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పర్యావరణం (paryāvaraṇaṁ) — the environment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to pollute, then the Telugu for pure.

You'll want to know: పర్యావరణం.
పర్యావరణం — paryāvaraṇaṁ — "the environment".
పర్యావరణం కోసం ప్లాస్టిక్ వాడకం తగ్గించాలి. paryāvaraṇaṁ kōsaṁ plāsṭik vāḍakaṁ taggiñcāli. For the sake of the environment we should use less plastic.
పర్యావరణ, environmental, before a noun: పర్యావరణ దినోత్సవం, Environment Day.

The word, taken apart.
పర్యావరణం (paryāvaraṇaṁ) is a modern Telugu word built from Sanskrit pari, "around", and āvaraṇa, "a covering, an enclosure" (Monier-Williams): what surrounds and covers us.

Lesson 3 of 5.
నరుకు (naruku) — to chop down; to fell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the environment, then the Telugu for greenery.

You'll want to know: నరుకు.
నరుకు — naruku — "to chop down; to fell".
రోడ్డు కోసం చాలా చెట్లు నరికారు. rōḍḍu kōsaṁ cālā ceṭlu narikāru. Many trees were cut down for the road.
Past (I …): నరికాను, narikānu.
Said of trees and of wood chopped with an axe.

The word, taken apart.
నరుకు (naruku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3625 lists Telugu naṟuku, "to cut, to hew, to fell", with Tamil naṟukku, "to chop".

Lesson 4 of 5.
నష్టపరచు (naṣṭaparacu) — to damage; to harm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for greenery, then the Telugu for to chop down.

You'll want to know: నష్టపరచు.
నష్టపరచు — naṣṭaparacu — "to damage; to harm".
వరదలు పంటలను నష్టపరిచాయి. varadalu paṇṭalanu naṣṭaparicāyi. The floods damaged the crops.
Past (I …): నష్టపరిచాను, naṣṭaparicānu.
నష్టం, a loss, + -పరచు, to cause: to cause a loss. The same -పరచు makes other verbs from nouns.

The word, taken apart.
నష్టపరచు (naṣṭaparacu) is a Telugu verb built from నష్టం, "a loss", borrowed from Sanskrit naṣṭa, "lost, destroyed" (Monier-Williams), and the native -పరచు, the causative of పడు, which DEDR 3853 lists as paṟacu, "to cause to feel or suffer".

Lesson 5 of 5.
సంరక్షించు (saṁrakṣiñcu) — to conserve; to protect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to chop down, then the Telugu for to damage.

You'll want to know: సంరక్షించు.
సంరక్షించు — saṁrakṣiñcu — "to conserve; to protect".
గ్రామస్తులు ఆ అడవిని సంరక్షిస్తున్నారు. grāmastulu ā aḍavini saṁrakṣistunnāru. The villagers are protecting that forest.
Past (I …): సంరక్షించాను, saṁrakṣiñcānu.
A formal word; the noun is సంరక్షణ, conservation.

The word, taken apart.
సంరక్షించు (saṁrakṣiñcu) is built from Sanskrit saṁ-rakṣ, "to guard, to preserve", whose noun is saṁrakṣaṇa, "preservation" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
