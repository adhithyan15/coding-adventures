[your turn — say: finally, lastly, on the spot, formerly, long since]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Breath? (anima, animae.) Memory? (memoria, memoriae.) Wisdom? (sapientia, sapientiae.) Talent? (ingenium, ingeniī.) A habit? (cōnsuētūdō, cōnsuētūdinis.) A generation? (saeculum, saeculī.) Age? (aetās, aetātis.) Sunset; a setting? (occāsus, occāsūs.) Sunrise; a rising? (ortus, ortūs.) First light? (dīlūculum, dīlūculī.) Finally? (dēnique.) Lastly? (postrēmō.) On the spot? (illicō.) Formerly? (quondam.) Long since? (iamdūdum.) A region? (regiō, regiōnis.) A province? (prōvincia, prōvinciae.) From there; then? (inde.) In the way? (obviam.) All the way? (usque.)

(tacitus, tacita, tacitum.) Foreign? (peregrīnus, peregrīna, peregrīnum.) Honest? (probus, proba, probum.) Patient? (patiēns, patientis.) Generous? (līberālis, līberāle.) Idle? (ignāvus, ignāva, ignāvum.) Savage? (saevus, saeva, saevum.) Wild? (ferus, fera, ferum.) Of the home? (domesticus, domestica, domesticum.) Rare? (rārus, rāra, rārum.) Frequent?
