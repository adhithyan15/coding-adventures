## You'll want to know: さぞ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C523-ANY523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さぞ** — *sazo* — "no doubt; I imagine".

> **もし かぞくで りょこうしたら、さぞ たのしいでしょう。** — *moshi kazoku de ryokō shitara, sazo tanoshii deshō* — "If the family went on a trip together, it would surely be fun."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さぞ** (*sazo*) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: JMdict marks it as usually written in kana, with one rare kanji spelling, and no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
