Telugu, chapter 446: Bumps on the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎగుడుదిగుడు (eguḍudiguḍu) — bumpy; uneven; ups and downs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to slow down, then the Telugu for to lose control.

You'll want to know: ఎగుడుదిగుడు.
ఎగుడుదిగుడు — eguḍudiguḍu — "bumpy; uneven; ups and downs".
పల్లెకు వెళ్ళే రోడ్డు ఎగుడుదిగుడుగా ఉంది. palleku veḷḷē rōḍḍu eguḍudiguḍugā undi. The road to the village is bumpy.
With ఉండు it takes -గా: ఎగుడుదిగుడుగా ఉంది, it is bumpy. It is also said of a life full of ups and downs.

The word, taken apart.
ఎగుడుదిగుడు (eguḍudiguḍu) is native Dravidian, a rhyming pair: ఎగుడు, "high, raised", which DEDR 3730 lists beside ega diga, "up and down", and దిగుడు, "a going down", from దిగు, "to go down" (DEDR 502).

Lesson 2 of 5.
కుదుపు (kudupu) — a jolt; a shake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to lose control, then the Telugu for bumpy.

You'll want to know: కుదుపు.
కుదుపు — kudupu — "a jolt; a shake".
బస్సు గుంటలో పడినప్పుడు పెద్ద కుదుపు వచ్చింది. bassu guṇṭalō paḍinappuḍu pedda kudupu vaccindi. When the bus hit the pothole there was a big jolt.
The same word is a verb, to shake something: సీసాను కుదుపు (kudupu), to shake a bottle.

The word, taken apart.
కుదుపు (kudupu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1705 gives Telugu kudupu, "to shake, to jolt", and as a noun "a jolting", in the family of Tamil kuti, "to leap".

Lesson 3 of 5.
తప్పించు (tappiñcu) — to avoid; to dodge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for bumpy, then the Telugu for a jolt.

You'll want to know: తప్పించు.
తప్పించు — tappiñcu — "to avoid; to dodge".
డ్రైవరు గుంటను తప్పించి వెళ్ళాడు. ḍraivaru guṇṭanu tappiñci veḷḷāḍu. The driver steered round the pothole.
Past (I …): తప్పించాను, tappiñcānu.
తప్పించుకో, to escape, is the same verb with -కో: you get yourself out of something. తప్పించు (tappiñcu) is to steer clear of a thing.

The word, taken apart.
తప్పించు (tappiñcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3071 lists Telugu tappincu beside tappu, "to slip, to miss, to escape".

Lesson 4 of 5.
బోల్తాపడు (bōltāpaḍu) — to overturn; to topple over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a jolt, then the Telugu for to avoid.

You'll want to know: బోల్తాపడు.
బోల్తాపడు — bōltāpaḍu — "to overturn; to topple over".
మలుపు దగ్గర ఒక ఆటో బోల్తాపడింది. malupu daggara oka āṭō bōltāpaḍindi. An auto overturned at the bend.
Past (I …): బోల్తాపడ్డాను, bōltāpaḍḍānu.
Said of a vehicle that rolls on its side. Spoken Telugu also uses it of a plan that falls through.

The word, taken apart.
బోల్తాపడు (bōltāpaḍu) is a Telugu compound: బోల్తా, "a tumble", whose origin is not settled, and the native పడు, "to fall into, to undergo" (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 5 of 5.
సొట్టపడు (soṭṭapaḍu) — to get dented.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to avoid, then the Telugu for to overturn.

You'll want to know: సొట్టపడు.
సొట్టపడు — soṭṭapaḍu — "to get dented".
చిన్న ప్రమాదంలో మా కారు తలుపు సొట్టపడింది. cinna pramādaṁlō mā kāru talupu soṭṭapaḍindi. In a small accident the door of our car got dented.
Past (it …): సొట్టపడింది, soṭṭapaḍindi.
సొట్ట, a dent, + పడు. A dented pot is a సొట్ట గిన్నె. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: సొట్టపడింది, it got dented.

The word, taken apart.
సొట్టపడు (soṭṭapaḍu) is native Dravidian: సొట్ట, "a dent, a crookedness" (DEDR 2838), with పడు, "to undergo" (DEDR 3853).
