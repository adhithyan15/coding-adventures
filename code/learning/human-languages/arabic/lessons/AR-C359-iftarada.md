## You'll want to know: افترض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C359-NAZAR359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**افترض** — *iftaraḍa* — "to assume".

> افترضنا أن القطار سيصل مبكرا.
>
> *iftaraḍnā anna al-qiṭār sa-yaṣilu mubakkiran.*
>
> We assumed that the train would arrive early.

Present: يفترض, *yaftariḍ*.

Followed by أن: افترض أن …, assumed that ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افترض** (*iftaraḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-ḍ*, "to make a notch; to make binding" (Lane). It is Form VIII: to take a thing as laid down, to assume it.

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
