## You'll want to know: betreffen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C344-EINFLUSS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**betreffen** — "to concern, to affect".

> Die neue Regel betrifft alle Schüler.
>
> *The new rule concerns all pupils.*

Inseparable and strong: *es betrifft, es hat betroffen*. *Was mich betrifft, …* — as far as I am concerned, …

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*betreffen* is built with *be-* on *treffen* "to hit, to meet", inherited from Old High German *treffan* "to hit" and Proto-Germanic *drepaną* (EtymDB): a rule hits the people it is about.
