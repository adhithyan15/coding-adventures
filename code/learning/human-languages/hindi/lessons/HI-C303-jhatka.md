## You'll want to know: झटका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C303-BHUKAMP303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**झटका** — *jhaṭkā* — "a jolt; a tremor".

> भूकंप का पहला झटका सुबह चार बजे आया।
>
> *bhūkamp kā pahlā jhaṭkā subah cār baje āyā.*
>
> The first tremor of the earthquake came at four in the morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from *\*jhaṭ*, a form CDIAL reconstructs for quick, sudden movements (CDIAL 5327). An earthquake comes in **झटके**; bad news can be a **झटका** too.
