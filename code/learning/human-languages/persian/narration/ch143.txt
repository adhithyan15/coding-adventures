Persian, chapter 143: To swallow, To forget, To teach, To fill, To hand in.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قورت دادن (qurt dâdan) — to swallow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to catch a cold, then the Persian for to get hurt.

You'll want to know: قورت دادن.
قورت دادن — qurt dâdan — "to swallow".

The word, taken apart.
qurt dâdan pairs qurt, "gulp", with dâdan, "to give". Where qurt comes from is not settled. dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".

پر کردن (por kardan) — to fill; to fill in (a form).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for to forget, then the Persian for to teach.

You'll want to know: پر کردن.
پر کردن — por kardan — "to fill; to fill in (a form)".

The word, taken apart.
por kardan pairs por, "full", with kardan, "to make". por is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian purr and further to the Indo-European word behind English full. kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: تحویل دادن.
تحویل دادن — tahvil dâdan — "to hand in, to deliver".

The word, taken apart.
tahvil dâdan pairs tahvil, borrowed from Arabic taḥwīl, "transfer, turning over" (root ḥ-w-l, Lane), with the Persian verb dâdan, "to give". dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".
