## You'll want to know: عقب انداختن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C396-MONSAREF-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عقب انداختن** (*'aqab andâkhtan*) — "to put off, to delay".

> جلسه را یک هفته عقب انداختند.
>
> *jalase râ yek hafte 'aqab andâkhtand.*
>
> They put the meeting off by a week.

*'aqab* "back" + *andâkhtan* "to throw" (present stem *andâz-*): to throw something back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقب انداختن** is literally "to throw back": *'aqab* "back" is usually traced to Arabic *'aqab*, and *andâkhtan* "to throw" is a native Persian verb listed in EtymDB.
