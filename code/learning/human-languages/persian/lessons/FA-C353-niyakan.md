## You'll want to know: نیاکان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C353-DOWRAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نیاکان** (*niyâkân*) — "ancestors, forefathers".

> نیاکان ما در این روستا زندگی میکردند.
>
> *Niyâkân-e mâ dar in rustâ zendegi mikardand.*
>
> Our ancestors lived in this village.

The plural of the rare *niyâ* "grandfather". Literary.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niyâkân* is the plural of *niyâ* "grandfather, forebear", a native Persian word; EtymDB lists no entry.
