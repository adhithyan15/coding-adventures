Sanskrit, chapter 432: Spending and Saving.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
व्ययः (vyayaḥ) — an expense.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for mixes, then the Sanskrit for a touchstone.

You'll want to know: व्ययः.
व्ययः — vyayaḥ — "an expense".
तीर्थयात्रायां महान् व्ययः भवति। tīrthayātrāyāṃ mahān vyayaḥ bhavati. A pilgrimage brings a big expense.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
व्ययः (vyayaḥ) is built from vi, "away", and the root √i, "to go": a going away of money; MW gives vyaya as spending, expense (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
व्ययति (vyayati) — spends; lays out money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an expense, then the Sanskrit for wasteful spending.

You'll want to know: व्ययति.
व्ययति — vyayati — "spends; lays out money".
सः यात्रायां बहु धनं व्ययति। saḥ yātrāyāṃ bahu dhanaṃ vyayati. He spends a lot of money on the journey.
Past (he …): सः यात्रायां धनं व्ययितवान्, saḥ yātrāyāṃ dhanaṃ vyayitavān.

The word, taken apart.
व्ययति (vyayati) comes from the root √vyay, "to spend", which MW takes rather as a verb made from vyaya, "an expense"; MW gives vyayati as to expend or spend, and KRM gives its participle vyayita (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सञ्चिनोति (sañcinoti) — saves up; gathers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for wasteful spending, then the Sanskrit for spends.

You'll want to know: सञ्चिनोति.
सञ्चिनोति — sañcinoti — "saves up; gathers".
सः प्रतिदिनं किञ्चित् धनं सञ्चिनोति। saḥ pratidinaṃ kiñcit dhanaṃ sañcinoti. Every day he saves up a little money.
Past (he …): सः धनं सञ्चितवान्, saḥ dhanaṃ sañcitavān.

The word, taken apart.
सञ्चिनोति (sañcinoti) is built from sam, "together", and the root √ci, "to gather, to heap"; MW gives saṃ-ci as to heap up, accumulate, with the participle saṃcita, "collected" (MW).
