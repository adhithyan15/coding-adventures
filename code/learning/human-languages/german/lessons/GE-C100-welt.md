**die Welt** — "the world".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**Welt** ← Old High German *weralt* ← Proto-Germanic *\*weraldiz*, built from *\*weraz*, "man", and *\*aldiz*, "age" (EtymDB 2.1): "the age of man". English *world* is the same compound.
