German, chapter 316: Wer erzählt?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Erzähler — the narrator, the storyteller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the giant, then the German for the dwarf.

You'll want to know: der Erzähler.
der Erzähler — "the narrator, the storyteller".
Der Erzähler spricht sehr leise. The narrator speaks very quietly.
Plural: die Erzähler. Feminine die Erzählerin.

The word, taken apart.
Erzähler is built on erzählen "to tell", from zählen "to count", Old High German zellen, Proto-Germanic taljaną (EtymDB): to count out events one by one.

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Figur — the character, the figure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the narrator, then the German for the plot.

You'll want to know: die Figur.
die Figur — "the character, the figure".
Die wichtigste Figur im Buch ist ein Junge. The most important character in the book is a boy.
Plural: die Figuren. Also a person's figure, or a chess piece.

The word, taken apart.
Figur was borrowed from Latin figūra "a shape, a form" (EtymDB), built on fingere "to form, to shape" (Lewis & Short).
