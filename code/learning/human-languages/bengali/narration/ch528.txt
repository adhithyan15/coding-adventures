Bengali, chapter 528: Ways and Means.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সঙ্গতি (sônggôti) — financial means.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a rehearsal, then the Bengali for applause.

You'll want to know: সঙ্গতি.
সঙ্গতি — sônggôti — "financial means".
যদি সঙ্গতি থাকত, তাহলে আমরা একটা বড় বাড়ি কিনতাম। Jôdi sônggôti thāktô, tāhôle āmrā ekṭā bôṛo bāṛi kintām. If we had the means, we would buy a big house.

The word, taken apart.
সঙ্গতি (sônggôti) is Sanskrit saṃgati, "coming together, meeting" (Monier-Williams); the sense "what one can afford" is Bengali's own.

ফিকির (phikir) — a way out; a scheme to manage.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Bengali for applause, then the Bengali for financial means.

You'll want to know: ফিকির.
ফিকির — phikir — "a way out; a scheme to manage".
একটা ফিকির পেলে আমরা ঝামেলাটা এড়াতে পারব। Ekṭā phikir pele āmrā jhāmelāṭā eṛāte pārbô. If we find a way out, we'll be able to get around the trouble.

The word, taken apart.
ফিকির (phikir) is usually traced to Arabic fikr, "thought", through Persian: a clever thought that gets you out of a fix.

Lesson 3 of 5.
সামর্থ্য (sāmôrthyô) — ability; the capacity (to do something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for financial means, then the Bengali for a way out.

You'll want to know: সামর্থ্য.
সামর্থ্য — sāmôrthyô — "ability; the capacity (to do something)".
এই কঠিন কাজ করার সামর্থ্য তার আছে। Ei kôṭhin kāj kôrār sāmôrthyô tār āchhe. She has the ability to do this hard work.

The word, taken apart.
সামর্থ্য (sāmôrthyô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sāmarthya, which Monier-Williams gives with a range of senses up to "ability, power".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a way out, then the Bengali for capacity.

You'll want to know: কুলোনো.
কুলোনো — kulono — "to be enough; to suffice".
এই টাকায় মাস না কুলোলে আমি ধার নেব। Ei ṭākāy mās nā kulole āmi dhār nebô. If this money doesn't last the month, I'll borrow.
Past (he/she …): সেই টাকায় তার কুলোল না, sei ṭākāy tār kulolô nā.

The word, taken apart.
কুলোনো (kulono) is said of money, time or food that will or will not stretch far enough; where the word comes from is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
জোটানো (joṭāno) — to scrape together; to manage to find.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for capacity, then the Bengali for to suffice.

You'll want to know: জোটানো.
জোটানো — joṭāno — "to scrape together; to manage to find".
টাকা জোটাতে পারলে মজুরটা ছেলেকে স্কুলে পাঠাবে। Ṭākā joṭāte pārle mojurṭā chheleke skule pāṭhābe. If the labourer can scrape the money together, he will send his son to school.
Past (he/she …): তিনি টিকিটের টাকা জোটালেন, tini ṭikiṭer ṭākā joṭālen.

The word, taken apart.
জোটানো (joṭāno) is the causative of a verb CDIAL lists as Bengali juṭā, "to come together, to come to hand", under a reconstructed yuṭati, "is joined" (CDIAL 10496): making things come to hand.
