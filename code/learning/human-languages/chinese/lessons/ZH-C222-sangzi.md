## You'll want to know: sǎngzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C222-CHILL222-03]; assesses=[] -->

*sǎngzi* — "throat; voice".

> *wǒ sǎngzi téng* — My throat hurts.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sǎngzi* is built from *sǎng* 'throat' and the noun ending 子, inherited from Middle Chinese *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). No dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.
