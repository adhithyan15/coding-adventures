# это зависит от … (éto zavísit ot …) — it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-FUTURE-01, RU-LEX-C59-WEATHER-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains on Saturday, we'll go to the museum". (*Yésli
v subbótu búdet dozhd, my poydyóm v muzéy.*) Then "the weather". (*Pogóda.*)
Sometimes the honest answer to a question is a condition.

## You'll want to know: это зависит от …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-ZAVISIT-OT-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C80-PLACES-04, RU-LEX-ZNAT] -->

> **— Ты пойдёшь в парк? — Не знаю. Это зависит от погоды.** — *ty
> poydyósh v park? ne znáyu. éto zavísit ot pogódy* — Are you going to
> the park? I don't know. It depends on the weather.

**Зависит** is "it depends", from *зависеть*. In quick conversation it can
shrink to **Зависит.** — "It depends."

## Grammar Lens: от and the "of" form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-OT-GENITIVE-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-DO-GENITIVE, RU-GRAMMAR-DO-NA-ROUTE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BILET-DO-NA-01, RU-LEX-C114-THINGS114-01, RU-LEX-C69-TIME2-05] -->

**от** — *ot* — "from, on". What a thing depends on takes the same form
as after *до*: *погода*, **от погоды**; *цена*, **от цены**.

> **Это зависит от цены.** — *éto zavísit ot tsený* — It depends on the
> price.
> **Это зависит от времени.** — *éto zavísit ot vrémeni* — It depends on
> the time. *Время* has its own shape here: **времени**.
> **Это зависит от тебя.** — *éto zavísit ot tebyá* — It's up to you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-ZAVISIT-OT-01] -->

**зависеть** is *за-* with **висеть**, "to hang", which EtymDB 2.1 traces
through Old East Slavic *visěti* to Proto-Slavic *\*visěti*. To depend is
to hang on something. English draws the same picture: *depend* is Latin
*dēpendēre*, "to hang down from" (Lewis & Short).
