## You'll want to know: совпадение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C355-SLUCHAYNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**совпадение** (*sovpadéniye*) — "a coincidence".

> Какое совпадение! Мы родились в один день.
>
> *Kakóye sovpadéniye! My rodílis v odín den.*
>
> What a coincidence! We were born on the same day.

Neuter. From *совпадать*, to coincide, built on *падать*, to fall: two things that fall together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**совпадение** is built on *совпадать* "to coincide", made of *со-* "together" and *падать* "to fall", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*padati* (EtymDB). A coincidence is two things that fall together.
