## You'll want to know: জেঠা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C482-JETHA482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**জেঠা** — *jeṭhā* — "a father's elder brother".

> জেঠা বাবার চেয়ে পাঁচ বছরের বড়।
>
> *jeṭhā bābār cheye pā̃ch bôchhôrer bôṛo.*
>
> My father's elder brother is five years older than my father.

কাকা (taught) is the father's younger brother; জেঠা the elder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জেঠা** (*jeṭhā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under Sanskrit *jyēṣṭhatāta*, "eldest father" (CDIAL 5289).
