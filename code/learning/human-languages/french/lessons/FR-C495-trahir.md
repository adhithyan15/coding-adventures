## You'll want to know: trahir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C495-DISPUTES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**trahir** — "to betray".

> Elle a trahi le secret de son amie.
>
> *She betrayed her friend's secret.*

A regular *-ir* verb: *nous trahissons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trahir* is inherited from Latin *trādere* "to hand over" (Lewis & Short), built from *trāns* and *dare* "to give".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
