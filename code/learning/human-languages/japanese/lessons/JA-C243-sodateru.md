## You'll want to know: そだてる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C243-KIN243-05]; assesses=[] -->

**そだてる** — *sodateru* — "to bring up; to raise".

> **あねは こどもを ひとりで そだてますか。** — *ane wa kodomo o hitori de sodatemasu ka* — "Will my elder sister bring up her child on her own?"

Polite **そだてます**, *sodatemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そだてる** (*sodateru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
