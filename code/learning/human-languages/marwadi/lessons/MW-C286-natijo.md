## You'll want to know: नतीजो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C286-IMTIHAN286-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नतीजो** — *natījo* — "a result".

> म्हारो नतीजो चोखो है।
>
> *mhāro natījo chokho hai.*
>
> My result is good.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नतीजो** (*natījo*) is usually traced, through Persian *natīja*, to Arabic *natīja*, "a result, an outcome"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.
