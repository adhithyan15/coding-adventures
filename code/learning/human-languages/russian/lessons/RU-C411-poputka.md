## You'll want to know: попутка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C411-POPUTCHIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**попутка** (*popútka*) — "a lift (in a passing car), a hitched ride".

> До деревни мы ехали на попутке.
>
> *Do derévni my yékhali na popútke.*
>
> We got to the village in a passing car.

Colloquial. *Ловить попутку* — to hitch a ride.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*попутка* is a colloquial short form of *попутная машина* 'a car going the same way', from *по пути* 'on the way'. *Путь* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *pǫtĭ* (EtymDB).
