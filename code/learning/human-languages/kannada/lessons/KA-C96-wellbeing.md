# ಕ್ಷೇಮವೇ (kṣēmavē) — are you well?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C96-HELLO-03, KA-LEX-C96-HELLO-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for long time, then the Kannada for hello.

## You'll want to know: ಕ್ಷೇಮವೇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C96-HELLO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕ್ಷೇಮವೇ?** (*kṣēmavē?*) — "are you well?", a formal, warm question.
**ಕ್ಷೇಮ** (*kṣēma*) is "welfare"; **‑ಏ** makes it a question.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕ್ಷೇಮವೇ** (*kṣēmavē*) is *kṣēma*, "well-being", from Sanskrit *kṣema*, "ease, happiness" (CDIAL 3745), with *-ē*, the ending that asks.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The formal cousin of hēgiddīrā.
