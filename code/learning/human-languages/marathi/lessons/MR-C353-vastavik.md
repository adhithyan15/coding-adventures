## You'll want to know: वास्तविक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C353-PUSHTI353-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वास्तविक** — *vāstavik* — "actual; real".

> त्याने सांगितलेलं कारण वास्तविक नव्हतं.
>
> *tyāne sāṅgitlelaṁ kāraṇ vāstavik navhataṁ.*
>
> The reason he gave was not the real one.

Also in fact: वास्तविक पाहता, in actual fact.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वास्तविक** (*vāstavik*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vāstavika*, "substantial, real" (Monier-Williams).
