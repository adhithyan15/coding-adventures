## You'll want to know: jiè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C64-HELP64-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jiè* — "to borrow; to lend".

> *wǒ jiè le tā de shū* — I borrowed his book.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiè* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsjaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to borrow'; today the same word also lends.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
