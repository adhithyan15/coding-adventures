## You'll want to know: கடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C68-SHOP-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கடை** (*kaḍai*) — "a shop".

Any shop at all: the tea shop, the cloth shop, the one at the end of the street. Tamil does not
split the word by what is sold — a கடை is a கடை, and what is inside it is said separately.

It ends in the same **ை** as தலை and சட்டை, and it is where every garment of the last chapter
came from.
