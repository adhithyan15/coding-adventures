## You'll want to know: hòubèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C475-BODY475-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hòubèi* — "the back of the body".

> *wǒ gǎnjué hòubèi yǒudiǎnr téng* — My back feels a bit sore.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hòubèi* is built from 后 'behind' and *bèi* 'the back', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwX* and *pwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the spine and back'.

## What you've built
