## You'll want to know: भाडे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C161-VACH161-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भाडे** — *bhāḍe* — "rent; a fare". The money for a room, or for a ride: **खोलीचे भाडे** — *kholīce bhāḍe* — "the rent for the room".
