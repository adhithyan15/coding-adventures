## You'll want to know: आवेगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C449-VISMAYA449-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आवेगः** — *āvegaḥ* — "agitation; a flurry of feeling".

> वार्तया मातुः आवेगः भवति।
>
> *vārtayā mātuḥ āvegaḥ bhavati.*
>
> The news throws mother into a flurry.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आवेगः** (*āvegaḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √vij, "to tremble, to start": a quick stirring of the mind; MW gives "hurry or haste produced by excitement" and "flurry, agitation" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
