## You'll want to know: la evaluación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C597-APRENDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la evaluación** — "the assessment; the exam period".

> La evaluación es en junio.
>
> *The exams are in June.*

In Spanish schools a school year has three *evaluaciones*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*evaluación* is built on *evaluar*, borrowed from French *évaluer*, from Latin *valere* 'to be worth' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
