# うえる (ueru) — to plant

## You'll want to know: うえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C210-PLANT210-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うえる** — *ueru* — "to plant".

> **らいしゅう にわに きを うえます。** — *raishū niwa ni ki o uemasu* — "Next week I will plant a tree in the garden."

Polite **うえます**, *uemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うえる** (*ueru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
