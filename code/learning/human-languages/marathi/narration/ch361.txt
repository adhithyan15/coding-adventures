Marathi, chapter 361: Money and the State.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
महसूल (mahsūl) — revenue; land tax.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for poverty, then the Marathi for literacy.

You'll want to know: महसूल.
महसूल — mahsūl — "revenue; land tax".
सरकारला करातून मोठा महसूल मिळतो. sarkārlā karātūn moṭhā mahsūl miḷto. The government gets a large revenue from taxes.
Masculine.
Masculine: मोठा महसूल (mahsūl); mostly money the state takes in, from taxes and land.

The word, taken apart.
महसूल (mahsūl) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic maḥṣūl, "produce, yield": what the land yields to the state.

You'll want to know: अर्थसंकल्प.
अर्थसंकल्प — arthasaṅkalpa — "a budget".
सरकारने या वर्षीचा अर्थसंकल्प मांडला. sarkārne yā varṣīcā arthasaṅkalpa māṇḍlā. The government presented this year's budget.
Masculine.
अर्थसंकल्प (arthasaṅkalpa) मांडणे, to present a budget.

The word, taken apart.
अर्थसंकल्प (arthasaṅkalpa) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit artha, "wealth, aim", and saṁkalpa, "will, resolve" (Monier-Williams): a plan for money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

आर्थिक — ārthik — "economic; financial".

Invariable; built on अर्थ, wealth.

The word, taken apart.
आर्थिक (ārthik) is a tatsama, Sanskrit ārthika (Monier-Williams), built on artha, "wealth, aim"; Marathi uses it for money matters.
