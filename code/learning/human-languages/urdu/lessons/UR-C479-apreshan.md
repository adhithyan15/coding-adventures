## You'll want to know: آپریشن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C479-WARD479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آپریشن** — *āpreshan* — "an operation (surgery)".

> کل صبح دادی کا آپریشن ہو گا۔
>
> *kal subah dādī kā āpreshan ho gā.*
>
> My grandmother is having an operation tomorrow morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آپریشن** (*āpreshan*) is borrowed from English *operation*.
