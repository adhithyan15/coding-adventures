# praecipitō, praecipitāre — to hurl headlong; to fall headlong

## You'll want to know: praecipitō, praecipitāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C314-OFFENDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**praecipitō, praecipitāre** — "to hurl headlong; to fall headlong".

> Equus subitō sē in fossam praecipitāvit.
>
> *The horse suddenly threw itself headlong into the ditch.*

*praecipitō, praecipitāre, praecipitāvī, praecipitātum*, from *praeceps*. English *precipitate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**praecipitō** is built on *praeceps*, "headlong" (EtymDB): to throw head first. English *precipitate* comes from it.
