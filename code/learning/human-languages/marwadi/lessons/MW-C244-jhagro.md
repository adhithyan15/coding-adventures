## You'll want to know: झगड़ो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C244-JHAGRO244-01]; assesses=[] -->

**झगड़ो** — *jhagṛo* — "a quarrel".

> झगड़ो मत करो।
>
> *jhagṛo mat karo.*
>
> Don't quarrel.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झगड़ो** (*jhagṛo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhagaḍ*, "quarrel"; CDIAL lists Marwari *jhagṛo* under it (CDIAL 5321).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
