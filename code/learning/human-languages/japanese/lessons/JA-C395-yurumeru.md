## You'll want to know: ゆるめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C395-KOSHOU395-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆるめる** — *yurumeru* — "to loosen; to ease off".

> **かどの まえで そくどを ゆるめました。** — *kado no mae de sokudo o yurumemashita* — "I slowed down before the corner."

Polite **ゆるめます**, *yurumemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **うんてんしゅが そくどを ゆるめました。**, *untenshu ga sokudo o yurumemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆるめる** (*yurumeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
