Arabic, chapter 411: Rules and wrongs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أجاز (ajāza) — to authorize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to comply, then the Arabic for to exempt.

You'll want to know: أجاز.
أجاز — ajāza — "to authorize".
أجاز الأستاذ استعمال القاموس. ajāza al-ustādh istiʿmāl al-qāmūs. The professor authorized the use of the dictionary.
Present: يجيز, yujīz.

The word, taken apart.
أجاز (ajāza) is built on the native Arabic root j-w-z, "to pass, to go beyond" (Lane). It is Form IV, to let a thing pass: to allow, to authorize.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to exempt, then the Arabic for to authorize.

You'll want to know: خضع.
خضع — khaḍaʿa — "to be subject to".
يخضع الجميع للقانون. yakhḍaʿu al-jamīʿ li-l-qānūn. Everyone is subject to the law.
Present: يخضع, yakhḍaʿ.
It takes لـ: خضع (khaḍaʿa) لـ …. خضع (khaḍaʿa) لعملية, underwent an operation.

The word, taken apart.
خضع (khaḍaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-ḍ-ʿ, "to be lowly, submissive" (Lane). Khaḍaʿa is a Form I verb: to submit, to be subject to.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ظلم (ẓalama) — to wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to authorize, then the Arabic for to be subject to.

You'll want to know: ظلم.
ظلم — ẓalama — "to wrong".
ظلم المدير العامل الجديد. ẓalama al-mudīr al-ʿāmil al-jadīd. The manager wronged the new worker.
Present: يظلم, yaẓlim.

The word, taken apart.
ظلم (ẓalama) is built on the native Arabic root ẓ-l-m, "to put a thing in the wrong place" (Lane). Ẓalama is a Form I verb: to do someone wrong. ظالم, unjust, is its active participle.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to be subject to, then the Arabic for to wrong.

You'll want to know: كافح.
كافح — kāfaḥa — "to struggle against".
تكافح الدولة الفقر. tukāfiḥu ad-dawla al-faqr. The state is fighting poverty.
Present: يكافح, yukāfiḥ.
What is fought is the direct object: كافح (kāfaḥa) الفساد, fought corruption.

The word, taken apart.
كافح (kāfaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root k-f-ḥ, "to meet face to face" (Lane). It is Form III, to face an enemy head on: to struggle against.

Lesson 5 of 5.
أقر (aqarra) — to approve formally.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to wrong, then the Arabic for to struggle against.

You'll want to know: أقر.
أقر — aqarra — "to approve formally".
أقر المجلس الخطة الجديدة. aqarra al-majlis al-khiṭṭa al-jadīda. The council formally approved the new plan.
Present: يقر, yuqirr.
Also to admit: أقر (aqarra) بالخطأ, admitted the mistake.

The word, taken apart.
أقر (aqarra) is built on the native Arabic root q-r-r, "to settle, to be still" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make a thing settled: to approve it formally, or to admit it.
