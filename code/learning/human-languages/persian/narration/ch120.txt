Lesson 2 of 5.
دمپایی (dampâyi) — slippers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a tie, then the Persian for a jacket.

You'll want to know: دمپایی.
دمپایی — dampâyi — "slippers".

The word, taken apart.
dampâyi is built on pâ, "foot", an inherited word (Old Persian pād-), with dam, "beside", and the ending -i: the thing at the foot.

Lesson 4 of 5.
چشمه (cheshme) — a spring (of water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for slippers, then the Persian for a pond.

You'll want to know: چشمه.
چشمه — cheshme — "a spring (of water)".

The word, taken apart.
cheshme is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian čašmag, "spring, source" (EtymDB). It is built on čašm, "eye" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
کویر (kavir) — a salt desert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pond, then the Persian for a spring.

You'll want to know: کویر.
کویر — kavir — "a salt desert".

The word, taken apart.
kavir is a native Iranian word: EtymDB traces it to a Parthian word for "salt desert, salt marsh", written gwyr. Parthian was a Middle Iranian sister of Middle Persian.
