## You'll want to know: le bilan
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C286-CHIFFRES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le bilan** — "the final count, the outcome; the review".

> Le bilan de l'année est très positif.
>
> *The outcome of the year is very positive.*

After a disaster, *le bilan* is the number of dead and injured. *Faire le bilan* — to take stock.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bilan* was borrowed from Italian *bilancio* "balance" (EtymDB), built on Late Latin *bilanx* "with two scales" (Lewis & Short).
