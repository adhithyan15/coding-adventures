## You'll want to know: फुलणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C445-ANKUR445-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फुलणे** — *phulṇe* — "to bloom".

> वसंतात बागेत गुलाब फुलले.
>
> *vasantāt bāget gulāb phulle.*
>
> In spring the roses bloomed in the garden.

Past (he …): तो फुलला, *to phullā*.

Flowers फुलतात; a happy face फुलतो too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फुलणे** (*phulṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *phulṇẽ*, "to swell, blossom", under Sanskrit *phullati*, "expands, opens" (CDIAL 9093).
