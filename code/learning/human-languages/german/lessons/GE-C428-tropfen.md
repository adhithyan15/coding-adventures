## You'll want to know: tropfen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C428-ABFLUSS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**tropfen** — "to drip".

> Die ganze Nacht tropft das Wasser in der Dusche.
>
> *The water drips in the shower all night.*

*Der Tropfen* — the drop. *Es tropft* — it is dripping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tropfen* is inherited through Middle High German *tropfen* from Old High German *tropfōn* (EtymDB). English *drop* is a cousin.
