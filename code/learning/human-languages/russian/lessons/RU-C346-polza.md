## You'll want to know: польза
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C346-PLYUSY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**польза** (*pólza*) — "benefit, use".

> От этого лекарства нет никакой пользы.
>
> *Ot étogo lekárstva net nikakóy pólzy.*
>
> This medicine does no good at all.

Feminine. *В пользу* + genitive — in favour of. *Полезный* (useful) is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**польза** was borrowed from Church Slavonic *польза*, from Proto-Slavic *\*polĭdza*, built from *\*po* "by, after" and *\*lĭga* "possibility", the root that also gives *нельзя* (EtymDB).
