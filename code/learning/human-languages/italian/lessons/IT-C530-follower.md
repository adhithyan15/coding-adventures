## You'll want to know: il follower
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C530-ONLINE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il follower** — "follower (online)".

> Ha più di mille follower.
>
> *She has more than a thousand followers.*

No plural *-s*: *i follower*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*follower* was borrowed from English *follower*, built on *follow*, from Proto-Germanic *fulgijaną* "to follow" (EtymDB).
