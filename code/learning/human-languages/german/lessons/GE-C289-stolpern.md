## You'll want to know: stolpern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C289-STURZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**stolpern** — "to trip, to stumble".

> Er stolperte über einen Stein.
>
> *He tripped over a stone.*

Perfekt with *sein*: *Er ist gestolpert*. *Über etwas stolpern*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stolpern* may be related to Middle High German *stolzen* "to limp", but EtymDB lists no parent and the origin is not settled.
