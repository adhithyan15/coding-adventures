## You'll want to know: раскатывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C331-TESTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**раскатывать** (*raskátyvat*) — "to roll out".

> Он раскатал тесто на столе.
>
> *On raskatál tésto na stolé.*
>
> He rolled out the dough on a table.

Imperfective; perfective *раскатать* (*raskatát*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*раскатывать* is built on *рас-* "apart" and *катать* "to roll", an inherited Slavic verb (EtymDB lists *катать* "to roll" with no parent).
