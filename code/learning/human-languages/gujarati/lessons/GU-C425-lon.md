## You'll want to know: લોન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C425-LOAN425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લોન** — *lon* — "a bank loan".

> તેણે ઘર માટે બેંકમાંથી લોન લીધી.
>
> *teṇe ghar māṭe beṅkmā̃thī lon līdhī.*
>
> He took a loan from the bank for a house.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લોન** (*lon*) is borrowed from English *loan*.
