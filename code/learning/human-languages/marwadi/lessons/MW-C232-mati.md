## You'll want to know: माटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C232-EARTH232-02]; assesses=[] -->

**माटी** — *māṭī* — "soil; earth (the material)".

> खेत मांय माटी गीली है।
>
> *khet mā̃y māṭī gīlī hai.*
>
> The soil in the field is wet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माटी** (*māṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mṛttikā*, "earth, clay"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *māṭī*. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 10286).
