## You'll want to know: છાપવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C250-COMPUTER250-05]; assesses=[] -->

**છાપવું** — *chāpvũ* — "to print".

> **તે અરજી છાપે છે.** — *te arjī chāpe che* — "He prints the application."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છાપવું** (*chāpvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*chapp*, "press, cover", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to print" (CDIAL 4994).
