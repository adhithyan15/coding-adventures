# नानी (nānī) — grandmother, the mother's mother

## You'll want to know: नानी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C116-GRAND-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नानी** (*nānī*) — "grandmother", your mother's mother.

**नानी का घर** — "grandmother's house", the mother's family home, where many summer holidays
are spent.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

All four grandparents, each with a name of their own.
