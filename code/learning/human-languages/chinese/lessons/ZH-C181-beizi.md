## You'll want to know: bèizi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C181-BED181-01]; assesses=[] -->

*bèizi* — "quilt; duvet".

> *tiānqì lěng le wǒmen yào huàn bèizi* — It's turned cold: we need to change the quilt.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bèizi* is built from *bèi* 'bedclothes' and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjeX* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). In the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
