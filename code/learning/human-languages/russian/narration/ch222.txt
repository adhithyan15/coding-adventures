Russian, chapter 222: Food, Dish, Menu, Bun, Kefir.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
еда (yedá) — food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a vase, then the Russian for a painting.

You'll want to know: еда.
еда (yedá) — "food".

The word, taken apart.
еда (yedá) from Proto-Slavic ěda, "food", on the root of есть, "to eat" — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian ėdà, "food (for animals)", and Old Norse át, "food"; English eat is a cousin.

You'll want to know: блюдо.
блюдо (blyúdo) — "a dish".

The word, taken apart.
блюдо (blyúdo) — an old loan from Gothic biuþs, "a table, a dish", from biudan, "to offer" (Vasmer); Finnish pöytä, "table", has the same source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
булка (búlka) — a bun, a roll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dish, then the Russian for a menu.

You'll want to know: булка.
булка (búlka) — "a bun, a roll".

The word, taken apart.
булка (búlka) — not found in older texts in this sense, so a loan from Polish bułka, and the Polish from Italian bulla or French boule, "a ball", has been assumed. Vasmer finds no evidence in the sounds for a loan and allows a native link with булава, "a mace".

Lesson 5 of 5.
кефир (kefír) — kefir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a menu, then the Russian for a bun.

You'll want to know: кефир.
кефир (kefír) — "kefir".

The word, taken apart.
кефир (kefír) — the drink and its name came from the Caucasus, but the first source is not established (Trubachev, in Vasmer); Mingrelian kipuri, "sour milk", has been compared.
