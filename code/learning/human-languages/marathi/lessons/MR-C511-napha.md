## You'll want to know: नफा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C511-DHANDA511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नफा** — *naphā* — "profit".

> या वर्षी दुकानाला चांगला नफा झाला.
>
> *yā varṣī dukānālā cāṅglā naphā jhālā.*
>
> The shop made a good profit this year.

Masculine.

Its opposite is तोटा: नफा होणे, तोटा होणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नफा** (*naphā*) is a loan from Persian *nafʿ*, "gain", usually traced to Arabic *nafʿ*, "benefit".
