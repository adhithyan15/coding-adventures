# dēspērātiō, dēspērātiōnis — despair, hopelessness

## You'll want to know: dēspērātiō, dēspērātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C498-SPES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dēspērātiō, dēspērātiōnis** — "despair, hopelessness".

> In dēspērātiōne nautae deōs ōrant.
>
> *In their despair the sailors pray to the gods.*

Feminine, third declension. English *desperation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēspērātiō** is the action noun of *dēspērō* "to despair" (Lewis & Short). English *desperation* comes from it.
