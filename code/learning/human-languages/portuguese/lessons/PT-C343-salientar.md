## You'll want to know: salientar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C343-ESCLARECER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**salientar** — "to point out, to highlight".

> Ela salientou que o hotel fica perto da praia.
>
> *She pointed out that the hotel is near the beach.*

Close to *sublinhar*: *é de salientar que…* — it is worth pointing out that…

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*salientar* is built on *saliente* "prominent" (EtymDB), from Latin *saliēns*, the present participle of *salīre* "to leap, to jump" (Lewis & Short): what jumps out at you.
