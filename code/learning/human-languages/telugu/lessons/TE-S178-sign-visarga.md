# ః (ḥ) — the visarga inside దుఃఖం

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-04, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-137, TE-LEX-C322-DUHKHAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **ం** and **ఖ** once each, and name them. (The sunna, *ṁ*, and *kha*.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **దుఃఖం** (*duḥkhaṁ*), "grief". Both of those are in it. This lesson takes out the one mark
you have not written and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ః
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-178]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C322-DUHKHAM-01] -->

**ః** — the visarga, *ḥ*: a short breath after a vowel. It is two small rings, one above the other, written after
its syllable: **దు** + **ః** → **దుః**, *duḥ*.

Do not confuse it with **ం**, one ring, which is *ṁ*. The visarga lives in words taken from Sanskrit, and most of
them are learned words; **దుఃఖం** is the one you will meet most.

## Writing: ః — copy the printed shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-178] -->

Write **ః** yourself, slowly, and larger than it is printed: two small rings the same size, one above the other,
both on the right of the syllable they follow.

> This book does not yet say where the pen starts on this shape, or which way it travels. That is taught with real variation from school to school, and it waits here until it can be written down from a source. Copying the printed shape needs no such source, and it puts the shape in your hand in the meantime.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-04, TE-LEX-C322-DUHKHAM-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-178] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which mark is this — ః? (**The visarga**, *ḥ*, a breath.) Which word did it come from? (**దుఃఖం**,
*duḥkhaṁ*, "grief".) How is it different from **ం**? (**Two rings, not one.**)
