## You'll want to know: intellegentia, intellegentiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C407-SCIENTIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**intellegentia, intellegentiae** — "understanding, intelligence".

> Intellegentia eius omnēs superat.
>
> *His understanding is greater than anyone's.*

*intellegentia, intellegentiae* f., the noun of *intellegō*. English *intelligence*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*intellegentia* is the noun built on *intellegō* "to understand" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *inter* "between" and *legō* "to pick": to choose between. English *intelligence* comes from it.
