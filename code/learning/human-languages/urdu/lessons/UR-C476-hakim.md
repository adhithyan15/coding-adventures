## You'll want to know: حکیم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C476-DOCTOR476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حکیم** — *hakīm* — "a traditional physician".

> دادا حکیم کی دوا کھاتے ہیں۔
>
> *dādā hakīm kī davā khāte haiṅ.*
>
> My grandfather takes medicine from a traditional physician.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حکیم** (*hakīm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥakīm*, "a wise man"; in Urdu it is the healer of the old Greek and Arabic medicine.
