Arabic, chapter 245: Necklace, Size, Telephone, Mobile phone, Screen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قلادة (qilāda) — a necklace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for pineapple, then the Arabic for almonds.

You'll want to know: قلادة.
قلادة — qilāda — "a necklace".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does قلادة (qilāda) mean? ("A necklace".) Say it once more.
