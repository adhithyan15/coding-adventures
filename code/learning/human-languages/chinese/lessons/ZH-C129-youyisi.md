## You'll want to know: yǒuyìsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C129-PLACE129-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒuyìsi* — "interesting".

> *xué hànyǔ hěn yǒuyìsi* — Learning Chinese is interesting.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒuyìsi* is built from 有 'to have' and *yìsi* 'meaning', the *yìsi* of *bù hǎoyìsi*: to have meaning. The parts are inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuwX*, *'iH* and *si* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
