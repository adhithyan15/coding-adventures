## You'll want to know: बोवणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C238-SOW238-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बोवणो** — *bovṇo* — "to sow".

> हूं काल खेत मांय बाजरो बोसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl khet mā̃y bājro bosū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll sow millet in the field.

Past (I …): म्हैं बाजरो बोयो, *mhaĩ bājro boyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बोवणो** (*bovṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vapati*, "scatters, sows"; CDIAL lists Hindi *boyā*, "sows", and names a Gujarati verb beside it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11282).
