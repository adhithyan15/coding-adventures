## You'll want to know: અનુમાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C365-SIDDHANT365-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અનુમાન** — *anumān* — "an inference".

> આકાશ જોઈને દાદાએ વરસાદનું અનુમાન કર્યું.
>
> *ākāś joīne dādāe varsādnũ anumān karyũ.*
>
> Looking at the sky, grandfather guessed that rain was coming.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અનુમાન** (*anumān*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *anumāna*, "an inference, a conclusion from signs" (Monier-Williams).
