## You'll want to know: أخفق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C372-AQABA372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أخفق** — *akhfaqa* — "to fall short".

> أخفق الفريق في المباراة الأخيرة.
>
> *akhfaqa al-farīq fī al-mubārā al-akhīra.*
>
> The team fell short in the last match.

Present: يخفق, *yukhfiq*.

It takes في: أخفق في …. Compare فشل, failure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أخفق** (*akhfaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-f-q*, "to flap, to beat" (Lane). Lane gives Form IV for one who sought a thing and did not get it: to fall short.
