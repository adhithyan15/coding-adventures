## Grammar Lens: four complete turns

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C267-SCHOOL-PLAN-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-04, SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-05, SA-LEX-C255-SCH255-04, SA-LEX-C18-ASK-01, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-02, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-04, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02] -->

Put four known pieces together:

> **परीक्षा कदा भविष्यति?**
>
> *Parīkṣā kadā bhaviṣyati?*
>
> When will the exam be?

Answer from the smallest possible timetable:

> **श्वः परीक्षा भविष्यति।**
>
> *Śvaḥ parīkṣā bhaviṣyati.*
>
> The exam will be tomorrow.

This is one useful school-time pattern. It does not teach a new future
paradigm or a complete timetable system. Complete the bounded plan with two
whole schoolwork frames:

> **गृहकार्यं करिष्यामि।**
>
> *Gṛhakāryaṃ kariṣyāmi.*
>
> I will do the homework.

> **पाठं पठिष्यामि।**
>
> *Pāṭhaṃ paṭhiṣyāmi.*
>
> I will read or study the lesson.

Own those two lines as complete frames. In particular, **पाठं** belongs to the
whole second frame here; do not use it to infer a masculine accusative rule.
