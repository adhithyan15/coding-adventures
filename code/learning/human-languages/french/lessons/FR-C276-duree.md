## You'll want to know: la durée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C276-EPOQUE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la durée** — "the length, the duration".

> La durée du film est de deux heures.
>
> *The film is two hours long.*

*Pour une durée de six mois* — for a period of six months.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*durée* is built on *durer* "to last", which is inherited from Latin *dūrāre* "to hold out, to last" (EtymDB).
