Hindi, chapter 516: A Leak in the Roof.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
टपकाना (ṭapkānā) — to let drip; to drip (something) in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to tighten, then the Hindi for to rattle.

You'll want to know: टपकाना.
टपकाना — ṭapkānā — "to let drip; to drip (something) in".
पुराना नल रात भर पानी टपकाता है। Purānā nal rāt bhar pānī ṭapkātā hai. The old tap drips water all night.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने आँख में दवा टपकाई, usne āṁkh meṁ davā ṭapkāī.

The word, taken apart.
टपकाना (ṭapkānā) is the causative of ṭapaknā, "to drip", which CDIAL files under ṭapp, a reconstructed form (CDIAL 5444).

Lesson 2 of 5.
दरकना (daraknā) — to crack, to split.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to rattle, then the Hindi for to let drip.

You'll want to know: दरकना.
दरकना — daraknā — "to crack, to split".
भूकंप के बाद दीवार दरक गई। Bhūkamp ke bād dīvār darak gaī. After the earthquake the wall cracked.
Past (it …): वह शीशा दरक गया, vah śīśā darak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
दरकना (daraknā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit darayati, "causes to split" (CDIAL 6192). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to be torn".

Lesson 4 of 5.
छेद (ched) — a hole.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to crack, to split, then the Hindi for a pipe.

You'll want to know: छेद.
छेद — ched — "a hole".
बाल्टी में एक छोटा छेद है। Bālṭī meṁ ek choṭā ched hai. There is a small hole in the bucket.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
छेद (ched) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit cheda, "cutting off; a section, an incision" (Monier-Williams); Hindi uses it for a hole.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ढलकना (ḍhalaknā) — to trickle down; to roll off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a pipe, then the Hindi for a hole.

You'll want to know: ढलकना.
ढलकना — ḍhalaknā — "to trickle down; to roll off".
छत से पानी ढलककर आँगन में गिरा। Chat se pānī ḍhalakkar āṁgan meṁ girā. Water ran off the roof and fell into the courtyard.
Past (it …): वह पानी ढलक गया, vah pānī ḍhalak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
ढलकना (ḍhalaknā) is a tadbhava, inherited from ḍhalati, "bends over, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5581). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to lean over, be spilt".
