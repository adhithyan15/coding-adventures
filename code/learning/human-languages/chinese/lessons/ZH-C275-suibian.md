## You'll want to know: suíbiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C275-SORRY275-05]; assesses=[] -->

*suíbiàn* — "as you like; casual".

> *nǐ suíbiàn chī* — Help yourself to anything.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suíbiàn* is built from *suí* 'to follow' and *biàn* 'convenient', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zjwe* and *bjienH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to follow' and the second 'to benefit'.
