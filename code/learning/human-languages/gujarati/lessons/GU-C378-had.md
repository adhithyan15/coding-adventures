## You'll want to know: હદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C378-HAD378-01]; assesses=[] -->

**હદ** — *had* — "a limit".

> જો તમે હદ ઓળંગશો તો હું ગુરુને ફરિયાદ કરીશ.
>
> *jo tame had oḷangśo to hũ gurune phariyād karīś.*
>
> If you cross the line, I will complain to the teacher.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હદ** (*had*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥadd*, "a boundary, a limit", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
