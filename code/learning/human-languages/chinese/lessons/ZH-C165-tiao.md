## You'll want to know: tiáo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C165-COAT165-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tiáo* — "measure word for long things (skirts, scarves, trousers, fish, roads)".

> *wǒ mǎi le liǎng tiáo kùzi* — I bought two pairs of trousers.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tiáo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a small branch': a long, thin thing. Its use for counting is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
