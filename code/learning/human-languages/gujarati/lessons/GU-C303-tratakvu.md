## You'll want to know: ત્રાટકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C303-VAVAZODU303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ત્રાટકવું** — *trāṭakvũ* — "to strike (of a storm or an attack)".

> વાવાઝોડું સવારે ગામ પર ત્રાટક્યું.
>
> *vāvājhoḍũ savāre gām par trāṭakyũ.*
>
> The cyclone struck the village in the morning.

Past (it …): તે વાવાઝોડું ત્રાટક્યું, *te vāvājhoḍũ trāṭakyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ત્રાટકવું** (*trāṭakvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. Storms and armies **ત્રાટકે છે**: they fall on a place suddenly and hard.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
