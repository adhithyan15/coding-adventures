# بستگی دارد (bastegi dârad) — it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-REAL-CONDITION-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BIYAYAD-01, FA-LEX-C37-VERBS37-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, we'll stay at home". (*Agar bârân biyâyad, dar
khâne mimânim.*) Then "to tie, to close". (*Bastan.*)

[PAUSE 1s] Asked about a plan, you often cannot answer yes or no yet. The
honest answer is "it depends", and then the condition.

## You'll want to know: بستگی دارد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-BASTEGI-DARAD-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C208-CHIZ208-01, FA-LEX-C87-LOC87-03, FA-LEX-BALE-01, FA-GRAMMAR-AGAR-SUBJ-01, FA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-MI-01] -->

**بستگی دارد** — *bastegi dârad* — "it depends".

> — **فردا به پارک میروید؟** — *fardâ be pârk miravid?* — Are you going to the
> park tomorrow?
>
> — **بستگی دارد. اگر هوا خوب باشد، بله.** — *bastegi dârad. agar havâ khub
> bâshad, bale.* — It depends. If the weather is good, yes.

To name what it depends on, add *be*, "to": **بستگی دارد به هوا**, *bastegi
dârad be havâ*, "it depends on the weather". The *be* phrase can also come
first: *be havâ bastegi dârad*. And *be shomâ bastegi dârad*, "it depends on
you", is a polite "it's up to you".

The verb is *dâshtan*, so it takes no *mi-*: *dârad*.

## The phrase, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-DASHTAN, FA-LEX-C290-GOZASHTE-01] -->

*bastegi* is *baste*, "bound, tied", with *-gi*, which makes a noun of it:
"boundness". *Baste* is the participle of *bastan*, "to tie, to close", built
the way *gozashte* was built from its past stem *gozasht*. So *bastegi dârad be havâ* is
"it has a binding to the weather".

EtymDB 2.1 takes *bastan* back to an Indo-European root meaning "to bind, to
tie", the same root it gives for English **bind**.
