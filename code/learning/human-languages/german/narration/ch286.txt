German, chapter 286: Vor Gericht.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Urteil — the verdict, the judgement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to forge, then the German for to blackmail.

You'll want to know: das Urteil.
das Urteil — "the verdict, the judgement".
Das Urteil kam am Freitag. The verdict came on Friday.
Plural: die Urteile. Ein Urteil fällen — to pass judgement.

The word, taken apart.
Urteil goes back to Proto-Germanic uzdailiją "judgement" (EtymDB), from uz- "out" and the root of Teil "part": what is dealt out. English ordeal is a cousin.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: der Richter.
der Richter — "the judge".
Der Richter hörte beiden Seiten zu. The judge listened to both sides.
Plural: die Richter. Feminine die Richterin.

The word, taken apart.
Richter is built on richten "to judge, to put right", from Old High German rihten, Proto-Germanic rihtijaną (EtymDB).

Der Richter verurteilte ihn zu einer hohen Strafe. The judge sentenced him to a heavy penalty.

verurteilen is built on Urteil "verdict", from Proto-Germanic uzdailiją "judgement" (EtymDB), with the prefix ver-.

Lesson 4 of 5.
schuldig — guilty; owing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the judge, then the German for to sentence.

You'll want to know: schuldig.
schuldig — "guilty; owing".
Das Gericht fand den Mann schuldig. The court found the man guilty.
Also 'owing': Du bist mir zehn Euro schuldig.

The word, taken apart.
schuldig is inherited from Old High German sculdig (EtymDB), built on Schuld "guilt, debt".

Lesson 5 of 5.

unschuldig — "innocent".
Am Ende war die Frau unschuldig. In the end the woman was innocent.
The opposite of schuldig.

The word, taken apart.
unschuldig joins un- "not" and schuldig "guilty", from Old High German sculdig (EtymDB).
