## You'll want to know: लक्ष्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C390-LAKSHYA390-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लक्ष्य** — *lakṣya* — "a goal, a target".

> अगर तुम्हारा लक्ष्य साफ़ होगा तो रास्ता आसान होगा।
>
> *agar tumhārā lakṣya sāf hogā to rāstā āsān hogā.*
>
> If your goal is clear, the way will be easy.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लक्ष्य** (*lakṣya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *lakṣya*, "what is kept in view; a mark" (Monier-Williams).
