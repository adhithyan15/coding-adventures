## A2 core — chapters 152-165

Chapters 152-165 are the track's first grammar. They teach one form, or one
tight pair, a lesson: **वो**, then *am / is / are*, then the present for *I*,
*he* and the polite *you*, the vowel stems, **म्हारो / म्हारी**, a noun with
**मांय, माथै, कनै** and **सूं**, then **कोनी** and **मत**, the rising yes-or-no
question, **घरै**, **गयो, आयो** and **-्यो**, **म्हैं** with a past that agrees
with the thing, and the first-person **-सूं** future. Two practical readings
close it. All five A2 spine nodes are now realized; letter anchoring stands at
cold 0, buildsToward 49, unread ऋ ऐ औ ञ ष, unwritten 0.

What the A2 rung still needs is vocabulary: 640 headwords and 89 verbs against
1,200 and 120, which the A2 vocabulary tranches (chapters 166-290) are planned
to supply. Every paradigm cell was taught only where a CDIAL Marwari or Old
Marwari row, or the track's own text, prints it. The cells no reachable source
prints wait: the feminine and plural pronouns, the first-person plural and its
endings, the past copula, the third-person and plural futures, noun genitives
with **रो / री / रा**, **नै**, **में** and the oblique.
