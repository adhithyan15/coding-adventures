## You'll want to know: బొప్పి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C518-GAYAM518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**బొప్పి** — *boppi* — "a bump; a lump on the head".

> తలుపుకు తగిలి తమ్ముడి నుదుటి మీద బొప్పి కట్టింది.
>
> *talupuku tagili tammuḍi nuduṭi mīda boppi kaṭṭindi.*
>
> My little brother hit the door and a bump came up on his forehead.

బొప్పి కట్టు, a bump comes up. It belongs with పొంగు, to swell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బొప్పి** (*boppi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4469 lists Telugu *boppi*, "a swelling", in the article of *poṅgu*, "to bubble up, to swell".
