Japanese, chapter 364: Damage and Compensation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
そんがい (songai) — damage; a loss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for half price, then the Japanese for luxury.

You'll want to know: そんがい.
そんがい — songai — "damage; a loss".
たいふうが きたら、そんがいが あるでしょう。 — taifū ga kitara, songai ga aru deshō — "If the typhoon comes, there will probably be damage."

The word, taken apart.
そんがい (songai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are son, "loss" (Middle Chinese swonX) and gai, "harm" (hajH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
べんしょうする (benshō suru) — to pay for damage; to compensate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for luxury, then the Japanese for damage to property.

You'll want to know: べんしょうする.
べんしょうする — benshō suru — "to pay for damage; to compensate".
おさらを わったら、べんしょうして ください。 — osara o wattara, benshō shite kudasai — "If you break a plate, please pay for it."
Polite べんしょうします, benshōshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): きゃくが べんしょうしました。, kyaku ga benshō shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
べんしょうする (benshō suru) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb suru, "to do". The noun, benshō, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: ben, "distinguish" and shō, "reparation" (KANJIDIC2; JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
おさめる (osameru) — to pay taxes; to pay (a fee); to put away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for damage to property, then the Japanese for to pay for damage.

You'll want to know: おさめる.
おさめる — osameru — "to pay taxes; to pay (a fee); to put away".
らいげつに なったら、ぜいきんを おさめます。 — raigetsu ni nattara, zeikin o osamemasu — "Next month, I will pay my taxes."
Polite おさめます, osamemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): ちちが ぜいきんを おさめました。, chichi ga zeikin o osamemashita.

The word, taken apart.
おさめる (osameru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 4 of 5.
うけもつ (ukemotsu) — to be in charge of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to pay for damage, then the Japanese for to pay taxes.

You'll want to know: うけもつ.
うけもつ — ukemotsu — "to be in charge of".
せんぱいが やめたら、あにが しごとを うけもつでしょう。 — senpai ga yametara, ani ga shigoto o ukemotsu deshō — "If my senior leaves, my brother will probably take charge of the work."
Polite うけもちます, ukemochimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): あにが しごとを うけもちました。, ani ga shigoto o ukemochimashita.

The word, taken apart.
うけもつ (ukemotsu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from uke, "receiving (ukeru)" and motsu, "to hold", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
