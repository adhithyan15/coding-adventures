## You'll want to know: अकाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C306-AKAL306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अकाल** — *akāl* — "a famine".

> अकाल की वजह यह थी कि उस साल बारिश नहीं हुई।
>
> *akāl kī vajah yah thī ki us sāl bāriś nahīṃ huī.*
>
> The reason for the famine was that the rains failed that year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अकाल** (*akāl*) is Sanskrit *akāla*, "a wrong or bad time" (Monier-Williams). In Hindi it means a famine; the dictionary gives the older sense, and the step to a time of hunger is the language's own. A famine **पड़ता है**: it falls.
