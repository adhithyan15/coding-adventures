Marathi, chapter 310: The Morning Paper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वृत्तपत्र (vṛttapatra) — a newspaper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to pass away, then the Marathi for to arise.

You'll want to know: वृत्तपत्र.
वृत्तपत्र — vṛttapatra — "a newspaper".
आजोबा रोज सकाळी वृत्तपत्र वाचतात. ājobā roj sakāḷī vṛttapatra vāctāt. Grandpa reads the newspaper every morning.
Neuter.
Also वर्तमानपत्र, and in speech पेपर.

The word, taken apart.
वृत्तपत्र (vṛttapatra) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit vṛtta, 'what has happened, an event', and patra, 'a leaf, a letter' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
दैनिक (dainik) — a daily paper; daily.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to arise, then the Marathi for a newspaper.

You'll want to know: दैनिक.
दैनिक — dainik — "a daily paper; daily".
माझे बाबा रोज दोन दैनिकं वाचतात. mājhe bābā roj don dainikaṁ vāctāt. My father reads two daily papers every day.
Neuter.
Neuter as a noun, plural दैनिकं; as an adjective: दैनिक (dainik) काम, daily work.

The word, taken apart.
दैनिक (dainik) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit dainika, 'daily' (Monier-Williams), built on dina, 'a day'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
साप्ताहिक (sāptāhik) — a weekly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a newspaper, then the Marathi for a daily paper.

You'll want to know: साप्ताहिक.
साप्ताहिक — sāptāhik — "a weekly".
हे साप्ताहिक दर गुरुवारी येतं. he sāptāhik dar guruvārī yetaṁ. This weekly comes out every Thursday.
Neuter.
Built on सप्ताह, a week; compare मासिक, a monthly.

The word, taken apart.
साप्ताहिक (sāptāhik) is built on Sanskrit saptāha, 'a week' (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ika.

Lesson 5 of 5.
छापणे (chāpṇe) — to print.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a weekly, then the Marathi for a headline.

You'll want to know: छापणे.
छापणे — chāpṇe — "to print".
त्या छापखान्याने हजार पुस्तकं छापली. tyā chāpkhānyāne hajār pustakaṁ chāplī. That press printed a thousand books.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने छापलं, tyāne chāplaṁ.
What is printed agrees: पुस्तक छापलं, बातमी छापली.

The word, taken apart.
छापणे (chāpṇe) is the Marathi verb for printing and stamping; its origin is not settled.
