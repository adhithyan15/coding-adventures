## You'll want to know: ತೂಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C438-TUKA438-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೂಗು** — *tūgu* — "to weigh; to swing".

> ಸಿಬ್ಬಂದಿ ನನ್ನ ಸೂಟ್ಕೇಸನ್ನು ತೂಗಿದರು.
>
> *sibbandi nanna sūṭkēsannu tūgidaru.*
>
> The staff weighed my suitcase.

Past (I …): ತೂಗಿದೆ, *tūgide*.

The noun beside it is ತೂಕ, weight; ತೊಟ್ಟಿಲು ತೂಗು is to rock a cradle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೂಗು** (*tūgu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3376 lists Kannada *tūgu*, *tūṅku*, "to weigh, to swing, to rock a cradle".
