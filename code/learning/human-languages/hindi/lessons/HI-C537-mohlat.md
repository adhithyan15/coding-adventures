## You'll want to know: मोहलत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C537-KARYAKRAM537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहलत** — *mohlat* — "extra time; a respite".

> मालिक ने हमें किराया देने के लिए एक हफ़्ते की मोहलत दी।
>
> *Mālik ne hamẽ kirāyā dene ke liye ek hafte kī mohlat dī.*
>
> The landlord gave us a week's grace to pay the rent.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहलत** (*mohlat*) is usually traced to Arabic *muhla*, "respite, delay", through Persian.
