## You'll want to know: やとう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C456-PAY456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**やとう** — *yatou* — "to hire; to employ".

> **かいしゃは がくせいを ふたり やといました。** — *kaisha wa gakusei o futari yatoimashita* — "The company hired two students."

Polite **やといます**, *yatoimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **みせが てんいんを やといました。**, *mise ga ten'in o yatoimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やとう** (*yatou*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
