## You'll want to know: नातू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C495-NATU495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नातू** — *nātū* — "a grandson".

> आजोबा नातवाबरोबर बागेत फिरतात.
>
> *ājobā nātvābarobar bāget phirtāt.*
>
> Grandfather walks in the garden with his grandson.

Masculine.

Oblique नातव-: नातवाला. नात, a granddaughter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नातू** (*nātū*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *naptṛ*, "grandson"; CDIAL lists Marathi *nātū* (CDIAL 6955b).
