Bengali, chapter 470: Hair and Scalp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
টাক (ṭāk) — a bald patch; baldness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a stain, then the Bengali for body hair.

You'll want to know: টাক.
টাক — ṭāk — "a bald patch; baldness".
চল্লিশ বছরে বাবার মাথায় টাক পড়ল। chôllish bôchhôre bābār māthāy ṭāk pôṛlô. At forty, my father started going bald.
টাক (ṭāk) পড়া, to go bald.

The word, taken apart.
টাক (ṭāk) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a bald patch, under a reconstructed ṭakka, "bald" (CDIAL 5422); it is not the টাকা (taught) of money, which is another entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ঝরা (jhôrā) — to fall out (of hair); to fall away; to drip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for dandruff, then the Bengali for a louse.

You'll want to know: ঝরা.
ঝরা — jhôrā — "to fall out (of hair); to fall away; to drip".
চিন্তায় অরুণের চুল ঝরছে। chintāy ôruner chul jhôrchhe. Arun's hair is falling out from worry.
Past (he/she …): সেই গাছের পাতা ঝরল, sei gāchher pātā jhôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
ঝরা (jhôrā) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as oozing, dripping and leaves falling, under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 5346); ঝরনা (taught), a waterfall, is probably its relative.

You'll want to know: খোঁপা.
খোঁপা — khõpā — "a bun; a knot of hair".
মীরা চুলে একটা খোঁপা বাঁধল। mīrā chule ekṭā khõpā bā̃dhlô. Mira put her hair up in a bun.
খোঁপা (khõpā) বাঁধা, to put one's hair up in a bun.

The word, taken apart.
খোঁপা (khõpā) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a knot of hair, under a reconstructed khōppa, "a bunch of hair" (CDIAL 3939), which CDIAL takes to be a borrowing from Dravidian.
