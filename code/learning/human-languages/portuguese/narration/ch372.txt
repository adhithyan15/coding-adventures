Portuguese, chapter 372: Mudança de planos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
cancelar — to cancel, to call off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the unexpected problem, then the Portuguese for the alternative.

You'll want to know: cancelar.
cancelar — "to cancel, to call off".
Cancelaram o concerto por causa da chuva. They cancelled the concert because of the rain.
Cancelar uma reserva — to cancel a booking.

The word, taken apart.
cancelar is a learned borrowing from Latin cancellāre "to make like a lattice" (Lewis & Short), built on cancellī "a lattice, bars": a cancelled text was crossed out with lines.

Lesson 2 of 5.
adiar — to postpone, to put off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the alternative, then the Portuguese for to cancel.

You'll want to know: adiar.
adiar — "to postpone, to put off".
Adiámos a viagem para setembro. We put the trip off until September.
European adiámos (past) beside adiamos (present). Adiar para — to put off until.

The word, taken apart.
adiar is built on dia "day", inherited from Latin diēs "day" (Lewis & Short), with the prefix a-: to put to another day.

You'll want to know: definitivo.
