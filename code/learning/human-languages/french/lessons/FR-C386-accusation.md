## You'll want to know: l'accusation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C386-ACCUSER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'accusation** — "the accusation, the charge".

> C'est une accusation très grave.
>
> *It is a very serious accusation.*

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accusation* is a learned borrowing from Latin *accūsātiō* "an accusation, an indictment" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *accūsāre* "to accuse".
