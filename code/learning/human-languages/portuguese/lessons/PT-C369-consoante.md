## You'll want to know: consoante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C369-CONDICAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**consoante** — "depending on, according to; the consonant".

> Vamos à praia ou ao cinema, consoante o tempo.
>
> *We will go to the beach or to the cinema, depending on the weather.*

*Consoante* + noun: *consoante o preço*. As a noun, *a consoante* is a consonant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consoante* is inherited from Old Portuguese *consõante* (EtymDB), from Latin *cōnsonāns* "sounding together, agreeing" (Lewis & Short), on *sonāre* "to sound": what agrees with something goes according to it.
