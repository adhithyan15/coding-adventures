## You'll want to know: tessella, tessellae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C547-IMAG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**tessella, tessellae** — "a small cube (of mosaic)".

> Faber pavīmentum ex multīs tessellīs facit.
>
> *The craftsman makes the floor out of many little cubes.*

Feminine, a diminutive of *tessera* — a cube, a die. English *tessellated*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**tessella** is a diminutive of *tessera* "a cube, die" (Lewis & Short). English *tessellated* comes from it.
