## You'll want to know: عرض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C455-WAJIHA455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عرض** — *ʿaraḍa* — "to display; to offer".

> عرض المتجر ملابس الشتاء في الواجهة.
>
> *ʿaraḍa al-matjar malābis ash-shitāʾ fī al-wājiha.*
>
> The shop displayed winter clothes in the window.

Present: يعرض, *yaʿriḍ*.

The noun *ʿarḍ* is an offer or a show.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عرض** (*ʿaraḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-ḍ*, "to show, to present" (Lane). It is the plain verb; *ʿarīḍ*, "wide", shares the root.
