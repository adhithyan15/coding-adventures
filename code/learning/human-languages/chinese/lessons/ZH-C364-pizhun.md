## You'll want to know: pīzhǔn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C364-ALLOW364-04]; assesses=[] -->

*pīzhǔn* — "to give official approval; to approve a plan".

> *xiàozhǎng pīzhǔn le wǒmen de jìhuà* — The head approved our plan.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pīzhǔn* is built from *pī* and 准 'level', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *phej* and *tsywinX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to strike' and 'to show', and the second 'level'. How the pair came to mean approving is not settled.
