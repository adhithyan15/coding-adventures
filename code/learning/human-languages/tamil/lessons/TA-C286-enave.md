# எனவே (eṉavē) — so, that is why, therefore

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C78-WHY-03, TA-GRAMMAR-C286-SEEMS-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "so, because of that". (*ataṉāl*.) Then "It seems to me it will rain tomorrow." (*eṉakku nāḷai maḻai peyyum eṉṟu tōṉṟukiṟatu*.)

## You'll want to know: அதனால் and அதனால்தான்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C286-SO-RESULT-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C78-WHY-03, TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-02, TA-LEX-VEEDU-01, TA-LEX-C61-FEEL-04, TA-LEX-C85-AVAN-AVAL-01] -->

> **நேற்று மழை பெய்தது. அதனால் நான் வீட்டில் இருந்தேன்.**
>
> *nēṟṟu maḻai peytatu. ataṉāl nāṉ vīṭṭil iruntēṉ.* — "It rained yesterday. So I stayed at home."

**அதனால்** is **அதன்**, the shape of **அது** that carries endings, with **-ஆல்**, "by, because of": "because of that". The cause comes first, in its own sentence; **அதனால்** opens the result.

Add **-தான்**, "exactly, that very", and it points back hard: "that is why".

> **அவனுக்குக் காய்ச்சல். அதனால்தான் அவன் வரவில்லை.**
>
> *avaṉukkuk kāyccal. ataṉāltāṉ avaṉ varavillai.* — "He has a fever. That is why he did not come."

## You'll want to know: எனவே and ஆகையால், the written 'therefore'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C286-THEREFORE-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C69-GOING-02, TA-LEX-C65-DOING-02, TA-PRAGMATICS-C81-REG-01, TA-LEX-C85-NAAM-NAANGAL-01] -->

> **பேருந்து வரவில்லை. எனவே நாங்கள் நடந்து போனோம்.**
>
> *pēruntu varavillai. eṉavē nāṅkaḷ naṭantu pōṉōm.* — "The bus did not come. Therefore we walked."

In written Tamil, **எனவே** and **ஆகையால்** (*ākaiyāl*, "therefore") do the job of **அதனால்**: a notice, a letter, the news. They are the formal side of the two Tamils. In talk, **அதனால்** carries the result.
