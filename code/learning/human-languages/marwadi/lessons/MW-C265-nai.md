## You'll want to know: नाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C265-MOCHI265-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नाई** — *nāī* — "a barber".

> नाई बाळ काटै है।
>
> *nāī bāḷ kāṭai hai.*
>
> The barber cuts hair.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाई** (*nāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nāpita*, "barber". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *nāū*, "barber" (CDIAL 13792).
