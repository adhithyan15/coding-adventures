## You'll want to know: déblayer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C443-HIVER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**déblayer** — "to clear away (snow, rubble)".

> Les voisins ont déblayé la neige devant l'immeuble.
>
> *The neighbours cleared the snow in front of the building.*

*Le déblaiement* — the clearing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*déblayer* comes from Old French *desbleer* 'to clear a field of its corn', usually traced to *blé* 'corn'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
