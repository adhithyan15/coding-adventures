## You'll want to know: देहळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C189-THANBHO189-04]; assesses=[] -->

**देहळी** — *dehḷī* — "a threshold".

> छोरो देहळी माथै बैठै है।
>
> *chhoro dehḷī māthai baiṭhai hai.*
>
> The boy sits on the threshold.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**देहळी** (*dehḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dehalī*, "a threshold"; CDIAL lists Hindi *dehlī* (CDIAL 6559). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
