## You'll want to know: der Anteil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C276-UMFRAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Anteil** — "the share, the proportion".

> Der Anteil der Kinder war sehr hoch.
>
> *The share of children was very high.*

Plural: *die Anteile*. *Anteil nehmen* — to sympathise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Anteil* is built on *Teil* "part", from Old High German *teil*, Proto-Germanic *dailą* "part, deal" (EtymDB), with *an*.
