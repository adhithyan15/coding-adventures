## You'll want to know: تعمیر کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C427-TAMIRGAH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تعمیر کردن** (*ta'mir kardan*) — "to repair, to fix".

> مکانیک ماشین را در دو روز تعمیر کرد.
>
> *Mekânik mâshin râ dar do ruz ta'mir kard.*
>
> The mechanic repaired the car in two days.

*ta'mir* + *kardan*. Present stem *kon-*: *ta'mir mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'mir kardan* is built from *ta'mir* 'repair', usually traced to Arabic *taʿmīr* 'restoring', and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
