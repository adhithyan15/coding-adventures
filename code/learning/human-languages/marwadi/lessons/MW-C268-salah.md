## You'll want to know: सलाह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C268-KAIVNO268-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सलाह** — *salāh* — "counsel; advice".

> दादी चोखी सलाह देवै है।
>
> *dādī chokhī salāh devai hai.*
>
> Grandmother gives good advice.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सलाह** (*salāh*) is usually traced, through Persian *salāh*, to Arabic *ṣalāḥ*, "counsel". It is feminine: **चोखी सलाह**, good advice.
