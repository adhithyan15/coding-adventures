Kannada, chapter 494: Shock and Dismay.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುದುಡು (muduḍu) — to shrink back; to curl up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for frustration, then the Kannada for agitation.

You'll want to know: ಮುದುಡು.
ಮುದುಡು — muduḍu — "to shrink back; to curl up".
ಗುಡುಗಿನ ಸದ್ದಿಗೆ ನಾಯಿ ಮೂಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮುದುಡಿ ಮಲಗಿತು. guḍugina saddige nāyi mūleyalli muduḍi malagitu. At the sound of thunder the dog curled up in a corner.
Past (I …): ಮುದುಡಿದೆ, muduḍide.
Of fear, cold or a wilting leaf; ಮುದುಡಿಕೊ, to huddle up.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುದುಡು (muduḍu) is a native Kannada verb, "to shrink, to curl up"; its origin is not settled.

You'll want to know: ತಡವರಿಸು.
ತಡವರಿಸು — taḍavarisu — "to fumble; to falter".
ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ ಕೇಳಿದಾಗ ಅವನು ಉತ್ತರಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ತಡವರಿಸಿದನು. praśne kēḷidāga avanu uttarakkāgi taḍavarisidanu. When he was asked, he fumbled for an answer.
Past (I …): ತಡವರಿಸಿದೆ, taḍavariside.
For words: ಮಾತಿಗೆ ತಡವರಿಸು (taḍavarisu), to grope for words. In the dark it is feeling your way.

The word, taken apart.
ತಡವರಿಸು (taḍavarisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3025 gives Kannada taḍavu, "to touch gently, grope", and taḍa-varisu, "to feel one's way with the hands or feet".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆ (digbhrame) — bewilderment; stunned shock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for amazement, then the Kannada for to lose heart.

You'll want to know: ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆ.
ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆ — digbhrame — "bewilderment; stunned shock".
ಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದಾಗ ಬಾಗಿಲು ತೆರೆದಿತ್ತು, ನಾನು ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆಗೊಂಡೆ. manege bandāga bāgilu teredittu, nānu digbhramegoṇḍe. When I came home the door was open; I was stunned.
ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆಗೊಳ್ಳು, to be stunned. Literally a whirling of the directions: not knowing which way to turn.

The word, taken apart.
ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆ (digbhrame) is borrowed from Sanskrit digbhrama, "perplexity about the points of the compass" (Monier-Williams): dik, "direction", and bhrama, "giddiness" (CDIAL 9646).
