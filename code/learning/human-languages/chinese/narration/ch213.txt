Mandarin Chinese, chapter 213: A Trip Abroad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
qiānzhèng — visa.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to make an appointment, then the Mandarin for to get a haircut.

You'll want to know: qiānzhèng.
qiānzhèng — "visa".
wǒ hái méiyǒu qiānzhèng — I haven't got a visa yet.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qiānzhèng is built from qiān 'to sign' and zhèng 'proof'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese tsyingH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to verify'; no dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for talking about plans.

Lesson 2 of 5.
dǎoyóu — tour guide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to get a haircut, then the Mandarin for visa.

You'll want to know: dǎoyóu.
dǎoyóu — "tour guide".
wǒmen de dǎoyóu shì běndì rén — Our tour guide is a local.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dǎoyóu is built from dǎo 'to lead' and yóu 'to travel about', the yóu of lǚyóu, both inherited from Middle Chinese, dawH and yuw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to lead'. The dictionary gives it a departing tone, so the rising tone of dǎo is not the regular outcome, and how it came is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
门票 (ménpiào) — admission ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for tour guide, then the Mandarin for tourist.

You'll want to know: 门票.
门票 — ménpiào — "admission ticket".
门票多少钱 ménpiào duōshao qián — How much is it to get in?

The word, taken apart.
门票 (ménpiào) is built from 门 'gate' and 票 'a ticket'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese mwon (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of 票, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
yílù píng'ān — have a safe journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for tourist, then the Mandarin for admission ticket.

You'll want to know: yílù píng'ān.
yílù píng'ān — "have a safe journey".
nǐ míngtiān huíguó yílù píng'ān — You're going home tomorrow: have a safe journey!
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
一 changes its tone here: yí, before the falling tone of lù.

The word, taken apart.
yílù píng'ān is built from 一路 'the whole way' and píng'ān 'safe and sound', all four parts inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit, luH, bjaeng and 'an (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
One more word for talking about plans.
