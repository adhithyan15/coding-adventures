Italian, chapter 384: Le regole.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la norma — the rule, the standard, the norm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the assessment, then the Italian for the score.

You'll want to know: la norma.
la norma — "the rule, the standard, the norm".
Le norme di sicurezza sono cambiate. The safety rules have changed.
Di norma — as a rule, usually. A norma di legge — in line with the law.

The word, taken apart.
norma is a learned borrowing from Latin nōrma "a carpenter's square; a rule, a pattern" (Lewis & Short): the tool that keeps a corner straight.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
il regolamento — the regulations, the rules (of a place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the score, then the Italian for the rule.

You'll want to know: il regolamento.
il regolamento — "the regulations, the rules (of a place)".
Secondo il regolamento, in piscina bisogna fare la doccia prima di entrare. According to the rules, you must shower before going into the pool.
The written rules of a school, a club or a building.

The word, taken apart.
regolamento is built on regolare "to regulate", from Late Latin rēgulāre, built on rēgula "a ruler, a rule" (Lewis & Short), from regere "to guide".

Lesson 3 of 5.
obbligatorio — compulsory, mandatory.

Before the new one: say the Italian for the rule, then the Italian for the regulations.

You'll want to know: obbligatorio.
obbligatorio — "compulsory, mandatory".
In macchina la cintura è obbligatoria. In the car the seat belt is compulsory.
Non è obbligatorio — it is not compulsory.

The word, taken apart.
obbligatorio is a learned borrowing from Late Latin obligātōrius "binding", from obligāre "to bind, to oblige" (Lewis & Short), built on ligāre "to tie".
