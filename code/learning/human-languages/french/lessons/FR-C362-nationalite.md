## You'll want to know: la nationalité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C362-CITOYEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la nationalité** — "the nationality".

> Elle a la double nationalité, française et canadienne.
>
> *She has dual nationality, French and Canadian.*

*De nationalité française* — of French nationality.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nationalité* is built from *nation* and *-ité*. *nation* comes from Latin *nātiō* "birth, a people" (Lewis & Short), from *nāscī* "to be born".
