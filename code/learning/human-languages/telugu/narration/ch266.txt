Telugu, chapter 266: Jasmine flower, Neem, Banyan tree, Holy basil, Bud.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
మల్లెపువ్వు (mallepuvvu) — a jasmine flower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a thief, then the Telugu for an animal.

You'll want to know: మల్లెపువ్వు.
మల్లెపువ్వు — mallepuvvu — "a jasmine flower".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mallepuvvu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mallepuvvu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mallepuvvu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for a thief, then the Telugu for an animal, then say mallepuvvu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does మల్లెపువ్వు (mallepuvvu) mean? ("A jasmine flower".) Say it once more.

Important? (ముఖ్యమైన, mukhyamaina.) Own? (సొంత, sonta.) Busy? (బిజీ, bijī.) Compulsory? (తప్పనిసరి, tappanisari.) Smooth? (నునుపైన, nunupaina.) Warm? (వెచ్చని, veccani.) Rare? (అరుదైన, arudaina.) Strange? (వింత, vinta.) Kind? (దయగల, dayagala.) Natural? (సహజమైన, sahajamaina.) Thick? (చిక్కని, cikkani.) Greasy? (జిడ్డు, jiḍḍu.) Sticky? (జిగట, jigaṭa.) Rough? (గరుకు, garuku.)
