## You'll want to know: सुरक्षा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C410-PASPORT410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सुरक्षा** — *surakṣā* — "security; safety".

> एयरपोर्ट पर सुरक्षा बहुत सख़्त थी।
>
> *eyarporṭ par surakṣā bahut sakht thī.*
>
> Security at the airport was very tight.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुरक्षा** (*surakṣā*) is a learned word made of Sanskrit *su-*, "well", and *rakṣā*, "protection, guarding" (Monier-Williams). सुरक्षित, safe, is its partner.
