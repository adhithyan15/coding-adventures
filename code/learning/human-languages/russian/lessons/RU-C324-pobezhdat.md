## You'll want to know: побеждать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C324-POBEDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**побеждать** (*pobezhdát*) — "to win, to defeat".

> Наша команда победила в финале.
>
> *Násha kománda pobedíla v finále.*
>
> Our team won the final.

Imperfective; perfective *победить* (*pobedít*), which has no *I* form in the future: Russians say *я одержу победу*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*побеждать* is built on *победа* "victory", Old East Slavic *poběda* (EtymDB), in the Church Slavonic shape *-жд-*.
