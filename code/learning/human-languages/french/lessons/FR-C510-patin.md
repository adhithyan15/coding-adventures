## You'll want to know: le patin
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C510-GLACE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le patin** — "skate".

> J'ai reçu des patins pour mon anniversaire.
>
> *I got skates for my birthday.*

*Le patin à glace* — ice skating; *le patin à roulettes* — roller skating.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le patin* is built on *patte* "paw, foot" with *-in* (EtymDB); the origin of *patte* is not settled.
