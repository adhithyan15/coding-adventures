## You'll want to know: せいけつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C163-HOME163-05]; assesses=[] -->

**せいけつ** — *seiketsu* — "hygienic; clean and tidy".

> **だいどころは いつも せいけつです。** — *daidokoro wa itsumo seiketsu desu* — "The kitchen is always hygienic."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいけつ** (*seiketsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "pure, clear" (Middle Chinese *tshjeng*) and *ketsu*, "clean, pure" (*ket*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
