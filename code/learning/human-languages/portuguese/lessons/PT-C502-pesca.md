## You'll want to know: a pesca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C502-PESCA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a pesca** — "fishing".

> A pesca é muito importante nesta vila.
>
> *Fishing is very important in this town.*

*Barco de pesca* — fishing boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pesca* comes from *pescar* "to fish" (EtymDB), inherited from Latin *piscārī*.
