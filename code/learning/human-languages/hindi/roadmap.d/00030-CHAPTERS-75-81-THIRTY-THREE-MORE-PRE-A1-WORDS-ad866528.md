## Chapters 75-81 - thirty-three more pre-A1 words

- **Authored.** Seven chapters, one new headword per lesson: *desh, Bharat,
  shahar, bhasha, angrezi*; *gari, rel, steshan, tikat, jana*; *khel, khelna,
  kriket, gana, aram*; *aaj, bhi, bahut*; *paisa, rupaya, dam, mahanga,
  kharidna*; *hotal, nashta, bil, vetar, pina*; *khula, band, pravesh, nikas,
  der*. A1 exam coverage 176/282 -> 192/282, measured on the merged tree.

  Thirty-five words were authored against chapters 68-74 before the joining
  column took those numbers. Two of them, **कब** and **क्यों**, are cut rather
  than renumbered: the joining tranche teaches both, and a headword introduced
  twice is a hard error. Chapter 78 therefore holds three words.

  The words were chosen **against the exam inventory rather than by topic**, and
  every one is spelled from glyphs the track's own writing lessons already
  teach, so closure and never-taught glyphs are unchanged. `jana`, `khelna`,
  `kharidna` and `pina` take Hindi's pre-A1 verb headwords from 2 to 6 and close
  the verb criterion.
