## You'll want to know: 多云
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C219-CLOUD219-02]; assesses=[] -->

**多云** — *duōyún* — "cloudy".

> **明天多云风不大** *míngtiān duōyún fēng bú dà* — Tomorrow will be cloudy, without much wind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**多云** (*duōyún*) is built from 多 'many' and 云 'cloud', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ta* and *hjun* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
