## You'll want to know: वळसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C404-VALSA404-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वळसा** — *vaḷsā* — "a detour; a roundabout way".

> पूल बंद होता म्हणून आम्ही लांबचा वळसा घेतला.
>
> *pūl band hotā mhaṇūn āmhī lāmbcā vaḷsā ghetlā.*
>
> The bridge was closed, so we took a long detour.

Masculine.

वळसा घेणे or वळसा घालणे, to go the long way round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वळसा** (*vaḷsā*) is built on the stem of **वळणे**, "to turn"; how the noun was formed is not settled.
