## You'll want to know: theoretisch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C366-HYPOTHESE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**theoretisch** — "in theory, theoretical".

> Theoretisch kann man die Strecke in zwei Stunden fahren.
>
> *In theory you can drive the route in two hours.*

*Theoretisch …, praktisch …* — in theory …, in practice …

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*theoretisch* goes with *Theorie*, a learned borrowing from Latin *theōria* "a philosophic speculation" (Lewis & Short), from Greek *theōría* "a looking at" (EtymDB).
