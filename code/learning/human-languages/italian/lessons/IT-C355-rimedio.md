## You'll want to know: il rimedio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C355-RIMEDIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il rimedio** — "the remedy, the cure".

> Il tè con il miele è un buon rimedio per la tosse.
>
> *Tea with honey is a good remedy for a cough.*

*Porre rimedio a* — to put right. *Non c'è rimedio* — there is nothing to be done.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rimedio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *remedium* "a cure, a remedy" (Lewis & Short), built from *re-* and *medērī* "to heal", the verb behind *medico*.
