## You'll want to know: 大量
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C526-WEIGHT526-04]; assesses=[] -->

**大量** — *dàliàng* — "a large amount of; in large amounts".

> **今天格外热最好大量喝水** *jīntiān géwài rè zuìhǎo dàliàng hē shuǐ* — It's especially hot today, so you'd better drink plenty of water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**大量** (*dàliàng*) is built from 大 'big' and 量 'an amount', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dajH* and *ljangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which gives the second as units of dry measure.
