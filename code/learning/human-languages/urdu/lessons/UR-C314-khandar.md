## You'll want to know: کھنڈر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C314-KHANDAR314-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کھنڈر** — *khanḍar* — "ruins".

> پہاڑی پر ایک پرانا کھنڈر ہے۔
>
> *pahāṛī par ek purānā khanḍar hai.*
>
> There are old ruins on the hill.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھنڈر** (*khanḍar*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khaṇḍhar*, "a dilapidated building, ruins", under *\*khaṇḍaghara*, a broken house, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3794).
