## You'll want to know: لٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C540-GHATA540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لٹنا** — *luṭnā* — "to be robbed; to be ruined".

> اگر آپ نے جوا کھیلا تو آپ لٹ جائیں گے۔
>
> *agar āp ne juā khelā to āp luṭ jāeṅ ge.*
>
> If you gamble, you will be fleeced.

Past (he …): وہ سفر میں لٹ گیا, *voh safar meṅ luṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لٹنا** (*luṭnā*) is the intransitive partner of *lūṭnā*, "to plunder", which CDIAL lists under *\*luṭṭati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11078).
