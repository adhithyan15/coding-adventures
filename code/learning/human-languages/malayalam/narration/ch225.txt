Malayalam, chapter 225: To drive, To move, To arrive, To return, To take.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഓടിക്കുക (ōṭikkuka) — to drive (a vehicle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to fill, then the Malayalam for to cover.

You'll want to know: ഓടിക്കുക.
ഓടിക്കുക — ōṭikkuka — "to drive (a vehicle)".

The word, taken apart.
ഓടിക്കുക (ōṭikkuka) is native Dravidian, the causative of the verb "to run": "make run"; DEDR 1041 lists the root with Tamil ōṭu, Kannada ōḍu and Telugu ōḍu.

കൊണ്ടുപോകുക (koṇṭupōkuka) is native Dravidian, "go having taken": koṇṭu, the past form of the verb "to take" (DEDR 2151, with Tamil koḷ), and pōkuka, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
