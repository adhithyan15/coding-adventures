# വിധിവശാൽ (vidhivaśāl) — as fate willed

## You'll want to know: വിധിവശാൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C360-YADRCCHIKAM360-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വിധിവശാൽ** — *vidhivaśāl* — "as fate willed".

> വിധിവശാൽ അന്ന് അവൻ ആ ബസിൽ കയറിയില്ല.
>
> *vidhivaśāl annŭ avan ā basil kayaṟiyilla.*
>
> As fate would have it, he did not get on that bus that day.

Built like ഭാഗ്യവശാൽ, fortunately: by the power of fate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിധിവശാൽ** (*vidhivaśāl*) is borrowed whole from Sanskrit *vidhivaśāt*, "through the power of fate or destiny" (Monier-Williams), from *vidhi*, "rule, fate" (CDIAL 11755), and *vaśa*, "power".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
