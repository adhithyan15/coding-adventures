## You'll want to know: डोकेदुखी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C482-DUKH482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**डोकेदुखी** — *ḍokedukhī* — "a headache".

> उन्हात फिरल्यामुळे मला डोकेदुखी झाली.
>
> *unhāt phirlyāmuḷe malā ḍokedukhī jhālī.*
>
> I got a headache from walking about in the sun.

Feminine.

डोके + दुखी, aching. Also, figuratively, a nuisance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डोकेदुखी** (*ḍokedukhī*) is a Marathi compound of डोके, "head", and दुखी, "aching", from the verb दुखणे, a tadbhava of Sanskrit *duḥkhati* (CDIAL 6376).
