## You'll want to know: fùjìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-03]; assesses=[] -->

*fùjìn* — "nearby; in the area".

> *fùjìn yǒu yínháng ma* — Is there a bank near here?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fùjìn* is built from fù 'to attach' and jìn 'near', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjuH* and *gj+nX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
