# लड़की (laṛkī) — a girl; a daughter

## You'll want to know: लड़की
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C118-SPOUSE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लड़की** (*laṛkī*) — "a girl", and in a family, "a daughter".

The **-ई** ending marks the girl, as in **दादी** beside **दादा**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Boy and girl as a pair.
