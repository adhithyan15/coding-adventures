## You'll want to know: бродить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C443-BRODIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**бродить** (*brodít*) — "to wander, to stroll".

> Мы любим бродить по старым улицам.
>
> *My lyúbim brodít po stárym úlitsam.*
>
> We love wandering the old streets.

Imperfective. Present *брожу, бродишь*. Of wine: to ferment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бродить* is inherited through Old East Slavic *broditi* from Proto-Slavic *broditi* (EtymDB).
