## You'll want to know: a dobragem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C526-SERIES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a dobragem** — "dubbing".

> Os desenhos animados para crianças têm dobragem.
>
> *Children's cartoons are dubbed.*

In Brazil: *a dublagem*. In Portugal most films for adults are subtitled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dobragem* is built on *dobrar* "to fold, to double, to dub", which goes back to Late Latin *duplāre* "to double" (EtymDB), from *duplus*.
