## You'll want to know: والد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C532-HAYAT532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**والد** — *wālid* — "a parent; a father".

> سافر والدي إلى المدينة.
>
> *sāfara wālidī ilā al-madīna.*
>
> My father travelled to the city.

الوالدان means the parents; والدة is a mother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**والد** (*wālid*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-l-d*, "to give birth" (Lane). Walad, a boy, and mīlād, birth, which you know, share the root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
