## You'll want to know: qiúzhù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C456-LOST456-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qiúzhù* — "to ask for help; to turn to someone for help".

> *wǒ zhǎo bu dào péngyou qù fúwùtái qiúzhù* — I can't find my friend, so I'm going to the information desk for help.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiúzhù* is built from *qiú* 'to seek' and *zhù* 'to help', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjuw* and *dzrjoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to search for' and the second 'to assist'.
