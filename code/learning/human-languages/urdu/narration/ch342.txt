Urdu, chapter 342: Citizens and Votes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ووٹ (voṭ) — a vote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the power to rule, then the Urdu for to hold to be.

You'll want to know: ووٹ.
ووٹ — voṭ — "a vote".
میں نے صبح اپنا ووٹ دیا۔ maiṅ ne subah apnā voṭ diyā. I cast my vote in the morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ووٹ (voṭ) is borrowed from English vote.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Lesson 3 of 5.
جمہوریت (jamhūriyat) — democracy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a vote, then the Urdu for an election.

You'll want to know: جمہوریت.
جمہوریت — jamhūriyat — "democracy".
جمہوریت میں ہر شہری کی رائے ضروری ہے۔ jamhūriyat meṅ har shahrī kī rāe zarūrī hai. In a democracy every citizen's opinion matters.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
جمہوریت (jamhūriyat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic jumhūriyya, "a republic", built on jumhūr, "the people at large".

Lesson 4 of 5.
امیدوار (ummīdvār) — a candidate (for a post or a seat).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an election, then the Urdu for democracy.

You'll want to know: امیدوار.
امیدوار — ummīdvār — "a candidate (for a post or a seat)".
اس انتخاب میں تین امیدوار ہیں۔ is intikhāb meṅ tīn ummīdvār haiṅ. There are three candidates in this election.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
امیدوار (ummīdvār) is usually traced to Persian ummīdvār, "hopeful", built on امید, "hope", with -vār, "having": the one who hopes to be chosen.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تھوپنا (thopnā) — to impose; to force (on someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for democracy, then the Urdu for a candidate.

You'll want to know: تھوپنا.
تھوپنا — thopnā — "to impose; to force (on someone)".
حکومت نے لوگوں پر نیا ٹیکس تھوپ دیا۔ hukūmat ne logoṅ par nayā ṭaiks thop diyā. The government forced a new tax on people.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اپنی رائے تھوپی, us ne apnī rāe thopī.

The word, taken apart.
تھوپنا (thopnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin thopnā, "to cover, to heap", under stōpyate, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13723); the sense "to force on someone" is a later one our sources do not trace.
