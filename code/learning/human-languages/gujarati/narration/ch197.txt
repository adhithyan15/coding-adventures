Gujarati, chapter 197: The watchman at the gate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચોકીદાર (cokīdār) — watchman, guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to go in, then the Gujarati for entry.

You'll want to know: ચોકીદાર.
ચોકીદાર — cokīdār — "watchman, guard".
ચોકીદાર રાત્રે જાગે છે. — cokīdār rātre jāge che — "The watchman stays awake at night."

The word, taken apart.
ચોકીદાર (cokīdār) is built from cokī, "guard post", and the Persian suffix -dār, "holder, keeper". CDIAL lists Gujarati cokī, "square low seat", with cok, "square", under Sanskrit catuṣka, "set of four" (CDIAL 4629).

The word, taken apart.
The origin of તપાસવું (tapāsvũ) is not settled. EtymDB links it to an Arabic verb for examining and lists Marathi tapāsṇe beside it, but CDIAL has no entry for it, so the Arabic link is unconfirmed (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઓળખપત્ર (oḷakhpatra) — identity card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for watchman, then the Gujarati for to check.

You'll want to know: ઓળખપત્ર.
ઓળખપત્ર — oḷakhpatra — "identity card".
ચોકીદારે મારું ઓળખપત્ર જોયું. — cokīdāre mārũ oḷakhpatra joyũ — "The watchman saw my identity card."
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઓળખપત્ર (oḷakhpatra) is built from oḷakh, "identity, recognition", and patra, "leaf, letter". CDIAL lists the verb oḷakhvũ, "to know, distinguish", under Sanskrit upalakṣayati, "beholds" (CDIAL 2226), and patra is Sanskrit páttra, "leaf" (CDIAL 7733).

Lesson 4 of 5.
બંધ (bandh) — closed, shut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to check, then the Gujarati for identity card.

You'll want to know: બંધ.
બંધ — bandh — "closed, shut".
દુકાન રવિવારે બંધ છે. — dukān ravivāre bandh che — "The shop is closed on Sundays."

The word, taken apart.
બંધ (bandh) is Sanskrit bandhá, "bond, damming, custody", taken over whole, a tatsama; CDIAL lists its inherited cousin bā̃dh, "dam" (CDIAL 9136). CDIAL does not give the sense "shut", which also matches Persian band, and the two words may have run together.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખૂલવું (khūlvũ) — to open up, to come open.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for identity card, then the Gujarati for closed.

You'll want to know: ખૂલવું.
ખૂલવું — khūlvũ — "to open up, to come open".
બેંક સવારે ખૂલે છે. — bẽk savāre khūle che — "The bank opens in the morning."
ખોલવું opens a thing; ખૂલવું (khūlvũ) is the thing opening.

The word, taken apart.
ખૂલવું (khūlvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to be open", under khull, "to be open", a reconstructed form; the taught kholvũ, "to open", is its partner from khōll (CDIAL 3945).
