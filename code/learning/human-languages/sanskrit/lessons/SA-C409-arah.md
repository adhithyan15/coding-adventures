## You'll want to know: अरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C409-CAKRA409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अरः** — *araḥ* — "a spoke".

> चक्रस्य दश अराः सन्ति।
>
> *cakrasya daśa arāḥ santi.*
>
> The wheel has ten spokes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अरः** (*araḥ*) comes from the root √ṛ, "to move, to fit"; MW gives *ara* as the spoke of a wheel, from the Rigveda on (MW).
