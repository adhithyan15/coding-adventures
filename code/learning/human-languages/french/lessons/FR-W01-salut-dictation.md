# One heard greeting — write what you know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-ORTHO-SALUT-01] -->

Cover the answer line lower on this page. You will hear one familiar casual
greeting. No new vocabulary is hiding in this lesson.

## Writing — short dictation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-ORTHO-SALUT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

[YOU HEAR: *sa-LU*]

Write the French greeting from the sound alone. Remember that your pen records
one final letter that your voice does not pronounce.

Now uncover and compare: **salut**. Check only the five letters and the silent
final **t**. Repair one place if needed.
