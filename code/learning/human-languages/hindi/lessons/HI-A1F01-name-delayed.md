# Hide the bank, then fill one line

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-FORM-NAME-CUE-MAP-01] -->

Look once: **A मीरा · B अरुण**. Cover the complete bank and wait ten seconds.

## Writing — delayed requested entry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-FORM-NAME-DELAYED-ENTRY-01]; assesses=[HI-FORM-LABEL-NAME-01, HI-FORM-NAME-ARUN-01, HI-FORM-NAME-CUE-MAP-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

Keep the bank hidden. Complete only this field:

> B — **नाम: __________**

The requested value comes from memory; there is no copyable answer beside the line.
Reveal the bank only after writing.

## Guided Practice — separate repair passes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-FORM-NAME-DELAYED-ENTRY-01] -->

Compare in order: (1) requested person, (2) four written positions, (3) vowel
marks and headline, (4) spacing after the colon. Repair the first differing
dimension and stop.
