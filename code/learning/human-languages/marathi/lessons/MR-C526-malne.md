# मळणे (maḷṇe) — to knead

## You'll want to know: मळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C526-OTA526-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मळणे** — *maḷṇe* — "to knead".

> पोळ्यांसाठी आईने पीठ मळलं.
>
> *poḷyāṁsāṭhī āīne pīṭh maḷlaṁ.*
>
> Mother kneaded the dough for chapatis.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पीठ मळलं, *tyāne pīṭh maḷlaṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मळणे** (*maḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *maḷṇẽ*, "to rub", under *\*marati*, "crushes, rubs" (CDIAL 9870); kneading is rubbing and pressing.
