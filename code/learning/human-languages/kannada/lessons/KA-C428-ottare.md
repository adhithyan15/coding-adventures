## You'll want to know: ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C428-ONDOMME428-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ** — *oṭṭāre* — "overall; on the whole".

> ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ ಪ್ರವಾಸ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿತ್ತು.
>
> *oṭṭāre pravāsa cennāgittu.*
>
> Overall the trip was good.

Built on ಒಟ್ಟು, the total.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ** (*oṭṭāre*) is native Dravidian, built on *oṭṭu*, "to come together; a mass, a union" (DEDR 958).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
