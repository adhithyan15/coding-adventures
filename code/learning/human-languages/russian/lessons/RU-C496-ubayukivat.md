## You'll want to know: убаюкивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C496-BABY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**убаюкивать** (*ubayúkivat*) — "to lull to sleep".

> Мама убаюкивает ребёнка тихой песней.
>
> *Máma ubayúkivayet rebyónka tíkhoy pésney.*
>
> Mum is lulling the baby to sleep with a quiet song.

Imperfective; perfective убаюкать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*убаюкивать* is built on *у-* and *баюкать* 'to lull', which is usually traced to the lullaby refrain *баю-бай*; EtymDB lists *баюкать* without a source and has no entry for *убаюкивать*.
