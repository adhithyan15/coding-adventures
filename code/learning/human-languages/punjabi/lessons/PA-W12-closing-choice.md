# End with the meeting you mean

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04] -->

The opening is already chosen. Now choose only an ending for a note whose
purpose is to meet Manan again.

## Why it's said this way — a promise, not a second hello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04, PA-LEX-SAT-SRI-AKAL-01] -->

**ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ** says “we will meet again.” It matches that purpose. The
Sikh greeting **ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ** can also serve as a goodbye in its own
context, but the reader's community has not been specified here.

## Writing — choose the ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy the everyday ending once: **ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ।** Notice the word space and the
mark after the final word. You are not assembling a message yet.
