Italian, chapter 279: Più tardi o prima.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'interruzione — the interruption, the break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the conclusion, then the Italian for to resume.

You'll want to know: l'interruzione.
l'interruzione — "the interruption, the break".
C'è stata un'interruzione di corrente. There was a power cut.
Senza interruzione — without a break.

The word, taken apart.
interruzione is a learned borrowing from Latin interruptiō "a breaking off" (EtymDB), from interrumpere, built on rumpere "to break".

Lesson 2 of 5.
sospendere — to suspend, to halt for a while.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to resume, then the Italian for the interruption.

You'll want to know: sospendere.
sospendere — "to suspend, to halt for a while".
Hanno sospeso la partita per la pioggia. They suspended the match because of the rain.
Past participle sospeso. It also means ‘to hang up’: sospendere una lampada.

The word, taken apart.
sospendere is inherited from Latin suspendere "to hang up, to suspend" (EtymDB), from sub "under" and pendere "to hang".

Lesson 3 of 5.
rimandare — to put off, to postpone; to send back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the interruption, then the Italian for to suspend.

You'll want to know: rimandare.
rimandare — "to put off, to postpone; to send back".
Abbiamo rimandato la cena a sabato. We put the dinner off until Saturday.
The everyday word for putting something off: rimandare a domani.

The word, taken apart.
rimandare is ri- "again, back" with mandare "to send", which is inherited from Latin mandāre "to hand over, to entrust" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
in anticipo — early, in advance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to suspend, then the Italian for to put off.

You'll want to know: in anticipo.
in anticipo — "early, in advance".
Siamo arrivati in anticipo di dieci minuti. We arrived ten minutes early.
The opposite of in ritardo.

The word, taken apart.
anticipo is built on anticipare "to do early", a learned borrowing from Latin anticipāre "to take beforehand", from ante "before" and capere "to take" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
la tappa — the stage, the stop (on a journey).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to put off, then the Italian for early.

You'll want to know: la tappa.
la tappa — "the stage, the stop (on a journey)".
La prima tappa è stata un piccolo paese di montagna. The first stop was a small mountain village.
A race or a tour goes tappa per tappa, stage by stage.

The word, taken apart.
tappa was borrowed from French étape "a stopping place" (EtymDB), which goes back to a Germanic word for a pile or a storehouse.
