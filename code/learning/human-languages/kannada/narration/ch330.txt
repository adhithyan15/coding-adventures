Lesson 1 of 5.
ಡಿಕ್ಕಿ ಹೊಡೆ (ḍikki hoḍe) — to collide; to crash into.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to announce, then the Kannada for a statement.

You'll want to know: ಡಿಕ್ಕಿ ಹೊಡೆ.
ಡಿಕ್ಕಿ ಹೊಡೆ — ḍikki hoḍe — "to collide; to crash into".
ಒಂದು ಕಾರು ಬಸ್ಸಿಗೆ ಡಿಕ್ಕಿ ಹೊಡೆಯಿತು. ondu kāru bassige ḍikki hoḍeyitu. A car crashed into a bus.
Past (I …): ಡಿಕ್ಕಿ ಹೊಡೆದೆ, ḍikki hoḍede.
What is hit takes -ಗೆ: ಮರಕ್ಕೆ ಡಿಕ್ಕಿ ಹೊಡೆ (ḍikki hoḍe), to crash into a tree.

The word, taken apart.
ಡಿಕ್ಕಿ ಹೊಡೆ (ḍikki hoḍe) is native Dravidian: DEDR 443 lists Kannada ḍikki, "a butt, a collision", with words for hitting against, and hoḍe is "to strike, to beat" (DEDR 4252).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪ್ರತ್ಯಕ್ಷದರ್ಶಿ (pratyakṣadarśi) — an eyewitness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a statement, then the Kannada for to collide.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತ್ಯಕ್ಷದರ್ಶಿ.
ಪ್ರತ್ಯಕ್ಷದರ್ಶಿ — pratyakṣadarśi — "an eyewitness".
ಒಬ್ಬ ಪ್ರತ್ಯಕ್ಷದರ್ಶಿ ಪೊಲೀಸರಿಗೆ ಎಲ್ಲವನ್ನೂ ಹೇಳಿದನು. obba pratyakṣadarśi polīsarige ellavannū hēḷidanu. An eyewitness told the police everything.
Plural: ಪ್ರತ್ಯಕ್ಷದರ್ಶಿಗಳು, pratyakṣadarśigaḷu.
ಪ್ರತ್ಯಕ್ಷ, before the eyes, and ದರ್ಶಿ, one who sees.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರತ್ಯಕ್ಷದರ್ಶಿ (pratyakṣadarśi) is borrowed from Sanskrit pratyakṣadarśin, "seeing a thing with one's own eyes" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮೃತ (mṛta) — the deceased; dead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an eyewitness, then the Kannada for damage.

You'll want to know: ಮೃತ.
ಮೃತ — mṛta — "the deceased; dead".
ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಮೃತರಾದವರ ಹೆಸರುಗಳು ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂದವು. apaghātadalli mṛtarādavara hesarugaḷu patrikeyalli bandavu. The names of those who died in the accident came out in the paper.
A news word: ಮೃತರಾದರು, they died; ಮೃತರ ಕುಟುಂಬ, the family of the dead.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೃತ (mṛta) is borrowed from Sanskrit mṛta, "dead, deceased" (Monier-Williams), from the root mṛ, "to die".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಅವಘಡ (avaghaḍa) — a mishap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for damage, then the Kannada for the deceased.

You'll want to know: ಅವಘಡ.
ಅವಘಡ — avaghaḍa — "a mishap".
ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಣ್ಣ ಅವಘಡ ಆಯಿತು, ಯಾರಿಗೂ ಏನೂ ಆಗಲಿಲ್ಲ. kārkhāneyalli saṇṇa avaghaḍa āyitu, yārigū ēnū āgalilla. There was a small mishap at the factory; nobody was hurt.
A news word for an accident with fire, gas or machines.

The word, taken apart.
ಅವಘಡ (avaghaḍa) is a Kannada word of the newspapers whose origin is not settled.
