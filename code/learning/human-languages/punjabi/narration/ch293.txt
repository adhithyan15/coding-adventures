Punjabi, chapter 293: The Lie of the Land.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮਿੱਟੀ (miṭṭī) — soil; earth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to dry up, then the Punjabi for to seep.

You'll want to know: ਮਿੱਟੀ.
ਮਿੱਟੀ — miṭṭī — "soil; earth".
ਸਾਡੇ ਖੇਤ ਦੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਨਰਮ ਹੈ। sāḍe khet dī miṭṭī naram hai. The soil of our field is soft.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਿੱਟੀ (miṭṭī) is inherited from Sanskrit mṛttikā, "earth, clay"; CDIAL lists Punjabi miṭṭī, "earth, soil, clay", under it (CDIAL 10286).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to seep, then the Punjabi for soil.

You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਮੀਨ.
ਜ਼ਮੀਨ — zamīn — "land; the ground".
ਮੇਰੇ ਪਿਤਾ ਜੀ ਕੋਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਜ਼ਮੀਨ ਹੈ। mere pitā jī kol bahut zamīn hai. My father has a lot of land.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਜ਼ਮੀਨ (zamīn) is a loan, usually traced to Persian zamīn, "earth, land".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਪੱਧਰਾ (paddhrā) — level; flat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for soil, then the Punjabi for land.

You'll want to know: ਪੱਧਰਾ.
ਪੱਧਰਾ — paddhrā — "level; flat".
ਪਿੰਡ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਜ਼ਮੀਨ ਪੱਧਰੀ ਹੈ। piṇḍ de neṛe zamīn paddhrī hai. Near the village the land is level.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੱਧਰਾ (paddhrā) is inherited from a reconstructed paddhara, "straight, level"; CDIAL lists Punjabi paddharā, "smooth and even", under it (CDIAL 7767).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਢਲਾਣ (ḍhalāṇ) — a slope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for land, then the Punjabi for level.

You'll want to know: ਢਲਾਣ.
ਢਲਾਣ — ḍhalāṇ — "a slope".
ਪਹਾੜ ਦੀ ਢਲਾਣ ਉੱਤੇ ਰੁੱਖ ਹਨ। pahāṛ dī ḍhalāṇ utte rukkh han. There are trees on the slope of the mountain.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਢਲਾਣ (ḍhalāṇ) is built on ḍhalṇā, "to fall, be poured out", from a reconstructed ḍhalati, "bends over, falls"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍhalṇā, "to be poured out, fall", under it (CDIAL 5581).
