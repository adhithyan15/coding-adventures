## You'll want to know: lanx, lancis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C464-STATERA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lanx, lancis** — "a scale pan; a dish, a platter".

> In ūnā lance merx est, in alterā pondus.
>
> *In one scale pan there are the goods, in the other the weight.*

Third declension, feminine: *lanx, lancis f.* English *balance* comes, through French, from *bilanx* 'having two pans'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lanx* is inherited; Lewis & Short and EtymDB compare Greek *lekos* 'dish, pan' as a cousin. English *balance* comes, through French, from Late Latin *bilanx* 'with two scale pans'.
