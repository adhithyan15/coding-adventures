7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
कारख़ाना (kārkhānā) — a factory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an owner, then the Hindi for a labourer.

You'll want to know: कारख़ाना.
कारख़ाना — kārkhānā — "a factory".

The word, taken apart.
कारख़ाना (kārkhānā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kārkhāna, "workshop". It is built from kār, "work", and khāna, "house".

You'll want to know: बैठक.
बैठक — baiṭhak — "a meeting, a sitting room".

The word, taken apart.
बैठक (baiṭhak) is the noun of बैठना, "to sit", with the ending -ak: a sitting, and a room to sit in. baiṭhnā comes from Sanskrit upaviśati, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245).

Lesson 5 of 7.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: रसोई.
रसोई — rasoī — "a kitchen".

The word, taken apart.
रसोई (rasoī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from rasavatī, "kitchen" (CDIAL 10656). It is built on rasa, "juice, flavour".
