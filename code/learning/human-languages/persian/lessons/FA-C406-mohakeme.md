## You'll want to know: محاکمه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C406-QAZI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**محاکمه** (*mohâkeme*) — "a trial".

> محاکمه او سه ماه طول کشید.
>
> *mohâkeme-ye u se mâh tul keshid.*
>
> His trial lasted three months.

*Mohâkeme kardan* — to put on trial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محاکمه** is usually traced to Arabic *muḥākama* "trial, bringing to judgement", from the same Arabic family as *ḥukm* "judgement".
