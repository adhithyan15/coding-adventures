## You'll want to know: きょうそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C462-CONTEST462-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうそう** — *kyōsō* — "competition; rivalry".

> **みせの きょうそうが きびしいです。** — *mise no kyōsō ga kibishii desu* — "Competition between shops is fierce."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうそう** (*kyōsō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyō*, "compete with" (Middle Chinese *gjaengH*) and *sō*, "contend" (*tsreang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
