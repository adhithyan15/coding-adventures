Bengali, chapter 165: Raisin, Jam, Cake, Sandesh, Paratha.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
কিশমিশ (kishmish) — a raisin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for shorts, then the Bengali for a sweater.

You'll want to know: কিশমিশ.
কিশমিশ — kishmish — "a raisin".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kishmish]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kishmish, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kishmish]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for shorts, then the Bengali for a sweater, then say kishmish again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does কিশমিশ (kishmish) mean? ("A raisin".) Say it once more.
