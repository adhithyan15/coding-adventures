## You'll want to know: lesionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C515-LESAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lesionar** — "to get injured (lesionar-se); to injure".

> O avançado lesionou-se no treino.
>
> *The striker got injured in training.*

Mostly reflexive, with the pronoun after the verb: *lesionou-se* — he got injured. *Estar lesionado* — to be injured.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lesionar* is built on *lesão* "injury", a learned borrowing from Latin *laesiō* (EtymDB).
