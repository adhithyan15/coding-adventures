## You'll want to know: थामना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C372-DAYA372-05]; assesses=[] -->

**थामना** — *thāmnā* — "to hold on to, to hold up".

> बच्चे ने माँ का हाथ थामा।
>
> *bacce ne māṁ kā hāth thāmā.*
>
> The child held on to his mother's hand.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मेरा हाथ थामा, *usne merā hāth thāmā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थामना** (*thāmnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *stambhate*, "props, stops" (CDIAL 13683).
