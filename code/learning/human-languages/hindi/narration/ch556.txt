Hindi, chapter 556: How Sure Am I?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संदेह (sandeh) — doubt (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a fall, then the Hindi for a downfall.

You'll want to know: संदेह.
संदेह — sandeh — "doubt (formal)".
उसकी बात पर मुझे कोई संदेह नहीं है। Uskī bāt par mujhe koī sandeh nahīṃ hai. I have no doubt at all about what he says.
Masculine.
A formal word; everyday speech says शक.

The word, taken apart.
संदेह (sandeh) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit saṃdeha, "doubt, uncertainty" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
