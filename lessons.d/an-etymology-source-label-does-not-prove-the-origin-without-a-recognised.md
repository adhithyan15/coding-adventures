# An etymology source label does not prove the origin without a recognised relation

The empty-etymology-block gate intentionally does not treat a bare phrase such
as “records English X as the source” as sufficient. Inside a taken-apart block,
state the relationship directly as well: “the word comes from English X.”

This keeps the learner-visible claim explicit, lets the conservative origin
detector distinguish descent from a general citation, and avoids weakening the
gate with a broad `source of` signal that could certify unrelated prose.
