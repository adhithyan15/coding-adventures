# आयो, आई, आया — came

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GAYO-01, MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-03, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *everyone went home*. Then *to come*, **आवणो**, and *he comes every
day*.

## Grammar Lens: the past of आवणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C160-AYO-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-03, MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GAYO-01, MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04] -->

**आयो**, **आई** and **आया** do for **आवणो** what **गयो**, **गई** and **गया** do
for **जाणो**:

> **राम काल आयो।**
>
> *rām kāl āyo.* — Ram came yesterday.

> **मां दिनूगै आई।**
>
> *mā̃ dinūgai āī.* — Mother came early in the morning.

> **सगळा आथण आया।**
>
> *sagḷā āthaṇ āyā.* — Everyone came in the evening.

CDIAL lists Old Marwari *āīyo*, "he came" (CDIAL 1045).
