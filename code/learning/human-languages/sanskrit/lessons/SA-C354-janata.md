## You'll want to know: जनता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C354-NAGARIKA354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जनता** — *janatā* — "the public".

> जनता नूतनां नीतिं न इच्छति।
>
> *janatā nūtanāṃ nītiṃ na icchati.*
>
> The public does not want the new policy.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जनता** (*janatā*) is built on *jana*, "people", with the collective ending *-tā*; MW gives *jana-tā* "a number of men, an assemblage of people, a community, subjects" (MW).
