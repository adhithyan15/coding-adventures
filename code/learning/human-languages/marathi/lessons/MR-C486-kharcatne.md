## You'll want to know: खरचटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C486-IJA486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खरचटणे** — *kharcaṭṇe* — "to get grazed".

> सायकलवरून पडल्यावर त्याच्या गुडघ्याला खरचटलं.
>
> *sāykalvarūn paḍlyāvar tyācyā guḍghyālā kharcaṭlaṁ.*
>
> When he fell off his bicycle he grazed his knee.

Past (to him …): त्याला खरचटलं, *tyālā kharcaṭlaṁ*.

Impersonal: the grazed part takes -ला: हाताला खरचटलं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खरचटणे** (*kharcaṭṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
