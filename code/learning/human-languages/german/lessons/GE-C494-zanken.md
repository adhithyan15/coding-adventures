## You'll want to know: zanken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C494-STREIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**zanken** — "to squabble (sich zanken)".

> Die Geschwister zanken sich um das letzte Stück Kuchen.
>
> *The brother and sister are squabbling over the last piece of cake.*

Mostly reflexive: *sich zanken um* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
