## You'll want to know: しんぱん

**しんぱん** — *shinpan* — "a referee; an umpire".

> **しんぱんが ふえを ふきました。** — *shinpan ga fue o fukimashita* — "The referee blew the whistle."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんぱん** (*shinpan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "judge" (Middle Chinese *syimX*) and *pan*, "judgement" (*phanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
