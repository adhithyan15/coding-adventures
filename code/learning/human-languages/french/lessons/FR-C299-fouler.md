## You'll want to know: fouler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C299-CHUTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**fouler** — "to sprain (as se fouler); to tread on".

> Il s'est foulé la cheville en courant.
>
> *He sprained his ankle while running.*

In this sense mostly reflexive, with a body part: *se fouler le poignet*. Without *se*, it means to tread on or to press: *fouler le sol*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fouler* is inherited through Old French *foler* "to crush, to trample" from a verb built on Latin *fullō* "a fuller", who trod cloth (EtymDB).
