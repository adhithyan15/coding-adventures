## You'll want to know: बेवकूफ़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C474-AQL474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बेवकूफ़** — *bevkūf* — "a fool; foolish".

> मैं कितना बेवकूफ़ हूँ, छाता घर पर भूल गया!
>
> *maiṁ kitnā bevkūf hūṁ, chātā ghar par bhūl gayā!*
>
> What a fool I am — I left my umbrella at home!

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेवकूफ़** (*bevkūf*) is usually traced to Persian *bēvuqūf*, "ignorant", which joins Persian *bē-*, "without", to Arabic *vuqūf*, "knowledge, understanding".
