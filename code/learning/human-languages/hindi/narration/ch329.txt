Hindi, chapter 329: A Festival of Colours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
छिड़कना (chiṛaknā) — to sprinkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a firecracker, then the Hindi for fireworks.

You'll want to know: छिड़कना.
छिड़कना — chiṛaknā — "to sprinkle".
दादी ने आँगन में पानी छिड़का। dādī ne āṁgan meṁ pānī chiṛkā. Grandmother sprinkled water in the courtyard.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पानी छिड़का, usne pānī chiṛkā.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited from chiṭ, a form CDIAL reconstructs for sudden splashes and scattering (CDIAL 5035). You छिड़कना (chiṛaknā) water, salt or colour.

The word, taken apart.
भिगोना (bhigonā) is the causative of bhīgnā, to get wet, which CDIAL 9500 sets under bhiyagna: to make someone or something wet through.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to drench, then the Hindi for to toss up.

You'll want to know: झूमना.
झूमना — jhūmnā — "to sway (with joy or music)".
ढोल बज रहा था और सब लोग झूम रहे थे। ḍhol baj rahā thā aur sab log jhūm rahe the. The drum was playing and everyone was swaying.
Past (he …): वह झूमा, vah jhūmā.

The word, taken apart.
झूमना (jhūmnā) is set by CDIAL under kṣumbhati, "shakes" (CDIAL 3726), with a query of its own: the link is not certain. It is a happy, rocking movement: trees in the wind and people at a festival झूमते हैं.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मस्ती (mastī) — merriment; high spirits.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to toss up, then the Hindi for to sway (with joy or music).

You'll want to know: मस्ती.
मस्ती — mastī — "merriment; high spirits".
त्योहार के दिन बच्चे सुबह से मस्ती में थे। tyohār ke din bacce subah se mastī meṁ the. On the day of the festival the children were in high spirits from the morning on.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
मस्ती (mastī) is mast, carefree and merry, usually traced to Persian mast, "intoxicated", with the noun ending -ī: the joy of a day with no work.
