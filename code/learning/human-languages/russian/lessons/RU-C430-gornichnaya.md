## You'll want to know: горничная
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C430-POSTEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**горничная** (*górnichnaya*) — "a chambermaid, a maid".

> Каждое утро горничная убирает номер.
>
> *Kázhdoye útro górnichnaya ubiráyet nómer.*
>
> Every morning the chambermaid cleans the room.

An adjective used as a noun; it declines like an adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*горничная* is built on *горница* 'a room', first an upper room. EtymDB traces *горница* to Old East Slavic *gorĭnĭ*, a word that goes with *гора* 'a mountain'.
