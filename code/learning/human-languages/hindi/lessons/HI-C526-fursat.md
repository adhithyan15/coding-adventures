## You'll want to know: फ़ुरसत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C526-FURSAT526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़ुरसत** — *fursat* — "free time; leisure".

> फ़ुरसत में मैं कहानियाँ पढ़ता हूँ।
>
> *Fursat meṁ maiṁ kahāniyā̃ paṛhtā hūṁ.*
>
> In my free time I read stories.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़ुरसत** (*fursat*) is usually traced to Arabic *furṣa*, "an occasion, a chance", which reached Hindi through Persian; in Hindi it is time with nothing that must be done.
