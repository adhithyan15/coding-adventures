## You'll want to know: غلاف جوي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C463-MUNAKH463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**غلاف جوي** — *ghilāf jawwī* — "the atmosphere".

> يحمي الغلاف الجوي الأرض.
>
> *yaḥmī al-ghilāf al-jawwī al-arḍ.*
>
> The atmosphere protects the earth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غلاف جوي** (*ghilāf jawwī*) is a modern coinage built from two native Arabic words (Lane gives both roots): *ghilāf*, "a cover, a sheath", on *gh-l-f*, and *jawwī*, "of the air", on *j-w-w*.
