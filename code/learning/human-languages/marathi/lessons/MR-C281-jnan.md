## You'll want to know: ज्ञान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C281-JNAN-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C234-GUN234-01] -->

**ज्ञान** — *jñān* — "knowledge".

> **पुस्तकांमुळे ज्ञान वाढतं.**
>
> *pustakāmmuḷe jñān vāḍhtaṁ.* — "Knowledge grows because of books."

The first block, **ज्ञ**, is said *dny* in Marathi: **ज्ञान** is said *dnyān*. The romanization keeps the letters, *jñ*. **ज्ञान महत्त्वाचं आहे** (*jñān mahattvācaṁ āhe*): knowledge is important.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्ञान** (*jñān*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *jñāna*, "knowledge", on the root *jñā*, "to know" (CDIAL 5281). Under the same entry CDIAL lists an inherited Marathi form, *jāṇ*, in the narrower sense "acknowledgement of favours"; **ज्ञान** is the learned word, taken whole from Sanskrit.
