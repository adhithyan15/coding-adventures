## You'll want to know: ਕਾਮਯਾਬ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C364-JOB364-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਮਯਾਬ** — *kāmyāb* — "successful".

> ਮਿਹਨਤ ਨਾਲ ਲੋਕ ਕਾਮਯਾਬ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *mihnat nāl lok kāmyāb hunde han.*
>
> People succeed through hard work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਮਯਾਬ** (*kāmyāb*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *kāmyāb*, "successful".
