## You'll want to know: 活
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C302-BIRTH302-02]; assesses=[] -->

**活** — *huó* — "to live; alive".

> **他活了一百岁** *tā huó le yìbǎi suì* — He lived to a hundred.

The new character, in pieces: 活 — nine strokes in three pieces: the water piece of 洗 on the left, three strokes; then 千, three strokes; then 口 under it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**活** (*huó*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'not dead' and also 'the sound of flowing water'.
