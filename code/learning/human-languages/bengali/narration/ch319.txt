Bengali, chapter 319: Digging Up the Past.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ধ্বংসাবশেষ (dhvôngsābôshesh) — ruins; remains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to found, then the Bengali for liberation.

You'll want to know: ধ্বংসাবশেষ.
ধ্বংসাবশেষ — dhvôngsābôshesh — "ruins; remains".
নদীর পাশে একটা পুরনো শহরের ধ্বংসাবশেষ আছে। nôdīr pāshe ekṭā purôno shôhôrer dhvôngsābôshesh āchhe. Beside the river are the ruins of an old city.

The word, taken apart.
ধ্বংসাবশেষ (dhvôngsābôshesh) joins ধ্বংস, ruin, to Sanskrit avaśeṣa, 'what is left' (Monier-Williams), the word in অবশেষে: what is left of a ruin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
খনন করা (khônôn kôrā) — to excavate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for ruins, then the Bengali for a statue.

You'll want to know: খনন করা.
খনন করা — khônôn kôrā — "to excavate".
বিজ্ঞানীরা দশ বছর ধরে জায়গাটা খনন করলেন। bigyānīrā dôsh bôchhôr dhôre jāygāṭā khônôn kôrlen. The scientists excavated the site for ten years.
Past (he/she …): তিনি পুরনো শহরটা খনন করলেন, tini purôno shôhôrṭā khônôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
খনন করা (khônôn kôrā) puts Sanskrit khanana, 'the act of digging or excavating' (Monier-Williams), before করা; খোঁড়া is the everyday word for digging.

শিলালিপিটা পড়ে বিজ্ঞানীরা রাজার নাম জানলেন। shilālipiṭā pôṛe bigyānīrā rājār nām jānlen. By reading the inscription the scientists learned the king's name.

The word, taken apart.
শিলালিপি (shilālipi) joins Sanskrit śilā, 'a stone, a rock', to lipi, 'writing, a script' (Monier-Williams): writing cut in stone.
