Tamil, chapter 505: The End of Life.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ஆயுள் (āyuḷ) — a lifespan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to fade, then the Tamil for to shrink.

You'll want to know: ஆயுள்.
ஆயுள் — āyuḷ — "a lifespan".
நல்ல உணவு ஆயுளை அதிகரிக்கும். nalla uṇavu āyuḷai atikarikkum. Good food lengthens one’s life.
ஆயுள் (āyuḷ) முழுவதும், all one’s life.

The word, taken apart.
ஆயுள் (āyuḷ) is usually traced to Sanskrit āyus, "life, the length of life"; our sources do not list the Tamil form.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: விதவை.
விதவை — vitavai — "a widow".
கணவர் இறந்த பிறகு அவள் விதவை ஆனாள். kaṇavar iṟanta piṟaku avaḷ vitavai āṉāḷ. After her husband died she became a widow.

The word, taken apart.
விதவை (vitavai) is borrowed from Sanskrit vidhavā, "widow" (CDIAL 11752); the final -ā becomes Tamil -ஐ.
