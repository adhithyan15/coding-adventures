# কারণ (kārôn) — a reason; because

## You'll want to know: কারণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C268-KARON-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C169-JINIS169-04, BN-LEX-C81-PLACE81-03, BN-LEX-C255-JINIS255-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-NOW-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

**কারণ** — *kārôn* — "a reason; because".

> **তোমার রাগের কারণ কী?**
>
> *tomār rāger kārôn kī?* — "What's the reason you're angry?"

As a noun it is "a reason": **রাগের কারণ**, "the reason for the anger". It is also the everyday "because", opening the reason after the result:

> **আজ আমি বাড়িতে থাকব, কারণ বাইরে ঝড় হচ্ছে।**
>
> *āj āmi bāṛite thākbô, kārôn bāire jhôṛ hôchchhe.* — "I'll stay at home today, because there's a storm outside."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কারণ** (*kārôn*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *kāraṇa*, "cause", which CDIAL (3057) lists with the Bengali word under it. CDIAL files *kāraṇa* under the root *kṛ*, "to do, to make": a cause is what makes a thing happen.
