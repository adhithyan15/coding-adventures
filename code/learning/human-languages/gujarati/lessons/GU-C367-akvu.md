## You'll want to know: આંકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C367-SAMBHAV367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**આંકવું** — *ā̃kvũ* — "to assess".

> જો તમે ઘરની કિંમત ઓછી આંકશો તો ખોટ જશે.
>
> *jo tame gharnī kimmat ochī ā̃kśo to khoṭ jaśe.*
>
> If you value the house too low, you will make a loss.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘરની કિંમત આંકી, *teṇe gharnī kimmat ā̃kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આંકવું** (*ā̃kvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aṅkáyati*, "marks"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to mark, to appraise" (CDIAL 104).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
