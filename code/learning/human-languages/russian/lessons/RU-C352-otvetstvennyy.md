## You'll want to know: ответственный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C352-VINA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ответственный** (*otvétstvennyy*) — "responsible, in charge".

> Кто ответственный за этот проект?
>
> *Kto otvétstvennyy za étot proyékt?*
>
> Who is responsible for this project?

*Ответственный за* + accusative. Short form *ответствен*. Of a moment: *ответственный момент*, a crucial moment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ответственный** is built on *ответ* "an answer", from *от-* "back" and the old word for counsel and speech, Old Russian *větŭ*. The person responsible is the one who must answer for it.
