## You'll want to know: உடல்நலம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C88-DOCTOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**உடல்நலம்** (*uṭalnalam*) — "health", literally "body-wellbeing".

**நலம்** is the wellbeing of your first greetings; **உடல்** is "the body".
