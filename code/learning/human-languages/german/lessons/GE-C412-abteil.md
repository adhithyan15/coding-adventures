## You'll want to know: das Abteil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C412-BAHNSTEIG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Abteil** — "the compartment (on a train)".

> In unserem Abteil sitzen nur zwei Leute.
>
> *Only two people are sitting in our compartment.*

Plural: *die Abteile*. Many new trains have open carriages instead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Abteil* is built on *abteilen* 'to divide off', from *Teil* 'part', inherited from Old High German *teil* and Proto-Germanic *dailą* (EtymDB).
