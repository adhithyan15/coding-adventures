# অপচয় (ôpôchôy) — waste; wastefulness

## You'll want to know: অপচয়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C419-ABHAB419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অপচয়** — *ôpôchôy* — "waste; wastefulness".

> আলো জ্বালিয়ে রাখা বিদ্যুতের অপচয়।
>
> *ālo jvāliye rākhā bidyuter ôpôchôy.*
>
> Leaving the lights on is a waste of electricity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অপচয়** (*ôpôchôy*) is Sanskrit *apacaya*, "diminution, decrease" (Monier-Williams); the sense of wasting is the Bengali use.
