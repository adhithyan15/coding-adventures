## You'll want to know: laetitia, laetitiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C489-GAUD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**laetitia, laetitiae** — "joy, gladness".

> Post victōriam magna laetitia in urbe erat.
>
> *After the victory there was great joy in the city.*

Feminine, first declension. *Laetitia* is lively joy that shows; *gaudium* is joy felt inside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**laetitia** is built on the adjective *laetus* "glad, happy" with the noun ending *-itia* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB derives it from *laetus*).
