## You'll want to know: der Gesichtspunkt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C330-STANDPUNKT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Gesichtspunkt** — "the aspect, the angle (of a question)".

> Unter diesem Gesichtspunkt ist die Idee gar nicht so schlecht.
>
> *From this angle, the idea is not so bad at all.*

Plural: *die Gesichtspunkte*. *Gesicht* here keeps its old sense, sight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gesichtspunkt* joins *Gesicht*, here in its older sense "sight", from the family of *sehen* "to see", to *Punkt* "point", from Latin *punctum* (EtymDB). It is usually explained as a loan translation of the Latin term of optics *punctum visus*, "the point of sight".
