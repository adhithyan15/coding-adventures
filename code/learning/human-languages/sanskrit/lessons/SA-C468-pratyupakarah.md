# प्रत्युपकारः (pratyupakāraḥ) — a favour in return

## You'll want to know: प्रत्युपकारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C468-KRTAVEDIN468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्युपकारः** — *pratyupakāraḥ* — "a favour in return".

> उपकारस्य प्रत्युपकारः उचितः अस्ति।
>
> *upakārasya pratyupakāraḥ ucitaḥ asti.*
>
> Returning a favour is right.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्युपकारः** (*pratyupakāraḥ*) is built from *prati*, "back", and **उपकारः**, "a favour"; MW gives "returning a service or favour, gratitude" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
