## You'll want to know: కానుక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C347-VINDU347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కానుక** — *kānuka* — "a present; an offering".

> అతిథులు పెళ్ళికూతురికి కానుకలు ఇచ్చారు.
>
> *atithulu peḷḷikūturiki kānukalu iccāru.*
>
> The guests gave the bride presents.

Plural: కానుకలు, *kānukalu*.

Close to బహుమతి; కానుక is warmer, a gift to someone you honour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కానుక** (*kānuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1443 lists Telugu *kānuka*, "a gift offered to a superior, a present", under *kanu*, "to see".
