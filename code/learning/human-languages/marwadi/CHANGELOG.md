# Changelog

## Unreleased -- the first transaction: one function over three noun sets

- Added sixty <=5-minute sessions across Chapters 26--31 that give the track's
  nineteen everyday nouns something to do. Before this tranche the course could
  name a shop, a rickshaw, and a plate of food and could not buy, price, or
  order any of them.
- Chose one function reused over all three existing noun sets rather than a
  fourth noun domain, as HL-C217 recommended. The five-turn counter exchange --
  **ये कपड़ा दिखावो। / ये कितणे कू है? / ये घणो महंगो है। / थोड़ु सस्तो करो। /
  पैसा ले लो।** -- runs unchanged at a shop, a food stall, and a roadside, with
  only the noun moving.
- Took the track from 53 to 71 distinct taught headwords against the 300-word
  pre-A1 target: **ये, दिखावो, कितणो, घणो, थोड़ु, करो, लो, दो, आखरी, लावो** and
  eight complete lines built from them.
- Closed the HL09 3.1 verb-vocabulary criterion at pre-A1. The track taught
  zero verb headwords against a target of five; **दिखावो, करो, लो, दो, लावो**
  are now tagged `MW-VERB-*`, and vocabulary is the only remaining blocker.
- Added exactly one new Devanagari sign in sixty lessons. **ख** is isolated
  against **क** by breath, one lesson before **दिखावो** needs it, and every
  other word and line in the tranche is written from signs the hand already
  owns. Chapters 27--31 add no sign at all.
- Checked every candidate sentence against the taught set before scheduling it,
  which is why **चाहिए** and **और** are absent: they need **ए** and **औ**,
  which have no lesson. Untaught inventory is unchanged: **ख** leaves the list,
  and **झ, ञ, ढ, ध, फ, ष, ळ**, the independent vowels other than **आ** and
  **ई**, and **ृ** remain.
- Raised script-atom review density deliberately, the pattern the corpus-wide
  2-reviews-per-`SCRIPT` versus 14-per-`LEX` gap asks for. Every chapter of the
  tranche ends in a script close that retrieves the chapter's most-leaned-on
  signs ALONE rather than inside a word, and Chapter 31 adds a lesson that does
  nothing else. Marwadi's `SCRIPT` atoms went from a mean of 7.59 reviews to
  9.81 and now sit above its `LEX` atoms at 9.64; the least-drilled single sign
  went from three reviews to six.
- Registered `aspirated-kh` in the track's own sound-tag owner, the one sound
  the tranche introduces.
- Grounded every word and line in Marwari Pathshala Lessons 6 and 9, and named
  the variation instead of flattening it: **सस्तो** is recorded as the printed
  **-o** form of the **सस्ता** taught in Chapter 19, and **कितणे** as the
  bent form of **कितणो** before **कू**, each learned whole now and left for a
  later lesson to explain.
- Kept the debt explicit. Nothing here understands a number, so no amount a
  seller actually names can be read; refusing a final price and walking away,
  thanking somebody for doing what you asked, and asking again more softly are
  all still untaught.
- The strict ramp remains all-zero at 257 lessons: no script-closure violation,
  no glyph shown before it is taught, no headword without romanization, no
  missed reinforcement window, no atom left unrevisited, and no lesson over the
  five-minute ceiling.
