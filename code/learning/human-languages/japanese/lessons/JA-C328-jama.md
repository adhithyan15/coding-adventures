## You'll want to know: じゃま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C328-KOUKYOU328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**じゃま** — *jama* — "in the way; a hindrance".

> **じてんしゃが みちに あるから、じゃまです。** — *jitensha ga michi ni aru kara, jama desu* — "The bicycle is on the road, so it is in the way."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゃま** (*jama*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ja*, "wicked" (Middle Chinese *zjae*) and *ma*, "demon" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
