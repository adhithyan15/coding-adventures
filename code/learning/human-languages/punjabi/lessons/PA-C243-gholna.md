## You'll want to know: ਘੋਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C243-KAM243-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੋਲਣਾ** — *gholṇā* — "to dissolve; to stir in".

> ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੰਡ ਘੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ pāṇī vich khaṇḍ gholdā hāṁ.*
>
> I dissolve sugar in water.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਖੰਡ ਘੋਲੀ, *maiṁ khaṇḍ gholī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੋਲਣਾ** (*gholṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ghōlayati*, "mixes, stirs together"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gholaṇā*, "to stir, mix, dissolve" (CDIAL 4526).
