## You'll want to know: 节气
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C340-CUSTOM340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**节气** — *jiéqì* — "solar term".

> **每年有二十四个节气** *měinián yǒu èrshísì ge jiéqì* — Every year has twenty-four solar terms.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**节气** (*jiéqì*) is built from 节 'a joint' and 气 'breath', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tset* and *khj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a joint of bamboo' and the second 'breath'. The calendar sense is not given there.
