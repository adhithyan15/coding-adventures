Marathi, chapter 194: To phone, To discuss, To forbid, To protect, To die.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फोन करणे (phon karṇe) — to phone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to dry, then the Marathi for to iron.

You'll want to know: फोन करणे.
फोन करणे — phon karṇe — "to phone".

The word, taken apart.
फोन is a loan from English phone, short for telephone. करणे is "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: मनाई करणे.
मनाई करणे — manāī karṇe — "to forbid".

The word, taken apart.
मनाई is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic manāhī, "forbidden things", whose root is n-h-y, "to forbid" (Lane). करणे is "to do".

Lesson 4 of 5.
रक्षण करणे (rakṣaṇ karṇe) — to protect.

You'll want to know: रक्षण करणे.
रक्षण करणे — rakṣaṇ karṇe — "to protect".

The word, taken apart.
रक्षण is Sanskrit rakṣaṇa, "guarding, tending", a tatsama; CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi form as rākhaṇ, "preserving" (CDIAL 10546). करणे is "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
मरणे (marṇe) — to die.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to forbid, then the Marathi for to protect.

You'll want to know: मरणे.
मरणे — marṇe — "to die".

The word, taken apart.
मरणे (marṇe) comes from Sanskrit marate, "will die" (CDIAL 9871). CDIAL thinks it possible, even probable, that the later mar- verbs continue this Vedic mar- directly, helped by forms such as mariṣyati, "will die".
