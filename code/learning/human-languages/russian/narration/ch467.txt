Russian, chapter 467: Frost and ice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
мороз (moróz) — frost, freezing cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to rumble, then the Russian for to flash.

You'll want to know: мороз.
мороз (moróz) — "frost, freezing cold".
Утром был мороз, и окна были белые. Útrom byl moróz, i ókna býli bélyye. In the morning there was a frost, and the windows were white.
Masculine. Минус двадцать — сильный мороз (moróz) — minus twenty is a hard frost.

The word, taken apart.
мороз (moróz) comes from Old East Slavic morozŭ (EtymDB), with the native -оро- shape; the same root gives мёрзнуть (myórznut) 'to freeze'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
гололёд (gololyód) — black ice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to flash, then the Russian for frost.

You'll want to know: гололёд.
гололёд (gololyód) — "black ice".
Из-за гололёда машины едут медленно. Iz-za gololyóda mashíny yédut médlenno. Because of the black ice the cars are going slowly.
Masculine. Built from голый — bare — and лёд — ice.

The word, taken apart.
гололёд (gololyód) is built from голый 'bare' and лёд 'ice': bare ice on the road. Лёд is inherited from Proto-Slavic ledŭ (EtymDB).

Из-за метели мы два дня сидели дома. Iz-za metéli my dva dnya sidéli dóma. Because of the blizzard we stayed at home for two days.
Feminine. Built on мести — to sweep.

The word, taken apart.
метель (metél) is built on мести 'to sweep': wind that sweeps the snow along. EtymDB lists метель (metél) without a deeper source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
мёрзнуть (myórznut) — to feel the cold, to freeze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for black ice, then the Russian for a blizzard.

You'll want to know: мёрзнуть.
мёрзнуть (myórznut) — "to feel the cold, to freeze".
Без шапки уши быстро мёрзнут. Bez shápki úshi býstro myórznut. Without a hat your ears soon get cold.
Imperfective; perfective замёрзнуть. Past мёрз, мёрзла.

The word, taken apart.
мёрзнуть (myórznut) is inherited from Proto-Slavic mĭrznǫti (EtymDB), on the same root as мороз (moróz) 'frost'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
поскользнуться (poskolznútsya) — to slip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a blizzard, then the Russian for to feel the cold.

You'll want to know: поскользнуться.
поскользнуться (poskolznútsya) — "to slip".
На гололёде бабушка поскользнулась и упала. Na gololyóde bábushka poskolznúlas i upála. Grandma slipped on the black ice and fell.
Perfective; imperfective поскальзываться.

The word, taken apart.
поскользнуться (poskolznútsya) is built from по-, скользнуть 'to slide once', from скользить 'to slide', and -ся. EtymDB lists the verb without a deeper source.
