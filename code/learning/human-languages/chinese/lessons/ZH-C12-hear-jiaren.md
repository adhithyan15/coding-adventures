# Hear *jiārén* — family members

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-JIA-01, ZH-LEX-REN-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **home or family**, then **person**.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-JIAREN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *jiārén* — **family members; family**

You already own both sounds: *jiā* + *rén*. The compound names the people of
the home. Say it once from the meaning before looking at either character.
