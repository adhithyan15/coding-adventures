## You'll want to know: দুর্যোগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C538-DURJOG538-01]; assesses=[] -->

**দুর্যোগ** — *durjog* — "a natural disaster".

> দুর্যোগ হলে আমরা স্কুলে আশ্রয় নেব।
>
> *Durjog hôle āmrā skule āshrôy nebô.*
>
> If disaster strikes, we will take shelter in the school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দুর্যোগ** (*durjog*) is Sanskrit *duryoga*, "a bad contrivance" (Monier-Williams): *dur-*, "bad", and *yoga*, "joining"; the sense "a natural disaster" is the modern language's own.
