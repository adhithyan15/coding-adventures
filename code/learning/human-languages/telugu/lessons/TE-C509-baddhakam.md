# బద్ధకం (baddhakaṁ) — laziness; sluggishness

## You'll want to know: బద్ధకం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C509-MANISHI509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**బద్ధకం** — *baddhakaṁ* — "laziness; sluggishness".

> ఆదివారం ఉదయం లేవడానికి నాకు బద్ధకం.
>
> *ādivāraṁ udayaṁ lēvaḍāniki nāku baddhakaṁ.*
>
> On Sunday mornings I feel too lazy to get up.

నాకు బద్ధకంగా ఉంది, I feel lazy. బద్ధకస్తుడు, a lazy man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బద్ధకం** (*baddhakaṁ*) is a Telugu word for sluggishness and laziness; its origin is not settled.
