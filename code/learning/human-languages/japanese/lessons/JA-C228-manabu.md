## You'll want to know: まなぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C228-LEARN228-01]; assesses=[] -->

**まなぶ** — *manabu* — "to learn; to study in depth".

> **あねは がっこうで にほんごを まなびます。** — *ane wa gakkō de nihongo o manabimasu* — "My elder sister studies Japanese at school."

Polite **まなびます**, *manabimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まなぶ** (*manabu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
