## You'll want to know: બોધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C335-SADHU335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બોધ** — *bodh* — "a moral (of a story)".

> આ વાર્તાનો બોધ શું છે?
>
> *ā vārtāno bodh śũ che?*
>
> What is the moral of this story?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બોધ** (*bodh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *bodha*, "knowing, understanding" (Monier-Williams): what a story makes you understand at the end.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
