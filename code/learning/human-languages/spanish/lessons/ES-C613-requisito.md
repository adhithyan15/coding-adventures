## You'll want to know: el requisito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C613-REQUIERE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el requisito** — "the requirement".

> El inglés es un requisito para el puesto.
>
> *English is a requirement for the post.*

*Cumplir los requisitos* — to meet the requirements.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*requisito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *requisitus* 'required', the participle of *requirere*, built on *quaerere* 'to seek' (EtymDB).
