সন্ধ্যায় দিদিমা বারান্দায় একটা প্রদীপ জ্বালালেন। sôndhyāy didimā bārāndāy ekṭā prôdīp jvālālen. In the evening my grandmother lit an oil lamp on the veranda.

Lesson 3 of 5.
আলপনা (ālpônā) — a floor painting (alpona).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an oil lamp, then the Bengali for fireworks.

You'll want to know: আলপনা.
আলপনা — ālpônā — "a floor painting (alpona)".
পুজোর আগে মেয়েরা দরজার সামনে আলপনা আঁকল। pujor āge meyerā dôrjār sāmne ālpônā ā̃klô. Before the puja the girls painted an alpona in front of the door.

The word, taken apart.
আলপনা (ālpônā) comes down from Sanskrit ālimpana, 'whitening, painting' (CDIAL 1385, which lists Bengali ālpanā): the white patterns drawn on a floor for a festival.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ঝলমল করা (jhôlmôl kôrā) — to glitter; to sparkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for fireworks, then the Bengali for a floor painting.

You'll want to know: ঝলমল করা.
ঝলমল করা — jhôlmôl kôrā — "to glitter; to sparkle".
আলোয় পুরো শহরটা ঝলমল করছিল। āloy puro shôhôrṭā jhôlmôl kôrchhilô. The whole town was glittering with lights.
Past (he/she …): সেই শহর আলোয় ঝলমল করল, sei shôhôr āloy jhôlmôl kôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
ঝলমল করা (jhôlmôl kôrā) puts ঝলমল, an expressive word whose doubled sound is the flicker of light, before করা. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

The word, taken apart.
ফানুস (phānus) is usually traced to Persian fānūs, 'a lantern'; in Bengal it is the paper lantern sent up into the sky.
