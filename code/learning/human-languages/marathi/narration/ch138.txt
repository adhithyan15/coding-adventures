Marathi, chapter 138: Peas, Pomegranate, Watermelon, Coconut, Flour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मटार (maṭār) — peas.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for garlic, then the Marathi for ginger.

You'll want to know: मटार.
मटार — maṭār — "peas".

The word, taken apart.
मटार (maṭār) matches Hindi maṭar, "pea", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed maṭṭara, "pea". CDIAL lists Marathi's own form, maṭh, "pea", under its variant maṭṭha (CDIAL 9724).

डाळिंब (ḍāḷimb) — a pomegranate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for ginger, then the Marathi for peas.

You'll want to know: डाळिंब.
डाळिंब — ḍāḷimb — "a pomegranate".

The word, taken apart.
डाळिंब (ḍāḷimb) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dāḍimba, "pomegranate" (CDIAL 6254). CDIAL gives dāḍimba as a variant of Sanskrit dāḍima.

नारळ (nāraḷ) — a coconut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pomegranate, then the Marathi for a watermelon.

You'll want to know: नारळ.
नारळ — nāraḷ — "a coconut".

The word, taken apart.
नारळ (nāraḷ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit nārikela, "coconut palm and fruit" (CDIAL 7075). CDIAL follows Bloch in taking the Sanskrit word from a Dravidian compound, comparing Tamil nāri, "fibre".
