## You'll want to know: нитка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C501-SEWING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**нитка** (*nítka*) — "a thread".

> Нужна чёрная нитка, чтобы пришить пуговицу.
>
> *Nuzhná chyórnaya nítka, chtóby prishít púgovitsu.*
>
> I need black thread to sew the button on.

Feminine; genitive plural ниток. A diminutive of *нить*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*нитка* is a diminutive of *нить* 'thread'; EtymDB lists *нитка* without a source, and *нить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *nitь* (EtymDB).
