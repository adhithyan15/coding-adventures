# తేమ (tēma) — humidity; moisture

## You'll want to know: తేమ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C476-USHNOGRATA476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తేమ** — *tēma* — "humidity; moisture".

> సముద్రం దగ్గర గాలిలో తేమ ఎక్కువ.
>
> *samudraṁ daggara gālilō tēma ekkuva.*
>
> Near the sea there is a lot of moisture in the air.

తేమగా ఉంది, it is damp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తేమ** (*tēma*) is a Telugu word for damp and moisture whose origin is not settled.
