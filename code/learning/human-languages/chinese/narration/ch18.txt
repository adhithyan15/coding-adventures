Lesson 2 of 23.
看 (kàn) — a hand above an eye.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — the shape until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 12 seconds]
Say look or watch as kàn. Then write the familiar 中 once and put it away before meeting the new shape.

Script — the shape.
Write 看 (kàn) in nine strokes. Make the short top stroke first, then the two crossing strokes and the long left fall above. Finish with 目 below: left, top-and-right, two inner bars, bottom. Nine strokes; eight lifts.

Guided Practice.
Trace 看 (kàn) once. Say hand above, eye below while keeping the lower box wide enough for its two inner bars.

Lesson 12 of 23.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. Warm-up.

Script — the shape.
Write 书 (shū) in four strokes: a short horizontal that folds down, a second horizontal that folds and hooks, the long central upright, then the small dot at the upper right. Four strokes; three lifts.
