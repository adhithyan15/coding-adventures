## You'll want to know: la hipótesis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C618-SUPONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la hipótesis** — "the hypothesis".

> Es solo una hipótesis.
>
> *It is only a hypothesis.*

The same form in the plural: *las hipótesis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hipótesis* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *hypothesis*, taken from Greek *hypothesis* 'a base, a supposition', from *hypo* 'under' and *thesis* 'a placing' (EtymDB).
