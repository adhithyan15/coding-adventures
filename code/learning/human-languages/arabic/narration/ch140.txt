Arabic, chapter 140: Palm, Fingernail, Brain, Heel, Lip.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
كف (kaff) — a palm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a waist, then the Arabic for an elbow.

You'll want to know: كف.
كف — kaff — "a palm".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kaff]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kaff, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kaff]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a waist, then the Arabic for an elbow, then say kaff again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does كف (kaff) mean? ("A palm".) Say it once more.
