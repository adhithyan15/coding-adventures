## You'll want to know: いせき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C313-KIROKU313-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いせき** — *iseki* — "ruins; remains".

> **やまの なかで ふるい いせきが みつかりました。** — *yama no naka de furui iseki ga mitsukarimashita* — "Old ruins were found in the mountains."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いせき** (*iseki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *i*, "leave behind" (Middle Chinese *ywij*) and *seki*, "tracks" (*tsjek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
