## You'll want to know: l'alluce
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C481-GAMBA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'alluce** — "big toe".

> Ho sbattuto l'alluce contro il letto.
>
> *I stubbed my big toe on the bed.*

The other toes are *le dita dei piedi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alluce* is usually traced to Latin *hallux*, a later form of *allex* "the big toe" (Lewis & Short, which also lists *hallus*).
