## You'll want to know: жилет
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C426-VESLO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**жилет** (*zhilét*) — "a waistcoat; a vest (спасательный жилет: a life jacket)".

> Всем дали спасательные жилеты перед прогулкой на лодке.
>
> *Vsem dáli spasátelnyye zhiléty péred progúlkoy na lódke.*
>
> Everyone was given a life jacket before the boat trip.

Masculine. *Спасательный жилет* — a life jacket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*жилет* was borrowed from French *gilet* 'a waistcoat' (EtymDB), a word EtymDB traces further to Arabic.
