## You'll want to know: দাদা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C481-MA481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**দাদা** — *dādā* — "an elder brother".

> আমার দাদা শহরে চাকরি করেন।
>
> *āmār dādā shôhôre chākri kôren.*
>
> My elder brother has a job in the city.

ভাই (taught) is a brother in general, often the younger; দাদা is the elder, and the word you call him by.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দাদা** (*dādā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as both a grandfather and an elder brother, under a reconstructed nursery word (CDIAL 6261).
