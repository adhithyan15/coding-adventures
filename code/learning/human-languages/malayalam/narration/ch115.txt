Malayalam, chapter 115: Not Feeling Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ജലദോഷം (jaladōṣaṁ) — a cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a cough, then the Malayalam for a fever.

You'll want to know: ജലദോഷം.
ജലദോഷം (jaladōṣaṁ) — "a cold", the kind with a runny nose.
എനിക്ക് ജലദോഷമുണ്ട് (enikkŭ jaladōṣamuṇṭŭ) — "I have a cold". The person sits in എനിക്ക്, "to me", and ഉണ്ട് says "there is".

What you've built.
An illness, said with to-me and there-is.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: jaladōṣaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jaladōṣaṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say enikkŭ jaladōṣamuṇṭŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for a cough, then the Malayalam for a fever, then say jaladōṣaṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ജലദോഷം (jaladōṣaṁ) mean? ("A cold".) Say it once more.
