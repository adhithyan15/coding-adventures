## You'll want to know: ਜੁੱਟਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C364-JOB364-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੁੱਟਣਾ** — *juṭṭṇā* — "to get down to work; to set to".

> ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਜੇ ਕੰਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੁੱਟਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ savere chhe vaje kamm vich juṭṭdā hāṁ.*
>
> I get down to work at six in the morning.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੁੱਟਿਆ, *maiṁ kamm vich juṭṭiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੁੱਟਣਾ** (*juṭṭṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*yuṭati*, "is joined"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *juṛnā*, "to be joined", and Lahnda *juṭaṇ*, "to be joined, be engaged in", under it (CDIAL 10496).
