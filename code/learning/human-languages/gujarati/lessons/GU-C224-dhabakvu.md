# ધબકવું (dhabakvũ) — to throb, to beat

## You'll want to know: ધબકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C224-DOCTOR224-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ધબકવું** — *dhabakvũ* — "to throb, to beat".

> **શું તેનું હૃદય ધબકે છે?** — *śũ tenũ hṛday dhabke che?* — "Is his heart beating?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ધબકવું** (*dhabakvũ*) is not settled. It is probably imitative of the beat, like *dhaṛakvũ*, "to throb", which CDIAL lists under *\*dhaḍ*, "throb, beat" (CDIAL 6711).
