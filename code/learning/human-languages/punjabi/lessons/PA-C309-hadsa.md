## You'll want to know: ਹਾਦਸਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C309-NEWS309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾਦਸਾ** — *hādsā* — "an accident".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਸੜਕ ਉੱਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਹਾਦਸਾ ਹੋਇਆ।
>
> *kall shām nū̃ saṛak utte ikk hādsā hoiā.*
>
> There was an accident on the road yesterday evening.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾਦਸਾ** (*hādsā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ḥāditha*, "an event".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
