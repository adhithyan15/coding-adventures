## You'll want to know: el rasgo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C721-CARACTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el rasgo** — "the trait, the feature".

> La paciencia es su mejor rasgo.
>
> *Patience is her best trait.*

*Los rasgos* are also facial features: *rasgos finos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rasgo* is built on *rasgar* "to tear", which is usually traced to Latin *resecāre* "to cut off", from *secāre* "to cut". A *rasgo* was first a stroke of the pen, then a feature.
