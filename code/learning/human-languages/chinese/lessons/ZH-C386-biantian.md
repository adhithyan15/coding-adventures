## You'll want to know: 变天
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C386-CHANGE386-05]; assesses=[] -->

**变天** — *biàntiān* — "to turn bad (of the weather)".

> **要变天了你最好早回家** *yào biàntiān le nǐ zuìhǎo zǎo huí jiā* — The weather's about to turn; you'd better get home early.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**变天** (*biàntiān*) is built from 变 'to change' and 天 'the sky', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjenH* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
