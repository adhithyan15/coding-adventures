## You'll want to know: تربية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C398-TALIM398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تربية** — *tarbiya* — "upbringing".

> تهتم أمي بتربية أطفالها.
>
> *tahtammu ummī bi-tarbiyat aṭfālihā.*
>
> My mother takes care over bringing up her children.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تربية** (*tarbiya*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-b-w*, "to grow, to increase" (Lane). *Tarbiya* is the Form II verbal noun, making a child grow: upbringing.
