## You'll want to know: ひがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C262-JIKO262-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ひがい** — *higai* — "damage; harm suffered".

> **ひがいは ばしょに よります。** — *higai wa basho ni yorimasu* — "How much harm is done depends on the place."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひがい** (*higai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hi*, "incur" (Middle Chinese *bjeX*) and *gai*, "harm" (*hajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
