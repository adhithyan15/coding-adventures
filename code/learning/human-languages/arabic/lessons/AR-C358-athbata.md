## You'll want to know: أثبت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C358-HUJJA358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أثبت** — *athbata* — "to prove".

> أثبت المحامي أن الرجل كان في المنزل.
>
> *athbata al-muḥāmī anna ar-rajul kāna fī al-manzil.*
>
> The lawyer proved that the man had been at home.

Present: يثبت, *yuthbit*.

Followed by أن: أثبت أن …, proved that ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أثبت** (*athbata*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-b-t*, "to be firm, to stand fast" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make firm: to establish a thing, to prove it.
