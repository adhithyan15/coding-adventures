## You'll want to know: प्रवालम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C418-JALA418-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवालम्** — *pravālam* — "coral".

> समुद्रे रक्तं प्रवालं भवति।
>
> *samudre raktaṃ pravālaṃ bhavati.*
>
> Red coral grows in the sea.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवालम्** (*pravālam*) probably comes from the root √val, "to turn", as MW suggests: MW gives *pravāla* as a young shoot and as coral (MW).
