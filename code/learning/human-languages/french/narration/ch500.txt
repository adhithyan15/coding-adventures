French, chapter 500: Cartes et paris.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la belote — belote (a card game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for chessboard, then the French for to cheat.

You'll want to know: la belote.
la belote — "belote (a card game)".
Le dimanche, mes oncles jouent à la belote. On Sundays my uncles play belote.
A very popular card game in France, played in pairs with 32 cards.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of la belote is not settled; the game took its name in France in the early twentieth century.

Lesson 5 of 5.
le hasard — chance, luck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to bet, then the French for bet.

You'll want to know: le hasard.
le hasard — "chance, luck".
Les jeux de hasard sont interdits aux mineurs. Games of chance are forbidden to minors.
Par hasard — by chance. The h is aspirated: le hasard.

The word, taken apart.
le hasard comes from Arabic az-zahr "the die", through Spanish azar (EtymDB): first a game of dice, then chance itself.
