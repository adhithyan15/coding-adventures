## You'll want to know: ദരിദ്രൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C427-DARIDRYAM427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ദരിദ്രൻ** — *daridran* — "a poor man".

> ആ ദരിദ്രന് ഗ്രാമം ഒരു വീട് കൊടുത്തു.
>
> *ā daridranŭ grāmaṁ oru vīṭŭ koṭuttu.*
>
> The village gave that poor man a house.

Plural: ദരിദ്രർ, *daridrar*.

Plural: ദരിദ്രർ, the poor. A poor woman is ദരിദ്ര.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ദരിദ്രൻ** (*daridran*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *daridra*, "roving", and later "poor" (CDIAL 6195), with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ.
