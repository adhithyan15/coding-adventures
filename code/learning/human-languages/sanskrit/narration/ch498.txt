Sanskrit, chapter 498: Brothers and Sisters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अग्रजः (agrajaḥ) — an elder brother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for offers into the fire, then the Sanskrit for good fortune.

You'll want to know: अग्रजः.
अग्रजः — agrajaḥ — "an elder brother".
मम अग्रजः नगरे कार्यं करोति। mama agrajaḥ nagare kāryaṃ karoti. My elder brother works in the city.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अग्रजः (agrajaḥ) is built from agra, "first, front", and ja, "born", from the root √jan; MW gives "the first-born, an elder brother" (MW).

The word, taken apart.
ज्येष्ठः (jyeṣṭhaḥ) MW gives it as a superlative (Pāṇini 5.3.61), "pre-eminent, greatest, eldest", without naming a root, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
यमलौ (yamalau) — twins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for eldest, then the Sanskrit for youngest.

You'll want to know: यमलौ.
यमलौ — yamalau — "twins".
यमलौ बालौ सह क्रीडतः। yamalau bālau saha krīḍataḥ. The twin boys play together.
A masculine noun, used in the dual.

The word, taken apart.
यमलौ (yamalau) is built on yama, "a twin"; MW gives "twin, paired" and, in the dual, "twins, a pair" (MW).
