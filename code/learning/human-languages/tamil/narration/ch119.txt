Tamil, chapter 119: Key, Lock, Towel, Pillow, Blanket.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சாவி (cāvi) — a key.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a cup, then the Tamil for a spoon.
