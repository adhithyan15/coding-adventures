Gujarati, chapter 301: A Building on Fire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઇમારત (imārat) — a large building.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to set alight, then the Gujarati for to be scalded.

You'll want to know: ઇમારત.
ઇમારત — imārat — "a large building".
ગઈકાલે રાત્રે શહેરની એક જૂની ઇમારતમાં આગ લાગી. gaīkāle rātre śahernī ek jūnī imāratmā̃ āg lāgī. Last night a fire broke out in an old building in the city.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઇમારત (imārat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿimāra, "building, construction". It is a large building, where મકાન is any building or house.

You'll want to know: ગૂંગળાવું.
ગૂંગળાવું — gū̃gḷāvũ — "to choke (for want of air)".
ધુમાડામાં ઘણા લોકો ગૂંગળાઈ ગયા. dhumāḍāmā̃ ghaṇā loko gū̃gḷāī gayā. Many people choked in the smoke.
Past (he …): તે માણસ ગૂંગળાઈ ગયો, te māṇas gū̃gḷāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
ગૂંગળાવું (gū̃gḷāvũ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. Smoke and a closed room make people ગૂંગળાઈ જવું: they cannot get their breath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a ladder, then the Gujarati for to choke.

You'll want to know: ફાટવું.
ફાટવું — phāṭvũ — "to burst; to split".
ગરમીથી દીવાલ ફાટી ગઈ. garmīthī dīvāl phāṭī gaī. The heat split the wall.
Past (it …): તે કપડું ફાટી ગયું, te kapḍũ phāṭī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
ફાટવું (phāṭvũ) belongs with Sanskrit sphāṭayati, "causes to split", where CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be split" (CDIAL 13825). Cloth tears and walls crack with it: ફાટી ગયું.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ધડાકો (dhaḍāko) — a bang; a blast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to choke, then the Gujarati for to burst.

You'll want to know: ધડાકો.
ધડાકો — dhaḍāko — "a bang; a blast".
અચાનક મોટો ધડાકો થયો. acānak moṭo dhaḍāko thayo. Suddenly there was a loud bang.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધડાકો (dhaḍāko) comes from dhaḍ, "throb, beat", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, glossed "a quick push" (CDIAL 6711); the sense of a loud bang, the commonest one today, is not in that gloss. It happens with થવું: ધડાકો થયો.
