Malayalam, chapter 241: Regarding, Although, And yet, However, If so.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സംബന്ധിച്ച് (sambandhiccŭ) — regarding, about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to here, then the Malayalam for a side.

You'll want to know: സംബന്ധിച്ച്.
സംബന്ധിച്ച് — sambandhiccŭ — "regarding, about".

The word, taken apart.
സംബന്ധിച്ച് (sambandhiccŭ) is a Malayalam verb form, "having connected", built on Sanskrit sambandha, "connection", from the root bandh, "to bind".

Lesson 2 of 5.
എങ്കിലും (eṅkiluṁ) — although, even so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a side, then the Malayalam for regarding.

You'll want to know: എങ്കിലും.
എങ്കിലും — eṅkiluṁ — "although, even so".

The word, taken apart.
എങ്കിലും (eṅkiluṁ) is native Dravidian: a conditional form of the verb "to say" (DEDR 868, with Tamil eṉ), with -uṁ, "even": "even if one says so".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for although, then the Malayalam for and yet.

You'll want to know: എന്നിരുന്നാലും.
എന്നിരുന്നാലും — ennirunnāluṁ — "however, nevertheless".

The word, taken apart.
എന്നിരുന്നാലും (ennirunnāluṁ) is native Dravidian, "even if it were so": the verb "to say" (DEDR 868), the verb "to be" (DEDR 480, with Tamil iru), the conditional -āl, and -uṁ, "even".
