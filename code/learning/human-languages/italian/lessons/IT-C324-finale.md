## You'll want to know: il finale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C324-MITO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il finale** — "the ending (of a story or film)".

> Il finale del film mi ha fatto piangere.
>
> *The ending of the film made me cry.*

*Il lieto fine* is a happy ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*finale* is inherited from Late Latin *fīnālis* (EtymDB), built on *fīnis* "end".
