# (el aviso de la impresora) — read it, then say what you will do

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-02, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **la impresora** and **imprimir**. The notice below is about
the first and stops you doing the second.

## Reading: the notice, and the message
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-01, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-02] -->

Taped to the machine:

> **AVISO**
>
> La **impresora** de la sala 2 está **fuera de servicio**.
>
> La de la tercera planta **funciona**.

And a message from a colleague:

> No me puedo **conectar** a internet desde mi ordenador. ¿Puedes **imprimir**
> tú el documento? Si no, te **devuelvo** la **llamada** en diez minutos.

## Grammar Lens: what each text asks of you
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-03, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-04] -->

The notice reports a state. The message asks for something and offers a way
out if the answer is no. They want different replies.

| the notice | the message |
|---|---|
| *está fuera de servicio* | *¿puedes imprimir tú el documento?* |

Two details in the message repay a second look.

**La de la tercera planta.** The notice drops *impresora* and leaves *la*
standing for it. Spanish does this freely once the noun is on the table, and a
notice will do it to save a line.

**¿Puedes imprimir tú?** The *tú* is not needed for the grammar — the verb
already says who. It is there to point: *you*, rather than me, because I
cannot. A pronoun that could be dropped and is not is doing work.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-01, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-02, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-03, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say which printer is broken and which one works, using *la* on its
own for the second the way the notice does.

[PAUSE 3s] Now answer the message: say you will print the document, and say
why your colleague could not — the connection, not the machine.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-01, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-02, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-03, ES-LEX-C469-OFICINA-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What is wrong with the machine? (**Está fuera de servicio**.) What
is wrong for your colleague? (**No se puede conectar**.) What will you do?
(**Imprimir el documento**.)
