Portuguese, chapter 146: To cough, To tremble, To vote, To welcome, To admire.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
tossir — to cough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to interrupt, then the Portuguese for to tie.

You'll want to know: tossir.
tossir — "to cough".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tossir]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tossir, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say tossir]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for to interrupt, then the Portuguese for to tie, then say tossir again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does tossir mean? ("To cough".) Say it once more.
