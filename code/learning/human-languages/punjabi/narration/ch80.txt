Punjabi, chapter 80: Bad, Long, Hot, Slowly, Fast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਬੁਰਾ (burā) — bad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for new, then the Punjabi for old.

You'll want to know: ਬੁਰਾ.
ਬੁਰਾ — burā — "bad".

The word, taken apart.
ਬੁਰਾ (burā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bura, "defective", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9289). CDIAL lists Punjabi burā, "wicked".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਲੰਮਾ (lammā) — long, tall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for old, then the Punjabi for bad.

You'll want to know: ਲੰਮਾ.
ਲੰਮਾ — lammā — "long, tall".

The word, taken apart.
ਲੰਮਾ (lammā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from lamba, "pendent" (CDIAL 10951). The Sanskrit word already meant "long" in compounds.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਗਰਮ (garam) — hot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for bad, then the Punjabi for long.

You'll want to know: ਗਰਮ.
ਗਰਮ — garam — "hot".

The word, taken apart.
ਗਰਮ (garam) is a loan, usually traced to Persian garm, "warm, hot". Punjabi breaks up the -rm with a vowel.

ਹੌਲੀ (haulī) goes with haulā, "light, gentle", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit laghu, "light", with its sounds swapped (CDIAL 10896).
