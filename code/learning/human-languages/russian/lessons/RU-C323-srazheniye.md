## You'll want to know: сражение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C323-VOYNA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**сражение** (*srazhéniye*) — "a pitched battle, a battle".

> После сражения солдаты отдыхали.
>
> *Pósle srazhéniya soldáty otdykháli.*
>
> After the battle the soldiers rested.

Neuter. A little more formal than *битва*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сражение* is built on *сразиться* "to fight", from *с-* and *разить* "to strike" (EtymDB glosses *разить* "to strike"); the *з* turns to *ж* in *сражать*, as in *возить*, *вожу*.
