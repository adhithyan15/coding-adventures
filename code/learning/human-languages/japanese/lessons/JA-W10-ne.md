# ね — finish the polite request

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ONEGAI-SHIMASU, JA-SCRIPT-O-01, JA-SCRIPT-KA-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01, JA-SCRIPT-I-01, JA-SCRIPT-MA-01, JA-SCRIPT-SU-01] -->

[PAUSE 18s] Write **お**, then known **が, い, し, ま, す**. Say the whole
request ending once. One written mora remains between **お** and **が**.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-NE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ね** — *ne*

Two strokes. First a short vertical. Second, cross it from the upper right,
hook left, sweep down, and finish with a small loop at the lower right.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-NE-01, JA-LEX-ONEGAI-SHIMASU] -->

1. Copy **ね** once.
2. Hide it and write it from *ne*.
3. Assemble **お | ね | が | い | し | ま | す**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-NE-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"JA-W10-ne-write","kind":"text","assesses":["JA-SCRIPT-NE-01"],"prompt":"Write the hiragana sign for ne.","answer":"ね","accepted":["hiragana ne"],"feedback":{"correct":"Right: ね completes the readable request ending.","incorrect":"Write ね: short vertical, then the crossing sweep and loop."},"response_seconds":10} -->
