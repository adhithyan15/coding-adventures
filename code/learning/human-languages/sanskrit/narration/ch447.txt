7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
केदारः (kedāraḥ) — a flooded field; a paddy field.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for kusha grass, then the Sanskrit for a young shoot.

You'll want to know: केदारः.
केदारः — kedāraḥ — "a flooded field; a paddy field".
केदारेषु जलम् अस्ति। kedāreṣu jalam asti. There is water in the flooded fields.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
केदारः (kedāraḥ) is the word MW gives for a field or meadow, especially one under water; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 3 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

कृषकः उर्वरायां धान्यं वपति। kṛṣakaḥ urvarāyāṃ dhānyaṃ vapati. The farmer sows grain in the fertile land.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उर्वरा (urvarā) is probably connected, as MW suggests, with uru, "wide", so its origin is uncertain; MW gives urvarā as fertile soil, a field yielding crops (MW).

Lesson 4 of 7.
लुनाति (lunāti) — reaps a crop; cuts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a crop, then the Sanskrit for fertile land.

You'll want to know: लुनाति.
लुनाति — lunāti — "reaps a crop; cuts".
कृषकः दात्रेण सस्यं लुनाति। kṛṣakaḥ dātreṇa sasyaṃ lunāti. The farmer reaps the crop with a sickle.
Past (he …): सः सस्यं लूनवान्, saḥ sasyaṃ lūnavān.

The word, taken apart.
लुनाति (lunāti) comes from the root √lū, "to cut"; MW gives lunāti as to cut, sever, reap, with the participle lūna, "reaped"; MW compares Greek luō, "to loose" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 7.
अवचिनोति (avacinoti) — picks fruit; plucks flowers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for fertile land, then the Sanskrit for reaps a crop.

You'll want to know: अवचिनोति.
अवचिनोति — avacinoti — "picks fruit; plucks flowers".
बालिका वृक्षात् फलानि अवचिनोति। bālikā vṛkṣāt phalāni avacinoti. The girl picks fruit from the tree.
Past (she …): सा फलानि अवचितवती, sā phalāni avacitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
अवचिनोति (avacinoti) is built from ava, "down", and the root √ci, "to gather"; MW gives ava-ci as to gather fruit from a tree, with the participle avacita, "gathered" (MW).
