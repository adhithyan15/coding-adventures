## You'll want to know: つかまえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C271-TSUKAMARU271-05]; assesses=[] -->

**つかまえる** — *tsukamaeru* — "to catch; to arrest".

> **けいさつは きのう はんにんを つかまえました。** — *keisatsu wa kinō hannin o tsukamaemashita* — "The police caught the culprit yesterday."

Polite **つかまえます**, *tsukamaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **けいさつが はんにんを つかまえました。**, *keisatsu ga hannin o tsukamaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つかまえる** (*tsukamaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the transitive partner of *tsukamaru*, "to be caught".
