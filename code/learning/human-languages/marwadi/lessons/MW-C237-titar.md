## You'll want to know: तीतर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C237-FIELD237-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तीतर** — *tītar* — "a partridge".

> खेत मांय तीतर है।
>
> *khet mā̃y tītar hai.*
>
> There's a partridge in the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तीतर** (*tītar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tittira*, "a partridge" (CDIAL 5809, which lists Marwari *tītar*).
