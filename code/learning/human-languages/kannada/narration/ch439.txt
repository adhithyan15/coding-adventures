Kannada, chapter 439: Crossing a Border.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವೀಸಾ (vīsā) — a visa.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for air travel, then the Kannada for a helicopter.

You'll want to know: ವೀಸಾ.
ವೀಸಾ — vīsā — "a visa".
ವಿದೇಶಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗಲು ಪಾಸ್ಪೋರ್ಟ್ ಮತ್ತು ವೀಸಾ ಬೇಕು. vidēśakke hōgalu pāspōrṭ mattu vīsā bēku. To go abroad you need a passport and a visa.
ವೀಸಾ (vīsā) ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು, the visa came through.

The word, taken apart.
ವೀಸಾ (vīsā) is a loan from English visa.

ಕಸ್ಟಮ್ಸ್ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳು ನಮ್ಮ ಚೀಲಗಳನ್ನು ತೆರೆದರು. kasṭams adhikārigaḷu namma cīlagaḷannu teredaru. The customs officers opened our bags.

ಕಸ್ಟಮ್ಸ್ (kasṭams) is a loan from English customs, the duties charged on goods and the office that collects them.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸುಂಕ (suṅka) — a customs duty; a toll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a visa, then the Kannada for customs.

You'll want to know: ಸುಂಕ.
ಸುಂಕ — suṅka — "a customs duty; a toll".
ಚಿನ್ನದ ಆಭರಣಗಳಿಗೆ ಸುಂಕ ಕಟ್ಟಬೇಕು. cinnada ābharaṇagaḷige suṅka kaṭṭabēku. Duty has to be paid on gold jewellery.
ಸುಂಕ (suṅka) ಕಟ್ಟು, to pay duty. ಶುಲ್ಕ, a fee, is the learned form of the same Sanskrit word.

The word, taken apart.
ಸುಂಕ (suṅka) comes from Sanskrit śulka, "price, toll", through a Prakrit form suṅka (CDIAL 12543). ಶುಲ್ಕ, a fee, is the same word borrowed again in its learned shape.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for customs, then the Kannada for a customs duty.

You'll want to know: ವಿನಿಮಯ ಮಾಡು.
ವಿನಿಮಯ ಮಾಡು — vinimaya māḍu — "to exchange money; to swap".
ವಿಮಾನ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ಡಾಲರನ್ನು ರೂಪಾಯಿಗೆ ವಿನಿಮಯ ಮಾಡಿದೆ. vimāna nildāṇadalli nānu ḍālarannu rūpāyige vinimaya māḍide. At the airport I changed dollars into rupees.
Past (I …): ವಿನಿಮಯ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, vinimaya māḍide.
ವಿನಿಮಯ ದರ, an exchange rate; what you get takes -ಗೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿನಿಮಯ ಮಾಡು (vinimaya māḍu) joins Sanskrit vinimaya, "exchange, barter" (Monier-Williams), and the native verb ಮಾಡು, to do.
