## You'll want to know: zǒusàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C456-LOST456-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒusàn* — "to get separated (in a crowd)".

> *wǒ hé péngyou zài huǒchēzhàn zǒusàn le* — My friend and I lost each other at the station.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒusàn* is built from 走 'to walk' and *sàn* 'to scatter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsuwX* and *sanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to run' and the second 'to break apart'.
