## You'll want to know: मरहम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C489-MARHAM489-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मरहम** — *marham* — "an ointment".

> मरहम से घाव को जल्दी आराम मिला।
>
> *marham se ghāv ko jaldī ārām milā.*
>
> The ointment soon brought relief to the wound.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मरहम** (*marham*) is usually traced to Arabic *marham*, "ointment", which reached Hindi through Persian.
