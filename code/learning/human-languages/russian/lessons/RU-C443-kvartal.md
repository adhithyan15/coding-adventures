## You'll want to know: квартал
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C443-BRODIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**квартал** (*kvartál*) — "a block (in a city); a quarter (of a year)".

> Аптека в соседнем квартале.
>
> *Aptéka v sosédnem kvartále.*
>
> The chemist's is in the next block.

Masculine. *Первый квартал* — the first quarter of the year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*квартал* was borrowed from German *Quartal* (EtymDB), a word built on Latin *quartus* 'fourth' (Lewis & Short).
