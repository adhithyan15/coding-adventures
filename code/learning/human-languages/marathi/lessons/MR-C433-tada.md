## You'll want to know: तडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C433-TADA433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तडा** — *taḍā* — "a split; a crack".

> भूकंपानंतर भिंतीला तडा गेला.
>
> *bhūkampānantar bhintīlā taḍā gelā.*
>
> After the earthquake the wall cracked.

Masculine.

तडा जाणे, to crack: भिंतीला तडा गेला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तडा** (*taḍā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *taḍā*, "a fissure", beside *taḍṇẽ*, "to crack, split", under *\*traṭ*, "crackle" (CDIAL 5988).
