## You'll want to know: ಸಂಸತ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C393-SAMSATTU393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಸತ್ತು** — *saṃsattu* — "a parliament".

> ಹೊಸ ನಿಯಮದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಸಂಸತ್ತಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಚರ್ಚೆ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *hosa niyamada bagge saṃsattinalli carce naḍeyitu.*
>
> There was a debate in parliament on the new rule.

ಸಂಸದ, a member of parliament.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಸತ್ತು** (*saṃsattu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃsad*, "a sitting together, an assembly" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada -*u* after the final consonant.
