## You'll want to know: zhǎyǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C471-EYES471-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǎyǎn* — "to blink; to wink".

> *tā duì wǒ zhǎyǎn* — He winked at me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: zhǎ is said with a rising tone before yǎn, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǎyǎn* is built from *zhǎ* and 眼 'eye', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsreap* and *ngeanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the eye moving'.
