Persian, chapter 400: Getting into university.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دانشکده (dâneshkade) — a faculty, a college.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to fail, then the Persian for a grade average.

You'll want to know: دانشکده.
دانشکده (dâneshkade) — "a faculty, a college".
دانشکده پزشکی نزدیک بیمارستان است. dâneshkade-ye pezeshki nazdik-e bimârestân ast. The medical faculty is near the hospital.
Dânesh "knowledge" + kade "house". A university has several dâneshkade.

The word, taken apart.
دانشکده (dâneshkade) is a native Persian compound built from dânesh "knowledge", from dânestan "to know", + kade "house, dwelling". A faculty is a house of knowledge.

Lesson 2 of 5.
شهریه (shahriye) — tuition fees.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a grade average, then the Persian for a faculty.

You'll want to know: شهریه.
شهریه (shahriye) — "tuition fees".
شهریه این دانشگاه گران است. shahriye-ye in dâneshgâh gerân ast. The fees at this university are high.
Also the monthly fee for a private class or a kindergarten.

The word, taken apart.
شهریه (shahriye) is usually traced to Arabic shahriyya "monthly", from Arabic shahr "month" (a different word from Persian shahr "city"): a fee once paid by the month.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پذیرفته شدن (pazirofte shodan) — to be admitted, to be accepted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a faculty, then the Persian for tuition fees.

You'll want to know: پذیرفته شدن.
پذیرفته شدن (pazirofte shodan) — "to be admitted, to be accepted".
او در رشته پزشکی پذیرفته شد. u dar reshte-ye pezeshki pazirofte shod. She was admitted to study medicine.
Pazirofte, the participle of paziroftan "to accept" (present stem pazir-), + shodan.

The word, taken apart.
پذیرفته شدن (pazirofte shodan) is native Persian, built from pazirofte, the participle of paziroftan "to accept", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian, and shodan "to become", which makes it passive.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بورسیه (bursiye) — a scholarship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for tuition fees, then the Persian for to be admitted.

You'll want to know: بورسیه.
بورسیه (bursiye) — "a scholarship".
او با بورسیه به خارج رفت. u bâ bursiye be khârej raft. She went abroad on a scholarship.
Bursiye gereftan — to win a scholarship.

The word, taken apart.
بورسیه (bursiye) is usually traced to French bourse "purse; grant", from Late Latin bursa "purse", with the ending -iye: money put in a student's purse.

Lesson 5 of 5.
واحد (vâhed) — a course credit; a unit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be admitted, then the Persian for a scholarship.

You'll want to know: واحد.
واحد (vâhed) — "a course credit; a unit".
این ترم بیست واحد گرفتم. in term bist vâhed gereftam. This term I took twenty credits.
Also a unit of measure, and a flat in a building. Its first meaning is "one".

The word, taken apart.
واحد (vâhed) is usually traced to Arabic wāḥid "one": a unit is one of something.
