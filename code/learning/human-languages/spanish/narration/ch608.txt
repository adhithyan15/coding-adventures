Spanish, chapter 608: Sube y baja.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
aumentar — to increase, to rise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the resource, then the Spanish for the measure.

You'll want to know: aumentar.
aumentar — "to increase, to rise".
Los precios aumentan cada año. Prices increase every year.
Used for numbers that grow; subir is more everyday.

The word, taken apart.
aumentar is inherited from Late Latin augmentare, built on augere 'to increase' (EtymDB).
