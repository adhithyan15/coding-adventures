# భావించు (bhāviñcu) — to regard as; to consider

## You'll want to know: భావించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C382-ALOCANA382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**భావించు** — *bhāviñcu* — "to regard as; to consider".

> నేను ఆ పనిని నా బాధ్యతగా భావించాను.
>
> *nēnu ā panini nā bādhyatagā bhāviñcānu.*
>
> I regarded that work as my responsibility.

Past (I …): భావించాను, *bhāviñcānu*.

The thing takes -ని and what you take it to be takes -గా: దాన్ని బాధ్యతగా భావించు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**భావించు** (*bhāviñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *bhāvana*, "causing to be", and as a noun "imagination" (CDIAL 9476), from the root *bhū*, "to be", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
