## You'll want to know: محکمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C343-MAHKAMA343-01]; assesses=[] -->

**محکمہ** — *mahkamā* — "a government department".

> وہ پولیس کے محکمے میں کام کرتا ہے۔
>
> *voh polīs ke mahkame meṅ kām kartā hai.*
>
> He works in the police department.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محکمہ** (*mahkamā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maḥkama*, "a court"; Urdu uses it for a department of government, a sense our sources do not trace.
