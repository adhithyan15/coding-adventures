## You'll want to know: સિદ્ધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C364-SHODH364-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સિદ્ધ** — *siddh* — "proven".

> આ પ્રયોગથી તેની વાત સિદ્ધ થઈ.
>
> *ā prayogthī tenī vāt siddh thaī.*
>
> This experiment proved what he said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સિદ્ધ** (*siddh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *siddha*, "accomplished, established" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
