Gujarati, chapter 452: A Flash of Temper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ખીજાવું (khījāvũ) — to get cross; to lose one's temper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for love, then the Gujarati for to clasp.

You'll want to know: ખીજાવું.
ખીજાવું — khījāvũ — "to get cross; to lose one's temper".
દાદા જલદી ખીજાઈ જાય છે. dādā jaldī khījāī jāy che. Grandfather gets cross quickly.
Past (he …): તે ખીજાઈ ગયો, te khījāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
ખીજાવું (khījāvũ) is the -āvũ form of the verb that CDIAL lists as Gujarati khījvũ, "to take offence", under Sanskrit kṣīyatē, "is worn away, is injured" (CDIAL 3695).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ક્રોધ (krodh) — wrath; fierce anger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to clasp, then the Gujarati for to get cross.

You'll want to know: ક્રોધ.
ક્રોધ — krodh — "wrath; fierce anger".
ક્રોધમાં તેણે ખોટો જવાબ આપ્યો. krodhmā̃ teṇe khoṭo javāb āpyo. In his anger he gave a false answer.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ક્રોધ (krodh) is a tatsama, Sanskrit krodha, "anger, wrath", as Monier-Williams records it; everyday Gujarati says ગુસ્સો (gusso).

Lesson 3 of 5.
મિજાજ (mijāj) — temper; mood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get cross, then the Gujarati for wrath.

You'll want to know: મિજાજ.
મિજાજ — mijāj — "temper; mood".
આજે સાહેબનો મિજાજ ખરાબ છે. āje sāhebno mijāj kharāb che. The boss is in a bad mood today.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મિજાજ (mijāj) is usually traced to Arabic mizāj, "temperament, mixture", which reached Gujarati through Persian.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ઉકળવું.
ઉકળવું — ukaḷvũ — "to seethe; to boil".
તેનો જવાબ સાંભળીને અરુણ ગુસ્સાથી ઉકળી ગયો. teno javāb sā̃bhḷīne Aruṇ gussāthī ukḷī gayo. Hearing his answer, Arun seethed with anger.
Past (it …): તે પાણી ઉકળી ગયું, te pāṇī ukḷī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઉકળવું (ukaḷvũ) goes back to a reconstructed utkalati, "goes out or up"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to boil", beside ઉકાળવું (ukāḷvũ), "to cause to boil" (CDIAL 1716).
