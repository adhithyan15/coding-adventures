## You'll want to know: семинар
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C381-SESSIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**семинар** (*seminár*) — "a seminar, a discussion class".

> На семинаре мы обсуждали статью.
>
> *Na semináre my obsuzhdáli statyú.*
>
> At the seminar we discussed an article.

Masculine. A class where students talk and answer, as against a *лекция*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**семинар** is usually traced, through German *Seminar*, to Latin *sēminārium* "a seed plot, a nursery" (Lewis & Short), built on *sēmen* "seed": a place where young minds are grown.
