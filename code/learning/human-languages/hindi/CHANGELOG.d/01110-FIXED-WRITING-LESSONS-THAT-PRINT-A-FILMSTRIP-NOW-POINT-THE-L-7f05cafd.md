## Fixed — writing lessons that print a filmstrip now point the learner at it

14 writing lessons print a stroke-order filmstrip in the book, but
their prose still said the book gave no stroke order ("this book does not yet
tell you where to start", "copy what you see"). That was true when the lessons
were written and stopped being true when the cited stroke data arrived.

- **Rewritten:** the Writing block now says to follow the numbered strip —
  start where movement 1 begins and let each panel show the next movement —
  and that the strip is one attested order with its source printed beneath it,
  not the only one. Where the heading said "copy what you see", it now says
  "follow the numbered strip".
- **Lessons:** HI-S01-letter-ma, HI-S02-letter-na, HI-S05-sign-virama,
  HI-S07-letter-ta, HI-S08-letter-sa, HI-S112-vowel-sign-e,
  HI-S118-sign-candrabindu, HI-S119-letter-ssa, HI-S120-vowel-sign-u,
  HI-S122-letter-kha, HI-S140-nuqta, HI-S144-vowel-sign-vocalic-r,
  HI-S148-sign-anusvara, HI-S149-vowel-sign-uu.
- Lessons that print no filmstrip keep their "no source yet" wording.
- Narration and modality were regenerated. Every edited lesson stays under
  the 300-second duration budget, and no declared duration changed.
