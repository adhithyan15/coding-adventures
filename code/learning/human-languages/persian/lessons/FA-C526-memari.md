## You'll want to know: معماری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C526-ART-02]; assesses=[] -->

**معماری** (*me'mâri*) — "architecture".

> او در دانشگاه معماری میخواند.
>
> *u dar dâneshgâh me'mâri mikhânad.*
>
> She studies architecture at university.

An architect is *me'mâr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*me'mâri* comes from Arabic *miʿmārī* (EtymDB), built on *miʿmār*, 'builder', which Persian keeps as *me'mâr*, 'architect'.
