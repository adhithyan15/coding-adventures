## You'll want to know: संकल्पः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C383-SANKALPA383-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संकल्पः** — *saṅkalpaḥ* — "a resolve".

> यदि संकल्पः दृढः, तर्हि लक्ष्यं प्राप्नोति।
>
> *yadi saṅkalpaḥ dṛḍhaḥ, tarhi lakṣyaṃ prāpnoti.*
>
> If the resolve is firm, one reaches the goal.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकल्पः** (*saṅkalpaḥ*) is built on *sam* and the root √kḷp, "to be fit, to form"; MW gives *saṃ-kalpa* "will, volition, purpose, a definite intention" (MW).
