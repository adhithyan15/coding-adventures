Urdu, chapter 70: Small, New, Good, Bad, Long.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چھوٹا (chhoṭā) — small.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a bear, then the Urdu for big.
