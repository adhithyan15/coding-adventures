## You'll want to know: l'engagement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C373-DISPONIBLE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'engagement** — "the commitment".

> C'est un engagement important : je ne peux pas dire non.
>
> *It is an important commitment: I cannot say no.*

*Prendre un engagement* — to make a commitment. Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*engagement* is built from *engager* and *-ment* (EtymDB). *engager* rests on *gage* "a pledge", usually traced to a Frankish word.
