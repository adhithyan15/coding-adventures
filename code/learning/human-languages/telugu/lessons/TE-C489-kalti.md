## You'll want to know: కల్తీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C489-KALTI489-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కల్తీ** — *kaltī* — "adulteration".

> ఆ పాలలో కల్తీ ఉందని తేలింది.
>
> *ā pālalō kaltī undani tēlindi.*
>
> It turned out that the milk was adulterated.

కల్తీ చేయు, to adulterate; కల్తీ నూనె, adulterated oil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కల్తీ** (*kaltī*) is a Telugu word for adulteration whose origin is not settled.
