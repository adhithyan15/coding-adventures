Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 232: Old Earth, Smooth Stone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बूढो (būḍho) — elderly; old (of a person).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for round, then the Marwadi for loose.

You'll want to know: बूढो.
बूढो — būḍho — "elderly; old (of a person)".
दादा बूढो है। dādā būḍho hai. Grandfather is old.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited from a reconstructed buḍḍha, "old"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari būḍhaü, "old" (CDIAL 9271). जूनो is old for things; बूढो (būḍho) is old for people and animals.

Lesson 2 of 5.
माटी (māṭī) — soil; earth (the material).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for loose, then the Marwadi for elderly.

You'll want to know: माटी.
माटी — māṭī — "soil; earth (the material)".
खेत मांय माटी गीली है। khet mā̃y māṭī gīlī hai. The soil in the field is wet.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
माटी (māṭī) is inherited from Sanskrit mṛttikā, "earth, clay"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati māṭī. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 10286).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for elderly, then the Marwadi for soil.

चिकणो — chikṇo — "smooth; slippery".
भाटो चिकणो है। bhāṭo chikṇo hai. The stone is smooth.

The word, taken apart.
चिकणो (chikṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit cikkaṇa, "slippery, unctuous"; CDIAL lists Hindi ciknā, "oily, smooth", and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4782).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कांकरो (kā̃kro) — a pebble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for soil, then the Marwadi for smooth.

You'll want to know: कांकरो.
कांकरो — kā̃kro — "a pebble".
जूती मांय कांकरो है। jūtī mā̃y kā̃kro hai. There's a pebble in my shoe.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कांकरो (kā̃kro) is inherited from Sanskrit karkara, "a stone"; CDIAL lists Hindi kā̃kar, a nodule of limestone, and a Gujarati word for a pebble. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2820).

Lesson 5 of 5.
बोरड़ी (borṛī) — a jujube bush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for smooth, then the Marwadi for a pebble.

You'll want to know: बोरड़ी.
बोरड़ी — borṛī — "a jujube bush".
खेत कनै बोरड़ी है। khet kanai borṛī hai. There's a jujube bush near the field.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बोरड़ी (borṛī) is inherited from Sanskrit badara, "the fruit of the jujube tree"; CDIAL lists Gujarati borṛī, the jujube tree. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 9125).
