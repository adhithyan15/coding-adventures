## You'll want to know: لوری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C484-RAISE484-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لوری** — *lorī* — "a lullaby".

> ماں رات کو لوری گاتی ہے۔
>
> *māṅ rāt ko lorī gātī hai.*
>
> The mother sings a lullaby at night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لوری** (*lorī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lorī* under *lōḍa*, "tossing, swinging", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11136): the song of the rocking cradle.
