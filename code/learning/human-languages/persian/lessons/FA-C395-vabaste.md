## You'll want to know: وابسته
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C395-BASTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**وابسته** (*vâbaste*) — "dependent; attached".

> او هنوز از نظر مالی به پدرش وابسته است.
>
> *u hanuz az nazar-e mâli be pedarash vâbaste ast.*
>
> He still depends on his father for money.

*Vâbaste be* — dependent on. Also emotionally attached to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وابسته** is native Persian, built from the prefix *vâ-* + *baste* "bound", the participle of *bastan* "to bind" (EtymDB traces it to PIE *bʰendʰ-*). To depend is to be tied to something.
