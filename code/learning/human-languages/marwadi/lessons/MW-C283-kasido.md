## You'll want to know: कसीदो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C283-MOTI283-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कसीदो** — *kasīdo* — "embroidery".

> यो कसीदो सुंदर है।
>
> *yo kasīdo sundar hai.*
>
> This embroidery is beautiful.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसीदो** (*kasīdo*) is usually traced to Persian *kashīda*, "drawn out", used of embroidered work; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.
