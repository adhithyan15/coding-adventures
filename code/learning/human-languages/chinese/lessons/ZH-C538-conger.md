# cóng'ér — and thereby; and in this way

## You'll want to know: cóng'ér
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C538-CAUSE538-02]; assesses=[] -->

*cóng'ér* — "and thereby; and in this way".

> *wǒmen gǎi le shíjiān cóng'ér jiějué le zhège wèntí* — We changed the time, and so solved the problem.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cóng'ér* is built from 从 'to follow' and *ér* 'and', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjowng* and *nyi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a grammar word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
