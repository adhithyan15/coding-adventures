## You'll want to know: da allora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C280-FINORA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**da allora** — "since then, ever since".

> Ci siamo conosciuti a scuola e da allora siamo amici.
>
> *We met at school and we have been friends ever since.*

*Da allora in poi* — from then on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allora* is inherited from Latin *ad illam hōram* "at that hour" (EtymDB); *da* "from" is usually traced to Latin *dē ab*.
