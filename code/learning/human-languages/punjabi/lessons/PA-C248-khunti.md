## You'll want to know: ਖੂੰਟੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C248-GHR248-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੂੰਟੀ** — *khūṇṭī* — "a wall peg".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਬਸਤਾ ਖੂੰਟੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā bastā khūṇṭī utte hai.*
>
> My school bag is on the peg.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੂੰਟੀ** (*khūṇṭī*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*khuṭṭa*, "peg, post", as a word taken from Hindi: CDIAL lists Punjabi *khū̃ṭī*, "stake, peg", as borrowed from Hindi *khū̃ṭī*, "small peg" (CDIAL 3893).
