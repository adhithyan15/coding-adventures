## You'll want to know: ولع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C540-NADI540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ولع** — *walaʿ* — "fondness, keen love".

> عند ابني ولع بالسيارات.
>
> *ʿinda ibnī walaʿ bi-s-sayyārāt.*
>
> My son is crazy about cars.

مولع بـ is keen on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ولع** (*walaʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-l-ʿ*, "to make eagerly desirous" (Lane). Lane gives Form IV awlaʿa as to make someone eagerly fond of a thing.
