## You'll want to know: कसौटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C434-SHUDDH434-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कसौटी** — *kasauṭī* — "a touchstone; a test".

> सुनार कसौटी पर सोना रगड़कर देखता है।
>
> *sunār kasauṭī par sonā ragaṛkar dekhtā hai.*
>
> The goldsmith tests gold by rubbing it on a touchstone.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसौटी** (*kasauṭī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kaṣapaṭṭikā*, "a touchstone" (CDIAL 2973), which lists Hindi *kasauṭī*; CDIAL suspects the Sanskrit spelling is itself a learned remaking of an older form. The goldsmith's dark stone also stands for any real test: समय की कसौटी, the test of time.
