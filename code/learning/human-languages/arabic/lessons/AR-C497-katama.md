## You'll want to know: كتم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C497-TAWATTUR497-05]; assesses=[] -->

**كتم** — *katama* — "to hold back (a feeling)".

> كتمت البنت ضحكتها في الصف.
>
> *katamat al-bint ḍaḥkatahā fī aṣ-ṣaff.*
>
> The girl held back her laugh in class.

Present: يكتم, *yaktum*.

It also means to keep a secret: كتم السر.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كتم** (*katama*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-t-m*, "to conceal, to keep secret" (Lane). Lane gives yaktumu as its present.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
