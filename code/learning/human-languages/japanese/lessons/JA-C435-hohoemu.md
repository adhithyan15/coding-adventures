## You'll want to know: ほほえむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C435-CONTENT435-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ほほえむ** — *hohoemu* — "to smile gently".

> **かのじょは すこし ほほえみました。** — *kanojo wa sukoshi hohoemimashita* — "She gave a little smile."

Polite **ほほえみます**, *hohoemimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **そぼが ほほえみました。**, *sobo ga hohoemimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほほえむ** (*hohoemu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
