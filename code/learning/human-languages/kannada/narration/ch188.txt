ಗೂಡು (gūḍu) — a nest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for shade, then the Kannada for darkness.

You'll want to know: ಗೂಡು.
ಗೂಡು — gūḍu — "a nest".

The word, taken apart.
ಗೂಡು (gūḍu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kūṭu (DEDR 1883). DEDR lists gūḍu itself, "nest". DEDR 1883 compares Sanskrit kūṭa.
