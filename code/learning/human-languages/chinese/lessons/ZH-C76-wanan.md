## You'll want to know: wǎn'ān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C76-GOODBYE76-04]; assesses=[] -->

*wǎn'ān* — "good night".

> *māma wǎn'ān* — Mum, good night.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wǎn'ān* is built from wǎn 'evening' and ān 'peace', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjonX* and *'an* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as 'calm, at rest'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
