## You'll want to know: मिस्त्री
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C506-KARIGAR506-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मिस्त्री** — *mistrī* — "a mechanic; a mason".

> हमारी गाड़ी बिगड़ गई, इसलिए हमने एक मिस्त्री को बुलाया।
>
> *Hamārī gāṛī bigaṛ gaī, isliye hamne ek mistrī ko bulāyā.*
>
> Our car broke down, so we called a mechanic.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिस्त्री** (*mistrī*) is usually traced to Portuguese *mestre*, "a master (of a trade)".
