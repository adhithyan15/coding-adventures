## You'll want to know: l'évier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C530-VAISSELLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'évier** — "(kitchen) sink".

> Les assiettes sales sont dans l'évier.
>
> *The dirty plates are in the sink.*

Masculine. A bathroom basin is *le lavabo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'évier* is inherited from Latin *aquārium* "a place for water" (EtymDB), built on *aqua* "water" (Lewis & Short).
