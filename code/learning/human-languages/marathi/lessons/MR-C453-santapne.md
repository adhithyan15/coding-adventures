## You'll want to know: संतापणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C453-TAPAT453-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संतापणे** — *santāpṇe* — "to be enraged; to fly into a rage".

> खोटं ऐकून वडील संतापले.
>
> *khoṭaṁ aikūn vaḍīl santāple.*
>
> Father flew into a rage when he heard the lie.

Past (he …): तो संतापला, *to santāplā*.

Stronger than रागावणे. The noun संताप, rage: त्याला संताप आला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संतापणे** (*santāpṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on संताप, "rage", a tatsama from Sanskrit *saṁtāpa*, "burning heat, affliction" (CDIAL 12885).
