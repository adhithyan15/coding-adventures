## You'll want to know: provocare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C349-CAUSA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**provocare** — "to bring about, to cause; to provoke".

> La pioggia ha provocato molti incidenti.
>
> *The rain caused many accidents.*

With bad events it means to cause; with a person, to provoke: *non provocarmi!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*provocare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōvocāre* "to call forth, to challenge" (Lewis & Short), built from *prō* "forth" and *vocāre* "to call".
