Gujarati, chapter 275: What was going on.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
હું વાંચતો હતો (hũ vā̃cto hato) — was reading, used to read.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I don't read", the A2 way. (hũ nathī vā̃cto.) Keep its -તો. Then say "I was at home". (hũ ghare hato.)

Grammar Lens: -તો with હતો.
હું પુસ્તક વાંચતો હતો. hũ pustak vā̃cto hato. — "I was reading a book."
The stem takes -તો, then comes હતો, and both follow the person:
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
who: a man. was reading: વાંચતો હતો (vā̃cto hato).
who: a woman. was reading: વાંચતી હતી (vā̃ctī hatī).
who: a neuter thing. was reading: વાંચતું હતું (vā̃ctũ hatũ).
who: several, or respect. was reading: વાંચતા હતા (vā̃ctā hatā).
There is no -એ here, even with an object: મીરા ચા પીતી હતી (Mīrā cā pītī hatī), "Mira was drinking tea". The doer form goes with the plain past, પીધી.
The same form tells what used to happen: પહેલાં અરુણ રોજ ચાલતો હતો (pahelā̃ Aruṇ roj cālto hato), "Arun used to walk every day".

You'll want to know: the scene and the event.
હું વાંચતો હતો ત્યારે ફોન આવ્યો. hũ vā̃cto hato tyāre phon āvyo. — "I was reading when the phone rang."
વાંચતો હતો is the scene: it was going on. આવ્યો is the event that broke in. ત્યારે (tyāre), "at that time", joins them, and જ્યારે can open the scene: જ્યારે અરુણ ઘરે પહોંચ્યો, ત્યારે મીરા રાંધતી હતી (jyāre Aruṇ ghare pahõcyo, tyāre Mīrā rā̃dhtī hatī), "when Arun got home, Mira was cooking".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "It was raining" — varsād paḍto hato]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Mira was cooking when Arun came home" — Mīrā rā̃dhtī hatī tyāre Aruṇ ghare āvyo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 22 seconds]
Say that you were reading when the phone rang.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"She was reading"? (તે વાંચતી હતી.) Which part is the scene in હું વાંચતો હતો (hũ vā̃cto hato) ત્યારે ફોન આવ્યો? (વાંચતો હતો.) "Mira was drinking tea": મીરા or મીરાએ? (મીરા.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
બનવું (banvũ) — to happen; to become.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: ask "what happened?" the A2 way. (શું થયું?, śũ thayũ?) Then say "it was raining". (varsād paḍto hato.)

You'll want to know: બનવું.
બનવું — banvũ — "to happen; to become".
ગઈકાલે રાતે શું બન્યું? gaīkāle rāte śũ banyũ? — "What happened last night?"
શું થયું? is the everyday question. બનવું (banvũ) is for an event, a thing that takes place, and it suits a story. It takes no object, so its past follows its subject, as ગયો does: બન્યું, બન્યો, બની.
Its second sense is "to become":
અરુણ દાક્તર બન્યો. Aruṇ dāktar banyo. — "Arun became a doctor."

The word, taken apart.
બનવું (banvũ) sits beside બનાવવું, "to make", which you know: one makes a thing, the other is what a thing does when it comes about. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit vanati, "desires, gains, makes ready", and marks the Gujarati verb as taken from Hindi bannā (CDIAL 11260).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: banvũ, then its past, banyũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What happened last night?" — gaīkāle rāte śũ banyũ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "This used to happen every day" — āvũ roj bantũ hatũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does બનવું (banvũ) mean? ("To happen; to become".) "Arun became a doctor"? (અરુણ દાક્તર બન્યો.) Which question is the everyday one? (શું થયું?)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — what was going on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "Arun has gone home". (Aruṇ ghare gayo che.) Then "I was going to go, but it rained". (hũ javāno hato, paṇ varsād paḍyo.)

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Which form paints the scene, વાંચતો હતો or આવ્યો? (વાંચતો હતો.) Which word joins it to the event? (ત્યારે.) "What happened?", in a story? (શું બન્યું?)
