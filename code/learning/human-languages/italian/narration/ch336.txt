Italian, chapter 336: In forno.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
preriscaldare — to preheat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to rise, then the Italian for the rolling pin.

You'll want to know: preriscaldare.
preriscaldare — "to preheat".
Preriscalda il forno a 180 gradi. Preheat the oven to 180 degrees.
Il forno preriscaldato — the preheated oven.

The word, taken apart.
preriscaldare joins pre- "before", ri- and scaldare "to warm", from Late Latin excaldāre, built on calidus "hot" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: la teglia.
la teglia — "the baking tray, the baking tin".
Metti le patate nella teglia. Put the potatoes in the baking tray.
La pizza in teglia — pizza baked in a tray.

The word, taken apart.
teglia is inherited from Latin tēgula "a tile" (EtymDB), from tegere "to cover".

la cottura — "the cooking; the cooking time".
La cottura dura un'ora. The cooking takes an hour.
Il tempo di cottura — cooking time; a metà cottura — halfway through.

cottura is inherited from Latin coctūra "a cooking" (EtymDB), from coquere "to cook".
