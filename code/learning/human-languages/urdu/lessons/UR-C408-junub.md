## You'll want to know: جنوب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C408-SIMT408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جنوب** — *junūb* — "the south".

> سمندر جنوب کی سمت میں ہے۔
>
> *samandar junūb kī simt meṅ hai.*
>
> The sea lies to the south.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جنوب** (*junūb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *janūb*, "the south".
