## You'll want to know: das Kapitel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C316-ERZAEHLER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Kapitel** — "the chapter".

> Im letzten Kapitel findet er seinen Vater.
>
> *In the last chapter he finds his father.*

Plural: *die Kapitel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kapitel* is a learned borrowing from Latin *capitulum* "a little head, a section", from *caput* "head" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent.
