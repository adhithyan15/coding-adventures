## You'll want to know: a gratidão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C499-GRATIDAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a gratidão** — "gratitude".

> Mostrou a sua gratidão com um bolo.
>
> *She showed her gratitude with a cake.*

Built on *grato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gratidão* is built on *grato* "grateful" with *-idão* (EtymDB); *grato* comes from Latin *grātus* "thankful".
