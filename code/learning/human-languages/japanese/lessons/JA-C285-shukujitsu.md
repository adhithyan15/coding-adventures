## You'll want to know: しゅくじつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C285-MOYOOSHI285-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅくじつ** — *shukujitsu* — "a public holiday".

> **あしたは しゅくじつです。** — *ashita wa shukujitsu desu* — "Tomorrow is a public holiday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅくじつ** (*shukujitsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shuku*, "celebrate" (Middle Chinese *tsyuwk*) and *jitsu*, "day" (*nyit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
