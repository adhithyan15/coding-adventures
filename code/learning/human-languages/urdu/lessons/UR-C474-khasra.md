## You'll want to know: خسرہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C474-SPREAD474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خسرہ** — *khasrā* — "measles".

> میرے بھائی کو خسرہ ہو گیا۔
>
> *mere bhāī ko khasrā ho gayā.*
>
> My brother caught measles.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خسرہ** (*khasrā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khasrā*, "scab, measles", under Sanskrit *khasa*, "itch, scab" (CDIAL 3854): the illness is named for its rash.
