Portuguese, chapter 306: Todos salvos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
evacuar — to evacuate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the first-aider, then the Portuguese for the stretcher.

You'll want to know: evacuar.
evacuar — "to evacuate".
Evacuaram a escola por causa do fumo. They evacuated the school because of the smoke.
Takes a direct object: evacuar o prédio.

The word, taken apart.
evacuar is a learned borrowing from Latin ēvacuāre "to empty out" (EtymDB), built on vacuus "empty".

Lesson 2 of 5.
resgatar — to rescue; to ransom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the stretcher, then the Portuguese for to evacuate.

You'll want to know: resgatar.
resgatar — "to rescue; to ransom".
Os bombeiros resgataram um gato da árvore. The firefighters rescued a cat from the tree.
Also to cash in: resgatar um prémio.

The word, taken apart.
resgatar is built on resgate (EtymDB), from Spanish rescatar "to rescue", which goes back to Vulgar Latin recaptāre, from Latin captāre "to catch".

Lesson 4 of 5.
sobreviver — to survive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to rescue, then the Portuguese for the rescue.

You'll want to know: sobreviver.
sobreviver — "to survive".
Todos sobreviveram ao acidente. Everyone survived the accident.
Sobreviver a + what you lived through.

The word, taken apart.
sobreviver comes from Latin supervīvere "to outlive, to survive" (EtymDB), from super "over" and vīvere "to live".
