Persian, chapter 470: Camping.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چادر (châdor) — a tent; a chador (a woman's full cloak).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a dam, then the Persian for to flow.

You'll want to know: چادر.
چادر (châdor) — "a tent; a chador (a woman's full cloak)".
شب را در چادر خوابیدیم. Shab râ dar châdor khâbidim. We slept in the tent that night.
Both senses are common; the context tells them apart.

The word, taken apart.
châdor comes from Middle Persian čādur 'a sheet, a veil', which EtymDB takes from Sanskrit chattra: a covering that became both a tent and a cloak.

Lesson 2 of 5.
کیسه خواب (kise-khâb) — a sleeping bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to flow, then the Persian for a tent.

You'll want to know: کیسه خواب.
کیسه خواب (kise-khâb) — "a sleeping bag".
شب سرد بود و در کیسه خواب لرزیدیم. Shab sard bud va dar kise-khâb larzidim. The night was cold and we shivered in our sleeping bags.
kise 'bag' + khâb 'sleep'.

The word, taken apart.
kise-khâb is a native compound of kise 'bag', from Middle Persian kīsag (EtymDB), and khâb 'sleep', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian.

هیزم (hizom) — "firewood".
پدرم با هیزم آتش روشن کرد. Pedaram bâ hizom âtash roshan kard. My father lit a fire with firewood.
Hizom shekastan — to chop firewood.

The word, taken apart.
hizom is a native word, usually traced to Middle Persian ēzm 'firewood'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
گردش کردن (gardesh kardan) — to go for an outing, to stroll, to go sightseeing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a sleeping bag, then the Persian for firewood.

You'll want to know: گردش کردن.
گردش کردن (gardesh kardan) — "to go for an outing, to stroll, to go sightseeing".
عصر کنار رودخانه گردش کردیم. 'Asr kenâr-e rudkhâne gardesh kardim. In the afternoon we went for a stroll by the river.
gardesh 'a turn, an outing' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
gardesh kardan is built from gardesh 'a turn, an outing', which EtymDB lists and which is built on gashtan 'to turn, to wander', and kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اتراق کردن (otrâq kardan) — to stop for the night, to camp (on a journey).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for firewood, then the Persian for to go for an outing.

You'll want to know: اتراق کردن.
اتراق کردن (otrâq kardan) — "to stop for the night, to camp (on a journey)".
شب کنار چشمه اتراق کردیم. Shab kenâr-e cheshme otrâq kardim. At night we camped by the spring.
otrâq 'a halt' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
otrâq kardan is built from otrâq 'a halt, a camp', a loan usually traced to Turkic otraq 'a stopping place', and kardan 'to do'. EtymDB does not list otrâq.
