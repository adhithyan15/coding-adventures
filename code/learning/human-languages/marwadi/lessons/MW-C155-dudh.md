## You'll want to know: दूध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C155-FOOD155-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01] -->

**दूध** — *dūdh* — "milk".

> **हूं रोज दूध पीऊं हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ roj dūdh pīū̃ hū̃.* — I drink milk every day.

It ends on a letter you have not written yet: *dh*, a **द** with a breath after
it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दूध** (*dūdh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dugdha*, "milk", a word built on the
verb for milking. CDIAL lists Marwari *dūdh*, masculine (CDIAL 6391).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C155-FOOD155-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01] -->

**दूध** is masculine: **दूध गरम है।** (*dūdh garam hai.*), the milk is hot.
