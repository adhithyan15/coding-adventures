## You'll want to know: यक़ीन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C374-ANDAZA374-03]; assesses=[] -->

**यक़ीन** — *yaqīn* — "certainty, belief".

> मुझे यक़ीन है कि अगर तुम रोज़ अभ्यास करोगे तो ज़रूर जीतोगे।
>
> *mujhe yaqīn hai ki agar tum roz abhyās karoge to zarūr jītoge.*
>
> I'm sure that if you practise every day, you'll win.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यक़ीन** (*yaqīn*) is usually traced to Arabic *yaqīn*, "certainty", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
