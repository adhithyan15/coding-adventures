## You'll want to know: llorar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C704-ALEGRIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**llorar** — "to cry, to weep".

> El bebé llora por la noche.
>
> *The baby cries at night.*

*Llorar de alegría* — to cry with joy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*llorar* is inherited from Latin *plōrāre* "to wail, to weep aloud" (EtymDB). Latin *pl-* at the start of a word became *ll-* in Spanish, the same change that turned *plēnus* into *lleno*.
