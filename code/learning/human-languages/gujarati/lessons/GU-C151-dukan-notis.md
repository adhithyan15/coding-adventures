# (reading) — a notice on a shop door

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C151-READ151-04, GU-LEX-C151-READ151-03, GU-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *tomorrow is a public holiday*, then *only in the morning*.

[PAUSE 1s] Nobody is talking to anybody on this page. A notice tells you what
you may do, and when.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SKILL-C151-READ-NOTICE-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C151-READ151-01, GU-LEX-C151-READ151-02, GU-LEX-C151-READ151-03, GU-LEX-C151-READ151-04, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-THASHE-01] -->

> **નોટિસ**
>
> **દુકાન રોજ સવારે અને સાંજે ખુલ્લી છે.**
> **બપોરે દુકાન ખુલ્લી નથી.**
> **રવિવારે રજા છે.**
> **આવતીકાલે, સોમવારે, જાહેર રજા છે.**
> **મંગળવારે દુકાન ફક્ત સવારે ખુલ્લી હશે.**
> **બુધવારે દુકાન સવારે અને સાંજે ફરી ખુલ્લી હશે.**
> **આજે કેરી સસ્તી છે!**
> **સાંજે દૂધ અને દહીં પણ છે.**
> **આભાર.**

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: the notice once, straight through, without translating]

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: it a second time, and find every word that says *when*]

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C94-TIME94-03, GU-LEX-C73-QUALITY-05, GU-LEX-C78-WEEK1-01, GU-LEX-C78-WEEK1-02, GU-LEX-C78-WEEK1-03, GU-LEX-C79-WEEK2-02, GU-JOIN-PAN-ALSO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Almost every line opens on a *when*: **રોજ**, **બપોરે**, then four
days, each with the **-ે** of *on*. Two lines say **ખુલ્લી હશે**, *will be
open*, about days still to come. The line with **!** is an advert, set into the
notice to bring you in.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SKILL-C151-READ-NOTICE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Which day is the shop open only in the morning? (**મંગળવારે**,
Tuesday.) Which two days is it closed? (**રવિવારે** and **સોમવારે**: Sunday,
and the public holiday.)
