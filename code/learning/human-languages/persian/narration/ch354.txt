Persian, chapter 354: Discoveries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کشف کردن (kashf kardan) — to discover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for ancestors, then the Persian for heritage.

You'll want to know: کشف کردن.
کشف کردن (kashf kardan) — "to discover".
دانشمندان یک شهر قدیمی را زیر زمین کشف کردند. Dâneshmandân yek shahr-e qadimi râ zir-e zamin kashf kardand. Scientists discovered an old city underground.
Present kashf mikonad. Kashf alone is "a discovery".

The word, taken apart.
kashf is borrowed from Arabic kashf, "an unveiling, an uncovering" (EtymDB). kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کتیبه (katibe) — an inscription.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to invent, then the Persian for to found.

You'll want to know: کتیبه.
کتیبه (katibe) — "an inscription".
روی سنگ یک کتیبه قدیمی بود. Ru-ye sang yek katibe-ye qadimi bud. There was an old inscription on the stone.
Carved on stone or set in tiles on a building.

The word, taken apart.
katibe is usually traced to Arabic katība (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), built on the root k-t-b "to write", the root of ketâb "book".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ویرانه (virâne) — a ruin, ruins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to found, then the Persian for an inscription.

You'll want to know: ویرانه.
ویرانه (virâne) — "a ruin, ruins".
از قلعه فقط یک ویرانه مانده است. Az qal'e faqat yek virâne mânde ast. Only a ruin is left of the castle.
From virân "ruined" + -e.

The word, taken apart.
virâne adds the suffix -e to virân "desolate, ruined", a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent.
