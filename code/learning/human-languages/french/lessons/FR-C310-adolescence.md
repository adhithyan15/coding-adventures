## You'll want to know: l'adolescence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C310-JEUNESSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'adolescence** — "adolescence, the teenage years".

> Son adolescence a été difficile.
>
> *His teenage years were difficult.*

Feminine: *une adolescence heureuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adolescence* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adulēscentia* "youth" (EtymDB), from *adolēscere* "to grow up", built on *alere* "to nourish" (Lewis & Short).
