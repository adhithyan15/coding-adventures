## You'll want to know: fángshàishuāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C444-ISLAND444-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fángshàishuāng* — "sunscreen".

> *wǒ de fángshàishuāng bú jiàn le* — My sunscreen has gone missing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fángshàishuāng* is built from *fáng* 'to guard against', *shài* 'to put in the sun' and *shuāng* 'frost', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjang*, *sreaH* and *srjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the sense 'a cream' for the last is modern.
