## Writing

<!-- mock-part:a2-writing-functional-response -->
The 25–35-word message must address **मीरा**, explain the absence, give the
book-return day, and ask whether another person should deliver it. Score the
learner's own coherent response; do not require one fixed reason.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-writing-functional-response -->
<!-- mock-part:a2-writing-connected-message-or-description -->
The 70–90-word account must identify the journey, time, destination and
companion, sequence an event and activities, and state one future change.
Accept internally consistent details and documented regional Marathi forms.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-writing-connected-message-or-description -->

## Speaking

<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-personal-interview -->
Score six relevant answers about the stated familiar domains, including a
reason, comparison, or detail where requested.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-personal-interview -->
<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-prepared-account -->
Score a 90–120-second organized account of a familiar helpful person, using
keyword notes rather than a written answer script.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-prepared-account -->
<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-information-exchange-role-play -->
The learner must request information about the school book room, ask what to
bring, which days and what time the room opens, and when the book must be
returned, clarify one incomplete detail, and close appropriately.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-information-exchange-role-play -->
