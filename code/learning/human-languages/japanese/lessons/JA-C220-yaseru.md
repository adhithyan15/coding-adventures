# やせる (yaseru) — to lose weight; to get thin

## You'll want to know: やせる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C220-WEIGHT220-04]; assesses=[] -->

**やせる** — *yaseru* — "to lose weight; to get thin".

> **ちちは やせませんでした。** — *chichi wa yasemasen deshita* — "My father did not lose weight."

Polite **やせます**, *yasemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やせる** (*yaseru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
