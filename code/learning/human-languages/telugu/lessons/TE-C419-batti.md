## You'll want to know: బట్టి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C419-KAKAPOTE419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**బట్టి** — *baṭṭi* — "depending on; going by".

> ధరను బట్టి నేను కొంటాను.
>
> *dharanu baṭṭi nēnu koṇṭānu.*
>
> I will buy it depending on the price.

The thing depended on takes -ని: ధరను బట్టి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బట్టి** (*baṭṭi*) is native Dravidian, usually taken as a form of *paṭṭu*, "to hold, to take hold of" (DEDR 4034): holding to a thing, going by it.
