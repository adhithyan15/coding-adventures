Sanskrit, chapter 69: Eye, Throat and Tongue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नेत्रम् (netram) — an eye.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for shyness, then the Sanskrit for joy.

You'll want to know: नेत्रम्.
नेत्रम् (netram) — "an eye", the word of poems, beside the everyday अक्षि.

What you've built.
A second word for the eye.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: netram]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: netram, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say netram]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for shyness, then the Sanskrit for joy, then say netram again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does नेत्रम् (netram) mean? ("An eye".) Say it once more.
