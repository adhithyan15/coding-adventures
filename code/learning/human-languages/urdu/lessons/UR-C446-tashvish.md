## You'll want to know: تشویش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C446-WORRY446-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تشویش** — *tashvīsh* — "anxiety; concern".

> بچے کے بخار پر ماں کو تشویش ہے۔
>
> *bachche ke bukhār par māṅ ko tashvīsh hai.*
>
> The mother is anxious about the child's fever.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تشویش** (*tashvīsh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tashwīsh*, "a stirring up, a disturbance".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
