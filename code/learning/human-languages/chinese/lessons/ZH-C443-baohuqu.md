## You'll want to know: 保护区
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C443-CLEAN443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**保护区** — *bǎohùqū* — "nature reserve; protected area".

> **我们坐车去山里的保护区** *wǒmen zuò chē qù shān lǐ de bǎohùqū* — We're driving to the nature reserve in the mountains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**保护区** (*bǎohùqū*) is built from 保护 'to protect' and 区 'an area', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX*, *huH* and *khju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
