# ભીંજાવું (bhī̃jāvũ) — to get wet through (in rain)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C434-SHOWER434-03, GU-LEX-C434-SHOWER434-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a raincoat, then the Gujarati for to gather (of clouds).

## You'll want to know: ભીંજાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C434-SHOWER434-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભીંજાવું** — *bhī̃jāvũ* — "to get wet through (in rain)".

> છત્રી ન હતી, એટલે હું ભીંજાઈ ગયો.
>
> *chatrī na hatī, eṭle hũ bhī̃jāī gayo.*
>
> I had no umbrella, so I got wet through.

Past (he …): તે ભીંજાઈ ગયો, *te bhī̃jāī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભીંજાવું** (*bhī̃jāvũ*) goes back to *\*bhiyañjati*, "anoints, wets", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be wetted" (CDIAL 9503).
