## You'll want to know: पाळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C514-KAMKAJ514-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पाळी** — *pāḷī* — "a shift; a turn".

> माझ्या भावाची रात्रीची पाळी असते.
>
> *mājhyā bhāvācī rātrīcī pāḷī aste.*
>
> My brother works the night shift.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाळी** (*pāḷī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *pāḷī*, "a turn", under *\*pāḍi*, "row, line, turn" (CDIAL 8041).
