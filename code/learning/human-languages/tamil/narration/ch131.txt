Tamil, chapter 131: Parrot, Pigeon, Owl, Peacock, Ant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கிளி (kiḷi) — a parrot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a frog, then the Tamil for a tortoise.

You'll want to know: கிளி.
கிளி — kiḷi — "a parrot".
