## You'll want to know: じゅうでん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C425-TSUUYAKU425-05]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうでん** — *jūden* — "charging a battery; recharging".

> **えきで でんわを じゅうでんしました。** — *eki de denwa o jūden shimashita* — "I charged my phone at the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうでん** (*jūden*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jū*, "fill" (Middle Chinese *tsyhuwng*) and *den*, "electricity" (*denH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
