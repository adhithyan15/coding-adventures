## You'll want to know: 走运
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C529-LUCK529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**走运** — *zǒuyùn* — "to be in luck".

> **如果你走运就能找到好工作** *rúguǒ nǐ zǒuyùn jiù néng zhǎo dào hǎo gōngzuò* — If you're in luck, you'll find a good job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**走运** (*zǒuyùn*) is built from 走 'to go' and 运 'luck', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsuwX* and *hjunH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hurry'; the sense 'luck' of the second is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
