# പ്രതിപക്ഷം (pratipakṣaṁ) — the opposition

## You'll want to know: പ്രതിപക്ഷം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C422-SABHA422-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രതിപക്ഷം** — *pratipakṣaṁ* — "the opposition".

> പ്രതിപക്ഷം പുതിയ നികുതിയെ എതിർത്തു.
>
> *pratipakṣaṁ putiya nikutiye etirttu.*
>
> The opposition opposed the new tax.

Its members sit across from the government in the assembly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രതിപക്ഷം** (*pratipakṣaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *prati-*, "against, opposite", and *pakṣa*, "a side" (CDIAL 7627): the side opposite.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
