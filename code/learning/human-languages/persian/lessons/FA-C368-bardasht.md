## You'll want to know: برداشت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C368-TAHLIL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**برداشت** (*bardâsht*) — "an impression, an interpretation".

> برداشت من این بود که او ناراحت است.
>
> *bardâsht-e man in bud ke u nârâhat ast.*
>
> My impression was that he was upset.

The past stem of *bardâshtan* "to pick up": what you take from something. It is also a harvest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برداشت** is native Persian, built from *bar* "up, on" + *dâsht*, the past stem of *dâshtan* "to have, to hold". It is what you pick up from a text or a meeting.
