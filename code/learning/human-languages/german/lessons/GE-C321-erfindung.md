## You'll want to know: die Erfindung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C321-ERFINDEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Erfindung** — "the invention".

> Das Rad war eine wichtige Erfindung.
>
> *The wheel was an important invention.*

Plural: *die Erfindungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Erfindung* is built on *erfinden* "to invent", from *finden* "to find", a cousin of English *find* (EtymDB).
