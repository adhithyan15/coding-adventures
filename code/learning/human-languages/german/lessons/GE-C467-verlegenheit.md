## You'll want to know: die Verlegenheit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C467-SCHAM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Verlegenheit** — "embarrassment; awkward situation".

> In seiner Verlegenheit sah er auf den Boden.
>
> *In his embarrassment, he looked at the floor.*

*Jemanden in Verlegenheit bringen* — to embarrass someone. Plural, for awkward situations: *die Verlegenheiten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Verlegenheit* is built on the adjective *verlegen* "embarrassed" with *-heit*; *verlegen* is usually traced to a Middle High German participle meaning "having lain too long".
