## You'll want to know: جمجمه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C497-BONES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جمجمه** (*jomjome*) — "a skull".

> در موزه یک جمجمه خیلی قدیمی بود.
>
> *dar muze yek jomjome-ye kheyli qadimi bud.*
>
> In the museum there was a very old skull.

Plural *jomjomehâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jomjome* was borrowed from Arabic *jumjuma*, 'skull' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
