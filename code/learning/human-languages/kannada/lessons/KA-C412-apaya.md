## You'll want to know: ಅಪಾಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C412-APAYA412-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಪಾಯ** — *apāya* — "a danger; a risk".

> ಮಳೆಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ನದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಈಜುವುದು ಅಪಾಯ.
>
> *maḷegāladalli nadiyalli ījuvudu apāya.*
>
> Swimming in the river in the rainy season is dangerous.

ಅಪಾಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಇರು, to be in danger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಪಾಯ** (*apāya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *apāya*, "going away; loss, harm" (CDIAL 481).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
