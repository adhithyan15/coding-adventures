## You'll want to know: 特色
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C451-SPECIAL451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**特色** — *tèsè* — "special character; speciality".

> *zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài hěn yǒu tèsè* — The food at this restaurant has a character all its own.

The new character, in pieces: 色 — six strokes in two pieces: on top a short stroke falling to the left and a small bar that turns down to the left; under them a bar that turns down, a short vertical, a bar, and a long stroke that runs down the left, along the foot and hooks up. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**特色** (*tèsè*) is built from 特 'single, special' and 色 'a look', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dok* and *srik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the colour of the face.
