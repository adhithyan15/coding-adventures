## You'll want to know: paveō, pavēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C496-PAV-01]; assesses=[] -->

**paveō, pavēre** — "to quake with fear, be terrified".

> Ovēs lupum vident et pavent.
>
> *The sheep catch sight of the wolf and quake with fear.*

*paveō, pavēre, pāvī*. The noun is *pavor* — terror.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**paveō** is a native Latin verb; EtymDB links it with a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to strike", but Lewis & Short calls its origin doubtful, so it is not settled.
