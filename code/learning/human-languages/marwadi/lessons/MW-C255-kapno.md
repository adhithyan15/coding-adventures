## You'll want to know: कांपणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C255-TABIYAT255-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कांपणो** — *kā̃pṇo* — "to tremble; to shiver".

> ठंडी मांय हूं कांपूं हूं।
>
> *ṭhaṇḍī mā̃y hū̃ kā̃pū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I shiver in the cold.

Past (I …): हूं रात मांय कांप्यो, *hū̃ rāt mā̃y kā̃pyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कांपणो** (*kā̃pṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kampate*, "trembles". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *kā̃pnā* (CDIAL 2767).
