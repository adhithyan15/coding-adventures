## You'll want to know: تاخیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C430-MAGHSAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تاخیر** (*ta'khir*) — "a delay".

> قطار ما دو ساعت تاخیر دارد.
>
> *Qatâr-e mâ do sâ'at ta'khir dârad.*
>
> Our train is two hours late.

*Bâ ta'khir* — late, delayed. *Ta'khir dârad* — it is running late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'khir* was borrowed from Arabic *taʾḫīr*, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'putting off, delay'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
