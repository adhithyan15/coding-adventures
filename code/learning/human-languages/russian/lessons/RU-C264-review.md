# Review — advice, and the reason for it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-STOIT-INFINITIVE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-LUCHSHE-INFINITIVE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-DOLZHEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Three pieces of advice: "you should rest", "you'd better take a
taxi", "you must ring Mum". (*Tebé stóit otdokhnút. Tebé lúchshe vzyat
taksí. Ty dólzhen pozvonít máme.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PRICHINA-V-TOM-01, RU-GRAMMAR-EXPLAIN-STEPS-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PO-MOEMU-01, RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-KAZHETSYA-01, RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-04] -->

Two lessons, and Russian can now advise and explain. Advice at three
strengths, each with an infinitive: **тебе стоит**, **лучше**, **ты
должен**. And **причина в том, что …**, with the explanation built
from what happened and **поэтому**.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-STOIT-INFINITIVE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-EXPLAIN-STEPS-01, RU-GRAMMAR-POETOMU-RESULT-01, RU-LEX-TAK-CHTO-01, RU-LEX-C228-VESHCH228-02, RU-LEX-C37-PEOPLE-03, RU-LEX-SPRASHIVAT, RU-LEX-PONIMAT, RU-LEX-POMOGAT] -->

> **— Я не понимаю правило.**
> **— По-моему, тебе стоит спросить учителя. Он поможет.**

*Ya ne ponimáyu právilo* — "I don't understand the rule." *Po-móyemu,
tebé stóit sprosít uchítelya. On pomózhet* — "I think you should ask the
teacher. He'll help." **Спросить** is the perfective partner of
*спрашивать*.
