## You'll want to know: सिग्नल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C401-PHATAK401-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सिग्नल** — *signal* — "a signal".

> सिग्नल लाल था, तो ट्रेन रुक गई।
>
> *signal lāl thā, to ṭren ruk gaī.*
>
> The signal was red, so the train stopped.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिग्नल** (*signal*) is borrowed from English *signal*, for the railway signal and the traffic light alike.
