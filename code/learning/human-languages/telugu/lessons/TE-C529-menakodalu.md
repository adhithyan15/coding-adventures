# మేనకోడలు (mēnakōḍalu) — a niece; a sister's daughter

## You'll want to know: మేనకోడలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C529-MENAMAMA529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మేనకోడలు** — *mēnakōḍalu* — "a niece; a sister's daughter".

> నా మేనకోడలు ఇవాళ బడికి వెళ్ళింది.
>
> *nā mēnakōḍalu ivāḷa baḍiki veḷḷindi.*
>
> My niece went to school today.

A man's sister's daughter or a woman's brother's daughter; కోడలు alone is a daughter-in-law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మేనకోడలు** (*mēnakōḍalu*) is built from *mēna-*, listed in DEDR's appendix of Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 53) and కోడలు, "daughter-in-law".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
