Russian, chapter 91: West, Underground, Tram, Ship, Boat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
запад (západ) — the west.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the south, then the Russian for the east.

You'll want to know: запад.
запад (západ) — "the west".

The word, taken apart.
запад (západ) is за-, "behind", with пад, from падать, "to fall": first "sunset", like Latin occidēns, "the falling (sun)" (Vasmer).

Lesson 4 of 5.
корабль (korábl') — a ship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the underground, then the Russian for a tram.

You'll want to know: корабль.
корабль (korábl') — "a ship".

The word, taken apart.
корабль (korábl') — an old loan from Greek karábion, kárabos, "a boat", first "a crab"; Vasmer dates it early because it has б, not в. Spanish carabela, "caravel", has the same source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
лодка (lódka) — a boat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a tram, then the Russian for a ship.

You'll want to know: лодка.
лодка (lódka) — "a boat".

The word, taken apart.
лодка (lódka) is old lodŭka, a diminutive of лодья, now ладья, "a boat", from Proto-Slavic oldĭji — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian aldijà, "canoe", and Swedish ålla, "a trough".
