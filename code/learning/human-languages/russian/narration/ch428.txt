Russian, chapter 428: A bed for the night.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
хостел (khóstel) — a hostel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a captain, then the Russian for to sink.

You'll want to know: хостел.
хостел (khóstel) — "a hostel".
Студенты жили в недорогом хостеле. Studénty zhíli v nedorogóm khóstele. The students stayed in a cheap hostel.
Masculine; the stress is on the first syllable.

The word, taken apart.
хостел (khóstel) is usually traced to English hostel, which EtymDB traces through Old French to Latin hospitāle, a place for guests. EtymDB does not list the Russian word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
турбаза (turbáza) — a holiday camp, a tourist centre.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to sink, then the Russian for a hostel.

You'll want to know: турбаза.
турбаза (turbáza) — "a holiday camp, a tourist centre".
Летом мы отдыхали на турбазе у озера. Létom my otdykháli na turbáze u ózera. In summer we stayed at a holiday camp by the lake.
Feminine; short for туристическая база: simple cabins in the countryside.

The word, taken apart.
турбаза (turbáza) is a short form of туристическая база 'a tourist base', from турист 'a tourist' and база 'a base'. EtymDB lists база and турист without a source; турист is usually traced through French touriste and tour to Latin tornāre 'to turn on a lathe' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ночлег (nochlég) — a bed for the night, a place to spend the night.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a hostel, then the Russian for a holiday camp.

You'll want to know: ночлег.
ночлег (nochlég) — "a bed for the night, a place to spend the night".
Уже темно, а ночлега у нас ещё нет. Uzhé temnó, a nochléga u nas yeshchyó net. It is already dark, and we still have nowhere to sleep.
Masculine. Искать ночлег (nochlég) — to look for a place to stay the night.

The word, taken apart.
ночлег (nochlég) is built from ночь 'night' and -лег, the root of лечь 'to lie down': a place to lie down for the night. EtymDB lists the word without a deeper source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ночевать (nochevát) — to spend the night.

Warm-up.

ночевать (nochevát) — "to spend the night".
Мы ночевали в маленькой гостинице. My nocheváli v málenkoy gostínitse. We spent the night in a small hotel.
Imperfective; perfective переночевать. Present ночую, ночуешь.

The word, taken apart.
ночевать (nochevát) is built on ночь 'night' with the verb ending -евать. EtymDB lists ночевать (nochevát) without a deeper source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
бронь (bron) — a booking, a reservation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bed for the night, then the Russian for to spend the night.

You'll want to know: бронь.
бронь (bron) — "a booking, a reservation".
У нас есть бронь на два номера. U nas yest bron na dva nómera. We have a booking for two rooms.
Feminine; colloquial for бронирование. Its cousin броня means armour.

The word, taken apart.
бронь (bron) goes with броня 'armour', which EtymDB traces through Old East Slavic brŭnja to Proto-Slavic brŭnja and on to Gothic brunjō 'a breastplate': a booking shields a place for you.
