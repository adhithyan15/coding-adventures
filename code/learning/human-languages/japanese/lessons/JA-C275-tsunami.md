## You'll want to know: つなみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C275-TSUNAMI275-01]; assesses=[] -->

**つなみ** — *tsunami* — "a tsunami; a tidal wave".

> **つなみの ばあいは、やまに にげて ください。** — *tsunami no baai wa, yama ni nigete kudasai* — "In case of a tsunami, please run to high ground."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つなみ** (*tsunami*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tsu*, "harbour", and *nami*, "wave" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
