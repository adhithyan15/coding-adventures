Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 144: Beautiful, Good-looking, Young, Thin, Short.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सुंदर (sundar) — beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for bitter, then the Marwadi for spicy.

You'll want to know: सुंदर.
सुंदर — sundar — "beautiful".

The word, taken apart.
सुंदर (sundar) is Sanskrit sundara, "beautiful", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13474). CDIAL asks whether the Sanskrit word is itself a Middle Indo-Aryan form of sūnara, "excellent".

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Marwadi for spicy, then the Marwadi for beautiful.

You'll want to know: रूपाळो.
रूपाळो — rūpāḷo — "good-looking".

The word, taken apart.
रूपाळो (rūpāḷo) is built with the suffix -āḷo on rūp, "beauty", from Sanskrit rūpa, "form, beauty". CDIAL lists Hindi rūṛā, "beautiful", under it (CDIAL 10803) but not रूपाळो (rūpāḷo) itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
दुबळो (dubaḷo) — thin, weak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for good-looking, then the Marwadi for young.

You'll want to know: दुबळो.
दुबळो — dubaḷo — "thin, weak".

The word, taken apart.
दुबळो (dubaḷo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from durbala, "weak". CDIAL lists Old Marwari dūbalaü under it (CDIAL 6438). It is built on dus-, "ill", and bala, "strength".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ठिगणो (ṭhigṇo) — short (of a person).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for young, then the Marwadi for thin.

You'll want to know: ठिगणो.
ठिगणो — ṭhigṇo — "short (of a person)".

The word, taken apart.
ठिगणो (ṭhigṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ṭhiṅga, "defective", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5501); the match is through Hindi ṭhĩgnā and Gujarati ṭhĩgṇũ. CDIAL sets it in a large group of "defective" words; Hindi and Gujarati forms mean "dwarfish".
