## You'll want to know: tùzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C223-ZOO223-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tùzi* — "rabbit; hare".

> *wǒ jiā yǒu liǎng zhī tùzi* — We've got two rabbits at home.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tùzi* is built from a word for the hare and the noun ending 子. The noun ending is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), and the dictionary lists the first as the name of a beast.
