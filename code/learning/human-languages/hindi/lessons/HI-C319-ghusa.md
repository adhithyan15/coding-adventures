## You'll want to know: घूँसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C319-MARPEET319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घूँसा** — *ghū̃sā* — "a punch".

> उसने चोर को एक घूँसा मारा।
>
> *usne cor ko ek ghū̃sā mārā.*
>
> He punched the thief once.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घूँसा** (*ghū̃sā*) is inherited from *\*ghūssa*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4498). You **घूँसा मारना**, strike a punch: the punch is the object, so the verb is masculine.
