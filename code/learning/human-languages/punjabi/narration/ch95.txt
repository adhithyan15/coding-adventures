Punjabi, chapter 95: Seven, Eight, Nine, Ten, Eleven.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸੱਤ (satt) — seven.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a shirt, then the Punjabi for six.

You'll want to know: ਸੱਤ.
ਸੱਤ — satt — "seven".

The word, taken apart.
ਸੱਤ (satt) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sapta, "seven" (CDIAL 13139).
