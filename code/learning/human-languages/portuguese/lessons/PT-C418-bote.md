## You'll want to know: o bote
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C418-BARCO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o bote** — "the dinghy, the small boat".

> Fomos de bote até à praia do outro lado.
>
> *We went by dinghy to the beach on the other side.*

*O bote salva-vidas* — the lifeboat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bote* came from Old French *bot*, which was taken from Middle English *boot*, Old English *bāt* 'boat' (EtymDB).
