## You'll want to know: desanuviar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C466-CHUVA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**desanuviar** — "to clear up (of the sky); to relax".

> À tarde, o céu desanuviou.
>
> *In the afternoon, the sky cleared.*

*Desanuviar a cabeça* — to clear your head.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desanuviar* is built from *des-* and *anuviar* 'to cloud over', on *nuvem* 'cloud', which goes back to Latin *nūbēs* (EtymDB).
