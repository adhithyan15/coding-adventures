## You'll want to know: अचंभो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C245-TEDNO245-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अचंभो** — *achambho* — "a surprise; a wonder".

> मामो आयो! यो अचंभो है।
>
> *māmo āyo! yo achambho hai.*
>
> My uncle has come! What a surprise.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अचंभो** (*achambho*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ācchambha*, "rigidity", and so "astonishment", the stiffness of surprise. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *acambhā*, "wonder" (CDIAL 1079).
