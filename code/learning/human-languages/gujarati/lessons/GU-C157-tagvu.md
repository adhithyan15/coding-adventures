## You'll want to know: ટાંગવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C157-PLACE157-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાંગવું** — *ṭā̃gvũ* — "to hang up".

> **તે કોટ કબાટમાં ટાંગે છે.** — *te koṭ kabāṭmā̃ ṭā̃ge che* — "He hangs his coat in the cupboard."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાંગવું** (*ṭā̃gvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*ṭaṅg*, "hang up", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb the same way (CDIAL 5436).
