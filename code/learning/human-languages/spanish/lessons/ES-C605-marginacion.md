## You'll want to know: la marginación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C605-PROBSOC-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la marginación** — "the social exclusion, the marginalisation".

> Muchas personas viven en la marginación.
>
> *Many people live on the margins of society.*

Built on *marginar* — to push to the edge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marginación* is built on *marginar*, a learned borrowing from Late Latin *marginare* (EtymDB), from *margo* 'edge, border'.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
