## You'll want to know: もちろん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C322-SHOUCHI322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**もちろん** — *mochiron* — "of course".

> **じかんが あるから、もちろん てつだいます。** — *jikan ga aru kara, mochiron tetsudaimasu* — "I have time, so of course I will help."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もちろん** (*mochiron*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mochi*, "not" and *ron*, "argument" (Middle Chinese *lwon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana.
