## You'll want to know: శిఖరం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C474-SIKHARAM474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**శిఖరం** — *śikharaṁ* — "a peak; a summit".

> ఉదయమే మేము కొండ శిఖరం చేరుకున్నాం.
>
> *udayamē mēmu koṇḍa śikharaṁ cērukunnāṁ.*
>
> Early in the morning we reached the hilltop.

The top of a hill or a mountain, and the top of a temple tower.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శిఖరం** (*śikharaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śikhara*, "a point, a peak" (CDIAL 12435).
