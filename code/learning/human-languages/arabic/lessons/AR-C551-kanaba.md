## You'll want to know: كنبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C551-GHURFA551-05]; assesses=[] -->

**كنبة** — *kanaba* — "a couch".

> جلسنا على الكنبة وشاهدنا فيلما.
>
> *jalasnā ʿalā al-kanaba wa-shāhadnā fīlman.*
>
> We sat on the couch and watched a film.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كنبة** (*kanaba*) is a modern word, usually traced to French *canapé*, a sofa.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
