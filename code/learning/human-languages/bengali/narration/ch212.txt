Lesson 1 of 5.
তুলনা করা (tulônā kôrā) — to compare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to quarrel, then the Bengali for to hug.

You'll want to know: তুলনা করা.
তুলনা করা — tulônā kôrā — "to compare".

The phrase, taken apart.
তুলনা করা (tulônā kôrā) is tulônā, "comparison", Sanskrit tulanā, "weighing, comparing", taken over whole, + kôrā, "to do", from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 3 of 5.
পাল্টানো (pālṭāno) — to swap, to change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to compare, then the Bengali for to rent.

You'll want to know: পাল্টানো.
পাল্টানো — pālṭāno — "to swap, to change".

The word, taken apart.
পাল্টানো (pālṭāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pallaṭṭ, "turn, overturn", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7968). CDIAL lists Bengali pālṭāna, "to turn, change", and calls the origin of the reconstructed form very doubtful.
