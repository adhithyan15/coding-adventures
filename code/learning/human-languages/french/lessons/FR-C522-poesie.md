## You'll want to know: la poésie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C522-GENRES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la poésie** — "poetry".

> Mon père lit de la poésie le soir.
>
> *My father reads poetry in the evening.*

*Un poème* is a single poem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la poésie* comes from Latin *poēsis* (EtymDB), a borrowing from Greek *poíēsis* "a making, a poem".
