## Script — a cap, a box and a hooked frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-LI-APART-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 离

**Three pieces, top to bottom.**

**The top, two strokes:** the dot and the bar that cap 文.

**The middle, four strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left and a dot falling to the right, crossing like a small x. Then a box open at the top round them: a vertical down the left that turns along the foot, and a short vertical on the right.

**The bottom, four strokes.** A vertical down the left. A bar that turns down the right side and hooks to the left at its foot. Inside, a stroke that falls to the left and turns right, then a dot.

2 + 4 + 4 = ten.
