## You'll want to know: બગલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C473-JANGH473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**બગલ** — *bagal* — "an armpit".

> નર્સે બગલમાં તાવ માપ્યો.
>
> *narse bagalmā̃ tāv māpyo.*
>
> The nurse took my temperature under my arm.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બગલ** (*bagal*) is usually traced to Persian *baġal*, "armpit, side".
