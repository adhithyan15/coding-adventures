## You'll want to know: तृष्णा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C359-TYAGA359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तृष्णा** — *tṛṣṇā* — "craving".

> तृष्णा दुःखस्य मूलम् अस्ति।
>
> *tṛṣṇā duḥkhasya mūlam asti.*
>
> Craving is the root of sorrow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तृष्णा** (*tṛṣṇā*) comes from the root √tṛṣ, "to be thirsty"; MW gives *tṛṣṇā* "thirst" and "desire, avidity" (MW).
