Sanskrit, chapter 529: A Change of Plan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परिवर्तनम् (parivartanam) — a change; an exchange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for makes ready, then the Sanskrit for an almanac.

You'll want to know: परिवर्तनम्.
परिवर्तनम् — parivartanam — "a change; an exchange".
यदि वर्षा भवति, तर्हि योजनायां परिवर्तनं भविष्यति। yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi yojanāyāṃ parivartanaṃ bhaviṣyati. If it rains, the plan will change.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
परिवर्तनम् (parivartanam) is built on pari, "around", and the root √vṛt, "to turn", which also gives parivartayati; MW gives parivartana as turning round, and barter, exchange; "a change of plan" grows from these senses (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उपसंहरति (upasaṃharati) — winds up; withdraws.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an almanac, then the Sanskrit for a change.

You'll want to know: उपसंहरति.
उपसंहरति — upasaṃharati — "winds up; withdraws".
यदि अतिथिः न आगच्छति, तर्हि माता उत्सवम् उपसंहरति। yadi atithiḥ na āgacchati, tarhi mātā utsavam upasaṃharati. If the guest does not come, my mother winds up the party.
Past (he …): सः सभाम् उपसंहृतवान्, saḥ sabhām upasaṃhṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपसंहरति (upasaṃharati) is built on upa, sam, "together", and the root √hṛ, "to carry", which also gives harati; MW gives upasaṃhṛ as to draw together, to sum up, to withdraw, to stop (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
संशोधयति (saṃśodhayati) — revises; corrects.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a change, then the Sanskrit for winds up.

You'll want to know: संशोधयति.
संशोधयति — saṃśodhayati — "revises; corrects".
यदि योजना कठिना अस्ति, तर्हि वयं तां संशोधयामः। yadi yojanā kaṭhinā asti, tarhi vayaṃ tāṃ saṃśodhayāmaḥ. If the plan is hard, we revise it.
Past (he …): सः योजनां संशोधितवान्, saḥ yojanāṃ saṃśodhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
संशोधयति (saṃśodhayati) is built on sam, "thoroughly", and the causative of the root √śudh, "to be pure", which also gives śodhayati; MW gives saṃśodhayati as to cleanse thoroughly, to clear; "to revise" is its modern use (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
त्वरयति (tvarayati) — hurries along.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for winds up, then the Sanskrit for revises.

You'll want to know: त्वरयति.
त्वरयति — tvarayati — "hurries along".
यदि समयः अल्पः अस्ति, तर्हि पिता अस्मान् त्वरयति। yadi samayaḥ alpaḥ asti, tarhi pitā asmān tvarayati. If time is short, my father hurries us along.
Past (he …): सः अस्मान् त्वरितवान्, saḥ asmān tvaritavān.

The word, taken apart.
त्वरयति (tvarayati) comes from the causative of the root √tvar, "to hurry, to make haste"; MW gives tvarayati as to cause to hasten, to urge forward (MW).
