## You'll want to know: ഇളവ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C444-KATAMA444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇളവ്** — *iḷavŭ* — "a concession; an exemption".

> പാവപ്പെട്ട കുട്ടികൾക്ക് ഫീസിൽ ഇളവ് ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *pāvappeṭṭa kuṭṭikaḷkkŭ phīsil iḷavŭ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> Poor children have a concession on the fee.

On something: -ഇൽ ഇളവ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇളവ്** (*iḷavŭ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 510 lists Malayalam *iḷavu*, "leave, holiday, rest", beside *iḷekkuka*, "to slacken, to remit". A concession slackens a rule.
