**さしみ** — *sashimi* — "sliced raw fish".

> **みなとで さしみを たべました。** — *minato de sashimi o tabemashita* — "We ate sliced raw fish at the harbour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さしみ** (*sashimi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *sashi*, "piercing" (from *sasu*) and *mi*, "flesh"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
