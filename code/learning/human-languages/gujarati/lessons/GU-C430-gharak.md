## You'll want to know: ઘરાક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C430-SCALES430-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘરાક** — *gharāk* — "a customer (everyday); a regular buyer".

> આ દુકાનના ઘરાક વરસોથી અહીં આવે છે.
>
> *ā dukānnā gharāk varsothī ahī̃ āve che.*
>
> This shop's customers have come here for years.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘરાક** (*gharāk*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *grāhaka*, "one who takes" (Monier-Williams), worn down in speech; ગ્રાહક keeps the learned form of the same word.
