Lesson 1 of 5.
телевизор (televízor) — a television.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for holidays, leave, then the Russian for a rug, a carpet.

You'll want to know: телевизор.
телевизор (televízor) — "a television".

The word, taken apart.
телевизор (televízor) is an international coinage: Greek tēle, "far", with -визор, from Latin vīsor, "one who sees", from vidēre, "to see": a far-seer.

Lesson 4 of 5.
плита (plitá) — a cooker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a computer, then the Russian for a fridge.

You'll want to know: плита.
плита (plitá) — "a cooker".

The word, taken apart.
плита (plitá) — Old Russian плита (plitá), "a stone slab, a brick". Vasmer thinks it most likely a cousin of Greek plínthos, "brick"; the common view takes it as a loan from that Greek word. A плита (plitá) is the slab you cook on.
