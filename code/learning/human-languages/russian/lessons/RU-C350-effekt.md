## You'll want to know: эффект
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C350-POSLEDSTVIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**эффект** (*effékt*) — "an effect".

> Новое лекарство дало хороший эффект.
>
> *Nóvoye lekárstvo dálo khoróshiy effékt.*
>
> The new medicine had a good effect.

Masculine. *Произвести эффект* — to make an impression.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**эффект** comes from Latin *effectus* "an effect" (EtymDB), from *efficere* "to bring about", built from *ex* "out" and *facere* "to do, to make" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
