## You'll want to know: a âncora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C420-TERRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a âncora** — "the anchor".

> O barco lançou a âncora perto da praia.
>
> *The boat dropped anchor near the beach.*

In Brazil, *o âncora* is also a TV news anchor; Portugal says *o pivô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âncora* is inherited from Latin *ancora*, which came from Greek *ánkyra* 'anchor' (EtymDB).
