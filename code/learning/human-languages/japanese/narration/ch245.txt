Japanese, chapter 245: Glad, Lonely and Laughing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
うれしい (ureshii) — glad; pleased.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to welcome, then the Japanese for marriage.

You'll want to know: うれしい.
うれしい — ureshii — "glad; pleased".
この おくりものは うれしいですか。 — kono okurimono wa ureshii desu ka — "Are you pleased with this present?"

The word, taken apart.
うれしい (ureshii) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 5 of 5.
えがお (egao) — a smile; a smiling face.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be delighted, then the Japanese for to laugh.

You'll want to know: えがお.
えがお — egao — "a smile; a smiling face".
そぼは いつも えがおですか。 — sobo wa itsumo egao desu ka — "Is my grandmother always smiling?"

The word, taken apart.
えがお (egao) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from e, from emu, "to smile", and kao, "face", with k voiced to g (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
