## You'll want to know: تأمين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C416-BADAL416-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تأمين** — *taʾmīn* — "insurance".

> عندي تأمين صحي.
>
> *ʿindī taʾmīn ṣiḥḥī.*
>
> I have health insurance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تأمين** (*taʾmīn*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-m-n*, "to be safe, secure" (Lane). *Taʾmīn* is the Form II verbal noun, making safe: insurance. آمن, safe, is from the same root.
