## You'll want to know: じゅけんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C515-EXAM515-01]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅけんする** — *juken suru* — "to sit an entrance exam".

> **もし らいねん じゅけんしたら、まいにち べんきょうします。** — *moshi rainen jukenshitara, mainichi benkyō shimasu* — "If I sit the entrance exam next year, I will study every day."

Polite **じゅけんします**, *jukenshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **むすこが ふたつの がっこうを じゅけんしました。**, *musuko ga futatsu no gakkō o jukenshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅけんする** (*juken suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *juken* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ju*, "accept" (Middle Chinese *dzyuwX*) and *ken*, "verification" (*ngjemH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
