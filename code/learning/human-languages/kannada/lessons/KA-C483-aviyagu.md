# ಆವಿಯಾಗು (āviyāgu) — to evaporate

## You'll want to know: ಆವಿಯಾಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C483-JALASHAYA483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆವಿಯಾಗು** — *āviyāgu* — "to evaporate".

> ಬಿಸಿಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆರೆಯ ನೀರು ಬೇಗ ಆವಿಯಾಯಿತು.
>
> *bisilinalli kereya nīru bēga āviyāyitu.*
>
> In the sun the water of the lake soon evaporated.

Past (it …): ಆವಿಯಾಯಿತು, *āviyāyitu*.

ಆವಿ is steam or vapour; ಆಗು, to become.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆವಿಯಾಗು** (*āviyāgu*) is native: DEDR 393 lists Kannada *āvi*, *āviri*, "steam, vapour, heat", here joined with ಆಗು, to become.
