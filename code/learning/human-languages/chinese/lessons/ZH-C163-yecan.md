## You'll want to know: yěcān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C163-WEEKEND163-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yěcān* — "to have a picnic; a picnic".

> *zhōumò wǒmen qù gōngyuán yěcān* — This weekend we're going to the park for a picnic.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yěcān* is built from *yě* 'open country' and *cān* 'meal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yaeX* and *tshan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second 'to swallow'. A meal out in the fields.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
