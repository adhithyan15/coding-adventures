Mandarin Chinese, chapter 501: Winners.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
yùndòngyuán — athlete.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the score of a game, then the Mandarin for to take the field.

You'll want to know: yùndòngyuán.
yùndòngyuán — "athlete".
wǒ xiǎoshíhou xiǎng dāng yùndòngyuán — When I was a kid I wanted to be an athlete.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yùndòngyuán is built from yùndòng 'sport' and yuán 'a member', all inherited from Middle Chinese, hjunH, duwngX and hjwen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move' and the third 'a count of things'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
guànjūn — champion.

You'll want to know: guànjūn.
guànjūn — "champion".
tā dāng guo liǎng cì guànjūn — He's been champion twice.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
guànjūn is built from guàn 'to put on a cap' and jūn 'an army', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwanH and kjun (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tie on the cap' and the second 'a body of troops'. How the pair came to mean 'champion' is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
球星 (qiúxīng) — star player; sports star.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a gold medal, then the Mandarin for to win (a prize).

You'll want to know: 球星.
球星 — qiúxīng — "star player; sports star".
那个球星今天没有来比赛 nàge qiúxīng jīntiān méiyǒu lái bǐsài — That star player didn't come to play today.

The word, taken apart.
球星 (qiúxīng) is built from 球 'a ball' and 星 'a star', both inherited from Middle Chinese, gjuw and seng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the stars'.
