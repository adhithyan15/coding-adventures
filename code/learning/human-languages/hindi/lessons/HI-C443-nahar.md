## You'll want to know: नहर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C443-NAHAR443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नहर** — *nahar* — "a canal".

> इस नहर से खेतों को पानी मिलता है।
>
> *is nahar se khetoṁ ko pānī miltā hai.*
>
> The fields get their water from this canal.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नहर** (*nahar*) is usually traced to Arabic *nahr*, "a river, a stream", through Persian; Hindi breaks the final hr with a vowel and uses it for a channel dug by people.
