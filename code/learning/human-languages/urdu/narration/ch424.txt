Urdu, chapter 424: Goods and Sacks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مال (māl) — goods (for sale); stock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a discount, then the Urdu for a sale (at low prices).

You'll want to know: مال.
مال — māl — "goods (for sale); stock".
دکان میں نیا مال آیا۔ dukān meṅ nayā māl āyā. New stock has come into the shop.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مال (māl) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic māl, "property, wealth"; in a shop it is the goods for sale.

ذخیرہ — zakhīrā — "a stockpile; a store (of goods)".
دکاندار کے پاس چینی کا بڑا ذخیرہ ہے۔ dukāndār ke pās chīnī kā baṛā zakhīrā hai. The shopkeeper has a big stock of sugar.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ذخیرہ (zakhīrā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic dhakhīra, "a store, provisions"; Urdu says its ذ as a z.

Lesson 3 of 5.
بوری (borī) — a sack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for goods (for sale), then the Urdu for a stockpile.

You'll want to know: بوری.
بوری — borī — "a sack".
مزدور نے چاول کی بوری اٹھائی۔ mazdūr ne chāval kī borī uṭhāī. The labourer lifted the sack of rice.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بوری (borī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins borā, "a gunny bag", and borī under bōra, "sacking, a sack", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9320).

Lesson 5 of 5.
لپیٹنا (lapeṭnā) — to wrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a sack, then the Urdu for a packet.

You'll want to know: لپیٹنا.
لپیٹنا — lapeṭnā — "to wrap".
دکاندار نے کتاب کاغذ میں لپیٹی۔ dukāndār ne kitāb kāghaz meṅ lapeṭī. The shopkeeper wrapped the book in paper.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے تحفہ لپیٹا, us ne tohfā lapeṭā.

The word, taken apart.
لپیٹنا (lapeṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lapeṭnā, "to wrap, to fold", under lappēṭṭ, "wrap", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10942).
