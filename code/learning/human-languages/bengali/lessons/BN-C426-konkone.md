# কনকনে (kônkône) — bitterly cold; biting (of cold)

## You'll want to know: কনকনে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C426-THANDA426-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কনকনে** — *kônkône* — "bitterly cold; biting (of cold)".

> কনকনে ঠান্ডায় আমার হাত জমে গেল।
>
> *kônkône ṭhānḍāy āmār hāt jôme gelô.*
>
> In the biting cold my hands went numb.

Only of cold: কনকনে ঠান্ডা, কনকনে হাওয়া.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কনকনে** (*kônkône*) is an expressive word whose sound carries the sting of the cold; its origin is not settled.

One more word for getting around.
