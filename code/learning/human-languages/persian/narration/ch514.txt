Persian, chapter 514: Ball Games.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
والیبال (vâlibâl) — volleyball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to set a new best, then the Persian for to score a goal.

You'll want to know: والیبال.
والیبال (vâlibâl) — "volleyball".
دانشجوها در سالن والیبال بازی میکنند. dâneshjuhâ dar sâlon vâlibâl bâzi mikonand. The students play volleyball in the hall.
vâlibâl bâzi kardan — to play volleyball.

The word, taken apart.
vâlibâl is a modern loan, usually traced to English volleyball. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
دروازه بان (darvâzebân) — a goalkeeper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for tennis, then the Persian for a racket.

You'll want to know: دروازه بان.
دروازه بان (darvâzebân) — "a goalkeeper".
دروازه بان توپ را گرفت. darvâzebân tup râ gereft. The goalkeeper caught the ball.
darvâze 'gate, goal' + -bân 'keeper'.

The word, taken apart.
darvâzebân is built on native Persian parts: darvâze, 'gate', usually linked with dar, 'door', and the ending -bân, 'keeper', as in bâghbân. EtymDB has no entry for it.
