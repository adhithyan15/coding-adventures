Arabic, chapter 51: Hair, Foot, Finger, Shoulder, Neck.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شعر (shaʿr) — hair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a mouth, then the Arabic for a tooth.

You'll want to know: شعر.
شعر — shaʿr — "hair".

The word, taken apart.
شعر (shaʿr) is built on the native Arabic root sh-ʿ-r, "hair; to perceive" (Lane). The same root gives shiʿr, "poetry"; Hebrew seʿar, "hair", is usually counted a cousin.

قدم (qadam) is built on the native Arabic root q-d-m, "to go before, to precede" (Lane). Lane gives qadam, the foot, under this root, which also gives qadīm, "old".
