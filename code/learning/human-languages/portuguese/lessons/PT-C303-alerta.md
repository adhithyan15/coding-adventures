## You'll want to know: o alerta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C303-CHEIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o alerta** — "the alert, the warning".

> O governo deu o alerta vermelho.
>
> *The government issued a red alert.*

*Estar alerta* — to be on guard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alerta* was borrowed from Italian *all’erta* "on the lookout" (EtymDB), from *erta* "a height, a watchtower".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
