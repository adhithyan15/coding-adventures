## You'll want to know: 里
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C81-CITY81-01]; assesses=[] -->

**里** — *lǐ* — "inside; in".

> **山里有马** *shān lǐ yǒu mǎ* — There are horses up in the hills.

The new character, in pieces: 里 — 日 with a vertical running down through it, standing on two bars. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**里** (*lǐ*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *liX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'inside' and, through an older book, 'the inside of a garment'.
