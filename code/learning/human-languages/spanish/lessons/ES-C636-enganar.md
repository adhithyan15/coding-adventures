## You'll want to know: engañar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C636-VERDAD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**engañar** — "to deceive, to cheat".

> El vendedor me engañó.
>
> *The seller cheated me.*

*Engañar a* someone. It can also mean to be unfaithful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*engañar* is usually traced to spoken Latin *ingannare* 'to mock, to trick'; EtymDB has no parent for it, and the origin is not settled.
