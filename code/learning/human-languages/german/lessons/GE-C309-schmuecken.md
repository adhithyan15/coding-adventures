## You'll want to know: schmücken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C309-SCHMUECKEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**schmücken** — "to decorate, to adorn".

> Wir schmückten den Saal mit Blumen.
>
> *We decorated the hall with flowers.*

*Der Schmuck* — jewellery or decoration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schmücken* is usually traced to Middle High German *smücken*, which EtymDB glosses "to dress, to adorn".
