## You'll want to know: رسم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C340-EYDI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رسم** (*rasm*) — "a custom, a tradition".

> در خانواده ما رسم است که شب عید همه دور هم جمع شوند.
>
> *Dar khânevâde-ye mâ rasm ast ke shab-e 'eyd hame dor-e ham jam' shavand.*
>
> In our family it is the custom for everyone to get together on New Year's Eve.

Broken plural *rosum*: *rasm-o rosum* "customs and traditions". In Arabic the word means "a drawing".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rasm* is borrowed from Arabic *rasm*, "a drawing, a picture" (EtymDB); in Persian it came to mean a custom, a traced-out way of doing things.
