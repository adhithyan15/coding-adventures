# ઊંઘવું (ū̃ghvũ) — to be asleep; to sleep soundly

## You'll want to know: ઊંઘવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C398-DOZE398-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંઘવું** — *ū̃ghvũ* — "to be asleep; to sleep soundly".

> ટ્રેન ચાલતી હતી ત્યારે બાળકો ઊંઘતાં હતાં.
>
> *ṭren cāltī hatī tyāre bāḷko ū̃ghtā̃ hatā̃.*
>
> While the train was moving the children were asleep.

Past (he …): તે આખી રાત ઊંઘ્યો, *te ākhī rāt ū̃ghyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંઘવું** (*ū̃ghvũ*) goes back to *\*uṅghati*, "sleeps", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 1632). ઊંઘ, sleep, is the noun beside it.
