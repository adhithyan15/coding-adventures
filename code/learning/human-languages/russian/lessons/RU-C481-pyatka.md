## You'll want to know: пятка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C481-BACK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**пятка** (*pyátka*) — "a heel".

> Новые туфли натёрли мне пятку.
>
> *Nóvyye túfli natyórli mne pyátku.*
>
> My new shoes have rubbed my heel.

Feminine. A diminutive in form of the old word *пята*, now the everyday word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пятка* is a diminutive in form of *пята* 'heel', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *pęta* 'heel' (EtymDB).
