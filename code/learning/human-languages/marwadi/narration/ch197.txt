Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 197: A Call and a Message.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
फोन (phon) — a phone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an address, then the Marwadi for to send off.

You'll want to know: फोन.
फोन — phon — "a phone".
फोन मेज माथै है। phon mej māthai hai. The phone is on the table.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
फोन (phon) is borrowed from English phone, short for telephone.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Lesson 3 of 6.
बाजणो (bājṇo) — to ring; to sound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a phone, then the Marwadi for a phone number.

You'll want to know: बाजणो.
बाजणो — bājṇo — "to ring; to sound".
फोन बाजै है। phon bājai hai. The phone is ringing.
Past (it …): वो फोन बाज्यो, vo phon bājyo.

The word, taken apart.
बाजणो (bājṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vādyate, "is made to sound"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb bājaï, "to sound" (CDIAL 11513).

Lesson 4 of 6.
संदेसो (sandeso) — a message.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a phone number, then the Marwadi for to ring.

You'll want to know: संदेसो.
संदेसो — sandeso — "a message".
म्हैं संदेसो सुण्यो। mhaĩ sandeso suṇyo. I listened to the message.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
संदेसो (sandeso) is inherited from Sanskrit sandeśa, "message, information". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi sãdesā, "news" (CDIAL 12904).

Lesson 5 of 6.
मिटावणो (miṭāvṇo) — to rub out; to delete.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to ring, then the Marwadi for a message.

You'll want to know: मिटावणो.
मिटावणो — miṭāvṇo — "to rub out; to delete".
संदेसो मत मिटावो। sandeso mat miṭāvo. Don't delete the message.
Past (I …): म्हैं संदेसो मिटायो, mhaĩ sandeso miṭāyo.

The word, taken apart.
मिटावणो (miṭāvṇo) is the causative of a verb inherited from Sanskrit mṛṣṭa, "rubbed, wiped"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari miṭaï, "to be rubbed out" (CDIAL 10299).
