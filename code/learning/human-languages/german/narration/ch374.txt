German, chapter 374: Sich entschließen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
entschließen — to make up your mind (reflexive, sich entschließen).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the forecast, then the German for to allow for.

You'll want to know: entschließen.
entschließen — "to make up your mind (reflexive, sich entschließen)".
Nach langem Nachdenken hat sie sich entschlossen, in eine größere Stadt zu ziehen. After thinking it over for a long time, she made up her mind to move to a bigger city.
Reflexive and strong: sie entschließt sich, sie hat sich entschlossen. Entschlossen — determined.

The word, taken apart.
entschließen is built with ent- on schließen "to close, to conclude", inherited from Old High German sliozan and Proto-Germanic sleutaną (EtymDB): you close a question.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Entschluss is formed from entschließen (EtymDB), built on schließen "to close", Old High German sliozan, Proto-Germanic sleutaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Ausweg — the way out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to make up your mind, then the German for the resolution.

You'll want to know: der Ausweg.
der Ausweg — "the way out".
Es gibt immer einen Ausweg. There is always a way out.
Plural: die Auswege. Der letzte Ausweg — the last resort.

The word, taken apart.
Ausweg joins aus "out" to Weg "way", inherited from Old High German weg and Proto-Germanic wegaz "road, path" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
flexibel — flexible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the resolution, then the German for the way out.

You'll want to know: flexibel.
flexibel — "flexible".
Bei der Arbeitszeit bin ich ganz flexibel. I am very flexible about working hours.
With an ending the e drops out: flexible Arbeitszeiten.

The word, taken apart.
flexibel is a learned borrowing from Latin flexibilis "that may be bent, pliant" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from flectere "to bend".

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Planung — the planning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the way out, then the German for flexible.

You'll want to know: die Planung.
die Planung — "the planning".
Die Planung der Hochzeit hat ein Jahr gedauert. Planning the wedding took a year.
Plural: die Planungen. In Planung — at the planning stage.

The word, taken apart.
Planung is the noun of planen, from Plan, borrowed from French plan and going back to Latin plānus "level, flat" (EtymDB).
