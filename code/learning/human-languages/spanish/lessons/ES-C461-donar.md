# donar — to give it away

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-PRAGMATICS-SORRY-03, ES-LEX-C300-BODY-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **perdón** and **la sangre**. There is a verb of giving hiding
in the first, and the second is the commonest thing it is used about.

## You'll want to know: donar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C461-DONAR-01]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C300-BODY-04] -->

**donar** — to donate.

*Donar sangre* — to give blood, the sign outside every mobile clinic. *Donar
ropa* — to donate clothes. **La donación** is the donation and **el donante**
the donor.

It is not the everyday verb for giving. *Dar* is that. *Donar* is giving to an
institution or a cause, and it carries no expectation of return.

## Grammar Lens: donar is not regalar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C461-DONAR-01, ES-LEX-C460-PEDIR-01] -->

Three verbs of giving, and the difference is who receives:

| what is happening | the verb |
|---|---|
| handing anything to anyone | **dar** |
| making somebody a present | **regalar** |
| giving to a cause or a body | **donar** |

*Te doy el taladro* — I'm passing it to you. *Te regalo el taladro* — it is
yours, a gift. *Dono el taladro al centro* — the neighbourhood centre has it
now.

Use *donar* about a person and it sounds like a charitable act rather than a
kindness, which is sometimes exactly the joke a Spanish speaker is making.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C461-DONAR-01, ES-PRAGMATICS-SORRY-03] -->

Latin **donare**, to give — and here is the payoff on a word you learned in
your first weeks.

**Perdón** is *per-* plus *donare*: to **give thoroughly**, to give completely
away. Forgiving somebody is giving the offence away rather than holding it, and
the metaphor is the same in English — *for-give* is built the identical way,
*for-* being an intensifier, so *pardon* and *forgive* are the same idea twice.

The family also gives *el don*, a gift or a talent, and **la dosis**, through
Greek — a dose is what is *given* to you.
