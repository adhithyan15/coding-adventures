## You'll want to know: o falcão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C460-SELVAGENS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o falcão** — "the falcon".

> Um falcão voa por cima do campo.
>
> *A falcon is flying over the field.*

Very fast when it dives.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*falcão* is inherited from Late Latin *falcō, falcōnis* 'falcon' (EtymDB), often linked with *falx* 'sickle' for its curved claws.
