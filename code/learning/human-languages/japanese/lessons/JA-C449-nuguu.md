## You'll want to know: ぬぐう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C449-BURN449-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬぐう** — *nuguu* — "to wipe away".

> **かれは あせを ぬぐいました。** — *kare wa ase o nuguimashita* — "He wiped away the sweat."

Polite **ぬぐいます**, *nuguimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せんしゅが なみだを ぬぐいました。**, *senshu ga namida o nuguimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬぐう** (*nuguu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
