## You'll want to know: زين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C552-TARMIM552-03]; assesses=[] -->

**زين** — *zayyana* — "to decorate".

> زين الأطفال الغرفة للعيد.
>
> *zayyana al-aṭfāl al-ghurfa li-l-ʿīd.*
>
> The children decorated the room for the feast.

Present: يزين, *yuzayyin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زين** (*zayyana*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-y-n*, "to adorn, to beautify" (Lane). Zīna, decorations, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
