Arabic, chapter 136: Crocodile, Mosquito, Fly, Bee, Butterfly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تمساح (timsāḥ) — a crocodile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a duck, then the Arabic for a turtle.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — read: each word whole, right to left — سلام، شكرًا، شاي]

You'll want to know: تمساح.
تمساح — timsāḥ — "a crocodile".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: timsāḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: timsāḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say timsāḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a duck, then the Arabic for a turtle, then say timsāḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does تمساح (timsāḥ) mean? ("A crocodile".) Say it once more.
