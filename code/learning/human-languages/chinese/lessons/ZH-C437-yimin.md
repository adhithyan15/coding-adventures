## You'll want to know: yímín
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C437-LAND437-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yímín* — "to emigrate; to settle abroad".

> *tāmen quánjiā yímín qù le guówài* — Their whole family emigrated abroad.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yímín* is built from *yí* 'to move' and *mín* 'the people', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ye* and *mjin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to shift' and the second 'the common people'.
