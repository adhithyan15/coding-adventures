## You'll want to know: सेहत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C107-DOCTOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सेहत** (*sehat*) — "health", the long-term kind.

**तबीयत** is how you feel today; **सेहत** is how healthy you are. **सेहत अच्छी है** — "the health
is good".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A doctor, a hospital, an injury, weakness, and health itself.
