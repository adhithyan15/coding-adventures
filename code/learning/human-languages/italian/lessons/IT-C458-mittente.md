## You'll want to know: il mittente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C458-POSTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il mittente** — "the sender".

> Il mittente di questa lettera è mia zia.
>
> *The sender of this letter is my aunt.*

The person you send to is *il destinatario*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mittente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mittēns* 'sending', from *mittere* 'to send' (Lewis & Short).
