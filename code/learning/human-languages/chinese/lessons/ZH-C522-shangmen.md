## You'll want to know: 上门
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C522-FIXIT522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**上门** — *shàngmén* — "to call at the house (to do a job)".

> *xiū kōngtiáo de shīfu míngtiān shàngmén* — The man who fixes air conditioners is coming round tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**上门** (*shàngmén*) is built from 上 'to go up' and 门 'a door', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangX* and *mwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to climb, to rise': to come up to someone's door.
