## You'll want to know: der Einfluss
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C344-EINFLUSS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Einfluss** — "the influence".

> Meine Großmutter hatte einen großen Einfluss auf mich.
>
> *My grandmother had a great influence on me.*

Plural: *die Einflüsse*. *Einfluss auf* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Einfluss* joins *ein* "in" to *Fluss* "flow, river". It is usually explained as a rendering of Latin *influxus* "a flowing in", which late Latin also used of the influence of the stars (Lewis & Short); EtymDB has no history for it.
