## You'll want to know: पर्णशाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C402-DHARMASHALA402-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्णशाला** — *parṇaśālā* — "a hut of leaves".

> परिव्राजकः वने पर्णशालायां वसति।
>
> *parivrājakaḥ vane parṇaśālāyāṃ vasati.*
>
> The wandering ascetic lives in a hut of leaves in the forest.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्णशाला** (*parṇaśālā*) is built from *parṇa*, "a leaf", and *śālā*, "a hall, a house"; MW gives the compound as a hut of leaves, an arbour (MW).
