## You'll want to know: guìzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C175-FURN175-04]; assesses=[] -->

*guìzi* — "cupboard; cabinet".

> *bēizi zài guìzi lǐ* — The cups are in the cupboard.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guìzi* is built from *guì* 'a chest' and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gwijH* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). In the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
