## You'll want to know: تناقض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C362-MANTIQ362-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تناقض** — *tanāquḍ* — "a contradiction".

> هناك تناقض بين كلامه وعمله.
>
> *hunāka tanāquḍ bayna kalāmihi wa-ʿamalih.*
>
> There is a contradiction between his words and his deeds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تناقض** (*tanāquḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-ḍ*, "to undo, to unravel" (Lane). It is the Form VI verbal noun: two things that undo each other, a contradiction.
