## You'll want to know: ਮਾਮਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C331-UNCLE331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਮਾ** — *māmā* — "a mother's brother".

> ਗਰਮੀ ਦੀ ਛੁੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਮਾਮੇ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਗਏ।
>
> *garmī dī chhuṭṭī vich asīṁ māme de ghar gae.*
>
> In the summer holidays we went to my mother's brother's house.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਮਾ** (*māmā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *māma*, "uncle", a nursery word; CDIAL lists Punjabi *māmmā* under it (CDIAL 10055).
