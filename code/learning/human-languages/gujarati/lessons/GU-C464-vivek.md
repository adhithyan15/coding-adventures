## You'll want to know: વિવેક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C464-SANU464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વિવેક** — *vivek* — "good sense; courtesy".

> મહેમાન સાથે વિવેકથી બોલો.
>
> *mahemān sāthe vivekthī bolo.*
>
> Speak to guests with courtesy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિવેક** (*vivek*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *viveka*, "discrimination, consideration", as Monier-Williams records it; Gujarati uses it for good sense and good manners.
