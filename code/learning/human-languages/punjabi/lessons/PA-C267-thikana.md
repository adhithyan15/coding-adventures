# ਠਿਕਾਣਾ (ṭhikāṇā) — whereabouts; a place to stay

## You'll want to know: ਠਿਕਾਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C267-OFFICE267-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠਿਕਾਣਾ** — *ṭhikāṇā* — "whereabouts; a place to stay".

> ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰਾ ਕੋਈ ਠਿਕਾਣਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *shahir vich merā koī ṭhikāṇā nahīṁ hai.*
>
> I have no place to stay in the city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠਿਕਾਣਾ** (*ṭhikāṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭhīkka*, "firm, right"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭhikāṇā*, "station", under it (CDIAL 5503).
