## You'll want to know: કસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C514-DHILU514-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કસવું** — *kasvũ* — "to tighten".

> અરુણે ખુરશીના બધા સ્ક્રૂ કસ્યા.
>
> *Aruṇe khurśīnā badhā skrū kasyā.*
>
> Arun tightened all the screws of the chair.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સ્ક્રૂ કસ્યો, *teṇe skrū kasyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કસવું** (*kasvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kárṣati*, "draws, pulls", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to tighten" (CDIAL 2908).
