## You'll want to know: choramingar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C474-RABUGENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**choramingar** — "to whine, to whimper".

> O cão choramingava à porta.
>
> *The dog was whimpering at the door.*

Built on *chorar* "to cry": a small, whining cry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*choramingar* is built on *chorar* "to cry", which is inherited from Latin *plōrāre* "to wail" (Lewis & Short).
