Sanskrit, chapter 287: The Chase.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अनुधावति (anudhāvati) — runs after; chases.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a witness, then the Sanskrit for runs away.

You'll want to know: अनुधावति.
अनुधावति — anudhāvati — "runs after; chases".
यामिकः चौरम् अनुधावति। yāmikaḥ cauram anudhāvati. The watchman runs after the thief.
Past (he …): सः चौरम् अनुधावितवान्, saḥ cauram anudhāvitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुधावति (anudhāvati) is built from anu, "after", and the root √dhāv, "to run", which also gives dhāvati, "runs"; MW gives anu-dhāv as to run after or pursue (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
वेगः (vegaḥ) — speed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for runs away, then the Sanskrit for runs after.

You'll want to know: वेगः.
वेगः — vegaḥ — "speed".
यामिकः वेगेन धावति। yāmikaḥ vegena dhāvati. The watchman runs at speed.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वेगः (vegaḥ) comes from the root √vij, "to move with a quick, darting motion"; MW gives vega as a violent rush and also as speed or velocity (MW).

उपसर्पति (upasarpati) is built from upa, "towards", and the root √sṛp, "to creep"; MW gives upa-sṛp as to creep towards or approach softly (MW).

यामिकः सर्वां रात्रिं जागर्ति। yāmikaḥ sarvāṃ rātriṃ jāgarti. The watchman stays awake all night.

The word, taken apart.
जागर्ति (jāgarti) comes from the root √jāgṛ, "to be awake, watch"; MW compares Greek egeirō and Latin vigilo (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
कोलाहलः (kolāhalaḥ) — an uproar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for creeps up to, then the Sanskrit for stays awake.

You'll want to know: कोलाहलः.
कोलाहलः — kolāhalaḥ — "an uproar".
यदि कोलाहलः अभविष्यत्, तर्हि यामिकः आगमिष्यत्। yadi kolāhalaḥ abhaviṣyat, tarhi yāmikaḥ āgamiṣyat. If there had been an uproar, then the watchman would have come.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कोलाहलः (kolāhalaḥ) comes from the sound itself: MW marks kolāhala as onomatopoeic, a loud and confused noise of people or animals (MW).
