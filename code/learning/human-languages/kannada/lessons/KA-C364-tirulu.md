## You'll want to know: ತಿರುಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C364-DINACHARI364-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಿರುಳು** — *tiruḷu* — "the gist; the core".

> ಅವನ ಮಾತಿನ ತಿರುಳು ಇಷ್ಟೇ, ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಸೇರಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡಬೇಕು.
>
> *avana mātina tiruḷu iṣṭē, ellarū sēri kelasa māḍabēku.*
>
> The gist of his talk was this: everyone must work together.

Also the kernel of a fruit or a nut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಿರುಳು** (*tiruḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3252 lists Kannada *tiruḷ*, *tiruḷe*, "the essential part, strength, value".
