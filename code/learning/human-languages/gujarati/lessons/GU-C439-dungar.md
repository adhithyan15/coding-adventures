## You'll want to know: ડુંગર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C439-HILL439-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ડુંગર** — *ḍungar* — "a rocky hill; a hill".

> ગામની પાછળ મોટો ડુંગર છે.
>
> *gāmnī pāchaḷ moṭo ḍungar che.*
>
> There is a big hill behind the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડુંગર** (*ḍungar*) goes back to a group of forms CDIAL reconstructs for "a hill", *\*ṭakka* and *\*ṭuṅga*; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a hillock" (CDIAL 5423).
