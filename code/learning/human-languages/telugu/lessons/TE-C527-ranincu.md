# రాణించు (rāṇiñcu) — to excel; to shine

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: రాణించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C527-VIJAYAM527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**రాణించు** — *rāṇiñcu* — "to excel; to shine".

> మా అమ్మాయి చదువులో బాగా రాణిస్తోంది.
>
> *mā ammāyi caduvulō bāgā rāṇistōndi.*
>
> Our daughter is doing very well at her studies.

Past (I …): రాణించాను, *rāṇiñcānu*.

-లో రాణించు, to excel at.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రాణించు** (*rāṇiñcu*) is a Telugu verb for doing well and standing out; its origin is not settled.

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
