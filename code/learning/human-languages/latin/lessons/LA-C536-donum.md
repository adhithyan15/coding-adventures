## You'll want to know: dōnum, dōnī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C536-BLAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dōnum, dōnī** — "a gift".

> Amīcus mihi dōnum pulchrum mittit.
>
> *My friend sends me a beautiful gift.*

Neuter, second declension, from *dō* — to give. English *donation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dōnum** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European word for "gift", built on the root of *dō* "to give" (EtymDB). English *donor* and *donation* come from the family.
