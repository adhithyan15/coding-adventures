## You'll want to know: सौदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C431-SAUDA431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सौदा** — *saudā* — "a deal; shopping".

> हमने दुकानदार से अच्छा सौदा किया।
>
> *hamne dukāndār se acchā saudā kiyā.*
>
> We got a good deal from the shopkeeper.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौदा** (*saudā*) is usually traced to Persian *saudā*, "trade, a bargain"; सौदा-सुलफ़ is the day's shopping.
