Marathi, chapter 159: Recently, Formerly, Already, the Past, To Happen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अलीकडे (alīkaḍe) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to obtain, then the Marathi for to save.

You'll want to know: अलीकडे.
अलीकडे — alīkaḍe — "recently". Marathi's past ends in -ले, -लो or -ली: अलीकडे मी एक पुस्तक वाचले. — alīkaḍe mī ek pustak vācle — "Recently I read a book."

Lesson 2 of 5.
पूर्वी (pūrvī) — formerly, before.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to save, then the Marathi for recently.

You'll want to know: पूर्वी.
पूर्वी — pūrvī — "formerly, before". पूर्वी इथे एक दुकान होते. — pūrvī ithe ek dukān hote — "There used to be a shop here." होते is the past of आहे.

Lesson 3 of 5.
आधीच (ādhīc) — already.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for recently, then the Marathi for formerly.

You'll want to know: आधीच.
आधीच — ādhīc — "already". आधी, "before", with the emphatic च: मी आधीच जेवलो. — mī ādhīc jevlo — "I have already eaten." A woman says जेवले.

Lesson 4 of 5.
भूतकाळ (bhūtkāḷ) — the past.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for formerly, then the Marathi for already.

You'll want to know: भूतकाळ.
भूतकाळ — bhūtkāḷ — "the past". It is भूत, "what has been", with काळ, "time". Grammar books use it for the past tense too.

Lesson 5 of 5.

घडणे — ghaḍṇe — "to happen". काय घडले? — kāy ghaḍle — "What happened?" Its past is घडले.
