## You'll want to know: شہید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C311-FATEH311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شہید** — *shahīd* — "a martyr".

> جنگ میں دو سو سپاہی شہید ہوئے۔
>
> *jang meṅ do sau sipāhī shahīd hue.*
>
> Two hundred soldiers fell in the war.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شہید** (*shahīd*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shahīd*, "a witness", and so one who dies for a cause. **شہید ہوئے** is said of soldiers killed in battle.
