Urdu, chapter 363: Hopes and Fears.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اندیشہ (andeshā) — an apprehension; a misgiving.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for conjecture, then the Urdu for to work out a riddle.

You'll want to know: اندیشہ.
اندیشہ — andeshā — "an apprehension; a misgiving".
اگر بارش ہوئی تو سیلاب کا اندیشہ ہے۔ agar bārish huī to sailāb kā andeshā hai. If it rains, there is a fear of floods.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اندیشہ (andeshā) is usually traced to Persian andēsha, "thought, anxiety": a thought that worries.

Lesson 4 of 5.
خدشہ (khadshā) — a nagging fear (that something may happen).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a hint, then the Urdu for a symptom.

You'll want to know: خدشہ.
خدشہ — khadshā — "a nagging fear (that something may happen)".
اگر دیر ہوئی تو گاڑی چھوٹنے کا خدشہ ہے۔ agar der huī to gāṛī chhūṭne kā khadshā hai. If we are late, we may miss the train.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
خدشہ (khadshā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khadsha, "a scratch"; Urdu uses it for a fear that something may go wrong, a sense our sources do not trace.
