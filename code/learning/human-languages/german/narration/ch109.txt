German, chapter 109: Up, Down, At the front, At the back, Opposite.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
oben — up, upstairs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the west, then the German for straight on.

You'll want to know: oben.
oben — "up, upstairs".

The word, taken apart.
oben from Old High German obana, "from above" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited, from the same root as English over and above.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for at the front, then the German for at the back.

You'll want to know: gegenüber.
gegenüber — "opposite".

The word, taken apart.
gegenüber is the word for "toward, against", Old High German gagan, + the word for "over", Old High German ubiri (EtymDB 2.1): "over against" you.
