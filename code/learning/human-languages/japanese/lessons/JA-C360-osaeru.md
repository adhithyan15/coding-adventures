# おさえる (osaeru) — to keep down (costs); to hold down

## You'll want to know: おさえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C360-SHUUNYUU360-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おさえる** — *osaeru* — "to keep down (costs); to hold down".

> **ねだんが あがったら、ししゅつを おさえた ほうが いいです。** — *nedan ga agattara, shishutsu o osaeta hō ga ii desu* — "If prices go up, we had better keep our spending down."

Polite **おさえます**, *osaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ははが ししゅつを おさえました。**, *haha ga shishutsu o osaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おさえる** (*osaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
