## You'll want to know: رنجش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C491-MENDING491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رنجش** — *ranjish* — "ill feeling; a grudge".

> دونوں بھائیوں میں رنجش ہے۔
>
> *donoṅ bhāiyoṅ meṅ ranjish hai.*
>
> There is ill feeling between the two brothers.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رنجش** (*ranjish*) is usually traced to Persian *ranjish*, "offence, vexation".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
