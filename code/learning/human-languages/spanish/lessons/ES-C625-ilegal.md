## You'll want to know: ilegal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C625-LEGAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ilegal** — "illegal".

> Aparcar aquí es ilegal.
>
> *Parking here is illegal.*

The opposite of *legal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ilegal* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *illegalis*, built from *in-* 'not' and *legalis*, from *lex* 'law' (EtymDB).
