## You'll want to know: विनोदः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C457-HASA457-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विनोदः** — *vinodaḥ* — "amusement".

> विनोदाय बालाः उद्याने क्रीडन्ति।
>
> *vinodāya bālāḥ udyāne krīḍanti.*
>
> The children play in the garden for fun.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विनोदः** (*vinodaḥ*) is built from *vi* and the root √nud, "to push", the root of **नुदति**: driving away dullness; MW gives "removal" first and then "diversion, pastime, pleasure" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
