## You'll want to know: ないよう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C357-KEIYAKU357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ないよう** — *naiyō* — "the contents; the details".

> **ないようを よんだら、はんこを おして ください。** — *naiyō o yondara, hanko o oshite kudasai* — "Once you have read the contents, please stamp it with your seal."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ないよう** (*naiyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nai*, "inside" (Middle Chinese *nwojH*) and *yō*, "contain" (*yowng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
