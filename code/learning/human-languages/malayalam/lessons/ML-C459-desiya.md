## You'll want to know: ദേശീയ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C459-NIKUTI459-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ദേശീയ** — *dēśīya* — "national".

> ഇന്ന് ഒരു ദേശീയ അവധി ദിവസമാണ്.
>
> *innŭ oru dēśīya avadhi divasamāṇŭ.*
>
> Today is a national holiday.

It stands before a noun: ദേശീയ പതാക, the national flag.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ദേശീയ** (*dēśīya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *deśīya*, "belonging to a country, native" (Monier-Williams), from *deśa*, "a country".
