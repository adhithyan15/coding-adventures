Telugu, chapter 475: Rivers and Dams.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సంగమం (saṅgamaṁ) — a confluence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a volcano, then the Telugu for peaceful.

You'll want to know: సంగమం.
సంగమం — saṅgamaṁ — "a confluence".
రెండు నదుల సంగమం దగ్గర పెద్ద జాతర జరుగుతుంది. reṇḍu nadula saṅgamaṁ daggara pedda jātara jarugutundi. A big fair is held where the two rivers meet.
The place where two rivers meet; also any meeting of two things.

The word, taken apart.
సంగమం (saṅgamaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṅgama, "a coming together, a confluence of rivers" (Monier-Williams), from sam-gam, "to go together".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఆనకట్ట (ānakaṭṭa) — a dam; a weir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for peaceful, then the Telugu for a confluence.

You'll want to know: ఆనకట్ట.
ఆనకట్ట — ānakaṭṭa — "a dam; a weir".
నది మీద కొత్త ఆనకట్ట కట్టారు. nadi mīda kotta ānakaṭṭa kaṭṭāru. They built a new dam on the river.
ఆన, a bank, + కట్ట, a bund. ఆనకట్ట (ānakaṭṭa) కట్టు, to build a dam.

The word, taken apart.
ఆనకట్ట (ānakaṭṭa) is native Dravidian: ఆన, "a bank, a dam", which DEDR 122 lists as Telugu ana with a query, and కట్ట, "a bund, an embankment", from కట్టు, "to tie, to build" (DEDR 1147).

Lesson 3 of 5.
జలాశయం (jalāśayaṁ) — a reservoir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a confluence, then the Telugu for a dam.

You'll want to know: జలాశయం.
జలాశయం — jalāśayaṁ — "a reservoir".
వర్షాలకు జలాశయం పూర్తిగా నిండింది. varṣālaku jalāśayaṁ pūrtigā niṇḍindi. With the rains the reservoir filled up completely.
జల-, water, + -ఆశయం, a resting place: a place where water rests.

The word, taken apart.
జలాశయం (jalāśayaṁ) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: jala, "water", and āśaya, "a resting place, a receptacle" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
నీటిపారుదల (nīṭipārudala) — irrigation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a dam, then the Telugu for a reservoir.

You'll want to know: నీటిపారుదల.
నీటిపారుదల — nīṭipārudala — "irrigation".
ఆ ఆనకట్ట వల్ల నీటిపారుదల సులభమైంది. ā ānakaṭṭa valla nīṭipārudala sulabhamaindi. Thanks to that dam, irrigation became easy.
నీటి, of water, + పారుదల, flowing: making water flow to the fields.

The word, taken apart.
నీటిపారుదల (nīṭipārudala) is native Dravidian: నీటి, from నీరు, "water" (DEDR 3690), and పారుదల, "flowing", from పారు, "to run, to flow" (DEDR 3963).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a reservoir, then the Telugu for irrigation.

You'll want to know: చిత్తడి.
చిత్తడి — cittaḍi — "a marsh; wet ground".
నది ఒడ్డున చిత్తడి నేల ఉంది. nadi oḍḍuna cittaḍi nēla undi. There is marshy ground on the riverbank.
చిత్తడి (cittaḍi) నేల, a marsh, a wetland. As an adjective, soggy: చిత్తడిగా ఉంది.

The word, taken apart.
చిత్తడి (cittaḍi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2525 lists Telugu cittaḍi, "damp, moisture; wet".
