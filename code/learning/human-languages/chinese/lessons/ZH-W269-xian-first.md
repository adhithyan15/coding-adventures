# 先 — a cross and a long bar, over 儿

> 儿 生

[YOU READ: each one aloud, with nothing said first — *ér*, *shēng*]

## Script — a cross and a long bar, over 儿
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-XIAN-FIRST-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 先

**Two pieces, top before bottom.**

**The top piece, four strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left. A short bar to the right of it. A vertical down through the bar. Then a long bar across, wider than everything above it.

**The bottom piece, 儿, two strokes,** as on its own: a stroke falling to the left, then a vertical that bends to the right along the foot and hooks up.

4 + 2 = six.
