## You'll want to know: senão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C370-SENAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**senão** — "otherwise, or else; but (rather)".

> Temos de sair já, senão perdemos o comboio.
>
> *We have to leave now, or else we miss the train.*

One word for or else; *se não* in two words means if not: *se não chover*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*senão* is built from *se* "if", from Latin *sī*, and *não* "not", from Latin *nōn* (EtymDB): "if not, then".
