## You'll want to know: મજૂર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C511-MAJUR511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મજૂર** — *majūr* — "a labourer".

> મજૂરો સવારથી સાંજ સુધી કામ કરે છે.
>
> *majūro savārthī sānj sudhī kām kare che.*
>
> The labourers work from morning till evening.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મજૂર** (*majūr*) is usually traced to Persian *mazdūr*, "one who works for a wage".
