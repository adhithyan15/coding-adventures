## You'll want to know: offensichtlich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C338-VERMUTUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**offensichtlich** — "obviously, evident".

> Offensichtlich hat er den Brief nicht gelesen.
>
> *Obviously he has not read the letter.*

An adjective and an adverb: *ein offensichtlicher Fehler* — an obvious mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*offensichtlich* joins *offen* "open" to *Sicht* "sight", from the family of *sehen* "to see", Old High German *sehan* (EtymDB), with *-lich*: open to sight.
