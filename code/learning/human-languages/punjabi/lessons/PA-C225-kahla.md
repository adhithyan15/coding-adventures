## You'll want to know: ਕਾਹਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C225-DIN225-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਹਲਾ** — *kāhlā* — "in a hurry; hasty".

> ਸਵੇਰੇ ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਕਾਹਲਾ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *savere maiṁ bahut kāhlā hundā hāṁ.*
>
> In the mornings I am in a great hurry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਹਲਾ** (*kāhlā*) is inherited: CDIAL sets it under Sanskrit *kāhala*, "speaking indistinctly, faint-hearted", and lists Punjabi *kāhlā*, "impatient, hasty" (CDIAL 3142); CDIAL does not give the everyday sense "in a hurry" as such.
