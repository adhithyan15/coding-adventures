## You'll want to know: kāiyào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C231-DOCTOR231-05]; assesses=[] -->

*kāiyào* — "to prescribe medicine".

> *zhōngyī yě huì kāiyào* — Chinese-medicine doctors prescribe medicine too.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kāiyào* is built from 开 'to open' and *yào* 'medicine', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *yak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the sense 'to write out a prescription' is not given there.
