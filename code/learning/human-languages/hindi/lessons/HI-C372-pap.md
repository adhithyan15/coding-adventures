## You'll want to know: पाप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C372-DAYA372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पाप** — *pāp* — "a sin, a wrong".

> झूठ बोलना पाप है।
>
> *jhūṭh bolnā pāp hai.*
>
> Lying is a sin.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाप** (*pāp*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pāpa*, "evil, wicked; a sin" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
