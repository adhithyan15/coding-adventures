Sanskrit, chapter 359: Giving Up for Others.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
त्यागः (tyāgaḥ) — renunciation; giving up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the right path, then the Sanskrit for holds in regard.

You'll want to know: त्यागः.
त्यागः — tyāgaḥ — "renunciation; giving up".
सैनिकस्य त्यागः महान् भवति। sainikasya tyāgaḥ mahān bhavati. A soldier's sacrifice is great.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
त्यागः (tyāgaḥ) comes from the root √tyaj, "to leave, to abandon"; MW gives tyāga "leaving, abandoning" and "giving up, a gift" (MW).

समर्पयति (samarpayati) — dedicates; hands over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for self-interest, then the Sanskrit for craving.

You'll want to know: समर्पयति.
समर्पयति — samarpayati — "dedicates; hands over".
सः धनं विद्यालयाय समर्पयति। saḥ dhanaṃ vidyālayāya samarpayati. He hands over the money to the school.
Past (he …): सः गृहं समर्पितवान्, saḥ gṛhaṃ samarpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
समर्पयति (samarpayati) is built on sam and the root √ṛ, "to go", in its causative form arpaya, "to hand over"; MW gives the sense "to deliver over, to consign, to commit" (MW).
