## You'll want to know: a esquadra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C443-SERVICOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a esquadra** — "the police station; the squadron".

> Fui à esquadra porque me roubaram a carteira.
>
> *I went to the police station because my wallet was stolen.*

In Brazil: *a delegacia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esquadra* was borrowed from Italian *squadra* 'square, team', which goes back to Vulgar Latin *exquadrāre* 'to make square', on *quadrus* 'square' (Lewis & Short).
