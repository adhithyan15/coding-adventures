## You'll want to know: रटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C396-SANSKAR396-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रटना** — *raṭnā* — "to learn by rote, to cram".

> अगर तुम केवल रटोगे तो कुछ नहीं समझोगे।
>
> *agar tum keval raṭoge to kuch nahīṃ samjhoge.*
>
> If you only learn by rote, you won't understand anything.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पूरा पाठ रटा, *usne pūrā pāṭh raṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रटना** (*raṭnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *raṭati*, "cries, yells" (CDIAL 10590). Hindi uses the sense of saying over and over.
