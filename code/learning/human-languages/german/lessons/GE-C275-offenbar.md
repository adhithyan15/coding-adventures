## You'll want to know: offenbar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C275-ANGEBLICH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**offenbar** — "apparently, evidently".

> Er war offenbar sehr müde.
>
> *He was evidently very tired.*

It sounds sure, because there is a sign: *Offenbar hat es geregnet, die Straße ist nass*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*offenbar* joins *offen* "open", an inherited Germanic word, and the ending *-bar*: plain to see. EtymDB lists no parent for the adverb.
