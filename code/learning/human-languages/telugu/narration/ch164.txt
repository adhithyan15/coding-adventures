Telugu, chapter 164: To grate, To strain, To dip, To tear, To wrap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తురుము (turumu) — to grate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to compete, then the Telugu for to kick.

You'll want to know: తురుము.
తురుము — turumu — "to grate".

The word, taken apart.
తురుము (turumu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3339 gives it as tuṟumu, "to scrape, as a coconut", with Kannada turi, "to grate, scrape out", and Tamil turuvu, "to scrape out".

చింపు — cimpu — "to tear".

The word, taken apart.
చింపు (cimpu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 2491 lists cimpu, "to tear, rend", with Malayalam cīntuka, "to tear". DEDR compares Sanskrit cīra, "a strip of bark or cloth" (CDIAL 4843), which CDIAL reports as taken from Dravidian.
