## You'll want to know: confirmer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C421-ANNULATION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**confirmer** — "to confirm".

> L'agence a confirmé notre vol par courriel.
>
> *The agency confirmed our flight by email.*

*La confirmation* — the confirmation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*confirmer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnfirmāre* 'to make firm', from *firmus* 'firm' (EtymDB).
