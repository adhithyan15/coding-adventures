वृद्ध — vṛddha — "an elderly person".

The word, taken apart.
वृद्ध (vṛddha) is Sanskrit vṛddha, "grown, large; grown-up, old", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 12073).

Lesson 2 of 5.
जुळे (juḷe) — twins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a poem, then the Marathi for an elderly person.

You'll want to know: जुळे.
जुळे — juḷe — "twins".

The word, taken apart.
जुळे (juḷe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from yugala, "pair, couple" (CDIAL 10489). It is built on yuga, "yoke". CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "one of twins".

सासरे (sāsre) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śvaśura, "husband's father" (CDIAL 12753). CDIAL adds that the Marathi word may come instead from śvāśura.
