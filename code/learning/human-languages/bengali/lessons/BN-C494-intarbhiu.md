## You'll want to know: ইন্টারভিউ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C494-KAJKHOJA494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ইন্টারভিউ** — *inṭārbhiu* — "a job interview".

> কাল আমার একটা ইন্টারভিউ আছে।
>
> *Kāl āmār ekṭā inṭārbhiu āchhe.*
>
> I have an interview tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ইন্টারভিউ** (*inṭārbhiu*) is borrowed from English *interview*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
