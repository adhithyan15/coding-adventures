## You'll want to know: सावरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C296-DHADAK296-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सावरणे** — *sāvarṇe* — "to recover; to steady oneself".

> थोड्या वेळाने तो त्या भीतीतून सावरला.
>
> *thoḍyā veḷāne to tyā bhītītūn sāvarlā.*
>
> After a while he recovered from that fright.

Past (he …): तो सावरला, *to sāvarlā*.

The trouble takes -तून: भीतीतून सावरणे. Transitive too: स्वतःला सावरणे, to steady oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सावरणे** (*sāvarṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *sāvarṇe*, 'to gather up, recover, rectify', under *\*saṁvarati*, 'restrains' (CDIAL 13015).
