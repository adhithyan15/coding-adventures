## You'll want to know: पार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C206-LANE206-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पार** — *pār* — "across; on the other side".

> मंदिर नदी पार है।
>
> *mandir nadī pār hai.*
>
> The temple is across the river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पार** (*pār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pāra*, "the further bank". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *pār*, "on the other side of, across" (CDIAL 8100).
