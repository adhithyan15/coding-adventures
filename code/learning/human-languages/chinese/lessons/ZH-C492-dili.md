## You'll want to know: 地理

**地理** — *dìlǐ* — "geography".

> *wǒmen de dìlǐ lǎoshī hěn yǒuyìsi* — Our geography teacher is really interesting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**地理** (*dìlǐ*) is built from 地 'the land' and 理 'a pattern', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *liX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the earth' and the second 'to work jade' and 'a pattern'.
