# から / ので (kara / node) — because

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C254-OPINION-01, JA-LEX-C53-ADJ53-04, JA-LEX-C98-WHY98-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I don't think my friend will come". (*tomodachi wa konai to omoimasu*.) Then say "cold" and "a reason". (*samui*, *riyū*.)

## You'll want to know: から after a clause
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C254-REASON-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-03, JA-LEX-C41-SCHOOL41-02, JA-LEX-C152-READY152-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C251-NAI-01] -->

> **さむいから、いきません。**
>
> *samui kara, ikimasen.* — "It is cold, so I won't go."

**から** (*kara*) after a clause gives the reason, and the result follows it. The clause before it takes a plain form:

> **じかんが ないから、いそぎます。**
>
> *jikan ga nai kara, isogimasu.* — "I have no time, so I will hurry."

> **あめが ふったから、いきませんでした。**
>
> *ame ga futta kara, ikimasen deshita.* — "It rained, so I did not go."

## You'll want to know: ので, and りゆうは …からです
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C254-REASON-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ATAMA, JA-LEX-C22-BODY22-05, JA-LEX-C29-VERBS29-04] -->

**ので** (*node*) gives a reason too, more softly; it is the one to use when asking for something or excusing yourself:

> **あたまが いたいので、やすみます。**
>
> *atama ga itai node, yasumimasu.* — "My head hurts, so I will rest."

When someone asks why, **りゆうは** (*riyū wa*), "the reason is", opens the answer, and **からです** closes it:

> **りゆうは じかんが ないからです。**
>
> *riyū wa jikan ga nai kara desu.* — "The reason is that I have no time."
