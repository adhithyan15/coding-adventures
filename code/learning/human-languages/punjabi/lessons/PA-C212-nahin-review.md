# Say what you don't do

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212] -->

[YOU HEAR: *I don't drink tea*] Say it with the ending that fits you.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-LEX-ROTI, PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *she doesn't eat roti*] Say **ਉਹ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *I don't go home, as a man*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਉਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।**] Give its meaning.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the model and write **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।** once]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say one thing you do every day, then one thing you don't.
