# સ્થાપવું (sthāpvũ) — to found; to establish

## You'll want to know: સ્થાપવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C326-ZANDO326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્થાપવું** — *sthāpvũ* — "to found; to establish".

> રાજાએ નદી કિનારે નવું શહેર સ્થાપ્યું.
>
> *rājāe nadī kināre navũ śaher sthāpyũ.*
>
> The king founded a new city on the river bank.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે શહેર સ્થાપ્યું, *teṇe śaher sthāpyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્થાપવું** (*sthāpvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *sthāpáyati*, "causes to stand, establishes" (CDIAL 13756): to set something up to last. A city, a school or a temple **સ્થાપે છે**.
