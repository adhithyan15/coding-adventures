## You'll want to know: へんきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C359-SHAKKIN359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**へんきん** — *henkin* — "a refund".

> **しなものが こわれたら、へんきんが あるでしょう。** — *shinamono ga kowaretara, henkin ga aru deshō* — "If the goods are broken, there will probably be a refund."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へんきん** (*henkin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hen*, "return" (Middle Chinese *pjonX*) and *kin*, "gold" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
