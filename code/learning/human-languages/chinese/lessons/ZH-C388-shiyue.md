## You'll want to know: shīyuē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C388-DATE388-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shīyuē* — "to miss an appointment".

> *rúguǒ nǐ shīyuē tā huì hěn shēngqì* — If you don't turn up, he'll be angry.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shīyuē* is built from *shī* 'to lose' and 约 'an agreement', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syit* and *'jak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to let slip'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
