## You'll want to know: માલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C188-GOODS188-04]; assesses=[] -->

**માલ** — *māl* — "goods, stock".

> **દુકાનમાં નવો માલ આવ્યો.** — *dukānmā̃ navo māl āvyo* — "New stock has come into the shop."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માલ** (*māl*) is a loan from Arabic *māl*, "property, wealth, whatever one owns", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *m-w-l* (Lane).
