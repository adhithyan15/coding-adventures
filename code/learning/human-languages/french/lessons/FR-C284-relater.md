## You'll want to know: relater
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C284-RAPPORTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**relater** — "to recount, to relate".

> Le livre relate la vie d'une famille pendant la guerre.
>
> *The book recounts the life of a family during the war.*

Formal and written; in speech, *raconter*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*relater* is built on Latin *relātus* (EtymDB), the past participle of *referre* "to carry back, to report" (Lewis & Short).
