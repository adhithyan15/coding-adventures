## You'll want to know: प्राधान्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C388-UDDISHTA388-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राधान्य** — *prādhānya* — "a priority".

> माझ्यासाठी आरोग्याला प्राधान्य आहे.
>
> *mājhyāsāṭhī ārogyālā prādhānya āhe.*
>
> For me, health comes first.

Neuter.

X ला प्राधान्य देणे, to give priority to X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राधान्य** (*prādhānya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prādhānya*, "pre-eminence, predominance" (Monier-Williams).
