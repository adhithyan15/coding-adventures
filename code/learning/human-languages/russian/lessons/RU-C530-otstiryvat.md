## You'll want to know: отстирывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C530-LAUNDRY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**отстирывать** (*otstíryvat*) — "to wash out (a stain)".

> Пятно от вина трудно отстирывать.
>
> *Pyatnó ot viná trúdno otstíryvat.*
>
> A wine stain is hard to wash out.

Imperfective; perfective отстирать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отстирывать* is built on *от-* 'off' and *стирать* 'to wash clothes', from the root of *тереть* 'to rub'; EtymDB has no entry.
