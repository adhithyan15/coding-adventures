## You'll want to know: سرکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C393-JAM393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سرکنا** — *saraknā* — "to edge forward; to slide".

> جام میں گاڑیاں آہستہ آہستہ سرکتی ہیں۔
>
> *jām meṅ gāṛiyāṅ āhistā āhistā saraktī haiṅ.*
>
> In a jam the cars inch forward.

Past (it …): وہ بس آگے سرکی, *voh bas āge sarkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرکنا** (*saraknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *saraknā* under Sanskrit *sarati*, "runs, glides" (CDIAL 13250); **سرکانا** is the form for moving something else.
