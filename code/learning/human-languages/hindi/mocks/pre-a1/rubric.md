# Hindi pre-A1 mock rubric

This rubric scores either pre-A1 mock against the exact task envelope in
[`task-shapes/pre-a1.json`](../../task-shapes/pre-a1.json). Reading, listening,
writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers. A learner passes the rung
only by earning at least 60/100 on **every** paper. A high score in one skill
never compensates for a failed skill.

## Reading — 100 points

- signs and words: 40 points across 6 keyed items;
- phrases and notices: 30 points across 4 keyed items;
- personal details: 30 points across 4 keyed items.

Within each part, divide its points equally among the keyed items and retain one
decimal place. Accept only the keyed meaning or situation. Reading aloud does
not earn reading credit.

## Listening — 100 points

- sounds and words: 40 points across 6 keyed items;
- greeting responses: 30 points across 4 keyed items;
- personal details: 30 points across 4 keyed items.

Play every item twice at 70–90 words per minute with an eight-second answer
pause. Score only the learner's selected answer. Do not show a transcript,
romanization, or answer-bearing picture label.

## Writing — 100 points

- delayed recall: 20 points, 10 per word;
- dictation: 40 points, 10 per item;
- bounded independent production: 40 points, 20 per response.

For each delayed-recall or dictation item award 5 points for recognizable
ordered Devanagari letters, 3 for required matras or nasal marks, and 2 for a
clean word boundary. For each independent response award 8 for answering the
stated purpose, 6 for recognizable Devanagari word formation, 4 for required
marks, and 2 for boundary control. A Romanized answer, traced answer, or visible
copy earns zero because it is not independent writing evidence. Minor
handwriting variation is acceptable when the intended Devanagari graphemes are
unambiguous.

## Speaking — 100 points

- greeting and identity: 30 points;
- three familiar questions: 40 points;
- short request: 30 points.

For each part award 40% for completing the communicative purpose, 25% for
intelligible Hindi sounds, 20% for using the expected known word or chunk, and
15% for responding without an answer script. The interlocutor may repeat once
slowly without penalty. Do not penalize gender variation when it is internally
consistent. A memorized chunk is valid at pre-A1; an English-only response is
not Hindi speaking evidence.

## Readiness boundary

These rubrics and keys make the project-defined mock executable. They do not by
themselves establish exam readiness. The repository must still record a clean
timed administration and qualified human validation before making a pass claim.
