## You'll want to know: કમાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C247-LIVING247-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કમાવું** — *kamāvũ* — "to earn".

> **હવે તે સારું કમાય છે.** — *have te sārũ kamāy che* — "Now he earns well."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કમાવું** (*kamāvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*karmāpayati*, "works, earns", a reconstructed form built on Sanskrit *karman*, "work"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to earn" (CDIAL 2897).
