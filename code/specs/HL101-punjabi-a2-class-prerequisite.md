# HL101 — Punjabi A2 `ਕਲਾਸ` prerequisite

Issue: #18214

After PR #18205 merged, an aggregate-only audit of the preserved confidential
Punjabi A2 mock drafts against merged main
`f5aa1c163a124f29398361286fd367cf2478ce06` finds **ਕਲਾਸ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs zero times in the confidential learner material
and three times in the confidential examiner material. Neither confidential
body belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 201 closes that single-word gap through one deliberately narrow school
identity frame: **ਇਹ ਕਲਾਸ ਹੈ** — “this is a class.” The voice-first opener
retrieves only familiar **ਇਹ** and **ਹੈ**. One lesson directly owns exact
**ਕਲਾਸ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound, meaning, and script, a
bounded frame lesson places it between the two familiar words, and a four-skill
payoff retrieves the complete label again. The target has one direct owner and
three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored, generated-book, or
narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-LEX-CLASS-201`. The lesson keeps the Punjabi form whole and
owns only the everyday school noun sense “class” or “grade.” It does not teach
gender, inflection, compounds, social or economic class, service rank,
programming classes, or productive derivation from another Punjabi form.

Punjabi University's Punjabipedia defines **ਕਲਾਸ** as a noun that can denote a
class, grade, category, or rank and explicitly records English **Class** as its
source:
<https://punjabipedia.org/topic.aspx?txt=%E0%A8%95%E0%A8%B2%E0%A8%BE%E0%A8%B8>.
The Punjab School Education Board's current circular page uses **ਕਲਾਸ** in
school scheduling prose:
<https://pseb.ac.in/public/pressnotescirculars>.

This lesson follows only the directly documented English source and the
current school use. It does not expand Punjabipedia's deeper historical chain
into a productive derivation or imply that the out-of-scope senses are
interchangeable.

This is the twenty-fourth prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
