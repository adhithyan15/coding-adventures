## You'll want to know: فرهنگسرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C455-SHAHRBAZI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فرهنگسرا** (*farhangsarâ*) — "a cultural centre, an arts centre".

> در فرهنگسرای محله کلاس موسیقی هست.
>
> *Dar farhangsarâ-ye mahalle kelâs-e musiqi hast.*
>
> There are music classes at the local cultural centre.

*farhang* 'culture' + *sarâ* 'house'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*farhangsarâ* is a native compound of *farhang* 'culture', from Middle Persian *frahang* (EtymDB), and *sarâ* 'house, hall'.
