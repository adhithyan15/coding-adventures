Malayalam, chapter 285: Flat, Wrinkled, Steep, Hollow, Faded.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പരന്ന (paranna) — flat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for boring, then the Malayalam for dangerous.

You'll want to know: പരന്ന.
പരന്ന — paranna — "flat".

The word, taken apart.
പരന്ന (paranna) is native Dravidian, a past form of the verb "to spread out"; DEDR 3949 lists the root with Tamil para, Kannada paraḍu and Telugu parapu.

The word, taken apart.
ചുളിഞ്ഞ (cuḷiñña) is native Dravidian, a past form of the verb "to be wrinkled". DEDR 2700 sets it beside Tamil cuḷi, "to be angry, show displeasure", and Tamil cuḷukku, "to screw one's face up in displeasure".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

കുത്തനെയുള്ള — kuttaneyuḷḷa — "steep".

The word, taken apart.
കുത്തനെയുള്ള (kuttaneyuḷḷa) is "that is steep": kuttane, "steeply", a word of uncertain origin, with native uḷḷa, "that is" (DEDR 697).

You'll want to know: പൊള്ളയായ.

The word, taken apart.
പൊള്ളയായ (poḷḷayāya) is native Dravidian: poḷḷa, "a tube; perforated, empty" (DEDR 4560), and āya, "having become" (DEDR 333). DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.
