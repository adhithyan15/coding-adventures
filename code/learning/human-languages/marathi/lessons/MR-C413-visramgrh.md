## You'll want to know: विश्रामगृह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C413-NIVAS413-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्रामगृह** — *viśrāmgṛh* — "a guest house; a rest house".

> घाटावर सरकारी विश्रामगृह आहे.
>
> *ghāṭāvar sarkārī viśrāmgṛh āhe.*
>
> There is a government guest house on the ghat.

Neuter.

Run by the government for travelling officers and visitors.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्रामगृह** (*viśrāmgṛh*) is built from Sanskrit *viśrāma*, "rest", and *gṛha*, "a house" (Monier-Williams).
