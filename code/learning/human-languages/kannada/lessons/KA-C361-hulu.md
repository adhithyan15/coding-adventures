## You'll want to know: ಹೂಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C361-SMARAKA361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೂಳು** — *hūḷu* — "to bury".

> ಅವರು ಸತ್ತ ನಾಯಿಯನ್ನು ತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೂಳಿದರು.
>
> *avaru satta nāyiyannu tōṭadalli hūḷidaru.*
>
> They buried the dead dog in the garden.

Past (I …): ಹೂಳಿದೆ, *hūḷide*.

What is buried takes -ಅನ್ನು; where takes -ಅಲ್ಲಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೂಳು** (*hūḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4376 lists the older Kannada forms of this verb, "to cover, to bury", beginning with *p-* and later *h-*.
