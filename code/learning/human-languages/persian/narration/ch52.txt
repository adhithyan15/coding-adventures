Persian, chapter 52: Mountain, Forest, River, Lake, Stone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کوه (kuh) — a mountain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a flower, then the Persian for a leaf.

You'll want to know: کوه.
کوه — kuh — "a mountain".

The word, taken apart.
kuh is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian kōf, "hill, mountain" (EtymDB). Old Persian had kaufa-, "mountain" (EtymDB).

jangal is an Indian loan: EtymDB traces it to Sanskrit jangala, "arid, wild land". English jungle comes from the same Indian word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
رودخانه (rudkhâne) — a river.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a mountain, then the Persian for a forest.

You'll want to know: رودخانه.
رودخانه — rudkhâne — "a river".

The word, taken apart.
rudkhâne is built from rud, "river" (usually traced to Middle Persian rōd), and khâne, "house": the river's house, its bed. Both parts are native Persian.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: دریاچه.
دریاچه — daryâche — "a lake".

The word, taken apart.
daryâche is daryâ, "sea", with the small-thing ending -che: a little sea. daryâ is inherited; Old Persian had drayah-, "sea" (EtymDB).
