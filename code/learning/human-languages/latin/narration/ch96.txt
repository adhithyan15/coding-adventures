Latin, chapter 96: Beautiful, Round, Empty, Bright, Dark.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pulcher, pulchra, pulchrum — beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for wet, then the Latin for sweet.

You'll want to know: pulcher, pulchra, pulchrum.
pulcher, pulchra, pulchrum — "beautiful".
