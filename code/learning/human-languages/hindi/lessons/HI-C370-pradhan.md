## You'll want to know: प्रधान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C370-SABHA370-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रधान** — *pradhān* — "a village head; a head, a chief".

> गाँव के प्रधान ने सभा बुलाई।
>
> *gā̃v ke pradhān ne sabhā bulāī.*
>
> The village head called a meeting.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रधान** (*pradhān*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pradhāna*, "the chief thing or person" (Monier-Williams).
