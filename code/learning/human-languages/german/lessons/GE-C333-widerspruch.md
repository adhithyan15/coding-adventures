## You'll want to know: der Widerspruch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C333-WIDERSPRUCH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Widerspruch** — "the contradiction; the objection".

> In seiner Geschichte gibt es einen klaren Widerspruch.
>
> *There is a clear contradiction in his story.*

Plural: *die Widersprüche*. *Ohne Widerspruch* — without objection.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Widerspruch* is the noun that goes with *widersprechen*, built from *wider* "against" and *Spruch* "saying", from the family of *sprechen* (EtymDB).
