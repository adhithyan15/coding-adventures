Tamil, chapter 88: At the Hospital.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
மருத்துவமனை (maruttuvamaṉai) — a hospital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for weariness, then the Tamil for rest.

You'll want to know: மருத்துவமனை.
மருத்துவமனை (maruttuvamaṉai) — "a hospital", literally "medicine-house".
மனை is "a house, a dwelling". மருத்துவமனை (maruttuvamaṉai) எங்கே? — "where is the hospital?".

What you've built.
A place to ask the way to.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: maruttuvamaṉai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: maruttuvamaṉai, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask maruttuvamaṉai eṅkē?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for weariness, then the Tamil for rest, then say maruttuvamaṉai again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does மருத்துவமனை (maruttuvamaṉai) mean? ("A hospital".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.
தலைவலி (talaivali) — a headache.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a hospital, then the Tamil for vomiting.

You'll want to know: தலைவலி.
தலைவலி (talaivali) — "a headache".
It is தலை, "head", and வலி, "pain", written as one word. எனக்குத் தலைவலி (talaivali) — "I have a headache".

What you've built.
Head and pain, joined.
