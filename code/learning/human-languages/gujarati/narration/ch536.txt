Gujarati, chapter 536: An Auspicious Hour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મુહૂર્ત (muhūrt) — an auspicious moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a planet, then the Gujarati for to make out.

You'll want to know: મુહૂર્ત.
મુહૂર્ત — muhūrt — "an auspicious moment".
જો સારું મુહૂર્ત હશે તો અમે રવિવારે નવા ઘરમાં જઈશું. jo sārũ muhūrt haśe to ame ravivāre navā gharmā̃ jaīśũ. If there is a good hour, we will move into the new house on Sunday.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
મુહૂર્ત (muhūrt) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit muhūrta, "a moment, a short space of time" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is the right moment to begin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ચોઘડિયું (coghḍiyũ) — a choghadiyu (a lucky or unlucky part of the day).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to make out, then the Gujarati for an auspicious moment.

You'll want to know: ચોઘડિયું.
ચોઘડિયું — coghḍiyũ — "a choghadiyu (a lucky or unlucky part of the day)".
જો ચોઘડિયું સારું હશે તો દાદા સવારે દુકાન ખોલશે. jo coghḍiyũ sārũ haśe to dādā savāre dukān kholśe. If the time of day is lucky, my grandfather will open the shop in the morning.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચોઘડિયું (coghḍiyũ) is usually explained as built from co-, "four", and ghaḍī, an old unit of time, from Sanskrit ghaṭikā (Monier-Williams): the day is cut into such periods, each good or bad for beginning a task.

જો તમે પંચાંગ જોશો તો તમને તહેવારની તારીખ મળશે. jo tame pancā̃g jośo to tamne tahevārnī tārīkh maḷśe. If you look in the almanac, you'll find the date of the festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a choghadiyu, then the Gujarati for a Hindu almanac.

You'll want to know: કુંડળી.
કુંડળી — kunḍḷī — "a birth chart".
જો બંનેની કુંડળી મળશે તો લગ્ન નક્કી થશે. jo bannenī kunḍḷī maḷśe to lagna nakkī thaśe. If both their birth charts match, the wedding will be fixed.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કુંડળી (kunḍḷī) goes back to Sanskrit kuṇḍala, "a ring, a circle" (Monier-Williams); the chart of the heavens at one's birth is drawn as a figure.
