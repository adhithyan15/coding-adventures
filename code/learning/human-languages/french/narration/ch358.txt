French, chapter 358: La grève.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le syndicat — the trade union.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the apprenticeship, then the French for the employer.

You'll want to know: le syndicat.
le syndicat — "the trade union".
Mon père travaille pour un syndicat. My father works for a trade union.
Le syndicat d'initiative is an old name for a tourist office.

The word, taken apart.
syndicat is built from syndic "representative" and -at (EtymDB). syndic comes from Late Latin syndicus "delegate of a body", from Greek sýndikos "advocate".

Lesson 2 of 5.
la grève — the strike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the employer, then the French for the trade union.

You'll want to know: la grève.
la grève — "the strike".
Les trains ne roulent pas: il y a une grève. The trains are not running: there is a strike.
Faire grève, être en grève — to be on strike. La grève is also a sandy shore.

The word, taken apart.
grève "strike" is built on grève "sandy shore": the usual explanation is a riverside square of that name where people looking for work gathered. The origin of grève "shore" is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
le salarié — the employee, the wage earner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the strike, then the French for the striker.

You'll want to know: le salarié.
le salarié — "the employee, the wage earner".
Cette entreprise a deux cents salariés. This company has two hundred employees.
From salaire. Feminine salariée.

The word, taken apart.
salarié is built on salaire "wages", from Latin salārium "salt money, an allowance" (Lewis & Short), from sāl "salt".

Lesson 5 of 5.
les heures supplémentaires — the overtime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the striker, then the French for the employee.

You'll want to know: les heures supplémentaires.
les heures supplémentaires — "the overtime".
Ce mois-ci, j'ai fait vingt heures supplémentaires. This month, I did twenty hours of overtime.
Often shortened to les heures sup.

The word, taken apart.
supplémentaire is built on supplément, from Latin supplēmentum "a filling up, a supply" (Lewis & Short); heure is inherited from Latin hōra "hour".
