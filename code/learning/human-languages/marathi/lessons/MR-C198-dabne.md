## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाबणे** (*dābṇe*) comes from a reconstructed *\*dabb-*, "press" (CDIAL 6173). CDIAL reports A. Master's view that it comes from Dravidian.
