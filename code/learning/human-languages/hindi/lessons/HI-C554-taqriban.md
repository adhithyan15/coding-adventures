# तक़रीबन (taqrīban) — approximately, roughly

## You'll want to know: तक़रीबन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C554-NATIJATAN554-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तक़रीबन** — *taqrīban* — "approximately, roughly".

> तक़रीबन सौ लोग मेले में आए।
>
> *Taqrīban sau log mele meṁ āe.*
>
> Roughly a hundred people came to the fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तक़रीबन** (*taqrīban*) is usually traced to Arabic *taqrīban*, "approximately", through Persian.
