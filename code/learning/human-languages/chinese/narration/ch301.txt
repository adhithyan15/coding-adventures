Mandarin Chinese, chapter 301: Getting Out.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
táo — to run away; to flee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the cause of death, then the Mandarin for to blow up.

You'll want to know: táo.
táo — "to run away; to flee".
fángzi zháohuǒ le, dàjiā kuài táo — The house is on fire, everyone run!
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
táo is inherited from Middle Chinese daw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'to go away', 'to avoid' and 'to flee'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.

Lesson 4 of 6.
gǔzhé — to break a bone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for in a panic, then the Mandarin for to help up someone who fell.

You'll want to know: gǔzhé.
gǔzhé — "to break a bone".
tā de tuǐ gǔzhé le — He broke his leg.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gǔzhé is built from gǔ 'a bone' and zhé 'to snap', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwot and tsyet (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, quoting an older dictionary, 'the core of the flesh' and the second 'to bend and break'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
果然 (guǒrán) — sure enough; as expected.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to help up someone who fell, then the Mandarin for to break a bone.

You'll want to know: 果然.
果然 — guǒrán — "sure enough; as expected".
他说会下雨果然下雨了 tā shuō huì xiàyǔ, guǒrán xiàyǔ le — He said it would rain, and sure enough it did.

The word, taken apart.
果然 (guǒrán) is built from 果 'resolute' and 然 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwaX and nyen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'resolute' and 'certain' and the second 'so, like this': so, for certain.

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.
