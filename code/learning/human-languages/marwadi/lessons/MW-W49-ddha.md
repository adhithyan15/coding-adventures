# ढ (ḍha) — the letter inside काढणो

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-DDA-01, MW-LEX-C161-VERB161-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **ड** once, and name it.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **काढणो** (*kāḍhṇo*), "to take out". Its middle is a **ड** with a
breath after it: *ḍh*. This lesson puts that letter in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ढ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-C161-DDHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ढ** — *ḍha*, the breathed *ḍ*.

It sits inside **काढणो**. It stands beside **ड** and **ड़** as **ध** stands beside
**द**. Its body is one stroke: a stem that runs into a broad outer bowl and a
closed loop inside it, then the headline.

## Writing: ढ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-C161-DDHA-01] -->

- **1.** start at the stem's headline junction, descend, turn left through the upper shoulder, sweep counterclockwise around the broad outer bowl, and continue counterclockwise around the closed inner loop without lifting
- **2.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> verified two-stroke teaching form fitted to the bundled printed outline

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
