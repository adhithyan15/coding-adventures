Sanskrit, chapter 499: Aunts and Nephews.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पितृष्वसा (pitṛṣvasā) — a father's sister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for youngest, then the Sanskrit for twins.

You'll want to know: पितृष्वसा.
पितृष्वसा — pitṛṣvasā — "a father's sister".
मम पितृष्वसा ग्रामे वसति। mama pitṛṣvasā grāme vasati. My father's sister lives in the village.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पितृष्वसा (pitṛṣvasā) is built from pitṛ, "father" (पिता), and svasṛ, "sister" (MW); CDIAL 8177 lists it beside pituḥṣvasṛ, the form behind Bengali pisi (CDIAL 8177).
