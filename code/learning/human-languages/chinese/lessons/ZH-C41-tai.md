## You'll want to know: tài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tài* — "too; too much".

> *tài xiǎo le* — That's too small.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

*tài* usually brings a *le* at the end of the sentence with it: *tài xiǎo le*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tài* is inherited from Middle Chinese *thajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'extreme' and 'great'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
