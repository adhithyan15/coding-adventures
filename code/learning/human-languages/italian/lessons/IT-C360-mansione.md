## You'll want to know: la mansione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C360-REPARTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la mansione** — "the duty, the task (in a job)".

> Le mie mansioni sono rispondere al telefono e organizzare le riunioni.
>
> *My duties are answering the phone and organising meetings.*

Mostly plural in job descriptions: *le mansioni*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mansione* is usually traced to Latin *mānsiō* "a stay, a lodging" (Lewis & Short), from *manēre* "to remain". How it came to mean a duty, perhaps through French, is not settled.
