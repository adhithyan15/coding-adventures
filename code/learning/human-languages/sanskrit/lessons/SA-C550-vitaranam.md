## You'll want to know: वितरणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C550-AMSA550-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वितरणम्** — *vitaraṇam* — "a handing out".

> यदि उत्सवे वितरणं भवति, तर्हि बालाः प्रसन्नाः सन्ति।
>
> *yadi utsave vitaraṇaṃ bhavati, tarhi bālāḥ prasannāḥ santi.*
>
> If things are handed out at the festival, the children are happy.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वितरणम्** (*vitaraṇam*) is built on *vi* and the root √tṝ, "to cross", which also gives *vitarati*, "distributes"; MW gives *vitaraṇa* as leading over, and granting, bestowal, a gift (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
