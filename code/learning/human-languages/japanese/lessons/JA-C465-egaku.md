## You'll want to know: えがく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C465-PAINT465-03]; assesses=[] -->

**えがく** — *egaku* — "to paint a picture; to depict".

> **かれは うみを えがきました。** — *kare wa umi o egakimashita* — "He painted the sea."

Polite **えがきます**, *egakimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **むすめが はなを えがきました。**, *musume ga hana o egakimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えがく** (*egaku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
