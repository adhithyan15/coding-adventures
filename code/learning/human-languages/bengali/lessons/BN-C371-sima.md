## You'll want to know: সীমা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C371-JOTHESHTO371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সীমা** — *sīmā* — "a limit; a boundary".

> খরচ সীমার বেশি হলে আমরা কাজটা বন্ধ করব।
>
> *khôrôch sīmār beshi hôle āmrā kājṭā bôndhô kôrbô.*
>
> If the cost goes over the limit, we'll stop the work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সীমা** (*sīmā*) is Sanskrit *sīmā*, a boundary (Monier-Williams). It is the border of a field and any limit not to be passed.
