## You'll want to know: دانشور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C352-MAZMUN352-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دانشور** — *dānishvar* — "an intellectual; a thinker".

> شہر کے دانشور اس بات پر متفق ہیں۔
>
> *shahr ke dānishvar is bāt par muttafiq haiṅ.*
>
> The city's thinkers agree on this point.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دانشور** (*dānishvar*) is usually traced to Persian *dānishvar*, "possessing knowledge", built on *dānish*, "knowledge".
