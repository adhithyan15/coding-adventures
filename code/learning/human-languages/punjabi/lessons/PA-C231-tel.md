## You'll want to know: ਤੇਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C231-DIN231-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੇਲ** — *tel* — "oil".

> ਮੈਂ ਵਾਲਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ ਤੇਲ ਮਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ vālāṁ utte tel maldī hāṁ.*
>
> I rub oil into my hair.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੇਲ** (*tel*) is inherited from Sanskrit *taila*, "sesame oil, oil"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tel*, "oil" (CDIAL 5958).
