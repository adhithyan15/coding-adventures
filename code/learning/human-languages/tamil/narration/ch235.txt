Tamil, chapter 235: Direction, North, South, East, West.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
திசை (ticai) — a direction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a capital city, then the Tamil for a crossroads.

You'll want to know: திசை.
திசை — ticai — "a direction".

The word, taken apart.
திசை (ticai) is a well-established loan from Sanskrit diśā, "direction, quarter of the sky".
