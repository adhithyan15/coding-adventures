## You'll want to know: وارث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C337-IRTH337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وارث** — *wārith* — "an heir".

> كان الابن الوارث الوحيد.
>
> *kāna al-ibn al-wārith al-waḥīd.*
>
> The son was the only heir.

Plural: ورثة, *waratha*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وارث** (*wārith*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-r-th* (Lane), "to inherit". *Wārith* is the active participle, "one who inherits".
