## You'll want to know: 变成
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C386-CHANGE386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**变成** — *biànchéng* — "to turn into".

> **那家饭店变成了书店** *nà jiā fàndiàn biànchéng le shūdiàn* — That restaurant has turned into a bookshop.

The new character, in pieces: 变 — eight strokes in two pieces: on top a dot and a bar over four short strokes; at the foot 又, two strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**变成** (*biànchéng*) is built from 变 'to change' and 成 'to complete', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjenH* and *dzyeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to transform'.
