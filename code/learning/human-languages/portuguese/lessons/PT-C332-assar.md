## You'll want to know: assar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C332-FRITAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**assar** — "to roast, to bake".

> Ao domingo, o meu pai assa um frango.
>
> *On Sundays my father roasts a chicken.*

*Frango assado* — roast chicken.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assar* is inherited from Latin *assāre* "to roast" (EtymDB), from *assus* "roasted, dry".
