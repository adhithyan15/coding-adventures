## You'll want to know: વિનંતી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C542-SAMADHAN542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વિનંતી** — *vinantī* — "a polite request".

> જો તમે વિનંતી કરશો તો તે ચોક્કસ મદદ કરશે.
>
> *jo tame vinantī karśo to te cokkas madad karśe.*
>
> If you ask him politely, he will surely help.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિનંતી** (*vinantī*) goes back to Sanskrit *vijñapti*, "a report; a request to a superior", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a request" (CDIAL 11706).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
