Tamil, chapter 137: Ring, Bangle, Sock, Glove, Class.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
மோதிரம் (mōtiram) — a ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a doll, then the Tamil for a purse.

You'll want to know: மோதிரம்.
மோதிரம் — mōtiram — "a ring".
