## You'll want to know: احتمال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C361-IMKAN361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمال** — *ehtimāl* — "a probability (formal)".

> اگر ہوا تیز ہوئی تو طوفان کا احتمال ہے۔
>
> *agar hawā tez huī to tūfān kā ehtimāl hai.*
>
> If the wind gets up, there may well be a storm.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمال** (*ehtimāl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iḥtimāl*, "bearing, enduring; likelihood".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
