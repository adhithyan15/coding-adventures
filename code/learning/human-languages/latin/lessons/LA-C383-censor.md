# cēnsor, cēnsōris — a censor

## You'll want to know: cēnsor, cēnsōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C383-MAGISTRATUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cēnsor, cēnsōris** — "a censor".

> Cēnsor cīvēs et bona eōrum numerat.
>
> *The censor counts the citizens and their property.*

*cēnsor, cēnsōris* m., built on *cēnseō*, to assess. Every five years two censors drew up the list of citizens and could strike a senator off the roll. English *censor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cēnsor* is built on *cēnseō* "to assess, to value" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the officer who assessed the citizens. English *censor* comes from it.
