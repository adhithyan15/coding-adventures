## You'll want to know: 小心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C115-CAREFUL115-01]; assesses=[] -->

**小心** — *xiǎoxīn* — "to be careful; to mind (something)".

> **出门小心车** *chūmén xiǎoxīn chē* — Mind the traffic when you go out.

The new character, in pieces: 心 — four strokes: a dot, a long hook lying along the bottom, and two dots, the foot of 想. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**小心** (*xiǎoxīn*) is built from 小 'small' and 心 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). How a small heart came to mean care is not settled.
