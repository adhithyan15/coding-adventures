## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பலவீனமான** (*palavīṉamāṉa*) is *palavīṉam*, "weakness", from Sanskrit *bala*, "strength" (CDIAL 9161), and *hīna*, "lacking" (CDIAL 14124), with *-āṉa*.
