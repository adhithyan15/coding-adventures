Kannada, chapter 417: Paying Back.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಂತು (kantu) — an instalment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to ask formally, then the Kannada for an oath of office.

You'll want to know: ಕಂತು.
ಕಂತು — kantu — "an instalment".
ನಾನು ಪ್ರತಿ ತಿಂಗಳು ಸಾಲದ ಕಂತು ಕಟ್ಟುತ್ತೇನೆ. nānu prati tiṅgaḷu sālada kantu kaṭṭuttēne. I pay an instalment of the loan every month.
ಕಂತು (kantu) ಕಟ್ಟು, to pay an instalment.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಂತು (kantu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1209 lists Kannada kanta, kantu, "an instalment, a fixed term of payment".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಬಾಕಿ (bāki) — arrears; the balance due.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an oath of office, then the Kannada for an instalment.

You'll want to know: ಬಾಕಿ.
ಬಾಕಿ — bāki — "arrears; the balance due".
ಅಂಗಡಿಗೆ ಇನ್ನೂ ನೂರು ರೂಪಾಯಿ ಬಾಕಿ ಇದೆ. aṅgaḍige innū nūru rūpāyi bāki ide. A hundred rupees are still owed to the shop.
ಬಾಕಿ (bāki) ಇರು, to be outstanding.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಾಕಿ (bāki) is a loan from Arabic bāqī, "remaining", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಉಳಿತಾಯ (uḷitāya) — savings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an instalment, then the Kannada for arrears.

You'll want to know: ಉಳಿತಾಯ.
ಉಳಿತಾಯ — uḷitāya — "savings".
ಉಳಿತಾಯದ ಹಣದಿಂದ ಅವಳು ಸೈಕಲ್ ಕೊಂಡಳು. uḷitāyada haṇadinda avaḷu saikal koṇḍaḷu. She bought a bicycle with her savings.
ಉಳಿತಾಯ (uḷitāya) ಖಾತೆ, a savings account.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಳಿತಾಯ (uḷitāya) is built on the Kannada verb uḷi, "to remain, to be left over"; DEDR does not list uḷitāya, so the formation is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡು (vasūli māḍu) — to recover dues.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for arrears, then the Kannada for savings.

You'll want to know: ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡು.
ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡು — vasūli māḍu — "to recover dues".
ಬ್ಯಾಂಕ್ ಸಾಲವನ್ನು ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡಿತು. byāṅk sālavannu vasūli māḍitu. The bank recovered the loan.
Past (I …): ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, vasūli māḍide.
ವಸೂಲಿ alone is collection of money owed.

The word, taken apart.
ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡು (vasūli māḍu) is built from vasūli, a loan from Hindustani vasūl, "collected", which comes from Arabic wuṣūl, "arrival, receipt", with māḍu, "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಲ್ಲಿಸು (sallisu) — to submit; to pay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for savings, then the Kannada for to recover dues.

You'll want to know: ಸಲ್ಲಿಸು.
ಸಲ್ಲಿಸು — sallisu — "to submit; to pay".
ನಾನು ಕಚೇರಿಗೆ ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿದೆ. nānu kacērige arji salliside. I submitted an application to the office.
Past (I …): ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿದೆ, salliside.
Also to offer: ಧನ್ಯವಾದ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸು (sallisu), to offer thanks.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಲ್ಲಿಸು (sallisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2781 lists Kannada sal, "to pass, to be current", and its causative sallisu.
