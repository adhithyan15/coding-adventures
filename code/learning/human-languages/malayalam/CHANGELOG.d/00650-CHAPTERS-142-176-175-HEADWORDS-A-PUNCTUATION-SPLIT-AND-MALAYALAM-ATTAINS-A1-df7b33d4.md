## Chapters 142-176: 175 headwords, a punctuation split, and Malayalam attains A1

Malayalam had four kinds of A1 gap:

- five A1 spine nodes had no lesson;
- it taught 444 headwords against 600, and 16 verbs against 40;
- the punctuation lesson *the rest* introduced four marks at once;
- eighty atoms were revisited fewer than twice.

**The words.** Thirty-five chapters of five, closed by eight review lessons:

- **can:** **കഴിവ്**, **ശക്തി**, **അവകാശം**, **അവസരം** and more
  (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO)
- **why:** **അതുകൊണ്ട്**, **ഹേതു**, **നിമിത്തം** and more (SAY-WHY)
- **want:** **ആഗ്രഹം**, **ആവശ്യം**, **ലക്ഷ്യം**, **പ്രതീക്ഷ** and more
  (SAY-WHAT-I-WANT)
- **things:** ninety of them, covering the house, the town, nature, food,
  animals, the body, clothes and tools (NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS)
- **qualities:** thirty-one describing words, from **ചൂടുള്ള** to
  **വിലകുറഞ്ഞ** (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)
- **verbs:** thirty-two, from **നടക്കുക** to **വീഴുക**
  (NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS)

**Checks.** Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword,
romanization and lesson text. Every word uses only letters the book has already
taught the reader to write. The track teaches ഏ, but
`data/scripts/malayalam.json` does not cover it yet, so no new headword uses
it. One candidate, **ജുബ്ബ**, was dropped.

**The split.** *The rest* now teaches the colon and brackets. A new
continuation, *the rest, more*, teaches the hyphen and the slash, with its own
practice and wrap-up. Its warm-up retrieves the colon and brackets, and the
marks recall now lists it as a prerequisite.

**The revisits.** Seventy-nine thin atoms each get warm-up retrievals in the
new chapters. An atom with no revisit gets two, in two lessons. The retrievals
include:

- the time-of-day words and their greetings
- *iṣṭaṁ*, "a liking"
- the ordinals
- the instrumental *-āl*
- the punctuation marks
- the chapter-69 reading skills

Every line is in romanization or English, so no retrieval shows a letter
before its lesson.
