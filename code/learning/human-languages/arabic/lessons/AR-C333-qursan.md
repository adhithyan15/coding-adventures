## You'll want to know: قرصان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C333-BAHR333-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قرصان** — *qurṣān* — "a pirate".

> كان القرصان يبحث عن الكنز.
>
> *kāna al-qurṣān yabḥathu ʿan al-kanz.*
>
> The pirate was looking for the treasure.

Plural: قراصنة, *qarāṣina*.

Today also a computer hacker: قرصان إنترنت.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قرصان** (*qurṣān*) is usually traced to Italian *corsaro*, "a corsair", the raider of the Mediterranean, from Latin *cursus*, a running.
