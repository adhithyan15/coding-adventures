Mandarin Chinese, chapter 283: Which Way?.
8 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
怎么 (zěnme) — how.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
A new chapter, and it opens on the word you ask a stranger with.
[your turn — recall: say 你应该去看医生 — nǐ yīnggāi qù kàn yīshēng — R1, the chapter you have finished]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say zěnmeyàng, how is it — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say zěnme bàn, what should I do — R3]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 怎么.
怎么 — zěnme — "how; in what way".
这个字怎么写 zhège zì zěnme xiě — How do you write this character?
汉语怎么说 Hànyǔ zěnme shuō — How do you say it in Chinese?
You have said it inside zěnmeyàng and zěnme bàn; here it stands alone, before a verb. The next lesson puts the first character in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
怎么 (zěnme) is built from 怎 (zěn) 'how' and the ending 么 of 什么. No dictionary we use records an older form of either, so its history is not settled.

What you've built.
怎么 (zěnme) goes right before the verb it asks about: 怎么写, 怎么说.
The answer shows the way, or gives it: 这么写, written like this.

Script — the right of 作, then 心.
怎 (zěn).
Two pieces, and you write both already.
The right of 作, five strokes, here on top: a short left-falling stroke; a horizontal from it to the right; a long vertical down from the horizontal; then two short horizontals to its right, the upper one first.
心, four strokes, at the foot: the left dot, the curve along the foot that hooks up, the middle dot, the right dot.
5 + 4 = nine.

Lesson 3 of 8.
去…怎么走 (qù … zěnme zǒu) — how do I get there?

Warm-up.

The word you wrote last, and a verb you have said for a long time.

Grammar Lens: 去 … 怎么走, asking the way.
去, the place, then 怎么走 asks the way there.
去火车站怎么走 qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu — How do I get to the station? 请问 去书店怎么走 qǐngwèn, qù shūdiàn zěnme zǒu — Excuse me, how do I get to the bookshop?
走 is the zǒu you know, to walk; the next lesson puts it in your hand. 请问 in front makes the question polite.
The answer shows the way: yìzhí zǒu, straight on; dào lùkǒu zuǒ guǎi, turn left at the crossing. zuǒ is the zuǒ of zuǒbian; yòu, the yòu of yòubian, turns you right.

Lesson 5 of 8.
wǎng — towards.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: the question, and the two sides of the street.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 去火车站怎么走, aloud — qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu]
[your turn — recall: say zuǒbian, the left — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say yòubian, the right — R3]

You'll want to know: wǎng.
wǎng — "towards; in the direction of".
wǎng qián zǒu — Go straight ahead.
dào lùkǒu wǎng zuǒ guǎi — At the crossing, turn left.
Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
wǎng is inherited from Middle Chinese hjwangX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to go' and 'to go to'; its use as 'towards' is not given there.

What you've built.
wǎng comes before a direction, and the verb follows: wǎng qián zǒu, wǎng zuǒ guǎi, wǎng yòu guǎi.
qián is the 前 of 前边: ahead.

Lesson 6 of 8.
再 (zài) — again.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: last lesson's word, and a goodbye you have said from the first chapters.
[your turn — recall: say wǎng qián zǒu, straight ahead — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 再见 — zàijiàn, goodbye — R3, durable]

You'll want to know: 再.
再 — zài — "again; once more", for what is still to come.
我明天再来 wǒ míngtiān zài lái — I'll come again tomorrow.
qǐng zài shuō yí cì — Please say it once more.
It is the 再 (zài) of 再见: see you again.

The word, taken apart.
再 (zài) is inherited from Middle Chinese tsojH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'twice; a second time'.

What you've built.
再 (zài) goes before the verb, after the person, and it looks forward: the again has not happened yet.
It is not the 在 of 我在家: the same sound, a different word, a different character.

Lesson 7 of 8.
路过 (lùguò) — to pass by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: last lesson's word, then two characters you write.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 我明天再来, aloud — wǒ míngtiān zài lái]
路 过.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — lù, guò]

You'll want to know: 路过.
路过 — lùguò — "to pass by", on the way somewhere else.
我每天路过这个书店 wǒ měitiān lùguò zhège shūdiàn — I go past this bookshop every day.
我路过你家 wǒ lùguò nǐ jiā — I'm going past your house.
Both characters are ones you write.

The word, taken apart.
路过 (lùguò) is built from 路 'road' and 过 'to pass', both inherited from Middle Chinese, luH and kwaH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a road' and the second, among other senses, 'to go beyond'.

What you've built.
路过 (lùguò) is passing a place on your way somewhere else: you do not stop there. 去 is going there.
It is a verb, and the place follows it: 路过这个书店.

The exchange.
A: 请问 去书店怎么走 qǐngwèn, qù shūdiàn zěnme zǒu — Excuse me, how do I get to the bookshop? B: wǎng qián zǒu — Go straight ahead. B: dào lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi — Turn right at the crossing. A: duìbuqǐ, qǐng zài shuō yí cì — Sorry, could you say that again? B: wǎng qián zǒu, dào lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi — Straight ahead, then right at the crossing. A: yuǎn ma — Is it far? B: bù yuǎn, wǒ měitiān lùguò — Not far. I go past it every day. A: 谢谢 再见 xièxie, zàijiàn — Thank you. Goodbye.
Directions run in the order you walk them, and the person is left out: wǎng qián zǒu, then dào lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi. Asking again is qǐng zài shuō yí cì.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]

qǐng zài shuō yí cì — once more, please.
