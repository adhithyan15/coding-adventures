Mandarin Chinese, chapter 141: Travel and Fun.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
lǚyóu — to travel; to tour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for most, then the Mandarin for to calculate.

You'll want to know: lǚyóu.
lǚyóu — "to travel; to tour".
wǒ xǐhuan lǚyóu — I love travelling.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
lǚyóu is built from lǚ and yóu, both inherited from Middle Chinese, ljoX and yuw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'a body of troops' and the second 'to float, to roam', the yóu of yóuyǒng. 'Journey' for the first is not given there; how it got that sense is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for what I like.

Lesson 3 of 5.
guàngjiē — to stroll the shops; to go window-shopping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to travel, then the Mandarin for a game.

You'll want to know: guàngjiē.
guàngjiē — "to stroll the shops; to go window-shopping".
zhōumò wǒmen qù guàngjiē — At the weekend we're going round the shops.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
guàngjiē is built from guàng and jiē 'street', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kjwangX and kea (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'the look of walking'; the regular sound change would give it a third tone, so the fourth tone of guàng is not settled.
