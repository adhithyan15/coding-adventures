## You'll want to know: gōngzī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C255-PARTTIME255-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngzī* — "pay; wages".

> *zhège gōngzuò de gōngzī hěn gāo* — This job pays well.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngzī* is built from 工 'work' and *zī* 'goods', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *tsij* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'goods' and 'to help'.
