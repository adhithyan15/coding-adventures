## You'll want to know: возвращать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C394-DOLG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**возвращать** (*vozvrashchát*) — "to give back, to return (something)".

> Он всегда возвращает книги вовремя.
>
> *On vsegdá vozvrashcháyet knígi vovrémya.*
>
> He always returns books on time.

Imperfective; perfective *вернуть* or *возвратить*. *Возвращать долг* — to pay back a debt. *Возвращаться* is to come back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**возвращать** is built from *воз-* "back" and *вращать* "to turn", the Church Slavonic twin of native *ворочать*: to turn a thing back to its owner.
