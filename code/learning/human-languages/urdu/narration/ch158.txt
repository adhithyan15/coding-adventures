Urdu, chapter 158: Nation, Settlement, Port, Restaurant, Café.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قوم (qaum) — a nation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a town square, then the Urdu for a canal.

You'll want to know: قوم.
قوم — qaum — "a nation".

The word, taken apart.
قوم (qaum) is a loan from Arabic qawm, "a people", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-w-m (Lane).
