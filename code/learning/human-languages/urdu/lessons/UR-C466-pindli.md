## You'll want to know: پنڈلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C466-LIMB466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پنڈلی** — *pinḍlī* — "the calf of the leg".

> دوڑنے کے بعد میری پنڈلی میں درد ہے۔
>
> *dauṛne ke bād merī pinḍlī meṅ dard hai.*
>
> My calf hurts after running.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پنڈلی** (*pinḍlī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *piṇḍlī*, "calf of the leg", under Sanskrit *píṇḍa*, "a lump", which already meant the calf (CDIAL 8168).
