## You'll want to know: پخش شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C533-MEDIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پخش شدن** (*pakhsh shodan*) — "to be broadcast; to spread".

> این سریال هر شب از تلویزیون پخش میشود.
>
> *in seriyâl har shab az televiziyon pakhsh mishavad.*
>
> This series is broadcast on TV every night.

*pakhsh* 'spreading' + *shodan*. *pakhsh kardan* — to broadcast; to hand out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pakhsh* is usually taken as a native Persian word for spreading, but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled. *shodan* is inherited.
