## You'll want to know: ननु
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C344-PAKSHA344-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ननु** — *nanu* — "is it not so; surely".

> ननु एतत् उचितम् अस्ति?
>
> *nanu etat ucitam asti?*
>
> Surely this is right, is it not?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ननु** (*nanu*) is built from *na*, "not", and *nu*, "now"; MW gives *nanu* "not?, is it not?" and so "certainly" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
