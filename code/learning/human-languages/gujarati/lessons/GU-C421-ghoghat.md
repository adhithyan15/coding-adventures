## You'll want to know: ઘોંઘાટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C421-NOISE421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘોંઘાટ** — *ghõghāṭ* — "noise; a din".

> શહેરમાં આખો દિવસ ઘોંઘાટ રહે છે.
>
> *śahermā̃ ākho divas ghõghāṭ rahe che.*
>
> In the city there is noise all day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘોંઘાટ** (*ghõghāṭ*) sounds like the noise it names. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
