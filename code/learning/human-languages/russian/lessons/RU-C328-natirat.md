## You'll want to know: натирать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C328-NAREZAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**натирать** (*natirát*) — "to grate; to rub".

> Я натёр сыр для пиццы.
>
> *Ya natyór syr dlya pítstsy.*
>
> I grated cheese for the pizza.

Imperfective; the perfective is *натереть* (*naterét*), *натру*, past *натёр* (*natyór*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*натирать* is built on *на-* and *тереть* "to rub", inherited from Proto-Slavic *terti*, from PIE *terh₁-* "to rub" (EtymDB).
