## You'll want to know: das Kinn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C484-GESICHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Kinn** — "chin".

> Der Mann hat einen Bart am Kinn.
>
> *The man has a beard on his chin.*

Plural: *die Kinne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kinn* is inherited from Old High German *kinni*, from Proto-Germanic *kinnuz* "chin" (EtymDB); English *chin* is a cousin.
