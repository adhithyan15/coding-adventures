## You'll want to know: અંતિમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C529-TABAKKO529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**અંતિમ** — *antim* — "final".

> જો અંતિમ દિવસે વરસાદ પડશે તો આપણે મેળો બંધ રાખીશું.
>
> *jo antim divse varsād paḍśe to āpaṇe meḷo bandh rākhīśũ.*
>
> If it rains on the final day, we will keep the fair closed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અંતિમ** (*antim*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *antima*, "last" (Monier-Williams), built on *anta*, "the end", the word behind **અંત**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
