## You'll want to know: そめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C467-CRAFT467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**そめる** — *someru* — "to dye".

> **ぬのを あおく そめました。** — *nuno o aoku somemashita* — "I dyed the cloth blue."

Polite **そめます**, *somemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **しょくにんが ぬのを そめました。**, *shokunin ga nuno o somemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そめる** (*someru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
