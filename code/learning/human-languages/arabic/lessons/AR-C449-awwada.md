## You'll want to know: عوض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C449-SHAKWA449-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عوض** — *ʿawwaḍa* — "to compensate".

> عوضت الشركة الركاب عن التأخير.
>
> *ʿawwaḍat ash-sharika ar-rukkāb ʿan at-taʾkhīr.*
>
> The company compensated the passengers for the delay.

Present: يعوض, *yuʿawwiḍ*.

Someone is compensated *ʿan* a loss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عوض** (*ʿawwaḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-w-ḍ*, "to give in exchange" (Lane). It is Form II.
