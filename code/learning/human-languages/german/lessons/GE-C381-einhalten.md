## You'll want to know: einhalten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C381-VORSCHRIFT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**einhalten** — "to keep to, to comply with".

> Wir müssen die Frist einhalten.
>
> *We have to meet the deadline.*

Separable and strong: *er hält ein*, *er hat eingehalten*. *Ein Versprechen einhalten* — to keep a promise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*einhalten* is built from *ein* and *halten* "to hold", inherited from Proto-Germanic *haldaną* (EtymDB): you hold to what was agreed.
