Malayalam, chapter 119: Slowly, Once More.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പതുക്കെ (patukke) — slowly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for mist, then the Malayalam for thunder and lightning.

You'll want to know: പതുക്കെ.
പതുക്കെ (patukke) — "slowly". പതുക്കെ പറയൂ (patukke paṟayū) — "please say it slowly".

What you've built.
The most useful request a learner can make.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: patukke]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: patukke, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say patukke paṟayū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for mist, then the Malayalam for thunder and lightning, then say patukke again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does പതുക്കെ (patukke) mean? ("Slowly".) Say it once more.

You'll want to know: ഉറക്കെ.
ഉറക്കെ (uṟakke) — "loudly, aloud". കുറച്ച് ഉറക്കെ — "a little louder", with കുറച്ച്, "a little".

A second way of saying how.

Lesson 4 of 5.
അർത്ഥം (arthaṁ) — meaning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for once more, then the Malayalam for loudly.

You'll want to know: അർത്ഥം.
അർത്ഥം (arthaṁ) — "meaning". ഇതിന്റെ അർത്ഥം എന്താണ്? — "what is the meaning of this?", with എന്താണ്, "what is".

What you've built.
The question behind every new word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
