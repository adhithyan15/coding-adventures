# ಸರಬರಾಜು (sarabarāju) — supplies; a supply

## You'll want to know: ಸರಬರಾಜು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C470-KAMAGARI470-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರಬರಾಜು** — *sarabarāju* — "supplies; a supply".

> ಮಂಗಳವಾರ ನಮ್ಮ ಬಡಾವಣೆಗೆ ನೀರಿನ ಸರಬರಾಜು ಇರುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *maṅgaḷavāra namma baḍāvaṇege nīrina sarabarāju iruvudilla.*
>
> On Tuesday there will be no water supply to our area.

ಸರಬರಾಜು ಮಾಡು, to supply.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರಬರಾಜು** (*sarabarāju*) is usually traced to Persian *sarbarāh*, "a manager, one who provides", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.
