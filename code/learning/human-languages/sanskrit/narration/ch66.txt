Sanskrit, chapter 66: Not Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ज्वरः (jvaraḥ) — a fever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for second, then the Sanskrit for first.

You'll want to know: ज्वरः.
ज्वरः (jvaraḥ) — "a fever". मम ज्वरः अस्ति (mama jvaraḥ asti) — "I have a fever", literally "of me a fever is", with मम and अस्ति, which you know.

What you've built.
An illness, said with of-me and is.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for second, then the Sanskrit for first, then say jvaraḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ज्वरः (jvaraḥ) mean? ("A fever".) Say it once more.

You'll want to know: आरोग्यम्.
आरोग्यम् (ārogyam) — "health": आ-, then रोग, "illness", with -य after it — the state of the body once the illness is gone.
