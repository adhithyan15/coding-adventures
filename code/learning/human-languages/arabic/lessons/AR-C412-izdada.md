## You'll want to know: ازداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C412-IZDIYAD412-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ازداد** — *izdāda* — "to become greater; to increase".

> ازداد عدد السكان.
>
> *izdāda ʿadad as-sukkān.*
>
> The number of inhabitants grew.

Present: يزداد, *yazdād*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ازداد** (*izdāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-y-d*, "to increase, to grow more" (Lane). It is Form VIII of *zāda*, to increase: to become greater.
