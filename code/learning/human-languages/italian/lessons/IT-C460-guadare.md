## You'll want to know: guadare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C460-BOSCO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**guadare** — "to wade across, to ford".

> Abbiamo guadato il ruscello a piedi.
>
> *We waded across the brook on foot.*

*Il guado* — the ford.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guadare* is built on *guado* 'ford', which is inherited from Latin *vadum* 'shallow place' (Lewis & Short), its *gu-* shaped by a Germanic word.

## What you've built
