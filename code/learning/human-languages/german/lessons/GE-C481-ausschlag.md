## You'll want to know: der Ausschlag
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C481-ALLERGIE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ausschlag** — "rash".

> Nach der neuen Creme bekam sie einen Ausschlag.
>
> *After the new cream she got a rash.*

Plural: *die Ausschläge*. *Den Ausschlag geben* — to tip the balance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ausschlag* is built on *aus* "out" and *Schlag* "blow", inherited from Old High German *slag*, from Proto-Germanic *slagiz* (EtymDB): something that breaks out on the skin.
