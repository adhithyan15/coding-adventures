## You'll want to know: o telejornal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C278-NOTICIARIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o telejornal** — "the TV news".

> O telejornal começa às oito.
>
> *The TV news starts at eight.*

Built on *televisão* and *jornal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*telejornal* joins *tele-*, from Greek *tēle* "far off", and *jornal*, from Latin *diurnālis* "daily", built on *diēs* "day" (Lewis & Short).
