## You'll want to know: مناخ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C463-MUNAKH463-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مناخ** — *munākh* — "climate".

> مناخ الصحراء جاف جدا.
>
> *munākh aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ jāff jiddan.*
>
> The climate of the desert is very dry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مناخ** (*munākh*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-w-kh*, "to make a camel kneel" (Lane). A *munākh* was a halting place where camels knelt; modern Arabic gave the old word the sense of climate.
