## You'll want to know: l'efficienza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C362-STRESS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'efficienza** — "the efficiency".

> Con il nuovo computer l'efficienza è aumentata.
>
> *With the new computer, efficiency has gone up.*

*In piena efficienza* — in full working order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*efficienza* is a learned borrowing from Latin *efficientia* "efficiency" (EtymDB), from *efficere* "to bring about", built on *facere* "to do".
