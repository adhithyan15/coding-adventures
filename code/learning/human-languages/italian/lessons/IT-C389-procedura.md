## You'll want to know: la procedura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C389-PRATICA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la procedura** — "the procedure".

> La procedura è lunga, ma non è difficile.
>
> *The procedure is long, but it is not difficult.*

*Seguire la procedura* — to follow the procedure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*procedura* came from French *procédure*, built on *procéder*, from Latin *prōcēdere* "to go forward" (Lewis & Short), built on *cēdere* "to go".
