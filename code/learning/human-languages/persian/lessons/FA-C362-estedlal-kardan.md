## You'll want to know: استدلال کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C362-ESTEDLAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**استدلال کردن** (*estedlâl kardan*) — "to argue, to reason".

> وکیل با دقت استدلال کرد.
>
> *vakil bâ deqqat estedlâl kard.*
>
> The lawyer argued carefully.

*Estedlâl* "reasoning" + *kardan* (stem *kon-*). To argue that …: *estedlâl mikonad ke* … .

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استدلال کردن** joins *estedlâl*, from Arabic *istidlāl* "reasoning" (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian verb *kardan* "to do".
