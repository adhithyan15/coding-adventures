## You'll want to know: نابرابری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C386-BARABARI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نابرابری** (*nâbarâbari*) — "inequality".

> نابرابری درآمد در این کشور زیاد است.
>
> *nâbarâbari-ye darâmad dar in keshvar ziyâd ast.*
>
> Income inequality in this country is high.

*Nâ-* "not" + *barâbari* "equality".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نابرابری** is native Persian, built from the negative prefix *nâ-* "not" + *barâbari* "equality", from *barâbar* "equal".
