## You'll want to know: तहसील
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C208-OFFICES208-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तहसील** — *tahsīl* — "a sub-district office; a tehsil".

> तहसील सोमवार बंद है।
>
> *tahsīl somvār band hai.*
>
> The tehsil office is closed on Monday.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तहसील** (*tahsīl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taḥṣīl*, "collecting": probably the office where land revenue was collected.
