## You'll want to know: लीपना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C517-EENT517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लीपना** — *līpnā* — "to smear (a floor or wall with mud)".

> गाँव में औरतें मिट्टी से आँगन लीपती हैं।
>
> *Gā̃v meṁ aurteṁ miṭṭī se āṁgan līptī haiṁ.*
>
> In the village the women smear the courtyard with mud.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चूल्हा लीपा, *usne cūlhā līpā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लीपना** (*līpnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *lipyatē*, "is smeared" (CDIAL 11061).
