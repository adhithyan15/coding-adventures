## You'll want to know: みかぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C519-GIVEUP519-03]; assesses=[] -->

**みかぎる** — *mikagiru* — "to give up on (someone)".

> **もし かれが いつも おくれたら、みんな かれを みかぎるでしょう。** — *moshi kare ga itsumo okuretara, minna kare o mikagiru deshō* — "If he is always late, everyone will give up on him."

Polite **みかぎります**, *mikagirimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **きゃくが みせを みかぎりました。**, *kyaku ga mise o mikagirimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みかぎる** (*mikagiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
