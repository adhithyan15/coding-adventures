Persian, chapter 88: Library, Pharmacy, Bakery, Cinema, Museum.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
کتابخانه (ketâbkhâne) — a library.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a coast, then the Persian for a church.

You'll want to know: کتابخانه.
کتابخانه — ketâbkhâne — "a library".

[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: noon, the day before yesterday, tonight, last night, time]

[your turn — say: spring, summer, autumn, winter, a season]

[your turn — say: Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: now, never, this, that, these]

[your turn — say: those, this very, that very, such, every]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Noon? (ظهر, zohr.) The day before yesterday? (پریروز, pariruz.) Tonight? (امشب, emshab.) Last night? (دیشب, dishab.) Time? (زمان, zamân.) Spring? (بهار, bahâr.) Summer? (تابستان, tâbestân.) Autumn? (پاییز, pâyiz.) Winter? (زمستان, zemestân.) A season? (فصل, fasl.) Saturday? (شنبه, shanbe.) Sunday? (یکشنبه, yekshanbe.) Monday? (دوشنبه, doshanbe.) Wednesday? (چهارشنبه, chahârshanbe.) Thursday? (پنجشنبه, panjshanbe.) Now? (اکنون, aknun.) Never? (هرگز, hargez.) This? (این, in.) That? (آن, ân.) These? (اینها, inhâ.) Those? (آنها, ânhâ.) This very? (همین, hamin.) That very? (همان, hamân.) Such? (چنین, chenin.) Every? (هر, har.)
