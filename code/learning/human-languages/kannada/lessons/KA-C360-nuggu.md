# ನುಗ್ಗು (nuggu) — to barge in; to rush in

## You'll want to know: ನುಗ್ಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C360-BETE360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನುಗ್ಗು** — *nuggu* — "to barge in; to rush in".

> ಆನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಹೊಲಕ್ಕೆ ನುಗ್ಗಿತು.
>
> *āne rātri holakke nuggitu.*
>
> An elephant barged into the field at night.

Past (I …): ನುಗ್ಗಿದೆ, *nuggide*.

Where one rushes takes -ಗೆ or -ಒಳಗೆ: ಮನೆಯೊಳಗೆ ನುಗ್ಗು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನುಗ್ಗು** (*nuggu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3714 lists Kannada *nuggu* beside Tamil words meaning "to creep through, to penetrate, to get into".
