## Added — Tamil B1 vocabulary, 1,325 words in chapters 294-558; Tamil attains B1

- Added chapters 294-558: 1,325 B1 headwords (399 verbs), one word per lesson,
  with eight reviews in each of five tranches (40 in all; 1,365 lessons). Each
  lesson gives the word, its romanization (strict ISO 15919, computed from the
  script), an example with romanization and translation, a note (the past, the
  plural, the case a verb takes, a spoken form named as such) and an origin
  citing DEDR (Dravidian), CDIAL or Monier-Williams (Sanskrit), a named donor
  for a loan, or hedged. Tranches sit on the B1 nodes: narrating (294-346),
  reasons and conditions (347-399), travel (400-452), experience (453-505),
  and work, the home, free time, then plans and risks (506-558).
- Verbs are given in the bare stem the A2 chapters use, with the 1sg past
  (திருடினேன் "I stole"); impersonal verbs show the 3rd person neuter past.
- The first words of chapters 294-301 practise the core's last grammar points
  (… -ஐப் பொறுத்தது, the unreal -இருந்தால் … -இருப்பேன், -ஆல், ஒருவேளை …
  -ஆல் / என்றால்), two words each; the first words of chapters 308 and 325
  bring back ஈ through ஈரம். No atom of the track is revisited fewer than
  twice.
- Re-screened after the core landed: two review cues narrowed because the
  core took them (நன்மை is now "goodness", கோல் "a goal in sport"). No
  vocabulary headword holds ஈ or ஃ, so ஈரம் stays the one ஈ glyph gap.
- With the B1 core, Tamil teaches 2,563 headwords at or below B1 and attains
  B1 on the level gate. Forward references stay at 0, root-slug splits add
  none, and data/scripts/tamil.d is unchanged.
