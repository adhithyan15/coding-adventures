## You'll want to know: ارتاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C496-HAMAS496-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتاح** — *irtāḥa* — "to feel relieved".

> ارتاح أبي عندما وصلنا إلى البيت.
>
> *irtāḥa abī ʿindamā waṣalnā ilā al-bayt.*
>
> My father felt relieved when we reached home.

Present: يرتاح, *yartāḥ*.

The same root gives استراح, to rest, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتاح** (*irtāḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-w-ḥ*, "wind, breath, rest" (Lane). It is Form VIII of the root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
