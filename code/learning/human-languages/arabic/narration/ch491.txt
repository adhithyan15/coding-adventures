Arabic, chapter 491: Pride and thanks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فخور (fakhūr) — proud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to be surprised, then the Arabic for admiration.

You'll want to know: فخور.
فخور — fakhūr — "proud".
الأم فخورة بابنها الطبيب. al-umm fakhūra bi-ibnihā aṭ-ṭabīb. The mother is proud of her son the doctor.
It takes بـ: فخور (fakhūr) بـ, proud of.

The word, taken apart.
فخور (fakhūr) is built on the native Arabic root f-kh-r, "to boast, to glory in" (Lane). Fakhūr is a pattern for a lasting trait: one who takes pride.

Lesson 3 of 5.
امتنان (imtinān) — gratitude.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for proud, then the Arabic for to take pride.

You'll want to know: امتنان.
امتنان — imtinān — "gratitude".
كتبت رسالة امتنان إلى الممرضة. katabtu risālat imtinān ilā al-mumarriḍa. I wrote a letter of thanks to the nurse.

The word, taken apart.
امتنان (imtinān) is built on the native Arabic root m-n-n, "to grant a favour" (Lane). Imtinān is the feeling of one who has been granted a favour.

Lesson 5 of 5.
أعجب (aʿjaba) — to impress, to please.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for gratitude, then the Arabic for grateful.

You'll want to know: أعجب.
أعجب — aʿjaba — "to impress, to please".
أعجبني الفيلم كثيرا. aʿjabanī al-fīlm kathīran. I liked the film a lot.
Present: يعجب, yuʿjib.
The one pleased is the object: أعجبني الفيلم, the film pleased me.

The word, taken apart.
أعجب (aʿjaba) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-j-b, "to wonder, to marvel" (Lane). It is Form IV: to make someone marvel. Iʿjāb, admiration, is its verbal noun.
