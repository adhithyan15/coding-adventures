## You'll want to know: حد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C376-MIQDAR376-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حد** — *had* — "a limit".

> اگر آپ نے حد پار کی تو جرمانہ ہو گا۔
>
> *agar āp ne had pār kī to jurmānā ho gā.*
>
> If you go past the limit, there will be a fine.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حد** (*had*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥadd*, "a boundary, a limit".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
