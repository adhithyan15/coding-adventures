## You'll want to know: दलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C523-CHAKU523-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दलना** — *dalnā* — "to grind coarsely; to split (pulses)".

> माँ ने घर पर दाल दली।
>
> *Māṁ ne ghar par dāl dalī.*
>
> Mother split the pulses at home.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चावल दले, *usne cāval dale*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दलना** (*dalnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *dalati*, "bursts, splits" (CDIAL 6216). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to grind coarsely".
