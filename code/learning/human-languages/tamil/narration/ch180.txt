சுறுசுறுப்பான — cuṟucuṟuppāṉa — "active, energetic".

The word, taken apart.
சுறுசுறுப்பான (cuṟucuṟuppāṉa) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2713). The entry lists cuṟucuṟuppu, "diligence, bustling", under cuṟukku, "quickness"; -āṉa makes it "active".

Lesson 3 of 5.
பிடிவாதமான (piṭivātamāṉa) — stubborn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for active, then the Tamil for naughty.

You'll want to know: பிடிவாதமான.
பிடிவாதமான — piṭivātamāṉa — "stubborn".

The word, taken apart.
பிடிவாதமான (piṭivātamāṉa) is piṭivātam, "stubbornness": native piṭi, "to seize, to hold" (DEDR 4148), and vātam, "argument", from Sanskrit vāda, "speech, dispute" (CDIAL 11507). It is "holding to one's argument".
