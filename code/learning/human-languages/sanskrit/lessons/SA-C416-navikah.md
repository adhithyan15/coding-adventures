## You'll want to know: नाविकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C416-NAVIKA416-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नाविकः** — *nāvikaḥ* — "a boatman".

> नाविकः नौकां नदीतीरम् आनयति।
>
> *nāvikaḥ naukāṃ nadītīram ānayati.*
>
> The boatman brings the boat to the riverbank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाविकः** (*nāvikaḥ*) is built on *nau*, "a boat", with the ending *-ika*; MW gives *nāvika* as a helmsman, a pilot, a sailor (MW).
