Hindi, chapter 384: Heat and Cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तड़कना (taṛaknā) — to crack (with a sharp sound).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to unravel, then the Hindi for to get wet.

You'll want to know: तड़कना.
तड़कना — taṛaknā — "to crack (with a sharp sound)".
अगर ठंडे गिलास में गरम चाय भरोगे तो गिलास तड़क जाएगा। agar ṭhaṇḍe gilās meṁ garam cāy bharoge to gilās taṛak jāegā. If you fill a cold glass with hot tea, the glass will crack.
Past (it …): वह गिलास तड़क गया, vah gilās taṛak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
तड़कना (taṛaknā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit traṭ, "crackle" (CDIAL 5988).
