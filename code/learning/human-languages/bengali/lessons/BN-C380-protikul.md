# প্রতিকূল (prôtikūl) — unfavourable; adverse

## You'll want to know: প্রতিকূল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C380-OPOJUKTO380-02]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রতিকূল** — *prôtikūl* — "unfavourable; adverse".

> পরিস্থিতি প্রতিকূল হলে আমরা ফিরে আসব।
>
> *pôristhiti prôtikūl hôle āmrā phire āsbô.*
>
> If the situation turns against us, we'll come back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রতিকূল** (*prôtikūl*) is Sanskrit *pratikūla*, 'against the bank', contrary (Monier-Williams), the opposite of অনুকূল.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
