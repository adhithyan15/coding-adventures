## You'll want to know: ખેડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C233-WORK233-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ખેડવું** — *kheḍvũ* — "to plough, to till".

> **ગઈકાલે તેણે ખેતર ખેડ્યું.** — *gaīkāle teṇe khetar kheḍyũ* — "Yesterday he ploughed the field."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખેડવું** (*kheḍvũ*) comes from *\*khēḍ-*, "open up, plough", a reconstructed form that CDIAL places under *\*khiḍ*, "be open, expand"; it glosses the Gujarati verb "to plough, drive" (CDIAL 3882).
