Lesson 1 of 6.
కాపురం (kāpuraṁ) — married life; a household.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to coax, then the Telugu for a tantrum.

You'll want to know: కాపురం.
కాపురం — kāpuraṁ — "married life; a household".
వాళ్ళ కాపురం సంతోషంగా సాగుతోంది. vāḷḷa kāpuraṁ santōṣaṅgā sāgutōndi. Their married life is going happily.
కాపురం (kāpuraṁ) పెట్టు, to set up house.

The word, taken apart.
కాపురం (kāpuraṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1456 lists Telugu kāpuramu, "dwelling, residence", beside kāpu, "a cultivator".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ముచ్చటించు.
ముచ్చటించు — muccaṭiñcu — "to chat; to have a heart-to-heart".
సాయంత్రం అమ్మమ్మ పక్కింటివాళ్ళతో ముచ్చటించింది. sāyantraṁ ammamma pakkiṇṭivāḷḷatō muccaṭiñcindi. In the evening grandma chatted with the neighbours.
Past (I …): ముచ్చటించాను, muccaṭiñcānu.
-తో ముచ్చటించు (muccaṭiñcu); ముచ్చట, a pleasant chat or a charming thing.

The word, taken apart.
ముచ్చటించు (muccaṭiñcu) is built on ముచ్చట, "a pleasant talk; a charming thing", with the verb ending -ఇంచు; the origin of ముచ్చట is not settled.
