## You'll want to know: صهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C529-AHL529-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صهر** — *ṣihr* — "a son-in-law; a brother-in-law".

> ساعد صهري أبي في نقل الأثاث.
>
> *sāʿada ṣihrī abī fī naql al-athāth.*
>
> My son-in-law helped my father move the furniture.

Plural: أصهار, *aṣhār*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صهر** (*ṣihr*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-h-r*, "kinship by marriage" (Lane). Lane gives ṣihr as a relation by marriage.
