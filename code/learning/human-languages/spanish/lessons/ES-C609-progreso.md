## You'll want to know: el progreso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C609-PROGRESO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el progreso** — "the progress".

> Has hecho muchos progresos.
>
> *You have made a lot of progress.*

*Hacer progresos* — to make progress; the plural is usual here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*progreso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *progressus* 'an advance', built on *progredi* 'to go forward', from *gradi* 'to step' (Lewis & Short).
