Tamil, chapter 411: When the Car Breaks Down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கோளாறு (kōḷāṟu) — a fault; a malfunction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to stroll, then the Tamil for to cross over.

You'll want to know: கோளாறு.
கோளாறு — kōḷāṟu — "a fault; a malfunction".
இயந்திரத்தில் ஏதோ கோளாறு இருக்கிறது. iyantirattil ētō kōḷāṟu irukkiṟatu. There is some fault in the engine.
Of the body too: வயிற்றுக் கோளாறு (kōḷāṟu), a stomach upset.

The word, taken apart.
கோளாறு (kōḷāṟu), "a fault, a disorder", is an everyday Tamil word whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
செயலிழ (ceyaliḻa) — to stop working; to fail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to cross over, then the Tamil for a fault.

You'll want to know: செயலிழ.
செயலிழ — ceyaliḻa — "to stop working; to fail".
நடுவழியில் எங்கள் காரின் இயந்திரம் செயலிழந்தது. naṭuvaḻiyil eṅkaḷ kāriṉ iyantiram ceyaliḻantatu. Halfway, the engine of our car stopped working.
Past (it …): செயலிழந்தது, ceyaliḻantatu.
செயல், an action, + இழ, to lose: to lose the power to act. Said of machines and of organs of the body.

The word, taken apart.
செயலிழ (ceyaliḻa) is built from செயல், "an action", the noun of செய், "to do" (DEDR 1957), and இழ, "to lose" (DEDR 501): to lose the power to act. Like இழ, it makes its past in -ந்த-.

Past (I …): உரசினேன், uraciṉēṉ.
