## You'll want to know: график
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C360-SOVESHCHANIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**график** (*gráfik*) — "a schedule; a graph".

> У меня свободный график работы.
>
> *U menyá svobódnyy gráfik rabóty.*
>
> I have flexible working hours.

Masculine. *Гибкий график* — flexible hours. It is also a chart of figures.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**график** is usually traced, through German, to Greek *graphikós* "of drawing or writing", built on *gráphein* "to scratch, to draw, to write". A schedule is a time plan drawn out.
