Arabic, chapter 194: To swallow, To chew, To touch, To smell, To hold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ابتلع (ibtalaʿa) — to swallow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to bargain, then the Arabic for to go shopping.

You'll want to know: ابتلع.
ابتلع — ibtalaʿa — "to swallow".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ibtalaʿa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ibtalaʿa, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ibtalaʿa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to bargain, then the Arabic for to go shopping, then say ibtalaʿa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ابتلع (ibtalaʿa) mean? ("To swallow".) Say it once more.
