## You'll want to know: કરજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C371-FAYDO371-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કરજ** — *karaj* — "a debt".

> જો તમે વધારે કરજ લેશો તો વ્યાજ પણ વધારે ભરવું પડશે.
>
> *jo tame vadhāre karaj leśo to vyāj paṇ vadhāre bharvũ paḍśe.*
>
> If you take on more debt, you will have to pay more interest too.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કરજ** (*karaj*) is usually traced to Arabic *qarḍ*, "a loan", through Persian *qarz*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
