## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-CONCEPT-C02-PRACTICE-01]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C02-ALAAP-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMAR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMARNAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-KI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TOMARNAAMKI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TUMIAPNI-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: "My name is Mira." — *āmār nām Mira.*]
- [YOU SAY: "What's your name?" (respectful) — *āpnār nām ki?*]
- [YOU SAY: "My name is Arun." — *āmār nām Arun.*]
- [YOU SAY: "Pleased to meet you." — *ālāp kore bhālo lāglo.*]

## The word, taken apart — the atoms
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C02-ALAAP-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMAR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMARNAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-KI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-PRACTICE-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TOMARNAAMKI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TUMIAPNI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: "name," "my" (*nām*, *āmār*)]
- [YOU SAY: the three "you" levels (*tui / tumi / āpni*)]
- [YOU SAY: "what," and the word Bengali leaves out (*ki*; there is no "is")]

## What you've built — roots you now carry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C02-ALAAP-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMAR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMARNAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-KI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-PRACTICE-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TOMARNAAMKI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TUMIAPNI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *nām* ← Sanskrit *nāman* → English **name**
- *āmār* ← the first-person *ām-*; no gender (unlike Hindi *merā/merī*)
- *ki* ← *ka-* → English **what**; *tumi* ← *\*tū* → **thou**
- *ālāp* ← Sanskrit *ālāpa*, "conversing"
