## You'll want to know: தறி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C538-NOOL538-04]; assesses=[] -->

**தறி** — *taṟi* — "a loom".

> நெசவாளர் தறியில் சேலை நெய்கிறார்.
>
> *necavāḷar taṟiyil cēlai neykiṟār.*
>
> The weaver weaves a sari on the loom.

A hand loom is a கைத்தறி.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தறி** (*taṟi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3140 gives தறி (*taṟi*), "to cut off", and the noun for a wooden post and a weaver's loom.
