## You'll want to know: রূপকথা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C321-KINGBODONTI321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**রূপকথা** — *rūpkôthā* — "a fairy tale".

> দিদিমা রোজ রাতে আমাদের একটা রূপকথা শোনাতেন।
>
> *didimā roj rāte āmāder ekṭā rūpkôthā shonāten.*
>
> Every night my grandmother used to tell us a fairy tale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রূপকথা** (*rūpkôthā*) is usually explained as রূপ, 'form, beauty' — Sanskrit *rūpa*, 'outward appearance' (Monier-Williams) — joined to **কথা**, a story: a tale of wonders.
