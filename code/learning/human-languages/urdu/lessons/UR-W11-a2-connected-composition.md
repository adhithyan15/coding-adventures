# A connected message about your day

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-AUR, UR-LEX-LEKIN, UR-LEX-KYUNKI, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-05, UR-LEX-C75-TIME75-01, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A connected message is not one long sentence. It is a short route
for one reader. Before writing, draw eight empty boxes and give them these jobs:

1. reader and greeting;
2. reason for writing;
3. where your day begins;
4. two things you usually do;
5. what happens next;
6. one contrast or reason;
7. one question for the reader;
8. a polite close.

Write only one or two English cue words in each box. Do not draft Urdu yet.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-AUR, UR-LEX-LEKIN, UR-LEX-KYUNKI, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-05, UR-LEX-C75-TIME75-01, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-04, UR-LEX-MERA-NAAM-HAI, UR-LEX-C50-VERBS50-04, UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-PASAND, UR-LEX-KAHAN] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

There is no model, sentence bank, or copyable answer on this page.

**Reader:** Sana. **Purpose:** help Sana understand your usual weekday and
choose a good time to visit you tomorrow.

Write **70–90 words in Urdu script**. Use every planning box. Include at least
four familiar actions, two time anchors, one preference, one contrast or
reason, one sequence link, and one direct question for Sana. Keep the reader
visible by making every detail help with the visit. The instructions give
content, not sentences: choose and connect the Urdu yourself.

This first connected attempt is untimed. Put away Roman Urdu, Devanagari,
dictionaries, translators, and spell-checkers. Either approved Nastaliq or
accessibility Naskh is fine. Ordinary legible Urdu handwriting is enough;
imitating Nastaliq calligraphic slope or ligatures is not part of the task.

When the whole message exists, count its words once. If it is short, add one
useful detail to a planning box. If it is long, remove a detail that does not
help Sana. Do not replace the message with a memorised model.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-AUR, UR-LEX-LEKIN, UR-LEX-KYUNKI, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-05, UR-LEX-C75-TIME75-01, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-04] -->

Review the same attempt in four separate passes:

1. **Task fulfilment and relevant content** — Sana, the weekday, tomorrow's
   visit, and every requested content point are present.
2. **Comprehensibility and organisation** — the eight jobs form a route rather
   than a list of disconnected sentences.
3. **Urdu vocabulary, grammar, and register** — connectors make addition,
   sequence, contrast, and reason clear in a polite everyday tone.
4. **Urdu orthographic control** — letters, dots, joins, non-joins, word
   boundaries, and punctuation remain readable. Unwritten short vowels and
   optional diacritics receive neither bonus nor penalty unless they change the
   intended word.

[PAUSE 2s] Mark one strength and one next step. Repair only the next step; keep
the original attempt so the next 70–90 word message can show real growth.
