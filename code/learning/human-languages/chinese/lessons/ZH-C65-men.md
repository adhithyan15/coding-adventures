## You'll want to know: 门
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-01]; assesses=[] -->

**门** — *mén* — "door; gate".

> **这是我家的门** *zhè shì wǒ jiā de mén* — This is the door to my home.

The new character, in pieces: 门 — a dot, a post down the left, and a frame that turns down the right and hooks: the right-hand piece of 们. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**门** (*mén*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *mwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary says its old character is made of two door leaves.
