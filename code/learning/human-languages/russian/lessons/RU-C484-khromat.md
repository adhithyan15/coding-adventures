## You'll want to know: хромать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C484-PLASTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**хромать** (*khromát*) — "to limp".

> Собака хромает на переднюю лапу.
>
> *Sobáka khromáyet na perédnyuyu lápu.*
>
> The dog is limping on its front paw.

Imperfective; present хромаю. The adjective is *хромой* — lame.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хромать* is built on *хромой* 'lame'; EtymDB lists both without a source, and *хромой* is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *xromъ*.
