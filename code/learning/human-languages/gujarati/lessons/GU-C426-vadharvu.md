## You'll want to know: વધારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C426-LAKH426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વધારવું** — *vadhārvũ* — "to increase; to raise (a price)".

> દુકાનદારે ભાવ વધાર્યો.
>
> *dukāndāre bhāv vadhāryo.*
>
> The shopkeeper put the price up.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ભાડું વધાર્યું, *teṇe bhāḍũ vadhāryũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વધારવું** (*vadhārvũ*) is built on વધારે, more, a tadbhava of Sanskrit *vṛddhatara*, "larger" (CDIAL 12074): to make something more. વધવું, to go up, is its partner.
