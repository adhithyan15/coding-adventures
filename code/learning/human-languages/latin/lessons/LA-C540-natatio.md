## You'll want to know: natātiō, natātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C540-PUPA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**natātiō, natātiōnis** — "swimming; a swimming place".

> Natātiō in marī mihi placet.
>
> *I like swimming in the sea.*

Feminine, third declension, from *natō* — to swim.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**natātiō** is the action noun of *natō* "to swim" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *natatorium*, a swimming pool, comes from the same verb.
