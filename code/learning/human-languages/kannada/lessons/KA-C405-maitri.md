## You'll want to know: ಮೈತ್ರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C405-SANDHANA405-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೈತ್ರಿ** — *maitri* — "an alliance; friendship".

> ಎರಡು ಊರುಗಳ ನಡುವೆ ಹಳೆಯ ಮೈತ್ರಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *eraḍu ūrugaḷa naḍuve haḷeya maitri ide.*
>
> There is an old friendship between the two towns.

Formal; between groups or countries.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೈತ್ರಿ** (*maitri*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *maitrī*, "friendship, goodwill" (Monier-Williams), from *mitra*, "a friend".
