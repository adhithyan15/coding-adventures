Russian, chapter 514: On the Ice and in the Water.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
каток (katók) — a skating rink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for dumbbells, then the Russian for stamina.

You'll want to know: каток.
каток (katók) — "a skating rink".
Зимой мы часто ходим на каток. Zimóy my chásto khódim na katók. In winter we often go to the skating rink.
Masculine; genitive катка.

The word, taken apart.
каток (katók) is built on the root of катать 'to roll' and кататься 'to ride, to skate'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
скользить (skolzít) — to slide, to glide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a skating rink, then the Russian for a figure skater.

You'll want to know: скользить.
скользить (skolzít) — "to slide, to glide".
Фигуристы легко скользят по льду. Figurísty legkó skolzyát po ldu. The figure skaters glide easily over the ice.
Imperfective; present скольжу, скользишь (з becomes ж). Perfective скользнуть.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists скользить (skolzít) without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic skolьzěti.
