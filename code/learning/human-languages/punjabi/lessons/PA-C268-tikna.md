# ਟਿਕਣਾ (ṭikṇā) — to stay put; to stay for a while

## You'll want to know: ਟਿਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C268-STAND268-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟਿਕਣਾ** — *ṭikṇā* — "to stay put; to stay for a while".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੋ ਦਿਨ ਟਿਕਾਂਗੇ।
>
> *asīṁ shahir vich do din ṭikāṅge.*
>
> We will stay two days in the town.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅੱਡੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਟਿਕਿਆ, *maiṁ aḍḍe utte ṭikiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟਿਕਣਾ** (*ṭikṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭakk*, "remain, stop"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭikṇā*, "to stay", under it (CDIAL 5420).
