## You'll want to know: दाहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C477-BHRAMA477-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दाहः** — *dāhaḥ* — "a burning feeling; inner heat".

> ज्वरेण मम देहे दाहः अस्ति।
>
> *jvareṇa mama dehe dāhaḥ asti.*
>
> With the fever my body burns.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाहः** (*dāhaḥ*) comes from the root √dah, "to burn", the root of **दहति**; MW gives "burning, heat" and "internal heat, fever" (MW).
