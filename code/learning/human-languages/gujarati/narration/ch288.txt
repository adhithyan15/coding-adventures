Gujarati, chapter 288: The Confession.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ધરપકડ (dharpakaḍ) — an arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hide oneself, then the Gujarati for to break (something).

You'll want to know: ધરપકડ.
ધરપકડ — dharpakaḍ — "an arrest".
પોલીસે ગઈકાલે બે ચોરોની ધરપકડ કરી. polīse gaīkāle be coronī dharpakaḍ karī. Yesterday the police arrested two thieves.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધરપકડ (dharpakaḍ) is built from the stem of dharvũ, to hold, from Sanskrit dhárati (CDIAL 6747), and pakaḍ, a grip, the noun of પકડવું: a holding and a seizing. The police ધરપકડ કરે છે, make an arrest.

Lesson 2 of 5.
કબૂલવું (kabūlvũ) — to confess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to break (something), then the Gujarati for an arrest.

You'll want to know: કબૂલવું.
કબૂલવું — kabūlvũ — "to confess".
ચોરે થાણામાં ગુનો કબૂલ્યો. core thāṇāmā̃ guno kabūlyo. At the police station the thief confessed to the crime.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગુનો કબૂલ્યો, teṇe guno kabūlyo.

The word, taken apart.
કબૂલવું (kabūlvũ) is built on kabūl, usually traced through Persian to Arabic qabūl, "acceptance": to confess is to accept what you did. ગુનો કબૂલ્યો, he admitted the crime.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: નકારવું.
નકારવું — nakārvũ — "to deny".
પહેલાં તેણે બધું નકાર્યું. pahelā̃ teṇe badhũ nakāryũ. At first he denied everything.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગુનો નકાર્યો, teṇe guno nakāryo.

The word, taken apart.
નકારવું (nakārvũ) is built on Sanskrit nakāra, "the word na, no" (CDIAL 6907): to deny is to say no to something. You deny a thing: ગુનો નકાર્યો.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ગુનેગાર (gunegār) — a criminal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to confess, then the Gujarati for to deny.

You'll want to know: ગુનેગાર.
ગુનેગાર — gunegār — "a criminal".
જો ત્યાં અજવાળું હોત તો મીરા ગુનેગારને ઓળખત. jo tyā̃ ajvāḷũ hot to Mīrā gunegārne oḷkhat. If there had been light, Mira would have recognized the criminal.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ગુનેગાર (gunegār) is usually traced to Persian gunahgār, "a sinner, an offender", built on gunāh, the word behind ગુનો. It names the person, a man or a woman.

જેલ — jel — "a jail".
ચોર હવે જેલમાં છે. cor have jelmā̃ che. The thief is in jail now.

The word, taken apart.
જેલ (jel) is borrowed from English jail. It is feminine: જેલમાં, in jail; જેલમાંથી, out of jail.
