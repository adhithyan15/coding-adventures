## You'll want to know: testardo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C472-DIFETTI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**testardo** — "stubborn".

> Mio nonno è testardo: non cambia mai idea.
>
> *My grandfather is stubborn: he never changes his mind.*

From *testa* "head": someone with a hard head. *Avere la testa dura* means the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testardo* is built on *testa* "head", which is inherited from Latin *testa* "an earthen pot, a shell" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short); its later sense "skull" became the everyday word for head.
