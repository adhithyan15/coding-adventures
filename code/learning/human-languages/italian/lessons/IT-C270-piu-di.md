# più … di — more than

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-ETYMON-A-PIU-TARDI-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "see you later". (*A più tardi.*) Literally, "to more late".

[PAUSE 1s] That **più** is the whole of this lesson's new grammar.

## You'll want to know: più … di
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-PIU-DI-01]; assesses=[IT-LEX-C74-AGG3-01, IT-LEX-C74-AGG3-05, IT-LEX-C82-CITTA2-03, IT-LEX-C82-CITTA2-02] -->

To compare two things, put **più**, "more", in front of the adjective and
**di**, "than", in front of the second thing:

> Il treno è **più** veloce **dell'**autobus. — The train is faster than the
> bus.
> L'aereo è **più** caro **del** treno. — The plane is dearer than the train.
> Sei **più** alto **di** me. — You are taller than me.

English sometimes builds the comparison into the adjective, *faster*, *taller*;
Italian builds it the same way almost every time, with *più*. The **di** welds to the
article, as *a* does: **del**, **della**, **dell'**. Before a name or a stressed
pronoun it stays plain: *di me*, *di te*.

Its opposite is **meno**, "less": *L'autobus è **meno** caro **del** treno* —
the bus is less expensive than the train.

## Grammar Lens: il più
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-GRAMMAR-IL-PIU-02]; assesses=[IT-LEX-C72-AGG1-01, IT-LEX-C75-AGG4-01, IT-LEX-C72-AGG1-05] -->

For the most of all, keep *più* and give the noun its article:

> *il treno **più** veloce* — the fastest train
> *la città **più** bella* — the most beautiful city

The adjective still comes after the noun, as Italian adjectives mostly do, and
the article does the work of English *-est*. Who it is the most among comes
with *di*: *il treno più veloce **del** mondo*, the fastest train in the world.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**più** is inherited from Latin *plūs*, "more" (EtymDB 2.1). Latin *pl* became
Italian *pi*, the same change that made *plicāre* into **piegare**. English
borrowed *plūs* whole as **plus**, and built on it **plural** and **surplus**.
