## You'll want to know: le risque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C372-RISQUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le risque** — "the risk".

> Il y a un risque de pluie demain.
>
> *There is a chance of rain tomorrow.*

*Prendre un risque* — to take a risk; the verb is *risquer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*risque* comes from Old Italian *risco* "danger" (EtymDB). The further origin is not settled.
