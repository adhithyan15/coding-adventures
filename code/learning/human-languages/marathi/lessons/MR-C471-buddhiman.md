## You'll want to know: बुद्धिमान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C471-SAHANA471-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बुद्धिमान** — *buddhimān* — "intelligent".

> मीरा अभ्यासात बुद्धिमान आहे.
>
> *mīrā abhyāsāt buddhimān āhe.*
>
> Meera is bright at her studies.

बुद्धी, intellect, + -मान, having. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुद्धिमान** (*buddhimān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *buddhimat* (nominative *buddhimān*), "endowed with understanding, intelligent" (Monier-Williams).
