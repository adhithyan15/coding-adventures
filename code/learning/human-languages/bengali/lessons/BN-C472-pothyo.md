## You'll want to know: পথ্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C472-OSHUDH472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পথ্য** — *pôthyô* — "a diet for the sick; food a patient may eat".

> জ্বরের পরে দাদুর পথ্য ছিল ভাত আর মাছ।
>
> *jbôrer pôre dādur pôthyô chhilô bhāt ār māchh.*
>
> After the fever, my grandfather's diet was rice and fish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পথ্য** (*pôthyô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pathya*, "belonging to the way", and so "suitable, wholesome", said especially of diet (Monier-Williams).
