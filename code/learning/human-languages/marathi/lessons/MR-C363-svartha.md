## You'll want to know: स्वार्थ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C363-SATTA363-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वार्थ** — *svārtha* — "self-interest".

> तो फक्त स्वतःचा स्वार्थ पाहतो.
>
> *to phakta svataḥcā svārtha pāhto.*
>
> He only looks after his own interest.

Masculine.

स्वार्थी, selfish; निःस्वार्थ, selfless.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वार्थ** (*svārtha*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *svārtha*, "one's own aim, self-interest" (Monier-Williams).
