## You'll want to know: मणि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C337-KHAZANA337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मणि** — *maṇi* — "a jewel; a gem".

> नाग के सिर पर एक चमकदार मणि थी।
>
> *nāg ke sir par ek camakdār maṇi thī.*
>
> The cobra had a shining jewel on its head.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मणि** (*maṇi*) is Sanskrit *maṇi*, "a jewel, a gem" (Monier-Williams). Old stories tell of a jewel in a serpent's hood.
