## You'll want to know: el sótano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C774-DESVAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el sótano** — "the basement, the cellar".

> El sótano siempre estaba frío y oscuro.
>
> *The basement was always cold and dark.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sótano* is usually traced to spoken Latin *\*subtulum* 'a place below', built on *subtus* 'beneath' (Lewis & Short).
