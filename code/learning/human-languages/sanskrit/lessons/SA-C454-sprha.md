## You'll want to know: स्पृहा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C454-IRSYA454-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पृहा** — *spṛhā* — "covetous longing".

> धनस्य स्पृहा सन्तोषं हरति।
>
> *dhanasya spṛhā santoṣaṃ harati.*
>
> Longing for money takes contentment away.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पृहा** (*spṛhā*) comes from the root √spṛh, "to desire eagerly"; MW gives "eager desire, covetousness, envy, longing for" (MW).
