## You'll want to know: fälschen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C285-BETRUG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fälschen** — "to forge, to fake".

> Er fälschte einen Ausweis.
>
> *He forged an identity card.*

*Gefälscht* — fake: *ein gefälschter Pass*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fälschen* comes from Old High German *felsken* (EtymDB), built on *falsch* "false", which came from Latin *falsus*, from *fallere* "to deceive" (Lewis & Short).
