Gujarati, chapter 434: Showers and Drizzle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઝાપટું (jhāpṭũ) — a sharp shower (of rain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to suit, then the Gujarati for to shrink.

You'll want to know: ઝાપટું.
ઝાપટું — jhāpṭũ — "a sharp shower (of rain)".
બપોરે વરસાદનું એક ઝાપટું આવ્યું. bapore varsādnũ ek jhāpṭũ āvyũ. In the afternoon there was a sharp shower.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઝાપટું (jhāpṭũ) goes back to jhapp, "a sudden movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a sharp shower of rain" (CDIAL 5336).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઝરમર (jharmar) — a drizzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to shrink, then the Gujarati for a sharp shower.

You'll want to know: ઝરમર.
ઝરમર — jharmar — "a drizzle".
સવારથી ઝરમર વરસે છે. savārthī jharmar varse che. It has been drizzling since the morning.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઝરમર (jharmar) goes back to Sanskrit jhara, "a waterfall, a stream"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a drizzle" (CDIAL 5343). Its doubled sound is like the rain itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઘેરાવું (gherāvũ) — to gather (of clouds); to close in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a drizzle, then the Gujarati for a raincoat.

You'll want to know: ઘેરાવું.
ઘેરાવું — gherāvũ — "to gather (of clouds); to close in".
સાંજે આકાશમાં કાળાં વાદળ ઘેરાયાં. sā̃je ākāśmā̃ kāḷā̃ vādaḷ gherāyā̃. In the evening dark clouds gathered in the sky.
Past (it …): તે વાદળ ઘેરાયું, te vādaḷ gherāyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઘેરાવું (gherāvũ) goes back to ghir, "to surround", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4474): clouds that close in round the sky.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભીંજાવું (bhī̃jāvũ) — to get wet through (in rain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a raincoat, then the Gujarati for to gather (of clouds).

You'll want to know: ભીંજાવું.
ભીંજાવું — bhī̃jāvũ — "to get wet through (in rain)".
છત્રી ન હતી, એટલે હું ભીંજાઈ ગયો. chatrī na hatī, eṭle hũ bhī̃jāī gayo. I had no umbrella, so I got wet through.
Past (he …): તે ભીંજાઈ ગયો, te bhī̃jāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
ભીંજાવું (bhī̃jāvũ) goes back to bhiyañjati, "anoints, wets", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be wetted" (CDIAL 9503).
