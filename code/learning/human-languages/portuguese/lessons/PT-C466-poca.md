## You'll want to know: a poça
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C466-CHUVA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a poça** — "the puddle".

> O menino salta para as poças.
>
> *The boy jumps into the puddles.*

*Uma poça de água* — a puddle of water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*poça* is built on *poço* 'well, pit' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *puteus*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
