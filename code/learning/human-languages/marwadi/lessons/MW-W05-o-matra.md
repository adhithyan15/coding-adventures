# ो — turn *ra* into *ro*

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01, MW-SCRIPT-VIRAMA-01, MW-SCRIPT-SAA-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Write known **सा** once and point to **ी** in **पाणी**. Write
**र** once, then draw yesterday's vowel-off mark beneath a spare **म**. Today
adds one sign to the right of **र**.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

> र + ो → रो

Attached **ो** replaces the built-in *a*. Read **रो** as *ro*. It is one
sign, even though it reaches above and to the right.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01, MW-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **रो** visible and copy it once. Point to **र**, then **ो**, and read *ro*.
