## You'll want to know: はしる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はしる** — *hashiru* — "to run".

> **けさ こうえんを はしりました。** — *kesa kōen o hashirimashita* — "This morning I ran in the park."

Polite **はしります**, *hashirimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はしる** (*hashiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
