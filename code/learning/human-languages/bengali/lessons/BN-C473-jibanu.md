## You'll want to know: জীবাণু
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C473-SONGKROMON473-02]; assesses=[] -->

**জীবাণু** — *jībānu* — "a germ".

> সাবান দিয়ে হাত ধুলে জীবাণু মরে।
>
> *sābān diye hāt dhule jībānu môre.*
>
> Washing your hands with soap kills germs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জীবাণু** (*jībānu*) is a modern formation from Sanskrit *jīva*, "living", and *aṇu*, "minute, an atom" (Monier-Williams): a tiny living thing.
