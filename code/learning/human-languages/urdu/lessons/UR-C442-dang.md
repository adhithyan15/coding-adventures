# دنگ (dang) — dumbfounded

## You'll want to know: دنگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C442-SURPRISE442-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دنگ** — *dang* — "dumbfounded".

> اس کا کام دیکھ کر میں دنگ رہ گیا۔
>
> *us kā kām dekh kar maiṅ dang rah gayā.*
>
> I was dumbfounded when I saw his work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دنگ** (*dang*) is usually traced to Persian *dang*, "stunned, stupefied".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
