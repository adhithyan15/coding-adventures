# అంశం (aṁśaṁ) — a point; an issue

## You'll want to know: అంశం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C384-SUCANA384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అంశం** — *aṁśaṁ* — "a point; an issue".

> సమావేశంలో మూడు ముఖ్యమైన అంశాలు చర్చించారు.
>
> *samāvēśaṁlō mūḍu mukhyamaina aṁśālu carciñcāru.*
>
> They discussed three important points at the meeting.

Plural: అంశాలు, *aṁśālu*.

In the plural the -ం drops and -ాలు comes in, as పుస్తకం becomes పుస్తకాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అంశం** (*aṁśaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *aṁśa*, "a share, a part" (CDIAL 2). In Telugu a part of a discussion is a point or an issue.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
