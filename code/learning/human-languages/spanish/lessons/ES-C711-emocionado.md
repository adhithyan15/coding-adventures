## You'll want to know: emocionado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C711-EMOCION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**emocionado** — "excited; moved".

> Estoy muy emocionado por el viaje.
>
> *I am very excited about the trip.*

Used with *estar*. It covers both excited and touched.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emocionado* is the participle of *emocionar*, built on *emoción*, which was borrowed from French *émotion* (EtymDB).
