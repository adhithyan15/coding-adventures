# أولا، ثم، بعد ذلك (awwalan, thumma, baʿda dhālika) — first, then, after that

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PAST-PERSONS-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C283-KANA-IMPERFECT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "we went", "we ate", "we came back". (*Dhahabnā*, *akalnā*,
*rajaʿnā*.)

[PAUSE 1s] Three events in a row are a list. A few small words turn them
into a story, and you already own most of them.

## Grammar Lens: first, then, after that
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C284-STORY-STEPS-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C268-SABAB268-05, AR-LEX-C176-SABAB176-01, AR-LEX-C88-TIME88-01, AR-LEX-C93-TOWN93-02, AR-LEX-C93-TOWN93-05, AR-LEX-C92-TOWN92-04] -->

*Awwalan* opens the first step. *Thumma* moves to the next, with a little
time between. *Baʿda dhālika*, "after that", is *baʿda* with *dhālika*,
"that", the same *dhālika* that sits inside *li-dhālika*.

> **أولا ذهبنا إلى السوق، ثم أكلنا في مطعم، وبعد ذلك رجعنا إلى الفندق.** —
> *awwalan dhahabnā ilā as-sūq, thumma akalnā fī maṭʿam, wa-baʿda dhālika
> rajaʿnā ilā al-funduq* — First we went to the market, then we ate in a
> restaurant, and after that we went back to the hotel.

Each joiner stands at the head of its own step, and each step has its own
past verb.

## Grammar Lens: closing the story
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C284-FI-AN-NIHAYA-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C203-WAQT203-02, AR-LEX-C165-ACT165-03, AR-LEX-C62-VERBS62-03, AR-LEX-C71-HOME71-03] -->

**في النهاية** — *fī an-nihāya* — "in the end". *Nihāya* is "an end", from
the root of *intahā*, "to end".

> **في النهاية وجدت المفتاح.** — *fī an-nihāya wajadtu al-miftāḥ* — In the
> end I found the key.

*Akhīran*, "at last", closes a story too, and it usually carries relief:
the thing you were waiting for came.
