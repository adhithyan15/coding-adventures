## You'll want to know: അനുസ്മരണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C393-SMARAKAM393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അനുസ്മരണം** — *anusmaraṇaṁ* — "a commemoration".

> കവിയുടെ അനുസ്മരണം ഗ്രന്ഥശാലയിൽ നടന്നു.
>
> *kaviyuṭe anusmaraṇaṁ granthaśālayil naṭannu.*
>
> The poet's commemoration took place in the library.

A gathering held to remember someone who has died.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അനുസ്മരണം** (*anusmaraṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anusmaraṇa*, "remembering, repeated recollection" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
