## You'll want to know: रियाज़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C532-GEET532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रियाज़** — *riyāz* — "practice (of music)".

> अच्छा गायक बनने के लिए रोज़ रियाज़ करना पड़ता है।
>
> *Acchā gāyak banne ke liye roz riyāz karnā paṛtā hai.*
>
> To become a good singer you have to practise every day.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रियाज़** (*riyāz*) is usually traced to Arabic *riyāḍa*, "exercise, training", through Persian.
