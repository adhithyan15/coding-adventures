## You'll want to know: उद्घाटयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C429-UDGHATANA429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्घाटयति** — *udghāṭayati* — "opens; unlocks".

> आपणिकः प्रातः आपणम् उद्घाटयति।
>
> *āpaṇikaḥ prātaḥ āpaṇam udghāṭayati.*
>
> The shopkeeper opens the shop in the morning.

Past (he …): सः आपणम् उद्घाटितवान्, *saḥ āpaṇam udghāṭitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्घाटयति** (*udghāṭayati*) is built from *ud*, "up, out", and the causative of the root √ghaṭ, which also gives *ghaṭate*, "happens"; MW gives *ud-ghāṭayati* as to open, unlock, unfasten, with the participle *udghāṭita* (MW).
