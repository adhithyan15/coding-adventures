## You'll want to know: عنوان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C330-SAFHA330-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عنوان** — *unvān* — "a title of a book; a heading".

> کتاب کا عنوان کیا ہے؟
>
> *kitāb kā unvān kyā hai?*
>
> What is the book's title?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عنوان** (*unvān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿunwān*, "the heading of a letter, a title".
