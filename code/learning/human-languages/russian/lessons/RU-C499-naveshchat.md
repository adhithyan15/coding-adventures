## You'll want to know: навещать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C499-VISITS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**навещать** (*naveshchát*) — "to visit (a person)".

> По воскресеньям мы навещаем бабушку.
>
> *Po voskresényam my naveshcháyem bábushku.*
>
> On Sundays we visit Grandma.

Imperfective; perfective навестить (навещу). Takes the accusative of the person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*навещать* is built on *на-* and the root of *весть* 'news'; EtymDB has no entry, and the link is usually made through *навестить*.
