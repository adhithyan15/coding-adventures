## You'll want to know: labōriōsus, labōriōsa, labōriōsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C379-QUAESTUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**labōriōsus, labōriōsa, labōriōsum** — "toilsome; hard-working".

> Vīta agricolae labōriōsa est.
>
> *A farmer's life is full of toil.*

*labōriōsus, labōriōsa, labōriōsum*: of a task, toilsome; of a person, hard-working. English *laborious*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*labōriōsus* is built on *labor, labōris* "toil" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-ōsus* "full of". English *laborious* comes from it.
