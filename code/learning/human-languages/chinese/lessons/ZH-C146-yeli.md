## You'll want to know: yèli
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C146-NIGHT146-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yèli* — "at night".

> *yèli hěn lěng* — It's cold at night.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yèli* is built from *yè* 'night' and 里 'inside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yaeH* and *liX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'dusk' and 'a time to rest'.
