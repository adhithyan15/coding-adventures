## You'll want to know: よそくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C514-GUESS514-02]; assesses=[] -->

**よそくする** — *yosoku suru* — "to predict; to forecast".

> **もし ゆきを よそくしたら、しあいを ちゅうしします。** — *moshi yuki o yosokushitara, shiai o chūshi shimasu* — "If they forecast snow, they will call off the match."

Polite **よそくします**, *yosokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ちちが ゆきを よそくしました。**, *chichi ga yuki o yosokushimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よそくする** (*yosoku suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *yosoku* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yo*, "beforehand" (Middle Chinese *yoH*) and *soku*, "fathom" (*tsrhik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
