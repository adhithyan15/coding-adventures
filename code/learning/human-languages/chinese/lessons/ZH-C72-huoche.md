## You'll want to know: 火车
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-01]; assesses=[] -->

**火车** — *huǒchē* — "train".

> **这儿的火车很长** *zhèr de huǒchē hěn cháng* — The trains here are long.

The new character, in pieces: 火 — a dot on each side, and 人 between them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**火车** (*huǒchē*) is built from 火 'fire' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwaX* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a fire carriage, from the days of steam.
