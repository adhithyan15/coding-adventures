## You'll want to know: तीमारदारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C494-DEKHBHAL494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तीमारदारी** — *tīmārdārī* — "nursing the sick".

> बीमारी में मीरा ने अपनी माँ की बहुत तीमारदारी की।
>
> *bīmārī meṁ Mīrā ne apnī māṁ kī bahut tīmārdārī kī.*
>
> Mira nursed her mother devotedly through her illness.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तीमारदारी** (*tīmārdārī*) is usually traced to Persian *tīmārdārī*, "tending the sick", built on *tīmār*, "care of the sick", and *-dārī*, "keeping". *tīmārdār* is the person at the bedside.
