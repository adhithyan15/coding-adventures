## You'll want to know: a humildade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C470-MODESTIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a humildade** — "humility".

> Ele aceitou as críticas com humildade.
>
> *He accepted the criticism with humility.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*humildade* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Latin *humilitās* "lowness" (EtymDB), from *humilis* "low".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
