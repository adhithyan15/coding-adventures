## You'll want to know: しょくどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C180-EATOUT180-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくどう** — *shokudō* — "cafeteria; dining hall, canteen".

> **がっこうの しょくどうで ひるごはんを たべます。** — *gakkō no shokudō de hirugohan o tabemasu* — "I eat lunch in the school cafeteria."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくどう** (*shokudō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shoku*, "eat, food" (Middle Chinese *zyik*) and *dō*, "hall" (*dang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
