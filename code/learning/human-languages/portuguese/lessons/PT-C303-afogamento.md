## You'll want to know: o afogamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C303-CHEIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o afogamento** — "the drowning".

> Houve um afogamento na praia ontem.
>
> *There was a drowning at the beach yesterday.*

Built on *afogar*, ‘to drown’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*afogamento* is built on *afogar* "to drown", which comes from Latin *offōcāre* "to choke", built on *faucēs* "the throat" (Lewis & Short).
