5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la relación — the relationship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the suitor, then the Spanish for closeness.

You'll want to know: la relación.
la relación — "the relationship".
Tenemos una buena relación. We have a good relationship.
Estar en una relación — to be in a relationship.

The word, taken apart.
relación comes from Latin relātiō "a carrying back, a report" (EtymDB), from referre, built on ferre "to carry".
