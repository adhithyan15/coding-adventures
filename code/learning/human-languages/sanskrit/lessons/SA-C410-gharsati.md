## You'll want to know: घर्षति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C410-CHIDRA410-05]; assesses=[] -->

**घर्षति** — *gharṣati* — "rubs against; scrapes".

> चक्रं भित्तिं घर्षति।
>
> *cakraṃ bhittiṃ gharṣati.*
>
> The wheel scrapes the wall.

Past (it …): चक्रं भित्तिं घृष्टवत्, *cakraṃ bhittiṃ ghṛṣṭavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घर्षति** (*gharṣati*) comes from the root √ghṛṣ, "to rub, to grind"; MW gives the participle *ghṛṣṭa*, "rubbed, grazed"; it lives on as Hindi *ghisnā*, "to rub" (MW; CDIAL 4450).
