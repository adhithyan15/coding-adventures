6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
پیٹنا (pīṭnā) — to beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to shudder, then the Urdu for a shock.

You'll want to know: پیٹنا.
پیٹنا — pīṭnā — "to beat".
لوگوں نے چور کو پیٹا۔ logoṅ ne chor ko pīṭā. People beat the thief.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دروازہ پیٹا, us ne darvāzā pīṭā.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin pīṭnā, "to beat", under piṭṭayati, "pounds" (CDIAL 8165). You can پیٹنا a person, a drum or a door.
