# ز (zāy) — the letter inside أزرق

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-DAD-01, AR-SCRIPT-C48-ALIF-MADDA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ض and آ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **أزرق** (*azraq*), "blue".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-ZAY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ز** — *zāy*, the sound *z*.

It is inside **أزرق** (*azraq*), "blue", from **أحمر أزرق**. It is **ر** (*rāʾ*),
which you already write, with **one dot above**, and like **ر** it never joins to
the letter after it.

## Writing: ز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-ZAY-01] -->

- **1.** start here: begin at the upper tip and descend through the short stroke
- **2.** without lifting, sweep left through the lower curve without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then place the dot above — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Introduction to Arabic: Egyptian Arabic for first-year students, Alphabet ر ز و, independent Zay body followed by its upper dot (University of Oregon Libraries Open Textbook, 2023; accessed 2026-08-23).

- [YOU LOOK: at **أزرق** and point at ز inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ز three times, saying *z* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: آ, then ز, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **أزرق** aloud once more]
