# डेढ (ḍeḍh) — one and a half

## You'll want to know: डेढ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C212-AMOUNT212-02]; assesses=[] -->

**डेढ** — *ḍeḍh* — "one and a half".

> डेढ किलो चावळ दो।
>
> *ḍeḍh kilo chāvaḷ do.*
>
> Give me one and a half kilos of rice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डेढ** (*ḍeḍh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dvyardha*, "one and a half". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *ḍeṛh* and Sindhi *ḍeḍhu*, "one and a half" (CDIAL 6698).
