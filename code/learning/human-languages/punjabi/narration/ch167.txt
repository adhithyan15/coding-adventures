Punjabi, chapter 167: Return to Believing and Changing.
2 lessons. All 2 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 2.
An old word for believing — to believe, to agree, recalled after a long gap.

Hear one familiar meaning, pause, and retrieve its Punjabi word before the model. This is a gentle return, not a new word or a scored mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to believe, to agree.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਮੰਨਣਾ (mannṇā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਮੰਨਣਾ (mannṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to believe, to agree. Then hear the English cue again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਮੰਨਣਾ (mannṇā) before the model. Only this old word is needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
One last cue: to believe, to agree.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਮੰਨਣਾ (mannṇā). Listen and retry once if it does not come back.


Lesson 2 of 2.
An old word for changing — to change, then a gentle mix of two familiar actions.

First retrieve to change alone. Then hear two familiar actions in a small shuffled mix. Pause before each model. This is not a scored mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to change.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਬਦਲਣਾ (badalṇā).

Guided Practice.
Pause after each familiar meaning, then hear its model:
to change —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਬਦਲਣਾ (badalṇā).
to believe, to agree —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਮੰਨਣਾ (mannṇā).
Only the order of these old words has changed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to believe, to agree, then to change. Pause after each cue and recall its Punjabi word before the model: ਮੰਨਣਾ (mannṇā), ਬਦਲਣਾ (badalṇā). Revisit only the old word you missed.
