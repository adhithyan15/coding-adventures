5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Passwort — the password.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to post, then the German for to comment on.

You'll want to know: das Passwort.
das Passwort — "the password".
Ich habe mein Passwort schon wieder vergessen. I have forgotten my password yet again.
Plural: die Passwörter. Also das Kennwort.

The word, taken apart.
Passwort was borrowed from English password and fitted to German Wort "word", inherited from Proto-Germanic wurdą (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
einloggen — to log in (reflexive, sich einloggen).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to comment on, then the German for the password.

You'll want to know: einloggen.
einloggen — "to log in (reflexive, sich einloggen)".
Ohne Passwort kann ich mich nicht einloggen. I cannot log in without a password.
Reflexive and separable: ich logge mich ein, ich habe mich eingeloggt. Also sich anmelden.

The word, taken apart.
einloggen takes English log in and gives it the German prefix ein- "in". English log in is usually traced to the ship's log, the daily book named after the wooden log once used to measure a ship's speed; EtymDB has no entry for the German verb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Zugang — the access; the way in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the password, then the German for to log in.

You'll want to know: der Zugang.
der Zugang — "the access; the way in".
Im Hotel gibt es kostenlosen Zugang zum Internet. The hotel has free internet access.
Plural: die Zugänge. Zugang zu + dative.

The word, taken apart.
Zugang joins zu "to" to Gang "going, path", inherited from Old High German gang and Proto-Germanic gangaz (EtymDB): the way to something.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Datenschutz — data protection, privacy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to log in, then the German for the access.

You'll want to know: der Datenschutz.
der Datenschutz — "data protection, privacy".
Wegen des Datenschutzes darf ich Ihnen die Nummer nicht geben. Because of data protection I am not allowed to give you the number.
No plural. Die Daten — the data.

The word, taken apart.
Datenschutz joins Daten "data", from Latin datum "something given" (EtymDB), to Schutz "protection", Middle High German schuz (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
verschlüsseln — to encrypt, to encode.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the access, then the German for data protection.

You'll want to know: verschlüsseln.
verschlüsseln — "to encrypt, to encode".
Die Nachrichten werden automatisch verschlüsselt. The messages are encrypted automatically.
Inseparable: es verschlüsselt, es hat verschlüsselt. Built on der Schlüssel — the key.

The word, taken apart.
verschlüsseln is built with ver- on Schlüssel "key", inherited from Old High German sluzzil and Proto-Germanic slutilaz (EtymDB), of the family of schließen "to lock".
