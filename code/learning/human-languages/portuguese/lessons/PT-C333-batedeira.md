## You'll want to know: a batedeira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C333-BOLO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a batedeira** — "the electric mixer".

> A minha mãe bate as claras na batedeira.
>
> *My mother beats the egg whites in the mixer.*

Built on *bater*, ‘to beat’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*batedeira* is built on *bater* "to beat", which comes from Latin *battuere* "to beat" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-deira* for a tool.
