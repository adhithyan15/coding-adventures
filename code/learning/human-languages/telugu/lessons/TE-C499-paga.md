## You'll want to know: పగ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C499-ASUYA499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పగ** — *paga* — "a grudge; revenge".

> ఆ రెండు కుటుంబాల మధ్య పాత పగ ఉంది.
>
> *ā reṇḍu kuṭumbāla madhya pāta paga undi.*
>
> There is an old grudge between those two families.

పగ తీర్చుకో, to take revenge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పగ** (*paga*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3808 lists Telugu *paga*, "enmity, hatred", and *pagavāḍu*, "an enemy", in the article of Tamil *paku*, "to be split, to be at variance".
