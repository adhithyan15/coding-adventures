Marathi, chapter 418: Town and Metropolis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नगर (nagar) — a town.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a strongbox, then the Marathi for an elevator.

You'll want to know: नगर.
नगर — nagar — "a town".
हे नगर नदीच्या काठावर वसलं आहे. he nagar nadīcyā kāṭhāvar vaslaṁ āhe. This town stands on the bank of the river.
Neuter.
नगर (nagar) also ends many town names.

The word, taken apart.
नगर (nagar) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nagara, "a town, a city" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
नगरपालिका (nagarpālikā) — a municipal council.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a town, then the Marathi for a metropolis.

You'll want to know: नगरपालिका.
नगरपालिका — nagarpālikā — "a municipal council".
नगरपालिकेने नवीन रस्ता बांधला. nagarpālikene navīn rastā bāndhlā. The municipal council built a new road.
Feminine.
Before an ending -का becomes -के-: नगरपालिकेने.

The word, taken apart.
नगरपालिका (nagarpālikā) is a modern word built from Sanskrit nagara, "a town", and pālaka, "a guardian, a protector" (Monier-Williams), here in its feminine pālikā.

Lesson 4 of 5.
खेडे (kheḍe) — a small village; a hamlet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a metropolis, then the Marathi for a municipal council.

You'll want to know: खेडे.
खेडे — kheḍe — "a small village; a hamlet".
माझे आजोबा एका लहान खेड्यात राहतात. mājhe ājobā ekā lahān kheḍyāt rāhtāt. My grandfather lives in a small village.
Neuter. Plural: खेडी, kheḍī.
A neuter noun: before an ending खेड्या-, in the plural खेडी.

The word, taken apart.
खेडे (kheḍe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi kheḍẽ, "hamlet", under Sanskrit khēṭa, "village" (CDIAL 3916).

Lesson 5 of 5.
तालुका (tālukā) — a taluka.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a municipal council, then the Marathi for a small village.

You'll want to know: तालुका.
तालुका — tālukā — "a taluka".
आमचा तालुका डोंगराळ आहे. āmcā tālukā ḍoṅgrāḷ āhe. Our taluka is hilly.
Masculine.
A part of a district, with its own town office; the plural is the same.

The word, taken apart.
तालुका (tālukā) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic taʿalluqa, "a dependency, an estate".
