## You'll want to know: la lágrima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C717-SUFRIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la lágrima** — "the tear (from the eye)".

> Tenía lágrimas en los ojos.
>
> *She had tears in her eyes.*

*Llorar a lágrima viva* — to cry your eyes out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lágrima* comes from Old Spanish *lagrima* (EtymDB), from Latin *lacrima* "tear".
