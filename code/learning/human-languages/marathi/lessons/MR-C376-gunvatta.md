## You'll want to know: गुणवत्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C376-MULYAMAPAN376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गुणवत्ता** — *guṇvattā* — "quality".

> या दुकानातल्या भाज्यांची गुणवत्ता चांगली आहे.
>
> *yā dukānātalyā bhājyāñcī guṇvattā cāṅglī āhe.*
>
> The quality of the vegetables in this shop is good.

Feminine.

गुण, merit, + -वत्ता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुणवत्ता** (*guṇvattā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *guṇavattā*, "excellence, possessing merit" (Monier-Williams).
