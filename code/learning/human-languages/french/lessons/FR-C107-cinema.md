## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cinéma** — short for *cinématographe*, a coinage of the 1890s from Greek *kinema*, "movement", and *graphein*, "to write". (EtymDB 2.1)
