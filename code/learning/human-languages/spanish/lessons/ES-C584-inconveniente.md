## You'll want to know: el inconveniente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C584-VENTAJA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el inconveniente** — "the drawback, the problem; inconvenient".

> No hay ningún inconveniente.
>
> *There is no problem at all.*

*No tengo inconveniente* — I do not mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inconveniente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inconveniens* 'unsuitable', built on *convenire* 'to come together, to suit' with *in-* 'not' (EtymDB).
