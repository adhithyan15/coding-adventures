## You'll want to know: دانشکده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C400-DANESHKADE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دانشکده** (*dâneshkade*) — "a faculty, a college".

> دانشکده پزشکی نزدیک بیمارستان است.
>
> *dâneshkade-ye pezeshki nazdik-e bimârestân ast.*
>
> The medical faculty is near the hospital.

*Dânesh* "knowledge" + *kade* "house". A university has several *dâneshkade*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دانشکده** is a native Persian compound built from *dânesh* "knowledge", from *dânestan* "to know", + *kade* "house, dwelling". A faculty is a house of knowledge.
