Marathi, chapter 314: Readers and Viewers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वाचक (vācak) — a reader.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for prominent, then the Marathi for to proclaim.

You'll want to know: वाचक.
वाचक — vācak — "a reader".
या मासिकाचे हजारो वाचक आहेत. yā māsikāce hajāro vācak āhet. This magazine has thousands of readers.
Masculine.
Plural वाचक (vācak).

The word, taken apart.
वाचक (vācak) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vācaka, 'speaking, expressing' (Monier-Williams); Marathi uses it for the one who reads, from vācṇe, 'to read'.

श्रोता — śrotā — "a listener".
कार्यक्रमानंतर एका श्रोत्याने प्रश्न विचारला. kāryakramānantar ekā śrotyāne praśna vicārlā. After the programme a listener asked a question.
Masculine. Plural: श्रोते, śrote.
The oblique is श्रोत्या-, the plural श्रोते.

The word, taken apart.
श्रोता (śrotā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit śrotṛ, 'one who hears, a hearer' (Monier-Williams), in its nominative form śrotā.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मुखपृष्ठ (mukhpṛṣṭha) — a front page; a cover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a listener, then the Marathi for a title.

You'll want to know: मुखपृष्ठ.
मुखपृष्ठ — mukhpṛṣṭha — "a front page; a cover".
पुस्तकाच्या मुखपृष्ठावर एक जुना किल्ला आहे. pustakācyā mukhpṛṣṭhāvar ek junā killā āhe. There is an old fort on the book's cover.
Neuter.
Built from मुख, face, and पृष्ठ, a page.

The word, taken apart.
मुखपृष्ठ (mukhpṛṣṭha) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit mukha, 'the face', and pṛṣṭha, 'the back, a surface, a page' (Monier-Williams).
