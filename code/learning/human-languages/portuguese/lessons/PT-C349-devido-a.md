## You'll want to know: devido a
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C349-VISTOQUE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**devido a** — "due to, because of".

> O voo chegou atrasado devido ao nevoeiro.
>
> *The flight arrived late due to the fog.*

*Devido a* + noun: *devido à chuva*. With a verb: *devido a ter chovido*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*devido a* is built on *devido* "owed, due", the participle of *dever*, inherited from Latin *dēbēre* "to owe" (Lewis & Short): what is owed to a cause.
