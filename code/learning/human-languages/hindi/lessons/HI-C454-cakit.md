## You'll want to know: चकित
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C454-ACHAMBHA454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चकित** — *cakit* — "taken aback; astonished".

> मीरा का नया घर देखकर सब चकित थे।
>
> *Mīrā kā nayā ghar dekhkar sab cakit the.*
>
> Everyone was astonished when they saw Mira's new house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चकित** (*cakit*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *cakita*, "trembling, timid, startled" (Monier-Williams). Hindi has turned the start of fright into the start of surprise.
