## You'll want to know: くつろぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C398-ONSEN398-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くつろぐ** — *kutsurogu* — "to relax; to make oneself at home".

> **りょかんの へやで くつろぎました。** — *ryokan no heya de kutsurogimashita* — "We relaxed in our room at the inn."

Polite **くつろぎます**, *kutsurogimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かぞくが へやで くつろぎました。**, *kazoku ga heya de kutsurogimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くつろぐ** (*kutsurogu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
