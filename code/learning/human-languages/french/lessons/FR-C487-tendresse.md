## You'll want to know: la tendresse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C487-AMOUREUX-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la tendresse** — "tenderness, affection".

> Elle regarde son fils avec tendresse.
>
> *She looks at her son with tenderness.*

Built on *tendre* "tender, soft".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la tendresse* is built on *tendre* "tender" with *-esse* (EtymDB); *tendre* is inherited from Latin *tener* "soft, delicate" (Lewis & Short).
