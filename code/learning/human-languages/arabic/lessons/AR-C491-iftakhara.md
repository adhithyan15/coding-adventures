# افتخر (iftakhara) — to take pride (in)

## You'll want to know: افتخر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C491-FAKHR491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**افتخر** — *iftakhara* — "to take pride (in)".

> يفتخر جدي بحديقته الجميلة.
>
> *yaftakhiru jaddī bi-ḥadīqatihi al-jamīla.*
>
> My grandfather takes pride in his beautiful garden.

Present: يفتخر, *yaftakhir*.

It takes بـ, like فخور.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افتخر** (*iftakhara*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-kh-r*, "to boast, to glory in" (Lane). It is Form VIII of the root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
