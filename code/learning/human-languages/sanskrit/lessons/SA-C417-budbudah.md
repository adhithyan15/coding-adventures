## You'll want to know: बुद्बुदः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C417-PULINA417-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बुद्बुदः** — *budbudaḥ* — "a bubble".

> जले बुद्बुदाः क्षणं तिष्ठन्ति।
>
> *jale budbudāḥ kṣaṇaṃ tiṣṭhanti.*
>
> Bubbles last a moment on the water.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुद्बुदः** (*budbudaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a bubble; MW sets it beside *buḍabuḍa*, the sound of bubbling, and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
