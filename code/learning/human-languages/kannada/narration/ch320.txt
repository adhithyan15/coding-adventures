Kannada, chapter 320: The Reporter on the Spot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವರದಿಗಾರ (varadigāra) — a reporter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a thief, then the Kannada for to run away.

You'll want to know: ವರದಿಗಾರ.
ವರದಿಗಾರ — varadigāra — "a reporter".
ಆ ವರದಿಗಾರ ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದಿದ್ದರೆ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಗೊತ್ತಾಗುತ್ತಿತ್ತು. ā varadigāra alli iddiddare ellarigū suddi gottāguttittu. If that reporter had been there, everyone would have known the news.
Plural: ವರದಿಗಾರರು, varadigāraru.
ವರದಿ is a report; -ಗಾರ makes "one who does it", as in ಕೆಲಸಗಾರ, a worker.

The word, taken apart.
ವರದಿಗಾರ (varadigāra) is a Kannada word built from ವರದಿ, "a report", and the ending -gāra, "one who does", as in ಕೆಲಸಗಾರ. The origin of ವರದಿ itself is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸಂಭವಿಸು (sambhavisu) — to occur; to happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to run away, then the Kannada for a reporter.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಭವಿಸು.
ಸಂಭವಿಸು — sambhavisu — "to occur; to happen".
ನಿನ್ನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ನಮ್ಮ ಬೀದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅಪಘಾತ ಸಂಭವಿಸಿತು. ninne rātri namma bīdiyalli apaghāta sambhavisitu. Last night an accident happened in our street.
Past (it …): ಸಂಭವಿಸಿತು, sambhavisitu.
An event is the subject, so the past is ಸಂಭವಿಸಿತು, it happened. It suits news reports.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಭವಿಸು (sambhavisu) is built on Sanskrit sambhava, "being or coming together, arising" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada ending -isu that makes verbs of Sanskrit nouns.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪ್ರಸಾರ (prasāra) — a broadcast; spreading.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a reporter, then the Kannada for to occur.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಸಾರ.
ಪ್ರಸಾರ — prasāra — "a broadcast; spreading".
ಆ ಪಂದ್ಯದ ಪ್ರಸಾರ ಸಂಜೆ ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಶುರುವಾಯಿತು. ā pandyada prasāra sañje āru gaṇṭege śuruvāyitu. The broadcast of that match began at six in the evening.
Radio and television both have a ಪ್ರಸಾರ (prasāra). ಪ್ರಸಾರವಾಗು means to be broadcast.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಸಾರ (prasāra) is borrowed from Sanskrit prasāra, "spreading out, extension" (CDIAL 8835). A broadcast spreads a programme far and wide.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸು (bittarisu) — to broadcast; to air.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to occur, then the Kannada for a broadcast.

You'll want to know: ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸು.
ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸು — bittarisu — "to broadcast; to air".
ಟೀವಿ ಆ ಸಂದರ್ಶನವನ್ನು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸಿತು. ṭīvi ā sandarśanavannu rātri bittarisitu. The television aired that interview at night.
Past (I …): ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸಿದೆ, bittariside.
What is aired takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಪ್ರಸಾರ (prasāra) ಮಾಡು says the same thing.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಿತ್ತರಿಸು (bittarisu) is built on ಬಿತ್ತರ, the Kannada form of Sanskrit vistara, "spreading out, extent" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu. Sanskrit v often turns into Kannada b.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ (śīrṣike) — a headline; a title.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a broadcast, then the Kannada for to broadcast.

You'll want to know: ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ.
ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ — śīrṣike — "a headline; a title".
ಇವತ್ತಿನ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಯ ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ ಆ ಯುದ್ಧದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಇತ್ತು. ivattina patrikeya śīrṣike ā yuddhada bagge ittu. Today's newspaper headline was about that war.
Plural: ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆಗಳು, śīrṣikegaḷu.
A book, a film and a song have a ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ (śīrṣike) too: its title.

The word, taken apart.
ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ (śīrṣike) is borrowed from Sanskrit śīrṣaka, built on śīrṣa, "head, top" (CDIAL 12497). A headline sits at the head of a story.
