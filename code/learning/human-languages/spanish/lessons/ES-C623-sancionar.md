## You'll want to know: sancionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C623-SANCION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sancionar** — "to penalise, to sanction".

> La empresa fue sancionada.
>
> *The company was penalised.*

Common in the news, often in the passive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sancionar* is built on *sanción*, a learned borrowing from Latin *sanctio* 'a decree', from *sancire* (EtymDB).
