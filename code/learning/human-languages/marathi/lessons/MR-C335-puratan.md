## You'll want to know: पुरातन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C335-UTKHANAN335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरातन** — *purātan* — "age-old; ancient".

> हे गावातलं सर्वात पुरातन मंदिर आहे.
>
> *he gāvātlaṁ sarvāt purātan mandir āhe.*
>
> This is the most ancient temple in the village.

पुरातन keeps one form: पुरातन मंदिर, पुरातन वस्तू.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरातन** (*purātan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *purātana*, 'belonging to the past, ancient' (Monier-Williams).
