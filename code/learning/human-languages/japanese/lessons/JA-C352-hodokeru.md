## You'll want to know: ほどける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C352-KOBORERU352-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ほどける** — *hodokeru* — "to come undone; to come untied".

> **くつの ひもが ほどけたら、むすんで ください。** — *kutsu no himo ga hodoketara, musunde kudasai* — "If your shoelace comes undone, please tie it."

Polite **ほどけます**, *hodokemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ひもが ほどけました。**, *himo ga hodokemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほどける** (*hodokeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
