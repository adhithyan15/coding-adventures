Arabic, chapter 142: Bicycle, Ship, Traveller, Factory, Square.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دراجة (darrāja) — a bicycle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for skin, then the Arabic for a bone.

You'll want to know: دراجة.
دراجة — darrāja — "a bicycle".

The word, taken apart.
دراجة (darrāja) is built on the native Arabic root d-r-j, "to go step by step" (Lane). Lane's darrāja is a wheeled frame on which a child or an old man steps along; the bicycle took over the word.

ساحة — sāḥa — "a square".

The word, taken apart.
ساحة (sāḥa), "an open court", is an old native noun, filed under the root s-w-ḥ (Lane). Lane's sāḥa is the open space of a house.
