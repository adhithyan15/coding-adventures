## You'll want to know: साँवला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C482-KAD482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**साँवला** — *sā̃vlā* — "dusky; dark-skinned".

> मेरी बहन का रंग साँवला है।
>
> *merī bahan kā raṅg sā̃vlā hai.*
>
> My sister has a dusky complexion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साँवला** (*sā̃vlā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *sā̃wlā*, "brown, dark-complexioned", under Sanskrit *śyāmalá*, "dark" (CDIAL 12665). Like *gorā*, it describes a complexion and nothing more.
