## You'll want to know: o rapto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C291-RAPTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o rapto** — "the kidnapping, the abduction".

> O rapto do empresário foi notícia em toda a cidade.
>
> *The businessman’s kidnapping was news all over the city.*

*O rapto de uma criança* — a child abduction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rapto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *raptus* "a carrying off", from *rapere* "to seize, to snatch" (Lewis & Short).
