Kannada, chapter 412: When Danger Comes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಪಾಯ (apāya) — a danger; a risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to fulfil, then the Kannada for an obligation.

You'll want to know: ಅಪಾಯ.
ಅಪಾಯ — apāya — "a danger; a risk".
ಮಳೆಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ನದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಈಜುವುದು ಅಪಾಯ. maḷegāladalli nadiyalli ījuvudu apāya. Swimming in the river in the rainy season is dangerous.
ಅಪಾಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಇರು, to be in danger.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಪಾಯ (apāya) is borrowed from Sanskrit apāya, "going away; loss, harm" (CDIAL 481).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮುನ್ನೆಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ (munneccarike) — a precaution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an obligation, then the Kannada for a danger.

You'll want to know: ಮುನ್ನೆಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ.
ಮುನ್ನೆಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ — munneccarike — "a precaution".
ಮುನ್ನೆಚ್ಚರಿಕೆಯಾಗಿ ನಾನು ಕೊಡೆ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಹೋದೆ. munneccarikeyāgi nānu koḍe tegedukoṇḍu hōde. I took an umbrella as a precaution.
ಮುನ್ (before) + ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ (warning).

The word, taken apart.
ಮುನ್ನೆಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ (munneccarike) is native Dravidian, built from mun, "before" (DEDR 5020), and eccaṟike, "a warning, watchfulness" (DEDR 851).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a danger, then the Kannada for a precaution.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಭವನೀಯ.
ಸಂಭವನೀಯ — sambhavanīya — "probable; likely".
ಮಳೆ ಬಂದರೆ ಪ್ರವಾಹ ಬರುವುದು ಸಂಭವನೀಯ. maḷe bandare pravāha baruvudu sambhavanīya. If it rains, a flood is likely.
ಸಂಭವ, a possibility or an event.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಭವನೀಯ (sambhavanīya) is built on Sanskrit saṃbhava, "coming together, coming into being" (Monier-Williams), with -anīya, "that may be".

ಪ್ರವಾಹದ ನಂತರ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಜನರು ಸಂಕಷ್ಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದರು. pravāhada nantara haḷḷiya janaru saṅkaṣṭadalli iddaru. After the flood the villagers were in distress.

ಸಂಕಷ್ಟ (saṅkaṣṭa) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃkaṣṭa, "distress, trouble" (Monier-Williams), built on kaṣṭa, "hardship" (CDIAL 2978).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ನಿವಾರಿಸು (nivārisu) — to ward off; to relieve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for probable, then the Kannada for hardship.

You'll want to know: ನಿವಾರಿಸು.
ನಿವಾರಿಸು — nivārisu — "to ward off; to relieve".
ವೈದ್ಯರು ನನ್ನ ನೋವನ್ನು ನಿವಾರಿಸಿದರು. vaidyaru nanna nōvannu nivārisidaru. The doctor relieved my pain.
Past (I …): ನಿವಾರಿಸಿದೆ, nivāriside.
What is removed takes -ಅನ್ನು. ನಿವಾರಣೆ, removal.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿವಾರಿಸು (nivārisu) is built on Sanskrit nivāraṇa, "keeping off, warding off" (CDIAL 7418), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
