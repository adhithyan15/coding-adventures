## You'll want to know: obtemperō, obtemperāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C416-IUSTITIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**obtemperō, obtemperāre** — "to obey, to comply (with)".

> Lēgibus obtemperāre dēbēmus.
>
> *We must obey the laws.*

*obtemperō, obtemperāre, obtemperāvī, obtemperātum* + dative, like *pāreō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obtemperō* is built from *ob* and *temperō* "to keep within measure, to restrain oneself" (EtymDB): to hold oneself in for someone else.
