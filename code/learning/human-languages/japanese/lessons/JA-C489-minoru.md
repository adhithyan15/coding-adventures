## You'll want to know: みのる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C489-FARM489-04]; assesses=[] -->

**みのる** — *minoru* — "to ripen; to bear fruit".

> **あきに いねが みのります。** — *aki ni ine ga minorimasu* — "The rice ripens in autumn."

Polite **みのります**, *minorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ことしは りんごが みのりました。**, *kotoshi wa ringo ga minorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みのる** (*minoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
