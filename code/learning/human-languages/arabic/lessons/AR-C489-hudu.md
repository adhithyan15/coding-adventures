# هدوء (hudūʾ) — calm, quiet

## You'll want to know: هدوء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C489-KHAWF489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**هدوء** — *hudūʾ* — "calm, quiet".

> أحب هدوء القرية في المساء.
>
> *uḥibbu hudūʾ al-qarya fī al-masāʾ.*
>
> I love the quiet of the village in the evening.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هدوء** (*hudūʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-d-ʾ*, "to become still, to be at rest" (Lane). Hudūʾ is the stillness itself: of a place, the sea or a person.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
