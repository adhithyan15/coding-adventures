## You'll want to know: لثة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C506-WAJH506-03]; assesses=[] -->

**لثة** — *litha* — "the gums".

> قال طبيب الأسنان إن لثتي سليمة.
>
> *qāla ṭabīb al-asnān inna lithatī salīma.*
>
> The dentist said that my gums are healthy.

It is one word for all the gums.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لثة** (*litha*) is a native Arabic word for the gums. Lane notes that some file it under *l-w-th*, from the flesh wound round the roots of the teeth; its root is not settled.
