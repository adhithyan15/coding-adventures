## You'll want to know: પીઠી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C497-KANKOTRI497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પીઠી** — *pīṭhī* — "the turmeric rite; turmeric paste put on before a wedding".

> લગ્ન પહેલાં બહેનપણીઓએ મીરાને પીઠી લગાડી.
>
> *lagna pahelā̃ bahenpaṇīoe Mīrāne pīṭhī lagāḍī.*
>
> Before the wedding Mira's friends put turmeric paste on her.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પીઠી** (*pīṭhī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *piṣṭa*, "crushed, ground"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, the yellow powder put on bride and groom (CDIAL 8218).
