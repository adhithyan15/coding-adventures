## You'll want to know: ভরসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C446-SANTVONA446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ভরসা** — *bhôrsā* — "reliance; trust".

> বিপদে বন্ধুরা আমার ভরসা।
>
> *bipôde bôndhurā āmār bhôrsā.*
>
> In trouble, my friends are the ones I rely on.

ভরসা করা, to rely on: আমি বন্ধুর ওপর ভরসা করি.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভরসা** (*bhôrsā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, with the sense of trust and reliance, under a reconstructed compound (CDIAL 9398).
