## You'll want to know: pêcher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C497-PECHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pêcher** — "to fish; to catch (fish)".

> Le dimanche, nous allons pêcher au bord du lac.
>
> *On Sundays we go fishing by the lake.*

Do not mix it up with *pécher* "to sin" or *le pêcher*, the peach tree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pêcher* is inherited, through Old French *peschier*, from Vulgar Latin *piscāre* (EtymDB), a form of Latin *piscārī* "to fish", from *piscis* "fish" (Lewis & Short).
