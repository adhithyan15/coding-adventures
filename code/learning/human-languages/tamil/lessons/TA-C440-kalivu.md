## You'll want to know: கழிவு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C440-MASU440-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கழிவு** — *kaḻivu* — "waste; refuse".

> தொழிற்சாலைக் கழிவு ஆற்றில் கலக்கிறது.
>
> *toḻiṟcālaik kaḻivu āṟṟil kalakkiṟatu.*
>
> Factory waste flows into the river.

From கழி, to pass away or discharge, + -வு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கழிவு** (*kaḻivu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1356), the noun of கழி, "to pass, to be discharged".
