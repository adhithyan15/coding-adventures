## You'll want to know: नाडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C479-NADI479-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नाडी** — *nāḍī* — "the pulse".

> दवाखान्यात त्यांनी माझी नाडी तपासली.
>
> *davākhānyāt tyānnī mājhī nāḍī tapāslī.*
>
> At the clinic they took my pulse.

Feminine.

नाडी तपासणे, to take the pulse; also a cord.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाडी** (*nāḍī*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *nāḍī*, "tube, vein"; CDIAL lists Marathi *nāḍī*, "pulse" (CDIAL 7047).
