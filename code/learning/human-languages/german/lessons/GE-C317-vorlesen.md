## You'll want to know: vorlesen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C317-ERZAEHLUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vorlesen** — "to read aloud".

> Der Vater las den Kindern eine Geschichte vor.
>
> *The father read the children a story.*

Separable, strong: *liest vor*, *las vor*, *hat vorgelesen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vorlesen* joins *vor* "before, in front of" and *lesen* "to read", from Old High German *lesan*, Proto-Germanic *lesaną* "to gather" (EtymDB): first gathering, then reading.
