## You'll want to know: besohlen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C445-SCHUSTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**besohlen** — "to resole".

> Der Schuster besohlt die Stiefel bis Freitag.
>
> *The cobbler will resole the boots by Friday.*

Inseparable: *er besohlt*, *er hat besohlt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*besohlen* is built from *be-* and *Sohle* 'sole', which goes back to Old High German *sola* (EtymDB) and is usually traced to Latin *solea* 'sandal' (Lewis & Short).
