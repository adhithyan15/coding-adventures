## You'll want to know: a inveja
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C468-INVEJA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a inveja** — "envy".

> Tenho inveja das tuas férias na praia.
>
> *I envy you your holiday at the beach.*

*Ter inveja de* — to envy. *O ciúme* is jealousy in love.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inveja* is inherited from Latin *invidia* "envy, ill will" (Lewis & Short), built on *invidēre* "to look askance at, to begrudge".
