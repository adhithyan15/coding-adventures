Bengali, chapter 172: Sports, Female student, Board, Chalk, Scholarship.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
খেলাধুলা (khelādhulā) — sports.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for paddy, then the Bengali for high tide.

You'll want to know: খেলাধুলা.
খেলাধুলা — khelādhulā — "sports".

The word, taken apart.
খেলাধুলা (khelādhulā) is khelā, "play", which CDIAL lists under khēḍ, "play", a form it reconstructs and thinks may be non-Aryan (CDIAL 3918), + dhulā, "dust", under dhūḍi, "dust", beside Sanskrit dhūli (CDIAL 6835): "play and dust".
