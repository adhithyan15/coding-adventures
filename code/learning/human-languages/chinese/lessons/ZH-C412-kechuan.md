## You'll want to know: 客船
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C412-HARBOUR412-05]; assesses=[] -->

**客船** — *kèchuán* — "passenger ship".

> **我们坐客船去那儿** *wǒmen zuò kèchuán qù nàr* — We're going there on a passenger ship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**客船** (*kèchuán*) is built from 客 'a guest' and 船 'a boat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaek* and *zywen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
