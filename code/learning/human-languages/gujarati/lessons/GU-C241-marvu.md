## You'll want to know: મરવું

**મરવું** — *marvũ* — "to die".

> **ગરમીમાં છોડ મર્યો.** — *garmīmā̃ choḍ maryo* — "The plant died in the heat."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મરવું** (*marvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *marate*, "will die" (CDIAL 9871).
