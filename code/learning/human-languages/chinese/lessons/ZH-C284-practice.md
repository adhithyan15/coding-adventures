## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-TICKET-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-TICKET-01, ZH-LEX-C284-DANCHENG-01, ZH-LEX-C284-ZHANTAI-01, ZH-LEX-C284-ZUOWEI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-JIA-MEASURE-01, ZH-LEX-C88-ZHANG-01, ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-05, ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-01] -->

> **A:** *wǒ yào liǎng zhāng qù Běijīng de piào* — Two tickets to Beijing, please.
>
> **B:** *dānchéng háishi wǎngfǎn* — One way or return?
>
> **A:** *dānchéng, wǒmen zuò bā diǎn de huǒchē* — One way. We're taking the eight o'clock train.
>
> **B:** *bā diǎn de huǒchē zài èr hào zhàntái* — The eight o'clock is at platform two.
>
> **A:** *yǒu zuòwèi ma* — Are there seats?
>
> **B:** 有
>
> *yǒu* — There are.
>
> **A:** 车站里有一家书店吗
>
> *chēzhàn lǐ yǒu yì jiā shūdiàn ma* — Is there a bookshop in the station?
>
> **B:** *yǒu, zài zhàntái qiánbian* — There is, in front of the platform.

The answers are short: *dānchéng*, 有. The station's bookshop is counted with 家, and the platform has a number before 号.

## What you've built — getting on a train
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-TICKET-01, ZH-SCRIPT-ZUO-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-TICKET-01, ZH-LEX-C284-DANCHENG-01, ZH-LEX-C284-TUIPIAO-01, ZH-LEX-C284-ZHANTAI-01, ZH-LEX-C284-ZUOWEI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-V-ZAI-PLACE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-JIA-MEASURE-01] -->

Everything you need between the window and your seat:

- **我们坐火车去** *wǒmen zuò huǒchē qù* — how you travel.
- *mǎi liǎng zhāng piào*, *dānchéng*, *tuìpiào* — buying, and giving back.
- *èr hào zhàntái*, *zuòwèi* — where to go, and where to sit.
- **你坐在这儿** *nǐ zuò zài zhèr* — where you land.
- **一家书店** *yì jiā shūdiàn* — a shop, counted.

**Listen.** *Nǐmen zuò jǐ diǎn de huǒchē?*

**Speak.** Buy a ticket for a trip you would like to take: where, how many, one way or return.

[YOU WRITE: 坐火车 — 人 twice, then the foot of 在]
