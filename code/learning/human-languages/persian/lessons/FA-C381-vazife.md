## You'll want to know: وظیفه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C381-SARPARAST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**وظیفه** (*vazife*) — "a duty, a task".

> کمک به مردم وظیفه ماست.
>
> *komak be mardom vazife-ye mâst.*
>
> Helping people is our duty.

Plural *vazâyef*, an Arabic broken plural. *Vazife dâshtan* — to be obliged to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وظیفه** was borrowed from Arabic *waẓīfa* "task, duty, office" (EtymDB).
