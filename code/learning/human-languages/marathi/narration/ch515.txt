Lesson 1 of 5.
अभियंता (abhiyantā) — an engineer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get done, then the Marathi for to struggle.

You'll want to know: अभियंता.
अभियंता — abhiyantā — "an engineer".
माझी मावशी अभियंता आहे आणि पूल बांधते. mājhī māvśī abhiyantā āhe āṇi pūl bāndhte. My aunt is an engineer and builds bridges.
Masculine.
One word for a man or a woman.

The word, taken apart.
अभियंता (abhiyantā) is a modern Marathi coinage built from Sanskrit parts: abhi-, "towards", and yantṛ, "one who guides or restrains", related to yantra, "a machine" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
आटोपणे (āṭopṇe) — to wrap up; to finish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a translator, then the Marathi for an accountant.

You'll want to know: आटोपणे.
आटोपणे — āṭopṇe — "to wrap up; to finish".
लेखापालाने संध्याकाळी काम आटोपलं आणि तो घरी गेला. lekhāpālāne sandhyākāḷī kām āṭoplaṁ āṇi to gharī gelā. The accountant wrapped up his work in the evening and went home.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने काम आटोपलं, tyāne kām āṭoplaṁ.
Close to उरकणे: both say a job was finished.

The word, taken apart.
आटोपणे (āṭopṇe) is a Marathi verb for bringing a task to a close; its origin is not settled.
