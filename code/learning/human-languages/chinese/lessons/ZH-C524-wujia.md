## You'll want to know: wùjià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C524-PRICE524-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wùjià* — "prices; the cost of living".

> *zhèr de wùjià bǐ Běijīng gāo* — The cost of living here is higher than in Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wùjià* is built from *wù* 'a thing' and 价 'a price', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjut* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'all things': the price of things.
