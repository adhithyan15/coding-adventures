## You'll want to know: ふるさと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C290-UTSURU290-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふるさと** — *furusato* — "one's home town; one's birthplace".

> **つまが ふるさとに かえる ことを 言い出しました。** — *tsuma ga furusato ni kaeru koto o iidashimashita* — "My wife suggested going back to her home town."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふるさと** (*furusato*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *furu*, "old" (from *furui*), and *sato*, "village" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
