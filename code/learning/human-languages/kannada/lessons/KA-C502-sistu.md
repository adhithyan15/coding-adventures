# ಶಿಸ್ತು (śistu) — discipline

## You'll want to know: ಶಿಸ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C502-SABHYA502-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಿಸ್ತು** — *śistu* — "discipline".

> ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಶಿಸ್ತು ತುಂಬಾ ಮುಖ್ಯ.
>
> *śāleyalli śistu tumbā mukhya.*
>
> Discipline is very important at school.

ಶಿಸ್ತಾಗಿ, neatly, in good order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಿಸ್ತು** (*śistu*) is an everyday Kannada word whose origin is not settled; it is often compared with Sanskrit *śiṣṭa*, "taught, disciplined" (CDIAL 12479).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
