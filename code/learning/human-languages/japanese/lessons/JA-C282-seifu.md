## You'll want to know: せいふ

**せいふ** — *seifu* — "the government".

> **せいふは なにも いいませんでした。** — *seifu wa nanimo iimasen deshita* — "The government said nothing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいふ** (*seifu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "government" (Middle Chinese *tsyengH*) and *fu*, "govt office" (*pjuX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
