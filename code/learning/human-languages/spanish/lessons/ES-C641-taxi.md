## You'll want to know: el taxi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C641-TAXI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el taxi** — "the taxi".

> Cogemos un taxi a la estación.
>
> *We are taking a taxi to the station.*

*Coger un taxi* in Spain; in much of Latin America say *tomar un taxi*, because *coger* is vulgar there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*taxi* is short for *taxímetro*, which came from French *taximètre*, a meter that measures the charge; it is built on Medieval Latin *taxa* "charge, rate" and Greek *métron* "measure" (EtymDB).
