### Added — Malayalam chapter 96, easy and difficult, on a frame that was already running

- `ML-A1-LEX-54` closes. Malayalam A1 coverage 202/243 -> **203/243 (84%)**, 40
  points unmapped. **The percentage rolls over, 83% -> 84%** -- computed, not
  assumed, after a stale 82% shipped past every check but the full suite in
  chapter 94.
- **Two words and no new grammar.** **എളുപ്പം + ആണ് → എളുപ്പമാണ്** is the same
  welding as **സുഖമാണ്** (chapter 3), **ഇഷ്ടമാണ്** (34), **കുടുംബമാണ്** (35) and
  **മോശമാണ്** (89).
- **Two things were wrong in the first draft, and the reviewer caught both. No
  gate here could have.**
- **The chapter claimed the join had never been pointed out. It had.**
  `ML-C89-mosham` states it outright, drills it in Guided Practice and **tests it
  in its own Wrap-up Recall** — seven chapters earlier, in the lesson that gives
  the learner മോശം, which this chapter cited as *silent* precedent. Reframed: it
  was named once, at മോശം, and here it stops being a fact about a given word and
  becomes something the learner does to words of their own.
- **"The ം turns into മ" described a spelling change as a sound change.** The
  sign already *is* the sound **m**; when **ആണ്** puts a vowel behind it, that
  same **m** needs the full letter to carry the vowel. **Nothing is heard to
  change** — which is the chapter's own best evidence that the learner has been
  doing this all along, and the false account quietly undercut it.
- That correction exposed a **pre-existing error in chapter 89**: its rule said
  the anusvara becomes **മ** *"before a word starting with a vowel"*, which
  **വെള്ളം + ഉം → വെള്ളവും** contradicts (chapter 70), and the plural takes the
  same sign to **ങ്ങൾ** instead (chapter 93). **Three fates for one sign.**
  `ML-C89-mosham` is narrowed to the **ആണ്** join, which is what it actually
  demonstrates.
- `ML-C96-prayaasam` needs no second explanation: **പ്രയാസം** ends in **ം** as
  well, so it behaves the way the first one did. One word apart and the sentence
  is its own opposite, with everything around it holding still.
- **The claims were settled before the prose**, which is the correction taken
  from chapter 95. Romanization was **derived from the track** -- final anusvara
  is `ṁ` across the recent corpus, ള is `ḷ`, doubled പ is `pp` -- rather than
  invented, which is how chapter 95's `th`/`tth` drift happened.
- **A draft line was cut:** it said the learner had been doing this *"since your
  third week"*. Chapters are not weeks and nothing in this repo knows how long a
  learner takes. It names the event instead. Same class as the `chapter 42`
  cross-reference and the lesson-count that rotted from 393 to 405.
- **ബുദ്ധിമുട്ട് was considered for a third lesson and dropped** rather than
  assert register nuances that could not be defended under review. The point asks
  for ease and difficulty; two words close it.
- `validate` caught one real omission: **ആണ്** was practised in two lessons
  without being assessed in any body block. Declared where it is actually tested.
