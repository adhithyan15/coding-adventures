## You'll want to know: なまけもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C441-PROUD441-04]; assesses=[] -->

**なまけもの** — *namakemono* — "a lazy person".

> **おとうとは なまけものです。** — *otōto wa namakemono desu* — "My younger brother is a lazybones."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なまけもの** (*namakemono*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *namake*, "idling (namakeru)" and *mono*, "person, thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
