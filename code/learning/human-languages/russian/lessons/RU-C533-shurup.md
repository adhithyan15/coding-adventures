## You'll want to know: шуруп
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C533-REPAIRS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**шуруп** (*shurúp*) — "a screw".

> Для полки нужны четыре шурупа.
>
> *Dlya pólki nuzhný chetýre shurúpa.*
>
> The shelf needs four screws.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*шуруп* was borrowed from German *Schraube* 'a screw' (EtymDB).
