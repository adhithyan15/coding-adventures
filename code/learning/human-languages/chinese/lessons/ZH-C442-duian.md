## You'll want to know: duì'àn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C442-RIVER442-01]; assesses=[] -->

*duì'àn* — "the far bank; the opposite bank".

> *wǒmen zuò chuán qù duì'àn* — We're taking a boat across to the other side.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duì'àn* is built from 对 'facing' and *àn* 'a bank', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *twojH* and *nganH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the high edge of the water.
