Gujarati, chapter 527: In the Wrestling Ring.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અખાડો (akhāḍo) — a wrestling ground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a raga, then the Gujarati for to strike up.

You'll want to know: અખાડો.
અખાડો — akhāḍo — "a wrestling ground".
અમે રોજ સવારે ગામના અખાડામાં કસરત કરીએ છીએ. ame roj savāre gāmnā akhāḍāmā̃ kasrat karīe chīe. Every morning we exercise at the village wrestling ground.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અખાડો (akhāḍo) is inherited from Sanskrit akṣavāṭa, "a wrestling ground", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 39).

Lesson 3 of 5.
હરીફ (harīph) — a rival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a wrestling ground, then the Gujarati for a wrestler.

You'll want to know: હરીફ.
હરીફ — harīph — "a rival".
આજે પહેલવાનનો હરીફ તેના કરતાં મોટો છે. āje pahelvānno harīph tenā kartā̃ moṭo che. Today the wrestler's rival is bigger than he is.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
હરીફ (harīph) is usually traced to Arabic ḥarīf, "a rival, a fellow player", through Persian. The contest itself is હરીફાઈ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
પછાડવું (pachāḍvũ) — to throw down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a wrestler, then the Gujarati for a rival.

You'll want to know: પછાડવું.
પછાડવું — pachāḍvũ — "to throw down".
પહેલવાને તેના હરીફને ધરતી પર પછાડ્યો. pahelvāne tenā harīphne dhartī par pachāḍyo. The wrestler threw his rival to the ground.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે હરીફને પછાડ્યો, teṇe harīphne pachāḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
પછાડવું (pachāḍvũ) comes from pracchaṭ, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to knock down" (CDIAL 8493).

Lesson 5 of 5.
હંફાવવું (hamphāvvũ) — to tire out (an opponent).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a rival, then the Gujarati for to throw down.

You'll want to know: હંફાવવું.
હંફાવવું — hamphāvvũ — "to tire out (an opponent)".
નાના પહેલવાને મોટા હરીફને ઘણો હંફાવ્યો. nānā pahelvāne moṭā harīphne ghaṇo hamphāvyo. The small wrestler wore out his big rival.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે હરીફને હંફાવ્યો, teṇe harīphne hamphāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
હંફાવવું (hamphāvvũ) is built from હાંફવું, "to pant", with the causative -āv-: to leave an opponent panting. CDIAL files the family under hamph, "pant", an imitative form (CDIAL 13973).
