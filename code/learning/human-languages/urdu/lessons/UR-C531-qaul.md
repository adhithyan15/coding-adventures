## You'll want to know: قول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C531-QAUL531-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قول** — *qaul* — "one's given word".

> اگر وہ قول کا پکا ہوتا تو آج آتا۔
>
> *agar voh qaul kā pakkā hotā to āj ātā.*
>
> If he kept his word, he would have come today.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قول** (*qaul*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qawl*, "a saying, a word".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
