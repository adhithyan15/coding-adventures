Hindi, chapter 542: Coming to Terms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वार्ता (vārtā) — talks; a negotiation (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a noose, then the Hindi for to tangle.

You'll want to know: वार्ता.
वार्ता — vārtā — "talks; a negotiation (formal)".
दोनों पक्षों की वार्ता अच्छी रही। Donõ pakṣoṁ kī vārtā acchī rahī. The talks between the two sides went well.
Feminine.
A formal word, heard in the news.

The word, taken apart.
वार्ता (vārtā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vārttā, "news, an account of what has happened" (Monier-Williams); Hindi uses it, in a formal register, for talks between two sides.

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सहमति (sahmati) — agreement; consent (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for talks, then the Hindi for a concession.

You'll want to know: सहमति.
सहमति — sahmati — "agreement; consent (formal)".
दोनों दोस्तों में पूरी सहमति है। Donõ dostoṁ meṁ pūrī sahmati hai. The two friends are in complete agreement.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सहमति (sahmati) is a modern compound of two Sanskrit words, saha, "with", and mati, "mind, opinion" (Monier-Williams): being of one mind with someone.
