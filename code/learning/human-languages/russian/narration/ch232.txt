Lesson 1 of 5.
копить (kopít) — to save up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a class, a lesson, then the Russian for a mark, a grade.

You'll want to know: копить.
копить (kopít) — "to save up".

The word, taken apart.
копить (kopít) comes from копа, "a heap, a stack" (Vasmer), which Vasmer relates to Lithuanian kõpti, "to rake together".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for a mark, a grade, then the Russian for to save up.

You'll want to know: занимать.
занимать (zanimát) — "to borrow; to take up (space)".

The word, taken apart.
занимать (zanimát) is за- with -нимать, from the old verb jęti, "to take": to take up space, or to take money as a loan.

You'll want to know: звонить.
звонить (zvonít) — "to ring, to phone".

The word, taken apart.
звонить (zvonít) comes from звон, "a ringing", Proto-Slavic zvonŭ — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Albanian zë, "voice", and Armenian jayn, "voice", and a variant root in Latin sonus, "sound".

Lesson 4 of 5.
отправлять (otpravlyát) — to send.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to borrow; to take up, then the Russian for to ring.

You'll want to know: отправлять.
отправлять (otpravlyát) — "to send".

The word, taken apart.
отправлять (otpravlyát) is от-, "off, away", with -правлять, from править, "to direct, to steer", on правый, "straight": to send is to direct off.

Lesson 5 of 5.
получать (poluchát) — to receive, to get.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to ring, then the Russian for to send.

You'll want to know: получать.
получать (poluchát) — "to receive, to get".

The word, taken apart.
получать (poluchát) is по- with -лучать, from лучить, "to hit upon", the root of случиться, "to happen" (Vasmer): to receive is to come upon.
