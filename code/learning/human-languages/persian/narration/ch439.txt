Persian, chapter 439: In the room.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ملافه (malâfe) — a bed sheet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a deposit, then the Persian for a safe.

You'll want to know: ملافه.
ملافه (malâfe) — "a bed sheet".
ملافه (malâfe) های تخت تمیز بود. Malâfe-hâ-ye takht tamiz bud. The sheets on the bed were clean.
Plural malâfe-hâ. Malâfe 'avaz kardan — to change the sheets.

The word, taken apart.
malâfe was borrowed from Arabic: EtymDB derives it from Arabic milḥafa, usually glossed 'a wrap, a covering cloth'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
روتختی (rutakhti) — a bedspread, a bedcover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a safe, then the Persian for a bed sheet.

You'll want to know: روتختی.
روتختی (rutakhti) — "a bedspread, a bedcover".
روتختی (rutakhti) اتاق ما آبی بود. Rutakhti-ye otâq-e mâ âbi bud. The bedspread in our room was blue.
ru 'on' + takht 'bed' + -i: what goes on the bed.

The word, taken apart.
rutakhti is a native compound of ru 'face, on top of', takht 'bed, throne' and the ending -i. EtymDB does not list the compound.

Lesson 3 of 5.
کولر (kuler) — an air conditioner; an air cooler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a bed sheet, then the Persian for a bedspread.

You'll want to know: کولر.
کولر (kuler) — "an air conditioner; an air cooler".
کولر (kuler) اتاق کار نمیکرد و هوا گرم بود. Kuler-e otâq kâr nemikard va havâ garm bud. The room's air conditioner wasn't working and it was hot.
In Iran the kuler-e âbi cools the air with water; the kuler-e gâzi is a true air conditioner.

The word, taken apart.
kuler was borrowed from English and is usually traced to English cooler. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
منظره (manzare) — a view, a scene.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an air conditioner, then the Persian for central heating.

You'll want to know: منظره.
منظره (manzare) — "a view, a scene".
از پنجره اتاق منظره دریا را میدیدیم. Az panjere-ye otâq manzare-ye daryâ râ mididim. From the room's window we had a view of the sea.
Arabic plural manâzer. Manzare-ye zibâ — a lovely view.

The word, taken apart.
manzare was borrowed from Arabic manẓara (EtymDB), a word built on the same root as nazar 'look, view'.
