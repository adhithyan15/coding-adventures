## You'll want to know: くもる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くもる** — *kumoru* — "to cloud over".

> **あしたの ごごは くもります。** — *ashita no gogo wa kumorimasu* — "It will cloud over tomorrow afternoon."

Polite **くもります**, *kumorimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くもる** (*kumoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
