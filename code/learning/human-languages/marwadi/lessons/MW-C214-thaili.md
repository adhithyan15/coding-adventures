## You'll want to know: थैली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C214-RETURN214-03]; assesses=[] -->

**थैली** — *thailī* — "a small bag".

> सामान थैली मांय राखो।
>
> *sāmān thailī mā̃y rākho.*
>
> Put the things in the bag.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थैली** (*thailī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sthavi*, "sack, bag". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *thailī*, "bag" (CDIAL 13746).
