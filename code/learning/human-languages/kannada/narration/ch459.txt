Kannada, chapter 459: Mountains and Camps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪರ್ವತ (parvata) — a mountain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to get back, then the Kannada for to stay overnight.

You'll want to know: ಪರ್ವತ.
ಪರ್ವತ — parvata — "a mountain".
ಆ ಪರ್ವತದ ಮೇಲೆ ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ ಹಿಮ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ. ā parvatada mēle yāvāgalū hima iruttade. There is always snow on that mountain.
ಬೆಟ್ಟ is a hill; ಪರ್ವತ (parvata) is a high mountain.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರ್ವತ (parvata) is borrowed from Sanskrit parvata, "a rock, a mountain" (CDIAL 7945).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮುಗ್ಗರಿಸು (muggarisu) — to stumble; to lurch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mountain, then the Kannada for a peak.

You'll want to know: ಮುಗ್ಗರಿಸು.
ಮುಗ್ಗರಿಸು — muggarisu — "to stumble; to lurch".
ಕಲ್ಲಿನ ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅಜ್ಜ ಮುಗ್ಗರಿಸಿದರು. kallina dāriyalli ajja muggarisidaru. Grandfather stumbled on the stony path.
Past (I …): ಮುಗ್ಗರಿಸಿದೆ, muggariside.
A heavier stumble than ಎಡವು, to trip.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಗ್ಗರಿಸು (muggarisu) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled; DEDR shows no clear source for it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಡೇರೆ (ḍēre) — a tent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a peak, then the Kannada for to stumble.

You'll want to know: ಡೇರೆ.
ಡೇರೆ — ḍēre — "a tent".
ರಾತ್ರಿ ನಾವು ಶಿಖರದ ಕೆಳಗೆ ಡೇರೆ ಹಾಕಿದೆವು. rātri nāvu śikharada keḷage ḍēre hākidevu. At night we pitched a tent below the summit.
ಡೇರೆ (ḍēre) ಹಾಕು, to pitch a tent.

The word, taken apart.
ಡೇರೆ (ḍēre) is usually traced to Persian dēra, "a tent, a camp", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಬಿಡಾರ (biḍāra) — an encampment; a camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to stumble, then the Kannada for a tent.

You'll want to know: ಬಿಡಾರ.
ಬಿಡಾರ — biḍāra — "an encampment; a camp".
ಕೂಲಿಗಳ ಬಿಡಾರ ನದಿಯ ದಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು. kūligaḷa biḍāra nadiya daḍadalli ittu. The labourers’ camp was on the riverbank.
A place of tents or huts put up for a while.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಿಡಾರ (biḍāra) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5393 lists Kannada biḍāra, "a halting place", in the family of ಬಿಡು, "to let go; to stop, to halt".
