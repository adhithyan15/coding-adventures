Portuguese, chapter 43: Close, Drink, Eat, Give, Live.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
fechar — to close.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to learn, then the Portuguese for to open.

You'll want to know: fechar.
fechar — "to close". The opposite of abrir.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: fechar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for to learn, then the Portuguese for to open, then say fechar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does fechar mean? ("To close".) Say it once more.
