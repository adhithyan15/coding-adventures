## You'll want to know: よぶん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C532-LIMIT532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**よぶん** — *yobun* — "extra; spare".

> **もし ふたつ かったら、ひとつは よぶんでしょう。** — *moshi futatsu kattara, hitotsu wa yobun deshō* — "If you buy two, one will be spare."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よぶん** (*yobun*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yo*, "too much" (Middle Chinese *yo*) and *bun*, "part" (*bjunH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
