## You'll want to know: circuitus, circuitūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C454-DEXTRORSUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**circuitus, circuitūs** — "a detour, a way round; a circuit".

> Pōns frāctus est; itaque longō circuitū ad oppidum pervenīmus.
>
> *The bridge is broken; so we reach the town by a long detour.*

Fourth declension, masculine: *circuitus, circuitūs m.*, from *circumeō*. English *circuit* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*circuitus* is built on *circumeō* 'to go round' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the noun ending *-tus*. English *circuit* comes from it.
