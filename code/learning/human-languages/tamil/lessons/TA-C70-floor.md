## You'll want to know: தரை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C70-ROOM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**தரை** (*tarai*) — "the floor".

The floor — and also the ground outside. Tamil does not need two words for it; what is under
your feet is தரை wherever you are standing.

This is where the கோலம் (*kōlam*) is drawn each morning, just inside the வாசல். Both of those
words are already yours, and this is the one that was missing to put them in a place.

It ends in the **ை** again — the fifth word in three chapters to do so.
