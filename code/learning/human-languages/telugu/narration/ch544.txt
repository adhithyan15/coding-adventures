Telugu, chapter 544: Sport.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
క్రీడాకారుడు (krīḍākāruḍu) — a sportsman; an athlete.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to savour, then the Telugu for a stick dance.

You'll want to know: క్రీడాకారుడు.
క్రీడాకారుడు — krīḍākāruḍu — "a sportsman; an athlete".
ఆ క్రీడాకారుడు దేశానికి పతకం తెచ్చాడు. ā krīḍākāruḍu dēśāniki patakaṁ teccāḍu. That sportsman won a medal for the country.
క్రీడ, sport, + -కారుడు, one who does it.

The word, taken apart.
క్రీడాకారుడు (krīḍākāruḍu) is built on Sanskrit krīḍā, "sport, play, pastime" (Monier-Williams), with -కారుడు, "one who does".

Lesson 3 of 5.
తలపడు (talapaḍu) — to clash; to take on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sportsman, then the Telugu for an opponent.

You'll want to know: తలపడు.
తలపడు — talapaḍu — "to clash; to take on".
రేపు రెండు జట్లు ఫైనల్లో తలపడతాయి. rēpu reṇḍu jaṭlu phainallō talapaḍatāyi. Tomorrow the two teams clash in the final.
Past (I …): తలపడ్డాను, talapaḍḍānu.
-తో తలపడు (talapaḍu), to take someone on.

The word, taken apart.
తలపడు (talapaḍu) is a native compound: tala, "head" (DEDR 3103), and the native verb paḍu, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853): to meet head to head.

Lesson 4 of 5.
నెగ్గు (neggu) — to prevail; to win through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an opponent, then the Telugu for to clash.

You'll want to know: నెగ్గు.
నెగ్గు — neggu — "to prevail; to win through".
చివరి బంతికి మా జట్టు నెగ్గింది. civari bantiki mā jaṭṭu neggindi. Our team won on the last ball.
Past (I …): నెగ్గాను, neggānu.
Also in arguments and elections; గెలుచు is the everyday word.

The word, taken apart.
నెగ్గు (neggu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3661 lists Telugu neggu, "to be successful, to succeed, to thrive".

Lesson 5 of 5.
పరుగు (parugu) — running; a sprint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to clash, then the Telugu for to prevail.

You'll want to know: పరుగు.
పరుగు — parugu — "running; a sprint".
ఉదయం పరుగు ఆరోగ్యానికి మంచిది. udayaṁ parugu ārōgyāniki mañcidi. A morning run is good for your health.
The noun; పరిగెత్తు, to run, is the verb.

The word, taken apart.
పరుగు (parugu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3963 lists Telugu parugu, "running, a run", in the article of Tamil pari, "to run, to go fast".
