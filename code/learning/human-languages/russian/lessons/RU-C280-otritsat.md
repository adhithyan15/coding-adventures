## You'll want to know: отрицать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C280-SOOBSHCHAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**отрицать** (*otritsát*) — "to deny".

> Он отрицает, что был там.
>
> *On otritsáyet, shto byl tam.*
>
> He denies that he was there.

Imperfective, with no everyday perfective. It takes the accusative: *отрицать всё* (*otritsát vsyo*) — to deny everything.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отрицать* is usually described as a Church Slavonic verb, traced to *от-* "away, against" and the root of *рещи* "to say", so "to speak against"; EtymDB lists no parent.
