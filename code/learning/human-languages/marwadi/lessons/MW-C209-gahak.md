## You'll want to know: गाहक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C209-SHOP209-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गाहक** — *gāhak* — "a customer".

> गाहक भाव पूछै है।
>
> *gāhak bhāv pūchhai hai.*
>
> The customer asks the price.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गाहक** (*gāhak*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *grāhaka*, "one who takes; a purchaser" (Monier-Williams), with the *r* lost.
