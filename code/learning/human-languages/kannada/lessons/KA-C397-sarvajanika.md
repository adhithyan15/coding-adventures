## You'll want to know: ಸಾರ್ವಜನಿಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C397-NAGARIKA397-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾರ್ವಜನಿಕ** — *sārvajanika* — "public".

> ಇದು ಸಾರ್ವಜನಿಕ ಉದ್ಯಾನ.
>
> *idu sārvajanika udyāna.*
>
> This is a public park.

ಸಾರ್ವಜನಿಕರು, the public.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾರ್ವಜನಿಕ** (*sārvajanika*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sārvajanika*, "belonging or suited to all people" (Monier-Williams).
