# HL99 — Punjabi A2 `ਸੂਚਨਾ` prerequisite

Issue: #18186

After PR #18155 merged, an exact aggregate-only audit of the preserved
confidential Punjabi A2 mock drafts against merged main
`ae2bf3c535a07d075d798e10e37b3f23babdab13` finds **ਸੂਚਨਾ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs once in the confidential learner material and
twice in the confidential examiner material. Neither confidential body belongs
in the public corpus.

Chapter 199 closes that single-word gap through one deliberately narrow
identity frame: **ਇਹ ਸੂਚਨਾ ਹੈ** — “this is information / a notice.” The
voice-first opener retrieves only familiar **ਇਹ** and **ਹੈ**. One lesson
directly owns exact **ਸੂਚਨਾ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound,
meaning, and script, a bounded frame lesson places it between the two familiar
words, and a four-skill payoff retrieves the complete label again. The target
has one direct owner and three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored,
generated-book, or narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-LEX-SUCHNA-199`. The lesson keeps the Punjabi form whole and
owns only the neutral noun sense “information” or “notice.” It does not teach
gender, plural or oblique forms, derive the word from smaller Punjabi parts,
teach compounds such as “notification” or “information technology,” introduce
synonyms, or claim that every English use of “information,” “notice,” and
“announcement” is interchangeable.

Punjabi University's Punjabipedia records **ਸੂਚਨਾ** with the senses
information, news, and notice:
<https://punjabipedia.org/topic.aspx?txt=%E0%A8%B8%E0%A9%82%E0%A8%9A%E0%A8%A8%E0%A8%BE>.
That source supports the bounded present-day identity taught here. A Government
of Punjab Language Department publication also uses **ਸੂਚਨਾ** in current
public-administration prose:
<https://punjab.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Language-Department-RTI2023-1.pdf>.

The exact history of modern written **ਸੂਚਨਾ** remains unresolved in the
sources used here. The lesson therefore makes no etymological claim and does
not turn spelling resemblance into a productive derivation.

This is the twenty-second prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
