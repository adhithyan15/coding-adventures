## You'll want to know: شادمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C441-JOY441-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شادمان** — *shādmān* — "joyful; glad at heart".

> بیٹی کی کامیابی پر ماں شادمان تھی۔
>
> *beṭī kī kāmyābī par māṅ shādmān thī.*
>
> The mother was overjoyed at her daughter's success.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شادمان** (*shādmān*) is usually traced to Persian *shādmān*, "glad", built on *shād*, "happy".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
