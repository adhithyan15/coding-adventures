## You'll want to know: अळगो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C206-LANE206-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अळगो** — *aḷgo* — "separate; apart".

> म्हारो घर गांव सूं अळगो है।
>
> *mhāro ghar gā̃v sū̃ aḷgo hai.*
>
> My house stands apart from the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अळगो** (*aḷgo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *alagna*, "unconnected"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *alago*, "separate" (CDIAL 700).
