## You'll want to know: さまよう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C396-YUKISAKI396-02]; assesses=[] -->

**さまよう** — *samayou* — "to wander about".

> **ちずを なくして、まちを さまよいました。** — *chizu o nakushite, machi o samayoimashita* — "I lost my map and wandered about the town."

Polite **さまよいます**, *samayoimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが まちを さまよいました。**, *ani ga machi o samayoimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さまよう** (*samayou*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
