## You'll want to know: el árbitro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C756-PARTIDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el árbitro** — "the referee".

> El árbitro no vio la falta.
>
> *The referee did not spot the foul.*

*La árbitra* for a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*árbitro* comes from Latin *arbiter* "judge, umpire" (EtymDB).
