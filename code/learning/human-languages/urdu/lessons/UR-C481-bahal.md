## You'll want to know: بحال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C481-BETTER481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بحال** — *bahāl* — "restored; back to normal".

> ایک ہفتے بعد اس کی صحت بحال ہو گئی۔
>
> *ek hafte bād us kī sehat bahāl ho gaī.*
>
> A week later her health was back to normal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بحال** (*bahāl*) is usually traced to Persian *bahāl*, "restored", from *ba*, "to, in", and Arabic *ḥāl*, "state": back in its proper state.
