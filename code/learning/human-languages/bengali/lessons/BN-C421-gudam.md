## You'll want to know: গুদাম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C421-GUDAM421-02]; assesses=[] -->

**গুদাম** — *gudām* — "a warehouse; a godown".

> দোকানদার গুদামে চালের বস্তা রাখলেন।
>
> *dokāndār gudāme chāler bôstā rākhlen.*
>
> The shopkeeper stored the sacks of rice in the warehouse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গুদাম** (*gudām*) is usually traced to Malay *gudang*, "a storehouse", carried round the Indian Ocean by Portuguese traders; English *godown* is the same word.
