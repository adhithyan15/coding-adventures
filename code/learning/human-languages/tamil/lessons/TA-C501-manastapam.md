# மனஸ்தாபம் (maṉastāpam) — ill feeling; estrangement

## You'll want to know: மனஸ்தாபம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C501-CANTAI501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**மனஸ்தாபம்** — *maṉastāpam* — "ill feeling; estrangement".

> ஒரு சிறிய பேச்சால் அவர்களுக்குள் மனஸ்தாபம் ஏற்பட்டது.
>
> *oru ciṟiya pēccāl avarkaḷukkuḷ maṉastāpam ēṟpaṭṭatu.*
>
> A small remark caused ill feeling between them.

Written with the grantha ஸ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மனஸ்தாபம்** (*maṉastāpam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *manastāpa*, "pain or anguish of the mind" (Monier-Williams); Tamil uses it for a rift between people.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
