## You'll want to know: प्रविशति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C75-TRAVEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रविशति** (*praviśati*) — "(he, she) goes in". **गृहं प्रविशति** — "goes into the
house".

## What you've built

The verb of every doorway.
