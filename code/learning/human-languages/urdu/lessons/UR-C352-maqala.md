## You'll want to know: مقالہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C352-MAZMUN352-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مقالہ** — *maqālā* — "a thesis; a research paper".

> اس نے اپنا مقالہ ختم کیا۔
>
> *us ne apnā maqālā khatm kiyā.*
>
> She finished her thesis.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقالہ** (*maqālā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maqāla*, "a saying; a treatise".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
