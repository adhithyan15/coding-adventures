# പരിഗണിക്കുക (parigaṇikkuka) — to take into account; to consider

## You'll want to know: പരിഗണിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C408-NILAPATU408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിഗണിക്കുക** — *parigaṇikkuka* — "to take into account; to consider".

> കമ്പനി എന്റെ അപേക്ഷ പരിഗണിച്ചു.
>
> *kampani enṟe apēkṣa parigaṇiccu.*
>
> The company considered my application.

Past: പരിഗണിച്ചു, *parigaṇiccu*.

What is considered takes -നെ or stays unmarked: അപേക്ഷ പരിഗണിക്കുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിഗണിക്കുക** (*parigaṇikkuka*) is built from Sanskrit *pari-*, "around, all over", and the root *gaṇ*, "to count" (*gaṇita*, "counted", CDIAL 3996), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക: to consider is to count a thing over.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
