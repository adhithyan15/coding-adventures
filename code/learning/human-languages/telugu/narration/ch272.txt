Telugu, chapter 272: Trellis, Fence, Bush, Stem, Wing.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
పందిరి (pandiri) — a trellis; a festive canopy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for foam, then the Telugu for a hole.

You'll want to know: పందిరి.
పందిరి — pandiri — "a trellis; a festive canopy".

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does పందిరి (pandiri) mean? ("A trellis; a festive canopy".) Say it once more.

(రేడియో, rēḍiyō.) A programme? (కార్యక్రమం, kāryakramaṁ.) The internet? (ఇంటర్నెట్, iṇṭarneṭ.) A website? (వెబ్సైట్, vebsaiṭ.) A tin? (డబ్బా, ḍabbā.) An eating plate? (కంచం, kañcaṁ.) A sieve? (జల్లెడ, jalleḍa.) Wood? (చెక్క, cekka.) Brass? (ఇత్తడి, ittaḍi.)
