## You'll want to know: prōnūntiō, prōnūntiāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C371-ROSTRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**prōnūntiō, prōnūntiāre** — "to proclaim, to announce; to deliver (a speech)".

> Praecō sententiam iūdicum prōnūntiāvit.
>
> *The herald announced the judges' verdict.*

*prōnūntiō, prōnūntiāre, prōnūntiāvī, prōnūntiātum*. Built on *nūntiō*, to announce. It is also a speaker's delivery. English *pronounce*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prōnūntiō* is built from *prō* "forth" and *nūntiō* "to announce" (Lewis & Short). English *pronounce* comes from it through French.
