## You'll want to know: традиция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C318-TRADITSIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**традиция** (*tradítsiya*) — "a tradition".

> В нашей семье есть старая традиция.
>
> *V náshey semyé yest stáraya tradítsiya.*
>
> Our family has an old tradition.

Feminine. *По традиции* — by tradition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*традиция* goes back to Latin *trāditiō* "a handing over" (EtymDB), from *trādere* "to hand over" (Lewis & Short).
