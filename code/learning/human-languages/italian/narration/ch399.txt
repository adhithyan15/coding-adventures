Italian, chapter 399: Il mio account.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'account — the online account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the loss, then the Italian for to go bankrupt.

You'll want to know: l'account.
l'account — "the online account".
Se non hai un account, devi crearne uno. If you do not have an account, you have to create one.
Masculine, invariable: gli account. A bank account is il conto.

The word, taken apart.
account was borrowed from English account (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Old French acont, built on conter "to count", from Latin computāre "to reckon".

la password — "the password".
Se dimentichi la password, clicca qui. If you forget your password, click here.
Feminine in Italian; less often la parola d'ordine.

The word, taken apart.
password was borrowed from English password (EtymDB), built from pass and word: the word that lets you pass a guard.

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'utente — the user.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the online account, then the Italian for the password.

You'll want to know: l'utente.
l'utente — "the user".
Il sito ha più di un milione di utenti. The website has more than a million users.
Also a customer of a service: gli utenti del treno. One form for men and women.

The word, taken apart.
utente is a learned borrowing from Latin ūtēns, ūtentis "using" (Lewis & Short), present participle of ūtī "to use".

Lesson 4 of 5.
accedere — to log in, to access; to enter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the password, then the Italian for the user.

You'll want to know: accedere.
accedere — "to log in, to access; to enter".
Per accedere, inserisci il tuo nome e la password. To log in, enter your name and your password.
Accedere a — to get into: accedere al conto, accedere all'università.

The word, taken apart.
accedere is a learned borrowing from Latin accēdere "to approach, to come near" (EtymDB), built from ad "to" and cēdere "to go".

Lesson 5 of 5.
registrarsi — to sign up, to register.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the user, then the Italian for to log in.

You'll want to know: registrarsi.
registrarsi — "to sign up, to register".
Se ti registri oggi, il primo mese è gratis. If you sign up today, the first month is free.
Without -si, registrare is to record: registrare un video.

The word, taken apart.
registrarsi is built on registro "register", from Medieval Latin registrum, from Late Latin regesta "a list", from regerere "to carry back, to record" (Lewis & Short), built on gerere "to carry".
