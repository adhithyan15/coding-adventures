## You'll want to know: दुष्ट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C469-KATHOR469-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दुष्ट** — *duṣṭa* — "wicked".

> गोष्टीतला दुष्ट राजा शेवटी हरला.
>
> *goṣṭītlā duṣṭa rājā śevṭī harlā.*
>
> The wicked king in the story was defeated in the end.

Unchanging; also a noun, a villain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुष्ट** (*duṣṭa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *duṣṭa*, "defiled, bad; a villain" (CDIAL 6472).
