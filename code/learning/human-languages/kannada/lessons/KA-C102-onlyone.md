## You'll want to know: ಒಂದೇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C102-THISWAY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಂದೇ** (*ondē*) — "only one", or "one and the same". It is **ಒಂದು**, "one",
with the emphatic **‑ಏ** of **ಅದೇ**.

## What you've built

One, made emphatic.
