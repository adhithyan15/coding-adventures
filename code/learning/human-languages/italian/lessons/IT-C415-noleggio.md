## You'll want to know: il noleggio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C415-NOLEGGIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il noleggio** — "the hire, the rental".

> Il noleggio costa quaranta euro al giorno.
>
> *The hire costs forty euros a day.*

*Un'auto a noleggio* — a hire car; *il noleggio bici* — bike hire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*noleggio* is built on *nolo* 'hire', which is usually traced through Medieval Latin *naulum* to Greek *naûlon* 'ship fare'; it came into Italian as a borrowing.
