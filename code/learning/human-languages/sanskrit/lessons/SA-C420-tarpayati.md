## You'll want to know: तर्पयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C420-PRAPA420-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्पयति** — *tarpayati* — "refreshes; satisfies".

> शीतलं जलं श्रान्तं पथिकं तर्पयति।
>
> *śītalaṃ jalaṃ śrāntaṃ pathikaṃ tarpayati.*
>
> Cool water refreshes the tired wayfarer.

Past (it …): जलं पथिकं तर्पितवत्, *jalaṃ pathikaṃ tarpitavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्पयति** (*tarpayati*) is built on the root √tṛp, "to be satisfied", in its causative, "to satisfy, to refresh"; MW gives the participle *tarpita*, "satisfied" (MW).
