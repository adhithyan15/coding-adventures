Kannada, chapter 487: Storms and Disasters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚಂಡಮಾರುತ (caṇḍamāruta) — a cyclone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to shroud, then the Kannada for snowfall.

You'll want to know: ಚಂಡಮಾರುತ.
ಚಂಡಮಾರುತ — caṇḍamāruta — "a cyclone".
ಚಂಡಮಾರುತದಿಂದ ಮೀನುಗಾರರು ಸಮುದ್ರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗಲಿಲ್ಲ. caṇḍamārutadinda mīnugāraru samudrakke hōgalilla. Because of the cyclone the fishermen did not go to sea.
ಚಂಡ, fierce, plus ಮಾರುತ, wind.

The word, taken apart.
ಚಂಡಮಾರುತ (caṇḍamāruta) is built from two Sanskrit words: caṇḍa, "passionate, fierce" (CDIAL 4584), and māruta, "windy; the wind" (CDIAL 10068).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ (suṇṭaragāḷi) — a whirlwind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for snowfall, then the Kannada for a cyclone.

You'll want to know: ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ.
ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ — suṇṭaragāḷi — "a whirlwind".
ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ ಹೊಲದ ಧೂಳನ್ನು ಮೇಲಕ್ಕೆ ಎತ್ತಿತು. suṇṭaragāḷi holada dhūḷannu mēlakke ettitu. A whirlwind lifted the dust of the field up into the air.
A spinning wind; ಬಿರುಗಾಳಿ is a storm.

The word, taken apart.
ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ (suṇṭaragāḷi) ends in native ಗಾಳಿ, "wind, air" (DEDR 1499); the source of its first part, suṇṭara, is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಅಬ್ಬರಿಸು (abbarisu) — to rage; to roar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cyclone, then the Kannada for a whirlwind.

You'll want to know: ಅಬ್ಬರಿಸು.
ಅಬ್ಬರಿಸು — abbarisu — "to rage; to roar".
ಬಿರುಗಾಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಮುದ್ರ ಅಬ್ಬರಿಸಿತು. birugāḷiyalli samudra abbarisitu. In the storm the sea raged.
Past (it …): ಅಬ್ಬರಿಸಿತು, abbarisitu.
ಅಬ್ಬರ is an uproar: of the sea, of storms and of loud speakers.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಬ್ಬರಿಸು (abbarisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 367 lists Kannada abbara, "a loud cry, a noise", and abbarisu, "to cry aloud, to howl".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಭೂಕುಸಿತ (bhūkusita) — a landslide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a whirlwind, then the Kannada for to rage.

You'll want to know: ಭೂಕುಸಿತ.
ಭೂಕುಸಿತ — bhūkusita — "a landslide".
ಘಾಟಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಭೂಕುಸಿತದಿಂದ ರಸ್ತೆ ಮುಚ್ಚಿತು. ghāṭiyalli bhūkusitadinda raste muccitu. The ghat road was closed by a landslide.
ಭೂ, earth, plus ಕುಸಿತ, a sinking or collapse.

The word, taken apart.
ಭೂಕುಸಿತ (bhūkusita) joins Sanskrit bhū, "the earth, the ground" (CDIAL 9546), and native kusita, "a collapse", from kusi, "to sink, to give way" (DEDR 1636).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸುನಾಮಿ (sunāmi) — a tsunami.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to rage, then the Kannada for a landslide.

You'll want to know: ಸುನಾಮಿ.
ಸುನಾಮಿ — sunāmi — "a tsunami".
ಸುನಾಮಿಯಿಂದ ಕರಾವಳಿಯ ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳು ನಾಶವಾದವು. sunāmiyinda karāvaḷiya haḷḷigaḷu nāśavādavu. Coastal villages were destroyed by the tsunami.
A great sea wave raised by an earthquake under the sea.

The word, taken apart.
ಸುನಾಮಿ (sunāmi) is a loan from English tsunami, which English took from Japanese.
