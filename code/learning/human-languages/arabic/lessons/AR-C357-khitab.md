## You'll want to know: خطاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C357-KHITAB357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خطاب** — *khiṭāb* — "a speech, an address".

> كان خطاب المدير قصيرا.
>
> *kāna khiṭāb al-mudīr qaṣīran.*
>
> The manager’s speech was short.

In some countries خطاب is also a letter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خطاب** (*khiṭāb*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-b* (Lane), "to address, to speak to", the root of *khaṭaba* and *khuṭūba*. *Khiṭāb* is the address itself.
