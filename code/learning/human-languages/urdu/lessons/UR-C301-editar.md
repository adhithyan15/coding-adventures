## You'll want to know: ایڈیٹر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C301-SAHAFI301-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ایڈیٹر** — *eḍīṭar* — "an editor".

> ایڈیٹر نے خبر چھاپی۔
>
> *eḍīṭar ne khabar chhāpī.*
>
> The editor printed the story.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is borrowed from English *editor*. The **ایڈیٹر** decides what the paper prints.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
