# फेर (pher) — again

## You'll want to know: फेर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C163-PLAN163-02]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04] -->

**फेर** — *pher* — "again".

> **हूं काल फेर आसूं।**
>
> *hū̃ kāl pher āsū̃.* — I'll come again tomorrow.

It opens on a letter you have not written yet: *ph*, a **प** with a breath after
it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फेर** (*pher*) is built on the old verb for turning. CDIAL gives Hindi and
Gujarati *pher*, "again", under a reconstructed *phirati*, "turns", and lists Old
Marwari *phiraï*, "turns", in the same entry (CDIAL 9078).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C163-PLAN163-02, MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01] -->

**फेर** sits right before the verb: **फेर आसूं**, **फेर जासूं**.
