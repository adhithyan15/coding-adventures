## You'll want to know: हिंदकळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C409-KHALASHI409-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हिंदकळणे** — *hindkaḷṇe* — "to slosh about; to rock".

> लाटांवर होडी हिंदकळत होती.
>
> *lāṭāṁvar hoḍī hindkaḷat hotī.*
>
> The boat was rocking on the waves.

Past (he …): तो हिंदकळला, *to hindkaḷlā*.

For water moving in a pot or a tank, and for a boat on waves.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिंदकळणे** (*hindkaḷṇe*) may belong with Sanskrit *hindolayati*, "swings", where CDIAL lists Marathi *hĩḍaḷṇẽ*, "to jog, shake" (CDIAL 14095); the link is not settled.
