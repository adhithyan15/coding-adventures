## You'll want to know: pà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C51-FEEL51-02]; assesses=[] -->

*pà* — "to be afraid of; to fear".

> *nǐ pà shénme* — What are you afraid of?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pà* is inherited from Middle Chinese *phaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to fear'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
