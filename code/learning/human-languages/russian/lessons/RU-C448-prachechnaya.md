## You'll want to know: прачечная
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C448-KHIMCHISTKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**прачечная** (*práchechnaya*) — "a laundry (shop), a launderette".

> В прачечной стирают бельё.
>
> *V práchechnoy stiráyut belyó.*
>
> At the laundry they wash linen.

An adjective used as a noun. Traditionally *чн* is pronounced *шн* here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прачечная* is built on *прачка* 'a washerwoman' with an adjective ending: the washerwomen's place. EtymDB lists *прачечная* beside *прачка*.
