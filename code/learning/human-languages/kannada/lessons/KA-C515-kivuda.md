## You'll want to know: ಕಿವುಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C515-KURUDA515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಿವುಡ** — *kivuḍa* — "deaf; a deaf man".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕಿವುಡ, ಜೋರಾಗಿ ಮಾತಾಡಬೇಕು.
>
> *ajja svalpa kivuḍa, jōrāgi mātāḍabēku.*
>
> Grandfather is a little deaf; you have to speak loudly.

Built on ಕಿವಿ, the ear. A deaf woman is ಕಿವುಡಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಿವುಡ** (*kivuḍa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1977 lists Kannada *kivi*, "ear", and *kivuḍa*, "a deaf man".
