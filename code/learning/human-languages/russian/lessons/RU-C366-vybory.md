## You'll want to know: выборы
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C366-VYBORY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**выборы** (*výbory*) — "an election".

> Выборы будут в сентябре.
>
> *Výbory búdut v sentyabré.*
>
> The election will be in September.

Plural only. *Выборы президента* — a presidential election.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**выборы** is built from *вы-* "out" and the root of *брать* "to take", as in *выбирать* "to choose": an election picks people out.
