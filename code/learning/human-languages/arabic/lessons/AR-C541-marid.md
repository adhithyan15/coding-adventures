## You'll want to know: معرض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C541-FANN541-02]; assesses=[] -->

**معرض** — *maʿriḍ* — "an exhibition".

> زرنا معرض الكتاب في المدينة.
>
> *zurnā maʿriḍ al-kitāb fī al-madīna.*
>
> We visited the book fair in the city.

Plural: معارض, *maʿāriḍ*.

معرض الكتاب is a book fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معرض** (*maʿriḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-ḍ*, "to appear, to come in the way" (Lane). A maʿriḍ is a place where things are shown.
