## You'll want to know: convertirse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C572-CAMBIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**convertirse** — "to become, to turn into".

> El pueblo se convirtió en una ciudad.
>
> *The village turned into a city.*

*Convertirse en* + noun. Stem change: *se convierte*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convertirse* comes from Latin *convertere* 'to turn around, to change' (Lewis & Short), built on *con-* and *vertere* 'to turn'.
