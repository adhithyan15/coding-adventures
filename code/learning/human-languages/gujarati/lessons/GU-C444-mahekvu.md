## You'll want to know: મહેકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C444-BUD444-05]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેકવું** — *mahekvũ* — "to be fragrant; to smell sweet".

> સાંજે બગીચામાં ગુલાબનાં ફૂલ મહેકે છે.
>
> *sā̃je bagīcāmā̃ gulābnā̃ phūl maheke che.*
>
> In the evening the roses smell sweet in the garden.

Past (it …): તે ફૂલ મહેક્યું, *te phūl mahekyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેકવું** (*mahekvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *magha*, a word CDIAL takes, with some doubt, to have meant "fragrance"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to give off a scent" (CDIAL 9703).
