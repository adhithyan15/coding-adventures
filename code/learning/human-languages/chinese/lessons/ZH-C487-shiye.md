## You'll want to know: 事业
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C487-CAREER487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**事业** — *shìyè* — "career; life's work".

> **他觉得事业最重要** *tā juéde shìyè zuì zhòngyào* — He thinks his career matters most.

The new character, in pieces: 业 — five strokes and no piece you have written yet: two verticals side by side, a dot on the left and a short stroke falling left on the right, then a long bar along the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**事业** (*shìyè*) is built from 事 'a matter' and 业 'a trade', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzriH* and *ngjaep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to employ' and the second 'affairs' and 'great'.
