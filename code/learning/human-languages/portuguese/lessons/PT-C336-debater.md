## You'll want to know: debater
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C336-DEBATE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**debater** — "to debate, to discuss".

> Os alunos debateram o tema durante uma hora.
>
> *The students debated the topic for an hour.*

*Debater um tema* — to debate a topic. More formal than *discutir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*debater* is usually traced to Old French *debatre* "to fight, to contend, to debate" (EtymDB), built from *de-* and *batre* "to beat", from Latin *battuere* "to beat, to strike" (Lewis & Short).
