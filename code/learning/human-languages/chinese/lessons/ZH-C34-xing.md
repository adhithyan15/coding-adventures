## You'll want to know: xìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C34-CALLED34-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xìng* — "to have the family name; family name".

> *nǐ xìng shénme* — What's your family name?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sjengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'clan name'.
