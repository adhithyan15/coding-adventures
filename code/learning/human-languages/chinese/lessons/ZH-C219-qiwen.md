## You'll want to know: qìwēn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C219-CLOUD219-04]; assesses=[] -->

*qìwēn* — "air temperature".

> *jīntiān de qìwēn bǐ zuótiān gāo* — It's warmer today than yesterday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qìwēn* is built from 气 'air' and *wēn* 'warm', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khj+jH* and *'won* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'breath'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
