## You'll want to know: pareggiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C511-RISULTATI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pareggiare** — "to draw, to equalize".

> Abbiamo pareggiato uno a uno.
>
> *We drew one all.*

Also to even out: *pareggiare i conti*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pareggiare* is built on *pari* "equal, even", inherited from Latin *pār, paris* "equal" (Lewis & Short).
