Urdu, chapter 528: Offerings and Blessings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
برکت (barkat) — a blessing; plenty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an excuse, then the Urdu for to get bored.

You'll want to know: برکت.
برکت — barkat — "a blessing; plenty".
اگر بارش ہوئی تو کھیتوں میں برکت ہو گی۔ agar bārish huī to khetoṅ meṅ barkat ho gī. If it rains, the fields will be blessed with plenty.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
برکت (barkat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic baraka, "a blessing, abundance".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نوازنا (navāznā) — to bestow favour on; to honour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an amulet, then the Urdu for an offering.

You'll want to know: نوازنا.
نوازنا — navāznā — "to bestow favour on; to honour".
اگر خدا نے نوازا تو ہم نیا گھر بنائیں گے۔ agar khudā ne navāzā to ham nayā ghar banāeṅ ge. If we are blessed, we will build a new house.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مہمان کو تحفے سے نوازا, us ne mehmān ko tohfe se navāzā.

The word, taken apart.
نوازنا (navāznā) is built on Persian navāz-, usually traced to the present stem of navākhtan, "to caress, to favour", with the Urdu verb ending -nā.
