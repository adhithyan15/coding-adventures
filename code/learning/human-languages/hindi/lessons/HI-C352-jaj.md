## You'll want to know: जज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C352-ADALAT352-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जज** — *jaj* — "a judge".

> जज ने दोनों पक्षों की बात सुनी।
>
> *jaj ne donõ pakṣoṁ kī bāt sunī.*
>
> The judge heard both sides.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जज** (*jaj*) is a loan from English *judge*.
