## You'll want to know: निरंतर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C391-PRAYAS391-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निरंतर** — *nirantar* — "continuous, without a break".

> अगर तुम निरंतर अभ्यास करोगे तो तुम्हारी हिंदी अच्छी हो जाएगी।
>
> *agar tum nirantar abhyās karoge to tumhārī hindī acchī ho jāegī.*
>
> If you practise without a break, your Hindi will become good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निरंतर** (*nirantar*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nirantara*, "without an interval, constant" (Monier-Williams).
