Hindi, chapter 486: Aches and Pains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दुखना (dukhnā) — to ache.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to retch, then the Hindi for a burp.

You'll want to know: दुखना.
दुखना — dukhnā — "to ache".
ठंड में दादा के घुटने दुखते हैं। ṭhaṇḍ meṁ dādā ke ghuṭne dukhte haiṁ. Grandfather's knees ache in the cold.
Past (it …): वह पैर बहुत दुखा, vah pair bahut dukhā.

The word, taken apart.
दुखना (dukhnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi dukhnā, "pains", under Sanskrit duḥkhati (CDIAL 6376), the entry that also holds dukhānā. The part of the body that aches is the subject.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कसकना (kasaknā) — to throb with pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to ache, then the Hindi for the calf of the leg.

You'll want to know: कसकना.
कसकना — kasaknā — "to throb with pain".
ठंड में पुरानी चोट कसकती है। ṭhaṇḍ meṁ purānī coṭ kasaktī hai. An old injury throbs in the cold.
Past (it …): वह चोट रात भर कसकी, vah coṭ rāt bhar kaskī.

The word, taken apart.
कसकना (kasaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi kasaknā, "to be racked by pain, ache", under Sanskrit kaṣati, "rubs, scratches" (CDIAL 2972). It is the dull, returning ache of an old hurt.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: रीढ़.
रीढ़ — rīṛh — "the spine; the backbone".
भारी बोझ उठाने से मज़दूर की रीढ़ में दर्द है। bhārī bojh uṭhāne se mazdūr kī rīṛh meṁ dard hai. The labourer's back hurts from lifting heavy loads.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
रीढ़ (rīṛh) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi rīṛh, "backbone", under Sanskrit rīḍhaka- (CDIAL 10749a). rīṛh kī haḍḍī, "the bone of the back", is the spine itself.
