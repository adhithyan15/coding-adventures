## You'll want to know: 现代
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C323-ERA323-05]; assesses=[] -->

**现代** — *xiàndài* — "modern times; modern".

> **现代的人都有手机** *xiàndài de rén dōu yǒu shǒujī* — Everyone has a mobile phone these days.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**现代** (*xiàndài*) is built from 现 'to appear, now' and 代 'an age', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *henH* and *dojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to show'; the sense 'now' is not given there.
