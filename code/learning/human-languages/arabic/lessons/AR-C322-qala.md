## You'll want to know: قلعة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C322-QALA322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قلعة** — *qalʿa* — "a castle, a citadel".

> صعدنا إلى قلعة على الجبل.
>
> *ṣaʿidnā ilā qalʿa ʿalā al-jabal.*
>
> We climbed up to a castle on the mountain.

Plural: قلاع, *qilāʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قلعة** (*qalʿa*) is usually filed under the native Arabic root *q-l-ʿ*, "to pull out by the roots" (Lane). Lane's article does not give the castle sense, and how a fortress took the name is not settled.
