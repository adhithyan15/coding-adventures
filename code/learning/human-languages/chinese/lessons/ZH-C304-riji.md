## You'll want to know: 日记
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C304-SCHOOL304-02]; assesses=[] -->

**日记** — *rìjì* — "diary".

> **我小时候写过日记** *wǒ xiǎoshíhou xiě guo rìjì* — I kept a diary when I was little.

The new character, in pieces: 记 — five strokes in two pieces: the speech piece of 说 on the left, two strokes; on the right the right piece of 起, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**日记** (*rìjì*) is built from 日 'a day' and 记 'to note down', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyit* and *kiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to record'.
