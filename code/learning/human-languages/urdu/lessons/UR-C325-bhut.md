## You'll want to know: بھوت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C325-DEV325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بھوت** — *bhūt* — "a ghost".

> بچوں کو بھوت سے ڈر لگتا ہے۔
>
> *bachchoṅ ko bhūt se ḍar lagtā hai.*
>
> Children are afraid of ghosts.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھوت** (*bhūt*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhūta*, "a being, a spirit" (Monier-Williams): what has been, and haunts.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
