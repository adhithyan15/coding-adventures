Malayalam, chapter 471: At the Garage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
എഞ്ചിൻ (eñcin) — an engine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a brake, then the Malayalam for to get a puncture.

You'll want to know: എഞ്ചിൻ.
എഞ്ചിൻ — eñcin — "an engine".
വണ്ടിയുടെ എഞ്ചിൻ വളരെ പഴയതാണ്. vaṇṭiyuṭe eñcin vaḷare paḻayatāṇŭ. The vehicle's engine is very old.
A train engine is also an എഞ്ചിൻ (eñcin).

The word, taken apart.
എഞ്ചിൻ (eñcin) was borrowed from English engine, the machine that drives a vehicle.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ചക്രം.
ചക്രം — cakraṁ — "a wheel".
ബസിന്റെ ഒരു ചക്രം ചെളിയിൽ താഴ്ന്നു. basinṟe oru cakraṁ ceḷiyil tāḻnnu. One of the bus's wheels sank into the mud.
Plural: ചക്രങ്ങൾ, cakraṅṅaḷ.
A two-wheeler, a scooter or a motorbike, is an ഇരുചക്രവാഹനം, a two-wheeled vehicle.

The word, taken apart.
ചക്രം (cakraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit cakra, "a wheel; a circle" (CDIAL 4538).

Lesson 3 of 5.
തകരാറ് (takarāṟŭ) — a fault; a breakdown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an engine, then the Malayalam for a wheel.

You'll want to know: തകരാറ്.
തകരാറ് — takarāṟŭ — "a fault; a breakdown".
എഞ്ചിന് എന്തോ തകരാറുണ്ട്. eñcinŭ entō takarāṟuṇṭŭ. There is some fault in the engine.
The thing with the fault takes -ന് / -ക്ക്: വണ്ടിക്ക് തകരാറുണ്ട്, the vehicle has a fault.

The word, taken apart.
തകരാറ് (takarāṟŭ) is usually traced to Arabic takrār, "repetition, dispute", which came south through Hindustani; in Malayalam it names a fault in a machine.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ഹോൺ (hōṇ) — a horn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a wheel, then the Malayalam for a fault.

You'll want to know: ഹോൺ.
ഹോൺ — hōṇ — "a horn".
പിന്നിലെ കാർ ഉറക്കെ ഹോൺ അടിച്ചു. pinnile kār uṟakke hōṇ aṭiccu. The car behind honked loudly.
To sound a horn is ഹോൺ (hōṇ) അടിക്കുക, to hit the horn.

The word, taken apart.
ഹോൺ (hōṇ) was borrowed from English horn, the device a vehicle sounds as a warning.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ചളുങ്ങുക (caḷuṅṅuka) — to get dented.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a fault, then the Malayalam for a horn.

You'll want to know: ചളുങ്ങുക.
ചളുങ്ങുക — caḷuṅṅuka — "to get dented".
ചെറിയ അപകടത്തിൽ കാറിന്റെ വാതിൽ ചളുങ്ങി. ceṟiya apakaṭattil kāṟinṟe vātil caḷuṅṅi. In the small accident the car's door got dented.
Past: ചളുങ്ങി, caḷuṅṅi.
Metal and tin things get dented: a pot, a car, a bucket.

The word, taken apart.
ചളുങ്ങുക (caḷuṅṅuka) is a Malayalam verb whose origin is not settled: DEDR 2407 lists a native caḷuṅṅuka, "to shake, to be frightened", but whether the sense of being dented comes from that word is unclear.
