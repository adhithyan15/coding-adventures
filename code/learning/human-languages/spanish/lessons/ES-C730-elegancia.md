## You'll want to know: la elegancia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C730-ENCANTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la elegancia** — "elegance".

> Viste con mucha elegancia.
>
> *She dresses with great elegance.*

The adjective is *elegante*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*elegancia* comes from Latin *ēlegantia* "refinement, elegance" (EtymDB), linked with *ēligere* "to pick out": elegance is good choosing.
