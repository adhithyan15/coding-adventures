## You'll want to know: రచించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C540-SAHITYAM540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**రచించు** — *raciñcu* — "to compose; to author".

> ఆ కవి వంద పాటలు రచించారు.
>
> *ā kavi vanda pāṭalu raciñcāru.*
>
> That poet composed a hundred songs.

Past (I …): రచించాను, *raciñcānu*.

Formal, for books, songs and plays.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రచించు** (*raciñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *racana*, "the act of making, arranging, composing" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
