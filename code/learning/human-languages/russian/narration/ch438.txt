Russian, chapter 438: Signs and landmarks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
указатель (ukazátel) — a signpost; an index.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a compass, then the Russian for a satnav.

You'll want to know: указатель.
указатель (ukazátel) — "a signpost; an index".
По указателю до озера три километра. Po ukazátelyu do ózera tri kilométra. According to the signpost it is three kilometres to the lake.
Masculine. Дорожный указатель (ukazátel) — a road sign.

The word, taken apart.
указатель (ukazátel) is built on указать 'to show the way' with -тель, from казать 'to show', the root of сказать 'to say'. EtymDB lists указатель (ukazátel) without a deeper source.

На двери висела табличка «Закрыто». Na dverí viséla tablíchka «Zakrýto». A sign saying Closed hung on the door.
Feminine; a diminutive of таблица.

The word, taken apart.
табличка (tablíchka) is a diminutive of таблица (EtymDB lists it without a source), which is usually traced to Latin tabula 'a board, a plank' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
стрелка (strélka) — an arrow (on a sign); a hand (of a clock).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a signpost, then the Russian for a sign.

You'll want to know: стрелка.
стрелка (strélka) — "an arrow (on a sign); a hand (of a clock)".
Стрелка на знаке показывает дорогу налево. Strélka na znáke pokázyvayet dorógu nalévo. The arrow on the sign shows the way to the left.
Feminine. Часовая стрелка (strélka) — the hour hand; по часовой стрелке — clockwise.

The word, taken apart.
стрелка (strélka) is a diminutive of стрела 'an arrow', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic strěla 'an arrow' (EtymDB). EtymDB lists стрелка (strélka) itself without a source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ориентир (oriyentír) — a landmark (to steer by).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sign, then the Russian for an arrow.

You'll want to know: ориентир.
ориентир (oriyentír) — "a landmark (to steer by)".
Хороший ориентир — церковь на площади. Khoróshiy oriyentír — tsérkov na plóshchadi. A good landmark is the church on the square.
Masculine. Also a guideline: ориентир (oriyentír) для работы.

The word, taken apart.
ориентир (oriyentír) is built on ориентировать 'to orient', usually traced to French orienter (EtymDB), on Latin oriēns 'the east' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
схема (skhéma) — a route map, a diagram.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an arrow, then the Russian for a landmark.

You'll want to know: схема.
схема (skhéma) — "a route map, a diagram".
У входа в метро висит схема линий. U vkhóda v metró visít skhéma líniy. There is a map of the lines at the metro entrance.
Feminine. Схема метро — the metro map.

The word, taken apart.
схема (skhéma) is usually traced to Greek skhêma 'a form, a shape' (EtymDB lists the Greek word), probably through Latin schēma or German Schema; EtymDB does not list the Russian word.
