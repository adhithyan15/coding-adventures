Bengali, chapter 72: Big, Small, New, Old, Bad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বড় (bôṛo) — big.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an ant, then the Bengali for a hen.

You'll want to know: বড়.
বড় — bôṛo — "big".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bôṛo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bôṛo, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bôṛo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for an ant, then the Bengali for a hen, then say bôṛo again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does বড় (bôṛo) mean? ("Big".) Say it once more.
