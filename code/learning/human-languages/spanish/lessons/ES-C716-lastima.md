## You'll want to know: la lástima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C716-LASTIMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la lástima** — "pity, shame".

> ¡Qué lástima que no vengas!
>
> *What a shame you are not coming!*

*¡Qué lástima!* — What a pity! *Qué lástima que* takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lástima* is built on *lastimar* "to hurt", which comes from a spoken Latin *blastemāre*, Latin *blasphēmāre* "to insult", from Greek *blasphēmía* (EtymDB). The sense moved from insulting to hurting to pity.
