## You'll want to know: il chiostro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C446-BORGO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il chiostro** — "the cloister".

> Nel chiostro c'è un pozzo antico.
>
> *In the cloister there is an old well.*

A square garden with arcades, inside a monastery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chiostro* is inherited from Latin *claustrum* 'bar, enclosure' (EtymDB), from *claudere* 'to shut'.
