## You'll want to know: લૂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C436-HEAT436-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લૂ** — *lū* — "a hot summer wind".

> મે મહિનામાં બપોરે ગરમ લૂ ફૂંકાય છે.
>
> *me mahināmā̃ bapore garam lū phū̃kāy che.*
>
> In May a hot wind blows in the afternoon.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લૂ** (*lū*) CDIAL lists Hindi *lūhar* and Sindhi *lūha*, "the hot summer wind", under *\*lūṣā*, "burning", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11099); it gives no Gujarati form, so the link is hedged.
