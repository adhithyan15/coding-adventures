Portuguese, chapter 164: Lawn, Path, Plain, Pebble, Earth.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
o relvado — a lawn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a castle, then the Portuguese for a tower.

You'll want to know: o relvado.
o relvado — "a lawn".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: o relvado]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: o relvado, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say o relvado]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a castle, then the Portuguese for a tower, then say o relvado again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o relvado mean? ("A lawn".) Say it once more.
