# సుంకం (suṅkaṁ) — a customs duty; a toll

## You'll want to know: సుంకం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C441-PASPORTU441-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సుంకం** — *suṅkaṁ* — "a customs duty; a toll".

> విదేశీ సరుకుల మీద సుంకం ఎక్కువ.
>
> *vidēśī sarukula mīda suṅkaṁ ekkuva.*
>
> The duty on foreign goods is high.

పన్ను is a tax in general; సుంకం is the duty paid on goods that cross a border or a toll gate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సుంకం** (*suṅkaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śulka*, "price, toll", in its Middle Indo-Aryan shape *suṅka* (CDIAL 12543); CDIAL notes that the Dravidian forms come from that spoken shape.
