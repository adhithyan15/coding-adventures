# ধুনুচি (dhunuchi) — an incense burner

## You'll want to know: ধুনুচি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C307-DHAK307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ধুনুচি** — *dhunuchi* — "an incense burner".

> পুজোর সন্ধ্যায় পুজারি ধুনুচি হাতে নিয়ে নাচলেন।
>
> *pujor sôndhyāy pujāri dhunuchi hāte niye nāchlen.*
>
> On the evening of the puja the priest danced with an incense burner in his hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধুনুচি** (*dhunuchi*) is built on ধুনো, the resin burned as incense — Sanskrit *dhūṇa*, 'gum, resin' (CDIAL 6838, which lists Bengali *dhunā*) — and is the clay cup it smoulders in.
