## You'll want to know: ふたご
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C476-KIN476-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふたご** — *futago* — "twins; a twin".

> **となりの こどもは ふたごです。** — *tonari no kodomo wa futago desu* — "The children next door are twins."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふたご** (*futago*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *futa*, "two" and *go*, "child (ko)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
