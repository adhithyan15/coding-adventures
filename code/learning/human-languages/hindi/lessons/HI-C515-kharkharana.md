# खड़खड़ाना (khaṛkhaṛānā) — to rattle

## You'll want to know: खड़खड़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C515-PENCH515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खड़खड़ाना** — *khaṛkhaṛānā* — "to rattle".

> हवा में पुरानी खिड़की खड़खड़ा रही है।
>
> *Havā meṁ purānī khiṛkī khaṛkhaṛā rahī hai.*
>
> The old window is rattling in the wind.

Past (it …): वह दरवाज़ा खड़खड़ाया, *vah darvāzā khaṛkhaṛāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खड़खड़ाना** (*khaṛkhaṛānā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *khaṭakhaṭāyatē*, "crackles", a sound word (CDIAL 3771). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to make a noise with".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
