7. Accept any independently written, recognizable Bengali-script place name,
   such as **কলকাতা** or **ঢাকা**. No single city is required.
8. Accept an independently written Bengali thanks expression, such as
   **ধন্যবাদ**. Do not credit a visible copy or romanization.

Apply the script-specific matra-line, vowel-sign-side, and hasanta criteria in
the rubric rather than scoring handwriting by its resemblance to a typeface.

## Speaking

1. Accept a relevant greeting such as **নমস্কার**.
2. Accept a chosen identity in a comprehensible frame such as **আমার নাম ...**.
3. Accept a comprehensible place response; do not require a particular city.
4. Accept a relevant yes/no answer about speaking Bengali.
5. Accept a relevant state such as **আমি ভালো আছি**.
6. Accept a comprehensible polite request for **চা**.

Examples guide raters; the learner need not reproduce a single fixed sentence.
