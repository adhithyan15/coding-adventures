## You'll want to know: o maestro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C516-ORQUESTRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o maestro** — "conductor".

> O maestro levantou os braços e a música começou.
>
> *The conductor raised his arms and the music began.*

Not a schoolteacher: in Portuguese, *maestro* is the conductor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maestro* was borrowed from Italian *maestro* "master" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *magister* "master, teacher".
