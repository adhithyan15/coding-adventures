## You'll want to know: प्रहेलिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C517-CATURANGA517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रहेलिका** — *prahelikā* — "a riddle".

> पितामहः प्रहेलिकां पृच्छति।
>
> *pitāmahaḥ prahelikāṃ pṛcchati.*
>
> Grandfather asks a riddle.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रहेलिका** (*prahelikā*) is the word MW records for a riddle, an enigma; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
