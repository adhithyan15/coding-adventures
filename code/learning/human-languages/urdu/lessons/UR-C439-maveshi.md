## You'll want to know: مویشی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C439-GIDAR439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مویشی** — *maveshī* — "livestock; cattle".

> کسان کے مویشی کھیت میں ہیں۔
>
> *kisān ke maveshī khet meṅ haiṅ.*
>
> The farmer's livestock are in the field.

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مویشی** (*maveshī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mawāshī*, "livestock, herds".
