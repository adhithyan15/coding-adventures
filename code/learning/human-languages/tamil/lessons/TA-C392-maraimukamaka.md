# மறைமுகமாக (maṟaimukamāka) — indirectly

## You'll want to know: மறைமுகமாக
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C392-ULAL392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**மறைமுகமாக** — *maṟaimukamāka* — "indirectly".

> அவர் தன் கோபத்தை மறைமுகமாகக் காட்டினார்.
>
> *avar taṉ kōpattai maṟaimukamākak kāṭṭiṉār.*
>
> He showed his anger indirectly.

Its opposite is நேரடியாக, directly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மறைமுகமாக** (*maṟaimukamāka*) is built from native *maṟai*, "to be hidden" (DEDR 4760), and *mukam*, "face", borrowed from Sanskrit *mukha* (CDIAL 10158), with -ஆக: with a hidden face.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
