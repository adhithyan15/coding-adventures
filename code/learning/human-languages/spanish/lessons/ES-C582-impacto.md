## You'll want to know: el impacto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C582-CONSEC-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el impacto** — "the impact".

> La noticia tuvo un gran impacto.
>
> *The news had a big impact.*

*Tener impacto en* — to have an impact on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impacto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *impactus* 'struck against' (EtymDB), the participle of *impingere*, built on *pangere* 'to fasten, to drive in'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
