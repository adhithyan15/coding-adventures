## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કઢી** (*kaḍhī*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *kvathita*, "boiled" (CDIAL 3637).
