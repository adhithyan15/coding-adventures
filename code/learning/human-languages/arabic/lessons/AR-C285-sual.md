# سؤال (suʾāl) — a question

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C285-OPINION-ANNA-01, AR-CONCEPT-C31-SAALA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think that", then "he asked". (*Aʿtaqidu anna*, *saʾala*.)

[PAUSE 1s] Before an opinion there is often a question. Here is the noun for
it.

## You'll want to know: سؤال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-02]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C105-LOC105-05, AR-LEX-C74-SCHOOL74-05] -->

**سؤال** — *suʾāl* — "a question".

> **عندي سؤال.** — *ʿindī suʾāl* — I have a question.

*ʿIndī*, "at me", is how Arabic says "I have". The answer to a question is
*jawāb*, from chapter seventy-four, and the two make a pair: *suʾāl wa-jawāb*,
question and answer.

Say it *su-ʾāl*, with the catch before the long *ā*: the same catch as in the
middle of *saʾala*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-W06-HAMZA-01] -->

**سؤال** (*suʾāl*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-a-l*, "to ask"
(Lane), the root of *saʾala*, "he asked". Lane gives *suʾāl* as "a
question", the counterpart of *jawāb*. The catch in the middle is the root's
own middle letter, a hamza. In *saʾala* it sits on **ا**. Here the vowel
before it is *u*, and it is written on a different seat, which the next
lesson puts in your hand.
