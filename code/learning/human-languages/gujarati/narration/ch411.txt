Gujarati, chapter 411: Quilts and Pillows.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રજાઈ (rajāī) — a quilt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to bail out, then the Gujarati for to wring out.

You'll want to know: રજાઈ.
રજાઈ — rajāī — "a quilt".
શિયાળામાં રાત્રે હું ભારે રજાઈ ઓઢું છું. śiyāḷāmā̃ rātre hũ bhāre rajāī oḍhũ chũ. On winter nights I wrap myself in a heavy quilt.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રજાઈ (rajāī) is usually traced to Persian razāī, "a quilt", a cover stuffed with cotton.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઓશીકું (ośīkũ) — a pillow; a cushion for the head.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to wring out, then the Gujarati for a quilt.

You'll want to know: ઓશીકું.
ઓશીકું — ośīkũ — "a pillow; a cushion for the head".
મને નરમ ઓશીકું ગમે છે. mane naram ośīkũ game che. I like a soft pillow.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઓશીકું (ośīkũ) goes back to Sanskrit ucchīrṣaka, "a pillow", from a form for raising the head; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 1853).

Lesson 3 of 5.
મચ્છરદાની (macchardānī) — a mosquito net.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a quilt, then the Gujarati for a cushion for the head.

You'll want to know: મચ્છરદાની.
મચ્છરદાની — macchardānī — "a mosquito net".
રાત્રે અમે પલંગ પર મચ્છરદાની બાંધીએ છીએ. rātre ame palaṅg par macchardānī bā̃dhīe chīe. At night we hang a mosquito net over the bed.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મચ્છરદાની (macchardānī) is built from મચ્છર, a mosquito, and -દાની, "a holder, a container", an ending usually traced to Persian -dān: a holder that keeps the mosquitoes out.

Lesson 4 of 5.
સંકેલવું (sankelvũ) — to roll up; to fold up (bedding).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a cushion for the head, then the Gujarati for a mosquito net.

You'll want to know: સંકેલવું.
સંકેલવું — sankelvũ — "to roll up; to fold up (bedding)".
સવારે ઉઠીને મેં પથારી સંકેલી. savāre uṭhīne mẽ pathārī sankelī. In the morning I got up and rolled up the bedding.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પથારી સંકેલી, teṇe pathārī sankelī.

The word, taken apart.
સંકેલવું (sankelvũ) may belong with Hindi sakilnā, "to gather up, to shrink", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit saṁkirati, "mixes together" (CDIAL 12823); it gives no Gujarati form, so the link is hedged.

Lesson 5 of 5.
સુવડાવવું (suvḍāvvũ) — to put to sleep (a child).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a mosquito net, then the Gujarati for to roll up.

You'll want to know: સુવડાવવું.
સુવડાવવું — suvḍāvvũ — "to put to sleep (a child)".
માતા બાળકને ગીત ગાઈને સુવડાવે છે. mātā bāḷakne gīt gāīne suvḍāve che. The mother sings the child to sleep.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને સુવડાવ્યું, teṇe bāḷakne suvḍāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
સુવડાવવું (suvḍāvvũ) is the causative partner of સૂવું, "to sleep, to lie down", a tadbhava of Sanskrit svápati, "sleeps" (CDIAL 13902): to make someone sleep.
