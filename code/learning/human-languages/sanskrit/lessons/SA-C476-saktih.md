## You'll want to know: शक्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C476-SAKTI476-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शक्तिः** — *śaktiḥ* — "strength".

> व्यायामेन शक्तिः वर्धते।
>
> *vyāyāmena śaktiḥ vardhate.*
>
> Exercise builds strength.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शक्तिः** (*śaktiḥ*) comes from the root √śak, "to be able", the root of **शक्नोति**; MW gives "power, ability, strength" (MW).
