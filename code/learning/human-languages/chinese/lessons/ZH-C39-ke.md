## You'll want to know: kě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C39-DRINK39-03]; assesses=[] -->

*kě* — "thirsty".

> *nǐ kě ma* — Are you thirsty?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kě* is inherited from Middle Chinese *khat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'hunger and thirst'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
