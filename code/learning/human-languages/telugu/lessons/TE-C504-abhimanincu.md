# అభిమానించు (abhimāniñcu) — to admire; to be fond of

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C503-PREMA503-05, TE-LEX-C504-ABHIMANAM504-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for fondness, then the Telugu for to adore.

## You'll want to know: అభిమానించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C504-ABHIMANAM504-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అభిమానించు** — *abhimāniñcu* — "to admire; to be fond of".

> ఆ డాక్టరును అందరూ అభిమానిస్తారు.
>
> *ā ḍākṭarunu andarū abhimānistāru.*
>
> Everyone admires that doctor.

Past (I …): అభిమానించాను, *abhimāniñcānu*.

The verb of అభిమానం, regard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అభిమానించు** (*abhimāniñcu*) is built on అభిమానం, from Sanskrit *abhimāna* (CDIAL 524), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
