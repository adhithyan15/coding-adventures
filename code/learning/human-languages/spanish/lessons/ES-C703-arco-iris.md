## You'll want to know: el arco iris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C703-ANOCHECER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el arco iris** — "the rainbow".

> Después de la lluvia salió un arco iris.
>
> *After the rain a rainbow came out.*

Also written *arcoíris*; the plural is *los arcoíris*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arco iris* is built from *arco* "bow", inherited from Latin *arcus* (EtymDB), and *iris* "rainbow", from Latin *īris* and Greek *îris* (EtymDB).
