# obēsus, obēsa, obēsum — fat, stout

## You'll want to know: obēsus, obēsa, obēsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C523-ROBU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**obēsus, obēsa, obēsum** — "fat, stout".

> Coquus obēsus multum edit.
>
> *The fat cook eats a lot.*

The past participle of *obedō* — to eat away; here, well fed. English *obese*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**obēsus** is the past participle of *obedō*, built on *ob-* and *edō* "to eat" (Lewis & Short). English *obese* comes from it.
