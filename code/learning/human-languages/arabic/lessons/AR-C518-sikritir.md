## You'll want to know: سكرتير
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C518-MIHNA518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سكرتير** — *sikritīr* — "a secretary".

> تعمل أختي سكرتيرة في شركة كبيرة.
>
> *taʿmalu ukhtī sikritīra fī sharika kabīra.*
>
> My sister works as a secretary in a big company.

The feminine is سكرتيرة.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سكرتير** (*sikritīr*) is a modern loan, usually traced to Italian *segretario* or French *secrétaire*, words that go back to Latin *secretum*, "a secret".
