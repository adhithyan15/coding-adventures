## You'll want to know: ਭੀੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C268-STAND268-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭੀੜ** — *bhīṛ* — "a crowd".

> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਅੱਡੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਬਹੁਤ ਭੀੜ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *shām nū̃ aḍḍe utte bahut bhīṛ hundī hai.*
>
> In the evening there is a big crowd at the stand.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭੀੜ** (*bhīṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*bhiṭ*, "meet, throng"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bhīṛ*, "crowd", under it (CDIAL 9490).
