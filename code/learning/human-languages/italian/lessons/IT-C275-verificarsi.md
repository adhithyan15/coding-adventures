## You'll want to know: verificarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C275-ACCADERE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**verificarsi** — "to occur, to take place (formal)".

> Ieri sera si è verificato un incidente in centro.
>
> *Last night an accident occurred in the centre.*

A news word. Without *-si*, *verificare* means ‘to check’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verificarsi* comes from *verificare* "to check, to show true", usually traced to Medieval Latin *vērificāre*, built on *vērus* "true" and *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short).
