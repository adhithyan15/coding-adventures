## You'll want to know: 前因后果
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C540-NOWONDER540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**前因后果** — *qiányīn hòuguǒ* — "the whole chain of causes and effects".

> *yàoshi nǐ zhīdào qiányīn hòuguǒ jiù bú huì guài tā le* — If you knew the whole story, you wouldn't blame him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**前因后果** (*qiányīn hòuguǒ*) is built from 前 'before', 因 'a cause', 后 'after' and 果 'fruit', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzen*, *'jin*, *huwX* and *kwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the cause that came before and the fruit that came after.
