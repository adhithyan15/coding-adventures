## You'll want to know: zérèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C297-FAULT297-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zérèn* — "responsibility; blame".

> *shìgù fāshēng yǐhòu, sījī shuō shì tā de zérèn* — After the accident, the driver said it was his fault.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zérèn* is built from *zé* 'to demand' and *rèn* 'to bear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsreak* and *nyim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to seek' and the second 'able to bear', and lists the second also in the departing tone, the source of rèn.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
