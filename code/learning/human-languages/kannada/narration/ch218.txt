Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಿದ್ಧ (siddha) — ready.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for loose, then the Kannada for thick.

You'll want to know: ಸಿದ್ಧ.
ಸಿದ್ಧ — siddha — "ready".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಿದ್ಧ (siddha) is Sanskrit siddha, "accomplished; prepared", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13401).
