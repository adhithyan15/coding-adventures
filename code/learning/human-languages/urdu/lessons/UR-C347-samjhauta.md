## You'll want to know: سمجھوتا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C347-MUAMALA347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سمجھوتا** — *samjhautā* — "a compromise; a settlement".

> دونوں فریقوں میں سمجھوتا ہو گیا۔
>
> *donoṅ farīqoṅ meṅ samjhautā ho gayā.*
>
> The two sides reached a compromise.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سمجھوتا** (*samjhautā*) is a noun made on **سمجھنا**, "to understand", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *samajhnā* under Sanskrit *saṃbudhyate* (CDIAL 12959). An understanding both sides reach.
