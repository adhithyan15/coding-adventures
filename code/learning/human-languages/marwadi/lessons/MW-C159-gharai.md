# घरै — at home

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C82-PLACE82-01, MW-LEX-C82-PLACE82-02, MW-LEX-C96-LOC96-05, MW-SCRIPT-GHAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *here*, *there* and *on top*: **अठै**, **बठै**, **माथै**. Then *a
house*: **घर**.

## Grammar Lens: घर with -ै
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GHARAI-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-GHAR-01, MW-LEX-C82-PLACE82-01] -->

**घर** with the **-ै** of **अठै**, **बठै** and **माथै** is **घरै**: at home, or home
as the place you go to.

> **हूं घरै हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ gharai hū̃.* — I am at home.

> **वो रोज आथण घरै आवै है।**
>
> *vo roj āthaṇ gharai āvai hai.* — He comes home every evening.

Old Marwari shows a place ending like it: *āgai*, "before", beside *āgo*, "front"
(CDIAL 68).

- [YOU SAY: **मां घरै है।** — *mā̃ gharai hai.* — Mother is at home.]
- [YOU SAY: **आप रोज घरै जावो हो?** — *āp roj gharai jāvo ho?* — Do you go home every day?]
- [YOU SAY: **हां सा, हूं रोज घरै जाऊं हूं।** — *hā̃ sā, hū̃ roj gharai jāū̃ hū̃.* — Yes, I go home every day.]
