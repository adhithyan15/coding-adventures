## You'll want to know: حطام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C354-HUTAM354-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حطام** — *ḥuṭām* — "wreckage, debris".

> بحث الرجال في حطام السيارة.
>
> *baḥatha ar-rijāl fī ḥuṭām as-sayyāra.*
>
> The men searched the wreckage of the car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حطام** (*ḥuṭām*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ṭ-m* (Lane), "to break a dry thing in pieces"; *ḥuṭām* is what is left in pieces.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
