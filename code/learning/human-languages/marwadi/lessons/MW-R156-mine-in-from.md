# Mine, in and from — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSS-AGREE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *my cow is here*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSS-AGREE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-LOC-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *my house*; *my cow*; *your roti*; *your milk*]
- [YOU SAY: *the milk is in the kitchen*; *the book is on the table*]
- [YOU SAY: *the shop is near the house*]
- [YOU SAY: *where do you come from?*; *I come from the market*]
- [YOU SAY: *he comes from the shop every day*]
