## You'll want to know: حوالہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C338-MISAL338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حوالہ** — *havālā* — "a reference (to a source)".

> اپنی بات کا حوالہ دیجیے۔
>
> *apnī bāt kā havālā dījiye.*
>
> Give a source for your point, please.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حوالہ** (*havālā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥawāla*, "a handing over"; Urdu uses it for pointing a reader on to a source, a sense our sources do not trace step by step.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
