## You'll want to know: embaciar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C403-VIDROS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**embaciar** — "to mist up, to steam up".

> Com o frio, os vidros embaciam por dentro.
>
> *In the cold, the windows mist up on the inside.*

*Os vidros estão embaciados* — the windows are misted up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*embaciar* is built from *em-* and *baço* 'dull, without shine'. *Baço* is inherited from Latin *badius* 'reddish-brown' (EtymDB).

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
