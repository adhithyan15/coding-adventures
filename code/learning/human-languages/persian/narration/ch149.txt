Persian, chapter 149: To waste, To economise, To divide, To compare, To expect.

Lesson 1 of 5.
هدر دادن (hadar dâdan) — to waste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get a flat tyre, then the Persian for to get stuck.

You'll want to know: هدر دادن.
هدر دادن — hadar dâdan — "to waste".

The word, taken apart.
hadar dâdan pairs hadar, borrowed from Arabic hadar, "something lost in vain" (root h-d-r, Lane), with the Persian verb dâdan, "to give". dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".

صرفه جویی کردن (sarfejuyi kardan) — to economise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get stuck, then the Persian for to waste.

You'll want to know: صرفه جویی کردن.
صرفه جویی کردن — sarfejuyi kardan — "to economise".

The word, taken apart.
sarfejuyi kardan pairs sarfejuyi, "thrift", with kardan. It is sarfe, "saving, profit", with -ju, "seeking", and -i. sarfe is usually traced to Arabic ṣarf, "spending, turning" (root ṣ-r-f, Lane); -ju is native. kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
