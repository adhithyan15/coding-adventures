Portuguese, chapter 313: Casamentos e separações.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a lua de mel — the honeymoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to propose, then the Portuguese for matrimony.

You'll want to know: a lua de mel.
a lua de mel — "the honeymoon".
Passaram a lua de mel numa ilha. They spent their honeymoon on an island.
Written without hyphens since the 1990 spelling reform.

The word, taken apart.
lua de mel joins lua "moon", from Latin lūna, and mel "honey", from Latin mel (EtymDB): the sweet first month.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: as bodas.
as bodas — "the wedding anniversary celebration; the wedding feast".
Os meus avós fizeram as bodas de ouro no ano passado. My grandparents celebrated their golden wedding last year.
Bodas de prata — 25 years; bodas de ouro — 50 years.

The word, taken apart.
bodas goes back to Latin vōta "vows", the plural of vōtum "a vow" (EtymDB): the promises made at a wedding.

Lesson 3 of 5.
o divórcio — the divorce.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the honeymoon, then the Portuguese for the wedding anniversary celebration.

You'll want to know: o divórcio.
o divórcio — "the divorce".
Depois do divórcio, ele mudou de cidade. After the divorce he moved to another city.
Pedir o divórcio — to ask for a divorce.

The word, taken apart.
divórcio is a learned borrowing from Latin dīvortium "a separation, a divorce" (EtymDB), from dīvertere "to turn aside".

Lesson 4 of 5.
a separação — the separation, the break-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the wedding anniversary celebration, then the Portuguese for the divorce.

You'll want to know: a separação.
a separação — "the separation, the break-up".
A separação foi difícil para os filhos. The separation was hard on the children.
Also the sorting of rubbish: a separação do lixo.

The word, taken apart.
separação was borrowed from Latin sēparātiō "a severing" (EtymDB), from sēparāre "to separate", built on parāre "to make ready".

Lesson 5 of 5.
a mudança — the move (to a new home); the change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the divorce, then the Portuguese for the separation.

You'll want to know: a mudança.
a mudança — "the move (to a new home); the change".
A mudança para o novo apartamento demorou dois dias. The move to the new flat took two days.
Fazer a mudança — to move house. In a car, a mudança is the gear.

The word, taken apart.
mudança is built on mudar "to change, to move", from Latin mūtāre "to change" (Lewis & Short).
