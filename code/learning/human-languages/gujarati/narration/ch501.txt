Lesson 1 of 5.
લાકડું (lākḍũ) — wood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pester, then the Gujarati for to hope.

You'll want to know: લાકડું.
લાકડું — lākḍũ — "wood".
આ ખુરશી જૂના લાકડાની છે. ā khurśī jūnā lākḍānī che. This chair is made of old wood.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
લાકડું (lākḍũ) goes back to Sanskrit lakuṭa, "a club, a stick", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with the sense "stick" (CDIAL 10875). In everyday Gujarati it names wood itself, the stuff a chair or a door is made of.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઓજાર (ojār) — a tool of a trade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wood, then the Gujarati for a craftsman.

You'll want to know: ઓજાર.
ઓજાર — ojār — "a tool of a trade".
સુથાર પોતાનાં ઓજારો એક પેટીમાં રાખે છે. suthār potānā̃ ojāro ek peṭīmā̃ rākhe che. The carpenter keeps his tools in a box.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઓજાર (ojār) is usually traced to Arabic awzār, a plural meaning "loads" or "gear", which reached Gujarati through Persian. The plural is ઓજારો.

Lesson 4 of 5.
આવડવું (āvaḍvũ) — to know how (to do something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a craftsman, then the Gujarati for a tool of a trade.

You'll want to know: આવડવું.
આવડવું — āvaḍvũ — "to know how (to do something)".
મીરાને લાકડાનું કામ આવડે છે. Mīrāne lākḍānũ kām āvḍe che. Mira knows how to do woodwork.
Past (it …): તે કામ તેને આવડી ગયું, te kām tene āvḍī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
આવડવું (āvaḍvũ) takes the person who has the skill with ને, and the skill itself is the subject: મને તરતાં આવડે છે, I know how to swim. CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to come to someone's knowledge", under Sanskrit āpatati, "flies towards; befalls" (CDIAL 1193); the step to knowing a skill is not explained there.

Lesson 5 of 5.
વ્યવસાય (vyavsāy) — a profession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a tool of a trade, then the Gujarati for to know how.

You'll want to know: વ્યવસાય.
વ્યવસાય — vyavsāy — "a profession".
મારા કાકાનો વ્યવસાય સુથારનો છે. mārā kākāno vyavsāy suthārno che. My uncle is a carpenter by profession.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વ્યવસાય (vyavsāy) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vyavasāya, "strenuous effort; resolve", which Monier-Williams also gives as "business, trade" (Monier-Williams).
