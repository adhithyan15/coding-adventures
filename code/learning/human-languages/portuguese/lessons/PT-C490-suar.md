## You'll want to know: suar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C490-FORMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**suar** — "to sweat".

> No ginásio suo muito.
>
> *I sweat a lot at the gym.*

Sweat is *o suor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suar* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Latin *sūdāre* "to sweat" (EtymDB).
