## You'll want to know: истина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C345-SPRAVEDLIVO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**истина** (*ístina*) — "the truth (in a deep sense)".

> Он думает, что знает истину.
>
> *On dúmayet, chto znáyet ístinu.*
>
> He thinks he knows the truth.

Feminine. More solemn than *правда*: a truth in philosophy or science.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**истина** is built on the old adjective *истый* "true, genuine"; EtymDB sets Latvian *īstens* "real, true" beside it as a cousin (EtymDB).
