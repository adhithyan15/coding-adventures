# పరిశోధన (pariśōdhana) — research

## You'll want to know: పరిశోధన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C412-PARISODHANA412-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిశోధన** — *pariśōdhana* — "research".

> ఆమె తెలుగు పాటల మీద పరిశోధన చేస్తోంది.
>
> *āme telugu pāṭala mīda pariśōdhana cēstōndi.*
>
> She is doing research on Telugu songs.

పరిశోధన చేయు, to do research.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిశోధన** (*pariśōdhana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pariśodhana*, "cleaning, purification" (Monier-Williams); in Telugu it is going through a subject thoroughly, research.
