Russian, chapter 284: Interviews and comments.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
интервью (intervyú) — an interview.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to find out, then the Russian for to warn.

You'll want to know: интервью.
интервью (intervyú) — "an interview".
Певица дала интервью газете. Pevítsa dalá intervyú gazéte. The singer gave an interview to a newspaper.
Neuter and indeclinable: в интервью (intervyú). Брать интервью (intervyú) у + genitive — to interview someone.

The word, taken apart.
интервью (intervyú) was borrowed from English interview (EtymDB), which comes from French entrevue "a meeting", from the reflexive verb entrevoir "to see each other".

Lesson 2 of 5.
опрос (oprós) — a survey, a poll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to warn, then the Russian for an interview.

You'll want to know: опрос.
опрос (oprós) — "a survey, a poll".
После опроса все узнали мнение студентов. Pósle oprósa vse uználi mnéniye studéntov. After the survey everyone learned what the students thought.
Опрос общественного мнения — an opinion poll.

The word, taken apart.
опрос (oprós) is built on о- and просить "to ask", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic prositi, from PIE preḱ- "to ask" (EtymDB): asking round.

Lesson 3 of 5.
редактор (redáktor) — an editor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an interview, then the Russian for a survey.

You'll want to know: редактор.
редактор (redáktor) — "an editor".
Редактор прочитал статью и улыбнулся. Redáktor prochitál statyú i ulybnúlsya. The editor read the article and smiled.
Главный редактор (redáktor) — the editor-in-chief.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB derives редактор (redáktor) from French rédacteur "editor", given a Latin-style ending -ор; the French word is built on Latin redāctus, the participle of redigere "to drive back, to bring back" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
комментировать (kommentírovat) — to comment on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a survey, then the Russian for an editor.

You'll want to know: комментировать.
комментировать (kommentírovat) — "to comment on".
Тренер не стал комментировать игру. Tréner ne stal kommentírovat igrú. The coach would not comment on the game.
Imperfective; perfective прокомментировать (prokommentírovat). It takes the accusative, with no preposition.

The word, taken apart.
комментировать (kommentírovat) is usually traced through German kommentieren to Latin commentārī "to think over, to write about" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent for the Russian word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
цитировать (tsitírovat) — to quote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an editor, then the Russian for to comment on.

You'll want to know: цитировать.
цитировать (tsitírovat) — "to quote".
Учитель любил цитировать стихи. Uchítel lyubíl tsitírovat stikhí. The teacher liked to quote poems.
Imperfective; perfective процитировать (protsitírovat). Цитата — a quotation.

The word, taken apart.
цитировать (tsitírovat) was borrowed from German zitieren with the Russian ending -овать (EtymDB); the German verb comes from Latin citāre "to set in motion, to summon" (Lewis & Short).
