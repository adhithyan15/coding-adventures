Malayalam, chapter 544: Starting a Venture.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഓവർടൈം (ōvarṭaiṁ) — overtime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to dismiss, then the Malayalam for an employer.

You'll want to know: ഓവർടൈം.
ഓവർടൈം — ōvarṭaiṁ — "overtime".
പണി തീർക്കാൻ അവൻ ഇന്നലെ ഓവർടൈം ചെയ്തു. paṇi tīrkkān avan innale ōvarṭaiṁ ceytu. He worked overtime yesterday to finish the job.
ഓവർടൈം (ōvarṭaiṁ) ചെയ്യുക, to work overtime.

The word, taken apart.
ഓവർടൈം (ōvarṭaiṁ) was borrowed from English overtime.

Lesson 2 of 5.
സംരംഭം (saṁrambhaṁ) — a venture; an enterprise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an employer, then the Malayalam for overtime.

You'll want to know: സംരംഭം.
സംരംഭം — saṁrambhaṁ — "a venture; an enterprise".
അവൾ ഒരു ചെറിയ ബേക്കറി സംരംഭം തുടങ്ങി. avaḷ oru ceṟiya bēkkaṟi saṁrambhaṁ tuṭaṅṅi. She started a small bakery venture.
A word of business news; സംരംഭം (saṁrambhaṁ) തുടങ്ങുക, to start a venture.

The word, taken apart.
സംരംഭം (saṁrambhaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṁrambha, which CDIAL glosses as vehemence (CDIAL 12993); Malayalam uses it for an undertaking pursued with zeal.

Lesson 3 of 5.
സംരംഭകൻ (saṁrambhakan) — an entrepreneur.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for overtime, then the Malayalam for a venture.

You'll want to know: സംരംഭകൻ.
സംരംഭകൻ — saṁrambhakan — "an entrepreneur".
ആ സംരംഭകൻ ഗ്രാമത്തിലെ പത്ത് പേർക്ക് ജോലി കൊടുത്തു. ā saṁrambhakan grāmattile pattŭ pērkkŭ jōli koṭuttu. That entrepreneur gave jobs to ten people in the village.
A woman is സംരംഭക. -അകൻ marks the one who does something.

The word, taken apart.
സംരംഭകൻ (saṁrambhakan) is built on സംരംഭം (saṁrambhaṁ), from Sanskrit saṁrambha (CDIAL 12993), with the agent ending -അകൻ.

Lesson 5 of 5.
മുടങ്ങുക (muṭaṅṅuka) — to be interrupted; to stop midway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an entrepreneur, then the Malayalam for a work shift.

You'll want to know: മുടങ്ങുക.
മുടങ്ങുക — muṭaṅṅuka — "to be interrupted; to stop midway".
മഴ കാരണം പണി രണ്ട് ദിവസം മുടങ്ങി. maḻa kāraṇaṁ paṇi raṇṭŭ divasaṁ muṭaṅṅi. Because of the rain, work stopped for two days.
Past: മുടങ്ങി, muṭaṅṅi.
മുടക്കുക is to stop something; മുടക്കം is an interruption.

The word, taken apart.
മുടങ്ങുക (muṭaṅṅuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4933 gives it as to be hindered or obstructed (DEDR 4933).
