## You'll want to know: छिद्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C410-CHIDRA410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**छिद्रम्** — *chidram* — "a hole".

> मम स्यूते छिद्रम् अस्ति।
>
> *mama syūte chidram asti.*
>
> There is a hole in my bag.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छिद्रम्** (*chidram*) comes from the root √chid, "to cut, to split", which also gives *chinatti*, "cuts"; MW gives *chidra* as torn, pierced and, as a noun, a hole (MW).
