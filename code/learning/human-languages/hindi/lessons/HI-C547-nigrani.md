## You'll want to know: निगरानी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C547-CHAUKANNA547-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निगरानी** — *nigrānī* — "watch; supervision".

> पुलिस रात भर गली की निगरानी करती है।
>
> *Pulis rāt bhar galī kī nigrānī kartī hai.*
>
> The police keep watch on the lane all night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निगरानी** (*nigrānī*) is usually traced to Persian *nigarānī*, "watching over".
