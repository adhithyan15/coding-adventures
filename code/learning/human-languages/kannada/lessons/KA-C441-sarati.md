## You'll want to know: ಸರತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C441-SHULKA441-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರತಿ** — *sarati* — "a turn in line; a queue".

> ಬಿಲ್ ಕಟ್ಟಲು ನಾವು ಸರತಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಂತೆವು.
>
> *bil kaṭṭalu nāvu saratiyalli nintevu.*
>
> We stood in the queue to pay the bill.

ಸರತಿ ಸಾಲು, a queue; ನನ್ನ ಸರತಿ, my turn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರತಿ** (*sarati*) is a Kannada word for a turn in a line whose origin is not settled.
