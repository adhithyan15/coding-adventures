## You'll want to know: अश्वशाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C408-VAHA408-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अश्वशाला** — *aśvaśālā* — "a stable".

> रात्रौ अश्वः अश्वशालायां तिष्ठति।
>
> *rātrau aśvaḥ aśvaśālāyāṃ tiṣṭhati.*
>
> At night the horse stands in the stable.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अश्वशाला** (*aśvaśālā*) is built from *aśva*, "a horse", and *śālā*, "a hall, a house"; MW gives the compound as a stable for horses (MW).
