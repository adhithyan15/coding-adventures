German, chapter 368: Stattdessen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
stattdessen — instead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for after all, then the German for perhaps even.

You'll want to know: stattdessen.
stattdessen — "instead".
Der Film war ausverkauft. Stattdessen sind wir essen gegangen. The film was sold out. Instead, we went out to eat.
The verb comes right after it: stattdessen sind wir ….

The word, taken apart.
stattdessen joins statt "instead of", from Statt "place", Old High German stat, to dessen "of that": in the place of that. EtymDB has no entry for the compound.

Lesson 2 of 5.
anstatt — instead of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for perhaps even, then the German for instead.

You'll want to know: anstatt.
anstatt — "instead of".
Anstatt zu lernen, hat er den ganzen Abend gespielt. Instead of studying, he played all evening.
With zu + infinitive, as here, or with the genitive: anstatt eines Briefes. Also anstatt dass …, with the verb at the end.

The word, taken apart.
anstatt joins an "at, in" to Statt "place", Old High German stat: in the place of something else. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ohne dass — without (someone) doing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for instead, then the German for instead of.

You'll want to know: ohne dass.
ohne dass — "without (someone) doing".
Er ist gegangen, ohne dass jemand es gemerkt hat. He left without anyone noticing.
Ohne dass sends the verb to the end. When the subject stays the same, German says ohne … zu: Er ging, ohne etwas zu sagen.

The word, taken apart.
ohne dass joins ohne "without", from Proto-Germanic ēnu (EtymDB), a cousin of Greek áneu "without", to dass "that".

Lesson 4 of 5.
verzichten — to do without, to give up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for instead of, then the German for without.

You'll want to know: verzichten.
verzichten — "to do without, to give up".
Im Urlaub verzichte ich auf mein Handy. On holiday I do without my mobile.
Auf etwas verzichten — to do without something. Inseparable: sie hat verzichtet.

The word, taken apart.
verzichten goes back to Middle High German verzihen "to renounce, to deny". EtymDB does not give a usable history, so this follows the usual account.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ersetzen — to replace; to make up for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for without, then the German for to do without.

You'll want to know: ersetzen.
ersetzen — "to replace; to make up for".
Die alte Lehrerin wird von einem jungen Kollegen ersetzt. The old teacher is being replaced by a young colleague.
Inseparable: er ersetzt, er hat ersetzt. Den Schaden ersetzen — to pay for the damage.

The word, taken apart.
ersetzen is inherited from Middle High German ersetzen (EtymDB), built with er- on setzen "to set", Proto-Germanic satjaną: you set something new in the old place.
