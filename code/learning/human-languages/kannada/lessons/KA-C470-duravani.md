# ದೂರವಾಣಿ (dūravāṇi) — a telephone

## You'll want to know: ದೂರವಾಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C470-KAMAGARI470-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೂರವಾಣಿ** — *dūravāṇi* — "a telephone".

> ಕರೆಂಟ್ ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೂ ದೂರವಾಣಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿತ್ತು.
>
> *kareṇṭ illadiddarū dūravāṇi kelasa māḍuttittu.*
>
> Even without power the telephone was working.

ದೂರ, far, plus ವಾಣಿ, voice: a coined word; in speech people say ಫೋನ್.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೂರವಾಣಿ** (*dūravāṇi*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: *dūra*, "distant" (CDIAL 6495), and *vāṇī*, "voice, sound" (CDIAL 11490).
