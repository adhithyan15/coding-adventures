## You'll want to know: मूढः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C336-NITI336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मूढः** — *mūḍhaḥ* — "bewildered; foolish".

> मूढः मकरः वानरं विश्वसिति।
>
> *mūḍhaḥ makaraḥ vānaraṃ viśvasiti.*
>
> The foolish crocodile trusts the monkey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूढः** (*mūḍhaḥ*) comes from the root √muh, "to be confused, go astray"; MW gives *mūḍha* as stupefied, bewildered, perplexed, confused (MW).
