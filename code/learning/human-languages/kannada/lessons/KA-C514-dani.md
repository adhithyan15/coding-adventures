## You'll want to know: ದಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C514-PATHYA514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಣಿ** — *daṇi* — "to get tired".

> ಇಡೀ ದಿನ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡಿ ಅವನು ದಣಿದನು.
>
> *iḍī dina kelasa māḍi avanu daṇidanu.*
>
> He got tired after working all day.

Past (I …): ದಣಿದೆ, *daṇide*.

ದಣಿವು, tiredness; ದಣಿವಾರಿಸಿಕೊ, to rest a while.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಣಿ** (*daṇi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3045 lists Kannada *daṇi*, *daṇivu*, beside *taṇi*, "to grow cool, get feeble or fatigued".
