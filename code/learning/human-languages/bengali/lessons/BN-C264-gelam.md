# আমি গেলাম (āmi gelām) — the past, person by person

## You'll want to know: the past, person by person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C03-AMI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TUMIAPNI-01, BN-LEX-C23-SHE-01, BN-LEX-C23-TINI-01, BN-LEX-C24-AMRA-01, BN-LEX-C24-TARA-01, BN-LEX-C07-JAOWA-01, BN-LEX-C84-TOWN84-03] -->

> **কাল আমি বাজারে গেলাম।**
>
> *kāl āmi bājāre gelām.* — "Yesterday I went to the market."

With a past, **কাল** is yesterday. **গেলাম** is **যাওয়া** in the past: the stem **গে-**, then **-ল-**, then the person.

- **আমি** / **আমরা** **গেলাম** (*gelām*) — I went, we went
- **তুমি** / **তোমরা** **গেলে** (*gele*) — you went, to a friend or to several
- **সে** / **তারা** **গেল** (*gelô*) — he or she went; they went
- **আপনি** / **তিনি** **গেলেন** (*gelen*) — you went, he or she went, with respect

They are the present's four slots: **যাই**, **যাও**, **যান**, and one not written before, **সে যায়** (*she jāy*), "he or she goes": **-য়** after a vowel, **-এ** after a consonant, **সে বসে**.

## You'll want to know: the past stems
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C07-ASHA-01, BN-LEX-C07-KHAOWA-01, BN-LEX-C09-NEOWA-01, BN-LEX-C07-HOWA-01, BN-LEX-C51-VERBS51-03, BN-LEX-C55-VERBS55-03, BN-LEX-C106-ACT106-04, BN-LEX-C49-VERBS49-02, BN-LEX-C07-DEKHA-01] -->

Most verbs put **-ল-** on the stem: **বসা** → **বসলাম** (*bôslām*), **দেখা** → **দেখলাম** (*dekhlām*), with a plain *e* for the *æ* of **দেখা**. An **এ** said *e* rises to **ি**, an **ও** to **ু**: **কেনা** → **কিনলাম** (*kinlām*), **ফেরা** → **ফিরলাম** (*phirlām*), **শোনা** → **শুনলাম** (*shunlām*).

Eight verbs change the stem:

- **যাওয়া** → **গেলাম** (*gelām*) — went
- **আসা** → **এলাম** (*elām*), **সে এল** (*she elô*) — came
- **খাওয়া** → **খেলাম** (*khelām*) — ate, drank
- **নেওয়া** → **নিলাম** (*nilām*) — took
- **দেওয়া** (of **জল দেওয়া**) → **দিলাম** (*dilām*) — gave
- **হওয়া** → **হলাম** (*hôlām*); **কী হল?** (*kī hôlô?*) — what happened?
- **চাওয়া** → **চাইলাম** (*chāilām*), **সে চাইল** (*she chāilô*) — wanted, asked for
- **পাওয়া** → **পেলাম** (*pelām*) — got, found
