Urdu, chapter 351: A Study.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مطالعہ (mutāliā) — a study (a piece of research); close reading.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a rate, then the Urdu for to bring out.

You'll want to know: مطالعہ.
مطالعہ — mutāliā — "a study (a piece of research); close reading".
یہ مطالعہ بچوں کی نیند پر ہے۔ yih mutāliā bachchoṅ kī nīṅd par hai. This study is about children's sleep.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مطالعہ (mutāliā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muṭālaʿa, "perusal, close reading".

Lesson 4 of 5.
موازنہ کرنا (muvāzanā karnā) — to compare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for according to, then the Urdu for a sample.

You'll want to know: موازنہ کرنا.
موازنہ کرنا — muvāzanā karnā — "to compare".
میں نے دونوں قیمتوں کا موازنہ کیا۔ maiṅ ne donoṅ qīmatoṅ kā muvāzanā kiyā. I compared the two prices.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دونوں کا موازنہ کیا, us ne donoṅ kā muvāzanā kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
موازنہ کرنا (muvāzanā karnā) is built from muvāzanā, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muwāzana, "weighing one thing against another", and کرنا, "to do".

The word, taken apart.
