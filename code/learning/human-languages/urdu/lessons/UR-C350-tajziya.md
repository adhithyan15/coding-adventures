## You'll want to know: تجزیہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C350-AUSAT350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تجزیہ** — *tajziyā* — "an analysis".

> ماہر نے ان اعداد کا تجزیہ کیا۔
>
> *māhir ne in adād kā tajziyā kiyā.*
>
> The expert analysed these figures.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تجزیہ** (*tajziyā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tajziya*, "dividing into parts".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
