# त्याने काम केलं (tyāne kām kelaṁ) — the past that agrees with the thing

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, MR-POSTP-NE-INSTR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I went" and "she came". (*mī gelo*, *tī ālī*.) Then "I see with my eye", with the **-ने** of a tool. (*mī ḍoḷyāne pāhto*.)

[PAUSE 1s] That **-ने** was promised a second job. This is it.

## Grammar Lens: the doer takes -ने
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-TO-TI-TE, MR-LEX-KARNE] -->

> **त्याने काम केलं.**
>
> *tyāne kām kelaṁ.* — "He did the work."

A verb with an object, such as eating, reading or doing, changes the past. The doer is marked: **तो** becomes **त्याने** (*tyāne*), **ती** becomes **तिने** (*tine*), and **ते**, "they", becomes **त्यांनी** (*tyānnī*). A name takes **-ने** too: **मीराने** (*mīrāne*).

**मी**, **तू**, **आम्ही** and **तुम्ही** take nothing: **मी काम केलं** (*mī kām kelaṁ*), "I did the work".

## You'll want to know: the verb follows the thing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C89-FOOD89-02, MR-LEX-C136-THINGS136-03, MR-LEX-C93-HOME93-03, MR-LEX-KHANE, MR-LEX-VACHNE, MR-LEX-PAHNE, MR-LEX-C275-VASTU275-03] -->

> **मी आंबा खाल्ला.**
>
> *mī āmbā khāllā.* — "I ate a mango."

**आंबा** is masculine, so the verb ends in **-ा**, whoever ate it. Change the thing and the verb changes:

- **मी पोळी** **खाल्ली** (*khāllī*) — I ate a chapati (feminine)
- **मी पुस्तक** **वाचलं** (*vāclaṁ*) — I read a book (neuter)
- **तिने चित्रपट** **पाहिला** (*pāhilā*) — she watched a film

After **तू** the verb adds **-स**: **तू पुस्तक वाचलंस** (*tū pustak vāclaṁs*); after **तुम्ही**, **-त**: **तुम्ही काय केलंत?** (*tumhī kāy kelant?*), "What did you do?"

Pasts to learn: **केलं**, **खाल्लं**, **घेतलं** (*ghetlaṁ*), **दिलं** (*dilaṁ*), **सांगितलं** (*sāṅgitlaṁ*), and from **पिणे**, **प्यायलं** (*pyāylaṁ*). With no object, **-ं**: **त्याने सांगितलं** (*tyāne sāṅgitlaṁ*), "he said so". Formal print writes this **-ं** as **-े** (**केले**). A few verbs with an object still follow the doer: **मी मराठी शिकलो** (*mī marāṭhī śiklo*).
