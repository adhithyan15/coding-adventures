## You'll want to know: ٹپکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C404-GHUSL404-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹپکنا** — *ṭapaknā* — "to drip".

> غسل خانے کا نل ٹپکتا ہے۔
>
> *ghusl khāne kā nal ṭapaktā hai.*
>
> The tap in the washroom drips.

Past (it …): وہ نل رات بھر ٹپکا, *voh nal rāt bhar ṭapkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹپکنا** (*ṭapaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭapaknā*, "to drip", under *ṭapp*, "drip, drop", an imitative form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5444).
