German, chapter 502: Angeln und fotografieren.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
angeln — to fish (with a rod).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to cheat, then the German for round.

You'll want to know: angeln.
angeln — "to fish (with a rod)".
Am Samstag angelt mein Vater am See. On Saturday my father goes fishing at the lake.
Angeln gehen — to go fishing. Die Angel — fishing rod.

The word, taken apart.
angeln is built on Angel "fishing rod", inherited from Middle High German angel and Old High German angul "fish hook", from Proto-Germanic angulō (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
anbeißen — to bite (of a fish); to take a bite of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to fish, then the German for bait.

You'll want to know: anbeißen.
anbeißen — "to bite (of a fish); to take a bite of".
Zwei Stunden lang hat kein Fisch angebissen. For two hours not a single fish bit.
Separable: ein Fisch beißt an. Also to bite into: einen Apfel anbeißen.

The word, taken apart.
anbeißen is built on an "on" and beißen "to bite", inherited from Old High German bīzan, from Proto-Germanic bītaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Objektiv — lens (of a camera).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for bait, then the German for to bite.

You'll want to know: das Objektiv.
das Objektiv — "lens (of a camera)".
Für Fotos von Vögeln braucht man ein gutes Objektiv. For photos of birds you need a good lens.
Plural: die Objektive. Not the adjective objektiv — objective, fair.

The word, taken apart.
Objektiv is a learned word built on Latin obiectus "set in front", from obicere "to throw in front", built on iacere "to throw" (Lewis & Short): the lens faces the object.

Lesson 5 of 5.
knipsen — to snap (a photo).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to bite, then the German for lens.

You'll want to know: knipsen.
knipsen — "to snap (a photo)".
Im Urlaub knipst sie jeden Tag Hunderte Fotos. On holiday she snaps hundreds of photos every day.
Informal for fotografieren. Also to punch a ticket.

The word, taken apart.
knipsen is usually explained as an imitative word for a short click; EtymDB sets it beside Dutch knippen "to snip".
