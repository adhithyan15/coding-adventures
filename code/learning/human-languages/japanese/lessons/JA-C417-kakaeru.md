## You'll want to know: かかえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C417-TENIMOTSU417-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かかえる** — *kakaeru* — "to carry in one's arms".

> **はこを かかえて、かいだんを のぼりました。** — *hako o kakaete, kaidan o noborimashita* — "I carried a box up the stairs in my arms."

Polite **かかえます**, *kakaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが はこを かかえました。**, *chichi ga hako o kakaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かかえる** (*kakaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
