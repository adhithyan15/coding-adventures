## You'll want to know: 正好
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C149-ZHENGHAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**正好** — *zhènghǎo* — "exactly right; as it happens".

> **正好 我也要去** *zhènghǎo, wǒ yě yào qù* — As it happens, I'm going too.

> **这个正好** *zhège zhènghǎo* — This one is exactly right.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**正好** (*zhènghǎo*) is built from 正 'exactly' and 好 'good': exactly right. 好 is inherited from Middle Chinese *xawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'good; fine'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C92-YE-01] -->

正好 can open a sentence, a happy coincidence: 正好, 我也要去.

Or it can follow a thing, which then fits: 这个正好.
