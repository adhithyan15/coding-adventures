Sanskrit, chapter 279: Advice, and What Follows.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
भवान् गच्छेत् (bhavān gacchet) — you should go.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I will not come, because I am ill". (ahaṃ na āgamiṣyāmi, yataḥ ahaṃ rugṇaḥ asmi.) A friend answers with advice.

You'll want to know: -एत्, "should".
भवान् गच्छेत्। bhavān gacchet. — "You should go."
गच्छेत् (gacchet) is गच्छति with -et in place of -ati: "should go". It speaks to भवान् (bhavān), "you", said with respect, and about anyone else:
भवान् वैद्यं गच्छेत्। bhavān vaidyaṃ gacchet. — "You should go to the doctor."
सः सत्यं वदेत्। saḥ satyaṃ vadet. — "He should tell the truth."
To a woman, भवती (bhavatī):
भवती गृहे तिष्ठेत्। bhavatī gṛhe tiṣṭhet. — "You should stay at home."
भवेत् (bhavet), from भवति, is "should be", and also "may be":
सः गृहे भवेत्। saḥ gṛhe bhavet. — "He may be at home."
Pāṇini (3.3.161) gives this form for bidding and asking.

You'll want to know: त्वं पठेः, and मया गन्तव्यम्.
To a friend, with त्वम्, the ending is -eḥ:
त्वं प्रतिदिनं पठेः। tvaṃ pratidinaṃ paṭheḥ. — "You should read every day."
For yourself, "I must" can be said another way, word for word "by me, to be gone":
मया गन्तव्यम्। mayā gantavyam. — "I must go."
गन्तव्यम् (gantavyam) is shaped like कर्तव्यम् (kartavyam), "a duty", and मया (mayā) is "by me". Meet it when you hear it; you do not need to build it.
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
भवान् गच्छेत् (bhavān gacchet), you should go.
त्वं पठेः, you (a friend) should read.
सः वदेत्, he should say.
मया गन्तव्यम्, I must go.
These verbs end in -ati, like गच्छति; a verb such as करोति has an optative of its own, met one at a time.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "You should go home" — bhavān gṛhaṃ gacchet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You should stay at home" — bhavān gṛhe tiṣṭhet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You should read at home" — bhavān gṛhe paṭhet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Advise an older person that they should go to the doctor, then tell a friend to read every day.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"You should go", with respect? (भवान् गच्छेत् (bhavān gacchet).) "You should read", to a friend? (त्वं पठेः.) "He may be at home"? (सः गृहे भवेत्.) What does मया गन्तव्यम् say? ("I must go".)


Lesson 2 of 4.
अतः, तस्मात् (ataḥ, tasmāt) — so, and therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "you should go to the doctor". (bhavān vaidyaṃ gacchet.) Now say why, and what follows.

You'll want to know: अतः.
यतः gives the reason after the event; अतः (ataḥ), "so", gives the result after the reason:
अहं रुग्णः अस्मि। ahaṃ rugṇaḥ asmi. — "I am ill."
अतः अहं न आगमिष्यामि। ataḥ ahaṃ na āgamiṣyāmi. — "So I will not come."
वर्षा भवति, अतः सः गृहे अस्ति। varṣā bhavati, ataḥ saḥ gṛhe asti. — "It is raining, so he is at home."

You'll want to know: तस्मात्, ततः.
तस्मात् (tasmāt), "from that", says the same with more weight: "therefore". ततः (tataḥ), "then" in a story, can also mean "so":
सा श्रान्ता अस्ति। sā śrāntā asti. — "She is tired."
तस्मात् सा गृहे तिष्ठति। tasmāt sā gṛhe tiṣṭhati. — "Therefore she is staying at home."
धूमयानं न आगतम्, ततः वयं गतवन्तः। dhūmayānaṃ na āgatam, tataḥ vayaṃ gatavantaḥ. — "The train did not come, so we left."
आगतम् (āgatam) is आगतः, "come", for a neuter noun such as धूमयानम्.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "So I will stay at home" — ataḥ ahaṃ gṛhe sthāsyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "So I will read at home" — ataḥ ahaṃ gṛhe paṭhiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "So I will eat at home" — ataḥ ahaṃ gṛhe khādiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 28 seconds]
Say that you are tired, so you will not come; then that she is ill, therefore she is staying at home.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word opens the reason, यतः or अतः? (यतः.) And the result? (अतः, or तस्मात्.) "She is tired"? (सा श्रान्ता अस्ति.)


Lesson 3 of 4.
उपदेशः (upadeśaḥ) — a piece of advice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "you should go to the doctor". (bhavān vaidyaṃ gacchet.) That sentence is a piece of advice.

You'll want to know: उपदेशः.
उपदेशः — upadeśaḥ — "a piece of advice". It is a masculine noun.
मम उपदेशः अस्ति। mama upadeśaḥ asti. — "I have a piece of advice."
सः मम उपदेशं न शृणोति। saḥ mama upadeśaṃ na śṛṇoti. — "He does not listen to my advice."
वैद्यः उपदेशं दास्यति। vaidyaḥ upadeśaṃ dāsyati. — "The doctor will give advice."

The word, taken apart.
उपदेशः (upadeśaḥ) is built from उप (upa), "towards", and the root √diś, "to point out". Monier-Williams gives its first sense as pointing something out to someone, and then instruction, teaching and advice.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: upadeśaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I have a piece of advice" — mama upadeśaḥ asti]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "My advice: you should rest at home" — mama upadeśaḥ: bhavān gṛhe tiṣṭhet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does उपदेशः (upadeśaḥ) mean? ("A piece of advice".) Masculine or neuter? (Masculine.)


Lesson 4 of 4.
Review — advice, and what follows.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "in my opinion this is beautiful". (mama matena etat sundaram.) Then "because of the rain". (varṣākāraṇāt.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: You are ill. You should go to the doctor.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: You should read every day, to a friend.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It is raining, so we will stay at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: She is tired, therefore she is not coming.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I have a piece of advice: he should tell the truth.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
गच्छेत् or गच्छति, for advice? (गच्छेत्.) पठेः: to whom? (A friend, त्वम्.) Now the other way round: "so, therefore"? (अतः, तस्मात्.) "I must go"? (मया गन्तव्यम्.)
