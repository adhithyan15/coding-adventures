# உலர் (ular) — to become dry; to dry

## You'll want to know: உலர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C431-VARATCHI431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**உலர்** — *ular* — "to become dry; to dry".

> வெயிலில் துணிகள் சீக்கிரம் உலர்ந்தன.
>
> *veyilil tuṇikaḷ cīkkiram ularntaṉa.*
>
> The clothes dried quickly in the sun.

Past (it …): உலர்ந்தது, *ularntatu*.

To dry something is உலர்த்து.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உலர்** (*ular*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 674), "to become dry, to wither".
