# ਉਸ ਨੇ — he or she, before the marker

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-PLACE-US-THAT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *Aman bought a book*. Then *he, she*, **ਉਹ**, and the familiar
**ਉਸ**.

## Grammar Lens: ਉਹ turns into ਉਸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-US-NE-220]; assesses=[PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-PLACE-US-THAT-01, PA-GRAMMAR-AGENT-NE-210, PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219] -->

Before **ਨੇ**, **ਉਹ** turns into **ਉਸ**. The verb still agrees with the thing:

> **ਉਸ ਨੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ।** — *us ne duddh pītā.* — He drank milk; she drank milk.

> **ਉਸ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ।** — *us ne kitāb kharīdī.* — He bought a book; she bought a book.

So **ਉਸ ਨੇ** can mean he or she: the sentence does not say which.
