## You'll want to know: 去
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-01]; assesses=[] -->

**去** — *qù* — "to go".

> **我去大学** *wǒ qù dàxué* — I'm going to the university.

The new character, in pieces: 去 — a shape like 十 standing on a long bar, and under it a two-stroke piece. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**去** (*qù*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *khjoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to leave'.
