# Review — a view, and the arrows behind it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-JE-PENSE-QUE-01, FR-GRAM-A-MON-AVIS-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think so". (*Je pense que oui.*) Then "in my opinion".
(*À mon avis.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-DEUX-FLECHES-01, FR-GRAM-ALORS-DONC-02, FR-LEX-PARCEQUE-01] -->

Two lessons, and the start of every argument you will make in French. An
opinion, with *je pense que*, *je crois que* or *à mon avis*. A reason
pointing back, with *parce que*. A consequence pointing forward, with
*donc* or *alors*.

[PAUSE 2s] Here is one view, built both ways:

> *Je prends le métro, parce que je pense que le train est cher.*
> *Je pense que le train est cher, donc je prends le métro.*

Same facts, same view. The first starts from the decision; the second starts
from the opinion that caused it.
