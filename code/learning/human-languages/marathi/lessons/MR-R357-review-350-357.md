Common? (**सर्वसामान्य**, *sarvasāmānya*.) Population? (**लोकसंख्या**, *loksaṅkhyā*.) Rural? (**ग्रामीण**, *grāmīṇ*.) Voting? (**मतदान**, *matdān*.) A representative? (**प्रतिनिधी**, *pratinidhī*.) A minority? (**अल्पसंख्याक**, *alpasaṅkhyāk*.) Public opinion? (**जनमत**, *janmat*.) To give a mandate? (**कौल देणे**, *kaul deṇe*.) Administration? (**प्रशासन**, *praśāsan*.) Parliament?
