## You'll want to know: मुसळधार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C438-VALIV438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मुसळधार** — *musaḷdhār* — "torrential".

> काल रात्री मुसळधार पाऊस पडला.
>
> *kāl rātrī musaḷdhār pāūs paḍlā.*
>
> Last night it rained in torrents.

मुसळ is a pestle and धार a stream: rain as thick as pestles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुसळधार** (*musaḷdhār*) is built from **मुसळ**, "a pestle", inherited from Sanskrit *musala* (CDIAL 10223), and **धार**, "a stream".
