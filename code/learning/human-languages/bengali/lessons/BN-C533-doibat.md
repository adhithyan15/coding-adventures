## You'll want to know: দৈবাৎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C533-DOIBAT533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**দৈবাৎ** — *doibāt* — "by chance".

> বাজারে দৈবাৎ আমার পুরনো বন্ধুর সঙ্গে দেখা হল।
>
> *Bājāre doibāt āmār purôno bôndhur sôngge dækhā hôlô.*
>
> By chance I met my old friend at the market.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দৈবাৎ** (*doibāt*) is the Sanskrit form *daivāt*, "from fate", of *daiva*, "coming from the gods, fate" (Monier-Williams): by the gods' doing.
