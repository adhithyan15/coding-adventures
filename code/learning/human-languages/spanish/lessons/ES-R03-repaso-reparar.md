# When you don't understand — putting it together

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-NO-ENTIENDO-01, ES-ETYMON-INTENDERE-02, ES-LEX-MAS-DESPACIO-01, ES-ETYMON-SPATIUM-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Two sentences. Between them they rescue almost any conversation.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-NO-ENTIENDO-01, ES-ETYMON-INTENDERE-02, ES-LEX-MAS-DESPACIO-01, ES-ETYMON-SPATIUM-02] -->

> — *(something too fast to follow)*
> — **No entiendo. Más despacio, por favor.**
> — *(again, slower)*
> — **Gracias.**

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-NO-ENTIENDO-01, ES-ETYMON-INTENDERE-02, ES-LEX-MAS-DESPACIO-01, ES-ETYMON-SPATIUM-02] -->

Until now, a sentence you missed was the end of the conversation. It is not any
more. You can say what went wrong and what would fix it, and then thank someone
for doing it — which is the difference between speaking a language badly and not
speaking it at all.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-NO-ENTIENDO-01, ES-ETYMON-INTENDERE-02, ES-LEX-MAS-DESPACIO-01, ES-ETYMON-SPATIUM-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: both sentences, one after the other, without a pause between them]
- [YOU SAY: them again, then "Gracias." — the whole repair, start to finish]

[REPEAT x2]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-NO-ENTIENDO-01, ES-ETYMON-INTENDERE-02, ES-LEX-MAS-DESPACIO-01, ES-ETYMON-SPATIUM-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What do you say when you did not follow? And what do you ask for
next? Which English words hide inside *entiendo* and *despacio*? (*Intend,
intense, attention* — and *space*.)
