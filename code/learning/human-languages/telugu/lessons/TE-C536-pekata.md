## You'll want to know: పేకాట
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C536-VIRAMAM536-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పేకాట** — *pēkāṭa* — "a card game".

> పండుగ రాత్రి పెద్దలు పేకాట ఆడారు.
>
> *paṇḍuga rātri peddalu pēkāṭa āḍāru.*
>
> On the festival night the grown-ups played cards.

పేక, playing cards, + ఆట, game.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పేకాట** (*pēkāṭa*) is built from పేక, "playing cards", and native ఆట, "play, game" (DEDR 347); the origin of పేక is not settled.
