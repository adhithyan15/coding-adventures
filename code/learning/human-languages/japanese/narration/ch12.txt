Japanese, chapter 12: Seven More Body Words, Spaced on Purpose.
20 lessons. All 20 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 20.
Retrieval 1 — mora, politeness, and し.

Warm-up.
[pause 80 seconds]
Tap one mora for い, then write い and し from memory. Hear, say, read, and write the first seven-word body map; finish by isolating あし.

Guided Practice.
Hear a polite thank-you, say it, read ありがとうございます, and choose it for someone outside your close circle.

Wrap-up Recall.
Stop after one accurate card.
[question — say your answer, then pause 40 seconds]
Write i, shi, the polite thank-you, and the seven-word body map; label each spoken unit a mora.


Lesson 2 of 20.
あたま (atama) — head.

Warm-up.
[pause 48 seconds]
Write は (ha) and 日. Read ございます, say yukkuri, then retrieve はな.

You'll want to know.
あたま (atama) — a-ta-ma — head.
Every sign is earned. Keep three even morae.

Guided Practice.
Hear, point, say, read, then hide and write あたま (atama).

Wrap-up Recall.

Seven more body words, spaced on purpose — head, hair, tooth, shoulder, belly, back, waist.

You'll want to know.
あたま・かみ・は・かた・おなか・せなか・こし (atama, kami, ha, kata, onaka, senaka, koshi).
Every sign is earned, and every new word has already had short and medium retrieval scheduled in this chapter.

Guided Practice.
Listen and point; say all seven; read all seven; then write any four from memory.

Wrap-up Recall.
Stop after one accurate seven-word map.
