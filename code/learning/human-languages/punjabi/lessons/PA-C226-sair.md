## You'll want to know: ਸੈਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C226-DIN226-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੈਰ** — *sair* — "a walk; a stroll".

> ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਪਿਤਾ ਨਾਲ ਸੈਰ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ roz shām nū̃ pitā nāl sair kardā hāṁ.*
>
> Every evening I go for a walk with my father.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੈਰ** (*sair*) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian *sair*, "a walk, an outing".
