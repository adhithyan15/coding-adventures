## You'll want to know: удочка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C505-FISHING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**удочка** (*údochka*) — "a fishing rod".

> Он взял удочку и пошёл к озеру.
>
> *On vzyal údochku i poshyól k ózeru.*
>
> He took his fishing rod and went to the lake.

Feminine. A diminutive of the old word *уда*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*удочка* is a diminutive of the old word *уда* 'a fishing line'; EtymDB has no entry, and *уда* is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *ǫda*.
