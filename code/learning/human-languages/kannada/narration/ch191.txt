Kannada, chapter 191: Picture, Sport, Match, Team, Cricket.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚಿತ್ರ (citra) — a picture, a painting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mattress, then the Kannada for an axe.

You'll want to know: ಚಿತ್ರ.
ಚಿತ್ರ — citra — "a picture, a painting".

The word, taken apart.
ಚಿತ್ರ (citra) is Sanskrit citra, "conspicuous, bright, variegated", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 4803). What is bright with colour became a picture.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪಂದ್ಯ (pandya) — a match.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a picture, then the Kannada for sport.

You'll want to know: ಪಂದ್ಯ.
ಪಂದ್ಯ — pandya — "a match".

The word, taken apart.
ಪಂದ್ಯ (pandya) is listed in DEDR 3921 as Kannada panta, pandya, "bet, wager", with Tamil pantayam, "contest for a prize". The entry notes that one scholar derives these words from Sanskrit paṇita, "a wager", so the origin is not settled. A match is a contest with a stake.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತಂಡ (taṇḍa) — a team.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for sport, then the Kannada for a match.

You'll want to know: ತಂಡ.
ತಂಡ — taṇḍa — "a team".

The word, taken apart.
ತಂಡ (taṇḍa) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil taṇṭu, Telugu taṇḍā and Malayalam taṇṭu (DEDR 3055). DEDR lists taṇḍa itself, "multitude, troop, party", and compares Apabhramsha taṇḍaa, "a group".
