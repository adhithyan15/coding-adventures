## You'll want to know: जूझना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C321-HAMLA321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जूझना** — *jūjhnā* — "to fight hard; to struggle".

> सिपाही तीन दिन तक दुश्मन से जूझे।
>
> *sipāhī tīn din tak duśman se jūjhe.*
>
> The soldiers fought the enemy for three days.

Past (he …): वह सिपाही जूझा, *vah sipāhī jūjhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जूझना** (*jūjhnā*) goes back to Sanskrit *yudhyate*, "fights" (CDIAL 10502), the family of **युद्ध**, war. It is a long, hard fight, against an enemy or against trouble.
