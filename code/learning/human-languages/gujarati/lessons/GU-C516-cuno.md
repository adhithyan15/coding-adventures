## You'll want to know: ચૂનો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C516-CHUNO516-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂનો** — *cūno* — "lime wash".

> દિવાળી પહેલાં અમે દીવાલો પર ચૂનો લગાડીએ છીએ.
>
> *divāḷī pahelā̃ ame dīvālo par cūno lagāḍīe chīe.*
>
> Before Diwali we put lime wash on the walls.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂનો** (*cūno*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *cūrṇa*, "powder", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "lime" (CDIAL 4889).
