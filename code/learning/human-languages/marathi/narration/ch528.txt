Marathi, chapter 528: Hobbies and Leisure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
छंद (chand) — a hobby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to stir, then the Marathi for to get burnt.

You'll want to know: छंद.
छंद — chand — "a hobby".
माझ्या बहिणीला नाणी गोळा करण्याचा छंद आहे. mājhyā bahiṇīlā nāṇī goḷā karṇyācā chand āhe. My sister's hobby is collecting coins.
Masculine.
The person takes -ला: तिला छंद (chand) आहे.

The word, taken apart.
छंद (chand) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit chanda, "pleasure, wish" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
फुरसत (phursat) — free time; leisure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a hobby, then the Marathi for a pastime.

फुरसत — phursat — "free time; leisure".
आज मला अजिबात फुरसत नाही. āj malā ajibāt phursat nāhī. I have no free time at all today.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
फुरसत (phursat) is a loan from Persian fursat, "leisure", usually traced to Arabic furṣa, "occasion".

जत्रेत मुलांच्या करमणुकीसाठी खूप खेळ होते. jatret mulāñcyā karamṇukīsāṭhī khūp kheḷ hote. At the fair there were lots of games to entertain the children.
Feminine.
The oblique is करमणुकी-: करमणुकीसाठी.

The word, taken apart.
करमणूक (karamṇūk) is built on करमणे, "to be diverted, to pass the time"; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दंग होणे (daṅg hoṇe) — to be absorbed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for free time, then the Marathi for entertainment.

You'll want to know: दंग होणे.
दंग होणे — daṅg hoṇe — "to be absorbed".
पुस्तक वाचण्यात अरुण इतका दंग झाला की जेवायला विसरला. pustak vācṇyāt aruṇ itkā daṅg jhālā kī jevāylā visarlā. Arun was so absorbed in his book that he forgot to eat.
Past (he …): तो खेळात दंग झाला, to kheḷāt daṅg jhālā.
What absorbs takes -त: खेळात दंग होणे (daṅg hoṇe).

The word, taken apart.
दंग होणे (daṅg hoṇe) is दंग, usually traced to Persian dang, "astonished, lost", with होणे.
