## You'll want to know: zhuānyè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C492-SUBJECTS492-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuānyè* — "major; field of study".

> *nǐ de zhuānyè shì shénme* — What's your major?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuānyè* is built from *zhuān* 'single-minded' and 业 'a trade', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsywen* and *ngjaep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'alone' and 'to have to oneself'.
