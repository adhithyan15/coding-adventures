## You'll want to know: трап
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C421-VZLYOT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**трап** (*trap*) — "the steps (to a plane), a gangway".

> Пассажиры спускаются по трапу.
>
> *Passazhíry spuskáyutsya po trápu.*
>
> The passengers come down the steps.

Masculine. *По трапу* — up or down the steps.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*трап* was borrowed from Dutch *trap* (EtymDB), the everyday Dutch word for stairs.
