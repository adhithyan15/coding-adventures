## You'll want to know: আয়েশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C404-ARAM404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**আয়েশ** — *āyesh* — "leisure; ease".

> ছুটির দিনে অরুণ আয়েশ করে চা খায়।
>
> *chhuṭir dine ôrun āyesh kore chā khāy.*
>
> On holidays Arun drinks his tea at leisure.

আয়েশ করে, at leisure, taking one's time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আয়েশ** (*āyesh*) is usually traced to Arabic *ʿaysh*, "living, a life of ease", which reached Bengali through Persian.
