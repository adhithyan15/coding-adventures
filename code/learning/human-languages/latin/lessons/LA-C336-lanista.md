## You'll want to know: lanista, lanistae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C336-GLADIATOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lanista, lanistae** — "a trainer of gladiators".

> Lanista novōs gladiātōrēs ēmit.
>
> *The trainer bought new gladiators.*

Masculine, 1st declension. He owned and trained a troop; in Cicero the word is an insult.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lanista** is a word whose origin is not settled (Lewis & Short).
