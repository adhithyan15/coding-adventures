## You'll want to know: la discrimination
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C365-PAUVRETE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la discrimination** — "the discrimination".

> La loi interdit la discrimination à l'embauche.
>
> *The law forbids discrimination in hiring.*

*Lutter contre la discrimination* — to fight discrimination.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discrimination* is a learned borrowing from Latin *discrīminātiō* "a distinguishing" (EtymDB), from *discrīmen* "a dividing line", built on *cernere* "to separate" (Lewis & Short).
