Arabic, chapter 439: Landmarks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منظر (manẓar) — a view; scenery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to wander around, then the Arabic for a great traveller.

You'll want to know: منظر.
منظر — manẓar — "a view; scenery".
كان المنظر من الجبل جميلا. kāna al-manẓar min al-jabal jamīlan. The view from the mountain was beautiful.
Plural: مناظر, manāẓir.

The word, taken apart.
منظر (manẓar) is built on the native Arabic root n-ẓ-r, "to look" (Lane). The pattern mafʿal makes it the thing looked at.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اصطحب (iṣṭaḥaba) — to take along; to bring along.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a monument, then the Arabic for to explore.

You'll want to know: اصطحب.
اصطحب — iṣṭaḥaba — "to take along; to bring along".
اصطحبنا الأطفال إلى المتحف. iṣṭaḥabnā al-aṭfāl ilā al-matḥaf. We took the children along to the museum.
Present: يصطحب, yaṣṭaḥib.
Form VIII turns its t into ṭ after ṣ: iṣṭaḥaba, not iṣtaḥaba.

The word, taken apart.
اصطحب (iṣṭaḥaba) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-ḥ-b, "to be a companion" (Lane). It is Form VIII; a ṣāḥib is a companion.
