## You'll want to know: einwenden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C333-WIDERSPRUCH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**einwenden** — "to object, to say against".

> Sie wendet ein, dass der Weg zu weit ist.
>
> *She objects that the way is too far.*

Separable: *sie wendet ein*. Perfekt: *sie hat eingewandt* or *eingewendet*; both are correct.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*einwenden* is built from *ein* and *wenden* "to turn", inherited from Old High German *wenten* and Proto-Germanic *wandijaną* (EtymDB).
