## You'll want to know: خدشہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C363-ANDESHA363-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خدشہ** — *khadshā* — "a nagging fear (that something may happen)".

> اگر دیر ہوئی تو گاڑی چھوٹنے کا خدشہ ہے۔
>
> *agar der huī to gāṛī chhūṭne kā khadshā hai.*
>
> If we are late, we may miss the train.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خدشہ** (*khadshā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khadsha*, "a scratch"; Urdu uses it for a fear that something may go wrong, a sense our sources do not trace.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
