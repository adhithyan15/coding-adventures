## You'll want to know: a tigela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C330-DESCASCAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a tigela** — "the bowl".

> Ele pôs a fruta numa tigela.
>
> *He put the fruit in a bowl.*

*Uma tigela de sopa* — a bowl of soup.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tigela* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *tēgella* (EtymDB), a little *tēgula* "a roof tile": a curved piece of baked clay.
