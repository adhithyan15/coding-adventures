## You'll want to know: અભ્યાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C246-MARKS246-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અભ્યાસ** — *abhyās* — "studies, study".

> **તે રોજ બે કલાક અભ્યાસ કરે છે.** — *te roj be kalāk abhyās kare che* — "She studies for two hours every day."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અભ્યાસ** (*abhyās*) is Sanskrit *abhyāsa*, "repetition, practice", taken over whole as a tatsama. CDIAL lists it under the verb *abhyasyati*, "practises", with Old Gujarati *ahīāsivaũ*, "to practise" (CDIAL 546).
