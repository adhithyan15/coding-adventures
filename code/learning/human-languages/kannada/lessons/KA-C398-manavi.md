# ಮನವಿ (manavi) — a petition; an appeal

## You'll want to know: ಮನವಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C398-PRATIBHATISU398-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮನವಿ** — *manavi* — "a petition; an appeal".

> ಜನರು ಮಂತ್ರಿಗೆ ಒಂದು ಮನವಿ ಕೊಟ್ಟರು.
>
> *janaru mantrige ondu manavi koṭṭaru.*
>
> The people gave the minister a petition.

ಮನವಿ ಮಾಡು, to appeal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮನವಿ** (*manavi*) is a Kannada word whose origin is not settled: neither DEDR nor CDIAL lists it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
