## You'll want to know: विश्लेषण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C362-NISHKARSH362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्लेषण** — *viśleṣaṇ* — "an analysis".

> शिक्षक ने कहानी का विश्लेषण किया।
>
> *śikṣak ne kahānī kā viśleṣaṇ kiyā.*
>
> The teacher analysed the story.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्लेषण** (*viśleṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *viśleṣaṇa*, "separation" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses the idea of taking apart for an analysis.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
