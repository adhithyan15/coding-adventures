## You'll want to know: আমদানি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C499-UTPADON499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**আমদানি** — *āmdāni* — "an import; imports".

> আমাদের দেশে তেলের আমদানি বাড়ল।
>
> *Āmāder deshe teler āmdāni bāṛlô.*
>
> Our country's oil imports went up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আমদানি** (*āmdāni*) is usually traced to Persian *āmadanī*, "what is to come in": the partner of রপ্তানি.
