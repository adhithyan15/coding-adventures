## You'll want to know: le millénaire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C276-EPOQUE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le millénaire** — "the millennium".

> Cette église a presque un millénaire.
>
> *This church is almost a thousand years old.*

*Le troisième millénaire* — the third millennium.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*millénaire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *millēnārius* "containing a thousand" (EtymDB), built on *mīlle* "thousand".
