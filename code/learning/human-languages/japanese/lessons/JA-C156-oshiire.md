## You'll want to know: おしいれ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C156-CLOSET156-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おしいれ** — *oshiire* — "a closet (for futons)".

> **ふとんを おしいれに しまいます。** — *futon o oshiire ni shimaimasu* — "I put the futon away in the closet."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おしいれ** (*oshiire*) is native Japanese, built from *oshi*, "pushing", the stem of *osu*, and *ire*, "putting in", the stem of *ireru*; both parts are inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
