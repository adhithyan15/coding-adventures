# إطلاقا (iṭlāqan) — not at all

## You'll want to know: إطلاقا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C402-HATMAN402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**إطلاقا** — *iṭlāqan* — "not at all".

> لا أحب القهوة إطلاقا.
>
> *lā uḥibbu al-qahwa iṭlāqan.*
>
> I do not like coffee at all.

With a negative: لا … إطلاقا, not … at all.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إطلاقا** (*iṭlāqan*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-l-q*, "to be free, let loose" (Lane). *Iṭlāqan* is the Form IV verbal noun, setting loose, with the adverb ending *-an*: without limit, and with a negative, not at all.

## What you've built
