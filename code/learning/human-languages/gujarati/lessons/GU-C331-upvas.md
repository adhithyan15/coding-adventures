## You'll want to know: ઉપવાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C331-UPVAS331-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપવાસ** — *upvās* — "a day of fasting; a fast".

> દાદી દરેક સોમવારે ઉપવાસ કરે છે.
>
> *dādī darek somvāre upvās kare che.*
>
> My grandmother fasts every Monday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપવાસ** (*upvās*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *upavāsa*, "fasting" (CDIAL 2244). It is kept with **કરવું**: **ઉપવાસ કરે છે**, fasts.
