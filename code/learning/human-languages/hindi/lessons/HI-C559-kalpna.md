## You'll want to know: कल्पना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C559-KALPANA559-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कल्पना** — *kalpnā* — "imagination; an idea of what could be".

> बच्चों की कल्पना बहुत तेज़ होती है।
>
> *Baccoṁ kī kalpnā bahut tez hotī hai.*
>
> Children have very lively imaginations.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कल्पना** (*kalpnā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *kalpanā*, "making; creating in the mind, fiction" (Monier-Williams).
