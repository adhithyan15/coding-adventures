## You'll want to know: garantir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C394-GARANTIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**garantir** — "to guarantee, to assure".

> Garanto-te que ele vem.
>
> *I assure you he is coming.*

*Garantir que* takes the indicative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garantir* is usually traced to French *garantir*, from Old French *guarantir* "to warrant, to vouch for" (EtymDB), built on *garant* "guarantor", a word of Germanic origin.
