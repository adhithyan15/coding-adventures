Punjabi, chapter 165: Return to Living, Spending, Saving, and Sowing.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
An old word for living — to live, recalled after a long gap.

Hear one familiar meaning, pause, and retrieve its Punjabi word before the model. This is a gentle return, not a new word or a scored mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to live.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਜੀਉਣਾ (jīuṇā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਜੀਉਣਾ (jīuṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to live. Then hear to live again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਜੀਉਣਾ (jīuṇā) before the model. Only this old word is needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
One last cue: to live.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਜੀਉਣਾ (jīuṇā). Listen and retry once if it does not come back.


Lesson 2 of 4.
An old word for spending — to spend, recalled after a long gap.

Retrieve one familiar action by itself. No new meaning, sentence, or scored writing is required.

Warm-up.
Hear to spend.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਖ਼ਰਚਣਾ (kharcṇā) before the model.

Guided Practice.
Hear ਖ਼ਰਚਣਾ (kharcṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to spend. Now hear to spend.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਖ਼ਰਚਣਾ (kharcṇā). One old word is enough.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to spend once more.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਖ਼ਰਚਣਾ (kharcṇā). If needed, listen to the model and try again once.


Lesson 3 of 4.
An old word for saving — to save, recalled after a long gap.

Pause after a familiar meaning and retrieve the taught Punjabi word. There is no new sense or scored assessment here.

Warm-up.
Hear to save.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਬਚਾਉਣਾ (bacāuṇā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਬਚਾਉਣਾ (bacāuṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to save. Then hear to save again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਬਚਾਉਣਾ (bacāuṇā) before the model. Keep to this one old meaning.

Wrap-up Recall.
One last cue: to save.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਬਚਾਉਣਾ (bacāuṇā). Listen and retry once if needed.


Lesson 4 of 4.
An old word for sowing — to sow, then a gentle mix of four familiar actions.

First retrieve to sow alone. Then hear four familiar actions in a small shuffled mix. Pause before each model. This is not a scored mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to sow.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਬੀਜਣਾ (bījṇā).

Guided Practice.
Pause after each familiar meaning, then hear its model:
to save —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਬਚਾਉਣਾ (bacāuṇā).
to live —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਜੀਉਣਾ (jīuṇā).
to sow —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਬੀਜਣਾ (bījṇā).
to spend —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਖ਼ਰਚਣਾ (kharcṇā).
Only the order of these old words has changed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to sow, to spend, to live, then to save. Pause after each cue and recall its Punjabi word before the model: ਬੀਜਣਾ (bījṇā), ਖ਼ਰਚਣਾ (kharcṇā), ਜੀਉਣਾ (jīuṇā), ਬਚਾਉਣਾ (bacāuṇā). Revisit only the old word you missed.
