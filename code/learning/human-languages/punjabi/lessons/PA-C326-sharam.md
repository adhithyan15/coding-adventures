## You'll want to know: ਸ਼ਰਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C326-FEAR326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ਰਮ** — *sharam* — "embarrassment".

> ਗਾਲ੍ਹ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਉਸ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਸ਼ਰਮ ਆਈ।
>
> *gālh ton bāad us nū̃ bahut sharam āī.*
>
> After the swear word he felt very ashamed.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ਰਮ** (*sharam*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *sharm*, "shame, modesty".
