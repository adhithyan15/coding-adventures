Kannada, chapter 161: To stick, To inform, To meet, To reserve, To cancel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಂಟಿಸು (aṇṭisu) — to stick, to paste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to sneeze, then the Kannada for to shiver.

You'll want to know: ಅಂಟಿಸು.
ಅಂಟಿಸು — aṇṭisu — "to stick, to paste".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂಟಿಸು (aṇṭisu) is native Dravidian, from aṇṭu, "to stick, adhere", which DEDR 96 lists, with -isu, "to make"; the entry also has Tamil aṭṭu, Telugu aṇṭu and Malayalam aṭṭuka.

ತಿಳಿಸು (tiḷisu) is native Dravidian, from tiḷi, "to become clear", with -isu, "to make": to inform is "to make clear"; DEDR 3433 lists the root with Tamil teḷ, Telugu teli and Malayalam teḷi.

ಕಾಯ್ದಿರಿಸು (kāydirisu) is native Dravidian: kāy, "to guard, keep" (DEDR 1416), and irisu, "to cause to stay, place", from iru, "to stay" (DEDR 480): "to put aside and keep".
