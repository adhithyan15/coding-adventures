Arabic, chapter 328: Holding a celebration.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أقام (aqāma) — to hold an event; to reside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for until, then the Arabic for throughout.

You'll want to know: أقام.
أقام — aqāma — "to hold an event; to reside".
أقامت المدرسة حفلا في نهاية السنة. aqāmat al-madrasa ḥaflan fī nihāyat as-sana. The school held a party at the end of the year.
Present: يقيم, yuqīm.
أقام (aqāma) حفلا: to throw a party. أقام (aqāma) في: to live in a place.

The word, taken apart.
أقام (aqāma) is built on the native Arabic root q-w-m (Lane), "to stand up", the root of qāma. It is Form IV, "to set a thing up", and "to stay".

Lesson 2 of 5.
أحيا (aḥyā) — to perform at; to revive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for throughout, then the Arabic for to hold an event.

You'll want to know: أحيا.
أحيا — aḥyā — "to perform at; to revive".
أحيا المطرب حفل العرس. aḥyā al-muṭrib ḥafl al-ʿurs. The singer performed at the wedding party.
Present: يحيي, yuḥyī.
أحيا (aḥyā) حفلا: a singer performs at a party. أحيا (aḥyā) ذكرى: to mark an anniversary.

The word, taken apart.
أحيا (aḥyā) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-y-y (Lane), "to live", the root of ḥayāh, life, and of the taught ḥayawān, an animal. It is Form IV, "to give life to": to bring a night or a memory to life.

Lesson 3 of 5.
موكب (mawkib) — a procession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to hold an event, then the Arabic for to perform at.

You'll want to know: موكب.
موكب — mawkib — "a procession".
مر موكب العرس في الشارع. marra mawkib al-ʿurs fī ash-shāriʿ. The wedding procession went down the street.
Plural: مواكب, mawākib.

The word, taken apart.
موكب (mawkib) is built on the native Arabic root w-k-b (Lane), "to walk along in a grave, leisurely way". A mawkib is a company moving at that pace.

طبخت النساء الطعام للوليمة. ṭabakhat an-nisāʾ aṭ-ṭaʿām li-l-walīma. The women cooked the food for the banquet.

The word, taken apart.
وليمة (walīma) is built on the native Arabic root w-l-m (Lane), where walīma is first the feast made for a wedding, and then for any occasion.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مدعو (madʿuww) — an invited guest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a procession, then the Arabic for a banquet.

You'll want to know: مدعو.
مدعو — madʿuww — "an invited guest".
كان كل مدعو يحمل هدية. kāna kull madʿuww yaḥmilu hadiyya. Every guest was carrying a present.
Plural مدعوون.

The word, taken apart.
مدعو (madʿuww) is built on the native Arabic root d-ʿ-w (Lane), "to call, to invite", the root of the taught daʿā. Madʿuww is the one called: an invited guest.
