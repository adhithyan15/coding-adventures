## You'll want to know: مغبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C466-GHUBAR466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مغبر** — *mughbarr* — "dusty".

> كان الطريق إلى القرية مغبرا.
>
> *kāna aṭ-ṭarīq ilā al-qarya mughbarran.*
>
> The road to the village was dusty.

## The word, taken apart

**مغبر** (*mughbarr*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-b-r*, "dust" (Lane).
