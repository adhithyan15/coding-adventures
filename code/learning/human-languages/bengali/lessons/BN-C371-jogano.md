# জোগানো (jogāno) — to supply; to provide (for)

## You'll want to know: জোগানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C371-JOTHESHTO371-04]; assesses=[] -->

**জোগানো** — *jogāno* — "to supply; to provide (for)".

> সরকার টাকা জোগালে নতুন স্কুল হবে।
>
> *sôrkār ṭākā jogāle nôtun skul hôbe.*
>
> If the government provides the money, there will be a new school.

Past (he/she …): তিনি সবার খাবার জোগালেন, *tini sôbār khābār jogālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জোগানো** (*jogāno*) is listed by CDIAL 10529 (*yōgyā́*) as Bengali jogāna, 'to supply': to fit out what is needed.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
