## You'll want to know: उन्नति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C368-VIKAS368-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्नति** — *unnati* — "progress, advancement".

> पढ़ाई से देश की उन्नति होती है।
>
> *paṛhāī se deś kī unnati hotī hai.*
>
> A country advances through education.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्नति** (*unnati*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *unnati*, "rising; advancement" (Monier-Williams).
