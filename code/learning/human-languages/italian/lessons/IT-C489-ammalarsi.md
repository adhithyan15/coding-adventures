## You'll want to know: ammalarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C489-GUARIRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ammalarsi** — "to fall ill".

> Ogni inverno mi ammalo almeno una volta.
>
> *Every winter I fall ill at least once.*

It takes *essere*: *mi sono ammalato*. Built on *malato* "ill".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ammalarsi* is built on *malato* "ill", usually traced to Latin *male habitus* "in poor condition" (Lewis & Short).
