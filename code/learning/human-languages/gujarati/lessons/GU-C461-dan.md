# દાન (dān) — a donation; a gift to charity

## You'll want to know: દાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C461-KRUPAN461-05]; assesses=[] -->

**દાન** — *dān* — "a donation; a gift to charity".

> દાદાએ મંદિરમાં દાન આપ્યું.
>
> *dādāe mandirmā̃ dān āpyũ.*
>
> Grandfather made a donation at the temple.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાન** (*dān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dāna*, "the act of giving", as Monier-Williams records it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
