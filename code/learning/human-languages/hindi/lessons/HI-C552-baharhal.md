## You'll want to know: बहरहाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C552-BAHARHAAL552-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बहरहाल** — *baharhāl* — "anyway; in any case".

> रास्ता लंबा था, बहरहाल हम समय पर पहुँच गए।
>
> *Rāstā lambā thā, baharhāl ham samay par pahũc gae.*
>
> The way was long; anyway, we arrived on time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहरहाल** (*baharhāl*) is usually traced to Persian *ba-har-hāl*, "in every state".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
