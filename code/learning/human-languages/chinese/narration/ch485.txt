Mandarin Chinese, chapter 485: Body and Mind.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
yālì — pressure; stress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for worn out, then the Mandarin for resistance to illness.

You'll want to know: yālì.
yālì — "pressure; stress".
wǒ zuìjìn yālì hěn dà — I've been under a lot of stress lately.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yālì is built from yā 'to press' and 力 'strength', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'aep and lik (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to press down' and the second 'sinew'.

Lesson 3 of 7.
身心 (shēnxīn) — body and mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for stress and pressure, then the Mandarin for get-up-and-go.

You'll want to know: 身心.
身心 — shēnxīn — "body and mind".
眼前最要紧的是身心都好 yǎnqián zuì yàojǐn de shì shēnxīn dōu hǎo — For now, what matters most is being well in body and mind.

The word, taken apart.
身心 (shēnxīn) is built from 身 'body' and 心 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, syin and sim (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'oneself'.

Lesson 5 of 7.
jiǎnyā — to relieve stress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for body and mind, then the Mandarin for a mindset.

You'll want to know: jiǎnyā.
jiǎnyā — "to relieve stress".
tīng yīnyuè kěyǐ jiǎnyā — Listening to music helps relieve stress.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jiǎnyā is built from jiǎn 'to lessen' and yā 'to press', both inherited from Middle Chinese, keamX and 'aep (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first under two readings and glosses keamX 'to reduce'; it glosses the second 'to press down'.
