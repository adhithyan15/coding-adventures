Japanese, chapter 304: Repaying a Kindness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
おれい (orei) — thanks; a thank-you gift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a small wayside shrine, then the Japanese for auspicious.

You'll want to know: おれい.
おれい — orei — "thanks; a thank-you gift".
ははに おれいを いいました。 — haha ni orei o iimashita — "I said thank you to my mother."

The word, taken apart.
おれい (orei) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: o-, "the polite prefix", is native Japanese, and rei, "thanks", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese lejX) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for auspicious, then the Japanese for thanks.

You'll want to know: おんがえし.
おんがえし — ongaeshi — "repaying a kindness".
その とりは おじいさんに おんがえしを しました。 — sono tori wa ojiisan ni ongaeshi o shimashita — "The bird repaid the old man's kindness."

The word, taken apart.
おんがえし (ongaeshi) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: on, "kindness", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese 'on), and gaeshi, "return", is native Japanese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

The word, taken apart.
