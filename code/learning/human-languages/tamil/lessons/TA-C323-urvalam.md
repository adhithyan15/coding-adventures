# ஊர்வலம் (ūrvalam) — a procession

## You'll want to know: ஊர்வலம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C323-IRUTHI323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊர்வலம்** — *ūrvalam* — "a procession".

> இறுதி ஊர்வலம் மெதுவாகச் சென்றது.
>
> *iṟuti ūrvalam metuvākac ceṉṟatu.*
>
> The funeral procession moved slowly.

Also for festivals and weddings: ஊர், town + வலம், going round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊர்வலம்** (*ūrvalam*) is a Tamil compound built from ஊர், "town" (DEDR 752), and வலம், "the right side", as in going round to the right (DEDR 5276).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
