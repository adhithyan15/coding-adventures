## You'll want to know: चिनोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C508-NIRMANA508-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चिनोति** — *cinoti* — "piles up; builds up".

> कर्मकरः इष्टकाः चिनोति।
>
> *karmakaraḥ iṣṭakāḥ cinoti.*
>
> The worker stacks the bricks.

Past (he …): सः भित्तिं चितवान्, *saḥ bhittiṃ citavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिनोति** (*cinoti*) comes from the root √ci, "to arrange in a row, to pile up, to build"; its past participle is *cita* (MW).
