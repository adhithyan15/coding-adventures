## You'll want to know: فلسفة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C376-ILM376-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فلسفة** — *falsafa* — "philosophy".

> يدرس ابن عمي الفلسفة في الجامعة.
>
> *yadrusu ibn ʿammī al-falsafa fī al-jāmiʿa.*
>
> My cousin studies philosophy at university.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فلسفة** (*falsafa*) was borrowed from Greek *philosophia*, "love of wisdom" (EtymDB). Arabic fitted the loan to its own four-consonant pattern.

## What you've built
