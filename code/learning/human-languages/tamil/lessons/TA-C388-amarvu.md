## You'll want to know: அமர்வு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C388-OPPUTAL388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அமர்வு** — *amarvu* — "a session; a sitting".

> நாடாளுமன்றத்தின் அடுத்த அமர்வு திங்கள்கிழமை தொடங்கும்.
>
> *nāṭāḷumaṉṟattiṉ aṭutta amarvu tiṅkaḷkiḻamai toṭaṅkum.*
>
> The next session of parliament begins on Monday.

From அமர், to sit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அமர்வு** (*amarvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 161 lists Tamil *amar*, "to abide, remain, rest", and *amarvu*, "abode"; a session is a sitting together.
