## You'll want to know: 起来
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C158-WAKE158-04]; assesses=[] -->

**起来** — *qǐlai* — "to get up; to stand up".

> **七点了你快起来** *qī diǎn le nǐ kuài qǐlai* — It's seven o'clock: up you get, quick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**起来** (*qǐlai*) is built from 起 'to rise' and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khiX* and *loj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rise' and 'to stand'. In the word the second loses its tone.
