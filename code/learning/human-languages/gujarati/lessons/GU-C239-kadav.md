## You'll want to know: કાદવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C239-POND239-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કાદવ** — *kādav* — "mud, mire".

> **તળાવ પાસે ઘણો કાદવ હતો.** — *taḷāv pāse ghaṇo kādav hato* — "There was a lot of mud near the pond."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાદવ** (*kādav*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *kardama*, "mud, mire" (CDIAL 2869).
