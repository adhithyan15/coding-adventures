## You'll want to know: दुबला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C482-KAD482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दुबला** — *dublā* — "lean; thin and weak".

> बीमारी के बाद दादा बहुत दुबले हो गए।
>
> *bīmārī ke bād dādā bahut duble ho gae.*
>
> Grandfather got very thin after his illness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुबला** (*dublā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *dublā*, "weak, emaciated", under Sanskrit *durbala*, "weak", from *dur-*, "ill", and *bala*, "strength" (CDIAL 6438). *patlā* is slim; *dublā* is thin from weakness.
