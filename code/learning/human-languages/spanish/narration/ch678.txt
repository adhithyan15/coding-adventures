Spanish, chapter 678: De visita.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
visitar — to visit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the palace, then the Spanish for the city wall.

You'll want to know: visitar.
visitar — "to visit".
Queremos visitar el museo mañana. We want to visit the museum tomorrow.
For people, use a: visitar a mis abuelos.

The word, taken apart.
visitar comes from Latin vīsitāre "to see, to go to see" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built on vīsere "to go and see", from vidēre "to see".

Lesson 2 of 5.
la ermita — the country chapel, the hermitage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the city wall, then the Spanish for to visit.

You'll want to know: la ermita.
la ermita — "the country chapel, the hermitage".
Subimos a la ermita del pueblo. We walked up to the village chapel.
Many villages walk to their ermita on a feast day.

The word, taken apart.
ermita goes back to Late Latin erēmīta "a hermit" (Lewis & Short), from Greek erēmítēs, built on érēmos "lonely, desert": the hermit lived alone.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la estatua — the statue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to visit, then the Spanish for the country chapel.

You'll want to know: la estatua.
la estatua — "the statue".
Hay una estatua en el centro de la plaza. There is a statue in the middle of the square.
Quedarse como una estatua — to stand stock-still.

The word, taken apart.
estatua is a learned borrowing from Latin statua "an image, a statue" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from statuere "to set up".

The word, taken apart.
torre is inherited, through Old Spanish torre, from Latin turris "a tower" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la galería.
la galería — "the gallery; the covered balcony".
La galería tiene cuadros modernos. The gallery has modern paintings.
Galerías comerciales — a shopping arcade.

The word, taken apart.
galería came from Italian galleria, a covered walk; where the word itself comes from is not settled.
