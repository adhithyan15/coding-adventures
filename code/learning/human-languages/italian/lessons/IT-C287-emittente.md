## You'll want to know: l'emittente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C287-DIRETTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'emittente** — "the broadcaster, the station".

> È un'emittente locale: trasmette solo in questa regione.
>
> *It is a local station: it only broadcasts in this region.*

Feminine: *un'emittente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emittente* is a learned word from Latin *ēmittēns*, the present participle of *ēmittere* "to send out" (Lewis & Short).
