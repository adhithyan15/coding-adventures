## You'll want to know: ಶಂಕಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C323-APARADHA323-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಂಕಿಸು** — *śaṅkisu* — "to suspect".

> ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಆ ಕೆಲಸಗಾರನನ್ನು ಶಂಕಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *polīsaru ā kelasagāranannu śaṅkisidaru.*
>
> The police suspected that worker.

Past (I …): ಶಂಕಿಸಿದೆ, *śaṅkiside*.

The person suspected takes -ಅನ್ನು. The noun is ಶಂಕೆ, a suspicion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಂಕಿಸು** (*śaṅkisu*) is built on Sanskrit *śaṅkā*, "fear, distrust, suspicion" (CDIAL 12258), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
