## You'll want to know: bǎozhòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C245-MASK245-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎozhòng* — "to look after yourself; to take care of oneself".

> *nǐ shēngbìng le yào bǎozhòng* — You're ill, so look after yourself.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎozhòng* is built from *bǎo* 'to keep' and 重 'heavy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX* and *drjowngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be responsible for' and 'to keep safe'.
