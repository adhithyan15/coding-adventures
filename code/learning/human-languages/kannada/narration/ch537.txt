Kannada, chapter 537: Poetry and Art.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕವಿ (kavi) — a poet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to perform, then the Kannada for to act.

You'll want to know: ಕವಿ.
ಕವಿ — kavi — "a poet".
ಆ ಕವಿ ಮಳೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಸುಂದರ ಹಾಡು ಬರೆದರು. ā kavi maḷeya bagge sundara hāḍu baredaru. That poet wrote a beautiful song about the rain.
Plural: ಕವಿಗಳು, kavigaḷu.
A woman poet is ಕವಯಿತ್ರಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ಕವಿ (kavi) is borrowed from Sanskrit kavi, "wise; a wise man, a poet" (CDIAL 2964).

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕಲಾಕೃತಿ (kalākṛti) — a work of art.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a poem, then the Kannada for literature.

You'll want to know: ಕಲಾಕೃತಿ.
ಕಲಾಕೃತಿ — kalākṛti — "a work of art".
ವಸ್ತುಸಂಗ್ರಹಾಲಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಹಳೆಯ ಕಲಾಕೃತಿಗಳು ಇವೆ. vastusaṅgrahālayadalli haḷeya kalākṛtigaḷu ive. There are old works of art in the museum.
Plural: ಕಲಾಕೃತಿಗಳು, kalākṛtigaḷu.
ಕಲೆ, art + ಕೃತಿ, a work.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಲಾಕೃತಿ (kalākṛti) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit kalā, "any practical art" (CDIAL 2927), and kṛti, "a work, a creation" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ರಚಿಸು (racisu) — to compose; to create.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for literature, then the Kannada for a work of art.

You'll want to know: ರಚಿಸು.
ರಚಿಸು — racisu — "to compose; to create".
ಈ ಹಾಡನ್ನು ಒಬ್ಬ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಕವಿ ರಚಿಸಿದರು. ī hāḍannu obba haḷḷiya kavi racisidaru. A village poet composed this song.
Past (I …): ರಚಿಸಿದೆ, raciside.
ರಚನೆ, a composition.

The word, taken apart.
ರಚಿಸು (racisu) is built on the Sanskrit root rac, "to arrange, to compose", seen in racanā (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.
