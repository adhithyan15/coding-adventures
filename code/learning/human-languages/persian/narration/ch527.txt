Persian, chapter 527: Talent and Masterpieces.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
استعداد (este'dâd) — talent, aptitude.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to design, then the Persian for to create.

You'll want to know: استعداد.
استعداد (este'dâd) — "talent, aptitude".
او در موسیقی استعداد زیادی دارد. u dar musiqi este'dâd-e ziyâdi dârad. He has a great talent for music.
dar chizi este'dâd dâshtan — to have a gift for something.

The word, taken apart.
este'dâd is a loan, usually traced to Arabic istiʿdād, 'readiness, aptitude'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

این کار خلاقیت زیادی میخواهد. in kâr khallâqiyat-e ziyâdi mikhâhad. This work takes a lot of creativity.
Built on khallâq 'creative'.

The word, taken apart.
khallâqiyat is built in Persian on khallâq, 'creative', a loan usually traced to Arabic ḫallāq, 'creator', with an abstract ending. EtymDB has no entry for it.

شاهکار (shâhkâr) — "a masterpiece".
این نقاشی شاهکار اوست. in naqqâshi shâhkâr-e ust. This painting is his masterpiece.
shâh 'king' + kâr 'work': the king of works.

The word, taken apart.
shâhkâr is built on two inherited words: shâh, 'king', and kâr, 'work', both of which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 6.
رقصنده (raqsande) — a dancer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for creativity, then the Persian for a masterpiece.

You'll want to know: رقصنده.
رقصنده (raqsande) — "a dancer".
رقصنده (raqsande) ها با لباس محلی روی صحنه آمدند. raqsandehâ bâ lebâs-e mahalli ru-ye sahne âmadand. The dancers came on stage in local dress.
From raqsidan 'to dance' (stem raqs-) + -ande 'one who'.

The word, taken apart.
raqsande is built in Persian on raqs, 'dance', a loan usually traced to Arabic raqṣ, with the native ending -ande, 'one who'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
تحسین کردن (tahsin kardan) — to admire, to praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a masterpiece, then the Persian for a dancer.

You'll want to know: تحسین کردن.
تحسین کردن (tahsin kardan) — "to admire, to praise".
همه کار او را تحسین کردند. hame kâr-e u râ tahsin kardand. Everyone admired her work.
tahsin 'admiration, praise' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
tahsin is a loan, usually traced to Arabic taḥsīn, 'finding something beautiful, praising'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited Persian.
