## You'll want to know: しんらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C343-SHINYOU343-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しんらい** — *shinrai* — "trust; confidence".

> **うそを 言ったら、しんらいを なくします。** — *uso o ittara, shinrai o nakushimasu* — "If you lie, you lose people's trust."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんらい** (*shinrai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "trust" (Middle Chinese *sinH*) and *rai*, "trust" (*lajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
