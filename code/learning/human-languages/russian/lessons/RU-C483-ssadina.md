## You'll want to know: ссадина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C483-SCRATCHES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ссадина** (*ssádina*) — "a graze".

> У него на колене большая ссадина.
>
> *U negó na koléne bolsháya ssádina.*
>
> He has a big graze on his knee.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ссадина* is usually linked to *ссадить* in the sense 'to scrape off skin'; EtymDB has no entry, so the link is not settled.
