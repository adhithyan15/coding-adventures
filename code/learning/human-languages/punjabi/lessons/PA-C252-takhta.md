## You'll want to know: ਤਖ਼ਤਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C252-GHR252-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਖ਼ਤਾ** — *takhtā* — "a wooden board".

> ਮੈਂ ਤਖ਼ਤੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਰੱਖਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ takhte utte bhāṇḍe rakkhdā hāṁ.*
>
> I keep the pots on a wooden board.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਖ਼ਤਾ** (*takhtā*) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian *takhta*, "a board, a plank".
