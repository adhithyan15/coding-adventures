## You'll want to know: もと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C511-CAUSE511-05]; assesses=[] -->

**もと** — *moto* — "the origin; the cause".

> **もし けんかの もとが わかったら、おしえて ください。** — *moshi kenka no moto ga wakattara, oshiete kudasai* — "If you find out what started the quarrel, please tell me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もと** (*moto*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
