## You'll want to know: mündlich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C375-KLAUSUR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mündlich** — "oral, spoken".

> Die mündliche Prüfung dauert fünfzehn Minuten.
>
> *The oral exam lasts fifteen minutes.*

*Mündlich* comes from *der Mund* — the mouth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mündlich* is built with *-lich* on *Mund* "mouth", inherited from Proto-Germanic *munþaz* (EtymDB): by mouth.
