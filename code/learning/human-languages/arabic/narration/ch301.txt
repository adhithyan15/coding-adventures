Arabic, chapter 301: An accident on the road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اصطدم (iṣṭadama) — to collide, to crash into.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to steal, then the Arabic for to arrest.

You'll want to know: اصطدم.
اصطدم — iṣṭadama — "to collide, to crash into".
اصطدمت السيارة بشجرة كبيرة. iṣṭadamat as-sayyāra bi-shajara kabīra. The car crashed into a big tree.
Present: يصطدم, yaṣṭadim.
It takes بـ for what is hit: اصطدم (iṣṭadama) بالجدار.

The word, taken apart.
اصطدم (iṣṭadama) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-d-m (Lane), "to strike, to knock against". It is Form VIII, where the t turns to ṭ after ṣ: "to strike against each other".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ضحية (ḍaḥiyya) — a victim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to collide, then the Arabic for to overturn.

You'll want to know: ضحية.
ضحية — ḍaḥiyya — "a victim".
كان الولد ضحية (ḍaḥiyya) الحادث. kāna al-walad ḍaḥiyyat al-ḥādith. The boy was the victim of the accident.
Plural: ضحايا, ḍaḥāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ضحية (ḍaḥiyya) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-ḥ-w (Lane), the root of the taught ḍuḥā, the bright late morning. Lane gives ḍaḥiyya first as an animal offered at that hour; today it is anyone who suffers harm.

جريح (jarīḥ) is built on the native Arabic root j-r-ḥ (Lane), "to wound", the root of the taught jurḥ, a wound. The faʿīl pattern gives the one who bears it.

The word, taken apart.
أسعف (asʿafa) is built on the native Arabic root s-ʿ-f (Lane), "to help, to give someone what he needs". It is Form IV; isʿāf is its verbal noun.
