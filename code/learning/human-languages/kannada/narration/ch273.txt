Kannada, chapter 273: To fly, To wander around, To cross, To drive, To slip.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಾರು (hāru) — to fly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to stir, then the Kannada for to roast.

You'll want to know: ಹಾರು.
ಹಾರು — hāru — "to fly".

The word, taken apart.
ಹಾರು (hāru) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil paṟa (DEDR 4020). DEDR lists older pāṟu, "to leap up, fly": the p- has become h-.
