## Script — a cliff over 白 and 小, then 心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YUAN-WISH-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 愿

**Four pieces: the cliff, then what it shelters, then 心 at the foot.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The cliff, two strokes**: a bar, then a long stroke falling to the left down from its left end.

**Under the cliff, 白, five strokes, then 小, three strokes**, as you wrote them: a short stroke falling to the left, a vertical, a bar that turns down, two short bars; then a vertical that hooks to the left, a short stroke on its left and a dot on its right.

**心, four strokes**, as you wrote it, across the foot: a dot, a long curve that hooks up, then two dots.

2 + 5 + 3 + 4 = fourteen.
