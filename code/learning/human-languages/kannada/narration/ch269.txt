Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ.
ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ — samasye — "a problem".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ (samasye) is Sanskrit samasyā, built on samasyati, "puts together, combines" (CDIAL 13197a), with the Kannada -e for final -ā: a problem is something to be put together.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸುದ್ದಿ (suddi) — news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a report, then the Kannada for a problem.

You'll want to know: ಸುದ್ದಿ.
ಸುದ್ದಿ — suddi — "news".

The word, taken apart.
ಸುದ್ದಿ (suddi) comes from Sanskrit śuddhi, "cleansing, purity; information, news", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 12523). Ś became s and ddh became dd.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಾಹಿತಿ (māhiti) — information.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a problem, then the Kannada for news.

You'll want to know: ಮಾಹಿತಿ.
ಮಾಹಿತಿ — māhiti — "information".

The word, taken apart.
Where ಮಾಹಿತಿ (māhiti) comes from is uncertain: DEDR and CDIAL do not list it. It is usually traced to Arabic māhiyat, "nature, state of affairs", through Persian and the languages to the north.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಅಂಕ (aṅka) — a mark, a score.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for news, then the Kannada for information.

You'll want to know: ಅಂಕ.
ಅಂಕ — aṅka — "a mark, a score".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂಕ (aṅka) is Sanskrit aṅka, "curved line, mark, sign", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 100). DEDR 29 has a Kannada aṅka, "fight", but that is a different word.
