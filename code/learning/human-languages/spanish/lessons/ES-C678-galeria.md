## You'll want to know: la galería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C678-VISITA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la galería** — "the gallery; the covered balcony".

> La galería tiene cuadros modernos.
>
> *The gallery has modern paintings.*

*Galerías comerciales* — a shopping arcade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*galería* came from Italian *galleria*, a covered walk; where the word itself comes from is not settled.
