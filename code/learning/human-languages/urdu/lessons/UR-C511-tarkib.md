## You'll want to know: ترکیب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C511-DASTARKHVAN511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ترکیب** — *tarkīb* — "a recipe; a method".

> یہ نانی کی ترکیب ہے۔
>
> *yih nānī kī tarkīb hai.*
>
> This is grandmother's recipe.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترکیب** (*tarkīb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tarkīb*, "putting together".
