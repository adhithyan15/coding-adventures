## You'll want to know: بدمعاش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C281-THAG281-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بدمعاش** — *badmāsh* — "a crook; a rascal".

> وہ آدمی بدمعاش ہے۔
>
> *voh ādmī badmāsh hai.*
>
> That man is a crook.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بدمعاش** (*badmāsh*) is usually traced to Persian *bad*, "bad", and Arabic *maʿāsh*, "a living": one who lives by bad means.
