Persian, chapter 466: Animals in the fields.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گله (galle) — a flock, a herd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a vineyard, then the Persian for to plough.

You'll want to know: گله.
گله (galle) — "a flock, a herd".
یک گله گوسفند از جاده رد شد. Yek galle gusfand az jâdde rad shod. A flock of sheep crossed the road.
Do not mix it up with gele 'a complaint', written the same way.

The word, taken apart.
galle 'herd' is a native word whose older form is not settled. EtymDB lists only its look-alike gele 'complaint'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
طویله (tavile) — a stable, a cowshed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a flock, then the Persian for a shepherd.

You'll want to know: طویله.
طویله (tavile) — "a stable, a cowshed".
اسبها شب در طویله میخوابند. Asb-hâ shab dar tavile mikhâband. The horses sleep in the stable at night.
For sheep the word is âghol.

The word, taken apart.
tavile was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic ṭawīla 'a long rope for tethering animals'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
چریدن (charidan) — to graze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a stable, then the Persian for a pasture.

You'll want to know: چریدن.
چریدن (charidan) — "to graze".
گاوها در دشت میچرند. Gâv-hâ dar dasht micharand. The cows are grazing on the plain.
Present stem char-: micharad. Charândan is to graze a flock.

The word, taken apart.
charidan is inherited: EtymDB traces it to the PIE root kʷel- 'to move, to turn (around)', as animals wander while they graze.
