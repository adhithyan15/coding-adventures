## You'll want to know: cantiō, cantiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C543-CANT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cantiō, cantiōnis** — "a song; a spell".

> Ancillae in culīnā cantiōnem canunt.
>
> *The maids sing a song in the kitchen.*

Feminine, third declension, from *canō*. Also a magic spell sung over someone. French *chanson* and Italian *canzone* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cantiō** is the action noun of *canō* "to sing" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). French *chanson* and Italian *canzone* come from it.
