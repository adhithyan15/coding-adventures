## You'll want to know: प्रमाणपत्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C371-PARIKSHA371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमाणपत्र** — *pramāṇpatra* — "a certificate".

> स्पर्धेनंतर सगळ्यांना प्रमाणपत्र मिळालं.
>
> *spardhenantar sagaḷyānnā pramāṇpatra miḷālaṁ.*
>
> After the competition everyone got a certificate.

Neuter.

Literally a letter of proof.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमाणपत्र** (*pramāṇpatra*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *pramāṇa*, "measure, proof" (CDIAL 8723), and *patra*, "leaf, letter".
