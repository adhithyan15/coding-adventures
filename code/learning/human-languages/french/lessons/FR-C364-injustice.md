## You'll want to know: l'injustice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C364-JUSTICE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'injustice** — "the injustice".

> L'injustice la met en colère.
>
> *Injustice makes her angry.*

Feminine: *une injustice*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*injustice* is a learned borrowing from Latin *iniūstitia* "injustice" (Lewis & Short), from *iniūstus* "unjust", built on *iūstus* "right, fair".

## What you've built
