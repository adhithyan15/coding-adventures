## You'll want to know: さいあく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C366-SAIAKU366-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さいあく** — *saiaku* — "the worst".

> **さいあく、でんしゃが とまったら、あるいて かえります。** — *saiaku, densha ga tomattara, aruite kaerimasu* — "If the worst comes to the worst and the trains stop, I will walk home."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さいあく** (*saiaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sai*, "utmost" (Middle Chinese *tswajH*) and *aku*, "bad" (*'ak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
