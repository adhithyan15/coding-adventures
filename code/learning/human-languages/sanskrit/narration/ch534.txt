Sanskrit, chapter 534: Coming to Terms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मध्यस्थः (madhyasthaḥ) — a go-between.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for moves out of the way, then the Sanskrit for trouble.

You'll want to know: मध्यस्थः.
मध्यस्थः — madhyasthaḥ — "a go-between".
यदि विवादः अस्ति, तर्हि मध्यस्थः समाधानं ददातु। yadi vivādaḥ asti, tarhi madhyasthaḥ samādhānaṃ dadātu. If there is a dispute, let a go-between offer a way out.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मध्यस्थः (madhyasthaḥ) is built from madhya, "the middle", and stha, "standing", from the root √sthā; MW gives madhyastha as standing between two parties, a mediator, and as impartial (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
संविद् (saṃvid) — an agreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for trouble, then the Sanskrit for a go-between.

You'll want to know: संविद्.
संविद् — saṃvid — "an agreement".
यदि भवान् तेन सह संविदं करोति, तर्हि कार्यं भविष्यति। yadi bhavān tena saha saṃvidaṃ karoti, tarhi kāryaṃ bhaviṣyati. If you come to an agreement with him, the work will get done.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
संविद् (saṃvid) is built on sam, "together", and the root √vid, "to know": a knowing together; MW gives saṃvid as consciousness, and as a mutual understanding, an agreement (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अंगीकरोति (aṅgīkaroti) — agrees to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a go-between, then the Sanskrit for an agreement.

You'll want to know: अंगीकरोति.
अंगीकरोति — aṅgīkaroti — "agrees to".
यदि सः मम योजनाम् अंगीकरोति, तर्हि वयं श्वः आरभामहे। yadi saḥ mama yojanām aṅgīkaroti, tarhi vayaṃ śvaḥ ārabhāmahe. If he agrees to my plan, we will start tomorrow.
Past (he …): सः योजनाम् अंगीकृतवान्, saḥ yojanām aṅgīkṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
अंगीकरोति (aṅgīkaroti) is built from aṅga, "a limb, a part", in the form aṅgī, and the root √kṛ, "to make": to make a thing part of oneself; MW gives aṅgīkṛ as to take the side of, to agree to (MW).

You'll want to know: प्रत्याख्याति.
प्रत्याख्याति — pratyākhyāti — "turns down".
यदि मूल्यम् उचितं न अस्ति, तर्हि अहं तत् प्रत्याख्यामि। yadi mūlyam ucitaṃ na asti, tarhi ahaṃ tat pratyākhyāmi. If the price is not fair, I turn it down.
Past (he …): सः निमन्त्रणं प्रत्याख्यातवान्, saḥ nimantraṇaṃ pratyākhyātavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रत्याख्याति (pratyākhyāti) is built on prati, "back", ā and the root √khyā, "to tell"; MW gives pratyākhyā as to refuse, to reject, to deny (MW).
