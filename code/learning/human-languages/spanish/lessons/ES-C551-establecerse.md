## You'll want to know: establecerse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C551-TRASLADO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**establecerse** — "to settle, to establish oneself".

> La familia se estableció en Argentina.
>
> *The family settled in Argentina.*

It takes a *z*: *me establezco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*establecerse* comes from spoken Latin *\*stabilīscere* (EtymDB), built on *stabilīre* 'to make firm' (Lewis & Short), from *stabilis* 'steady', built on *stāre* 'to stand'.
