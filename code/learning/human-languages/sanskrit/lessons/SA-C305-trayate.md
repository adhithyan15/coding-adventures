## You'll want to know: त्रायते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C305-UDDHARANA305-05]; assesses=[] -->

**त्रायते** — *trāyate* — "saves; protects".

> वैद्यः आहतं त्रायते।
>
> *vaidyaḥ āhataṃ trāyate.*
>
> The doctor saves the injured man.

Past (he …): सः आहतं त्रातवान्, *saḥ āhataṃ trātavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्रायते** (*trāyate*) comes from the root √trai, "to protect, preserve, rescue" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
