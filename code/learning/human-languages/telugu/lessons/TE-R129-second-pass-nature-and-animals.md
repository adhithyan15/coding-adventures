[PAUSE 3s] A pigeon? (**పావురం**, *pāvuraṁ*.) An owl? (**గుడ్లగూబ**, *guḍlagūba*.) A peacock? (**నెమలి**, *nemali*.) A butterfly? (**సీతాకోకచిలుక**, *sītākōkacilaka*.) A crow? (**కాకి**, *kāki*.) A duck? (**బాతు**, *bātu*.) A pig? (**పంది**, *pandi*.) A donkey? (**గాడిద**, *gāḍida*.) A camel? (**ఒంటె**, *oṇṭe*.) The face? (**ముఖం**, *mukhaṁ*.) The tongue? (**నాలుక**, *nāluka*.) A knee? (**మోకాలు**, *mōkālu*.) A shoulder? (**భుజం**, *bhujaṁ*.) An eyebrow? (**కనుబొమ్మ**, *kanubomma*.) A cheek? (**బుగ్గ**, *bugga*.) The chin; a beard? (**గడ్డం**, *gaḍḍaṁ*.) A wrist? (**మణికట్టు**, *maṇikaṭṭu*.) An elbow? (**మోచేయి**, *mōcēyi*.) The skin? (**చర్మం**, *carmaṁ*.) Blood? (**రక్తం**, *raktaṁ*.) A window curtain? (**కిటికీతెర**, *kiṭikītera*.) A clay pot? (**చట్టి**, *caṭṭi*.) A water pot? (**బిందె**, *bindē*.) A cupboard? (**బీరువా**, *bīruvā*.) A table? (**టేబుల్**, *ṭēbul*.)
