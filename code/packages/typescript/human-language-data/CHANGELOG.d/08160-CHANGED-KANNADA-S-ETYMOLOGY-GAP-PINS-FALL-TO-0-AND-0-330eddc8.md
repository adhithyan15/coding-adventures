### Changed — Kannada's etymology gap pins fall to 0 and 0

`ETYMOLOGY_GAP_PINS.kannada` in `tests/root-ledger.test.ts` is lowered. Word lessons without an `etymology_hook` fall from 1330 to **0**. Lessons without visible etymology fall from 1281 to **0**. Every Kannada lesson that introduces a word now says where that word comes from (HL24 §9).

- **Sources.** Native words cite the *Dravidian Etymological Dictionary* (Burrow & Emeneau, DEDR). Sanskrit tatsamas and tadbhavas cite Turner's CDIAL. Both are cited by entry number and were read locally from the moli-mandala/data (Jambu) files; DEDR was read from the full article texts. A script checked every cited number and every cited Kannada form and cognate against those files. It also checked that every hedge in a cited article ("?", "probably", "Cf. Skt.", "< IA") survives into the lesson. No dataset file is vendored, and no source text is copied: each statement is our own sentence.
- **Shape.** 1,280 lessons gained a `## The word, taken apart` block (or `## The phrase, taken apart`) after the introducing block. Each also has a matching `etymology_hook` of 120 characters or fewer. The 64 lessons whose body already stated an origin gained the hook and, where needed, one sentence in an existing section. KA-C06-dative-subject's hook, a grammar note, became an etymology.
- **Answers.** 644 native Dravidian, 381 Sanskrit, 79 English, 69 Persian or Arabic through Hindustani, 4 DEDR appendix loans, 3 other, 97 built from parts of mixed origin, and 68 uncertain. An uncertain one is marked only after both sources were searched by form and by gloss, and it gives the usual account as a hedge where there is one. The added text has 1,864 DEDR citations and 777 CDIAL citations.
- **Ledger side effects.** 1,283 Kannada lessons gained `roots:` slugs: 1,150 distinct slugs, 70 reused and 1,080 new. `check:root-slug-splits` reports no new split. Twenty-three lessons had `duration.max_seconds` raised to their computed length, all under 300 s. The Kannada changelog lists them.
- **No code changed.** Only the pin and its comment moved.
