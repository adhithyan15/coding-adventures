## You'll want to know: सोधणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C220-PLAN220-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सोधणो** — *sodhṇo* — "to find out; to search out".

> हूं डूंगर कनै नुवो गेलो सोधसूं।
>
> *hū̃ ḍū̃gar kanai nuvo gelo sodhsū̃.*
>
> I'll search out a new path near the hill.

Past (I …): म्हैं गेलो सोध्यो, *mhaĩ gelo sodhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सोधणो** (*sodhṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śoddhum*, "to purify"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *sodhaï*, "finds out", and a Gujarati verb "to search" under it (CDIAL 12626).
