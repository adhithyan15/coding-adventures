## You'll want to know: مفت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C425-TARAZU425-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مفت** — *muft* — "free of charge".

> دو پیکٹ خریدیے، ایک مفت ہے۔
>
> *do paikaṭ kharīdiye, ek muft hai.*
>
> Buy two packets and you get one free.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مفت** (*muft*) is usually traced to Persian *muft*, "free, for nothing".
