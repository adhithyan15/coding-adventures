## You'll want to know: कोसना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C336-SHAAP336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कोसना** — *kosnā* — "to curse; to rail at".

> बूढ़ी औरत चोर को कोस रही थी।
>
> *būṛhī aurat cor ko kos rahī thī.*
>
> The old woman was cursing the thief.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चोर को कोसा, *usne cor ko kosā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोसना** (*kosnā*) goes back to Sanskrit *krośati*, "cries out" (CDIAL 3612). It is everyday cursing in words, not a sage's **शाप**.
