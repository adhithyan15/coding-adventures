## You'll want to know: نوجوانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C333-NOWJAVANI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نوجوانی** (*nowjavâni*) — "adolescence, the teenage years".

> در نوجوانی فوتبال بازی میکردم.
>
> *Dar nowjavâni futbâl bâzi mikardam.*
>
> In my teens I used to play football.

From *nowjavân* "a teenager": *now* "new" + *javân* "young". The past continuous tells what you used to do.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nowjavâni* is built on *nowjavân* "a teenager", from *now* "new" and *javân* "young", with the noun suffix *-i*. All of it is native Persian.
