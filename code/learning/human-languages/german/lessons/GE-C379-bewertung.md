## You'll want to know: die Bewertung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C379-BEWERTUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Bewertung** — "the evaluation, the rating; the review".

> Das Restaurant hat im Internet sehr gute Bewertungen.
>
> *The restaurant has very good reviews online.*

Plural: *die Bewertungen*. At school, the marks and the comments together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bewertung* is the noun of *bewerten*, built on *Wert* "worth", Proto-Germanic *werþaz* (EtymDB), with *-ung*.
