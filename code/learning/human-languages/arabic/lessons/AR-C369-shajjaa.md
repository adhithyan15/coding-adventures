## You'll want to know: شجع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C369-NASIHA369-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شجع** — *shajjaʿa* — "to encourage".

> شجعني أبي على تعلم الرسم.
>
> *shajjaʿanī abī ʿalā taʿallum ar-rasm.*
>
> My father encouraged me to learn drawing.

Present: يشجع, *yushajjiʿ*.

It takes على: شجعه على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شجع** (*shajjaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-j-ʿ*, "to be brave" (Lane). It is Form II, to make brave: to encourage.
