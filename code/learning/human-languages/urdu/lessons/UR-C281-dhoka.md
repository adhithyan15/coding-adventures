## You'll want to know: دھوکا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C281-THAG281-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دھوکا** — *dhokā* — "a deception; a trick".

> اگر یہ دھوکا ہے تو پولیس بلائیے۔
>
> *agar yih dhokā hai to polīs bulāiye.*
>
> If this is a trick, call the police.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دھوکا** (*dhokā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *dhokā*, "deceit", under *\*dhrōkṣa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6894). **دھوکا دینا** is to deceive someone.
