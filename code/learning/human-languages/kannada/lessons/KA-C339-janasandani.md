# ಜನಸಂದಣಿ (janasandaṇi) — a crowd; a throng

## You'll want to know: ಜನಸಂದಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C339-GALABHE339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜನಸಂದಣಿ** — *janasandaṇi* — "a crowd; a throng".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ದಿನ ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನದ ಮುಂದೆ ಜನಸಂದಣಿ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *habbada dina dēvasthānada munde janasandaṇi ittu.*
>
> On the festival day there was a crowd in front of the temple.

ಜನ, people, and ಸಂದಣಿ, crowding: a press of people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜನಸಂದಣಿ** (*janasandaṇi*) is a Kannada compound of ಜನ, "people", from Sanskrit *jana* (Monier-Williams), and ಸಂದಣಿ, "crowding"; the origin of ಸಂದಣಿ is not settled.
