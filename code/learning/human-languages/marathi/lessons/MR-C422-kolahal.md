## You'll want to know: कोलाहल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C422-MANDAI422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कोलाहल** — *kolāhal* — "an uproar".

> बातमी ऐकून सभेत कोलाहल माजला.
>
> *bātmī aikūn sabhet kolāhal mājlā.*
>
> Hearing the news, an uproar broke out in the meeting.

Masculine.

A literary word; कोलाहल माजणे, an uproar breaks out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोलाहल** (*kolāhal*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kolāhala*, "a great din" (CDIAL 3534), where CDIAL also lists the inherited Marathi *kolhāḷ*.
