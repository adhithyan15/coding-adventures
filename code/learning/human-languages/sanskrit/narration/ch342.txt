Sanskrit, chapter 342: On What Grounds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आधारः (ādhāraḥ) — a basis; a support.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for urges on, then the Sanskrit for has something done.

You'll want to know: आधारः.
आधारः — ādhāraḥ — "a basis; a support".
तव मतस्य आधारः कः? tava matasya ādhāraḥ kaḥ? What is the basis of your opinion?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आधारः (ādhāraḥ) is built on ā and the root √dhṛ, "to hold, to support"; MW gives ā-dhāra the senses "support, prop, substratum" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अर्थात् (arthāt) — that is to say.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a basis, then the Sanskrit for a line of reasoning.

You'll want to know: अर्थात्.
अर्थात् — arthāt — "that is to say".
सः कृपणः अस्ति, अर्थात् धनं न ददाति। saḥ kṛpaṇaḥ asti, arthāt dhanaṃ na dadāti. He is stingy, that is to say he gives no money.

The word, taken apart.
अर्थात् (arthāt) is built on artha, "aim, meaning", in its ablative; MW gives arthāt "as a matter of fact" and "according to the sense, that is to say" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
फलतः (phalataḥ) — as a result.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a line of reasoning, then the Sanskrit for that is to say.

You'll want to know: फलतः.
फलतः — phalataḥ — "as a result".
सः बहु पठितवान्, फलतः परीक्षायां प्रथमः अभवत्। saḥ bahu paṭhitavān, phalataḥ parīkṣāyāṃ prathamaḥ abhavat. He studied hard; as a result he came first in the exam.

The word, taken apart.
फलतः (phalataḥ) is built on phala, "fruit, result", with the ending -tas, "from"; MW gives phala-tas "in relation to the reward or result" and "consequently, accordingly" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for that is to say, then the Sanskrit for as a result.

You'll want to know: उपपादयति.
उपपादयति — upapādayati — "demonstrates; argues for".
अधिवक्ता प्रमाणेन पक्षम् उपपादयति। adhivaktā pramāṇena pakṣam upapādayati. The lawyer argues his side with evidence.
Past (he …): सः मतम् उपपादितवान्, saḥ matam upapāditavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपपादयति (upapādayati) is built on upa, "towards", and the root √pad, "to go", in its causative form; MW gives upa-√pad the senses "to accomplish, to produce" and "to prove, to justify" (MW).
