# అభ్యంతరం (abhyantaraṁ) — an objection

## You'll want to know: అభ్యంతరం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C386-ABHYANTARAM386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అభ్యంతరం** — *abhyantaraṁ* — "an objection".

> మీకు ఏమైనా అభ్యంతరం ఉందా?
>
> *mīku ēmainā abhyantaraṁ undā?*
>
> Do you have any objection?

Plural: అభ్యంతరాలు, *abhyantarālu*.

A polite opening: మీకు అభ్యంతరం లేకపోతే, if you have no objection.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అభ్యంతరం** (*abhyantaraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *abhyantara*, "interior", and as a noun "the inside" (CDIAL 544). In Telugu the word has taken the sense of an objection.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
