## You'll want to know: شوخ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C488-CHARACTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شوخ** (*shukh*) — "witty, playful".

> آموزگار ما خیلی شوخ است و کلاس را شاد میکند.
>
> *âmuzgâr-e mâ kheyli shukh ast va kelâs râ shâd mikonad.*
>
> Our teacher is very witty and cheers up the class.

*shukhi* 'a joke' and *shukhi kardan* 'to joke' are built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shukh* is usually taken as a native Persian word, and *shukhi*, 'a joke', is built on it. No source used here gives its older form, and its origin is not settled.
