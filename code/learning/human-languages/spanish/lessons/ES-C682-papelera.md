## You'll want to know: la papelera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C682-CALLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la papelera** — "the litter bin, the wastepaper basket".

> Tira el papel a la papelera.
>
> *Throw the paper in the bin.*

For paper and small rubbish; *la basura* is the rubbish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*papelera* is built on *papel* "paper", which came from Catalan *paper*, from Latin *papyrus*, Greek *pápyros* (EtymDB).
