## You'll want to know: पछताना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C336-SHAAP336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पछताना** — *pachtānā* — "to regret; to repent".

> शाप के बाद राजा बहुत पछताया।
>
> *śāp ke bād rājā bahut pachtāyā.*
>
> After the curse the king bitterly regretted what he had done.

Past (he …): वह पछताया, *vah pachtāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पछताना** (*pachtānā*) is inherited from *\*paścōttāpa*, "repentance", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 8010): a burning that comes after, from *paścā*, behind, and *tāpa*, heat.
