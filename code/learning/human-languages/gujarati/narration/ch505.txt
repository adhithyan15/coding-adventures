Gujarati, chapter 505: Cotton and the Spinning Wheel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કપાસ (kapās) — the cotton plant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to knead clay, then the Gujarati for a clay lamp.

You'll want to know: કપાસ.
કપાસ — kapās — "the cotton plant".
અમારા ગામનાં ખેતરોમાં ઘણો કપાસ ઊગે છે. amārā gāmnā̃ khetromā̃ ghaṇo kapās ūge che. A lot of cotton grows in our village's fields.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કપાસ (kapās) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit karpāsa, "the cotton plant", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 2877).

Lesson 2 of 5.
રૂ (rū) — raw cotton (for spinning).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a clay lamp, then the Gujarati for the cotton plant.

You'll want to know: રૂ.
રૂ — rū — "raw cotton (for spinning)".
મારી દાદી રૂમાંથી દોરો બનાવતી હતી. mārī dādī rūmā̃thī doro banāvtī hatī. My grandmother used to make thread from cotton.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
રૂ (rū) comes from rū-a-, "cotton", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as carded cotton (CDIAL 10798).

Lesson 3 of 5.
રેંટિયો (rẽṭiyo) — a spinning wheel.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: રેંટિયો.
રેંટિયો — rẽṭiyo — "a spinning wheel".
દાદીનો રેંટિયો હજી અમારા ઘરમાં છે. dādīno rẽṭiyo hajī amārā gharmā̃ che. Grandmother's spinning wheel is still in our house.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રેંટિયો (rẽṭiyo) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit araghaṭṭa, "a wheel for raising water", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as a spinning wheel (CDIAL 596): one turning wheel gave its name to another.

દાદી રેંટિયા પર રૂ કાંતતી હતી. dādī rẽṭiyā par rū kā̃ttī hatī. Grandmother used to spin cotton on the spinning wheel.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દોરો કાંત્યો, teṇe doro kā̃tyo.

The word, taken apart.
કાંતવું (kā̃tvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit kṛntati, "spins", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 3433).
