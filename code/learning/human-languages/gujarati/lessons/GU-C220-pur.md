## You'll want to know: પૂર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C220-RIVER220-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂર** — *pūr* — "flood".

> **નદીમાં પૂર આવશે.** — *nadīmā̃ pūr āvśe* — "The river will flood."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂર** (*pūr*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pūra*, "filling", which already meant "flood" in the medical texts (CDIAL 8330).
