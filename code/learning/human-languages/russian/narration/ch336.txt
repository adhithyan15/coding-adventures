Lesson 1 of 7.
добавлять (dobavlyát) — to add.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to melt, then the Russian for to turn over.

You'll want to know: добавлять.
добавлять (dobavlyát) — "to add".
В суп я добавил немного соли. V sup ya dobávil nemnógo sóli. I added a little salt to the soup.
Imperfective; perfective добавить (dobávit), добавлю. It takes the accusative, or the genitive for "some": добавить сахару.

The word, taken apart.
добавлять (dobavlyát) is built on до- and an old verb бавить "to make, to keep busy", as in забава "amusement"; the link is usually traced, and EtymDB lists no parent.

You'll want to know: выкладывать.
выкладывать (vykládyvat) — "to lay out, to put out".
Она выложила печенье на тарелку. Oná výlozhila pechénye na tarélku. She put the biscuits out on a plate.
Imperfective; perfective выложить (výlozhit). In speech also "to tell everything": выложить всё (výlozhit vsyo).

The word, taken apart.
выкладывать (vykládyvat) is built on вы- "out" and the stem клад- of класть "to put", an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists no parent.

You'll want to know: варёный.
варёный (varyónyy) — "boiled".
На завтрак он съел варёное яйцо. Na závtrak on syel varyónoye yaytsó. He had a boiled egg for breakfast.
Варёная колбаса (Varyónaya kolbasá) — a soft boiled sausage.

The word, taken apart.
варёный (varyónyy) is the old participle of варить "to boil", inherited from Proto-Slavic variti (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 7.
жареный (zhárenyy) — fried, roast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to lay out, then the Russian for boiled.

You'll want to know: жареный.
жареный (zhárenyy) — "fried, roast".
На ужин была жареная рыба. Na úzhin bylá zhárenaya rýba. There was fried fish for dinner.
One н, as a plain adjective; жаренный на масле takes two.

The word, taken apart.
жареный (zhárenyy) is the old participle of жарить "to fry", from жар "heat", the same Slavic root as гореть "to burn"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 7.

You'll want to know: вынимать.
вынимать (vynimát) — "to take out".
Мама вынула пирог из духовки. Máma výnula piróg iz dukhóvki. Mum took the pie out of the oven.
Imperfective; perfective вынуть (výnut), выну.

The word, taken apart.
вынимать (vynimát) is built on вы- "out" and the old Slavic root -ним-, -ять "to take", seen in взять and понимать; EtymDB traces this root to PIE h₁em-.
