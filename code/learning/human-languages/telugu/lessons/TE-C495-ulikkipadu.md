# ఉలిక్కిపడు (ulikkipaḍu) — to be startled; to jump with fright

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C495-BHAYAM495-01, TE-LEX-C495-BHAYAM495-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be afraid, then the Telugu for to frighten.

## You'll want to know: ఉలిక్కిపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C495-BHAYAM495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉలిక్కిపడు** — *ulikkipaḍu* — "to be startled; to jump with fright".

> తలుపు చప్పుడు విని ఆమె ఉలిక్కిపడింది.
>
> *talupu cappuḍu vini āme ulikkipaḍindi.*
>
> Hearing the knock at the door, she started.

Past (I …): ఉలిక్కిపడ్డాను, *ulikkipaḍḍānu*.

The cause often takes -కి: చప్పుడుకి ఉలిక్కిపడు, to start at a noise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉలిక్కిపడు** (*ulikkipaḍu*) ends in the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853); the origin of the first part, *ulikki*, is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
