## You'll want to know: じゅうぎょういん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C487-SHOP487-05]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうぎょういん** — *jūgyōin* — "an employee; a worker".

> **この みせには じゅうぎょういんが ごにん います。** — *kono mise ni wa jūgyōin ga gonin imasu* — "This shop has five employees."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうぎょういん** (*jūgyōin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jū*, "accompany" (Middle Chinese *dzjowng*), *gyō*, "business" (*ngjaep*) and *in*, "employee" (*hjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
