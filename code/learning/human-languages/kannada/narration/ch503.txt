Kannada, chapter 503: Quick Minds and Slow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚುರುಕು (curuku) — quick; brisk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to take note, then the Kannada for to react.

You'll want to know: ಚುರುಕು.
ಚುರುಕು — curuku — "quick; brisk".
ಆ ಹುಡುಗಿ ಓದಿನಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಚುರುಕು. ā huḍugi ōdinalli tumbā curuku. That girl is very quick at her studies.
ಚುರುಕಾಗಿ, briskly. Also used of a sharp sun: ಚುರುಕು (curuku) ಬಿಸಿಲು.

The word, taken apart.
ಚುರುಕು (curuku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2713 groups Kannada cuṟuku with Tamil cuṟukku, "quickness, briskness, keenness".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ (pratibhe) — talent; flair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to react, then the Kannada for quick.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ.
ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ — pratibhe — "talent; flair".
ಆ ಹುಡುಗನಿಗೆ ಚಿತ್ರ ಬಿಡಿಸುವ ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ ಇದೆ. ā huḍuganige citra biḍisuva pratibhe ide. That boy has a talent for painting.
ಪ್ರತಿಭಾವಂತ, a talented person.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ (pratibhe) is borrowed from Sanskrit pratibhā, "light, splendour; intelligence, understanding" (Monier-Williams). The final -ā became -ೆ.

ದಡ್ಡ — daḍḍa — "slow-witted; dull".
ಗಣಿತದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ದಡ್ಡ. gaṇitadalli nānu svalpa daḍḍa. I am a bit slow at mathematics.
It can sting; ಜಾಣ is the opposite.

The word, taken apart.
ದಡ್ಡ (daḍḍa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2314 lists Kannada daḍḍa, "a dull, ignorant man", beside Kodagu daḍḍë, "a dull man".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮರೆವು (marevu) — forgetfulness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for talent, then the Kannada for slow-witted.

You'll want to know: ಮರೆವು.
ಮರೆವು — marevu — "forgetfulness".
ವಯಸ್ಸಾದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಜ್ಜನಿಗೆ ಮರೆವು ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗಿದೆ. vayassāda mēle ajjanige marevu heccāgide. With age, grandfather's forgetfulness has grown.
The verb is ಮರೆ, to forget; ಮರೆಯಬೇಡಿ, please do not forget.

The word, taken apart.
ಮರೆವು (marevu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4760 lists Kannada maṟe, "to forget, be forgotten"; -vu makes the noun.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಏಕಾಗ್ರತೆ (ēkāgrate) — concentration; focus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for slow-witted, then the Kannada for forgetfulness.

You'll want to know: ಏಕಾಗ್ರತೆ.
ಏಕಾಗ್ರತೆ — ēkāgrate — "concentration; focus".
ಓದುವಾಗ ನನಗೆ ಏಕಾಗ್ರತೆ ಬೇಕು. ōduvāga nanage ēkāgrate bēku. I need concentration while I study.
ಏಕ, one + ಅಗ್ರ, point: a mind on one point.

The word, taken apart.
ಏಕಾಗ್ರತೆ (ēkāgrate) is borrowed from Sanskrit ekāgratā, "intentness in the pursuit of one object" (Monier-Williams).
