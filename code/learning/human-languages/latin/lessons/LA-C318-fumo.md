## You'll want to know: fūmō, fūmāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C318-CREMO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fūmō, fūmāre** — "to smoke".

> Post incendium ruīnae diū fūmābant.
>
> *After the fire the ruins smoked for a long time.*

*fūmō, fūmāre, fūmāvī*, from *fūmus*, 'smoke' (taught). English *fume*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fūmō** is built on *fūmus*, "smoke" (EtymDB). English *fume* comes from the same family through French.
