## You'll want to know: لحق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C347-KHATF347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لحق** — *laḥiqa* — "to catch up with".

> لحق الشرطي باللص في الشارع.
>
> *laḥiqa ash-shurṭī bi-l-liṣṣ fī ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> The policeman caught up with the thief in the street.

Present: يلحق, *yalḥaq*.

لحق بالقطار: he caught the train.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لحق** (*laḥiqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-ḥ-q* (Lane), "to overtake, to come up with", the root of the taught *lāḥiqan*, later.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
