## You'll want to know: die Prellung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C482-VERLETZUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Prellung** — "bruise".

> Nach dem Sturz hatte er eine Prellung am Bein.
>
> *After the fall he had a bruise on his leg.*

Plural: *die Prellungen*. In speech also *der blaue Fleck*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Prellung* is built on *prellen* "to strike, to bump" (EtymDB) with *-ung*; the older origin of *prellen* is not settled.
