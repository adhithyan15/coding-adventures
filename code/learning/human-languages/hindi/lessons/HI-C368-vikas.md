## You'll want to know: विकास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C368-VIKAS368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विकास** — *vikās* — "development".

> गाँव के विकास के लिए सड़क ज़रूरी है।
>
> *gā̃v ke vikās ke liye saṛak zarūrī hai.*
>
> A road is necessary for the village's development.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विकास** (*vikās*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vikāsa*, "opening, expanding" (Monier-Williams).
