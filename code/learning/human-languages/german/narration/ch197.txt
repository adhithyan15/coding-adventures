Lesson 1 of 5.
die Küste — a coast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a meadow, then the German for a valley.

You'll want to know: die Küste.
die Küste — "a coast".

The word, taken apart.
Küste came through Low German and Dutch from Old French coste, "side, coast", from Latin costa, "rib, side" — borrowed, like English coast. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for the German word.

You'll want to know: der Nebel.

The word, taken apart.
Nebel from Old High German nebul, "cloud, fog" from Proto-Germanic nebulaz — inherited, a cousin of Latin nebula, "mist". (EtymDB 2.1).
