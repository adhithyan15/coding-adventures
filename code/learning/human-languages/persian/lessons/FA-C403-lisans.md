## You'll want to know: لیسانس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C403-TAHSILAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لیسانس** (*lisâns*) — "a bachelor's degree".

> او لیسانس زبان انگلیسی دارد.
>
> *u lisâns-e zabân-e engelisi dârad.*
>
> She has a bachelor's degree in English.

A French loan; the official term is *kârshenâsi*. *Fowq-e lisâns* — a master's degree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لیسانس** is usually traced to French *licence*, a university degree, from Latin *licentia* "freedom, leave" (Lewis & Short).
