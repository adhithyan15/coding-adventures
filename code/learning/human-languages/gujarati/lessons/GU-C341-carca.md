## You'll want to know: ચર્ચા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C341-DALIL341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચર્ચા** — *carcā* — "a discussion".

> બેઠકમાં નવા નિયમ પર લાંબી ચર્ચા થઈ.
>
> *beṭhakmā̃ navā niyam par lā̃bī carcā thaī.*
>
> There was a long discussion about the new rule at the meeting.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચર્ચા** (*carcā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *carcā*, "repetition, going over a thing again" (Monier-Williams); "a discussion" is the sense Gujarati uses.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
