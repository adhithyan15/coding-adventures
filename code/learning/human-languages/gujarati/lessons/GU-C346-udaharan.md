## You'll want to know: ઉદાહરણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C346-DAKHLO346-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદાહરણ** — *udāharaṇ* — "an instance".

> ગુરુએ પાઠ સમજાવવા માટે એક ઉદાહરણ આપ્યું.
>
> *gurue pāṭh samjāvvā māṭe ek udāharaṇ āpyũ.*
>
> The teacher gave an instance to explain the lesson.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદાહરણ** (*udāharaṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *udāharaṇa*, "relating, declaring" (Monier-Williams), and so a case cited to make a point.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
