# ફ — the consonant pha

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PA-01, GU-SCRIPT-MA-01] -->

- [YOU READ: **પ**, then **મ**]

Both are already yours. Today adds one shape, and it is the only new letter in
this whole run of chapters — everything else ahead is spelled with signs you
have had for a long time.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-PHA-01]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PA-01] -->

Keep **ફ** visible beside **પ**. They are relatives and they are not the same:
*pa* is the plain sound, *pha* is the same sound with a push of breath behind
it. The shape says so too — **ફ** carries an extra stroke that **પ** does not.

Set them side by side once and look at the difference rather than at the whole:

> **પ** — pa · **ફ** — pha

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ફ** once, then trace it once in the air. Say *pha* with the
breath audible as your finger finishes.

## Writing — guided copy, model in view
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep the model in view and copy **ફ** once. Compare it against **પ** beside it
and repair only the stroke that separates them. Do not start a row.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-PA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which of **પ** and **ફ** carries the extra breath, and how does the
written shape tell you? (**ફ** — it carries the extra stroke.) The next lesson
is the word this letter was bought for.
