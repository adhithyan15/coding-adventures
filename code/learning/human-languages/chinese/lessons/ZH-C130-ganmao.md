## You'll want to know: gǎnmào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C130-ILL130-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnmào* — "to catch a cold; a cold".

> *wǒ gǎnmào le* — I've caught a cold.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnmào* is built from *gǎn* and *mào*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *komX* and *mawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to move' and the second 'to cover' and 'to wade'; how the pair came to mean a cold is not settled.
