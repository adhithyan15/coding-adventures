# પોતું (potũ) — floor cloth, mop

## You'll want to know: પોતું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C158-SWEEP158-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પોતું** — *potũ* — "floor cloth, mop".

> **તે સવારે પોતું કરે છે.** — *te savāre potũ kare che* — "He mops the floor in the morning."

**પોતું કરવું**, *potũ karvũ*, to mop the floor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પોતું** (*potũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*pōtta*, "cloth", a reconstructed by-form of Sanskrit *pōta*, "cloth", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "rag" (CDIAL 8400).
