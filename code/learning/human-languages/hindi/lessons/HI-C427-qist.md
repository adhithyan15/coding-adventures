## You'll want to know: क़िस्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C427-UDHAR427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**क़िस्त** — *qist* — "an instalment".

> हम हर हफ़्ते एक क़िस्त देते हैं।
>
> *ham har hafte ek qist dete haiṁ.*
>
> We pay one instalment every week.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़िस्त** (*qist*) is usually traced to Arabic *qisṭ*, "a portion, a share", through Persian: a debt paid in portions.
