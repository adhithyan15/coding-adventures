Lesson 3 of 12.
Sibling retrieval — brother and sister — one secure English cousin and one unrelated root.

Warm-up.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say brother, sister: भाऊ, बहीण.

The two words and their histories.
भाऊ is a secure cousin of English brother. Do not extend that claim to बहीण and English sister; those roots are unrelated.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
Answer once, then repair only the missing piece.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
