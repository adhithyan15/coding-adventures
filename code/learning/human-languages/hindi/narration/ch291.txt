Hindi, chapter 291: What the Witness Saw.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चश्मदीद (caśmdīd) — an eyewitness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a police inspector, then the Hindi for a lock-up.

You'll want to know: चश्मदीद.
चश्मदीद — caśmdīd — "an eyewitness".
अगर कोई चश्मदीद होता तो पुलिस का काम आसान होता। agar koī caśmdīd hotā to pulis kā kām āsān hotā. If there had been an eyewitness, the police's job would have been easy.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
चश्मदीद (caśmdīd) is usually traced to Persian čašm-dīd, built from čašm, "eye", the word behind चश्मा, and dīd, "seen": a person who saw it with their own eyes.

बयान (bayān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic bayān, "an explanation, a making clear". बयान देना is to give a statement.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पहचानना (pahcānnā) — to recognize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an eyewitness, then the Hindi for a statement.

You'll want to know: पहचानना.
पहचानना — pahcānnā — "to recognize".
मीरा ने चोर को पहचान लिया। Mīrā ne cor ko pahcān liyā. Mira recognized the thief.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चोर को पहचाना, usne cor ko pahcānā.

The word, taken apart.
पहचानना (pahcānnā) goes back to Sanskrit pratyabhijānāti, "recognizes" (CDIAL 8637). The noun पहचान, identity or recognition, belongs with it; the verb is what you do when a face is one you know.

Masculine.

शक (śak) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic shakk, "doubt". The person you suspect takes पर: मुझे उस पर शक है.

Lesson 5 of 5.
घूरना (ghūrnā) — to stare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to recognize, then the Hindi for suspicion.

You'll want to know: घूरना.
घूरना — ghūrnā — "to stare".
वह आदमी मुझे घूर रहा था। vah ādmī mujhe ghūr rahā thā. That man was staring at me.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे घूरा, usne mujhe ghūrā.

The word, taken apart.
घूरना (ghūrnā) comes from ghūra, a frown, a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4496). It is a hard, long look, never a friendly one.
