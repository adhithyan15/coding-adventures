## You'll want to know: wénzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C242-FARMANIMAL242-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wénzi* — "mosquito".

> *xiàtiān zhèr yǒu wénzi* — There are mosquitoes here in summer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wénzi* is built from *wén* 'mosquito' and the noun ending 子. The noun ending is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the first, *mjun* there, beside another form of the character.
