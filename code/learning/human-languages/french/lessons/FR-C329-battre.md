## You'll want to know: battre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C329-FOUETTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**battre** — "to beat".

> Elle bat les œufs avec une fourchette.
>
> *She beats the eggs with a fork.*

*Battre les blancs en neige* — to beat egg whites until stiff. Also to beat someone at a game.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*battre* is inherited from Late Latin *battere* "to beat" (EtymDB), from Latin *battuere* (Lewis & Short).
