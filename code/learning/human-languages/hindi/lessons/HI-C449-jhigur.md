## You'll want to know: झींगुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C449-SIYAR449-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झींगुर** — *jhī̃gur* — "a cricket (insect)".

> रात को झींगुर बहुत शोर करते हैं।
>
> *rāt ko jhī̃gur bahut śor karte haiṁ.*
>
> Crickets make a lot of noise at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झींगुर** (*jhī̃gur*) goes back to *\*jhiṅgura*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5387) and glosses as a cockroach; it lists Hindi *jhĩgur*, "a cricket".
