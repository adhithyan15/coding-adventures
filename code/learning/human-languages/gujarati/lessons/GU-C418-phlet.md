# ફ્લેટ (phleṭ) — a flat (an apartment)

## You'll want to know: ફ્લેટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C418-SOCIETY418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફ્લેટ** — *phleṭ* — "a flat (an apartment)".

> તેમનો ફ્લેટ ત્રીજા માળે છે.
>
> *temno phleṭ trījā māḷe che.*
>
> Their flat is on the third floor.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફ્લેટ** (*phleṭ*) is borrowed from English *flat*, a home on one floor of a building.
