Malayalam, chapter 180: A Timetable, a Notice, the Rent, an Advert, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
സമയപ്പട്ടിക (samayappaṭṭika) — a timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to decide, then the Malayalam for to expect.

You'll want to know: സമയപ്പട്ടിക.
സമയപ്പട്ടിക — samayappaṭṭika — "a timetable". Read the board: ബസ്: രാവിലെ എട്ട് മണി — basŭ: rāvile eṭṭŭ maṇi — "Bus: 8 a.m." The bus leaves at eight, so be there before eight.

(ഇതിനകം, itinakaṁ.) Last? (കഴിഞ്ഞ, kaḻiñña.) Once? (ഒരിക്കൽ, orikkal.) To spend? (ചെലവഴിക്കുക, celavaḻikkuka.)
