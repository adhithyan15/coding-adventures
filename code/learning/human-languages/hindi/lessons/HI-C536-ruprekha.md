## You'll want to know: रूपरेखा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C536-YOJNA536-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रूपरेखा** — *rūprekhā* — "an outline; a rough plan".

> अरुण ने योजना की एक रूपरेखा बनाई।
>
> *Aruṇ ne yojnā kī ek rūprekhā banāī.*
>
> Arun drew up an outline of the plan.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रूपरेखा** (*rūprekhā*) is a modern compound of two Sanskrit words, *rūpa*, "outward appearance, form", and *rekhā*, "a line" (Monier-Williams): the lines that give a plan its shape.
