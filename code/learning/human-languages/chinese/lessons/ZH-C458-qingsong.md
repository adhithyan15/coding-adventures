## You'll want to know: qīngsōng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C458-MOOD458-04]; assesses=[] -->

*qīngsōng* — "relaxed; light and easy".

> *kǎoshì yǐhòu wǒ gǎnjué hěn qīngsōng* — After the exam I feel really relaxed.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qīngsōng* is built from *qīng* 'light' and *sōng* 'loose', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khjieng* and *sowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as light against heavy, and lists the second under the spelling for loose, tousled hair; the sense 'relaxed' is not given there.
