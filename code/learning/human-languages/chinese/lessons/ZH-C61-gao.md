## You'll want to know: gāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C61-LONG61-03]; assesses=[] -->

*gāo* — "tall; high".

> *wǒ gēge hěn gāo tā xǐhuan yùndòng* — My brother is tall, and he loves sport.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gāo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kaw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'up', 'lofty' and 'far'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
