French, chapter 525: Devant l'écran.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
zapper — to channel-hop; to skip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for (film) director, then the French for shooting.

You'll want to know: zapper.
zapper — "to channel-hop; to skip".
Le soir, il zappe d'une chaîne à l'autre. In the evening he flicks from one channel to another.
Informally, to forget or skip: j'ai zappé le rendez-vous.

The word, taken apart.
zapper was borrowed from English zap (EtymDB), an imitative word.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 2 of 5.
la télécommande — remote control.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for shooting, then the French for to channel-hop.

You'll want to know: la télécommande.
la télécommande — "remote control".
Personne ne trouve la télécommande. Nobody can find the remote control.
Informally also la zapette.

The word, taken apart.
la télécommande is built from Greek tēle "far" and commande "command", which goes back, through commander, to Latin commendāre "to entrust", built on mandāre "to entrust, to order" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: l'émission.
l'émission — "(television or radio) programme; emission".
Cette émission de cuisine passe tous les jeudis. This cooking programme is on every Thursday.
Feminine. Also emissions: les émissions de CO2.

The word, taken apart.
l'émission is a learned borrowing from Latin ēmissiō "a sending out" (EtymDB), from ēmittere, built on mittere "to send" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
le documentaire — documentary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: le documentaire.
le documentaire — "documentary".
Hier, nous avons vu un documentaire sur les baleines. Yesterday we watched a documentary about whales.
Also an adjective: un film documentaire.

The word, taken apart.
le documentaire is built on document (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin documentum "a lesson, a proof", from docēre "to teach" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
rediffuser — to repeat, to rebroadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for (television or radio) programme, then the French for documentary.

You'll want to know: rediffuser.
rediffuser — "to repeat, to rebroadcast".
La chaîne rediffuse ce vieux film chaque été. The channel repeats this old film every summer.
Une rediffusion — a repeat.

The word, taken apart.
rediffuser is built from re- and diffuser "to broadcast", from Latin diffundere "to pour out, to spread", built on fundere "to pour" (Lewis & Short).
