## You'll want to know: el ascenso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C553-EMPLEO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el ascenso** — "the promotion; the climb".

> Le dieron un ascenso en enero.
>
> *She got a promotion in January.*

In sport, *el ascenso* is going up a division.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ascenso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ascēnsus* 'a going up' (EtymDB), from *ascendere* 'to climb'.
