## You'll want to know: la communauté
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C366-BENEVOLAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la communauté** — "the community".

> Toute la communauté a aidé cette famille.
>
> *The whole community helped this family.*

*La vie en communauté* — living together in a group.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*communauté* is usually explained as built on *commun* "common", which is inherited from Latin *commūnis* "shared, common" (EtymDB), after Latin *commūnitās* "fellowship" (Lewis & Short).
