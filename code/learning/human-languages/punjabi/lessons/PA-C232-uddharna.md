# ਉੱਧੜਨਾ (uddhaṛnā) — to come unstitched

## You'll want to know: ਉੱਧੜਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C232-DIN232-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਧੜਨਾ** — *uddhaṛnā* — "to come unstitched".

> ਜੇ ਧਾਗਾ ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰ ਹੈ ਤਾਂ ਜੇਬ ਉੱਧੜੇਗੀ।
>
> *je dhāgā kamzor hai tāṁ jeb uddhṛegī.*
>
> If the thread is weak, the pocket will come unstitched.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਜੇਬ ਉੱਧੜੀ, *oh jeb uddhṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਧੜਨਾ** (*uddhaṛnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *uddharati*, "takes out"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *uddhaṛṇā*, "to be ripped" (CDIAL 2009).
