## You'll want to know: समाजः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C354-NAGARIKA354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**समाजः** — *samājaḥ* — "society".

> समाजे सर्वे जनाः समाः सन्ति।
>
> *samāje sarve janāḥ samāḥ santi.*
>
> In society all people are equal.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समाजः** (*samājaḥ*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √aj, "to drive"; MW gives *sam-āja* "a meeting, an assembly, a society, a company" (MW).
