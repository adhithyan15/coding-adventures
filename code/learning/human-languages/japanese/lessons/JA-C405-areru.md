# あれる (areru) — to become rough (of the sea or weather); to fall into ruin

## You'll want to know: あれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C405-TEIEN405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**あれる** — *areru* — "to become rough (of the sea or weather); to fall into ruin".

> **たいふうの あとで、ていえんが あれていました。** — *taifū no ato de, teien ga arete imashita* — "After the typhoon, the garden was in a mess."

Polite **あれます**, *aremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **たいふうで うみが あれました。**, *taifū de umi ga aremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あれる** (*areru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
