# दुजोरा देणे (dujorā deṇe) — to corroborate

## You'll want to know: दुजोरा देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C353-PUSHTI353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दुजोरा देणे** — *dujorā deṇe* — "to corroborate".

> मीराच्या बोलण्याला तिच्या भावाने दुजोरा दिला.
>
> *mīrācyā bolaṇyālā ticyā bhāvāne dujorā dilā.*
>
> Her brother backed up what Meera said.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दुजोरा दिला, *tyāne dujorā dilā*.

The statement backed takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुजोरा देणे** (*dujorā deṇe*) joins दुजोरा, "support, backing", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to देणे, "to give".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
