# Six words, read cold

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-PORA-READ-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] The verb for what you are doing this second, in Bengali script. Say
it before you look.

## Reading: the six, with nothing beside them
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-HOWA-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-DUDH-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-PORA-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-PORIBAR-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-BHAI-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-HRIDOY-READ-01] -->

> পড়া · দুধ · ভাই · পরিবার · হওয়া · হৃদয়

Read down the line and say each one aloud before checking:

| word | |
|---|---|
| পড়া · দুধ | to read · milk |
| ভাই · পরিবার | brother · family |
| হওয়া · হৃদয় | to be · heart |

Each was taught with its own point attached. **পরিবার** is where the inherent
vowel is finally heard rather than explained. **ভাই** is an independent vowel
sitting in the middle of a word, where nothing prepares you for it. **দুধ** is
the plain-against-breathy pair inside one short word.

## Script: the sign that came last
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-RI-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-HRIDOY-READ-01] -->

> ৃ

**হৃদয়** needed a sign nothing before it had needed: **ৃ**, for a vowel that
was once an *r* and is no longer one. It is the last piece of the reading
ladder, and it exists for a sound that has moved away from the letter that
records it — the same drift you meet in every script old enough to have one.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-HOWA-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-DUDH-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-PORA-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-PORIBAR-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-BHAI-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-RI-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-HRIDOY-READ-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which of the six is the longest, and what does it let you hear? (**পরিবার** — the inherent vowel.) Where does **ভাই** put an independent vowel? (**In the middle of the word.**) Which word needed **ৃ**? (**হৃদয়**.) And what was that sign once? (**An *r*** — it is not one now.)
