## You'll want to know: پیشرفت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C374-BEHBUD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پیشرفت کردن** (*pishraft kardan*) — "to make progress, to advance".

> او در کارش خیلی پیشرفت کرده است.
>
> *u dar kârash kheyli pishraft karde ast.*
>
> He has made a lot of progress in his work.

*Pishraft* + *kardan*. *Dar chizi pishraft kardan* — to make progress in something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیشرفت کردن** is native Persian, built from *pishraft* "progress", from *pish* "forward" and *raft* "went", with *kardan* "to do".
