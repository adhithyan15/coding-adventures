# ਤਪਣਾ (tapṇā) — to blaze with heat; to become very hot

## You'll want to know: ਤਪਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C288-HEAT288-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਪਣਾ** — *tapṇā* — "to blaze with heat; to become very hot".

> ਜੂਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਧਰਤੀ ਤਪੇਗੀ।
>
> *jūn vich dhartī tapegī.*
>
> In June the earth will blaze with heat.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੱਥਰ ਤਪਿਆ, *oh patthar tapiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਪਣਾ** (*tapṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tapyati*, "is hot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tapṇā*, "to be heated", under it (CDIAL 5684).
