# Chinese pre-A1 mock 2 — examiner rubric

This rubric applies **only to form 2**, its independent [paper](mock-2-paper.md)
and [answer key](mock-2-answer-key.md). This is a project-defined HSK
precursor, not an official HSK level or a calibrated human result.

Reading takes 5 minutes, listening 5, writing 10, continuously; speaking
takes 5 after 1 minute of preparation. The full mock is separate from the
book's at-most-five-minute lessons. Hold the audio and delayed card away from
the candidate. Play listening and dictation cues exactly twice; allow one slow
repeat of a speaking prompt. Record speaking and preserve the written sheet.
No dictionary, translator, pinyin input aid, visible writing model, or script
to read aloud is allowed.

Each skill is worth **25**, total **100**. Pass only with **15/25 or more in
each of the four skills and 60/100 overall in one sitting**, with every
section attempted. An omitted part scores zero and cannot be offset by a
different skill.

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->
Reading 1–5: five independent meaning choices, **5 points each**. Use the
form-2 key, not form 1. No partial credit. Item 3 assesses pinyin as printed;
the character items have no pinyin hint.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->
Listening 6–10: five independent selections, **5 points each**. Use the
exact form-2 recording text twice, naturally, without an English translation.
Score only the selected response.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-character-recall -->
Writing 11: show the form-2 card for ten seconds, cover it, and score the
recalled simplified character out of **5**: recognizable identity 3,
complete shape 1, legibility 1. Do not count copying as delayed retrieval.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-character-recall -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation-and-transcription -->
Writing 12: **5** = pinyin syllable 3 + correct tone mark 2. Writing 13:
**5** = correct simplified-character identity 4 + legibility 1. Accept
legible handwriting, not only a font-like shape; invisible stroke order is
not scored. No transcript or visible character model.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation-and-transcription -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
Writing 14–15: **5 each** = task fulfilment 2, independent retrieval of a
taught Chinese chunk 1, recognizable simplified form 1, legibility 1. The
keyed answers are examples; accept an equally appropriate taught response.
English fallback and copied models do not establish independent production.
Longer answers earn no length bonus.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->
Speaking 16–20: **5 each** = answers the prompt 2, intelligibility 1, taught
tone control 1, turn-taking 1. Item 18 accepts the candidate's authentic name
without scoring its spelling, but requires the taught **wǒ shì** frame. Item
20 targets **xiè**, with a clear fourth tone. A permitted slow-repeat request
does not lower the score. A rehearsed but irrelevant reply earns no task
fulfilment points.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->

Two trained raters score writing and speaking independently. Keep item marks;
adjudicate with a third rater if a skill total differs by more than 2 points
or pass/fail decisions differ. Credit intelligible documented regional
pronunciation without abandoning standard Mandarin as the target. Structural
completeness of this pair is not a human validation study: both forms passed
can support only **mock-ready**, never **learner-proven**, status.
