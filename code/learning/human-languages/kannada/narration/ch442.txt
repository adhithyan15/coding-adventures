Kannada, chapter 442: On the Highway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿ (heddāri) — a highway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for cash, then the Kannada for a card.

You'll want to know: ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿ.
ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿ — heddāri — "a highway".
ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ವಾಹನಗಳು ವೇಗವಾಗಿ ಓಡುತ್ತವೆ. heddāriyalli vāhanagaḷu vēgavāgi ōḍuttave. Vehicles go fast on the highway.
ಹೆ- is the old big, as in ಹೆಬ್ಬೆರಳು, thumb: the big road.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿ (heddāri) is a native Kannada compound of per, hē-, "big, great" (DEDR 4411), and ದಾರಿ, "a way, a road" (DEDR 3170).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಟೋಲ್ (ṭōl) — a toll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a card, then the Kannada for a highway.

You'll want to know: ಟೋಲ್.
ಟೋಲ್ — ṭōl — "a toll".
ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾವು ಎರಡು ಕಡೆ ಟೋಲ್ ಕಟ್ಟಿದೆವು. heddāriyalli nāvu eraḍu kaḍe ṭōl kaṭṭidevu. We paid a toll at two places on the highway.
ಟೋಲ್ (ṭōl) ಕಟ್ಟು, to pay a toll; ಟೋಲ್ (ṭōl) ಗೇಟ್, a toll gate.

The word, taken apart.
ಟೋಲ್ (ṭōl) is a loan from English toll, a charge for using a road or a bridge.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮೇಲ್ಸೇತುವೆ (mēlsētuve) — a flyover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a highway, then the Kannada for a toll.

You'll want to know: ಮೇಲ್ಸೇತುವೆ.
ಮೇಲ್ಸೇತುವೆ — mēlsētuve — "a flyover".
ಹೊಸ ಮೇಲ್ಸೇತುವೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಕಾರುಗಳು ಓಡುತ್ತವೆ. hosa mēlsētuveya mēle kārugaḷu ōḍuttave. Cars run along the new flyover.
ಮೇಲ್, above, plus ಸೇತುವೆ, a bridge: a bridge over a road.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೇಲ್ಸೇತುವೆ (mēlsētuve) is a Kannada compound of native mēl, "upper, above", and ಸೇತುವೆ, "a bridge", which is built on Sanskrit sētu, "a bond, a dam, a bridge" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಘಾಟಿ (ghāṭi) — a mountain pass; a ghat road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a toll, then the Kannada for a flyover.

You'll want to know: ಘಾಟಿ.
ಘಾಟಿ — ghāṭi — "a mountain pass; a ghat road".
ಘಾಟಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ತಿರುವುಗಳಿವೆ. ghāṭiyalli tumbā tiruvugaḷive. There are many bends on the ghat road.
ಘಾಟಿ (ghāṭi) ರಸ್ತೆ, a ghat road, winds up through the hills.

The word, taken apart.
ಘಾಟಿ (ghāṭi) is usually traced to Sanskrit ghaṭṭa, "a landing place, a ferry", whose basic sense, Turner notes, is a passage through mountains or over a river (CDIAL 4414).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮಾರ್ಗ (mārga) — a route; a way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a flyover, then the Kannada for a mountain pass.

You'll want to know: ಮಾರ್ಗ.
ಮಾರ್ಗ — mārga — "a route; a way".
ಈ ಬಸ್ಸಿನ ಮಾರ್ಗ ಬದಲಾಯಿತು. ī bassina mārga badalāyitu. The route of this bus has changed.
ಮಾರ್ಗ (mārga) is the formal word; ದಾರಿ is the everyday way or path.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾರ್ಗ (mārga) is borrowed from Sanskrit mārga, "a track, a road" (CDIAL 10071), first the trail of a wild animal.
