Lesson 1 of 5.
আলমারি (ālmāri) — a cupboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a roof, then the Bengali for a bed.

You'll want to know: আলমারি.
আলমারি — ālmāri — "a cupboard".

The word, taken apart.
আলমারি (ālmāri) is a loan from Portuguese armário, "cupboard".

Lesson 2 of 5.
মেঝে (mejhe) — the floor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bed, then the Bengali for a cupboard.

You'll want to know: মেঝে.
মেঝে — mejhe — "the floor".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of মেঝে (mejhe) is not settled. CDIAL has no Bengali word for "floor" of this shape; the nearest it gives is mejha, "the middle", under Sanskrit madhyama, "middle" (CDIAL 9810), and it does not connect the two.

Lesson 3 of 5.
সিঁড়ি (sĩṛi) — stairs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cupboard, then the Bengali for the floor.

You'll want to know: সিঁড়ি.
সিঁড়ি — sĩṛi — "stairs".

The word, taken apart.
সিঁড়ি (sĩṛi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śrīḍhi, "resting-place, ladder", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12709).
