5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
хобби (khóbbi) — a hobby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fly, then the Russian for a beetle.

You'll want to know: хобби.
хобби (khóbbi) — "a hobby".

The word, taken apart.
хобби (khóbbi) comes from English hobby, cut short from hobby-horse, a toy horse ridden for fun.

гитара (gitára) — "a guitar".

The word, taken apart.
гитара (gitára) — first китара, in Peter the Great's time; it came through Polish gitara or German Gitarre from Latin cithara, Greek kithára, "a lyre" (Vasmer).
