## You'll want to know: اغراق کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C365-QEZAVAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اغراق کردن** (*eghrâq kardan*) — "to exaggerate".

> او همیشه اغراق میکند.
>
> *u hamishe eghrâq mikonad.*
>
> He exaggerates all the time.

*Eghrâq* "exaggeration" + *kardan*. *Dar chizi eghrâq kardan* — to exaggerate something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اغراق** is usually traced to Arabic *ighrāq* "exaggeration"; *kardan* "to do" is the inherited Persian verb.
