## You'll want to know: आयुर्वेद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C492-SUDHAR492-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आयुर्वेद** — *āyurved* — "ayurveda; traditional Indian medicine".

> आयुर्वेद की दवा से दादा जल्दी ठीक हो गए।
>
> *āyurved kī davā se dādā jaldī ṭhīk ho gae.*
>
> With ayurvedic medicine, my grandfather soon got well.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आयुर्वेद** (*āyurved*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āyurveda*, which Monier-Williams explains as the learning of health and healing (Monier-Williams), built on *āyus*, "life", and *veda*, "knowledge". It names India's traditional medicine and its herbal remedies.
