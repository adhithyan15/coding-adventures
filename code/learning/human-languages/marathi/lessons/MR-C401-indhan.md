## You'll want to know: इंधन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C401-INDHAN401-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इंधन** — *indhan* — "fuel".

> गाडीतलं इंधन कमी झालं आहे.
>
> *gāḍītlaṁ indhan kamī jhālaṁ āhe.*
>
> The fuel in the car has run low.

Neuter.

Any fuel: wood, gas or petrol; इंधन भरणे, to fill up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इंधन** (*indhan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *indhana*, "kindling; fuel" (Monier-Williams).
