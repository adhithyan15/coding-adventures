## You'll want to know: મરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C164-SPICE164-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મરી** — *marī* — "black pepper".

> **મરી તીખી છે.** — *marī tīkhī che* — "Black pepper is hot."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મરી** (*marī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *marica*, "peppercorn", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "black pepper" (CDIAL 9875).
