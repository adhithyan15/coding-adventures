6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
সুদ (sud) — interest (on money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cheque, then the Bengali for cash.

You'll want to know: সুদ.
সুদ — sud — "interest (on money)".
ব্যাংক সঞ্চয়ের ওপর সুদ দেয়। bængk sôñchôyer opôr sud dey. The bank pays interest on savings.

The word, taken apart.
সুদ (sud) is usually traced to Persian sūd, "profit, gain, interest".

Lesson 3 of 6.
লেনদেন (lenden) — transactions; dealings (in money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for interest (on money), then the Bengali for a passbook.

You'll want to know: লেনদেন.
লেনদেন — lenden — "transactions; dealings (in money)".
আজ ব্যাংকে অনেক লেনদেন হল। āj bængke ônek lenden hôlô. There were a lot of transactions at the bank today.

The word, taken apart.
লেনদেন (lenden) pairs লেন, "taking", built on নেওয়া, to take, with দেন, "giving", built on দেওয়া, to give: taking and giving, the whole business of money.

Lesson 4 of 6.
বিনিয়োগ করা (biniyog kôrā) — to invest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a passbook, then the Bengali for transactions.

You'll want to know: বিনিয়োগ করা.
বিনিয়োগ করা — biniyog kôrā — "to invest".
অরুণ তার সঞ্চয় একটা ব্যবসায় বিনিয়োগ করল। ôrun tār sôñchôy ekṭā bæbsāy biniyog kôrlô. Arun invested his savings in a business.
Past (he/she …): তিনি জমিতে টাকা বিনিয়োগ করলেন, tini jômite ṭākā biniyog kôrlen.
The noun alone is বিনিয়োগ, an investment.

The word, taken apart.
বিনিয়োগ করা (biniyog kôrā) joins Sanskrit viniyoga, "apportioning, putting to use" (Monier-Williams), to করা, to do; the money sense is the modern one.

Lesson 5 of 6.
খোয়ানো (khoyāno) — to lose (money or property); to forfeit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for transactions, then the Bengali for to invest.

You'll want to know: খোয়ানো.
খোয়ানো — khoyāno — "to lose (money or property); to forfeit".
ব্যবসায় অরুণ তার সব সঞ্চয় খোয়াল। bæbsāy ôrun tār sôb sôñchôy khoyālô. Arun lost all his savings in the business.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সব টাকা খোয়ালেন, tini sôb ṭākā khoyālen.
হারানো is to lose anything, a key or the way; খোয়ানো (khoyāno) is to lose what you had of value, often by your own doing.

The word, taken apart.
খোয়ানো (khoyāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali khoyā, "thrown away, lost", under Sanskrit kṣapita, "cast away, destroyed" (CDIAL 3653).
