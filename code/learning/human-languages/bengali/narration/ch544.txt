Lesson 1 of 5.
তৎপর (tôtpôr) — prompt; quick to act.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to secure, then the Bengali for a desired goal.

You'll want to know: তৎপর.
তৎপর — tôtpôr — "prompt; quick to act".
যদি দমকল তৎপর না হত, তাহলে আগুন ছড়াত। Jôdi dômkôl tôtpôr nā hôtô, tāhôle āgun chhôṛātô. If the fire brigade hadn't been quick, the fire would have spread.

The word, taken apart.
তৎপর (tôtpôr) is a tatsama, Sanskrit tatpara, "following on that, intent on it" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: অবকাশ.
অবকাশ — ôbôkāsh — "a respite; a break".
দুপুরে অবকাশ পেলে আমি একটু ঘুমাই। Dupure ôbôkāsh pele āmi ekṭu ghumāi. If I get a break at midday, I take a short nap.

The word, taken apart.
অবকাশ (ôbôkāsh) is a tatsama, Sanskrit avakāśa, "room, space", which Monier-Williams also gives for a stretch of time in between: a gap in the day.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ঘনিয়ে আসা (ghôniye āsā) — to draw near (of a date or danger).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for prompt, then the Bengali for a respite.

You'll want to know: ঘনিয়ে আসা.
ঘনিয়ে আসা — ghôniye āsā — "to draw near (of a date or danger)".
বিয়ের দিন ঘনিয়ে এল। Biyer din ghôniye elô. The wedding day drew near.
Past (he/she …): সেই দিন ঘনিয়ে এল, sei din ghôniye elô.

The word, taken apart.
ঘনিয়ে আসা (ghôniye āsā) joins ঘনিয়ে, the -এ form of a verb built on ঘন, "dense, close", to আসা, "to come": coming closer and closer. CDIAL lists Bengali ghana, "thick, dense", under Sanskrit ghana, and notes a Bengali verb from it meaning to come close (CDIAL 4424).

Lesson 4 of 5.
হুড়োহুড়ি (huṛohuṛi) — a scramble; a rush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a respite, then the Bengali for to draw near.

You'll want to know: হুড়োহুড়ি.
হুড়োহুড়ি — huṛohuṛi — "a scramble; a rush".
স্টেশনে লোকজনের হুড়োহুড়ি ছিল। Sṭeshône lokjôner huṛohuṛi chhilô. There was a scramble of people at the station.

The word, taken apart.
হুড়োহুড়ি (huṛohuṛi) is an expressive doubled word for people pushing and rushing. It perhaps belongs with হুড়, "a crowd", which CDIAL lists as Bengali huṛ under huḍati, "collects" (CDIAL 14136); the link is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ঝটপট (jhôṭpôṭ) — in a jiffy; quickly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to draw near, then the Bengali for a scramble.

You'll want to know: ঝটপট.
ঝটপট — jhôṭpôṭ — "in a jiffy; quickly".
মেয়েটা ঝটপট জামা পরল। Meyeṭā jhôṭpôṭ jāmā pôrlô. The girl put on her dress in a jiffy.

The word, taken apart.
ঝটপট (jhôṭpôṭ) is an expressive doubled word built on ঝট, "quickly"; CDIAL lists Bengali jhaṭ and the doubled jhaṭ-phaṭ, both "quickly", under a reconstructed jhaṭ, "sudden movement" (CDIAL 5327).
