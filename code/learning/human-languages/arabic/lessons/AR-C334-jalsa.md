## You'll want to know: جلسة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C334-MUHAKAMA334-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جلسة** — *jalsa* — "a hearing, a session".

> تأجلت جلسة المحكمة أسبوعا.
>
> *taʾajjalat jalsat al-maḥkama usbūʿan.*
>
> The court hearing was put off for a week.

Any sitting: جلسة عمل, a working session.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جلسة** (*jalsa*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-l-s* (Lane), the root of the taught *jalasa*, to sit. The *faʿla* pattern gives one sitting.
