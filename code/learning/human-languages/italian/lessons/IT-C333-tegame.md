## You'll want to know: il tegame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C333-PENTOLA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il tegame** — "the shallow pan, the casserole".

> Cuoci le uova nel tegame.
>
> *Cook the eggs in the shallow pan.*

A wide, low pan with two handles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tegame* is usually traced to Greek *tḗganon* "a frying pan", through a Latin form (EtymDB has no entry).
