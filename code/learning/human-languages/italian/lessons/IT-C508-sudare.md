## You'll want to know: sudare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C508-PALESTRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sudare** — "to sweat".

> Con questo caldo sudo anche da fermo.
>
> *In this heat I sweat even standing still.*

The noun is *il sudore*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sudare* is inherited from Latin *sūdāre* "to sweat" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
