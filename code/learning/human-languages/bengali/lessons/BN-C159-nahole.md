## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নাহলে** (*nāhôle*) is *nā*, "not", from Sanskrit *na* (CDIAL 6906), + *hôle*, "if it is", a form of *hôoyā*, "to be", from Sanskrit *bhavati*, "becomes, is" (CDIAL 9416): "if not so".
