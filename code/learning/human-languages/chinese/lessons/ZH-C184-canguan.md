## You'll want to know: cānguān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C184-HOLIDAY184-03]; assesses=[] -->

*cānguān* — "to look round a place; to visit (a sight)".

> *míngtiān wǒmen qù cānguān dàxué* — Tomorrow we're going to look round the university.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cānguān* is built from *cān* 'to present oneself' and *guān* 'to look', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshom* and *kwan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
