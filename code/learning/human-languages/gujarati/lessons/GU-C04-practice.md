# Chapter 6 — The farewells

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-AAVJO-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-KAALE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-MALISHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHA-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHAMALISHUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. Three ways to part — none of them a blunt "goodbye."

## Why it's said this way — the farewells
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-CONCEPT-C04-PRACTICE-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-AAVJO-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-KAALE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-MALISHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHA-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHAMALISHUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: the quick "come again" from the earlier courtesy chapter — *āvjo*]
- [YOU SAY: the explicit "we'll meet again" — *pāchhā maḷīshũ*]
- [YOU SAY: "see you tomorrow" — *kāle maḷīshũ*]

## The word, taken apart — the atoms
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-AAVJO-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-KAALE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-MALISHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHA-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHAMALISHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PRACTICE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: "again" and "(we) will meet" (*pāchhā*, *maḷīshũ*)]
- [YOU SAY: the word that means BOTH tomorrow and yesterday (*kāl*)]
- [YOU SAY: the retroflex letter in *maḷīshũ* (ળ, *ḷ*)]

## What you've built — roots you now carry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-AAVJO-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-KAALE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-MALISHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHA-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHAMALISHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PRACTICE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *āvjo* ("goodbye") = "come [again]," from *āvvũ* "to come"
- *maḷīshũ* ← *maḷvũ* "to meet" (Sanskrit *mil-*); the retroflex ળ
- *kāl* ← *kāla* "time" (Hindi *kal*, Punjabi *akāl*)

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-AAVJO-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-KAALE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-MALISHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHA-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PACHHAMALISHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-PRACTICE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You're leaving a friend and will see them tomorrow. Give the goodbye
and the "tomorrow" version. (*Āvjo* — and *kāle maḷīshũ*.)
