## You'll want to know: o convívio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C498-CONVIVIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o convívio** — "get-together; social life".

> No sábado há um convívio na associação do bairro.
>
> *On Saturday there is a get-together at the residents' association.*

In Portugal *um convívio* is often a party or a shared meal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convívio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *convīvium* "a feast, a dinner party" (Lewis & Short), built on *vīvere* "to live".
