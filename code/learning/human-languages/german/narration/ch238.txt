Lesson 1 of 5.
die Schwierigkeit — a difficulty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a shadow, shade, then the German for a step.

You'll want to know: die Schwierigkeit.
die Schwierigkeit — "a difficulty".

The word, taken apart.
Schwierigkeit is schwierig, "difficult", + -keit, the ending that makes a noun. The origin of schwierig itself is uncertain.
