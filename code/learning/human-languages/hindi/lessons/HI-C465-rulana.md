## You'll want to know: रुलाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C465-VILAP465-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रुलाना** — *rulānā* — "to make someone cry".

> दादी की पुरानी कहानी ने मीरा को रुला दिया।
>
> *dādī kī purānī kahānī ne Mīrā ko rulā diyā.*
>
> Grandmother's old story moved Mira to tears.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को रुलाया, *usne bacce ko rulāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुलाना** (*rulānā*) is the causative partner of *ronā*, "to weep", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *rōdati*, "weeps" (CDIAL 10840); the vowel shortens before the causative ending, as it does in *sulānā* beside *sonā*.
