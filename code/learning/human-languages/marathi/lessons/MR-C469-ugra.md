## You'll want to know: उग्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C469-KATHOR469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उग्र** — *ugra* — "fierce; sharp".

> रागावल्यावर त्याचा चेहरा उग्र दिसतो.
>
> *rāgāvlyāvar tyācā cehrā ugra disto.*
>
> When he gets angry his face looks fierce.

Unchanging; of a face, a smell or a medicine: उग्र वास, a sharp smell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उग्र** (*ugra*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ugra*, "strong, terrible, fierce" (CDIAL 1631).
