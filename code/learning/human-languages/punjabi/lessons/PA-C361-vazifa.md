## You'll want to know: ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C361-COLLEGE361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ** — *vazīfā* — "a scholarship; a grant".

> ਹੁਸ਼ਿਆਰ ਵਿਦਿਆਰਥੀਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ ਮਿਲਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *hushiār vidiārthīāṁ nū̃ vazīfā mildā hai.*
>
> Clever students get a scholarship.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ** (*vazīfā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *waẓīfa*, "duty".
