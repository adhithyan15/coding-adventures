## You'll want to know: پشیمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C451-REGRET451-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پشیمان** — *pashemān* — "contrite; sorry (for what one did)".

> وہ اپنی بات پر پشیمان تھا۔
>
> *voh apnī bāt par pashemān thā.*
>
> He was sorry for what he had said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پشیمان** (*pashemān*) is usually traced to Persian *pashēmān*, "regretful".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
