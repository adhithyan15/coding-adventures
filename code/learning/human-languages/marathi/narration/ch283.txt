Marathi, chapter 283: Tickets, Times and Trouble.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
तिकीट (tikīṭ) — a ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "the station is opposite the temple" (sṭeśan mandirācyā samor āhe), then "a bus" and "a train". (bas, gāḍī.)

You'll want to know: तिकीट.
तिकीट — tikīṭ — "a ticket".
मला पुण्याचं एक तिकीट हवं आहे. malā puṇyācaṁ ek tikīṭ havaṁ āhe. — "I want a ticket to Pune."
तिकीट is neuter, so हवं. Two tickets: दोन तिकिटं (don tikiṭaṁ). Marathi buys a ticket with काढणे, "to take out": मी तिकीट काढलं (mī tikīṭ kāḍhlaṁ), "I bought a ticket".

The word, taken apart.
तिकीट (tikīṭ) is a loan from English ticket, reshaped to Marathi sounds: a long ī and a retroflex ṭ.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tikīṭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I want a ticket to Pune" — malā puṇyācaṁ ek tikīṭ havaṁ āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I bought a ticket" — mī tikīṭ kāḍhlaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does तिकीट (tikīṭ) mean? ("A ticket".) "Two tickets"? (दोन तिकिटं.)


Lesson 2 of 4.
एक तिकीट द्या (ek tikīṭ dyā) — at the counter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask how far the station is from here. (ithūn sṭeśan kitī lāmb āhe?) Then say "I want a ticket to Pune". (malā puṇyācaṁ ek tikīṭ havaṁ āhe.) The polite "please give" is द्या.

You'll want to know: buying a ticket.
पुण्याचं एक तिकीट द्या. puṇyācaṁ ek tikīṭ dyā. — "One ticket to Pune, please."
पुण्याचं is "of Pune": the ticket belongs to where it goes. A return: परतीचं तिकीट (partīcaṁ tikīṭ), with the परत, "back", of परतणे. Then the price: किती पैसे झाले? (kitī paise jhāle?), "How much is it?"

You'll want to know: when it leaves.
पुण्याची गाडी किती वाजता निघते? puṇyācī gāḍī kitī vājtā nighte? — "What time does the Pune train leave?"
निघते, "sets out", is the present of निघणे, said of the train. The answers come as times: सात वाजता (sāt vājtā). And the other end: गाडी किती वाजता पोहोचते? (gāḍī kitī vājtā pohocte?), "What time does it arrive?" For the platform, ask with कोणत्या (koṇtyā, "which"): कोणत्या फलाटावर? (koṇtyā phalāṭāvar?)

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "One ticket to Pune, please" — puṇyācaṁ ek tikīṭ dyā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "A return ticket, please" — partīcaṁ tikīṭ dyā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What time does the Pune train leave?" — puṇyācī gāḍī kitī vājtā nighte?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Ask for one ticket to Pune, then ask what time the train leaves.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"A ticket to Pune, please"? (पुण्याचं एक तिकीट द्या (ek tikīṭ dyā).) "A return ticket"? (परतीचं तिकीट.) "What time does it leave?" (किती वाजता निघते?)


Lesson 3 of 4.
माझी पिशवी हरवली (mājhī piśvī haravlī) — when something goes wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "a bag" and "to lose". (piśvī, haravṇe.) Then "she came". (tī ālī.)

You'll want to know: lost.
माझी पिशवी हरवली. mājhī piśvī haravlī. — "My bag is lost."
The bag is what got lost, so the past agrees with it, as ती आली agrees with her: पिशवी हरवली, पुस्तक हरवलं (pustak haravlaṁ). No -ने here. Then the report: मी पोलिसांकडे तक्रार केली (mī polisāṅkaḍe takrār kelī), "I complained to the police", with तक्रार feminine.

You'll want to know: missed.
माझी गाडी चुकली. mājhī gāḍī cuklī. — "I missed my train."
Word for word "my train went wrong": with चुकणे the train is the subject. The cause comes first: मला उशीर झाला, म्हणून माझी गाडी चुकली (malā uśīr jhālā, mhaṇūn mājhī gāḍī cuklī), "I was late, so I missed my train".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "My bag is lost" — mājhī piśvī haravlī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I complained to the police" — mī polisāṅkaḍe takrār kelī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I was late, so I missed my train" — malā uśīr jhālā, mhaṇūn mājhī gāḍī cuklī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that your bag is lost, then that you missed your train.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"My bag is lost"? (माझी पिशवी हरवली (mājhī piśvī haravlī).) "My book is lost"? (माझं पुस्तक हरवलं.) "I missed my train"? (माझी गाडी चुकली.)


Lesson 4 of 4.
Review — at the station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask the way to the station and how far it is, then give it: straight, then right, opposite the temple. (sṭeśanlā kasaṁ jāycaṁ? ithūn kitī lāmb āhe? saraḷ jā, mag ujvīkaḍe vaḷā. sṭeśan mandirācyā samor āhe.) Then say ऋतू and ज्ञान, and name the letters ऋ and ञ.
