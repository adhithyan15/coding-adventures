## You'll want to know: నమూనా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C486-KOLATA486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నమూనా** — *namūnā* — "a sample; a pattern".

> దుకాణదారు బట్ట నమూనా చూపించాడు.
>
> *dukāṇadāru baṭṭa namūnā cūpiñcāḍu.*
>
> The shopkeeper showed a sample of the cloth.

A sample of goods, and a pattern or model to copy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నమూనా** (*namūnā*) is a loan from Persian *namūna*, "a sample, a specimen, a model", which reached Telugu through Hindustani.
