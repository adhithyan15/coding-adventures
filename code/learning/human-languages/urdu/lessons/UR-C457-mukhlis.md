## You'll want to know: مخلص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C457-TRUST457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مخلص** — *mukhlis* — "sincere".

> وہ ایک مخلص دوست ہے۔
>
> *voh ek mukhlis dost hai.*
>
> He is a sincere friend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخلص** (*mukhlis*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mukhliṣ*, "sincere, devoted".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
