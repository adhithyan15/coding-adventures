## You'll want to know: la cérémonie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C489-NOCES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la cérémonie** — "ceremony".

> La cérémonie a eu lieu dans un petit jardin.
>
> *The ceremony took place in a small garden.*

*Sans cérémonie* — informally.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la cérémonie* is a learned borrowing from Latin *caerimōnia* "a sacred rite, reverence" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
