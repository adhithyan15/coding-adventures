Arabic, chapter 476: Small creatures.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عقرب (ʿaqrab) — a scorpion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a squirrel, then the Arabic for a bat.

You'll want to know: عقرب.
عقرب — ʿaqrab — "a scorpion".
وجدنا عقربا تحت حجر. wajadnā ʿaqraban taḥta ḥajar. We found a scorpion under a stone.
Plural: عقارب, ʿaqārib.
The hands of a clock are also ʿaqārib.

The word, taken apart.
عقرب (ʿaqrab) goes back to an old Semitic word, with relatives in Hebrew, Syriac and Ugaritic (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
جرادة (jarāda) — a locust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bat, then the Arabic for a scorpion.

You'll want to know: جرادة.
جرادة — jarāda — "a locust".
رأيت جرادة خضراء على الشجرة. raʾaytu jarāda khaḍrāʾ ʿalā ash-shajara. I saw a green locust on the tree.
jarād is locusts as a swarm; jarāda is one.

The word, taken apart.
جرادة (jarāda) is built on the native Arabic root j-r-d, "to strip bare" (Lane). Locusts strip the land bare.

Lesson 4 of 5.
مخلب (mikhlab) — a claw; a talon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a locust, then the Arabic for moss.

You'll want to know: مخلب.
مخلب — mikhlab — "a claw; a talon".
أمسك الصقر السمكة بمخالبه. amsaka aṣ-ṣaqr as-samaka bi-makhālibihi. The falcon held the fish in its talons.
Plural: مخالب, makhālib.

The word, taken apart.
مخلب (mikhlab) is built on the native Arabic root kh-l-b, "to wound with the claws" (Lane). The tool pattern mifʿal makes it the thing one claws with.

Lesson 5 of 5.
افترس (iftarasa) — to prey on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for moss, then the Arabic for a claw.

You'll want to know: افترس.
افترس — iftarasa — "to prey on".
يفترس النسر الأرانب. yaftarisu an-nasr al-arānib. The eagle preys on rabbits.
Present: يفترس, yaftaris.

The word, taken apart.
افترس (iftarasa) is built on the native Arabic root f-r-s, "to break the neck of prey" (Lane). It is Form VIII; faras, "a horse", is on the same root.
