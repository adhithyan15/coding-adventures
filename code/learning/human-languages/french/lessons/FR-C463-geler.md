## You'll want to know: geler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C463-FROID-03]; assesses=[] -->

**geler** — "to freeze".

> Cette nuit, il a gelé et le lac est blanc.
>
> *Last night it froze and the lake is white.*

*Il gèle* — it is freezing; *je suis gelé* — I am frozen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*geler* is inherited through Old French from Latin *gelāre* 'to freeze', from *gelū* 'frost' (EtymDB).
