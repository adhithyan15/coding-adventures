## You'll want to know: das Schicksal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C317-ERZAEHLUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Schicksal** — "the fate, destiny".

> Sie konnte ihr Schicksal nicht ändern.
>
> *She could not change her fate.*

Plural: *die Schicksale*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schicksal* is built on *schicken*, which in Middle High German meant "to arrange, to order" (EtymDB), with the ending *-sal*: what has been arranged for you.
