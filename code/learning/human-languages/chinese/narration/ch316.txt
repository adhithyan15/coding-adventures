Mandarin Chinese, chapter 316: A New Place.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dēngjì — to register officially.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to my surprise, then the Mandarin for the same as before.

You'll want to know: dēngjì.
dēngjì — "to register officially".
wǒmen qù pàichūsuǒ dēngjì — We went to the police station to register.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dēngjì is built from dēng 'to go up' and 记 'to note down', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tong and kiH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rise' and 'to go forward': to enter in the record.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: 当地.
当地 — dāngdì — "of that place; in the place in question".
我到了当地才找到房子 wǒ dào le dāngdì cái zhǎodào fángzi — I only found somewhere to live once I got there.

The word, taken apart.
当地 (dāngdì) is built from 当 'at' and 地 'a place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tang and dijH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to face' and 'to meet': the very place.

Lesson 5 of 5.
自然 (zìrán) — naturally; nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for of that place, then the Mandarin for life from birth to death.

You'll want to know: 自然.
自然 — zìrán — "naturally; nature".
又下雨了我们自然不出门 yòu xiàyǔ le, wǒmen zìrán bù chūmén — It's raining again, so naturally we're staying in.

The word, taken apart.
自然 (zìrán) is built from 自 and 然 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzijH and nyen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'from' and 'by way of'; its sense 'by itself' is not given there. So of itself.
