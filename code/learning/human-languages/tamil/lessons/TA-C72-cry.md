## You'll want to know: அழு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C72-FEEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அழு** (*aḻu*) — "to cry".

To cry — the mirror of the verb before it, and just as short.

It opens on அ, the free-standing letter, because the word begins with that vowel: the same
pattern you saw when ஓ opened ஓடு. And in the middle is **ழ** again, the third time you have
met it after தமிழ் and கழுவு.

Five feelings now: three you can have — கோபம், பயம், வருத்தம் — and two you can do, சிரி and
அழு.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Five: three nouns and two verbs.
