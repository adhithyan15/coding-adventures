## Chapter 3 — the visarga moves from recognition to recall

The visarga no longer stops at recognition. Its existing trace block now owns
the observe/trace rung, and three new lessons reuse the familiar *namas* “a
bow” from *namaste* in the pause form **नमः**.

The learner copies the visible word, recalls it after the model is covered,
then writes it from sound and meaning alone. Every lesson is two and a half
minutes or less, no new vocabulary is introduced, and the final task hides
both Devanagari and romanization until the attempt is complete. The roadmap,
session map, curriculum extension, and focused corpus test all pin the new
visarga tranche while leaving vocalic ṛ, conjuncts, connected text, and
longer independent recall as later work in #13413.
