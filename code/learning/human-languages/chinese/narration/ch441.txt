Mandarin Chinese, chapter 441: Out in the Open.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
jiāoqū — suburbs; outskirts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a villager, then the Mandarin for to lodge with a local family.

You'll want to know: jiāoqū.
jiāoqū — "suburbs; outskirts".
wǒmen zhù zài jiāoqū zuò dìtiě qù shìzhōngxīn — We live in the suburbs and take the underground into the centre.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jiāoqū is built from jiāo 'the land beyond the walls' and 区 'an area', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kaew and khju (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the land outside a walled town.

The word, taken apart.
yěwài is built from yě 'open fields' and 外 'outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, yaeX and ngwajH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, after an older dictionary, as the land beyond the town.

Lesson 4 of 6.
生火 (shēnghuǒ) — to light a fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for out in the wild, then the Mandarin for a tent.

You'll want to know: 生火.
生火 — shēnghuǒ — "to light a fire".
天冷了我们在村子外边生火 tiān lěng le wǒmen zài cūnzi wàibian shēnghuǒ — It's got cold, so we're lighting a fire outside the village.

The word, taken apart.
生火 (shēnghuǒ) is built from 生 'to bring to life' and 火 'fire', both inherited from Middle Chinese, srjaeng and xwaX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to grow'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
天然 (tiānrán) — natural; made by nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a tent, then the Mandarin for to light a fire.

You'll want to know: 天然.
天然 — tiānrán — "natural; made by nature".
zhè shì yí ge tiānrán de hú — This is a natural lake.

The word, taken apart.
天然 (tiānrán) is built from 天 'heaven' and 然 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, then and nyen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a particle and 'thus': so by heaven, not by people. How the pair came to mean 'natural' is not settled.
