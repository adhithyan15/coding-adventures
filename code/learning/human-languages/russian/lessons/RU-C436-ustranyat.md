## You'll want to know: устранять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C436-VOZVRAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**устранять** (*ustranyát*) — "to fix, to eliminate (a fault)".

> Сантехник быстро устраняет неполадки.
>
> *Santékhnik býstro ustranyáyet nepoládki.*
>
> The plumber fixes faults quickly.

Imperfective; perfective *устранить*. Formal: *устранить проблему*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*устранять* is built from *у-* and *-странять*, on *страна* in its old sense 'a side': to move something to one side. EtymDB takes *страна* from Church Slavonic, from Proto-Slavic *storna* 'side, land'.
