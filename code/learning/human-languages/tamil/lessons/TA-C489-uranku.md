## You'll want to know: உறங்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C489-TUKKAM489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**உறங்கு** — *uṟaṅku* — "to slumber".

> குழந்தை அமைதியாக உறங்கியது.
>
> *kuḻantai amaitiyāka uṟaṅkiyatu.*
>
> The baby slept peacefully.

Past (I …): உறங்கினேன், *uṟaṅkiṉēṉ*.

A softer, more written word than தூங்கு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உறங்கு** (*uṟaṅku*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 707); the entry also gives உறக்கு *uṟakku*, "to put to sleep".
