Arabic, chapter 465: Energy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طاقة (ṭāqa) — energy; power.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to pollute, then the Arabic for to sort.

You'll want to know: طاقة.
طاقة — ṭāqa — "energy; power".
نستعمل طاقة (ṭāqa) الشمس في منزلنا. nastaʿmilu ṭāqat ash-shams fī manzilinā. We use the sun's energy in our house.

The word, taken apart.
طاقة (ṭāqa) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-w-q, "to be able" (Lane). A ṭāqa was first strength, what one is able to do.

Lesson 5 of 5.
استهلك (istahlaka) — to consume; to use up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for crude oil, then the Arabic for coal.

You'll want to know: استهلك.
استهلك — istahlaka — "to consume; to use up".
تستهلك السيارة الكبيرة بنزينا كثيرا. tastahliku as-sayyāra al-kabīra banzīnan kathīran. The big car uses a lot of petrol.
Present: يستهلك, yastahlik.

The word, taken apart.
استهلك (istahlaka) is built on the native Arabic root h-l-k, "to perish" (Lane). It is Form X, "to make perish, to use up".
