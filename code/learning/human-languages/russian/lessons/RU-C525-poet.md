## You'll want to know: поэт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C525-POETRY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поэт** (*poét*) — "a poet".

> Этот поэт писал о любви и природе.
>
> *Étot poét pisál o lyubví i priróde.*
>
> This poet wrote about love and nature.

Masculine; feminine поэтесса, though many women prefer *поэт*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поэт* was borrowed through German *Poet* from Latin *poēta*, which comes from Greek *poiētḗs* 'a maker, a poet' (EtymDB).
