## You'll want to know: ఆరోపణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C406-AVINITI406-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరోపణ** — *ārōpaṇa* — "an allegation; a charge".

> అతని మీద అవినీతి ఆరోపణలు వచ్చాయి.
>
> *atani mīda avinīti ārōpaṇalu vaccāyi.*
>
> Allegations of corruption were made against him.

Plural: ఆరోపణలు, *ārōpaṇalu*.

The person accused takes మీద: అతని మీద ఆరోపణ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరోపణ** (*ārōpaṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āropaṇa*, "placing or fixing on" (Monier-Williams), of the family of *āropa*, "charging with, attributing to".
