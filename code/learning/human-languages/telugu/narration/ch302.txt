Telugu, chapter 302: Ox, Calf, Sheep, Wolf, Crocodile.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎద్దు (eddu) — an ox, a bull.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a taxi, then the Telugu for petrol.

You'll want to know: ఎద్దు.
ఎద్దు — eddu — "an ox, a bull".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: eddu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: eddu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say eddu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for a taxi, then the Telugu for petrol, then say eddu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ఎద్దు (eddu) mean? ("An ox, a bull".) Say it once more.
