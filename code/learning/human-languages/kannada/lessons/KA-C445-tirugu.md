# ತಿರುಗು (tirugu) — to turn round; to go round

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C444-NIYAMA444-05, KA-LEX-C445-PARK445-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for one-way, then the Kannada for to park a vehicle.

## You'll want to know: ತಿರುಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C445-PARK445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಿರುಗು** — *tirugu* — "to turn round; to go round".

> ಮುಂದಿನ ವೃತ್ತದಲ್ಲಿ ಬಸ್ ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *mundina vṛttadalli bas eḍakke tiruguttade.*
>
> At the next circle the bus turns left.

Past (I …): ತಿರುಗಿದೆ, *tirugide*.

ಹಿಂತಿರುಗು, to come back, is ಹಿಂ-, back, plus this verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಿರುಗು** (*tirugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3246 lists Kannada *tirigu*, *tirugu*, "to go round, to turn, to roll, to go back".
