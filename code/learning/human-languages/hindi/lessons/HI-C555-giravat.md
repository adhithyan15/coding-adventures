## You'll want to know: गिरावट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C555-GIRAVAT555-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गिरावट** — *girāvaṭ* — "a fall; a drop (in prices, in level)".

> सब्ज़ियों के दाम में गिरावट आई है।
>
> *Sabziyoṁ ke dām meṁ girāvaṭ āī hai.*
>
> Vegetable prices have dropped.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गिरावट** (*girāvaṭ*) is the noun of *girnā*, "to fall", which comes from *\*girati*, "drips, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4159), with the ending -āvaṭ that makes nouns of verbs.
