## You'll want to know: নেভা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C505-ALO505-05]; assesses=[] -->

**নেভা** — *nebhā* — "to go out (of a fire); to go out (of a light)".

> হঠাৎ বাতাসে মোমবাতিটা নিভল।
>
> *Hôṭhāt bātāse mombātiṭā nibhlô.*
>
> Suddenly the candle went out in the wind.

Past (he/she …): সেই আগুন নিভল, *sei āgun nibhlô*.

The stem vowel rises in the past: নিভল, it went out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নেভা** (*nebhā*) is the plain partner of নেভানো, "to put out", from Sanskrit *nirvāyati*, "is extinguished" (CDIAL 7399).
