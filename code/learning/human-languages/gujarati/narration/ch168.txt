Gujarati, chapter 168: Kneading and rolling rotli.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મસળવું (masaḷvũ) — to knead, to mash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to steam, then the Gujarati for to fry.

You'll want to know: મસળવું.
મસળવું — masaḷvũ — "to knead, to mash".
તે લોટ મસળે છે. — te loṭ masaḷe che — "She kneads the dough."

The word, taken apart.
મસળવું (masaḷvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to rub, crumble, knead", under Sanskrit maṣati, "rubs, crushes", extended with -l- (CDIAL 9919).

Lesson 2 of 5.
વેલણ (velaṇ) — rolling pin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to fry, then the Gujarati for to knead.

You'll want to know: વેલણ.
વેલણ — velaṇ — "rolling pin".
આ વેલણ નવું છે. — ā velaṇ navũ che — "This rolling pin is new."

The word, taken apart.
વેલણ (velaṇ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit vēllana, "shaking, tossing; rolling-pin", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "rolling-pin" (CDIAL 12120).

Lesson 3 of 5.
વણવું (vaṇvũ) — to roll out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to knead, then the Gujarati for rolling pin.

You'll want to know: વણવું.
વણવું — vaṇvũ — "to roll out".
તે વેલણથી રોટલી વણે છે. — te velaṇthī roṭlī vaṇe che — "She rolls out the rotli with a rolling pin."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of વણવું (vaṇvũ) in this sense is not settled. CDIAL lists Gujarati vaṇvũ only as "weaves", under vināti, and does not list the sense of rolling dough (CDIAL 11773).

Lesson 4 of 5.
તવો (tavo) — griddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for rolling pin, then the Gujarati for to roll out.

You'll want to know: તવો.
તવો — tavo — "griddle".
તવો ગરમ છે. — tavo garam che — "The griddle is hot."

The word, taken apart.
તવો (tavo) is a tadbhava. It comes from tapaka, "frying pan", a reconstructed form on the root tap, "to heat", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "griddle" (CDIAL 5670).

Lesson 5 of 5.
શેકવું (śekvũ) — to roast, to toast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to roll out, then the Gujarati for griddle.

You'll want to know: શેકવું.
શેકવું — śekvũ — "to roast, to toast".
તે ધીમે રોટલી શેકે છે. — te dhīme roṭlī śeke che — "She roasts the rotli slowly."

The word, taken apart.
શેકવું (śekvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from sēkk, "heat, foment, roast", a reconstructed form; CDIAL lists the Gujarati sekvũ, "to foment, bake" (CDIAL 13581).
