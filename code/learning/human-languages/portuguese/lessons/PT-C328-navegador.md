## You'll want to know: o navegador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C328-NAVEGAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o navegador** — "the navigator, the seafarer; the web browser".

> O navegador desenhou um mapa da costa.
>
> *The navigator drew a map of the coast.*

On a computer, *o navegador* is the web browser.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navegador* comes from Latin *nāvigātor* "a sailor" (EtymDB), from *nāvigāre* "to sail".
