## You'll want to know: gratinar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C334-GRATINAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gratinar** — "to brown on top in the oven, to gratinate".

> No fim, gratinamos o bacalhau no forno.
>
> *At the end we brown the salt cod in the oven.*

With cheese or breadcrumbs on top.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gratinar* was borrowed from French *gratiner*, from *gratin* "the crust", built on *gratter* "to scrape", a verb of Germanic origin (EtymDB has no entry).
