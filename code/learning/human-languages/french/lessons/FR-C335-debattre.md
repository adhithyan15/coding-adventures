## You'll want to know: débattre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C335-DEBAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**débattre** — "to debate, to discuss".

> Nous avons débattu de ce problème pendant deux heures.
>
> *We debated this problem for two hours.*

*Débattre de quelque chose*. Conjugated like *battre*: *je débats, nous débattons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*débattre* is inherited from Old French *debatre* "to fight, to contend" (EtymDB), built from *dé-* and *battre*, which comes from Latin *battuere* "to beat" (Lewis & Short).
