## You'll want to know: häkeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C499-HANDARBEIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**häkeln** — "to crochet".

> Sie häkelt eine kleine Mütze für das Baby.
>
> *She is crocheting a little hat for the baby.*

Done with one hooked needle, *die Häkelnadel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*häkeln* is built on *Häkel*, a small form of *Haken* "hook"; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
