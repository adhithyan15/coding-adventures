# ফিনাইল (phināil) — phenyl (a floor disinfectant)

## You'll want to know: ফিনাইল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C510-SAPHAI510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ফিনাইল** — *phināil* — "phenyl (a floor disinfectant)".

> আমি জলে ফিনাইল মিশিয়ে মেঝে মুছলাম।
>
> *Āmi jôle phināil mishiye mejhe muchhlām.*
>
> I mixed phenyl into the water and mopped the floor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফিনাইল** (*phināil*) is borrowed from English *phenyl*, the name of a disinfectant used for washing floors.
