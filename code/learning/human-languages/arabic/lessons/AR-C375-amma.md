## You'll want to know: أما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C375-TADADD375-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أما** — *ammā* — "as for".

> أما أخي فيحب الرياضة.
>
> *ammā akhī fa-yuḥibbu ar-riyāḍa.*
>
> As for my brother, he loves sport.

The next part starts with فـ: أما … فـ ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أما** (*ammā*) is an old native Arabic particle that sets one topic apart from the others. Its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
