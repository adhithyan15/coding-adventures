# ತರುವಾಯ (taruvāya) — thereafter; after

## You'll want to know: ತರುವಾಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C368-KRAMA368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತರುವಾಯ** — *taruvāya* — "thereafter; after".

> ಯುದ್ಧದ ತರುವಾಯ ದೇಶ ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಬೆಳೆಯಿತು.
>
> *yuddhada taruvāya dēśa nidhānavāgi beḷeyitu.*
>
> After the war the country grew slowly.

A written partner of ನಂತರ: ಊಟದ ತರುವಾಯ, after the meal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತರುವಾಯ** (*taruvāya*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3142 lists Kannada *taṟu-vāy* beside Tamil *taṟuvāy*, "an occasion, a particular juncture, a stage".

## What you've built
