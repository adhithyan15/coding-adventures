## You'll want to know: компас
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C437-ZABLUDITSYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**компас** (*kómpas*) — "a compass".

> Без компаса в лесу легко заблудиться.
>
> *Bez kómpasa v lesú legkó zabludítsya.*
>
> Without a compass it is easy to get lost in the forest.

Masculine. Sailors stress the second syllable: *kompás*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*компас* was borrowed from German *Kompass* (EtymDB).
