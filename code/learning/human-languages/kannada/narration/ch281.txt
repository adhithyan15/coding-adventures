Kannada, chapter 281: To vanish, To pack, To take off, To fail, To get stuck.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮಾಯವಾಗು (māyavāgu) — to vanish, to disappear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to deliver, then the Kannada for to overtake.

You'll want to know: ಮಾಯವಾಗು.
ಮಾಯವಾಗು — māyavāgu — "to vanish, to disappear".

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾಯವಾಗು (māyavāgu) is Sanskrit māyā, "illusion, magic" (CDIAL 10060), with native āgu, "to become" (DEDR 333): "to become magic", to vanish as if by a trick. DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

ಕಳಚು — kaḷacu — "to take off (clothes), to detach".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಳಚು (kaḷacu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kaḷ, Telugu kalupu and Malayalam kaḷa (DEDR 1373). DEDR lists kaḷacu, "to remove, pull off".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಿಕ್ಕಿಹಾಕಿಕೊಳ್ಳು (sikkihākikoḷḷu) — to get stuck, to get caught.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to take off, then the Kannada for to fail.

You'll want to know: ಸಿಕ್ಕಿಹಾಕಿಕೊಳ್ಳು.
ಸಿಕ್ಕಿಹಾಕಿಕೊಳ್ಳು — sikkihākikoḷḷu — "to get stuck, to get caught".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಿಕ್ಕಿಹಾಕಿಕೊಳ್ಳು (sikkihākikoḷḷu) is sikki, "having got caught", from native sikku, "to become entangled, be caught" (DEDR 2498), with hāku, "to put", whose history neither DEDR nor CDIAL gives, and koḷḷu, "to take" (DEDR 2151), which turns the action back on yourself.
