## You'll want to know: 成本
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C384-BECOME384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**成本** — *chéngběn* — "production cost; cost".

> **请保安的成本很高** *qǐng bǎo'ān de chéngběn hěn gāo* — It costs a lot to hire security guards.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**成本** (*chéngběn*) is built from 成 'to complete' and 本 'the root', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *pwonX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the foot of a tree': what a finished thing rests on. The sense 'cost' is not given there.
