Malayalam, chapter 295: Pressure cooker, Mixer-grinder, Grinding stone, Mortar, Pestle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കുക്കർ (kukkar) — a pressure cooker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a chain, then the Malayalam for a stick.

You'll want to know: കുക്കർ.
കുക്കർ — kukkar — "a pressure cooker".

The word, taken apart.
കുക്കർ (kukkar) from English cooker, as in pressure cooker.
