## You'll want to know: la pièce montée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C311-OUI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la pièce montée** — "the tiered wedding cake".

> À la fin du repas, on a servi la pièce montée.
>
> *At the end of the meal, the wedding cake was served.*

A tower of small cream puffs, a classic of French weddings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pièce* is inherited from Late Latin *pettia* "a piece", of Gaulish origin (EtymDB); *montée* is built on *monter* "to go up", from Latin *mōns* "mountain" (Lewis & Short).
