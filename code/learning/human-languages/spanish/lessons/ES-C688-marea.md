## You'll want to know: la marea
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C688-MAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la marea** — "the tide".

> Con la marea baja se puede pasar a la isla.
>
> *At low tide you can walk across to the island.*

*Marea alta* / *baja* — high / low tide.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marea* was borrowed from French *marée* "tide", built on *mer* "sea", from Latin *mare* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
