# haré · tendré · podré — three stems, two ending sets

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-HACER, ES-LEX-TENER, ES-LEX-PODER, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-FUTURE-SINGULAR, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Regular verbs keep the whole infinitive. Three common verbs you
already know use a shorter or reshaped stem. Learn only these three today. In
each row, the **same** stem serves the future and the conditional.

## Grammar Lens: hacer becomes har-
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-GRAMMAR-HACER-FUTURE-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR]; assesses=[ES-LEX-HACER, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-FUTURE-SINGULAR, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR] -->

| person | future | conditional |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **haré** | **haría** |
| tú | **harás** | **harías** |
| él / ella / usted | **hará** | **haría** |

Use the known noun in **Haré café** — “I will make coffee” — and **Haría
café** — “I would make coffee.”

## Grammar Lens: tener becomes tendr-
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-GRAMMAR-TENER-FUTURE-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR]; assesses=[ES-LEX-TENER, ES-GRAMMAR-HACER-FUTURE-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR] -->

| person | future | conditional |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **tendré** | **tendría** |
| tú | **tendrás** | **tendrías** |
| él / ella / usted | **tendrá** | **tendría** |

The **d** in **tendr-** arose during the history of the compact future and
conditional. Treat **tendr-** as the learned stem, not as permission to insert
**d** into a new verb.

## Grammar Lens: poder becomes podr-
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-GRAMMAR-PODER-FUTURE-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR]; assesses=[ES-LEX-PODER, ES-GRAMMAR-TENER-FUTURE-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR] -->

| person | future | conditional |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **podré** | **podría** |
| tú | **podrás** | **podrías** |
| él / ella / usted | **podrá** | **podría** |

**Podré hablar español** says “I will be able to speak Spanish.” **Podría
hablar español** says “I could / would be able to speak Spanish.”

These stems reflect inherited reductions and sound changes. The useful modern
fact is bounded: **har- · tendr- · podr-**, each followed by the same endings
you already learned.
