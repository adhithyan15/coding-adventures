# اشتعل (ishtaʿala) — to catch fire, to blaze

## You'll want to know: اشتعل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C298-HARIQ298-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتعل** — *ishtaʿala* — "to catch fire, to blaze".

> اشتعلت النار في الخشب بسرعة.
>
> *ishtaʿalat an-nār fī al-khashab bi-surʿa.*
>
> The fire caught in the wood quickly.

Present: يشتعل, *yashtaʿil*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتعل** (*ishtaʿala*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-ʿ-l* (Lane), "to kindle a fire". It is Form VIII, "to take fire"; *shuʿla* is a flame.
