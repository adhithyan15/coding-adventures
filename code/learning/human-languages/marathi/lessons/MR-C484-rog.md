## You'll want to know: रोग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C484-ROG484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रोग** — *rog* — "a disease".

> स्वच्छ पाण्यामुळे बरेच रोग टळतात.
>
> *svaccha pāṇyāmuḷe barec rog ṭaḷtāt.*
>
> Clean water keeps many diseases away.

Masculine.

Masculine; plural रोग.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोग** (*rog*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *roga*, "disease" (CDIAL 10829).
