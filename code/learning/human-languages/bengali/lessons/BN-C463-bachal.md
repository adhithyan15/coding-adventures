## You'll want to know: বাচাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C463-DUSHTU463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বাচাল** — *bāchāl* — "talkative; chatty".

> বাচাল মেয়েটা সারাদিন গল্প করে।
>
> *bāchāl meyeṭā sārādin gôlpo kôre.*
>
> The chatty girl talks all day long.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাচাল** (*bāchāl*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vācāla*, "talkative, chattering", said also of birds (Monier-Williams).
