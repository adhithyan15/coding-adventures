Gujarati, chapter 447: A Morning Full of Joy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હરખ (harakh) — delight; joy at good news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pester, then the Gujarati for to hope.

You'll want to know: હરખ.
હરખ — harakh — "delight; joy at good news".
દીકરાનું લગ્ન નક્કી થયું, તેથી દાદીને ઘણો હરખ થયો. dīkrānũ lagna nakkī thayũ, tethī dādīne ghaṇo harakh thayo. My grandmother was filled with delight when her son's wedding was fixed.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હરખ (harakh) probably goes back to Sanskrit harṣa, "joy, delight", which Monier-Williams records; it is the everyday spoken shape of that learned word.

હરખાવું — harkhāvũ — "to rejoice; to be delighted".
પરિણામ સાંભળીને મીરા હરખાઈ ગઈ. pariṇām sā̃bhḷīne Mīrā harkhāī gaī. Mira was delighted when she heard the result.
Past (she …): તે હરખાઈ ગઈ, te harkhāī gaī.

The word, taken apart.
હરખાવું (harkhāvũ) is built on હરખ (harakh), "delight", with the verb ending -āvũ; the noun probably goes back to Sanskrit harṣa, "joy" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઉમળકો (umaḷko) — warmth of feeling; eager affection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for delight, then the Gujarati for to rejoice.

You'll want to know: ઉમળકો.
ઉમળકો — umaḷko — "warmth of feeling; eager affection".
માસીએ ઉમળકાથી બારણું ખોલ્યું. māsīe umaḷkāthī bārṇũ kholyũ. My aunt opened the door with warm delight.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉમળકો (umaḷko) goes back to a reconstructed ummal-, "rise, bubble up": a feeling that wells over. CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "emotion, ardent love" (CDIAL 2346).

Lesson 4 of 5.
અનુભવવું (anubhavvũ) — to feel; to experience.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to rejoice, then the Gujarati for warmth of feeling.

You'll want to know: અનુભવવું.
અનુભવવું — anubhavvũ — "to feel; to experience".
લગ્નમાં મેં ઘણી ખુશી અનુભવી. lagnamā̃ mẽ ghaṇī khuśī anubhavī. I felt great happiness at the wedding.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ખુશી અનુભવી, teṇe khuśī anubhavī.

The word, taken apart.
અનુભવવું (anubhavvũ) is built on અનુભવ (anubhav), "experience", a tatsama of Sanskrit anubhava, "perception, fruition" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -vũ.
