## You'll want to know: ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C300-CROP300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ** — *hāṛhī* — "the spring harvest; the winter-sown crop".

> ਕਣਕ ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ ਦੀ ਫ਼ਸਲ ਹੈ।
>
> *kaṇak hāṛhī dī fasal hai.*
>
> Wheat is a crop of the spring harvest.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ** (*hāṛhī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *āṣāḍhīya*, "pertaining to the month Āṣāḍha", the month you know as ਹਾੜ; CDIAL lists Punjabi *hāṛhī* and *hāṛī*, "crops sown in winter", under it (CDIAL 1474).
