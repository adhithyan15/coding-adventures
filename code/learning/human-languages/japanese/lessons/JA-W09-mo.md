# も — two bars, then the curve

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-MOU, JA-SCRIPT-U-01, JA-SCRIPT-MI-01] -->

[PAUSE 10s] Write known **う** and **み**, then say *mo | o*.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-MO-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **も** — *mo*

Three strokes. Draw the central curve first, from the upper left down and around.
Then add the upper short horizontal and the lower short horizontal. Both bars
cross the central stroke.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-MO-01, JA-LEX-MOU, JA-SCRIPT-U-01] -->
1. Copy **も** once.
2. Hide it for ten seconds and write it again.
3. Add known **う**: **もう**. Read *mo | o*.
