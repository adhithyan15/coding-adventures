## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-PLANS-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C148-XIAGEYUE-01, ZH-LEX-C148-XIAXINGQI-01, ZH-LEX-C148-SHANGGEYUE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YAO-FUTURE-01, ZH-LEX-C148-DASUAN-01, ZH-LEX-C148-JIANGLAI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-BU-MEI-01] -->

> **A:** 下星期你要做什么
>
> *xià xīngqī nǐ yào zuò shénme* — What are you going to do next week?
>
> **B:** 下星期我要开车去大学
>
> *xià xīngqī wǒ yào kāichē qù dàxué* — Next week I'm going to drive to the university.
>
> **A:** 下个月呢
>
> *xià ge yuè ne* — And next month?
>
> **B:** 下个月我要去中国
>
> *xià ge yuè wǒ yào qù Zhōngguó* — Next month I'm going to China.
>
> **A:** 上个月你也去了中国吗
>
> *shàng ge yuè nǐ yě qù le Zhōngguó ma* — Did you go to China last month too?
>
> **B:** 没有 上个月我在家
>
> *méiyǒu, shàng ge yuè wǒ zài jiā* — No, last month I was at home.
>
> **A:** *jiānglái nǐ dǎsuàn zuò shénme* — What do you plan to do in the future?
>
> **B:** *wǒ xiǎng zuò lǎoshī* — I'd like to be a teacher.

要 before each verb says what is coming; 上个月 takes 了 because it is over. *méiyǒu* answers the question about the past, as 没 reports what did not happen.

## What you've built — next week, next month, some day
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-PLANS-01, ZH-SCRIPT-YUE-MONTH-01, ZH-LEX-C148-XIAGEYUE-01, ZH-LEX-C148-XIAXINGQI-01, ZH-LEX-C148-SHANGGEYUE-01, ZH-LEX-C148-JIANGLAI-01, ZH-LEX-C86-NAR-01] -->

Time words go first; 要 or *dǎsuàn* goes before the verb:

- **下星期我要开车去** *xià xīngqī wǒ yào kāichē qù* — next week I'm going to drive.
- **下个月我要去中国** *xià ge yuè wǒ yào qù Zhōngguó* — next month I'm going to China.
- *jiānglái wǒ xiǎng zuò lǎoshī* — some day I'd like to teach.

**Listen.** *Xià ge yuè nǐ yào qù nǎr?*

**Speak.** Say what you are going to do next week, then next month.

[YOU WRITE: 下个月 — 月 is the moon piece of 明, standing alone]
