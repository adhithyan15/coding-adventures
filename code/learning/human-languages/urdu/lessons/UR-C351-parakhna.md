## You'll want to know: پرکھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C351-MUTALAA351-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پرکھنا** — *parakhnā* — "to assess; to put to the test".

> استاد نے بچوں کا کام پرکھا۔
>
> *ustād ne bachchoṅ kā kām parkhā.*
>
> The teacher assessed the children's work.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کام پرکھا, *us ne kām parkhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پرکھنا** (*parakhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *parakhnā*, "to test", under Sanskrit *párīkṣate*, "examines" (CDIAL 7904).
