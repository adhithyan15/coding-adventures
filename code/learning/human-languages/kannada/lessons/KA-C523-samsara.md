# ಸಂಸಾರ (saṃsāra) — family life; a household

## You'll want to know: ಸಂಸಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C523-SAMSARA523-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಸಾರ** — *saṃsāra* — "family life; a household".

> ಅಕ್ಕ ತನ್ನ ಸಂಸಾರವನ್ನು ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ನಡೆಸುತ್ತಾಳೆ.
>
> *akka tanna saṃsāravannu cennāgi naḍesuttāḷe.*
>
> My elder sister runs her household well.

ಸಂಸಾರಿ, a family man or woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಸಾರ** (*saṃsāra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃsāra*, from *saṃ-sṛ*, "to go round", the round of worldly life (Monier-Williams); in Kannada it is the life of a family.

## What you've built
