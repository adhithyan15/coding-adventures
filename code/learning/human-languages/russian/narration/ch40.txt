Russian, chapter 40: Sleep, Sit, Stand, Run, Play.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
спать (spat) — to sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to sell, then the Russian for to swim.

You'll want to know: спать.
спать (spat) — "to sleep".

The word, taken apart.
спать (spat) from Proto-Slavic sŭpati — inherited from PIE swep-, "to sleep". Vasmer lists Sanskrit svápiti, "sleeps", Old English swefan, "to sleep", and Latin sōpiō, "I lull to sleep".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to stand, then the Russian for to run.

You'll want to know: играть.
играть (igrát) — "to play".

The word, taken apart.
играть (igrát) goes with игра, "a game", from Proto-Slavic jĭgra, jĭgrati — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian áikštytis, "to be playful", and Sanskrit ejati, "stirs, moves".
