## You'll want to know: yǐfáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C389-INCASE389-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yǐfáng* — "so as to guard against; as a precaution".

> *dài yìdiǎnr qián yǐfáng wànyī* — Take a bit of money with you, in case.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǐfáng* is built from 以 'by means of' and *fáng* 'to guard against', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yiX* and *bjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to use' and the second 'to defend' and 'a dyke'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
