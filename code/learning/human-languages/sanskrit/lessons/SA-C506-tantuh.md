## You'll want to know: तन्तुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C506-VAYATI506-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तन्तुः** — *tantuḥ* — "a strand; a thread of the warp".

> सा सूच्यां तन्तुं स्थापयति।
>
> *sā sūcyāṃ tantuṃ sthāpayati.*
>
> She threads the needle.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तन्तुः** (*tantuḥ*) comes from the root √tan, "to stretch, to spin out"; MW gives *tantu* as a thread, a cord, the warp of a web, a fibre (MW).
