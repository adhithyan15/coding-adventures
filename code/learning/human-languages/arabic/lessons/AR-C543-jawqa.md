## You'll want to know: جوقة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C543-MUSIQA543-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جوقة** — *jawqa* — "a choir".

> تغني ابنتي في جوقة المدرسة.
>
> *tughannī ibnatī fī jawqat al-madrasa.*
>
> My daughter sings in the school choir.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جوقة** (*jawqa*) is a word for a band or company of people, and today a choir; the origin is not settled.
