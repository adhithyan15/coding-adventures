## You'll want to know: происшествие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C285-SOBYTIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**происшествие** (*proisshéstviye*) — "an incident".

> Вчера на улице было странное происшествие.
>
> *Vcherá na úlitse býlo stránnoye proisshéstviye.*
>
> Yesterday there was a strange incident in the street.

Neuter. Usually something unpleasant or unexpected.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*происшествие* is built on *произойти* "to happen" with the stem *шеств-* "going", seen in Church Slavonic *šĭstvie* "a going, a procession" (EtymDB).
