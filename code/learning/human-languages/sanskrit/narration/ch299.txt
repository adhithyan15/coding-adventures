Lesson 1 of 5.
घोषयति (ghoṣayati) — announces.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a citizen, then the Sanskrit for what is left.

You'll want to know: घोषयति.
घोषयति — ghoṣayati — "announces".
अधिकारी उच्चैः घोषयति। adhikārī uccaiḥ ghoṣayati. The official announces it loudly.
Past (he …): सः उच्चैः घोषितवान्, saḥ uccaiḥ ghoṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
घोषयति (ghoṣayati) comes from the root √ghuṣ, "to sound, cry aloud, announce publicly"; ghoṣayati is its causative (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
संकटम् (saṅkaṭam) — danger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for what is left, then the Sanskrit for announces.

You'll want to know: संकटम्.
संकटम् — saṅkaṭam — "danger".
नद्याः समीपे संकटम् अस्ति। nadyāḥ samīpe saṅkaṭam asti. There is danger near the river.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
संकटम् (saṅkaṭam) MW thinks it is probably a Prakrit form of saṃkṛta, "brought together", built from sam and the root √kṛ; its senses run from narrow to dangerous (MW).

सावधानः — sāvadhānaḥ — "careful".
भवान् सावधानः भवतु। bhavān sāvadhānaḥ bhavatu. Please be careful.

The word, taken apart.
सावधानः (sāvadhānaḥ) is built from sa, "with", and avadhāna, "attention"; MW gives sāvadhāna as attentive, heedful, careful (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
निवारयति (nivārayati) — keeps away; prevents.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for danger, then the Sanskrit for careful.

You'll want to know: निवारयति.
निवारयति — nivārayati — "keeps away; prevents".
रक्षकः जनान् नद्याः निवारयति। rakṣakaḥ janān nadyāḥ nivārayati. The guard keeps people away from the river.
Past (he …): सः जनान् निवारितवान्, saḥ janān nivāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
निवारयति (nivārayati) is built from ni and the causative of the root √vṛ, "to ward off"; MW gives ni-vārayati as to hold back from, prevent and stop (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
आपत्तिः (āpattiḥ) — a misfortune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for careful, then the Sanskrit for keeps away.

You'll want to know: आपत्तिः.
आपत्तिः — āpattiḥ — "a misfortune".
सहसा ग्रामे आपत्तिः आगच्छति। sahasā grāme āpattiḥ āgacchati. Suddenly misfortune comes to the village.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आपत्तिः (āpattiḥ) is built from ā and the root √pad, "to fall": MW gives āpatti as happening, incurring and misfortune or calamity (MW).
