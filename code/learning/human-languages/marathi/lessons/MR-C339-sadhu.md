## You'll want to know: साधू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C339-NAYAK339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**साधू** — *sādhū* — "a holy man; an ascetic".

> जंगलात एक म्हातारा साधू राहत होता.
>
> *jaṅglāt ek mhātārā sādhū rāhat hotā.*
>
> An old holy man lived in the forest.

Masculine.

Plural साधू.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साधू** (*sādhū*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sādhu*, 'straight, good; a holy man' (Monier-Williams).
