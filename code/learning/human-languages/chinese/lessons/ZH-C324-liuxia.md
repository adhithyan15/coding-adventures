## You'll want to know: liúxià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C324-RECALL324-03]; assesses=[] -->

*liúxià* — "to leave behind".

> *yéye gěi wǒmen liúxià le yì běn gǔshū* — Grandpa left us an old book.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liúxià* is built from *liú* 'to stay' and 下 'down', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljuw* and *haeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stay' and 'to stop' and the second 'below'.

## What you've built
