## You'll want to know: abwaschen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C531-KUECHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**abwaschen** — "to wash up, to do the dishes".

> Nach dem Abendessen wäscht mein Bruder ab.
>
> *After dinner my brother does the washing-up.*

Separable, irregular: *wusch ab, hat abgewaschen*. Also *spülen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abwaschen* is built on *ab* "off" and *waschen* "to wash", inherited from Old High German *wascan*, from Proto-Germanic *waskaną* (EtymDB).
