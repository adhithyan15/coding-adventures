## You'll want to know: تصفيق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C542-MASRAH542-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تصفيق** — *taṣfīq* — "applause".

> سمعنا تصفيقا طويلا بعد الأغنية.
>
> *samiʿnā taṣfīqan ṭawīlan baʿda al-ughniya.*
>
> We heard long applause after the song.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تصفيق** (*taṣfīq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-f-q*, "to strike, to clap" (Lane). Taṣfīq is the verbal noun of Form II.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
