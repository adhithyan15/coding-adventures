# ಕೈಗೂಡು (kaigūḍu) — to come true; to work out

## You'll want to know: ಕೈಗೂಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C419-VIPHALA419-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈಗೂಡು** — *kaigūḍu* — "to come true; to work out".

> ಮನೆ ಕಟ್ಟುವ ಅವರ ಕನಸು ಕೈಗೂಡಿತು.
>
> *mane kaṭṭuva avara kanasu kaigūḍitu.*
>
> Their dream of building a house came true.

Past (it …): ಕೈಗೂಡಿತು, *kaigūḍitu*.

Of plans and wishes, never of people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈಗೂಡು** (*kaigūḍu*) is native Dravidian, built from *kai*, "hand" (DEDR 2023), and *kūḍu*, "to join" (DEDR 1882): to come into the hand.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
