## You'll want to know: убыток
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C361-PRIBYL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**убыток** (*ubýtok*) — "a loss (of money)".

> Магазин работает в убыток.
>
> *Magazín rabótayet v ubýtok.*
>
> The shop is running at a loss.

Masculine; genitive *убытка*. Usually plural: *большие убытки*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**убыток** is built on *убыть* "to decrease", from *у-* "away" and *быть* "to be", from Proto-Slavic *\*byti*: what goes away.
