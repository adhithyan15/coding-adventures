# மூன்று மாதமாக (mūṉṟu mātamāka) — for how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C291-THAN-01, TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-04, TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-05, TA-LEX-C202-NERAM202-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "The train is better than the bus." (*pēruntaiviṭa rayil nallatu*.) Then the words for a month, a year and "until". (*mātam*, *āṇṭu*, *varai*.)

## You'll want to know: -ஆக for a span of time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C291-SPAN-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-NUMBERS-1-5-01, TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-04, TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-05, TA-LEX-PADI-01, TA-LEX-C91-WEATHER-04, TA-LEX-DATIVE-SUBJECT-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

> **நான் மூன்று மாதமாகத் தமிழ் படிக்கிறேன்.**
>
> *nāṉ mūṉṟu mātamākat tamiḻ paṭikkiṟēṉ.* — "I have been learning Tamil for three months."

The span takes **-ஆக**, the **-ஆக** of **நன்றாக**: **மூன்று மாதமாக**, "for three months". Tamil keeps the verb in the present, because the learning is still going on; English says "have been". After **-ஆக** the first letter of the next word doubles in writing: **மாதமாகத் தமிழ்**.

**வருடம்** (*varuṭam*, "a year"), the everyday word beside **ஆண்டு**, works the same: **இரண்டு வருடமாக** (*iraṇṭu varuṭamāka*, "for two years").

For a span that is over, the past needs no **-ஆக**: **நான் இரண்டு ஆண்டு மதுரையில் இருந்தேன்** (*nāṉ iraṇṭu āṇṭu maturaiyil iruntēṉ*, "I lived in Madurai for two years").

## You'll want to know: from … until, and how long
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C291-FROM-UNTIL-01]; assesses=[TA-ETYMON-KAALAI-01, TA-ETYMON-MAALAI-01, TA-LEX-C202-NERAM202-02, TA-LEX-EVVALAVU-01, TA-LEX-C48-LEAVE-02, TA-ETYMON-MANI-01] -->

> **காலையிலிருந்து மாலை வரை மழை பெய்தது.**
>
> *kālaiyiliruntu mālai varai maḻai peytatu.* — "It rained from morning until evening."

**காலையிலிருந்து** (*kālaiyiliruntu*, "from the morning") is **காலை** with **-இல்** and **-இருந்து**, "from", the same **இருந்து** as in **இங்கிருந்து**. **வரை** closes the span.

> **எவ்வளவு நேரம் ஆகும்?**
>
> *evvaḷavu nēram ākum?* — "How long will it take?"

> **ஒரு மணி நேரம் ஆகும்.**
>
> *oru maṇi nēram ākum.* — "It will take an hour."

**ஆகும்** (*ākum*, "it will become, it will take"), the "it" future of **ஆகு**; **மணி நேரம்** is "an hour" of time.
