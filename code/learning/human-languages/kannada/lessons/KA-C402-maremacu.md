## You'll want to know: ಮರೆಮಾಚು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C402-LANCHA402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರೆಮಾಚು** — *maremācu* — "to conceal".

> ಅವನು ತನ್ನ ತಪ್ಪನ್ನು ಮರೆಮಾಚಿದನು.
>
> *avanu tanna tappannu maremācidanu.*
>
> He concealed his mistake.

Past (I …): ಮರೆಮಾಚಿದೆ, *maremācide*.

What you hide takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರೆಮಾಚು** (*maremācu*) is native Kannada, a compound whose first part is *maṟe*, "concealment, a cover" (DEDR 4760); the second part, *mācu*, is not settled.
