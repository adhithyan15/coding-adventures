Telugu, chapter 206: To roll, To slip, To hang, To tread on, To hit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
దొర్లు (dorlu) — to roll (over).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to find out, then the Telugu for to borrow.

You'll want to know: దొర్లు.
దొర్లు — dorlu — "to roll (over)".

The word, taken apart.
దొర్లు (dorlu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3514 lists doralu, "to roll, turn over", with Kui tronga, "to roll", and Gondi dorg-, "to wallow".

జారు (jāru) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil cāṟu and Kannada jāṟu (DEDR 2482). DEDR lists Telugu jāṟu, "to slide, slip".

Lesson 4 of 5.
తొక్కు (tokku) — to tread on, to pedal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to slip, then the Telugu for to hang.

You'll want to know: తొక్కు.
తొక్కు — tokku — "to tread on, to pedal".

The word, taken apart.
తొక్కు (tokku) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3522 gives it as trokku, "to tread, trample", with Kannada tuḻi, "to tread". DEDR 3477 tokku, "a chutney", is a different word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
కొట్టు (koṭṭu) — to hit, to beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: కొట్టు.
కొట్టు — koṭṭu — "to hit, to beat".

The word, taken apart.
కొట్టు (koṭṭu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil koṭṭu and Malayalam koṭṭuka (DEDR 2063). DEDR lists Telugu koṭṭu, "to beat, strike". DEDR compares Pali koṭṭeti, "beats" (CDIAL 3241).
