# Punjabi A1 mock rubric

This shared rubric scores either Punjabi A1 mock against
[`task-shapes/a1.json`](../../task-shapes/a1.json). Reading, listening, writing,
and speaking are separate 100-point papers. A learner meets this form's scoring
threshold only by earning at least 60/100 on every paper in the same
administration and attempting every required part. The 400-point headline sum
cannot compensate for a paper below its independent threshold.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-forms -->
### Signs and forms — 35 points

Divide 35 points equally across the seven keyed items. Score meaning,
practical-detail recognition, and Gurmukhi script recognition only.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-forms -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->
### Short messages — 35 points

Divide 35 points equally across the seven keyed items. Score gist, explicit
detail, and communicative-purpose recognition only.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-personal-descriptions -->
### Personal descriptions — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the six keyed items. Score person-detail
association, explicit information, and simple inference. Reading aloud earns
no reading credit.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-personal-descriptions -->

## Listening — 100 points

Use only the confidential mastered files, play every file twice at 90–110
words per minute, and keep each mastered part inside its published duration
band. Do not show a transcript or romanization.

<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-announcements -->
### Announcements — 35 points

Divide 35 points equally across the six keyed items. Score gist and stated
time, place, or practical detail.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-announcements -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->
### Short exchanges — 35 points

Divide 35 points equally across the six keyed items. Score exchange gist,
speaker intention, and specific detail.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-personal-account -->
### Personal account — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the five keyed items. Score account gist,
sequence, and personal detail.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-personal-account -->

## Writing — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
### Practical form — 40 points

Score seven requested fields analytically: task fulfilment and requested
content 16; Punjabi control 8; Gurmukhi orthographic control 8; and legibility
and field boundaries 8. A blank field earns no content credit. A romanized,
traced, translated, or copied response earns no independent-writing credit.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->
### Named-reader purpose message — 60 points

Score task fulfilment and relevant content 20; named reader and purpose 10;
comprehensibility and organisation 10; Punjabi vocabulary and grammar 12; and
Gurmukhi orthographic control 8. A response outside 30–40 words may still earn
language credit but cannot earn full task-fulfilment credit.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->

## Speaking — 100 points

Within each part score task fulfilment and relevant content, interaction and
repair, Punjabi vocabulary and grammar, and pronunciation and fluency. The
examiner may repeat a prompt once but may not supply an answer. Support-language
only speech earns no Punjabi task credit.

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
### Personal interview — 30 points

Score five relevant short responses. Natural pauses, a consistent gender
choice, and one genuine self-repair are acceptable.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
### Prepared description — 30 points

Score a coherent one-minute description of a familiar place. Preparation
notes may contain keywords but not a written answer script.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
### Transactional role-play — 40 points

Score completion of the request, price handling, interaction and repair, and a
polite close. Do not require a form-specific answer-key example word for word.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->

## Readiness boundary

These artifacts make the project-defined mock structurally executable. They do
not by themselves establish pedagogical quality, exam readiness, attainment,
or external equivalence. The repository must still record a clean timed
administration, qualified Punjabi review, standard setting, and book-only
human validation before making a pass or learner-readiness claim.
