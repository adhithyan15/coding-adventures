Tamil, chapter 125: Idli, Dosa, Vadai, Coffee, Sea.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
இட்லி (iṭli) — an idli, a steamed rice cake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for garlic, then the Tamil for honey.

You'll want to know: இட்லி.
இட்லி — iṭli — "an idli, a steamed rice cake".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of இட்லி (iṭli) is uncertain, and DEDR has no entry for it. Food historians usually connect it with iḍḍalige, a food named in a Kannada text of the tenth century.

Lesson 2 of 5.
தோசை (tōcai) — a dosa, a thin pancake.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Tamil for honey, then the Tamil for an idli.

You'll want to know: தோசை.
தோசை — tōcai — "a dosa, a thin pancake".

The word, taken apart.
தோசை (tōcai) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3542 lists Malayalam dōśa and Kannada dōse. The entry also compares Sanskrit dhosaka.
