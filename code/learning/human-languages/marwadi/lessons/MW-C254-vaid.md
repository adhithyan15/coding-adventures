## You'll want to know: वैद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C254-VAID254-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वैद** — *vaid* — "a traditional healer; a physician".

> गांव मांय एक वैद है।
>
> *gā̃v mā̃y ek vaid hai.*
>
> There is a healer in the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वैद** (*vaid*) goes back to Sanskrit *vaidya*, "learned; a physician" (Monier-Williams), with its last syllable worn away in speech.
