# привет — watch one word move

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-PRIVET, RU-SCRIPT-P-01, RU-SCRIPT-R-01, RU-SCRIPT-I-01, RU-SCRIPT-V-01, RU-SCRIPT-E-01, RU-SCRIPT-T-01] -->

Look at **привет**. Point from left to right and say the six sounds slowly:
*p, r, ee, v, ye, t*. Every shape is already known.

## Writing — observe and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-PRIVET, RU-SCRIPT-P-01, RU-SCRIPT-R-01, RU-SCRIPT-I-01, RU-SCRIPT-V-01, RU-SCRIPT-E-01, RU-SCRIPT-T-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Keep **привет** visible. Trace it once with a finger or a capped pen. Pause on
**р** and say *r*; pause on **в** and say *v*. Stop after one careful trace.
