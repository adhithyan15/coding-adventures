## You'll want to know: zhàoxiàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C207-PLACE207-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhàoxiàng* — "to take a photo".

> *zhèr kěyǐ zhàoxiàng ma* — Can I take photos here?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhàoxiàng* is built from *zhào* 'to shine on' and *xiàng* 'a likeness', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyewH* and *sjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'bright' and the second 'to look at'.
