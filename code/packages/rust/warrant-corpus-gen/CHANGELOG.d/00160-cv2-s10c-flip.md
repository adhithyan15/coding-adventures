## 0.6.0 - CV2-S10c: the atomic flip, the fault-evaluation gate opens for `ir.fixed` and `ir.tuple`

The generator half of the one atomic PR of VER04r section 13 "S10c (flip)" and [D29], with
`warrant-oracle` 0.5.0 (the generator's `--check` and the oracle's `verify-v2` both read the ct-compare fixture, so
the two gates open together).

### Changed

- `faults::SHIPPED_GATE` is `[Level::Core, Level::Fixed, Level::Tuple]` (was `[Level::Core]`); the type is
  `[Level; 3]`. `emit::generate` (and so the command line) now searches the witnesses of every `ir.fixed` and
  `ir.tuple` fault instead of recording `deferred`. The closed gate stays reachable as
  `generate_gated(.., &[Level::Core])`. Version 0.5.0 to 0.6.0 (`Cargo.toml`, the `Cargo.lock` entry).
- Regenerated fixtures (each diff reviewed): ct-compare v2 `corpus.json` (`generator.version`, and the `slim_witness`
  of `eq_u64/ignore-high-bit` from `{"deferred": "ir.fixed"}` to `{"claim": ".../eq_u64_agrees", "chunk": 0}`; no chunk
  file, digest or `cases.tfv` byte moved); the oracle's `synth-v2` (`generator.version`, and the `bump/xor-lsb` and
  `wide/xor-lsb` witnesses from `deferred` to chunk 0; slim sets and digests unchanged), `accept-blind-v2` and
  `omit-example-v2` (`generator.version` only). The committed parity dumps did not move (P1/P2 pinned them to the
  closed gate).
- Tests: `tests/emission.rs` expects `eq_u64/ignore-high-bit` as `chunk 0`, asserts the shipped gate is open and
  adds `the_closed_gate_still_defers_the_ir_fixed_control` (keeps the `deferred` path covered through
  `generate_gated(.., &[Level::Core])`).

Specification: VER04r section 13, the S10c (flip) row is marked done.
