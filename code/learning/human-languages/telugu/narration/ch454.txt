Telugu, chapter 454: Flying Abroad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పైలటు (pailaṭu) — a pilot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to float away, then the Telugu for to be swept away.

You'll want to know: పైలటు.
పైలటు — pailaṭu — "a pilot".
విమానం దిగే ముందు పైలటు మాతో మాట్లాడాడు. vimānaṁ digē mundu pailaṭu mātō māṭlāḍāḍu. Before the plane landed, the pilot spoke to us.
Like other English loans it takes -ఉ: పైలటు (pailaṭu). A woman pilot is also పైలటు (pailaṭu).

The word, taken apart.
పైలటు (pailaṭu) is a loan from English pilot, with the Telugu final -ఉ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
అంతర్జాతీయ (antarjātīya) — international.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be swept away, then the Telugu for a pilot.

You'll want to know: అంతర్జాతీయ.
అంతర్జాతీయ — antarjātīya — "international".
మా నగరంలో కొత్త అంతర్జాతీయ విమానాశ్రయం ఉంది. mā nagaraṁlō kotta antarjātīya vimānāśrayaṁ undi. There is a new international airport in our city.
An adjective: అంతర్జాతీయ (antarjātīya) విమానం, an international flight. It stands before its noun and does not change.

The word, taken apart.
అంతర్జాతీయ (antarjātīya) is a learned word built from Sanskrit: antar, "between", jāti, "birth, a people, a nation", and the adjective ending -īya (Monier-Williams): between the nations.

Lesson 3 of 5.
స్వదేశం (svadēśaṁ) — one's homeland.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a pilot, then the Telugu for international.

You'll want to know: స్వదేశం.
స్వదేశం — svadēśaṁ — "one's homeland".
చాలా సంవత్సరాల తరువాత అతను స్వదేశానికి తిరిగి వచ్చాడు. cālā saṁvatsarāla taruvāta atanu svadēśāniki tirigi vaccāḍu. After many years he came back to his homeland.
స్వ, own, + దేశం, country. Its opposite is విదేశం, abroad.

The word, taken apart.
స్వదేశం (svadēśaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit svadeśa, "one's own place or country" (Monier-Williams), from sva, "own", and deśa, "a place, a country".

Lesson 4 of 5.
రవాణా (ravāṇā) — transport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for international, then the Telugu for one's homeland.

You'll want to know: రవాణా.
రవాణా — ravāṇā — "transport".
నగరంలో ప్రజా రవాణా బాగుంది. nagaraṁlō prajā ravāṇā bāgundi. Public transport in the city is good.
ప్రజా రవాణా (ravāṇā), public transport; సరుకు రవాణా (ravāṇā), the carrying of goods.

The word, taken apart.
రవాణా (ravāṇā) is usually traced to Persian ravāna, "setting out, dispatched", which came into Telugu through Hindustani; sending goods on their way became transport.

Lesson 5 of 5.
సాగనంపు (sāganampu) — to see off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for one's homeland, then the Telugu for transport.

You'll want to know: సాగనంపు.
సాగనంపు — sāganampu — "to see off".
మేము అత్తను విమానాశ్రయంలో సాగనంపాం. mēmu attanu vimānāśrayaṁlō sāganampāṁ. We saw our aunt off at the airport.
Past (I …): సాగనంపాను, sāganampānu.
సాగను, to go on, + అంపు, to send: to send someone on their way. Telugu families often go to the station to see a guest off.

The word, taken apart.
సాగనంపు (sāganampu) is native Dravidian: సాగను, from సాగు, "to go on, to proceed" (DEDR 2430), and అంపు, "to send" (DEDR 329).
