Punjabi, chapter 362: Skills I Have.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕਾਬਲ (kābal) — capable; able.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a scholarship, then the Punjabi for a master of an art.

You'll want to know: ਕਾਬਲ.
ਕਾਬਲ — kābal — "capable; able".
ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਕਾਬਲ ਹੈ। merā bharā bahut kābal hai. My brother is very capable.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਾਬਲ (kābal) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic qābil, "able".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਨਿਪੁੰਨ (nipunn) — skilled; expert at.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a master of an art, then the Punjabi for capable.

You'll want to know: ਨਿਪੁੰਨ.
ਨਿਪੁੰਨ — nipunn — "skilled; expert at".
ਉਹ ਲੱਕੜ ਦੇ ਕੰਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਨਿਪੁੰਨ ਹੈ। oh lakkaṛ de kamm vich nipunn hai. He is skilled at woodwork.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਿਪੁੰਨ (nipunn) is taken over from Sanskrit nipuṇa, "skilful", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਚਮਕਾਉਣਾ (chamkāuṇā) — to polish; to make something shine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for capable, then the Punjabi for skilled.

You'll want to know: ਚਮਕਾਉਣਾ.
ਚਮਕਾਉਣਾ — chamkāuṇā — "to polish; to make something shine".
ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਪਿੱਤਲ ਦੇ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਚਮਕਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ har hafte pittal de bhāṇḍe chamkāundā hāṁ. I polish the brass pots every week.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਚਮਕਾਏ, maiṁ bhāṇḍe chamkāe.

The word, taken apart.
ਚਮਕਾਉਣਾ (chamkāuṇā) is the causative partner of camakṇā, "to shine", which goes back to a reconstructed camakka, "sudden movement"; CDIAL lists Punjabi camakṇā, "to shine", under it (CDIAL 4676).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ਤਰਾਸ਼ਣਾ.
ਤਰਾਸ਼ਣਾ — tarāshṇā — "to carve; to cut to shape".
ਤਰਖਾਣ ਲੱਕੜ ਤਰਾਸ਼ਦਾ ਹੈ। tarkhāṇ lakkaṛ tarāshdā hai. The carpenter carves the wood.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਲੱਕੜ ਤਰਾਸ਼ੀ, maiṁ lakkaṛ tarāshī.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਰਾਸ਼ਣਾ (tarāshṇā) is a verb made in Punjabi from a loan, usually traced to Persian tarāsh, "cutting".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਣਾ (azmāuṇā) — to try out; to put to the test.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to polish, then the Punjabi for to carve.

You'll want to know: ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਣਾ.
ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਣਾ — azmāuṇā — "to try out; to put to the test".
ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਨਵਾਂ ਸੰਦ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ har navāṁ sand pahilāṁ azmāundā hāṁ. I try out every new tool first.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਨਵਾਂ ਸੰਦ ਅਜ਼ਮਾਇਆ, maiṁ navāṁ sand azmāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਣਾ (azmāuṇā) is a verb made in Punjabi from a loan, usually traced to Persian āzmā-, "to try, test", the stem of āzmūdan.
