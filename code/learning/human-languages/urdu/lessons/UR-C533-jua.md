## You'll want to know: جوا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C533-JUA533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جوا** — *juā* — "gambling".

> اگر تم نے جوا کھیلا تو سب ہار جاؤ گے۔
>
> *agar tum ne juā khelā to sab hār jāo ge.*
>
> If you gamble, you will lose everything.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جوا** (*juā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dyūta*, "gambling" (CDIAL 6608).
