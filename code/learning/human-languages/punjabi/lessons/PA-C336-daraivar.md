## You'll want to know: ਡਰਾਈਵਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C336-TRADE336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਰਾਈਵਰ** — *ḍarāīvar* — "a driver".

> ਸਾਡਾ ਡਰਾਈਵਰ ਬੱਸ ਬਹੁਤ ਹੌਲੀ ਚਲਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍā ḍarāīvar bass bahut haulī chalāundā hai.*
>
> Our driver drives the bus very slowly.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਰਾਈਵਰ** (*ḍarāīvar*) is borrowed from English *driver*.
