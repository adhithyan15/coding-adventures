## You'll want to know: battezzare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C308-BATTESIMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**battezzare** — "to christen, to baptise".

> Lo hanno battezzato a giugno.
>
> *They christened him in June.*

Also to give a name: *hanno battezzato la nave*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*battezzare* is inherited from Church Latin *baptizāre*, from Greek *baptízein* "to dip" (Lewis & Short).
