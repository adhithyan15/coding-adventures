## You'll want to know: برودة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C483-BARD483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**برودة** — *burūda* — "coldness; a chill".

> شعرت ببرودة في الغرفة.
>
> *shaʿartu bi-burūda fī al-ghurfa.*
>
> I felt a chill in the room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برودة** (*burūda*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-r-d*, "to be cold" (Lane). It is a verbal noun.
