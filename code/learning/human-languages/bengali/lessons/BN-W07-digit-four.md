## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FOUR-01]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-DIGIT-EIGHT-01, BN-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> ৪

That is the Bengali digit **four**, and you can say it: **চার**.

It is two loops, stacked. It is the shape of an **8**.

| shape | in the digits you already read | in Bengali |
|---|---|---|
| two stacked loops | eight | **four** |
| upright with one bowl | *nothing* | **eight** |

Read the top row again. Every reflex you have says *eight*, and it is wrong by
four every single time — on a price, on a bus number, on a year.

The first three digits of this chapter arrived meaning exactly what their shape
already meant to you. This one arrives meaning something else, and it is not a
near miss: **৪** and **8** are the same drawing.

The defence is the lesson before this one. If you are unsure, ask how many bowls:
**two bowls is four; one bowl is eight.**
