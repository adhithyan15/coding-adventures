# સગાઈ (sagāī) — engagement, betrothal

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C265-LOVE265-01, GU-LEX-C265-LOVE265-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to love, then the Gujarati for to marry.

## You'll want to know: સગાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C265-LOVE265-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સગાઈ** — *sagāī* — "engagement, betrothal".

> **શું તેની સગાઈ થઈ?** — *śũ tenī sagāī thaī?* — "Did she get engaged?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **સગાઈ** (*sagāī*) is not settled. It looks built on *sagũ*, "related", with *-āī*, but CDIAL has no entry for either word.
