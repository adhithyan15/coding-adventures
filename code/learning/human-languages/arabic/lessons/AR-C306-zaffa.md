## You'll want to know: زف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C306-URS306-04]; assesses=[] -->

**زف** — *zaffa* — "to escort a bride; to bring good news".

> زف الأهل العروس إلى بيتها الجديد.
>
> *zaffa al-ahl al-ʿarūs ilā baytihā al-jadīd.*
>
> The family led the bride to her new home.

Present: يزف, *yazuff*.

The taught زفاف, a wedding, is its verbal noun. زف إليه الخبر: he brought him the good news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زف** (*zaffa*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-f-f* (Lane), where *zaffa al-ʿarūs* is to conduct the bride to her husband with music and pageantry; *zifāf* is its verbal noun.
