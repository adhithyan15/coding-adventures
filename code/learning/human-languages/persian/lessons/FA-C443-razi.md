## You'll want to know: راضی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C443-ANAM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**راضی** (*râzi*) — "satisfied, pleased".

> ما از اتاق و صبحانه راضی بودیم.
>
> *Mâ az otâq va sobhâne râzi budim.*
>
> We were pleased with the room and the breakfast.

The cause takes *az*. Note the letter ض, said *z*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*râzi* was borrowed from Arabic *rāḍī* 'content, pleased' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
