## You'll want to know: 前
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C78-AGE78-03]; assesses=[] -->

**前** — *qián* — "before; ago; front".

> **三年前我八岁** *sān nián qián wǒ bā suì* — Three years ago I was eight.

The new character, in pieces: 前 — two horns over a bar, the moon piece read yuè on the left, and a blade of two verticals on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**前** (*qián*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'before, first'.
