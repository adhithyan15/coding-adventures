# યાદ રાખવું (yād rākhvũ) — to keep in mind

## You'll want to know: યાદ રાખવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C182-NOW182-04]; assesses=[] -->

**યાદ રાખવું** — *yād rākhvũ* — "to keep in mind".

> **હું આ તારીખ યાદ રાખીશ.** — *hũ ā tārīkh yād rākhīś* — "I will remember this date."

Word for word, to keep in memory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**યાદ રાખવું** (*yād rākhvũ*) is built from *yād*, "memory", usually traced to Persian *yād*, "memory", and the inherited *rākhvũ*, "to keep", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *rakṣati*, "guards" (CDIAL 10547).
