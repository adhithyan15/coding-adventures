Hindi, chapter 481: Lying Down and Getting About.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लेटना (leṭnā) — to lie down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an aroma, then the Hindi for to lick.

You'll want to know: लेटना.
लेटना — leṭnā — "to lie down".
खाने के बाद दादा थोड़ी देर लेटते हैं। khāne ke bād dādā thoṛī der leṭte haiṁ. After eating, my grandfather lies down for a while.
Past (he …): वह बिस्तर पर लेट गया, vah bistar par leṭ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
लेटना (leṭnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi leṭnā, "to lie down", under Sanskrit lēṭyáti (CDIAL 11109).

ठंड में दादी ने रजाई में पैर सिकोड़े। ṭhaṇḍ meṁ dādī ne rajāī meṁ pair sikoṛe. In the cold, my grandmother drew her feet up under the quilt.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नाक सिकोड़ी, usne nāk sikoṛī.

The word, taken apart.
सिकोड़ना (sikoṛnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi sikoṛnā, "to contract, compress, crumple together", under saṁkōṭayati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12833). nāk sikoṛnā, "to screw up one's nose", is to show distaste.
