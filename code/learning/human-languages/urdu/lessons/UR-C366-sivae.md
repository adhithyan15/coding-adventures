## You'll want to know: سوائے
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C366-IJAZAT366-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سوائے** — *sivāe* — "except (سوائے … کے)".

> اگر سوائے اس کے کوئی راستہ نہیں تو چلیے۔
>
> *agar sivāe is ke koī rāstā nahīṅ to chaliye.*
>
> If there is no other way but this, let us go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سوائے** (*sivāe*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *siwā*, "other than".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
