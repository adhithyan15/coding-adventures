# HL109 — Punjabi A2 `ਪਿਆ` past-necessity prerequisite

Issue: #18528

After PR #18524 merged, the maintained aggregate-only audit of the preserved
confidential Punjabi A2 mock drafts against exact merged main
`d70b1afc3de8ae83b641d8ca8fe9565d901798f6` selects **ਪਿਆ** as the next
dependency-ordered form without an instructional owner. It occurs twice in the
confidential learner material and once in the confidential examiner material.
Neither confidential body nor the remaining candidate set belongs in the
public corpus.

Chapter 208 closes that one-form gap through one deliberately narrow frame:
**ਮੈਨੂੰ ਜਾਣਾ ਪਿਆ** means “I had to go.” The voice-first opener retrieves only
already-owned **ਮੈਨੂੰ** and **ਜਾਣਾ**. One lesson introduces base auxiliary
**ਪੈਣਾ** only far enough to own exact masculine singular perfective **ਪਿਆ**,
an immediate lesson retrieves its sound, meaning, and script, a bounded frame
lesson combines it with the familiar dative experiencer and infinitive, and a
four-skill payoff retrieves the complete frame again. The target has one direct
owner and three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored,
generated-book, or narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-FORM-PIA-208`. The direct owner presents **ਪਿਆ** only as the
masculine singular perfective form used after the infinitive in this one past
necessity construction. The chapter does not teach present or future
obligation, feminine or plural forms, the wider agreement paradigm, literal
“fall” or “lie” senses, or emphatic and idiomatic variants. Every Gurmukhi
codepoint is already script-taught, while the rendered **ਪੈ** and **ਪਿਆ**
sequences are already visible in earlier owned headwords such as **ਪੈਰ**,
**ਪਿਆਸ**, and **ਪਿਆਰ**; this chapter therefore adds no script owner.

Punjabi University Patiala's reference grammar describes the `-ਣਾ ਪੈ-`
construction as “have to,” says that its experiencer uses **ਨੂੰ**, and gives
both **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਨਾ ਪੈਣਾ** and **ਤੈਨੂੰ ਜਾਣਾ ਪੈਣਾ** together with a past
“had to study” example on page 128:
<https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/assets/A%20Reference%20Grammar_Final.pdf>.
AQA's official Panjabi grammar independently includes the infinitive,
past/perfective forms, and auxiliary **ਪੈ**:
<https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/panjabi/a-level/panjabi-7682/specification/subject-content/grammar>.

Those sources support the construction and the one bounded past frame. They do
not justify a new etymology for an inflected form: the course keeps **ਪਿਆ**
whole, relates it only to the just-presented auxiliary base, and makes no
broader historical claim.

This is the thirty-first prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test
case; no shared aggregate is introduced.
