## You'll want to know: चौको
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C184-OKHLI184-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चौको** — *chauko* — "the cooking place".

> चौको साफ है।
>
> *chauko sāph hai.*
>
> The cooking place is clean.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चौको** (*chauko*) is inherited from Sanskrit *catuṣka*, "a set of four", also "a square courtyard"; CDIAL lists Hindi *caukā*, a place for cooking and eating (CDIAL 4629). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
