## You'll want to know: dàtáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C425-HOTEL425-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dàtáng* — "hotel lobby".

> *wǒ zài jiǔdiàn de dàtáng děng nǐ* — I'll wait for you in the hotel lobby.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàtáng* is built from 大 'big' and *táng* 'a hall', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *daH* and *dang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
