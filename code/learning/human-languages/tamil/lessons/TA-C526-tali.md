## You'll want to know: தாளி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C526-THALIPPU526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**தாளி** — *tāḷi* — "to temper spices".

> அம்மா கடுகையும் கறிவேப்பிலையையும் எண்ணெயில் தாளித்தாள்.
>
> *ammā kaṭukaiyum kaṟivēppilaiyaiyum eṇṇeyil tāḷittāḷ.*
>
> Mother tempered mustard seeds and curry leaves in oil.

Past (I …): தாளித்தேன், *tāḷittēṉ*.

Spices fried in hot oil and poured over a dish. The noun is தாளிப்பு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தாளி** (*tāḷi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3186 gives தாளி (*tāḷi*), "to season and flavour a curry with spices fried in ghee or oil".
