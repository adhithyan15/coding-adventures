## You'll want to know: le fou rire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C474-GAIETE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le fou rire** — "fit of the giggles".

> Pendant le cours, nous avons eu un fou rire.
>
> *During the lesson we got the giggles.*

*Avoir le fou rire* — to be unable to stop laughing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le fou rire* is "mad laughter": *fou* is inherited from Latin *follis* "bellows, a bag of wind", and *rire* from Latin *rīdēre* "to laugh" (Lewis & Short).
