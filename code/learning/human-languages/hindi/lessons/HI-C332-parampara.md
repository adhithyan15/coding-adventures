## You'll want to know: परंपरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C332-PARAMPARA332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परंपरा** — *paramparā* — "a tradition".

> यह परंपरा सौ साल पुरानी है।
>
> *yah paramparā sau sāl purānī hai.*
>
> This tradition is a hundred years old.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परंपरा** (*paramparā*) is Sanskrit *paramparā*, "an unbroken series, one after another" (Monier-Williams): what passes from each generation to the next.
