## You'll want to know: যোগ দেওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C494-KAJKHOJA494-05]; assesses=[] -->

**যোগ দেওয়া** — *jog deoyā* — "to join a job; to join (a group)".

> আমি সোমবার নতুন অফিসে যোগ দিলাম।
>
> *Āmi sombār nôtun ôphise jog dilām.*
>
> I joined the new office on Monday.

Past (he/she …): তিনি নতুন কোম্পানিতে যোগ দিলেন, *tini nôtun kompānite jog dilen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**যোগ দেওয়া** (*jog deoyā*) joins যোগ, Sanskrit *yoga*, "joining" (Monier-Williams), to দেওয়া, "to give": to give oneself to a place of work.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
