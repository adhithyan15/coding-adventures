## You'll want to know: saler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C443-HIVER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**saler** — "to salt (food, roads)".

> La ville sale les routes quand il gèle.
>
> *The town puts salt on the roads when it freezes.*

*C'est salé !* — that is pricey (informal).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saler* goes back to Vulgar Latin *salāre*, from Latin *sal* 'salt' (EtymDB).
