Punjabi, chapter 166: Return to Running, Wandering, and Stopping.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
An old word for running — to run, recalled after a long gap.

Hear one familiar meaning, pause, and retrieve its Punjabi word before the model. This is a gentle return, not a new word or a scored mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to run.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਭੱਜਣਾ (bhajjṇā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਭੱਜਣਾ (bhajjṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to run. Then hear to run again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਭੱਜਣਾ (bhajjṇā) before the model. Only this old word is needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
One last cue: to run.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਭੱਜਣਾ (bhajjṇā). Listen and retry once if it does not come back.


Lesson 2 of 3.
An old word for wandering — to wander or go around, recalled after a long gap.

You already met this word for going around. Retrieve it gently by itself; there is no new Punjabi and no scored mock here.

Warm-up.
Hear to wander, to go around.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਘੁੰਮਣਾ (ghummṇā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਘੁੰਮਣਾ (ghummṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to wander, to go around. Then hear the English cue again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਘੁੰਮਣਾ (ghummṇā) before the model. Only this old word is needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
One last cue: to wander, to go around.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਘੁੰਮਣਾ (ghummṇā). Listen and retry once if it does not come back.


Lesson 3 of 3.
An old word for stopping — to stop, then a gentle mix of three familiar actions.

First retrieve to stop alone. Then hear three familiar actions in a small shuffled mix. Pause before each model. This is not a scored mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to stop.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਰੁਕਣਾ (rukṇā).

Guided Practice.
Pause after each familiar meaning, then hear its model:
to wander, to go around —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਘੁੰਮਣਾ (ghummṇā).
to stop —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਰੁਕਣਾ (rukṇā).
to run —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਭੱਜਣਾ (bhajjṇā).
Only the order of these old words has changed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to stop, to run, then to wander. Pause after each cue and recall its Punjabi word before the model: ਰੁਕਣਾ (rukṇā), ਭੱਜਣਾ (bhajjṇā), ਘੁੰਮਣਾ (ghummṇā). Revisit only the old word you missed.
