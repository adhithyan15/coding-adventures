# బలహీనపరచు (balahīnaparacu) — to weaken; to undermine

## You'll want to know: బలహీనపరచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C430-SAMATULYAM430-02]; assesses=[] -->

**బలహీనపరచు** — *balahīnaparacu* — "to weaken; to undermine".

> అవినీతి ప్రజాస్వామ్యాన్ని బలహీనపరుస్తుంది.
>
> *avinīti prajāsvāmyānni balahīnaparustundi.*
>
> Corruption weakens democracy.

Past (I …): బలహీనపరిచాను, *balahīnaparicānu*.

బలహీన, weak, + -పరచు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బలహీనపరచు** (*balahīnaparacu*) is built from Sanskrit *balahīna*, "destitute of strength, weak" (Monier-Williams), and -పరచు, a native ending that makes a verb (DEDR 3853 lists *paṟacu*, "to cause to feel", beside *paḍu*).
