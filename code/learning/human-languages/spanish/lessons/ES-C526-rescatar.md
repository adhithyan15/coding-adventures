## You'll want to know: rescatar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C526-RESCATE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rescatar** — "to rescue".

> Rescataron a los niños del río.
>
> *They rescued the children from the river.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rescatar* comes from spoken Latin *\*reexcaptāre* 'to take back' (EtymDB), built on *captāre* 'to try to catch', from *capere* 'to take'.
