## You'll want to know: સ્વિચ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C517-VIJLI517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વિચ** — *svic* — "a switch".

> બારણા પાસે બત્તીની સ્વિચ છે.
>
> *bārṇā pāse battīnī svic che.*
>
> The light switch is by the door.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વિચ** (*svic*) is borrowed from English *switch*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
