# దాక్కో (dākkō) — to hide oneself

## You'll want to know: దాక్కో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C352-VEMBADI352-02]; assesses=[] -->

**దాక్కో** — *dākkō* — "to hide oneself".

> దొంగ చెట్టు వెనక దాక్కున్నాడు.
>
> *doṅga ceṭṭu venaka dākkunnāḍu.*
>
> The thief hid behind a tree.

Past (I …): దాక్కున్నాను, *dākkunnānu*.

దాచు is to hide a thing; దాక్కో is to hide yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దాక్కో** (*dākkō*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 63 lists Telugu *dāgu*, "to lie hidden", beside *dācu*, "to hide, conceal"; దాక్కో adds the self-ending *-kō*.
