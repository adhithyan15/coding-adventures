## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**భౌతికశాస్త్రం** (*bhautikaśāstraṁ*) is Sanskrit: *bhautika*, "of the elements, material", from *bhūta*, "any being" (CDIAL 9552), and *śāstra*, "treatise" (CDIAL 12422).
