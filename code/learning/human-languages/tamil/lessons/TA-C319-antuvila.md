# ஆண்டுவிழா (āṇṭuviḻā) — an annual day

## You'll want to know: ஆண்டுவிழா
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C319-AANDU319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆண்டுவிழா** — *āṇṭuviḻā* — "an annual day".

> பள்ளி ஆண்டுவிழாவில் என் மகள் நடனம் ஆடினாள்.
>
> *paḷḷi āṇṭuviḻāvil eṉ makaḷ naṭaṉam āṭiṉāḷ.*
>
> My daughter danced at the school's annual day.

A yearly celebration, often at schools and offices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆண்டுவிழா** (*āṇṭuviḻā*) is a Tamil compound built from ஆண்டு, "year" (DEDR 5153), and விழா, "festival" (DEDR 5427).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
