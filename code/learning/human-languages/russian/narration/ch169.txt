Russian, chapter 169: Request, Help, Opinion, Truth, Lie.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
просьба (prósba) — a request.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a relative, then the Russian for a pensioner.

You'll want to know: просьба.
просьба (prósba) — "a request".

The word, taken apart.
просьба (prósba) is a noun from просить, "to ask, to request", which Vasmer relates to Latin precor, "I pray", and German fragen, "to ask".

Lesson 2 of 5.
помощь (pómoshch) — help.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pensioner, then the Russian for a request.

You'll want to know: помощь.
помощь (pómoshch) — "help".

The word, taken apart.
помощь (pómoshch) is по- with мощь, "might", whose -щ- is the Church Slavonic shape of native мочь, "power", from могу, "I can". Vasmer relates it to Gothic mahts, "might".

Lesson 4 of 5.
правда (právda) — the truth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for help, then the Russian for an opinion.

You'll want to know: правда.
правда (právda) — "the truth".

The word, taken apart.
правда (právda) — Old Russian правьда, "justice, truth, law"; it is built on правый, "right, straight": the truth is what is right.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ложь (lozh) — a lie.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an opinion, then the Russian for the truth.

You'll want to know: ложь.
ложь (lozh) — "a lie".

The word, taken apart.
ложь (lozh) goes with лгать, "to tell lies" (Vasmer), Proto-Slavic lŭgati; Gothic liugan, "to lie", and English lie, "a falsehood", come from the same root.
