## You'll want to know: tóutiáo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C293-PAPER293-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tóutiáo* — "lead story; headline; the front-page story".

> *jīntiān de tóutiáo shì shénme* — What's today's lead story?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tóutiáo* is built from 头 'the head' and *tiáo* 'a strip', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duw* and *dew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the head' and the second 'a small branch': the first strip, the story at the top.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
