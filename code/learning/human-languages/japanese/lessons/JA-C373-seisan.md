## You'll want to know: せいさん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C373-KAISATSU373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**せいさん** — *seisan* — "fare adjustment; settling up".

> **えきの まどぐちで せいさんを しました。** — *eki no madoguchi de seisan o shimashita* — "I paid the fare difference at the station window."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいさん** (*seisan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "refined" (Middle Chinese *tsjeng*) and *san*, "calculate" (*swanX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
