## You'll want to know: استقبال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C441-ISTIQBAL441-01]; assesses=[] -->

**استقبال** — *istiqbāl* — "reception; a welcome".

> أعطاني موظف الاستقبال المفتاح.
>
> *aʿṭānī muwaẓẓaf al-istiqbāl al-miftāḥ.*
>
> The receptionist gave me the key.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استقبال** (*istiqbāl*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-l*, "to be in front of; to accept" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form X, "going out to meet".
