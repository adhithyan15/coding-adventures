Arabic, chapter 83: Slow, Fast, Easy, Difficult, Cheap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بطيء (baṭīʾ) — slow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for long, then the Arabic for short.

You'll want to know: بطيء.
بطيء — baṭīʾ — "slow".

The word, taken apart.
بطيء (baṭīʾ) is built on the native Arabic root b-ṭ-ʾ, "to be slow" (Lane). Baṭīʾ is the faʿīl adjective, "slow".
