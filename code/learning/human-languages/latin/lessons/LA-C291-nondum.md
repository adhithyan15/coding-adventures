## You'll want to know: nōndum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C291-PRIMO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**nōndum** — "not yet".

> Nūntius nōndum vēnerat; itaque exspectābāmus.
>
> *The messenger had not yet come, so we were waiting.*

The opposite of *iam* (taught), 'already'. *Nōndum* + pluperfect sets a scene in which something is still awaited.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**nōndum** joins *nōn*, "not", and *dum*, "yet, still" (Lewis & Short): not as yet.
