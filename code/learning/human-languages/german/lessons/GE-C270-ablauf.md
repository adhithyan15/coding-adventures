## You'll want to know: der Ablauf
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C270-ABWARTEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ablauf** — "the sequence of events, the process".

> Der Ablauf des Tages war genau geplant.
>
> *The running order of the day was planned exactly.*

Plural: *die Abläufe*. Also the end of a period: *nach Ablauf der Frist*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ablauf* is built on *Lauf* "a run", from *laufen*, Old High German *hloufan* (EtymDB), with *ab*: the way things run off.
