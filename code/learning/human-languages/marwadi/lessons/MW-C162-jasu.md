# हूं काल जासूं — I will go

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GAYO-01, MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I went to the market yesterday*. The same **काल** also points the
other way, to tomorrow.

## Grammar Lens: -सूं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-02, MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04, MW-SCRIPT-BAJAR-01] -->

Add **-सूं** to a stem and you have *I will*:

> **हूं काल बाजार जासूं।**
>
> *hū̃ kāl bājār jāsū̃.* — I will go to the market tomorrow.

No **हूं** at the end: the future stands on its own. Every stem takes it the same
way: **आसूं**, **खासूं**, **बोलसूं**, **समझसूं**. CDIAL quotes an Old Marwari line
with this future in it, "I will fan" (CDIAL 5593).
