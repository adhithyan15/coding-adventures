## You'll want to know: efficiente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C362-STRESS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**efficiente** — "efficient".

> La nuova segretaria è molto efficiente.
>
> *The new secretary is very efficient.*

One form for both genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*efficiente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *efficiēns* (EtymDB), present participle of *efficere* "to bring about", built from *ex* and *facere* "to do".
