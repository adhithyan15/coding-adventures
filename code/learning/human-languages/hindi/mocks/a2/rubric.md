# Hindi A2 mock rubric

Score either A2 mock against [`task-shapes/a2.json`](../../task-shapes/a2.json).
The four 100-point papers are independent; the learner must earn at least
60/100 in reading, listening, writing, and speaking separately.

## Reading — 100 points

- notices and correspondence: 30 points across 8 keyed items;
- connected everyday text one: 35 points across 8 keyed items;
- connected everyday text two: 35 points across 8 keyed items.

Divide each part's points equally among its keyed items and retain one decimal
place. Credit the keyed meaning, sequence, purpose, or simple inference only.
Do not award reading points for oral delivery.

## Listening — 100 points

- announcements: 30 points across 7 keyed items;
- everyday conversations: 35 points across 7 keyed items;
- connected two-to-three-minute account: 35 points across 6 keyed items.

Play every recording twice at 110–130 words per minute. Score only the selected
answers. Do not provide a transcript, Romanization, dictionary, translator, or
learner-controlled replay.

## Writing — 100 points

The 25–35 word functional response is worth 40 points: task fulfilment 14,
reader and purpose 8, relevant content 6, vocabulary and grammar 7, and
orthographic control 5. The 70–90 word connected message or description is
worth 60 points: task fulfilment and development 18, organisation and cohesion
12, reader and purpose 8, vocabulary and grammar 14, and orthographic control
8. Responses outside the range lose only the content that cannot be evidenced;
there is no automatic length penalty. Romanization, translation, or a copied
model earns no independent-writing credit.

## Speaking — 100 points

The interview is worth 30 points, the prepared two-minute account 35, and the
information-exchange role-play 35. In each part award 35% for task fulfilment
and relevant development, 25% for interaction and repair, 25% for Hindi
vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for pronunciation and fluency. The learner may
use paper keyword notes made during five minutes of preparation, never a written
answer script. Consistent gender agreement and comprehensible regional
pronunciation are acceptable.

## Readiness boundary

Passing a locally scored paper is not yet a readiness claim. Record a clean
timed administration and qualified human review of stimuli, recordings,
productive responses, and scoring before treating the result as evidence.
