## You'll want to know: प्रहरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C524-SPARDHA524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रहरति** — *praharati* — "hits".

> क्रीडकः कन्दुकं दण्डेन प्रहरति।
>
> *krīḍakaḥ kandukaṃ daṇḍena praharati.*
>
> The player hits the ball with a stick.

Past (he …): सः कन्दुकं प्रहृतवान्, *saḥ kandukaṃ prahṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रहरति** (*praharati*) is built on *pra*, "forth", and the root √hṛ, "to carry", which also gives *harati*; MW gives *prahṛ* as to strike, to hit (MW).
