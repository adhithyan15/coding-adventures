## You'll want to know: a jardinagem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C501-JARDIM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a jardinagem** — "gardening".

> A jardinagem é o passatempo preferido do meu pai.
>
> *Gardening is my father's favourite hobby.*

Built on *o jardim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jardinagem* comes from French *jardinage* "gardening" (EtymDB), built on *jardin* "garden", a word of Germanic origin.
