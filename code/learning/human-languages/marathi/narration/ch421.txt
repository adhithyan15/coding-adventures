Marathi, chapter 421: Building Sites and Boundaries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बांधकाम (bāndhkām) — construction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a resident, then the Marathi for the surroundings.

You'll want to know: बांधकाम.
बांधकाम — bāndhkām — "construction".
आमच्या गल्लीत नवीन बांधकाम चालू आहे. āmcyā gallīt navīn bāndhkām cālū āhe. New construction is going on in our lane.
Neuter.
बांधकाम (bāndhkām) चालू आहे, building work is going on; बांधकाम (bāndhkām) करणे, to build.

The word, taken apart.
बांधकाम (bāndhkām) is built from the stem of बांधणे, "to tie, to build", and काम, "work", inherited from Sanskrit karman (CDIAL 2892).

Lesson 2 of 5.
मजला (majlā) — a floor; a storey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the surroundings, then the Marathi for construction.

You'll want to know: मजला.
मजला — majlā — "a floor; a storey".
आमचं घर चौथ्या मजल्यावर आहे. āmcaṁ ghar cauthyā majalyāvar āhe. Our flat is on the fourth floor.
Masculine.
तळमजला, the ground floor; पहिला मजला (majlā), the first floor.

The word, taken apart.
मजला (majlā) is a loan, usually traced to Arabic manzil, "a stage of a journey, a halting place", through Persian.

नदीजवळ एक मोठी झोपडपट्टी आहे. nadījavaḷ ek moṭhī jhopaḍpaṭṭī āhe. There is a big slum near the river.
Feminine.
झोपडी, a hut, and पट्टी, a strip: a row of huts.

The word, taken apart.
झोपडपट्टी (jhopaḍpaṭṭī) is built from झोपडी, "a hut", inherited from jhuppa (CDIAL 5403), and पट्टी, "a strip".

Lesson 4 of 5.
वेस (ves) — a village gate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a floor, then the Marathi for a slum.

You'll want to know: वेस.
वेस — ves — "a village gate".
गावाच्या वेशीवर एक जुनं झाड आहे. gāvācyā veśīvar ek junaṁ jhāḍ āhe. There is an old tree at the village gate.
Feminine.
Before an ending वेस (ves) becomes वेशी-: वेशीवर, at the gate; also the edge of the village.

The word, taken apart.
वेस (ves) is a Marathi word for the gateway of an old village, and for the village edge; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

नदी ही दोन जिल्ह्यांची हद्द आहे. nadī hī don jilhyāñcī hadda āhe. The river is the boundary between two districts.
Feminine.
Also a limit of patience: हद्द (hadda) झाली, that is the limit.

The word, taken apart.
हद्द (hadda) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic ḥadd, "a limit, a border".
