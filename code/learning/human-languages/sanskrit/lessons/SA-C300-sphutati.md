## You'll want to know: स्फुटति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C300-BHUKAMPA300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्फुटति** — *sphuṭati* — "cracks open; bursts".

> भित्तिः सहसा स्फुटति।
>
> *bhittiḥ sahasā sphuṭati.*
>
> The wall suddenly cracks open.

Past (it …): भित्तिः सहसा स्फुटितवती, *bhittiḥ sahasā sphuṭitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्फुटति** (*sphuṭati*) comes from the root √sphuṭ, "to burst or split open with a sound" (MW).
