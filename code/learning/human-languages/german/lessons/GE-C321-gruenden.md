## You'll want to know: gründen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C321-ERFINDEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**gründen** — "to found, to set up".

> Mein Großvater gründete die Firma vor fünfzig Jahren.
>
> *My grandfather founded the company fifty years ago.*

*Die Gründung* — the founding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gründen* is built on *Grund* "ground", an inherited Germanic word from Proto-Germanic *grunduz* (EtymDB), a cousin of English *ground*: to lay the ground.
