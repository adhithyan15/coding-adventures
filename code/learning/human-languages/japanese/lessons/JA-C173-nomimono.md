## You'll want to know: のみもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C173-FOOD173-02]; assesses=[] -->

**のみもの** — *nomimono* — "beverage; something to drink".

> **のみものは おちゃを たのみます。** — *nomimono wa ocha o tanomimasu* — "For a drink, I order tea."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のみもの** (*nomimono*) is native Japanese, built from *nomi*, the stem of *nomu*, "to drink", and *mono*, "thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
