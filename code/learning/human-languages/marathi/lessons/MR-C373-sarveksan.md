## You'll want to know: सर्वेक्षण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C373-PADDHAT373-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सर्वेक्षण** — *sarvekṣaṇ* — "a survey".

> संस्थेने गावात आरोग्याचं सर्वेक्षण केलं.
>
> *saṁsthene gāvāt ārogyācaṁ sarvekṣaṇ kelaṁ.*
>
> The organization carried out a health survey in the village.

Neuter.

सर्व + ईक्षण, all + looking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सर्वेक्षण** (*sarvekṣaṇ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *sarva*, "all", and *īkṣaṇa*, "look, view" (Monier-Williams): a view over everything.
