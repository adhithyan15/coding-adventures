## You'll want to know: चुगणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C237-FIELD237-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चुगणो** — *chugṇo* — "to peck; to pick up (grain or berries)".

> कबूतर बाजरो चुगै है।
>
> *kabūtar bājro chugai hai.*
>
> The pigeon pecks at the millet.

Past (I …): म्हैं केर चुग्या, *mhaĩ ker chugyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुगणो** (*chugṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*cugyati*, "pecks"; CDIAL lists Marwari *cugṇo*, "to peck, to eat" (CDIAL 4852).
