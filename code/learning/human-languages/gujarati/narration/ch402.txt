Gujarati, chapter 402: Carts and Chhakdas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
છકડો (chakḍo) — a chhakda (a country motor cart).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to drag, then the Gujarati for to overturn.

You'll want to know: છકડો.
છકડો — chakḍo — "a chhakda (a country motor cart)".
ગામના માણસો છકડામાં બેસીને શહેર ગયા. gāmnā māṇso chakḍāmā̃ besīne śaher gayā. The village people rode into town in a chhakda.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
છકડો (chakḍo) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit śakaṭa, "a cart"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word for a cart (CDIAL 12236). Today the name belongs to the open three-wheeled motor cart of the Saurashtra roads.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ટેમ્પો (ṭempo) — a tempo (a small goods van).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to overturn, then the Gujarati for a chhakda.

You'll want to know: ટેમ્પો.
ટેમ્પો — ṭempo — "a tempo (a small goods van)".
અમે ટેમ્પોમાં બધો સામાન ભર્યો. ame ṭempomā̃ badho sāmān bharyo. We loaded all the luggage into a tempo.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટેમ્પો (ṭempo) is borrowed from Indian English tempo, which comes from the name of a firm whose three-wheeled goods vans were common in India.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ગાડું (gāḍũ) — a cart (a bullock cart).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a chhakda, then the Gujarati for a small goods van.

You'll want to know: ગાડું.
ગાડું — gāḍũ — "a cart (a bullock cart)".
બળદનું ગાડું ધીમે ચાલે છે. baḷadnũ gāḍũ dhīme cāle che. The bullock cart moves slowly.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગાડું (gāḍũ) goes back to gāḍḍa, "a cart", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 4116). ગાડી, a car, is its relative: the cart came first.

Lesson 5 of 5.
હાંકવું (hā̃kvũ) — to drive a cart; to drive (animals) on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bullock cart, then the Gujarati for a bus conductor.

You'll want to know: હાંકવું.
હાંકવું — hā̃kvũ — "to drive a cart; to drive (animals) on".
દાદા રોજ સવારે ગાડું હાંકે છે. dādā roj savāre gāḍũ hā̃ke che. Grandfather drives the cart every morning.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગાડું હાંક્યું, teṇe gāḍũ hā̃kyũ.

The word, taken apart.
હાંકવું (hā̃kvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit hakkayati, "calls to" (CDIAL 13939): you drive oxen on by shouting at them. CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to drive".
