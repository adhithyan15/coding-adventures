## You'll want to know: बहुत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बहुत** (*bahut*) — "very, a lot, much".

Sanskrit बहु (*bahu*), "much, many". The same ancient root gave Greek *pakhús*,
"thick", so the family has always been about quantity of stuff.

बहुत goes **before** what it strengthens, which is the opposite of भी. You now
have one small word that leans forward and one that leans back, and putting each
on the wrong side is the commonest mistake a beginner makes with them.

Three you can say immediately, from words you already hold: **बहुत बड़ा**,
**बहुत धन्यवाद**, **बहुत जल्दी**. That last one means "very soon" as readily as
"very fast", which is how बहुत behaves throughout: it takes the colour of
whatever it is standing in front of.

Set it against **कम** and **ज़्यादा**, which you learnt for measuring. बहुत is
not a comparison; it is an intensity.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Three: आज, भी, बहुत. One that places the day, one that adds, one that raises.
