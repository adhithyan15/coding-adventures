## You'll want to know: রক্তচাপ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C474-HANPANI474-03]; assesses=[] -->

**রক্তচাপ** — *rôktôchāp* — "blood pressure".

> ডাক্তার বাবার রক্তচাপ মাপলেন।
>
> *ḍāktār bābār rôktôchāp māplen.*
>
> The doctor measured my father's blood pressure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রক্তচাপ** (*rôktôchāp*) puts রক্ত (taught), blood, with *chāp*, "pressure", from the family CDIAL lists for Bengali under a reconstructed *capp*, "press" (CDIAL 4674).
