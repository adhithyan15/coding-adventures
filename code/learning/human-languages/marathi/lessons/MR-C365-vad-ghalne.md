## You'll want to know: वाद घालणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C365-VATAGHATI365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वाद घालणे** — *vād ghālṇe* — "to argue".

> मुलं खेळण्यांवरून वाद घालत होती.
>
> *mulaṁ kheḷaṇyāṁvarūn vād ghālat hotī.*
>
> The children were arguing over the toys.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वाद घातला, *tyāne vād ghātlā*.

The subject of the quarrel takes -वरून; घालणे's past is घातला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाद घालणे** (*vād ghālṇe*) joins the tatsama वाद, Sanskrit *vāda*, "speech, dispute" (Monier-Williams), to घालणे, "to put on".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
