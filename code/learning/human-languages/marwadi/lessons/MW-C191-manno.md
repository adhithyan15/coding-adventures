# मानणो (mānṇo) — to obey; to follow (a rule)

## You'll want to know: मानणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C191-NOTICE191-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मानणो** — *mānṇo* — "to obey; to follow (a rule)".

> हूं हरेक नियम मानूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ harek niyam mānū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I follow every rule.

Past (I …): म्हैं नियम मान्यो, *mhaĩ niyam mānyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मानणो** (*mānṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *manyate*, "thinks, honours"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *mānaï*, "to respect, obey" (CDIAL 9857).
