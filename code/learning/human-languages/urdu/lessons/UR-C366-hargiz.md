## You'll want to know: ہرگز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C366-IJAZAT366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ہرگز** — *hargiz* — "on no account (with نہیں)".

> اگر بارش ہوئی تو میں ہرگز نہیں جاؤں گا۔
>
> *agar bārish huī to maiṅ hargiz nahīṅ jāūṅ gā.*
>
> If it rains, I will on no account go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہرگز** (*hargiz*) is usually traced to Persian *hargiz*, "ever"; beside **نہیں** it means "never, on no account".
