Japanese, chapter 524: Imagining.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
かてい (katei) — a supposition; a hypothesis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for as it happens, then the Japanese for somehow (or other).

You'll want to know: かてい.
かてい — katei — "a supposition; a hypothesis".
こたえは かていに よります。 — kotae wa katei ni yorimasu — "The answer depends on the assumption."

The word, taken apart.
かてい (katei) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ka, "temporary" (Middle Chinese kaeX) and tei, "determine" (dengH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
おもいえがく (omoiegaku) — to picture (in one's mind).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for somehow (or other), then the Japanese for a supposition.

You'll want to know: おもいえがく.
おもいえがく — omoiegaku — "to picture (in one's mind)".
もし しょうらいを おもいえがいたら、なにが みえますか。 — moshi shōrai o omoiegaitara, nani ga miemasu ka — "If you picture your future, what do you see?"
Polite おもいえがきます, omoiegakimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): かれが しょうらいを おもいえがきました。, kare ga shōrai o omoiegakimashita.

The word, taken apart.
おもいえがく (omoiegaku) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

もし こどもが えほんを よんだら、つきの まちを そうぞうするでしょう。 — moshi kodomo ga ehon o yondara, tsuki no machi o sōzō suru deshō — "If children read the picture book, they would imagine a town on the moon."
Polite そうぞうします, sōzōshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): こどもが つきの まちを そうぞうしました。, kodomo ga tsuki no machi o sōzōshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
そうぞうする (sōzō suru) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun sōzō with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are sō, "concept" (Middle Chinese sjangX) and zō, "statue" (zjangX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
