## You'll want to know: दिक़्क़त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C380-HAL380-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दिक़्क़त** — *diqqat* — "a difficulty, a hassle".

> अगर कोई दिक़्क़त होगी तो मुझे फ़ोन करो।
>
> *agar koī diqqat hogī to mujhe fon karo.*
>
> If there's any difficulty, phone me.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिक़्क़त** (*diqqat*) is usually traced to Arabic *diqqa*, "fineness, exactness", which reached Hindi through Persian. The sense of a difficulty is a Persian and Hindi one.
