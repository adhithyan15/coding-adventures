## You'll want to know: kratzen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C481-ALLERGIE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**kratzen** — "to scratch".

> Die Katze hat mich am Arm gekratzt.
>
> *The cat scratched my arm.*

*Sich kratzen* — to scratch yourself: *Er kratzt sich am Kopf.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kratzen* is inherited from Middle High German *kratzen* and Old High German *krazzōn*, from Proto-Germanic *krattōną* "to scratch" (EtymDB).
