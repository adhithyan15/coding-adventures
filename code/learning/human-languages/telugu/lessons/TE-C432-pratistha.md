## You'll want to know: ప్రతిష్ఠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C432-ADARSAM432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రతిష్ఠ** — *pratiṣṭha* — "prestige; reputation".

> ఆ పని వల్ల కుటుంబ ప్రతిష్ఠ పెరిగింది.
>
> *ā pani valla kuṭumba pratiṣṭha perigindi.*
>
> That deed raised the family’s standing.

ప్రతిష్ఠాత్మకమైన, prestigious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రతిష్ఠ** (*pratiṣṭha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pratiṣṭhā*, "foundation, prop" (CDIAL 8608), and "standing still, stability" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for standing in people’s eyes.
