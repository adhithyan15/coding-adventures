## You'll want to know: a máscara
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C321-CARNAVAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a máscara** — "the mask".

> A criança usava uma máscara de leão.
>
> *The child wore a lion mask.*

Also a face mask for health: *usar máscara*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*máscara* came from Italian *maschera* "a mask, a disguise" (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *masca*.
