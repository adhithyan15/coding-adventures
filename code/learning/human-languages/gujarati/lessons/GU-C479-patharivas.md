# પથારીવશ (pathārīvaś) — bedridden

## You'll want to know: પથારીવશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C479-MANDU479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પથારીવશ** — *pathārīvaś* — "bedridden".

> તાવને લીધે દાદી એક અઠવાડિયું પથારીવશ રહ્યાં.
>
> *tāvne līdhe dādī ek aṭhvāḍiyũ pathārīvaś rahyā̃.*
>
> Because of the fever my grandmother was bedridden for a week.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પથારીવશ** (*pathārīvaś*) is built from **પથારી** (*pathārī*), "bedding", and *vaś*, from Sanskrit *vaśa*, "will, power" (Monier-Williams): in the power of the bed.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
