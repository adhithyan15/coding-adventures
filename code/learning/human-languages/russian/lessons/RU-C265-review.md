# Review — asking the way, and giving it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KAK-PROYTI-01, RU-GRAMMAR-K-DATIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask the way to the park, then to the theatre. (*Kak proytí k
párku? Kak proytí k teátru?*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-IDITE-POVERNITE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-DO-NA-ROUTE-01, RU-LEX-C80-PLACES-04, RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-04] -->

Two lessons, and a stranger's street is no longer a wall. The question:
**Извините, как пройти к …?**, with the place in its "to" form. The
answer: **идите прямо**, **поверните направо** or **налево**, **до**
something, **на** a corner.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-STOIT-INFINITIVE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-LUCHSHE-INFINITIVE-01, RU-LEX-C72-WHERE1-03, RU-LEX-C80-PLACES-05, RU-GRAMMAR-PRICHINA-V-TOM-01] -->

> **— Извините, как пройти к театру?**
> **— Это далеко. Вам лучше взять такси.**

*Vam lúchshe vzyat taksí* — "You'd better take a taxi." Advice from the
last chapter, now with the polite **вам**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KAK-PROYTI-01, RU-GRAMMAR-K-DATIVE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-IDITE-POVERNITE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-DO-NA-ROUTE-01, RU-LEX-C89-LOC89-04, RU-LEX-BRAT] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *Izvinítye, kak proytí k párku?*]
- [YOU SAY: *Idítye pryámo do perekryóstka, potóm povernítye naprávo.*]
- [YOU SAY: *Éto dalekó. Vam lúchshe vzyat taksí.*]
- [YOU ANSWER: a stranger asks *Kak proytí k vokzálu?* Give the way from where you live, in two or three steps]
