## You'll want to know: પોકારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C262-OUTING262-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પોકારવું** — *pokārvũ* — "to call out, to shout".

> **મેં મારા મિત્રને પોકાર્યો.** — *mẽ mārā mitrane pokāryo* — "I called out to my friend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પોકારવું** (*pokārvũ*) is inherited. It comes from *\*pukkār*, "shout", a reconstructed form that imitates the sound; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to shout" (CDIAL 8246).
