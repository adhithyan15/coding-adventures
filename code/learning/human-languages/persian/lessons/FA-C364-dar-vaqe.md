## You'll want to know: در واقع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C364-VAQEIYYAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**در واقع** (*dar vâqe'*) — "in fact, actually".

> در واقع او برادر من نیست، پسرعموی من است.
>
> *dar vâqe' u barâdar-e man nist, pesar'amu-ye man ast.*
>
> In fact he is not my brother; he is my cousin.

*Dar* "in" + *vâqe'* "real". It corrects or sharpens what came before, and takes a whole clause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**در واقع** joins the inherited Persian preposition *dar* "in" to *vâqe'*, usually traced to Arabic *wāqi'* "happening, real": in what really happens.
