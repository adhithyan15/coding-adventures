## You'll want to know: فتح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C311-FATEH311-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فتح** — *fateh* — "a victory".

> فتح کے بعد جشن ہوا۔
>
> *fateh ke bād jashn huā.*
>
> After the victory there was a celebration.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فتح** (*fateh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fatḥ*, "an opening", and so the taking of a city. It is feminine.
