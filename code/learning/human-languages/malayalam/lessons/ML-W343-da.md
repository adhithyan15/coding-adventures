# ഡ (ḍa) — the letter inside റോഡ്

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-117, ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-123] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **ട** and **ദ** once each, and name them. (*ṭa*, with the
tongue curled back; *da*, with the voice on.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **റോഡ്** (*ṟōḍŭ*), "a road". This lesson takes its last consonant out and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ഡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C343-DA-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C343-RODU-01] -->

**ഡ** — *ḍa*: the tongue curled back as for **ട**, and the voice on as for **ദ**. It is the voiced
partner of **ട**.

In **റോഡ്** it carries the half-u sign **്**, so the word ends *-ḍŭ*. Native Malayalam words
rarely use it; it is the letter Malayalam reaches for when an English word has a *d*.

## Writing: ഡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C343-DA-01] -->

Put your pen on **ഡ** and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see, slowly, and larger than it
is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to travel**. It is
> not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying what is in front of
> you needs no such source, and it is how the shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
