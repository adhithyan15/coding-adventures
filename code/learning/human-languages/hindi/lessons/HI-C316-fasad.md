## You'll want to know: फ़साद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C316-UPADRAV316-02]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़साद** — *fasād* — "trouble; strife".

> कुछ लोग फ़साद करना चाहते थे।
>
> *kuch log fasād karnā cāhte the.*
>
> Some people wanted to make trouble.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़साद** (*fasād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fasād*, "disorder, a spoiling". **फ़साद करना** is to stir up trouble.
