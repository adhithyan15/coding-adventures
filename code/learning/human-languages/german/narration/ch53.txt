German, chapter 53: Writing --- Your Own Line.
1 lesson. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 1.
Your own line — the first writing with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
You have written Hallo four ways: traced, copied, copied from memory, and written from the sound alone.
[pause 1 second]
Every one of those had something to reach for. This one does not.

Writing — controlled composition.
[pause 2 seconds]
Write one line about yourself: a greeting, then your name.
Nothing is supplied. You choose the words, and the supply you are choosing from is closed — only what this book has taught you.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before you write, one thing to decide deliberately rather than by accident: which nouns get a capital? In German the answer is all of them, not only the ones that name a person or a place. Your line will have at least one, and an English-trained hand will lower-case it without noticing.
[pause 3 seconds]
Write the line. Then read it back and check the capitals before you check anything else.

Wrap-up recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Which German nouns take a capital letter? (All of them.)
[pause 2 seconds]
And what did you have to choose, that the last four lessons chose for you? (The words.)
