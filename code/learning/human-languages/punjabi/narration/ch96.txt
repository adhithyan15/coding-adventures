Punjabi, chapter 96: Twelve, Twenty, Thirty, Fifty, Hundred.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਬਾਰਾਂ (bārāṁ) — twelve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for ten, then the Punjabi for eleven.

You'll want to know: ਬਾਰਾਂ.
ਬਾਰਾਂ — bārāṁ — "twelve".

The word, taken apart.
ਬਾਰਾਂ (bārāṁ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dvādaśa, "twelve" (CDIAL 6658).
