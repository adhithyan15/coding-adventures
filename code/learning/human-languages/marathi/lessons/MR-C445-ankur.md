## You'll want to know: अंकुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C445-ANKUR445-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अंकुर** — *aṅkur* — "a sprout; a shoot".

> बीमधून हिरवा अंकुर बाहेर आला.
>
> *bīmadhūn hirvā aṅkur bāher ālā.*
>
> A green shoot came out of the seed.

Masculine.

अंकुर फुटणे, a shoot comes up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंकुर** (*aṅkur*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *aṅkura*, "a sprout, a shoot" (Monier-Williams).
