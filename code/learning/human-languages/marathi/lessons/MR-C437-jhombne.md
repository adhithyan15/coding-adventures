# झोंबणे (jhombṇe) — to sting; to nip

## You'll want to know: झोंबणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C437-GARVA437-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झोंबणे** — *jhombṇe* — "to sting; to nip".

> पहाटेचा थंड वारा अंगाला झोंबला.
>
> *pahāṭecā thaṇḍ vārā aṅgālā jhomblā.*
>
> The cold dawn wind stung my body.

Past (he …): तो झोंबला, *to jhomblā*.

What is stung takes -ला: अंगाला, कानाला झोंबणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झोंबणे** (*jhombṇe*) is a Marathi verb for cold, smoke or spice that stings; its origin is not settled.
