## You'll want to know: رویداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C314-RUYDAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رویداد** (*ruydâd*) — "an event".

> جشنواره فیلم مهمترین رویداد فرهنگی شهر است.
>
> *Jashnvâre-ye film mohemtarin ruydâd-e farhangi-ye shahr ast.*
>
> The film festival is the most important cultural event in the city.

A modern word, built on *ruy dâdan* "to occur". Plural *ruydâd-hâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruydâd* is derived from *ruy dâdan* "to occur" (EtymDB): *ruy* "face" with *dâd*, the past stem of *dâdan* "to give". Both parts are native Persian.
