## You'll want to know: گیدڑ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C439-GIDAR439-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گیدڑ** — *gīdaṛ* — "a jackal".

> رات کو کھیتوں میں گیدڑ بولتے ہیں۔
>
> *rāt ko khetoṅ meṅ gīdaṛ bolte haiṅ.*
>
> At night jackals howl in the fields.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گیدڑ** (*gīdaṛ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *gīdaṛ*, "a jackal", under *gidda*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4158).
