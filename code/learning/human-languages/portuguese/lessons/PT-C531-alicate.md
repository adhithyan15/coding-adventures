## You'll want to know: o alicate
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o alicate** — "pliers".

> Usou o alicate para tirar o prego.
>
> *He used the pliers to pull out the nail.*

One tool in Portuguese: *um alicate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alicate* comes from Arabic *laqqāṭ* "pincers" (EtymDB), with the Arabic article *al-*.
