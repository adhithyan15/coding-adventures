# محتمل (muḥtamal) — probable

## You'll want to know: محتمل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C401-IHTIMAL401-02]; assesses=[] -->

**محتمل** — *muḥtamal* — "probable".

> من المحتمل أن يتأخر أخي.
>
> *min al-muḥtamal an yataʾakhkhara akhī.*
>
> It is probable that my brother will be late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محتمل** (*muḥtamal*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-m-l*, "to carry, to bear" (Lane). *Muḥtamal* is the Form VIII passive participle: what can be allowed for, probable.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
