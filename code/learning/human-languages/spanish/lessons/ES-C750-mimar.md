## You'll want to know: mimar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C750-CONVIVIR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**mimar** — "to spoil, to pamper".

> Los abuelos miman mucho a los nietos.
>
> *Grandparents really spoil their grandchildren.*

*Mimado* — spoilt. *Los mimos* — cuddles, pampering.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mimar* is built on *mimo* "a cuddle, pampering", which is usually traced to Latin *mīmus* "mime, clown", from Greek *mîmos*: the playful faces made to a child.
