# કપટ (kapaṭ) — deceit

## You'll want to know: કપટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C460-BHOLU460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કપટ** — *kapaṭ* — "deceit".

> તેના વચનમાં કપટ હતું.
>
> *tenā vacanmā̃ kapaṭ hatũ.*
>
> There was deceit in his promise.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કપટ** (*kapaṭ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kapaṭa*, "fraud, deceit", as Monier-Williams records it.
