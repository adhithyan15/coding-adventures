# Review — if this, then that would

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-SAREBBE-01, IT-GRAMMAR-CONDIZIONALE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "it would be difficult". (*Sarebbe difficile.*) Then "I could".
(*Potrei.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-SE-PRESENTE-01, IT-LEX-DIPENDE-01, IT-GRAMMAR-DIPENDE-DA-02, IT-GRAMMAR-CONDIZIONALE-01, IT-GRAMMAR-SAREBBE-BELLO-02] -->

Two chapters, and a whole way of talking about what has not happened yet. A
real branch: *se piove, resto a casa*. A branch not yet named: *dipende*, or
*dipende da te*. And the softened result, in the condizionale: *potrei*,
*dovrei*, *sarebbe*.

> — *Andiamo al mare domani?*
> — *Dipende. Se c'è il sole, sarebbe bello. Se piove,
> potremmo andare al museo.*

[PAUSE 2s] *Andiamo* is "shall we go", and *potremmo* is "we could", the *noi*
form of *potrei*. Both are the forms you already know how to build, worn by a
new person.
