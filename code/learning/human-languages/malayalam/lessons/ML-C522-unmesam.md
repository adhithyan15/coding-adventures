# ഉന്മേഷം (unmēṣaṁ) — freshness; vigour

## You'll want to know: ഉന്മേഷം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C522-JIJNASA522-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉന്മേഷം** — *unmēṣaṁ* — "freshness; vigour".

> രാവിലെ ഒരു ചായ കുടിച്ചാൽ എനിക്ക് ഉന്മേഷം കിട്ടും.
>
> *rāvile oru cāya kuṭiccāl enikkŭ unmēṣaṁ kiṭṭuṁ.*
>
> If I drink a tea in the morning, I feel fresh.

The fresh, wide-awake feeling after rest or a bath: കുളി കഴിഞ്ഞ് ഉന്മേഷം തോന്നി.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉന്മേഷം** (*unmēṣaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *unmeṣa*, "the opening of the eyes" (Monier-Williams). Malayalam took it for the feeling of being wide awake and fresh.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
