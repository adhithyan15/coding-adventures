## You'll want to know: वेला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C417-PULINA417-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वेला** — *velā* — "the coast; the shoreline".

> वेलायां सिकता उष्णा अस्ति।
>
> *velāyāṃ sikatā uṣṇā asti.*
>
> The sand on the coast is hot.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेला** (*velā*) is the word MW gives for a limit or boundary, a time, and the boundary of sea and land, the coast; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
