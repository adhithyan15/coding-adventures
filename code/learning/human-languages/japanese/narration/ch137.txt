You'll want to know: いっぱい.
いっぱい — ippai — "full", as a cup is full, or as you are after a big meal. It also means "a lot": a lot of people, a lot of work.
Four beats: i–p–pa–i. い, the small っ that holds a beat, and the last い you already write. The third sign is は with the small circle at its upper right, and that circle makes a p: ha becomes pa. The next lesson writes it.

Script — the change.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
は ha + ゜ becomes ぱ pa.
Write は in full, in the order you learned it. Then add the small circle of the handakuten at the upper right, where the voicing mark would go. Keep it small and open, and do not let it touch the sign.
The circle works only on the h row, and it always makes a p: ha becomes pa. You saw it on ほ for ぽ, in いっぽん.
With いっ in front and い after it, you can now write the word from the last lesson: いっぱい, ippai, full.

Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]

Write いっぱい from memory and say ippai.
