## You'll want to know: प्रयासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C552-PRAYASA552-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयासः** — *prayāsaḥ* — "exertion".

> यदि प्रयासः सत्यः अस्ति, तर्हि फलम् अवश्यं भवति।
>
> *yadi prayāsaḥ satyaḥ asti, tarhi phalam avaśyaṃ bhavati.*
>
> If the effort is real, a result surely comes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयासः** (*prayāsaḥ*) is built on *pra* and the root √yas, "to heat oneself, to exert oneself"; MW gives *prayāsa* as exertion, effort, pains, trouble (MW).
