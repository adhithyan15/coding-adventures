## You'll want to know: ਹੱਡੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C323-BODY323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੱਡੀ** — *haḍḍī* — "a bone".

> ਹਾਦਸੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਮਨ ਦੀ ਲੱਤ ਦੀ ਹੱਡੀ ਟੁੱਟੀ।
>
> *hādse vich Aman dī latt dī haḍḍī ṭuṭṭī.*
>
> Aman broke a bone in his leg in the accident.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੱਡੀ** (*haḍḍī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *haḍḍa*, "bone"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *haḍḍī*, "bone", under it (CDIAL 13952).
