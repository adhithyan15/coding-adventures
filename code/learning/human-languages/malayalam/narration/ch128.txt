Malayalam, chapter 128: Come In, This Is Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വീട് (vīṭŭ) — a house, home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a holiday, then the Malayalam for I'll call.

You'll want to know: വീട്.
വീട് (vīṭŭ) — "a house", and also "home".
എന്റെ വീട് (enṟe vīṭŭ) — "my house", with എന്റെ, "my", which you already know.

What you've built.
The place every visit starts.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vīṭŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vīṭŭ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say enṟe vīṭŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for a holiday, then the Malayalam for I'll call, then say vīṭŭ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does വീട് (vīṭŭ) mean? ("A house, home".) Say it once more.
