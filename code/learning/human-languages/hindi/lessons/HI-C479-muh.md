## You'll want to know: मुँह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C479-MUNH479-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मुँह** — *mũh* — "the mouth; the face".

> खाने से पहले मुँह और हाथ धो लो।
>
> *khāne se pahle mũh aur hāth dho lo.*
>
> Wash your face and hands before eating.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुँह** (*mũh*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *mũh*, "mouth, face", under Sanskrit *múkha*, "mouth, face" (CDIAL 10158); the *kh* wore down to *h* and left a nasal vowel.
