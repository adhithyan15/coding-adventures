## You'll want to know: stonare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C518-CANTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**stonare** — "to sing or play out of tune".

> Quando canto stono sempre.
>
> *When I sing I am always out of tune.*

*Stonato* — out of tune; also out of place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stonare* is built from *s-* and *tono* "tone", from Latin *tonus*, from Greek *tónos* "a pitch" (EtymDB): to go off the note.
