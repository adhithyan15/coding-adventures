Lesson 1 of 5.
বাটি (bāṭi) — a bowl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to return, then the Bengali for to drive.

You'll want to know: বাটি.
বাটি — bāṭi — "a bowl".

The word, taken apart.
বাটি (bāṭi) goes back to varta, "a circular object", or more probably "something made of metal", a form CDIAL reconstructs. It lists Assamese bāṭi, "cup", Oriya bāṭi, "cup, saucer", and Bengali bāṭā, "box for betel", under it (CDIAL 11347).

Lesson 2 of 5.
হাঁড়ি (hā̃ṛi) — a cooking pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to drive, then the Bengali for a bowl.

You'll want to know: হাঁড়ি.
হাঁড়ি — hā̃ṛi — "a cooking pot".

The word, taken apart.
হাঁড়ি (hā̃ṛi) (hāṁṛi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from hāṇḍa, "pot", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 14050). Late Sanskrit has haṇḍikā, "earthen pot"; CDIAL calls any link with bhāṇḍa, "pot", unclear.
