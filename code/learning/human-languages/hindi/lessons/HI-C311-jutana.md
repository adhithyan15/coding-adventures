# जुटाना (juṭānā) — to collect; to raise (money)

## You'll want to know: जुटाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C311-MADAD311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जुटाना** — *juṭānā* — "to collect; to raise (money)".

> स्वयंसेवकों ने एक लाख रुपये जुटाए।
>
> *svayaṁsevakoṁ ne ek lākh rupaye juṭāe.*
>
> The volunteers raised a hundred thousand rupees.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पैसा जुटाया, *usne paisā juṭāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जुटाना** (*juṭānā*) is the causative of **जुटना**, to gather, from the same *\*yuṭati* (CDIAL 10496): to make things come together. You **जुटाते हो** money, help or courage.
