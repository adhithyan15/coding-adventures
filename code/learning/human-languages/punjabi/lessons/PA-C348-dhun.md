## You'll want to know: ਧੁਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C348-MUSIC348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੁਨ** — *dhun* — "a tune; a melody".

> ਉਸ ਗੀਤ ਦੀ ਧੁਨ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਿੱਠੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *us gīt dī dhun bahut miṭṭhī hai.*
>
> The tune of that song is very sweet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੁਨ** (*dhun*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhuni*, "roaring, boisterous"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dhun*, "tune", under it (CDIAL 6823).
