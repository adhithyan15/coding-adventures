## You'll want to know: ночевать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C428-NOCHLEG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ночевать** (*nochevát*) — "to spend the night".

> Мы ночевали в маленькой гостинице.
>
> *My nocheváli v málenkoy gostínitse.*
>
> We spent the night in a small hotel.

Imperfective; perfective *переночевать*. Present *ночую, ночуешь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ночевать* is built on *ночь* 'night' with the verb ending *-евать*. EtymDB lists *ночевать* without a deeper source.
