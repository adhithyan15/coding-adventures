## You'll want to know: ફાટક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C208-HIGHWAY208-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાટક** — *phāṭak* — "level crossing, gate".

> **ફાટક બંધ હતું.** — *phāṭak bandh hatũ* — "The level-crossing gate was closed."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાટક** (*phāṭak*) is usually traced to *\*phāṭṭakka*, "gate, door", a reconstructed form whose own source CDIAL leaves in doubt. CDIAL lists Hindi *phāṭak*, "gate", but for Gujarati only *phaṭkiyũ bārṇũ*, "a single shutter to close a door" (CDIAL 9069).
