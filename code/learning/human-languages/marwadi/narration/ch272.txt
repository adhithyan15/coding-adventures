6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
फगत (phagat) — only.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a witness, then the Marwadi for an oath.

You'll want to know: फगत.
फगत — phagat — "only".
हूं फगत चाय पीऊं हूं। hū̃ phagat chāy pīū̃ hū̃. I only drink tea.

The word, taken apart.
फगत (phagat) is usually traced, through Persian faqat, to Arabic faqaṭ, "only". It goes right before the thing it limits.

You'll want to know: बी.
बी — bī — "also".
हूं बी आसूं। hū̃ bī āsū̃. I'll come too.

The word, taken apart.
बी (bī) is inherited from Sanskrit api ca, "and also"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bī, "also" (CDIAL 486). It follows the word it adds.

Lesson 3 of 6.
जरूर (jarūr) — certainly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for only, then the Marwadi for also.

You'll want to know: जरूर.
जरूर — jarūr — "certainly".
हूं जरूर आसूं। hū̃ jarūr āsū̃. I'll certainly come.

The word, taken apart.
जरूर (jarūr) is usually traced, through Persian zarūr, "necessary; certainly", to Arabic ḍarūra, "necessity". जरूरी, necessary, and जरूरत, a need, come from the same word.

Lesson 4 of 6.
बेसक (besak) — of course.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for also, then the Marwadi for certainly.

You'll want to know: बेसक.
बेसक — besak — "of course".
बेसक, हूं आसूं। besak, hū̃ āsū̃. Of course I'll come.

The word, taken apart.
बेसक (besak) is usually traced to Persian bē-shakk, "without doubt", built from Persian bē, "without", and shakk, "doubt", which Persian took from Arabic.

The word, taken apart.
मंजूर (mañjūr) is usually traced, through Persian manzūr, to Arabic manẓūr, "looked at", hence "approved".
