## You'll want to know: सेतुबन्धः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C415-SROTAS415-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सेतुबन्धः** — *setubandhaḥ* — "a causeway; a dam".

> ग्रामस्य समीपे नद्यां सेतुबन्धः अस्ति।
>
> *grāmasya samīpe nadyāṃ setubandhaḥ asti.*
>
> Near the village there is a causeway across the river.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेतुबन्धः** (*setubandhaḥ*) is built from *setu*, "a bridge, a dam", and *bandha*, "a binding", from the root √bandh, "to bind"; MW gives the compound as the making of a causeway, a dam or a bridge (MW).
