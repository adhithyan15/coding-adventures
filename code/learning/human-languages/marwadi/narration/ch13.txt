Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 13: Wife and Child: One New Sign.
8 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.

Lesson 4 of 8.
च (cha) — unaspirated beside familiar छ.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one sign until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 14 seconds]
Say child and wife. Write familiar छ and independent आ; today's sound has no following breath.

Script — one sign.
च — cha.

Guided Practice.
Trace once, copy once, cover the model, and write it once from sound.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 7 of 8.
Payoff — a twelve-label family map.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Recall the seven-label and three-label checkpoints, then give the formal gratitude response. These are vocabulary options, not a prescribed family structure.

Guided Practice.
Listen: identify six randomly heard labels.
Speak: produce six different labels from relationship cues.
Read: match all twelve printed labels to meanings.
[once you have stopped driving — write: four heard labels without a model]
Score each skill separately. A strong spoken score cannot compensate for a missed reading or writing score.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
