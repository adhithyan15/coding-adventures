## You'll want to know: commoror, commorārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C455-CONSPICOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**commoror, commorārī** — "to stop for a while, to stay".

> Mercātor trēs diēs in oppidō commorātur.
>
> *The merchant stays three days in the town.*

Deponent: *commoror, commorārī, commorātus sum*. A short stay on the way, not an end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*commoror* is built from *con-* and *moror* 'to delay, to linger' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
