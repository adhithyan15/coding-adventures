## You'll want to know: quaesītor, quaesītōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C412-COGNITIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**quaesītor, quaesītōris** — "an investigator; a presiding judge".

> Quaesītor testēs audīvit.
>
> *The investigator heard the witnesses.*

*quaesītor, quaesītōris* m., built on *quaerō*, to seek, to inquire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quaesītor* is built on *quaerō* "to seek, to inquire" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from its participle *quaesītus*.
