Gujarati, chapter 446: Herds and Herders.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ધણ (dhaṇ) — a herd (of cattle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to call (of a bird), then the Gujarati for to chirp.

You'll want to know: ધણ.
ધણ — dhaṇ — "a herd (of cattle)".
સાંજે ગાયોનું ધણ ગામમાં પાછું આવે છે. sā̃je gāyonũ dhaṇ gāmmā̃ pāchũ āve che. In the evening the herd of cows comes back to the village.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધણ (dhaṇ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit dhana, "wealth"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a herd of cattle" (CDIAL 6717): cattle were a family's wealth.

You'll want to know: ભરવાડ.
ભરવાડ — bharvāḍ — "a shepherd (of the Bharvad herders)".
ભરવાડ ઘેટાં લઈને ડુંગર પર ગયો. bharvāḍ gheṭā̃ laīne ḍungar par gayo. The shepherd took the sheep up the hill.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભરવાડ (bharvāḍ) goes back to bhēḍrapāla, "a shepherd", a form CDIAL reconstructs from bhēḍra, a sheep, and pāla, a keeper; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a shepherd or goatherd" (CDIAL 9608).

ગોવાળ — govāḷ — "a cowherd".
ગોવાળ ગાયો સાથે નદી પાસે બેઠો હતો. govāḷ gāyo sāthe nadī pāse beṭho hato. The cowherd was sitting by the river with the cows.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગોવાળ (govāḷ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit gōpāla, "a protector of cows", made of gō, "a cow", and pāla, "a keeper" (CDIAL 4293).

જંગલમાં ભરવાડોનો નાનો નેસ છે. jangalmā̃ bharvāḍono nāno nes che. There is a small herders' hamlet in the forest.

Lesson 5 of 7.
ચરાવવું (carāvvũ) — to take to graze (animals).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a cowherd, then the Gujarati for a herders' hamlet.

You'll want to know: ચરાવવું.
ચરાવવું — carāvvũ — "to take to graze (animals)".
ગોવાળ રોજ ગાયોને મેદાનમાં ચરાવે છે. govāḷ roj gāyone medānmā̃ carāve che. Every day the cowherd takes the cows to graze in the field.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગાયો ચરાવી, teṇe gāyo carāvī.

The word, taken apart.
ચરાવવું (carāvvũ) is the causative partner of ચરવું, "to graze", a tadbhava of Sanskrit carati, "moves, grazes"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 4686).
