# తుదిశ్వాస (tudiśvāsa) — one's last breath

## You'll want to know: తుదిశ్వాస
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C344-SANTAPAM344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తుదిశ్వాస** — *tudiśvāsa* — "one's last breath".

> తాతగారు ఆసుపత్రిలో తుదిశ్వాస విడిచారు.
>
> *tātagāru āsupatrilō tudiśvāsa viḍicāru.*
>
> Grandfather breathed his last in hospital.

తుదిశ్వాస విడుచు, to let go the last breath: a gentle way to say died.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తుదిశ్వాస** (*tudiśvāsa*) is a mixed compound: native *tudi*, "the end" (DEDR 3314), and Sanskrit *śvāsa*, "breath" (CDIAL 12769).
