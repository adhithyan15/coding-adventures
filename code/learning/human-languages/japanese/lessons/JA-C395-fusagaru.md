## You'll want to know: ふさがる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C395-KOSHOU395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふさがる** — *fusagaru* — "to be blocked; to be closed off".

> **じこで みちが ふさがっています。** — *jiko de michi ga fusagatte imasu* — "The road is blocked because of an accident."

Polite **ふさがります**, *fusagarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ゆきで みちが ふさがりました。**, *yuki de michi ga fusagarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふさがる** (*fusagaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and JMdict marks it as usually written in kana (JMdict). It is the intransitive partner of *fusagu*, "to block" (JMdict).
