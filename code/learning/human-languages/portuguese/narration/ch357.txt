Portuguese, chapter 357: O primeiro emprego.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o estágio — the internship, the traineeship; the stage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for unemployed, then the Portuguese for the unemployment.

You'll want to know: o estágio.
o estágio — "the internship, the traineeship; the stage".
Fiz um estágio de três meses num hotel. I did a three-month internship in a hotel.
Estágio profissional — work placement. Also a stage or phase.

The word, taken apart.
estágio is usually traced to French stage "a period of training", which EtymDB derives from Medieval Latin stagium (EtymDB), a formation on Latin stāre "to stand" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
o aprendiz — the apprentice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the internship, then the Portuguese for the intern.

You'll want to know: o aprendiz.
o aprendiz — "the apprentice".
Ele começou como aprendiz numa padaria. He started as an apprentice in a bakery.
Plural os aprendizes; close to aprender.

The word, taken apart.
aprendiz is usually traced to Old French aprentis "learner", the older form of French apprenti (EtymDB), built on aprendre "to learn", from Latin apprehendere "to lay hold of, to seize", on prehendere (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
empregar — to employ; to use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the intern, then the Portuguese for the apprentice.

You'll want to know: empregar.
empregar — "to employ; to use".
A fábrica emprega duzentas pessoas. The factory employs two hundred people.
Also to use: empregar bem o tempo — to use your time well.

The word, taken apart.
empregar is inherited from Latin implicāre "to fold into, to involve" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese empregar; the learned form of the same verb is implicar.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a entidade patronal — the employer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the apprentice, then the Portuguese for to employ.

You'll want to know: a entidade patronal.
a entidade patronal — "the employer".
A entidade patronal tem de pagar as férias. The employer has to pay for the holidays.
European Portuguese, formal; in speech o patrão. In Brazil o empregador.

The word, taken apart.
entidade is usually traced to Medieval Latin entitās, built on ēns "being", and patronal is built on patrono, from Latin patrōnus "protector, patron", on pater "father" (Lewis & Short).
