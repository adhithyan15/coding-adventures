Gujarati, chapter 386: Trust and Dependence.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
આસ્થા (āsthā) — belief; faith (in God or an idea).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to attain, then the Gujarati for to honour with an award.

You'll want to know: આસ્થા.
આસ્થા — āsthā — "belief; faith (in God or an idea)".
જો તમારી આસ્થા મજબૂત હશે તો તમે હિંમત નહીં હારો. jo tamārī āsthā majbūt haśe to tame himmat nahī̃ hāro. If your faith is strong, you will not lose heart.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આસ્થા (āsthā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit āsthā, "regard, care, confidence" (Monier-Williams).
