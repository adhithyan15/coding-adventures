Arabic, chapter 56: Child, Boy, Man, Woman, Guest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طفل (ṭifl) — a child.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a husband, then the Arabic for a wife.

You'll want to know: طفل.
طفل — ṭifl — "a child".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ṭifl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ṭifl, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ṭifl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a husband, then the Arabic for a wife, then say ṭifl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does طفل (ṭifl) mean? ("A child".) Say it once more.
