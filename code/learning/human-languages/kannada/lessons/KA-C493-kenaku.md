# ಕೆಣಕು (keṇaku) — to provoke; to needle

## You'll want to know: ಕೆಣಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C493-KOPA493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೆಣಕು** — *keṇaku* — "to provoke; to needle".

> ಅಣ್ಣ ತಂಗಿಯನ್ನು ಕೆಣಕಿದಾಗ ಅವಳು ಅತ್ತಳು.
>
> *aṇṇa taṅgiyannu keṇakidāga avaḷu attaḷu.*
>
> When the elder brother needled his younger sister, she cried.

Past (I …): ಕೆಣಕಿದೆ, *keṇakide*.

The one provoked takes -ಅನ್ನು. Close to ರೇಗಿಸು, but meaner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೆಣಕು** (*keṇaku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1944 lists Kannada *keṇaku*, "to irritate, provoke, by words or deeds".
