**работа** (*rabóta*) — "work, a job".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**работа** ← Proto-Slavic *\*orbota*: Old Russian *робота* meant "work, slavery" (Vasmer). It is built on the root of *раб*, "slave", and Vasmer relates it to Gothic *arbaiþs*, "hardship", and German *Arbeit*, "work". Its *ра-* is the Church Slavonic shape; East Slavic dialects say *робота*.
