You'll want to know: びょういん.
びょういん — byōin — "a hospital".
Four beats: byo–o–i–n. The first sign is ひ with the voicing mark, so hi becomes bi, and a small ょ joins it into one beat, byo. The rest, う, い and ん, you already write. The next lesson writes the first sign.
Keep the ょ small. With a full-size よ the word would be biyōin, a hair salon.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. Script — the change.
ひ hi + ゛ becomes び bi.
Write ひ in full, in the order you learned it. Then add the two short strokes of the voicing mark at the upper right, beside the high right end, the left one first.
The h row voices to b here too. With び (bi), you write all five: ば, び (bi), ぶ, べ and ぼ.
With a small ょ and ういん after it, you can now write the word from the last lesson: びょういん, byōin, a hospital.
