## You'll want to know: દોરડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C302-BAMBO302-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દોરડું** — *dorḍũ* — "a rope".

> તેમણે દોરડાથી એક બાળકને નીચે ઉતાર્યું.
>
> *temṇe dorḍāthī ek bāḷakne nīce utāryũ.*
>
> They let a child down on a rope.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દોરડું** (*dorḍũ*) belongs with **દોરો**, a thread: both go back to Sanskrit *davara*, "string", where CDIAL lists Gujarati *doro*, "string, rope" (CDIAL 6225). The ending *-ḍũ* makes the thicker thing, a rope.
