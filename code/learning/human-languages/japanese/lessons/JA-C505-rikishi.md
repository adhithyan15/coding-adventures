## You'll want to know: りきし

**りきし** — *rikishi* — "a sumo wrestler".

> **りきしは おおきいです。** — *rikishi wa ōkii desu* — "Sumo wrestlers are big."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りきし** (*rikishi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *riki*, "power" (Middle Chinese *lik*) and *shi*, "gentleman" (*dzriX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
