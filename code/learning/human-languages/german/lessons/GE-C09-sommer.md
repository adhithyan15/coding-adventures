the chapter.

## You'll want to know: der Sommer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-SOMMER-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-JAHRESZEIT-01] -->

> **der Sommer** — "summer."

One Germanic word, kept whole by both languages: *Sommer*, **summer**. The
difference between them is the vowel letter and nothing else.

Words survive like this when nobody ever has a reason to replace them, and a hot
season is one of those things every generation needs to talk about.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-SOMMER-01] -->

- `vowel-o-german` — a short, rounded *o*, steadier than the English vowel.
- `r-final` — and the ending is the soft *r* you have on *Uhr* and *November*.

*SOM-mer*, weight on the first.
