## Fixed — twenty-two writing lessons lift only where their filmstrips lift

The native-writer refits (#16733 and the LipiTk audit) cut many Telugu
letters to fewer pen-down strokes, and their records and filmstrips changed,
but the lesson prose still described the old paths. A learner read "Lift,
then movement 2 …" beside a strip headed "one unbroken stroke". Each lesson
now lifts exactly where its record and strip do, and numbers its movements
the way the strip does: where the strip gives a short joining climb its own
panel, the prose says so ("the three movements are numbered as four").
Headings are unchanged.

- **No lift at all (prose had 1 to 4):** త TE-S01, ర TE-S06, న TE-S109,
  ల TE-S114, ద TE-S116, వ TE-S117, ళ TE-S121, ఎ TE-S124 (also its YOU TRACE
  line, which said "lower loop, lift, broad outer arch"), బ TE-S157 and
  శ TE-S159. Where the record cites a native-writer share, the prose says
  "most" or "nearly all" native writers draw the letter in one stroke; for
  త, where only about half do, it says so.
- **Fewer lifts:** హ TE-S125 (3 → 1, before the chevron), థ TE-S130
  (5 → 2, before the stem and the dot), ధ TE-S151 (4 → 1, before the stem),
  ఠ TE-S155 (2 → 1, before the dot; its YOU TRACE line now follows the
  strip's four movements), ష TE-S158 (3 → 2, before the tail and the
  chevron), ఫ TE-S160 (3 → 2), ప TE-S113 (2 → 1), డ TE-S123 and ఢ TE-S139
  ("movement 3 restarts" removed: the body is one run).
- **One more lift:** య TE-S110 (the prose lifted only before movement 4; the
  record lifts before movements 3 and 4).
- **The lift in the right place:** భ TE-S128 and మ TE-S150 lifted before the
  flourish; the record runs on into the flourish and lifts before the stem
  (భ) or the lower-right bowl (మ).
- **Checked and unchanged:** ఛ TE-S172 already lifts once, before the stem,
  as its record does.
- Narration and modality records regenerate from the new prose; every
  lesson stays under the 300 s budget (TE-S128 299 s, TE-S130 298 s).
