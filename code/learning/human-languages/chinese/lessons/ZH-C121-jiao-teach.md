## You'll want to know: jiāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C121-LEARN121-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāo* — "to teach".

> *tā jiāo wǒ hànyǔ* — She teaches me Chinese.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kaewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to teach'; the dictionary also lists the character with a level tone, *kaew*, glossed 'to imitate', and the level jiāo of the verb matches that one.
