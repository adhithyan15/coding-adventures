## You'll want to know: o cidadão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C363-CIDADAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o cidadão** — "the citizen".

> Todos os cidadãos podem votar aos dezoito anos.
>
> *All citizens can vote at eighteen.*

Plural *os cidadãos*; feminine *a cidadã*. *O cartão de cidadão* is the Portuguese identity card.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cidadão* is inherited from Old Portuguese *çidadão* (EtymDB), built on *cidade* "city", from Latin *cīvitās* "citizenship, city", on *cīvis* "citizen" (Lewis & Short).
