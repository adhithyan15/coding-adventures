## You'll want to know: il flauto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C516-STRUMENTI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il flauto** — "flute".

> A scuola tutti imparano il flauto dolce.
>
> *At school everyone learns the recorder.*

*Il flauto dolce* — the recorder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flauto* comes from Old Occitan *flaüt* (EtymDB); the deeper origin is not settled, though it may imitate a breath.
