# બિલકુલ (bilkul) — absolutely; at all (with a negative)

## You'll want to know: બિલકુલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C379-ATYANT379-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બિલકુલ** — *bilkul* — "absolutely; at all (with a negative)".

> જો તમે ઝડપી ગાડી ચલાવશો તો હું બિલકુલ નહીં બેસું.
>
> *jo tame jhaḍpī gāḍī calāvśo to hũ bilkul nahī̃ besũ.*
>
> If you drive fast, I absolutely will not get in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બિલકુલ** (*bilkul*) is usually traced to Arabic *bil-kull*, "with the whole, entirely", through Persian.
