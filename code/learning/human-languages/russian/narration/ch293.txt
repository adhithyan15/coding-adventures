Russian, chapter 293: In court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
суд (sud) — a court; a trial.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for questioning, then the Russian for a statement.

You'll want to know: суд.
суд (sud) — "a court; a trial".
Суд будет в понедельник. Sud búdet v ponedélnik. The trial will be on Monday.
В суде — in court; подать в суд (sud) — to sue.

The word, taken apart.
суд (sud) is inherited through Old East Slavic sudŭ from Proto-Slavic sǫdŭ, which EtymDB builds from PIE som- "together" and dʰeh₁- "to put": a putting together.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: адвокат.
адвокат (advokát) — "a lawyer (for the defence)".
Адвокат говорил спокойно и уверенно. Advokát govoríl spokóyno i uvérenno. The lawyer spoke calmly and confidently.
Masculine, also of a woman.

The word, taken apart.
адвокат (advokát) goes back to Latin advocātus (EtymDB), "one called in to help", the participle of advocāre "to call to" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
приговор (prigovór) — a sentence, a verdict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a court, then the Russian for a lawyer.

You'll want to know: приговор.
приговор (prigovór) — "a sentence, a verdict".
Приговор был очень строгим. Prigovór byl óchen strógim. The sentence was very harsh.
Вынести приговор (prigovór) — to pass sentence.

The word, taken apart.
приговор (prigovór) is built on при- and the root of говорить "to speak", inherited from Proto-Slavic govoriti (EtymDB): what is pronounced.

тюрьма (tyurmá) — "a prison".
Он провёл в тюрьме пять лет. On provyól v tyurmé pyat let. He spent five years in prison.
Genitive plural тюрем.

The word, taken apart.
тюрьма (tyurmá) is traced by EtymDB to Proto-Turkic türmä "prison"; others derive it through Polish from German Turm "a tower", so the origin is not fully settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
обвинять (obvinyát) — to accuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sentence, then the Russian for a prison.

You'll want to know: обвинять.
обвинять (obvinyát) — "to accuse".
Его обвиняют в краже. Yevó obvinyáyut v krázhe. He is accused of theft.
Imperfective; perfective обвинить (obvinít). The person is in the accusative, the charge with в + prepositional.

The word, taken apart.
обвинять (obvinyát) is built on об- and вина "guilt", an inherited Slavic word: to lay guilt on someone. EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.
