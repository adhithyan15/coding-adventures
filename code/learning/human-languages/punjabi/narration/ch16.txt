Punjabi, chapter 16: Build the Language Label and Values.
11 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 11.
ਭ (bha) — one new base for the next label.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one tiny step until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Keep only the familiar long-vowel mark ਾ in view. Today adds one base letter.

Script — one tiny step.
Trace ਭ slowly once, then once more. Say bha. Do not attach a vowel mark yet.

Guided Practice.
Complete one careful attempt. Compare only the new shape or word, repair one difference, and stop.

Lesson 5 of 11.
