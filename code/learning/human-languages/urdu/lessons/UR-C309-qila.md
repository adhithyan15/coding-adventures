## You'll want to know: قلعہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C309-HAMLA309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قلعہ** — *qilā* — "a fort".

> قلعہ پہاڑی پر تھا۔
>
> *qilā pahāṛī par thā.*
>
> The fort stood on a hill.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قلعہ** (*qilā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qalʿa*, "a fortress". The oblique is **قلعے**: **قلعے میں**.
