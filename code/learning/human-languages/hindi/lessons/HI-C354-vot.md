## You'll want to know: वोट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C354-NAGRIK354-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वोट** — *voṭ* — "a vote".

> मैंने पिछले चुनाव में वोट दिया।
>
> *maiṁne pichle cunāv meṁ voṭ diyā.*
>
> I voted in the last election.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वोट** (*voṭ*) is a loan from English *vote*.
