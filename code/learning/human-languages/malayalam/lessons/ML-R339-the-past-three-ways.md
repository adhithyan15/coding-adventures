# Review — the past three ways

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C339-AYIRUNNU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-UNDAYIRUNNU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "he was at home yesterday". (*avan innale vīṭṭil āyirunnu.*) Then "there was a
small shop in the village". (*grāmattil oru ceṟiya kaṭa uṇṭāyirunnu.*)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C339-AYIRUNNU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-UNDAYIRUNNU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-PAST-PROGRESSIVE-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-SCENE-EVENT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-PAST-HABITUAL-01, ML-CONCEPT-C32-POKUKA-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: "That was a good film" — *atŭ oru nalla sinima āyirunnu*]
- [YOU SAY: "Grandmother had a big house", then "there was no phone there"]
- [YOU SAY: "I was reading a book. Then Mother called."]
- [YOU SAY: "We were walking on the beach. Suddenly it rained."]
- [YOU SAY: "In my childhood I used to bathe in the pond every day."]
- [YOU SAY: a scene from yesterday evening, in the -ukayāyirunnu past, and one thing that cut into it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C339-AYIRUNNU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-UNDAYIRUNNU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-PAST-PROGRESSIVE-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-SCENE-EVENT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-PAST-HABITUAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "Was"? (**ആയിരുന്നു**.) "There was", "had"? (**ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു**.) "Was reading"?
(**വായിക്കുകയായിരുന്നു**.) Which past paints the scene, and which drops in the event? (The
*-ukayāyirunnu* past is the scene; the plain past, ***viḷiccu***, ***peytu***, is the event.)
"Used to walk"? (**നടക്കുമായിരുന്നു**, *naṭakkumāyirunnu*.)
