## You'll want to know: చూపు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C516-INDRIYAM516-01]; assesses=[] -->

**చూపు** — *cūpu* — "eyesight; a look".

> వయసుతో తాతయ్య చూపు తగ్గింది.
>
> *vayasutō tātayya cūpu taggindi.*
>
> With age grandpa's eyesight has weakened.

Also a glance: ఒక చూపు, one look. The noun of చూడు, to see.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చూపు** (*cūpu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2735 lists Telugu *cūcu*, "to see", and *cūpu*, "sight, vision, look, glance".
