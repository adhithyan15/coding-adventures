## You'll want to know: sostener
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C576-OPINA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sostener** — "to maintain, to claim; to hold up".

> Sostengo que el plan es bueno.
>
> *I maintain that the plan is good.*

Like *tener*: *sostengo, sostuve*. In a debate *sostener que* is to argue that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sostener* is inherited from Latin *sustinere* 'to hold up, to support', built from *sub(s)* and *tenere* 'to hold' (EtymDB).
