## You'll want to know: क़ाफ़िला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C407-NAKA407-04]; assesses=[] -->

**क़ाफ़िला** — *qāfilā* — "a convoy; a caravan".

> राजमार्ग पर ट्रकों का एक लंबा क़ाफ़िला जा रहा था।
>
> *rājmārg par ṭrakoṁ kā ek lambā qāfilā jā rahā thā.*
>
> A long convoy of lorries was moving along the highway.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़ाफ़िला** (*qāfilā*) is usually traced to Arabic *qāfila*, "a caravan", which came to Hindi through Persian: once a line of camels and travellers crossing the desert together, now any line of vehicles on the move.
