## You'll want to know: yóukè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C213-TRIP213-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yóukè* — "tourist".

> *zhèr de yóukè dōu shì wàiguó rén* — The tourists here are all from abroad.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yóukè* is built from *yóu* 'to travel about' and *kè* 'a guest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yuw* and *khaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
