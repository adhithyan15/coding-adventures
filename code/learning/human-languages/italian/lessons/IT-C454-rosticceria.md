## You'll want to know: la rosticceria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C454-ALIMENTARI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la rosticceria** — "the rotisserie, the hot-food takeaway".

> Stasera prendiamo il pollo in rosticceria.
>
> *Tonight we get chicken from the rotisserie.*

Roast chicken, pizza slices and ready meals.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rosticceria* is built on *arrostire* 'to roast', which is usually traced to a Germanic verb that came into Italian as a borrowing.
