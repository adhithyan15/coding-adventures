## You'll want to know: なんとか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C523-ANY523-05]; assesses=[] -->

**なんとか** — *nantoka* — "somehow (or other)".

> **もし がんばったら、かれは なんとか まにあうでしょう。** — *moshi ganbattara, kare wa nantoka maniau deshō* — "If he tries hard, he will make it somehow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なんとか** (*nantoka*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: JMdict spells it with the kanji of *nan*, "what", followed by the particles *to* and *ka* (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
