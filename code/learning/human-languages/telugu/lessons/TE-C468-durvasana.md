# దుర్వాసన (durvāsana) — a stench; a bad smell

## You'll want to know: దుర్వాసన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C468-MURUGU468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**దుర్వాసన** — *durvāsana* — "a stench; a bad smell".

> చెత్త కుప్ప నుంచి దుర్వాసన వస్తోంది.
>
> *cetta kuppa nuñci durvāsana vastōndi.*
>
> A stench is coming from the heap of rubbish.

దుర్, bad, + వాసన, a smell. Its opposite is సువాసన, a fragrance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దుర్వాసన** (*durvāsana*) is built from Sanskrit *dur-*, "bad, ill", and *vāsana*, "perfuming, scent" (Monier-Williams).
