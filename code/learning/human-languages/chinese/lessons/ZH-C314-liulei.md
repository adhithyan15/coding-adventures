## You'll want to know: liúlèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C314-FUNERAL314-05]; assesses=[] -->

*liúlèi* — "to shed tears".

> *dàjiā dōu liúlèi le* — Everyone was in tears.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liúlèi* is built from *liú* 'to flow' and *lèi* 'tears', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljuw* and *lwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, quoting an older dictionary, 'water moving' and the second 'tears'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
