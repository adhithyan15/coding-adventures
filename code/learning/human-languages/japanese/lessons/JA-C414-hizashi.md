## You'll want to know: ひざし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C414-SHITSUDO414-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひざし** — *hizashi* — "sunlight; the sun's rays".

> **なつの ひざしは つよいです。** — *natsu no hizashi wa tsuyoi desu* — "The summer sun is strong."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひざし** (*hizashi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hi*, "sun" and *zashi*, "shining" (from *sasu*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
