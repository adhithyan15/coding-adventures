## You'll want to know: مهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C530-ATFAL530-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مهد** — *mahd* — "a cradle".

> نام الطفل في المهد.
>
> *nāma aṭ-ṭifl fī al-mahd.*
>
> The baby slept in the cradle.

من المهد is from the cradle, from early childhood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مهد** (*mahd*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-h-d*, "to smooth, to spread out" (Lane). A mahd is a bed made smooth for a child.
