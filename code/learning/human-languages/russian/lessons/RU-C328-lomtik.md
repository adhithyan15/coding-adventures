## You'll want to know: ломтик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C328-NAREZAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ломтик** (*lómtik*) — "a slice".

> Она положила на тарелку два ломтика сыра.
>
> *Oná polozhíla na tarélku dva lómtika sýra.*
>
> She put two slices of cheese on the plate.

Diminutive of *ломоть* "a hunk, a slice".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ломтик* is a diminutive of *ломоть* "a hunk of bread", built on the root of *ломать* "to break": a piece broken off. EtymDB lists no parent.
