## You'll want to know: વિવાદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C342-VIVAD342-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વિવાદ** — *vivād* — "a dispute".

> મિલકત માટે બે ભાઈઓ વચ્ચે વિવાદ થયો.
>
> *milkat māṭe be bhāīo vacce vivād thayo.*
>
> A dispute arose between two brothers over property.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિવાદ** (*vivād*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vivāda*, "a dispute, a quarrel" (Monier-Williams).
