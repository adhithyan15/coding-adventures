## You'll want to know: اخراجات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C540-GHATA540-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اخراجات** — *ikhrājāt* — "expenses".

> اگر اخراجات بڑھے تو ہمیں زیادہ کام کرنا پڑے گا۔
>
> *agar ikhrājāt baṛhe to hameṅ zyādā kām karnā paṛe gā.*
>
> If our expenses go up, we will have to work more.

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اخراجات** (*ikhrājāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ikhrājāt*, "outgoings", the plural of *ikhrāj*, "taking out".
