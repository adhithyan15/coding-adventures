## You'll want to know: ثقة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C501-HIKMA501-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ثقة** — *thiqa* — "confidence; trust".

> تتكلم أختي الإنجليزية بثقة.
>
> *tatakallamu ukhtī al-injilīziyya bi-thiqa.*
>
> My sister speaks English with confidence.

ثقة بالنفس is self-confidence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ثقة** (*thiqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-th-q*, "to trust, to rely on" (Lane). The verb wathiqa, to trust, which you know, is from the same root; thiqa drops the first letter w.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
