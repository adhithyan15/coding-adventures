## You'll want to know: a franquia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C411-ALUGUER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a franquia** — "the excess (on insurance); the franchise".

> Com este seguro, a franquia é de trezentos euros.
>
> *With this insurance, the excess is three hundred euros.*

The excess is the part you pay yourself after an accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*franquia* is built on *franco* 'free, exempt', which was borrowed from French *franc* (EtymDB), first the name of the Franks: a *franquia* was a freedom from a charge.
