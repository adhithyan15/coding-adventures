Portuguese, chapter 32: Back, Hair, Belly, Face, Blood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
as costas — the back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a shoulder, then the Portuguese for a knee.

You'll want to know: as costas.
as costas — "the back". Plural in form, like English "trousers".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
