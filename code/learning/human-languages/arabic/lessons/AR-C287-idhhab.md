# اذهب مستقيما (idhhab mustaqīman) — go straight on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C287-TARIQ287-01, AR-FUNC-C41-KARRIR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask the way to the station. (*Min faḍlik, kayfa aṣilu ilā
al-maḥaṭṭa?*) Then ask someone to say it again. (*Karrir min faḍlik*.)

[PAUSE 1s] *Karrir* was an order. The answer to your question will be a
string of them.

## Grammar Lens: an order from the present
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C287-IMPERATIVE-01]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PRESENT-PERSONS-01, AR-CONCEPT-C28-DHAHABA-01, AR-LEX-C159-ACT159-05] -->

Start from "you do it": *tadhhabu*, you go. Take off the *ta-* and the final
vowel, and *-dhhab* is left, which cannot open with two consonants. Arabic
puts a short vowel in front: *u-* when the stem's vowel is *u*, and *i-*
otherwise.

> *tadhhabu* → **اذهب** *idhhab*, go · *taʿburu* → **اعبر** *uʿbur*, cross ·
> *taktubu* → **اكتب** *uktub*, write

To a woman, add *-ī*: *idhhabī*, *uʿburī*. One common order is short and
irregular: **خذ** *khudh*, take, from *akhadha*; to a woman, *khudhī*.

## Grammar Lens: the way, in orders
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C287-DIRECTIONS-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C105-LOC105-03, AR-LEX-C90-PLACE90-03, AR-LEX-C103-LOC103-02, AR-LEX-C90-PLACE90-05, AR-LEX-C92-TOWN92-02, AR-CONCEPT-C32-AKHADHA-01] -->

> **اذهب مستقيما، ثم خذ الشارع الأول على اليمين.** — *idhhab mustaqīman,
> thumma khudh ash-shāriʿa al-awwala ʿalā al-yamīn* — Go straight on, then
> take the first street on the right.

> **اعبر الجسر، والمحطة على اليسار.** — *uʿbur al-jisr, wa-l-maḥaṭṭa ʿalā
> al-yasār* — Cross the bridge, and the station is on the left.

*Mustaqīman*, with *-an*, says how you go. *ʿAlā al-yamīn* and *ʿalā
al-yasār* say where a thing is. *Thumma* strings the steps together, as it
did in a story.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C287-IMPERATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-DIRECTIONS-01, AR-LEX-C91-TOWN91-03, AR-GRAMMAR-C284-STORY-STEPS-01] -->

- [YOU SAY: *tadhhabu* … *idhhab*; *taʿburu* … *uʿbur*]
- [YOU SAY: "Go straight on" — *idhhab mustaqīman*]
- [YOU SAY: "Take the first street on the right" — *khudh ash-shāriʿa al-awwala ʿalā al-yamīn*]
- [YOU SAY: "Cross the bridge", to a woman — *uʿburī al-jisr*]

taken off.) "Go"? (***Idhhab.***) "Take"? (***Khudh***, the short one.) "On
