## You'll want to know: पतनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C547-UTTHANA547-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पतनम्** — *patanam* — "a fall".

> यदि पतनं भवति, तर्हि पुनः उत्तिष्ठतु।
>
> *yadi patanaṃ bhavati, tarhi punaḥ uttiṣṭhatu.*
>
> If you fall, get up again.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पतनम्** (*patanam*) comes from the root √pat, "to fall", which also gives *patati*, with the ending *-ana*; MW gives *patana* as flying, falling (MW).
