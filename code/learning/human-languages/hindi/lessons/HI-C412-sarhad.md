## You'll want to know: सरहद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C412-VIDESH412-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सरहद** — *sarhad* — "a frontier; a border".

> सरहद पर बहुत सिपाही थे।
>
> *sarhad par bahut sipāhī the.*
>
> There were many soldiers at the frontier.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सरहद** (*sarhad*) is usually traced to Persian *sarhad*, "a frontier", literally the head of the boundary.
