# మద్దతు (maddatu) — backing; support

## You'll want to know: మద్దతు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C385-ANGIKARAM385-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మద్దతు** — *maddatu* — "backing; support".

> ఆ ఆలోచనకు చాలా మంది మద్దతు ఇచ్చారు.
>
> *ā ālōcanaku cālā mandi maddatu iccāru.*
>
> Many people backed that idea.

మద్దతు ఇచ్చు, to give support; the person or idea backed takes -కి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మద్దతు** (*maddatu*) is usually traced to Hindustani *madad*, "help", a word of Arabic origin; the Telugu form and its sense of backing a cause are its own.
