Sanskrit, chapter 307: Lost and Found.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वियोगः (viyogaḥ) — a separation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a refugee, then the Sanskrit for comforts.

You'll want to know: वियोगः.
वियोगः — viyogaḥ — "a separation".
जलौघे कुटुम्बस्य वियोगः अभवत्। jalaughe kuṭumbasya viyogaḥ abhavat. In the flood the family was separated.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वियोगः (viyogaḥ) is built from vi, "apart", and the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives viyoga as disjunction, separation, and the loss of something (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अश्रु (aśru) — a tear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for comforts, then the Sanskrit for a separation.

You'll want to know: अश्रु.
अश्रु — aśru — "a tear".
मातुः नेत्रात् अश्रु पतति। mātuḥ netrāt aśru patati. A tear falls from the mother's eye.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अश्रु (aśru) MW reports that it is supposed to stand for daśru, from the root √daṃś, and MW compares Greek dakru, Latin lacryma and English tear; that origin is not settled (MW).

Past (she …): सा बालेन सह मिलितवती, sā bālena saha militavatī.
