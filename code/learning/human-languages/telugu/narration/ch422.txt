Telugu, chapter 422: Promises and Intentions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
హామీ (hāmī) — a guarantee; an assurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to prevent, then the Telugu for a dilemma.

You'll want to know: హామీ.
హామీ — hāmī — "a guarantee; an assurance".
ప్రభుత్వం రైతులకు సహాయం చేస్తామని హామీ ఇచ్చింది. prabhutvaṁ raitulaku sahāyaṁ cēstāmani hāmī iccindi. The government assured the farmers that it would help.
హామీ (hāmī) ఇచ్చు, to promise, to guarantee.

The word, taken apart.
హామీ (hāmī) has an origin that is not settled; it may have come into Telugu through Hindustani.

Lesson 2 of 5.
నెరవేర్చు (neravērcu) — to fulfil; to carry out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a dilemma, then the Telugu for a guarantee.

You'll want to know: నెరవేర్చు.
నెరవేర్చు — neravērcu — "to fulfil; to carry out".
అతను తన వాగ్దానాన్ని నెరవేర్చాడు. atanu tana vāgdānānni neravērcāḍu. He kept his promise.
Past (I …): నెరవేర్చాను, neravērcānu.
For promises, wishes and duties: కోరిక నెరవేర్చు (neravērcu), to fulfil a wish.

The word, taken apart.
నెరవేర్చు (neravērcu) is a Telugu verb whose origin is uncertain; DEDR has no article for it. Its intransitive partner is నెరవేరు, to come true.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఉద్దేశించు (uddēśiñcu) — to intend; to mean for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a guarantee, then the Telugu for to fulfil.

You'll want to know: ఉద్దేశించు.
ఉద్దేశించు — uddēśiñcu — "to intend; to mean for".
నేను మిమ్మల్ని బాధపెట్టాలని ఉద్దేశించలేదు. nēnu mimmalni bādhapeṭṭālani uddēśiñcalēdu. I did not mean to hurt you.
Past (I …): ఉద్దేశించాను, uddēśiñcānu.
The verb of ఉద్దేశం, an intention, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
ఉద్దేశించు (uddēśiñcu) is built on Sanskrit uddeśa, "the act of pointing to; direction" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; ఉద్దేశం is the same word.

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
నిశ్చయించు (niścayiñcu) — to decide firmly; to make up one’s mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to intend, then the Telugu for a resolve.

You'll want to know: నిశ్చయించు.
నిశ్చయించు — niścayiñcu — "to decide firmly; to make up one’s mind".
మేము ఆదివారం బయలుదేరాలని నిశ్చయించాము. mēmu ādivāraṁ bayaludērālani niścayiñcāmu. We made up our minds to set off on Sunday.
Past (I …): నిశ్చయించాను, niścayiñcānu.
The verb of నిశ్చయం, certainty; నిశ్చయించుకో, to resolve for oneself.

The word, taken apart.
నిశ్చయించు (niścayiñcu) is built on Sanskrit niścaya, "inquiry, conviction" (CDIAL 7439), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
