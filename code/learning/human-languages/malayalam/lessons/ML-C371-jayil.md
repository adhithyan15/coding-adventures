## You'll want to know: ജയിൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C371-JAIL371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ജയിൽ** — *jayil* — "a jail; a prison".

> കള്ളൻ ഇപ്പോൾ ജയിലിൽ ആണ്.
>
> *kaḷḷan ippōḷ jayilil āṇŭ.*
>
> The thief is in jail now.

In jail is ജയിലിൽ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ജയിൽ** (*jayil*) was borrowed from English *jail*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
