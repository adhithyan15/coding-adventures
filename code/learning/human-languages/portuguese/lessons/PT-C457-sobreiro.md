## You'll want to know: o sobreiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C457-ARVORES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o sobreiro** — "the cork oak".

> O sobreiro cresce bem no sul do país.
>
> *The cork oak grows well in the south of the country.*

It is a protected tree in Portugal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sobreiro* is built on old *sobro* (EtymDB) with *-eiro*; *sobro* is inherited from Latin *sūber* 'cork oak' (Lewis & Short).
