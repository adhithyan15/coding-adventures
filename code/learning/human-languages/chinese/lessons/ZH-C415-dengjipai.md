## You'll want to know: dēngjīpái
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C415-TERMINAL415-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngjīpái* — "boarding pass".

> *wǒ de dēngjīpái bú jiàn le* — My boarding pass has gone missing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngjīpái* is built from *dēngjī* 'to board a plane' and *pái* 'a card', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tong*, *kj+j* and *bea* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last as a signboard.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
