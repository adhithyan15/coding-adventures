## You'll want to know: مصیبت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C365-HALAT365-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مصیبت** — *musībat* — "a hardship; trouble".

> اگر گاڑی رک گئی تو ہم مصیبت میں ہوں گے۔
>
> *agar gāṛī ruk gaī to ham musībat meṅ hoṅ ge.*
>
> If the car breaks down, we will be in trouble.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصیبت** (*musībat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muṣība*, "a misfortune".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
