## You'll want to know: 星星
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C68-NATURE68-02]; assesses=[] -->

**星星** — *xīngxing* — "star".

> **天气很好晚上有星星** *tiānqì hěn hǎo wǎnshang yǒu xīngxing* — Fine weather: there'll be stars tonight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**星星** (*xīngxing*) is built from 星 'star' said twice, the second time light. 星 is inherited from Middle Chinese *seng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
