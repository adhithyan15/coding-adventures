## You'll want to know: ਢੇਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C305-HARVEST305-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੇਰ** — *ḍher* — "a heap; a pile".

> ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਾਣਿਆਂ ਦਾ ਵੱਡਾ ਢੇਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *khet vich dāṇiāṁ dā vaḍḍā ḍher hai.*
>
> There is a big heap of grain in the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੇਰ** (*ḍher*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhēra*, "lump, heap"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍher*, "heap", under it (CDIAL 5599).
