# われる (wareru) — to shatter; to split

## You'll want to know: われる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C162-REPAIR162-02]; assesses=[] -->

**われる** — *wareru* — "to shatter; to split".

> **この いけの こおりは よく われます。** — *kono ike no kōri wa yoku waremasu* — "The ice on this pond often cracks."

Polite **われます**, *waremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**われる** (*wareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
