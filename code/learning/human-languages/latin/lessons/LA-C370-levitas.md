## You'll want to know: levitās, levitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C370-AUCTORITAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**levitās, levitātis** — "lightness; fickleness".

> Levitātem eius omnēs reprehendunt.
>
> *Everyone blames his fickleness.*

*levitās, levitātis* f., from *levis*, light: the opposite of *gravitās*. English *levity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*levitās* is built on *levis* "light" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): lightness of weight, then of character. English *levity* comes from it.
