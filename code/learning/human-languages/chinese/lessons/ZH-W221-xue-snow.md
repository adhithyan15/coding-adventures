## Script — 雨 on top, three bars below
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-XUE-SNOW-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 雪

**Two pieces, top before bottom.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**On top, 雨, eight strokes,** pressed flat: a short bar; a short stroke down on the left; a bar that runs right and turns down with a hook; a vertical through the middle; then four dots, two on the left and two on the right.

**Under it, three strokes.** A bar that runs right and turns down, a bar across the middle, and a bar along the foot, all three meeting the stroke on the right.

8 + 3 = eleven.
