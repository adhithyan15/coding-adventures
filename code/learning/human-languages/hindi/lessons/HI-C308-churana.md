## You'll want to know: छुड़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C308-BACHAV308-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छुड़ाना** — *chuṛānā* — "to set free; to get loose".

> अरुण ने रस्सी काटकर बकरी को छुड़ाया।
>
> *Aruṇ ne rassī kāṭkar bakrī ko chuṛāyā.*
>
> Arun cut the rope and set the goat free.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बकरी को छुड़ाया, *usne bakrī ko chuṛāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छुड़ाना** (*chuṛānā*) is the causative of **छूटना**, to be released, which CDIAL 3707 sets under *\*kṣuṭyatē*, "is released": to make something come loose.
