## You'll want to know: ڈھیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C433-TILA433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھیر** — *ḍher* — "a heap; a pile".

> کسان کے پاس گندم کا ڈھیر ہے۔
>
> *kisān ke pās gandum kā ḍher hai.*
>
> The farmer has a heap of wheat.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھیر** (*ḍher*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍher*, "a heap", under *ḍhēra*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5599).
