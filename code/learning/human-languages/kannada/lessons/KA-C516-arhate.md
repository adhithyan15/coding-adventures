## You'll want to know: ಅರ್ಹತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C516-VRITTI516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅರ್ಹತೆ** — *arhate* — "a qualification; eligibility".

> ಈ ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಬೇಕಾದ ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ī kelasakke bēkāda arhate avanige ide.*
>
> He has the qualification this job needs.

ಅರ್ಹ, worthy, eligible + -ತೆ, -ness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅರ್ಹತೆ** (*arhate*) is built from Sanskrit *arha*, "meriting, deserving, worthy" (Monier-Williams), with the noun ending -*tā*, Kannada -ತೆ.
