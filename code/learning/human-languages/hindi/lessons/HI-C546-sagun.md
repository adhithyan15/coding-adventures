## You'll want to know: शगुन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C546-TAQDIR546-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शगुन** — *śagun* — "an omen (a good sign)".

> दादी मानती हैं कि यह एक अच्छा शगुन है।
>
> *Dādī māntī haiṁ ki yah ek acchā śagun hai.*
>
> Grandmother believes that this is a good omen.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शगुन** (*śagun*) is probably a later borrowing of Sanskrit *śakuna*, "a bird", which also meant "an omen", read from birds. The forms Hindi inherited, *saun* and *son*, "a good omen", are the ones CDIAL lists (CDIAL 12245); *śagun* itself is not among them.
