## You'll want to know: остывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C335-OSTYVAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**остывать** (*ostyvát*) — "to cool down, to get cold".

> Чай уже остыл.
>
> *Chay uzhé ostýl.*
>
> The tea has already gone cold.

Imperfective; perfective *остыть* (*ostýt*), *остынет*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*остывать* is built on *о-* and *стыть* "to grow cold", the Slavic root of *студ* "cold" and *простуда* "a cold"; EtymDB lists no parent.
