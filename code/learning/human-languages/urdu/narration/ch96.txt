5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لمحہ (lamhā) — a moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for last, then the Urdu for next.

You'll want to know: لمحہ.
لمحہ — lamhā — "a moment".

The word, taken apart.
لمحہ (lamhā) is a loan from Arabic lamḥa, "a glance, a moment", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is l-m-ḥ (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
جب (jab) — when (not a question).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for next, then the Urdu for a moment.

You'll want to know: جب.
جب — jab — "when (not a question)".

The word, taken apart.
جب (jab) is inherited. CDIAL lists it beside ab, "now", under Sanskrit evam eva, "exactly so", crossed with the relative stem ya-, "which" (CDIAL 2528).

Lesson 3 of 5.
کدھر (kidhar) — where, which way?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a moment, then the Urdu for when.

You'll want to know: کدھر.
کدھر — kidhar — "where, which way?".

The word, taken apart.
کدھر (kidhar) is inherited, but its history is not settled. It puts the question stem k- of kyā and kaun in front of the ending of idhar, "here", and CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دائیں (dāeṅ) — to the right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for when, then the Urdu for where.

You'll want to know: دائیں.
دائیں — dāeṅ — "to the right".

The word, taken apart.
دائیں (dāeṅ) is a form of dāyāṅ, "right", which comes from Sanskrit dākṣiṇa, "relating to the right; southern". CDIAL notes that its form was crossed with vāma, "left" (CDIAL 6251).
