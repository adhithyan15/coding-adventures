## You'll want to know: ಹಿಂಜರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C499-MANASTHITI499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿಂಜರಿ** — *hiñjari* — "to hesitate; to hold back".

> ಸಹಾಯ ಕೇಳಲು ಅವನು ಹಿಂಜರಿದನು.
>
> *sahāya kēḷalu avanu hiñjaridanu.*
>
> He hesitated to ask for help.

Past (I …): ಹಿಂಜರಿದೆ, *hiñjaride*.

What you shrink from takes the -ಅಲು form: ಕೇಳಲು ಹಿಂಜರಿ, to hesitate to ask.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿಂಜರಿ** (*hiñjari*) is native Kannada, built on *hin-*, "back, behind" (DEDR 4205), as in ಹಿಂದೆ; the origin of the second part is not settled. The picture is stepping back.
