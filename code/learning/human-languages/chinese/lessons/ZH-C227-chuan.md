## You'll want to know: chuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C227-SEA227-02]; assesses=[] -->

*chuán* — "boat; ship".

> *zuótiān wǒmen zuò chuán le* — We went out on a boat yesterday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chuán* is inherited from Middle Chinese *zywen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which notes, after an older book, that people west of the passes said this word and people east of them another.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
