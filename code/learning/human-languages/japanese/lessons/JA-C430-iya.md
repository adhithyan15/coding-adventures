## You'll want to know: いや
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C430-ANGER430-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いや** — *iya* — "unpleasant; unwanted".

> **さむい あさは いやです。** — *samui asa wa iya desu* — "I do not like cold mornings."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いや** (*iya*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
