## You'll want to know: l'obligation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C380-NORME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'obligation** — "the obligation, the duty".

> Tu n'as aucune obligation de venir.
>
> *You are under no obligation to come.*

Feminine: *une obligation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obligation* is a learned borrowing from Latin *obligātiō* "a binding, an engagement" (EtymDB), from *obligāre* "to bind", built on *ligāre* "to tie" (Lewis & Short).
