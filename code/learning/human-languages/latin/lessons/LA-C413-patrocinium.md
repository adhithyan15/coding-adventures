## You'll want to know: patrōcinium, patrōciniī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C413-CONDEMNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**patrōcinium, patrōciniī** — "defence in court, advocacy; protection".

> Patrōcinium causidicī reum nōn adiūvit.
>
> *The advocate's defence did not help the accused.*

*patrōcinium, patrōciniī* n., built on *patrōnus*: the patron's protection, and his pleading in court.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patrōcinium* is built on *patrōnus* "a patron, a defender" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which comes from *pater* "a father".
