## You'll want to know: l'elettore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C369-PARTITO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'elettore** — "the voter".

> Molti elettori non sono andati a votare.
>
> *Many voters did not go and vote.*

A woman is *l'elettrice*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*elettore* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēlēctor* "one who chooses" (EtymDB), from *ēligere* "to pick out", built on *legere* "to choose".
