## You'll want to know: अपराध्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C349-NYAYALAYA349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अपराध्यति** — *aparādhyati* — "does wrong; offends".

> यः अपराध्यति, सः दण्डं प्राप्नोति।
>
> *yaḥ aparādhyati, saḥ daṇḍaṃ prāpnoti.*
>
> Whoever does wrong is punished.

Past (he …): सः अपराद्धवान्, *saḥ aparāddhavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपराध्यति** (*aparādhyati*) is built on *apa*, "away", and the root √rādh, "to succeed"; MW gives *apa-√rādh* "to wrong, to offend against, to sin" (MW).
