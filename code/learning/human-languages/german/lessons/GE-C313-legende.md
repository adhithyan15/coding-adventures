## You'll want to know: die Legende
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C313-MAERCHEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Legende** — "the legend".

> Eine alte Legende erzählt von dieser Brücke.
>
> *An old legend tells of this bridge.*

Plural: *die Legenden*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Legende* comes from Middle High German *legende* "the story of a saint" (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *legenda* "things to be read", built on *legere* "to read" (Lewis & Short).
