Sanskrit, chapter 423: Ways In and Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रवेशः (praveśaḥ) — an entrance; entry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a tunnel, then the Sanskrit for a ditch.

You'll want to know: प्रवेशः.
प्रवेशः — praveśaḥ — "an entrance; entry".
प्रवेशः वामतः अस्ति। praveśaḥ vāmataḥ asti. The entrance is on the left.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रवेशः (praveśaḥ) is built from pra, "forward", and the root √viś, "to enter", which also gives praviśati, "enters"; MW gives praveśa as entering, entrance (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a ditch, then the Sanskrit for an entrance.

You'll want to know: निर्गमः.
निर्गमः — nirgamaḥ — "a way out; an exit".
निर्गमः दक्षिणतः अस्ति। nirgamaḥ dakṣiṇataḥ asti. The exit is on the right.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
निर्गमः (nirgamaḥ) is built from nir, "out", and the root √gam, "to go", as nirgacchati, "goes out", is; MW gives nirgama as going forth, departure, and the way out of a building is that sense in modern use (MW).

मम गृहं विपरीतायां दिशायाम् अस्ति। mama gṛhaṃ viparītāyāṃ diśāyām asti. My house is in the opposite direction.

The word, taken apart.
विपरीतः (viparītaḥ) is built from vi, pari and the root √i, "to go": gone round the other way; MW gives viparīta as turned round, reversed, opposite (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अन्तरम् (antaram) — a gap; the distance between.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an exit, then the Sanskrit for opposite.

You'll want to know: अन्तरम्.
अन्तरम् — antaram — "a gap; the distance between".
गृहस्य मार्गस्य च अन्तरम् अल्पम् अस्ति। gṛhasya mārgasya ca antaram alpam asti. The gap between the house and the road is small.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अन्तरम् (antaram) is built on antar, "within, between"; MW gives antara as interior and, as a noun, an interval, a distance, a gap (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: द्वारपालः.
द्वारपालः — dvārapālaḥ — "a gatekeeper".
द्वारपालः प्रवेशे तिष्ठति। dvārapālaḥ praveśe tiṣṭhati. The gatekeeper stands at the entrance.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
द्वारपालः (dvārapālaḥ) is built from dvāra, "a door, a gate", and pāla, "a guard", from the root √pā, "to protect"; MW gives the compound as a doorkeeper (MW).
