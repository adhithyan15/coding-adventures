Urdu, chapter 501: Metal and Jewellery.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
لوہا (lohā) — iron.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to darn, then the Urdu for to come unstitched.

You'll want to know: لوہا.
لوہا — lohā — "iron".
لوہار لوہے سے اوزار بناتا ہے۔ lohār lohe se auzār banātā hai. The blacksmith makes tools out of iron.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
لوہا (lohā) is inherited from Sanskrit lōha, "red, copper-coloured", later "copper" and "iron" (CDIAL 11158).

Lesson 4 of 6.
پگھلانا (pighlānā) — to melt (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for brass, then the Urdu for jewellery.

You'll want to know: پگھلانا.
پگھلانا — pighlānā — "to melt (something)".
لوہار نے آگ میں لوہا پگھلایا۔ lohār ne āg meṅ lohā pighlāyā. The blacksmith melted the iron in the fire.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پیتل پگھلایا, us ne pītal pighlāyā.

The word, taken apart.
پگھلانا (pighlānā) is the causative of pighalnā, "to melt", which CDIAL lists under praghirati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8486).

Lesson 5 of 6.
مڑھنا (maṛhnā) — to plate (with metal); to encase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for jewellery, then the Urdu for to melt (something).

You'll want to know: مڑھنا.
مڑھنا — maṛhnā — "to plate (with metal); to encase".
کاریگر نے تختے پر پیتل مڑھا۔ kārīgar ne takhte par pītal maṛhā. The craftsman plated the board with brass.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دروازے پر لوہا مڑھا, us ne darvāze par lohā maṛhā.

The word, taken apart.
مڑھنا (maṛhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin maṛhnā, "to cover, to overspread, to encrust", under maḍh, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9729).
