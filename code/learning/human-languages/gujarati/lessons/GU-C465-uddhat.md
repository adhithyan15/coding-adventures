## You'll want to know: ઉદ્ધત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C465-SAKHAT465-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્ધત** — *uddhat* — "insolent; rude".

> ઉદ્ધત છોકરાએ ગુરુને ખરાબ જવાબ આપ્યો.
>
> *uddhat chokrāe gurune kharāb javāb āpyo.*
>
> The insolent boy gave the teacher a rude answer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્ધત** (*uddhat*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *uddhata*, "raised, lifted up", as Monier-Williams records it; the sense "haughty, insolent" grew from holding one's head too high.
