## You'll want to know: la garanzia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C397-GARANZIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la garanzia** — "the guarantee, the warranty".

> Se si rompe, è ancora in garanzia.
>
> *If it breaks, it is still under warranty.*

*In garanzia* — under warranty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garanzia* is built on *garante* "guarantor", which came from French *garant*, a word of Germanic, probably Frankish, origin; EtymDB sets it beside Old High German *werento* "guarantor" (EtymDB), but the exact form behind it is not settled.
