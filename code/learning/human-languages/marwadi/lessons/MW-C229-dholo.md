## You'll want to know: धोळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C229-LIGHT229-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धोळो** — *dhoḷo* — "white".

> म्हारो कुरतो धोळो है।
>
> *mhāro kurto dhoḷo hai.*
>
> My kurta is white.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धोळो** (*dhoḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhavala*, "dazzling white"; CDIAL lists Hindi *dhaulā*, "white", and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6767).
