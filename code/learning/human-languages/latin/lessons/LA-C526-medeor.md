## You'll want to know: medeor, medērī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C526-SANO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**medeor, medērī** — "to remedy, be good for".

> Haec herba morbō medētur.
>
> *This herb is good for the disease.*

Deponent, with the dative: *morbō medērī*. No perfect in use. *Medicus*, *medicīna* and *remedium* come from the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**medeor** is inherited through Proto-Italic from a Proto-Indo-European root *med-* "to measure, take care" (EtymDB). *medicus*, *medicīna* and *remedium* belong to it, and so English *medicine* and *remedy*.
