Russian, chapter 73: Below, Left, Right, A Lot, A Little.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
внизу (vnizú) — below, downstairs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for near, then the Russian for up above.
