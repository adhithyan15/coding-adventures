## You'll want to know: ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C440-PARAVANAGI440-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿ** — *rāyabhāra kacēri* — "an embassy".

> ನಾನು ವೀಸಾಗಾಗಿ ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿಗೆ ಹೋದೆ.
>
> *nānu vīsāgāgi rāyabhāra kacērige hōde.*
>
> I went to the embassy for a visa.

ರಾಯಭಾರಿ is an ambassador; ಕಚೇರಿ is an office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿ** (*rāyabhāra kacēri*) joins ರಾಯಭಾರ, "an envoy's charge", and ಕಚೇರಿ, "an office". ರಾಯ comes from Sanskrit *rājan*, "a king", through Prakrit *rāya* (CDIAL 10679); how *bhāra*, "a burden", came to mean an envoy's office is not settled.
