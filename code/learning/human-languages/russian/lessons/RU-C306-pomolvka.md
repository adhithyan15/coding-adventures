## You'll want to know: помолвка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C306-POMOLVKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**помолвка** (*pomólvka*) — "an engagement".

> После помолвки они поехали к родителям.
>
> *Pósle pomólvki oní poyékhali k rodítelyam.*
>
> After the engagement they went to visit the parents.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*помолвка* is built on *помолвить* "to promise in marriage", from *молвить* "to say", inherited from Proto-Slavic *mŭlviti* (EtymDB): a spoken promise.
