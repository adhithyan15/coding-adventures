## You'll want to know: ちきゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C336-KANKYOU336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ちきゅう** — *chikyū* — "the earth; the globe".

> **ちきゅうが あつく なったと おもいます。** — *chikyū ga atsuku natta to omoimasu* — "I think the earth has got hotter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちきゅう** (*chikyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chi*, "ground" (Middle Chinese *dijH*) and *kyū*, "sphere" (*gjuw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
