Persian, chapter 53: Sand, Cloud, Sea, Fire, Snow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ماسه (mâse) — sand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a lake, then the Persian for a stone.

You'll want to know: ماسه.
ماسه — mâse — "sand".

The word, taken apart.
mâse is the everyday word for sand, but no source used here gives its origin, which is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ابر (abr) — a cloud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a stone, then the Persian for sand.

You'll want to know: ابر.
ابر — abr — "a cloud".

The word, taken apart.
abr is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian abr, "cloud" (EtymDB). EtymDB takes it back to an Indo-European word for "cloud", the same word as Sanskrit abhra.
