## You'll want to know: la opción
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C617-DECIDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la opción** — "the option, the choice".

> Tenemos dos opciones.
>
> *We have two options.*

*No tener opción* — to have no choice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*opción* is a learned borrowing from Latin *optio* 'free choice', built on *optare* 'to choose, to wish' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
