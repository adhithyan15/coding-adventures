Lesson 1 of 5.
les déchets — rubbish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a path, then the French for a cave.

You'll want to know: les déchets.
les déchets — "rubbish".

The word, taken apart.
déchets is the plural of a word formed from the verb for "to fall away", built on Latin cadere, "to fall" (Lewis & Short): what falls away.
