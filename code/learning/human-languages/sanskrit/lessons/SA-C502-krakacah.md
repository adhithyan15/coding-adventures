## You'll want to know: क्रकचः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C502-KARMASALA502-04]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रकचः** — *krakacaḥ* — "a saw".

> सः क्रकचेन दारु छिनत्ति।
>
> *saḥ krakacena dāru chinatti.*
>
> He cuts the wood with a saw.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रकचः** (*krakacaḥ*) is the word MW records for a saw; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
