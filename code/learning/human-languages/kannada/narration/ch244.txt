Kannada, chapter 244: To move aside, To shrink, To lick, To suck, To install.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸರಿಸು (sarisu) — to move aside, to shift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to manage, then the Kannada for to shake.

You'll want to know: ಸರಿಸು.
ಸರಿಸು — sarisu — "to move aside, to shift".

The word, taken apart.
ಸರಿಸು (sarisu) is native Dravidian, from sari, "to move, go to one side", which DEDR 2360 lists, with -isu, "to make"; the entry also has Tamil cari and Telugu jaragu.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಅಳವಡಿಸು.
ಅಳವಡಿಸು — aḷavaḍisu — "to install, to fit".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ಅಳವಡಿಸು (aḷavaḍisu) is uncertain. It is often linked to aḷavu, "ability, possibility", which DEDR 291 lists, but DEDR does not list aḷavaḍisu itself.
