## You'll want to know: યોગ્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C543-APVAD543-02]; assesses=[] -->

**યોગ્ય** — *yogya* — "suitable".

> જો આ છોકરો યોગ્ય હશે તો અમે તેને નોકરી આપીશું.
>
> *jo ā chokro yogya haśe to ame tene nokrī āpīśũ.*
>
> If this boy is suitable, we will give him the job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**યોગ્ય** (*yogya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *yogya*, "fit for the yoke; fit, suitable" (Monier-Williams).
