Arabic, chapter 505: Bones and joints.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جمجمة (jumjuma) — a skull.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the intestines, then the Arabic for the pulse.

You'll want to know: جمجمة.
جمجمة — jumjuma — "a skull".
الجمجمة تحمي الدماغ. al-jumjuma taḥmī ad-dimāgh. The skull protects the brain.
Plural: جماجم, jamājim.

The word, taken apart.
جمجمة (jumjuma) is a native Arabic word on a doubled four-letter shape, j-m-j-m. EtymDB sets it beside Hebrew gulgolet and Aramaic gulgaltā, "a skull", but how they are related is not settled. Its plural jamājim follows the usual four-letter pattern.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ضلع (ḍilʿ) — a rib.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the pulse, then the Arabic for a skull.

You'll want to know: ضلع.
ضلع — ḍilʿ — "a rib".
كسر اللاعب ضلعا في المباراة. kasara al-lāʿib ḍilʿan fī al-mubārā. The player broke a rib in the match.
Plural: أضلاع, aḍlāʿ.
In geometry ضلع (ḍilʿ) is also the side of a triangle.

The word, taken apart.
ضلع (ḍilʿ) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-l-ʿ, "a rib; to be crooked" (Lane). Lane links the rib to the sense of being bent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مفصل (mafṣil) — a joint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a thigh, then the Arabic for a hip.

You'll want to know: مفصل.
مفصل — mafṣil — "a joint".
عند جدي ألم في المفاصل. ʿinda jaddī alam fī al-mafāṣil. My grandfather has pain in his joints.
Plural: مفاصل, mafāṣil.

The word, taken apart.
مفصل (mafṣil) is built on the native Arabic root f-ṣ-l, "to separate, to divide" (Lane). Faṣl, a season, which you know, shares the root: both name a division. A mafṣil is where the body divides.
