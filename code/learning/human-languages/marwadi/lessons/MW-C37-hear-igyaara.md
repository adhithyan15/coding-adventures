## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-IGYAARA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *igyārā* — **eleven**

The count so far stopped at *das*, ten, and jumped to *bīs*, twenty. This is the
first of the nine words that fill the gap.

Say it in three beats: *i-gyā-rā*. The middle beat carries a **gy** cluster that
has to stay one sound, not two — the tongue is already at the palate for the
**y** while the **g** is still closing.
