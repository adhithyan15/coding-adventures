# र — one sign, and the beginning of a greeting

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You do not need an alphabet first. Today your eye and hand need one
sign.

## Script — notice the sign
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **र** — *ra*

Devanagari consonants carry a short *a* by default, so this is **ra**, not a
bare *r*. The *r* is a quick tongue-tip tap.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

Put a finger over **र** and follow the printed shape. Then copy it three times,
large and slow. Say *ra* each time your pen stops.

This lesson asks you to copy the shape you can see. It does not invent one
universal stroke order where school traditions differ.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->
