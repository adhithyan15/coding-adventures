## You'll want to know: ایثار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C346-KHIDMAT346-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ایثار** — *īsār* — "selflessness; self-sacrifice".

> ماں کا ایثار کوئی نہیں بھولتا۔
>
> *māṅ kā īsār koī nahīṅ bhūltā.*
>
> No one forgets a mother's selflessness.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ایثار** (*īsār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *īthār*, "putting another before oneself".
