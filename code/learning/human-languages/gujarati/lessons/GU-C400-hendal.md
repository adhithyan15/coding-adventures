# હેન્ડલ (henḍal) — a handlebar; a handle

## You'll want to know: હેન્ડલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C400-SCOOTER400-04]; assesses=[] -->

**હેન્ડલ** — *henḍal* — "a handlebar; a handle".

> સ્કૂટરનું હેન્ડલ તૂટી ગયું.
>
> *skūṭarnũ henḍal tūṭī gayũ.*
>
> The scooter's handlebar broke.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હેન્ડલ** (*henḍal*) is borrowed from English *handle*; on a scooter or a bicycle it is the handlebar.
