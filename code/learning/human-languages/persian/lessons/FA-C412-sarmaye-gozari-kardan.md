## You'll want to know: سرمایه گذاری کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C412-SARMAYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سرمایه گذاری کردن** (*sarmâye gozâri kardan*) — "to invest".

> پدرم در یک شرکت کوچک سرمایه گذاری کرد.
>
> *pedaram dar yek sherkat-e kuchek sarmâye gozâri kard.*
>
> My father invested in a small company.

*Sarmâye* + *gozâri* "placing", from *gozâshtan* "to put", + *kardan*. Takes *dar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرمایه گذاری کردن** is native Persian, built from *sarmâye* "capital" + *gozâri* "placing", built on *gozâr-*, the present stem of *gozâshtan* "to put", + *kardan*. To invest is to place capital.
