## You'll want to know: राणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C325-RAJA325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**राणी** — *rāṇī* — "a queen".

> राणीने गरीब लोकांसाठी विहिरी बांधल्या.
>
> *rāṇīne garīb lokāṁsāṭhī vihirī bāndhalyā.*
>
> The queen built wells for poor people.

Feminine.

Plural राण्या.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राणी** (*rāṇī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *rāṇī*, 'a queen', under Sanskrit *rājñī*, 'a queen' (CDIAL 10692).
