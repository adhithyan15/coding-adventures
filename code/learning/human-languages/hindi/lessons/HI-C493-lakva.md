## You'll want to know: लकवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C493-DAMA493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लकवा** — *lakvā* — "paralysis; a stroke".

> लकवे के बाद दादा का एक हाथ कमज़ोर है।
>
> *lakve ke bād dādā kā ek hāth kamzor hai.*
>
> Since his stroke, my grandfather has one weak hand.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लकवा** (*lakvā*) is usually traced to Arabic *laqwa*, "facial palsy", which reached Hindi through Persian and widened to any paralysis.
