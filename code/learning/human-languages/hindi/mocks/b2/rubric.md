# Hindi B2 mock rubric

Score either B2 mock against [`task-shapes/b2.json`](../../task-shapes/b2.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are four independent 100-point
papers. The learner must earn at least 60/100 in every paper; a strong paper
cannot compensate for a failed one.

## Reading — 100 points

Argument, reporting, instructions, and literary/cultural prose parts are worth
25 points each. Divide a part's points equally among its eight keyed items and
retain one decimal place. Items may test explicit detail, structure,
claim-evidence relations, source attribution, conditions and exceptions,
figurative meaning, cultural inference, and implicit stance. Credit only an
answer supported by the text.

## Listening — 100 points

The multiparty transaction, interview/panel, extended report, and cultural
narrative parts are worth 25 points each. Each recording plays once, after an
unscored orienting preview, at 150–170 words per minute. At least two regional
Hindi voices must be represented across a mock without turning accent into a
test trick. Score the seven keyed items in each part equally. No transcript,
Romanization, dictionary, translator, or learner-controlled audio is allowed.

## Writing — 100 points

The 100–130 word interaction text is worth 35 points: task fulfilment and
relevant content 10, audience/register 7, organisation/cohesion 6, vocabulary
and grammar 8, and orthographic control 4. The 220–280 word extended text is
worth 65 points: task fulfilment/development 18, organisation/cohesion 14,
genre/register 10, vocabulary and grammar 16, and orthographic control 7. A
response outside the range loses only credit for content or control it does not
demonstrate. Romanized, translated, copied, or memorised model text earns no
independent-writing credit.

## Speaking — 100 points

The four-minute presentation is worth 35 points, collaborative problem-solving
30, and defended discussion 35. For the presentation award task development 12,
organisation/cohesion 8, vocabulary/grammar 9, and pronunciation/fluency 6. For
collaboration award task achievement 8, interaction/repair 8, comparison and
negotiation 6, vocabulary/grammar 5, and pronunciation/fluency 3. For defended
discussion award reasoned defence 10, interaction/repair 7, register 5,
vocabulary/grammar 8, and pronunciation/fluency 5. Score a paired learner only
on their own contribution. Paper keywords made during ten minutes of
preparation are allowed; a written answer script is not.

## Readiness boundary

These mocks are project-defined B2 target evidence, not an official CEFR
qualification. They become readiness evidence only after clean timed
administrations, calibrated scoring, qualified human review of Hindi language
and cultural content, and recording review. Until then, report an executable
target rather than a proven pass.
