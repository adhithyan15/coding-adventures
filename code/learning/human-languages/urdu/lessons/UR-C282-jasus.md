## You'll want to know: جاسوس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C282-TAFTISH282-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جاسوس** — *jāsūs* — "a detective; a spy".

> جاسوس نے سب کچھ دیکھا۔
>
> *jāsūs ne sab kuchh dekhā.*
>
> The detective saw everything.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جاسوس** (*jāsūs*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jāsūs*, "a spy". In stories it is the detective who works out who did it.
