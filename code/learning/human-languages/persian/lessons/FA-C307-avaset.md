## You'll want to know: اواسط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C307-AVAYEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اواسط** (*avâset*) — "the middle of, mid-".

> اواسط تابستان هوا خیلی گرم بود.
>
> *Avâset-e tâbestân havâ kheyli garm bud.*
>
> In the middle of summer the weather was very hot.

Broken plural of *vasat* "middle": *avâset-e hafte* "midweek".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avâset* is usually traced to Arabic *awāsiṭ*, the broken plural of *wasaṭ* "middle", which Persian also has as *vasat*. EtymDB lists no entry for the plural.
