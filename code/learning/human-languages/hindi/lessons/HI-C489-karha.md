## You'll want to know: काढ़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C489-MARHAM489-05]; assesses=[] -->

**काढ़ा** — *kāṛhā* — "a herbal brew; a decoction".

> ज़ुकाम में दादी अदरक का काढ़ा बनाती हैं।
>
> *zukām meṁ dādī adrak kā kāṛhā banātī haiṁ.*
>
> When someone has a cold, my grandmother makes a ginger brew.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काढ़ा** (*kāṛhā*) is probably the noun of *kāṛhnā*, "to pull out", which CDIAL lists under *\*kaḍḍhati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2660): what boiling draws out of herbs. The link of senses is not given by CDIAL.
