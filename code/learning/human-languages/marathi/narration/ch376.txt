Marathi, chapter 376: Weighing Things Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तोलणे (tolṇe) — to weigh up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to comprehend, then the Marathi for to practise.

You'll want to know: तोलणे.
तोलणे — tolṇe — "to weigh up".
निर्णय घेण्यापूर्वी त्याने दोन्ही बाजू तोलल्या. nirṇay gheṇyāpūrvī tyāne donhī bājū tolalyā. Before deciding, he weighed up both sides.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने तोललं, tyāne tollaṁ.
Also to weigh on scales.

The word, taken apart.
तोलणे (tolṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi tol, "a weight for weighing", under Sanskrit tōlya, "to be weighed" (CDIAL 5980), and the verb is built on that noun.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पारखणे (pārakhṇe) — to assess; to judge the worth of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to practise, then the Marathi for to weigh up.

You'll want to know: पारखणे.
पारखणे — pārakhṇe — "to assess; to judge the worth of".
माणसं पारखायला वेळ लागतो. māṇsaṁ pārkhāylā veḷ lāgto. It takes time to judge people.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पारखलं, tyāne pārakhlaṁ.
पारख, the eye of a judge: त्याला सोन्याची पारख आहे.

The word, taken apart.
पारखणे (pārakhṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi pārakhṇẽ, "to examine", under Sanskrit parīkṣate, "observes, examines" (CDIAL 7904); परीक्षा, an exam, is its learned cousin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
मूल्यमापन करणे (mūlyamāpan karṇe) — to evaluate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to weigh up, then the Marathi for to assess.

You'll want to know: मूल्यमापन करणे.
मूल्यमापन करणे — mūlyamāpan karṇe — "to evaluate".
शिक्षक वर्षभर मुलांच्या कामाचं मूल्यमापन करतात. śikṣak varṣabhar mulāñcyā kāmācaṁ mūlyamāpan kartāt. Teachers evaluate the children's work all year round.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मूल्यमापन केलं, tyāne mūlyamāpan kelaṁ.
What is evaluated takes -चं: कामाचं मूल्यमापन.

The word, taken apart.
मूल्यमापन करणे (mūlyamāpan karṇe) joins मूल्यमापन, a learned compound built from Sanskrit mūlya, "value", and māpana, "measuring" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: गुणवत्ता.
गुणवत्ता — guṇvattā — "quality".
या दुकानातल्या भाज्यांची गुणवत्ता चांगली आहे. yā dukānātalyā bhājyāñcī guṇvattā cāṅglī āhe. The quality of the vegetables in this shop is good.
Feminine.
गुण, merit, + -वत्ता.

The word, taken apart.
गुणवत्ता (guṇvattā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit guṇavattā, "excellence, possessing merit" (Monier-Williams).
