## You'll want to know: مدعو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C328-HAFL328-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مدعو** — *madʿuww* — "an invited guest".

> كان كل مدعو يحمل هدية.
>
> *kāna kull madʿuww yaḥmilu hadiyya.*
>
> Every guest was carrying a present.

Plural مدعوون.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مدعو** (*madʿuww*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-ʿ-w* (Lane), "to call, to invite", the root of the taught *daʿā*. *Madʿuww* is the one called: an invited guest.
