## You'll want to know: भुनाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C429-PARCHI429-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भुनाना** — *bhunānā* — "to cash; to change (a note)".

> मैंने बैंक में चेक भुनाया।
>
> *maiṁne baiṅk meṁ cek bhunāyā.*
>
> I cashed the cheque at the bank.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सौ का नोट भुनाया, *usne sau kā noṭ bhunāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भुनाना** (*bhunānā*) is listed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *bhajyate*, "is broken" (CDIAL 9361), with Hindi *bhunnā*, "to be changed (of money)", and its causative *bhunānā*: to break a big note into smaller money. CDIAL calls the u of these forms unexplained.
