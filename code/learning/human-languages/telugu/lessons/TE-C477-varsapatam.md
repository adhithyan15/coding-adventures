## You'll want to know: వర్షపాతం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C477-RUTUPAVANAM477-02]; assesses=[] -->

**వర్షపాతం** — *varṣapātaṁ* — "rainfall".

> ఆ సంవత్సరం వర్షపాతం చాలా తక్కువ.
>
> *ā saṁvatsaraṁ varṣapātaṁ cālā takkuva.*
>
> Rainfall that year was very low.

వర్ష, rain, + పాతం, a fall: the amount of rain that falls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వర్షపాతం** (*varṣapātaṁ*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *varṣa*, "rain" (CDIAL 11392), and *pāta*, "a fall, a falling" (Monier-Williams).
