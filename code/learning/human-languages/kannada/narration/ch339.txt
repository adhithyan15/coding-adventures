Kannada, chapter 339: A Riot in the Market.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಗಲಭೆ (galabhe) — a riot; a disturbance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the truth, then the Kannada for to listen closely.

You'll want to know: ಗಲಭೆ.
ಗಲಭೆ — galabhe — "a riot; a disturbance".
ಸಂತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಣ್ಣ ಜಗಳ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಗಲಭೆ ಆಯಿತು. santeyalli saṇṇa jagaḷa doḍḍa galabhe āyitu. A small quarrel at the market became a big riot.
Plural: ಗಲಭೆಗಳು, galabhegaḷu.
In the news: ಗಲಭೆ (galabhe) ನಡೆಯಿತು, there was a riot.

The word, taken apart.
ಗಲಭೆ (galabhe) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1310 lists Kannada galabe, "hubbub, clamour", and galabili, "disorder", beside Telugu galaba, "confusion, noise".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕೋಲಾಹಲ (kōlāhala) — an uproar; a commotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to listen closely, then the Kannada for a riot.

You'll want to know: ಕೋಲಾಹಲ.
ಕೋಲಾಹಲ — kōlāhala — "an uproar; a commotion".
ಗೆದ್ದ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಕೇಳಿ ಮೈದಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೋಲಾಹಲ ಎದ್ದಿತು. gedda suddi kēḷi maidānadalli kōlāhala edditu. Hearing of the win, an uproar rose in the ground.
An uproar "rises": ಕೋಲಾಹಲ (kōlāhala) ಏಳು. It can be joyful or angry.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೋಲಾಹಲ (kōlāhala) is borrowed from Sanskrit kolāhala, "a loud and confused sound, uproar" (Monier-Williams), a word that imitates its own noise.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಜನಸಂದಣಿ (janasandaṇi) — a crowd; a throng.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a riot, then the Kannada for an uproar.

You'll want to know: ಜನಸಂದಣಿ.
ಜನಸಂದಣಿ — janasandaṇi — "a crowd; a throng".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ದಿನ ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನದ ಮುಂದೆ ಜನಸಂದಣಿ ಇತ್ತು. habbada dina dēvasthānada munde janasandaṇi ittu. On the festival day there was a crowd in front of the temple.
ಜನ, people, and ಸಂದಣಿ, crowding: a press of people.

The word, taken apart.
ಜನಸಂದಣಿ (janasandaṇi) is a Kannada compound of ಜನ, "people", from Sanskrit jana (Monier-Williams), and ಸಂದಣಿ, "crowding"; the origin of ಸಂದಣಿ is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an uproar, then the Kannada for a crowd.

You'll want to know: ಚದುರು.
ಚದುರು — caduru — "to scatter; to disperse".
ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಬಂದ ಕೂಡಲೇ ಜನ ಚದುರಿದರು. polīsaru banda kūḍalē jana caduridaru. As soon as the police came, the people scattered.
Past (it …): ಚದುರಿತು, caduritu.
A crowd or clouds disperse: ಮೋಡ ಚದುರಿತು. To scatter them is ಚದುರಿಸು.

The word, taken apart.
ಚದುರು (caduru) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1546 lists Kannada cadaṟu, "to be scattered about, disperse", beside Tamil citar, "to scatter".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ (kāltuḷita) — a stampede.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a crowd, then the Kannada for to scatter.

You'll want to know: ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ.
ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ — kāltuḷita — "a stampede".
ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ ಆಗಿ ಹಲವರಿಗೆ ಗಾಯವಾಯಿತು. jātreyalli kāltuḷita āgi halavarige gāyavāyitu. There was a stampede at the fair and many were hurt.
ಕಾಲು, foot, and ತುಳಿತ, trampling.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ (kāltuḷita) is a native Dravidian compound: kāl, "foot, leg" (DEDR 1479), and a noun from tuḷi, "to tread, to trample" (DEDR 3522).
