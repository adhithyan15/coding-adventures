## You'll want to know: خالص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C426-KHALIS426-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خالص** — *khālis* — "unmixed; pure".

> یہ دودھ خالص ہے۔
>
> *yih dūdh khālis hai.*
>
> This milk is pure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خالص** (*khālis*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khāliṣ*, "pure, unmixed".
