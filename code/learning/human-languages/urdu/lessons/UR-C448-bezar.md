## You'll want to know: بیزار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C448-CURIOUS448-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بیزار** — *bezār* — "fed up; weary (of something)".

> وہ روز ایک ہی کام سے بیزار ہے۔
>
> *voh roz ek hī kām se bezār hai.*
>
> He is fed up with doing the same work every day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بیزار** (*bezār*) is usually traced to Persian *bēzār*, "weary of, sick of".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
