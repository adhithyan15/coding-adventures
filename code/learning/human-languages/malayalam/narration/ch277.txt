Malayalam, chapter 277: Auspicious moment, Anniversary, Nowadays, On that day, Meanwhile.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
മുഹൂർത്തം (muhūrttaṁ) — an auspicious moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for usually, then the Malayalam for rarely.

You'll want to know: മുഹൂർത്തം.
മുഹൂർത്തം — muhūrttaṁ — "an auspicious moment".

The word, taken apart.
മുഹൂർത്തം (muhūrttaṁ) from Sanskrit muhūrta, "moment", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 10229).

Lesson 2 of 5.
വാർഷികം (vārṣikaṁ) — an anniversary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for rarely, then the Malayalam for an auspicious moment.

You'll want to know: വാർഷികം.
വാർഷികം — vārṣikaṁ — "an anniversary".

The word, taken apart.
വാർഷികം (vārṣikaṁ) from Sanskrit vārṣika, "belonging to the rains; annual", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 11571). It is built on varṣa, "rain; year".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ഇക്കാലത്ത് (ikkālattŭ) — nowadays.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an auspicious moment, then the Malayalam for an anniversary.

You'll want to know: ഇക്കാലത്ത്.
ഇക്കാലത്ത് — ikkālattŭ — "nowadays".

The word, taken apart.
ഇക്കാലത്ത് (ikkālattŭ) is "in this time": the near-pointing base (DEDR 410), Sanskrit kāla, "time" (CDIAL 3084), and the Malayalam place ending.

Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an anniversary, then the Malayalam for nowadays.

You'll want to know: അന്ന്.
അന്ന് — annŭ — "on that day, back then".

The word, taken apart.
അന്ന് (annŭ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1 lists it, "that day, then", among the far-pointing words, with Tamil aṉṟu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ഇതിനിടെ (itiniṭe) — meanwhile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for nowadays, then the Malayalam for on that day.

You'll want to know: ഇതിനിടെ.
ഇതിനിടെ — itiniṭe — "meanwhile".

The word, taken apart.
ഇതിനിടെ (itiniṭe) is native Dravidian: itŭ, "this" (DEDR 410), and iṭa, "interval" (DEDR 448, with Tamil iṭai): "in the meantime of this".
