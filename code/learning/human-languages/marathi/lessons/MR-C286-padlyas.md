# पाऊस पडल्यास (pāūs paḍalyās) — should it happen, and otherwise

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C286-JAR-TAR-01, MR-GRAMMAR-LYAAVAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home". (*jar udyā pāūs paḍlā, tar mī gharī rāhīn*.) Then "after reading". (*vācalyāvar*.)

## You'll want to know: -ल्यास, 'should it'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C286-LYAS-01]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-LYAAVAR, MR-LEX-C99-NATURE99-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

> **पाऊस पडल्यास आम्ही घरी राहू.**
>
> *pāūs paḍalyās āmhī gharī rāhū.* — "Should it rain, we will stay at home."

The **-ल्-** of **-ल्यावर**, with **-ल्यास**: **पडल्यास** says **जर … पडला तर** in one word. It is the careful, written way, and you will read it on notices more than you will hear it: **उशीर झाल्यास** (*uśīr jhālyās*), "in case of delay".

## You'll want to know: नाहीतर, 'otherwise'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C286-NAHITAR-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C154-ACT154-02, MR-LEX-C111-TIME111-01, MR-LEX-C165-ACT165-01] -->

> **लवकर निघा, नाहीतर गाडी चुकेल.**
>
> *lavkar nighā, nāhītar gāḍī cukel.* — "Leave early, otherwise you'll miss the train."

**नाहीतर** (*nāhītar*) is **नाही** and **तर**: "if not, then". **निघा** is the polite "set out"; **चुकेल** is the future of **चुकणे**, with the train as its subject, as in **माझी गाडी चुकली**.
