## You'll want to know: la ventisca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C700-NIEVE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la ventisca** — "the blizzard, the snowstorm".

> Con la ventisca no se ve nada.
>
> *In the blizzard you cannot see a thing.*

Snow and strong wind together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ventisca* is built on *viento* "wind", which is inherited from Latin *ventus*.
