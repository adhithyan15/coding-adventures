## You'll want to know: ਤਾਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C347-DRUM347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਾਲ** — *tāl* — "rhythm; the beat".

> ਉਹ ਤਾਲ ਨਾਲ ਨੱਚਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *oh tāl nāl nachchdī hai.*
>
> She dances in time with the beat.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਾਲ** (*tāl*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tāla*, "clap, rhythm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tāl*, "beating time with the hands", under it (CDIAL 5748).
