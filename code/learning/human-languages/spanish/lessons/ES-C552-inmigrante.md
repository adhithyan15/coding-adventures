## You'll want to know: el inmigrante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C552-EXILIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el inmigrante** — "the immigrant".

> Muchos inmigrantes trabajan en el campo.
>
> *Many immigrants work on the land.*

The same form for a woman: *la inmigrante*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inmigrante* is built on *inmigrar*, a learned word from Latin *immigrāre* 'to move into' (Lewis & Short), built on *in* and *migrāre* 'to move'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
