## You'll want to know: le sinistré
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C307-DEGATS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le sinistré** — "the disaster victim".

> Les sinistrés ont dormi dans une école.
>
> *The people hit by the disaster slept in a school.*

Also an adjective: *une région sinistrée*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sinistré* is built on *sinistre* "a disaster", from Latin *sinister* "left, unlucky" (Lewis & Short).
