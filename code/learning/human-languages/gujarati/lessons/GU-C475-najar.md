## You'll want to know: નજર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C475-SENSES475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**નજર** — *najar* — "eyesight; a glance".

> ઉંમર સાથે દાદીની નજર નબળી થઈ.
>
> *ummar sāthe dādīnī najar nabḷī thaī.*
>
> With age my grandmother's eyesight has grown weak.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નજર** (*najar*) is usually traced to Arabic *naẓar*, "sight, a look", which reached Gujarati through Persian.
