## You'll want to know: beurteilen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C340-KRITIK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**beurteilen** — "to judge, to assess".

> Das kann ich nicht beurteilen, ich kenne das Buch nicht.
>
> *I cannot judge that; I do not know the book.*

Inseparable: *er beurteilt, er hat beurteilt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*beurteilen* is built on *Urteil* "judgement", inherited from Old High German *urteil* and Proto-Germanic *uzdailiją* "a dealing out" (EtymDB), on the word for "part": a judge deals out to each his part.
