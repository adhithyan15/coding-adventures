## You'll want to know: fūnis, fūnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C437-RUDENS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**fūnis, fūnis** — "a rope, a cord".

> Nāvis fūne ad lītus ligātur.
>
> *The ship is tied to the shore with a rope.*

Third declension, usually masculine: *fūnis, fūnis m.* English *funicular*, a railway pulled by a rope, comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fūnis* is inherited and is usually traced to PIE *bʰendʰ-* 'to bind, to tie' (EtymDB); Lewis & Short also suggest, with a perhaps, the Sanskrit root for binding. English *funicular* comes from its diminutive *fūniculus*.
