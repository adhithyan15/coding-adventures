## You'll want to know: хостел
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C428-NOCHLEG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**хостел** (*khóstel*) — "a hostel".

> Студенты жили в недорогом хостеле.
>
> *Studénty zhíli v nedorogóm khóstele.*
>
> The students stayed in a cheap hostel.

Masculine; the stress is on the first syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хостел* is usually traced to English *hostel*, which EtymDB traces through Old French to Latin *hospitāle*, a place for guests. EtymDB does not list the Russian word.
