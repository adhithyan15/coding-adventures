Gujarati, chapter 5: Four More Forms Before Your Name.
5 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
લ — the consonant la.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — meet one form until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Write retroflex ળ once from memory. Say lla, then put it away. The new letter is the ordinary l sound.

Script — meet one form.
Keep લ visible. It is la. Circle counterclockwise around the broad rounded left body. Lift once and sweep the separate middle shoulder from left to right. Lift again; descend the tall right spine and turn through its lower foot.

Writing — notice and trace.
Finger-trace લ (la) once, then air-trace it once. Notice rounded body, separate shoulder, separate spine; two pen lifts.

Writing — copy with the model.
Copy visible લ once. Compare only the rounded body and separate shoulder; repair one landmark if needed.

Writing — recall after hiding.
Hide the model and write la once. Uncover લ, compare, and stop after one repair.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Hide the model and write ordinary la from memory.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Point to ordinary la in લ ળ. Then point to retroflex lla.

Lesson 5 of 5.
લ — પ — ચ — બ (la — pa — cha — ba): four more forms return.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Keep the four cards visible. Point as you say la, pa, unaspirated cha, and ba. Notice one landmark at a time: rounded body, high hook, middle loop, compact inner turn.
