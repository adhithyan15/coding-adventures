## You'll want to know: کوسن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C534-FURNISH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کوسن** (*kusan*) — "a cushion".

> او کوسنها را روی مبل چید.
>
> *u kusanhâ râ ru-ye mobl chid.*
>
> She arranged the cushions on the sofa.

Plural *kusanhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kusan* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *coussin*, 'cushion'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
