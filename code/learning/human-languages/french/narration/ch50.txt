French, chapter 50: Neck, Shoulder, Knee, Face, Blood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le cou — the neck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a finger, then the French for a tooth.

You'll want to know: le cou.
le cou — "the neck".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: le cou]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: le cou, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say le cou]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the French for a finger, then the French for a tooth, then say le cou again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does le cou mean? ("The neck".) Say it once more.
