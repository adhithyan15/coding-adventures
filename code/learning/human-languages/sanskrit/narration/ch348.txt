Sanskrit, chapter 348: Fair and Unfair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शोषणम् (śoṣaṇam) — exploitation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for makes (someone) understand, then the Sanskrit for what someone means.

You'll want to know: शोषणम्.
शोषणम् — śoṣaṇam — "exploitation".
धनिकः कर्मकरस्य शोषणं करोति। dhanikaḥ karmakarasya śoṣaṇaṃ karoti. The rich man exploits the worker.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
शोषणम् (śoṣaṇam) comes from the root √śuṣ, "to dry", in its causative form, "to make dry"; MW gives śoṣaṇa "drying up, draining, suction", and "exploitation" is the sense modern Sanskrit gives it (MW).

पक्षपातः — pakṣapātaḥ — "partiality; bias".
न्यायाधीशः पक्षपातं त्यजेत्। nyāyādhīśaḥ pakṣapātaṃ tyajet. A judge should put aside partiality.

पक्षपातः (pakṣapātaḥ) is built from pakṣa, "a side", and pāta, "a falling"; MW gives pakṣa-pāta "adopting a side, siding with, partiality" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उल्लंघयति (ullaṅghayati) — breaks (a rule).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a precept, then the Sanskrit for partiality.

You'll want to know: उल्लंघयति.
उल्लंघयति — ullaṅghayati — "breaks (a rule)".
यः नियमम् उल्लंघयति, सः दण्डं प्राप्नोति। yaḥ niyamam ullaṅghayati, saḥ daṇḍaṃ prāpnoti. Whoever breaks a rule is punished.
Past (he …): सः नियमम् उल्लंघितवान्, saḥ niyamam ullaṅghitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उल्लंघयति (ullaṅghayati) is built on ut, "up, over", and the root √laṅgh, "to leap", in its causative form; MW gives the senses "to leap over, overstep" and "to transgress, violate" (MW).
