## You'll want to know: ফোটানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C514-UNUNE514-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ফোটানো** — *phoṭāno* — "to bring to the boil".

> আমি চায়ের জন্য জল ফোটালাম।
>
> *Āmi chāyer jônyô jôl phoṭālām.*
>
> I boiled water for tea.

Past (he/she …): তিনি দুধ ফোটালেন, *tini dudh phoṭālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফোটানো** (*phoṭāno*) is the causative of ফোটা, "to burst open, to boil", from a reconstructed *sphuṭyati* (CDIAL 13845): making the water burst into bubbles.
