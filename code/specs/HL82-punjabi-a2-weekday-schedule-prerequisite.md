# HL82 — Punjabi A2 weekday-schedule prerequisite

## Status

Implemented by issue #17903. This specification owns one prerequisite curriculum tranche discovered by exact taught-content closure on the unpublished Punjabi A2 mock-1 draft.

## Contract

- Chapter 182 stays on `SPINE-READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS` and required A2 extension `PA-EXT-182-A2-WEEKDAY-SCHEDULE`.
- It introduces exactly seven weekday atoms in calendar order: Monday through Sunday, plus source-honest recognition/copying of independent vowel **ਐ** before the Sunday spelling appears.
- Every new atom has one owner and at least two later retrieval lessons.
- Monday through Saturday start with a hear/say bridge before reading. Sunday is learned wholly by ear, **ਐ** is then recognised and copied with no invented stroke order, and only the review reveals the full Sunday spelling. Independent notice writing waits for the payoff.
- The review introduces no atom. The payoff retrieves all seven labels and reuses already taught schedule language in one two-line notice.
- Every lesson remains at or below 300 seconds.

## Boundary

This chapter is the fifth prerequisite tranche exposed by mock drafting. It is not Punjabi A2 mock 1, does not by itself unblock that mock, and makes no readiness, attainment, accreditation, equivalence, recording-validation, or qualified-human-review claim.
