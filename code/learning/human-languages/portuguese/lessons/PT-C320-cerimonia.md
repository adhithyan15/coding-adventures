## You'll want to know: a cerimónia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C320-CERIMONIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a cerimónia** — "the ceremony".

> A cerimónia durou uma hora.
>
> *The ceremony lasted an hour.*

In Brazil: *cerimônia*. *Sem cerimónia* — without fuss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cerimónia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *caerimōnia* "a sacred rite, reverence" (EtymDB).
