## You'll want to know: parar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C641-TAXI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**parar** — "to stop".

> ¿Puede parar aquí, por favor?
>
> *Can you stop here, please?*

*Pararse* is to come to a stop; in Latin America it also means to stand up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parar* is inherited from Latin *parāre* "to make or get ready, to prepare" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
