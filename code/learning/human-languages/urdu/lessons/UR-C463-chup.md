## You'll want to know: چپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C463-TALK463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چپ** — *chup* — "hushed; not speaking".

> استاد کو دیکھ کر سب بچے چپ ہو گئے۔
>
> *ustād ko dekh kar sab bachche chup ho gae.*
>
> When they saw the teacher all the children fell silent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چپ** (*chup*) is inherited from *cuppa*, "silent", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4864).
