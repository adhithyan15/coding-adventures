## Writing

Task 1 must compare rather than serially summarise both sources, distinguish
speed from accountable accuracy, identify the rare-household and appeal evidence,
and produce an independent 180–220-word Malayalam synthesis. Task 2 must sustain
a 300–380-word critical argument across at least two domains, answer a credible
counterargument, state workable correction mechanisms, acknowledge limits, and
reach a qualified conclusion.

## Speaking

Allow keyword notes only. The presentation must integrate both sources rather
than announce a preference. In collaboration require comparison, challenge,
repair, and a conditional joint conclusion. The six follow-ups must elicit
defence, qualification, counterargument, and reformulation without supplying
language. Score the complete recording against the shared criteria.
