German, chapter 159: An address, A phone number, The internet, An email, A message.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Adresse — an address.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a parcel, then the German for a stamp.

You'll want to know: die Adresse.
die Adresse — "an address".

The word, taken apart.
Adresse from French adresse (EtymDB 2.1), from adresser, "to direct" — borrowed, like English address.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Telefonnummer — a phone number.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a stamp, then the German for an address.

You'll want to know: die Telefonnummer.
die Telefonnummer — "a phone number".

The word, taken apart.
Telefonnummer is Telefon + the word for "number", which came from Italian numero from Latin numerus, "number". EtymDB 2.1 has the Latin word.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the internet, then the German for an email.

You'll want to know: die Nachricht.
die Nachricht — "a message, the news".

The word, taken apart.
Nachricht is the word for "after" + a noun from the verb "to direct, to set right", Old High German rihten (EtymDB 2.1): first an instruction to go by, then news.
