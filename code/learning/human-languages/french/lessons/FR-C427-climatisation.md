## You'll want to know: la climatisation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C427-CHAMBRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la climatisation** — "the air conditioning".

> La climatisation fait trop de bruit la nuit.
>
> *The air conditioning makes too much noise at night.*

In speech, *la clim*. *Climatisé* — air-conditioned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*climatisation* is built on *climat* 'climate', from Latin *clima*, a learned word from Greek *klíma* 'slope, region' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists it without a source.
