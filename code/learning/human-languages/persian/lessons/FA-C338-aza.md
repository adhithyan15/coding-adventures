## You'll want to know: عزا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C338-KHATM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عزا** (*'azâ*) — "mourning".

> خانواده یک هفته عزا داشتند.
>
> *Khânevâde yek hafte 'azâ dâshtand.*
>
> The family were in mourning for a week.

*'Azâdâr* "a mourner"; black clothes are worn in *'azâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'azâ* is borrowed from Arabic *'azā'*, "solace, mourning" (EtymDB).
