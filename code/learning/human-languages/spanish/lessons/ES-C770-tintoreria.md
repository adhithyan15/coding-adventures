## You'll want to know: la tintorería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C770-COMERCIOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la tintorería** — "the dry cleaner's".

> Llevo el traje a la tintorería porque no se puede lavar.
>
> *I take the suit to the dry cleaner's because it can't be washed.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tintorería* is built on *tintorero* 'a dyer', from *tinto* 'dyed', which comes from Latin *tīnctus*, the participle of *tingere* 'to wet, to dye' (Lewis & Short). Dry cleaners were first dyers.
