## You'll want to know: こがす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C473-SIMMER473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こがす** — *kogasu* — "to scorch; to burn (food)".

> **なべの ごはんを こがしました。** — *nabe no gohan o kogashimashita* — "I scorched the rice in the pot."

Polite **こがします**, *kogashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが さかなを こがしました。**, *ani ga sakana o kogashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こがす** (*kogasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: the transitive partner of *kogeru*, "to get burnt", and its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
