## You'll want to know: le minibar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C429-AMPOULE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le minibar** — "the minibar".

> Les boissons du minibar sont très chères.
>
> *The drinks in the minibar are very dear.*

Plural *les minibars*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*minibar* is built from *mini-* 'small' and *bar*, which was borrowed from English *bar*. EtymDB does not list it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
