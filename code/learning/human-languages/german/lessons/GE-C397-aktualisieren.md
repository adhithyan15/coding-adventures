## You'll want to know: aktualisieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C397-ABSTURZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aktualisieren** — "to update".

> Das Handy aktualisiert die Apps in der Nacht.
>
> *The phone updates the apps during the night.*

Perfekt: *hat aktualisiert*. From *aktuell* — current, up to date; a false friend, not actual.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aktualisieren* is built on *aktuell* "current", from Late Latin *āctuālis* "active, practical" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *āctus* "a doing", of the family of *agere* "to do, to drive".
