## You'll want to know: どようび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C135-DOYOUBI]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-DO-01, JA-SCRIPT-YO-01, JA-SCRIPT-U-01] -->

**どようび** — *doyōbi* — "Saturday".

The new sign is spent at once. **ど**, **よ** and **う**, then **び**: four
beats, *do–yo–o–bi*. The **う** stretches the *o*, as it does in **きのう**. The
last part, *yōbi*, means a day of the week, and the name of every day ends in
it. You write every sign in it now.
