## You'll want to know: egestās, egestātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C422-DIVITIAE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**egestās, egestātis** — "want, extreme poverty".

> In summā egestāte vīvēbat.
>
> *He lived in the deepest poverty.*

*egestās, egestātis* f., built on *egeō*, to be in need.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*egestās* is built on *egeō* "to be in need" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tās* that makes a state.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
