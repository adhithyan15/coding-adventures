## You'll want to know: ròu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C70-FOOD70-01]; assesses=[] -->

*ròu* — "meat".

> *wǒ bù chī ròu* — I don't eat meat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ròu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *nyuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed with the pair 'bone and flesh'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
