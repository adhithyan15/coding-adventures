## You'll want to know: ઘોળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C538-SANSAY538-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘોળવું** — *ghoḷvũ* — "to mull over".

> જો તમે આ વાત બે દિવસ ઘોળશો તો તમને જવાબ મળશે.
>
> *jo tame ā vāt be divas ghoḷśo to tamne javāb maḷśe.*
>
> If you mull this over for two days, you'll find the answer.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વાત ઘોળી, *teṇe vāt ghoḷī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘોળવું** (*ghoḷvũ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *ghōlayati*, "stirs, mixes", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to shake about; to ponder on" (CDIAL 4526): a thought stirred round and round.
