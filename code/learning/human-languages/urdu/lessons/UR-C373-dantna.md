# ڈانٹنا (ḍānṭnā) — to scold; to tell off

## You'll want to know: ڈانٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C373-BIKHARNA373-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈانٹنا** — *ḍānṭnā* — "to scold; to tell off".

> اگر تم دیر سے آئے تو ابو ڈانٹیں گے۔
>
> *agar tum der se āe to abbū ḍānṭeṅ ge.*
>
> If you are late, your father will tell you off.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو ڈانٹا, *us ne bachche ko ḍānṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈانٹنا** (*ḍānṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍā̃ṭnā*, "to threaten, to check", under *\*draṭ*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6618).
