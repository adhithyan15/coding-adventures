## You'll want to know: persuadir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C337-ARGUMENTAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**persuadir** — "to persuade".

> Tentei persuadir o meu pai a comprar um carro mais pequeno.
>
> *I tried to persuade my father to buy a smaller car.*

*Persuadir alguém a fazer algo* — to persuade someone to do something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*persuadir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *persuādēre* "to convince, to persuade" (EtymDB), built from *per-* and *suādēre* "to advise, to urge" (Lewis & Short).
