## You'll want to know: फ़ैसला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C352-ADALAT352-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़ैसला** — *faislā* — "a verdict, a decision".

> अदालत का फ़ैसला कल आएगा।
>
> *adālat kā faislā kal āegā.*
>
> The court's verdict will come tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़ैसला** (*faislā*) is usually traced to Arabic *faiṣala*, "a decision", which reached Hindi through Persian.
