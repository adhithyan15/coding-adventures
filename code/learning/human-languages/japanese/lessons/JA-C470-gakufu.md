## You'll want to know: がくふ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C470-SONG470-02]; assesses=[] -->

**がくふ** — *gakufu* — "sheet music; a score".

> **がくふを よんで、うたいました。** — *gakufu o yonde, utaimashita* — "I read the sheet music and sang."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がくふ** (*gakufu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gaku*, "music" (Middle Chinese *ngaewk*) and *fu*, "musical score" (*puX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
