# ಊರು (ūru) — town, home town

## You'll want to know: ಊರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C91-ABOUTYOU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಊರು** (*ūru*) — "town", and in particular one's **home town**. It is the *-ur*
at the end of place names across the south: Bengaluru, Mysuru.

**ನಿಮ್ಮ ಊರು ಯಾವುದು?** (*nimma ūru yāvudu?*) — "where is your home town?"

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The word hiding in every -uru city name.
