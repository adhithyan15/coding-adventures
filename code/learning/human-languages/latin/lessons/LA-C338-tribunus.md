## You'll want to know: tribūnus, tribūnī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C338-LEGIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tribūnus, tribūnī** — "a tribune".

> Tribūnus mīlitum ad castra advēnit.
>
> *The military tribune arrived at the camp.*

Masculine. *Tribūnus mīlitum*: an officer of a legion; the people's tribunes protected ordinary citizens.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**tribūnus** is built on *tribus*, "tribe" (EtymDB): first the head of a tribe. English *tribune* comes from it.
