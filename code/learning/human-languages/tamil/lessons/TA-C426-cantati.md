## You'll want to know: சந்தடி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C426-SANTHATI426-01]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்தடி** — *cantaṭi* — "bustle; commotion".

> காலையில் சந்தையில் ஒரே சந்தடி.
>
> *kālaiyil cantaiyil orē cantaṭi.*
>
> In the morning there is nothing but bustle in the market.

சந்தடி இல்லாமல் means quietly, without fuss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்தடி** (*cantaṭi*), "bustle, noise of a crowd", is a Tamil word whose origin is not settled.
