## You'll want to know: पुतळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C278-KAHANI278-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पुतळी** — *putḷī* — "a doll".

> म्हारी पुतळी नुवी है।
>
> *mhārī putḷī nuvī hai.*
>
> My doll is new.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुतळी** (*putḷī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*putrala*, "puppet"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *pūtaḷī*, "image", and Hindi *putlī*, "doll" (CDIAL 8269).
