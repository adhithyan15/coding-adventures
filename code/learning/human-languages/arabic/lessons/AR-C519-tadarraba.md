## You'll want to know: تدرب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C519-WAZIFA519-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تدرب** — *tadarraba* — "to practise".

> تدرب الفريق في الملعب كل مساء.
>
> *tadarraba al-farīq fī al-malʿab kull masāʾ.*
>
> The team practised at the stadium every evening.

Present: يتدرب, *yatadarrab*.

It takes على for the skill: تدرب على السباحة.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تدرب** (*tadarraba*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-r-b*, "to be used to, to be practised in" (Lane). Form V tadarraba is to get oneself trained.
