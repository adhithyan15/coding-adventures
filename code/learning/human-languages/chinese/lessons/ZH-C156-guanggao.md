## You'll want to know: guǎnggào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C156-GUANGGAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

*guǎnggào* — "advert; advertisement".

> *zhè shì shǒujī de guǎnggào* — This is an advert for a phone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guǎnggào* is built from guǎng 'wide' and gào 'to tell'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwangX* and *kawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'broad' and 'to report': telling widely. 'An advert' is the pair's modern sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-DE-01] -->

的 joins the thing to the advert: *shǒujī de guǎnggào*, an advert for a phone.

An advert often has a price and a day on it, and little else.
