## You'll want to know: प्रतिशृणोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C361-SHRADDHA361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिशृणोति** — *pratiśṛṇoti* — "gives one's word; promises".

> मीरा साहाय्यं प्रतिशृणोति।
>
> *mīrā sāhāyyaṃ pratiśṛṇoti.*
>
> Mira gives her word that she will help.

Past (he …): सः साहाय्यं प्रतिश्रुतवान्, *saḥ sāhāyyaṃ pratiśrutavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिशृणोति** (*pratiśṛṇoti*) is built on *prati*, "towards", and the root √śru, "to hear"; MW gives *prati-√śru* "to assure, to agree, to promise" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
