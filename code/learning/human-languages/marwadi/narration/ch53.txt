Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 53: To play, To sell, To give, To take, To keep.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खेलणो (khelṇo) — to play.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to walk, then the Marwadi for to run.

You'll want to know: खेलणो.
खेलणो — khelṇo — "to play".

The word, taken apart.
खेलणो (khelṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khel, "play", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari khelaï under it (CDIAL 3918). CDIAL heads a group of reconstructed words for "play" khēḍ, and says that if Sanskrit krīḍati, "plays", is of non-Aryan origin, these may share its source.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to run, then the Marwadi for to play.

You'll want to know: बेचणो.
बेचणो — bechṇo — "to sell".

The word, taken apart.
बेचणो (bechṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vetyayati, "makes a price", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 12100); the match is through Hindi becnā and Gujarati vecvũ. CDIAL builds it on Middle Indo-Aryan vēcā, "hire, wages".
