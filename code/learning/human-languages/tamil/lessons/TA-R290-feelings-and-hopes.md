# Review — feelings and hopes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C290-ACAI-01, TA-LEX-C290-ILAKKU-01, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a wish" and "a goal". Then read **ஈரம்** aloud. (*īram*.)
- [YOU WRITE: **ஆசை** and **இலக்கு**, once each]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C290-FEELING-DATIVE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C290-FEELING-STATE-01, TA-LEX-C290-ACAI-01, TA-LEX-C290-ILAKKU-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C290-WISH-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C290-BECOME-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C289-MISSED-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-BECAUSE-DID-01, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I missed the bus. I got angry.]
- [YOU SAY: When my friend came, I was happy.]
- [YOU SAY: I long to swim in the sea one day.]
- [YOU SAY: My goal is to speak Tamil well.]
- [YOU SAY: one feeling from last week, and why you felt it]
