# মচকানো (môchkāno) — to sprain; to twist (a joint)

## You'll want to know: মচকানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C475-KHOTO475-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মচকানো** — *môchkāno* — "to sprain; to twist (a joint)".

> সিঁড়িতে মীরার গোড়ালি মচকাল।
>
> *sĩṛite mīrār goṛāli môchkālô.*
>
> Mira twisted her ankle on the stairs.

Past (he/she …): সেই খেলোয়াড়ের হাঁটু মচকাল, *sei kheloyāṛer hā̃ṭu môchkālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মচকানো** (*môchkāno*) is a Bengali verb for twisting a joint whose origin is not settled.
