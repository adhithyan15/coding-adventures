Kannada, chapter 479: Grassland and Swamp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಸ್ಯ (sasya) — vegetation; a plant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a crab, then the Kannada for a whale.

You'll want to know: ಸಸ್ಯ.
ಸಸ್ಯ — sasya — "vegetation; a plant".
ಈ ಬೆಟ್ಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಪರೂಪದ ಸಸ್ಯಗಳು ಬೆಳೆಯುತ್ತವೆ. ī beṭṭadalli aparūpada sasyagaḷu beḷeyuttave. Rare plants grow on this hill.
Plural: ಸಸ್ಯಗಳು, sasyagaḷu.
A word of science and botany; ಗಿಡ is the everyday plant.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಸ್ಯ (sasya) is borrowed from Sanskrit sasya, "grain, fruit, crops" (CDIAL 13295); Kannada uses it for plants in general.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪೊದೆ (pode) — a bush; a thicket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a whale, then the Kannada for vegetation.

You'll want to know: ಪೊದೆ.
ಪೊದೆ — pode — "a bush; a thicket".
ಮೊಲ ಪೊದೆಯೊಳಗೆ ಓಡಿತು. mola podeyoḷage ōḍitu. The rabbit ran into the bush.
Low, thick growth where small animals hide.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೊದೆ (pode) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4509 lists Kannada pode, "a thatch, a bush, a bundle", beside podaṟ, "a thicket".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲು (hullugāvalu) — a grassland; a pasture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for vegetation, then the Kannada for a bush.

You'll want to know: ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲು.
ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲು — hullugāvalu — "a grassland; a pasture".
ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಜಿಂಕೆಗಳು ಮೇಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು. hullugāvalinalli jiṅkegaḷu mēyuttiddavu. Deer were grazing on the grassland.
ಹುಲ್ಲು, grass, plus ಕಾವಲು, guarded land.

The word, taken apart.
ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲು (hullugāvalu) is a native Kannada compound of ಹುಲ್ಲು, "grass" (DEDR 4300), and kāvalu, "guarded land, a reserve", built on kā, "to guard" (DEDR 1416).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಚಿಗುರು (ciguru) — to sprout; to put out shoots.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a bush, then the Kannada for a grassland.

You'll want to know: ಚಿಗುರು.
ಚಿಗುರು — ciguru — "to sprout; to put out shoots".
ಮೊದಲ ಮಳೆಯ ನಂತರ ಮರಗಳು ಚಿಗುರಿದವು. modala maḷeya nantara maragaḷu ciguridavu. After the first rain the trees put out new shoots.
Past (it …): ಚಿಗುರಿತು, ciguritu.
Also a noun, a tender shoot: ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು (ciguru).

The word, taken apart.
ಚಿಗುರು (ciguru) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2489 lists Kannada igaru, cigur, cigaru, "a sprout, a young leaf", and cigi, "to sprout".
