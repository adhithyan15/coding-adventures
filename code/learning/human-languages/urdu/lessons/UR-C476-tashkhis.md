## You'll want to know: تشخیص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C476-DOCTOR476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تشخیص** — *tashkhīs* — "a diagnosis".

> ڈاکٹر کی تشخیص بالکل ٹھیک تھی۔
>
> *ḍākṭar kī tashkhīs bilkul ṭhīk thī.*
>
> The doctor's diagnosis was quite right.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تشخیص** (*tashkhīs*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tashkhīṣ*, "telling one thing from another".
