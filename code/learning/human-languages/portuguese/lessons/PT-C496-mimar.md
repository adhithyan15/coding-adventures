## You'll want to know: mimar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C496-CARINHO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mimar** — "to spoil, to pamper".

> Os avós mimam os netos com doces.
>
> *The grandparents spoil their grandchildren with sweets.*

*Mimado* — spoiled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mimar* is built on *mimo* "a treat, a caress", whose origin is not settled.
