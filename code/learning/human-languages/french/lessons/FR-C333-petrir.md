## You'll want to know: pétrir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C333-PETRIR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**pétrir** — "to knead".

> Le boulanger pétrit la pâte pendant dix minutes.
>
> *The baker kneads the dough for ten minutes.*

Regular, like *finir*: *il pétrit*, *nous pétrissons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pétrir* is usually traced to Late Latin *pistrīre* "to knead", built on *pistor* "a miller, a baker" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent.
