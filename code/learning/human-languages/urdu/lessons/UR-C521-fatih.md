## You'll want to know: فاتح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C521-MAICH521-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فاتح** — *fātih* — "a winner; a conqueror".

> آخر میں ہماری ٹیم فاتح رہی۔
>
> *ākhir meṅ hamārī ṭīm fātih rahī.*
>
> In the end our team came out the winner.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فاتح** (*fātih*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fātiḥ*, "one who opens; a conqueror".
