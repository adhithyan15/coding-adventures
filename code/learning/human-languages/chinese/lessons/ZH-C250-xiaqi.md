## You'll want to know: xiàqí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C250-HOBBY250-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàqí* — "to play chess".

> *wǒmen xiàwǔ yìqǐ xiàqí* — We're playing chess together this afternoon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàqí* is built from 下 'to go down' and *qí* 'a board game', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *gi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to descend' and lists the second under another spelling of the character, quoting an older book on who first made the board game weiqi.
