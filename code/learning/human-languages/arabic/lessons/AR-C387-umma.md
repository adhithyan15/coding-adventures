## You'll want to know: أمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C387-UMMA387-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أمة** — *umma* — "a nation".

> تحتفل الأمة بعيدها الوطني.
>
> *taḥtafilu al-umma bi-ʿīdihā al-waṭanī.*
>
> The nation is celebrating its national day.

Plural: أمم, *umam*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أمة** (*umma*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-m-m*, "to aim at, to head for" (Lane). An *umma* is a people that head the same way: a nation. أم, mother, is from the same root.
