# Have or be — age in six languages

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-ETA-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say how old you are in Italian: **Ho … anni.** Which verb did you use?

## Grammar Lens: who has and who is
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-GRAMMAR-ETA-05]; assesses=[] -->

| language | |
|---|---|
| Italian | *h**o** venti anni* — **have** |
| French | *j'**ai** vingt ans* — **have** |
| Spanish | *t**engo** veinte años* — **have** |
| Portuguese | *t**enho** vinte anos* — **have** |
| German | *ich **bin** zwanzig Jahre alt* — **be** |
| English | *I **am** twenty* — **be** |

**Romance has its years; Germanic is its years.**

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-ETA-05, IT-LEX-AVERE-02] -->

- [YOU SAY: "Ho venti anni" — and notice the **have**]
- [YOU SAY: the English "I am twenty" beside it — and notice the **be**]
