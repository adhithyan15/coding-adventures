## You'll want to know: kāfēi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C55-LIKE55-05]; assesses=[] -->

*kāfēi* — "coffee".

> *nǐ hē kāfēi ma* — Do you drink coffee?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kāfēi* is borrowed from a European word for coffee; it is usually traced to English *coffee*, though the path is not documented in what we can open.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
