## HL-C346 — Hindi's introduction formula declares every practised word

After the time-frame repair, **आपसे** is the highest-ranked modern Hindi form
in the glossed-but-never-taught queue, and **मिलकर** is another undeclared part
of the same Chapter 2 formula. The lesson explicitly translates, rehearses, and
recalls **आपसे मिलकर ख़ुशी हुई**, but its headword declares only the noun
**ख़ुशी**.

Declare the complete introduction formula alongside its key noun. This keeps
the book's phrase-first treatment intact while making every practised word in
"pleased to meet you" visible to reports and downstream study artifacts.
