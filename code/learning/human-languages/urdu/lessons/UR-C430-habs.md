## You'll want to know: حبس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C430-PARA430-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حبس** — *habs* — "airless heat; sultriness".

> بارش سے پہلے بڑا حبس تھا۔
>
> *bārish se pehle baṛā habs thā.*
>
> Before the rain it was very close and airless.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حبس** (*habs*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥabs*, "shutting in, confinement"; the sense "airless heat" probably grew in Urdu.
