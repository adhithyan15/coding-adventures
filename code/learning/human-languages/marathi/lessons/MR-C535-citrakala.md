## You'll want to know: चित्रकला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C535-CHITRA535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चित्रकला** — *citrakalā* — "painting as an art".

> मीराला लहानपणापासून चित्रकलेची आवड आहे.
>
> *mīrālā lahānpaṇāpāsūn citrakalecī āvaḍ āhe.*
>
> Meera has loved painting since childhood.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चित्रकला** (*citrakalā*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *citra*, "picture", and *kalā*, "art" (Monier-Williams).
