Russian, chapter 304: Stages of life.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
детство (détstvo) — childhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for pregnant, then the Russian for to give birth.

You'll want to know: детство.
детство (détstvo) — "childhood".
Детство я провёл в деревне. Détstvo ya provyól v derévne. I spent my childhood in the countryside.
Neuter. С детства — since childhood.

The word, taken apart.
детство (détstvo) is built on дети "children", from Proto-Slavic dětę "a child", which EtymDB traces to PIE dʰeh₁(y)- "to suckle".

Lesson 2 of 5.
юность (yúnost) — youth, the teenage years.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to give birth, then the Russian for childhood.

You'll want to know: юность.
юность (yúnost) — "youth, the teenage years".
В юности он играл на гитаре. V yúnosti on igrál na gitáre. In his youth he played the guitar.
Feminine. Roughly the years from fifteen to twenty.

The word, taken apart.
юность (yúnost) is built on юный "young", which EtymDB marks as a borrowing from Church Slavonic junŭ, from Proto-Slavic junŭ and PIE h₂yuh₁en- "young"; English young is a cousin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
молодость (mólodost) — one's young years, youth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for childhood, then the Russian for youth.

You'll want to know: молодость.
молодость (mólodost) — "one's young years, youth".
В молодости бабушка много путешествовала. V mólodosti bábushka mnógo puteshéstvovala. In her youth Grandma travelled a lot.
Feminine. Молодость reaches into the twenties and thirties; юность (yúnost) is the teens.

The word, taken apart.
молодость (mólodost) is built on молодой "young" and the ending -ость (EtymDB); молодой is inherited from Proto-Slavic moldŭ "young".

Lesson 5 of 5.
взрослеть (vzroslét) — to grow up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for one's young years, then the Russian for old age.

You'll want to know: взрослеть.
взрослеть (vzroslét) — "to grow up".
Дети быстро взрослеют. Déti býstro vzrosléyut. Children grow up fast.
Imperfective; perfective повзрослеть (povzroslét). Взрослый — an adult.

The word, taken apart.
взрослеть (vzroslét) is built on взрослый "adult", from вз- "up" and the root рос- of расти "to grow": grown up. EtymDB lists no parent.
