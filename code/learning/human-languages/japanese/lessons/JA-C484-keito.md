## You'll want to know: けいと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C484-THREAD484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**けいと** — *keito* — "knitting wool; yarn".

> **けいとで てぶくろを あみます。** — *keito de tebukuro o amimasu* — "I knit gloves from wool."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けいと** (*keito*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *ke*, "hair" and *ito*, "thread" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
