## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-FOTOGRAFIA]; assesses=[] -->

**la fotografía** means both “photography” and “a photograph.” Its international
parts come from Greek **phôs, phōtós**, “light,” and **graphía**, “writing or
description.” A photograph is, quite literally, writing with light.

Spanish commonly says **hacer fotografías**, “to take photographs.” The verb is
the familiar **hacer**: Spanish pictures the activity as making photographs.
