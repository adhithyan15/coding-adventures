# تجسس (tajassasa) — to spy

## You'll want to know: تجسس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C346-JASUS346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تجسس** — *tajassasa* — "to spy".

> تجسس الرجل على جيرانه.
>
> *tajassasa ar-rajul ʿalā jīrānihi.*
>
> The man spied on his neighbours.

Present: يتجسس, *yatajassas*.

A spy is جاسوس.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تجسس** (*tajassasa*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-s-s* (Lane), "to feel with the hand, to search out news". It is Form V; a spy is *jāsūs*.
