## You'll want to know: مصرف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C388-ALUDEGI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مصرف** (*masraf*) — "consumption, use".

> مصرف برق در تابستان بالا میرود.
>
> *masraf-e barq dar tâbestân bâlâ miravad.*
>
> Electricity use goes up in summer.

Also the dosage of a medicine: *tarz-e masraf*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصرف** was borrowed from Arabic *maṣraf* (EtymDB), a word usually glossed as a place or way of spending; in Persian it is consumption.
