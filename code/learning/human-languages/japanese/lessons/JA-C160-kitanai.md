## You'll want to know: きたない
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C160-DIRT160-05]; assesses=[] -->

**きたない** — *kitanai* — "dirty; messy".

> **この へやは きたないです。** — *kono heya wa kitanai desu* — "This room is dirty."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きたない** (*kitanai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
