## You'll want to know: بدتر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C377-BADTAR377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بدتر** — *badtar* — "worse".

> دوا لیجیے، ورنہ حالت بدتر ہو گی۔
>
> *davā lījiye, varnā hālat badtar ho gī.*
>
> Take the medicine, or you will get worse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بدتر** (*badtar*) is usually traced to Persian *badtar*, "worse": *bad*, "bad", with *-tar*, "more", the ending that also makes **بہتر**, "better".
