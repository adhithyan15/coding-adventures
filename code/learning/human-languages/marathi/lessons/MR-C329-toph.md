## You'll want to know: तोफ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C329-SHASTRA329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तोफ** — *toph* — "a cannon".

> किल्ल्याच्या दारात एक मोठी तोफ आहे.
>
> *killyācyā dārāt ek moṭhī toph āhe.*
>
> There is a big cannon at the fort's gate.

Feminine.

Plural तोफा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तोफ** (*toph*) is a loan from Persian *top*, 'a cannon', usually traced to Turkish.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
