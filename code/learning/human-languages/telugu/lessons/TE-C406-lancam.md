## You'll want to know: లంచం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C406-AVINITI406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**లంచం** — *lañcaṁ* — "a bribe".

> ఆ అధికారి లంచం అడిగాడు.
>
> *ā adhikāri lañcaṁ aḍigāḍu.*
>
> That officer asked for a bribe.

Plural: లంచాలు, *lañcālu*.

లంచం ఇచ్చు, to give a bribe; లంచం తీసుకో, to take one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లంచం** (*lañcaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *lañcā*, "a present, a bribe" (CDIAL 10914).
