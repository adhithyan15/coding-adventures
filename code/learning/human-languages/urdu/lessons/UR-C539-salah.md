## You'll want to know: صلاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C539-MUKHTAR539-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صلاح** — *salāh* — "advice".

> اگر آپ میری صلاح سنتے تو یہ مشکل نہ ہوتی۔
>
> *agar āp merī salāh sunte to yih mushkil na hotī.*
>
> If you had listened to my advice, this trouble wouldn't have happened.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صلاح** (*salāh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣalāḥ*, "soundness, what is right"; the sense "advice" came later, and the sources we use do not show how it arose.
