## You'll want to know: faenum, faenī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C434-STABULUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**faenum, faenī** — "hay".

> Aestāte agricolae faenum in prātīs colligunt.
>
> *In summer the farmers gather hay in the meadows.*

Second declension, neuter: *faenum, faenī n.*, also spelled *fēnum*. English *fennel* comes from *faeniculum* 'little hay'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*faenum* is inherited; EtymDB traces it to PIE *dʰeh₁(y)-no-*, and Lewis & Short tie it to the root of *fēmina* and *fēlīx*. English *fennel* comes from its diminutive *faeniculum* (Lewis & Short).
