## You'll want to know: സത്യം ചെയ്യുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C370-MOZHI370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**സത്യം ചെയ്യുക** — *satyaṁ ceyyuka* — "to swear an oath".

> സാക്ഷി കോടതിയിൽ സത്യം ചെയ്തു.
>
> *sākṣi kōṭatiyil satyaṁ ceytu.*
>
> The witness swore an oath in court.

Past: സത്യം ചെയ്തു, *satyaṁ ceytu*.

More than telling the truth: promising by oath that you are. A new verb, though both parts are known.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സത്യം ചെയ്യുക** (*satyaṁ ceyyuka*) joins സത്യം (*satyaṁ*), borrowed from Sanskrit *satya*, "true", and also "a promise, an oath" (CDIAL 13112), and ചെയ്യുക, "to do": to make an oath.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
