Urdu, chapter 507: Making Rotis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چکلا (chaklā) — a board for rolling dough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be washed, then the Urdu for to rinse out.

You'll want to know: چکلا.
چکلا — chaklā — "a board for rolling dough".
چکلا باورچی خانے میں میز پر ہے۔ chaklā bāvarchī khāne meṅ mez par hai. The rolling board is on the table in the kitchen.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
چکلا (chaklā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin caklā, "round and flat", under cakrala, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4548).

Lesson 2 of 5.
گوندھنا (gūṅdhnā) — to knead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to rinse out, then the Urdu for a board for rolling dough.

You'll want to know: گوندھنا.
گوندھنا — gūṅdhnā — "to knead".
امی صبح آٹا گوندھتی ہیں۔ ammī subah āṭā gūṅdhtī haiṅ. Mum kneads the dough in the morning.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے آٹا گوندھا, us ne āṭā gūṅdhā.

The word, taken apart.
گوندھنا (gūṅdhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin gū̃dhnā, "to plait, to mix, to knead", under Sanskrit guphati, "strings together" (CDIAL 4205).
