Marathi, chapter 445: Blossom and Fall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अंकुर (aṅkur) — a sprout; a shoot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to seep, then the Marathi for to overflow.

You'll want to know: अंकुर.
अंकुर — aṅkur — "a sprout; a shoot".
बीमधून हिरवा अंकुर बाहेर आला. bīmadhūn hirvā aṅkur bāher ālā. A green shoot came out of the seed.
Masculine.
अंकुर (aṅkur) फुटणे, a shoot comes up.

The word, taken apart.
अंकुर (aṅkur) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit aṅkura, "a sprout, a shoot" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
रुजणे (rujṇe) — to take root.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to overflow, then the Marathi for a sprout.

You'll want to know: रुजणे.
रुजणे — rujṇe — "to take root".
पावसानंतर रोपं लवकर रुजली. pāvsānantar ropaṁ lavkar rujlī. After the rain the saplings took root quickly.
Past (he …): तो रुजला, to rujlā.
Also for ideas and habits: सवय रुजली.

The word, taken apart.
रुजणे (rujṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ruj̈ṇẽ, "to sprout, grow", under ruhyati, "grows" (CDIAL 10797).

Lesson 4 of 5.
बहरणे (baharṇe) — to blossom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to take root, then the Marathi for to bloom.

You'll want to know: बहरणे.
बहरणे — baharṇe — "to blossom".
आंब्याचं झाड फुलांनी बहरलं. āmbyācaṁ jhāḍ phulānnī baharlaṁ. The mango tree blossomed all over.
Past (he …): तो बहरला, to baharlā.
A whole tree in flower बहरतो; the noun is बहर.

The word, taken apart.
बहरणे (baharṇe) is built on बहर, "full bloom", a loan usually traced to Persian bahār, "spring".
