## You'll want to know: o atraso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C416-ATRASOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o atraso** — "the delay".

> O comboio tem um atraso de vinte minutos.
>
> *The train is twenty minutes late.*

*Estar atrasado* — to be late. *Com atraso* — late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atraso* is built on *atrasar*, which is built on *atrás* 'behind' (from *a* and *trás*) with *-ar* (EtymDB); *trás* goes back to Latin *trāns* 'across, beyond'.
