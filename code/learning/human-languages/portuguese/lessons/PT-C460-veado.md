## You'll want to know: o veado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C460-SELVAGENS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o veado** — "the deer".

> Vimos um veado à beira da estrada.
>
> *We saw a deer at the side of the road.*

*O cervo* is the red deer; *veado* covers most deer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*veado* is inherited through Old Portuguese *veado* (EtymDB) from Latin *vēnātus* 'hunting, game' (Lewis & Short): the animal people hunted.
