# ഈ (ī) — the letter that opens ഈയിടെ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-140, ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-138] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **ഇ** and **ീ** once each, and name them. (Short *i* at the
start of a word; long *ī* after a consonant, as in **വീട്**.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ഈയിടെ** (*īyiṭe*), "recently". You met it a moment ago. This lesson takes
its first letter out and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ഈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C340-II-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C340-IYITE-01] -->

**ഈ** — *ī*, a long *ī*, at the start of a word.

After a consonant, long *ī* is the sign **ീ**: **വീട്**, *vīṭŭ*. At the start of a word there is
no consonant to carry a sign, so the vowel stands as a letter of its own, **ഈ**.

It is **ഇ** with a tail added on the right: a small lobe, a notch, and a broader lobe that sweeps
back along its foot. You have read the same tail on **ഊ**, which is **ഉ** with that tail.

On its own, **ഈ** is also a word: "this", in front of a noun. **ഈ വീട്** is "this house".

## Writing: ഈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C340-II-01] -->

One recorded hand writes **ഈ** in two strokes. First the whole of **ഇ**: from the inner tip of
the small spiral, round it, up to the stem, over the broad right lobe, round the bottom, a curl
below the line, and the long baseline to the right. Then, after one lift, the tail: over its
small left lobe, down into the notch, over the broader right lobe, and back along its foot.

Write **ഈ** yourself, slowly, and larger than it is printed. Copy the printed shape, and keep the
order above.
