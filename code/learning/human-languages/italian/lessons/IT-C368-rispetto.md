## You'll want to know: il rispetto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C368-CONVIVENZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il rispetto** — "the respect".

> Bisogna parlare agli anziani con rispetto.
>
> *You should speak to old people with respect.*

*Rispetto a* — compared with: *rispetto a ieri fa più caldo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rispetto* comes from Latin *respectus* "a looking back, regard" (Lewis & Short), from *respicere* "to look back at", built on *specere* "to look": you look again at someone.
