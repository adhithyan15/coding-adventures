## You'll want to know: ઇનકાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C345-KHULASO345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇનકાર** — *inkār* — "a refusal".

> દુકાનદારે પૈસા પાછા આપવાનો ઇનકાર કર્યો.
>
> *dukāndāre paisā pāchā āpvāno inkār karyo.*
>
> The shopkeeper refused to give the money back.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇનકાર** (*inkār*) is usually traced to Arabic *inkār*, "denial", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
