## You'll want to know: بسکتبال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C514-BALL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بسکتبال** (*basketbâl*) — "basketball".

> پسرم قد بلندی دارد و بسکتبال بازی میکند.
>
> *pesaram qadd-e bolandi dârad va basketbâl bâzi mikonad.*
>
> My son is tall and plays basketball.

*basketbâl bâzi kardan* — to play basketball.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*basketbâl* is a modern loan, usually traced to English *basketball*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
