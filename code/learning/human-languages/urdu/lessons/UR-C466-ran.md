## You'll want to know: ران
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C466-LIMB466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ران** — *rān* — "a thigh".

> گرنے سے اس کی ران پر چوٹ آئی۔
>
> *girne se us kī rān par choṭ āī.*
>
> He hurt his thigh when he fell.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ران** (*rān*) is usually traced to Persian *rān*, "thigh".
