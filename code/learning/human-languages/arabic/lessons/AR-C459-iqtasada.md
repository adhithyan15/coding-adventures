## You'll want to know: اقتصد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C459-MIZANIYYA459-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتصد** — *iqtaṣada* — "to economise; to save".

> اقتصدنا في المصاريف هذا الشهر.
>
> *iqtaṣadnā fī al-maṣārīf hādhā ash-shahr.*
>
> We economised on expenses this month.

Present: يقتصد, *yaqtaṣid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتصد** (*iqtaṣada*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ṣ-d*, "to aim at; to keep to the middle" (Lane). It is Form VIII, "to be moderate".
