Tamil, chapter 102: Elders and In-Laws.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பெரியப்பா (periyappā) — the father's elder brother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an aunt, then the Tamil for the father's younger brother.

You'll want to know: பெரியப்பா.
பெரியப்பா (periyappā) — your father's elder brother, the big father.
பெரிய is "big". Beside சித்தப்பா, the little father.

What you've built.
Big father and little father.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: periyappā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: periyappā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say eṉ periyappā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for an aunt, then the Tamil for the father's younger brother, then say periyappā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does பெரியப்பா (periyappā) mean? ("The father's elder brother".) Say it once more.
