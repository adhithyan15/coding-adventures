## You'll want to know: juédìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C184-HOLIDAY184-02]; assesses=[] -->

*juédìng* — "to decide; a decision".

> *wǒmen hái méi juédìng qù nǎr* — We haven't decided where to go yet.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juédìng* is built from *jué* and *dìng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwet* and *dengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to cut off' and 'to break', and the second 'settled': to cut off and settle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
