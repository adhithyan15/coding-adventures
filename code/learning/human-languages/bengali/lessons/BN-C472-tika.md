## You'll want to know: টিকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C472-OSHUDH472-04]; assesses=[] -->

**টিকা** — *ṭikā* — "a vaccine; a vaccination".

> নার্সটি শিশুদের টিকা দিলেন।
>
> *nārsṭi shishuder ṭikā dilen.*
>
> The nurse vaccinated the children.

টিকা দেওয়া, to vaccinate; টিকা নেওয়া, to be vaccinated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টিকা** (*ṭikā*) is *ṭikā*, "a mark", which CDIAL lists for Bengali under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 5458); the vaccination sense probably comes from the mark it leaves on the skin.
