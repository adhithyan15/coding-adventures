## You'll want to know: मुकुट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C320-SAMRAT320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुकुट** — *mukuṭ* — "a crown".

> रानी ने मुकुट पहना।
>
> *rānī ne mukuṭ pahnā.*
>
> The queen put on her crown.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुकुट** (*mukuṭ*) is Sanskrit *mukuṭa*, "a crown, a diadem" (Monier-Williams). You **पहनना** a crown as you wear clothes.
