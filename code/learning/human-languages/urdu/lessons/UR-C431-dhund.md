## You'll want to know: دھند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C431-ABR431-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دھند** — *dhund* — "mist; haze".

> صبح دریا پر دھند تھی۔
>
> *subah daryā par dhund thī.*
>
> There was mist on the river in the morning.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دھند** (*dhund*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *dhundh*, "haze", under *dhūmāndha*, "dim-sighted", built from words for smoke and blindness (CDIAL 6858).
