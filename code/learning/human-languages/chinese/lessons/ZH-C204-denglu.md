## You'll want to know: dēnglù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C204-ONLINE204-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dēnglù* — "to log in".

> *nǐ dēnglù le ma* — Have you logged in?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēnglù* is built from *dēng* 'to go up' and *lù* 'to record', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tong* and *ljowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to climb' and the second 'to take down'. Logging in is a modern sense, and how the pair came to it is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
