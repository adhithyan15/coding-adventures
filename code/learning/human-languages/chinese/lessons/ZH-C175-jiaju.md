## You'll want to know: jiājù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C175-FURN175-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiājù* — "furniture".

> *wǒmen yào mǎi xīn jiājù* — We're going to buy new furniture.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiājù* is built from 家 'home' and *jù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kae* and *gjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'complete' and 'to provide': what a home is provided with.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
