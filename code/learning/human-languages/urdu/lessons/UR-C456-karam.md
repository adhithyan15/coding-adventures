## You'll want to know: کرم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C456-KIND456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کرم** — *karam* — "grace; generosity".

> یہ سب آپ کا کرم ہے۔
>
> *yih sab āp kā karam hai.*
>
> All this is thanks to your kindness.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کرم** (*karam*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *karam*, "generosity, nobility".
