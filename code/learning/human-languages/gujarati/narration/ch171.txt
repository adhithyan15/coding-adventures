Gujarati, chapter 171: Bitter, salty and sweet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સ્વાદ (svād) — taste, flavour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sift, then the Gujarati for to stick.

You'll want to know: સ્વાદ.
સ્વાદ — svād — "taste, flavour".
આ દાળનો સ્વાદ સારો છે. — ā dāḷno svād sāro che — "This dal tastes good."

The word, taken apart.
સ્વાદ (svād) is Sanskrit svāda, "taste, flavour", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL lists the Sanskrit word but no Gujarati form (CDIAL 13923).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ચાખવું (cākhvũ) — to taste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to stick, then the Gujarati for taste.

You'll want to know: ચાખવું.
ચાખવું — cākhvũ — "to taste".
માતા શાક ચાખે છે. — mātā śāk cākhe che — "Mother tastes the curry."

The word, taken apart.
ચાખવું (cākhvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from cakṣati, "tastes", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to taste" (CDIAL 4557).
