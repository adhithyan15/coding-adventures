## You'll want to know: विधुरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C453-VIRAHA453-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विधुरः** — *vidhuraḥ* — "bereft; alone".

> मित्रं दूरे वसति, अतः अहं विधुरः अस्मि।
>
> *mitraṃ dūre vasati, ataḥ ahaṃ vidhuraḥ asmi.*
>
> My friend lives far away, so I feel alone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विधुरः** (*vidhuraḥ*) MW gives "bereft, bereaved (esp. of any loved person), alone, solitary"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
