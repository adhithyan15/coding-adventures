## Stroke order for ttha

ಠ (KA-S173, chapter 80), the last Kannada consonant without one, now has a
cited stroke order. Its writing block stops saying "copy the shape you can
see" and lists the movements, the pen lifts and the source, in the same form
as the other consonants, and the letter lesson now prints a stroke-order
filmstrip.

- **Source.** Gopala Krishna A's `Kannada-alphabet-tta.gif` animation on
  Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0, 25 May 2016), the series the vowels and the
  other consonants cite.
- **Lifts.** The round bowl is one run, from its upper left down the left
  side, round the base, up the right side and back along the top. The pen
  lifts for the hooked bar and again for the dot: two lifts. Most people
  copying ಠ in the Omniglot study also used three strokes.
- **The file name needs a note.** That series spells ಟ "ta" and ಥ "thha", so
  ಠ is filed as "tta". The KA-S173 writing block says so next to the
  citation.
- ಞ (KA-S133) still asks only for copying: no Commons file for it has been
  confirmed.
