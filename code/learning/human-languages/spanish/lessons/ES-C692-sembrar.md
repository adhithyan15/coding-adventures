## You'll want to know: sembrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C692-SEMBRAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**sembrar** — "to sow".

> En primavera siembran el maíz.
>
> *In spring they sow the maize.*

The e becomes ie under stress: *siembro*, *siembra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sembrar* is inherited from Latin *sēmināre* "to sow" (EtymDB), from *sēmen* "seed".
