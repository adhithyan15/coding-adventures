## You'll want to know: とのさま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C299-OUSAMA299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**とのさま** — *tonosama* — "a feudal lord".

> **とのさまは さむらいを よびました。** — *tonosama wa samurai o yobimashita* — "The lord sent for the samurai."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とのさま** (*tonosama*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tono*, "lord", and *sama*, "the honorific" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
