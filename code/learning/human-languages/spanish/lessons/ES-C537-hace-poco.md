## You'll want to know: hace poco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C537-ENTONCES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**hace poco** — "a short time ago, recently".

> Hace poco me mudé a esta ciudad.
>
> *I moved to this city a short time ago.*

*Hace* + time = ago: *hace dos años*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hace poco* is built on *hace*, from *hacer*, inherited from Latin *facere* 'to make', and *poco*, from Latin *paucus* 'few, little' (Lewis & Short).
