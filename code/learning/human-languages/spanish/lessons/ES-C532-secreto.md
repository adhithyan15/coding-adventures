## You'll want to know: el secreto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C532-SECRETO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el secreto** — "the secret; secret".

> Es un secreto, no se lo digas a nadie.
>
> *It is a secret, don't tell anyone.*

As an adjective: *una puerta secreta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*secreto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sēcrētus* 'set apart' (EtymDB), the participle of *sēcernere* 'to separate', built on *cernere* 'to sift'.
