## You'll want to know: 南下
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C423-SOUTH423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**南下** — *nánxià* — "to go south; to head down south".

> **下个月我们开车南下** *xià ge yuè wǒmen kāichē nánxià* — Next month we're driving south.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**南下** (*nánxià*) is built from 南 'south' and 下 'to go down', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nom* and *haeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'low' and 'to come down'.
