## You'll want to know: оправдание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C352-VINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**оправдание** (*opravdániye*) — "an excuse, a justification".

> Для такого поведения нет оправдания.
>
> *Dlya takógo povedéniya net opravdániya.*
>
> There is no excuse for such behaviour.

Neuter. In court it means an acquittal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**оправдание** is built on *оправдать* "to justify", from *правда* "truth", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*pravĭda*, built on *\*pravŭ* "right, correct" (EtymDB). To justify is to show that someone was in the right.
