## You'll want to know: das Schaufenster
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C441-FASSADE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**das Schaufenster** — "the shop window".

> Im Schaufenster stehen schöne Schuhe.
>
> *There are nice shoes in the shop window.*

Same form in the plural. *Einen Schaufensterbummel machen* — to go window-shopping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schaufenster* is built from *schauen* 'to look', inherited from Old High German *scouwōn* (EtymDB), and *Fenster* 'window', an early borrowing from Latin *fenestra* (Lewis & Short).
