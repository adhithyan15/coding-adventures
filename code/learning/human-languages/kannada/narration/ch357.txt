Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಂಚಿಸು (vañcisu) — to swindle; to cheat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ballad, then the Kannada for a moral.

You'll want to know: ವಂಚಿಸು.
ವಂಚಿಸು — vañcisu — "to swindle; to cheat".
ಆ ಮನುಷ್ಯ ಊರಿನ ಜನರನ್ನು ವಂಚಿಸಿ ಓಡಿಹೋದನು. ā manuṣya ūrina janarannu vañcisi ōḍihōdanu. That man cheated the people of the town and ran away.
Past (I …): ವಂಚಿಸಿದೆ, vañciside.
The person cheated takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಮೋಸ ಮಾಡು says the same in everyday talk.

The word, taken apart.
ವಂಚಿಸು (vañcisu) is built on Sanskrit vañcana, "cheating, deception, fraud" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಗೌಪ್ಯ (gaupya) — confidential; secret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a moral, then the Kannada for to swindle.

You'll want to know: ಗೌಪ್ಯ.
ಗೌಪ್ಯ — gaupya — "confidential; secret".
ಈ ಪತ್ರ ಗೌಪ್ಯ, ಅದನ್ನು ಬೇರೆ ಯಾರೂ ಓದಬಾರದು. ī patra gaupya, adannu bēre yārū ōdabāradu. This letter is confidential; nobody else may read it.
ಗೌಪ್ಯವಾಗಿ ಇಡು, to keep confidential. ಗೌಪ್ಯತೆ is privacy.

The word, taken apart.
ಗೌಪ್ಯ (gaupya) is usually traced to Sanskrit gopya, "to be kept secret or hidden" (Monier-Williams), from the root gup, "to guard, to hide"; the dictionary has no form with au.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮುಚ್ಚಿಡು (mucciḍu) — to cover up; to hide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to swindle, then the Kannada for confidential.

You'll want to know: ಮುಚ್ಚಿಡು.
ಮುಚ್ಚಿಡು — mucciḍu — "to cover up; to hide".
ಅಧಿಕಾರಿ ತನ್ನ ತಪ್ಪನ್ನು ಮುಚ್ಚಿಟ್ಟನು. adhikāri tanna tappannu mucciṭṭanu. The officer covered up his mistake.
Past (I …): ಮುಚ್ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ, mucciṭṭe.
ಮುಚ್ಚಿ, having closed, and ಇಡು, to keep: to keep closed. The past is ಮುಚ್ಚಿಟ್ಟೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಚ್ಚಿಡು (mucciḍu) is a native Dravidian compound: muccu, "to close, to cover, to conceal" (DEDR 4915), and iḍu, "to put, to keep" (DEDR 442).

You'll want to know: ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸು.
ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸು — bahiraṅgapaḍisu — "to reveal; to expose".
ಒಬ್ಬ ಪತ್ರಕರ್ತ ಆ ಮೋಸವನ್ನು ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸಿದನು. obba patrakarta ā mōsavannu bahiraṅgapaḍisidanu. A journalist exposed that fraud.
Past (I …): ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, bahiraṅgapaḍiside.
ಬಹಿರಂಗ, open, public, with -ಪಡಿಸು, to make: to make public.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸು (bahiraṅgapaḍisu) is built from Sanskrit bahiraṅga, "relating to the exterior, external" (Monier-Williams), and the Kannada verb-maker -paḍisu, from native paḍu (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಂಚು (sañcu) — a plot; a conspiracy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to cover up, then the Kannada for to reveal.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಚು.
ಸಂಚು — sañcu — "a plot; a conspiracy".
ಅರಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆಲವರು ರಾಜನ ವಿರುದ್ಧ ಸಂಚು ಮಾಡಿದರು. aramaneyalli kelavaru rājana viruddha sañcu māḍidaru. In the palace some people plotted against the king.
Plural: ಸಂಚುಗಳು, sañcugaḷu.
A plot is "made": ಸಂಚು (sañcu) ಮಾಡು, to plot.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಚು (sañcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2293 lists Kannada sañcu, "an expedient, an artifice, a stratagem, an intrigue".
