## You'll want to know: らん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C418-SHOMEI418-04]; assesses=[] -->

**らん** — *ran* — "a box on a form; a column".

> **この らんに なまえを かいて ください。** — *kono ran ni namae o kaite kudasai* — "Please put your name in this box."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**らん** (*ran*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese: *ran*, "column" (Middle Chinese *lan*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
