## You'll want to know: मनहूस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C546-TAQDIR546-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मनहूस** — *manhūs* — "unlucky; ill-omened".

> पुराने लोग कहते थे कि टूटा शीशा मनहूस होता है।
>
> *Purāne log kahte the ki ṭūṭā śīśā manhūs hotā hai.*
>
> Old people used to say that a broken mirror is unlucky.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मनहूस** (*manhūs*) is usually traced to Arabic *manḥūs*, "ill-omened", through Persian.
