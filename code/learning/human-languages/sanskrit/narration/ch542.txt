Sanskrit, chapter 542: Making Up One's Mind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्वीकारः (svīkāraḥ) — acceptance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the other one, then the Sanskrit for different.

You'll want to know: स्वीकारः.
स्वीकारः — svīkāraḥ — "acceptance".
यदि भवतः स्वीकारः अस्ति, तर्हि वयं कार्यम् आरभामहे। yadi bhavataḥ svīkāraḥ asti, tarhi vayaṃ kāryam ārabhāmahe. If you accept, we will begin the work.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
स्वीकारः (svīkāraḥ) is built from sva, "one's own", in the form svī, and kāra, "making", from the root √kṛ; MW gives svīkāra as making one's own, and reception (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अवधारयति (avadhārayati) — makes sure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for different, then the Sanskrit for acceptance.

You'll want to know: अवधारयति.
अवधारयति — avadhārayati — "makes sure".
यदि भवान् मार्गं न जानाति, तर्हि पूर्वम् अवधारयतु। yadi bhavān mārgaṃ na jānāti, tarhi pūrvam avadhārayatu. If you don't know the way, make sure of it first.
Past (he …): सः मार्गम् अवधारितवान्, saḥ mārgam avadhāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
अवधारयति (avadhārayati) is built on ava, "down", and the causative of the root √dhṛ, "to hold", which also gives dhārayati; MW gives avadhārayati as to consider, to ascertain, to determine accurately (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for acceptance, then the Sanskrit for makes sure.

You'll want to know: उत्कृष्टः.
उत्कृष्टः — utkṛṣṭaḥ — "excellent".
यदि एतत् वस्त्रम् उत्कृष्टम् अस्ति, तर्हि अहं तत् क्रीणामि। yadi etat vastram utkṛṣṭam asti, tarhi ahaṃ tat krīṇāmi. If this cloth is excellent, I'll buy it.

The word, taken apart.
उत्कृष्टः (utkṛṣṭaḥ) is built on ud, "up", and the root √kṛṣ, "to draw", which also gives karṣati, as its past participle: drawn up high; MW gives utkṛṣṭa as excellent, eminent (MW).

You'll want to know: रुचिरः.
रुचिरः — ruciraḥ — "pleasing; lovely".
यदि एतत् पुष्पं रुचिरम् अस्ति, तर्हि अहं तत् चिनोमि। yadi etat puṣpaṃ ruciram asti, tarhi ahaṃ tat cinomi. If this flower is lovely, I'll pick it.

The word, taken apart.
रुचिरः (ruciraḥ) comes from the root √ruc, "to shine, to please", which also gives rocate; MW gives rucira as bright, beautiful, and pleasant, liked by someone (MW).

You'll want to know: तटस्थः.
तटस्थः — taṭasthaḥ — "neutral".
यदि भवान् तटस्थः अस्ति, तर्हि भवान् निर्णयं करोतु। yadi bhavān taṭasthaḥ asti, tarhi bhavān nirṇayaṃ karotu. If you are neutral, you decide.

The word, taken apart.
तटस्थः (taṭasthaḥ) is built from taṭa, "a bank, a slope", and stha, "standing": standing on the bank, out of the stream; MW gives taṭastha also as an indifferent person, neither friend nor foe (MW).
