## You'll want to know: mendum, mendī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C408-MENDUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**mendum, mendī** — "a mistake, a blemish (in writing)".

> In hōc librō multa menda sunt.
>
> *There are many mistakes in this book.*

*mendum, mendī* n. *Ēmendō*, to correct, is to take the mistakes out. English *amend* and *emend*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mendum* is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "a fault, a flaw" (EtymDB), and *ēmendō* "to correct" is built on it (Lewis & Short). English *amend* and *emend* come from *ēmendō*.
