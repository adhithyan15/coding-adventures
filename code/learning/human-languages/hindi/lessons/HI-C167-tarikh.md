## You'll want to know: तारीख़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C167-READ167-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तारीख़** — *tārīkh* — "a date". Read the note: **तारीख़ पाँच, सुबह नौ बजे।** (*tārīkh pā̃c, subah nau baje.*) The fifth, at nine in the morning.
