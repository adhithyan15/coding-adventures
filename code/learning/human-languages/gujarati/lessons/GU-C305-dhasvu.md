# ધસવું (dhasvũ) — to give way; to cave in

## You'll want to know: ધસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C305-BHUKAMP305-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ધસવું** — *dhasvũ* — "to give way; to cave in".

> ભૂકંપમાં જૂનું મકાન ધસી પડ્યું.
>
> *bhūkampmā̃ jūnũ makān dhasī paḍyũ.*
>
> The old building caved in during the earthquake.

Past (it …): તે મકાન ધસી પડ્યું, *te makān dhasī paḍyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધસવું** (*dhasvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhváṁsati*, "falls to pieces", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to push through, be undermined" (CDIAL 6896). With **પડવું** it is a building giving way: **ધસી પડ્યું**.
