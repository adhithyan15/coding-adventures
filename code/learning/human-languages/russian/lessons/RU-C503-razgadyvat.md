## You'll want to know: разгадывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C503-COLLECTING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**разгадывать** (*razgádyvat*) — "to solve (a riddle, a puzzle)".

> Мы вместе разгадываем загадки.
>
> *My vméste razgádyvayem zagádki.*
>
> We solve riddles together.

Imperfective; perfective разгадать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*разгадывать* is built on *раз-* and *гадать* 'to guess'; EtymDB has no entry for the verb, and *гадать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *gadati* (EtymDB).
