## You'll want to know: بنوانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C524-FEHRIST524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بنوانا** — *banvānā* — "to have (something) made or built".

> اگر نقشہ ٹھیک ہے تو ہم گھر بنوائیں گے۔
>
> *agar naqshā ṭhīk hai to ham ghar banvāeṅ ge.*
>
> If the plan is right, we'll have the house built.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نیا گھر بنوایا, *us ne nayā ghar banvāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بنوانا** (*banvānā*) is the second causative of *bannā*, "to be made", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *vanati*, "desires, makes ready" (CDIAL 11260): to have someone make a thing.
