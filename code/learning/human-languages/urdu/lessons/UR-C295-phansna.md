## You'll want to know: پھنسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C295-PHANSA295-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پھنسنا** — *phansnā* — "to be trapped; to be stuck".

> تین آدمی ایک کمرے میں پھنس گئے۔
>
> *tīn ādmī ek kamre meṅ phans gae.*
>
> Three men were trapped in a room.

Past (he …): وہ پھنس گیا, *voh phans gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *phā̃snā*, "to ensnare", and *phãsānā* under Sanskrit *spāśáyati* (CDIAL 13814); **پھنسنا** is to be caught in the snare oneself.
