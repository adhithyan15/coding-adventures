## You'll want to know: おどす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C272-SAGI272-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おどす** — *odosu* — "to threaten".

> **はんにんは でんわで ははを おどしました。** — *hannin wa denwa de haha o odoshimashita* — "The culprit threatened my mother on the phone."

Polite **おどします**, *odoshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **どろぼうが おとこを おどしました。**, *dorobō ga otoko o odoshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おどす** (*odosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
