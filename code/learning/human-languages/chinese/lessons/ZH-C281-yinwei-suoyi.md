## Grammar Lens: 因为 … 所以 …, a reason and what follows
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-YINWEI-SUOYI-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C96-SUOYI-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01, ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-03, ZH-LEX-C50-SLEEP50-05, ZH-LEX-C79-SCHOOL79-01] -->

> **因为 opens the reason, 所以 opens what follows from it, and together they make one sentence.**

> **因为下雨了 所以我们没去** *yīnwèi xiàyǔ le, suǒyǐ wǒmen méi qù* — Because it rained, we didn't go.
> **因为他病了 所以没来上课** *yīnwèi tā bìng le, suǒyǐ méi lái shàngkè* — He was ill, so he didn't come to class.

You have had 因为 and *suǒyǐ* as separate words; now they frame one sentence, and the next lesson puts 所 in your hand so the frame prints whole.

English keeps one of the two words; Mandarin is happy with both.

To answer 为什么, 因为 alone is enough: **为什么没去 — 因为下雨了** *wèishénme méi qù — yīnwèi xiàyǔ le*.
