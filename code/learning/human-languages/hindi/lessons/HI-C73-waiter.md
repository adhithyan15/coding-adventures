## You'll want to know: वेटर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C73-EATING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वेटर** (*veṭar*) — "a waiter".

English *waiter*, and the spelling shows two habits you have now seen three
times. The English *w* is written व, because Hindi has one letter where English
splits *v* and *w*. The English *t* is written ट, retroflex, as it was in
**टिकट** and **स्टेशन**.

This is the word on the sign and on the pay slip, so it is the one to be able to
read. Across a table it is used far less than you would expect: people catch an
eye rather than call a title, and a raised hand does the work.

So hold वेटर for reading, and expect the room itself to be quieter than the
word.
