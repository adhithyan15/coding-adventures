## You'll want to know: मतदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C355-MATDAN355-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मतदान** — *matdān* — "voting; a poll".

> रविवारी सकाळपासून मतदान सुरू झालं.
>
> *ravivārī sakāḷpāsūn matdān surū jhālaṁ.*
>
> Voting began on Sunday morning.

Neuter.

मतदान करणे, to vote; मतदान केंद्र, a polling station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मतदान** (*matdān*) is a learned Marathi compound built from Sanskrit *mata*, "opinion", and *dāna*, "giving" (Monier-Williams): the giving of one's opinion.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
