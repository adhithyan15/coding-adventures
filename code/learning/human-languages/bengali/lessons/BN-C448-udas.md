## You'll want to know: উদাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C448-BIROHO448-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উদাস** — *udās* — "wistful; far away in one's thoughts".

> জানালার পাশে বসে মীরা উদাস হয়ে আকাশ দেখছিল।
>
> *jānālār pāshe bôse mīrā udās hôye ākāsh dekhchhilô.*
>
> Sitting by the window, Mira gazed wistfully at the sky.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উদাস** (*udās*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *udāsa*, "indifference, apathy" (Monier-Williams); in Bengali it is the far-away look of someone lost in thought.
