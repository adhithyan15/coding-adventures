## You'll want to know: عنف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C381-MUJTAMA381-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عنف** — *ʿunf* — "violence".

> يرفض الناس العنف.
>
> *yarfuḍu an-nās al-ʿunf.*
>
> People reject violence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عنف** (*ʿunf*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-n-f*, "to be harsh, rough" (Lane). *ʿUnf* is harshness, the opposite of gentleness: violence.
