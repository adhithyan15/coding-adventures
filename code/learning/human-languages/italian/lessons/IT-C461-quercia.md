## You'll want to know: la quercia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C461-ALBERI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la quercia** — "the oak".

> Dietro casa c'è una quercia di cent'anni.
>
> *Behind the house there is a hundred-year-old oak.*

*Forte come una quercia* — as strong as an oak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quercia* is inherited from Latin *quercea* (EtymDB), from *quercus* 'oak'.
