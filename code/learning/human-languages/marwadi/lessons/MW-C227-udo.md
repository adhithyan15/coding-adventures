# ऊंडो (ū̃ḍo) — deep down; deep (of a well or a pit)

## You'll want to know: ऊंडो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C227-WELL227-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊंडो** — *ū̃ḍo* — "deep down; deep (of a well or a pit)".

> कूवो घणो ऊंडो है।
>
> *kūvo ghaṇo ū̃ḍo hai.*
>
> The well is very deep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ūṇḍa*, "deep"; CDIAL lists Marwari *ū̃ṛo* (CDIAL 2402). **गैरो** is deep too; **ऊंडो** is said especially of a well or a pit that goes far down.
