## You'll want to know: भ्रम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C366-VAJIB366-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रम** — *bhram* — "a misconception, an illusion".

> यह एक भ्रम है कि पैसे से सब कुछ मिलता है।
>
> *yah ek bhram hai ki paise se sab kuch miltā hai.*
>
> It's a misconception that money buys everything.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रम** (*bhram*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *bhrama*, "wandering; confusion" (Monier-Williams).
