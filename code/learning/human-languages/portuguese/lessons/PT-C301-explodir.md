## You'll want to know: explodir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C301-INCENDIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**explodir** — "to explode, to blow up".

> Uma botija de gás explodiu no prédio.
>
> *A gas bottle exploded in the building.*

Mostly in the third person: *explodiu, explodem*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*explodir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *explōdere* "to drive off by clapping, to hiss off the stage" (Lewis & Short); the sense of a bang came later.
