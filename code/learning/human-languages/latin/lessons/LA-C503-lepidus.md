## You'll want to know: lepidus, lepida, lepidum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C503-INDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lepidus, lepida, lepidum** — "charming, delightful".

> Fābula lepida et brevis est.
>
> *The story is charming and short.*

A word of comedy and of friendly letters: neat, pleasant, nicely done.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lepidus** is built on *lepōs* "charm, grace" (Lewis & Short); the origin of *lepōs* is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
