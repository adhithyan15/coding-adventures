## You'll want to know: le bouchon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C410-BOUCHONS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le bouchon** — "the traffic jam; the cork".

> Il y a dix kilomètres de bouchon avant le tunnel.
>
> *There is a ten-kilometre jam before the tunnel.*

*Un bouchon* is also a cork for a bottle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bouchon* is built on Middle French *bousche* 'handful of straw' (EtymDB): first a twist of straw that stopped up a bottle, now a cork and a road blocked by cars.
