## You'll want to know: بنگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C456-PEYK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بنگاه** (*bongâh*) — "an estate agency; a firm, an agency".

> از طریق بنگاه یک آپارتمان پیدا کردیم.
>
> *Az tariq-e bongâh yek âpârtemân peydâ kardim.*
>
> We found a flat through an estate agency.

In full *bongâh-e moshâver-e amlâk*. Also a firm: *bongâh-e bârbari* — a removals firm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bongâh* is usually analysed as native *bon* 'base, foundation' and *gâh* 'place'. EtymDB does not list it.
