## You'll want to know: धर्मशाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C413-SARAY413-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धर्मशाला** — *dharmśālā* — "a rest house (for pilgrims)".

> मंदिर के पास एक सस्ती धर्मशाला है।
>
> *mandir ke pās ek sastī dharmśālā hai.*
>
> There is a cheap rest house near the temple.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धर्मशाला** (*dharmśālā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dharmaśālā* (Monier-Williams), from *dharma*, "duty, charity", and *śālā*, "a hall": a house kept for pilgrims and travellers at little or no cost.
