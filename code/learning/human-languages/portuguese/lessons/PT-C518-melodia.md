## You'll want to know: a melodia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C518-CORO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a melodia** — "melody, tune".

> Esta melodia não me sai da cabeça.
>
> *I cannot get this tune out of my head.*

The adjective is *melodioso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*melodia* comes from Latin *melōdia* (EtymDB), from Greek *melōidía* "singing", built on *mélos* "song".
