Bengali, chapter 290: Waves and Wreckage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঢেউ (ḍheu) — a wave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a raft, then the Bengali for to be flooded.

You'll want to know: ঢেউ.
ঢেউ — ḍheu — "a wave".
বড় একটা ঢেউ এসে নৌকাটা উল্টে দিল। bôṛo ekṭā ḍheu ese noukāṭā ulṭe dilô. A big wave came and overturned the boat.

The word, taken apart.
ঢেউ (ḍheu) is filed in CDIAL 5580 under a Middle Indo-Aryan ḍhappa-, 'lump', where a Bengali ḍheu, 'a swelling', appears; how it became a wave is not set out there. It opens with ঢ, the breathy ḍh.

Lesson 2 of 5.
তীর (tīr) — a riverbank; a shore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to be flooded, then the Bengali for a wave.

You'll want to know: তীর.
তীর — tīr — "a riverbank; a shore".
ঢেউ নৌকাটাকে তীরে নিয়ে এল। ḍheu noukāṭāke tīre niye elô. The waves carried the boat to the shore.
A second তীর (tīr), an arrow, is a different word.

The word, taken apart.
তীর (tīr) is Sanskrit tīra, 'a shore, a bank' (Monier-Williams), unchanged. নদীর তীরে, on the riverbank.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ভাঙন (bhāngôn) — the eating away of a riverbank; erosion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a wave, then the Bengali for a riverbank.

You'll want to know: ভাঙন.
ভাঙন — bhāngôn — "the eating away of a riverbank; erosion".
নদীর ভাঙনে গ্রামের স্কুলটা নদীতে চলে গেল। nôdīr bhāngône grāmer skulṭā nôdīte chôle gelô. The river ate away its bank and the village school went into the water.

The word, taken apart.
ভাঙন (bhāngôn) is ভাঙা, to break — CDIAL 9353, Sanskrit bhaṅga — with the -ন ending that names the act: the breaking away of a riverbank, a word the river delta needs.

You'll want to know: গৃহহীন.
গৃহহীন — grihôhīn — "homeless".
বন্যার পরে অনেক পরিবার গৃহহীন হল। bônyār pôre ônek pôribār grihôhīn hôlô. After the flood many families were left homeless.

The word, taken apart.
গৃহহীন (grihôhīn) joins Sanskrit gṛha, 'a house, a home', to hīna, 'left, deprived of' (Monier-Williams): without a house. It is a word of the news more than of talk.
