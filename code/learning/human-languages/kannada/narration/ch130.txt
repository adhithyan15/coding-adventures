Kannada, chapter 130: Bear, Monkey, Rabbit, Deer, Fox.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕರಡಿ (karaḍi) — a bear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tiger, then the Kannada for a lion.

You'll want to know: ಕರಡಿ.
ಕರಡಿ — karaḍi — "a bear".
