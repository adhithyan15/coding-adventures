Arabic, chapter 481: Rain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
زخة (zakhkha) — a shower of rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to drop, then the Arabic for mild.

You'll want to know: زخة.
زخة — zakhkha — "a shower of rain".
نزلت زخة (zakhkha) مطر قصيرة. nazalat zakhkhat maṭar qaṣīra. A short shower of rain fell.

The word, taken apart.
زخة (zakhkha) names a short, sudden shower; the origin is not settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for mild, then the Arabic for a shower of rain.

You'll want to know: رذاذ.
رذاذ — radhādh — "drizzle; spray".
كان الرذاذ خفيفا في الصباح. kāna ar-radhādh khafīfan fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ. The drizzle was light in the morning.

The word, taken apart.
رذاذ (radhādh) is usually traced to the native Arabic root r-dh-dh, "fine rain".

وابل (wābil) is built on the native Arabic root w-b-l, "heavy rain of large drops" (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
هطل (haṭala) — to pour down (of rain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for drizzle, then the Arabic for a downpour.

You'll want to know: هطل.
هطل — haṭala — "to pour down (of rain)".
هطل المطر في الليل. haṭala al-maṭar fī al-layl. The rain poured down in the night.
Present: يهطل, yahṭil.

The word, taken apart.
هطل (haṭala) is built on the native Arabic root h-ṭ-l, "to pour (of water)" (Lane). It is the plain verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a downpour, then the Arabic for to pour down.

You'll want to know: صفا.
صفا — ṣafā — "to clear up; to become clear".
صفت السماء بعد المطر. ṣafat as-samāʾ baʿda al-maṭar. The sky cleared after the rain.
Present: يصفو, yaṣfū.

The word, taken apart.
صفا (ṣafā) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-f-w, "to be clear, pure" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
