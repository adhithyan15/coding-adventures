## You'll want to know: набережная
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C440-PEREKHOD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**набережная** (*náberezhnaya*) — "an embankment, a riverside walk".

> Вечером мы гуляли по набережной.
>
> *Vécherom my gulyáli po náberezhnoy.*
>
> In the evening we walked along the embankment.

An adjective used as a noun; the stress is on the first syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*набережная* is built from *на* 'on' and *берег* 'a bank, a shore' with an adjective ending: the street along the bank. *Берег* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *bergŭ* (EtymDB).
