# تمسك (tamassaka) — to hold on to

## You'll want to know: تمسك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C373-MAWQIF373-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تمسك** — *tamassaka* — "to hold on to".

> تمسك الرجل برأيه.
>
> *tamassaka ar-rajul bi-raʾyih.*
>
> The man held on to his opinion.

Present: يتمسك, *yatamassak*.

It takes بـ: تمسك بـ ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تمسك** (*tamassaka*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-s-k*, "to hold, to grasp" (Lane). It is Form V, to keep a firm hold: to cling to a thing. أمسك, to hold, is from the same root.
