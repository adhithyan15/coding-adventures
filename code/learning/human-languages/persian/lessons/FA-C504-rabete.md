## You'll want to know: رابطه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C504-FRIENDS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رابطه** (*râbete*) — "a relationship; a connection".

> رابطه او با پدرش خیلی خوب است.
>
> *râbete-ye u bâ pedarash kheyli khub ast.*
>
> Her relationship with her father is very good.

Plural *ravâbet* (Arabic) in formal Persian; *râbetehâ* in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*râbete* comes from Arabic *rābiṭa*, 'bond, tie' (EtymDB).
