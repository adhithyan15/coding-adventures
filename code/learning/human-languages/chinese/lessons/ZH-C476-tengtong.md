## You'll want to know: téngtòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C476-NECK476-05]; assesses=[] -->

*téngtòng* — "pain; ache".

> *chī yào yǐhòu téngtòng hǎo le yìdiǎnr* — After taking the medicine, the pain eased a little.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*téngtòng* is built from 疼 'pain' and *tòng* 'to hurt', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *downg* and *thuwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'pain' and the second 'illness' and 'to hurt'.
