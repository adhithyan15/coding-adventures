Urdu, chapter 444: Losing One's Temper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غصہ (ghussā) — anger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to fret inwardly, then the Urdu for dejected.

You'll want to know: غصہ.
غصہ — ghussā — "anger".
دیر سے آنے پر استاد کو غصہ آیا۔ der se āne par ustād ko ghussā āyā. The teacher got angry at someone coming late.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
غصہ (ghussā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ghuṣṣa, "a choking, a lump in the throat"; the sense "anger" is the one Urdu gives it.

What you've built.
