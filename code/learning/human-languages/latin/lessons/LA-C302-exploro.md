## You'll want to know: explōrō, explōrāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C302-SPECULATOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**explōrō, explōrāre** — "to search out, to explore".

> Mīlitēs silvam explōrāvērunt, sed nēminem invēnērunt.
>
> *The soldiers searched the forest, but found nobody.*

*explōrō, explōrāre, explōrāvī, explōrātum*. English *explore*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**explōrō** is usually traced to *ex* and *plōrō*, "to cry out", perhaps first the shout of hunters driving game out of cover; the details are not settled. English *explore* comes from it.
