## You'll want to know: tōu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C216-WRONG216-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tōu* — "to steal".

> *yǒu rén tōule wǒ de zìxíngchē* — Somebody has stolen my bike.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tōu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *thuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to rob'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
