## You'll want to know: காய்ச்சல்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C61-FEEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**காய்ச்சல்** (*kāyccal*) — "a fever".

The body running hot. Tamil built the word on its own idea of heating rather than importing one, which is why it looks nothing like Kannada's ಜ್ವರ (*jvara*), taken straight from the Sanskrit.

It is the first thing anybody tells a மருத்துவர், and it is said flatly, the way you would report weather.
