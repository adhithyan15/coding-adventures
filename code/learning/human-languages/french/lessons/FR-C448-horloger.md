## You'll want to know: l'horloger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C448-OPTICIEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'horloger** — "the watchmaker, the clockmaker".

> L'horloger a changé la pile de ma montre.
>
> *The watchmaker changed the battery in my watch.*

A masculine noun. *L'horlogerie* — the watchmaker's shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*horloger* is built on *horloge* 'clock', which comes from Latin *hōrologium*, from Greek *hōrológion* 'thing that tells the hour' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
