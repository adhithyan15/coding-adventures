# ചട്ടം (caṭṭaṁ) — a regulation; a rule

## You'll want to know: ചട്ടം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C417-CATTAM417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ചട്ടം** — *caṭṭaṁ* — "a regulation; a rule".

> ഓഫീസിലെ ചട്ടം അനുസരിച്ച് ഫോൺ ഉപയോഗിക്കാൻ പാടില്ല.
>
> *ōphīsile caṭṭaṁ anusariccŭ phōṇ upayōgikkān pāṭilla.*
>
> Under the office rules, phones may not be used.

Plural: ചട്ടങ്ങൾ, *caṭṭaṅṅaḷ*.

Plural: ചട്ടങ്ങൾ, regulations.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചട്ടം** (*caṭṭaṁ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2304 lists Malayalam *caṭṭam* as "a frame, a mould, a plan, a regulation, a rule, a law". A rule is the frame things are made in.
