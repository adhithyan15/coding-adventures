## You'll want to know: ব্রণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C469-GHAM469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ব্রণ** — *brôn* — "a pimple; acne".

> মুখে ব্রণ হলে অরুণ লজ্জা পায়।
>
> *mukhe brôn hôle ôrun lôjjā pāy.*
>
> When he gets spots on his face, Arun feels shy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ব্রণ** (*brôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vraṇa*, "an abscess, a boil, a scar" (CDIAL 12227); Bengali has narrowed it to a pimple.
