# -मुळे (-muḷe) — because of, and the reason in full

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C280-TYAMULE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C280-MHANUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "because of that". (*tyāmuḷe*.) Its second half is the ending this lesson takes apart.

## You'll want to know: -मुळे, 'because of'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C281-MULE-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C99-NATURE99-01, MR-LEX-C127-TIME127-02, MR-LEX-C113-TOWN113-05, MR-LEX-C111-TIME111-02] -->

> **पावसामुळे गाडी उशिरा आली.**
>
> *pāvsāmuḷe gāḍī uśirā ālī.* — "Because of the rain, the train came late."

**-मुळे** goes on a noun in its bent form. **पाऊस** bends to **पावसा-**, the same as in **पावसाळा**: **पावसामुळे** (*pāvsāmuḷe*). **उशिरा** (*uśirā*, "late") is **उशीर** made into "how". On a pronoun: **तुमच्यामुळे** (*tumcyāmuḷe*), "because of you"; and **त्यामुळे**, "because of that".

## You'll want to know: the reason in full
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C281-REASON-ORDER-01]; assesses=[MR-JOIN-KAARAN, MR-LEX-C73-HEALTH73-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-01, MR-JOIN-KAA-WHY] -->

> **मी काल आलो नाही, कारण मी आजारी होतो.**
>
> *mī kāl ālo nāhī, kāraṇ mī ājārī hoto.* — "I didn't come yesterday, because I was ill."

When someone asks **तुम्ही का आला नाहीत?** (*tumhī kā ālā nāhīt?*), "Why didn't you come?", answer with the event and then **कारण** and the reason, a whole sentence in the past. Or the reason first and then **म्हणून**: **मी आजारी होतो, म्हणून मी आलो नाही** (*mī ājārī hoto, mhaṇūn mī ālo nāhī*).
