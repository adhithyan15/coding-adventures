## You'll want to know: l'incendie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C302-INCENDIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'incendie** — "the fire, the blaze".

> Un incendie a brûlé trois maisons.
>
> *A fire burnt down three houses.*

A fire out of control is *un incendie*; a fire in a fireplace is *un feu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incendie* is a learned borrowing from Latin *incendium* "a burning, a fire", from *incendere* "to set on fire" (Lewis & Short).
