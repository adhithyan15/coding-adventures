## You'll want to know: સીટી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C393-ENGINE393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સીટી** — *sīṭī* — "a whistle".

> ટ્રેનની સીટી સાંભળીને બાળકો દોડ્યાં.
>
> *ṭrennī sīṭī sā̃bhḷīne bāḷko doḍyā̃.*
>
> Hearing the train's whistle, the children ran.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સીટી** (*sīṭī*) goes back to *\*sīṭṭa*, "a whistle", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as imitative of the sound itself; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 13427).
