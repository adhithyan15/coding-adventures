## You'll want to know: తోయు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C545-SADHANA545-04]; assesses=[] -->

**తోయు** — *tōyu* — "to shove; to push".

> జనం గుంపులో ఎవరో నన్ను తోశారు.
>
> *janaṁ gumpulō evarō nannu tōśāru.*
>
> Someone in the crowd shoved me.

Past (I …): తోశాను, *tōśānu*.

Rougher than నెట్టు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తోయు** (*tōyu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3340 lists Telugu *trōcu*, "to push, to shove, to thrust", the older form of this verb.
