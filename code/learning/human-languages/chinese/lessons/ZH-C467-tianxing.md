## You'll want to know: 天性
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C467-PERSON467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**天性** — *tiānxìng* — "one's nature; an inborn trait".

> **安静是他的天性** *ānjìng shì tā de tiānxìng* — Being quiet is in his nature.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**天性** (*tiānxìng*) is built from 天 'heaven' and 性 'nature', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *then* and *sjengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the dark heaven above' and the second 'nature and conduct': the nature heaven gives.
