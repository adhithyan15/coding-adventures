## You'll want to know: chuān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C59-WEAR59-01]; assesses=[] -->

*chuān* — "to wear; to put on".

> *míngtiān nǐ chuān shénme* — What are you wearing tomorrow?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chuān* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyhwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to go through' and 'a hole'. Wearing is not given there; putting arms and legs through clothes is the usual explanation, but it is not settled.
