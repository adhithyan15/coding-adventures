## You'll want to know: ਹੰਝੂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C327-UPSET327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੰਝੂ** — *hanjhū* — "a teardrop".

> ਵਿਆਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਦੀ ਅੱਖ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੰਝੂ ਆਇਆ।
>
> *viāh vich merī bhaiṇ dī akkh vich hanjhū āiā.*
>
> At the wedding a tear came to my sister's eye.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੰਝੂ** (*hanjhū*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aśru*, "a tear"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *hañjhū*, "tear", under it (CDIAL 919).
