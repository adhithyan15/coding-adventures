## You'll want to know: баловать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C497-UPBRINGING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**баловать** (*balovát*) — "to spoil (a child)".

> Бабушка балует внуков сладостями.
>
> *Bábushka balúyet vnúkov sládostyami.*
>
> Grandma spoils her grandchildren with sweets.

Imperfective; present балую, балуешь (-ова- becomes -у-). Perfective избаловать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*баловать* is inherited from Old East Slavic *balovati* 'to flatter, to heal', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic *badlovati*.
