# കട (kaṭa) — a shop

## You'll want to know: കട
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C91-SHOP-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കട** — *kaṭa* — a shop.

This book has taught you rice, water, tea, coffee, milk, salt and a knife, and
**not one word for the place any of them comes from.** That is the hole this
chapter is here to fill, and it starts with the building.

It takes the in-ending exactly as you would expect, joining with a **-യ-**
because the word ends in **അ**:

**കടയിൽ** — *kaṭayil* — in the shop, at the shop.

Nothing about that join is new. **It is the same choice the owner-ending makes**,
decided by the same thing — the last sound of the noun.
