Malayalam, chapter 562: Rhythm, Raga and the Screen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
താളം (tāḷaṁ) — rhythm; a beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a guitar, then the Malayalam for a flute.

You'll want to know: താളം.
താളം — tāḷaṁ — "rhythm; a beat".
കുട്ടികൾ പാട്ടിന്റെ താളത്തിൽ കൈ കൊട്ടി. kuṭṭikaḷ pāṭṭinṟe tāḷattil kai koṭṭi. The children clapped to the rhythm of the song.
താളം (tāḷaṁ) തെറ്റുക, to lose the beat, also means to go wrong.

The word, taken apart.
താളം (tāḷaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit tāla, which CDIAL gives as a cymbal (CDIAL 5801); in Indian music it came to mean the beat the cymbals keep.

Lesson 2 of 5.
രാഗം (rāgaṁ) — a raga; a melody.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a flute, then the Malayalam for rhythm.

You'll want to know: രാഗം.
രാഗം — rāgaṁ — "a raga; a melody".
ആ പാട്ട് ഏത് രാഗത്തിലാണ്? ā pāṭṭŭ ētŭ rāgattilāṇŭ? Which raga is that song in?
A raga is a set of notes and turns that a melody moves within.

The word, taken apart.
രാഗം (rāgaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit rāga, "colour, redness" (CDIAL 10673); Indian music traditionally explains a raga as what colours the listener's mood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for rhythm, then the Malayalam for a raga.

You'll want to know: സംവിധായകൻ.
സംവിധായകൻ — saṁvidhāyakan — "a director".
ആ സിനിമയുടെ സംവിധായകന് സമ്മാനം കിട്ടി. ā sinimayuṭe saṁvidhāyakanŭ sammānaṁ kiṭṭi. The director of that film won a prize.
സംവിധാനം ചെയ്യുക is to direct a film.

The word, taken apart.
സംവിധായകൻ (saṁvidhāyakan) is built on Sanskrit saṁvidhāna, "arrangement, management" (Monier-Williams), with the agent ending -അകൻ: the one who arranges.

Lesson 4 of 5.
തിരക്കഥ (tirakkatha) — a screenplay; a script.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a raga, then the Malayalam for a director.

You'll want to know: തിരക്കഥ.
തിരക്കഥ — tirakkatha — "a screenplay; a script".
അവൾ ഒരു സിനിമയ്ക്ക് തിരക്കഥ എഴുതി. avaḷ oru sinimaykkŭ tirakkatha eḻuti. She wrote a screenplay for a film.
തിര a screen, a curtain + കഥ a story.

The word, taken apart.
തിരക്കഥ (tirakkatha) is a Malayalam compound built from തിര, "a curtain", which is native (DEDR 3244), and കഥ, from Sanskrit kathā, "a story" (CDIAL 2704).
