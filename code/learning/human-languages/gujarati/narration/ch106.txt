Gujarati, chapter 106: Cupboard, Mirror, Comb, Soap, Towel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કબાટ (kabāṭ) — a cupboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to win, then the Gujarati for to lose.

You'll want to know: કબાટ.
કબાટ (kabāṭ) — "a cupboard".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of કબાટ (kabāṭ) is not settled. It matches Sanskrit kapāṭa, "leaf of a door", under which CDIAL lists Gujarati kamāṛ, "door" (CDIAL 2963), but CDIAL does not list કબાટ (kabāṭ) itself.

The word, taken apart.
અરીસો (arīso) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ādarśa, "mirror" (CDIAL 1143).

Lesson 4 of 5.
સાબુ (sābu) — soap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a mirror, then the Gujarati for a comb.

You'll want to know: સાબુ.
સાબુ (sābu) — "soap".

The word, taken apart.
સાબુ (sābu) is a loan. It is traced either to Portuguese sabão or, like Hindi sābun, to Arabic ṣābūn through Persian (Lane, root ṣ-b-n). Both go back to Latin sāpō.
