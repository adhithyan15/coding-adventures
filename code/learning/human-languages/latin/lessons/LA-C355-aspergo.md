## You'll want to know: aspergō, aspergere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C355-CONDIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aspergō, aspergere** — "to sprinkle".

> Coquus piper in carnem aspersit.
>
> *The cook sprinkled pepper on the meat.*

*aspergō, aspergere, aspersī, aspersum*: *ad* + *spargō*, 'to scatter'. English *asperse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**aspergō** is built on *spargō*, "to scatter", with *ad-* (Lewis & Short); the vowel changes inside the compound.
