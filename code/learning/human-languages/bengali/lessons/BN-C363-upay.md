## You'll want to know: উপায়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C363-SOMOSSHA363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**উপায়** — *upāy* — "a way; a means".

> আর কোনো উপায় নেই।
>
> *ār kono upāy nei.*
>
> There is no other way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপায়** (*upāy*) is Sanskrit *upāya*, 'coming near', a means of reaching an end (Monier-Williams).
