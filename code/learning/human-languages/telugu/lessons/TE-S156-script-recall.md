## Script: the six, side by side
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-150, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-151, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-152, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-153, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-154, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-155] -->

> మ · ధ · ట · ో · ొ · ఠ

Four are letters and two are signs. **A letter can stand where a word begins; a
sign has to hang on a letter**, and telling which is which is the first question
to ask of an unfamiliar shape.

| letters | signs |
|---|---|
| మ ధ ట ఠ | ో ొ |
