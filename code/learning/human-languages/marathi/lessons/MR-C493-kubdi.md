## You'll want to know: कुबडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C493-TOL493-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कुबडी** — *kubḍī* — "a crutch".

> पाय मोडल्यामुळे तो कुबडी घेऊन चालतो.
>
> *pāy moḍlyāmuḷe to kubḍī gheūn cālto.*
>
> Since he broke his leg he walks with a crutch.

Feminine. Plural: कुबड्या, *kubḍyā*.

Plural कुबड्या, a pair of crutches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुबडी** (*kubḍī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
