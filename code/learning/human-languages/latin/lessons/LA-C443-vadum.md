## You'll want to know: vadum, vadī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C443-FLUCTUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vadum, vadī** — "a shallow, a shoal; a ford".

> Equī flūmen per vadum trānseunt.
>
> *The horses cross the river by the ford.*

Second declension, neuter: *vadum, vadī n.* A place where water is shallow enough to walk through, or to run aground on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vadum* is inherited from Proto-Italic *waðom* 'shallow, ford', from PIE *wh₂dʰóm* (EtymDB). Lewis & Short link it with *vādō* 'to go': a place where you can walk through water.
