## You'll want to know: সংবাদপত্র
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C296-SONGBAD296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সংবাদপত্র** — *sôngbādpôtrô* — "a newspaper".

> বাবা প্রতিদিন সকালে সংবাদপত্র পড়েন।
>
> *bābā prôtidin sôkāle sôngbādpôtrô pôṛen.*
>
> Father reads the newspaper every morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংবাদপত্র** (*sôngbādpôtrô*) joins Sanskrit *saṃvāda*, 'a speaking together, a conversation' (Monier-Williams), to *pattra*, 'a leaf, a written sheet': a sheet of news. Everyday speech says খবরের কাগজ.
