Sanskrit, chapter 520: Learning a Raga.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्वरः (svaraḥ) — a musical note.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a creation, then the Sanskrit for fashions.

You'll want to know: स्वरः.
स्वरः — svaraḥ — "a musical note".
गुरुः सप्त स्वरान् पाठयति। guruḥ sapta svarān pāṭhayati. The teacher teaches the seven notes.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
स्वरः (svaraḥ) comes from the root √svṛ, "to sound, to sing"; MW gives svara as sound, voice, tone, and a note of the musical scale (MW).

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 3 of 5.
लयः (layaḥ) — a tempo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a musical note, then the Sanskrit for a raga.

You'll want to know: लयः.
लयः — layaḥ — "a tempo".
एतस्य गीतस्य लयः मन्दः अस्ति। etasya gītasya layaḥ mandaḥ asti. This song has a slow tempo.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
लयः (layaḥ) comes from the root √lī, "to cling, to rest"; MW gives laya as rest, and in music the time, of three kinds, quick, middle and slow (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
वादयति (vādayati) — plays an instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a tempo, then the Sanskrit for singing.

You'll want to know: वादयति.
वादयति — vādayati — "plays an instrument".
सः वेणुं वादयति। saḥ veṇuṃ vādayati. He plays the flute.
Past (he …): सः वीणां वादितवान्, saḥ vīṇāṃ vāditavān.

The word, taken apart.
वादयति (vādayati) comes from the causative of the root √vad, "to speak"; MW gives vādayati as to make sound, to strike, to play an instrument (MW).
