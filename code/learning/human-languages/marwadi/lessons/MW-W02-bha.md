# भ — *bha*, with a small breath

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-SA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-INDEPENDENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Write **स**, then **आ**, from memory. Check both before moving on.

## Script — one new sign and sound
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-BHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **भ** — *bha*

Begin like English *b*, then let a small puff of air follow. Hold a hand near
your mouth: *bha*. The breath is part of the sound.

## Writing — observe, trace, copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-BHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

1. look at **भ**
2. finger-trace its lower shape, then the upright line
3. copy **भ** twice
4. say *bha* after each copy

Stop there. The next lesson will join this sign to signs already in your hand.
