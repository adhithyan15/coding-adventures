## You'll want to know: ذائقہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C415-DHABA415-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ذائقہ** — *zāiqā* — "a flavour; a taste".

> اس ڈھابے کے کھانے کا ذائقہ اچھا ہے۔
>
> *is ḍhābe ke khāne kā zāiqā achchhā hai.*
>
> The food at this eatery tastes good.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dhāʾiqa*, "the sense of taste"; a dish with a good **ذائقہ** is **مزیدار**.
