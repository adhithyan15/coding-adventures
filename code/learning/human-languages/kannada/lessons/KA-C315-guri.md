## You'll want to know: ಗುರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C315-GURI-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C172-KALA172-02, KA-LEX-C05-KANNADA-01, KA-LEX-C260-VASTU260-03, KA-LEX-C33-OODU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C312-OBLIGATIVE-01, KA-LEX-C102-THISWAY-02] -->

**ಗುರಿ** — *guri* — "a goal, an aim".

> **ಈ ವರ್ಷ ನನಗೆ ಒಂದು ಗುರಿ ಇದೆ: ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ಕನ್ನಡ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ ಓದಬೇಕು.**
>
> *ī varṣa nanage ondu guri ide: pratidina kannaḍa patrike ōdabēku.* — "This year I have a goal: I must read a Kannada newspaper every day."

**ವರ್ಷ** (*varṣa*, "a year"). **ನನಗೆ … ಇದೆ** is "I have", the way **ನನಗೆ ಜ್ವರ ಇದೆ** is "I have a fever".

**ಗುರಿ** is also the mark an archer aims at, and the aim itself:

> **ಅವನ ಗುರಿ ತಪ್ಪಿತು.**
>
> *avana guri tappitu.* — "His aim missed."

**ಅವನ** (*avana*, "his"); **ತಪ್ಪಿತು**, "it slipped, it went astray", the verb of **ಬಸ್ ತಪ್ಪಿಹೋಯಿತು**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುರಿ** (*guri*) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1847 lists Kannada *kuṟi* and *guṟi*, "a mark, sign, aim, object of aim", in an older spelling, with a letter for *ṟ* that modern Kannada no longer writes; beside it are Tamil *kuṟi*, "mark, aim, goal", and Telugu *guṟi*, "an aim". A mark you aim at became the goal you aim for.
