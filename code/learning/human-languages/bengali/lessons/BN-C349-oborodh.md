## You'll want to know: অবরোধ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C349-PROTIBAD349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অবরোধ** — *ôbôrodh* — "a blockade; a sit-in (blocking a road)".

> অবরোধের জন্য রাস্তায় বাস নেই।
>
> *ôbôrodher jônyô rāstāy bās nei.*
>
> There are no buses on the road because of the blockade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অবরোধ** (*ôbôrodh*) is Sanskrit *avarodha*, hindrance or obstruction (Monier-Williams). In Bengali news it is a protest that blocks a road or a railway.
