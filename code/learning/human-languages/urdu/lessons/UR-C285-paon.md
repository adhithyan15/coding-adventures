## You'll want to know: پاؤں
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C285-PHISAL285-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پاؤں** — *pāoṅ* — "a foot".

> میرا پاؤں پھسل گیا۔
>
> *merā pāoṅ phisal gayā.*
>
> My foot slipped.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پاؤں** (*pāoṅ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pāda*, "foot" (CDIAL 8056), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pā̃u*. Urdu writes it with **ؤ** and the nasal **ں**.
