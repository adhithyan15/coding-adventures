## You'll want to know: مسیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C434-PELLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مسیر** (*masir*) — "a route, a way".

> مسیر این اتوبوس خیلی طولانی است.
>
> *Masir-e in otobus kheyli tulâni ast.*
>
> This bus's route is very long.

*Dar masir* — on the way, en route.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*masir* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *masīr* 'going, the way of a journey'. EtymDB does not list it.
