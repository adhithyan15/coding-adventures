## You'll want to know: o enfeite
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C322-FOGUETE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o enfeite** — "the decoration, the ornament".

> Ela fez um enfeite de papel para a janela.
>
> *She made a paper decoration for the window.*

Built on *enfeitar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enfeite* is built on *enfeitar* "to decorate", which is usually traced to *en-* and *feito* "made", from Latin *factus* (Lewis & Short).
