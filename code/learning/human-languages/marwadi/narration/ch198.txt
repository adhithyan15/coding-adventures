Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 198: At the Office.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दफ्तर (daphtar) — an office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a message, then the Marwadi for to rub out.

You'll want to know: दफ्तर.
दफ्तर — daphtar — "an office".
दफ्तर आज बंद है। daphtar āj band hai. The office is closed today.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दफ्तर (daphtar) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic daftar, "a register".

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अरजी (arjī) — an application.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: अरजी.
अरजी — arjī — "an application".
हूं अरजी लिखूं हूं। hū̃ arjī likhū̃ hū̃. I am writing an application.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अरजी (arjī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿarḍ, "a presenting": an application lays a matter before an officer.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

बिनती — bintī — "a request".
म्हारी बिनती सुणो। mhārī bintī suṇo. Please hear my request.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बिनती (bintī) is inherited from Sanskrit vijñapti, "a request to a superior"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bīnatī, "request" (CDIAL 11706).

Lesson 5 of 5.
समझावणो (samjhāvṇo) — to explain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an application, then the Marwadi for a request.

You'll want to know: समझावणो.
समझावणो — samjhāvṇo — "to explain".
अफसर नियम समझावै है। aphsar niyam samjhāvai hai. The officer explains the rule.
Past (I …): म्हैं बात समझाई, mhaĩ bāt samjhāī.

The word, taken apart.
समझावणो (samjhāvṇo) is the causative of समझणो, "to understand": to make someone understand. Both are inherited from Sanskrit sambudhyate, "wakes up, understands"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari samajhāi, "persuades" (CDIAL 12959).
