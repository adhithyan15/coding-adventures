Lesson 1 of 5.
ಗುಂಪು (gumpu) — a group, a crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a thorn, then the Kannada for holy basil.

You'll want to know: ಗುಂಪು.
ಗುಂಪು — gumpu — "a group, a crowd".

The word, taken apart.
ಗುಂಪು (gumpu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1741). DEDR lists gumpu among gumi, gummu, "heap, crowd".

You'll want to know: ಜಾತ್ರೆ.
ಜಾತ್ರೆ — jātre — "a village fair, a temple fair".

The word, taken apart.
ಜಾತ್ರೆ (jātre) comes from Sanskrit yātrā, "journey; festival", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 10456). The y- became j-, as in Prakrit jattā.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೂಜೆ (pūje) is Sanskrit pūjā, "honour, worship", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with Kannada's -e for the Sanskrit -ā (CDIAL 8317).
