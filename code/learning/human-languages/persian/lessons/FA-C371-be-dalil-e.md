## You'll want to know: به دلیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C371-DALIL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**به دلیل** (*be dalil-e*) — "because of, due to".

> جاده به دلیل برف بسته شد.
>
> *jâdde be dalil-e barf baste shod.*
>
> The road was closed because of snow.

Takes a noun with ezafe; with a clause, *be dalil-e inke* … .

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به دلیل** joins the Persian preposition *be* to *dalil*, from Arabic *dalīl* (EtymDB glosses it "guide"): by reason of.
