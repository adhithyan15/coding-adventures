Hindi, chapter 194: A washerman, A cook, A driver, An engineer, A writer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धोबी (dhobī) — a washerman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a tailor, then the Hindi for a barber.

You'll want to know: धोबी.
धोबी — dhobī — "a washerman".

The word, taken apart.
धोबी (dhobī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhauvati, "washes", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6886). CDIAL notes that the forms with -b- began in the east and spread into every language around.

Lesson 5 of 5.
लेखक (lekhak) — a writer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a driver, then the Hindi for an engineer.

You'll want to know: लेखक.
लेखक — lekhak — "a writer".

The word, taken apart.
लेखक (lekhak) is Sanskrit lekhaka, "scribe", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 11102). It is built on likh-, "to write", the root of लिखना.
