## You'll want to know: вспыльчивый
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C472-ANGER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**вспыльчивый** (*vspýlchivyy*) — "quick-tempered".

> Мой дядя добрый, но вспыльчивый.
>
> *Moy dyádya dóbryy, no vspýlchivyy.*
>
> My uncle is kind but quick-tempered.

Built on *вспылить* — to flare up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вспыльчивый* is built on *вспылить* 'to flare up', from *пыл* 'heat, ardour'; EtymDB has no entry, and *пыл* is usually traced to the root of *пылать* 'to blaze'.
