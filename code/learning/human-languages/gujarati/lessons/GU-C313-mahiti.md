## You'll want to know: માહિતી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C313-AFVA313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**માહિતી** — *māhitī* — "information".

> આ માહિતી ખોટી હતી.
>
> *ā māhitī khoṭī hatī.*
>
> This information was wrong.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માહિતી** (*māhitī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *māhiyya*, "what a thing is, its nature": the facts of a matter. It is feminine and does not take a plural: **ખોટી માહિતી**, wrong information.
