## You'll want to know: まう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C503-SONG503-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まう** — *mau* — "to flutter; to dance (in the air)".

> **ゆきが かぜに まっています。** — *yuki ga kaze ni matteimasu* — "The snow is dancing in the wind."

Polite **まいます**, *maimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ゆきが まいました。**, *yuki ga maimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まう** (*mau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
