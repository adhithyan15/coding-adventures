## You'll want to know: परिवार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C494-NATE494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परिवार** — *parivār* — "a household; one's family".

> आमचा परिवार मोठा आहे.
>
> *āmcā parivār moṭhā āhe.*
>
> Ours is a big family.

Masculine.

More formal than कुटुंब.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिवार** (*parivār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *parivāra*, "surroundings; followers" (CDIAL 7876).
