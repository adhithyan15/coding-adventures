## You'll want to know: excepto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C638-LIMITE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**excepto** — "except".

> Abrimos todos los días excepto el lunes.
>
> *We are open every day except Monday.*

Means the same as *salvo* and *menos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*excepto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exceptus* 'taken out', the participle of *excipere*, from *capere* 'to take' (EtymDB).
