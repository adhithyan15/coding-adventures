## You'll want to know: la platea
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C519-TEATRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la platea** — "stalls; audience".

> Abbiamo i posti in platea, in quinta fila.
>
> *We have seats in the stalls, in the fifth row.*

Also the audience as a whole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*platea* is a learned borrowing from Latin *platēa* "a broad street, a courtyard" (EtymDB), from Greek *plateîa* "broad".
