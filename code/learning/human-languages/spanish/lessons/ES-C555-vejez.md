## You'll want to know: la vejez
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C555-VEJEZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la vejez** — "old age".

> Vivió una vejez tranquila en el campo.
>
> *He lived a quiet old age in the country.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vejez* is built on *viejo* 'old' with the ending *-ez*. *Viejo* is inherited from Latin *vetulus*, from *vetus* 'old' (Lewis & Short).
