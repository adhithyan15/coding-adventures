## You'll want to know: आसन्नः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C540-ASANNA540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आसन्नः** — *āsannaḥ* — "close at hand".

> यदि परीक्षा आसन्ना अस्ति, तर्हि अद्य एव पठतु।
>
> *yadi parīkṣā āsannā asti, tarhi adya eva paṭhatu.*
>
> If the exam is close, study today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आसन्नः** (*āsannaḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √sad, "to sit", as its past participle: sat down near; MW gives *āsanna* as seated down, and near, close at hand (MW).
