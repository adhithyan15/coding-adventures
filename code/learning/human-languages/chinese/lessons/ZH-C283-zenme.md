## You'll want to know: 怎么
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C283-ZENME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**怎么** — *zěnme* — "how; in what way".

> **这个字怎么写** *zhège zì zěnme xiě* — How do you write this character?

> **汉语怎么说** *Hànyǔ zěnme shuō* — How do you say it in Chinese?

You have said it inside *zěnmeyàng* and *zěnme bàn*; here it stands alone, before a verb. The next lesson puts the first character in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**怎么** (*zěnme*) is built from 怎 'how' and the ending 么 of 什么. No dictionary we use records an older form of either, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-ZENMEYANG-01, ZH-LEX-C214-HELP214-05, ZH-LEX-C280-ZHEME-01] -->

怎么 goes right before the verb it asks about: 怎么写, 怎么说.

The answer shows the way, or gives it: 这么写, written like this.
