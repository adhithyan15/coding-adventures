## You'll want to know: चन्दनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C331-NADITIRA331-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चन्दनम्** — *candanam* — "sandalwood; sandal paste".

> जनाः ललाटे चन्दनं धारयन्ति।
>
> *janāḥ lalāṭe candanaṃ dhārayanti.*
>
> People wear sandal paste on the forehead.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चन्दनम्** (*candanam*) MW gives *candana* as sandal, the tree, the wood or the fragrant paste made from it, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
