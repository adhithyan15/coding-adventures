## You'll want to know: उखाणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C317-AHER317-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उखाणा** — *ukhāṇā* — "a rhyming couplet; a riddle".

> लग्नात नवरीने एक सुंदर उखाणा घेतला.
>
> *lagnāt navrīne ek sundar ukhāṇā ghetlā.*
>
> At the wedding the bride recited a lovely rhyming couplet.

Masculine.

उखाणा घेणे, to recite one: at a wedding the bride, and often the groom, weaves the spouse's name into a short rhyme.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उखाणा** (*ukhāṇā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *ukhāṇā*, 'a riddle', under Sanskrit *upākhyāna*, 'a subordinate story, an episode' (CDIAL 2289).
