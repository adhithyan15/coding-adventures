## You'll want to know: двигатель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C404-KAPOT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**двигатель** (*dvígatel*) — "an engine, a motor".

> У новой машины тихий двигатель.
>
> *U nóvoy mashíny tíkhiy dvígatel.*
>
> The new car has a quiet engine.

Masculine, though it ends in -ь: *мой двигатель*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*двигатель* is built on *двигать* 'to move' with the ending *-тель*, 'the thing that does it'. *Двигать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dvigati* 'to lift, to move' (EtymDB).
