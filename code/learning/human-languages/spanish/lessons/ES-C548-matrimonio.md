## You'll want to know: el matrimonio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C548-BANQUETE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el matrimonio** — "the marriage; the married couple".

> Su matrimonio duró cincuenta años.
>
> *Their marriage lasted fifty years.*

*Un matrimonio* is also a married couple: *En el quinto vive un matrimonio mayor.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*matrimonio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mātrimōnium* 'marriage, wedlock' (EtymDB), built on *māter* 'mother'.
