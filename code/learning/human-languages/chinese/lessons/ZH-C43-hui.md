## You'll want to know: huí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-02]; assesses=[] -->

*huí* — "to go back; to return".

> *wǒ huí jiā* — I'm going home.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huí* is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to turn'.

## What you've built
