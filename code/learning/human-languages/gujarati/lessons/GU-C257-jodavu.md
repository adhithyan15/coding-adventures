## You'll want to know: જોડાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C257-TEAM257-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જોડાવું** — *joḍāvũ* — "to join".

> **તે અમારા વર્ગમાં જોડાયો.** — *te amārā vargmā̃ joḍāyo* — "He joined our class."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જોડાવું** (*joḍāvũ*), to become joined, is built on *joḍvũ*, "to join, fasten", with the intransitive *-ā-*. CDIAL lists *joḍvũ* under *\*yōṭayati*, "joins", a reconstructed form in its entry *\*yuṭati*, "is joined", and calls the history of the group obscure (CDIAL 10496).
