## You'll want to know: معتدل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C480-NASHRA480-05]; assesses=[] -->

**معتدل** — *muʿtadil* — "mild; moderate".

> الطقس في الربيع معتدل.
>
> *aṭ-ṭaqs fī ar-rabīʿ muʿtadil.*
>
> The weather in spring is mild.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معتدل** (*muʿtadil*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-d-l*, "to be even, balanced" (Lane). It is the Form VIII participle, "evenly balanced".
