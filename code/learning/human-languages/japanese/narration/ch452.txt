Japanese, chapter 452: People at Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ぶちょう (buchō) — a department head.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to take a break, then the Japanese for to work overtime.

You'll want to know: ぶちょう.
ぶちょう — buchō — "a department head".
ぶちょうは かいぎで はなしました。 — buchō wa kaigi de hanashimashita — "The department head spoke at the meeting."

The word, taken apart.
ぶちょう (buchō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are bu, "section" (Middle Chinese buX) and chō, "leader" (trjangX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
