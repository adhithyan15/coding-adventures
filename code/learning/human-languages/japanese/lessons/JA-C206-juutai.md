## You'll want to know: じゅうたい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C206-TRAFFIC206-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうたい** — *jūtai* — "a traffic jam; congestion".

> **あさは どうろが じゅうたいです。** — *asa wa dōro ga jūtai desu* — "The roads are jammed in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうたい** (*jūtai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jū*, "astringent, reluctant" (Middle Chinese *srip*) and *tai*, "to stagnate" (*drjejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
