## You'll want to know: ખૂંચવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C483-VEDNA483-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ખૂંચવું** — *khū̃cvũ* — "to prick; to dig in".

> નવાં ચંપલ પગમાં ખૂંચે છે.
>
> *navā̃ campal pagmā̃ khū̃ce che.*
>
> The new sandals dig into my feet.

Past (it …): તે સોય આંગળીમાં ખૂંચી, *te soy ā̃gḷīmā̃ khū̃cī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખૂંચવું** (*khū̃cvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*khucc-*, "pierce, tear"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to pierce" (CDIAL 3890).
