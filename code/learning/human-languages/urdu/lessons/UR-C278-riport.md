## You'll want to know: رپورٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C278-THANA278-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رپورٹ** — *riporṭ* — "a report (to the police)".

> پولیس نے رپورٹ لکھی۔
>
> *polīs ne riporṭ likhī.*
>
> The police wrote up a report.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رپورٹ** (*riporṭ*) is borrowed from English *report*. At the police station it is the written account of a crime, and it is feminine: **رپورٹ لکھی**.
