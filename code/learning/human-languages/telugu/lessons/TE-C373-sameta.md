## You'll want to know: సామెత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C373-ATMAKATHA373-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సామెత** — *sāmeta* — "a proverb; a saying".

> పెద్దలు ఒక సామెత చెప్పారు.
>
> *peddalu oka sāmeta ceppāru.*
>
> The elders quoted a proverb.

Plural: సామెతలు, *sāmetalu*.

Telugu is rich in proverbs; elders drop them into talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సామెత** (*sāmeta*) is the Telugu word for a proverb; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
