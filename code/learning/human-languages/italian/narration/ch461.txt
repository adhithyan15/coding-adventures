Italian, chapter 461: Gli alberi.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la quercia — the oak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the bush, then the Italian for to wade across.

You'll want to know: la quercia.
la quercia — "the oak".
Dietro casa c'è una quercia di cent'anni. Behind the house there is a hundred-year-old oak.
Forte come una quercia — as strong as an oak.

The word, taken apart.
quercia is inherited from Latin quercea (EtymDB), from quercus 'oak'.

What you've built.
