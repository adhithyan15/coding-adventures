## You'll want to know: exspectātiō, exspectātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C395-DIFFERO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**exspectātiō, exspectātiōnis** — "expectation, anticipation".

> Omnēs in magnā exspectātiōne erant.
>
> *Everyone was in great expectation.*

*exspectātiō, exspectātiōnis* f., the noun of *exspectō*. *Praeter exspectātiōnem*, beyond expectation. English *expectation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exspectātiō* is the noun built on *exspectō* "to wait for" (Lewis & Short), from *ex* "out" and *spectō* "to look": to look out for. English *expectation* comes from it.
