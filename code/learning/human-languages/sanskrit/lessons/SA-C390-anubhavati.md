## You'll want to know: अनुभवति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C390-LAKSHANA390-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुभवति** — *anubhavati* — "experiences; feels".

> यदि भवान् ध्यायति, तर्हि शान्तिम् अनुभवति।
>
> *yadi bhavān dhyāyati, tarhi śāntim anubhavati.*
>
> If you meditate, you feel peace.

Past (he …): सः शान्तिम् अनुभूतवान्, *saḥ śāntim anubhūtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुभवति** (*anubhavati*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √bhū, "to be"; MW gives *anu-√bhū* "to attain, to equal" and *anu-bhūta* "perceived, understood", and "to experience" grows from those senses (MW).
