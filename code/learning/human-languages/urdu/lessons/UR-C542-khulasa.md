## You'll want to know: خلاصہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C542-KHAIR542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خلاصہ** — *khulāsā* — "a summary; the gist".

> خلاصہ یہ کہ اگر وقت ہوا تو ہم آئیں گے۔
>
> *khulāsā yih ki agar vaqt huā to ham āeṅ ge.*
>
> In short, if there's time, we'll come.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خلاصہ** (*khulāsā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khulāṣa*, "an extract, the essence".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
