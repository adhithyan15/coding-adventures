Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪੋਣਾ (poṇā) — a straining cloth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to sift, then the Punjabi for to taste.

You'll want to know: ਪੋਣਾ.
ਪੋਣਾ — poṇā — "a straining cloth".
ਪੋਣਾ ਚਿੱਟਾ ਅਤੇ ਸਾਫ਼ ਹੈ। poṇā chiṭṭā ate sāf hai. The straining cloth is white and clean.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੋਣਾ (poṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit pavana, "sieve, strainer"; CDIAL lists Punjabi poṇā, "strainer, sieve" (CDIAL 7977).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਨਿਤਾਰਨਾ (nitārnā) — to pour off (water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a straining cloth, then the Punjabi for to strain.

You'll want to know: ਨਿਤਾਰਨਾ.
ਨਿਤਾਰਨਾ — nitārnā — "to pour off (water)".
ਮੈਂ ਚੌਲਾਂ ਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਨਿਤਾਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ chaulāṁ dā pāṇī nitārdī hāṁ. I pour off the water from the rice.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਨਿਤਾਰਿਆ, maiṁ pāṇī nitāriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਿਤਾਰਨਾ (nitārnā) is inherited from Sanskrit nistārayati, "delivers from"; CDIAL lists Punjabi nitārṇā, "to clarify" (CDIAL 7532). The sense "pour off" is CDIAL's Lahnda gloss: nitāraṇ, "to skim, decant, pour off".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਪਰੋਸਣਾ (parosṇā) — to serve food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to strain, then the Punjabi for to pour off.

You'll want to know: ਪਰੋਸਣਾ.
ਪਰੋਸਣਾ — parosṇā — "to serve food".
ਮਾਤਾ ਮਹਿਮਾਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਖਾਣਾ ਪਰੋਸਦੀ ਹੈ। mātā mahimānāṁ nū̃ khāṇā parosdī hai. Mother serves food to the guests.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਖਾਣਾ ਪਰੋਸਿਆ, maiṁ khāṇā parosiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਰੋਸਣਾ (parosṇā) goes back to Sanskrit parivēṣati, "attends, serves food", as a word taken from Hindi: CDIAL lists Punjabi parosṇā, "to offer food to a guest", as borrowed from Hindi parosnā; the Punjabi form inherited directly is parīhṇā, "to serve a meal" (CDIAL 7888).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਘੋਟਣਾ (ghoṭṇā) — to mash; to grind smooth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to pour off, then the Punjabi for to serve food.

You'll want to know: ਘੋਟਣਾ.
ਘੋਟਣਾ — ghoṭṇā — "to mash; to grind smooth".
ਦਾਦੀ ਸਾਗ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੇਰ ਘੋਟਦੀ ਹੈ। dādī sāg bahut der ghoṭdī hai. Grandmother mashes the greens for a long time.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਾਗ ਘੋਟਿਆ, maiṁ sāg ghoṭiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਘੋਟਣਾ (ghoṭṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit ghaṭṭayati, "rubs, touches, shakes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ghoṭṇā, "to grind, rub, smoothe" (CDIAL 4417).
