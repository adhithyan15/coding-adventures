## You'll want to know: plausibile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C372-IPOTESI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**plausibile** — "plausible, believable".

> La tua spiegazione è plausibile, ma non mi convince.
>
> *Your explanation is plausible, but it does not convince me.*

One form for both genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*plausibile* is a learned borrowing from Latin *plausibilis* "deserving applause, acceptable" (Lewis & Short), from *plaudere* "to clap the hands".
