## You'll want to know: la aventura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C566-AVENTURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la aventura** — "the adventure; the affair".

> El viaje fue toda una aventura.
>
> *The trip was quite an adventure.*

It can also mean a love affair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aventura* comes from spoken Latin *\*adventūra* 'the things that are going to happen' (EtymDB), from *advenīre* 'to arrive', built on *venīre* 'to come'.
