## You'll want to know: لیگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C512-FANS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لیگ** (*lig*) — "a league".

> تیم ما در لیگ اول شد.
>
> *tim-e mâ dar lig avval shod.*
>
> Our team came first in the league.

*lig-e bartar* — the top league.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lig* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *ligue* or English *league*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
