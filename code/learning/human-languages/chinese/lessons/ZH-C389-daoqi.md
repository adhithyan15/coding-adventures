## You'll want to know: 到期
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C389-INCASE389-05]; assesses=[] -->

**到期** — *dàoqī* — "to fall due; to run out".

> *fángzū xià ge yuè jiù dàoqī le* — The rent falls due next month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**到期** (*dàoqī*) is built from 到 'to arrive' and 期 'a set time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tawH* and *gi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a limit' and 'an appointed meeting'. Middle Chinese *gi* would regularly give a rising tone, qí; the level tone of qī is irregular.
