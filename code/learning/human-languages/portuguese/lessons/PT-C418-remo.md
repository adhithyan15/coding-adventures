## You'll want to know: o remo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C418-BARCO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o remo** — "the oar".

> Um dos remos caiu à água.
>
> *One of the oars fell into the water.*

In sport: *o remo* — rowing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remo* is inherited from Latin *rēmus* 'oar' (EtymDB).
