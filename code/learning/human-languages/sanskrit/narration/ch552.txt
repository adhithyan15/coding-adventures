Lesson 1 of 5.
परिशीलयति (pariśīlayati) — practises regularly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for detached, then the Sanskrit for lets go of.

You'll want to know: परिशीलयति.
परिशीलयति — pariśīlayati — "practises regularly".
यदि त्वं प्रतिदिनं परिशीलयसि, तर्हि त्वं कुशलः भविष्यसि। yadi tvaṃ pratidinaṃ pariśīlayasi, tarhi tvaṃ kuśalaḥ bhaviṣyasi. If you practise every day, you will become good at it.
Past (he …): सः संगीतं परिशीलितवान्, saḥ saṅgītaṃ pariśīlitavān.

The word, taken apart.
परिशीलयति (pariśīlayati) is built on pari and the root √śīl, "to practise, to do repeatedly"; MW gives pariśīlayati as to practise, to use frequently (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: दिनचर्या.
दिनचर्या — dinacaryā — "a daily routine".
यदि दिनचर्या उत्तमा अस्ति, तर्हि स्वास्थ्यम् अपि उत्तमं भवति। yadi dinacaryā uttamā asti, tarhi svāsthyam api uttamaṃ bhavati. If your daily routine is good, your health is good too.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दिनचर्या (dinacaryā) is built from dina, "a day", and caryā, "going about, conduct", from the root √car, "to move"; MW gives dinacaryā as daily work (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for practises regularly, then the Sanskrit for a daily routine.

You'll want to know: प्रयासः.
प्रयासः — prayāsaḥ — "exertion".
यदि प्रयासः सत्यः अस्ति, तर्हि फलम् अवश्यं भवति। yadi prayāsaḥ satyaḥ asti, tarhi phalam avaśyaṃ bhavati. If the effort is real, a result surely comes.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रयासः (prayāsaḥ) is built on pra and the root √yas, "to heat oneself, to exert oneself"; MW gives prayāsa as exertion, effort, pains, trouble (MW).

You'll want to know: प्रयुंक्ते.
प्रयुंक्ते — prayuṅkte — "makes use of".
यदि भवान् संगणकं प्रयुंक्ते, तर्हि कार्यं शीघ्रं भविष्यति। yadi bhavān saṅgaṇakaṃ prayuṅkte, tarhi kāryaṃ śīghraṃ bhaviṣyati. If you use a computer, the work will be quick.
Past (he …): सः संगणकं प्रयुक्तवान्, saḥ saṅgaṇakaṃ prayuktavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रयुंक्ते (prayuṅkte) is built on pra, "forth", and the root √yuj, "to yoke"; MW gives prayuj as to use, to employ, to practise (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उपकारकः (upakārakaḥ) — helpful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for exertion, then the Sanskrit for makes use of.

You'll want to know: उपकारकः.
उपकारकः — upakārakaḥ — "helpful".
यदि उपायः उपकारकः अस्ति, तर्हि अहं तं करिष्यामि। yadi upāyaḥ upakārakaḥ asti, tarhi ahaṃ taṃ kariṣyāmi. If the method is helpful, I will use it.

The word, taken apart.
उपकारकः (upakārakaḥ) is built on upa, "towards", and the root √kṛ, "to do", with the agent ending -aka, like upakāra, "a favour"; MW gives upakāraka as doing a service, helping (MW).
