## You'll want to know: raspeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C323-SCHAELEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**raspeln** — "to grate (coarsely)".

> Sie raspelte den Käse für die Pizza.
>
> *She grated the cheese for the pizza.*

*Reiben* is finer grating.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raspeln* is usually traced to Middle High German *raspen* "to scrape together"; EtymDB lists no parent.
