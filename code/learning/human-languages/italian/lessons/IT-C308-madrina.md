## You'll want to know: la madrina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C308-BATTESIMO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la madrina** — "the godmother".

> La madrina mi ha regalato una catenina d'oro.
>
> *My godmother gave me a little gold chain.*

A new ship has *una madrina* too, who names it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*madrina* is the feminine pair of *padrino*, built on *madre* "mother", from Latin *māter* (Lewis & Short).
