## You'll want to know: ورزشکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C512-FANS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ورزشکار** (*varzeshkâr*) — "an athlete, a sportsperson".

> او ورزشکار است و هر روز تمرین میکند.
>
> *u varzeshkâr ast va har ruz tamrin mikonad.*
>
> She's an athlete and trains every day.

*varzesh* 'sport' + *-kâr* 'one who does'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*varzeshkâr* is built on native Persian parts: *varzesh*, 'sport', from the verb *varzidan*, 'to practise', and *kâr*, 'work', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
