## You'll want to know: इनकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C269-BAHANO269-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इनकार** — *inkār* — "a refusal".

> म्हैं इनकार कियो।
>
> *mhaĩ inkār kiyo.*
>
> I refused.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इनकार** (*inkār*) is usually traced, through Persian *inkār*, to Arabic *inkār*, "denial". With **कियो** it says you refused: **म्हैं इनकार कियो**.
