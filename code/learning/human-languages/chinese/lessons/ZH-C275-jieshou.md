## You'll want to know: jiēshòu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C275-SORRY275-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jiēshòu* — "to accept".

> *wǒ jiēshòu nǐ de dàoqiàn* — I accept your apology.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiēshòu* is built from *jiē* and *shòu*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsjep* and *dzyuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to join' and 'to hold', and the second 'to take in'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
