## You'll want to know: દશા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C377-DASHA377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**દશા** — *daśā* — "a plight; a state of affairs".

> જો આ વરસે પણ વરસાદ ઓછો પડશે તો ખેતરોની દશા ખરાબ થશે.
>
> *jo ā varse paṇ varsād ocho paḍśe to khetronī daśā kharāb thaśe.*
>
> If the rain falls short again this year, the fields will be in a bad way.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દશા** (*daśā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *daśā*, "a state, a condition of life" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
