## You'll want to know: condenar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C522-JUEZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**condenar** — "to sentence, to condemn".

> Lo condenaron a pagar una multa.
>
> *He was sentenced to pay a fine.*

*Condenar a alguien a...* — to sentence someone to...

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condenar* is inherited from Latin *condemnāre* 'to sentence, to condemn' (EtymDB), built on *con-* and *damnāre* 'to condemn'.
