## You'll want to know: adapter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C370-IMPREVU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**adapter** — "to adapt (mostly reflexive, s'adapter)".

> Les enfants s'adaptent vite à une nouvelle école.
>
> *Children adapt quickly to a new school.*

Mostly reflexive: *s'adapter à* — to adapt to. *Adapter un livre* — to adapt a book for the screen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adapter* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adaptāre* "to fit to" (EtymDB), built from *ad* "to" and *aptāre* "to fit", from *aptus* "fitted" (Lewis & Short).
