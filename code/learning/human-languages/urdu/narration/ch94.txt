Lesson 2 of 5.
موسم (mausam) — the weather, a season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for when?, then the Urdu for every day.

You'll want to know: موسم.
موسم — mausam — "the weather, a season".

The word, taken apart.
موسم (mausam) is a loan from Arabic mawsim, "a season, a time of assembly", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is w-s-m (Lane). English monsoon comes from the same Arabic word, through Portuguese.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the weather, then the Urdu for spring.

You'll want to know: گرمیاں.
گرمیاں — garmiyāṅ — "summer".

The word, taken apart.
گرمیاں (garmiyāṅ) is the plural of garmī, "heat", which is usually traced to Persian garmī, "heat", built on garm, "warm".
