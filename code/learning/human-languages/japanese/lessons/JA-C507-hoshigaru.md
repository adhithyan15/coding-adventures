## You'll want to know: ほしがる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C507-HOPE507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ほしがる** — *hoshigaru* — "to want (said of someone else)".

> **もし こどもが ほしがったら、かって ください。** — *moshi kodomo ga hoshigattara, katte kudasai* — "If the child wants it, please buy it."

Polite **ほしがります**, *hoshigarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **いもうとが にんぎょうを ほしがりました。**, *imōto ga ningyō o hoshigarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほしがる** (*hoshigaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built on *hoshii*, "wanted", with the ending *-garu*, "to show signs of"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
