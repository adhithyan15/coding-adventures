## You'll want to know: дежурный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C299-SIRENA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**дежурный** (*dezhúrnyy*) — "the person on duty; on duty".

> Дежурный врач сразу пришёл.
>
> *Dezhúrnyy vrach srázu prishyól.*
>
> The doctor on duty came at once.

An adjective that also serves as a noun: *дежурный* — the one on duty. *Дежурить* — to be on duty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дежурный* is built on French *de jour* "for the day" (EtymDB), with the Russian ending *-ный*: the one whose day it is.
