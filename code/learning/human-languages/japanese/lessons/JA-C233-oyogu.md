## You'll want to know: およぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-02]; assesses=[] -->

**およぐ** — *oyogu* — "to swim".

> **せんしゅう うみで およぎました。** — *senshū umi de oyogimashita* — "Last week I swam in the sea."

Polite **およぎます**, *oyogimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**およぐ** (*oyogu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
