## You'll want to know: गालिचा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C523-GALICHA523-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गालिचा** — *gālicā* — "a carpet; a rug".

> दिवाणखान्यात लाल गालिचा आहे.
>
> *divāṇkhānyāt lāl gālicā āhe.*
>
> There is a red carpet in the living room.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गालिचा** (*gālicā*) is a loan from Persian *qālīča*, "a small carpet".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
