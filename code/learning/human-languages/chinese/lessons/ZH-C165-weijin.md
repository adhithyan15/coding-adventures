## You'll want to know: wéijīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C165-COAT165-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wéijīn* — "scarf".

> *wàibian hěn lěng nǐ yào dài yì tiáo wéijīn* — It's cold outside, so take a scarf.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wéijīn* is built from *wéi* 'to go round' and *jīn* 'a cloth'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *kin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the first is listed there as *hjw+j*, glossed 'to surround': a cloth that goes round the neck.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
