Kannada, chapter 102: This Way, That Way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬೇರೆ (bēre) — other, different.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for please call, then the Kannada for urgent.

You'll want to know: ಬೇರೆ.
ಬೇರೆ (bēre) — "other, different".
ಬೇರೆ ಬೇಕು (bēre bēku) — "I want a different one".

What you've built.
When the first choice is wrong.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bēre]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bēre, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bēre bēku]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for please call, then the Kannada for urgent, then say bēre again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಬೇರೆ (bēre) mean? ("Other, different".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ತಪ್ಪು (tappu) — wrong; a mistake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for urgent, then the Kannada for other.

You'll want to know: ತಪ್ಪು.
ತಪ್ಪು (tappu) — "wrong", and also "a mistake".
ತಪ್ಪು ಆಯ್ತು (tappu āytu) — "I made a mistake", literally "a mistake happened". It is the honest partner of ಸರಿ (sari), "right".

What you've built.
The opposite of sari.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಒಂದೇ (ondē) — only one, the same one.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for wrong, then the Kannada for all.

You'll want to know: ಒಂದೇ.
ಒಂದೇ (ondē) — "only one", or "one and the same". It is ಒಂದು, "one", with the emphatic ‑ಏ of ಅದೇ.

What you've built.
One, made emphatic.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸುಮ್ಮನೆ (summane) — for no reason, idly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for all, then the Kannada for only one.

You'll want to know: ಸುಮ್ಮನೆ.
ಸುಮ್ಮನೆ (summane) — "for no reason, idly, quietly".
ಸುಮ್ಮನೆ (summane) answers ಯಾಕೆ?, "why?", when there is no reason at all.

What you've built.
The answer to why when there is none.
