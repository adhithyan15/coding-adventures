## You'll want to know: घाट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C333-GHAT333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घाट** — *ghāṭ* — "a ghat, steps down to a river".

> माफ़ कीजिए, घाट कैसे जाते हैं?
>
> *māf kījiye, ghāṭ kaise jāte haiṁ?*
>
> Excuse me, how do I get to the ghat?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घाट** (*ghāṭ*) goes back to Sanskrit *ghaṭṭa*, "a landing place" (CDIAL 4414): the steps where people come down to bathe.
