## You'll want to know: गारवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C437-GARVA437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गारवा** — *gārvā* — "coolness; a chill".

> संध्याकाळी हवेत गारवा आला.
>
> *sandhyākāḷī havet gārvā ālā.*
>
> In the evening a chill came into the air.

Masculine.

A pleasant coolness; गारवा येणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गारवा** (*gārvā*) is built on *gār*, "cool, cold", with a noun ending; its origin is not settled.
