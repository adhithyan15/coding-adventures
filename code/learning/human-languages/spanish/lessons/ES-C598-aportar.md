## You'll want to know: aportar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C598-SABER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**aportar** — "to contribute, to bring".

> Cada persona aporta algo al equipo.
>
> *Each person brings something to the team.*

*Aportar ideas* — to contribute ideas. *La aportación* — the contribution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aportar* is inherited from Latin *apportare* 'to carry to, to bring', built from *ad* and *portare* 'to carry' (EtymDB).
