## You'll want to know: مشق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C380-MASHQ380-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مشق** — *mashq* — "practice; an exercise".

> اگر روز مشق کرو گے تو اچھا لکھو گے۔
>
> *agar roz mashq karo ge to achchhā likho ge.*
>
> If you practise every day, you will write well.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشق** (*mashq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mashq*, "writing quickly, practice".
