## You'll want to know: مٹکا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C498-AVA498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مٹکا** — *maṭkā* — "a large clay pot".

> گرمیوں میں ہم مٹکے میں پانی رکھتے ہیں۔
>
> *garmiyoṅ meṅ ham maṭke meṅ pānī rakhte haiṅ.*
>
> In summer we keep water in a big clay pot.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مٹکا** (*maṭkā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *maṭkā*, "an earthen jar", under Sanskrit *mṛdā*, "earth, clay" (CDIAL 10291).
