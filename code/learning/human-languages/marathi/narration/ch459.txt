Marathi, chapter 459: Longing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ओढ (oḍh) — a longing; a pull.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to feel relieved, then the Marathi for to be choked up.

You'll want to know: ओढ.
ओढ — oḍh — "a longing; a pull".
सुट्टीत गावाची ओढ लागते. suṭṭīt gāvācī oḍh lāgte. In the holidays one longs for the village.
Feminine.
ओढ (oḍh) लागणे, to feel drawn to, with the genitive: घराची ओढ (oḍh). It belongs with ओढणे, to pull.

The word, taken apart.
ओढ (oḍh) is a Marathi noun beside ओढणे, "to pull", which goes back to the reconstructed avakaḍḍhati, "pulls away" (CDIAL 14245).

शाळेचा शेवटचा दिवस होता, मनात हुरहूर होती. śāḷecā śevaṭcā divas hotā, manāt hurhūr hotī. It was the last day of school, and there was an ache in the heart.
Feminine.
The ache at an ending or a parting: हुरहूर (hurhūr) लागणे, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
हुरहूर (hurhūr) is a Marathi noun built on a doubled syllable; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
आतुरता (āturtā) — eagerness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a longing, then the Marathi for a wistful unease.

You'll want to know: आतुरता.
आतुरता — āturtā — "eagerness".
निकालाची सर्वांना आतुरता होती. nikālācī sarvānnā āturtā hotī. Everyone was eager for the result.
Feminine.
आतुर, eager, + -ता; आतुरतेने वाट पाहणे, to wait eagerly.

The word, taken apart.
आतुरता (āturtā) is built on the tatsama आतुर, Sanskrit ātura, glossed "suffering, sick" (Monier-Williams); Marathi आतुर has moved to "eager", and -tā makes the noun.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: आसुसणे.
आसुसणे — āsusṇe — "to long for".
पावसासाठी सगळं गाव आसुसलं होतं. pāvsāsāṭhī sagḷaṁ gāv āsuslaṁ hotaṁ. The whole village was longing for rain.
Past (he …): तो आसुसला, to āsuslā.
Takes -साठी, or the -ायला form: भेटायला आसुसणे (āsusṇe), to long to meet.

The word, taken apart.
आसुसणे (āsusṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
झुरणे (jhurṇe) — to pine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for eagerness, then the Marathi for to long for.

You'll want to know: झुरणे.
झुरणे — jhurṇe — "to pine".
दूर गेलेल्या मुलासाठी आई झुरत होती. dūr gelelyā mulāsāṭhī āī jhurat hotī. The mother was pining for her son who had gone far away.
Past (he …): तो झुरला, to jhurlā.
Slow and wearing: एखाद्यासाठी झुरणे (jhurṇe), to pine for someone.

The word, taken apart.
झुरणे (jhurṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi jhurṇẽ, "to waste away", under the reconstructed jhurati (CDIAL 5405).
