## You'll want to know: まもる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C299-OUSAMA299-04]; assesses=[] -->

**まもる** — *mamoru* — "to protect; to guard".

> **さむらいは おうさまを まもっていました。** — *samurai wa ōsama o mamotte imashita* — "The samurai was guarding the king."

Polite **まもります**, *mamorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **さむらいが むらを まもりました。**, *samurai ga mura o mamorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まもる** (*mamoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
