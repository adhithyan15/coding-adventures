## You'll want to know: বেয়াদব
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C459-BINOYI459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বেয়াদব** — *beyādôb* — "rude; ill-mannered (towards elders)".

> বাবা বেয়াদব ছেলেটাকে বকলেন।
>
> *bābā beyādôb chheleṭāke bôklen.*
>
> Father scolded the ill-mannered boy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেয়াদব** (*beyādôb*) is usually traced to Persian *be-adab*, "without manners", where *adab* is Arabic for good manners.
