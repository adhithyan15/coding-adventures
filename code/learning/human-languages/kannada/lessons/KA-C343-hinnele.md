## You'll want to know: ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C343-NIRUPANE343-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ** — *hinnele* — "a background".

> ಈ ಕಥೆಯ ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ ಒಂದು ಹಳೆಯ ಯುದ್ಧ.
>
> *ī katheya hinnele ondu haḷeya yuddha.*
>
> This story's background is an old war.

ಹಿನ್, behind, and ನೆಲೆ, ground: what lies behind. ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ ಸಂಗೀತ is background music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ** (*hinnele*) is a native Dravidian compound: *hin*, "behind" (DEDR 4205), and *nele*, "a footing, a place" (DEDR 3675).
