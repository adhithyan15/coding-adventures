## You'll want to know: डायन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C338-PARI338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**डायन** — *ḍāyan* — "a witch".

> कहानी की डायन जंगल में अकेली रहती थी।
>
> *kahānī kī ḍāyan jaṅgal meṁ akelī rahtī thī.*
>
> The witch in the story lived alone in the forest.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डायन** (*ḍāyan*) goes back to Sanskrit *ḍākinī*, a female attendant of the goddess Kālī (CDIAL 5542), where CDIAL lists Hindi *ḍāin* and *ḍākin*: in village tales, a witch.
