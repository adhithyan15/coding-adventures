## You'll want to know: はたけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C410-JIMOTO410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**はたけ** — *hatake* — "a field (for crops)".

> **そふは はたけで やさいを そだてています。** — *sofu wa hatake de yasai o sodatete imasu* — "My grandfather grows vegetables in his field."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はたけ** (*hatake*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
