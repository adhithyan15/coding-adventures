## You'll want to know: গদি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C509-GALICHA509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**গদি** — *gôdi* — "a cushion; a padded seat".

> এই চেয়ারের গদিটা নরম।
>
> *Ei cheyārer gôdiṭā nôrôm.*
>
> This chair's cushion is soft.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গদি** (*gôdi*) is usually traced to Hindi *gaddī*, "a cushion, a padded seat"; its earlier origin is not settled.
