# ದೇಶೀಯ (dēśīya) — domestic; of the country

## You'll want to know: ದೇಶೀಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C441-SHULKA441-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೇಶೀಯ** — *dēśīya* — "domestic; of the country".

> ದೇಶೀಯ ವಿಮಾನಗಳು ಒಂದನೇ ಟರ್ಮಿನಲ್ನಿಂದ ಹೊರಡುತ್ತವೆ.
>
> *dēśīya vimānagaḷu ondanē ṭarminalninda horaḍuttave.*
>
> Domestic flights leave from terminal one.

Its partner on airport signs is ಅಂತರರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ, international.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೇಶೀಯ** (*dēśīya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dēśīya*, "of the country", built on *dēśa*, "a region, a country" (CDIAL 6547), the source of ದೇಶ.
