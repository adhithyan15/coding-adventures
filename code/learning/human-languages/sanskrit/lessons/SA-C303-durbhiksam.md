## You'll want to know: दुर्भिक्षम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C303-DURBHIKSHA303-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्भिक्षम्** — *durbhikṣam* — "a famine".

> तस्मिन् संवत्सरे दुर्भिक्षम् अभवत्।
>
> *tasmin saṃvatsare durbhikṣam abhavat.*
>
> That year there was a famine.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्भिक्षम्** (*durbhikṣam*) is built from *dur*, "bad, hard", and *bhikṣā*, "alms, food begged"; MW gives *durbhikṣa* as scarcity of provisions, famine (MW).
