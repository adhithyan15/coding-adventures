# ഐ (ai) — the letter that opens ഐക്യം

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-135, ML-SCRIPT-C114-AI-SIGN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **എ** and **ൈ** once each, and name them. (*e* at the start of
a word; the *ai* sign, printed before its consonant, as in **കൈ**.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ഐക്യം** (*aikyaṁ*), "unity". This lesson takes its first letter out and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ഐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C341-AI-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C341-AIKYAM-01] -->

**ഐ** — *ai*, at the start of a word.

After a consonant, *ai* is the sign **ൈ**, printed before the consonant it follows in speech:
**കൈ**, *kai*. At the start of a word it stands as a letter of its own, **ഐ**.

Its left half has the shape of **എ**. The right half is a second piece: a small loop, a short
base, and a tall loop that drops below the line.

## Writing: ഐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C341-AI-01] -->

One recorded hand writes **ഐ** in two strokes. First the left half, as you write **എ**: from the
inner tip, round the small loop and out over the arch to its foot. Then, after one lift, the right
half in one run: up and over its small loop, along the base, up the short stem, down below the
line, and up the tall loop, over its top and down the right side.

Write **ഐ** yourself, slowly, and larger than it is printed. Copy the printed shape and keep the
order above.
