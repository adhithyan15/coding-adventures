8 lessons. All 8 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 8.
el atardecer — dusk, sunset.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the mud, then the Spanish for the raincoat.

You'll want to know: el atardecer.
el atardecer — "dusk, sunset".
El atardecer en la playa es precioso. Sunset on the beach is beautiful.
Also a verb: atardece — evening is falling.

The word, taken apart.
atardecer is built on tarde "afternoon, late", which is inherited from Latin tardē "late" (EtymDB), from tardus "slow".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Después de la lluvia salió un arco iris. After the rain a rainbow came out.

arco iris is built from arco "bow", inherited from Latin arcus (EtymDB), and iris "rainbow", from Latin īris and Greek îris (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: contemplar.
contemplar — "to gaze at, to contemplate".
Contemplamos el paisaje en silencio. We gazed at the landscape in silence.
Slower and calmer than mirar.

The word, taken apart.
contemplar is a learned borrowing from Latin contemplārī "to observe, to gaze at" (EtymDB), built on templum, an open space marked out for watching the sky.
