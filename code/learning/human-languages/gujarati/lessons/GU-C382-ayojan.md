## You'll want to know: આયોજન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C382-DHYEY382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આયોજન** — *āyojan* — "planning".

> જો આયોજન સારું હશે તો કાર્યક્રમ સમયસર પતશે.
>
> *jo āyojan sārũ haśe to kāryakram samaysar patśe.*
>
> If the planning is good, the programme will finish on time.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આયોજન** (*āyojan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āyojana*, "junction, combination" (Monier-Williams); "planning", putting the parts together, is the Gujarati sense.
