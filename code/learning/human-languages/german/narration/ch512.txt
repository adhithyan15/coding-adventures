German, chapter 512: Im Schnee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Schlitten — sledge, sled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to paddle, then the German for canoe.

You'll want to know: der Schlitten.
der Schlitten — "sledge, sled".
Die Kinder fahren mit dem Schlitten den Hügel hinunter. The children sledge down the hill.
Plural: die Schlitten. Schlitten fahren — to go sledging.

The word, taken apart.
Schlitten is inherited from Middle High German slite and Old High German slito "sled", from Proto-Germanic slidô (EtymDB); English sled is a cousin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
rodeln — to toboggan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for canoe, then the German for sledge.

You'll want to know: rodeln.
rodeln — "to toboggan".
Am Sonntag sind wir im Wald gerodelt. On Sunday we went tobogganing in the woods.
Perfekt with sein or haben. Common in southern Germany and Austria; elsewhere also Schlitten fahren.

The word, taken apart.
rodeln is a word from the south of the German-speaking area; its origin is not settled, and EtymDB lists it without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Schlittschuh — ice skate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for sledge, then the German for to toboggan.

You'll want to know: der Schlittschuh.
der Schlittschuh — "ice skate".
Im Winter laufen wir auf dem See Schlittschuh. In winter we go ice-skating on the lake.
Plural: die Schlittschuhe. Schlittschuh laufen — to skate.

The word, taken apart.
Schlittschuh is built on a first part related to Schlitten "sledge" and Schuh "shoe", inherited from Old High German scuoh, from Proto-Germanic skōhaz (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: die Piste.
die Piste — "ski run, slope".
Die Piste war voll mit Skifahrern. The slope was full of skiers.
Plural: die Pisten. Also a runway.

The word, taken apart.
Piste was borrowed from French piste "track, trail", which came from Italian pista; EtymDB has no entry for the German word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Skilift — ski lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for ice skate, then the German for ski run.

You'll want to know: der Skilift.
der Skilift — "ski lift".
Am Skilift mussten wir lange warten. We had to wait a long time at the ski lift.
Plural: die Skilifte. Ski sounds like Schi.

The word, taken apart.
Skilift is built on Ski, borrowed from Norwegian ski "a strip of wood", and Lift, borrowed from English lift; EtymDB lists both German words without a source.
