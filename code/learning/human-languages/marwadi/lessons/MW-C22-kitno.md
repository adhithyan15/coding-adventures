# कितणो — the question word on the page

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 20s] Say *how much*, write **ग**, then write heat.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-KA-01, MW-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-TA-01, MW-SCRIPT-NNA-01, MW-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-PAANI-01] -->

> **कि** + **त** + **णो** → **कितणो** — *kitṇo* — how much

Every unit is old. The **ण** is the same retroflex sign that sits in the middle
of **पाणी**, and it is what makes this word Marwadi rather than the Hindi form
a reader may expect. Write **पाणी** and **कितणो** one under the other and check
that the **ण** is drawn the same way in both.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-KITNO-01, MW-SCRIPT-NNA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **कितणो**, cover it, wait five seconds, and write it. Check the **ि**
sits before **क** and that the last sign carries **ो**.
