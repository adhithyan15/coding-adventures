## You'll want to know: प्रकृति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C446-PRAKRITI446-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रकृति** — *prakṛti* — "nature".

> पहाड़ों में प्रकृति बहुत सुंदर है।
>
> *pahāṛoṁ meṁ prakṛti bahut sundar hai.*
>
> Nature is very beautiful in the mountains.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रकृति** (*prakṛti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *prakṛti*, "the original or natural form of a thing" (Monier-Williams); it also means a person's nature, their temperament.
