# समर्थन करणे (samarthan karṇe) — to justify; to defend (a view)

## You'll want to know: समर्थन करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C347-VIRODH347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**समर्थन करणे** — *samarthan karṇe* — "to justify; to defend (a view)".

> तो नेहमी आपल्या चुकीचं समर्थन करतो.
>
> *to nehmī āpalyā cukīcaṁ samarthan karto.*
>
> He always justifies his mistakes.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने समर्थन केलं, *tyāne samarthan kelaṁ*.

The view defended takes -चं: निर्णयाचं समर्थन करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समर्थन करणे** (*samarthan karṇe*) joins the tatsama समर्थन, Sanskrit *samarthana*, "establishing, supporting an argument" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
