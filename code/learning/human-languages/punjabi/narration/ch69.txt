Punjabi, chapter 69: Stairs, Mirror, Light, Key, Plate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪੌੜੀ (pauṛī) — stairs, a ladder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a cupboard, then the Punjabi for the floor.

You'll want to know: ਪੌੜੀ.
ਪੌੜੀ — pauṛī — "stairs, a ladder".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists ਪੌੜੀ (pauṛī), "step, rung", in its entry for Sanskrit pādu, "foot", while doubting whether such words come from pādu or from the commoner pāda, "foot" (CDIAL 8075).

ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ਾ — shīshā — "a mirror".

The word, taken apart.
ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ਾ (shīshā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian shīsha, "glass". Punjabi uses it for a mirror.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਬੱਤੀ (battī) — a light, a lamp.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for stairs, then the Punjabi for a mirror.

You'll want to know: ਬੱਤੀ.
ਬੱਤੀ — battī — "a light, a lamp".

The word, taken apart.
ਬੱਤੀ (battī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from varti, "wick; lamp" (CDIAL 11359).
