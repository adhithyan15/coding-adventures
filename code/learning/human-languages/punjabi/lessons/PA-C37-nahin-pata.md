## The sentence, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-PATA-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MAINU, PA-NEG-CLAUSE-01] -->

> **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਹੀਂ ਪਤਾ** — *mainū̃ nahī̃ patā* — "I don't know"

**ਪਤਾ** is the new word, and it is a **noun** — the knowledge itself, not an act
of knowing. So the sentence says, word for word, *to me not knowledge*. The same
dative frame again: the third sentence in three lessons to put **ਮੈਨੂੰ** at the
front and leave you out of the subject slot.

Punjabi keeps these two admissions apart, and so should you:

- **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ** — I did not **follow** you
- **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਹੀਂ ਪਤਾ** — I do not **have the fact**
