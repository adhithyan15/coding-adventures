## You'll want to know: a barata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C433-BARULHO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a barata** — "the cockroach".

> Encontrámos uma barata na cozinha.
>
> *We found a cockroach in the kitchen.*

Same spelling as *barata* 'cheap', the feminine of *barato*, but a different word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barata* is inherited from Latin *blatta* 'cockroach, moth' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
