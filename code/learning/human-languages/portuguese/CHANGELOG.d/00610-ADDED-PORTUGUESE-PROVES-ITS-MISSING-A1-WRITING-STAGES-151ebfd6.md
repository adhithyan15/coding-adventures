## Added — Portuguese proves its missing A1 writing stages

Three writing lessons, chapters 256-258, on SPINE-SAY-WHY, where por isso
already lives. None introduces a word or any other knowledge atom; every
Portuguese form in them is taught earlier in sequence.

- **Chapter 256, PT-W256-my-own-line: controlled composition.** One line about
  yourself (name, home, a language) from the closed supply of chamo-me, moro
  and falo, with nothing to copy. The Portuguese-specific check arrives with it:
  the til in pão and irmã is not an accent but a nasal sign, so a hand that
  writes pao for pão has written a word Portuguese does not have.
- **Chapter 257, PT-W257-with-a-clock: timed production.** States what
  portuguese/assessment-spec.md records from CAPLE's own pages (checked
  2026-09-13): ACESSO certifies A1 in four components over about one hour
  thirty-five minutes, scaled 55-100%, and passes on a single 55% overall. It
  says plainly that ACESSO's writing minutes and word counts were not verified
  and quotes none. The twenty-minute form-and-message paper is named as the
  project's own task-shapes/a1.json shape, not CAPLE's. The clocked task is
  that paper's first part only: six form fields against a three-minute timer,
  then at most one minute checking, so the directed time (240 s) stays inside
  the lesson's declared 270 s. It ends on the pass-rule contrast: CAPLE lets a
  strong reader carry a weak writer; this book's contract asks 60% of every
  paper on its own.
- **Chapter 258, PT-W258-four-lines: connected composition.** Four lines to one
  reader, joined with e and por isso. The track has not taught a word for
  because, and por isso points the other way: "for that" looks back at the
  reason, so the reason comes first and the result follows. Swapping the halves
  stays grammatical and reverses the claim, which is what the wrap-up check
  catches.

Writing-stage evidence for Portuguese now runs observe-trace, guided-copy,
delayed-copy, dictation-transcription, controlled-composition,
timed-assessment-production, connected-composition, so nothing is missing at
A1 (or at A2, where connected composition is first required).
