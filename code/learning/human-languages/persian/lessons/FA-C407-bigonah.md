## You'll want to know: بی گناه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C407-JORM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بی گناه** (*bigonâh*) — "innocent".

> قاضی گفت که او بی گناه است.
>
> *qâzi goft ke u bigonâh ast.*
>
> The judge said that he was innocent.

*Bi-* "without" + *gonâh* "guilt". The opposite is *gonâhkâr*, guilty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بی گناه** is native Persian, built from *bi-* "without" + *gonâh* "sin, guilt", usually traced to Middle Persian *wināh*.
