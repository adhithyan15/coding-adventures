## You'll want to know: пешеход
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C412-PESHEKHOD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**пешеход** (*peshekhód*) — "a pedestrian".

> Водитель остановился, и пешеход перешёл улицу.
>
> *Vodítel ostanovílsya, i peshekhód pereshyól úlitsu.*
>
> The driver stopped, and the pedestrian crossed the street.

Masculine; built on *пешком* — on foot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пешеход* is built from *пеш-* 'on foot', as in *пешком*, and *ход* 'a going', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *xodŭ* 'motion' (EtymDB).
