## Part II onward *(sketch)*

Build through everyday location, possession, food, time, family, and routine.
Introduce postpositions with their oblique forms, then the habitual and
progressive aspects, compound verbs, perfective agreement, and subordinate
clauses toward B1. Hindi stays available as a close comparison: shared grammar
must be surfaced without erasing Urdu's script, register, or Persian-Arabic
lexical history.
