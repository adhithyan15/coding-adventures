## You'll want to know: le camping
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C425-CAMPING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le camping** — "the campsite; camping".

> Le camping est à cinq minutes de la plage.
>
> *The campsite is five minutes from the beach.*

In French, *le camping* is also the place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
