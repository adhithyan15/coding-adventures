## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مزارع** (*muzāriʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-r-ʿ*, "to sow" (Lane). *Muzāriʿ* is the Form III participle, "one who works the sown land".
