## You'll want to know: સરહદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C326-ZANDO326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સરહદ** — *sarhad* — "a border; a frontier".

> સૈનિકો સરહદ પર રહે છે.
>
> *sainiko sarhad par rahe che.*
>
> Soldiers are posted on the border.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સરહદ** (*sarhad*) is usually traced to Persian *sarḥadd*, "a frontier", built from *sar*, head, and Arabic *ḥadd*, a limit: the edge of a land. It is feminine: **સરહદ પર**, on the border.
