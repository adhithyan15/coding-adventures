## You'll want to know: चिंता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C258-CHINTA258-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चिंता** — *chintā* — "worry".

> चिंता मत करो।
>
> *chintā mat karo.*
>
> Don't worry.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिंता** (*chintā*) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit *cintā*, "thought, care", taken over unchanged (CDIAL 4816).
