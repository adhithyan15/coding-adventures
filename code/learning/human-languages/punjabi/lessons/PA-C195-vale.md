## Grammar Lens: direct owner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-VALE-MASC-OBLIQUE-195]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-VALA-ASSOCIATIVE-195, PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-04, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-01, PA-PLACE-KAMRE-ROOM-01, PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01] -->

> **ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਕਮਰਾ** — *khiṛkī vālā kamrā* — a room with a window

> **ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ** — *khiṛkī vāle kamre vicc* — in the room with a window

In this second phrase, **ਵਾਲੇ** is masculine singular oblique. It agrees with
masculine singular oblique **ਕਮਰੇ** before **ਵਿੱਚ**. Here **ਵਾਲੇ** is not a
plural or honorific form. This chapter does not extend the pattern to every
noun or postposition.
