Arabic, chapter 460: When things break.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عطل (ʿuṭl) — a breakdown; a fault.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for purchases, then the Arabic for to economise.

You'll want to know: عطل.
عطل — ʿuṭl — "a breakdown; a fault".
كان في السيارة عطل صغير. kāna fī as-sayyāra ʿuṭl ṣaghīr. The car had a small fault.
Plural: أعطال, aʿṭāl.

The word, taken apart.
عطل (ʿuṭl) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-ṭ-l, "to be idle, without work" (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
انكسر (inkasara) — to get broken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a breakdown, then the Arabic for a malfunction.

You'll want to know: انكسر.
انكسر — inkasara — "to get broken".
انكسرت نظارتي في الطريق. inkasarat naẓẓāratī fī aṭ-ṭarīq. My glasses got broken on the way.
Present: ينكسر, yankasir.
kasara breaks a thing; inkasara is what the thing does.

The word, taken apart.
انكسر (inkasara) is built on the native Arabic root k-s-r, "to break" (Lane). It is Form VII, "to be broken".
