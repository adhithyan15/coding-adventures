# ಬ್ಯಾಟರಿ (byāṭari) — a battery

## You'll want to know: ಬ್ಯಾಟರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C448-INDHANA448-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬ್ಯಾಟರಿ** — *byāṭari* — "a battery".

> ನನ್ನ ಫೋನಿನ ಬ್ಯಾಟರಿ ಮುಗಿಯಿತು.
>
> *nanna phōnina byāṭari mugiyitu.*
>
> My phone battery ran out.

In speech ಬ್ಯಾಟರಿ also names an electric torch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬ್ಯಾಟರಿ** (*byāṭari*) is a loan from English *battery*.
