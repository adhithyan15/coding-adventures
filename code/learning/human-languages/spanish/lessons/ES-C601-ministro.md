## You'll want to know: el ministro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C601-GOBIERNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el ministro** — "the minister".

> El ministro habló en la tele.
>
> *The minister spoke on TV.*

A woman is *la ministra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ministro* is a learned borrowing from Latin *minister* 'servant, helper' (EtymDB), built on *minus* 'less' as *magister* is on *magis*: the lesser one who serves (Lewis & Short).
