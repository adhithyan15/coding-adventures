## You'll want to know: じかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C346-KONDO346-04]; assesses=[] -->

**じかい** — *jikai* — "the next occasion".

> **じかいの かいぎは、てんきに よります。** — *jikai no kaigi wa, tenki ni yorimasu* — "Whether the next meeting goes ahead depends on the weather."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じかい** (*jikai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "next" (Middle Chinese *tshijH*) and *kai*, "-times" (*hwoj*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
