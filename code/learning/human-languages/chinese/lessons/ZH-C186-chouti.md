## You'll want to know: chōuti
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C186-THINGS186-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chōuti* — "drawer".

> *jiǎndāo zài chōuti lǐ* — The scissors are in the drawer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chōuti* is built from *chōu* 'to pull out' and *ti* 'a tray'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *trhjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to pull out'; for the second no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. In the word it loses its tone.
