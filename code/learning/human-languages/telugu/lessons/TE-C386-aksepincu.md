# ఆక్షేపించు (ākṣēpiñcu) — to object; to take exception

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to disagree, then the Telugu for an objection.

## You'll want to know: ఆక్షేపించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C386-ABHYANTARAM386-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆక్షేపించు** — *ākṣēpiñcu* — "to object; to take exception".

> కొంత మంది ఆ కొత్త నియమాన్ని ఆక్షేపించారు.
>
> *konta mandi ā kotta niyamānni ākṣēpiñcāru.*
>
> Some people objected to the new rule.

Past (I …): ఆక్షేపించాను, *ākṣēpiñcānu*.

Formal and written; in talk people say అభ్యంతరం చెప్పు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆక్షేపించు** (*ākṣēpiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ākṣepa*, "a drawing together; a throwing away", and also "reviling, objection" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
