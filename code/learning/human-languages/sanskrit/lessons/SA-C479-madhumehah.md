## You'll want to know: मधुमेहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C479-VYADHI479-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मधुमेहः** — *madhumehaḥ* — "diabetes".

> मधुमेहे शर्करां न खादेत्।
>
> *madhumehe śarkarāṃ na khādet.*
>
> With diabetes one should not eat sugar.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मधुमेहः** (*madhumehaḥ*) is built from **मधु**, "honey", and *meha*, "urine", from the root √mih; MW gives "honey-like urine, diabetes" (MW).
