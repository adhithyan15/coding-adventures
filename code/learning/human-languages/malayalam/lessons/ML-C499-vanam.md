## You'll want to know: വനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C499-VANAM499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**വനം** — *vanaṁ* — "a forest".

> ആ വനത്തിൽ ധാരാളം ആനകൾ ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *ā vanattil dhārāḷaṁ ānakaḷ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> There are many elephants in that forest.

A formal word; in speech people say കാട്. The forest department is the വനംവകുപ്പ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വനം** (*vanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vana*, "a forest, a wood" (Monier-Williams).
