## You'll want to know: റിപ്പോർട്ടർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C351-SAMPREKSHANAM351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**റിപ്പോർട്ടർ** — *ṟippōrṭṭar* — "a reporter".

> റിപ്പോർട്ടർ ക്യാമറയുമായി ഗ്രാമത്തിൽ എത്തി.
>
> *ṟippōrṭṭar kyāmaṟayumāyi grāmattil etti.*
>
> The reporter reached the village with a camera.

The spoken word beside the formal ലേഖകൻ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**റിപ്പോർട്ടർ** (*ṟippōrṭṭar*) was borrowed from English *reporter*. Malayalam had already taken റിപ്പോർട്ട് (*ṟippōrṭṭŭ*), "a report", from English; the person who writes one followed.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
