Japanese, chapter 512: When Things Change.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
へんかする (henka suru) — to change; to vary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for how well something works, then the Japanese for the origin.

You'll want to know: へんかする.
へんかする — henka suru — "to change; to vary".
もし てんきが へんかしたら、しあいは ちゅうしでしょう。 — moshi tenki ga henkashitara, shiai wa chūshi deshō — "If the weather changes, the match will probably be called off."
Polite へんかします, henkashimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): まちが へんかしました。, machi ga henkashimashita.

The word, taken apart.
へんかする (henka suru) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun henka with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are hen, "change" (Middle Chinese pjenH) and ka, "change" (xwaeH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
きりかえる (kirikaeru) — to switch (over).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the origin, then the Japanese for to change.

You'll want to know: きりかえる.
きりかえる — kirikaeru — "to switch (over)".
もし さむく なったら、ふゆの ふくに きりかえます。 — moshi samuku nattara, fuyu no fuku ni kirikaemasu — "If it gets cold, I will switch to winter clothes."
Polite きりかえます, kirikaemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): みせが ふゆの ふくに きりかえました。, mise ga fuyu no fuku ni kirikaemashita.

The word, taken apart.
きりかえる (kirikaeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from kiru, "to cut", and kaeru, "to change"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
