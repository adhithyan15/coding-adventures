## You'll want to know: terminare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C278-PRINCIPIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**terminare** — "to finish, to end".

> La lezione termina alle sei.
>
> *The lesson ends at six.*

A formal *finire*; with or without an object: *ho terminato il lavoro*, *lo spettacolo è terminato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terminare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *termināre* "to mark the bounds; to end" (EtymDB), built on *terminus* "boundary, limit".
