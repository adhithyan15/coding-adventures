## You'll want to know: かわく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C161-LAUNDRY161-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かわく** — *kawaku* — "to get dry".

> **なつは せんたくものが よく かわきます。** — *natsu wa sentakumono ga yoku kawakimasu* — "In summer the laundry dries well."

Polite **かわきます**, *kawakimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かわく** (*kawaku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
