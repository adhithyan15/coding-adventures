## You'll want to know: el delincuente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C520-DELITO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el delincuente** — "the offender, the criminal".

> Detuvieron al delincuente en la estación.
>
> *They arrested the offender at the station.*

The same form for a woman: *la delincuente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*delincuente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēlinquēns* 'doing wrong' (EtymDB), the participle of *dēlinquere* 'to fall short, to do wrong'.
