## You'll want to know: بگڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C377-BADTAR377-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بگڑنا** — *bigaṛnā* — "to get worse; to go wrong".

> اگر موسم بگڑا تو ہم نہیں جائیں گے۔
>
> *agar mausam bigṛā to ham nahīṅ jāeṅ ge.*
>
> If the weather turns bad, we will not go.

Past (it …): وہ کام بگڑ گیا, *voh kām bigaṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بگڑنا** (*bigaṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bigaṛnā*, "to be spoilt", under Sanskrit *vighaṭate*, "breaks apart" (CDIAL 11673); **بگاڑنا**, "to spoil", is its partner.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
