## You'll want to know: der Karneval
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C310-KARNEVAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Karneval** — "the carnival".

> Im Februar feiert die Stadt Karneval.
>
> *In February the town celebrates carnival.*

Plural: *die Karnevale*. The name differs by region; in the southwest it is *die Fastnacht*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Karneval* comes from Italian *carnevale*, from Medieval Latin *carnelevale*, built on *caro* "flesh, meat" and *levāre* "to lighten, to raise", here "to take away" (EtymDB): the farewell to meat before Lent.
