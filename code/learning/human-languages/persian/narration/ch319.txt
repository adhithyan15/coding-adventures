Persian, chapter 319: Police and arrests.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مامور (ma'mur) — an officer, an agent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to attack, then the Persian for a weapon.

You'll want to know: مامور.
مامور (ma'mur) — "an officer, an agent".
دو مامور پلیس جلوی در ایستاده بودند. Do ma'mur-e polis jelo-ye dar istâde budand. Two police officers were standing at the door.
Plural ma'murân. Careful print adds a hamza sign over the alef.

The word, taken apart.
ma'mur is borrowed from Arabic ma'mūr (EtymDB), the passive participle "one who is given an order".

Lesson 2 of 5.
گشت (gasht) — a patrol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a weapon, then the Persian for an officer.

You'll want to know: گشت.
گشت (gasht) — "a patrol".
گشت (gasht) پلیس هر شب از کوچه ما رد میشود. Gasht-e polis har shab az kuche-ye mâ radd mishavad. A police patrol goes through our street every night.
From gashtan "to go round". Gasht zadan is "to stroll about".

The word, taken apart.
gasht is the past stem of the native verb gashtan "to turn, to go round", used as a noun. EtymDB lists it without a parent.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دستگیر شدن (dastgir shodan) — to be arrested, to be caught.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a patrol, then the Persian for a suspect.

You'll want to know: دستگیر شدن.
دستگیر شدن (dastgir shodan) — "to be arrested, to be caught".
دزد دو روز بعد دستگیر شد. Dozd do ruz ba'd dastgir shod. The thief was arrested two days later.
dast "hand" + gir "taking": caught by the hand. Present dastgir mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
dastgir joins dast "hand", inherited from Old Persian dasta- (EtymDB), and gir-, the present stem of gereftan "to take". shodan "to become" is inherited too.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بازداشت کردن (bâzdâsht kardan) — to detain, to take into custody.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a suspect, then the Persian for to be arrested.

You'll want to know: بازداشت کردن.
بازداشت کردن (bâzdâsht kardan) — "to detain, to take into custody".
پلیس راننده را برای چند ساعت بازداشت کرد. Polis rânande râ barâ-ye chand sâ'at bâzdâsht kard. The police detained the driver for a few hours.
bâz "back" + dâsht "holding": to hold back. Present bâzdâsht mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
bâzdâsht joins bâz "back, again" and dâsht, the past stem of dâshtan "to hold, to have"; kardan "to do" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.
