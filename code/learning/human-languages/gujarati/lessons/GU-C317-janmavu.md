# જન્મવું (janmavũ) — to be born

## You'll want to know: જન્મવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C317-ITIHAS317-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જન્મવું** — *janmavũ* — "to be born".

> મારા દાદા આ ગામમાં જન્મ્યા હતા.
>
> *mārā dādā ā gāmmā̃ janmyā hatā.*
>
> My grandfather was born in this village.

Past (he …): તે અહીં જન્મ્યો, *te ahī̃ janmyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જન્મવું** (*janmavũ*) is built on *janma*, birth, from Sanskrit *jánman*, "birth" (CDIAL 5113), the word in **જન્મદિવસ**, a birthday. The past is plural for an elder, out of respect: **દાદા જન્મ્યા હતા**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
