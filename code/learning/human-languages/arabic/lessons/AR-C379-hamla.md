## You'll want to know: حملة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C379-HIZB379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حملة** — *ḥamla* — "a campaign".

> بدأت الحملة قبل شهرين.
>
> *badaʾat al-ḥamla qabla shahrayn.*
>
> The campaign began two months ago.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حملة** (*ḥamla*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-m-l*, "to carry, to bear" (Lane). A *ḥamla* is one charge, one carrying forward: an attack, and so a campaign.
