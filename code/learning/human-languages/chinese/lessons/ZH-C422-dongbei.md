## You'll want to know: 东北
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C422-NORTH422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**东北** — *dōngběi* — "northeast; the Northeast (of China)".

> **火车进入了东北** *huǒchē jìnrù le dōngběi* — The train has come into the northeast of China.

The new character, in pieces: 北 — five strokes in two halves: on the left a vertical, a short bar into it from the left and a bar rising to the right at its foot; on the right a stroke falling to the left, then a vertical that bends right along the foot and hooks up. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**东北** (*dōngběi*) is built from 东 'east' and 北 'north', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tuwng* and *pok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'north' and 'to flee'.
