## You'll want to know: अधीते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C363-ADHYAYANA363-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अधीते** — *adhīte* — "studies".

> अरुणः विश्वविद्यालये विज्ञानम् अधीते।
>
> *aruṇaḥ viśvavidyālaye vijñānam adhīte.*
>
> Arun studies science at the university.

Past (he …): सः गणितम् अधीतवान्, *saḥ gaṇitam adhītavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधीते** (*adhīte*) is built on *adhi*, "over", and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives *adhī* "to study, to learn by heart, to read" (MW).
