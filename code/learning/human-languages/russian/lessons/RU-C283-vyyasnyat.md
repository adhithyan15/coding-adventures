## You'll want to know: выяснять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C283-UTVERZHDAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**выяснять** (*vyyasnyát*) — "to find out, to clear up".

> Мы выяснили, кто взял ключ.
>
> *My výyasnili, kto vzyal klyuch.*
>
> We found out who had taken the key.

Imperfective; perfective *выяснить* (*výyasnit*), stressed on *вы-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*выяснять* is built on *вы-* "out" and *ясный* "clear, bright", inherited from Proto-Slavic *ěsnŭ* "clear" (EtymDB): to bring out into the light.
