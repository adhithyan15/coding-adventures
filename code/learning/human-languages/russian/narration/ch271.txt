Russian, chapter 271: Suddenly and at once.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
внезапно (vnezápno) — suddenly, unexpectedly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a task, then the Russian for experience.

You'll want to know: внезапно.
внезапно (vnezápno) — "suddenly, unexpectedly".
Внезапно в комнате стало темно. Vnezápno v kómnate stálo temnó. Suddenly it went dark in the room.
The adverb of внезапный (vnezápnyy) "sudden". A little more bookish than вдруг, and common in stories.

The word, taken apart.
внезапно (vnezápno) is the adverb of внезапный "sudden", built with the old prefix вне-. The origin of the stem -запн- is not settled, and EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 2 of 5.
мгновенно (mgnovénno) — instantly, in a flash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for experience, then the Russian for suddenly.

You'll want to know: мгновенно.
мгновенно (mgnovénno) — "instantly, in a flash".
Он мгновенно понял, что случилось. On mgnovénno pónyal, shto sluchílos. He instantly understood what had happened.
The adverb of мгновенный (mgnovénnyy) "instant". Мгновенно is faster still than быстро.

The word, taken apart.
мгновенно (mgnovénno) is built on мгновение (mgnovéniye) "an instant", which is inherited through Old East Slavic mĭgnovenije from Proto-Slavic mĭgnovenĭje, built on mĭgnǫti "to blink" (EtymDB): as quick as a blink.

Lesson 3 of 5.
тотчас (totchás) — at once, immediately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for suddenly, then the Russian for instantly.

You'll want to know: тотчас.
тотчас (totchás) — "at once, immediately".
Врач тотчас пришёл. Vrach totchás prishyól. The doctor came at once.
Stressed on the end, totchás (tótchas is also heard). Bookish and a little old-fashioned; in speech Russians say сразу. Often strengthened: тотчас же.

The word, taken apart.
тотчас (totchás) joins тот "that" and час "hour", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic časŭ "time" (EtymDB): that very hour.

Lesson 4 of 5.
немедленно (nemédlenno) — immediately, without delay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for instantly, then the Russian for at once.

You'll want to know: немедленно.
немедленно (nemédlenno) — "immediately, without delay".
Он немедленно позвонил домой. On nemédlenno pozvoníl domóy. He phoned home immediately.
Literally "not slowly". Firmer than сразу; common in orders and official notices.

The word, taken apart.
немедленно (nemédlenno) is built on не "not" and медленно "slowly" (EtymDB lists медленно as its source), the adverb of медленный "slow": without lingering.

Lesson 5 of 5.
мгновение (mgnovéniye) — an instant, a moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for at once, then the Russian for immediately.

You'll want to know: мгновение.
мгновение (mgnovéniye) — "an instant, a moment".
На одно мгновение все замолчали. Na odnó mgnovéniye vse zamolcháli. For an instant everyone fell silent.
A neuter noun. В одно мгновение (mgnovéniye) — in an instant; в последнее мгновение (mgnovéniye) — at the last moment.

The word, taken apart.
мгновение (mgnovéniye) is inherited through Old East Slavic mĭgnovenije from Proto-Slavic mĭgnovenĭje, built on mĭgnǫti "to blink", a cousin of мигать "to blink" (EtymDB).
