# દેખાવું (dekhāvũ) — to appear; to be seen

## You'll want to know: દેખાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C333-PARI333-05]; assesses=[] -->

**દેખાવું** — *dekhāvũ* — "to appear; to be seen".

> રાત્રે બારીમાં એક પરી દેખાઈ.
>
> *rātre bārīmā̃ ek parī dekhāī.*
>
> At night a fairy appeared at the window.

Past (she …): તે પરી દેખાઈ, *te parī dekhāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દેખાવું** (*dekhāvũ*) belongs with *dekhvũ*, to see, which CDIAL lists under *\*dṛkṣati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6507): to be seen. What appears is the subject: **પરી દેખાઈ**, a fairy appeared.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
