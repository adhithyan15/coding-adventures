## You'll want to know: દંડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C295-VALANK295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**દંડ** — *danḍ* — "a fine (penalty)".

> પોલીસે ડ્રાઈવરને હજાર રૂપિયાનો દંડ કર્યો.
>
> *polīse ḍrāīvarne hajār rūpiyāno danḍ karyo.*
>
> The police fined the driver a thousand rupees.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દંડ** (*danḍ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *daṇḍa*, "a stick, a rod", the rod of punishment; its verb *daṇḍáyati* means "chastises, fines" (CDIAL 6136). A fine is made with **કરવું**: **દંડ કર્યો**.
