## You'll want to know: पंचर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C406-TAIKSI406-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पंचर** — *pañcar* — "a puncture; punctured".

> रास्ते में टायर पंचर हो गया।
>
> *rāste meṁ ṭāyar pañcar ho gayā.*
>
> The tyre got a puncture on the way.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पंचर** (*pañcar*) is borrowed from English *puncture*, shortened in the mouth; पंचर होना, to get a flat tyre.
