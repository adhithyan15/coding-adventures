# francés / francesa — language and person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-ACADEMIA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] At **una academia de idiomas**, one possible subject is
**francés**, French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-FRANCES]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish **francés** came from French **français**. The language name is the
masculine singular form: **hablo francés**. For people and adjectives, the
ending agrees: **un estudiante francés**, **una estudiante francesa**.

## Grammar Lens: the accent comes and goes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-FRANCES] -->

The final stress of **francés** needs its written accent. Adding **-a** moves
the natural stress: **francesa**, with no accent mark. The plural follows the
same pattern: **franceses, francesas**.
