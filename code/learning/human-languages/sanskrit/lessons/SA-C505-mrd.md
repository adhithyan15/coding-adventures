## You'll want to know: मृद्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C505-MRD505-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मृद्** — *mṛd* — "clay".

> कुम्भकारः नद्याः तीरात् मृदम् आनयति।
>
> *kumbhakāraḥ nadyāḥ tīrāt mṛdam ānayati.*
>
> The potter brings clay from the riverbank.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मृद्** (*mṛd*) is the word MW records for earth, soil and clay, set beside the root √mṛd, "to press, to crush"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
