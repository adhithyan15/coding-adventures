## You'll want to know: विभाग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C513-KARYASHALA513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विभाग** — *vibhāg* — "a department; a division".

> माझी बहीण हिशेब विभागात काम करते.
>
> *mājhī bahīṇ hiśeb vibhāgāt kām karte.*
>
> My sister works in the accounts department.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विभाग** (*vibhāg*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vibhāga*, "distribution, a share" (Monier-Williams).
