## You'll want to know: نزدیک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C409-JANIB409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نزدیک** — *nazdīk* — "close by; near".

> سرائے اسٹیشن کے نزدیک ہے۔
>
> *sarāe isṭeshan ke nazdīk hai.*
>
> The inn is close to the station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نزدیک** (*nazdīk*) is usually traced to Persian *nazdīk*, "near"; **قریب** says the same.
