## You'll want to know: ਢਾਬਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C266-TOWN266-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਾਬਾ** — *ḍhābā* — "a roadside eatery".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਸੜਕ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਢਾਬੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਖਾਣਾ ਖਾਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *asīṁ saṛak de neṛe ḍhābe utte khāṇā khānde hāṁ.*
>
> We eat at a roadside eatery near the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਾਬਾ** (*ḍhābā*) has no settled source: the Punjabi origin remains unknown in the sources used here.
