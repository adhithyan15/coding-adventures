## You'll want to know: پناه بردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C331-VIRAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پناه بردن** (*panâh bordan*) — "to take shelter, to take refuge".

> مردم از باران به زیر پل پناه بردند.
>
> *Mardom az bârân be zir-e pol panâh bordand.*
>
> People took shelter from the rain under the bridge.

*panâh* "refuge" + *bordan* "to carry". Present stem *bar-*: *panâh mibarad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*panâh* "refuge" is inherited from Middle Persian *panāh*, "protector, refuge" (EtymDB); *bordan* "to carry" is inherited Persian too.
