Arabic, chapter 348: Charges and penalties.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
تهمة (tuhma) — an accusation, a charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a ransom, then the Arabic for a kidnapper.

You'll want to know: تهمة.
تهمة — tuhma — "an accusation, a charge".
كانت التهمة سرقة سيارة. kānat at-tuhma sariqat sayyāra. The charge was stealing a car.
Plural: تهم, tuham.

The word, taken apart.
تهمة (tuhma) is built on the native Arabic root w-h-m (Lane), "to fancy, to suspect". In tuhma the w of the root shows as t, as in ittahama.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.
جناية (jināya) — a felony, a serious crime.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a kidnapper, then the Arabic for an accusation.

You'll want to know: جناية.
جناية — jināya — "a felony, a serious crime".
كانت السرقة بالسلاح جناية. kānat as-sariqa bi-s-silāḥ jināya. The armed robbery was a felony.

The word, taken apart.
جناية (jināya) is built on the native Arabic root j-n-y (Lane), where janā is "to gather fruit", and janā ʿalā "to commit an offence against someone": to reap harm for him.

كانت العقوبة سنة في السجن. kānat al-ʿuqūba sana fī as-sijn. The penalty was a year in prison.

عقوبة (ʿuqūba) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-q-b (Lane), "to come after, to follow at the heel". A punishment is what comes after the deed.

Lesson 4 of 6.
عاقب (ʿāqaba) — to punish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a felony, then the Arabic for a penalty.

You'll want to know: عاقب.
عاقب — ʿāqaba — "to punish".
عاقب المعلم الطالب على كذبه. ʿāqaba al-muʿallim aṭ-ṭālib ʿalā kadhibihi. The teacher punished the pupil for lying.
Present: يعاقب, yuʿāqib.
It takes على for the deed.

The word, taken apart.
عاقب (ʿāqaba) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-q-b (Lane), "to follow after". It is Form III, "to requite someone for what he did".

Lesson 5 of 6.
بلغ (ballagha) — to inform the police; to report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a penalty, then the Arabic for to punish.

You'll want to know: بلغ.
بلغ — ballagha — "to inform the police; to report".
بلغ الجار الشرطة عن الصوت. ballagha al-jār ash-shurṭa ʿan aṣ-ṣawt. The neighbour told the police about the noise.
Present: يبلغ, yuballigh.
بلغ (ballagha) عن: to report something. The plain verb, balagha, is to reach.

The word, taken apart.
بلغ (ballagha) is built on the native Arabic root b-l-gh (Lane), "to reach". It is Form II, "to make news reach someone".
