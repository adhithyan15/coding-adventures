## You'll want to know: xiánzhì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C386-CHANGE386-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xiánzhì* — "unused; lying idle".

> *jiā lǐ xiánzhì de dōngxi kěyǐ sòng gěi péngyou* — Things you don't use at home can go to friends.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiánzhì* is built from *xián* 'idle' and *zhì* 'to put', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hean* and *triH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'leisure' and the second 'to set down'.
