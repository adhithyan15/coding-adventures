## You'll want to know: iludir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C471-MENTIRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**iludir** — "to deceive, to fool".

> As fotografias do hotel iludiram os turistas.
>
> *The hotel photos fooled the tourists.*

*Iludir-se* — to fool yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*iludir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *illūdere* "to mock, to make sport of" (Lewis & Short), built on *lūdere* "to play".
