Russian, chapter 474: Worry and Calm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
нервничать (nérvnichat) — to be nervous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for despair, then the Russian for to sigh.

You'll want to know: нервничать.
нервничать (nérvnichat) — "to be nervous".
Перед экзаменом я всегда нервничаю. Péred ekzámenom ya vsegdá nérvnichayu. I always get nervous before an exam.
Imperfective; present нервничаю. Perfective понервничать (for a while).

The word, taken apart.
нервничать (nérvnichat) is built on нервный 'nervous', from нерв 'nerve', which EtymDB traces to Latin nervus 'sinew' (Lewis & Short: a sinew, a tendon).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

тревожный (trevózhnyy) — "anxious; alarming".
У неё был тревожный голос. U neyó byl trevózhnyy gólos. Her voice sounded anxious.
Of a person, anxious; of news, alarming.

The word, taken apart.
тревожный (trevózhnyy) is built on тревога 'alarm, anxiety', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic trьvoga (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
беспокоиться (bespokóitsya) — to worry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be nervous, then the Russian for anxious.

You'll want to know: беспокоиться.
беспокоиться (bespokóitsya) — "to worry".
Родители беспокоятся, когда я поздно прихожу домой. Rodíteli bespokóyatsya, kogdá ya pózdno prikhozhú domóy. My parents worry when I come home late.
Imperfective. Беспокоиться о ком or за кого — to worry about someone. Perfective побеспокоиться.

The word, taken apart.
беспокоиться (bespokóitsya) is built on без 'without' and покой 'rest, peace', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic pokojь (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
успокаиваться (uspokáivatsya) — to calm down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to worry, then the Russian for worry.

You'll want to know: успокаиваться.
успокаиваться (uspokáivatsya) — "to calm down".
После чашки чая она быстро успокаивается. Pósle cháshki cháya oná býstro uspokáivayetsya. After a cup of tea she soon calms down.
Imperfective; perfective успокоиться. Built like спокойный — calm.

The word, taken apart.
успокаиваться (uspokáivatsya) is the reflexive of успокаивать, the partner of успокоить 'to calm', built on покой 'rest', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic pokojь (EtymDB).
