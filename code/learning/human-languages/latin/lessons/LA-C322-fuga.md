## You'll want to know: fuga, fugae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C322-FUGA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**fuga, fugae** — "flight, running away".

> Hostēs sē in fugam dedērunt.
>
> *The enemy took to flight.*

Feminine, from *fugiō* (taught). *In fugam dare*: to put to flight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fuga** belongs with *fugiō*, "to flee" (Lewis & Short), and matches Greek *phygē*, "flight". English *fugitive* comes from the same family.
