## You'll want to know: ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C321-NYAYA321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ** — *nyāyālaya* — "a court of law".

> ಅವನ ಭವಿಷ್ಯ ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯದ ನಿರ್ಧಾರದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *avana bhaviṣya nyāyālayada nirdhārada mēle avalambiside.*
>
> His future depends on the court's decision.

ನ್ಯಾಯ, justice, and ಆಲಯ, a house: the house of justice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ** (*nyāyālaya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *nyāya*, "rule, method, a judicial sentence" (CDIAL 7614), and *ālaya*, "a house, a dwelling" (CDIAL 1366).
