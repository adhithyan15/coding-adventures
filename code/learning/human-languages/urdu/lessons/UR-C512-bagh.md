## You'll want to know: باغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C512-KYARI512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**باغ** — *bāgh* — "a garden".

> ہمارے گھر کے پیچھے ایک باغ ہے۔
>
> *hamāre ghar ke pīchhe ek bāgh hai.*
>
> There is a garden behind our house.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**باغ** (*bāgh*) is usually traced to Persian *bāgh*, "a garden, an orchard".
