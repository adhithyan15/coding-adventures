## You'll want to know: कचरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C524-SAPHAI524-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कचरा** — *kacrā* — "rubbish; litter".

> उद्यानात खूप कचरा पडला होता.
>
> *udyānāt khūp kacrā paḍlā hotā.*
>
> There was a lot of litter lying in the park.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कचरा** (*kacrā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *kacrā*, "rubbish", under Sanskrit *kaccara*, "dirty, vile" (CDIAL 2615).
