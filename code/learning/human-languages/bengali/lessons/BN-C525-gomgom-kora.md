## You'll want to know: গমগম করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C525-SUR525-04]; assesses=[] -->

**গমগম করা** — *gômgôm kôrā* — "to resound; to boom".

> গানের সময় পুরো ঘরটা গমগম করছিল।
>
> *Gāner sômôy puro ghôrṭā gômgôm kôrchhilô.*
>
> During the song the whole room was resounding.

Past (he/she …): সেই ঘর গানে গমগম করল, *sei ghôr gāne gômgôm kôrlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গমগম করা** (*gômgôm kôrā*) joins গমগম, an imitative word for a deep, filling sound, to করা, "to do". No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
