## You'll want to know: கட்சி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C375-KATCI375-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கட்சி** — *kaṭci* — "a political party; a side".

> இந்தத் தேர்தலில் பல கட்சிகள் போட்டியிடுகின்றன.
>
> *intat tērtalil pala kaṭcikaḷ pōṭṭiyiṭukiṉṟaṉa.*
>
> Many parties are contesting this election.

Plural: கட்சிகள், *kaṭcikaḷ*.

Also a side in an argument: அவர் என் கட்சி, he is on my side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கட்சி** (*kaṭci*) is a word whose origin is not settled; it is often compared with Sanskrit *pakṣa*, "a wing, a side" (Monier-Williams), which also names a side in a debate.
