Sanskrit, chapter 399: Holy Pools and Gateways.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ह्रदः (hradaḥ) — a deep pool; a lake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for purifying, then the Sanskrit for sprinkles over.

You'll want to know: ह्रदः.
ह्रदः — hradaḥ — "a deep pool; a lake".
वनस्य मध्ये गभीरः ह्रदः अस्ति। vanasya madhye gabhīraḥ hradaḥ asti. In the middle of the forest there is a deep pool.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ह्रदः (hradaḥ) is the word MW gives for a large or deep piece of water, a lake or pool; MW connects it doubtfully with √hlād, "to refresh", so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
स्नापयति (snāpayati) — bathes (someone); gives a bath to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sprinkles over, then the Sanskrit for a deep pool.

You'll want to know: स्नापयति.
स्नापयति — snāpayati — "bathes (someone); gives a bath to".
माता शिशुं स्नापयति। mātā śiśuṃ snāpayati. Mother gives the baby a bath.
Past (she …): सा शिशुं स्नापितवती, sā śiśuṃ snāpitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
स्नापयति (snāpayati) is built on the root √snā, "to bathe", which gives snāti, "bathes"; its causative, "makes bathe", is to bathe someone, and MW gives the participle snāpita (MW).

मन्दिरे दिव्यः प्रकाशः अस्ति। mandire divyaḥ prakāśaḥ asti. There is a heavenly light in the temple.

The word, taken apart.
दिव्यः (divyaḥ) is built on div, "the sky, heaven", with the ending -ya; MW gives divya as divine, heavenly, celestial (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
तोरणम् (toraṇam) — an arched gateway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gives a bath to, then the Sanskrit for divine.

You'll want to know: तोरणम्.
तोरणम् — toraṇam — "an arched gateway".
मन्दिरस्य द्वारे तोरणम् अस्ति। mandirasya dvāre toraṇam asti. There is an arched gateway at the temple door.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
तोरणम् (toraṇam) is the word MW gives for an arched doorway or portal, often hung with garlands; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it lives on as Sinhala toraṇa, "an arched gateway" (MW; CDIAL 5976).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पताका (patākā) — a pennant; a banner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for divine, then the Sanskrit for an arched gateway.

You'll want to know: पताका.
पताका — patākā — "a pennant; a banner".
तोरणे पताका वायुना चलति। toraṇe patākā vāyunā calati. A pennant flutters in the wind on the gateway.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पताका (patākā) is the word MW gives for a flag, a pennon or a banner; MW gives no derivation, and CDIAL 7726 takes it as probably from a non-Aryan word, so its origin is uncertain (MW; CDIAL 7726).
