Russian, chapter 389: Allowed and forbidden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
запрещать (zapreshchát) — to forbid, to ban.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to break, then the Russian for a violation.

You'll want to know: запрещать.
запрещать (zapreshchát) — "to forbid, to ban".
Врач запрещает мне есть сладкое. Vrach zapreshcháyet mne yest sládkoye. The doctor forbids me to eat sweet things.
Imperfective; the perfective is запретить (запрещу, запретишь). Запрещать кому + infinitive. Запрещено — it is forbidden.

The word, taken apart.
запрещать (zapreshchát) is built from за- and the root of претить "to be repugnant", once "to threaten"; the щ is the Church Slavonic softening of т, where Russian has ч.

Lesson 3 of 5.
разрешать (razreshát) — to allow, to permit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to forbid, then the Russian for a ban.

You'll want to know: разрешать.
разрешать (razreshát) — "to allow, to permit".
Мама разрешает мне гулять до девяти. Máma razresháyet mne gulyát do devyatí. Mum lets me stay out until nine.
Imperfective; perfective разрешить. Разрешать кому + infinitive. It also means to resolve: разрешить спор.

The word, taken apart.
разрешать (razreshát) is built from раз- and решить, from Proto-Slavic rěšiti "to untie, to loosen": to allow is to untie a restriction.

Lesson 4 of 5.
разрешение (razreshéniye) — permission, a permit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a ban, then the Russian for to allow.

You'll want to know: разрешение.
разрешение (razreshéniye) — "permission, a permit".
Для этого нужно разрешение. Dlya étogo núzhno razreshéniye. You need permission for this.
Neuter. Разрешение на работу — a work permit. It is also the resolution of a screen.

The word, taken apart.
разрешение (razreshéniye) is built on разрешить "to allow" with the noun suffix -ение; the root is Proto-Slavic rěšiti "to untie".

Lesson 5 of 5.
позволять (pozvolyát) — to let, to allow; to afford.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to allow, then the Russian for permission.

You'll want to know: позволять.
позволять (pozvolyát) — "to let, to allow; to afford".
Зарплата не позволяет нам купить машину. Zarpláta ne pozvolyáyet nam kupít mashínu. Our pay does not let us buy a car.
Imperfective; perfective позволить. Позволять себе — to afford: я не могу себе это позволить.

The word, taken apart.
позволять (pozvolyát) is built from по- and воля "will, freedom", from Proto-Slavic voľa (EtymDB): to allow someone is to give them their will.
