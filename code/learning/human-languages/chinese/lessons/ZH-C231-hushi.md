## You'll want to know: hùshi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C231-DOCTOR231-04]; assesses=[] -->

*hùshi* — "nurse".

> *tā shì zhèr de hùshi* — She's a nurse here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hùshi* is built from *hù* 'to protect' and *shi* 'a scholar', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huH* and *dzriX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to save' and 'to help'; in the word the second loses its tone.
