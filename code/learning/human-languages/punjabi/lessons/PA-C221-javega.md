# ਜਾਵੇਗਾ, ਜਾਵੇਗੀ — will go

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I will go tomorrow*. Then *Aman will come* and *the train will
come*.

## Grammar Lens: like ਆਵੇਗਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-04] -->

For one other person the ending is the one **ਆਵੇਗਾ** has: **-ੇਗਾ** for a man,
**-ੇਗੀ** for a woman.

> **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ।** — *Aman kallh ghar jāvegā.* — Aman will go home tomorrow.

> **ਕੁੜੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ।** — *kuṛī kallh sakūl jāvegī.* — The girl will go to school tomorrow.
