# মেতে ওঠা (mete oṭhā) — to get carried away; to be swept up in excitement

## You'll want to know: মেতে ওঠা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C439-SUKH439-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মেতে ওঠা** — *mete oṭhā* — "to get carried away; to be swept up in excitement".

> খেলা দেখে ছেলেরা আনন্দে মেতে উঠল।
>
> *khælā dekhe chhelerā ānônde mete uṭhlô.*
>
> Watching the game, the boys got carried away with joy.

Past (he/she …): তিনি গল্পে মেতে উঠলেন, *tini gôlpe mete uṭhlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মেতে ওঠা** (*mete oṭhā*) is *mete*, the -এ form of *mātā*, "to be wild with excitement", which belongs with the Bengali *māt*, "delight", that CDIAL lists under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 9753), with *oṭhā*, "to rise" (CDIAL 1793).
