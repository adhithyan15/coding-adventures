## You'll want to know: मुक्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C350-MUKTI350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मुक्तिः** — *muktiḥ* — "release; freedom".

> बन्दी मुक्तिं याचते।
>
> *bandī muktiṃ yācate.*
>
> The captive begs for release.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुक्तिः** (*muktiḥ*) comes from the root √muc, "to set free"; MW gives *mukti* "setting or becoming free, release, liberation" (MW).
