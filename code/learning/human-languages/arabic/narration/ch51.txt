Arabic, chapter 51: Hair, Foot, Finger, Shoulder, Neck.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شعر (shaʿr) — hair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a mouth, then the Arabic for a tooth.

You'll want to know: شعر.
شعر — shaʿr — "hair".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: shaʿr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: shaʿr, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say shaʿr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a mouth, then the Arabic for a tooth, then say shaʿr again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does شعر (shaʿr) mean? ("Hair".) Say it once more.
