## You'll want to know: غرق شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C330-TUFAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**غرق شدن** (*gharq shodan*) — "to drown; to sink".

> قایق در دریا غرق شد.
>
> *Qâyeq dar daryâ gharq shod.*
>
> The boat sank in the sea.

Of people "to drown", of boats "to sink". Present *gharq mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gharq* "drowning, sinking" is borrowed from Arabic, of the root of *gharaqa* "to drown" (EtymDB lists the Arabic verb). *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian.
