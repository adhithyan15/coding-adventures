## You'll want to know: बोर्ड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C419-TIRAHA419-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बोर्ड** — *borḍ* — "a signboard; a board".

> बोर्ड पर लिखा है कि अस्पताल दाईं तरफ़ है।
>
> *borḍ par likhā hai ki aspatāl dāīṁ taraf hai.*
>
> The board says the hospital is on the right.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बोर्ड** (*borḍ*) is borrowed from English *board*, above all the signboard over a shop or at a turning.
