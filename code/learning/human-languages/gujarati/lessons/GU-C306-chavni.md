## You'll want to know: છાવણી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C306-RAHAT306-03]; assesses=[] -->

**છાવણી** — *chāvṇī* — "a camp".

> ગામના લોકો હવે એક છાવણીમાં રહે છે.
>
> *gāmnā loko have ek chāvṇīmā̃ rahe che.*
>
> The villagers are living in a camp now.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છાવણી** (*chāvṇī*) goes back to Sanskrit *chādana*, "covering", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "huts, a cantonment" (CDIAL 5017): a place of shelters put up for a time.
