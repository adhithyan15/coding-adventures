# ث (se) — the letter inside حادثہ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C270-ZAL-01, UR-LEX-C271-HADSA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ذ once, and name it. (*zāl*.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **حادثہ** (*hādsā*), "an accident". This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ث** — *se*, the sound *s*, the same as **س**.

It is the fourth letter of **حادثہ** (*hādsā*). You already write **ت**: **ث** is that shape with **three dots** above it instead of two. Inside a word it is a single short tooth wearing the three dots. Urdu keeps it for words from Arabic.

## Writing: ث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01] -->

Put your pen on ث and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly, and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C270-ZAL-01, UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **حادثہ** and point at ث inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ث three times, saying *s* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: **حادثہ** once, joined, with three dots on the tooth]
- [YOU READ: **حادثہ** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ث? (**se**, *s*.) What tells it from ت? (Three dots, not two.) Which word did it come from? (**حادثہ**, *hādsā*, "an accident".)
