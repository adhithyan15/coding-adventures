Italian, chapter 459: In banca.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
prelevare — to withdraw (money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to put postage on, then the Italian for to deliver.

You'll want to know: prelevare.
prelevare — "to withdraw (money)".
Devo prelevare al bancomat. I need to withdraw cash at the cash machine.
Prelevare il sangue — to take a blood sample.

The word, taken apart.
prelevare is a learned borrowing from Late Latin praelevāre 'to lift first', from levāre 'to lift' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

addebitare — to charge (to an account), to debit.

You'll want to know: addebitare.
addebitare — "to charge (to an account), to debit".
La banca mi ha addebitato due euro. The bank charged me two euros.

The word, taken apart.
addebitare is built from a- and debito 'debt', a learned borrowing from Latin dēbitum 'what is owed', from dēbēre 'to owe' (Lewis & Short).
