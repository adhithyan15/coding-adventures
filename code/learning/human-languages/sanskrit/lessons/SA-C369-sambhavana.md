## You'll want to know: सम्भावना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C369-BHAGYA369-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भावना** — *sambhāvanā* — "a likelihood".

> यदि आकाशः कृष्णः, तर्हि वर्षायाः सम्भावना अस्ति।
>
> *yadi ākāśaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ, tarhi varṣāyāḥ sambhāvanā asti.*
>
> If the sky is dark, rain is likely.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भावना** (*sambhāvanā*) is built on *sam* and the root √bhū, "to be", in its causative form; MW gives *sam-bhāvanā* "imagination, supposition, assumption", and "a likelihood" is its modern sense (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
