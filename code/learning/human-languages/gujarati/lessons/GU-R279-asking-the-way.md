# Review — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C278-VU-JOIE-01, GU-LEX-C278-ETLE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "you should go to the doctor". (*tamāre dāktar pāse javũ joīe*.) Then "so", the everyday way. (*eṭle*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C279-RIT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C279-WAY-ASK-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C279-WAY-GIVE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C278-REASON-TURN-01, GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-03, GU-LEX-C84-TOWN-02, GU-LEX-C84-TOWN-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Excuse me, where is the station? How far is it?]
- [YOU SAY: How do I get to the station? Will you show me the way?]
- [YOU SAY: Go straight, then turn left at the bridge.]
- [YOU SAY: It's not far, ten minutes. It's opposite the bank.]
- [YOU SAY: How did you come? By bus, because the train was late. Now turn it round, with eṭle.]
