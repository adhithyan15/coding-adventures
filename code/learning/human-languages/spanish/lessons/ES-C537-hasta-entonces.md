## You'll want to know: hasta entonces
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C537-ENTONCES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**hasta entonces** — "until then".

> Hasta entonces, nadie sabía nada.
>
> *Until then, nobody knew anything.*

In a goodbye: *¡Hasta entonces!* — see you then!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hasta entonces* is built on *hasta*, which is usually traced to Arabic *ḥattā* 'until', and *entonces*, from Latin *in tunc* 'at that time' (EtymDB).
