# బాధపెట్టు (bādhapeṭṭu) — to hurt; to upset

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be distraught, then the Telugu for to feel sad.

## You'll want to know: బాధపెట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C496-BADHA496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**బాధపెట్టు** — *bādhapeṭṭu* — "to hurt; to upset".

> నా మాటలు ఆమెను బాధపెట్టాయి.
>
> *nā māṭalu āmenu bādhapeṭṭāyi.*
>
> My words hurt her.

Past (I …): బాధపెట్టాను, *bādhapeṭṭānu*.

For feelings rather than the body: మనసును బాధపెట్టు, to hurt someone deeply.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బాధపెట్టు** (*bādhapeṭṭu*) joins బాధ, "pain", a loan from Sanskrit *bādhā*, to the native verb *peṭṭu*, "to put": to cause someone pain.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
