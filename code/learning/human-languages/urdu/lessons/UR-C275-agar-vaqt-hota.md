# اگر وقت ہوتا (agar vaqt hotā) — what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C274-REAL-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "it depends on the weather", then "it depends on whether it will rain". (*yih mausam par munhasir hai*, *yih is par munhasir hai ki bārish ho gī yā nahīṅ*.) Then "if it rains, we'll stay at home". (*agar bārish huī, to ham ghar par raheṅ ge*.) That may still happen. This lesson is for what did not.

## You'll want to know: -تا in both halves
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C78-TIME78-01, UR-LEX-ANA, UR-LEX-JANA] -->

> **میں آتا۔**
>
> *maiṅ ātā.* — "I would come."

Here, with no **ہوں** (*hūṅ*) after it and no **نہیں** before it, **آتا** (*ātā*) is not "I come": it is "I would come", or "I would have come".

> **اگر وقت ہوتا،**
>
> *agar vaqt hotā,*
>
> **تو میں آتا۔**
>
> *to maiṅ ātā.* — "If I had time, I would come."

Both halves take **-تا** with nothing after it, and each follows its own subject: **ہوتا** for **وقت**, **آتا** for me. The story around it says whether this is now or then.

## You'll want to know: with نہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-02]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-NA-NA, UR-LEX-C77-TIME77-03, UR-LEX-C47-VERBS47-03, UR-LEX-C271-CHHUTNA-01] -->

> **اگر بارش نہ ہوتی،**
>
> *agar bārish na hotī,*
>
> **تو ہم جاتے۔**
>
> *to ham jāte.* — "If it hadn't rained, we would have gone."

**نہ** (*na*), not **نہیں**, says "not" here, as in **نہ … نہ …** (*na … na …*).

> **اگر میں جلدی اٹھتا،**
>
> *agar maiṅ jaldī uṭhtā,*
>
> **تو گاڑی نہ چھوٹتی۔**
>
> *to gāṛī na chhūṭtī.* — "If I had got up early, I wouldn't have missed the train."
