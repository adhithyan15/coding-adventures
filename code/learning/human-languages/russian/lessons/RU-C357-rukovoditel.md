## You'll want to know: руководитель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C357-SOTRUDNIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**руководитель** (*rukovodítel*) — "a manager, a head".

> Мой руководитель часто ездит в командировки.
>
> *Moy rukovodítel chásto yézdit v komandiróvki.*
>
> My manager often goes on business trips.

Masculine, also of a woman. *Научный руководитель* — a thesis supervisor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**руководитель** is built on *руководить*, a compound of *рука* "hand" and *водить* "to lead", from Proto-Slavic *\*voditi* (EtymDB): one who leads others by the hand.
