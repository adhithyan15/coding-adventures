# ఆహ్వానించు (āhvāniñcu) — to invite

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: ఆహ్వానించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C346-VIVAHAM346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆహ్వానించు** — *āhvāniñcu* — "to invite".

> వాళ్ళు మమ్మల్ని పెళ్ళికి ఆహ్వానించారు.
>
> *vāḷḷu mammalni peḷḷiki āhvāniñcāru.*
>
> They invited us to the wedding.

Past (I …): ఆహ్వానించాను, *āhvāniñcānu*.

You know the noun ఆహ్వానం, an invitation; the event takes -కి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆహ్వానించు** (*āhvāniñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *āhvāna*, "calling, an invitation" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
