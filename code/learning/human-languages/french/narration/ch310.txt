French, chapter 310: La jeunesse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'adolescence — adolescence, the teenage years.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the pushchair, then the French for childhood.

You'll want to know: l'adolescence.
l'adolescence — "adolescence, the teenage years".
Son adolescence a été difficile. His teenage years were difficult.
Feminine: une adolescence heureuse.

The word, taken apart.
adolescence is a learned borrowing from Latin adulēscentia "youth" (EtymDB), from adolēscere "to grow up", built on alere "to nourish" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la rentrée — the start of the school year; the return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the teenager, then the French for youth.

You'll want to know: la rentrée.
la rentrée — "the start of the school year; the return".
La rentrée a lieu en septembre. The school year starts in September.
A very French notion: after the summer, everyone goes back to school and to work.

The word, taken apart.
rentrée is built on rentrer "to go back in", from re- and entrer, which is inherited from Latin intrāre "to go in" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
majeur — of age, adult; major.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for youth, then the French for the start of the school year.

You'll want to know: majeur.
majeur — "of age, adult; major".
Il est majeur depuis hier: il a dix-huit ans. He came of age yesterday: he is eighteen.
In France you are majeur at eighteen. La majeure partie — most of.

The word, taken apart.
majeur is a learned borrowing from Latin māior "greater" (EtymDB), the comparative of magnus "great".
