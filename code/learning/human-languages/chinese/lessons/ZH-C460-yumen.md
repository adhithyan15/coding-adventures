## You'll want to know: yùmèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C460-LOW460-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yùmèn* — "down; gloomy".

> *wǒ bìng le yí ge xīngqī hěn yùmèn* — I've been ill for a week, and I'm feeling really down.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùmèn* is built from *yù* 'dense' and *mèn* 'vexed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jut* and *mwonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as thick and blocked and the second 'vexed'.
