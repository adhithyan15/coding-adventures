Gujarati, chapter 228: Swelling and itching.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સોજો (sojo) — swelling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sprain, then the Gujarati for to limp.

You'll want to know: સોજો.
સોજો — sojo — "swelling".
શું તમારા પગમાં સોજો છે? — śũ tamārā pagmā̃ sojo che? — "Is there swelling in your foot?"
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સોજો (sojo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. CDIAL lists it, glossed "a swelling", under śūyate, "is swollen" (CDIAL 12568).
