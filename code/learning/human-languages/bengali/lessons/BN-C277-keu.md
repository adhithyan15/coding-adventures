## You'll want to know: কেউ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C277-SAKSHI277-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কেউ** — *keu* — "someone; anyone".

> দরজায় কেউ ছিল, কিন্তু আমি তার মুখ দেখলাম না।
>
> *dôrjāy keu chhilô, kintu āmi tār mukh dekhlām nā.*
>
> Someone was at the door, but I didn't see their face.

With না it means no one: কেউ এল না, nobody came.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কেউ** (*keu*) comes down from Sanskrit *ko 'pi*, 'someone' (CDIAL 3518, which lists Bengali *keu*): *kaḥ* 'who' with *api* 'even', whoever it may be.
