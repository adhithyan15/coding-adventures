Urdu, chapter 257: Tears, Smile, Affection, Friendship, Shame.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آنسو (ā̃sū) — tears.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a host, then the Urdu for people.

You'll want to know: آنسو.
آنسو — ā̃sū — "tears".

The word, taken apart.
آنسو (ā̃sū) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aśru, "tear" (CDIAL 919). CDIAL notes that the word took a nasal vowel early, as the word for "eye" did.

Lesson 3 of 5.
محبت (mohabbat) — affection, love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for tears, then the Urdu for a smile.

You'll want to know: محبت.
محبت — mohabbat — "affection, love".

The word, taken apart.
محبت (mohabbat) is a loan from Arabic maḥabba, "love, affection", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḥ-b-b (Lane). It is built on the same root as ḥubb, "love".
