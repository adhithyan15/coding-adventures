## You'll want to know: bāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C64-HELP64-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bāng* — "to help".

> *nǐ bāng wǒ xiě hǎo bu hǎo* — Will you help me write it?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bāng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *pang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to mend shoes'. The sense 'to help' is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.
