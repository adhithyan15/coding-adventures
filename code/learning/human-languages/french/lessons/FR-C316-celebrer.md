## You'll want to know: célébrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C316-CELEBRER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**célébrer** — "to celebrate".

> La ville a célébré la fin de la guerre.
>
> *The town celebrated the end of the war.*

More formal than *fêter*; also for a mass or a wedding: *célébrer un mariage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*célébrer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *celebrāre* "to frequent, to celebrate, to honour" (EtymDB), built on *celeber* "crowded, famous".
