## You'll want to know: উপস্থাপক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C299-SOMPROCHAR299-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উপস্থাপক** — *upôsthāpôk* — "a presenter; a host".

> উপস্থাপক ঝড়ের খবর পড়লেন।
>
> *upôsthāpôk jhôṛer khôbôr pôṛlen.*
>
> The presenter read the news about the storm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপস্থাপক** (*upôsthāpôk*) is Sanskrit *upasthāpaka*, 'causing to turn one's attention, reminding' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a presenter', the one who puts the news before you, is the modern one.
