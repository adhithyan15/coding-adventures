## You'll want to know: ಹಂತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C423-HANTA423-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಂತ** — *hanta* — "a stage; a phase".

> ಕೆಲಸದ ಮೊದಲ ಹಂತ ಮುಗಿಯಿತು.
>
> *kelasada modala hanta mugiyitu.*
>
> The first stage of the work is over.

ಹಂತಹಂತವಾಗಿ, step by step.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಂತ** (*hanta*) is a Kannada word whose origin is not settled: neither DEDR nor CDIAL lists it.
