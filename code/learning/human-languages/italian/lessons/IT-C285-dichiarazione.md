## You'll want to know: la dichiarazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C285-COMUNICATO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la dichiarazione** — "the statement, the declaration".

> Il sindaco ha fatto una breve dichiarazione.
>
> *The mayor made a short statement.*

*Fare una dichiarazione* — to make a statement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dichiarazione* is built on *dichiarare* "to declare", inherited from Latin *dēclārāre* "to make clear" (EtymDB), from *clārus* "clear".
