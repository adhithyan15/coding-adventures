Bengali, chapter 86: Shoe, Six, Seven, Eight, Ten.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
জুতো (juto) — a shoe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a ring, then the Bengali for a cap.

You'll want to know: জুতো.
জুতো — juto — "a shoe".

The word, taken apart.
জুতো (juto) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from yukta, "joined, yoked" (CDIAL 10479). CDIAL lists Bengali jutā, "shoe", under it.

ছয় (chhôy) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kṣaṭ, "six", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12803). It is a variant CDIAL reconstructs beside Sanskrit ṣaṣ, "six".
