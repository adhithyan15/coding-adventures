## You'll want to know: હરીફ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C527-AKHADO527-03]; assesses=[] -->

**હરીફ** — *harīph* — "a rival".

> આજે પહેલવાનનો હરીફ તેના કરતાં મોટો છે.
>
> *āje pahelvānno harīph tenā kartā̃ moṭo che.*
>
> Today the wrestler's rival is bigger than he is.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હરીફ** (*harīph*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥarīf*, "a rival, a fellow player", through Persian. The contest itself is **હરીફાઈ**.
