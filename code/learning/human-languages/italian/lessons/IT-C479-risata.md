## You'll want to know: la risata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C479-ALLEGRIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la risata** — "laugh, burst of laughter".

> Abbiamo fatto una bella risata.
>
> *We had a good laugh.*

*Fare una risata* — to have a laugh.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*risata* is built on *riso* "laughter", which is inherited from Latin *rīsus* (Lewis & Short), from *rīdēre* "to laugh"; *-ata* makes one burst of it.
