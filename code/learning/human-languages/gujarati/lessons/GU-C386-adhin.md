## You'll want to know: આધીન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C386-AASTHA386-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આધીન** — *ādhīn* — "subject to; dependent on".

> આ યોજના સરકારની મંજૂરીને આધીન છે; જો મંજૂરી આવશે તો કામ શરૂ થશે.
>
> *ā yojnā sarkārnī manjūrīne ādhīn che; jo manjūrī āvśe to kām śarū thaśe.*
>
> This plan is subject to government approval; if approval comes, work will start.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આધીન** (*ādhīn*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ādhīna*, "dependent on, subject to" (Monier-Williams).
