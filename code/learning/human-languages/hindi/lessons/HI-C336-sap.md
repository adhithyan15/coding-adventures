## You'll want to know: शाप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C336-SHAAP336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शाप** — *śāp* — "a curse".

> ऋषि ने गुस्से में राजा को शाप दिया।
>
> *ṛṣi ne gusse meṁ rājā ko śāp diyā.*
>
> In anger the sage laid a curse on the king.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शाप** (*śāp*) is Sanskrit *śāpa*, "a curse" (Monier-Williams). Speech also says **श्राप**. **शाप देना** is to lay a curse.
