## You'll want to know: die Klimaanlage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C427-KLIMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Klimaanlage** — "the air conditioning".

> Die Klimaanlage im Zimmer macht viel Lärm.
>
> *The air conditioning in the room makes a lot of noise.*

Plural: *die Klimaanlagen*. Short: *die Klima*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Klimaanlage* is built from *Klima* 'climate', from Latin *clima*, from Greek *klíma* 'slope, region' (EtymDB), and *Anlage* 'installation', from *anlegen* 'to lay out' (EtymDB).
