## You'll want to know: la strofa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C518-CANTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la strofa** — "verse, stanza".

> Non ricordo la seconda strofa della canzone.
>
> *I do not remember the second verse of the song.*

Also *la strofe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*strofa* is a learned borrowing, through Latin *stropha*, from Greek *strophḗ* "a turning" (EtymDB, under English *strophe*), the turn of the chorus as it danced.
