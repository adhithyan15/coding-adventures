## You'll want to know: سرحال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C486-JOY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سرحال** (*sarhâl*) — "in good spirits; lively".

> امروز صبح خیلی سرحال هستم.
>
> *emruz sobh kheyli sarhâl hastam.*
>
> This morning I feel full of beans.

*sar* 'head' + *hâl* 'state'. The opposite is *bihâl*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarhâl* is built in Persian on *sar*, 'head', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *hâl*, 'state', a loan from Arabic *ḥāl*.
