# ಆಸ್ಥಾನ (āsthāna) — a royal court

## You'll want to know: ಆಸ್ಥಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C354-SAMRAJYA354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಸ್ಥಾನ** — *āsthāna* — "a royal court".

> ರಾಜನ ಆಸ್ಥಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಕವಿಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಸಂಗೀತಗಾರರು ಇದ್ದರು.
>
> *rājana āsthānadalli kavigaḷu mattu saṅgītagāraru iddaru.*
>
> There were poets and musicians at the king's court.

The king's hall and the people around him: ಆಸ್ಥಾನ ಸಂಗೀತಗಾರ, a court musician.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಸ್ಥಾನ** (*āsthāna*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āsthāna*, "a place, an assembly" (CDIAL 1514).
