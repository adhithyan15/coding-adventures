## You'll want to know: مگدر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C520-AKHARA520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مگدر** — *mugdar* — "an Indian club (for exercise)".

> پہلوان کے پاس دو بھاری مگدر ہیں۔
>
> *pahalvān ke pās do bhārī mugdar haiṅ.*
>
> The wrestler has two heavy clubs.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مگدر** (*mugdar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mudgara*, "a mallet, a club", under which CDIAL lists Hindi *mūgrā*, "a mallet" (CDIAL 10199).
