## You'll want to know: кафедра
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C385-OBRAZOVANIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**кафедра** (*káfedra*) — "a university department; a lectern".

> Он работает на кафедре физики.
>
> *On rabótayet na káfedre fíziki.*
>
> He works in the physics department.

Feminine. A department within a faculty: *на кафедре*. It is also the lecturer’s desk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**кафедра** is usually traced, through Latin *cathedra*, to Greek *kathédra* "a seat, a chair", built from *katá* "down" and *hédra* "a seat": the teacher’s chair, then the department round it.
