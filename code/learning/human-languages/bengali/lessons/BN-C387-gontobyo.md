## You'll want to know: গন্তব্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C387-POTH387-02]; assesses=[] -->

**গন্তব্য** — *gôntôbyô* — "a destination".

> আমাদের গন্তব্য একটা ছোট গ্রাম।
>
> *āmāder gôntôbyô ekṭā chhoṭo grām.*
>
> Our destination is a small village.

A word of signs and tickets as much as of talk: গন্তব্য স্টেশন, the destination station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গন্তব্য** (*gôntôbyô*) is Sanskrit *gantavya*, "to be gone to, to be reached" (Monier-Williams), a form of *gam*, to go: the place you must go to.
