# ভাঙন (bhāngôn) — the eating away of a riverbank; erosion

## You'll want to know: ভাঙন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C290-DHEU290-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাঙন** — *bhāngôn* — "the eating away of a riverbank; erosion".

> নদীর ভাঙনে গ্রামের স্কুলটা নদীতে চলে গেল।
>
> *nôdīr bhāngône grāmer skulṭā nôdīte chôle gelô.*
>
> The river ate away its bank and the village school went into the water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাঙন** (*bhāngôn*) is **ভাঙা**, to break — CDIAL 9353, Sanskrit *bhaṅga* — with the -ন ending that names the act: the breaking away of a riverbank, a word the river delta needs.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
