Italian, chapter 395: Mettere da parte.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il risparmio — the saving; savings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the regular fee, then the Italian for the mortgage.

You'll want to know: il risparmio.
il risparmio — "the saving; savings".
Ho usato i miei risparmi per il viaggio. I used my savings for the trip.
I risparmi — your savings. Built on risparmiare.

The word, taken apart.
risparmio is built on risparmiare "to save", from ri- and an older sparmiare, a form of sparagnare, which EtymDB traces to Frankish sparanjan "to spare" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
l'investimento — the investment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the mortgage, then the Italian for the saving.

You'll want to know: l'investimento.
l'investimento — "the investment".
Comprare casa è un buon investimento, se i prezzi salgono. Buying a house is a good investment, if prices go up.
Fare un investimento — to make an investment.

The word, taken apart.
investimento is built on investire, from Latin investīre "to clothe, to cover" (Lewis & Short), built on vestis "garment"; the money sense, putting money into something, came later.

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'interesse — the interest (on money); the interest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the saving, then the Italian for the investment.

You'll want to know: l'interesse.
l'interesse — "the interest (on money); the interest".
Se lasci i soldi in banca, prendi un piccolo interesse. If you leave the money in the bank, you earn a little interest.
Also curiosity and advantage: nel tuo interesse — in your interest.

The word, taken apart.
interesse is the Latin verb interesse "to be between, to make a difference" (Lewis & Short) used as a noun; in Medieval Latin it meant the compensation owed on a loan. It is built from inter and esse "to be".

Lesson 4 of 5.
il tasso — the rate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the investment, then the Italian for the interest.

You'll want to know: il tasso.
il tasso — "the rate".
Il tasso di interesse è del due per cento. The interest rate is two per cent.
Il tasso di cambio — the exchange rate. A different tasso is the badger.

The word, taken apart.
tasso "rate" is built on tassare "to assess", a learned borrowing from Latin taxāre "to value, to assess" (Lewis & Short). The animal tasso is a different word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'inflazione — the inflation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the interest, then the Italian for the rate.

You'll want to know: l'inflazione.
l'inflazione — "the inflation".
Con l'inflazione alta, i soldi valgono meno. With high inflation, money is worth less.
Literally a swelling: prices blow up.

The word, taken apart.
inflazione is a learned borrowing from Latin īnflātiō "a blowing into, a swelling" (Lewis & Short), from īnflāre "to blow into", built on flāre "to blow"; the economic sense probably came through English.
