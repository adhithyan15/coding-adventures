Lesson 1 of 5.
بزرگسال (bozorgsâl) — an adult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a visa, then the Persian for a form.

You'll want to know: بزرگسال.
بزرگسال — bozorgsâl — "an adult".

The word, taken apart.
bozorgsâl is bozorg, "big", with sâl, "year": big in years. Both are inherited; EtymDB traces bozorg to Middle Persian wuzurg and sâl to Middle Persian sāl.

Lesson 2 of 5.
همکلاسی (hamkelâsi) — a classmate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a form, then the Persian for an adult.

You'll want to know: همکلاسی.
همکلاسی — hamkelâsi — "a classmate".

The word, taken apart.
hamkelâsi is ham-, "same", with kelâs, "class", and -i: one of the same class. ham- is native Persian; kelâs is borrowed from French classe (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
میزبان (mizbân) — a host.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an adult, then the Persian for a classmate.

You'll want to know: میزبان.
میزبان — mizbân — "a host".

The word, taken apart.
mizbân is usually traced to Middle Persian mēzbān: mēzd, "ritual meal" (EtymDB), with -bân, "keeper". The host is the keeper of the feast.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دشمن (doshman) — an enemy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a classmate, then the Persian for a host.

You'll want to know: دشمن.
دشمن — doshman — "an enemy".

The word, taken apart.
doshman is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian dušman, "enemy" (EtymDB). It is usually explained as duš-, "bad", with man, "mind": "ill-minded".
