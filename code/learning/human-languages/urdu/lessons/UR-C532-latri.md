## You'll want to know: لاٹری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C532-QISMAT532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**لاٹری** — *lāṭrī* — "a lottery".

> اگر میری لاٹری نکلی تو میں نیا گھر خریدوں گا۔
>
> *agar merī lāṭrī niklī to maiṅ nayā ghar kharīdūṅ gā.*
>
> If I win the lottery, I'll buy a new house.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لاٹری** (*lāṭrī*) is borrowed from English *lottery*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
