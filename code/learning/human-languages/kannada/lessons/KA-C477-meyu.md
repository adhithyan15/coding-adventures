# ಮೇಯು (mēyu) — to graze

## You'll want to know: ಮೇಯು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C477-PRANI477-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೇಯು** — *mēyu* — "to graze".

> ಹಸುಗಳು ಹೊಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಹುಲ್ಲು ಮೇಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು.
>
> *hasugaḷu holadalli hullu mēyuttiddavu.*
>
> The cows were grazing on grass in the field.

Past (it …): ಮೇಯಿತು, *mēyitu*.

Of cattle and deer; ಮೇಯಿಸು is to take animals to graze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೇಯು** (*mēyu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5093 lists Kannada *mē*, *mēyu*, "to graze, to eat grass".
