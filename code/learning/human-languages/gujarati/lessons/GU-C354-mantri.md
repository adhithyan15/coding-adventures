## You'll want to know: મંત્રી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C354-ADHIKARI354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મંત્રી** — *mantrī* — "a minister".

> મંત્રી કાલે અમારા ગામની મુલાકાત લેશે.
>
> *mantrī kāle amārā gāmnī mulākāt leśe.*
>
> The minister will visit our village tomorrow.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મંત્રી** (*mantrī*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *mantrin*, "wise; a counsellor, a minister" (Monier-Williams).
