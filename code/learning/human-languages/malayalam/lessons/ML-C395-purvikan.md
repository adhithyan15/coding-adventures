## You'll want to know: പൂർവികൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C395-PURVIKAN395-01]; assesses=[] -->

**പൂർവികൻ** — *pūrvikan* — "an ancestor".

> ഞങ്ങളുടെ പൂർവികർ കർഷകർ ആയിരുന്നു.
>
> *ñaṅṅaḷuṭe pūrvikar karṣakar āyirunnu.*
>
> Our ancestors were farmers.

Plural: പൂർവികർ, *pūrvikar*.

Usually in the plural, പൂർവികർ, our forefathers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പൂർവികൻ** (*pūrvikan*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pūrvika*, "former, ancient" (Monier-Williams), from *pūrva*, "before, earlier", with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ.
