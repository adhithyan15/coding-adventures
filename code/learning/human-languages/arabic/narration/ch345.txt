Arabic, chapter 345: Facing danger.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تحدى (taḥaddā) — to challenge, to defy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to come true, then the Arabic for to take revenge.

You'll want to know: تحدى.
تحدى — taḥaddā — "to challenge, to defy".
تحدى الولد صديقه في السباق. taḥaddā al-walad ṣadīqahu fī as-sibāq. The boy challenged his friend to a race.
Present: يتحدى, yataḥaddā.
A challenge is تحد; plural تحديات.

The word, taken apart.
تحدى (taḥaddā) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-d-w (Lane), where ḥadā is to drive camels on with song, and Form V taḥaddā is "to vie with someone, to dare him".

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.
