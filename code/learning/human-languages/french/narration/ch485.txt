French, chapter 485: Tomber malade et guérir.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
guérir — to heal, to cure; to get better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for injection, then the French for waiting room.

You'll want to know: guérir.
guérir — "to heal, to cure; to get better".
Ma grand-mère a guéri de sa grippe en une semaine. My grandmother got over her flu in a week.
Guérir de — to recover from. A regular -ir verb: nous guérissons.

The word, taken apart.
guérir comes from Frankish warjan "to protect, to defend" (EtymDB), a Germanic word; the sense moved from "protect" to "heal".

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
rétablir — to restore; (se rétablir) to recover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to heal, then the French for recovery.

You'll want to know: rétablir.
rétablir — "to restore; (se rétablir) to recover".
Après l'opération, elle s'est vite rétablie. After the operation, she recovered quickly.
Mostly reflexive for health: se rétablir. Rétablir le courant — to restore the power.

The word, taken apart.
rétablir is built from re- and établir "to set up", inherited from Latin stabilīre "to make firm", from stabilis, built on stāre "to stand" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la convalescence — convalescence, recovery period.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for recovery, then the French for to restore.

You'll want to know: la convalescence.
la convalescence — "convalescence, recovery period".
Pendant sa convalescence, il a lu beaucoup de livres. During his recovery he read a lot of books.
Être en convalescence — to be convalescing.

The word, taken apart.
la convalescence is a learned borrowing from Late Latin convalēscentia "regaining of health" (EtymDB), from convalēscere "to grow strong", built on valēre "to be strong" (Lewis & Short).

The word, taken apart.
soigner is built on soin "care", usually traced to a Frankish word sunnija "care, concern" (EtymDB).
