## You'll want to know: lo specchietto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C407-VETRI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lo specchietto** — "the wing mirror, the car mirror".

> Guarda nello specchietto prima di girare.
>
> *Check the mirror before you turn.*

A little *specchio*; *lo specchietto retrovisore* is the rear-view mirror.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*specchietto* is a little *specchio* 'mirror', which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *speclum*, from Latin *speculum* 'mirror' (EtymDB).
