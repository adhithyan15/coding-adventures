## You'll want to know: কৈশোর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C488-SHOISHOB488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কৈশোর** — *koishor* — "adolescence; the early teens".

> কৈশোরে অরুণ ফুটবল খেলত।
>
> *koishore ôrun phuṭbôl kheltô.*
>
> As a teenager Arun used to play football.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কৈশোর** (*koishor*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kaiśora*, "youth, boyhood from ten to fifteen" (Monier-Williams).
