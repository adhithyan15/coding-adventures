Lesson 1 of 7.
حریم خصوصی (harim-e khosusi) — privacy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to upload, then the Persian for technical support.

You'll want to know: حریم خصوصی.
حریم خصوصی (harim-e khosusi) — "privacy".
این برنامه به حریم خصوصی ما احترام نمیگذارد. in barnâme be harim-e khosusi-ye mâ ehterâm nemigozârad. This app does not respect our privacy.
Harim "protected space" + ezafe + khosusi "private".

The word, taken apart.
حریم خصوصی (harim-e khosusi) is literally "the private protected space": harim is usually traced to Arabic ḥarīm "a protected precinct", and khosusi to Arabic khuṣūṣī "particular, private".

کسی حساب ایمیل او را هک کرد. kasi hesâb-e imeyl-e u râ hak kard. Someone hacked his email account.
An English loan + kardan. Hak shodan — to be hacked.

Lesson 3 of 7.
ویروس (virus) — a virus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for privacy, then the Persian for to hack.

You'll want to know: ویروس.
ویروس (virus) — "a virus".
کامپیوترم ویروس گرفته است. kâmpyuteram virus gerefte ast. My computer has caught a virus.
For computers and for illness alike.

The word, taken apart.
ویروس came into Persian from European languages, usually traced through French virus to Latin vīrus "slime, poison" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 7.
به اشتراک گذاشتن (be eshterâk gozâshtan) — to share (online).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to hack, then the Persian for a virus.

You'll want to know: به اشتراک گذاشتن.
به اشتراک گذاشتن (be eshterâk gozâshtan) — "to share (online)".
او عکسهای سفرش را به اشتراک گذاشت. u 'akshâ-ye safarash râ be eshterâk gozâsht. She shared the photos from her trip.
Eshterâk "sharing; subscription" + gozâshtan "to put" (stem gozâr-).

The word, taken apart.
به اشتراک گذاشتن (be eshterâk gozâshtan) is literally "to put into sharing": eshterâk is usually traced to Arabic ishtirāk "sharing, taking part", and gozâshtan "to put" is native Persian.

هوش مصنوعی (hush-e masnu'i) — artificial intelligence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a virus, then the Persian for to share.

You'll want to know: هوش مصنوعی.
هوش مصنوعی (hush-e masnu'i) — "artificial intelligence".
هوش مصنوعی کار خیلی از مردم را عوض میکند. hush-e masnu'i kâr-e kheyli az mardom râ 'avaz mikonad. Artificial intelligence is changing many people's work.
Hush "intelligence" + ezafe + masnu'i "artificial".

The word, taken apart.
هوش مصنوعی (hush-e masnu'i) joins hush "intelligence", which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian word for consciousness, to masnu'i "artificial", built on Arabic maṣnū' "made".
