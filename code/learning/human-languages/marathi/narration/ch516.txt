Lesson 1 of 5.
संगणक (saṅgaṇak) — a computer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an accountant, then the Marathi for to wrap up.

You'll want to know: संगणक.
संगणक — saṅgaṇak — "a computer".
माझ्या भावाने नवीन संगणक घेतला. mājhyā bhāvāne navīn saṅgaṇak ghetlā. My brother bought a new computer.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
संगणक (saṅgaṇak) is a modern Marathi coinage built from Sanskrit saṁ-, "together", and gaṇaka, "one who counts" (Monier-Williams): the machine that reckons.

Lesson 4 of 5.
चित्रफीत (citraphīt) — a video clip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a mobile phone, then the Marathi for a telephone.

You'll want to know: चित्रफीत.
चित्रफीत — citraphīt — "a video clip".
मी सहलीची एक छोटी चित्रफीत बनवली. mī sahlīcī ek choṭī citraphīt banavlī. I made a short video of the trip.
Feminine.
The oblique is चित्रफिती-: चित्रफितीत, in the video.

The word, taken apart.
चित्रफीत (citraphīt) is a Marathi compound of चित्र, "picture", from Sanskrit citra (Monier-Williams), and फीत, "ribbon, tape", a loan from Portuguese fita.

Lesson 5 of 5.
वापरणे (vāparṇe) — to use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a telephone, then the Marathi for a video clip.

You'll want to know: वापरणे.
वापरणे — vāparṇe — "to use".
आजोबांनी पहिल्यांदा भ्रमणध्वनी वापरला. ājobānnī pahilyāndā bhramaṇdhvanī vāparlā. Grandfather used a mobile phone for the first time.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने संगणक वापरला, tyāne saṅgaṇak vāparlā.

The word, taken apart.
वापरणे (vāparṇe) is usually traced to Sanskrit vyāpārayati, "employs". CDIAL lists under it the related Marathi vāvarṇẽ, "to manage, to do the business of" (CDIAL 12206), but not वापरणे itself, so the path is not settled.
