## You'll want to know: भय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C457-BHAY457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भय** — *bhay* — "dread; fear".

> बच्चों को साँप से बहुत भय लगता है।
>
> *baccoṁ ko sā̃p se bahut bhay lagtā hai.*
>
> Children are terribly afraid of snakes.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भय** (*bhay*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *bhaya*, "fear, alarm, dread" (Monier-Williams). It is more formal than *ḍar*.
