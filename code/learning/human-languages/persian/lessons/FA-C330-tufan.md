## You'll want to know: طوفان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C330-TUFAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**طوفان** (*tufân*) — "a storm".

> طوفان درخت های زیادی را انداخت.
>
> *Tufân derakht-hâ-ye ziyâdi râ andâkht.*
>
> The storm brought down a lot of trees.

Also *tufân-e shen* "a sandstorm".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tufân* is borrowed from Arabic *ṭūfān*, "storm, typhoon" (EtymDB).
