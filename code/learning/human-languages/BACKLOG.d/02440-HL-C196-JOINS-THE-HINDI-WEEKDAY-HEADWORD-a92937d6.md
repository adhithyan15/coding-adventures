## HL-C196 — वार joins the Hindi weekday headword

After the gain-noun repair, **वार** is the next independent modern Hindi word
in the ranked queue. The rows before it are already classified script or
morphology fragments, historical ancestors, or components inside declared
forms.

Chapter 10 defines **वार** as “day” and uses it as the productive second half
of all seven weekday names. The lesson introduces the `[deity] + वार` pattern,
guided practice asks learners to produce that pattern, and wrap-up recall asks
what every Hindi weekday ends in and what the ending means. The old headword
declared only the five weekday compounds, leaving the assessed standalone word
invisible to reports and downstream cards.

The headword is now **सोमवार मंगलवार बुधवार गुरुवार शुक्रवार / वार**. This is
a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new vocabulary claim. Rerun the
ranked report next and continue the evidence-first audit after the explicitly
classified script, morphology, and historical-source rows.
