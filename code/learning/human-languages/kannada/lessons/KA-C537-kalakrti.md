# ಕಲಾಕೃತಿ (kalākṛti) — a work of art

## You'll want to know: ಕಲಾಕೃತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C537-KAVYA537-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಲಾಕೃತಿ** — *kalākṛti* — "a work of art".

> ವಸ್ತುಸಂಗ್ರಹಾಲಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಹಳೆಯ ಕಲಾಕೃತಿಗಳು ಇವೆ.
>
> *vastusaṅgrahālayadalli haḷeya kalākṛtigaḷu ive.*
>
> There are old works of art in the museum.

Plural: ಕಲಾಕೃತಿಗಳು, *kalākṛtigaḷu*.

ಕಲೆ, art + ಕೃತಿ, a work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಲಾಕೃತಿ** (*kalākṛti*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *kalā*, "any practical art" (CDIAL 2927), and *kṛti*, "a work, a creation" (Monier-Williams).
