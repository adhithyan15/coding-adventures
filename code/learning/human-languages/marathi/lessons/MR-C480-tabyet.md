## You'll want to know: तब्येत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C480-TABYET480-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तब्येत** — *tabyet* — "one's health".

> आजीची तब्येत आता बरी आहे.
>
> *ājīcī tabyet ātā barī āhe.*
>
> Grandmother's health is better now.

Feminine.

तब्येत बरी नाही, I am unwell; तब्येतीची काळजी, care of one's health.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तब्येत** (*tabyet*) is a loan from Persian *tabiyat*, "nature, health", usually traced to Arabic.
