German, chapter 449: An der Kasse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Kassierer — the cashier.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the small parcel, then the German for the registered letter.

You'll want to know: der Kassierer.
der Kassierer — "the cashier".
Der Kassierer gibt mir die Quittung. The cashier gives me the receipt.
Same form in the plural. A woman: die Kassiererin.

The word, taken apart.
Kassierer is built on kassieren 'to take money in', from Kasse 'till, cash box', which came from Italian cassa, from Latin capsa 'box' (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
kassieren — to take the money, to collect (payment).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the registered letter, then the German for the cashier.

You'll want to know: kassieren.
kassieren — "to take the money, to collect (payment)".
Im Restaurant kassiert der Kellner an jedem Tisch. In the restaurant the waiter takes the money at each table.
Past participle without ge-: er hat kassiert. Colloquial: eine Strafe kassieren — to get a penalty.

The word, taken apart.
kassieren is built on Kasse 'till, cash box' (EtymDB lists the verb), which came from Italian cassa, from Latin capsa 'box' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Bargeld — the cash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the cashier, then the German for to take the money.

You'll want to know: das Bargeld.
das Bargeld — "the cash".
Auf dem Markt zahlt man oft mit Bargeld. At the market people often pay in cash.
No plural. Bar zahlen — to pay cash.

The word, taken apart.
Bargeld is built from bar 'bare, ready', an inherited Germanic word that is a cousin of English bare (EtymDB), and Geld 'money'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Kartenlesegerät — the card reader, the card machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to take the money, then the German for the cash.

You'll want to know: das Kartenlesegerät.
das Kartenlesegerät — "the card reader, the card machine".
Das Kartenlesegerät funktioniert heute nicht. The card machine is not working today.
Plural: die Kartenlesegeräte.

The word, taken apart.
Kartenlesegerät is built from Karte 'card', from Latin charta (Lewis & Short), lesen 'to read', inherited from Proto-Germanic lesaną, and Gerät 'device'.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: das Wechselgeld.
das Wechselgeld — "the change (money given back)".
Sie zählt das Wechselgeld und lächelt. She counts her change and smiles.
No plural. Small coins in a pocket are das Kleingeld.

The word, taken apart.
Wechselgeld is built from Wechsel 'change, exchange', inherited from Old High German wehsal (EtymDB), and Geld 'money'.
