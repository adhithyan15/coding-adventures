## You'll want to know: கொடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C331-KODAI331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கொடை** — *koṭai* — "a donation; a gift".

> இந்தப் பள்ளி ஒரு வணிகரின் கொடை.
>
> *intap paḷḷi oru vaṇikariṉ koṭai.*
>
> This school is a merchant's donation.

From கொடு, to give.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கொடை** (*koṭai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2053 gives it under கொடு, "to give", as "giving away as a gift".
