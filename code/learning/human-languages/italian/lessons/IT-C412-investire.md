## You'll want to know: investire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C412-VIGILE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**investire** — "to run over; to invest".

> Una macchina ha investito un gatto.
>
> *A car ran over a cat.*

With money it means to invest: *investire in una casa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *investīre* 'to clothe, to cover' (EtymDB), built on *vestis* 'clothing'.
