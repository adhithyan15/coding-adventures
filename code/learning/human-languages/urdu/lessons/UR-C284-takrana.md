## You'll want to know: ٹکرانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C284-TAKKAR284-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹکرانا** — *ṭakrānā* — "to collide; to crash into".

> بس ایک ٹرک سے ٹکرائی۔
>
> *bas ek ṭrak se ṭakrāī.*
>
> The bus crashed into a lorry.

Past (it …): وہ گاڑی ٹکرائی, *voh gāṛī ṭakrāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹکرانا** (*ṭakrānā*) is built on *ṭakkar*, "a blow", which CDIAL lists for Hindi under *ṭakkarā* (CDIAL 5424). What you crash into takes **سے**: **ٹرک سے ٹکرائی**.

## What you've built
