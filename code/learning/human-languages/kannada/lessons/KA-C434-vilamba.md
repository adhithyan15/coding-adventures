## You'll want to know: ವಿಳಂಬ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C434-VILAMBA434-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಳಂಬ** — *viḷamba* — "a delay".

> ಮಳೆಯ ಕಾರಣ ರೈಲು ಎರಡು ಗಂಟೆ ವಿಳಂಬವಾಯಿತು.
>
> *maḷeya kāraṇa railu eraḍu gaṇṭe viḷambavāyitu.*
>
> Because of the rain the train was two hours late.

With ಆಗು it says that something ran late: ವಿಳಂಬವಾಯಿತು, it was delayed. In speech people say ತಡವಾಯಿತು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಳಂಬ** (*viḷamba*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vilamba*, "delay" (CDIAL 11890), from the root *lamb*, "to hang down, to lag behind". Kannada writes its *l* as ಳ.
