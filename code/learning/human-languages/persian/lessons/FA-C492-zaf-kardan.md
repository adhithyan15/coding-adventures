## You'll want to know: ضعف کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C492-UNWELL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ضعف کردن** (*za'f kardan*) — "to feel faint (from hunger)".

> صبحانه نخوردم و ضعف کردم.
>
> *sobhâne nakhordam va za'f kardam.*
>
> I skipped breakfast and felt faint.

*za'f* 'weakness' + *kardan*. The adjective is *za'if* 'weak'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*za'f* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *ḍaʿf*, 'weakness'. EtymDB has no entry for it. *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
