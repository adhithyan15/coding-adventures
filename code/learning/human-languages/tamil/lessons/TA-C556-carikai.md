## You'll want to know: சரிகை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C556-POTTI556-05]; assesses=[] -->

**சரிகை** — *carikai* — "gold thread; zari".

> சேலையின் ஓரத்தில் சரிகை இருந்தால் விலை அதிகம்.
>
> *cēlaiyiṉ ōrattil carikai iruntāl vilai atikam.*
>
> If there is gold thread on the border of the sari, the price is higher.

The gold or silver thread woven into the border of a silk sari.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **சரிகை** (*carikai*) is not settled: it may be the same word as *zarī* in other Indian languages, but no source we can check shows the path.
