## You'll want to know: فساد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C304-FASAD304-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فساد** — *fasād* — "a riot; strife".

> شہر میں فساد ہوا۔
>
> *shahr meṅ fasād huā.*
>
> There was a riot in the city.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فساد** (*fasād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fasād*, "corruption, disorder"; in Urdu it is most often violence between groups.
