Marathi, chapter 384: Situations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परिस्थिती (paristhitī) — circumstances; a situation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a trend, then the Marathi for to size up.

You'll want to know: परिस्थिती.
परिस्थिती — paristhitī — "circumstances; a situation".
घरची परिस्थिती चांगली नव्हती, तरीही तो शिकला. gharcī paristhitī cāṅglī navhatī, tarīhī to śiklā. Things were hard at home; even so, he studied.
Feminine.
Feminine and singular: घरची परिस्थिती (paristhitī), the situation at home.

The word, taken apart.
परिस्थिती (paristhitī) is a learned word built from Sanskrit pari-, "around", and sthiti, "standing, state" (Monier-Williams): how things stand all round.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पेच (pec) — a tight spot; a dilemma.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a state, then the Marathi for a plight.

You'll want to know: पेच.
पेच — pec — "a tight spot; a dilemma".
कोणता पर्याय निवडावा, हा मोठा पेच होता. koṇtā paryāy nivḍāvā, hā moṭhā pec hotā. Which option to choose was a real dilemma.
Masculine.
पेचात पडणे, to land in a fix; also a screw thread, a twist.

The word, taken apart.
पेच (pec) is a loan from Persian pech, "a twist, a coil": a problem that turns back on itself.

Warm-up.

कठीण काळ हीच मैत्रीची खरी कसोटी असते. kaṭhīṇ kāḷ hīc maitrīcī kharī kasoṭī aste. Hard times are the true test of friendship.
Feminine.
कसोटीला उतरणे, to stand the test.

The word, taken apart.
कसोटी (kasoṭī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi kasoṭī, "touchstone", under Sanskrit kaṣapaṭṭikā, "touchstone" (CDIAL 2973).
