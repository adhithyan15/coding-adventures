## You'll want to know: आदळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C296-DHADAK296-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आदळणे** — *ādaḷṇe* — "to slam into; to dash against".

> सायकल घसरली आणि तो खांबावर आदळला.
>
> *sāykal ghasarlī āṇi to khāmbāvar ādaḷlā.*
>
> The bicycle skidded and he slammed into a pole.

Past (he …): तो आदळला, *to ādaḷlā*.

The thing struck takes -वर: जमिनीवर आदळणे, to hit the ground hard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आदळणे** (*ādaḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *adaḷṇẽ*, 'to dash down or against; to rush against', beside *ādaḷṇẽ*, under *\*ādalati*, 'crushes' (CDIAL 1144).
