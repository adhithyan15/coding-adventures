## You'll want to know: la gratitud
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C720-GRACIAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la gratitud** — "gratitude".

> Quiero expresar mi gratitud a todos.
>
> *I want to express my gratitude to everyone.*

More formal than *el agradecimiento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gratitud* is a learned borrowing from Latin *grātitūdō* (EtymDB), built on *grātus* "thankful, pleasing".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
