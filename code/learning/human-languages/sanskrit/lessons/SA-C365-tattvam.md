## You'll want to know: तत्त्वम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C365-SIDDHANTA365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्त्वम्** — *tattvam* — "reality; the truth of a thing".

> पण्डितः धर्मस्य तत्त्वं जानाति।
>
> *paṇḍitaḥ dharmasya tattvaṃ jānāti.*
>
> The scholar knows the true nature of dharma.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्त्वम्** (*tattvam*) is built from *tat*, "that", with the abstract ending *-tva*; MW gives *tat-tva* "true or real state, truth, reality" (MW).
