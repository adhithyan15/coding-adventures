Italian, chapter 285: Le parole ufficiali.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la dichiarazione — the statement, the declaration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to testify, then the Italian for to report.

You'll want to know: la dichiarazione.
la dichiarazione — "the statement, the declaration".
Il sindaco ha fatto una breve dichiarazione. The mayor made a short statement.
Fare una dichiarazione — to make a statement.

The word, taken apart.
dichiarazione is built on dichiarare "to declare", inherited from Latin dēclārāre "to make clear" (EtymDB), from clārus "clear".

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: il portavoce.
il portavoce — "the spokesperson".
Il portavoce non ha voluto rispondere. The spokesperson would not answer.
One form for a man or a woman; plural unchanged: i portavoce.

The word, taken apart.
portavoce joins porta, from portare "to carry", and voce "voice", from Latin vōx (Lewis & Short): the one who carries the voice.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la conferenza stampa — the press conference.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the statement, then the Italian for the spokesperson.

You'll want to know: la conferenza stampa.
la conferenza stampa — "the press conference".
Alle undici c'è una conferenza stampa. There is a press conference at eleven.
La stampa is the press; una conferenza on its own is a lecture or a talk.

The word, taken apart.
conferenza is a learned word usually traced to Medieval Latin cōnferentia, built on cōnferre "to bring together" (Lewis & Short); stampa "the press" is built on stampare "to print", which EtymDB traces to a Frankish verb.

Lesson 4 of 5.
il comunicato — the press release, the official bulletin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the spokesperson, then the Italian for the press conference.

You'll want to know: il comunicato.
il comunicato — "the press release, the official bulletin".
L'azienda ha mandato un comunicato ai giornali. The company sent a press release to the papers.
Un comunicato stampa — a press release.

The word, taken apart.
comunicato is the past participle of comunicare "to make known", a learned borrowing from Latin commūnicāre "to share", built on commūnis "common" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
la smentita — the denial (of a report).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the press conference, then the Italian for the press release.

You'll want to know: la smentita.
la smentita — "the denial (of a report)".
Il giorno dopo è arrivata la smentita. The next day the denial came.
Official and journalistic: a statement that a story is not true.

The word, taken apart.
smentita is built on smentire "to give the lie to", from s- and mentire "to lie", which is inherited from Latin mentīrī (Lewis & Short).
