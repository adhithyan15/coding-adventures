## You'll want to know: copiar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C398-FICHEIRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**copiar** — "to copy".

> Copiei as fotos para o portátil.
>
> *I copied the photos to the laptop.*

*Copiar num exame* — to cheat in an exam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*copiar* is built on *cópia* "copy", from Medieval Latin *copia* "transcript" (EtymDB), from Latin *cōpia* "plenty, abundance" (Lewis & Short): a copy multiplies a text.
