## You'll want to know: la senda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C687-EXCURSION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la senda** — "the path, the trail".

> La senda sube por el bosque.
>
> *The path goes up through the woods.*

Close to *el sendero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*senda* is inherited from Latin *sēmita* "a narrow way, a footpath" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
