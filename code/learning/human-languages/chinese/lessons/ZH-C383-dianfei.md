## You'll want to know: diànfèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C383-ENERGY383-01]; assesses=[] -->

*diànfèi* — "electricity bill".

> *xiàtiān kāi kōngtiáo diànfèi hěn guì* — In summer, with the air conditioning on, the electricity bill is high.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diànfèi* is built from 电 'electricity' and *fèi* 'a cost', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'lightning' and the second 'to use up'.
