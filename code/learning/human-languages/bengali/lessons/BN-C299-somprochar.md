## You'll want to know: সম্প্রচার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C299-SOMPROCHAR299-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্প্রচার** — *sômprôchār* — "a broadcast".

> রেডিওতে সকাল আটটায় খবরের সম্প্রচার হয়।
>
> *reḍiote sôkāl āṭṭāy khôbôrer sômprôchār hôy.*
>
> The news is broadcast on the radio at eight in the morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্প্রচার** (*sômprôchār*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit *sam-*, 'together', and *pracāra*, 'a going forth, a spreading' (Monier-Williams): a message sent out to everyone at once.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
