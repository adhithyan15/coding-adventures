## You'll want to know: erwischen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C407-BLITZER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**erwischen** — "to catch (someone at something)".

> Der Blitzer hat ihn mit 80 erwischt.
>
> *The speed camera caught him doing 80.*

Everyday word. *Den Bus gerade noch erwischen* — to manage to catch the bus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erwischen* is usually explained as built from *er-* and *wischen* 'to wipe, to make a quick movement', inherited from Middle High German *wischen* (EtymDB lists *wischen*): first a quick grab.
