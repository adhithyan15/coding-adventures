Russian, chapter 355: Chance and coincidence.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
случайный (slucháynyy) — accidental, chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to lower, then the Russian for to raise.

You'll want to know: случайный.
случайный (slucháynyy) — "accidental, chance".
Это была случайная встреча. Éto bylá slucháynaya vstrécha. It was a chance meeting.
Случайно — by chance. Ты случайно не знаешь…? — you would not happen to know…?

The word, taken apart.
случайный (slucháynyy) is built on случай "an event, a chance", inherited from Proto-Slavic sŭlučajĭ, from lučiti, whose sense EtymDB gives only tentatively, as "to find, to reach" (EtymDB). A chance is what you happen to come upon.

Lesson 2 of 5.
случайность (slucháynost) — chance, an accident.

You'll want to know: случайность.
случайность (slucháynost) — "chance, an accident".
Это не случайность, а план. Éto ne slucháynost, a plan. This is not chance but a plan.
Feminine. По случайности — by chance.

The word, taken apart.
случайность (slucháynost) is built on случайный (slucháynyy) with the abstract suffix -ость; the root is случай, from Proto-Slavic sŭlučajĭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
совпадение (sovpadéniye) — a coincidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for accidental, then the Russian for chance.

You'll want to know: совпадение.
совпадение (sovpadéniye) — "a coincidence".
Какое совпадение! Мы родились в один день. Kakóye sovpadéniye! My rodílis v odín den. What a coincidence! We were born on the same day.
Neuter. From совпадать, to coincide, built on падать, to fall: two things that fall together.

The word, taken apart.
совпадение (sovpadéniye) is built on совпадать "to coincide", made of со- "together" and падать "to fall", inherited from Proto-Slavic padati (EtymDB). A coincidence is two things that fall together.

Lesson 4 of 5.
неизбежный (neizbézhnyy) — inevitable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for chance, then the Russian for a coincidence.

You'll want to know: неизбежный.
неизбежный (neizbézhnyy) — "inevitable".
Изменения неизбежны. Izmenéniya neizbézhny. Changes are inevitable.
Short forms неизбежен, неизбежна. Built on избежать, to avoid: what cannot be avoided.

The word, taken apart.
неизбежный (neizbézhnyy) is built from не- "not", из- "out of" and бежать "to run", inherited from Proto-Slavic běžati: what you cannot run away from.

Lesson 5 of 5.
возникать (voznikát) — to arise, to come up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a coincidence, then the Russian for inevitable.

You'll want to know: возникать.
возникать (voznikát) — "to arise, to come up".
На работе часто возникают новые проблемы. Na rabóte chásto voznikáyut nóvyye problémy. New problems often come up at work.
Imperfective; perfective возникнуть (past возник, возникла). Of questions, problems and ideas.

The word, taken apart.
возникать (voznikát) was taken from Church Slavonic vŭzniknǫti (EtymDB), built from воз- "up" and никнуть "to bow down, to droop": something that rises up into view.
