## You'll want to know: dùjià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C184-HOLIDAY184-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dùjià* — "to go on holiday; to spend a holiday".

> *xià ge yuè wǒmen qù Běijīng dùjià* — Next month we're going on holiday to Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dùjià* is built from *dù* and *jià* 'leave', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duH* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'leave from duty' and the first 'a rule'; 'to spend time' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
