Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 147: Hard-working, Honest, Kind, Miserly, Good-natured.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मेहनती (mehantī) — hard-working.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for silly, then the Marwadi for lazy.

You'll want to know: मेहनती.
मेहनती — mehantī — "hard-working".

The word, taken apart.
मेहनती (mehantī) is a loan from Arabic miḥna, "trial, ordeal", which reached Marwadi through Persian. Its Arabic root is m-ḥ-n (Lane). Persian mihnat means "toil"; Marwadi added the ending -ī.

ईमानदार — īmāndār — "honest".

The word, taken apart.
ईमानदार (īmāndār) joins Arabic īmān, "faith", whose root is ʾ-m-n (Lane), with Persian -dār, "holder": "one who keeps faith". It reached Marwadi through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
दयाळु (dayāḷu) — kind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for hard-working, then the Marwadi for honest.

You'll want to know: दयाळु.
दयाळु — dayāḷu — "kind".

The word, taken apart.
दयाळु (dayāḷu) is a semi-tatsama: Sanskrit dayālu, "compassionate", borrowed after the old sound changes had run their course and then lightly reshaped. Marwadi says the l curled back; CDIAL has no entry for the word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
भलो (bhalo) — good-natured.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for kind, then the Marwadi for miserly.

You'll want to know: भलो.
भलो — bhalo — "good-natured".

The word, taken apart.
भलो (bhalo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhalla, "auspicious". CDIAL lists Old Marwari bhalaü under it (CDIAL 9408). CDIAL builds the Sanskrit word on bhadra, "good".
