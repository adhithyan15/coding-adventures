## You'll want to know: محکوم شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C322-MAHKUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**محکوم شدن** (*mahkum shodan*) — "to be sentenced, to be convicted".

> مرد به دو سال زندان محکوم شد.
>
> *Mard be do sâl zendân mahkum shod.*
>
> The man was sentenced to two years in prison.

The punishment takes *be*. Present *mahkum mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mahkum* is usually traced to Arabic *maḥkūm*, "judged, ruled on"; EtymDB lists no entry. *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian.
