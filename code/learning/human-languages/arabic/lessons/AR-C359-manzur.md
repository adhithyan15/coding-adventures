## You'll want to know: منظور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C359-NAZAR359-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منظور** — *manẓūr* — "a perspective".

> من منظور الطبيب، النوم مهم جدا.
>
> *min manẓūr aṭ-ṭabīb, an-nawm muhimm jiddan.*
>
> From the doctor's perspective, sleep is very important.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منظور** (*manẓūr*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ẓ-r*, "to look, to regard" (Lane). *Manẓūr* is the passive participle, what is looked at; in modern use it is the angle you look from.
