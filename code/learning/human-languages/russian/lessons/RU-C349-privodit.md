## You'll want to know: приводить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C349-VLIYANIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**приводить** (*privodít*) — "to lead to, to result in".

> Спешка часто приводит к ошибкам.
>
> *Spéshka chásto privódit k oshíbkam.*
>
> Haste often leads to mistakes.

Imperfective; present *привожу, приводишь*; perfective *привести*. *Приводить к* + dative — to lead to. It also means to bring someone along: *привести друга*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**приводить** is built from *при-* "to, towards" and *водить* "to lead", from Proto-Slavic *\*voditi* "to lead, to conduct" (EtymDB). A cause leads to its result.
