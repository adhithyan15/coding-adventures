Russian, chapter 274: Little by little.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
постепенно (postépenno) — gradually, little by little.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to stop, then the Russian for to come.

You'll want to know: постепенно.
постепенно (postépenno) — "gradually, little by little".
Постепенно все поняли правила игры. Postépenno vse pónyali právila igrý. Gradually everyone understood the rules of the game.
The adverb of постепенный (postépennyy) "gradual".

The word, taken apart.
постепенно (postépenno) is built on по and степень "a degree, a step", inherited from Proto-Slavic stepenĭ (EtymDB): step by step.

Lesson 2 of 5.
одновременно (odnovreménno) — at the same time, simultaneously.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to come, then the Russian for gradually.

You'll want to know: одновременно.
одновременно (odnovreménno) — "at the same time, simultaneously".
Они пришли одновременно. Oní prishlí odnovreménno. They arrived at the same time.
Одновременно с + instrumental means "at the same time as": одновременно (odnovreménno) с нами.

The word, taken apart.
одновременно (odnovreménno) joins один "one", inherited from Proto-Slavic edinŭ (EtymDB), and время "time", usually taken as a Church Slavonic shape beside Old Russian веремя; EtymDB lists время with no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
поначалу (ponachálu) — at first.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for gradually, then the Russian for at the same time.

You'll want to know: поначалу.
поначалу (ponachálu) — "at first".
Поначалу мне было трудно. Ponachálu mne býlo trúdno. At first it was hard for me.
Colloquial, and it hints that things changed later. The neutral word is сначала (snachála).

The word, taken apart.
поначалу (ponachálu) is built on по and начало "beginning", inherited through Old East Slavic načęlo from Proto-Slavic načędlo (EtymDB), the root of начать "to begin".

Lesson 4 of 5.
впоследствии (vposlédstvii) — later on, subsequently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for at the same time, then the Russian for at first.

You'll want to know: впоследствии.
впоследствии (vposlédstvii) — "later on, subsequently".
Впоследствии он стал известным врачом. Vposlédstvii on stal izvéstnym vrachóm. Later on he became a well-known doctor.
Formal and written. In speech: потом, позже.

The word, taken apart.
впоследствии (vposlédstvii) is built on в and последствие "a consequence", from след "a track, a trace" (EtymDB): what comes in the footsteps of an event.

Lesson 5 of 5.
напоследок (naposlédok) — at the very end, last of all.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for at first, then the Russian for later on.

You'll want to know: напоследок.
напоследок (naposlédok) — "at the very end, last of all".
Напоследок он улыбнулся и ушёл. Naposlédok on ulybnúlsya i ushyól. At the very end he smiled and left.
Colloquial: the last thing before parting.

The word, taken apart.
напоследок (naposlédok) is built on на and последок "the last bit", from последний "last", which goes back to след "a track" (EtymDB).
