## You'll want to know: पुतण्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C495-NATU495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पुतण्या** — *putaṇyā* — "a brother's son".

> माझा पुतण्या पुण्यात शिकतो.
>
> *mājhā putaṇyā puṇyāt śikto.*
>
> My nephew studies in Pune.

Masculine.

A brother's son; a brother's daughter is पुतणी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुतण्या** (*putaṇyā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *putṇyā*, "brother's son", under the reconstructed *\*pitriyajāni*, "wife of one of the father's family" (CDIAL 8189).
