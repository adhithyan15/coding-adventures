## You'll want to know: xià cì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C163-WEEKEND163-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xià cì* — "next time".

> *jīntiān wǒ méiyǒu kòng xià cì wǒmen yìqǐ qù* — I'm busy today; let's go together next time.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xià cì* is built from 下 'below, next' and *cì* 'a turn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *tshijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'order, sequence': the next turn.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
