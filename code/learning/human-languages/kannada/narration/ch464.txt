Kannada, chapter 464: Breakfast at the Hotel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉಪಹಾರ (upahāra) — breakfast; a light meal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a water heater, then the Kannada for a resort.

You'll want to know: ಉಪಹಾರ.
ಉಪಹಾರ — upahāra — "breakfast; a light meal".
ಹೋಟೆಲ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಉಪಹಾರಕ್ಕೆ ಇಡ್ಲಿ ಮತ್ತು ದೋಸೆ ಇತ್ತು. hōṭelnalli upahārakke iḍli mattu dōse ittu. At the hotel there were idlis and dosas for breakfast.
ಬೆಳಗಿನ ಉಪಹಾರ (upahāra), morning breakfast; ತಿಂಡಿ is the everyday word.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಪಹಾರ (upahāra) is borrowed from Sanskrit: Monier-Williams gives upahāra as "an offering, a present". The sense of a light meal is a later Indian one.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪರಿಚಾರಕ (paricāraka) — a waiter; an attendant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for breakfast, then the Kannada for a menu.

You'll want to know: ಪರಿಚಾರಕ.
ಪರಿಚಾರಕ — paricāraka — "a waiter; an attendant".
ಪರಿಚಾರಕ ನಮಗೆ ಬಿಸಿ ಕಾಫಿ ತಂದನು. paricāraka namage bisi kāphi tandanu. The waiter brought us hot coffee.
Plural: ಪರಿಚಾರಕರು, paricārakaru.
A formal word; people often call out ಸರ್ or ಅಣ್ಣ to a waiter.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಿಚಾರಕ (paricāraka) is borrowed from Sanskrit paricāraka, "an assistant, an attendant" (Monier-Williams), from pari-car, "to move about, to attend on".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಭಕ್ಷೀಸು (bhakṣīsu) — a gratuity; a tip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a menu, then the Kannada for a waiter.

You'll want to know: ಭಕ್ಷೀಸು.
ಭಕ್ಷೀಸು — bhakṣīsu — "a gratuity; a tip".
ನಾವು ಪರಿಚಾರಕನಿಗೆ ಹತ್ತು ರೂಪಾಯಿ ಭಕ್ಷೀಸು ಕೊಟ್ಟೆವು. nāvu paricārakanige hattu rūpāyi bhakṣīsu koṭṭevu. We gave the waiter a ten rupee tip.
ಭಕ್ಷೀಸು (bhakṣīsu) ಕೊಡು, to tip.

The word, taken apart.
ಭಕ್ಷೀಸು (bhakṣīsu) is usually traced to Persian bakhshish, "a gift, a present", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಆರ್ಡರ್ ಮಾಡು (ārḍar māḍu) — to order.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a waiter, then the Kannada for a gratuity.

You'll want to know: ಆರ್ಡರ್ ಮಾಡು.
ಆರ್ಡರ್ ಮಾಡು — ārḍar māḍu — "to order".
ನಾನು ಎರಡು ಕಾಫಿ ಆರ್ಡರ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆ. nānu eraḍu kāphi ārḍar māḍide. I ordered two coffees.
Past (I …): ಆರ್ಡರ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, ārḍar māḍide.
Food in a hotel, or goods online: ಆನ್ಲೈನ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಆರ್ಡರ್ ಮಾಡು (ārḍar māḍu).

The word, taken apart.
ಆರ್ಡರ್ ಮಾಡು (ārḍar māḍu) joins a loan from English order and the native verb ಮಾಡು, to do.
