# ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿ (diksūci) — a compass

## You'll want to know: ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C453-HADAGU453-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿ** — *diksūci* — "a compass".

> ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿಯ ಮುಳ್ಳು ಉತ್ತರಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *diksūciya muḷḷu uttarakke tiruguttade.*
>
> The needle of a compass turns to the north.

ದಿಕ್ಕು, a direction, plus ಸೂಚಿ, a pointer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಿಕ್ಸೂಚಿ** (*diksūci*) is built from two Sanskrit words: *diś*, "a direction" (CDIAL 6339), and *sūci*, "a needle" (CDIAL 13551).
