# Chapter 2 — The introduction exchange

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. The whole introduction, assembled from atoms you now own.

## The exchange

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: "My name is Mira." — *āmār nām Mira.*]
- [YOU SAY: "What's your name?" (respectful) — *āpnār nām ki?*]
- [YOU SAY: "My name is Arun." — *āmār nām Arun.*]
- [YOU SAY: "Pleased to meet you." — *ālāp kore bhālo lāglo.*]

## The atoms

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: "name," "my" (*nām*, *āmār*)]
- [YOU SAY: the three "you" levels (*tui / tumi / āpni*)]
- [YOU SAY: "what," and the word Bengali leaves out (*ki*; there is no "is")]

## Roots you now carry

[PAUSE 2s]
- *nām* ← Sanskrit *nāman* → English **name**
- *āmār* ← the first-person *ām-*; no gender (unlike Hindi *merā/merī*)
- *ki* ← *ka-* → English **what**; *tumi* ← *\*tū* → **thou**
- *ālāp* ← Sanskrit *ālāpa*, "conversing"

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Introduce yourself and ask a stranger's name in Bengali — and note the
one word you never say. (*Āmār nām …. Āpnār nām ki?* — no word for "is.")
