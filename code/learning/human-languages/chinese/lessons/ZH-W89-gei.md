## Script — the silk piece, then 合
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-GEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 给

**Two pieces, left before right. The left piece is the one new shape.**

**The silk piece 纟, three strokes**: a stroke that falls to the left and turns right, a second, longer one the same way, then a stroke rising to the right.

**合, six strokes**: a roof like 人, a left-falling stroke and a right-falling one; a short horizontal under it; then 口.

3 + 6 = nine.
