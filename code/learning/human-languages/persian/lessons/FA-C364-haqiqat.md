## You'll want to know: حقیقت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C364-VAQEIYYAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حقیقت** (*haqiqat*) — "the truth".

> بالاخره حقیقت را به ما گفت.
>
> *bâlâkhare haqiqat râ be mâ goft.*
>
> In the end she told us the truth.

*Dar haqiqat* — in truth. Plural *haqâyeq*, an Arabic broken plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حقیقت** comes from Arabic *ḥaqīqa* "truth, reality" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as *ḥaqq* "right, true", the source of Persian *haq*.
