## You'll want to know: la diretta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C287-DIRETTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la diretta** — "the live broadcast".

> Guardiamo la partita in diretta.
>
> *Let us watch the match live.*

*In diretta* — live; the opposite is *in differita*, recorded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diretta* is the feminine of *diretto* "direct", from Latin *dīrēctus* "straight", the past participle of *dīrigere* "to set straight" (Lewis & Short).
