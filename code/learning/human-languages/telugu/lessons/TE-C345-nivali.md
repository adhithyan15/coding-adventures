# నివాళి (nivāḷi) — a tribute

## You'll want to know: నివాళి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C345-SMRTI345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నివాళి** — *nivāḷi* — "a tribute".

> విద్యార్థులు తమ మాస్టారికి నివాళి అర్పించారు.
>
> *vidyārthulu tama māsṭāriki nivāḷi arpiñcāru.*
>
> The students paid tribute to their teacher.

నివాళి అర్పించు, to pay tribute: the phrase every news report uses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నివాళి** (*nivāḷi*) is a Telugu word for a tribute, an offering of honour; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
