## You'll want to know: મેનેજર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C409-LODGE409-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મેનેજર** — *menejar* — "a manager".

> હોટેલના મેનેજરે અમને ચાવી આપી.
>
> *hoṭelnā menejre amne cāvī āpī.*
>
> The hotel manager gave us the key.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મેનેજર** (*menejar*) is borrowed from English *manager*.
