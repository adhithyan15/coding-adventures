## You'll want to know: le menteur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C468-FRANCHISE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le menteur** — "liar; lying".

> Ne le crois pas, c'est un menteur.
>
> *Do not believe him, he is a liar.*

A woman is *la menteuse*. A lie is *un mensonge*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le menteur* is inherited from Old French *menteor* (EtymDB), built on *mentir* "to lie", from Latin *mentīrī* "to lie" (Lewis & Short).
