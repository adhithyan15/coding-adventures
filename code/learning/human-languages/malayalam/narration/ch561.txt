Malayalam, chapter 561: Drums, Guitars and Flutes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കച്ചേരി (kaccēri) — a concert; a recital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a sculptor, then the Malayalam for an exhibition.

You'll want to know: കച്ചേരി.
കച്ചേരി — kaccēri — "a concert; a recital".
ഇന്നലെ രാത്രി ഞങ്ങൾ ഒരു സംഗീത കച്ചേരിക്ക് പോയി. innale rātri ñaṅṅaḷ oru saṁgīta kaccērikkŭ pōyi. Last night we went to a music concert.
A classical concert; the same word also names a court or a government office.

The word, taken apart.
കച്ചേരി (kaccēri) is usually traced to the North Indian word for a court-house, Hindi kacaharī, which CDIAL sets under a reconstructed compound meaning court-house (CDIAL 3429). Malayalam keeps the sense of a court or an office; how it came to mean a concert is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
വാദ്യം (vādyaṁ) — a musical instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an exhibition, then the Malayalam for a concert.

You'll want to know: വാദ്യം.
വാദ്യം — vādyaṁ — "a musical instrument".
ഉത്സവത്തിന് പല വാദ്യങ്ങൾ ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു. utsavattinŭ pala vādyaṅṅaḷ uṇṭāyirunnu. There were many instruments at the festival.
Plural: വാദ്യങ്ങൾ, vādyaṅṅaḷ.
വാദ്യോപകരണം is the full formal word for a musical instrument.

The word, taken apart.
വാദ്യം (vādyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit vādya, "to be played", and as a noun "instrumental music" (CDIAL 11511).

Lesson 3 of 5.
കൊട്ടുക (koṭṭuka) — to beat a drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a concert, then the Malayalam for a musical instrument.

You'll want to know: കൊട്ടുക.
കൊട്ടുക — koṭṭuka — "to beat a drum".
ഉത്സവത്തിന് അവർ ചെണ്ട കൊട്ടി. utsavattinŭ avar ceṇṭa koṭṭi. They played the chenda drum at the festival.
Past: കൊട്ടി, koṭṭi.
Also to knock or tap; കൈ കൊട്ടുക (koṭṭuka) is to clap.

The word, taken apart.
കൊട്ടുക (koṭṭuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2063 gives it as to beat so as to make a sound, as on a drum or a bell (DEDR 2063).

ഗിറ്റാർ — giṟṟār — "a guitar".
എന്റെ സുഹൃത്ത് ഗിറ്റാർ നന്നായി വായിക്കും. enṟe suhṛttŭ giṟṟār nannāyi vāyikkuṁ. My friend plays the guitar well.
One plays an instrument with വായിക്കുക, the same verb as to read.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ഓടക്കുഴൽ (ōṭakkuḻal) — a flute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to beat a drum, then the Malayalam for a guitar.

You'll want to know: ഓടക്കുഴൽ.
ഓടക്കുഴൽ — ōṭakkuḻal — "a flute".
ഓടക്കുഴലിന്റെ ശബ്ദം ദൂരെ നിന്ന് കേട്ടു. ōṭakkuḻalinṟe śabdaṁ dūre ninnŭ kēṭṭu. The sound of a flute was heard from far away.
ഓട a kind of reed + കുഴൽ a tube.

The word, taken apart.
ഓടക്കുഴൽ (ōṭakkuḻal) is native Dravidian, a compound of ഓട, "a large reed" (DEDR 1043), and കുഴൽ, "a tube, a flute" (DEDR 1818).
