## You'll want to know: सुनावणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C359-KHATLA359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सुनावणी** — *sunāvṇī* — "a hearing (in court)".

> खटल्याची पुढची सुनावणी सोमवारी आहे.
>
> *khaṭalyācī puḍhcī sunāvṇī somvārī āhe.*
>
> The next hearing of the case is on Monday.

Feminine.

Built on सुनावणे, to pronounce: शिक्षा सुनावणे, to pass sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुनावणी** (*sunāvṇī*) is built on सुनावणे, "to tell to, to pronounce", with the noun ending -णी; the origin of the verb is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
