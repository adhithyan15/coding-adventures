## You'll want to know: مهما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C400-KULLAMA400-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مهما** — *mahmā* — "whatever".

> مهما حدث، سأبقى صديقك.
>
> *mahmā ḥadatha, sa-abqā ṣadīqak.*
>
> Whatever happens, I will stay your friend.

Like other condition words, it is often followed by the past: مهما حدث.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مهما** (*mahmā*) is a native Arabic particle of condition, whatever. Grammarians usually trace it to *mā*, "what", said twice; its origin is not settled.
