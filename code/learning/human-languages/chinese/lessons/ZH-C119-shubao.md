## You'll want to know: 书包
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C119-BAGS119-01]; assesses=[] -->

**书包** — *shūbāo* — "schoolbag".

> **我的书包里有两本书** *wǒ de shūbāo lǐ yǒu liǎng běn shū* — There are two books in my schoolbag.

The new character, in pieces: 包 — a wrapping stroke that bends round from the top, and inside it three strokes: a turn, a short bar, and a vertical that bends right along the bottom and hooks up. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**书包** (*shūbāo*) is built from 书 'book' and 包 'to wrap', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syo* and *paew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a wrapper for books.
