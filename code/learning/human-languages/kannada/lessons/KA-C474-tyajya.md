## You'll want to know: ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C474-TYAJYA474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ** — *tyājya* — "waste matter; refuse".

> ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆಯ ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ ನದಿಗೆ ಹರಿಯುತ್ತಿತ್ತು.
>
> *kārkhāneya tyājya nadige hariyuttittu.*
>
> Waste from the factory was flowing into the river.

ಘನ ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ, solid waste; ಕಸ is the everyday word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ** (*tyājya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tyājya*, "to be left, to be abandoned" (Monier-Williams): what is thrown away.
