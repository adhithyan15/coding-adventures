## You'll want to know: व्रत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C331-VRAT331-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्रत** — *vrat* — "a fast; a vow".

> दादी ने पूरे दिन व्रत रखा।
>
> *dādī ne pūre din vrat rakhā.*
>
> Grandmother fasted all day.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्रत** (*vrat*) is Sanskrit *vrata*, "a vow, a rule of conduct" (Monier-Williams). **व्रत रखना** is to keep a fast, a vow to go without food.
