## You'll want to know: ہڈی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C465-BONE465-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ہڈی** — *haḍḍī* — "a bone".

> کتا ہڈی کھا رہا تھا۔
>
> *kuttā haḍḍī khā rahā thā.*
>
> The dog was eating a bone.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہڈی** (*haḍḍī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *haḍḍī* under Sanskrit *haḍḍa*, "bone" (CDIAL 13952).
