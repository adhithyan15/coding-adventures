## You'll want to know: حكومة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C377-HUKUMA377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حكومة** — *ḥukūma* — "a government".

> قررت الحكومة فتح مستشفى جديد.
>
> *qarrarat al-ḥukūma fatḥ mustashfā jadīd.*
>
> The government decided to open a new hospital.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حكومة** (*ḥukūma*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-k-m*, "to judge, to rule" (Lane). *Ḥukūma* is a verbal noun, ruling, which came to mean the body that rules: a government.
