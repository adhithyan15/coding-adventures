## You'll want to know: fullō, fullōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C377-FULLO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**fullō, fullōnis** — "a fuller, a cleaner of cloth".

> Fullō vestēs sordidās lavat.
>
> *The fuller washes dirty clothes.*

*fullō, fullōnis* m. The fuller cleaned and finished woollen cloth by treading it in tubs; his shop was the *fullōnica*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *fullō* is not settled: Lewis & Short give none, and EtymDB has no parent for it.
