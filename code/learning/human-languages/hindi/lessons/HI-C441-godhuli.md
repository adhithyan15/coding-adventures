## You'll want to know: गोधूलि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C441-SAVERA441-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गोधूलि** — *godhūli* — "dusk (when the cattle come home)".

> गोधूलि में गायें गाँव लौट रही थीं।
>
> *godhūli meṁ gāyẽ gā̃v lauṭ rahī thīṁ.*
>
> At dusk the cows were coming back to the village.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोधूलि** (*godhūli*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *godhūli* (Monier-Williams), literally the dust of *go*, a word for both the cow and the earth. Monier-Williams reads it as earth-dust, the haze that seems to rise at that hour; the usual telling is the dust raised by the cattle coming home. It is still an auspicious time for weddings.
