## You'll want to know: আলপনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C305-PRODIP305-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আলপনা** — *ālpônā* — "a floor painting (alpona)".

> পুজোর আগে মেয়েরা দরজার সামনে আলপনা আঁকল।
>
> *pujor āge meyerā dôrjār sāmne ālpônā ā̃klô.*
>
> Before the puja the girls painted an alpona in front of the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আলপনা** (*ālpônā*) comes down from Sanskrit *ālimpana*, 'whitening, painting' (CDIAL 1385, which lists Bengali *ālpanā*): the white patterns drawn on a floor for a festival.
