## You'll want to know: पीड़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C252-HAD252-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पीड़** — *pīṛ* — "an ache; a deep pain".

> म्हारी पीठ मांय पीड़ है।
>
> *mhārī pīṭh mā̃y pīṛ hai.*
>
> I have an ache in my back.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पीड़** (*pīṛ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pīḍā*, "pain"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *pīḍa* under it (CDIAL 8227).
