## You'll want to know: shìpín
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C204-ONLINE204-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shìpín* — "video".

> *wǒ xǐhuan kàn shìpín* — I like watching videos.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shìpín* is built from *shì* 'to look at' and *pín* 'frequent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyijH* and *bjin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The word is modern, and how the pair came to mean a video is not settled.
