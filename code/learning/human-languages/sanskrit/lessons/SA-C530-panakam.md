## You'll want to know: पानकम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C530-BHOJYA530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पानकम्** — *pānakam* — "a sherbet; a cool drink".

> यदि अतिथिः श्रान्तः अस्ति, तर्हि तस्मै पानकं ददातु।
>
> *yadi atithiḥ śrāntaḥ asti, tarhi tasmai pānakaṃ dadātu.*
>
> If the guest is tired, please give him a sherbet.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पानकम्** (*pānakam*) is built on *pāna*, "drinking", from the root √pā, "to drink"; MW gives *pānaka* as a draught, a drink, a beverage (MW).
