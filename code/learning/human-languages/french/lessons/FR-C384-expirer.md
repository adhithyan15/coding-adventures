## You'll want to know: expirer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C384-VALABLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**expirer** — "to expire; to breathe out".

> Mon passeport expire au mois de mai.
>
> *My passport expires in May.*

Of breathing: *inspirer*, then *expirer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*expirer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exspīrāre* "to breathe out, to die away" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *spīrāre* "to breathe".
