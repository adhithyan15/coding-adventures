## You'll want to know: ثابت شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C363-EDDEA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ثابت شدن** (*sâbet shodan*) — "to be proven".

> حرف او ثابت شد.
>
> *harf-e u sâbet shod.*
>
> What she said was proven.

*Sâbet* "fixed, firm" + *shodan* "to become" (present stem *shav-*). *Sâbet kardan* — to prove.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ثابت** comes from Arabic *thābit* "firm, constant, unchanging" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as *ithbāt*. A claim that is proven has become firm.
