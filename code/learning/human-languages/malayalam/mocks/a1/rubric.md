# Malayalam A1 mock rubric

Score either A1 mock against
[task-shapes/a1.json](../../task-shapes/a1.json). Reading, listening, writing,
and speaking are separate 100-point papers; every paper must independently
reach 60/100.

## Reading and listening

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-forms -->
Reading assigns 35 points to signs/forms.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-forms -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->
Reading assigns 35 points to short messages.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-personal-descriptions -->
Reading assigns 30 points to personal descriptions.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-personal-descriptions -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-announcements -->
Listening assigns 35 points to announcements.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-announcements -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->
Listening assigns 35 points to short exchanges.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-personal-account -->
Listening assigns 30 points to the personal account.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-personal-account -->

Divide each part's points equally among its keyed items and retain one decimal
place. Play listening material twice at 90–110 words per minute. No transcript,
romanization, dictionary, or translator is allowed.

## Writing

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
The practical form is worth 40 points: requested content 16, task fulfilment 8,
Malayalam control 8, and orthographic control 8.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->
The 30–40 word message is worth 60 points: relevant content 20, named reader and
purpose 10, organisation 10, vocabulary and grammar 12, and orthographic control
8. Either traditional or reformed Malayalam orthography earns full credit when
used consistently. Romanized, translated, non-Malayalam-script, or visibly
copied answers earn zero independent-writing credit.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->

## Speaking

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
The personal interview is worth 30 points.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
The one-minute prepared description is worth 30 points.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
The transactional role-play is worth 40 points.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->

Within each part award 35% for task fulfilment and relevant content, 25% for
interaction and repair, 25% for Malayalam vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for
pronunciation and fluency. Natural pauses are acceptable. No written answer
script is allowed; paper preparation notes may contain keywords, not sentences.

## Readiness boundary

The mock is executable but not self-validating. A readiness claim additionally
requires clean timed administration and qualified human review of the paper,
recordings, productive responses, and scoring decisions.
