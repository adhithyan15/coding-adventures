Punjabi, chapter 337: Asking Nicely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮੰਗਣਾ (maṅgṇā) — to ask for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to take out, then the Punjabi for perhaps.

You'll want to know: ਮੰਗਣਾ.
ਮੰਗਣਾ — maṅgṇā — "to ask for".
ਮੈਂ ਕਦੇ ਪੈਸੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਮੰਗਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ kade paise nahīṁ maṅgdā hāṁ. I never ask for money.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਮੰਗਿਆ, maiṁ pāṇī maṅgiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੰਗਣਾ (maṅgṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit mārgati, "seeks, requests"; CDIAL lists Punjabi maṅgṇā, "to ask for", under it (CDIAL 10074).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਬੇਨਤੀ (bentī) — a request.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for perhaps, then the Punjabi for to ask for.

You'll want to know: ਬੇਨਤੀ.
ਬੇਨਤੀ — bentī — "a request".
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਇੱਕ ਬੇਨਤੀ ਮੰਨੋਗੇ? kī tusī̃ merī ikk bentī mannoge? Will you grant me one request?
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਬੇਨਤੀ (bentī) is inherited from Sanskrit vijñapti, "information, a request to a superior"; CDIAL lists Punjabi beṇtī, "request", under it (CDIAL 11706).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ.
ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ — thoṛhā — "a little; not much".
ਕੀ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਮਿਲੇਗਾ? kī mainū̃ thoṛhā pāṇī milegā? May I have a little water?

The word, taken apart.
ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ (thoṛhā) is inherited from Sanskrit stoka, "a drop" (as an adverb, "a little"); CDIAL lists Punjabi thoṛā, "a little, few", under it (CDIAL 13720).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਜ਼ਰਾ (zarā) — a bit (to soften a request); a moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a request, then the Punjabi for a little.

You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਰਾ.
ਜ਼ਰਾ — zarā — "a bit (to soften a request); a moment".
ਜ਼ਰਾ ਰੁਕੋ ਅਤੇ ਹੁਣ ਨਾ ਬੋਲੋ। zarā ruko ate huṇ nā bolo. Wait a moment and don't speak now.

The word, taken apart.
ਜ਼ਰਾ (zarā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic dharra, "a tiny particle".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਮੰਗਵਾਉਣਾ (maṅgvāuṇā) — to send for; to order (goods).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a little, then the Punjabi for a bit (to soften a request).

You'll want to know: ਮੰਗਵਾਉਣਾ.
ਮੰਗਵਾਉਣਾ — maṅgvāuṇā — "to send for; to order (goods)".
ਅਸੀਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਤੋਂ ਸਾਮਾਨ ਨਹੀਂ ਮੰਗਵਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ। asīṁ bāzār ton sāmān nahīṁ maṅgvāunde hāṁ. We don't order goods from the market.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਤੋਂ ਚਾਹ ਮੰਗਵਾਈ, maiṁ bāzār ton chāh maṅgvāī.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੰਗਵਾਉਣਾ (maṅgvāuṇā) is the causative partner of maṅgṇā, "to ask for", which goes back to Sanskrit mārgati, "seeks, requests"; CDIAL lists Punjabi maṅgṇā under it (CDIAL 10074).
