## You'll want to know: آمد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C390-RAVANA390-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آمد** — *āmad* — "an arrival".

> گاڑی کی آمد شام کو ہے۔
>
> *gāṛī kī āmad shām ko hai.*
>
> The train gets in this evening.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آمد** (*āmad*) is usually traced to Persian *āmad*, "a coming", from the verb for "to come"; station boards list arrivals under it.
