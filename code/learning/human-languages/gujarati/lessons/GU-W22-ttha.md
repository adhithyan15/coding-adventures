# ઠ — retroflex ttha

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-SAAT, GU-LEX-CHOTHU, GU-JOIN-JYAARE-01, GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01, GU-SCRIPT-THA-01] -->

- [YOU RECALL: say *seven* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *fourth*, and the letter it ends on — **R2**, five lessons back]
- [YOU RECALL: say **જ્યારે** and use it to locate one event against another — **R3**, twenty lessons back]

Six and seven cost you no new sign at all. **જ્યારે** the count reaches eight,
that stops — so the sign is bought here, one lesson before the number needs it.

## You'll want to know: the square you already hold three corners of
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TA-01, GU-SCRIPT-THA-01, GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01] -->

Gujarati keeps two rows of *t*. One is dental — tongue at the teeth. One is
retroflex — tongue curled back. Each row has a plain letter and a breathy one.

| | plain | aspirated |
|---|---|---|
| dental | **ત** *ta* | **થ** *tha* |
| retroflex | **ટ** *ṭa* | ? |

**ચોથું** ends on **થ**, the dental aspirate. You have three of the four. The
missing corner is the retroflex aspirate, and it is the one the word for eight
needs.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ઠ** — *ṭha*

Keep **ઠ** visible. Start high, a little to the right of where the ring will
sit. Curl left across the top and down the outside of a small hook. From the
foot of the hook, sweep down and to the right until you reach the widest point
on the right. Now carry the ring round **clockwise** — down the right side,
along the bottom, up the left — and close it exactly where your diagonal crossed
on the way down. One stroke, no lift.

**ટ** turns the other way. Its bowl runs anticlockwise and finishes still open
on the right; **ઠ** runs clockwise and finishes closed. If the ring will not
close, you have made a **ટ**.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ઠ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice the hook, the diagonal
into the ring, and the closing point.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ઠ** once. Compare only two things: does the ring close, and does
it close where the diagonal came in? Repair one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W22-ttha-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write retroflex ttha from memory.","answer":"ઠ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ઠ — hook, diagonal, and a ring that closes.","incorrect":"Look once at ઠ, hide it, and repair only the landmark that differs."},"response_seconds":20} -->

after one repair.
