## You'll want to know: imprimer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C279-REDIGER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**imprimer** — "to print".

> Le journal est imprimé pendant la nuit.
>
> *The paper is printed during the night.*

At home too: *imprimer un billet de train*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imprimer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *imprimere* "to press into" (EtymDB), from *in* and *premere* "to press" (Lewis & Short).
