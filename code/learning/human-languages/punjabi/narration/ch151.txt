Punjabi, chapter 151: To say, To live, To spend, To save, To sow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕਹਿਣਾ (kahiṇā) — to say.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to wake up, then the Punjabi for to drive.

You'll want to know: ਕਹਿਣਾ.
ਕਹਿਣਾ — kahiṇā — "to say".

The word, taken apart.
ਕਹਿਣਾ (kahiṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kathayati, "converses with, describes" (CDIAL 2703).

The word, taken apart.
ਖ਼ਰਚਣਾ (kharcṇā) is built on kharc, "expense", usually traced through Persian kharch to Arabic kharj, "expenditure"; its Arabic root is kh-r-j (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਬਚਾਉਣਾ (bacāuṇā) — to save.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to live, then the Punjabi for to spend.

You'll want to know: ਬਚਾਉਣਾ.
ਬਚਾਉਣਾ — bacāuṇā — "to save".

The word, taken apart.
ਬਚਾਉਣਾ (bacāuṇā) goes back to Sanskrit vañcati, "moves; escapes from". CDIAL lists the Punjabi form vacāuṇā, "to save", under it, and marks bacāuṇā, with b-, as taken from Hindi (CDIAL 11208).
