You'll want to know: કમ્પ્યૂટર.
કમ્પ્યૂટર — kampyūṭar — "computer".
અમારી કચેરીમાં નવું કમ્પ્યૂટર છે. — amārī kacerīmā̃ navũ kampyūṭar che — "There is a new computer in our office."

The word, taken apart.
કમ્પ્યૂટર (kampyūṭar) is a loan from English computer.

Lesson 3 of 5.
દબાવવું (dabāvvũ) — to press.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for computer, then the Gujarati for screen.

You'll want to know: દબાવવું.
દબાવવું — dabāvvũ — "to press".
તે લાલ બટન દબાવે છે. — te lāl baṭan dabāve che — "She presses the red button."

The word, taken apart.
દબાવવું (dabāvvũ) comes from dabb, "press", a reconstructed form that CDIAL, citing A. Master, traces to Dravidian, so it may not be inherited from Sanskrit at all; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to press, intimidate" (CDIAL 6173).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: સાચવવું.
સાચવવું — sācavvũ — "to keep safe, to save".
હું મારું કામ કમ્પ્યૂટરમાં સાચવું છું. — hũ mārũ kām kampyūṭarmā̃ sācavũ chũ — "I save my work on the computer."

The word, taken apart.
સાચવવું (sācavvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to preserve", under Sanskrit sájati, "is attached, clings", as built on an analogical Middle Indo-Aryan stem ṣacc- (CDIAL 13085).
