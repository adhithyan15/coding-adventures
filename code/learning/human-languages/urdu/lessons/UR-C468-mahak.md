## You'll want to know: مہک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C468-SMELL468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مہک** — *mahak* — "a scent".

> کمرے میں پھولوں کی مہک ہے۔
>
> *kamre meṅ phūloṅ kī mahak hai.*
>
> There is a scent of flowers in the room.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مہک** (*mahak*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *mahak*, "fragrance", under Sanskrit *magha* (CDIAL 9703).
