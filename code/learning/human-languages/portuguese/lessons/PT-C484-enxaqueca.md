## You'll want to know: a enxaqueca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C484-ENXAQUECA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a enxaqueca** — "migraine".

> Quando tenho enxaqueca, fico às escuras no quarto.
>
> *When I have a migraine, I stay in my room in the dark.*

Stronger than *uma dor de cabeça*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enxaqueca* is usually traced to Arabic *aš-šaqīqa*, a word for a headache on one side of the head.
