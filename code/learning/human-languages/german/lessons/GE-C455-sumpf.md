## You'll want to know: der Sumpf
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C455-WASSERFALL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Sumpf** — "the marsh, the swamp".

> Im Sumpf wachsen hohes Gras und Schilf.
>
> *Tall grass and reeds grow in the marsh.*

Plural: *die Sümpfe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sumpf* is inherited from Proto-Germanic *sumpaz* 'swamp' (EtymDB). English *swamp* and *sump* are cousins.
