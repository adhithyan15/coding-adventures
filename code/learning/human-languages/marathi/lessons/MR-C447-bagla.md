## You'll want to know: बगळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C447-BAGLA447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बगळा** — *bagḷā* — "a heron; an egret".

> शेतात पांढरे बगळे बसले आहेत.
>
> *śetāt pāṇḍhre bagḷe basle āhet.*
>
> White egrets are sitting in the field.

Masculine. Plural: बगळे, *bagḷe*.

The plural is बगळे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बगळा** (*bagḷā*) belongs with Sanskrit *baka*, "a heron" (CDIAL 9115); how the Marathi form was shaped is not settled.
