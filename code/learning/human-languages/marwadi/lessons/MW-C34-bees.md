# बीस — twenty on the page

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 20s] Recall the four-skill show payoff, then write ten.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-BEES-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-BA-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-SA-01, MW-SCRIPT-DAS-01, MW-LEX-BEES-01] -->

> **ब** + **ी** + **स** → **बीस** — *bīs* — twenty

Every piece is second-hand. **ब** came in with **बस**, the bus. **ी** came in
with **पाणी**. **स** closes **दस** as well.

Write **दस** and **बीस** one under the other and look at the last sign of each.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-BEES-01, MW-SCRIPT-DAS-01, MW-SCRIPT-TEEN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **बीस**, cover it, wait five seconds, write it. Then write **दस** and
**तीन** beneath and mark the long **ी** where it appears.
