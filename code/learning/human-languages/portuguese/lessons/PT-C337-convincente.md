## You'll want to know: convincente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C337-ARGUMENTAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**convincente** — "convincing".

> Não foi uma resposta muito convincente.
>
> *It was not a very convincing answer.*

The opposite is *pouco convincente*. One form for both genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convincente* goes back to Latin *convincēns*, the present participle of *convincere* "to overcome, to prove" (Lewis & Short); it is a learned form beside the inherited verb *convencer*.
