## You'll want to know: स्तूपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C323-STUPA323-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्तूपः** — *stūpaḥ* — "a stupa; a mound".

> पर्वतस्य समीपे प्राचीनः स्तूपः अस्ति।
>
> *parvatasya samīpe prācīnaḥ stūpaḥ asti.*
>
> There is an ancient stupa near the hill.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्तूपः** (*stūpaḥ*) MW gives *stūpa* as a knot of hair, a top, and a heap of earth or bricks, especially a Buddhist monument, and reports two old derivations, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
