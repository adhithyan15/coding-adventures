## You'll want to know: ਅਫ਼ਸੋਸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C346-SORRY346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਫ਼ਸੋਸ** — *afsos* — "regret; it's a pity".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਅਫ਼ਸੋਸ ਹੈ ਕਿ ਮੈਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਇਆ।
>
> *mainū̃ bahut afsos hai ki maiṁ nahīṁ āiā.*
>
> I am very sorry that I didn't come.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਫ਼ਸੋਸ** (*afsos*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *afsōs*, "regret".
