## You'll want to know: とうし

**とうし** — *tōshi* — "an investment".

> **とうしの けっかは、けいきに よります。** — *tōshi no kekka wa, keiki ni yorimasu* — "How an investment turns out depends on business conditions."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とうし** (*tōshi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tō*, "throw" (Middle Chinese *duw*) and *shi*, "resources" (*tsij*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
