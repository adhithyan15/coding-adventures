## You'll want to know: হরিণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C437-BAGH437-02]; assesses=[] -->

**হরিণ** — *hôrin* — "a deer".

> বাঘ দেখে হরিণগুলো দৌড়ে পালাল।
>
> *bāgh dekhe hôringulo douṛe pālālô.*
>
> Seeing the tiger, the deer ran away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হরিণ** (*hôrin*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *harin*, "a deer", under Sanskrit *hariṇá*, "yellowish; a deer" (CDIAL 13982).
