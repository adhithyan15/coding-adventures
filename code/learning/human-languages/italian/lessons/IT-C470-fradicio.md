## You'll want to know: fradicio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C470-PIOGGIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fradicio** — "soaked, drenched".

> Sono arrivato a casa fradicio.
>
> *I got home soaked.*

*Bagnato fradicio* — soaking wet; also rotten: *una mela fradicia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fradicio* is usually traced to Latin *fracidus* 'soft, over-ripe' (Lewis & Short), but the origin is not settled.
