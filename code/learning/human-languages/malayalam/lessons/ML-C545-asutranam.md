# ആസൂത്രണം (āsūtraṇaṁ) — planning

## You'll want to know: ആസൂത്രണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C545-CUMATALA545-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ആസൂത്രണം** — *āsūtraṇaṁ* — "planning".

> നല്ല ആസൂത്രണം ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ ജോലി എളുപ്പമാകും.
>
> *nalla āsūtraṇaṁ uṇṭeṅkil jōli eḷuppamākuṁ.*
>
> With good planning the work becomes easy.

ആസൂത്രണം ചെയ്യുക is to plan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആസൂത്രണം** (*āsūtraṇaṁ*) is a learned word built on Sanskrit *sūtra*, "a thread, a cord" (CDIAL 13561); the exact model of the formation is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
