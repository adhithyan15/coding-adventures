## You'll want to know: نذری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C344-NAZR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نذری** (*nazri*) — "votive food, food given out for a vow".

> همسایه ها برای همه نذری پخش کردند.
>
> *Hamsâye-hâ barâ-ye hame nazri pakhsh kardand.*
>
> The neighbours handed out votive food to everyone.

*nazr* + *-i*: food cooked to keep a vow and shared with neighbours and strangers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nazri* adds the Persian suffix *-i* to *nazr* "a vow", which is borrowed from Arabic *nadhr* (EtymDB).
