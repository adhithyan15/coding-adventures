## You'll want to know: ताप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C439-TAP439-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ताप** — *tāp* — "heat; a fever (formal)".

> दोपहर का ताप सहना मुश्किल था।
>
> *dopahar kā tāp sahnā muśkil thā.*
>
> The midday heat was hard to bear.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताप** (*tāp*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *tāpa*, "heat, glow" (Monier-Williams), from the root *tap*, "to be hot", which comes back in a verb later in this chapter.
