## You'll want to know: rūs, rūris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C473-RUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rūs, rūris** — "the countryside, the country; a farm".

> Aestāte multī cīvēs rūs eunt.
>
> *In summer many citizens go to the country.*

Third declension, neuter: *rūs, rūris n.* Like *domus*, it takes no preposition: *rūs* 'to the country', *rūre* 'from the country', *rūrī* 'in the country'. English *rural* and *rustic* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rūs* is inherited from Proto-Italic *rowos*, from PIE *rewh₁-* 'open space, field' (EtymDB). English *rural* and, through *rūsticus*, *rustic* come from it; German *Raum* 'space' is a cousin.
