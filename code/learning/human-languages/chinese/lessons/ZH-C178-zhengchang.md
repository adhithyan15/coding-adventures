## You'll want to know: 正常
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C178-DAY178-01]; assesses=[] -->

**正常** — *zhèngcháng* — "normal; as usual".

> **没事我觉得很正常** *méishì wǒ juéde hěn zhèngcháng* — It's nothing: I think that's perfectly normal.

The new character, in pieces: 常 — eleven strokes: three short strokes on top, the cover piece of 学, 口 under it, and at the foot a frame with a long vertical down through it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**正常** (*zhèngcháng*) is built from 正 'right, proper' and 常, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyengH* and *dzyang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second as a length of two fathoms and as an official's title; the sense 'usual' is not given there, so how the pair came to mean normal is not settled.
