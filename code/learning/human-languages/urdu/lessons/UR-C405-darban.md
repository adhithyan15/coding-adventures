## You'll want to know: دربان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C405-RAJISTAR405-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دربان** — *darbān* — "a doorman".

> دربان نے ہمارے لیے دروازہ کھولا۔
>
> *darbān ne hamāre liye darvāzā kholā.*
>
> The doorman opened the door for us.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دربان** (*darbān*) is built from Persian *dar*, "a door", and *-bān*, "a keeper": it is usually traced to Persian *darbān*, "a doorkeeper".
