## You'll want to know: multar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C623-SANCION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**multar** — "to fine".

> Me multaron por ir rápido.
>
> *They fined me for speeding.*

The noun is *la multa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*multar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *multare* 'to punish, to fine', built on *multa* 'a fine' (EtymDB).
