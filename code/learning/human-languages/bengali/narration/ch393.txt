Bengali, chapter 393: Fuel and Flat Tyres.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ডিজেল (ḍijel) — diesel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a signboard, then the Bengali for to keep close to.

You'll want to know: ডিজেল.
ডিজেল — ḍijel — "diesel".
ট্রাকগুলো ডিজেলে চলে। ṭrākgulo ḍijele chôle. Lorries run on diesel.

The word, taken apart.
ডিজেল (ḍijel) is borrowed from English diesel, named after the engineer Rudolf Diesel; the English z sound is said with জ.

You'll want to know: হাওয়া.
হাওয়া — hāoyā — "air; a breeze".
পেট্রোল পাম্পে আমরা চাকায় হাওয়া ভরলাম। peṭrol pāmpe āmrā chākāy hāoyā bhôrlām. At the petrol station we put air in the tyres.
Also the breeze you feel: বাতাস is the wind; হাওয়া (hāoyā) is the air moving, or the air in a tyre.

The word, taken apart.
হাওয়া (hāoyā) is usually traced to Arabic hawāʾ, "air", which came into Bengali through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ফুরানো (phurāno) — to run out; to be used up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for air, then the Bengali for a puncture.

You'll want to know: ফুরানো.
ফুরানো — phurāno — "to run out; to be used up".
পথে গাড়ির পেট্রোল ফুরিয়ে গেল। pôthe gāṛir peṭrol phuriye gelô. The car ran out of petrol on the way.
Past (he/she …): সেই পেট্রোল ফুরিয়ে গেল, sei peṭrol phuriye gelô.
The thing that runs out is the subject: টাকা ফুরিয়ে গেল, the money ran out.

The word, taken apart.
ফুরানো (phurāno) is an everyday Bengali verb whose origin is not settled; CDIAL gives no Bengali form that matches it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
চালু করা (chālu kôrā) — to start (an engine); to switch on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a puncture, then the Bengali for to run out.

You'll want to know: চালু করা.
চালু করা — chālu kôrā — "to start (an engine); to switch on".
চালক ইঞ্জিন চালু করলেন। chālôk injin chālu kôrlen. The driver started the engine.
Past (he/she …): তিনি পাখা চালু করলেন, tini pākhā chālu kôrlen.
চালু on its own is running, in use: দোকানটা চালু, the shop is open for business.

The word, taken apart.
চালু করা (chālu kôrā) joins চালু, "going, in working order", probably a Bengali formation on চাল, "gait, motion", which CDIAL lists under the reconstructed calyā (CDIAL 4722), to করা, to do.
