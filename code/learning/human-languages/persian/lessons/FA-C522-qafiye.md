## You'll want to know: قافیه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C522-VERSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قافیه** (*qâfiye*) — "a rhyme".

> این دو کلمه با هم قافیه دارند.
>
> *in do kalame bâ ham qâfiye dârand.*
>
> These two words rhyme.

*qâfiye dâshtan* — to rhyme. Plural *qavâfi* (Arabic).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qâfiye* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *qāfiya*, 'rhyme'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
