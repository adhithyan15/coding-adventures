## You'll want to know: किंकिणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C522-PADANYASA522-03]; assesses=[] -->

**किंकिणी** — *kiṅkiṇī* — "a small bell".

> नर्तकस्य पादे किंकिण्यः सन्ति।
>
> *nartakasya pāde kiṅkiṇyaḥ santi.*
>
> The dancer has small bells on his foot.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किंकिणी** (*kiṅkiṇī*) is the word MW records for a small bell; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
