## You'll want to know: حجره
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C457-RASTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حجره** (*hojre*) — "a shop or office in the bazaar; a small room".

> پدربزرگم در بازار یک حجره داشت.
>
> *Pedarbozorgam dar bâzâr yek hojre dâsht.*
>
> My grandfather had a shop in the bazaar.

Arabic plural *hojarât*. Also a student's cell in an old school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hojre* was borrowed from Arabic *ḥujra*, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'a room, a chamber'.
