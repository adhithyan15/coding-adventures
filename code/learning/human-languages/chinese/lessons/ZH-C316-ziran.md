## You'll want to know: 自然
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C316-PLACE316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**自然** — *zìrán* — "naturally; nature".

> **又下雨了我们自然不出门** *yòu xiàyǔ le, wǒmen zìrán bù chūmén* — It's raining again, so naturally we're staying in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**自然** (*zìrán*) is built from 自 and 然 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzijH* and *nyen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'from' and 'by way of'; its sense 'by itself' is not given there. So of itself.
