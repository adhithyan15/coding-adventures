## You'll want to know: el propósito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C615-PLAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el propósito** — "the purpose, the aim; the resolution".

> Mi propósito es aprender inglés.
>
> *My aim is to learn English.*

*A propósito* — on purpose; by the way. *Los propósitos de Año Nuevo* — New Year resolutions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*propósito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *propositum* 'that which is proposed, an aim', built on *proponere* 'to put forward' (EtymDB).
