## You'll want to know: ਪੁੱਗਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C280-CHANGE280-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੁੱਗਣਾ** — *puggṇā* — "to be affordable; to be worth it".

> ਮਹਿੰਗਾ ਕਿਰਾਇਆ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੁੱਗਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *mahiṅgā kirāiā mainū̃ nahīṁ puggdā hai.*
>
> I cannot afford an expensive fare.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਸੌਦਾ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੁੱਗਿਆ, *oh saudā mainū̃ puggiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੁੱਗਣਾ** (*puggṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pūryate*, "is filled"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *puggṇā*, "to be completed, reach", under it (CDIAL 8342). The sense "to be affordable" is not given there.
