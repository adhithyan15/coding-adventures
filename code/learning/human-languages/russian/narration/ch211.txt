Russian, chapter 211: Muscle, Bone, Pulse, Pressure, Strength.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
мышца (mýshtsa) — a muscle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sticking plaster, then the Russian for a bandage.

You'll want to know: мышца.
мышца (mýshtsa) — "a muscle".

The word, taken apart.
мышца (mýshtsa) is built on мышь, "a mouse", as Latin mūsculus, "a little mouse; a muscle", and Greek mŷs, "mouse; muscle" (Vasmer): a flexing muscle looks like a mouse under the skin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
кость (kost) — a bone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bandage, then the Russian for a muscle.

You'll want to know: кость.
кость (kost) — "a bone".

The word, taken apart.
кость (kost) from Proto-Slavic kostĭ — inherited, related to Latin costa, "a rib" (Vasmer). Some also link it with Greek ostéon and Latin os, "bone".

Lesson 4 of 5.
давление (davléniye) — pressure; blood pressure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bone, then the Russian for a pulse.

You'll want to know: давление.
давление (davléniye) — "pressure; blood pressure".

The word, taken apart.
давление (davléniye) is a noun from давить, "to press, to squeeze". Vasmer relates давить to Avestan dav-, "to press".
