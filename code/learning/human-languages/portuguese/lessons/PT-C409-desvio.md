## You'll want to know: o desvio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C409-FILAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o desvio** — "the diversion, the detour".

> Por causa das obras, há um desvio pela aldeia.
>
> *Because of the roadworks, there is a diversion through the village.*

The verb is *desviar*, to divert or to turn aside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desvio* is built on *desviar* 'to turn aside', which is inherited from Latin *dēviāre*, on *via* 'way' (EtymDB).
