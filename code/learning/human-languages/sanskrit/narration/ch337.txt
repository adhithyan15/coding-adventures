Sanskrit, chapter 337: Exile in the Forest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वनवासः (vanavāsaḥ) — life in the forest; exile in the forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for bewildered, then the Sanskrit for tempts.

You'll want to know: वनवासः.
वनवासः — vanavāsaḥ — "life in the forest; exile in the forest".
वनवासे सः फलानि खादति। vanavāse saḥ phalāni khādati. In his forest exile he lives on fruit.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वनवासः (vanavāsaḥ) is built from vana, "a forest", and vāsa, "dwelling", from the root √vas, as in vasati; MW gives vanavāsa as dwelling in a forest (MW).
