## You'll want to know: dēfōrmis, dēfōrme
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C548-VENU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dēfōrmis, dēfōrme** — "ugly, misshapen".

> Statua dēfōrmis est, sed antīqua.
>
> *The statue is ugly, but old.*

Two endings. *dē-* 'away from' + *fōrma* 'shape, beauty'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēfōrmis** is built on *dē-* "away from" and *fōrma* "shape, beauty" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *deformed* comes from the related verb *dēfōrmō*.
