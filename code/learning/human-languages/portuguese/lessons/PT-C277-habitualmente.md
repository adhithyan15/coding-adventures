## You'll want to know: habitualmente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C277-FREQUENCIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**habitualmente** — "usually, habitually".

> Habitualmente, almoço na cantina.
>
> *I usually have lunch in the canteen.*

*Normalmente* means much the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*habitualmente* is built on *habitual* (EtymDB), from Latin *habitus* "condition, habit", from *habēre* "to have, to hold" (Lewis & Short).
