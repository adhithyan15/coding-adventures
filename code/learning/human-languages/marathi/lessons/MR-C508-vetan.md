## You'll want to know: वेतन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C508-PAGAR508-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वेतन** — *vetan* — "a wage; pay".

> या महिन्याचं वेतन उशिरा आलं.
>
> *yā mahinyācaṁ vetan uśirā ālaṁ.*
>
> This month's pay came late.

Neuter.

वेतन is the formal word beside पगार.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेतन** (*vetan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vetana*, "wages, hire, salary" (Monier-Williams).
