# এই জামাটা (ei jāmāṭā) — this shirt, the one here

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C21-EI-01, BN-LEX-C21-OI-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-CLASSIFIER-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **this shirt**, then **that shirt**, then **the shirt**.

## You'll want to know: both ends at once
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C21-DEM-ORDER-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C21-EI-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-CLASSIFIER-01] -->

> **এই জামাটা** — *ei jāmāṭā* — **this shirt** (the one here, and we both know
> which).

> **ওই কাপড়টা** — *oi kāpôṛṭā* — **that cloth.**

English cannot do this. *The this shirt* is not English, because *the* and
*this* are fighting over one slot in front of the noun and only one of them can
win. In Bengali they are nowhere near each other:

**এই** + **জামা** + **টা**
· pointer · noun · classifier

Front and back. No competition, so no choice to make — and a Bengali speaker
uses both far more often than an English speaker would expect, because leaving
the classifier off sounds unfinished.

## Grammar Lens: the fixed order
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C21-DEM-ORDER-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-EKTA-01, BN-LEX-C14-LAL-01] -->

Once a number and an adjective join in, the order still does not move:

| slot | what goes there | example |
|---|---|---|
| first | pointer | **এই** |
| second | number | **একটা** |
| third | adjective | **লাল** |
| last | noun + classifier | **জামাটা** |

Everything before the noun is in a fixed queue, and the only thing that ever
attaches to the noun itself is the classifier at the back. Bengali will keep
adding suffixes at that back end for the rest of this book, always one at a
time, always in order.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C21-DEM-ORDER-01, BN-LEX-C21-EI-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-CLASSIFIER-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Can a pointer and a classifier appear in the same phrase? (**Yes** —
they sit at opposite ends.) Why can English not do the same? (**Its *the* and
its *this* want the same slot**.) And which of the two attaches to the noun
itself? (**The classifier**, at the back.)
