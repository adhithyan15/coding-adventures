# Fictional date B — change two small pieces

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-A-01] -->

Point to A, **੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫**. Its middle box means January.

## Reading — one contrast
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-DATE-B-01]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-MONTH-MAP-01] -->

Practice card B says “the twenty-fifth of February, two thousand
twenty-five.” The day changes to **੨੫**; February changes the middle box to
**੦੨**; the year stays **੨੦੨੫**. The complete fictional date is
**੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫**. Compare one box at a time with A.

## Guided Practice — copy, then compare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-B-01, PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy B once. Circle only the two boxes that differ from A. Say “day” for the
first and “month” for the second; do not change the year or slash marks.
