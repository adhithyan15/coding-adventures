## You'll want to know: निर्णय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C536-YOJNA536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्णय** — *nirṇay* — "a decision (formal)".

> हमने गाँव में घर बनाने का निर्णय लिया।
>
> *Hamne gā̃v meṁ ghar banāne kā nirṇay liyā.*
>
> We decided to build a house in the village.

Masculine.

A formal word; everyday speech says फ़ैसला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्णय** (*nirṇay*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *nirṇaya*, "decision, determination, settlement" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
