## You'll want to know: biānpào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C335-LANTERN335-05]; assesses=[] -->

*biānpào* — "firecrackers".

> *Chúxī wǎnshang shí'èr diǎn wàibian de rén dōu zài fàng biānpào* — At midnight on the last night of the year everyone outside is letting off firecrackers.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biānpào* is built from *biān* 'a whip' and *pào*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjien* and *phaewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a horse whip' and the second 'the look of something scorched'.
