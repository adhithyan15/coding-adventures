Marathi, chapter 118: Forty, Fifty, Sixty, Seventy, Ninety.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चाळीस (chāḷīs) — forty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sandal, then the Marathi for thirty.

You'll want to know: चाळीस.
चाळीस — chāḷīs — "forty".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does चाळीस (chāḷīs) mean? ("Forty".) Say it once more.
