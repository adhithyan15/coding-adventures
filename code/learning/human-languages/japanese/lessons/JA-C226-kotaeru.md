## You'll want to know: こたえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C226-EXAM226-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こたえる** — *kotaeru* — "to reply; to answer".

> **せんせいは しつもんに ていねいに こたえます。** — *sensei wa shitsumon ni teinei ni kotaemasu* — "The teacher replies to questions politely."

Polite **こたえます**, *kotaemasu*; an ichidan verb. The noun **こたえ**, *kotae*, "an answer", comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こたえる** (*kotaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
