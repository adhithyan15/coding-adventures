## You'll want to know: perezoso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C729-PEREZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**perezoso** — "lazy".

> Los domingos soy muy perezoso.
>
> *On Sundays I am very lazy.*

*Un perezoso* is also the animal, a sloth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perezoso* is built on *pereza* "laziness" with the ending *-oso*; *pereza* is usually traced to Latin *pigritia*.
