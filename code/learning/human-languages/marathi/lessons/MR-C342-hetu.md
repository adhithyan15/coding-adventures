## You'll want to know: हेतू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C342-HETU342-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हेतू** — *hetū* — "a motive; an intention".

> त्याच्या मदतीमागचा हेतू चांगला होता.
>
> *tyācyā madtīmāgcā hetū cāṅglā hotā.*
>
> The motive behind his help was good.

Masculine.

हेतूने, with the intention: शिकण्याच्या हेतूने, with the aim of learning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हेतू** (*hetū*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *hetu*, "impulse, motive, cause" (Monier-Williams). Marathi lengthens the final vowel.
