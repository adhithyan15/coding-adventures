# झ — the puff, on the letter you drew last

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-132] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: **ज** — what sound does it carry?

[PAUSE 2s] You know what is coming. It has happened twice already.

## Script you'll notice: झ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-133]; assesses=[] -->

**झ** — *jha*.

It is the **breathy partner of ज**, the third such pair you have met. Say *ja*,
then say it again with a puff of air behind it.

What it is made of:

- a joined short upper stem, upper bowl, lower loop, and diagonal tail
- a left-to-right middle crossbar
- a top-to-bottom right stem
- the top shirorekhā

## Script: the third pair
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-133, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-132] -->

| plain | breathy |
|---|---|
| त | थ |
| च | छ |
| ज | झ |

Three pairs now, one distinction. **This script pairs its letters by breath**,
and each pair has cost less than the last because the idea was learned once and
only the shapes change.

This one arrives **before** any word that needs it — the first letter in this
book to do so. There is nothing to find it inside yet. Learn the shape and the
breath, and the word will arrive already readable.

## Writing: झ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-133] -->

- **1.** descend the short upper stem, turn left and curve clockwise around the upper bowl, continue through the waist and clockwise around the lower loop, then finish down-right through the diagonal tail without lifting
- **2.** lift at the central junction and draw the middle crossbar left-to-right
- **3.** lift and draw the right stem top-to-bottom
- **4.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 3.** The pen comes up 3 times and no more — one more than **ज**,
because of the crossbar.

> Verified four-stroke teaching form fitted to the bundled printed outline; everyday handwriting may join the crossbar to the bowl.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Opiaterein, ‘Deva-झ-order.gif’, strokes 1–4, Wikimedia Commons, 10 May 2009.
