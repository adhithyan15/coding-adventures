## You'll want to know: अशक्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C480-TABYET480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अशक्त** — *aśakta* — "weak".

> तापामुळे ती अशक्त झाली आहे.
>
> *tāpāmuḷe tī aśakta jhālī āhe.*
>
> The fever has left her weak.

अ-, not, + शक्त, able; अशक्तपणा, weakness. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अशक्त** (*aśakta*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *aśakta*, "unable, incompetent" (CDIAL 905).
