## You'll want to know: बन्दी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C319-VIJAYA319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बन्दी** — *bandī* — "a captive; a prisoner of war".

> एकः बन्दी कारागारे अस्ति।
>
> *ekaḥ bandī kārāgāre asti.*
>
> One captive is in prison.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बन्दी** (*bandī*) MW gives this *bandin* (also written *vandin*) as a prisoner, captive, slave, without a derivation in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
