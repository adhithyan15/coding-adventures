## You'll want to know: непослушный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C497-UPBRINGING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**непослушный** (*neposlúshnyy*) — "disobedient, naughty".

> Непослушный ребёнок убежал от мамы.
>
> *Neposlúshnyy rebyónok ubezhál ot mámy.*
>
> The naughty child ran away from his mum.

The opposite is *послушный* — obedient.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*непослушный* is built on *не-* and *послушный* 'obedient', from *слушать* 'to listen'; EtymDB has no entry.
