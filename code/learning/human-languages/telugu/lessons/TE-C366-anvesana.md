# అన్వేషణ (anvēṣaṇa) — a quest; a search

## You'll want to know: అన్వేషణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C366-NIDHI366-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అన్వేషణ** — *anvēṣaṇa* — "a quest; a search".

> నిధి కోసం అతని అన్వేషణ ఏళ్ళ తరబడి సాగింది.
>
> *nidhi kōsaṁ atani anvēṣaṇa ēḷḷa tarabaḍi sāgindi.*
>
> His search for the treasure went on for years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అన్వేషణ** (*anvēṣaṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anveṣaṇa*, "seeking for, searching, investigating" (Monier-Williams).
