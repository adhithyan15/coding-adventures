## You'll want to know: व्यतिरिक्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C390-EKUN390-05]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यतिरिक्त** — *vyatirikta* — "apart from; besides".

> मराठीच्या व्यतिरिक्त त्याला आणखी दोन भाषा येतात.
>
> *marāṭhīcyā vyatirikta tyālā āṇkhī don bhāṣā yetāt.*
>
> Apart from Marathi he knows two more languages.

After -च्या, or attached: मराठीव्यतिरिक्त.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यतिरिक्त** (*vyatirikta*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vyatirikta*, "left apart, excluded" (Monier-Williams).
