## You'll want to know: 读音
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C249-STUDY249-03]; assesses=[] -->

**读音** — *dúyīn* — "pronunciation; how a character is read".

> **这个字的读音是什么** *zhège zì de dúyīn shì shénme* — How do you say this character?

The new character, in pieces: 读 — ten strokes: the speech piece 讠 on the left; on the right a short 十 over 买. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**读音** (*dúyīn*) is built from 读 'to read aloud' and 音 'a sound', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duwk* and *'im* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to recite' and quotes an older dictionary for the second: 'sound'.
