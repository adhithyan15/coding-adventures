# খিটখিটে (khiṭkhiṭe) — irritable; crotchety

## You'll want to know: খিটখিটে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C443-KHOBH443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**খিটখিটে** — *khiṭkhiṭe* — "irritable; crotchety".

> জ্বর হলে দাদু খিটখিটে হয়ে যান।
>
> *jbôr hôle dādu khiṭkhiṭe hôye jān.*
>
> When he has a fever, my grandfather gets crotchety.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খিটখিটে** (*khiṭkhiṭe*) is an expressive word, its sound standing for a scratchy, easily annoyed temper; its origin is not settled.
