## You'll want to know: le radiateur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C427-CHAMBRE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le radiateur** — "the radiator".

> Elle a mis ses chaussettes sur le radiateur.
>
> *She put her socks on the radiator.*

Also the radiator of a car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*radiateur* is a learned formation on Latin *radiāre* 'to send out rays', from *radius* 'ray' (EtymDB).
