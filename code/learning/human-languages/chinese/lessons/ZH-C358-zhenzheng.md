## You'll want to know: 真正
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C358-MANNERS358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**真正** — *zhēnzhèng* — "real; genuine".

> **我觉得这是真正的中国菜** *wǒ juéde zhè shì zhēnzhèng de Zhōngguó cài* — I think this is real Chinese food.

The new character, in pieces: 真 — ten strokes: 十 *shí* on top, a narrow box with two bars inside it under that, a long bar across, and two short dots under it, left then right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**真正** (*zhēnzhèng*) is built from 真 'true' and 正 'proper', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyin* and *tsyengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'true, as against false' and the second 'proper'.
