## You'll want to know: ત્રાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C453-BIK453-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ત્રાસ** — *trās* — "torment; harassment".

> મચ્છરનો ત્રાસ આખી રાત ચાલ્યો.
>
> *maccharno trās ākhī rāt cālyo.*
>
> The mosquitoes tormented us all night.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ત્રાસ** (*trās*) is inherited from Sanskrit *trāsa*, "fear"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "fear, vexation" (CDIAL 6013), and today it is mostly the vexation.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
