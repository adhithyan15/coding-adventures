## You'll want to know: हिमम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C31-SKY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हिमम्** (*himam*) — "snow, frost".

**हिमम्** is snow, and cold generally: the word covers the frost on a field and the season it belongs to.

Its cousins name the season rather than the snow: Greek *kheimōn* and Latin *hiems*, both "winter", the second giving English *hibernate*. And the mountains carry it: *Himālaya* is **हिमम्** joined to *ālaya*, "a dwelling" — the abode of snow. Neuter **-अम्**, like **वस्त्रम्**.
