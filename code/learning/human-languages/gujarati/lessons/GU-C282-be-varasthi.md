# બે વરસથી (be varasthī) — for how long, since when

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C282-SUDHI-01, GU-LEX-C92-TIME92-04, GU-LEX-SAVAAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "until evening". (*sānj sudhī*.) Then "a year" and "morning". (**વરસ**, **સવાર**.)

## Grammar Lens: -થી and the present
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C282-THI-SINCE-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C101-DEF101-04, GU-CONCEPT-C05-RAHEVUN-01, GU-LEX-C177-OFTEN177-03, GU-Q-KETLA-01] -->

> **હું બે વરસથી અહીં રહું છું.**
>
> *hũ be varasthī ahī̃ rahũ chũ.* — "I have lived here for two years."

**-થી**, "from", on a length of time or a starting point says how long something has gone on, and the verb stays in the present: it is still going on. English says "have lived"; Gujarati says "I live, from two years". **સવારથી વરસાદ પડે છે** (*savārthī varsād paḍe che*): "it has been raining since morning". To ask: **તમે કેટલા વખતથી અહીં રહો છો?** (*tame keṭlā vakhatthī ahī̃ raho cho?*), "how long have you lived here?"

## You'll want to know: from … until, and all day
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C282-THI-SUDHI-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-DUKAAN-01, GU-LEX-C151-READ151-02, GU-LEX-C173-WEEK173-05, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01] -->

> **દુકાન સવારથી સાંજ સુધી ખુલ્લી છે.**
>
> *dukān savārthī sānj sudhī khullī che.* — "The shop is open from morning until evening."

**-થી** marks the start and **સુધી** the end. A span with no start or end named takes **આખું**, "whole", and neither **-થી** nor **સુધી**: **મેં આખો દિવસ કામ કર્યું** (*mẽ ākho divas kām karyũ*), "I worked all day".
