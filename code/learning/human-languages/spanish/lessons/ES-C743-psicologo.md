## You'll want to know: el psicólogo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C743-MENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el psicólogo** — "the psychologist".

> Mi hermana es psicóloga.
>
> *My sister is a psychologist.*

The *p* is often silent in speech; *sicólogo* is also accepted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*psicólogo* is built on Greek *psykhḗ* "breath, soul, mind" and *lógos* "word, study": one who studies the mind.
