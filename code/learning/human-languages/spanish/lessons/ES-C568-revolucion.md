## You'll want to know: la revolución
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C568-REY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la revolución** — "the revolution".

> La revolución cambió el país.
>
> *The revolution changed the country.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revolución* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *revolūtiō* 'a rolling back, a revolving' (EtymDB), built on *re-* and *volvere* 'to roll, to turn'.
