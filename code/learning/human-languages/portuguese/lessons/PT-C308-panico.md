## You'll want to know: o pânico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C308-SUSTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o pânico** — "panic".

> Houve pânico no centro comercial.
>
> *There was panic in the shopping centre.*

*Entrar em pânico* — to panic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pânico* comes from Greek *panikós* "of Pan" (EtymDB), the god of the wild whose sudden cry was said to spread terror.
