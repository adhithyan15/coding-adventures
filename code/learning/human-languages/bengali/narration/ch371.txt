Bengali, chapter 371: Enough or Too Much.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
যথেষ্ট (jôtheshṭô) — enough; plenty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for less, then the Bengali for a bit.

You'll want to know: যথেষ্ট.
যথেষ্ট — jôtheshṭô — "enough; plenty".
যথেষ্ট টাকা থাকলে আমরা নতুন বাড়ি কিনব। jôtheshṭô ṭākā thākle āmrā nôtun bāṛi kinbô. If we have enough money, we'll buy a new house.

The word, taken apart.
যথেষ্ট (jôtheshṭô) is Sanskrit yatheṣṭa, 'as much as one wishes' (Monier-Williams). Bengali turned it into plain 'enough'.

You'll want to know: অতিরিক্ত.
অতিরিক্ত — ôtiriktô — "extra; excessive".
অতিরিক্ত ব্যাগ নিলে বেশি ভাড়া দিতে হবে। ôtiriktô byāg nile beshi bhāṛā dite hôbe. If you take an extra bag, you will have to pay a higher fare.

The word, taken apart.
অতিরিক্ত (ôtiriktô) is Sanskrit atirikta, left over as a surplus (Monier-Williams): more than is needed, extra or too much.

Lesson 3 of 5.
অল্প (ôlpô) — a little; a small amount.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for enough, then the Bengali for excessive.

You'll want to know: অল্প.
অল্প — ôlpô — "a little; a small amount".
অল্প নুন দিলে তরকারি ভালো হবে। ôlpô nun dile tôrkāri bhālo hôbe. If you add a little salt, the curry will be good.

The word, taken apart.
অল্প (ôlpô) is Sanskrit alpa, small or little (Monier-Williams). It is a little more formal than একটু and can stand before a noun.

You'll want to know: জোগানো.
জোগানো — jogāno — "to supply; to provide (for)".
সরকার টাকা জোগালে নতুন স্কুল হবে। sôrkār ṭākā jogāle nôtun skul hôbe. If the government provides the money, there will be a new school.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সবার খাবার জোগালেন, tini sôbār khābār jogālen.

The word, taken apart.
জোগানো (jogāno) is listed by CDIAL 10529 (yōgyā́) as Bengali jogāna, 'to supply': to fit out what is needed.

Lesson 5 of 5.
সীমা (sīmā) — a limit; a boundary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a small amount, then the Bengali for to supply.

You'll want to know: সীমা.
সীমা — sīmā — "a limit; a boundary".
খরচ সীমার বেশি হলে আমরা কাজটা বন্ধ করব। khôrôch sīmār beshi hôle āmrā kājṭā bôndhô kôrbô. If the cost goes over the limit, we'll stop the work.

The word, taken apart.
সীমা (sīmā) is Sanskrit sīmā, a boundary (Monier-Williams). It is the border of a field and any limit not to be passed.
