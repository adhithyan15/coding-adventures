Gujarati, chapter 487: Caring for Grandmother.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચાકરી (cākrī) — nursing care; looking after.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for diet, then the Gujarati for to waste away.

You'll want to know: ચાકરી.
ચાકરી — cākrī — "nursing care; looking after".
વહુએ સાસુની ઘણી ચાકરી કરી. vahue sāsunī ghaṇī cākrī karī. The daughter-in-law looked after her mother-in-law devotedly.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચાકરી (cākrī) is usually traced to Persian čākarī, "service", built on čākar, "a servant".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for consoling words, then the Gujarati for a massage.

You'll want to know: દાબવું.
દાબવું — dābvũ — "to massage by pressing; to press (the legs) to ease pain".
રાતે મીરા દાદીના પગ દાબે છે. rāte Mīrā dādīnā pag dābe che. At night Mira presses her grandmother's legs to ease them.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દાદીના પગ દાબ્યા, teṇe dādīnā pag dābyā.

The word, taken apart.
દાબવું (dābvũ) goes back to a reconstructed dabb-, "press", which some trace to Dravidian; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to press" (CDIAL 6173).

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દાદીને ધાબળો ઓઢાડ્યો, teṇe dādīne dhābaḷo oḍhāḍyo.

ઓઢાડવું (oḍhāḍvũ) is the causative of ઓઢવું (oḍhvũ), "to wrap oneself in", which goes back to a reconstructed ōḍḍh-, "put on, cover" (CDIAL 2547).
