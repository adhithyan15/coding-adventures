## You'll want to know: verwirrt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C474-STAUNEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**verwirrt** — "confused".

> Nach der langen Reise war ich müde und verwirrt.
>
> *After the long journey I was tired and confused.*

The participle of *verwirren* — to confuse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verwirrt* is built on *ver-* and *wirren* "to move in confusion", from Middle High German *werren* and Old High German *werran*, from Proto-Germanic *werraną* "to confuse" (EtymDB).
