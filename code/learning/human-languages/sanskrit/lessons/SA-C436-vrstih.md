## You'll want to know: वृष्टिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C436-ABHRA436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वृष्टिः** — *vṛṣṭiḥ* — "rainfall; a shower".

> अद्य बहु वृष्टिः भविष्यति।
>
> *adya bahu vṛṣṭiḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> Today there will be a lot of rainfall.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वृष्टिः** (*vṛṣṭiḥ*) is built on the root √vṛṣ, "to rain", which also gives *varṣati*, "rains", with the ending *-ti*; MW gives *vṛṣṭi* as rain (MW).
