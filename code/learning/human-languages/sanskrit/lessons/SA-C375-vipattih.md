## You'll want to know: विपत्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C375-VIPATTI375-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विपत्तिः** — *vipattiḥ* — "adversity; a calamity".

> यदि विपत्तिः आगच्छति, तर्हि मित्राणि साहाय्यं करिष्यन्ति।
>
> *yadi vipattiḥ āgacchati, tarhi mitrāṇi sāhāyyaṃ kariṣyanti.*
>
> If adversity comes, friends will help.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विपत्तिः** (*vipattiḥ*) is built on *vi*, "apart", and the root √pad, "to go, to fall"; MW gives *vi-patti* "going wrongly, adversity, misfortune, failure, disaster" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
