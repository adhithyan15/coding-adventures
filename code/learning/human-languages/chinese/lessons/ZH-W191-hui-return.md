## Script — 口 inside a larger frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HUI-RETURN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 回

**Two pieces: a large frame, and 口 inside it.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The frame, first two strokes.** A vertical down the left side, then a stroke along the top that turns down the right side.

**口 inside, three strokes:** a short vertical, a stroke that runs right and turns down, and the bar that closes it.

**Last, the frame's foot.** One bar along the bottom closes the large frame, the way the last stroke of 国 does.

2 + 3 + 1 = six.
