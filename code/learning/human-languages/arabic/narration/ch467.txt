Arabic, chapter 467: Protecting nature.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
محمية (maḥmiyya) — a nature reserve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a sand dune, then the Arabic for to choke.

You'll want to know: محمية.
محمية — maḥmiyya — "a nature reserve".
زرنا محمية للطيور على الساحل. zurnā maḥmiyya li-ṭ-ṭuyūr ʿalā as-sāḥil. We visited a bird reserve on the coast.

The word, taken apart.
محمية (maḥmiyya) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-m-y, "to protect, to defend" (Lane). It is a passive participle: a protected place.
