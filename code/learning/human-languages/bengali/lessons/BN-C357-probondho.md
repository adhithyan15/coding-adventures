## You'll want to know: প্রবন্ধ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C357-PROBONDHO357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রবন্ধ** — *prôbôndhô* — "an essay; an article".

> মীরা পত্রিকায় একটা প্রবন্ধ লিখল।
>
> *mīrā pôtrikāy ekṭā prôbôndhô likhlô.*
>
> Mira wrote an article in a magazine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রবন্ধ** (*prôbôndhô*) is Sanskrit *prabandha*, a binding together (Monier-Williams), and so a connected piece of writing. Bengali uses it for an essay or an article.
