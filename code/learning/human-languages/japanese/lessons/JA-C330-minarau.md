## You'll want to know: みならう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C330-CHANTO330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**みならう** — *minarau* — "to follow an example; to model oneself on".

> **あには よく べんきょうするから、みならった ほうが いいです。** — *ani wa yoku benkyō suru kara, minaratta hō ga ii desu* — "Your brother studies hard, so you should follow his example."

Polite **みならいます**, *minaraimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おとうとは あにを みならいました。**, *otōto wa ani o minaraimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みならう** (*minarau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *mi*, "seeing (miru)" and *narau*, "to learn", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
