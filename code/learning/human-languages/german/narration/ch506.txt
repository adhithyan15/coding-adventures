German, chapter 506: Auf dem Platz.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Torwart — goalkeeper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to stretch, then the German for sore muscles.

You'll want to know: der Torwart.
der Torwart — "goalkeeper".
Der Torwart hat den Ball sicher gefangen. The goalkeeper caught the ball safely.
Plural: die Torwarte. Also der Torhüter.

The word, taken apart.
Torwart is built on Tor "gate, goal" (EtymDB) and Wart "keeper", from warten, inherited from Old High German wartēn "to watch, to look after" (EtymDB).

der Verteidiger — "defender".
Der Verteidiger stoppte den Stürmer kurz vor dem Tor. The defender stopped the striker shortly before the goal.
Plural: die Verteidiger. Also a defence lawyer.

The word, taken apart.
Verteidiger is built on verteidigen "to defend", usually traced to Middle High German vertagedingen "to plead before a court"; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
pfeifen — to whistle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for defender, then the German for referee.

You'll want to know: pfeifen.
pfeifen — "to whistle".
Der Schiedsrichter pfeift, und das Spiel beginnt. The referee whistles, and the game begins.
Irregular: pfiff, hat gepfiffen. Anpfeifen, abpfeifen — to start, to end a match.

The word, taken apart.
pfeifen is inherited from Middle High German pfīfen and Old High German phīfōn, an early borrowing from Latin pīpāre "to peep, to chirp" (EtymDB).
