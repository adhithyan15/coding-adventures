Portuguese, chapter 91: Underground, Tram, Journey, Holidays, Bicycle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o metro — the underground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a bakery, then the Portuguese for a butcher's.

You'll want to know: o metro.
o metro — "the underground". In Brazil, o metrô.

The word, taken apart.
metro from French métro (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. It is short for métropolitain, built from Greek mētēr, "mother", and polis, "city".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a butcher's, then the Portuguese for the underground.

You'll want to know: o elétrico.
o elétrico — "a tram". In Brazil, o bonde.

The word, taken apart.
elétrico from New Latin electricus, "of amber" (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing from New Latin. The tram is the electric car; electricus is built on Greek ēlektron, "amber".

The word, taken apart.
