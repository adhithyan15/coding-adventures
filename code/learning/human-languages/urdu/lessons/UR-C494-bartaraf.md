## You'll want to know: برطرف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C494-ROZGAR494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**برطرف** — *bartaraf* — "dismissed from a job".

> کمپنی نے دو ملازموں کو برطرف کیا۔
>
> *kampanī ne do mulāzimoṅ ko bartaraf kiyā.*
>
> The company dismissed two employees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برطرف** (*bartaraf*) is usually traced to Persian *bar-taraf*, "set aside", built from *bar*, "on, to", and *taraf*, "a side", the word Urdu also has as **طرف** (*taraf*); Urdu uses it for someone put out of a job.
