# My own line — the first Japanese with no model

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C138-OKIMASU] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Everything you have written so far had something to reach for: a
sign to trace, a word to copy, a word someone said.

[PAUSE 1s] This does not. You choose the words, from a supply that is closed:
only what this book has taught you.

## Writing — controlled composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C53-ADJ53-05, JA-LEX-C138-OKIMASU, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YU-01, JA-PARTICLE-WA-SPELLING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **one line** about your own day, in hiragana. A time word
first, then what you do, ending on a polite verb in **ます** and a small
circle, **。**, after it. You do not need to say who; you learned in chapter
138 that Japanese leaves that out when it is clear. If you mark a topic with
*wa*, write it **は**.

[PAUSE 2s] Before you start, one thing to decide on purpose rather than by
accident: **a small sign is a different sign, not a smaller copy.**

[PAUSE 2s] **っ** is not **つ** written lazily. It is one held beat, and it
sits small and low in its square. Write **きつぷ** for **きっぷ**, a ticket,
and you have written a word that does not exist. The same goes for
**ゅ** in **しゅみ**: full size, it reads as two beats instead of one.

[PAUSE 2s] Two more checks belong to the hand, not the eye. Give every sign
**the same size of square**, small signs included. And write each sign in the
**stroke order** you learned: the order does not show on the finished line,
but it is what keeps the shape right when you write fast, which the next
lesson will ask you to do.

[PAUSE 3s] Write the line. Then read it back and check the **small signs**
before you check anything else.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01, JA-PARTICLE-WA-SPELLING] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Is a small **っ** just **つ**, written smaller? (**No** — it is
another sign, one held beat.)

[PAUSE 2s] If your line has a topic marked **wa**, which sign did you write
for it? (**は**, as you learned in the first chapters.)

[PAUSE 2s] And what did you choose here that the earlier lessons chose for
you? (**The words.**)
