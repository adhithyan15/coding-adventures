## You'll want to know: ਗੰਢ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C229-DIN229-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੰਢ** — *gaṇḍh* — "a knot".

> ਰੱਸੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਗੰਢ ਹੈ।
>
> *rassī vich ikk gaṇḍh hai.*
>
> There is a knot in the rope.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੰਢ** (*gaṇḍh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *granthi*, "knot, bunch"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gaṇḍh*, "knot, joint" (CDIAL 4354). CDIAL marks the Punjabi row masculine and the Lahnda row feminine; here ਗੰਢ is taught as feminine, as the Lahnda row has it.
