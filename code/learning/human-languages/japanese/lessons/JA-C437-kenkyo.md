## You'll want to know: けんきょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C437-HONEST437-04]; assesses=[] -->

**けんきょ** — *kenkyo* — "modest; humble".

> **あの せんしゅは けんきょです。** — *ano senshu wa kenkyo desu* — "That athlete is modest."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けんきょ** (*kenkyo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ken*, "be modest" (Middle Chinese *khem*) and *kyo*, "emptiness" (*xjo*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
