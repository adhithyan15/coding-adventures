## You'll want to know: ろせん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C376-SHINKANSEN376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ろせん** — *rosen* — "a route; a line (of trains or buses)".

> **ちかてつの ろせんは ふくざつです。** — *chikatetsu no rosen wa fukuzatsu desu* — "The subway routes are complicated."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ろせん** (*rosen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ro*, "route" (Middle Chinese *luH*) and *sen*, "line" (*sjenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
