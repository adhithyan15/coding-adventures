## You'll want to know: રબર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C245-SPELL245-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રબર** — *rabar* — "eraser, rubber".

> **મારું રબર ક્યાં છે?** — *mārũ rabar kyā̃ che?* — "Where is my eraser?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રબર** (*rabar*) is a loan from English *rubber*, the material and the eraser made of it.
