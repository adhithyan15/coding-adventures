# વિપરીત (viprīt) — adverse (going against you)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C547-CHATA547-03, GU-LEX-C547-CHATA547-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for despite, then the Gujarati for rather.

## You'll want to know: વિપરીત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C547-CHATA547-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વિપરીત** — *viprīt* — "adverse (going against you)".

> જો હવામાન વિપરીત હશે તો અમે પ્રવાસ ઠેલીશું.
>
> *jo havāmān viprīt haśe to ame pravās ṭhelīśũ.*
>
> If the weather turns against us, we will put off the trip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિપરીત** (*viprīt*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *viparīta*, "turned round; contrary" (Monier-Williams). Said of weather or of luck, it means going against you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
