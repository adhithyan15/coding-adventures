## You'll want to know: точка зрения
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C337-TOCHKAZRENIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**точка зрения** (*tóchka zréniya*) — "a point of view".

> С моей точки зрения, это хорошая идея.
>
> *S moyéy tóchki zréniya, éto khoróshaya idéya.*
>
> From my point of view, it is a good idea.

Feminine, after *точка*: genitive *точки зрения*. *С моей точки зрения* — from my point of view.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**точка** is usually traced to the verb *ткнуть* "to poke, to jab": a dot is the mark a jab leaves (EtymDB lists Lithuanian *taškas* "a dot" beside it). **зрение** "sight" is built on the old verb *зреть* "to look", so a point of view is literally the point you look from.
