## You'll want to know: আতশবাজি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C305-PRODIP305-02]; assesses=[] -->

**আতশবাজি** — *ātôshbāji* — "fireworks".

> রাতে আকাশে আতশবাজি জ্বলছিল।
>
> *rāte ākāshe ātôshbāji jvôlchhilô.*
>
> Fireworks were going off in the night sky.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আতশবাজি** (*ātôshbāji*) is usually traced to Persian *ātash-bāzī*: *ātash*, 'fire', and *bāzī*, 'play'.
