Japanese, chapter 380: Lunch on the Train.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
えきべん (ekiben) — a station lunch box.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for inside the train, then the Japanese for a fare.

You'll want to know: えきべん.
えきべん — ekiben — "a station lunch box".
でんしゃで えきべんを たべました。 — densha de ekiben o tabemashita — "I ate a station lunch box on the train."

The word, taken apart.
えきべん (ekiben) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are eki, "station" (Middle Chinese yek), and ben, the first sign of bentō, "a boxed lunch", a word whose older history is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ねすごす (nesugosu) — to oversleep; to sleep past one's stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a station lunch box, then the Japanese for a train conductor.

You'll want to know: ねすごす.
ねすごす — nesugosu — "to oversleep; to sleep past one's stop".
でんしゃで ねすごして、しゅうてんまで いきました。 — densha de nesugoshite, shūten made ikimashita — "I slept past my stop and went all the way to the end of the line."
Polite ねすごします, nesugoshimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): あにが でんしゃで ねすごしました。, ani ga densha de nesugoshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
ねすごす (nesugosu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from ne, "sleeping" (from neru) and sugosu, "to let pass"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a train conductor, then the Japanese for to oversleep.

You'll want to know: うとうと.
うとうと — utōto — "dozing off".
でんしゃで うとうとしました。 — densha de utōto shimashita — "I dozed off on the train."

The word, taken apart.
うとうと (utōto) is a native mimetic word that pictures someone nodding off into a light sleep: an imitative Japanese form, inherited rather than borrowed, whose older origin is not settled (JMdict).
