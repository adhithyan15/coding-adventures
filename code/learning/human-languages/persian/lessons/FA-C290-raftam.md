# رفتم (raftam) — the past, and the mark on an object you mean

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-MI-01, FA-GRAMMAR-NEGATIVE-NE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'm going", then "I'm not going". (*Miravam*, *nemiravam*.)

[PAUSE 1s] Now move it to yesterday. The past needs no new stem at all: the
infinitive already holds it.

## Grammar Lens: the past stem, and the same endings
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-INFINITIVE-TAN-DAN, FA-LEX-RAFTAN, FA-LEX-BUDAN, FA-LEX-C35-VERBS35-01, FA-LEX-C35-VERBS35-03] -->

Take an infinitive and drop its *-an*: *raftan* gives **رفت**, *raft*, the past
stem chapter six named. Add the endings you know, with one change: "he" or
"she" takes no ending at all.

> **رفتم** — *raftam*, I went · *rafti*, you went · *raft*, she went ·
> *raftim*, we went · *raftid*, you went · *raftand*, they went

Every verb gives up its past stem this way, *-tan* and *-dan* alike:
*budan*, *budam*, I was, and *bud*, it was; *khordan*, *khordam*, I ate;
*didan*, *didam*, I saw. "Not" here is *na-*: *naraftam*, I didn't go.

> **دیروز به خانه رفتم.** — *diruz be khâne raftam* — Yesterday I went home.

*Be* is "to". The verb comes last, and the time usually comes first.

## Grammar Lens: râ, for the one you mean
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-RA-OBJECT-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C243-CHIZ243-03, FA-LEX-KETAB] -->

> **فیلم را دیدم.** — *film râ didam* — I saw the film.

**را**, *râ*, follows an object that is a particular one, the film both of
you have in mind. Leave it out and the object is any film: *film didam*, "I
watched a film". **کتاب را خواندم**, *ketâb râ khândam*: I read the book.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Grammars of Persian usually trace the *-t* or *-d* at the end of a past stem to
an old participle ending, so *raft* first meant "gone" and *kard*, "done". Old
Persian *kṛta*, "done", is usually given as the older shape of *kard*. That
is why the stem stands alone for "she went": *raft* is "gone" with nobody
added.

"I ate"? (***Khordam.***) Which person takes no ending? ("He" or "she":
***raft***.) What does *râ* tell you? (That the object is **a particular
one**.)
