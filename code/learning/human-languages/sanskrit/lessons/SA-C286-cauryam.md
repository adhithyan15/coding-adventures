## You'll want to know: चौर्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C286-CAURYA286-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चौर्यम्** — *cauryam* — "a theft".

> ह्यः रात्रौ ग्रामे चौर्यं घटितम्।
>
> *hyaḥ rātrau grāme cauryaṃ ghaṭitam.*
>
> A theft took place in the village last night.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चौर्यम्** (*cauryam*) is built on *caura*, "a thief", with the ending *-ya* that makes a noun of the deed; MW gives *caurya* as the act of a thief, theft (MW).
