## You'll want to know: die Eifersucht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C466-NEID-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Eifersucht** — "jealousy".

> Seine Eifersucht macht seine Freundin traurig.
>
> *His jealousy makes his girlfriend sad.*

No plural in everyday use. The adjective is *eifersüchtig* — jealous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Eifersucht* is built on *Eifer* "zeal, eagerness" and *Sucht* "sickness, craving"; *Sucht* is inherited from Old High German *suht*, from Proto-Germanic *suhtiz* (EtymDB).
