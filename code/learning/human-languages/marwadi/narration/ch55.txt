Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 55: To meet, To learn, To forget, To bring, To send.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मिलणो (milṇo) — to meet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to sing, then the Marwadi for to dance.

You'll want to know: मिलणो.
मिलणो — milṇo — "to meet".

The word, taken apart.
मिलणो (milṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from milati, "meets, encounters". CDIAL lists Old Marwari milaï under it (CDIAL 10133).

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL gives no Marwari row for लावणो (lāvṇo), "to bring". It lists Hindi lānā, "to bring", under Sanskrit labhate, "takes", in forms it explains as crossed with dadāti, "gives" (CDIAL 10948). The match is through Hindi.
