## You'll want to know: ほんどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C407-SANPAI407-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ほんどう** — *hondō* — "the main hall of a temple".

> **ほんどうで くつを ぬぎました。** — *hondō de kutsu o nugimashita* — "We took off our shoes in the main hall."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほんどう** (*hondō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hon*, "main" (Middle Chinese *pwonX*) and *dō*, "hall" (*dang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
