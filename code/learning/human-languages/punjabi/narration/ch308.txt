Punjabi, chapter 308: Feeding the Animals.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਚਾਰਾ (chārā) — fodder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a teat, then the Punjabi for to graze.

You'll want to know: ਚਾਰਾ.
ਚਾਰਾ — chārā — "fodder".
ਮੈਂ ਮੱਝ ਲਈ ਹਰਾ ਚਾਰਾ ਲਿਆਵਾਂਗਾ। maiṁ majjh laī harā chārā liāvāṅgā. I will bring green fodder for the buffalo.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਚਾਰਾ (chārā) is inherited from Sanskrit cāra, "grazing, fodder"; CDIAL lists Punjabi cārā, "fodder", under it (CDIAL 4755).

ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਮੱਝਾਂ ਘਰ ਹੱਕਾਂਗਾ। shām nū̃ maiṁ majjhāṁ ghar hakkāṅgā. In the evening I will drive the buffaloes home.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੱਝਾਂ ਹੱਕੀਆਂ, maiṁ majjhāṁ hakkīāṁ.

The word, taken apart.
ਹੱਕਣਾ (hakkṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit hakkayati, "calls to"; CDIAL lists Punjabi hakkṇā, "to drive away", under it (CDIAL 13939).
