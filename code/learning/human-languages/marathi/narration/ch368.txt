Marathi, chapter 368: Duties and Rules.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पालन करणे (pālan karṇe) — to comply with; to observe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tendency, then the Marathi for social.

You'll want to know: पालन करणे.
पालन करणे — pālan karṇe — "to comply with; to observe".
सगळ्यांनी वाहतुकीच्या नियमांचं पालन केलं पाहिजे. sagaḷyānnī vāhtukīcyā niymāñcaṁ pālan kelaṁ pāhije. Everyone must observe the traffic rules.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पालन केलं, tyāne pālan kelaṁ.
The rule takes -चं: नियमांचं पालन करणे (pālan karṇe); also to bring up: मुलांचं पालन.

The word, taken apart.
पालन करणे (pālan karṇe) joins the tatsama पालन, Sanskrit pālana, "protecting, keeping" (CDIAL 8128), to करणे, "to do".

You'll want to know: उल्लंघन करणे.
उल्लंघन करणे — ullaṅghan karṇe — "to violate; to break (a rule)".
त्याने नियमांचं उल्लंघन केलं, म्हणून त्याला दंड झाला. tyāne niymāñcaṁ ullaṅghan kelaṁ, mhaṇūn tyālā daṇḍ jhālā. He broke the rules, so he was fined.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बजावणे (bajāvṇe) — to discharge (a duty).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to comply with, then the Marathi for to violate.

You'll want to know: बजावणे.
बजावणे — bajāvṇe — "to discharge (a duty)".
पोलिसाने आपलं कर्तव्य प्रामाणिकपणे बजावलं. polisāne āplaṁ kartavya prāmāṇikpaṇe bajāvlaṁ. The policeman did his duty honestly.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कर्तव्य बजावलं, tyāne kartavya bajāvlaṁ.
कर्तव्य बजावणे (bajāvṇe), to do one's duty; also to warn sternly: त्याला बजावलं.

The word, taken apart.
बजावणे (bajāvṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सोपवणे (sopavṇe) — to entrust; to hand over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to violate, then the Marathi for to discharge.

You'll want to know: सोपवणे.
सोपवणे — sopavṇe — "to entrust; to hand over".
आईने घराची जबाबदारी मोठ्या मुलीवर सोपवली. āīne gharācī jabābdārī moṭhyā mulīvar sopavlī. Mother entrusted the running of the house to her elder daughter.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सोपवलं, tyāne sopavlaṁ.
The person entrusted takes -वर or -कडे.

The word, taken apart.
सोपवणे (sopavṇe) is built on an older verb, Marathi sõpṇẽ, "hands over", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit samarpayati, "delivers over" (CDIAL 13192).

Lesson 5 of 5.
हाताळणे (hātāḷṇe) — to handle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to discharge, then the Marathi for to entrust.

You'll want to know: हाताळणे.
हाताळणे — hātāḷṇe — "to handle".
मीराने तो प्रश्न शांतपणे हाताळला. mīrāne to praśna śāntpaṇe hātāḷlā. Meera handled the issue calmly.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने हाताळलं, tyāne hātāḷlaṁ.
Built on हात, hand.

The word, taken apart.
हाताळणे (hātāḷṇe) is a Marathi verb built on हात, "hand", which is inherited from Sanskrit hasta, "hand" (CDIAL 14024).
