## You'll want to know: બગડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C209-BREAKDOWN209-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બગડવું** — *bagaḍvũ* — "to break down, to go bad".

> **રસ્તામાં ગાડી બગડી.** — *rastāmā̃ gāḍī bagaḍī* — "The car broke down on the way."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બગડવું** (*bagaḍvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *vighaṭatē*, "flies apart, is marred, is broken", but CDIAL marks the Gujarati verb, glossed "to be spoilt", as borrowed from Hindi (CDIAL 11673).
