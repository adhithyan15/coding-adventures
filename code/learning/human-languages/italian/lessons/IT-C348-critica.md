## You'll want to know: la critica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C348-VANTAGGIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la critica** — "the criticism; the critics".

> Accetto la critica, ma il lavoro è finito.
>
> *I accept the criticism, but the work is finished.*

*Fare una critica* — to make a criticism. *La critica* can also mean the critics as a group.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*critica* is built on *critico*, a learned borrowing from Latin *criticus* "able to judge" (Lewis & Short), from Greek *kritikós*, from *krínein* "to separate, to decide".
