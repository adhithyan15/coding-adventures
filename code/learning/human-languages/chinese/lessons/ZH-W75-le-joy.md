## Script — a fall, a turn, and 小
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-LE-JOY-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 乐

**One piece, five strokes.**

**A left-falling stroke** across the top, from right to left, nearly flat.

**A vertical that turns right**, under the left end of the first stroke.

**Then the three strokes of 小**: the vertical down the middle that hooks to the left, a dot falling left, a dot falling right.

2 + 3 = five.
