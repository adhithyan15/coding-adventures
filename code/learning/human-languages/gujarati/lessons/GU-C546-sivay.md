## You'll want to know: સિવાય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C546-VAGAR546-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સિવાય** — *sivāy* — "except".

> જો રવિવાર સિવાય કોઈ દિવસ તમને અનુકૂળ હશે તો મને કહો.
>
> *jo ravivār sivāy koī divas tamne anukūḷ haśe to mane kaho.*
>
> If any day apart from Sunday suits you, tell me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સિવાય** (*sivāy*) is usually traced to Arabic *siwā*, "other than, except", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
