## You'll want to know: ہچکی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C469-THROAT469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہچکی** — *hichkī* — "a hiccup".

> پانی پینے سے ہچکی رک گئی۔
>
> *pānī pīne se hichkī ruk gaī.*
>
> Drinking some water stopped the hiccups.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi *hīk*, "hiccup", under Sanskrit *hikkā*, "hiccup" (CDIAL 14075); **ہچکی** is probably a longer form of the same imitative word.
