## You'll want to know: yígòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C132-BIG132-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yígòng* — "altogether; in all".

> *wǒmen yígòng yǒu wǔ ge rén* — There are five of us in all.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The tone of 一 changes: yī is said yí before the fourth tone of gòng.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yígòng* is built from 一 'one' and *gòng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit* and *gjowngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'together' and 'all'.
