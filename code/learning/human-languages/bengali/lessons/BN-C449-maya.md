## You'll want to know: মায়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C449-SNEHO449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**মায়া** — *māyā* — "tender attachment; fond pity".

> পুরনো বাড়িটা ছাড়তে আমার মায়া লাগল।
>
> *purôno bāṛiṭā chhāṛte āmār māyā lāglô.*
>
> Leaving the old house, I felt a pang of attachment.

মায়া is the soft pull you feel toward a person, a pet or a place; the older sense, illusion, belongs to philosophy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মায়া** (*māyā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *māyā*, "supernatural power, illusion" (CDIAL 10060), where CDIAL, like Monier-Williams, also records a sense of compassion, from the lexicographers; the everyday Bengali sense of tender attachment probably grew from that one.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
