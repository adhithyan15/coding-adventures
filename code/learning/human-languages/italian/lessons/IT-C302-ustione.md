## You'll want to know: l'ustione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C302-INCENDIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ustione** — "the burn (on the skin)".

> Ha un'ustione sul braccio.
>
> *He has a burn on his arm.*

A doctor's word for a burn on the skin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ustione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ustiō* "a burning", from *ūrere* "to burn" (Lewis & Short).
