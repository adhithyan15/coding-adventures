## Script — the food piece, then four strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-FAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 饭

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The food piece, three strokes.** A left-falling stroke. A short horizontal that hooks down at its end. A vertical that flicks up to the right at the foot.

**The right piece, four strokes.** A short left-falling stroke along the top. A long left-falling stroke down the left. A horizontal that turns and falls to the left. Then a right-falling stroke across it.

3 + 4 = seven.
