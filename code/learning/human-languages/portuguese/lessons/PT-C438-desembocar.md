## You'll want to know: desembocar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C438-SUBIDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**desembocar** — "to come out (into); to flow (into)".

> Esta rua desemboca na praça da igreja.
>
> *This street comes out into the church square.*

A river *desemboca* in the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desembocar* is built from *des-*, *em* and *boca* 'mouth', inherited from Latin *bucca* 'cheek, mouth' (Lewis & Short): to come out of a mouth.
