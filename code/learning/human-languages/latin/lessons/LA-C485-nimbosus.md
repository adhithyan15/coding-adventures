## You'll want to know: nimbōsus, nimbōsa, nimbōsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C485-IMBER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**nimbōsus, nimbōsa, nimbōsum** — "stormy, full of rain clouds".

> Caelum nimbōsum est; nautae in portū manent.
>
> *The sky is stormy; the sailors stay in the harbour.*

An adjective of three endings, from *nimbus* 'rain cloud' with *-ōsus* 'full of'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nimbōsus* is built on *nimbus* 'rain cloud, storm' with the suffix *-ōsus* 'full of' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
