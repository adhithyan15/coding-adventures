## You'll want to know: परोपकारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C468-SAUMYA468-05]; assesses=[] -->

**परोपकारी** — *paropkārī* — "helpful to others".

> आमचे शेजारी परोपकारी आहेत.
>
> *āmce śejārī paropkārī āhet.*
>
> Our neighbours are always ready to help others.

पर, other, + उपकार, a kindness: परोपकार, doing good to others.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परोपकारी** (*paropkārī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *paropakārin*, "assisting others, beneficent" (Monier-Williams).
