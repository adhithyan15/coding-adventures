## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C305-NAME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**encantado** — what you say on being introduced.

Latin **incantare** is *in-* plus *cantare*, "to sing" — to **sing a spell onto**
something. English took it as *enchanted*, and kept the raw noun as
*incantation*.

So the polite thing a Spanish speaker says on meeting you is, literally, "I have
been put under a spell." English does the identical thing and nobody notices:
*charmed*, and *delighted* — *de-* plus *lacere*, "to lure."

A man says *encantado*; a woman says *encantada*. You already have *mucho
gusto*, which does the same job more plainly — either is right.
