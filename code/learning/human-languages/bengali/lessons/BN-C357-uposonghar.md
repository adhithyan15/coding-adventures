## You'll want to know: উপসংহার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C357-PROBONDHO357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**উপসংহার** — *upôsônghār* — "a conclusion of a text; a closing section".

> প্রবন্ধের উপসংহারটা ছোট।
>
> *prôbôndher upôsônghārṭā chhoṭo.*
>
> The essay's conclusion is short.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপসংহার** (*upôsônghār*) is Sanskrit *upasaṃhāra*, summing up (Monier-Williams): the closing part of an essay or a speech.
