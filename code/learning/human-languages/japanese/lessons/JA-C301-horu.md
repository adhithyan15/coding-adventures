## You'll want to know: ほる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C301-TAKARA301-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ほる** — *horu* — "to dig".

> **いぬが にわを ほっています。** — *inu ga niwa o hotte imasu* — "The dog is digging in the garden."

Polite **ほります**, *horimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おじいさんが にわを ほりました。**, *ojiisan ga niwa o horimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほる** (*horu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
