## You'll want to know: la bahía
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C688-MAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la bahía** — "the bay".

> El hotel está en una bahía tranquila.
>
> *The hotel is on a quiet bay.*

A small bay is *una cala*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bahía* is usually traced to French *baie* "bay"; where the French word comes from is not settled.
