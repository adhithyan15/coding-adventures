## You'll want to know: la dépense
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C390-BUDGET-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la dépense** — "the expense, the spending".

> Le loyer est notre plus grosse dépense.
>
> *Rent is our biggest expense.*

From *dépenser*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dépense* comes through Middle French *despense* (EtymDB) from Late Latin *dispēnsa*, from *dispendere* "to weigh out, to pay out", built on *pendere* "to weigh" (Lewis & Short).
