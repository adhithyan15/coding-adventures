## You'll want to know: यकृत्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C470-PHUPPHUSA470-02]; assesses=[] -->

**यकृत्** — *yakṛt* — "the liver".

> यकृत् उदरे दक्षिणतः अस्ति।
>
> *yakṛt udare dakṣiṇataḥ asti.*
>
> The liver is on the right side of the belly.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यकृत्** (*yakṛt*) MW compares Greek *hēpar* and Latin *jecur*, but gives no derivation within Sanskrit, so its origin there is uncertain (MW).
