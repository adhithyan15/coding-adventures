## You'll want to know: یخ زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C476-KULAK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**یخ زدن** (*yakh zadan*) — "to freeze".

> آب حوض شب یخ زد.
>
> *Âb-e howz shab yakh zad.*
>
> The water in the pool froze overnight.

*yakh* 'ice' + *zadan*. Of people: *yakh zadam* — I'm frozen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yakh zadan* is built from native *yakh* 'ice', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Iranian *aixa-*, and the native verb *zadan* 'to strike'.
