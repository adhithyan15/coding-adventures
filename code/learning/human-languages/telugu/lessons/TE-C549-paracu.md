## You'll want to know: పరచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C549-GRHAM549-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పరచు** — *paracu* — "to spread out; to lay".

> అతిథుల కోసం అమ్మ నేల మీద చాప పరిచింది.
>
> *atithula kōsaṁ amma nēla mīda cāpa paricindi.*
>
> Mum spread a mat on the floor for the guests.

Past (I …): పరిచాను, *paricānu*.

For mats, sheets and beds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పరచు** (*paracu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3949 lists Telugu *paravu*, "to spread", and *parapu*, "spreading; bedding".
