## You'll want to know: сниматься
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C520-SCREENACTING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сниматься** (*snimátsya*) — "to act in a film; to have one's photo taken".

> Она снимается в кино с детства.
>
> *Oná snimáyetsya v kinó s détstva.*
>
> She has been acting in films since childhood.

Imperfective; perfective сняться (снимусь). *Сниматься в фильме* — to be in a film.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сниматься* is the reflexive of *снимать* 'to take off; to film', built on *с-* and the root *-ним-/-ня-* 'to take'; EtymDB has no entry for the reflexive.
