## You'll want to know: असर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C343-KARAN343-02]; assesses=[] -->

**असर** — *asar* — "an effect".

> दवा का असर दो घंटे बाद हुआ।
>
> *davā kā asar do ghaṇṭe bād huā.*
>
> The medicine took effect two hours later.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**असर** (*asar*) is usually traced to Arabic *athar*, "a trace, a mark", which reached Hindi through Persian. Hindi uses it for the effect one thing has on another.
