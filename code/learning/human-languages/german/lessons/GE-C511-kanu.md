## You'll want to know: das Kanu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C511-WASSERSPORT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Kanu** — "canoe".

> Mit dem Kanu sind wir den Fluss hinuntergefahren.
>
> *We went down the river by canoe.*

Plural: *die Kanus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kanu* came into German through English *canoe* and Spanish *canoa*, usually traced to a Caribbean word for a dugout boat; EtymDB has no entry for the German word.
