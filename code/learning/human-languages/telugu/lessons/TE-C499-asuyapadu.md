# అసూయపడు (asūyapaḍu) — to be jealous; to envy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-04, TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to hold back politely, then the Telugu for to hang one's head.

## You'll want to know: అసూయపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C499-ASUYA499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అసూయపడు** — *asūyapaḍu* — "to be jealous; to envy".

> అతని కొత్త కారు చూసి స్నేహితులు అసూయపడ్డారు.
>
> *atani kotta kāru cūsi snēhitulu asūyapaḍḍāru.*
>
> Seeing his new car, his friends were jealous.

Past (I …): అసూయపడ్డాను, *asūyapaḍḍānu*.

Who you envy takes మీద: అతని మీద అసూయపడు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అసూయపడు** (*asūyapaḍu*) is built from అసూయ, "envy", from Sanskrit *asūyā*, resentment at another person's good fortune (Monier-Williams) and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
