## You'll want to know: crédible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C340-FIABLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**crédible** — "believable, credible".

> Son histoire n'est pas très crédible.
>
> *His story is not very believable.*

One form for both genders. Close to *croyable*, but more formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crédible* is a learned borrowing from Latin *crēdibilis* "worthy of belief" (Lewis & Short), from *crēdere* "to believe".
