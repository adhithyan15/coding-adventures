# (una lesión en el polideportivo) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-01, ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *el polideportivo* and *la natación*. Both are on the notice you
are about to read, and one of them is cancelled.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-01, ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-02, ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-03] -->

Read the notice once straight through, then answer.

> **AVISO — POLIDEPORTIVO MUNICIPAL**
> Los cursos de **natación** de la tarde quedan suspendidos del 3 al 10 de
> octubre por obras en los vestuarios. Las clases de la mañana siguen igual.
> Para cualquier duda, escriba a avisos@polideportivo.invalid.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: which classes are suspended, and for how long]
- [YOU SAY: which classes are not affected]
- [YOU SAY: where to write if you have a question]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-04, ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-05] -->

Now a message from a friend who went anyway, on a morning.

> Fui por la mañana y **me torcí** el tobillo saliendo de la piscina. Nada
> grave: el médico dice que tengo que **descansar** tres días. Luego **nos
> reímos**, porque llevaba veinte años sin caerme en ningún sitio.

Notice what each verb is doing. **Me torcí** puts the injury on the speaker
without blaming anyone. **Descansar** is an instruction with a duration attached.
**Nos reímos** closes the episode, and that closing move is what a Spanish
speaker expects at the end of a small misfortune: name it, size it, laugh it off.

A report that stops after *me torcí el tobillo* sounds like a complaint. The
same report with *nos reímos* on the end sounds like a story. Same facts, and
the last clause decides which one the listener hears.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-03, ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-04, ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-05, ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-01, ES-LEX-C438-CUERPO-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four sentences: where
you went, what you were doing there, what you twisted, and what you have to do
now. End it the way the message above ends it.

Then check yourself: did you say *me torcí **el** tobillo* and not *mi tobillo*?
