Marathi, chapter 354: Citizens and the Public.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नागरिक (nāgarik) — a citizen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for actual, then the Marathi for to react.

You'll want to know: नागरिक.
नागरिक — nāgarik — "a citizen".
प्रत्येक नागरिकाला मत देण्याचा हक्क आहे. pratyek nāgarikālā mat deṇyācā hakka āhe. Every citizen has the right to vote.
Masculine.
नागरिक (nāgarik), both a man and a woman; नागरिकत्व, citizenship.

The word, taken apart.
नागरिक (nāgarik) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nāgarika, "born or living in a town, civic" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
जनता (jantā) — the general public; the people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to react, then the Marathi for a citizen.

You'll want to know: जनता.
जनता — jantā — "the general public; the people".
नव्या पुलाचा जनतेला खूप फायदा झाला. navyā pulācā jantelā khūp phāydā jhālā. The public benefited greatly from the new bridge.
Feminine.
Feminine and singular, though it means many people: जनता (jantā) जमली.

The word, taken apart.
जनता (jantā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit janatā, "a number of people, the people" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सर्वसामान्य (sarvasāmānya) — common; ordinary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a citizen, then the Marathi for the general public.

You'll want to know: सर्वसामान्य.
सर्वसामान्य — sarvasāmānya — "common; ordinary".
सर्वसामान्य माणसाला हा कायदा समजत नाही. sarvasāmānya māṇsālā hā kāydā samjat nāhī. The ordinary person does not understand this law.
सर्व + सामान्य, all + common. Invariable.

The word, taken apart.
सर्वसामान्य (sarvasāmānya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sarvasāmānya, "common to all" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
लोकसंख्या (loksaṅkhyā) — population.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the general public, then the Marathi for common.

You'll want to know: लोकसंख्या.
लोकसंख्या — loksaṅkhyā — "population".
पुण्याची लोकसंख्या दरवर्षी वाढत आहे. puṇyācī loksaṅkhyā darvarṣī vāḍhat āhe. Pune's population is growing every year.
Feminine.
लोक + संख्या, people + number.

The word, taken apart.
लोकसंख्या (loksaṅkhyā) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit loka, "world, people", and saṁkhyā, "number" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ग्रामीण (grāmīṇ) — rural.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for common, then the Marathi for population.

You'll want to know: ग्रामीण.
ग्रामीण — grāmīṇ — "rural".
ग्रामीण भागात अजून चांगले रस्ते नाहीत. grāmīṇ bhāgāt ajūn cāṅgle raste nāhīt. There are still no good roads in rural areas.
From ग्राम, a village (formal); the opposite is शहरी, urban.

The word, taken apart.
ग्रामीण (grāmīṇ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit grāmīṇa, "rural, of a village" (Monier-Williams).
