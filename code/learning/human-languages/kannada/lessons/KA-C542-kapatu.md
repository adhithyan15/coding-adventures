# ಕಪಾಟು (kapāṭu) — a cabinet; a shelved cupboard

## You'll want to know: ಕಪಾಟು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C542-MANE542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಪಾಟು** — *kapāṭu* — "a cabinet; a shelved cupboard".

> ಪುಸ್ತಕಗಳು ಗಾಜಿನ ಕಪಾಟಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಇವೆ.
>
> *pustakagaḷu gājina kapāṭinalli ive.*
>
> The books are in the glass cabinet.

ಬೀರು is a big wardrobe; a ಕಪಾಟು is often set into the wall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಪಾಟು** (*kapāṭu*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *kapāṭa*, "a door, the panel of a door" (Monier-Williams): a cupboard is what has the doors. The path is not settled.
