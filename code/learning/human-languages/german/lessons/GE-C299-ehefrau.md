## You'll want to know: die Ehefrau
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C299-EHE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ehefrau** — "the wife".

> Seine Ehefrau spricht drei Sprachen.
>
> *His wife speaks three languages.*

Plural: *die Ehefrauen*. In speech, *meine Frau* is more common.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ehefrau* joins *Ehe* "marriage" and *Frau* "woman, wife", from Middle High German *vrouwe* (EtymDB).
