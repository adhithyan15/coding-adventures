## You'll want to know: ਬੇਨਤੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C337-ASK337-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੇਨਤੀ** — *bentī* — "a request".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਇੱਕ ਬੇਨਤੀ ਮੰਨੋਗੇ?
>
> *kī tusī̃ merī ikk bentī mannoge?*
>
> Will you grant me one request?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੇਨਤੀ** (*bentī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vijñapti*, "information, a request to a superior"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *beṇtī*, "request", under it (CDIAL 11706).
