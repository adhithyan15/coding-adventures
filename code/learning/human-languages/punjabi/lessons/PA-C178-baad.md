# ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ — place one step after a time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-TIME-VAJE-01, PA-POSTPOSITION-TON-SOURCE-01, PA-POSTPOSITION-TAK-LIMIT-01, PA-POSTPOSITION-NAAL-COMITATIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the schedule span **ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੱਕ**. Then recall
the companion pair **ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ**. Nothing in those pieces changes.

## Grammar Lens: ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ marks the later step
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-TIME-TON-BAAD-SEQUENCE-01]; assesses=[PA-TIME-VAJE-01, PA-POSTPOSITION-TON-SOURCE-01] -->

> **ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ** — *das vaje tõ bāad* — after ten o'clock

Keep **ਤੋਂ** before **ਬਾਅਦ**. The time comes first, then the two-word sequence.
This lesson owns only this bounded time use; it does not claim every temporal
or clausal use of **ਬਾਅਦ**.
