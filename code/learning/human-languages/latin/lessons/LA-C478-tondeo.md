## You'll want to know: tondeō, tondēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C478-OPILIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**tondeō, tondēre** — "to shear, to clip; to shave".

> Vēre agricolae ovēs tondent.
>
> *In spring the farmers shear the sheep.*

*tondeō, tondēre, totondī, tōnsum*, with a reduplicated perfect. *Tōnsor* 'barber' comes from it, and English *tonsure*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tondeō* is inherited and is usually traced to PIE *temh₂-* 'to cut' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short compare Greek *temnō* 'to cut'). English *tonsure* comes from its participle *tōnsus*.
