## You'll want to know: भाषा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भाषा** (*bhāshā*) — "a language".

Sanskrit भाषा (*bhāṣā*), "speech, what is spoken", from भाष् (*bhāṣ-*), "to
speak". The word names the act before it names the thing: a भाषा is speaking,
turned into a noun.

You have been saying **मैं हिंदी बोलता हूँ** for a long time. भाषा is the word
that lets you talk about हिंदी rather than only in it — and it is a printed line
on both of the writing papers you will one day sit, right under देश and शहर.
