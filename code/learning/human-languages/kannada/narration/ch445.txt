Kannada, chapter 445: Parking and Turning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಮಾಡು (pārk māḍu) — to park a vehicle; to park.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to ban, then the Kannada for one-way.

You'll want to know: ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಮಾಡು.
ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಮಾಡು — pārk māḍu — "to park a vehicle; to park".
ನಾನು ಕಾರನ್ನು ಅಂಗಡಿಯ ಎದುರು ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆ. nānu kārannu aṅgaḍiya eduru pārk māḍide. I parked the car opposite the shop.
Past (I …): ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, pārk māḍide.
The vehicle takes -ಅನ್ನು; ಪಾರ್ಕಿಂಗ್ is a parking place.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಮಾಡು (pārk māḍu) joins a loan from English park and the native verb ಮಾಡು, to do.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ತಿರುಗು (tirugu) — to turn round; to go round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for one-way, then the Kannada for to park a vehicle.

You'll want to know: ತಿರುಗು.
ತಿರುಗು — tirugu — "to turn round; to go round".
ಮುಂದಿನ ವೃತ್ತದಲ್ಲಿ ಬಸ್ ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗುತ್ತದೆ. mundina vṛttadalli bas eḍakke tiruguttade. At the next circle the bus turns left.
Past (I …): ತಿರುಗಿದೆ, tirugide.
ಹಿಂತಿರುಗು, to come back, is ಹಿಂ-, back, plus this verb.

The word, taken apart.
ತಿರುಗು (tirugu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3246 lists Kannada tirigu, tirugu, "to go round, to turn, to roll, to go back".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಅಲುಗಾಡು.
ಅಲುಗಾಡು — alugāḍu — "to wobble; to shake".
ಹಳೆಯ ಬಸ್ ಕಲ್ಲಿನ ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅಲುಗಾಡುತ್ತಿತ್ತು. haḷeya bas kallina rasteyalli alugāḍuttittu. The old bus was rattling along the stony road.
Past (it …): ಅಲುಗಾಡಿತು, alugāḍitu.
It shakes by itself; ಅಲ್ಲಾಡಿಸು is to shake something.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಲುಗಾಡು (alugāḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 240 lists Kannada alaku, alugu, "to move about, to shake, to tremble", here with ಆಡು.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಚಲಿಸು (calisu) — to move; to be in motion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to turn round, then the Kannada for to wobble.

You'll want to know: ಚಲಿಸು.
ಚಲಿಸು — calisu — "to move; to be in motion".
ರೈಲು ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಚಲಿಸಲು ಶುರು ಮಾಡಿತು. railu nidhānavāgi calisalu śuru māḍitu. The train slowly began to move.
Past (it …): ಚಲಿಸಿತು, calisitu.
ಚಲಿಸುವ ಮೆಟ್ಟಿಲು, moving stairs, is an escalator.

The word, taken apart.
ಚಲಿಸು (calisu) is built from the Sanskrit root cal, "to move, to stir" (CDIAL 4715, calati), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಮೀಪಿಸು (samīpisu) — to approach; to draw near.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to wobble, then the Kannada for to move.

You'll want to know: ಸಮೀಪಿಸು.
ಸಮೀಪಿಸು — samīpisu — "to approach; to draw near".
ವಿಮಾನ ನಗರವನ್ನು ಸಮೀಪಿಸಿತು. vimāna nagaravannu samīpisitu. The plane drew near the city.
Past (I …): ಸಮೀಪಿಸಿದೆ, samīpiside.
ಸಮೀಪ means near: ಸಮೀಪದಲ್ಲಿ, nearby.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮೀಪಿಸು (samīpisu) is built from Sanskrit samīpa, "near" (CDIAL 13226), with the verb ending -isu: to come near.
