## You'll want to know: अंत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C307-ANT307-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अंत** — *ant* — "an ending; an end".

> या कथेचा अंत फार अनपेक्षित होता.
>
> *yā kathecā ant phār anpekṣit hotā.*
>
> The ending of this story was very unexpected.

Masculine.

शेवट is the everyday word; अंत is also the end of a life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंत** (*ant*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *anta*, 'an end, a limit' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
