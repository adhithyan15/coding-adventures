## You'll want to know: પ્લમ્બર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C515-POPDO515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્લમ્બર** — *plambar* — "a plumber".

> પ્લમ્બરે રસોડાની જૂની પાઈપ બદલી.
>
> *plambare rasoḍānī jūnī pāīp badlī.*
>
> The plumber changed the old kitchen pipe.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્લમ્બર** (*plambar*) is borrowed from English *plumber*.
