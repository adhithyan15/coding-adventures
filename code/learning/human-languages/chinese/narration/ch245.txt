Mandarin Chinese, chapter 245: Take Care.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
tǐwēn — body temperature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for in case, then the Mandarin for to carry on.

You'll want to know: tǐwēn.
tǐwēn — "body temperature".
nǐ de tǐwēn yǒudiǎnr gāo — Your temperature's a bit high.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
tǐwēn is built from 体 'body' and wēn 'warm', both inherited from Middle Chinese, thejX and 'won (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for body temperature, then the Mandarin for to be catching.

You'll want to know: kǒuzhào.
kǒuzhào — "face mask".
nǐ yǒu kǒuzhào ma — Have you got a face mask?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kǒuzhào is built from 口 'mouth' and zhào 'a cover', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khuwX and traewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a bamboo basket for catching fish, so how it came to mean a cover is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
xīnqíng — mood; frame of mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to be catching, then the Mandarin for a face mask.

You'll want to know: xīnqíng.
xīnqíng — "mood; frame of mind".
shēngbìng le xīnqíng bù hǎo — Being ill puts me in a bad mood.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xīnqíng is built from 心 'heart' and qíng 'feeling', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sim and dzjeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the second, after an older book, as what a person desires.
