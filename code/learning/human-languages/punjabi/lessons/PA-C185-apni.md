## Grammar Lens: the same *-ii* agreement

> **ਆਪਣੀ** — *aapnii* — one's own, with a feminine noun

The reflexive meaning stays “one's own.” The ending follows the feminine noun.
Read **ਆਪਣੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ**.
