## You'll want to know: सत्कर्म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C351-VIVEKA351-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्कर्म** — *satkarma* — "a virtuous act".

> सेवा सत्कर्म अस्ति।
>
> *sevā satkarma asti.*
>
> Service is a virtuous act.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्कर्म** (*satkarma*) is built from *sat*, "good", and *karman*, "an act", from the root √kṛ; MW gives *sat-karman* "a good work, a virtuous act" (MW).
