## You'll want to know: l'airbag
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C298-CHOC-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'airbag** — "the airbag".

> L'airbag s'est ouvert pendant le choc.
>
> *The airbag opened in the crash.*

Official French says *le coussin gonflable*, the usual word in Québec.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*airbag* was borrowed from English *airbag* (EtymDB), a bag filled with air.
