## You'll want to know: píngjìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C466-CALM466-05]; assesses=[] -->

*píngjìng* — "calm; peaceful inside".

> *tīng le yīnyuè yǐhòu wǒ de xīnqíng hěn píngjìng* — After listening to some music, I feel at peace.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*píngjìng* is built from *píng* 'level' and 静 'still', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjaeng* and *dzjengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'straight' and 'calm' and the second 'at peace'.
