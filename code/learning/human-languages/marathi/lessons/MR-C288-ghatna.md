## You'll want to know: घटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C288-GHATANA288-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घटना** — *ghaṭnā* — "an event; an incident".

> ही घटना किती मोठी होती, हे तुम्ही कोणाला विचारता यावर अवलंबून आहे.
>
> *hī ghaṭnā kitī moṭhī hotī, he tumhī koṇālā vicārtā yāvar avlambūn āhe.*
>
> How big this incident was depends on whom you ask.

Feminine.

घटना घडणे, an event takes place; the oblique is घटने-: घटनेचा, of the event.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घटना** (*ghaṭnā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ghaṭanā*, 'exertion; a taking effect, a coming about' (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for anything that happens.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
