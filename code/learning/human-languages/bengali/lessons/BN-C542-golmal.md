# গোলমাল (golmāl) — a muddle; trouble

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C542-BHESTE542-03, BN-LEX-C542-BHESTE542-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for in chaos, then the Bengali for to come loose.

## You'll want to know: গোলমাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C542-BHESTE542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**গোলমাল** — *golmāl* — "a muddle; trouble".

> টিকিটে নামের গোলমাল ছিল।
>
> *Ṭikiṭe nāmer golmāl chhilô.*
>
> There was a mix-up with the name on the ticket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গোলমাল** (*golmāl*) is an expressive word for noise, trouble or a mix-up; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
