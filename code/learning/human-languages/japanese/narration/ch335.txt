Japanese, chapter 335: Groups and Members.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
だんたい (dantai) — a group; an organization.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for looking after, then the Japanese for to handle.

You'll want to know: だんたい.
だんたい — dantai — "a group; an organization".
だんたいで いったら、やすく なります。 — dantai de ittara, yasuku narimasu — "If you go as a group, it is cheaper."

The word, taken apart.
だんたい (dantai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are dan, "group" (Middle Chinese dwan) and tai, "body" (thejX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
