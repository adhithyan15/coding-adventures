## You'll want to know: esclarecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C343-ESCLARECER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**esclarecer** — "to clarify, to clear up".

> Quero esclarecer uma coisa antes de começar.
>
> *I want to clarify one thing before I start.*

*Esclarecer uma dúvida* — to clear up a doubt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esclarecer* is built in Portuguese from *claro* "clear", inherited from Latin *clārus* "bright, clear" (Lewis & Short), with *es-* and the verb ending *-ecer*.
