## You'll want to know: منتشر کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C312-MONTASHER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**منتشر کردن** (*montasher kardan*) — "to publish, to release".

> روزنامه عکس های جشن را منتشر کرد.
>
> *Ruznâme 'aks-hâ-ye jashn râ montasher kard.*
>
> The newspaper published the photos of the celebration.

Present *montasher mikonad*; the passive is *montasher shod*. *montasher* means "spread, published".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*montasher* is usually traced to Arabic *muntashir*, "spread out"; EtymDB lists no entry. *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
