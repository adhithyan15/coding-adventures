## You'll want to know: ઓચિંતું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C531-JHANJHAT531-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓચિંતું** — *ocintũ* — "sudden".

> જો ઓચિંતો વરસાદ આવશે તો મેચ બંધ થઈ જશે.
>
> *jo ocinto varsād āvśe to mec bandh thaī jaśe.*
>
> If a sudden shower comes, the match will be stopped.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓચિંતું** (*ocintũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*apacitta*, "without thought", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "suddenly" (CDIAL 420): what comes without being thought of. It agrees with its noun: **ઓચિંતો વરસાદ**.
