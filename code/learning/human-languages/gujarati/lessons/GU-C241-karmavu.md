# કરમાવું (karmāvũ) — to wilt, to wither

## You'll want to know: કરમાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C241-BLOOM241-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કરમાવું** — *karmāvũ* — "to wilt, to wither".

> **તડકામાં ફૂલ કરમાયું.** — *taḍkāmā̃ phūl karmāyũ* — "The flower wilted in the sun."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કરમાવું** (*karmāvũ*) comes from *\*kurumm-*, "wither", a reconstructed form that CDIAL lists beside *\*komh*; it glosses the Gujarati verb "wither" (CDIAL 3524).
