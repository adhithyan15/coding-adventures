## You'll want to know: चेतावनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C302-AILAN302-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चेतावनी** — *cetāvnī* — "a warning".

> चेतावनी के बाद लोगों को गाँव छोड़ना पड़ा।
>
> *cetāvnī ke bād logoṁ ko gā̃v choṛnā paṛā.*
>
> After the warning people had to leave the village.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चेतावनी** (*cetāvnī*) is built on *cetānā*, to make someone aware, probably from the Sanskrit family of *cetana*, "awareness" (Monier-Williams). It is feminine: **चेतावनी दी**, gave a warning.
