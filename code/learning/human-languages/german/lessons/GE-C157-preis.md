## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**Preis** ← Middle High German *prīs*, from Old French *pris* ← Latin *pretium*, "worth, price, reward" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. English *price*, *prize* and *praise* share it.
