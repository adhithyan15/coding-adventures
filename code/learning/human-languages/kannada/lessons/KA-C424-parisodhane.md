## You'll want to know: ಪರಿಶೋಧನೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C424-AVALOKISU424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಶೋಧನೆ** — *pariśōdhane* — "an audit; a scrutiny".

> ಕಂಪನಿಯ ಲೆಕ್ಕಗಳ ಪರಿಶೋಧನೆ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *kampaniya lekkagaḷa pariśōdhane naḍeyitu.*
>
> The company's accounts were audited.

ಪರಿಶೋಧಕ, an auditor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಶೋಧನೆ** (*pariśōdhane*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pariśodhana*, "cleaning, purification; paying off" (Monier-Williams).
