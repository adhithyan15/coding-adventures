## You'll want to know: മൊഴി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C370-MOZHI370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**മൊഴി** — *moḻi* — "testimony; a statement".

> പോലീസ് സാക്ഷിയുടെ മൊഴി എടുത്തു.
>
> *pōlīsŭ sākṣiyuṭe moḻi eṭuttu.*
>
> The police took the witness's statement.

The police take it: മൊഴി എടുക്കുക. In old poetry it is any word or speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മൊഴി** (*moḻi*) is native Dravidian: its Tamil twin *moḻi*, "to say, to speak; a word, a language", stands in DEDR 4989. In the police station it is the word a witness gives.
