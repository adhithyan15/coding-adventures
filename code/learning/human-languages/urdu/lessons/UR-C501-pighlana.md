## You'll want to know: پگھلانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C501-LOHA501-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پگھلانا** — *pighlānā* — "to melt (something)".

> لوہار نے آگ میں لوہا پگھلایا۔
>
> *lohār ne āg meṅ lohā pighlāyā.*
>
> The blacksmith melted the iron in the fire.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پیتل پگھلایا, *us ne pītal pighlāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پگھلانا** (*pighlānā*) is the causative of *pighalnā*, "to melt", which CDIAL lists under *\*praghirati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8486).
