## You'll want to know: యుగం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C368-SASANAM368-05]; assesses=[] -->

**యుగం** — *yugaṁ* — "an era; an age".

> ఇది కంప్యూటర్ల యుగం.
>
> *idi kampyūṭarla yugaṁ.*
>
> This is the age of computers.

Plural: యుగాలు, *yugālu*.

The old stories count time in యుగాలు, ages; today the word also names an era of history.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**యుగం** (*yugaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *yuga* (CDIAL 10482), whose first sense is "a yoke"; it came to mean an age of the world.
