## You'll want to know: नेता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C357-SHASAN357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नेता** — *netā* — "a leader".

> चांगला नेता लोकांचं ऐकतो.
>
> *cāṅglā netā lokāñcaṁ aikto.*
>
> A good leader listens to people.

Masculine. Plural: नेते, *nete*.

Plural नेते; नेतृत्व, leadership.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नेता** (*netā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *netṛ*, "a leader, a guide" (Monier-Williams). The Sanskrit nominative is *netā*.
