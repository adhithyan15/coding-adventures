## You'll want to know: आलस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C548-AVADHI548-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आलस** — *ālas* — "laziness; sloth".

> आलस की वजह से मेरी बस छूट गई।
>
> *Ālas kī vajah se merī bas chūṭ gaī.*
>
> Out of laziness I missed my bus.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आलस** (*ālas*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *ālasya*, "sloth" (CDIAL 1371).
