## You'll want to know: inchado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C485-ARRANHAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**inchado** — "swollen".

> Tenho o joelho inchado.
>
> *My knee is swollen.*

The participle of *inchar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inchado* is the participle of *inchar* "to swell", inherited from Latin *inflāre* "to puff up" (EtymDB).
