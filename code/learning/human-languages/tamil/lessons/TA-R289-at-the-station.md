# Review — at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C289-IRUKKAI-01, TA-LEX-C216-PORUL216-04, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a seat" and "a ticket". Then read **ஈரம்** aloud: which letter opens it? (**ஈ**, *ī*.)
- [YOU WRITE: **இருக்கை** and **சீட்டு**, once each]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C289-IRUKKAI-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C289-BUY-TICKET-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C289-WHEN-LEAVES-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C289-LOST-REPORT-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C289-MISSED-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C288-ASK-WAY-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-BECAUSE-DID-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: One ticket to Madurai, please. What time does the bus leave?]
- [YOU SAY: Is this your seat? No, my seat is there.]
- [YOU SAY: I missed the train, because I came late.]
- [YOU SAY: My bag is missing. Where is the police station?]
- [YOU SAY: one thing that went wrong on a journey of yours, and what you did]
