## You'll want to know: ٹوہنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C448-CURIOUS448-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹوہنا** — *ṭohnā* — "to feel about for; to probe".

> اس نے جیب میں چابی ٹوہی۔
>
> *us ne jeb meṅ chābī ṭohī.*
>
> He felt about in his pocket for the key.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دیوار ٹوہی, *us ne dīvār ṭohī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹوہنا** (*ṭohnā*) is inherited from *ṭōh*, "grope", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5486).
