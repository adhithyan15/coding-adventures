# મારી થેલી ખોવાઈ ગઈ (mārī thelī khovāī gaī) — when things go wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C280-PHARIYAD-01, GU-LEX-C195-LOST195-01, GU-LEX-C88-CARRY-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a complaint". (*phariyād*.) Then "to get lost" and "a bag". (**ખોવાવું**, **થેલી**.)

## Grammar Lens: the lost thing is the subject
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C280-LOST-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C88-CARRY-03, GU-LEX-C86-JOURNEY-04, GU-LEX-CHAAVI-01] -->

> **મારી થેલી ખોવાઈ ગઈ.**
>
> *mārī thelī khovāī gaī.* — "My bag is lost."

The bag is what got lost, so **ગઈ** follows it, and there is no **-એ**. Change the thing and the ending changes: **મારું પાકીટ ખોવાઈ ગયું** (*mārũ pākīṭ khovāī gayũ*), neuter; **મારો ફોન ખોવાઈ ગયો** (*māro phon khovāī gayo*), masculine.

## You'll want to know: a missed train, and help
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C280-MISSED-HELP-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C204-TIMES204-05, GU-LEX-MADAD-KARVI, GU-GRAMMAR-C278-VU-PADE-01] -->

> **મારી ટ્રેન છૂટી ગઈ.**
>
> *mārī ṭren chūṭī gaī.* — "I missed my train."

Word for word, "my train got away": the train is the subject again. Then ask for help: **મને મદદ કરશો?** (*mane madad karśo?*), "will you help me?" And the next step: **મારે પોલીસમાં ફરિયાદ કરવી પડશે** (*māre polīsmā̃ phariyād karvī paḍśe*), "I'll have to make a complaint to the police". **કરવી** follows **ફરિયાદ**.
