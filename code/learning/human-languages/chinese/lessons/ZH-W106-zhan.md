## Script — the standing piece, then a mark over 口
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZHAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 站

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The standing piece, lì, five strokes**: a dot at the top, a horizontal, a dot on the left and a short left-falling stroke on the right, and a stroke along the bottom that rises to the right.

**The right piece, five strokes**: a vertical with a short horizontal to its right; then 口 below, as you write it alone: the left side, the top that turns down, the bottom.

5 + 5 = ten.
