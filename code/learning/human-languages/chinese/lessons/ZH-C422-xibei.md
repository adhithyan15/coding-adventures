## You'll want to know: 西北
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C422-NORTH422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**西北** — *xīběi* — "northwest; the Northwest (of China)".

> **风从西北来** *fēng cóng xīběi lái* — The wind is coming from the northwest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**西北** (*xīběi*) is built from 西 'west' and 北 'north', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sej* and *pok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
