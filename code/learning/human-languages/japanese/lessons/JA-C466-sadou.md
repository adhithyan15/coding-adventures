## You'll want to know: さどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C466-ARTS466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さどう** — *sadō* — "the tea ceremony".

> **さどうの せんせいに あいました。** — *sadō no sensei ni aimashita* — "I met a teacher of the tea ceremony."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さどう** (*sadō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sa*, "tea" (Middle Chinese *drae*) and *dō*, "way" (*dawX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
