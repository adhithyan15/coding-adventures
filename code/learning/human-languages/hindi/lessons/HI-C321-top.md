## You'll want to know: तोप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C321-HAMLA321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तोप** — *top* — "a cannon".

> क़िले की दीवार पर चार तोपें थीं।
>
> *qile kī dīvār par cār topeṁ thīṁ.*
>
> There were four cannons on the fort wall.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तोप** (*top*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Turkish *top*, "a ball", and so a gun that fires balls. It is feminine in Hindi: **तोपें थीं**.
