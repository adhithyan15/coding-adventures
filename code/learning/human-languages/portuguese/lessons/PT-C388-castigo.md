## You'll want to know: o castigo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C388-PENA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o castigo** — "the punishment".

> O castigo foi ficar sem telemóvel uma semana.
>
> *The punishment was a week without a mobile phone.*

For children: *ficar de castigo* — to be grounded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*castigo* is built on *castigar* "to punish" (EtymDB), inherited from Latin *castīgāre* "to correct, to punish", on *castus* "pure" (Lewis & Short).
