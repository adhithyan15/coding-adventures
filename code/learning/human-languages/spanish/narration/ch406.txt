Spanish, chapter 406: Clients, Documents and Data.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
el cliente, la cliente — the person on the other side of the counter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the shop. (La tienda.) Now name the person who buys there: el cliente or la cliente.

The word, taken apart.
Cliente comes from Latin cliens, a person connected to and protected by a patron. The relationship changed over time. In modern Spanish, a cliente is a customer of a shop or a client who uses a professional service.
The word itself can stay the same for a man or a woman; the article does the work: el cliente, la cliente. You may also meet clienta for a woman.

Grammar Lens: meaning in a scene.
Picture a counter. One person works for the shop; the other asks for a product or service. That second person is el cliente or la cliente.
el cliente de la tienda — the shop's customer.
la cliente necesita ayuda — the customer needs help.

Lesson 2 of 7.
el documento — a paper that shows or proves.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the passport and the paper. (El pasaporte, el papel.) A passport is one kind of documento.

The word, taken apart.
El documento comes from Latin documentum, something that gives evidence or instruction. Its deeper verb is docēre, “to teach.” English document, doctor, doctrine and docent belong to the same family: each points to showing, teaching or establishing something.
A papel is the material. A documento is the meaningful record on it — or the digital equivalent. A passport is both a small book and an official documento.

Grammar Lens: useful combinations.
el documento de identidad — the identity document.
el pasaporte es un documento — the passport is a document.
necesito el documento — I need the document.

Lesson 3 of 7.
el dato — one thing given as information.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
A form may ask for your name and address: el nombre, la dirección. Each answer is un dato.

The word, taken apart.
Dato comes straight from Latin datum, “something given.” English kept datum for one item and data for several; Spanish made the everyday noun el dato, plural los datos.
A name, address or telephone number can each be un dato. Together they are los datos personales, personal details.

Grammar Lens: one or several.
un dato — one piece of information.
los datos — the information / details.
nombre y dirección: dos datos — name and address: two details.

Lesson 4 of 7.
el impreso — a form waiting for your data.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the document, the data and the paper. (El documento, los datos, el papel.) Put blank spaces on that paper and you have el impreso.

The word, taken apart.
Impreso is the irregular past participle of imprimir, “to print,” from Latin impressus, “pressed into.” Printing once meant pressing inked type onto paper. English impress preserves the same image.
As a noun, el impreso can mean a printed sheet, especially a form with blank spaces for a procedure. In much of the Spanish-speaking world you will also hear el formulario. Here the exam-derived word is impreso.

Grammar Lens: a form has two sides.
The impreso supplies the labels; you supply los datos:
Nombre: Ana.
Dirección: Calle Sol, 4.

Lesson 5 of 7.
rellenar — fill the blanks with what they need.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the form, the data and full. (El impreso, los datos, lleno.) The action that connects them is rellenar.

The word, taken apart.
Rellenar shows its construction on the surface: re- + llenar, “to fill.” Its broad sense is to fill something again or completely. In an office, it has the practical meaning “to fill in” or “to fill out.”
The DLE gives the form sense precisely: putting the necessary data into blank spaces in forms or documents. That is why the noun chain from this chapter ends in an action:
rellenar el impreso con los datos.

Grammar Lens: a regular -ar action.
You already know the regular -ar machine:
relleno el impreso — I fill in the form.
rellena el impreso — he/she fills in the form; fill in the form.
The noun relleno can also mean a filling, but the sentence and article tell you what job the form is doing.
