## You'll want to know: देणगी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C430-MOBADLA430-02]; assesses=[] -->

**देणगी** — *deṇgī* — "a donation".

> त्यांनी शाळेला मोठी देणगी दिली.
>
> *tyānnī śāḷelā moṭhī deṇgī dilī.*
>
> They gave the school a large donation.

Feminine.

Built on देणे, to give; the receiver takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**देणगी** (*deṇgī*) is built on **देणे**, "to give", with a noun ending; the shape of the ending is not settled.
