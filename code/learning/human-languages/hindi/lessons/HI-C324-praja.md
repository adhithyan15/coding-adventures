## You'll want to know: प्रजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C324-VIDROH324-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रजा** — *prajā* — "the subjects (of a king)".

> राजा की प्रजा बहुत ग़रीब थी।
>
> *rājā kī prajā bahut ġarīb thī.*
>
> The king's subjects were very poor.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रजा** (*prajā*) is Sanskrit *prajā*, "offspring, people, subjects" (Monier-Williams): the people a king rules. It is singular and feminine: **प्रजा ग़रीब थी**.
