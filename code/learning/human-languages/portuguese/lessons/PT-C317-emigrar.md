## You'll want to know: emigrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C317-REFORMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**emigrar** — "to emigrate".

> Nos anos sessenta, o meu avô emigrou para o estrangeiro.
>
> *In the sixties my grandfather emigrated abroad.*

*Emigrar* is leaving; *imigrar* is arriving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emigrar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēmigrāre* "to move out", built on *migrāre* "to move from place to place" (Lewis & Short).
