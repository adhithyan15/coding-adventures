## You'll want to know: पारदर्शकता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C363-SATTA363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पारदर्शकता** — *pārdarśaktā* — "transparency".

> सरकारी कामात पारदर्शकता हवी.
>
> *sarkārī kāmāt pārdarśaktā havī.*
>
> Government work needs transparency.

Feminine.

पारदर्शक, transparent, + -ता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पारदर्शकता** (*pārdarśaktā*) is a learned noun built from Sanskrit *pāradarśaka*, "showing the opposite shore" (Monier-Williams), with *-tā*, "-ness": what lets one see through.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
