French, chapter 286: Les chiffres.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le sondage — the poll, the survey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for censorship, then the French for the controversy.

You'll want to know: le sondage.
le sondage — "the poll, the survey".
Un sondage montre que les jeunes lisent moins. A poll shows that young people read less.
Un sondage d'opinion — an opinion poll.

The word, taken apart.
sondage is built on sonder "to sound, to probe", from sonde "a sounding line", which is usually traced to Old English sund "water, sea"; the origin is not fully settled (EtymDB lists no parent).

Lesson 2 of 5.
le bilan — the final count, the outcome; the review.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the controversy, then the French for the poll.

You'll want to know: le bilan.
le bilan — "the final count, the outcome; the review".
Le bilan de l'année est très positif. The outcome of the year is very positive.
After a disaster, le bilan is the number of dead and injured. Faire le bilan — to take stock.

The word, taken apart.
bilan was borrowed from Italian bilancio "balance" (EtymDB), built on Late Latin bilanx "with two scales" (Lewis & Short).

Les prix sont en hausse. Prices are going up.
En hausse — going up.

The word, taken apart.
hausse is built on hausser "to raise" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Vulgar Latin altiāre, built on Latin altus "high".

Lesson 5 of 5.
la statistique — the statistic; statistics.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the rise, then the French for the drop.

You'll want to know: la statistique.
la statistique — "the statistic; statistics".
Les statistiques montrent une baisse des accidents. The statistics show a fall in accidents.
Plural in the sense of figures; la statistique is also the field of study.

The word, taken apart.
statistique was borrowed from German Statistik (EtymDB), built on Latin status "state", from stāre "to stand": first the science of the state.
