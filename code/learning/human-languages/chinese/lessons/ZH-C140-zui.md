## You'll want to know: zuì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C140-ORDER140-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zuì* — "most".

> *wǒ zuì xǐhuan chūntiān* — Spring is my favourite.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuì* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tswajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'utmost'.
