## You'll want to know: વણવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C168-ROTLI168-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વણવું** — *vaṇvũ* — "to roll out".

> **તે વેલણથી રોટલી વણે છે.** — *te velaṇthī roṭlī vaṇe che* — "She rolls out the rotli with a rolling pin."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **વણવું** (*vaṇvũ*) in this sense is not settled. CDIAL lists Gujarati *vaṇvũ* only as "weaves", under *\*vināti*, and does not list the sense of rolling dough (CDIAL 11773).
