# ਉੱਠਿਆ, ਬੈਠਿਆ — got up, sat down

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-01, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-04, PA-FORM-MILIA-188] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *to get up*, *to sit*, then *I met Aman*.

## Grammar Lens: stem + ਿਆ, stem + ੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-IA-218]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-01, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-04, PA-FORM-MILIA-188, PA-LEX-MAIN-03] -->

Most stems make their past by adding **ਿਆ** for a man and **ੀ** for a woman:

> **ਉੱਠ** + **ਿਆ** → **ਉੱਠਿਆ** · **ਉੱਠ** + **ੀ** → **ਉੱਠੀ**

> **ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜਲਦੀ ਉੱਠਿਆ।** — *maiṁ savere jaldī uṭṭhiā.* — I got up early in the morning (a man speaking).

> **ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜਲਦੀ ਉੱਠੀ।** — *maiṁ savere jaldī uṭṭhī.* — I got up early in the morning (a woman speaking).

> **ਉਹ ਇੱਥੇ ਬੈਠਿਆ।** — *uh itthe baiṭhiā.* — He sat here.

Getting up and sitting are done by you, not to anything, so the ending agrees
with you.
