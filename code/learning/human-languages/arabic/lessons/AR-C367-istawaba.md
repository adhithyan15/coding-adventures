## You'll want to know: استوعب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C367-IDRAK367-03]; assesses=[] -->

**استوعب** — *istawʿaba* — "to grasp".

> لم يستوعب الطلاب الدرس الأول.
>
> *lam yastawʿib aṭ-ṭullāb ad-dars al-awwal.*
>
> The students did not take in the first lesson.

Present: يستوعب, *yastawʿib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استوعب** (*istawʿaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ʿ-b*, "to take the whole of" (Lane). It is Form X, to take a thing in whole: to grasp it fully.
