## You'll want to know: 成才
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C512-DREAMS512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**成才** — *chéngcái* — "to make something of oneself".

> *tā cóngxiǎo nǔlì xuéxí hòulái chéngcái le* — He studied hard from childhood and later made something of himself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**成才** (*chéngcái*) is built from 成 'to become' and 才 'talent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *dzoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to complete' and the second 'ability' and 'a gift for letters'.
