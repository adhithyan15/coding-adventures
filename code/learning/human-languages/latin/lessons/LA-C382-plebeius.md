## You'll want to know: plēbēius, plēbēia, plēbēium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C382-SENATOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**plēbēius, plēbēia, plēbēium** — "plebeian, of the common people".

> Prīmus cōnsul plēbēius ā populō laudātus est.
>
> *The first plebeian consul was praised by the people.*

*plēbēius, plēbēia, plēbēium*, built on *plēbs*, the common people. English *plebeian*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*plēbēius* is built on *plēbs, plēbis* "the common people" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *plebeian* comes from it.
