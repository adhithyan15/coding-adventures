# ಮುಸುಕು (musuku) — to shroud; to cover over

## You'll want to know: ಮುಸುಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C486-HINGARU486-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಸುಕು** — *musuku* — "to shroud; to cover over".

> ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಬೆಟ್ಟಗಳನ್ನು ಮಂಜು ಮುಸುಕಿತು.
>
> *beḷagge beṭṭagaḷannu mañju musukitu.*
>
> In the morning mist shrouded the hills.

Past (it …): ಮುಸುಕಿತು, *musukitu*.

As a noun, a veil: ಮುಸುಕು ಹಾಕು, to cover one's head.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಸುಕು** (*musuku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5030 lists Kannada *musuku*, *musugu*, "to cover, to hide".
