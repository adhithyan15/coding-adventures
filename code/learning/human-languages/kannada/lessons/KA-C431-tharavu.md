# ಠರಾವು (ṭharāvu) — a formal resolution

## You'll want to know: ಠರಾವು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C431-TIRMANISU431-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಠರಾವು** — *ṭharāvu* — "a formal resolution".

> ಸಭೆ ಹೊಸ ಶಾಲೆಯ ಪರವಾಗಿ ಠರಾವು ಮಾಡಿತು.
>
> *sabhe hosa śāleya paravāgi ṭharāvu māḍitu.*
>
> The meeting passed a resolution in favour of a new school.

ಠರಾವು ಮಾಡು / ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು, to pass a resolution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಠರಾವು** (*ṭharāvu*) is usually traced to Hindustani or Marathi *ṭharāv*, "a fixing, a resolution"; DEDR and CDIAL do not list the Kannada form.
