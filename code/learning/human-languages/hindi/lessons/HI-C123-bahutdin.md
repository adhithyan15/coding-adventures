## You'll want to know: बहुत दिन हो गए
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C123-HAAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बहुत दिन हो गए** (*bahut din ho gae*) — "it's been a long time", literally "many days have
become".

**बहुत** and **दिन** are both yours already.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A greeting for someone you have not seen in a while.
