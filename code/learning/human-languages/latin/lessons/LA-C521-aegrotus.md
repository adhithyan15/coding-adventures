## You'll want to know: aegrōtus, aegrōta, aegrōtum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C521-AEGR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**aegrōtus, aegrōta, aegrōtum** — "sick, ill".

> Fīlius aegrōtus in lectō iacet.
>
> *The sick son lies in bed.*

Built on *aeger*. As a noun, *aegrōtus* — a patient.

## The word, taken apart
