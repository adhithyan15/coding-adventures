Gujarati, chapter 510: At the Goldsmith's.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સોની (sonī) — a goldsmith.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for rust, then the Gujarati for to rust.

You'll want to know: સોની.
સોની — sonī — "a goldsmith".
અમારી ગલીમાં એક સોનીની દુકાન છે. amārī galīmā̃ ek sonīnī dukān che. There is a goldsmith's shop in our lane.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સોની (sonī) is built on the Gujarati word for gold, which goes back to Sanskrit suvárṇa, "gold" (CDIAL 13519). CDIAL also lists the older sonār, "a goldsmith", from Sanskrit suvarṇakāra (CDIAL 13520).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઓગાળવું (ogāḷvũ) — to melt down (metal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to rust, then the Gujarati for a goldsmith.

You'll want to know: ઓગાળવું.
ઓગાળવું — ogāḷvũ — "to melt down (metal)".
સોનીએ જૂની વીંટી ઓગાળી. sonīe jūnī vī̃ṭī ogāḷī. The goldsmith melted down the old ring.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વીંટી ઓગાળી, teṇe vī̃ṭī ogāḷī.

The word, taken apart.
ઓગાળવું (ogāḷvũ) goes back to Sanskrit avagalati, "falls down, slips down"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb for making something melt beside the plain verb for oozing and melting (CDIAL 742). The past agrees with what is melted: વીંટી ઓગાળી.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

તાંબું — tā̃bũ — "copper".
દાદીનું જૂનું વાસણ તાંબાનું છે. dādīnũ jūnũ vāsaṇ tā̃bānũ che. Grandmother's old pot is made of copper.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
તાંબું (tā̃bũ) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit tāmrá, "coppery red; copper", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 5779).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઢાળવું (ḍhāḷvũ) — to cast (metal in a mould).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to melt down, then the Gujarati for copper.

You'll want to know: ઢાળવું.
ઢાળવું — ḍhāḷvũ — "to cast (metal in a mould)".
સોનીએ ગરમ તાંબું એક નાના વાસણમાં ઢાળ્યું. sonīe garam tā̃bũ ek nānā vāsaṇmā̃ ḍhāḷyũ. The goldsmith poured the hot copper into a small pot.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે તાંબું ઢાળ્યું, teṇe tā̃bũ ḍhāḷyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઢાળવું (ḍhāḷvũ) comes from ḍhalati, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "to pour" (CDIAL 5581): metal poured into a mould is cast.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઘડાવવું (ghaḍāvvũ) — to have (something) made.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for copper, then the Gujarati for to cast.

You'll want to know: ઘડાવવું.
ઘડાવવું — ghaḍāvvũ — "to have (something) made".
મીરાએ સોની પાસે એક વીંટી ઘડાવી. Mīrāe sonī pāse ek vī̃ṭī ghaḍāvī. Mira had a ring made by the goldsmith.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વીંટી ઘડાવી, teṇe vī̃ṭī ghaḍāvī.

The word, taken apart.
ઘડાવવું (ghaḍāvvũ) is built from ઘડવું, "to shape", with the causative -āv-: to have something shaped by another person, who takes પાસે. The family goes back to Sanskrit ghaṭ, whose causative ghaṭayati Monier-Williams gives as "to join, to put together" (Monier-Williams).
