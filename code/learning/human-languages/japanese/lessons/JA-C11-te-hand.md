# て — hand

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-TE-01] -->

[PAUSE 10s] Write **て** once and say its mora.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-TE-HAND]; assesses=[] -->

> **て** — *te* — hand

The one-sign word names the hand. Sound, meaning, reading, and writing can meet
without adding another shape.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-TE-HAND, JA-SCRIPT-TE-01] -->

1. Hear *te*; touch one hand.
2. See **て**; say “hand.”
3. Hide the word and write its one sign.
