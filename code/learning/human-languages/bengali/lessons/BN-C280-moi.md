## You'll want to know: মই
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C280-UDDHAR280-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মই** — *môi* — "a ladder".

> তারা জানালায় একটা মই লাগাল।
>
> *tārā jānālāy ekṭā môi lāgālô.*
>
> They put a ladder up against the window.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মই** (*môi*) comes down from Sanskrit *matya*, a kind of harrow (CDIAL 9755); CDIAL lists Bengali *moi* as both a harrow and a ladder.
