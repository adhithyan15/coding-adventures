Portuguese, chapter 528: Dia de limpezas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
aspirar — to vacuum, to hoover; to breathe in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for documentary, then the Portuguese for cast.

You'll want to know: aspirar.
aspirar — "to vacuum, to hoover; to breathe in".
Ao sábado aspiro a casa toda. On Saturdays I vacuum the whole house.
Also to aspire: aspirar a uma vida melhor.

The word, taken apart.
aspirar is a learned borrowing from Latin aspīrāre "to breathe on, to aspire" (EtymDB), built on spīrāre "to breathe".
