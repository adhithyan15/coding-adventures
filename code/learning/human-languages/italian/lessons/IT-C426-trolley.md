## You'll want to know: il trolley
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C426-BAGAGLI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il trolley** — "the wheeled suitcase".

> Porto solo un trolley piccolo.
>
> *I am taking only a small wheeled case.*

An English word with an Italian meaning: in English a trolley is a cart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trolley* was borrowed from English *trolley* 'a small cart', and in Italian it came to mean a suitcase on wheels.
