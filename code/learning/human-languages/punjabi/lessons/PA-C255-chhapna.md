## You'll want to know: ਛਾਪਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C255-POST255-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਾਪਣਾ** — *chhāpṇā* — "to print".

> ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਹਰ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਛਾਪਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *daftar har hafte fāram chhāpdā hai.*
>
> The office prints forms every week.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਛਾਪਿਆ, *maiṁ fāram chhāpiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਾਪਣਾ** (*chhāpṇā*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*chapp*, "press, cover, hide"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chāpṇā*, "to print", under it, as a word taken from Hindi *chāpnā* (CDIAL 4994).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
