## You'll want to know: संकेतशब्द
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C517-JAL517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संकेतशब्द** — *saṅketśabda* — "a password".

> मी माझा संकेतशब्द कोणालाही सांगत नाही.
>
> *mī mājhā saṅketśabda koṇālāhī sāṅgat nāhī.*
>
> I do not tell anyone my password.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकेतशब्द** (*saṅketśabda*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *saṁketa*, "an agreed sign", and *śabda*, "word" (Monier-Williams).
