Urdu, chapter 291: When the Ground Shook.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ہلنا (hilnā) — to shake (by itself); to move.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be averted, then the Urdu for caution.

You'll want to know: ہلنا.
ہلنا — hilnā — "to shake (by itself); to move".
رات کو زمین ہلی۔ rāt ko zamīn hilī. The ground shook in the night.
Past (it …): وہ زمین ہلی, voh zamīn hilī.

The word, taken apart.
ہلنا (hilnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin hilnā, "to shake", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 14120). It is a thing moving by itself: the ground, a tooth, a leaf.
