6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la recensione — review.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for bookmark, then the Italian for paperback.

You'll want to know: la recensione.
la recensione — "review".
Ho letto una recensione molto positiva del film. I read a very good review of the film.
Scrivere una recensione — to write a review.

The word, taken apart.
recensione is a learned borrowing from Latin recēnsiō "a review, a counting" (Lewis & Short), from cēnsēre "to assess".

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 6.
il critico — critic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for review, then the Italian for to review.

You'll want to know: il critico.
il critico — "critic".
Il critico ha parlato male dello spettacolo. The critic spoke badly of the show.
The plural is i critici. Also an adjective: un momento critico.

The word, taken apart.
critico is a learned borrowing, through Latin criticus (Lewis & Short), from Greek kritikós "able to judge", from krínein "to judge".

Lesson 5 of 6.

You'll want to know: rileggere.
rileggere — "to reread".
Rileggo spesso i miei libri preferiti. I often reread my favourite books.
Like leggere: past participle riletto.

The word, taken apart.
rileggere is built from ri- "again" and leggere "to read", inherited from Latin legere "to gather, to read" (Lewis & Short).
