## You'll want to know: тёща
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C491-INLAWS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**тёща** (*tyóshcha*) — "a wife's mother, a mother-in-law".

> Тёща часто приезжает к нам на выходные.
>
> *Tyóshcha chásto priyezzháyet k nam na vykhodnýye.*
>
> My mother-in-law often comes to us for the weekend.

Feminine. The wife's mother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB has no entry for *тёща*; it is often linked to *тесть* 'wife's father', but the origin is not settled.
