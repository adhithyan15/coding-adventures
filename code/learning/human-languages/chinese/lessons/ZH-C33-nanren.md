## You'll want to know: 男人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C33-FAMILY33-02]; assesses=[] -->

**男人** — *nánrén* — "man".

> **我家有三个男人** *wǒ jiā yǒu sān ge nánrén* — There are three men in my family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**男人** (*nánrén*) is built from 男 'male' and 人 'person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nom* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
