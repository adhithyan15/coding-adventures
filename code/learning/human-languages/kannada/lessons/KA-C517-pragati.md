## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಗತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C517-PRAGATI517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಗತಿ** — *pragati* — "progress".

> ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ರಸ್ತೆ ಕೆಲಸ ಪ್ರಗತಿಯಲ್ಲಿದೆ.
>
> *ūrinalli raste kelasa pragatiyallide.*
>
> Road work in the town is in progress.

ಪ್ರಗತಿಪರ, progressive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಗತಿ** (*pragati*) is built from Sanskrit *pra-*, "forward", and *gati*, "going, progress" (Monier-Williams).
