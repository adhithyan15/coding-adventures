## You'll want to know: zhíyè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C259-JOB259-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíyè* — "occupation; profession".

> *nǐ de zhíyè shì shénme* — What do you do for a living?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíyè* is built from *zhí* 'a duty' and *yè* 'a task', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyik* and *ngjaep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes two older dictionaries for the first: 'to be in charge' and 'work'.
