## You'll want to know: cautēs, cautis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C480-RUPES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cautēs, cautis** — "a jagged rock, a crag".

> Nāvis in cautēs cadit et frangitur.
>
> *The ship runs onto the jagged rocks and breaks up.*

Third declension, feminine: *cautēs, cautis f.*, mostly plural. Sharp rocks on a coast or a mountain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cautēs* is inherited; Lewis & Short and EtymDB connect it with *cōs* 'whetstone', from PIE *ḱeh₃-* 'to sharpen': a sharp rock.
