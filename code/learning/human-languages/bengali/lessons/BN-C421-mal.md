## You'll want to know: মাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C421-GUDAM421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মাল** — *māl* — "goods; merchandise".

> কুলিরা ট্রাক থেকে মাল নামাল।
>
> *kulirā ṭrāk theke māl nāmālô.*
>
> The porters unloaded the goods from the lorry.

মালপত্র, luggage, is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মাল** (*māl*) is usually traced to Arabic *māl*, "property, wealth, goods", which reached Bengali through Persian.
