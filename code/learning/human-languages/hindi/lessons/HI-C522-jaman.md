## You'll want to know: जामन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C522-CHAACH522-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जामन** — *jāman* — "a starter (for setting curd)".

> रात को दूध में थोड़ा जामन मिलाइए, सुबह तक दही बन जाएगा।
>
> *Rāt ko dūdh meṁ thoṛā jāman milāie, subah tak dahī ban jāegā.*
>
> Mix a little starter into the milk at night, and by morning it will be yoghurt.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जामन** (*jāman*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *yamyatē*, "is held, is fixed" (CDIAL 10428).
