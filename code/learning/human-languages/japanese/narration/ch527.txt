Japanese, chapter 527: Now and the Future.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
げんざい (genzai) — the present; now.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to turn for the better, then the Japanese for rapidly.

You'll want to know: げんざい.
げんざい — genzai — "the present; now".
げんざいの じかんは くにに よります。 — genzai no jikan wa kuni ni yorimasu — "The time right now depends on the country."

The word, taken apart.
げんざい (genzai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are gen, "present" (Middle Chinese henH) and zai, "exist" (dzojX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 6.
これから (korekara) — from now on; after this.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the distant future, then the Japanese for recently.

You'll want to know: これから.
これから — korekara — "from now on; after this".
これから さむく なったら、かぜに ちゅういして ください。 — korekara samuku nattara, kaze ni chūi shite kudasai — "If it gets colder from now on, take care not to catch a cold."

The word, taken apart.
これから (korekara) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
