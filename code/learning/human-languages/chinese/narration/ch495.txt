Mandarin Chinese, chapter 495: Marks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
期中 (qīzhōng) — the middle of the term; midterm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to pass the entrance exam for, then the Mandarin for to fail an entrance exam.

You'll want to know: 期中.
期中 — qīzhōng — "the middle of the term; midterm".
zhè cì qīzhōng kǎoshì wǒ gǎnjué hái xíng — I think this midterm went all right.

The word, taken apart.
期中 (qīzhōng) is built from 期 'a set time' and 中 'the middle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, gi and trjuwng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'an appointed time'. The modern first tone of qī is irregular: gi would regularly give qí.

Lesson 2 of 5.
考场 (kǎochǎng) — exam hall; exam room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to fail an entrance exam, then the Mandarin for the middle of the term.

You'll want to know: 考场.
考场 — kǎochǎng — "exam hall; exam room".
进考场以前我喝了一杯水 jìn kǎochǎng yǐqián wǒ hē le yì bēi shuǐ — Before going into the exam hall, I drank a glass of water.
Two third tones meet in this word: kǎo is said with a rising tone before chǎng, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
考场 (kǎochǎng) is built from 考 'to examine' and 场 'a ground', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khawX and drjang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a threshing floor'; the third tone of chǎng is irregular for drjang.

Lesson 3 of 5.
mǎnfēn — full marks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the middle of the term, then the Mandarin for an exam hall.

You'll want to know: mǎnfēn.
mǎnfēn — "full marks".
zhè cì shùxué kǎoshì tā shì mǎnfēn — He got full marks in this maths test.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
mǎnfēn is built from mǎn 'full' and fēn 'a part', both inherited from Middle Chinese, manX and pjun (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'brimming' and the second 'to divide'.
