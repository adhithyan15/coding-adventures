## You'll want to know: نما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C413-TATAWWUR413-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نما** — *namā* — "to grow".

> نما الاقتصاد هذه السنة.
>
> *namā al-iqtiṣād hādhihi as-sana.*
>
> The economy grew this year.

Present: ينمو, *yanmū*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نما** (*namā*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-m-w*, usually traced to the sense "to grow, to increase". *Namā* is a Form I verb with a weak last letter: to grow. Its present is *yanmū*.
