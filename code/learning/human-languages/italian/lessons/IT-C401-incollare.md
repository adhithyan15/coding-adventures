## You'll want to know: incollare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C401-FILE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**incollare** — "to paste; to glue".

> Ho copiato l'indirizzo e l'ho incollato nel messaggio.
>
> *I copied the address and pasted it into the message.*

*Copia e incolla* — copy and paste.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incollare* is built from *in-* and *colla* "glue", which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *colla* (EtymDB), from Greek *kólla* "glue".
