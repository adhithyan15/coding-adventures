Urdu, chapter 537: Two Ways to Go.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
متبادل (mutabādil) — an alternative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for cover (to shelter behind), then the Urdu for an emergency.

You'll want to know: متبادل.
متبادل — mutabādil — "an alternative".
اگر بس نہیں آئی تو رکشہ ایک متبادل ہے۔ agar bas nahīṅ āī to rikshā ek mutabādil hai. If the bus doesn't come, a rickshaw is another option.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
متبادل (mutabādil) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mutabādil, "exchanged, interchangeable".

One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 5.
منتخب کرنا (muntakhab karnā) — to select (formally).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for something in exchange (for), then the Urdu for a touchstone.

You'll want to know: منتخب کرنا.
منتخب کرنا — muntakhab karnā — "to select (formally)".
اگر میں نے یہ کتاب منتخب کی تو پڑھوں گی۔ agar maiṅ ne yih kitāb muntakhab kī to paṛhūṅ gī. If I pick this book, I will read it.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے ایک کتاب منتخب کی, us ne ek kitāb muntakhab kī.

The word, taken apart.
منتخب کرنا (muntakhab karnā) is built from muntakhab, "chosen", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muntakhab, and karnā, "to do".
