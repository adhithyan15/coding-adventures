## You'll want to know: かんじる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C316-MONOGATARI316-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かんじる** — *kanjiru* — "to feel; to sense".

> **いなかで あきを かんじました。** — *inaka de aki o kanjimashita* — "In the countryside I could feel the autumn."

Polite **かんじます**, *kanjimasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ははが あきを かんじました。**, *haha ga aki o kanjimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんじる** (*kanjiru*) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: *kan*, "feeling", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *komX*), and *jiru*, "a native verb ending", is native Japanese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
