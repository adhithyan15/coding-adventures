## You'll want to know: заводить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C404-KAPOT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**заводить** (*zavodít*) — "to start (an engine); to wind up (a clock)".

> Каждое утро сосед заводит машину в семь часов.
>
> *Kázhdoye útro soséd zavódit mashínu v sem chasóv.*
>
> Every morning the neighbour starts his car at seven.

Imperfective; the perfective is *завести* (*заведу, заведёшь*). Present *завожу, заводишь*. *Машина не заводится* — the car will not start.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*заводить* is built from *за-* and *водить* 'to lead'. *Водить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *voditi* 'to lead' (EtymDB).
