## You'll want to know: नगद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C193-MONEY193-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नगद** — *nagad* — "in cash".

> हूं नगद देऊं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ nagad deū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I pay in cash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नगद** (*nagad*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *naqd*, "ready money".
