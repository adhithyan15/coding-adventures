## You'll want to know: અધૂરું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C529-TABAKKO529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અધૂરું** — *adhūrũ* — "half-finished".

> જો કામ અધૂરું રહેશે તો સાહેબ નારાજ થશે.
>
> *jo kām adhūrũ raheśe to sāheb nārāj thaśe.*
>
> If the work is left half-finished, the boss will be displeased.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અધૂરું** (*adhūrũ*) comes from *\*ardhapūraka*, "half-filled", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "half-done" (CDIAL 664).
