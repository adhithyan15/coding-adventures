## You'll want to know: अनुसरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C351-VIVEKA351-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुसरति** — *anusarati* — "abides by; keeps to".

> छात्रः नियमान् अनुसरति।
>
> *chātraḥ niyamān anusarati.*
>
> The student abides by the rules.

Past (he …): सः उपदेशम् अनुसृतवान्, *saḥ upadeśam anusṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुसरति** (*anusarati*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √sṛ, "to run, to flow"; MW gives *anu-sṛta* "followed, conformed to" (MW).
