## You'll want to know: हँसाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C465-VILAP465-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हँसाना** — *hãsānā* — "to make someone laugh".

> अरुण अपने मज़ाक़ से सब लोगों को हँसाता है।
>
> *Aruṇ apne mazāq se sab logoṁ ko hãsātā hai.*
>
> Arun makes everyone laugh with his jokes.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चों को हँसाया, *usne baccoṁ ko hãsāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हँसाना** (*hãsānā*) is the causative partner of *hãsnā*, "to laugh", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *hásati*, "laughs" (CDIAL 14021).
