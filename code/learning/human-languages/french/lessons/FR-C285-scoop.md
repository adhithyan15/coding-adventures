## You'll want to know: le scoop
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C285-RUMEUR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le scoop** — "the scoop, the exclusive story".

> Ce journal a eu le scoop avant tout le monde.
>
> *This paper got the scoop before everyone else.*

Borrowed from English; official French proposes *l'exclusivité*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scoop* was borrowed from English *scoop* "a ladle; a story got ahead of rivals" (EtymDB).
