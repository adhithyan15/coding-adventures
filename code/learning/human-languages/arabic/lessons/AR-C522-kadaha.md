# كدح (kadaḥa) — to toil

## You'll want to know: كدح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C522-AMAL522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**كدح** — *kadaḥa* — "to toil".

> كدح المزارع في الحقل طوال اليوم.
>
> *kadaḥa al-muzāriʿ fī al-ḥaql ṭūla al-yawm.*
>
> The farmer toiled in the field all day.

Present: يكدح, *yakdaḥ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كدح** (*kadaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-d-ḥ*, "to labour, to strive" (Lane). Lane gives yakdaḥu as its present.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
