## You'll want to know: ಓದುಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C370-PATRIKODYAMA370-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಓದುಗ** — *ōduga* — "a reader".

> ಈ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಗೆ ಲಕ್ಷ ಓದುಗರು ಇದ್ದಾರೆ.
>
> *ī patrikege lakṣa ōdugaru iddāre.*
>
> This newspaper has a hundred thousand readers.

Plural: ಓದುಗರು, *ōdugaru*.

-ಗ makes "one who does": ಓದು, to read, gives ಓದುಗ, a reader.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಓದುಗ** (*ōduga*) is a native Kannada noun: *ōdu*, "to read, to recite" (DEDR 1052), with the ending -*ga*, "one who does".
