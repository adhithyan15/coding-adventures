## You'll want to know: سازمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C382-PROZHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سازمان** (*sâzmân*) — "an organisation".

> او در یک سازمان بین المللی کار میکند.
>
> *u dar yek sâzmân-e beyn ol-melali kâr mikonad.*
>
> She works for an international organisation.

From *sâz-*, the present stem of *sâkhtan* "to make", + *-mân*. Plural *sâzmânhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سازمان** is native Persian, built on *sâz-*, the present stem of *sâkhtan* "to make, to build", with the suffix *-mân*: an organisation is something built and arranged.
