## You'll want to know: झगडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C340-RAHASYA340-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झगडणे** — *jhagaḍṇe* — "to wrestle with; to struggle".

> नायक शेवटपर्यंत संकटांशी झगडला.
>
> *nāyak śevaṭparyant saṅkaṭāṁśī jhagaḍlā.*
>
> The hero wrestled with troubles to the very end.

Past (he …): तो झगडला, *to jhagaḍlā*.

The opponent takes -शी: संकटांशी झगडणे; also to quarrel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झगडणे** (*jhagaḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *jhagaḍṇe*, 'to quarrel', under *\*jhagaḍ*, 'quarrel' (CDIAL 5321).
