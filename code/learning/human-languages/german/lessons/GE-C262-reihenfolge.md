## You'll want to know: die Reihenfolge
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C262-ZUNAECHST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Reihenfolge** — "the order, the sequence".

> Die Reihenfolge der Bilder war falsch.
>
> *The order of the pictures was wrong.*

Plural: *die Reihenfolgen*. *In der richtigen Reihenfolge* — in the right order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Reihenfolge* joins *Reihe* "row", from Middle High German *rīhe* "row, line", and *Folge* "what follows", from Middle High German *volge* (EtymDB). Both are inherited Germanic words.
