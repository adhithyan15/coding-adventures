## You'll want to know: జాతీయ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C401-PAURUDU401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**జాతీయ** — *jātīya* — "national".

> జాతీయ సెలవు రోజున బ్యాంకులు మూసి ఉంటాయి.
>
> *jātīya selavu rōjuna byāṅkulu mūsi uṇṭāyi.*
>
> Banks are closed on a national holiday.

It stands before a noun and does not change: జాతీయ సెలవు, a national holiday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జాతీయ** (*jātīya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jātīya*, "belonging to a species or kind" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for what belongs to the nation.
