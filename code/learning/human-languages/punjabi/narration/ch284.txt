Punjabi, chapter 284: Getting Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉਤਰਨਾ (utarnā) — to get off; to come down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to get stuck, then the Punjabi for to sway.

You'll want to know: ਉਤਰਨਾ.
ਉਤਰਨਾ — utarnā — "to get off; to come down".
ਮੈਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਉਤਰਾਂਗਾ। maiṁ agle saṭeshan utte utrāṅgā. I will get off at the next station.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੱਸ ਤੋਂ ਉਤਰਿਆ, maiṁ bass ton utriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਉਤਰਨਾ (utarnā) is inherited from Sanskrit uttarati, "alights, descends"; CDIAL lists Punjabi uttarṇā, "to descend", under it (CDIAL 1770).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਉਤਾਰਨਾ (utārnā) — to take down; to unload.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to sway, then the Punjabi for to get off.

You'll want to know: ਉਤਾਰਨਾ.
ਉਤਾਰਨਾ — utārnā — "to take down; to unload".
ਅਮਨ ਬੱਸ ਦੀ ਛੱਤ ਤੋਂ ਅਟੈਚੀ ਉਤਾਰੇਗਾ। Aman bass dī chhatt ton aṭaichī utāregā. Aman will take the suitcase down from the roof of the bus.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਟੈਚੀ ਉਤਾਰੀ, maiṁ aṭaichī utārī.

The word, taken apart.
ਉਤਾਰਨਾ (utārnā) is the causative partner of utarnā, inherited from Sanskrit uttārayati, "makes descend"; CDIAL lists Punjabi utārṇā, "to make descend", under it (CDIAL 1770).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਧੱਕਾ (dhakkā) — a push; a shove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to get off, then the Punjabi for to take down.

You'll want to know: ਧੱਕਾ.
ਧੱਕਾ — dhakkā — "a push; a shove".
ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ੇ ਨੂੰ ਧੱਕਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ। Aman ne darvāze nū̃ dhakkā dittā. Aman gave the door a push.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਧੱਕਾ (dhakkā) is inherited from a reconstructed dhakk, "push, strike"; CDIAL lists Punjabi dhakkā, "a push, blow", under it (CDIAL 6701).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to take down, then the Punjabi for a push.

You'll want to know: ਆਖ਼ਰੀ.
ਆਖ਼ਰੀ — ākhrī — "last; final".
ਇਹ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਹੈ। eh ākhrī saṭeshan hai. This is the last station.

The word, taken apart.
ਆਖ਼ਰੀ (ākhrī) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic ākhir, "last", with the ending -ī.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਅੱਪੜਨਾ (appaṛnā) — to get to; to reach a place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a push, then the Punjabi for last.

You'll want to know: ਅੱਪੜਨਾ.
ਅੱਪੜਨਾ — appaṛnā — "to get to; to reach a place".
ਬੱਸ ਦੁਪਹਿਰ ਤੱਕ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਅੱਪੜੇਗੀ। bass dupahir tak shahir appṛegī. The bus will get to the city by noon.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਅੱਪੜਿਆ, maiṁ saṭeshan appṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਅੱਪੜਨਾ (appaṛnā) is inherited from Sanskrit āpatati, "flies towards"; CDIAL lists Punjabi appaṛṇā, "to arrive, come to hand", under it (CDIAL 1193).
