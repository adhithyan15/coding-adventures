# ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ (varadakṣiṇe) — a dowry

## You'll want to know: ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C325-VIVAHA325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ** — *varadakṣiṇe* — "a dowry".

> ಆ ಕುಟುಂಬ ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ ಕೇಳಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ಮದುವೆ ನಿಂತುಹೋಯಿತು.
>
> *ā kuṭumba varadakṣiṇe kēḷiddarinda maduve nintuhōyitu.*
>
> The wedding was called off because that family asked for a dowry.

The money or gifts demanded from the bride's family; the practice is against the law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ** (*varadakṣiṇe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *vara*, "a suitor, a bridegroom" (CDIAL 11309), and *dakṣiṇā*, "a fee, a gift" (CDIAL 6120).
