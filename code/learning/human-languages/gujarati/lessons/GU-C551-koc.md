## You'll want to know: કોચ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C551-TALIM551-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કોચ** — *koc* — "a sports coach".

> જો કોચ ના કહેશે તો હું મેચમાં નહીં રમું.
>
> *jo koc nā kaheśe to hũ mecmā̃ nahī̃ ramũ.*
>
> If the coach says no, I won't play in the match.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કોચ** (*koc*) is borrowed from English *coach*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
