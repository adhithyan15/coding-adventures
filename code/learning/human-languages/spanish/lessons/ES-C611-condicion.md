## You'll want to know: la condición
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C611-CONDIC-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la condición** — "the condition".

> Te ayudo con una condición.
>
> *I will help you on one condition.*

*Las condiciones de trabajo* — working conditions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condición* is a learned borrowing from Latin *condicio* 'agreement, terms', built on *condicere* 'to agree together', from *dicere* 'to say' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
