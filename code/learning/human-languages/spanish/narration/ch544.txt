Spanish, chapter 544: Un bebé en camino.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el nacimiento — the birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to bring forward, then the Spanish for to remain.

You'll want to know: el nacimiento.
el nacimiento — "the birth".
Celebramos el nacimiento de mi sobrina. We celebrated my niece's birth.
La fecha de nacimiento — the date of birth.

The word, taken apart.
nacimiento is built on nacer 'to be born' with the ending -miento. Nacer comes from Latin nāscī 'to be born' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
el parto — the delivery (of a baby), the birth.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: el parto.
el parto — "the delivery (of a baby), the birth".
El parto fue largo pero todo salió bien. The birth was long but everything went well.
Estar de parto — to be in labour.

The word, taken apart.
parto is inherited from Latin partus 'a birth' (EtymDB), built on parere 'to bring forth, to give birth'. It is not the parere 'to appear' behind parecer.

Lesson 3 of 5.
dar a luz — to give birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the birth, then the Spanish for the delivery.

You'll want to know: dar a luz.
dar a luz — "to give birth".
Dio a luz a una niña el martes. She gave birth to a girl on Tuesday.
Dar a luz a + baby. Word for word, to give to the light.

The word, taken apart.
dar a luz is built on dar, inherited from Latin dare 'to give', and luz, inherited from Latin lūx 'light' (Lewis & Short). To give birth is to give the child to the light.

Lesson 4 of 5.
el embarazo — the pregnancy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the delivery, then the Spanish for to give birth.

You'll want to know: el embarazo.
el embarazo — "the pregnancy".
Tuvo un embarazo tranquilo. She had an easy pregnancy.
It once meant an obstacle, and in formal Spanish it still can.

The word, taken apart.
embarazo is built on embarazar, first 'to get in the way, to hinder'. The origin of embarazar is not settled; it is often linked to Portuguese embaraçar and an old word baraça 'a cord'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
el recién nacido — the newborn baby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to give birth, then the Spanish for the pregnancy.

You'll want to know: el recién nacido.
el recién nacido — "the newborn baby".
El recién nacido pesó tres kilos. The newborn baby weighed three kilos.
It agrees: una niña recién nacida.

The word, taken apart.
el recién nacido is built on recién, the short form of reciente, from Latin recēns 'fresh, new', and nacido, the participle of nacer, from Latin nāscī 'to be born' (Lewis & Short).
