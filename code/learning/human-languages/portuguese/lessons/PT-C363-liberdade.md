## You'll want to know: a liberdade

**a liberdade** — "the freedom, the liberty".

> A liberdade de expressão é muito importante.
>
> *Freedom of speech is very important.*

*Liberdade de expressão* — freedom of speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liberdade* is inherited from Latin *lībertās* "the condition of a free person, freedom" (Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese *liberdade* (EtymDB), built on *līber* "free".
