## You'll want to know: случайный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C355-SLUCHAYNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**случайный** (*slucháynyy*) — "accidental, chance".

> Это была случайная встреча.
>
> *Éto bylá slucháynaya vstrécha.*
>
> It was a chance meeting.

*Случайно* — by chance. *Ты случайно не знаешь…?* — you would not happen to know…?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**случайный** is built on *случай* "an event, a chance", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*sŭlučajĭ*, from *\*lučiti*, whose sense EtymDB gives only tentatively, as "to find, to reach" (EtymDB). A chance is what you happen to come upon.
