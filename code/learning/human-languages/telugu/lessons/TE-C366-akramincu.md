# ఆక్రమించు (ākramiñcu) — to occupy; to invade

## You'll want to know: ఆక్రమించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C366-NIDHI366-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆక్రమించు** — *ākramiñcu* — "to occupy; to invade".

> శత్రువులు కోటను ఆక్రమించారు.
>
> *śatruvulu kōṭanu ākramiñcāru.*
>
> The enemies occupied the fort.

Past (I …): ఆక్రమించాను, *ākramiñcānu*.

Also to take land unlawfully: స్థలం ఆక్రమించు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆక్రమించు** (*ākramiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ākramaṇa*, "stepping on, approaching" (CDIAL 1016), from *kram*, "to step", with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
