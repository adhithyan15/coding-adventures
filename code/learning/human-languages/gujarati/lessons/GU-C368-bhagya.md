## You'll want to know: ભાગ્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C368-NASIB368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાગ્ય** — *bhāgya* — "destiny".

> જો ભાગ્ય સારું હોત તો તે આજે અમીર હોત.
>
> *jo bhāgya sārũ hot to te āje amīr hot.*
>
> If fate had been kind, he would be rich today.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાગ્ય** (*bhāgya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *bhāgya*, "fortune, fate" (Monier-Williams).
