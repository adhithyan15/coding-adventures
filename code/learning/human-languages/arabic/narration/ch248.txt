إنترنت (intarnit) — the internet.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: إنترنت.
إنترنت — intarnit — "the internet".

The word, taken apart.
إنترنت (intarnit) is a modern loan from English internet (EtymDB); it has no Arabic root.

Lesson 2 of 5.
موقع (mawqiʿ) — a website, a site.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for jasmine, then the Arabic for the internet.

You'll want to know: موقع.
موقع — mawqiʿ — "a website, a site".

The word, taken apart.
موقع (mawqiʿ) is built on the native Arabic root w-q-ʿ, "to fall" (Lane). Mawqiʿ is the ma- place noun, "a place where a thing falls", hence "a site".

تطبيق (taṭbīq) — an app, an application.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the internet, then the Arabic for a website.

You'll want to know: تطبيق.
تطبيق — taṭbīq — "an app, an application".

The word, taken apart.
تطبيق (taṭbīq) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-b-q, "to cover, to fit one on another" (Lane). Taṭbīq is the verbal noun of Form II, "applying"; the app is a modern sense.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مسلسل (musalsal) — a TV series.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an app, then the Arabic for a device.

You'll want to know: مسلسل.
مسلسل — musalsal — "a TV series".

The word, taken apart.
مسلسل (musalsal) is the passive participle of salsala, "to link in a chain", built on the doubled root s-l-s-l, which Lane lists under s-l-l with silsila, "a chain": linked one to the next, a serial.
