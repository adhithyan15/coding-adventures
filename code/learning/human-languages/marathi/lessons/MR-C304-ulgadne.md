## You'll want to know: उलगडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C304-VARNAN304-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उलगडणे** — *ulgaḍṇe* — "to unfold; to come clear".

> हळूहळू त्या घटनेचा अर्थ उलगडला.
>
> *haḷūhaḷū tyā ghaṭnecā artha ulgaḍlā.*
>
> Little by little the meaning of that event came clear.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने उलगडलं, *tyāne ulgaḍlaṁ*.

Intransitive (अर्थ उलगडतो, the meaning unfolds) and transitive (कागद उलगडणे, to unfold a paper).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उलगडणे** (*ulgaḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *algaḍṇẽ*, 'to get unravelled; to unravel', most likely a variant of this verb, under *\*ullagyati*, 'is unfastened' (CDIAL 2363).
