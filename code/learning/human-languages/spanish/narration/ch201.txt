Spanish, chapter 201: Comieron --- One Ending, Two Families.
1 lesson. All 1 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 1.
comieron — they ate, they lived — the two families that already share a past, sharing its plural too.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
In the past singular, how many ending sets do -er and -ir verbs need between them? (One — comí and viví take the same endings.)
[pause 1 second]
The plural keeps that bargain.

Grammar Lens: one ending, two families.
comieron, vivieron — they ate, they lived.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
comer. present: comen. past: comieron.
vivir. present: viven. past: vivieron.
The past ending is -ieron for both families, exactly as -í served both in the singular. And we follows the same shape:
comimos, vivimos.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now the interesting part, and it is not the one you would guess.
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
hablar. present: hablamos. past: hablamos — the same.
comer. present: comemos. past: comimos — different.
vivir. present: vivimos. past: vivimos — the same.
Two families out of three collapse. Vivimos is we live and we lived, exactly as hablamos is both. Only -er keeps the two apart — and only because its past borrowed -imos from the -ir family and so moved away from its own present -emos.
[pause 2 seconds]
You met comemos and vivimos together as the first place -er and -ir stop agreeing. This is the second place, and it goes the other way.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: comieron, vivieron]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: comemos, comimos — and vivimos, which is both]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[repeat that twice]
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say: they ate.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Now the same form from vivir.
[question — say your answer, then pause 14 seconds]
Which of hablamos, comimos and vivimos is a past form ONLY?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
The past they ending for -er and -ir? (-ieron, for both.)
[pause 2 seconds]
Which families lose the present-past distinction in the we form? (-ar and -ir — only -er keeps it.)
