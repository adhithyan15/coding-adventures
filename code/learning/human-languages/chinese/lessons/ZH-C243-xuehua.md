## You'll want to know: 雪花
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C243-SEASON243-02]; assesses=[] -->

**雪花** — *xuěhuā* — "snowflake".

> **今天多云外边有雪花** *jīntiān duōyún wàibian yǒu xuěhuā* — It's cloudy today, and there are snowflakes outside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**雪花** (*xuěhuā*) is built from 雪 'snow' and 花 'flower', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjwet* and *xwae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
