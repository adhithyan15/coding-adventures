## You'll want to know: કપડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C154-CLOTH154-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કપડું** — *kapḍũ* — "cloth, garment".

> **આ કપડું નરમ છે.** — *ā kapḍũ naram che* — "This cloth is soft."

The plural **કપડાં**, *kapḍā̃*, means clothes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કપડું** (*kapḍũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *karpaṭa*, "patched garment, rag"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "any garment" and asks whether it came by way of Hindi (CDIAL 2871).
