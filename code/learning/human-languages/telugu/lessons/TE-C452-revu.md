## You'll want to know: రేవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C452-NAUKA452-01]; assesses=[] -->

**రేవు** — *rēvu* — "a harbour; a landing place".

> సాయంత్రం పడవలు రేవుకు తిరిగి వచ్చాయి.
>
> *sāyantraṁ paḍavalu rēvuku tirigi vaccāyi.*
>
> In the evening the boats came back to the harbour.

A harbour for ships, and also the steps down to a river where people bathe and boats land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రేవు** (*rēvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 516 lists Telugu *rēvu*, "a landing place, a port, a ford", in the family of Tamil *iṟaṅku*, "to descend, to disembark".
