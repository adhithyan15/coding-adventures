## You'll want to know: اصطحب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C439-MANZAR439-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اصطحب** — *iṣṭaḥaba* — "to take along; to bring along".

> اصطحبنا الأطفال إلى المتحف.
>
> *iṣṭaḥabnā al-aṭfāl ilā al-matḥaf.*
>
> We took the children along to the museum.

Present: يصطحب, *yaṣṭaḥib*.

Form VIII turns its t into ṭ after ṣ: *iṣṭaḥaba*, not *iṣtaḥaba*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اصطحب** (*iṣṭaḥaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-ḥ-b*, "to be a companion" (Lane). It is Form VIII; a *ṣāḥib* is a companion.
