## You'll want to know: いりぐち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-IRIGUCHI]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-I-01, JA-SCRIPT-RI-01, JA-SCRIPT-CHI-01] -->

**いりぐち** — *iriguchi* — "an entrance, the way in". You see it on signs at
stations and shops.

Four beats: *i–ri–gu–chi*. **い**, **り** and **ち** you already write. The
third sign is **く** with the voicing mark, so *ku* becomes *gu*. The next
lesson writes it.

The *guchi* is *kuchi*, "a mouth", voiced where it joins the word in front of
it: an entrance is the mouth you go in by.
