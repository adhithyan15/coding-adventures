## You'll want to know: ગુનેગાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C288-KABUL288-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુનેગાર** — *gunegār* — "a criminal".

> જો ત્યાં અજવાળું હોત તો મીરા ગુનેગારને ઓળખત.
>
> *jo tyā̃ ajvāḷũ hot to Mīrā gunegārne oḷkhat.*
>
> If there had been light, Mira would have recognized the criminal.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુનેગાર** (*gunegār*) is usually traced to Persian *gunahgār*, "a sinner, an offender", built on *gunāh*, the word behind **ગુનો**. It names the person, a man or a woman.
