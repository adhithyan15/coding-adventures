## You'll want to know: seguidamente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C269-PRIMEIRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**seguidamente** — "next, then (in a series)".

> Lavámos os pratos e, seguidamente, fomos dormir.
>
> *We washed the dishes and next we went to bed.*

Formal, common in reports. In everyday talk, *a seguir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seguidamente* is built on *seguido* "followed", the past participle of *seguir*, which comes from Latin *sequī* "to follow" (Lewis & Short).
