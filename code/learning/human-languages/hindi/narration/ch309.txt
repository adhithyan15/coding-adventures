Hindi, chapter 309: The Relief Camp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
राहत (rāhat) — relief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to set free, then the Hindi for to dart.

You'll want to know: राहत.
राहत — rāhat — "relief".
सरकार ने गाँव में राहत भेजी। sarkār ne gā̃v meṁ rāhat bhejī. The government sent relief to the village.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
राहत (rāhat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic rāḥa, "rest, ease". It is both the feeling of relief and the food and blankets sent after a disaster.

Lesson 2 of 5.
शिविर (śivir) — a camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to dart, then the Hindi for relief.

You'll want to know: शिविर.
शिविर — śivir — "a camp".
माफ़ कीजिए, राहत शिविर कैसे जाते हैं? māf kījiye, rāhat śivir kaise jāte haiṁ? Excuse me, how do I get to the relief camp?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
शिविर (śivir) is Sanskrit śibira (also written śivira), "a royal camp, a tent" (Monier-Williams). Today it is a camp of any kind: राहत (rāhat) शिविर, a relief camp.

Lesson 3 of 5.
डेरा (ḍerā) — an encampment; a temporary shelter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for relief, then the Hindi for a camp.

You'll want to know: डेरा.
डेरा — ḍerā — "an encampment; a temporary shelter".
बाढ़ के बाद लोग नदी के किनारे डेरे में रहे। bāṛh ke bād log nadī ke kināre ḍere meṁ rahe. After the flood people lived in a camp on the riverbank.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
डेरा (ḍerā) is inherited from ḍēra, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5564), which gathers words for tents and resting places. डेरे में रहना is to live in a camp or a shelter, away from home.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पीड़ित (pīṛit) — a victim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a camp, then the Hindi for an encampment.

You'll want to know: पीड़ित.
पीड़ित — pīṛit — "a victim".
पीड़ितों को खाना और कंबल मिले। pīṛitoṁ ko khānā aur kambal mile. The victims were given food and blankets.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
पीड़ित (pīṛit) is Sanskrit pīḍita, "pressed, afflicted" (Monier-Williams). As a noun it is the person who suffered: बाढ़ पीड़ित, flood victims.

Lesson 5 of 5.
शरण (śaraṇ) — refuge; shelter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an encampment, then the Hindi for a victim.

You'll want to know: शरण.

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
शरण (śaraṇ) is Sanskrit śaraṇa, "a shelter, a protection" (Monier-Williams), feminine in Hindi. शरण लेना is to take refuge.
