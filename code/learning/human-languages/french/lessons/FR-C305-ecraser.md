## You'll want to know: écraser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C305-NAUFRAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**écraser** — "to crash (as s'écraser); to crush".

> Un petit avion s'est écrasé dans la forêt.
>
> *A small plane crashed in the forest.*

Reflexive for a plane that crashes: *s'écraser*. Without *se* it is common too, to crush or to mash: *écraser des pommes de terre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*écraser* comes from Middle French *escraser* "to squash" (EtymDB), usually traced to a Scandinavian word, Old Norse *krasa* "to shatter".
