## You'll want to know: अड़णो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C269-BAHANO269-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अड़णो** — *aṛṇo* — "to dig in one's heels; to insist".

> छोरो अड़ै है अर स्कूल कोनी जावै।
>
> *chhoro aṛai hai ar skūl konī jāvai.*
>
> The boy digs in his heels and won't go to school.

Past (he …): वो अड़्यो, *vo aṛyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अड़णो** (*aṛṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*aḍ*, "obstruct, stop"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *aṛnā*, "to come to a stop", and Kumaoni *aṛṇo*, "to persist" (CDIAL 187). Someone who stops and will not move on is digging in.
