## You'll want to know: cautiō, cautiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C392-CAVEO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cautiō, cautiōnis** — "caution; a guarantee, a bond".

> Sine cautiōne pecūniam nōn crēdit.
>
> *He does not lend money without a guarantee.*

*cautiō, cautiōnis* f., from *caveō*: carefulness, and in law a written security. English *caution*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cautiō* is the noun built on *caveō* "to beware, to take care" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *caution* comes from it.
