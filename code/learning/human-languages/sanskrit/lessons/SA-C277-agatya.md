# आगत्य (āgatya) — one action, then the next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-GERUND-TVA-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-TAVAT-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-TAVAT-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "the boy, having gone home, reads". (*bālaḥ gṛhaṃ gatvā paṭhati*.) Then say "he went home". (*saḥ gṛhaṃ gatavān*.) Now put the two together.

## You'll want to know: -त्वा with the past
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-01]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-GERUND-TVA-01, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-01, SA-LEX-KHADATI-EAT, SA-LEX-C170-WRK170-01] -->

> **सः खादित्वा गतवान्।**
>
> *saḥ khāditvā gatavān.* — "He ate, then he left."

The **-त्वा** form finishes the first action, and the last verb carries the past for both. **खादित्वा** (*khāditvā*) is "having eaten", and **कृत्वा** (*kṛtvā*) "having done":

> **सा गृहं गत्वा पठितवती।**
>
> *sā gṛhaṃ gatvā paṭhitavatī.* — "She went home and read."

> **ते कार्यं कृत्वा गतवन्तः।**
>
> *te kāryaṃ kṛtvā gatavantaḥ.* — "They did the work and left."

Both actions belong to one doer, and the **-त्वा** action comes first.

## You'll want to know: -य after a prefix
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-AGACCHATI-COME, SA-LEX-C75-TRAVEL-05, SA-LEX-C71-MOVE-03, SA-LEX-PATHATI-READ] -->

A verb with a prefix, such as **आगच्छति** (*āgacchati*), "comes", or **प्रविशति** (*praviśati*), "goes in", takes **-य** (*-ya*) in place of **-त्वा**: one short ending, after a prefix (Pāṇini 7.1.37).

> **सः आगत्य पठति।**
>
> *saḥ āgatya paṭhati.* — "He comes and reads."

> **मीरा गृहं प्रविश्य उपविशति।**
>
> *mīrā gṛhaṃ praviśya upaviśati.* — "Mira goes into the house and sits down."

> **सा उपविश्य खादितवती।**
>
> *sā upaviśya khāditavatī.* — "She sat down and ate."

| the verb | the action finished |
|---|---|
| **गच्छति** | **गत्वा** (*gatvā*) |
| **आगच्छति** | **आगत्य** (*āgatya*) |
| **प्रविशति** | **प्रविश्य** (*praviśya*) |
| **उपविशति** | **उपविश्य** (*upaviśya*) |
