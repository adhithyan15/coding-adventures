# ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠೆ (pratiṣṭhe) — prestige

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C389-SHLAGHISU389-01, KA-LEX-C389-SHLAGHISU389-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to commend, then the Kannada for to felicitate.

## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C389-SHLAGHISU389-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠೆ** — *pratiṣṭhe* — "prestige".

> ಈ ಗೆಲುವು ನಮ್ಮ ಊರಿನ ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠೆಯ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ.
>
> *ī geluvu namma ūrina pratiṣṭheya praśne.*
>
> This win is a matter of our town's prestige.

ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತ, prestigious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠೆ** (*pratiṣṭhe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pratiṣṭhā*, "a foundation, a firm standing" (CDIAL 8608).

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
