Hindi, chapter 422: Houses Large and Small.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
झोंपड़ी (jhõpṛī) — a hut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for next door, then the Hindi for the countryside.

You'll want to know: झोंपड़ी.
झोंपड़ी — jhõpṛī — "a hut".
खेत के पास एक झोंपड़ी है। khet ke pās ek jhõpṛī hai. There is a hut near the field.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
झोंपड़ी (jhõpṛī) goes back to jhuppa, "a hut", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5403), which lists Hindi jhõpṛī.

Before the new one: say the Hindi for the countryside, then the Hindi for a hut.

उस बड़ी कोठी में एक अमीर परिवार रहता है। us baṛī koṭhī meṁ ek amīr parivār rahtā hai. A rich family lives in that big bungalow.

Lesson 3 of 5.
मकान (makān) — a house (the building).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a hut, then the Hindi for a bungalow.

You'll want to know: मकान.
मकान — makān — "a house (the building)".
हमने किराये पर एक मकान लिया। hamne kirāye par ek makān liyā. We rented a house.
Masculine.
घर is home; मकान (makān) is the building, the thing you rent, buy or build.

The word, taken apart.
मकान (makān) is usually traced to Arabic makān, "a place", which came through Persian and narrowed in Hindi to a house as a building.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a bungalow, then the Hindi for a house (the building).

You'll want to know: अहाता.
अहाता — ahātā — "a compound, an enclosure".
स्कूल के अहाते में बच्चे खेल रहे थे। skūl ke ahāte meṁ bacce khel rahe the. The children were playing in the school compound.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अहाता (ahātā) is usually traced to Arabic iḥāṭa, "an enclosing, an enclosure", through Persian: the walled ground round a house, a school or an office.

Warm-up.
