## You'll want to know: élever
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C494-EDUCATION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**élever** — "to bring up, to raise".

> Ses grands-parents l'ont élevé à la campagne.
>
> *His grandparents brought him up in the country.*

Accent change: *j'élève*, *nous élevons*. Also to raise animals: *élever des moutons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*élever* is built from *é-* and *lever* "to raise", which is inherited from Latin *levāre* "to lift" (Lewis & Short).
