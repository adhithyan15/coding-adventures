# আমি পড়ছিলাম (āmi pôṛchhilām) — going on now, going on then

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was at home" and "I will go". (*chhilām*, *jābô*.) Both are whole facts. This lesson is about things in the middle of happening.

## You'll want to know: going on now
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-NOW-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C08-PORA-01, BN-LEX-C63-HOME63-04, BN-LEX-C79-TIME79-04, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-EKTA-01] -->

> **আমি এখন একটা বই পড়ছি।**
>
> *āmi ekhôn ekṭā bôi pôṛchhi.* — "I'm reading a book now."

What is going on at this moment takes **-ছ-** after the stem: **আমি পড়ছি** (*āmi pôṛchhi*), **তুমি পড়ছ** (*tumi pôṛchhô*), **সে পড়ছে** (*she pôṛchhe*), **আপনি পড়ছেন** (*āpni pôṛchhen*). After a stem that ends in a vowel it is **-চ্ছ-**: **যাচ্ছি** (*jāchchhi*), "I'm going"; **সে খাচ্ছে** (*she khāchchhe*); **কী হচ্ছে?** (*kī hôchchhe?*), "what's happening?". An **এ** said *e* rises, as it does in the past: **শেখা** → **শিখছি** (*shikhchhi*).

## You'll want to know: going on then
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

Put **-ছিল-** where **-ছ-** was, and the same scene is in the past:

> **কাল সন্ধ্যায় আমি বই পড়ছিলাম।**
>
> *kāl sôndhyāy āmi bôi pôṛchhilām.* — "Yesterday evening I was reading."

- **আমি পড়ছিলাম** (*āmi pôṛchhilām*) — I was reading
- **তুমি পড়ছিলে** (*tumi pôṛchhile*) — you were reading
- **সে পড়ছিল** (*she pôṛchhilô*) — he or she was reading
- **আপনি** / **তিনি** **পড়ছিলেন** (*pôṛchhilen*) — with respect

and **যাচ্ছিলাম** (*jāchchhilām*), "I was going". The plain past, **পড়লাম**, is an event; **পড়ছিলাম** is the scene around one.
