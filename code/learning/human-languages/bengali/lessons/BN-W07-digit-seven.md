## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SEVEN-01]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-DIGIT-NINE-01, BN-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FOUR-01] -->

> ৭

That is the Bengali digit **seven**, and it is the shape of a **9**: a closed
body at the top with a straight stem dropping from its right side.

The chapter's second false friend, and the last shape in the set:

| shape | in the digits you already read | in Bengali |
|---|---|---|
| two stacked loops | eight | **four** |
| body with a dropped stem | nine | **seven** |

Both lies run the same way. In each case the Bengali digit **for** that value —
**৮** for eight, **৯** for nine — took a different shape, leaving the familiar one
standing empty; and in each case a smaller number moved into it.

Two out of ten shapes will actively mislead you. The other eight will not. That
ratio is worth carrying, because it is the reason to slow down on exactly two
shapes rather than to distrust the whole page.
