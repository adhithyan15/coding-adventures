## You'll want to know: कुचलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C298-GHAYAL298-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुचलना** — *kucalnā* — "to crush; to run over".

> एक ट्रक ने उसकी साइकिल कुचल दी।
>
> *ek ṭrak ne uskī sāikil kucal dī.*
>
> A lorry ran over his bicycle.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने साँप को कुचला, *usne sā̃p ko kuclā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुचलना** (*kucalnā*) is listed by CDIAL under *\*kucyatē*, "is contracted, is bent" (CDIAL 3221), with Hindi *kucalnā*, to trample on. A wheel or a foot that **कुचलता है** leaves the thing flattened.
