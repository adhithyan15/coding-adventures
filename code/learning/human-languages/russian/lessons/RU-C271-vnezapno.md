## You'll want to know: внезапно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C271-VNEZAPNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**внезапно** (*vnezápno*) — "suddenly, unexpectedly".

> Внезапно в комнате стало темно.
>
> *Vnezápno v kómnate stálo temnó.*
>
> Suddenly it went dark in the room.

The adverb of *внезапный* (*vnezápnyy*) "sudden". A little more bookish than *вдруг*, and common in stories.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*внезапно* is the adverb of *внезапный* "sudden", built with the old prefix *вне-*. The origin of the stem *-запн-* is not settled, and EtymDB lists no parent.
