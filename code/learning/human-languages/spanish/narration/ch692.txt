Spanish, chapter 692: Sembrar y regar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
sembrar — to sow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the vineyard, then the Spanish for to grow.

You'll want to know: sembrar.
sembrar — "to sow".
En primavera siembran el maíz. In spring they sow the maize.
The e becomes ie under stress: siembro, siembra.

The word, taken apart.
sembrar is inherited from Latin sēmināre "to sow" (EtymDB), from sēmen "seed".
