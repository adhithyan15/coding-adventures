## You'll want to know: 上午
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C49-DAY49-01]; assesses=[] -->

**上午** — *shàngwǔ* — "morning (before noon)".

> **上午十点我去大学** *shàngwǔ shí diǎn wǒ qù dàxué* — At ten in the morning I go to the university.

The new character, in pieces: 午 — the top of 年: a short fall, two bars and a vertical. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**上午** (*shàngwǔ*) is built from 上 'upper, earlier' and 午 'noon', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangH* and *nguX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary gives *nguX* as the name of one of the branches of the old calendar.
