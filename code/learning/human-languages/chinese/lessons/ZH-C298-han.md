## You'll want to know: hǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C298-FIRE298-04]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎn* — "to shout; to call out".

> *wǒ tīngdào yǒu rén zài hǎn* — I heard someone shouting.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *xamX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'a sound'; the Guangyun also lists it under two other readings, *heamX* and *xeamX*, both glossed as a sound or a cry.
