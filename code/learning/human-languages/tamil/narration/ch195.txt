Tamil, chapter 195: To bring, To give back, To drive, To use, To try.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கொண்டு வா (koṇṭu vā) — to bring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to deep-fry, then the Tamil for to knead.

You'll want to know: கொண்டு வா.
கொண்டு வா — koṇṭu vā — "to bring".

The word, taken apart.
கொண்டு வா (koṇṭu vā) is native: koṇṭu, "having taken", from koḷ, "to take" (DEDR 2151), and vā, "come" (DEDR 5270): "take and come".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ஓட்டு (ōṭṭu) — to drive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to bring, then the Tamil for to give back.

You'll want to know: ஓட்டு.
ஓட்டு — ōṭṭu — "to drive".

The word, taken apart.
ஓட்டு (ōṭṭu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1041). It is the causative of ōṭu, "to run": to drive is "to make run".

Lesson 4 of 5.
பயன்படுத்து (payaṉpaṭuttu) — to use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to give back, then the Tamil for to drive.

You'll want to know: பயன்படுத்து.
பயன்படுத்து — payaṉpaṭuttu — "to use".

The word, taken apart.
பயன்படுத்து (payaṉpaṭuttu) is payaṉ, "use", with paṭuttu, "to make become", from paṭu (DEDR 3853): "put to use". Payaṉ is usually connected with paya, "to yield" (DEDR 3937), but DEDR does not list it.
