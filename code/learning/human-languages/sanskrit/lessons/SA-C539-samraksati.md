## You'll want to know: संरक्षति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C539-AVAHITA539-05]; assesses=[] -->

**संरक्षति** — *saṃrakṣati* — "keeps safe".

> यदि त्वं धनं संरक्षसि, तर्हि तत् न नश्यति।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ dhanaṃ saṃrakṣasi, tarhi tat na naśyati.*
>
> If you keep the money safe, it will not be lost.

Past (he …): सः धनं संरक्षितवान्, *saḥ dhanaṃ saṃrakṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संरक्षति** (*saṃrakṣati*) is built on *sam*, "wholly", and the root √rakṣ, "to protect", which also gives *rakṣati*; MW gives *saṃrakṣ* as to protect, to guard, to keep, to secure (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
