## You'll want to know: بازو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C466-LIMB466-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بازو** — *bāzū* — "an arm".

> بھائی کے بازو بڑے مضبوط ہیں۔
>
> *bhāī ke bāzū baṛe mazbūt haiṅ.*
>
> My brother's arms are very strong.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بازو** (*bāzū*) is usually traced to Persian *bāzū*, "arm".
