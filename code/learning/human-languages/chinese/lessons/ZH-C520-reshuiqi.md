## You'll want to know: rèshuǐqì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C520-APPLIANCE520-03]; assesses=[] -->

*rèshuǐqì* — "water heater".

> *wǒmen de rèshuǐqì yòng le shí nián le* — Our water heater has been going for ten years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rèshuǐqì* is built from 热 'hot', 水 'water' and *qì* 'a vessel', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyet*, *sywijX* and *khijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last 'vessels and dishes'.
