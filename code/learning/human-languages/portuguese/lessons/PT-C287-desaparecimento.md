## You'll want to know: o desaparecimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C287-ROUBO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o desaparecimento** — "the disappearance".

> O desaparecimento do quadro foi notícia em todo o mundo.
>
> *The painting’s disappearance was news all over the world.*

Built on *desaparecer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desaparecimento* is built on *desaparecer* "to disappear", from *des-* and *aparecer*, which goes back to Latin *appārēre* "to come into sight" (Lewis & Short).
