## You'll want to know: ਯਕੀਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C341-DOUBT341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਯਕੀਨ** — *yakīn* — "certainty; belief".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਉਸ ਦੀ ਗੱਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਯਕੀਨ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ us dī gall utte yakīn nahīṁ hai.*
>
> I don't believe what he says.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਯਕੀਨ** (*yakīn*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *yaqīn*, "certainty".
