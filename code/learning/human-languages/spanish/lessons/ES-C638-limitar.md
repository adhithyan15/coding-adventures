## You'll want to know: limitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C638-LIMITE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**limitar** — "to limit".

> Tenemos que limitar los gastos.
>
> *We have to limit our spending.*

*Limitarse a* — to confine yourself to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*limitar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *limitare* 'to bound', built on *limes* 'boundary' (EtymDB).
