## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-COMPARE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C153-BI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-BI-COMPARE-01, ZH-LEX-C153-GENG-01, ZH-LEX-C153-GEN-01, ZH-LEX-C153-NAME-01, ZH-LEX-C78-AGE78-01, ZH-LEX-SUI-01, ZH-LEX-C61-LONG61-03] -->

> **A:** 你多大
>
> *nǐ duō dà* — How old are you?
>
> **B:** 我二十岁
>
> *wǒ èrshí suì* — I'm twenty.
>
> **A:** 他呢
>
> *tā ne* — And him?
>
> **B:** 他比我大两岁
>
> *tā bǐ wǒ dà liǎng suì* — He's two years older than me.
>
> **A:** *nǐ bǐ tā gāo ma* — Are you taller than him?
>
> **B:** *wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme gāo* — I'm not as tall as he is.
>
> **A:** 你妈妈呢
>
> *nǐ māma ne* — And your mum?
>
> **B:** *tā gēn wǒ yíyàng gāo* — She's as tall as me.
>
> **A:** *wǒ bǐ tā dà, tā gèng gāo* — I'm older than him, and he's taller still.

比 puts the difference last: 大两岁. 没有 … 那么 turns it round. *gēn* … *yíyàng* says neither is more.

## What you've built — more, less and the same
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-COMPARE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-BI-COMPARE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-BI-COMPARE-01, ZH-LEX-C153-NAME-01, ZH-LEX-C153-GEN-01] -->

Three ways to set two things side by side:

- **他比我大两岁** *tā bǐ wǒ dà liǎng suì* — he's two years older than me.
- **我没有他那么大** *wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme dà* — I'm not as old as he is.
- *wǒ gēn tā yíyàng gāo* — I'm as tall as he is.

**Listen.** *Nǐ bǐ nǐ māma gāo ma?*

**Speak.** Compare yourself with somebody you know: older or younger, taller or not as tall.
