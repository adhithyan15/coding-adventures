## You'll want to know: el escándalo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C570-PROTESTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el escándalo** — "the scandal; the racket".

> El escándalo salió en todos los periódicos.
>
> *The scandal was in all the newspapers.*

It also means a lot of noise: *¡Qué escándalo!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escándalo* is a learned borrowing from Church Latin *scandalum*, taken from Greek *skándalon* 'a stumbling block' (Lewis & Short).
