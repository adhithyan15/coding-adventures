## You'll want to know: ضاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C456-HAJM456-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ضاق** — *ḍāqa* — "to be too tight; to become narrow".

> ضاق الحذاء على قدمي.
>
> *ḍāqa al-ḥidhāʾ ʿalā qadamī.*
>
> The shoe was too tight on my foot.

Present: يضيق, *yaḍīq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضاق** (*ḍāqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-y-q*, "to be narrow" (Lane). It is the plain verb beside *ḍayyiq*, "narrow".
