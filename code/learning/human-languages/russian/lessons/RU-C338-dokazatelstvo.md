## You'll want to know: доказательство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C338-DOVOD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**доказательство** (*dokazátelstvo*) — "proof, evidence".

> У полиции нет доказательств.
>
> *U polítsii net dokazátelstv.*
>
> The police have no evidence.

Neuter. The plural *доказательства* means pieces of evidence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**доказательство** is built on *доказать* "to prove" with the abstract suffix *-тельство*; the root is *казать* "to show", from Proto-Slavic *\*kazati* (EtymDB).
