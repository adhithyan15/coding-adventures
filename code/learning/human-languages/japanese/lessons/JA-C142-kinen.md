## You'll want to know: きんえん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C142-KINEN]; assesses=[] -->

**きんえん** — *kin'en* — "no smoking". The apostrophe in the romanization
keeps *kin* and *en* apart: four beats, *ki–n–e–n*.
On a real sign this word is usually printed in kanji, which this book has
not reached yet; the reading is the same.

> **きんえん**
>
> **ここで たばこを すわないで ください。** — *koko de tabako o suwanaide
> kudasai* — "Please do not smoke here."

**すう**, *suu*, the verb you know for breathing in, also means to smoke.
Its negative stem **すわ** with **ないで ください** asks you politely not to
do it.
