## You'll want to know: ओळखणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C158-VERB158-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C158-POLAR-01, MW-LEX-C88-WORLD88-02] -->

**ओळखणो** — *oḷakhṇo* — "to recognise".

> **हूं यो गीत ओळखूं हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ yo gīt oḷakhū̃ hū̃.* — I recognise this song.

> **आप यो गीत ओळखो हो?**
>
> *āp yo gīt oḷakho ho?* — Do you recognise this song?

It opens on a letter you have not written yet: the vowel *o*, standing alone. The
next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओळखणो** (*oḷakhṇo*) goes back to Sanskrit *upalakṣayati*, which CDIAL glosses
"beholds". In that entry CDIAL gives Gujarati *oḷakhvũ*, "to know, distinguish",
and no Marwari form (CDIAL 2226).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C158-VERB158-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01] -->

Its stem **ओळख** ends in a consonant: **ओळखूं हूं**, **ओळखै है**, **ओळखो हो**.
