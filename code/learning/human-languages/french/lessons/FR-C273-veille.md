## You'll want to know: la veille
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C273-VEILLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la veille** — "the day before, the eve".

> La veille de son examen, elle n'a pas dormi.
>
> *The day before her exam, she did not sleep.*

*La veille au soir* — the evening before. The verb *veiller* means to stay up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*veille* is inherited from Latin *vigilia* "wakefulness, a night watch" (EtymDB), built on *vigil* "awake": the night of waiting before a feast.
