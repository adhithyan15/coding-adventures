Lesson 1 of 6.
नवल (naval) — wonder; a marvel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for zest, then the Marathi for to hold out.

You'll want to know: नवल.
नवल — naval — "wonder; a marvel".
पहिल्यांदा समुद्र पाहून मुलांना नवल वाटलं. pahilyāndā samudra pāhūn mulānnā naval vāṭlaṁ. Seeing the sea for the first time, the children marvelled.
Neuter.
नवल (naval) वाटणे, to marvel, with the dative; नवलाची गोष्ट, a wonderful thing.

The word, taken apart.
नवल (naval) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi naval, "new", under navala, "new" (CDIAL 7012); a new thing is a wonder.

You'll want to know: भारावणे.
भारावणे — bhārāvṇe — "to be overwhelmed".
सगळ्यांचं प्रेम पाहून ती भारावली. sagḷyāñcaṁ prem pāhūn tī bhārāvlī. She was overwhelmed by everyone's love.
Past (he …): तो भारावला, to bhārāvlā.
Built on भार, a load: weighed down with feeling, usually glad. भारावून जाणे.

The word, taken apart.
भारावणे (bhārāvṇe) is a Marathi verb built on भार, "a load", from Sanskrit bhāra (CDIAL 9459).

Lesson 5 of 6.
थक्क होणे (thakka hoṇe) — to be amazed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be overwhelmed, then the Marathi for to be baffled.

You'll want to know: थक्क होणे.
थक्क होणे — thakka hoṇe — "to be amazed".
त्याचं चित्र पाहून आम्ही थक्क झालो. tyācaṁ citra pāhūn āmhī thakka jhālo. We were amazed to see his painting.
Past (he …): तो थक्क झाला, to thakka jhālā.
थक्क, struck still, + होणे; stronger than नवल (naval) वाटणे.

The word, taken apart.
थक्क होणे (thakka hoṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on थक्क, "struck still", whose origin is not settled, with होणे, "to become".
