Portuguese, chapter 371: Os imprevistos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
prever — to foresee, to predict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for possible, then the Portuguese for possibly.

You'll want to know: prever.
prever — "to foresee, to predict".
Ninguém previu tanta chuva. Nobody predicted so much rain.
Conjugated like ver: prevejo, prevês, prevê. Estar previsto — to be scheduled.

The word, taken apart.
prever is built from pre- "before" and ver "to see", modelled on Latin praevidēre "to foresee" (Lewis & Short), on vidēre "to see".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: previsível.
previsível — "predictable".
O final do filme era muito previsível. The end of the film was very predictable.
Built on prever + -ível.

The word, taken apart.
previsível is built on previsto, the participle of prever "to foresee", with -ível; the verb rests on Latin vidēre "to see" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
a alternativa — the alternative, the other option.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: a alternativa.
a alternativa — "the alternative, the other option".
Não temos alternativa: vamos de táxi. We have no alternative: we are going by taxi.
Em alternativa — alternatively.

The word, taken apart.
alternativa is built on alternativo (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin alternātīvus, on alternus "one after the other", from alter "the other" (Lewis & Short).
