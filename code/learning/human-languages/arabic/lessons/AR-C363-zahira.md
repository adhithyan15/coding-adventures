## You'll want to know: ظاهرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C363-NAZARIYYA363-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ظاهرة** — *ẓāhira* — "a phenomenon".

> المطر في الصيف ظاهرة نادرة هنا.
>
> *al-maṭar fī aṣ-ṣayf ẓāhira nādira hunā.*
>
> Rain in summer is a rare phenomenon here.

Plural: ظواهر, *ẓawāhir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ظاهرة** (*ẓāhira*) is built on the native Arabic root *ẓ-h-r*, "to appear, to come out" (Lane). *Ẓāhira* is the feminine active participle, what appears: a phenomenon.
