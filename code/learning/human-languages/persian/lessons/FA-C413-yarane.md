## You'll want to know: یارانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C413-EQTESAD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**یارانه** (*yârâne*) — "a subsidy".

> دولت به خانواده ها یارانه میدهد.
>
> *dowlat be khânevâde hâ yârâne midahad.*
>
> The government gives families a subsidy.

A modern coinage built on *yâr* "helper".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**یارانه** is a modern Persian coinage, usually explained as built on *yâr* "friend, helper" with the suffix *-âne*: money that helps.
