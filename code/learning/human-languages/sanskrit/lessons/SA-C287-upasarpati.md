## You'll want to know: उपसर्पति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C287-ANUDHAVANA287-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उपसर्पति** — *upasarpati* — "creeps up to".

> चौरः शनैः गृहम् उपसर्पति।
>
> *cauraḥ śanaiḥ gṛham upasarpati.*
>
> The thief creeps slowly up to the house.

Past (he …): सः गृहम् उपसृप्तवान्, *saḥ gṛham upasṛptavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपसर्पति** (*upasarpati*) is built from *upa*, "towards", and the root √sṛp, "to creep"; MW gives *upa-sṛp* as to creep towards or approach softly (MW).
