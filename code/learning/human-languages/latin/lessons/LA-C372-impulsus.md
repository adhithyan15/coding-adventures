## You'll want to know: impulsus, impulsūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C372-AUCTOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**impulsus, impulsūs** — "a push; an impulse; instigation".

> Impulsū amīcōrum hoc fēcit.
>
> *He did this at his friends' urging.*

*impulsus, impulsūs* m., built on *impellō*, to push on (*in* + *pellō*). *Impulsū alicuius*, at someone's instigation. English *impulse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impulsus* is the noun built on *impellō* "to push on" (Lewis & Short), from *in* and *pellō* "to drive". English *impulse* comes from it.
