# ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳು (otteyāḷu) — a hostage

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a trace, then the Kannada for to kidnap.

## You'll want to know: ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C326-KANE326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳು** — *otteyāḷu* — "a hostage".

> ಕಳ್ಳರು ಅಂಗಡಿಯವನನ್ನು ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳಾಗಿ ಇಟ್ಟುಕೊಂಡರು.
>
> *kaḷḷaru aṅgaḍiyavanannu otteyāḷāgi iṭṭukoṇḍaru.*
>
> The robbers kept the shopkeeper as a hostage.

Plural: ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳುಗಳು, *otteyāḷugaḷu*.

ಒತ್ತೆ, a pledge, and ಆಳು, a person: a person held as a pledge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒತ್ತೆಯಾಳು** (*otteyāḷu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *otte*, "a pledge, a pawn" (DEDR 1020), and *āḷ*, "a person, a servant" (DEDR 399).
