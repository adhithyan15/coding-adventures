## You'll want to know: ಸೆಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C93-WEATHER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೆಕೆ** (*seke*) — "sultry, sticky heat", the heat that makes you **ಬೆವರು**, "sweat".

**ತುಂಬಾ ಸೆಕೆ** (*tumbā seke*) — "it's very hot and sticky".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Heat you can feel on the skin.
