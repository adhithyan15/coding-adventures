## You'll want to know: prevedibile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C374-IMPREVISTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**prevedibile** — "predictable, foreseeable".

> Il finale del film era prevedibile.
>
> *The ending of the film was predictable.*

Built on *prevedere* "to foresee".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prevedibile* is built on *prevedere* "to foresee", from Latin *praevidēre* "to see beforehand" (Lewis & Short), on *vidēre* "to see".
