Hindi, chapter 425: Commotion and Drains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हल्ला (hallā) — an uproar, a commotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a hawker, then the Hindi for a betel leaf.

You'll want to know: हल्ला.
हल्ला — hallā — "an uproar, a commotion".
गली में अचानक हल्ला हुआ। galī meṁ acānak hallā huā. Suddenly there was an uproar in the lane.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
हल्ला (hallā) goes back to halla, "movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 14017), which lists Hindi hallā, "tumult, noise".

You'll want to know: खदेड़ना.
खदेड़ना — khadeṛnā — "to chase away".
लोगों ने बंदरों को गली से खदेड़ा। logoṁ ne bandroṁ ko galī se khadeṛā. People chased the monkeys out of the lane.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बकरी को खेत से खदेड़ा, usne bakrī ko khet se khadeṛā.

Lesson 3 of 5.
गटर (gaṭar) — a sewer; a covered drain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an uproar, then the Hindi for to chase away.

You'll want to know: गटर.
गटर — gaṭar — "a sewer; a covered drain".
बारिश में गटर भर गया। bāriś meṁ gaṭar bhar gayā. The sewer filled up in the rain.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
गटर (gaṭar) is borrowed from English gutter; in Indian cities it names the covered sewer under the street.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बदबू (badbū) — a bad smell, a stink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to chase away, then the Hindi for a sewer.

You'll want to know: बदबू.
बदबू — badbū — "a bad smell, a stink".
गटर से बहुत बदबू आ रही थी। gaṭar se bahut badbū ā rahī thī. There was a terrible smell coming from the sewer.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
बदबू (badbū) is usually traced to Persian badbū, from bad, "bad", and bū, "smell": the opposite of ख़ुशबू.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a sewer, then the Hindi for a stink.

You'll want to know: रिसना.
रिसना — risnā — "to seep; to leak".
छत से पानी रिस रहा था। chat se pānī ris rahā thā. Water was seeping through the roof.
Past (it …): वह तेल डिब्बे से रिसा, vah tel ḍibbe se risā.

The word, taken apart.
रिसना (risnā) is filed by CDIAL under riśyate, "is torn off", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10745), with Hindi risnā, "to leak out, drip".
