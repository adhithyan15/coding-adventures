## You'll want to know: ভিড়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C277-SAKSHI277-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ভিড়** — *bhiṛ* — "a crowd".

> দুর্ঘটনার পরে রাস্তায় বড় ভিড় হল।
>
> *durghôṭônār pôre rāstāy bôṛo bhiṛ hôlô.*
>
> A big crowd gathered on the road after the accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভিড়** (*bhiṛ*) is filed in CDIAL 9490 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *bhiṭ-*, 'meet, throng', which lists Bengali *bhiṛ*, 'a crowd'. ভিড় হওয়া, a crowd gathers.
