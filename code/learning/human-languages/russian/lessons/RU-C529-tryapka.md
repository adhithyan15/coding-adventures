## You'll want to know: тряпка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C529-CLEANING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**тряпка** (*tryápka*) — "a cloth, a rag".

> Мама вытерла кухонный стол мокрой тряпкой.
>
> *Máma výterla kúkhonnyy stol mókroy tryápkoy.*
>
> Mum wiped the kitchen table with a damp cloth.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *тряпка* without a source; it is usually linked to *тряпьё* 'rags', and the further origin is not settled.
