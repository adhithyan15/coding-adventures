## You'll want to know: 星期
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C46-WEEK46-01]; assesses=[] -->

**星期** — *xīngqī* — "week".

> **一个星期有七天** *yí ge xīngqī yǒu qī tiān* — There are seven days in a week.

The new character, in pieces: 星 — 日 above 生; 期 — an eight-stroke piece on the left and a moon piece on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**星期** (*xīngqī*) is built from 星 'star' and 期 'appointed time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *seng* and *gi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). How the pair came to mean a week is not settled.
