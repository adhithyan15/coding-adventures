## You'll want to know: पिळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C416-HAMAL416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पिळणे** — *piḷṇe* — "to wring".

> तिने ओला सदरा पिळला.
>
> *tine olā sadrā piḷlā.*
>
> She wrung out the wet shirt.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पिळलं, *tyāne piḷlaṁ*.

Also for fruit: लिंबू पिळणे, to squeeze a lemon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पिळणे** (*piḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *piḷṇẽ*, "to squeeze", under Sanskrit *pīḍayati*, "presses, squeezes" (CDIAL 8226).
