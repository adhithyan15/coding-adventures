## You'll want to know: suǒyǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C138-EVERY138-04]; assesses=[] -->

*suǒyǒu* — "all; every".

> *suǒyǒu de rén dōu lái le* — Everyone has come.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones: suǒyǒu is said suóyǒu, the first rising.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suǒyǒu* is built from *suǒ* 'a place', the *suǒ* of *suǒyǐ*, and 有 'to have', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjoX* and *hjuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). How the pair came to mean all is not settled.
