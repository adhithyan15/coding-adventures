# ದೊಡ್ಡಪ್ಪ (doḍḍappa) — father's elder brother

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C523-SAMSARA523-05, KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for married life, then the Kannada for father's younger brother.

## You'll want to know: ದೊಡ್ಡಪ್ಪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೊಡ್ಡಪ್ಪ** — *doḍḍappa* — "father's elder brother".

> ದೊಡ್ಡಪ್ಪ ನಮ್ಮ ಕುಟುಂಬದ ಹಿರಿಯರು.
>
> *doḍḍappa namma kuṭumbada hiriyaru.*
>
> My father's elder brother is an elder of our family.

ದೊಡ್ಡ, big + ಅಪ್ಪ, father: a "big father".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೊಡ್ಡಪ್ಪ** (*doḍḍappa*) is a native Dravidian compound: *doḍḍa*, "big, great" (DEDR 3491), and *appa*, "father" (DEDR 156).
