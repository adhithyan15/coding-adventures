Bengali, chapter 325: A Story's Lesson.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উপদেশ (upôdesh) — a moral lesson; counsel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to coax, then the Bengali for to get away with it.

You'll want to know: উপদেশ.
উপদেশ — upôdesh — "a moral lesson; counsel".
গল্পের শেষে দাদু একটা উপদেশ দিলেন। gôlper sheshe dādu ekṭā upôdesh dilen. At the end of the story my grandfather gave us a lesson to remember.

The word, taken apart.
উপদেশ (upôdesh) is Sanskrit upadeśa, 'pointing out; instruction, advice' (Monier-Williams); it has nothing to do with দেশ, a country. Everyday advice is পরামর্শ.

লোভের পরিণাম ভালো হয় না। lobher pôrinām bhālo hôy nā. Greed does not end well.

পরিণাম (pôrinām) is Sanskrit pariṇāma, 'change, development, ripening' (Monier-Williams): what a thing turns into in the end.

Lesson 4 of 5.
শোধরানো (shodhrāno) — to mend one's ways; to put right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an outcome, then the Bengali for a sin.

You'll want to know: শোধরানো.
শোধরানো — shodhrāno — "to mend one's ways; to put right".
শাস্তির পরে লোভী রাজা শোধরালেন। shāstir pôre lobhī rājā shodhrālen. After his punishment the greedy king mended his ways.
Past (he/she …): সেই ছেলে শোধরাল, sei chhele shodhrālô.

The word, taken apart.
শোধরানো (shodhrāno) is usually explained as built on শোধ, 'a clearing, a setting right' — Sanskrit śodha, 'purification, correction' (Monier-Williams) — as a verb: to put oneself right.

Lesson 5 of 5.
বাধ্য হওয়া (bādhyô hôoyā) — to be forced; to have no choice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a sin, then the Bengali for to mend one's ways.

You'll want to know: বাধ্য হওয়া.
বাধ্য হওয়া — bādhyô hôoyā — "to be forced; to have no choice".
ঝড়ের জন্য জেলেরা ফিরে আসতে বাধ্য হল। jhôṛer jônyô jelerā phire āste bādhyô hôlô. Because of the storm the fishermen were forced to come back.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বাড়ি বেচতে বাধ্য হলেন, tini bāṛi bechte bādhyô hôlen.

The word, taken apart.
বাধ্য হওয়া (bādhyô hôoyā) puts Sanskrit bādhya, 'to be pressed hard, held back' (Monier-Williams), before হওয়া: to be pressed into something. It takes the -তে form: যেতে বাধ্য হল, had to go.
