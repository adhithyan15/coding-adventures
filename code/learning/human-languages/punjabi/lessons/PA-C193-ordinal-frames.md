## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-PAHILE-ORDINAL-193, PA-FORM-DUJE-ORDINAL-193, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-01, PA-PLACE-KAMRE-ROOM-01, PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *pahile kamre vich*; YOU SAY: **ਪਹਿਲੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ**]
- [YOU HEAR: *duuje kamre vich*; YOU SAY: **ਦੂਜੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ**]

- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write one phrase, then the other]

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-PAHILE-ORDINAL-193, PA-FORM-DUJE-ORDINAL-193] -->

The choice changes the ordinal meaning. The familiar **ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ** frame and
the final *-e* stay stable.
