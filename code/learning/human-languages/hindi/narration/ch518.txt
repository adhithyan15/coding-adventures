Hindi, chapter 518: Wires and Switches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तार (tār) — a wire; a string.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to smear (a floor or wall with mud), then the Hindi for to lay (bricks).

You'll want to know: तार.
तार — tār — "a wire; a string".
बिजली का तार बहुत पुराना है। Bijlī kā tār bahut purānā hai. The electric wire is very old.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
तार (tār) is usually traced to Persian tār, "a thread, a string".

What you've built.

दरवाज़े के पीछे एक खूँटी है। Darvāze ke pīche ek khū̃ṭī hai. There is a peg behind the door.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
खूँटी (khū̃ṭī) is a tadbhava, inherited from khuṭṭa, "a peg, a post", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3893).

Lesson 5 of 5.
टाँगना (ṭā̃gnā) — to hang up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a switch, then the Hindi for a peg.

You'll want to know: टाँगना.
टाँगना — ṭā̃gnā — "to hang up".
मीरा ने अपना छाता खूँटी पर टाँगा। Mīrā ne apnā chātā khū̃ṭī par ṭā̃gā. Mira hung her umbrella on the peg.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कोट टाँगा, usne koṭ ṭā̃gā.

The word, taken apart.
टाँगना (ṭā̃gnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from ṭaṅg, "hang", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5436).
