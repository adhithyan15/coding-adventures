## You'll want to know: রিসেপশন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C403-GHOR403-04]; assesses=[] -->

**রিসেপশন** — *risepshôn* — "a reception desk".

> রিসেপশনে আমরা ঘরের চাবি নিলাম।
>
> *risepshône āmrā ghôrer chābi nilām.*
>
> We collected the room key at reception.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রিসেপশন** (*risepshôn*) is borrowed from English *reception*, the desk where guests arrive.
