# ಅಭಿಮಾನ (abhimāna) — pride; fond regard

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C496-ASHE496-05, KA-LEX-C497-ABHIMANA497-01] -->

## You'll want to know: ಅಭಿಮಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C497-ABHIMANA497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಭಿಮಾನ** — *abhimāna* — "pride; fond regard".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಭಾಷೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ನಮಗೆ ಅಭಿಮಾನ ಇದೆ.
>
> *namma bhāṣeya bagge namage abhimāna ide.*
>
> We are proud of our language.

Warm pride in what you love; ಅಭಿಮಾನಿ is a fan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಭಿಮಾನ** (*abhimāna*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *abhimāna*, "pride" (CDIAL 524), from the root *man*, "to think".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
