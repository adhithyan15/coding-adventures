Spanish, chapter 742: Comer bien.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la dieta — the diet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for insomnia, then the Spanish for the nap.

You'll want to know: la dieta.
la dieta — "the diet".
Estoy a dieta. I am on a diet.
Estar a dieta — to be on a diet.

The word, taken apart.
dieta comes from Latin diaeta, which was borrowed from Greek díaita "way of life" (EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
sobrepeso is built on sobre "over", from Latin super, and peso "weight", from Latin pēnsum "something weighed".

Lesson 5 of 5.
masticar — to chew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for appetite, then the Spanish for exercise.

You'll want to know: masticar.
masticar — "to chew".
Hay que masticar bien la comida. You have to chew your food well.
Spelling: mastiqué. Mascar is the older, everyday twin.

The word, taken apart.
masticar is a learned borrowing from Late Latin masticāre "to chew" (EtymDB); the inherited form of the same word is mascar.
