## You'll want to know: مسافت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C402-MUSAFAT402-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مسافت** — *masāfat* — "a distance travelled; a long way".

> پہاڑوں تک کی مسافت لمبی ہے۔
>
> *pahāṛoṅ tak kī masāfat lambī hai.*
>
> It is a long way to the mountains.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسافت** (*masāfat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *masāfa*, "a distance"; it is a cousin of **مسافر** and **سفر**.
