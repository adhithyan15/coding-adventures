## You'll want to know: しつもん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C226-EXAM226-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しつもん** — *shitsumon* — "a question".

> **がくせいは せんせいに しつもんを します。** — *gakusei wa sensei ni shitsumon o shimasu* — "The students ask the teacher questions."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しつもん** (*shitsumon*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shitsu*, "substance" (Middle Chinese *tsyit*) and *mon*, "ask" (*mjunH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
