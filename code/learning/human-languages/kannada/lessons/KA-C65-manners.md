## You'll want to know: ಸೌಜನ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C65-MANNERS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೌಜನ್ಯ** (*saujanya*) — "courtesy, good manners".

ಸೌಜನ್ಯ is Sanskrit, and underneath it is *a good person* — courtesy named as a quality of the one behaving rather than as a list of rules being followed. That is a real difference, and Kannada means it.

It is the word for everything this book has been quietly teaching alongside the vocabulary: ಧನ್ಯವಾದ, ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು, ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ, ಗೌರವ, ವಂದನೆ, and now the four words in front of this one. ಸೌಜನ್ಯ is the name of the whole habit.
