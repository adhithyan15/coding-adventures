## You'll want to know: निष्कर्ष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C362-NISHKARSH362-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कर्ष** — *niṣkarṣ* — "a conclusion".

> बहस के बाद हम एक निष्कर्ष पर पहुँचे।
>
> *bahas ke bād ham ek niṣkarṣ par pahuñce.*
>
> After the debate we reached a conclusion.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कर्ष** (*niṣkarṣ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *niṣkarṣa*, "drawing out, extracting" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for the conclusion drawn out of an argument.
