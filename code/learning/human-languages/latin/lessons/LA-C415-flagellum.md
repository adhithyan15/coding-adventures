## You'll want to know: flagellum, flagellī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C415-CASTIGO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**flagellum, flagellī** — "a whip, a scourge".

> Aurīga flagellum in manū tenet.
>
> *The charioteer holds a whip in his hand.*

*flagellum, flagellī* n., a small form of *flagrum*, a whip. English *flagellate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flagellum* is a small form of *flagrum* "a whip, a scourge" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *flagellate* comes from it.
