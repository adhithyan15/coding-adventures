Gujarati, chapter 140: To cut, To sew, To fill, To pull, To throw.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કાપવું (kāpvũ) — to cut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to use, then the Gujarati for to make.

You'll want to know: કાપવું.
કાપવું (kāpvũ) — "to cut".

The word, taken apart.
કાપવું (kāpvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kalpayati, "sets in order; trims, cuts" (CDIAL 2944).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ખેંચવું.
ખેંચવું (khẽcvũ) — "to pull".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists Gujarati khīṁcvũ, "to pull", under a reconstructed khiñc, "drag, pull", and the noun khẽc, "pulling", under its variant khaiñc (CDIAL 3881). ખેંચવું (khẽcvũ) belongs with them.
