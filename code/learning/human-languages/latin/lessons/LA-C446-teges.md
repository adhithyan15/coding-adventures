## You'll want to know: teges, tegetis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C446-LECTULUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**teges, tegetis** — "a mat, a rush mat; a covering".

> In caupōnā parvā tantum teges in pavīmentō est.
>
> *In a small inn there is only a mat on the floor.*

Third declension, feminine: *teges, tegetis f.* A coarse mat of rushes, the poor man's bed in Roman satire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*teges* is built on *tegō* 'to cover' (Lewis & Short): a covering, a mat.
