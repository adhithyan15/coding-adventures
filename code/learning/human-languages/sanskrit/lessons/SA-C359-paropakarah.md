## You'll want to know: परोपकारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C359-TYAGA359-02]; assesses=[] -->

**परोपकारः** — *paropakāraḥ* — "helping others".

> परोपकारः पुण्यं भवति।
>
> *paropakāraḥ puṇyaṃ bhavati.*
>
> Helping others earns merit.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परोपकारः** (*paropakāraḥ*) is built from *para*, "another", and *upakāra*, "a favour, help"; MW gives *paropakāra* "assisting others, benevolence, charity" (MW).
