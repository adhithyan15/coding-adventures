## You'll want to know: きけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C223-SAFETY223-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きけん** — *kiken* — "danger".

> **いのちに きけんは ありません。** — *inochi ni kiken wa arimasen* — "There is no danger to life."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きけん** (*kiken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "danger" (Middle Chinese *ngjwe*) and *ken*, "steep, risky" (*xjemX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
