## You'll want to know: التواء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C516-HARAKA516-05]; assesses=[] -->

**التواء** — *iltiwāʾ* — "a sprain".

> عنده التواء في الكاحل.
>
> *ʿindahu iltiwāʾ fī al-kāḥil.*
>
> He has a sprained ankle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**التواء** (*iltiwāʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-w-y*, "to twist, to bend" (Lane). Iltiwāʾ is the verbal noun of Form VIII: a twisting.
