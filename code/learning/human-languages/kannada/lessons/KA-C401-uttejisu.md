# ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸು (uttējisu) — to promote; to boost

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for economic, then the Kannada for finance.

## You'll want to know: ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C401-ABHIVRIDDHI401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸು** — *uttējisu* — "to promote; to boost".

> ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಸಣ್ಣ ಉದ್ಯಮಗಳನ್ನು ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸಿತು.
>
> *sarkāra saṇṇa udyamagaḷannu uttējisitu.*
>
> The government boosted small businesses.

Past (I …): ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸಿದೆ, *uttējiside*.

ಉತ್ತೇಜನ, encouragement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉತ್ತೇಜಿಸು** (*uttējisu*) is built on Sanskrit *uttejana*, "incitement, encouragement, stimulation" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
