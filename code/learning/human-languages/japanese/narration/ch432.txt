Lesson 1 of 5.
なやむ (nayamu) — to be troubled; to agonize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to surprise someone, then the Japanese for to be on edge.

You'll want to know: なやむ.
なやむ — nayamu — "to be troubled; to agonize".
あには しごとで なやんでいます。 — ani wa shigoto de nayande imasu — "My elder brother is troubled about his work."
Polite なやみます, nayamimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): ともだちが なやみました。, tomodachi ga nayamimashita.

The word, taken apart.
なやむ (nayamu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

Lesson 2 of 5.
くよくよする (kuyokuyo suru) — to brood; to fret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be on edge, then the Japanese for to be troubled.

You'll want to know: くよくよする.
くよくよする — kuyokuyo suru — "to brood; to fret".
ちいさい ことで くよくよしないで ください。 — chiisai koto de kuyokuyo shinaide kudasai — "Please do not brood over small things."
Polite くよくよします, kuyokuyoshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): かれが くよくよしました。, kare ga kuyokuyo shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
くよくよする (kuyokuyo suru) is a native Japanese mimetic word, kuyokuyo, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, made a verb with suru, "to do"; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
こうかいする (kōkai suru) — to regret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be troubled, then the Japanese for to brood.

You'll want to know: こうかいする.
こうかいする — kōkai suru — "to regret".
あの ときの ことを こうかいしています。 — ano toki no koto o kōkai shite imasu — "I regret what happened that time."
Polite こうかいします, kōkaishimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): かれは こうかいしました。, kare wa kōkai shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
こうかいする (kōkai suru) is Sino-Japanese (kango) kōkai with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kō, "later" (Middle Chinese huwX) and kai, "regret" (xwojH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
