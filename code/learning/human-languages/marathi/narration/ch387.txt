Lesson 1 of 5.
वगळणे (vagaḷṇe) — to leave out; to omit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to take notice, then the Marathi for a loophole.

You'll want to know: वगळणे.
वगळणे — vagaḷṇe — "to leave out; to omit".
अहवालातून त्याने महत्त्वाचा भाग वगळला. ahvālātūn tyāne mahattvācā bhāg vagaḷlā. He left an important part out of the report.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वगळलं, tyāne vagaḷlaṁ.
The source takes -तून; वगळता, except.

The word, taken apart.
वगळणे (vagaḷṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi vagaḷṇẽ, "to drop disregardfully, cast out, omit", under Sanskrit avagalati, "falls down, slips down" (CDIAL 742).

Lesson 2 of 5.
समाविष्ट करणे (samāviṣṭa karṇe) — to include.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a loophole, then the Marathi for to leave out.

You'll want to know: समाविष्ट करणे.
समाविष्ट करणे — samāviṣṭa karṇe — "to include".
नव्या अभ्यासक्रमात पर्यावरणाचा विषय समाविष्ट केला आहे. navyā abhyāsakramāt paryāvarṇācā viṣay samāviṣṭa kelā āhe. The environment has been included in the new syllabus.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने समाविष्ट केलं, tyāne samāviṣṭa kelaṁ.
Where it goes takes -त: अभ्यासक्रमात समाविष्ट.

The word, taken apart.
समाविष्ट करणे (samāviṣṭa karṇe) joins the tatsama समाविष्ट, Sanskrit samāviṣṭa, "entered, filled" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to make".

Lesson 3 of 5.
विसंबणे (visambṇe) — to rely on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to leave out, then the Marathi for to include.

You'll want to know: विसंबणे.
विसंबणे — visambṇe — "to rely on".
मी फक्त स्वतःवर विसंबतो. mī phakta svataḥvar visambto. I rely only on myself.
Past (he …): तो विसंबला, to visamblā.
The one relied on takes -वर; विसंबून राहणे, to depend.

The word, taken apart.
विसंबणे (visambṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi visãbṇẽ, "to pause, take rest", under Sanskrit viśrāmyati, "takes rest" (CDIAL 11959); today it means to rest one's weight on someone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ठरणे (ṭharṇe) — to turn out; to be settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to include, then the Marathi for to rely on.

You'll want to know: ठरणे.
ठरणे — ṭharṇe — "to turn out; to be settled".
त्याचा निर्णय योग्य ठरला. tyācā nirṇay yogya ṭharlā. His decision turned out right.
Past (he …): तो ठरला, to ṭharlā.
Also to be fixed: लग्न ठरलं; ठरवणे, to decide, is its causative.

The word, taken apart.
ठरणे (ṭharṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ṭharṇẽ, "to be settled", under stharati, "is firm" (CDIAL 13742).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पटणे (paṭṇe) — to be convinced; to make sense (to).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rely on, then the Marathi for to turn out.

You'll want to know: पटणे.
पटणे — paṭṇe — "to be convinced; to make sense (to)".
तुमचं म्हणणं मला पटलं. tumcaṁ mhaṇṇaṁ malā paṭlaṁ. What you said convinced me.
Past (to him …): त्याला पटलं, tyālā paṭlaṁ.
Dative: मला पटलं, I am convinced; पटवणे, to convince, is its causative.

The word, taken apart.
पटणे (paṭṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled; पटवणे, to convince, is built on it.
