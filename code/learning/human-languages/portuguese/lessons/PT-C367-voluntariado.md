## You'll want to know: o voluntariado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C367-COMUNIDADE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o voluntariado** — "the voluntary work, volunteering".

> Faço voluntariado num hospital aos domingos.
>
> *I do voluntary work in a hospital on Sundays.*

*Fazer voluntariado* — to volunteer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*voluntariado* is built on *voluntário* "volunteer", from Latin *voluntārius* "willing, voluntary" (Lewis & Short), on *velle* "to want".
