## You'll want to know: بيان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C293-AKHBAR293-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بيان** — *bayān* — "a statement; a clear account".

> قرأ المدير بيانا قصيرا.
>
> *qaraʾa al-mudīr bayānan qaṣīran.*
>
> The manager read out a short statement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بيان** (*bayān*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-y-n* (Lane), "to be apart; to become clear", the root of *bayna*, "between". A *bayān* makes a thing clear.
