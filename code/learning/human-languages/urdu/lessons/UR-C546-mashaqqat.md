## You'll want to know: مشقت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C546-MASHAQQAT546-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مشقت** — *mashaqqat* — "toil; hard labour".

> اگر مشقت نہ ہوتی تو یہ پل کبھی نہ بنتا۔
>
> *agar mashaqqat na hotī to yih pul kabhī na bantā.*
>
> Without hard labour this bridge would not have been built at all.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشقت** (*mashaqqat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mashaqqa*, "hardship, toil".
