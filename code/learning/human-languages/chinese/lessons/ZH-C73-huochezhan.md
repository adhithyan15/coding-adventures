## You'll want to know: huǒchēzhàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-03]; assesses=[] -->

*huǒchēzhàn* — "railway station".

> *huǒchēzhàn hěn yuǎn* — The station is a long way off.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huǒchēzhàn* is built from 火车 'train' and zhàn 'a stop', inherited from Middle Chinese *treamH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary glosses as the everyday word for standing alone. The sense 'station' is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
