# The old fictional date B

Stop before reading or writing this date card. B is the second example in
the same closed fictional bank. This is not a real personal form or a scored
A1 writing task.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-B-01] -->

Look at **੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫**. B has day **੨੫** and February month position **੦੨**;
the familiar year **੨੦੨੫** stays the same as A. The two slashes still divide
day, month, and year.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-B-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

With B visible, copy **੨੫**, then **੦੨**, then **੨੦੨੫**, placing one slash
between each pair of boxes. Compare your line with **੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫**. If a
box differs, repair just that box before moving on.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-B-01] -->

Hide B for a few seconds. From the old cue “fictional date B,” try its day
and month boxes once, then uncover the card and check. The visible model
remains available for repair; no independent mock score is assigned.
