Persian, chapter 421: Horn, lights and mirrors.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بوق (buq) — a horn (of a car).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to change gear, then the Persian for a clutch.

You'll want to know: بوق.
بوق (buq) — "a horn (of a car)".
بوق (buq) این ماشین خیلی بلند است. Buq-e in mâshin kheyli boland ast. This car's horn is very loud.
Also an old trumpet made of horn or brass.

The word, taken apart.
buq was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic būq 'a trumpet'. EtymDB does not list the word, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بوق زدن (buq zadan) — to honk, to sound the horn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a clutch, then the Persian for a horn.

You'll want to know: بوق زدن.
بوق زدن (buq zadan) — "to honk, to sound the horn".
راننده تاکسی جلوی خانه ما دو بار بوق زد. Rânande-ye tâksi jolow-ye khâne-ye mâ do bâr buq zad. The taxi driver honked twice in front of our house.
buq + zadan (to strike). Present stem zan-: buq mizanad — he honks.

The word, taken apart.
buq zadan is built from buq 'horn', a loan usually traced to Arabic būq 'a trumpet', and the native verb zadan 'to strike'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
چراغ (cherâgh) — a light, a lamp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a horn, then the Persian for to honk.

You'll want to know: چراغ.
چراغ (cherâgh) — "a light, a lamp".
شب چراغهای ماشین را روشن میکنیم. Shab cherâgh-hâ-ye mâshin râ roshan mikonim. At night we switch on the car's lights.
Cherâgh-e qermez — a red light; cherâgh-e râhnamâ — an indicator.

The word, taken apart.
cherâgh is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian čirāgh 'lamp' and also links it with a Parthian form of the same word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
برف پاک کن (barfpâkkon) — a windscreen wiper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to honk, then the Persian for a light.

You'll want to know: برف پاک کن.
برف پاک کن (barfpâkkon) — "a windscreen wiper".
در باران برف پاک کن تند کار میکند. Dar bârân barfpâkkon tond kâr mikonad. In the rain the wiper works fast.
Literally 'snow-cleaner': barf 'snow' + pâk 'clean' + kon 'doing'.

The word, taken apart.
barfpâkkon is a native compound of barf 'snow', inherited from Middle Persian wafr (EtymDB), pâk 'clean', from Middle Persian pāk (EtymDB), and kon, the present stem of kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
آینه بغل (âyene-baghal) — a side mirror (of a car).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a light, then the Persian for a windscreen wiper.

You'll want to know: آینه بغل.
آینه بغل (âyene-baghal) — "a side mirror (of a car)".
آینه بغل (âyene-baghal) ماشین من شکست. Âyene-baghal-e mâshin-e man shekast. The side mirror of my car broke.
âyene 'mirror' + baghal 'side'. The mirror inside the car is âyene-ye vasat.

The word, taken apart.
âyene-baghal is built from native âyene 'mirror' and baghal 'side, armpit', whose origin is not settled. EtymDB lists neither âyene with older forms nor the Persian baghal, so both parts are hedged.
