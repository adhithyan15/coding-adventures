## You'll want to know: കുടൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C537-SARIRAM537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**കുടൽ** — *kuṭal* — "the intestines; the gut".

> പഴകിയ ഭക്ഷണം കുടലിന് ദോഷം ചെയ്യും.
>
> *paḻakiya bhakṣaṇaṁ kuṭalinŭ dōṣaṁ ceyyuṁ.*
>
> Stale food harms the gut.

വൻകുടൽ is the large intestine, ചെറുകുടൽ the small one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുടൽ** (*kuṭal*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1652 gives the Malayalam word as the bowels (DEDR 1652).
