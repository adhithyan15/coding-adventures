## You'll want to know: தடவு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C486-VAITTIYAM486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**தடவு** — *taṭavu* — "to rub on; to apply".

> காயத்தில் அம்மா களிம்பு தடவினாள்.
>
> *kāyattil ammā kaḷimpu taṭaviṉāḷ.*
>
> Mother put ointment on the wound.

Past (I …): தடவினேன், *taṭaviṉēṉ*.

Also to stroke, or to grope one’s way in the dark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தடவு** (*taṭavu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3025), of stroking and of smearing on oil.
