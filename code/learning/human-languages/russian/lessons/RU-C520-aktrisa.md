## You'll want to know: актриса
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C520-SCREENACTING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**актриса** (*aktrísa*) — "an actress".

> Эта актриса часто играет в комедиях.
>
> *Éta aktrísa chásto igráyet v komédiyakh.*
>
> This actress often appears in comedies.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*актриса* is the feminine of *актёр* 'actor', usually traced to French *actrice*, ultimately from Latin *āctor*; EtymDB lists *актриса* without a source.
