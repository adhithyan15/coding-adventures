## You'll want to know: водосток
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C446-VITRINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**водосток** (*vodostók*) — "a drainpipe, a gutter".

> Во время дождя из водостока бежит вода.
>
> *Vo vrémya dozhdyá iz vodostóka bezhít vodá.*
>
> During the rain water runs out of the drainpipe.

Masculine. *Водосточная труба* — a drainpipe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*водосток* is built from *вода* 'water' and *сток* 'a draining off', on the root of *течь* 'to flow' (EtymDB lists *водосток*).
