## You'll want to know: じょうず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C240-LIKES240-01]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうず** — *jōzu* — "skilful; good (at)".

> **あねは うたが じょうずでした。** — *ane wa uta ga jōzu deshita* — "My elder sister was good at singing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうず** (*jōzu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jō*, "above" (Middle Chinese *dzyangH*) and *zu*, "hand" (*syuwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
