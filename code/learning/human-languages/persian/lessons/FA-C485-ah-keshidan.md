## You'll want to know: آه کشیدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C485-WORRY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**آه کشیدن** (*âh keshidan*) — "to sigh".

> او به پنجره نگاه کرد و آه کشید.
>
> *u be panjere negâh kard va âh keshid.*
>
> She looked at the window and sighed.

*âh* 'a sigh' + *keshidan* 'to pull, to draw'; present stem *kesh-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âh* is usually explained as imitative, built on the sound of a sigh itself, and *keshidan*, 'to pull, to draw', is inherited Persian: to draw a sigh.
