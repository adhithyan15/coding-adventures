## You'll want to know: mìyuè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C307-SPLIT307-04]; assesses=[] -->

*mìyuè* — "honeymoon".

> *mìyuè de shíhou, wǒmen qù le hǎibiān* — On our honeymoon we went to the seaside.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mìyuè* is built from *mì* 'honey' and 月 'a month', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjit* and *ngjwot* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the food that bees make: the sweet month after a wedding.
