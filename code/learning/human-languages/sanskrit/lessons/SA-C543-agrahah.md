## You'll want to know: आग्रहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C543-HATHA543-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आग्रहः** — *āgrahaḥ* — "insistence".

> यदि भवतः आग्रहः अस्ति, तर्हि अहम् आगच्छामि।
>
> *yadi bhavataḥ āgrahaḥ asti, tarhi aham āgacchāmi.*
>
> If you insist, I will come.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आग्रहः** (*āgrahaḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √grah, "to seize"; MW gives *āgraha* as insisting on, a strong inclination, obstinacy (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
