## You'll want to know: cultivar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C691-CAMPO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cultivar** — "to grow (crops), to farm; to cultivate".

> Aquí cultivan arroz.
>
> *They grow rice here.*

*El cultivo* is the crop or the growing of it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cultivar* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *cultīvāre* (EtymDB), built on *colere* "to till, to tend".
