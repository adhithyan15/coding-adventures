## You'll want to know: కాచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C548-VANTA548-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కాచు** — *kācu* — "to boil milk; to heat".

> అమ్మ పొద్దున్నే పాలు కాచింది.
>
> *amma poddunnē pālu kācindi.*
>
> Mum boiled the milk early in the morning.

Past (I …): కాచాను, *kācānu*.

For milk and water; అన్నం వండు for rice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కాచు** (*kācu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1458 lists Telugu *kācu*, "to heat, to warm, to boil", with Tamil *kāy*, "to grow hot".
