বালতিতে একটা ফুটো আছে। Bāltite ekṭā phuṭo āchhe. There's a hole in the bucket.

Lesson 3 of 5.
উপচে পড়া (upôche pôṛā) — to overflow; to brim over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a water tank, then the Bengali for a hole right through.

You'll want to know: উপচে পড়া.
উপচে পড়া — upôche pôṛā — "to overflow; to brim over".
কলটা বন্ধ না করলে বালতি থেকে জল উপচে পড়বে। Kôlṭā bôndhô nā kôrle bālti theke jôl upôche pôṛbe. If you don't turn off the tap, the water will overflow the bucket.
Past (he/she …): সেই দুধ উপচে পড়ল, sei dudh upôche pôṛlô.
Said of milk boiling over too, as in the past form.

The word, taken apart.
উপচে পড়া (upôche pôṛā) joins উপচে, the -এ form of a verb that CDIAL lists as Bengali upacāna, "to boil over, to overflow", under a reconstructed utpacyatē, "boils over" (CDIAL 1808), to পড়া, "to fall": the water falls over the rim as it spills.

বৃষ্টির পরে আমরা উঠোনের জল সেঁচলাম। Brishṭir pôre āmrā uṭhoner jôl sẽchlām. After the rain we bailed the water out of the courtyard.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নৌকার জল সেঁচলেন, tini noukār jôl sẽchlen.

The word, taken apart.
সেঁচা (sẽchā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali sẽcā, "to irrigate, to bale out", under Sanskrit siñcati, "pours out" (CDIAL 13394).

You'll want to know: চুঁইয়ে পড়া.
চুঁইয়ে পড়া — chũiye pôṛā — "to seep; to leak through".
বৃষ্টিতে ছাদ থেকে জল চুঁইয়ে পড়ল। Brishṭite chhād theke jôl chũiye pôṛlô. In the rain, water seeped through the roof.

The word, taken apart.
চুঁইয়ে পড়া (chũiye pôṛā) joins চুঁইয়ে, the -এ form of a verb for oozing that CDIAL lists as Bengali cuyā, "to leak", under Sanskrit cyotati, "trickles, oozes" (CDIAL 4948), to পড়া, "to fall".
