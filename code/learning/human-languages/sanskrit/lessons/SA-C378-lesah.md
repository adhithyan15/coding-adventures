## You'll want to know: लेशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C378-LESHA378-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लेशः** — *leśaḥ* — "a tiny portion; a little bit".

> यदि दोषस्य लेशः अपि अस्ति, तर्हि पुनः चिन्तयतु।
>
> *yadi doṣasya leśaḥ api asti, tarhi punaḥ cintayatu.*
>
> If there is even a little of a mistake in it, think again.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लेशः** (*leśaḥ*) MW gives *leśa* "a small part or portion, a particle, a little bit" and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
