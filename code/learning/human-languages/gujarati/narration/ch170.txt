6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
મીઠાઈ (mīṭhāī) — sweets, sweetmeat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wok, then the Gujarati for lid.

You'll want to know: મીઠાઈ.
મીઠાઈ — mīṭhāī — "sweets, sweetmeat".
દિવાળીમાં અમે મીઠાઈ ખાઈએ છીએ. — divāḷīmā̃ ame mīṭhāī khāīe chīe — "We eat sweets at Diwali."
Built on મીઠું, mīṭhũ, sweet.

The word, taken apart.
મીઠાઈ (mīṭhāī) is built on the inherited mīṭhũ, "sweet", with the noun ending -āī; EtymDB derives it from that word. CDIAL traces mīṭhũ to Sanskrit miṣṭa, "dainty, sweet" (CDIAL 10299).

Lesson 2 of 6.
લાડુ (lāḍu) — laddoo, sweet ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for lid, then the Gujarati for sweets.

You'll want to know: લાડુ.
લાડુ — lāḍu — "laddoo, sweet ball".
દાદી લાડુ બનાવે છે. — dādī lāḍu banāve che — "Grandmother makes laddoos."

The word, taken apart.
લાડુ (lāḍu) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit laḍḍu, "a kind of sweetmeat", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "a sweetmeat of sweetened meal in a ball" (CDIAL 10926).

Warm-up.

બાળકને માખણ ગમે છે. — bāḷakne mākhaṇ game che — "The child likes butter."

The origin of ચોંટવું (cõṭvũ) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it with the sense of sticking, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
