## You'll want to know: سقوط کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C330-TUFAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سقوط کردن** (*soqut kardan*) — "to crash down, to fall (from a height)".

> هواپیما در کوه سقوط کرد.
>
> *Havâpeymâ dar kuh soqut kard.*
>
> The plane crashed in the mountains.

For planes, buildings and governments. Present *soqut mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soqut* is borrowed from Arabic *suqūṭ*, "a fall" (EtymDB). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
