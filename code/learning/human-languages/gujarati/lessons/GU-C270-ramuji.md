## You'll want to know: રમૂજી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C270-NATURE270-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રમૂજી** — *ramūjī* — "funny, witty".

> **મારા કાકા રમૂજી છે, પણ મારા પિતા નથી.** — *mārā kākā ramūjī che, paṇ mārā pitā nathī* — "My uncle is funny, but my father is not."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રમૂજી** (*ramūjī*) is built on *ramūj*, "jest", with *-ī*. *Ramūj* is usually traced to Arabic *rumūz*, plural of *ramz*, "a sign, a hint", through Persian; its Arabic root is *r-m-z* (Lane).
