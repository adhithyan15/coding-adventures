# Where they went: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *went* for a man, a woman and a group. Then *yesterday*.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-LEX-KALL-216, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-E-206, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-COPULA-212] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *Aman went to school yesterday*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਗਿਆ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *where did they go?*] Say **ਉਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਏ?**
- [YOU READ: **ਕੁੜੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਗਈ।**] Give its meaning. Then point to **ਏ** in **ਗਏ**.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ।**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say where you went yesterday, with the went form that fits you.
