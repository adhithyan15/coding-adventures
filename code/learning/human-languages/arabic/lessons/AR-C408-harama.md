## You'll want to know: حرم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C408-MASLAHA408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حرم** — *ḥarama* — "to deprive".

> حرمت الحمى الطفل من المدرسة.
>
> *ḥaramat al-ḥummā aṭ-ṭifl min al-madrasa.*
>
> The fever deprived the child of school.

Present: يحرم, *yaḥrim*.

It takes من for what is lost: حرمه من ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حرم** (*ḥarama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-r-m*, "to be forbidden" (Lane). *Ḥarama* is a Form I verb, to make a thing forbidden to someone: to deprive.
