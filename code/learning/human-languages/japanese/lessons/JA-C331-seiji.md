## You'll want to know: せいじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C331-SHAKAI331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**せいじ** — *seiji* — "politics".

> **しょうらいの ために、せいじを べんきょうしたいです。** — *shōrai no tame ni, seiji o benkyō shitai desu* — "I want to study politics for my future."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいじ** (*seiji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "politics" (Middle Chinese *tsyengH*) and *ji*, "reign" (*dri*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
