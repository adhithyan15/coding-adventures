# धणी (dhaṇī) — a husband (in his wife's words); a master

## You'll want to know: धणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C259-SASU259-05]; assesses=[] -->

**धणी** — *dhaṇī* — "a husband (in his wife's words); a master".

> म्हारो धणी खेत गयो।
>
> *mhāro dhaṇī khet gayo.*
>
> My husband went to the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धणी** (*dhaṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhanin*, "rich"; CDIAL lists Marwari *dhaṇī̃*, "owner", Old Marwari *dhaṇī*, "master", and Gujarati *dhaṇī*, "husband", under it (CDIAL 6722).
