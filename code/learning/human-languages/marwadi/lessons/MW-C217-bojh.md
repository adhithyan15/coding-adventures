## You'll want to know: बोझ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C217-CROSS217-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बोझ** — *bojh* — "a burden; a heavy load".

> ऊंट माथै घणो बोझ है।
>
> *ū̃ṭ māthai ghaṇo bojh hai.*
>
> The camel is carrying a heavy load.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बोझ** (*bojh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vahya*, "to be carried"; CDIAL lists Hindi *bojh*, "a load", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11465).
