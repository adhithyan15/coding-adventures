## You'll want to know: بحر

**بحر** — *baḥr* — "the sea".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بحر** (*baḥr*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-ḥ-r*, "the sea; to slit" (Lane). Lane also gives a verb *baḥara*, "to slit, to cleave".
