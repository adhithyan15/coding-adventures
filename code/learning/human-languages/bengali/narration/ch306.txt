Bengali, chapter 306: Guests and a Feast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
অতিথি (ôtithi) — a guest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to glitter, then the Bengali for a sky lantern.

You'll want to know: অতিথি.
অতিথি — ôtithi — "a guest".
পুজোর দিন বাড়িতে অনেক অতিথি এলেন। pujor din bāṛite ônek ôtithi elen. Many guests came to the house on the day of the puja.

The word, taken apart.
অতিথি (ôtithi) is Sanskrit atithi, 'a guest, one owed hospitality' (Monier-Williams), which records an old explanation: a-tithi, one with no fixed day for coming.

Lesson 4 of 5.
উপহার (upôhār) — a gift; a present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to invite, then the Bengali for a feast.

You'll want to know: উপহার.
উপহার — upôhār — "a gift; a present".
জন্মদিনে বন্ধুরা আমাকে একটা বই উপহার দিল। jônmôdine bôndhurā āmāke ekṭā bôi upôhār dilô. My friends gave me a book as a birthday present.

The word, taken apart.
উপহার (upôhār) is Sanskrit upahāra, an offering to a god or a present to a superior (Monier-Williams); it has nothing to do with হার, a necklace. উপহার দেওয়া, to give a present.

Lesson 5 of 5.
আপ্যায়ন করা (āpyāyôn kôrā) — to entertain guests; to treat hospitably.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a feast, then the Bengali for a gift.

You'll want to know: আপ্যায়ন করা.
আপ্যায়ন করা — āpyāyôn kôrā — "to entertain guests; to treat hospitably".
বাবা অতিথিদের চা আর সন্দেশ দিয়ে আপ্যায়ন করলেন। bābā ôtithider chā ār sôndesh diye āpyāyôn kôrlen. Father welcomed the guests with tea and sweets.
Past (he/she …): তিনি অতিথিদের আপ্যায়ন করলেন, tini ôtithider āpyāyôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
আপ্যায়ন করা (āpyāyôn kôrā) puts Sanskrit āpyāyana, 'causing fullness, gladdening' (Monier-Williams), before করা: to fill your guests up and make them glad.
