## You'll want to know: गीत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C532-GEET532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गीत** — *gīt* — "a song, a lyric".

> शादी में औरतों ने पुराने गीत गाए।
>
> *Śādī meṁ aurtoṁ ne purāne gīt gāe.*
>
> At the wedding the women sang old songs.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गीत** (*gīt*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *gīta*, "sung; a song" (Monier-Williams).
