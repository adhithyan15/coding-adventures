## You'll want to know: うちあける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C309-OKURU309-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うちあける** — *uchiakeru* — "to confide; to open up".

> **ともだちに ひみつを うちあけました。** — *tomodachi ni himitsu o uchiakemashita* — "I told my friend my secret."

Polite **うちあけます**, *uchiakemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あねが ひみつを うちあけました。**, *ane ga himitsu o uchiakemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うちあける** (*uchiakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *uchi*, "striking" (from *utsu*), and *akeru*, "to open" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
