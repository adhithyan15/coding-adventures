## You'll want to know: conquistar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C568-REY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**conquistar** — "to conquer; to win over".

> Los romanos conquistaron la península.
>
> *The Romans conquered the peninsula.*

It also means to win someone's heart: *La conquistó con su humor.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conquistar* is usually traced to spoken Latin *\*conquīstāre*, built on *conquīsītus*, the participle of *conquīrere* 'to seek out, to bring together' (Lewis & Short), from *quaerere* 'to seek'.
