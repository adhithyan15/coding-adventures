# व — between English v and w

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Relax the lips for a light sound between English *v* and *w*.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-VA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> व

Marathi *va* can sound lighter than English *v*, toward *w*. Travel left around
the top and circle the loop counterclockwise; descend the right stem; finish the
headline.

## Writing — heard cue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-VA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Cover the model. [YOU HEAR: *va*.] Write one consonant from sound. Uncover **व**
and compare loop, right stem, headline.
