# ಒದಗಿಸು (odagisu) — to provide; to supply

## You'll want to know: ಒದಗಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C463-SEVE463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒದಗಿಸು** — *odagisu* — "to provide; to supply".

> ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಪ್ರವಾಸಿಗರಿಗೆ ಉಚಿತ ತಿಂಡಿ ಒದಗಿಸುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *hōṭel pravāsigarige ucita tiṇḍi odagisuttade.*
>
> The hotel provides tourists with a free breakfast snack.

Past (I …): ಒದಗಿಸಿದೆ, *odagiside*.

What is given takes -ಅನ್ನು or no ending; the receiver takes -ಗೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒದಗಿಸು** (*odagisu*) is native Dravidian: the causative of *odagu*, "to come to hand, to be of use" (DEDR 609): to make a thing be at hand.
