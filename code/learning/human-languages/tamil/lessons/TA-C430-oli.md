## You'll want to know: ஒலி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C430-VANAM430-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒலி** — *oli* — "to sound; to ring out".

> ரயில் புறப்படும்போது மணி ஒலித்தது.
>
> *rayil puṟappaṭumpōtu maṇi olittatu.*
>
> As the train was leaving, a bell rang out.

Past (it …): ஒலித்தது, *olittatu*.

ஒலி is also the noun, a sound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒலி** (*oli*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 996), "to sound; a sound, a noise".
