## You'll want to know: a trovoada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C463-TROVOADA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a trovoada** — "the thunderstorm".

> Ontem à noite houve uma trovoada forte.
>
> *Last night there was a heavy thunderstorm.*

Built on *o trovão*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trovoada* is built on *trovão* 'thunder'. *Trovão* is usually traced to Latin *tonitrus* 'thunder' (Lewis & Short), with its sounds rearranged, but the details are not settled; EtymDB lists Galician *trebón* as a cousin.
