Lesson 1 of 5.
a mochila — a rucksack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a calendar, then the Portuguese for a message.

You'll want to know: a mochila.
a mochila — "a rucksack".

The word, taken apart.
mochila from Spanish mochila, "rucksack" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. The Spanish word is built on Basque motxil, "errand boy".

The word, taken apart.
pessoas from Latin persona, "an actor's mask, a role" (EtymDB 2.1), through Vulgar Latin pessona — inherited. It is the plural of pessoa, "person".

Lesson 4 of 5.
o rapaz — a boy, a young man.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the world, then the Portuguese for people.

You'll want to know: o rapaz.
o rapaz — "a boy, a young man".

The word, taken apart.
rapaz from Latin rapax, rapacis, "grasping", from rapere, "to seize" (Lewis & Short) — inherited. EtymDB 2.1 traces it only to Old Portuguese rapaz. A young rascal became simply a boy.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for people, then the Portuguese for a boy.

You'll want to know: o arco-íris.
o arco-íris — "a rainbow".

The word, taken apart.
arco-íris is built from arco, "bow, arch" from Latin arcus (EtymDB 2.1), and íris, "rainbow" from Latin iris, from Greek iris (EtymDB 2.1).
