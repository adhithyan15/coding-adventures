## You'll want to know: ঝরা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C470-TAK470-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝরা** — *jhôrā* — "to fall out (of hair); to fall away; to drip".

> চিন্তায় অরুণের চুল ঝরছে।
>
> *chintāy ôruner chul jhôrchhe.*
>
> Arun's hair is falling out from worry.

Past (he/she …): সেই গাছের পাতা ঝরল, *sei gāchher pātā jhôrlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝরা** (*jhôrā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as oozing, dripping and leaves falling, under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 5346); ঝরনা (taught), a waterfall, is probably its relative.
