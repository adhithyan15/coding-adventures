## You'll want to know: سوئچ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C504-SVICH504-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سوئچ** — *svich* — "a switch".

> بتی کا سوئچ دروازے کے پاس ہے۔
>
> *battī kā svich darvāze ke pās hai.*
>
> The light switch is by the door.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سوئچ** (*svich*) is borrowed from English *switch*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
