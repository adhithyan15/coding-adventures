## You'll want to know: сквер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C446-VITRINA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**сквер** (*skver*) — "a small public garden, a square".

> Возле театра есть маленький сквер.
>
> *Vózle teátra yest málenkiy skver.*
>
> Near the theatre there is a small public garden.

Masculine. A small green square with benches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сквер* was borrowed from English *square* (EtymDB); in Russian it names a small garden on a square.
