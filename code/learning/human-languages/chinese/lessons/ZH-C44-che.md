## You'll want to know: 车
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C44-PLACES44-01]; assesses=[] -->

**车** — *chē* — "car; vehicle".

> **你有车吗** *nǐ yǒu chē ma* — Have you got a car?

The new character, in pieces: 车 — four strokes: a bar, a stroke that falls and turns, a long bar, and a vertical through them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**车** (*chē*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as a cart, first made, it says, by the Yellow Emperor.
