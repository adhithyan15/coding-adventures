## You'll want to know: へた
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C240-LIKES240-02]; assesses=[] -->

**へた** — *heta* — "unskilful; poor (at)".

> **おとうとは やきゅうが へたでした。** — *otōto wa yakyū ga heta deshita* — "My younger brother was bad at baseball."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へた** (*heta*) is written with two characters meaning "below" and "hand", read as a whole word rather than character by character; its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
