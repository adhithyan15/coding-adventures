# Review --- six words that arrived last

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C309-KIN-19, ES-LEX-C309-KIN-20] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Two people to begin. Who is *la pareja*? (A couple, or one half of
one.) And who is *el bebé*? (A baby.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C309-KIN-19, ES-LEX-C309-KIN-20, ES-LEX-C313-ASK-19, ES-LEX-C313-ASK-20, ES-LEX-C334-BODY-34, ES-LEX-C334-BODY-35] -->

Six nouns, sharing something that has nothing to do with meaning: each one
arrived at the end of its run. A word met in the middle of a run gets picked up
by everything that comes after it. A word met last has nothing after it, so it
was said once and then left alone. These six are here for a second hearing.

[PAUSE 2s] **Two people.** *La pareja* --- a couple, from Latin *par*, equal.
*El bebé* --- a baby, and the accent pulls the weight onto the last beat.

[PAUSE 2s] **Two things a stranger holds out to you.** *La entrada* --- the way
in, and the ticket that buys the way in. *El cambio* --- the exchange, and the
coins pressed back into your hand.

[PAUSE 2s] **Two reports from your own body.** *El calor* --- heat. *La edad*
--- age.
