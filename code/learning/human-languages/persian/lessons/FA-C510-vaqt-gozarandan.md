## You'll want to know: وقت گذراندن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C510-LEISURE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**وقت گذراندن** (*vaqt gozarândan*) — "to spend time".

> او با نوه هایش وقت میگذراند.
>
> *u bâ navehâyash vaqt migozarânad.*
>
> She spends time with her grandchildren.

*vaqt* 'time' + *gozarândan* 'to pass (something)'; present stem *gozarân-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vaqt gozarândan* is built in Persian on *vaqt*, 'time', which was borrowed from Arabic *waqt* (EtymDB), and native *gozarândan*, 'to make pass', the causative of *gozashtan*.
