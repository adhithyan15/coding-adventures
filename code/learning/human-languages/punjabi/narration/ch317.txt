Punjabi, chapter 317: Days Gone By.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਵੇਲਾ (velā) — a particular time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a swear word, then the Punjabi for a reconciliation.

You'll want to know: ਵੇਲਾ.
ਵੇਲਾ — velā — "a particular time".
ਉਸ ਵੇਲੇ ਮੈਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀ। us vele maiṁ sakūl vich sī. At that time I was at school.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵੇਲਾ (velā) is inherited from Sanskrit velā, "a limit, a time"; CDIAL lists Punjabi velā, "time", under it (CDIAL 12115).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਬੀਤਣਾ (bītṇā) — to go by (of time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a reconciliation, then the Punjabi for a particular time.

You'll want to know: ਬੀਤਣਾ.
ਬੀਤਣਾ — bītṇā — "to go by (of time)".
ਛੁੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਦਿਨ ਬਹੁਤ ਜਲਦੀ ਬੀਤੇ। chhuṭṭī de din bahut jaldī bīte. The days of the holiday went by very quickly.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਸਾਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਔਖਾ ਬੀਤਿਆ, oh sāl bahut aukhā bītiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਬੀਤਣਾ (bītṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit vṛtta, "turned; passed (of time)"; CDIAL lists Punjabi batāuṇā, "to pass (time)", under it, and gives the intransitive bītnā, "to pass (of time)", only for Hindi (CDIAL 12069).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਜ਼ਮਾਨਾ (zamānā) — an era.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a particular time, then the Punjabi for to go by (of time).

You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਮਾਨਾ.
ਜ਼ਮਾਨਾ — zamānā — "an era".
ਉਸ ਜ਼ਮਾਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੋਕ ਪੈਦਲ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਸਨ। us zamāne vich lok paidal jānde san. In those days people went on foot.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਜ਼ਮਾਨਾ (zamānā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic zamān, "time, a period".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to go by (of time), then the Punjabi for an era.

You'll want to know: ਪਛਤਾਉਣਾ.
ਪਛਤਾਉਣਾ — pachhtāuṇā — "to regret".
ਝਗੜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਛਤਾਇਆ। jhagṛe ton bāad maiṁ bahut pachhtāiā. After the quarrel I regretted it a lot.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਛਤਾਇਆ, maiṁ bāad vich pachhtāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਛਤਾਉਣਾ (pachhtāuṇā) goes back to a reconstructed paścottāpa, "repentance"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pachotāvā, "repentance", under it, beside the -ṇā forms pachõtāṇā and pastāṇā (CDIAL 8010).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਗੁਜ਼ਾਰਨਾ (guzārnā) — to spend time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an era, then the Punjabi for to regret.

You'll want to know: ਗੁਜ਼ਾਰਨਾ.
ਗੁਜ਼ਾਰਨਾ — guzārnā — "to spend time".
ਗਰਮੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਮਹੀਨਾ ਨਾਨੀ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਗੁਜ਼ਾਰਿਆ। garmī vich maiṁ ikk mahīnā nānī de ghar guzāriā. In the summer I spent a month at my grandmother's house.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਛੁੱਟੀ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੁਜ਼ਾਰੀ, maiṁ chhuṭṭī ghar vich guzārī.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੁਜ਼ਾਰਨਾ (guzārnā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian guzār, "passing", with a Punjabi verb ending.
