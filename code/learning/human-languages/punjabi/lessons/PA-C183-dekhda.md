# ਦੇਖਦਾ — he sees or watches

[PAUSE 2s] Recall the twin infinitives *vekhnaa* and *dekhnaa*, then read **ਉੱਠਦਾ**.

## Grammar Lens: use the dekh- twin
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-DEKHDA-183]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਦੇਖਦਾ** — *dekhdaa* — sees or watches, for one masculine subject

*Vekhnaa* and *dekhnaa* are living twins. This mock-facing form uses the *dekh-* twin: **ਉਹ ਦੇਖਦਾ ਹੈ** — “He sees” or “He watches.”
