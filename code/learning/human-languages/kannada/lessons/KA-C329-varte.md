# ವಾರ್ತೆ (vārte) — a news bulletin; news

## You'll want to know: ವಾರ್ತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C329-MADHYAMA329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಾರ್ತೆ** — *vārte* — "a news bulletin; news".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಎಂಟು ಗಂಟೆಯ ವಾರ್ತೆ ಕೇಳುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *ajja pratidina rātri eṇṭu gaṇṭeya vārte kēḷuttāre.*
>
> Grandfather listens to the eight o'clock news bulletin every night.

ವಾರ್ತೆ is the formal word on radio and television; in talk people say ಸುದ್ದಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಾರ್ತೆ** (*vārte*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vārttā*, "an account of what has happened, tidings, news" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
