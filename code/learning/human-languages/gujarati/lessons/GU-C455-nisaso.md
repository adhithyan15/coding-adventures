## You'll want to know: નિસાસો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C455-GAMGIN455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નિસાસો** — *nisāso* — "a sigh".

> દાદીએ જૂના ફોટા જોઈને નિસાસો નાખ્યો.
>
> *dādīe jūnā phoṭā joīne nisāso nākhyo.*
>
> Looking at the old photos, my grandmother sighed.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિસાસો** (*nisāso*) is inherited from Sanskrit *niḥśvāsa*, "a sigh", a breathing out; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 7111).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
