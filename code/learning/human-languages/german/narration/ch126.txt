Lesson 1 of 5.
der Briefträger — a postman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a lawyer, then the German for an engineer.

You'll want to know: der Briefträger.
der Briefträger — "a postman".

The word, taken apart.
Briefträger is the word for "letter" + Träger, "carrier", from tragen. The letter word from Old High German briaf from Latin brevis, "short (document)" (EtymDB 2.1).
