## Script — the speech piece, then 十 over 买
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-DU-READ-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 读

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, 讠, two strokes,** as in 说 and 请: a dot, then a short bar to the right that turns down and flicks up to the right, all one stroke.

**The right piece, eight strokes, top to bottom.** First a short 十: a bar and a vertical through it. Then 买, six strokes, as you wrote it: a bar that turns down at its right end and hooks, two short dots, then a long bar, a stroke falling to the left, and a short dot at the foot.

2 + 8 = ten.
