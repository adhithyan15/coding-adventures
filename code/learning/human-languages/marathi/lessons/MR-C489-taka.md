## You'll want to know: टाका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C489-LAS489-04]; assesses=[] -->

**टाका** — *ṭākā* — "a stitch (in a wound)".

> जखमेला चार टाके घातले.
>
> *jakhmelā cār ṭāke ghātle.*
>
> They put four stitches in the wound.

Masculine. Plural: टाके, *ṭāke*.

Usually plural, टाके: टाके घालणे, to put in stitches; टाके काढणे, to take them out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टाका** (*ṭākā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ṭākā*, of stitching, under Sanskrit *ṭaṅkati*, "ties" (CDIAL 5432).
