## You'll want to know: اعتقل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C300-JARIMA300-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتقل** — *iʿtaqala* — "to arrest, to detain".

> اعتقلت الشرطة الرجل في المطار.
>
> *iʿtaqalat ash-shurṭa ar-rajul fī al-maṭār.*
>
> The police arrested the man at the airport.

Present: يعتقل, *yaʿtaqil*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتقل** (*iʿtaqala*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-l* (Lane), "to tie up a camel's foreleg", the root of *ʿaql*, the mind that holds one back. Form VIII *iʿtaqala* is "to hold a person in".
