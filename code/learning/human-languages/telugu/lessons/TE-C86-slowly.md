# నెమ్మదిగా (nemmadigā) — slowly

## You'll want to know: నెమ్మదిగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C86-REPAIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**నెమ్మదిగా** (*nemmadigā*) — "slowly".

**నెమ్మదిగా మాట్లాడండి** — "please speak slowly". You have the verb from your first chapters
and the polite ending from *raṇḍi*.

Notice the **-గా** at the end: the same ending that made *alasaṭagā* and *caligā*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The request a beginner needs most.
