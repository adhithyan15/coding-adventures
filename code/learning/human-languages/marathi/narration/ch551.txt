Marathi, chapter 551: Waiting Lists.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रतीक्षा (pratīkṣā) — waiting; expectation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a coin toss, then the Marathi for to bet.

You'll want to know: प्रतीक्षा.
प्रतीक्षा — pratīkṣā — "waiting; expectation".
पाहुण्यांची आम्ही खूप वेळ प्रतीक्षा केली. pāhuṇyāñcī āmhī khūp veḷ pratīkṣā kelī. We waited a long time for the guests.
Feminine.
The one awaited takes -ची: पाहुण्यांची प्रतीक्षा (pratīkṣā) करणे.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतीक्षा (pratīkṣā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pratīkṣā, "expectation" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
