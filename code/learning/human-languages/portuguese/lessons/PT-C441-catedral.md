## You'll want to know: a catedral
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C441-PRACA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a catedral** — "the cathedral".

> A catedral é o edifício mais antigo da praça.
>
> *The cathedral is the oldest building on the square.*

In Portugal a cathedral is often called *a Sé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*catedral* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *cathedrālis* 'of the bishop's seat', built on *cathedra* 'chair', from Greek *kathédra* 'seat' (Lewis & Short has *cathedra*).
