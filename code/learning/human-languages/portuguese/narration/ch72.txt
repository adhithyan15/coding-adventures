You'll want to know: ali.

The word, taken apart.
ali from Latin illic, "there" (Lewis & Short), with a- from ad, "to" — inherited. EtymDB 2.1 traces it only to Old Portuguese ali.

You'll want to know: perto.
perto — "near".

The word, taken apart.
perto — its origin is uncertain. Old Portuguese said preto, "close, tight" (EtymDB 2.1), and the usual account traces that word to Latin pressus, "pressed close".

Lesson 5 of 5.
depressa — quickly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for far, then the Portuguese for near.

You'll want to know: depressa.
depressa — "quickly". The opposite of devagar.

The word, taken apart.
depressa is built from de and the word for haste, which from Latin pressa, "pressed" (EtymDB 2.1): "in a press, in a hurry".
