## You'll want to know: буря
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C466-BURYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**буря** (*búrya*) — "a storm".

> Ночью была сильная буря.
>
> *Nóchyu bylá sílnaya búrya.*
>
> There was a strong storm in the night.

Feminine. *Буря* is a storm of wind; *гроза* is a thunderstorm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*буря* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *buřa* (EtymDB).
