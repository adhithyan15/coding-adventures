Marathi, chapter 154: To return, To set out, To run away, To break off, To break.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परतणे (paratṇe) — to return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get down, then the Marathi for to arrive.

You'll want to know: परतणे.
परतणे — paratṇe — "to return".

The word, taken apart.
परतणे (paratṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from parivartate, "revolves, turns round" (CDIAL 7872).

Lesson 5 of 5.
मोडणे (moḍṇe) — to break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to run away, then the Marathi for to break off.

You'll want to know: मोडणे.
मोडणे — moḍṇe — "to break".

The word, taken apart.
मोडणे (moḍṇe) comes from mōṭati, a form beside Sanskrit muṭati, "twists" (CDIAL 10186). CDIAL notes that the verb partly collides with another muṭati, "crushes", which it takes from Dravidian.
