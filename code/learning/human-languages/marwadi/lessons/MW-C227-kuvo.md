## You'll want to know: कूवो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C227-WELL227-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कूवो** — *kūvo* — "a well".

> गांव मांय एक कूवो है।
>
> *gā̃v mā̃y ek kūvo hai.*
>
> There's a well in the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कूवो** (*kūvo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kūpa*, "a hole, a pit, a well"; CDIAL lists Marwari *kūvo*, a well without a masonry lining (CDIAL 3400).
