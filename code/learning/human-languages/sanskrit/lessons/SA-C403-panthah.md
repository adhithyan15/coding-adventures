## You'll want to know: पन्थाः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C403-PANTHA403-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पन्थाः** — *panthāḥ* — "a route; a path".

> पन्थाः वनस्य मध्ये गच्छति।
>
> *panthāḥ vanasya madhye gacchati.*
>
> The route runs through the middle of the forest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पन्थाः** (*panthāḥ*) is the nominative of *pathin*, "a way, a road", whose strong stem is *panthan*; it comes from the same word as *pātheya*, "food for the journey"; MW gives *pathin* as a way, path, road or route (MW).
