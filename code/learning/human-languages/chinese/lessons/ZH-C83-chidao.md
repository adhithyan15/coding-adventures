## You'll want to know: chídào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chídào* — "to be late; to arrive late".

> *duìbuqǐ wǒ chídào le* — Sorry I'm late.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chídào* is built from chí 'slow' and dào 'to arrive', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drij* and *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
