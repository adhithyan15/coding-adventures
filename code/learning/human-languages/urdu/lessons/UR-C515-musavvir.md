## You'll want to know: مصور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C515-TASVIR515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مصور** — *musavvir* — "a painter (an artist)".

> اس مصور نے پہاڑوں کی تصویریں بنائیں۔
>
> *is musavvir ne pahāṛoṅ kī tasvīreṅ banāīṅ.*
>
> This painter made pictures of the mountains.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصور** (*musavvir*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muṣawwir*, "one who gives form".
