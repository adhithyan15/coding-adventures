## You'll want to know: રાહત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C306-RAHAT306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**રાહત** — *rāhat* — "relief".

> વરસાદ અટક્યો ત્યારે લોકોને રાહત થઈ.
>
> *varsād aṭakyo tyāre lokone rāhat thaī.*
>
> When the rain stopped, people felt relieved.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રાહત** (*rāhat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rāḥa*, "rest, ease". Gujarati uses it for the ease that comes after trouble, **રાહત થઈ**, and for the help sent to people in need.
