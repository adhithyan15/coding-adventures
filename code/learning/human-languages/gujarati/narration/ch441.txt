Gujarati, chapter 441: Lakes, Dams and Stepwells.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સરોવર (sarovar) — a large lake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a canal, then the Gujarati for to let flow.

You'll want to know: સરોવર.
સરોવર — sarovar — "a large lake".
આ સરોવરમાં ઘણી માછલીઓ છે. ā sarovarmā̃ ghaṇī māchlīo che. There are many fish in this lake.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સરોવર (sarovar) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sarovara, "a lake, a large pond" (Monier-Williams); a તળાવ is usually smaller.

Lesson 3 of 5.
વાવ (vāv) — a stepwell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a large lake, then the Gujarati for a dam.

You'll want to know: વાવ.
વાવ — vāv — "a stepwell".
ગામની જૂની વાવમાં હજી પાણી છે. gāmnī jūnī vāvmā̃ hajī pāṇī che. There is still water in the village's old stepwell.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વાવ (vāv) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit vāpī, "an oblong pond"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a large well with steps" (CDIAL 11529): steps that go down to the water.

Lesson 4 of 5.
કાંઠો (kā̃ṭho) — a riverbank; the bank of a lake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a dam, then the Gujarati for a stepwell.

You'll want to know: કાંઠો.
કાંઠો — kā̃ṭho — "a riverbank; the bank of a lake".
નદીના કાંઠે બાળકો રમે છે. nadīnā kā̃ṭhe bāḷko rame che. Children play on the river bank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કાંઠો (kā̃ṭho) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit kaṇṭha, "the throat", and so a rim or edge; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a bank, a coast" (CDIAL 2680).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખૂંપવું (khū̃pvũ) — to sink in (mud); to get stuck deep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a stepwell, then the Gujarati for a riverbank.

You'll want to know: ખૂંપવું.
ખૂંપવું — khū̃pvũ — "to sink in (mud); to get stuck deep".
કાદવમાં મારા પગ ખૂંપી ગયા. kādavmā̃ mārā pag khū̃pī gayā. My feet sank into the mud.
Past (it …): તે ગાડું કાદવમાં ખૂંપી ગયું, te gāḍũ kādavmā̃ khū̃pī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
ખૂંપવું (khū̃pvũ) goes back to skumpati, "presses in", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to pierce" (CDIAL 13658).
