## You'll want to know: ਵਾਹੁਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C301-PLOUGH301-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਹੁਣਾ** — *vāhuṇā* — "to plough".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਖੇਤ ਵਾਹਾਂਗੇ।
>
> *asīṁ kall khet vāhāṅge.*
>
> We will plough the field tomorrow.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਖੇਤ ਵਾਹਿਆ, *maiṁ khet vāhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਹੁਣਾ** (*vāhuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vāhayati*, "drives (a chariot)"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vāhuṇā*, "to drive (a beast), plough", under it (CDIAL 11612).
