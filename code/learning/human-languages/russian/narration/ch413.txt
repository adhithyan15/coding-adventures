Russian, chapter 413: A train ticket.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
проезд (proyézd) — a fare; a passage, a way through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to let through, then the Russian for road markings.

You'll want to know: проезд.
проезд (proyézd) — "a fare; a passage, a way through".
Сколько стоит проезд на метро? Skólko stóit proyézd na metró? How much is the metro fare?
Masculine. Проезд закрыт — no through road.

The word, taken apart.
проезд (proyézd) is built from про- 'through' and -езд 'riding', the root of ездить 'to ride, to travel'. EtymDB lists проезд (proyézd) without a deeper source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
плацкарт (platskárt) — an open sleeping car (on a train).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for road markings, then the Russian for a fare.

You'll want to know: плацкарт.
плацкарт (platskárt) — "an open sleeping car (on a train)".
Студенты ехали на море в плацкарте. Studénty yékhali na móre v platskárte. The students travelled to the seaside in an open sleeping car.
Masculine; colloquial for плацкартный вагон, a cheap carriage of open bunks.

The word, taken apart.
плацкарт (platskárt) is usually traced to German Platzkarte 'a seat reservation', built from Platz 'place' and Karte 'card'. EtymDB lists the German word but not the Russian, so the route is hedged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
купе (kupé) — a compartment (on a train).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fare, then the Russian for an open sleeping car.

You'll want to know: купе.
купе (kupé) — "a compartment (on a train)".
В купе четыре полки. V kupé chetýre pólki. There are four berths in a compartment.
Neuter and indeclinable: в купе (kupé), из купе (kupé).

The word, taken apart.
купе (kupé) is usually traced to French coupé 'cut off', from couper 'to cut': a carriage cut into small rooms. EtymDB does not list the Russian word, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 4 of 5.
тариф (taríf) — a rate, a tariff; a fare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an open sleeping car, then the Russian for a compartment.

You'll want to know: тариф.
тариф (taríf) — "a rate, a tariff; a fare".
С января в автобусе новый тариф. S yanvaryá v avtóbuse nóvyy taríf. From January there is a new fare on the bus.
Masculine. Тариф is the official rate; цена the everyday word for a price.

The word, taken apart.
тариф (taríf) was borrowed from Italian tariffa 'a tariff', which EtymDB traces to Arabic taʿrīfa 'a rate'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
льгота (lgóta) — a concession, a reduced fare; a benefit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a compartment, then the Russian for a rate.

You'll want to know: льгота.
льгота (lgóta) — "a concession, a reduced fare; a benefit".
У пенсионеров есть льгота на проезд. U pensionérov yest lgóta na proyézd. Pensioners have a reduced fare on public transport.
Feminine. Льготный билет — a concession ticket.

The word, taken apart.
льгота (lgóta) is usually linked with an old root meaning 'light, easy', the one in лёгкий: a concession makes things lighter. EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the line is hedged.
