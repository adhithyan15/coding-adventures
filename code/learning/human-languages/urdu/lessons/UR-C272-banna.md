# بننا (bannā) — to become, to be made

## You'll want to know: بننا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C272-BANNA-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C46-FAMILY46-01, UR-LEX-C57-FOOD57-05, UR-LEX-C139-ACT139-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-01] -->

**بننا** — *bannā* — "to become; to be made".

> **وہ استاد بنی۔**
>
> *voh ustād banī.* — "She became a teacher."

> **یہ گھر پچھلے سال بنا۔**
>
> *yih ghar pichhle sāl banā.* — "This house was built last year."

It has no object, so no **نے**: **بنی** follows her. Its partner **بنانا** (*banānā*), "to make", takes an object and **نے**: **اس نے گھر بنایا** (*us ne ghar banāyā*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بننا** (*bannā*) is inherited. CDIAL traces it to Sanskrit *vanati*, "desires, gains, makes ready" (CDIAL 11260), and gives "to be made" as a Hindi sense.
