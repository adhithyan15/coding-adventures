# ص (sâd) — the letter that opens صد

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-GAF-01, FA-SCRIPT-C25-SHIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write گ and ش once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **صد** (*sad*), "a hundred".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-SAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ص** — *sâd*, the sound *s*.

It opens **صد** (*sad*), "a hundred". In Persian it sounds **exactly like س**: the
two letters are spelled differently and said the same, because **ص** came in with
Arabic words and kept its Arabic spelling. The letter is a closed **oval** with a
bowl after it.

## Writing: ص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-SAD-01] -->

Put your pen on ص and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
