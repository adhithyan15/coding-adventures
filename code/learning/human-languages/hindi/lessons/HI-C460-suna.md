## You'll want to know: सूना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C460-BICHHAD460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सूना** — *sūnā* — "desolate; empty".

> बच्चों के बिना घर सूना लगता है।
>
> *baccoṁ ke binā ghar sūnā lagtā hai.*
>
> The house feels empty without the children.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूना** (*sūnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *sūnā*, "empty, hollow", under Sanskrit *śūnyá*, "empty" (CDIAL 12567). It is said of places that feel empty because someone is missing.
