# Fill one date line with help

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-DATE-01, PA-FORM-DATE-CUE-MAP-01] -->

Point to the **ਤਾਰੀਖ਼** label. Keep the closed bank in view: A
**੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫**, B **੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫**.

## Writing — supported entry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-A-01, PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01, PA-FORM-DATE-CUE-MAP-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

For the fictional card **ਕ**, select A. Copy its day into the first box,
January's numeric month into the middle box, and its year into the final box:
**ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫**. Keep both slash marks.

## Guided Practice — check one dimension
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-MONTH-MAP-01, PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->

Read the line from left to right. Check cue, day, month, year, and separators
one at a time. If a piece differs, change only that piece.
