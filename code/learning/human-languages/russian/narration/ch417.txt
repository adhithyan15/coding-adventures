Lesson 1 of 5.
турникет (turnikét) — a turnstile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a trolley, then the Russian for to present.

You'll want to know: турникет.
турникет (turnikét) — "a turnstile".
У входа в метро стоят турникеты. U vkhóda v metró stoyát turnikéty. There are turnstiles at the metro entrance.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
турникет (turnikét) was borrowed from French tourniquet (EtymDB), built on tourner 'to turn', which goes back to Latin tornāre 'to turn on a lathe' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
проездной (proyezdnóy) — a travel pass, a season ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to present, then the Russian for a turnstile.

You'll want to know: проездной.
проездной (proyezdnóy) — "a travel pass, a season ticket".
У мамы проездной на месяц. U mámy proyezdnóy na mésyats. Mum has a monthly travel pass.
An adjective used as a noun, short for проездной (proyezdnóy) билет; it declines like an adjective.

The word, taken apart.
проездной (proyezdnóy) is built on проезд 'a fare, a passage' with an adjective ending, from про- and the root of ездить 'to ride'. EtymDB lists проезд without a deeper source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
безбилетник (bezbilétnik) — a fare dodger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a turnstile, then the Russian for a travel pass.

You'll want to know: безбилетник.
безбилетник (bezbilétnik) — "a fare dodger".
В автобусе поймали двух безбилетников. V avtóbuse poymáli dvukh bezbilétnikov. Two fare dodgers were caught on the bus.
Built on без билета — without a ticket.

The word, taken apart.
безбилетник (bezbilétnik) is built from без 'without', билет 'a ticket' and -ник 'one who'. Билет came from French billet 'a note, a ticket'; EtymDB does not list the compound.

Lesson 4 of 5.
маршрут (marshrút) — a route.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a travel pass, then the Russian for a fare dodger.

You'll want to know: маршрут.
маршрут (marshrút) — "a route".
Этот автобус ходит по новому маршруту. Étot avtóbus khódit po nóvomu marshrútu. This bus runs on a new route.
Masculine. Маршрут is the planned way; дорога the road itself.

The word, taken apart.
маршрут (marshrút) was borrowed from German Marschroute 'a line of march', built from Marsch 'march' and Route 'route' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
маршрутка (marshrútka) — a fixed-route minibus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fare dodger, then the Russian for a route.

You'll want to know: маршрутка.
маршрутка (marshrútka) — "a fixed-route minibus".
До работы я езжу на маршрутке. Do rabóty ya yézzhu na marshrútke. I go to work by minibus.
Colloquial for маршрутное такси: a minibus that runs on a set route and stops on request.

The word, taken apart.
маршрутка (marshrútka) is a colloquial short form of маршрутное такси 'a route taxi', built on маршрут (marshrút) 'a route' (EtymDB lists маршрутка (marshrútka)). Маршрут came from German Marschroute.
