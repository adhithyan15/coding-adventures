## You'll want to know: सराय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C413-SARAY413-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सराय** — *sarāy* — "an inn".

> पहले मुसाफ़िर सराय में रुकते थे।
>
> *pahle musāfir sarāy meṁ rukte the.*
>
> In the old days, travellers used to stop at an inn.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सराय** (*sarāy*) is usually traced to Persian *sarāy*, "a large house, a palace", which also named the walled inns built along the old trade roads; many towns still have a lane or a quarter called सराय.
