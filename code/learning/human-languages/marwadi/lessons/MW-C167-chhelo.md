## You'll want to know: छेलो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C167-KAM167-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छेलो** — *chhelo* — "last".

> छेलो काम सोरो है।
>
> *chhelo kām soro hai.*
>
> The last job is easy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छेलो** (*chhelo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cheda*, "a piece cut off; a stopping"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *chehalaü*, "last, final" (CDIAL 5064). It agrees like **पैलो**: **छेली बात**, the last thing.
