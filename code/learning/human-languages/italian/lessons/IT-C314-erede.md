## You'll want to know: l'erede
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C314-EREDITA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'erede** — "the heir".

> È l'unico erede della famiglia.
>
> *He is the family's only heir.*

One form for a man or a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erede* is inherited from Latin *hērēs* "heir, heiress" (EtymDB).
