## You'll want to know: el piloto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C654-AVION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el piloto** — "the pilot; the driver (racing)".

> El piloto habla por el altavoz.
>
> *The pilot is speaking over the loudspeaker.*

For a woman, *la piloto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piloto* was borrowed from Italian *piloto* (EtymDB), first the man who steered a ship into port. Its deeper origin is not settled.
