Russian, chapter 285: Events and incidents.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
событие (sobýtiye) — an event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to comment on, then the Russian for to quote.

You'll want to know: событие.
событие (sobýtiye) — "an event".
Это было важное событие для всей семьи. Éto býlo vázhnoye sobýtiye dlya vsey semí. It was an important event for the whole family.
Neuter. Место событий — the scene.

The word, taken apart.
событие (sobýtiye) is built on со- "together" and бытие "being", from быть "to be", inherited from Proto-Slavic byti, from PIE bʰuH- (EtymDB): what came to be.

Lesson 2 of 5.
происшествие (proisshéstviye) — an incident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to quote, then the Russian for an event.

You'll want to know: происшествие.
происшествие (proisshéstviye) — "an incident".
Вчера на улице было странное происшествие. Vcherá na úlitse býlo stránnoye proisshéstviye. Yesterday there was a strange incident in the street.
Neuter. Usually something unpleasant or unexpected.

The word, taken apart.
происшествие (proisshéstviye) is built on произойти "to happen" with the stem шеств- "going", seen in Church Slavonic šĭstvie "a going, a procession" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
хроника (khrónika) — a news round-up; a chronicle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an event, then the Russian for an incident.

You'll want to know: хроника.
хроника (khrónika) — "a news round-up; a chronicle".
Каждый вечер он слушает хронику новостей. Kázhdyy vécher on slúshayet khróniku novostéy. Every evening he listens to the news round-up.
Feminine. Хроника — a record of events in order.

The word, taken apart.
хроника (khrónika) came through German Chronik from Latin chronica "a chronicle", from Greek khronikós "of time", built on khrónos "time" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
сводка (svódka) — a summary, a bulletin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an incident, then the Russian for a news round-up.

You'll want to know: сводка.
сводка (svódka) — "a summary, a bulletin".
По радио передавали сводку погоды. Po rádio peredaváli svódku pogódy. The radio gave the weather report.
Сводка погоды — the weather report. Genitive plural сводок.

The word, taken apart.
сводка (svódka) is built on сводить "to bring together", from водить "to lead", inherited from Proto-Slavic voditi (EtymDB): facts brought together.

Lesson 5 of 5.
итог (itóg) — the result, the total.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a news round-up, then the Russian for a summary.

You'll want to know: итог.
итог (itóg) — "the result, the total".
В итоге мы решили остаться дома. V itóge my reshíli ostátsya dóma. In the end we decided to stay at home.
В итоге — in the end; подвести итоги — to sum up.

The word, taken apart.
итог (itóg) is usually traced to и-, a form of из "out", and the root of течь "to flow": what flows out at the end. EtymDB lists no parent.
