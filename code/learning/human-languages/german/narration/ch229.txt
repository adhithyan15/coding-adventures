You'll want to know: das Erlebnis.
das Erlebnis — "an experience (something lived)".

The word, taken apart.
Erlebnis is er- + leben, "to live", + -nis: something lived through. leben from Proto-Germanic libjaną. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Führung — a guided tour, leadership.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an explanation, then the German for an experience.

You'll want to know: die Führung.
die Führung — "a guided tour, leadership".

The word, taken apart.
Führung is the verb "to lead" + -ung. That verb first meant "to make go", built on fahren: a guide makes you travel. EtymDB 2.1 has the verb but not its source.

The word, taken apart.
Gebühr comes from Middle High German gebür, "what is due", from an old verb meaning "to befall, to be fitting". EtymDB 2.1 has no source for it.

You'll want to know: die Gegend.
die Gegend — "an area, a region".

The word, taken apart.
Gegend from Middle High German gegende (EtymDB 2.1), built on the word for "towards, against": the land that lies before you, a copy of Medieval Latin contrāta.
