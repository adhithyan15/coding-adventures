## You'll want to know: બનેવી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C491-JAMAI491-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બનેવી** — *banevī* — "a sister's husband".

> મારા બનેવીએ મને જન્મદિવસે ઘડિયાળ આપી.
>
> *mārā banevīe mane janmadivse ghaḍiyāḷ āpī.*
>
> My sister's husband gave me a watch for my birthday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બનેવી** (*banevī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhaginīpati*, "sister's husband", from *bhaginī*, "sister", and *pati*, "husband"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9350).
