## You'll want to know: il fallo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C509-ARBITRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il fallo** — "foul".

> Questo è fallo, arbitro!
>
> *That is a foul, referee!*

*Fare fallo* — to commit a foul.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fallo* "fault, foul" comes from the old verb *fallare* "to err", usually traced to Latin *fallere* "to deceive, to fail" (Lewis & Short).
