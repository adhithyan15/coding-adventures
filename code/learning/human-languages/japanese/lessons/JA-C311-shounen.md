## You'll want to know: しょうねん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C311-OMOIDE311-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうねん** — *shōnen* — "a boy; boyhood".

> **しょうねんの ころ、そふは やまで はたらいていました。** — *shōnen no koro, sofu wa yama de hataraite imashita* — "As a boy, my grandfather worked in the mountains."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうねん** (*shōnen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "little" (Middle Chinese *syewH*) and *nen*, "year" (*nen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
