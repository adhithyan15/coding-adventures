# சாயம் (cāyam) — a dye; colour

## You'll want to know: சாயம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C557-KAMPALI557-03]; assesses=[] -->

**சாயம்** — *cāyam* — "a dye; colour".

> மழையில் நனைந்தால் இந்தத் துணியின் சாயம் போகும்.
>
> *maḻaiyil naṉaintāl intat tuṇiyiṉ cāyam pōkum.*
>
> If it gets wet in the rain, this cloth will lose its dye.

Colour coming out of cloth is சாயம் போ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சாயம்** (*cāyam*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2457 gives சாய் (*cāy*), "brilliance, colour", and சாயம் (*cāyam*), "colour, tint, dye".

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
