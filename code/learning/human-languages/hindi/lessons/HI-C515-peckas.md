## You'll want to know: पेचकस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C515-PENCH515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पेचकस** — *peckas* — "a screwdriver".

> पेचकस अलमारी में ऊपर रखा है।
>
> *Peckas almārī meṁ ūpar rakhā hai.*
>
> The screwdriver is up in the cupboard.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेचकस** (*peckas*) is usually traced to Persian *pēch*, "a screw", and *kash*, "pulling, drawing": the thing that turns screws.
