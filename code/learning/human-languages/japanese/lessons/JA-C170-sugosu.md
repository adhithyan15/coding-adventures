## You'll want to know: すごす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C170-NEWYEAR170-05]; assesses=[] -->

**すごす** — *sugosu* — "to spend time; to pass (the days)".

> **しょうがつは かぞくと すごします。** — *shōgatsu wa kazoku to sugoshimasu* — "I will spend the New Year with my family."

Polite **すごします**, *sugoshimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すごす** (*sugosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
