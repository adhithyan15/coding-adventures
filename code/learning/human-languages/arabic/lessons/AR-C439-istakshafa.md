## You'll want to know: استكشف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C439-MANZAR439-04]; assesses=[] -->

**استكشف** — *istakshafa* — "to explore".

> استكشفنا الكهف مع مرشد.
>
> *istakshafnā al-kahf maʿa murshid.*
>
> We explored the cave with a guide.

Present: يستكشف, *yastakshif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استكشف** (*istakshafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-sh-f*, "to uncover" (Lane). It is Form X, "to seek to uncover".
