## You'll want to know: mōmentum, mōmentī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C296-INITIUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mōmentum, mōmentī** — "a moment; weight, importance".

> Mōmentō temporis omnia mūtāta sunt.
>
> *In a moment of time everything was changed.*

Neuter. A slight movement that tips the scales, then an instant, and also 'weight, importance'. English *moment*, *momentum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mōmentum** is built on *moveō*, "to move" (EtymDB): a movement, the tilt of a balance, and so an instant. English *moment* and *momentum* come from it.
