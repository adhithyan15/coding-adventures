Lesson 4 of 5.
সোজা (sojā) — straight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for calm, then the Bengali for urgent.

You'll want to know: সোজা.
সোজা — sojā — "straight".

The word, taken apart.
সোজা (sojā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śodhya, "to be cleaned" (CDIAL 12632). CDIAL prefers this source to a form śudhya, "to be made pure", that Chatterji proposed (CDIAL 12525).

Lesson 5 of 5.
বাঁকা (bā̃kā) — crooked.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for urgent, then the Bengali for straight.

You'll want to know: বাঁকা.
বাঁকা — bā̃kā — "crooked".

The word, taken apart.
বাঁকা (bā̃kā) (bāṁkā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vaṅka, "bent, crooked" (CDIAL 11191). CDIAL thinks the word belongs to, or was influenced by, a probably non-Aryan group of words for "bent".
