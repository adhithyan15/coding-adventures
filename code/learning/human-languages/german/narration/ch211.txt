German, chapter 211: Anxious, Tiring, Excited, Excellent, Working.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ängstlich — anxious, timid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a butcher's, then the German for a department store.

You'll want to know: ängstlich.
ängstlich — "anxious, timid".

The word, taken apart.
ängstlich is Angst, "fear", + -lich: fearful. Angst from Proto-Germanic angustiz, from a word for "narrow". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.
anstrengend — tiring, demanding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a department store, then the German for anxious.

You'll want to know: anstrengend.
anstrengend — "tiring, demanding".

The word, taken apart.
anstrengend is the present participle of the verb "to strain", built on the word for "tight, strict", Old High German strengi, which EtymDB 2.1 lists beside English strong.

You'll want to know: ausgezeichnet.
ausgezeichnet — "excellent".

The word, taken apart.
ausgezeichnet is the past participle of aus- + zeichnen, "to mark": marked out from the rest. zeichnen is built on the word for "sign", English token. (EtymDB 2.1).
