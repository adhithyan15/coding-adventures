## You'll want to know: रेडियो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C312-SAMACHAR312-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रेडियो** — *reḍiyo* — "a radio".

> दादा जी हर सुबह रेडियो सुनते हैं।
>
> *dādā jī har subah reḍiyo sunte haiṁ.*
>
> Grandfather listens to the radio every morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेडियो** (*reḍiyo*) is borrowed from English *radio*, taken into Hindi as a masculine noun: **रेडियो पर**, on the radio.
