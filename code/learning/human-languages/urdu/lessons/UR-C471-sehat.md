## You'll want to know: صحت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C471-HEALTH471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صحت** — *sehat* — "health".

> ورزش صحت کے لیے اچھی ہے۔
>
> *varzish sehat ke liye achchhī hai.*
>
> Exercise is good for your health.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صحت** (*sehat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣiḥḥa*, "health, soundness".
