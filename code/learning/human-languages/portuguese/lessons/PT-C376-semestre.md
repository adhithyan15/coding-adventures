## You'll want to know: o semestre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C376-FACULDADE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o semestre** — "the semester, the half-year".

> Este semestre tenho cinco cadeiras.
>
> *This semester I have five course units.*

In Portuguese universities a course unit is *uma cadeira*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*semestre* comes from Latin *sēmestris* "of six months, half-yearly" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *sex* "six" and *mēnsis* "month".
