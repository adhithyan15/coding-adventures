## You'll want to know: der Staub
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C526-PUTZEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Staub** — "dust".

> Auf dem alten Schrank liegt viel Staub.
>
> *There's a lot of dust on the old cupboard.*

No plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Staub* is inherited from Old High German *stoup* "dust" (EtymDB).
