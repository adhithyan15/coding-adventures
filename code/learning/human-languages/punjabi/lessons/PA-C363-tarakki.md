## You'll want to know: ਤਰੱਕੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C363-WORK363-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਰੱਕੀ** — *tarakkī* — "a promotion; progress".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਪਿਤਾ ਨੂੰ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਤਰੱਕੀ ਮਿਲੀ।
>
> *mere pitā nū̃ daftar vich tarakkī milī.*
>
> My father got a promotion at the office.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਰੱਕੀ** (*tarakkī*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *taraqqī*, "rising".
