## You'll want to know: जी नहीं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जी नहीं** (*jī nahīṁ*) — "no", said politely.

It pairs with the **जी हाँ** you know: **जी** makes either answer respectful.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Come in, please come, here you are, by all means, and a polite no.
