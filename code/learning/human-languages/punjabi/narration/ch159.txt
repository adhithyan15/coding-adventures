Lesson 1 of 4.
A later correction stays on another page — a short unscored return to keeping a later repair separate.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up and Guided Practice.

The earlier timed form remains the first-attempt evidence. This little return practises only one known line and its later repair; it is not another scored form or a complete A1 mock.

Warm-up.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Cover the earlier answer.
[pause 4 seconds]
Read the familiar label ਨਾਂ and remember the A, ਕ card value. No new card or person is being introduced.

Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Set a 45-second practice clock. Write the one known line in Gurmukhi, then stop when the clock ends:
ਨਾਂ (A, ਕ): ______.
After stopping, check ਨਾਂ: ਅਮਨ (nāṁ: aman). If the value or spelling slipped, write one corrected line on a second page. Keep the first practice line and the earlier timed form visible and unchanged. Say which is the later correction; it cannot replace either first attempt.

Wrap-up Recall.
Cover the check.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall the order: first attempt, stop, inspect, then repair separately. The repair helps learning but never raises the first attempt's score.
