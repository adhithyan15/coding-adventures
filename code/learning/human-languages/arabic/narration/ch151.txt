Arabic, chapter 151: Thin, Fat, Wet, Dry, Dirty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نحيف (naḥīf) — thin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for wide, then the Arabic for narrow.
[pause 1 second]
A green dog and a yellow cat: say the four words. (Akhḍar, kalb; aṣfar, qiṭṭ.)

You'll want to know: نحيف.
نحيف — naḥīf — "thin".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: naḥīf]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: naḥīf, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say naḥīf]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for wide, then the Arabic for narrow, then say naḥīf again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does نحيف (naḥīf) mean? ("Thin".) Say it once more.
