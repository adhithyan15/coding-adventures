## You'll want to know: inocente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C390-CULPA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**inocente** — "innocent".

> Ele diz que está inocente.
>
> *He says he is innocent.*

Also naive: *uma pergunta inocente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inocente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *innocēns* "harmless, blameless", built from *in-* "not" and *nocēre* "to harm" (Lewis & Short).
