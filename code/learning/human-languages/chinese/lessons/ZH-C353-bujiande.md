## You'll want to know: 不见得
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C353-POINT353-03]; assesses=[] -->

**不见得** — *bújiànde* — "not necessarily".

> *guì de dōngxi bújiànde hǎo* — Expensive things aren't necessarily good.

The tone of bù changes here: before a falling tone it is said bú, and the spelling writes the change.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**不见得** (*bújiànde*) is built from 不 'not', 见 'to see' and 得 'to get', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjut*, *kenH* and *tok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as another word for not, the second 'to see' and the third 'gain'. How the three came to mean not necessarily is not settled.
