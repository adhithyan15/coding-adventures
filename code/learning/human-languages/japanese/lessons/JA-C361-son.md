## You'll want to know: そん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C361-MOUKERU361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**そん** — *son* — "a loss; a disadvantage".

> **いま うったら、そんを するでしょう。** — *ima uttara, son o suru deshō* — "If you sell now, you will probably make a loss."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そん** (*son*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *son*, "loss" (Middle Chinese *swonX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
