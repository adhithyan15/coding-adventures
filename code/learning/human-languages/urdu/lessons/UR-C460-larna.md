## You'll want to know: لڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C460-BRAVE460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لڑنا** — *laṛnā* — "to fight; to stand up (to)".

> وہ اپنے حق کے لیے لڑا۔
>
> *voh apne haq ke liye laṛā.*
>
> He fought for his rights.

Past (she …): وہ بیماری سے لڑی, *voh bīmārī se laṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لڑنا** (*laṛnā*) is inherited from *laḍ*, "fight", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10920).
