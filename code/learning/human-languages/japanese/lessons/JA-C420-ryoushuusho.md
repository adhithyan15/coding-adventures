## You'll want to know: りょうしゅうしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C420-RYOUSHUUSHO420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**りょうしゅうしょ** — *ryōshūsho* — "a receipt".

> **りょうしゅうしょを ください。** — *ryōshūsho o kudasai* — "A receipt, please."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りょうしゅうしょ** (*ryōshūsho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ryō*, "jurisdiction" (Middle Chinese *ljengX*), *shū*, "obtain" (*syuw*) and *sho*, "write" (*syo*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
