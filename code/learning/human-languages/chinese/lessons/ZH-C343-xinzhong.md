## You'll want to know: 心中
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C343-FARAWAY343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**心中** — *xīnzhōng* — "in one's heart".

> **在我心中这是最好的中秋** *zài wǒ xīnzhōng zhè shì zuì hǎo de Zhōngqiū* — To my mind, this is the best moon festival ever.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**心中** (*xīnzhōng*) is built from 心 'the heart' and 中 'the middle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sim* and *trjuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
