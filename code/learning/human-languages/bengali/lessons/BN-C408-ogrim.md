## You'll want to know: অগ্রিম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C408-BASA408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অগ্রিম** — *ôgrim* — "an advance (payment); in advance".

> বাসার জন্য দুই মাসের ভাড়া অগ্রিম দিতে হবে।
>
> *bāsār jônyô dui māser bhāṛā ôgrim dite hôbe.*
>
> For the flat you have to pay two months' rent in advance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অগ্রিম** (*ôgrim*) is Sanskrit *agrima*, "being in front, preceding" (Monier-Williams), from *agra*, the front: money paid before.
