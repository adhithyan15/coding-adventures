## Added — the A2 vocabulary, chapters 158-277; Chinese attains A2

- Four tranches of 30 chapters of five words each (600 words, 218 verbs), every
  chapter on an A1 or A2 spine node, with reviews closing each block: home, food,
  clothes and the daily round (158-187); money, shops, dates, roads, tickets and
  services (188-217); weather, nature, the body and seeing a doctor (218-247); and
  school, work, family, feelings and opinions (248-277).
- 48 writing lessons, each directly after the word whose headword first shows the
  character, with Hanzi Writer Data's stroke order and a stroke-by-stroke strip:
  房 米 衣 茶 事 觉 杯 候 面 常 间 冷 (chapters 158-182), 钱 号 店 回 路 票 找 关 东 离 公 地
  (188-212), 风 云 雪 花 草 头 疼 身 体 医 西 现 (218-236) and 校 高 读 作 写 孩 工 热 假 笑 先 老
  (248-272).
- 126 words are headed in characters the reader writes; 474 are taught by ear with
  pinyin headwords. Examples print hanzi only for written characters.
- The independent reviews replaced transparent combinations of taught words (后面
  beside 后边, 十字路口 beside 路口, 大号 / 小号, 搬家 beside 搬, 大风, 好玩, 读书, 写字,
  老人 and others) with words of their own, reworded examples that put 个 with
  shops and buildings or needed untaught grammar, swapped the Taiwan 冷气 for the
  mainland 空调, and kept every origin to what the Guangyun gives, with a
  sense-tagged slug wherever a character is a different word from the corpus's
  bare slug (按 anh-press, 湖 hu-lake, 裝 tsrjang-pack, 宝 pawx-treasure and others);
  合同's 同 is duwng-same, as in 同学.
- Stroke text checked against the medians: 读 is the speech piece with 十 over 买,
  西's left stroke runs down the box's whole side, 东's hooked vertical starts above
  the turned stroke, and 钱's first stroke is long.
- 洗脸 (chapter 99) no longer says the Guangyun has 臉 only as *leamX*: it lists 臉
  under two readings, and the lesson now says it glosses *leamX* as a kind of stew.
- With chapters 147-277 Chinese attains A2: 1,290 headwords and 403 verbs at or
  below A2, all five A2 spine nodes and every A2 writing stage.
