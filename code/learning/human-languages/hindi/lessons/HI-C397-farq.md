## You'll want to know: फ़र्क़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C397-VISHAY397-04]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़र्क़** — *farq* — "a difference".

> अगर तुम रोज़ एक घंटा पढ़ोगे तो बड़ा फ़र्क़ पड़ेगा।
>
> *agar tum roz ek ghaṇṭā paṛhoge to baṛā farq paṛegā.*
>
> If you study for an hour every day, it will make a big difference.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़र्क़** (*farq*) is usually traced to Arabic *farq*, "a separation, a difference", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
