Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 63: String bed, Cupboard, Lamp, Fan, Key.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खाट (khāṭ) — a string bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a wall, then the Marwadi for a roof.

You'll want to know: खाट.
खाट — khāṭ — "a string bed".

The word, taken apart.
खाट (khāṭ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khaṭvā, "bedstead". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 3781); the match is through Hindi khāṭ and Gujarati khāṭ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पंखो (paṅkho) — a fan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a cupboard, then the Marwadi for a lamp.

You'll want to know: पंखो.
पंखो — paṅkho — "a fan".

The word, taken apart.
पंखो (paṅkho) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pakṣa, "wing, feather; side". CDIAL lists Old Marwari pāṃkha under it (CDIAL 7627). Sanskrit pakṣaka already meant "fan"; CDIAL asks whether the nasal comes from a blend with puṅkha, "feathered end of an arrow".
