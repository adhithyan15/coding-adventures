# నిరాశపడు (nirāśapaḍu) — to be disappointed

## You'll want to know: నిరాశపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C502-NIRASA502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నిరాశపడు** — *nirāśapaḍu* — "to be disappointed".

> వర్షం వల్ల ఆట రద్దు అయింది, పిల్లలు నిరాశపడ్డారు.
>
> *varṣaṁ valla āṭa raddu ayindi, pillalu nirāśapaḍḍāru.*
>
> The game was called off because of rain; the children were disappointed.

Past (I …): నిరాశపడ్డాను, *nirāśapaḍḍānu*.

నిరాశ, disappointment, + పడు. నిరాశపరచు, to disappoint someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిరాశపడు** (*nirāśapaḍu*) is built from నిరాశ, from Sanskrit *nirāśa*, "without hope, despondent" (Monier-Williams) and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
