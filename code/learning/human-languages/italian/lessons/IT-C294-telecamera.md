## You'll want to know: la telecamera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C294-PATTUGLIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la telecamera** — "the video camera, the CCTV camera".

> Le telecamere hanno ripreso tutto.
>
> *The cameras filmed everything.*

For photos, *la macchina fotografica*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*telecamera* joins Greek *tēle* "far" and *camera*, from Latin *camera* "a vault, a room" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB links it with English *television camera*.
