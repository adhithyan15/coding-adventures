## You'll want to know: xīnwén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C290-NEWS290-01]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnwén* — "the news; news on TV and radio".

> *wǒ měitiān tīng xīnwén* — I listen to the news every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnwén* is built from *xīn* 'new' and *wén* 'to hear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sin* and *mjun* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'new, as against old' and the second 'to know by its sound': what is newly heard.
