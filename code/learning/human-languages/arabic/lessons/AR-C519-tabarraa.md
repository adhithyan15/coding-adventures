## You'll want to know: تبرع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C519-WAZIFA519-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تبرع** — *tabarraʿa* — "to donate".

> تبرع أبي بالمال للمدرسة.
>
> *tabarraʿa abī bi-l-māl li-l-madrasa.*
>
> My father donated money to the school.

Present: يتبرع, *yatabarraʿ*.

It takes بـ for the gift: تبرع بالدم, to give blood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تبرع** (*tabarraʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-r-ʿ*, "to excel, to surpass" (Lane). Form V tabarraʿa is to give more than is asked: to give freely.
