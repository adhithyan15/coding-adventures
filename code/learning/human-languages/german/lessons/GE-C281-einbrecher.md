## You'll want to know: der Einbrecher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C281-EINBRUCH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Einbrecher** — "the burglar".

> Der Einbrecher kam durch das Fenster.
>
> *The burglar came through the window.*

Plural: *die Einbrecher*. Feminine *die Einbrecherin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Einbrecher* is built on *einbrechen* "to break in", from *brechen* "to break" (EtymDB), with the ending *-er* for a person.
