Gujarati, chapter 210: Papers and baggage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
એરપોર્ટ (erporṭ) — airport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get stuck, then the Gujarati for to slide.

You'll want to know: એરપોર્ટ.
એરપોર્ટ — erporṭ — "airport".
અમે બપોરે એરપોર્ટ ગયા. — ame bapore erporṭ gayā — "We went to the airport at midday."

The word, taken apart.
એરપોર્ટ (erporṭ) is a loan from English airport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for airport, then the Gujarati for passport.

You'll want to know: પરવાનો.
પરવાનો — parvāno — "permit, licence".
ડ્રાઈવર પાસે પરવાનો ન હતો. — ḍraivar pāse parvāno na hato — "The driver had no licence."

The word, taken apart.
પરવાનો (parvāno) is a loan, usually traced to Persian parvāna, "warrant, licence". CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
લાદવું (lādvũ) — to load.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for passport, then the Gujarati for permit.

You'll want to know: લાદવું.
લાદવું — lādvũ — "to load".
અમે સામાન ગાડીમાં લાદ્યો. — ame sāmān gāḍīmā̃ lādyo — "We loaded the luggage into the car."

The word, taken apart.
લાદવું (lādvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit lardayati, "loads", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to heap up, load" (CDIAL 10966).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for permit, then the Gujarati for to load.

You'll want to know: ઊંચકવું.
ઊંચકવું — ū̃cakvũ — "to lift, to carry".
તેણે ભારે થેલી ઊંચકી. — teṇe bhāre thelī ū̃cakī — "She lifted the heavy bag."

The word, taken apart.
ઊંચકવું (ū̃cakvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to raise", under Sanskrit ucca, "tall, lofty", built with the suffix -akka- (CDIAL 1634).
