Marathi, chapter 308: Generations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पिढी (piḍhī) — a generation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a period, then the Marathi for a remembrance.

You'll want to know: पिढी.
पिढी — piḍhī — "a generation".
आमच्या घरात तीन पिढ्या एकत्र राहतात. āmacyā gharāt tīn piḍhyā ekatra rāhtāt. Three generations live together in our house.
Feminine. Plural: पिढ्या, piḍhyā.
Plural पिढ्या; पुढची पिढी (piḍhī), the next generation.

The word, taken apart.
पिढी (piḍhī) is the Marathi word for a generation; its origin is not settled.

आमचे पूर्वज या गावात तीनशे वर्षांपूर्वी आले. āmce pūrvaj yā gāvāt tīnśe varṣāmpūrvī āle. Our ancestors came to this village three hundred years ago.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पूर्वज (pūrvaj) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pūrvaja, 'born before; an ancestor' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
वारसा (vārsā) — heritage; an inheritance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a generation, then the Marathi for an ancestor.

You'll want to know: वारसा.
वारसा — vārsā — "heritage; an inheritance".
हा किल्ला आपला ऐतिहासिक वारसा आहे. hā killā āplā aitihāsik vārsā āhe. This fort is our historical heritage.
Masculine.
वारसा (vārsā) जपणे, to cherish a heritage.

The word, taken apart.
वारसा (vārsā) is built on vāras, 'an heir', a loan from Persian vāris, usually traced to Arabic wārith, 'an heir'.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

वंश — vaṁś — "a lineage; a dynasty".
त्या घराण्याचा वंश अजून चालू आहे. tyā gharāṇyācā vaṁś ajūn cālū āhe. That family's line still continues.
Masculine.
राजवंश, a royal dynasty.

The word, taken apart.
वंश (vaṁś) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vaṁśa, 'a bamboo; a lineage' (Monier-Williams): a family line grows joint by joint, like bamboo.

Lesson 5 of 5.
जतन करणे (jatan karṇe) — to preserve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for heritage, then the Marathi for a lineage.

You'll want to know: जतन करणे.
जतन करणे — jatan karṇe — "to preserve".
संग्रहालयात जुनी नाणी जतन केली आहेत. saṅgrahālayāt junī nāṇī jatan kelī āhet. Old coins have been preserved in the museum.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने जतन केलं, tyāne jatan kelaṁ.
Formal; what is kept agrees: परंपरा जतन केली.

The word, taken apart.
जतन करणे (jatan karṇe) joins jatan, 'careful keeping', to karṇe, 'to do'. jatan is usually traced to Sanskrit yatna, 'effort' (Monier-Williams), which Prakrit has as jatta (CDIAL 10404a); CDIAL gives no Marathi form.
