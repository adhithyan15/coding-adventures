# താമസക്കാരൻ (tāmasakkāran) — a resident; a lodger

## You'll want to know: താമസക്കാരൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C481-TAMASAM481-04]; assesses=[] -->

**താമസക്കാരൻ** — *tāmasakkāran* — "a resident; a lodger".

> ആ വീട്ടിലെ പുതിയ താമസക്കാരൻ ഒരു അധ്യാപകനാണ്.
>
> *ā vīṭṭile putiya tāmasakkāran oru adhyāpakanāṇŭ.*
>
> The new resident of that house is a teacher.

Plural: താമസക്കാർ, *tāmasakkār*.

The ending -കാരൻ means one who: താമസം, a stay, so one who stays. The plural is താമസക്കാർ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**താമസക്കാരൻ** (*tāmasakkāran*) is built from താമസം, a stay, usually traced to Sanskrit *tāmasa*, "dark, inert" (CDIAL 5775), with -കാരൻ, the Malayalam ending for a person who does something.
