## You'll want to know: போதை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C491-PAZHAKKAM491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**போதை** — *pōtai* — "intoxication; addiction".

> போதைப் பழக்கம் குடும்பத்தைக் கெடுக்கும்.
>
> *pōtaip paḻakkam kuṭumpattaik keṭukkum.*
>
> Addiction ruins families.

போதைப் பழக்கம், an addiction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **போதை** (*pōtai*) is not settled; we could not find it in DEDR or CDIAL.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
