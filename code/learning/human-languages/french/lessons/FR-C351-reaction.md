## You'll want to know: la réaction
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C351-REACTION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la réaction** — "the reaction".

> Sa réaction m'a surpris.
>
> *Her reaction surprised me.*

Feminine: *une réaction*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réaction* comes from Medieval Latin *reactiō* (EtymDB), built from *re-* "back" and *āctiō* "a doing", from *agere* "to do" (Lewis & Short).
