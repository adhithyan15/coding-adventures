## You'll want to know: ख़ूब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C387-BEHAD387-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ूब** — *khūb* — "plenty, a lot".

> अगर ख़ूब बारिश होगी तो फ़सल अच्छी होगी।
>
> *agar khūb bāriś hogī to fasal acchī hogī.*
>
> If there is plenty of rain, the harvest will be good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ूब** (*khūb*) is usually traced to Persian *khūb*, "good, fine".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
