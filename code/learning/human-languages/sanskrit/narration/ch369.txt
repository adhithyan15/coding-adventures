Sanskrit, chapter 369: Luck and Fate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भाग्यम् (bhāgyam) — luck; fortune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an inference, then the Sanskrit for so they say.

You'll want to know: भाग्यम्.
भाग्यम् — bhāgyam — "luck; fortune".
यदि भाग्यम् अस्ति, तर्हि सर्वं सुखं भविष्यति। yadi bhāgyam asti, tarhi sarvaṃ sukhaṃ bhaviṣyati. If we have luck, all will be well.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
भाग्यम् (bhāgyam) comes from the root √bhaj, "to share" (MW takes bhāgya as "to be shared"), and MW gives the neuter bhāgya "fate, destiny, fortune, good fortune" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अकस्मात् (akasmāt) — by accident; without warning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for luck, then the Sanskrit for fate.

You'll want to know: अकस्मात्.
अकस्मात् — akasmāt — "by accident; without warning".
यदि अकस्मात् वर्षा भवति, तर्हि अहं गृहे स्थास्यामि। yadi akasmāt varṣā bhavati, tarhi ahaṃ gṛhe sthāsyāmi. If it rains without warning, I will stay at home.

The word, taken apart.
अकस्मात् (akasmāt) is built from a-, "not", and kasmāt, "from what? why?"; MW gives a-kasmāt "without a why or a wherefore, accidentally, suddenly" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
उपलभते (upalabhate) — comes by; obtains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for fate, then the Sanskrit for by accident.

You'll want to know: उपलभते.
उपलभते — upalabhate — "comes by; obtains".
यदि भाग्यम् अस्ति, तर्हि सः धनम् उपलभते। yadi bhāgyam asti, tarhi saḥ dhanam upalabhate. If he is lucky, he comes by money.
Past (he …): सः धनम् उपलब्धवान्, saḥ dhanam upalabdhavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपलभते (upalabhate) is built on upa, "towards", and the root √labh, "to take, to get"; MW gives upa-√labh "to acquire, to receive, to obtain, to find" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सम्भावना (sambhāvanā) — a likelihood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for by accident, then the Sanskrit for comes by.

You'll want to know: सम्भावना.
सम्भावना — sambhāvanā — "a likelihood".
यदि आकाशः कृष्णः, तर्हि वर्षायाः सम्भावना अस्ति। yadi ākāśaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ, tarhi varṣāyāḥ sambhāvanā asti. If the sky is dark, rain is likely.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सम्भावना (sambhāvanā) is built on sam and the root √bhū, "to be", in its causative form; MW gives sam-bhāvanā "imagination, supposition, assumption", and "a likelihood" is its modern sense (MW).
