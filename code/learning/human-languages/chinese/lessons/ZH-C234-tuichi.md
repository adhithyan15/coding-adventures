## You'll want to know: tuīchí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C234-RAIN234-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tuīchí* — "to put off; to postpone".

> *míngtiān xiàyǔ wǒmen de yěcān yào tuīchí* — It's going to rain tomorrow, so our picnic will have to wait.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuīchí* is built from *tuī* 'to push' and *chí* 'slow', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thwoj* and *drij* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'slow' and 'leisurely'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
