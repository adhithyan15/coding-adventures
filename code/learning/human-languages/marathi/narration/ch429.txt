Marathi, chapter 429: Auctions and Pledges.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लिलाव (lilāv) — an auction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for what one can afford, then the Marathi for economical.

You'll want to know: लिलाव.
लिलाव — lilāv — "an auction".
जुन्या गाड्यांचा लिलाव उद्या आहे. junyā gāḍyāñcā lilāv udyā āhe. The auction of old cars is tomorrow.
Masculine.
लिलाव (lilāv) करणे, to auction; लिलावात घेणे, to buy at auction.

The word, taken apart.
लिलाव (lilāv) is a loan from Portuguese leilão, "an auction", from the trade of the west coast.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तारण (tāraṇ) — collateral; security.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for economical, then the Marathi for an auction.

You'll want to know: तारण.
तारण — tāraṇ — "collateral; security".
त्याने कर्जासाठी शेत तारण ठेवलं. tyāne karjāsāṭhī śet tāraṇ ṭhevlaṁ. He put up his field as security for the loan.
Neuter.
तारण (tāraṇ) ठेवणे, to give as security for a loan.

The word, taken apart.
तारण (tāraṇ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi tāraṇ, "deposit made against a debt", under Sanskrit tāraṇa, "crossing, safe passage" (CDIAL 5795).

Lesson 3 of 5.
गहाण ठेवणे (gahāṇ ṭhevṇe) — to pawn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an auction, then the Marathi for collateral.

You'll want to know: गहाण ठेवणे.
गहाण ठेवणे — gahāṇ ṭhevṇe — "to pawn".
तिने सोन्याची बांगडी गहाण ठेवली. tine sonyācī bāṅgḍī gahāṇ ṭhevlī. She pawned her gold bangle.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने गहाण ठेवलं, tyāne gahāṇ ṭhevlaṁ.
The verb agrees with the thing pawned: बांगडी ठेवली, घर ठेवलं.

The word, taken apart.
गहाण ठेवणे (gahāṇ ṭhevṇe) joins गहाण, "a pledge, a pawn", inherited from Sanskrit grahaṇa, "seizing, holding", where CDIAL lists Marathi gahāṇ (CDIAL 4364), to ठेवणे, "to put".

Lesson 4 of 5.
वसूल करणे (vasūl karṇe) — to recover money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for collateral, then the Marathi for to pawn.

You'll want to know: वसूल करणे.
वसूल करणे — vasūl karṇe — "to recover money".
कंपनीने थकबाकी वसूल केली. kampanīne thakbākī vasūl kelī. The company recovered the arrears.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वसूल केलं, tyāne vasūl kelaṁ.
The money recovered is the object; वसूल होणे, to be recovered.

The word, taken apart.
वसूल करणे (vasūl karṇe) joins वसूल, "recovered, collected", a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic wuṣūl, "arrival, receipt", to करणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
भाडेपट्टा (bhāḍepaṭṭā) — a lease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to pawn, then the Marathi for to recover money.

You'll want to know: भाडेपट्टा.
भाडेपट्टा — bhāḍepaṭṭā — "a lease".
त्यांनी जमीन दहा वर्षांच्या भाडेपट्ट्याने दिली. tyānnī jamīn dahā varṣāñcyā bhāḍepaṭṭyāne dilī. They gave the land on a ten-year lease.
Masculine.
भाडेपट्ट्याने, on lease; पट्टा here is a written deed.

The word, taken apart.
भाडेपट्टा (bhāḍepaṭṭā) is built from भाडे, "rent", and paṭṭā, "a strip; a written deed"; the origin of भाडे is not settled.
