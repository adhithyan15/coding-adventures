## Chapters 193-222: 150 more A2 headwords

The second of four Marathi A2 vocabulary tranches. Thirty chapters of five
words each, in four runs of at most nine chapters. Each run closes with two
reviews.

- **Chapters 193-199: thirty-five verbs**, including *खर्च करणे*,
  *बचत करणे*, *मागवणे*, *इस्त्री करणे*, *चर्चा करणे*, *रक्षण करणे*,
  *तुलना करणे*, *तयारी करणे*, *उसने देणे*, *गप्पा मारणे*, *पोचवणे*,
  *स्वागत करणे* and *भेट देणे*.
- **Chapter 200: wounds and colds** (*स्नायू*, *जखम*, *व्रण*, *सर्दी*,
  *चिठ्ठी*).
- **Chapter 201: time** (*पर्यंत*, *दशक*, *सूर्यास्त*, *सूर्योदय*, *शेवट*).
- **Chapter 202: place** (*सीमा*, *प्रदेश*, *राजधानी*, *उपनगर*, *वरती*).
- **Chapter 203: joining ideas** (*नक्की*, *खरोखर*, *जवळजवळ*, *सुमारे*,
  *फक्त*).
- **Chapters 204-207: twenty qualities**, such as *गंभीर*, *शूर*,
  *उत्सुक*, *मोफत*, *आधुनिक*, *सार्वजनिक*, *खाजगी*, *व्यस्त* and *मोकळा*.
- **Chapters 208-222: seventy-five nouns**:
  - war and peace (*युद्ध*, *शांतता*, *सैन्य*);
  - trades (*दंतवैद्य*, *कारागीर*, *कामगार*);
  - choices (*अपयश*, *अनुभव*, *निवड*, *निर्णय*);
  - the post (*अर्ज*, *पाकीट*, *पत्ता*);
  - the house (*कारंजे*, *फाटक*, *तळघर*, *माळा*);
  - materials (*कापूस*, *लोकर*, *रेशीम*);
  - the Marathi kitchen (*श्रीखंड*, *ताक*, *हळद*, *जिरे*);
  - the harbour and the well (*बंदर*, *विहीर*);
  - weather (*धुके*, *दव*);
  - trade (*व्यापार*, *दुकानदार*, *ग्राहक*);
  - the face (*पापणी*, *भुवई*, *अश्रू*);
  - school and work (*अहवाल*, *विषय*, *खडू*, *शब्दकोश*, *विद्यापीठ*).
- **Glosses.** *शूर* is glossed "valiant, brave", against the taught
  *धाडसी*, and *बचत करणे* is "to put money by", against *वाचवणे*.
- **Book.** It passes page 999, so the preamble widens the contents
  page-number box: `\@pnumwidth` goes to 2.4em and `\@tocrmarg` to 3.4em, as
  in the other A2 books. With the narrower title column, chapter 222's
  contents line ("Career, Monthly magazine, …") set loose, so it is titled
  "Career, Magazine, Dictionary, University, Mistake". The strict compile is
  clean at 1305 pages.
- **Pins:** the Marathi lesson-content budget goes from 1030 to 1188, and the
  per-chapter size pin gains chapters 193-222.
- **Level gate at A2:** vocabulary 399 short -> 249.
