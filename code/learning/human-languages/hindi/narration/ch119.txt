Hindi, chapter 119: Work and Marriage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नौकरी (naukrī) — a job.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a wife, then the Hindi for a relative.

You'll want to know: नौकरी.
नौकरी (naukrī) — "a job", employment.
आपकी नौकरी (naukrī) कहाँ है? — "where is your job?", "where do you work?".

What you've built.
The second question after a name.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: naukrī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: naukrī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask āpkī naukrī kahā̃ hai?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for a wife, then the Hindi for a relative, then say naukrī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does नौकरी (naukrī) mean? ("A job".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
शादीशुदा (śādīśudā) — married.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a profession, then the Hindi for a wedding.

You'll want to know: शादीशुदा.
शादीशुदा (śādīśudā) — "married".
It is शादी with -शुदा, "having been done". क्या आप शादीशुदा (śādīśudā) हैं? — "are you married?".

What you've built.
A question you will be asked early and often.

Lesson 5 of 5.
जन्मदिन (janmdin) — a birthday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a wedding, then the Hindi for married.

You'll want to know: जन्मदिन.
जन्मदिन (janmdin) — "a birthday", literally "birth-day".
दिन is the day you know. जन्मदिन (janmdin) की शुभकामनाएँ — "happy birthday", with the good wishes you have.

What you've built.
A job, a profession, a wedding, married, a birthday.
