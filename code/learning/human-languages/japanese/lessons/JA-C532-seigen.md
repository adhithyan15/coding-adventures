## You'll want to know: せいげん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C532-LIMIT532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**せいげん** — *seigen* — "a limit; a restriction".

> **もし じかんの せいげんが あったら、おしえて ください。** — *moshi jikan no seigen ga attara, oshiete kudasai* — "If there is a time limit, please tell me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいげん** (*seigen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "rule" (Middle Chinese *tsyejH*) and *gen*, "limit" (*heanX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
