## You'll want to know: قوالی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C519-DHOL519-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قوالی** — *qavvālī* — "qawwali (devotional song)".

> ہم نے کل رات قوالی سنی۔
>
> *ham ne kal rāt qavvālī sunī.*
>
> Last night we listened to qawwali.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قوالی** (*qavvālī*) is built on *qavvāl*, "a qawwali singer", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qawwāl*, "one who says much", with the noun ending *-ī*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
