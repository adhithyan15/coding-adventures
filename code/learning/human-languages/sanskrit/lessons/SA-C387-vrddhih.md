## You'll want to know: वृद्धिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C387-UNNATI387-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वृद्धिः** — *vṛddhiḥ* — "growth; increase".

> यदि वर्षा उत्तमा, तर्हि धान्यस्य वृद्धिः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi varṣā uttamā, tarhi dhānyasya vṛddhiḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If the rain is good, the grain will grow well.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वृद्धिः** (*vṛddhiḥ*) comes from the root √vṛdh, "to grow"; MW gives *vṛddhi* "growth, increase, augmentation, rise" (MW).
