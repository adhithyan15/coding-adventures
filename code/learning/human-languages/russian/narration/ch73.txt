Russian, chapter 73: Below, Left, Right, A Lot, A Little.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
внизу (vnizú) — below, downstairs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for near, then the Russian for up above.

You'll want to know: внизу.
внизу (vnizú) — "below, downstairs".

The word, taken apart.
внизу (vnizú) is в, "in", with низу, a form of низ, "the bottom". Vasmer explains низ as an extension of the PIE ni, "down", in Sanskrit ni-, "down", and Old High German nidar, "down": English nether is a cousin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
слева (sléva) — on the left.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for up above, then the Russian for below.

You'll want to know: слева.
слева (sléva) — "on the left".

The word, taken apart.
слева (sléva) is с, "from", with лева, a form of левый, "left": "from the left". левый from Proto-Slavic lěvŭ, related to Latin laevus and Greek laiós, both "left" (Vasmer).

You'll want to know: справа.

The word, taken apart.
справа (správa) is с, "from", with права, a form of правый, "right", first "straight, righteous". Vasmer derives правый, probably, from prō-vos, "forward", related to Latin probus, "upright, good".

Lesson 4 of 5.
много (mnógo) — a lot, many.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for on the left, then the Russian for on the right.

You'll want to know: много.
много (mnógo) — "a lot, many".

The word, taken apart.
много (mnógo) from Proto-Slavic mŭnogŭ, "much, many" — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Gothic manags, "many", and Old Irish menicc, "frequent", and finds no grounds for a Germanic loan: English many is a cousin.

Lesson 5 of 5.
мало (málo) — a little, few.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for on the right, then the Russian for a lot.

You'll want to know: мало.
мало (málo) — "a little, few".

The word, taken apart.
мало (málo) is малый, "small", made into an adverb with -о. малый is a cousin, Vasmer says, of Gothic smals, "small", and so of English small.
