## You'll want to know: artifex, artificis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C376-OPUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**artifex, artificis** — "an artist, a skilled craftsman; a master (of)".

> Artifex statuam pulchram fēcit.
>
> *The artist made a beautiful statue.*

*artifex, artificis* m. Built from *ars* and *faciō*: a maker by skill. *Artifex dīcendī*, a master of speaking. English *artifice* and *artificial*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*artifex* is built from *ars, artis* "skill" and *faciō* "to make" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *artifice* and *artificial* come from its family.
