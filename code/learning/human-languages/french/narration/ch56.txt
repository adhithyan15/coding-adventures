French, chapter 56: Sad, Pleased, Daughter, Grandfather, Grandmother.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
triste — sad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for sadness, then the French for happy.

You'll want to know: triste.
triste — "sad". The adjective beside la tristesse.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
