## You'll want to know: بڑھاپا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C492-OLDAGE492-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بڑھاپا** — *buṛhāpā* — "old age".

> بڑھاپے میں نیند کم ہو جاتی ہے۔
>
> *buṛhāpe meṅ nīṅd kam ho jātī hai.*
>
> In old age people sleep less.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بڑھاپا** (*buṛhāpā*) is inherited: it is built on **بوڑھا**, "old", from *buḍḍha*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9271), with the abstract ending *-āpā*.
