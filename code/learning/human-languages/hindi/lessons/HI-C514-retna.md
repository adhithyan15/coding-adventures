## You'll want to know: रेतना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C514-SUNAR514-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रेतना** — *retnā* — "to file (metal or wood)".

> लोहार ने चाबी का किनारा रेता।
>
> *Lohār ne cābī kā kinārā retā.*
>
> The blacksmith filed the edge of the key.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कील रेती, *usne kīl retī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेतना** (*retnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *rētra*, "perfumed powder" (CDIAL 10816), the entry under which CDIAL lists Hindi *ret*, "sand, a file", and *retnā*, "to file".
