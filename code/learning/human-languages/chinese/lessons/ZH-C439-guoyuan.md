## You'll want to know: guǒyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C439-COUNTRY439-02]; assesses=[] -->

*guǒyuán* — "orchard".

> *zhōumò wǒmen kāichē qù guǒyuán* — At the weekend we're driving out to the orchard.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guǒyuán* is built from 果 'fruit' and *yuán* 'a garden', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaX* and *hjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the fruit of a tree and the second as a garden.
