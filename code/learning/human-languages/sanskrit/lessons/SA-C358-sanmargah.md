## You'll want to know: सन्मार्गः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C358-ACHARA358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्मार्गः** — *sanmārgaḥ* — "the right path".

> गुरुः शिष्यं सन्मार्गं नयति।
>
> *guruḥ śiṣyaṃ sanmārgaṃ nayati.*
>
> The teacher leads his pupil on the right path.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्मार्गः** (*sanmārgaḥ*) is built from *sat*, "good", and *mārga*, "a path"; MW gives *san-mārga* "the right path" (MW).
