## You'll want to know: つく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C198-RIDE198-05]; assesses=[] -->

**つく** — *tsuku* — "to get to; to arrive at".

> **でんしゃは ゆうがた えきに つきます。** — *densha wa yūgata eki ni tsukimasu* — "The train gets to the station in the evening."

Polite **つきます**, *tsukimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つく** (*tsuku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
