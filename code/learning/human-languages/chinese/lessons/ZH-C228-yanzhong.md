## You'll want to know: yánzhòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C228-ACHE228-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yánzhòng* — "serious (of illness); serious (of a problem)".

> *yīshēng shuō bù yánzhòng* — The doctor says it isn't serious.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yánzhòng* is built from *yán* 'stern' and 重 'heavy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjaem* and *drjowngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'stern' and 'awe-inspiring'.
