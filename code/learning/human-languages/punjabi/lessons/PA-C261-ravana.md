# ਰਵਾਨਾ (ravānā) — departing; leaving, with ਹੋਣਾ

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C261-TRAIN261-03, PA-LEX-C261-TRAIN261-04] -->

## You'll want to know: ਰਵਾਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C261-TRAIN261-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਵਾਨਾ** — *ravānā* — "departing; leaving, with ਹੋਣਾ".

> ਰੇਲ ਸਾਢੇ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਰਵਾਨਾ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *rel sāḍhe das vaje ravānā hundī hai.*
>
> The train leaves at half past ten.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਵਾਨਾ** (*ravānā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *ravāna*, "going, setting out".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
