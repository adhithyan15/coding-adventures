Lesson 1 of 5.
आंदोलन (āndolan) — a movement; an agitation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a subsidy, then the Marathi for economic.

You'll want to know: आंदोलन.
आंदोलन — āndolan — "a movement; an agitation".
पाण्यासाठी गावात मोठं आंदोलन झालं. pāṇyāsāṭhī gāvāt moṭhaṁ āndolan jhālaṁ. There was a big agitation in the village for water.
Neuter.
आंदोलन (āndolan) करणे, to agitate; आंदोलक, an agitator.

The word, taken apart.
आंदोलन (āndolan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit āndolana, "swinging, oscillation" (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for a movement that stirs people.

Lesson 4 of 5.
निषेध करणे (niṣedh karṇe) — to protest; to condemn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a demonstration, then the Marathi for a hunger strike.

You'll want to know: निषेध करणे.
निषेध करणे — niṣedh karṇe — "to protest; to condemn".
सगळ्यांनी या निर्णयाचा निषेध केला. sagaḷyānnī yā nirṇayācā niṣedh kelā. Everyone condemned this decision.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने निषेध केला, tyāne niṣedh kelā.
What is protested takes -चा: निर्णयाचा निषेध.

The word, taken apart.
निषेध करणे (niṣedh karṇe) joins the tatsama निषेध, Sanskrit niṣedha, "keeping off, prohibition, denial" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: बहिष्कार घालणे.
बहिष्कार घालणे — bahiṣkār ghālṇe — "to boycott".
ग्राहकांनी त्या दुकानावर बहिष्कार घातला. grāhkānnī tyā dukānāvar bahiṣkār ghātlā. The customers boycotted that shop.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बहिष्कार घातला, tyāne bahiṣkār ghātlā.
What is boycotted takes -वर; घालणे's past is घातला.

The word, taken apart.
बहिष्कार घालणे (bahiṣkār ghālṇe) joins the tatsama बहिष्कार, Sanskrit bahiṣkāra, "expulsion" (Monier-Williams), to घालणे, "to put on".
