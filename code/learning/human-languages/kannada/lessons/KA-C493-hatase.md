## You'll want to know: ಹತಾಶೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C493-KOPA493-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹತಾಶೆ** — *hatāśe* — "frustration; despair".

> ಮೂರು ಸಲ ಸೋತ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಹತಾಶೆ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *mūru sala sōta mēle avanige hatāśe āyitu.*
>
> After losing three times, he felt frustrated.

From ಹತ, struck down, and ಆಶೆ, hope: a hope that has been knocked down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹತಾಶೆ** (*hatāśe*) is built from Sanskrit *hatāśa*, "whose hopes are destroyed, desperate" (Monier-Williams): *hata*, "struck", and *āśā*, "hope".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
