[PAUSE 4s] Every verb the book owns can now carry a reason. Say each pair twice
— once with *denn* and the verb in place, once with *weil* and the verb at the
end.

- [YOU SAY: "Ich lerne Deutsch, denn ich wohne in Berlin." → "…, weil ich in
  Berlin wohne."]
- [YOU SAY: "Ich bin müde, denn ich habe keine Zeit." → "…, weil ich keine Zeit
  habe."]
- [YOU SAY: the verbs of taking in and putting out, each given a reason — *lesen,
  schreiben, hören, sehen*]
- [YOU SAY: the verbs of the body — *schlafen, sitzen, stehen, gehen, laufen,
  rennen*]
- [YOU SAY: the pair with a prefix that already goes to the end — *öffnen* and
  *schließen*]
- [YOU SAY: the three things German sends last — a prefix, *nicht*, the verb
  after *weil*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-ODER-01, GE-LEX-ABER-01, GE-LEX-SONDERN-01, GE-LEX-DENN-01, GE-LEX-WEIL-01, GE-GRAMMAR-VERB-LETZT-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Which joiner corrects a negative? (***Sondern***.) Which two give a
reason, and what is the difference? (***Denn***, verb in slot two;
***weil***, verb at the end.) Which of the six is also a tag question?
(***Oder?***) And which one is *die Weile* in disguise? (***Weil***.)
