Lesson 1 of 6.
সম্প্রচার (sômprôchār) — a broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to whisper, then the Bengali for a sensation.

You'll want to know: সম্প্রচার.
সম্প্রচার — sômprôchār — "a broadcast".
রেডিওতে সকাল আটটায় খবরের সম্প্রচার হয়। reḍiote sôkāl āṭṭāy khôbôrer sômprôchār hôy. The news is broadcast on the radio at eight in the morning.

The word, taken apart.
সম্প্রচার (sômprôchār) is a learned word built from Sanskrit sam-, 'together', and pracāra, 'a going forth, a spreading' (Monier-Williams): a message sent out to everyone at once.

এই চ্যানেলে সারাদিন খবর দেখায়। ei chænele sārādin khôbôr dekhāy. This channel shows news all day.

The word, taken apart.
চ্যানেল (chænel) is borrowed from English channel. The চ্যা at the start is read æ, as in ব্যাটারি.

Lesson 3 of 6.
উপস্থাপক (upôsthāpôk) — a presenter; a host.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a broadcast, then the Bengali for a channel.

You'll want to know: উপস্থাপক.
উপস্থাপক — upôsthāpôk — "a presenter; a host".
উপস্থাপক ঝড়ের খবর পড়লেন। upôsthāpôk jhôṛer khôbôr pôṛlen. The presenter read the news about the storm.

The word, taken apart.
উপস্থাপক (upôsthāpôk) is Sanskrit upasthāpaka, 'causing to turn one's attention, reminding' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a presenter', the one who puts the news before you, is the modern one.

Lesson 4 of 6.
সরাসরি (sôrāsôri) — live; directly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a channel, then the Bengali for a presenter.

You'll want to know: সরাসরি.
সরাসরি — sôrāsôri — "live; directly".
টিভিতে খেলাটা সরাসরি দেখাল। ṭibhite khelāṭā sôrāsôri dekhālô. The match was shown live on television.

The word, taken apart.
সরাসরি (sôrāsôri) is usually traced to Persian sarāsar, 'from end to end, throughout'; Bengali uses it for 'straight, directly' and for a broadcast shown live.
