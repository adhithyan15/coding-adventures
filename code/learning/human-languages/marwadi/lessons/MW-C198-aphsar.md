## You'll want to know: अफसर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C198-OFFICE198-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अफसर** — *aphsar* — "an officer".

> अफसर दफ्तर मांय कोनी।
>
> *aphsar daphtar mā̃y konī.*
>
> The officer isn't in the office.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अफसर** (*aphsar*) is borrowed from English *officer*.
