## You'll want to know: आशंकते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C539-AVAHITA539-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आशंकते** — *āśaṅkate* — "suspects; fears".

> यदि माता चौरम् आशंकते, तर्हि सा द्वारं पिदधाति।
>
> *yadi mātā cauram āśaṅkate, tarhi sā dvāraṃ pidadhāti.*
>
> If my mother suspects a thief, she shuts the door.

Past (she …): सा चौरम् आशंकितवती, *sā cauram āśaṅkitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आशंकते** (*āśaṅkate*) is built on *ā* and the root √śaṅk, "to doubt, to fear"; MW gives *āśaṅk* as to suspect, to fear, to doubt (MW).
