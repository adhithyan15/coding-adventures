## You'll want to know: biéde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C92-BIEDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

*biéde* — "something else; other".

> *nǐ yào biéde ma* — Would you like anything else?

> *wǒ xiǎngyào biéde dōngxi* — I'd like something else.

Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biéde* is built from bié 'other; apart' and de, the 的 you write. bié is inherited from Middle Chinese *bjet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'different; to part'; where de comes from is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C91-BUYAO-01] -->

*nǐ yào biéde ma* is the waiter's question; *bú yào, xièxie* is the answer when you have all you need.
