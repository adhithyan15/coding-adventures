## You'll want to know: نسيم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C482-RIH482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نسيم** — *nasīm* — "a breeze".

> شعرنا بنسيم لطيف على الشرفة.
>
> *shaʿarnā bi-nasīm laṭīf ʿalā ash-shurfa.*
>
> We felt a pleasant breeze on the balcony.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نسيم** (*nasīm*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-s-m*, "to blow gently; breath" (Lane).
