## You'll want to know: बहकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C474-AQL474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बहकना** — *bahaknā* — "to go astray".

> बुरे दोस्तों के साथ रहकर अरुण का भाई बहक गया।
>
> *bure dostoṁ ke sāth rahkar Aruṇ kā bhāī bahak gayā.*
>
> Mixing with bad friends, Arun's brother went astray.

Past (he …): वह बहक गया, *vah bahak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहकना** (*bahaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *bahaknā*, "to be misled", under Sanskrit *vyáthatē*, "wavers, is disturbed" (CDIAL 12164).
