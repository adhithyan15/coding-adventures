## You'll want to know: घडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C159-PAS159-05]; assesses=[] -->

**घडणे** — *ghaḍṇe* — "to happen". **काय घडले?** — *kāy ghaḍle* — "What happened?" Its past is **घडले**.
