# ઋ — the standing vowel ṛ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C282-RTU-01, GU-LEX-C48-BODY3-02] -->

- [YOU RECALL: say *a season* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say **હૃદય**, "the heart", and find the sign **ૃ** under its **હ**]

**ઋતુ** opens on the same vowel *ṛ*, with no consonant to carry it.

## You'll want to know: ૃ, standing alone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C48-BODY3-02] -->

| | after a consonant | standing alone |
|---|---|---|
| *ṛ* | **ૃ**, as in **હૃદય** | ? |

The missing letter is **ઋ**. Its print has three parts: a bent left body, a separate central stem with a foot, and a right loop flowing into a descending tail.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-VOCALIC-R-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ઋ** — *ṛ*

Keep **ઋ** visible. Follow the numbered strip:

1. sweep right along the upper left body and turn back down-left
2. lift and descend the central stem into its foot
3. lift again, circle the right loop, and finish through the descending tail

Three strokes, two pen lifts: a verified three-stroke teaching-app variant fitted to the bundled printed outline (t30apps.com, ‘Gujarati Alphabet Writing Practice’, version 1.0, ઋ animation, first through third SVG paths).

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-VOCALIC-R-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ઋ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice the two lifts: before the stem, and before the loop.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-VOCALIC-R-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ઋ** once. Compare only two things: are there three parts, and does the loop come last? Repair one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-VOCALIC-R-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id": "GU-W53-vocalic-r-letter-recall", "kind": "text", "assesses": ["GU-SCRIPT-C282-VOCALIC-R-01"], "prompt": "Hide the model and write the standing vowel r from memory.", "answer": "ઋ", "accepted": [], "feedback": {"correct": "ઋ — the left body, the stem, then the loop and tail.", "incorrect": "Look once at ઋ, hide it, and repair only the part that differs."}, "response_seconds": 20} -->

Hide the model and write the standing vowel *ṛ* once. Uncover **ઋ**, compare, and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-VOCALIC-R-01, GU-LEX-C282-RTU-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **ૃ ઋ ઋતુ** aloud. Which part of **ઋ** is written last? (**The right loop and its tail.**)
