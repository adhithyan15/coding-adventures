## You'll want to know: उपनगरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C421-PALLI421-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपनगरम्** — *upanagaram* — "a suburb".

> मम मित्रम् उपनगरे वसति।
>
> *mama mitram upanagare vasati.*
>
> My friend lives in a suburb.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपनगरम्** (*upanagaram*) is built from *upa*, "near", and *nagara*, "a city"; MW gives the compound as a suburb (MW).
