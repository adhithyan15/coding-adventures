## You'll want to know: किफ़ायती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C431-SAUDA431-05]; assesses=[] -->

**किफ़ायती** — *kifāyatī* — "economical; good value".

> यह होटल किफ़ायती है और साफ़ भी।
>
> *yah hoṭal kifāyatī hai aur sāf bhī.*
>
> This hotel is good value, and clean too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किफ़ायती** (*kifāyatī*) is built on *kifāyat*, "thrift", usually traced to Arabic *kifāya*, "what is enough", through Persian: spending no more than is enough.
