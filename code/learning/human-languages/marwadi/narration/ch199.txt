Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 199: Seal and Signature.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मोहर (mohar) — an official stamp; a seal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a request, then the Marwadi for to explain.

You'll want to know: मोहर.
मोहर — mohar — "an official stamp; a seal".
अरजी माथै मोहर कोनी। arjī māthai mohar konī. There is no stamp on the application.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मोहर (mohar) is usually traced to Persian muhr, "seal, stamp".

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Lesson 4 of 5.
लेखो (lekho) — an account; a reckoning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a signature, then the Marwadi for a receipt.

You'll want to know: लेखो.
लेखो — lekho — "an account; a reckoning".
म्हारो लेखो पूरो है। mhāro lekho pūro hai. My account is settled.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
लेखो (lekho) is inherited from Sanskrit lekhya, "a writing, a document"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari lekha, "writings", and Hindi lekhā, "account" (CDIAL 11108).

Lesson 5 of 5.
मांडणो (mā̃ḍṇo) — to write down; to enter (in a ledger).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a receipt, then the Marwadi for a reckoning.

You'll want to know: मांडणो.
मांडणो — mā̃ḍṇo — "to write down; to enter (in a ledger)".
मास्टर नाम मांडै है। māsṭar nām mā̃ḍai hai. The teacher writes the names down.
Past (I …): म्हैं नाम मांड्यो, mhaĩ nām mā̃ḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
मांडणो (mā̃ḍṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit maṇḍayati, "adorns, decorates"; CDIAL lists an Old Marwari form of it glossed "writes" (CDIAL 9741).
