## You'll want to know: やけど

**やけど** — *yakedo* — "a burn".

> **なべで てに やけどを しました。** — *nabe de te ni yakedo o shimashita* — "I burned my hand on the pot."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やけど** (*yakedo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana; its usual kanji spelling writes the meaning, "fire" and "wound", not the sound (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
