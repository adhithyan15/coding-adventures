## You'll want to know: ગુલાબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C240-GARDEN240-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુલાબ** — *gulāb* — "rose".

> **મેં મારી માતાને ગુલાબ આપ્યું.** — *mẽ mārī mātāne gulāb āpyũ* — "I gave my mother a rose."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુલાબ** (*gulāb*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *gulāb*, "rose water", built from *gul*, "rose", and *āb*, "water".
