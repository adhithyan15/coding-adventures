## You'll want to know: વહેમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C535-SHUKAN535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વહેમ** — *vahem* — "a superstition".

> જો તમે આ વહેમ છોડી દેશો તો તમારી ચિંતા ઓછી થશે.
>
> *jo tame ā vahem choḍī deśo to tamārī cintā ochī thaśe.*
>
> If you give up this superstition, you'll worry less.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વહેમ** (*vahem*) is usually traced to Arabic *wahm*, "a fancy, a groundless idea", through Persian.
