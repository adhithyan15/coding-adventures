Punjabi, chapter 20: Build the Work Label and Values.
6 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਕ (ka) — one new base for the work label.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one tiny step until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Write the familiar ਮ once and place the familiar nasal mark ੰ above it once. Today adds only one base letter.

Script — one tiny step.
Trace ਕ slowly twice. Say ka. Do not build the whole label yet.

Guided Practice.
Cover the model and write ਕ (ka) once. Compare only the base shape and repair one difference.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after this one new base.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which new Gurmukhi base letter did you trace for ka?


Lesson 2 of 6.
ਕੰਮ (kamm) — assemble the work label.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one label until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Touch the pieces in order: ਕ (ka), ੰ, ਮ, ਮ.

Script — one label.
Join ਕ + ੰ + ਮ + ਮ = ਕੰਮ (kamm). On this beginner form, ਕੰਮ (kamm) labels the work line. Copy the label once and leave its answer blank.

Guided Practice.
Point to the label and say what information belongs there. Do not add a value yet.

Wrap-up recall.
This is a closed practice form, not a request for real personal information.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
A practice form shows ਕੰਮ (kamm). What information belongs on that line?


Lesson 3 of 6.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. Wrap-up recall.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ਖੇਤੀ (khetī) — assemble one work value.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one value until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Touch ਖ (kha), then ੇ, then ਤ, then ੀ.

Script — one value.
Build ਖੇ + ਤੀ = ਖੇਤੀ (khetī). It means “farming.” Copy ਖੇਤੀ (khetī) once.

Guided Practice.
Cover the model, say the meaning, and write the value once. Reveal it and repair one piece.

Wrap-up recall.
Only this value is scored today.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
Copy the Gurmukhi work value meaning farming.


Lesson 5 of 6.
ੌ (au-matra) — one new vowel sign.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one tiny step until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Write the familiar base ਨ once. Keep the top line clear.

Script — one tiny step.
Trace the vowel sign ੌ (au-matra) twice. Attach it to ਨ once: ਨੌ. The mark belongs above the base.

Guided Practice.
Cover the model and write ਨੌ once. Repair only the new sign or its placement.

Wrap-up recall.
Do not build the whole value yet.
[question — say your answer, then pause 14 seconds]
Add the new au vowel sign to ਨ.


Lesson 6 of 6.
ਨੌਕਰੀ (naukarī) — assemble the other work value.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one value until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Touch the known pieces ਨੌ, ਕ (ka), ਰੀ in order.

Script — one value.
Join ਨੌ + ਕ + ਰੀ = ਨੌਕਰੀ (naukarī). It means “job.” Copy ਨੌਕਰੀ (naukarī) once.

Guided Practice.
Cover the model, say the meaning, and write the value once. Repair only the first differing piece.

Wrap-up recall.
The two values remain separate until the next lesson.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
Copy the Gurmukhi work value meaning job.
