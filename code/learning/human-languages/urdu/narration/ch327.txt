Urdu, chapter 327: Buried Treasure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خزانہ (khazānā) — a treasure; a treasury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to sulk, then the Urdu for torment.

You'll want to know: خزانہ.
خزانہ — khazānā — "a treasure; a treasury".
زمین کے نیچے ایک خزانہ تھا۔ zamīn ke nīche ek khazānā thā. There was a treasure under the ground.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
خزانہ (khazānā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khizāna, "a storehouse". خزانچی, a cashier, is the one who keeps it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
گاڑنا (gāṛnā) — to bury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for torment, then the Urdu for a treasure.

You'll want to know: گاڑنا.
گاڑنا — gāṛnā — "to bury".
چور نے خزانہ زمین میں گاڑا۔ chor ne khazānā zamīn meṅ gāṛā. The thief buried the treasure in the ground.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بیج گاڑا, us ne bīj gāṛā.

The word, taken apart.
گاڑنا (gāṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin gāṛnā, "to bury, to drive in", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3979).
