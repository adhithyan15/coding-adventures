## You'll want to know: پلانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C484-RAISE484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پلانا** — *pilānā* — "to give (someone) a drink".

> نرس نے مریض کو پانی پلایا۔
>
> *nars ne marīz ko pānī pilāyā.*
>
> The nurse gave the patient some water to drink.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو دودھ پلایا, *us ne bachche ko dūdh pilāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پلانا** (*pilānā*) is the causative of **پینا**, "to drink", inherited from Sanskrit *píbati*, "drinks" (CDIAL 8209).
