Marathi, chapter 488: At the Clinic.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रुग्ण (rugṇa) — a sick person; a patient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to roast, then the Marathi for to scratch.

You'll want to know: रुग्ण.
रुग्ण — rugṇa — "a sick person; a patient".
दवाखान्यात सकाळपासून रुग्णांची रांग होती. davākhānyāt sakāḷpāsūn rugṇāñcī rāṅg hotī. There was a queue of patients at the clinic from the morning.
Masculine.
As in रुग्णालय, a hospital, and रुग्णवाहिका, an ambulance.

The word, taken apart.
रुग्ण (rugṇa) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit rugṇa, "broken, diseased" (CDIAL 10760).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a nurse, then the Marathi for a physician.

You'll want to know: निदान.
निदान — nidān — "a diagnosis".
रक्त तपासल्यानंतर निदान झालं. rakta tapāslyānantar nidān jhālaṁ. The diagnosis was made after a blood test.
Neuter.
निदान (nidān) करणे, to diagnose. As an adverb निदान (nidān) means at least.

The word, taken apart.
निदान (nidān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nidāna, "band; primary cause" (CDIAL 7196).

[pause 2 seconds]

योग्य उपचारांनंतर तो बरा झाला. yogya upcārānnantar to barā jhālā. After proper treatment he got well.
