Lesson 1 of 5.
vespa, vespae — a wasp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for smoke, then the Latin for ash.

You'll want to know: vespa, vespae.
vespa, vespae — "a wasp".

The word, taken apart.
vespa is inherited, usually traced to Proto-Indo-European wobhseh2, "wasp", from webh-, "to weave" (EtymDB 2.1): the insect that weaves its nest. English wasp is a cousin (Lewis & Short).

The word, taken apart.
