## You'll want to know: en realidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C589-MATIZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**en realidad** — "actually, really".

> En realidad, no es tan caro.
>
> *Actually, it is not so expensive.*

It corrects what someone thinks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*en realidad* is built on *realidad*, a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *realitas*, built on *res* 'thing' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
