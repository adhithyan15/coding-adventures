## You'll want to know: meckern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C431-BESCHWERDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**meckern** — "to grumble, to moan".

> Mein Onkel meckert oft über das Wetter.
>
> *My uncle often moans about the weather.*

Everyday speech. First the sound a goat makes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*meckern* first described the bleating of a goat and is usually explained as a sound word. EtymDB lists it without a source.
