## You'll want to know: клубок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C500-KNITTING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**клубок** (*klubók*) — "a ball of yarn".

> Котёнок играет с клубком шерсти.
>
> *Kotyónok igráyet s klubkóm shérsti.*
>
> The kitten is playing with a ball of wool.

Masculine; genitive клубка (the vowel drops).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*клубок* is built on *клуб* 'a ball, a puff (of smoke)' with *-ок*; EtymDB lists *клубок* without a source.
