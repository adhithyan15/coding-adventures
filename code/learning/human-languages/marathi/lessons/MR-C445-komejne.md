## You'll want to know: कोमेजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C445-ANKUR445-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कोमेजणे** — *komejṇe* — "to wither".

> पाणी न मिळाल्यामुळे फुलं कोमेजली.
>
> *pāṇī na miḷālyāmuḷe phulaṁ komejlī.*
>
> The flowers withered because they got no water.

Past (he …): तो कोमेजला, *to komejlā*.

Flowers and faces कोमेजतात.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोमेजणे** (*komejṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *komej̈ṇẽ*, "wither", under *\*kōmh* (CDIAL 3524).
