Tamil, chapter 133: Shoulder, Chest, Knee, Hair, Skin.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தோள் (tōḷ) — a shoulder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for the tongue, then the Tamil for a finger.

You'll want to know: தோள்.
தோள் — tōḷ — "a shoulder".

The word, taken apart.
தோள் (tōḷ) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3564 lists Malayalam tōḷ and Kannada tōḷ.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: முடி.
முடி — muṭi — "hair".

The word, taken apart.
முடி (muṭi) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4921 lists Malayalam muṭi, "knot" and Kannada muḍi, "to tie". The entry gives muṭi, "to tie", and the noun muṭi, "knot, tuft or coil of hair".
