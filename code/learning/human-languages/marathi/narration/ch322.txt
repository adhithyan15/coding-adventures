Marathi, chapter 322: Honours and Awards.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पुरस्कार (puraskār) — an award.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to attend, then the Marathi for attendance.

You'll want to know: पुरस्कार.
पुरस्कार — puraskār — "an award".
तिला तिच्या कामासाठी मोठा पुरस्कार मिळाला. tilā ticyā kāmāsāṭhī moṭhā puraskār miḷālā. She received a big award for her work.
Masculine.
पुरस्कार (puraskār) मिळणे, to receive an award, with the receiver in -ला: तिला. The letter ञ hides inside ज्ञ: ज्ञान, knowledge.

The word, taken apart.
पुरस्कार (puraskār) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit puraskāra, 'placing in front; honouring' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सत्कार करणे (satkār karṇe) — to felicitate; to honour publicly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for attendance, then the Marathi for an award.

You'll want to know: सत्कार करणे.
सत्कार करणे — satkār karṇe — "to felicitate; to honour publicly".
गावाने विजेत्या खेळाडूचा सत्कार केला. gāvāne vijetyā kheḷāḍūcā satkār kelā. The village honoured the winning player.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सत्कार केला, tyāne satkār kelā.
The person honoured takes -चा: शिक्षकांचा सत्कार करणे (satkār karṇe); सत्कार is masculine.

The word, taken apart.
सत्कार करणे (satkār karṇe) joins satkār, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit satkāra, 'kind treatment, honour' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
अभिनंदन करणे (abhinandan karṇe) — to congratulate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to felicitate, then the Marathi for an honour.

You'll want to know: अभिनंदन करणे.
अभिनंदन करणे — abhinandan karṇe — "to congratulate".
सगळ्यांनी पुरस्कार मिळाल्याबद्दल तिचं अभिनंदन केलं. sagaḷyānnī puraskār miḷālyābaddal ticaṁ abhinandan kelaṁ. Everyone congratulated her on receiving the award.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अभिनंदन केलं, tyāne abhinandan kelaṁ.
The person takes -चं: तिचं अभिनंदन केलं; the reason takes -बद्दल.

The word, taken apart.
अभिनंदन करणे (abhinandan karṇe) joins abhinandan, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit abhinandana, 'rejoicing at, praising' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.

महापौरांनी नव्या पुलाचं उद्घाटन केलं. mahāpaurānnī navyā pulācaṁ udghāṭan kelaṁ. The mayor inaugurated the new bridge.
