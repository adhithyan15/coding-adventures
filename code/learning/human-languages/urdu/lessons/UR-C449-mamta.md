## You'll want to know: ممتا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C449-LOVE449-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ممتا** — *mamtā* — "a mother's love".

> ماں کی ممتا سب سے بڑی ہے۔
>
> *māṅ kī mamtā sab se baṛī hai.*
>
> A mother's love is the greatest of all.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ممتا** (*mamtā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mamatā*, "the feeling of mine" (Monier-Williams), built on *mama*, "my"; Urdu uses it for a mother's love.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
