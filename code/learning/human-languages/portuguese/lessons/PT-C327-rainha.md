## You'll want to know: a rainha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C327-REINO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a rainha** — "the queen".

> A rainha usava um vestido azul.
>
> *The queen wore a blue dress.*

Also the queen in chess.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rainha* comes down from Old Portuguese *raỹa* (EtymDB), from Latin *rēgīna* "a queen", from *rēx* "a king".
