## You'll want to know: le col
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C454-RELIEF-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le col** — "the mountain pass; the collar".

> La route du col est fermée en hiver.
>
> *The road over the pass is closed in winter.*

*Le col de la chemise* — the shirt collar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*col* is inherited through Old French *col* 'neck' from Latin *collum* 'neck' (EtymDB): a low point between two mountains, like a neck.
