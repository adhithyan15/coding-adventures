## You'll want to know: ਤੰਦਰੁਸਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C319-HEALTH319-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੰਦਰੁਸਤ** — *tandrusat* — "healthy".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਦਾਦੀ ਤੰਦਰੁਸਤ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸੈਰ ਕਰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī dādī tandrusat hai ate roz sair kardī hai.*
>
> My grandmother is healthy and goes for a walk every day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੰਦਰੁਸਤ** (*tandrusat*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *tandurust*, "sound in body, healthy".
