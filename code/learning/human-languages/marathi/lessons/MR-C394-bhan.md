## You'll want to know: भान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C394-BHAN394-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भान** — *bhān* — "awareness; consciousness".

> खेळताना मुलांना वेळेचं भान राहिलं नाही.
>
> *kheḷtānā mulānnā veḷecaṁ bhān rāhilaṁ nāhī.*
>
> While playing, the children lost track of time.

Neuter.

X चं भान ठेवणे, to keep X in mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भान** (*bhān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bhāna*, "appearance" (CDIAL 9451): what appears to the mind.
