## You'll want to know: पसली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C478-PHEPHDA478-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पसली** — *paslī* — "a rib".

> गिरने से दादा की एक पसली टूट गई।
>
> *girne se dādā kī ek paslī ṭūṭ gaī.*
>
> Grandfather broke a rib in a fall.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पसली** (*paslī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *paslī*, "rib", under Sanskrit *párśu*, "rib" (CDIAL 7948).
