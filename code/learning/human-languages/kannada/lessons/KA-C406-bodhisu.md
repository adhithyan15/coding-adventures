## You'll want to know: ಬೋಧಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C406-VISHVAVIDYALAYA406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೋಧಿಸು** — *bōdhisu* — "to instruct; to preach".

> ಅವರು ಮೂವತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ ಬೋಧಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *avaru mūvattu varṣa vijñāna bōdhisidaru.*
>
> They taught science for thirty years.

Past (I …): ಬೋಧಿಸಿದೆ, *bōdhiside*.

More formal than ಕಲಿಸು. ಬೋಧಕ, a teacher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೋಧಿಸು** (*bōdhisu*) is built on Sanskrit *bodhana*, "arousing, awakening" (Monier-Williams), from *budh*, "to wake, to know", with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
