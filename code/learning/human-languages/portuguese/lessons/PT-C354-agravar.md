## You'll want to know: agravar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C354-INFLUENCIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**agravar** — "to make worse, to aggravate".

> O frio agravou a minha tosse.
>
> *The cold made my cough worse.*

*Agravar-se* — to get worse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*agravar* comes from Latin *aggravāre* "to add to the weight of, to make worse" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *gravis* "heavy".
