# むかえる (mukaeru) — to welcome; to go to meet

## You'll want to know: むかえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C244-MEET244-04]; assesses=[] -->

**むかえる** — *mukaeru* — "to welcome; to go to meet".

> **ははは えきで ともだちを むかえますか。** — *haha wa eki de tomodachi o mukaemasu ka* — "Is my mother meeting her friend at the station?"

Polite **むかえます**, *mukaemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むかえる** (*mukaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
