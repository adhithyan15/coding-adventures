## You'll want to know: o acordeão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C517-SOPROS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o acordeão** — "accordion".

> Nas festas da aldeia, o avô tocava acordeão.
>
> *At the village fêtes, Grandpa played the accordion.*

In Brazil: *o acordeom* or *a sanfona*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acordeão* was borrowed from French *accordéon* (EtymDB), from German *Akkordion*, built on *Akkord* "a chord".
