Punjabi, chapter 158: Recall Familiar Quality Words at a Distance.
2 lessons. All 2 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 2.
Three old quality words at a food stall — stale, ripe or firm, raw or unripe — a distant recall.

These are old words, not a new food lesson. The short A1 form and message clocks are finished; this is a separate, untimed return to three words from Chapter 146.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the old word for stale. (ਬੇਹਾ, behā.) Cover the answer before the next cue.

Guided Practice.
Imagine familiar food at a stall. Say only the old Punjabi describing word; the English scenes do not add a Punjabi noun to learn.
Food left too long is stale.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਬੇਹਾ (behā).
Fruit ready to eat is ripe.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਪੱਕਾ (pakkā).
Fruit not yet ripe is unripe.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਕੱਚਾ (kaccā).
The words already carried these meanings in Chapter 146. Pakkā can also mean firm; kaccā can also mean raw. Do not add a new sense from this review.

Wrap-up Recall.
Cover the three answers.
[pause 4 seconds after each]
Say the Punjabi for unripe, stale, then ripe. Check only after all three: ਕੱਚਾ, ਬੇਹਾ, ਪੱਕਾ. If one slipped, revisit just that old Chapter 146 word.


Lesson 2 of 2.
Is it true? Return to all five — true and false or lying, then all five old quality words.

This second short review is not a scored form or message. It retrieves two more known words and then mixes them with the first review's three food descriptions.

Warm-up.
[pause 4 seconds after each]
Say the word for true, then false or lying. Check: ਸੱਚਾ (saccā), then ਝੂਠਾ (jhūṭhā).

Guided Practice.
An account that matches what happened is true:
[pause 3 seconds]
ਸੱਚਾ. An account that does not is false:
[pause 3 seconds]
ਝੂਠਾ. These English cues add no new Punjabi statement to memorise.

Wrap-up Recall.
Cover the earlier answers.
[pause 4 seconds after each]
Say one old Punjabi word for each: false, ripe, stale, true, unripe.
Check in that same order: ਝੂਠਾ, ਪੱਕਾ, ਬੇਹਾ, ਸੱਚਾ, ਕੱਚਾ. Reopen only a missed Chapter 146 word. These five distant retrievals do not turn the earlier short writing drills into an A1 mock.
