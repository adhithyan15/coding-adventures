## You'll want to know: prallen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C288-ZUSAMMENSTOSS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**prallen** — "to crash (into), to hit hard".

> Das Auto prallte gegen einen Baum.
>
> *The car crashed into a tree.*

Perfekt with *sein*: *Es ist gegen die Wand geprallt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prallen* is usually traced to Middle High German *prellen* "to bounce off"; EtymDB lists no parent.
