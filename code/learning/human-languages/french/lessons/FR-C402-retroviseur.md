## You'll want to know: le rétroviseur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C402-VITRES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le rétroviseur** — "the rear-view mirror; the wing mirror".

> Avant de tourner, il regarde dans le rétroviseur.
>
> *Before turning, he checks the mirror.*

In speech, often *le rétro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rétroviseur* is built from *rétro-*, from Latin *retrō* 'backwards', and *viseur* 'sight', from *viser* 'to aim', which goes back to Latin *vidēre* 'to see' (EtymDB).
