## You'll want to know: شخصیت اصلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C530-STORIES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شخصیت اصلی** (*shakhsiyat-e asli*) — "the main character".

> شخصیت اصلی این رمان یک دختر جوان است.
>
> *shakhsiyat-e asli-ye in român yek dokhtar-e javân ast.*
>
> The main character of this novel is a young girl.

*shakhsiyat* 'character, personality' + *asli* 'main'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shakhsiyat-e asli* is built in Persian from two Arabic loans: *shakhsiyat*, from Arabic *šaḫṣiyya* (EtymDB), and *asli*, from Arabic *aṣlī* (EtymDB).
