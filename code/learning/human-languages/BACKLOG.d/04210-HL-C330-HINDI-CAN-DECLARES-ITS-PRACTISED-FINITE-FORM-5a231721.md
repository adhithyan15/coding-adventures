## HL-C330 — Hindi can declares its practised finite form

After the possessive repair, **सकता** is the highest-ranked independent modern
Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 70 already explains
that **सकना** carries the grammar in an ability construction, models
**सकता** (*saktā*) repeatedly in complete sentences, and asks learners to
produce the form in guided practice and an assessed activity.

The old metadata declared only infinitive **सकना**, leaving the explicitly
taught masculine-singular form invisible to reports and downstream cards.

Declare **सकना / सकता** as the lesson headword, print the Devanagari sentences
in guided practice, and regenerate the derived Hindi artifacts. This is a
metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new vocabulary claim.
