Sanskrit, chapter 230: Success, Failure, Effort, Opportunity, Experience.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सफलता (saphalatā) — success.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for authority, then the Sanskrit for a question paper.

You'll want to know: सफलता.
सफलता — saphalatā — "success".

The word, taken apart.
saphalatā is built on saphala, "with fruit, fruitful" (CDIAL 13166), from sa-, "with", and phala, "fruit", with -tā, "-ness" (MW). Success is bearing fruit. CDIAL 9051 compares phala with Dravidian words for ripe fruit.

The word, taken apart.
prayatna is built on pra-, "forth", and the root √yat, "to strive" (MW), the root of yatate, "tries". MW glosses it "persevering effort".

Lesson 4 of 5.
अवसरः (avasaraḥ) — an opportunity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for failure, then the Sanskrit for an effort.

You'll want to know: अवसरः.
अवसरः — avasaraḥ — "an opportunity".

The word, taken apart.
avasara is built on ava, "down", and the root √sṛ, "to run, flow" (MW). MW's first sense is "descent of water, rain", and then "occasion, favourable moment" (CDIAL 858). Hindi osrā, "turn, season", comes from it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अनुभवः (anubhavaḥ) — experience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an effort, then the Sanskrit for an opportunity.

You'll want to know: अनुभवः.
अनुभवः — anubhavaḥ — "experience".

The word, taken apart.
anubhava is built on anu, "after, along", and the root √bhū, "to be, become" (MW): to be along with something is to perceive it. MW glosses it "perception, understanding", and the root is the one MW compares with English be.
