## You'll want to know: der Sturz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C289-STURZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Sturz** — "the fall".

> Nach dem Sturz konnte sie nicht mehr gehen.
>
> *After the fall she could not walk any more.*

Plural: *die Stürze*. Also the fall of a government.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sturz* is built on the root of *stürzen* "to plunge", Old High German *sturzen* (EtymDB).
