## You'll want to know: 面试
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C276-NEWJOB276-01]; assesses=[] -->

**面试** — *miànshì* — "to have an interview (for a job); interview".

> **上个月我老公去面试了** *shàng ge yuè wǒ lǎogōng qù miànshì le* — My husband went for a job interview last month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**面试** (*miànshì*) is built from 面 'the face' and 试 'a test', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjienH* and *syiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to face' and the second 'to use'.
