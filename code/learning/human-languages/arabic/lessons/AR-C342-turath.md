## You'll want to know: تراث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C342-TURATH342-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تراث** — *turāth* — "heritage".

> تحافظ القرية على تراثها القديم.
>
> *tuḥāfiẓu al-qarya ʿalā turāthihā al-qadīm.*
>
> The village takes care of its old heritage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تراث** (*turāth*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-r-th* (Lane), "to inherit". Lane explains *turāth* as *wurāth* with *t* in place of the *w*: what a people inherits.
