# అనురాగం (anurāgaṁ) — tender love; affection

## You'll want to know: అనురాగం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C503-PREMA503-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అనురాగం** — *anurāgaṁ* — "tender love; affection".

> అక్కాచెల్లెళ్ళ మధ్య అనురాగం ఎక్కువ.
>
> *akkācelleḷḷa madhya anurāgaṁ ekkuva.*
>
> There is deep affection between the sisters.

A warm, slightly literary word: అనురాగంతో, lovingly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అనురాగం** (*anurāgaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anurāga*, "attachment, love" (CDIAL 331).
