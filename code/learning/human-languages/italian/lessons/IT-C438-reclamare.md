## You'll want to know: reclamare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C438-RECLAMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**reclamare** — "to complain, to protest; to claim".

> I clienti reclamano per il rumore.
>
> *The guests complain about the noise.*

*Reclamare un diritto* — to claim a right.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reclamare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *reclāmāre* 'to cry out against' (EtymDB), from *clāmāre* 'to shout'.
