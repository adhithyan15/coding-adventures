# ಆಳು (āḷu) — to reign; to rule

## You'll want to know: ಆಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C322-RAJA322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಳು** — *āḷu* — "to reign; to rule".

> ಆ ರಾಜ ನಲವತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ಈ ನಾಡನ್ನು ಆಳಿದನು.
>
> *ā rāja nalavattu varṣa ī nāḍannu āḷidanu.*
>
> That king ruled this land for forty years.

Past (I …): ಆಳಿದೆ, *āḷide*.

What is ruled takes -ಅನ್ನು. A different ಆಳು, a noun, means a servant or a worker.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಳು** (*āḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5157 lists Kannada *āḷ*, "to possess, govern, rule", beside Tamil *āḷ*, "to rule, reign over".
