Spanish, chapter 683: Regiones y pueblos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la región — the region.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the kerb, then the Spanish for pedestrian.

You'll want to know: la región.
la región — "the region".
Es una región famosa por su vino. It is a region famous for its wine.
La región is wider than la zona.

The word, taken apart.
región is a learned borrowing from Latin regiō "a direction, a boundary, a district" (EtymDB), from regere "to guide, to rule".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la comarca.
la comarca — "the district, the area (of several villages)".
Es el pueblo más grande de la comarca. It is the biggest village in the district.
Smaller than a province, bigger than a municipality.

The word, taken apart.
comarca is usually traced to marca, an old word for a border district, which is of Germanic origin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la aldea — the hamlet, the small village.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the district, then the Spanish for the municipality.

You'll want to know: la aldea.
la aldea — "the hamlet, the small village".
En la aldea viven veinte personas. Twenty people live in the hamlet.
Smaller than un pueblo.

The word, taken apart.
aldea comes from Arabic aḍ-ḍayʿa "the farm, the village" (EtymDB), with the Arabic article al- fused on.
