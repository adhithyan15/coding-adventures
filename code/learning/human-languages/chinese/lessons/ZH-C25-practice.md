## What you've built — two strokes, and time itself
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-LE-FOUR-SKILL-01]; assesses=[] -->

Before this chapter every sentence in this book happened now. After it:

1. **我看了书** — a finished action. 了 behind the verb.
2. **我是大学生了** — a changed state. 了 at the end.
3. **Neither is a tense.** Mandarin marks whether, not when.

Two strokes did that. Compare the price: the verb chapter needed a character each
for 看 and 书, and neither of them changed what could be *said about time*.

**Listen.** *Wǒ kàn le shū. Wǒ shì dàxuéshēng le. Nǐ kàn le ma?*

**Speak.** Say you have read the book; then say you have become a student.

**Read.** 我看了书 — 有人了 — 你看了吗

**Write.** 了 — the turn, then the hook.
