## You'll want to know: obsōnō, obsōnāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C350-OBSONO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**obsōnō, obsōnāre** — "to buy food, to shop for a meal".

> Coquus māne in macellō obsōnābat.
>
> *In the morning the cook was buying food at the market.*

*obsōnō, obsōnāre, obsōnāvī, obsōnātum*, also *opsōnō*. Comedies are full of cooks sent off to shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**obsōnō** was borrowed from Greek *opsōneō*, "to buy fish or other dainties" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).
