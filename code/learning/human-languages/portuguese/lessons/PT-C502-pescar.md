## You'll want to know: pescar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C502-PESCA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**pescar** — "to fish; to catch (a fish)".

> Ao domingo, o meu tio vai pescar para o rio.
>
> *On Sundays, my uncle goes fishing on the river.*

*Ir pescar* — to go fishing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pescar* is inherited, through Vulgar Latin *piscāre*, from Latin *piscārī* "to fish" (EtymDB), built on *piscis* "fish".
