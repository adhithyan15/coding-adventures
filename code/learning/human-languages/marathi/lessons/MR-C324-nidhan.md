## You'll want to know: निधन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C324-SHRADDHA324-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निधन** — *nidhan* — "a passing; a death".

> प्रसिद्ध गायकाच्या निधनाची बातमी काल आली.
>
> *prasiddha gāykācyā nidhanācī bātmī kāl ālī.*
>
> News of the famous singer's passing came yesterday.

Neuter.

The respectful word: X-चं निधन झालं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निधन** (*nidhan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nidhana*, 'a settling, a close; death' (Monier-Williams).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
