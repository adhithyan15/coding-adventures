## You'll want to know: удалять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C401-PRILOZHENIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**удалять** (*udalyát*) — "to delete, to remove".

> Я удаляю старые фотографии.
>
> *Ya udalyáyu stáryye fotográfii.*
>
> I am deleting old photos.

Imperfective; perfective *удалить*. It also means to take out a tooth, or to send a player off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**удалять** is built from *у-* and the root of *даль* "distance" and *далёкий* "far": to move a thing far away.
