## You'll want to know: हित
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C366-MULYA366-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हित** — *hit* — "welfare; good".

> हा निर्णय सगळ्यांच्या हिताचा आहे.
>
> *hā nirṇay sagaḷyāñcyā hitācā āhe.*
>
> This decision is for everyone's good.

Neuter.

X च्या हिताचा, in X's interest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हित** (*hit*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *hita*, "beneficial; welfare, good" (Monier-Williams).
