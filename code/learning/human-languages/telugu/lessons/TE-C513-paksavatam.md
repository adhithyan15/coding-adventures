# పక్షవాతం (pakṣavātaṁ) — paralysis; a stroke

## You'll want to know: పక్షవాతం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C513-VYADHI513-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పక్షవాతం** — *pakṣavātaṁ* — "paralysis; a stroke".

> పక్షవాతం వల్ల ఆయన కుడి చెయ్యి పనిచేయదు.
>
> *pakṣavātaṁ valla āyana kuḍi ceyyi panicēyadu.*
>
> Because of the stroke, his right hand does not work.

పక్షం, side, + వాతం, wind: in old medicine, a wind that strikes one side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పక్షవాతం** (*pakṣavātaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *pakṣa*, "wing, side" (CDIAL 7627), and *vāta*, "wind; wind as a humour of the body" (CDIAL 11491).
