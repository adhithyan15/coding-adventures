# అంతిమయాత్ర (antimayātra) — a funeral procession

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C344-SANTAPAM344-02, TE-LEX-C344-SANTAPAM344-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for death, then the Telugu for one's last breath.

## You'll want to know: అంతిమయాత్ర
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C344-SANTAPAM344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతిమయాత్ర** — *antimayātra* — "a funeral procession".

> అంతిమయాత్రలో వందల మంది నడిచారు.
>
> *antimayātralō vandala mandi naḍicāru.*
>
> Hundreds walked in the funeral procession.

అంతిమ, last, and యాత్ర, a journey: the last journey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతిమయాత్ర** (*antimayātra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *antima*, "last" (Monier-Williams), and *yātrā*, "a journey" (CDIAL 10456).
