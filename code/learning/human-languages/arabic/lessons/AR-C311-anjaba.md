## You'll want to know: أنجب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C311-WILADA311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أنجب** — *anjaba* — "to have a child, to bear".

> أنجبت جارتنا بنتا جميلة.
>
> *anjabat jāratunā bintan jamīla.*
>
> Our neighbour had a beautiful baby girl.

Present: ينجب, *yunjib*.

Of either parent: أنجب الرجل ثلاثة أولاد.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أنجب** (*anjaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-j-b* (Lane), the root of *najīb*, noble, excellent. Form IV *anjaba* was first "to beget a noble child", then to have a child.
