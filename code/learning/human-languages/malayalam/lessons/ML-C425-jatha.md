## You'll want to know: ജാഥ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C425-SAMARAM425-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ജാഥ** — *jātha* — "a march; a procession".

> കുട്ടികൾ ഗ്രാമത്തിലൂടെ ഒരു ജാഥ നടത്തി.
>
> *kuṭṭikaḷ grāmattilūṭe oru jātha naṭatti.*
>
> The children held a march through the village.

A march is നടത്തുക, organised.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ജാഥ** (*jātha*) is usually traced to Hindustani *jathā*, "a band, a group of people"; its history before that is not settled.
