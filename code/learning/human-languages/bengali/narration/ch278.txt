Bengali, chapter 278: A Crash on the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ধাক্কা (dhākkā) — a jolt; a collision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a crowd, then the Bengali for to turn up.

You'll want to know: ধাক্কা.
ধাক্কা — dhākkā — "a jolt; a collision".
আস্তে না চালালে ধাক্কা লাগবে। āste nā chālāle dhākkā lāgbe. If you don't drive slowly, you will crash into something.
ধাক্কা (dhākkā) লাগা, to collide; ধাক্কা (dhākkā) দেওয়া, to push or bump into.

The word, taken apart.
ধাক্কা (dhākkā) is filed in CDIAL 6701 under a Middle Indo-Aryan dhakk-, 'push, strike', which lists Bengali dhākkā, 'a push'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
চলা (chôlā) — to move; to go along.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to turn up, then the Bengali for a jolt.

You'll want to know: চলা.
চলা — chôlā — "to move; to go along".
বাসটা দ্রুত চলছিল, তাই হঠাৎ থামতে পারল না। bāsṭā drutô chôlchhilô, tāi hôṭhāt thāmte pārlô nā. The bus was going fast, so it couldn't stop suddenly.
Past (he/she …): সেই বাস চলল, sei bās chôllô.

The word, taken apart.
চলা (chôlā) comes down from Sanskrit calati, 'goes away; shakes' (CDIAL 4715, which lists Bengali calā, 'to move, walk'). It is the plain partner of চালানো: a driver চালায়, and the car চলে.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ভাঙা (bhāngā) — to break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a jolt, then the Bengali for to move.

You'll want to know: ভাঙা.
ভাঙা — bhāngā — "to break".
ধাক্কায় গাড়ির জানালা ভাঙল। dhākkāy gāṛir jānālā bhānglô. The car window broke in the collision.
Past (he/she …): সেই জানালা ভাঙল, sei jānālā bhānglô.
It works both ways: কেউ জানালা ভাঙল, someone broke the window; জানালা ভাঙল, the window broke. The -এ form is ভেঙে.

The word, taken apart.
ভাঙা (bhāngā) comes down from Sanskrit bhaṅga, 'breaking; a break' (CDIAL 9353, which lists Bengali bhāṅā, 'to be broken'). Its ঙ is the nasal ng before the vowel.

You'll want to know: উল্টানো.
উল্টানো — ulṭāno — "to overturn; to turn over".
বাসের ধাক্কায় রিকশাটা উল্টে গেল। bāser dhākkāy rikshāṭā ulṭe gelô. The rickshaw overturned when the bus hit it.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বইয়ের পাতা উল্টালেন, tini bôiyer pātā ulṭālen.

The word, taken apart.
উল্টানো (ulṭāno) is filed in CDIAL 2368 under a Middle Indo-Aryan ullaṭati, 'turns over', which lists Bengali ulṭāna, 'to turn over'. উল্টে যাওয়া, to tip over; উল্টানো on its own, to turn something over.
