## You'll want to know: ಹತ್ತನೆಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-05]; assesses=[KA-ETYMON-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-02, KA-GRAMMAR-C74-ORDINAL-02] -->

**ಹತ್ತನೆಯ** (*hattaneya*) — "**tenth**".

**ಹತ್ತು** is the word that showed you the **p → h** law: Tamil kept *pattu*,
Kannada softened the p to h. The ordinal keeps whatever the cardinal became — the
ending has no opinion about the history of the word it lands on.

**ಹತ್ತನೆಯ ದಿನ** (*hattaneya dina*), the tenth day.
