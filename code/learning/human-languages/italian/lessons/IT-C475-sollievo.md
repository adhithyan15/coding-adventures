## You'll want to know: il sollievo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C475-SPAVENTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il sollievo** — "relief".

> Che sollievo, sei arrivato!
>
> *What a relief, you have arrived!*

*Tirare un sospiro di sollievo* — to breathe a sigh of relief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sollievo* is built on *sollevare* "to lift up", which is inherited from Latin *sublevāre* "to lift from below" (Lewis & Short), from *levāre* "to raise".
