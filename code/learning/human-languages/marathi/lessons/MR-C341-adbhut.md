## You'll want to know: अद्भुत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C341-KADAMBARI341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अद्भुत** — *adbhut* — "wondrous; marvellous".

> पुस्तकातलं जग अद्भुत होतं.
>
> *pustakātlaṁ jag adbhut hotaṁ.*
>
> The world in the book was wondrous.

अद्भुत keeps one form: अद्भुत कथा, अद्भुत दृश्य.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अद्भुत** (*adbhut*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *adbhuta*, 'wonderful, marvellous' (Monier-Williams).
