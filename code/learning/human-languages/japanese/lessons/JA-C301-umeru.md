## You'll want to know: うめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C301-TAKARA301-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うめる** — *umeru* — "to bury".

> **おとこは たからを やまに うめました。** — *otoko wa takara o yama ni umemashita* — "The man buried the treasure in the mountains."

Polite **うめます**, *umemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **とのさまが たからを うめました。**, *tonosama ga takara o umemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うめる** (*umeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
