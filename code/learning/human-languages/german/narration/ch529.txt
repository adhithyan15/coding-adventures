German, chapter 529: Renovieren.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
renovieren — to renovate, to do up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for dustbin, then the German for to dispose of.

You'll want to know: renovieren.
renovieren — "to renovate, to do up".
Vor dem Einzug haben wir die Wohnung renoviert. Before moving in, we did up the flat.
Regular, Perfekt without ge-: hat renoviert.

The word, taken apart.
renovieren was borrowed from Middle French renover (EtymDB), from Latin renovāre "to make new", built on novus "new".

Lesson 5 of 6.
der Dachboden — attic, loft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to wallpaper, then the German for tile.

You'll want to know: der Dachboden.
der Dachboden — "attic, loft".
Die alten Spielsachen liegen auf dem Dachboden. The old toys are in the attic.
Plural: die Dachböden.

The word, taken apart.
Dachboden is built on Dach "roof", inherited from Old High German dah, from Proto-Germanic þaką (EtymDB), and Boden "floor".
