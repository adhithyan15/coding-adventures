## You'll want to know: 白天
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C146-NIGHT146-01]; assesses=[] -->

**白天** — *báitiān* — "daytime".

> **白天这儿很吵** *báitiān zhèr hěn chǎo* — It's noisy here in the daytime.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**白天** (*báitiān*) is built from 白 'white' and 天 'day, sky', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *baek* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the white, light part of the day.
