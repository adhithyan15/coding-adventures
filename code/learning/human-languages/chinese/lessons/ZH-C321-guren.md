## You'll want to know: 古人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C321-ANCIENT321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**古人** — *gǔrén* — "the ancients; people of long ago".

> **古人没有电话** *gǔrén méiyǒu diànhuà* — People long ago had no telephones.

The new character, in pieces: 古 — five strokes: 十 on top, and 口 under it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**古人** (*gǔrén*) is built from 古 'ancient' and 人 'a person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'former'.
