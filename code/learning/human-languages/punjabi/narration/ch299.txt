Punjabi, chapter 299: By the Water.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੰਢਾ (kaṇḍhā) — a riverbank; a shore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for dense, then the Punjabi for to wilt.

You'll want to know: ਕੰਢਾ.
ਕੰਢਾ — kaṇḍhā — "a riverbank; a shore".
ਅਸੀਂ ਦਰਿਆ ਦੇ ਕੰਢੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਬੈਠਦੇ ਹਾਂ। asīṁ dariā de kaṇḍhe utte baiṭhde hāṁ. We sit on the bank of the river.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੰਢਾ (kaṇḍhā) is inherited from Sanskrit kaṇṭha, "throat, neck", also "border"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kaṇḍhā, "bank, shore", under it (CDIAL 2680).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਖਾਲ (khāl) — a watercourse; a field channel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to wilt, then the Punjabi for a riverbank.

You'll want to know: ਖਾਲ.
ਖਾਲ — khāl — "a watercourse; a field channel".
ਖਾਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਾਣੀ ਖੇਤ ਤੱਕ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ। khāl vich pāṇī khet tak jāndā hai. In the channel the water goes to the field.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਖਾਲ (khāl) is inherited from Sanskrit khalla, "trench, creek, canal"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khāl, "hollow, gutter, inlet", under it (CDIAL 3849).
