## You'll want to know: مولود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C311-WILADA311-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مولود** — *mawlūd* — "a newborn".

> نام المولود بعد أن شرب الحليب.
>
> *nāma al-mawlūd baʿda an shariba al-ḥalīb.*
>
> The newborn fell asleep after drinking his milk.

Plural: مواليد, *mawālīd*.

The plural مواليد also names everyone born in a given year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مولود** (*mawlūd*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-l-d* (Lane), "to give birth". *Mawlūd* is its passive participle, "one who is born".
