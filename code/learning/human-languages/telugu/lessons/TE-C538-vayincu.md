## You'll want to know: వాయించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C538-SANGITAM538-03]; assesses=[] -->

**వాయించు** — *vāyiñcu* — "to play an instrument".

> మా అన్నయ్య గిటారు బాగా వాయిస్తాడు.
>
> *mā annayya giṭāru bāgā vāyistāḍu.*
>
> My elder brother plays the guitar well.

Past (I …): వాయించాను, *vāyiñcānu*.

For instruments; ఆడు is for games.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వాయించు** (*vāyiñcu*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *vādayati*, "causes to sound" (CDIAL 11510), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
