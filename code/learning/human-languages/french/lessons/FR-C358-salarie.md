## You'll want to know: le salarié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C358-GREVE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le salarié** — "the employee, the wage earner".

> Cette entreprise a deux cents salariés.
>
> *This company has two hundred employees.*

From *salaire*. Feminine *salariée*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*salarié* is built on *salaire* "wages", from Latin *salārium* "salt money, an allowance" (Lewis & Short), from *sāl* "salt".
