## You'll want to know: o ciclismo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C513-CORRIDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o ciclismo** — "cycling".

> O ciclismo é muito popular no verão.
>
> *Cycling is very popular in summer.*

A cyclist is *um ciclista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ciclismo* is usually traced to French *cyclisme*, built on Greek *kýklos* "a circle, a wheel".
