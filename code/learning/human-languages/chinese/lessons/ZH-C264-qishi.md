## You'll want to know: qíshí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C264-REFUSE264-05]; assesses=[] -->

*qíshí* — "actually; as a matter of fact".

> *qíshí wǒ hěn máng* — Actually, I'm really busy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qíshí* is built from *qí* 'its' and *shí* 'real', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gi* and *zyit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which calls the first a particle and glosses the second 'full' and 'true'.
