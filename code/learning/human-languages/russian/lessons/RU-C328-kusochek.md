## You'll want to know: кусочек
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C328-NAREZAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**кусочек** (*kusóchek*) — "a little piece, a bit".

> Он съел кусочек торта.
>
> *On syel kusóchek tórta.*
>
> He ate a piece of cake.

Diminutive of *кусок* "a piece"; genitive *кусочка*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*кусочек* is a diminutive of *кусок* "a piece", built on *кус* "a mouthful", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *kǫsŭ* "piece", from *kǫsati* "to bite" (EtymDB), the root of *кусать*.
