## You'll want to know: ڈرائیور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C394-GARHA394-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈرائیور** — *ḍrāivar* — "a driver".

> ڈرائیور نے گڑھا نہیں دیکھا۔
>
> *ḍrāivar ne gaṛhā nahīṅ dekhā.*
>
> The driver didn't see the pothole.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈرائیور** (*ḍrāivar*) is borrowed from English *driver*.
