## You'll want to know: विवशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C543-HATHA543-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विवशः** — *vivaśaḥ* — "helpless".

> यदि सः विवशः अस्ति, तर्हि तस्य साहाय्यं करोतु।
>
> *yadi saḥ vivaśaḥ asti, tarhi tasya sāhāyyaṃ karotu.*
>
> If he is helpless, please help him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विवशः** (*vivaśaḥ*) is built on *vi*, "without", and *vaśa*, "will, control"; MW gives *vivaśa* as deprived of will, powerless, helpless (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
