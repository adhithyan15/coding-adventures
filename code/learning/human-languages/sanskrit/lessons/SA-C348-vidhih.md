## You'll want to know: विधिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C348-AUCITYA348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विधिः** — *vidhiḥ* — "a precept; a rule laid down".

> जनः विधिं पालयेत्।
>
> *janaḥ vidhiṃ pālayet.*
>
> One should keep to the precept.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विधिः** (*vidhiḥ*) is built on *vi* and the root √dhā, "to put, to place"; MW gives *vidhi* "a rule, formula, injunction, precept, law" (MW).
