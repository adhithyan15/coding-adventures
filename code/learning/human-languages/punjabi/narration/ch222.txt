Punjabi, chapter 222: What Will You Do Tomorrow?.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
Return to going tomorrow — retrieve I will go, he will go and we will go by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: jāvāṅgā; jāvegā; jāvāṅge]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ, ਜਾਵੇਗਾ, ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ.

Grammar Lens.
Those three futures needed ਵ after the vowel of ਜਾ. The verb ਕਰਨਾ, "to do", has a stem that ends in a consonant, ਕਰ. Keep it ready.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: I will go tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: she will go tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ.
[your turn — hear: to do]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕਰਨਾ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say we will go, then to do.


Lesson 2 of 6.
ਕਰਾਂਗਾ, ਕਰੇਗਾ — will do.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I will go and he will go. Then to do.

Grammar Lens: the ending straight on.
A consonant stem takes the endings with no ਵ between:
ਕਰ + ਾਂਗਾ becomes ਕਰਾਂਗਾ, ਕਰ + ੇਗਾ becomes ਕਰੇਗਾ.
ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਾਂਗਾ। — maiṁ kallh kamm karāṅgā. — I will work tomorrow (a man speaking).
ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਾਂਗੀ। — maiṁ kallh kamm karāṅgī. — I will work tomorrow (a woman speaking).
ਅਮਨ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੇਗਾ। — Aman kamm karegā. — Aman will work.
ਕਰੇਗੀ is for a woman, and ਕਰਾਂਗੇ for ਅਸੀਂ.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: she will work tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੇਗੀ।.
[your turn — hear: we will work]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਸੀਂ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਾਂਗੇ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕਰਾਂਗਾ]
Point to the stem ਕਰ, then the ending.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਕਰਾਂਗਾ and ਕਰੇਗਾ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I will do? (ਕਰਾਂਗਾ or ਕਰਾਂਗੀ.) He will do? (ਕਰੇਗਾ.)


Lesson 3 of 6.
ਕਰੋਗੇ, ਕਰਨਗੇ — you will do, they will do.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I will work tomorrow and we will work. Then ਤੁਸੀਂ.

Grammar Lens: two more cells.
With ਤੁਸੀਂ the future of ਕਰਨਾ is ਕਰੋਗੇ; with ਉਹ, for a group, it is ਕਰਨਗੇ:
ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੀ ਕਰੋਗੇ? — tusīṁ kallh kī karoge? — What will you do tomorrow?
ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਨਗੇ। — uh kallh kamm karange. — They will work tomorrow.
Here ਕੀ is "what": it stands where the answer goes.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: what will you do tomorrow?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੀ ਕਰੋਗੇ? (tusīṁ kallh kī karoge?)
[your turn — hear: I will go to the market]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Answer it with the ending that fits you.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਨਗੇ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਕਰੋਗੇ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
You will do? (ਕਰੋਗੇ.) They will do? (ਕਰਨਗੇ.)

Lesson 5 of 6.
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋਗੇ? (kī tusīṁ kallh kamm karoge?) — will you work tomorrow?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say what will you do tomorrow? Then are you a traveller?

Grammar Lens: ਕੀ and ਨਹੀਂ with the future.
Nothing moves. ਕੀ goes in front to ask yes or no; ਨਹੀਂ goes before the verb to say no:
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋਗੇ? — kī tusīṁ kallh kamm karoge? — Will you work tomorrow?
ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਰਾਂਗੀ। — nahīṁ jī, maiṁ kallh kamm nahīṁ karāṅgī. — No, I won't work tomorrow.
ਕੀ ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ? — kī Aman kallh āvegā? — Will Aman come tomorrow?

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: will she go to school tomorrow?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ?
[your turn — hear: no, she won't go]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਉਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕੀ ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ?]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਨਹੀਂ ਕਰਾਂਗਾ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask will you work tomorrow? and answer it truthfully.
