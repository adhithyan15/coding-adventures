## You'll want to know: रोषणाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C319-ROSHNAI319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रोषणाई** — *roṣṇāī* — "festive lights; illuminations".

> सणाला संपूर्ण गल्लीत रोषणाई केली होती.
>
> *saṇālā sampūrṇa gallīt roṣṇāī kelī hotī.*
>
> For the festival the whole lane had been lit up.

Feminine.

रोषणाई करणे, to put up lights. The letter ऋ opens ऋतू, a season; every ऋतू has its festival.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोषणाई** (*roṣṇāī*) is a loan from Persian *rošanā'ī*, 'brightness, light', built on *rošan*, 'bright'.
