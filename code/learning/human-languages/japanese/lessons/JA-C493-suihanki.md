## You'll want to know: すいはんき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C493-FREEZE493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**すいはんき** — *suihanki* — "a rice cooker".

> **すいはんきで ごはんを たきます。** — *suihanki de gohan o takimasu* — "I cook rice in the rice cooker."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいはんき** (*suihanki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sui*, "cook" (Middle Chinese *tsyhwe*), *han*, "meal" (*bjonH*) and *ki*, "utensil" (*khijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
