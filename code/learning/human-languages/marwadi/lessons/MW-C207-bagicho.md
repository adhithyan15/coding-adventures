## You'll want to know: बगीचो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C207-GARDEN207-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बगीचो** — *bagīcho* — "a garden".

> बगीचो कोट कनै है।
>
> *bagīcho koṭ kanai hai.*
>
> The garden is near the fort.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बगीचो** (*bagīcho*) is usually traced to Persian *bāghcha*, "a small garden".
