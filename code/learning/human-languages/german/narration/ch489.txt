German, chapter 489: Neffen und Nichten.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
verwandt — related (by family).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to get some rest, then the German for to recover.

You'll want to know: verwandt.
verwandt — "related (by family)".
Wir haben den gleichen Nachnamen, aber wir sind nicht verwandt. We have the same surname, but we are not related.
Verwandt mit + dative. Der Verwandte or die Verwandte — a relative.

The word, taken apart.
verwandt is the old past participle of verwenden "to turn, to turn towards" (EtymDB), built on wenden "to turn".

Lesson 2 of 6.
die Verwandtschaft — relatives; kinship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to recover, then the German for related.

You'll want to know: die Verwandtschaft.
die Verwandtschaft — "relatives; kinship".
Zu Weihnachten kommt die ganze Verwandtschaft. All the relatives come for Christmas.
A collective noun: all your relatives together. Plural, rare: die Verwandtschaften.

The word, taken apart.
Verwandtschaft is built on verwandt "related" with -schaft; verwandt is the old participle of verwenden "to turn towards" (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: der Neffe.
der Neffe — "nephew".
Mein Neffe ist zehn und spielt gern Fußball. My nephew is ten and likes playing football.
Weak noun: den Neffen, dem Neffen. Plural: die Neffen.

The word, taken apart.
Neffe is inherited from Old High German nefo (EtymDB), an old Germanic family word.

Lesson 5 of 6.
der Schwager — brother-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for nephew, then the German for niece.

You'll want to know: der Schwager.
der Schwager — "brother-in-law".
Mein Schwager hilft uns beim Umzug. My brother-in-law is helping us move.
Plural: die Schwager. A sister-in-law is die Schwägerin.

The word, taken apart.
Schwager is inherited from Middle High German swāger and Old High German suāgur, from Proto-Germanic swēgraz "husband's brother" (EtymDB).
