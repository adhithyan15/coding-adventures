## You'll want to know: غم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C484-SADNESS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**غم** (*gham*) — "sorrow, grief".

> او غم بزرگی در دل دارد.
>
> *u gham-e bozorgi dar del dârad.*
>
> She carries a great sorrow in her heart.

Short and often poetic. *gham khordan* — to grieve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gham* was borrowed from Arabic *ġamm*, 'grief, distress' (EtymDB).
