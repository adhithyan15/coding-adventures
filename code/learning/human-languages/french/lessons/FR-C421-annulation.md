## You'll want to know: l'annulation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C421-ANNULATION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'annulation** — "the cancellation".

> Après l'annulation, la compagnie nous a donné une chambre.
>
> *After the cancellation the airline gave us a room.*

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*annulation* is built on *annuler* 'to cancel', a learned borrowing from Late Latin *annullāre* 'to make nothing', from *nullus* 'none' (Lewis & Short).
