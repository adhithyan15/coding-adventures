## You'll want to know: xuě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xuě* — "snow".

> *xuě hěn dà wǒ de ěrduo hěn lěng* — It's snowing hard and my ears are freezing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuě* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sjwet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'frozen rain'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
