## You'll want to know: obstupēscō, obstupēscere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C495-MIRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**obstupēscō, obstupēscere** — "to be struck dumb, be astounded".

> Hospitēs ad hanc fābulam obstupēscunt.
>
> *The guests are astounded at this story.*

*obstupēscō, obstupēscere, obstupuī*. The inceptive of *stupeō*: the moment the wonder strikes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**obstupēscō** is built on *ob-* and *stupēscō*, the inceptive form of *stupeō* "to be stunned" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
