## You'll want to know: il portavoce
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C285-COMUNICATO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il portavoce** — "the spokesperson".

> Il portavoce non ha voluto rispondere.
>
> *The spokesperson would not answer.*

One form for a man or a woman; plural unchanged: *i portavoce*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*portavoce* joins *porta*, from *portare* "to carry", and *voce* "voice", from Latin *vōx* (Lewis & Short): the one who carries the voice.
