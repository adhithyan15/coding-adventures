Hindi, chapter 153: Garden, Field, Bridge, Office, Bank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बगीचा (bagīcā) — a garden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a temple, then the Hindi for a mosque.

You'll want to know: बगीचा.
बगीचा — bagīcā — "a garden".

The word, taken apart.
बगीचा (bagīcā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian bāghcha, "small garden". It is built on bāgh, "garden".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a field, then the Hindi for a bridge.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the twelve borrowed months, जनवरी to दिसंबर. An older calendar still runs beside them.

You'll want to know: दफ़्तर.
दफ़्तर — daftar — "an office".

The word, taken apart.
दफ़्तर (daftar) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic daftar, "register, account book" (Lane), a word Arabic itself took from Greek.
