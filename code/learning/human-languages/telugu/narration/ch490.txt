Telugu, chapter 490: Torn and Crushed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
చెడగొట్టు (ceḍagoṭṭu) — to spoil; to ruin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to rot, then the Telugu for to be left over.

You'll want to know: చెడగొట్టు.
చెడగొట్టు — ceḍagoṭṭu — "to spoil; to ruin".
వర్షం మా విహారయాత్రను చెడగొట్టింది. varṣaṁ mā vihārayātranu ceḍagoṭṭindi. The rain spoiled our outing.
Past (I …): చెడగొట్టాను, ceḍagoṭṭānu.
చెడ, bad, + కొట్టు, to strike: to make something go bad. Said of plans, food and children who are spoilt.

The word, taken apart.
చెడగొట్టు (ceḍagoṭṭu) is native Dravidian: చెడు, "to go bad" (DEDR 2760), and కొట్టు, "to strike" (DEDR 2063).

Lesson 2 of 5.
చిరిగిపోవు (cirigipōvu) — to get torn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be left over, then the Telugu for to spoil.

You'll want to know: చిరిగిపోవు.
చిరిగిపోవు — cirigipōvu — "to get torn".
ముళ్ళకు తగిలి నా చొక్కా చిరిగిపోయింది. muḷḷaku tagili nā cokkā cirigipōyindi. My shirt caught on the thorns and got torn.
Past (it …): చిరిగిపోయింది, cirigipōyindi.
Said of cloth and paper. చిరుగు is a tear. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: చిరిగిపోయింది, it got torn.

The word, taken apart.
చిరిగిపోవు (cirigipōvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2491 lists Telugu cirugu, "to be torn", beside cīru, "to tear", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: మాసిపోవు.
మాసిపోవు — māsipōvu — "to get grubby; to get soiled".
రెండు రోజుల ప్రయాణంలో నా బట్టలు మాసిపోయాయి. reṇḍu rōjula prayāṇaṁlō nā baṭṭalu māsipōyāyi. After two days on the road my clothes were grubby.
Past (it …): మాసిపోయింది, māsipōyindi.
Said of clothes, walls and hands that dirt has dulled. మాసిన బట్టలు, dirty clothes. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: మాసిపోయింది, it got grubby.

The word, taken apart.
మాసిపోవు (māsipōvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4792 lists Telugu māsi, "dirt", and māyu, "to become dirty, soiled", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 5 of 5.
విరిగిపోవు (virigipōvu) — to break off; to snap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to get grubby, then the Telugu for to split.

You'll want to know: విరిగిపోవు.
విరిగిపోవు — virigipōvu — "to break off; to snap".
గాలికి చెట్టు కొమ్మ విరిగిపోయింది. gāliki ceṭṭu komma virigipōyindi. A branch of the tree snapped in the wind.
Past (it …): విరిగిపోయింది, virigipōyindi.
Said of sticks, bones and chairs; glass and pots crack instead. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: విరిగిపోయింది, it snapped.

The word, taken apart.
విరిగిపోవు (virigipōvu) is native Dravidian: విరుగు, "to break", is of the family of Telugu viriyu, "to burst, to break open" (DEDR 5411), here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
