## You'll want to know: वहिनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C496-SASAR496-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वहिनी** — *vahinī* — "a brother's wife".

> वहिनीने सगळ्यांसाठी स्वयंपाक केला.
>
> *vahinīne sagḷyāṁsāṭhī svayampāk kelā.*
>
> My brother's wife cooked for everyone.

Feminine.

Also a polite way to address a friend's wife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वहिनी** (*vahinī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *vahinī*, "elder brother's wife", under Sanskrit *vadhū*, "bride" (CDIAL 11250).
