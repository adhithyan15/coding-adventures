## Writing

Task 1 is a 220–280-word audience-reframed synthesis of all three night-market
sources. Credit accurate integration of livelihood, unequal distribution,
sleep/air/emergency burdens, access, hidden labour, and a monitored, accountable
revision. A source-by-source list or copied phrases do not satisfy synthesis.

Task 2 is an independent 420–520-word policy text. It should define consent,
representation, equipment validation, uncertainty, privacy, ownership,
attribution or payment, correction, prohibited surveillance/commercial uses,
appeal, review, and decision-relevant success measures. Score external sandhi,
word-final visarga or anusvara, continuous śirorekhā, Devanagari word boundaries,
and Sanskrit vocabulary and grammar. Romanization, non-Devanagari response,
dictionary, grammar reference, translator, generative assistant, spell-checker,
or a copyable answer model is forbidden.

## Speaking

Task 1 is a 330–360-second oral synthesis or mediation presentation using the
complete warehouse packet. Task 2 is a 360–450-second live multiparty
negotiation. Credit explicit decisions, mediation, repair, register control,
safety minima, rights, paid responsibility, evidence, and predeclared review.

Use all six confidential challenge cards in order. Each examiner stimulus is
20–90 seconds; the six candidate responses total 390–480 seconds. Responses are
oral live speech, never writing. Credit reasoned defence, qualification,
responsive reformulation for the named second audience, and conditions for
revision. Preparation is exactly fifteen minutes with paper notes on supplied
materials only. Score pronunciation and fluency in the candidate's documented
Sanskrit tradition. Classical non-Vedic Sanskrit is in scope; Vedic accent and
morphology are out. This project-defined source bundle does not establish
qualified recordings, independent human review, standard setting, mock
readiness, learner proof, or an external qualification.
