## You'll want to know: chuáncāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C412-HARBOUR412-03]; assesses=[] -->

*chuáncāng* — "ship's cabin".

> *nǐ de bāo fàng zài chuáncāng lǐ* — Leave your bag in the cabin.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chuáncāng* is built from 船 'a boat' and *cāng* 'a cabin'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *zywen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the second, so its history is not settled.
