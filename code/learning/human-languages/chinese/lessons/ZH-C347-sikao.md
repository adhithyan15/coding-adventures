## You'll want to know: sīkǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C347-MEANING347-03]; assesses=[] -->

*sīkǎo* — "to think hard; to reflect".

> *tā xǐhuan yí ge rén sīkǎo* — He likes to think things through alone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sīkǎo* is built from 思 'to think' and *kǎo* 'to check', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *si* and *khawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to think of' and the second 'to check'.
