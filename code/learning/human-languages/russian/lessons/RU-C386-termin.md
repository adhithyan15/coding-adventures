## You'll want to know: термин
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C386-DOKLAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**термин** (*términ*) — "a term (a technical word)".

> Этот термин трудно перевести.
>
> *Étot términ trúdno perevestí.*
>
> This term is hard to translate.

Masculine. A technical word, not a period of time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**термин** is usually traced, through western languages, to Latin *terminus* "a boundary, a limit" (Lewis & Short): a word with fixed limits to its meaning.
