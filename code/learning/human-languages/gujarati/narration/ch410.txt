Gujarati, chapter 410: Bathrooms and Hot Water.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બાથરૂમ (bāthrūm) — a bathroom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an inconvenience, then the Gujarati for to welcome.

You'll want to know: બાથરૂમ.
બાથરૂમ — bāthrūm — "a bathroom".
અમારા ઓરડામાં બાથરૂમ નથી. amārā orḍāmā̃ bāthrūm nathī. There is no bathroom in our room.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
બાથરૂમ (bāthrūm) is borrowed from English bathroom.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઉલેચવું (ulecvũ) — to bail out (water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a toilet, then the Gujarati for a water heater.

You'll want to know: ઉલેચવું.
ઉલેચવું — ulecvũ — "to bail out (water)".
વરસાદ પછી અમે ઓરડામાંથી પાણી ઉલેચ્યું. varsād pachī ame orḍāmā̃thī pāṇī ulecyũ. After the rain we bailed the water out of the room.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પાણી ઉલેચ્યું, teṇe pāṇī ulecyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઉલેચવું (ulecvũ) goes back to udriñcati, "empties", a form CDIAL lists beside Sanskrit údricyatē; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to draw out water" (CDIAL 2061).

Lesson 5 of 5.
નિચોવવું (nicovvũ) — to wring out; to squeeze out (water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a water heater, then the Gujarati for to bail out.

You'll want to know: નિચોવવું.
નિચોવવું — nicovvũ — "to wring out; to squeeze out (water)".
મીરાએ નાહીને ભીનો ટુવાલ નિચોવ્યો. Mīrāe nāhīne bhīno ṭuvāl nicovyo. After her bath Mira wrung out the wet towel.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ટુવાલ નિચોવ્યો, teṇe ṭuvāl nicovyo.

The word, taken apart.
નિચોવવું (nicovvũ) goes back to niścyōtayati, "causes to trickle out", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to squeeze out" (CDIAL 7451): you twist a cloth until the water runs out.
