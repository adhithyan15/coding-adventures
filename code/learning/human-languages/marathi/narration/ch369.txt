Lesson 1 of 5.
प्राध्यापक (prādhyāpak) — a professor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to entrust, then the Marathi for to handle.

You'll want to know: प्राध्यापक.
प्राध्यापक — prādhyāpak — "a professor".
आमचे प्राध्यापक खूप छान शिकवतात. āmce prādhyāpak khūp chān śikavtāt. Our professor teaches very well.
Masculine.
Respect takes the plural: प्राध्यापक (prādhyāpak) शिकवतात.

The word, taken apart.
प्राध्यापक (prādhyāpak) is a learned word built from Sanskrit pra-, "forth, before", and adhyāpaka, "teacher" (CDIAL 278).

अभ्यासक्रम — abhyāsakram — "a syllabus; a course of study".
या वर्षी अभ्यासक्रम बदलला आहे. yā varṣī abhyāsakram badallā āhe. The syllabus has changed this year.
Masculine.
Built on अभ्यास, study: the order of study.

The word, taken apart.
अभ्यासक्रम (abhyāsakram) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit abhyāsa, "repetition, study", and krama, "step, series" (Monier-Williams).

शिष्यवृत्ती — śiṣyavṛttī — "a scholarship".
हुशार विद्यार्थ्यांना शिष्यवृत्ती मिळते. huśār vidyārthyānnā śiṣyavṛttī miḷte. Bright students get a scholarship.
Feminine.
शिष्य + वृत्ती, pupil + livelihood.

The word, taken apart.
शिष्यवृत्ती (śiṣyavṛttī) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit śiṣya, "pupil", and vṛtti, "way of life, livelihood" (Monier-Williams): a pupil's living.
