## You'll want to know: べんり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C163-HOME163-03]; assesses=[] -->

**べんり** — *benri* — "convenient; handy".

> **この だいどころは べんりです。** — *kono daidokoro wa benri desu* — "This kitchen is convenient."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**べんり** (*benri*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ben*, "convenience" (Middle Chinese *bjienH*) and *ri*, "profit, advantage" (*lijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
