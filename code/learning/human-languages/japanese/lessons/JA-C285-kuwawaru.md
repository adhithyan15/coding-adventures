## You'll want to know: くわわる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C285-MOYOOSHI285-03]; assesses=[] -->

**くわわる** — *kuwawaru* — "to join (in); to take part".

> **らいねんは ちちも まつりに くわわるでしょう。** — *rainen wa chichi mo matsuri ni kuwawaru deshō* — "Next year my father will probably join the festival too."

Polite **くわわります**, *kuwawarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ともだちが くわわりました。**, *tomodachi ga kuwawarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くわわる** (*kuwawaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
