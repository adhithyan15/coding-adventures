## You'll want to know: 开通
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C410-COACH410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**开通** — *kāitōng* — "to launch a new transport line".

> *wǒmen zhèr kāitōng le gāotiě* — High-speed rail has opened in our area.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开通** (*kāitōng*) is built from 开 'to open' and 通 'to go through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *thuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
