# ওখানে (okhāne) — there

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C26-EKHANE-01, BN-LEX-C21-OI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **the tea is here**. Then say **that shirt**. Now guess this
lesson's word before you read it.

## You'll want to know: ওখানে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C26-OKHANE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C26-EKHANE-01, BN-LEX-C21-OI-01] -->

> **সে ওখানে** — *she okhāne* — **he is there.**

If you guessed it, that is the lesson. Swap the head and cross the room, exactly
as **এই** became **ওই** five chapters ago. The frame does not change; only the
first letter does.

- **এখানে** — here, where I am
- **ওখানে** — there, where I am not

Two places, as there were two distances. Bengali does not add a third.

## Grammar Lens: eight of nine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C26-OKHANE-01, BN-LEX-C26-EKHANE-01, BN-LEX-C23-O-PRON-01, BN-LEX-C21-EI-01] -->

| head | frame | word |
|---|---|---|
| **এ-** near | place | **এখানে** |
| **ও-** far | place | **ওখানে** |
| **এ-** near | thing | **এটা** |
| **ও-** far | thing | **ওটা** |
| **ক-** asking | thing | **কী** |
| **ক-** asking | person | **কে** |
| **ক-** asking | place | *one cell left* |

Eight cells filled. The ninth is the next lesson, and it is the question an A1
examiner asks in the first minute of a speaking test.
