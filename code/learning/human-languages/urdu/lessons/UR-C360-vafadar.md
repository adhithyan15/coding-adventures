## You'll want to know: وفادار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C360-RAHM360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**وفادار** — *vafādār* — "loyal; faithful".

> کتا ایک وفادار جانور ہے۔
>
> *kuttā ek vafādār jānvar hai.*
>
> The dog is a loyal animal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وفادار** (*vafādār*) is usually traced to Persian *vafādār*, "keeping faith": *vafā*, "faithfulness", with *-dār*, "holding".
