## You'll want to know: दोहितो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C261-MASI261-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दोहितो** — *dohito* — "a daughter's son; a grandson".

> म्हारो दोहितो गीत गावै है।
>
> *mhāro dohito gīt gāvai hai.*
>
> My daughter's son sings songs.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दोहितो** (*dohito*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dauhitra*, "a daughter's son". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *dohtā* and Gujarati *dohitra* (CDIAL 6605).
