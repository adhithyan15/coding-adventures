## You'll want to know: ડગવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C389-NIRNAY389-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ડગવું** — *ḍagvũ* — "to waver".

> જો તમે તમારા નિર્ણયથી ડગશો તો બીજા તમારો વિશ્વાસ નહીં કરે.
>
> *jo tame tamārā nirṇaythī ḍagśo to bījā tamāro viśvās nahī̃ kare.*
>
> If you waver from your decision, others will not trust you.

Past (he …): તે એક પળ માટે ડગ્યો, *te ek paḷ māṭe ḍagyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડગવું** (*ḍagvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to tremble", under a reconstructed *ḍag* (CDIAL 5522); "to waver", a shaking of the mind, is a sense CDIAL does not give.
