## You'll want to know: biāozhǔn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C364-ALLOW364-03]; assesses=[] -->

*biāozhǔn* — "standard to measure by".

> *wǒ juéde zhège biāozhǔn tài gāo le* — I think the standard is set too high.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biāozhǔn* is built from *biāo* and 准 'level', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjiew* and *tsywinX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to raise' and 'the tip of a tree', and the second 'level'.
