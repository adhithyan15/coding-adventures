# از دست دادن (az dast dâdan) — I missed the train

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C141-KONESH141-01, FA-LEX-C66-TIME66-04, FA-LEX-RAFT-O-BARGASHT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to lose", then "late". (*Gom kardan*, *dir*.) Then ask for a
return ticket to Tehran. (*Yek bilit-e raft-o bargasht be Tehrân mikhâham.*)

[PAUSE 1s] A ticket does not guarantee the train. Sooner or later something
goes wrong, and you have to say what.

## You'll want to know: از دست دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-AZ-DAST-DADAN-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-DAST, FA-LEX-C37-VERBS37-01, FA-LEX-C70-TOWN70-05, FA-GRAMMAR-RA-OBJECT-01] -->

**از دست دادن** — *az dast dâdan* — "to miss" a train or a bus, "to lose" a
chance.

> **قطار را از دست دادم.** — *qatâr râ az dast dâdam* — I missed the train.

Word for word, "I gave the train from the hand". The train is a particular
one, so it takes *râ*. Only *dâdan* changes: *az dast midaham*, I miss; *az
dast dâdam*, I missed.

## Grammar Lens: saying what went wrong
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-PAST-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C49-SCHOOL49-04, FA-LEX-C133-ACT133-04, FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01, FA-GRAMMAR-SUBJUNCTIVE-BE-01, FA-LEX-C217-KONESH217-02] -->

A problem is reported in the plain past: it happened, and it is done.

> **کیف من را گم کردم.** — *kif-e man râ gom kardam* — I lost my bag.

> **دیر رسیدم و قطار رفت.** — *dir residam va qatâr raft* — I arrived late,
> and the train left.

Then the question that asks for help, in the subjunctive, because the doing
has not happened yet:

> **چه کار کنم؟** — *che kâr konam?* — What should I do?

*Che*, "what", is the *che* inside *chetor*; *kâr konam* is *kâr kardan*, "to
work, to do", in the subjunctive without *be-*, as compounds often go.

## The phrase, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

All three words are Persian. *Dast* is "hand", inherited from Old Persian
(EtymDB 2.1). *Dâdan* is "to give", on the Indo-European root EtymDB also
gives for Latin *dō*. *Az* is "from". What you let slip from your hand is
gone: a train, a chance.
