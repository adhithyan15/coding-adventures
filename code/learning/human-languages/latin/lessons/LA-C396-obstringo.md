## You'll want to know: obstringō, obstringere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C396-POLLICEOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**obstringō, obstringere** — "to bind, to put under an obligation".

> Beneficiō mē obstrīnxistī.
>
> *You have bound me by your kindness.*

*obstringō, obstringere, obstrīnxī, obstrictum*: to tie fast, then to bind by a promise or a favour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obstringō* is built from *ob* and *stringō* "to draw tight, to bind" (Lewis & Short): to tie someone fast to a duty.
