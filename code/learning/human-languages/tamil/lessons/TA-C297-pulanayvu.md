# புலனாய்வு (pulaṉāyvu) — an investigation

## You'll want to know: புலனாய்வு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C297-KADATHAL297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**புலனாய்வு** — *pulaṉāyvu* — "an investigation".

> இந்தக் கொள்ளை பற்றிப் புலனாய்வு தொடங்கியது.
>
> *intak koḷḷai paṟṟip pulaṉāyvu toṭaṅkiyatu.*
>
> An investigation into this robbery began.

News reports say புலனாய்வு நடக்கிறது, an investigation is under way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**புலனாய்வு** (*pulaṉāyvu*) is a Tamil compound built from புலன், "the senses, perception", and ஆய்வு, "examination", from ஆய், "to search, examine" (DEDR 363).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
