## You'll want to know: oblīquus, oblīqua, oblīquum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C454-DEXTRORSUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**oblīquus, oblīqua, oblīquum** — "slanting, sideways, at an angle".

> Sēmita oblīqua per collem ascendit.
>
> *The path climbs across the hill at a slant.*

An adjective of three endings. *Oblīquō itinere*, by a sideways route. English *oblique* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*oblīquus* is built from *ob-* and an old root meaning 'bent, slanting' (Lewis & Short); the exact source of that root is not settled. English *oblique* comes from it.
