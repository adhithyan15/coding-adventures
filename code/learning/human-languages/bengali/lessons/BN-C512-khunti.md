## You'll want to know: খুন্তি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C512-UNUN512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**খুন্তি** — *khunti* — "a spatula; a flat cooking spoon".

> আমি খুন্তিটা কড়াইয়ের পাশে রাখলাম।
>
> *Āmi khuntiṭā kôṛāiyer pāshe rākhlām.*
>
> I put the spatula beside the wok.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খুন্তি** (*khunti*) is a tadbhava from Sanskrit *khanitra*, "a digging tool"; CDIAL lists Bengali *khunti* for a long-handled digging tool (CDIAL 3814). The kitchen sense, a spatula, is the modern language's own.
