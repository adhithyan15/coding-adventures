## You'll want to know: حظر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C451-MAKHFAR451-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حظر** — *ḥaẓara* — "to ban".

> حظرت البلدية السيارات في وسط المدينة.
>
> *ḥaẓarat al-baladiyya as-sayyārāt fī wasaṭ al-madīna.*
>
> The town hall banned cars in the city centre.

Present: يحظر, *yaḥẓur*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حظر** (*ḥaẓara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ẓ-r*, "to forbid; to fence in" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
