## You'll want to know: امکان پذیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C398-AMALI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**امکان پذیر** (*emkân pazir*) — "possible, feasible".

> این طرح با این پول امکان پذیر نیست.
>
> *in tarh bâ in pul emkân pazir nist.*
>
> This plan is not feasible with this money.

*Emkân* "possibility" + *pazir* "accepting", from *paziroftan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امکان پذیر** joins *emkân* "possibility", usually traced to Arabic *imkān*, to *pazir* "accepting", the present stem of the inherited verb *paziroftan* (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian): open to being possible.
