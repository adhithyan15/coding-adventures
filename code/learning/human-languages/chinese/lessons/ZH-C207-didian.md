## You'll want to know: 地点
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C207-PLACE207-01]; assesses=[] -->

**地点** — *dìdiǎn* — "place; venue".

> **我们见面的地点离这儿两公里** *wǒmen jiànmiàn de dìdiǎn lí zhèr liǎng gōnglǐ* — The place where we're meeting is two kilometres from here.

The new character, in pieces: 地 — six strokes: on the left the earth piece, a short bar, a vertical and a stroke rising to the right, as at the lower right of 在; on the right 也. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**地点** (*dìdiǎn*) is built from 地 'ground, place' and 点 'a point', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *temX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the earth'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
