## You'll want to know: અંદાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C529-TABAKKO529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અંદાજ** — *andāj* — "an estimate".

> જો મારો અંદાજ સાચો હશે તો આ કામમાં બે દિવસ લાગશે.
>
> *jo māro andāj sāco haśe to ā kāmmā̃ be divas lāgśe.*
>
> If my estimate is right, this job will take two days.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અંદાજ** (*andāj*) is usually traced to Persian *andāza*, "a measure, an estimate".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
