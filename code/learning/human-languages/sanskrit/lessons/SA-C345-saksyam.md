## You'll want to know: साक्ष्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C345-PRATYAYA345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**साक्ष्यम्** — *sākṣyam* — "testimony; evidence".

> न्यायाधीशः साक्ष्यं शृणोति।
>
> *nyāyādhīśaḥ sākṣyaṃ śṛṇoti.*
>
> The judge hears the testimony.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साक्ष्यम्** (*sākṣyam*) is built on *sākṣin*, "an eyewitness"; MW gives *sākṣya* "testimony, evidence, attestation" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
