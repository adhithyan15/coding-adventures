## You'll want to know: مرآب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C436-KUSHK436-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مرآب** — *mirʾāb* — "a garage; a car park".

> ترك أبي السيارة في المرآب.
>
> *taraka abī as-sayyāra fī al-mirʾāb.*
>
> My father left the car in the garage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرآب** (*mirʾāb*) is a modern coinage, usually traced to the native root *r-ʾ-b*, "to repair, to mend" (Lane), in the tool-and-place pattern *mifʿāl*: a place where cars are kept and seen to.
