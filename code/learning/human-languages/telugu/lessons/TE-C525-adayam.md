## You'll want to know: ఆదాయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C525-SAMPADANA525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆదాయం** — *ādāyaṁ* — "income".

> పంట బాగా పండితే రైతుల ఆదాయం పెరుగుతుంది.
>
> *paṇṭa bāgā paṇḍitē raitula ādāyaṁ perugutundi.*
>
> If the harvest is good, the farmers' income rises.

The opposite of ఖర్చు, expense.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆదాయం** (*ādāyaṁ*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *ādāya*, "taking, seizing" (Monier-Williams); compare Sanskrit *āya*, "arrival; income" (CDIAL 1283).
