# ನಾಟಕ (nāṭaka) — a stage play; a drama

## You'll want to know: ನಾಟಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C536-NATAKA536-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಟಕ** — *nāṭaka* — "a stage play; a drama".

> ಶಾಲೆಯ ನಾಟಕದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ರಾಜನ ಪಾತ್ರ ಮಾಡಿದೆ.
>
> *śāleya nāṭakadalli nānu rājana pātra māḍide.*
>
> I played the king's part in the school play.

ನಾಟಕ ಆಡು, to stage a play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಟಕ** (*nāṭaka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nāṭaka*, "a dancer, an actor", and later a drama (CDIAL 7045), built on *naṭa*, "an actor".
