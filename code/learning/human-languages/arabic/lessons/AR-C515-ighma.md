# إغماء (ighmāʾ) — fainting, a faint

## You'll want to know: إغماء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C515-NAWM515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**إغماء** — *ighmāʾ* — "fainting, a faint".

> نقلوا اللاعب إلى المستشفى بعد الإغماء.
>
> *naqalū al-lāʿib ilā al-mustashfā baʿda al-ighmāʾ.*
>
> They took the player to hospital after he fainted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إغماء** (*ighmāʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-m-y*, "to cover, to veil" (Lane). Ighmāʾ is the verbal noun of Form IV: being covered over, losing one's senses.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
