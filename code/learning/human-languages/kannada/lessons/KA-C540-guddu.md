# ಗುದ್ದು (guddu) — to punch

## You'll want to know: ಗುದ್ದು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C540-KUSTI540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುದ್ದು** — *guddu* — "to punch".

> ಆಟದಲ್ಲಿ ತಮ್ಮ ನನ್ನ ಬೆನ್ನಿಗೆ ಗುದ್ದಿದನು.
>
> *āṭadalli tamma nanna bennige guddidanu.*
>
> In the game my younger brother punched my back.

Past (I …): ಗುದ್ದಿದೆ, *guddide*.

Also a noun, a punch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುದ್ದು** (*guddu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1850 lists Kannada *kuttu*, "to beat, strike", and *guddu*, "to strike with the fist".
