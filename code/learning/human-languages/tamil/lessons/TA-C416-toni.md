## You'll want to know: தோணி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C416-PATAKU416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**தோணி** — *tōṇi* — "a dinghy; a small boat".

> மீனவர்கள் தோணியில் கடலுக்குப் போனார்கள்.
>
> *mīṉavarkaḷ tōṇiyil kaṭalukkup pōṉārkaḷ.*
>
> The fishermen went to sea in a small boat.

The plural is தோணிகள்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தோணி** (*tōṇi*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *droṇī*, "a wooden trough, a tub" (CDIAL 6641).
