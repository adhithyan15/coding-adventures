## You'll want to know: इंटरव्यू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C508-NAUKRI508-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इंटरव्यू** — *iṇṭarvyū* — "an interview (for a job or a place)".

> कल सुबह दस बजे मेरा इंटरव्यू है।
>
> *Kal subah das baje merā iṇṭarvyū hai.*
>
> My interview is at ten tomorrow morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इंटरव्यू** (*iṇṭarvyū*) is a loan from English *interview*.
