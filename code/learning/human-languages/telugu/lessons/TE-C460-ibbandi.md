# ఇబ్బంది (ibbandi) — trouble; difficulty

## You'll want to know: ఇబ్బంది
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C460-ASAUKARYAM460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇబ్బంది** — *ibbandi* — "trouble; difficulty".

> కొత్త నగరంలో భాష వల్ల ఇబ్బంది పడ్డాం.
>
> *kotta nagaraṁlō bhāṣa valla ibbandi paḍḍāṁ.*
>
> In the new city we had trouble because of the language.

ఇబ్బంది పడు, to have trouble; ఇబ్బంది పెట్టు, to cause someone trouble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇబ్బంది** (*ibbandi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 524 lists Telugu *ibbandi*, "difficulty, inconvenience", beside *iṟuku*, "to be squeezed, to be stuck in", and *ikkaṭṭu*, "hardship". A tight spot became a difficulty.
