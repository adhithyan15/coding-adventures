## You'll want to know: el olor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C775-NEVERA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el olor** — "the smell".

> Me encanta el olor del pan recién hecho.
>
> *I love the smell of freshly baked bread.*

*Olor a* — a smell of: *olor a café*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*olor* is usually traced to Latin *olor* 'a smell' (Lewis & Short), a rare by-form of *odor* shaped by the verb *olēre* 'to give off a smell'.
