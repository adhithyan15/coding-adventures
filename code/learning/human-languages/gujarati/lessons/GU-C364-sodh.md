## You'll want to know: શોધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C364-SHODH364-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શોધ** — *śodh* — "a discovery".

> આ દવા એક મોટી શોધ છે.
>
> *ā davā ek moṭī śodh che.*
>
> This medicine is a major discovery.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શોધ** (*śodh*) is the noun of **શોધવું** (*śodhvũ*), "to look for", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *śoddhum*, "to purify" (CDIAL 12626): what a search turns up.
