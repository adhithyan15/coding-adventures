## You'll want to know: शोक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C505-SHOK505-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शोक** — *śok* — "mourning; grief".

> दादा की मौत पर पूरे गाँव ने शोक मनाया।
>
> *dādā kī maut par pūre gā̃v ne śok manāyā.*
>
> The whole village mourned my grandfather's death.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शोक** (*śok*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *śoka*, "sorrow, anguish, grief" (Monier-Williams). *śok manānā* is to observe mourning.
