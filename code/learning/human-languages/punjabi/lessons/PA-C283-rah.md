## You'll want to know: ਰਾਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C283-ROAD283-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਾਹ** — *rāh* — "a route; the way".

> ਪਿੰਡ ਦਾ ਰਾਹ ਲੰਮਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *piṇḍ dā rāh lammā hai.*
>
> The way to the village is long.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਾਹ** (*rāh*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *rāh*, "road, way".
