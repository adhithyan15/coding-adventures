## You'll want to know: کیاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C512-KYARI512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کیاری** — *kyārī* — "a flower bed".

> مالی نے نئی کیاری بنائی۔
>
> *mālī ne naī kyārī banāī.*
>
> The gardener made a new flower bed.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کیاری** (*kyārī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kyārī*, "a garden bed", under Sanskrit *kēdāra*, "a field, especially a flooded one" (CDIAL 3463).
