## You'll want to know: અગવડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C409-LODGE409-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અગવડ** — *agvaḍ* — "an inconvenience; a discomfort".

> નાના ઓરડામાં અમને ઘણી અગવડ પડી.
>
> *nānā orḍāmā̃ amne ghaṇī agvaḍ paḍī.*
>
> We were very uncomfortable in the small room.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અગવડ** (*agvaḍ*) is the opposite of સગવડ, a convenience, and the two are usually heard together. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
