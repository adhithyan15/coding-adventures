## You'll want to know: motivar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C594-EQUIPO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**motivar** — "to motivate".

> Un buen jefe sabe motivar a la gente.
>
> *A good boss knows how to motivate people.*

*Estar motivado* — to be motivated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*motivar* is built on *motivo*, a learned borrowing from Late Latin *motivus* 'moving' (EtymDB), from *movere* 'to move'.
