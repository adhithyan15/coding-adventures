## You'll want to know: पंगत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C321-PANGAT321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पंगत** — *paṅgat* — "a row of diners".

> लग्नात पाहुण्यांच्या पंगती बसल्या.
>
> *lagnāt pāhuṇyāñcyā paṅgtī basalyā.*
>
> At the wedding the guests sat down in rows to eat.

Feminine. Plural: पंगती, *paṅgtī*.

पंगत बसणे, a row sits down to a meal; plural पंगती.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पंगत** (*paṅgat*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *paṅkti*, 'a row, a troop'. CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi *pā̃t*, 'a row', under that word (CDIAL 7646) but not *paṅgat* itself, so its path is not settled.
