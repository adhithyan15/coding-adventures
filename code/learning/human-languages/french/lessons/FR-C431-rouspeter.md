## You'll want to know: rouspéter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C431-RECLAMATION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rouspéter** — "to grumble, to moan".

> Mon oncle rouspète toujours contre le service.
>
> *My uncle is forever grumbling about the service.*

Informal. *Il rouspète* — he moans.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *rouspéter* is not settled; it is an old slang word. EtymDB lists it without a source.
