# জাহির করা (jāhir kôrā) — to show off; to flaunt

## You'll want to know: জাহির করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C452-DEMAK452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**জাহির করা** — *jāhir kôrā* — "to show off; to flaunt".

> অরুণ বন্ধুদের সামনে নতুন ফোনটা জাহির করছিল।
>
> *ôrun bôndhuder sāmne nôtun phonṭā jāhir kôrchhilô.*
>
> Arun was showing off his new phone in front of his friends.

Past (he/she …): তিনি ছেলের সাফল্য জাহির করলেন, *tini chheler sāphôlyô jāhir kôrlen*.

What is shown off is the object: জ্ঞান জাহির করা, to show off what you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জাহির করা** (*jāhir kôrā*) is *jāhir*, "made plain, on show", usually traced to Arabic *ẓāhir*, "manifest", through Persian, with *kôrā*, "to do" (CDIAL 2814): to put something where everyone must see it.
