## You'll want to know: गलत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C271-SAHI271-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गलत** — *galat* — "wrong".

> यो सबद गलत है।
>
> *yo sabad galat hai.*
>
> This word is wrong.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गलत** (*galat*) is usually traced, through Persian *ghalat*, to Arabic *ghalaṭ*, "error". Like **सही**, it does not change.
