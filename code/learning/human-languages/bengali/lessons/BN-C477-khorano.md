## You'll want to know: খোঁড়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C477-JBOLUNI477-04]; assesses=[] -->

**খোঁড়ানো** — *khõṛāno* — "to limp".

> চোট পেয়ে কুকুরটা খোঁড়াচ্ছিল।
>
> *choṭ peye kukurṭā khõṛāchchhilô.*
>
> The dog was limping after hurting its leg.

Past (he/she …): তিনি খোঁড়ালেন, *tini khõṛālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খোঁড়ানো** (*khõṛāno*) is the verb of *khõṛā*, "lame", which CDIAL lists for Bengali (CDIAL 3941).
