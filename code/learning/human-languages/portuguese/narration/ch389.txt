Portuguese, chapter 389: O crime não compensa.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o crime — the crime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the conviction, then the Portuguese for the punishment.

You'll want to know: o crime.
o crime — "the crime".
O crime aconteceu de madrugada. The crime happened in the early hours.
Cometer um crime — to commit a crime.

The word, taken apart.
crime was borrowed from French crime (EtymDB), from Latin crīmen "an accusation, a charge, a crime" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: o criminoso.

The word, taken apart.
criminoso is a learned borrowing from Latin crīminōsus "reproachful, criminal" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on crīmen "a charge, a crime".

Lesson 4 of 5.
a sanção — the sanction, the penalty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the criminal, then the Portuguese for the corruption.

You'll want to know: a sanção.
a sanção — "the sanction, the penalty".
A empresa sofreu sanções por poluir o rio. The company faced sanctions for polluting the river.
Aplicar uma sanção — to impose a sanction.

The word, taken apart.
sanção is a learned borrowing from Latin sānctiō "a decree, a penalty clause" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from sancīre "to make sacred, to decree".
