## You'll want to know: अर्पयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C328-VRATA328-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्पयति** — *arpayati* — "offers".

> बालिका पुष्पाणि अर्पयति।
>
> *bālikā puṣpāṇi arpayati.*
>
> The girl offers flowers.

Past (she …): सा पुष्पाणि अर्पितवती, *sā puṣpāṇi arpitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्पयति** (*arpayati*) comes from the root √ṛ, "to go, move": MW lists *arpaya* as its causative, to make go, hand over, offer (MW).
