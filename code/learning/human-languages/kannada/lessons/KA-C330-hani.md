# ಹಾನಿ (hāni) — damage; harm

## You'll want to know: ಹಾನಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C330-DIKKI330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾನಿ** — *hāni* — "damage; harm".

> ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಕಾರಿಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಹಾನಿ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *apaghātadalli kārige tumbā hāni āyitu.*
>
> The car was badly damaged in the accident.

What is damaged takes -ಗೆ: ಕಾರಿಗೆ ಹಾನಿ ಆಯಿತು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾನಿ** (*hāni*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *hāni*, "abandonment, loss, decrease" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
