## You'll want to know: o judo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C514-RINGUE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o judo** — "judo".

> A minha filha anda no judo há três anos.
>
> *My daughter has been doing judo for three years.*

In Brazil: *o judô*. *Andar no judo* — to do judo regularly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*judo* was borrowed from Japanese *jūdō* "the gentle way".
