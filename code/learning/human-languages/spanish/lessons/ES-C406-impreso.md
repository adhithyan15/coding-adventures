# el impreso — a form waiting for your data

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-DOCUMENTO, ES-LEX-DATO, ES-LEX-C301-ASK-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *the document*, *the data* and *the paper*. (*El documento, los
datos, el papel*.) Put blank spaces on that paper and you have **el impreso**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-IMPRESO]; assesses=[] -->

**Impreso** is the irregular past participle of **imprimir**, “to print,” from
Latin **impressus**, “pressed into.” Printing once meant pressing inked type
onto paper. English **impress** preserves the same image.

As a noun, **el impreso** can mean a printed sheet, especially a form with blank
spaces for a procedure. In much of the Spanish-speaking world you will also
hear **el formulario**. Here the exam-derived word is **impreso**.

## Grammar Lens: a form has two sides
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-IMPRESO, ES-LEX-DATO] -->

The **impreso** supplies the labels; you supply **los datos**:

- **Nombre:** Ana
- **Dirección:** Calle Sol, 4
