# おさめる (osameru) — to pay taxes; to pay (a fee); to put away

## You'll want to know: おさめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C364-SONGAI364-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おさめる** — *osameru* — "to pay taxes; to pay (a fee); to put away".

> **らいげつに なったら、ぜいきんを おさめます。** — *raigetsu ni nattara, zeikin o osamemasu* — "Next month, I will pay my taxes."

Polite **おさめます**, *osamemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが ぜいきんを おさめました。**, *chichi ga zeikin o osamemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おさめる** (*osameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
