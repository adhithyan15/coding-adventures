Lesson 1 of 6.
दन्तधावनम् (dantadhāvanam) — brushing the teeth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an oil massage, then the Sanskrit for the hour before dawn.

You'll want to know: दन्तधावनम्.
दन्तधावनम् — dantadhāvanam — "brushing the teeth".
प्रातः दन्तधावनं करोतु। prātaḥ dantadhāvanaṃ karotu. Brush your teeth in the morning.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
दन्तधावनम् (dantadhāvanam) is built from दन्तः, "a tooth", and dhāvana, "cleansing", from the root √dhāv, "to rinse"; MW records it from the Kauśika Sūtra on (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
परिदधाति (paridadhāti) — puts on clothes; wears.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for kohl, then the Sanskrit for a scented cosmetic.

You'll want to know: परिदधाति.
परिदधाति — paridadhāti — "puts on clothes; wears".
उत्सवे मीरा नवं वस्त्रं परिदधाति। utsave mīrā navaṃ vastraṃ paridadhāti. At the festival Mira puts on new clothes.
Past (she …): सा नवं वस्त्रं परिहितवती, sā navaṃ vastraṃ parihitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
परिदधाति (paridadhāti) is built from pari, "around", and the root √dhā, "to put": to put round oneself; MW gives "to put on, wear, dress", and its ppp parihita is "put on, clothed" (MW).
