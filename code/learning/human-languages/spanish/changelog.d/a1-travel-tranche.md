### Spanish A1 travel tranche — five exam-derived words (chapter 400)

Chapter 400 adds **el tren**, **el vuelo**, **el aparcamiento**, **el abono**, and
**la playa** as a connected travel sequence. The lessons keep the easily confused
*tren/tres* and *playa/plaza* apart, distinguish the transport sense of *abono*,
and trace *aparcamiento* through Spanish *parque* rather than English *park*.

The chapter ships with its authored capability, curriculum path and required
extension, generated book chapter, narration, modality owners, and progress and
gentle-ramp evidence.
