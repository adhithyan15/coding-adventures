## You'll want to know: اجداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C315-AJDAD315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اجداد** — *ajdād* — "ancestors".

> ہمارے اجداد کسان تھے۔
>
> *hamāre ajdād kisān the.*
>
> Our ancestors were farmers.

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجداد** (*ajdād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ajdād*, the plural of *jadd*, "a grandfather": the grandfathers of the grandfathers.
