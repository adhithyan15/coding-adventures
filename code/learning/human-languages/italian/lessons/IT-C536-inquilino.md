## You'll want to know: l'inquilino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C536-CASA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'inquilino** — "tenant".

> I nuovi inquilini del secondo piano sono molto gentili.
>
> *The new tenants on the second floor are very nice.*

A woman is *l'inquilina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inquilino* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inquilīnus* "a tenant, a lodger" (EtymDB).
