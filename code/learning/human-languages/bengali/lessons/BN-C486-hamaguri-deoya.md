# হামাগুড়ি দেওয়া (hāmāguṛi deoyā) — to crawl (on all fours)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C486-LALON486-03, BN-LEX-C486-LALON486-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for to suck, then the Bengali for to put to sleep.

## You'll want to know: হামাগুড়ি দেওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C486-LALON486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**হামাগুড়ি দেওয়া** — *hāmāguṛi deoyā* — "to crawl (on all fours)".

> আমার ভাইঝি এখন হামাগুড়ি দেয়।
>
> *āmār bhāijhi ekhôn hāmāguṛi dey.*
>
> My niece crawls now.

Past (he/she …): সেই শিশু হামাগুড়ি দিল, *sei shishu hāmāguṛi dilô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হামাগুড়ি দেওয়া** (*hāmāguṛi deoyā*) is *hāmāguṛi*, "crawling", built on the *hāmā-* that CDIAL lists for Bengali as crawling on all fours (CDIAL 13975), with *deoyā*, "to give" (CDIAL 6141).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
