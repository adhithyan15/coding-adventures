# ੱ · ਧ — two pieces met inside a word you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-RRA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-GHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-KHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-JHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PAIRIN-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AUNKAR-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TIPPI-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DULAINKAR-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-OORA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-CHA-01, PA-LEX-PAANI, PA-PHRASE-KIRPA-KARKE, PA-ETYMON-PAANI-DRINK, PA-LEX-CHA, PA-ETYMON-CHA-CHINESE, PA-SOUND-TONE-HIGH-LEVEL] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A mark that makes no sound of its own, and still changes what a word means.

First, say the last things you learned once more, out loud and from
memory:

- [YOU SAY: *pāṇī* · *cāh*]

## Script you'll notice: ੱ · ਧ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-ADDAK-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ੱ

**aḍḍak** — *(doubled)*. Not a sound of its own. It sits above the line and says **double the consonant that follows** — and doubling changes meaning in Punjabi, so this small mark is load-bearing.

> ਧ

**dhaddā** — *dha*. A third letter whose breath became **pitch**: written breathy-*d*, said today with a falling tone.

Now put them where you have already heard them:

> **ਦ** + **ੁ** + **ੱ** + **ਧ** → **ਦੁੱਧ** (*dudh*)
>
> **ਦੁੱਧ** — *dudh* — **milk**

You are not being asked to write any of this. You are being asked to
**recognise** it the next time it goes past.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-ADDAK-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DHA-01] -->

Keep ੱ · ਧ in front of you and do not cover any of them. Follow each shape once with a
finger, then copy it once with a pencil, larger than it is printed. Say its
sound as your hand stops.

> This is **observe-and-trace with the model visible**. Nothing in these
> chapters asks you to write a Punjabi word from memory; that ladder starts
> later, and it starts from these same shapes.
