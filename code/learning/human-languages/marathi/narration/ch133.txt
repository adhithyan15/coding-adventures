Marathi, chapter 133: Soap, Box, Bag, Basket, Candle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
साबण (sābaṇ) — soap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a thread, then the Marathi for a comb.

You'll want to know: साबण.
साबण — sābaṇ — "soap".

The word, taken apart.
साबण (sābaṇ) is a loan from Arabic ṣābūn, "soap", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṣ-b-n (Lane). Latin sāpō lies further back.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पेटी (peṭī) — a box.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a comb, then the Marathi for soap.

You'll want to know: पेटी.
पेटी — peṭī — "a box".

The word, taken apart.
पेटी (peṭī) comes from pēṭṭa, one of the forms CDIAL reconstructs beside Sanskrit piṭaka, "basket, box" (CDIAL 8164). CDIAL calls the word non-Aryan.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मेणबत्ती (meṇbattī) — a candle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a bag, then the Marathi for a basket.

You'll want to know: मेणबत्ती.
मेणबत्ती — meṇbattī — "a candle".

The word, taken apart.
मेणबत्ती (meṇbattī) is "wax-lamp": meṇ, "beeswax", from Sanskrit madana, "beeswax" (CDIAL 9778), and battī, "wick, lamp", from Sanskrit varti, "wick" (CDIAL 11359).
