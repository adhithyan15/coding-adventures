## You'll want to know: निर्बंध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C378-ATI378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्बंध** — *nirbandh* — "a curb; a restriction".

> पुरामुळे प्रवासावर निर्बंध घातले आहेत.
>
> *purāmuḷe pravāsāvar nirbandh ghātle āhet.*
>
> Restrictions have been placed on travel because of the flood.

Masculine.

Mostly plural: निर्बंध घालणे, to impose restrictions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्बंध** (*nirbandh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nirbandha*, "perseverance, insistence" (CDIAL 7355); Marathi uses it for a binding limit.
