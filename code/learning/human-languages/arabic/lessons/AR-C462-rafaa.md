## You'll want to know: رفع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C462-ISTIDAD462-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رفع** — *rafaʿa* — "to raise; to lift up".

> رفع الحمال الحقيبة الثقيلة.
>
> *rafaʿa al-ḥammāl al-ḥaqība ath-thaqīla.*
>
> The porter lifted the heavy bag.

Present: يرفع, *yarfaʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رفع** (*rafaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-f-ʿ*, "to raise" (Lane). It is the plain verb; *murtafiʿ*, "high", shares the root.
