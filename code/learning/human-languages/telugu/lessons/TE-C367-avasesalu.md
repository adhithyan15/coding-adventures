# అవశేషాలు (avaśēṣālu) — remains; relics

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for ancient, then the Telugu for excavations.

## You'll want to know: అవశేషాలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C367-TAVVAKALU367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అవశేషాలు** — *avaśēṣālu* — "remains; relics".

> అక్కడ పాత నగరం అవశేషాలు దొరికాయి.
>
> *akkaḍa pāta nagaraṁ avaśēṣālu dorikāyi.*
>
> The remains of an old city were found there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అవశేషాలు** (*avaśēṣālu*) is built on Sanskrit *avaśeṣa*, "leavings, remainder" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for telling what happened.
