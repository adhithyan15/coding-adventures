## You'll want to know: la vittima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C290-DELITTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la vittima** — "the victim".

> La vittima è un uomo di cinquant'anni.
>
> *The victim is a fifty-year-old man.*

Feminine in grammar, whoever the person is.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vittima* is a learned borrowing from Latin *victima* "a beast for sacrifice" (EtymDB).
