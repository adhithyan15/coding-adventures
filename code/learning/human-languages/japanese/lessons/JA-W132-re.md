## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-RE-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-WA-01, JA-SCRIPT-NE-01] -->

> **れ** — *re*

Two strokes, one lift:

1. the long **vertical**, from top to bottom
2. a short **bar** from the left across the vertical; without lifting, turn
   sharply and cut **down and to the left** through the vertical, climb back up
   that line and rise to the right over an **arch**, come down its right side,
   round the foot and **flick** up to the right

The first stroke and the zigzag are the ones your hand already makes in **わ** and
**ね**. Only the ending of the second stroke is new:

| sign | how the second stroke ends |
|---|---|
| **わ** | a broad belly that curves back to the left |
| **ね** | a small loop at the lower right |
| **れ** | a drop and a flick out to the right |

With **こ** in front of it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**これ**, *kore*, this.
