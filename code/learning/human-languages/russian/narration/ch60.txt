Russian, chapter 60: Dog, Cat, Horse, Cow, Pig.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
собака (sobáka) — a dog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the weather, then the Russian for the world; peace.

You'll want to know: собака.
собака (sobáka) — "a dog".

What you've built.
One more word for dog, cat, horse, cow, pig.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 3 seconds]
