## You'll want to know: der Schlitten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C512-WINTERSPORT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schlitten** — "sledge, sled".

> Die Kinder fahren mit dem Schlitten den Hügel hinunter.
>
> *The children sledge down the hill.*

Plural: *die Schlitten*. *Schlitten fahren* — to go sledging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schlitten* is inherited from Middle High German *slite* and Old High German *slito* "sled", from Proto-Germanic *slidô* (EtymDB); English *sled* is a cousin.
