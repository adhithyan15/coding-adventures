Gujarati, chapter 127: Brain, Bicycle, Ship, Traveller, Hospital.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
મગજ (magaj) — a brain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a palm, then the Gujarati for a fingernail.

You'll want to know: મગજ.
મગજ (magaj) — "a brain".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: magaj]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: magaj, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say magaj]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a palm, then the Gujarati for a fingernail, then say magaj again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does મગજ (magaj) mean? ("A brain".) Say it once more.

(અંધારું.) Light? (પ્રકાશ.) Shade? (છાંયો.) A rabbit? (સસલું.) A deer? (હરણ.) A bear? (રીંછ.) A jackal? (શિયાળ.) A wolf? (વરુ.) A pigeon? (કબૂતર.) A duck? (બતક.)
