Marathi, chapter 303: Once Upon a Time.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
एकेकाळी (ekekāḷī) — once upon a time; at one time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for at that very moment, then the Marathi for immediately.

You'll want to know: एकेकाळी.
एकेकाळी — ekekāḷī — "once upon a time; at one time".
एकेकाळी या डोंगरावर एक मोठा किल्ला होता. ekekāḷī yā ḍoṅgrāvar ek moṭhā killā hotā. Once upon a time there was a big fort on this hill.
Built from एक, one, and काळ, time, with -ई: at one time. Stories open with it.

The word, taken apart.
एकेकाळी (ekekāḷī) is built from ek, 'one', and kāḷ, 'time', which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit kāla, 'time' (CDIAL 3084), in the locative: at one time.

आजी रोज रात्री मुलांना एक कथा सांगते. ājī roj rātrī mulānnā ek kathā sāṅgte. Every night Grandma tells the children a story.
Feminine.
Plural कथा (kathā). गोष्ट is the everyday word; कथा (kathā) is the written, literary one.

The word, taken apart.
कथा (kathā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kathā, 'a talk, a tale' (Monier-Williams).

किस्सा — kissā — "an anecdote".
बाबांनी त्यांच्या लहानपणीचा एक मजेदार किस्सा सांगितला. bābānnī tyāñcyā lahānapṇīcā ek majedār kissā sāṅgitlā. Dad told a funny anecdote from his childhood.
Masculine. Plural: किस्से, kisse.
Plural किस्से.

Lesson 5 of 6.
कथन करणे (kathan karṇe) — to narrate; to recount.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tale, then the Marathi for an anecdote.

You'll want to know: कथन करणे.
कथन करणे — kathan karṇe — "to narrate; to recount".
साक्षीदाराने घडलेली घटना न्यायालयात कथन केली. sākṣīdārāne ghaḍlelī ghaṭnā nyāyālayāt kathan kelī. The witness recounted the event in court.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कथन केलं, tyāne kathan kelaṁ.
Formal; in speech सांगणे. What is told agrees: घटना कथन केली.

The word, taken apart.
कथन करणे (kathan karṇe) joins kathan, 'a telling', a tatsama taken from Sanskrit kathana, 'narration' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.
