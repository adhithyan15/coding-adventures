## You'll want to know: ಮೂರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C07-MUURU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂರು** (*mūru*) — "**three**."

Tamil *mūṉṟu*, Kannada *mūru*, Telugu *mūḍu*. The endings drift, but the long
**ū** at the front holds firm in all three. Three is the number the family never
quarrelled over, and it is a useful anchor: when you meet a Dravidian language
you have never studied, look for a word that starts *mū-*.

The long vowel is written with the sign **ೂ** hanging under ಮ.
