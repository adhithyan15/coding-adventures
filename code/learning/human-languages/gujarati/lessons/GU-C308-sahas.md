## You'll want to know: સાહસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C308-SAHAS308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સાહસ** — *sāhas* — "daring; an adventure".

> નદીમાં કૂદીને અરુણે મોટું સાહસ કર્યું.
>
> *nadīmā̃ kūdīne Aruṇe moṭũ sāhas karyũ.*
>
> By jumping into the river Arun showed great daring.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાહસ** (*sāhas*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sāhasa*, "boldness, rashness" (CDIAL 13378). Gujarati makes it good: daring, and the bold deed itself: **સાહસ કર્યું**.
