## You'll want to know: मक़सद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C345-UDDESHYA345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मक़सद** — *maqsad* — "a motive, an intent".

> उसका मक़सद मदद करना था।
>
> *uskā maqsad madad karnā thā.*
>
> His aim was to help.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मक़सद** (*maqsad*) is usually traced to Arabic *maqṣad*, "a place aimed at", which reached Hindi through Persian.
