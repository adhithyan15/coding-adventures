German, chapter 92: Clean, Dirty, Thick, Thin, Quiet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
sauber — clean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for ugly, then the German for sweet.

You'll want to know: sauber.
sauber — "clean".

The word, taken apart.
sauber from Old High German sūbar, from Latin sōbrius, "sober" — an early loan. EtymDB 2.1 traces English sober to the same Latin word.
