## You'll want to know: греметь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C466-BURYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**греметь** (*gremét*) — "to rumble, to thunder".

> Всю ночь гремел гром.
>
> *Vsyu noch gremél grom.*
>
> Thunder rumbled all night.

Imperfective; perfective *прогреметь*. Present *гремлю, гремишь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*греметь* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *grĭměti* (EtymDB), which EtymDB takes back to the PIE root *gʰrem-* 'to resound, to thunder'; *гром* 'thunder' is its noun.
