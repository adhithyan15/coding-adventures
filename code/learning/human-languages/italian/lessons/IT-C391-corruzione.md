## You'll want to know: la corruzione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C391-ACCUSA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la corruzione** — "the corruption".

> Il sindaco è accusato di corruzione.
>
> *The mayor is accused of corruption.*

*Corrotto* — corrupt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*corruzione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *corruptiō, corruptiōnis* "a spoiling, a bribing" (EtymDB), from *corrumpere* "to break up, to spoil", built on *rumpere* "to break".
