## You'll want to know: মেয়াদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C378-AYOJON378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মেয়াদ** — *meyād* — "a term; a period of validity".

> ভিসার মেয়াদ শেষ হলে তোমাকে দেশে ফিরতে হবে।
>
> *bhisār meyād shesh hôle tomāke deshe phirte hôbe.*
>
> When your visa runs out, you will have to go back home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মেয়াদ** (*meyād*) is usually traced to Arabic *mīʿād*, an appointed time, which would have come into Bengali through Persian: the time a visa, a lease or a loan runs for. When it ends, মেয়াদ শেষ হয়.
