## You'll want to know: ਫ਼ੈਸਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C345-DECIDE345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ੈਸਲਾ** — *faislā* — "a decision".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਫ਼ੈਸਲਾ ਪੱਕਾ ਹੈ?
>
> *kī tuhāḍā faislā pakkā hai?*
>
> Is your decision final?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ੈਸਲਾ** (*faislā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *faiṣala*, "decision".
