French, chapter 398: Sur internet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le réseau — the network.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to paste, then the French for to save.

You'll want to know: le réseau.
le réseau — "the network".
Il n'y a pas de réseau en montagne. There is no signal in the mountains.
Les réseaux sociaux — social media.

The word, taken apart.
réseau is usually traced to Old French reseuil, a small form of rets "net" (EtymDB has rets from Old French reiz), which goes back to Latin rēte "a net" (Lewis & Short).
