## You'll want to know: مورتی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C516-KHATTATI516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مورتی** — *mūrtī* — "a figurine; a small statue".

> کمہار نے مٹی کی ایک مورتی بنائی۔
>
> *kumhār ne miṭṭī kī ek mūrtī banāī.*
>
> The potter made a little clay figure.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مورتی** (*mūrtī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mūrti*, "a solid form; an image" (Monier-Williams).
