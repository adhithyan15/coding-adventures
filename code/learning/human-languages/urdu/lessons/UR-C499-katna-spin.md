## You'll want to know: کاتنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C499-CHARKHA499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کاتنا** — *kātnā* — "to spin (thread)".

> وہ چرخے پر دھاگا کاتتی ہے۔
>
> *voh charkhe par dhāgā kāttī hai.*
>
> She spins thread on the spinning wheel.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دھاگا کاتا, *us ne dhāgā kātā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کاتنا** (*kātnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kātnā*, "spins", under *\*kartati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2855).
