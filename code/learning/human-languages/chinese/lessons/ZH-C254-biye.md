## You'll want to know: bìyè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C254-BORN254-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bìyè* — "to graduate; to finish school".

> *tā míngnián dàxué bìyè* — She finishes university next year.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bìyè* is built from *bì* 'to finish' and *yè* 'a task', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjit* and *ngjaep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to end' and the second 'an affair'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
