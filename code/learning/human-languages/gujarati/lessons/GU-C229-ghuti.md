# ઘૂંટી (ghū̃ṭī) — ankle

## You'll want to know: ઘૂંટી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C229-LIMBS229-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘૂંટી** — *ghū̃ṭī* — "ankle".

> **શું તમારી ઘૂંટી સૂજે છે?** — *śũ tamārī ghū̃ṭī sūje che?* — "Is your ankle swelling?"

Not **ઘૂંટણ**, the knee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘૂંટી** (*ghū̃ṭī*) is inherited. CDIAL lists it, glossed "ankle joint", under the lexical Sanskrit *ghuṭa*, "ankle", which it takes from Dravidian, after T. Burrow (CDIAL 4479).
