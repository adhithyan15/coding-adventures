## You'll want to know: der Luftballon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C309-SCHMUECKEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Luftballon** — "the balloon".

> Jedes Kind bekam einen roten Luftballon.
>
> *Every child got a red balloon.*

Plural: *die Luftballons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Luftballon* joins *Luft* "air", Old High German *luft* (EtymDB), and *Ballon*, borrowed from French *ballon* "a large ball".
