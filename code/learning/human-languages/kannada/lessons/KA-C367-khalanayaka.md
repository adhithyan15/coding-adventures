# ಖಳನಾಯಕ (khaḷanāyaka) — a villain

## You'll want to know: ಖಳನಾಯಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C367-KHALA367-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖಳನಾಯಕ** — *khaḷanāyaka* — "a villain".

> ಸಿನಿಮಾದ ಕೊನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಖಳನಾಯಕ ಸೋತನು.
>
> *sinimāda koneyalli khaḷanāyaka sōtanu.*
>
> At the end of the film the villain lost.

Plural: ಖಳನಾಯಕರು, *khaḷanāyakaru*.

ಖಳ is wicked: the wicked lead of a story or a film.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖಳನಾಯಕ** (*khaḷanāyaka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *khala*, "a mischievous person" (CDIAL 3835), and *nāyaka*, "a guide, a leader" (Monier-Williams).
