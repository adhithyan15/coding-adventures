## You'll want to know: eincremen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C481-ALLERGIE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**eincremen** — "to put cream on".

> Bevor wir an den Strand gehen, cremen wir die Kinder gut ein.
>
> *Before the beach we put plenty of cream on the children.*

Separable: *ich creme ein*. Also reflexive: *sich eincremen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eincremen* is built on *ein* "in" and *Creme*, which was borrowed from French *crème* "cream" (EtymDB); EtymDB has no entry for the verb.
