Gujarati, chapter 80: Hours, Minutes and Holidays.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કલાક (kalāk) — an hour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for yesterday, then the Gujarati for time.
