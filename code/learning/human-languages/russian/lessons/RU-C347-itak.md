## You'll want to know: итак
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C347-ODNAKO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**итак** (*iták*) — "so, and so (summing up)".

> Итак, все готовы? Тогда начинаем.
>
> *Iták, vse gotóvy? Togdá nachináyem.*
>
> So, is everyone ready? Then we begin.

It opens a summary or a conclusion. Stress on the second syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**итак** is fused from *и* "and" and *так* "so", both inherited Slavic words.
