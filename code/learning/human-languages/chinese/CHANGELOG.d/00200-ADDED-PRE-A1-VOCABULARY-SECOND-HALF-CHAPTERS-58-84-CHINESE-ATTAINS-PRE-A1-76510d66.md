## Added — the pre-A1 vocabulary, second half: chapters 58-84; Chinese attains pre-A1

- 27 chapters of five words each (135 words, 34 of them verbs), with reviews
  closing chapters 63, 69, 74, 79 and 84 and a final review: things you carry,
  clothes and colours, size and speed, the phone, asking for help and saying sorry,
  the weather, animals, out of town, meals and tastes, getting about and the
  station, visiting, celebrating, parting, thanks and replies, age, school, jobs,
  the city, directions, running late and doing things together.
- 24 writing lessons, 机 白 长 快 电 打 起 门 问 气 雨 马 山 做 火 开 下 进 客 乐 会 前 课 里,
  each directly after the word whose headword first shows the character, with its
  stroke order from Hanzi Writer Data and a stroke-by-stroke strip.
- 42 words are headed in characters the reader has written; 93 are taught by ear
  with pinyin headwords.
- The review replaced three phrases that were only two taught words side by side
  (请进, 生日快乐, 快点) with 门口, 蛋糕 and 时间, reworded every example that needed a
  measure word the track has not taught (部 for films, 顶 for hats), and hedged
  every sense the Guangyun does not give.
- Spine: 对不起, 欢迎, 明天见, 晚安 and 拜拜 realize COURTESY-SORRY, GREETING-WELCOME,
  FAREWELL-TOMORROW, GREETING-GOODNIGHT and FAREWELL-CASUAL, which leave the
  nodes' omits.
- With chapters 30-84 Chinese attains pre-A1: 324 headwords and 71 verbs, all seven
  pre-A1 spine nodes realized.
