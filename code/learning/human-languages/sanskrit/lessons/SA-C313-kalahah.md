## You'll want to know: कलहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C313-UPADRAVA313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कलहः** — *kalahaḥ* — "a quarrel".

> विपणौ कलहः भवति।
>
> *vipaṇau kalahaḥ bhavati.*
>
> There is a quarrel in the market.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कलहः** (*kalahaḥ*) MW gives *kalaha* as strife, contention, a quarrel or fight, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
