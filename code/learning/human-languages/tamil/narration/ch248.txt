Tamil, chapter 248: Drought, Earthquake, Nature, Rock, Fire.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
வறட்சி (vaṟaṭci) — a drought.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a squirrel, then the Tamil for a worm.

You'll want to know: வறட்சி.
வறட்சி — vaṟaṭci — "a drought".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does வறட்சி (vaṟaṭci) mean? ("A drought".) Say it once more.

A voice? (குரல், kural.) A conversation? (உரையாடல், uraiyāṭal.) A man? (ஆண், āṇ.) A woman? (பெண், peṇ.)
