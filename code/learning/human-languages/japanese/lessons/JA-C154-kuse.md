## You'll want to know: くせ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C154-HABIT154-02]; assesses=[] -->

**くせ** — *kuse* — "a habit; a quirk".

> **それは ちちの くせです。** — *sore wa chichi no kuse desu* — "That is my father's habit."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くせ** (*kuse*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
