## You'll want to know: последствие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C350-POSLEDSTVIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**последствие** (*poslédstviye*) — "a consequence".

> Это решение будет иметь серьёзные последствия.
>
> *Éto reshéniye búdet imét seryóznyye poslédstviya.*
>
> This decision will have serious consequences.

Neuter; mostly plural, *последствия*, and usually unpleasant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**последствие** is built from *после* "after" and *след* "a track, a trail, a mark" (EtymDB), with the suffix *-ствие*: what comes after, in the wake of an event.
