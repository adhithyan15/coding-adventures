Punjabi, chapter 274: Yards and Metres.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮਿਣਨਾ (miṇnā) — to measure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for weight, then the Punjabi for to carry a load.

You'll want to know: ਮਿਣਨਾ.
ਮਿਣਨਾ — miṇnā — "to measure".
ਦਰਜ਼ੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਮੀਜ਼ ਮਿਣਦਾ ਹੈ। darzī merī kamīz miṇdā hai. The tailor measures my shirt.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਮਰਾ ਮਿਣਿਆ, maiṁ kamrā miṇiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਿਣਨਾ (miṇnā) is inherited from a reconstructed mināti, "measures"; CDIAL lists Punjabi miṇnā, "to measure", under it (CDIAL 10132).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਗਜ਼ (gaz) — a yard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to carry a load, then the Punjabi for to measure.

You'll want to know: ਗਜ਼.
ਗਜ਼ — gaz — "a yard".
ਮੇਰਾ ਕਮਰਾ ਪੰਜ ਗਜ਼ ਲੰਮਾ ਹੈ। merā kamrā panj gaz lammā hai. My room is five yards long.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗਜ਼ (gaz) is a loan, usually traced to Persian gaz, "a yard, an ell".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਨਾਪ (nāp) — a size; a measurement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a yard, then the Punjabi for a metre.

You'll want to know: ਨਾਪ.
ਨਾਪ — nāp — "a size; a measurement".
ਦਰਜ਼ੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਪ ਲੈਂਦਾ ਹੈ। darzī merā nāp laindā hai. The tailor takes my measurements.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਾਪ (nāp) goes with nāpṇā, "to measure"; CDIAL lists Punjabi nāpṇā under Sanskrit jñāpyate, "is made known" (CDIAL 5283).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਜੋੜਾ (joṛā) — a pair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a metre, then the Punjabi for a size.

You'll want to know: ਜੋੜਾ.
ਜੋੜਾ — joṛā — "a pair".
ਮੈਂ ਜੁੱਤੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਇੱਕ ਨਵਾਂ ਜੋੜਾ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਾਂਗਾ। maiṁ juttīāṁ dā ikk navāṁ joṛā kharīdāṅgā. I will buy a new pair of shoes.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਜੋੜਾ (joṛā) is inherited from a reconstructed yuṭati, "is joined"; CDIAL lists Punjabi joṛā, "couple", under it (CDIAL 10496).
