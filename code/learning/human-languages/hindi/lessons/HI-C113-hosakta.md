## You'll want to know: हो सकता है
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C113-NOTHING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हो सकता है** (*ho saktā hai*) — "maybe", literally "it can be".

It is **होना**, "to be", and the **सकना** of "can". Compare **शायद**, "perhaps".
