## You'll want to know: मालिश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C492-SUDHAR492-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मालिश** — *māliś* — "a massage; a rub".

> रोज़ की मालिश से दादी के घुटनों का दर्द कम हो गया।
>
> *roz kī māliś se dādī ke ghuṭnoṁ kā dard kam ho gayā.*
>
> With a daily massage, the pain in my grandmother's knees eased.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मालिश** (*māliś*) is usually traced to Persian *mālish*, "rubbing", the noun of the Persian verb for "to rub". *māliś karnā* is to massage; *malnā*, "to rub in", is a separate, inherited word.
