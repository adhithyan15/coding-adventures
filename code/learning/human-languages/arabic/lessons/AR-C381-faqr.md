## You'll want to know: فقر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C381-MUJTAMA381-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فقر** — *faqr* — "poverty".

> يعيش كثير من الناس في فقر.
>
> *yaʿīshu kathīr min an-nās fī faqr.*
>
> Many people live in poverty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فقر** (*faqr*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-q-r*, "to be needy, poor" (Lane). *Faqr* is the verbal noun: poverty. فقير, poor, is from the same root.
