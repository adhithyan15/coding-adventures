# ஆனந்தம் (āṉantam) — bliss; great joy

## You'll want to know: ஆனந்தம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C459-MAKIZHCCI459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆனந்தம்** — *āṉantam* — "bliss; great joy".

> பேரனைப் பார்த்ததும் தாத்தாவுக்கு ஆனந்தம்.
>
> *pēraṉaip pārttatum tāttāvukku āṉantam.*
>
> Grandfather was overjoyed to see his grandson.

ஆனந்தக் கண்ணீர், tears of joy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆனந்தம்** (*āṉantam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ānanda*, "happiness" (CDIAL 1172), with the Tamil ending -அம்.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
