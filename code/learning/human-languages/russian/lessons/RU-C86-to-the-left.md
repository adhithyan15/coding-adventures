## You'll want to know: налево

**налево** (*nalévo*) — "to the left".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**налево** is *на*, "onto", with *лево*, from *левый*, "left", a cousin of Latin *laevus*, "left" (Vasmer).
