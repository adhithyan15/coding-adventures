## You'll want to know: قبطان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C429-QARIB429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قبطان** — *qubṭān* — "a captain (of a ship)".

> تكلم القبطان مع الركاب.
>
> *takallama al-qubṭān maʿa ar-rukkāb.*
>
> The captain spoke with the passengers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قبطان** (*qubṭān*) was borrowed from Latin *capitaneus*, "a chief" (EtymDB).
