## You'll want to know: rischioso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C376-RISCHIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**rischioso** — "risky".

> Guidare con la nebbia è rischioso.
>
> *Driving in fog is risky.*

Built on *rischio*. *Rischiare* is the verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rischioso* is built on *rischio* "risk", usually traced to Medieval Latin *risicum*, a word whose deeper origin is not settled; EtymDB lists *rischio* without a source (EtymDB).
