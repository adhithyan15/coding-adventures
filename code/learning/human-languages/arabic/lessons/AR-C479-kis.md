## You'll want to know: كيس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C479-TAZALLUJ479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**كيس** — *kīs* — "a plastic bag; a sack".

> وضعنا الطعام في كيس كبير.
>
> *waḍaʿnā aṭ-ṭaʿām fī kīs kabīr.*
>
> We put the food in a big bag.

Plural: أكياس, *akyās*.

*ḥaqība* is a bag one carries; *kīs* is a sack or a plastic bag.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كيس** (*kīs*) is given by Lane as a purse for money; the origin of the word is not settled.
