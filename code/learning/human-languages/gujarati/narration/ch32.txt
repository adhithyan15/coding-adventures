Gujarati, chapter 32: Inside the House.
8 lessons. You can do the first 6 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
બારણું (bārṇũ) — hear door.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the Gujarati word for home, from the first three-place map. Now listen twice: bārṇũ. It means door — the one you open to come in, and listen to that ending: the nasalized -ũ that marks a neuter word.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "bārṇũ"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "bārṇũ" twice, once slowly and once at speed]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does bārṇũ mean? (Door.)
Source: Cambridge: door.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]

Lesson 6 of 8.

Lesson 7 of 8.
Bārī reaches the page — window, now written with signs taught long before this chapter.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
Before anything is uncovered, say the Gujarati word for window.

Guided Practice.
Uncover બારી (bārī) once. Four pieces, none new: બ from the forms that come before your name, the ા stroke from the greeting, ર from the courtesy chapter, and the ી sign from the doorway nine. Name the pieces aloud in order, then read the whole word once. This is the only word of the chapter the hand is asked for; the other four stay in the ear for now, which is the gloss-first order rather than an omission.
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Read this word aloud and give its meaning: બારી (bārī).

Writing — guided copy, model in view.
Keep the model in view and copy બારી (bārī) once. Repair one shape if it needs it; do not start a row.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
With the model in view, copy window once.

Guided Practice — the eight time words at their third window.
The lamp above is lit at sānj, so the time words are already in the room. All eight were first written twenty-five and more positions ago; this is their third spacing window, and this row is cold reading, not recall from a list.
[question — say your answer, then pause 40 seconds]
