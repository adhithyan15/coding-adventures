## You'll want to know: rimediare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C438-RECLAMO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rimediare** — "to put right, to make up for".

> L'albergo ha rimediato con una camera più grande.
>
> *The hotel made up for it with a bigger room.*

*Il rimedio* — the remedy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rimediare* is built on *rimedio* 'remedy', a learned borrowing from Latin *remedium* 'cure', from *medērī* 'to heal' (Lewis & Short).
