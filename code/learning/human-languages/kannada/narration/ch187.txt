Kannada, chapter 187: Number, Rainbow, Snow, Shade, Darkness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ (saṅkhye) — a number.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a file, then the Kannada for a document.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ.
ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ — saṅkhye — "a number".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ (saṅkhye) is Sanskrit saṅkhyā, "number, counting", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with Kannada's -e for the Sanskrit -ā (CDIAL 12841).

ಕಾಮನಬಿಲ್ಲು (kāmanabillu) is "Kāma's bow": kāmana, "of Kāma", the god of love, from Sanskrit kāma, "love" (CDIAL 3042), and native billu, "bow" (DEDR 5422, with Tamil vil).

ನೆರಳು — neraḷu — "shade, a shadow".

The word, taken apart.
ನೆರಳು (neraḷu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil niḻal, Telugu nīḍa and Malayalam niḻal (DEDR 3679). DEDR lists Kannada neraḷ, "shade, shadow".
