# प्रांशुः (prāṃśuḥ) — tall of stature

## You'll want to know: प्रांशुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C476-SAKTI476-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रांशुः** — *prāṃśuḥ* — "tall of stature".

> मम भ्राता प्रांशुः अस्ति।
>
> *mama bhrātā prāṃśuḥ asti.*
>
> My brother is tall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रांशुः** (*prāṃśuḥ*) MW reports that it is said to be built from *pra* and *aṃśu*, "a ray", so its origin is not settled; it gives "high, tall, long" (MW).
