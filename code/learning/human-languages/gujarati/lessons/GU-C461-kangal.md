# કંગાળ (kangāḷ) — penniless; destitute

## You'll want to know: કંગાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C461-KRUPAN461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કંગાળ** — *kangāḷ* — "penniless; destitute".

> ધંધામાં ખોટ ગઈ અને તે કંગાળ થઈ ગયો.
>
> *dhandhāmā̃ khoṭ gaī ane te kangāḷ thaī gayo.*
>
> His business made a loss and he became penniless.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કંગાળ** (*kangāḷ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*kaṅkāla-*, "poor, miserable"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "poor" (CDIAL 2604).
