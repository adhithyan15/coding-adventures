## You'll want to know: ब्रेक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C407-VEG407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्रेक** — *brek* — "a brake".

> चालकाने अचानक ब्रेक दाबला.
>
> *cālakāne acānak brek dāblā.*
>
> The driver suddenly pressed the brake.

Masculine.

ब्रेक दाबणे or ब्रेक लावणे, to brake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्रेक** (*brek*) is a loan from English *brake*.
