## You'll want to know: સ્કૂટર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C400-SCOOTER400-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્કૂટર** — *skūṭar* — "a scooter".

> મારા ભાઈ પાસે લાલ સ્કૂટર છે.
>
> *mārā bhāī pāse lāl skūṭar che.*
>
> My brother has a red scooter.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્કૂટર** (*skūṭar*) is borrowed from English *scooter*.
