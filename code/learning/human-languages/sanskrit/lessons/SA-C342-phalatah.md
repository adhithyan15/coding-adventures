## You'll want to know: फलतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C342-ADHARA342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**फलतः** — *phalataḥ* — "as a result".

> सः बहु पठितवान्, फलतः परीक्षायां प्रथमः अभवत्।
>
> *saḥ bahu paṭhitavān, phalataḥ parīkṣāyāṃ prathamaḥ abhavat.*
>
> He studied hard; as a result he came first in the exam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फलतः** (*phalataḥ*) is built on *phala*, "fruit, result", with the ending *-tas*, "from"; MW gives *phala-tas* "in relation to the reward or result" and "consequently, accordingly" (MW).
