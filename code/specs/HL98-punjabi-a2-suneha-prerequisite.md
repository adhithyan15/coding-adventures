# HL98 — Punjabi A2 `ਸੁਨੇਹਾ` prerequisite

Issue: #18127

After chapter 197 merged, an exact aggregate-only audit of the preserved
confidential Punjabi A2 mock drafts against merged main
`94caabddc2cdf71d0ba0800bbe124201af8d7c16` finds **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs once in the confidential learner material and
twice in the confidential examiner material. Neither confidential body belongs
in the public corpus.

Chapter 198 closes that single-word gap through one deliberately narrow
identity frame: **ਇਹ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ ਹੈ** — “this is a message.” The voice-first opener
retrieves only familiar **ਇਹ** and **ਹੈ**. One lesson directly owns exact
**ਸੁਨੇਹਾ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound, meaning, and script, a
bounded frame lesson places it between the two familiar words, and a four-skill
payoff retrieves the complete label again. The target has one direct owner and
three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored, generated-book, or
narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-LEX-SUNEHA-198`. The lesson keeps the Punjabi form whole and
owns only the neutral noun sense “message” or “information communicated from
one party to another.” It does not derive the word from smaller Punjabi parts,
teach gender, plural or oblique forms, teach a send-message construction,
specialise it to a text or digital message, introduce synonyms, or claim that
the existing named-reader writing runway already owned this exact Punjabi noun.

Punjabi University's Punjabipedia records **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ** as information given from
one party to another and identifies it as a noun:
<https://punjabipedia.org/topic.aspx?txt=%E0%A8%B8%E0%A9%81%E0%A8%A8%E0%A9%87%E0%A8%B9%E0%A8%BE>.
That source supports the bounded present-day sense taught here. A Press
Information Bureau Punjabi release also uses **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ** in current public prose:
<https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2221364&lang=2&reg=48>.

The exact history of modern written **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ** remains unresolved in the
sources used here. As a narrow historical comparison, Turner's CDIAL entry
12904 groups Punjabi *sanehā̃* with Sanskrit *saṁdēśa* “message,
information”:
<https://dsal.uchicago.edu/cgi-bin/app/soas_query.py?qs=sa%E1%B9%81d%C4%93%C5%9Ba&searchhws=yes&matchtype=exact>.
The lesson may report that comparison, but it must not claim an exact
letter-by-letter path into **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ**, turn the comparison into a productive
Punjabi derivation, or connect the word to Punjabi **ਸਨੇਹ** “affection.”

This is the twenty-first prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
