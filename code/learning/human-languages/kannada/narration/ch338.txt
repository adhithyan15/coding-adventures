Kannada, chapter 338: Rumours in the Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವದಂತಿ (vadanti) — a rumour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tribute, then the Kannada for to comfort.

You'll want to know: ವದಂತಿ.
ವದಂತಿ — vadanti — "a rumour".
ಶಾಲೆ ಮುಚ್ಚುತ್ತಾರೆ ಎಂಬ ವದಂತಿ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು. śāle muccuttāre emba vadanti ūrinalli ittu. There was a rumour in town that the school would close.
Plural: ವದಂತಿಗಳು, vadantigaḷu.
A rumour "spreads": ವದಂತಿ (vadanti) ಹಬ್ಬಿತು. ಎಂಬ, "called, that says", links it to its content.

The word, taken apart.
ವದಂತಿ (vadanti) is borrowed from Sanskrit vadanti, a noun that Monier-Williams gives beside kiṃvadantī, "the common saying, a rumour", literally "what do they say", from vad, "to speak".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪಸರಿಸು (pasarisu) — to spread far.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to comfort, then the Kannada for a rumour.

You'll want to know: ಪಸರಿಸು.
ಪಸರಿಸು — pasarisu — "to spread far".
ಆ ಸುದ್ದಿ ನಗರದ ತುಂಬಾ ಪಸರಿಸಿತು. ā suddi nagarada tumbā pasarisitu. That news spread all over the city.
Past (it …): ಪಸರಿಸಿತು, pasarisitu.
News, fame and fragrance spread far: ಸುಗಂಧ ಪಸರಿಸಿತು, a fragrance spread.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಸರಿಸು (pasarisu) is built on Sanskrit prasara, "advance, extension", in its Prakrit shape pasara (CDIAL 8824), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಊಹಾಪೋಹ (ūhāpōha) — speculation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a rumour, then the Kannada for to spread far.

You'll want to know: ಊಹಾಪೋಹ.
ಊಹಾಪೋಹ — ūhāpōha — "speculation".
ಆ ಘಟನೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಊಹಾಪೋಹ ಇತ್ತು. ā ghaṭaneya bagge tumbā ūhāpōha ittu. There was a lot of speculation about that incident.
ಊಹೆ, a guess, and ಅಪೋಹ, a denial: guesses for and against.

The word, taken apart.
ಊಹಾಪೋಹ (ūhāpōha) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of ūha, "consideration" (CDIAL 2443), and apoha, "reasoning, denying" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸತ್ಯ (satya) — the truth; true.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to spread far, then the Kannada for speculation.

You'll want to know: ಸತ್ಯ.
ಸತ್ಯ — satya — "the truth; true".
ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಸತ್ಯ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಗೊತ್ತಾಯಿತು. konege satya ellarigū gottāyitu. In the end everyone came to know the truth.
ನಿಜ is the everyday word for "true"; ಸತ್ಯ (satya) is weightier: ಸತ್ಯ (satya) ಹೇಳು, to tell the truth.

The word, taken apart.
ಸತ್ಯ (satya) is borrowed from Sanskrit satya, "true", and as a noun "truth" (CDIAL 13112).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಆಲಿಸು (ālisu) — to listen closely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for speculation, then the Kannada for the truth.

You'll want to know: ಆಲಿಸು.
ಆಲಿಸು — ālisu — "to listen closely".
ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಕಥೆಯನ್ನು ಗಮನವಿಟ್ಟು ಆಲಿಸಿದರು. ellarū ajjana katheyannu gamanaviṭṭu ālisidaru. Everyone listened closely to Grandfather's story.
Past (I …): ಆಲಿಸಿದೆ, āliside.
A more careful listening than ಕೇಳು; what one listens to takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಲಿಸು (ālisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 383 lists Kannada ālisu, ālaysu, "to listen, to attend to".
