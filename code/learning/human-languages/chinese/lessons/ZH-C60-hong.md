## You'll want to know: hóng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C60-COLOR60-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hóng* — "red".

> *zhège píngguǒ hěn hóng* — This apple is really red.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hóng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *huwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a colour'; which colour is not said there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
