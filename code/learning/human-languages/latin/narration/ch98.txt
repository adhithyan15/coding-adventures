Latin, chapter 98: Rich, Poor, Foolish, Enough, Too Much.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dīves, dīvitis — rich.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for brave, then the Latin for wise.

You'll want to know: dīves, dīvitis.
dīves, dīvitis — "rich".

What you've built.
One more word for rich, poor, foolish, enough, too much.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dīves]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dīves, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dīves]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Latin for brave, then the Latin for wise, then say dīves again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does dīves, dīvitis mean? ("Rich".) Say it once more.
