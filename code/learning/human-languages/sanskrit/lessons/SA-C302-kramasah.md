## You'll want to know: क्रमशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C302-JALAUGHA302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रमशः** — *kramaśaḥ* — "by degrees; step by step".

> जलं क्रमशः वर्धते।
>
> *jalaṃ kramaśaḥ vardhate.*
>
> The water rises by degrees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रमशः** (*kramaśaḥ*) is built on *krama*, "a step, an order", with the ending *-śas*; MW gives *kramaśas* as gradually, by degrees (MW).
