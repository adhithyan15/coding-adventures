5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
коллекционировать (kollektsionírovat) — to collect (as a hobby).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to make things by hand, then the Russian for a handmade craft item.

You'll want to know: коллекционировать.
коллекционировать (kollektsionírovat) — "to collect (as a hobby)".
Мой сосед коллекционирует старые монеты. Moy soséd kollektsioníruyet stáryye monéty. My neighbour collects old coins.
Imperfective; present коллекционирую.

The word, taken apart.
коллекционировать (kollektsionírovat) is built on коллекция 'a collection', a learned borrowing usually traced to Latin collēctiō 'a collecting together' (Lewis & Short), from colligere; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

You'll want to know: значок.
значок (znachók) — "a badge".
У него на рюкзаке много значков. U negó na ryukzaké mnógo znachkóv. He has a lot of badges on his backpack.
Masculine; genitive значка. A diminutive of знак — a sign.

The word, taken apart.
значок (znachók) is a diminutive of знак 'a sign', built on the root of знать 'to know'; EtymDB has no entry for значок (znachók).

Lesson 5 of 5.
разгадывать (razgádyvat) — to solve (a riddle, a puzzle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a badge, then the Russian for a crossword.

You'll want to know: разгадывать.
разгадывать (razgádyvat) — "to solve (a riddle, a puzzle)".
Мы вместе разгадываем загадки. My vméste razgádyvayem zagádki. We solve riddles together.
Imperfective; perfective разгадать.

The word, taken apart.
разгадывать (razgádyvat) is built on раз- and гадать 'to guess'; EtymDB has no entry for the verb, and гадать is inherited from Proto-Slavic gadati (EtymDB).
