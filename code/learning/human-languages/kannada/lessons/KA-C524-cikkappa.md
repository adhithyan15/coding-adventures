# ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ (cikkappa) — father's younger brother

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C523-SAMSARA523-04, KA-LEX-C523-SAMSARA523-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for the pain of parting, then the Kannada for married life.

## You'll want to know: ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ** — *cikkappa* — "father's younger brother".

> ನನ್ನ ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ರೈತರು.
>
> *nanna cikkappa haḷḷiyalli raitaru.*
>
> My father's younger brother is a farmer in the village.

ಚಿಕ್ಕ, small + ಅಪ್ಪ, father: a "small father".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ** (*cikkappa*) is a native Dravidian compound: *cikka*, "small, young" (DEDR 2495), and *appa*, "father" (DEDR 156).
