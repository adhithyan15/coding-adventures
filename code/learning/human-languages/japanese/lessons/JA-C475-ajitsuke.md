## You'll want to know: あじつけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C475-TABLE475-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あじつけ** — *ajitsuke* — "flavouring (a dish)".

> **ははの あじつけは うすいです。** — *haha no ajitsuke wa usui desu* — "My mother flavours food lightly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あじつけ** (*ajitsuke*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *aji*, "taste" and *tsuke*, "applying (tsukeru)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
