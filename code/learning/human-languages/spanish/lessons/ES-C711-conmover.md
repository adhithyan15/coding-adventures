## You'll want to know: conmover
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C711-EMOCION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**conmover** — "to move, to touch (emotionally)".

> Su historia me conmovió.
>
> *Her story moved me.*

Stem change: *conmueve*. *Conmovido* — moved, touched.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conmover* is inherited from Latin *commovēre* "to stir deeply" (EtymDB), built on *movēre* "to move".
