## You'll want to know: la refurtiva
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C291-SCIPPO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la refurtiva** — "the stolen goods".

> La polizia ha trovato la refurtiva in un garage.
>
> *The police found the stolen goods in a garage.*

A police and news word, singular only.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*refurtiva* is usually traced to Latin *rēs fūrtīva* "a stolen thing", built on *fūrtīvus* "stolen", from *fūrtum* "theft" and *fūr* "thief" (Lewis & Short).
