## You'll want to know: しりあい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C244-MEET244-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しりあい** — *shiriai* — "an acquaintance".

> **かれは ちちの しりあいですか。** — *kare wa chichi no shiriai desu ka* — "Is he an acquaintance of my father's?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しりあい** (*shiriai*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *shiri*, from *shiru*, "to know", and *ai*, "mutual", from *au* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
