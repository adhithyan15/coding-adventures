## You'll want to know: secourir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C300-SECOURS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**secourir** — "to rescue, to help".

> Des voisins ont secouru la vieille dame.
>
> *Neighbours helped the old lady.*

Conjugated like *courir*: *il a secouru*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*secourir* is inherited from Latin *succurrere* "to run to help", from *sub* and *currere* "to run" (Lewis & Short).
