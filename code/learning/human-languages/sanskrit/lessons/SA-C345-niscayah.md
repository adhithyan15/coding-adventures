## You'll want to know: निश्चयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C345-PRATYAYA345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चयः** — *niścayaḥ* — "certainty".

> मम निश्चयः अस्ति यत् सः श्वः गमिष्यति।
>
> *mama niścayaḥ asti yat saḥ śvaḥ gamiṣyati.*
>
> I am certain that he will go tomorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चयः** (*niścayaḥ*) is built on *niś*, "out", and the root √ci, "to gather"; MW gives *niś-caya* "ascertainment, conviction, certainty" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
