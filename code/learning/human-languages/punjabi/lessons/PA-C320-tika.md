## You'll want to know: ਟੀਕਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C320-CLINIC320-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੀਕਾ** — *ṭīkā* — "an injection".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਹਸਪਤਾਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਂ ਟੀਕਾ ਲਿਆ।
>
> *kall haspatāl vich maiṁ ṭīkā liā.*
>
> Yesterday I had an injection at the hospital.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੀਕਾ** (*ṭīkā*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ṭikka*, "a mark, a spot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭikkā*, "mark", and Hindi *ṭīkā*, "caste-mark", under it (CDIAL 5458). How it came to mean an injection is not given there.
