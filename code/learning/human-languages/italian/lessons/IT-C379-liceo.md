## You'll want to know: il liceo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C379-LICEO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il liceo** — "the upper secondary school (academic)".

> Mia figlia va al liceo scientifico.
>
> *My daughter goes to a science high school.*

From about fourteen to nineteen. *Il liceo classico* teaches Latin and Greek.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liceo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *lycēum*, from Greek *lýkeion*, the name of a gymnasium with gardens where a great teacher of ancient Greece taught (Lewis & Short).
