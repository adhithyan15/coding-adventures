Gujarati, chapter 234: Milking time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વાછરડું (vācharḍũ) — calf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for ox, then the Gujarati for to plough.

You'll want to know: વાછરડું.
વાછરડું — vācharḍũ — "calf".
વાછરડું ગાય પાસે ગયું. — vācharḍũ gāy pāse gayũ — "The calf went to the cow."

The word, taken apart.
વાછરડું (vācharḍũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit vatsatara, "young bull or goat before weaning", built on vatsa, "calf" (CDIAL 11241).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ભાંભરવું (bhā̃bharvũ) — to moo, to low.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to plough, then the Gujarati for calf.

You'll want to know: ભાંભરવું.
ભાંભરવું — bhā̃bharvũ — "to moo, to low".
સવારે વાછરડું ભાંભર્યું. — savāre vācharḍũ bhā̃bharyũ — "The calf mooed in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ભાંભરવું (bhā̃bharvũ) is not settled. It is probably imitative of the sound a cow makes, and CDIAL lists no Gujarati form like it.

સવારે મેં ગાય દોહી. — savāre mẽ gāy dohī — "I milked the cow in the morning."

ચરવું (carvũ) — to graze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to milk, then the Gujarati for sheep.

You'll want to know: ચરવું.
ચરવું — carvũ — "to graze".
ઘેટાં મેદાનમાં ચર્યાં. — gheṭā̃ medānmā̃ caryā̃ — "The sheep grazed in the meadow."

The word, taken apart.
ચરવું (carvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit carati, "moves; grazes", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to move about, graze" (CDIAL 4686).
