## You'll want to know: ಜೌಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C479-SASYA479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೌಗು** — *jaugu* — "a swamp; marshy ground".

> ನದಿಯ ಪಕ್ಕದ ಜೌಗಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕಪ್ಪೆಗಳು ಕೂಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು.
>
> *nadiya pakkada jauginalli kappegaḷu kūguttiddavu.*
>
> Frogs were calling in the marsh beside the river.

ಜೌಗು ನೆಲ, marshy ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೌಗು** (*jaugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2398 lists Kannada *javugu*, *jōgu*, "swampy ground".
