## You'll want to know: अंगीकरोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C534-SAMVID534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगीकरोति** — *aṅgīkaroti* — "agrees to".

> यदि सः मम योजनाम् अंगीकरोति, तर्हि वयं श्वः आरभामहे।
>
> *yadi saḥ mama yojanām aṅgīkaroti, tarhi vayaṃ śvaḥ ārabhāmahe.*
>
> If he agrees to my plan, we will start tomorrow.

Past (he …): सः योजनाम् अंगीकृतवान्, *saḥ yojanām aṅgīkṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगीकरोति** (*aṅgīkaroti*) is built from *aṅga*, "a limb, a part", in the form *aṅgī*, and the root √kṛ, "to make": to make a thing part of oneself; MW gives *aṅgīkṛ* as to take the side of, to agree to (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
