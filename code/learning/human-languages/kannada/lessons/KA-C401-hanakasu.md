## You'll want to know: ಹಣಕಾಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C401-ABHIVRIDDHI401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಣಕಾಸು** — *haṇakāsu* — "finance".

> ಮನೆಯ ಹಣಕಾಸು ಅಮ್ಮನೇ ನೋಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾಳೆ.
>
> *maneya haṇakāsu ammanē nōḍikoḷḷuttāḷe.*
>
> Mother herself looks after the household finances.

ಹಣ (money) + ಕಾಸು (coin).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಣಕಾಸು** (*haṇakāsu*) is built from *haṇa*, "money", paired with native *kāsu*, "a small coin, money" (DEDR 1431).
