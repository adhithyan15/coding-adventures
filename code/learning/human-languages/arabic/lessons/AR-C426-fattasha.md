## You'll want to know: فتش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C426-JAMARIK426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فتش** — *fattasha* — "to search; to inspect".

> فتش الموظف حقيبتي في الجمارك.
>
> *fattasha al-muwaẓẓaf ḥaqībatī fī al-jamārik.*
>
> The officer searched my bag at customs.

Present: يفتش, *yufattish*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فتش** (*fattasha*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-t-sh*, "to search" (Lane). It is Form II, "to search thoroughly".
