Spanish, chapter 576: Lo que pienso.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
considerar — to consider; to regard as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to seem, then the Spanish for to keep.

You'll want to know: considerar.
considerar — "to consider; to regard as".
Considero que es una buena idea. I consider it a good idea.
Considerar que + indicative gives your opinion; considerar algo importante is to regard it as important.

The word, taken apart.
considerar is a learned borrowing from Latin considerare 'to examine closely, to scrutinize', built from con- and, by the usual account, sidus 'star', as if first to watch the stars (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
sostener — to maintain, to claim; to hold up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to keep, then the Spanish for to consider.

You'll want to know: sostener.
sostener — "to maintain, to claim; to hold up".
Sostengo que el plan es bueno. I maintain that the plan is good.
Like tener: sostengo, sostuve. In a debate sostener que is to argue that.

The word, taken apart.
sostener is inherited from Latin sustinere 'to hold up, to support', built from sub(s) and tenere 'to hold' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: la creencia.
la creencia — "the belief".

Las creencias — beliefs, often religious ones.

The word, taken apart.
creencia is built on creer with the ending -encia; creer is inherited from Latin credere 'to trust, to believe' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
la postura — the stance, the position; posture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the belief, then the Spanish for the conviction.

You'll want to know: la postura.
la postura — "the stance, the position; posture".
¿Cuál es tu postura sobre el tema? What is your position on the issue?
In a debate, la postura is the side you take; for the body it means posture.

The word, taken apart.
postura comes from Latin positura 'position, arrangement', built on ponere 'to put, to place' (EtymDB).
