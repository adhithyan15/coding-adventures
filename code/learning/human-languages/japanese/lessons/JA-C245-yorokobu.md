# よろこぶ (yorokobu) — to be delighted; to rejoice

## You'll want to know: よろこぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C245-JOY245-03]; assesses=[] -->

**よろこぶ** — *yorokobu* — "to be delighted; to rejoice".

> **ははは この おくりものを よろこびますか。** — *haha wa kono okurimono o yorokobimasu ka* — "Will my mother be delighted with this present?"

Polite **よろこびます**, *yorokobimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よろこぶ** (*yorokobu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
