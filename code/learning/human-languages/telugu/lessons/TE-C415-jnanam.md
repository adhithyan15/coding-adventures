## You'll want to know: జ్ఞానం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C415-VIDYA415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**జ్ఞానం** — *jñānaṁ* — "knowledge; wisdom".

> పుస్తకాలు చదివితే జ్ఞానం పెరుగుతుంది.
>
> *pustakālu cadivitē jñānaṁ perugutundi.*
>
> If you read books, your knowledge grows.

జ్ఞాని, a wise person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జ్ఞానం** (*jñānaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jñāna*, "knowledge" (CDIAL 5281), from *jñā*, "to know".
