## You'll want to know: રંગોળી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C329-RANGOLI329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**રંગોળી** — *rangoḷī* — "a rangoli (a coloured floor pattern)".

> દિવાળીમાં મીરાએ આંગણામાં રંગોળી બનાવી.
>
> *divāḷīmā̃ Mīrāe ā̃gṇāmā̃ rangoḷī banāvī.*
>
> At Diwali Mira made a rangoli in the courtyard.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રંગોળી** (*rangoḷī*) has a history that is not settled: Monier-Williams records a Sanskrit *raṅgavallī*, built from *raṅga*, colour, and *vallī*, a creeper, but only as the name of a plant used at sacrifices. It is a pattern of coloured powder laid on the floor at a doorway for a festival; it is feminine: **રંગોળી બનાવી**.
