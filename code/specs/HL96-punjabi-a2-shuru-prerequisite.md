Issue: #18081

After chapter 195 merged, the confidential Punjabi A2 mock audit against exact
merged main `6d9ac8d2b25a852cee1dfc216d7ddbf272a7dcd2` finds **ਸ਼ੁਰੂ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs five times in confidential examiner material
and does not occur in the confidential learner material. This spec records
counts only; neither confidential body belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 196 closes that single-word gap through one deliberately narrow polite
work instruction: **ਕੰਮ ਸ਼ੁਰੂ ਕਰੋ** — “start the work.” The voice-first opener
retrieves only familiar **ਕੰਮ** and **ਕਰੋ**. One lesson directly owns exact
**ਸ਼ੁਰੂ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound, meaning, and script, a
bounded frame lesson inserts it between the two familiar words, and a
four-skill payoff retrieves the complete instruction again. The target has one
direct owner and three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored,
generated-book, or narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-LEX-SHURU-196`. The lesson keeps the Punjabi form whole and
owns its noun sense “beginning” or “start” before retrieving the already-owned
noun-plus-`ਕਰਨਾ` pattern in the taught command. **ਕਰੋ** carries the familiar
polite command; the chapter does not mislabel **ਸ਼ੁਰੂ** as a Punjabi finite
verb. It does not teach **ਸ਼ੁਰੂਆਤ**, **ਸ਼ੁਰੂ ਵਿੱਚ**,
**ਸ਼ੁਰੂ ਹੋਣਾ**, tense or agreement variants, noun/adjective uses, or a broader
light-verb paradigm.
