## You'll want to know: der Erzähler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C316-ERZAEHLER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Erzähler** — "the narrator, the storyteller".

> Der Erzähler spricht sehr leise.
>
> *The narrator speaks very quietly.*

Plural: *die Erzähler*. Feminine *die Erzählerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Erzähler* is built on *erzählen* "to tell", from *zählen* "to count", Old High German *zellen*, Proto-Germanic *taljaną* (EtymDB): to count out events one by one.
