# げ — け, voiced

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-GETSUYOUBI, JA-LEX-C135-DOYOUBI, JA-LEX-C125-QUAL125-02] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Three recalls, then the sign.

- [YOU RECALL: say *Monday* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *Saturday* — **R2**, five lessons back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *terrible* — **R4**, eighty lessons back]

## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-GE-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KE-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01] -->

> **け** *ke* + **゛** → **げ** *ge*

Write **け** in full: the left stroke with its flick, the bar, then the long
stroke that sweeps away to the lower left. Then add the two short strokes of
the voicing mark at the upper right, the left one first.

The *k* row voices to *g*: **が**, **ぐ**, **げ**, **ご**.

With **つようび** after it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**げつようび**, *getsuyōbi*, Monday.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-GE-01, JA-SCRIPT-KE-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-GETSUYOUBI] -->

1. Write **け**, then **げ**, and say *ke*, *ge*.
2. Write **が**, **ぐ**, **げ** and **ご**, and say each one.
3. Write **げつようび** and **どようび** from memory, and say which day comes first in the week.
