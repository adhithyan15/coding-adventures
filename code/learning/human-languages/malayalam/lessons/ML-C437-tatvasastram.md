## You'll want to know: തത്വശാസ്ത്രം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C437-GANITAM437-03]; assesses=[] -->

**തത്വശാസ്ത്രം** — *tatvaśāstraṁ* — "philosophy".

> പ്രൊഫസർ തത്വശാസ്ത്രത്തെക്കുറിച്ച് ഒരു പുസ്തകം എഴുതി.
>
> *prophasar tatvaśāstrattekkuṟiccŭ oru pustakaṁ eḻuti.*
>
> The professor wrote a book on philosophy.

A philosopher is തത്വചിന്തകൻ, one who thinks about principles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തത്വശാസ്ത്രം** (*tatvaśāstraṁ*) is a Malayalam compound built from Sanskrit *tattva*, "the real state, truth" (CDIAL 5642), and *śāstra*, "a treatise, a science" (CDIAL 12422): the science of what is real.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
