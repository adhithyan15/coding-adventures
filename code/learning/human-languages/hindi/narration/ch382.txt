Hindi, chapter 382: Haste and Care.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
जल्दबाज़ी (jaldbāzī) — haste, rushing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to waste, then the Hindi for decent.

You'll want to know: जल्दबाज़ी.
जल्दबाज़ी — jaldbāzī — "haste, rushing".
जल्दबाज़ी मत करो, वरना ग़लती होगी। jaldbāzī mat karo, varnā ġaltī hogī. Don't rush, or you'll make a mistake.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
जल्दबाज़ी (jaldbāzī) is usually traced to Persian jald, "quick", and bāzī, "play, a game": playing at speed.

You'll want to know: मसलना.
मसलना — masalnā — "to rub hard, to crush".
आँखें मत मसलो, वरना वे और लाल हो जाएँगी। āṅkheṁ mat maslo, varnā ve aur lāl ho jāeṅgī. Don't rub your eyes, or they'll get even redder.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने फूल मसला, usne phūl maslā.

The word, taken apart.
मसलना (masalnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit maṣati, "rubs, crushes" (CDIAL 9919).
