## You'll want to know: निरीक्षते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C289-SAKSHYA289-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निरीक्षते** — *nirīkṣate* — "observes closely".

> साक्षी सर्वं निरीक्षते।
>
> *sākṣī sarvaṃ nirīkṣate.*
>
> The witness observes everything closely.

Past (he …): सः सर्वं निरीक्षितवान्, *saḥ sarvaṃ nirīkṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निरीक्षते** (*nirīkṣate*) is built from *nir* and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives *nir-īkṣ* as to look at, regard and observe (MW).
