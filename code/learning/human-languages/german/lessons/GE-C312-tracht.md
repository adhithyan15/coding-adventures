## You'll want to know: die Tracht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C312-VOLKSFEST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Tracht** — "the traditional dress".

> Viele Leute trugen Tracht.
>
> *Many people wore traditional dress.*

Plural: *die Trachten*. In the south and in Austria, the *Dirndl* and the *Lederhose*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tracht* is inherited from Old High German *draht*, built on the root of *tragen* "to carry, to wear", Proto-Germanic *draganą* (EtymDB): what one wears.
