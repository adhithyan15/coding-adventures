## You'll want to know: પ્રમાણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C378-HAD378-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રમાણ** — *pramāṇ* — "a proportion".

> જો દાળમાં મીઠાનું પ્રમાણ વધારે હશે તો તે ખારી લાગશે.
>
> *jo dāḷmā̃ mīṭhānũ pramāṇ vadhāre haśe to te khārī lāgśe.*
>
> If there is too much salt in the dal, it will taste salty.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રમાણ** (*pramāṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pramāṇa*, "a measure, a scale, a standard" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
