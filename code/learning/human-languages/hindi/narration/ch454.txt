Hindi, chapter 454: A Surprise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चकित (cakit) — taken aback; astonished.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for elation, then the Hindi for pleased.

You'll want to know: चकित.
चकित — cakit — "taken aback; astonished".
मीरा का नया घर देखकर सब चकित थे। Mīrā kā nayā ghar dekhkar sab cakit the. Everyone was astonished when they saw Mira's new house.

The word, taken apart.
चकित (cakit) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit cakita, "trembling, timid, startled" (Monier-Williams). Hindi has turned the start of fright into the start of surprise.

The word, taken apart.
