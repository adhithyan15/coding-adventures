## You'll want to know: سگنل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C388-SIGNAL388-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سگنل** — *signal* — "a signal (for trains or traffic)".

> سگنل لال تھا، اس لیے گاڑی رک گئی۔
>
> *signal lāl thā, is liye gāṛī ruk gaī.*
>
> The signal was red, so the train stopped.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سگنل** (*signal*) is borrowed from English *signal*.
