## You'll want to know: поздравлять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C312-POZDRAVLYAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поздравлять** (*pozdravlyát*) — "to congratulate".

> Друзья поздравили меня с днём рождения.
>
> *Druzyá pozdrávili menyá s dnyom rozhdéniya.*
>
> My friends wished me a happy birthday.

Imperfective; perfective *поздравить* (*pozdrávit*), *поздравлю*. The occasion takes *с* + instrumental: *с праздником!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поздравлять* (EtymDB: "to congratulate") is built on *по-* and *здравый* "healthy", the Church Slavonic shape (EtymDB: Church Slavonic *sŭdravŭ*) of native *здоровый*: to wish someone health.
