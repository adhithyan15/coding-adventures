# と おもいます (to omoimasu) — I think that …

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C28-VERBS28-04, JA-GRAMMAR-C251-FUTURE-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C251-NAI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to think", and "I think", politely. (*omou*, *omoimasu*.) Then say, plainly, "it will rain" and "he won't come". (*ame ga furu*, *konai*.) Those plain forms are about to get their second job.

## You'll want to know: a plain clause, then と
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C254-OPINION-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C48-ANIMALS48-03, JA-LEX-C54-TIME54-02, JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-02, JA-LEX-C25-FAMILY25-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C250-TA-SOUND-01] -->

> **いいと おもいます。**
>
> *ii to omoimasu.* — "I think it is good."

What you think goes first, in the plain form; **と** (*to*) closes it, and **おもいます** (*omoimasu*) keeps the whole sentence polite.

> **あした あめが ふると おもいます。**
>
> *ashita ame ga furu to omoimasu.* — "I think it will rain tomorrow."

The plain "not" and the plain past go before **と** the same way:

> **ともだちは こないと おもいます。**
>
> *tomodachi wa konai to omoimasu.* — "I don't think my friend will come."

> **ちちは うちに かえったと おもいます。**
>
> *chichi wa uchi ni kaetta to omoimasu.* — "I think my father has gone home."

## You'll want to know: どう おもいますか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C254-OPINION-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-01, JA-LEX-C83-DEF83-01, JA-LEX-C124-QUAL124-05] -->

To ask what someone thinks, **どう** (*dō*), "how", goes where the opinion would be:

> **どう おもいますか。**
>
> *dō omoimasu ka.* — "What do you think?"

> **この えいがを どう おもいますか。**
>
> *kono eiga o dō omoimasu ka.* — "What do you think of this film?"

> **おもしろいと おもいます。**
>
> *omoshiroi to omoimasu.* — "I think it is interesting."
