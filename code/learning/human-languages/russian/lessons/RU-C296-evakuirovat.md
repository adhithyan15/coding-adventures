## You'll want to know: эвакуировать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C296-SPASATEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**эвакуировать** (*evakuírovat*) — "to evacuate".

> Жителей эвакуировали из дома.
>
> *Zhíteley evakuírovali iz dóma.*
>
> The residents were evacuated from the building.

One form for both aspects. It takes the accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*эвакуировать* goes back to Latin *ēvacuāre* "to empty out", built on *vacuus* "empty" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the Russian verb with no parent.
