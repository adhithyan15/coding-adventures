## You'll want to know: परस्परम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C544-PARASPARA544-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परस्परम्** — *parasparam* — "each other".

> यदि भ्रातरौ परस्परं स्निह्यतः, तर्हि माता प्रसन्ना भवति।
>
> *yadi bhrātarau parasparaṃ snihyataḥ, tarhi mātā prasannā bhavati.*
>
> If the two brothers love each other, my mother is happy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परस्परम्** (*parasparam*) is built from *para*, "other", said twice, the first time in its nominative *paras*; MW gives *paraspara* as mutual, each other's (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
