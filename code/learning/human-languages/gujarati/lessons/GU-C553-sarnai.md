## You'll want to know: શરણાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C553-NAGARU553-02]; assesses=[] -->

**શરણાઈ** — *śarṇāī* — "a shehnai (a reed pipe)".

> જો લગ્નમાં શરણાઈ નહીં હોય તો મજા નહીં આવે.
>
> *jo lagnamā̃ śarṇāī nahī̃ hoy to majā nahī̃ āve.*
>
> If there's no shehnai at the wedding, it won't be any fun.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શરણાઈ** (*śarṇāī*) is usually traced to Persian *surnāy*, "a festive reed pipe".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
