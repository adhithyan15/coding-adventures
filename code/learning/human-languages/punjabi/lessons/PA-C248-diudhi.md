# ਡਿਉਢੀ (ḍiuḍhī) — a covered entrance; a threshold

## You'll want to know: ਡਿਉਢੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C248-GHR248-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਿਉਢੀ** — *ḍiuḍhī* — "a covered entrance; a threshold".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਜੁੱਤੀਆਂ ਡਿਉਢੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੱਖਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *asīṁ juttīāṁ ḍiuḍhī vich rakkhde hāṁ.*
>
> We keep our shoes in the covered entrance.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਿਉਢੀ** (*ḍiuḍhī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dēhuḍī*, "mound, threshold"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍeuḍhī*, "threshold" (CDIAL 6565).
