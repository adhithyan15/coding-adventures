## You'll want to know: il racconto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C323-FAVOLA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il racconto** — "the story, the short story".

> Il nonno ci leggeva un racconto ogni sera.
>
> *Grandfather used to read us a story every evening.*

Built on *raccontare*, ‘to tell’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*racconto* is built on *raccontare* "to tell", from *ri-*, *a-* and *contare* "to count, to tell", from Latin *computāre* (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
