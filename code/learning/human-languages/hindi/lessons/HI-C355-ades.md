## You'll want to know: आदेश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C355-VIBHAG355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आदेश** — *ādeś* — "a command, an official order".

> अधिकारी ने दफ़्तर बंद करने का आदेश दिया।
>
> *adhikārī ne daftar band karne kā ādeś diyā.*
>
> The official ordered the office to be closed.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आदेश** (*ādeś*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ādeśa*, "an instruction; a declaration" (Monier-Williams).
