## You'll want to know: दुर्गमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C403-PANTHA403-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्गमः** — *durgamaḥ* — "hard to get through; impassable".

> वर्षायां पन्थाः दुर्गमः भवति।
>
> *varṣāyāṃ panthāḥ durgamaḥ bhavati.*
>
> In the rains the route becomes hard to get through.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्गमः** (*durgamaḥ*) is built from *dur*, "hard, ill", and the root √gam, "to go": hard to go through; MW gives *durgama* as difficult to be traversed, impassable (MW).
