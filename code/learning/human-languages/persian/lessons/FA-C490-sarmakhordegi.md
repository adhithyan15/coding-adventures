## You'll want to know: سرماخوردگی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C490-COLD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سرماخوردگی** (*sarmâkhordegi*) — "a cold (illness)".

> او سرماخوردگی دارد و سر کار نمیرود.
>
> *u sarmâkhordegi dârad va sar-e kâr nemiravad.*
>
> He has a cold and isn't going to work.

The noun from *sarmâ khordan* 'to catch a cold', with the ending *-egi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarmâkhordegi* is built on native Persian pieces: *sarmâ*, 'cold', *khord*, the past stem of *khordan*, 'to eat', and the noun ending *-egi*: the state of having eaten the cold.
