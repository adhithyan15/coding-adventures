## You'll want to know: wǔbàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C505-DANCE505-03]; assesses=[] -->

*wǔbàn* — "dance partner".

> *tā shì wǒ zuì hǎo de wǔbàn* — She's my best dance partner.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wǔbàn* is built from 舞 'to dance' and *bàn* 'a companion', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjuX* and *banX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a companion'.
