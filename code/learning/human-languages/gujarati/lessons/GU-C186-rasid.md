## You'll want to know: રસીદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C186-TILL186-05]; assesses=[] -->

**રસીદ** — *rasīd* — "receipt".

> **દુકાનદાર રસીદ આપે છે.** — *dukāndār rasīd āpe che* — "The shopkeeper gives a receipt."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રસીદ** (*rasīd*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *rasīd*, "receipt"; EtymDB gives the same Persian source. CDIAL has no entry for it.
