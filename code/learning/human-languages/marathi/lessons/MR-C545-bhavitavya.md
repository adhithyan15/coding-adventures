## You'll want to know: भवितव्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C545-BHAVISHYA545-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भवितव्य** — *bhavitavya* — "destiny; what lies ahead".

> मुलांचं भवितव्य शिक्षणावर अवलंबून असतं.
>
> *mulāñcaṁ bhavitavya śikṣaṇāvar avlambūn astaṁ.*
>
> Children's future depends on their education.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भवितव्य** (*bhavitavya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bhavitavya*, "what is to be" (Monier-Williams).
