## You'll want to know: l'imprévu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C370-IMPREVU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'imprévu** — "the hitch, the unexpected event".

> J'ai un imprévu, je ne peux pas venir ce soir.
>
> *Something has come up, I cannot come tonight.*

Also an adjective: *une visite imprévue*. Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imprévu* is built from *in-* "not" and *prévu*, the participle of *prévoir* "to foresee" (EtymDB), from Latin *praevidēre* "to foresee", built on *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
