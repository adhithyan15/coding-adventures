German, chapter 384: Die Genehmigung.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Genehmigung — the permit, the approval.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the form, then the German for the signature.

You'll want to know: die Genehmigung.
die Genehmigung — "the permit, the approval".
Für das Straßenfest braucht man eine Genehmigung der Stadt. For the street party you need a permit from the town.
Plural: die Genehmigungen.

The word, taken apart.
Genehmigung is the noun of genehmigen "to approve", built on the old adjective genehm "acceptable", of the family of nehmen "to take", Proto-Germanic nemaną (EtymDB): something you can take.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: gültig.
gültig — "valid".
Die Fahrkarte ist nur heute gültig. The ticket is only valid today.
The opposite is ungültig — invalid.

The word, taken apart.
gültig is inherited from Middle High German gültic (EtymDB), from gülte "income, payment", of the family of gelten "to be worth", Old High German geltan "to pay".

Lesson 4 of 5.
verlängern — to extend, to renew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to approve, then the German for valid.

You'll want to know: verlängern.
verlängern — "to extend, to renew".
Ich muss meinen Ausweis verlängern lassen. I have to have my identity card renewed.
Inseparable: er verlängert, er hat verlängert. Built on länger — longer.

The word, taken apart.
verlängern is built with ver- on länger "longer", from lang "long", inherited from Old High German lang and Proto-Germanic langaz (EtymDB): you make the time longer.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ablaufen — to expire; to go, to proceed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for valid, then the German for to extend.

You'll want to know: ablaufen.
ablaufen — "to expire; to go, to proceed".
Mein Ausweis läuft im Mai ab. My identity card expires in May.
Separable, Perfekt with sein: er läuft ab, er ist abgelaufen. Also to go: Wie ist das Gespräch abgelaufen? — how did the conversation go?

The word, taken apart.
ablaufen is built from ab "off, away" and laufen "to run", inherited from Old High German hloufan and Proto-Germanic hlaupaną (EtymDB): the time runs out.
