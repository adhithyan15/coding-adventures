Lesson 1 of 5.
a enfermeira — a nurse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for pepper, then the Portuguese for a planet.

You'll want to know: a enfermeira.
a enfermeira — "a nurse".

The word, taken apart.
enfermeira is built from an old word for "sick" and -eira. That word from Latin infirmus, "weak, not strong" (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
o empregado — a waiter; an employee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a nurse, then the Portuguese for a cook.

You'll want to know: o empregado.
o empregado — "a waiter; an employee". In Brazil, o garçom, for a waiter.

The word, taken apart.
empregado is "employed": the participle of the verb "to employ", which from Latin implicare, "to involve, to enfold" (Lewis & Short).
