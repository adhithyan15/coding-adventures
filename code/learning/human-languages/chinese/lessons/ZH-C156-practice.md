## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-PRICES-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C156-YUAN-01, ZH-LEX-C156-JIAQIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C156-MIANFEI-01, ZH-LEX-C156-GUANGGAO-01, ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-01, ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-02, ZH-LEX-C151-KANDAO-01] -->

> **A:** *zhè běn shū duōshao qián* — How much is this book?
>
> **B:** 一本书十元
>
> *yì běn shū shí yuán* — Ten yuan a book.
>
> **A:** *jiàqián tài guì le* — That's too dear.
>
> **B:** 星期六和星期天 两本书十五元
>
> *xīngqīliù hé xīngqītiān, liǎng běn shū shíwǔ yuán* — At the weekend, two books for fifteen.
>
> **A:** *nǐ kàndào guǎnggào le ma* — Did you see the advert?
>
> **B:** *kàndào le, shuǐ miǎnfèi* — Yes, and the water's free.
>
> **A:** 本子呢
>
> *běnzi ne* — And notebooks?
>
> **B:** 两元一本
>
> *liǎng yuán yì běn* — Two yuan each.

The list says 元 and the speakers read it as written; at a counter, most people would say *kuài*. Both are right.

## What you've built — reading prices, saying prices
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-PRICES-01, ZH-SCRIPT-YUAN-MONEY-01, ZH-LEX-C156-YUAN-01, ZH-LEX-C156-JIAQIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-03] -->

Written in 元, said in *kuài*:

- **一本书十元** *yì běn shū shí yuán* — said *yì běn shū shí kuài*.
- **两本书十五元** *liǎng běn shū shíwǔ yuán* — two books, fifteen yuan.
- *jiàqián tài guì le* — the price is too high.

**Listen.** *Yí ge shǒujī duōshao qián?*

**Speak.** Read three prices from a list you know, saying *kuài* each time.
