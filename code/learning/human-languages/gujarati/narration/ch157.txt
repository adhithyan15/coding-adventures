Gujarati, chapter 157: Putting things in their place.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગોઠવવું (goṭhavvũ) — to arrange, to set out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to decorate, then the Gujarati for to tie.

You'll want to know: ગોઠવવું.
ગોઠવવું — goṭhavvũ — "to arrange, to set out".
તે પુસ્તકો ગોઠવે છે. — te pustako goṭhave che — "He arranges the books."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ગોઠવવું (goṭhavvũ) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
મૂકવું (mūkvũ) — to put, to place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to tie, then the Gujarati for to arrange.

You'll want to know: મૂકવું.
મૂકવું — mūkvũ — "to put, to place".
હું ચાવી કબાટમાં મૂકું છું. — hũ cāvī kabāṭmā̃ mūkũ chũ — "I put the key in the cupboard."

The word, taken apart.
મૂકવું (mūkvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from mukna, "loosed", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit mukta, "released"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to let go, put, place" (CDIAL 10157).

Lesson 4 of 5.
પડદો (paḍdo) — curtain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to put, then the Gujarati for to hang up.

You'll want to know: પડદો.
પડદો — paḍdo — "curtain".
બારીનો પડદો ભૂરો છે. — bārīno paḍdo bhūro che — "The window curtain is blue."

The word, taken apart.
પડદો (paḍdo) is a loan, usually traced to Persian parda, "curtain, veil". CDIAL, which follows inherited words, lists no Gujarati form.

Lesson 5 of 5.
છાજલી (chājlī) — shelf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hang up, then the Gujarati for curtain.

You'll want to know: છાજલી.
છાજલી — chājlī — "shelf".
છાજલીમાં ત્રણ પુસ્તકો છે. — chājlīmā̃ traṇ pustako che — "There are three books on the shelf."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of છાજલી (chājlī) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it; it lists Gujarati chāj, "thatch", and chājũ, "thatched lean-to shed", but does not link them to a shelf (CDIAL 5023).
