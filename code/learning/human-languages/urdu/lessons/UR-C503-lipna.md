## You'll want to know: لیپنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C503-SILAN503-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لیپنا** — *līpnā* — "to smear (with mud)".

> دادی نے آنگن کو مٹی سے لیپا۔
>
> *dādī ne āṅgan ko miṭṭī se līpā.*
>
> Grandmother smeared the courtyard with fresh mud.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے فرش لیپا, *us ne farsh līpā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لیپنا** (*līpnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *līpnā*, "to smear", under Sanskrit *lipyatē*, "is smeared" (CDIAL 11061).
