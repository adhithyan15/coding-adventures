# das Wasser — "water"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-BROT-01, GE-SOUND-W-AS-V-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Bread, and then something to drink beside it. Its first letter you
have met twice already.

## You'll want to know: das Wasser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-WASSER-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-DER-DIE-DAS-02, GE-LEX-BROT-01] -->

> **das Wasser** — "water."

Another ***das***-word, so it takes the same article as *Brot*. Two neuters in a
row is a useful accident: it lets you say *das Brot und das Wasser* without
having to decide anything.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-SOUND-W-AS-V-01, GE-LEX-WASSER-01] -->

- `w-as-v` — German *w* is an English **v**. *Wasser* opens *VAH-*.
- `vowel-a-german` — a clean open *a*.
- `r-final` — and the soft closing *r*.

*VAH-ser*. You have had that *w* on *Winter*, and inside the *shv-* of
*Schwester*; this is the third time and the first where it sits on its own at
the front of a word.
