## You'll want to know: বাঘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C437-BAGH437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বাঘ** — *bāgh* — "a tiger".

> জঙ্গলে আমরা একটা বাঘ দেখলাম।
>
> *jônggôle āmrā ekṭā bāgh dekhlām.*
>
> We saw a tiger in the forest.

The tiger of the Sundarbans mangroves is the pride of Bengal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাঘ** (*bāgh*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *bāgh*, "a tiger", under Sanskrit *vyāghrá*, "a tiger" (CDIAL 12193).
