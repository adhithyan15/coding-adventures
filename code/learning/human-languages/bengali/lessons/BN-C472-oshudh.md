## You'll want to know: ওষুধ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C472-OSHUDH472-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ওষুধ** — *oshudh* — "a medicine; medicine".

> সকালে আর রাতে এই ওষুধটা খেতে হবে।
>
> *sôkāle ār rāte ei oshudhṭā khete hôbe.*
>
> This medicine has to be taken morning and night.

Bengali eats its medicine: ওষুধ খাওয়া, to take medicine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ওষুধ** (*oshudh*) comes from Sanskrit *auṣadha*, "consisting of herbs", and so "herbs, a remedy" (CDIAL 2571), worn down in Bengali speech.
