## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मानना** (*mānnā*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *manyate*, "thinks, honours; agrees" (CDIAL 9857).
