## You'll want to know: स्फुलिंगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C296-DAVAGNI296-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्फुलिंगः** — *sphuliṅgaḥ* — "a spark".

> अग्नेः स्फुलिंगाः वायुना उड्डयन्ते।
>
> *agneḥ sphuliṅgāḥ vāyunā uḍḍayante.*
>
> Sparks from the fire fly on the wind.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्फुलिंगः** (*sphuliṅgaḥ*) MW gives *sphuliṅga* as a spark of fire and suggests, with a perhaps, that it is built from *sphulam* and *ga*, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
