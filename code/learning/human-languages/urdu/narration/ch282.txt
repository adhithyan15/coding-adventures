Urdu, chapter 282: The Investigation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تفتیش (taftīsh) — an investigation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a crook, then the Urdu for forged.

You'll want to know: تفتیش.
تفتیش — taftīsh — "an investigation".
تفتیش میں ایک مہینہ لگا۔ taftīsh meṅ ek mahīnā lagā. The investigation took a month.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تفتیش (taftīsh) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taftīsh, "a searching, an inspection". It is the word of police reports and the news.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سراغ (surāgh) — a clue; a trace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for forged, then the Urdu for an investigation.

You'll want to know: سراغ.
سراغ — surāgh — "a clue; a trace".
اگر سراغ ملا تو پولیس آئے گی۔ agar surāgh milā to polīs āe gī. If a clue turns up, the police will come.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
سراغ (surāgh) is usually traced to Persian surāgh, "a trace, a track". سراغ ملا says a clue was found; سراغ لگانا is to track something down.

جاسوس — jāsūs — "a detective; a spy".
جاسوس نے سب کچھ دیکھا۔ jāsūs ne sab kuchh dekhā. The detective saw everything.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
جاسوس (jāsūs) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic jāsūs, "a spy". In stories it is the detective who works out who did it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

پولیس کے پاس کوئی ثبوت نہیں تھا۔ polīs ke pās koī subūt nahīṅ thā. The police had no proof.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ثبوت (subūt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic thubūt, "firmness, a being established": what makes a claim stand. Urdu says its first letter as s.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ڈھونڈنا.
ڈھونڈنا — ḍhūnḍnā — "to look for".
پولیس نے ہر جگہ چور کو ڈھونڈا۔ polīs ne har jagah chor ko ḍhūnḍā. The police looked for the thief everywhere.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چابی ڈھونڈی, us ne chābī ḍhūnḍī.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ḍhū̃ḍhnā, "to seek", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6839). Urdu writes the second ḍ without the breath: ڈھونڈنا (ḍhūnḍnā).
