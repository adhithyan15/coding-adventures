Italian, chapter 448: Panchine e lampioni.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la panchina — the bench (in a park); the substitutes' bench.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to report, then the Italian for the identity card.

You'll want to know: la panchina.
la panchina — "the bench (in a park); the substitutes' bench".
Ci sediamo su una panchina al sole. We sit on a bench in the sun.
In football, in panchina — on the bench.

The word, taken apart.
panchina is a little panca 'bench', which was borrowed from Lombardic panch 'bench' (EtymDB), a Germanic word.

You'll want to know: il lampione.
il lampione — "the street lamp".
Il lampione davanti a casa è spento. The street lamp in front of the house is off.
A big lampa, a lamp.

The word, taken apart.
lampione is a big lampa 'lamp' (EtymDB), which was borrowed from Old French lampe, from Latin lampas and Greek lampás 'torch'.

cestino is a little cesta 'basket', which is inherited from Latin cista, from Greek kístē 'box, basket' (EtymDB).

il tombino — "the drain cover, the manhole".
Con la pioggia i tombini sono pieni. With the rain the drains are full.
A little tomba: a covered hole in the street.

The word, taken apart.
tombino is a little tomba 'tomb', which is inherited from Late Latin tumba, from Greek týmbos 'burial mound' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: ingombrare.
ingombrare — "to clutter, to block (a space)".
Le biciclette ingombrano il marciapiede. The bikes are blocking the pavement.
Ingombrante — bulky.

The word, taken apart.
ingombrare is built from in- and an old word for a heap or an obstacle; the origin of that word is not settled.
