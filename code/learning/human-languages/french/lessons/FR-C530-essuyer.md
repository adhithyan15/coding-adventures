## You'll want to know: essuyer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C530-VAISSELLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**essuyer** — "to wipe, to dry".

> Il essuie les assiettes après le repas.
>
> *He dries the plates after the meal.*

*J'essuie*, *nous essuyons*. *S'essuyer les mains* — to dry one's hands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*essuyer* is inherited from Latin *exsūcāre* "to squeeze the juice out", built on *sūcus* "juice" (Lewis & Short).
