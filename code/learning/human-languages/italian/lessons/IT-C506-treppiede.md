## You'll want to know: il treppiede
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C506-FOTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il treppiede** — "tripod".

> Per le foto di notte serve il treppiede.
>
> *For night photos you need a tripod.*

Also spelt *treppiedi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*treppiede* is built from *tre* "three" and *piede* "foot", inherited from Latin *pēs, pedis* (Lewis & Short).
