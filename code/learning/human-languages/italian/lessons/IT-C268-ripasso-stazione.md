# Review — one morning at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-HO-PERSO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I've missed the train". (*Ho perso il treno.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-SCUSI-01, IT-GRAMMAR-PER-ANDARE-A-02, IT-LEX-ANDATA-E-RITORNO-01, IT-GRAMMAR-BIGLIETTO-PER-02, IT-GRAMMAR-HO-UN-PROBLEMA-02] -->

Two chapters of travel, and they make one morning. You find the station, you
buy the ticket, something goes wrong, and you ask your way out of it.

[PAUSE 2s] Every step is a sentence you have said: *Scusi, per andare alla
stazione?* · *Vorrei un biglietto per Roma, andata e ritorno.* · *Scusi, ho un
problema: ho perso il treno.* · *C'è un altro treno oggi?*

[PAUSE 2s] And when you tell someone about it afterwards, the morning becomes
a story: *Il treno era in ritardo, quindi ho perso la coincidenza.* Scene,
arrow, event.
