## You'll want to know: shōushi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C133-HOUSE133-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shōushi* — "to tidy up".

> *jīntiān wǒ shōushi fángjiān* — Today I'm tidying my room.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shōushi* is built from *shōu* 'to gather' and *shi*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuw* and *dzyip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second with this very word, and with 'to pick up'.
