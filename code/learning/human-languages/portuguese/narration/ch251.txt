Portuguese, chapter 251: Gas, Plastic, Cotton, Wool, Silk.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o gás — gas.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a trunk, then the Portuguese for a thorn.

You'll want to know: o gás.
o gás — "gas".
