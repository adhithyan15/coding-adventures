# അടിമ (aṭima) — a slave

## You'll want to know: അടിമ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C401-VIPLAVAM401-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അടിമ** — *aṭima* — "a slave".

> പണ്ട് ആളുകളെ അടിമകളായി വിറ്റിരുന്നു.
>
> *paṇṭŭ āḷukaḷe aṭimakaḷāyi viṟṟirunnu.*
>
> Long ago people were sold as slaves.

Plural: അടിമകൾ, *aṭimakaḷ*.

Plural അടിമകൾ; slavery is അടിമത്തം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അടിമ** (*aṭima*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 72 lists Malayalam *aṭima*, "slavery, slave", under അടി, "the sole of the foot, the bottom": one who is kept at the feet of a master.

One more word for telling what happened.
