# Chapter 16 checkpoint — the months, ம, ஆ and ஆம்

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Nothing new here. The months out loud first, then a letter, a vowel
and the word *yes* on the page.

## Guided Practice — the months
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-THAMIZH-MAADANGAL-01, TA-PRAGMATICS-THAMIZH-MAADANGAL-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the twelve Tamil months, from the first]

(*Chithirai, Vaikāsi, Āṉi, Āḍi, Āvaṇi, Puraṭṭāsi, Aippasi, Kārthigai,
Mārgazhi, Thai, Māsi, Panguṉi*.)

- Is this a variant of the Hindu lunisolar calendar? (No: it is Tamil's own
  solar calendar, a third system beside that one and the Gregorian.)
- Which day is Tamil New Year, and which is Pongal? (*Chithirai* 1; *Thai* 1.)

## Script — ம, ஆ and ஆம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-DRIZZLE-07, TA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-VOWEL-AA-01, TA-SCRIPT-WRITE-AAM-02, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-120] -->

- Which side of **ம** is the upright on? (The left, with the curve on the
  right.)
- Why does **ஆம்** open with **ஆ** rather than the sign **◌ா**? (The word
  starts on a vowel, so the vowel takes its full letter; the sign needs a
  consonant to hang from.)
- What is **ஆ** made of? (The whole of **அ**, plus a tail on the right.)
- Which two pieces build **ஆம்**? (**ஆ** · **ம்**: the puḷḷi closes it on a bare
  *m*.)

[YOU READ: **ஆம்** aloud, and point to the ஆ and the puḷḷi]
