## You'll want to know: ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C379-OMMATA379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ** — *bhinnābhiprāya* — "a difference of opinion".

> ಅಣ್ಣ ತಮ್ಮನ ನಡುವೆ ಸಣ್ಣ ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *aṇṇa tammana naḍuve saṇṇa bhinnābhiprāya ittu.*
>
> There was a small difference of opinion between the brothers.

ಭಿನ್ನ, different, + ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ, opinion; the two vowels merge into ಆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ** (*bhinnābhiprāya*) is built from Sanskrit *bhinna*, "split, different" (CDIAL 9498), and *abhiprāya*, "aim, opinion" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
