## You'll want to know: सादर करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C513-KARYASHALA513-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सादर करणे** — *sādar karṇe* — "to present; to submit".

> मीराने बैठकीत तिचा अहवाल सादर केला.
>
> *mīrāne baiṭhkīt ticā ahvāl sādar kelā.*
>
> Meera presented her report at the meeting.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अहवाल सादर केला, *tyāne ahvāl sādar kelā*.

A report submitted or a song performed: गाणं सादर करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सादर करणे** (*sādar karṇe*) is सादर, a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic *ṣādir*, "issued, going forth", with करणे.
