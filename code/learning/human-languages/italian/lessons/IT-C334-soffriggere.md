## You'll want to know: soffriggere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C334-SOFFRITTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**soffriggere** — "to fry gently, to sauté".

> Soffriggi la cipolla nell'olio.
>
> *Fry the onion gently in the oil.*

Low heat, a few minutes, a little oil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soffriggere* joins *so-* "under", here "gently", from Latin *sub*, and *friggere* "to fry", from Latin *frīgere* (EtymDB).
