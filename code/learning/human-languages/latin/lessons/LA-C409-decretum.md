## You'll want to know: dēcrētum, dēcrētī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C409-EDICTUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dēcrētum, dēcrētī** — "a decree, a decision".

> Senātūs dēcrētum factum est.
>
> *A decree of the senate was passed.*

*dēcrētum, dēcrētī* n., the participle of *dēcernō*, to decide. English *decree*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēcrētum* is the participle of *dēcernō* "to decide, to decree" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *dē* and *cernō* "to sift". English *decree* comes from it through French.
