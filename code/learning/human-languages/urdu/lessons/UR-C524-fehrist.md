## You'll want to know: فہرست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C524-FEHRIST524-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فہرست** — *fehrist* — "a list".

> اگر فہرست تیار ہے تو مجھے دیجیے۔
>
> *agar fehrist tayyār hai to mujhe dījiye.*
>
> If the list is ready, please give it to me.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فہرست** (*fehrist*) is usually traced to Persian *fihrist*, "a list, an index".
