## You'll want to know: سفال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C508-CRAFTS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سفال** (*sofâl*) — "pottery, earthenware".

> در این کارگاه سفال میسازند.
>
> *dar in kârgâh sofâl misâzand.*
>
> In this workshop they make pottery.

*sofâlgari* — the craft of pottery; *sofâlgar* — a potter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sofâl* is the Persian word for earthenware, but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled.
