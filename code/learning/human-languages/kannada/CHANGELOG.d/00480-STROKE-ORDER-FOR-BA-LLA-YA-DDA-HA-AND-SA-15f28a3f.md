## Stroke order for ba, lla, ya, dda, ha and sa

Six more consonants now have a cited stroke order: ಳ and ಬ (KA-S113 and
KA-S110, chapter 4), ಯ (KA-S117, chapter 8), ಡ (KA-S118, chapter 9), ಹ
(KA-S120, chapter 11) and ಸ (KA-S121, chapter 12). Their writing blocks stop
saying "copy what you see" and list the movements, the pen lifts and the
source, in the same form as ನ and ಕ, and all six letter lessons now print a
stroke-order filmstrip.

- **Source.** Gopala Krishna A's `Kannada-alphabet-<slug>.gif` animations on
  Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0, 25 May 2016), the series the vowels and
  the first six consonants cite.
- **Lifts.** ಬ has no top bar and is one unbroken run. ಳ, ಡ and ಹ draw the
  whole body in one run and lift once for the hooked bar. ಸ lifts twice: the
  body, then the hooked bar, then the dot. ಯ lifts three times, as ಕ does:
  the round bowl, a fresh stroke from its foot up the middle arm, the hooked
  bar, and the small bowl on the right.
- **ಡ is filed as "da".** That series keeps "da" for retroflex ಡ and spells
  dental ದ "dha". The KA-S118 writing block says so next to the citation, and
  notes that ಡ is ದ with a small loop tucked inside its right lobe.
