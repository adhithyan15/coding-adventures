## Chapter 153 makes Punjabi A1 date fields writable from taught parts

Eleven sessions of at most three minutes take the already-taught date label,
January and February, and Gurmukhi digits into a day/month/year form line.
Learners recognise the label, assemble two fictional examples, select one from
a nonverbal cue, then progress through supported copying, delayed entry,
dictation, repair, and a no-model writing checkpoint. No real personal date is
requested. This date-field slice prepares the later integrated A1 form task;
it does not claim that task is complete.
