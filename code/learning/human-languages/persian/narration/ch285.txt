Lesson 1 of 5.
فیزیک (fizik) — physics.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a librarian, then the Persian for an accountant.

You'll want to know: فیزیک.
فیزیک — fizik — "physics".

The word, taken apart.
fizik is a loan, usually traced to French physique, "physics".
