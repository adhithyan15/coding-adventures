# ਛ — the letter the last word was waiting for

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01, PA-SCRIPT-ADDAK-DOUBLING, PA-LEX-ACHCHHA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *achchhā* aloud once. You have met the word by ear and you have
met two of its four pieces on the page already: the standalone **ਅ**, and the
little doubling mark **ੱ** that sat over **ਰੱਬ**.

## Script — one letter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-CHHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Trace **ਛ** slowly, twice. Say *chha* as you finish each one. Hang it from the
top line the way every base letter hangs. Add nothing above and nothing below.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-CHHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Cover the model. Write **ਛ** once. Compare its shape alone, repair one
difference, and stop there.
