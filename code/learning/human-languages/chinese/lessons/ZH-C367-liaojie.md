## You'll want to know: 了解
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C367-SEE367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**了解** — *liǎojiě* — "to know in depth; to find out about".

> **我很了解他** *wǒ hěn liǎojiě tā* — I know him well.

The new character, in pieces: 解 — thirteen strokes in three pieces: on the left a horn piece, seven strokes; on the top right a knife piece, two strokes; on the bottom right a cow piece, four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

Two third tones meet in this word: liǎo is said with a rising tone before jiě, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**了解** (*liǎojiě*) is built from 了 and 解, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lewX* and *keaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'clever' and 'finished', and the second 'to explain' and 'to loosen'.
