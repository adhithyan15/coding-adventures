Marathi, chapter 424: Sellers and Hawkers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विक्री (vikrī) — a sale; selling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to bargain, then the Marathi for to buy.

You'll want to know: विक्री.
विक्री — vikrī — "a sale; selling".
या महिन्यात दुकानाची विक्री वाढली. yā mahinyāt dukānācī vikrī vāḍhlī. This month the shop’s sales went up.
Feminine.
विक्रीला, for sale: घर विक्रीला आहे, the house is for sale.

The word, taken apart.
विक्री (vikrī) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi vikrī, "sale", under Sanskrit vikraya, "sale" (CDIAL 11638).

Lesson 2 of 5.
फेरीवाला (pherīvālā) — a hawker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to buy, then the Marathi for a sale.

You'll want to know: फेरीवाला.
फेरीवाला — pherīvālā — "a hawker".
फेरीवाला गल्लीत भाजी विकतो. pherīvālā gallīt bhājī vikto. The hawker sells vegetables in the lane.
Masculine.
फेरी, a round, and -वाला, the one who does it.

The word, taken apart.
फेरीवाला (pherīvālā) is built from फेरी, "a round", from the family of phirati, "turns" (CDIAL 9078), and -वाला, "the one who".

Lesson 3 of 5.
घाऊक (ghāūk) — wholesale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sale, then the Marathi for a hawker.

You'll want to know: घाऊक.
घाऊक — ghāūk — "wholesale".
तो घाऊक बाजारातून कांदे आणतो. to ghāūk bājārātūn kānde āṇto. He brings onions from the wholesale market.
It does not change: घाऊक (ghāūk) बाजार, घाऊक (ghāūk) खरेदी.

The word, taken apart.
घाऊक (ghāūk) is a Marathi word for buying and selling in bulk; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
दलाल (dalāl) — a broker; an agent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a hawker, then the Marathi for wholesale.

You'll want to know: दलाल.
दलाल — dalāl — "a broker; an agent".
दलालाने आम्हाला भाड्याचं घर दाखवलं. dalālāne āmhālā bhāḍyācaṁ ghar dākhavlaṁ. The broker showed us a house for rent.
Masculine.
दलाली is his fee.

The word, taken apart.
दलाल (dalāl) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic dallāl, "a broker, a guide".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for wholesale, then the Marathi for a broker.

You'll want to know: बिल.
बिल — bil — "a bill".
जेवणानंतर मी बिल भरलं. jevṇānantar mī bil bharlaṁ. After the meal I paid the bill.
Neuter.
बिल (bil) भरणे, to pay a bill.

The word, taken apart.
बिल (bil) is a loan from English bill.
