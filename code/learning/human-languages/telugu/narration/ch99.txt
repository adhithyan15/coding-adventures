Telugu, chapter 99: Welcomes, Gifts, Invitations.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
నమస్తే (namastē) — namaste, hello.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for that's true, then the Telugu for that's the one.

You'll want to know: నమస్తే.
నమస్తే (namastē) — "namaste", said with the palms together.
It sits beside నమస్కారం, your first word. Both bow: నమస్తే (namastē) is "a bow to you", heard all across India; నమస్కారం is the fuller Telugu form.

What you've built.
A second hello, shared with most of India.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: namastē]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: namastē, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say namastē, then namaskāraṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for that's true, then the Telugu for that's the one, then say namastē again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does నమస్తే (namastē) mean? ("Namaste, hello".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 7.
అభినందనలు (abhinandanalu) — congratulations, compliments.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for namaste, then the Telugu for delight.

You'll want to know: అభినందనలు.
అభినందనలు (abhinandanalu) — "congratulations", "compliments" on something done well.
శుభాకాంక్షలు wishes someone well for what is coming; అభినందనలు (abhinandanalu) praises what they have done. A passed exam gets both.

What you've built.
Praise for what is done, beside wishes for what comes.

Lesson 5 of 7.

You'll want to know: ఆహ్వానం.
ఆహ్వానం (āhvānaṁ) — "an invitation".

Lesson 6 of 7.
A warm welcome and short answers — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
A friend arrives at your door. Greet them with palms together, and say you are delighted.

Grammar Lens: the ending -ఏ, "exactly this".
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
word: నిజమే. built from: truth + -ఏ.
word: అదే. built from: that + -ఏ.
The -ఏ points: this truth and no other, that one exactly.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: hand over a gift; congratulate your friend]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say the wedding invitation came]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: someone asks what happened: it's nothing. Have you eaten? Not yet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: agree that it's true; say that will do; and "that's the one!"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Nothing? (ఏమీ లేదు.) Not yet? (ఇంకా లేదు.) That will do? (సరిపోతుంది.) That's true? (నిజమే.) That's the one? (అదే.) Namaste? (నమస్తే (namastē).) Delight? (ఆనందం (ānandaṁ).) Congratulations? (అభినందనలు (abhinandanalu).) A gift? (బహుమతి (bahumati).) An invitation? (ఆహ్వానం (āhvānaṁ).)


Lesson 7 of 7.
Who you are and how you are — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Give your surname first, then your nickname, then ask someone their age.

Grammar Lens: a name in two parts.
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
word: ఇంటిపేరు. literally: house name.
word: ముద్దుపేరు. literally: affection name.
word: పుట్టినరోజు. literally: born day.
Each is built from pieces you know, and the house name goes first.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: wish someone a happy birthday; hand them a gift]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask them to sign here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tell a friend to smile; then say you felt like crying]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tell a worried friend not to worry; say you regret it; say you are alone]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say an invitation came]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A surname? (ఇంటిపేరు.) A nickname? (ముద్దుపేరు.) Age? (వయసు.) A birthday? (పుట్టినరోజు.) A signature? (సంతకం.) A laugh? (నవ్వు.) Crying? (ఏడుపు.) Regret? (విచారం.) Worry? (ఆందోళన.) Alone? (ఒంటరిగా.)
