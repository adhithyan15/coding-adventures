## You'll want to know: طبل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C544-ALAT544-03]; assesses=[] -->

**طبل** — *ṭabl* — "a drum".

> ضرب الولد على الطبل بقوة.
>
> *ḍaraba al-walad ʿalā aṭ-ṭabl bi-quwwa.*
>
> The boy beat hard on the drum.

Plural: طبول, *ṭubūl*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طبل** (*ṭabl*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-b-l*, "a drum; to beat a drum" (Lane). Ṭabbāl is a drummer.
