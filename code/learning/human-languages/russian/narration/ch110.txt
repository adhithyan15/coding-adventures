Lesson 1 of 5.
клиент (kliyént) — a customer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a postman, then the Russian for a waiter.

You'll want to know: клиент.
клиент (kliyént) — "a customer".

The word, taken apart.
клиент (kliyént) came through German Klient from Latin cliēns, clientis, "a dependant, a protégé" (Vasmer).

Lesson 3 of 5.
начальник (nachálnik) — a boss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a customer, then the Russian for a till.

You'll want to know: начальник.
начальник (nachálnik) — "a boss".

The word, taken apart.
начальник (nachálnik) is начало, "beginning; authority", with -ник, "a person": the one in charge. начало goes with начать, "to begin", and конец, "end", and Vasmer relates the root to Greek kainós, "new".
