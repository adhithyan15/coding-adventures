## You'll want to know: नुस्ख़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C488-JANCH488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नुस्ख़ा** — *nuskhā* — "a prescription".

> डाक्टर ने नया नुस्ख़ा लिखा।
>
> *ḍākṭar ne nayā nuskhā likhā.*
>
> The doctor wrote out a new prescription.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नुस्ख़ा** (*nuskhā*) is usually traced to Arabic *nuskha*, "a copy, a written text", which reached Hindi through Persian; a recipe for medicine is a written text. It is also a household remedy.
