## You'll want to know: మారాం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C532-PENPAKAM532-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మారాం** — *mārāṁ* — "a tantrum; whining".

> బొమ్మ కోసం పాప మారాం చేసింది.
>
> *bomma kōsaṁ pāpa mārāṁ cēsindi.*
>
> The little girl threw a tantrum for a toy.

మారాం చేయు, to whine and sulk to get something, as small children do.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మారాం** (*mārāṁ*) is a Telugu word for a child's whining tantrum; its origin is not settled.
