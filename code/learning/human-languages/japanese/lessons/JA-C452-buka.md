## You'll want to know: ぶか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C452-BOSS452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶか** — *buka* — "a subordinate".

> **かれの ぶかは よく はたらきます。** — *kare no buka wa yoku hatarakimasu* — "His staff work hard."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶか** (*buka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bu*, "section" (Middle Chinese *buX*) and *ka*, "below" (*haeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
