## You'll want to know: दरार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C303-BHUKAMP303-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दरार** — *darār* — "a crack".

> झटके से दीवार में दरार आ गई।
>
> *jhaṭke se dīvār meṁ darār ā gaī.*
>
> The jolt left a crack in the wall.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरार** (*darār*) is listed by CDIAL under *\*darākāra*, "shaped like a crack", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 6193). A crack **आती है** or **पड़ती है** in a wall.
