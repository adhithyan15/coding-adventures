## You'll want to know: خجالت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C451-REGRET451-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خجالت** — *khijālat* — "embarrassment".

> غلط نام لے کر مجھے خجالت ہوئی۔
>
> *ghalat nām le kar mujhe khijālat huī.*
>
> I was embarrassed at using the wrong name.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خجالت** (*khijālat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khajāla*, "bashfulness, shame".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
