## You'll want to know: la gouttière
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C442-FACADES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la gouttière** — "the roof gutter, the rain gutter".

> L'eau de pluie coule dans la gouttière.
>
> *The rainwater runs into the gutter.*

On the roof; the gutter in the street has another name.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gouttière* is built on *goutte* 'drop', which is inherited from Latin *gutta* (Lewis & Short).
