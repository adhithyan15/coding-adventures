## You'll want to know: মনোবল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C453-MONOBOL453-02]; assesses=[] -->

**মনোবল** — *mônobôl* — "morale; strength of mind".

> দলটা খেলায় জিতল না, কিন্তু খেলোয়াড়দের মনোবল কমল না।
>
> *dôlṭā khelāy jitlô nā, kintu kheloyāṛder mônobôl kômlô nā.*
>
> The team did not win the match, but the players' morale did not drop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মনোবল** (*mônobôl*) joins Sanskrit *manas*, "mind" (CDIAL 9822), in its compound form *mano-*, and *bala*, "strength" (Monier-Williams): strength of mind.
