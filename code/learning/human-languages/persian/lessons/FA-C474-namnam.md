## You'll want to know: نم نم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C474-RAGBAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نم نم** (*namnam*) — "a drizzle; drizzling".

> صبح باران نم نم میبارید.
>
> *Sobh bârân namnam mibârid.*
>
> In the morning it was drizzling.

*Bârân-e namnam* — light rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*namnam* doubles native *nam* 'damp, moisture', which EtymDB lists and traces to Middle Persian.
