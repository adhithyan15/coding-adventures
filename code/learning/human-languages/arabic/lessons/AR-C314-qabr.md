## You'll want to know: قبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C314-JANAZA314-05]; assesses=[] -->

**قبر** — *qabr* — "a grave".

> زارت الأسرة قبر الجدة.
>
> *zārat al-usra qabr al-jadda.*
>
> The family visited the grandmother’s grave.

Plural: قبور, *qubūr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قبر** (*qabr*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-r* (Lane), "to bury"; *qabr* is the place of burial.
