## You'll want to know: बुद्धिबळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C532-BAITHE532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बुद्धिबळ** — *buddhibaḷ* — "chess".

> आजोबा आणि मी रविवारी बुद्धिबळ खेळतो.
>
> *ājobā āṇi mī ravivārī buddhibaḷ kheḷto.*
>
> Grandfather and I play chess on Sundays.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुद्धिबळ** (*buddhibaḷ*) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit compound *buddhibala*, *buddhi*, "intellect", and *bala*, "strength", listed as the name of a kind of game (Monier-Williams); Marathi uses it for chess.
