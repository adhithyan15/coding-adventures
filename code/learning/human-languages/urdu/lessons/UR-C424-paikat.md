## You'll want to know: پیکٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C424-MAAL424-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پیکٹ** — *paikaṭ* — "a packet".

> میں نے بسکٹ کا ایک پیکٹ خریدا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne biskuṭ kā ek paikaṭ kharīdā.*
>
> I bought a packet of biscuits.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیکٹ** (*paikaṭ*) is borrowed from English *packet*.
