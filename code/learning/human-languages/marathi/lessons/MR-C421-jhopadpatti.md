## You'll want to know: झोपडपट्टी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C421-BANDHKAM421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झोपडपट्टी** — *jhopaḍpaṭṭī* — "a slum".

> नदीजवळ एक मोठी झोपडपट्टी आहे.
>
> *nadījavaḷ ek moṭhī jhopaḍpaṭṭī āhe.*
>
> There is a big slum near the river.

Feminine.

झोपडी, a hut, and पट्टी, a strip: a row of huts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झोपडपट्टी** (*jhopaḍpaṭṭī*) is built from **झोपडी**, "a hut", inherited from *\*jhuppa* (CDIAL 5403), and **पट्टी**, "a strip".
