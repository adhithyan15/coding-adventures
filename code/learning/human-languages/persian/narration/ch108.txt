Persian, chapter 108: Wrist, Thumb, Fingernail, Chest, Waist.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
مچ (moch) — a wrist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cheek, then the Persian for an elbow.

You'll want to know: مچ.
مچ — moch — "a wrist".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: moch]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: moch, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say moch]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for a cheek, then the Persian for an elbow, then say moch again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does مچ (moch) mean? ("A wrist".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Food and nature — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for the chest and the waist.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: rice pilaf, a thick soup, a watermelon, a melon, a peach]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an apricot, cherries, a date, a pomegranate, a walnut]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an almond, a pistachio, a cucumber, an aubergine, garlic]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ginger, pepper, coffee, a sweet drink, a plain]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a hill, an island, a waterfall, a wave, a root]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 3 seconds]

A date? (خرما, khormâ.) A pomegranate? (انار, anâr.) A walnut? (گردو, gerdu.) An almond? (بادام, bâdâm.) A pistachio? (پسته, peste.) A cucumber? (خیار, khiyâr.) An aubergine? (بادمجان, bâdemjân.) Garlic? (سیر, sir.) Ginger? (زنجبیل, zanjabil.) Pepper? (فلفل, felfel.) Coffee? (قهوه, ghahve.) A sweet drink? (شربت, sharbat.) A plain? (دشت, dasht.) A hill? (تپه, tappe.) An island? (جزیره, jazire.) A waterfall? (آبشار, âbshâr.) A wave? (موج, mowj.) A root? (ریشه, rishe.)
