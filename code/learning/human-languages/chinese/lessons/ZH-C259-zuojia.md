## You'll want to know: 作家
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C259-JOB259-02]; assesses=[] -->

**作家** — *zuòjiā* — "writer; author".

> **他是作家写了十本书** *tā shì zuòjiā xiěle shí běn shū* — He's an author and has written ten books.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**作家** (*zuòjiā*) is built from 作 'to make' and 家, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsak* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a dwelling'. 家 also names a master of a craft, a sense the dictionary does not give, so how it came is not settled.
