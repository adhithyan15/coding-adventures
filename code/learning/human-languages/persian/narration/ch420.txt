Persian, chapter 420: Behind the wheel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فرمان (farmân) — a steering wheel; a command, an order.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an invoice, then the Persian for a cash machine.

You'll want to know: فرمان.
فرمان (farmân) — "a steering wheel; a command, an order".
پدرم فرمان ماشین را با دو دست میگیرد. Pedaram farmân-e mâshin râ bâ do dast migirad. My father holds the steering wheel with both hands.
The older sense is a command or a royal decree. Posht-e farmân — at the wheel, driving.

The word, taken apart.
farmân is inherited: EtymDB traces it through Middle Persian framān to Old Persian framānā 'command'. The wheel is the part of the car that gives the orders.

Lesson 2 of 5.
رانندگی کردن (rânandegi kardan) — to drive (a car).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cash machine, then the Persian for a steering wheel.

You'll want to know: رانندگی کردن.
رانندگی کردن (rânandegi kardan) — "to drive (a car)".
خواهرم هر روز تا دفتر رانندگی میکند. Khâharam har ruz tâ daftar rânandegi mikonad. My sister drives to the office every day.
rânandegi — driving, from rânande (a driver), + kardan. Present stem kon-: rânandegi mikonam — I drive.

The word, taken apart.
rânandegi kardan is built from rânandegi 'driving', formed on the native verb rândan 'to drive', and kardan 'to do', which is inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
دنده (dande) — a gear (of a car); a rib.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a steering wheel, then the Persian for to drive.

You'll want to know: دنده.
دنده (dande) — "a gear (of a car); a rib".
این ماشین پنج دنده دارد. In mâshin panj dande dârad. This car has five gears.
Also a rib of the body. Dande-ye 'aqab — reverse gear.

The word, taken apart.
dande is a native Persian word for a rib, later also for the teeth and gears of a wheel. Its older history is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دنده عوض کردن (dande 'avaz kardan) — to change gear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to drive, then the Persian for a gear.

You'll want to know: دنده عوض کردن.
دنده عوض کردن (dande 'avaz kardan) — "to change gear".
او قبل از پیچ دنده عوض میکند. U qabl az pich dande 'avaz mikonad. He changes gear before the bend.
dande 'gear' + 'avaz 'a change, an exchange' + kardan. 'Avaz kardan on its own means to change or swap.

The word, taken apart.
dande 'avaz kardan is built from native dande 'gear', 'avaz 'exchange', a loan usually traced to Arabic ʿiwaḍ 'a substitute', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
کلاچ (kelâch) — a clutch (pedal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a gear, then the Persian for to change gear.

You'll want to know: کلاچ.
کلاچ (kelâch) — "a clutch (pedal)".
کلاچ (kelâch) ماشین برادرم خیلی سفت است. Kelâch-e mâshin-e barâdaram kheyli seft ast. The clutch in my brother's car is very stiff.
Kelâch gereftan — to press the clutch. Automatic cars have no kelâch.

The word, taken apart.
kelâch was borrowed from a European language and is usually traced to English clutch, the pedal that grips and frees the engine. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
