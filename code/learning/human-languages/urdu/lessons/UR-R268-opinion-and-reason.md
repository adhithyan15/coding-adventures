# Review — what I think, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-01, UR-LEX-C267-BAD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "having eaten" and "after the meal". (*khā kar*, *khāne ke bād*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C268-LAGNA-01, UR-LEX-C268-KHAYAL-01, UR-LEX-C268-GHALAT-01, UR-SCRIPT-C268-GHAIN-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-02, UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-LEX-C41-HEALTH41-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I'm hungry. I liked this film.]
- [YOU SAY: I think it will rain. What do you think?]
- [YOU SAY: In my view this is right. In my opinion that is wrong.]
- [YOU SAY: I didn't come because I was ill.]
- [YOU SAY: Because of the rain we stayed at home.]
- [YOU WRITE: **غلط** once, and point at غ inside it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I think he won't come"? (**مجھے لگتا ہے کہ وہ نہیں آئے گا**.) Now the other way round: **چونکہ** or **کیونکہ** to put the reason first? (**چونکہ**.) "Because of the delay"? (**دیر کی وجہ سے**.)
