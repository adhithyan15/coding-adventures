## You'll want to know: মন্তব্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C357-PROBONDHO357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মন্তব্য** — *môntôbyô* — "a comment; a remark".

> তোমার মন্তব্যটা দরকারি ছিল।
>
> *tomār môntôbyôṭā dôrkāri chhilô.*
>
> Your comment was useful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মন্তব্য** (*môntôbyô*) is Sanskrit *mantavya*, 'what is to be thought' (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for a short remark or comment.
