## You'll want to know: l'adoption
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C492-FAMILLES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'adoption** — "adoption".

> L'adoption a pris trois ans.
>
> *The adoption took three years.*

Feminine. *Un enfant adopté* — an adopted child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'adoption* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adoptiō* "adoption", from *adoptāre*, built on *optāre* "to choose" (Lewis & Short).
