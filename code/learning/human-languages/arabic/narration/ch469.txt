Arabic, chapter 469: Soil and crops.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تربة (turba) — soil; earth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to bloom, then the Arabic for to bear fruit.

You'll want to know: تربة.
تربة — turba — "soil; earth".
التربة هنا جيدة للقمح. at-turba hunā jayyida li-l-qamḥ. The soil here is good for wheat.

The word, taken apart.
تربة (turba) is built on the native Arabic root t-r-b, "earth, dust" (Lane). It shares its root with turāb, "dust, soil".

سماد — samād — "fertiliser; manure".
وضع المزارع السماد في الحقل. waḍaʿa al-muzāriʿ as-samād fī al-ḥaql. The farmer put fertiliser on the field.

The word, taken apart.
سماد (samād) is built on the native Arabic root s-m-d, "to manure the land" (Lane). Lane gives samād as manure of dung and earth.

كان محصول القمح كبيرا. kāna maḥṣūl al-qamḥ kabīran. The wheat crop was large.

The word, taken apart.
محصول (maḥṣūl) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-ṣ-l, "to remain, to be obtained" (Lane). It is a passive participle: what is obtained.

اقتلع (iqtalaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root q-l-ʿ, "to pull up, to uproot" (Lane). It is Form VIII.
