## You'll want to know: प्रतिकूलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C370-STHITI370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिकूलः** — *pratikūlaḥ* — "adverse; unfavourable".

> यदि वायुः प्रतिकूलः, तर्हि नौका न गमिष्यति।
>
> *yadi vāyuḥ pratikūlaḥ, tarhi naukā na gamiṣyati.*
>
> If the wind is against us, the boat will not go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिकूलः** (*pratikūlaḥ*) is built from *prati*, "against", and *kūla*, "a bank"; MW glosses it as "against the bank" and gives "contrary, adverse, opposite" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
