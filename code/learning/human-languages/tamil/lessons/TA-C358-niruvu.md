## You'll want to know: நிறுவு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C358-KOTPATU358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**நிறுவு** — *niṟuvu* — "to establish; to found".

> அவர் இந்த ஊரில் ஒரு பள்ளியை நிறுவினார்.
>
> *avar inta ūril oru paḷḷiyai niṟuviṉār.*
>
> He founded a school in this town.

Past (I …): நிறுவினேன், *niṟuviṉēṉ*.

What is founded takes -ஐ. A fact can be established too: உண்மையை நிறுவு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நிறுவு** (*niṟuvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3675 lists Tamil *niṟuvu*, "to set up, establish", in the family of *nil*, "to stand": to make something stand.
