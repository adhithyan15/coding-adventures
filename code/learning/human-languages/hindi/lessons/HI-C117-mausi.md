# मौसी (mausī) — aunt, the mother's sister

## You'll want to know: मौसी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C117-AUNT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मौसी** (*mausī*) — "aunt", your mother's sister.

It is often explained as **माँ जैसी**, "like a mother": the aunt closest to a mother's place.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

An aunt, on your mother's side.
