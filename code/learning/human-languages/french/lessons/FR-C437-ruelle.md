## You'll want to know: la ruelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C437-RUELLES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la ruelle** — "the alley, the lane".

> Nous nous promenons dans les ruelles du vieux quartier.
>
> *We stroll through the alleys of the old quarter.*

A small *rue*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruelle* is a small *rue* 'street', which is inherited from Latin *rūga* 'wrinkle, fold' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists it without a source.
