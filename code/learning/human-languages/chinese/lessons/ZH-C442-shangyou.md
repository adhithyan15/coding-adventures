## You'll want to know: 上游
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C442-RIVER442-03]; assesses=[] -->

**上游** — *shàngyóu* — "upper reaches (of a river); upstream".

> **我们坐船去河的上游** *wǒmen zuò chuán qù hé de shàngyóu* — We're taking a boat up the river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**上游** (*shàngyóu*) is built from 上 'above' and 游 'to float', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangH* and *yuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); how the second came to name a stretch of a river is not settled.
