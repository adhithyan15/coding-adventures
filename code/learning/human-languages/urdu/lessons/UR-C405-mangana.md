## You'll want to know: منگانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C405-RAJISTAR405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منگانا** — *mangānā* — "to send for; to order (to be brought)".

> ہم نے کمرے میں چائے منگائی۔
>
> *ham ne kamre meṅ chāe mangāī.*
>
> We ordered tea to the room.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کھانا منگایا, *us ne khānā mangāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منگانا** (*mangānā*) is built on **مانگنا**, "to ask for", whose Hindi cousin *māgnā* CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *mārgati*, "seeks" (CDIAL 10074): it is to have something brought to you.
