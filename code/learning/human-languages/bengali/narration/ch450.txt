Bengali, chapter 450: Hurt Feelings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
তুচ্ছ (tuchchhô) — trifling; petty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for romantic love, then the Bengali for to be devoted to.

You'll want to know: তুচ্ছ.
তুচ্ছ — tuchchhô — "trifling; petty".
একটা তুচ্ছ ব্যাপার নিয়ে দুই ভাই ঝগড়া করল। ekṭā tuchchhô bæpār niye dui bhāi jhôgṛā kôrlô. The two brothers quarrelled over a trifling matter.
তুচ্ছ (tuchchhô) করা, to brush aside, to treat as nothing.

The word, taken apart.
তুচ্ছ (tuchchhô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit tuccha, "empty, vain, small, trifling" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
উপহাস (upôhās) — mockery; derision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to be devoted to, then the Bengali for trifling.

You'll want to know: উপহাস.
উপহাস — upôhās — "mockery; derision".
মীরার হাসিতে উপহাস ছিল। mīrār hāsite upôhās chhilô. There was mockery in Mira's laugh.
উপহাস (upôhās) করা, to mock, to make fun of.

The word, taken apart.
উপহাস (upôhās) is a tatsama, Sanskrit upahāsa, "sport, laughter" (CDIAL 2282), on the root has, to laugh, the হাসা (taught) of Bengali; Bengali uses it for laughter aimed at someone.

Lesson 3 of 5.
অপমান করা (ôpômān kôrā) — to insult; to humiliate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for trifling, then the Bengali for mockery.

You'll want to know: অপমান করা.
অপমান করা — ôpômān kôrā — "to insult; to humiliate".
দোকানদার খদ্দেরকে অপমান করল। dokāndār khôdderke ôpômān kôrlô. The shopkeeper insulted the customer.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কর্মচারীকে অপমান করলেন, tini kôrmôchārīke ôpômān kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
অপমান করা (ôpômān kôrā) is Sanskrit apamāna, "disrespect, contempt, disgrace" (Monier-Williams), with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

খোঁটা দেওয়া — khõṭā deoyā — "to taunt; to throw something in someone's face".
প্রতিবেশী মহিলাটি রোজ মীরাকে খোঁটা দিতেন। prôtibeshī môhilāṭi roj mīrāke khõṭā diten. The neighbour woman used to taunt Mira every day.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বোনকে খোঁটা দিলেন, tini bonke khõṭā dilen.

The word, taken apart.
খোঁটা দেওয়া (khõṭā deoyā) is khõṭā, "a jibe", whose origin is not settled, with deoyā, "to give" (CDIAL 6141).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to insult, then the Bengali for to taunt.

You'll want to know: হেয় করা.
হেয় করা — heyô kôrā — "to belittle; to look down on".
ধনী লোকটা গরিবদের হেয় করত। dhônī lokṭā gôribder heyô kôrtô. The rich man used to look down on the poor.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ছাত্রটিকে হেয় করলেন, tini chhātrôṭike heyô kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
হেয় করা (heyô kôrā) is Sanskrit heya, "to be left, to be rejected or avoided" (Monier-Williams), with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
