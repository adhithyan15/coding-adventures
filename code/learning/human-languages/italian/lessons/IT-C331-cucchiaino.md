## You'll want to know: il cucchiaino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C331-RICETTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il cucchiaino** — "the teaspoon".

> Un cucchiaino di zucchero, per favore.
>
> *A teaspoon of sugar, please.*

A little *cucchiaio*, ‘spoon’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cucchiaino* is a little *cucchiaio* "spoon", from Latin *cochlear* "a spoon", first a tool for eating snails, *cochlea* (Lewis & Short).
