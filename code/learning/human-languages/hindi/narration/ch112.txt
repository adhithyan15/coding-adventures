Hindi, chapter 112: Right, Wrong, Sure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सही (sahī) — right, correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for I could not hear it, then the Hindi for please write it down.

You'll want to know: सही.
सही (sahī) — "right, correct".
सही (sahī) जवाब — "the right answer". Said alone, सही (sahī)! agrees: "right!".

What you've built.
A word for getting it right.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sahī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sahī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sahī!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for I could not hear it, then the Hindi for please write it down, then say sahī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सही (sahī) mean? ("Right, correct".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ग़लत (ġalat) — wrong, incorrect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for please write it down, then the Hindi for right.

You'll want to know: ग़लत.
ग़लत (ġalat) — "wrong".
The opposite of सही (sahī). यह ग़लत (ġalat) है — "this is wrong".

What you've built.
Right and wrong, as a pair.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: ġalat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ġalat, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say yah ġalat hai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for please write it down, then the Hindi for right, then say ġalat again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ग़लत (ġalat) mean? ("Wrong, incorrect".) Say it once more.


Lesson 3 of 5.
पक्का (pakkā) — sure, certain; firm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for right, then the Hindi for wrong.

You'll want to know: पक्का.
पक्का (pakkā) — "sure, certain", and of a thing, "firm, ripe".
पक्का (pakkā)? — "for sure?". पक्का (pakkā)! — "definitely!".

What you've built.
A question and an answer in one word.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pakkā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask pakkā?, then answer pakkā!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for right, then the Hindi for wrong, then say pakkā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does पक्का (pakkā) mean? ("Sure, certain; firm".) Say it once more.


Lesson 4 of 5.
सच में? (sac meṁ?) — really?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for wrong, then the Hindi for sure.

You'll want to know: सच में?
सच में? (sac meṁ?) — "really?", literally "in truth?".
सच is the "true" you know, with में, "in".

What you've built.
A way to show surprise.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sac meṁ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sac meṁ?, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask sac meṁ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for wrong, then the Hindi for sure, then say sac meṁ? again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सच में? (sac meṁ?) mean? ("Really?".) Say it once more.


Lesson 5 of 5.
अरे (are) — oh!, hey!

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for sure, then the Hindi for really.

You'll want to know: अरे.
अरे (are) — "oh!", "hey!", a cry of surprise.
अरे (are), सच में? (sac meṁ?) — "oh, really?". It is informal: use it with friends.

What you've built.
Right, wrong, sure, really, oh: five reactions.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: are]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: are, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say are, sac meṁ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for sure, then the Hindi for really, then say are again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does अरे (are) mean? ("Oh!, hey!".) Say it once more.
