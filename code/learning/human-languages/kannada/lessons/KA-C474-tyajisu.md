## You'll want to know: ತ್ಯಜಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C474-TYAJYA474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ತ್ಯಜಿಸು** — *tyajisu* — "to forsake; to give up".

> ಅವನು ಪ್ಲಾಸ್ಟಿಕ್ ಚೀಲಗಳನ್ನು ತ್ಯಜಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *avanu plāsṭik cīlagaḷannu tyajisidanu.*
>
> He gave up plastic bags.

Past (I …): ತ್ಯಜಿಸಿದೆ, *tyajiside*.

A formal word; what is given up takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತ್ಯಜಿಸು** (*tyajisu*) is built from the Sanskrit root *tyaj*, "to leave, to let go" (CDIAL 5984, *tyajati*), with the verb ending -*isu*.
