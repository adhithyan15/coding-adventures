## You'll want to know: वेशभूषा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C537-NATYA537-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वेशभूषा** — *veśbhūṣā* — "a costume".

> नाटकासाठी मुलांनी जुन्या काळची वेशभूषा केली.
>
> *nāṭkāsāṭhī mulānnī junyā kāḷcī veśbhūṣā kelī.*
>
> For the play the children dressed in costumes of the old days.

Feminine.

वेशभूषा करणे, to put on a costume.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेशभूषा** (*veśbhūṣā*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *veśa*, "dress", and *bhūṣā*, "ornament" (Monier-Williams).
