## You'll want to know: 公正
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C362-MUST362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**公正** — *gōngzhèng* — "fair; impartial".

> **我觉得校长很公正** *wǒ juéde xiàozhǎng hěn gōngzhèng* — I think the head is very fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**公正** (*gōngzhèng*) is built from 公 and 正 'proper', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *tsyengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'upright' and 'selfless', and the second 'proper'.
