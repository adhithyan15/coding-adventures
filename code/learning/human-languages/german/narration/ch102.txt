German, chapter 102: Zero, Word, Answer, Work, Music.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
null — zero.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a hundred, then the German for a thousand.

You'll want to know: null.
null — "zero".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: null]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: null, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say null]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for a hundred, then the German for a thousand, then say null again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does null mean? ("Zero".) Say it once more.
