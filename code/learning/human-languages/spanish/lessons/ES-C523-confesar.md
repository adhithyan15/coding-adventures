## You'll want to know: confesar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C523-PRESO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**confesar** — "to confess".

> Al final confesó la verdad.
>
> *In the end he confessed the truth.*

Stem change: *confieso*, *confiesas*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*confesar* is usually traced to a spoken Latin verb made from *cōnfessus*, the participle of *cōnfitērī* 'to acknowledge, to confess' (Lewis & Short), from *fatērī* 'to admit'.
