## You'll want to know: ਪੋਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C244-KAM244-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੋਣਾ** — *poṇā* — "a straining cloth".

> ਪੋਣਾ ਚਿੱਟਾ ਅਤੇ ਸਾਫ਼ ਹੈ।
>
> *poṇā chiṭṭā ate sāf hai.*
>
> The straining cloth is white and clean.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੋਣਾ** (*poṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pavana*, "sieve, strainer"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *poṇā*, "strainer, sieve" (CDIAL 7977).
