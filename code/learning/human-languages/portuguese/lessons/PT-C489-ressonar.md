## You'll want to know: ressonar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C489-SONO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ressonar** — "to snore".

> O meu marido ressona tanto que não consigo dormir.
>
> *My husband snores so much that I cannot sleep.*

In Brazil: *roncar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ressonar* comes from Latin *resonāre* "to resound, to echo" (EtymDB), built on *sonāre* "to sound".
