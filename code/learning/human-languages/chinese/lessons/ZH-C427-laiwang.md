## You'll want to know: 来往
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C427-COMEGO427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**来往** — *láiwǎng* — "to come and go".

> **路口来往的车很快你要小心** *lùkǒu láiwǎng de chē hěn kuài nǐ yào xiǎoxīn* — The cars coming and going at the junction are fast: be careful.

The new character, in pieces: 往 — eight strokes in two pieces: on the left the left piece of 很, three strokes, two short strokes falling to the left and a vertical; on the right a dot, two bars, a vertical through them and a long bar at the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**来往** (*láiwǎng*) is built from 来 'to come' and 往 'to go', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *loj* and *hjwangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to go'.
