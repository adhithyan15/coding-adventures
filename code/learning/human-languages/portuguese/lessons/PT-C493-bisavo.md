## You'll want to know: o bisavô
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C493-GERACOES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o bisavô** — "great-grandfather".

> O meu bisavô nasceu numa aldeia perto do mar.
>
> *My great-grandfather was born in a village near the sea.*

*A bisavó* — great-grandmother; *os bisavós* — great-grandparents.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bisavô* is built from *bis-* "twice" and *avô* "grandfather", which goes back to Latin *avus* "grandfather" (Lewis & Short).
