# خفا (khafā) — displeased (with someone)

## You'll want to know: خفا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C444-ANGER444-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خفا** — *khafā* — "displeased (with someone)".

> سارا اپنے بھائی سے خفا ہے۔
>
> *sārā apne bhāī se khafā hai.*
>
> Sara is upset with her brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خفا** (*khafā*) is usually traced to Persian *khafa*, "stifled, vexed".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
