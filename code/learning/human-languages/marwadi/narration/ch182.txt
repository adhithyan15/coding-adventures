Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 182: Shake, Stir and Serve.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
झटकणो (jhaṭakṇo) — to shake out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to strain, then the Marwadi for rice pudding.

You'll want to know: झटकणो.
झटकणो — jhaṭakṇo — "to shake out".
हूं दिनूगै चादर झटकूं हूं। hū̃ dinūgai chādar jhaṭkū̃ hū̃. I shake out the sheet early in the morning.
Past (I …): म्हैं चादर झटकी, mhaĩ chādar jhaṭkī.

The word, taken apart.
झटकणो (jhaṭakṇo) comes from a reconstructed jhaṭ, "a sudden movement", the word behind झट; CDIAL lists Hindi jhaṭaknā, "to jerk" (CDIAL 5327). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
घोळणो (ghoḷṇo) — to dissolve; to stir in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for rice pudding, then the Marwadi for to shake out.

You'll want to know: घोळणो.
घोळणो — ghoḷṇo — "to dissolve; to stir in".
हूं पाणी मांय गुड़ घोळूं हूं। hū̃ pāṇī mā̃y guṛ ghoḷū̃ hū̃. I dissolve jaggery in water.
Past (I …): म्हैं गुड़ घोळ्यो, mhaĩ guṛ ghoḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
घोळणो (ghoḷṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit gholayati, "mixes, stirs together"; CDIAL lists Hindi gholnā, "to mix, dissolve", and Gujarati ghoḷvũ (CDIAL 4526). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
मिलावणो (milāvṇo) — to mix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to shake out, then the Marwadi for to dissolve.

You'll want to know: मिलावणो.
मिलावणो — milāvṇo — "to mix".
हूं दूध मांय खांड मिलाऊं हूं। hū̃ dūdh mā̃y khā̃ḍ milāū̃ hū̃. I mix sugar into the milk.
Past (I …): म्हैं दूध मांय खांड मिलाई, mhaĩ dūdh mā̃y khā̃ḍ milāī.

The word, taken apart.
मिलावणो (milāvṇo) is built on Sanskrit milati, "meets"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari milaï (CDIAL 10133). It is the causative partner of मिलणो, to meet: to make things meet is to mix them.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to dissolve, then the Marwadi for to mix.

You'll want to know: बघारणो.
बघारणो — baghārṇo — "to temper (spices in hot oil)".
मां दाल बघारै है। mā̃ dāl baghārai hai. Mother tempers the dal.
Past (I …): म्हैं दाल बघारी, mhaĩ dāl baghārī.

The word, taken apart.
बघारणो (baghārṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vyāghārayati, "sprinkles over" (with ghee); CDIAL lists Hindi baghārnā, for frying spices in hot oil (CDIAL 12191). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पुरसणो (purasṇo) — to serve food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to mix, then the Marwadi for to temper.

You'll want to know: पुरसणो.
पुरसणो — purasṇo — "to serve food".
मां थाळी मांय रोटी पुरसै है। mā̃ thāḷī mā̃y roṭī pursai hai. Mother serves rotis onto the plate.
Past (I …): म्हैं खीचड़ो पुरस्यो, mhaĩ khīchṛo purasyo.

The word, taken apart.
पुरसणो (purasṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit pariveṣati, "serves food"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari purasaï, "serves a meal" (CDIAL 7888).
