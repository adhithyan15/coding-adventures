## You'll want to know: विनती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C65-GRACE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विनती** (*vintī*) — "an entreaty".

Usually derived from Sanskrit विज्ञप्ति (*vijñapti*), 'a respectful making-known', which is what a petition to a king was called. The heavy conjunct in the middle collapsed on its way through Prakrit and left विनती.

It sits above कृपया in weight. कृपया goes in front of a request the way 'please' does; विनती is the request itself, offered from below, and it turns up in prayer and in a plea as often as in ordinary speech.
