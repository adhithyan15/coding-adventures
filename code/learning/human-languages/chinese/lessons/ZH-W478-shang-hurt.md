## Script — 亻, then a short top over 力
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHANG-HURT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 伤

**Three pieces: the left, then the top right, then the bottom right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, 亻, two strokes.** A stroke falling to the left, then a vertical down.

**The top right, two strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left, then a bar from left to right beside it.

**The bottom right, 力, two strokes.** A stroke that runs right, turns down and hooks to the left, then a long stroke falling to the left through it.

2 + 2 + 2 = six.
