## You'll want to know: признаваться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C294-NAKAZANIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**признаваться** (*priznavátsya*) — "to confess, to admit".

> Он признался, что взял деньги.
>
> *On priználsya, shto vzyal déngi.*
>
> He admitted that he had taken the money.

Imperfective; perfective *признаться* (*priznátsya*). Present *признаюсь*, *признаёшься* (*priznayóshsya*). *Признаться в любви* — to declare one's love.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*признаваться* is built on *при-* and *знать* "to know", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *znati*, from PIE *ǵneh₃-* "to know" (EtymDB): to make known.
