## You'll want to know: регистрация
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C419-REGISTRATSIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**регистрация** (*registrátsiya*) — "check-in; registration".

> Регистрация начинается в шесть утра.
>
> *Registrátsiya nachináyetsya v shest utrá.*
>
> Check-in opens at six in the morning.

Feminine. *Стойка регистрации* — the check-in desk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*регистрация* is a learned word built on *регистр* 'a register', usually traced to Medieval Latin *registrum* 'a list'. EtymDB lists the Russian word without a source, so the line is hedged.
