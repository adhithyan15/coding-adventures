# виноватый (vinovátyy) — guilty, to blame

## You'll want to know: виноватый
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C352-VINA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**виноватый** (*vinovátyy*) — "guilty, to blame".

> Никто не виноват, так получилось.
>
> *Niktó ne vinovát, tak poluchílos.*
>
> Nobody is to blame; that is how it turned out.

The short form *виноват* is the usual one: *я виноват* — it is my fault. *Виноват!* is also a quick apology.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**виноватый** is built on *вина* "guilt" with the suffix *-оватый*; *вина* is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *\*vina*.
