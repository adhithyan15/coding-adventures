# వీడ్కోలు (vīḍkōlu) — a farewell

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C356-UTSAVAM356-02, TE-LEX-C356-UTSAVAM356-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to felicitate, then the Telugu for a housewarming.

## You'll want to know: వీడ్కోలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C356-UTSAVAM356-04]; assesses=[] -->

**వీడ్కోలు** — *vīḍkōlu* — "a farewell".

> ఆఫీసులో అతనికి ఘనంగా వీడ్కోలు ఇచ్చారు.
>
> *āphīsulō ataniki ghanaṅgā vīḍkōlu iccāru.*
>
> At the office they gave him a grand farewell.

వీడ్కోలు ఇచ్చు, to see someone off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వీడ్కోలు** (*vīḍkōlu*) is a Telugu word for a send-off; its origin is not settled.
