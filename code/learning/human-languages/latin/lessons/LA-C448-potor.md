## You'll want to know: pōtor, pōtōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C448-STERTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pōtor, pōtōris** — "a drinker, a heavy drinker".

> In tabernā pōtōrēs multum vīnum bibunt.
>
> *In the tavern the drinkers drink a lot of wine.*

Third declension, masculine: *pōtor, pōtōris m.*, from the same root as *pōtus* 'drunk'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pōtor* is built on the root of *pōtō* 'to drink' with the suffix *-tor* 'one who' (Lewis & Short). Greek *pinō* 'to drink' is a cousin of that root.
