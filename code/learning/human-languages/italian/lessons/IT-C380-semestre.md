## You'll want to know: il semestre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C380-UNIVERSITA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il semestre** — "the semester, the half-year".

> Nel secondo semestre ho meno lezioni.
>
> *In the second semester I have fewer lessons.*

Six months; a *trimestre* is three.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*semestre* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sēmestris* "of six months" (Lewis & Short), built from *sex* "six" and *mēnsis* "month".
