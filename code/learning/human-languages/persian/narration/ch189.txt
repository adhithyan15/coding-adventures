Lesson 1 of 5.
آمپول (âmpul) — an injection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to swing, then the Persian for to install.

You'll want to know: آمپول.
آمپول — âmpul — "an injection".

The word, taken apart.
âmpul is borrowed from French ampoule, "phial" (EtymDB). The French word goes back to Latin ampulla, "small flask".

Lesson 3 of 5.
آزمایش (âzmâyesh) — a medical test.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an injection, then the Persian for a prescription.

You'll want to know: آزمایش.
آزمایش — âzmâyesh — "a medical test".

The word, taken apart.
âzmâyesh is âzmâ-, the present stem of âzmudan, "to test", with -yesh: a test. The verb is native, usually traced to Middle Persian uzmūdan.
