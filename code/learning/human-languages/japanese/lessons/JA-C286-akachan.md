## You'll want to know: あかちゃん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C286-AKACHAN286-01]; assesses=[] -->

**あかちゃん** — *akachan* — "a baby".

> **あかちゃんが ないています。** — *akachan ga naite imasu* — "The baby is crying."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あかちゃん** (*akachan*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *aka*, "red" (from *akai*), and *-chan*, "the affectionate suffix" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
