## You'll want to know: gōngchǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C487-CAREER487-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngchǎng* — "factory".

> *wǒ bàba zài gōngchǎng gōngzuò le èrshí nián* — My dad worked in a factory for twenty years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngchǎng* is built from 工 'work' and *chǎng* 'a shed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *tsyhangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'skilled work' and the second 'a roofed building'.
