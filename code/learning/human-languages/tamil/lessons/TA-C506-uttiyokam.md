# உத்தியோகம் (uttiyōkam) — a salaried job; a post

## You'll want to know: உத்தியோகம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C506-VELAI506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**உத்தியோகம்** — *uttiyōkam* — "a salaried job; a post".

> அவருக்கு வங்கியில் உத்தியோகம் கிடைத்தது.
>
> *avarukku vaṅkiyil uttiyōkam kiṭaittatu.*
>
> He got a job in a bank.

A more formal word than வேலை (*vēlai*): உத்தியோகம் suggests a salaried post, often in an office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உத்தியோகம்** (*uttiyōkam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *udyoga*, "active preparation, exertion" (CDIAL 2055). Tamil wrote the Sanskrit cluster *dy* as த்திய and gave the noun the ending -அம்.
