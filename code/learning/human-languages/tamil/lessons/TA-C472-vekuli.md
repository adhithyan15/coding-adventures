## You'll want to know: வெகுளி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C472-MANITAR472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**வெகுளி** — *vekuḷi* — "a naive person; an innocent".

> அவள் ஒரு வெகுளி, எல்லோரையும் நம்புவாள்.
>
> *avaḷ oru vekuḷi, ellōraiyum nampuvāḷ.*
>
> She is naive; she trusts everyone.

வெகுளித்தனம், naivety.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வெகுளி** (*vekuḷi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5464 gives it the older sense "anger". How it came to mean an artless person is not settled.
