## You'll want to know: ಮೂಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C515-KURUDA515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂಕ** — *mūka* — "mute; unable to speak".

> ಆ ಮೂಕ ಹುಡುಗ ಕೈಸನ್ನೆಯಿಂದ ಮಾತಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *ā mūka huḍuga kaisanneyinda mātāḍuttāne.*
>
> That mute boy talks with hand signs.

ಮೂಕ ಪ್ರಾಣಿ, a dumb creature, an animal that cannot complain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂಕ** (*mūka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mūka*, "tied, tongue-tied, dumb, silent" (Monier-Williams).
