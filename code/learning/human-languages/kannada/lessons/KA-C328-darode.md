## You'll want to know: ದರೋಡೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C328-DAROODE328-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ದರೋಡೆ** — *darōḍe* — "a robbery".

> ನಿನ್ನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಬ್ಯಾಂಕಿನಲ್ಲಿ ದರೋಡೆ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *ninne rātri byāṅkinalli darōḍe āyitu.*
>
> There was a robbery at the bank last night.

ದರೋಡೆ is robbery by force, often by a gang.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದರೋಡೆ** (*darōḍe*) is usually traced to Marathi *daroḍā*, "a gang robbery"; the earlier history of the word is not settled.
