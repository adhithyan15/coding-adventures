## You'll want to know: प्रभावः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C340-KARANA340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रभावः** — *prabhāvaḥ* — "an influence; power".

> अध्यापकस्य प्रभावः छात्रेषु महान् अस्ति।
>
> *adhyāpakasya prabhāvaḥ chātreṣu mahān asti.*
>
> The teacher's influence on the students is great.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रभावः** (*prabhāvaḥ*) is built on *pra*, "forth", and the root √bhū, "to be, to become"; MW gives *pra-bhāva* the senses "might, power, efficacy" (MW).
