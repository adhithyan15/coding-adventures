## You'll want to know: tīng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C53-SPEAK53-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tīng* — "to listen; to hear".

> *nǐ tīng wǒ shuō* — Listen to me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tīng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *theng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to listen'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
