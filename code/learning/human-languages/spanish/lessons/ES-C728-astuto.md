## You'll want to know: astuto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C728-SABIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**astuto** — "clever, cunning".

> El zorro es astuto.
>
> *The fox is cunning.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*astuto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *astūtus* "crafty" (EtymDB), built on *astus* "craft, cunning".

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
