## You'll want to know: gàosu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C214-HELP214-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gàosu* — "to tell".

> *qǐng gàosu wǒ nǐ de hàomǎ* — Please tell me your number.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gàosu* is built from *gào* 'to report' and *su* 'to tell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kawH* and *suH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); in the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
