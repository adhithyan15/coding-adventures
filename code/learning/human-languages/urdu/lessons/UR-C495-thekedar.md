## You'll want to know: ٹھیکیدار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C495-AJRAT495-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھیکیدار** — *ṭhekedār* — "a contractor".

> ٹھیکیدار نے مزدوروں کو جلدی بلایا۔
>
> *ṭhekedār ne mazdūroṅ ko jaldī bulāyā.*
>
> The contractor called the labourers early.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھیکیدار** (*ṭhekedār*) is built from *ṭhekā*, "a contract for piecework", which CDIAL lists under *\*ṭhīkka*, "firm, right" (CDIAL 5503), and the ending *-dār*, usually traced to Persian *-dār*, "holding".
