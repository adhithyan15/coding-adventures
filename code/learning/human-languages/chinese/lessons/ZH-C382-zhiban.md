## You'll want to know: zhíbān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C382-GUARD382-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíbān* — "to be on duty".

> *jīntiān wǎnshang wǒ zhíbān* — I'm on duty tonight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíbān* is built from *zhí* 'to meet, to hold' and *bān* 'a turn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *driH* and *paen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hold' and 'to meet with' and the second 'to hand out tokens'. The sense 'a shift' is not given there.
