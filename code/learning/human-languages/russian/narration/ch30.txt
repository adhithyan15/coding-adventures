Russian, chapter 30: Hair, Finger, Neck, Shoulder, Knee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
волосы (vólosy) — hair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the belly, then the Russian for a tooth.

You'll want to know: волосы.
волосы (vólosy) — "hair". Plural, like English "locks".

What you've built.
One more word for hair, finger, neck, shoulder, knee.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vólosy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vólosy, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say vólosy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for the belly, then the Russian for a tooth, then say vólosy again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does волосы (vólosy) mean? ("Hair".) Say it once more.
