# ولادة (wilāda) — childbirth; a birth

## You'll want to know: ولادة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C311-WILADA311-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ولادة** — *wilāda* — "childbirth; a birth".

> كانت الولادة في الليل.
>
> *kānat al-wilāda fī al-layl.*
>
> The birth was at night.

Birth certificate: شهادة ولادة; many countries say شهادة ميلاد.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ولادة** (*wilāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-l-d* (Lane), "to give birth", the root of the taught *walad*, a child, and *mīlād*, a birthday.
