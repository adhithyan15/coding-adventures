# افسردہ (afsurdā) — dejected; downcast

## You'll want to know: افسردہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C443-SAD443-05]; assesses=[] -->

**افسردہ** — *afsurdā* — "dejected; downcast".

> بارش کے دن وہ افسردہ رہتی ہے۔
>
> *bārish ke din voh afsurdā rahtī hai.*
>
> On rainy days she feels low.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افسردہ** (*afsurdā*) is usually traced to Persian *afsurda*, "frozen, withered": a downcast person is pictured as withered.
