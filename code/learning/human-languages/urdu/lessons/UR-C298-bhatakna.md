## You'll want to know: بھٹکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C298-BICHHAR298-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بھٹکنا** — *bhaṭaknā* — "to wander; to lose one's way".

> وہ رات بھر جنگل میں بھٹکا۔
>
> *voh rāt bhar jangal meṅ bhaṭkā.*
>
> He wandered lost in the forest all night.

Past (she …): وہ بھٹکی, *voh bhaṭkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھٹکنا** (*bhaṭaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bhaṭaknā*, "to wander", under Sanskrit *bhraṣṭá*, "fallen" (CDIAL 9655).
