## You'll want to know: zhàopiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C109-SCREEN109-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhàopiàn* — "photo; photograph".

> *zhè shì wǒ jiārén de zhàopiàn* — This is a photo of my family.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhàopiàn* is built from zhào and piàn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyewH* and *phenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'bright' and the second as a piece of split wood. The photo is a modern sense of the pair.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
