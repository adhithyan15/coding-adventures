Kannada, chapter 394: Politics and Leaders.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ರಾಜಕೀಯ (rājakīya) — politics; political.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for democracy, then the Kannada for to represent.

You'll want to know: ರಾಜಕೀಯ.
ರಾಜಕೀಯ — rājakīya — "politics; political".
ನನ್ನ ಅಜ್ಜನಿಗೆ ರಾಜಕೀಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಆಸಕ್ತಿ ಇಲ್ಲ. nanna ajjanige rājakīyadalli āsakti illa. My grandfather has no interest in politics.
As an adjective: ರಾಜಕೀಯ (rājakīya) ಸುದ್ದಿ, political news.

The word, taken apart.
ರಾಜಕೀಯ (rājakīya) is borrowed from Sanskrit rājakīya, "belonging to a king, royal" (Monier-Williams); Kannada uses it for politics.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ (rājakāraṇi) — a politician.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to represent, then the Kannada for politics.

You'll want to know: ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ.
ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ — rājakāraṇi — "a politician".
ಆ ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳಿಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಜನರ ಜೊತೆ ಮಾತಾಡಿದನು. ā rājakāraṇi haḷḷigaḷige hōgi janara jote mātāḍidanu. That politician went to the villages and talked with people.
Plural: ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿಗಳು, rājakāraṇigaḷu.
ರಾಜಕಾರಣ, politics, + -ಇ, the one who does it.

The word, taken apart.
ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ (rājakāraṇi) is a modern Kannada word built on rājakāraṇa, "politics", from Sanskrit rāja, "king", and kāraṇa, "cause, means" (CDIAL 3057).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಬಹಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು (bahiṣkarisu) — to boycott.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a politician, then the Kannada for a political party.

You'll want to know: ಬಹಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು.
ಬಹಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು — bahiṣkarisu — "to boycott".
ಕೆಲಸಗಾರರು ಸಭೆಯನ್ನು ಬಹಿಷ್ಕರಿಸಿದರು. kelasagāraru sabheyannu bahiṣkarisidaru. The workers boycotted the meeting.
Past (I …): ಬಹಿಷ್ಕರಿಸಿದೆ, bahiṣkariside.
ಬಹಿಷ್ಕಾರ, a boycott.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಹಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು (bahiṣkarisu) is built on Sanskrit bahiṣkāra, "expulsion, removal" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ನಾಯಕತ್ವ (nāyakatva) — leadership.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a political party, then the Kannada for to boycott.

You'll want to know: ನಾಯಕತ್ವ.
ನಾಯಕತ್ವ — nāyakatva — "leadership".
ಅವಳ ನಾಯಕತ್ವದಲ್ಲಿ ತಂಡ ಗೆದ್ದಿತು. avaḷa nāyakatvadalli taṇḍa gedditu. Under her leadership the team won.
Built on the Sanskrit word for a leader, with -ತ್ವ, -ship.

The word, taken apart.
ನಾಯಕತ್ವ (nāyakatva) is built on Sanskrit nāyaka, "a guide, a leader" (Monier-Williams), with -tva, "-ship".
