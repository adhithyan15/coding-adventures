Bengali, chapter 478: At the Sickbed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
যত্ন (jôtnô) — care; looking after.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to limp, then the Bengali for to moan.

You'll want to know: যত্ন.
যত্ন — jôtnô — "care; looking after".
নার্সরা রোগীদের যত্ন নেন। nārsrā rogīder jôtnô nen. The nurses take care of the patients.
যত্ন (jôtnô) নেওয়া, to take care of; যত্ন (jôtnô) করে, carefully.

The word, taken apart.
যত্ন (jôtnô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit yatna, "effort" (CDIAL 10404a); Bengali uses it for the effort of care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for nursing, then the Bengali for a thermometer.

You'll want to know: জলপট্টি.
জলপট্টি — jôlpôṭṭi — "a cold compress (a wet cloth on the forehead)".
জ্বর কমাতে দিদিমা নাতির কপালে জলপট্টি দিলেন। jbôr kômāte didimā nātir kôpāle jôlpôṭṭi dilen. To bring the fever down, the grandmother laid a cold compress on her grandson's forehead.

The word, taken apart.
জলপট্টি (jôlpôṭṭi) puts জল (taught), water, with pôṭṭi, "a strip of cloth", probably from Sanskrit paṭṭa, "a slab" (CDIAL 7699), which Monier-Williams also gives for a bandage or strip of cloth.

Lesson 5 of 5.
শুশ্রূষা করা (shushrūshā kôrā) — to nurse (a sick person).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a thermometer, then the Bengali for a cold compress.

You'll want to know: শুশ্রূষা করা.
শুশ্রূষা করা — shushrūshā kôrā — "to nurse (a sick person)".
মীরা রাত জেগে মামার শুশ্রূষা করল। mīrā rāt jege māmār shushrūshā kôrlô. Mira stayed up through the night nursing her uncle.
Past (he/she …): তিনি রোগীর শুশ্রূষা করলেন, tini rogīr shushrūshā kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
শুশ্রূষা করা (shushrūshā kôrā) is Sanskrit śuśrūṣā, first "the wish to hear" and then "obedient service" (Monier-Williams), which Bengali uses for nursing the sick, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
