## You'll want to know: حقيبة ظهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C427-TAKHTIT427-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حقيبة ظهر** — *ḥaqībat ẓahr* — "a backpack".

> حمل أخي حقيبة ظهر كبيرة.
>
> *ḥamala akhī ḥaqībat ẓahr kabīra.*
>
> My brother carried a big backpack.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حقيبة ظهر** (*ḥaqībat ẓahr*) is built from two native Arabic words: *ḥaqība*, "a bag", on the root *ḥ-q-b* (Lane), and *ẓahr*, "the back", on the root *ẓ-h-r* (Lane): a bag for the back.
