## You'll want to know: ગોલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C549-GOAL549-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોલ** — *gol* — "a goal (in a game)".

> જો તું એક ગોલ કરીશ તો આપણે જીતી જઈશું.
>
> *jo tũ ek gol karīś to āpaṇe jītī jaīśũ.*
>
> If you score one goal, we'll win.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોલ** (*gol*) is borrowed from English *goal*. One "does" a goal: **ગોલ કરવો**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
