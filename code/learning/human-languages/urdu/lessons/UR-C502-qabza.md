## You'll want to know: قبضہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C502-ZANG502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قبضہ** — *qabzā* — "a hinge; possession (a hold on something)".

> دروازے کا ایک قبضہ ٹوٹ گیا۔
>
> *darvāze kā ek qabzā ṭūṭ gayā.*
>
> One of the door's hinges has broken.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قبضہ** (*qabzā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qabḍa*, "a grip, a handle"; Urdu also uses it for "possession", a hold on something, while the sense "a hinge" came later, and the sources we use do not show how it arose.
