Telugu, chapter 367: Digging Up the Past.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
శిథిలాలు (śithilālu) — ruins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a lord, then the Telugu for to occupy.

You'll want to know: శిథిలాలు.
శిథిలాలు — śithilālu — "ruins".
అడవిలో పాత కోట శిథిలాలు ఉన్నాయి. aḍavilō pāta kōṭa śithilālu unnāyi. There are ruins of an old fort in the forest.
A plural noun; శిథిల, loose or decayed, makes the adjective.

The word, taken apart.
శిథిలాలు (śithilālu) is built on Sanskrit śithila, "loose, slack" (Monier-Williams): what has come loose and fallen.

తవ్వకాలలో పాత నాణేలు దొరికాయి. tavvakālalō pāta nāṇēlu dorikāyi. Old coins were found in the excavations.
From తవ్వు, to dig.

The word, taken apart.
తవ్వకాలు (tavvakālu) is native Dravidian, the plural noun of tavvu, "to dig, excavate" (DEDR 3122).

Lesson 5 of 5.
వెలికితీయు (velikitīyu) — to unearth; to dig out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for excavations, then the Telugu for remains.

You'll want to know: వెలికితీయు.
వెలికితీయు — velikitīyu — "to unearth; to dig out".
శాస్త్రవేత్తలు ఒక పాత కుండను వెలికితీశారు. śāstravēttalu oka pāta kuṇḍanu velikitīśāru. The scientists unearthed an old pot.
Past (I …): వెలికితీశాను, velikitīśānu.
Literally to pull to the outside; also to bring a secret to light.

The word, taken apart.
వెలికితీయు (velikitīyu) is a native compound: veliki, "to the outside", from veli, "the outside" (DEDR 5498), and tīyu, "to draw, pull out".
