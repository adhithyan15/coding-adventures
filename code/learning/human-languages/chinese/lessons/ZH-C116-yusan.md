## You'll want to know: yǔsǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C116-CARRY116-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yǔsǎn* — "umbrella".

> *xiàyǔ le nǐ dài yǔsǎn le ma* — It's raining; have you got an umbrella?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones: yǔ rises before sǎn, and is still written yǔ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǔsǎn* is built from 雨 'rain' and sǎn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuX* and *sanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as a canopy.
