## You'll want to know: la carroza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C559-DESFILE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la carroza** — "the float (in a parade); the carriage".

> Los niños saludaban desde las carrozas.
>
> *The children waved from the floats.*

*La carroza de los Reyes Magos* — the Three Kings' float on 5 January.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carroza* was borrowed from Italian *carrozza* 'a carriage', built on *carro* 'a cart', which comes from Latin *carrus* (Lewis & Short).
