# Review — a story in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FIRST-THEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I went to the station and bought a ticket". (*āmi sṭeshône giye ṭikiṭ kinlām*.) Then "after that". (*tārpôr*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-SAME-DOER-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FIRST-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FINALLY-01, BN-LEX-C267-HOTHAT-01, BN-SCRIPT-C267-KHANDA-TA-01, BN-SKILL-C267-READ-STORY-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-SCENE-EVENT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-LEX-C84-TOWN84-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: First I went to the village by bus.]
- [YOU SAY: After that I got off the bus and walked to the river.]
- [YOU SAY: I was sitting by the river. Suddenly Mira came.]
- [YOU SAY: We ate and talked. In the end I came back home.]
- [YOU SAY: Mira's grandmother told a story. What happened in it?]
- [YOU WRITE: **হঠাৎ**, then **শেষে**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-SAME-DOER-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FIRST-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FINALLY-01, BN-LEX-C267-HOTHAT-01, BN-SCRIPT-C267-KHANDA-TA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **আসা** in the *-e* form? (**এসে**.) What opens, steps and closes a story? (**প্রথমে**, **তারপর**, **শেষে**.) Which letter ends **হঠাৎ**? (**ৎ**.)
