# નામકરણ (nāmkaraṇ) — a naming ceremony

## You'll want to know: નામકરણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C496-DHAVVU496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નામકરણ** — *nāmkaraṇ* — "a naming ceremony".

> નામકરણમાં ફોઈએ બાળકનું નામ નક્કી કર્યું.
>
> *nāmkaraṇmā̃ phoīe bāḷaknũ nām nakkī karyũ.*
>
> At the naming ceremony the baby's paternal aunt chose its name.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નામકરણ** (*nāmkaraṇ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *nāmakaraṇa*, "the giving of a name", as Monier-Williams records it.
