## You'll want to know: 为什么
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**为什么** — *wèishénme* — "why?".

> **你为什么不去** *nǐ wèishénme bú qù* — Why aren't you going?

The next lesson puts the new character in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**为什么** (*wèishénme*) is built from 为 'for' and 什么 'what': for what? 为 is inherited from Middle Chinese *hjweH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to help'; 'for' is the sense of acting on somebody's behalf, which the gloss does not spell out. Where 什么 comes from is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-SHENME, ZH-TONE-SANDHI-BU] -->

为什么 sits after the person and before the verb: 你, 为什么, 不去. 不 before the fall of 去 rises: *bú qù*.
