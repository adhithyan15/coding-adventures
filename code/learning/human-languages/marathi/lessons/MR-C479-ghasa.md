## You'll want to know: घसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C479-NADI479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घसा** — *ghasā* — "the gullet; the throat".

> थंड पाणी प्यायल्यामुळे माझा घसा बसला.
>
> *thaṇḍ pāṇī pyāylyāmuḷe mājhā ghasā baslā.*
>
> Drinking cold water made me hoarse.

Masculine.

घसा बसणे, to go hoarse; beside गळा, the neck and throat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घसा** (*ghasā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
