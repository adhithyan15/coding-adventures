## You'll want to know: ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C321-COLD321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ** — *zukām* — "a head cold".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਮੈਂ ਅੱਜ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *mainū̃ zukām hai ate maiṁ ajj ghar vich hāṁ.*
>
> I have a cold, and I am at home today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ** (*zukām*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *zukām*, "a cold, catarrh".
