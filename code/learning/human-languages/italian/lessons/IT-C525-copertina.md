## You'll want to know: la copertina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C525-LIBRERIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la copertina** — "cover (of a book)".

> Mi piace la copertina di questo libro.
>
> *I like the cover of this book.*

Built on *coperta* "blanket, cover".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*copertina* is a little *coperta* "cover", from *coprire* "to cover", inherited from Latin *cooperīre* "to cover over" (Lewis & Short).
