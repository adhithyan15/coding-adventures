Italian, chapter 187: Still, Then, During, While, Decade.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ancora — still, again.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for friendship, then the Italian for hope.

You'll want to know: ancora.
ancora — "still, again".

The word, taken apart.
ancora goes back to Latin hanc horam, "(at) this hour". EtymDB 2.1 has no row for its origin; it lists French encore as its cognate.

Lesson 3 of 5.
durante — during.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for still, then the Italian for then.

You'll want to know: durante.
durante — "during".

The word, taken apart.
durante is the present participle of durare, "to last": while (it is) lasting. durare from Latin durare. (EtymDB 2.1).
