## You'll want to know: la mostra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C523-MOSTRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la mostra** — "exhibition, show".

> Domenica andiamo a vedere una mostra di fotografia.
>
> *On Sunday we are going to see a photography exhibition.*

The same form is a verb: *mi mostra la casa* — he shows me the house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mostra* is built on *mostrare* "to show", inherited from Latin *mōnstrāre* "to point out" (EtymDB).
