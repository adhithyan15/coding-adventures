## You'll want to know: ખંડન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C342-VIVAD342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખંડન** — *khanḍan* — "a refutation".

> અરુણે મીરાની દલીલનું ખંડન કર્યું.
>
> *Aruṇe Mīrānī dalīlnũ khanḍan karyũ.*
>
> Arun refuted Mira's argument.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખંડન** (*khanḍan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *khaṇḍana*, "breaking, cutting to pieces" (Monier-Williams): an argument broken down.
