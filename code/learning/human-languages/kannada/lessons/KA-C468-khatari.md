## You'll want to know: ಖಾತರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C468-GATRA468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖಾತರಿ** — *khātari* — "a guarantee; an assurance".

> ಈ ಫ್ಯಾನಿಗೆ ಎರಡು ವರ್ಷದ ಖಾತರಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ī phyānige eraḍu varṣada khātari ide.*
>
> This fan has a two-year guarantee.

ಖಾತರಿ ಕೊಡು, to give an assurance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖಾತರಿ** (*khātari*) came into Kannada through Hindustani; it is usually traced to Arabic *khāṭir*, "the mind, regard", but how it came to mean an assurance is not settled.
