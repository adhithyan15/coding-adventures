## You'll want to know: ਫਸਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C283-ROAD283-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫਸਣਾ** — *phasṇā* — "to get stuck; to be caught".

> ਬਰਸਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੱਡੀਆਂ ਰਸਤੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਫਸਦੀਆਂ ਹਨ।
>
> *barsāt vich gaḍḍīāṁ raste vich phasdīāṁ han.*
>
> In the rainy season, cars get stuck on the road.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਰਸਤੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਫਸਿਆ, *maiṁ raste vich phasiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫਸਣਾ** (*phasṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*spaśyatē*, "is bound"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *phasṇā*, "to be ensnared", under it (CDIAL 13812).
