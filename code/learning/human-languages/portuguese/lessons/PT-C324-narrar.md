## You'll want to know: narrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C324-CONTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**narrar** — "to narrate, to tell".

> O livro narra a vida de um pescador.
>
> *The book tells the life of a fisherman.*

More formal than *contar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narrar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nārrāre* "to tell, to relate" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
