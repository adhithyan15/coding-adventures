## You'll want to know: स्पृहयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C549-LALASA549-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पृहयति** — *spṛhayati* — "is eager for; longs for".

> यदि भवान् ज्ञानाय स्पृहयति, तर्हि प्रतिदिनं पठतु।
>
> *yadi bhavān jñānāya spṛhayati, tarhi pratidinaṃ paṭhatu.*
>
> If you long for knowledge, read every day.

Past (he …): सः ज्ञानाय स्पृहितवान्, *saḥ jñānāya spṛhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पृहयति** (*spṛhayati*) comes from the root √spṛh, "to long for, to desire", which takes the dative; MW compares Greek *sperchō* and English *spring* (MW).
