## You'll want to know: इनकार करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C349-INKAR349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इनकार करना** — *inkār karnā* — "to deny, to refuse".

> नेता ने हर सवाल का जवाब देने से इनकार किया।
>
> *netā ne har savāl kā javāb dene se inkār kiyā.*
>
> The politician refused to answer any question.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने इनकार किया, *usne inkār kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इनकार करना** (*inkār karnā*) joins इनकार to करना, "to do". इनकार is usually traced to Arabic *inkār*, "denial", which reached Hindi through Persian.
