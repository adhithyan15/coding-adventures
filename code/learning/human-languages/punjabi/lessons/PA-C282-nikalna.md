# ਨਿਕਲਣਾ (nikalṇā) — to come out; to head out

## You'll want to know: ਨਿਕਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C282-BAGS282-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਕਲਣਾ** — *nikalṇā* — "to come out; to head out".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਜੇ ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਨਿਕਲਾਂਗੇ।
>
> *asīṁ savere chhe vaje ghar ton niklāṅge.*
>
> We will head out from home at six in the morning.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਨਿਕਲਿਆ, *maiṁ ghar ton nikliā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਕਲਣਾ** (*nikalṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*niṣkalati*, "comes out"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nikkalṇā* under it (CDIAL 7478).
