Spanish, chapter 520: El delito.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el delito — the offence, the crime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the patrol, then the Spanish for to chase.

You'll want to know: el delito.
el delito — "the offence, the crime".
Robar es un delito. Stealing is a crime.
Cometer un delito — to commit an offence.

The word, taken apart.
delito is a learned borrowing from Latin dēlictum 'a fault, a misdeed' (EtymDB), from dēlinquere 'to fall short, to do wrong' (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: el delincuente.
el delincuente — "the offender, the criminal".
Detuvieron al delincuente en la estación. They arrested the offender at the station.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la víctima — the victim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the crime, then the Spanish for the offender.

You'll want to know: la víctima.
la víctima — "the victim".
La víctima era un hombre de cuarenta años. The victim was a man of forty.
Always feminine, even for a man: la víctima.

The word, taken apart.
víctima is a learned borrowing from Latin victima 'an animal offered in sacrifice' (EtymDB). The word moved from the altar to anyone who suffers harm.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la investigación — the investigation; research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the offender, then the Spanish for the victim.

You'll want to know: la investigación.
la investigación — "the investigation; research".
La investigación duró dos años. The investigation lasted two years.
It also means scientific research: la investigación médica.

The word, taken apart.
investigación is a learned borrowing from Latin investīgātiō 'a searching out', from investīgāre 'to track, as a dog does' (Lewis & Short), built on vestīgium 'a footprint'.
