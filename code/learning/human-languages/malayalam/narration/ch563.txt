Malayalam, chapter 563: On Stage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കഥാപാത്രം (kathāpātraṁ) — a story character.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a screenplay, then the Malayalam for a novel.

You'll want to know: കഥാപാത്രം.
കഥാപാത്രം — kathāpātraṁ — "a story character".
നോവലിലെ പ്രധാന കഥാപാത്രം ഒരു കർഷകനാണ്. nōvalile pradhāna kathāpātraṁ oru karṣakanāṇŭ. The main character of the novel is a farmer.
കഥ a story + പാത്രം a vessel: the vessel that carries the story.

The word, taken apart.
കഥാപാത്രം (kathāpātraṁ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit kathā, "a story" (CDIAL 2704), and pātra, "a vessel, a dish" (CDIAL 8055).

Lesson 3 of 5.
വേദി (vēdi) — a stage; a venue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a story character, then the Malayalam for a stage scene.

You'll want to know: വേദി.
വേദി — vēdi — "a stage; a venue".
ഗായകൻ വേദിയിൽ കയറിയപ്പോൾ എല്ലാവരും എഴുന്നേറ്റു. gāyakan vēdiyil kayaṟiyappōḷ ellāvaruṁ eḻunnēṟṟu. When the singer came on stage, everyone stood up.
Any stage or meeting place: സമ്മേളന വേദി (vēdi), a conference venue.

The word, taken apart.
വേദി (vēdi) is borrowed from Sanskrit vedi, the raised ground prepared for a rite (Monier-Williams); a raised place for a rite became a raised place for a show.

[pause 2 seconds]

കാണി — kāṇi — "an onlooker; a spectator".
വഴിയിൽ നടന്ന വഴക്ക് കാണാൻ ഒരുപാട് കാണികൾ കൂടി. vaḻiyil naṭanna vaḻakkŭ kāṇān orupāṭŭ kāṇikaḷ kūṭi. Lots of onlookers gathered to watch the quarrel in the street.
Plural: കാണികൾ, kāṇikaḷ.
Plural: കാണികൾ, the spectators at a match or a show.

The word, taken apart.
കാണി (kāṇi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1443 lists it with കാണുക, "to see", as a visitor or spectator (DEDR 1443).

Lesson 5 of 5.
കയ്യടി (kayyaṭi) — applause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a stage, then the Malayalam for an onlooker.

You'll want to know: കയ്യടി.
കയ്യടി — kayyaṭi — "applause".
പാട്ട് കഴിഞ്ഞപ്പോൾ വലിയ കയ്യടി ഉണ്ടായി. pāṭṭŭ kaḻiññappōḷ valiya kayyaṭi uṇṭāyi. There was loud applause when the song ended.
കൈ hand + അടി a stroke; കയ്യടിക്കുക is to applaud.

The word, taken apart.
കയ്യടി (kayyaṭi) is native Dravidian, a compound of കൈ, "hand" (DEDR 2023), and അടി, "a blow, a stroke" (DEDR 77).
