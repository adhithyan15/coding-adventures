## You'll want to know: proibido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C382-PROIBIDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**proibido** — "forbidden, not allowed".

> É proibido fumar no restaurante.
>
> *Smoking is not allowed in the restaurant.*

The participle of *proibir*. *Entrada proibida* — no entry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*proibido* is the participle of *proibir*, from Latin *prohibēre* "to hold back, to forbid", built on *habēre* "to have, to hold" (Lewis & Short).
