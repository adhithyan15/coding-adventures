## You'll want to know: culpado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C390-CULPA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**culpado** — "guilty; to blame".

> O juiz considerou-o culpado.
>
> *The judge found him guilty.*

*Considerar culpado* — to find guilty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*culpado* is the participle of *culpar* "to blame", built on *culpa* "fault", from Latin *culpa* (Lewis & Short).
