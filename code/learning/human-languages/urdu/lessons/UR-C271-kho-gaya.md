# کھو گیا (kho gayā) — when things go wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C271-CHHUTNA-01, UR-LEX-C271-HADSA-01, UR-LEX-C52-VERBS52-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I missed my train" and "there was an accident". (*merī gāṛī chhūṭ gaī*, *ek hādsā huā*.) Then "to lose". (*khonā*.)

## You'll want to know: it got lost
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C111-THINGS111-03, UR-LEX-C52-VERBS52-05, UR-LEX-C61-HOME61-02, UR-LEX-MERA-NAAM-HAI] -->

> **تھیلا کھو گیا۔**
>
> *thailā kho gayā.* — "The bag got lost."

> **میرا تھیلا کھو گیا۔**
>
> *merā thailā kho gayā.* — "My bag got lost."

> **میری کتاب کھو گئی۔**
>
> *merī kitāb kho gaī.* — "My book got lost."

The lost thing is the subject, as in **گاڑی چھوٹ گئی**: **گیا** for the masculine **تھیلا** (*thailā*), **گئی** for **کتاب**. The stem with **گیا** says it is gone, and no **نے** comes in.

## You'll want to know: telling someone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C81-TOWN81-03, UR-LEX-C52-VERBS52-04, UR-LEX-C100-LOC100-05, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-02] -->

> **میں نے پولیس کو بتایا۔**
>
> *maiṅ ne polīs ko batāyā.* — "I told the police."

> **میں تھانے گیا۔**
>
> *maiṅ thāne gayā.* — "I went to the police station."

> **جب حادثہ ہوا،**
>
> *jab hādsā huā,*
>
> **تو ہم سڑک پر تھے۔**
>
> *to ham saṛak par the.* — "When the accident happened, we were on the road."
