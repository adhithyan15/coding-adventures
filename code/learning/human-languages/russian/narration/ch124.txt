Russian, chapter 124: Purple, Brown, Grey, Orange, Busy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
фиолетовый (fiolétovyy) — purple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for stairs, then the Russian for pink.

You'll want to know: фиолетовый.
фиолетовый (fiolétovyy) — "purple".

The word, taken apart.
фиолетовый (fiolétovyy) — its ф- shows it came through German violett from French violet, from violette, Latin viola, "the violet" (Vasmer).

Lesson 3 of 5.
серый (séryy) — grey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for purple, then the Russian for brown.

You'll want to know: серый.
серый (séryy) — "grey".

The word, taken apart.
серый (séryy) from Proto-Slavic sěrŭ, from xoiro- — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Old Norse hárr, "grey, hoary", and Old English hār, so English hoar is a cousin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
оранжевый (oránzhevyy) — orange (the colour).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for brown, then the Russian for grey.

You'll want to know: оранжевый.
оранжевый (oránzhevyy) — "orange (the colour)".

The word, taken apart.
оранжевый (oránzhevyy) is a late borrowing from French orange, "an orange", perhaps through German; the source is Persian and Arabic nāranj, whose home is sought in India, Sanskrit nāraṅga- (Vasmer).

Lesson 5 of 5.
занятый (zányatyy) — busy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for grey, then the Russian for orange.

You'll want to know: занятый.
занятый (zányatyy) — "busy".

The word, taken apart.
занятый (zányatyy) is "taken up": за- with -нятый, from -нять, the old Slavic verb jęti, "to take", as in взять, "to take", and понимать, "to understand" (Vasmer).
