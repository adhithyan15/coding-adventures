## You'll want to know: ઉદ્યમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C469-TARANGI469-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્યમ** — *udyam* — "industry; steady effort".

> ઉદ્યમથી ગરીબ માણસ પણ અમીર બને છે.
>
> *udyamthī garīb māṇas paṇ amīr bane che.*
>
> Through steady effort even a poor man becomes rich.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્યમ** (*udyam*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *udyama*, "lifting up" and "undertaking, exertion", as Monier-Williams records it.
