## You'll want to know: gǎnjí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C440-HILLS440-01]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnjí* — "to go to market day; to go to a country market".

> *nǎinai měi ge xīngqī qù gǎnjí* — Grandma goes to market day every week.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnjí* is built from *gǎn* 'to hurry to' and *jí* 'a gathering'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to gather'; no dictionary we use records an older form of *gǎn* in this sense, so its history is not settled.
