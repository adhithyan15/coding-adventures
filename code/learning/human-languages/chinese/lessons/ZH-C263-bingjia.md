## You'll want to know: 病假
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C263-LEAVE263-04]; assesses=[] -->

**病假** — *bìngjià* — "sick leave".

> **他今天请了病假** *tā jīntiān qǐngle bìngjià* — He's off sick today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**病假** (*bìngjià*) is built from 病 'illness' and 假 'leave', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjaengH* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'grief' and quotes an older dictionary for 'an illness getting worse'.
