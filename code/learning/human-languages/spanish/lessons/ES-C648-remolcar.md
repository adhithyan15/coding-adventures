## You'll want to know: remolcar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C648-APARCAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**remolcar** — "to tow".

> La grúa remolca el coche hasta el taller.
>
> *The tow truck tows the car to the garage.*

The c becomes qu before e: *remolqué*. *El remolque* is a trailer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remolcar* comes from a Vulgar Latin verb (EtymDB) built on Latin *remulcum* "a tow-rope" (Lewis & Short), a word of seafaring that the Romans took from Greek.
