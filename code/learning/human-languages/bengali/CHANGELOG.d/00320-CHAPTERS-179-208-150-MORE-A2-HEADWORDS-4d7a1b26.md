## Chapters 179-208: 150 more A2 headwords

The second of four Bengali A2 vocabulary tranches. It has thirty chapters of
five words each, in four runs, and each run closes with two reviews. With its
thirty verbs, Bengali now teaches the 120 verb headwords that A2 asks for.

- **Verbs (30):** eating (*চাখা*, *গেলা*), earning and looking after
  (*রোজগার করা*), printing and study (*ছাপা*, *মুখস্থ করা*), paper and
  cloth (*ছেঁড়া*, *ভাঁজ করা*), the start of the day (*হাই তোলা*, *স্নান
  করা*) and agreement (*মেনে নেওয়া*, *ঝুঁকি নেওয়া*).
- **Nouns and qualities (120):** first aid (*বড়ি*, *ফোসকা*), the body
  (*মগজ*), long stretches of time (*শতাব্দী*, *সপ্তাহান্ত*), institutions
  (*বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়*, *আদালত*), landscape (*ঝরনা*), sentence adverbs
  (*হয়তো*, *দুর্ভাগ্যবশত*), age and size (*আধুনিক*, *প্রাচীন*, *জটিল*,
  *বিশাল*), the kitchen (*কেটলি*, *কুকার*), rainwear (*রেনকোট*), rice
  dishes (*বিরিয়ানি*, *খিচুড়ি*), games (*দাবা*), flying (*বিমান*,
  *প্ল্যাটফর্ম*), citizenship (*নাগরিক*, *যুদ্ধ*, *স্বাধীনতা*), the
  bathroom (*বেসিন*), crime and voting (*চোর*, *ভোট*), character
  (*স্বভাব*, *সম্মান*), fruit (*পেয়ারা*), maps (*ম্যাপ*), the shop
  (*খদ্দের*, *কোম্পানি*) and the sea (*নাবিক*).
- The language review's corrections in this tranche: *khôdder* (the closed
  e, not *æ*).

  Tamil, German, Sanskrit, Italian and French preambles already do. Without
