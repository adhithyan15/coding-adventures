## You'll want to know: أينما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C400-KULLAMA400-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أينما** — *aynamā* — "wherever".

> أينما ذهبت، وجدت ناسا طيبين.
>
> *aynamā dhahabtu, wajadtu nāsan ṭayyibīn.*
>
> Wherever I went, I found kind people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أينما** (*aynamā*) joins the native Arabic *ayna*, "where", with *mā*: wherever. The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.
