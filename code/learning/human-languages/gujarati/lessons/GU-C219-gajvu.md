## You'll want to know: ગાજવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C219-THUNDER219-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાજવું** — *gājvũ* — "to thunder, to rumble".

> **રાત્રે વાદળ ગાજશે.** — *rātre vādaḷ gājśe* — "The clouds will thunder at night."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાજવું** (*gājvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *garjati*, "thunders, roars" (CDIAL 4046).
