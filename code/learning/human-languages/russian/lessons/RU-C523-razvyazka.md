## You'll want to know: развязка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C523-PLOT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**развязка** (*razvyázka*) — "an ending, a denouement".

> Развязка фильма меня удивила.
>
> *Razvyázka fílma menyá udivíla.*
>
> The ending of the film surprised me.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*развязка* is built on *развязать* 'to untie', from *вязать* 'to tie', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *vęzati* (EtymDB): the ending unties the knot of the story.
