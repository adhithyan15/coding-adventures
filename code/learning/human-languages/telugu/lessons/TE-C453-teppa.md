## You'll want to know: తెప్ప
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C453-TEPPA453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తెప్ప** — *teppa* — "a raft".

> వాళ్ళు తెప్ప మీద వాగు దాటారు.
>
> *vāḷḷu teppa mīda vāgu dāṭāru.*
>
> They crossed the stream on a raft.

A raft of logs or bamboo tied together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తెప్ప** (*teppa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3414 lists Telugu *teppa*, "a raft, a catamaran", with Tamil *teppam*.
