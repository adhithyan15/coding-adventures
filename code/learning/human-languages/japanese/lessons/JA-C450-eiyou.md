## You'll want to know: えいよう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C450-CLINIC450-03]; assesses=[] -->

**えいよう** — *eiyō* — "nutrition; nourishment".

> **やさいは えいようが あります。** — *yasai wa eiyō ga arimasu* — "Vegetables are nutritious."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えいよう** (*eiyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ei*, "flourish" (Middle Chinese *hjwaeng*) and *yō*, "nurture" (*yangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
