## You'll want to know: le butin
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C287-CAMBRIOLAGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le butin** — "the loot, the haul".

> Le butin était caché dans une valise.
>
> *The loot was hidden in a suitcase.*

Also the haul of a happy day: *le butin des soldes*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*butin* comes from Old French *butin* "booty" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Middle Low German *būte* "exchange, a share of booty".
