## You'll want to know: der Uhrmacher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C447-FRISEUR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Uhrmacher** — "the watchmaker, the clockmaker".

> Der Uhrmacher wechselt die Batterie in meiner Uhr.
>
> *The watchmaker changes the battery in my watch.*

Same form in the plural. A woman: *die Uhrmacherin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Uhrmacher* is built from *Uhr* 'clock', which goes back to Latin *hōra* 'hour' (EtymDB), and *Macher* 'maker', from *machen* 'to make' (EtymDB).
