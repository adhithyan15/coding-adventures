# മറയുക (maṟayuka) — to vanish; to go out of sight

## You'll want to know: മറയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C383-RAHASYAM383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**മറയുക** — *maṟayuka* — "to vanish; to go out of sight".

> സൂര്യൻ കുന്നിന്റെ പിന്നിൽ മറഞ്ഞു.
>
> *sūryan kunninṟe pinnil maṟaññu.*
>
> The sun vanished behind the hill.

Past: മറഞ്ഞു, *maṟaññu*.

To go behind something and out of sight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മറയുക** (*maṟayuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4760 lists Malayalam *maṟayuka*, "to disappear, to hide oneself", with മറ, "a screen, a cover".
