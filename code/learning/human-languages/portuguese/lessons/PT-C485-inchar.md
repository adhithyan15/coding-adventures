## You'll want to know: inchar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C485-ARRANHAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**inchar** — "to swell".

> O tornozelo inchou depois da queda.
>
> *The ankle swelled after the fall.*

Regular: *incha*, *inchou*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inchar* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *enchar*, from Latin *inflāre* "to blow into, to puff up" (EtymDB).
