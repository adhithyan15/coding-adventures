# مربية (murabbiya) — a nanny

## You'll want to know: مربية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C531-KIBAR531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مربية** — *murabbiya* — "a nanny".

> تعتني المربية بالأطفال في النهار.
>
> *taʿtanī al-murabbiya bi-l-aṭfāl fī an-nahār.*
>
> The nanny looks after the children during the day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مربية** (*murabbiya*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-b-w*, "to grow, to increase" (Lane). Murabbiya is the feminine participle of rabbā, to bring up.
