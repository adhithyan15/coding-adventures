# پسوانا (pisvānā) — to have (something) ground

## You'll want to know: پسوانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C510-CHHALNI510-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پسوانا** — *pisvānā* — "to have (something) ground".

> امی نے گندم کا آٹا پسوایا۔
>
> *ammī ne gandum kā āṭā pisvāyā.*
>
> Mum had the wheat ground into flour.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مسالہ پسوایا, *us ne masālā pisvāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پسوانا** (*pisvānā*) is the second causative of *pīsnā*, "to grind", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *piṃṣati*, "grinds" (CDIAL 8142): to have someone grind.
