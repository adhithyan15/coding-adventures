Japanese, chapter 481: A Skilled Hand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
うでまえ (udemae) — skill; ability.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for time and effort, then the Japanese for to finish off.

You'll want to know: うでまえ.
うでまえ — udemae — "skill; ability".
あの だいくは うでまえが いいです。 — ano daiku wa udemae ga ii desu — "That carpenter is very skilled."

The word, taken apart.
うでまえ (udemae) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from ude, "arm" and mae, "front" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ぶきよう (bukiyō) — clumsy; awkward (with one's hands).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to finish off, then the Japanese for skill.

You'll want to know: ぶきよう.
ぶきよう — bukiyō — "clumsy; awkward (with one's hands)".
あには てが ぶきようです。 — ani wa te ga bukiyō desu — "My elder brother is clumsy with his hands."
A na-adjective: な before a noun.

The word, taken apart.
ぶきよう (bukiyō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are bu, "negative", ki, "utensil" (Middle Chinese khijH) and yō, "utilize" (yowngH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
くふうする (kufū suru) — to devise; to find a better way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for technology, then the Japanese for wisdom.

You'll want to know: くふうする.
くふうする — kufū suru — "to devise; to find a better way".
ははは りょうりを くふうします。 — haha wa ryōri o kufūshimasu — "My mother keeps finding better ways to cook."
Polite くふうします, kufūshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): ははが りょうりを くふうしました。, haha ga ryōri o kufūshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
くふうする (kufū suru) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun kufū with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ku, "craft" (Middle Chinese kuwng) and fū, "man" (pju) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
