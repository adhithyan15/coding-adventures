Persian, chapter 430: Departures and delays.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مقصد (maqsad) — a destination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a compartment, then the Persian for a rail.

You'll want to know: مقصد.
مقصد (maqsad) — "a destination".
مقصد (maqsad) ما یک روستای کوچک است. Maqsad-e mâ yek rustâ-ye kuchek ast. Our destination is a small village.
Arabic plural maqâsed. The starting point is mabda'.

The word, taken apart.
maqsad was borrowed from Arabic maqṣad, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'the place one aims at', and it shares its root with qasd 'intention'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
لغو شدن (laghv shodan) — to be cancelled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a destination, then the Persian for a delay.

You'll want to know: لغو شدن.
لغو شدن (laghv shodan) — "to be cancelled".
قطار امشب لغو شد. Qatâr-e emshab laghv shod. Tonight's train was cancelled.
laghv 'cancellation' + shodan. The active verb is laghv kardan — to cancel.

The word, taken apart.
laghv shodan is built from laghv 'annulment', a loan usually traced to Arabic laġw 'idle talk, something of no effect' (EtymDB lists the Arabic word without a gloss), and the native verb shodan 'to become'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دیر کردن (dir kardan) — to be late, to run late.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a delay, then the Persian for to be cancelled.

You'll want to know: دیر کردن.
دیر کردن (dir kardan) — "to be late, to run late".
اتوبوس امروز نیم ساعت دیر کرد. Otobus emruz nim sâ'at dir kard. Today the bus was half an hour late.
dir 'late' + kardan. Present stem kon-: dir mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
dir kardan is built from native dir 'late, long', usually traced to Middle Persian dēr, and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
بلندگو (bolandgu) — a loudspeaker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be cancelled, then the Persian for to be late.

You'll want to know: بلندگو.
بلندگو (bolandgu) — "a loudspeaker".
از بلندگو اسم ایستگاه بعدی را گفتند. Az bolandgu esm-e istgâh-e ba'di râ goftand. They announced the name of the next station over the loudspeaker.
boland 'loud, high' + gu 'saying', from goftan (present stem gu-).

The word, taken apart.
bolandgu is a native compound of inherited words: boland 'high, loud', usually traced to Middle Persian buland, and gu, the present stem of goftan 'to say': a thing that speaks loudly.
