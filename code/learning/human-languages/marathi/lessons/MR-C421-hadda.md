## You'll want to know: हद्द
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C421-BANDHKAM421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हद्द** — *hadda* — "a boundary; a limit".

> नदी ही दोन जिल्ह्यांची हद्द आहे.
>
> *nadī hī don jilhyāñcī hadda āhe.*
>
> The river is the boundary between two districts.

Feminine.

Also a limit of patience: हद्द झाली, that is the limit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हद्द** (*hadda*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *ḥadd*, "a limit, a border".
