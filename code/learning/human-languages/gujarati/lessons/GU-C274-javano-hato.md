# હું જવાનો હતો (hũ javāno hato) — was going to

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C150-VAANO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C274-NEGATIVE-FUTURE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'm going to go tomorrow", as a plan already made. (*hũ kāle javāno chũ*.) Then "I won't go". (*hũ nahī̃ jāũ*.)

## Grammar Lens: છું becomes હતો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C274-WAS-GOING-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-BAJAR-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01, GU-LEX-JAVU, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATU-HATA-01] -->

> **હું ગઈકાલે બજાર જવાનો હતો.**
>
> *hũ gaīkāle bajār javāno hato.* — "I was going to go to the market yesterday."

Put **હતો** where **છું** stood, and the plan moves into the past. Both halves agree, as **જવાનો છું** did: a woman says **જવાની હતી**, a group **જવાના હતા**.

With a verb that has an object, the doer stays plain: **હું પત્ર લખવાનો હતો** (*hũ patra lakhvāno hato*), not **મેં**. It is a plan, not a past of **લખવું**.

## You'll want to know: ..., પણ ...
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C274-WAS-GOING-BUT-01]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-PAN-01, GU-LEX-VARSAAD-01, GU-LEX-C214-RAIN214-01, GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-02, GU-LEX-C147-PAST147-01] -->

> **અમે જવાના હતા, પણ વરસાદ પડ્યો.**
>
> *ame javānā hatā, paṇ varsād paḍyo.* — "We were going to go, but it rained."

A plan in the past often did not happen. **પણ** names what got in the way: **મીરા આવવાની હતી, પણ ટ્રેન મોડી હતી** (*Mīrā āvvānī hatī, paṇ ṭren moḍī hatī*), "Mira was going to come, but the train was late."
