## You'll want to know: hóuzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C223-ZOO223-04]; assesses=[] -->

*hóuzi* — "monkey".

> *nà zhī hóuzi hěn cōngming* — That monkey's very clever.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hóuzi* is built from *hóu* 'monkey' and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huw* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with a word for the macaque.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
