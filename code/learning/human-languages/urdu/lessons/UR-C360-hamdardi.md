## You'll want to know: ہمدردی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C360-RAHM360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہمدردی** — *hamdardī* — "sympathy".

> مجھے اس سے ہمدردی ہے۔
>
> *mujhe us se hamdardī hai.*
>
> I feel for him.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہمدردی** (*hamdardī*) is usually traced to Persian *hamdardī*, "sharing pain": *ham*, "together", **درد**, "pain", and the noun ending *-ī*.
