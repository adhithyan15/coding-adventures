## You'll want to know: せいじつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C437-HONEST437-03]; assesses=[] -->

**せいじつ** — *seijitsu* — "sincere; faithful".

> **じょうしは せいじつです。** — *jōshi wa seijitsu desu* — "My boss is sincere."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいじつ** (*seijitsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "sincerity" (Middle Chinese *dzyeng*) and *jitsu*, "reality" (*zyit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
