## You'll want to know: انجام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C329-AFSANA329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**انجام** — *anjām* — "an ending; an outcome".

> ناول کا انجام اداس تھا۔
>
> *nāvil kā anjām udās thā.*
>
> The novel had a sad ending.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انجام** (*anjām*) is usually traced to Persian *anjām*, "an end, a completion". **انجام کیا ہوا؟** is how did it end?
