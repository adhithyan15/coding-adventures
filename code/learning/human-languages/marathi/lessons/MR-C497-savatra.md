## You'll want to know: सावत्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C497-THORLA497-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सावत्र** — *sāvatra* — "step-".

> त्याची सावत्र आई त्याच्यावर खूप प्रेम करते.
>
> *tyācī sāvatra āī tyācyāvar khūp prem karte.*
>
> His stepmother loves him very much.

Unchanging: सावत्र आई, a stepmother; सावत्र भाऊ, a half-brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सावत्र** (*sāvatra*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *sāvatra*, "born of a co-wife", under Sanskrit *sāpatna*, "of a rival" (CDIAL 13342).
