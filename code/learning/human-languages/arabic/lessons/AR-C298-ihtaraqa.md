# احترق (iḥtaraqa) — to burn down, to get burnt

## You'll want to know: احترق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C298-HARIQ298-04]; assesses=[] -->

**احترق** — *iḥtaraqa* — "to burn down, to get burnt".

> احترق الخبز في الفرن.
>
> *iḥtaraqa al-khubz fī al-furn.*
>
> The bread got burnt in the oven.

Present: يحترق, *yaḥtariq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احترق** (*iḥtaraqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-r-q* (Lane), "to burn". It is Form VIII, "to be burnt, to burn up".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
