## You'll want to know: jìngzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C105-BATH105-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jìngzi* — "mirror".

> *xǐshǒujiān lǐ yǒu jìngzi ma* — Is there a mirror in the toilets?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìngzi* is built from jìng 'mirror' and the noun ending zi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjaengH* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary's entry tells of a mirror sent as a gift to a king of old.
