# உபசரி (upacari) — to host; to look after a guest

## You'll want to know: உபசரி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C503-NATPU503-04]; assesses=[] -->

**உபசரி** — *upacari* — "to host; to look after a guest".

> வீட்டுக்கு வந்த விருந்தினர்களை அம்மா நன்றாக உபசரித்தாள்.
>
> *vīṭṭukku vanta viruntiṉarkaḷai ammā naṉṟāka upacarittāḷ.*
>
> Mother looked after the guests who came home very well.

Past (I …): உபசரித்தேன், *upacarittēṉ*.

The guest takes -ஐ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உபசரி** (*upacari*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *upacāra*, "approach, behaviour, service" (CDIAL 2163), made into a Tamil verb in -இ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
