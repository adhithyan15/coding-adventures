## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C335-PATRIKE335-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕ** — *prakāśaka* — "a publisher".

> ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕರು ಅಜ್ಜನ ಕಥೆಗಳನ್ನು ಪುಸ್ತಕವಾಗಿ ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *prakāśakaru ajjana kathegaḷannu pustakavāgi prakaṭisidaru.*
>
> The publisher brought out Grandfather's stories as a book.

Plural: ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕರು, *prakāśakaru*.

ಪ್ರಕಾಶ is light: a publisher brings a work into the light.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕ** (*prakāśaka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prakāśaka*, "bright, shining, making known" (Monier-Williams), from *prakāśa*, "light, publicity".
