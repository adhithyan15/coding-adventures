# ಹಳಹಳಿಸು (haḷahaḷisu) — to rue a loss; to pine for the past

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C495-MAMATE495-05, KA-LEX-C496-ASHE496-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for care, then the Kannada for to long for.

## You'll want to know: ಹಳಹಳಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C496-ASHE496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಳಹಳಿಸು** — *haḷahaḷisu* — "to rue a loss; to pine for the past".

> ಮಾರಿದ ಹಳೆಯ ಮನೆಗಾಗಿ ಅಜ್ಜ ಹಳಹಳಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *mārida haḷeya manegāgi ajja haḷahaḷisidaru.*
>
> Grandfather pined for the old house that had been sold.

Past (I …): ಹಳಹಳಿಸಿದೆ, *haḷahaḷiside*.

A longing mixed with regret; ಹಳಹಳಿಕೆ is the feeling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಳಹಳಿಸು** (*haḷahaḷisu*) is a native Kannada verb for longing after something gone; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
