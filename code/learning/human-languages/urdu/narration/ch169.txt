5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بانسری (bāṅsurī) — a flute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for cricket, then the Urdu for football.

You'll want to know: بانسری.
بانسری — bāṅsurī — "a flute".

The word, taken apart.
بانسری (bāṅsurī) belongs with Hindi bãsī, "flute", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit vaṃśī, "flute, pipe", built on vaṃśa, "bamboo" (CDIAL 11180). CDIAL does not list bāṅsurī itself.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ناچ (nāch) — a dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for football, then the Urdu for a flute.

You'll want to know: ناچ.
ناچ — nāch — "a dance".

The word, taken apart.
ناچ (nāch) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nṛtya, "dancing" (CDIAL 7582). It is built on nṛt-, "to dance", the root of nāchnā.
