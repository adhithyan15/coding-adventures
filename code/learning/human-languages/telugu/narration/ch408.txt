Telugu, chapter 408: Society.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సమాజం (samājaṁ) — society; a community.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a claim, then the Telugu for a reform.

You'll want to know: సమాజం.
సమాజం — samājaṁ — "society; a community".
సమాజంలో ప్రతి ఒక్కరికీ ఒక బాధ్యత ఉంది. samājaṁlō prati okkarikī oka bādhyata undi. Everyone has a responsibility in society.
Also a smaller community: మన సమాజం (samājaṁ), our community.

The word, taken apart.
సమాజం (samājaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit samāja, "a festive gathering" (CDIAL 13207), which Monier-Williams also glosses "an assembly, a society".

Lesson 2 of 5.
సామాజిక (sāmājika) — social.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a reform, then the Telugu for society.

You'll want to know: సామాజిక.
సామాజిక — sāmājika — "social".
పేదరికం ఒక పెద్ద సామాజిక సమస్య. pēdarikaṁ oka pedda sāmājika samasya. Poverty is a big social problem.
It stands before a noun: సామాజిక (sāmājika) సమస్య, a social problem.

The word, taken apart.
సామాజిక (sāmājika) is borrowed from Sanskrit sāmājika, "relating to an assembly" (Monier-Williams), from samāja, the word Telugu has as సమాజం (samājaṁ).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Telugu for society, then the Telugu for social.

సంఘం — saṅghaṁ — "an association; a union".
మా వీధిలో ఒక యువజన సంఘం ఉంది. mā vīdhilō oka yuvajana saṅghaṁ undi. There is a youth association in our street.
Plural: సంఘాలు, saṅghālu.
Also a trade union. Plural సంఘాలు.

The word, taken apart.
సంఘం (saṅghaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṁgha, "an association, a community" (CDIAL 12854).

Lesson 4 of 5.
సభ్యుడు (sabhyuḍu) — a member.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for social, then the Telugu for an association.

You'll want to know: సభ్యుడు.
సభ్యుడు — sabhyuḍu — "a member".
నేను ఆ సంఘంలో సభ్యుడిని. nēnu ā saṅghaṁlō sabhyuḍini. I am a member of that association.
Plural: సభ్యులు, sabhyulu.
A woman member is సభ్యురాలు.

The word, taken apart.
సభ్యుడు (sabhyuḍu) is borrowed from Sanskrit sabhya, "being in an assembly" (CDIAL 13171), from sabhā, "an assembly", with the Telugu masculine ending -డు.

Lesson 5 of 5.
వివక్ష (vivakṣa) — discrimination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an association, then the Telugu for a member.

You'll want to know: వివక్ష.
వివక్ష — vivakṣa — "discrimination".
ఆడపిల్లల మీద వివక్ష తప్పు. āḍapillala mīda vivakṣa tappu. Discrimination against girls is wrong.
The group treated unfairly takes మీద or పట్ల.

The word, taken apart.
వివక్ష (vivakṣa) is borrowed from Sanskrit vivakṣā, "the wish to speak; meaning, intention" (Monier-Williams); the sense "discrimination" is the modern Telugu one.
