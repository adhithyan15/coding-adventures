## You'll want to know: накрывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C329-KIPYATIT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**накрывать** (*nakryvát*) — "to cover; to lay (a table)".

> Он накрыл сковороду крышкой.
>
> *On nakrýl skovorodú krýshkoy.*
>
> He covered the pan with a lid.

Imperfective; perfective *накрыть* (*nakrýt*), *накрою*. *Накрыть на стол* — to lay a table for a meal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*накрывать* is built on *на-* "on" and *крыть* "to cover", an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
