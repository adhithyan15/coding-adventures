# ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು (aṅgīkarisu) — to approve; to pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C395-ADALITA395-05, KA-LEX-C396-MASUDE396-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a committee, then the Kannada for a draft law.

## You'll want to know: ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C396-MASUDE396-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು** — *aṅgīkarisu* — "to approve; to pass".

> ಸಂಸತ್ತು ಮಸೂದೆಯನ್ನು ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸಿತು.
>
> *saṃsattu masūdeyannu aṅgīkarisitu.*
>
> Parliament passed the bill.

Past (I …): ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ, *aṅgīkariside*.

ಅಂಗೀಕಾರ, approval.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು** (*aṅgīkarisu*) is built on Sanskrit *aṅgīkāra*, "agreement, promise" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
