## You'll want to know: ചവറ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C504-CAVAR504-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ചവറ്** — *cavaṟŭ* — "litter; rubbish".

> കടപ്പുറത്ത് ധാരാളം ചവറ് ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു.
>
> *kaṭappuṟattŭ dhārāḷaṁ cavaṟŭ uṇṭāyirunnu.*
>
> There was a lot of litter on the beach.

Before some endings ചവറ് doubles its last consonant: ചവറ്റിൽ, in the rubbish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചവറ്** (*cavaṟŭ*) is native Dravidian, "rubbish, sweepings"; DEDR also lists under it green leaves and rubbish used as manure (DEDR 2395).
