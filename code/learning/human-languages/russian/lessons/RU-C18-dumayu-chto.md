# я думаю, что … — saying that an opinion is yours

## Grammar lens — the opinion frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-DUMAYU-CHTO-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-CHTO-COMPLEMENTISER-01, RU-LEX-DUMAT, RU-LEX-CHTO-01] -->

**думать** has been in this book since the verbs of the mind, and until this
chapter it had nothing to think **about** — no object bigger than a single word
could be attached to it. **что** fixes that in one move:

- **я думаю, что …** — *ya dúmayu, shto …* — **I think that …**

Whatever you can already say in Russian, you can now say you **think** it. That
is a genuine step up rather than one more phrase: it turns every sentence you own
into an opinion you have marked as your own, which is the difference between
reporting a fact and taking a position.

- **я думаю, что ты понимаешь** — *ya dúmayu, shto ty panimáyesh* — I think that
  you understand.

The comma before **что** is compulsory here as everywhere.
