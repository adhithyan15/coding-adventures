## You'll want to know: কর্মস্থল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C500-BIBHAG500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**কর্মস্থল** — *kôrmôsthôl* — "a workplace".

> আমার কর্মস্থল বাড়ি থেকে দূরে।
>
> *Āmār kôrmôsthôl bāṛi theke dūre.*
>
> My workplace is far from home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কর্মস্থল** (*kôrmôsthôl*) joins Sanskrit *karman*, "work", and *sthala*, "a place, ground" (Monier-Williams).
