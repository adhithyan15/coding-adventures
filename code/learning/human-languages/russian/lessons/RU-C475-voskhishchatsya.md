## You'll want to know: восхищаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C475-PRIDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**восхищаться** (*voskhishchátsya*) — "to admire".

> Я восхищаюсь её терпением.
>
> *Ya voskhishcháyus yeyó terpéniyem.*
>
> I admire her patience.

Imperfective; perfective восхититься (восхищусь). Takes the instrumental: *восхищаться кем, чем*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*восхищаться* is built on *восхитить*, which was borrowed from Church Slavonic *vъsxytiti* 'to snatch away, to fascinate' (EtymDB): admiration carries you away.
