## You'll want to know: નિયમિત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C177-OFTEN177-04]; assesses=[] -->

**નિયમિત** — *niyamit* — "regular, regularly".

> **હવે હું નિયમિત ચાલીશ.** — *have hũ niyamit cālīś* — "From now on I will walk regularly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિયમિત** (*niyamit*) is Sanskrit *niyamita*, "restrained, regulated", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, built on *niyama*, "restraint, rule".
