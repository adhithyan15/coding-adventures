## You'll want to know: endommager
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C307-DEGATS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**endommager** — "to damage".

> L'eau a endommagé la cuisine.
>
> *The water damaged the kitchen.*

For things, not people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*endommager* is built on *dommage* "harm", from Old French *damage*, which goes back to Latin *damnum* "loss" (Lewis & Short).
