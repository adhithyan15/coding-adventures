## You'll want to know: il sarto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C455-ARTIGIANI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il sarto** — "the tailor".

> Il sarto mi fa un vestito per il matrimonio.
>
> *The tailor is making me a suit for the wedding.*

A woman is *la sarta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarto* is inherited from Late Latin *sartor* 'tailor' (EtymDB), from *sarcīre* 'to patch, to mend'.
