Latin, chapter 100: Fight, Sail, Prepare, Touch, Pull.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pugnō, pugnāre — to fight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to praise, then the Latin for to move.
