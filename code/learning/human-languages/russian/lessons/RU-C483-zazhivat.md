## You'll want to know: заживать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C483-SCRATCHES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**заживать** (*zazhivát*) — "to heal (of a wound)".

> Рана заживает очень медленно.
>
> *Rána zazhiváyet óchen médlenno.*
>
> The wound is healing very slowly.

Imperfective; perfective зажить (заживёт). Of a wound or a cut: *рана заживает*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*заживать* is built on *за-* and *жить* 'to live', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *žiti* (EtymDB): a wound that heals comes back to life.
