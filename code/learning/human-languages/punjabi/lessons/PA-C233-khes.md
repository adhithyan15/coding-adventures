## You'll want to know: ਖੇਸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C233-DIN233-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੇਸ** — *khes* — "a thick cotton sheet".

> ਮੈਂ ਮੰਜੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਖੇਸ ਵਿਛਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ manje utte khes vichhāundā hāṁ.*
>
> I spread a thick cotton sheet on the cot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੇਸ** (*khes*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kheśśa*, "a particular sort of cloth"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khes*, "a kind of cloth" (CDIAL 3924).
