## You'll want to know: обновлять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C401-PRILOZHENIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**обновлять** (*obnovlyát*) — "to update, to renew".

> Телефон сам обновляет приложения.
>
> *Telefón sam obnovlyáyet prilozhéniya.*
>
> The phone updates its apps by itself.

Imperfective; the perfective is *обновить* (*обновлю, обновишь*). *Обновление* — an update.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**обновлять** is built from *об-* and *новый* "new", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*novŭ*: to make a thing new again.
