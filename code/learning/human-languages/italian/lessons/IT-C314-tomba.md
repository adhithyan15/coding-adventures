## You'll want to know: la tomba
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C314-EREDITA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la tomba** — "the grave, the tomb".

> Ogni anno portiamo fiori sulla tomba dei nonni.
>
> *Every year we take flowers to our grandparents' grave.*

*Muto come una tomba* — silent as the grave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tomba* is inherited from Late Latin *tumba* (EtymDB), from Greek *týmbos* "a burial mound".
