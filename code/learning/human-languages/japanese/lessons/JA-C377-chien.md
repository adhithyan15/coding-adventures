## You'll want to know: ちえん

**ちえん** — *chien* — "a delay".

> **たいふうで でんしゃに ちえんが ありました。** — *taifū de densha ni chien ga arimashita* — "The typhoon delayed the trains."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちえん** (*chien*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chi*, "late" (Middle Chinese *drij*) and *en*, "prolong" (*yen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
