## You'll want to know: એમ્બ્યુલન્સ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C296-AMBULANS296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**એમ્બ્યુલન્સ** — *embyulans* — "an ambulance".

> દસ મિનિટમાં એમ્બ્યુલન્સ આવી.
>
> *das miniṭmā̃ embyulans āvī.*
>
> The ambulance came within ten minutes.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**એમ્બ્યુલન્સ** (*embyulans*) is borrowed from English *ambulance*, and it is feminine: **એમ્બ્યુલન્સ આવી**, the ambulance came.
