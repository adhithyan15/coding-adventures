## You'll want to know: कोंबणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C400-VAHAN400-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कोंबणे** — *kombṇe* — "to stuff in; to cram".

> त्याने पिशवीत सगळे कपडे कोंबले.
>
> *tyāne piśvīt sagḷe kapḍe komble.*
>
> He stuffed all the clothes into the bag.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कोंबलं, *tyāne komblaṁ*.

The container takes -त: पिशवीत कोंबणे; people crammed into a rickshaw are कोंबलेले.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोंबणे** (*kombṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *kõbṇẽ*, "to thump, thrust, force in, cram in", under *\*kōbb*, "strike" (CDIAL 3522).
