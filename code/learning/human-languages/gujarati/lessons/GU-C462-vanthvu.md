## You'll want to know: વંઠવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C462-CANCAL462-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વંઠવું** — *vanṭhvũ* — "to go astray; to be spoilt".

> ખરાબ દોસ્તોને લીધે દીકરો વંઠી ગયો.
>
> *kharāb dostone līdhe dīkro vanṭhī gayo.*
>
> Because of bad friends the son went astray.

Past (he …): તે વંઠી ગયો, *te vanṭhī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વંઠવું** (*vanṭhvũ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be dissipated", under Sanskrit *vaṇṭa*, "tailless, unmarried" (CDIAL 11236).
