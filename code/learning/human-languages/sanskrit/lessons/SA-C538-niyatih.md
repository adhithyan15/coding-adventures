## You'll want to know: नियतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C538-SAKUNA538-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नियतिः** — *niyatiḥ* — "destiny".

> यदि नियतिः सत्यम् अस्ति, तर्हि अहं किमर्थं चिन्तयामि?
>
> *yadi niyatiḥ satyam asti, tarhi ahaṃ kimarthaṃ cintayāmi?*
>
> If destiny is real, why do I worry?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियतिः** (*niyatiḥ*) is built on *ni*, "down", and the root √yam, "to hold, to restrain"; MW gives *niyati* as the fixed order of things, necessity, destiny (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
