## You'll want to know: دائمی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C475-LONG475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دائمی** — *dāimī* — "chronic; lasting".

> دادی کو دائمی کھانسی ہے۔
>
> *dādī ko dāimī khāṅsī hai.*
>
> My grandmother has a chronic cough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دائمی** (*dāimī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dāʾimī*, "lasting, perpetual".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
