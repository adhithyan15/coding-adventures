## You'll want to know: বিদ্যুৎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C288-GHURNIJHOR288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**বিদ্যুৎ** — *bidyut* — "electricity; lightning".

> ঝড়ের রাতে গ্রামে বিদ্যুৎ ছিল না।
>
> *jhôṛer rāte grāme bidyut chhilô nā.*
>
> There was no electricity in the village on the night of the storm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিদ্যুৎ** (*bidyut*) is Sanskrit *vidyut*, lightning, from *vi-√dyut*, 'to flash forth' (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for lightning and for the power in the wires.
