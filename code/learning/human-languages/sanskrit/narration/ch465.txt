Sanskrit, chapter 465: Steady and Careless.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चञ्चलः (cañcalaḥ) — fickle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for reviles, then the Sanskrit for prattle.

You'll want to know: चञ्चलः.
चञ्चलः — cañcalaḥ — "fickle".
वानरस्य मनः चञ्चलं भवति। vānarasya manaḥ cañcalaṃ bhavati. A monkey's mind is fickle.

The word, taken apart.
चञ्चलः (cañcalaḥ) is built on the intensive of the root √cal, "to move", the root of चलति: moving to and fro; MW gives "unsteady, inconstant" (MW).

सः लाभे हानौ च उदासीनः अस्ति। saḥ lābhe hānau ca udāsīnaḥ asti. He is indifferent to gain and loss.

उदासीनः (udāsīnaḥ) is built from ud, "up, apart", and āsīna, "sitting", from the root √ās of आस्ते: sitting apart; MW gives "indifferent, free from affection" (MW).

प्रमाद्यति — pramādyati — "is careless".
चालकः कदापि न प्रमाद्येत्। cālakaḥ kadāpi na pramādyet. A driver should never be careless.
Past (he …): सः प्रमत्तवान्, saḥ pramattavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रमाद्यति (pramādyati) is built from pra and the root √mad, "to be drunk, to rejoice"; MW gives "to be careless or negligent", and its ppp pramatta is "careless, heedless" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अवधानम् (avadhānam) — attention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for indifferent, then the Sanskrit for is careless.

You'll want to know: अवधानम्.
अवधानम् — avadhānam — "attention".
अवधानेन पाठं शृणोतु। avadhānena pāṭhaṃ śṛṇotu. Listen to the lesson with attention.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अवधानम् (avadhānam) is built from ava, "down", and the root √dhā, "to put": putting the mind down on a thing; MW gives "attention, attentiveness" (MW).
