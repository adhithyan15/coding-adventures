## You'll want to know: હૂંફાળું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C215-FOG215-05]; assesses=[] -->

**હૂંફાળું** — *hū̃phāḷũ* — "lukewarm, pleasantly warm".

> **હું હૂંફાળું પાણી પીશ.** — *hũ hū̃phāḷũ pāṇī pīś* — "I will drink warm water."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હૂંફાળું** (*hū̃phāḷũ*) is built on *hū̃ph*, "warmth", a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Gujarati *hũph*, "genial warmth", under Sanskrit *ūṣman*, "heat, steam" (CDIAL 2441). The next entry, *ūṣmāyate*, "emits heat", holds the close Gujarati *ũphāylũ*, "lukewarm, tepid" (CDIAL 2442).
