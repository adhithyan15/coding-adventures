## You'll want to know: प्रज्ञा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C365-SIDDHANTA365-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रज्ञा** — *prajñā* — "wisdom".

> वृद्धस्य प्रज्ञा महती भवति।
>
> *vṛddhasya prajñā mahatī bhavati.*
>
> An elder's wisdom is great.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रज्ञा** (*prajñā*) is built on *pra* and the root √jñā, "to know"; MW gives *pra-jñā* "wisdom, intelligence, knowledge, judgement" (MW).
