# ಒಳಗೆ ಬನ್ನಿ (oḷage banni) — please come in

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C88-WELCOME-02, KA-LEX-C88-WELCOME-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for an invitation, then the Kannada for a gift.

## You'll want to know: ಒಳಗೆ ಬನ್ನಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C88-WELCOME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಳಗೆ ಬನ್ನಿ** (*oḷage banni*) — "please come in". **ಒಳಗೆ** is "inside", and
**ಬನ್ನಿ** is the polite form of **ಬಾ**, "come".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The first thing said at the door.
