## You'll want to know: 也
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C92-YE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**也** — *yě* — "also; too".

> **我也要** *wǒ yě yào* — Me too; I'd like one too.

> **他也是学生** *tā yě shì xuésheng* — He's a student too.

The next lesson puts the character in your hand. You have written it before, as the right half of 他.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**也** (*yě*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *yaeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a particle that closes a sentence'. 'Also' is not given there, so how it came to mean 'also' is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C31-PEOPLE31-02] -->

也 goes in front of the verb: 我也要, 他也是学生. English puts *too* at the end; Mandarin does not.
