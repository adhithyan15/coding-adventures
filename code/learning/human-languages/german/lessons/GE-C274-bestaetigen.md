## You'll want to know: bestätigen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C274-GERUECHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bestätigen** — "to confirm".

> Die Polizei bestätigte die Meldung.
>
> *The police confirmed the news item.*

Perfekt: *hat bestätigt*. Also of a booking: *Das Hotel bestätigte die Reservierung*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bestätigen* is usually traced to Middle High German *bestætigen* "to make firm", built on *stætec* "steady", from the root of *stehen* "to stand"; EtymDB lists no parent.
