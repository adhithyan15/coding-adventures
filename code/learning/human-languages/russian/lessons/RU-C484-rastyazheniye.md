## You'll want to know: растяжение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C484-PLASTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**растяжение** (*rastyazhéniye*) — "a strain (of a muscle)".

> У футболиста растяжение мышцы.
>
> *U futbolísta rastyazhéniye mýshtsy.*
>
> The footballer has a pulled muscle.

Neuter. *Растяжение связок* — a sprain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*растяжение* is built on *растянуть* 'to stretch', from *тянуть* 'to pull', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *tęgnǫti* (EtymDB).
