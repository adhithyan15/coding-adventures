## You'll want to know: દુર્ગંધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C423-TOWN423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**દુર્ગંધ** — *durgandh* — "a stink; a bad smell".

> ગટરમાંથી દુર્ગંધ આવે છે.
>
> *gaṭarmā̃thī durgandh āve che.*
>
> A bad smell is coming from the drain.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દુર્ગંધ** (*durgandh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *durgandha*, "a bad smell" (Monier-Williams), made of *dur-*, "bad", and *gandha*, "a smell".
