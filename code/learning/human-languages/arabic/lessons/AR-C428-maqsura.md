## You'll want to know: مقصورة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C428-SIKKA428-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مقصورة** — *maqṣūra* — "a compartment; a cabin".

> كانت المقصورة مزدحمة بالمسافرين.
>
> *kānat al-maqṣūra muzdaḥima bi-l-musāfirīn.*
>
> The compartment was crowded with travellers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقصورة** (*maqṣūra*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ṣ-r*, "to confine, to keep within bounds" (Lane). It is a passive participle: a space closed off, a compartment.
