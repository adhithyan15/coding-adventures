## You'll want to know: 来
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C36-WHERE36-01]; assesses=[] -->

**来** — *lái* — "to come".

> **妈妈来了** *māma lái le* — Mum's here.

The new character, in pieces: 来 — one piece built from the top down: a short bar, a dot and a short stroke under it, a long bar, a vertical through it, and two strokes spreading at the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**来** (*lái*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *loj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to arrive'.
