Spanish, chapter 601: El gobierno.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el gobierno — the government.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the citizen, then the Spanish for to belong.

You'll want to know: el gobierno.
el gobierno — "the government".
El gobierno subió los impuestos. The government raised taxes.
The verb is gobernar (e becomes ie).

The word, taken apart.
gobierno is built on gobernar, inherited from Latin gubernare 'to steer' (EtymDB), a verb Latin took from Greek kybernan (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
la política — the politics; the policy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to belong, then the Spanish for the government.

You'll want to know: la política.
la política — "the politics; the policy".
No me interesa la política. I am not interested in politics.
Also a policy: la política de la empresa.

The word, taken apart.
la política is a learned borrowing from Greek politikē 'the affairs of the city', built on polis 'city', through Latin politicus (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
el ministro — the minister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the politics, then the Spanish for political.

You'll want to know: el ministro.
el ministro — "the minister".
El ministro habló en la tele. The minister spoke on TV.
A woman is la ministra.

The word, taken apart.
ministro is a learned borrowing from Latin minister 'servant, helper' (EtymDB), built on minus 'less' as magister is on magis: the lesser one who serves (Lewis & Short).
