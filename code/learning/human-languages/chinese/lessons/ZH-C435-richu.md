## You'll want to know: 日出
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C435-SUMMIT435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**日出** — *rìchū* — "sunrise".

> **我们早上五点起来看日出** *wǒmen zǎoshang wǔ diǎn qǐlai kàn rìchū* — We're getting up at five in the morning to watch the sunrise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**日出** (*rìchū*) is built from 日 'the sun' and 出 'to come out', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyit* and *tsyhwit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
