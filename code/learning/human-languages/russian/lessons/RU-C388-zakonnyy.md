## You'll want to know: законный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C388-ZAKON-02]; assesses=[] -->

**законный** (*zakónnyy*) — "legal, lawful".

> Это вполне законное требование.
>
> *Éto vpolné zakónnoye trébovaniye.*
>
> That is a perfectly lawful demand.

*Законно* — legally.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**законный** is the adjective made from *закон* "a law", built from *за-* and old *кон* "a limit".
