## You'll want to know: o embarque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C421-AEROPORTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o embarque** — "the boarding".

> O embarque começa meia hora antes do voo.
>
> *Boarding starts half an hour before the flight.*

*A porta de embarque* — the gate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*embarque* is built on *embarcar* 'to board', on *barca* 'boat', inherited from Late Latin *barca* (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
