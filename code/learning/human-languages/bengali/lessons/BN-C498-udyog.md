# উদ্যোগ (udyog) — an initiative; an enterprise

## You'll want to know: উদ্যোগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C498-UDYOG498-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উদ্যোগ** — *udyog* — "an initiative; an enterprise".

> এই নতুন উদ্যোগে গ্রামের মহিলারা কাজ পেল।
>
> *Ei nôtun udyoge grāmer môhilārā kāj pelô.*
>
> Through this new initiative the village women found work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উদ্যোগ** (*udyog*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *udyoga*, "the act of undertaking something, exertion" (Monier-Williams).
