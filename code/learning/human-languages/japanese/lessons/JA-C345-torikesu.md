## You'll want to know: とりけす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C345-HENKOU345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とりけす** — *torikesu* — "to cancel; to take back".

> **びょうきに なったら、よやくを とりけして ください。** — *byōki ni nattara, yoyaku o torikeshite kudasai* — "If you fall ill, please cancel the booking."

Polite **とりけします**, *torikeshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが よやくを とりけしました。**, *chichi ga yoyaku o torikeshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とりけす** (*torikesu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *tori*, "taking (toru)" and *kesu*, "to put out", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
