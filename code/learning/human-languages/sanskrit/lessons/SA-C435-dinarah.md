## You'll want to know: दीनारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C435-DINARA435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दीनारः** — *dīnāraḥ* — "a dinar (a gold coin)".

> धनिकस्य स्यूते दीनाराः सन्ति।
>
> *dhanikasya syūte dīnārāḥ santi.*
>
> There are dinars in the rich man's bag.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीनारः** (*dīnāraḥ*) is borrowed from Greek *dēnarion*, the Roman *denarius*, as MW derives it; MW gives *dīnāra* as a gold ornament and a gold coin (MW).
