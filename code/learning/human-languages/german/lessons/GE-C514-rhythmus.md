## You'll want to know: der Rhythmus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C514-MELODIE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Rhythmus** — "rhythm".

> Die Kinder tanzen im Rhythmus der Musik.
>
> *The children dance to the rhythm of the music.*

Plural: *die Rhythmen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Rhythmus* is a learned borrowing from Latin *rhythmus*, from Greek *rhythmós* "measured flow, time" (Lewis & Short).
