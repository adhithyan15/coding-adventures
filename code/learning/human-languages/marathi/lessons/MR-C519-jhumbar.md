## You'll want to know: झुंबर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C519-UJED519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**झुंबर** — *jhumbar* — "a chandelier".

> जुन्या घरात काचेचं मोठं झुंबर होतं.
>
> *junyā gharāt kācecaṁ moṭhaṁ jhumbar hotaṁ.*
>
> There was a big glass chandelier in the old house.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झुंबर** (*jhumbar*) names a hanging cluster of lamps; its origin is not settled.
