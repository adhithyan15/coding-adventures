## You'll want to know: ملل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C488-MAZAJ488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ملل** — *malal* — "boredom".

> شعر الطالب بالملل في المحاضرة الطويلة.
>
> *shaʿara aṭ-ṭālib bi-l-malal fī al-muḥāḍara aṭ-ṭawīla.*
>
> The student felt bored in the long lecture.

Arabic says شعر بالملل, to feel boredom, where English says to be bored.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملل** (*malal*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-l-l*, "to be weary of a thing" (Lane). Malal is the noun of that weariness.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
