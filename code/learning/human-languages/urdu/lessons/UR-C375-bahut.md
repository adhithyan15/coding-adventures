# بہت (bahut) — very; a lot

## You'll want to know: بہت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C375-BAHUT375-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بہت** — *bahut* — "very; a lot".

> اگر بہت سردی ہوئی تو ہم گھر پر رہیں گے۔
>
> *agar bahut sardī huī to ham ghar par raheṅ ge.*
>
> If it is very cold, we will stay at home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بہت** (*bahut*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bahut*, "much, many", under Sanskrit *bahutva*, "abundance" (CDIAL 9190).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
