# ಬೆನ್ನುಮೂಳೆ (bennumūḷe) — the spine; the backbone

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a nerve, then the Kannada for a rib.

## You'll want to know: ಬೆನ್ನುಮೂಳೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C509-ELUBU509-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೆನ್ನುಮೂಳೆ** — *bennumūḷe* — "the spine; the backbone".

> ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ಕುಳಿತರೆ ಬೆನ್ನುಮೂಳೆಗೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದು.
>
> *sariyāgi kuḷitare bennumūḷege oḷḷeyadu.*
>
> Sitting properly is good for the spine.

ಬೆನ್ನು, the back + ಮೂಳೆ, a bone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೆನ್ನುಮೂಳೆ** (*bennumūḷe*) is a compound of two native Dravidian words: *bennu*, "the back" (DEDR 5488), and *mūḷe*, "a bone" (DEDR 5050).
