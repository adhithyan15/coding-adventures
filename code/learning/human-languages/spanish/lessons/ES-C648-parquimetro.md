## You'll want to know: el parquímetro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C648-APARCAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el parquímetro** — "the parking meter".

> Pon dinero en el parquímetro.
>
> *Put money in the parking meter.*

You pay at the machine and leave the ticket in the car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parquímetro* is built on *parking*, borrowed from English (EtymDB), with *-metro*, from Greek *métron* "measure".
