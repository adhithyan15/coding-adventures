## You'll want to know: सूर्यास्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C443-ASTA443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सूर्यास्तः** — *sūryāstaḥ* — "sunset".

> सूर्यास्ते काकः नीडं गच्छति।
>
> *sūryāste kākaḥ nīḍaṃ gacchati.*
>
> At sunset the crow goes to its nest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूर्यास्तः** (*sūryāstaḥ*) is built from *sūrya*, "the sun", and *asta*, "home, setting"; MW gives the compound as sunset (MW).
