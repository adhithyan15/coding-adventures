## You'll want to know: l'objectif
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C374-BUT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'objectif** — "the objective, the target; the lens".

> Notre objectif est de finir avant juin.
>
> *Our objective is to finish before June.*

On a camera, *l'objectif* is the lens. As an adjective, *objectif* means objective. Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*objectif* comes from Medieval Latin *obiectīvus* (EtymDB), from *obicere* "to throw before, to put before" (Lewis & Short), built on *iacere* "to throw".
