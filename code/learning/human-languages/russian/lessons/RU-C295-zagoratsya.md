## You'll want to know: загораться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C295-POZHAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**загораться** (*zagorátsya*) — "to catch fire".

> От свечи загорелась штора.
>
> *Ot svechí zagorélas shtóra.*
>
> The curtain caught fire from a candle.

Imperfective; perfective *загореться* (*zagorétsya*). Also of lights coming on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*загораться* is built on *за-*, which marks a start, and *гореть* "to burn", inherited from Proto-Slavic *gorěti* (EtymDB): to start burning.
