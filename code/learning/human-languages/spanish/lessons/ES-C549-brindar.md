## You'll want to know: brindar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C549-CASADO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**brindar** — "to toast, to drink to; to offer".

> Brindamos por el nuevo año.
>
> *We drank a toast to the new year.*

*Brindar por* — to drink to. *Brindar ayuda* — to offer help.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brindar* is built on *brindis* 'a toast' (EtymDB), which comes from the German words *bring dir's* 'I bring it to you'.
