## You'll want to know: кипятить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C329-KIPYATIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**кипятить** (*kipyatít*) — "to bring to the boil, to boil".

> Бабушка кипятит молоко.
>
> *Bábushka kipyatít molokó.*
>
> Grandma is boiling milk.

Imperfective; perfective *вскипятить* (*vskipyatít*), *кипячу*. *Кипяток* — boiling water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*кипятить* is built on *кипеть* "to boil", inherited from Proto-Slavic *kypěti* (EtymDB): to make something boil.
