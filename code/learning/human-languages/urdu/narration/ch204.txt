Warm-up.

You'll want to know: خبر.
خبر — khabar — "news".

The word, taken apart.
خبر (khabar) is a loan from Arabic khabar, "news, report", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is kh-b-r (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
بات (bāt) — a matter, something said.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a number, then the Urdu for news.

You'll want to know: بات.
بات — bāt — "a matter, something said".

The word, taken apart.
بات (bāt) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vārttā, "livelihood; tidings" (CDIAL 11564).

Lesson 4 of 5.
بات (bāt) چیت (bātchīt) — a conversation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for news, then the Urdu for a matter.

You'll want to know: بات چیت.
بات (bāt) چیت — bātchīt — "a conversation".

The word, taken apart.
بات چیت (bātchīt) is a rhyming pair: bāt, "talk", from Sanskrit vārttā (CDIAL 11564), and chīt, which CDIAL lists in this very pair under citti, "thought" (CDIAL 4801).
