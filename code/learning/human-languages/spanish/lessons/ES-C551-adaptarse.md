## You'll want to know: adaptarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C551-TRASLADO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**adaptarse** — "to adapt, to get used to".

> Me adapté rápido a la nueva ciudad.
>
> *I quickly got used to the new city.*

*Adaptarse a* — to adapt to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adaptarse* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adaptāre* 'to fit to' (Lewis & Short), built on *ad* and *aptus* 'fitted, suited'.
