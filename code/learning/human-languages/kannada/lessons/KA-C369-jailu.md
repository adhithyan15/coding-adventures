## You'll want to know: ಜೈಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C369-ANTHU369-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೈಲು** — *jailu* — "a jail".

> ಅಂತೂ ಅವನು ಐದು ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಜೈಲಿನಿಂದ ಹೊರಗೆ ಬಂದನು.
>
> *antū avanu aidu varṣada nantara jailininda horage bandanu.*
>
> At last, after five years, he came out of jail.

Plural: ಜೈಲುಗಳು, *jailugaḷu*.

The formal word is ಕಾರಾಗೃಹ; ಜೈಲು ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ is a prison sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೈಲು** (*jailu*) is a loan from English *jail*, with the final -*u* Kannada gives many loans.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
