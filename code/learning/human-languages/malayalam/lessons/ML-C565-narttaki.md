## You'll want to know: നർത്തകി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C565-KALAKARAN565-03]; assesses=[] -->

**നർത്തകി** — *narttaki* — "a dancer".

> ആ നർത്തകി വേദിയിൽ മനോഹരമായി നൃത്തം ചെയ്തു.
>
> *ā narttaki vēdiyil manōharamāyi nṛttaṁ ceytu.*
>
> That dancer danced beautifully on the stage.

A male dancer is നർത്തകൻ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നർത്തകി** (*narttaki*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nartakī*, "a female dancer, an actress" (Monier-Williams), from the root *nṛt*, "to dance", the root of നൃത്തം.
