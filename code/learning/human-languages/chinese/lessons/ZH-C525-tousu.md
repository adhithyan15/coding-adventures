## You'll want to know: tóusù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C525-SERVICE525-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tóusù* — "to make a formal complaint".

> *wǒ tóusù guo zhè jiā shāngdiàn* — I've complained about this shop before.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tóusù* is built from *tóu* 'to throw, to hand in' and *sù* 'to accuse', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duw* and *suH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to entrust' and 'to throw' and the second 'to bring a case' and 'to tell'.
