## You'll want to know: der Dozent
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C377-HOCHSCHULE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Dozent** — "the lecturer".

> Der Dozent erklärt das Thema sehr gut.
>
> *The lecturer explains the topic very well.*

A weak noun: *den Dozenten*, plural *die Dozenten*. A woman is *die Dozentin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Dozent* is a learned borrowing from Latin *docēns* "teaching" (EtymDB), the present participle of *docēre* "to teach, to instruct" (Lewis & Short).
