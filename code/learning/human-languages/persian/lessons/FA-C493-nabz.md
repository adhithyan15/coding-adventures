## You'll want to know: نبض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C493-DOCTOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نبض** (*nabz*) — "pulse".

> پرستار نبض بیمار را گرفت.
>
> *parastâr nabz-e bimâr râ gereft.*
>
> The nurse took the patient's pulse.

*nabz gereftan* — to take a pulse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nabz* was borrowed from Arabic *nabḍ*, 'pulse' (EtymDB). EtymDB takes Urdu and Hindi *nabz* from the same Arabic word (EtymDB).
