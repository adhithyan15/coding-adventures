## You'll want to know: せつめいする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C318-SETSUMEI318-01]; assesses=[] -->

**せつめいする** — *setsumei suru* — "to explain".

> **わかりませんから、もういちど せつめいして ください。** — *wakarimasen kara, mō ichido setsumei shite kudasai* — "I don't understand, so please explain it once more."

Polite **せつめいします**, *setsumeishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが せつめいしました。**, *sensei ga setsumei shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せつめいする** (*setsumei suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *setsumei*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *setsu*, "explanation" (Middle Chinese *sywet*) and *mei*, "bright" (*mjaeng*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
