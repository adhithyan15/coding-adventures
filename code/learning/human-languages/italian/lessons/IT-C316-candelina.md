## You'll want to know: la candelina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C316-CANDELINE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la candelina** — "the birthday candle".

> Sulla torta ci sono trenta candeline.
>
> *There are thirty candles on the cake.*

A little *candela*, ‘candle’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*candelina* is a little *candela* "candle", inherited from Latin *candēla*, from *candēre* "to shine white" (Lewis & Short).
