## You'll want to know: মতামত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C334-APOTTI334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মতামত** — *môtāmôt* — "opinions; views".

> সবার মতামত শুনে আমরা সিদ্ধান্ত নেব।
>
> *sôbār môtāmôt shune āmrā siddhāntô nebô.*
>
> We will decide after hearing everyone's views.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মতামত** (*môtāmôt*) is usually explained as a Bengali pairing of Sanskrit *mata*, an opinion, with *a-mata*, not approved of (both in Monier-Williams), so 'views for and against'; the pair itself is not in the dictionary. It asks for everyone's opinions at once.
