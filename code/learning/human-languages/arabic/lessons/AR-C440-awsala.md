## You'll want to know: أوصل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C440-TIDHKAR440-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أوصل** — *awṣala* — "to give a lift; to deliver".

> أوصلني صديقي إلى المحطة.
>
> *awṣalanī ṣadīqī ilā al-maḥaṭṭa.*
>
> My friend gave me a lift to the station.

Present: يوصل, *yūṣil*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أوصل** (*awṣala*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ṣ-l*, "to arrive, to reach" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to make arrive".
