# વૃદ્ધાશ્રમ (vṛddhāśram) — an old people's home

## You'll want to know: વૃદ્ધાશ્રમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C494-BALPAN494-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વૃદ્ધાશ્રમ** — *vṛddhāśram* — "an old people's home".

> મીરા રવિવારે વૃદ્ધાશ્રમમાં ગીતો ગાવા જાય છે.
>
> *Mīrā ravivāre vṛddhāśrammā̃ gīto gāvā jāy che.*
>
> Every Sunday Mira goes to sing at the old people's home.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વૃદ્ધાશ્રમ** (*vṛddhāśram*) is built from **વૃદ્ધ** (*vṛddh*), "old", from the entry before, and *āśram*, Sanskrit *āśrama*, "a hermitage, a place of rest" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
