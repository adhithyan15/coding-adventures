## You'll want to know: жертвовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C368-POMOSHCH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**жертвовать** (*zhértvovat*) — "to donate, to give; to sacrifice".

> Каждый год мы жертвуем деньги детскому дому.
>
> *Kázhdyy god my zhértvuyem déngi détskomu dómu.*
>
> Every year we donate money to a children’s home.

Imperfective; the present is *жертвую, жертвуешь*; the perfective is *пожертвовать*. With the instrumental it means to sacrifice: *жертвовать временем*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**жертвовать** is built on *жертва* "a sacrifice, a victim", which is usually traced to an old Slavic verb meaning to praise or to offer to the gods.
