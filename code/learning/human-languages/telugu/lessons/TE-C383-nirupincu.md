# నిరూపించు (nirūpiñcu) — to prove

## You'll want to know: నిరూపించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C383-VADANA383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నిరూపించు** — *nirūpiñcu* — "to prove".

> ఆమె తన మాట నిజమని నిరూపించింది.
>
> *āme tana māṭa nijamani nirūpiñcindi.*
>
> She proved that what she said was true.

Past (I …): నిరూపించాను, *nirūpiñcānu*.

What you prove is often a clause ending in అని: ... నిజమని నిరూపించు, to prove that ... is true.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిరూపించు** (*nirūpiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *nirūpaṇa*, "stating, determining, defining", and also "examination" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
