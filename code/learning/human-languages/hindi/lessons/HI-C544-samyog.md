## You'll want to know: संयोग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C544-KISMAT544-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संयोग** — *saṁyog* — "a coincidence".

> क्या संयोग है, हम दोनों का जन्मदिन एक ही दिन है!
>
> *Kyā saṁyog hai, ham donõ kā janmdin ek hī din hai!*
>
> What a coincidence: we both have our birthday on the same day!

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संयोग** (*saṁyog*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *saṃyoga*, "conjunction, connection" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for things that come together by chance.
