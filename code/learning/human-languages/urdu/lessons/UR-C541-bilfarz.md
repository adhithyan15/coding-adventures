## You'll want to know: بالفرض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C541-SURAT541-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بالفرض** — *bilfarz* — "supposing (for argument's sake)".

> بالفرض اگر بارش ہوئی تو ہم کیا کریں گے؟
>
> *bilfarz agar bārish huī to ham kyā kareṅ ge?*
>
> Supposing it rains, what will we do?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بالفرض** (*bilfarz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bi-l-farḍ*, "by way of supposing", built from *bi*, "by", *al*, "the", and *farḍ*, the word Urdu also has as **فرض** (*farz*), "a duty".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
