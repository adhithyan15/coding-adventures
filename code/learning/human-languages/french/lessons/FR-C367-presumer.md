## You'll want to know: présumer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C367-HYPOTHESE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**présumer** — "to presume".

> Je présume que le magasin est fermé le dimanche.
>
> *I presume the shop is closed on Sundays.*

Formal: *je présume que oui* — I presume so.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*présumer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praesūmere* "to take beforehand, to suppose" (Lewis & Short), built on *sūmere* "to take".
