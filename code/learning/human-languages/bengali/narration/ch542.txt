Bengali, chapter 542: When Plans Fall Through.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ভেস্তে যাওয়া (bheste jāoyā) — to fall through; to come to nothing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a queue token, then the Bengali for a phase.

You'll want to know: ভেস্তে যাওয়া.
ভেস্তে যাওয়া — bheste jāoyā — "to fall through; to come to nothing".
বৃষ্টি হলে আমাদের বেড়ানোর পরিকল্পনা ভেস্তে যাবে। Brishṭi hôle āmāder beṛānor pôrikôlpônā bheste jābe. If it rains, our plan for an outing will fall through.
Past (he/she …): সেই পরিকল্পনা ভেস্তে গেল, sei pôrikôlpônā bheste gelô.

The word, taken apart.
ভেস্তে যাওয়া (bheste jāoyā) joins ভেস্তে, a word heard only in this phrase, to যাওয়া, "to go"; where ভেস্তে comes from is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: বিশৃঙ্খল.
বিশৃঙ্খল — bishringkhôl — "disorderly; in chaos".
ঝড়ের পরে বাজারটা বিশৃঙ্খল ছিল। Jhôṛer pôre bājārṭā bishringkhôl chhilô. After the storm the market was in chaos.

The word, taken apart.
বিশৃঙ্খল (bishringkhôl) is a tatsama, Sanskrit viśṛṅkhala, "unfettered", from śṛṅkhala, "a chain" (Monier-Williams): with nothing holding it in order.

Lesson 4 of 5.
খসা (khôsā) — to come loose; to fall off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for fruitless, then the Bengali for in chaos.

You'll want to know: খসা.
খসা — khôsā — "to come loose; to fall off".
জামার একটা বোতাম খসল। Jāmār ekṭā botām khôslô. A button came off my shirt.
Past (he/she …): সেই ইট খসল, sei iṭ khôslô.

The word, taken apart.
খসা (khôsā) comes from a reconstructed khasati, "slips"; CDIAL lists Bengali khasā, "to fall off, to slip away" (CDIAL 3856).
