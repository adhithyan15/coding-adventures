## You'll want to know: rescrībō, rescrībere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C551-CAPS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rescrībō, rescrībere** — "to write back, reply".

> Epistulam tuam accēpī et statim rescrībō.
>
> *I have received your letter and I am writing back at once.*

*rescrībō, rescrībere, rescrīpsī, rescrīptum*. An emperor's written answer was a *rescrīptum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**rescrībō** is built on *re-* "back" and *scrībō* "to write" (Lewis & Short). English *rescript* comes from its participle.
