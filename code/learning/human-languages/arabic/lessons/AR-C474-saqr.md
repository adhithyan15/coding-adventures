## You'll want to know: صقر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C474-SAQR474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صقر** — *ṣaqr* — "a falcon".

> طار صقر فوق الوادي.
>
> *ṭāra ṣaqr fawqa al-wādī.*
>
> A falcon flew over the valley.

Plural: صقور, *ṣuqūr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صقر** (*ṣaqr*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ṣ-q-r*, "a bird of prey". It is an old native name.
