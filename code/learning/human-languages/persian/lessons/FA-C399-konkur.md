## You'll want to know: کنکور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C399-KONKUR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کنکور** (*konkur*) — "the university entrance exam".

> برادرم برای کنکور درس میخواند.
>
> *barâdaram barâye konkur dars mikhânad.*
>
> My brother is studying for the entrance exam.

The national exam for university places in Iran.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کنکور** was borrowed from French *concours* "competition" (EtymDB), from Latin *concursus* "a running together", built on *currere* "to run" (Lewis & Short).
