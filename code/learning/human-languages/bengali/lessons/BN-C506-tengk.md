# ট্যাংক (ṭængk) — a water tank

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C505-ALO505-04, BN-LEX-C505-ALO505-05] -->

## You'll want to know: ট্যাংক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C506-TANK506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ট্যাংক** — *ṭængk* — "a water tank".

> ছাদের ট্যাংকে জল নেই।
>
> *Chhāder ṭængke jôl nei.*
>
> There's no water in the tank on the roof.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ট্যাংক** (*ṭængk*) is borrowed from English *tank*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
