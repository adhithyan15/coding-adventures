## You'll want to know: ਡਾਕੂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C315-BANDIT315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕੂ** — *ḍākū* — "a bandit".

> ਪਿਛਲੇ ਸਾਲ ਸਾਡੇ ਕਸਬੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਡਾਕੂ ਆਏ।
>
> *pichhle sāl sāḍe kasbe vich ḍākū āe.*
>
> Last year bandits came to our small town.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕੂ** (*ḍākū*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍākka*, "a robber"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍākū*, "robber", under it (CDIAL 5543).
