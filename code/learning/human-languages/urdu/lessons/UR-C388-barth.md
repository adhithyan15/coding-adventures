## You'll want to know: برتھ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C388-SIGNAL388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**برتھ** — *barth* — "a berth (on a train)".

> میری برتھ اوپر ہے۔
>
> *merī barth ūpar hai.*
>
> My berth is the top one.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برتھ** (*barth*) is borrowed from English *berth*: a bed on a night train.
