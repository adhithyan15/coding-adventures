## You'll want to know: la valutazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C383-BOCCIATURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la valutazione** — "the assessment, the evaluation".

> La valutazione finale dipende anche dai compiti a casa.
>
> *The final assessment also depends on the homework.*

Built on *valutare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*valutazione* is built on *valutare* "to assess", from *valuta* "value" and *valere* "to be worth", inherited from Latin *valēre* "to be strong, to be worth" (Lewis & Short).
