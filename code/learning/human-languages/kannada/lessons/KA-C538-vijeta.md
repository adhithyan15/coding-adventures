## You'll want to know: ವಿಜೇತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C538-PADAKA538-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಜೇತ** — *vijēta* — "a winner".

> ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆಯ ವಿಜೇತರಿಗೆ ಬಹುಮಾನ ಕೊಟ್ಟರು.
>
> *spardheya vijētarige bahumāna koṭṭaru.*
>
> They gave prizes to the winners of the competition.

Plural: ವಿಜೇತರು, *vijētaru*.

ವಿಜಯ, victory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಜೇತ** (*vijēta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vijetṛ*, "a vanquisher, a conqueror" (Monier-Williams).
