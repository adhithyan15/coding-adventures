Mandarin Chinese, chapter 248: A New Term.
7 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
学期 (xuéqī) — school term; semester.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for souvenir, then the Mandarin for to recall.

You'll want to know: 学期.
学期 — xuéqī — "school term; semester".
这个学期很长 zhège xuéqī hěn cháng — This term is a long one.

The word, taken apart.
学期 (xuéqī) is built from 学 'to study' and 期 'a set time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, haewk and gi (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to meet' and 'a limit'. Middle Chinese gi would regularly give a rising tone, qí; the level tone of qī is irregular.

Lesson 3 of 7.
校长 (xiàozhǎng) — head teacher; principal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a school term, then the Mandarin for a year group at school.

You'll want to know: 校长.
校长 — xiàozhǎng — "head teacher; principal".
他是我们学校的校长 tā shì wǒmen xuéxiào de xiàozhǎng — He's the head of our school.
The new character, in pieces: 校 (xiào) — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left the tree piece, four strokes, as on the left of 机; on the right a dot and a bar, two short strokes under them, then a long stroke falling to the left crossed by a long stroke falling to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
校长 (xiàozhǎng) is built from 校 (xiào) 'a school' and 长 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, haewH and trjangX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'big'; it lists the first in the title of an army officer, and the sense 'school' is not given there, so how it came is not settled.

机 学.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — jī, xué]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — the tree piece, then a lid and a cross.
校 (xiào).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, the tree piece, four strokes. A short bar. A vertical down through it. A stroke falling to the left from the crossing. Then a short dot to the right: on the left of a character the last stroke is a dot, not a long sweep.
The right piece, six strokes. A dot on top and a bar under it. Two short strokes under the bar, the left one falling to the left, the right one a dot. Then a long stroke falling to the left and, across it, a long stroke falling to the right to the foot.
4 + 6 = ten.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 校 (xiào), all ten, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 校 (xiào)? (Ten.) What is the left piece? (The tree piece, four strokes, as in 机.) What are the last two strokes? (A long fall to the left, crossed by a long fall to the right.)

Lesson 5 of 7.
高中 (gāozhōng) — senior high school.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a year group at school, then the Mandarin for a head teacher.

You'll want to know: 高中.
高中 — gāozhōng — "senior high school".
我们学校有高中 wǒmen xuéxiào yǒu gāozhōng — Our school has a senior high section.
The new character, in pieces: 高 (gāo) — ten strokes: a dot and a long bar, then 口; under them a frame open at the foot, with a small 口 inside it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
高中 (gāozhōng) is built from 高 (gāo) 'high' and 中 'middle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kaw and trjuwng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'up' and 'lofty': the higher middle school, a short form of a longer name.

口 中.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — kǒu, zhōng]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — a lid, 口, then a frame around a small 口.
高 (gāo).
Three pieces, top to bottom.
The lid, two strokes. A dot, then a long bar across.
The middle piece, 口, three strokes. A short vertical, a stroke that runs right and turns down, then the bar that closes the foot.
The bottom piece, five strokes. A vertical down the left. A bar across the top that turns down the right side and hooks to the left at the foot. Then a small 口 inside the frame, three strokes as before.
2 + 3 + 5 = ten.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 高 (gāo)? (Ten.) How many 口 does it hold? (Two: one in the middle, a small one inside the frame.) Is the frame closed at the foot? (No, it is open.)
