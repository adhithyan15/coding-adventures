## You'll want to know: ગૌરવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C359-IJJAT359-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ગૌરવ** — *gaurav* — "dignity; pride in something worthy".

> અમને અમારા ગામનું ગૌરવ છે.
>
> *amne amārā gāmnũ gaurav che.*
>
> We are proud of our village.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગૌરવ** (*gaurav*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *gaurava*, "weight, importance, dignity" (Monier-Williams).
