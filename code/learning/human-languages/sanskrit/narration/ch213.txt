Sanskrit, chapter 213: At once, Instantly, Formerly, Afterwards, As long as.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सद्यः (sadyaḥ) — at once.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a dream, then the Sanskrit for satisfaction.

You'll want to know: सद्यः.
सद्यः — sadyaḥ — "at once".

The word, taken apart.
sadyaḥ is built on sa-, "same", and dyu, "day" (MW; CDIAL 13123): "on the same day", and so "at once". It is already in the Rigveda.

तत्क्षणम् — tatkṣaṇam — "instantly".

The word, taken apart.
tatkṣaṇam is built on tat, "that", and kṣaṇa, "moment" (MW): "at that very moment". CDIAL 5640 traces Hindi takhan, "then", to it, and CDIAL 3642 links kṣaṇa to akṣi, "eye", as the twinkling of an eye.

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्राक् (prāk) — formerly, before.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for at once, then the Sanskrit for instantly.

You'll want to know: प्राक्.
प्राक् — prāk — "formerly, before".

The word, taken apart.
prāk is a form of prāñc, "turned forward, facing", built on pra, "forth", and √añc, "to bend, turn" (MW). What lies forward is "before" in time and, facing the sunrise, the east.

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनन्तरम् (anantaram) — afterwards, next.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: अनन्तरम्.
अनन्तरम् — anantaram — "afterwards, next".

The word, taken apart.
anantaram is built on an-, "not", and antara, "interval" (MW): "with no interval", and so "immediately after" (CDIAL 287). CDIAL lists Marathi nãtar, "afterwards", under it, asking whether Marathi took it from Sanskrit.

Lesson 5 of 5.
यावत् (yāvat) — as long as, until.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for formerly, then the Sanskrit for afterwards.

You'll want to know: यावत्.
यावत् — yāvat — "as long as, until".

The word, taken apart.
yāvat is built on the relative stem ya-, "which" (MW), and MW pairs it with its correlative tāvat, "so long". It is a Rigvedic word for "as long as, until" (CDIAL 10474).
