## You'll want to know: बी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C272-PHAGAT272-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बी** — *bī* — "also".

> हूं बी आसूं।
>
> *hū̃ bī āsū̃.*
>
> I'll come too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बी** (*bī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *api ca*, "and also"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bī*, "also" (CDIAL 486). It follows the word it adds.
