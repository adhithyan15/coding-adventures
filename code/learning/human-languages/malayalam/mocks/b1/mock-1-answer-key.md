## Writing

Task 1 must address Jayan, identify a genuine benefit and practical problem,
propose a concrete change, and request a response in 50–70 independently
produced Malayalam words. Task 2 must be a coherent 130–170-word narrative and
reflection covering all six cues, with register suitable for a general reader.

## Speaking

Use follow-up prompts that require reasons and examples, without supplying
language. Give both candidates equal opportunity in collaborative planning and
require a real joint decision. Preparation notes may contain Malayalam
keywords, not scripted sentences. Score the complete recording.
