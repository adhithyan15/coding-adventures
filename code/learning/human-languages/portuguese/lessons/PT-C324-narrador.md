## You'll want to know: o narrador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C324-CONTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o narrador** — "the narrator".

> O narrador é um menino de dez anos.
>
> *The narrator is a ten-year-old boy.*

A woman is *a narradora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narrador* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nārrātor* "a teller, a narrator" (EtymDB), from *nārrāre* "to tell".
