Hindi, chapter 417: Which Way?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दिशा (diśā) — a direction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to spend (time), then the Hindi for to pass (time).

You'll want to know: दिशा.
दिशा — diśā — "a direction".
स्टेशन किस दिशा में है? sṭeśan kis diśā meṁ hai? Which direction is the station in?
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
दिशा (diśā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit diśā, "a direction, a quarter of the sky" (CDIAL 6339); the same entry lists Hindi dis, the form worn down in speech.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तरफ़ (taraf) — a side (of a place); towards (with की).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to pass (time), then the Hindi for a direction.

You'll want to know: तरफ़.
तरफ़ — taraf — "a side (of a place); towards (with की)".
नदी की दूसरी तरफ़ एक मंदिर है। nadī kī dūsrī taraf ek mandir hai. There is a temple on the other side of the river.
Feminine.
की तरफ़ (taraf), towards: स्टेशन की तरफ़ (taraf) चलिए, walk towards the station.

The word, taken apart.
तरफ़ (taraf) is usually traced to Arabic ṭaraf, "a side, an edge", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ओर (or) — a side; towards (with की).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a direction, then the Hindi for a side (of a place).

You'll want to know: ओर.
ओर — or — "a side; towards (with की)".
हम पहाड़ की ओर चल पड़े। ham pahāṛ kī or cal paṛe. We set off towards the mountain.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ओर (or) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit avarā, "the near side" (CDIAL 812), which lists Hindi or, "side, nearness". It is the inherited partner of तरफ़ (taraf): की ओर, towards.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मार्ग (mārg) — a route; a way (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a side (of a place), then the Hindi for towards.

You'll want to know: मार्ग.
मार्ग — mārg — "a route; a way (formal)".
यह बस किस मार्ग से जाती है? yah bas kis mārg se jātī hai? Which route does this bus take?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मार्ग (mārg) is a tatsama, Sanskrit mārga, first the track of a wild animal and then any road or way (Monier-Williams); street signs and bus boards use it, and it is the second half of राजमार्ग.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रुख़ (rukh) — a direction; the way something faces.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for towards, then the Hindi for a route.

You'll want to know: रुख़.
रुख़ — rukh — "a direction; the way something faces".
शाम को हवा का रुख़ बदल गया। śām ko havā kā rukh badal gayā. In the evening the wind changed direction.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
रुख़ (rukh) is usually traced to Persian rukh, "the face, the cheek": the way a face is turned, and so the direction of the wind, a road or a person's mood.
