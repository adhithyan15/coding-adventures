## You'll want to know: der Igel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C458-WILDTIERE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Igel** — "the hedgehog".

> Im Garten wohnt ein Igel unter dem Laub.
>
> *A hedgehog lives under the leaves in the garden.*

Same form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Igel* is inherited through Middle High German *igel* from Old High German *igil* and Proto-Germanic *igilaz* (EtymDB); EtymDB lists Lithuanian *ežỹs* 'hedgehog' as a distant cousin.
