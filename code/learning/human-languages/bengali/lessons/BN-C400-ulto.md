## You'll want to know: উল্টো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C400-THIKANA400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উল্টো** — *ulṭo* — "opposite; the other way; reversed".

> আমরা ভুল করে উল্টো দিকের বাসে উঠলাম।
>
> *āmrā bhul kore ulṭo diker bāse uṭhlām.*
>
> By mistake we got on the bus going the other way.

উল্টো দিকে, in the opposite direction; উল্টো দিকের দোকান, the shop across the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উল্টো** (*ulṭo*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ulṭo*, "reversed", under the reconstructed *ullaṭati*, "turns over" (CDIAL 2368), the same root as উল্টানো, to overturn.
