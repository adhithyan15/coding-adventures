Gujarati, chapter 320: War Comes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
યુદ્ધ (yuddh) — a war.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a statue, then the Gujarati for to settle.

You'll want to know: યુદ્ધ.
યુદ્ધ — yuddh — "a war".
બે રાજાઓ વચ્ચે યુદ્ધ થયું. be rājāo vacce yuddh thayũ. War broke out between two kings.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
યુદ્ધ (yuddh) is a tatsama, Sanskrit yuddha, "fought", and as a noun "a battle" (Monier-Williams). It is neuter: યુદ્ધ થયું, there was a war.

The word, taken apart.
સૈનિક (sainik) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sainika, "belonging to an army" (Monier-Williams), built on sēnā, the word behind સેના (senā): one who serves in it.

Warm-up.

દુશ્મને રાત્રે કિલ્લો ઘેર્યો. duśmane rātre killo gheryo. The enemy surrounded the fort at night.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
દુશ્મન (duśman) is usually traced to Persian dušman, "an enemy". As the doer it takes એ: દુશ્મને કિલ્લો ઘેર્યો.

Lesson 5 of 5.
આક્રમણ (ākramaṇ) — an invasion; an attack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a soldier, then the Gujarati for an enemy.

You'll want to know: આક્રમણ.
આક્રમણ — ākramaṇ — "an invasion; an attack".
દુશ્મને સવારે આક્રમણ કર્યું. duśmane savāre ākramaṇ karyũ. The enemy invaded in the morning.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
આક્રમણ (ākramaṇ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit ākramaṇa, "stepping upon" (CDIAL 1016), and so marching into another's land. It is done: આક્રમણ કર્યું.
