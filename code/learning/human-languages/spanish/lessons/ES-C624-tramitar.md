## You'll want to know: tramitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C624-PERMISO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**tramitar** — "to process, to deal with (paperwork)".

> Tardan un mes en tramitar el permiso.
>
> *It takes them a month to process the permit.*

Formal, for official paperwork.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tramitar* is built on *trámite*, a learned borrowing from Latin *trames, tramitis* 'a path, a way across' (EtymDB).
