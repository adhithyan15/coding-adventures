## You'll want to know: दुख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C256-DAR256-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दुख** — *dukh* — "sorrow; grief".

> गाय मरी, घर मांय दुख है।
>
> *gāy marī, ghar mā̃y dukh hai.*
>
> The cow died; there is sorrow in the house.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुख** (*dukh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *duḥkha*, "difficulty, pain"; CDIAL lists Marwari *dukh*, "sorrow", under it (CDIAL 6375).
