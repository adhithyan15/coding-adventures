Sanskrit, chapter 75: Getting There.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वाहनम् (vāhanam) — a vehicle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a shop, then the Sanskrit for a market.

You'll want to know: वाहनम्.
वाहनम् (vāhanam) — "a vehicle", anything that carries you on a यात्रा.

What you've built.
The general word.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vāhanam]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vāhanam, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say vāhanam]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for a shop, then the Sanskrit for a market, then say vāhanam again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does वाहनम् (vāhanam) mean? ("A vehicle".) Say it once more.
