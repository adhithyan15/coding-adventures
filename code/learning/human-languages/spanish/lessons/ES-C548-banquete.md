## You'll want to know: el banquete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C548-BANQUETE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el banquete** — "the banquet, the wedding meal".

> Después de la boda hubo un banquete.
>
> *After the wedding there was a banquet.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*banquete* is usually traced to French *banquet* or Italian *banchetto*, a small form of *banco* 'bench', a Germanic word. The feast was named after the little benches the guests sat on.
