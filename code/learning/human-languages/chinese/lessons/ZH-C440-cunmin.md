## You'll want to know: cūnmín
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C440-HILLS440-04]; assesses=[] -->

*cūnmín* — "villager".

> *zhèr de cūnmín dōu hěn rèqíng* — The villagers here are all very friendly.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cūnmín* is built from 村 'a village' and *mín* 'the people', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshwon* and *mjin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
