Telugu, chapter 541: Stage and Screen.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
నటన (naṭana) — acting; a performance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to compose, then the Telugu for a translation.

You'll want to know: నటన.
నటన — naṭana — "acting; a performance".
ఆ సినిమాలో ఆమె నటన అద్భుతం. ā sinimālō āme naṭana adbhutaṁ. Her acting in that film is wonderful.
నటుడు is an actor; నటించు is to act.

The word, taken apart.
నటన (naṭana) is borrowed from Sanskrit naṭana, "dancing, dance, pantomime" (Monier-Williams).

ఆ సినిమాలో కథానాయకుడు ఒక రైతు. ā sinimālō kathānāyakuḍu oka raitu. In that film the hero is a farmer.
