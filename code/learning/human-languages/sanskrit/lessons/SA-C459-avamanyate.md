## You'll want to know: अवमन्यते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C459-ADARA459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अवमन्यते** — *avamanyate* — "despises".

> धनिकः दरिद्रम् अवमन्यते, तत् अनुचितम्।
>
> *dhanikaḥ daridram avamanyate, tat anucitam.*
>
> The rich man despises the poor man; that is wrong.

Past (he …): सः दरिद्रम् अवमतवान्, *saḥ daridram avamatavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवमन्यते** (*avamanyate*) is built from *ava*, "down", and the root √man, "to think": to think down on; MW gives "to despise, treat contemptuously" (MW).
