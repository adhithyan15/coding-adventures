## You'll want to know: la indicación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C670-INDICAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la indicación** — "the instruction, the direction (how to get somewhere)".

> Sigue las indicaciones del hotel.
>
> *Follow the hotel’s directions.*

In the plural, *las indicaciones* are directions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indicación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *indicātiō* (EtymDB), from *indicāre* "to point out".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
