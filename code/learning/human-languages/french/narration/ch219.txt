French, chapter 219: Boring, Fun, Strange, Different, Same.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ennuyeux / ennuyeuse — boring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for nervous, then the French for quiet.

You'll want to know: ennuyeux / ennuyeuse.
ennuyeux / ennuyeuse — "boring".

The word, taken apart.
ennuyeux — built from the noun beside s'ennuyer, with -eux, "full of": full of boredom.
