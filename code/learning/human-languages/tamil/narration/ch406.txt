Tamil, chapter 406: Fuel and Tyres.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
எரிபொருள் (eriporuḷ) — fuel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a lounge, then the Tamil for an interval.

You'll want to know: எரிபொருள்.
எரிபொருள் — eriporuḷ — "fuel".
எங்கள் காரில் எரிபொருள் குறைவாக இருக்கிறது. eṅkaḷ kāril eriporuḷ kuṟaivāka irukkiṟatu. Our car is low on fuel.
எரி, to burn, + பொருள், a thing: a thing that burns.

The word, taken apart.
எரிபொருள் (eriporuḷ) is built from எரி, "to burn" (DEDR 811), and பொருள், "a thing" (DEDR 4544).

Lesson 4 of 5.
தீர் (tīr) — to run out; to be used up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for petrol, then the Tamil for a tyre.

You'll want to know: தீர்.
தீர் — tīr — "to run out; to be used up".
நடுவழியில் பெட்ரோல் தீர்ந்தது. naṭuvaḻiyil peṭrōl tīrntatu. The petrol ran out halfway.
Past (it …): தீர்ந்தது, tīrntatu.
தீர்வு, a solution, comes from the same verb, which also means to be settled.

The word, taken apart.
தீர் (tīr) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3278), "to come to an end, to be completed".

Lesson 5 of 5.
தேய் (tēy) — to wear down; to rub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a tyre, then the Tamil for to run out.

You'll want to know: தேய்.
தேய் — tēy — "to wear down; to rub".
இந்த டயர் நன்றாகத் தேய்ந்துவிட்டது. inta ṭayar naṉṟākat tēyntuviṭṭatu. This tyre has worn right down.
Past (it …): தேய்ந்தது, tēyntatu.
With an object it means to rub or scrub: பாத்திரம் தேய் (tēy), to scrub a pot.

The word, taken apart.
தேய் (tēy) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3458), "to wear away by rubbing, to wane".
