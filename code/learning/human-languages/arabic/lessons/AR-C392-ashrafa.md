## You'll want to know: أشرف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C392-MUHADIR392-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أشرف** — *ashrafa* — "to supervise".

> أشرف المعلم على الرحلة.
>
> *ashrafa al-muʿallim ʿalā ar-riḥla.*
>
> The teacher supervised the trip.

Present: يشرف, *yushrif*.

It takes على: أشرف على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أشرف** (*ashrafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-f*, "to be high, noble" (Lane). It is Form IV, to look down from a height: to oversee, to supervise.
