Russian, chapter 32: Pain, Illness, Medicine, Doctor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
боль (bol) — pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the face, then the Russian for blood.

You'll want to know: боль.
боль (bol) — "pain".

What you've built.
One more word for pain, illness, medicine, doctor.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bol]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bol, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bol]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for the face, then the Russian for blood, then say bol again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does боль (bol) mean? ("Pain".) Say it once more.
