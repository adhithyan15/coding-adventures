## You'll want to know: ಸೇವೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C463-SEVE463-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೇವೆ** — *sēve* — "a service".

> ಈ ಹೋಟೆಲ್ನ ಸೇವೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *ī hōṭelna sēve tumbā cennāgide.*
>
> The service in this hotel is very good.

ಕೊಠಡಿ ಸೇವೆ, room service; ಬಸ್ ಸೇವೆ, a bus service.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೇವೆ** (*sēve*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sēvā*, "attendance on, service" (CDIAL 13595); Kannada ends it in -*e*.
