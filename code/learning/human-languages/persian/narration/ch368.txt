Persian, chapter 368: Looking closely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تحلیل (tahlil) — an analysis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for nevertheless, then the Persian for although.

You'll want to know: تحلیل.
تحلیل (tahlil) — "an analysis".
تحلیل او از بازی خیلی خوب بود. tahlil-e u az bâzi kheyli khub bud. His analysis of the game was very good.
Plural tahlilhâ. Tahlilgar — an analyst.

The word, taken apart.
تحلیل (tahlil) is usually traced to Arabic taḥlīl "analysis", a verbal noun usually explained as untying or breaking something into its parts.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تحلیل کردن (tahlil kardan) — to analyse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for although, then the Persian for an analysis.

You'll want to know: تحلیل کردن.
تحلیل کردن (tahlil kardan) — "to analyse".
استاد نتیجه آزمایش را تحلیل کرد. ostâd natije-ye âzmâyesh râ tahlil kard. The professor analysed the result of the experiment.
Tahlil + kardan (stem kon-). The object takes râ.

The word, taken apart.
تحلیل کردن (tahlil kardan) joins tahlil, usually traced to Arabic taḥlīl "analysis", to the inherited Persian verb kardan "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.
برداشت (bardâsht) — an impression, an interpretation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an analysis, then the Persian for to analyse.

You'll want to know: برداشت.
برداشت (bardâsht) — "an impression, an interpretation".
برداشت من این بود که او ناراحت است. bardâsht-e man in bud ke u nârâhat ast. My impression was that he was upset.
The past stem of bardâshtan "to pick up": what you take from something. It is also a harvest.

The word, taken apart.
برداشت (bardâsht) is native Persian, built from bar "up, on" + dâsht, the past stem of dâshtan "to have, to hold". It is what you pick up from a text or a meeting.

Lesson 4 of 5.
نتیجه گیری (natije giri) — a conclusion (drawn).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to analyse, then the Persian for an impression.

You'll want to know: نتیجه گیری.
نتیجه گیری (natije giri) — "a conclusion (drawn)".
نتیجه گیری او منطقی بود. natije giri-ye u manteqi bud. His conclusion was logical.
Natije "result" + giri "taking", from gereftan. Also the last part of an essay.

The word, taken apart.
نتیجه گیری (natije giri) joins natije, from Arabic natīja "result" (EtymDB), to giri "taking", built on gir-, the present stem of the inherited Persian verb gereftan "to take".

Lesson 5 of 5.
اشاره کردن (eshâre kardan) — to mention, to refer to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an impression, then the Persian for a conclusion.

You'll want to know: اشاره کردن.
اشاره کردن (eshâre kardan) — "to mention, to refer to".
او در نامه به این مشکل اشاره کرد. u dar nâme be in moshkel eshâre kard. In the letter he mentioned this problem.
Takes be: be chizi eshâre kardan. Literally to make a sign towards something.

The word, taken apart.
اشاره was borrowed from Arabic ishāra "a sign, a gesture" (EtymDB); with the Persian verb kardan it means to refer to something, in a gesture or in words.
