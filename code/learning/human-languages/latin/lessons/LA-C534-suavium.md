## You'll want to know: suāvium, suāviī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C534-OSCU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**suāvium, suāviī** — "a kiss on the lips".

> Puella iuvenī suāvium dat.
>
> *The girl gives the young man a kiss on the lips.*

Neuter, second declension; also spelt *sāvium*. A word of comedy and love letters.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**suāvium** is built on *suāvis* "sweet" (Lewis & Short).
