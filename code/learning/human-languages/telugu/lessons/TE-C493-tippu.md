## You'll want to know: తిప్పు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C493-TIRUGU493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తిప్పు** — *tippu* — "to turn round; to twist".

> డ్రైవరు బండిని వెనక్కి తిప్పాడు.
>
> *ḍraivaru baṇḍini venakki tippāḍu.*
>
> The driver turned the cart back.

Past (I …): తిప్పాను, *tippānu*.

The causative of తిరుగు, to turn: to make something turn. తాళంచెవి తిప్పు, to turn a key.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తిప్పు** (*tippu*) is native Dravidian, usually traced to the family of తిరుగు, "to turn, to go round" (DEDR 3246), as its causative.
