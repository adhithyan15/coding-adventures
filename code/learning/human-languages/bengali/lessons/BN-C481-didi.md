## You'll want to know: দিদি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C481-MA481-03]; assesses=[] -->

**দিদি** — *didi* — "an elder sister".

> দিদি আমাকে সাঁতার শেখাল।
>
> *didi āmāke sā̃tār shekhālô.*
>
> My elder sister taught me to swim.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দিদি** (*didi*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as an elder sister, under a reconstructed nursery word (CDIAL 6327).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
