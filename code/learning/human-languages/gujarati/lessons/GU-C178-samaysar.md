## You'll want to know: સમયસર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C178-START178-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સમયસર** — *samaysar* — "on time".

> **હું સમયસર આવીશ.** — *hũ samaysar āvīś* — "I will come on time."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સમયસર** (*samaysar*) is built from the taught *samay*, "time", a tatsama from Sanskrit *samaya*, and the ending *-sar*, "in keeping with".
