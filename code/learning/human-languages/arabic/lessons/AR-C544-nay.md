## You'll want to know: ناي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C544-ALAT544-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ناي** — *nāy* — "a reed flute".

> سمعنا صوت الناي في الليل.
>
> *samiʿnā ṣawt an-nāy fī al-layl.*
>
> We heard the sound of the reed flute in the night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ناي** (*nāy*) was borrowed from Persian *nāy*, "a pipe, a flute", whose Middle Persian ancestor also meant a reed (EtymDB): the flute is a hollow reed.
