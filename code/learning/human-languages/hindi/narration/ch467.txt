6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
कपटी (kapṭī) — deceitful; two-faced.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a gentleman, then the Hindi for harsh.

You'll want to know: कपटी.
कपटी — kapṭī — "deceitful; two-faced".
वह आदमी मीठा बोलता है, लेकिन बहुत कपटी है। vah ādmī mīṭhā boltā hai, lekin bahut kapṭī hai. That man talks sweetly, but he is very two-faced.

The word, taken apart.
कपटी (kapṭī) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kapaṭin, the adjective of kapaṭa, "fraud, deceit" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 6.
भोला (bholā) — guileless; trusting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for deceitful, then the Hindi for crafty.

You'll want to know: भोला.
भोला — bholā — "guileless; trusting".
मेरा भाई बहुत भोला है, हर आदमी की बात मान लेता है। merā bhāī bahut bholā hai, har ādmī kī bāt mān letā hai. My brother is very trusting; he believes whatever anyone tells him.

The word, taken apart.
भोला (bholā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi bholā, "foolish, guileless", under bhulla, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9539). In Hindi it is mostly kind: simple, trusting, without tricks.

Lesson 4 of 6.

You'll want to know: विश्वासघात.
विश्वासघात — viśvāsghāt — "a betrayal of trust".
दोस्त का विश्वासघात अरुण कभी नहीं भूलेगा। dost kā viśvāsghāt Aruṇ kabhī nahīṃ bhūlegā. Arun will never forget his friend's betrayal.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विश्वासघात (viśvāsghāt) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit viśvāsa-ghāta, "destruction of confidence, violation of trust" (Monier-Williams): viśvās, "trust", and ghāt, "a blow".

मक्कार — makkār — "crooked; a cheat".
वह दुकानदार मक्कार है, हमेशा कम तोलता है। vah dukāndār makkār hai, hameśā kam toltā hai. That shopkeeper is a crook; he always gives short weight.

The word, taken apart.
मक्कार (makkār) is usually traced to Arabic makkār, "crafty, wily", which reached Hindi through Persian.
