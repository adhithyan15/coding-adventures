## You'll want to know: qí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C106-STOP106-05]; assesses=[] -->

*qí* — "to ride (a bike or a horse)".

> *wǒ qí zìxíngchē qù xuéxiào* — I ride my bike to school.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qí* is inherited from Middle Chinese *gje* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to sit astride a horse'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
