## You'll want to know: قیام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C403-SARAE403-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قیام** — *qiyām* — "a stay".

> سرائے میں ہمارا قیام تین دن کا تھا۔
>
> *sarāe meṅ hamārā qiyām tīn din kā thā.*
>
> We stayed at the inn for three days.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قیام** (*qiyām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qiyām*, "standing, remaining"; in Urdu it is a stay somewhere.
