## You'll want to know: かわかす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C161-LAUNDRY161-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かわかす** — *kawakasu* — "to dry (something)".

> **ふとんを かわかします。** — *futon o kawakashimasu* — "I dry the futon."

Polite **かわかします**, *kawakashimasu*; a godan verb. Its intransitive partner is **かわく**, *kawaku*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かわかす** (*kawakasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as *kawaku*, "to get dry"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
