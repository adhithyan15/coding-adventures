## You'll want to know: جلد بازی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C370-JALDBAZI370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جلد بازی** — *jald-bāzī* — "haste; rushing".

> اگر تم نے جلد بازی کی تو حادثہ ہو گا۔
>
> *agar tum ne jald-bāzī kī to hādsā ho gā.*
>
> If you rush, there will be an accident.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جلد بازی** (*jald-bāzī*) is usually traced to Persian *jaldbāzī*, "haste": *jald*, "quick", and *bāzī*, "play". Playing at speed.
