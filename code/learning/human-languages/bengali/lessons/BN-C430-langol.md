## You'll want to know: লাঙল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C430-KHET430-02]; assesses=[] -->

**লাঙল** — *lāngôl* — "a plough".

> গরু লাঙল টানে।
>
> *gôru lāngôl ṭāne.*
>
> The cattle pull the plough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লাঙল** (*lāngôl*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *lāṅal*, "a plough", under Sanskrit *lā́ṅgala*, "a plough" (CDIAL 11006).
