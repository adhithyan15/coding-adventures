Two letters, both already yours: **د ل** — *d*, *l*.

## The word, taken apart — one of the family's oldest survivors
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-ETYMON-DEL]; assesses=[] -->

**دل** continues Middle Persian **dil**, from the ancient Indo-European word
for heart, \**ḱḗr* — the same root English wore down to **heart**, Armenian
kept as **sirt**, and Sanskrit built into **hṛdaya**. Persian uses **دل** far
beyond anatomy, the way English leans on *heart* for courage and feeling
rather than only the organ — a later chapter's word for "want" will lean on
it too.
