Italian, chapter 286: Carta stampata.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'edizione — the edition; the news bulletin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the press release, then the Italian for the denial.

You'll want to know: l'edizione.
l'edizione — "the edition; the news bulletin".
L'edizione della sera ha una foto in prima pagina. The evening edition has a photo on the front page.
Un'edizione straordinaria — a special edition. On TV, l'edizione delle venti is the eight o'clock news.

The word, taken apart.
edizione is a learned borrowing from Latin ēditiō "a putting forth, a publishing" (Lewis & Short), from ēdere "to give out".

Borrowed from English; plural unchanged: gli scoop.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the caption, then the Italian for the scoop.

You'll want to know: la censura.
la censura — "censorship".
Il film ha avuto problemi con la censura. The film had trouble with the censors.
Also a formal rebuke: un voto di censura.

The word, taken apart.
censura is a learned borrowing from Latin cēnsūra "the office of the censor" (Lewis & Short), from cēnsēre "to assess, to judge".

censurare is built on censura "censorship", from Latin cēnsūra and cēnsēre "to assess, to judge" (Lewis & Short).
