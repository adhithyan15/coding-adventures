Marathi, chapter 397: Held Up on the Way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विलंब (vilamb) — a delay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a goods train, then the Marathi for to rattle.

You'll want to know: विलंब.
विलंब — vilamb — "a delay".
पावसामुळे गाडीला विलंब झाला. pāvsāmuḷe gāḍīlā vilamb jhālā. The train was delayed because of the rain.
Masculine.
विलंब (vilamb) होणे, to be delayed: what is late takes -ला, गाडीला विलंब (vilamb) झाला.

The word, taken apart.
विलंब (vilamb) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vilamba, "hanging down; slowness, delay" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
खोळंबणे (khoḷambṇe) — to be held up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rattle, then the Marathi for a delay.

You'll want to know: खोळंबणे.
खोळंबणे — khoḷambṇe — "to be held up".
मी स्टेशनवर दोन तास खोळंबलो. mī sṭeśanvar don tās khoḷamblo. I was held up at the station for two hours.
Past (he …): तो खोळंबला, to khoḷamblā.
Work can be held up too: काम खोळंबलं, the work got held up.

The word, taken apart.
खोळंबणे (khoḷambṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled; it says that someone or something is stuck waiting.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ताटकळणे (tāṭkaḷṇe) — to be kept waiting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a delay, then the Marathi for to be held up.

You'll want to know: ताटकळणे.
ताटकळणे — tāṭkaḷṇe — "to be kept waiting".
पाहुणे दारात बराच वेळ ताटकळले. pāhuṇe dārāt barāc veḷ tāṭkaḷle. The guests were kept waiting at the door for a long time.
Past (he …): तो ताटकळला, to tāṭkaḷlā.
It stresses a long, tiring wait; a woman says मी ताटकळले.

The word, taken apart.
ताटकळणे (tāṭkaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for waiting a long time against your will; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
रखडणे (rakhaḍṇe) — to be stalled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be held up, then the Marathi for to be kept waiting.

You'll want to know: रखडणे.
रखडणे — rakhaḍṇe — "to be stalled".
पुलाचं काम दोन वर्षं रखडलं. pulācaṁ kām don varṣaṁ rakhaḍlaṁ. Work on the bridge was stalled for two years.
Past (he …): तो रखडला, to rakhaḍlā.
काम रखडणे (rakhaḍṇe) is the usual pair: work that stopped and drags on.

The word, taken apart.
रखडणे (rakhaḍṇe) is a Marathi verb for work or plans that stop and drag on; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रवाना होणे (ravānā hoṇe) — to set off; to depart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be kept waiting, then the Marathi for to be stalled.

You'll want to know: रवाना होणे.
रवाना होणे — ravānā hoṇe — "to set off; to depart".
शेवटी बस पुण्याकडे रवाना झाली. śevṭī bas puṇyākaḍe ravānā jhālī. At last the bus set off for Pune.
Past (he …): तो रवाना झाला, to ravānā jhālā.
The place you head for takes -कडे or -ला: पुण्याला रवाना होणे (ravānā hoṇe).

The word, taken apart.
रवाना होणे (ravānā hoṇe) joins रवाना, "sent off, on the way", a loan usually traced to Persian ravāna, to the light verb होणे, "to become".
