## You'll want to know: धड़कणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C253-SARDI253-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धड़कणो** — *dhaṛakṇo* — "to throb; to pound (of the heart)".

> म्हारो दिल घणो धड़कै है।
>
> *mhāro dil ghaṇo dhaṛakai hai.*
>
> My heart is pounding hard.

Past (it …): वो दिल धड़क्यो, *vo dil dhaṛakyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धड़कणो** (*dhaṛakṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhaḍ*, "throb, beat". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *dhaṛaknā*, "to throb" (CDIAL 6711).
