## You'll want to know: o esclarecimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C343-ESCLARECER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o esclarecimento** — "the clarification".

> O professor deu um esclarecimento sobre o exame.
>
> *The teacher gave a clarification about the exam.*

*Pedir esclarecimentos* — to ask for clarification.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esclarecimento* is built on *esclarecer* "to clarify" with *-mento*; the verb rests on *claro*, inherited from Latin *clārus* "bright, clear" (Lewis & Short).
