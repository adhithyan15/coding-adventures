# Review — at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C321-AUSADHAM-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-177, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-176, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a remedy". (*auṣadhaṁ*.) Then the three words whose first letters came before it. (*ūru*,
*ōṭu*, *īlōgā*.)
- [YOU WRITE: **ఔ**, **ఊ**, **ఓ** and **ఈ**, once each from memory]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C321-SITU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-BUY-TICKET-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-WHEN-LEAVES-01, TE-LEX-C321-AUSADHAM-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-177, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-176, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-MISSED-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-LOST-REPORT-01, TE-LEX-C320-URU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C320-ASK-WAY-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C320-GIVE-WAY-01, TE-LEX-C320-SUTIGA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: One ticket to my home town, please. At what time does the bus leave?]
- [YOU SAY: Is this seat free? I would like a seat by the window.]
- [YOU SAY: Oh no, the bus has gone. How do I get to the station?]
- [YOU SAY: My bag is lost. My phone was in the bag.]
- [YOU WRITE: **ఔషధం**, **ఊరు**, **ఓటు**, **ఈలోగా**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C321-SITU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-BUY-TICKET-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-WHEN-LEAVES-01, TE-LEX-C321-AUSADHAM-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-MISSED-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-LOST-REPORT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "A seat"? (**సీటు**.) "At what time does it leave?" (**ఎన్ని గంటలకు బయలుదేరుతుంది?**) "It has
gone"? (**వెళ్ళిపోయింది**.) "It is lost"? (**పోయింది**.) "A remedy"? (**ఔషధం**, which opens with **ఔ**.)
