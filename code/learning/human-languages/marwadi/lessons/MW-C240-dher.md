## You'll want to know: ढेर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C240-HARV240-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ढेर** — *ḍher* — "a heap; a pile".

> खेत मांय मोटो ढेर है।
>
> *khet mā̃y moṭo ḍher hai.*
>
> There's a big heap in the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढेर** (*ḍher*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhēra*, "a heap"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *ḍher*. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5599).
