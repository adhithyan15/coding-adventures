## You'll want to know: श्लाघते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C458-DARPA458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**श्लाघते** — *ślāghate* — "boasts".

> धनिकः धनेन श्लाघते।
>
> *dhanikaḥ dhanena ślāghate.*
>
> The rich man boasts of his wealth.

Past (he …): सः धनेन श्लाघितवान्, *saḥ dhanena ślāghitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्लाघते** (*ślāghate*) comes from the root √ślāgh, which MW glosses "to talk confidently, vaunt, boast" and also "to praise" (MW).
