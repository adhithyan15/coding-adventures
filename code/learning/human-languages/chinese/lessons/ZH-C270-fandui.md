## You'll want to know: fǎnduì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C270-AGREE270-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fǎnduì* — "to be against; to oppose".

> *wǒ bù fǎnduì* — I'm not against it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fǎnduì* is built from *fǎn* and 对, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjonX* and *twojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn over' and 'contrary', and the second 'to answer'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
