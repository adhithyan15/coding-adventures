## You'll want to know: فائدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C333-MAQSAD333-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فائدہ** — *fāidā* — "a benefit; an advantage".

> ورزش کا بڑا فائدہ ہے۔
>
> *varzish kā baṛā fāidā hai.*
>
> Exercise does a great deal of good.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فائدہ** (*fāidā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fāʾida*, "a benefit, a gain".
