## You'll want to know: পলি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C431-UPOSAGOR431-04]; assesses=[] -->

**পলি** — *pôli* — "silt".

> বন্যার পরে খেতে নতুন পলি পড়ল।
>
> *bônyār pôre khete nôtun pôli pôṛlô.*
>
> After the flood fresh silt settled on the fields.

The river mud that makes Bengal's fields rich: পলিমাটি, silt soil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পলি** (*pôli*) is the fine river mud left by floods; its origin is not settled.
