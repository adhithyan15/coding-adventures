## You'll want to know: ध्यानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C486-UPAVASA486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ध्यानम्** — *dhyānam* — "meditation".

> ध्यानेन मनः शान्तं भवति।
>
> *dhyānena manaḥ śāntaṃ bhavati.*
>
> Meditation calms the mind.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ध्यानम्** (*dhyānam*) comes from the root √dhyai, "to think of", the root of **ध्यायति**; MW gives "meditation, thought, reflection" (MW).
