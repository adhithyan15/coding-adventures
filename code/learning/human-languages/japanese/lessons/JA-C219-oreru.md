# おれる (oreru) — to snap; to break (of a bone or stick)

## You'll want to know: おれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C219-FALL219-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おれる** — *oreru* — "to snap; to break (of a bone or stick)".

> **ほねが おれましたか。** — *hone ga oremashita ka* — "Did you break a bone?"

Polite **おれます**, *oremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おれる** (*oreru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
