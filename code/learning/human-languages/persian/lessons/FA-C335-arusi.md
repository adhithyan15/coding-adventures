## You'll want to know: عروسی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C335-ARUSI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عروسی** (*'arusi*) — "a wedding".

> عروسی دوستم تا نیمه شب طول کشید.
>
> *'Arusi-ye dustam tâ nime shab tul keshid.*
>
> My friend's wedding went on until midnight.

Built on *'arus* "bride". In speech *'arusi kardan* is "to get married".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'arusi* adds the Persian suffix *-i* to *'arus* "bride", which is borrowed from Arabic *'arūs* (EtymDB).
