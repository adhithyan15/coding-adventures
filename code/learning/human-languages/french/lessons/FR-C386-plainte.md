## You'll want to know: la plainte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C386-ACCUSER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la plainte** — "the complaint (to the police); the moan".

> Elle a porté plainte à la police.
>
> *She filed a complaint with the police.*

*Porter plainte* — to file a complaint. From *se plaindre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*plainte* comes from Old French *plainte* "lament, complaint" (EtymDB), formed on *plaindre*, from Latin *plangere* "to beat the breast, to lament" (Lewis & Short).
