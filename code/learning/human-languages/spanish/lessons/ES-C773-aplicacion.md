## You'll want to know: la aplicación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C773-COBERTURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la aplicación** — "the app; the application".

> Descarga la aplicación del metro antes de salir.
>
> *Download the metro app before you leave.*

In speech it is often *la app*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aplicación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *applicātiō* 'a joining to' (EtymDB), from *applicāre* 'to attach', built on *plicāre* 'to fold'.
