Lesson 1 of 5.
कार्यालयः (kāryālayaḥ) — an office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a library, then the Sanskrit for a university.

You'll want to know: कार्यालयः.
कार्यालयः — kāryālayaḥ — "an office".

The word, taken apart.
kāryālaya is built from kārya "work, business to be done", formed from the root √kṛ "do" (MW), and ālaya "dwelling, house". The pairing for an office is modern.

Lesson 3 of 5.
स्थानकम् (sthānakam) — a station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an office, then the Sanskrit for a restaurant.

You'll want to know: स्थानकम्.
स्थानकम् — sthānakam — "a station".

The word, taken apart.
sthānaka is sthāna "place, standing" with the suffix -ka; MW already has it as "a place, a spot", and "station" for trains is a modern use. sthāna comes from the root √sthā "stand", which MW sets beside Latin stare and English stand.

The word, taken apart.
kāraṇa "cause, reason" comes from the root √kṛ "do, make" (CDIAL 3057): a cause is what brings a thing about. Hindi, Gujarati and Punjabi kāraṇ keep it almost unchanged (CDIAL 3057).

Lesson 5 of 5.
यतः (yataḥ) — because, since.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a station, then the Sanskrit for a reason.

You'll want to know: यतः.
यतः — yataḥ — "because, since".

The word, taken apart.
yataḥ is built from the relative stem ya- "which" and the ending -tas "from": "from which", so "since, because" (MW). MW compares the relative ya- with Greek hos "who". Pali kept the word as yato (CDIAL 10401).
