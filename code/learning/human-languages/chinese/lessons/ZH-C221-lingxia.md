## You'll want to know: língxià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C221-SNOW221-04]; assesses=[] -->

*língxià* — "below zero".

> *jīntiān língxià wàibian hěn lěng* — It's below zero today; it's freezing outside.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*língxià* is built from *líng* 'zero' and 下 'below', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *leng* and *haeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to fall' and 'a gentle rain', so the sense zero came later, and how is not settled.
