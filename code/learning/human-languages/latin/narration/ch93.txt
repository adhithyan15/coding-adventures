Latin, chapter 93: Big and Small, Long and Short, High.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
magnus, magna, magnum — big, great.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for an elephant, then the Latin for a deer.

You'll want to know: magnus, magna, magnum.
magnus, magna, magnum — "big, great". English magnify.

What you've built.
One more word for big and small, long and short, high.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: magnus]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: magnus, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say magnus]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Latin for an elephant, then the Latin for a deer, then say magnus again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does magnus, magna, magnum mean? ("Big, great".) Say it once more.
