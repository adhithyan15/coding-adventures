## You'll want to know: فیلمبردار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C524-FILM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فیلمبردار** (*filmbardâr*) — "a cameraman, a camera operator".

> فیلمبردار از کوه فیلم گرفت.
>
> *filmbardâr az kuh film gereft.*
>
> The camera operator filmed the mountain.

*film* + *bardâr* 'taking' (stem of *bardâshtan*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*filmbardâr* is built in Persian on *film*, a loan usually traced to English, and *bardâr*, the stem of native *bardâshtan*, 'to take up'.
