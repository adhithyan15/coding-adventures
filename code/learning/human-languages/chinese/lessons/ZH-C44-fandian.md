## You'll want to know: fàndiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C44-PLACES44-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fàndiàn* — "restaurant".

> *wǒmen qù fàndiàn chī fàn* — We're going out to a restaurant.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fàndiàn* is built from 饭 'meal' and diàn 'shop', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjonH* and *temH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
