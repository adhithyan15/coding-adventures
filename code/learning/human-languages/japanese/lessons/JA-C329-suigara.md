## You'll want to know: すいがら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C329-JOUSHIKI329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**すいがら** — *suigara* — "a cigarette end".

> **きたないから、すいがらを すてないで ください。** — *kitanai kara, suigara o sutenaide kudasai* — "It's dirty, so please do not throw away cigarette ends."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいがら** (*suigara*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *sui*, "inhaling (suu)" and *gara*, "husk (kara)", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
