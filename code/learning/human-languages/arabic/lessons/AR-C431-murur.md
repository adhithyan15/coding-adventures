## You'll want to know: مرور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C431-MURUR431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مرور** — *murūr* — "traffic; passing".

> كان المرور خفيفا يوم الجمعة.
>
> *kāna al-murūr khafīfan yawm al-jumʿa.*
>
> Traffic was light on Friday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرور** (*murūr*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-r-r*, "to pass" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of *marra*, "to pass by".
