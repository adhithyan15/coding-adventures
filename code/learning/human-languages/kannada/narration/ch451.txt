Kannada, chapter 451: Lorries, Jeeps and Taxis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಲಾರಿ (lāri) — a lorry; a truck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to break down, then the Kannada for repair work.

You'll want to know: ಲಾರಿ.
ಲಾರಿ — lāri — "a lorry; a truck".
ಲಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ತರಕಾರಿ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಿಂದ ನಗರಕ್ಕೆ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ. lāriyalli tarakāri haḷḷiyinda nagarakke baruttade. Vegetables come from the village to the city by lorry.
Plural: ಲಾರಿಗಳು, lārigaḷu.
The plural takes -ಗಳು: ಲಾರಿಗಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ಲಾರಿ (lāri) is a loan from English lorry.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a jeep, then the Kannada for a taxi.

You'll want to know: ಬೈಕ್.
ಬೈಕ್ — baik — "a motorbike".
ನನ್ನ ತಮ್ಮ ಬೈಕ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಕಚೇರಿಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾನೆ. nanna tamma baiknalli kacērige hōguttāne. My younger brother goes to the office on a motorbike.
A bicycle is a ಸೈಕಲ್; a ಬೈಕ್ (baik) has an engine.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೈಕ್ (baik) is a loan from English bike, which in India names a motorbike.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಎತ್ತಿನಗಾಡಿ (ettinagāḍi) — a bullock cart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a taxi, then the Kannada for a motorbike.

You'll want to know: ಎತ್ತಿನಗಾಡಿ.
ಎತ್ತಿನಗಾಡಿ — ettinagāḍi — "a bullock cart".
ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ರೈತರು ಎತ್ತಿನಗಾಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಬ್ಬು ಒಯ್ಯುತ್ತಾರೆ. haḷḷiyalli raitaru ettinagāḍiyalli kabbu oyyuttāre. In the village farmers carry sugarcane in bullock carts.
ಎತ್ತಿನ, of an ox, plus ಗಾಡಿ, a cart.

The word, taken apart.
ಎತ್ತಿನಗಾಡಿ (ettinagāḍi) is a Kannada compound: ಎತ್ತಿನ, "of an ox", from native ettu, "an ox" (DEDR 815), and ಗಾಡಿ, "a cart", usually traced to Hindustani gāṛī.
