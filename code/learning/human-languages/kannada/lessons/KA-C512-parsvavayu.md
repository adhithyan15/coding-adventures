## You'll want to know: ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C512-ROGA512-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯು** — *pārśvavāyu* — "a stroke; paralysis".

> ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯುವಿನಿಂದ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಎಡಗೈ ಚಲಿಸುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *pārśvavāyuvininda ajjana eḍagai calisuvudilla.*
>
> Since his stroke, grandfather's left hand does not move.

Literally side-wind: ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವ, a side + ವಾಯು, air, the old name for a disorder of the body.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯು** (*pārśvavāyu*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *pārśva*, "the side, the ribs" (CDIAL 8118), and *vāyu*, "wind" (CDIAL 11544): a "wind" that strikes one side of the body.
