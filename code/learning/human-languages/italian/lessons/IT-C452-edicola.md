## You'll want to know: l'edicola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C452-EDICOLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'edicola** — "the newsstand, the kiosk".

> All'edicola compro il giornale ogni mattina.
>
> *At the newsstand I buy the paper every morning.*

It also means a small shrine with a statue in a wall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*edicola* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aedicula* 'small shrine' (EtymDB), from *aedēs* 'temple, dwelling': the kiosk looks like a little shrine.
