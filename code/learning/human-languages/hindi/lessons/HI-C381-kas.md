## You'll want to know: काश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C381-KASH381-01]; assesses=[] -->

**काश** — *kāś* — "if only, I wish".

> काश मैं वहाँ होता, तो तुम्हारी मदद करता!
>
> *kāś maiṁ vahā̃ hotā, to tumhārī madad kartā!*
>
> If only I had been there, I would have helped you!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काश** (*kāś*) is usually traced to Persian *kāsh*, "would that".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
