## You'll want to know: le défibrillateur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C301-ALERTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le défibrillateur** — "the defibrillator".

> Il y a un défibrillateur à l'entrée de la mairie.
>
> *There is a defibrillator at the entrance of the town hall.*

Often in public places, in a box on the wall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*défibrillateur* is a learned medical word built on *fibrillation*, from Latin *fibrilla* "a small fibre", from *fibra* (Lewis & Short).
