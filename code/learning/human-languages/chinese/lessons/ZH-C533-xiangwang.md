## You'll want to know: xiàngwǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C533-WISH533-01]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàngwǎng* — "to yearn for".

> *rúguǒ yǒu jīhuì wǒ jiù qù wǒ xiàngwǎng de dìfang* — If I get the chance, I'll go to the place I've always longed for.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàngwǎng* is built from *xiàng* 'towards' and 往 'to go', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xjangH* and *hjwangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which gives the first as the same word as its usual spelling and glosses the second 'to go to'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
