## You'll want to know: さわぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C269-DOROBOU269-05]; assesses=[] -->

**さわぐ** — *sawagu* — "to make a noise; to make a fuss".

> **となりの へやで おとこが さわいでいました。** — *tonari no heya de otoko ga sawaide imashita* — "A man was making a racket in the next room."

Polite **さわぎます**, *sawagimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが さわぎました。**, *kodomo ga sawagimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さわぐ** (*sawagu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
