## You'll want to know: افاقہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C481-BETTER481-01]; assesses=[] -->

**افاقہ** — *ifāqā* — "an easing (of an illness); relief".

> دوا سے مریض کو افاقہ ہوا۔
>
> *davā se marīz ko ifāqā huā.*
>
> The medicine brought the patient some relief.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افاقہ** (*ifāqā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ifāqa*, "recovering, coming to oneself".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
