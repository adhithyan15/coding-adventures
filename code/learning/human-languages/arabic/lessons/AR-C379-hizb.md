## You'll want to know: حزب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C379-HIZB379-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حزب** — *ḥizb* — "a political party".

> لكل حزب برنامج مختلف.
>
> *li-kulli ḥizb barnāmaj mukhtalif.*
>
> Each party has a different programme.

Plural: أحزاب, *aḥzāb*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حزب** (*ḥizb*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-z-b* (Lane), where it names a band of people who hold together. Modern Arabic gave it the political party.
