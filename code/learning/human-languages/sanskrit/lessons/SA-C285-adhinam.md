# अधीनम् (adhīnam) — depending on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C284-IF-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C284-CONDITIONAL-REAL-01, SA-LEX-C86-WEEKEND-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "if there is time, I will come". (*yadi samayaḥ bhaviṣyati, tarhi aham āgamiṣyāmi*.) A shorter answer says only what it depends on.

## You'll want to know: अधीनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C285-ADHINAM-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C86-WEEKEND-04, SA-LEX-C76-WEATHER-01, SA-LEX-C36-REPLY-02, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-02] -->

**अधीनम्** — *adhīnam* — "depending on". It comes at the end of what it depends on, joined to it as one word: *samaya* and *adhīnam* meet as **समयाधीनम्** (*samayādhīnam*).

> **तत् समयाधीनम्।**
>
> *tat samayādhīnam.* — "That depends on time."

> **सर्वं वर्षाधीनम्।**
>
> *sarvaṃ varṣādhīnam.* — "Everything depends on the rain."

> **एतत् धनाधीनम्।**
>
> *etat dhanādhīnam.* — "This depends on money."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधीनम्** (*adhīnam*) is built on **अधि** (*adhi*), "over, on": what rests on something. Monier-Williams gives *adhīna* as "resting on", and "depending on, subject to", always at the end of a compound, and Macdonell gives it the same way, which is why this book joins it to the word before it. Pali kept it as *adhīna*, "dependent on" (CDIAL 266).
