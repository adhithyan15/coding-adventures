## You'll want to know: 通车
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C409-TRAFFIC409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**通车** — *tōngchē* — "to open to traffic".

> *zhèr de dìtiě míngnián tōngchē* — The metro here opens next year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**通车** (*tōngchē*) is built from 通 'to go through' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thuwng* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): vehicles can go through.
