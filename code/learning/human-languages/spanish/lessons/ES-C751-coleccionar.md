## You'll want to know: coleccionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C751-AFICION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**coleccionar** — "to collect".

> Colecciono sellos desde niño.
>
> *I have collected stamps since I was a boy.*

For hobbies; to pick someone up is *recoger*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coleccionar* is built on *colección*, a learned borrowing from Latin *collēctiō* "a gathering together" (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
