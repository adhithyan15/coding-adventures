# The opening exchange, read off the page

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Write آپ. The wave on the alif, then the boat with three dots
under it.

## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-NAME-QUESTION, UR-LEX-MERA-NAAM-HAI, UR-LEX-KYA, UR-LEX-AAP-TUM-TU] -->

Here is the exchange you learned in your first hour of Urdu. Every letter in it
is now one your own hand can make. Read each line from the right, naming its
words, then say it:

> سلام
>
> آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟
>
> میرا نام ... ہے۔
>
> آپ کیسے ہیں؟

*salām* — *āp kā nām kyā hai?* — *merā nām ... hai.* — *āp kaise haiṅ?*

Look at the second line. **آپ** ends with pe wearing its full boat, and **ہے**
and **ہیں** are the same gol he under two different endings.

Only the reply *maiṅ ṭhīk hūṅ* is still out of reach, and by three shapes —
one of which arrives next lesson.
