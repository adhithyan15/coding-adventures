## You'll want to know: ಉಪನ್ಯಾಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C406-VISHVAVIDYALAYA406-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಪನ್ಯಾಸ** — *upanyāsa* — "a lecture".

> ಇಂದಿನ ಉಪನ್ಯಾಸ ಒಂದು ಗಂಟೆ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *indina upanyāsa ondu gaṇṭe naḍeyitu.*
>
> Today's lecture went on for an hour.

ಉಪನ್ಯಾಸಕ, a lecturer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಪನ್ಯಾಸ** (*upanyāsa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *upanyāsa*, "putting down near; a statement" (Monier-Williams).
