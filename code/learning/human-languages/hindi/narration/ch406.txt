Hindi, chapter 406: Taxis and Tyres.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
टैक्सी (ṭaiksī) — a taxi.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to bounce, then the Hindi for to be cut.

You'll want to know: टैक्सी.
टैक्सी — ṭaiksī — "a taxi".
हमने स्टेशन से टैक्सी ली। hamne sṭeśan se ṭaiksī lī. We took a taxi from the station.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
टैक्सी (ṭaiksī) is borrowed from English taxi; it is feminine in Hindi, like गाड़ी.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.
