## You'll want to know: りょかん

**りょかん** — *ryokan* — "a Japanese inn".

> **りょかんで ゆっくり やすみます。** — *ryokan de yukkuri yasumimasu* — "I rest well at the inn."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りょかん** (*ryokan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ryo*, "journey" (Middle Chinese *ljoX*) and *kan*, "building, lodge" (*kwanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
