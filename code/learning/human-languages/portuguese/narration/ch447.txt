Portuguese, chapter 447: Dar uma volta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a marginal — the seafront road, the riverside road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the patron saint, then the Portuguese for to parade.

You'll want to know: a marginal.
a marginal — "the seafront road, the riverside road".
Ao domingo, passeamos pela marginal. On Sundays, we walk along the seafront.
Also an adjective: marginal, on the edge.

The word, taken apart.
marginal is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin marginālis (EtymDB), from margō 'edge, border': the road along the water's edge.

What you've built.

Espreitar uma oportunidade — to watch for a chance.

The word, taken apart.
espreitar is inherited from Old Portuguese espreitar (EtymDB); it is usually traced to Latin explicitāre 'to unfold, to examine', but that is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: o miradouro.
o miradouro — "the viewpoint, the lookout".
Do miradouro vemos o rio e as pontes. From the viewpoint we can see both the river and the bridges.

The word, taken apart.
miradouro is built on mirar 'to gaze', which is inherited from Vulgar Latin mīrāre (EtymDB), from Latin mīrārī 'to wonder at'.
