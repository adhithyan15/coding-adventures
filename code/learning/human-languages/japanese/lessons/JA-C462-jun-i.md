## You'll want to know: じゅんい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C462-CONTEST462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅんい** — *jun'i* — "a ranking; a placing".

> **しあいの あとで、じゅんいが きまりました。** — *shiai no ato de, jun'i ga kimarimashita* — "After the match the rankings were decided."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅんい** (*jun'i*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jun*, "order" (Middle Chinese *zywinH*) and *i*, "rank" (*hwijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
