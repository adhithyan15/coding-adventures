## You'll want to know: epulor, epulārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C333-FESTUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**epulor, epulārī** — "to feast, to banquet".

> Tōtam noctem amīcī epulābantur.
>
> *The friends feasted all night long.*

*epulor, epulārī, epulātus sum*, a deponent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**epulor** is built on *epulum*, "a banquet" (Lewis & Short): to hold a feast.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
