## You'll want to know: उत्सृजति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C551-PARITYAJATI551-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सृजति** — *utsṛjati* — "lets go of".

> यदि त्वं क्रोधम् उत्सृजसि, तर्हि मनः शान्तं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ krodham utsṛjasi, tarhi manaḥ śāntaṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you let go of your anger, your mind will be calm.

Past (he …): सः कपोतम् उत्सृष्टवान्, *saḥ kapotam utsṛṣṭavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सृजति** (*utsṛjati*) is built on *ud*, "out", and the root √sṛj, "to let go, to emit"; MW gives *utsṛj* as to let loose, to let go, to set free; its past participle is *utsṛṣṭa* (MW).
