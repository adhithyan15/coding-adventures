## You'll want to know: shénxiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C331-MAGIC331-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shénxiān* — "immortal; supernatural being".

> *zuìhòu yí ge shénxiān bāng le tā* — In the end an immortal helped him.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shénxiān* is built from *shén* 'a god' and *xiān* 'an immortal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zyin* and *sjen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a spirit' and says of the second that one who grows old and does not die is called so.
