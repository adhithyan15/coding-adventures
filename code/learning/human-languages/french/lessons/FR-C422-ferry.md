## You'll want to know: le ferry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C422-TRAVERSEE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le ferry** — "the ferry".

> Nous prenons le ferry pour aller sur l'île.
>
> *We take the ferry to go over to the island.*

In Québec: *le traversier*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferry* was borrowed from English *ferry* (EtymDB), from a verb meaning 'to carry over water'.
