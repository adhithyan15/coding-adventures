## You'll want to know: la dieta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C742-DIETA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la dieta** — "the diet".

> Estoy a dieta.
>
> *I am on a diet.*

*Estar a dieta* — to be on a diet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dieta* comes from Latin *diaeta*, which was borrowed from Greek *díaita* "way of life" (EtymDB).
