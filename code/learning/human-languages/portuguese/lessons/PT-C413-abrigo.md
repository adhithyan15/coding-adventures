## You'll want to know: o abrigo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C413-PARAGEM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o abrigo** — "the shelter".

> Esperamos o autocarro no abrigo da paragem.
>
> *We wait for the bus in the bus shelter.*

*Ficar ao abrigo da chuva* — to stay out of the rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abrigo* is built on *abrigar* 'to shelter' (EtymDB). *Abrigar* is usually traced to Latin *aprīcārī* 'to warm oneself in the sun' (Lewis & Short), but that is not settled.
