## You'll want to know: આગાહી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C435-STORM435-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આગાહી** — *āgāhī* — "a forecast".

> હવામાન ખાતાએ ભારે વરસાદની આગાહી કરી.
>
> *havāmān khātāe bhāre varsādnī āgāhī karī.*
>
> The weather office forecast heavy rain.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આગાહી** (*āgāhī*) is usually traced to Persian *āgāhī*, "awareness, being told in advance", built on *āgāh*, "aware".
