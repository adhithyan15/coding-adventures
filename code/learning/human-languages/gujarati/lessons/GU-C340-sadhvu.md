## You'll want to know: સાધવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C340-HETU340-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સાધવું** — *sādhvũ* — "to accomplish".

> મહેનતથી તે પોતાનો હેતુ સાધશે.
>
> *mahenatthī te potāno hetu sādhśe.*
>
> With hard work he will achieve his aim.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પોતાનો હેતુ સાધ્યો, *teṇe potāno hetu sādhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાધવું** (*sādhvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sādhnōti*, "accomplishes"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 13339).
