## You'll want to know: جام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C393-JAM393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جام** — *jām* — "a traffic jam".

> پل پر بڑا جام تھا۔
>
> *pul par baṛā jām thā.*
>
> There was a huge traffic jam on the bridge.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جام** (*jām*) is borrowed from English *jam*, as in a traffic jam.
