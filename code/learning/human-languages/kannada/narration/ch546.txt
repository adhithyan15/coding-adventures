Lesson 1 of 7.
ಕೈತೋಟ (kaitōṭa) — a kitchen garden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to wash clothes, then the Kannada for starch.

You'll want to know: ಕೈತೋಟ.
ಕೈತೋಟ — kaitōṭa — "a kitchen garden".
ನಮ್ಮ ಕೈತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಟೊಮೇಟೊ ಬೆಳೆಯುತ್ತದೆ. namma kaitōṭadalli ṭomēṭo beḷeyuttade. Tomatoes grow in our kitchen garden.
ಕೈ, hand + ತೋಟ, garden: a garden small enough to tend by hand.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೈತೋಟ (kaitōṭa) is a Kannada compound: native kai, "hand" (DEDR 2023), and tōṭa, "a garden".

ನಮ್ಮ ನೆರೆಹೊರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಸ್ನೇಹದಿಂದ ಇದ್ದಾರೆ. namma nerehoreyalli ellarū snēhadinda iddāre. Everyone in our neighbourhood is friendly.

ನೆರೆಹೊರೆ (nerehore) is a native Kannada pair-word built on nere, "adjoining, proximity, neighbourhood" (DEDR 3770); the second half echoes it.

ಹಬ್ಬಕ್ಕೆ ಮುಂಚೆ ನಾವು ಗೋಡೆಗಳಿಗೆ ಸುಣ್ಣ ಬಳಿದೆವು. habbakke muñce nāvu gōḍegaḷige suṇṇa baḷidevu. Before the festival we whitewashed the walls.

ಸುಣ್ಣ (suṇṇa) goes back to Sanskrit cūrṇa, "something ground, a powder" (CDIAL 4889), through a Prakrit form.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for lime, then the Kannada for a terrace.

You'll want to know: ಊದು.
ಊದು — ūdu — "to puff; to blow into".
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಒಲೆಯ ಬೆಂಕಿಯನ್ನು ಕೊಳವೆಯಿಂದ ಊದಿದರು. ajji oleya beṅkiyannu koḷaveyinda ūdidaru. Grandmother blew on the stove fire through a pipe.
Past (I …): ಊದಿದೆ, ūdide.
With the mouth; the wind's blowing is ಬೀಸು.

The word, taken apart.
ಊದು (ūdu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 741 lists Kannada ūdu, "to blow, to become inflated, swell".
