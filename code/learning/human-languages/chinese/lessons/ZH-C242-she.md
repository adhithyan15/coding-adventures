## You'll want to know: shé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C242-FARMANIMAL242-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shé* — "snake".

> *wǒ hěn pà shé* — I'm terrified of snakes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shé* is inherited from Middle Chinese *zyae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a venomous creature'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
