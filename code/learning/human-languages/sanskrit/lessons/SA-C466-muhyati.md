## You'll want to know: मुह्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C466-MEDHA466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मुह्यति** — *muhyati* — "is bewildered".

> कठिनेन प्रश्नेन छात्रः मुह्यति।
>
> *kaṭhinena praśnena chātraḥ muhyati.*
>
> The student is bewildered by the hard question.

Past (he …): सः मुग्धवान्, *saḥ mugdhavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुह्यति** (*muhyati*) comes from the root √muh, "to become stupefied, to be bewildered", the root of **मोहः** (MW).
