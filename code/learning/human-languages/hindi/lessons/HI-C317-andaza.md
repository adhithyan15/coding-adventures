## You'll want to know: अंदाज़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C317-RAHASYA317-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अंदाज़ा** — *andāzā* — "a guess; an estimate".

> मेरा अंदाज़ा सही निकला।
>
> *merā andāzā sahī niklā.*
>
> My guess turned out to be right.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंदाज़ा** (*andāzā*) is usually traced to Persian *andāza*, "a measure": a guess is measuring by eye. **अंदाज़ा लगाना** is to guess.
