## You'll want to know: گلدستہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C401-ISTIQBAL401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**گلدستہ** — *guldastā* — "a bouquet".

> سارا نے اپنی بہن کو گلدستہ دیا۔
>
> *sārā ne apnī bahan ko guldastā diyā.*
>
> Sara gave her sister a bouquet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گلدستہ** (*guldastā*) is built from Persian *gul*, "a flower", and *dasta*, "a handful": it is usually traced to Persian *guldasta*, "a bunch of flowers".
