## You'll want to know: die Planung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C374-ENTSCHLUSS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Planung** — "the planning".

> Die Planung der Hochzeit hat ein Jahr gedauert.
>
> *Planning the wedding took a year.*

Plural: *die Planungen*. *In Planung* — at the planning stage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Planung* is the noun of *planen*, from *Plan*, borrowed from French *plan* and going back to Latin *plānus* "level, flat" (EtymDB).
