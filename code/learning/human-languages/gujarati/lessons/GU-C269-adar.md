## You'll want to know: આદર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C269-CARE269-05]; assesses=[] -->

**આદર** — *ādar* — "respect".

> **શું તમે તમારા ગુરુનો આદર કરો છો?** — *śũ tame tamārā guruno ādar karo cho?* — "Do you respect your teacher?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આદર** (*ādar*) is Sanskrit *ādara*, "respect", taken over whole as a tatsama (CDIAL 1142); EtymDB also lists it as borrowed from Sanskrit.
