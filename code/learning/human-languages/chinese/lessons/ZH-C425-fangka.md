## You'll want to know: fángkǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C425-HOTEL425-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fángkǎ* — "room key card".

> *wǒ de fángkǎ bú jiàn le* — My room card has gone missing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fángkǎ* is built from 房 'a room' and *kǎ* 'a card'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *bjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); *kǎ* is usually traced to English *card*.
