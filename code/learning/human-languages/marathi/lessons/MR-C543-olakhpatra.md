## You'll want to know: ओळखपत्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C543-HOKAR543-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ओळखपत्र** — *oḷakhpatra* — "an identity card".

> प्रवेशद्वारावर सगळ्यांनी ओळखपत्र दाखवलं.
>
> *praveśadvārāvar sagḷyānnī oḷakhpatra dākhavlaṁ.*
>
> Everyone showed an identity card at the entrance.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओळखपत्र** (*oḷakhpatra*) is a Marathi compound built from ओळख, "knowing someone", and पत्र, "a leaf, a letter", from Sanskrit *patra* (Monier-Williams).
