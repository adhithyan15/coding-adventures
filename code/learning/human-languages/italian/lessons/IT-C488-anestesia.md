## You'll want to know: l'anestesia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C488-OSPEDALE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'anestesia** — "anaesthesia, anaesthetic".

> Con l'anestesia non ho sentito niente.
>
> *With the anaesthetic I did not feel anything.*

*In anestesia locale* — under local anaesthetic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anestesia* is a learned borrowing from Greek *anaisthēsía* "lack of sensation" (EtymDB), from *an-* "not" and *aísthēsis* "feeling".
