## You'll want to know: le pickpocket
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C288-ARNAQUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le pickpocket** — "the pickpocket".

> Un pickpocket m'a pris mon téléphone dans le métro.
>
> *A pickpocket took my phone on the underground.*

Borrowed from English. Signs warn: *attention aux pickpockets*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pickpocket* was borrowed from English *pickpocket* (EtymDB), one who picks pockets.
