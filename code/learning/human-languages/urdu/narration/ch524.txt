Urdu, chapter 524: Making a Plan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فہرست (fehrist) — a list.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a cheer, then the Urdu for to leap over.

You'll want to know: فہرست.
فہرست — fehrist — "a list".
اگر فہرست تیار ہے تو مجھے دیجیے۔ agar fehrist tayyār hai to mujhe dījiye. If the list is ready, please give it to me.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
فہرست (fehrist) is usually traced to Persian fihrist, "a list, an index".

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
چھپوانا (chhapvānā) — to have (something) printed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a list, then the Urdu for a draft.

You'll want to know: چھپوانا.
چھپوانا — chhapvānā — "to have (something) printed".
اگر کتاب تیار ہوئی تو ہم اسے چھپوائیں گے۔ agar kitāb tayyār huī to ham use chhapvāeṅ ge. If the book is ready, we'll have it printed.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اپنی کتاب چھپوائی, us ne apnī kitāb chhapvāī.

The word, taken apart.
چھپوانا (chhapvānā) is the causative of chhāpnā, "to print", which CDIAL lists under chapp, "press", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4994).

Lesson 5 of 5.
بنوانا (banvānā) — to have (something) made or built.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (something) printed, then the Urdu for a way out.

You'll want to know: بنوانا.
بنوانا — banvānā — "to have (something) made or built".
اگر نقشہ ٹھیک ہے تو ہم گھر بنوائیں گے۔ agar naqshā ṭhīk hai to ham ghar banvāeṅ ge. If the plan is right, we'll have the house built.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نیا گھر بنوایا, us ne nayā ghar banvāyā.

The word, taken apart.
بنوانا (banvānā) is the second causative of bannā, "to be made", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit vanati, "desires, makes ready" (CDIAL 11260): to have someone make a thing.
