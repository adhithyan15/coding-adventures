## You'll want to know: ഒമ്പതാം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C68-ORDINAL-04]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C67-ORDINAL-02, ML-CONCEPT-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-03] -->

**ഒമ്പതാം** (*ompatāṁ*) — "**ninth**".

**ഒമ്പത്** was already a built word when you learned it: an old word for **one**
plus *pattŭ*, **ten** — usually read as one short of ten, with the *n* turning to *m* before the
*p*. The ordinal ending goes on top of that, and none of the machinery
underneath reacts to it.

**ഒമ്പതാം ദിവസം** (*ompatāṁ divasaṁ*), the ninth day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഒമ്പതാം** (*ompatāṁ*) is native Dravidian: "nine" (DEDR 1025, with Tamil *oṉpatu*, *ompatu* and Kannada *ombattu*) with the ordinal ending *-āṁ*.
