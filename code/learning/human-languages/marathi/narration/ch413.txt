Marathi, chapter 413: A Place to Stay.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निवास (nivās) — lodging; a residence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to stray, then the Marathi for to hang about.

You'll want to know: निवास.
निवास — nivās — "lodging; a residence".
सहलीत आमचा निवास एका जुन्या वाड्यात होता. sahalīt āmcā nivās ekā junyā vāḍyāt hotā. On the trip we stayed in an old mansion.
Masculine.
A formal word on signs and forms: निवास (nivās) आणि जेवण, board and lodging.

The word, taken apart.
निवास (nivās) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nivāsa, "dwelling, abode", from ni-vas, "to dwell" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: धर्मशाळा.
धर्मशाळा — dharmaśāḷā — "a rest house; a pilgrims’ lodge".
मंदिराजवळ एक धर्मशाळा आहे. mandirājavaḷ ek dharmaśāḷā āhe. There is a rest house near the temple.
Feminine.
Often near a temple; a bed there is cheap or free.

The word, taken apart.
धर्मशाळा (dharmaśāḷā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit dharmaśālā, built from dharma, "duty, charity", and śālā, "a hall" (Monier-Williams): a hall kept open as a good deed.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मुक्काम (mukkām) — an overnight stay; a halt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rest house, then the Marathi for a guest house.

You'll want to know: मुक्काम.
मुक्काम — mukkām — "an overnight stay; a halt".
आज रात्री आमचा मुक्काम एका लहान गावात आहे. āj rātrī āmcā mukkām ekā lahān gāvāt āhe. Tonight we halt in a small village.
Masculine.
मुक्काम (mukkām) करणे, to stop for the night; मुक्कामी, staying over.

The word, taken apart.
मुक्काम (mukkām) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic muqām, "a place where one stays".

Lesson 5 of 5.
खानावळ (khānāvaḷ) — an eating house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a guest house, then the Marathi for an overnight stay.

You'll want to know: खानावळ.
खानावळ — khānāvaḷ — "an eating house".
आम्ही खानावळीत गरम जेवण घेतलं. āmhī khānāvaḷīt garam jevaṇ ghetlaṁ. We had a hot meal at the eating house.
Feminine.
A simple place serving home-style meals for a fixed price; before an ending खानावळी-.

The word, taken apart.
खानावळ (khānāvaḷ) is a Marathi word for a cheap, homely place to eat; its origin is not settled.
