## You'll want to know: метель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C467-MOROZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**метель** (*metél*) — "a blizzard, a snowstorm".

> Из-за метели мы два дня сидели дома.
>
> *Iz-za metéli my dva dnya sidéli dóma.*
>
> Because of the blizzard we stayed at home for two days.

Feminine. Built on *мести* — to sweep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*метель* is built on *мести* 'to sweep': wind that sweeps the snow along. EtymDB lists *метель* without a deeper source.
