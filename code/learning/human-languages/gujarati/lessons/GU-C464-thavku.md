## You'll want to know: ઠાવકું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C464-SANU464-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠાવકું** — *ṭhāvkũ* — "composed; sober".

> આટલી નાની ઉંમરે પણ મીરા ઘણી ઠાવકી છે.
>
> *āṭlī nānī ummre paṇ Mīrā ghaṇī ṭhāvkī che.*
>
> Even at so young an age Mira is very composed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠાવકું** (*ṭhāvkũ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "steady, firm, discreet", under Sanskrit *sthātavya*, "to be stood in" (CDIAL 13751): someone who stands firm.
