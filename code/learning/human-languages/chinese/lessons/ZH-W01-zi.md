# 子 — three strokes, a child

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-NU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Three strokes, crossing bar last. Draw the seated figure you met a
moment ago.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 子

**子** *zǐ* — **child**. Three strokes again, and again a figure you can still
see once you are told where to look.

This one is an infant drawn **from the front**: a large head on top, two arms
out to the sides, and the legs bound together so they read as one stroke rather
than two. Babies were carried swaddled, and the character kept the bundle.

Write it:

1. the short stroke across the **top**, turning down — the head
2. the **vertical with a hook**, dropping through the middle — the wrapped body
3. the **horizontal** across the middle — the arms

The pitch is **third tone**. The *z* is not the English *z* of *zoo*: it is the
*ds* at the end of English *beds*, made with the tongue tip against the teeth.
