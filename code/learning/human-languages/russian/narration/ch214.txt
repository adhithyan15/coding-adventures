Russian, chapter 214: From here, Back, Forward, Up, Down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
отсюда (otsyúda) — from here.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a flat, then the Russian for a floor.

You'll want to know: отсюда.
отсюда (otsyúda) — "from here".
