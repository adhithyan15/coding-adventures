Russian, chapter 351: Since and thanks to.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
поскольку (poskólku) — since, as (giving a reason).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an effect, then the Russian for as a result.

You'll want to know: поскольку.
поскольку (poskólku) — "since, as (giving a reason)".
Поскольку шёл дождь, мы остались дома. Poskólku shyol dozhd, my ostális dóma. Since it was raining, we stayed at home.
It takes an ordinary clause in the indicative, like так как, and sounds a little more formal. It can open the sentence.

The word, taken apart.
поскольку (poskólku) is built from по and сколько "how much", from Proto-Slavic koliko "how much" (EtymDB): first "in so far as", then "since".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ведь (ved) — after all, you know.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for as a result, then the Russian for since.

You'll want to know: ведь.
ведь (ved) — "after all, you know".
Он не придёт: он ведь болен. On ne pridyót: on ved bólen. He will not come: he is ill, after all.
It gives a reason the listener already knows. It is unstressed and sits near the front of the clause.

The word, taken apart.
ведь (ved) comes from Old East Slavic vědě "I know" (EtymDB), a form of the old verb ведать "to know"; EtymDB names English wit as a cousin. Ведь still appeals to what both speakers know.

Lesson 3 of 5.
благодаря (blagodaryá) — thanks to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for since, then the Russian for after all.

You'll want to know: благодаря.
благодаря (blagodaryá) — "thanks to".
Благодаря тебе я сдал экзамен. Blagodaryá tebé ya sdal ekzámen. Thanks to you I passed the exam.
It takes the dative and names a good cause: благодаря (blagodaryá) помощи. For a bad cause Russian says из-за + genitive.

The word, taken apart.
благодаря (blagodaryá) is the gerund of благодарить "to thank", taken from Church Slavonic blagodariti (EtymDB), a compound of благо "good" and дар "gift". The -ла- is the Church Slavonic shape.

Lesson 4 of 5.
вследствие (vslédstviye) — owing to, in consequence of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for after all, then the Russian for thanks to.

You'll want to know: вследствие.
вследствие (vslédstviye) — "owing to, in consequence of".
Поезд опаздывает вследствие снегопада. Póyezd opázdyvayet vslédstviye snegopáda. The train is late owing to the snowfall.
It takes the genitive. Formal, written style: notices and the news.

The word, taken apart.
вследствие (vslédstviye) is fused from в "in" and следствие "a result", built on след "a track, a trail" (EtymDB): in the wake of something.

Lesson 5 of 5.
следовательно (slédovatelno) — therefore, consequently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for thanks to, then the Russian for owing to.

You'll want to know: следовательно.
следовательно (slédovatelno) — "therefore, consequently".
Он не звонил, следовательно, всё в порядке. On ne zvoníl, slédovatelno, vsyo v poryádke. He has not called, so everything is all right.
A formal link between a premise and a conclusion, set off by commas. In conversation people say значит.

The word, taken apart.
следовательно (slédovatelno) is built on следовать "to follow", from след "a track, a trail" (EtymDB): what follows from what came before.
