## You'll want to know: la peluquería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C680-TIENDAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la peluquería** — "the hairdresser’s".

> La peluquería cierra los lunes.
>
> *The hairdresser’s is closed on Mondays.*

The hairdresser is *el peluquero*, *la peluquera*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*peluquería* is built on *peluca* "wig" (EtymDB), which came from French *perruque* and Italian before it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
