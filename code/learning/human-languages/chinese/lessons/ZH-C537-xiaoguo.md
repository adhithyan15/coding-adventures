## You'll want to know: xiàoguǒ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C537-TURNS537-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàoguǒ* — "the effect it has (how well it works)".

> *yàoshi méiyǒu xiàoguǒ nǐ jiù huàn yí ge bànfǎ* — If it doesn't work, try another way.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàoguǒ* is built from *xiào* and 果 'fruit', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haewH* and *kwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to imitate' and 'proven effect': what the effort bears.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
