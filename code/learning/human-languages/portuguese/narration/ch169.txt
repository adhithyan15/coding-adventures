Portuguese, chapter 169: Sandal, Pocket, Zip, Sleeve, Wardrobe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a sandália — a sandal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a pumpkin, then the Portuguese for a pea.
