റാങ്ക് — ṟāṅkŭ — "a rank".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ബിരുദധാരി (birudadhāri) — a graduate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to pass an exam, then the Malayalam for a rank.

You'll want to know: ബിരുദധാരി.
ബിരുദധാരി — birudadhāri — "a graduate".
ആ ബിരുദധാരിക്ക് ഇതുവരെ ജോലി കിട്ടിയില്ല. ā birudadhārikkŭ ituvare jōli kiṭṭiyilla. That graduate has not found a job yet.
Plural: ബിരുദധാരികൾ, birudadhārikaḷ.
Plural: ബിരുദധാരികൾ.

The word, taken apart.
ബിരുദധാരി (birudadhāri) is a Malayalam compound built from ബിരുദം, "a degree", from Sanskrit biruda, "a panegyric, a song of praise" (Monier-Williams, under viruda), and Sanskrit dhārin, "bearing, holding" (Monier-Williams): one who holds a degree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a graduation ceremony, then the Malayalam for higher education.

You'll want to know: മൂല്യനിർണ്ണയം.
മൂല്യനിർണ്ണയം — mūlyanirṇṇayaṁ — "an evaluation; marking".
പരീക്ഷയുടെ മൂല്യനിർണ്ണയം അടുത്ത ആഴ്ച തീരും. parīkṣayuṭe mūlyanirṇṇayaṁ aṭutta āḻca tīruṁ. The marking of the exam will finish next week.
The marking of exam papers is its everyday use.

The word, taken apart.
മൂല്യനിർണ്ണയം (mūlyanirṇṇayaṁ) is built from Sanskrit mūlya, "value, price" (CDIAL 10257), and nirṇaya, "settling, decision" (Monier-Williams): deciding the value of a thing.
