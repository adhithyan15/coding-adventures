## You'll want to know: la vipera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C465-PICCOLI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la vipera** — "the viper, the adder".

> In montagna attenzione alle vipere.
>
> *In the mountains watch out for vipers.*

Figuratively, a spiteful person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vipera* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vīpera* 'viper' (EtymDB).
