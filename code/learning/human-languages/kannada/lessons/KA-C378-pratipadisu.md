## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C378-NILUVU378-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸು** — *pratipādisu* — "to advocate; to argue for".

> ಅವರು ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಉಚಿತ ಶಿಕ್ಷಣವನ್ನು ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *avaru ellarigū ucita śikṣaṇavannu pratipādisidaru.*
>
> They argued for free education for all.

Past (I …): ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸಿದೆ, *pratipādiside*.

What you argue for takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸು** (*pratipādisu*) is built on Sanskrit *pratipādana*, "presenting, setting forth" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
