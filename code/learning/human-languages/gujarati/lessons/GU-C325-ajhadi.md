## You'll want to know: આઝાદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C325-AZADI325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**આઝાદી** — *ājhādī* — "freedom".

> લોકો આઝાદી માટે લડ્યા.
>
> *loko ājhādī māṭe laḍyā.*
>
> People fought for freedom.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આઝાદી** (*ājhādī*) is usually traced to Persian *āzādī*, "freedom", the noun of *āzād*, free. It is feminine: **આઝાદી માટે**, for freedom.
