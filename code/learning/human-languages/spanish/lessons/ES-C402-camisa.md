# la camisa — a garment with a long family

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COSTAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask how much it costs. (*¿Cuánto cuesta?*)

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- *ca-MI-sa*: three open syllables, with the weight on *mi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-CAMISA]; assesses=[] -->

**la camisa** — "the shirt."

Spanish continues Late Latin **camisia**, a garment worn close to the body.
English **chemise** and **camisole** travelled by another route from the same
old garment word, so their opening sound changed while Spanish kept *camisa*.

In the shop: **¿Cuánto cuesta la camisa?**
