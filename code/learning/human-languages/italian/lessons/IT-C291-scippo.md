## You'll want to know: lo scippo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C291-SCIPPO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**lo scippo** — "the bag-snatching".

> Alla stazione c'è stato uno scippo.
>
> *There was a bag-snatching at the station.*

A theft from the hand, often by someone on a moped; common on the local news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *scippo* is not settled; it is usually linked with southern Italian dialect words for snatching (EtymDB has no entry).
