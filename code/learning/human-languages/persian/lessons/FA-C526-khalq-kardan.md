## You'll want to know: خلق کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C526-ART-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خلق کردن** (*khalq kardan*) — "to create".

> او با چند رنگ ساده یک نقاشی زیبا خلق کرد.
>
> *u bâ chand rang-e sâde yek naqqâshi-ye zibâ khalq kard.*
>
> With a few simple colours she created a beautiful painting.

*khalq* 'creation' + *kardan*. Formal; in speech *dorost kardan* or *sâkhtan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khalq* comes from Arabic *ḫalq* (EtymDB), which EtymDB glosses 'people, creature': what has been created. *kardan* is inherited Persian.
