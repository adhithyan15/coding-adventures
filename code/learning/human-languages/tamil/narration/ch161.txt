Tamil, chapter 161: To sell, To boil, To fry, To grind, To mix.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
வில் (vil) — to sell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an advert, then the Tamil for a receipt.

You'll want to know: வில்.
வில் — vil — "to sell".

The word, taken apart.
வில் (vil) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5421 lists Malayalam vilkka, "to sell" and Kannada bil.

கலக்கு — kalakku — "to mix, to stir".

The word, taken apart.
கலக்கு (kalakku) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1303 lists Malayalam kalaṅṅuka, "to be mixed, agitated". It is the causative of kalaṅku, "to be stirred up". The entry compares Sanskrit kaluṣa, "turbid".
