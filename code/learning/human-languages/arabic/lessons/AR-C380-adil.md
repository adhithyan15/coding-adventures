## You'll want to know: عادل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C380-ADALA380-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عادل** — *ʿādil* — "fair".

> كان المعلم عادلا مع كل الطلاب.
>
> *kāna al-muʿallim ʿādilan maʿa kulli aṭ-ṭullāb.*
>
> The teacher was fair to all the students.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عادل** (*ʿādil*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-d-l*, "to be equal, straight" (Lane). *ʿĀdil* is the active participle: one who acts fairly, fair-minded.
