## You'll want to know: безработица
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C367-RAVENSTVO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**безработица** (*bezrabótitsa*) — "unemployment".

> В этом районе высокая безработица.
>
> *V étom rayóne vysókaya bezrabótitsa.*
>
> Unemployment is high in this area.

Feminine. A person out of work is *безработный*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**безработица** is built from *без* "without" and *работа* "work", which goes back to Proto-Slavic *\*orbota* "toil", on *раб* "a slave".
