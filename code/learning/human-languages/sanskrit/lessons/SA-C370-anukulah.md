## You'll want to know: अनुकूलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C370-STHITI370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुकूलः** — *anukūlaḥ* — "favourable".

> यदि वायुः अनुकूलः, तर्हि नौका शीघ्रं गमिष्यति।
>
> *yadi vāyuḥ anukūlaḥ, tarhi naukā śīghraṃ gamiṣyati.*
>
> If the wind is favourable, the boat will go fast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुकूलः** (*anukūlaḥ*) is built from *anu*, "along", and *kūla*, "a slope, a bank"; MW gives *anu-kūla* "favourable, agreeable" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
