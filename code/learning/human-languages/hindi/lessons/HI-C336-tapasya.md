## You'll want to know: तपस्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C336-SHAAP336-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तपस्या** — *tapasyā* — "penance; austerity".

> ऋषि ने जंगल में बारह साल तपस्या की।
>
> *ṛṣi ne jaṅgal meṁ bārah sāl tapasyā kī.*
>
> The sage did penance in the forest for twelve years.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तपस्या** (*tapasyā*) is Sanskrit *tapasyā* (Monier-Williams), from *tapas*, heat and then the hard discipline of a holy life.
