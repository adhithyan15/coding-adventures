## You'll want to know: استدار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C435-MASAR435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استدار** — *istadāra* — "to turn around".

> استدار الولد عندما سمع اسمه.
>
> *istadāra al-walad ʿindamā samiʿa ismahu.*
>
> The boy turned around when he heard his name.

Present: يستدير, *yastadīr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استدار** (*istadāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-w-r*, "to turn, to go round" (Lane). It is Form X.
