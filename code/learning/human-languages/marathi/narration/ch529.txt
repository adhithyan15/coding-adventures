Marathi, chapter 529: A Day at the Match.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सामना (sāmnā) — a match; a contest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for entertainment, then the Marathi for to be absorbed.

You'll want to know: सामना.
सामना — sāmnā — "a match; a contest".
काल क्रिकेटचा सामना खूप रंगला. kāl krikeṭcā sāmnā khūp raṅglā. Yesterday's cricket match was very exciting.
Masculine.
The oblique is सामन्या-: सामन्यात.

The word, taken apart.
सामना (sāmnā) is usually linked with Hindi sāmnā, "a facing, a confrontation". CDIAL sets the words for facing and in front under Sanskrit saṁmukha, "facing" (CDIAL 12982), but lists no सामना, so the path is not settled.

Masculine.
One word for a man or a woman.

The word, taken apart.
कर्णधार (karṇadhār) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit karṇadhāra, "helmsman" (Monier-Williams): the captain steers the team.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पंच (pañc) — an umpire; a referee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a match, then the Marathi for a team captain.

You'll want to know: पंच.
पंच — pañc — "an umpire; a referee".
पंचांनी पावसामुळे खेळ थांबवला. pañcānnī pāvsāmuḷe kheḷ thāmbavlā. The umpires stopped play because of the rain.
Masculine.
A match has two, so often plural: पंचांनी, the umpires.

The word, taken apart.
पंच (pañc) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit pañca, "five" (CDIAL 7655): a council of five once settled disputes, and the word came to mean a judge of fair play.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मात करणे (māt karṇe) — to overcome; to beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an umpire, then the Marathi for a playing field.

You'll want to know: मात करणे.
मात करणे — māt karṇe — "to overcome; to beat".
आमच्या संघाने मोठ्या संघावर मात केली. āmcyā saṅghāne moṭhyā saṅghāvar māt kelī. Our team beat a bigger team.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अडचणींवर मात केली, tyāne aḍcaṇīṁvar māt kelī.
The one beaten takes -वर: संघावर मात करणे (māt karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
मात करणे (māt karṇe) is मात, a loan from Persian māt, "checkmated", usually traced to Arabic, with करणे.
