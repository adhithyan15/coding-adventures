## You'll want to know: نصر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C315-NASR315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نصر** — *naṣr* — "a victory in battle".

> احتفل الناس بالنصر في الشوارع.
>
> *iḥtafala an-nās bi-n-naṣr fī ash-shawāriʿ.*
>
> People celebrated the victory in the streets.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نصر** (*naṣr*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ṣ-r* (Lane), "to aid someone against an enemy". Help that wins the day is victory.
