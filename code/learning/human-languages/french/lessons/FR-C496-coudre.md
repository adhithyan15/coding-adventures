## You'll want to know: coudre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C496-COUTURE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**coudre** — "to sew".

> Ma grand-mère m'a appris à coudre.
>
> *My grandmother taught me to sew.*

Irregular: *je couds, nous cousons*; past *j'ai cousu*. *Coudre un bouton* — to sew on a button.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coudre* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *cōsere* (EtymDB), a form of Latin *cōnsuere* "to sew together", built on *suere* "to sew" (Lewis & Short).
