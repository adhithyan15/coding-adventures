## You'll want to know: ранить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C297-TRAVMA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ранить** (*ránit*) — "to wound".

> Его ранили в руку.
>
> *Yevó ránili v rúku.*
>
> He was wounded in the arm.

One form for both aspects. *Раненый* — wounded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ранить* is built on *рана* "a wound", inherited from Proto-Slavic *rana*; EtymDB lists the verb with no parent.
