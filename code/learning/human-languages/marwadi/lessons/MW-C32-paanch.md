# पांच — five on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HAAN-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAR-01, MW-PERFORMANCE-FOOD-FIVE-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-LEX-LAVO-01] -->

[PAUSE 22s] Write the respectful yes, write four, say the bring-it word, and
recall the five-word food payoff.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-PAANCH-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-PA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-HAAN-01] -->

> **प** + **ा** + **ं** + **च** → **पांच** — *pāṅch* — five

The dot above the head-line is the anusvara from chapter 3. It sits over
**हां** and it sits over **पां** for the same reason: the vowel under it is
nasal.

That is the whole cost of the first five numbers — one letter, **ए**, and one
mark the hand had already been making since the third chapter.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-PAANCH-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAR-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **पांच**, cover it, wait five seconds, write it. Check the dot. Then
write **चार** beneath it and mark the **च** in both.
