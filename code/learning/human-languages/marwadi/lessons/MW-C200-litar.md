# लीटर (līṭar) — a litre

## You'll want to know: लीटर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C200-WEIGH200-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लीटर** — *līṭar* — "a litre".

> एक लीटर दूध दो।
>
> *ek līṭar dūdh do.*
>
> Give me a litre of milk.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लीटर** (*līṭar*) is borrowed from English *litre*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
