## You'll want to know: тошнить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C485-NAUSEA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**тошнить** (*toshnít*) — "to feel sick (impersonal)".

> В автобусе меня часто тошнит.
>
> *V avtóbuse menyá chásto toshnít.*
>
> I often feel sick on the bus.

Impersonal: *меня тошнит* — I feel sick. Imperfective; perfective стошнить — to be sick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тошнить* is built on *тошно* 'sickening, miserable'; EtymDB has no entry, and the root is usually linked to the one in *тоска* 'longing'.
