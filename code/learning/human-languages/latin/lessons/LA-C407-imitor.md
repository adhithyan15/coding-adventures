## You'll want to know: imitor, imitārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C407-SCIENTIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**imitor, imitārī** — "to imitate, to copy".

> Fīlius patrem imitātur.
>
> *The son imitates his father.*

Deponent: *imitor, imitārī, imitātus sum*, with the accusative. English *imitate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *imitor* is not settled; Lewis & Short give no sure account, though it is often grouped with the word for an image. English *imitate* comes from it.
