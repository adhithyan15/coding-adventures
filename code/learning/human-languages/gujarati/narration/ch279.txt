Gujarati, chapter 279: Asking the way.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
રીત (rīt) — a way; a manner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "it was raining, so we stayed at home". (varsād paḍto hato, eṭle ame ghare rahyā.) Then "by bus". (બસમાં.)

You'll want to know: રીત.
રીત — rīt — "a way; a manner": the way a thing is done. It is feminine: તેની રીત, "his way".
તેની રીત સારી છે. tenī rīt sārī che. — "His way of doing it is good."
કેવી રીતે (kevī rīte), "in what way", is how Gujarati says "how": કેવી asks "what kind of", agreeing with રીત (rīt), and રીતે takes the -ે of સવારે.
તે કેવી રીતે આવી? te kevī rīte āvī? — "How did she come?"
The answer can be બસમાં: by bus.

The word, taken apart.
રીત (rīt) goes back to Sanskrit rīti, "going, a course", and later "a way of doing things" (Monier-Williams). CDIAL's entry for rīti (CDIAL 10751) lists an older Gujarati rī, "method, manner"; રીત keeps the Sanskrit -t, so it is probably the learned form.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: rīt, then kevī rīte]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "How did she come?" — te kevī rīte āvī?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "How did you come?", with respect — tame kevī rīte āvyā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does રીત (rīt) mean? ("A way; a manner".) "How?" (કેવી રીતે?) Why કેવી, not કેવો? (રીત (rīt) is feminine.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
સ્ટેશન કેવી રીતે જવું? (sṭeśan kevī rīte javũ?) — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "how?" (kevī rīte?) Then "where" and "far". (ક્યાં, દૂર.) And "excuse me". (માફ કરો.)

Grammar Lens: three questions.
માફ કરો, સ્ટેશન કેવી રીતે જવું? māph karo, sṭeśan kevī rīte javũ? — "Excuse me, how do I get to the station?"
The verb stays in its -વું form: "how to go to the station?" Three questions do most of the work: where it is, સ્ટેશન ક્યાં છે?; how far, કેટલું દૂર છે?; and how, સ્ટેશન કેવી રીતે જવું? (sṭeśan kevī rīte javũ?) One more asks to be shown: મને રસ્તો બતાવશો? It is a polite future, "will you show me the way?": a request, not an order.

You'll want to know: the answer.
સીધા જાઓ, પછી પુલ પાસે ડાબી બાજુ વળો. sīdhā jāo, pachī pul pāse ḍābī bāju vaḷo. — "Go straight, then turn left at the bridge."
Directions are said with -ઓ, the form of માફ કરો: જાઓ, વળો. ડાબી and જમણી follow બાજુ, which is feminine. Other pieces: બેંકની સામે (beṅknī sāme), "opposite the bank"; સિગ્નલ પછી (signal pachī), "after the signal"; દૂર નથી, દસ મિનિટ છે (dūr nathī, das miniṭ che), "it's not far, ten minutes".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Will you show me the way?" — mane rasto batāvśo?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It's opposite the bank" — beṅknī sāme che]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Ask how to get to the station, then give the answer: go straight, then turn right after the signal.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"How far is it?" (કેટલું દૂર છે?) "How do I get to the station?" (સ્ટેશન કેવી રીતે જવું? (sṭeśan kevī rīte javũ?)) "Turn left"? (ડાબી બાજુ વળો.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "you should go to the doctor". (tamāre dāktar pāse javũ joīe.) Then "so", the everyday way. (eṭle.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Excuse me, where is the station? How far is it?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: How do I get to the station? Will you show me the way?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Go straight, then turn left at the bridge.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It's not far, ten minutes. It's opposite the bank.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: How did you come? By bus, because the train was late. Now turn it round, with eṭle.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"How?" (કેવી રીતે?) Which form gives a direction? (-ઓ: જાઓ, વળો.) Why ડાબી બાજુ? (બાજુ is feminine.)
