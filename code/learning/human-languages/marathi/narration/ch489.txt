Lesson 1 of 6.
लस (las) — a vaccine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a diagnosis, then the Marathi for treatment.

You'll want to know: लस.
लस — las — "a vaccine".
लहान बाळांना वेळेवर लस द्यायला हवी. lahān bāḷānnā veḷevar las dyāylā havī. Small babies should be vaccinated on time.
Feminine.
लस (las) देणे, to vaccinate; लस (las) घेणे, to get vaccinated.

The word, taken apart.
लस (las) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi las, "serum, pus", under the reconstructed lasa, "stickiness" (CDIAL 10992). The sense "vaccine" is modern, usually explained by the serum vaccines were made from.

Lesson 2 of 6.
शस्त्रक्रिया (śastrakriyā) — an operation (surgery).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: शस्त्रक्रिया.
शस्त्रक्रिया — śastrakriyā — "an operation (surgery)".
आजोबांच्या डोळ्याची शस्त्रक्रिया यशस्वी झाली. ājobāñcyā ḍoḷyācī śastrakriyā yaśasvī jhālī. Grandfather's eye operation was a success.
Feminine.
शस्त्र, a blade, + क्रिया, an act.

The word, taken apart.
शस्त्रक्रिया (śastrakriyā) is a modern learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, śastra, "knife, blade", and kriyā, "doing, act" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 6.
भूल (bhūl) — anaesthesia.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a vaccine, then the Marathi for an operation.

You'll want to know: भूल.
भूल — bhūl — "anaesthesia".
शस्त्रक्रियेआधी त्याला भूल दिली. śastrakriyeādhī tyālā bhūl dilī. He was given anaesthesia before the operation.
Feminine.
भूल (bhūl) देणे, to anaesthetise. The same word means a slip of memory.

The word, taken apart.
भूल (bhūl) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi bhulṇẽ, "errs, forgets", under the reconstructed bhull (CDIAL 9538); भूल is the noun, and modern Marathi gave it the sense "anaesthesia".

Lesson 4 of 6.
टाका (ṭākā) — a stitch (in a wound).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an operation, then the Marathi for anaesthesia.

You'll want to know: टाका.
टाका — ṭākā — "a stitch (in a wound)".
जखमेला चार टाके घातले. jakhmelā cār ṭāke ghātle. They put four stitches in the wound.
Masculine. Plural: टाके, ṭāke.
Usually plural, टाके: टाके घालणे, to put in stitches; टाके काढणे, to take them out.

The word, taken apart.
टाका (ṭākā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ṭākā, of stitching, under Sanskrit ṭaṅkati, "ties" (CDIAL 5432).

Lesson 5 of 6.
टोचणे (ṭocṇe) — to prick; to inject.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for anaesthesia, then the Marathi for a stitch.

You'll want to know: टोचणे.
टोचणे — ṭocṇe — "to prick; to inject".
परिचारिकेने माझ्या हातावर सुई टोचली. paricārikene mājhyā hātāvar suī ṭoclī. The nurse pricked my arm with a needle.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सुई टोचली, tyāne suī ṭoclī.
Of thorns, needles and injections: काटा टोचला, a thorn pricked.

The word, taken apart.
टोचणे (ṭocṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled; CDIAL lists a different Marathi verb of pricking, ṭoviṇẽ, under trōpati (CDIAL 6083).
