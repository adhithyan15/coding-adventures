## You'll want to know: पित्तम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C484-AYURVEDA484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पित्तम्** — *pittam* — "bile".

> ग्रीष्मे पित्तं वर्धते।
>
> *grīṣme pittaṃ vardhate.*
>
> Bile increases in summer.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पित्तम्** (*pittam*) MW calls its etymology unknown, so its origin is unknown; it gives "bile, the bilious humour, one of the three humours" (MW).
