## You'll want to know: acordar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C634-PACTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**acordar** — "to agree on".

> Acordamos una fecha.
>
> *We agreed on a date.*

*Acordar algo* — to agree on it. Stem change o→ue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acordar* is inherited from spoken Latin *accordare*, built on *cor* 'heart' (EtymDB): to agree is to be of one heart.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
