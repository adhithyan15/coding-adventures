## You'll want to know: würfeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C323-SCHAELEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**würfeln** — "to dice; to throw dice".

> Dann würfelte er die Karotten.
>
> *Then he diced the carrots.*

To cut into small cubes. With dice, *würfeln* is to throw them.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*würfeln* is built on *Würfel* "a dice", Old High German *wurfil*, Proto-Germanic *wurpilaz* (EtymDB), from the root of *werfen* "to throw".
