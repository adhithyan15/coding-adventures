Kannada, chapter 123: Vegetables, Banana, Mango, Coconut, Onion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತರಕಾರಿ (tarakāri) — vegetables.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a fish, then the Kannada for meat.

You'll want to know: ತರಕಾರಿ.
ತರಕಾರಿ — tarakāri — "vegetables".

The word, taken apart.
ತರಕಾರಿ (tarakāri) came from Hindustani tarkārī, "vegetables, a cooked vegetable dish", a Persian word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು (bāḷehaṇṇu) — a banana.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for meat, then the Kannada for vegetables.

You'll want to know: ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು.
ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು — bāḷehaṇṇu — "a banana".

The word, taken apart.
ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು (bāḷehaṇṇu) is native Dravidian: bāḷe, "plantain" (DEDR 5373, with Tamil vāḻai), and haṇṇu, "fruit" (DEDR 4004, older Kannada paṇṇu). DEDR 5373 compares Sanskrit vārabuṣā. DEDR 4004 compares Sanskrit phala.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾವಿನಹಣ್ಣು (māvinahaṇṇu) is native Dravidian: māvina, "of the mango tree", from māvu, "mango tree" (DEDR 4782, with Tamil mā), and haṇṇu, "fruit" (DEDR 4004). DEDR 4782 compares Sanskrit mākanda. DEDR 4004 compares Sanskrit phala.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a banana, then the Kannada for a mango.

You'll want to know: ತೆಂಗು.
ತೆಂಗು — teṅgu — "a coconut".

The word, taken apart.
ತೆಂಗು (teṅgu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil teṅku and Telugu teṅkāya (DEDR 3408). DEDR lists teṅgu itself, "coconut palm".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ (īruḷḷi) — an onion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mango, then the Kannada for a coconut.

You'll want to know: ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ.
ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ — īruḷḷi — "an onion".

The word, taken apart.
ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ (īruḷḷi) ends in uḷḷi, "bulb, onion", native Dravidian (DEDR 705, with Tamil uḷḷi). DEDR does not explain the first part, īr-. DEDR 705 compares Sanskrit ūlī.
