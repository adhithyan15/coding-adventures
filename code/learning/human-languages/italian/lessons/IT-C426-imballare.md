## You'll want to know: imballare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C426-BAGAGLI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**imballare** — "to pack, to wrap (for transport)".

> Imballiamo bene i piatti per il viaggio.
>
> *We pack the plates carefully for the journey.*

*L'imballaggio* — the packaging. For clothes in a case you say *fare la valigia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imballare* is built from *in-* and *balla* 'bale', which is usually traced to a Germanic word that came into Italian as a borrowing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
