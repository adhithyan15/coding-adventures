# Sanskrit A1 mock rubric

This rubric scores either A1 mock against
[`task-shapes/a1.json`](../../task-shapes/a1.json). Reading, listening, writing,
and speaking are separate 100-point papers. A learner passes only by earning at
least **60/100 on every paper** in the same administration. The 240-point sum
cannot compensate for a failed skill, and every required part must be attempted.

## Reading — 100 points

- signs and forms: 35 points across 7 keyed items;
- short messages: 35 points across 7 keyed items;
- personal descriptions: 30 points across 6 keyed items.

Divide each part evenly among its items. Score only the selected answer. A1
sources preserve visible word boundaries; correct external sandhi is accepted
but never required.

## Listening — 100 points

- announcements: 35 points across 6 keyed items;
- short exchanges: 35 points across 6 keyed items;
- personal account: 30 points across 5 keyed items.

Use only the confidential mastered files, twice each, at 90–110 words per
minute and inside the task-shape duration bands. Do not show a transcript or
romanization. Divide a part's points evenly and retain one decimal place.

## Writing — 100 points

- practical form: 40 points;
- 30–40 word message for the named reader and purpose: 60 points.

Score each task analytically on four equal dimensions: task fulfilment and
relevant content; comprehensibility and organisation; Sanskrit vocabulary and
grammar; and Devanagari orthographic control. On the form, seven requested
fields provide the content ceiling. On the message, a response outside the
30–40 word band can still earn language credit but cannot earn full task
fulfilment.

Orthographic control explicitly includes recognisable ordered letter bodies,
vowel signs and conjuncts, a continuous śirorekhā where the written word
requires it, the correct word-final visarga or anusvara, and visible unjoined
word boundaries. Correct external sandhi is accepted without bonus or penalty.
A romanized, traced, copied, or answer-model-dependent response is not
independent Sanskrit writing evidence.

## Speaking — 100 points

- personal interview: 30 points;
- one-minute prepared description: 30 points;
- transactional role-play: 40 points.

For each part, score four equal dimensions: task fulfilment and relevant
content; comprehensibility, organisation, and interaction; Sanskrit vocabulary
and grammar; and pronunciation and fluency. Accept a consistent documented
pronunciation tradition. The examiner may repeat a prompt once but may not
supply an answer. Support-language-only speech earns no Sanskrit task credit.

## Evidence boundary

These sources, keys, and rubrics make two parallel project-defined A1 forms
executable. They are not qualified Sanskrit recordings, independent human
review, standard setting, or book-only validation. No readiness or validated
pass claim follows from checking in source masters. Those artifacts remain
separate prerequisites, and `assessment.json` remains absent until they exist.
