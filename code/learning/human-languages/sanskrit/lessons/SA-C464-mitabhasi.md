## You'll want to know: मितभाषी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C464-VACALA464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मितभाषी** — *mitabhāṣī* — "of few words".

> मम पिता मितभाषी अस्ति।
>
> *mama pitā mitabhāṣī asti.*
>
> My father is a man of few words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मितभाषी** (*mitabhāṣī*) is built from *mita*, "measured", and *bhāṣin*, "speaking", from the root √bhāṣ of **भाषते**: one who speaks by measure (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
