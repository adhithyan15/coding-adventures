## You'll want to know: ಕೈತೋಟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C546-NEMMADI546-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈತೋಟ** — *kaitōṭa* — "a kitchen garden".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಕೈತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಟೊಮೇಟೊ ಬೆಳೆಯುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *namma kaitōṭadalli ṭomēṭo beḷeyuttade.*
>
> Tomatoes grow in our kitchen garden.

ಕೈ, hand + ತೋಟ, garden: a garden small enough to tend by hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈತೋಟ** (*kaitōṭa*) is a Kannada compound: native *kai*, "hand" (DEDR 2023), and *tōṭa*, "a garden".
