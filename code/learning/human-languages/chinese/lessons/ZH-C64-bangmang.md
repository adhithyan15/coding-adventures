## You'll want to know: bāngmáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C64-HELP64-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bāngmáng* — "to help out; to do a favour".

> *wǒ xiǎng bāngmáng* — I'd like to help.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bāngmáng* is built from bāng 'to help' and máng 'busy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pang* and *mang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses *mang* as 'afraid', so the sense busy is not given there; helping with what keeps somebody busy is the likely picture, but it is not settled.
