# گ (gāf) — the letter that opens گرمی

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C36-RRE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C36-DO-CHASHMI-HE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ڑ and ھ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **گرمی** (*garmī*), "heat".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: گ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C36-GAF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گ** — *gāf*, the sound *g*.

It opens **گرمی** (*garmī*), "heat". It is **ک**, which you already write, with a
**second slash** above the first.

## Writing: گ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C36-GAF-01] -->

- **1.** start here: draw the independent stem downward
- **2.** flow right to left through the flatter bowl and finish with the hook without lifting
- **3.** after one lift, draw the long slash down from the upper right toward the stem
- **4.** after another lift, draw the shorter floating slash above the first — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, Pe, gāf, alif, and lām, independent گ calligraphic and handwriting animations and Gāf instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).
