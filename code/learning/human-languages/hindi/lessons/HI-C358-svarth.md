## You'll want to know: स्वार्थ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C358-SEVA358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वार्थ** — *svārth* — "self-interest, selfishness".

> उसकी हर बात में स्वार्थ होता है।
>
> *uskī har bāt meṁ svārth hotā hai.*
>
> There is self-interest in everything he says.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वार्थ** (*svārth*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *svārtha*, "one's own aim" (Monier-Williams).
