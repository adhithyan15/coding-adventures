## You'll want to know: वाहक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C399-VAHAK399-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहक** — *vāhak* — "a bus conductor".

> वाहकाने माझं तिकीट तपासलं.
>
> *vāhakāne mājhaṁ tikīṭ tapāslaṁ.*
>
> The conductor checked my ticket.

Masculine.

On a bus the चालक drives and the वाहक sells and checks tickets.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहक** (*vāhak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vāhaka*, "one who carries, a carrier" (Monier-Williams); on a bus it names the conductor.
