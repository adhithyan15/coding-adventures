German, chapter 271: Die Meldung.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
melden — to report, to announce; reflexive: to get in touch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to go, then the German for the sequence of events.

You'll want to know: melden.
melden — "to report, to announce; reflexive: to get in touch".
Die Zeitung meldete einen großen Unfall. The newspaper reported a big accident.
Reflexive, it means to get in touch: Ich melde mich morgen.

The word, taken apart.
melden is inherited from Old High German meldōn, which EtymDB traces to Proto-Germanic melþōną "to make known, to give away".

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Meldung — the news item, the announcement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the sequence of events, then the German for to report.

You'll want to know: die Meldung.
die Meldung — "the news item, the announcement".
Die Meldung kam am Abend im Radio. The news item came on the radio in the evening.
Plural: die Meldungen. Also an official notice to an office.

The word, taken apart.
Meldung is built on melden "to report", inherited from Old High German meldōn (EtymDB), with the noun ending -ung.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Bericht — the report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to report, then the German for the news item.

You'll want to know: der Bericht.
der Bericht — "the report".
Der Bericht war sehr lang. The report was very long.
Plural: die Berichte. Built on berichten, already known.

The word, taken apart.
Bericht comes from Middle High German beriht, built on berihten "to put in order, to instruct" (EtymDB), a relative of richten and recht.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Schlagzeile — the headline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the news item, then the German for the report.

You'll want to know: die Schlagzeile.
die Schlagzeile — "the headline".
Die Schlagzeile war groß und rot. The headline was big and red.
Plural: die Schlagzeilen. Schlagzeilen machen — to hit the headlines.

The word, taken apart.
Schlagzeile joins Schlag "a blow", from schlagen "to strike", and Zeile "a line"; both are inherited Germanic words. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Artikel — the article.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the report, then the German for the headline.

You'll want to know: der Artikel.
der Artikel — "the article".
Ich habe einen interessanten Artikel über Bienen gelesen. I read an interesting article about bees.
Plural: die Artikel. Also the grammar word: der, die and das are Artikel.

The word, taken apart.
Artikel is a learned borrowing from Latin articulus "a joint, a part, the article in grammar" (EtymDB), a diminutive of artus "joint" (Lewis & Short).
