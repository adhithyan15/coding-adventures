## You'll want to know: líkāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C107-MOVE107-05]; assesses=[] -->

*líkāi* — "to leave a place".

> *wǒmen jǐ diǎn líkāi* — What time are we leaving?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*líkāi* is built from lí 'to part' and 开 'to open', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lje* and *khoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary says parting at a short distance is *lje*, at a long one is another word.
