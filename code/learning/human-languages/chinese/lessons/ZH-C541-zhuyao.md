## You'll want to know: zhǔyào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C541-FINAL541-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔyào* — "main; chief".

> *wǒ bú qù de zhǔyào yuányīn shì tiānqì bù hǎo* — The main reason I'm not going is the bad weather.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔyào* is built from *zhǔ* 'to be in charge' and 要 'important', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyuX* and *'jiewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to manage, to lead' and the second 'to bind by agreement'.

## What you've built
