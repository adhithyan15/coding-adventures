## You'll want to know: かいがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C385-KAIGAI385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かいがい** — *kaigai* — "overseas; abroad".

> **らいねん かいがいに いきたいです。** — *rainen kaigai ni ikitai desu* — "I want to go abroad next year."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいがい** (*kaigai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "sea" (Middle Chinese *xojX*) and *gai*, "outside" (*ngwajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
