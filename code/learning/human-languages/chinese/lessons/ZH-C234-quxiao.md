## You'll want to know: qǔxiāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C234-RAIN234-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qǔxiāo* — "to cancel; to call off".

> *xiàyǔ le bǐsài qǔxiāo le* — It's raining, so the match is off.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qǔxiāo* is built from *qǔ* 'to take' and *xiāo* 'to melt away', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjuX* and *sjew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to take in' and the second 'to wipe out' and 'to use up'.
