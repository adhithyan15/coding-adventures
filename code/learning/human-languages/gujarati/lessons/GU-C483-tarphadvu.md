# તરફડવું (tarphaḍvũ) — to writhe; to toss in pain

## You'll want to know: તરફડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C483-VEDNA483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**તરફડવું** — *tarphaḍvũ* — "to writhe; to toss in pain".

> પેટના દુખાવાથી દર્દી આખી રાત તરફડતો હતો.
>
> *peṭnā dukhāvāthī dardī ākhī rāt tarphaḍto hato.*
>
> The patient was writhing all night with a stomach ache.

Past (he …): તે દુખાવાથી તરફડ્યો, *te dukhāvāthī tarphaḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તરફડવું** (*tarphaḍvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*taḍapphaḍ-*, "agitate"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to toss about, be agitated" (CDIAL 5634).
