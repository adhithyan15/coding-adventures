## You'll want to know: هتف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C353-MUZAHARA353-03]; assesses=[] -->

**هتف** — *hatafa* — "to cheer, to chant".

> هتف الأطفال فرحا.
>
> *hatafa al-aṭfāl faraḥan.*
>
> The children cheered for joy.

Present: يهتف, *yahtif*.

The taught هاتف, a telephone, is the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هتف** (*hatafa*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *h-t-f*, "to call out", where *hātif* was a voice heard when the speaker is not seen; modern Arabic gave that word the telephone.
