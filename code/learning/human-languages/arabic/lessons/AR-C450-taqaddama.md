## You'll want to know: تقدم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C450-BALADIYYA450-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تقدم** — *taqaddama* — "to apply (for); to step forward".

> تقدمت أختي بطلب تأشيرة.
>
> *taqaddamat ukhtī bi-ṭalab taʾshīra.*
>
> My sister applied for a visa.

Present: يتقدم, *yataqaddam*.

*taqaddama bi-ṭalab*: to put in an application.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تقدم** (*taqaddama*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-d-m*, "to come forward, to precede" (Lane). It is Form V.
