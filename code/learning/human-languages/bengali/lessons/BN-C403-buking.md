## You'll want to know: বুকিং
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C403-GHOR403-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বুকিং** — *buking* — "a booking; a reservation".

> আমরা এক সপ্তাহ আগে হোটেলের বুকিং করলাম।
>
> *āmrā ek sôptāhô āge hoṭeler buking kôrlām.*
>
> We made the hotel booking a week ahead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বুকিং** (*buking*) is borrowed from English *booking*; বুক করা, to book, is heard too, but বুক alone is the chest.
