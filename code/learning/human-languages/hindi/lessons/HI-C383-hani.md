## You'll want to know: हानि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C383-SANKAT383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हानि** — *hāni* — "damage, harm (formal)".

> अगर तुम रात भर जागोगे तो सेहत को हानि होगी।
>
> *agar tum rāt bhar jāgoge to sehat ko hāni hogī.*
>
> If you stay up all night, your health will suffer.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हानि** (*hāni*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *hāni*, "giving up; loss" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
