# When you are lost, and short answers — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-02, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You did not understand. Say so, and ask for it once more.

## Grammar Lens: two ways to say I don't know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-04, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-01] -->

| phrase | literally |
|---|---|
| **मुझे नहीं पता** | to me, no knowing |
| **मालूम नहीं** | not known |

Both keep the knower out of the subject slot, the way Hindi does for wanting.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-01, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-02, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-03, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-04, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-05, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-01, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-02, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-03, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-04, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: ask what someone said; ask for it one more time]
- [YOU SAY: say you don't know, two ways]
- [YOU SAY: say "not at all"; then "truly?"]
- [YOU SAY: show you are listening politely; accept a plan with "that'll do"]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-01, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-02, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-03, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-04, HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-05, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-01, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-02, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-03, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-04, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Once more? (**फिर से**.) Didn't understand? (**समझ नहीं आया**.) What did you say?
(**क्या कहा?**) I don't know? (**मुझे नहीं पता**.) One more time? (**एक बार और**.) Don't know?
(**मालूम नहीं**.) Not at all? (**बिलकुल नहीं**.) Truly? (**सचमुच**.) Is that so? (**अच्छा जी**.)
That'll do? (**चलेगा**.)
