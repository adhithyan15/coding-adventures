## You'll want to know: l'égout
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C443-HIVER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'égout** — "the sewer, the drain".

> Après l'orage, les égouts sont pleins.
>
> *After the storm the sewers are full.*

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*égout* is built on *égoutter* 'to let drip, to drain' (EtymDB), from *goutte* 'drop', which goes back to Latin *gutta*.
