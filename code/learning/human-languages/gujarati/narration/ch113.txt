Lesson 1 of 5.
ખિસ્સું (khissũ) — a pocket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a sweater, then the Gujarati for a coat.

You'll want to know: ખિસ્સું.
ખિસ્સું (khissũ) — "a pocket".

The word, taken apart.
ખિસ્સું (khissũ) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kīsa, "purse, pouch".
