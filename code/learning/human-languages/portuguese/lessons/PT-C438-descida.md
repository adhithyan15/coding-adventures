## You'll want to know: a descida
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C438-SUBIDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a descida** — "the way down, the descent".

> Na descida, vamos devagar por causa das pedras.
>
> *On the way down, we go slowly because of the stones.*

Also a fall: *uma descida da temperatura*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*descida* is built on *descer* 'to go down', which is inherited from Latin *dēscendere*, on *scandere* 'to climb' (Lewis & Short).
