# চকিতে (chôkite) — in a flash; in an instant

## You'll want to know: চকিতে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C287-MUHURTO287-02]; assesses=[] -->

**চকিতে** — *chôkite* — "in a flash; in an instant".

> চকিতে চোরটা জানালা দিয়ে লাফিয়ে পালাল।
>
> *chôkite chorṭā jānālā diye lāphiye pālālô.*
>
> In a flash the thief jumped out of the window and ran.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চকিতে** (*chôkite*) is Sanskrit *cakita*, 'trembling, startled' (Monier-Williams), in the -e form: 'in a start'. The sense 'in a flash' is the Bengali one.
