## You'll want to know: みんよう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C470-SONG470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**みんよう** — *min'yō* — "a folk song".

> **そぼは みんようを うたいます。** — *sobo wa min'yō o utaimasu* — "My grandmother sings folk songs."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みんよう** (*min'yō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *min*, "people" (Middle Chinese *mjin*) and *yō*, "song" (*yew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
