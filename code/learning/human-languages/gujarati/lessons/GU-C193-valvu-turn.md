## You'll want to know: વળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C193-WAY193-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વળવું** — *vaḷvũ* — "to turn".

> **બસ મંદિર પાસે ડાબે વળે છે.** — *bas mandir pāse ḍābe vaḷe che* — "The bus turns left by the temple."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વળવું** (*vaḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *válati*, "turns, turns to", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to turn, turn round, bend" (CDIAL 11405).
