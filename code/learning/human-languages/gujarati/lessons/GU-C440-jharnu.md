# ઝરણું (jharṇũ) — a mountain stream; a spring

## You'll want to know: ઝરણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C440-STREAM440-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝરણું** — *jharṇũ* — "a mountain stream; a spring".

> પર્વત પરથી ઠંડું ઝરણું વહે છે.
>
> *parvat parthī ṭhanḍũ jharṇũ vahe che.*
>
> A cold stream flows down the mountain.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝરણું** (*jharṇũ*) is built on ઝરવું, to ooze, to trickle, which CDIAL lists under *\*jharati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5346): water that trickles out of the rock.
