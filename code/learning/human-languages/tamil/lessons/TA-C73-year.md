## You'll want to know: ஆண்டு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆண்டு** (*āṇḍu*) — "a year".

A year — and it is the unit hiding inside a question you can already ask.

When you ask about someone's வயது (*vayathu*), their age, what is being counted is ஆண்டு. The
question was yours a long time ago; this is the word for what the answer is made of.

It opens on the free-standing **ஆ**, because the word begins with that vowel — the same reason
இன்று opens on இ. In the middle is the **ண்ட** you first read in வண்டி.

Three sizes now, each holding the one before it: நாள், மாதம், ஆண்டு.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Five: today, yesterday, and the three units above the day.
