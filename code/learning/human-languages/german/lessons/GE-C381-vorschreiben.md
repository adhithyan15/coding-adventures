## You'll want to know: vorschreiben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C381-VORSCHRIFT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**vorschreiben** — "to lay down (a rule), to prescribe".

> Das Gesetz schreibt vor, dass Kinder im Auto einen Kindersitz brauchen.
>
> *The law lays down that children need a child seat in the car.*

Separable and strong: *es schreibt vor*, *es hat vorgeschrieben*. *Du kannst mir nichts vorschreiben* — you cannot tell me what to do.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vorschreiben* is built from *vor* "before" and *schreiben*, from Old High German *scrīban*, a very early borrowing from Latin *scrībere* (EtymDB); EtymDB lists English *scribe* as a cousin.
