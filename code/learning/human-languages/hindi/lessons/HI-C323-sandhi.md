## You'll want to know: संधि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C323-VIJAY323-05]; assesses=[] -->

**संधि** — *sandhi* — "a treaty; a peace pact".

> आख़िर में दोनों राजाओं ने संधि की।
>
> *ākhir meṁ donõ rājāoṁ ne sandhi kī.*
>
> In the end the two kings made a treaty.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संधि** (*sandhi*) is Sanskrit *saṁdhi*, "a joining, an agreement" (Monier-Williams). Grammar uses the same word for the joining of sounds; history uses it for the joining of two sides.
