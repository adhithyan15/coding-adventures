## You'll want to know: خطط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C523-INJAZ523-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خطط** — *khaṭṭaṭa* — "to make plans".

> يخطط أخي لرحلة في الصيف.
>
> *yukhaṭṭiṭu akhī li-riḥla fī aṣ-ṣayf.*
>
> My brother is planning a trip in the summer.

Present: يخطط, *yukhaṭṭiṭ*.

It takes لـ for what is planned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خطط** (*khaṭṭaṭa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-ṭ*, "to draw a line" (Lane). Khuṭṭa, a plan, which you know, is from the same root: a plan is lines drawn ahead.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
