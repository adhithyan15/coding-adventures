Arabic, chapter 503: Tempers and habits.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
متسامح (mutasāmiḥ) — tolerant, forgiving.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for affectionate, then the Arabic for friendly.

You'll want to know: متسامح.
متسامح — mutasāmiḥ — "tolerant, forgiving".
أبي رجل متسامح ولا يغضب كثيرا. abī rajul mutasāmiḥ wa-lā yaghḍabu kathīran. My father is a tolerant man and does not get angry often.

The word, taken apart.
متسامح (mutasāmiḥ) is built on the native Arabic root s-m-ḥ, "to be generous, to allow" (Lane). Samaḥa, to allow, and sāmaḥa, to forgive, which you know, share the root.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for tolerant, then the Arabic for irritable.

You'll want to know: مرح.
مرح — mariḥ — "cheerful, fun-loving".
خالي رجل مرح ويضحك كثيرا. khālī rajul mariḥ wa-yaḍḥaku kathīran. My uncle is a cheerful man and laughs a lot.
The noun مرح (mariḥ), read maraḥ, is merriment.

The word, taken apart.
مرح (mariḥ) is built on the native Arabic root m-r-ḥ, "to be lively, merry" (Lane). Mariḥ is the adjective; maraḥ, with the same letters, is the noun.

مكتب أختي منظم ونظيف. maktab ukhtī munaẓẓam wa-naẓīf. My sister's desk is tidy and clean.

منظم (munaẓẓam) is built on the native Arabic root n-ẓ-m, "to string pearls in order" (Lane). Munaẓẓam is the passive participle of Form II: set in order.

مهمل — muhmil — "careless".
كسر العامل المهمل الزجاج. kasara al-ʿāmil al-muhmil az-zujāj. The careless worker broke the glass.

The word, taken apart.
مهمل (muhmil) is built on the native Arabic root h-m-l, "to leave neglected" (Lane). Muhmil is the participle of Form IV: one who leaves things neglected.
