## You'll want to know: आचारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C358-ACHARA358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आचारः** — *ācāraḥ* — "conduct; custom".

> मीरायाः आचारः उत्तमः अस्ति।
>
> *mīrāyāḥ ācāraḥ uttamaḥ asti.*
>
> Mira's conduct is excellent.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आचारः** (*ācāraḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √car, "to move"; MW gives *ā-cāra* "conduct, behaviour" and "custom, practice" (MW).
