## You'll want to know: వార్షికోత్సవం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C355-VARSHIKAM355-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వార్షికోత్సవం** — *vārṣikōtsavaṁ* — "an anniversary".

> మా బడి వార్షికోత్సవం ఘనంగా జరిగింది.
>
> *mā baḍi vārṣikōtsavaṁ ghanaṅgā jarigindi.*
>
> Our school's anniversary was celebrated in style.

వార్షిక, yearly: a yearly celebration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వార్షికోత్సవం** (*vārṣikōtsavaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *vārṣika*, "annual" (CDIAL 11571), and *utsava*, "a festival" (CDIAL 1876).
