## You'll want to know: elementum, elementī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C404-RHETORICA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**elementum, elementī** — "a first principle, an element; (plural) the ABC".

> Puer prīma elementa discit.
>
> *The boy is learning his ABC.*

*elementum, elementī* n. In the plural, *elementa* are the letters, the first steps of any subject, and the four elements: earth, water, air and fire. English *element*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *elementum* is not settled: Lewis & Short connect it with *alō* "to nourish", and EtymDB pairs it with Greek *stoicheion* "an element", the word it translates. English *element* comes from it.
