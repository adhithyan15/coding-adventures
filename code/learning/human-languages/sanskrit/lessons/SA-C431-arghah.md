## You'll want to know: अर्घः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C431-ARGHA431-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्घः** — *arghaḥ* — "worth; value".

> रत्नस्य अर्घः महान् अस्ति।
>
> *ratnasya arghaḥ mahān asti.*
>
> The jewel's worth is great.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्घः** (*arghaḥ*) comes from the root √arh, "to deserve, to be worth"; MW gives *argha* as worth, value, price (MW).
