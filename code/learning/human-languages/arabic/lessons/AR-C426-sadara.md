## You'll want to know: صادر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C426-JAMARIK426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صادر** — *ṣādara* — "to confiscate".

> صادر موظف الجمارك العسل من حقيبتي.
>
> *ṣādara muwaẓẓaf al-jamārik al-ʿasal min ḥaqībatī.*
>
> The customs officer confiscated the honey from my bag.

Present: يصادر, *yuṣādir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صادر** (*ṣādara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-d-r*, "to come forth" (Lane). It is Form III.
