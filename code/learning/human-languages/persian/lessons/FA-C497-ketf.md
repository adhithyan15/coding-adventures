## You'll want to know: کتف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C497-BONES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کتف** (*ketf*) — "a shoulder blade; shoulder".

> او کیف سنگینی روی کتفش داشت.
>
> *u kif-e sangini ru-ye ketfash dâsht.*
>
> She had a heavy bag on her shoulder.

*shâne* is the shoulder and *ketf* the shoulder blade, but in speech they overlap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ketf* comes from Arabic *kitf*, 'shoulder' (EtymDB).
