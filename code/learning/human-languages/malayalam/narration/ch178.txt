Malayalam, chapter 178: Recently, Already, Last, Once, To Spend (Time).
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
അടുത്തിടെ (aṭuttiṭe) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for anyone, then the Malayalam for anything.

You'll want to know: അടുത്തിടെ.
അടുത്തിടെ — aṭuttiṭe — "recently". Malayalam's past does not change for "I", "you" or "he": ഞാൻ അടുത്തിടെ ഒരു പുസ്തകം വായിച്ചു — ñān aṭuttiṭe oru pustakaṁ vāyiccu — "I recently read a book."
