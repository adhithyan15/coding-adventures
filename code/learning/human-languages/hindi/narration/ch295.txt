Lesson 1 of 6.
गिरफ़्तार करना (giraftār karnā) — to arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for evidence, then the Hindi for to search for.

You'll want to know: गिरफ़्तार करना.
गिरफ़्तार करना — giraftār karnā — "to arrest".
अगर पुलिस उसे गिरफ़्तार करेगी तो वह जेल जाएगा। agar pulis use giraftār karegī to vah jel jāegā. If the police arrest him, he will go to prison.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चोर को गिरफ़्तार किया, usne cor ko giraftār kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
गिरफ़्तार करना (giraftār karnā) is Persian giriftār, "seized, taken captive", usually traced to the Persian verb for taking hold, with करना. The person arrested takes को: चोर को गिरफ़्तार किया.

Lesson 3 of 6.
सज़ा (sazā) — a punishment; a sentence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to arrest, then the Hindi for a court case.

You'll want to know: सज़ा.
सज़ा — sazā — "a punishment; a sentence".
अगर आपको सज़ा का डर है, तो सच कहिए। agar āpko sazā kā ḍar hai, to sac kahiye. If you are afraid of punishment, tell the truth.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सज़ा (sazā) is usually traced to Persian sazā, "what is fitting, what one deserves". It is feminine: अदालत ने चोर को दो साल की सज़ा दी, the court gave the thief two years.

चोर को पाँच हज़ार रुपये का जुर्माना भरना पड़ा। cor ko pā̃c hazār rupaye kā jurmānā bharnā paṛā. The thief had to pay a fine of five thousand rupees.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
जुर्माना (jurmānā) is usually traced to Persian jurmāna, "a fine", built on jurm, a crime: the money a crime costs. You भरना a fine, fill it up, as you pay a bill.

Lesson 5 of 6.
हथकड़ी (hathkaṛī) — handcuffs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a punishment, then the Hindi for a fine.

You'll want to know: हथकड़ी.
हथकड़ी — hathkaṛī — "handcuffs".
पुलिस चोर को हथकड़ी में अदालत ले गई। pulis cor ko hathkaṛī meṁ adālat le gaī. The police took the thief to court in handcuffs.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
हथकड़ी (hathkaṛī) joins हाथ, hand, with kaṛī, a metal ring, which CDIAL 2629 lists for Hindi under Sanskrit kaṭa: rings for the hands. The first part shortens, as हाथ does in compounds.
