## You'll want to know: いわう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C170-NEWYEAR170-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いわう** — *iwau* — "to celebrate".

> **らいしゅう ちちの たんじょうびを いわいます。** — *raishū chichi no tanjōbi o iwaimasu* — "Next week we will celebrate my father's birthday."

Polite **いわいます**, *iwaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いわう** (*iwau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
