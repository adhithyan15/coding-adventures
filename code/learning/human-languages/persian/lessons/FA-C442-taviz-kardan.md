## You'll want to know: تعویض کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C442-JOBRAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تعویض کردن** (*ta'viz kardan*) — "to exchange, to replace".

> فروشگاه کفش پاره را تعویض کرد.
>
> *Forushgâh kafsh-e pâre râ ta'viz kard.*
>
> The shop exchanged the torn shoes.

More formal than *'avaz kardan*. Note the letter ض, said *z*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'viz kardan* is built from *ta'viz* 'replacement', a loan usually traced to Arabic *taʿwīḍ* 'replacing, compensating', and *kardan* 'to do'. EtymDB does not list *ta'viz*.
