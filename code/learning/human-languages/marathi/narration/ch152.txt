Marathi, chapter 152: Safe, Dangerous, False, Purple, Orange.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सुरक्षित (surakṣit) — safe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for foolish, then the Marathi for famous.

You'll want to know: सुरक्षित.
सुरक्षित — surakṣit — "safe".

The word, taken apart.
सुरक्षित (surakṣit) is Sanskrit surakṣita, "well protected", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13499). It is su-, "well", and rakṣita, "guarded".

Lesson 2 of 5.
धोकादायक (dhokādāyak) — dangerous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for famous, then the Marathi for safe.

You'll want to know: धोकादायक.
धोकादायक — dhokādāyak — "dangerous".

The word, taken apart.
धोकादायक (dhokādāyak) is dhokā, "danger", with Sanskrit dāyaka, "giving". CDIAL lists Marathi dhokā, "fear", under a reconstructed dhrōkṣa, "deceit" (CDIAL 6894).

Lesson 5 of 5.
नारिंगी (nāriṅgī) — orange (the colour).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for false, then the Marathi for purple.

You'll want to know: नारिंगी.
नारिंगी — nāriṅgī — "orange (the colour)".

The word, taken apart.
नारिंगी (nāriṅgī) goes back to Sanskrit nāraṅga, "orange tree"; CDIAL lists Marathi nārãg and nārãgī (CDIAL 7073). The colour is named after the fruit.
