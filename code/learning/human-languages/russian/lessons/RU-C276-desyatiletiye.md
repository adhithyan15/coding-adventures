## You'll want to know: десятилетие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C276-DESYATILETIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**десятилетие** (*desyatilétiye*) — "a decade".

> За последнее десятилетие город сильно изменился.
>
> *Za poslédneye desyatilétiye górod sílno izmenílsya.*
>
> Over the last decade the town has changed a lot.

Neuter. Ten years: *десять лет*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*десятилетие* joins *десять* "ten" and *лето* "summer", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *lěto* "year" (EtymDB): ten years.
