# … કરતાં મોટું (… kartā̃ moṭũ) — bigger than …

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C282-SAUTHI-01, GU-SCRIPT-C282-AU-SIGN-01, GU-LEX-C72-SIZE-01, GU-LEX-SHAHAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Mira is the youngest". (*Mīrā sauthī nānī che*.) Then "big" and "a city". (**મોટું**, **શહેર**.)

## Grammar Lens: કરતાં, than
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C282-KARTAN-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-GAAM-01, GU-LEX-C72-SIZE-02, GU-LEX-C187-PRICE187-03, GU-LEX-C185-HAGGLE185-03] -->

> **શહેર ગામ કરતાં મોટું છે.**
>
> *śaher gām kartā̃ moṭũ che.* — "The city is bigger than the village."

The thing being described, then the one it is measured against with **કરતાં** (*kartā̃*), "than", then the describing word, which follows the first: **મીરા અરુણ કરતાં નાની છે** (*Mīrā Aruṇ kartā̃ nānī che*), "Mira is younger than Arun".

A word with no ending of its own takes **વધારે**, "more", or **ઓછું**, "less": **ટ્રેન બસ કરતાં વધારે ઝડપી છે** (*ṭren bas kartā̃ vadhāre jhaḍpī che*).

## You'll want to know: the most of all
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C282-SAUTHI-MOST-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C73-QUALITY-05, GU-LEX-C74-WHERE1-01, GU-LEX-C280-JHADPI-01] -->

> **ઝડપી ટ્રેન સૌથી મોંઘી છે.**
>
> *jhaḍpī ṭren sauthī moṅghī che.* — "The fast train is the most expensive."

Two things take **કરતાં**; the top of a whole group takes **સૌથી**:

| | say |
|---|---|
| the bus is cheaper than the train | **બસ ટ્રેન કરતાં સસ્તી છે** |
| the bus is the cheapest | **બસ સૌથી સસ્તી છે** |
