## You'll want to know: بجھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C289-BUJHI289-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بجھنا** — *bujhnā* — "to go out (of a fire); to go dark".

> صبح تک آگ بجھ گئی۔
>
> *subah tak āg bujh gaī.*
>
> By morning the fire had gone out.

Past (it …): وہ چراغ بجھ گیا, *voh chirāgh bujh gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bujhnā*, "to be extinguished", under *\*vijjhāyati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11703). It is the partner of **بجھانا**: you **بجھانا** a fire, and it **بجھنا**.
