## You'll want to know: наводнение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C300-NAVODNENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**наводнение** (*navodnéniye*) — "a flood".

> После дождей началось наводнение.
>
> *Pósle dozhdéy nachalós navodnéniye.*
>
> After the rains a flood began.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наводнение* is built on *наводнить* "to flood", from *вода* "water", inherited from Proto-Slavic *voda* "water" (EtymDB); English *water* is a cousin.
