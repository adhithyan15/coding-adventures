# અફાળવું (aphāḷvũ) — to bang; to dash against

## You'll want to know: અફાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C482-THOKAR482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**અફાળવું** — *aphāḷvũ* — "to bang; to dash against".

> અંધારામાં મેં માથું દીવાલ સાથે અફાળ્યું.
>
> *andhārāmā̃ mẽ māthũ dīvāl sāthe aphāḷyũ.*
>
> In the dark I banged my head on the wall.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે માથું અફાળ્યું, *teṇe māthũ aphāḷyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અફાળવું** (*aphāḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *āsphālayati*, "causes to flap, shakes, throws"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to dash against" (CDIAL 1531).
