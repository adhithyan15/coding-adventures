## You'll want to know: zázhì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C293-PAPER293-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zázhì* — "magazine".

> *wǒ mǎi le liǎng běn zázhì* — I bought two magazines.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zázhì* is built from *zá* 'mixed' and *zhì* 'a record', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzop* and *tsyiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to gather' and 'five colours together' and the second 'to note down': a record of many things.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
