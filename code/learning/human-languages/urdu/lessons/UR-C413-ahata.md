## You'll want to know: احاطہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C413-KOTHI413-03]; assesses=[] -->

**احاطہ** — *ahātā* — "a compound; an enclosure".

> کوٹھی کے احاطے میں دو گاڑیاں ہیں۔
>
> *koṭhī ke ahāte meṅ do gāṛiyāṅ haiṅ.*
>
> There are two cars in the compound of the house.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احاطہ** (*ahātā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iḥāṭa*, "a surrounding, an enclosing".
