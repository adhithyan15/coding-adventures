## You'll want to know: زایمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C332-BARDAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**زایمان** (*zâymân*) — "childbirth, a delivery".

> زایمان او در بیمارستان خوب پیش رفت.
>
> *Zâymân-e u dar bimârestân khub pish raft.*
>
> Her delivery at the hospital went well.

From *zâ-*, the present stem of *zâyidan* "to give birth".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zâymân* is built on *zâ-*, the present stem of the native verb *zâyidan* "to give birth", with the noun suffix *-mân*. EtymDB lists no entry.
