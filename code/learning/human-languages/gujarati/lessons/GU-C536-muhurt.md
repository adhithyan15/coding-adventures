## You'll want to know: મુહૂર્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C536-MUHURT536-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મુહૂર્ત** — *muhūrt* — "an auspicious moment".

> જો સારું મુહૂર્ત હશે તો અમે રવિવારે નવા ઘરમાં જઈશું.
>
> *jo sārũ muhūrt haśe to ame ravivāre navā gharmā̃ jaīśũ.*
>
> If there is a good hour, we will move into the new house on Sunday.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુહૂર્ત** (*muhūrt*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *muhūrta*, "a moment, a short space of time" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is the right moment to begin.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
