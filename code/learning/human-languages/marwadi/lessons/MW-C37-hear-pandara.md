## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-PANDARA-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-PAANCH-01, MW-LEX-CHAUDA-01] -->

> *pandarā* — **fifteen**

Five is *pāṅch*. Fifteen keeps the **p** and the nasal, and the **-arā** rhyme is
back after fourteen broke it.

Three beats: *pan-da-rā*. The nasal in the middle is the same one already in
*pāṅch* — the sound the anusvara writes.
