Persian, chapter 360: Opinions and beliefs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عقیده ('aqide) — an opinion, a belief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an invoice, then the Persian for a cash machine.

You'll want to know: عقیده.
عقیده ('aqide) — "an opinion, a belief".
عقیده من این است که او راست میگوید. 'aqide-ye man in ast ke u râst miguyad. My opinion is that he is telling the truth.
Plural 'aqâyed, an Arabic broken plural. Be 'aqide-ye man — in my opinion, a little more formal than be nazar-e man.

The word, taken apart.
عقیده ('aqide) was borrowed from Arabic 'aqīda "a creed, a firm belief" (EtymDB). A belief, in this picture, is something you have tied fast.

Lesson 2 of 5.
دیدگاه (didgâh) — a point of view, a viewpoint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cash machine, then the Persian for an opinion.

You'll want to know: دیدگاه.
دیدگاه (didgâh) — "a point of view, a viewpoint".
از دیدگاه پدرم، این کار خطرناک است. az didgâh-e pedaram, in kâr khatarnâk ast. From my father's point of view, this is dangerous.
Az didgâh-e … — from the point of view of …. Plural didgâhhâ.

The word, taken apart.
دیدگاه (didgâh) is a native Persian compound built from did "sight", the past stem of didan "to see", and -gâh "place". A point of view is the place you look from.

Lesson 3 of 5.
نگرش (negaresh) — an attitude, an outlook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an opinion, then the Persian for a point of view.

You'll want to know: نگرش.
نگرش (negaresh) — "an attitude, an outlook".
نگرش او به کار عوض شده است. negaresh-e u be kâr 'avaz shode ast. His attitude to work has changed.
Negaresh be — an attitude towards. Formal and written; in speech people often say tarz-e fekr.

The word, taken apart.
نگرش (negaresh) is built on negar-, the present stem of the native verb negaristan "to look" (EtymDB lists the verb), with the noun suffix -esh: an attitude is a way of looking.

Lesson 4 of 5.
معتقد بودن (mo'taqed budan) — to believe, to be convinced.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a point of view, then the Persian for an attitude.

You'll want to know: معتقد بودن.
معتقد بودن (mo'taqed budan) — "to believe, to be convinced".
من معتقدم که ورزش برای همه لازم است. man mo'taqedam ke varzesh barâye hame lâzem ast. I believe that sport is necessary for everyone.
Mo'taqed "convinced" + budan "to be": mo'taqedam ke … — I believe that …. A belief in something takes be: be chizi mo'taqed budan.

The word, taken apart.
معتقد is usually traced to Arabic mu'taqid "believing, holding firmly", from the same Arabic family as 'aqīda. Budan "to be" is the inherited Persian verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نظریه (nazariye) — a theory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an attitude, then the Persian for to believe.

You'll want to know: نظریه.
نظریه (nazariye) — "a theory".
این نظریه خیلی قدیمی است. in nazariye kheyli qadimi ast. This theory is very old.
Built on nazar "view" with the suffix -iye. Plural nazariyehâ.

The word, taken apart.
نظریه (nazariye) comes from Arabic naẓariyya "theory" (EtymDB), built on naẓar "a look, a view", the same word that gave Persian nazar. A theory is a way of viewing the facts.
