## You'll want to know: دلاور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C460-BRAVE460-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دلاور** — *dilāvar* — "valiant".

> دلاور سپاہی آگے بڑھا۔
>
> *dilāvar sipāhī āge baṛhā.*
>
> The valiant soldier went forward.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دلاور** (*dilāvar*) is usually traced to Persian *dilāwar*, "brave", built on *dil*, "heart".
