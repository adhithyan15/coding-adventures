## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तूप** (*tūp*) goes back to Prakrit *tuppa*, "ghee", from a reconstructed *\*tuppa*, "grease" (CDIAL 5864). CDIAL thinks the word probably has a non-Aryan source and takes Kannada *tuppa*, "ghee", as borrowed from Middle Indo-Aryan; DEDR compares it with Kannada *tuppa* and Tamil *tuppu*, "ghee" (DEDR 3282).
