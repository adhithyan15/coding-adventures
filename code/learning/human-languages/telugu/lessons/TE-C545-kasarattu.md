## You'll want to know: కసరత్తు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C545-SADHANA545-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కసరత్తు** — *kasarattu* — "a workout; drill".

> క్రీడాకారులు ఉదయమే కసరత్తు చేశారు.
>
> *krīḍākārulu udayamē kasarattu cēśāru.*
>
> The athletes worked out early in the morning.

కసరత్తు చేయు, to work out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కసరత్తు** (*kasarattu*) came into Telugu through Hindustani *kasrat*, "exercise", which is usually traced to Arabic.
