## You'll want to know: dāngshí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C187-BADDAY187-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dāngshí* — "at that time; then".

> *dāngshí wǒ bú zài jiā* — I wasn't at home at the time.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dāngshí* is built from *dāng* and 时 'time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tang* and *dzyi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to match' and 'to meet with': the time it met with.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
