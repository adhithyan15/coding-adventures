## You'll want to know: അധികാരം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C424-ADHIKARAM424-04]; assesses=[] -->

**അധികാരം** — *adhikāraṁ* — "authority; power".

> അവന് അത് ചെയ്യാൻ അധികാരം ഇല്ല.
>
> *avanŭ atŭ ceyyān adhikāraṁ illa.*
>
> He has no authority to do that.

An official is അധികാരി, one with authority.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അധികാരം** (*adhikāraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *adhikāra*, "authority; government, rule" (Monier-Williams).
