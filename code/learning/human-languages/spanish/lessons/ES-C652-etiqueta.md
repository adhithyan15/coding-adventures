## You'll want to know: la etiqueta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C652-AEROPUERTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la etiqueta** — "the label, the tag; etiquette".

> Pon una etiqueta con tu nombre en la maleta.
>
> *Put a label with your name on the suitcase.*

*De etiqueta* — formal dress.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*etiqueta* was borrowed from French *étiquette* "a label, a ticket", from Old French *estiquier* "to attach, to stick" (EtymDB).
