Malayalam, chapter 572: Settling Into a Home.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ശീലിക്കുക (śīlikkuka) — to get used to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to hang up, then the Malayalam for to water plants.

You'll want to know: ശീലിക്കുക.
ശീലിക്കുക — śīlikkuka — "to get used to".
നേരത്തെ എഴുന്നേൽക്കാൻ ഞാൻ ശീലിച്ചു. nēratte eḻunnēlkkān ñān śīliccu. I got used to getting up early.
Past: ശീലിച്ചു, śīliccu.
From ശീലം, a habit. What one gets used to doing takes -ആൻ: എഴുന്നേൽക്കാൻ ശീലിച്ചു. In speech ശീലമായി, it became a habit, is common.

The word, taken apart.
ശീലിക്കുക (śīlikkuka) is built from Sanskrit śīlayati, "practises repeatedly" (CDIAL 12502), the verb of śīla, "habit", with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക.

Lesson 2 of 7.
ഉടയുക (uṭayuka) — to shatter; to break into pieces.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to water plants, then the Malayalam for to get used to.

You'll want to know: ഉടയുക.
ഉടയുക — uṭayuka — "to shatter; to break into pieces".
കയ്യിൽ നിന്ന് വീണ് ഗ്ലാസ് ഉടഞ്ഞു. kayyil ninnŭ vīṇŭ glāsŭ uṭaññu. The glass fell from my hand and shattered.
Past: ഉടഞ്ഞു, uṭaññu.
Of glass, pots and eggs; ഉടയ്ക്കുക is to break something. The past is ഉടഞ്ഞു.

The word, taken apart.
ഉടയുക (uṭayuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 946 gives the Malayalam verb as to break, as pots or nuts do, beside a transitive partner for breaking something to pieces (DEDR 946).

Lesson 3 of 7.

വീട്ടുജോലി — vīṭṭujōli — "housework; chores".
ഞായറാഴ്ച എല്ലാവരും വീട്ടുജോലിയിൽ സഹായിച്ചു. ñāyaṟāḻca ellāvaruṁ vīṭṭujōliyil sahāyiccu. On Sunday everyone helped with the housework.
വീട് house + ജോലി work; വീട് becomes വീട്ടു- in a compound.

The word, taken apart.
വീട്ടുജോലി (vīṭṭujōli) is a Malayalam compound built from വീട്, "a house", which is native (DEDR 5393), and ജോലി, "work".

Lesson 4 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: അടുക്കുക.
അടുക്കുക — aṭukkuka — "to stack; to arrange".
അവൾ പുസ്തകങ്ങൾ ഷെൽഫിൽ അടുക്കി. avaḷ pustakaṅṅaḷ ṣelphil aṭukki. She stacked the books on the shelf.
Past: അടുക്കി, aṭukki.
അടുക്ക്, a pile, a layer, is the noun.

The word, taken apart.
അടുക്കുക (aṭukkuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 80 lists അടുക്ക്, "a row, a pile, a layer", and the verb, to pile up (DEDR 80).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for housework, then the Malayalam for to stack.

You'll want to know: മാറാല.
മാറാല — māṟāla — "a cobweb".
പഴയ മുറിയുടെ മൂലകളിൽ മാറാല ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു. paḻaya muṟiyuṭe mūlakaḷil māṟāla uṇṭāyirunnu. There were cobwebs in the corners of the old room.
Old cobwebs full of dust; a fresh web is ചിലന്തിവല.

The word, taken apart.
മാറാല (māṟāla) is a household word; no source we can open explains it, so its origin is not settled.
