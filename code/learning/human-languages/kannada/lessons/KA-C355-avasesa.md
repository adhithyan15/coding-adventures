## You'll want to know: ಅವಶೇಷ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C355-UTKHANANA355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅವಶೇಷ** — *avaśēṣa* — "ruins; remains".

> ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಮೇಲೆ ಹಳೆಯ ಕೋಟೆಯ ಅವಶೇಷಗಳು ಇವೆ.
>
> *beṭṭada mēle haḷeya kōṭeya avaśēṣagaḷu ive.*
>
> There are the ruins of an old fort on the hill.

Plural: ಅವಶೇಷಗಳು, *avaśēṣagaḷu*.

Usually plural: ಅವಶೇಷಗಳು, what is left of a building or a city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅವಶೇಷ** (*avaśēṣa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *avaśeṣa*, "leavings, remainder" (Monier-Williams).
