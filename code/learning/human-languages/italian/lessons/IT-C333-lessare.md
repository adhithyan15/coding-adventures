## You'll want to know: lessare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C333-PENTOLA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lessare** — "to cook in boiling water, to boil (food)".

> Ho lessato le patate per venti minuti.
>
> *I boiled the potatoes for twenty minutes.*

*La carne lessa* or *il lesso* — boiled meat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lessare* is inherited from Late Latin *ēlixāre* "to boil" (EtymDB), built on *lixa* "hot water".
