## You'll want to know: tánhuà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C354-TOPIC354-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tánhuà* — "to have a serious talk".

> *lǎoshī xiǎng gēn nǐ tánhuà* — The teacher wants a word with you.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tánhuà* is built from *tán* and 话 'talk', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dam* and *hwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to talk' and the second 'speech'.
