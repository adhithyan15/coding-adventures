## You'll want to know: la palpebra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C482-VISO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la palpebra** — "eyelid".

> Ho le palpebre pesanti, ho sonno.
>
> *My eyelids are heavy, I am sleepy.*

The plural is *le palpebre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*palpebra* comes from Latin *palpebra* "eyelid" (EtymDB), related to *palpāre* "to touch softly" (Lewis & Short).
