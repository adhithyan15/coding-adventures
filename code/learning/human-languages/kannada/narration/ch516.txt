Kannada, chapter 516: Careers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವೃತ್ತಿ (vṛtti) — a profession; a career.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to groan, then the Kannada for to droop.

You'll want to know: ವೃತ್ತಿ.
ವೃತ್ತಿ — vṛtti — "a profession; a career".
ಅವನು ಶಿಕ್ಷಕ ವೃತ್ತಿಯನ್ನು ಆರಿಸಿದನು. avanu śikṣaka vṛttiyannu ārisidanu. He chose the teaching profession.
ವೃತ್ತಿಪರ, professional.

The word, taken apart.
ವೃತ್ತಿ (vṛtti) is borrowed from Sanskrit vṛtti, "rolling, course; mode of life, livelihood" (Monier-Williams), from the root vṛt, "to turn".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಹುದ್ದೆ (hudde) — a post; a position.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to droop, then the Kannada for a profession.

You'll want to know: ಹುದ್ದೆ.
ಹುದ್ದೆ — hudde — "a post; a position".
ಅವಳಿಗೆ ಬ್ಯಾಂಕಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಹುದ್ದೆ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. avaḷige byāṅkinalli oḷḷeya hudde sikkitu. She got a good post at the bank.
A rank or seat in an office.

The word, taken apart.
ಹುದ್ದೆ (hudde) is usually traced to Hindustani ohdā, "an office, a rank", itself from Arabic; it came in with the language of offices.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಅರ್ಹತೆ (arhate) — a qualification; eligibility.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a profession, then the Kannada for a post.

You'll want to know: ಅರ್ಹತೆ.
ಅರ್ಹತೆ — arhate — "a qualification; eligibility".
ಈ ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಬೇಕಾದ ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಇದೆ. ī kelasakke bēkāda arhate avanige ide. He has the qualification this job needs.
ಅರ್ಹ, worthy, eligible + -ತೆ, -ness.

The word, taken apart.
ಅರ್ಹತೆ (arhate) is built from Sanskrit arha, "meriting, deserving, worthy" (Monier-Williams), with the noun ending -tā, Kannada -ತೆ.

ಅಪ್ಪನನ್ನು ಹೃದಯ ತಜ್ಞರ ಬಳಿ ಕರೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಹೋದೆವು. appanannu hṛdaya tajñara baḷi karedukoṇḍu hōdevu. We took father to a heart specialist.

The word, taken apart.
ತಜ್ಞ (tajña) is borrowed from Sanskrit tajjña, "knowing that; a man who knows" (Monier-Williams).
