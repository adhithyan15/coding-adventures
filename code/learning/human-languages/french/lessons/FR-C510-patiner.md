## You'll want to know: patiner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C510-GLACE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**patiner** — "to skate".

> L'hiver, nous patinons sur le lac gelé.
>
> *In winter we skate on the frozen lake.*

Of wheels: to spin: *la voiture patine*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patiner* is built on *patin* "skate", from *patte* "paw", whose origin is not settled.
