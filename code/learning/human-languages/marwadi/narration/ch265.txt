Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 265: Shoes, Shaves and Shirts.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
मोची (mochī) — a cobbler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a potter, then the Marwadi for a carpenter.

You'll want to know: मोची.
मोची — mochī — "a cobbler".
मोची जूती सीवै है। mochī jūtī sīvai hai. The cobbler stitches a shoe.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मोची (mochī) is inherited from Sanskrit mocika, "tanner and cobbler", which CDIAL takes from an Iranian word for a shoe. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi mocī (CDIAL 10349).

मूंडणो — mū̃ḍṇo — "to shave".
नाई माथो मूंडै है। nāī mātho mū̃ḍai hai. The barber shaves a head.
Past (I …): म्हैं माथो मूंड्यो, mhaĩ mātho mū̃ḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
मूंडणो (mū̃ḍṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit muṇḍayati, "shaves". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi mū̃ḍnā (CDIAL 10194).
