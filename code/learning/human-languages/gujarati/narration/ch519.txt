Gujarati, chapter 519: Fire and Steam.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સગડી (sagḍī) — a charcoal stove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for mould, then the Gujarati for to stick (something) on.

You'll want to know: સગડી.
સગડી — sagḍī — "a charcoal stove".
શિયાળામાં દાદી સગડી પાસે બેસતી હતી. śiyāḷāmā̃ dādī sagḍī pāse bestī hatī. In winter my grandmother used to sit by the charcoal stove.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સગડી (sagḍī) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
આંચ (ā̃c) — cooking heat (the flame under a pot).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a charcoal stove, then the Gujarati for a tongue of flame.

You'll want to know: આંચ.
આંચ — ā̃c — "cooking heat (the flame under a pot)".
દૂધને ધીમી આંચ પર ઉકાળો. dūdhne dhīmī ā̃c par ukāḷo. Boil the milk on a low flame.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આંચ (ā̃c) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit arcís, "a flame, a ray", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "heat" (CDIAL 635). A cook speaks of ધીમી આંચ, a low flame.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઠારવું (ṭhārvũ) — to let cool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for cooking heat, then the Gujarati for steam.

You'll want to know: ઠારવું.
ઠારવું — ṭhārvũ — "to let cool".
મીરાએ ગરમ દૂધ ઠાર્યું. Mīrāe garam dūdh ṭhāryũ. Mira let the hot milk cool.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દૂધ ઠાર્યું, teṇe dūdh ṭhāryũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઠારવું (ṭhārvũ) is the causative partner of ઠરવું, "to cool down", and CDIAL lists it under stharati, "is firm", as "to let cool" (CDIAL 13742).
