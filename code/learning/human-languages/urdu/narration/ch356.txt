Urdu, chapter 356: Making Things Better.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اصلاح (islāh) — a reform; a correction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to commend, then the Urdu for to endure.

You'll want to know: اصلاح.
اصلاح — islāh — "a reform; a correction".
اسکولوں میں اصلاح کی ضرورت ہے۔ iskūloṅ meṅ islāh kī zarūrat hai. The schools need reform.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
اصلاح (islāh) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic iṣlāḥ, "putting right".

سدھارنا — sudhārnā — "to improve; to put right".
اس نے اپنا کام سدھارا۔ us ne apnā kām sudhārā. She improved her work.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گھر سدھارا, us ne ghar sudhārā.

The word, taken apart.
سدھارنا (sudhārnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sudhārnā, "to mend", under śuddhakāra, "making clean", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12521).

Lesson 5 of 5.
نکھارنا (nikhārnā) — to polish a skill; to bring out the best in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a knack, then the Urdu for to put right.

You'll want to know: نکھارنا.
نکھارنا — nikhārnā — "to polish a skill; to bring out the best in".
استاد نے اس کا ہنر نکھارا۔ ustād ne us kā hunar nikhārā. The teacher brought out the best in her skill.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اپنا ہنر نکھارا, us ne apnā hunar nikhārā.

The word, taken apart.
نکھارنا (nikhārnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin nikhārnā, "to clarify, to make settle", under niḥkṣārayati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 7098). As water is left to settle until it runs clear.
