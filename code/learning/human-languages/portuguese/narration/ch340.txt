Portuguese, chapter 340: Tenho as minhas dúvidas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
duvidar — to doubt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the consensus, then the Portuguese for to give in.

You'll want to know: duvidar.
duvidar — "to doubt".
Duvido que ele chegue a horas. I doubt he will arrive on time.
Duvidar que takes the subjunctive: duvido que venha. Duvidar de alguém — to doubt someone.

The word, taken apart.
duvidar is inherited from Latin dubitāre "to doubt, to waver" (Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese dovidar (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
cético — sceptical.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to give in, then the Portuguese for to doubt.

You'll want to know: cético.
cético — "sceptical".
Estou cético: essa promessa parece boa demais. I am sceptical: that promise seems too good.
Many speakers in Portugal pronounce the p and write céptico; the agreement accepts both spellings. Feminine cética.

The word, taken apart.
cético is a learned word that goes back to Greek skeptikós "inquiring, reflective", built on sképtesthai "to consider, to examine"; Latin had Sceptici for the philosophers of that school (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
absurdo — absurd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for sceptical, then the Portuguese for to deny.

You'll want to know: absurdo.
absurdo — "absurd".
É absurdo pagar tanto por um café. It is absurd to pay so much for a coffee.
Also a noun: é um absurdo! — it is outrageous!

The word, taken apart.
absurdo is a learned borrowing from Latin absurdus "out of tune, incongruous" (EtymDB), built from ab and surdus "deaf, dull-sounding" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
discutível — debatable, questionable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to deny, then the Portuguese for absurd.

You'll want to know: discutível.
discutível — "debatable, questionable".
Essa decisão foi discutível. That decision was debatable.
Built on discutir + -ível: something you can argue about.

The word, taken apart.
discutível is built on discutir "to discuss" with the suffix -ível; discutir comes from Latin discutere "to strike asunder, to shatter" (Lewis & Short), which later meant "to examine".
