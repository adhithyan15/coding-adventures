Italian, chapter 419: In treno.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il vagone — the railway carriage, the wagon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the ticket inspector, then the Italian for the supplement.

You'll want to know: il vagone.
il vagone — "the railway carriage, the wagon".
Il nostro vagone è in fondo al treno. Our carriage is at the back of the train.
Il vagone letto — sleeping car; il vagone merci — goods wagon.

The word, taken apart.
vagone was borrowed from French wagon, which took it from English waggon, from Dutch wagen (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
la carrozza — the coach (of a train); the horse-drawn carriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the supplement, then the Italian for the railway carriage.

You'll want to know: la carrozza.
la carrozza — "the coach (of a train); the horse-drawn carriage".
Abbiamo i posti nella carrozza sette. We have seats in coach seven.
On a ticket carrozza is the coach number; la carrozza is also a horse-drawn carriage.

The word, taken apart.
carrozza is built on carro 'cart' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin carrus, a word of Gaulish origin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
lo scompartimento — the compartment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the railway carriage, then the Italian for the coach.

You'll want to know: lo scompartimento.
lo scompartimento — "the compartment".
Lo scompartimento ha sei posti. The compartment has six seats.
Old trains have them; modern ones are open.

The word, taken apart.
scompartimento is built on compartimento, from Late Latin compartīrī 'to share out' (EtymDB), a learned borrowing built on pars 'part'.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: la cuccetta.
la cuccetta — "the couchette, the berth".
Di notte dormiamo in cuccetta. At night we sleep in a couchette.
On a ship too: a bunk.

The word, taken apart.
cuccetta is a little cuccia 'dog bed, bed' (EtymDB), which was borrowed from French couche 'bed'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
il capotreno — the guard, the train manager.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the compartment, then the Italian for the couchette.

You'll want to know: il capotreno.
il capotreno — "the guard, the train manager".
Il capotreno annuncia la prossima fermata. The guard announces the next stop.
Capo + treno: the head of the train crew.

The word, taken apart.
capotreno is built from capo 'head', inherited from Latin caput, and treno 'train', which was borrowed from French train (EtymDB).
