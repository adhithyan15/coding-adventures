# சூறையாடு (cūṟaiyāṭu) — to ravage; to plunder

## You'll want to know: சூறையாடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C302-AZHIVU302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**சூறையாடு** — *cūṟaiyāṭu* — "to ravage; to plunder".

> வெள்ளம் பல கிராமங்களைச் சூறையாடியது.
>
> *veḷḷam pala kirāmaṅkaḷaic cūṟaiyāṭiyatu.*
>
> The flood ravaged many villages.

Past (I …): சூறையாடினேன், *cūṟaiyāṭiṉēṉ*.

What is ravaged takes -ஐ; said of robbers too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சூறையாடு** (*cūṟaiyāṭu*) is a Tamil compound built from சூறை, "robbery, pillage" (DEDR 2744), and ஆடு, "to move, act" (DEDR 347).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
