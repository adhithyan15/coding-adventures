## You'll want to know: дым
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C295-POZHAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**дым** (*dym*) — "smoke".

> Из окна шёл чёрный дым.
>
> *Iz okná shyol chyórnyy dym.*
>
> Black smoke was coming out of the window.

*Нет дыма без огня* — there is no smoke without fire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дым* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dymŭ* "smoke", from PIE *dʰuh₂mós* "smoke" (EtymDB); Latin *fūmus* "smoke" is a cousin.
