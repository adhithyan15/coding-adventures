## You'll want to know: डरकाळी फोडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C446-ABHAYARANYA446-04]; assesses=[] -->

**डरकाळी फोडणे** — *ḍarkāḷī phoḍṇe* — "to roar".

> जंगलात वाघाने डरकाळी फोडली.
>
> *jaṅgalāt vāghāne ḍarkāḷī phoḍlī.*
>
> In the forest the tiger roared.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने डरकाळी फोडली, *tyāne ḍarkāḷī phoḍlī*.

डरकाळी, a roar, is feminine, so the past is डरकाळी फोडली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डरकाळी फोडणे** (*ḍarkāḷī phoḍṇe*) joins **डरकाळी**, "a roar", whose origin is not settled, to **फोडणे**, "to break open": to let out a roar.
