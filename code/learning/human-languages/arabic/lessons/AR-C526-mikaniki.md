## You'll want to know: ميكانيكي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C526-HIRAF526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ميكانيكي** — *mīkānīkī* — "a mechanic".

> أصلح الميكانيكي سيارة أبي.
>
> *aṣlaḥa al-mīkānīkī sayyārat abī.*
>
> The mechanic repaired my father's car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ميكانيكي** (*mīkānīkī*) is a modern loan, usually traced to French *mécanique*, from Greek *mēkhanē*, "a device", with the Arabic ending *-ī*.
