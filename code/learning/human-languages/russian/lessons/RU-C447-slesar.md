## You'll want to know: слесарь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C447-MASTERSKAYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**слесарь** (*slésar*) — "a locksmith, a fitter".

> Слесарь поменял замок на двери.
>
> *Slésar pomenyál zamók na dverí.*
>
> The locksmith changed the lock on the door.

Masculine; plural *слесаря* or *слесари*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*слесарь* was borrowed from German *Schlosser* 'a locksmith' (EtymDB), built on *Schloss* 'a lock'.
