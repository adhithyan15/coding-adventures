## You'll want to know: اہل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C374-SAHARA374-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اہل** — *ahl* — "eligible; qualified".

> اگر آپ اہل ہیں تو درخواست دیجیے۔
>
> *agar āp ahl haiṅ to darkhāst dījiye.*
>
> If you are eligible, please apply.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اہل** (*ahl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ahl*, "people; those worthy of something".
