## You'll want to know: دلیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C334-BAHAS334-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دلیل** — *dalīl* — "a reasoned argument; a plea".

> آپ کی دلیل مضبوط ہے۔
>
> *āp kī dalīl mazbūt hai.*
>
> Your argument is strong.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دلیل** (*dalīl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dalīl*, "a guide; a proof": the point that leads a listener to your view.
