## You'll want to know: منظر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C279-GAVAHI279-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منظر** — *manzar* — "a scene; a sight".

> میں وہ منظر نہیں بھولوں گا۔
>
> *maiṅ voh manzar nahīṅ bhūlūṅ gā.*
>
> I will not forget that scene.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منظر** (*manzar*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *manẓar*, "a place looked at, a sight". It is what lay before the eyes at one moment.
