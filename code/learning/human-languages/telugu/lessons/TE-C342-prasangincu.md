# ప్రసంగించు (prasaṅgiñcu) — to give a speech

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a public meeting, then the Telugu for a speech.

## You'll want to know: ప్రసంగించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C342-SABHA342-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రసంగించు** — *prasaṅgiñcu* — "to give a speech".

> మా మాస్టారు సభలో ప్రసంగించారు.
>
> *mā māsṭāru sabhalō prasaṅgiñcāru.*
>
> Our teacher gave a speech at the meeting.

Past (I …): ప్రసంగించాను, *prasaṅgiñcānu*.

The verb of ప్రసంగం, made with -ఇంచు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రసంగించు** (*prasaṅgiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *prasaṅga* (Monier-Williams), the source of ప్రసంగం, a speech, with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
