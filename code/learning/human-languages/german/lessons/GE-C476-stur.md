## You'll want to know: stur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C476-LAUNISCH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**stur** — "stubborn".

> Mein Opa ist stur und ändert nie seine Meinung.
>
> *My grandpa is stubborn and never changes his mind.*

Informal: *stur wie ein Esel* — stubborn as a mule.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stur* is usually traced to Low German *stūr* "stiff, unyielding", which spread into standard German; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
