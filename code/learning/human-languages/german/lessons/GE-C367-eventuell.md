## You'll want to know: eventuell
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C367-EVENTUELL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**eventuell** — "possibly (not eventually); perhaps".

> Eventuell komme ich später, ich weiß es noch nicht.
>
> *I may possibly come later; I do not know yet.*

A false friend: *eventuell* means possibly, not eventually. Eventually is *irgendwann*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eventuell* was borrowed from French *éventuel* "possible", a learned formation on Latin *ēventus* "an outcome, an event", from *ēvenīre* "to come out, to happen" (Lewis & Short).
