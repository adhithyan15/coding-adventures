тренироваться (trenirovátsya) — to train, to practise.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for to quarrel, then the Russian for to make up.

You'll want to know: тренироваться.
тренироваться (trenirovátsya) — "to train, to practise".

The word, taken apart.
тренироваться (trenirovátsya) is тренировать, "to train", with -ся, "oneself"; the verb comes from English train, through German trainieren.

Lesson 2 of 5.
бегать (bégat) — to run about, to jog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to make up, then the Russian for to train.

You'll want to know: бегать.
бегать (bégat) — "to run about, to jog".

The word, taken apart.
бегать (bégat) is the repeated-action partner of бежать, "to run" (Vasmer): running about. Vasmer relates the root to Lithuanian bėgti, "to run", and Greek phóbos, "flight".

Lesson 3 of 5.
прыгать (prýgat) — to jump.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to train, then the Russian for to run about.

You'll want to know: прыгать.
прыгать (prýgat) — "to jump".

The word, taken apart.
прыгать (prýgat) from Proto-Slavic prygati — inherited, with прыжок, "a jump". Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian sprùkti, "to slip away", and Latvian sprukt, "to escape".

Lesson 4 of 5.
кататься (katátsya) — to go for a ride, to skate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to run about, then the Russian for to jump.

You'll want to know: кататься.
кататься (katátsya) — "to go for a ride, to skate".

The word, taken apart.
кататься (katátsya) is катать, "to roll", with -ся: to roll along, to go for a ride. Vasmer doubts a link with English skate.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to jump, then the Russian for to go for a ride.

You'll want to know: загорать.
загорать (zagorát) — "to sunbathe".

The word, taken apart.
загорать (zagorát) is за- with -горать, from гореть, "to burn": to sunbathe is to get burnt brown.
