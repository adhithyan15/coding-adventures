## You'll want to know: फुर्तीला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C472-PHURTI472-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फुर्तीला** — *phurtīlā* — "nimble; brisk".

> फुर्तीला खिलाड़ी गेंद को जल्दी पकड़ लेता है।
>
> *phurtīlā khilāṛī gẽd ko jaldī pakaṛ letā hai.*
>
> A nimble player catches the ball quickly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फुर्तीला** (*phurtīlā*) is *phurtī*, "briskness", with the ending *-īlā*, "full of"; CDIAL lists Hindi *phurtī*, "activity", under Sanskrit *sphuráti*, "darts, quivers" (CDIAL 13849).
