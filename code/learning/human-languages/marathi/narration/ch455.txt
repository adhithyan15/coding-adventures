Marathi, chapter 455: Dread and Restless Nights.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धास्ती (dhāstī) — dread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to coax round, then the Marathi for to be crestfallen.

You'll want to know: धास्ती.
धास्ती — dhāstī — "dread".
परीक्षेची धास्ती तिच्या मनात होती. parīkṣecī dhāstī ticyā manāt hotī. Dread of the exam was on her mind.
Feminine.
Feminine: परीक्षेची धास्ती (dhāstī), dread of the exam. धास्ती (dhāstī) वाटणे, to feel dread, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
धास्ती (dhāstī) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
बेचैन (becain) — restless; uneasy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be crestfallen, then the Marathi for dread.

You'll want to know: बेचैन.
बेचैन — becain — "restless; uneasy".
बाळ रात्रभर बेचैन होतं. bāḷ rātrabhar becain hotaṁ. The baby was restless all night.
Unchanging. The prefix बे- means without: here, without rest.

The word, taken apart.
बेचैन (becain) is a loan from Persian bechain, "restless", built on be-, "without", and chain, "rest".

Lesson 3 of 5.
दगदग (dagdag) — a wearing rush; exhausting bustle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: दगदग.
दगदग — dagdag — "a wearing rush; exhausting bustle".
रोजच्या दगदगीमुळे आई थकते. rojcyā dagdagīmuḷe āī thakte. Mother gets tired from the daily rush.
Feminine.
Feminine; oblique दगदगी-. दगदग (dagdag) करणे, to wear oneself out rushing about.

The word, taken apart.
दगदग (dagdag) is a Marathi noun built by doubling a syllable; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
तळमळणे (taḷmaḷṇe) — to toss and turn; to fret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for restless, then the Marathi for a wearing rush.

You'll want to know: तळमळणे.
तळमळणे — taḷmaḷṇe — "to toss and turn; to fret".
काळजीमुळे तो रात्रभर तळमळला. kāḷjīmuḷe to rātrabhar taḷmaḷlā. He tossed and turned all night with worry.
Past (he …): तो तळमळला, to taḷmaḷlā.
Of a sleepless night or a restless longing; तळमळ, the restlessness itself.

The word, taken apart.
तळमळणे (taḷmaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled syllable, तळ-मळ; its origin is not settled.

You'll want to know: घुसमटणे.
घुसमटणे — ghusmaṭṇe — "to feel stifled".
गर्दीच्या बसमध्ये मी घुसमटलो. gardīcyā basmadhye mī ghusmaṭlo. I felt stifled in the crowded bus.
Past (he …): तो घुसमटला, to ghusmaṭlā.
Of a body in a stuffy room, and of a mind with no one to talk to: तिचा जीव घुसमटत होता.
