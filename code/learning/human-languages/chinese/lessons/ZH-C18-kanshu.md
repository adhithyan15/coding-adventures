# 看书 — look at a book, read

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-KANSHU-01, ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-SHU-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **read a book** as *kànshū*. Write 看 and 书 separately.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-ORTHO-KANSHU-01]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-SHU-01] -->

> **看书** — *kànshū* — **read a book; read books**

Read the action first and its object second. No new character is hiding here:
看 + 书 gives the whole reading activity.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-ORTHO-KANSHU-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **看书** once. Keep the long upright in 书 centred after the denser 看.
