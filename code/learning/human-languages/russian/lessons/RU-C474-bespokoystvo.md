## You'll want to know: беспокойство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C474-WORRY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**беспокойство** (*bespokóystvo*) — "worry, anxiety".

> Его беспокойство было понятно.
>
> *Yegó bespokóystvo býlo ponyátno.*
>
> His worry was understandable.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*беспокойство* is built on *беспокойный* 'restless', from *без* 'without' and *покой* 'rest', inherited from Proto-Slavic *pokojь* (EtymDB).
