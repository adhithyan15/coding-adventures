# Review — tell it back

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-GRAMMAR-DEPOIS-ORDEM-01, PT-GRAMMAR-NO-FIM-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "first", "then" and "in the end". (*Primeiro, depois, no fim.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-GRAMMAR-HISTORIA-EM-ORDEM-01, PT-SKILL-READ-NARRATIVE-01, PT-GRAMMAR-CENA-EVENTO-01, PT-LEX-MAS-02] -->

You have read a story. Now you tell one, which is the same skill turned round:
a reader sorts verbs into scene and event, and a teller has to choose them.

[PAUSE 2s] The journey from the reading, in five cues. Answer each one in
Portuguese, out loud, in a full sentence. Choose the past as you go: is this
cue the scene, or something that happened? And keep one *mas* for the turn.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-GRAMMAR-IMPERFEITO-01, PT-LEX-HAVIA-02, PT-LEX-TINHA-01, PT-LEX-FAZIA-FRIO-01, PT-GRAMMAR-ESTAVA-A-01, PT-GRAMMAR-QUANDO-CENA-02, PT-GRAMMAR-DEPOIS-ORDEM-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the weather that morning (*De manhã fazia frio e chovia.*)]
- [YOU SAY: where you were, and what the train was not doing (*Eu estava na estação, mas o comboio não chegava.*)]
- [YOU SAY: the first thing you did, then the second (*Primeiro esperámos no cais. Depois tomámos um café.*)]
- [YOU SAY: what your friend was doing on the train (*O meu amigo estava a contar histórias.*)]
- [YOU SAY: where you were in the end, and how you felt (*No fim chegámos a Lisboa. Eu estava contente.*)]

[PAUSE 3s] Now tell the whole journey once more, in your own order and your
own words, without the cues.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-GRAMMAR-HISTORIA-EM-ORDEM-01, PT-ETYMON-CONTAR-03, PT-ETYMON-DEPOIS-03, PT-GRAMMAR-NO-FIM-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which of your five cues were scene? (The weather, where you were,
the friend's telling, how you felt.) Which were events? (Waiting, the coffee,
arriving.) Which Latin word is behind *depois*? (***Post***.) And what are you
doing, word for word, when you *contar uma história*? (**Counting it** — from
*computāre*.)
