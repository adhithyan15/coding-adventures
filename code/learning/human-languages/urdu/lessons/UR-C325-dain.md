## You'll want to know: ڈائن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C325-DEV325-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈائن** — *ḍāin* — "a witch".

> ڈائن جنگل میں رہتی تھی۔
>
> *ḍāin jangal meṅ rahtī thī.*
>
> The witch lived in the forest.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈائن** (*ḍāin*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍāin*, "a witch", under Sanskrit *ḍākínī* (CDIAL 5542).
