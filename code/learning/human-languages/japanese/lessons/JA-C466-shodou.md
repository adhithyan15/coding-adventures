## You'll want to know: しょどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C466-ARTS466-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しょどう** — *shodō* — "calligraphy".

> **しゅうまつに しょどうを ならっています。** — *shūmatsu ni shodō o naratte imasu* — "I take calligraphy lessons at the weekend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょどう** (*shodō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sho*, "write" (Middle Chinese *syo*) and *dō*, "way" (*dawX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
