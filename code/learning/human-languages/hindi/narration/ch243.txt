Hindi, chapter 243: Unsuccessful, Proud, Simple, Cunning, Quarrelsome.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नाकाम (nākām) — unsuccessful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for useless, then the Hindi for successful.

You'll want to know: नाकाम.
नाकाम — nākām — "unsuccessful".

The word, taken apart.
नाकाम (nākām) is a loan, usually traced to Persian nākām, "unsuccessful". It is built from nā-, "not", and kām, "desire".
