## You'll want to know: chuàngzuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C503-ART503-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chuàngzuò* — "to create (art or writing)".

> *tā yòng le wǔ nián chuàngzuò zhège zuòpǐn* — He spent five years creating this work.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chuàngzuò* is built from *chuàng* 'to found' and 作 'to make', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrhjangH* and *tsak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the level-tone reading 'a wound', and gives the falling-tone one as another spelling of a word glossed 'to begin' and 'to found a craft'.
