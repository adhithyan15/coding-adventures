## You'll want to know: صبا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C323-NASHAA323-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صبا** — *ṣibā* — "boyhood, early youth".

> قضى جدي صباه في الريف.
>
> *qaḍā jaddī ṣibāhu fī ar-rīf.*
>
> My grandfather spent his boyhood in the countryside.

A literary word; the east wind is also صبا, *ṣabā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صبا** (*ṣibā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-b-w* (Lane), "to incline, to be youthful", the root of *ṣabiyy*, a boy. *Ṣibā* is the time of being one.
