Marathi, chapter 532: Games at Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बुद्धिबळ (buddhibaḷ) — chess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to hit away, then the Marathi for to catch a ball.

You'll want to know: बुद्धिबळ.
बुद्धिबळ — buddhibaḷ — "chess".
आजोबा आणि मी रविवारी बुद्धिबळ खेळतो. ājobā āṇi mī ravivārī buddhibaḷ kheḷto. Grandfather and I play chess on Sundays.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
बुद्धिबळ (buddhibaḷ) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit compound buddhibala, buddhi, "intellect", and bala, "strength", listed as the name of a kind of game (Monier-Williams); Marathi uses it for chess.

Lesson 3 of 5.
फासा (phāsā) — a die for games.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for chess, then the Marathi for a game piece.

You'll want to know: फासा.
फासा — phāsā — "a die for games".
मी फासा टाकला आणि सहा पडले. mī phāsā ṭāklā āṇi sahā paḍle. I threw the die and got a six.
Masculine. Plural: फासे, phāse.
फासा (phāsā) टाकणे, to throw the die.

The word, taken apart.
फासा (phāsā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi phāsā, "die", under Sanskrit pāśa, "die, dice" (CDIAL 8132).

You'll want to know: शब्दकोडे.
शब्दकोडे — śabdakoḍe — "a crossword".
आजोबा रोज सकाळी एक शब्दकोडं सोडवतात. ājobā roj sakāḷī ek śabdakoḍaṁ soḍavtāt. Grandfather solves a crossword every morning.
Neuter.
शब्दकोडं सोडवणे, to solve a crossword; the dictionary writes शब्दकोडे (śabdakoḍe).

The word, taken apart.
शब्दकोडे (śabdakoḍe) is a Marathi compound built from शब्द, "word", from Sanskrit śabda (Monier-Williams), and कोडे, "a puzzle".
