Telugu, chapter 217: Together, Except, Tradition, Custom, Habit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కలిసి (kalisi) — together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a bakery, then the Telugu for a hair salon.

You'll want to know: కలిసి.
కలిసి — kalisi — "together".

The word, taken apart.
కలిసి (kalisi) is native Dravidian, from kaliyu, "to join, meet" (DEDR 1299), one word with Tamil kala, "to unite": "having joined".
