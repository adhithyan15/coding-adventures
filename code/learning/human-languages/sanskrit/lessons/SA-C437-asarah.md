## You'll want to know: आसारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C437-PRAVRSH437-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आसारः** — *āsāraḥ* — "a downpour".

> आसारेण मार्गाः जलेन पूर्णाः सन्ति।
>
> *āsāreṇa mārgāḥ jalena pūrṇāḥ santi.*
>
> Because of the downpour the roads are full of water.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आसारः** (*āsāraḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √sṛ, "to run, to flow"; MW gives *āsāra* as a hard shower, a downpour (MW).
