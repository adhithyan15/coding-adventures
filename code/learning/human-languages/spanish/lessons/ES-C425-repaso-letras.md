# veintisiete letras — the whole set, sorted three ways

## Grammar Lens: what you now know about each group
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTH-C425-01, ES-ORTH-C425-03, ES-ORTH-C425-04] -->

| group | the useful fact |
|---|---|
| the five vowels | one sound each, and it never varies |
| most consonants | named by adding **-e**, or **e-** in front |
| nine consonants | have names of their own to learn |
| five digraphs | two letters, one sound |

And the marks, which only vowels take:

| mark | on | job |
|---|---|---|
| la tilde | any vowel | marks the stressed syllable |
| la diéresis | only **ü** | makes a silent **u** speak |

## Grammar Lens: the three things that stop a line break
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTH-C425-05, ES-ORTH-C425-04, ES-ORTH-C425-02] -->

A break may fall between syllables but never inside:

- a **digraph** — *chi-co*, not *c-hico*
- a consonant plus **l** or **r** — *ha-blar*, not *hab-lar*
- a **diphthong**, or a number in figures

Those three cover nearly everything a written paper can test, and all three come
from one idea: **do not break what is said in one beat.**
