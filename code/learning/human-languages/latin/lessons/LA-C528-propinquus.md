## You'll want to know: propinquus, propinqua, propinquum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C528-GENS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**propinquus, propinqua, propinquum** — "near; a relative".

> Multī propinquī ad nūptiās veniunt.
>
> *Many relatives come to the wedding.*

From *prope* — near. As a noun, *propinquus* or *propinqua* — a relative. English *propinquity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**propinquus** is built on *prope* "near" (Lewis & Short). English *propinquity* "nearness" comes from it.
