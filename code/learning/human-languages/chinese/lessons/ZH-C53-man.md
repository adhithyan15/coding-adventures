## You'll want to know: màn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C53-SPEAK53-05]; assesses=[] -->

*màn* — "slow".

> *tā shuō huà hěn màn* — He speaks slowly.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*màn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *maenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'lazy, haughty'. The sense 'slow' is not given there; from lazy to slow is a likely step, but it is not settled.
