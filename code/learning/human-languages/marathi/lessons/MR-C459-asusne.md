## You'll want to know: आसुसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C459-ODH459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आसुसणे** — *āsusṇe* — "to long for".

> पावसासाठी सगळं गाव आसुसलं होतं.
>
> *pāvsāsāṭhī sagḷaṁ gāv āsuslaṁ hotaṁ.*
>
> The whole village was longing for rain.

Past (he …): तो आसुसला, *to āsuslā*.

Takes -साठी, or the -ायला form: भेटायला आसुसणे, to long to meet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आसुसणे** (*āsusṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
