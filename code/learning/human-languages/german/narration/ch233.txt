Lesson 1 of 5.
der Lärm — noise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for art, then the German for a landscape.

You'll want to know: der Lärm.
der Lärm — "noise".

The word, taken apart.
Lärm from Italian allarme, "to arms!" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed, and its first syllable dropped. English alarm is the same word.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for a landscape, then the German for noise.

You'll want to know: die Laune.

The word, taken apart.
Laune from Middle High German lūne, from Latin lūna, "moon" (EtymDB 2.1 has the Latin word) — borrowed: moods were thought to change with the moon.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Lebenslauf — a CV.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for noise, then the German for a mood.

You'll want to know: der Lebenslauf.
der Lebenslauf — "a CV".

The word, taken apart.
Lebenslauf is the noun of leben, "to live", + -s- + the noun of laufen, "to run": the run of a life, a copy of Latin curriculum vītae.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Leistung — an achievement, performance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a mood, then the German for a CV.

You'll want to know: die Leistung.
die Leistung — "an achievement, performance".

The word, taken apart.
Leistung is the verb "to carry out, to achieve", Old High German leisten (EtymDB 2.1), + -ung. The verb first meant "to follow a track", like the shoemaker's last.
