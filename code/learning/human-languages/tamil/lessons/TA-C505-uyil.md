## You'll want to know: உயில்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C505-MARANAM505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**உயில்** — *uyil* — "a will".

> தாத்தா தன் வீட்டை உயிலில் பேரனுக்கு எழுதி வைத்தார்.
>
> *tāttā taṉ vīṭṭai uyilil pēraṉukku eḻuti vaittār.*
>
> Grandfather left his house to his grandson in his will.

உயில் எழுது, to make a will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உயில்** (*uyil*) is a loan from English *will*, reshaped to Tamil sounds.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
