## You'll want to know: o pincel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C505-PINTURA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o pincel** — "paintbrush".

> Ela pinta com um pincel muito fino.
>
> *She paints with a very fine brush.*

Plural *pincéis*. A hairbrush is *uma escova*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pincel* comes from Catalan *pinzell* "paintbrush" (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *pēnicillum* "a painter's brush".
