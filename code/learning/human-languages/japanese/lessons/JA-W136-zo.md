## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-ZO-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SO-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01] -->

> **そ** *so* + **゛** → **ぞ** *zo*

Write **そ** in full: one stroke that zigzags twice before it drops into an
open curve. Then add the two short strokes of the voicing mark at the upper
right, the left one first. The base shape does not change.

The mark voices the opening consonant: *s* becomes *z*, and the vowel stays
*o*. It is the change you made on **さ** for **ざ**, in **ございます**.

With **か** in front and **く** after it, you can now write the word from the
last lesson: **かぞく**, *kazoku*, a family.
