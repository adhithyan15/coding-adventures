## You'll want to know: つぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C171-ORDER171-03]; assesses=[] -->

**つぎ** — *tsugi* — "next; the next one".

> **つぎは らいげつでしょう。** — *tsugi wa raigetsu deshō* — "The next one will probably be next month."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つぎ** (*tsugi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
