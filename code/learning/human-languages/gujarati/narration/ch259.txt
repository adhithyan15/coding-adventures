Gujarati, chapter 259: Matches and wrestling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મેચ (mec) — match.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hit, then the Gujarati for to bounce.

You'll want to know: મેચ.
મેચ — mec — "match".
ગઈકાલે અમારી શાળાની મેચ હતી. — gaīkāle amārī śāḷānī mec hatī — "Yesterday our school had a match."

The word, taken apart.
મેચ (mec) is a loan from English match, a game between two sides.

Lesson 2 of 5.
હરાવવું (harāvvũ) — to defeat, to beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to bounce, then the Gujarati for match.

You'll want to know: હરાવવું.
હરાવવું — harāvvũ — "to defeat, to beat".
અમે તેમને મેચમાં હરાવ્યા. — ame temne mecmā̃ harāvyā — "We beat them in the match."
The causative of હારવું, hārvũ, "to lose": to make someone lose.

The word, taken apart.
હરાવવું (harāvvũ) is built on the taught hārvũ, "to lose", with the causative -āv-. CDIAL derives hārvũ from Sanskrit hārayati, "has taken from oneself, loses" (CDIAL 14061).

Lesson 3 of 5.
દોડવું (doḍvũ) — to run.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for match, then the Gujarati for to defeat.

You'll want to know: દોડવું.
દોડવું — doḍvũ — "to run".
હું સવારે મેદાનમાં દોડ્યો. — hũ savāre medānmā̃ doḍyo — "I ran in the field in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
દોડવું (doḍvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit drávati, "runs", extended with -ḍ-; CDIAL lists Gujarati doṛvũ, "to run" (CDIAL 6624).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to defeat, then the Gujarati for to run.

You'll want to know: કસરત.
કસરત — kasrat — "exercise, workout".
મેં સવારે કસરત કરી. — mẽ savāre kasrat karī — "I exercised in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of કસરત (kasrat) is not settled. It is usually linked with a Persian word of the same shape, but CDIAL has no entry for it and our sources do not confirm the link.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to run, then the Gujarati for exercise.

You'll want to know: કુસ્તી.
કુસ્તી — kustī — "wrestling".

કુસ્તી (kustī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kushtī, "wrestling". CDIAL has no entry for it.
