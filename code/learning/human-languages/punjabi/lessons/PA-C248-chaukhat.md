## You'll want to know: ਚੌਖਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C248-GHR248-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੌਖਟ** — *chaukhaṭ* — "a door frame".

> ਚੌਖਟ ਪੁਰਾਣੀ ਹੈ ਪਰ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ਾ ਨਵਾਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *chaukhaṭ purāṇī hai par darvāzā navāṁ hai.*
>
> The door frame is old, but the door is new.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੌਖਟ** (*chaukhaṭ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*catuṣkāṣṭha*, "rectangular frame"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *caukhaṭ*, "doorframe" (CDIAL 4631).
