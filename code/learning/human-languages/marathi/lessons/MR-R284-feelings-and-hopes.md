# Review — feelings and hopes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C283-TIKIT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-BUY-TICKET-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-WHEN-LEAVES-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-LOST-REPORT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-MISSED-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask for one ticket to Pune and when the train leaves. (*puṇyācaṁ ek tikīṭ dyā. gāḍī kitī vājtā nighte?*) Say "I bought a ticket, but I missed my train". (*mī tikīṭ kāḍhlaṁ, paṇ mājhī gāḍī cuklī*.) Then **ऋतू** and **ज्ञान**: two words, two letters, **ऋ** and **ञ**.

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I missed my train, so I got angry.]
- [YOU SAY: She is afraid of snakes.]
- [YOU SAY: We were glad. He got bored.]
- [YOU SAY: I want to become a teacher. That is my goal.]
- [YOU SAY: one thing you want to do this year, with **-ायचं आहे**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C284-RAG-01, MR-LEX-C284-BHITI-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C284-FEELING-DATIVE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C284-FEELING-VERBS-01, MR-LEX-C284-DHYEY-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C284-AYCHA-AHE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C284-VHAYCHA-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Anger? (**राग**.) Fear? (**भीती**.) "I got angry"? (**मला राग आला**.) A goal? (**ध्येय**.) "I want to become"? (**मला … व्हायचं आहे**.) Which letter starts **ऋतू**? (**ऋ**.)
