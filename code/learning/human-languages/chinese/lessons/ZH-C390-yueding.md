## You'll want to know: 约定
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C390-AGREE390-02]; assesses=[] -->

**约定** — *yuēdìng* — "to agree on a time; to fix (a time or place) together".

> **我们约定明天早上八点见面** *wǒmen yuēdìng míngtiān zǎoshang bā diǎn jiànmiàn* — We agreed to meet at eight tomorrow morning.

The new character, in pieces: 定 — eight strokes in two pieces: the roof of 安 on top, and under it the five strokes at the foot of 是. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**约定** (*yuēdìng*) is built from 约 'to bind' and 定 'settled', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jak* and *dengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'calm'.
