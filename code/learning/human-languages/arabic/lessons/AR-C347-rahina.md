## You'll want to know: رهينة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C347-KHATF347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رهينة** — *rahīna* — "a hostage".

> بقيت الرهينة ثلاثة أيام في البيت.
>
> *baqiyat ar-rahīna thalāthat ayyām fī al-bayt.*
>
> The hostage stayed in the house for three days.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رهينة** (*rahīna*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-h-n* (Lane), "to give a thing as a pledge"; *rahn* is a pawn. A *rahīna* is a person held as one.
