## You'll want to know: पर्यावरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C446-PRAKRITI446-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्यावरण** — *paryāvaraṇ* — "the environment".

> पेड़ काटना पर्यावरण के लिए बुरा है।
>
> *peṛ kāṭnā paryāvaraṇ ke liye burā hai.*
>
> Cutting down trees is bad for the environment.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्यावरण** (*paryāvaraṇ*) is a modern coinage from Sanskrit *pari*, "around", and *āvaraṇa*, "covering" (Monier-Williams): what covers us all round.
