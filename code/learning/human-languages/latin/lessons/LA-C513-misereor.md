## You'll want to know: misereor, miserērī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C513-MISE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**misereor, miserērī** — "to pity, feel sorry for".

> Miserēmur puerōrum, quī nūllōs parentēs habent.
>
> *We pity the children who have no parents.*

Deponent: *misereor, miserērī, miseritus sum*, with the genitive: *tuī misereor* — I pity you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**misereor** is built on *miser* "wretched, pitiable" (Lewis & Short). English *misery* comes from *miseria*, from the same adjective.
