# क़रीब (qarīb) — close to, nearly

## You'll want to know: क़रीब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C388-PARYAPT388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**क़रीब** — *qarīb* — "close to, nearly".

> अगर बस समय पर चली तो हम क़रीब दो घंटे में पहुँचेंगे।
>
> *agar bas samay par calī to ham qarīb do ghaṇṭe meṁ pahuñceṅge.*
>
> If the bus leaves on time, we'll get there in close to two hours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़रीब** (*qarīb*) is usually traced to Arabic *qarīb*, "near", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
