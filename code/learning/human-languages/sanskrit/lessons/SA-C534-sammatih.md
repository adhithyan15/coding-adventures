## You'll want to know: सम्मतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C534-SAMVID534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्मतिः** — *sammatiḥ* — "consent; approval".

> यदि पितुः सम्मतिः अस्ति, तर्हि अहं गमिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi pituḥ sammatiḥ asti, tarhi ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi.*
>
> If my father agrees, I will go.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्मतिः** (*sammatiḥ*) is built on *sam*, "together", and *mati*, "thought", from the root √man, "to think": a thinking alike; MW gives *sammati* as agreement, approval (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
