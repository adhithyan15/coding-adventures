# ભૂતકાળ (bhūtkāḷ) — the past

## You'll want to know: ભૂતકાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C317-ITIHAS317-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભૂતકાળ** — *bhūtkāḷ* — "the past".

> ભૂતકાળમાં આ શહેર ખૂબ નાનું હતું.
>
> *bhūtkāḷmā̃ ā śaher khūb nānũ hatũ.*
>
> In the past this city was very small.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભૂતકાળ** (*bhūtkāḷ*) is built from Sanskrit *bhūta*, "been, gone, past", and *kāla*, "time" (Monier-Williams): the time that has been. It is masculine: **ભૂતકાળમાં**, in the past.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
