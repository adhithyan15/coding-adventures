## You'll want to know: સિપાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C286-GUNO286-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સિપાઈ** — *sipāī* — "a constable".

> જો ત્યાં એક સિપાઈ હોત તો તે ચોરને પકડત.
>
> *jo tyā̃ ek sipāī hot to te corne pakḍat.*
>
> If a constable had been there, he would have caught the thief.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સિપાઈ** (*sipāī*) is usually traced to Persian *sipāhī*, "a soldier", the word English took as *sepoy*. In Gujarati it is the constable on the beat.
