## You'll want to know: le trépied
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C502-PHOTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le trépied** — "tripod".

> Pour les photos de nuit, il utilise un trépied.
>
> *For night photos he uses a tripod.*

Three feet: *tré-* "three" and *pied* "foot".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le trépied* comes from Latin *tripēs* "three-footed" (EtymDB), built from *trēs* "three" and *pēs* "foot" (Lewis & Short).
