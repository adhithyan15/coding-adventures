## You'll want to know: કોરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C502-SUTHAR502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કોરવું** — *korvũ* — "to hollow out (wood)".

> સુથારે લાકડું કોરીને એક વાટકી બનાવી.
>
> *suthāre lākḍũ korīne ek vāṭkī banāvī.*
>
> The carpenter hollowed out the wood and made a bowl.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે લાકડું કોર્યું, *teṇe lākḍũ koryũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કોરવું** (*korvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*kōrati*, "scrapes", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb for scooping out, carving or boring a hole (CDIAL 3530).
