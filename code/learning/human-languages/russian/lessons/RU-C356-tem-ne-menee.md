## You'll want to know: тем не менее
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C356-NESMOTRYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**тем не менее** (*tem ne méneye*) — "nevertheless".

> Было трудно, тем не менее мы всё сделали.
>
> *Býlo trúdno, tem ne méneye my vsyo sdélali.*
>
> It was hard; nevertheless, we did everything.

Formal. It may open the clause or follow *но*: *но тем не менее*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**тем не менее** is built from *тем* "by that", *не* "not" and *менее* "less", the old comparative: not the less for that.
