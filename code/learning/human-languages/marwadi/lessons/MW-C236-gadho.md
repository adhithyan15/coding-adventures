## You'll want to know: गधो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C236-BEAST236-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गधो** — *gadho* — "a donkey".

> गधो भारी बोझ उठावै है।
>
> *gadho bhārī bojh uṭhāvai hai.*
>
> The donkey carries a heavy load.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गधो** (*gadho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gardabha*, "an ass"; CDIAL lists Hindi *gadahā* and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4054).
