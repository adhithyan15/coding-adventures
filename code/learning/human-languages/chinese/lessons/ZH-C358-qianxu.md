## You'll want to know: qiānxū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C358-MANNERS358-04]; assesses=[] -->

*qiānxū* — "modest".

> *nǐ tài qiānxū le* — You're being too modest.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiānxū* is built from *qiān* and *xū*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khem* and *xjo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'respectful' and 'to yield', and the second 'empty'.
