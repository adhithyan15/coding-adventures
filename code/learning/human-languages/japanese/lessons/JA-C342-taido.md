## You'll want to know: たいど
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C342-GENIN342-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たいど** — *taido* — "an attitude; a manner".

> **てんいんの たいどが きらいだから、あの みせに いきません。** — *ten'in no taido ga kirai da kara, ano mise ni ikimasen* — "I dislike the shop assistant's attitude, so I don't go to that shop."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいど** (*taido*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "attitude" (Middle Chinese *thojH*) and *do*, "degrees" (*duH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
