Lesson 1 of 5.
कद्दूकस (kaddūkas) — a grater.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a rolling pin, then the Hindi for a sieve.

You'll want to know: कद्दूकस.
कद्दूकस — kaddūkas — "a grater".

The word, taken apart.
कद्दूकस (kaddūkas) is kaddū, "gourd", usually traced to Persian kadū, with kas, "scraping". CDIAL has no entry for the compound.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ढक्कन (dhakkan) — a lid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a sieve, then the Hindi for a grater.

You'll want to know: ढक्कन.
ढक्कन — dhakkan — "a lid".

The word, taken apart.
ढक्कन (dhakkan) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ḍhakk, "cover", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5574). CDIAL compares a Jain Sanskrit ḍhakkana, "shutting".

Lesson 4 of 5.
सुराही (surāhī) — a water jar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a lid, then the Hindi for a jug.

You'll want to know: सुराही.
सुराही — surāhī — "a water jar".

The word, taken apart.
सुराही (surāhī) is a loan from Arabic ṣurāḥī, "a long-necked flask", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṣ-r-ḥ (Lane).

The word, taken apart.
मटका (maṭkā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mṛdā, "earth, clay" (CDIAL 10291). CDIAL notes an added -k-.
