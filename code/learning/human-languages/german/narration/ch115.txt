German, chapter 115: Comb, Bin, Broom, Washing machine, Armchair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Kamm — a comb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a toothbrush, then the German for toothpaste.

You'll want to know: der Kamm.
der Kamm — "a comb".

The word, taken apart.
Kamm from Old High German chamb from Proto-Germanic kambaz, "comb" — inherited, the same word as English comb. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Mülleimer — a bin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for toothpaste, then the German for a comb.

You'll want to know: der Mülleimer.
der Mülleimer — "a bin".

The word, taken apart.
Mülleimer is the word for "rubbish" + the word for "bucket" (EtymDB 2.1). The bucket word, Middle High German eimer, goes back to Latin amphora, reshaped as if it meant "one-carrier".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a bin, then the German for a broom.

You'll want to know: die Waschmaschine.
die Waschmaschine — "a washing machine".

The word, taken apart.
Waschmaschine is the verb "to wash", Old High German wascan, English wash, + the word for "machine" from French machine from Latin māchina (EtymDB 2.1).
