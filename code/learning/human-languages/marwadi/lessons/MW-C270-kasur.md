## You'll want to know: कसूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C270-MAPHI270-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कसूर** — *kasūr* — "a wrongdoing; a fault".

> थारो कोई कसूर कोनी।
>
> *thāro koī kasūr konī.*
>
> It isn't your fault at all.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसूर** (*kasūr*) is usually traced, through Persian *qusūr*, to Arabic *quṣūr*, "falling short".
