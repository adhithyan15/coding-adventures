## You'll want to know: राष्ट्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C353-SHASANA353-03]; assesses=[] -->

**राष्ट्रम्** — *rāṣṭram* — "a nation; a realm".

> भारतं महत् राष्ट्रम् अस्ति।
>
> *bhārataṃ mahat rāṣṭram asti.*
>
> India is a great nation.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राष्ट्रम्** (*rāṣṭram*) comes from the root √rāj, "to reign"; MW gives *rāṣṭra* "a kingdom, a realm" and "a people, a nation" (MW).
