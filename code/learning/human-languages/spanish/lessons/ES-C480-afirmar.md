## You'll want to know: afirmar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C480-DECIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**afirmar** — "to state, to say that something is so".

> El director afirma que no hay problema.
>
> *The director states that there is no problem.*

More formal than *decir*; news reports use it a lot.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.
