## You'll want to know: der Anker
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C423-ANKER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Anker** — "the anchor".

> Der Anker fällt ins Wasser.
>
> *The anchor drops into the water.*

Same form in the plural. *Vor Anker liegen* — to lie at anchor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Anker* is an early borrowing from Latin *ancora* 'anchor' (Lewis & Short), already in Old High German as *anker* (EtymDB). English *anchor* comes from the same Latin word.
