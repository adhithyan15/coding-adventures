## You'll want to know: duìzhǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C499-TEAM499-03]; assesses=[] -->

*duìzhǎng* — "team captain; team leader".

> *wǒmen dōu xiǎng dāng duìzhǎng* — We all want to be captain.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duìzhǎng* is built from *duì* 'a troop' and *zhǎng* 'a head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dwojH* and *trjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'great'; the sense 'a head of a group' is not given there.
