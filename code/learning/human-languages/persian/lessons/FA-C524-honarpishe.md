## You'll want to know: هنرپیشه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C524-FILM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**هنرپیشه** (*honarpishe*) — "an actor, an actress".

> این هنرپیشه در تئاتر هم بازی میکند.
>
> *in honarpishe dar te'âtr ham bâzi mikonad.*
>
> This actor also works in the theatre.

*honar* 'art' + *pishe* 'trade'. It means the same as *bâzigar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*honarpishe* is built on native Persian *honar*, 'art', usually traced to Middle Persian *hunar*, and *pishe*, 'trade, craft': one whose trade is art.
