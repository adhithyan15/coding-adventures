## You'll want to know: தியாகி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C339-VEERAM339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**தியாகி** — *tiyāki* — "a martyr; one who sacrifices".

> நாட்டுக்காக உயிரைக் கொடுத்த தியாகிகளை நினைவுகூர்ந்தோம்.
>
> *nāṭṭukkāka uyiraik koṭutta tiyākikaḷai niṉaivukūrntōm.*
>
> We remembered the martyrs who gave their lives for the country.

One who gives up everything for a cause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தியாகி** (*tiyāki*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tyāgin*, "one who abandons, gives up" (Monier-Williams).
