## You'll want to know: నలత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C512-JABBU512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నలత** — *nalata* — "a slight indisposition".

> నిన్నటి నుంచి నాకు కొంచెం నలతగా ఉంది.
>
> *ninnaṭi nuñci nāku koñceṁ nalatagā undi.*
>
> Since yesterday I have been feeling a bit off.

Milder than జబ్బు: నలతగా ఉంది, (I) feel a bit off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నలత** (*nalata*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3611 lists Telugu *nalata*, "disease", beside *naliyu*, "to be crushed, to be bruised", and Tamil *nalaṅku*, "to grow faint".
