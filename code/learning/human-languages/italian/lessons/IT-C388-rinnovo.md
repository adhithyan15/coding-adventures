## You'll want to know: il rinnovo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C388-VALIDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il rinnovo** — "the renewal".

> Per il rinnovo serve una foto nuova.
>
> *For the renewal you need a new photo.*

Built on *rinnovare*. *Il rinnovo del contratto* — the contract renewal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rinnovo* is built on *rinnovare* "to renew", from Latin *renovāre* "to make new again" (Lewis & Short), on *novus* "new".
