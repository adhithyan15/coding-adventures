Sanskrit, chapter 356: Notices from an Office.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विज्ञापयति (vijñāpayati) — notifies; makes a formal request.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a president, then the Sanskrit for consults.

You'll want to know: विज्ञापयति.
विज्ञापयति — vijñāpayati — "notifies; makes a formal request".
कार्यालयः नूतनं नियमं विज्ञापयति। kāryālayaḥ nūtanaṃ niyamaṃ vijñāpayati. The office gives notice of a new rule.
Past (he …): सः अध्यक्षं विज्ञापितवान्, saḥ adhyakṣaṃ vijñāpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
विज्ञापयति (vijñāpayati) is built on vi and the root √jñā, "to know", in its causative form, "to make known"; MW gives the senses "to inform", "to apprise" and "to request" (MW).

You'll want to know: नियोजयति.
नियोजयति — niyojayati — "appoints".
राजा नूतनं लिपिकं नियोजयति। rājā nūtanaṃ lipikaṃ niyojayati. The king appoints a new clerk.
Past (he …): सः सदस्यं नियोजितवान्, saḥ sadasyaṃ niyojitavān.

The word, taken apart.
नियोजयति (niyojayati) is built on ni, "down, in", and the root √yuj, "to join", in its causative form; MW gives ni-√yuj the senses "to enjoin, to order" and "to appoint, to instal" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
विभागः (vibhāgaḥ) — a department; a division.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for appoints, then the Sanskrit for approves of.

You'll want to know: विभागः.
विभागः — vibhāgaḥ — "a department; a division".
कार्यालये पञ्च विभागाः सन्ति। kāryālaye pañca vibhāgāḥ santi. The office has five departments.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विभागः (vibhāgaḥ) is built on vi, "apart", and the root √bhaj, "to divide, to share"; MW gives vi-bhāga "a share, a section, a division", and "a department" is its modern sense (MW).
