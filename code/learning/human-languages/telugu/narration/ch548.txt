Telugu, chapter 548: In the Kitchen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కాచు (kācu) — to boil milk; to heat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to shake out, then the Telugu for to hang up.

You'll want to know: కాచు.
కాచు — kācu — "to boil milk; to heat".
అమ్మ పొద్దున్నే పాలు కాచింది. amma poddunnē pālu kācindi. Mum boiled the milk early in the morning.
Past (I …): కాచాను, kācānu.
For milk and water; అన్నం వండు for rice.

The word, taken apart.
కాచు (kācu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1458 lists Telugu kācu, "to heat, to warm, to boil", with Tamil kāy, "to grow hot".

Lesson 2 of 5.

మాడ్చు — māḍcu — "to scorch; to burn food".
ఫోనులో మాట్లాడుతూ నేను కూర మాడ్చాను. phōnulō māṭlāḍutū nēnu kūra māḍcānu. Talking on the phone, I burnt the curry.
Past (I …): మాడ్చాను, māḍcānu.
మాడు, to get burnt, is its intransitive partner.

The word, taken apart.
మాడ్చు (māḍcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4801 lists Telugu māḍu, "to be burnt, to be scorched black", and māḍ(u)cu, "to burn, to scorch".

Lesson 3 of 5.
తరుగు (tarugu) — to chop; to shred.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to boil milk, then the Telugu for to scorch.

You'll want to know: తరుగు.
తరుగు — tarugu — "to chop; to shred".
వంట కోసం నేను ఉల్లిపాయలు తరిగాను. vaṇṭa kōsaṁ nēnu ullipāyalu tarigānu. I chopped the onions for the cooking.
Past (I …): తరిగాను, tarigānu.
Fine cutting of vegetables; కోయు is the general word for cutting.

The word, taken apart.
తరుగు (tarugu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3140 lists Telugu taṟugu, "to slice, to chop", with Tamil taṟi, "to cut off".

Lesson 4 of 5.
జల్లించు (jalliñcu) — to sift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to scorch, then the Telugu for to chop.

You'll want to know: జల్లించు.
జల్లించు — jalliñcu — "to sift".
అమ్మ పిండిని జల్లించింది. amma piṇḍini jalliñcindi. Mum sifted the flour.
Past (I …): జల్లించాను, jalliñcānu.
Flour, rice or sand is sifted through a జల్లెడ, a sieve, to take out the lumps and grit.

The word, taken apart.
జల్లించు (jalliñcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2370 lists Telugu jallincu, "to sift", and jalleḍa, "a sieve", with Tamil cali.

Lesson 5 of 5.
తాలింపు (tālimpu) — tempering; seasoning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to chop, then the Telugu for to sift.

You'll want to know: తాలింపు.
తాలింపు — tālimpu — "tempering; seasoning".
ఆవాలు, కరివేపాకుతో పప్పుకు తాలింపు పెట్టారు. āvālu, karivēpākutō pappuku tālimpu peṭṭāru. They tempered the dal with mustard seeds and curry leaves.
Spices fried in oil and poured over a dish: తాలింపు (tālimpu) వేయు.

The word, taken apart.
తాలింపు (tālimpu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3186 lists Telugu tālimpu, "seasoning", and tālincu, "to season", with Tamil tāḷi, "to season with spices fried in ghee".
