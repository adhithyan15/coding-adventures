## You'll want to know: el pintor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C763-ARTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el pintor** — "the painter".

> Picasso fue un pintor muy famoso.
>
> *Picasso was a very famous painter.*

*La pintora* for a woman. Also a house painter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pintor* is inherited from spoken Latin *pinctor*, Latin *pictor* "painter" (EtymDB), from *pingere* "to paint".
