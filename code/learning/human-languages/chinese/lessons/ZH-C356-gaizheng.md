## You'll want to know: gǎizhèng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C356-MISS356-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎizhèng* — "to put a mistake right".

> *nǐ yīnggāi gǎizhèng zhège cuòwù* — You should put this mistake right.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎizhèng* is built from *gǎi* and 正 'proper', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kojX* and *tsyengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to change' and the second 'proper' and 'right'.
