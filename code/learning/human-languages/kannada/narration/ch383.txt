Kannada, chapter 383: Doubts and Judgements.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಸಂದೇಹ (sandēha) — a misgiving; a doubt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for existence, then the Kannada for to realise.

You'll want to know: ಸಂದೇಹ.
ಸಂದೇಹ — sandēha — "a misgiving; a doubt".
ಅವನ ಕಥೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ನನಗೆ ಸಂದೇಹ ಇದೆ. avana katheya bagge nanage sandēha ide. I have doubts about his story.
Who doubts takes -ಗೆ: ನನಗೆ ಸಂದೇಹ (sandēha) ಇದೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂದೇಹ (sandēha) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃdeha, "doubt, uncertainty" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sandēha, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: avana katheya bagge nanage sandēha ide. — I have doubts about his story.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for existence, then the Kannada for to realise, then say sandēha again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಸಂದೇಹ (sandēha) mean? ("A misgiving; a doubt".) Say it once more.

ಆ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ ಅವರ ಮಾತನ್ನು ತಿರುಚಿತು. ā patrike avara mātannu tirucitu. That newspaper twisted their words.
Past (I …): ತಿರುಚಿದೆ, tirucide.
Of facts, words and limbs: ಕೈ ತಿರುಚು (tirucu), to twist an arm.

The word, taken apart.
ತಿರುಚು (tirucu) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled: DEDR does not list it.

ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸು — nirṇayisu — "to determine; to judge".
ಪುರಾವೆಗಳನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ಅವರು ಸತ್ಯವನ್ನು ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸಿದರು. purāvegaḷannu nōḍi avaru satyavannu nirṇayisidaru. Looking at the evidence, they determined the truth.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸು (nirṇayisu) is built on Sanskrit nirṇaya, "complete ascertainment, decision" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to twist, then the Kannada for to determine.

You'll want to know: ಅಸಾಧ್ಯ.
ಅಸಾಧ್ಯ — asādhya — "impossible".
ಒಂದೇ ದಿನದಲ್ಲಿ ಈ ಕೆಲಸ ಮುಗಿಸುವುದು ಅಸಾಧ್ಯ. ondē dinadalli ī kelasa mugisuvudu asādhya. Finishing this job in a single day is impossible.
ಅ- (not) + ಸಾಧ್ಯ (possible).

The word, taken apart.
ಅಸಾಧ್ಯ (asādhya) is borrowed from Sanskrit asādhya, "not to be accomplished", from a-, "not", and sādhya, "to be done" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆ (allagaḷe) — to deny; to refute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to determine, then the Kannada for impossible.

You'll want to know: ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆ.
ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆ — allagaḷe — "to deny; to refute".
ಅವನು ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಆರೋಪಗಳನ್ನು ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆದನು. avanu ellā ārōpagaḷannu allagaḷedanu. He denied all the accusations.
Past (I …): ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆದೆ, allagaḷede.
What you deny takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆ (allagaḷe) is built from two parts: native alla, "it is not so" (DEDR 234), and kaḷe, "to cast off": to throw a claim out as untrue.
