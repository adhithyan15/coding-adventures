## You'll want to know: éclaircir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C341-MALENTENDU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**éclaircir** — "to clear up, to make clearer".

> Nous devons éclaircir ce problème avant lundi.
>
> *We need to clear up this problem before Monday.*

A *-ir* verb like *finir*. Of the sky too: *le ciel s'éclaircit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*éclaircir* is built from *é-* and *clair* "clear", which is inherited from Latin *clārus* "clear, bright" (Lewis & Short).
