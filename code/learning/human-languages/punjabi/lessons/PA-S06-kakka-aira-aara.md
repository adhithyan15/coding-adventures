# ਅ · ਆ — two pieces met inside a word you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-NANNA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-BIHARI-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DULAVA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TAINKA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TTHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-GA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-SIHARI-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-BA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-LA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-HORA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The two letters a Punjabi word uses when it opens on a vowel and has no consonant to hang a mātrā on.

First, say the last things you learned once more, out loud and from
memory:

- [YOU SAY: *rahiṇā* · *kamm karnā*]

## Script you'll notice: ਅ · ਆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AIRA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AARA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ਅ

**aiṛā** — *a*. The bare **vowel carrier**. On its own it is *a*; with a mātrā hung on it, it is whatever that mātrā says.

> ਆ

**aiṛā + kannā** — *ā*. The vowel carrier wearing the *kannā* you already know — so it is long *ā*, at the start of a word.

Now put them where you have already heard them:

> **ਅ** alone is *a*; **ਆ** is the same carrier wearing the *kannā* you already know.
>
> **ਆ** + **ਸ** → **ਆਸ** (*ās*)
>
> **ਆਸ** — *ās* — **hope**

You are not being asked to write any of this. You are being asked to
**recognise** it the next time it goes past.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AIRA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AARA-01] -->

Keep ਅ · ਆ in front of you and do not cover any of them. Follow each shape once with a
finger, then copy it once with a pencil, larger than it is printed. Say its
sound as your hand stops.

> This is **observe-and-trace with the model visible**. Nothing in these
> chapters asks you to write a Punjabi word from memory; that ladder starts
> later, and it starts from these same shapes.
