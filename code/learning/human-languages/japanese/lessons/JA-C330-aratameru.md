## You'll want to know: あらためる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C330-CHANTO330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あらためる** — *aratameru* — "to revise; to change for the better".

> **きそくが ふるいので、あらためた ほうが いいです。** — *kisoku ga furui node, aratameta hō ga ii desu* — "The rules are out of date, so they should be revised."

Polite **あらためます**, *aratamemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **かいしゃが きそくを あらためました。**, *kaisha ga kisoku o aratamemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あらためる** (*aratameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
