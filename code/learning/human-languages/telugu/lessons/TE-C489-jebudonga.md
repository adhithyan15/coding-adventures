## You'll want to know: జేబుదొంగ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C489-KALTI489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**జేబుదొంగ** — *jēbudoṅga* — "a pickpocket".

> రద్దీ బస్సులో జేబుదొంగ నా పర్సు కొట్టేశాడు.
>
> *raddī bassulō jēbudoṅga nā parsu koṭṭēśāḍu.*
>
> In the crowded bus a pickpocket took my purse.

జేబు, a pocket, + దొంగ, a thief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జేబుదొంగ** (*jēbudoṅga*) is a Telugu compound: జేబు, "a pocket", a loan from Arabic *jayb* through Persian and Hindustani, and దొంగ, "a thief", native Dravidian (DEDR 2982).
