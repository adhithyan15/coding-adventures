## You'll want to know: प्रस्थान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C411-VAZAN411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रस्थान** — *prasthān* — "departure (formal)".

> प्रस्थान से पहले अपना सामान जाँचिए।
>
> *prasthān se pahle apnā sāmān jā̃ciye.*
>
> Check your luggage before departure.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रस्थान** (*prasthān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *prasthāna*, "setting out, departure" (Monier-Williams): the partner of आगमन on every departures board.
