## You'll want to know: నిశ్చితార్థం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C346-VIVAHAM346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**నిశ్చితార్థం** — *niścitārthaṁ* — "an engagement (to marry)".

> వాళ్ళ నిశ్చితార్థం గత నెల జరిగింది.
>
> *vāḷḷa niścitārthaṁ gata nela jarigindi.*
>
> Their engagement took place last month.

నిశ్చిత, settled, and అర్థం, a matter: the day the match is settled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిశ్చితార్థం** (*niścitārthaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *niścita*, "ascertained, settled, decided" (Monier-Williams), and *artha*, "a matter, an aim".
