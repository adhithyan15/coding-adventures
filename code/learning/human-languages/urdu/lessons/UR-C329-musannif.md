## You'll want to know: مصنف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C329-AFSANA329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مصنف** — *musannif* — "an author".

> اس افسانے کا مصنف مشہور ہے۔
>
> *is afsāne kā musannif mashhūr hai.*
>
> The author of this story is famous.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصنف** (*musannif*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muṣannif*, "one who composes a book".
