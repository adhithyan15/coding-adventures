## You'll want to know: profectiō, profectiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C428-PROFECTIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**profectiō, profectiōnis** — "a departure, a setting out".

> Ante profectiōnem mercātor sarcinās parat.
>
> *Before the departure the merchant gets his baggage ready.*

Third declension, feminine: *profectiō, profectiōnis f.*, the noun of *proficīscor* 'to set out'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*profectiō* is built on *proficīscor* 'to set out' (Lewis & Short), through its participle *profectus*. EtymDB does not list it.
