## You'll want to know: la solución
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C620-SOLUCION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la solución** — "the solution".

> Tenemos que buscar una solución.
>
> *We have to look for a solution.*

*No tiene solución* — it cannot be fixed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*solución* is a learned borrowing from Latin *solutio* 'a loosening, a solving', built on *solvere* 'to loosen' (EtymDB).
