## You'll want to know: nèiróng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C354-TOPIC354-04]; assesses=[] -->

*nèiróng* — "content; what something is about".

> *zhè běn shū de nèiróng hěn yǒu yìsi* — This book has interesting content.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nèiróng* is built from *nèi* and *róng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nwojH* and *yowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to enter' and the second 'to hold'.
