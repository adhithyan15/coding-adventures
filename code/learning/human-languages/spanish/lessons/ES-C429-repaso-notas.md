# el curso entero — one school year, in five words

## Grammar Lens: the year, in order
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C429-EDU-01, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-02, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-03, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-04, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-05, ES-LEX-EXAMEN, ES-LEX-C358-REF-14] -->

| what happens | the word |
|---|---|
| you go to the building | el instituto |
| you sit in the room | la clase |
| you sit the thing | el examen |
| a number comes back | la nota |
| the document prints it | la calificación |
| five or above | aprobar |
| below five | suspender |

Two of those seven were already yours. The chapter added the other five, and
with them the track can follow a school year from the door to the report.

## Grammar Lens: two words for one number
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C429-EDU-02, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-03] -->

| where you meet it | which word |
|---|---|
| a friend asking in a corridor | la nota |
| a column heading on a document | la calificación |

Same number, two registers. A reading paper prints the formal one; a listening
paper says the short one.

## Grammar Lens: three Latin pictures
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C429-EDU-02, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-04, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-05] -->

| word | the picture under it |
|---|---|
| nota | a scratch cut into a surface |
| aprobar | a thing tested and found good |
| suspender | a thing left hanging |

The third one is still literally true in Spain, where a failed subject hangs
over a student until the resit.
