## You'll want to know: gradualmente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C274-OUTRORA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gradualmente** — "gradually".

> A temperatura subiu gradualmente.
>
> *The temperature rose gradually.*

Built on *gradual*, ‘step by step’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gradualmente* is built on *gradual*, a learned word from Medieval Latin *graduālis*, from *gradus* "a step" (Lewis & Short).
