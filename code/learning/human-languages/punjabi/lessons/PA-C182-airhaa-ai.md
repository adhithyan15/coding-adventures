# ਐ — one new independent vowel from the Sunday word you can say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-WEEKDAY-AITVAR-01, PA-WEEKDAY-SHANIVAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *aitvaar*, then **ਸ਼ਨੀਵਾਰ**.

## Script you'll notice: ਐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AI-01]; assesses=[PA-WEEKDAY-AITVAR-01] -->

> ਐ

**aiṛā with dulāvā̃** — *ai*. Keep the independent vowel in view. It opens the
Sunday word *aitvaar* that you already know by ear.

No stroke order is taught here: the track does not yet have a verified source
for one.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AI-01] -->

Keep **ਐ** in front of you. Trace its printed shape once with a finger, then
copy it once with a pencil while the model stays visible. Say *ai* when you
finish.

> This is observe-and-copy with the model visible, not writing from memory.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-WEEKDAY-AITVAR-01, PA-WEEKDAY-SHANIVAR-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AI-01] -->

- [YOU LOOK: at **ਐ**; YOU SAY: *ai*]
- Look away. Then look back and point to **ਐ**.
- [YOU SAY: *aitvaar*, then point to its opening vowel **ਐ** and say *ai*]
- [YOU SAY: **ਸ਼ਨੀਵਾਰ**, then *aitvaar*]
