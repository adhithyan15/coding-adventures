## You'll want to know: der Sanitäter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C291-NOTRUF-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Sanitäter** — "the paramedic".

> Die Sanitäter halfen dem Verletzten.
>
> *The paramedics helped the injured man.*

Plural: *die Sanitäter*. Colloquially *die Sanis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sanitäter* is built on *Sanität* "health service", a learned borrowing from Latin *sānitās* "health", from *sānus* "healthy" (Lewis & Short).
