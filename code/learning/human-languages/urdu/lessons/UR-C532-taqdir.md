## You'll want to know: تقدیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C532-QISMAT532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تقدیر** — *taqdīr* — "destiny".

> اگر تقدیر نے ساتھ دیا تو ہم ضرور ملیں گے۔
>
> *agar taqdīr ne sāth diyā to ham zarūr mileṅ ge.*
>
> If destiny is on our side, we will surely meet.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تقدیر** (*taqdīr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taqdīr*, "measuring out, decreeing".
