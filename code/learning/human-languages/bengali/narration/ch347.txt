Bengali, chapter 347: Public Offices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
দপ্তর (dôptôr) — a government department; an office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to resign, then the Bengali for to take an oath.

You'll want to know: দপ্তর.
দপ্তর — dôptôr — "a government department; an office".
আমি আবেদনটা দপ্তরে জমা দিলাম। āmi ābedônṭā dôptôre jômā dilām. I handed in the application at the department.

The word, taken apart.
দপ্তর (dôptôr) is Persian daftar, a register and then the office that keeps it, usually traced back through Arabic to Greek diphthérā, a prepared hide. Bengali sounds it with p.

পৌরসভা পাড়ার রাস্তায় নতুন বাতি লাগাল। pourôsôbhā pāṛār rāstāy nôtun bāti lāgālô. The town council put up new lamps in the neighbourhood's streets.

The word, taken apart.
পৌরসভা (pourôsôbhā) is Sanskrit paura, a townsman (Monier-Williams), with সভা, an assembly: the town's council.

Lesson 3 of 5.
পঞ্চায়েত (pônchāyet) — a village council.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a government department, then the Bengali for a town council.

You'll want to know: পঞ্চায়েত.
পঞ্চায়েত — pônchāyet — "a village council".
পঞ্চায়েতের সভায় গ্রামের সবাই এল। pônchāyeter sôbhāy grāmer sôbāi elô. Everyone in the village came to the council meeting.

The word, taken apart.
পঞ্চায়েত (pônchāyet) has a first part usually taken as Sanskrit pañca, five (CDIAL 7655 lists Bengali pā̃c), after an old village council of five elders; the rest of the word is not settled, and the whole word is in neither CDIAL nor Monier-Williams. Today it is the elected council of a village or a group of villages.

নিয়োগ করা — niyog kôrā — "to appoint; to take on (staff)".
কোম্পানি দশজন নতুন কর্মী নিয়োগ করল। kompāni dôshjôn nôtun kôrmī niyog kôrlô. The company took on ten new workers.
Past (he/she …): তিনি একজন নতুন সচিব নিয়োগ করলেন, tini ekjôn nôtun sôchib niyog kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
নিয়োগ করা (niyog kôrā) is Sanskrit niyoga, a charge or appointed task (Monier-Williams), with করা: to put someone in a post.

Warm-up.

জারি করা — jāri kôrā — "to issue an order; to issue (a notice)".
সরকার একটা নতুন নোটিশ জারি করল। sôrkār ekṭā nôtun noṭish jāri kôrlô. The government issued a new notice.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নোটিশটা জারি করলেন, tini noṭishṭā jāri kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
জারি করা (jāri kôrā) is Persian jārī, flowing or current, usually traced to Arabic, with করা: to set an order flowing, to bring it into force.
