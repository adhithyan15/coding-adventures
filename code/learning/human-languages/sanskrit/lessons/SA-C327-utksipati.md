## You'll want to know: उत्क्षिपति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C327-HOLIKA327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्क्षिपति** — *utkṣipati* — "tosses up".

> बालः कन्दुकम् उपरि उत्क्षिपति।
>
> *bālaḥ kandukam upari utkṣipati.*
>
> The boy tosses the ball up.

Past (he …): सः कन्दुकम् उत्क्षिप्तवान्, *saḥ kandukam utkṣiptavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्क्षिपति** (*utkṣipati*) is built from *ud*, "up", and the root √kṣip, "to throw", as in *kṣipati*; MW gives *ut-kṣip* as to throw up, raise (MW).
