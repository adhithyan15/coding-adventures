## You'll want to know: màoxiǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C394-TRY394-03]; assesses=[] -->

*màoxiǎn* — "to take a risk".

> *rúguǒ nǐ bù xiǎng màoxiǎn jiù bié qù* — If you don't want to take the risk, don't go.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*màoxiǎn* is built from *mào* 'to wade into' and *xiǎn* 'danger', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mawH* and *xjemX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to cover' and 'to wade' and the second 'perilous'.
