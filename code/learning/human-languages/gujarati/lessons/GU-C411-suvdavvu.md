## You'll want to know: સુવડાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C411-QUILT411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સુવડાવવું** — *suvḍāvvũ* — "to put to sleep (a child)".

> માતા બાળકને ગીત ગાઈને સુવડાવે છે.
>
> *mātā bāḷakne gīt gāīne suvḍāve che.*
>
> The mother sings the child to sleep.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને સુવડાવ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne suvḍāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સુવડાવવું** (*suvḍāvvũ*) is the causative partner of સૂવું, "to sleep, to lie down", a tadbhava of Sanskrit *svápati*, "sleeps" (CDIAL 13902): to make someone sleep.
