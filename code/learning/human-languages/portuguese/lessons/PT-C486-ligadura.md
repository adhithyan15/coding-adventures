## You'll want to know: a ligadura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C486-LIGADURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a ligadura** — "bandage".

> O médico pôs-me uma ligadura no pulso.
>
> *The doctor put a bandage on my wrist.*

In Brazil: *a atadura*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ligadura* is built on *ligar* "to tie", which is inherited from Latin *ligāre* "to bind" (Lewis & Short).
