## You'll want to know: خیس کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C355-DASTURPOKHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خیس کردن** (*khis kardan*) — "to soak".

> نخودها را از شب قبل خیس کرد.
>
> *Nokhod-hâ râ az shab-e qabl khis kard.*
>
> She soaked the chickpeas from the night before.

*Khis* "wet". Present *khis mikonad*; *khis khordan* "to stand soaking".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khis* "wet" is a native Persian word whose deeper origin is not settled; *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
