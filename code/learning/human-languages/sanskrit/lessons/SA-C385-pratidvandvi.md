## You'll want to know: प्रतिद्वन्द्वी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C385-SPARDHA385-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिद्वन्द्वी** — *pratidvandvī* — "a rival".

> यदि प्रतिद्वन्द्वी बलवान्, तर्हि अधिकः अभ्यासः आवश्यकः।
>
> *yadi pratidvandvī balavān, tarhi adhikaḥ abhyāsaḥ āvaśyakaḥ.*
>
> If the rival is strong, more practice is needed.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिद्वन्द्वी** (*pratidvandvī*) is built on *prati*, "against", and *dvandva*, "a pair, a strife"; MW gives *prati-dvaṃdvin* "an adversary, a rival" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
