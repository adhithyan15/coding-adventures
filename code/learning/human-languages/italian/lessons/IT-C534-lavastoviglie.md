## You'll want to know: la lavastoviglie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C534-PIATTI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la lavastoviglie** — "dishwasher".

> Dopo cena carico la lavastoviglie.
>
> *After dinner I load the dishwasher.*

The same form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lavastoviglie* is built from *lava* "washes", from Latin *lavāre* (Lewis & Short), and *stoviglie* "dishes", whose origin is not settled.
