## You'll want to know: задувать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C317-IMENINNIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**задувать** (*zaduvát*) — "to blow out".

> Он задул все свечи на торте.
>
> *On zadúl vse svéchi na tórte.*
>
> He blew out all the candles on the cake.

Imperfective; perfective *задуть* (*zadút*), *задую*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*задувать* is built on *за-* and *дуть* "to blow", an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists *дуть* with no parent.
