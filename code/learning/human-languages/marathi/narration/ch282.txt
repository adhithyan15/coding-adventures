Marathi, chapter 282: Asking the Way.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
स्टेशनला कसं जायचं? (sṭeśanlā kasaṁ jāycaṁ?) — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "where is the station?" (sṭeśan kuṭhe āhe?) Then "I used to go". (mī jāyco.)

You'll want to know: … ला कसं जायचं?
कृपया, स्टेशनला कसं जायचं? kṛpayā, sṭeśanlā kasaṁ jāycaṁ? — "Please, how do I get to the station?"
जायचं is the -ायच- of जायचो with -ं: "how is it to be gone". Put -ला on the place: बसस्थानकाला (basasthānakālā), विमानतळाला (vimāntaḷālā). To ask for a place by name, the question you know stays the best: बसस्थानक कुठे आहे?

You'll want to know: how far.
इथून किती लांब आहे? ithūn kitī lāmb āhe? — "How far is it from here?"
इथून (ithūn, "from here") is इथे with -ऊन, "from". The answers: जवळ आहे (javaḷ āhe), "it is near"; खूप लांब आहे (khūp lāmb āhe), "it is a long way"; दहा मिनिटं (dahā miniṭaṁ), "ten minutes".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Please, how do I get to the station?" — kṛpayā, sṭeśanlā kasaṁ jāycaṁ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "How far is it from here?" — ithūn kitī lāmb āhe?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Is it near?" — javaḷ āhe kā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Ask the way to the bus station, then ask how far it is.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"How do I get to the airport?" (विमानतळाला कसं जायचं?) "How far is it from here?" (इथून किती लांब आहे?) "From here"? (इथून.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
उजवीकडे वळा (ujvīkaḍe vaḷā) — giving the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "go right" with the -कडे you know. (ujvīkaḍe jā.) Then "straight". (saraḷ.)

You'll want to know: go, turn.
सरळ जा, मग चौकात उजवीकडे वळा. saraḷ jā, mag caukāt ujvīkaḍe vaḷā. — "Go straight, then turn right at the square."
वळा is वळणे with -आ, the polite "turn", built like घ्या and बसा. To a friend: वळ (vaḷ). जा serves both. With पहिल्या, "the first", the way gets precise: पहिल्या चौकात डावीकडे वळा (pahilyā caukāt ḍāvīkaḍe vaḷā), "turn left at the first square"; पुढे जा (puḍhe jā), "go on ahead".

You'll want to know: what stands beside it.
स्टेशन मंदिराच्या समोर आहे. sṭeśan mandirācyā samor āhe. — "The station is opposite the temple."
-च्या on the landmark, then the place word: दुकानाच्या जवळ (dukānācyā javaḷ), "near the shop"; मंदिराच्या मागे (mandirācyā māge), "behind the temple".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Go straight, then turn right at the square" — saraḷ jā, mag caukāt ujvīkaḍe vaḷā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Turn left at the first square" — pahilyā caukāt ḍāvīkaḍe vaḷā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "The station is opposite the temple" — sṭeśan mandirācyā samor āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Tell someone to go straight and turn left at the square; the bus station is near the shop.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Turn right"? (उजवीकडे वळा (ujvīkaḍe vaḷā).) "Go straight"? (सरळ जा.) "Opposite the temple"? (मंदिराच्या समोर.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I must go to the station" and "you shouldn't go at night". (malā sṭeśanlā jāylā pāhije, tū rātrī jāū nayes.) Then say ज्ञान and ऋतू, and name the letter each one holds: ञ and ऋ.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Please, how do I get to the airport?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: How far is it from here? Is it near?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Go straight, then turn left at the square.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: The bus station is near the shop, opposite the temple.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the way from your door to the nearest shop, in three sentences]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"How do I get to …?" (… ला कसं जायचं?) "Turn"? (वळा.) "Near the shop"? (दुकानाच्या जवळ.) Which letters did ऋतू and ज्ञान bring? (ऋ and ञ.)
