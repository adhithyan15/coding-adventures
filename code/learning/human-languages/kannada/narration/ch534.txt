Kannada, chapter 534: Music.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ರಾಗ (rāga) — a melody; a raga.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to string beads, then the Kannada for to adorn.

You'll want to know: ರಾಗ.
ರಾಗ — rāga — "a melody; a raga".
ಆ ಗಾಯಕ ಸಂಜೆಯ ರಾಗವನ್ನು ಹಾಡಿದರು. ā gāyaka sañjeya rāgavannu hāḍidaru. That singer sang an evening raga.
Each ರಾಗ (rāga) has its own set of notes and mood.

The word, taken apart.
ರಾಗ (rāga) is borrowed from Sanskrit rāga, "colour", and "passion, love" (CDIAL 10674): a melody that colours the mind.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಗುನುಗು (gunugu) — to hum a tune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a rhythm, then the Kannada for a musical note.

You'll want to know: ಗುನುಗು.
ಗುನುಗು — gunugu — "to hum a tune".
ಅಡುಗೆ ಮಾಡುವಾಗ ಅಮ್ಮ ಒಂದು ಹಾಡು ಗುನುಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು. aḍuge māḍuvāga amma ondu hāḍu gunuguttiddaru. While cooking, mother was humming a song.
Past (I …): ಗುನುಗಿದೆ, gunugide.
Softly, with the lips nearly closed.

The word, taken apart.
ಗುನುಗು (gunugu) imitates the sound itself; DEDR 1685 lists the related Kannada guṇuguṇu, "murmuring, buzzing". The exact origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ನುಡಿಸು (nuḍisu) — to play an instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a musical note, then the Kannada for to hum a tune.

You'll want to know: ನುಡಿಸು.
ನುಡಿಸು — nuḍisu — "to play an instrument".
ನನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣ ಗಿಟಾರ್ ನುಡಿಸುತ್ತಾನೆ. nanna aṇṇa giṭār nuḍisuttāne. My elder brother plays the guitar.
Past (I …): ನುಡಿಸಿದೆ, nuḍiside.
Literally to make it speak: ನುಡಿ, to speak.

The word, taken apart.
ನುಡಿಸು (nuḍisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3784 lists Kannada nuḍi, "to sound, utter, speak", and nuḍisu, "to cause to utter": you make the instrument speak.
