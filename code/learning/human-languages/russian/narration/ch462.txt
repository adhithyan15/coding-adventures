Russian, chapter 462: A night in a tent.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
палатка (palátka) — a tent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for moss, then the Russian for a cone.

You'll want to know: палатка.
палатка (palátka) — "a tent".
Мы поставили палатку у реки. My postávili palátku u rekí. We put up the tent by the river.
Feminine. Ставить палатку — to pitch a tent.

The word, taken apart.
палатка (palátka) is usually traced to палата 'a chamber, a hall', which goes back through Greek to Latin palātium 'a palace' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists палатка (palátka) with the sense 'tent' but no source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
спальник (spálnik) — a sleeping bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cone, then the Russian for a tent.

You'll want to know: спальник.
спальник (spálnik) — "a sleeping bag".
Ночью в спальнике было тепло. Nóchyu v spálnike býlo tepló. At night it was warm in the sleeping bag.
Colloquial for спальный мешок.

The word, taken apart.
спальник (spálnik) is a colloquial short form of спальный мешок 'a sleeping bag', from спать 'to sleep', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic sŭpati (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
разжигать (razzhigát) — to light (a fire), to kindle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sleeping bag, then the Russian for a campfire.

You'll want to know: разжигать.
разжигать (razzhigát) — "to light (a fire), to kindle".
Папа разжигает костёр одной спичкой. Pápa razzhigáyet kostyór odnóy spíchkoy. Dad lights the campfire with one match.
Imperfective; perfective разжечь (разожгу, разожжёшь).

The word, taken apart.
разжигать (razzhigát) is built from раз- and -жигать, from жечь 'to burn', the same root as зажигать 'to light'. EtymDB lists разжигать (razzhigát) without a deeper source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
тушить (tushít) — to put out (a fire); to stew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a campfire, then the Russian for to light.

You'll want to know: тушить.
тушить (tushít) — "to put out (a fire); to stew".
Перед сном мы тушим костёр водой. Péred snom my túshim kostyór vodóy. Before going to sleep we put out the campfire with water.
Imperfective; perfective потушить. Present тушу, тушишь. In cooking: тушить (tushít) мясо — to stew meat.

The word, taken apart.
тушить (tushít) goes with тухнуть 'to go out (of a fire)'. EtymDB lists тушить (tushít) without a source, so the deeper origin is left open.
