## You'll want to know: પરવાનગી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C354-ADHIKARI354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પરવાનગી** — *parvāngī* — "permission".

> અંદર જવા માટે ચોકીદારની પરવાનગી લેવી પડશે.
>
> *andar javā māṭe cokīdārnī parvāngī levī paḍśe.*
>
> You will need the watchman's permission to go in.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરવાનગી** (*parvāngī*) is usually traced to Persian *parvānagī*, "permission", from *parvāna*, "a written order".
