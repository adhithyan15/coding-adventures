## You'll want to know: شهربازی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C455-SHAHRBAZI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شهربازی** (*shahrbâzi*) — "an amusement park, a funfair".

> بچه ها روز جمعه به شهربازی رفتند.
>
> *Bachche-hâ ruz-e jom'e be shahrbâzi raftand.*
>
> The children went to the funfair on Friday.

Literally 'play-town'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shahrbâzi* is a native compound of *shahr* 'town', from Middle Persian *šahr* (EtymDB), and *bâzi* 'play', usually traced to Middle Persian *wāzīg*.
