## You'll want to know: fortuītus, fortuīta, fortuītum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C375-EXSISTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**fortuītus, fortuīta, fortuītum** — "by chance, accidental".

> Nōn fortuītus erat ille cāsus.
>
> *That event was no accident.*

*fortuītus, fortuīta, fortuītum*, built on *fors*, chance; *fortūna* is its cousin. English *fortuitous*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fortuītus* is built on *fors* "chance" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), the same word that gives *fortūna*. English *fortuitous* comes from it.
