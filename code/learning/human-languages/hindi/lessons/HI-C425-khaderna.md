## You'll want to know: खदेड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C425-HALLA425-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खदेड़ना** — *khadeṛnā* — "to chase away".

> लोगों ने बंदरों को गली से खदेड़ा।
>
> *logoṁ ne bandroṁ ko galī se khadeṛā.*
>
> People chased the monkeys out of the lane.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बकरी को खेत से खदेड़ा, *usne bakrī ko khet se khadeṛā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खदेड़ना** (*khadeṛnā*) goes back to *\*khadd*, "drive away", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3807), which lists Hindi *khadeṛnā*, "to drive away".
