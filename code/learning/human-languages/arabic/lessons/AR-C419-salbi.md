## You'll want to know: سلبي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C419-IJABI419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سلبي** — *salbī* — "negative".

> لا يحب أحد الشخص السلبي.
>
> *lā yuḥibbu aḥad ash-shakhṣ as-salbī.*
>
> Nobody likes a negative person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سلبي** (*salbī*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-l-b*, "to strip, to take away" (Lane). *Salbī* is a modern adjective from *salb*, taking away: negative.
