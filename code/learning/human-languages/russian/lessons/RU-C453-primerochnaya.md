## You'll want to know: примерочная
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C453-PRIMERKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**примерочная** (*primérochnaya*) — "a fitting room".

> Примерочная слева от кассы.
>
> *Primérochnaya sléva ot kássy.*
>
> The fitting room is to the left of the till.

An adjective used as a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*примерочная* is built on *примерка* 'a fitting', from *примерять* 'to try on', on *мера* 'a measure'. EtymDB builds *мерить* on Proto-Slavic *měra*.
