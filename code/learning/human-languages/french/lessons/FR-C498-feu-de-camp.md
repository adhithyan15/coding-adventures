## You'll want to know: le feu de camp
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C498-PLEIN-AIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le feu de camp** — "campfire".

> Le soir, nous chantions autour du feu de camp.
>
> *In the evening we used to sing round the campfire.*

*Faire un feu de camp* — to make a campfire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le feu de camp* joins *feu*, inherited from Latin *focus* "hearth", and *camp*, from Latin *campus* "a field" (Lewis & Short).
