## You'll want to know: ऐनवेळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C554-VEL554-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ऐनवेळी** — *ainveḷī* — "at the last moment".

> ऐनवेळी गाडी बिघडली आणि आम्ही उशिरा पोहोचलो.
>
> *ainveḷī gāḍī bighaḍlī āṇi āmhī uśirā pohoclo.*
>
> At the last moment the car broke down and we arrived late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऐनवेळी** (*ainveḷī*) is a Marathi compound of ऐन, "the very", a loan usually traced to Arabic *ʿain*, "eye, essence", and वेळ, "time".
