## You'll want to know: déclarer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C417-DOUANE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**déclarer** — "to declare".

> Elle n'a rien à déclarer.
>
> *She has nothing to declare.*

Also *déclarer ses revenus* — to file a tax return.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*déclarer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēclārāre* 'to make clear', from *clārus* 'clear' (EtymDB).
