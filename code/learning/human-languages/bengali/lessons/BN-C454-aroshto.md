# আড়ষ্ট (āṛôshṭô) — stiff; awkward and tongue-tied

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C454-SONGKOCH454-01, BN-LEX-C454-SONGKOCH454-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for reluctance, then the Bengali for being in two minds.

## You'll want to know: আড়ষ্ট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C454-SONGKOCH454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আড়ষ্ট** — *āṛôshṭô* — "stiff; awkward and tongue-tied".

> নতুন লোকের সামনে মীরা আড়ষ্ট হয়ে যায়।
>
> *nôtun loker sāmne mīrā āṛôshṭô hôye jāy.*
>
> In front of new people Mira goes stiff and tongue-tied.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আড়ষ্ট** (*āṛôshṭô*) is a Bengali word for stiffness, of the body or of the manner; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
