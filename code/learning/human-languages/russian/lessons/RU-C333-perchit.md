## You'll want to know: перчить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C333-SOLIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**перчить** (*perchít*) — "to pepper".

> Он всегда много перчит мясо.
>
> *On vsegdá mnógo perchít myáso.*
>
> He always puts a lot of pepper on meat.

Imperfective; perfective *поперчить* (*poperchít*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*перчить* is built on *перец* "pepper", inherited through Old East Slavic *pĭpĭrĭcĭ* from Proto-Slavic *pĭpĭrĭ*, which EtymDB traces to Latin *piper* "pepper".
