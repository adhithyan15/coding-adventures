## You'll want to know: 去世
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C325-PASSED325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**去世** — *qùshì* — "to pass away".

> **他的外公去年去世了** *tā de wàigōng qùnián qùshì le* — His grandfather died last year.

The new character, in pieces: 世 — five strokes: a long bar; two verticals through it; a short bar joining their feet; then a stroke down the left that turns right along the bottom. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**去世** (*qùshì*) is built from 去 'to leave' and 世 'the world', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khjoH* and *syejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to leave' and the second 'a generation'. The sense 'the world' is not given there.
