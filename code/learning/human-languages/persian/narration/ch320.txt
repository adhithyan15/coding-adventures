Persian, chapter 320: Detectives and clues.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کارآگاه (kârâgâh) — a detective.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be arrested, then the Persian for to detain.

You'll want to know: کارآگاه.
کارآگاه (kârâgâh) — "a detective".
کارآگاه (kârâgâh) همه چیز را با دقت نگاه کرد. Kârâgâh hame chiz râ bâ deqqat negâh kard. The detective looked at everything carefully.
kâr "work" + âgâh "aware": one who is aware of the case.

The word, taken apart.
kârâgâh is a modern compound built from Persian kâr "work" and âgâh "aware, informed", both native words.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سرنخ (sarnakh) — a clue, a lead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to detain, then the Persian for a detective.

You'll want to know: سرنخ.
سرنخ (sarnakh) — "a clue, a lead".
پلیس هنوز هیچ سرنخی ندارد. Polis hanuz hich sarnakhi nadârad. The police still have no clue.
Literally "the end of the thread": sar "head, end" + nakh "thread".

The word, taken apart.
sarnakh joins sar "head, end" and nakh "thread", both native Persian: the loose end that leads to the rest. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ردپا (radd-e pâ) — a footprint, a trace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a detective, then the Persian for a clue.

You'll want to know: ردپا.
ردپا (radd-e pâ) — "a footprint, a trace".
روی برف ردپای یک گرگ بود. Ru-ye barf radd-e pâ-ye yek gorg bud. There were a wolf's footprints in the snow.
radd "trace" + pâ "foot". Also figurative: the trace someone left behind.

The word, taken apart.
pâ "foot" is inherited Persian. The origin of radd "trace, track" is not settled, and EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
اثر انگشت (asar-e angosht) — a fingerprint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a clue, then the Persian for a footprint.

You'll want to know: اثر انگشت.
اثر انگشت (asar-e angosht) — "a fingerprint".
روی لیوان اثر انگشت بود. Ru-ye livân asar-e angosht bud. There were fingerprints on the glass.
asar "trace, mark" + angosht "finger". The Arabic asar is written with ث.

The word, taken apart.
asar is borrowed from Arabic athar, "track, trace" (EtymDB); angosht "finger" is native Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بازجویی (bâzjuyi) — an interrogation, questioning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a footprint, then the Persian for a fingerprint.

You'll want to know: بازجویی.
بازجویی (bâzjuyi) — "an interrogation, questioning".
بازجویی (bâzjuyi) مظنون سه ساعت طول کشید. Bâzjuyi-ye maznun se sâ'at tul keshid. The questioning of the suspect took three hours.
bâz "again" + ju "seeking", from jostan "to seek". Bâzjuyi kardan is "to interrogate".

The word, taken apart.
bâzjuyi joins bâz "again" and ju-, the present stem of jostan "to seek", with the noun suffix -i. All of it is native Persian; EtymDB lists no entry.
