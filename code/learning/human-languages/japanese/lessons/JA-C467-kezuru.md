## You'll want to know: けずる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C467-CRAFT467-05]; assesses=[] -->

**けずる** — *kezuru* — "to whittle; to shave down".

> **ちちは いたを けずりました。** — *chichi wa ita o kezurimashita* — "My father planed the board."

Polite **けずります**, *kezurimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **だいくが いたを けずりました。**, *daiku ga ita o kezurimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けずる** (*kezuru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
