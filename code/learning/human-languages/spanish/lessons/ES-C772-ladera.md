## You'll want to know: la ladera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C772-MONTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la ladera** — "the hillside, the slope".

> El camino sube por la ladera de la montaña.
>
> *The path climbs up the side of the mountain.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ladera* is built on *lado* 'side' with the ending *-era*. *Lado* is inherited from Latin *latus* 'side, flank' (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
