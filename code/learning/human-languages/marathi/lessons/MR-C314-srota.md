## You'll want to know: श्रोता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C314-VACHAK314-03]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रोता** — *śrotā* — "a listener".

> कार्यक्रमानंतर एका श्रोत्याने प्रश्न विचारला.
>
> *kāryakramānantar ekā śrotyāne praśna vicārlā.*
>
> After the programme a listener asked a question.

Masculine. Plural: श्रोते, *śrote*.

The oblique is श्रोत्या-, the plural श्रोते.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रोता** (*śrotā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śrotṛ*, 'one who hears, a hearer' (Monier-Williams), in its nominative form *śrotā*.
