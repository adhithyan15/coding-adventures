## You'll want to know: a decoração
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C532-ACOLHEDORA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a decoração** — "decoration, decor".

> Gosto muito da decoração da tua casa.
>
> *I really like the decor of your house.*

Built on *decorar* in the sense "to decorate".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*decoração* is built on *decorar* "to decorate", a learned borrowing from Latin *decorāre* "to adorn" (Lewis & Short), built on *decus* "ornament, grace".
