Punjabi, chapter 307: The Buffalo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮੱਝ (majjh) — a buffalo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for rice straw, then the Punjabi for a sack.

You'll want to know: ਮੱਝ.
ਮੱਝ — majjh — "a buffalo".
ਸਾਡੀ ਮੱਝ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੁੱਧ ਦਿੰਦੀ ਹੈ। sāḍī majjh bahut dudh dindī hai. Our buffalo gives a lot of milk.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੱਝ (majjh) is inherited from a reconstructed mahyā, "buffalo"; CDIAL lists Punjabi majjh under it (CDIAL 9980).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਚੋਣਾ (choṇā) — to milk an animal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a buffalo, then the Punjabi for a buffalo calf.

You'll want to know: ਚੋਣਾ.
ਚੋਣਾ — choṇā — "to milk an animal".
ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਮੱਝ ਚੋਵਾਂਗਾ। maiṁ savere majjh chovāṅgā. I will milk the buffalo in the morning.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੱਝ ਚੋਈ, maiṁ majjh choī.

The word, taken apart.
ਚੋਣਾ (choṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit cyōtati, "flows, trickles"; CDIAL lists Punjabi coṇā, "to leak, milk", under it (CDIAL 4948).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਥਣ (thaṇ) — a teat; an udder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a buffalo calf, then the Punjabi for to milk an animal.

You'll want to know: ਥਣ.
ਥਣ — thaṇ — "a teat; an udder".
ਮੱਝ ਦੇ ਥਣਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੁੱਧ ਹੈ। majjh de thaṇāṁ vich bahut dudh hai. There is a lot of milk in the buffalo's teats.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਥਣ (thaṇ) is inherited from Sanskrit stana, "female breast, udder"; CDIAL lists Punjabi thaṇ, "teat", under it (CDIAL 13666).

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬੱਕਰੀ ਚਰੀ, oh bakkrī charī.

The word, taken apart.
ਚਰਨਾ (charnā) is inherited from Sanskrit carati, "moves, grazes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi carnā, "to graze", under it (CDIAL 4686).
