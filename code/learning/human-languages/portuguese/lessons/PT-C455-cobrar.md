## You'll want to know: cobrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C455-BANCO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cobrar** — "to charge; to collect (a debt)".

> O banco cobra dois euros por cada transferência.
>
> *The bank charges two euros for each transfer.*

*Cobrar caro* — to charge a lot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cobrar* is a shortening of *recobrar* 'to recover' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *recuperāre* 'to get back'.
