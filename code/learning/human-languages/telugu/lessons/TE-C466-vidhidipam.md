# వీధిదీపం (vīdhidīpaṁ) — a street lamp

## You'll want to know: వీధిదీపం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C466-CAURASTA466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**వీధిదీపం** — *vīdhidīpaṁ* — "a street lamp".

> మా వీధిలో వీధిదీపాలు వెలగడం లేదు.
>
> *mā vīdhilō vīdhidīpālu velagaḍaṁ lēdu.*
>
> The street lamps in our street are not working.

Plural: వీధిదీపాలు, *vīdhidīpālu*.

వీధి, a street, + దీపం, a lamp. Plural వీధిదీపాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వీధిదీపం** (*vīdhidīpaṁ*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *vīthi*, "a road, a street" (CDIAL 12050), and *dīpa*, "a lamp" (CDIAL 6348).
