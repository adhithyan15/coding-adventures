## You'll want to know: じゅうしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C196-ADDR196-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうしょ** — *jūsho* — "address; residence".

> **てがみに じゅうしょを かきます。** — *tegami ni jūsho o kakimasu* — "I write the address on the letter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうしょ** (*jūsho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jū*, "dwell" (Middle Chinese *drjuH*) and *sho*, "place" (*srjoX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
