## You'll want to know: бандероль
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C451-POSOLSTVO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**бандероль** (*banderól*) — "a small parcel; printed matter (by post)".

> Я отправил книги бандеролью.
>
> *Ya otprávil knígi banderólyu.*
>
> I sent the books as a small parcel.

Feminine. Smaller and cheaper than a *посылка*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бандероль* was borrowed from French *banderole*, from Italian *banderuola* (EtymDB), a little banner; the word probably first named the paper band round a packet.
