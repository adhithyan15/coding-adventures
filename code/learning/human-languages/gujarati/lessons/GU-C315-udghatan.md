# ઉદ્ઘાટન (udghāṭan) — an inauguration; a formal opening

## You'll want to know: ઉદ્ઘાટન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C315-UDGHATAN315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્ઘાટન** — *udghāṭan* — "an inauguration; a formal opening".

> આજે નવા પુલનું ઉદ્ઘાટન થયું.
>
> *āje navā pulnũ udghāṭan thayũ.*
>
> The new bridge was opened today.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્ઘાટન** (*udghāṭan*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *udghāṭana*, "opening, unlocking" (Monier-Williams): the formal opening of a bridge, a road or a school.
