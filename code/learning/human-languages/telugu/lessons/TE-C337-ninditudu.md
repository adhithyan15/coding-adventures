# నిందితుడు (nindituḍu) — the accused; a suspect

## You'll want to know: నిందితుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C337-ARESTU337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**నిందితుడు** — *nindituḍu* — "the accused; a suspect".

> నిందితుడు తాను దొంగ కాదని చెప్పాడు.
>
> *nindituḍu tānu doṅga kādani ceppāḍu.*
>
> The accused said that he was not the thief.

Built on the Sanskrit word for blame, నింద; a woman is నిందితురాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిందితుడు** (*nindituḍu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nindita*, "blamed, censured" (CDIAL 7213), with the Telugu masculine ending *-uḍu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
