## You'll want to know: dissentiō, dissentīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C358-ASSENSUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dissentiō, dissentīre** — "to disagree, to differ in opinion".

> Saepe ā frātre meō dissentiō.
>
> *I often disagree with my brother.*

*dissentiō, dissentīre, dissēnsī, dissēnsum*. The person you disagree with goes in the ablative with *ā / ab*. English *dissent*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dissentiō* is built from *dis-* "apart" and *sentiō* "to feel, to think" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to feel apart. English *dissent* comes from it.
