## You'll want to know: капот
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C404-KAPOT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**капот** (*kapót*) — "a bonnet (of a car); a hood".

> Водитель вышел и открыл капот.
>
> *Vodítel výshel i otkrýl kapót.*
>
> The driver got out and opened the bonnet.

Masculine. In American English, the hood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*капот* was borrowed from French *capote*, a hooded cloak (EtymDB): the engine wears a hood. EtymDB traces *capote* back to Latin *caput* 'head'.
