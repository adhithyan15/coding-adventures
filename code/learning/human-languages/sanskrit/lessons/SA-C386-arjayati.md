## You'll want to know: अर्जयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C386-SIDDHI386-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्जयति** — *arjayati* — "earns; acquires".

> यदि सः प्रतिदिनं कार्यं करोति, तर्हि धनम् अर्जयति।
>
> *yadi saḥ pratidinaṃ kāryaṃ karoti, tarhi dhanam arjayati.*
>
> If he works every day, he earns money.

Past (he …): सः बहु धनम् अर्जितवान्, *saḥ bahu dhanam arjitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्जयति** (*arjayati*) comes from the root √ṛj, "to procure", in its causative form; MW gives the senses "to procure, to acquire, to obtain" and *arjita* "acquired, gained, earned" (MW).
