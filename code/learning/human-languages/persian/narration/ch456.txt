Persian, chapter 456: Couriers, phones and agents.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پیک (peyk) — a courier, a delivery rider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cultural centre, then the Persian for to close.

You'll want to know: پیک.
پیک (peyk) — "a courier, a delivery rider".
پیک (peyk) غذا را جلوی در آورد. Peyk ghazâ râ jolow-ye dar âvard. The delivery rider brought the food to the door.
Peyk-e motori — a motorbike courier.

The word, taken apart.
peyk is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian payg, which is usually glossed 'a messenger, a runner'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
صندوق پست (sanduq-e post) — a postbox (US: a mailbox).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to close, then the Persian for a courier.

You'll want to know: صندوق پست.
صندوق پست (sanduq-e post) — "a postbox (US: a mailbox)".
نامه را در صندوق پست انداختم. Nâme râ dar sanduq-e post andâkhtam. I posted the letter in the postbox.
sanduq 'box' + post 'post'.

The word, taken apart.
sanduq-e post is built from sanduq 'chest, box', borrowed from Arabic ṣundūq (EtymDB), and post, a loan usually traced to French poste.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سیم کارت (simkârt) — a SIM card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a courier, then the Persian for a postbox.

You'll want to know: سیم کارت.
سیم کارت (simkârt) — "a SIM card".
در فرودگاه یک سیم کارت تازه خریدم. Dar forudgâh yek simkârt-e tâze kharidam. At the airport I bought a new SIM card.
Written like native sim 'wire' + kârt, but taken from English.

The word, taken apart.
simkârt was borrowed from English SIM card. It is spelled like native sim 'wire', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian, and kârt 'card', from French carte.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بنگاه (bongâh) — an estate agency; a firm, an agency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a postbox, then the Persian for a SIM card.

You'll want to know: بنگاه.
بنگاه (bongâh) — "an estate agency; a firm, an agency".
از طریق بنگاه یک آپارتمان پیدا کردیم. Az tariq-e bongâh yek âpârtemân peydâ kardim. We found a flat through an estate agency.
In full bongâh-e moshâver-e amlâk. Also a firm: bongâh-e bârbari — a removals firm.

The word, taken apart.
bongâh is usually analysed as native bon 'base, foundation' and gâh 'place'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تماس گرفتن (tamâs gereftan) — to get in touch, to call (someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a SIM card, then the Persian for an estate agency.

You'll want to know: تماس گرفتن.
تماس گرفتن (tamâs gereftan) — "to get in touch, to call (someone)".
با هتل تماس گرفتیم و اتاق را رزرو کردیم. Bâ hotel tamâs gereftim va otâq râ rezerv kardim. We called the hotel and booked the room.
The person called takes bâ. More formal than zang zadan.

The word, taken apart.
tamâs gereftan is built from tamâs 'contact', a loan usually traced to Arabic tamāss 'touching one another', and the native verb gereftan 'to take'. EtymDB does not list tamâs.
