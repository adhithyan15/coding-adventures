Mandarin Chinese, chapter 93: Too Much.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
太好了 (tài hǎo le) — great!

Warm-up.

[your turn — recall: say 我也要 — wǒ yě yào, me too]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[your turn — recall: say tài guì le, too expensive — R4, from the chapter on money]

You'll want to know: 太好了.
太好了 — tài hǎo le — "great!; that's wonderful".
我们明天去 太好了 wǒmen míngtiān qù, tài hǎo le — We're going tomorrow? Great!
The next lesson puts the first character in your hand. It is the tài you have said since tài guì le.

The word, taken apart.
太好了 (tài hǎo le) is built from 太 'too; extremely', 好 'good' and 了. 太 and 好 are inherited from Middle Chinese, thajH and xawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), thajH glossed 'extreme; great'.

What you've built.
tài … le around an adjective says too much: tài guì le, too expensive. Around 好 it is not 'too good'. It is the everyday 'great!'

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say tài guì le, then tài hǎo le, and hear one complaint and one delight]

Script — 大, then a dot.
太 (tài).
大 with one stroke added, and the added stroke comes last.
大, three strokes: the bar, the left-falling stroke through it, the right-falling stroke from the crossing.
One dot, between the two legs.
3 + 1 = four.

Lesson 3 of 6.
有点儿 (yǒudiǎnr) — a bit, and not in a good way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: read the last lesson's character, then two words this one is made from.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 太好了, aloud — tài hǎo le]
[your turn — recall: say 一点儿 — yìdiǎnr, a little]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say lěng, cold — R4, recognition at distance]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 有点儿.
有点儿 — yǒudiǎnr — "a bit (of something unwanted); rather".
有点儿大 yǒudiǎnr dà — It's a bit big.
太大了 tài dà le — It's too big.
Two dips in a row: spoken, the first rises, yóudiǎnr. It is written yǒudiǎnr.

The word, taken apart.
有点儿 (yǒudiǎnr) is built from 有 'to have', 点 'a dot' and the r ending: having a dot of it. 有 and 点 are inherited from Middle Chinese, hjuwX and temX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
有点儿 + an adjective is a mild complaint: yǒudiǎnr lěng, it's a bit cold. 很 is neutral; 太 (tài) … 了 is strong. And 一点儿 is a different job: it goes after a verb, 要一点儿水.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: yǒudiǎnr — and hear it as yóudiǎnr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: 有点儿大 — yǒudiǎnr dà — It's a bit big.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: 太大了 — tài dà le — It's too big.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: yǒudiǎnr lěng — It's a bit cold.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say yào yìdiǎnr shuǐ, then yǒudiǎnr dà, and hear the two jobs]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does 有点儿 (yǒudiǎnr) say about an adjective? (A bit, and not welcome.) Which is stronger, 有点儿大 or 太大了 (tài dà le)? (太大了 (tài dà le).)

You'll want to know: gānjìng.
gānjìng — "clean".

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gānjìng is built from gān 'dry' and jìng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, kan and dzjengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'dry' and 'without dirt'.

What you've built.
An adjective takes a plain 很 where English has is: fángjiān hěn gānjìng. No 是.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: zāng.
zāng — "dirty".
xié tài zāng le — The shoes are too dirty.
yīfu yǒudiǎnr zāng — The clothes are a bit dirty.
Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
The older character for zāng is in the Guangyun as tsangX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), but only inside a two-character word for a sturdy body, and that reading would give zǎng, not zāng. No dictionary we use records the word 'dirty' in an older form, so the history of zāng is not settled.

What you've built.
gānjìng and zāng, clean and dirty, and three strengths for each: 很, 有点儿 (yǒudiǎnr), 太 (tài) … 了.

The exchange.
A: zhège zěnmeyàng — How's this one? B: 太大了 tài dà le — Too big. A: 那个呢 nàge ne — And that one? B: 有点儿小 yǒudiǎnr xiǎo — A bit small. A: 这个很好看 zhège hěn hǎokàn — This one's nice. B: yǒudiǎnr zāng — It's a bit dirty. A: 我们去吃饭 wǒmen qù chī fàn — Let's go and eat, then. B: 太好了 tài hǎo le — Great!
Three strengths of complaint, 有点儿 (yǒudiǎnr), 很 and 太 … 了, and one delight to finish: 太好了 (tài hǎo le).

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
What is wrong with the second one?

What you've built — three strengths.
很大 hěn dà — big; a plain statement.
有点儿大 yǒudiǎnr dà — a bit big, and I'd rather it wasn't.
太大了 tài dà le — too big.
太好了 tài hǎo le — great!
Listen. Fángjiān zěnmeyàng?
Speak. Answer it three ways: gānjìng, a bit dirty, too dirty.
