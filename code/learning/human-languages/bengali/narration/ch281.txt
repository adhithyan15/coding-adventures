Bengali, chapter 281: A Lucky Escape.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ভাগ্যক্রমে (bhāgyôkrôme) — luckily; by good luck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a siren, then the Bengali for refuge.

You'll want to know: ভাগ্যক্রমে.
ভাগ্যক্রমে — bhāgyôkrôme — "luckily; by good luck".
ভাগ্যক্রমে আমরা বাইরে ছিলাম; যদি ভিতরে থাকতাম, তাহলে আহত হতাম। bhāgyôkrôme āmrā bāire chhilām; jôdi bhitôre thāktām, tāhôle āhôtô hôtām. Luckily we were outside; if we had been inside, we would have been hurt.

The word, taken apart.
ভাগ্যক্রমে (bhāgyôkrôme) joins ভাগ্য, luck, to Sanskrit krama, 'a step, an order' (Monier-Williams), in the -e form: 'by the steps of luck', luckily.

Lesson 2 of 5.
নিখোঁজ (nikhõj) — missing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for refuge, then the Bengali for luckily.

You'll want to know: নিখোঁজ.
নিখোঁজ — nikhõj — "missing".
ঝড়ের পরে অনেক জেলে নিখোঁজ ছিল। jhôṛer pôre ônek jele nikhõj chhilô. After the storm many fishermen were missing.

The word, taken apart.
নিখোঁজ (nikhõj) puts the prefix ni-, 'without', before খোঁজ, a trace or news of someone, the noun behind খোঁজা, to look for (CDIAL 3929 lists Bengali khõj, 'trace'): without a trace, missing.

Lesson 3 of 5.
রক্ষা পাওয়া (rôkkhā pāoyā) — to be spared; to escape harm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: রক্ষা পাওয়া.
রক্ষা পাওয়া — rôkkhā pāoyā — "to be spared; to escape harm".
ভাগ্যক্রমে নৌকার সবাই রক্ষা পেল। bhāgyôkrôme noukār sôbāi rôkkhā pelô. Luckily everyone on the boat was spared.
Past (he/she …): সেই শিশু রক্ষা পেল, sei shishu rôkkhā pelô.

The word, taken apart.
রক্ষা পাওয়া (rôkkhā pāoyā) puts Sanskrit rakṣā, 'protection, care' (Monier-Williams), before পাওয়া: to get protection, to come through unharmed.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ছোটা (chhoṭā) — to run; to rush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for missing, then the Bengali for to be spared.

You'll want to know: ছোটা.
ছোটা — chhoṭā — "to run; to rush".
আগুন দেখে লোকজন জল নিয়ে ছুটল। āgun dekhe lokjôn jôl niye chhuṭlô. When they saw the fire, people ran with water.
Past (he/she …): সেই কুকুর ছুটল, sei kukur chhuṭlô.
The stem vowel rises in the past: ছুটল, ran.

The word, taken apart.
ছোটা (chhoṭā) is filed in CDIAL 3707 under a Middle Indo-Aryan kṣuṭyatē, 'is released', which lists Bengali chuṭā, 'to get loose, run away, run'. ছুটি, a holiday, a time let loose, is listed beside it.

অবশেষে — ôbôsheshe — "at last; finally".
অনেক খোঁজার পরে অবশেষে পুলিশ শিশুটিকে খুঁজে পেল। ônek khõjār pôre ôbôsheshe pulish shishuṭike khũje pelô. After a long search the police finally found the child.

The word, taken apart.
অবশেষে (ôbôsheshe) is Sanskrit avaśeṣa, 'what is left over' (Monier-Williams), in the -e form: 'at what remains', at the very end.
