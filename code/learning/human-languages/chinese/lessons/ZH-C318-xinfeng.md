## You'll want to know: xìnfēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C318-LETTER318-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xìnfēng* — "envelope".

> *tā de xìnfēng lǐ yǒu yì zhāng zhàopiàn* — There was a photo in his envelope.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìnfēng* is built from *xìn* 'a letter' and *fēng* 'to seal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sinH* and *pjowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'loyal and true' and the second 'great', 'a domain' and 'a rank'; the senses 'a letter' and 'to seal' are not given there.
