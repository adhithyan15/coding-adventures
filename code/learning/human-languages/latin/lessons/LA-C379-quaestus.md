## You'll want to know: quaestus, quaestūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C379-QUAESTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**quaestus, quaestūs** — "gain, profit; a way of earning a living".

> Piscātor ex marī quaestum facit.
>
> *The fisherman makes his living from the sea.*

*quaestus, quaestūs* m., built on *quaerō*, to seek: what you go out to get. *Quaestum facere*, to make money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quaestus* is the noun built on *quaerō* "to seek, to get" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): what a man goes out to win.
