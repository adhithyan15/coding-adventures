## You'll want to know: il rullino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C506-FOTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il rullino** — "roll of film".

> Ho trovato un vecchio rullino nel cassetto.
>
> *I found an old roll of film in the drawer.*

From the days before digital photos.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rullino* is a little form of *rullo* "roller", usually traced to Latin *rotulus* "a little wheel" (Lewis & Short), from *rota* "wheel".
