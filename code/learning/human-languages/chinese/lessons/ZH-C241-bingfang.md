## You'll want to know: 病房
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C241-WARD241-02]; assesses=[] -->

**病房** — *bìngfáng* — "hospital ward".

> **医生现在在病房里** *yīshēng xiànzài zài bìngfáng lǐ* — The doctor's in the ward now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**病房** (*bìngfáng*) is built from 病 'illness' and 房 'room', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjaengH* and *bjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a chamber'.
