## You'll want to know: a promoção
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C360-PROMOCAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a promoção** — "the promotion; the special offer".

> Ela recebeu uma promoção depois de dois anos.
>
> *She got a promotion after two years.*

In shops, *em promoção* — on special offer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*promoção* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōmōtiō* "advancement, promotion" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *prōmovēre* "to move forward", on *movēre* "to move".
