7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਪੇਸ਼ਾ (peshā) — a profession; a trade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to set in order, then the Punjabi for a promotion.

You'll want to know: ਪੇਸ਼ਾ.
ਪੇਸ਼ਾ — peshā — "a profession; a trade".
ਪੜ੍ਹਾਉਣਾ ਮੇਰਾ ਪੇਸ਼ਾ ਹੈ। paṛhāuṇā merā peshā hai. Teaching is my profession.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੇਸ਼ਾ (peshā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian pēsha, "craft".

Lesson 5 of 7.
ਜੁੱਟਣਾ (juṭṭṇā) — to get down to work; to set to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for successful, then the Punjabi for a certificate.

You'll want to know: ਜੁੱਟਣਾ.
ਜੁੱਟਣਾ — juṭṭṇā — "to get down to work; to set to".
ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਜੇ ਕੰਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੁੱਟਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ savere chhe vaje kamm vich juṭṭdā hāṁ. I get down to work at six in the morning.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੁੱਟਿਆ, maiṁ kamm vich juṭṭiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਜੁੱਟਣਾ (juṭṭṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed yuṭati, "is joined"; CDIAL lists Punjabi juṛnā, "to be joined", and Lahnda juṭaṇ, "to be joined, be engaged in", under it (CDIAL 10496).
