# తవ్వకాలు (tavvakālu) — excavations

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: తవ్వకాలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C367-TAVVAKALU367-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తవ్వకాలు** — *tavvakālu* — "excavations".

> తవ్వకాలలో పాత నాణేలు దొరికాయి.
>
> *tavvakālalō pāta nāṇēlu dorikāyi.*
>
> Old coins were found in the excavations.

From తవ్వు, to dig.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తవ్వకాలు** (*tavvakālu*) is native Dravidian, the plural noun of *tavvu*, "to dig, excavate" (DEDR 3122).
