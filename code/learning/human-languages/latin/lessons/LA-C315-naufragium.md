## You'll want to know: naufragium, naufragiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C315-NAUFRAGIUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**naufragium, naufragiī** — "a shipwreck".

> Naufragium prope lītus factum est.
>
> *A shipwreck happened near the shore.*

Neuter, built from the words for 'ship' and 'to break'. *Naufragium facere*: to be shipwrecked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**naufragium** stands for an older *\*nāvifragium*, from *nāvis*, "ship", and *frangō*, "to break" (Lewis & Short): a ship-breaking.
