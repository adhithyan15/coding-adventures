## You'll want to know: пластилин
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C502-CRAFTS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**пластилин** (*plastilín*) — "modelling clay".

> Дети лепят из пластилина зверей.
>
> *Déti lépyat iz plastilína zveréy.*
>
> The children are making animals out of modelling clay.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пластилин* is usually traced to Italian *plastilina*, built on Greek *plastós* 'moulded'; EtymDB has no entry.
