## You'll want to know: आयाति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C405-JAVA405-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आयाति** — *āyāti* — "arrives; comes".

> अन्तिमं लोकयानं विलम्बेन आयाति।
>
> *antimaṃ lokayānaṃ vilambena āyāti.*
>
> The last bus arrives late.

Past (it …): लोकयानम् आयातवत्, *lokayānam āyātavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आयाति** (*āyāti*) is built from *ā*, "towards", and the root √yā, "to go"; MW gives *ā-yā* as to come near, to arrive, with the participle *āyāta* (MW).
