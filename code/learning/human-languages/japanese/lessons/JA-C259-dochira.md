# どちら (dochira) — which (of two)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C259-COMPARE-02, JA-LEX-C78-LOC78-01, JA-LEX-C83-DEF83-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say "I like the sea more than the mountains". (*yama yori umi no hō ga suki desu*.) Then "where" and "which". (*doko*, *dono*.)

## You'll want to know: どちら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C259-DOCHIRA-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C55-TIME55-02, JA-LEX-C55-TIME55-03, JA-LEX-C240-LIKES240-03] -->

**どちら** — *dochira* — "which" of two. Name the two with **と**, then ask:

> **なつと ふゆと どちらが すきですか。**
>
> *natsu to fuyu to dochira ga suki desu ka.* — "Which do you like better, summer or winter?"

> **なつの ほうが すきです。**
>
> *natsu no hō ga suki desu.* — "I like summer better."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どちら** (*dochira*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: the *do-* of **どこ** and **どの**, "which", with *-chira*, a direction ending. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana and glosses it "which one (esp. of two alternatives)" and "which way" (JMdict).
