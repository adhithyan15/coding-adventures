## You'll want to know: tíngkào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C405-GATES405-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tíngkào* — "to call at a station or port".

> *gāotiě zài xiǎo zhàn bù tíngkào* — The high-speed train doesn't stop at small stations.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tíngkào* is built from *tíng* 'to stop' and *kào* 'to lean against', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *deng* and *khawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rest, to stop' and the second 'to go against each other'; the sense 'to lean on' is not given there.
