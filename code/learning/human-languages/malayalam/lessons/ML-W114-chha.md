# ഛ (chha) — the letter inside അച്ഛൻ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C114-BA-01, ML-SCRIPT-C114-CHILLU-RR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ബ and ർ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **അച്ഛൻ** (*acchan*), "father".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ഛ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C114-CHHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഛ** — *chha*, a *ch* with a puff of breath.

It is inside **അച്ഛൻ** (*acchan*), "father", joined under **ച** in **ച്ഛ**. You have
been reading it inside that join since the family chapter.

## Writing: ഛ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C114-CHHA-01] -->

Put your pen on ഛ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
