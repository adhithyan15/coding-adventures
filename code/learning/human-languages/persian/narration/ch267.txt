Lesson 1 of 5.
ورزشگاه (varzeshgâh) — a stadium.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an entrance, then the Persian for an exit.

You'll want to know: ورزشگاه.
ورزشگاه — varzeshgâh — "a stadium".

The word, taken apart.
varzeshgâh is built from varzesh, "sport, exercise" (from varzidan, "to practise"), and gâh, "place": the place of sport. varzidan is inherited, usually traced to Middle Persian warzīdan.

The word, taken apart.
pâytakht is pâ, "foot", with takht, "throne": the foot of the throne, the seat of the ruler. Both are inherited; takht continues Middle Persian taxt (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
مرز (marz) — a border.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a stadium, then the Persian for a capital city.

You'll want to know: مرز.
مرز — marz — "a border".

The word, taken apart.
marz is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian marz, "border". EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root mereǵ-, "edge, boundary", the root usually also given for English mark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a border, then the Persian for a rock.

You'll want to know: اقیانوس.
اقیانوس — oqyânus — "an ocean".

The word, taken apart.
oqyânus is borrowed from Arabic ūqiyānūs, which goes back to Greek ōkeanos, the great outer sea (EtymDB). English ocean is the same Greek word.
