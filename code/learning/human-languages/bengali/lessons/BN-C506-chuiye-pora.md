# চুঁইয়ে পড়া (chũiye pôṛā) — to seep; to leak through

## You'll want to know: চুঁইয়ে পড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C506-TANK506-05]; assesses=[] -->

**চুঁইয়ে পড়া** — *chũiye pôṛā* — "to seep; to leak through".

> বৃষ্টিতে ছাদ থেকে জল চুঁইয়ে পড়ল।
>
> *Brishṭite chhād theke jôl chũiye pôṛlô.*
>
> In the rain, water seeped through the roof.

Past (he/she …): সেই জল চুঁইয়ে পড়ল, *sei jôl chũiye pôṛlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চুঁইয়ে পড়া** (*chũiye pôṛā*) joins চুঁইয়ে, the -এ form of a verb for oozing that CDIAL lists as Bengali *cuyā*, "to leak", under Sanskrit *cyotati*, "trickles, oozes" (CDIAL 4948), to পড়া, "to fall".
