## You'll want to know: shòushāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C232-HURT232-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shòushāng* — "to get hurt; to be injured".

> *tā de tuǐ shòushāng le* — He hurt his leg.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shòushāng* is built from *shòu* 'to receive' and *shāng* 'a wound', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyuwX* and *syang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to receive' and the second 'harm'.
