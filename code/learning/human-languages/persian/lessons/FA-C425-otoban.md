## You'll want to know: اتوبان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C425-OTOBAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اتوبان** (*otobân*) — "a motorway (US: a freeway)".

> از این شهر تا پایتخت اتوبان است.
>
> *Az in shahr tâ pâytakht otobân ast.*
>
> There is a motorway from this town to the capital.

The more formal word is *bozorgrâh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*otobân* was borrowed from German *Autobahn* 'motorway' (EtymDB), a compound of *Auto* 'car' and *Bahn* 'way'.
