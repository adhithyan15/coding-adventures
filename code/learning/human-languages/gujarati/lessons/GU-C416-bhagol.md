## You'll want to know: ભાગોળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C416-AREA416-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાગોળ** — *bhāgoḷ* — "the outskirts of a village (by its gate)".

> સાંજે ગામની ભાગોળે બાળકો રમે છે.
>
> *sā̃je gāmnī bhāgoḷe bāḷko rame che.*
>
> In the evening children play at the edge of the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાગોળ** (*bhāgoḷ*) goes back to *\*bhagnatōlī*, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word for the gate in a village wall and the ground around it (CDIAL 9352).
