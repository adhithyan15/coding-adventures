## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-VIDIARTHI-194, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-LEX-HAI-02, PA-GRAMMAR-VERB-LAST-02] -->

> **ਉਹ ਵਿਦਿਆਰਥੀ ਹੈ** — *oh vidiārthī hai* — he or she is a student

Punjabi **ਉਹ** is gender-neutral in this frame; the pronoun itself does not
mark “he” versus “she.” Keep the noun in the middle and the copula last.
