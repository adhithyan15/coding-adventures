## You'll want to know: 外界
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C326-WORLD326-03]; assesses=[] -->

**外界** — *wàijiè* — "the outside world".

> **外界都很关心他的病** *wàijiè dōu hěn guānxīn tā de bìng* — People everywhere are concerned about his illness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**外界** (*wàijiè*) is built from 外 'outside' and 界 'a boundary', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngwajH* and *keajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'outside' and 'far' and the second 'a border'.
