Lesson 1 of 5.
মনোযোগ (mônojog) — attention; concentration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a university degree, then the Bengali for to tutor.

You'll want to know: মনোযোগ.
মনোযোগ — mônojog — "attention; concentration".
মনোযোগ দিয়ে শোনো। mônojog diye shono. Listen carefully.

The word, taken apart.
মনোযোগ (mônojog) is a learned compound of Sanskrit manas, mind, and yoga, joining (Monier-Williams): joining your mind to the task. The compound itself is not in the dictionary. মনোযোগ দিয়ে means 'carefully, with attention'.

বিভ্রান্ত — bibhrāntô — "confused; misled".
ভুল খবর শুনে অনেকে বিভ্রান্ত হল। bhul khôbôr shune ôneke bibhrāntô hôlô. Many people were misled by the false news.

The word, taken apart.
বিভ্রান্ত (bibhrāntô) is Sanskrit vibhrānta, wandering about, and also confused or bewildered (Monier-Williams): a mind that has lost its way.

Lesson 3 of 5.
উপলব্ধি করা (upôlôbdhi kôrā) — to realise; to grasp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for concentration, then the Bengali for misled.

You'll want to know: উপলব্ধি করা.
উপলব্ধি করা — upôlôbdhi kôrā — "to realise; to grasp".
পরে আমি আমার ভুল উপলব্ধি করলাম। pôre āmi āmār bhul upôlôbdhi kôrlām. Later I realised my mistake.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কথাটার গুরুত্ব উপলব্ধি করলেন, tini kôthāṭār gurutvô upôlôbdhi kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
উপলব্ধি করা (upôlôbdhi kôrā) is Sanskrit upalabdhi, becoming aware, understanding (Monier-Williams), with করা: to come to see a thing clearly.

Lesson 4 of 5.
বোধ করা (bodh kôrā) — to feel (a state); to sense.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for misled, then the Bengali for to realise.

You'll want to know: বোধ করা.
বোধ করা — bodh kôrā — "to feel (a state); to sense".
সারাদিন কাজ করে আমি ক্লান্ত বোধ করছি। sārādin kāj kôre āmi klāntô bodh kôrchhi. After working all day I feel tired.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ক্লান্ত বোধ করলেন, tini klāntô bodh kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
বোধ করা (bodh kôrā) is Sanskrit bodha, knowing or understanding (Monier-Williams), with করা: to become aware of a state in yourself, tiredness or relief. It is a little more formal than মনে হওয়া.

Warm-up.

স্পষ্ট — spôshṭô — "clear; distinct".
তোমার কথাটা আমার কাছে স্পষ্ট নয়। tomār kôthāṭā āmār kāchhe spôshṭô nôy. What you said isn't clear to me.

The word, taken apart.
স্পষ্ট (spôshṭô) is Sanskrit spaṣṭa, clearly seen, distinct (Monier-Williams). Speech, writing or a picture can be স্পষ্ট.
