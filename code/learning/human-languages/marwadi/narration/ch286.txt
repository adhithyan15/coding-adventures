Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 286: Test Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
इम्तिहान (imtihān) — an exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to learn by heart, then the Marwadi for to pronounce.

You'll want to know: इम्तिहान.
इम्तिहान — imtihān — "an exam".
काल इम्तिहान है। kāl imtihān hai. There's an exam tomorrow.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
इम्तिहान (imtihān) is usually traced, through Persian imtihān, to Arabic imtiḥān, "a test, a trial".

Lesson 2 of 5.
हिसाब (hisāb) — sums.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to pronounce, then the Marwadi for an exam.

You'll want to know: हिसाब.
हिसाब — hisāb — "sums".
हिसाब दोरो कोनी। hisāb doro konī. Sums aren't hard.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
हिसाब (hisāb) is usually traced, through Persian hisāb, to Arabic ḥisāb, "reckoning": sums at school, and the reckoning of money.

Lesson 3 of 5.
नतीजो (natījo) — a result.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an exam, then the Marwadi for sums.

You'll want to know: नतीजो.
नतीजो — natījo — "a result".
म्हारो नतीजो चोखो है। mhāro natījo chokho hai. My result is good.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
नतीजो (natījo) is usually traced, through Persian natīja, to Arabic natīja, "a result, an outcome"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.

Lesson 5 of 5.
आंकणो (ā̃kṇo) — to mark schoolwork; to assess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a result, then the Marwadi for a class at school.

You'll want to know: आंकणो.
आंकणो — ā̃kṇo — "to mark schoolwork; to assess".
मास्टर हिसाब आंकै है। māsṭar hisāb ā̃kai hai. The teacher marks the sums.
Past (I …): म्हैं हिसाब आंक्यो, mhaĩ hisāb ā̃kyo.

The word, taken apart.
आंकणो (ā̃kṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit aṅkayati, "marks, brands"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Gujarati ā̃kvũ and Hindi ā̃knā, both "to mark, appraise" (CDIAL 104).
