Tamil, chapter 87: Not Feeling Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
களைப்பு (kaḷaippu) — tiredness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for hunger, then the Tamil for sleep.

You'll want to know: களைப்பு.
களைப்பு (kaḷaippu) — "tiredness".
எனக்குக் களைப்பாக இருக்கிறது — "I feel tired", literally "to me it is tired-ly". The person sits in எனக்கு, "to me".

What you've built.
A first word for how the body feels.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kaḷaippu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kaḷaippu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say eṉakkuk kaḷaippāka irukkiṟatu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for hunger, then the Tamil for sleep, then say kaḷaippu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does களைப்பு (kaḷaippu) mean? ("Tiredness".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
சோர்வு (cōrvu) — weariness, fatigue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a cold, then the Tamil for a cough.

You'll want to know: சோர்வு.
சோர்வு (cōrvu) — "weariness", the drained feeling after an illness.
களைப்பு (kaḷaippu) is the tiredness of a long day; சோர்வு (cōrvu) lasts longer. சோர்வாக இருக்கிறது — "I feel worn out".

What you've built.
Two kinds of tired.
