## You'll want to know: agresser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C289-AGRESSION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**agresser** — "to attack, to assault".

> Deux hommes ont agressé un jeune homme dans la rue.
>
> *Two men attacked a young man in the street.*

Also figuratively: *ce bruit m'agresse* — this noise gets to me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*agresser* is formed on the stem of *agression*, a learned borrowing from Latin *aggressiō*, from *aggredī* "to attack" (Lewis & Short).
