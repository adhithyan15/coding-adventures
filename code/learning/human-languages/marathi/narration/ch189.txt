Marathi, chapter 189: Danger, Favour, Hard work, Benefit, Loss.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धोका (dhokā) — danger, a risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cuckoo, then the Marathi for a bee.

You'll want to know: धोका.
धोका — dhokā — "danger, a risk".

The word, taken apart.
धोका (dhokā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhrokṣa, "deceit", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6894). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "fear".

The word, taken apart.
कष्ट (kaṣṭa) is Sanskrit kaṣṭa, "bad, painful; hardship", a tatsama. CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi form as kaṭ, "pain, work" (CDIAL 2978).

Lesson 4 of 5.
फायदा (phāydā) — a benefit, a profit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a favour, then the Marathi for hard work.

You'll want to know: फायदा.
फायदा — phāydā — "a benefit, a profit".

The word, taken apart.
फायदा (phāydā) is a loan from Arabic fāʾida, "a benefit, a gain", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is f-y-d (Lane).
