## You'll want to know: کلاهبرداری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C408-KOLAHBARDARI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کلاهبرداری** (*kolâhbardâri*) — "fraud, a scam".

> این ایمیل یک کلاهبرداری است.
>
> *in imeyl yek kolâhbardâri ast.*
>
> This email is a scam.

Literally hat-lifting: *kolâh* "hat" + *bardâri* "taking up". *Kolâhbardâr* — a swindler.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کلاهبرداری** is a native Persian compound built from *kolâh* "hat", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian *kulāf*, + *bardâri* "lifting", from *bar* "up" and *dâshtan* "to hold". A swindler lifts your hat.
