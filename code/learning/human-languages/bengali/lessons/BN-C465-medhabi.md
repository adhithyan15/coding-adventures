## You'll want to know: মেধাবী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C465-BIBEK465-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মেধাবী** — *medhābī* — "gifted; brilliant (at study)".

> মেধাবী ছাত্রীটি বৃত্তি পেল।
>
> *medhābī chhātrīṭi britti pelô.*
>
> The gifted student won a scholarship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মেধাবী** (*medhābī*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *medhāvin*, "intelligent, wise, a learned man" (Monier-Williams).
