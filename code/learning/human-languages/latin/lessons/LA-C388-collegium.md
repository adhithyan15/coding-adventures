## You'll want to know: collēgium, collēgiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C388-MUNICEPS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**collēgium, collēgiī** — "an association, a guild; a board of officials".

> Fabrī in collēgium convēnērunt.
>
> *The craftsmen met in their guild.*

*collēgium, collēgiī* n., built on *collēga*, a colleague. Guilds of craftsmen and boards of priests were both *collēgia*. English *college*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*collēgium* is built on *collēga* "a colleague, one chosen with another" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *college* comes from it.
