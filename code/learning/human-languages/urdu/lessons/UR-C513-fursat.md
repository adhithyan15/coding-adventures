## You'll want to know: فرصت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C513-FURSAT513-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فرصت** — *fursat* — "free time; leisure".

> آج مجھے بالکل فرصت نہیں ہے۔
>
> *āj mujhe bilkul fursat nahīṅ hai.*
>
> I have no free time at all today.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فرصت** (*fursat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *furṣa*, "an occasion, a chance".
