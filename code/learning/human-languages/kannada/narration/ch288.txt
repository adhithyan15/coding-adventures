Kannada, chapter 288: Moreover, Suddenly, Except, Without, Anyway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಲ್ಲದೆ (allade) — moreover, besides.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the world, then the Kannada for a wall.

You'll want to know: ಅಲ್ಲದೆ.
ಅಲ್ಲದೆ — allade — "moreover, besides".
