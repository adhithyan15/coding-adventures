## You'll want to know: der Fahrschein
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C411-FAHRSCHEIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Fahrschein** — "the travel ticket (formal)".

> Ohne Fahrschein darf man nicht in die Straßenbahn.
>
> *You may not get on the tram without a ticket.*

Plural: *die Fahrscheine*. The word on official signs; people mostly say *die Fahrkarte* or *das Ticket*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fahrschein* is built from *fahren* 'to travel' and *Schein* 'certificate, slip', both inherited: *Schein* goes back to Proto-Germanic *skīnaną* 'to shine' (EtymDB).
