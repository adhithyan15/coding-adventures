Hindi, chapter 268: Rubbish, A dustbin, Smoke, Fire, Ash.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कूड़ा (kūṛā) — rubbish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a drain, then the Hindi for a tank.

You'll want to know: कूड़ा.
कूड़ा — kūṛā — "rubbish".

The word, taken apart.
कूड़ा (kūṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kūṭa, "heap, multitude" (CDIAL 3392). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word, probably, from Dravidian; it glosses the Hindi "dustheap".
