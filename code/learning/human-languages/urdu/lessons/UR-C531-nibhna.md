## You'll want to know: نبھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C531-QAUL531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نبھنا** — *nibhnā* — "to be kept up; to last (of a bond)".

> اگر دونوں سچے ہیں تو دوستی نبھے گی۔
>
> *agar donoṅ sachche haiṅ to dostī nibhe gī.*
>
> If both of them are true friends, the friendship will last.

Past (it …): وہ دوستی نبھ گئی, *voh dostī nibh gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نبھنا** (*nibhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *nibhnā*, "to last, to succeed", under Sanskrit *nirvahati*, "leads out, accomplishes" (CDIAL 7397).
