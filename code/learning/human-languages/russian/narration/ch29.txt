Russian, chapter 29: Head, Hand, Leg, Belly, Tooth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
голова (golová) — the head.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for first, then the Russian for second.

You'll want to know: голова.
голова (golová) — "the head".

What you've built.
One more word for head, hand, leg, belly, tooth.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does голова (golová) mean? ("The head".) Say it once more.
