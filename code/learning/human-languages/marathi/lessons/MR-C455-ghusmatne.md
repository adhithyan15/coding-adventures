## You'll want to know: घुसमटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C455-DHASTI455-05]; assesses=[] -->

**घुसमटणे** — *ghusmaṭṇe* — "to feel stifled".

> गर्दीच्या बसमध्ये मी घुसमटलो.
>
> *gardīcyā basmadhye mī ghusmaṭlo.*
>
> I felt stifled in the crowded bus.

Past (he …): तो घुसमटला, *to ghusmaṭlā*.

Of a body in a stuffy room, and of a mind with no one to talk to: तिचा जीव घुसमटत होता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घुसमटणे** (*ghusmaṭṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
