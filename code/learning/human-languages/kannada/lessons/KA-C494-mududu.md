# ಮುದುಡು (muduḍu) — to shrink back; to curl up

## You'll want to know: ಮುದುಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C494-BERAGU494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುದುಡು** — *muduḍu* — "to shrink back; to curl up".

> ಗುಡುಗಿನ ಸದ್ದಿಗೆ ನಾಯಿ ಮೂಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮುದುಡಿ ಮಲಗಿತು.
>
> *guḍugina saddige nāyi mūleyalli muduḍi malagitu.*
>
> At the sound of thunder the dog curled up in a corner.

Past (I …): ಮುದುಡಿದೆ, *muduḍide*.

Of fear, cold or a wilting leaf; ಮುದುಡಿಕೊ, to huddle up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುದುಡು** (*muduḍu*) is a native Kannada verb, "to shrink, to curl up"; its origin is not settled.
