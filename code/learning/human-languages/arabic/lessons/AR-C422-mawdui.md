## You'll want to know: موضوعي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C422-MAWDUI422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**موضوعي** — *mawḍūʿī* — "objective".

> كان الحكم موضوعيا.
>
> *kāna al-ḥakam mawḍūʿiyyan.*
>
> The referee was objective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**موضوعي** (*mawḍūʿī*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ḍ-ʿ*, "to put, to lay down" (Lane). *Mawḍūʿī* is a modern adjective from *mawḍūʿ*, a subject: keeping to the thing itself, objective.
