## You'll want to know: die Gänsehaut
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C479-SCHNUPFEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Gänsehaut** — "goose bumps".

> Bei dieser Musik bekomme ich Gänsehaut.
>
> *This music gives me goose bumps.*

No plural. As in English, built on the goose: *die Gans*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gänsehaut* is built on *Gans* "goose", inherited from Old High German *gans*, from Proto-Germanic *gans* (EtymDB), and *Haut* "skin": skin like a plucked goose.
