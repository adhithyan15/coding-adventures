## You'll want to know: ころ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C311-OMOIDE311-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ころ** — *koro* — "the time (when); those days".

> **こどもの ころ、いなかに すんでいました。** — *kodomo no koro, inaka ni sunde imashita* — "As a child I lived in the countryside."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ころ** (*koro*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
