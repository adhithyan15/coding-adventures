## You'll want to know: o cunhado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C492-FAMILIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o cunhado** — "brother-in-law".

> O meu cunhado é casado com a minha irmã.
>
> *My brother-in-law is married to my sister.*

*A cunhada* — sister-in-law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cunhado* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *cunnado*, from Latin *cognātus* "related by blood" (EtymDB).
