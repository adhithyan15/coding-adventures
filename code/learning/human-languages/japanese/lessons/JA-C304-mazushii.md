## You'll want to know: まずしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C304-ONGAESHI304-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まずしい** — *mazushii* — "needy; poor".

> **まずしい おじいさんと おばあさんが いました。** — *mazushii ojiisan to obāsan ga imashita* — "There lived a poor old man and his wife."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まずしい** (*mazushii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
