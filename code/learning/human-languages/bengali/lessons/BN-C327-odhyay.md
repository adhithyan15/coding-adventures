## You'll want to know: অধ্যায়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C327-UPONNAS327-04]; assesses=[] -->

**অধ্যায়** — *ôdhyāy* — "a chapter of a book; a section".

> উপন্যাসের প্রথম অধ্যায়ে নায়ক গ্রাম ছেড়ে শহরে আসে।
>
> *upônyāser prothom ôdhyāye nāyôk grām chheṛe shôhôre āse.*
>
> In the novel's opening section the hero leaves his village for the city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অধ্যায়** (*ôdhyāy*) is Sanskrit *adhyāya*, 'a lesson, a lecture; a reading' (Monier-Williams), a part of a text read at one sitting.
