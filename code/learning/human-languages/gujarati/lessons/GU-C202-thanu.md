## You'll want to know: થાણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C202-THEFT202-02]; assesses=[] -->

**થાણું** — *thāṇũ* — "police station".

> **પોલીસ થાણું સ્ટેશન પાસે છે.** — *polīs thāṇũ sṭeśan pāse che* — "The police station is near the railway station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થાણું** (*thāṇũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "place, police station", under Sanskrit *sthā́na*, "firm stance; place of abode" (CDIAL 13753).
