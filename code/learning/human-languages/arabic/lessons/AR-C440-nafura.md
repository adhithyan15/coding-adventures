>
> *laʿiba al-aṭfāl ḥawla an-nāfūra.*
>

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نافورة** (*nāfūra*) names a fountain; the origin of the word is not settled.
