Hindi, chapter 364: Ask an Expert.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विशेषज्ञ (viśeṣajña) — an expert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to compare, then the Hindi for to assess.

You'll want to know: विशेषज्ञ.
विशेषज्ञ — viśeṣajña — "an expert".
हमें एक विशेषज्ञ से मिलना चाहिए। hamẽ ek viśeṣajña se milnā cāhiye. We should see an expert.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
विशेषज्ञ (viśeṣajña) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit viśeṣajña, "knowing the differences" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
छाँटना (chā̃ṭnā) — to sort out, to pick out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an article, then the Hindi for an essay.

You'll want to know: छाँटना.
छाँटना — chā̃ṭnā — "to sort out, to pick out".
माँ अच्छे आम छाँट रही है। māṁ acche ām chā̃ṭ rahī hai. Mother is picking out the good mangoes.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अच्छे फल छाँटे, usne acche phal chā̃ṭe.

The word, taken apart.
छाँटना (chā̃ṭnā) is inherited from chaṇṭ, "scatter", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4970). Hindi uses it for sifting and picking out.
