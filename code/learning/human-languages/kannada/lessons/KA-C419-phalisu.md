## You'll want to know: ಫಲಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C419-VIPHALA419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಫಲಿಸು** — *phalisu* — "to bear fruit".

> ಅವಳ ಪ್ರಯತ್ನ ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಫಲಿಸಿತು.
>
> *avaḷa prayatna konege phalisitu.*
>
> Her effort finally paid off.

Past (it …): ಫಲಿಸಿತು, *phalisitu*.

Of efforts, prayers and trees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಫಲಿಸು** (*phalisu*) is built on Sanskrit *phala*, "fruit" (CDIAL 9051), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
