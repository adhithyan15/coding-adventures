Bengali, chapter 284: At the Police Station.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
কবুল করা (kôbul kôrā) — to confess; to own up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an investigation, then the Bengali for a police inspector.

You'll want to know: কবুল করা.
কবুল করা — kôbul kôrā — "to confess; to own up".
অনেক প্রশ্নের পরে চোর অপরাধ কবুল করল। ônek prôshner pôre chor ôpôrādh kôbul kôrlô. After many questions the thief confessed to the crime.
Past (he/she …): সেই চোর অপরাধ কবুল করল, sei chor ôpôrādh kôbul kôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
কবুল করা (kôbul kôrā) puts কবুল — usually traced through Persian to Arabic qabūl, 'acceptance' — before করা: to accept the charge as true, to confess.

Lesson 2 of 5.
সূত্র (sūtrô) — a clue; a lead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a police inspector, then the Bengali for to confess.

You'll want to know: সূত্র.
সূত্র — sūtrô — "a clue; a lead".
পুলিশ জানালার কাছে একটা সূত্র পেল। pulish jānālār kāchhe ekṭā sūtrô pelô. The police found a clue near the window.

The word, taken apart.
সূত্র (sūtrô) is Sanskrit sūtra, 'a thread, a line' (Monier-Williams); in a mystery it is the thread you follow. সুতো, thread, is the everyday word from the same source.

পাহারা দেওয়া (pāhārā deoyā) puts পাহারা — usually traced to Persian pahra, a watch or a guard — before দেওয়া: to give watch, to stand guard.

Lesson 5 of 5.
জামিন (jāmin) — bail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to guard, then the Bengali for a prisoner.

You'll want to know: জামিন.
জামিন — jāmin — "bail".
দুই দিন পরে আদালত তাকে জামিন দিল। dui din pôre ādālôt tāke jāmin dilô. Two days later the court granted him bail.
জামিন (jāmin) দেওয়া, to grant bail; জামিন (jāmin) পাওয়া, to get bail.

The word, taken apart.
জামিন (jāmin) is usually traced to Persian zāmin, 'a surety', a word Persian took from Arabic; it is the guarantee that lets a prisoner go home until the trial.
