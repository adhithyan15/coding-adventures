## You'll want to know: खतम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C290-SARU290-04]; assesses=[] -->

**खतम** — *khatam* — "finished".

> म्हारो पाठ खतम है।
>
> *mhāro pāṭh khatam hai.*
>
> My lesson is finished.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खतम** (*khatam*) is usually traced, through Persian *khatm*, to Arabic *khatm*, "sealing; the end".
