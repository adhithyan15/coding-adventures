Mandarin Chinese, chapter 35: Who Does What.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
gōngzuò — to work; job.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to know someone, then the Mandarin for friend.

You'll want to know: gōngzuò.
gōngzuò — "to work; job".
wǒ jiějie gōngzuò, wǒ shàngxué — My sister works; I go to school.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gōngzuò is built from gōng 'craft' and zuò 'to do', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kuwng and tsak (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'office' and 'skill', the second 'to do'.

What you've built.
One more word about people.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gōngzuò]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gōngzuò, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wǒ jiějie gōngzuò, wǒ shàngxué — My sister works; I go to school.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for to know someone, then the Mandarin for friend, then say gōngzuò again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does gōngzuò mean? ("To work; job".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
xiānsheng — Mr; sir; husband.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for old, then the Mandarin for teacher.

You'll want to know: xiānsheng.
xiānsheng — "Mr; sir; husband".
tā xiānsheng shì lǎoshī — Her husband is a teacher.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xiānsheng is built from xiān 'before' and 生 'to be born, to grow', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sen and srjaeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). 'The one born first' is the usual explanation of how it came to mean an older man to respect, and then Mr or husband; the path is not settled.

The word, taken apart.
zhù is inherited from Middle Chinese drjuH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to stop, to stay'. Staying somewhere becoming living there is a likely step, but it is not settled.
