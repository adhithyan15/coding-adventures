## You'll want to know: раздражать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C472-ANGER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**раздражать** (*razdrazhát*) — "to irritate".

> Меня раздражает шум за стеной.
>
> *Menyá razdrazháyet shum za stenóy.*
>
> The noise behind the wall irritates me.

Imperfective; perfective раздражить. The person who is irritated goes in the accusative: *меня раздражает*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*раздражать* is built on *раз-* and a stem usually linked to the one in *дразнить* 'to tease'; EtymDB lists the verb, glossed 'to irritate', without a source.
