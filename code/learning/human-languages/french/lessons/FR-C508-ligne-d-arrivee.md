## You'll want to know: la ligne d'arrivée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C508-COURSE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la ligne d'arrivée** — "finishing line".

> Elle a franchi la ligne d'arrivée en premier.
>
> *She crossed the finishing line first.*

The start is *la ligne de départ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la ligne d'arrivée* joins *ligne*, from Latin *līnea* "a line", and *arrivée*, built on *arriver*, from Vulgar Latin *arrīpāre* "to come to the shore", built on *rīpa* "bank" (Lewis & Short).
