German, chapter 500: Sammeln und rätseln.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Sammlung — collection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to crochet, then the German for to embroider.

You'll want to know: die Sammlung.
die Sammlung — "collection".
Mein Opa hat eine große Sammlung von alten Briefmarken. My grandpa has a big collection of old stamps.
Plural: die Sammlungen.

The word, taken apart.
Sammlung is built on sammeln "to collect" with -ung (EtymDB); sammeln is inherited from Middle High German sameln.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Münze — coin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to embroider, then the German for collection.

You'll want to know: die Münze.
die Münze — "coin".
Er sammelt Münzen aus aller Welt. He collects coins from all over the world.
Plural: die Münzen. Also a mint, where coins are made.

The word, taken apart.
Münze is inherited from Old High German muniz, from Proto-Germanic munitą, an early borrowing from Latin monēta "place for making coins, coined money" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Rätsel — riddle, puzzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for coin, then the German for to do crafts.

You'll want to know: das Rätsel.
das Rätsel — "riddle, puzzle".
Das Kind löst gern Rätsel in der Zeitung. The child likes solving puzzles in the newspaper.
Plural: die Rätsel. Built on raten — to guess.

The word, taken apart.
Rätsel is built on raten "to guess, to advise", inherited from Old High German rātan, from Proto-Germanic rēdaną (EtymDB): something to guess.

Lesson 5 of 5.
das Kreuzworträtsel — crossword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to do crafts, then the German for riddle.

You'll want to know: das Kreuzworträtsel.
das Kreuzworträtsel — "crossword".
Jeden Sonntag macht mein Vater das Kreuzworträtsel. Every Sunday my father does the crossword.
Plural: die Kreuzworträtsel.

The word, taken apart.
Kreuzworträtsel is built on Kreuz "cross", which came through Old High German chruzi from Latin crux (EtymDB), Wort "word" and Rätsel "puzzle".
