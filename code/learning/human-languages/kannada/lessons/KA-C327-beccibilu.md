# ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು (beccibīḷu) — to be startled

## You'll want to know: ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C327-THATKSHANA327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು** — *beccibīḷu* — "to be startled".

> ಗುಡುಗು ಕೇಳಿ ನಾನು ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬಿದ್ದೆ.
>
> *guḍugu kēḷi nānu beccibidde.*
>
> I was startled when I heard the thunder.

Past (I …): ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬಿದ್ದೆ, *beccibidde*.

ಬೆಚ್ಚಿ, startled, and ಬೀಳು, to fall: to jump with fright. The past is ಬಿದ್ದೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು** (*beccibīḷu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *beccu*, "to be startled, alarmed" (DEDR 5489), and *bīḷ*, "to fall" (DEDR 5430).
