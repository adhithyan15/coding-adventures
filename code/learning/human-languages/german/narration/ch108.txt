German, chapter 108: North, South, East, West, Straight on.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Norden — the north.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for traffic lights, then the German for a car park.
[pause 2 seconds]
Two old family words from earlier chapters: say die Katze, then the colour gelb.

You'll want to know: der Norden.
