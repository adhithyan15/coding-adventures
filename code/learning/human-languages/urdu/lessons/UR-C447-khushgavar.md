## You'll want to know: خوشگوار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C447-CALM447-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خوشگوار** — *khushgavār* — "pleasant".

> آج کا موسم بڑا خوشگوار ہے۔
>
> *āj kā mausam baṛā khushgavār hai.*
>
> The weather is very pleasant today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خوشگوار** (*khushgavār*) is usually traced to Persian *khushgawār*, "agreeable"; its first part is **خوش**, "happy".
