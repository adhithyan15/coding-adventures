## You'll want to know: huìyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C527-ORDER527-03]; assesses=[] -->

*huìyuán* — "a member of a club or a shop's scheme".

> *wǒ dāng le sān nián huìyuán* — I've been a member for three years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huìyuán* is built from 会 'to meet' and *yuán*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwajH* and *hjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to come together' and the second as the number of things; the sense 'a member' is not given there.
