## You'll want to know: dēditiō, dēditiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C343-TRIUMPHUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**dēditiō, dēditiōnis** — "a surrender".

> Post longam obsidiōnem dēditiō facta est.
>
> *After a long siege a surrender took place.*

Feminine, from *dēdō*. In a *dēditiō*, the defeated put everything into the winner's hands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēditiō** is built on *dēdō*, "to give up, to surrender" (EtymDB), with the ending *-tiō*.
