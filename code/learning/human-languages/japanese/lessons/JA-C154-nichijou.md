## You'll want to know: にちじょう

**にちじょう** — *nichijō* — "everyday life; daily".

> **にちじょうは たのしいです。** — *nichijō wa tanoshii desu* — "Everyday life is enjoyable."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にちじょう** (*nichijō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nichi*, "day, sun" (Middle Chinese *nyit*) and *jō*, "usual, regular" (*dzyang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
