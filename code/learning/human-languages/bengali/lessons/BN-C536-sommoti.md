## You'll want to know: সম্মতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C536-ROKOM536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্মতি** — *sômmôti* — "consent".

> বাবা সম্মতি দিলে আমরা বিয়ে করব।
>
> *Bābā sômmôti dile āmrā biye kôrbô.*
>
> If my father gives his consent, we'll get married.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্মতি** (*sômmôti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sammati*, "sameness of opinion, approval" (Monier-Williams).
