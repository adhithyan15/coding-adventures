## You'll want to know: yǒushíhou
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C126-OFTEN126-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒushíhou* — "sometimes".

> *wǒ yǒushíhou qù Běijīng* — Sometimes I go to Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒushíhou* is built from 有 'there is' and *shíhou* 'time', all three parts inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuwX*, *dzyi* and *huwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): there are times.
