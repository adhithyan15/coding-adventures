Gujarati, chapter 546: With or Without.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વગર (vagar) — without.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a try-out, then the Gujarati for a clever trick.

You'll want to know: વગર.
વગર — vagar — "without".
જો તમે છત્રી વગર જશો તો તમે પલળી જશો. jo tame chatrī vagar jaśo to tame palḷī jaśo. If you go without an umbrella, you'll get soaked.

The word, taken apart.
વગર (vagar) is usually traced to Persian baghair, "without". It follows its noun: છત્રી વગર.

Lesson 2 of 5.
વિના (vinā) — without (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a clever trick, then the Gujarati for without.

You'll want to know: વિના.
વિના — vinā — "without (formal)".
જો છોડ પાણી વિના રહેશે તો તે કરમાઈ જશે. jo choḍ pāṇī vinā raheśe to te karmāī jaśe. If the plant goes without water, it will wilt.

The word, taken apart.
વિના (vinā) goes back to Sanskrit vínā, "without" (Monier-Williams), which CDIAL lists with Gujarati forms (CDIAL 11772). It is a little more formal than વગર (vagar).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઉપરાંત (uprā̃t) — in addition to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for without (formal), then the Gujarati for except.

You'll want to know: ઉપરાંત.
ઉપરાંત — uprā̃t — "in addition to".
જો પગાર ઉપરાંત ઘર પણ મળશે તો હું આ નોકરી લઈશ. jo pagār uprā̃t ghar paṇ maḷśe to hũ ā nokrī laīś. If I get a house as well as the pay, I'll take this job.

The word, taken apart.
ઉપરાંત (uprā̃t) follows its noun: પગાર ઉપરાંત, on top of the pay. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
કદી (kadī) — ever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for except, then the Gujarati for in addition to.

You'll want to know: કદી.
કદી — kadī — "ever".
જો તમે કદી અમારા ગામ આવશો તો તમારે અમારા ઘરે જ રહેવું પડશે. jo tame kadī amārā gām āvśo to tamāre amārā ghare ja rahevũ paḍśe. If you ever come to our village, you'll have to stay at our house.

The word, taken apart.
કદી (kadī) is usually traced to Sanskrit kadā, "when?" (Monier-Williams). With a negative it is "never": કદી નહીં.
