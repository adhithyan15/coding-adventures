## You'll want to know: معاون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C381-SARPARAST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**معاون** (*mo'âven*) — "a deputy, an assistant manager".

> معاون مدرسه با والدین صحبت کرد.
>
> *mo'âven-e madrese bâ vâledeyn sohbat kard.*
>
> The deputy head of the school talked with the parents.

Plural *mo'âvenân*. *Mo'âven-e vazir* — a deputy minister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معاون** is usually traced to Arabic *mu'āwin* "helper, assistant": the person who helps the head.
