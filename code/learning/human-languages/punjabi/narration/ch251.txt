Punjabi, chapter 251: Spoons and Baskets.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਡੋਈ (ḍoī) — a wooden spoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a water pitcher, then the Punjabi for brass.

You'll want to know: ਡੋਈ.
ਡੋਈ — ḍoī — "a wooden spoon".
ਡੋਈ ਲੱਕੜ ਦੀ ਹੈ। ḍoī lakkaṛ dī hai. The spoon is made of wood.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਡੋਈ (ḍoī) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍōva, "spoon"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍoī, "spoon" (CDIAL 5573).

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for brass, then the Punjabi for a wooden spoon.

You'll want to know: ਛਿੱਕਾ.

ਦੁੱਧ ਦੀ ਹਾਂਡੀ ਛਿੱਕੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ। dudh dī hāṇḍī chhikke vich hai. The milk pot is in the hanging net.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਚੰਗੇਰ (chaṅger) — a bread basket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a hanging net, then the Punjabi for a flat basket.

You'll want to know: ਚੰਗੇਰ.
ਚੰਗੇਰ — chaṅger — "a bread basket".
ਚੰਗੇਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਗਰਮ ਰੋਟੀਆਂ ਹਨ। chaṅger vich garam roṭīāṁ han. There are hot rotis in the bread basket.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਚੰਗੇਰ (chaṅger) is inherited from Sanskrit caṅgērī, "basket"; CDIAL lists Punjabi caṅger, "shallow basket" (CDIAL 4565). CDIAL notes that the varied modern forms point to a source outside Indo-Aryan.
