Hindi, chapter 410: Passports and Customs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पासपोर्ट (pāsporṭ) — a passport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a flight, then the Hindi for arrival.

You'll want to know: पासपोर्ट.
पासपोर्ट — pāsporṭ — "a passport".
अपना पासपोर्ट हमेशा पास रखिए। apnā pāsporṭ hameśā pās rakhiye. Always keep your passport on you.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पासपोर्ट (pāsporṭ) is borrowed from English passport.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सुरक्षा (surakṣā) — security; safety.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a passport, then the Hindi for a visa.

You'll want to know: सुरक्षा.
सुरक्षा — surakṣā — "security; safety".
एयरपोर्ट पर सुरक्षा बहुत सख़्त थी। eyarporṭ par surakṣā bahut sakht thī. Security at the airport was very tight.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सुरक्षा (surakṣā) is a learned word made of Sanskrit su-, "well", and rakṣā, "protection, guarding" (Monier-Williams). सुरक्षित, safe, is its partner.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: कस्टम.
कस्टम — kasṭam — "customs".
कस्टम पर एक आदमी ने मेरी अटैची खोली। kasṭam par ek ādmī ne merī aṭaicī kholī. At customs a man opened my suitcase.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
कस्टम (kasṭam) is borrowed from English customs, the check on goods at an airport or a border; Hindi has dropped the final s.

Lesson 5 of 5.
शुल्क (śulk) — a fee; a duty (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for security, then the Hindi for customs.

You'll want to know: शुल्क.
शुल्क — śulk — "a fee; a duty (formal)".
नई घड़ी के लिए हमें कस्टम पर शुल्क देना पड़ा। naī ghaṛī ke liye hamẽ kasṭam par śulk denā paṛā. We had to pay duty at customs on the new watch.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
शुल्क (śulk) is a tatsama, Sanskrit śulka, "a price" and also a toll or duty taken at roads and ferries (Monier-Williams); offices, customs and the ticket window at a monument all use it for a fee.
