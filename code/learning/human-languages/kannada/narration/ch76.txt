Kannada, chapter 76: Reading — Words, Lines, and a First Passage.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
(reading) — six words, and a script shaped by a leaf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Every word below you have written. Not one is new.
[pause 1 second]
What is new is that nobody says them first.

Reading.
ಬಾಗಿಲು ಕುರ್ಚಿ ನದಿ ಮರ ಗಾಳಿ ಹೂವು.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read down the list once, without stopping.
[pause 3 seconds]
Again — and notice how few straight lines there are.

You'll want to know.
[pause 2 seconds]
Almost none. Kannada rounds nearly every letter, and the reason is the material: the script was cut into palm leaf for a thousand years, and a straight stroke splits a leaf along its grain.
[pause 2 seconds]
So the curves are not decoration. They are what a scribe had to do to keep the page from tearing, kept long after the page became paper.
[pause 2 seconds]
A reader gains from it too. Rounded shapes are easier to tell apart at a glance than straight ones, which is most of what reading a word cold is.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: the six words down the list, once, without stopping]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[repeat that twice]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which word is the door?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
How many of the six were new? (None.)
[pause 2 seconds]
Why is Kannada round? (Palm leaf — a straight cut splits it.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
(reading) — six lines, two verbs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The last lesson gave you words. These put them somewhere.
[pause 1 second]
Nothing here is new.

Reading.
ಇದು ಬಾಗಿಲು. ಇದು ಕುರ್ಚಿ. ಕುರ್ಚಿ ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ. ಮರ ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ. ನಿಜ. ಈಗ.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read down once, without stopping.
[pause 3 seconds]
Again — and count how many different verbs there are.

You'll want to know.
[pause 2 seconds]
One, and two of the lines have none at all.
[pause 2 seconds]
ಇದು ಬಾಗಿಲು puts two words side by side and is finished: this door means this is a door. Kannada does not say is when it is only naming something.
[pause 2 seconds]
But ಇದೆ appears the moment you say where a thing is. Naming needs no verb; locating does. That is the distinction these six lines are for, and it is visible on the page before anyone names it.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: the six lines, once, in order, without stopping]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[repeat that twice]
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Which lines need ಇದೆ, and which do not?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
What joins ಇದು and ಬಾಗಿಲು? (Nothing.)
[pause 2 seconds]
When does Kannada need ಇದೆ? (When it says where.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
(reading) — the first passage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Six words, then six lines. Now eleven of them together.
[pause 1 second]
Thirty-one words, and not one of them is new.

Reading.
ಇದು ಬಾಗಿಲು. ಇದು ಕುರ್ಚಿ. ಕುರ್ಚಿ ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ. ಚಾಕು ಮತ್ತು ಪಾತ್ರೆ ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ. ಮರ ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ. ಬೇರು ದೊಡ್ಡ. ನದಿ ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ. ಹಸು ಅಥವಾ ಮೇಕೆ. ಗಾಳಿ ಈಗ ಹೆಚ್ಚು. ಹೂವು ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇಲ್ಲ. ಇದು ನಿಜ.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read it once without stopping. Do not translate as you go — let the sentences arrive.
[pause 3 seconds]
Now read it again, and watch ಇಲ್ಲಿ and ಅಲ್ಲಿ take turns.

You'll want to know.
[pause 2 seconds]
The passage stands in one spot and points. Everything near is ಇಲ್ಲಿ, everything far is ಅಲ್ಲಿ, and the last line before the ending is the only one that says a thing is not here.
[pause 2 seconds]
ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇಲ್ಲ — here, not. Two words one letter apart doing opposite work, which is the hardest pair on this page to read quickly and the one worth slowing down for.
[pause 2 seconds]
Two joiners carry the whole thing: ಮತ್ತು adds, ಅಥವಾ offers. That is what a language can do before it has taught a third of either, and it is more than enough to describe a place.
[pause 2 seconds]
This is the first whole passage the book has asked you to hold at once, and it works because everything in it was paid for.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: the passage aloud, once, without stopping]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[repeat that twice]
[question — say your answer, then pause 14 seconds]
What is not here?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Which two words are one letter apart and mean opposite things? (ಇಲ್ಲಿ and ಇಲ್ಲ.)
[pause 2 seconds]
How many joiners does the passage use? (Two.)
