## You'll want to know: سرمایه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C412-SARMAYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سرمایه** (*sarmâye*) — "capital, an investment".

> او با سرمایه کمی یک فروشگاه باز کرد.
>
> *u bâ sarmâye-ye kami yek forushgâh bâz kard.*
>
> He opened a shop with a little capital.

*Sar* "head" + *mâye* "substance, stock".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرمایه** is a native Persian compound built from *sar* "head" + *mâye* "substance, stock", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian "essence, substance". Capital is the head stock of a business.
