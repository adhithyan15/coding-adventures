## You'll want to know: la sinceridad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C724-MENTIRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la sinceridad** — "sincerity, frankness".

> Te lo digo con toda sinceridad.
>
> *I am telling you in all honesty.*

*Con sinceridad* — honestly, frankly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sinceridad* is usually described as a learned borrowing from Latin *sinceritās* "purity, soundness", built on *sincērus* "genuine", the source of *sincero* (EtymDB).
