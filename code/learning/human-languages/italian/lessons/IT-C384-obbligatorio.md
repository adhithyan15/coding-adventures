## You'll want to know: obbligatorio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C384-NORMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**obbligatorio** — "compulsory, mandatory".

> In macchina la cintura è obbligatoria.
>
> *In the car the seat belt is compulsory.*

*Non è obbligatorio* — it is not compulsory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obbligatorio* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *obligātōrius* "binding", from *obligāre* "to bind, to oblige" (Lewis & Short), built on *ligāre* "to tie".
