## You'll want to know: きげん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C168-DELAY168-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きげん** — *kigen* — "a deadline; a time limit".

> **しゅくだいの きげんは あしたです。** — *shukudai no kigen wa ashita desu* — "The homework deadline is tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きげん** (*kigen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "period, time" (Middle Chinese *gi*) and *gen*, "limit" (*heanX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
