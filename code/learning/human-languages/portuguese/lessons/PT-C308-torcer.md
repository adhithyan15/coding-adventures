## You'll want to know: torcer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C308-SUSTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**torcer** — "to twist, to sprain; to support (a team)".

> Torci o pé nas escadas.
>
> *I twisted my ankle on the stairs.*

*Torcer por uma equipa* — to support a team.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*torcer* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *torcēre*, from Latin *torquēre* "to twist" (EtymDB).
