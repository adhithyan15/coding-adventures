# ಭಾವ (bhāva) — a brother-in-law

## You'll want to know: ಭಾವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C525-NENTARU525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭಾವ** — *bhāva* — "a brother-in-law".

> ನನ್ನ ಅಕ್ಕನ ಗಂಡ, ಅಂದರೆ ನನ್ನ ಭಾವ, ವೈದ್ಯರು.
>
> *nanna akkana gaṇḍa, andare nanna bhāva, vaidyaru.*
>
> My elder sister's husband, that is, my brother-in-law, is a doctor.

An elder sister's husband, or a husband's elder brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭಾವ** (*bhāva*), the kinship word, is usually traced to Sanskrit *bhāvuka*, "a sister's husband" (Monier-Williams). It is a different word from *bhāva*, "being, feeling".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
