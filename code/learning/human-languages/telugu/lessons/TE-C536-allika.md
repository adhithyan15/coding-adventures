## You'll want to know: అల్లిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C536-VIRAMAM536-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అల్లిక** — *allika* — "knitting; weaving".

> అమ్మమ్మ ఖాళీ సమయంలో అల్లిక చేస్తుంది.
>
> *ammamma khāḷī samayaṁlō allika cēstundi.*
>
> Grandma knits in her free time.

The noun of అల్లు, to knit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అల్లిక** (*allika*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 260 lists Telugu *allu*, "to plait, to braid, to weave", with Tamil *allu*, "to knit".
