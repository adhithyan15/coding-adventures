## You'll want to know: टोकरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C183-HATH183-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टोकरी** — *ṭokrī* — "a basket".

> टोकरी मांय सबजी है।
>
> *ṭokrī mā̃y sabjī hai.*
>
> There are vegetables in the basket.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टोकरी** (*ṭokrī*) comes from a reconstructed *\*ṭōkka*, "a basket"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ṭokrī* (CDIAL 5477). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
