## You'll want to know: वादः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C343-VADA343-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वादः** — *vādaḥ* — "a debate; a thesis".

> श्वः सभायां वादः भविष्यति।
>
> *śvaḥ sabhāyāṃ vādaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> There will be a debate in the assembly tomorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वादः** (*vādaḥ*) comes from the root √vad, "to speak"; MW gives *vāda* the senses "a thesis, an argument" and "discussion, controversy, dispute" (MW).
