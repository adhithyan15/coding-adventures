# ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ (bujhāuṇā) — to put out (a light or a fire)

## You'll want to know: ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C234-DIN234-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ** — *bujhāuṇā* — "to put out (a light or a fire)".

> ਮੈਂ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬੱਤੀ ਬੁਝਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ rāt nū̃ battī bujhāundī hāṁ.*
>
> At night I put out the light.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੀਵਾ ਬੁਝਾਇਆ, *maiṁ dīvā bujhāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ** (*bujhāuṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*vijjhāyati*, "is burnt out, is extinguished"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bujhāuṇā*, "to extinguish" (CDIAL 11703).
