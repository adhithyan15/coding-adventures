## You'll want to know: たたく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C469-INSTR469-03]; assesses=[] -->

**たたく** — *tataku* — "to beat; to tap".

> **こどもが たいこを たたいています。** — *kodomo ga taiko o tataite imasu* — "The children are beating a drum."

Polite **たたきます**, *tatakimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おとうとが たいこを たたきました。**, *otōto ga taiko o tatakimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たたく** (*tataku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
