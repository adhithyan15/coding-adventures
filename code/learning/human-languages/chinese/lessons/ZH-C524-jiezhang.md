## You'll want to know: jiézhàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C524-PRICE524-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiézhàng* — "to settle the bill".

> *wǒmen děng le èrshí fēnzhōng cái jiézhàng* — We waited twenty minutes before we could settle the bill.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiézhàng* is built from *jié* 'to tie' and *zhàng* 'an account'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *ket* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to tie'; no dictionary we use records an older form of the second, so its history is not settled.
