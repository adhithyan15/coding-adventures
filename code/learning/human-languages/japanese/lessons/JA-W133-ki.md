# き — さ, with a second bar

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-EKI, JA-LEX-C132-SORE, JA-SCRIPT-SA-01, JA-LEX-C119-THINGS119-04] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Four recalls, then the sign.

- [YOU RECALL: say *a station* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *that*, a thing near the listener — **R2**, five lessons back]
- [YOU RECALL: write **さ**: the bar, the diagonal with its hook, the curve]
- [YOU RECALL: say *a barber* — **R4**, eighty lessons back]

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KI-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SA-01] -->

> **き** — *ki*

Four strokes, three lifts:

1. the upper **bar**, left to right
2. the lower **bar**, left to right
3. the long **diagonal** from the top, down to the right through both bars; at
   its foot, turn sharply and **hook** back to the left
4. a separate **curve**: start at the left, drop down, and finish along the base
   to the right

The diagonal, the hook and the curve are the ones your hand already makes in
**さ**. Only the top differs:

| you write | bars at the top |
|---|---|
| **さ**, *sa* | one |
| **き**, *ki* | two |

With **え** in front of it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**えき**, *eki*, a station.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KI-01, JA-SCRIPT-SA-01, JA-SCRIPT-E-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-EKI] -->

1. Trace **き** saying "bar, bar, diagonal and hook, curve".
2. Write **さ** and **き** side by side, and count the bars.
3. Write **えき** from memory and say *eki*.
