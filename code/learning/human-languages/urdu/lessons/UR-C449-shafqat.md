## You'll want to know: شفقت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C449-LOVE449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شفقت** — *shafqat* — "tender affection (of an elder)".

> دادی ہم پر بڑی شفقت کرتی ہیں۔
>
> *dādī ham par baṛī shafqat kartī haiṅ.*
>
> My grandmother is very tender with us.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شفقت** (*shafqat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shafaqa*, "tenderness, pity".
