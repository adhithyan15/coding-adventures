## You'll want to know: ترتب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C414-NATIJA414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ترتب** — *tarattaba* — "to follow from".

> ترتب على القرار تغييرات كثيرة.
>
> *tarattaba ʿalā al-qarār taghyīrāt kathīra.*
>
> Many changes followed from the decision.

Present: يترتب, *yatarattab*.

It takes على: ترتب على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترتب** (*tarattaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-t-b*, "to be firm, fixed in place" (Lane). It is Form V, to fall into rank after a thing: to follow from it. رتب, to tidy, is from the same root.
