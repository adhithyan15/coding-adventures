## You'll want to know: ಹೊಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C386-SAMAJAYISHI386-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಣೆ** — *hoṇe* — "blame; responsibility".

> ಈ ನಷ್ಟಕ್ಕೆ ಯಾರು ಹೊಣೆ?
>
> *ī naṣṭakke yāru hoṇe?*
>
> Who is to blame for this loss?

ಹೊಣೆ ಹೊರು, to take the blame.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಣೆ** (*hoṇe*) is an everyday Kannada word whose origin is not settled: neither DEDR nor CDIAL lists it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
