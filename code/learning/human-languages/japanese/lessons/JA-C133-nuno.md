## You'll want to know: ぬの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C133-NUNO]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-NU-01, JA-SCRIPT-NO-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-INU] -->

**ぬの** — *nuno* — "cloth", a piece of woven material.

The new sign is spent at once. **ぬ** with **の** after it: two beats, *nu–no*,
and both signs are ones you write. In **いぬ** the new sign comes last; in
**ぬの** it comes first.
