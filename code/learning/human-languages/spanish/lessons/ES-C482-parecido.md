## You'll want to know: parecido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C482-COMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**parecido** — "similar, alike".

> Tu bolso es parecido al mío.
>
> *Your bag is similar to mine.*

It goes with *a*: *parecido a*.
