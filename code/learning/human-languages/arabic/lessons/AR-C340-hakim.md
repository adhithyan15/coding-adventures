## You'll want to know: حاكم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C340-HUKM340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حاكم** — *ḥākim* — "a governor; a ruler".

> كان الحاكم عادلا مع الناس.
>
> *kāna al-ḥākim ʿādilan maʿa an-nās.*
>
> The ruler was fair to the people.

Plural: حكام, *ḥukkām*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حاكم** (*ḥākim*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-k-m* (Lane), "to judge, to rule". *Ḥākim* is the active participle, "one who rules".
