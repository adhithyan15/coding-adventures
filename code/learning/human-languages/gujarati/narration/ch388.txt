5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રૂપરેખા (rūprekhā) — an outline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for lovely, then the Gujarati for to deteriorate.

You'll want to know: રૂપરેખા.
રૂપરેખા — rūprekhā — "an outline".
જો રૂપરેખા તૈયાર હશે તો કામ ઝડપી થશે. jo rūprekhā taiyār haśe to kām jhaḍpī thaśe. If the outline is ready, the work will go faster.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રૂપરેખા (rūprekhā) is built from Sanskrit rūpa, "form, shape", and rekhā, "a line" (Monier-Williams): the lines of a shape.

You'll want to know: અભિયાન.
અભિયાન — abhiyān — "a public drive; a campaign".
જો આ અભિયાન ચાલશે તો ગામ ચોખ્ખું રહેશે. jo ā abhiyān cālśe to gām cokhkhũ raheśe. If this campaign keeps going, the village will stay clean.

You'll want to know: આરંભવું.
આરંભવું — ārambhvũ — "to commence".
જો બધા સમયસર આવશે તો આપણે કાર્યક્રમ આરંભીશું. jo badhā samaysar āvśe to āpaṇe kāryakram ārambhīśũ. If everyone arrives on time, we will begin the programme.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નવું કામ આરંભ્યું, teṇe navũ kām ārambhyũ.

The word, taken apart.
આરંભવું (ārambhvũ) is built on Sanskrit ārambha, "undertaking, beginning" (Monier-Williams), with the Gujarati verb ending.

Lesson 5 of 5.
આદરવું (ādarvũ) — to undertake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a public drive, then the Gujarati for to commence.

You'll want to know: આદરવું.
આદરવું — ādarvũ — "to undertake".
જો તમે આ કામ આદરશો તો હું પૈસાની મદદ કરીશ. jo tame ā kām ādarśo to hũ paisānī madad karīś. If you take on this work, I will help with money.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મોટું કામ આદર્યું, teṇe moṭũ kām ādaryũ.

The word, taken apart.
આદરવું (ādarvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit ādriyatē, "regards, is careful about"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to work with zeal, to begin" (CDIAL 1161). આદર (ādar), "respect", comes from the same root.
