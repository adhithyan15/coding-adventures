# బాధితుడు (bādhituḍu) — a victim

## You'll want to know: బాధితుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C379-BADHITUDU379-03]; assesses=[] -->

**బాధితుడు** — *bādhituḍu* — "a victim".

> బాధితులకు ప్రభుత్వం సహాయం చేసింది.
>
> *bādhitulaku prabhutvaṁ sahāyaṁ cēsindi.*
>
> The government helped the victims.

Plural: బాధితులు, *bādhitulu*.

You know బాధ, pain: బాధితుడు, one who is made to suffer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బాధితుడు** (*bādhituḍu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bādhita*, "hard-pressed, oppressed" (CDIAL 9205).
