## You'll want to know: あいさつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C306-AISATSU306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**あいさつ** — *aisatsu* — "a greeting".

> **あさ、となりの おばあさんに あいさつしました。** — *asa, tonari no obāsan ni aisatsu shimashita* — "In the morning I said hello to the old lady next door."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あいさつ** (*aisatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ai*, "push open" (Middle Chinese *'eajX*) and *satsu*, "draw close" (*tsat*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
