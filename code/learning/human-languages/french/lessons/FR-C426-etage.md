## You'll want to know: l'étage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C426-RECEPTION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'étage** — "the storey, the floor (of a building)".

> Notre chambre est au troisième étage.
>
> *Our room is on the third floor.*

A masculine noun. *Le rez-de-chaussée* is the ground floor; the *premier étage* is one floor up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*étage* comes from Old French *estage* 'dwelling, level', which is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin word built on *stāre* 'to stand'. EtymDB lists it without a source.
