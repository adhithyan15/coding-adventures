## You'll want to know: der Wasserfall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C455-WASSERFALL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Wasserfall** — "the waterfall".

> Der Wasserfall ist nach dem Regen sehr laut.
>
> *The waterfall is very loud after the rain.*

Plural: *die Wasserfälle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wasserfall* is built from *Wasser* 'water' and *Fall* 'fall', both inherited: *Fall* goes back to Old High German *val* (EtymDB).
