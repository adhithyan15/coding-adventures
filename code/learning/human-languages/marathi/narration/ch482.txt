Marathi, chapter 482: Aches and Pains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
डोकेदुखी (ḍokedukhī) — a headache.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to blow one's nose, then the Marathi for to clear one's throat.

You'll want to know: डोकेदुखी.
डोकेदुखी — ḍokedukhī — "a headache".
उन्हात फिरल्यामुळे मला डोकेदुखी झाली. unhāt phirlyāmuḷe malā ḍokedukhī jhālī. I got a headache from walking about in the sun.
Feminine.
डोके + दुखी, aching. Also, figuratively, a nuisance.

The word, taken apart.
डोकेदुखी (ḍokedukhī) is a Marathi compound of डोके, "head", and दुखी, "aching", from the verb दुखणे (dukhṇe), a tadbhava of Sanskrit duḥkhati (CDIAL 6376).

Lesson 2 of 5.
दुखणे (dukhṇe) — to ache; to hurt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to clear one's throat, then the Marathi for a headache.

You'll want to know: दुखणे.
दुखणे — dukhṇe — "to ache; to hurt".
जास्त वेळ बसल्यामुळे माझी पाठ दुखते. jāsta veḷ baslyāmuḷe mājhī pāṭh dukhte. My back aches from sitting too long.
Past (to him …): त्याला दुखलं, tyālā dukhlaṁ.
The aching part is the subject: माझं डोकं दुखतं. With the dative and no part named: मला खूप दुखलं, it hurt me a lot. दुखणं, an ailment.

The word, taken apart.
दुखणे (dukhṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit duḥkhati, "pains"; CDIAL lists Marathi dukhṇẽ (CDIAL 6376).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कळ येणे (kaḷ yeṇe) — to get a twinge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a headache, then the Marathi for to ache.

You'll want to know: कळ येणे.
कळ येणे — kaḷ yeṇe — "to get a twinge".
जिना चढताना माझ्या गुडघ्यात कळ आली. jinā caḍhtānā mājhyā guḍghyāt kaḷ ālī. I felt a twinge in my knee climbing the stairs.
Past (to him …): त्याला कळ आली, tyālā kaḷ ālī.
कळ, a sharp twinge: पोटात कळ येणे (kaḷ yeṇe), a cramp in the stomach. Dative: मला कळ आली.

The word, taken apart.
कळ येणे (kaḷ yeṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on कळ, "a sharp twinge", whose origin is not settled, with येणे, "to come".

Lesson 4 of 5.
कण्हणे (kaṇhṇe) — to groan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to ache, then the Marathi for to get a twinge.

You'll want to know: कण्हणे.
कण्हणे — kaṇhṇe — "to groan".
तापामुळे तो रात्रभर कण्हत होता. tāpāmuḷe to rātrabhar kaṇhat hotā. He groaned all night with fever.
Past (he …): तो कण्हला, to kaṇhlā.
A low sound of pain.

The word, taken apart.
कण्हणे (kaṇhṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi kanhṇẽ, "to groan", under Sanskrit kvaṇati, "sounds" (CDIAL 3632).

Lesson 5 of 5.
विव्हळणे (vivhaḷṇe) — to moan in pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get a twinge, then the Marathi for to groan.

You'll want to know: विव्हळणे.
विव्हळणे — vivhaḷṇe — "to moan in pain".
पाय मोडल्यामुळे कुत्रा विव्हळत होता. pāy moḍlyāmuḷe kutrā vivhaḷat hotā. The dog was moaning with a broken leg.
Past (he …): तो विव्हळला, to vivhaḷlā.
Louder than कण्हणे (kaṇhṇe).

The word, taken apart.
विव्हळणे (vivhaḷṇe) is usually traced to Sanskrit vihvala, "agitated" (CDIAL 12038), with the verb ending -ṇe; CDIAL gives no Marathi form there.
