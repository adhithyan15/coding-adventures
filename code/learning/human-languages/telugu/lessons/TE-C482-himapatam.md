# హిమపాతం (himapātaṁ) — snowfall

## You'll want to know: హిమపాతం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C482-HIMAPATAM482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**హిమపాతం** — *himapātaṁ* — "snowfall".

> కొండల మీద భారీ హిమపాతం కురిసింది.
>
> *koṇḍala mīda bhārī himapātaṁ kurisindi.*
>
> Heavy snow fell on the mountains.

హిమ, snow, + పాతం, a fall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**హిమపాతం** (*himapātaṁ*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *hima*, "snow, frost", and *pāta*, "a fall" (Monier-Williams).
