## You'll want to know: o telespectador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C285-OUVINTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o telespectador** — "the TV viewer".

> Cada telespectador pode votar por telefone.
>
> *Each viewer can vote by phone.*

Built on *tele-* and *espectador*, ‘onlooker’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*telespectador* joins *tele-*, from Greek *tēle* "far off", and *espectador*, from Latin *spectātor* "an onlooker", from *spectāre* "to watch" (Lewis & Short).
