Lesson 1 of 5.
der Grund — a reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for desire, then the German for an opinion.

You'll want to know: der Grund.
der Grund — "a reason".

The word, taken apart.
Grund from Old High German grunt from Proto-Germanic grunduz — inherited, the same word as English ground. (EtymDB 2.1) A reason is the ground something stands on.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Lösung — a solution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an opinion, then the German for a reason.

You'll want to know: die Lösung.
die Lösung — "a solution".

The word, taken apart.
Lösung is the verb "to loosen, to release", Old High German lōsen from Proto-Germanic lausijaną, built on lausaz, "loose" (EtymDB 2.1), + -ung. A solution unties a knot.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Möglichkeit — a possibility.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a solution, then the German for experience.

You'll want to know: die Möglichkeit.
die Möglichkeit — "a possibility".

The word, taken apart.
Möglichkeit is the word for "possible" + -keit, the ending that makes a noun. "Possible" is built on mögen, "to be able", which EtymDB 2.1 traces to Proto-Germanic maganą, the verb behind English may.
