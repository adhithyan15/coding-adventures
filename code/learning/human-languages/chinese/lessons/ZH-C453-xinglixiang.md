## You'll want to know: xínglixiāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C453-BAGS453-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xínglixiāng* — "luggage case; a travel case".

> *wǒ de xínglixiāng fàng zài fángjiān lǐ* — My suitcase is in the room.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xínglixiāng* is built from *xíngli* 'luggage' and *xiāng* 'a box', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeng*, *liX* and *sjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses *lǐ* as a fruit tree and gives *xíngli* itself under it, and glosses *xiāng* as a box or a basket.
