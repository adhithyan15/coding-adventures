## You'll want to know: ટકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C187-PRICE187-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ટકો** — *ṭako* — "per cent".

> **પગાર પાંચ ટકા વધે છે.** — *pagār pā̃c ṭakā vadhe che* — "The salary goes up by five per cent."

After a number it takes the plural **ટકા**, *ṭakā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટકો** (*ṭako*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit *ṭaṅka*, "a weight; a stamped coin", with the Gujarati sense "two-paisā coin"; the sense "per cent" is not in CDIAL (CDIAL 5426).
