## You'll want to know: vēnālis, vēnāle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C164-LEG164-04]; assesses=[] -->

**vēnālis, vēnāle** — "for sale".

[YOU READ: the sign]

The sign says **Domus vēnālis.** — "House for sale." It comes from **vēnum**,
"sale", like **vendō**, "to sell".
