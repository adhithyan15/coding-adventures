## You'll want to know: la leggenda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C324-MITO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la leggenda** — "the legend".

> Secondo la leggenda, la città è nata in una notte.
>
> *According to the legend, the city was born in a single night.*

*Una leggenda vivente* — a living legend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*leggenda* comes from Medieval Latin *legenda* "things to be read", from *legere* "to read" (Lewis & Short): first, the lives of saints read aloud.
