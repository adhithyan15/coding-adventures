## You'll want to know: વરદાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C332-DANTKATHA332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વરદાન** — *vardān* — "a boon".

> ભગવાને રાજાને વરદાન આપ્યું.
>
> *bhagvāne rājāne vardān āpyũ.*
>
> The god granted the king a boon.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વરદાન** (*vardān*) is Sanskrit *varadāna*, "the granting of a boon" (Monier-Williams), built from *vara*, a wish chosen, and *dāna*, a giving. A god **વરદાન આપે છે**.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
