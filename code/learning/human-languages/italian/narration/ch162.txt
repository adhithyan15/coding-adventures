You'll want to know: il sito.

The word, taken apart.
sito from Latin situs, "a place, a position" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) The web sense follows English site.

Lesson 2 of 5.
lo schermo — a screen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for elections, then the Italian for a website.

You'll want to know: lo schermo.
lo schermo — "a screen".

The word, taken apart.
schermo from Lombardic skirm, "a shield" — borrowed from the Germanic language of the Lombards: a screen shields. EtymDB 2.1 derives it from schermire, "to fence", but the verb comes from the noun, not the other way round.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la tastiera — a keyboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a website, then the Italian for a screen.

You'll want to know: la tastiera.
la tastiera — "a keyboard".

The word, taken apart.
tastiera — built from tasto, "a key", with -iera: a row of keys. tasto comes from tastare, "to touch" from Vulgar Latin tastare. (EtymDB 2.1).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a keyboard, then the Italian for a battery.

You'll want to know: il caricabatterie.
il caricabatterie — "a charger".

The word, taken apart.
caricabatterie is "charges batteries": carica, "it loads, it charges", plus batterie, "batteries". caricare from Late Latin carricare. (EtymDB 2.1).
