## You'll want to know: هدنة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C341-FARIS341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**هدنة** — *hudna* — "a truce".

> وقع الطرفان هدنة لمدة شهر.
>
> *waqqaʿa aṭ-ṭarafān hudna li-muddat shahr.*
>
> The two sides signed a truce for a month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هدنة** (*hudna*) is a native Arabic word built on the root *h-d-n*, recorded in *hudna ʿalā dakhan*, an illusory truce (Lane). The root is usually explained as "to be still, to be quiet": a time when the fighting is still.
