## You'll want to know: 快速
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C422-NORTH422-05]; assesses=[] -->

**快速** — *kuàisù* — "rapid; high-speed".

> **我们坐快速公交去机场** *wǒmen zuò kuàisù gōngjiāo qù jīchǎng* — We take the rapid bus to the airport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**快速** (*kuàisù*) is built from 快 'fast' and 速 'swift', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khwaejH* and *suwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
