## You'll want to know: సంధ్య
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C456-SHIKARU456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సంధ్య** — *sandhya* — "dusk; twilight".

> సంధ్య వేళ ఆకాశం ఎర్రగా ఉంది.
>
> *sandhya vēḷa ākāśaṁ erragā undi.*
>
> At dusk the sky is red.

సంధ్య వేళ, at dusk. The word also names the meeting of day and night at dawn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంధ్య** (*sandhya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sandhyā*, "a joining, the twilight" where day and night meet (Monier-Williams).
