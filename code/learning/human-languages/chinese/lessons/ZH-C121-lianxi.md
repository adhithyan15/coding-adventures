## You'll want to know: liànxí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C121-LEARN121-02]; assesses=[] -->

*liànxí* — "to practise".

> *wǒ měitiān liànxí shuō hànyǔ* — I practise speaking Chinese every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liànxí* is built from liàn and xí, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lenH* and *zip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as white boiled silk and the second 'to learn', explaining it as flying again and again.

## What you've built

One more word for everyday actions.
