## You'll want to know: превращаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C320-VOLSHEBNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**превращаться** (*prevrashchátsya*) — "to turn into, to become".

> Ночью ведьма превращалась в чёрную кошку.
>
> *Nóchyu védma prevrashchálas v chyórnuyu kóshku.*
>
> At night the witch turned into a black cat.

Imperfective; perfective *превратиться* (*prevratítsya*), *превращусь*. It takes *в* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*превращаться* is built on *пре-* and *вращать* "to turn", which EtymDB marks as borrowed from Church Slavonic *vraščati*, from Proto-Slavic *vorťati*; the native cousin is *воротить*, Proto-Slavic *vortiti* (EtymDB).
