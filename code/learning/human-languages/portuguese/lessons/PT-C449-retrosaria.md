## You'll want to know: a retrosaria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C449-LOJAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a retrosaria** — "the haberdashery".

> Na retrosaria encontrei botões para o casaco.
>
> *At the haberdashery I found buttons for my coat.*

Thread, buttons, zips and ribbons.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*retrosaria* is built on *retrós* 'silk thread' with *-aria*; the origin of *retrós* is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
