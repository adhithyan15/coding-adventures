## You'll want to know: よそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C349-YOSOU349-01]; assesses=[] -->

**よそう** — *yosō* — "an expectation; a forecast".

> **けっかが よそうと ちがったら、もう いちど しらべます。** — *kekka ga yosō to chigattara, mō ichido shirabemasu* — "If the result is not what we expected, we will investigate again."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よそう** (*yosō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yo*, "beforehand" (Middle Chinese *yoH*) and *sō*, "idea" (*sjangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
