## You'll want to know: けす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C158-LIGHTS158-02]; assesses=[] -->

**けす** — *kesu* — "to switch off; to put out".

> **あかりを けします。** — *akari o keshimasu* — "I switch off the lamp."

Polite **けします**, *keshimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けす** (*kesu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
