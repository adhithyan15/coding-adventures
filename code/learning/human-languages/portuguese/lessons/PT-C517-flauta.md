## You'll want to know: a flauta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C517-SOPROS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a flauta** — "flute; recorder".

> Na escola, todos aprendem a tocar flauta.
>
> *At school, everyone learns to play the recorder.*

*Flauta de bisel* — recorder; *flauta transversal* — flute.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flauta* is usually traced to Old Occitan *flaut*, a word usually taken to imitate the sound of blowing; the origin is not settled.
