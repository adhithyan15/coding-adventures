## You'll want to know: دیو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C325-DEV325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دیو** — *dev* — "a giant in tales; a demon".

> پہاڑ پر ایک دیو رہتا تھا۔
>
> *pahāṛ par ek dev rahtā thā.*
>
> A giant lived on the mountain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *dēv*, "a demon", the word inside **دیوانہ**. In Urdu tales the **دیو** is a huge, cruel giant.
