## You'll want to know: jiānqiáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C468-GOOD468-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiānqiáng* — "strong; tough (in spirit)".

> *tā bìng le liǎng nián kěshì tā hěn jiānqiáng* — She has been ill for two years, but she's very strong.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiānqiáng* is built from *jiān* 'firm' and *qiáng* 'strong', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ken* and *gjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'firm' and the second 'healthy' and 'strong'.
