Lesson 1 of 5.
doppiare — to dub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for spoiler, then the Italian for remote control.

You'll want to know: doppiare.
doppiare — "to dub".
Quasi tutti i film stranieri sono doppiati. Almost all foreign films are dubbed.
Also to lap another runner, or to sail round a cape.

The word, taken apart.
doppiare is inherited from Late Latin dūplāre "to double" (EtymDB), from duplus "double".

doppiaggio is built on doppiare "to dub", from Late Latin dūplāre "to double" (EtymDB), with -aggio for the work.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: i sottotitoli.
i sottotitoli — "subtitles".
Guardo i film in inglese con i sottotitoli. I watch films in English with subtitles.
The singular il sottotitolo is also a subheading.

The word, taken apart.
sottotitoli is built from sotto "under", from Latin subtus, and titolo "title", from Latin titulus (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la colonna sonora — soundtrack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for dubbing, then the Italian for subtitles.

You'll want to know: la colonna sonora.
la colonna sonora — "soundtrack".
La colonna sonora del film è bellissima. The film's soundtrack is beautiful.
Literally "the sound strip": on old film, the strip that carried the sound.

The word, taken apart.
colonna sonora is built from colonna, inherited from Latin columna "a pillar, a post" (EtymDB), and sonoro "of sound", from Latin sonōrus (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
il trailer — trailer (of a film).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for subtitles, then the Italian for soundtrack.

You'll want to know: il trailer.
il trailer — "trailer (of a film)".
Il trailer mi è piaciuto, andiamo a vederlo. I liked the trailer, let us go and see it.
From English; said as in English.

The word, taken apart.
trailer was borrowed from English trailer, built on trail "to drag behind", usually traced to Latin trāgula "a drag-net" (Lewis & Short).
