## You'll want to know: なべ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C174-KITCH174-01]; assesses=[] -->

**なべ** — *nabe* — "pot; saucepan, hot pot".

> **ふゆは なべを よく たべます。** — *fuyu wa nabe o yoku tabemasu* — "In winter we often eat hot pot."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なべ** (*nabe*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi; its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
