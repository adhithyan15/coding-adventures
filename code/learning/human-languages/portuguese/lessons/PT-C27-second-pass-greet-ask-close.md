# Open it, ask, close it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-C04-PRACTICE-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] From memory, and without the page: the final goodbye, and one of the
three that are not final.

## You'll want to know: one goodbye ends it, three do not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-C04-PRACTICE-02, PT-NOTICE-C04-PRACTICE-03] -->

| | |
|---|---|
| **adeus** | the final one — "to God", the *adiós · adieu · addio* family |
| **até logo** | see you later |
| **até amanhã** | see you tomorrow |
| **até breve** | see you soon |

Three of the four open with **até**, "until", which Portuguese took from Arabic
*ḥattā* and Spanish took as *hasta*. What follows **até** is when you expect to
be back, so choosing between them is choosing a distance in time. **adeus** names
no return at all, which is why it is the heavy one.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-SOUND-ATE-BREVE-02] -->

- **até breve** = *ah-TEH **BREH**-vee*. The *e* of *breve* is open, and the
  final *-e* softens toward *ee* rather than disappearing.

## What you've built — a gentler way to ask
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-CORACAO-02, PT-NOTICE-C26-CORPO-04] -->

**o coração** gives you somewhere warmer to go than *Tudo bem?*:

> **Como vai o seu coração?** — half-figurative, and kinder than a flat *how
> are you*.

It is masculine, and its **-ão** got there by a route worth remembering: *cor*
**grew** a suffix, where *pão* and *irmão* reached the same ending by **losing**
sounds. Kept, eroded, folded, grown — four roots, four different fates, one
ending.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-C04-PRACTICE-02, PT-NOTICE-C04-PRACTICE-03, PT-SOUND-ATE-BREVE-02, PT-LEX-CORACAO-02, PT-NOTICE-C26-CORPO-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which goodbye names no return? (**Adeus**.) What does *até* mean, and where is it from? (**Until**, from Arabic *ḥattā*.) How does *até breve* end? (**Softened toward *ee***.) And how did *coração* get its **-ão**? (**A suffix was added** — it grew, it did not erode.)
