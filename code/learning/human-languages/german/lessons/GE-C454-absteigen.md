## You'll want to know: absteigen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C454-WANDERUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**absteigen** — "to climb down; to get off (a bike); to go down (a league)".

> Am Nachmittag steigen wir langsam ins Tal ab.
>
> *In the afternoon we slowly climb down into the valley.*

Separable; Perfekt with *sein*: *wir sind abgestiegen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*absteigen* is inherited from Middle High German *abestīgen* (EtymDB), built from *ab* 'down' and *steigen* 'to climb'; Dutch *afstijgen* is a cousin.
