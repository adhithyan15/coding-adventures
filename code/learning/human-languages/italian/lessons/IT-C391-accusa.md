## You'll want to know: l'accusa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C391-ACCUSA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'accusa** — "the accusation, the charge; the prosecution".

> L'accusa è falsa: quella sera ero a casa.
>
> *The accusation is false: that evening I was at home.*

In court, *l'accusa* is the prosecution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accusa* is built on *accusare* "to accuse", inherited from Latin *accūsāre* (EtymDB), on *causa* "a case, a lawsuit".
