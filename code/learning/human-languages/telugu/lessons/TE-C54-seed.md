## You'll want to know: విత్తనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C54-TREE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**విత్తనం** (*vittanaṁ*) — "a seed".

విత్తనం is the seed, and it is built on విత్తు, "to sow". The word for the thing comes out of the word for the act, which is the ordinary direction of travel: people had been sowing for a long time before anybody needed a name for what they held.

Sanskrit's *bīja* was borrowed in as *bījaṁ*, and it does the abstract work — the seed of an idea, the germ of a quarrel. విత్తనం stays in the sack.

The whole chapter is inside this word. A విత్తనం becomes a చెట్టు with a వేరు below and a కొమ్మ above.
