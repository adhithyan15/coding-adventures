## You'll want to know: నిల్వ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C488-TOKU488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నిల్వ** — *nilva* — "a stock; a store".

> దుకాణంలో పంచదార నిల్వ అయిపోయింది.
>
> *dukāṇaṁlō pañcadāra nilva ayipōyindi.*
>
> The shop has run out of its stock of sugar.

నిల్వ చేయు, to store. నిల్వ పచ్చడి is a pickle made to keep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిల్వ** (*nilva*) is native Dravidian, of the family of నిలుచు, "to stand, to remain, to be preserved" (DEDR 3675): what remains is the stock.
