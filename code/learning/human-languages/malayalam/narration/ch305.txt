Malayalam, chapter 305: To deposit, To withdraw, To join, To meet, To visit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
നിക്ഷേപിക്കുക (nikṣēpikkuka) — to deposit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to swell, then the Malayalam for to itch.

You'll want to know: നിക്ഷേപിക്കുക.
നിക്ഷേപിക്കുക — nikṣēpikkuka — "to deposit".

The word, taken apart.
നിക്ഷേപിക്കുക (nikṣēpikkuka) is built on Sanskrit nikṣēpa, "a deposit, a thing laid down", with the ending that makes a Malayalam verb.
