## You'll want to know: le lycéen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C375-LYCEE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le lycéen** — "the secondary-school pupil".

> Les lycéens ont des cours jusqu'à six heures.
>
> *The pupils have lessons until six o'clock.*

Feminine *lycéenne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lycéen* is built from *lycée* and *-en*. *lycée* comes from Latin *lycēum*, from Greek *Lúkeion* (EtymDB).
