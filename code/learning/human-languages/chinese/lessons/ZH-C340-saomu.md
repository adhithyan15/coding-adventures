## You'll want to know: sǎomù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C340-CUSTOM340-05]; assesses=[] -->

*sǎomù* — "to tend the family graves".

> *měinián chūntiān wǒmen dōu qù sǎomù* — Every spring we go to tend the family graves.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sǎomù* is built from 扫 'to sweep' and *mù* 'a grave', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sawX* and *muH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to sweep clean' and the second 'a grave mound'.
