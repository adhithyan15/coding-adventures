## You'll want to know: دید و بازدید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C340-EYDI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دید و بازدید** (*did o bâzdid*) — "a round of visits (at the New Year)".

> در روزهای عید دید و بازدید داریم.
>
> *Dar ruz-hâ-ye 'eyd did o bâzdid dârim.*
>
> During the New Year holidays we go round visiting family.

Literally "seeing and seeing back": you visit, then they return the visit. The elders are visited first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*did* "a seeing" and *bâzdid* "a return visit" (*bâz* "back" + *did*) are both built on the past stem of the native verb *didan* "to see".
