## You'll want to know: lǐwù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C75-PARTY75-04]; assesses=[] -->

*lǐwù* — "present; gift".

> *wǒ mǎi le yí ge lǐwù* — I've bought a present.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǐwù* is built from lǐ 'rite, courtesy' and wù 'thing', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lejX* and *mjut* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a thing given out of courtesy.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
