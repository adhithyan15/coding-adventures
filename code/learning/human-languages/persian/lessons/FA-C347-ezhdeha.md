## You'll want to know: اژدها
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C347-AFSANE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اژدها** (*ezhdehâ*) — "a dragon".

> در قصه یک اژدها کنار رودخانه زندگی میکرد.
>
> *Dar qesse yek ezhdehâ kenâr-e rudkhâne zendegi mikard.*
>
> In the tale a dragon lived by the river.

Written with ژ; also said *azhdahâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ezhdehâ* is inherited from Middle Persian *azdahāg* (EtymDB), which continues the Avestan dragon name *aži dahāka*.
