Gujarati, chapter 50: How It Feels.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ખુશી (khuśī) — happiness, joy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for hunger, then the Gujarati for thirst.

You'll want to know: ખુશી.
ખુશી (khuśī) — "happiness, joy".

What you've built.
One more word for how it feels.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: khuśī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: khuśī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say khuśī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for hunger, then the Gujarati for thirst, then say khuśī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ખુશી (khuśī) mean? ("Happiness, joy".) Say it once more.
