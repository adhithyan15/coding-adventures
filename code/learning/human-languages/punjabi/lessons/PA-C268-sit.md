## You'll want to know: ਸੀਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C268-STAND268-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੀਟ** — *sīṭ* — "a seat".

> ਕੀ ਬੱਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀਟ ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਹੈ?
>
> *kī bass vich sīṭ khālī hai?*
>
> Is there a free seat on the bus?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੀਟ** (*sīṭ*) is borrowed from English *seat*.
