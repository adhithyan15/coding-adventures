## You'll want to know: উন্নতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C377-UNNOTI377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উন্নতি** — *unnôti* — "improvement; progress".

> রোজ পড়লে তোমার বাংলার উন্নতি হবে।
>
> *roj pôṛle tomār bānglār unnôti hôbe.*
>
> If you read every day, your Bengali will improve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উন্নতি** (*unnôti*) is Sanskrit *unnati*, a rising up (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for any getting better: a skill, a town, a patient.
