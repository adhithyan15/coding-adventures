## You'll want to know: بطل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C303-USTURA303-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بطل** — *baṭal* — "a hero; a champion".

> كان جدي بطلا في قريته.
>
> *kāna jaddī baṭalan fī qaryatihi.*
>
> My grandfather was a hero in his village.

Plural: أبطال, *abṭāl*.

In sport بطل is a champion, and the hero of a film or novel is its بطل too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بطل** (*baṭal*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-ṭ-l* (Lane), "to come to nothing". Lane explains *baṭal*, a brave man, as one whose wound goes for nothing, so that it does not hold him back.
