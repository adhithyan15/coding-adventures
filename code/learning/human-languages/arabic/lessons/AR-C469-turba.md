## You'll want to know: تربة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C469-TURBA469-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تربة** — *turba* — "soil; earth".

> التربة هنا جيدة للقمح.
>
> *at-turba hunā jayyida li-l-qamḥ.*
>
> The soil here is good for wheat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تربة** (*turba*) is built on the native Arabic root *t-r-b*, "earth, dust" (Lane). It shares its root with *turāb*, "dust, soil".
