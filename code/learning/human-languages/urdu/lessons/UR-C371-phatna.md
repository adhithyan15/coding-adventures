## You'll want to know: پھٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C371-BUHRAN371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پھٹنا** — *phaṭnā* — "to split open; to burst".

> اگر پائپ پھٹا تو گھر میں پانی آئے گا۔
>
> *agar pāip phaṭā to ghar meṅ pānī āe gā.*
>
> If the pipe bursts, water will get into the house.

Past (it …): وہ پائپ پھٹ گیا, *voh pāip phaṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھٹنا** (*phaṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *phāṭnā*, "to be split", under Sanskrit *sphāṭayati*, "causes to split" (CDIAL 13825).
