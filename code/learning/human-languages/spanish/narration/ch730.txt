Spanish, chapter 730: Encanto y belleza.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
encantador — charming, delightful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for nosy, then the Spanish for curiosity.

You'll want to know: encantador.
encantador — "charming, delightful".
Tu madre es encantadora. Your mother is charming.
Feminine encantadora. Un encantador de serpientes is a snake charmer.

The word, taken apart.
encantador is built on encantar, which comes from Latin incantāre "to chant a spell over" (EtymDB), from cantāre "to sing".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la belleza — beauty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for charm, then the Spanish for attractive.

You'll want to know: la belleza.
la belleza — "beauty".
Me impresionó la belleza del paisaje. The beauty of the landscape struck me.
Un salón de belleza — a beauty salon.

The word, taken apart.
belleza is built on bello "beautiful" with the ending -eza (EtymDB); bello comes from Latin bellus "pretty, handsome". Some dictionaries think the form came in through Catalan bellesa or Occitan.

elegancia comes from Latin ēlegantia "refinement, elegance" (EtymDB), linked with ēligere "to pick out": elegance is good choosing.
