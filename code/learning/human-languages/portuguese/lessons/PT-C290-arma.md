## You'll want to know: a arma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C290-ARMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a arma** — "the weapon, the gun".

> A polícia encontrou a arma no rio.
>
> *The police found the weapon in the river.*

*Uma arma de fogo* — a firearm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arma* is inherited from Latin *arma* "tools, arms" (EtymDB), a plural that Portuguese came to treat as a singular.
