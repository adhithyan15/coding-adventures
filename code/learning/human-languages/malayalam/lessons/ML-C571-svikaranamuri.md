## You'll want to know: സ്വീകരണമുറി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C571-ATTAKURRAM571-03]; assesses=[] -->

**സ്വീകരണമുറി** — *svīkaraṇamuṟi* — "a living room".

> അതിഥികൾ സ്വീകരണമുറിയിൽ ഇരുന്നു.
>
> *atithikaḷ svīkaraṇamuṟiyil irunnu.*
>
> The guests sat in the living room.

സ്വീകരണം a welcome + മുറി a room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സ്വീകരണമുറി** (*svīkaraṇamuṟi*) is a Malayalam compound built from സ്വീകരണം, from Sanskrit *svīkaraṇa*, "making one's own, accepting" (Monier-Williams), and മുറി, "a room", which is native (DEDR 5008).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
