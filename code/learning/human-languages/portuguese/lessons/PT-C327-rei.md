## You'll want to know: o rei
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C327-REINO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o rei** — "the king".

> O rei vivia num castelo grande.
>
> *The king lived in a big castle.*

*Viver como um rei* — to live like a king.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rei* is inherited from Latin *rēx, rēgem* "a king" (EtymDB).
