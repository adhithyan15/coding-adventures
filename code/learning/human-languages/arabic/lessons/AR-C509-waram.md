## You'll want to know: ورم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C509-ADWA509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ورم** — *waram* — "a swelling; a tumour".

> ظهر ورم صغير على ركبتي.
>
> *ẓahara waram ṣaghīr ʿalā rukbatī.*
>
> A small swelling appeared on my knee.

Plural: أورام, *awrām*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ورم** (*waram*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-r-m*, "to swell" (Lane). Tawarrama, to swell up, is from the same root.
