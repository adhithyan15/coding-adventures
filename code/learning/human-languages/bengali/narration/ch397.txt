Bengali, chapter 397: Ghats and Ferries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘাট (ghāṭ) — a landing place; riverside steps; a ghat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the hood of a country boat, then the Bengali for to row.

You'll want to know: ঘাট.
ঘাট — ghāṭ — "a landing place; riverside steps; a ghat".
সকালে ঘাটে অনেক লোক স্নান করে। sôkāle ghāṭe ônek lok snān kôre. In the morning many people bathe at the ghat.
The steps down to a river or pond, and the place where boats come in.

The word, taken apart.
ঘাট (ghāṭ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali ghāṭ, "a mountain pass, a landing place", under Sanskrit ghaṭṭa, "a landing place, a quay" (CDIAL 4414); English ghat is the same word, borrowed.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to row, then the Bengali for a landing place.

You'll want to know: লঞ্চ.
লঞ্চ — lônch — "a launch; a motor ferry".
লঞ্চটা ভোরে ঘাট ছাড়ল। lônchṭā bhore ghāṭ chhāṛlô. The launch left the ghat at dawn.
The motor boat that carries passengers on the big rivers.

The word, taken apart.
লঞ্চ (lônch) is borrowed from English launch, a motor boat; ঞ্চ is the n-ch joined letter.

Lesson 3 of 5.
খেয়া (kheyā) — a ferry (across a river).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a landing place, then the Bengali for a launch.

You'll want to know: খেয়া.
খেয়া — kheyā — "a ferry (across a river)".
আমরা খেয়ায় নদী পার হলাম। āmrā kheyāy nôdī pār hôlām. We crossed the river by ferry.
A খেয়া (kheyā) is a short crossing back and forth; a লঞ্চ (lônch) makes longer runs.

The word, taken apart.
খেয়া (kheyā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali kheyā, "a ferry", under Sanskrit kṣēpa, "a sending, a throw" (CDIAL 3738); the step from sending to the boat that sends you across is the Bengali side's own.

Lesson 4 of 5.
পাড় (pāṛ) — a bank (of a pond or river); a border (of a sari).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a launch, then the Bengali for a ferry.

You'll want to know: পাড়.
পাড় — pāṛ — "a bank (of a pond or river); a border (of a sari)".
পুকুরের পাড়ে একটা আমগাছ আছে। pukurer pāṛe ekṭā āmgāchh āchhe. There is a mango tree on the bank of the pond.
Also the coloured border of a sari: লাল পাড়ের শাড়ি, a sari with a red border.

The word, taken apart.
পাড় (pāṛ) probably belongs with the reconstructed pāḍi, under which CDIAL lists Bengali pāṛi, "an edge, a border" (CDIAL 8041): a bank is the edge of the water, a sari's পাড় the edge of the cloth.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ভেড়ানো (bheṛāno) — to bring (a boat) alongside; to moor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a ferry, then the Bengali for a bank (of a pond or river).

You'll want to know: ভেড়ানো.
ভেড়ানো — bheṛāno — "to bring (a boat) alongside; to moor".
মাঝি নৌকাটা ঘাটে ভেড়াল। mājhi noukāṭā ghāṭe bheṛālô. The boatman brought the boat in to the ghat.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নৌকা ঘাটে ভেড়ালেন, tini noukā ghāṭe bheṛālen.
Not ভেড়া, a sheep: the same first letters, a different word.

The word, taken apart.
ভেড়ানো (bheṛāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali bhiṛāna, "to bring alongside", under the reconstructed bhiṭ, "meet, throng" (CDIAL 9490), the same root as ভিড়, a crowd.
