## You'll want to know: सिरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C474-SIRA474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सिरा** — *sirā* — "a vein".

> रक्तं सिरासु वहति।
>
> *raktaṃ sirāsu vahati.*
>
> Blood flows in the veins.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिरा** (*sirā*) comes from the root √sṛ, "to flow": MW's first sense is "a stream", then "any tubular vessel of the body, a vein, an artery" (MW).
