Italian, chapter 71: Lion, Monkey, Bee, Ant, Elephant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il leone — a lion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a sheep, then the Italian for a goat.

You'll want to know: il leone.
il leone — "a lion".

What you've built.
