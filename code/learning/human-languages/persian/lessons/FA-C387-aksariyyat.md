## You'll want to know: اکثریت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C387-MOHAJERAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اکثریت** (*aksariyat*) — "a majority".

> اکثریت مردم با این طرح موافق هستند.
>
> *aksariyat-e mardom bâ in tarh movâfeq hastand.*
>
> The majority of people agree with this plan.

The opposite is *aqalliyat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اکثریت** is usually traced to Arabic *akthariyya* "majority", built on *akthar* "more, most"; Persian says the Arabic *th* as *s*.
