Italian, chapter 325: Nel regno lontano.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il principe — the prince.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the ending, then the Italian for the moral.

You'll want to know: il principe.
il principe — "the prince".
Il principe è arrivato a cavallo. The prince arrived on horseback.
Plural i principi.

The word, taken apart.
principe is inherited from Latin prīnceps "the first, the chief" (EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
principessa is principe with -essa, from Late Latin -issa; the root is Latin prīnceps "chief" (EtymDB).
