## You'll want to know: wān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C475-BODY475-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wān* — "to bend".

> *wǒ de gēbo bù néng wān* — I can't bend my arm.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wān* is inherited from Middle Chinese *'waen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), explained after an older book as drawing a bow with the arrow set: the bow bends.
