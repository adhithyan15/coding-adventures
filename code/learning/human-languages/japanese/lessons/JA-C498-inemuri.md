## You'll want to know: いねむり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C498-REST498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いねむり** — *inemuri* — "nodding off; dozing (while sitting)".

> **でんしゃで いねむりを しました。** — *densha de inemuri o shimashita* — "I dozed off on the train."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いねむり** (*inemuri*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *i*, "being, sitting" and *nemuri*, "sleep" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
