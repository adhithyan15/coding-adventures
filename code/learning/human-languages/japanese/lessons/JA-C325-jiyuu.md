## You'll want to know: じゆう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C325-SHITAGAU325-05]; assesses=[] -->

**じゆう** — *jiyū* — "freedom; free".

> **この がっこうは ふくが じゆうです。** — *kono gakkō wa fuku ga jiyū desu* — "At this school, what you wear is up to you."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゆう** (*jiyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "oneself" (Middle Chinese *dzijH*) and *yū*, "a reason" (*yuw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
