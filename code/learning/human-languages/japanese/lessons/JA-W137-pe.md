# ぺ — へ, with the circle

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-PERAPERA, JA-LEX-C137-IPPIKI, JA-LEX-C130-QUAL130-01] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Three recalls, then the sign.

- [YOU RECALL: say *fluent*, of someone who speaks with ease — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *one small animal*, one cat — **R2**, five lessons back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *pure white* — **R4**, eighty lessons back]

## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-PE-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-HE-01, JA-SCRIPT-HANDAKUTEN-01] -->

> **へ** *he* + **゜** → **ぺ** *pe*

Write **へ** in full: one stroke, up to the low peak and down to the right.
Then add the small circle at the upper right, above the long way down. Keep
it small and open.

The circle makes a *p*: *he* becomes *pe*. With it you write every sign of
the *p* row: **ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ**.

With **ら** after it, twice over, you can now write the word from the last
lesson: **ぺらぺら**, *perapera*, fluent.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-PE-01, JA-SCRIPT-HE-01, JA-SCRIPT-HANDAKUTEN-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-PERAPERA] -->

1. Write **へ**, then **べ**, then **ぺ**, and say *he*, *be*, *pe*.
2. Write **ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ** in order, and say each one.
3. Write **ぺらぺら** from memory and say *perapera*.
