Hindi, chapter 500: A Wedding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दूल्हा (dūlhā) — a bridegroom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the prime of youth, then the Hindi for to whimper.

You'll want to know: दूल्हा.
दूल्हा — dūlhā — "a bridegroom".
दूल्हा घोड़े पर बैठकर आया। dūlhā ghoṛe par baiṭhkar āyā. The bridegroom arrived on horseback.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
दूल्हा (dūlhā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi dūlhā, "bridegroom", under Sanskrit durlabha, "hard to get, precious" (CDIAL 6446): the dear one. The bride is dulhan, the feminine of the same word, listed in the same entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मेहंदी (mehndī) — henna.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to whimper, then the Hindi for a bridegroom.

You'll want to know: मेहंदी.
मेहंदी — mehndī — "henna".
शादी के दिन मीरा के हाथों में मेहंदी लगी थी। śādī ke din Mīrā ke hāthoṁ meṁ mehndī lagī thī. On her wedding day Mira's hands were patterned with henna.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
मेहंदी (mehndī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi mẽhdī, the henna plant, under Sanskrit mēndhī (CDIAL 10328). Its leaves are ground to a paste that leaves red-brown patterns on the hands, above all before a wedding.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सगाई (sagāī) — an engagement (to marry).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a bridegroom, then the Hindi for henna.

You'll want to know: सगाई.
सगाई — sagāī — "an engagement (to marry)".
पिछले साल मीरा की सगाई हुई। pichle sāl Mīrā kī sagāī huī. Mira got engaged last year.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सगाई (sagāī) is probably the noun of sagā, "of full blood relationship", which CDIAL lists under svagya-, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13896b): the promise that makes two families kin. CDIAL does not give the "engagement" sense itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.
विदाई (vidāī) — a farewell; the bride's leave-taking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an engagement (to marry), then the Hindi for the groom's wedding procession.

You'll want to know: विदाई.
विदाई — vidāī — "a farewell; the bride's leave-taking".
विदाई के समय मीरा और उसकी माँ दोनों रोईं। vidāī ke samay Mīrā aur uskī māṁ donõ roīṃ. At the bride's farewell, Mira and her mother both wept.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
विदाई (vidāī) is vidā, "farewell", with the ending -ī; vidā is usually traced to Arabic vidāʿ, "farewell", which reached Hindi through Persian. At a wedding it is the moment the bride leaves her parents' home.
