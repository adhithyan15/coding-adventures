## You'll want to know: पल्लवः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C446-SHIKHARA446-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पल्लवः** — *pallavaḥ* — "a young shoot".

> वसन्ते वृक्षेषु नवाः पल्लवाः सन्ति।
>
> *vasante vṛkṣeṣu navāḥ pallavāḥ santi.*
>
> In spring there are new shoots on the trees.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पल्लवः** (*pallavaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a sprout, a shoot, a twig; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
