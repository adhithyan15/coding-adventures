## You'll want to know: ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಕಿಚ್ಚು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C499-MANASTHITI499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಕಿಚ್ಚು** — *hoṭṭekiccu* — "envy".

> ಸ್ನೇಹಿತನ ಹೊಸ ಕಾರು ನೋಡಿ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಕಿಚ್ಚು ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *snēhitana hosa kāru nōḍi avanige hoṭṭekiccu āyitu.*
>
> Seeing his friend's new car, he felt envious.

Literally belly-fire: ಹೊಟ್ಟೆ, belly + ಕಿಚ್ಚು, fire. Plainer and more everyday than ಅಸೂಯೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಕಿಚ್ಚು** (*hoṭṭekiccu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *hoṭṭe*, "belly" (DEDR 4494), and *kiccu*, "fire" (DEDR 1514). Envy burns in the stomach.
