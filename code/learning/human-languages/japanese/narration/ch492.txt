Japanese, chapter 492: Simmer, Soak and Steam.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
にえる (nieru) — to be cooked through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for tap water, then the Japanese for to come to the boil.

You'll want to know: にえる.
にえる — nieru — "to be cooked through".
だいこんが よく にえました。 — daikon ga yoku niemashita — "The radish is well cooked."
Polite にえます, niemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): だいこんが にえました。, daikon ga niemashita.

The word, taken apart.
にえる (nieru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
