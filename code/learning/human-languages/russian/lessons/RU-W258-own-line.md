# Your own line — the first writing with no model

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-YA, RU-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-FIRST-PERSON-U] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Everything you have written so far had something to reach for — a
letter to trace, a word to copy, a word you heard.

[PAUSE 1s] This does not. You choose the words, from a supply that is closed:
only what this book has taught you.

## Writing — controlled composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-YA, RU-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-FIRST-PERSON-U, RU-SCRIPT-L-01, RU-SCRIPT-M-01, RU-SCRIPT-T-01, RU-SCRIPT-YA-NOT-R, RU-PUNCT-TOCHKA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **one line** about yourself, by hand, on paper. Start with **я**,
then a verb you own with its *I*-ending, then the rest. End on a dot.

[PAUSE 2s] Copying hid one thing from you: the model always had the right shapes
in it. Now your hand chooses them, and three are worth checking on purpose.

[PAUSE 2s] **л** and **м** — **л** comes to a **point**, **м** has a **dip**.

[PAUSE 2s] **т** — if your hand writes it like an *m*, give it the bar over the
top. The bar is a habit, not a rule, but it keeps the shape apart from **ш**
in a run of humps.

[PAUSE 2s] **я** — it faces the other way from Latin *R*. A hand that knows
English will try to turn it round.

[PAUSE 3s] Write the line. Then read it back and check those three letters
**before** you check anything else.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-L-01, RU-SCRIPT-M-01, RU-SCRIPT-T-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] What tells **л** from **м** by hand? (The top — a **point** or a
**dip**.)

[PAUSE 2s] And what did you choose here that the earlier lessons chose for you?
(**The words**, and so **the shapes**.)
