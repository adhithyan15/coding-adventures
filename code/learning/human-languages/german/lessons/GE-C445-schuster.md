## You'll want to know: der Schuster
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C445-SCHUSTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schuster** — "the cobbler, the shoemaker".

> Der Schuster repariert meine alten Stiefel.
>
> *The cobbler repairs my old boots.*

Same form in the plural. Also *der Schuhmacher*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schuster* comes from Middle High German *schuochsūtære* 'shoe-stitcher' (EtymDB), built from *Schuh* 'shoe' and *sūtære*, from Latin *sūtor* 'cobbler', from *suere* 'to sew' (Lewis & Short).
