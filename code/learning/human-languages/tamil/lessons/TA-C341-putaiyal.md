# புதையல் (putaiyal) — a treasure; buried treasure

## You'll want to know: புதையல்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C341-AGAZHVU341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**புதையல்** — *putaiyal* — "a treasure; buried treasure".

> ஒரு விவசாயி தன் நிலத்தில் புதையலைக் கண்டுபிடித்தார்.
>
> *oru vivacāyi taṉ nilattil putaiyalaik kaṇṭupiṭittār.*
>
> A farmer found treasure in his field.

From புதை, to bury: buried treasure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**புதையல்** (*putaiyal*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4509 gives it under புதை, "to be buried", as "hidden treasure".
