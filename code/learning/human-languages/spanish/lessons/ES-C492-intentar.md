## You'll want to know: intentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C492-LOGRO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**intentar** — "to try".

> Intento llegar a tiempo.
>
> *I try to arrive on time.*

Followed by an infinitive, with no preposition.
