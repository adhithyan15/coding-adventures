Marathi, chapter 391: Rising and Falling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घटणे (ghaṭṇe) — to decline; to decrease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for particular, then the Marathi for apart from.

You'll want to know: घटणे.
घटणे — ghaṭṇe — "to decline; to decrease".
थंडीत भाज्यांचे दर घटले. thaṇḍīt bhājyāñce dar ghaṭle. Vegetable prices fell in the cold season.
Past (he …): तो घटला, to ghaṭlā.
Intransitive: what decreases is the subject.

The word, taken apart.
घटणे (ghaṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ghaṭṇẽ, "to decrease", under ghaṭṭati, "decreases, is wanting" (CDIAL 4415).

Lesson 3 of 5.
टक्का (ṭakkā) — a percent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to decline, then the Marathi for to hold up.

You'll want to know: टक्का.
टक्का — ṭakkā — "a percent".
या वर्षी परीक्षेत नव्वद टक्के मुलं उत्तीर्ण झाली. yā varṣī parīkṣet navvad ṭakke mulaṁ uttīrṇa jhālī. This year ninety percent of the children passed the exam.
Masculine. Plural: टक्के, ṭakke.
Plural टक्के: पाच टक्के, five percent.

The word, taken apart.
टक्का (ṭakkā) is usually traced to Sanskrit ṭaṅka, "a stamped coin", under which CDIAL lists Marathi ṭakā, "a two-paisa coin" (CDIAL 5426); the sense one in a hundred came later, and Gujarati ṭako has it too.

Lesson 4 of 5.
प्रमाण (pramāṇ) — a proportion; a rate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to hold up, then the Marathi for a percent.

You'll want to know: प्रमाण.
प्रमाण — pramāṇ — "a proportion; a rate".
शहरात अपघातांचं प्रमाण वाढलं आहे. śahrāt apghātāñcaṁ pramāṇ vāḍhlaṁ āhe. The rate of accidents in the city has gone up.
Neuter.
Also a standard: प्रमाणभाषा, the standard language.

The word, taken apart.
प्रमाण (pramāṇ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pramāṇa, "measure, standard, proof" (CDIAL 8723).

Lesson 5 of 5.
स्तर (star) — a level.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a percent, then the Marathi for a proportion.

You'll want to know: स्तर.
स्तर — star — "a level".
नदीतील पाण्याचा स्तर वाढला. nadītīl pāṇyācā star vāḍhlā. The water level in the river rose.
Masculine.
Also a layer, a tier: समाजाचे स्तर (star).

The word, taken apart.
स्तर (star) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit stara, "layer" (CDIAL 13685); the same entry lists an inherited Marathi thar, "layer".
