German, chapter 139: To begin, To visit, To answer, To lay, To put.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
beginnen — to begin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for ninety, then the German for a million.

You'll want to know: beginnen.
beginnen — "to begin".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
