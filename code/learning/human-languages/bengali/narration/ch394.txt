Bengali, chapter 394: Breakdowns and Spare Parts.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বিগড়ানো (bigṛāno) — to break down; to go wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to run out, then the Bengali for to start (an engine).

You'll want to know: বিগড়ানো.
বিগড়ানো — bigṛāno — "to break down; to go wrong".
পথে আমাদের গাড়িটা বিগড়ে গেল। pôthe āmāder gāṛiṭā bigṛe gelô. Our car broke down on the way.
Past (he/she …): সেই গাড়ি বিগড়ে গেল, sei gāṛi bigṛe gelô.
Mostly in the -এ form with যাওয়া: বিগড়ে যাওয়া, of a machine, and of a mood.

The word, taken apart.
বিগড়ানো (bigṛāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali bigṛā, "to go bad", under Sanskrit vighaṭatē, "flies apart, is marred" (CDIAL 11673); the machine sense is the modern use.

Lesson 2 of 5.
যন্ত্রাংশ (jôntrāngshô) — a spare part; a part of a machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to start (an engine), then the Bengali for to break down.

You'll want to know: যন্ত্রাংশ.
যন্ত্রাংশ — jôntrāngshô — "a spare part; a part of a machine".
মিস্ত্রি দোকান থেকে নতুন যন্ত্রাংশ আনলেন। mistri dokān theke nôtun jôntrāngshô ānlen. The mechanic brought a new part from the shop.
A careful word, common on bills and in shops that sell them.

The word, taken apart.
যন্ত্রাংশ (jôntrāngshô) joins Sanskrit yantra, "an instrument, a machine", to aṃśa, "a part, a share" (Monier-Williams for both): a part of a machine; যন্ত্র on its own is already taught.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ঘষা (ghôshā) — to rub; to scrub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a screw, then the Bengali for a steering wheel.

You'll want to know: ঘষা.
ঘষা — ghôshā — "to rub; to scrub".
অরুণ সাবান নিয়ে সাইকেলের চাকা ঘষল। ôrun sābān niye sāikeler chākā ghôshlô. Arun took some soap and scrubbed the bicycle wheel.
Past (he/she …): তিনি চাকাটা ঘষলেন, tini chākāṭā ghôshlen.
The second letter is ষ, said sh like শ.

The word, taken apart.
ঘষা (ghôshā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali ghasā, "to rub", under Sanskrit ghárṣati, "rubs" (CDIAL 4450).
