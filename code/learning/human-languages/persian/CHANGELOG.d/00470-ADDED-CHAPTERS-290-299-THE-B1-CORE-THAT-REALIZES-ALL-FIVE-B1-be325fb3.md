## Added — chapters 290-299, the B1 core that realizes all five B1 spine nodes

- 49 lessons at sequences 14900-15284, two chapters per B1 node. The track
  had almost no verb grammar, so the core builds it: the present with *mi-*
  (written joined, **میروم**, with no ZWNJ), the simple past from the
  infinitive, *râ*, the *mi-* past as scene against the plain past as event,
  and the subjunctive with *be-*.
- SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS (290-291): *miravam*, *raftam*, *miraftam*
  (ASPECT-ONGOING-PAST); *avval / ba'd / dar âkhar* (CONNECTIVE-THEN); *vaqti*,
  *bâ* and the order of a told story (NARRATIVE-SEQUENCE); a 93-word reading,
  FA-C291-khandani, using only taught words.
- SPINE-GIVE-REASONS (292-293): *be nazar-e man* and *fekr mikonam ke*
  (OPINION-I-THINK); *barâ-ye hamin* and *pas* against *chon* and *zirâ*
  (CONNECTIVE-SO); *beravam*, *mikhâham* + subjunctive, *bâyad* and *behtar
  ast* (MODAL-SHOULD); *dalil in ast ke*, *be khâter-e* against *chon*
  (EXPLANATION-GIVE).
- SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL (294-295): *bebakhshid, chetor mitavânam be ... beravam?*
  (DIRECTION-ASK); *dast-e râst / chap* and the polite instruction;
  *bilit-e raft-o bargasht* (TRANSPORT-TICKET); *az dast dâdan* and *che kâr
  konam?* (PROBLEM-REPORT); *mas'ul*.
- SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE (296-297): *khoshhâl budam / shodam*
  (ADJECTIVE-FEELING); *ghazâ*; *omidvâram ke*, *dust dâram* and *ârezu dâram*
  + subjunctive (AMBITION-EXPRESS); *-tar az*, *-tarin*, *mesl*
  (COMPARISON-BASIC); *... ast ke* with the present and *pish* (TIME-DURATION).
- SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION (298-299): *agar* + subjunctive (CONNECTIVE-IF);
  *biyâyad* and the three shapes of a real result (CONDITIONAL-REAL);
  *bastegi dârad* (CONDITION-DEPENDS); *agar vaqt dâshtam, miraftam*
  (MODAL-WOULD); *dust dâshtam* and *kâsh*; a six-line guided writing task,
  FA-W299-shesh-khat.
- Seven letter lessons, each after the word that holds its letter: ذ
  (*gozashte*), ع (*ta'rif kardan*), ض (*towzih dâdan*), ئ (*mas'ul*), غ
  (*ghazâ*), ث (*mesl*), ژ (*zhâkat*). None claims a stroke order: no
  Persian-scoped source has been verified for them, so the lessons use the
  track's copy-the-shape wording, and the inventory entries leave
  `strokeOrder` empty. The letters join the perso-arabic inventory with owner
  declarations and evidence.
- Romanization: ق is *q* (*qatâr*, *vaqt*), غ is *gh* (*ghazâ*), ع and hamze
  are *'*, ژ is *zh*. Chapter 70's *ghatâr* is noted once as now *qatâr*.
- The five B1 spine ledgers no longer omit any concept. Pins: Persian lessons
  1485 -> 1534; the writing ladder gains a second connected composition;
  Persian book chapters 289 -> 299; perso-arabic letter owners 26 -> 33;
  Persian joins the tracks whose reach is above A2 (attained stays A2 until
  the B1 vocabulary lands).
