## You'll want to know: अर्थात्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C342-ADHARA342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्थात्** — *arthāt* — "that is to say".

> सः कृपणः अस्ति, अर्थात् धनं न ददाति।
>
> *saḥ kṛpaṇaḥ asti, arthāt dhanaṃ na dadāti.*
>
> He is stingy, that is to say he gives no money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्थात्** (*arthāt*) is built on *artha*, "aim, meaning", in its ablative; MW gives *arthāt* "as a matter of fact" and "according to the sense, that is to say" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
