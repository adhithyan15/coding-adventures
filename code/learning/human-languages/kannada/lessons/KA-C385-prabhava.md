## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಭಾವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C385-PRABHAVA385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಭಾವ** — *prabhāva* — "influence".

> ಅವನ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಪ್ರಭಾವ ಹೆಚ್ಚು.
>
> *avana mēle ajjana prabhāva heccu.*
>
> His grandfather's influence on him is strong.

ಮೇಲೆ marks who is influenced.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಭಾವ** (*prabhāva*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prabhāva*, "might, power, majesty" (Monier-Williams).
