Gujarati, chapter 381: If Only.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પસ્તાવું (pastāvũ) — to regret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for slightly, then the Gujarati for to cope.

You'll want to know: પસ્તાવું.
પસ્તાવું — pastāvũ — "to regret".
જો તમે આ તક ખોશો તો પછી પસ્તાશો. jo tame ā tak khośo to pachī pastāśo. If you lose this chance, you will regret it later.
Past (he …): તે પોતાની ભૂલ પર પસ્તાયો, te potānī bhūl par pastāyo.

The word, taken apart.
પસ્તાવું (pastāvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati word under a reconstructed paścōttāpa, "repentance" (CDIAL 8010), which is built from paścāt, "after", and tāpa, "burning": the heat that comes afterwards.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cope, then the Gujarati for to regret.

You'll want to know: અફસોસ.
અફસોસ — aphsos — "a pity; regret".
જો હું ત્યાં હોત તો તમને મદદ કરત; મને તેનો અફસોસ છે. jo hũ tyā̃ hot to tamne madad karat; mane teno aphsos che. If I had been there I would have helped you; I am sorry for that.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અફસોસ (aphsos) is usually traced to Persian afsōs, "regret, alas".

Lesson 3 of 5.
કાશ (kāś) — if only.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to regret, then the Gujarati for a pity.

You'll want to know: કાશ.
કાશ — kāś — "if only".
કાશ મારી પાસે વધારે સમય હોત! kāś mārī pāse vadhāre samay hot! If only I had more time!

The word, taken apart.
કાશ (kāś) is usually traced to Persian kāsh, "would that"; in Gujarati it opens a wish that did not come true.

Past (he …): તે ઘર માટે ઘણું ઝૂર્યો, te ghar māṭe ghaṇũ jhūryo.

The word, taken apart.
ઝૂરવું (jhūrvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to languish", under a reconstructed jhūrati, "wastes away" (CDIAL 5409).
