## You'll want to know: le podium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C507-TOURNOI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le podium** — "podium".

> Trois athlètes français sont montés sur le podium.
>
> *Three French athletes went up on the podium.*

Said *po-dyom*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le podium* is a learned borrowing from Latin *podium* "a raised platform" (EtymDB), from Greek *pódion* "a little foot, a base".
