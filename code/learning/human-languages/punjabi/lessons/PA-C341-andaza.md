## You'll want to know: ਅੰਦਾਜ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C341-DOUBT341-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅੰਦਾਜ਼ਾ** — *andāzā* — "a guess; an estimate".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਅੰਦਾਜ਼ਾ ਗ਼ਲਤ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā andāzā ghalat nahīṁ hai.*
>
> My guess is not wrong.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅੰਦਾਜ਼ਾ** (*andāzā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *andāza*, "measure".
