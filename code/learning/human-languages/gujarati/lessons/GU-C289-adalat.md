## You'll want to know: અદાલત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C289-ADALAT289-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અદાલત** — *adālat* — "a court (of law)".

> આજે સવારે પોલીસ ગુનેગારને અદાલતમાં લઈ ગઈ.
>
> *āje savāre polīs gunegārne adālatmā̃ laī gaī.*
>
> This morning the police took the criminal to court.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અદાલત** (*adālat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿadāla*, "justice, fairness": the place where justice is done. It is feminine: **અદાલતમાં**, in court.
