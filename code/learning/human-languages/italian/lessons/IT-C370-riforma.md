## You'll want to know: la riforma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C370-MAGGIORANZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la riforma** — "the reform".

> La riforma della scuola non piace agli insegnanti.
>
> *Teachers do not like the school reform.*

The verb is *riformare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*riforma* is built on *riformare* "to reform", a learned borrowing from Latin *refōrmāre* "to shape again" (Lewis & Short), built on *fōrma* "shape".
