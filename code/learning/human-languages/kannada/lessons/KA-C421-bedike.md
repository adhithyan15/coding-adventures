# ಬೇಡಿಕೆ (bēḍike) — a demand

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C420-ALUKU420-04, KA-LEX-C420-ALUKU420-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for uncertain, then the Kannada for a calamity.

## You'll want to know: ಬೇಡಿಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C421-BEDIKE421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೇಡಿಕೆ** — *bēḍike* — "a demand".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೂವಿಗೆ ಬೇಡಿಕೆ ಹೆಚ್ಚು.
>
> *habbada samayadalli hūvige bēḍike heccu.*
>
> At festival time flowers are in high demand.

Also a demand made by people: ಕಾರ್ಮಿಕರ ಬೇಡಿಕೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೇಡಿಕೆ** (*bēḍike*) is native Dravidian, a noun from *bēḍu*, "to ask for", listed with *bēḍike* in DEDR 5528.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
