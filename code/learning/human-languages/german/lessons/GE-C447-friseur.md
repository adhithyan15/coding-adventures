## You'll want to know: der Friseur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C447-FRISEUR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Friseur** — "the hairdresser, the barber".

> Einmal im Monat geht er zum Friseur.
>
> *He goes to the hairdresser once a month.*

Plural: *die Friseure*. A woman: *die Friseurin*. Also spelled *Frisör*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Friseur* was borrowed from French *friseur* 'hairdresser', from *friser* 'to curl, to frizz' (EtymDB); French today mostly says *coiffeur*.
