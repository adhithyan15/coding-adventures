## You'll want to know: sīve
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C391-ETIAMSI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sīve** — "or if; whether … or".

> Sīve pluit sīve sōl lūcet, ambulō.
>
> *Whether it rains or the sun shines, I go for a walk.*

*Sīve* (or *seu*) = *sī* + *-ve*, or: *sīve … sīve*, whether … or.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sīve* is built from *sī* "if" and the particle *-ve* "or" (Lewis & Short).
