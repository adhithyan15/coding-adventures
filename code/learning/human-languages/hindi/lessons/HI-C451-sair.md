## You'll want to know: सैर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C451-SAIR451-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सैर** — *sair* — "an outing; a stroll".

> छुट्टी में हम पहाड़ों की सैर पर गए।
>
> *chuṭṭī meṁ ham pahāṛoṁ kī sair par gae.*
>
> On holiday we went on a trip to the hills.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सैर** (*sair*) is usually traced to Arabic *sayr*, "travel, going about", through Persian: a trip for pleasure.
