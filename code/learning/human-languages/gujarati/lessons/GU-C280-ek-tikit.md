# એક ટિકિટ આપો (ek ṭikiṭ āpo) — at the ticket window

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C280-JHADPI-01, GU-SCRIPT-C280-JHA-01, GU-LEX-C86-JOURNEY-02, GU-LEX-C55-HOUSEWORK-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the fast train". (*jhaḍpī ṭren*.) Then "a ticket" and "to give". (**ટિકિટ**, **આપવું**.)

## Grammar Lens: asking for a ticket
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C280-TICKET-ASK-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C152-READ152-03, GU-LEX-C83-NUM3-04, GU-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **બે ટિકિટ આપો.**
>
> *be ṭikiṭ āpo.* — "Two tickets, please."

**આપો** is **આપવું** in the **-ઓ** form of **જાઓ** and **વળો**: said with respect at a window, it is polite enough. Then the price: **ભાડું કેટલું છે?** (*bhāḍũ keṭlũ che?*), "how much is the fare?" **ઝડપી ટ્રેનની ટિકિટ મોંઘી છે** (*jhaḍpī ṭrennī ṭikiṭ moṅghī che*): the fast train's ticket costs more.

## You'll want to know: the next train
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C280-NEXT-TRAIN-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C173-WEEK173-03, GU-Q-KYAARE-01, GU-LEX-C204-TIMES204-03, GU-LEX-C83-NUM3-05, GU-LEX-C204-TIMES204-04] -->

> **આવતી ટ્રેન ક્યારે છે?**
>
> *āvtī ṭren kyāre che?* — "When is the next train?"

**આવતું**, "coming, next", follows **ટ્રેન**: **આવતી**. Then the platform: **ટ્રેન કયા પ્લેટફોર્મથી ઊપડશે?** (*ṭren kayā pleṭphormthī ūpaḍśe?*), "which platform does the train leave from?" The answer: **પ્લેટફોર્મ બેથી** (*pleṭphorm bethī*), "from platform two".
