## Added — ং (anusvar) prints a stroke-order filmstrip

BN-W41-anusvar now prints a strip (9 -> 10 Bengali lessons): the ring
counterclockwise from its top, a lift, then the tail down to the right. The
order is counted from native Bengali writers' pen traces in HP Labs India's
LipiTk Bangla recognizer (two strokes in 183 of 189; ring first in 103 of
183, the commonest form; counts and shares only). Its Writing block swaps the
"copy what you see" disclaimer for the numbered-strip wording BN-W41-bisarga
uses.
