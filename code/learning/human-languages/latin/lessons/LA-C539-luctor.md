## You'll want to know: luctor, luctārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C539-ALEA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**luctor, luctārī** — "to wrestle, struggle".

> Iuvenēs in campō luctantur.
>
> *The young men wrestle on the field.*

Deponent: *luctor, luctārī, luctātus sum*. Also to struggle against something. English *reluctant* comes from *reluctor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**luctor** is built on *lucta* "wrestling" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB links it with a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to bend, twist". English *reluctant* comes from the compound *reluctor*.
