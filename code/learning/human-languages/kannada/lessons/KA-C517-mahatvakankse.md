# ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ (mahatvākāṅkṣe) — ambition

## You'll want to know: ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C517-PRAGATI517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ** — *mahatvākāṅkṣe* — "ambition".

> ಅವನಿಗೆ ದೊಡ್ಡ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಿ ಆಗುವ ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *avanige doḍḍa vijñāni āguva mahatvākāṅkṣe ide.*
>
> He has the ambition of becoming a great scientist.

ಮಹತ್ವ, greatness + ಆಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ, desire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ** (*mahatvākāṅkṣe*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *mahattva*, "greatness" (CDIAL 9933), and *ākāṅkṣā*, "desire, wish" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
