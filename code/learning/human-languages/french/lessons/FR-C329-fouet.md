## You'll want to know: le fouet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C329-FOUETTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le fouet** — "the whisk; the whip".

> Il mélange la crème avec un fouet.
>
> *He mixes the cream with a whisk.*

*Un coup de fouet* — a boost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fouet* is usually explained as a diminutive of Old French *fou* "beech", from Latin *fāgus* "beech" (Lewis & Short): first a beech switch.
