सदनिका (sadnikā) — a flat, an apartment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for humble, then the Marathi for arrogant.

You'll want to know: सदनिका.
सदनिका — sadnikā — "a flat, an apartment".

The word, taken apart.
सदनिका (sadnikā) is a modern word built on Sanskrit sadana, "seat, dwelling", with the ending -ikā.

The word, taken apart.
जेवणघर (jevaṇghar) is "meal-house": jevaṇ, "a meal", from Sanskrit jemana, "eating" (CDIAL 5268), and घर, "house".

Lesson 4 of 5.
घंटा (ghaṇṭā) — a bell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a dining room, then the Marathi for a balcony.

You'll want to know: घंटा.
घंटा — ghaṇṭā — "a bell".

The word, taken apart.
घंटा (ghaṇṭā) is Sanskrit ghaṇṭā, "bell", a tatsama; CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi form as ghāṁṭ, "bell" (CDIAL 4421). EWA derives it from granthi, "bell", but CDIAL takes that granthi as probably a Sanskrit rendering of Prakrit ghaṁṭiā.
