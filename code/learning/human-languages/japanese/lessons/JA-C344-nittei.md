## You'll want to know: にってい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C344-KEIKAKU344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**にってい** — *nittei* — "a schedule (of days)".

> **にっていが きまったら、おしえて ください。** — *nittei ga kimattara, oshiete kudasai* — "When the dates are fixed, please tell me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にってい** (*nittei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nichi*, "day" (Middle Chinese *nyit*) and *tei*, "extent" (*drjeng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as *nit-tei*.
