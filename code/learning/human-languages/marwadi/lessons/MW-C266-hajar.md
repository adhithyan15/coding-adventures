## You'll want to know: हाजर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C266-HAJAR266-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हाजर** — *hājar* — "present; at your service".

> हूं हाजर हूं सा।
>
> *hū̃ hājar hū̃ sā.*
>
> I'm here, at your service.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हाजर** (*hājar*) is usually traced, through Persian *hāzir*, to Arabic *ḥāḍir*, "present". With **सा** it tells a host or an elder that you are here and ready to help.
