# আমি ছিলাম (āmi chhilām) — was, and was not

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I went" and "she came". (*gelām*, *she elô*.) Then **আছি**, "I am, I am there": this lesson puts it in the past.

## You'll want to know: ছিলাম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C59-FOOD59-04, BN-LEX-C137-QUAL137-04, BN-LEX-C84-TOWN84-04, BN-LEX-C66-NATURE66-04, BN-LEX-C72-ADJ72-01] -->

> **কাল আমি বাড়িতে ছিলাম।**
>
> *kāl āmi bāṛite chhilām.* — "Yesterday I was at home."

**আছি** has a past of its own, **ছিলাম**, with the endings of the last lesson:

- **আমি** / **আমরা** **ছিলাম** (*chhilām*) — I was, we were
- **তুমি** **ছিলে** (*chhile*) — you were
- **সে** / **তারা** **ছিল** (*chhilô*) — he or she was; they were
- **আপনি** / **তিনি** **ছিলেন** (*chhilen*) — with respect

In the present, Bengali says "is" with no verb at all: **সে ভালো**. In the past it needs one: **সে ক্লান্ত ছিল** (*she klāntô chhilô*), "she was tired". **ছিল** also says "there was": **গ্রামে একটা বড় পুকুর ছিল** (*grāme ekṭā bôṛo pukur chhilô*), "there was a big pond in the village".

## You'll want to know: ছিল না
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-NOT-01]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C16-NEI-01, BN-LEX-C55-VERBS55-05, BN-LEX-C79-TIME79-03] -->

**নেই**, "there is not", which you know, has no past of its own. Its past is **ছিল না**, with **না** after the verb, as in the present:

> **আজ বাজারে মাছ নেই। কালও ছিল না।**
>
> *āj bājāre māchh nei. kālo chhilô nā.* — "There is no fish at the market today. There was none yesterday either."
