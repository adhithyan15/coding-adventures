## You'll want to know: allaiter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C308-NAISSANCE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**allaiter** — "to breastfeed".

> Elle a allaité son bébé pendant six mois.
>
> *She breastfed her baby for six months.*

Built on *lait*, milk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allaiter* is usually traced to Late Latin *allactāre* "to suckle", built on *lac*, *lactis* "milk" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists its Catalan and Italian cousins.
