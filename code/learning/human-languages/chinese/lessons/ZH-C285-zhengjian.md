## You'll want to know: zhèngjiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C285-ZHENGJIAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngjiàn* — "papers; an identity document".

> *wǒ de zhèngjiàn bú jiàn le* — My papers have gone missing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngjiàn* is built from zhèng 'proof' and jiàn 'an item', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyingH* and *gjenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, under its traditional form, 'to verify' and the second 'a portion'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C116-CARRY116-02, ZH-LEX-C189-FORM189-05] -->

*hùzhào* and *shēnfènzhèng* are both *zhèngjiàn*: it is the word for any of them, the one a desk asks to see.
