## You'll want to know: பயம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C72-FEEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**பயம்** (*payam*) — "fear".

Fear.

Three letters and not one vowel sign anywhere: ப, ய, and ம with a புள்ளி. Every letter is
carrying its own built-in *a*, which is the plainest a Tamil word can possibly be written.

It ends in the same **ம்** as கோபம், which is not a coincidence — Tamil keeps a whole shelf of
feeling-nouns with that ending.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two, and both of them end the same way.
