## You'll want to know: تھوکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C467-MOUTH467-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تھوکنا** — *thūknā* — "to spit".

> سڑک پر نہیں تھوکنا چاہیے۔
>
> *saṛak par nahīṅ thūknā chāhiye.*
>
> You should not spit in the street.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پانی تھوکا, *us ne pānī thūkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تھوکنا** (*thūknā*) is inherited from *thukk*, a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as an imitation of the sound (CDIAL 6097).
