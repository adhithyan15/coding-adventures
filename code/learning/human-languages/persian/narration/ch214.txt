Persian, chapter 214: Experience, Employer, Employee, Colleague, Manager.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تجربه (tajrobe) — experience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a circle, then the Persian for a dot.

You'll want to know: تجربه.
تجربه — tajrobe — "experience".

The word, taken apart.
tajrobe is borrowed from Arabic tajriba, "trial, experience". Its Arabic root is j-r-b (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
کارفرما (kârfarmâ) — an employer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a dot, then the Persian for experience.

You'll want to know: کارفرما.
کارفرما — kârfarmâ — "an employer".

The word, taken apart.
kârfarmâ is built from kâr, "work", and -farmâ, "commanding" (from farmudan, "to command"): the one who orders the work. kâr is inherited, from Middle Persian kār (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
همکار (hamkâr) — a colleague.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an employer, then the Persian for an employee.

You'll want to know: همکار.
همکار — hamkâr — "a colleague".

The word, taken apart.
hamkâr is built from ham-, "same, together", and kâr, "work": one who works with you. Both are inherited: EtymDB traces ham to Old Persian hama- and kâr to Middle Persian kār.
