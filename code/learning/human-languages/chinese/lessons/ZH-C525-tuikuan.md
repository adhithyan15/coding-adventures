## You'll want to know: tuìkuǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C525-SERVICE525-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìkuǎn* — "to give the money back; to refund".

> *wǒ děng le yí ge xīngqī tāmen cái tuìkuǎn* — I waited a week before they gave me my money back.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìkuǎn* is built from *tuì* 'to go back' and *kuǎn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thwojH* and *khwanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to retreat' and the second 'sincere' and 'to knock'; the sense 'a sum of money' is not given there.
