## You'll want to know: गवाक्षः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C425-SMARAKA425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गवाक्षः** — *gavākṣaḥ* — "a round window".

> राज्ञी गवाक्षात् नगरं पश्यति।
>
> *rājñī gavākṣāt nagaraṃ paśyati.*
>
> The queen looks out at the city from the round window.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गवाक्षः** (*gavākṣaḥ*) is built from *go*, "a bull", and *akṣa*, "an eye": a bull's eye, as MW glosses it; MW gives *gavākṣa* as an air-hole, a round window (MW).
