## You'll want to know: репетиция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C518-REHEARSAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**репетиция** (*repetítsiya*) — "a rehearsal".

> Репетиция начинается в семь часов.
>
> *Repetítsiya nachináyetsya v sem chasóv.*
>
> The rehearsal starts at seven.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*репетиция* was borrowed from Polish *repetycja* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *repetītiō* 'a repetition' (Lewis & Short).
