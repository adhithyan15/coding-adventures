## You'll want to know: બચત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C191-LEND191-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બચત** — *bacat* — "savings".

> **મારી બચત બેંકમાં છે.** — *mārī bacat bẽkmā̃ che* — "My savings are in the bank."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બચત** (*bacat*) is built on the verb *bacvũ*, "to be left over", with the noun ending *-at*: what is left over. *bacvũ* came into Gujarati from Hindi and goes back to Sanskrit *váñcati*, "escapes from" (CDIAL 11208).
