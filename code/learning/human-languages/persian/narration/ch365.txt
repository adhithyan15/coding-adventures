Persian, chapter 365: Judging and criticising.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قضاوت کردن (qezâvat kardan) — to judge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a rumour, then the Persian for to deny.

You'll want to know: قضاوت کردن.
قضاوت کردن (qezâvat kardan) — "to judge".
او زود درباره دیگران قضاوت میکند. u zud darbâre-ye digarân qezâvat mikonad. She is quick to judge other people.
Qezâvat "judgement" + kardan (stem kon-). Darbâre-ye kasi qezâvat kardan — to judge someone. It also means to referee a match.

The word, taken apart.
قضاوت was borrowed from Arabic qaḍāwa "judging" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as qāḍī "a judge"; kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
انتقاد کردن (enteqâd kardan) — to criticise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to judge, then the Persian for criticism.

You'll want to know: انتقاد کردن.
انتقاد کردن (enteqâd kardan) — "to criticise".
مردم از دولت انتقاد کردند. mardom az dowlat enteqâd kardand. People criticised the government.
Takes az: az kasi enteqâd kardan. Stem kon-.

The word, taken apart.
انتقاد کردن (enteqâd kardan) joins enteqâd, from Arabic intiqād "criticism" (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian verb kardan "to do".

Lesson 4 of 5.
اغراق کردن (eghrâq kardan) — to exaggerate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for criticism, then the Persian for to criticise.

You'll want to know: اغراق کردن.
اغراق کردن (eghrâq kardan) — "to exaggerate".
او همیشه اغراق میکند. u hamishe eghrâq mikonad. He exaggerates all the time.
Eghrâq "exaggeration" + kardan. Dar chizi eghrâq kardan — to exaggerate something.

The word, taken apart.
اغراق is usually traced to Arabic ighrāq "exaggeration"; kardan "to do" is the inherited Persian verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
توجیه کردن (towjih kardan) — to justify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to criticise, then the Persian for to exaggerate.

You'll want to know: توجیه کردن.
توجیه کردن (towjih kardan) — "to justify".
او نمیتواند رفتارش را توجیه کند. u nemitavânad raftârash râ towjih konad. He cannot justify his behaviour.
Towjih "justification; explanation" + kardan. It also means to brief someone on a task.

The word, taken apart.
توجیه was borrowed from Arabic tawjīh (EtymDB), a verbal noun usually explained as turning something to face the right way; kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.
