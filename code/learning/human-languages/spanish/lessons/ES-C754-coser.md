## You'll want to know: coser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C754-PESCA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**coser** — "to sew".

> Mi madre me enseñó a coser.
>
> *My mother taught me to sew.*

*Ser coser y cantar* — to be a piece of cake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coser* is inherited from Latin *cōnsuere* "to sew together", through spoken *cosēre* (EtymDB).
