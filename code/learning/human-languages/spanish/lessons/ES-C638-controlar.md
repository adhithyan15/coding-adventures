## You'll want to know: controlar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C638-LIMITE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**controlar** — "to control; to check".

> No puedo controlar todo.
>
> *I cannot control everything.*

Regular -ar verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*controlar* was borrowed from French *contrôler*, built on Old French *contrerole* 'a duplicate register', from Medieval Latin *contrarotulum*, from *contra* and *rotulus* 'roll' (EtymDB).
