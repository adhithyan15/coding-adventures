## You'll want to know: అదృశ్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C353-ADRSYAM353-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అదృశ్యం** — *adṛśyaṁ* — "a disappearance; vanished".

> ఆ పాప అదృశ్యం అందరినీ భయపెట్టింది.
>
> *ā pāpa adṛśyaṁ andarinī bhayapeṭṭindi.*
>
> The child's disappearance frightened everyone.

అదృశ్యమైంది, has gone missing: the news form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అదృశ్యం** (*adṛśyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *adṛśya*, "invisible, latent" (Monier-Williams), from *a-*, "not", and *dṛś*, "to see".
