## You'll want to know: xìxīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C273-CHAR273-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xìxīn* — "careful; attentive to detail".

> *tā gōngzuò hěn xìxīn* — She's very careful in her work.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìxīn* is built from *xì* 'fine' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sejH* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'small'.
