## You'll want to know: mèimei
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C33-FAMILY33-04]; assesses=[] -->

*mèimei* — "younger sister".

> *wǒ mèimei shì xiǎoxuésheng* — My little sister is at primary school.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mèimei* is inherited from Middle Chinese *mwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), where it is glossed with the word for sisters.
