# ਮੋਹਰ (mohar) — an office stamp; a seal

## You'll want to know: ਮੋਹਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C255-POST255-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋਹਰ** — *mohar* — "an office stamp; a seal".

> ਅਰਜ਼ੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਦੀ ਮੋਹਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *arzī utte daftar dī mohar hai.*
>
> The application has the office stamp on it.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋਹਰ** (*mohar*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *muhr*, "a seal".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
