## You'll want to know: مراسل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C293-AKHBAR293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مراسل** — *murāsil* — "a correspondent, a reporter".

> كتب المراسل عن المطر في القرية.
>
> *kataba al-murāsil ʿan al-maṭar fī al-qarya.*
>
> The correspondent wrote about the rain in the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مراسل** (*murāsil*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-s-l* (Lane), the root of *arsala*, "to send". *Murāsil* is the Form III participle, "one who writes back and forth": a correspondent.
