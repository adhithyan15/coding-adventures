## You'll want to know: പരിശീലകൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C568-KAYIKAM568-02]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിശീലകൻ** — *pariśīlakan* — "a coach; a trainer".

> പരിശീലകൻ ടീമിനെ രാവിലെ ആറ് മണിക്ക് വിളിച്ചു.
>
> *pariśīlakan ṭīmine rāvile āṟŭ maṇikkŭ viḷiccu.*
>
> The coach called the team at six in the morning.

From പരിശീലനം, training, already familiar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിശീലകൻ** (*pariśīlakan*) is built on പരിശീലനം, from Sanskrit *pariśīlana*, "contact, constant occupation, study" (Monier-Williams), with the agent ending -അകൻ.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
