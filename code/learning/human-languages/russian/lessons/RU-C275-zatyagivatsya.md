## You'll want to know: затягиваться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C275-ZATYAGIVATSYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**затягиваться** (*zatyágivatsya*) — "to drag on".

> Собрание затянулось до вечера.
>
> *Sobrániye zatyanúlos do véchera.*
>
> The meeting dragged on until the evening.

Imperfective; perfective *затянуться* (*zatyanútsya*). Of a wound it means "to heal over".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*затягиваться* is built on *за-* and *тянуть* "to pull", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *tęgnǫti*, from PIE *tengʰ-* "to pull" (EtymDB): to be pulled out long.
