Persian, chapter 391: Supposing.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
فرض کردن (farz kardan) — to suppose, to assume.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for unless, then the Persian for otherwise.

You'll want to know: فرض کردن.
فرض کردن (farz kardan) — "to suppose, to assume".
من فرض کردم که او خبر دارد. man farz kardam ke u khabar dârad. I assumed that he knew.
Farz "assumption" + kardan (stem kon-). Farz konim ke … — let us suppose that ….

The word, taken apart.
فرض was borrowed from Arabic farḍ (EtymDB), a word for something laid down; in Persian it means an assumption. Kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 6.
فرضیه (farziye) — a hypothesis.

You'll want to know: فرضیه.
فرضیه (farziye) — "a hypothesis".
دانشمندها این فرضیه را آزمایش کردند. dâneshmandhâ in farziye râ âzmâyesh kardand. The scientists tested this hypothesis.
Farz "assumption" + -iye. Plural farziyehâ.

The word, taken apart.
فرضیه (farziye) is built on farz "assumption", from Arabic farḍ (EtymDB), with the ending -iye, as in nazariye "theory".

Lesson 3 of 6.
چنانچه (chenânche) — if (formal), in case.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to suppose, then the Persian for a hypothesis.

You'll want to know: چنانچه.
چنانچه (chenânche) — "if (formal), in case".
چنانچه هوا خوب باشد، مسابقه برگزار میشود. chenânche havâ khub bâshad, mosâbeqe bargozâr mishavad. If the weather is good, the match will be held.
Formal and written, like agar; it takes the subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
چنانچه (chenânche) is a native Persian compound built from chenân "so, in that way" and che "what": in such a way that, if.

Lesson 4 of 6.
با وجود اینکه (bâ vojud-e inke) — even though, despite the fact that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a hypothesis, then the Persian for if.

You'll want to know: با وجود اینکه.
با وجود اینکه (bâ vojud-e inke) — "even though, despite the fact that".
با وجود اینکه باران میآمد، بیرون رفتیم. bâ vojud-e inke bârân miâmad, birun raftim. Even though it was raining, we went out.
It takes an indicative clause, like agarche. With a noun, bâ vojud-e alone.

The word, taken apart.
با وجود اینکه (bâ vojud-e inke) is literally "with the existence of the fact that": vojud comes from Arabic wujūd "existence" (EtymDB), and bâ "with" and inke "that" are Persian.

Lesson 5 of 6.
خیال کردن (khiyâl kardan) — to imagine; to think wrongly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for if, then the Persian for even though.

You'll want to know: خیال کردن.
خیال کردن (khiyâl kardan) — "to imagine; to think wrongly".
خیال کردم او رفته است، اما خانه بود. khiyâl kardam u rafte ast, ammâ khâne bud. I thought he had gone, but he was at home.
Often a belief that turns out wrong. Khiyâl "imagination" + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
خیال was borrowed from Arabic khayāl "imagination" (EtymDB); with the inherited verb kardan "to do", it means to imagine, often wrongly.
