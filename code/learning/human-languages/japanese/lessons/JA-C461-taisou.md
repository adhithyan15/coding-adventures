## You'll want to know: たいそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C461-SUMO461-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たいそう** — *taisō* — "gymnastics; physical exercises".

> **まいあさ たいそうを します。** — *maiasa taisō o shimasu* — "I do exercises every morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいそう** (*taisō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "body" (Middle Chinese *thejX*) and *sō*, "maneuver" (*tshaw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
