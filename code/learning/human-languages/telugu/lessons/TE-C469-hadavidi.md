# హడావిడి (haḍāviḍi) — a rush; a fuss

## You'll want to know: హడావిడి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C469-SAMME469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**హడావిడి** — *haḍāviḍi* — "a rush; a fuss".

> ఉదయం స్టేషన్లో చాలా హడావిడి ఉంది.
>
> *udayaṁ sṭēṣanlō cālā haḍāviḍi undi.*
>
> In the morning there is a great rush at the station.

హడావిడిగా, in a rush. Also the fuss before a wedding or a festival.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**హడావిడి** (*haḍāviḍi*) is an everyday Telugu word for a bustle whose origin is not settled.
