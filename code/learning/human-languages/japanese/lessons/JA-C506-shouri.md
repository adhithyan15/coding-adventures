## You'll want to know: しょうり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C506-RACE506-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうり** — *shōri* — "a victory".

> **みんなで しょうりを よろこびました。** — *minna de shōri o yorokobimashita* — "We all celebrated the victory."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうり** (*shōri*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "victory" (Middle Chinese *syingH*) and *ri*, "profit" (*lijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
