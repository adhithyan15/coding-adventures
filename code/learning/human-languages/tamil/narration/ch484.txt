Tamil, chapter 484: Hair and Scalp.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
பொடுகு (poṭuku) — dandruff.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to scratch an itch, then the Tamil for a mole.

You'll want to know: பொடுகு.
பொடுகு — poṭuku — "dandruff".
குளிர் காலத்தில் எனக்குப் பொடுகு அதிகமாகும். kuḷir kālattil eṉakkup poṭuku atikamākum. In winter my dandruff gets worse.
A mass noun: no plural.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of பொடுகு (poṭuku) is not settled; we could not find it in DEDR.

Lesson 4 of 6.
உதிர் (utir) — to fall out; to drop off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a louse, then the Tamil for baldness.

You'll want to know: உதிர்.
உதிர் — utir — "to fall out; to drop off".
கவலையால் அவருடைய முடி உதிர்ந்தது. kavalaiyāl avaruṭaiya muṭi utirntatu. His hair fell out from worry.
Past (it …): உதிர்ந்தது, utirntatu.
Of hair, leaves and petals: the subject is the thing that falls, so the past is உதிர்ந்தது.

The word, taken apart.
உதிர் (utir) is native Dravidian (DEDR 615), for leaves, fruit, hair and tears.

நரை (narai) — to turn grey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for baldness, then the Tamil for to fall out.

You'll want to know: நரை.
நரை — narai — "to turn grey".
ஐம்பது வயதில் அம்மாவின் முடி நரைத்தது. aimpatu vayatil ammāviṉ muṭi naraittatu. At fifty mother’s hair turned grey.
Past (it …): நரைத்தது, naraittatu.
As a noun, grey hair: நரை (narai) முடி.

The word, taken apart.
நரை (narai) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3609), both the grey hair and the greying.
