## You'll want to know: যথেষ্ট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C371-JOTHESHTO371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**যথেষ্ট** — *jôtheshṭô* — "enough; plenty".

> যথেষ্ট টাকা থাকলে আমরা নতুন বাড়ি কিনব।
>
> *jôtheshṭô ṭākā thākle āmrā nôtun bāṛi kinbô.*
>
> If we have enough money, we'll buy a new house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**যথেষ্ট** (*jôtheshṭô*) is Sanskrit *yatheṣṭa*, 'as much as one wishes' (Monier-Williams). Bengali turned it into plain 'enough'.
