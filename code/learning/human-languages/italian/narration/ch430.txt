Italian, chapter 430: In campeggio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
piantare — to pitch (a tent); to plant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the guesthouse, then the Italian for the campsite.

You'll want to know: piantare.
piantare — "to pitch (a tent); to plant".
Piantiamo la tenda vicino al fiume. We pitch the tent near the river.
Also piantare un albero — to plant a tree; in speech, piantare qualcuno is to leave someone.

The word, taken apart.
piantare is inherited from Latin plantāre 'to plant' (EtymDB), from planta 'sole of the foot, young shoot'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
il sacco a pelo — the sleeping bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the campsite, then the Italian for to pitch.

You'll want to know: il sacco a pelo.
il sacco a pelo — "the sleeping bag".
Di notte fa freddo, porta il sacco a pelo. It is cold at night, bring the sleeping bag.
Literally a sack with fur: the first ones were lined with it.

The word, taken apart.
sacco a pelo is built from sacco 'sack', inherited from Latin saccus, from Greek sákkos, and pelo 'hair, fur', inherited from Latin pilus (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la roulotte — the caravan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to pitch, then the Italian for the sleeping bag.

You'll want to know: la roulotte.
la roulotte — "the caravan".
I nonni passano l'estate in roulotte. My grandparents spend the summer in a caravan.
Said the French way; towed behind a car. Plural unchanged.

The word, taken apart.
roulotte was borrowed from French roulotte (EtymDB), built on rouler 'to roll', which goes back to Latin rota 'wheel'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
il camper — the camper van, the motorhome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the sleeping bag, then the Italian for the caravan.

You'll want to know: il camper.
il camper — "the camper van, the motorhome".
Con il camper dormiamo dove vogliamo. With the camper van we sleep where we like.
Il camperista is the owner. Plural unchanged.

The word, taken apart.
camper was borrowed from English camper (EtymDB), built on camp, which goes back to Latin campus 'field'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la zanzariera — the mosquito net, the fly screen.

You'll want to know: la zanzariera.
la zanzariera — "the mosquito net, the fly screen".
Chiudi la zanzariera, entrano gli insetti. Close the fly screen, the insects are getting in.
Built on la zanzara, the mosquito.

The word, taken apart.
zanzariera is built on zanzara 'mosquito', which is inherited from Late Latin zinzala (EtymDB), a word that imitates the buzzing.
