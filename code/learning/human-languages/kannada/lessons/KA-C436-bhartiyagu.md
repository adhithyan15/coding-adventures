# ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಗು (bhartiyāgu) — to fill up; to be full

## You'll want to know: ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C436-HAMALI436-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಗು** — *bhartiyāgu* — "to fill up; to be full".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ಕಾರಣ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಬಸ್ಸುಗಳು ಭರ್ತಿಯಾದವು.
>
> *habbada kāraṇa ellā bassugaḷu bhartiyādavu.*
>
> Because of the festival all the buses filled up.

Past (it …): ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಯಿತು, *bhartiyāyitu*.

ಭರ್ತಿ alone means full or filled; ಭರ್ತಿ ಮಾಡು is to fill in, as a form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಗು** (*bhartiyāgu*) joins *bharti*, "full, filled", usually traced to Hindustani *bhartī*, "filling, recruitment", and the native verb ಆಗು, to become.
