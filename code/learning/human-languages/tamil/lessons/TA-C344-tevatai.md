## You'll want to know: தேவதை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C344-MAYAM344-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தேவதை** — *tēvatai* — "a fairy; an angel".

> ஒரு தேவதை ஏழைச் சிறுமிக்கு உதவினாள்.
>
> *oru tēvatai ēḻaic ciṟumikku utaviṉāḷ.*
>
> A fairy helped the poor girl.

Also of a very kind person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தேவதை** (*tēvatai*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dēvatā*, "divinity" (CDIAL 6530).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
