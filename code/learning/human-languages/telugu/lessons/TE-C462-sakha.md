## You'll want to know: శాఖ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C462-KAUNTARU462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**శాఖ** — *śākha* — "a branch office; a branch".

> ఆ బ్యాంకుకు మా పట్టణంలో కొత్త శాఖ వచ్చింది.
>
> *ā byāṅkuku mā paṭṭaṇaṁlō kotta śākha vaccindi.*
>
> That bank has opened a new branch in our town.

A branch of a tree, of a bank, of a government: వాతావరణ శాఖ, the weather department.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శాఖ** (*śākha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śākhā*, "a branch" of a tree (CDIAL 12376); Telugu uses it for the branches of offices too.
