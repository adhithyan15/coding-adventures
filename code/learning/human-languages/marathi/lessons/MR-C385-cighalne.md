## You'll want to know: चिघळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C385-CHIGHALNE385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चिघळणे** — *cighaḷṇe* — "to worsen; to fester".

> वेळेवर बोलणं झालं नाही, म्हणून वाद चिघळला.
>
> *veḷevar bolṇaṁ jhālaṁ nāhī, mhaṇūn vād cighaḷlā.*
>
> Nobody talked in time, so the dispute festered.

Past (he …): तो चिघळला, *to cighaḷlā*.

Of a wound or a quarrel left alone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिघळणे** (*cighaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
