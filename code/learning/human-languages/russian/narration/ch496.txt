Russian, chapter 496: A Baby at Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
подгузник (podgúznik) — a nappy, a diaper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pram, then the Russian for to crawl.

You'll want to know: подгузник.
подгузник (podgúznik) — "a nappy, a diaper".
Папа научился быстро менять подгузники. Pápa naúchilsya býstro menyát podgúzniki. Dad has learnt to change nappies quickly.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
подгузник (podgúznik) is built on под 'under' and the colloquial гузно 'rump'; EtymDB lists the word without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
колыбельная (kolybélnaya) — a lullaby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a nappy, then the Russian for a dummy.

You'll want to know: колыбельная.
колыбельная (kolybélnaya) — "a lullaby".
Бабушка поёт внучке колыбельную. Bábushka poyót vnúchke kolybélnuyu. Grandma sings her granddaughter a lullaby.
Short for колыбельная (kolybélnaya) песня: an adjective used as a noun. Built on колыбель — a cradle.

The word, taken apart.
колыбельная (kolybélnaya) is built on колыбель 'cradle', usually linked to колыхать 'to sway'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
убаюкивать (ubayúkivat) — to lull to sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dummy, then the Russian for a lullaby.

You'll want to know: убаюкивать.
убаюкивать (ubayúkivat) — "to lull to sleep".
Мама убаюкивает ребёнка тихой песней. Máma ubayúkivayet rebyónka tíkhoy pésney. Mum is lulling the baby to sleep with a quiet song.
Imperfective; perfective убаюкать.

The word, taken apart.
убаюкивать (ubayúkivat) is built on у- and баюкать 'to lull', which is usually traced to the lullaby refrain баю-бай; EtymDB lists баюкать without a source and has no entry for убаюкивать (ubayúkivat).

Lesson 5 of 5.
пеленать (pelenát) — to swaddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a lullaby, then the Russian for to lull to sleep.

You'll want to know: пеленать.
пеленать (pelenát) — "to swaddle".
Медсестра показала нам, как пеленать ребёнка. Medsestrá pokazála nam, kak pelenát rebyónka. The nurse showed us how to swaddle the baby.
Imperfective; perfective запеленать. The cloth is a пелёнка.

The word, taken apart.
пеленать (pelenát) is built on пелена 'a cloth, a veil', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic pelena 'thin skin, thin fabric' (EtymDB); EtymDB has no entry for the verb.
