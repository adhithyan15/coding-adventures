## Writing

Score the form for seven relevant fields and the message for its named reader,
recognisable identity, place, everyday activity, preference, location question,
meeting place and time, and polite close. Do not require the learner to copy a
particular sentence or disclose real personal information. The message must be
30–40 independently produced Sanskrit words. Apply the analytic bands in the
rubric, including word-final visarga or anusvara, continuous śirorekhā, and
unjoined word-boundary control.

## Speaking

Score communicative achievement rather than verbatim reproduction. The
interview needs relevant personal responses; the description needs a place,
people or objects, an activity, and a reason; the role-play needs a book request,
price handling, and a return arrangement for tomorrow. Accept a documented
pronunciation tradition when it is internally consistent. Apply the rubric to
the recording independently; examiner assistance beyond the permitted repeat
is recorded and reflected in interaction scoring.
