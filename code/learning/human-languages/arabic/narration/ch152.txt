Arabic, chapter 152: Sour, Bitter, Soft, Hard, Clever.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حامض (ḥāmiḍ) — sour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for dry, then the Arabic for dirty.

You'll want to know: حامض.
حامض — ḥāmiḍ — "sour".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does حامض (ḥāmiḍ) mean? ("Sour".) Say it once more.
