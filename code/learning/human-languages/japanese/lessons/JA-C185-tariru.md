## You'll want to know: たりる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C185-SALE185-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たりる** — *tariru* — "to be enough; to suffice".

> **おかねが たりません。** — *okane ga tarimasen* — "There is not enough money."

Polite **たります**, *tarimasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たりる** (*tariru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
