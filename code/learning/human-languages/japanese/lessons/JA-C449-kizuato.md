## You'll want to know: きずあと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C449-BURN449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きずあと** — *kizuato* — "a scar".

> **ひざに ちいさい きずあとが あります。** — *hiza ni chiisai kizuato ga arimasu* — "I have a small scar on my knee."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きずあと** (*kizuato*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *kizu*, "wound" and *ato*, "mark" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
