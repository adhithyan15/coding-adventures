## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-PERFORMANCE-FOOD-SEVEN-FOUR-SKILL-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DAAL-01, MW-SCRIPT-DAAL-01, MW-LEX-GHEE-01, MW-SCRIPT-GHEE-01, MW-LEX-SABJI-01, MW-SCRIPT-SABJI-01, MW-LEX-ROTI-01, MW-SCRIPT-ROTI-01, MW-LEX-BAATI-01, MW-SCRIPT-BAATI-01, MW-LEX-CHURMA-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHURMA-01, MW-LEX-CHAAY-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01] -->

1. Identify seven heard words.
2. Produce seven words from meaning cues.
3. Match seven printed cards to meanings.
4. Write all seven heard words without a model.

Pass each skill separately. Seven food names are a vocabulary map; offering
food, accepting it, asking for more, and refusing politely are all still
untaught.
