Malayalam, chapter 123: Maybe, and Not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പറ്റില്ല (paṟṟilla) — it's not possible, I can't.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for the same, then the Malayalam for oh, I see.

You'll want to know: പറ്റില്ല.
പറ്റില്ല (paṟṟilla) — "it's not possible, (I) can't". A firm, everyday no.

What you've built.
The polite but firm no.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ആയിരിക്കാം (āyirikkāṁ) — maybe, it may be.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for not possible, then the Malayalam for not like that.

You'll want to know: ആയിരിക്കാം.
ആയിരിക്കാം (āyirikkāṁ) — "maybe, it could be". ഒരുപക്ഷേ (orupakṣē), "perhaps", often comes before it.

A guess, softly put.

Lesson 4 of 5.
വേറെ (vēṟe) — other, different.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for not like that, then the Malayalam for maybe.

You'll want to know: വേറെ.
വേറെ (vēṟe) — "other, different". വേറെ വേണം (vēṟe vēṇaṁ) — "I want a different one".

What you've built.
When the first choice is wrong.

Lesson 5 of 5.
തെറ്റ് (teṟṟŭ) — wrong; a mistake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for maybe, then the Malayalam for other.

You'll want to know: തെറ്റ്.
തെറ്റ് (teṟṟŭ) — "wrong", and also "a mistake". It is the honest partner of ശരി, "right".

What you've built.
The opposite of śari.
