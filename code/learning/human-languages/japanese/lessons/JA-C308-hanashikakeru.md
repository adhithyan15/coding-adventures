## You'll want to know: はなしかける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C308-MACHIGAI308-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はなしかける** — *hanashikakeru* — "to speak to (someone)".

> **えきで おばあさんに はなしかけました。** — *eki de obāsan ni hanashikakemashita* — "At the station I spoke to an old woman."

Polite **はなしかけます**, *hanashikakemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **おとこが こどもに はなしかけました。**, *otoko ga kodomo ni hanashikakemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はなしかける** (*hanashikakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hanashi*, "speaking" (from *hanasu*), and *kakeru*, "to set about" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
