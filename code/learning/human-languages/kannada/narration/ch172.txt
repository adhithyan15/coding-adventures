Kannada, chapter 172: Rarely, Every day, Beginning, Century, Moment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಪರೂಪಕ್ಕೆ (aparūpakke) — rarely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the weekend, then the Kannada for before.

You'll want to know: ಅಪರೂಪಕ್ಕೆ.
ಅಪರೂಪಕ್ಕೆ — aparūpakke — "rarely".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಪರೂಪಕ್ಕೆ (aparūpakke) is aparūpa, "rare", with -kke, "for": "rarely". Aparūpa is usually traced to Sanskrit apūrva, "unprecedented" (CDIAL 494), but no source settles it.
