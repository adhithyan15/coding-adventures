German, chapter 507: Halbzeit und Elfmeter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Halbzeit — half-time; half.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for referee, then the German for to whistle.

You'll want to know: die Halbzeit.
die Halbzeit — "half-time; half".
In der Halbzeit trinken die Spieler Wasser. At half-time the players drink water.
Plural: die Halbzeiten. Die erste Halbzeit — the first half.

The word, taken apart.
Halbzeit is built on halb "half", from Proto-Germanic halbaz, and Zeit "time", inherited from Old High German zīt, from Proto-Germanic tīdiz (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Elfmeter — penalty (kick).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for to whistle, then the German for half-time.

You'll want to know: der Elfmeter.
der Elfmeter — "penalty (kick)".
In der letzten Minute gab es einen Elfmeter. In the last minute there was a penalty.
Plural: die Elfmeter. Named after the eleven metres to the goal.

The word, taken apart.
Elfmeter is built on elf "eleven" and Meter, which was borrowed from French mètre, from Greek métron "measure" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: die Verlängerung.
die Verlängerung — "extra time; extension".
Nach der Verlängerung stand es immer noch eins zu eins. After extra time it was still one all.
Plural: die Verlängerungen. Also the extension of a contract or a cable.

The word, taken apart.
Verlängerung is built on verlängern "to lengthen", from lang "long", inherited from Old High German lang, from Proto-Germanic langaz (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Unentschieden — tied game, level score.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for penalty, then the German for extra time.

You'll want to know: das Unentschieden.
das Unentschieden — "tied game, level score".
Das Spiel endete mit einem Unentschieden. The game ended level.
Plural: die Unentschieden. As an adjective: Das Spiel ging unentschieden aus.

The word, taken apart.
Unentschieden is built on un- "not" and entschieden "decided", the participle of entscheiden "to decide" (EtymDB lists the verb): a game that nobody decided.

Lesson 5 of 5.
das Abseits — offside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for extra time, then the German for tied game.

You'll want to know: das Abseits.
das Abseits — "offside".
Das Tor zählte nicht, weil der Spieler im Abseits stand. The goal didn't count because the player was offside.
Im Abseits stehen — to be offside; also to be left out.

The word, taken apart.
Abseits is built on ab "off" and seits "on the side"; EtymDB links the football sense to English offside, which it renders.
