## You'll want to know: دش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C447-DUSH447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دش** — *dush* — "a shower".

> أخذت دشا بعد الرحلة.
>
> *akhadhtu dushan baʿda ar-riḥla.*
>
> I took a shower after the trip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دش** (*dush*) is usually traced to French *douche*.
