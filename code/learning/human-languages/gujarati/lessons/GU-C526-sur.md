## You'll want to know: સૂર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C526-SUR526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂર** — *sūr* — "a musical note".

> મીરાનો સૂર ઘણો મીઠો છે.
>
> *Mīrāno sūr ghaṇo mīṭho che.*
>
> Mira sings very sweetly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂર** (*sūr*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *sura*, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "a modulation of the voice, a tune" (CDIAL 13498).
