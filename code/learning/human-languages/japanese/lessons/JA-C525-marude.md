## You'll want to know: まるで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C525-REAL525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**まるで** — *marude* — "(not) in the least; completely".

> **もし ふゆに いったら、うみには まるで だれも いません。** — *moshi fuyu ni ittara, umi ni wa marude daremo imasen* — "If you go in winter, there is nobody at all at the sea."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まるで** (*marude*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
