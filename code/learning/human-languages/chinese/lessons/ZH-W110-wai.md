## Script — the top of 多, then a vertical and a dot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-WAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 外

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, three strokes**, the top half of 多: a short left-falling stroke, a stroke across that turns and sweeps down to the left, and a dot inside.

**The right piece, two strokes**: a long vertical, and a dot to its right.

3 + 2 = five.
