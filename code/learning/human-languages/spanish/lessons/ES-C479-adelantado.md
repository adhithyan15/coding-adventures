## You'll want to know: adelantado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C479-TRAMITE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**adelantado** — "in advance; early, ahead".

> Hay que pagar por adelantado.
>
> *You have to pay in advance.*

*El reloj va adelantado* — the clock is fast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**adelantado** is the participle of *adelantar*, "to move forward", built on *adelante*, "forward", and *delante*, "in front", from Old Spanish *denante* (EtymDB 2.1), Latin *de in ante*, "from in front".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.
