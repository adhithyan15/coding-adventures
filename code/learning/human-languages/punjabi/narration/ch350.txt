Lesson 1 of 6.
ਸ਼ੌਕ (shauk) — a hobby; a liking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a poem, then the Punjabi for to praise.

You'll want to know: ਸ਼ੌਕ.
ਸ਼ੌਕ — shauk — "a hobby; a liking".
ਮੇਰਾ ਸ਼ੌਕ ਸੰਗੀਤ ਹੈ। merā shauk saṅgīt hai. My hobby is music.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸ਼ੌਕ (shauk) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic shawq, "longing".

Lesson 4 of 6.
ਨਚਾਉਣਾ (nachāuṇā) — to make someone dance; to get someone dancing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a stage play, then the Punjabi for a film.

You'll want to know: ਨਚਾਉਣਾ.
ਨਚਾਉਣਾ — nachāuṇā — "to make someone dance; to get someone dancing".
ਢੋਲ ਦੀ ਥਾਪ ਸਭ ਨੂੰ ਨਚਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। ḍhol dī thāp sabh nū̃ nachāundī hai. The beat of the drum gets everyone dancing.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਨਚਾਇਆ, maiṁ bachchiāṁ nū̃ nachāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਚਾਉਣਾ (nachāuṇā) is the causative partner of naccṇā, "to dance", which goes back to Sanskrit nṛtyati, "dances"; CDIAL lists Punjabi naccṇā under it (CDIAL 7583).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ਪਰਚਾਉਣਾ (parchāuṇā) — to entertain; to amuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a film, then the Punjabi for to make someone dance.

You'll want to know: ਪਰਚਾਉਣਾ.
ਪਰਚਾਉਣਾ — parchāuṇā — "to entertain; to amuse".
ਨਾਨੀ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨਾਲ ਪਰਚਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। nānī bachchiāṁ nū̃ gītāṁ nāl parchāundī hai. Grandmother keeps the children amused with songs.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮਹਿਮਾਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਪਰਚਾਇਆ, maiṁ mahimānāṁ nū̃ parchāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਰਚਾਉਣਾ (parchāuṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed prarajyate, "is pleased, is excited"; CDIAL lists Punjabi parcāuṇā, "to divert, entertain", under it (CDIAL 8737).
