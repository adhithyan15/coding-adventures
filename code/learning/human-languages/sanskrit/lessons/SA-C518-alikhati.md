## You'll want to know: आलिखति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C518-TULIKA518-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आलिखति** — *ālikhati* — "paints".

> मीरा पुष्पाणि आलिखति।
>
> *mīrā puṣpāṇi ālikhati.*
>
> Mira paints flowers.

Past (she …): सा मयूरम् आलिखितवती, *sā mayūram ālikhitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आलिखति** (*ālikhati*) is built on *ā* and the root √likh, "to scratch, to write", which also gives *likhati*; MW gives *ālikh* as to mark, to draw, to delineate, to paint (MW).
