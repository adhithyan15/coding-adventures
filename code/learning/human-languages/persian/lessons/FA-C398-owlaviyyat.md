## You'll want to know: اولویت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C398-AMALI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اولویت** (*owlaviyat*) — "a priority".

> سلامت بچه ها اولویت اول ماست.
>
> *salâmat-e bachche hâ owlaviyat-e avval-e mâst.*
>
> The children's health is our first priority.

Plural *owlaviyathâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اولویت** comes from Arabic *awlawiyya* "priority" (EtymDB), which EtymDB derives from *awlā* "more deserving, nearer".
