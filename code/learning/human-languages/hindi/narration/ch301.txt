Hindi, chapter 301: Putting the Fire Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सायरन (sāyran) — a siren.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for light, then the Hindi for scorching heat.

You'll want to know: सायरन.
सायरन — sāyran — "a siren".
सड़क पर सायरन बज रहा था। saṛak par sāyran baj rahā thā. A siren was sounding on the road.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
सायरन (sāyran) is borrowed from English siren, taken into Hindi as a masculine noun. Like a bell, a siren बजता है.

Lesson 2 of 5.
हड़बड़ाना (haṛbaṛānā) — to be flustered; to rush in a panic.

You'll want to know: हड़बड़ाना.
हड़बड़ाना — haṛbaṛānā — "to be flustered; to rush in a panic".
सायरन सुनकर लोग हड़बड़ा गए। sāyran sunkar log haṛbaṛā gae. Hearing the siren, people got flustered.
Past (he …): वह हड़बड़ा गया, vah haṛbaṛā gayā.

The word, taken apart.
हड़बड़ाना (haṛbaṛānā) is set by CDIAL under haḍabaḍa (CDIAL 13949), a word CDIAL gives for confusion and very probably imitative. It is the rush when nobody knows what to do first.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बुझना (bujhnā) — to go out (of a fire or light).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a siren, then the Hindi for to be flustered.

You'll want to know: बुझना.
बुझना — bujhnā — "to go out (of a fire or light)".
दो घंटे बाद आग बुझ गई। do ghaṇṭe bād āg bujh gaī. Two hours later the fire went out.
Past (it …): वह आग बुझ गई, vah āg bujh gaī.

The word, taken apart.
बुझना (bujhnā) is inherited from vijjhāyati, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 11703). It is the intransitive partner of बुझाना, to put out: you बुझाते हैं a fire, and the fire बुझती है.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पिघलना (pighalnā) — to melt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be flustered, then the Hindi for to go out (of a fire or light).

You'll want to know: पिघलना.
पिघलना — pighalnā — "to melt".
आग की गर्मी से खिड़की का प्लास्टिक पिघल गया। āg kī garmī se khiṛkī kā plāsṭik pighal gayā. The heat of the fire melted the plastic on the window.
Past (it …): वह बर्फ़ पिघल गई, vah barf pighal gaī.

The word, taken apart.
पिघलना (pighalnā) is listed by CDIAL under praghārayati, a reconstructed form (CDIAL 8485). Ice, wax and plastic पिघलते हैं; so, in speech, does a hard heart.

आग से उसके हाथ पर फफोले पड़ गए। āg se uske hāth par phaphole paṛ gae. The fire left blisters on his hand.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
फफोला (phapholā) is inherited from prasphōṭa, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 8883), which gathers words for bubbles and blisters. Blisters पड़ते हैं: they come up on the skin.
