## You'll want to know: hūxī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C239-HABIT239-04]; assesses=[] -->

*hūxī* — "to breathe".

> *tā gǎnmào le bù néng yòng bízi hūxī* — He's got a cold and can't breathe through his nose.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hūxī* is built from *hū* 'to breathe out' and *xī* 'to breathe in', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xu* and *xip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to call' and, after an older book, 'the outward breath', and the second 'the inward breath'.
