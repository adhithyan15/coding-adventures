## You'll want to know: ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C408-PATHYAKRAMA408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸು** — *kēndrīkarisu* — "to focus".

> ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಗೆ ಮೊದಲು ನಾನು ಗಣಿತದ ಮೇಲೆ ಗಮನ ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *parīkṣege modalu nānu gaṇitada mēle gamana kēndrīkariside.*
>
> Before the exam I focused on mathematics.

Past (I …): ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ, *kēndrīkariside*.

What you focus on takes ಮೇಲೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸು** (*kēndrīkarisu*) is built on Sanskrit *kendra*, "the centre of a circle" (Monier-Williams), itself taken from Greek *kentron*, with -*īkarisu*, "to make into".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
