## You'll want to know: ஊதியம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C508-OOTHIYAM508-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊதியம்** — *ūtiyam* — "wages; pay".

> இந்த மாதம் ஊதியம் தாமதமாக வந்தது.
>
> *inta mātam ūtiyam tāmatamāka vantatu.*
>
> This month the pay came late.

A formal word beside சம்பளம் (*campaḷam*); it is the second half of ஓய்வூதியம் (*ōyvūtiyam*), a pension, the pay of rest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ஊதியம்** (*ūtiyam*) is not settled: DEDR 609, under உதவு (*utavu*), "to help, to be of use", lists ஊதியம், "profit, gain", but marks the link with a question mark.
