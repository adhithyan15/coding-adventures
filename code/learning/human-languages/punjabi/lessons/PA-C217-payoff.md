# Who came: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217, PA-LEX-AURAT-217] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *came* three ways. Then *a woman*.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AGREE-PERSON-217, PA-LEX-AURAT-217, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AU-217, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *who came yesterday?*] Say **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੌਣ ਆਇਆ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *a woman came*] Say **ਔਰਤ ਆਈ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਆਦਮੀ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ।**] Give its meaning, then point to **ਔ** in **ਔਰਤ**.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਔਰਤ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ।**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AGREE-PERSON-217, PA-LEX-AURAT-217] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Tell who came to your home yesterday, a man, a woman or a group.
