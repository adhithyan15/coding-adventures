## You'll want to know: ન્યાયાધીશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C348-KES348-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ન્યાયાધીશ** — *nyāyādhīś* — "a judge".

> ન્યાયાધીશે બંને બાજુની વાત ધ્યાનથી સાંભળી.
>
> *nyāyādhīśe banne bājunī vāt dhyānthī sā̃bhḷī.*
>
> The judge listened carefully to both sides.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ન્યાયાધીશ** (*nyāyādhīś*) is built from Sanskrit *nyāya*, "rule, justice", and *adhīśa*, "a lord, a master" (Monier-Williams): the master of justice.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
