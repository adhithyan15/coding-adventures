# కాపుకాచు (kāpukācu) — to lie in wait

## You'll want to know: కాపుకాచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C348-DOPIDI348-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కాపుకాచు** — *kāpukācu* — "to lie in wait".

> దొంగలు చీకట్లో దారి పక్కన కాపుకాశారు.
>
> *doṅgalu cīkaṭlō dāri pakkana kāpukāśāru.*
>
> The robbers lay in wait by the road in the dark.

Past (I …): కాపుకాశాను, *kāpukāśānu*.

కాపు, watch, with a verb meaning to guard: to keep watch for someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కాపుకాచు** (*kāpukācu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1416 lists Telugu *kācu*, "to guard, watch; to wait for", and *kāpu*, "protection, a guard".
