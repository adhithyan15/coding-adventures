## You'll want to know: なにも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C139-NANIMO]; assesses=[] -->

**なにも** — *nani mo* — "nothing", "not anything". It pairs with
a negative verb: Japanese says "anything ... not", where English says
"nothing".

> **なにも のみません。** — *nani mo nomimasen* — "I don't drink anything."

**のむ**, *nomu*, "to drink", gives **のみます** *nomimasu* and then
**のみません** *nomimasen*. With **ます** instead of **ません** the sentence
does not work: **なにも** needs the *not*.
