## Grammar Lens: direct owner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-YATRI-202]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਯਾਤਰੀ** — *yātrī* — traveller; person who travels

Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole person noun
rather than inventing smaller Punjabi pieces. This lesson owns the word alone;
the complete identity frame comes only after a separate retrieval.

## The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-YATRI-202] -->

Current Punjab School Education Board Class 11 material gives this word as the
meaning of another traveller term. PSEB's Class 5 book independently uses it
for a person who travels beyond Earth. Together they support the bounded
person-who-travels meaning in current educational Punjabi.
Sources: [PSEB Class 11](https://static.pseb.ac.in/media/1749801072_11%20%20Adhunik%20Punjabi%202025.pdf)
and [PSEB Class 5](https://static.pseb.ac.in/media/1687845902_Punjabi%20Pustak-5%28Pehli%20Bhasha%29.pdf).

The exact origin of modern written **ਯਾਤਰੀ** remains unknown in the cited
sources. Its origin remains unknown here. This lesson therefore makes no
etymological claim. Keep the present-day noun whole rather than turning a
spelling resemblance into a derivation.
