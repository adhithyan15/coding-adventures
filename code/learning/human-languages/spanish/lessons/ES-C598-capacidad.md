## You'll want to know: la capacidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C598-SABER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la capacidad** — "the ability, the capacity".

> Tiene capacidad para dirigir.
>
> *She has the ability to lead.*

Also how much a place holds: *capacidad para cien personas*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*capacidad* is a learned borrowing from Latin *capacitas* 'room, capacity', built on *capax* 'able to hold', from *capere* 'to take' (EtymDB).
