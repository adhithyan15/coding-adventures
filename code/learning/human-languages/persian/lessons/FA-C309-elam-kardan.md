## You'll want to know: اعلام کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C309-SOKHANGU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اعلام کردن** (*e'lâm kardan*) — "to announce".

> مدرسه اعلام کرد که فردا تعطیل است.
>
> *Madrese e'lâm kard ke fardâ ta'til ast.*
>
> The school announced that it would be closed tomorrow.

Present stem *kon-*: *e'lâm mikonad*. The passive *e'lâm shod* is "it was announced".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*e'lâm* is borrowed from Arabic *i'lām*, "notice, instruction" (EtymDB). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
