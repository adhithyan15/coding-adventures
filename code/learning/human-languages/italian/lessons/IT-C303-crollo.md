## You'll want to know: il crollo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C303-ESPLOSIONE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il crollo** — "the collapse".

> Il crollo del ponte ha bloccato la città.
>
> *The collapse of the bridge brought the city to a halt.*

Also for prices: *il crollo della borsa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crollo* is built on *crollare* "to collapse", which is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin *corrotulāre* "to roll down" (Lewis & Short gives *rotula* "a small wheel").
