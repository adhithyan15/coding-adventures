# ذعر (dhuʿr) — panic, alarm

## You'll want to know: ذعر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C489-KHAWF489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ذعر** — *dhuʿr* — "panic, alarm".

> أصاب الناس ذعر بعد الزلزال.
>
> *aṣāba an-nās dhuʿr baʿda az-zilzāl.*
>
> Panic seized the people after the earthquake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذعر** (*dhuʿr*) is built on the native Arabic root *dh-ʿ-r*, "to frighten, to alarm" (Lane). Dhuʿr is a sudden fright that runs through a crowd.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
