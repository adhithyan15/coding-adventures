## You'll want to know: निष्कः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C435-DINARA435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कः** — *niṣkaḥ* — "a gold piece".

> राजा कवये शतं निष्कान् ददाति।
>
> *rājā kavaye śataṃ niṣkān dadāti.*
>
> The king gives the poet a hundred gold pieces.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कः** (*niṣkaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a gold ornament for the neck and a coin or weight of gold; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
