## You'll want to know: der Nacken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C485-GELENKE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Nacken** — "back of the neck, nape".

> Nach der Arbeit am Computer tut mir der Nacken weh.
>
> *After working at the computer my neck hurts.*

Plural: *die Nacken*. *Der Hals* is the whole neck; *der Nacken* is the back of it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Nacken* is inherited from Middle High German *nacke*, from Proto-Germanic *hnakkô* "nape, neck" (EtymDB).
