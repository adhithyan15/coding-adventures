## You'll want to know: lùkǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-04]; assesses=[] -->

*lùkǒu* — "crossroads; junction".

> *yínháng zài lùkǒu* — The bank is at the crossroads.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lùkǒu* is built from lù 'road' and 口 'mouth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *luH* and *khuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the mouth of a road.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
