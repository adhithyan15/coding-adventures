## You'll want to know: прилив
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C463-PRILIV-01]; assesses=[] -->

**прилив** (*prilív*) — "a high tide, a flood tide; a surge".

> Во время прилива вода подходит к дороге.
>
> *Vo vrémya prilíva vodá podkhódit k doróge.*
>
> At high tide the water comes up to the road.

Masculine. *Прилив сил* — a surge of energy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прилив* is built from *при-* 'up to' and *-лив* 'a pouring', from *лить* 'to pour', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic *liti*.
