## You'll want to know: કબજો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C324-SAMRAT324-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કબજો** — *kabjo* — "control; possession".

> દુશ્મને કિલ્લા પર કબજો કર્યો.
>
> *duśmane killā par kabjo karyo.*
>
> The enemy took control of the fort.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કબજો** (*kabjo*) is usually traced to Persian *qabza*, "a grasp, a hold", a word Persian took from Arabic. A place is taken with **પર**: **કિલ્લા પર કબજો કર્યો**.
