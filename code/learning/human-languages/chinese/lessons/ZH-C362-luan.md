## You'll want to know: luàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C362-MUST362-03]; assesses=[] -->

*luàn* — "messy; in a mess".

> *nǐ de fángjiān tài luàn le* — Your room is far too messy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*luàn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *lwanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it both 'to put in order' and 'disorder'.
