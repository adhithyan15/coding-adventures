## You'll want to know: さすが
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C529-ANYWAY529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**さすが** — *sasuga* — "as one would expect".

> **もし あの せんしゅが かったら、さすがでしょう。** — *moshi ano senshu ga kattara, sasuga deshō* — "If that athlete wins, it will be what you would expect of her."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さすが** (*sasuga*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
