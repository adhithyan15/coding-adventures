Japanese, chapter 257: Something Went Wrong.
5 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
なくしました / こわれました (nakushimashita / kowaremashita) — something went wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "to lose" and "to break down". (nakusu, kowareru.) Then "a wallet". (saifu.)

You'll want to know: を なくしました, が こわれました.
さいふを なくしました。 saifu o nakushimashita. — "I have lost my wallet."
なくす is something you did, so the lost thing takes を. こわれる is something that happened by itself, so the broken thing takes が:
かさが こわれました。 kasa ga kowaremashita. — "My umbrella broke."
きのう かぎを なくしました。 kinō kagi o nakushimashita. — "I lost my key yesterday."
でんわが こわれました。 denwa ga kowaremashita. — "The telephone stopped working."

You'll want to know: たすけて ください.
たすけて ください (tasukete kudasai), the -て form of たすける with ください, is "please help me":
たすけて ください。 tasukete kudasai. — "Please help me."
さいふを なくしたので、こまっています。 saifu o nakushita node, komatte imasu. — "I have lost my wallet, so I am in trouble."
こうばんは どこですか。 kōban wa doko desu ka. — "Where is the police box?"

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I have lost my key" — kagi o nakushimashita]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I have lost my ticket" — kippu o nakushimashita]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I have lost my bag" — kaban o nakushimashita]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say that you have lost your wallet.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"My umbrella broke"? (かさが こわれました: it happened by itself, so が.) "I lost my key"? (かぎを なくしました.) "Please help me"? (たすけて ください.)


Lesson 2 of 5.
のりおくれる (noriokureru) — to miss (a train or bus).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say "to ride" and "to be late". (noru, okureru.)

You'll want to know: のりおくれる.
のりおくれる — noriokureru — "to miss" a train or a bus: to be too late to get on. Polite のりおくれます, noriokuremasu; an ichidan verb, like おくれる. Past (polite): でんしゃに のりおくれました, densha ni noriokuremashita.
でんしゃに のりおくれました。 densha ni noriokuremashita. — "I missed the train."
あさねぼうを しました。 asanebō o shimashita. — "I overslept."
それで、でんしゃに のりおくれました。 sore de, densha ni noriokuremashita. — "And so I missed the train."

The word, taken apart.
のりおくれる (noriokureru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: the stem of のる (noru), "to ride", and おくれる (okureru), "to be late". JMdict glosses it "to miss (a train, bus, etc.)" (JMdict).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: noriokureru]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I missed the train" — densha ni noriokuremashita]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I missed the subway" — chikatetsu ni noriokuremashita]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that you missed the train.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"To miss a train"? (のりおくれる (noriokureru).) Which particle goes with the train? (に.)


Lesson 3 of 5.
日本中 (nihonjū) — all over Japan.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: You'll want to know: 日本中.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say "a Japanese person" and "all year round". (nihonjin, nenjū.)

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
You'll want to know: 日本中.
日本中 — nihonjū — "all over Japan", the whole country through. The じゅう at its end is the one in 年じゅう.
日本中に ともだちが います。 nihonjū ni tomodachi ga imasu. — "I have friends all over Japan."
You write 日本. The third sign is new, read じゅう (jū) here; the next lesson writes it.

The word, taken apart.
日本中 (nihonjū) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ni, "sun" (Middle Chinese nyit), hon, "origin" (pwonX) and jū, "in, inside, middle" (trjuwng), the voiced chū of 年じゅう (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict glosses it "throughout Japan" (JMdict).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nihonjū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I have friends all over Japan" — nihonjū ni tomodachi ga imasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "There are shrines all over Japan" — nihonjū ni jinja ga arimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Japanese for 'all over Japan'.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"All over Japan"? (日本中, nihonjū.) How is the new sign read here? (じゅう, jū.)


Lesson 4 of 5.
中 (jū) — the middle, four strokes.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Script — the shape and Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say nihonjū, all over Japan. Then call up 口, a mouth: three strokes, a box.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — the shape.
中 — jū.
Its parts:
口 (box).
a long centre stem through it.
Four strokes, three lifts:
draw the left side of the box from the top down.
lift, draw the top from left to right, then without lifting turn down the right side.
lift, close the base of the box from left to right.
lift, draw the long centre stem from above the box straight down through it.
The box is 口, wider than it is tall, and the stem comes last, running through it from above to below. Here it is read じゅう (jū), as in 日本中 (nihonjū); this lesson asks only for that reading.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
Find 中 (jū) in 日本中 and say nihonjū.
Copy 中 three times, saying jū at each one.
Find it once more, in 日本中に ともだちが います (nihonjū ni tomodachi ga imasu), and say the line.

Wrap-up Recall.
Which sign do you know inside it? (口.)
