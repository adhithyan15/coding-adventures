## You'll want to know: singultus, singultūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C520-SPIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**singultus, singultūs** — "a sob; a hiccup".

> Puella inter singultūs loquitur.
>
> *The girl speaks between sobs.*

Masculine, fourth declension. Breath broken by weeping, or a hiccup.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**singultus** is usually traced to *singulī* "one at a time" (Lewis & Short): speech broken into single sounds.
