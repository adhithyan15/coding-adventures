## You'll want to know: हाड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C252-HAD252-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हाड** — *hāḍ* — "a bone".

> म्हारो हाड कोनी टूट्यो।
>
> *mhāro hāḍ konī ṭūṭyo.*
>
> My bone did not break.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हाड** (*hāḍ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *haḍḍa*, "bone"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *hāḍī* under it (CDIAL 13952).
