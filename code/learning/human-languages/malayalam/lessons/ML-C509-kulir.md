## You'll want to know: കുളിർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C509-CUTU509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**കുളിർ** — *kuḷir* — "a chill; coolness".

> പുലർച്ചെ മലയിൽ നല്ല കുളിർ ആയിരുന്നു.
>
> *pularcce malayil nalla kuḷir āyirunnu.*
>
> At dawn it was very chilly in the hills.

A pleasant coolness, as of a morning breeze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുളിർ** (*kuḷir*) is native Dravidian, "coolness, chill" (DEDR 1834).
