## You'll want to know: じょうきゃく

**じょうきゃく** — *jōkyaku* — "a passenger".

> **ふねの じょうきゃくは すくないです。** — *fune no jōkyaku wa sukunai desu* — "The ship has few passengers."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうきゃく** (*jōkyaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jō*, "to ride" (Middle Chinese *zying*) and *kyaku*, "guest" (*khaek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
