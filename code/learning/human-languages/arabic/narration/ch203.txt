Arabic, chapter 203: Previously, Finally, During, Century, Weekly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سابقا (sābiqan) — previously, formerly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a date; history, then the Arabic for a moment.

You'll want to know: سابقا.
سابقا — sābiqan — "previously, formerly".

The word, taken apart.
سابقا (sābiqan) is built on the native Arabic root s-b-q, "to go before, to outstrip" (Lane). Sābiqan is the active participle, "preceding", with the adverb ending -an.

Lesson 3 of 5.
أثناء (athnāʾa) — during.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for previously, then the Arabic for finally.

You'll want to know: أثناء.
أثناء — athnāʾa — "during".

The word, taken apart.
أثناء (athnāʾa) is built on the native Arabic root th-n-y, "to fold, to bend; two" (Lane). Athnāʾ is a plural, "folds, intervals": "during" is "in the folds of".

Lesson 4 of 5.
قرن (qarn) — a century.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for finally, then the Arabic for during.

You'll want to know: قرن.
قرن — qarn — "a century".

The word, taken apart.
قرن (qarn) is a native noun on the root q-r-n, "to join, to couple" (Lane). Lane's qarn is a horn and a generation of men; the century is a later sense.
