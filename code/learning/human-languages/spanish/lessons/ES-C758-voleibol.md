## You'll want to know: el voleibol
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C758-DEPORTES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el voleibol** — "volleyball".

> Jugamos al voleibol en la playa.
>
> *We play volleyball on the beach.*

Also *el vóley*. In some countries *el vóleibol*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*voleibol* was borrowed from English *volleyball* (EtymDB).
