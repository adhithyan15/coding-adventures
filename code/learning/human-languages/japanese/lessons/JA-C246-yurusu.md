## You'll want to know: ゆるす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C246-ANGER246-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆるす** — *yurusu* — "to forgive; to allow".

> **いもうとは おとうとを ゆるしますか。** — *imōto wa otōto o yurushimasu ka* — "Does my younger sister forgive my younger brother?"

Polite **ゆるします**, *yurushimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆるす** (*yurusu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
