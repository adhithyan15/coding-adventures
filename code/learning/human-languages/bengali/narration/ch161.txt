Bengali, chapter 161: Polite, Gentle, Strict, Contented, Annoyed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ভদ্র (bhôdrô) — polite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for worried, then the Bengali for elderly.

You'll want to know: ভদ্র.
ভদ্র — bhôdrô — "polite".

The word, taken apart.
ভদ্র (bhôdrô) is Sanskrit bhadra, "fortunate, good", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 9377).

Lesson 3 of 5.
কড়া (kôṛā) — strict; strong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for polite, then the Bengali for gentle.

You'll want to know: কড়া.
কড়া — kôṛā — "strict; strong".

The word, taken apart.
কড়া (kôṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kaḍḍati, "is rough" (CDIAL 2657). CDIAL lists Bengali kaṛā, "rough".
