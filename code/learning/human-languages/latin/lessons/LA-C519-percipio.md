## You'll want to know: percipiō, percipere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C519-SENS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**percipiō, percipere** — "to perceive, take in".

> Oculī nostrī lūcem percipiunt.
>
> *Our eyes perceive light.*

*percipiō, percipere, percēpī, perceptum*: *per* 'thoroughly' + *capiō* 'to take'. English *perceive*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**percipiō** is built on *per-* "thoroughly" and *capiō* "to take" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *perceive* and *perception* come from it.
