# رحل (raḥala) — to move out; to depart

## You'll want to know: رحل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C332-HIJRA332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رحل** — *raḥala* — "to move out; to depart".

> رحل الجيران عن الحي الشهر الماضي.
>
> *raḥala al-jīrān ʿan al-ḥayy ash-shahr al-māḍī.*
>
> The neighbours moved out of the area last month.

Present: يرحل, *yarḥal*.

Of a person who died: رحل عنا, he has left us.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رحل** (*raḥala*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ḥ-l* (Lane), where *raḥl* is a camel saddle and *raḥala*, "to set off"; the taught *riḥla*, a journey, shares it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
