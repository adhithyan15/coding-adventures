## You'll want to know: کیسه خواب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C470-CHADOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کیسه خواب** (*kise-khâb*) — "a sleeping bag".

> شب سرد بود و در کیسه خواب لرزیدیم.
>
> *Shab sard bud va dar kise-khâb larzidim.*
>
> The night was cold and we shivered in our sleeping bags.

*kise* 'bag' + *khâb* 'sleep'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kise-khâb* is a native compound of *kise* 'bag', from Middle Persian *kīsag* (EtymDB), and *khâb* 'sleep', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian.
