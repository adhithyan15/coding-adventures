## You'll want to know: इतिवृत्तम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C324-PURA324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इतिवृत्तम्** — *itivṛttam* — "a chronicle; an account of events".

> एतत् पुस्तकं राज्यस्य इतिवृत्तं वर्णयति।
>
> *etat pustakaṃ rājyasya itivṛttaṃ varṇayati.*
>
> This book tells the chronicle of the kingdom.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इतिवृत्तम्** (*itivṛttam*) is built from *iti*, "thus", and *vṛtta*, "happened": what happened thus; MW gives *itivṛtta* as an occurrence, an event, and a chronicle is its modern use (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
