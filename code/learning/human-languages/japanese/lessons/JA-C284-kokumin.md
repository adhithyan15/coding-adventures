## You'll want to know: こくみん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C284-SENKYO284-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こくみん** — *kokumin* — "the people (of a country); citizens".

> **せんきょで こくみんが せいふを えらびます。** — *senkyo de kokumin ga seifu o erabimasu* — "In an election the people choose the government."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こくみん** (*kokumin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *koku*, "country" (Middle Chinese *kwok*) and *min*, "people" (*mjin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
