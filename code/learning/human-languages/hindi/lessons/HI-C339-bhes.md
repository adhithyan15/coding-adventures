## You'll want to know: भेस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C339-RAJKUMAR339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भेस** — *bhes* — "a disguise; a guise".

> राजकुमार ने किसान का भेस बनाया।
>
> *rājkumār ne kisān kā bhes banāyā.*
>
> The prince disguised himself as a farmer.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भेस** (*bhes*) goes back to Sanskrit *veṣa*, "dress, appearance" (CDIAL 12129), where CDIAL lists Hindi *bhes*. **भेस बदलना** is to go in disguise.
