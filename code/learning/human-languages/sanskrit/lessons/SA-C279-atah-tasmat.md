# अतः · तस्मात् (ataḥ · tasmāt) — so, and therefore

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-EXPLANATION-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "you should go to the doctor". (*bhavān vaidyaṃ gacchet*.) Now say why, and what follows.

## You'll want to know: अतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SO-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C98-WHY98-01, SA-LEX-C243-QUAL243-02, SA-LEX-C76-WEATHER-01] -->

**यतः** gives the reason after the event; **अतः** (*ataḥ*), "so", gives the result after the reason:

> **अहं रुग्णः अस्मि।**
>
> *ahaṃ rugṇaḥ asmi.* — "I am ill."

> **अतः अहं न आगमिष्यामि।**
>
> *ataḥ ahaṃ na āgamiṣyāmi.* — "So I will not come."

> **वर्षा भवति, अतः सः गृहे अस्ति।**
>
> *varṣā bhavati, ataḥ saḥ gṛhe asti.* — "It is raining, so he is at home."

## You'll want to know: तस्मात्, ततः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SO-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C98-WHY98-02, SA-LEX-C153-LOC153-02, SA-LEX-C157-QUAL157-04] -->

**तस्मात्** (*tasmāt*), "from that", says the same with more weight: "therefore". **ततः** (*tataḥ*), "then" in a story, can also mean "so":

> **सा श्रान्ता अस्ति।**
>
> *sā śrāntā asti.* — "She is tired."

> **तस्मात् सा गृहे तिष्ठति।**
>
> *tasmāt sā gṛhe tiṣṭhati.* — "Therefore she is staying at home."

> **धूमयानं न आगतम्, ततः वयं गतवन्तः।**
>
> *dhūmayānaṃ na āgatam, tataḥ vayaṃ gatavantaḥ.* — "The train did not come, so we left."

**आगतम्** (*āgatam*) is **आगतः**, "come", for a neuter noun such as **धूमयानम्**.
