## You'll want to know: नमुना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C373-PADDHAT373-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नमुना** — *namunā* — "a sample; a specimen".

> प्रयोगासाठी नदीच्या पाण्याचा नमुना घेतला.
>
> *prayogāsāṭhī nadīcyā pāṇyācā namunā ghetlā.*
>
> A sample of river water was taken for the experiment.

Masculine. Plural: नमुने, *namune*.

Plural नमुने; also a model, a pattern.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नमुना** (*namunā*) is a loan from Persian *namūna*, "a sample, a pattern".
