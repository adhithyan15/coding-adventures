Marathi, chapter 25: The Twelve, Further Apart.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
The twelve, further apart — twelve verbs, at a wider distance — the second pass over the whole run.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
The four you ask for: देणे, पिणे, आणणे, ठेवणे. Say them without looking anywhere.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
Then the four that pass between two people: sāṅgṇe, म्हणणे, शिकणे, मिळणे.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Name the four verbs of a visit: sitting, feeling, sleeping, walking.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Twelve verbs, three runs of four. Say the one you would use to accept an offer. (Chālel.) Say the one you would use to ask for water. (Dyā.) Then stop.


Lesson 2 of 4.
One frame, four sentences — the dative frame, four sentences deep — knowing, liking, feeling, getting.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
मला is where things arrive. Say the two oldest ones: मला माहीत आहे, मला मराठी आवडते.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
Say each one, then say the word-for-word English under it.
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say that you feel fine, that you are sleepy, and that you get it — all on the mala frame.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does मला do in every one of these? (It is where the knowing, the liking, the feeling and the getting arrive.) And name the body word this book gave you for where feeling is said to sit. (हृदय.) Then stop.


Lesson 3 of 4.
Recognition at distance: two signs and one sound — two signs from the very first doorway and one sound, recognised at a long distance.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
The first two shapes this book ever asked you to make were ह and the ो mark, and together they wrote हो. Make them both from memory.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
Say चालणे and चार one after the other and hear the same leaning in both.
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
How is cha said before a long aa in most Marathi mouths?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Write हो once without a model, say चार with its leaning consonant, then stop.


Lesson 4 of 4.
Recognition at distance: five things from further back — the copula, the two family words, counting, and understanding — recognised at a long distance.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
Count to five out loud — ek, don, tīn, chār, pāch — and then put आहे at the end of a sentence about yourself, where Marathi keeps it.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
Both were taken up from Sanskrit whole rather than worn down, and कुटुंब takes a gender Hindi does not give it.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Give friend and family in Marathi, and say which gender kutumb takes.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say that Marathi is understandable to you (मला मराठी समजते) and then that you are learning it (मी मराठी शिकतो / शिकते). What did मित्र's root mean before it meant friend? (To bind.) Then stop.
