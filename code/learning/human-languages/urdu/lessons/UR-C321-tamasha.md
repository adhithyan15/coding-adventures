## You'll want to know: تماشا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C321-MELA321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تماشا** — *tamāshā* — "a show; a spectacle".

> میلے میں بندر کا تماشا تھا۔
>
> *mele meṅ bandar kā tamāshā thā.*
>
> There was a monkey show at the fair.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تماشا** (*tamāshā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tamāshā*, "a walking about together", and so a sight people gather to watch.
