## You'll want to know: راحت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C297-RAHAT297-02]; assesses=[] -->

**راحت** — *rāhat* — "relief".

> کیمپ میں لوگوں کو راحت ملی۔
>
> *kaimp meṅ logoṅ ko rāhat milī.*
>
> People found relief in the camp.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**راحت** (*rāhat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rāḥa*, "rest, ease". It is feminine: **راحت ملی**.
