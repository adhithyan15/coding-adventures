## You'll want to know: بارك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C336-HADIYYA336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بارك** — *bāraka* — "to bless; to congratulate".

> بارك الأصدقاء للعروسين.
>
> *bāraka al-aṣdiqāʾ li-l-ʿarūsayn.*
>
> The friends congratulated the bride and groom.

Present: يبارك, *yubārik*.

بارك لـ someone: to congratulate them. The wish مبروك is the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بارك** (*bāraka*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-r-k* (Lane), said of a camel that kneels and stays. *Baraka*, a blessing, is good that stays; Form III *bāraka* is "to wish blessing on".
