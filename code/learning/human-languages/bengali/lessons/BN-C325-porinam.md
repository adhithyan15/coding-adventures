## You'll want to know: পরিণাম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C325-UPODESH325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিণাম** — *pôrinām* — "an outcome; a consequence".

> লোভের পরিণাম ভালো হয় না।
>
> *lobher pôrinām bhālo hôy nā.*
>
> Greed does not end well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিণাম** (*pôrinām*) is Sanskrit *pariṇāma*, 'change, development, ripening' (Monier-Williams): what a thing turns into in the end.
