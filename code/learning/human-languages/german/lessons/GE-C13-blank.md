# blank — "shiny"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WEISS-01, GE-ETYMON-WEISS-WHITE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A word that looks English, is German, and means neither of the
things you would guess.

## You'll want to know: blank
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-BLANK-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WEISS-01] -->

> **blank** — "shiny," "polished," "bare."

Not "blank" in the English sense of empty-of-writing, and **not** a colour. A
polished shoe is *blank*; a bare sword is *blank*. The idea underneath is
**shining**.

Hold on to that meaning exactly as it is, because the next lesson is about what
happened when the neighbours borrowed it and let it drift.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-BLANK-01] -->

- `vowel-a` — short and clipped, the *a* of English *hat*.
- `ng-cluster` — the *-nk* closes with the back-of-the-mouth *n* that English
  puts in *bank*, and then a real *k*.
