## You'll want to know: उद्देश्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C341-PRAYOJANA341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्देश्यम्** — *uddeśyam* — "an aim".

> मम उद्देश्यं स्पष्टम् अस्ति।
>
> *mama uddeśyaṃ spaṣṭam asti.*
>
> My aim is clear.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्देश्यम्** (*uddeśyam*) is built on *ud*, "up", and the root √diś, "to point out"; MW gives *ud-deśya* as "anything which one has in view", and "an aim" is the sense modern Sanskrit gives it (MW).
