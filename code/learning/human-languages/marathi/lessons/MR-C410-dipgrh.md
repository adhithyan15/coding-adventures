## You'll want to know: दीपगृह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C410-KHADI410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपगृह** — *dīpgṛh* — "a lighthouse".

> रात्री दीपगृहाचा दिवा दूरवर दिसतो.
>
> *rātrī dīpgṛhācā divā dūrvar disto.*
>
> At night the lighthouse lamp can be seen far away.

Neuter.

दीप is a lamp and गृह a house: a lamp house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपगृह** (*dīpgṛh*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *dīpa*, "a lamp", and *gṛha*, "a house" (Monier-Williams).
