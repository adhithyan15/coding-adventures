## You'll want to know: a náusea
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C484-ENXAQUECA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a náusea** — "nausea".

> O cheiro do peixe deu-lhe náuseas.
>
> *The smell of the fish made her feel sick.*

Often plural: *ter náuseas*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*náusea* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nausea* (EtymDB), from Greek *nausía* "seasickness", built on *naûs* "ship".
