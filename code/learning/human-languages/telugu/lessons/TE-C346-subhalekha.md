# శుభలేఖ (śubhalēkha) — a wedding invitation

## You'll want to know: శుభలేఖ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C346-VIVAHAM346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**శుభలేఖ** — *śubhalēkha* — "a wedding invitation".

> మా బంధువులు శుభలేఖ పంపించారు.
>
> *mā bandhuvulu śubhalēkha pampiñcāru.*
>
> Our relatives sent a wedding invitation.

శుభ, auspicious, and లేఖ, a letter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శుభలేఖ** (*śubhalēkha*) is a compound borrowed from Sanskrit: *śubha*, "bright, auspicious" (CDIAL 12532), and *lekhā*, "a line, a stroke" (Monier-Williams).
