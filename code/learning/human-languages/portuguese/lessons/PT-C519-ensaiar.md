## You'll want to know: ensaiar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C519-ENSAIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ensaiar** — "to rehearse".

> A banda ensaia na garagem.
>
> *The band rehearses in the garage.*

Built on *o ensaio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ensaiar* is built on *ensaio* "a trial, a rehearsal", inherited from Late Latin *exagium* "a weighing" (EtymDB).
