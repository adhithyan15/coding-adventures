## Script — the roof piece over 大
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QING-CELEBRATE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 庆

**Two pieces: the roof first, then what sits under it.**

**The roof piece, three strokes,** as on 店: a dot, a bar, then a long stroke falling to the left down the side.

**The piece under it, 大, three strokes:** a bar, a stroke falling to the left through it, then a stroke falling to the right.

3 + 3 = six.
