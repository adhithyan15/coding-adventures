## You'll want to know: వేతనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C525-SAMPADANA525-04]; assesses=[] -->

**వేతనం** — *vētanaṁ* — "a wage; pay".

> కార్మికులు ఎక్కువ వేతనం కోరారు.
>
> *kārmikulu ekkuva vētanaṁ kōrāru.*
>
> The workers asked for higher wages.

Formal; జీతం is the everyday word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వేతనం** (*vētanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vetana*, "wages, hire, salary, livelihood" (Monier-Williams).
