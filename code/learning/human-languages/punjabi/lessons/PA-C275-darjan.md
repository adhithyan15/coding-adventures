## You'll want to know: ਦਰਜਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C275-FIT275-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਰਜਨ** — *darjan* — "a dozen".

> ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਦਰਜਨ ਕੇਲੇ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ ikk darjan kele kharīdāṅgā.*
>
> I will buy a dozen bananas.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਰਜਨ** (*darjan*) is borrowed from English *dozen*.
