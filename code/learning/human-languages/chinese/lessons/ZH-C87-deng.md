## You'll want to know: dēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C87-DENG-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dēng* — "lamp; light".

> *kāi dēng* — Switch on the light.

> *guān dēng* — Switch off the light.

Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tong* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a lamp'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-04, ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-05] -->

*kāi* and *guān* open and shut doors, and the same two verbs switch a light on and off.
