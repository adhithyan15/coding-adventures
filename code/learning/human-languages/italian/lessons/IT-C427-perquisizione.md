## You'll want to know: la perquisizione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C427-DOGANA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la perquisizione** — "the search (by police or security)".

> La perquisizione è durata dieci minuti.
>
> *The search took ten minutes.*

*Un mandato di perquisizione* — a search warrant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perquisizione* is built on *perquisire* 'to search', a learned borrowing from Latin *perquīrere* 'to search everywhere', from *quaerere* 'to seek' (Lewis & Short).
