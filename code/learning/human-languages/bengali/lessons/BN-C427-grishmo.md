## You'll want to know: গ্রীষ্ম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C427-GRISHMO427-01]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রীষ্ম** — *grīshmô* — "summer; the hot season".

> গ্রীষ্মে দুপুরে রাস্তা ফাঁকা থাকে।
>
> *grīshme dupure rāstā phā̃kā thāke.*
>
> In summer the streets are empty at midday.

The calendar word; in talk people say গরম, the heat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রীষ্ম** (*grīshmô*) is Sanskrit *grīṣma*, "summer, the hot season" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.
