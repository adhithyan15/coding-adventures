## You'll want to know: দায়িত্ব
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C339-ODHIKAR339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**দায়িত্ব** — *dāyitvô* — "a responsibility".

> পরিবারকে সাহায্য করা আমার দায়িত্ব।
>
> *pôribārke sāhājjo kôrā āmār dāyitvô.*
>
> It is my responsibility to help my family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দায়িত্ব** (*dāyitvô*) is a learned formation: Sanskrit *dāyin*, which Monier-Williams gives as giving and also as owing or having to pay, with the abstract ending *-tva*: the state of owing something to others. The word itself is not in the dictionary.
