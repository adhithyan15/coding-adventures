Portuguese, chapter 510: Ganhar, empatar, perder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
empatar — to draw, to tie.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to shoot, then the Portuguese for to head.

You'll want to know: empatar.
empatar — "to draw, to tie".
As duas equipas empataram a zero. The two teams drew nil-nil.
Empatar a um — to draw one-all.

The word, taken apart.
empatar was borrowed from Spanish empatar (EtymDB), which goes back to Italian patta "a draw".

A equipa da casa derrotou o campeão. The home team beat the champions.

The word, taken apart.
derrotar is linked in EtymDB with French dérouter "to throw off course, to rout", built on route "a road".
