## You'll want to know: तुरुंग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C290-POLICE290-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तुरुंग** — *turuṅg* — "a prison; a jail".

> न्यायाधीशांनी त्या चोराला तुरुंगात पाठवलं.
>
> *nyāyādhīśānnī tyā corālā turuṅgāt pāṭhavlaṁ.*
>
> The judge sent that thief to prison.

Masculine.

तुरुंगात, in prison; तुरुंगातून, out of prison.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तुरुंग** (*turuṅg*) is the everyday Marathi word for a jail; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
