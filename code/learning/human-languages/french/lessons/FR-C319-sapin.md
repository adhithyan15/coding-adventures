## You'll want to know: le sapin
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C319-REVEILLON-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le sapin** — "the fir tree; the Christmas tree".

> Les enfants décorent le sapin.
>
> *The children decorate the Christmas tree.*

Decorated in December, often with *des guirlandes*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sapin* is inherited from Latin *sappīnus* "a fir tree" (EtymDB), probably of Gaulish origin.
