## You'll want to know: शिष्यवृत्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C369-VIDYAPITH369-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिष्यवृत्ती** — *śiṣyavṛttī* — "a scholarship".

> हुशार विद्यार्थ्यांना शिष्यवृत्ती मिळते.
>
> *huśār vidyārthyānnā śiṣyavṛttī miḷte.*
>
> Bright students get a scholarship.

Feminine.

शिष्य + वृत्ती, pupil + livelihood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिष्यवृत्ती** (*śiṣyavṛttī*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *śiṣya*, "pupil", and *vṛtti*, "way of life, livelihood" (Monier-Williams): a pupil's living.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
