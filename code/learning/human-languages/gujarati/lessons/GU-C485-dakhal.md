# દાખલ (dākhal) — admitted (to hospital or school)

## You'll want to know: દાખલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C485-DAKHAL485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**દાખલ** — *dākhal* — "admitted (to hospital or school)".

> મીરા બે દિવસ હોસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ હતી.
>
> *Mīrā be divas hospiṭalmā̃ dākhal hatī.*
>
> Mira was in hospital for two days.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાખલ** (*dākhal*) is usually traced to Persian *dāxil*, "entered, inside", which comes from Arabic.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
