## You'll want to know: વેવાણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C491-JAMAI491-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વેવાણ** — *vevāṇ* — "the mother-in-law of one's child".

> વેવાણે મહેમાનોનું ઉમળકાથી સ્વાગત કર્યું.
>
> *vevāṇe mahemānonũ umaḷkāthī svāgat karyũ.*
>
> Our child's mother-in-law welcomed the guests warmly.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વેવાણ** (*vevāṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vivāhin*, "connected by marriage"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "son's or daughter's mother-in-law" (CDIAL 11925).
