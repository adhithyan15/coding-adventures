## You'll want to know: la solidarité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C366-BENEVOLAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la solidarité** — "the solidarity".

> Après l'incendie, il y a eu beaucoup de solidarité dans le village.
>
> *After the fire, there was a lot of solidarity in the village.*

From *solidaire*, standing together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*solidarité* is built on *solidaire* (EtymDB), from the Latin legal phrase *in solidum* "for the whole sum", from *solidus* "solid, whole" (Lewis & Short).
