## You'll want to know: ग़लत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C112-RIGHT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ग़लत** (*ġalat*) — "wrong".

The opposite of **सही**. **यह ग़लत है** — "this is wrong".

## What you've built
