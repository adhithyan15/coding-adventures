## You'll want to know: el monte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C685-PAISAJE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el monte** — "the mountain, the hill; the woodland".

> Subimos al monte por la mañana.
>
> *We went up the mountain in the morning.*

*El monte* is also wild land with trees and bushes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*monte* is inherited from Latin *montem*, a form of *mōns* "a mountain" (EtymDB).
