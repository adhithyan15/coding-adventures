Gujarati, chapter 378: Within Limits.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
હદ (had) — a limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to become different, then the Gujarati for to worsen.

You'll want to know: હદ.
હદ — had — "a limit".
જો તમે હદ ઓળંગશો તો હું ગુરુને ફરિયાદ કરીશ. jo tame had oḷangśo to hũ gurune phariyād karīś. If you cross the line, I will complain to the teacher.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હદ (had) is usually traced to Arabic ḥadd, "a boundary, a limit", through Persian.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 6.
મર્યાદા (maryādā) — propriety; a bound one keeps to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to worsen, then the Gujarati for a limit.

You'll want to know: મર્યાદા.
મર્યાદા — maryādā — "propriety; a bound one keeps to".
જો તમે મર્યાદામાં રહીને બોલશો તો બધા સાંભળશે. jo tame maryādāmā̃ rahīne bolśo to badhā sā̃bhaḷśe. If you speak within bounds, everyone will listen.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મર્યાદા (maryādā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit maryādā, "a frontier, a bound" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati also uses it for the bounds of decent behaviour.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ખમવું (khamvũ) — to tolerate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for excessive, then the Gujarati for a proportion.

You'll want to know: ખમવું.
ખમવું — khamvũ — "to tolerate".
જો તે ફરી ખોટું બોલશે તો હું નહીં ખમું. jo te pharī khoṭũ bolśe to hũ nahī̃ khamũ. If he lies again, I will not stand for it.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગુરુનો ઠપકો ખમ્યો, teṇe guruno ṭhapko khamyo.

The word, taken apart.
ખમવું (khamvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit kṣámatē, "is patient, endures"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 3657).
