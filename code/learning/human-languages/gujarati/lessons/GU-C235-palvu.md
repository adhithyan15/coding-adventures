# પાળવું (pāḷvũ) — to keep a pet, to rear; to obey

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C235-DOG235-01, GU-LEX-C235-DOG235-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to bark, then the Gujarati for to bite.

## You'll want to know: પાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C235-DOG235-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પાળવું** — *pāḷvũ* — "to keep a pet, to rear; to obey".

> **તેણે એક કૂતરો પાળ્યો.** — *teṇe ek kūtro pāḷyo* — "He kept a dog."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાળવું** (*pāḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *pālayati*, "guards, protects", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to rear, keep" (CDIAL 8129).
