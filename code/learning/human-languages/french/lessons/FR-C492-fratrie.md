## You'll want to know: la fratrie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C492-FAMILLES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la fratrie** — "siblings (as a group)".

> Dans ma fratrie, nous sommes quatre.
>
> *There are four of us siblings.*

A useful word with no plain everyday English match: all the brothers and sisters of one family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la fratrie* is a modern learned word built on Latin *frāter* "brother" (Lewis & Short).
