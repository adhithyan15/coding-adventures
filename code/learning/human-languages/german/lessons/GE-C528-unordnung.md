## You'll want to know: die Unordnung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C528-MUELL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Unordnung** — "mess, disorder".

> In seinem Zimmer herrscht immer Unordnung.
>
> *His room is forever in a mess.*

No plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Unordnung* is built on *un-* "not" and *Ordnung* "order", inherited from Old High German *ordinunga* (EtymDB), built on a verb borrowed from Latin *ōrdināre*, from *ōrdō* "row, order".
