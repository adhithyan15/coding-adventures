## You'll want to know: جھلکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C515-TASVIR515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جھلکنا** — *jhalaknā* — "to show through; to glint".

> پانی میں چاند جھلکتا ہے۔
>
> *pānī meṅ chā̃d jhalaktā hai.*
>
> The moon glints in the water.

Past (it …): وہ چاند پانی میں جھلکا, *voh chā̃d pānī meṅ jhalkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھلکنا** (*jhalaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhalaknā*, "to sparkle", under *\*jhal*, "flash", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5352).
