## You'll want to know: अक्षता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C317-AHER317-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अक्षता** — *akṣatā* — "blessing rice".

> पाहुण्यांनी नवरा नवरीवर अक्षता टाकल्या.
>
> *pāhuṇyānnī navrā navrīvar akṣatā ṭākalyā.*
>
> The guests showered blessing rice over the bride and groom.

Feminine.

A feminine plural: whole grains of rice, coloured with turmeric or kumkum, showered on a couple as a blessing or offered in worship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अक्षता** (*akṣatā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *akṣata*, 'unbroken; unhusked grain' (Monier-Williams): the grains are kept whole.
