# ਢ — one letter from the verb to take out

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KADDHNA-219] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਕੱਢਣਾ**, "to take out", the verb from the previous lesson.

## Script you'll notice: ਢ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDHA-219]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KADDHNA-219, PA-SCRIPT-DDA-01] -->

> **ਕ** + **ੱ** + **ਢ** + **ਣ** + **ਾ** → **ਕੱਢਣਾ**

> **ਢ** — *ḍha* — a Gurmukhi consonant

Keep **ਢ** in view. It is the third piece of **ਕੱਢਣਾ**, after the doubling sign
**ੱ**. It belongs to the same row as **ਡ**, which you know.

No stroke order is taught here: the track does not yet have a verified source
for one.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDHA-219] -->

Keep **ਢ** in front of you. Trace its printed shape once with a finger, then
copy it once with a pencil while the model stays visible. Say *ḍha* when you
finish.

> This is observe-and-copy with the model visible, not writing from memory.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KADDHNA-219, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDHA-219] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point to **ਢ**, say *ḍha*, then say the whole word **ਕੱਢਣਾ**.
