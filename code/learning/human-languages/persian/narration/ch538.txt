Persian, chapter 538: Renting and Appliances.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مستاجر (mosta'jer) — a tenant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to break down, then the Persian for a landlord.

You'll want to know: مستاجر.
مستاجر (mosta'jer) — "a tenant".
مستاجر طبقه بالا خیلی ساکت است. mosta'jer-e tabaqe-ye bâlâ kheyli sâkat ast. The tenant upstairs is very quiet.
Plural mosta'jerân. The opposite is sâhebkhâne.

The word, taken apart.
mosta'jer is a loan, usually traced to Arabic mustaʾjir, 'one who rents', from the root of ejâre, 'rent'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
لوازم خانگی (lavâzem-e khânegi) — household appliances.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a tenant, then the Persian for a microwave.

You'll want to know: لوازم خانگی.
لوازم خانگی (lavâzem-e khânegi) — "household appliances".
این فروشگاه لوازم خانگی میفروشد. in forushgâh lavâzem-e khânegi miforushad. This shop sells household appliances.
lavâzem 'things needed' (an Arabic plural) + khânegi 'of the home'.

The word, taken apart.
lavâzem-e khânegi is built in Persian on lavâzem, from Arabic lawāzim, 'necessities' (EtymDB), and khânegi, 'of the home', from native khâne, 'house'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
گرم کردن (garm kardan) — to heat, to warm up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a microwave, then the Persian for household appliances.

You'll want to know: گرم کردن.
گرم کردن (garm kardan) — "to heat, to warm up".
او شیر بچه را گرم کرد. u shir-e bachche râ garm kard. She warmed the baby's milk.
garm 'warm' + kardan. Players also garm mikonand 'warm up' before a match.

The word, taken apart.
garm kardan is built on two inherited words: garm, 'warm', which EtymDB traces to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning 'to heat, to warm' (EtymDB), and kardan, 'to do'.

The word, taken apart.
sard shodan is built on two inherited words: sard, 'cold', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and shodan, 'to become'.
