## You'll want to know: نعى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C319-AZA319-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نعى** — *naʿā* — "to announce a death".

> نعت الصحيفة الكاتب الكبير.
>
> *naʿat aṣ-ṣaḥīfa al-kātib al-kabīr.*
>
> The newspaper announced the death of the great writer.

Present: ينعى, *yanʿā*.

A death notice in the paper is نعي.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نعى** (*naʿā*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ʿ-y* (Lane), "to bring the news of someone's death"; *naʿy* is that news.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
