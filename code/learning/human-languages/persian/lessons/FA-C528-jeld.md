## You'll want to know: جلد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C528-BOOKS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جلد** (*jeld*) — "a volume; a (book) cover".

> این رمان دو جلد دارد.
>
> *in român do jeld dârad.*
>
> This novel is in two volumes.

Also the counter for books: *se jeld ketâb* 'three books'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jeld* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *jild*, which EtymDB glosses 'skin, hide' (EtymDB): the leather a book was bound in, then the book itself. Urdu *jild* is usually traced to the same word. EtymDB has no Persian entry for it.
