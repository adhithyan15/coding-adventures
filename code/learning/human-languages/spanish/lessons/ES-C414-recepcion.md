# la recepción — the place that receives you

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-APUNTARSE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **apuntarse**, to put your name on a list. At a sports centre,
the list may be kept at **la recepción**, the reception desk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-RECEPCION]; assesses=[] -->

Latin **receptio** meant a receiving. Spanish **recepción** and English
**reception** belong to the same family. The place receives visitors, questions
and sign-up requests.

## Grammar Lens: place after en
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-RECEPCION, ES-LEX-APUNTARSE] -->

Use **en la recepción** for “at the reception desk.” The written accent marks
the final stress: re-cep-**CIÓN**. A useful notice says **Hay que apuntarse en
la recepción**.
