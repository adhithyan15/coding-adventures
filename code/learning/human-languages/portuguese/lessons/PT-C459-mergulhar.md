## You'll want to know: mergulhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C459-ROCHEDOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mergulhar** — "to dive (in), to plunge".

> Ele mergulhou do rochedo para a água.
>
> *He dived off the rock into the water.*

Also to dip something: *mergulhar o pão no café*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mergulhar* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *merguliāre* (EtymDB), from *mergulus* 'little diver (bird)', on *mergere* 'to dip'.
