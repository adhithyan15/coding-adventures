## You'll want to know: 图书
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C454-INFO454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**图书** — *túshū* — "books on the shelves (of a library or bookshop)".

> **这家书店的中文图书很多** *zhè jiā shūdiàn de Zhōngwén túshū hěn duō* — This bookshop has lots of Chinese books.

The new character, in pieces: 图 — eight strokes: the frame of 国, and inside it a short stroke falling to the left, a bar that turns down to the left, a long stroke falling to the right, and two dots. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**图书** (*túshū*) is built from 图 'a picture' and 书 'a book', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *du* and *syo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to plan'; the sense 'a picture, a map' is not given there.
