## You'll want to know: desarrollar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C572-CAMBIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**desarrollar** — "to develop".

> La empresa desarrolló un coche nuevo.
>
> *The company developed a new car.*

*Desarrollarse* is to take place or to develop: *La historia se desarrolla en Lima.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desarrollar* is built on *arrollar* 'to roll up' with *des-* 'un-'. *Arrollar* comes from *rollo*, usually traced to Latin *rotulus* 'a little wheel', built on *rota* 'wheel'. To develop is to unroll.
