## You'll want to know: सचमुच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सचमुच** (*sacmuc*) — "truly, really".

It opens on the **सच** you know. **सचमुच?** — "really?", a stronger **सच में?**.
