## You'll want to know: कुरेदना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C490-CHHALA490-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुरेदना** — *kurednā* — "to pick at; to scrape at".

> डाक्टर ने कहा कि पपड़ी को मत कुरेदो।
>
> *ḍākṭar ne kahā ki papṛī ko mat kuredo.*
>
> The doctor said not to pick at the scab.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पपड़ी कुरेदी, *usne papṛī kuredī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुरेदना** (*kurednā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *kurednā*, "to scrape", under *\*kura*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3319). One can also *kurednā* an old memory: stir it up again.
