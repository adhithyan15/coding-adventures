# সাপলুডো (sāpluḍo) — snakes and ladders

## You'll want to know: সাপলুডো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C518-GHOROA518-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সাপলুডো** — *sāpluḍo* — "snakes and ladders".

> ছুটির দিনে আমরা সাপলুডো খেলি।
>
> *Chhuṭir dine āmrā sāpluḍo kheli.*
>
> On holidays we play snakes and ladders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাপলুডো** (*sāpluḍo*) is built from সাপ, "a snake", and লুডো, "ludo": the board game of snakes and ladders.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
