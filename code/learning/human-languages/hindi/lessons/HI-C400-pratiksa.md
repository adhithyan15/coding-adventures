## You'll want to know: प्रतीक्षा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C400-AARAKSHAN400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतीक्षा** — *pratīkṣā* — "waiting; a wait (formal)".

> हम दो घंटे से ट्रेन की प्रतीक्षा कर रहे हैं।
>
> *ham do ghaṇṭe se ṭren kī pratīkṣā kar rahe haiṁ.*
>
> We have been waiting for the train for two hours.

Feminine.

Station signs read प्रतीक्षालय, the waiting room; a ticket on the waiting list is on the प्रतीक्षा सूची.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतीक्षा** (*pratīkṣā*) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit word *pratīkṣā*, "expectation, looking out for" (Monier-Williams), kept unchanged. It belongs to signs and announcements; in talk people say इंतज़ार.
