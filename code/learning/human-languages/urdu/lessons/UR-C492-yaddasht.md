## You'll want to know: یادداشت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C492-OLDAGE492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**یادداشت** — *yāddāsht* — "memory (the power to remember)".

> دادی کی یادداشت کمزور ہے۔
>
> *dādī kī yāddāsht kamzor hai.*
>
> My grandmother's memory is weak.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**یادداشت** (*yāddāsht*) is usually traced to Persian *yāddāsht*, "a remembering, a note".
