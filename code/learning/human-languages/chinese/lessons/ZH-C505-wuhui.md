## You'll want to know: 舞会
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C505-DANCE505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**舞会** — *wǔhuì* — "dance party; ball".

> *zhè shì wǒ dìyī cì cānjiā wǔhuì* — This is the first time I've been to a dance.

The new character, in pieces: 舞 — fourteen strokes: on top eight, a short stroke falling left, two bars with four short verticals through the lower one, and a long bar across; below, on the left the top of 多 *duō*, three strokes, and on the right three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**舞会** (*wǔhuì*) is built from 舞 'to dance' and 会 'a gathering', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjuX* and *hwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'singing and dancing' and the second 'to meet'.
