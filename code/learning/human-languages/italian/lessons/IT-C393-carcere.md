## You'll want to know: il carcere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C393-CARCERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il carcere** — "the prison, the jail".

> Se ti prendono, vai in carcere.
>
> *If they catch you, you go to prison.*

Stress on the first syllable: *càrcere*. The plural is feminine: *le carceri*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carcere* is inherited from Latin *carcer, carceris* "a prison, an enclosure" (EtymDB); it also named the starting gates of a racecourse.
