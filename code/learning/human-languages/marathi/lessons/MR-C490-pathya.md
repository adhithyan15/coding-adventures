## You'll want to know: पथ्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C490-PATHYA490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पथ्य** — *pathya* — "a diet regimen".

> आजारपणात आजीने कडक पथ्य पाळलं.
>
> *ājārpaṇāt ājīne kaḍak pathya pāḷlaṁ.*
>
> During her illness Grandmother kept to a strict diet.

Neuter.

पथ्य पाळणे, to keep to a prescribed diet; its opposite is कुपथ्य.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पथ्य** (*pathya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pathya*, "suitable" (CDIAL 7746): what suits the patient.
