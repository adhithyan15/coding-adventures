## You'll want to know: സമരം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C425-SAMARAM425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**സമരം** — *samaraṁ* — "a strike; a struggle".

> തൊഴിലാളികൾ ഇന്ന് മുതൽ സമരം തുടങ്ങി.
>
> *toḻilāḷikaḷ innŭ mutal samaraṁ tuṭaṅṅi.*
>
> The workers began a strike from today.

To strike is സമരം ചെയ്യുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സമരം** (*samaraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samara*, "a coming together; a hostile encounter, a struggle" (Monier-Williams).
