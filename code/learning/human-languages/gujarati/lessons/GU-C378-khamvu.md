## You'll want to know: ખમવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C378-HAD378-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ખમવું** — *khamvũ* — "to tolerate".

> જો તે ફરી ખોટું બોલશે તો હું નહીં ખમું.
>
> *jo te pharī khoṭũ bolśe to hũ nahī̃ khamũ.*
>
> If he lies again, I will not stand for it.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગુરુનો ઠપકો ખમ્યો, *teṇe guruno ṭhapko khamyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખમવું** (*khamvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kṣámatē*, "is patient, endures"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 3657).
