Kannada, chapter 514: Diet and Strength.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪಥ್ಯ (pathya) — a diet; a special diet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for nursing, then the Kannada for a herbal decoction.

You'll want to know: ಪಥ್ಯ.
ಪಥ್ಯ — pathya — "a diet; a special diet".
ಮಧುಮೇಹ ಇರುವುದರಿಂದ ಅಪ್ಪ ಪಥ್ಯ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ. madhumēha iruvudarinda appa pathya māḍuttāre. Because he has diabetes, father keeps to a diet.
ಪಥ್ಯ (pathya) ಮಾಡು, to keep to a diet.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಥ್ಯ (pathya) is borrowed from Sanskrit pathya, "suitable" (CDIAL 7746): food that suits the patient.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಜೀವಸತ್ವ (jīvasatva) — a vitamin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a herbal decoction, then the Kannada for a diet.

You'll want to know: ಜೀವಸತ್ವ.
ಜೀವಸತ್ವ — jīvasatva — "a vitamin".
ಹಣ್ಣುಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಜೀವಸತ್ವಗಳು ಇವೆ. haṇṇugaḷalli tumbā jīvasatvagaḷu ive. Fruits have lots of vitamins.
Plural: ಜೀವಸತ್ವಗಳು, jīvasatvagaḷu.
ಜೀವ, life + ಸತ್ವ, essence. Everyday talk also says ವಿಟಮಿನ್.

The word, taken apart.
ಜೀವಸತ್ವ (jīvasatva) is a modern learned compound built from Sanskrit jīva, "life" (CDIAL 5239), and sattva, "being, true character" (CDIAL 13111).

ಸೊರಗು (soragu) — to waste away; to grow thin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a diet, then the Kannada for a vitamin.

You'll want to know: ಸೊರಗು.
ಸೊರಗು — soragu — "to waste away; to grow thin".
ಕಾಯಿಲೆಯಿಂದ ಅವನು ತುಂಬಾ ಸೊರಗಿದನು. kāyileyinda avanu tumbā soragidanu. He grew very thin from the illness.
Past (I …): ಸೊರಗಿದೆ, soragide.
Of people, plants and animals.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೊರಗು (soragu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2687 lists Kannada soragu, "to wither, grow dry, grow lean", beside Tamil curuṅku, "to shrink".

[pause 2 seconds]

ದಣಿ — daṇi — "to get tired".
ಇಡೀ ದಿನ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡಿ ಅವನು ದಣಿದನು. iḍī dina kelasa māḍi avanu daṇidanu. He got tired after working all day.
Past (I …): ದಣಿದೆ, daṇide.
ದಣಿವು, tiredness; ದಣಿವಾರಿಸಿಕೊ, to rest a while.

ದಣಿ (daṇi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3045 lists Kannada daṇi, daṇivu, beside taṇi, "to grow cool, get feeble or fatigued".
