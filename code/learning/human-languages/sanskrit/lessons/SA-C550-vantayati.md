## You'll want to know: वण्टयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C550-AMSA550-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वण्टयति** — *vaṇṭayati* — "divides up; shares out".

> यदि माता मोदकान् वण्टयति, तर्हि सर्वे एकं लभन्ते।
>
> *yadi mātā modakān vaṇṭayati, tarhi sarve ekaṃ labhante.*
>
> If my mother shares out the sweets, everyone gets one.

Past (she …): सा मोदकान् वण्टितवती, *sā modakān vaṇṭitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वण्टयति** (*vaṇṭayati*) comes from the root √vaṇṭ, "to partition, to share, to divide"; its past participle is *vaṇṭita* (MW).
