## You'll want to know: ਗਾਇਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C349-SONG349-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਇਕ** — *gāik* — "a singer".

> ਉਹ ਗਾਇਕ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਿੱਠਾ ਗਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *oh gāik bahut miṭṭhā gāundā hai.*
>
> That singer sings very sweetly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਇਕ** (*gāik*) is taken over from Sanskrit *gāyaka*, "a singer", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
