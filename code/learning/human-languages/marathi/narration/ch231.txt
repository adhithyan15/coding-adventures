Marathi, chapter 231: Together, Perhaps, Besides, Of course, That is to say.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
एकत्र (ekatra) — together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for meanwhile, then the Marathi for for good.

You'll want to know: एकत्र.
एकत्र — ekatra — "together".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ekatra]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ekatra, once more]

[your turn — say: say ekatra]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for meanwhile, then the Marathi for for good, then say ekatra again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does एकत्र (ekatra) mean? ("Together".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To repair? (दुरुस्त करणे, durust karṇe.) To take a photo? (फोटो काढणे, phoṭo kāḍhṇe.) To explain? (समजावणे, samjāvṇe.) To translate? (भाषांतर करणे, bhāṣāntar karṇe.) To make a decision? (निर्णय घेणे, nirṇay gheṇe.) To grow old? (म्हातारे होणे, mhātāre hoṇe.) To get divorced? (घटस्फोट घेणे, ghaṭasphoṭ gheṇe.) To move house? (घर बदलणे, ghar badalṇe.) To rest? (आराम करणे, ārām karṇe.) To get bored? (कंटाळणे, kaṇṭāḷṇe.) To convince? (पटवणे, paṭavṇe.) To criticise? (टीका करणे, ṭīkā karṇe.) To prove? (सिद्ध करणे, siddha karṇe.) To depend? (अवलंबून असणे, avlambūn asṇe.) To avoid? (टाळणे, ṭāḷṇe.) To suggest? (सुचवणे, sucavṇe.) To give back? (परत देणे, parat deṇe.) To refuse? (नाकारणे, nākārṇe.) To take a risk? (धोका पत्करणे, dhokā patkarṇe.) To solve? (सोडवणे, soḍavṇe.)
