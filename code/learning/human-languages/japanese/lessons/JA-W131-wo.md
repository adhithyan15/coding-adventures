## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-WO-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-O-01, JA-PARTICLE-WA-SPELLING] -->

> **を** — said *o*

Three strokes, two lifts:

1. the short **bar** at the top, left to right
2. starting above the bar, a long **diagonal** down and to the left through it;
   at its foot, without lifting, turn sharply back up and to the right into an
   **arch**, and come down its right side in a short stem
3. a separate **curve**: start at the right, sweep down and left across the
   middle, round the lower left, and finish along the base to the right

It is said exactly like **お**. The two are not interchangeable, and what
separates them is not sound:

- **お** spells *o* inside words, as in **おちゃ**.
- **を** spells the particle that marks the thing asked for or acted on, and
  nothing else.

You have met this once already. **は** is read *wa* when it is the
topic marker; **を** is read *o* when it is the object marker. Both are spellings
kept for a grammatical word after the sound moved.
