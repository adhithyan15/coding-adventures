# മോഷണം (mōṣaṇaṁ) — a theft

## You'll want to know: മോഷണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C364-KALLAN364-02]; assesses=[] -->

**മോഷണം** — *mōṣaṇaṁ* — "a theft".

> ബാങ്കിലെ മോഷണം പത്രത്തിൽ വാർത്തയായി.
>
> *bāṅkile mōṣaṇaṁ patrattil vārttayāyi.*
>
> The theft at the bank was in the news.

The formal word; in speech people often say കളവ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മോഷണം** (*mōṣaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *moṣaṇa*, "robbing, plundering; the act of robbing" (Monier-Williams).
