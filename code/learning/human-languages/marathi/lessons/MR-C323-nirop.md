## You'll want to know: निरोप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C323-NIROP323-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निरोप** — *nirop* — "a farewell; a message".

> शाळेत गुरुजींना निवृत्तीच्या दिवशी निरोप दिला.
>
> *śāḷet gurujīnnā nivṛttīcyā divśī nirop dilā.*
>
> At school the teacher was given a farewell on the day he retired.

Masculine.

निरोप देणे, to see someone off; निरोप घेणे, to take one's leave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निरोप** (*nirop*) is the Marathi word for a message and a leave-taking; its origin is not settled.
