## You'll want to know: ஓய்வறை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C405-METRO405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ஓய்வறை** — *ōyvaṟai* — "a lounge; a retiring room".

> பயணிகள் ஓய்வறையில் காத்திருந்தார்கள்.
>
> *payaṇikaḷ ōyvaṟaiyil kāttiruntārkaḷ.*
>
> The passengers waited in the lounge.

ஓய்வு, rest, + அறை, room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஓய்வறை** (*ōyvaṟai*) is built from ஓய்வு, "rest" (DEDR 1057), and அறை, "a room".
