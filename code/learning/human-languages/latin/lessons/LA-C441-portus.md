## You'll want to know: portus, portūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C441-PORTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**portus, portūs** — "a harbour, a port".

> Tandem nāvis in portum intrat, et nautae gaudent.
>
> *At last the ship comes into the harbour, and the sailors are glad.*

Fourth declension, masculine: *portus, portūs m.* Figurative too: a safe place after trouble. English *port* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*portus* is inherited from Proto-Italic *portus*, from PIE *pértus* 'crossing, passage' (EtymDB), the same root as *porta* 'gate'. English *port* comes from it.
