Marathi, chapter 533: Keeping Fit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कसरत (kasrat) — a workout; exercise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a crossword, then the Marathi for hide and seek.

You'll want to know: कसरत.
कसरत — kasrat — "a workout; exercise".
धावपटू रोज दोन तास कसरत करतात. dhāvpaṭū roj don tās kasrat kartāt. Runners work out for two hours every day.
Feminine.
कसरत (kasrat) करणे, to work out.

The word, taken apart.
कसरत (kasrat) is usually traced to Persian kasrat, "exercise".

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for hide and seek, then the Marathi for a workout.

You'll want to know: योगासन.
योगासन — yogāsan — "a yoga posture".
आजी रोज सकाळी योगासनं करते. ājī roj sakāḷī yogāsanaṁ karte. Grandmother does yoga every morning.
Neuter. Plural: योगासनं, yogāsanaṁ.

The word, taken apart.
योगासन (yogāsan) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit yoga and āsana, "a seat, a posture" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a yoga posture, then the Marathi for a whistle.

You'll want to know: वाकणे.
वाकणे — vākṇe — "to bend".
चेंडू उचलण्यासाठी तो खाली वाकला. ceṇḍū ucalṇyāsāṭhī to khālī vāklā. He bent down to pick up the ball.
Past (he …): तो वाकला, to vāklā.

The word, taken apart.
वाकणे (vākṇe) is built on वाक, "a curve", which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit vaṅka, "bent, crooked" (CDIAL 11191).
