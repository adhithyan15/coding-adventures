Urdu, chapter 416: Rubbish and Drains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کوڑا (kūṛā) — rubbish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a flavour, then the Urdu for to serve (food).

You'll want to know: کوڑا.
کوڑا — kūṛā — "rubbish".
گلی میں کوڑا نہیں پھینکنا چاہیے۔ galī meṅ kūṛā nahīṅ pheṅknā chāhiye. You shouldn't throw rubbish in the lane.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
کوڑا (kūṛā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin kūṛā, "a dust heap", under Sanskrit kūṭa, "a heap" (CDIAL 3392).

Lesson 2 of 5.
نالی (nālī) — a drain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to serve (food), then the Urdu for rubbish.

You'll want to know: نالی.
نالی — nālī — "a drain".
بارش کے بعد نالی بھر گئی۔ bārish ke bād nālī bhar gaī. After the rain the drain filled up.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
نالی (nālī) is built on nāl, "a hollow stalk, a tube", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit nāḍī, "a tube" (CDIAL 7047), with the ending -ī.

Lesson 3 of 5.
گٹر (gaṭar) — a sewer; a covered drain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for rubbish, then the Urdu for a drain.

You'll want to know: گٹر.
گٹر — gaṭar — "a sewer; a covered drain".
سڑک کے نیچے ایک بڑا گٹر ہے۔ saṛak ke nīche ek baṛā gaṭar hai. There's a big sewer under the road.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
گٹر (gaṭar) is borrowed from English gutter; in Urdu it names a sewer.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بدبو (badbū) — a stink; a bad smell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: بدبو.
بدبو — badbū — "a stink; a bad smell".
گٹر سے بدبو آتی ہے۔ gaṭar se badbū ātī hai. The sewer stinks.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بدبو (badbū) is built from Persian bad, "bad", and bū, "a smell": it is usually traced to Persian badbū.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پھیلانا (phailānā) — to spread (something) around; to strew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a sewer, then the Urdu for a stink.

You'll want to know: پھیلانا.
پھیلانا — phailānā — "to spread (something) around; to strew".
کچھ لوگ گلی میں کوڑا پھیلاتے ہیں۔ kuchh log galī meṅ kūṛā phailāte haiṅ. Some people strew rubbish about the lane.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے میز پر نقشہ پھیلایا, us ne mez par naqshā phailāyā.

The word, taken apart.
پھیلانا (phailānā) is built on پھیلنا, "to spread": CDIAL lists both, as Hindi phailnā and phailānā, under Sanskrit prathita, "spread out" (CDIAL 8651). This is the form for spreading something else.
