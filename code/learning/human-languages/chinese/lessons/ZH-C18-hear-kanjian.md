# Hear *kànjiàn* — see

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-KAN-01, ZH-LEX-ZAIJIAN] -->

[PAUSE 10s] Say **look or watch** as *kàn*, then say **goodbye** as *zàijiàn*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-KANJIAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *kànjiàn* — **see; catch sight of**

Both syllables fall. The familiar *jiàn* now follows *kàn*: you look and
successfully perceive. Keep the characters hidden for this sound-first step.
