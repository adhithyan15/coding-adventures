## You'll want to know: あんぜん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C268-KYUUJO268-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あんぜん** — *anzen* — "safe; safety".

> **うちの なかは あんぜんでした。** — *uchi no naka wa anzen deshita* — "It was safe inside the house."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あんぜん** (*anzen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *an*, "quiet" (Middle Chinese *'an*) and *zen*, "whole" (*dzjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
