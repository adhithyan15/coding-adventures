## HL-C443: six later cold letters get words first

Gentle writing asks for every letter to be taken from a word the reader
already says. Six Marathi letter lessons after the opening runways still came
before any word holding their letter. Each now follows a short word lesson
that holds it:

| letter | word placed just before it |
|---|---|
| ौ | **मौज** (*mauj*, fun) |
| ढ | **ढोल** (*ḍhol*, a drum) |
| फ | **फणस** (*phaṇas*, a jackfruit) |
| ई | **ईश्वर** (*īśvar*, God) |
| ओ | **ओला** (*olā*, wet) |
| ऐ | **ऐवज** (*aivaj*, valuables) |

Each word is revisited by its letter lesson and by the lesson after it.

The other 37 cold letters are in the opening script runways, chapters 1-8.
Those runways teach the letters before any word by design, and their lesson
lists and atom budgets are pinned. Anchoring them means redesigning the
runways word-first, which is its own piece of work.
