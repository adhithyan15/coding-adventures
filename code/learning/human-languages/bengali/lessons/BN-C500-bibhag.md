## You'll want to know: বিভাগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C500-BIBHAG500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিভাগ** — *bibhāg* — "a department; a division".

> আমার বোন এই কোম্পানির নতুন বিভাগে কাজ করে।
>
> *Āmār bon ei kompānir nôtun bibhāge kāj kôre.*
>
> My sister works in the company's new department.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিভাগ** (*bibhāg*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vibhāga*, "distribution, a share" (Monier-Williams): a part of the whole.
