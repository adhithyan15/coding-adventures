# Tomorrow — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *I will come tomorrow*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-NEG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C162-KAL-TENSE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C161-MHAI-AGENT-01, MW-LEX-C161-VERB161-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *I will go to the market tomorrow*; *I won't go to the market today*]
- [YOU SAY: *I will drink milk in the evening*; *I won't eat meat*]
- [YOU SAY: *I went yesterday*; *I will go tomorrow*]
- [YOU SAY: *I drew water*; *I will draw water tomorrow*]
