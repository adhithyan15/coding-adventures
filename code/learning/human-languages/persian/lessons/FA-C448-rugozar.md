## You'll want to know: روگذر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C448-MIYANBOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**روگذر** (*rugozar*) — "a flyover, an overpass".

> روی روگذر ترافیک سنگین بود.
>
> *Ru-ye rugozar terâfik sangin bud.*
>
> The traffic on the flyover was heavy.

*ru* 'over' + *gozar* 'passage'; the opposite of *zirgozar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rugozar* is a native compound of *ru* 'face, on top' and *gozar* 'passage', the present stem of *gozashtan* 'to pass', which is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Middle Persian verb 'to pass, to cross'.
