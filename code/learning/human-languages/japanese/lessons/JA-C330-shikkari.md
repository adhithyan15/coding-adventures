## You'll want to know: しっかり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C330-CHANTO330-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しっかり** — *shikkari* — "firmly; steadily".

> **かぜが つよいから、しっかり もって ください。** — *kaze ga tsuyoi kara, shikkari motte kudasai* — "The wind is strong, so hold on firmly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しっかり** (*shikkari*) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
