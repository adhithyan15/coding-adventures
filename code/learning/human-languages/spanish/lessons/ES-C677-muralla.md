## You'll want to know: la muralla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C677-MONUMENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la muralla** — "the city wall, the ramparts".

> La muralla rodea el casco antiguo.
>
> *The city wall runs round the old town.*

A garden wall is *un muro*; a city wall is *la muralla*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*muralla* is built on *muro* "wall", which is inherited from Latin *mūrus* (Lewis & Short).
