## You'll want to know: disfrazarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C760-PERSONAJE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**disfrazarse** — "to dress up, to disguise oneself".

> En carnaval nos disfrazamos.
>
> *At carnival we dress up.*

*Disfrazarse de* — to dress up as.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disfrazarse* comes from Old Spanish *desfrezar* "to disguise". For *desfrezar* itself the origin is not settled.
