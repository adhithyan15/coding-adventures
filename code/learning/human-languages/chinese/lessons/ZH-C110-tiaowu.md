## You'll want to know: tiàowǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C110-SKILL110-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tiàowǔ* — "to dance".

> *nǐ xǐhuan tiàowǔ ma* — Do you like dancing?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tiàowǔ* is built from tiào 'to leap' and wǔ 'dance', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dew* and *mjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as song and dance.

## What you've built

One more word for what I have and can do.
