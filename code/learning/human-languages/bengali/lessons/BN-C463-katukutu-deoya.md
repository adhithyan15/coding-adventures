# কাতুকুতু দেওয়া (kātukutu deoyā) — to tickle

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C463-DUSHTU463-03, BN-LEX-C463-DUSHTU463-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for talkative, then the Bengali for to tease.

## You'll want to know: কাতুকুতু দেওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C463-DUSHTU463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**কাতুকুতু দেওয়া** — *kātukutu deoyā* — "to tickle".

> দুষ্টু ভাইটা আমাকে কাতুকুতু দিল।
>
> *dushṭu bhāiṭā āmāke kātukutu dilô.*
>
> My naughty brother tickled me.

Past (he/she …): তিনি নাতিকে কাতুকুতু দিলেন, *tini nātike kātukutu dilen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাতুকুতু দেওয়া** (*kātukutu deoyā*) is *kātukutu*, an expressive word for tickling, with *deoyā*, "to give" (CDIAL 6141): to give someone a tickle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
