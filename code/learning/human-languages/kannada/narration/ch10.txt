Kannada, chapter 10: Days of the Week.
1 lesson. All 1 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 1.
ಸೋಮವಾರ to ಭಾನುವಾರ — the Sanskrit week.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Unlike Tamil and Malayalam, Kannada's week is built entirely from Sanskrit — the same recipe as Hindi's. But even here, one day picks a different Sanskrit word than Hindi does.

You'll want to know: The pattern: [deity] + ವಾರ, "day".
[a table of 7 rows, read aloud]
Kannada: ಸೋಮವಾರ (Somavāra). deity/planet: Moon (Soma). day: Monday.
Kannada: ಮಂಗಳವಾರ (Maṅgaḷavāra). deity/planet: Mars (Maṅgala). day: Tuesday.
Kannada: ಬುಧವಾರ (Budhavāra). deity/planet: Mercury (Budha). day: Wednesday.
Kannada: ಗುರುವಾರ (Guruvāra). deity/planet: Jupiter (Guru/Bṛhaspati). day: Thursday.
Kannada: ಶುಕ್ರವಾರ (Śukravāra). deity/planet: Venus (Śukra). day: Friday.
Kannada: ಶನಿವಾರ (Śanivāra). deity/planet: Saturn (Śani). day: Saturday.
Kannada: ಭಾನುವಾರ (Bhānuvāra). deity/planet: the Sun (Bhānu, "light, splendor"). day: Sunday.
Five of these are identical in shape to Hindi's — same deity, same ‑vāra/ ‑vār ending. Sunday is the interesting exception: where Hindi says रविवार (Ravivār, from Ravi, "sun"), Kannada says ಭಾನುವಾರ (Bhānuvāra), built on a different Sanskrit word for the sun — Bhānu, "light, splendor," rather than Ravi. Sanskrit has many names for the sun, and different languages downstream picked different ones for their week.
