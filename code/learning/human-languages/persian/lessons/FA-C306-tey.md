## You'll want to know: طی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C306-TADRIJ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**طی** (*tey*) — "during, in the space of".

> طی سفر چند بار باران آمد.
>
> *Tey-e safar chand bâr bârân âmad.*
>
> It rained several times during the trip.

Formal, with the ezafe: *tey-e do sâl* "over two years".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tey* is borrowed from Arabic *ṭayy* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a folding up, a covering of distance": time folded up as it passes.
