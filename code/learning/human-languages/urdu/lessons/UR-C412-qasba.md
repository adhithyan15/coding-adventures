## You'll want to know: قصبہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C412-QASBA412-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قصبہ** — *qasbā* — "a small town".

> ہمارا قصبہ شہر سے دور ہے۔
>
> *hamārā qasbā shahr se dūr hai.*
>
> Our little town is a long way from the city.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قصبہ** (*qasbā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qaṣaba*, "a chief town, a citadel"; in Urdu it is a town smaller than a **شہر**.
