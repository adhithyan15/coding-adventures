## You'll want to know: ゆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C147-WASH147-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆ** — *yu* — "hot water; a hot bath".

> **おふろの ゆは あついです。** — *ofuro no yu wa atsui desu* — "The bath water is hot."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆ** (*yu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
