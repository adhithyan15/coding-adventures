## You'll want to know: die Kündigung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C301-KUENDIGEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Kündigung** — "the notice, the dismissal".

> Er bekam die Kündigung am Montag.
>
> *He got his notice on Monday.*

Plural: *die Kündigungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kündigung* is built on *kündigen*, from *kund* "known", an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
