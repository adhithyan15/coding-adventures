## You'll want to know: 王子
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C330-KING330-02]; assesses=[] -->

**王子** — *wángzǐ* — "prince".

> **王子一个人走了很长的路** *wángzǐ yí ge rén zǒu le hěn cháng de lù* — The prince walked a long way on his own.

The new character, in pieces: 王 — four strokes: three bars, the middle one the shortest and the bottom one the longest, with a vertical through the middle. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**王子** (*wángzǐ*) is built from 王 'a king' and 子 'a son', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjwang* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'great' and 'a ruler' and the second 'a child'.
