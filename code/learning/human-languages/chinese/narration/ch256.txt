Mandarin Chinese, chapter 256: What Do You Think?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
rènwéi — to think; to hold a view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to work overtime, then the Mandarin for the boss.

You'll want to know: rènwéi.
rènwéi — "to think; to hold a view".
nǐ rènwéi zěnmeyàng — What do you think?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
rènwéi is built from rèn 'to know' and wéi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, nyinH and hjwe (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to know' and the second 'to make'.

What you've built.
One more word for saying no and asking.

Lesson 3 of 5.
看来 (kànlai) — it seems; it looks as if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to hold a view, then the Mandarin for a point of view.

You'll want to know: 看来.
看来 — kànlai — "it seems; it looks as if".
看来他今天不来了 kànlai tā jīntiān bù lái le — It looks as if he isn't coming today.

The word, taken apart.
看来 (kànlai) is built from 看 'to look' and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khanH and loj (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). In the word the second loses its tone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
yěxǔ — it may be that; perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a point of view, then the Mandarin for it looks as if.

You'll want to know: yěxǔ.
yěxǔ — "it may be that; perhaps".
yěxǔ tā wàng le — Maybe he forgot.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
Two third tones meet in this word: yě is said with a rising tone before xǔ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
yěxǔ is built from 也 'also' and xǔ 'to allow', both inherited from Middle Chinese, yaeX and xjoX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a particle that closes a sentence and the second 'to permit'. How the pair came to mean perhaps is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for saying no and asking.

Lesson 5 of 5.
不同 (bùtóng) — different.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for it looks as if, then the Mandarin for it may be that.

You'll want to know: 不同.
不同 — bùtóng — "different".
我们两个人很不同 wǒmen liǎng ge rén hěn bùtóng — The two of us are very different.
Tone: 不 keeps its own falling tone here, bù, because tóng after it is a rising tone, not a falling one.

The word, taken apart.
不同 (bùtóng) is built from 不 'not' and 同 'the same', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjut and duwng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
