## You'll want to know: einfetten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C326-VORHEIZEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**einfetten** — "to grease".

> Sie fettete die Form mit Butter ein.
>
> *She greased the tin with butter.*

Separable. Built on *das Fett*, fat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*einfetten* is built on *Fett* "fat", which came from Middle Low German *vet* (EtymDB), a cousin of English *fat*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
