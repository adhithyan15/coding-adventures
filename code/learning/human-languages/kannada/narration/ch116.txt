Kannada, chapter 116: Pillow, Blanket, Curtain, Lock, Nail.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ದಿಂಬು (dimbu) — a pillow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for because, then the Kannada for a bed.

You'll want to know: ದಿಂಬು.
ದಿಂಬು — dimbu — "a pillow".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dimbu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dimbu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dimbu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for because, then the Kannada for a bed, then say dimbu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ದಿಂಬು (dimbu) mean? ("A pillow".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: right, left, straight, far; distance, above]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: below, outside, in front, in the middle, a road]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a bridge, a park, a temple, a library, a bank]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a market, a beach, a garden, a corner, a turn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a hotel, a train, an aeroplane, a car, a bicycle]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

(ನಡುವೆ, naḍuve.) A road? (ರಸ್ತೆ, rastē.) A bridge? (ಸೇತುವೆ, sētuve.) A park? (ಉದ್ಯಾನ, udyāna.) A temple? (ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನ, dēvasthāna.) A library? (ಗ್ರಂಥಾಲಯ, granthālaya.) A bank? (ಬ್ಯಾಂಕು, byāṅku.) A market? (ಸಂತೆ, sante.) A beach? (ಕಡಲತೀರ, kaḍalatīra.) A garden? (ತೋಟ, tōṭa.) A corner? (ಮೂಲೆ, mūle.) A turn? (ತಿರುವು, tiruvu.) A hotel? (ಹೋಟೆಲ್, hōṭel.) A train? (ರೈಲು, railu.) An aeroplane? (ವಿಮಾನ, vimāna.) A car? (ಕಾರು, kāru.) A bicycle? (ಸೈಕಲ್, saikal.)
