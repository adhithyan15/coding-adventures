## You'll want to know: はつか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C165-DATES165-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はつか** — *hatsuka* — "the twentieth of the month; twenty days".

> **はつかに いなかに かえります。** — *hatsuka ni inaka ni kaerimasu* — "I will go back to the countryside on the twentieth."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はつか** (*hatsuka*) is native Japanese, built from an old native word for "twenty" and *ka*, "day", both inherited rather than borrowed; the older origin of the first part is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
