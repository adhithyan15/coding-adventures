# ಲಾಟರಿ (lāṭari) — a lottery

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C412-APAYA412-04, KA-LEX-C412-APAYA412-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for hardship, then the Kannada for to ward off.

## You'll want to know: ಲಾಟರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C413-PANA413-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಾಟರಿ** — *lāṭari* — "a lottery".

> ಅವನಿಗೆ ಲಾಟರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹಣ ಬಂತು.
>
> *avanige lāṭariyalli haṇa bantu.*
>
> He won money in the lottery.

ಲಾಟರಿ ಹೊಡೆ, to win the lottery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಾಟರಿ** (*lāṭari*) is a loan from English *lottery*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
