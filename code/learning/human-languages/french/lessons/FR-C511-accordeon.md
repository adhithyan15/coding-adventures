## You'll want to know: l'accordéon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C511-INSTRUMENTS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'accordéon** — "accordion".

> Au bal du village, un vieil homme jouait de l'accordéon.
>
> *At the village dance an old man was playing the accordion.*

Masculine. A typical sound of the French *bal musette*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'accordéon* was borrowed from German *Akkordion* (EtymDB), built on *Akkord* "chord", which itself was borrowed from French *accord*; *accord* goes back to Latin *cor* "heart" (Lewis & Short).
