## You'll want to know: اتسع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C462-ISTIDAD462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اتسع** — *ittasaʿa* — "to have room for; to widen".

> لم تتسع السيارة لكل الأمتعة.
>
> *lam tattasiʿ as-sayyāra li-kull al-amtiʿa.*
>
> The car did not have room for all the luggage.

Present: يتسع, *yattasiʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اتسع** (*ittasaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-s-ʿ*, "to be wide" (Lane). It is Form VIII; *wāsiʿ*, "spacious", shares the root.
