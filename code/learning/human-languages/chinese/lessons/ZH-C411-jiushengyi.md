## You'll want to know: 救生衣
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C411-FERRY411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**救生衣** — *jiùshēngyī* — "life jacket".

> *zuò chuán de shíhou yào chuān jiùshēngyī* — On a boat you have to wear a life jacket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**救生衣** (*jiùshēngyī*) is built from *jiù* 'to save', 生 'life' and 衣 'clothes', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjuwH*, *srjaeng* and *'j+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to protect'.
