## You'll want to know: émouvoir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C472-NOSTALGIE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**émouvoir** — "to move, to touch (emotionally)".

> Sa lettre m'a beaucoup ému.
>
> *Her letter moved me a lot.*

Past participle *ému*: *j'étais ému* — I was moved.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*émouvoir* comes from Latin *ēmovēre* "to move out, to stir", built on *movēre* "to move" (Lewis & Short).
