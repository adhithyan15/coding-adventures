# మురిసిపోవు (murisipōvu) — to be delighted

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for contempt, then the Telugu for to enjoy.

## You'll want to know: మురిసిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C500-ANANDAM500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మురిసిపోవు** — *murisipōvu* — "to be delighted".

> మనవడిని చూసి తాతయ్య మురిసిపోయారు.
>
> *manavaḍini cūsi tātayya murisipōyāru.*
>
> Seeing his grandson, grandpa was delighted.

Past (I …): మురిసిపోయాను, *murisipōyānu*.

A warm word for the glow of a parent or a grandparent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మురిసిపోవు** (*murisipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5011 lists Telugu *muriyu*, "to exult, to be in great glee", and the verb ends in *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
