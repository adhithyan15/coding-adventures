Italian, chapter 69: Bird, Mouse, Wolf, Bear, Fox.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'uccello — a bird.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a cow, then the Italian for a pig.

You'll want to know: l'uccello.
l'uccello — "a bird".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does l'uccello mean? ("A bird".) Say it once more.
