## Script — a bundle, then the walking piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SU-SPEED-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 速

**Two pieces, and the walking piece comes last.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The upper piece, seven strokes.** A bar across the top. A small 口 under it, three strokes. A long vertical down through the bar and 口. Then a stroke falling to the left from the vertical and a dot to the right, as at the foot of the tree piece.

**The walking piece, three strokes**, as in 这: a dot, a stroke that zigzags down, then a long sweep along the foot.

7 + 3 = ten.
