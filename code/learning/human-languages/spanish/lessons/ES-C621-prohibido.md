## You'll want to know: prohibido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C621-IMPIDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prohibido** — "forbidden, prohibited".

> Prohibido aparcar.
>
> *No parking.*

Common on signs: *prohibido el paso* — no entry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prohibido* is the participle of *prohibir*, a learned borrowing from Latin *prohibere* 'to hold back', built on *habere* 'to hold' (EtymDB).
