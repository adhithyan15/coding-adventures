## You'll want to know: 要不是
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C541-FINAL541-01]; assesses=[] -->

**要不是** — *yàobúshì* — "if it were not for".

> **要不是你我们就找不到这儿了** *yàobúshì nǐ wǒmen jiù zhǎo bu dào zhèr le* — If it weren't for you, we'd never have found this place.

Tone: 不 is said bú before the falling tone of shì, and the spelling shows it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**要不是** (*yàobúshì*) is built from 要 'if', 不 'not' and 是 'to be', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jiewH*, *pjut* and *dzyeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'to bind by agreement', and its use for 'if' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
