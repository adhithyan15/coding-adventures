Lesson 1 of 6.
वाटाघाटी करणे (vāṭāghāṭī karṇe) — to negotiate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a spokesperson, then the Marathi for an activist.

You'll want to know: वाटाघाटी करणे.
वाटाघाटी करणे — vāṭāghāṭī karṇe — "to negotiate".
दोन्ही बाजूंनी रात्रभर वाटाघाटी केल्या. donhī bājūnnī rātrabhar vāṭāghāṭī kelyā. Both sides negotiated all night.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वाटाघाटी केल्या, tyāne vāṭāghāṭī kelyā.
वाटाघाटी is feminine plural, so the past is केल्या.

The word, taken apart.
वाटाघाटी करणे (vāṭāghāṭī karṇe) joins वाटाघाटी, "talks, bargaining", a Marathi word whose origin is not settled, to करणे, "to do".

Lesson 2 of 6.
वाद घालणे (vād ghālṇe) — to argue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an activist, then the Marathi for to negotiate.

You'll want to know: वाद घालणे.
वाद घालणे — vād ghālṇe — "to argue".
मुलं खेळण्यांवरून वाद घालत होती. mulaṁ kheḷaṇyāṁvarūn vād ghālat hotī. The children were arguing over the toys.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वाद घातला, tyāne vād ghātlā.
The subject of the quarrel takes -वरून; घालणे's past is घातला.

The word, taken apart.
वाद घालणे (vād ghālṇe) joins the tatsama वाद, Sanskrit vāda, "speech, dispute" (Monier-Williams), to घालणे, "to put on".

Lesson 3 of 6.
रेटणे (reṭṇe) — to push through.

[pause 2 seconds]

रेटणे — reṭṇe — "to push through".
विरोध असूनही त्याने आपला मुद्दा रेटला. virodh asūnhī tyāne āplā muddā reṭlā. Despite opposition he pushed his point through.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मुद्दा रेटला, tyāne muddā reṭlā.
Also to shove a load: गाडी रेटणे (reṭṇe), to push a cart.

The word, taken apart.
रेटणे (reṭṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
मिटवणे (miṭavṇe) — to resolve (a dispute).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to argue, then the Marathi for to push through.

You'll want to know: मिटवणे.
मिटवणे — miṭavṇe — "to resolve (a dispute)".
आजोबांनी दोघांचं भांडण मिटवलं. ājobānnī doghāñcaṁ bhāṇḍaṇ miṭavlaṁ. Grandfather settled the quarrel between the two.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भांडण मिटवलं, tyāne bhāṇḍaṇ miṭavlaṁ.
मिटणे, to be settled: भांडण मिटलं.

The word, taken apart.
मिटवणे (miṭavṇe) is built on मिटणे, "to be wiped out", as its causative; CDIAL lists Marathi miṭṇẽ, "to efface", under Sanskrit mṛṣṭa, "rubbed, wiped" (CDIAL 10299).

Lesson 5 of 6.
संघर्ष करणे (saṅgharṣa karṇe) — to fight for (a cause); to struggle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to push through, then the Marathi for to resolve.

You'll want to know: संघर्ष करणे.
संघर्ष करणे — saṅgharṣa karṇe — "to fight for (a cause); to struggle".
त्याने शिक्षणासाठी खूप संघर्ष केला. tyāne śikṣaṇāsāṭhī khūp saṅgharṣa kelā. He struggled hard for his education.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने संघर्ष केला, tyāne saṅgharṣa kelā.
X शी संघर्ष, a struggle with X.

The word, taken apart.
संघर्ष करणे (saṅgharṣa karṇe) joins the tatsama संघर्ष, Sanskrit saṁgharṣa, "rubbing together, rivalry" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".
