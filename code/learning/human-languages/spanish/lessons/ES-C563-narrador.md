## You'll want to know: el narrador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C563-RELATO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el narrador** — "the narrator".

> El narrador es un niño de diez años.
>
> *The narrator is a ten-year-old boy.*

A woman is *la narradora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narrador* comes from Latin *nārrātor* 'a storyteller' (EtymDB), from *nārrāre* 'to tell'.
