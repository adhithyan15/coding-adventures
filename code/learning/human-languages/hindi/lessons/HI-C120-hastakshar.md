## You'll want to know: हस्ताक्षर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C120-FORM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हस्ताक्षर** (*hastākṣar*) — "a signature", literally "hand-letters".

**हाथ** and this word's **हस्त-** are the same old word for hand. **यहाँ हस्ताक्षर कीजिए** — "please
sign here".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A signature, built on the word for hand.
