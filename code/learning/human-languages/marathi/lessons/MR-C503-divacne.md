## You'll want to know: डिवचणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C503-MAPH503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**डिवचणे** — *ḍivacṇe* — "to goad; to needle".

> भावाने मला सारखं डिवचलं.
>
> *bhāvāne malā sārkhaṁ ḍivaclaṁ.*
>
> My brother kept needling me.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मला डिवचलं, *tyāne malā ḍivaclaṁ*.

To poke at someone till they react; the one provoked takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डिवचणे** (*ḍivacṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
