## You'll want to know: meridionale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C443-CARDINALI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**meridionale** — "southern".

> La cucina meridionale ha molte verdure.
>
> *Southern cooking has a lot of vegetables.*

*Il Meridione* is the South; *un meridionale* is a southerner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*meridionale* is a learned borrowing from Latin *merīdiōnālis* (EtymDB), from *merīdiēs* 'midday': at noon the sun stands in the south.
