## You'll want to know: cambrioler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C287-CAMBRIOLAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cambrioler** — "to burgle, to break into".

> Quelqu'un a cambriolé la maison pendant les vacances.
>
> *Someone burgled the house during the holidays.*

Said of a place, not a person: *cambrioler un appartement*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cambrioler* is built on the old slang word *cambriole* "a small room", usually traced to Provençal *cambro* "room", which goes back to Latin *camera* "a vault" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the verb without a parent.
