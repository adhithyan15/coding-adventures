## You'll want to know: клумба
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C445-FONAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**клумба** (*klúmba*) — "a flower bed".

> Перед школой большая клумба с розами.
>
> *Péred shkóloy bolsháya klúmba s rózami.*
>
> There is a big flower bed with roses in front of the school.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*клумба* is usually traced to English *clump* 'a cluster of plants'. EtymDB lists the word beside a Polish cousin, *klomb*, but gives no source.
