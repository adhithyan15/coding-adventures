## You'll want to know: periūrium, periūriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C310-IURO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**periūrium, periūriī** — "perjury, a false oath".

> Periūrium grave crīmen erat.
>
> *Perjury was a serious crime.*

Neuter, from *periūrus*, 'oath-breaking': *per-* here means 'wrongly'. English *perjury*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**periūrium** is built on *periūrus*, "oath-breaking" (EtymDB), from *per-*, here "wrongly", and the root of *iūrō*, "to swear". English *perjury* comes from it.
