# бесплатный (besplátnyy) — free (of charge)

## You'll want to know: бесплатный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C397-OPLATA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**бесплатный** (*besplátnyy*) — "free (of charge)".

> Вход в музей бесплатный.
>
> *Vkhod v muzéy besplátnyy.*
>
> Entry to the museum is free.

*Бесплатно* — for free. Free in the sense of not busy is *свободный*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**бесплатный** is built from *без* "without" and *плата* "a payment"; the *з* turns to *с* before *п*.
