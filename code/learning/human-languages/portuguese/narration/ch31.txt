Portuguese, chapter 31: Tongue, Nose, Neck, Shoulder, Knee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a língua — the tongue; a language.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a finger; a toe, then the Portuguese for a tooth.

You'll want to know: a língua.
a língua — "the tongue; a language". Both meanings, as in English tongue.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: a língua]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a língua, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say a língua]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a finger; a toe, then the Portuguese for a tooth, then say a língua again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does a língua mean? ("The tongue; a language".) Say it once more.
