## You'll want to know: iūmentum, iūmentī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C433-IUMENTUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**iūmentum, iūmentī** — "a beast of burden, a draught animal".

> Iūmenta sarcinās mercātōrum per montēs portant.
>
> *Pack animals carry the merchants' bags over the mountains.*

Second declension, neuter: *iūmentum, iūmentī n.* Any animal that pulls or carries: horse, mule, ass or ox.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*iūmentum* is usually traced to *iungō* 'to yoke' (Lewis & Short, from an older *iugimentum*): an animal put under the yoke. EtymDB lists it without a parent, so the line is hedged.
