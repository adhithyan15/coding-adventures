## You'll want to know: 山区
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C437-LAND437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**山区** — *shānqū* — "mountain area".

> *wǒ nǎinai zhù zài shānqū* — My grandmother lives up in the mountains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**山区** (*shānqū*) is built from 山 'a mountain' and 区 'an area', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srean* and *khju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary gives the second as the name of a marsh, and the sense 'an area' is not given there.
