## You'll want to know: garantir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C393-GARANTIE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**garantir** — "to guarantee".

> Le vendeur garantit la réparation pendant un an.
>
> *The seller guarantees the repair for a year.*

A *-ir* verb like *finir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garantir* is inherited from Old French *garantir* "to protect, to vouch for" (EtymDB), from *garant* "protector", usually traced to a Frankish word.
