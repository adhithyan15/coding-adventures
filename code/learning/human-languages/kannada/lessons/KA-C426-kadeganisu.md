# ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸು (kaḍegaṇisu) — to disregard; to overlook

## You'll want to know: ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C426-HIMMETTU426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸು** — *kaḍegaṇisu* — "to disregard; to overlook".

> ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳು ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆಗಳನ್ನು ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *adhikārigaḷu haḷḷiya samasyegaḷannu kaḍegaṇisidaru.*
>
> The officials disregarded the village's problems.

Past (I …): ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸಿದೆ, *kaḍegaṇiside*.

What is disregarded takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸು** (*kaḍegaṇisu*) is built as a compound whose first part is native *kaḍe*, "end, a low or inferior place" (DEDR 1109), as in ಕಡಿಮೆ; the second part looks like ಗಣಿಸು, "to count", but how the word was formed is not settled.
