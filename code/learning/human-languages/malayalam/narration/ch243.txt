Malayalam, chapter 243: Excuse, Blame, Responsibility, Possibility, Basis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഒഴികഴിവ് (oḻikaḻivŭ) — an excuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a matter, then the Malayalam for a situation.

You'll want to know: ഒഴികഴിവ്.
ഒഴികഴിവ് — oḻikaḻivŭ — "an excuse".

The word, taken apart.
ഒഴികഴിവ് (oḻikaḻivŭ) is native Dravidian, a pair of verbal nouns, "escape and slipping away": the verb "to quit, give way" (DEDR 1009, with Tamil oḻi) and the verb "to pass, slip away" (DEDR 1356, with Tamil kaḻi).

ഉത്തരവാദിത്തം (uttaravādittaṁ) is built from Sanskrit uttara, "answer" (CDIAL 1767), and vāditva, "being a speaker": responsibility is being the one who must answer.
