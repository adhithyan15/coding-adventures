Gujarati, chapter 64: Flower, Mountain and Forest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ફૂલ (phūl) — a flower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a garden, then the Gujarati for an address.
