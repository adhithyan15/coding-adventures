## You'll want to know: そだつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C210-PLANT210-04]; assesses=[] -->

**そだつ** — *sodatsu* — "to grow; to grow up".

> **この きは よく そだちます。** — *kono ki wa yoku sodachimasu* — "This tree grows well."

Polite **そだちます**, *sodachimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そだつ** (*sodatsu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
