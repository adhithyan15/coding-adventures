## You'll want to know: దృశ్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C456-SHIKARU456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**దృశ్యం** — *dṛśyaṁ* — "a view; a scene".

> కొండ మీద నుంచి దృశ్యం చాలా అందంగా ఉంది.
>
> *koṇḍa mīda nuñci dṛśyaṁ cālā andaṅgā undi.*
>
> The view from the top of the hill is very beautiful.

A view of nature, and also a scene in a film or a play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దృశ్యం** (*dṛśyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dṛśya*, "visible, what is to be seen" (Monier-Williams), from *dṛś*, "to see".
