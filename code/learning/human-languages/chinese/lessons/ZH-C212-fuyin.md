## You'll want to know: fùyìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C212-SERVICE212-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fùyìn* — "to photocopy".

> *qǐng bāng wǒ fùyìn shēnfènzhèng* — Could you copy my identity card for me, please?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fùyìn* is built from *fù* 'double' and *yìn* 'a seal, a print', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjuwk* and *'jinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a lined garment.
