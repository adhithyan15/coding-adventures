## You'll want to know: معدل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C393-TAQYIM393-03]; assesses=[] -->

**معدل** — *muʿaddal* — "an average".

> معدل أختي ممتاز هذه السنة.
>
> *muʿaddal ukhtī mumtāz hādhihi as-sana.*
>
> My sister's average is excellent this year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معدل** (*muʿaddal*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-d-l*, "to be equal, straight" (Lane). *Muʿaddal* is the Form II passive participle, made even: an average, a rate.
