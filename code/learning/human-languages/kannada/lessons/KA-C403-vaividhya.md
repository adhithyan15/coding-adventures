## You'll want to know: ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C403-SAMAJA403-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ** — *vaividhya* — "diversity; variety".

> ಈ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಭಾಷೆಗಳ ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ī ūrinalli bhāṣegaḷa vaividhya ide.*
>
> This town has a variety of languages.

ವೈವಿಧ್ಯಮಯ, varied.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ** (*vaividhya*) is built on Sanskrit *vividha*, "of various sorts, manifold" (Monier-Williams), with the vowel strengthening that makes an abstract noun.
