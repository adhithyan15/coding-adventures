## You'll want to know: guǎnlǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C490-PRESSURE490-01]; assesses=[] -->

*guǎnlǐ* — "to manage; to be in charge of".

> *tā guǎnlǐ yì jiā fàndiàn* — She manages a restaurant.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: guǎn is said with a rising tone before lǐ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guǎnlǐ* is built from *guǎn* 'to be in charge' and 理 'to put in order', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwanX* and *liX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a pipe (a wind instrument)' and 'to be in charge', and the second 'to work jade' and 'a pattern'.
