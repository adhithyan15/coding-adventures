## You'll want to know: আদর্শ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C350-ADORSHO350-01]; assesses=[] -->

**আদর্শ** — *ādôrshô* — "an ideal; a model to follow".

> সত্যি বলা আমাদের পরিবারের আদর্শ।
>
> *sôtti bôlā āmāder pôribārer ādôrshô.*
>
> Telling the truth is our family's ideal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আদর্শ** (*ādôrshô*) is Sanskrit *ādarśa*, a mirror (Monier-Williams). The sense 'an ideal', the image you shape yourself by, is a later one.
