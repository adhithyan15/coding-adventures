## You'll want to know: महात्मा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C461-SATPURUSA461-01]; assesses=[] -->

**महात्मा** — *mahātmā* — "a great soul; a noble person".

> महात्मा दरिद्रस्य साहाय्यं करोति।
>
> *mahātmā daridrasya sāhāyyaṃ karoti.*
>
> A noble soul helps the poor.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**महात्मा** (*mahātmā*) is built from **महत्**, "great", and **आत्मा**, "the self, the soul"; MW gives "high-souled, magnanimous, noble" (MW).

## What you've built
