## You'll want to know: じこくひょう

**じこくひょう** — *jikokuhyō* — "a timetable".

> **じこくひょうで しはつの じかんを しらべました。** — *jikokuhyō de shihatsu no jikan o shirabemashita* — "I checked the time of the first train in the timetable."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じこくひょう** (*jikokuhyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "time" (Middle Chinese *dzyi*), *koku*, "engrave" (*khok*) and *hyō*, "table" (*pjewX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
