## You'll want to know: نشال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C321-SARIQ321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نشال** — *nashshāl* — "a pickpocket".

> سرق نشال محفظتي في الحافلة.
>
> *saraqa nashshāl miḥfaẓatī fī al-ḥāfila.*
>
> A pickpocket stole my wallet on the bus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نشال** (*nashshāl*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *n-sh-l*, "to snatch out quickly". The *faʿʿāl* pattern names someone who does a thing as his trade.
