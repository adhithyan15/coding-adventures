## You'll want to know: மரியாதை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C49-COURTESY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**மரியாதை** (*mariyādai*) — "respect".

Sanskrit *maryādā*, which started life as a boundary mark — the line at the edge of a field. Respect, in that picture, is knowing where the line runs and not stepping over it.

Tamil shows it in its endings as much as in its words. You have been doing it since *nīṅgaḷ*, where the ending meant for many is aimed at one person on purpose: *mariyādai* built out of shape rather than out of vocabulary.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two: what you feel, and what you show.
