## You'll want to know: আচরণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C456-CHORITRO456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**আচরণ** — *āchôrôn* — "conduct; the way someone behaves".

> ছেলেটির ভদ্র আচরণ আমার ভালো লাগল।
>
> *chheleṭir bhôdrô āchôrôn āmār bhālo lāglô.*
>
> I liked the boy's polite conduct.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আচরণ** (*āchôrôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ācaraṇa*, "undertaking, practising, conduct" (Monier-Williams).
