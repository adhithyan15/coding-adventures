## You'll want to know: resmungão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C474-RABUGENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**resmungão** — "grumbler; grumbling".

> O meu avô é um resmungão, mas tem bom coração.
>
> *My grandfather is a grumbler, but he has a good heart.*

Built on *resmungar* "to grumble" with *-ão*; a woman is *resmungona*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*resmungão* is built on *resmungar* "to grumble" with *-ão* (EtymDB); the origin of the verb itself is not settled.
