Bengali, chapter 127: King, Queen, Priest, Factory, Labourer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
রাজা (rājā) — a king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a grocer, then the Bengali for a nurse.

You'll want to know: রাজা.
রাজা — rājā — "a king".

The word, taken apart.
রাজা (rājā) is Sanskrit rājan, "chieftain, king", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 10679). The word inherited from it is rāy, "prince", which CDIAL lists.

You'll want to know: রানি.
রানি — rāni — "a queen".

The word, taken apart.
রানি (rāni) goes back to Sanskrit rājñī, "queen". CDIAL gives no Bengali form under that entry, but it lists Assamese rāni and Oriya rāṇi, "queen", the forms nearest to Bengali (CDIAL 10692).
