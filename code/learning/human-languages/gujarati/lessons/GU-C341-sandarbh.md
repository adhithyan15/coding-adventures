## You'll want to know: સંદર્ભ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C341-DALIL341-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સંદર્ભ** — *sandarbh* — "a context".

> આ શબ્દનો અર્થ સંદર્ભ પર આધાર રાખે છે.
>
> *ā śabdano arth sandarbh par ādhār rākhe che.*
>
> The meaning of this word depends on the context.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંદર્ભ** (*sandarbh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃdarbha*, "a stringing together" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati uses it for the setting that gives words their sense.
