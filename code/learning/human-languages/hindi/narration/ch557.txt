Hindi, chapter 557: Fears and Reassurance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अंदेशा (andeśā) — a worry; a misgiving.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a groundless fear, then the Hindi for a firm decision.

You'll want to know: अंदेशा.
अंदेशा — andeśā — "a worry; a misgiving".
मुझे अंदेशा था कि ट्रेन देर से आएगी। Mujhe andeśā thā ki ṭren der se āegī. I had a feeling that the train would be late.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अंदेशा (andeśā) is usually traced to Persian andēsha, "thought, anxiety".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
