## You'll want to know: प्रावारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C440-HIMA440-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रावारः** — *prāvāraḥ* — "a cloak".

> शीते वृद्धः प्रावारं धारयति।
>
> *śīte vṛddhaḥ prāvāraṃ dhārayati.*
>
> In the cold the old man wears a cloak.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रावारः** (*prāvāraḥ*) is built from *prā*, a form of *pra*, and the root √vṛ, "to cover"; MW gives *prāvāra* as an outer garment, a mantle, a cloak (MW).
