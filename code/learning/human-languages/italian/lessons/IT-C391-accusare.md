## You'll want to know: accusare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C391-ACCUSA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**accusare** — "to accuse".

> Mi accusano di aver preso i soldi, ma non è vero.
>
> *They accuse me of taking the money, but it is not true.*

*Accusare qualcuno di qualcosa*. *Accusare un dolore* — to feel a pain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accusare* is inherited from Latin *accūsāre* "to call to account, to accuse" (EtymDB), built from *ad* "to" and *causa* "a case, a lawsuit".
