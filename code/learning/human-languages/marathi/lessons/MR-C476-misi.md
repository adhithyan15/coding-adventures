## You'll want to know: मिशी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C476-CEHRA476-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मिशी** — *miśī* — "a moustache".

> माझ्या वडिलांना जाड मिशी आहे.
>
> *mājhyā vaḍilānnā jāḍ miśī āhe.*
>
> My father has a thick moustache.

Feminine. Plural: मिशा, *miśā*.

Often plural: मिशा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिशी** (*miśī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
