## You'll want to know: विक्री
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C424-VIKRI424-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विक्री** — *vikrī* — "a sale; selling".

> या महिन्यात दुकानाची विक्री वाढली.
>
> *yā mahinyāt dukānācī vikrī vāḍhlī.*
>
> This month the shop’s sales went up.

Feminine.

विक्रीला, for sale: घर विक्रीला आहे, the house is for sale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विक्री** (*vikrī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *vikrī*, "sale", under Sanskrit *vikraya*, "sale" (CDIAL 11638).
