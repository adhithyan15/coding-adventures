## You'll want to know: नापणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C211-SIZE211-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नापणो** — *nāpṇo* — "to measure".

> मिस्त्री भींत नापै है।
>
> *mistrī bhī̃t nāpai hai.*
>
> The mason measures the wall.

Past (I …): म्हैं कमरो नाप्यो, *mhaĩ kamro nāpyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नापणो** (*nāpṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *jñāpyate*, "is made known". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *nāpnā*, "to measure" (CDIAL 5283).
