## You'll want to know: جھالر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C514-KARHAI514-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جھالر** — *jhālar* — "a fringe (on cloth)".

> دلہن کے دوپٹے پر سنہری جھالر ہے۔
>
> *dulhan ke dupaṭṭe par sunahrī jhālar hai.*
>
> The bride's scarf has a golden fringe.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھالر** (*jhālar*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhālar*, "a fringe", under Sanskrit *jhallarī*, "a curl" (CDIAL 5356).
