Kannada, chapter 329: The News on Every Channel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ (mādhyama) — the media; a medium.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to abscond, then the Kannada for a track.

You'll want to know: ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ.
ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ — mādhyama — "the media; a medium".
ಮಾಧ್ಯಮಗಳು ಆ ಘಟನೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ದಿನವಿಡೀ ಮಾತಾಡಿದವು. mādhyamagaḷu ā ghaṭaneya bagge dinaviḍī mātāḍidavu. The media talked about that incident all day.
Also a medium of teaching: ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ (mādhyama) ಶಾಲೆ, a Kannada-medium school.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ (mādhyama) is borrowed from Sanskrit mādhyama, "relating to the middle, central" (Monier-Williams), from madhya, "middle" (CDIAL 9804); the sense "a medium, the media" is modern.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವಾಹಿನಿ (vāhini) — a TV channel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a track, then the Kannada for the media.

You'll want to know: ವಾಹಿನಿ.
ವಾಹಿನಿ — vāhini — "a TV channel".
ಈ ವಾಹಿನಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ದಿನವಿಡೀ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ. ī vāhiniyalli dinaviḍī suddi baruttade. This channel has news all day.
Plural: ವಾಹಿನಿಗಳು, vāhinigaḷu.
ಸುದ್ದಿ ವಾಹಿನಿ (vāhini), a news channel.

The word, taken apart.
ವಾಹಿನಿ (vāhini) is borrowed from Sanskrit vāhinī, "an army", and in later use "a river", that which flows and carries (Monier-Williams); the sense "a channel" is modern.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ವಾರ್ತೆ (vārte) — a news bulletin; news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the media, then the Kannada for a TV channel.

You'll want to know: ವಾರ್ತೆ.
ವಾರ್ತೆ — vārte — "a news bulletin; news".
ಅಜ್ಜ ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಎಂಟು ಗಂಟೆಯ ವಾರ್ತೆ ಕೇಳುತ್ತಾರೆ. ajja pratidina rātri eṇṭu gaṇṭeya vārte kēḷuttāre. Grandfather listens to the eight o'clock news bulletin every night.
ವಾರ್ತೆ (vārte) is the formal word on radio and television; in talk people say ಸುದ್ದಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ವಾರ್ತೆ (vārte) is borrowed from Sanskrit vārttā, "an account of what has happened, tidings, news" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಘೋಷಿಸು (ghōṣisu) — to announce; to declare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a TV channel, then the Kannada for a news bulletin.

You'll want to know: ಘೋಷಿಸು.
ಘೋಷಿಸು — ghōṣisu — "to announce; to declare".
ಸರ್ಕಾರ ನಾಳೆ ರಜೆ ಅಂತ ಘೋಷಿಸಿತು. sarkāra nāḷe raje anta ghōṣisitu. The government announced that tomorrow is a holiday.
Past (I …): ಘೋಷಿಸಿದೆ, ghōṣiside.
What is announced comes before ಎಂದು or ಅಂತ: ರಜೆ ಅಂತ ಘೋಷಿಸು (ghōṣisu).

The word, taken apart.
ಘೋಷಿಸು (ghōṣisu) is built on Sanskrit ghoṣa, "a noise, the cry of a crowd", whose derivative ghoṣaṇā means "a proclamation" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹೇಳಿಕೆ (hēḷike) — a statement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a news bulletin, then the Kannada for to announce.

You'll want to know: ಹೇಳಿಕೆ.
ಹೇಳಿಕೆ — hēḷike — "a statement".
ಪೊಲೀಸರ ಹೇಳಿಕೆ ಎಲ್ಲ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂತು. polīsara hēḷike ella patrikegaḷalli bantu. The police statement appeared in every newspaper.
ಹೇಳು, to say, with -ಇಕೆ, which makes a noun of a verb: what is said.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೇಳಿಕೆ (hēḷike) is a native Kannada noun made from the verb hēḷu, "to say" (DEDR 4430), with the ending -ike that turns verbs into nouns.
