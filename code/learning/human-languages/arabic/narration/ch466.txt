Arabic, chapter 466: Dust and dunes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غبار (ghubār) — fine dust; dust in the air.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for coal, then the Arabic for to consume.

You'll want to know: غبار.
غبار — ghubār — "fine dust; dust in the air".
كان الغبار على كل الكراسي. kāna al-ghubār ʿalā kull al-karāsī. There was dust on all the chairs.
turāb is earth and soil; ghubār is the dust that rises and settles.

The word, taken apart.
غبار (ghubār) is built on the native Arabic root gh-b-r, "dust" (Lane).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for fine dust, then the Arabic for dusty.

You'll want to know: تصحر.
تصحر — taṣaḥḥur — "desertification".
التصحر مشكلة في جنوب البلد. at-taṣaḥḥur mushkila fī janūb al-balad. Desertification is a problem in the south of the country.

The word, taken apart.
تصحر (taṣaḥḥur) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-ḥ-r, "a desert" (Lane). It is a modern Form V verbal noun, "becoming a desert".

You'll want to know: كثيب.
كثيب — kathīb — "a sand dune".

Plural: كثبان, kuthbān.

The word, taken apart.
كثيب (kathīb) is built on the native Arabic root k-th-b, "to collect together" (Lane). A dune is sand gathered into a heap.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اختنق (ikhtanaqa) — to choke; to suffocate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for desertification, then the Arabic for a sand dune.

You'll want to know: اختنق.
اختنق — ikhtanaqa — "to choke; to suffocate".
اختنق جدي من الغبار في العاصفة. ikhtanaqa jaddī min al-ghubār fī al-ʿāṣifa. My grandfather choked on the dust in the storm.
Present: يختنق, yakhtaniq.

The word, taken apart.
اختنق (ikhtanaqa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-n-q, "to strangle" (Lane). It is Form VIII, "to be choked".
