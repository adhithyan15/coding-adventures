## You'll want to know: واجه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C345-MUWAJAHA345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**واجه** — *wājaha* — "to confront, to face".

> واجه الفريق مشكلة كبيرة.
>
> *wājaha al-farīq mushkila kabīra.*
>
> The team faced a big problem.

Present: يواجه, *yuwājih*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**واجه** (*wājaha*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-j-h* (Lane), the root of the taught *wajh*, a face. It is Form III, "to meet face to face".
