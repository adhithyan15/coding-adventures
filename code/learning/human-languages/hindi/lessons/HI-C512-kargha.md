## You'll want to know: करघा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C512-KARGHA512-04]; assesses=[] -->

**करघा** — *karghā* — "a loom".

> करघे पर वह एक दरी बुन रहा है।
>
> *Karghe par vah ek darī bun rahā hai.*
>
> He is weaving a rug on the loom.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**करघा** (*karghā*) is usually traced to Persian *kārgāh*, "a workplace". Hindi narrowed it to the weaver's loom.
