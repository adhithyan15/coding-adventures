Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 45: Stomach, Finger, Lip, Face, Knee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पेट (peṭ) — the stomach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a tooth, then the Marwadi for the throat.

You'll want to know: पेट.
पेट — peṭ — "the stomach".

The word, taken apart.
पेट (peṭ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from peṭṭa, "belly", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari peṭa under it (CDIAL 8376). CDIAL notes that the word may be the same as one for "basket".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: आंगळी.
आंगळी — āṅgḷī — "a finger".

The word, taken apart.
आंगळी (āṅgḷī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aṅguli, "finger, toe". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 135); the match is through Gujarati ā̃gḷī. Marwadi, like Gujarati, says the l curled back.

Lesson 3 of 5.
होठ (hoṭh) — a lip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the stomach, then the Marwadi for a finger.

You'll want to know: होठ.
होठ — hoṭh — "a lip".

The word, taken apart.
होठ (hoṭh) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit oṣṭha, "lip", under which CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati hoṭh (CDIAL 2563). Where the h- came from is not settled: a proposal CDIAL records traces it to adhaoṣṭha, "lower lip" (CDIAL 245a).

Lesson 4 of 5.
मूंडो (mū̃ḍo) — the face, the mouth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a finger, then the Marwadi for a lip.

You'll want to know: मूंडो.
मूंडो — mū̃ḍo — "the face, the mouth".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists Old Marwari mūḍho, "face", under Sanskrit mūrdhan, "head, top" (CDIAL 10247). मूंडो (mū̃ḍo) is a close form of the same word, with a nasal vowel and no breath after the ḍ; CDIAL does not list that exact shape.

Lesson 5 of 5.
गोडो (goḍo) — a knee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a lip, then the Marwadi for the face.

You'll want to know: गोडो.
गोडो — goḍo — "a knee".

The word, taken apart.
गोडो (goḍo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from goḍḍa, "foot, leg, knee", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4272); the match is through Punjabi goḍḍā and Hindi goṛ. CDIAL sets it in a group of words for "heel, ankle, knee, wrist".
