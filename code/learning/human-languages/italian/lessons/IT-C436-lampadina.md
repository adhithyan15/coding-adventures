## You'll want to know: la lampadina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C436-LUCE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la lampadina** — "the light bulb".

> In bagno serve una lampadina nuova.
>
> *A new bulb is needed in the bathroom.*

*La lampada* is a lamp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lampadina* is a little *lampada* 'lamp', which is inherited from Latin *lampas*, from Greek *lampás* 'torch' (Lewis & Short).
