## You'll want to know: narrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C322-RECIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**narrer** — "to narrate, to tell".

> Le grand-père narrait des histoires de guerre.
>
> *The grandfather used to tell war stories.*

Literary; in speech, *raconter*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narrer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *narrāre* "to tell, to relate" (EtymDB).
