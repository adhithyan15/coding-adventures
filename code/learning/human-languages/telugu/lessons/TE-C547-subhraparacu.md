# శుభ్రపరచు (śubhraparacu) — to cleanse; to clean

## You'll want to know: శుభ్రపరచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C547-INTIPANI547-01]; assesses=[] -->

**శుభ్రపరచు** — *śubhraparacu* — "to cleanse; to clean".

> పండుగకు ముందు ఇల్లంతా శుభ్రపరిచాము.
>
> *paṇḍugaku mundu illantā śubhraparicāmu.*
>
> Before the festival we cleaned the whole house.

Past (I …): శుభ్రపరిచాను, *śubhraparicānu*.

Formal; శుభ్రం చేయు in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శుభ్రపరచు** (*śubhraparacu*) is built from శుభ్రం, "clean", from Sanskrit *śubhra*, "bright, white" (CDIAL 12539) and *paracu*, "to cause, to make", as in ఆశ్చర్యపరచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
