## You'll want to know: xuéxiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xuéxiào* — "school".

> *wǒmen qù xuéxiào* — We're off to school.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuéxiào* is built from 学 'learning', inherited from Middle Chinese *haewk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), and a second character the dictionary records as *haewH*, the title of an officer. The school sense is not given there, so how it came to mean school is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
