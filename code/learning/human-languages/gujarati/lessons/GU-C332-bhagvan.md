## You'll want to know: ભગવાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C332-DANTKATHA332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ભગવાન** — *bhagvān* — "god; the Lord".

> લોકોએ ભગવાનને પ્રાર્થના કરી.
>
> *lokoe bhagvānne prārthanā karī.*
>
> The people prayed to god.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભગવાન** (*bhagvān*) is Sanskrit *bhagavān*, the nominative of *bhagavat*, "fortunate, blessed", and so "the Lord" (Monier-Williams). Gujarati keeps the nominative ending as the whole word.
