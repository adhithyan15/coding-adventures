Tamil, chapter 197: To remind, To earn, To avoid, To move house, To contact.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
நினைவூட்டு (niṉaivūṭṭu) — to remind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to feel, then the Tamil for to soak.

You'll want to know: நினைவூட்டு.
நினைவூட்டு — niṉaivūṭṭu — "to remind".

The word, taken apart.
நினைவூட்டு (niṉaivūṭṭu) is native: niṉaivu, "memory", from niṉai, "to think, to remember" (DEDR 3683), and ūṭṭu, "to feed" (DEDR 600): to remind is "to feed the memory".

Lesson 4 of 5.
குடிபெயர் (kuṭipeyar) — to move house, to relocate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to earn, then the Tamil for to avoid.

You'll want to know: குடிபெயர்.
குடிபெயர் — kuṭipeyar — "to move house, to relocate".

The word, taken apart.
குடிபெயர் (kuṭipeyar) is native: kuṭi, "house, home" (DEDR 1655, which compares Sanskrit kuṭi, "hut"), and peyar, "to move" (DEDR 4409): "move house". The peyar that means "name" is a different entry.
