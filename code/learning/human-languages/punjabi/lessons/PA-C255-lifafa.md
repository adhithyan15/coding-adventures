## You'll want to know: ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C255-POST255-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ** — *lifāfā* — "an envelope".

> ਚਿੱਠੀ ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੱਖੋ।
>
> *chiṭṭhī lifāfe vich rakkho.*
>
> Put the letter in the envelope.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ** (*lifāfā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *lifāfa*, "a wrapper".
