## You'll want to know: حکم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C312-BAGHAVAT312-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حکم** — *hukm* — "an order; a command".

> بادشاہ نے ایک نیا حکم دیا۔
>
> *bādshāh ne ek nayā hukm diyā.*
>
> The king gave a new order.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حکم** (*hukm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥukm*, "a judgement, a command", a cousin of **حکومت**.
