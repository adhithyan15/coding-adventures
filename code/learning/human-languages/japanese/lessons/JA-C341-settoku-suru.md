## You'll want to know: せっとくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C341-SETTOKU341-01]; assesses=[] -->

**せっとくする** — *settoku suru* — "to persuade".

> **ははが せっとくしたので、ちちは やめました。** — *haha ga settoku shita node, chichi wa yamemashita* — "My mother talked him round, so my father gave it up."

Polite **せっとくします**, *settokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ははが ちちを せっとくしました。**, *haha ga chichi o settoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せっとくする** (*settoku suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *settoku*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *setsu*, "explanation" (Middle Chinese *sywet*) and *toku*, "gain" (*tok*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as *set-toku*.
