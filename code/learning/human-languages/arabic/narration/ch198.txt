Arabic, chapter 198: To allow, To prevent, To happen, To fall, To dig.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سمح (samaḥa) — to allow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to pay attention, then the Arabic for to consider.

You'll want to know: سمح.
سمح — samaḥa — "to allow".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does سمح (samaḥa) mean? ("To allow".) Say it once more.
