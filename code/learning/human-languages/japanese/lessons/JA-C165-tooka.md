## You'll want to know: とおか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C165-DATES165-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とおか** — *tōka* — "the tenth of the month; ten days".

> **とおかに ともだちと あう つもりです。** — *tōka ni tomodachi to au tsumori desu* — "I plan to meet a friend on the tenth."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とおか** (*tōka*) is native Japanese, built from *tō*, "ten", and *ka*, "day", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
