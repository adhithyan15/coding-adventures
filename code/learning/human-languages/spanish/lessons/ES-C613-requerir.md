## You'll want to know: requerir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C613-REQUIERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**requerir** — "to require, to call for".

> El puesto requiere experiencia.
>
> *The post requires experience.*

Like *sentir*: *requiere, requirió*. Common in job offers and rules.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*requerir* is inherited from Latin *requirere* 'to look for again, to need', built from *re* and *quaerere* 'to seek', and it follows *querer* in its forms (EtymDB).
