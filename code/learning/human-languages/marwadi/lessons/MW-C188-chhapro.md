## You'll want to know: छपरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C188-PHUL188-05]; assesses=[] -->

**छपरो** — *chhapro* — "a thatched shelter".

> छपरो जूनो है।
>
> *chhapro jūno hai.*
>
> The thatched shelter is old.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छपरो** (*chhapro*) is inherited from Sanskrit *chattvara*, "a house, a bower"; CDIAL lists Hindi *chaprā*, "a sloping thatch, a hut" (CDIAL 4976). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
