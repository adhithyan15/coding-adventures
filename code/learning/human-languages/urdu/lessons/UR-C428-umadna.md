## You'll want to know: امڈنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C428-BOOND428-05]; assesses=[] -->

**امڈنا** — *umaḍnā* — "to gather (of clouds); to well up".

> شام کو کالے بادل امڈے۔
>
> *shām ko kāle bādal umaḍe.*
>
> In the evening dark clouds piled up.

Past (it …): وہ بادل امڈا, *voh bādal umaḍā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امڈنا** (*umaḍnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *umaḍnā*, "to swell, to heave", under *ummaḍ*, "rise, bubble up", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2344).
