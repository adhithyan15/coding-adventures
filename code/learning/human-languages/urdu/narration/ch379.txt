Urdu, chapter 379: Effort.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
محنت (mehnat) — hard work; effort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a scheme, then the Urdu for to achieve.

You'll want to know: محنت.
محنت — mehnat — "hard work; effort".
اگر تم نے محنت کی تو نتیجہ اچھا ہو گا۔ agar tum ne mehnat kī to natījā achchhā ho gā. If you work hard, the result will be good.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
محنت (mehnat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic miḥna, "a trial, a hardship"; محنتی, "hardworking", is built on it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
لگن (lagan) — dedication; devotion (to a task).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for hard work, then the Urdu for enthusiasm.

You'll want to know: لگن.
لگن — lagan — "dedication; devotion (to a task)".
اگر اس میں لگن ہوتی تو وہ آج ماہر ہوتا۔ agar us meṅ lagan hotī to voh āj māhir hotā. If he had been dedicated, he would be an expert today.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
لگن (lagan) is built on لگنا, which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lagnā, "to be attached to", under Sanskrit lagyati, "adheres" (CDIAL 10895). Sticking to a task.

Lesson 5 of 5.
دہرانا (dohrānā) — to go over again; to repeat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for dedication, then the Urdu for without a break.

You'll want to know: دہرانا.
دہرانا — dohrānā — "to go over again; to repeat".
اگر تم سبق دہراؤ گے تو یاد رہے گا۔ agar tum sabaq dohrāo ge to yād rahe gā. If you go over the lesson, you will remember it.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سبق دہرایا, us ne sabaq dohrāyā.

The word, taken apart.
دہرانا (dohrānā) is a verb made on dohrā, "double", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin dohrā under dudhāra, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6407). To do a thing twice over.
