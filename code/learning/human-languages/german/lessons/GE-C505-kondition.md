## You'll want to know: die Kondition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C505-TRAINING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Kondition** — "fitness, physical condition".

> Nach dem Winter ist meine Kondition schlecht.
>
> *After the winter my fitness is poor.*

No plural in this sense. *Die Konditionen* — terms of a deal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kondition* comes from Medieval Latin *conditio*, from Latin *condiciō* "agreement, condition" (EtymDB).
