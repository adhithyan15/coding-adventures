## You'll want to know: વિધવા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C500-VARSO500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વિધવા** — *vidhvā* — "a widow".

> પતિ ગુજરી ગયા પછી મારી કાકી વિધવા થઈ.
>
> *pati gujrī gayā pachī mārī kākī vidhvā thaī.*
>
> My aunt was widowed when her husband passed away.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિધવા** (*vidhvā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vidhavā*, "a widow", as Monier-Williams records it.
