## You'll want to know: ਖੱਡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C295-CRACK295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੱਡ** — *khaḍḍ* — "a ravine; a deep pit".

> ਪਹਾੜ ਦੇ ਹੇਠਾਂ ਡੂੰਘੀ ਖੱਡ ਹੈ।
>
> *pahāṛ de heṭhāṁ ḍūṅghī khaḍḍ hai.*
>
> There is a deep ravine below the mountain.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੱਡ** (*khaḍḍ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khaḍḍa*, "hole, pit"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khaḍḍ*, "pit, ravine", under it (CDIAL 3790).
