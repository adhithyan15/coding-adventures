## You'll want to know: el tratamiento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C738-CONSULTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el tratamiento** — "the treatment".

> El tratamiento dura dos semanas.
>
> *The treatment lasts two weeks.*

Also a form of address: *el tratamiento de usted*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tratamiento* is built on *tratar* "to treat", which is usually traced to Latin *tractāre* "to handle".
