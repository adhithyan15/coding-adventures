## You'll want to know: うるさい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C328-KOUKYOU328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うるさい** — *urusai* — "annoying; noisy".

> **そとが うるさいから、まどは あけないで ください。** — *soto ga urusai kara, mado wa akenaide kudasai* — "It is noisy outside, so please do not open the window."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うるさい** (*urusai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
