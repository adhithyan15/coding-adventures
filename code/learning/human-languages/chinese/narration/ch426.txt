Mandarin Chinese, chapter 426: Booking a Room.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dàchuángfáng — room with a double bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a room key card, then the Mandarin for the hotel lobby.

You'll want to know: dàchuángfáng.
dàchuángfáng — "room with a double bed".
wǒmen yào yì jiān dàchuángfáng — We'd like a double room.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dàchuángfáng is built from 大 'big', chuáng 'a bed' and 房 'a room', all inherited from Middle Chinese, daH, dzrjang and bjang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 5.
旅店 (lǚdiàn) — inn; small hotel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a room with a double bed, then the Mandarin for fully booked.

You'll want to know: 旅店.
旅店 — lǚdiàn — "inn; small hotel".
这家旅店很小 zhè jiā lǚdiàn hěn xiǎo — This inn is very small.

The word, taken apart.
旅店 (lǚdiàn) is built from 旅 'to travel' and 店 'an inn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, ljoX and temH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a body of troops, so the sense 'to travel' is not given there.
