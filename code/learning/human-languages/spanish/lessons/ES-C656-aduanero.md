## You'll want to know: el aduanero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C656-FRONTERA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el aduanero** — "the customs officer".

> El aduanero abre mi maleta.
>
> *The customs officer opens my suitcase.*

A woman is *la aduanera*. As an adjective: *el control aduanero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aduanero* is built on *aduana* "customs", which comes from Andalusian Arabic *ad-dīwān* "the office", Arabic *dīwān* (EtymDB).
