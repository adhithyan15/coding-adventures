## You'll want to know: काम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C167-KAM167-01]; assesses=[] -->

**काम** — *kām* — "work".

> हूं रोज घणो काम करूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ roj ghaṇo kām karū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I do a lot of work every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काम** (*kām*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karman*, "act, work"; CDIAL lists Marwari *kām* (CDIAL 2892). With **करणो** it is to work: **काम करो**.
