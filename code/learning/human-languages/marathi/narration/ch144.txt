Marathi, chapter 144: Fox, Donkey, Ox, Mosquito, Forehead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कोल्हा (kolhā) — a fox.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rabbit, then the Marathi for a wolf.

You'll want to know: कोल्हा.
कोल्हा — kolhā — "a fox".

The word, taken apart.
कोल्हा (kolhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kroṣṭṛ, "crying; jackal" (CDIAL 3615). It is built on kruś, "to cry out". CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "jackal".

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an ox, then the Marathi for a mosquito.

You'll want to know: कपाळ.
कपाळ — kapāḷ — "the forehead".

The word, taken apart.
कपाळ (kapāḷ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit kapāla, "skull, cranium"; CDIAL lists it under the variant kappāla (CDIAL 2744). CDIAL thinks the word Indo-European but its later variants at least influenced by Austroasiatic.
