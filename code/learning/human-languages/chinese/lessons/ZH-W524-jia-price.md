## Script — the person piece, then 人 with two legs under it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JIA-PRICE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 价

**Three pieces: the person piece, then 人, then what stands under it.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, the person piece, two strokes**, as in 你: a stroke falling to the left, then a vertical down to the foot.

**The top right piece, 人, two strokes**, as you wrote it, spread wide like a roof: a long stroke falling to the left, then a long stroke falling to the right.

**Under it, two strokes**: a short stroke that drops and falls to the left, then a vertical on the right.

2 + 2 + 2 = six.
