## You'll want to know: ਕਾਰੋਬਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C364-JOB364-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਰੋਬਾਰ** — *kārobār* — "a business".

> ਅਮਨ ਦਾ ਆਪਣਾ ਕਾਰੋਬਾਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *Aman dā āpṇā kārobār hai.*
>
> Aman has his own business.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਰੋਬਾਰ** (*kārobār*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *kār-o-bār*, "work and business".
