## You'll want to know: zumindest
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C367-EVENTUELL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**zumindest** — "at any rate, at least".

> Zumindest hat er sich entschuldigt.
>
> *At any rate, he apologized.*

Close to *wenigstens*. The verb comes right after it: *zumindest hat er …*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zumindest* grew out of *zum mindesten* "at the least", built on *minder* "less", from Old High German *minniro* and Proto-Germanic *minnizô* (EtymDB).
