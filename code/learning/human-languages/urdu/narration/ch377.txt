Urdu, chapter 377: Better and Worse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بدتر (badtar) — worse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for approximately, then the Urdu for a limit.

You'll want to know: بدتر.
بدتر — badtar — "worse".
دوا لیجیے، ورنہ حالت بدتر ہو گی۔ davā lījiye, varnā hālat badtar ho gī. Take the medicine, or you will get worse.

The word, taken apart.
بدتر (badtar) is usually traced to Persian badtar, "worse": bad, "bad", with -tar, "more", the ending that also makes بہتر, "better".

Lesson 4 of 5.
بگڑنا (bigaṛnā) — to get worse; to go wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for excellent, then the Urdu for common.

You'll want to know: بگڑنا.
بگڑنا — bigaṛnā — "to get worse; to go wrong".
اگر موسم بگڑا تو ہم نہیں جائیں گے۔ agar mausam bigṛā to ham nahīṅ jāeṅ ge. If the weather turns bad, we will not go.
Past (it …): وہ کام بگڑ گیا, voh kām bigaṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
بگڑنا (bigaṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bigaṛnā, "to be spoilt", under Sanskrit vighaṭate, "breaks apart" (CDIAL 11673); بگاڑنا, "to spoil", is its partner.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سدھرنا (sudharnā) — to get better; to mend one's ways.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for common, then the Urdu for to get worse.

You'll want to know: سدھرنا.
سدھرنا — sudharnā — "to get better; to mend one's ways".
اگر تم سدھرے نہیں تو مصیبت میں پڑو گے۔ agar tum sudhre nahīṅ to musībat meṅ paṛo ge. If you do not mend your ways, you will land in trouble.
Past (he …): وہ لڑکا سدھر گیا, voh laṛkā sudhar gayā.

The word, taken apart.
سدھرنا (sudharnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sudharnā, "to be mended", under śuddhakāra, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12521); سدھارنا, "to put right", is its partner.
