## You'll want to know: avantageux
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C393-GARANTIE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**avantageux** — "good value, favourable".

> Cet abonnement est très avantageux.
>
> *This subscription is very good value.*

Feminine *avantageuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avantageux* is inherited from Old French *avantageus* (EtymDB), built on *avantage* "advantage", from *avant* "ahead".
