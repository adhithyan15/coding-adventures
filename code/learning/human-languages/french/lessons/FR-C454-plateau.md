## You'll want to know: le plateau
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C454-RELIEF-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le plateau** — "the plateau, the high plain; the tray".

> Le village est sur un plateau, au-dessus de la vallée.
>
> *The village is on a plateau above the valley.*

Plural *les plateaux*. *Un plateau* is also a tray, and a TV set.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*plateau* is built on *plat* 'flat', which goes back to Vulgar Latin *plattus*, from Greek *platýs* 'broad' (EtymDB).
