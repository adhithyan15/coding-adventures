## You'll want to know: أقلية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C387-UMMA387-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أقلية** — *aqalliyya* — "a minority".

> تعيش في البلد أقلية صغيرة.
>
> *taʿīshu fī al-balad aqalliyya ṣaghīra.*
>
> A small minority lives in the country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أقلية** (*aqalliyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-l-l*, "to be few, little" (Lane). *Aqalliyya* is a modern abstract noun from *aqall*, fewer: a minority.
