## You'll want to know: खटकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C377-PRABHAV377-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खटकणे** — *khaṭakṇe* — "to bother; to jar on".

> त्याचं उद्धट बोलणं मला खटकलं.
>
> *tyācaṁ uddhaṭ bolṇaṁ malā khaṭaklaṁ.*
>
> His rude way of talking bothered me.

Past (to him …): त्याला खटकलं, *tyālā khaṭaklaṁ*.

Dative: मला खटकलं, it bothered me; what jars is the subject.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खटकणे** (*khaṭakṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
