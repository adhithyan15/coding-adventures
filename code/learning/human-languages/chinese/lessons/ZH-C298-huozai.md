## You'll want to know: huǒzāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C298-FIRE298-01]; assesses=[] -->

*huǒzāi* — "big fire; a fire that destroys buildings".

> *huǒzāi de yuányīn shì shénme* — What caused the fire?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huǒzāi* is built from 火 'fire' and *zāi* 'a disaster', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwaX* and *tsoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists the second as a spelling of the word for fire sent from heaven.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
