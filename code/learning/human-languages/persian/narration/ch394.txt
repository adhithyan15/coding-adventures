Persian, chapter 394: Wishes and risks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ای کاش (ey kâsh) — if only, I wish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to make sure, then the Persian for apparently.

You'll want to know: ای کاش.
ای کاش (ey kâsh) — "if only, I wish".
ای کاش وقت بیشتری داشتم. ey kâsh vaqt-e bishtari dâshtam. If only I had more time.
It takes the past for a wish about now: ey kâsh dâshtam. Kâsh alone means the same.

The word, taken apart.
ای کاش (ey kâsh) is native Persian, built from ey "oh", the calling word, + kâsh "would that". The origin of kâsh is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ارزیابی کردن (arzyâbi kardan) — to assess, to evaluate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for apparently, then the Persian for if only.

You'll want to know: ارزیابی کردن.
ارزیابی کردن (arzyâbi kardan) — "to assess, to evaluate".
معلم کار هر دانش آموز را ارزیابی میکند. mo'allem kâr-e har dânesh âmuz râ arzyâbi mikonad. The teacher assesses each pupil's work.
Arz "worth" + yâbi "finding", from yâftan, + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
ارزیابی is a modern native coinage built from arz "worth", the stem behind arzesh "value", + yâbi "finding", built on yâb-, the present stem of yâftan "to find". To assess is to find the worth.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تضمین کردن (tazmin kardan) — to guarantee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for if only, then the Persian for to assess.

You'll want to know: تضمین کردن.
تضمین کردن (tazmin kardan) — "to guarantee".
هیچ کس نمیتواند موفقیت را تضمین کند. hich kas nemitavânad movaffaqiyat râ tazmin konad. Nobody can guarantee success.
Tazmin "guarantee" + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
تضمین is usually traced to Arabic taḍmīn "guaranteeing"; Persian says the Arabic ḍ as z. Kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خطر کردن (khatar kardan) — to take a risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to guarantee, then the Persian for a risk.

You'll want to know: خطر کردن.
خطر کردن (khatar kardan) — "to take a risk".
او خطر کرد و شغلش را عوض کرد. u khatar kard va shoghlash râ 'avaz kard. He took a risk and changed his job.
Khatar "danger" + kardan. Literary; everyday speech says risk kardan.

The word, taken apart.
خطر was borrowed from Arabic khaṭar "danger" (EtymDB); with the inherited Persian verb kardan it means to face the danger.
