## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-HOW-WELL-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C152-DE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-COMPLEMENT-01, ZH-LEX-C152-LIULI-01, ZH-LEX-C152-BUCUO-01, ZH-LEX-C152-QINGCHU-01, ZH-LEX-C53-SPEAK53-05, ZH-LEX-C144-MATTER144-05, ZH-LEX-C144-MATTER144-03] -->

> **A:** 他说汉语说得很快
>
> *tā shuō Hànyǔ shuō de hěn kuài* — He speaks Chinese fast.
>
> **B:** 对 他说得很好
>
> *duì, tā shuō de hěn hǎo* — Yes, and he speaks well.
>
> **A:** *tā Hànyǔ shuō de hěn liúlì* — His Chinese is fluent.
>
> **B:** 你呢
>
> *nǐ ne* — And you?
>
> **A:** *wǒ shuō de hěn màn* — I speak slowly.
>
> **B:** *nǐ shuō de hěn qīngchu* — You speak clearly.
>
> **A:** 你做饭做得很好
>
> *nǐ zuò fàn zuò de hěn hǎo* — You cook well.
>
> **B:** *búcuò, wǒ juéde hěn jiǎndān* — Not bad. I find it simple.

Every compliment has the same order: the verb, 得, then how well. When the object comes along, the verb is said twice; when the language goes first as the topic, once.

## What you've built — how well
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-HOW-WELL-01, ZH-SCRIPT-DE-COMPLEMENT-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-COMPLEMENT-01, ZH-LEX-C152-QINGCHU-01, ZH-LEX-C152-BUCUO-01, ZH-LEX-ZENMEYANG-01] -->

The verb, 得, then how well; say the verb twice when it has an object:

- **你说得很好** *nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo* — you speak well.
- **他说汉语说得很快** *tā shuō Hànyǔ shuō de hěn kuài* — he speaks Chinese fast.
- *tā shuō de hěn qīngchu* — he speaks clearly.
- *nǐ zuò de búcuò* — you did pretty well.

**Listen.** *Tā Hànyǔ shuō de zěnmeyàng?*

**Speak.** Say how well somebody you know cooks, then how fast you speak.

[YOU WRITE: 得 — the left of 很, then 日, a long bar, and the right of 对]
