## You'll want to know: ধ্যান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C480-PUSHTI480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ধ্যান** — *dhæn* — "meditation".

> দাদু রোজ ভোরে ধ্যান করেন।
>
> *dādu roj bhore dhæn kôren.*
>
> Grandfather meditates every day at dawn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধ্যান** (*dhæn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dhyāna*, "meditation, reflection" (Monier-Williams).
