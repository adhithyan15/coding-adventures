Japanese, chapter 330: Doing Things Properly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ちゃんと (chanto) — as one should; properly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for one's turn, then the Japanese for a cigarette end.

You'll want to know: ちゃんと.
ちゃんと — chanto — "as one should; properly".
びょうきに なるから、ちゃんと てを あらって ください。 — byōki ni naru kara, chanto te o aratte kudasai — "You will get ill, so wash your hands properly."

The word, taken apart.
ちゃんと (chanto) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
みならう (minarau) — to follow an example; to model oneself on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for as one should, then the Japanese for firmly.

You'll want to know: みならう.
みならう — minarau — "to follow an example; to model oneself on".
あには よく べんきょうするから、みならった ほうが いいです。 — ani wa yoku benkyō suru kara, minaratta hō ga ii desu — "Your brother studies hard, so you should follow his example."
Polite みならいます, minaraimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): おとうとは あにを みならいました。, otōto wa ani o minaraimashita.

The word, taken apart.
みならう (minarau) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from mi, "seeing (miru)" and narau, "to learn", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

You'll want to know: そろえる.
そろえる — soroeru — "to line up neatly; to put in order".
げんかんで くつを そろえた ほうが いいです。 — genkan de kutsu o soroeta hō ga ii desu — "You should line your shoes up neatly in the entrance."
Polite そろえます, soroemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): こどもが くつを そろえました。, kodomo ga kutsu o soroemashita.

The word, taken apart.
そろえる (soroeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
