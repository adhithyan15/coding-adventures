Russian, chapter 406: Parts of a car.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
багажник (bagázhnik) — a boot (of a car); a roof rack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to pump up, then the Russian for to spin.

You'll want to know: багажник.
багажник (bagázhnik) — "a boot (of a car); a roof rack".
Чемоданы лежат в багажнике. Chemodány lezhát v bagázhnike. The suitcases are in the boot.
Built on багаж — luggage. In American English, the trunk.

The word, taken apart.
багажник (bagázhnik) is built on багаж 'luggage' with the ending -ник, the place for it. Багаж was borrowed from French bagage 'baggage, luggage' (EtymDB); EtymDB does not list багажник (bagázhnik) itself.

Lesson 3 of 5.
фара (fára) — a headlight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a boot, then the Russian for a bumper.

You'll want to know: фара.
фара (fára) — "a headlight".
Вечером водитель включил фары. Vécherom vodítel vklyuchíl fáry. In the evening the driver switched on the headlights.
Usually plural: фары.

The word, taken apart.
фара (fára) was borrowed from French phare 'a lighthouse; a headlight' (EtymDB). Phare goes back to Latin pharus 'a lighthouse', from Greek pháros (EtymDB).

Дети сидят на заднем сиденье. Déti sidyát na zádnem sidénye. The children are sitting on the back seat.

Lesson 5 of 5.
дворник (dvórnik) — a windscreen wiper; a caretaker, a street sweeper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a headlight, then the Russian for a seat.

You'll want to know: дворник.
дворник (dvórnik) — "a windscreen wiper; a caretaker, a street sweeper".
Шёл дождь, и дворники работали всю дорогу. Shyol dozhd, i dvórniki rabótali vsyu dorógu. It was raining, and the wipers worked all the way.
The first meaning is a person who sweeps a yard; the wipers sweep the glass.

The word, taken apart.
дворник (dvórnik) is built on двор 'a yard' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Proto-Slavic dvorŭ 'a court'. EtymDB glosses дворник (dvórnik) as the one who keeps the yard and the pavement; the wiper that sweeps the glass took the name later.
