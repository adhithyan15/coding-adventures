## You'll want to know: اختنق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C466-GHUBAR466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اختنق** — *ikhtanaqa* — "to choke; to suffocate".

> اختنق جدي من الغبار في العاصفة.
>
> *ikhtanaqa jaddī min al-ghubār fī al-ʿāṣifa.*
>
> My grandfather choked on the dust in the storm.

Present: يختنق, *yakhtaniq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اختنق** (*ikhtanaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-n-q*, "to strangle" (Lane). It is Form VIII, "to be choked".
