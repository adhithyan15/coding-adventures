## You'll want to know: syngrapha, syngraphae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C419-PERSOLVO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**syngrapha, syngraphae** — "a written bond, a promissory note".

> Syngrapham manū suā scrīpsit.
>
> *He wrote out the bond in his own hand.*

*syngrapha, syngraphae* f., borrowed from Greek *syngraphē*, a written contract signed by both sides.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*syngrapha* was borrowed from Greek *syngraphē* "a written contract" (Lewis & Short), built from *syn* "together" and *graphein* "to write". English *syngraph* comes from it (EtymDB).
