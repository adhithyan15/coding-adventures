Kannada, chapter 458: Pilgrims and Hill Stations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಯಾತ್ರೆ (yātre) — a pilgrimage; a long journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a spot, then the Kannada for to look splendid.

You'll want to know: ಯಾತ್ರೆ.
ಯಾತ್ರೆ — yātre — "a pilgrimage; a long journey".
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಪ್ರತಿ ವರ್ಷ ಯಾತ್ರೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾರೆ. ajji prati varṣa yātrege hōguttāre. Grandmother goes on a pilgrimage every year.
ಯಾತ್ರೆ (yātre) alone can be any long journey; ಯಾತ್ರಿಕ is a pilgrim.

The word, taken apart.
ಯಾತ್ರೆ (yātre) is borrowed from Sanskrit yātrā, "a journey, a festival procession" (CDIAL 10456), from yā, "to go".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಗಿರಿಧಾಮ (giridhāma) — a hill station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to look splendid, then the Kannada for a pilgrimage.

You'll want to know: ಗಿರಿಧಾಮ.
ಗಿರಿಧಾಮ — giridhāma — "a hill station".
ಬೇಸಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಗಿರಿಧಾಮದಲ್ಲಿ ತಣ್ಣಗೆ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ. bēsigeyalli giridhāmadalli taṇṇage iruttade. In summer it is cool at a hill station.
ಗಿರಿ, a hill, plus ಧಾಮ, a dwelling.

The word, taken apart.
ಗಿರಿಧಾಮ (giridhāma) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: giri, "a rock, a mountain" (CDIAL 4161), and dhāman, "a dwelling place" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕರೆದೊಯ್ಯು (karedoyyu) — to take along; to escort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a pilgrimage, then the Kannada for a hill station.

You'll want to know: ಕರೆದೊಯ್ಯು.
ಕರೆದೊಯ್ಯು — karedoyyu — "to take along; to escort".
ನಾನು ಅಜ್ಜಿಯನ್ನು ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನಕ್ಕೆ ಕರೆದೊಯ್ದೆ. nānu ajjiyannu dēvasthānakke karedoyde. I took grandmother to the temple.
Past (I …): ಕರೆದೊಯ್ದೆ, karedoyde.
ಕರೆ, to call, plus ಒಯ್ಯು, to take away: to call someone and take them.

The word, taken apart.
ಕರೆದೊಯ್ಯು (karedoyyu) is a native Dravidian compound: kare, "to call" (DEDR 1291), and oy, "to carry off, to take along" (DEDR 984).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ವಾಪಸಾಗು (vāpasāgu) — to get back; to return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a hill station, then the Kannada for to take along.

You'll want to know: ವಾಪಸಾಗು.
ವಾಪಸಾಗು — vāpasāgu — "to get back; to return".
ಯಾತ್ರೆ ಮುಗಿಸಿ ನಾವು ಮನೆಗೆ ವಾಪಸಾದೆವು. yātre mugisi nāvu manege vāpasādevu. After the pilgrimage we got back home.
Past (I …): ವಾಪಸಾದೆ, vāpasāde.
ವಾಪಸ್, back, is common in speech: ವಾಪಸ್ ಕೊಡು, to give back.

The word, taken apart.
ವಾಪಸಾಗು (vāpasāgu) joins vāpas, "back", usually traced to Persian vāpas, "back, returned", through Hindustani, and the native verb ಆಗು, to become.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತಂಗು (taṅgu) — to stay overnight; to halt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to take along, then the Kannada for to get back.

You'll want to know: ತಂಗು.
ತಂಗು — taṅgu — "to stay overnight; to halt".
ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾವು ಒಂದು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ತಂಗಿದೆವು. dāriyalli nāvu ondu rātri haḷḷiyalli taṅgidevu. On the way we stayed one night in a village.
Past (I …): ತಂಗಿದೆ, taṅgide.
A stop on a journey; ಇರು is to stay in general.

The word, taken apart.
ತಂಗು (taṅgu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3014 lists Kannada taṅgu, "to stop, to stay, to sojourn, to lodge".
