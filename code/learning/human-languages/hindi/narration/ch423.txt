Hindi, chapter 423: Guards and Street Dogs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गार्ड (gārḍ) — a guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a compound, then the Hindi for a thatched roof.

You'll want to know: गार्ड.
गार्ड — gārḍ — "a guard".
बैंक के दरवाज़े पर एक गार्ड खड़ा था। baiṅk ke darvāze par ek gārḍ khaṛā thā. A guard was standing at the door of the bank.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
गार्ड (gārḍ) is borrowed from English guard, the security guard at a bank, a shop or a block of flats; on the railway it is also the guard at the back of the train.

Lesson 2 of 5.
खंभा (khambhā) — a pole; a pillar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a thatched roof, then the Hindi for a guard.

You'll want to know: खंभा.
खंभा — khambhā — "a pole; a pillar".
बिजली का खंभा सड़क पर गिर गया। bijlī kā khambhā saṛak par gir gayā. The electricity pole fell onto the road.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
खंभा (khambhā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit skambha, "a prop, a pillar" (CDIAL 13639), which lists Hindi khambh, "a pillar, a pole"; sk- became kh-.

Lesson 4 of 5.
गुर्राना (gurrānā) — to growl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a pole, then the Hindi for noise.

You'll want to know: गुर्राना.
गुर्राना — gurrānā — "to growl".
रात को गली का कुत्ता गुर्रा रहा था। rāt ko galī kā kuttā gurrā rahā thā. At night the dog in the lane was growling.
Past (it …): वह शेर गुर्राया, vah śer gurrāyā.

The word, taken apart.
गुर्राना (gurrānā) goes back to guragura, "growl", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as imitative (CDIAL 4207), which lists Hindi gurrānā: the word rumbles like the sound.

Past (it …): वह कुत्ता रात भर भौंका, vah kuttā rāt bhar bhaũkā.

The word, taken apart.
भौंकना (bhaũknā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit bukkati, "barks" (CDIAL 9265), which lists Hindi bhaũknā.
