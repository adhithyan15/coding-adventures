## You'll want to know: проигрыш
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C510-RESULTS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**проигрыш** (*próigrysh*) — "a loss (in a game)".

> Проигрыш не испортил нам настроение.
>
> *Próigrysh ne ispórtil nam nastroyéniye.*
>
> Losing didn't spoil our mood.

Masculine. Built on *проиграть* — to lose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*проигрыш* is built on *проиграть* 'to lose', from *игра* 'a game'; EtymDB has no entry.
