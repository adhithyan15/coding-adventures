# ਸ਼ਾਇਦ (shāid) — perhaps

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-PLAN-223, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say *next week we will go to the market*, then
*Aman will come*.

## You'll want to know: ਸ਼ਾਇਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-SHAID-223]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-PLAN-223, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

**ਸ਼ਾਇਦ** — *shāid* — "perhaps". It usually opens the sentence and softens a
plan:

> **ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ।** — *shāid Aman kallh āvegā.* — Perhaps Aman will come tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ਾਇਦ** (*shāid*) is a loan: it is usually traced to Persian *shāyad*, "it may
be; perhaps". CDIAL gives no Punjabi form.
