# చిరిగిపోవు (cirigipōvu) — to get torn

## You'll want to know: చిరిగిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C490-CIRUGU490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**చిరిగిపోవు** — *cirigipōvu* — "to get torn".

> ముళ్ళకు తగిలి నా చొక్కా చిరిగిపోయింది.
>
> *muḷḷaku tagili nā cokkā cirigipōyindi.*
>
> My shirt caught on the thorns and got torn.

Past (it …): చిరిగిపోయింది, *cirigipōyindi*.

Said of cloth and paper. చిరుగు is a tear. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: చిరిగిపోయింది, it got torn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చిరిగిపోవు** (*cirigipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2491 lists Telugu *cirugu*, "to be torn", beside *cīru*, "to tear", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
