## You'll want to know: تصویر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C515-TASVIR515-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تصویر** — *tasvīr* — "a picture".

> دیوار پر دادا کی ایک پرانی تصویر ہے۔
>
> *dīvār par dādā kī ek purānī tasvīr hai.*
>
> There is an old picture of grandfather on the wall.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تصویر** (*tasvīr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taṣwīr*, "a picturing, a portrait".
