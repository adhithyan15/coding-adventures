## You'll want to know: بت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C516-KHATTATI516-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بت** — *but* — "an idol; a carved figure".

> اس نے پتھر سے ایک بت بنایا۔
>
> *us ne patthar se ek but banāyā.*
>
> He carved a figure out of stone.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بت** (*but*) is usually traced to Persian *but*, "an idol, a carved figure".
