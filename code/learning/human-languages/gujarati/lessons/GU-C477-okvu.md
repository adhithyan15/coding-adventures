## You'll want to know: ઓકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C477-ODKAR477-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓકવું** — *okvũ* — "to throw up".

> બાળકે દૂધ ઓકી નાખ્યું.
>
> *bāḷke dūdh okī nākhyũ.*
>
> The baby threw up its milk.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દૂધ ઓક્યું, *teṇe dūdh okyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓકવું** (*okvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ōkk-*, "vomit", an imitative word; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 2538).
