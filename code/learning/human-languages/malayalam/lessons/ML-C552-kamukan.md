# കാമുകൻ (kāmukan) — a boyfriend; a lover

## You'll want to know: കാമുകൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C552-BANDHAM552-04]; assesses=[] -->

**കാമുകൻ** — *kāmukan* — "a boyfriend; a lover".

> അവൾ കാമുകനോടൊപ്പം സിനിമയ്ക്ക് പോയി.
>
> *avaḷ kāmukanōṭoppaṁ sinimaykkŭ pōyi.*
>
> She went to the cinema with her boyfriend.

A girlfriend is കാമുകി.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കാമുകൻ** (*kāmukan*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kāmuka*, "longing for, in love with" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ.
