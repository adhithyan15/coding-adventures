Lesson 3 of 7.
ർ (chillu r) — the r that ends ഞായർ.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ർ and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write ധ (dha) and ബ (ba) once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say ഞായർ (ñāyar), "Sunday". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ർ.
ർ — chillu r, an r with no vowel after it.
It ends ഞായർ (ñāyar), "Sunday". A chillu is the form a consonant takes when it closes a word with no vowel of its own. You already write other chillus; this is the r one.

Writing: ർ.
1. start here: climb around the left arch and carry the upper shoulder right.
2. without lifting, sweep clockwise around the right loop and return to the upper crossing.
3. without lifting, rise into the chillu hook and curl left above the line — and only now lift.
Pen lifts: 0. The pen never leaves the paper.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Sriveenkat, Ml ർ (chillu r) order.gif, Malayalam chillu RR stroke-order animation, 00:03.0–00:08.5 (Wikimedia Commons, 2 July 2023).
