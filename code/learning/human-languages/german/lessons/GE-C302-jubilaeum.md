## You'll want to know: das Jubiläum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C302-RUHESTAND-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Jubiläum** — "the anniversary, the jubilee".

> Die Firma feierte ihr hundertjähriges Jubiläum.
>
> *The company celebrated its hundredth anniversary.*

Plural: *die Jubiläen*. For a firm, a club or years at work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Jubiläum* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *iūbilaeus* "of the jubilee", which came from Hebrew through Greek; EtymDB lists no parent, so this follows the usual account.
