Kannada, chapter 530: Free Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಿರಾಮ (virāma) — a pause; a break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for infatuation, then the Kannada for to live.

You'll want to know: ವಿರಾಮ.
ವಿರಾಮ — virāma — "a pause; a break".
ಎರಡು ಗಂಟೆ ಓದಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ನಾನು ವಿರಾಮ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡೆ. eraḍu gaṇṭe ōdida mēle nānu virāma tegedukoṇḍe. After studying for two hours I took a break.
ವಿರಾಮದ ವೇಳೆ, leisure time. In grammar ಪೂರ್ಣವಿರಾಮ, a full pause, is the full stop.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿರಾಮ (virāma) is borrowed from Sanskrit virāma, "cessation, termination" (Monier-Williams), from vi-ram, "to stop, to rest".

ಟೀವಿ ಅಜ್ಜಿಯ ಮುಖ್ಯ ಮನರಂಜನೆ. ṭīvi ajjiya mukhya manarañjane. The television is grandmother's main entertainment.

The word, taken apart.
ಮನರಂಜನೆ (manarañjane) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit manas, "mind", and rañjana, "pleasing" (CDIAL 10587).

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a pause, then the Kannada for entertainment.

You'll want to know: ಹರಟೆ.
ಹರಟೆ — haraṭe — "a chat; idle talk".
ಸಂಜೆ ಅಜ್ಜಂದಿರು ಜಗಲಿಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಹರಟೆ ಹೊಡೆಯುತ್ತಾರೆ. sañje ajjandiru jagaliya mēle haraṭe hoḍeyuttāre. In the evening the old men chat on the veranda.
ಹರಟೆ (haraṭe) ಹೊಡೆ, to chat away the time.

The word, taken apart.
ಹರಟೆ (haraṭe) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4031 lists Kannada haraṭu, "to talk idly", and haraṭe, "idle talk", beside Tamil paṟai, "to say".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸು.
ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸು — āsvādisu — "to savour; to relish".
ಅಜ್ಜ ಪ್ರತಿ ಹಾಡನ್ನು ಕಣ್ಣು ಮುಚ್ಚಿ ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ. ajja prati hāḍannu kaṇṇu mucci āsvādisuttāre. Grandfather savours every song with his eyes closed.
Past (I …): ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸಿದೆ, āsvādiside.
Of food, music or a view.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸು (āsvādisu) is built on Sanskrit āsvāda, "eating with relish, tasting, enjoying" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮೈಮರೆ (maimare) — to be absorbed; to be lost in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a chat, then the Kannada for to savour.

You'll want to know: ಮೈಮರೆ.
ಮೈಮರೆ — maimare — "to be absorbed; to be lost in".
ಸಂಗೀತ ಕೇಳುತ್ತಾ ಅವಳು ಮೈಮರೆತಳು. saṅgīta kēḷuttā avaḷu maimaretaḷu. Listening to the music, she lost herself in it.
Past (I …): ಮೈಮರೆತೆ, maimarete.
ಮೈ, the body + ಮರೆ, to forget: to forget yourself.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೈಮರೆ (maimare) is a native Dravidian compound: mai, "the body" (DEDR 5073), and maṟe, "to forget" (DEDR 4760).
