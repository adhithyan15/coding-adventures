# Hear *érzi* — son

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-REN-01, ZH-LEX-NU-01] -->

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-ERZI-01]; assesses=[ZH-TONE-NEUTRAL] -->

> *érzi* — **son**

Hear two beats: rising *ér*, then a short, light *zi*. Say the whole word once
from the meaning. Keep the characters covered; the new first shape comes next.
