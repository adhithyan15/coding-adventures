## You'll want to know: गरदन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C251-KANDHO251-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गरदन** — *gardan* — "the neck".

> म्हारी गरदन लांबी है।
>
> *mhārī gardan lā̃bī hai.*
>
> My neck is long.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गरदन** (*gardan*) is usually traced to Persian *gardan*, "neck".
