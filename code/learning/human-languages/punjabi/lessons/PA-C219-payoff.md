# Breakfast: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I drank tea*, then *I ate a roti*.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219, PA-LEX-KADDHNA-219, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDHA-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *we drank milk*] Say **ਅਸੀਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *I took out the book*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਕੱਢੀ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਧੀ।**] Give its meaning, then point to **ਢ** in **ਕੱਢਣਾ**.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।**]

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Who decides **ਪੀਤਾ** or **ਪੀਤੀ** after **ਮੈਂ**? (The thing drunk.)
