## You'll want to know: നൈപുണ്യം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C549-PUROGATI549-01]; assesses=[] -->

**നൈപുണ്യം** — *naipuṇyaṁ* — "proficiency; expertise".

> കമ്പ്യൂട്ടറിൽ അവൾക്ക് നല്ല നൈപുണ്യം ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *kampyūṭṭaṟil avaḷkkŭ nalla naipuṇyaṁ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> She is very skilled with computers.

Formal; കഴിവ് is the everyday word for skill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നൈപുണ്യം** (*naipuṇyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *naipuṇya*, "dexterity, skill" (Monier-Williams), the noun of *nipuṇa*, "clever".
