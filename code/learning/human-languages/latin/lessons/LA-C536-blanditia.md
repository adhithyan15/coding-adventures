## You'll want to know: blanditia, blanditiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C536-BLAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**blanditia, blanditiae** — "flattery, coaxing".

> Blanditiīs fīliae pater nōn resistit.
>
> *The father does not resist his daughter's coaxing.*

Feminine, first declension; often plural *blanditiae* — coaxing words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**blanditia** is built on *blandus* "coaxing, flattering" (Lewis & Short). English *blandishment* comes from the related verb *blandior*.
