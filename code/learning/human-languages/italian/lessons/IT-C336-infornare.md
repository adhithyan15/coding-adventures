## You'll want to know: infornare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C336-FORNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**infornare** — "to put in the oven".

> Inforna la torta per quaranta minuti.
>
> *Bake the cake for forty minutes.*

Built on *forno*, ‘oven’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*infornare* is built on *forno* "oven", inherited from Latin *furnus* (Lewis & Short).
