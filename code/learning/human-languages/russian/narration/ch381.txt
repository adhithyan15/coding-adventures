Russian, chapter 381: Exam time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сессия (séssiya) — an exam period (at university); a session.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for permanent, then the Russian for temporary.

You'll want to know: сессия.
сессия (séssiya) — "an exam period (at university); a session".
В январе у студентов сессия. V yanvaré u studéntov séssiya. In January the students have their exams.
Feminine. At a Russian university, the weeks of exams at the end of each term. Сдавать сессию — to sit one’s exams.

The word, taken apart.
сессия (séssiya) is usually traced, through western languages, to Latin sessiō "a sitting" (Lewis & Short), built on sedēre "to sit": a time when a body sits to do its business.

Lesson 2 of 5.
зачёт (zachyót) — a pass-or-fail test, a credit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for temporary, then the Russian for an exam period.

You'll want to know: зачёт.
зачёт (zachyót) — "a pass-or-fail test, a credit".
Завтра у меня зачёт по истории. Závtra u menyá zachyót po istórii. Tomorrow I have a pass-or-fail test in history.
Masculine. A test graded pass or fail, without a mark. Получить зачёт (zachyót) — to pass it.

The word, taken apart.
зачёт (zachyót) is built on зачесть "to count in, to credit", on the old root of считать and число "number": a test that is counted in or not.

семинар (seminár) — "a seminar, a discussion class".
На семинаре мы обсуждали статью. Na semináre my obsuzhdáli statyú. At the seminar we discussed an article.
Masculine. A class where students talk and answer, as against a лекция.

семинар (seminár) is usually traced, through German Seminar, to Latin sēminārium "a seed plot, a nursery" (Lewis & Short), built on sēmen "seed": a place where young minds are grown.

Я потерял конспект лекций. Ya poteryál konspékt léktsiy. I have lost my lecture notes.
Masculine. Конспект лекций — notes from lectures.

The word, taken apart.
конспект (konspékt) is usually traced to Latin cōnspectus "a view, a survey" (Lewis & Short), from cōnspicere "to catch sight of", built on specere "to see": a summary that lets you take in a subject at a glance.

Lesson 5 of 5.
балл (ball) — a point, a mark (in a test).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a seminar, then the Russian for lecture notes.

You'll want to know: балл.
балл (ball) — "a point, a mark (in a test)".
Он получил пять баллов. On poluchíl pyat bállov. He got five points.
Masculine. Средний балл (ball) — a grade average. A dance is бал, with one л.

The word, taken apart.
балл was borrowed from French balle "a ball, a bundle" (EtymDB); votes and marks were once counted with small balls.
