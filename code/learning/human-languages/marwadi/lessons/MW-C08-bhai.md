# भाई — brother from familiar units

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-BHAI-01, MW-SCRIPT-BHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-LEX-MAA-01, MW-LEX-AAP-01, MW-LEX-KAISO-01, MW-QUESTION-WELLBEING-HEARD-01] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Write **भ**, **ा**, and independent **ई**. Say mother, then ask the
known polite wellbeing question with *āp* and *kaiso*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-BHAI-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-BHAI-01, MW-SCRIPT-BHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-INDEPENDENT-01] -->

> **भ** + **ा** + **ई** → **भाई** — *bhāī* — brother

The written pieces are old; only their family meaning is new.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-BHAI-01, MW-SCRIPT-BHAI-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **भाई**, cover it, wait five seconds, and write it once. Read the word
without romanization and give its meaning.
