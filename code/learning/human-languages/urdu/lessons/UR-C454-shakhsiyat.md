## You'll want to know: شخصیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C454-CHARACTER454-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شخصیت** — *shakhsiyat* — "a personality".

> ہمارے استاد کی شخصیت بڑی دلچسپ ہے۔
>
> *hamāre ustād kī shakhsiyat baṛī dilchasp hai.*
>
> Our teacher has a very interesting personality.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شخصیت** (*shakhsiyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shakhṣiyya*, "personality", built on *shakhṣ*, "a person".

## What you've built
