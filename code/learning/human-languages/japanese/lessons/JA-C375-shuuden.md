## You'll want to know: しゅうでん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C375-SHUUDEN375-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうでん** — *shūden* — "the last train of the day".

> **しゅうでんに まにあいました。** — *shūden ni maniaimashita* — "I made it in time for the last train."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうでん** (*shūden*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shū*, "end" (Middle Chinese *tsyuwng*) and *den*, "electricity" (*denH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
