## You'll want to know: der Klimawandel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C363-UMWELT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Klimawandel** — "climate change".

> Der Klimawandel macht die Sommer immer heißer.
>
> *Climate change is making the summers hotter and hotter.*

No plural. *Das Klima* — the climate — and *der Wandel* — change.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Klimawandel* joins *Klima*, from Latin *clima* and Greek *klíma* "slope, zone of latitude", from *klínein* "to slope" (EtymDB), to *Wandel* "change", inherited from Old High German *wandil* (EtymDB).
