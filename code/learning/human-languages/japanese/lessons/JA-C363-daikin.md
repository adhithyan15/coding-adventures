## You'll want to know: だいきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C363-KAIKEI363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**だいきん** — *daikin* — "the payment; the price of goods".

> **しなものが とどいたら、だいきんを はらいます。** — *shinamono ga todoitara, daikin o haraimasu* — "When the goods arrive, I will pay for them."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だいきん** (*daikin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dai*, "fee" (Middle Chinese *dojH*) and *kin*, "gold" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
