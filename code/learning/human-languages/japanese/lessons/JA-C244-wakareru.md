## You'll want to know: わかれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C244-MEET244-03]; assesses=[] -->

**わかれる** — *wakareru* — "to part; to break up".

> **あねは かれと わかれません。** — *ane wa kare to wakaremasen* — "My elder sister is not breaking up with him."

Polite **わかれます**, *wakaremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わかれる** (*wakareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
