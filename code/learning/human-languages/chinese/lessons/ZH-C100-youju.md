## You'll want to know: yóujú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C100-TOWN100-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yóujú* — "post office".

> *yóujú zài yínháng pángbiān* — The post office is next to the bank.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yóujú* is built from yóu 'post' and jú 'office', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuw* and *gjowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a lodge on the border and the second as a government office.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
