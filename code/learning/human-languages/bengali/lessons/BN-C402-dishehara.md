## You'll want to know: দিশেহারা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C402-MAJHKHAN402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**দিশেহারা** — *dishehārā* — "at a loss; not knowing which way to go".

> নতুন শহরে এসে আমি দিশেহারা হয়ে গেলাম।
>
> *nôtun shôhôre ese āmi dishehārā hôye gelām.*
>
> When I came to the new city I was at a loss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দিশেহারা** (*dishehārā*) joins দিশে, a spoken form of দিশা, "direction", Sanskrit *diśā*, "a direction, a quarter" (Monier-Williams), to হারা, "having lost", the stem of হারানো: one who has lost the direction.
