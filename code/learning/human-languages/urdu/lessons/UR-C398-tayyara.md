## You'll want to know: طیارہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C398-PARVAZ398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**طیارہ** — *tayyārā* — "an aircraft".

> طیارہ بادلوں کے اوپر اڑ رہا تھا۔
>
> *tayyārā bādaloṅ ke ūpar uṛ rahā thā.*
>
> The aircraft was flying above the clouds.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طیارہ** (*tayyārā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṭayyāra*, "an aeroplane", from a word for flying; the news prefers it to **ہوائی جہاز**.
