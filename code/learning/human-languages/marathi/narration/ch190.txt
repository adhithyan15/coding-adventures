Marathi, chapter 190: Neighbourhood, Traffic, Passenger, Queue, Crowd.

Lesson 1 of 5.
शेजार (śejār) — the neighbourhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a benefit, then the Marathi for a loss.

You'll want to know: शेजार.
शेजार — śejār — "the neighbourhood".

The word, taken apart.
शेजार (śejār) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sagṛhya, "neighbouring", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13077). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "neighbourhood, neighbours".

Lesson 2 of 5.
वाहतूक (vāhtūk) — traffic, transport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a loss, then the Marathi for the neighbourhood.

You'll want to know: वाहतूक.
वाहतूक — vāhtūk — "traffic, transport".

The word, taken apart.
वाहतूक (vāhtūk) is built on the stem vāh-, "carry", of Marathi vāhṇẽ, "to carry", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit vāhayati, "drives" (CDIAL 11612).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the neighbourhood, then the Marathi for traffic.

You'll want to know: प्रवासी.
प्रवासी — pravāsī — "a passenger, a traveller".

The word, taken apart.
प्रवासी (pravāsī) is Sanskrit pravāsin, "one away from home, a traveller", a tatsama built on pravāsa, "dwelling abroad" (CDIAL 8802).
