## You'll want to know: પ્લગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C517-VIJLI517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્લગ** — *plag* — "a plug".

> ફોનનો પ્લગ ક્યાં છે?
>
> *phonno plag kyā̃ che?*
>
> Where is the phone's plug?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્લગ** (*plag*) is borrowed from English *plug*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
