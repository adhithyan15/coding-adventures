## You'll want to know: સ્મિત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C448-HASYA448-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્મિત** — *smit* — "a smile".

> દાદીના મોઢા પર મીઠું સ્મિત હતું.
>
> *dādīnā moḍhā par mīṭhũ smit hatũ.*
>
> There was a sweet smile on my grandmother's face.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્મિત** (*smit*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *smita*, "smiling", as Monier-Williams records it; Gujarati uses it as the noun "a smile".
