## You'll want to know: विभाग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C355-VIBHAG355-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विभाग** — *vibhāg* — "a department".

> मेरा भाई बिजली विभाग में काम करता है।
>
> *merā bhāī bijlī vibhāg meṁ kām kartā hai.*
>
> My brother works in the electricity department.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विभाग** (*vibhāg*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vibhāga*, "a division, a share" (Monier-Williams).
