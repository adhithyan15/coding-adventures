Sanskrit, chapter 305: The Rescue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उद्धरति (uddharati) — rescues; pulls clear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for laments, then the Sanskrit for brings out.

You'll want to know: उद्धरति.
उद्धरति — uddharati — "rescues; pulls clear".
सैनिकाः जलात् जनान् उद्धरन्ति। sainikāḥ jalāt janān uddharanti. Soldiers rescue people from the water.
Past (he …): सः बालं जलात् उद्धृतवान्, saḥ bālaṃ jalāt uddhṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
उद्धरति (uddharati) is built from ud, "up, out", and the root √hṛ, "to take"; MW gives ud-dhṛ as to take out, pull out, extricate (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for brings out, then the Sanskrit for rescues.

You'll want to know: प्राणाः.
प्राणाः — prāṇāḥ — "life; the breath of life".
सैनिकः बालस्य प्राणान् रक्षति। sainikaḥ bālasya prāṇān rakṣati. The soldier saves the child's life.
A masculine noun, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
प्राणाः (prāṇāḥ) is built from pra and the root √an, "to breathe"; MW gives prāṇa as the breath of life, breath, vitality, and the plural stands for life itself (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अवतारयति (avatārayati) — brings down; lowers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for life, then the Sanskrit for daring.

You'll want to know: अवतारयति.
अवतारयति — avatārayati — "brings down; lowers".
सैनिकाः आहतं पर्वतात् अवतारयन्ति। sainikāḥ āhataṃ parvatāt avatārayanti. Soldiers bring the injured man down from the hill.
Past (he …): सः बालं वृक्षात् अवतारितवान्, saḥ bālaṃ vṛkṣāt avatāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
अवतारयति (avatārayati) is built from ava, "down", and the causative of the root √tṝ, "to cross"; MW gives ava-tārita as caused to descend, fetched down (MW).
