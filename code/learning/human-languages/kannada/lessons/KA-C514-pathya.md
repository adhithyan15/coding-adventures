## You'll want to know: ಪಥ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C514-PATHYA514-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಥ್ಯ** — *pathya* — "a diet; a special diet".

> ಮಧುಮೇಹ ಇರುವುದರಿಂದ ಅಪ್ಪ ಪಥ್ಯ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *madhumēha iruvudarinda appa pathya māḍuttāre.*
>
> Because he has diabetes, father keeps to a diet.

ಪಥ್ಯ ಮಾಡು, to keep to a diet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಥ್ಯ** (*pathya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pathya*, "suitable" (CDIAL 7746): food that suits the patient.
