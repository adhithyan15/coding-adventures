German, chapter 341: Obwohl es regnet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
obwohl — although.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to criticize, then the German for the criticism.

You'll want to know: obwohl.
obwohl — "although".
Wir gehen spazieren, obwohl es regnet. We are going for a walk although it is raining.
Obwohl sends the verb to the end, like weil: …, obwohl es regnet. Trotzdem says the same thing from the other side: Es regnet. Trotzdem gehen wir spazieren.

The word, taken apart.
obwohl joins ob "if, whether" and wohl "well", inherited from Old High German wola and Proto-Germanic wela (EtymDB): "even if it may well be so".

Lesson 2 of 5.
zwar — admittedly (zwar … aber); to be precise (und zwar).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the criticism, then the German for although.

You'll want to know: zwar.
zwar — "admittedly (zwar … aber); to be precise (und zwar)".
Das Hotel ist zwar teuer, aber es liegt direkt am Strand. The hotel is expensive, admittedly, but it is right on the beach.
Zwar … aber grants one point and then turns; the verb stays second: das Hotel ist zwar teuer. Und zwar adds detail: heute, und zwar um acht.

The word, taken apart.
zwar is inherited from Middle High German ze wāre "in truth, indeed" (EtymDB), from ze "to, in" and wār "true": "true, but …".

Lesson 3 of 5.
allerdings — however; certainly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for although, then the German for admittedly.

You'll want to know: allerdings.
allerdings — "however; certainly".
Die Wohnung ist schön. Allerdings ist die Küche sehr klein. The flat is nice. However, the kitchen is very small.
When it opens the sentence, the verb comes right after it: Allerdings ist …. As an answer it means certainly: Allerdings!

The word, taken apart.
allerdings grew out of the genitive aller Dinge "of all things", built on Ding "thing", from Old High German ding "assembly" and Proto-Germanic þingą (EtymDB), with the adverb ending -s.

Lesson 4 of 5.
dennoch — nevertheless, still.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for admittedly, then the German for however.

You'll want to know: dennoch.
dennoch — "nevertheless, still".
Es war kalt und windig. Dennoch sind wir an den See gefahren. It was cold and windy. Nevertheless, we drove to the lake.
A more formal trotzdem: the verb comes right after it, dennoch sind wir ….

The word, taken apart.
dennoch joins denn "then", from the family of dann (EtymDB), and noch "still", from Middle High German noch (EtymDB): "even then, still".

Lesson 5 of 5.
tatsächlich — in fact, actually; real.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for however, then the German for nevertheless.

You'll want to know: tatsächlich.
tatsächlich — "in fact, actually; real".
Ich dachte, der Laden ist zu. Er war aber tatsächlich bis acht Uhr offen. I thought the shop was closed. In fact, it was open until eight o'clock.
As an answer: Tatsächlich? — Really? As an adjective: der tatsächliche Preis — the real price.

The word, taken apart.
tatsächlich is built on Tatsache "fact" (EtymDB), from Tat "deed", Old High German tāt, and Sache "thing, matter", Old High German sahha. Tatsache is usually said to have been coined in the 18th century as a rendering of English matter of fact.
