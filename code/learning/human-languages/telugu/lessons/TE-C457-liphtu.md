## You'll want to know: లిఫ్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C457-VASATI457-04]; assesses=[] -->

**లిఫ్టు** — *liphṭu* — "an elevator; a lift".

> లిఫ్టు పనిచేయలేదు, అందుకే మెట్లు ఎక్కాం.
>
> *liphṭu panicēyalēdu, andukē meṭlu ekkāṁ.*
>
> The lift was not working, so we climbed the stairs.

లిఫ్టు ఎక్కు, to take the lift. In Telugu it also means a free ride: లిఫ్టు ఇచ్చాడు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లిఫ్టు** (*liphṭu*) is a loan from English *lift*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
