## You'll want to know: zhíbō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C292-RADIO292-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíbō* — "to broadcast live; a live show".

> *diànshì zhèngzài zhíbō zúqiú bǐsài* — The football match is on live on TV right now.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíbō* is built from *zhí* 'straight' and *bō* 'to scatter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drik* and *paH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'upright' and 'straight'. The level tone of bō is irregular for *paH*.
