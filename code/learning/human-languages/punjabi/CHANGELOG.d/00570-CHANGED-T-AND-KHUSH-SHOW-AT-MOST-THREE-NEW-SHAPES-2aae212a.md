## Changed — tū̃ and khushī show at most three new shapes

The script-ramp report (`ramp.script`, policy `maxNewGlyphsPerLesson: 3`)
counts every target-script shape the first time a lesson body shows it.
It listed PA-C02-tu-tusi (5) and PA-C02-khushi (4); it now lists no Punjabi
lesson.

- **PA-C02-tu-tusi** (5 → 3) reads **ਤੁਸੀਂ** — the respectful form, the one a
  first meeting needs. *Tū̃* stays spoken; its ੂ and ੰ first appear later (ੂ in
  PA-S09-dulainkar-oora). This also retires the old line that called ਤੂੰ's
  nasal mark a *bindi* and printed ਂ for it.
- **PA-C02-khushi** (4 → 2) reads **ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ੀ**; *hoī* stays spoken. The old claim
  that the reader "saw" ਸ਼ in *shukrīā* is corrected: that lesson is spoken.
- **PA-C03-koi-gall-nahin** keeps *koī* in romanization so it stays at 3; ਈ
  now first appears in PA-S12-eeri, its own letter lesson.
- Headings of the three lessons are romanized. Regenerated book chapters 2 and
  3, narration, modality and hashes.
