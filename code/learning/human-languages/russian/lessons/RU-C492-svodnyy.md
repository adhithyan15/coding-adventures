## You'll want to know: сводный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C492-STEPFAMILY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**сводный** (*svódnyy*) — "step- (as in stepbrother)".

> У меня есть сводный брат.
>
> *U menyá yest svódnyy brat.*
>
> I have a stepbrother.

*Сводный брат / сводная сестра* — a stepbrother, a stepsister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сводный* is built on *сводить* 'to bring together', from *водить* 'to lead'; EtymDB has no entry for *сводный*.
