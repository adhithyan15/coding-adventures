5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ದಂಗೆ (daṅge) — a revolt; an uprising.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to engulf, then the Kannada for to be washed away.

You'll want to know: ದಂಗೆ.
ದಂಗೆ — daṅge — "a revolt; an uprising".
ಜನರು ಆ ರಾಜನ ವಿರುದ್ಧ ದಂಗೆ ಎದ್ದರು. janaru ā rājana viruddha daṅge eddaru. The people rose in revolt against that king.
One "rises" in revolt: ದಂಗೆ (daṅge) ಏಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ದಂಗೆ (daṅge) is usually traced to Hindustani daṅgā, "a riot, a disturbance"; its earlier history is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕ್ರಾಂತಿ (krānti) — a revolution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to be washed away, then the Kannada for a revolt.

You'll want to know: ಕ್ರಾಂತಿ.
ಕ್ರಾಂತಿ — krānti — "a revolution".
ಆ ಕ್ರಾಂತಿ ದೇಶದ ಇತಿಹಾಸವನ್ನೇ ಬದಲಿಸಿತು. ā krānti dēśada itihāsavannē badalisitu. That revolution changed the country's history.
Plural: ಕ್ರಾಂತಿಗಳು, krāntigaḷu.
Also a great change: ಹಸಿರು ಕ್ರಾಂತಿ (krānti), the green revolution in farming.

The word, taken apart.
ಕ್ರಾಂತಿ (krānti) is borrowed from Sanskrit krānti, "going, proceeding, a step", and "overcoming" (Monier-Williams); "a revolution" is a modern sense.

ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆ ಗುಲಾಮ ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಸ್ವತಂತ್ರನಾದನು. katheyalli ā gulāma konege svatantranādanu. In the story the slave became free at last.

ಗುಲಾಮ (gulāma) is a loan from Arabic ġulām, "a boy, a servant, a slave", which came through Persian and Hindustani.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಮಾಡು (biḍugaḍe māḍu) — to set free; to release.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a revolution, then the Kannada for a slave.

You'll want to know: ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಮಾಡು.
ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಮಾಡು — biḍugaḍe māḍu — "to set free; to release".
ಹೊಸ ರಾಜ ಎಲ್ಲ ಗುಲಾಮರನ್ನು ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಮಾಡಿದನು. hosa rāja ella gulāmarannu biḍugaḍe māḍidanu. The new king set all the slaves free.
Past (I …): ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, biḍugaḍe māḍide.
Also used of a film or a book coming out: ಸಿನಿಮಾ ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಆಯಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಮಾಡು (biḍugaḍe māḍu) is native Dravidian: ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ, "release", is built on biḍu, "to let loose, to let go" (DEDR 5393), with māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ (vaśapaḍisiko) — to capture; to take control of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a slave, then the Kannada for to set free.

You'll want to know: ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ.
ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ — vaśapaḍisiko — "to capture; to take control of".
ದಂಗೆಕೋರರು ಕೋಟೆಯನ್ನು ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊಂಡರು. daṅgekōraru kōṭeyannu vaśapaḍisikoṇḍaru. The rebels captured the fort.
Past (I …): ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊಂಡೆ, vaśapaḍisikoṇḍe.
ವಶ, control, with -ಪಡಿಸು, to bring, and -ಕೊ, for oneself. The police ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ (vaśapaḍisiko) stolen goods too.

The word, taken apart.
ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ (vaśapaḍisiko) is built from Sanskrit vaśa, "will, authority, power" (CDIAL 11430), with the native verb-makers -paḍisu (DEDR 3853) and -ko, from koḷ, "to take" (DEDR 2151).
