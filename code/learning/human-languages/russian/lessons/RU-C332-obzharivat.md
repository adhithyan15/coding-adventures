## You'll want to know: обжаривать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C332-ZAPEKAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**обжаривать** (*obzhárivat*) — "to brown, to fry lightly".

> Он обжарил лук на масле.
>
> *On obzháril luk na másle.*
>
> He browned the onion in oil.

Imperfective; perfective *обжарить* (*obzhárit*). *Жарить* — to fry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обжаривать* is built on *об-* "all round" and *жарить* "to fry", from *жар* "heat", the same Slavic root as *гореть* "to burn".
