## You'll want to know: गोंजारणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C501-MITHI501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गोंजारणे** — *goñjārṇe* — "to stroke; to pet".

> मुलीने मांजराला गोंजारलं.
>
> *mulīne māñjrālā goñjārlaṁ.*
>
> The girl stroked the cat.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मांजराला गोंजारलं, *tyāne māñjrālā goñjārlaṁ*.

Soft strokes for a pet or a child; the one stroked takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोंजारणे** (*goñjārṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
