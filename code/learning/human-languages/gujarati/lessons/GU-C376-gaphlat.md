## You'll want to know: ગફલત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C376-BHUL376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ગફલત** — *gaphlat* — "negligence".

> જો ડ્રાઈવર ગફલત કરશે તો ગાડી ખાડામાં પડશે.
>
> *jo ḍrāīvar gaphlat karśe to gāḍī khāḍāmā̃ paḍśe.*
>
> If the driver is careless, the car will end up in a ditch.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગફલત** (*gaphlat*) is usually traced to Arabic *ghaflat*, "heedlessness", through Persian.

## What you've built
