## You'll want to know: mentir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C636-VERDAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**mentir** — "to lie, to tell lies".

> No me mientas.
>
> *Do not lie to me.*

Like *sentir*: *miento, mintió*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mentir* is inherited from Latin *mentiri* 'to lie, to invent', usually linked with *mens* 'mind' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
