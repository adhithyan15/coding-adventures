Punjabi, chapter 224: A Message About Tomorrow.
5 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
Return to a message and a time — retrieve a message and a clock time by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: sunehā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਸੁਨੇਹਾ, "a message". Then at nine: ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ.

Grammar Lens.
A message from a friend often says three things: what happened, what did not, and what will happen when. You can say all three now. Listen for ਕੱਲ੍ਹ: the verb tells you whether it is yesterday or tomorrow.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: a message]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਸੁਨੇਹਾ.
[your turn — hear: at eight]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ.
[your turn — hear: I went to the market yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: we will go tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ with ਗਿਆ? (Yesterday.) With ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ? (Tomorrow.)


Lesson 2 of 5.
A message about tomorrow — a short message from a friend, read for what happened and what will happen when.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, to read the printed text yourself. The questions are about what you read, so save the whole lesson for when you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say a message. Then Aman.
[pause 1 second]
Aman sends you a message. Read it for one thing first: what time you have to be at the station.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Reading.
ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ ਜੀ! ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਹਾਂ। ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਗਿਆ। ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ, ਪਰ ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਤੀ। ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਰੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ। ਰੇਲ ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ। ਮੈਂ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ। ਟਿਕਟ ਨਾ ਭੁੱਲੋ! ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਾ ਆਵੇਗਾ। ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ।.
[pause 3 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — read: the message once, straight through, without translating]
[pause 3 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — read: it a second time, and find every line with a clock time]

You'll want to know.
[pause 2 seconds]
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ comes twice: with ਗਿਆ it is yesterday, with ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ tomorrow. ਟਿਕਟ ਨਾ ਭੁੱਲੋ! is a request built like ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ, on ਭੁੱਲਣਾ, "to forget". The last line but one is the only one that is not sure.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — answer: what did Aman buy yesterday? (A book: ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ।)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: what did he not do? (He didn't drink tea.)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: when will the train come? (At nine: ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ.)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: who will perhaps come? (His brother.)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
When will Aman be at the station? (At eight.) What must you not forget? (The ticket.)


Lesson 3 of 5.
The message, by ear — answer questions about the message by ear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Hold Aman's message in mind: yesterday, and tomorrow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — hear: Aman kall kitthe giā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Answer: ਉਹ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਗਿਆ।.
[your turn — hear: kī Aman ne chāh pītī?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Answer: ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਉਸ ਨੇ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਤੀ।.
[your turn — hear: rel kadoṁ āvegī?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Answer: ਰੇਲ ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ।.
[your turn — hear: Aman kinne vaje saṭeshan jāvegā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Answer: ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ।.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Tell the message back in three lines: what Aman did, what he didn't, and what will happen tomorrow.


Lesson 4 of 5.
Looking back: saying no and asking — the first pass of the core review: saying no, asking, and the present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I don't drink tea. Then ask do you drink tea?

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — hear: I go to school every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: we eat roti every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।.
[your turn — hear: the garden is not open]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।.
[your turn — hear: please sit here; don't get up yet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਇੱਥੇ ਬੈਠੋ। ਅਜੇ ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ।.
[your turn — hear: are you a traveller? No.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ? ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹਾਂ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕੀ ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ?]
Give its meaning.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
ਨਹੀਂ tells, ਨਾ asks someone not to, ਕੀ at the front asks yes or no. Say one sentence for each.


Lesson 5 of 5.
Looking back: yesterday and tomorrow — the second pass of the core review: the past, the future and the message.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say Aman went home yesterday. Then we will go tomorrow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — hear: a woman came yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਔਰਤ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ।.
[your turn — hear: I got up early and drank tea]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: Aman bought a book and she gave money]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ ਅਤੇ ਉਸ ਨੇ ਪੈਸਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ।.
[your turn — hear: I took out the book]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਕੱਢੀ।.
[your turn — hear: what will you do tomorrow?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੀ ਕਰੋਗੇ?
[your turn — hear: perhaps we will go next week]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਰੇਲ ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ।]
Give its meaning, then say when Aman will be at the station.
[once you have stopped driving — write: cover the models and write ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ। or its woman's form]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Tell your yesterday in two lines and your tomorrow in two lines.
