## You'll want to know: percontor, percontārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C451-PERCONTOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**percontor, percontārī** — "to inquire, to ask questions".

> Viātor ā pastōre percontātur ubi oppidum sit.
>
> *The traveller asks a shepherd where the town is.*

Deponent: *percontor, percontārī, percontātus sum*, with *ā* or *ex* and the person asked. An indirect question follows in the subjunctive: *ubi sit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *percontor* is not settled. Lewis & Short build it on *per* and a root they connect with hesitating; the older spelling is *percunctor*. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
