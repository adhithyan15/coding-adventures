Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 31: One Exchange, Three Counters.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
The shopping map, inside the exchange — the whole shopping map run through the exchange this slice built.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall all three shopping payoffs in the order they were earned: three words, five words, seven words.

Guided Practice.
Take the seven shopping words one at a time. For each one, say its meaning, then put it into the request from Chapter 26 and the price question from Chapter 27. Two of the seven do not fit a request slot at all: name them and say why.
[once you have stopped driving — write: all seven from dictation, then the two lines you built with the word that fits them best]

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Write clothes, goods, and expensive.


Lesson 2 of 6.
The transport map, inside the exchange — the whole transport map run through the same exchange.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall both transport payoffs and say lentils.
[once you have stopped driving — write: the total question]

Guided Practice.
Take the five transport words one at a time. Say each meaning, then price it with the question from Chapter 27 and answer with the counter-offer from Chapter 28. A fare is bargained exactly like a price, which is why no new line is needed here.
[once you have stopped driving — write: all five from dictation, then the price question with the word you would actually use standing at a roadside]

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10; Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Write rickshaw, horse, and cart.


Lesson 3 of 6.
The food map, inside the ordering line — the whole food map run through the ordering line.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall all three food payoffs in the order they were earned.
[once you have stopped driving — write: ट and य]

Guided Practice.
Take the seven food words one at a time. Say each meaning, then order it with the line from Chapter 30 and price it with the question from Chapter 27. Only the first word of the order changes; the asking word never does.
[once you have stopped driving — write: all seven from dictation, then three complete orders — the one you would ask for first, the one you would ask for last, and the drink]

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Write lentils, clarified butter, and the sweet crumbled wheat dessert.


Lesson 4 of 6.
Five signs, on their own — the five least-drilled signs in the track, retrieved on their own.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the six-word weather payoff.
[once you have stopped driving — write: the words for weather, heat, and cold]

Guided Practice.
Some signs in this track have been read a hundred times inside words and asked for on their own only once. This lesson asks for them alone, which is the only way to know whether they were learned.
[once you have stopped driving — write: each from a spoken cue, with nothing else on the line — ख (kha), ट, श, ड़, ौ, ग, ड, ु, ई]
[once you have stopped driving — write: one word that carries each — आखरी, रोटी, रिक्शा, थोड़ु, मौसम, गर्मी, ठंडी, दुकान, घी; repair only the missed sign and rewrite its word once]

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Unicode Devanagari chart.
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Write the sign inside rickshaw, then the mark inside weather, then the word for clarified butter.


Lesson 5 of 6.
Payoff — one exchange, three counters.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the five payoffs of this slice in order.
[once you have stopped driving — write: the word for clarified butter]

Guided Practice.
Five turns, and the only thing that changes between counters is the noun.
Hear the five turns shuffled and put them in order, twice: once with a shopping noun, once with a transport noun.
Produce the whole exchange from meaning cues at a food stall, using the ordering line in place of the first turn.
[once you have stopped driving — read: all five printed turns, then say who speaks each]
[once you have stopped driving — write: the whole exchange from dictation, without a model]
Pass each skill separately. This is one function taught once and reused over three noun sets, not three exchanges learned three times. It still stops where numbers begin: no amount a seller names can yet be understood, and refusing the final price and leaving is untaught.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9; Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6; Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 50 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write the first and last turns of the exchange.


Lesson 6 of 6.
Close the writing loop for ख (kha) — final closure for the slice's only new sign, against the words that now carry it.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the five-turn exchange payoff.
[once you have stopped driving — write: the words for vegetables and clarified butter]

Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: ख (kha) and क from breath cues alone, with nothing else on the line]
[once you have stopped driving — write: four words from dictation, marking which of the two each one uses — दिखावो, आखरी, करो, कितणो]
Two take the breath and two do not.
[once you have stopped driving — write: every word this slice added, from meaning cues rather than sounds — ये, लो, दो, लावो, घणो, थोड़ु, and the five turns of the exchange; repair only what was missed and rewrite that alone]

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9; Unicode Devanagari chart.
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Write the breathed k, then the plain one.
