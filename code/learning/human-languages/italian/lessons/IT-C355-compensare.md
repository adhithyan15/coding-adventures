## You'll want to know: compensare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C355-RIMEDIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**compensare** — "to make up for, to compensate".

> Il panorama compensa la fatica del viaggio.
>
> *The view makes up for the tiring journey.*

*Compensare la mancanza di qualcosa*. The noun *compenso* is a fee for work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*compensare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *compēnsāre* "to weigh one thing against another, to balance" (EtymDB), built on *pēnsāre* "to weigh", from *pendere* "to hang, to weigh".
