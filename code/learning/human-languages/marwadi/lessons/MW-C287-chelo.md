## You'll want to know: चेलो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C287-GURU287-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चेलो** — *chelo* — "a pupil".

> यो म्हारो चेलो है।
>
> *yo mhāro chelo hai.*
>
> This is my pupil.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चेलो** (*chelo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*cēlla*, "boy"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *celā*, "pupil, apprentice", Gujarati *celo*, "pupil", and Sindhi *celo*, "disciple" (CDIAL 4911).
