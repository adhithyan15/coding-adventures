# اعتمد (iʿtamada) — to depend on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C291-FA-RESULT-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IDHA-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, we'll watch a film". (*Idhā amṭarat,
fa-sa-nushāhidu fīlman*.)

[PAUSE 1s] Sometimes you cannot give the result yet, because it hangs on
something else. This is the verb for hanging on something.

## You'll want to know: اعتمد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C292-SHART292-01]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-C35-SADIQ-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PAST-PERSONS-01] -->

**اعتمد** — *iʿtamada* — "he depended, he relied", with **على**,
*ʿalā*, "on".

> **اعتمدت على صديقي.** — *iʿtamadtu ʿalā ṣadīqī* — I relied on my friend.

The present is *yaʿtamidu*, he depends; *aʿtamidu ʿalayka*, I'm counting on
you. It has the same shape as *iʿtaqada*, to believe, and its present
*aʿtaqidu*: one letter apart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C164-ACT164-04] -->

**اعتمد** (*iʿtamada*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-m-d*, "to prop
up, to support" (Lane). Lane gives *iʿtamadtu ʿalā ash-shayʾ* as leaning or
resting on a thing, and then relying on a person. To depend on something, in
Arabic, is to lean your weight on it.
