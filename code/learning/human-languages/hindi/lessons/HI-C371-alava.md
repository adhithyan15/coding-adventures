# अलावा (alāvā) — besides, apart from (के अलावा)

## You'll want to know: अलावा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C371-HALANKI371-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अलावा** — *alāvā* — "besides, apart from (के अलावा)".

> हिंदी के अलावा मैं अंग्रेज़ी भी बोलता हूँ।
>
> *hindī ke alāvā maiṁ angrezī bhī boltā hūṁ.*
>
> Besides Hindi, I also speak English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अलावा** (*alāvā*) is usually traced to Arabic *ʿalāwa*, "an addition", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
