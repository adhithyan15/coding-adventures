## You'll want to know: заявлять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C337-TOCHKAZRENIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**заявлять** (*zayavlyát*) — "to declare, to state publicly".

> Директор заявляет, что завод не закроют.
>
> *Diréktor zayavlyáyet, chto zavód ne zakróyut.*
>
> The director states that the factory will not be closed.

Imperfective; perfective *заявить* (*заявлю, заявишь*). Formal: officials and newspapers *заявляют*, while among friends people *говорят*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**заявлять** is built from *за-* and *являть* "to show, to reveal", the verb behind *явный* "obvious". *Явить* comes through Old East Slavic *javiti* from Proto-Slavic *\*aviti* "to make visible" (EtymDB): to declare is to make a thing plain.
