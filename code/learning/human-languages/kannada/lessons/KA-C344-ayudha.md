## You'll want to know: ಆಯುಧ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C344-AYUDHA344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಯುಧ** — *āyudha* — "a weapon".

> ಸೈನಿಕರು ತಮ್ಮ ಆಯುಧಗಳನ್ನು ಕೆಳಗೆ ಇಟ್ಟರು.
>
> *sainikaru tamma āyudhagaḷannu keḷage iṭṭaru.*
>
> The soldiers laid down their weapons.

Plural: ಆಯುಧಗಳು, *āyudhagaḷu*.

Also a tool for work: ರೈತನ ಆಯುಧ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಯುಧ** (*āyudha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āyudha*, "a weapon" (CDIAL 1291), from the root *yudh*, "to fight", as in ಯುದ್ಧ.
