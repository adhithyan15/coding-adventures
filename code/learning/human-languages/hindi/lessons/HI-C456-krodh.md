## You'll want to know: क्रोध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C456-KRODH456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रोध** — *krodh* — "wrath; rage".

> क्रोध में आदमी सही और ग़लत भूल जाता है।
>
> *krodh meṁ ādmī sahī aur ġalat bhūl jātā hai.*
>
> In a rage a person forgets right from wrong.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रोध** (*krodh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *krodha*, "anger, wrath, passion" (Monier-Williams). Everyday speech says *ġussā*; *krodh* is the word of books and sermons.
