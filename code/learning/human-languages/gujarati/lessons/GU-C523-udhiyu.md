## You'll want to know: ઊંધિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C523-FARSAN523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંધિયું** — *ū̃dhiyũ* — "undhiyu (a mixed winter vegetable dish)".

> શિયાળામાં અમારા ઘરે ઊંધિયું બને છે.
>
> *śiyāḷāmā̃ amārā ghare ū̃dhiyũ bane che.*
>
> In winter undhiyu is made in our house.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંધિયું** (*ū̃dhiyũ*) is built on *ū̃dhũ*, "upside down", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit *avamūrdha*, "head downwards" (CDIAL 804); the dish is said to take its name from the pot once cooked upside down in the ground.
