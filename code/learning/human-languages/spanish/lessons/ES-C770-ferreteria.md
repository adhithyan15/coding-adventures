## You'll want to know: la ferretería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C770-COMERCIOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la ferretería** — "the hardware shop".

> Voy a la ferretería porque necesito tornillos.
>
> *I'm going to the hardware shop because I need screws.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferretería* is built on *ferrete* 'a small piece of iron' with the ending *-ería* (EtymDB). *Ferrete* comes from Latin *ferrum* 'iron', the word that gave *hierro*.
