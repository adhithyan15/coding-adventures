## You'll want to know: bouder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C466-TIMIDITE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**bouder** — "to sulk".

> Le petit garçon boude parce qu'il ne peut pas sortir.
>
> *The little boy is sulking because he cannot go out.*

*Bouder quelqu'un* means to give someone the cold shoulder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *bouder* is not settled. It is usually traced to an expressive root *bod-* for something swollen, the picture being pushed-out lips.
