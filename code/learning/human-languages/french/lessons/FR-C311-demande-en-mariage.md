## You'll want to know: la demande en mariage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C311-OUI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la demande en mariage** — "the marriage proposal".

> Il a fait sa demande en mariage au restaurant.
>
> *He proposed at the restaurant.*

*Demander quelqu'un en mariage* — to propose to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mariage* is built on *marier*, inherited from Latin *marītāre* "to wed" (EtymDB); *demande* is built on *demander*, from Latin *dēmandāre* "to entrust" (Lewis & Short).
