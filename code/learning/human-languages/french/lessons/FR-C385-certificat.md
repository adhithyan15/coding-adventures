## You'll want to know: le certificat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C385-DEMARCHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le certificat** — "the certificate".

> Le médecin m'a donné un certificat pour l'école.
>
> *The doctor gave me a certificate for school.*

*Un certificat médical* — a doctor's note.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*certificat* comes from Medieval Latin *certificātum* (EtymDB), built on Latin *certus* "sure" and *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short): a paper that makes a fact sure.
