## You'll want to know: دستگیر کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C407-JORM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دستگیر کردن** (*dastgir kardan*) — "to arrest".

> پلیس دزد را در خیابان دستگیر کرد.
>
> *polis dozd râ dar khiyâbân dastgir kard.*
>
> The police arrested the thief in the street.

*Dast* "hand" + *gir* "taking" + *kardan*. *Dastgir shodan* — to be arrested.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دستگیر کردن** is native Persian, built from *dast* "hand" + *gir-*, the present stem of *gereftan* "to take", + *kardan* "to do". To arrest is to take someone by the hand.
