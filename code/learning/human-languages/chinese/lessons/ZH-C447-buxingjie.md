## You'll want to know: bùxíngjiē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C447-WALK447-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bùxíngjiē* — "pedestrian street; a street closed to cars".

> *qù bùxíngjiē zěnme zǒu* — How do I get to the pedestrian street?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bùxíngjiē* is built from *bùxíng* 'on foot' and *jiē* 'a street', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *buH*, *haeng* and *kea* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last as a road that runs four ways.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
