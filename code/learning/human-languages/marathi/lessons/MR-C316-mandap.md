## You'll want to know: मंडप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C316-LAGNA316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मंडप** — *maṇḍap* — "a marquee; a pavilion".

> अंगणात लग्नासाठी मोठा मंडप उभा केला.
>
> *aṅgṇāt lagnāsāṭhī moṭhā maṇḍap ubhā kelā.*
>
> A big marquee was put up in the yard for the wedding.

Masculine.

मंडप उभा करणे / घालणे, to put up a marquee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मंडप** (*maṇḍap*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *maṇḍapa*, 'an open hall, a temporary pavilion' (Monier-Williams).
