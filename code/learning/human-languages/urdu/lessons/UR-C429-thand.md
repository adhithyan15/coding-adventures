## You'll want to know: ٹھنڈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C429-THAND429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھنڈ** — *ṭhanḍ* — "a chill; the cold".

> صبح کی ٹھنڈ میں ہم نے چائے پی۔
>
> *subah kī ṭhanḍ meṅ ham ne chāe pī.*
>
> We drank tea in the morning chill.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھنڈ** (*ṭhanḍ*) is inherited: it belongs with Hindi *ṭhaṇḍā*, "cold", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *stabdha*, "stiff, numb" (CDIAL 13676).
