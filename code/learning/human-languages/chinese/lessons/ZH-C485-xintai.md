## You'll want to know: xīntài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C485-MIND485-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xīntài* — "mindset; attitude of mind".

> *kǎoshì de shíhou xīntài zuì zhòngyào* — In an exam, your mindset matters most.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīntài* is built from 心 'heart' and *tài* 'a manner', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sim* and *thojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'bearing and manner'.
