## You'll want to know: આરોગ્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C486-AAROGYA486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**આરોગ્ય** — *ārogya* — "good health".

> રોજ ચાલવું આરોગ્ય માટે સારું છે.
>
> *roj cālvũ ārogya māṭe sārũ che.*
>
> Walking every day is good for your health.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આરોગ્ય** (*ārogya*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ārogya*, "freedom from disease, health", as Monier-Williams records it; CDIAL lists an older spoken form, *ārog* (CDIAL 1330).
