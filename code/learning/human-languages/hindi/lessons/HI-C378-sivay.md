## You'll want to know: सिवाय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C378-ZARURAT378-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सिवाय** — *sivāy* — "except (के सिवाय)".

> अगर तुम्हारे सिवाय कोई नहीं आएगा तो हम क्या करेंगे?
>
> *agar tumhāre sivāy koī nahīṃ āegā to ham kyā kareṅge?*
>
> If nobody but you comes, what will we do?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिवाय** (*sivāy*) is usually traced to Arabic *siwā*, "other than", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
