# взламывать (vzlámyvat) — to break into, to hack

## You'll want to know: взламывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C400-BEZOPASNOST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**взламывать** (*vzlámyvat*) — "to break into, to hack".

> Кто-то взломал мою почту.
>
> *Kto-to vzlomál moyú póchtu.*
>
> Someone has hacked my email.

Imperfective; perfective *взломать*. Of doors and of accounts alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**взламывать** is built from *вз-* and *ломать* "to break", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*lomiti*; the *о* turns to *а* in the imperfective.
