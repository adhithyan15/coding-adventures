Japanese, chapter 163: A Clean, Quiet and Handy Home.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
きれい (kirei) — clean; pretty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for repair, then the Japanese for old.

You'll want to know: きれい.
きれい — kirei — "clean; pretty".
へやは いつも きれいです。 — heya wa itsumo kirei desu — "The room is always clean."

The word, taken apart.
きれい (kirei) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ki, "figured cloth, beautiful" (Middle Chinese khjeX) and rei, "lovely" (lejH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). It is usually written in kana (JMdict).

しずか — shizuka — "quiet; calm".
この まちは しずかです。 — kono machi wa shizuka desu — "This town is quiet."

The word, taken apart.
しずか (shizuka) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
