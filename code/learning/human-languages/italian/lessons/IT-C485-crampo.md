## You'll want to know: il crampo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C485-STORTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il crampo** — "cramp".

> Di notte mi viene un crampo al polpaccio.
>
> *At night I get a cramp in my calf.*

*Avere un crampo* — to have a cramp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crampo* is usually traced to a Germanic word, Proto-Germanic *krampō* "cramp, clasp" (EtymDB, under French *crampe*).
