Kannada, chapter 416: Promises and Rewards.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ (muccaḷike) — a written undertaking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to sanction, then the Kannada for an official order.

You'll want to know: ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ.
ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ — muccaḷike — "a written undertaking".
ಮತ್ತೆ ತಪ್ಪು ಮಾಡುವುದಿಲ್ಲ ಅಂತ ಅವನು ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ ಬರೆದುಕೊಟ್ಟನು. matte tappu māḍuvudilla anta avanu muccaḷike baredukoṭṭanu. He gave a written undertaking not to do wrong again.
ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ (muccaḷike) ಬರೆದುಕೊಡು, to sign an undertaking.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ (muccaḷike) is a Kannada word of the offices and courts whose origin is not settled: neither DEDR nor CDIAL lists it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕೋರು (kōru) — to ask formally.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a vow, then the Kannada for a reward.

You'll want to know: ಕೋರು.
ಕೋರು — kōru — "to ask formally".
ನಾನು ಕಚೇರಿಯಿಂದ ಒಂದು ವಾರದ ರಜೆ ಕೋರಿದೆ. nānu kacēriyinda ondu vārada raje kōride. I asked the office for a week of leave.
Past (I …): ಕೋರಿದೆ, kōride.
Formal. ಕೋರಿಕೆ, a request.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೋರು (kōru) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2232 lists Kannada kōṟu, kōru, "to purpose, to wish, to desire", beside Tamil kōru, "to request".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ (pramāṇavacana) — an oath of office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a reward, then the Kannada for to ask formally.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ.
ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ — pramāṇavacana — "an oath of office".
ಹೊಸ ಮಂತ್ರಿಗಳು ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡರು. hosa mantrigaḷu pramāṇavacana tegedukoṇḍaru. The new ministers took the oath of office.
ಪ್ರಮಾಣ (proof, oath) + ವಚನ (word).

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ (pramāṇavacana) is built from Sanskrit pramāṇa, "a standard, a proof" (CDIAL 8723), and vacana, "speech, a word" (CDIAL 11199).
