## You'll want to know: ऐलान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C302-AILAN302-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ऐलान** — *ailān* — "an announcement".

> गाँव के मंदिर से ऐलान हुआ कि नदी में पानी ऊपर आ रहा है।
>
> *gā̃v ke mandir se ailān huā ki nadī meṁ pānī ūpar ā rahā hai.*
>
> An announcement came from the village temple that the river was rising.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऐलान** (*ailān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iʿlān*, "a making known". It begins with the letter **ऐ**, the vowel of **ऐसा**. **ऐलान करना** is to announce.
