## You'll want to know: आराखडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C544-AARAKHDA544-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आराखडा** — *ārākhḍā* — "an outline; a layout".

> अभियंत्याने नवीन इमारतीचा आराखडा बनवला.
>
> *abhiyantyāne navīn imārtīcā ārākhḍā banavlā.*
>
> The engineer made the layout of the new building.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आराखडा** (*ārākhḍā*) is a Marathi word for an outline plan; its origin is not settled.
