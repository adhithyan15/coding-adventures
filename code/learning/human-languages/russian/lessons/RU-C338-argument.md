## You'll want to know: аргумент
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C338-DOVOD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**аргумент** (*argumént*) — "an argument (a reason)".

> Это сильный аргумент.
>
> *Éto sílnyy argumént.*
>
> That is a strong argument.

Masculine. A reason in a debate, not a quarrel: a quarrel is *спор* or *ссора*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**аргумент** is usually traced, through German and Polish, to Latin *argūmentum* "proof, evidence" (Lewis & Short), built on *arguere* "to make clear, to prove".
