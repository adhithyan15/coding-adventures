## You'll want to know: la couchette
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C414-COUCHETTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la couchette** — "the berth, the couchette".

> Dans le train de nuit, j'ai dormi sur la couchette du haut.
>
> *On the night train I slept on the top berth.*

A small *couche* 'bed'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*couchette* is a small *couche* 'bed', from *coucher* 'to lay down', which is inherited from Latin *collocāre* 'to put in place', built on *locus* 'place' (Lewis & Short).
