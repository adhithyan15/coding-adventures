# Review — what I think, and so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PO-MOEMU-01, RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-KAZHETSYA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "in my view, it's a good film". (*Po-móyemu, éto khoróshiy
film.*) Then softer: "it seems to me the film is boring". (*Mne
kázhetsya, shto film skúchnyy.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-POETOMU-RESULT-01, RU-LEX-TAK-CHTO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-DUMAYU-CHTO-01, RU-LEX-POTOMU-CHTO-01, RU-LEX-C126-QUAL126-04, RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-03, RU-LEX-C65-SIZE-03] -->

Two lessons, and an opinion now comes with its reason. Three frames:
**я думаю, что**, **по-моему**, **мне кажется, что**. And two
directions: **потому что** before the cause, **поэтому** or **так что**
before the result.

[PAUSE 2s] All of it in two sentences:

> **По-моему, музей интересный, потому что он старый.** — *po-móyemu,
> muzéy interésnyy, potomú shto on stáryy* — In my view the museum is
> interesting, because it's old.
> **Музей старый, поэтому, мне кажется, он интересный.** — the same
> reason, pointed forward, and said more softly.
