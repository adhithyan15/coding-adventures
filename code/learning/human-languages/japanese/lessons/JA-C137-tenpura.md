## You'll want to know: てんぷら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C137-TENPURA]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-TE-01, JA-SCRIPT-N-01, JA-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

**てんぷら** — *tenpura* — "tempura": fish, prawns or vegetables, dipped in a
light batter and fried. English borrowed the word from Japanese.

The new sign is spent at once. **て** and **ん**, then **ぷ** and **ら**: four
beats, *te–n–pu–ra*. After **ん** the *p* comes out clearly, as in
**しんぱい**. You write every sign in it now.
