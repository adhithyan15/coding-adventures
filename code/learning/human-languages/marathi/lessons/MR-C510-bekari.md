## You'll want to know: बेकारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C510-SODNE510-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बेकारी** — *bekārī* — "unemployment".

> गावात बेकारी वाढली, म्हणून तरुण शहरात गेले.
>
> *gāvāt bekārī vāḍhlī, mhaṇūn taruṇ śahrāt gele.*
>
> Unemployment grew in the village, so the young people went to the city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेकारी** (*bekārī*) is a loan from Persian *bēkārī*, "idleness", from *bē-*, "without", and *kār*, "work".
