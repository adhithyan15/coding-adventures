# ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು (kaṇmareyāgu) — to disappear; to vanish

## You'll want to know: ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C367-KHALA367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು** — *kaṇmareyāgu* — "to disappear; to vanish".

> ಮಾಂತ್ರಿಕ ಹೊಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾದನು.
>
> *māntrika hogeyalli kaṇmareyādanu.*
>
> The sorcerer vanished in the smoke.

Past (it …): ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಯಿತು, *kaṇmareyāyitu*.

ಕಣ್, eye, ಮರೆ, hidden, and ಆಗು: to become hidden from the eye.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಣ್ಮರೆಯಾಗು** (*kaṇmareyāgu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *kaṇ*, "eye" (DEDR 1159), *mare*, "hidden, screened" (DEDR 4760), and *āgu*, "to become" (DEDR 333).
