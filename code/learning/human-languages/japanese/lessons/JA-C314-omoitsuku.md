## You'll want to know: おもいつく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C314-SUKKARI314-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいつく** — *omoitsuku* — "to think of; to hit upon".

> **いい ほうほうを おもいつきました。** — *ii hōhō o omoitsukimashita* — "I have had a good idea how to do it."

Polite **おもいつきます**, *omoitsukimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが いい ほうほうを おもいつきました。**, *chichi ga ii hōhō o omoitsukimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいつく** (*omoitsuku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *omoi*, "thinking" (from *omou*), and *tsuku*, "to arrive at" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
