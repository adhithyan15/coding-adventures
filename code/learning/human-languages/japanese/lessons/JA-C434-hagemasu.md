## You'll want to know: はげます
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C434-THANK434-05]; assesses=[] -->

**はげます** — *hagemasu* — "to encourage; to cheer up".

> **ともだちが おちこんだので、はげましました。** — *tomodachi ga ochikonda node, hagemashimashita* — "My friend was feeling down, so I cheered him up."

Polite **はげまします**, *hagemashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが むすこを はげましました。**, *haha ga musuko o hagemashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はげます** (*hagemasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
