## You'll want to know: hòuhuǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C187-BADDAY187-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hòuhuǐ* — "to regret".

> *wǒ hěn hòuhuǐ zuótiān méi qù wǎnhuì* — I really regret not going to the party yesterday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hòuhuǐ* is built from 后 'after' and *huǐ* 'to repent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwX* and *xwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to repent and change'; today's third tone goes with its other reading, *xwojX*. To repent afterwards.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
