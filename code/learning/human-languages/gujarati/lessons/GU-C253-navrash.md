# નવરાશ (navrāś) — leisure, spare time

## You'll want to know: નવરાશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C253-HOBBY253-02]; assesses=[] -->

**નવરાશ** — *navrāś* — "leisure, spare time".

> **રવિવારે મને નવરાશ હતી.** — *ravivāre mane navrāś hatī* — "I had spare time on Sunday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **નવરાશ** (*navrāś*) is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it, and our other sources do not trace it.
