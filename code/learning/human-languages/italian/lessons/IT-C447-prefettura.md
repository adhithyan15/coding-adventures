## You'll want to know: la prefettura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C447-UFFICI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la prefettura** — "the prefecture (the local office of the state)".

> La prefettura è nel palazzo in piazza.
>
> *The prefecture is in the building on the square.*

The office of *il prefetto*, the state's representative in a province.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prefettura* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praefectūra* 'the office of a prefect', from *praefectus* 'one put in charge', built on *facere* 'to make' (Lewis & Short).
