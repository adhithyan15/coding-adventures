Punjabi, chapter 242: Frying and Roasting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਤਲਣਾ (talṇā) — to fry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a chilli, then the Punjabi for gram flour.

You'll want to know: ਤਲਣਾ.
ਤਲਣਾ — talṇā — "to fry".
ਮੈਂ ਪਕੌੜੇ ਤੇਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਤਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ pakauṛe tel vich taldī hāṁ. I fry the pakoras in oil.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਕੌੜੇ ਤਲੇ, maiṁ pakauṛe tale.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਲਣਾ (talṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed talati, "fries"; CDIAL lists Punjabi talṇā, "to fry, cook in ghee" (CDIAL 5736).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਭੁੰਨਣਾ (bhunnṇā) — to roast; to parch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for gram flour, then the Punjabi for to fry.

You'll want to know: ਭੁੰਨਣਾ.
ਭੁੰਨਣਾ — bhunnṇā — "to roast; to parch".
ਸਰਦੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਮੂੰਗਫਲੀ ਭੁੰਨਦੇ ਹਾਂ। sardī vich asīṁ mū̃gphalī bhunnde hāṁ. In winter we roast peanuts.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੱਕੀ ਭੁੰਨੀ, maiṁ makkī bhunnī.

ਭੁੰਨਣਾ (bhunnṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed bhṛgṇa, "fried, roasted"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bhunnaṇā, "to parch, fry" (CDIAL 9577).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਕੜਛੀ (kaṛchhī) — a ladle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to fry, then the Punjabi for to roast.

You'll want to know: ਕੜਛੀ.
ਕੜਛੀ — kaṛchhī — "a ladle".
ਕੜਛੀ ਪਤੀਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ। kaṛchhī patīle vich hai. The ladle is in the cooking pot.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੜਛੀ (kaṛchhī) is inherited from a reconstructed kaṭacchu, "ladle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kaṛchī, "iron ladle" (CDIAL 2633).

ਮੈਂ ਸਬਜ਼ੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਲੂਣ ਛਿੜਕਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ sabzī utte lūṇ chhiṛakdī hāṁ. I sprinkle salt on the vegetables.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਲੂਣ ਛਿੜਕਿਆ, maiṁ lūṇ chhiṛkiā.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਸੜਨਾ (saṛnā) — to get burnt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a ladle, then the Punjabi for to sprinkle.

You'll want to know: ਸੜਨਾ.
ਸੜਨਾ — saṛnā — "to get burnt".
ਜੇ ਤਵਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਗਰਮ ਹੈ ਤਾਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਸੜਦੀ ਹੈ। je tavā bahut garam hai tāṁ roṭī saṛdī hai. If the griddle is too hot, the roti burns.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਰੋਟੀ ਸੜੀ, oh roṭī saṛī.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੜਨਾ (saṛnā) is inherited from Sanskrit śaṭati, "disintegrates"; CDIAL lists Punjabi saṛnā, "to rot, be burnt" (CDIAL 12268).
