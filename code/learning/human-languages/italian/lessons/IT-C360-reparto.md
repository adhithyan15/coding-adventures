## You'll want to know: il reparto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C360-REPARTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il reparto** — "the department, the section".

> Lavoro nel reparto vendite.
>
> *I work in the sales department.*

In a hospital, *il reparto* is the ward; in a shop, the department.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reparto* is built on *ripartire* "to share out", from *ri-* and *partire* "to divide", inherited from Latin *partīrī* "to divide", from *pars, partis* "a part" (Lewis & Short).
