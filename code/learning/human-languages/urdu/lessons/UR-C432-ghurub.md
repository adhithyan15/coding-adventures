## You'll want to know: غروب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C432-TULU432-02]; assesses=[] -->

**غروب** — *ghurūb* — "the setting (of the sun)".

> سورج شام سات بجے غروب ہوا۔
>
> *sūraj shām sāt baje ghurūb huā.*
>
> The sun set at seven in the evening.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غروب** (*ghurūb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghurūb*, "setting"; it shares its letters with **مغرب**, "the west".
