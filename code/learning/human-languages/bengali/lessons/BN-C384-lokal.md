## You'll want to know: লোকাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C384-REL384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**লোকাল** — *lokāl* — "a local train; a stopping train".

> লোকাল ট্রেন সব স্টেশনে থামে।
>
> *lokāl ṭren sôb sṭeshône thāme.*
>
> The local train stops at every station.

On its own লোকাল is the train: লোকালে এলাম, I came on the local.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লোকাল** (*lokāl*) is borrowed from English *local*, short for *local train*, the slow suburban train that stops everywhere.
