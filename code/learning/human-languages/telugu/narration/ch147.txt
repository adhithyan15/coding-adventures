Telugu, chapter 147: To wipe, To tie, To sew, To win, To hide.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తుడుచు (tuḍucu) — to wipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to count, then the Telugu for to wash.

You'll want to know: తుడుచు.
తుడుచు — tuḍucu — "to wipe".

The word, taken apart.
తుడుచు (tuḍucu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil tuṭai and Kannada toḍe (DEDR 3301). DEDR lists Telugu tuḍucu, "to wipe, sweep".

Lesson 5 of 5.
దాచు (dācu) — to hide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to sew, then the Telugu for to win.

You'll want to know: దాచు.
దాచు — dācu — "to hide".

The word, taken apart.
దాచు (dācu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 63 lists ḍācu, "to hide, conceal", and a nasalised form of dācu, with Kannada aḍagu, "to hide", and Tamil aṭaṅku, "to submit". DEDR compares the northern verbs for "to cover" in CDIAL 5574 and 5579.
