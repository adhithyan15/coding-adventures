## You'll want to know: गर्व
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C463-GARVA463-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गर्व** — *garva* — "pride; conceit".

> बक्षीस मिळाल्याचा त्याला गर्व झाला.
>
> *bakṣīs miḷālyācā tyālā garva jhālā.*
>
> Winning the prize made him conceited.

Masculine.

Often a fault: गर्व होणे, to grow conceited; गर्विष्ठ, arrogant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गर्व** (*garva*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *garva*, "pride, arrogance" (CDIAL 4064).
