Kannada, chapter 474: Waste and Recycling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ (tyājya) — waste matter; refuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for soot, then the Kannada for carbon.

You'll want to know: ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ.
ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ — tyājya — "waste matter; refuse".
ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆಯ ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ ನದಿಗೆ ಹರಿಯುತ್ತಿತ್ತು. kārkhāneya tyājya nadige hariyuttittu. Waste from the factory was flowing into the river.
ಘನ ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ (tyājya), solid waste; ಕಸ is the everyday word.

The word, taken apart.
ತ್ಯಾಜ್ಯ (tyājya) is borrowed from Sanskrit tyājya, "to be left, to be abandoned" (Monier-Williams): what is thrown away.

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಮರುಬಳಕೆ.
ಮರುಬಳಕೆ — marubaḷake — "recycling; reuse".
ಪ್ಲಾಸ್ಟಿಕ್ ಬಾಟಲಿಗಳ ಮರುಬಳಕೆ ಸಾಧ್ಯ. plāsṭik bāṭaligaḷa marubaḷake sādhya. Plastic bottles can be recycled.
ಮರು-, again, plus ಬಳಕೆ, use, the noun of ಬಳಸು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮರುಬಳಕೆ (marubaḷake) joins the native prefix maṟu, "again" (DEDR 4766), and baḷake, "use", the noun of ಬಳಸು.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತ್ಯಜಿಸು (tyajisu) — to forsake; to give up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for recycling, then the Kannada for to sort.

You'll want to know: ತ್ಯಜಿಸು.
ತ್ಯಜಿಸು — tyajisu — "to forsake; to give up".
ಅವನು ಪ್ಲಾಸ್ಟಿಕ್ ಚೀಲಗಳನ್ನು ತ್ಯಜಿಸಿದನು. avanu plāsṭik cīlagaḷannu tyajisidanu. He gave up plastic bags.
Past (I …): ತ್ಯಜಿಸಿದೆ, tyajiside.
A formal word; what is given up takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ತ್ಯಜಿಸು (tyajisu) is built from the Sanskrit root tyaj, "to leave, to let go" (CDIAL 5984, tyajati), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿ (sauraśakti) — solar power.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to sort, then the Kannada for to forsake.

You'll want to know: ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿ.
ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿ — sauraśakti — "solar power".
ನಮ್ಮ ಶಾಲೆ ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿಯಿಂದ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತದೆ. namma śāle sauraśaktiyinda naḍeyuttade. Our school runs on solar power.
ಸೌರ, of the sun, plus ಶಕ್ತಿ, power.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿ (sauraśakti) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: saura, "relating to the sun", and śakti, "power, energy" (Monier-Williams).
