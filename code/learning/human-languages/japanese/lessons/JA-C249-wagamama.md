## You'll want to know: わがまま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C249-HEART249-05]; assesses=[] -->

**わがまま** — *wagamama* — "selfish; wilful".

> **あの こどもは わがままですか。** — *ano kodomo wa wagamama desu ka* — "Is that child selfish?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わがまま** (*wagamama*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *waga*, "one's own", and *mama*, "as it is", and it is usually written in kana (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
