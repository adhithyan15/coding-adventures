## You'll want to know: o obstáculo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C353-OBSTACULO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o obstáculo** — "the obstacle".

> O maior obstáculo é a língua.
>
> *The biggest obstacle is the language.*

*Superar um obstáculo* — to overcome an obstacle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obstáculo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *obstāculum* "a hindrance, an obstacle" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *obstāre* "to stand in the way", built on *stāre* "to stand".
