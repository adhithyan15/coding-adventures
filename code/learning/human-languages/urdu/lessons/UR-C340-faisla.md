## You'll want to know: فیصلہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C340-FAISLA340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فیصلہ** — *faislā* — "a verdict; a decision".

> جج نے آج فیصلہ دیا۔
>
> *jaj ne āj faislā diyā.*
>
> The judge gave the verdict today.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فیصلہ** (*faislā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *faiṣala*, "a decision".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
