## You'll want to know: अंतर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C407-VEG407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अंतर** — *antar* — "a distance; a gap".

> घर ते शाळा हे अंतर किती आहे?
>
> *ghar te śāḷā he antar kitī āhe?*
>
> What is the distance from home to school?

Neuter.

X ते Y, from X to Y: घर ते शाळा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंतर** (*antar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *antara*, "interior; interval, space between" (Monier-Williams).
