## You'll want to know: ですか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C139-DESUKA]; assesses=[] -->

**ですか** — *desu ka* — "is it ...?". **です**, *desu*, says politely
that something is so. Put **か**, *ka*, at the end, and the sentence
becomes a question that can be answered yes or no. The order of the words
does not change, and the voice rises a little on *ka*.

> **これは みずですか。** — *kore wa mizu desu ka* — "Is this water?"
> **はい、みずです。** — *hai, mizu desu* — "Yes, it is water."

A verb takes **か** the same way: **ます** becomes **ますか**.

> **あした はたらきますか。** — *ashita hatarakimasu ka* — "Are you working
> tomorrow?"
