## You'll want to know: صخرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C472-QIMMA472-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صخرة** — *ṣakhra* — "a rock".

> جلسنا على صخرة كبيرة.
>
> *jalasnā ʿalā ṣakhra kabīra.*
>
> We sat on a big rock.

Plural: صخور, *ṣukhūr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صخرة** (*ṣakhra*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-kh-r*, "great hard rocks" (Lane).
