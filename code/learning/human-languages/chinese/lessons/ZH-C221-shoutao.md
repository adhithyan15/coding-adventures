## You'll want to know: shǒutào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C221-SNOW221-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shǒutào* — "gloves".

> *wàibian hěn lěng nǐ dài shǒutào le ma* — It's cold out. Have you got your gloves?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shǒutào* is built from 手 'hand' and *tào* 'a cover', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuwX* and *thawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'long', so how it came to mean a cover is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
