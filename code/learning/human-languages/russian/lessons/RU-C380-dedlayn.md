## You'll want to know: дедлайн
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C380-BUDUSHCHEYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**дедлайн** (*dedláyn*) — "a deadline".

> Дедлайн — в пятницу.
>
> *Dedláyn — v pyátnitsu.*
>
> The deadline is on Friday.

Masculine. Colloquial, from office talk; in formal Russian, *крайний срок*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**дедлайн** is a recent borrowing from English *deadline*.
