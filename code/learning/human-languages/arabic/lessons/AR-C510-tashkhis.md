## You'll want to know: تشخيص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C510-TASHKHIS510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تشخيص** — *tashkhīṣ* — "a diagnosis".

> كان تشخيص الطبيب صحيحا.
>
> *kāna tashkhīṣ aṭ-ṭabīb ṣaḥīḥan.*
>
> The doctor's diagnosis was correct.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تشخيص** (*tashkhīṣ*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-kh-ṣ*, "a person, a figure seen from afar" (Lane). Shakhṣ, a person, which you know, is from the same root; Form II is to make a thing stand out clearly.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
