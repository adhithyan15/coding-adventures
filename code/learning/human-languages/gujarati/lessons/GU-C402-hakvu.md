# હાંકવું (hā̃kvũ) — to drive a cart; to drive (animals) on

## You'll want to know: હાંકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C402-CART402-05]; assesses=[] -->

**હાંકવું** — *hā̃kvũ* — "to drive a cart; to drive (animals) on".

> દાદા રોજ સવારે ગાડું હાંકે છે.
>
> *dādā roj savāre gāḍũ hā̃ke che.*
>
> Grandfather drives the cart every morning.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગાડું હાંક્યું, *teṇe gāḍũ hā̃kyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હાંકવું** (*hā̃kvũ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *hakkayati*, "calls to" (CDIAL 13939): you drive oxen on by shouting at them. CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to drive".
