## You'll want to know: fulminarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C436-LUCE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**fulminarsi** — "to burn out (of a bulb)".

> Si è fulminata la lampadina della cucina.
>
> *The kitchen bulb has burnt out.*

*Fulminare* also means to strike with lightning, or with a look.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fulminarsi* is built on *fulmine* 'lightning', which is inherited from Latin *fulmen* 'thunderbolt' (EtymDB).
