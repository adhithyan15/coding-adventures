## HL-C337 — Hindi taking declares its stem and present forms

After the coming-form repair, **ले** is the highest-ranked independent modern
Hindi verb form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 35 already
derives **लेता**, **लेती**, and **लेते** from **लेना**, practises the gendered
forms, and uses the respectful form in its explanation. Its headword declares
only the infinitive and the related noun **लाभ**, however, so reports and
downstream cards cannot see the forms learners are explicitly taught.

Declare **ले**, **लेता**, **लेती**, and **लेते** beside **लेना**, make the
derivation explicit, practise the respectful form, and recall the complete
family. Chapter 4's spelling walk also stops isolating the syllable **ले** from
inside **मिलेंगे**: it now builds **मिले** as one chunk, so the continuity
report does not mistake script practice for an earlier use of the verb stem.
This closes the reporting gap without introducing a new construction or a new
forward reference.
