## You'll want to know: शुभ यात्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C121-CARE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शुभ यात्रा** (*śubh yātrā*) — "have a good journey".

**शुभ** is the auspicious of **शुभ रात्रि**; **यात्रा** is "a journey", a formal cousin of **सफ़र**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A wish for someone setting off.
