## You'll want to know: shìjiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C291-INVENT291-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shìjiàn* — "incident; event (in the news)".

> *zhège shìjiàn fāshēng zài Běijīng* — The incident happened in Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shìjiàn* is built from 事 'a matter' and *jiàn* 'an item', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzriH* and *gjenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to divide into parts'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
