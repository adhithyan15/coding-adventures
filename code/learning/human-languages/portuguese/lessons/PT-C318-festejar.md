## You'll want to know: festejar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C318-FESTEJAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**festejar** — "to celebrate (with a party)".

> Festejámos o aniversário dela num restaurante.
>
> *We celebrated her birthday in a restaurant.*

Livelier than *celebrar*: music, food, friends.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*festejar* is built on *festa* "a party", from Latin *fēsta* "feast days" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists it beside Occitan *festejar* and French *festoyer*.
