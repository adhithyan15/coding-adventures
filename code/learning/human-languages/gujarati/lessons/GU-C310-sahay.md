## You'll want to know: સહાય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C310-TUKDI310-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સહાય** — *sahāy* — "assistance".

> ટુકડીએ ગામના લોકોની સહાય લીધી.
>
> *ṭukḍīe gāmnā lokonī sahāy līdhī.*
>
> The squad took the villagers' help.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સહાય** (*sahāy*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sahāya*, "a companion, a helper" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is the help itself, a more formal word than **મદદ**.
