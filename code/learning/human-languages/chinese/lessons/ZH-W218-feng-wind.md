## Script — a frame, and two short strokes inside
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-FENG-WIND-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 风

**Two pieces: a frame, then what sits inside it.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The frame, two strokes.** A long stroke falling to the left, from the top down to the foot. Then a bar from its top that runs right, turns and falls to the lower right, flicking up at its end.

**Inside, two strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left, then a dot falling to the right across it, like a small cross.

2 + 2 = four.
