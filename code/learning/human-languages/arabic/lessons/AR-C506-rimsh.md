## You'll want to know: رمش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C506-WAJH506-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رمش** — *rimsh* — "an eyelash".

> للطفلة رموش طويلة.
>
> *li-ṭ-ṭifla rumūsh ṭawīla.*
>
> The little girl has long eyelashes.

Plural: رموش, *rumūsh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رمش** (*rimsh*) is a native Arabic word for an eyelash, usually traced to the root *r-m-sh*; the origin of the sense is not settled. Its plural *rumūsh* is the common form.
