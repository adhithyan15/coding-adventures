Kannada, chapter 511: Catching Something.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ತಗುಲು (tagulu) — to catch (an illness); to touch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the gums, then the Kannada for the jaw.

You'll want to know: ತಗುಲು.
ತಗುಲು — tagulu — "to catch (an illness); to touch".
ಮಳೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನೆನೆದಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ಮಗುವಿಗೆ ಶೀತ ತಗುಲಿತು. maḷeyalli nenediddarinda maguvige śīta tagulitu. Because the child got soaked in the rain, it caught a cold.
Past (it …): ತಗುಲಿತು, tagulitu.
The one who falls ill takes -ಗೆ: ಅವನಿಗೆ ಸೋಂಕು ತಗುಲಿತು. Costs too: ಖರ್ಚು ತಗುಲುತ್ತದೆ, it will cost.

The word, taken apart.
ತಗುಲು (tagulu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3004 lists Kannada tagalu, tagilu and tagulu, "to come in contact with, touch, hit".

ಮಾತ್ರೆ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡ ಮೇಲೆ ನನಗೆ ಮಂಪರು ಬಂದಿತು. mātre tegedukoṇḍa mēle nanage mamparu banditu. After taking the tablet I felt groggy.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಉಲ್ಬಣಿಸು (ulbaṇisu) — to get worse; to flare up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಉಲ್ಬಣಿಸು.
ಉಲ್ಬಣಿಸು — ulbaṇisu — "to get worse; to flare up".
ಚಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಕೆಮ್ಮು ಉಲ್ಬಣಿಸಿತು. caḷiyalli ajjana kemmu ulbaṇisitu. In the cold, grandfather's cough flared up.
Past (it …): ಉಲ್ಬಣಿಸಿತು, ulbaṇisitu.
Of an illness, a quarrel or a problem that grows worse.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಲ್ಬಣಿಸು (ulbaṇisu) is built on Sanskrit ulbaṇa, "superfluous, abundant, excessive, strong" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu: to become too much.

You'll want to know: ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣ.
ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣ — rōgalakṣaṇa — "a symptom".
ಜ್ವರ ಮತ್ತು ಕೆಮ್ಮು ಈ ಕಾಯಿಲೆಯ ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣಗಳು. jvara mattu kemmu ī kāyileya rōgalakṣaṇagaḷu. Fever and a cough are the symptoms of this illness.
Plural: ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣಗಳು, rōgalakṣaṇagaḷu.
ರೋಗ, disease + ಲಕ್ಷಣ, a mark.

The word, taken apart.
ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣ (rōgalakṣaṇa) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit rōga, "disease" (CDIAL 10829), and lakṣaṇa, "a line, a mark" (CDIAL 10882).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಲಸಿಕೆ (lasike) — a vaccine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to get worse, then the Kannada for a symptom.

You'll want to know: ಲಸಿಕೆ.
ಲಸಿಕೆ — lasike — "a vaccine".
ಮಕ್ಕಳಿಗೆ ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಲಸಿಕೆ ಹಾಕಿದರು. makkaḷige śāleyalli lasike hākidaru. The children were vaccinated at school.
ಲಸಿಕೆ (lasike) ಹಾಕು, to vaccinate.

The word, taken apart.
ಲಸಿಕೆ (lasike), the Kannada word for a vaccine, is usually traced to Sanskrit lasīkā, which Monier-Williams glosses as "watery humour in the body, lymph, serum" (Monier-Williams); how it came to name a vaccine is not settled.
