## You'll want to know: ખમીર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C466-SHAURYA466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખમીર** — *khamīr* — "grit; mettle".

> મારા દાદામાં ઘણું ખમીર હતું.
>
> *mārā dādāmā̃ ghaṇũ khamīr hatũ.*
>
> My grandfather had a lot of grit.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખમીર** (*khamīr*) is usually traced to Arabic *xamīr*, "leaven", through Persian. Gujarati also uses it for the spirit that makes a person rise to a challenge, probably a picture of dough rising.
