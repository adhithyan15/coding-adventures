## You'll want to know: مجرم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C407-JORM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مجرم** (*mojrem*) — "a criminal, an offender".

> پلیس مجرم را پیدا کرد.
>
> *polis mojrem râ peydâ kard.*
>
> The police found the criminal.

Plural *mojremân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجرم** is usually traced to Arabic *mujrim* "offender", one who has committed a *jurm*.
