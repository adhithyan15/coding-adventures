## You'll want to know: کلید ساز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C454-KAFFASH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کلید ساز** (*kelidsâz*) — "a locksmith, a key cutter".

> کلید ساز برای ما یک کلید تازه ساخت.
>
> *Kelidsâz barâ-ye mâ yek kelid-e tâze sâkht.*
>
> The locksmith made a new key for us.

*kelid* 'key' + *sâz* 'maker'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kelidsâz* is built from *kelid* 'key', which EtymDB traces through Middle Persian *kilēd* to Greek *kleís* 'key', and native *sâz*, the present stem of *sâkhtan* 'to make'.
