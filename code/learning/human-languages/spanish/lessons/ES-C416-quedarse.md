# quedarse — come to rest and stay

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-HABITACION] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **una habitación**, a room. A traveller who keeps that room
for several nights is going to **quedarse**, to stay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-QUEDARSE]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish **quedar** reaches back to Latin **quietāre**, to make quiet or bring
to rest. The **se** turns the action toward the person: **quedarse** is to remain
or stay. Think of a traveller coming to rest in one place.

## Grammar Lens: say who stays
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-QUEDARSE] -->

The pronoun changes with the person: **me quedo** means “I stay,” while the
infinitive follows another verb in **quiero quedarme**, “I want to stay.” A
duration can follow directly: **quedarse tres noches**.
