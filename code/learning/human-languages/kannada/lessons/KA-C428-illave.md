# ಇಲ್ಲವೇ (illavē) — or else; or

## You'll want to know: ಇಲ್ಲವೇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C428-ONDOMME428-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇಲ್ಲವೇ** — *illavē* — "or else; or".

> ಇಂದು ಇಲ್ಲವೇ ನಾಳೆ ಮಳೆ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *indu illavē nāḷe maḷe baruttade.*
>
> It will rain today or tomorrow.

Like ಅಥವಾ, but stronger: one or the other.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇಲ್ಲವೇ** (*illavē*) is native Dravidian: *illa*, "it is not", with -*ē*; DEDR 2559 lists the Kannada family of *illa*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
