## You'll want to know: তুষার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C426-THANDA426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**তুষার** — *tushār* — "snow; frost".

> শীতে উঁচু পাহাড়ে তুষার পড়ে।
>
> *shīte ũchu pāhāṛe tushār pôṛe.*
>
> In winter snow falls on the high mountains.

বরফ is ice, and snow in everyday talk; তুষার is the careful word for snow and frost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তুষার** (*tushār*) is Sanskrit *tuṣāra*, "cold, frigid", and as a noun "frost, snow" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.
