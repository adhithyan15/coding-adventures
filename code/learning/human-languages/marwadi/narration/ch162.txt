Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 162: Tomorrow.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
हूं काल (kāl) जासूं (jāsū̃) — I will go.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I went to the market yesterday. The same काल (kāl) also points the other way, to tomorrow.

Grammar Lens: -सूं.
Add -सूं to a stem and you have I will:
हूं काल बाजार जासूं। hū̃ kāl bājār jāsū̃. — I will go to the market tomorrow.
No हूं at the end: the future stands on its own. Every stem takes it the same way: आसूं, खासूं, बोलसूं, समझसूं. CDIAL quotes an Old Marwari line with this future in it, "I will fan" (CDIAL 5593).

Guided Practice.
You know two set phrases that look ahead: पाछे मिलसू and कितणो आखरी भाव दोगे? Keep saying them as you learned them.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: हूं काल आसूं। — hū̃ kāl āsū̃. — I will come tomorrow.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं आथण दूध पीसूं। — hū̃ āthaṇ dūdh pīsū̃. — I will drink milk in the evening.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं काल बाजार जासूं। — hū̃ kāl bājār jāsū̃. — I will go to the market tomorrow.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I will go? (जासूं (jāsū̃).) I will come? (आसूं.) What comes after it? (Nothing.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Say that you will go to the market tomorrow.


Lesson 2 of 4.
हूं काल (kāl) कोनी जासूं (konī jāsū̃) — I won't go.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I will come tomorrow. Then I don't drink tea.

Grammar Lens: कोनी before the future.
कोनी goes in front of the future, as it does in front of the present:
हूं काल कोनी जासूं। hū̃ kāl konī jāsū̃. — I won't go tomorrow.
हूं परसूं कोनी आसूं। hū̃ parsū̃ konī āsū̃. — I won't come the day after tomorrow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: हूं मांस कोनी खासूं। — hū̃ mā̃s konī khāsū̃. — I won't eat meat.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं आज बाजार कोनी जासूं। — hū̃ āj bājār konī jāsū̃. — I won't go to the market today.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I won't go? (कोनी जासूं (konī jāsū̃).) I don't go? (कोनी जाऊं.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that you won't go tomorrow.


Lesson 3 of 4.
काल (kāl) — yesterday or tomorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I won't go tomorrow. Then say काल (kāl) and परसूं on their own.

Grammar Lens: the verb decides.
काल (kāl) is one word for two days, and the verb tells you which:
हूं काल गयो। hū̃ kāl gayo. — I went yesterday.
हूं काल जासूं। hū̃ kāl jāsū̃. — I will go tomorrow.
परसूं works the same way, for the day before yesterday or the day after tomorrow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: राम काल आयो। — rām kāl āyo. — Ram came yesterday.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं काल आसूं। — hū̃ kāl āsū̃. — I will come tomorrow.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: मां परसूं आई। — mā̃ parsū̃ āī. — Mother came the day before yesterday.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
In हूं काल (kāl) गयो।, which day? (Yesterday.) In हूं काल (kāl) जासूं (jāsū̃)।? (Tomorrow.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that Ram came yesterday.


Lesson 4 of 4.
Tomorrow — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say I will come tomorrow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I will go to the market tomorrow; I won't go to the market today]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I will drink milk in the evening; I won't eat meat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I went yesterday; I will go tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I drew water; I will draw water tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I will go? (जासूं.) I won't go? (कोनी जासूं (konī jāsū̃).) I went? (गयो.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say: I went yesterday, but I won't go tomorrow.
