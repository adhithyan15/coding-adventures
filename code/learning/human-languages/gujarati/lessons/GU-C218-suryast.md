## You'll want to know: સૂર્યાસ્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C218-SUN218-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂર્યાસ્ત** — *sūryāst* — "sunset".

> **આજે સૂર્યાસ્ત સુંદર હશે.** — *āje sūryāst sundar haśe* — "The sunset will be beautiful today."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂર્યાસ્ત** (*sūryāst*) is Sanskrit *sūryāsta*, "sunset", built from *sūrya*, "sun", and *asta*, "setting" (CDIAL 973), taken over whole as a tatsama.
