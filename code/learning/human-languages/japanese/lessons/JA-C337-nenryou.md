## You'll want to know: ねんりょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C337-SETSUYAKU337-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ねんりょう** — *nenryō* — "fuel".

> **ねんりょうが たかく なったので、こまっています。** — *nenryō ga takaku natta node, komatte imasu* — "Fuel has become expensive, so we are in trouble."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねんりょう** (*nenryō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nen*, "burn" and *ryō*, "materials" (Middle Chinese *lew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
