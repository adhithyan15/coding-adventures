## You'll want to know: أرملة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C337-IRTH337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أرملة** — *armala* — "a widow".

> عاشت الأرملة مع ابنتها.
>
> *ʿāshat al-armala maʿa ibnatihā.*
>
> The widow lived with her daughter.

Plural: أرامل, *arāmil*.

A widower is أرمل.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أرملة** (*armala*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-m-l* (Lane), where Lane gives *armala* as a woman without a husband, and *armal*, a man in need.
