# बताना (batānā) — to tell, to let someone know

## You'll want to know: बताना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C282-BATANA-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C181-THG181-02, HI-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01, HI-LEX-C84-POSS-04, HI-LEX-SAKNA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01] -->

**बताना** — *batānā* — "to tell, to let someone know".

> **मुझे रास्ता बताइए।**
>
> *mujhe rāstā batāie.* — "Please tell me the way."

The one who is told takes **को** or its short form: **मुझे**, **उसे**. What is told is the thing, and in the past the verb follows it:

- **उसने मुझे अपना नाम बताया।** (*usne mujhe apnā nām batāyā.*) — She told me her name.
- **क्या आप मुझे बता सकते हैं?** (*kyā āp mujhe batā sakte haiṁ?*) — Can you tell me?

**कहना** reports what someone said: **उसने कहा कि …**. **बताना** gives someone news or directions: **उसने मुझे बताया कि …** (*usne mujhe batāyā ki …*), "she told me that …".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बताना** (*batānā*) has no row in CDIAL, and its origin is not settled. Its ending is the **-आना** of **दिखाना**, "to show", and **समझाना**, "to explain": verbs of passing something on to someone.
