## You'll want to know: kichern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C473-KICHERN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**kichern** — "to giggle".

> Die Mädchen kicherten in der letzten Reihe.
>
> *The girls were giggling in the back row.*

A softer, higher laugh than *lachen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kichern* is usually explained as an imitative word, copying the sound of a high, held-back laugh; EtymDB lists it without a source, so its origin is not settled.
