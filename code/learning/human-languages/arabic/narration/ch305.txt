Arabic, chapter 305: In court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
محكمة (maḥkama) — a court of law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for at that time, then the Arabic for a reign.

You'll want to know: محكمة.
محكمة — maḥkama — "a court of law".
في المدينة محكمة كبيرة. fī al-madīna maḥkama kabīra. There is a big court in the city.
Plural: محاكم, maḥākim.

The word, taken apart.
محكمة (maḥkama) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-k-m (Lane), "to restrain from wrong, to judge". The mafʿala pattern names a place: "the place of judging".

Lesson 2 of 5.
شاهد عيان (shāhid ʿiyān) — an eyewitness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a reign, then the Arabic for a court of law.

You'll want to know: شاهد عيان.
شاهد عيان — shāhid ʿiyān — "an eyewitness".
تكلم شاهد عيان أمام القاضي. takallama shāhid ʿiyān amāma al-qāḍī. An eyewitness spoke before the judge.
Plural شهود عيان. The taught verb شاهد, to watch, is the same root.

The word, taken apart.
شاهد عيان (shāhid ʿiyān) is "a witness by sight": shāhid is built on the native Arabic root sh-h-d, "to be present, to witness", and ʿiyān on ʿ-y-n, the root of ʿayn, an eye (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
اتهم (ittahama) — to accuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a court of law, then the Arabic for an eyewitness.

You'll want to know: اتهم.
اتهم — ittahama — "to accuse".
اتهمت الشرطة الرجل بالسرقة. ittahamat ash-shurṭa ar-rajul bi-s-sariqa. The police accused the man of theft.
Present: يتهم, yattahim.
The person accused, then بـ before the deed: اتهمه بالكذب.

The word, taken apart.
اتهم (ittahama) is built on the native Arabic root w-h-m (Lane), "to fancy, to suspect". It is Form VIII, where w merges into the t: "to cast suspicion on"; tuhma is a charge.

Lesson 5 of 5.
حكم (ḥakama) — to pass judgment; to rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to accuse, then the Arabic for a defendant.

You'll want to know: حكم.
حكم — ḥakama — "to pass judgment; to rule".
حكم القاضي على اللص بالسجن. ḥakama al-qāḍī ʿalā al-liṣṣ bi-s-sijn. The judge sentenced the thief to prison.
Present: يحكم, yaḥkum.
حكم (ḥakama) على someone بـ: to sentence; حكم (ḥakama) البلد: to rule the country. The noun حكم (ḥakama) is a verdict.

The word, taken apart.
حكم (ḥakama) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-k-m (Lane), first "to restrain from wrong", then "to judge" and "to rule"; ḥikma, wisdom, shares it.
