## You'll want to know: احتمال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C401-IHTIMAL401-01]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمال** — *iḥtimāl* — "a probability".

> هناك احتمال كبير للمطر غدا.
>
> *hunāka iḥtimāl kabīr li-l-maṭar ghadan.*
>
> There is a high probability of rain tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمال** (*iḥtimāl*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-m-l*, "to carry, to bear" (Lane). *Iḥtimāl* is the Form VIII verbal noun, bearing a thing, allowing for it: a probability.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
