## You'll want to know: سائرن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C289-BUJHI289-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سائرن** — *sāiran* — "a siren".

> سائرن سن کر سب باہر آئے۔
>
> *sāiran sun kar sab bāhar āe.*
>
> Hearing the siren, everyone came outside.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سائرن** (*sāiran*) is borrowed from English *siren*. Fire engines, ambulances and the police all have one.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
