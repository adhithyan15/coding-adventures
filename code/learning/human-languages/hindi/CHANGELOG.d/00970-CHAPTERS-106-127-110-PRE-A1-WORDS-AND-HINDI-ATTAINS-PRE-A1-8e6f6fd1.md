Built like the Telugu tranches: each word lesson practises the two atoms before
it, and chapters 115 and 127 each close on two review lessons. Two things the
gates caught and the lessons now handle:

- `HI-CONCEPT-C97-MESSAGE-01` (chapter 105's message-writing atom) had one
  revisit and only became visible to the gate once the track grew past its
  window; the chapter-114 invitation lesson now revisits it.
- Independent **इ** is not taught as a letter in this book, so no lesson prints
  it outside a word's own headword. Prose names "the respectful ending of
  **बोलिए**" instead of writing the ending alone, and the review lists *āie* in
  romanization.

Words already taught or printed in earlier lessons were left out (दवा, गर्म,
ख़ुश, बैठिए, ज़रूरत, कहाँ से, पता, कैसे हो, सुनिए), and डॉक्टर is spelled
डाक्टर because the Devanagari data does not carry the candra-o sign.

This is a claim about the corpus, not a learner: no exam item has been scored
against Hindi. A1's blockers: vocabulary 184, reinforcement 60,
verb-vocabulary 24, atom-budget 1, spine-nodes 1.
