## You'll want to know: dédommager
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C433-DEDOMMAGEMENT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**dédommager** — "to compensate".

> La compagnie a dédommagé les passagers du vol annulé.
>
> *The airline compensated the passengers of the cancelled flight.*

*Dédommager quelqu'un de quelque chose*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dédommager* is built from *dé-* 'un-' and *dommage* 'harm', which goes back to Latin *damnum* 'loss' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the verb.
