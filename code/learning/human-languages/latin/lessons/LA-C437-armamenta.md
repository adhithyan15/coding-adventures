## You'll want to know: armāmenta, armāmentōrum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C437-RUDENS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**armāmenta, armāmentōrum** — "a ship's tackle, the rigging; equipment".

> Procella armāmenta nāvis frangit.
>
> *The storm breaks the ship's rigging.*

Second declension, neuter plural: *armāmenta, armāmentōrum n.* Masts, sails and ropes together. English *armament* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*armāmenta* is built on *arma* 'gear, equipment' (Lewis & Short) with the suffix *-mentum*. English *armament* comes from it.
