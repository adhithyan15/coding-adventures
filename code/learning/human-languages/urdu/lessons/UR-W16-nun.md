# ن — a deep bowl, and one dot

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-KAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write ک. Bowl and hook, lift, slash.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-NUN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ن

A **bowl** swept from right to left, dropping well **below the baseline** and
rising again — then one lift, and a single **dot** placed near the baseline,
inside the curve.

Two parts, one lift. The bowl is deeper and rounder than the flatter one you
drew in kāf, and the dot sits low rather than floating above.

Its name is **nūn** and it says **n**.
