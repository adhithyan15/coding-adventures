## You'll want to know: ఐతిహ్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C370-GATHA370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఐతిహ్యం** — *aitihyaṁ* — "a legend; lore".

> ఆ కోట గురించి ఒక ఐతిహ్యం ఉంది.
>
> *ā kōṭa guriñci oka aitihyaṁ undi.*
>
> There is a legend about that fort.

A story handed down by word of mouth, not written in history books.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఐతిహ్యం** (*aitihyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *aitihya*, "traditional instruction, tradition" (Monier-Williams), from *iti ha*, "thus indeed".
