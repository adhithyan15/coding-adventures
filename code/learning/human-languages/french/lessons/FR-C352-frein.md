## You'll want to know: le frein
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C352-FREIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le frein** — "the brake; the obstacle".

> Le prix est un frein pour beaucoup de familles.
>
> *The price is an obstacle for many families.*

On a car or a bike, *les freins* are the brakes; the verb is *freiner*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frein* is inherited from Latin *frēnum* "a bridle, a bit" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): what holds a horse back.
