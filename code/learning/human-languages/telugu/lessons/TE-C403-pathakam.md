# పథకం (pathakaṁ) — a scheme; a programme

## You'll want to know: పథకం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C403-PARIPALANA403-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పథకం** — *pathakaṁ* — "a scheme; a programme".

> ప్రభుత్వం పేదల కోసం కొత్త పథకం తెచ్చింది.
>
> *prabhutvaṁ pēdala kōsaṁ kotta pathakaṁ teccindi.*
>
> The government brought in a new scheme for the poor.

Plural: పథకాలు, *pathakālu*.

Plural పథకాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పథకం** (*pathakaṁ*) has an origin that is not settled: it looks like a Sanskrit loan, but neither CDIAL nor Monier-Williams gives a source with this sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
