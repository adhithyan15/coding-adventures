Punjabi, chapter 94: Turban, Shoe, Cap, Shirt, Six.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪੱਗ (pagg) — a turban.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a ring, then the Punjabi for a watch.

You'll want to know: ਪੱਗ.
ਪੱਗ — pagg — "a turban".

The word, taken apart.
ਪੱਗ (pagg) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from paggā, "headdress", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7644). CDIAL doubts a link with Sanskrit pragraha, "rein, bridle".

The word, taken apart.
ਜੁੱਤੀ (juttī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from yukta, "joined, yoked" (CDIAL 10479). CDIAL lists Punjabi juttī, "shoe".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਕਮੀਜ਼ (kamīz) — a shirt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a shoe, then the Punjabi for a cap.

You'll want to know: ਕਮੀਜ਼.
ਕਮੀਜ਼ — kamīz — "a shirt".

The word, taken apart.
ਕਮੀਜ਼ (kamīz) is a loan from Arabic qamīṣ, "shirt", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-m-ṣ (Lane). The Arabic word is itself probably from Late Latin camisia.
