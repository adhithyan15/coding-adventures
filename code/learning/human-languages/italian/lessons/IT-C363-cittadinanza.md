## You'll want to know: la cittadinanza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C363-CITTADINO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la cittadinanza** — "the citizenship".

> Dopo dieci anni ha chiesto la cittadinanza.
>
> *After ten years she applied for citizenship.*

It can also mean the citizens of a town as a group.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cittadinanza* is built on *cittadino* "citizen", from *città*, which is inherited from Latin *cīvitās* "citizenship, city" (EtymDB).
