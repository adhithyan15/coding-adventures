## You'll want to know: কিছু
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C276-GHOTONA276-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কিছু** — *kichhu* — "something; some".

> গতকাল রাতে পাড়ায় কিছু একটা ঘটল।
>
> *gôtôkāl rāte pāṛāy kichhu ekṭā ghôṭlô.*
>
> Something happened in the neighbourhood last night.

Before a noun it means some: কিছু টাকা, some money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কিছু** (*kichhu*) comes down from Sanskrit *kiṁcid*, 'anything' (CDIAL 3144, which lists Bengali *kichu*). কিছু একটা is 'some one thing', something you cannot yet name.
