## You'll want to know: toben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C468-WUT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**toben** — "to rage; to romp".

> Die Kinder toben im Garten.
>
> *The children are romping in the garden.*

Of people in anger, of storms, and of children playing wildly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The verb *toben* goes back to Old High German *tobēn* "to rage, to be mad"; its older origin is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
