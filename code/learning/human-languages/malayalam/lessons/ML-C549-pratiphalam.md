## You'll want to know: പ്രതിഫലം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C549-PUROGATI549-02]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രതിഫലം** — *pratiphalaṁ* — "a fee; a reward".

> ആ ജോലിക്ക് അവന് നല്ല പ്രതിഫലം കിട്ടി.
>
> *ā jōlikkŭ avanŭ nalla pratiphalaṁ kiṭṭi.*
>
> He got a good fee for that job.

പ്രതി- in return + ഫലം a fruit, a result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രതിഫലം** (*pratiphalaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *prati*, "back, in return", and *phala*, "fruit, result" (CDIAL 9051): what comes back for one's work.
