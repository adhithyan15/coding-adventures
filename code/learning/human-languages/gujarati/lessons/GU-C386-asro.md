## You'll want to know: આશરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C386-AASTHA386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**આશરો** — *āśro* — "a refuge".

> જો પૂર આવશે તો આખું ગામ શાળામાં આશરો લેશે.
>
> *jo pūr āvśe to ākhũ gām śāḷāmā̃ āśro leśe.*
>
> If the flood comes, the whole village will take shelter in the school.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આશરો** (*āśro*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "protection", under a reconstructed *āśara* (CDIAL 1455).
