## You'll want to know: प्रसादयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C456-SAMA456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसादयति** — *prasādayati* — "appeases".

> पुत्रः कुपितं पितरं प्रसादयति।
>
> *putraḥ kupitaṃ pitaraṃ prasādayati.*
>
> The son calms his angry father.

Past (he …): सः कुपितं पितरं प्रसादितवान्, *saḥ kupitaṃ pitaraṃ prasāditavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसादयति** (*prasādayati*) is built on *pra* and the root √sad, "to sit": *pra-√sad* is "to become placid, to be pleased", and this causative is "to make calm"; MW's ppp *prasādita* is "pleased, conciliated" (MW).
