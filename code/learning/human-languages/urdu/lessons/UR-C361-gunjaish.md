## You'll want to know: گنجائش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C361-IMKAN361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گنجائش** — *gunjāish* — "room; scope (for)".

> اگر گاڑی میں گنجائش ہے تو میں بھی آؤں گا۔
>
> *agar gāṛī meṅ gunjāish hai to maiṅ bhī āūṅ gā.*
>
> If there is room in the car, I will come too.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گنجائش** (*gunjāish*) is usually traced to Persian *gunjāyish*, "room, capacity".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
