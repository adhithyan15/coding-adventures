## You'll want to know: ਡਾਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C255-POST255-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕ** — *ḍāk* — "the post; the mail".

> ਡਾਕ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *ḍāk savere das vaje āundī hai.*
>
> The post comes at ten in the morning.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕ** (*ḍāk*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ḍakka*, "relay, post"; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists Hindi *ḍāk*, "relay, post" (CDIAL 5519).
