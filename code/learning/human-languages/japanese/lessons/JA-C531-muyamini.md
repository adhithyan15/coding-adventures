## You'll want to know: むやみに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C531-PURPOSE531-03]; assesses=[] -->

**むやみに** — *muyamini* — "recklessly; without thinking".

> **もし むやみに くすりを のんだら、あぶないでしょう。** — *moshi muyamini kusuri o nondara, abunai deshō* — "If you take medicine without thinking, it will probably be dangerous."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むやみに** (*muyamini*) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, with the particle *ni*; JMdict marks it as usually written in kana and its kanji spelling as ateji, characters chosen for their sound, and no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).
