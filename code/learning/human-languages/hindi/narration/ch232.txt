Lesson 1 of 5.
काँपना (kā̃pnā) — to shiver, to tremble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to yawn, then the Hindi for to cough.

You'll want to know: काँपना.
काँपना — kā̃pnā — "to shiver, to tremble".

The word, taken apart.
काँपना (kā̃pnā) (kāṁpnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kampate, "trembles, shivers" (CDIAL 2767).

Lesson 3 of 5.
चीखना (cīkhnā) — to scream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to shiver, then the Hindi for to smile.

You'll want to know: चीखना.
चीखना — cīkhnā — "to scream".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of चीखना (cīkhnā) is uncertain. CDIAL lists a cīkhnā only in the sense "to taste" (CDIAL 4557), which is a different word. It may be imitative, like cĩghāṛnā, "to screech" (CDIAL 4787).
