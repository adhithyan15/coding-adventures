## You'll want to know: こうはい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C230-BOSS230-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こうはい** — *kōhai* — "a junior; a younger schoolmate or colleague".

> **こうはいは よく せんぱいに しつもんを します。** — *kōhai wa yoku senpai ni shitsumon o shimasu* — "The juniors often ask their seniors questions."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうはい** (*kōhai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "after, behind" (Middle Chinese *huwX*) and *hai*, "fellows" (*pwojH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
