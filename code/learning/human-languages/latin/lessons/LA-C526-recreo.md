## You'll want to know: recreō, recreāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C526-SANO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**recreō, recreāre** — "to refresh, restore".

> Somnus corpus fessum recreat.
>
> *Sleep refreshes the tired body.*

*recreō, recreāre, recreāvī, recreātum*: *re-* 'again' + *creō* 'to make'. English *recreation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**recreō** is built on *re-* "again" and *creō* "to create" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB): to make new again. English *recreation* comes from it.
