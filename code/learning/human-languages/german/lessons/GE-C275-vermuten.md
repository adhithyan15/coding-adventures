## You'll want to know: vermuten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C275-ANGEBLICH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**vermuten** — "to suspect, to presume".

> Ich vermutete, dass er krank war.
>
> *I suspected that he was ill.*

With *dass* and the verb at the end: *Ich vermute, dass er recht hat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vermuten* is built on *Mut*, once "mind, mood", from Proto-Germanic *mōdaz* (EtymDB), with the prefix *ver-*: to have something in mind.
