## You'll want to know: জুয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C534-BAJI534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**জুয়া** — *juyā* — "gambling".

> জুয়ায় লোকটা তার সঞ্চয় হারাল।
>
> *Juyāy lokṭā tār sôñchôy hārālô.*
>
> The man lost his savings gambling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জুয়া** (*juyā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *juyā*, "gambling", under Sanskrit *dyūta* (CDIAL 6608).
