German, chapter 290: Bewusstlos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bewusstlos — unconscious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the fall, then the German for to bleed.

You'll want to know: bewusstlos.
bewusstlos — "unconscious".
Der Fahrer war kurz bewusstlos. The driver was briefly unconscious.
Bewusst 'aware' plus -los 'without'.

The word, taken apart.
bewusstlos joins bewusst "aware", built on wissen "to know", Proto-Germanic witaną (EtymDB), and -los "without".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ohnmächtig — faint, passed out; powerless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to bleed, then the German for unconscious.

You'll want to know: ohnmächtig.
ohnmächtig — "faint, passed out; powerless".
Sie wurde in der Hitze ohnmächtig. She fainted in the heat.
Ohnmächtig werden — to faint. Also 'powerless'.

The word, taken apart.
ohnmächtig is built on Ohnmacht, usually traced to Middle High German āmaht "lack of strength", later heard as ohne "without" plus Macht "might", a cousin of English might (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Bewusstsein — consciousness; awareness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for unconscious, then the German for faint.

You'll want to know: das Bewusstsein.
das Bewusstsein — "consciousness; awareness".
Nach ein paar Minuten kam er wieder zu Bewusstsein. After a few minutes he regained consciousness.
Das Bewusstsein verlieren — to lose consciousness.

The word, taken apart.
Bewusstsein joins bewusst "aware", from wissen "to know", Proto-Germanic witaną (EtymDB), and sein "to be": being aware.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ersticken — to choke, to suffocate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for faint, then the German for consciousness.

You'll want to know: ersticken.
ersticken — "to choke, to suffocate".
Der Hund erstickte fast an einem Knochen. The dog almost choked on a bone.
Perfekt with sein: Er ist fast erstickt. An etwas ersticken — to choke on something.

The word, taken apart.
ersticken is usually traced to Middle High German ersticken, built on an old verb sticken "to get stuck, to choke"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ertrinken — to drown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for consciousness, then the German for to choke.

You'll want to know: ertrinken.
ertrinken — "to drown".
Ein Mann holte das Kind aus dem Wasser, bevor es ertrank. A man pulled the child out of the water before it drowned.
Strong, with sein: ertrank, ist ertrunken.

The word, taken apart.
ertrinken is built on trinken "to drink", from Proto-Germanic drinkaną (EtymDB), with er-: to drink until the end.
