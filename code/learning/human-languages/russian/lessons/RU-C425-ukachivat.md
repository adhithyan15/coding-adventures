## You'll want to know: укачивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C425-KACHKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**укачивать** (*ukáchivat*) — "to make seasick or carsick; to rock to sleep".

> Меня всегда укачивает на море.
>
> *Menyá vsegdá ukáchivayet na móre.*
>
> I always get seasick at sea.

Mostly impersonal with the accusative: *меня укачивает* — I get motion sick. Perfective *укачать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*укачивать* is built from *у-* and *-качивать*, from *качать* 'to rock': to rock someone until they are drowsy or sick. EtymDB does not list the verb.
