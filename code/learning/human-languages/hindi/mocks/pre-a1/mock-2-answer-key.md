## Writing

1. **शुक्रिया**
2. **हिंदी**
3. **नाम**
4. **पानी**
5. **नमस्ते**
6. **ठीक**
7. Accept an independently written recognizable Devanagari place name, for
   example **दिल्ली**, **आगरा**, or **जयपुर**.
8. Accept an independently written appropriate thanks expression such as
   **शुक्रिया** or **धन्यवाद**. Romanization or copying earns no credit.

## Speaking

1. Accept **नमस्ते** or an equally appropriate taught Hindi greeting.
2. Accept a comprehensible identity frame such as **मेरा नाम … है।**
3. Accept a relevant state such as **मैं ठीक हूँ।**
4. Accept a comprehensible place response such as **मैं जयपुर से हूँ।**
5. Accept a relevant yes/no response or short Hindi-speaking frame, with any
   internally consistent gender agreement.
6. Accept a comprehensible polite tea request containing **चाय** and an
   appropriate request signal such as **कृपया**.

Score communicative success and intelligibility with the rubric; do not require
word-for-word reproduction of the examples.
