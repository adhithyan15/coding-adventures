बुद्धी (buddhī) — intellect; intelligence.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: बुद्धी.
बुद्धी — buddhī — "intellect; intelligence".
हा खेळ बुद्धीचा आहे, ताकदीचा नाही. hā kheḷ buddhīcā āhe, tākdīcā nāhī. This game is one of brains, not strength.
Feminine.
बुद्धीचा खेळ, a game of wits.

The word, taken apart.
बुद्धी (buddhī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit buddhi, "intelligence, discernment" (CDIAL 9277).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आकलन होणे (ākalan hoṇe) — to comprehend; to grasp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for memory power, then the Marathi for concentration.

You'll want to know: आकलन होणे.
आकलन होणे — ākalan hoṇe — "to comprehend; to grasp".
पुन्हा वाचल्यावर मला धड्याचं नीट आकलन झालं. punhā vācalyāvar malā dhaḍyācaṁ nīṭ ākalan jhālaṁ. After reading it again, I grasped the lesson properly.
Past (to him …): त्याला आकलन झालं, tyālā ākalan jhālaṁ.
Dative subject: मला आकलन होतं; what is grasped takes -चं.

The word, taken apart.
आकलन होणे (ākalan hoṇe) joins the tatsama आकलन, Sanskrit ākalana, "fastening, reckoning" (Monier-Williams), to होणे, "to become"; Marathi uses आकलन for taking in what something means.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सराव करणे (sarāv karṇe) — to practise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for concentration, then the Marathi for to comprehend.

You'll want to know: सराव करणे.
सराव करणे — sarāv karṇe — "to practise".
ती रोज एक तास गाण्याचा सराव करते. tī roj ek tās gāṇyācā sarāv karte. She practises singing for an hour every day.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सराव केला, tyāne sarāv kelā.
What is practised takes -चा: गाण्याचा सराव.

The word, taken apart.
सराव करणे (sarāv karṇe) joins सराव, "practice", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to करणे, "to do".
