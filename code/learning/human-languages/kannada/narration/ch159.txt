Kannada, chapter 159: To peel, To grind, To serve, To taste, To chew.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸುಲಿ (suli) — to peel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to pay, then the Kannada for to bathe.

You'll want to know: ಸುಲಿ.
ಸುಲಿ — suli — "to peel".

The word, taken apart.
ಸುಲಿ (suli) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil coli- (DEDR 2856). DEDR lists suli itself, "to strip off, peel". Its note compares an Indo-Aryan verb, Prakrit chollaï, "to skin" (CDIAL 5073), and leaves the direction of borrowing open.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ರುಚಿ ನೋಡು (ruci nōḍu) — to taste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to grind, then the Kannada for to serve.

You'll want to know: ರುಚಿ ನೋಡು.
ರುಚಿ ನೋಡು — ruci nōḍu — "to taste".

The word, taken apart.
ರುಚಿ ನೋಡು (ruci nōḍu) is ruci, "taste", from Sanskrit ruci, "light, beauty; relish" (CDIAL 10762), and nōḍu, "to look" (DEDR 3794), native. To taste is to "look at the taste".
