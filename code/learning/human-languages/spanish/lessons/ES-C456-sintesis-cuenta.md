# (revisar la cuenta) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-02, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el cargo** and **la ensalada**. One of them is on the bill
twice.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-01, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-02, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-03] -->

A bill on the table. Read it, then answer.

> **Mesa 7**

> 2 **ensaladas** mixtas .......... 14,00

> 1 **ensalada** mixta ............ 7,00

> 2 cafés ......................... 3,60

> **Cargo** por servicio .......... 2,50

> No se **aceptan** tarjetas.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: what has been counted twice, and what it should say]
- [YOU SAY: what the 2,50 is for]
- [YOU SAY: how you are going to have to pay]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-02, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-04, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-03] -->

To query this politely you need the right noun, and it is **cargo** rather
than *precio*: the 2,50 was not asked for in advance, it was added. *Hay un
cargo que no entiendo* is the sentence, and *hay un precio que no entiendo*
would sound as though you were confused by the menu.

**No se aceptan tarjetas** is the *se* construction again, and it is plural
because *tarjetas* is. Spanish agrees the verb with the thing, not with the
restaurant, so a single line telling you the same thing about one card would
read *no se acepta tarjeta*.

A useful softener for the whole exchange: *perdone, ¿me lo mira?* — literally
"will you look at it for me", and far more common than accusing anybody of an
error.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-01, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-02, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-03, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-04, ES-LEX-C456-CUENTA-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four lines querying a
bill: ask them to look at it, say there is a charge you do not understand, say
the salad appears twice, and ask whether they take card.

Then check yourself: did you use *cargo* rather than *precio*, and did *se
aceptan* agree with the cards?
