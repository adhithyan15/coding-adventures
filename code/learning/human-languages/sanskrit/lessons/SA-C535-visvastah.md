## You'll want to know: विश्वस्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C535-SATYASANDHA535-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वस्तः** — *viśvastaḥ* — "trusted; trustworthy".

> यदि मित्रं विश्वस्तम् अस्ति, तर्हि भयं न अस्ति।
>
> *yadi mitraṃ viśvastam asti, tarhi bhayaṃ na asti.*
>
> If a friend is trustworthy, there is nothing to fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वस्तः** (*viśvastaḥ*) is built on *vi* and the root √śvas, "to breathe", as its past participle, the root of *viśvasiti*, "trusts"; MW gives *viśvasta* as full of confidence, and as trusted, faithful (MW).
