## You'll want to know: cuòwù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C356-MISS356-04]; assesses=[] -->

*cuòwù* — "mistake; error".

> *wǒ juéde zhège cuòwù bú dà* — I don't think it's a big mistake.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cuòwù* is built from 错 'wrong' and *wù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshak* and *nguH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'an error'.
