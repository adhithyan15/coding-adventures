French, chapter 102: Police, Theatre, Library, Bakery, Town hall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la police — the police.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a village, then the French for the post office.

You'll want to know: la police.
la police — "the police".
