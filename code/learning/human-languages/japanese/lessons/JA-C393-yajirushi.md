## You'll want to know: やじるし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C393-MEJIRUSHI393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**やじるし** — *yajirushi* — "an arrow (on a sign)".

> **やじるしの ほうに あるいて ください。** — *yajirushi no hō ni aruite kudasai* — "Please walk the way the arrow points."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やじるし** (*yajirushi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ya*, "arrow" and *jirushi*, "mark"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
