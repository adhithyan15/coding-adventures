## You'll want to know: ねいろ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C469-INSTR469-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ねいろ** — *neiro* — "the tone of an instrument".

> **この ふえは ねいろが きれいです。** — *kono fue wa neiro ga kirei desu* — "This flute has a beautiful tone."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねいろ** (*neiro*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ne*, "sound" and *iro*, "colour" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
