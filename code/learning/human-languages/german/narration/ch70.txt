German, chapter 70: Banana, Potato, Tomato, Onion, Carrot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Banane — a banana.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for fruit, then the German for an apple.

You'll want to know: die Banane.
die Banane — "a banana".

The word, taken apart.
Banane from Portuguese or Spanish banana, from a West African language — borrowed. EtymDB 2.1 traces English banana to Wolof banaana.

Kartoffel from older German Tartuffel from Italian tartufolo, "truffle" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed: the potato, another lump dug from the earth, was named after the truffle.
