## You'll want to know: la violazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C386-VIOLAZIONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la violazione** — "the violation, the breach".

> Questa è una violazione della privacy.
>
> *This is a breach of privacy.*

*In violazione di* — in breach of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*violazione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *violātiō* "an injury, a profaning" (Lewis & Short), from *violāre* "to treat with violence".
