## You'll want to know: मासी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C261-MASI261-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मासी** — *māsī* — "a mother's sister; an aunt".

> म्हारी मासी गांव मांय रैवै है।
>
> *mhārī māsī gā̃v mā̃y raivai hai.*
>
> My mother's sister lives in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मासी** (*māsī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mātuḥṣvasṛ*, "a mother's sister". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *māsī* (CDIAL 10001).
