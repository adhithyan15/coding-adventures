## You'll want to know: تلاشی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C399-HIFAZAT399-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تلاشی** — *talāshī* — "a security search; a search of bags".

> حفاظت کے لیے ہمارے تھیلوں کی تلاشی ہوئی۔
>
> *hifāzat ke liye hamāre thailoṅ kī talāshī huī.*
>
> Our bags were searched for safety.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is built on *talāsh*, "a search", usually traced to Persian *talāsh* and perhaps further to Turkic, with the ending *-ī*: **تلاش کرنا** is to look for something, **تلاشی** the search of a bag or a person.
