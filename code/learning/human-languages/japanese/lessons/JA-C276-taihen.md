## You'll want to know: たいへん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C276-OOAME276-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たいへん** — *taihen* — "awful; serious".

> **おおあめで、きのうは たいへんでした。** — *ōame de, kinō wa taihen deshita* — "With the downpour, yesterday was awful."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいへん** (*taihen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "big" (Middle Chinese *dajH*) and *hen*, "unusual" (*pjenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
