## You'll want to know: 报
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C293-PAPER293-02]; assesses=[] -->

**报** — *bào* — "newspaper; the daily paper".

> **我每天早上看报** *wǒ měitiān zǎoshang kàn bào* — I read the paper every morning.

The new character, in pieces: 报 — seven strokes in two pieces: on the left the hand piece of 打, three strokes; on the right a bar that turns down and hooks back, a long vertical down its left side, then a bar that turns into a sweep to the lower left and a long stroke falling to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**报** (*bào*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *pawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'to report'; the sense 'a newspaper', the sheet that reports, is later and is not given there.
