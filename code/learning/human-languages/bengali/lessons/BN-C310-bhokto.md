## You'll want to know: ভক্ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C310-SHONKHO310-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ভক্ত** — *bhôktô* — "a devotee".

> পুজোর দিন মন্দিরে অনেক ভক্ত এলেন।
>
> *pujor din mondire ônek bhôktô elen.*
>
> Many devotees came to the temple on the day of the puja.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভক্ত** (*bhôktô*) is Sanskrit *bhakta*, first 'allotted, shared' and then 'devoted to, a worshipper' (Monier-Williams); **ভাত**, cooked rice, comes down from the same word (CDIAL 9331).
