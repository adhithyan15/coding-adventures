## You'll want to know: ねあげ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C421-NEGIRU421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ねあげ** — *neage* — "a price rise".

> **らいげつ うんちんの ねあげが あります。** — *raigetsu unchin no neage ga arimasu* — "Fares are going up next month."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねあげ** (*neage*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ne*, "price" and *age*, "raising" (from *ageru*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
