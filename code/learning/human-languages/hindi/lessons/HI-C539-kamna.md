## You'll want to know: कामना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C539-DUA539-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कामना** — *kāmnā* — "a wish (for someone's good)".

> हम आपकी अच्छी सेहत की कामना करते हैं।
>
> *Ham āpkī acchī sehat kī kāmnā karte haiṁ.*
>
> We wish you good health.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कामना** (*kāmnā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *kāmanā*, "wish, desire" (Monier-Williams).
