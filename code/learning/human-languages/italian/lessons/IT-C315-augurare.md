## You'll want to know: augurare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C315-AUGURI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**augurare** — "to wish (someone something)".

> Ti auguro buon viaggio!
>
> *I wish you a good journey!*

*Augurare qualcosa a qualcuno*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*augurare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *augurāre* "to read omens" (Lewis & Short), from *augur*, a priest who read the signs.
