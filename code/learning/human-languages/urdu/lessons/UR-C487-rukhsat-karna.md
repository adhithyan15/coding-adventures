## You'll want to know: رخصت کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C487-WEDDING487-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رخصت کرنا** — *rukhsat karnā* — "to see off; to send off (a bride)".

> رات کو سب نے دلہن کو رخصت کیا۔
>
> *rāt ko sab ne dulhan ko rukhsat kiyā.*
>
> At night everyone saw the bride off.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مہمانوں کو رخصت کیا, *us ne mehmānoṅ ko rukhsat kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رخصت کرنا** (*rukhsat karnā*) is built from **رخصت**, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rukhṣa*, "leave, permission", and **کرنا**, "to do": to give someone leave to go.
