Kannada, chapter 342: The Siege of the Fort.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹಾಕು (muttige hāku) — to besiege.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a family line, then the Kannada for a monarch.

You'll want to know: ಮುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹಾಕು.
ಮುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹಾಕು — muttige hāku — "to besiege".
ಆ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಸೈನ್ಯ ಕೋಟೆಗೆ ಮುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹಾಕಿತು. ā doḍḍa sainya kōṭege muttige hākitu. That great army besieged the fort.
Past (I …): ಮುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹಾಕಿದೆ, muttige hākide.
What is besieged takes -ಗೆ. Today people also ಮುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹಾಕು (muttige hāku) an office in protest.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹಾಕು (muttige hāku) is native Dravidian: ಮುತ್ತಿಗೆ, "a siege", belongs with Kannada muttu, "to enclose", and Tamil muṟṟu, "to surround, besiege" (DEDR 5018), with ಹಾಕು, "to put".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕಂದಕ (kandaka) — a moat; a trench.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a monarch, then the Kannada for to besiege.

You'll want to know: ಕಂದಕ.
ಕಂದಕ — kandaka — "a moat; a trench".
ಕೋಟೆಯ ಸುತ್ತ ಆಳವಾದ ಕಂದಕ ಇತ್ತು. kōṭeya sutta āḷavāda kandaka ittu. There was a deep moat around the fort.
A ditch dug for defence, often full of water.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಂದಕ (kandaka) is usually traced to Arabic khandaq, "a ditch, a trench", which came into the Indian languages through Persian.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಸುತ್ತುವರಿ.
ಸುತ್ತುವರಿ — suttuvari — "to surround".
ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಆ ಮನೆಯನ್ನು ಸುತ್ತುವರಿದರು. polīsaru ā maneyannu suttuvaridaru. The police surrounded that house.
Past (I …): ಸುತ್ತುವರಿದೆ, suttuvaride.
What is surrounded takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಸುತ್ತುವರಿ (suttuvari) is built on native suttu, "to surround, go round" (DEDR 2715), as in ಸುತ್ತ, "around"; the origin of the second part is not settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a moat, then the Kannada for to surround.

You'll want to know: ಶರಣಾಗು.
ಶರಣಾಗು — śaraṇāgu — "to surrender".
ಊಟ ಇಲ್ಲದೆ ಕೋಟೆಯ ಸೈನಿಕರು ಶರಣಾದರು. ūṭa illade kōṭeya sainikaru śaraṇādaru. With no food, the soldiers in the fort surrendered.
Past (I …): ಶರಣಾದೆ, śaraṇāde.
The one surrendered to takes -ಗೆ: ಪೊಲೀಸರಿಗೆ ಶರಣಾಗು (śaraṇāgu).

The word, taken apart.
ಶರಣಾಗು (śaraṇāgu) is built from Sanskrit śaraṇa, "protecting, shelter" (CDIAL 12326), and native āgu, "to become" (DEDR 333): to seek shelter with the stronger side.

You'll want to know: ಫಿರಂಗಿ.
ಫಿರಂಗಿ — phiraṅgi — "a cannon".
ಕೋಟೆಯ ಗೋಡೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಹಳೆಯ ಫಿರಂಗಿ ಇದೆ. kōṭeya gōḍeya mēle haḷeya phiraṅgi ide. There is an old cannon on the fort wall.
Plural: ಫಿರಂಗಿಗಳು, phiraṅgigaḷu.
Many old forts still show their ಫಿರಂಗಿಗಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ಫಿರಂಗಿ (phiraṅgi) is a loan from Persian firangī, "a Frank, a European": the guns came with European traders and soldiers.
