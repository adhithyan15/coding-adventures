# ചാരൻ (cāran) — a spy

## You'll want to know: ചാരൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C400-SATRU400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ചാരൻ** — *cāran* — "a spy".

> രാജാവ് ശത്രുവിന്റെ കൊട്ടാരത്തിലേക്ക് ഒരു ചാരനെ അയച്ചു.
>
> *rājāvŭ śatruvinṟe koṭṭārattilēkkŭ oru cārane ayaccu.*
>
> The king sent a spy to the enemy's palace.

One who goes about and watches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചാരൻ** (*cāran*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *cāra*, "one who moves about; a spy" (CDIAL 4754), from *car*, "to move", with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ.
