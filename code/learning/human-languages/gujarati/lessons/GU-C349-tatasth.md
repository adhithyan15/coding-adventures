## You'll want to know: તટસ્થ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C349-SAMAN349-04]; assesses=[] -->

**તટસ્થ** — *taṭasth* — "neutral".

> આ વિવાદમાં હું તટસ્થ રહીશ.
>
> *ā vivādmā̃ hũ taṭasth rahīś.*
>
> I will stay neutral in this dispute.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તટસ્થ** (*taṭasth*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *taṭastha*, "standing on the bank" (Monier-Williams): one who watches the river from the bank. Gujarati uses it for taking neither side.
