## You'll want to know: зять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C491-INLAWS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**зять** (*zyat*) — "a son-in-law; a sister's husband".

> На свадьбе тёща танцевала с зятем.
>
> *Na svádbe tyóshcha tantsevála s zyátem.*
>
> At the wedding the mother-in-law danced with her son-in-law.

Masculine; plural зятья.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*зять* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *zętь*, which EtymDB traces to PIE *ǵenh₁- 'to beget'.
