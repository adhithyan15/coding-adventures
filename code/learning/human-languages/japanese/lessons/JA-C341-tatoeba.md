## You'll want to know: たとえば
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C341-SETTOKU341-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たとえば** — *tatoeba* — "for example".

> **たとえば、だいこんは からだに いいと おもいます。** — *tatoeba, daikon wa karada ni ii to omoimasu* — "For example, I think daikon is good for you."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たとえば** (*tatoeba*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
