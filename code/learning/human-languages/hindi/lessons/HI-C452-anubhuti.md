## You'll want to know: अनुभूति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C452-BHAVNA452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुभूति** — *anubhūti* — "a deep feeling; an inner sense".

> गाना सुनकर मीरा को शांति की अनुभूति हुई।
>
> *gānā sunkar Mīrā ko śānti kī anubhūti huī.*
>
> Listening to the song, Mira felt a deep sense of peace.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुभूति** (*anubhūti*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *anubhūti*, "perception, knowledge" (Monier-Williams). It is a formal word for a feeling sensed deep inside; *anubhav* is the experience itself.
