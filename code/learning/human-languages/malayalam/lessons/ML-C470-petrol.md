# പെട്രോൾ (peṭrōḷ) — petrol

## You'll want to know: പെട്രോൾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C470-INDHANAM470-01]; assesses=[] -->

**പെട്രോൾ** — *peṭrōḷ* — "petrol".

> പെട്രോളിന്റെ വില വീണ്ടും കൂടി.
>
> *peṭrōḷinṟe vila vīṇṭuṁ kūṭi.*
>
> The price of petrol went up again.

A petrol station is a പെട്രോൾ പമ്പ് in everyday Malayalam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പെട്രോൾ** (*peṭrōḷ*) was borrowed from English *petrol*, the fuel of cars and motorbikes.
