## You'll want to know: o entrevistado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C285-OUVINTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o entrevistado** — "the interviewee".

> O entrevistado respondeu com calma.
>
> *The interviewee answered calmly.*

A woman is *a entrevistada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entrevistado* is the past participle of *entrevistar* used as a noun; *vista* goes back to Latin *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
