## You'll want to know: दूतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C317-DUTA317-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दूतः** — *dūtaḥ* — "an envoy; a messenger".

> शत्रोः दूतः सभाम् आगच्छति।
>
> *śatroḥ dūtaḥ sabhām āgacchati.*
>
> The enemy's envoy comes to the court.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दूतः** (*dūtaḥ*) MW thinks it probably comes from the root √du, "to go", and compares *dūra*, "far"; MW gives *dūta* as a messenger, envoy, ambassador (MW).
