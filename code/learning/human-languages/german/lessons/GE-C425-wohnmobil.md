## You'll want to know: das Wohnmobil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C425-CAMPING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Wohnmobil** — "the camper van, the motorhome".

> Mit dem Wohnmobil fahren sie drei Wochen durch das Land.
>
> *They travel round the country for three weeks in the camper van.*

Plural: *die Wohnmobile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wohnmobil* is built from *wohnen* 'to live' and *mobil* 'mobile' (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *mōbilis* 'movable' (Lewis & Short).
