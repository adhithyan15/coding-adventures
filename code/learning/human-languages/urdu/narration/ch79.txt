Urdu, chapter 79: Above, Below, Shop, Road, Bank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اوپر (ūpar) — above, up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for inside, then the Urdu for outside.

You'll want to know: اوپر.
اوپر — ūpar — "above, up".

The word, taken apart.
اوپر (ūpar) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from uppari, "above", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2333). It is Sanskrit upari, "above", with its consonant doubled for emphasis.

Lesson 3 of 5.
دکان (dukān) — a shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for above, then the Urdu for below.

You'll want to know: دکان.
دکان — dukān — "a shop".

The word, taken apart.
دکان (dukān) is a loan from Arabic dukkān, "shop", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is d-k-n (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
سڑک (saṛak) — a road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for below, then the Urdu for a shop.

You'll want to know: سڑک.
سڑک — saṛak — "a road".

The word, taken apart.
سڑک (saṛak) is inherited, but CDIAL gives two sources. It first lists the word under Sanskrit sṛti, "road, path" (CDIAL 13577), and in its addenda under śaṭati, "goes" (CDIAL 12269).
