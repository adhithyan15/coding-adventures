Itchy? (かゆい, kayui.) Smelly? (くさい, kusai.) Humid? (むしあつい, mushiatsui.) Funny; strange? (おかしい, okashii.) Interesting? (おもしろい, omoshiroi.) Amazing? (すごい, sugoi.) Terrible? (ひどい, hidoi.) Young?
