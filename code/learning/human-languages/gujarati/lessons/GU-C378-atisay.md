## You'll want to know: અતિશય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C378-HAD378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અતિશય** — *atiśay* — "excessive".

> જો તમે અતિશય મીઠાઈ ખાશો તો દાંત બગડશે.
>
> *jo tame atiśay mīṭhāī khāśo to dā̃t bagaḍśe.*
>
> If you eat too many sweets, your teeth will go bad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અતિશય** (*atiśay*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *atiśaya*, "surpassing, excess" (Monier-Williams).
