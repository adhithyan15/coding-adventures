## You'll want to know: la madrina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C546-BAUTIZO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la madrina** — "the godmother".

> Mi madrina me regaló una bicicleta.
>
> *My godmother gave me a bicycle.*

At a wedding she is the woman who accompanies the groom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*madrina* is inherited from spoken Latin *\*mātrīna* (EtymDB), built on *māter* 'mother'.
