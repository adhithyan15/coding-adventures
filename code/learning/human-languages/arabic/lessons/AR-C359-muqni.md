## You'll want to know: مقنع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C359-NAZAR359-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مقنع** — *muqniʿ* — "convincing".

> كان كلام المدير مقنعا.
>
> *kāna kalām al-mudīr muqniʿan.*
>
> The manager's words were convincing.

After كان the adjective takes -an: كان مقنعا.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقنع** (*muqniʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-n-ʿ*, "to be content, satisfied" (Lane). *Muqniʿ* is the Form IV active participle: that which convinces.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
