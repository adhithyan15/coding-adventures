## You'll want to know: возврат
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C436-VOZVRAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**возврат** (*vozvrát*) — "a refund; a return".

> Без чека возврат невозможен.
>
> *Bez chéka vozvrát nevozmózhen.*
>
> Without a receipt a refund is not possible.

Masculine. *Возврат денег* — a refund.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*возврат* is built from *воз-* 'back' and *-врат* 'a turning', the Church Slavonic shape of native *-ворот*, on the root of *вертеть* 'to turn' (EtymDB lists *возврат*).
