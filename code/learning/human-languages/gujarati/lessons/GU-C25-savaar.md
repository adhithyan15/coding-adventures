# Savār reaches the page

## Warm-up

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-SAVAAR-01, GU-LEX-SAVAAR-01, GU-SCRIPT-SA-01, GU-SCRIPT-VA-01, GU-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, GU-SCRIPT-RA-01, GU-PERFORMANCE-TIME-EIGHT-FOUR-SKILL-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-C25-savaar-reading","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-SAVAAR-01","GU-LEX-SAVAAR-01"],"prompt":"Read this word aloud and give its meaning: સવાર.","answer":"savār — morning","accepted":["savār morning"],"feedback":{"correct":"You read savār from signs you already knew.","incorrect":"Name the four pieces one at a time, then read the word again."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Uncover **સવાર** once. Four pieces, none of them new: **સ** and the **ા** stroke both come from the greeting lesson, and **વ** and **ર** from the courtesy chapter after it. Name the four pieces aloud in order, then
read the whole word once.
