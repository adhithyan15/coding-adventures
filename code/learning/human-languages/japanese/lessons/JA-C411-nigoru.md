## You'll want to know: にごる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C411-OGAWA411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**にごる** — *nigoru* — "to become muddy; to cloud over (of water)".

> **あめで かわの みずが にごりました。** — *ame de kawa no mizu ga nigorimashita* — "The rain made the river water muddy."

Polite **にごります**, *nigorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かわの みずが にごりました。**, *kawa no mizu ga nigorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にごる** (*nigoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
