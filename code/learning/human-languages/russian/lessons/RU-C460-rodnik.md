## You'll want to know: родник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C460-RUCHEY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**родник** (*rodník*) — "a spring (of water)".

> Вода в роднике холодная и чистая.
>
> *Vodá v rodniké kholódnaya i chístaya.*
>
> The water in the spring is cold and clean.

Masculine; the stress moves: *родника* (*rodniká*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*родник* is usually derived from *род-* 'birth', as in *родить* 'to give birth': water born from the earth. EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the line is hedged.
