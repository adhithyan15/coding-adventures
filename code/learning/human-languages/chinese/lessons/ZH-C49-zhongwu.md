## You'll want to know: 中午
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C49-DAY49-02]; assesses=[] -->

**中午** — *zhōngwǔ* — "noon".

> **中午我们吃饭** *zhōngwǔ wǒmen chī fàn* — We have lunch at noon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**中午** (*zhōngwǔ*) is built from 中 'middle' and 午 'noon', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trjuwng* and *nguX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
