# ح (baṛī he) — the letter that opens حافظ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C34-TTE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-JIM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ٹ and ج once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **حافظ** (*hāfiz*), "guardian".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-BARI-HE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ح** — *baṛī he*, "big *he*", the sound *h*.

It opens **حافظ** (*hāfiz*), the second half of **خدا حافظ**. Urdu already has an
*h*, **ہ**, which you write; this one came in with Arabic words and sounds the same.
It is the hook-and-bowl skeleton of **ج**, with **no dots**.

## Writing: ح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-BARI-HE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: sweep left through the pointed hooked head
- **2.** continue down and around the deep bowl without lifting — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 0.** The pen never leaves the paper.

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, Sīn, shīn, baṛī he, nūn, and nūn-e ġhunna, independent ح handwriting animation and Baṛī he instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).
