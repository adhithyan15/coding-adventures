## You'll want to know: നിവേദനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C426-NIVEDANAM426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**നിവേദനം** — *nivēdanaṁ* — "a memorandum; a petition".

> നാട്ടുകാർ മന്ത്രിക്ക് ഒരു നിവേദനം നൽകി.
>
> *nāṭṭukār mantrikkŭ oru nivēdanaṁ nalki.*
>
> The local people gave the minister a memorandum.

It is given, നൽകുക, to an official.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നിവേദനം** (*nivēdanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nivedana*, "announcing, making known" (Monier-Williams): a memorandum makes a need known.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
