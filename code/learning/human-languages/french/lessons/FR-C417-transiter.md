## You'll want to know: transiter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C417-DOUANE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**transiter** — "to change planes, to pass through".

> Ce vol est moins cher, mais il transite par deux aéroports.
>
> *This flight is cheaper, but it goes through two airports.*

*Le transit* — transit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*transiter* is built on *transit*, a learned borrowing from Latin *trānsitus* 'a passing across', from *trānsīre*, built on *īre* 'to go' (Lewis & Short).
