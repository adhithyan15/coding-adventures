Italian, chapter 349: Cause ed effetti.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'effetto — the effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the criticism, then the Italian for to reiterate.

You'll want to know: l'effetto.
l'effetto — "the effect".
La medicina ha fatto effetto dopo dieci minuti. The medicine took effect after ten minutes.
Fare effetto — to take effect. In effetti — in fact, indeed.

The word, taken apart.
effetto comes from Latin effectus "an effect, a result, a purpose" (EtymDB), from efficere "to bring about", built from ex "out" and facere "to do, to make".

Lesson 2 of 5.
provocare — to bring about, to cause; to provoke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to reiterate, then the Italian for the effect.

You'll want to know: provocare.
provocare — "to bring about, to cause; to provoke".
La pioggia ha provocato molti incidenti. The rain caused many accidents.
With bad events it means to cause; with a person, to provoke: non provocarmi!

The word, taken apart.
provocare is a learned borrowing from Latin prōvocāre "to call forth, to challenge" (Lewis & Short), built from prō "forth" and vocāre "to call".

Lesson 3 of 5.
causare — to cause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the effect, then the Italian for to bring about.

You'll want to know: causare.
causare — "to cause".
Il fumo può causare molte malattie. Smoking can cause many illnesses.
Neutral and frequent; the phrase a causa di means because of.

The word, taken apart.
causare is built on causa "cause", a learned borrowing from Latin causa "cause, reason, lawsuit" (EtymDB). The everyday word cosa "thing" is the same Latin word, inherited and worn down.

Lesson 4 of 5.
il fattore — the factor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to bring about, then the Italian for to cause.

You'll want to know: il fattore.
il fattore — "the factor".
Il prezzo è un fattore importante nella scelta. The price is an important factor in the choice.
Il fattore tempo — the time factor. In the country, il fattore was also the farm manager.

The word, taken apart.
fattore comes from Latin factor "maker, doer, performer" (EtymDB), built on facere "to make": one of the things that make a result.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la ragione — the reason; reason (the faculty).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to cause, then the Italian for the factor.

You'll want to know: la ragione.
la ragione — "the reason; reason (the faculty)".
Per quale ragione sei arrivato tardi? For what reason did you arrive late?
Avere ragione — to be right; dare ragione a qualcuno — to say someone is right.

The word, taken apart.
ragione is inherited from Latin ratiō, ratiōnis "a reckoning, an account, reason" (Lewis & Short), built on rērī "to think, to reckon".
