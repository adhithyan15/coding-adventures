## You'll want to know: स्पर्शः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C469-INDRIYA469-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्शः** — *sparśaḥ* — "touch".

> मातुः स्पर्शः कोमलः भवति।
>
> *mātuḥ sparśaḥ komalaḥ bhavati.*
>
> A mother's touch is gentle.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्शः** (*sparśaḥ*) comes from the root √spṛś, "to touch", the root of **स्पृशति**; MW gives "touch, the sense of touch" (MW).
