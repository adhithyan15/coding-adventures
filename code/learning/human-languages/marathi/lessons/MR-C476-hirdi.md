## You'll want to know: हिरडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C476-CEHRA476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हिरडी** — *hirḍī* — "a gum (of the mouth)".

> दंतवैद्याने माझ्या हिरड्या तपासल्या.
>
> *dantvaidyāne mājhyā hirḍyā tapāslyā.*
>
> The dentist checked my gums.

Feminine. Plural: हिरड्या, *hirḍyā*.

Usually plural: हिरड्या, the gums.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिरडी** (*hirḍī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
