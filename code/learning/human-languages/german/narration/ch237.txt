German, chapter 237: A role, Quiet, A thing, A shadow, A step.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
die Rolle — a role.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a row, then the German for religion.

You'll want to know: die Rolle.
die Rolle — "a role".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Rolle mean? ("A role".) Say it once more.
