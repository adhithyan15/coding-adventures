Punjabi, chapter 283: On the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਰਾਹ (rāh) — a route; the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to keep safe, then the Punjabi for to come out.

You'll want to know: ਰਾਹ.
ਰਾਹ — rāh — "a route; the way".
ਪਿੰਡ ਦਾ ਰਾਹ ਲੰਮਾ ਹੈ। piṇḍ dā rāh lammā hai. The way to the village is long.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਰਾਹ (rāh) is a loan, usually traced to Persian rāh, "road, way".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਸਾਥੀ (sāthī) — a companion; a comrade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to come out, then the Punjabi for a route.

You'll want to know: ਸਾਥੀ.
ਸਾਥੀ — sāthī — "a companion; a comrade".
ਸਫ਼ਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਮਨ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਥੀ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ। safar vich Aman merā sāthī hovegā. Aman will be my companion on the journey.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਾਥੀ (sāthī) is inherited from a reconstructed sārthin, "having part in a company"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sāthī, "comrade", under it (CDIAL 13366).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a route, then the Punjabi for a companion.

You'll want to know: ਪਹੁੰਚਣਾ.
ਪਹੁੰਚਣਾ — pahunchṇā — "to arrive; to reach".
ਅਸੀਂ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਪਿੰਡ ਪਹੁੰਚਾਂਗੇ। asīṁ shām nū̃ piṇḍ pahunchāṅge. We will reach the village in the evening.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਪਹੁੰਚਿਆ, maiṁ ghar pahunchiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਹੁੰਚਣਾ (pahunchṇā) goes back to Sanskrit prabhūta, "much, great"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pahũcṇā, "to arrive", under it (CDIAL 8716).
