## You'll want to know: वाचालः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C464-VACALA464-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वाचालः** — *vācālaḥ* — "talkative".

> वाचालः नापितः सदा जल्पति।
>
> *vācālaḥ nāpitaḥ sadā jalpati.*
>
> The talkative barber is always chattering.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाचालः** (*vācālaḥ*) is built on *vāc*, "speech, voice"; MW gives "talkative, chattering" and "boasting" (MW).
