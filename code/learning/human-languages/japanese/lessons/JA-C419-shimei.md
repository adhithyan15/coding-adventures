## You'll want to know: しめい

**しめい** — *shimei* — "one's full name".

> **しめいと じゅうしょを かいて ください。** — *shimei to jūsho o kaite kudasai* — "Please write your full name and address."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しめい** (*shimei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "family name" (Middle Chinese *dzyeX*) and *mei*, "name" (*mjieng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
