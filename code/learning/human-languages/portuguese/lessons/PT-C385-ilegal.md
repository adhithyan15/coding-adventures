## You'll want to know: ilegal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C385-BUROCRACIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ilegal** — "illegal".

> Estacionar em cima do passeio é ilegal.
>
> *Parking on the pavement is illegal.*

The opposite of *legal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ilegal* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *illēgālis* (EtymDB), built from *in-* "not" and *lēgālis* "legal", on *lēx* "law" (Lewis & Short).
