## You'll want to know: 通往
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C428-MOVEON428-01]; assesses=[] -->

**通往** — *tōngwǎng* — "to lead to".

> **这个门通往停车场** *zhège mén tōngwǎng tíngchēchǎng* — This door leads to the car park.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**通往** (*tōngwǎng*) is built from 通 'to go through' and 往 'to go towards', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thuwng* and *hjwangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
