Hindi, chapter 349: Denial and Confession.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उगलना (ugalnā) — to spit out; to blurt out (a secret, the truth).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for conclusive, then the Hindi for an accusation.

You'll want to know: उगलना.
उगलना — ugalnā — "to spit out; to blurt out (a secret, the truth)".
आख़िर चोर ने सच उगल दिया। ākhir cor ne sac ugal diyā. In the end the thief came out with the truth.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सच उगल दिया, usne sac ugal diyā.

The word, taken apart.
उगलना (ugalnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit udgirati, "spits out" (CDIAL 1960).

Lesson 2 of 5.
इनकार करना (inkār karnā) — to deny, to refuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an accusation, then the Hindi for to blurt out.

You'll want to know: इनकार करना.
इनकार करना — inkār karnā — "to deny, to refuse".
नेता ने हर सवाल का जवाब देने से इनकार किया। netā ne har savāl kā javāb dene se inkār kiyā. The politician refused to answer any question.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने इनकार किया, usne inkār kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
इनकार करना (inkār karnā) joins इनकार to करना, "to do". इनकार is usually traced to Arabic inkār, "denial", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पुष्टि करना (puṣṭi karnā) — to confirm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to blurt out, then the Hindi for to deny.

You'll want to know: पुष्टि करना.
पुष्टि करना — puṣṭi karnā — "to confirm".
डाक्टर ने ख़बर की पुष्टि की। ḍākṭar ne khabar kī puṣṭi kī. The doctor confirmed the news.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने ख़बर की पुष्टि की, usne khabar kī puṣṭi kī.

The word, taken apart.
पुष्टि करना (puṣṭi karnā) joins पुष्टि to करना, "to do". पुष्टि is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit puṣṭi, "growth, thriving" (Monier-Williams). The Hindi sense, a confirmation, is not among the senses Monier-Williams gives.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ग़लतफ़हमी (ġalatfahmī) — a misunderstanding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to confirm, then the Hindi for to admit.

You'll want to know: ग़लतफ़हमी.
ग़लतफ़हमी — ġalatfahmī — "a misunderstanding".
यह बहस एक ग़लतफ़हमी की वजह से हुई। yah bahas ek ġalatfahmī kī vajah se huī. This argument happened because of a misunderstanding.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ग़लतफ़हमी (ġalatfahmī) is usually traced to Persian ġalat-fahmī, "mistaken understanding". Both parts are Arabic words that Persian put together.
