Mandarin Chinese, chapter 252: Happy and Sad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
开心 (kāixīn) — happy; in high spirits.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a new character, then the Mandarin for to revise a piece of work.

You'll want to know: 开心.
开心 — kāixīn — "happy; in high spirits".
今天我很开心 jīntiān wǒ hěn kāixīn — I'm in a really good mood today.

The word, taken apart.
开心 (kāixīn) is built from 开 'to open' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khoj and sim (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): an open heart.

What you've built.
One more word for describing things.

gǎndào is built from gǎn and 到 'to reach', both inherited from Middle Chinese, komX and tawH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move'.
