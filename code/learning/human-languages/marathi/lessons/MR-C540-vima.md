## You'll want to know: विमा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C540-VIMA540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विमा** — *vimā* — "insurance".

> जर घराचा विमा असेल तर नुकसान भरून मिळतं.
>
> *jar gharācā vimā asel tar nuksān bharūn miḷtaṁ.*
>
> If the house is insured, the damage is made good.

Masculine.

विमा उतरवणे, to take out insurance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विमा** (*vimā*) is a loan from Persian *bīma*, "insurance, fear".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
