## HL-C354 — Hindi eating and drinking declare their practised present forms

Chapter 80 introduces **पीना** as half of the everyday eating-and-drinking pair,
then teaches **मैं चाय पीता हूँ** and **मैं खाना खाता हूँ** before asking the
learner to produce the drinking sentence. The declaration exposes only
**पीना**, so both masculine present forms appear as glossed-but-never-taught
candidates despite being the lesson's central worked contrast.

Declare **पीता** and **खाता** beside the two infinitives. This makes the complete
pair visible to reports and downstream study artifacts without inventing
feminine or plural forms the lesson does not teach.
