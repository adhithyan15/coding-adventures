## You'll want to know: a freguesia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C440-MORADA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a freguesia** — "the parish (local district)".

> A minha avó mora numa freguesia pequena.
>
> *My grandmother lives in a small parish.*

The smallest unit of local government in Portugal, run by *a junta de freguesia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*freguesia* is built on *freguês* 'parishioner, customer' with *-ia* (EtymDB); *freguês* goes back to Latin *fīlius ecclēsiae* 'son of the church'.
