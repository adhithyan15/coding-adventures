## You'll want to know: পর্যায়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C541-PROTIKSHA541-05]; assesses=[] -->

**পর্যায়** — *pôrjāy* — "a phase; a stage (of a process)".

> প্রথম পর্যায়ের কাজ শেষ হলে আমরা ছুটি নেব।
>
> *Prothom pôrjāyer kāj shesh hôle āmrā chhuṭi nebô.*
>
> When the first stage of the work is done, we'll take a holiday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পর্যায়** (*pôrjāy*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *paryāya*, which Monier-Williams gives as "a turning round" and as "succession, a turn"; the sense "a stage, a phase" is the modern language's own.
