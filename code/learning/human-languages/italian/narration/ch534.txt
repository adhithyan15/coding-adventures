Italian, chapter 534: Lavare i piatti.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la lavastoviglie — dishwasher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for napkin, then the Italian for tray.

You'll want to know: la lavastoviglie.
la lavastoviglie — "dishwasher".
Dopo cena carico la lavastoviglie. After dinner I load the dishwasher.
The same form in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
lavastoviglie is built from lava "washes", from Latin lavāre (Lewis & Short), and stoviglie "dishes", whose origin is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

lucidare — "to polish, to shine".
Ho lucidato le scarpe per il matrimonio. I polished my shoes for the wedding.

The word, taken apart.
lucidare is built on lucido "shiny", a learned borrowing from Latin lūcidus "bright" (Lewis & Short), from lūx "light".
