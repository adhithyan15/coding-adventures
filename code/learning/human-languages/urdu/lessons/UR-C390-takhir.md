## You'll want to know: تاخیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C390-RAVANA390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تاخیر** — *tākhīr* — "lateness; a delay (formal)".

> بارش کی وجہ سے گاڑی کی آمد میں تاخیر ہے۔
>
> *bārish kī vajah se gāṛī kī āmad meṅ tākhīr hai.*
>
> The train is arriving late because of the rain.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تاخیر** (*tākhīr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taʾkhīr*, "a putting off"; station announcements use it for a late train, where everyday talk says **دیر**.
