# ગાયબ (gāyab) — vanished

## You'll want to know: ગાયબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C333-PARI333-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાયબ** — *gāyab* — "vanished".

> અચાનક પરી ગાયબ થઈ ગઈ.
>
> *acānak parī gāyab thaī gaī.*
>
> Suddenly the fairy vanished.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાયબ** (*gāyab*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ġāʾib*, "absent, hidden". A thing or a person **ગાયબ થાય છે**, disappears all at once.
