## You'll want to know: કુશળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C489-KUSHAL489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કુશળ** — *kuśaḷ* — "faring well; safe".

> ઘરમાં બધા કુશળ છે કે નહીં?
>
> *gharmā̃ badhā kuśaḷ che ke nahī̃?*
>
> Is everyone at home well?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કુશળ** (*kuśaḷ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kuśala*, "right, proper" and "well, healthy", as Monier-Williams records it.
