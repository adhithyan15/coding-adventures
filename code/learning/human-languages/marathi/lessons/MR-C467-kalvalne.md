## You'll want to know: कळवळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C467-YATANA467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कळवळणे** — *kaḷvaḷṇe* — "to ache with pity".

> भुकेल्या मुलाला पाहून आईचा जीव कळवळला.
>
> *bhukelyā mulālā pāhūn āīcā jīv kaḷvaḷlā.*
>
> Seeing the hungry child, the mother's heart was wrung.

Past (he …): तो कळवळला, *to kaḷvaḷlā*.

Usually of the जीव or the मन: pity that hurts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कळवळणे** (*kaḷvaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled syllable; its origin is not settled.
