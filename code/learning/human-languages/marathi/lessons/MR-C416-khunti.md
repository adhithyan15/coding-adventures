## You'll want to know: खुंटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C416-HAMAL416-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खुंटी** — *khuṇṭī* — "a peg; a hook".

> दाराच्या मागे खुंटीवर पिशवी टांगली आहे.
>
> *dārācyā māge khuṇṭīvar piśvī ṭāṅglī āhe.*
>
> A bag is hanging on a peg behind the door.

Feminine.

A peg in the wall for clothes and bags; खुंटीवर, on the peg.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खुंटी** (*khuṇṭī*) is a Marathi word for a wall peg; its origin is not settled.
