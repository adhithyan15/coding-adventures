## You'll want to know: باور داشتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C361-MOVAFEQAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**باور داشتن** (*bâvar dâshtan*) — "to believe in, to have faith in".

> او به خودش باور دارد.
>
> *u be khodash bâvar dârad.*
>
> She believes in herself.

*Bâvar* "belief" + *dâshtan* "to have" (present stem *dâr-*). Takes *be*: *be chizi bâvar dâshtan*. Compare *bâvar kardan*, to believe a statement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**باور داشتن** is native Persian, built from *bâvar* "belief", usually traced to Middle Persian *wābar*, and *dâshtan* "to have", the inherited verb.
