Persian, chapter 308: News and reports.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گزارش (gozâresh) — a report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the eve, then the Persian for roughly.

You'll want to know: گزارش.
گزارش (gozâresh) — "a report".
گزارش (gozâresh) او درباره مدرسه های روستا بود. Gozâresh-e u darbâre-ye madrese-hâ-ye rustâ bud. Her report was about the village schools.
Plural gozâresh-hâ. Built on gozâr-, the stem of gozârdan "to set out, to render".

The word, taken apart.
gozâresh is a native Persian noun: the stem gozâr- "to set out, to render" with the noun suffix -esh. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
گزارش دادن (gozâresh dâdan) — to report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for roughly, then the Persian for a report.

You'll want to know: گزارش دادن.
گزارش دادن (gozâresh dâdan) — "to report".
راننده تصادف را به پلیس گزارش داد. Rânande tasâdof râ be polis gozâresh dâd. The driver reported the crash to the police.
Present stem dah-: gozâresh midahad. The thing reported takes râ; the person told takes be.

The word, taken apart.
gozâresh dâdan joins the native noun gozâresh "a report" with dâdan "to give", which EtymDB takes back to the Indo-European root deh₃-: to give a report.

گزارشگر (gozâreshgar) — "a reporter, a correspondent".
گزارشگر (gozâreshgar) از جلوی بیمارستان صحبت میکرد. Gozâreshgar az jelo-ye bimârestân sohbat mikard. The reporter was speaking from outside the hospital.
-gar makes a person who does something, as in kârgar "worker".

The word, taken apart.
gozâreshgar adds the native suffix -gar "one who does" to gozâresh "a report". EtymDB lists no entry.

تیتر (titr) روزنامه امروز خیلی بزرگ بود. Titr-e ruznâme-ye emruz kheyli bozorg bud. Today's newspaper headline was very big.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اخبار (akhbâr) — the news (programme).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a reporter, then the Persian for a headline.

You'll want to know: اخبار.
اخبار (akhbâr) — "the news (programme)".
پدرم هر شب ساعت نه اخبار را تماشا میکند. Pedaram har shab sâ'at-e noh akhbâr râ tamâshâ mikonad. My father watches the news every night at nine.
The Arabic broken plural of khabar "a piece of news". Akhbâr is the news in general or the news programme.

The word, taken apart.
akhbâr is borrowed from Arabic akhbār (EtymDB), the broken plural of khabar "news", which Persian also has.
