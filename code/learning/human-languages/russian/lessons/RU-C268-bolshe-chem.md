# быстрее, чем; лучше, хуже — comparing two things

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C66-TEMP-04, RU-LEX-C67-WORTH-03, RU-LEX-C67-WORTH-04, RU-GRAMMAR-LUCHSHE-INFINITIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "fast", "expensive" and "cheap". (*Býstryy. Dorogóy.
Deshyóvyy.*) Then "you'd better take a taxi". (*Tebé lúchshe vzyat
taksí.*) That *лучше* was already a comparison: "better".

## Grammar Lens: -ее, and чем
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-EE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-CHEM-THAN-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C126-QUAL126-04, RU-LEX-C66-TEMP-03, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-02, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-01, RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01] -->

Most adjectives compare by swapping their ending for **-ее**: *быстрый*,
**быстрее** (*bystréye*); *тёплый*, **теплее** (*tepléye*); *интересный*,
**интереснее** (*interésneye*). The form does not change for gender or
number.

"Than" is **чем**, with a comma in front:

> **Поезд быстрее, чем автобус.** — *póyezd bystréye, chem avtóbus* — The
> train is faster than the bus.

In a short adjective the stress usually moves onto *-ее*: *bystréye*.
In a longer one it usually stays where it was: *interésneye*.

## Grammar Lens: the ones that change shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-IRREGULAR-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C65-SIZE-01, RU-LEX-C65-SIZE-02, RU-LEX-C64-COLOUR2-03, RU-LEX-C64-COLOUR2-04, RU-LEX-C67-WORTH-03, RU-LEX-C67-WORTH-04, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-03, RU-GRAMMAR-PO-MOEMU-01] -->

A few common ones are learned whole: *большой*, **больше** (*bólshe*),
bigger, more; *маленький*, **меньше** (*ménshe*), smaller, less;
*хороший*, **лучше** (*lúchshe*), better; *плохой*, **хуже** (*khúzhe*),
worse; *дорогой*, **дороже** (*dorózhe*); *дешёвый*, **дешевле**
(*deshévle*).

> **Самолёт быстрее, но поезд дешевле.** — *samolyót bystréye, no póyezd
> deshévle* — The plane is faster, but the train is cheaper.
> **По-моему, поезд лучше.** — *po-móyemu, póyezd lúchshe* — In my view the
> train is better.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-ETYMON-CHTO-KWID, RU-LEX-CHTO-01] -->

**чем** is a form of **что**, the "by what" form, on the question-root
PIE *\*kʷo-* behind *что*, *кто* and English *what*. EtymDB 2.1 lists
*чем* without taking it apart, so the link to *что* is the plain reading
of the forms.
