# Two ways to ask, and how to tell them apart

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-QUESTION-KAA, MR-SOUND-QUESTION-RISE] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Marathi now gives you two kinds of question. One ends in **का** and
is answered **हो** or **नाही**. The other carries a **क-** word and is answered
with information.
