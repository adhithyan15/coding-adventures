## You'll want to know: じむしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C229-OFFICE229-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じむしょ** — *jimusho* — "an office".

> **ははは えきの ちかくの じむしょで はたらきます。** — *haha wa eki no chikaku no jimusho de hatarakimasu* — "My mother works in an office near the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じむしょ** (*jimusho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "affair" (Middle Chinese *dzriH*), *mu*, "duty" (*mjuH*) and *sho*, "place" (*srjoX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
