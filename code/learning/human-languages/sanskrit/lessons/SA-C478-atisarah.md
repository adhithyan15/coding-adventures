## You'll want to know: अतिसारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C478-VEDANA478-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिसारः** — *atisāraḥ* — "dysentery".

> मलिनेन जलेन अतिसारः भवति।
>
> *malinena jalena atisāraḥ bhavati.*
>
> Dirty water causes dysentery.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिसारः** (*atisāraḥ*) is built from *ati*, "beyond", and the root √sṛ, "to flow": a flowing over; MW gives "purging, dysentery" (MW).
