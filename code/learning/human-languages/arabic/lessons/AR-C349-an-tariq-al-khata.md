## You'll want to know: عن طريق الخطأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C349-MARRA349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عن طريق الخطأ** — *ʿan ṭarīq al-khaṭaʾ* — "by mistake".

> أخذت حقيبة أخي عن طريق الخطأ.
>
> *akhadhtu ḥaqībat akhī ʿan ṭarīq al-khaṭaʾ.*
>
> I took my brother’s bag by mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عن طريق الخطأ** (*ʿan ṭarīq al-khaṭaʾ*) is "by way of the mistake": *khaṭaʾ* is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-ʾ*, "to miss the mark" (Lane), the root of the taught *khaṭaʾ*.
