# అధ్యయనం (adhyayanaṁ) — study; a study

## You'll want to know: అధ్యయనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C415-VIDYA415-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అధ్యయనం** — *adhyayanaṁ* — "study; a study".

> ఆ అధ్యయనంలో కొత్త విషయాలు తెలిశాయి.
>
> *ā adhyayanaṁlō kotta viṣayālu teliśāyi.*
>
> New facts came to light in that study.

అధ్యయనం చేయు, to study a subject closely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అధ్యయనం** (*adhyayanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *adhyayana*, "reading, studying" (Monier-Williams).
