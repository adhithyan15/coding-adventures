5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आराखडा (ārākhḍā) — an outline; a layout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for valid, then the Marathi for an identity card.

You'll want to know: आराखडा.
आराखडा — ārākhḍā — "an outline; a layout".
अभियंत्याने नवीन इमारतीचा आराखडा बनवला. abhiyantyāne navīn imārtīcā ārākhḍā banavlā. The engineer made the layout of the new building.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
आराखडा (ārākhḍā) is a Marathi word for an outline plan; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
नियोजन करणे (niyojan karṇe) — to plan ahead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an outline, then the Marathi for a draft.

You'll want to know: नियोजन करणे.
नियोजन करणे — niyojan karṇe — "to plan ahead".
सहलीसाठी आम्ही आधीच सगळ्याचं नियोजन केलं. sahlīsāṭhī āmhī ādhīc sagḷyācaṁ niyojan kelaṁ. We planned everything for the trip in advance.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नियोजन केलं, tyāne niyojan kelaṁ.
What is planned takes -चं: खर्चाचं नियोजन करणे (niyojan karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
नियोजन करणे (niyojan karṇe) is the tatsama नियोजन, from Sanskrit niyojana, "fastening, setting to a task" (Monier-Williams), with करणे.

Lesson 4 of 5.
लांबणीवर टाकणे (lāmbṇīvar ṭākṇe) — to postpone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a draft, then the Marathi for to plan ahead.

You'll want to know: लांबणीवर टाकणे.
लांबणीवर टाकणे — lāmbṇīvar ṭākṇe — "to postpone".
पावसामुळे पंचांनी सामना लांबणीवर टाकला. pāvsāmuḷe pañcānnī sāmnā lāmbṇīvar ṭāklā. Because of the rain the umpires postponed the match.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बैठक लांबणीवर टाकली, tyāne baiṭhak lāmbṇīvar ṭāklī.
Literally to put on the long side.

The word, taken apart.
लांबणीवर टाकणे (lāmbṇīvar ṭākṇe) is built on लांबणी, "lengthening", from लांब, "long", inherited from Sanskrit lamba, "hanging down, long" (CDIAL 10951), with टाकणे, "to put".

Lesson 5 of 5.
तडीस नेणे (taḍīs neṇe) — to see through; to complete.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to plan ahead, then the Marathi for to postpone.

You'll want to know: तडीस नेणे.
तडीस नेणे — taḍīs neṇe — "to see through; to complete".
पैसे कमी होते, तरी त्याने योजना तडीस नेली. paise kamī hote, tarī tyāne yojnā taḍīs nelī. Money was short, but he saw the plan through.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने काम तडीस नेलं, tyāne kām taḍīs nelaṁ.
Literally to take it to the shore: तड is a bank.

The word, taken apart.
तडीस नेणे (taḍīs neṇe) is तडीस, "to the bank", built on तड, which is inherited from Sanskrit taṭa, "slope, shore" (CDIAL 5629), with नेणे, "to take".
