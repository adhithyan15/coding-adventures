# كان يكتب (kāna yaktubu) — the past that is still running

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C283-MADI283-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PAST-PERSONS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was at the office", then "I wrote". (*Kuntu fī al-maktab*,
*katabtu*.)

[PAUSE 1s] *Katabtu* has an edge: the writing happened, and it is over. A
story also needs a past with no edge on it.

## Grammar Lens: kāna in front of the present
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-KANA-IMPERFECT-01]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PRESENT-PERSONS-01, AR-LEX-C58-VERBS58-02, AR-LEX-C73-SCHOOL73-05] -->

Put *kāna* in its past form, then the present of the verb you mean, both for
the same person:

> *kuntu* + *aktubu* → **كنت أكتب** — *kuntu aktubu* — I was writing; I used to
> write

So *kānat taqraʾu*, she was reading; *kunnā nadhhabu*, we used to go. It
answers two English shapes. "Was ‑ing": the action was under way. "Used
to": it happened again and again.

> **كنت أمشي إلى المدرسة كل يوم.** — *kuntu amshī ilā al-madrasa kulla yawm*
> — I used to walk to school every day.

*Mā* goes in front of *kāna*: *mā kuntu aʿrifu*, I didn't know.

## Grammar Lens: the scene, and the event
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-SCENE-EVENT-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C40-HINAMA-01, AR-LEX-C255-ACT255-03, AR-CONCEPT-C35-SADIQ-01] -->

A told story splits its verbs in two. *Kāna* with the present paints the
**scene**: what was going on. The plain past drops the **events** into it:
what happened.

> **كنت أقرأ كتابا حينما اتصل صديقي.** — *kuntu aqraʾu kitāban ḥīnamā
> ittaṣala ṣadīqī* — I was reading a book when my friend phoned.

The reading is scene, still running when the call cuts into it. The call is
an event, over in a moment, and *ḥīnamā* is the joint between them.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-KANA-IMPERFECT-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C283-SCENE-EVENT-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PAST-PERSONS-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] How do you build "I was writing"? (*Kuntu* + *aktubu*:
***kuntu aktubu***.) In the phone sentence, which verb is the scene?
(***Kuntu aqraʾu***, still running.) Which is the event? (***Ittaṣala***,
over in a moment.) Do both verbs agree with the person? (**Yes**: *kunnā
naktubu*.)
