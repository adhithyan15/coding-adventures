Gujarati, chapter 360: Loyalty and Betrayal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઈમાન (īmān) — integrity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for conceit, then the Gujarati for to bow.

You'll want to know: ઈમાન.
ઈમાન — īmān — "integrity".
સાચો માણસ પૈસા માટે પોતાનું ઈમાન વેચતો નથી. sāco māṇas paisā māṭe potānũ īmān vecto nathī. An honest person does not sell his integrity for money.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઈમાન (īmān) is usually traced to Arabic īmān, "faith", through Persian; in Gujarati it is the faith one keeps with others.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ફસાવવું (phasāvvũ) — to entrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for loyal, then the Gujarati for a betrayal.

You'll want to know: ફસાવવું.
ફસાવવું — phasāvvũ — "to entrap".
તે નકલી માલ વેચીને ગ્રાહકોને ફસાવે છે. te naklī māl vecīne grāhkone phasāve che. He traps customers by selling them fake goods.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે એક ગ્રાહકને ફસાવ્યો, teṇe ek grāhakne phasāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
ફસાવવું (phasāvvũ) is the causative partner of ફસાવું (phasāvũ), "to get stuck"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to ensnare", under Sanskrit spāśáyati, "causes to be bound" (CDIAL 13814).
