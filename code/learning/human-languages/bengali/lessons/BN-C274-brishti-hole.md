# বৃষ্টি হলে … (brishṭi hôle …) — the -le form, and otherwise

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01, BN-LEX-C159-KARON159-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "If there's a storm tomorrow, we won't go". (*jôdi kāl jhôṛ hôy, tāhôle āmrā jābô nā*.) Then **নাহলে**, "otherwise".

## You'll want to know: the -le form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C274-LE-FORM-01]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01, BN-LEX-C255-JINIS255-02, BN-LEX-C45-HEALTH45-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

Bengali can say "if" with no **যদি**: the verb takes **-লে**, on the stem of the past, and stays the same for every person.

> **বৃষ্টি হলে আমরা বাড়িতে থাকব।**
>
> *brishṭi hôle āmrā bāṛite thākbô.* — "If it rains, we'll stay at home."

**বৃষ্টি** (*brishṭi*) is "rain", and **বৃষ্টি হওয়া** is "to rain": "rain happens".

**হওয়া** → **হলে**, **আসা** → **এলে**, **যাওয়া** → **গেলে**, **খাওয়া** → **খেলে**, **করা** → **করলে**, **কেনা** → **কিনলে**: **তুমি এলে আমি খুশি হব** (*tumi ele āmi khushi hôbô*), "if you come, I'll be happy". The person is in the noun before it, never in the ending.

## You'll want to know: otherwise
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C274-NAHOLE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C159-KARON159-05, BN-LEX-C73-ADJ73-04, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-MISSED-01] -->

**নাহলে**, "otherwise", which you know, is **না** with this same **হলে**: "if it is not so".

> **তাড়াতাড়ি যেতে হবে, নাহলে ট্রেন ছেড়ে যাবে।**
>
> *tāṛātāṛi jete hôbe, nāhôle ṭren chheṛe jābe.* — "We have to go quickly, otherwise the train will leave."
