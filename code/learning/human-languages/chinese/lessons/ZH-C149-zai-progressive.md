# 你在做什么 — 在 before a verb

## Grammar Lens: 在 before a place, 在 before a verb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-ZAI-PROGRESSIVE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C85-ZAI-01, ZH-LEX-NE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-BU-MEI-01, ZH-LEX-C149-ZHENGZAI-01] -->

> **在 before a place says where. 在 before a verb says what is going on right now.**

> **他在家** *tā zài jiā* — He's at home.
> **他在看书** *tā zài kàn shū* — He's reading.

正在 says it more firmly, in the very middle of it: 我正在吃饭. 呢 at the end can round off either one:

> **他在看书呢** *tā zài kàn shū ne* — He's busy reading.

The negative is 没在, since 没 reports what is not going on:

> **我没在看书** *wǒ méi zài kàn shū* — I'm not reading.

> **你在做什么** *nǐ zài zuò shénme* — What are you doing?
