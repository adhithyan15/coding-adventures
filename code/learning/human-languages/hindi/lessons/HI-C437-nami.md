## You'll want to know: नमी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C437-MANSUN437-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नमी** — *namī* — "damp; moisture".

> बरसात में दीवारों पर नमी आ जाती है।
>
> *barsāt meṁ dīvāroṁ par namī ā jātī hai.*
>
> In the rainy season the walls get damp.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नमी** (*namī*) is built on *nam*, "damp, moist", usually traced to Persian *nam*, with the ending -ī that makes a noun.
