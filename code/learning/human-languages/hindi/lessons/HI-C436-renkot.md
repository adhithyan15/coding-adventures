## You'll want to know: रेनकोट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C436-BUND436-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रेनकोट** — *renkoṭ* — "a raincoat".

> बारिश में मैं रेनकोट पहनता हूँ।
>
> *bāriś meṁ maiṁ renkoṭ pahantā hūṁ.*
>
> I wear a raincoat in the rain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेनकोट** (*renkoṭ*) is borrowed from English *raincoat*.
