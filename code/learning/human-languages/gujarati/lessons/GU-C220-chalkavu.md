# છલકાવું (chalkāvũ) — to overflow, to brim over

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C219-THUNDER219-05, GU-LEX-C220-RIVER220-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for unseasonal rain, then the Gujarati for to flow.

## You'll want to know: છલકાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C220-RIVER220-02]; assesses=[] -->

**છલકાવું** — *chalkāvũ* — "to overflow, to brim over".

> **કાલે તળાવ છલકાશે.** — *kāle taḷāv chalkāśe* — "The lake will overflow tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છલકાવું** (*chalkāvũ*) comes from *\*chala*, "splash", an imitative reconstructed form; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, glossed "to be spilt" (CDIAL 5002).
