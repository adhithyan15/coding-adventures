## You'll want to know: la trama
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C565-ESCENA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la trama** — "the plot".

> La trama de la novela es muy complicada.
>
> *The plot of the novel is very complicated.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trama* is inherited from Latin *trāma* 'the weft, the threads woven across' (EtymDB). A plot is the threads of a story woven together.
