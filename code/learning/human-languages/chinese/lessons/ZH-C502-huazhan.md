## You'll want to know: huàzhǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C502-PAINT502-05]; assesses=[] -->

*huàzhǎn* — "art exhibition".

> *xīngqīliù wǒmen qù kàn huàzhǎn* — We're going to an art exhibition on Saturday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huàzhǎn* is built from 画 'a painting' and *zhǎn* 'to unfold', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hweaH* and *trjenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to spread out'.
