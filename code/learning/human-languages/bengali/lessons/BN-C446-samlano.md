## You'll want to know: সামলানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C446-SANTVONA446-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সামলানো** — *sāmlāno* — "to cope with; to hold back (feelings)".

> কান্না সামলে মীরা হাসল।
>
> *kānnā sāmle mīrā hāslô.*
>
> Holding back her tears, Mira smiled.

Past (he/she …): তিনি ঝামেলাটা সামলালেন, *tini jhāmelāṭā sāmlālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সামলানো** (*sāmlāno*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as bearing, holding and taking care of, under Sanskrit *saṁbharati*, "brings together" (CDIAL 12961).
