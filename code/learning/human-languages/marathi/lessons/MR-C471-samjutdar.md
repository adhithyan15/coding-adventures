## You'll want to know: समजूतदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C471-SAHANA471-04]; assesses=[] -->

**समजूतदार** — *samjūtdār* — "understanding; mature".

> माझा मोठा भाऊ खूप समजूतदार आहे.
>
> *mājhā moṭhā bhāū khūp samjūtdār āhe.*
>
> My big brother is very understanding.

समजूत, understanding, from समजणे, + -दार, having.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समजूतदार** (*samjūtdār*) is a Marathi derivation: समजूत, "understanding", the noun of समजणे, with *-dār*, "having", an ending usually traced to Persian *-dār*.
