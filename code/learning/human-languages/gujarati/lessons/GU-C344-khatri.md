## You'll want to know: ખાતરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C344-SABITI344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાતરી** — *khātrī* — "an assurance; being sure".

> મને ખાતરી છે કે તે સમયસર આવશે.
>
> *mane khātrī che ke te samaysar āvśe.*
>
> I am sure he will come on time.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાતરી** (*khātrī*) is usually traced to Persian *khātir*, "the mind, regard", a word of Arabic origin, with the Gujarati ending *-ī*; "being sure" is the Gujarati sense.
