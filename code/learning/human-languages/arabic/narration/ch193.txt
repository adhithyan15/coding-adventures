Arabic, chapter 193: To take off, To try, To measure, To bargain, To go shopping.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خلع (khalaʿa) — to take off (clothes).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to receive, then the Arabic for to cost.

You'll want to know: خلع.
خلع — khalaʿa — "to take off (clothes)".

The word, taken apart.
خلع (khalaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-l-ʿ, "to pull off" (Lane). Khalaʿa is the plain verb, "he took off".
