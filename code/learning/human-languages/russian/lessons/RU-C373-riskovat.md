## You'll want to know: рисковать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C373-OSTOROZHNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**рисковать** (*riskovát*) — "to take a risk, to risk".

> Я не хочу рисковать своими деньгами.
>
> *Ya ne khochú riskovát svoími dengámi.*
>
> I do not want to risk my money.

Imperfective; the present is *рискую, рискуешь*; perfective *рискнуть*. *Рисковать* + instrumental — to risk something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**рисковать** "to risk" (EtymDB) is built on *риск*, which is usually traced, through French *risque*, to Italian *risco*.
