You'll want to know: проблема.
проблема (probléma) — "a problem".

The word, taken apart.
проблема (probléma) — from Peter the Great's time; it came through Polish problema or older German Problema from Latin problēma, Greek próblēma, "something put forward" (Vasmer).

Lesson 2 of 5.
предложение (predlozhéniye) — a sentence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an idea, then the Russian for a problem.

You'll want to know: предложение.
предложение (predlozhéniye) — "a sentence".

The word, taken apart.
предложение (predlozhéniye) is пред-, "before", with ложение, "laying", from -ложить, "to lay": a sentence is a thing laid before the listener; it also means "an offer". The root is that of лежать, "to lie", a cousin of English lie (Vasmer).

The word, taken apart.
музыка (múzyka) — Old Russian said мусикия; the form музыка (múzyka) came through Ukrainian and Polish muzyka from Latin mūsica, Greek mousikḗ, "the art of the Muses" (Vasmer).
