## You'll want to know: a espada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C514-RINGUE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a espada** — "sword".

> No museu há uma espada muito antiga.
>
> *In the museum there is a very old sword.*

*Espadas* is also a suit in a pack of cards: spades.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*espada* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *espada* (EtymDB), from Latin *spatha* "a broad two-edged sword" (Lewis & Short), which was taken from Greek *spáthē* "a broad blade".
