## You'll want to know: ручей
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C460-RUCHEY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ручей** (*ruchéy*) — "a stream, a brook".

> В лесу мы пили воду из ручья.
>
> *V lesú my píli vódu iz ruchyá.*
>
> In the forest we drank water from a stream.

Masculine; the е drops out: *ручья*, *ручьи*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ручей* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *ručajĭ* 'a loudly flowing stream' (EtymDB), which EtymDB builds on a verb *ručati* 'to roar, to hum'.
