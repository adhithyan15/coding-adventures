## You'll want to know: बेहद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C387-BEHAD387-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बेहद** — *behad* — "extremely, beyond measure".

> अगर आज भी बेहद गर्मी रही तो स्कूल बंद रहेंगे।
>
> *agar āj bhī behad garmī rahī to skūl band raheṅge.*
>
> If it is extremely hot again today, the schools will stay shut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेहद** (*behad*) is usually traced to Persian *behadd*, "without limit". It joins Persian *be-*, "without", to Arabic *ḥadd*, "a limit".
