## You'll want to know: انتقاد کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C365-QEZAVAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقاد کردن** (*enteqâd kardan*) — "to criticise".

> مردم از دولت انتقاد کردند.
>
> *mardom az dowlat enteqâd kardand.*
>
> People criticised the government.

Takes *az*: *az kasi enteqâd kardan*. Stem *kon-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقاد کردن** joins *enteqâd*, from Arabic *intiqād* "criticism" (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian verb *kardan* "to do".
