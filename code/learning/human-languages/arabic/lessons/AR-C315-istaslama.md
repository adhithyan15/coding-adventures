## You'll want to know: استسلم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C315-NASR315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استسلم** — *istaslama* — "to surrender, to give in".

> استسلم الجنود بعد يومين.
>
> *istaslama al-junūd baʿda yawmayn.*
>
> The soldiers surrendered after two days.

Present: يستسلم, *yastaslim*.

لا تستسلم! Do not give up!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استسلم** (*istaslama*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-l-m* (Lane), "to be safe, sound", the root of *salām*, peace. It is Form X, "to yield oneself up".
