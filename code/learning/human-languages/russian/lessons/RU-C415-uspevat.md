## You'll want to know: успевать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C415-OTPRAVLYATSYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**успевать** (*uspevát*) — "to make it (in time), to have time (to)".

> Мы бежим и всё-таки успеваем на поезд.
>
> *My bezhím i vsyo-taki uspeváyem na póyezd.*
>
> We run and still make the train.

Imperfective; perfective *успеть* (*успею, успеешь*). *Успеть на поезд* — to catch the train.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*успевать* is built from *у-* and an old verb *спеть* 'to get on, to succeed', the root also found in *спешить* 'to hurry'. EtymDB lists *успевать* without a source; the link with *спешить* is the usual one.
