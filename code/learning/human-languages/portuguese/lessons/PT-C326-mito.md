## You'll want to know: o mito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C326-LENDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o mito** — "the myth".

> É um mito: a água fria não faz mal.
>
> *It is a myth: cold water does no harm.*

Also a false belief, as here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mito* comes from Greek *mŷthos* "a word, a speech, a story" (EtymDB).
