## You'll want to know: ગમગીન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C455-GAMGIN455-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગમગીન** — *gamgīn* — "gloomy; sorrowful".

> મિત્ર ગયા પછી અરુણ આખો દિવસ ગમગીન રહ્યો.
>
> *mitra gayā pachī Aruṇ ākho divas gamgīn rahyo.*
>
> After his friend left, Arun stayed gloomy all day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગમગીન** (*gamgīn*) is usually traced to Persian *ġamgīn*, "sorrowful", built on *ġam*, "grief".
