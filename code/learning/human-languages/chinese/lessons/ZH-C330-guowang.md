## You'll want to know: 国王
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C330-KING330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**国王** — *guówáng* — "king".

> **传说里有一个很老的国王** *chuánshuō lǐ yǒu yí ge hěn lǎo de guówáng* — In the legend there is a very old king.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**国王** (*guówáng*) is built from 国 'a state' and 王 'a ruler', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwok* and *hjwang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'great' and 'a ruler'.
