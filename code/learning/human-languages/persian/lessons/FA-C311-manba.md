## You'll want to know: منبع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C311-MANBA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**منبع** (*manba'*) — "a source".

> این خبر از یک منبع مطمئن بود.
>
> *In khabar az yek manba'-e motma'en bud.*
>
> This news came from a reliable source.

Broken plural *manâbe'*. Its first meaning is a spring of water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manba'* is borrowed from Arabic *manba'*, "spring, source" (EtymDB).
