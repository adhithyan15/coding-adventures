## You'll want to know: ふうけい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C404-FUUKEI404-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ふうけい** — *fūkei* — "scenery; a landscape".

> **いなかの ふうけいは うつくしいです。** — *inaka no fūkei wa utsukushii desu* — "The countryside scenery is beautiful."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふうけい** (*fūkei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fū*, "style" (Middle Chinese *pjuwng*) and *kei*, "scenery" (*kjaengX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
