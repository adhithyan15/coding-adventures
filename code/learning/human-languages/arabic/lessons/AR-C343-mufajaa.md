## You'll want to know: مفاجأة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C343-MUGHAMARA343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مفاجأة** — *mufājaʾa* — "a surprise".

> كانت الهدية مفاجأة جميلة.
>
> *kānat al-hadiyya mufājaʾa jamīla.*
>
> The present was a lovely surprise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مفاجأة** (*mufājaʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-j-ʾ* (Lane), "to come upon unawares", the root of *fajʾatan*, suddenly. It is the verbal noun of Form III.

## What you've built
