## You'll want to know: occidentale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C443-CARDINALI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**occidentale** — "western".

> Il sole tramonta sul lato occidentale.
>
> *The sun sets on the western side.*

Built on *occidente*, where the sun goes down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*occidentale* is a learned borrowing from Latin *occidentālis* (EtymDB), from *occidēns* 'setting (sun)', built on *cadere* 'to fall'.
