Marathi, chapter 289: Crimes and Witnesses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
साक्षीदार (sākṣīdār) — a witness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to sink, then the Marathi for minor.

You'll want to know: साक्षीदार.
साक्षीदार — sākṣīdār — "a witness".
त्या साक्षीदाराने सगळं पाहिलं असतं तर त्याने पोलिसांना सांगितलं असतं. tyā sākṣīdārāne sagḷaṁ pāhilaṁ astaṁ tar tyāne polisānnā sāṅgitlaṁ astaṁ. If that witness had seen everything, he would have told the police.
Masculine.
साक्षीदाराने, by the witness; a past form with असतं says what would have happened.

The word, taken apart.
साक्षीदार (sākṣīdār) is built from Sanskrit sākṣin, 'an eyewitness' (Monier-Williams), and the Persian suffix -dār, 'one who holds': the one who holds the testimony.

Lesson 2 of 5.
गुन्हा (gunhā) — a crime; an offence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for minor, then the Marathi for a witness.

You'll want to know: गुन्हा.
गुन्हा — gunhā — "a crime; an offence".
खोटी सही करणं हा मोठा गुन्हा आहे. khoṭī sahī karṇaṁ hā moṭhā gunhā āhe. Forging a signature is a serious crime.
Masculine.
The oblique is गुन्ह्या-: गुन्ह्यात, in the crime.

The word, taken apart.
गुन्हा (gunhā) is a loan from Persian gunāh, 'a sin, a fault'; in Marathi it became the everyday and the legal word for an offence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: दरोडा.
दरोडा — daroḍā — "a robbery; a gang raid".
रात्री चार माणसांनी दुकानावर दरोडा टाकला. rātrī cār māṇsānnī dukānāvar daroḍā ṭāklā. At night four men raided the shop.
Masculine.
दरोडा (daroḍā) पडणे, a robbery takes place; दरोडा (daroḍā) टाकणे, to carry one out.

The word, taken apart.
दरोडा (daroḍā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi daroḍā, 'an attack by a band of robbers', under daḍavaḍa, 'an onrush' (CDIAL 6127).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पाकीटमार (pākīṭmār) — a pickpocket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a crime, then the Marathi for a robbery.

You'll want to know: पाकीटमार.
पाकीटमार — pākīṭmār — "a pickpocket".
गर्दीत एका पाकीटमाराने माझं पाकीट मारलं. gardīt ekā pākīṭmārāne mājhaṁ pākīṭ mārlaṁ. In the crowd a pickpocket stole my wallet.
Masculine.
Built from पाकीट, a wallet, and मार-, from मारणे: पाकीट मारणे, to pick a pocket.

The word, taken apart.
पाकीटमार (pākīṭmār) is a Marathi compound built from pākīṭ, 'a wallet', a loan from English packet, and mār-, the stem of mārṇe, 'to strike' (CDIAL 10066).

Lesson 5 of 5.
फसवणे (phasavṇe) — to cheat; to deceive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a robbery, then the Marathi for a pickpocket.

You'll want to know: फसवणे.
फसवणे — phasavṇe — "to cheat; to deceive".
त्या दुकानदाराने आम्हाला फसवलं. tyā dukāndārāne āmhālā phasavlaṁ. That shopkeeper cheated us.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने फसवलं, tyāne phasavlaṁ.
The one cheated takes -ला: लोकांना फसवणे (phasavṇe), to cheat people.

The word, taken apart.
फसवणे (phasavṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi phasavṇe, 'to deceive', under Sanskrit spāśayati, 'causes to be bound or stopped' (CDIAL 13814). To cheat is to snare.
