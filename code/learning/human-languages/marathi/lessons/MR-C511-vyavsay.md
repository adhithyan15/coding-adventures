## You'll want to know: व्यवसाय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C511-DHANDA511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवसाय** — *vyavsāy* — "a business; a profession".

> माझ्या काकांचा कपड्यांचा व्यवसाय आहे.
>
> *mājhyā kākāñcā kapaḍyāñcā vyavsāy āhe.*
>
> My uncle has a clothes business.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवसाय** (*vyavsāy*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vyavasāya*, "effort, undertaking" (Monier-Williams).
