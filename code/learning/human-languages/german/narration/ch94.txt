Lesson 1 of 5.
grau — grey.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for simple, then the German for brown.

You'll want to know: grau.
grau — "grey".

The word, taken apart.
grau from Old High German grāo from Proto-Germanic grēwaz — inherited, the same word as English grey. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 5 of 5.
sofort — immediately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a second, then the German for always.

You'll want to know: sofort.
sofort — "immediately".

The word, taken apart.
sofort is so + an adverb meaning "forth": "so forth", straight on without a pause. The two grew together into one word. So from Proto-Germanic swa (EtymDB 2.1), and fort is the same word as English forth.
