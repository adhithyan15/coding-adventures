## You'll want to know: ਅੰਗਿਆਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C249-GHR249-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅੰਗਿਆਰ** — *aṅgiār* — "a glowing coal; an ember".

> ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਲਾਲ ਅੰਗਿਆਰ ਹਨ।
>
> *chullhe vich lāl aṅgiār han.*
>
> There are red embers in the stove.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅੰਗਿਆਰ** (*aṅgiār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aṅgāra*, "glowing charcoal"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *aṅgyār*, "charcoal" (CDIAL 125).
