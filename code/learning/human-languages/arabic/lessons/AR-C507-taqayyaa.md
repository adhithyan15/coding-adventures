# تقيأ (taqayyaʾa) — to vomit, to be sick

## You'll want to know: تقيأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C507-MARAD507-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تقيأ** — *taqayyaʾa* — "to vomit, to be sick".

> تقيأ الطفل بعد الرحلة الطويلة.
>
> *taqayyaʾa aṭ-ṭifl baʿda ar-riḥla aṭ-ṭawīla.*
>
> The child was sick after the long journey.

Present: يتقيأ, *yataqayyaʾ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تقيأ** (*taqayyaʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-y-ʾ*, "to vomit" (Lane). Form V taqayyaʾa is the usual verb today.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
