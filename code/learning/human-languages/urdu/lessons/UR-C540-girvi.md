## You'll want to know: گروی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C540-GHATA540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گروی** — *girvī* — "pawned; held as a pledge (for a loan)".

> اگر قرض نہیں ملا تو گھر گروی رکھنا پڑے گا۔
>
> *agar qarz nahīṅ milā to ghar girvī rakhnā paṛe gā.*
>
> If we don't get a loan, we'll have to mortgage the house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گروی** (*girvī*) is usually traced to Persian *girav*, "a pledge, a pawn", with the ending *-ī*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
