Spanish, chapter 582: Las consecuencias.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la consecuencia — the consequence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for because of, then the Spanish for thanks to.

You'll want to know: la consecuencia.
la consecuencia — "the consequence".
Todo lo que haces tiene consecuencias. Everything you do has consequences.
Como consecuencia — as a result.

The word, taken apart.
consecuencia is a learned borrowing from Latin consequentia, built on consequi 'to follow closely' (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
el impacto — the impact.

You'll want to know: el impacto.
el impacto — "the impact".
La noticia tuvo un gran impacto. The news had a big impact.
Tener impacto en — to have an impact on.

The word, taken apart.
impacto is a learned borrowing from Latin impactus 'struck against' (EtymDB), the participle of impingere, built on pangere 'to fasten, to drive in'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
conllevar — to involve, to entail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the consequence, then the Spanish for the impact.

You'll want to know: conllevar.
conllevar — "to involve, to entail".
Este trabajo conlleva mucha responsabilidad. This job involves a lot of responsibility.
Formal. It is conjugated like llevar.

The word, taken apart.
conllevar is built on llevar with the prefix con-; llevar is inherited from Latin levare 'to lift, to raise' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
por consiguiente — consequently, therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the impact, then the Spanish for to involve.

You'll want to know: por consiguiente.
por consiguiente — "consequently, therefore".
No hay tren; por consiguiente, vamos en coche. There is no train; therefore, we are going by car.
Formal; in speech people say por eso or así que.

The word, taken apart.
por consiguiente is built on consiguiente, inherited from Latin consequentem 'following', from consequi 'to follow' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
de modo que — so; so that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to involve, then the Spanish for consequently.

You'll want to know: de modo que.
de modo que — "so; so that".
Estoy cansado, de modo que me voy. I am tired, so I am leaving.
With the indicative it gives a result; with the subjunctive a purpose.

The word, taken apart.
de modo que is built on modo, inherited from Latin modus 'measure, manner' (EtymDB).
