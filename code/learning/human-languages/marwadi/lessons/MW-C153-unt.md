# ऊंट (ū̃ṭ) — a camel

## You'll want to know: ऊंट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C153-ANIMAL153-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-THAT-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01] -->

**ऊंट** — *ū̃ṭ* — "a camel".

> **वो ऊंट है।**
>
> *vo ū̃ṭ hai.* — That is a camel.

It opens on a letter you have not written yet: the long *ū*, standing alone. The
next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊंट** (*ū̃ṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *uṣṭra*, which named a buffalo as well as
a camel. CDIAL lists Old Marwari *uṭa* in the same entry (CDIAL 2387).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C153-ANIMAL153-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01] -->

**ऊंट** is masculine, and takes **है** like anything you talk about: **ऊंट अठै है।**
(*ū̃ṭ aṭhai hai.*), the camel is here.
