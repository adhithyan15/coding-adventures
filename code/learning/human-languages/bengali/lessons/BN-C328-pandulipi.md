## You'll want to know: পাণ্ডুলিপি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C328-PRISHTHA328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পাণ্ডুলিপি** — *pānḍulipi* — "a manuscript".

> নাতি দাদুর বাক্সে কবিতার পাণ্ডুলিপি পেল।
>
> *nāti dādur bākse kôbitār pānḍulipi pelô.*
>
> The grandson found the manuscript of the poems in his grandfather's box.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পাণ্ডুলিপি** (*pānḍulipi*) joins Sanskrit *pāṇḍu*, 'pale, yellowish white' (Monier-Williams), to *lipi*, 'writing'. Monier-Williams has the older *pāṇḍulekha*, a first sketch in chalk; পাণ্ডুলিপি is a work in the writer's own hand, before it is printed.
