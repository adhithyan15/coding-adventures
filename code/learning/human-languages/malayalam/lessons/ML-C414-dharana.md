## You'll want to know: ധാരണ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C414-TATVAM414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ധാരണ** — *dhāraṇa* — "an understanding; a notion".

> ആ വിഷയത്തെക്കുറിച്ച് എനിക്ക് നല്ല ധാരണയുണ്ട്.
>
> *ā viṣayattekkuṟiccŭ enikkŭ nalla dhāraṇayuṇṭŭ.*
>
> I have a good understanding of that subject.

അവർ തമ്മിൽ ധാരണയായി, they reached an understanding: it is also an agreement between two sides.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ധാരണ** (*dhāraṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dhāraṇā*, "holding, bearing; keeping in the mind" (Monier-Williams). An understanding is what the mind holds.
