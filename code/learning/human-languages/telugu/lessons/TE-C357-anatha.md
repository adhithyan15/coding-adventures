## You'll want to know: అనాథ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C357-VAMSAM357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అనాథ** — *anātha* — "an orphan".

> ఆ అనాథ పిల్లలను ఒక ఆశ్రమం చూసుకుంటుంది.
>
> *ā anātha pillalanu oka āśramaṁ cūsukuṇṭundi.*
>
> A home looks after those orphan children.

Plural: అనాథలు, *anāthalu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అనాథ** (*anātha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anātha*, "having no master or protector" (Monier-Williams), from *a-*, "without", and *nātha*, "a protector".
