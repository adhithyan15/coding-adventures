Lesson 2 of 5.
फाटक (phāṭak) — a gate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a guide, then the Marathi for a fountain.

You'll want to know: फाटक.
फाटक — phāṭak — "a gate".

The word, taken apart.
फाटक (phāṭak) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from phāṭṭakka, "gate, door", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9069). CDIAL marks its own guess at the reconstruction's source as very doubtful.

Lesson 4 of 5.
तळघर (taḷghar) — a cellar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a gate, then the Marathi for a fence.

You'll want to know: तळघर.
तळघर — taḷghar — "a cellar".

The word, taken apart.
तळघर (taḷghar) is "bottom-house": taḷ, "bottom", from Sanskrit tala, "base, bottom" (CDIAL 5731), and घर, "house".

Lesson 5 of 5.
माळा (māḷā) — a loft, an attic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fence, then the Marathi for a cellar.

You'll want to know: माळा.
माळा — māḷā — "a loft, an attic".

The word, taken apart.
माळा (māḷā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from māḍa, "upper storey of a house", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9996). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "loft, granary".
