7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
じょうだん (jōdan) — a joke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for roughly, then the Japanese for perfectly.

You'll want to know: じょうだん.
じょうだん — jōdan — "a joke".
じょうだんを 言ったら、みんな わらうでしょう。 — jōdan o ittara, minna warau deshō — "If you tell a joke, everyone will probably laugh."

The word, taken apart.
じょうだん (jōdan) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are jō, "superfluous" and dan, "discuss" (Middle Chinese dam) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 3 of 7.
わるぐち (waruguchi) — speaking ill of someone; abuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a joke, then the Japanese for a reference.

You'll want to know: わるぐち.
わるぐち — waruguchi — "speaking ill of someone; abuse".
わるぐちを 言ったら、ともだちが おこるでしょう。 — waruguchi o ittara, tomodachi ga okoru deshō — "If you speak ill of people, your friends will be angry."

The word, taken apart.
わるぐち (waruguchi) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from waru, "bad", the stem of warui, and guchi, "mouth", the native kuchi voiced, and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 4 of 7.
てれる (tereru) — to be shy; to feel embarrassed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a reference, then the Japanese for speaking ill of someone.

You'll want to know: てれる.
てれる — tereru — "to be shy; to feel embarrassed".
ほめたら、あには てれるでしょう。 — hometara, ani wa tereru deshō — "If you praise him, my brother will probably get embarrassed."
Polite てれます, teremasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): あには てれました。, ani wa teremashita.

The word, taken apart.
てれる (tereru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

しゃべる — shaberu — "to chat; to talk".
じゅぎょうで しゃべったら、めいわくです。 — jugyō de shabettara, meiwaku desu — "Chatting in class is a nuisance to others."
Polite しゃべります, shaberimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): いもうとが しゃべりました。, imōto ga shaberimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しゃべる (shaberu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
