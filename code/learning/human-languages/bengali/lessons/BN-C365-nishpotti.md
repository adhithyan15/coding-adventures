## You'll want to know: নিষ্পত্তি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C365-SOMJHOTA365-03]; assesses=[] -->

**নিষ্পত্তি** — *nishpôtti* — "a settlement; a resolution (of a case or a dispute)".

> মামলাটার নিষ্পত্তি হয়ে গেল।
>
> *māmlāṭār nishpôtti hôye gelô.*
>
> The case was settled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নিষ্পত্তি** (*nishpôtti*) is Sanskrit *niṣpatti*, completion, a thing brought to its end (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the final settling of a case or a quarrel.
