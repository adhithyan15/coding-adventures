## You'll want to know: die Flöte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C513-INSTRUMENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Flöte** — "flute; recorder".

> In der Grundschule lernen viele Kinder Flöte.
>
> *Many children learn the recorder at primary school.*

Plural: *die Flöten*. A recorder is *die Blockflöte*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Flöte* goes back to Middle High German *floite*, borrowed from Old French *flaüte*, whose own origin is not settled; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
