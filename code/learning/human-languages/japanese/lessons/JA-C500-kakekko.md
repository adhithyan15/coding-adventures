## You'll want to know: かけっこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C500-TOY500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かけっこ** — *kakekko* — "a running race (between children)".

> **こどもが こうえんで かけっこを しています。** — *kodomo ga kōen de kakekko o shiteimasu* — "Children are having a race in the park."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かけっこ** (*kakekko*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
