## You'll want to know: کہاوت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C324-DASTAN324-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کہاوت** — *kahāvat* — "a saying; a proverb".

> دادی ہر بات پر کہاوت سناتی تھیں۔
>
> *dādī har bāt par kahāvat sunātī thīṅ.*
>
> Grandmother had a proverb for everything.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کہاوت** (*kahāvat*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kahāwat*, "a saying", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2706). It belongs with **کہنا**, to say.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
