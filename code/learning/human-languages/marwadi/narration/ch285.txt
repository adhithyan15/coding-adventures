Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 285: Learning by Heart.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आखर (ākhar) — a letter of the alphabet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an inkpot, then the Marwadi for to study.

You'll want to know: आखर.
आखर — ākhar — "a letter of the alphabet".
यो आखर नुवो है। yo ākhar nuvo hai. This letter is new.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आखर (ākhar) is inherited from Sanskrit akṣara, "a syllable, a letter"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi ākhar, "letter" (CDIAL 38).

You'll want to know: पानो.
पानो — pāno — "a page".
किताब मांय सो पाना है। kitāb mā̃y so pānā hai. The book has a hundred pages.

पानो (pāno) is inherited from Sanskrit parṇa, "a leaf"; CDIAL lists Marwari pān, "leaf", Sindhi pano, "page of book", and Hindi pannā, "leaf, page" (CDIAL 7918).

Lesson 4 of 5.
रटणो (raṭṇo) — to learn by heart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a page, then the Marwadi for a lesson.

You'll want to know: रटणो.
रटणो — raṭṇo — "to learn by heart".
छोरो पाठ रटै है। chhoro pāṭh raṭai hai. The boy learns the lesson by heart.
Past (I …): म्हैं पाठ रट्यो, mhaĩ pāṭh raṭyo.

The word, taken apart.
रटणो (raṭṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit raṭati, "cries, yells"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi raṭnā, "to cry out, repeat" (CDIAL 10590). Saying a thing over and over is how it is learnt by heart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उचारणो (uchārṇo) — to pronounce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a lesson, then the Marwadi for to learn by heart.

You'll want to know: उचारणो.
उचारणो — uchārṇo — "to pronounce".
छोरो आखर उचारै है। chhoro ākhar uchārai hai. The boy pronounces the letters.
Past (I …): म्हैं सबद उचार्यो, mhaĩ sabad uchāryo.

The word, taken apart.
उचारणो (uchārṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit uccārayati, "utters"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ūcaraï, "recites", and Hindi ucārnā, "to pronounce" (CDIAL 1641).
