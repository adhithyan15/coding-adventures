## You'll want to know: どれでも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C523-ANY523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**どれでも** — *doredemo* — "any one (of them)".

> **もし ほしい おかしが あったら、どれでも たべて ください。** — *moshi hoshii okashi ga attara, doredemo tabete kudasai* — "If there are any sweets you want, eat whichever you like."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どれでも** (*doredemo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: built from *dore*, "which", and *demo*, "even" (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
