## You'll want to know: દાદર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C153-ROOMS153-05]; assesses=[] -->

**દાદર** — *dādar* — "stairs, staircase".

> **તે દાદર ચઢે છે.** — *te dādar caḍhe che* — "He climbs the stairs."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાદર** (*dādar*) is counted as inherited. CDIAL lists Gujarati *dādar*, "ladder", under Sanskrit *dardara*, "split, broken", without saying how the sense moved (CDIAL 6196).
