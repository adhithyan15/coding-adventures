## You'll want to know: సన్నాయి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C538-SANGITAM538-05]; assesses=[] -->

**సన్నాయి** — *sannāyi* — "a reed pipe; a shehnai".

> పెళ్ళిలో సన్నాయి మోగింది.
>
> *peḷḷilō sannāyi mōgindi.*
>
> The reed pipe played at the wedding.

The festive reed instrument of weddings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సన్నాయి** (*sannāyi*) is the Telugu name of the festive reed pipe; its origin is not settled.
