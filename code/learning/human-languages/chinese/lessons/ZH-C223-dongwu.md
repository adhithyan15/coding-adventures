## You'll want to know: dòngwù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C223-ZOO223-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dòngwù* — "animal".

> *nǐ xǐhuan shénme dòngwù* — What animals do you like?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dòngwù* is built from *dòng* 'to move' and *wù* 'thing', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duwngX* and *mjut* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to shake' and the second 'the ten thousand things'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
