# ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನ (gamyasthāna) — a destination

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a whirlpool, then the Kannada for tourism.

## You'll want to know: ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C455-PRAVASA455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನ** — *gamyasthāna* — "a destination".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಬಸ್ ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನವನ್ನು ಮಧ್ಯರಾತ್ರಿ ತಲುಪಿತು.
>
> *namma bas gamyasthānavannu madhyarātri talupitu.*
>
> Our bus reached its destination at midnight.

ಗಮ್ಯ, to be gone to, plus ಸ್ಥಾನ, a place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನ** (*gamyasthāna*) is built from two Sanskrit words: *gamya*, "to be gone to, accessible" (CDIAL 4032), and *sthāna*, "a place" (CDIAL 13753).
