## You'll want to know: la bilancia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C426-BAGAGLI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la bilancia** — "the scales".

> Metti la valigia sulla bilancia.
>
> *Put the suitcase on the scales.*

*La bilancia pesapersone* — bathroom scales.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bilancia* is inherited from Late Latin *bilancia* 'balance', from *bilanx* 'having two pans', built from *bi-* and *lanx* 'dish' (EtymDB).
