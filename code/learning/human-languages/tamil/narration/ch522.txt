Tamil, chapter 522: An Old Village House.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
முற்றம் (muṟṟam) — a courtyard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a latch, then the Tamil for a flowerpot.

You'll want to know: முற்றம்.
முற்றம் — muṟṟam — "a courtyard".
பாட்டியின் வீட்டில் நடுவில் ஒரு பெரிய முற்றம் இருக்கிறது. pāṭṭiyiṉ vīṭṭil naṭuvil oru periya muṟṟam irukkiṟatu. Grandmother's house has a big courtyard in the middle.
An open yard inside a house, under the sky. As a -அம் noun: முற்றத்தில்.

The word, taken apart.
முற்றம் (muṟṟam) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5016 gives முற்றம் (muṟṟam), "the courtyard of a house, an open space".

Lesson 3 of 5.
தாழ்வாரம் (tāḻvāram) — a veranda; a corridor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a courtyard, then the Tamil for a raised veranda seat.

You'll want to know: தாழ்வாரம்.
தாழ்வாரம் — tāḻvāram — "a veranda; a corridor".
மழை பெய்தபோது நாங்கள் தாழ்வாரத்தில் நின்றோம். maḻai peytapōtu nāṅkaḷ tāḻvārattil niṉṟōm. When it rained we stood in the veranda.
The covered walkway under the low edge of a roof.

The word, taken apart.
தாழ்வாரம் (tāḻvāram) begins with the native தாழ் (tāḻ), "to be low" (DEDR 3178), for the low eaves; the origin of the second part, -வாரம், is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
தூண் (tūṇ) — a pillar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a raised veranda seat, then the Tamil for a veranda.

You'll want to know: தூண்.
தூண் — tūṇ — "a pillar".
கோயிலின் தூண்களில் அழகான சிலைகள் இருக்கின்றன. kōyiliṉ tūṇkaḷil aḻakāṉa cilaikaḷ irukkiṉṟaṉa. There are beautiful statues on the temple pillars.
Plural: தூண்கள், tūṇkaḷ.
The plural is தூண்கள். கம்பம் (kampam) is a thinner post or pole.

The word, taken apart.
தூண் (tūṇ) stands in DEDR's appendix of words shared with Indo-Aryan (DEDR App. 44), beside Sanskrit sthūṇā, "a post, a pillar" (CDIAL 13774); which way the word travelled is not settled.
