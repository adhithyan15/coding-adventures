## You'll want to know: दमा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C485-MADHUMEH485-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दमा** — *damā* — "asthma".

> धुळीमुळे त्याचा दमा वाढतो.
>
> *dhuḷīmuḷe tyācā damā vāḍhto.*
>
> Dust makes his asthma worse.

Masculine.

Masculine; दम लागणे, to get breathless.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दमा** (*damā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *dam*, "breath".
