Punjabi, chapter 217: Who Came?.
7 lessons. You can do the first 4 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
Return to come — retrieve to come and the familiar ਆਈ by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: āuṇā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਆਉਣਾ, "to come". Then ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ.

Grammar Lens.
Listen to the last word of ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ: āī. It is a came form, and you have been saying it since the early lessons. Next to it, hold the went forms ਗਿਆ, ਗਈ.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: to come]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਆਉਣਾ.
[your turn — hear: Aman went home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ.
[your turn — hear: I did not understand]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say went, for a man and a woman, once more.


Lesson 2 of 7.
ਆਇਆ, ਆਈ, ਆਏ — came.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say to come. Then Aman went home yesterday.

Grammar Lens: came, three ways.
ਆਉਣਾ has three came forms, like the three went forms: ਆਇਆ for a man, ਆਈ for a woman, ਆਏ for a group or for respect.
ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਇਆ। — Aman kallh āiā. — Aman came yesterday.
ਕੁੜੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ। — kuṛī kallh āī. — The girl came yesterday.
ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਏ। — uh kallh āe. — They came yesterday.
The ਆਈ in ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ is the woman's form: ਸਮਝ, "understanding", is a feminine word, and it is the one that did not come. CDIAL lists Punjabi āiā, "he came" (CDIAL 1045).

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: the boy came yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੁੰਡਾ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਇਆ।.
[your turn — hear: they came home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਘਰ ਆਏ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕੁੜੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਆਇਆ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Came, for a man? (ਆਇਆ.) A woman? (ਆਈ.) A group? (ਆਏ.)

Lesson 4 of 7.
ਔਰਤ (aurat) — a woman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say a man, ਆਦਮੀ. Then the girl came yesterday.

You'll want to know: ਔਰਤ.
ਔਰਤ — aurat — "a woman". A feminine noun, so the past that agrees with her ends like ਆਈ:
ਔਰਤ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ। — aurat kallh āī. — The woman came yesterday.

The word, taken apart.
ਔਰਤ (aurat) is a loan: it is usually traced to Persian aurat, "a woman", a word Persian itself took from Arabic. CDIAL gives no Punjabi form.

What you've built.
One more word for talking about the past.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: aurat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — hear: the woman came yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਔਰਤ (aurat) ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ।.
[your turn — recall: say a man, then a woman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਔਰਤ (aurat) once from the model]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਔਰਤ (aurat) mean? ("A woman".) Say it once more.


Lesson 5 of 7.
ਔ (au) — one vowel from the word for a woman.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Script you'll notice: ਔ, Guided Practice and Wrap-up Recall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਔਰਤ (aurat), "a woman", the word from the previous lesson.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script you'll notice: ਔ.
ਅ + ੌ becomes ਔ (au).
ਔ — au — an independent Gurmukhi vowel.
You know ਅ and the sign ੌ. Together they make the standing vowel ਔ (au), the first letter of ਔਰਤ (aurat).
No stroke order is taught here: the track does not yet have a verified source for one.

Writing: copy what you can see.
Keep ਔ in front of you. Trace its printed shape once with a finger, then copy it once with a pencil while the model stays visible. Say au when you finish.
This is observe-and-copy with the model visible, not writing from memory.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — look: at ਔ; YOU SAY: au]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਔਰਤ (aurat), then point to ਔ (au)]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਔ (au) once while the model remains visible]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Point to ਔ, say au, then say the whole word ਔਰਤ (aurat).

Lesson 6 of 7.
ਕੌਣ ਆਇਆ? (kauṇ āiā?) — who came?

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say who?, ਕੌਣ. Then the woman came yesterday.

Grammar Lens: the one who came decides.
Asking, you do not know who came, so you use the plain ਆਇਆ. The answer fits the person:
ਕੌਣ ਆਇਆ? — kauṇ āiā? — Who came?
ਆਦਮੀ ਆਇਆ। — ādmī āiā. — A man came.
ਔਰਤ ਆਈ। — aurat āī. — A woman came.
The went forms work the same way: ਆਦਮੀ ਗਿਆ, ਔਰਤ (aurat) ਗਈ.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: a woman went home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਔਰਤ (aurat) ਘਰ ਗਈ।.
[your turn — hear: a man came yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਆਦਮੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਇਆ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਔਰਤ (aurat) ਆਈ।]
Point to ਔ (au), then give the meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਔਰਤ (aurat) ਆਈ। once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask who came? Then answer: a woman came.
