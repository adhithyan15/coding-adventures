## You'll want to know: le hasard
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C500-CARTES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le hasard** — "chance, luck".

> Les jeux de hasard sont interdits aux mineurs.
>
> *Games of chance are forbidden to minors.*

*Par hasard* — by chance. The *h* is aspirated: *le hasard*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le hasard* comes from Arabic *az-zahr* "the die", through Spanish *azar* (EtymDB): first a game of dice, then chance itself.
