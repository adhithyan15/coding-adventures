## You'll want to know: ஆதரி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C527-OYVUNERAM527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆதரி** — *ātari* — "to support; to back".

> நான் எப்போதும் எங்கள் ஊர் அணியை ஆதரிக்கிறேன்.
>
> *nāṉ eppōtum eṅkaḷ ūr aṇiyai ātarikkiṟēṉ.*
>
> I always support our town team.

Past (I …): ஆதரித்தேன், *ātarittēṉ*.

The one supported takes -ஐ: அணியை ஆதரி. The noun is ஆதரவு, support.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆதரி** (*ātari*) is built on Sanskrit *ādara*, "respect, regard" (CDIAL 1142), made into a Tamil verb with -இ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
