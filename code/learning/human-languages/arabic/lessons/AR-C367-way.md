## You'll want to know: وعي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C367-IDRAK367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وعي** — *waʿy* — "awareness".

> عند الشباب وعي كبير بالصحة.
>
> *ʿinda ash-shabāb waʿy kabīr bi-ṣ-ṣiḥḥa.*
>
> Young people have great awareness of health.

فقد وعيه, *faqada waʿyahu*, he lost consciousness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وعي** (*waʿy*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ʿ-y*, "to retain, to keep in mind" (Lane). *Waʿy* is the verbal noun of *waʿā*, "to hold in mind": awareness.
