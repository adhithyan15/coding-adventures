## You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಾಗತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C88-WELCOME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಾಗತ** (*svāgata*) — "welcome".

**ನಿಮಗೆ ಸ್ವಾಗತ** (*nimage svāgata*) — "welcome to you", the formal greeting on a
signboard or at a door.
