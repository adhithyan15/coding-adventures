## You'll want to know: a repercussão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C351-IMPACTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a repercussão** — "the repercussion, the knock-on effect".

> A decisão teve repercussões em todo o país.
>
> *The decision had repercussions across the whole country.*

Mostly plural: *as repercussões*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*repercussão* is a learned borrowing from Latin *repercussiō* "a rebounding, a reflection" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *repercutere* "to strike back", built on *quatere* "to shake, to strike".
