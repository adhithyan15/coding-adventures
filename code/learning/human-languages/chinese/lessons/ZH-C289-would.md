## Grammar Lens: 如果 … 就会 …, a result you picture
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-RUGUO-JIUHUI-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-HUI-FUTURE-01, ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-02, ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-02, ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-01, ZH-LEX-C288-JIU-01] -->

> **会 after 就 says what would happen: 如果 … 就会 … for a result you picture.**

> **如果我有钱 我就会去中国** *rúguǒ wǒ yǒu qián, wǒ jiù huì qù Zhōngguó* — If I had the money, I would go to China.
> **如果我有时间 我就会学开车** *rúguǒ wǒ yǒu shíjiān, wǒ jiù huì xué kāichē* — If I had the time, I would learn to drive.

There is no new form here. It is the 会 of 明天会下雨, a forecast, now forecasting a result you picture.

Nothing in the verb says whether the condition is real: 如果我有钱 can be if I have the money, or if I had it. The rest of the conversation tells you.
