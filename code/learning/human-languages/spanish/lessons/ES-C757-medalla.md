## You'll want to know: la medalla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C757-CAMPEON-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la medalla** — "the medal".

> Ganó una medalla de oro.
>
> *She won a gold medal.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*medalla* is usually traced to Italian *medaglia*, which comes from a spoken Latin *metallea* "made of metal", from *metallum* "metal".
