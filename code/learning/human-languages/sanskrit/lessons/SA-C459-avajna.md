## You'll want to know: अवज्ञा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C459-ADARA459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अवज्ञा** — *avajñā* — "contempt".

> गुरोः अवज्ञा न उचिता।
>
> *guroḥ avajñā na ucitā.*
>
> Contempt for a teacher is not proper.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवज्ञा** (*avajñā*) is built from *ava*, "down", and the root √jñā, "to know", the root of **जानाति**: to think little of; MW gives "contempt, disesteem, disrespect" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
