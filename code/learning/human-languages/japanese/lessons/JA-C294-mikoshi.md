## You'll want to know: みこし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C294-MIKOSHI294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**みこし** — *mikoshi* — "a portable shrine".

> **まつりで みこしが まちを とおります。** — *matsuri de mikoshi ga machi o tōrimasu* — "At the festival a portable shrine goes through the town."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みこし** (*mikoshi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
