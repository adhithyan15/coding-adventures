## You'll want to know: ਦਹੀਂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C237-KAM237-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਹੀਂ** — *dahīṁ* — "yoghurt; curd".

> ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਾਲ ਦਹੀਂ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ roṭī nāl dahīṁ khāndā hāṁ.*
>
> I eat yoghurt with my roti.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਹੀਂ** (*dahīṁ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dadhi*, "thick sour milk"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dahī̃*, "thick sour milk" (CDIAL 6146).
