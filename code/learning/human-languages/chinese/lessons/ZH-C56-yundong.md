## You'll want to know: yùndòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C56-PLAY56-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yùndòng* — "sport; exercise".

> *wǒ xǐhuan yùndòng* — I like sport.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùndòng* is built from yùn 'to move round, to carry' and dòng 'to move', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjunH* and *duwngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The sense 'sport' is not given there.
