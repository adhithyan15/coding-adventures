## You'll want to know: সরবরাহ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C499-UTPADON499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সরবরাহ** — *sôrbôrāh* — "a supply; supplying".

> বন্যার পরে গ্রামে খাবারের সরবরাহ থামল।
>
> *Bônyār pôre grāme khābārer sôrbôrāh thāmlô.*
>
> After the flood the food supply to the village stopped.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সরবরাহ** (*sôrbôrāh*) is usually traced to Persian *sarbarāh*, "management, provision".
