## You'll want to know: واحة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C473-WAHA473-01]; assesses=[] -->

**واحة** — *wāḥa* — "an oasis".

> نزلنا في واحة صغيرة في الصحراء.
>
> *nazalnā fī wāḥa ṣaghīra fī aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ.*
>
> We stopped at a small oasis in the desert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**واحة** (*wāḥa*) was borrowed from Coptic, which goes back to ancient Egyptian (EtymDB); English *oasis* comes from the same word.
