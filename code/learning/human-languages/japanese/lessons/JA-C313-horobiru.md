## You'll want to know: ほろびる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C313-KIROKU313-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ほろびる** — *horobiru* — "to perish; to die out".

> **ふるい くには さいがいで ほろびました。** — *furui kuni wa saigai de horobimashita* — "The old kingdom perished in a disaster."

Polite **ほろびます**, *horobimasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **その くにが ほろびました。**, *sono kuni ga horobimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほろびる** (*horobiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
