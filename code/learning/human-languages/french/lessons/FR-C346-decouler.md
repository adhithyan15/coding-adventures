## You'll want to know: découler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C346-CONSEQUENCE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**découler** — "to follow (from), to result from".

> Tous nos problèmes découlent de cette erreur.
>
> *All our problems follow from this mistake.*

*Découler de* — to flow from. Formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*découler* is built from *dé-* "from" and *couler* "to flow", which is inherited from Latin *cōlāre* "to filter, to strain" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
