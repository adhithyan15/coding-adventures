## You'll want to know: ribadire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C348-VANTAGGIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ribadire** — "to reiterate, to restate firmly".

> Ribadisco quello che ho detto: io non vengo.
>
> *I repeat what I said: I am not coming.*

Conjugated with *-isc-*: *ribadisco*. The first sense is to clinch a nail by bending its point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *ribadire* is not settled; EtymDB records the word with no source (EtymDB). Its first sense was "to clinch a nail by hammering its point flat", and from there it came to mean to hammer a point home with words.
