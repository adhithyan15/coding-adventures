## You'll want to know: आठवाँ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C76-ORDINAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आठवाँ** (*āṭhvā̃*) — "**eighth**".

**आठवाँ दिन** (*āṭhvā̃ din*), **आठवीं किताब** (*āṭhvī̃ kitāb*).

**आठ** + **-वाँ**. The retroflex **ठ** of the cardinal is kept exactly as it is —
the ending arrives after it and changes nothing on its way.

Hold *chaṭhā* and *āṭhvā̃* side by side and hear the difference. Both have a
retroflex **ठ**; in the sixth it belongs to the ordinal and not to the cardinal,
and in the eighth it belongs to the cardinal and the ordinal borrowed nothing.
