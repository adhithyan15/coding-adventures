## You'll want to know: viziato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C497-EDUCARE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**viziato** — "spoilt".

> È un bambino viziato: vuole tutto subito.
>
> *He is a spoilt child: he wants everything at once.*

The feminine is *viziata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viziato* is the past participle of *viziare* "to spoil", from Latin *vitiāre* (EtymDB), built on *vitium* "a fault".
