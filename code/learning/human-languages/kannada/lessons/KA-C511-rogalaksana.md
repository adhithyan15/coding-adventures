# ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣ (rōgalakṣaṇa) — a symptom

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C511-SONKU511-02, KA-LEX-C511-SONKU511-03] -->

## You'll want to know: ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C511-SONKU511-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣ** — *rōgalakṣaṇa* — "a symptom".

> ಜ್ವರ ಮತ್ತು ಕೆಮ್ಮು ಈ ಕಾಯಿಲೆಯ ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣಗಳು.
>
> *jvara mattu kemmu ī kāyileya rōgalakṣaṇagaḷu.*
>
> Fever and a cough are the symptoms of this illness.

Plural: ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣಗಳು, *rōgalakṣaṇagaḷu*.

ರೋಗ, disease + ಲಕ್ಷಣ, a mark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರೋಗಲಕ್ಷಣ** (*rōgalakṣaṇa*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *rōga*, "disease" (CDIAL 10829), and *lakṣaṇa*, "a line, a mark" (CDIAL 10882).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
