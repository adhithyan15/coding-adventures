## You'll want to know: অনেক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C276-GHOTONA276-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অনেক** — *ônek* — "many; a lot".

> ঘটনাটা দেখতে অনেক লোক রাস্তায় এল।
>
> *ghôṭônāṭā dekhte ônek lok rāstāy elô.*
>
> A lot of people came out onto the road to see what had happened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনেক** (*ônek*) is Sanskrit *aneka*, *an-* 'not' and *eka* 'one' (Monier-Williams): 'not one' came to mean 'many'. **এক**, one, is the same *eka*.
