Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 118: Hearth, Griddle, Wok, Cooking pot, Water pot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चूल्हो (chūlho) — a hearth, a stove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for because of, then the Marwadi for because.

You'll want to know: चूल्हो.
चूल्हो — chūlho — "a hearth, a stove".

The word, taken apart.
चूल्हो (chūlho) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cullī, "fireplace". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4879); the match is through Hindi cūlhā. CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word itself from Dravidian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तवो (tavo) — a griddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for because, then the Marwadi for a hearth.

You'll want to know: तवो.
तवो — tavo — "a griddle".

The word, taken apart.
तवो (tavo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from tapaka, "frying pan", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5670); the match is through Hindi tawā and Gujarati tavo. It is built on the root tap, "to heat".

Lesson 5 of 5.
घड़ो (ghaṛo) — a water pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a wok, then the Marwadi for a cooking pot.

You'll want to know: घड़ो.
घड़ो — ghaṛo — "a water pot".

The word, taken apart.
घड़ो (ghaṛo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ghaṭa, "pot". CDIAL lists Marwari ghaṛī under it (CDIAL 4406). The Marwari row is ghaṛī, "water-clock"; Hindi ghaṛā and Gujarati ghaṛo mean "water pot", as घड़ो (ghaṛo) does.
