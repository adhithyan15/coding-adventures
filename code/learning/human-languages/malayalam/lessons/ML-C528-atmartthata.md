# ആത്മാർത്ഥത (ātmārtthata) — sincerity

## You'll want to know: ആത്മാർത്ഥത
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C528-SATHYAM528-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ആത്മാർത്ഥത** — *ātmārtthata* — "sincerity".

> അവളുടെ ജോലിയിലെ ആത്മാർത്ഥത എല്ലാവർക്കും ഇഷ്ടമാണ്.
>
> *avaḷuṭe jōliyile ātmārtthata ellāvarkkuṁ iṣṭamāṇŭ.*
>
> Everyone likes the sincerity of her work.

The adjective is ആത്മാർത്ഥമായ, sincere.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആത്മാർത്ഥത** (*ātmārtthata*) is built from Sanskrit *ātman*, "self, soul", and *artha*, "aim, purpose" (Monier-Williams), with the noun ending -ത: acting from one's own heart.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
