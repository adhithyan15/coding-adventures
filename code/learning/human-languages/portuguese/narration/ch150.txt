Portuguese, chapter 150: Rarely, Then, Finally, From now on, Still.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
raramente — rarely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for friendship, then the Portuguese for hope.

You'll want to know: raramente.
raramente — "rarely".

The word, taken apart.
raramente is built from raro, "rare" ( from Latin rarus), and -mente, "-ly".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for then, then the Portuguese for finally.

You'll want to know: a partir de agora.
a partir de agora — "from now on".

The word, taken apart.
a partir de agora is "starting from now": partir from Latin partire, "to divide, to share out" (EtymDB 2.1), and agora from Latin hac hora, "at this hour" (EtymDB 2.1).
