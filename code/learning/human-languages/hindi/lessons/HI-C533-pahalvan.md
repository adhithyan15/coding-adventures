## You'll want to know: पहलवान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C533-KUSHTI533-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पहलवान** — *pahalvān* — "a wrestler".

> पहलवान सुबह चार बजे उठकर दौड़ता है।
>
> *Pahalvān subah cār baje uṭhkar dauṛtā hai.*
>
> The wrestler gets up at four in the morning and runs.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पहलवान** (*pahalvān*) is usually traced to Persian *pahlavān*, "a champion, a hero".
