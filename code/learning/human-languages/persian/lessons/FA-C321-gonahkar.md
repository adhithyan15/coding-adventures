## You'll want to know: گناهکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C321-QASAM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گناهکار** (*gonâhkâr*) — "guilty; a guilty person".

> همه فکر میکردند او گناهکار است.
>
> *Hame fekr mikardand u gonâhkâr ast.*
>
> Everyone thought he was guilty.

*gonâh* "sin, fault" + *-kâr* "doer".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gonâhkâr* joins *gonâh* "sin, fault" and the suffix *-kâr* "doer", both native Persian. EtymDB lists *gonâh* without a parent.
