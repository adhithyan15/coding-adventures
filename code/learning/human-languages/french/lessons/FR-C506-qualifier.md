## You'll want to know: qualifier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C506-RESULTATS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**qualifier** — "(se qualifier) to qualify; to describe as".

> L'équipe s'est qualifiée pour la finale.
>
> *The team qualified for the final.*

Reflexive in sport: *se qualifier pour*. Also to describe: *qualifier quelqu'un de menteur*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qualifier* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *quālificāre*, built from *quālis* "of what kind" and *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short).
