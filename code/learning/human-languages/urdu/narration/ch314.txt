Urdu, chapter 314: Ruins of the Past.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کھنڈر (khanḍar) — ruins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a sacrifice, then the Urdu for to entrust.

You'll want to know: کھنڈر.
کھنڈر — khanḍar — "ruins".
پہاڑی پر ایک پرانا کھنڈر ہے۔ pahāṛī par ek purānā khanḍar hai. There are old ruins on the hill.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
کھنڈر (khanḍar) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin khaṇḍhar, "a dilapidated building, ruins", under khaṇḍaghara, a broken house, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3794).

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے زمین کھودی, us ne zamīn khodī.

Lesson 3 of 5.
مجسمہ (mujassamā) — a statue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for ruins, then the Urdu for to dig.

You'll want to know: مجسمہ.
مجسمہ — mujassamā — "a statue".
انہیں ایک پرانا مجسمہ ملا۔ unheṅ ek purānā mujassamā milā. They found an old statue.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مجسمہ (mujassamā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mujassam, "given a body, made solid": a figure in stone or metal.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to dig, then the Urdu for a statue.

You'll want to know: کتبہ.
کتبہ — katbā — "an inscription".
پتھر پر ایک کتبہ تھا۔ patthar par ek katbā thā. There was an inscription on the stone.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
کتبہ (katbā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic katba, "a piece of writing". It is writing cut into stone or metal.

Lesson 5 of 5.
آثار (āsār) — ancient remains; traces.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a statue, then the Urdu for an inscription.

You'll want to know: آثار.
آثار — āsār — "ancient remains; traces".
یہاں پرانے شہر کے آثار ہیں۔ yahāṅ purāne shahr ke āsār haiṅ. Here are the remains of an old city.
Masculine, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
آثار (āsār) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic āthār, "traces", the plural of the word behind اثر. It takes a plural verb: آثار ہیں.
