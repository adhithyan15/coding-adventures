## You'll want to know: अध्ययन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C363-ADHYAYAN363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अध्ययन** — *adhyayan* — "a study (a piece of research)".

> मीरा बच्चों की नींद पर अध्ययन कर रही है।
>
> *Mīrā baccoṁ kī nīnd par adhyayan kar rahī hai.*
>
> Mira is doing a study on children's sleep.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अध्ययन** (*adhyayan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *adhyayana*, "reading, studying" (Monier-Williams).
