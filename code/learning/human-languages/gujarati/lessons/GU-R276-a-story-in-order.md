# Review — a story in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C275-ONGOING-PAST-01, GU-LEX-C275-BANVU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was reading". (*hũ vā̃cto hato*.) Then "what happened?", in a story. (*śũ banyũ?*)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C276-INE-FORM-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C276-YA-PACHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C276-FIRST-THEN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C276-TYAR-PACHI-01, GU-LEX-C276-CHEVTE-01, GU-SKILL-C276-READ-STORY-01, GU-LEX-BAJAR-01, GU-LEX-CHA] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I went home and ate.]
- [YOU SAY: After eating, we went to the market.]
- [YOU SAY: First we drank tea, then we went to the market.]
- [YOU SAY: After that we came home, and in the end we slept.]
- [YOU SAY: In Mira's Sunday, what happened in the field?]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C276-INE-FORM-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C276-YA-PACHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C276-FIRST-THEN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C276-TYAR-PACHI-01, GU-LEX-C276-CHEVTE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "Having eaten"? (**ખાઈને**.) "After the work"? (**કામ કર્યા પછી**.) Put these in order: **છેવટે**, **પહેલાં**, **ત્યાર પછી**, **પછી**. (**પહેલાં**, **પછી**, **ત્યાર પછી**, **છેવટે**.)
