Arabic, chapter 324: Danger and warnings.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
حذر (ḥadhdhara) — to warn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for boyhood, then the Arabic for middle age.

You'll want to know: حذر.
حذر — ḥadhdhara — "to warn".
حذرت الأم ابنها من النار. ḥadhdharat al-umm ibnahā min an-nār. The mother warned her son about the fire.
Present: يحذر, yuḥadhdhir.
It takes من for the danger: حذره من البرد.

The word, taken apart.
حذر (ḥadhdhara) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-dh-r (Lane), "to be wary, on one's guard"; ḥadhar is caution. It is Form II, "to put someone on guard".

Lesson 2 of 6.
هدد (haddada) — to threaten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for middle age, then the Arabic for to warn.

You'll want to know: هدد.
هدد — haddada — "to threaten".
هدد الرجل جاره بالشرطة. haddada ar-rajul jārahu bi-sh-shurṭa. The man threatened his neighbour with the police.
Present: يهدد, yuhaddid.
The danger itself is تهديد.

The word, taken apart.
هدد (haddada) is built on the native Arabic root h-d-d (Lane), "to pull down a building". Form II haddada, to threaten, is usually explained as threatening with that ruin.

إنذار — indhār — "an alarm; a warning".
انطلق إنذار الحريق في المدرسة. inṭalaqa indhār al-ḥarīq fī al-madrasa. The fire alarm went off in the school.

The word, taken apart.
إنذار (indhār) is built on the native Arabic root n-dh-r (Lane), "to vow; to warn". Indhār is the verbal noun of Form IV andhara, "to warn of danger".

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to threaten, then the Arabic for an alarm.

You'll want to know: صفارة.
صفارة — ṣaffāra — "a whistle; a siren".
سمعنا صفارة (ṣaffāra) سيارة الإسعاف. samiʿnā ṣaffārat sayyārat al-isʿāf. We heard the ambulance siren.
صفارة (ṣaffāra) الإنذار: an alarm siren.

The word, taken apart.
صفارة (ṣaffāra) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-f-r (Lane), where ṣafara is "to whistle". The faʿʿāla pattern names a tool that does a thing again and again.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

فوضى — fawḍā — "chaos, disorder".
كانت الغرفة في فوضى كبيرة. kānat al-ghurfa fī fawḍā kabīra. The room was in a terrible mess.

The word, taken apart.
فوضى (fawḍā) is built on the native Arabic root f-w-ḍ (Lane), where qawm fawḍā is a people who are all equal, with no chief over them: everyone at once.
