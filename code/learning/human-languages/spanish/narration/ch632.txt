Spanish, chapter 632: Pagos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
abonar — to pay; to credit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the demand, then the Spanish for to be worth.

You'll want to know: abonar.
abonar — "to pay; to credit".
Puede abonar la cantidad con tarjeta. You can pay the amount by card.
Formal, on bills and notices. It also means to fertilise.

The word, taken apart.
abonar is built on bueno, inherited from Latin bonus 'good' (EtymDB): it first meant to make good, then to pay.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ingresar — to pay in, to deposit; to be admitted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the monthly payment, then the Spanish for to split into instalments.

You'll want to know: ingresar.
ingresar — "to pay in, to deposit; to be admitted".
Ingresé el dinero en mi cuenta. I paid the money into my account.
In Spain ingresar dinero is to pay it in; much of Latin America says depositar. Ingresar en el hospital — to be admitted.

The word, taken apart.
ingresar is built on ingreso, a learned borrowing from Latin ingressus 'an entering', from ingredi and gradi 'to step' (EtymDB).
