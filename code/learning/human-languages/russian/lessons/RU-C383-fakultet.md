## You'll want to know: факультет
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C383-POSTUPLENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**факультет** (*fakultét*) — "a faculty (of a university)".

> Сестра учится на историческом факультете.
>
> *Sestrá úchitsya na istorícheskom fakultéte.*
>
> My sister studies at the history faculty.

Masculine. Students study *на факультете*, with *на*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**факультет** was borrowed from German *Fakultät*, from Latin *facultās* "capability, ability, skill" (EtymDB): a branch of learning as a power of the mind.
