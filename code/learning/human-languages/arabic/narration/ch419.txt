Arabic, chapter 419: Positive and negative.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
إيجابي (ījābī) — positive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for effectiveness, then the Arabic for independent.

You'll want to know: إيجابي.
إيجابي — ījābī — "positive".
كان للقرار أثر إيجابي. kāna li-l-qarār athar ījābī. The decision had a positive effect.

The word, taken apart.
إيجابي (ījābī) is built on the native Arabic root w-j-b, "to be due, binding" (Lane). Ījābī is a modern adjective from ījāb, the Form IV verbal noun, affirming: positive.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
