## You'll want to know: शोला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C299-AAG299-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शोला** — *śolā* — "a flame; a burst of fire".

> खिड़की से आग के शोले निकल रहे थे।
>
> *khiṛkī se āg ke śole nikal rahe the.*
>
> Flames were leaping out of the window.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शोला** (*śolā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shuʿla*, "a flame, a blaze". A burning house sends out **शोले**; so, in speech, do angry eyes.
