## You'll want to know: ರಫ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C421-BEDIKE421-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಫ್ತು** — *raphtu* — "export".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಕಾಫಿ ಬೇರೆ ದೇಶಗಳಿಗೆ ರಫ್ತು ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *namma kāphi bēre dēśagaḷige raphtu āguttade.*
>
> Our coffee is exported to other countries.

ರಫ್ತು ಮಾಡು, to export. ಆಮದು, import.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಫ್ತು** (*raphtu*) is usually traced to Persian *raft*, "gone", through Hindustani *raftanī*, "export".
