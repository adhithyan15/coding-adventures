## You'll want to know: de lo contrario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C612-SINO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**de lo contrario** — "otherwise".

> Date prisa; de lo contrario, perdemos el tren.
>
> *Hurry up; otherwise, we will miss the train.*

In speech people also say *si no*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*de lo contrario* is built on *contrario*, a learned borrowing from Latin *contrarius* 'opposite', from *contra* 'against' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
