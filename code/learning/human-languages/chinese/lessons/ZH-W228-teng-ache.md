## Script — the frame of 病, and a shape of five inside
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-TENG-ACHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 疼

**Two pieces: a frame, and what sits inside it.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The frame, five strokes,** as in 病: a dot at the top, a bar across, a long stroke falling to the left, then a small dot and a short stroke rising to the right on the far left.

**Inside, five strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left. A bar that turns and falls to the left. A long stroke falling to the right. Then two dots, one under the other.

5 + 5 = ten.
