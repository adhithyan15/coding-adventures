## You'll want to know: ચંદ્રક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C549-GOAL549-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચંદ્રક** — *candrak* — "a medal".

> જો મીરા પહેલી આવશે તો તેને ચંદ્રક મળશે.
>
> *jo Mīrā pahelī āvśe to tene candrak maḷśe.*
>
> If Mira comes first, she'll get a medal.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચંદ્રક** (*candrak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *candraka*, "the moon; a ring shaped like the moon" (Monier-Williams): a round disc. It is built on **ચંદ્ર**, the moon.
