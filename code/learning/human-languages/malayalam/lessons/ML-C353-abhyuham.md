# അഭ്യൂഹം (abhyūhaṁ) — speculation; a guess

## You'll want to know: അഭ്യൂഹം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C353-KIMVADANTI353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**അഭ്യൂഹം** — *abhyūhaṁ* — "speculation; a guess".

> കമ്പനി അടയ്ക്കും എന്ന് അഭ്യൂഹം ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *kampani aṭaykkuṁ ennŭ abhyūhaṁ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> There is speculation that the company will close.

Plural: അഭ്യൂഹങ്ങൾ, *abhyūhaṅṅaḷ*.

In the news the plural is common: അഭ്യൂഹങ്ങൾ, speculation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അഭ്യൂഹം** (*abhyūhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *abhyūha*, "reasoning, inference, conjecture" (Monier-Williams). In Malayalam news it is a guess that is going round without proof.

One more word for telling what happened.
