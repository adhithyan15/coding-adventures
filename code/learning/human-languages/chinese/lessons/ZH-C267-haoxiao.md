## You'll want to know: 好笑
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C267-FILM267-05]; assesses=[] -->

**好笑** — *hǎoxiào* — "funny".

> **这个笑话很好笑** *zhège xiàohua hěn hǎoxiào* — This joke is really funny.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**好笑** (*hǎoxiào*) is built from 好 'good' and 笑 'to laugh', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xawX* and *sjewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): good to laugh at, the way 好吃 is good to eat.
