Lesson 1 of 6.
रोषणाई (roṣṇāī) — festive lights; illuminations.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a thread ceremony, then the Marathi for an invitation card.

You'll want to know: रोषणाई.
रोषणाई — roṣṇāī — "festive lights; illuminations".
सणाला संपूर्ण गल्लीत रोषणाई केली होती. saṇālā sampūrṇa gallīt roṣṇāī kelī hotī. For the festival the whole lane had been lit up.
Feminine.
रोषणाई (roṣṇāī) करणे, to put up lights. The letter ऋ opens ऋतू, a season; every ऋतू has its festival.

The word, taken apart.
रोषणाई (roṣṇāī) is a loan from Persian rošanā'ī, 'brightness, light', built on rošan, 'bright'.

आईने दारासमोर सुंदर रांगोळी काढली. āīne dārāsamor sundar rāṅgoḷī kāḍhlī. Mother made a beautiful rangoli in front of the door.

रांगोळी (rāṅgoḷī) काढणे, to make one with coloured powder.

The word, taken apart.
रांगोळी (rāṅgoḷī) is the Marathi word for a pattern of coloured powder on the floor; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for festive lights, then the Marathi for rangoli.

You'll want to know: तोरण.
तोरण — toraṇ — "a door garland".
सणाच्या दिवशी दाराला आंब्याच्या पानांचं तोरण लावलं. saṇācyā divśī dārālā āmbyācyā pānāñcaṁ toraṇ lāvlaṁ. On the festival day a garland of mango leaves was hung on the door.
Neuter.
दाराला तोरण (toraṇ) बांधणे, to hang a festoon over the door.

The word, taken apart.
तोरण (toraṇ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit toraṇa, 'an arched doorway, a festive gateway' (Monier-Williams).

फटाका — phaṭākā — "a firecracker".
रात्री मुलांनी खूप फटाके फोडले. rātrī mulānnī khūp phaṭāke phoḍle. At night the children set off lots of firecrackers.
Masculine. Plural: फटाके, phaṭāke.
Plural फटाके; फटाके फोडणे, to set off crackers.

The word, taken apart.
फटाका (phaṭākā) is a Marathi word that seems built on the sound phaṭ, a bang; its origin is not settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a door garland, then the Marathi for a firecracker.

You'll want to know: आतषबाजी.
आतषबाजी — ātaṣbājī — "fireworks".
जत्रेच्या शेवटी आकाशात आतषबाजी झाली. jatrecyā śevṭī ākāśāt ātaṣbājī jhālī. At the end of the fair there were fireworks in the sky.
Feminine.
आतषबाजी (ātaṣbājī) करणे, to put on fireworks.

The word, taken apart.
आतषबाजी (ātaṣbājī) is a loan from Persian ātaš-bāzī, built from ātaš, 'fire', and bāzī, 'play'.
