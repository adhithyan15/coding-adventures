## You'll want to know: dǎpò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C187-BADDAY187-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎpò* — "to break; to smash".

> *zuótiān wǒ dǎpò le yí ge bēizi* — Yesterday I broke a glass.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎpò* is built from 打 'to strike' and *pò* 'to break', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *taengX* and *phaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), though dǎ is not the sound *taengX* would regularly give.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
