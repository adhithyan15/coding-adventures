## You'll want to know: तड़कना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C384-BARF384-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तड़कना** — *taṛaknā* — "to crack (with a sharp sound)".

> अगर ठंडे गिलास में गरम चाय भरोगे तो गिलास तड़क जाएगा।
>
> *agar ṭhaṇḍe gilās meṁ garam cāy bharoge to gilās taṛak jāegā.*
>
> If you fill a cold glass with hot tea, the glass will crack.

Past (it …): वह गिलास तड़क गया, *vah gilās taṛak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तड़कना** (*taṛaknā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *traṭ*, "crackle" (CDIAL 5988).
