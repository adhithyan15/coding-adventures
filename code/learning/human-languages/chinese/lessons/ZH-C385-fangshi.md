## You'll want to know: fāngshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C385-WHY385-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fāngshì* — "a way of doing things; a mode".

> *qí zìxíngchē shì yí ge hěn huánbǎo de fāngshì* — Cycling is a very green way to get about.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fāngshì* is built from *fāng* 'a method' and *shì* 'a pattern', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjang* and *syik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'square' and 'a method' and the second 'a rule'.
