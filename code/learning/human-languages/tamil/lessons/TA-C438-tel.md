## You'll want to know: தேள்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C438-PARAVAI438-05]; assesses=[] -->

**தேள்** — *tēḷ* — "a scorpion".

> பாறையின் கீழே ஒரு தேள் இருந்தது.
>
> *pāṟaiyiṉ kīḻē oru tēḷ iruntatu.*
>
> There was a scorpion under the rock.

Its sting is very painful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தேள்** (*tēḷ*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3470), "a scorpion".
