## You'll want to know: すずしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C136-SUZUSHII]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SU-01, JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01, JA-SCRIPT-I-01] -->

**すずしい** — *suzushii* — "cool": the air on a summer evening, or a shady
room on a hot day. It is a pleasant word; it is not *cold*.

The new sign is spent at once. **す**, **ず**, **し** and **い**: four beats,
*su–zu–shi–i*. The plain **す** and the voiced **ず** stand side by side, so
you can see the mark do its work. You write every sign in it now.
