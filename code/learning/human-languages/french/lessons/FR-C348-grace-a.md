## You'll want to know: grâce à
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C348-GRACE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**grâce à** — "thanks to".

> Grâce à toi, j'ai trouvé un travail.
>
> *Thanks to you, I found a job.*

For good results; for bad ones, *à cause de*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grâce* comes through Old French *grace* from Latin *grātia* "favour, thanks" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *grātus* "pleasing".
