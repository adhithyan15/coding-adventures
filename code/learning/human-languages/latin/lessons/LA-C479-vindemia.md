## You'll want to know: vindēmia, vindēmiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C479-POMARIUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vindēmia, vindēmiae** — "the grape harvest, the vintage".

> Autumnō vindēmia est; tōta familia ūvās legit.
>
> *In autumn it is the grape harvest; the whole household gathers grapes.*

First declension, feminine: *vindēmia, vindēmiae f.* English *vintage* comes from it through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vindēmia* is built from *vīnum* 'wine, grapes' and *dēmō* 'to take off, to remove' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): taking the grapes off the vine. English *vintage* comes from it through French.
