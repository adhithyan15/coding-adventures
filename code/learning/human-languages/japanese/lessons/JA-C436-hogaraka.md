## You'll want to know: ほがらか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C436-BRIGHT436-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ほがらか** — *hogaraka* — "cheerful; sunny-natured".

> **あねは いつも ほがらかです。** — *ane wa itsumo hogaraka desu* — "My elder sister is cheerful all the time."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほがらか** (*hogaraka*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
