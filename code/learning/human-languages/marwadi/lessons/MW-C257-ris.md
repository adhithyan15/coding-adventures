## You'll want to know: रीस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C257-RIS257-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रीस** — *rīs* — "anger".

> रीस मत करो।
>
> *rīs mat karo.*
>
> Don't get angry.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रीस** (*rīs*) is inherited from Sanskrit *riṣ*, "injury". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati *rīs* and Hindi *ris*, both "anger", under it; how harm became anger is not spelled out there (CDIAL 10746).
