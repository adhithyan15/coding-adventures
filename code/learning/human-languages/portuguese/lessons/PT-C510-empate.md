## You'll want to know: o empate
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C510-RESULTADO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o empate** — "draw, tie".

> O jogo acabou num empate.
>
> *The match ended in a draw.*

The noun of *empatar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*empate* comes from *empatar* "to draw", which was borrowed from Spanish (EtymDB).
