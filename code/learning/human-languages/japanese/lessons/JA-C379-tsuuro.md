## You'll want to know: つうろ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C379-SHARYOU379-03]; assesses=[] -->

**つうろ** — *tsūro* — "an aisle; a passage".

> **つうろに にもつを おかないで ください。** — *tsūro ni nimotsu o okanaide kudasai* — "Please do not leave your bags in the aisle."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つうろ** (*tsūro*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tsū*, "pass through" (Middle Chinese *thuwng*) and *ro*, "path" (*luH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
