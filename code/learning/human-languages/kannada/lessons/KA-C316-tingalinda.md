# ಎರಡು ತಿಂಗಳಿಂದ (eraḍu tiṅgaḷinda) — how long

## You'll want to know: -ಇಂದ, for … now
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C316-SPAN-INDA-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C07-ERADU-01, KA-LEX-C05-KANNADA-01, KA-LEX-C148-ACT148-03, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-ONGOING-PAST-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C313-HOW-FAR-01, KA-LEX-C07-MUURU-01, KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-03] -->

> **ನಾನು ಎರಡು ತಿಂಗಳಿಂದ ಕನ್ನಡ ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ.**
>
> *nānu eraḍu tiṅgaḷinda kannaḍa kaliyuttiddēne.* — "I have been learning Kannada for two months."

**ತಿಂಗಳು** (*tiṅgaḷu*, "a month") with **-ಇಂದ**, "from": "from two months (back to now)". The **-ಇಂದ** of **ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ**, "from here", now measures time. The verb stays in the present, because the learning is still going on: **ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ** is **ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತ** + **ಇದ್ದೇನೆ**, "I am, learning", the present twin of **ಓದುತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ**. English says "have been"; Kannada says "am".

> **ನೀವು ಎಷ್ಟು ಸಮಯದಿಂದ ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದೀರಿ?**
>
> *nīvu eṣṭu samayadinda illi iddīri?* — "How long have you been here?"

**ಇದ್ದೀರಿ** (*iddīri*, "you are"), said with respect. The answer: **ಮೂರು ವರ್ಷದಿಂದ** (*mūru varṣadinda*, "for three years").

A stretch that is over takes no ending at all: **ನಾನು ಒಂದು ವಾರ ಮೈಸೂರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದೆ** (*nānu ondu vāra maisūrinalli idde*, "I was in Mysuru for a week"). **ವಾರ** (*vāra*, "a week").

## You'll want to know: from … until
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C316-FROM-UNTIL-01]; assesses=[KA-ETYMON-C26-BELAGGE-01, KA-LEX-C07-OMBATTU-01, KA-ETYMON-C18-GANTE-01, KA-ETYMON-C27-SANJE-01, KA-LEX-C07-AIDU-01, KA-LEX-C211-KALA211-03, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01] -->

> **ನಾನು ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಒಂಬತ್ತು ಗಂಟೆಯಿಂದ ಸಂಜೆ ಐದು ಗಂಟೆಯವರೆಗೆ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತೇನೆ.**
>
> *nānu beḷagge ombattu gaṇṭeyinda sañje aidu gaṇṭeyavarege kelasa māḍuttēne.* — "I work from nine in the morning until five in the evening."

**-ಇಂದ** marks where the stretch starts, **ವರೆಗೆ** where it ends: **ಗಂಟೆಯಿಂದ** … **ಗಂಟೆಯವರೆಗೆ**. The same pair works for places: **ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದವರೆಗೆ** (*illinda nildāṇadavarege*, "from here to the station").
