## You'll want to know: बन्धनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C350-MUKTI350-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बन्धनम्** — *bandhanam* — "captivity; a bond".

> पक्षी बन्धनं न इच्छति।
>
> *pakṣī bandhanaṃ na icchati.*
>
> A bird does not want captivity.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बन्धनम्** (*bandhanam*) comes from the root √bandh, "to bind"; MW gives *bandhana* "the act of binding, tying, fettering" (MW).
