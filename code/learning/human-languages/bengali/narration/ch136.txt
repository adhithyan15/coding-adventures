Bengali, chapter 136: Deep, Wide, Narrow, Round, Purple.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গভীর (gôbhīr) — deep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for fast, then the Bengali for slow.

You'll want to know: গভীর.
গভীর — gôbhīr — "deep".

The word, taken apart.
গভীর (gôbhīr) is Sanskrit gabhīra, "deep", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 4024). The word inherited from it is gahir, which CDIAL lists.

Lesson 2 of 5.
চওড়া (chôoṛā) — wide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for slow, then the Bengali for deep.

You'll want to know: চওড়া.
চওড়া — chôoṛā — "wide".

The word, taken apart.
চওড়া (chôoṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ca-uḍa, "wide, open", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4534). CDIAL suggests it may be built on caü-, the "four" of compounds.

Lesson 5 of 5.
বেগুনি (beguni) — purple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for narrow, then the Bengali for round.

You'll want to know: বেগুনি.
বেগুনি — beguni — "purple".

The word, taken apart.
বেগুনি (beguni) is begun, "aubergine", with the ending -i: "aubergine-coloured". CDIAL lists Bengali bāguniyā, "purple", as derived from the word for the aubergine, Sanskrit vātiṅgaṇa (CDIAL 11503).
