## You'll want to know: shénhuà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C328-LEGEND328-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shénhuà* — "myth".

> *wǒ xiǎoshíhou tīng guo yí ge shénhuà* — When I was little I heard a myth.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shénhuà* is built from *shén* 'a god' and 话 'talk', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zyin* and *hwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a spirit' and the second 'talk'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
