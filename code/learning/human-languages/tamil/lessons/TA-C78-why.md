## You'll want to know: ஏன்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C78-WHY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ஏன்** (*ēṉ*) — "why".

Chapter forty gave you the shape of every Tamil question word: they begin on an **e-**.
என்ன, எங்கே, எப்படி, எவ்வளவு. ஏன் belongs to the same family, and it is the one that asks
for a reason.

It holds a long **ஏ** where the others hold a short **எ**. The family is real; the length is
part of this particular member.

On its own it is a whole question:

> **ஏன்?** — "Why?"

Or in front of a sentence:

> **ஏன் தேநீர் வேண்டும்?** — "Why do you want tea?"

This is the first question in the book that cannot be answered by naming a thing. Its answer
is a sentence, and the next lesson is the word that lets you give one.
