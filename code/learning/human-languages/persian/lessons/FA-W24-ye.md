# ی (ye) — the letter inside چیست

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-CHE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write چ and پ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **چیست** (*chist*), "what is?".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ی** — *ye*, the sound *y*, or a long *i*.

It is inside **چیست** (*chist*), where it gives the long *i*. Persian **ی** changes
its coat by position: **two dots below** at the start or in the middle of a word,
and **no dots** when it ends a word or stands alone, which is the shape this
lesson writes.

## Writing: ی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: sweep left from the upper right and descend through the S curve
- **2.** continue around the below-baseline bowl and finish at its rising tip without lifting — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 0.** The pen never leaves the paper.

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, closing ی demonstration at 02:55–02:58 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-CHE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **چیست** and point at ی inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ی three times, saying *y* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: پ, then ی, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **چیست** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-CHE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ی? (**ye**, *y*.) Which word did it
come from? (**چیست**, *chist*, "what is?".)
