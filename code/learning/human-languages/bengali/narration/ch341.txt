Bengali, chapter 341: Bending the Rules.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
লঙ্ঘন করা (lôngghôn kôrā) — to violate; to break (a law, an agreement).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to carry out, then the Bengali for to enforce.

You'll want to know: লঙ্ঘন করা.
লঙ্ঘন করা — lôngghôn kôrā — "to violate; to break (a law, an agreement)".
কোম্পানিটা চুক্তি লঙ্ঘন করল। kompāniṭā chukti lôngghôn kôrlô. The company broke the contract.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নিয়মটা লঙ্ঘন করলেন, tini niyômṭā lôngghôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
লঙ্ঘন করা (lôngghôn kôrā) is Sanskrit laṅghana, leaping over and so overstepping, a violation (Monier-Williams), with করা: to step across the line a law draws.

You'll want to know: অমান্য করা.
অমান্য করা — ômānyô kôrā — "to disobey; to defy".
ছাত্ররা নিয়ম অমান্য করল। chhātrôrā niyôm ômānyô kôrlô. The students disobeyed the rules.
Past (he/she …): তিনি আইন অমান্য করলেন, tini āin ômānyô kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
অমান্য করা (ômānyô kôrā) is Sanskrit a- 'not' with mānya, 'to be respected' (Monier-Williams), and করা: to treat a rule as not worth heeding, on purpose.

Lesson 3 of 5.
জালিয়াতি (jāliyāti) — forgery; fraud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to violate, then the Bengali for to disobey.

You'll want to know: জালিয়াতি.
জালিয়াতি — jāliyāti — "forgery; fraud".
পুলিশ একটা বড় জালিয়াতি ধরল। pulish ekṭā bôṛo jāliyāti dhôrlô. The police uncovered a big fraud.

The word, taken apart.
জালিয়াতি (jāliyāti) is built on জাল, 'counterfeit', usually traced to Arabic jaʿl, fabrication, through Persian; it is a different word from জাল 'a net'. The -ইয়াতি ending makes the act.

Lesson 4 of 5.
দুর্নীতি (durnīti) — corruption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to disobey, then the Bengali for forgery.

You'll want to know: দুর্নীতি.
দুর্নীতি — durnīti — "corruption".
দুর্নীতির বিরুদ্ধে সবাই একমত। durnītir biruddhe sôbāi ekmôt. Everyone is united against corruption.

The word, taken apart.
দুর্নীতি (durnīti) is Sanskrit durnīti, dur- 'bad' with nīti 'conduct, policy': bad government (Monier-Williams). Bengali narrowed it to bribes and abuse of office.

Lesson 5 of 5.
অন্যায় (ônyāy) — unfair; an injustice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for forgery, then the Bengali for corruption.

You'll want to know: অন্যায়.
অন্যায় — ônyāy — "unfair; an injustice".
এই শাস্তিটা অন্যায়। ei shāstiṭā ônyāy. This punishment is unfair.

The word, taken apart.
অন্যায় (ônyāy) is Sanskrit anyāya, a- 'not' with nyāya 'what is right': unjust action (Monier-Williams). It works as a noun, a wrong, and as an adjective, unfair.
