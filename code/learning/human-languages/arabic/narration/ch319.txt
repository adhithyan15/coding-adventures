Arabic, chapter 319: Mourning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عزاء (ʿazāʾ) — condolence; a gathering of mourners.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a temple, then the Arabic for a pyramid.

You'll want to know: عزاء.
عزاء — ʿazāʾ — "condolence; a gathering of mourners".
ذهبنا إلى بيت العزاء مساء أمس. dhahabnā ilā bayt al-ʿazāʾ masāʾa ams. We went to the house of mourning yesterday evening.
بيت العزاء is the home where the family receives visitors after a death.

The word, taken apart.
عزاء (ʿazāʾ) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-z-y (Lane), where ʿazāʾ is patience in the face of loss. To offer it to a family is to wish them that patience.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: عزى.
عزى — ʿazzā — "to console, to offer condolences".
عزى الجيران الأسرة بعد الجنازة. ʿazzā al-jīrān al-usra baʿda al-janāza. The neighbours offered the family their condolences after the funeral.
Present: يعزي, yuʿazzī.
عزى (ʿazzā) someone في the person who died: عزاه في أبيه.

The word, taken apart.
عزى (ʿazzā) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-z-y (Lane), patience under loss. It is Form II, "to bid someone be patient": to console.

Lesson 3 of 5.
رثى (rathā) — to lament, to mourn in verse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for condolence, then the Arabic for to console.

You'll want to know: رثى.
رثى — rathā — "to lament, to mourn in verse".
رثى الشاعر صديقه في قصيدة طويلة. rathā ash-shāʿir ṣadīqahu fī qaṣīda ṭawīla. The poet mourned his friend in a long poem.
Present: يرثي, yarthī.
A poem of mourning is رثاء, an elegy.

The word, taken apart.
رثى (rathā) is built on the native Arabic root r-th-y (Lane), "to weep for the dead and recount his good deeds"; rithāʾ, the elegy, is one of the oldest kinds of Arabic poem.

Lesson 5 of 5.
حداد (ḥidād) — mourning, a period of mourning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to lament, then the Arabic for to announce a death.

You'll want to know: حداد.
حداد — ḥidād — "mourning, a period of mourning".
لبست الأسرة الأسود في أيام الحداد. labisat al-usra al-aswad fī ayyām al-ḥidād. The family wore black in the days of mourning.
أعلنت الحكومة الحداد ثلاثة أيام: three days of national mourning.

The word, taken apart.
حداد (ḥidād) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-d-d (Lane), the root of ḥadd, a limit, and of ḥadīd, iron. Lane gives ḥidād as a widow's holding back from finery and perfume: mourning.
