## Chapter 254 says "a female friend", not "a"

*सहेली* was glossed "a (girl's) friend". The chapter title and every prompt
that names a word take the gloss up to its first bracket, so the title read
"A, A companion, A relative, A mother-in-law, A father-in-law", the goal read
"I can say a, a companion, …", and three lessons asked the learner to "say
the Hindi for a". The gloss is now "a female friend (of a girl)". The title,
the goal, the warm-ups and recalls of *साथी* and *संबंधी*, and the chapter
252-255 review all read "a female friend". No word, atom or lesson count
changed.
