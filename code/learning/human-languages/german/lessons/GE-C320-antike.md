## You'll want to know: die Antike
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C320-MITTELALTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Antike** — "antiquity, the ancient world".

> Der Tempel ist aus der Antike.
>
> *The temple dates from antiquity.*

No plural for the period. *Antik* — antique.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Antike* is built on *antik* "ancient", borrowed from French *antique*, from Latin *antīquus* "old, former" (EtymDB).
