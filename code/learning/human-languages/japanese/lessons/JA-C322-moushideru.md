## You'll want to know: もうしでる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C322-SHOUCHI322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**もうしでる** — *mōshideru* — "to come forward; to offer".

> **あには じかんが あったので、もうしでました。** — *ani wa jikan ga atta node, mōshidemashita* — "My brother had time, so he offered to help."

Polite **もうしでます**, *mōshidemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あにが もうしでました。**, *ani ga mōshidemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もうしでる** (*mōshideru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *mōsu*, "to say (humble)" and *deru*, "to come out", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
