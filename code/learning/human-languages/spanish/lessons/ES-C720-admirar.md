## You'll want to know: admirar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C720-GRACIAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**admirar** — "to admire".

> Admiro mucho a mi profesora.
>
> *I really admire my teacher.*

Takes personal *a* with people: *admiro a mi madre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*admirar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *admīrārī* "to wonder at" (EtymDB); *mirar* "to look" is the inherited form of *mīrārī*.
