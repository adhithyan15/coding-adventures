## You'll want to know: il tamponamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C413-INGORGO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il tamponamento** — "the rear-end collision, the pile-up".

> C'è stato un tamponamento in tangenziale.
>
> *There was a pile-up on the ring road.*

*Un tamponamento a catena* — a multi-car pile-up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tamponamento* is built on *tamponare* 'to bump into', from *tampone* 'plug', which was borrowed from French *tampon*.
