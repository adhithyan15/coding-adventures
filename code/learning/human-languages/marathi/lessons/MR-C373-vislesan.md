## You'll want to know: विश्लेषण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C373-PADDHAT373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्लेषण** — *viśleṣaṇ* — "an analysis".

> तिने आकडेवारीचं नीट विश्लेषण केलं.
>
> *tine ākḍevārīcaṁ nīṭ viśleṣaṇ kelaṁ.*
>
> She analysed the figures carefully.

Neuter.

विश्लेषण करणे, to analyse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्लेषण** (*viśleṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *viśleṣaṇa*, "separating, dissolving" (Monier-Williams).
