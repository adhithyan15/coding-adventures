## You'll want to know: آسودہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C447-CALM447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**آسودہ** — *āsūdā* — "at ease; content".

> اچھا کھانا کھا کر سب آسودہ تھے۔
>
> *achchhā khānā khā kar sab āsūdā the.*
>
> After a good meal everyone was content.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آسودہ** (*āsūdā*) is usually traced to Persian *āsūda*, "rested, at ease".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
