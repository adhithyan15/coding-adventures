# সংকল্প (sôngkôlpô) — a resolve; a firm intention

## You'll want to know: সংকল্প
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C379-SONGKOLPO379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সংকল্প** — *sôngkôlpô* — "a resolve; a firm intention".

> সংকল্প থাকলে কাজ শেষ হবেই।
>
> *sôngkôlpô thākle kāj shesh hôbei.*
>
> If you are determined, the work will get done.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংকল্প** (*sôngkôlpô*) is Sanskrit *saṃkalpa*, a firm intention formed in the mind (Monier-Williams).
