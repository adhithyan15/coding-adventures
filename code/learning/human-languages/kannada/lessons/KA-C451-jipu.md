## You'll want to know: ಜೀಪು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C451-VAHANA451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೀಪು** — *jīpu* — "a jeep".

> ಕಾಡಿನ ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಜೀಪು ಮಾತ್ರ ಹೋಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *kāḍina dāriyalli jīpu mātra hōguttade.*
>
> Only a jeep can go on the forest track.

Written with a final -ು, as older loans often are.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೀಪು** (*jīpu*) is a loan from English *jeep*, written with a final -*u*.
