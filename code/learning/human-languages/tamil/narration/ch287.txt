Tamil, chapter 287: Must, Must Not and Because.
3 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
போக வேண்டும் (pōka vēṇṭum) — must, must not, no need.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I want water". (eṉakkut taṇṇīr vēṇṭum.) Then "let's go". (pōkalām.)
[pause 1 second]
Both hold a piece this lesson takes out.

You'll want to know: the to-form and வேண்டும்.
போகலாம் is போக, "to go", with -லாம், "may". போக is the to-form of போ. Put வேண்டும் after it and you have "must":
நான் இன்று மருத்துவரைப் பார்க்க வேண்டும். nāṉ iṉṟu maruttuvaraip pārkka vēṇṭum. — "I have to see a doctor today."
The same வேண்டும் as எனக்குத் தண்ணீர் வேண்டும்: there "is wanted" takes a thing, and the one who wants takes -க்கு. Here it takes an action, and the person stands in front plainly: நான், நீங்கள்.
Learn each to-form with its verb:
போ becomes போக (pōka); வா becomes வர (vara); இரு becomes இருக்க (irukka).
பார் becomes பார்க்க (pārkka); படி becomes படிக்க (paṭikka); கொடு becomes கொடுக்க (koṭukka).
செய் becomes செய்ய (ceyya); சாப்பிடு becomes சாப்பிட (cāppiṭa); பேசு becomes பேச (pēca).
In speech, போக வேண்டும் (pōka vēṇṭum) shrinks to போகணும்; the written form keeps the long one.

You'll want to know: கூடாது and வேண்டாம்.
இரவில் தனியாகப் போகக் கூடாது. iravil taṉiyākap pōkak kūṭātu. — "You must not go alone at night."
கூடாது (kūṭātu, "must not, it is not right") forbids, and it does not change for the person. After the to-form the first letter doubles in writing: போகக் கூடாது, சாப்பிடக் கூடாது.
நீங்கள் நாளை வர வேண்டாம். nīṅkaḷ nāḷai vara vēṇṭām. — "You don't need to come tomorrow."
வேண்டாம், "is not wanted", releases: "no need to", or a gentle "don't". கூடாது is a ban; வேண்டாம் is advice.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I have to see a doctor today" — nāṉ iṉṟu maruttuvaraip pārkka vēṇṭum]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You must not go alone at night" — iravil taṉiyākap pōkak kūṭātu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You don't need to come tomorrow" — nīṅkaḷ nāḷai vara vēṇṭām]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: one thing you must do this week, and one thing you must not]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I must go"? (நான் போக வேண்டும் (pōka vēṇṭum).) "You must not eat"? (சாப்பிடக் கூடாது.) "No need to come"? (வர வேண்டாம்.) Which one forbids, and which one advises? (கூடாது forbids; வேண்டாம் advises.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
வந்ததால் (vantatāl) — because it happened.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I have to see a doctor today." (nāṉ iṉṟu maruttuvaraip pārkka vēṇṭum.) Then "So I stayed at home." (ataṉāl nāṉ vīṭṭil iruntēṉ.)

You'll want to know: -ததால், 'because (it) did'.
மழை பெய்ததால் நான் வரவில்லை. maḻai peytatāl nāṉ varavillai. — "Because it rained, I did not come."
பெய்ததால் is three pieces. பெய்த is the past with -அ, the piece you met in வந்த போது; அது, "it", turns the event into a thing; -ஆல், "because of", is the -ஆல் of அதனால். "Because of its having rained."
எனக்குக் காய்ச்சல் இருந்ததால் நான் பள்ளிக்குப் போகவில்லை. eṉakkuk kāyccal iruntatāl nāṉ paḷḷikkup pōkavillai. — "Because I had a fever, I did not go to school."
The reason comes first and the result after it, in one sentence: வந்ததால் (vantatāl), போனதால், இருந்ததால்.

You'll want to know: the reason after: ஏனென்றால், and காரணம்.
நான் வரவில்லை. ஏனென்றால் மழை பெய்தது. nāṉ varavillai. ēṉeṉṟāl maḻai peytatu. — "I did not come. Because it rained."
The same reason in the other order: the result first, then ஏனென்றால் opens a second sentence with the cause. In speech you hear ஏன்னா.
அதற்குக் காரணம் மழை. ataṟkuk kāraṇam maḻai. — "The reason for that is the rain."
அதற்கு (ataṟku, "for that") is அது with -க்கு; காரணம், "the reason", stands in the middle, and the reason itself comes last.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Because it rained, I did not come" — maḻai peytatāl nāṉ varavillai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Because I had a fever, I did not go to school" — eṉakkuk kāyccal iruntatāl nāṉ paḷḷikkup pōkavillai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I did not come. Because it rained." — nāṉ varavillai. ēṉeṉṟāl maḻai peytatu.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "The reason for that is the rain" — ataṟkuk kāraṇam maḻai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Because it rained"? (மழை பெய்ததால்.) "Because he came"? (அவன் வந்ததால் (vantatāl).) Which word puts the reason after the result? (ஏனென்றால்.) "The reason for that"? (அதற்குக் காரணம்.)
