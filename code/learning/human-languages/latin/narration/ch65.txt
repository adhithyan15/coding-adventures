Latin, chapter 65: Hair, Belly, Back, Forehead, Beard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
capillus, capillī — a hair, the hair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for the heart, then the Latin for blood.

You'll want to know: capillus, capillī.
capillus, capillī — "a hair, the hair". A capillary is hair-thin.

What you've built.
One more word for hair, belly, back, forehead, beard.
