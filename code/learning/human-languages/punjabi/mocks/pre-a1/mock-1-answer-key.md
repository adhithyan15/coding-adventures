### Part 3 — bounded independent writing

7. Accept the learner's chosen name when it is independently written in
   recognizable Gurmukhi after **ਨਾਂ:**. Do not require a particular name.
8. Accept an appropriate independently written taught greeting, including
   **ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ**, **ਨਮਸਤੇ**, or **ਸਲਾਮ**. Do not accept a romanized or
   visibly copied answer.

## Speaking

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
### Part 1 — greeting and identity

1. Accept **ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ**, **ਨਮਸਤੇ**, **ਸਲਾਮ**, or an equally appropriate
   taught greeting.
2. Accept a chosen identity in a comprehensible frame such as **ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਂ … ਹੈ।**
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
### Part 2 — familiar questions

3. Accept a relevant state such as **ਮੈਂ ਠੀਕ ਹਾਂ।**
4. Accept a comprehensible taught language statement, with gender agreement
   appropriate to the speaker, such as **ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** or **ਮੈਂ
   ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ।**
5. Accept a comprehensible taught residence statement, with gender agreement
   appropriate to the speaker, such as **ਮੈਂ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਸਰ ਰਹਿੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** or **ਮੈਂ
   ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਸਰ ਰਹਿੰਦੀ ਹਾਂ।**
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->
### Part 3 — short request

6. Accept a comprehensible polite water request containing **ਪਾਣੀ** and a
   taught courtesy signal such as **ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰਕੇ** or **ਮਿਹਰਬਾਨੀ ਕਰਕੇ**.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->

The productive examples guide scoring; they are not scripts the learner must
reproduce word for word. Accept internally consistent taught pronunciation
variants.
