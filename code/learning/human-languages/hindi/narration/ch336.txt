Hindi, chapter 336: The Curse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तपस्या (tapasyā) — penance; austerity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for immortal, then the Hindi for to swallow.

You'll want to know: तपस्या.
तपस्या — tapasyā — "penance; austerity".
ऋषि ने जंगल में बारह साल तपस्या की। ṛṣi ne jaṅgal meṁ bārah sāl tapasyā kī. The sage did penance in the forest for twelve years.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तपस्या (tapasyā) is Sanskrit tapasyā (Monier-Williams), from tapas, heat and then the hard discipline of a holy life.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कोसना (kosnā) — to curse; to rail at.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for penance, then the Hindi for a curse.

You'll want to know: कोसना.
कोसना — kosnā — "to curse; to rail at".
बूढ़ी औरत चोर को कोस रही थी। būṛhī aurat cor ko kos rahī thī. The old woman was cursing the thief.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चोर को कोसा, usne cor ko kosā.

The word, taken apart.
कोसना (kosnā) goes back to Sanskrit krośati, "cries out" (CDIAL 3612). It is everyday cursing in words, not a sage's शाप (śāp).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पछताना (pachtānā) — to regret; to repent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a curse, then the Hindi for to curse.

You'll want to know: पछताना.
पछताना — pachtānā — "to regret; to repent".
शाप के बाद राजा बहुत पछताया। śāp ke bād rājā bahut pachtāyā. After the curse the king bitterly regretted what he had done.
Past (he …): वह पछताया, vah pachtāyā.

The word, taken apart.
पछताना (pachtānā) is inherited from paścōttāpa, "repentance", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 8010): a burning that comes after, from paścā, behind, and tāpa, heat.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रूठना (rūṭhnā) — to sulk; to take offence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to curse, then the Hindi for to regret.

You'll want to know: रूठना.
रूठना — rūṭhnā — "to sulk; to take offence".
रानी राजा से रूठ गई। rānī rājā se rūṭh gaī. The queen took offence at the king.
Past (she …): वह रूठ गई, vah rūṭh gaī.

The word, taken apart.
रूठना (rūṭhnā) goes back to Sanskrit ruṣṭa, "angry, offended" (CDIAL 10791). It is a quiet anger that waits to be soothed with मनाना.
