## You'll want to know: das Stadttor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C438-ALTSTADT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Stadttor** — "the town gate, the city gate".

> Durch das alte Stadttor fahren keine Autos.
>
> *No cars drive through the old town gate.*

Plural: *die Stadttore*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Stadttor* is built from *Stadt* 'town' and *Tor* 'gate', both inherited: *Tor* goes back through Middle High German to Old High German *tor* (EtymDB).
