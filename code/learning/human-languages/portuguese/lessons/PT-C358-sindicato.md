## You'll want to know: o sindicato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C358-GREVE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o sindicato** — "the trade union".

> Ela entrou para o sindicato no primeiro ano.
>
> *She joined the union in her first year.*

*O sindicato dos professores* — the teachers' union.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sindicato* is built on *síndico* "representative", from Latin *syndicus*, from Greek *súndikos* "advocate"; French *syndicat* "trade union", from Medieval Latin *syndicātus*, shaped the modern sense (EtymDB).
