## You'll want to know: दबना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C303-BHUKAMP303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दबना** — *dabnā* — "to be pressed down; to be trapped under".

> दीवार गिरी और दो बकरियाँ उसके नीचे दब गईं।
>
> *dīvār girī aur do bakriyā̃ uske nīce dab gaīṁ.*
>
> The wall fell and two goats were trapped under it.

Past (he …): वह आदमी दब गया, *vah ādmī dab gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दबना** (*dabnā*) is inherited from *\*dabb*, "press", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6173). It is the intransitive partner of **दबाना**, to press: what is pressed down **दबता है**.
