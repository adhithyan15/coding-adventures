## You'll want to know: శిల్పం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C542-SILPAM542-03]; assesses=[] -->

**శిల్పం** — *śilpaṁ* — "a sculpture; a carving".

> ఆ గుడిలో అందమైన శిల్పాలు ఉన్నాయి.
>
> *ā guḍilō andamaina śilpālu unnāyi.*
>
> There are beautiful sculptures in that temple.

Plural: శిల్పాలు, *śilpālu*.

శిల్పకళ, the art of sculpture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శిల్పం** (*śilpaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śilpa*, "artistic work; manual craft" (CDIAL 12470).
