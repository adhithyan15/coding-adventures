# Two turns: why, and because

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-JOIN-KYON-01, HI-JOIN-KI-01, HI-JOIN-YA-01, HI-JOIN-LEKIN-01, HI-GRAMMAR-VERB-FINAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 5s] **क्योंकि** is two words you already had. Say them apart, then
together, then say where the verb of each half sits.

Reach back three chapters while you are here: **या** offers a choice and
**लेकिन** contradicts. Neither can give a reason, which is what this chapter
added.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-JOIN-KYONKI-01, HI-JOIN-KYON-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which word asks for a reason, and which gives one? (**क्यों** asks,
**क्योंकि** gives.) What is the second one made of? (**The first one, plus कि**.)
Then stop.
