## You'll want to know: あまやどり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C415-YUUDACHI415-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あまやどり** — *amayadori* — "sheltering from the rain".

> **えきで あまやどりを しました。** — *eki de amayadori o shimashita* — "I sheltered from the rain at the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あまやどり** (*amayadori*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ama*, "rain" (from *ame*) and *yadori*, "lodging"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
