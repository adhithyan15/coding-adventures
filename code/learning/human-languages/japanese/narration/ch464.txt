Japanese, chapter 464: At the Match.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
かんきゃく (kankyaku) — spectators; the audience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to bounce, then the Japanese for to vie.

You'll want to know: かんきゃく.
かんきゃく — kankyaku — "spectators; the audience".
かんきゃくが おうえんしています。 — kankyaku ga ōen shite imasu — "The spectators are cheering."

The word, taken apart.
かんきゃく (kankyaku) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kan, "view" (Middle Chinese kwan) and kyaku, "guest" (khaek) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.
せめる (semeru) — to attack (in a game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a referee, then the Japanese for points scored.

You'll want to know: せめる.
せめる — semeru — "to attack (in a game)".
さいごまで せめて ください。 — saigo made semete kudasai — "Please keep attacking to the end."
Polite せめます, sememasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): せんしゅが せめました。, senshu ga sememashita.

The word, taken apart.
せめる (semeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
