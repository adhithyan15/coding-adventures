## You'll want to know: गूँजना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C318-DHAMAKA318-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गूँजना** — *gū̃jnā* — "to echo; to resound".

> धमाका पूरे शहर में गूँजा।
>
> *dhamākā pūre śahar meṁ gū̃jā.*
>
> The blast echoed through the whole city.

Past (it …): वह घंटा गूँजा, *vah ghaṇṭā gū̃jā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गूँजना** (*gū̃jnā*) goes back to Sanskrit *guñjati*, "hums, buzzes" (CDIAL 4175). A sound **गूँजता है** when it fills a place and comes back.
