# पुष्टि करना (puṣṭi karnā) — to confirm

## You'll want to know: पुष्टि करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C349-INKAR349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पुष्टि करना** — *puṣṭi karnā* — "to confirm".

> डाक्टर ने ख़बर की पुष्टि की।
>
> *ḍākṭar ne khabar kī puṣṭi kī.*
>
> The doctor confirmed the news.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने ख़बर की पुष्टि की, *usne khabar kī puṣṭi kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुष्टि करना** (*puṣṭi karnā*) joins पुष्टि to करना, "to do". पुष्टि is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *puṣṭi*, "growth, thriving" (Monier-Williams). The Hindi sense, a confirmation, is not among the senses Monier-Williams gives.

## What you've built
