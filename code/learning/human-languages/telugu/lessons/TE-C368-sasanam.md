## You'll want to know: శాసనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C368-SASANAM368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**శాసనం** — *śāsanaṁ* — "an inscription; an edict".

> ఆ రాతి మీద ఒక పాత శాసనం ఉంది.
>
> *ā rāti mīda oka pāta śāsanaṁ undi.*
>
> There is an old inscription on that stone.

Kings carved their orders and gifts on stone and copper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శాసనం** (*śāsanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śāsana*, "a command; a royal edict" (CDIAL 12420).
