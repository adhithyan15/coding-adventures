Persian, chapter 438: Checking in and out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تحویل گرفتن (tahvil gereftan) — to receive, to take delivery of; to check in (to a room).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a suite, then the Persian for to stay.

You'll want to know: تحویل گرفتن.
تحویل گرفتن (tahvil gereftan) — "to receive, to take delivery of; to check in (to a room)".
ساعت دو اتاق را تحویل گرفتیم. Sâ'at-e do otâq râ tahvil gereftim. We got our room at two o'clock.
The opposite of tahvil dâdan 'to hand over'. Colloquially also to make a fuss of someone.

The word, taken apart.
tahvil gereftan is built from tahvil 'handing over', a loan usually traced to Arabic taḥwīl 'transfer, turning over' (EtymDB does not list it), and the native verb gereftan 'to take', usually traced to Old Persian grab-.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تسویه حساب کردن (tasviye hesâb kardan) — to settle the bill, to settle up; to check out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to stay, then the Persian for to receive.

You'll want to know: تسویه حساب کردن.
تسویه حساب کردن (tasviye hesâb kardan) — "to settle the bill, to settle up; to check out".
صبح با هتل تسویه حساب کردیم و رفتیم. Sobh bâ hotel tasviye hesâb kardim va raftim. In the morning we settled up with the hotel and left.
tasviye 'settling' + hesâb 'account' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
tasviye hesâb kardan is built from tasviye 'levelling, settling', a loan usually traced to Arabic taswiya, hesâb 'account', borrowed from Arabic ḥisāb (EtymDB), and kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
کارت شناسایی (kârt-e shenâsâyi) — an identity card, an ID.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to receive, then the Persian for to settle the bill.

You'll want to know: کارت شناسایی.
کارت شناسایی (kârt-e shenâsâyi) — "an identity card, an ID".
در پذیرش کارت شناسایی مرا خواستند. Dar paziresh kârt-e shenâsâyi-ye marâ khâstand. At reception they asked for my ID card.
shenâsâyi 'identification', from shenâkhtan 'to know, to recognize'.

The word, taken apart.
kârt-e shenâsâyi is built from kârt 'card', which EtymDB takes from French carte, and native shenâsâyi 'identification', from shenâs, the present stem of shenâkhtan 'to know, to recognize'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پیش پرداخت (pishpardâkht) — a deposit, an advance payment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to settle the bill, then the Persian for an identity card.

You'll want to know: پیش پرداخت.
پیش پرداخت (pishpardâkht) — "a deposit, an advance payment".
برای رزرو اتاق پیش پرداخت دادیم. Barâ-ye rezerv-e otâq pishpardâkht dâdim. We paid a deposit to book the room.
pish 'before' + pardâkht 'payment'.

The word, taken apart.
pishpardâkht is a native compound of pish 'before', usually traced to Middle Persian pēš, and pardâkht 'payment', the past stem of pardâkhtan 'to pay'.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an identity card, then the Persian for a deposit.

You'll want to know: گاوصندوق.
گاوصندوق (gâvsanduq) — "a safe, a strongbox".
پولمان را در گاوصندوق هتل گذاشتیم. Pulemân râ dar gâvsanduq-e hotel gozâshtim. We put our money in the hotel safe.
gâv 'ox', here 'big and strong', + sanduq 'chest'.

The word, taken apart.
gâvsanduq is built from native gâv 'ox', used for things that are big and heavy, and sanduq 'chest, box', borrowed from Arabic ṣundūq (EtymDB).
