## You'll want to know: بھیس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C328-SHAHZADA328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بھیس** — *bhes* — "a disguise".

> اس نے کسان کا بھیس بدلا۔
>
> *us ne kisān kā bhes badlā.*
>
> He disguised himself as a farmer.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھیس** (*bhes*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bhes*, "dress, an assumed appearance", under Sanskrit *vēṣa* (CDIAL 12129). **بھیس بدلنا** is to put on a disguise.
