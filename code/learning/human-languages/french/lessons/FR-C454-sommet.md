## You'll want to know: le sommet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C454-RELIEF-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le sommet** — "the summit, the top; the summit meeting".

> Nous sommes arrivés au sommet à midi.
>
> *We reached the summit at noon.*

*Un sommet européen* — a European summit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sommet* comes from Old French *somet* (EtymDB), a small *som* 'top', from Latin *summum* 'highest point', from *summus* 'highest'.
