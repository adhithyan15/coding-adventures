## You'll want to know: मेंहदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C248-MEHDI248-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मेंहदी** — *mẽhdī* — "henna".

> बहन हाथ माथै मेंहदी लगावै है।
>
> *bahan hāth māthai mẽhdī lagāvai hai.*
>
> My sister puts henna on her hands.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेंहदी** (*mẽhdī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mendhī*, the henna plant. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *mẽhdī* (CDIAL 10328).
