## You'll want to know: আহত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C278-DHAKKA278-05]; assesses=[] -->

**আহত** — *āhôtô* — "injured; hurt".

> দুর্ঘটনায় অনেক মানুষ আহত হল।
>
> *durghôṭônāy ônek mānush āhôtô hôlô.*
>
> Many people were injured in the accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আহত** (*āhôtô*) is Sanskrit *āhata*, 'struck, beaten, hurt' (Monier-Williams), taken over whole. আহত হওয়া, to be injured.
