## You'll want to know: смена
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C360-SOVESHCHANIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**смена** (*sména*) — "a shift (at work); a change".

> Сегодня у медсестры ночная смена.
>
> *Segódnya u medsestrý nochnáya sména.*
>
> Today the nurse has a night shift.

Feminine. *Работать в две смены* — to work in two shifts. *Смена погоды* — a change in the weather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**смена** is built on *менять* "to change", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*měniti*: a shift is one team changing places with another.
