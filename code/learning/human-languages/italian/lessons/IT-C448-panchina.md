## You'll want to know: la panchina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C448-ARREDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la panchina** — "the bench (in a park); the substitutes' bench".

> Ci sediamo su una panchina al sole.
>
> *We sit on a bench in the sun.*

In football, *in panchina* — on the bench.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*panchina* is a little *panca* 'bench', which was borrowed from Lombardic *panch* 'bench' (EtymDB), a Germanic word.
