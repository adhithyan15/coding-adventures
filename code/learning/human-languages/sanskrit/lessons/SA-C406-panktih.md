## You'll want to know: पंक्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C406-ADHIROHA406-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पंक्तिः** — *paṅktiḥ* — "a queue; a line of people".

> जनाः यानपत्राय पंक्तौ तिष्ठन्ति।
>
> *janāḥ yānapatrāya paṅktau tiṣṭhanti.*
>
> People stand in a queue for tickets.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पंक्तिः** (*paṅktiḥ*) is built on *pañcan*, "five": MW gives *paṅkti* first as a set of five, then as a row, a line, a series; a queue is the row in modern use (MW).
