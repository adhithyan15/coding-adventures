## You'll want to know: l'héritage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C315-DEUIL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'héritage** — "the inheritance; the heritage".

> Il a reçu un petit héritage.
>
> *He received a small inheritance.*

Also a cultural legacy: *l'héritage des anciens*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*héritage* is built on *hériter* "to inherit" (EtymDB), from Late Latin *hērēditāre*, built on *hērēs* "heir".
