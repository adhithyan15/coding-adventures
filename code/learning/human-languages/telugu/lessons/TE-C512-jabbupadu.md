# జబ్బుపడు (jabbupaḍu) — to fall ill

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to remind, then the Telugu for a sickness.

## You'll want to know: జబ్బుపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C512-JABBU512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**జబ్బుపడు** — *jabbupaḍu* — "to fall ill".

> వర్షంలో తడిసి తమ్ముడు జబ్బుపడ్డాడు.
>
> *varṣaṁlō taḍisi tammuḍu jabbupaḍḍāḍu.*
>
> My younger brother got wet in the rain and fell ill.

Past (I …): జబ్బుపడ్డాను, *jabbupaḍḍānu*.

జబ్బు + పడు: to fall into illness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జబ్బుపడు** (*jabbupaḍu*) is native Dravidian: *jabbu*, "illness" (DEDR 2338), and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).
