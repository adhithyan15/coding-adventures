# ೯ — the digit for ombattu

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-147] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new shape: **೮** — which number is it?

[PAUSE 2s] One digit this time, and you already know its name.

## Script you'll notice: ೯
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-148]; assesses=[] -->

**೯** — **9**, the digit for **ಒಂಬತ್ತು** (*ombattu*), "nine".

Nine — the last of the shapes that stand alone. One more to go, and it is
the one that makes every larger number possible.

## Writing: ೯ — copy what you see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-148] -->

Put your pen on ೯ and follow its line. Copy the shape in front of you —
slowly, and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start the character or which way to
> travel**. That is taught with real variation from school to school, and it is
> not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying what
> you can see needs no such source.
