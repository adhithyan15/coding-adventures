## You'll want to know: die Epoche
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C269-JAHRZEHNT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Epoche** — "the era, the epoch".

> Das war eine schwierige Epoche für das Land.
>
> *That was a difficult era for the country.*

Plural: *die Epochen*. Three syllables: *E-po-che*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Epoche* was borrowed, through Late Latin *epocha*, from Greek *epokhē* "a holding back, a fixed point in time"; EtymDB lists no parent, so this follows the usual account.
