## You'll want to know: થરથરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C437-COLD437-05]; assesses=[] -->

**થરથરવું** — *thartharvũ* — "to shiver; to tremble all over".

> ટાઢમાં બાળકો થરથરતાં હતાં.
>
> *ṭāḍhmā̃ bāḷko tharthartā̃ hatā̃.*
>
> The children were shivering in the cold.

Past (he …): તે ટાઢમાં થરથર્યો, *te ṭāḍhmā̃ thartharyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થરથરવું** (*thartharvũ*) goes back to *\*thar*, "to tremble", a form CDIAL reconstructs and lists doubled in this verb; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to tremble" (CDIAL 6092).
