## You'll want to know: bàifǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C338-VISIT338-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bàifǎng* — "to pay a visit to".

> *guòjié de shíhou wǒmen qù bàifǎng le lǎoshī* — Over the festival we went to visit our teacher.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàifǎng* is built from *bài* 'to bow' and *fǎng* 'to call on', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *peajH* and *phjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to consult'.
