# વ્યાકુળ (vyākuḷ) — distraught

## You'll want to know: વ્યાકુળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C454-UCAT454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યાકુળ** — *vyākuḷ* — "distraught".

> બાળક ખોવાઈ ગયું ત્યારે માતા વ્યાકુળ થઈ ગઈ.
>
> *bāḷak khovāī gayũ tyāre mātā vyākuḷ thaī gaī.*
>
> When the child got lost, the mother was distraught.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યાકુળ** (*vyākuḷ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vyākula*, "entirely filled with, confused, bewildered", as Monier-Williams records it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
