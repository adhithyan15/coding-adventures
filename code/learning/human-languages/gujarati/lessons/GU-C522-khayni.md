## You'll want to know: ખાયણી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C522-KHAYNI522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાયણી** — *khāyṇī* — "a mortar (for pounding)".

> દાદીની ખાયણી પથ્થરની છે.
>
> *dādīnī khāyṇī paththarnī che.*
>
> Grandmother's mortar is made of stone.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાયણી** (*khāyṇī*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *khaṇḍana*, "breaking", with the Gujarati word for a mortar (CDIAL 3796).
