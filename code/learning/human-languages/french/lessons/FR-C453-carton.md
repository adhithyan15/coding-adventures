## You'll want to know: le carton
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C453-EMBALLAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le carton** — "the cardboard box; cardboard".

> Nous avons mis les livres dans des cartons.
>
> *We put the books in cardboard boxes.*

*Un carton jaune* — a yellow card, in sport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carton* was borrowed from Italian *cartone* 'thick paper' (EtymDB), from *carta*, which goes back to Latin *charta* 'sheet of papyrus'.
