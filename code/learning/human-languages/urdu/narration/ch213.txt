Urdu, chapter 213: To deliver, To lift, To drop, To throw, To pull.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پہنچانا (pahuṅchānā) — to deliver, to take (somewhere).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to boil, then the Urdu for to taste.

You'll want to know: پہنچانا.
پہنچانا — pahuṅchānā — "to deliver, to take (somewhere)".

The word, taken apart.
پہنچانا (pahuṅchānā) is the causing form of pahuṅchnā, "to arrive": to deliver is to make something arrive. CDIAL lists pahuṅchnā under Sanskrit prabhūta, "much, great", which Prakrit made into a verb, "is strong, reaches" (CDIAL 8716).

Lesson 2 of 5.
اٹھانا (uṭhānā) — to lift, to pick up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to taste, then the Urdu for to deliver.

You'll want to know: اٹھانا.
اٹھانا — uṭhānā — "to lift, to pick up".

The word, taken apart.
اٹھانا (uṭhānā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ut-sthāpayati, "raises", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 1903). It is built on sthā-, "to stand": to lift is to make something stand up.
