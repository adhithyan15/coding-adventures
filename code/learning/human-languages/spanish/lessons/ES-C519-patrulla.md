## You'll want to know: la patrulla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C519-POLICIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la patrulla** — "the patrol; the patrol car".

> Una patrulla llegó en cinco minutos.
>
> *A patrol car arrived in five minutes.*

*Estar de patrulla* — to be on patrol. *Patrullar* is to patrol.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patrulla* is built on *patrullar* 'to patrol', which was borrowed from French *patrouiller* (EtymDB). In Old French that verb meant 'to paddle, to paw about', and EtymDB builds it on *patte* 'a paw'. The patrol tramped round on foot.
