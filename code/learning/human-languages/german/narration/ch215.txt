Lesson 1 of 5.
gerecht — fair, even-handed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for stingy, then the German for cosy.

You'll want to know: gerecht.
gerecht — "fair, even-handed".

The word, taken apart.
gerecht from Middle High German gereht from Old High German gireht — inherited, built on the word for "right", Proto-Germanic rehtaz, English right. EtymDB 2.1 lists Gothic garaihts, "righteous", beside it.

gleichzeitig is gleich, "same", + Zeit, "time", + -ig: at the same time. Both are inherited; Zeit from Proto-Germanic tīdiz. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for divorced, then the German for at the same time.

You'll want to know: großzügig.
großzügig — "generous".

The word, taken apart.
großzügig is the word for "great" + Zug, here "a trait", + -ig: of great traits, generous. groß from Old High German grōz from Proto-Germanic grautaz. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 5 of 5.
günstig — good value, favourable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for at the same time, then the German for generous.

You'll want to know: günstig.
günstig — "good value, favourable".

The word, taken apart.
günstig is built on the word for "favour", Middle High German gunst, from the verb "to grant, not to begrudge" — inherited. Good value is a favourable price. EtymDB 2.1 has no source for it.
