## You'll want to know: o alcatrão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C410-CURVAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o alcatrão** — "tarmac, tar".

> A estrada nova tem alcatrão; a velha é de terra.
>
> *The new road is tarmac; the old one is dirt.*

In Brazil: *o asfalto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alcatrão* comes from Arabic *qaṭrān* 'tar' with the article *al-* (EtymDB), like Spanish *alquitrán*.
