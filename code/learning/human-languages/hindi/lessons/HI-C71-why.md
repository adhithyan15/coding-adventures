## You'll want to know: क्यों
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-05]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-04] -->

**क्यों** (*kyoṁ*) — "why".

The sixth of the asking words, and it opens on the same **क** as the other five.
The spelling stacks क on य with the vowel-killer, then puts ों on the end: the
ो matra and the dot that sends the vowel through the nose.

Now the piece that makes it useful twice over. Add **कि** and you get
**क्योंकि** (*kyoṁki*) — "because". कि on its own is the little word that means
"that", and क्योंकि is literally "why-that": the question turned round to face
the answer.

So one shape gives you both halves of a reason.

**आप क्यों नहीं आए?** asks it, and any answer that opens on **क्योंकि** is
giving the reason back. You will fill in what follows as your words grow.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Five: आज, भी, बहुत, कब, क्यों. Six asking words in all, and the way to answer
the last of them.
