## You'll want to know: schiantarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C299-SCONTRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**schiantarsi** — "to crash into something".

> La moto si è schiantata contro un muro.
>
> *The motorbike crashed into a wall.*

*Schiantarsi contro* qualcosa.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schiantarsi* is built on *schiantare* "to split, to break with a crack"; its origin is not settled (EtymDB has no entry).
