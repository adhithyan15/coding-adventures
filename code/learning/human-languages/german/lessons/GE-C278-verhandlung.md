## You'll want to know: die Verhandlung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C278-VERHANDELN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Verhandlung** — "the negotiation; the hearing (in court)".

> Die Verhandlung dauerte bis in die Nacht.
>
> *The negotiation went on into the night.*

Plural: *die Verhandlungen*. In court, the hearing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Verhandlung* is built on *verhandeln*, from *handeln* "to deal", which EtymDB derives from Proto-Germanic *handuz* "hand".
