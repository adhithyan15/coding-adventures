## You'll want to know: うんてん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C202-DRIVE202-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うんてん** — *unten* — "driving; operating (a machine)".

> **うんてんは たのしいです。** — *unten wa tanoshii desu* — "Driving is fun."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うんてん** (*unten*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *un*, "to carry" (Middle Chinese *hjunH*) and *ten*, "to turn" (*trjwenX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
