## HL-C340 — Hindi seeing teaches and declares its present forms

After the listening-form repair, **देखता** is the highest-ranked independent
modern Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 70 models and
assesses **मैं किताब देखता हूँ**, but Chapter 43 introduces only infinitive
**देखना** and mentions its stem without deriving or practising the present
family.

Derive **देख**, **देखता**, **देखती**, and **देखते** in Chapter 43, practise the
gendered first-person forms, recall the set, and declare the complete family as
the lesson headword. This closes the gap before the later object construction
and makes every taught form visible to reports and downstream cards.
