Hindi, chapter 289: The Thief Got Away.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जेबकतरा (jebkatrā) — a pickpocket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a witness, then the Hindi for outside.

You'll want to know: जेबकतरा.
जेबकतरा — jebkatrā — "a pickpocket".
बाज़ार में एक जेबकतरा घूम रहा था। bāzār meṁ ek jebkatrā ghūm rahā thā. A pickpocket was wandering through the market.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
जेबकतरा (jebkatrā) is जेब, a pocket, and katrā, one who snips, from katarnā, to clip, which CDIAL 2858 lists for Hindi under Sanskrit kartarī, "scissors": the one who cuts into pockets.

Lesson 2 of 5.
छीनना (chīnnā) — to snatch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for outside, then the Hindi for a pickpocket.

You'll want to know: छीनना.
छीनना — chīnnā — "to snatch".
उसने मीरा के हाथ से मोबाइल छीन लिया। usne Mīrā ke hāth se mobāil chīn liyā. He snatched the mobile phone out of Mira's hand.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने थैला छीना, usne thailā chīnā.

The word, taken apart.
छीनना (chīnnā) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit chinna, "cut off" (CDIAL 5047): what is snatched is torn away from its owner. It often takes लेना: छीन लिया, snatched it away.

Lesson 3 of 5.
झपटना (jhapaṭnā) — to lunge; to swoop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a pickpocket, then the Hindi for to snatch.

You'll want to know: झपटना.
झपटना — jhapaṭnā — "to lunge; to swoop".
चोर मेरे थैले पर झपटा और भाग गया। cor mere thaile par jhapṭā aur bhāg gayā. The thief lunged at my bag and ran off.
Past (he …): वह थैले पर झपटा, vah thaile par jhapṭā.

The word, taken apart.
झपटना (jhapaṭnā) comes from jhapp, a sudden movement, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5336). A hawk झपटता है on its prey; a thief झपटता है on a bag: थैले पर झपटा.

Lesson 4 of 5.
फ़रार (farār) — on the run.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to snatch, then the Hindi for to lunge.

You'll want to know: फ़रार.
फ़रार — farār — "on the run".
चोर दो हफ़्ते से फ़रार है। cor do hafte se farār hai. The thief has been on the run for two weeks.

The word, taken apart.
फ़रार (farār) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic firār, "flight". It describes the person, not the running: चोर फ़रार है, the thief got away and has not been found.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पीछा (pīchā) — a chase; the pursuit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to lunge, then the Hindi for on the run.

You'll want to know: पीछा.
पीछा — pīchā — "a chase; the pursuit".
अगर मेरे पास साइकिल होती तो मैं चोर का पीछा करता। agar mere pās sāikil hotī to maiṁ cor kā pīchā kartā. If I had had a bicycle, I would have gone after the thief.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पीछा (pīchā) is the noun behind पीछे, behind, from paśca, the back part, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 7990). किसी का पीछा करना is to go after someone: to make yourself the one behind them.
