# おそらく (osoraku) — very likely; in all likelihood

## You'll want to know: おそらく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C355-TABUN355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おそらく** — *osoraku* — "very likely; in all likelihood".

> **たいふうが きたら、おそらく でんしゃは とまるでしょう。** — *taifū ga kitara, osoraku densha wa tomaru deshō* — "If the typhoon comes, the trains will very likely stop."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おそらく** (*osoraku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
