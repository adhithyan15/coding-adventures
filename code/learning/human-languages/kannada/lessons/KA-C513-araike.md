## You'll want to know: ಆರೈಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C513-CHETARIKE513-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರೈಕೆ** — *āraike* — "nursing; looking after".

> ಅಮ್ಮನ ಆರೈಕೆಯಿಂದ ನಾನು ಬೇಗ ಗುಣವಾದೆ.
>
> *ammana āraikeyinda nānu bēga guṇavāde.*
>
> With mother's nursing, I got well quickly.

ಆರೈಕೆ ಮಾಡು, to nurse, to tend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರೈಕೆ** (*āraike*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 377 lists Kannada *āray*, *ārayu*, "to search, to take care of", with the nouns *ārayike*, *ārayke*.
