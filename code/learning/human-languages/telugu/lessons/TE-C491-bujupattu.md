# బూజుపట్టు (būjupaṭṭu) — to go mouldy

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to break off, then the Telugu for to wear down.

## You'll want to know: బూజుపట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C491-TUPPU491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**బూజుపట్టు** — *būjupaṭṭu* — "to go mouldy".

> తేమ వల్ల రొట్టె బూజుపట్టింది.
>
> *tēma valla roṭṭe būjupaṭṭindi.*
>
> Because of the damp the bread went mouldy.

Past (it …): బూజుపట్టింది, *būjupaṭṭindi*.

బూజు, mould, + పట్టు, to catch. A house cobweb is also బూజు. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: బూజుపట్టింది, it went mouldy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బూజుపట్టు** (*būjupaṭṭu*) is native Dravidian: బూజు, "mould, mildew" (DEDR 4357), and పట్టు, "to catch" (DEDR 4034).
