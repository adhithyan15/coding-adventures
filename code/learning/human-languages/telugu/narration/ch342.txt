Lesson 1 of 5.
దండోరా (daṇḍōrā) — a drum proclamation.

You'll want to know: దండోరా.
దండోరా — daṇḍōrā — "a drum proclamation".
గ్రామంలో ఒక దండోరా వినిపించింది. grāmaṁlō oka daṇḍōrā vinipiñcindi. A drum proclamation was heard in the village.
దండోరా (daṇḍōrā) వేయు, to proclaim with a drum: the old way to spread news through the whole ఊరు.

The word, taken apart.
దండోరా (daṇḍōrā) is an old word for news cried with a drum through the streets; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ప్రముఖుడు (pramukhuḍu) — a well-known person; a notable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to certify, then the Telugu for a drum proclamation.

You'll want to know: ప్రముఖుడు.
ప్రముఖుడు — pramukhuḍu — "a well-known person; a notable".
ఆ పెళ్ళికి చాలా మంది ప్రముఖులు వచ్చారు. ā peḷḷiki cālā mandi pramukhulu vaccāru. Many well-known people came to that wedding.
Plural: ప్రముఖులు, pramukhulu.
ప్రముఖ alone works as an adjective: ప్రముఖ రచయిత, a well-known writer.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రముఖుడు (pramukhuḍu) is built on Sanskrit pramukha, "facing forward; foremost, chief" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu ending -uḍu.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ప్రసంగం (prasaṅgaṁ) — a speech.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a well-known person, then the Telugu for a public meeting.

You'll want to know: ప్రసంగం.
ప్రసంగం — prasaṅgaṁ — "a speech".
సభలో అతని ప్రసంగం చాలా బాగుంది. sabhalō atani prasaṅgaṁ cālā bāgundi. His speech at the meeting was very good.
Plural: ప్రసంగాలు, prasaṅgālu.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రసంగం (prasaṅgaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit prasaṅga (Monier-Williams), a word whose first sense is "attachment, connection"; in Telugu it means a public talk.

Past (I …): ప్రసంగించాను, prasaṅgiñcānu.
