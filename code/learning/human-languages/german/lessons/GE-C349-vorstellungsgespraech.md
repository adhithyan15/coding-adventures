## You'll want to know: das Vorstellungsgespräch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C349-STELLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**das Vorstellungsgespräch** — "the job interview".

> Morgen habe ich ein Vorstellungsgespräch bei einer Bank.
>
> *Tomorrow I have a job interview at a bank.*

Plural: *die Vorstellungsgespräche*. *Sich vorstellen* — to introduce yourself — is at its heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vorstellungsgespräch* joins *Vorstellung* "introduction", from *vorstellen*, built on *stellen* "to put" (EtymDB), and *Gespräch* "conversation", from Old High German *gisprāhhi* (EtymDB): the talk where you introduce yourself.
