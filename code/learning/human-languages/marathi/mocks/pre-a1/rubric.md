# Marathi pre-A1 mock rubric

This rubric scores either pre-A1 form against the exact envelope in
[`task-shapes/pre-a1.json`](../../task-shapes/pre-a1.json). Reading, listening,
writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers. A learner passes the rung
only by earning at least 60/100 on **every** paper. A high score in one skill
never compensates for a failed skill.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->
- signs and words: 40 points across six keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->
- phrases and notices: 30 points across four keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->
- personal details: 30 points across four keyed items.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->

Divide each part's points equally among its keyed items and retain one decimal
place. Accept only the keyed meaning or situation. Reading aloud does not earn
reading credit.

## Listening — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->
- sounds and words: 40 points across six keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->
- greeting responses: 30 points across four keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->
- personal details: 30 points across four keyed items.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->

Read every examiner-script item twice at 70–90 words per minute and leave an
eight-second answer pause. Score only the learner's selected answer. Do not
show the script, romanization, or an answer-bearing picture label. The checked-
in script is an administration boundary, not evidence that a qualified
recording exists.

## Writing — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->
- delayed recall: 20 points, 10 per word;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
- dictation: 40 points, 10 per item;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
- bounded independent production: 40 points, 20 per response.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

For each delayed-recall or dictation item award 4 points for a recognizable
ordered Devanagari sequence, 3 for matra and anusvara placement, 2 for the
expected letters including Marathi **ळ** where required, and 1 for a clean word
boundary. For each independent response award 8 for answering the stated
purpose, 6 for recognizable Devanagari word formation, 4 for required marks,
and 2 for boundary control. A romanized, traced, or visibly copied answer earns
zero because it is not independent writing evidence.

## Speaking — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
- greeting and identity: 30 points;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
- three familiar questions: 40 points;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->
- short request: 30 points.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->

For each part award 40% for completing the communicative purpose, 25% for
intelligibility to a trained Marathi speaker, 20% for an expected known word or
chunk, and 15% for responding without an answer script. The interlocutor may
repeat once slowly without penalty. Accept a documented regional Marathi form
when it is used consistently. Accept masculine, feminine, or other natural
first-person choices with internally consistent gender agreement. A
support-language-only response is not Marathi speaking evidence.

## Readiness boundary

These forms, keys, scripts, manifests, and this rubric make the two
project-defined mocks structurally executable. Their existence does not establish exam readiness,
recording provenance, qualified recordings,
independent human validation, standard setting, learner readiness, or learner
attainment. Those remain separate evidence obligations in `assessment.json`.
