## You'll want to know: लिपटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C447-JHADI447-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लिपटना** — *lipaṭnā* — "to cling; to wind round".

> बेल पेड़ से लिपट गई।
>
> *bel peṛ se lipaṭ gaī.*
>
> The creeper wound itself round the tree.

Past (he …): वह बच्चा माँ से लिपट गया, *vah baccā māṁ se lipaṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लिपटना** (*lipaṭnā*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *lipyate*, "is smeared" (CDIAL 11061), with Hindi *lipaṭnā*, "to stick": to stick close round something.
