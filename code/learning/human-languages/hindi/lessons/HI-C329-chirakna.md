## You'll want to know: छिड़कना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C329-RANG329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**छिड़कना** — *chiṛaknā* — "to sprinkle".

> दादी ने आँगन में पानी छिड़का।
>
> *dādī ne āṁgan meṁ pānī chiṛkā.*
>
> Grandmother sprinkled water in the courtyard.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पानी छिड़का, *usne pānī chiṛkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from *\*chiṭ*, a form CDIAL reconstructs for sudden splashes and scattering (CDIAL 5035). You **छिड़कना** water, salt or colour.
