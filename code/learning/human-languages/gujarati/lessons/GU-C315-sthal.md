## You'll want to know: સ્થળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C315-UDGHATAN315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્થળ** — *sthaḷ* — "a place; a site".

> પત્રકારો અકસ્માતના સ્થળે પહોંચ્યા.
>
> *patrakāro akasmātnā sthaḷe pahõcyā.*
>
> Journalists reached the scene of the accident.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્થળ** (*sthaḷ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sthala*, "ground, place, spot" (Monier-Williams). It is the formal word for the place where something happened: **અકસ્માતના સ્થળે**, at the scene.
