## You'll want to know: اسپرنگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C502-ZANG502-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اسپرنگ** — *ispring* — "a metal spring (a coil)".

> اس پرانے پلنگ کے اسپرنگ کمزور ہیں۔
>
> *is purāne palang ke ispring kamzor haiṅ.*
>
> The springs of this old bed are weak.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اسپرنگ** (*ispring*) is borrowed from English *spring*, the coil of metal.
