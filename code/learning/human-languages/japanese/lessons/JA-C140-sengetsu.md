## You'll want to know: せんげつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C140-SENGETSU]; assesses=[] -->

**せんげつ** — *sengetsu* — "last month". The **せん** at its start is the
same as in **せんしゅう**: "the one before".

> **せんげつ うみに いきました。** — *sengetsu umi ni ikimashita* — "I went
> to the sea last month."

**いく**, *iku*, "to go", gives **いきます** and then **いきました**.
