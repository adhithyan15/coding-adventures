## You'll want to know: салют
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C314-SALYUT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**салют** (*salyút*) — "a firework display; a salute".

> В полночь начался салют.
>
> *V pólnoch nachalsyá salyút.*
>
> At midnight the firework display began.

*Праздничный салют* — a festive firework display.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*салют* is usually traced to French *salut* "greeting", from Latin *salūs* "health, safety" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB glosses the Russian word "salute; fireworks" but lists no parent.
