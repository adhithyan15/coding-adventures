## You'll want to know: la frusta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C338-FRUSTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la frusta** — "the whisk; the whip".

> Prendi la frusta, non il cucchiaio.
>
> *Take the whisk, not the spoon.*

*La frusta elettrica* — the electric whisk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frusta* is traced in EtymDB to Latin *fūstis* "a stick, a club".
