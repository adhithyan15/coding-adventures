## You'll want to know: रोखणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C386-PRATIBANDH386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रोखणे** — *rokhṇe* — "to restrain; to stop".

> पोलिसांनी गर्दीला दरवाज्याजवळ रोखलं.
>
> *polisānnī gardīlā darvājyājavaḷ rokhlaṁ.*
>
> The police held the crowd back near the gate.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने रोखलं, *tyāne rokhlaṁ*.

What is stopped takes -ला: गर्दीला रोखणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोखणे** (*rokhṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *rokhṇẽ*, "to hinder", under *\*rōkk-*, "stop" (CDIAL 10827).
