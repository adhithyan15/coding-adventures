# റിസപ്ഷൻ (ṟisapṣan) — a reception desk

## You'll want to know: റിസപ്ഷൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C481-TAMASAM481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**റിസപ്ഷൻ** — *ṟisapṣan* — "a reception desk".

> ഹോട്ടലിലെ റിസപ്ഷനിൽ ഞാൻ എന്റെ പേര് പറഞ്ഞു.
>
> *hōṭṭalile ṟisapṣanil ñān enṟe pērŭ paṟaññu.*
>
> I gave my name at the hotel reception.

A wedding reception is also a റിസപ്ഷൻ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**റിസപ്ഷൻ** (*ṟisapṣan*) was borrowed from English *reception*, the front desk of a hotel or office.
