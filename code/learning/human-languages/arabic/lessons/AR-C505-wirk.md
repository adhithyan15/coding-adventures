## You'll want to know: ورك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C505-ADHM505-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ورك** — *wirk* — "a hip".

> تشعر جدتي بألم في الورك.
>
> *tashʿuru jaddatī bi-alam fī al-wirk.*
>
> My grandmother feels a pain in her hip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ورك** (*wirk*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-r-k*, "the hip, the haunch" (Lane). The word is old and common to the language.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
