## You'll want to know: निंदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C360-APATTI360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निंदा** — *nindā* — "condemnation, criticism".

> सब लोगों ने उसके झूठ की निंदा की।
>
> *sab logoṁ ne uske jhūṭh kī nindā kī.*
>
> Everyone condemned his lie.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निंदा** (*nindā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nindā*, "blame, censure" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
