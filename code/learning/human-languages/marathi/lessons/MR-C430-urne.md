## You'll want to know: उरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C430-MOBADLA430-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उरणे** — *urṇe* — "to be left over".

> जेवणानंतर थोडा भात उरला.
>
> *jevṇānantar thoḍā bhāt urlā.*
>
> After the meal a little rice was left over.

Past (he …): तो उरला, *to urlā*.

What is left is the subject: पैसे उरले, money was left.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उरणे** (*urṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *urṇẽ*, "to be left as a residue", under *\*urvara*, "surplus, left over" (CDIAL 2356).
