## You'll want to know: पटमण्डपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C427-VASATI427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पटमण्डपः** — *paṭamaṇḍapaḥ* — "a tent".

> पर्वते वयं पटमण्डपे स्वपिमः।
>
> *parvate vayaṃ paṭamaṇḍape svapimaḥ.*
>
> On the mountain we sleep in a tent.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पटमण्डपः** (*paṭamaṇḍapaḥ*) is built from *paṭa*, "cloth, canvas", and *maṇḍapa*, "a pavilion, an open hall"; MW gives the compound as a canvas house, a tent (MW).
