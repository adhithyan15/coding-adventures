# पठिष्यामि (paṭhiṣyāmi) — I will read

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Retrieve “I will go” and “I will do.” (*Gamiṣyāmi; kariṣyāmi.*)

## You'll want to know: पठिष्यामि

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पठिष्यामि** — *paṭhiṣyāmi* — means “I will read” or “I will study.” Learn
the complete form; its future stem is given, not guessed.

## Taken Apart

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02] -->

**पठिष्यामि** comes from the root **पठ्** (*paṭh*), “read, recite.” Its future here is
**पठिष्य-** (*paṭhiṣya-*), followed by first-person **-आमि**. Whitney,
*Sanskrit Grammar* §§932–935, describes the Sanskrit s-future and the
**-iṣya- / -sya-** formation used here.
