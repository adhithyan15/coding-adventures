## You'll want to know: ટ્રાફિક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C208-HIGHWAY208-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ટ્રાફિક** — *ṭrāphik* — "traffic".

> **શહેરમાં ઘણો ટ્રાફિક હતો.** — *śahermā̃ ghaṇo ṭrāphik hato* — "There was a lot of traffic in the city."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટ્રાફિક** (*ṭrāphik*) is a loan from English *traffic*.
