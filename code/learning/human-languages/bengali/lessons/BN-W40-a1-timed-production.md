# Your first complete A1 writing paper

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C01-NOMOSHKAR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMARNAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-AMIBANGLABOLI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-THAKA-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-KAJKORA-01, BN-LEX-C09-BHALOLAGA-01, BN-LEX-C10-JOL-01, BN-LEX-C10-CHA-01, BN-LEX-C11-PORIBAR-01, BN-LEX-C26-KOTHAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] This is an attempt, not a model. You will write the answers, and
this page will not show you any copyable Bengali answer.

Put away romanization, dictionaries, translators, and spell-checkers. Set one
timer for **20 minutes**. Keep it running through both tasks. Write in Bengali
script. Ordinary legible handwriting is enough; decorative calligraphy is not
scored.

## Writing — timed assessment production
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C01-NOMOSHKAR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMARNAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-AMIBANGLABOLI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-THAKA-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-KAJKORA-01, BN-LEX-C09-BHALOLAGA-01, BN-LEX-C10-JOL-01, BN-LEX-C10-CHA-01, BN-LEX-C11-PORIBAR-01, BN-LEX-C26-KOTHAY-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: timed-assessment-production -->

### Task 1 — practical form: 7 minutes, 40 points

Complete **all six fields in Bengali script** for a new neighbourhood activity
group. Short answers are enough.

1. Your name
2. Where you live or stay
3. A language you speak
4. One everyday action or kind of work you do
5. One drink or food you like
6. One person in your family

At seven minutes, move on even if a field is unfinished. Do not restart the
timer and do not polish the form with time meant for the message.

### Task 2 — message: 13 minutes, 60 points

**Reader:** Mira. **Purpose:** help Mira recognise you and arrange your first
meeting tomorrow.

Write **30–40 words in Bengali script**. Greet Mira, introduce yourself, say
where you live and which language you speak, mention one everyday action or
kind of work and one drink or food you like, ask where Mira is, and close
politely. The instructions give content, not sentences: choose and connect the
Bengali yourself.

When the 20-minute timer rings, **stop**, even if a line is unfinished. Record
the number of completed form fields and the message word count. An honest timed
attempt is more useful than a perfect untimed one.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C01-NOMOSHKAR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMARNAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-AMIBANGLABOLI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-THAKA-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-KAJKORA-01, BN-LEX-C09-BHALOLAGA-01, BN-LEX-C10-JOL-01, BN-LEX-C10-CHA-01, BN-LEX-C11-PORIBAR-01, BN-LEX-C26-KOTHAY-01] -->

Only after you have stopped, review the same attempt. Do not rewrite it yet.
Mark one strength and one next step under each assessment criterion:

1. **Task fulfilment and relevant content** — six fields are attempted; the
   named reader, meeting purpose, and requested message points are present.
2. **Comprehensibility and organisation** — Mira can follow the message and see
   how its ideas connect.
3. **Bengali vocabulary, grammar, and register** — familiar words and sentence
   patterns carry the intended meaning in a suitable everyday tone.
4. **Bengali orthographic control** — headline strokes, hasanta, vowel-sign
   placement, word spacing, and punctuation remain readable.

[PAUSE 2s] Keep the paper. On another day, repeat the same two-task shape with
new personal details. The goal is not to memorise this prompt; it is to make a
complete Bengali attempt calmly inside the real limit.
