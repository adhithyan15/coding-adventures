## You'll want to know: यमलौ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C498-AGRAJA498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**यमलौ** — *yamalau* — "twins".

> यमलौ बालौ सह क्रीडतः।
>
> *yamalau bālau saha krīḍataḥ.*
>
> The twin boys play together.

A masculine noun, used in the dual.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यमलौ** (*yamalau*) is built on *yama*, "a twin"; MW gives "twin, paired" and, in the dual, "twins, a pair" (MW).
