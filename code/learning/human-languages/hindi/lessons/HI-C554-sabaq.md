## You'll want to know: सबक़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C554-NATIJATAN554-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सबक़** — *sabaq* — "a lesson (learned)".

> अपनी ग़लती से मैंने एक बड़ा सबक़ सीखा।
>
> *Apnī ġaltī se maiṁne ek baṛā sabaq sīkhā.*
>
> I learned a big lesson from my mistake.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सबक़** (*sabaq*) is usually traced to Arabic *sabaq*, "a lesson", through Persian.
