## You'll want to know: l'horodateur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C407-STATIONNEMENT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'horodateur** — "the parking ticket machine".

> Elle paie à l'horodateur avec sa carte.
>
> *She pays at the ticket machine with her card.*

A masculine noun. Older single meters were *parcmètres*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*horodateur* is built from *horo-*, from Greek *hṓra* 'hour', and *dateur* 'something that dates', from *date*: it prints the time on the ticket. EtymDB does not list it.
