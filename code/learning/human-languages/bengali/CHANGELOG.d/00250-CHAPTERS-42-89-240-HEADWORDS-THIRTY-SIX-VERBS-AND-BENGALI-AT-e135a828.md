## Chapters 42-89: 240 headwords, thirty-six verbs, and Bengali attains pre-A1

Bengali had two pre-A1 gaps: 236 headwords and 4 verbs.

**The vocabulary.** Forty-eight chapters of five words each, and four reviews:
two after chapter 65 and two after chapter 89. Every word is chained into the
next two lessons. Verbs are taught in their verbal-noun form (**শোনা**,
**খেলা**, **কেনা**), which is how the track already names **খাওয়া** and
**দেখা**.

- **42-65**:
  - the body and health
  - the family on both sides
  - thirty-six verbs
  - food and the home
- **66-89**:
  - school
  - nature and animals
  - describing words and colours
  - time and place
  - the town
  - clothes
  - six to ten, and the tens to a thousand

Every headword is written only with letters the reader has already written.
Fifty-four candidates wait for that reason, among them words with ঘ, ঠ, ষ, ঙ,
উ, অ, ূ or ৌ, such as **ঘর**, **মাঠ**, **ভাষা**, **উত্তর** and **সূর্য**.
**মা**, **দাদা**, **দিদি**, **চিনি**, **হাঁস** and **ভালোবাসা** were also held
back, because earlier lesson text already uses them. Bengali's
forward-reference count stays at 9.
