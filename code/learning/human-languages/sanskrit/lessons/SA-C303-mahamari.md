## You'll want to know: महामारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C303-DURBHIKSHA303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**महामारी** — *mahāmārī* — "an epidemic".

> नगरे महामारी प्रसरति।
>
> *nagare mahāmārī prasarati.*
>
> An epidemic is spreading in the city.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**महामारी** (*mahāmārī*) is built from *mahā*, "great", and *mārī*, "pestilence"; MW gives *mahāmārī* as a pestilence that kills many, cholera (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
