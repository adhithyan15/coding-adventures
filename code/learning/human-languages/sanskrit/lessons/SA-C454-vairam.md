## You'll want to know: वैरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C454-IRSYA454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वैरम्** — *vairam* — "enmity".

> सिंहस्य वृकस्य च वैरम् अस्ति।
>
> *siṃhasya vṛkasya ca vairam asti.*
>
> There is enmity between the lion and the wolf.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वैरम्** (*vairam*) is built on *vīra*, "a hero, a fighting man", by lengthening its first vowel; MW gives "enmity, hostility, a feud" (MW).
