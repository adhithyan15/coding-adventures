# あける (akeru) — to open up; to open (a door, a window)

## You'll want to know: あける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C158-LIGHTS158-04]; assesses=[] -->

**あける** — *akeru* — "to open up; to open (a door, a window)".

> **あさ まどを あけます。** — *asa mado o akemasu* — "I open the window in the morning."

Polite **あけます**, *akemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あける** (*akeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
