## You'll want to know: नाटक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C529-NATAK529-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नाटक** — *nāṭak* — "a play; a drama".

> मेले में बच्चों ने एक नाटक किया।
>
> *Mele meṁ baccoṁ ne ek nāṭak kiyā.*
>
> At the fair the children put on a play.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाटक** (*nāṭak*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *nāṭaka*, "acting, dancing; a play" (Monier-Williams).
