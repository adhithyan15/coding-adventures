## You'll want to know: разрешать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C389-ZAPRET-03]; assesses=[] -->

**разрешать** (*razreshát*) — "to allow, to permit".

> Мама разрешает мне гулять до девяти.
>
> *Máma razresháyet mne gulyát do devyatí.*
>
> Mum lets me stay out until nine.

Imperfective; perfective *разрешить*. *Разрешать кому* + infinitive. It also means to resolve: *разрешить спор*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**разрешать** is built from *раз-* and *решить*, from Proto-Slavic *\*rěšiti* "to untie, to loosen": to allow is to untie a restriction.
