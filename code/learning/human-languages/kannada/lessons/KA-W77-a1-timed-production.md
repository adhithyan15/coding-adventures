# Your first complete Kannada A1 writing paper

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C01-NAMASKARA-01, KA-LEX-C02-NANNA-HESARU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C02-NANNA-HESARU-02, KA-LEX-C04-MATTE-SIGONA-01, KA-LEX-C05-MATANADU-01, KA-LEX-C05-KANNADA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C05-KANNADA-02, KA-LEX-C39-CHAHA-01, KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-06, KA-GRAMMAR-C69-ASK-02, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-153] -->

[PAUSE 2s] This is an attempt, not a model. You will write the answers, and
this page will not show you any copyable Kannada answer.

Put away romanization, dictionaries, translators, and spell-checkers. Set one
timer for **20 minutes**. Keep it running through both tasks. Write in Kannada
script. Ordinary legible handwriting is enough; decorative calligraphy is not
scored.

## Writing — timed assessment production
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C01-NAMASKARA-01, KA-LEX-C02-NANNA-HESARU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C02-NANNA-HESARU-02, KA-LEX-C04-MATTE-SIGONA-01, KA-LEX-C05-MATANADU-01, KA-LEX-C05-KANNADA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C05-KANNADA-02, KA-LEX-C39-CHAHA-01, KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-06, KA-GRAMMAR-C69-ASK-02, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-153] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: timed-assessment-production -->

### Task 1 — practical form: 7 minutes, 40 points

Complete **all six fields in Kannada script** for a neighbourhood language
exchange. Short answers are enough.

1. Your name
2. A language you speak
3. One greeting you would use
4. One drink you would ask for
5. One place-question word
6. One polite closing you would use

At seven minutes, move on even if a field is unfinished. Do not restart the
timer and do not polish the form with time meant for the message.

### Task 2 — message: 13 minutes, 60 points

**Reader:** Mira. **Purpose:** help Mira recognise you and arrange a first
conversation tomorrow.

Write **20–30 words in Kannada script**. Greet Mira, give your name, say that
you speak Kannada, mention the drink you would ask for, ask where Mira is, ask
one familiar yes-or-no question, and close politely. The instructions give
content, not sentences: choose and connect the Kannada yourself.

When the 20-minute timer rings, **stop**, even if a line is unfinished. Record
the number of completed form fields and the message word count. An honest timed
attempt is more useful than a perfect untimed one.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C01-NAMASKARA-01, KA-LEX-C02-NANNA-HESARU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C02-NANNA-HESARU-02, KA-LEX-C04-MATTE-SIGONA-01, KA-LEX-C05-MATANADU-01, KA-LEX-C05-KANNADA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C05-KANNADA-02, KA-LEX-C39-CHAHA-01, KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-06, KA-GRAMMAR-C69-ASK-02, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-153] -->

Only after you have stopped, review the same attempt. Do not rewrite it yet.
Mark one strength and one next step under each assessment criterion:

1. **Task fulfilment and relevant content** — six fields are attempted; the
   named reader, conversation purpose, and requested message points are present.
2. **Comprehensibility and organisation** — Mira can follow the message and see
   how its ideas connect.
3. **Kannada vocabulary, grammar, and register** — familiar words and sentence
   patterns carry the intended meaning in a suitable everyday tone.
4. **Kannada orthographic control** — vowel-sign placement, consonant clusters,
   word spacing, and punctuation remain readable.

[PAUSE 2s] Keep the paper. On another day, repeat the same two-task shape with
new personal details. The goal is not to memorise this prompt; it is to make a
complete Kannada attempt calmly inside the real limit.
