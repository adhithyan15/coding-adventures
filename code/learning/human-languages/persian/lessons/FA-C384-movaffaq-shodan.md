## You'll want to know: موفق شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C384-ETESAB-04]; assesses=[] -->

**موفق شدن** (*movaffaq shodan*) — "to succeed".

> بالاخره در امتحان موفق شد.
>
> *bâlâkhare dar emtehân movaffaq shod.*
>
> In the end she passed the exam.

*Movaffaq* "successful" + *shodan*. *Dar chizi movaffaq shodan* — to succeed in something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**موفق شدن** joins *movaffaq* "successful", usually traced to Arabic *muwaffaq* "granted success", to the inherited Persian verb *shodan* "to become".
