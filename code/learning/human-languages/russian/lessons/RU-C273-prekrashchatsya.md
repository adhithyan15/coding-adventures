## You'll want to know: прекращаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C273-PROISKHODIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**прекращаться** (*prekrashchátsya*) — "to stop, to cease".

> Музыка не прекращалась всю ночь.
>
> *Múzyka ne prekrashchálas vsyu noch.*
>
> The music did not stop all night.

Imperfective; perfective *прекратиться* (*prekratítsya*). Without *-ся*, *прекращать* stops something else.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прекращаться* is built on *пре-* and *кратить* "to shorten", from *краткий* "short", usually explained as the Church Slavonic shape of native *короткий*, Proto-Slavic *kortŭkŭ* (EtymDB): to cut short.
