## You'll want to know: चरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C553-KRAM553-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चरण** — *caraṇ* — "a stage, a phase".

> योजना का पहला चरण पूरा हो गया।
>
> *Yojnā kā pahlā caraṇ pūrā ho gayā.*
>
> The first stage of the plan is complete.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरण** (*caraṇ*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *caraṇa*, "a foot; a step" (Monier-Williams). A step of a plan is a stage of it.
