## You'll want to know: dǎoméi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C529-LUCK529-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎoméi* — "to be out of luck; to have bad luck".

> *jīntiān zhēn dǎoméi wǒ de qiánbāo bú jiàn le* — What bad luck today: my wallet's gone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎoméi* is built from *dǎo* 'to fall over' and *méi* 'mould'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *tawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to fall flat'; no dictionary we use records an older form of the second, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
