## You'll want to know: ശിക്ഷ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C418-SIKSA418-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ശിക്ഷ** — *śikṣa* — "punishment; a sentence".

> തെറ്റിന് അവന് ചെറിയ ഒരു ശിക്ഷ കിട്ടി.
>
> *teṟṟinŭ avanŭ ceṟiya oru śikṣa kiṭṭi.*
>
> He got a small punishment for the mistake.

A punishment is കിട്ടുക, received, or വിധിക്കുക, passed by a court.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശിക്ഷ** (*śikṣa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śikṣā*, "learning, study", which later also came to mean "punishment" (CDIAL 12432). The lesson and the penalty share one word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
