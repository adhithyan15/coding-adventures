## You'll want to know: じだい

**じだい** — *jidai* — "an era; a period".

> **さむらいの じだいは ながかったです。** — *samurai no jidai wa nagakatta desu* — "The age of the samurai was long."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じだい** (*jidai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "time" (Middle Chinese *dzyi*) and *dai*, "age" (*dojH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
