## You'll want to know: चाकर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C265-MOCHI265-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चाकर** — *chākar* — "a servant".

> चाकर पाणी लावै है।
>
> *chākar pāṇī lāvai hai.*
>
> The servant brings water.

A masculine noun.
