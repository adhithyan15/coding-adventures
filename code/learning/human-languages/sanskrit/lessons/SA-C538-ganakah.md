## You'll want to know: गणकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C538-SAKUNA538-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गणकः** — *gaṇakaḥ* — "an astrologer".

> यदि गणकः सत्यं वदति, तर्हि श्वः वर्षा भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi gaṇakaḥ satyaṃ vadati, tarhi śvaḥ varṣā bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If the astrologer is right, it will rain tomorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गणकः** (*gaṇakaḥ*) comes from the root √gaṇ, "to count", which also gives *gaṇayati*, with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *gaṇaka* as one who calculates nativities, an astrologer (MW).
