## You'll want to know: नाता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C501-NATA501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नाता** — *nātā* — "a bond; a tie of kinship".

> मीरा का अपने गाँव से गहरा नाता है।
>
> *Mīrā kā apne gā̃v se gahrā nātā hai.*
>
> Mira has a deep bond with her village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाता** (*nātā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *nātā*, "kinship, alliance", under *\*jñātra*, a form it reconstructs, close to Sanskrit *jñāti*, "a kinsman" (CDIAL 5279).
