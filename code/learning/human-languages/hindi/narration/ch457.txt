Hindi, chapter 457: Fear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भय (bhay) — dread; fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to fly into a rage, then the Hindi for to get fed up.

You'll want to know: भय.
भय — bhay — "dread; fear".
बच्चों को साँप से बहुत भय लगता है। baccoṁ ko sā̃p se bahut bhay lagtā hai. Children are terribly afraid of snakes.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
भय (bhay) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit bhaya, "fear, alarm, dread" (Monier-Williams). It is more formal than ḍar.

You'll want to know: घबराना.
घबराना — ghabrānā — "to panic; to get flustered".
परीक्षा से पहले अरुण घबरा गया। parīkṣā se pahle Aruṇ ghabrā gayā. Arun panicked before the exam.
Past (he …): वह घबरा गया, vah ghabrā gayā.

The word, taken apart.
घबराना (ghabrānā) is probably inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi ghābrā, "startled", under ghabbhara, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4427), and ghabrānā belongs with it. ghabrāhaṭ, "nervousness", is its noun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
डराना (ḍarānā) — to frighten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for dread, then the Hindi for to panic.

You'll want to know: डराना.
डराना — ḍarānā — "to frighten".
अरुण ने कहानी सुनाकर छोटे भाई को डराया। Aruṇ ne kahānī sunākar choṭe bhāī ko ḍarāyā. Arun frightened his little brother with a story.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने भाई को डराया, usne bhāī ko ḍarāyā.

The word, taken apart.
डराना (ḍarānā) is the causative partner of ḍarnā, "to be afraid", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit darati, "fears" (CDIAL 6190): to make someone afraid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to panic, then the Hindi for to frighten.

You'll want to know: कायर.
कायर — kāyar — "cowardly; a coward".
मेरा कुत्ता बहुत कायर है, चूहे से भी डरता है। merā kuttā bahut kāyar hai, cūhe se bhī ḍartā hai. My dog is a real coward; it's even afraid of a mouse.

The word, taken apart.
कायर (kāyar) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi kāyar, "coward", under Sanskrit kātara, "cowardly, timid" (CDIAL 3027); the t between vowels wore away to y.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सिहरना (siharnā) — to shudder; to shiver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to frighten, then the Hindi for cowardly.

You'll want to know: सिहरना.
सिहरना — siharnā — "to shudder; to shiver".
साँप देखकर मीरा सिहर उठी। sā̃p dekhkar Mīrā sihar uṭhī. Mira shuddered when she saw the snake.
Past (she …): वह सिहर उठी, vah sihar uṭhī.

The word, taken apart.
सिहरना (siharnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi siharnā, "to shiver", under Sanskrit śikhara, a word for a peak or point, perhaps through the hair standing on end (CDIAL 12435). Fear, cold and delight all make one shudder with it.
