## You'll want to know: أتم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C523-INJAZ523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أتم** — *atamma* — "to complete".

> أتمت أختي دراستها في الجامعة.
>
> *atammat ukhtī dirāsatahā fī al-jāmiʿa.*
>
> My sister completed her studies at the university.

Present: يتم, *yutimm*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أتم** (*atamma*) is built on the native Arabic root *t-m-m*, "to be complete, whole" (Lane). Tamāman, completely, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
