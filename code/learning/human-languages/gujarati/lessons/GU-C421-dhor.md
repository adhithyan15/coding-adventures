## You'll want to know: ઢોર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C421-NOISE421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢોર** — *ḍhor* — "cattle; livestock".

> રસ્તા પર ઢોર બેઠાં છે, એટલે ગાડીઓ ધીમે ચાલે છે.
>
> *rastā par ḍhor beṭhā̃ che, eṭle gāḍīo dhīme cāle che.*
>
> Cattle are sitting on the road, so the cars move slowly.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢોર** (*ḍhor*) CDIAL lists Hindi *ḍhor*, "cattle", under Sanskrit *dhaurēya*, "a beast of burden" (CDIAL 6884), the word behind ધોરી in ધોરીમાર્ગ; it gives no Gujarati form for ઢોર, so the link is hedged.
