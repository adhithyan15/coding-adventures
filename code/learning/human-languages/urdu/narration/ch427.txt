Urdu, chapter 427: Sizes and Outfits.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سائز (sāiz) — a size.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to rub, then the Urdu for to polish.

You'll want to know: سائز.
سائز — sāiz — "a size".
اس قمیض کا سائز چھوٹا ہے۔ is qamīz kā sāiz chhoṭā hai. This shirt is a small size.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
سائز (sāiz) is borrowed from English size.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ڈھیلا (ḍhīlā) — loose; slack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to polish, then the Urdu for a size.

ڈھیلا — ḍhīlā — "loose; slack".
یہ پتلون ڈھیلی ہے۔ yih patlūn ḍhīlī hai. These trousers are loose.

The word, taken apart.
ڈھیلا (ḍhīlā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ḍhīlā, "slack, loose", under ḍhila, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5590).

Lesson 4 of 5.
رومال (rūmāl) — a handkerchief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for loose, then the Urdu for an outfit.

You'll want to know: رومال.
رومال — rūmāl — "a handkerchief".
میری جیب میں ایک رومال ہے۔ merī jeb meṅ ek rūmāl hai. There's a handkerchief in my pocket.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
رومال (rūmāl) is built from Persian rū, "the face", and māl, "wiping": it is usually traced to Persian rūmāl.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سجنا (sajnā) — to look good (on someone); to be decorated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an outfit, then the Urdu for a handkerchief.

You'll want to know: سجنا.
سجنا — sajnā — "to look good (on someone); to be decorated".
یہ پوشاک سارا پر سجتی ہے۔ yih poshāk sārā par sajtī hai. This outfit suits Sara.
Past (it …): وہ کمرہ پھولوں سے سجا, voh kamrā phūloṅ se sajā.

The word, taken apart.
سجنا (sajnā) is probably related to Hindi sājnā, "to be prepared", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit sajjyate, "is equipped" (CDIAL 13093); سجانا, "to decorate", is its partner.
