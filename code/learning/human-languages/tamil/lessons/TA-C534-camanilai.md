## You'll want to know: சமநிலை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C534-VETRI534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**சமநிலை** — *camanilai* — "a draw; balance".

> ஆட்டம் சமநிலையில் முடிந்தது.
>
> *āṭṭam camanilaiyil muṭintatu.*
>
> The match ended in a draw.

In a game it is a draw; elsewhere it is balance: மனச் சமநிலை, balance of mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சமநிலை** (*camanilai*) joins சம (*cama*), "equal", from Sanskrit *sama* (CDIAL 13173), and the native நிலை (*nilai*), "a state", from நில், "to stand" (DEDR 3675).
