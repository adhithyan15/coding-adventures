Lesson 1 of 5.
ছোড়া (chhõṛā) — to throw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to fry, then the Bengali for to mix.

You'll want to know: ছোড়া.
ছোড়া — chhõṛā — "to throw".

The word, taken apart.
ছোড়া (chhõṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kṣoṭayati, "throws" (CDIAL 3747).

The word, taken apart.
The origin of টেপা (ṭepā) is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it; the nearest form it gives is Bengali ṭipā, "to pinch", under ṭipp (CDIAL 5464), and it does not join the two.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ফেরা (pherā) — to return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to press, then the Bengali for to draw.

You'll want to know: ফেরা.
ফেরা — pherā — "to return".

The word, taken apart.
ফেরা (pherā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pherayati, "makes move, turns", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9078). CDIAL lists Bengali pherā, "to turn".

চালানো (chālāno) — to drive, to run (a machine).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to draw, then the Bengali for to return.

You'll want to know: চালানো.
চালানো — chālāno — "to drive, to run (a machine)".

The word, taken apart.
চালানো (chālāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from calyati, "goes", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4721). Chālāno is the causing form of a verb CDIAL lists as Bengali cālā, "to be moved".
