## You'll want to know: अपघात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C288-GHATANA288-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अपघात** — *apghāt* — "an accident".

> शाळेजवळ काल एक मोठा अपघात झाला.
>
> *śāḷejvaḷ kāl ek moṭhā apghāt jhālā.*
>
> There was a big accident near the school yesterday.

Masculine.

अपघात होणे, an accident happens; अपघातात, in an accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपघात** (*apghāt*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *apaghāta*, 'a striking off, a warding off' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'an accident' is the modern one.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
