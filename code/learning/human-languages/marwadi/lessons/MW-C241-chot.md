## You'll want to know: चोट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C241-FALL241-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चोट** — *choṭ* — "an injury; a hurt".

> म्हारी चोट घणी माड़ी कोनी।
>
> *mhārī choṭ ghaṇī māṛī konī.*
>
> My injury is not very bad.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चोट** (*choṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cuṭati*, "cuts off, strikes". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *coṭ*, "wound" (CDIAL 4857).
