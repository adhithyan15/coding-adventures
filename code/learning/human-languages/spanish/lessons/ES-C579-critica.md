## You'll want to know: la crítica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C579-CRITICA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la crítica** — "the criticism; the review".

> Acepto la crítica con calma.
>
> *I accept the criticism calmly.*

Also a review of a book or film: *la crítica de la película*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la crítica* is built on *crítico*, a learned borrowing from Latin *criticus*, taken from Greek *kritikos* 'able to judge', from *krinein* 'to judge' (EtymDB).
