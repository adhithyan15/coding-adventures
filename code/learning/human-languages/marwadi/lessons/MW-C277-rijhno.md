## You'll want to know: रीझणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C277-BHAJAN277-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रीझणो** — *rījhṇo* — "to be delighted".

> दादी गीत सुणै है अर रीझै है।
>
> *dādī gīt suṇai hai ar rījhai hai.*
>
> Grandmother listens to the song and is delighted.

Past (he …): वो रीझ्यो, *vo rījhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रीझणो** (*rījhṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ṛdhyati*, "increases, prospers"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *rījhaï*, "is satisfied", and Hindi *rījhnā*, "to be enchanted" (CDIAL 2457).
