## You'll want to know: जाड़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C438-JADA438-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जाड़ा** — *jāṛā* — "winter; the cold".

> जाड़े में यहाँ बहुत ठंड पड़ती है।
>
> *jāṛe meṁ yahā̃ bahut ṭhaṇḍ paṛtī hai.*
>
> It gets very cold here in winter.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जाड़ा** (*jāṛā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *jaḍa*, "dull, numb" and also "cold" (CDIAL 5090), which lists Hindi *jaṛā*, "cold, frost, winter".
