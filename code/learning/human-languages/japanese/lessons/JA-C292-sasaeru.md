## You'll want to know: ささえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C292-JINSEI292-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ささえる** — *sasaeru* — "to support; to hold up".

> **かぞくが いつも ちちを ささえています。** — *kazoku ga itsumo chichi o sasaete imasu* — "The family is always there for my father."

Polite **ささえます**, *sasaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **かぞくが そぼを ささえました。**, *kazoku ga sobo o sasaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ささえる** (*sasaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
