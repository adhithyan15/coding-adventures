## You'll want to know: अंगरागः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C487-PRASADHANA487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगरागः** — *aṅgarāgaḥ* — "a scented cosmetic".

> स्नानात् अनन्तरं सा अंगरागं लिम्पति।
>
> *snānāt anantaraṃ sā aṅgarāgaṃ limpati.*
>
> After her bath she puts on a scented cream.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगरागः** (*aṅgarāgaḥ*) is built from **अंगम्**, "a limb, the body", and *rāga*, "colour"; MW gives "application of unguents or cosmetics to the body" and "scented cosmetic" (MW).
