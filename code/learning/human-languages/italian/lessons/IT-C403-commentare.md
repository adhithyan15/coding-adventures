## You'll want to know: commentare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C403-PUBBLICARE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**commentare** — "to comment (on)".

> Molti hanno commentato la sua foto.
>
> *Many people commented on her photo.*

*Commentare una partita* — to commentate a match.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*commentare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commentārī* "to think over, to make notes on" (EtymDB), a verb formed on *comminīscī* "to devise, to recall".
