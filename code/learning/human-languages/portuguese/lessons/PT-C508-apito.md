## You'll want to know: o apito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C508-ARBITRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o apito** — "whistle".

> Ouviu-se o apito final.
>
> *The final whistle was heard.*

The sound itself and the object.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apito* is probably imitative of a sharp whistling sound; the origin is not settled.
