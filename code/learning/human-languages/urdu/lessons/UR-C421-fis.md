## You'll want to know: فیس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C421-KAGHAZ421-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فیس** — *fīs* — "a fee".

> بینک اس کام کی فیس لیتا ہے۔
>
> *baink is kām kī fīs letā hai.*
>
> The bank charges a fee for this.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فیس** (*fīs*) is borrowed from English *fees*.
