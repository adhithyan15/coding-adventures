## You'll want to know: तीव्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C377-ATIVA377-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तीव्रः** — *tīvraḥ* — "intense; severe".

> यदि ज्वरः तीव्रः, तर्हि वैद्यम् आह्वयतु।
>
> *yadi jvaraḥ tīvraḥ, tarhi vaidyam āhvayatu.*
>
> If the fever is severe, call the doctor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तीव्रः** (*tīvraḥ*) MW gives *tīvra* "strong, severe, violent, intense" and takes it to come from the root √tu, "to be strong" (MW).
