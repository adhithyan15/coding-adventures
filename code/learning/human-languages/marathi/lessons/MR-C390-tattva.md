## You'll want to know: तत्त्व
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C390-EKUN390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्त्व** — *tattva* — "a principle".

> खोटं न बोलणं हे त्याचं तत्त्व आहे.
>
> *khoṭaṁ na bolṇaṁ he tyācaṁ tattva āhe.*
>
> Not telling lies is his principle.

Neuter.

तत्त्वतः, in principle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्त्व** (*tattva*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tattva*, "real state, truth" (CDIAL 5642), from *tat*, "that": thatness.
