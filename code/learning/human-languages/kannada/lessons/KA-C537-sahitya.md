# ಸಾಹಿತ್ಯ (sāhitya) — literature; lyrics

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C537-KAVYA537-01, KA-LEX-C537-KAVYA537-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a poet, then the Kannada for a poem.

## You'll want to know: ಸಾಹಿತ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C537-KAVYA537-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಹಿತ್ಯ** — *sāhitya* — "literature; lyrics".

> ಕನ್ನಡ ಸಾಹಿತ್ಯ ತುಂಬಾ ಹಳೆಯದು.
>
> *kannaḍa sāhitya tumbā haḷeyadu.*
>
> Kannada literature is very old.

Also the words of a song.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಹಿತ್ಯ** (*sāhitya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sāhitya*, "association, union", from *sahita*, "joined" (Monier-Williams): words and meaning joined well.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
