## You'll want to know: لوہا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C501-LOHA501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**لوہا** — *lohā* — "iron".

> لوہار لوہے سے اوزار بناتا ہے۔
>
> *lohār lohe se auzār banātā hai.*
>
> The blacksmith makes tools out of iron.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لوہا** (*lohā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lōha*, "red, copper-coloured", later "copper" and "iron" (CDIAL 11158).
