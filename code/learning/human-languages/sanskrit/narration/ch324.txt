Lesson 1 of 5.
पुरा (purā) — long ago; formerly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for digs up, then the Sanskrit for brings to light.

You'll want to know: पुरा.
पुरा — purā — "long ago; formerly".
पुरा अत्र एकः राजा वसति स्म। purā atra ekaḥ rājā vasati sma. Long ago a king lived here.

The word, taken apart.
पुरा (purā) MW gives purā as before, formerly, of old, and compares puras and pūrva, but gives no root, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
इतिवृत्तम् (itivṛttam) — a chronicle; an account of events.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for brings to light, then the Sanskrit for long ago.

You'll want to know: इतिवृत्तम्.
इतिवृत्तम् — itivṛttam — "a chronicle; an account of events".
एतत् पुस्तकं राज्यस्य इतिवृत्तं वर्णयति। etat pustakaṃ rājyasya itivṛttaṃ varṇayati. This book tells the chronicle of the kingdom.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
इतिवृत्तम् (itivṛttam) is built from iti, "thus", and vṛtta, "happened": what happened thus; MW gives itivṛtta as an occurrence, an event, and a chronicle is its modern use (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
पुराणम् (purāṇam) — an ancient tale; a Purana.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for long ago, then the Sanskrit for a chronicle.

You'll want to know: पुराणम्.
पुराणम् — purāṇam — "an ancient tale; a Purana".
पितामहः पुराणं कथयति। pitāmahaḥ purāṇaṃ kathayati. Grandfather tells an ancient tale.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पुराणम् (purāṇam) is usually traced to purā, "formerly"; MW gives purāṇa as belonging to olden times and, as a noun, an ancient tale or legend, old traditional history (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनुस्मरति (anusmarati) — calls to mind; recollects.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a chronicle, then the Sanskrit for an ancient tale.

You'll want to know: अनुस्मरति.
अनुस्मरति — anusmarati — "calls to mind; recollects".
जनाः वीरस्य शौर्यम् अनुस्मरन्ति। janāḥ vīrasya śauryam anusmaranti. People call to mind the hero's valour.
Past (he …): सः पितामहम् अनुस्मृतवान्, saḥ pitāmaham anusmṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुस्मरति (anusmarati) is built from anu, "after", and the root √smṛ, "to remember", as in smarati; MW gives anu-smṛ as to remember, recollect (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
कीर्तयति (kīrtayati) — tells of; celebrates.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an ancient tale, then the Sanskrit for calls to mind.

You'll want to know: कीर्तयति.
कीर्तयति — kīrtayati — "tells of; celebrates".
ग्रन्थः तस्य युद्धस्य शौर्यं कीर्तयति। granthaḥ tasya yuddhasya śauryaṃ kīrtayati. The book tells of the valour shown in that war.
Past (he …): सः वीरस्य शौर्यं कीर्तितवान्, saḥ vīrasya śauryaṃ kīrtitavān.

The word, taken apart.
कीर्तयति (kīrtayati) comes from the root √kīrt, "to mention, tell, name, celebrate", which belongs with kīrti, "fame" (MW).
