## You'll want to know: yōudiǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C372-ANYWAY372-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yōudiǎn* — "strong point; merit".

> *wǒ juéde zhè shì tā zuì dà de yōudiǎn* — I think that's his greatest strength.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yōudiǎn* is built from *yōu* and 点 'a point', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'juw* and *temX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'abundant' and the second 'a dot'.
