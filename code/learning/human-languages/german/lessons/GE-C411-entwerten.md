## You'll want to know: entwerten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C411-FAHRSCHEIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**entwerten** — "to stamp, to validate (a ticket)".

> Im Bus entwertet sie ihre Fahrkarte.
>
> *On the bus she stamps her ticket.*

Literally to take the value away: once stamped, the ticket cannot be used again. Inseparable: *sie hat entwertet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entwerten* is built from *ent-* 'away, un-' and *Wert* 'value, worth', an inherited Germanic word that is a cousin of English *worth*.
