## You'll want to know: સંકોચાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C433-OUTFIT433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સંકોચાવું** — *sankocāvũ* — "to shrink".

> ધોયા પછી મારું ખમીસ સંકોચાઈ ગયું.
>
> *dhoyā pachī mārũ khamīs sankocāī gayũ.*
>
> My shirt shrank after it was washed.

Past (it …): તે ખમીસ સંકોચાઈ ગયું, *te khamīs sankocāī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંકોચાવું** (*sankocāvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *saṁkoca*, "contraction, shrinking together" (Monier-Williams).
