## You'll want to know: ਲਾਟੂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C353-PLAY353-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਾਟੂ** — *lāṭū* — "a spinning top".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਛੋਟੀ ਭੈਣ ਕੋਲ ਇੱਕ ਲਾਲ ਲਾਟੂ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī chhoṭī bhaiṇ kol ikk lāl lāṭū hai.*
>
> My little sister has a red spinning top.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਾਟੂ** (*lāṭū*) is inherited from Sanskrit *laṭati*, "shakes" (a sense CDIAL infers); CDIAL lists Punjabi *lāṭū*, "spinning top", under it, with a query (CDIAL 10916).
