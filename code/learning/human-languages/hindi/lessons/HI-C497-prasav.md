## You'll want to know: प्रसव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C497-JANM497-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसव** — *prasav* — "childbirth; delivery".

> प्रसव के बाद माँ और बच्चा दोनों ठीक हैं।
>
> *prasav ke bād māṁ aur baccā donõ ṭhīk haiṁ.*
>
> After the delivery, mother and baby are both well.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसव** (*prasav*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *prasava*, "begetting, delivery, birth" (Monier-Williams). It is the word of hospitals and forms.
