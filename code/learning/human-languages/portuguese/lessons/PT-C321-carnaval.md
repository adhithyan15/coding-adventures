## You'll want to know: o carnaval
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C321-CARNAVAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o carnaval** — "the carnival".

> No carnaval, as crianças vão mascaradas para a escola.
>
> *At carnival the children go to school in fancy dress.*

The days before Lent; often written with a capital, *o Carnaval*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carnaval* came from Italian *carnevale* (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *carnelevale* "the putting away of meat", built on *carō* "flesh" and *levāre* "to lift".
