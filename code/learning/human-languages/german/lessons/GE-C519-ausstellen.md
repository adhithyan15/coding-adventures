## You'll want to know: ausstellen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C519-GALERIE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ausstellen** — "to exhibit; to issue".

> Das Museum stellt Fotos aus den Sechzigerjahren aus.
>
> *The museum is exhibiting photos from the sixties.*

Separable: *stellt aus*, *hat ausgestellt*. Also to issue a document: *einen Pass ausstellen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ausstellen* is built on *aus* "out" and *stellen* "to set, to place" (EtymDB), inherited from Old High German *stellen*, from Proto-Germanic *stallijaną*.
