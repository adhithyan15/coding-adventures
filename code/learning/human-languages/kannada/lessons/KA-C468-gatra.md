## You'll want to know: ಗಾತ್ರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C468-GATRA468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಾತ್ರ** — *gātra* — "size".

> ಈ ಅಂಗಿ ನನ್ನ ಗಾತ್ರಕ್ಕೆ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *ī aṅgi nanna gātrakke sariyāgilla.*
>
> This shirt is not my size.

In Sanskrit a limb or the body; Kannada uses it for the size of things.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಾತ್ರ** (*gātra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gātra*, "a limb, the body" (CDIAL 4124); in Kannada it came to mean bulk and size.
