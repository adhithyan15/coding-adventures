# ಉಂಟಾಗು (uṇṭāgu) — to come about; to arise

## You'll want to know: ಉಂಟಾಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C385-PRABHAVA385-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಂಟಾಗು** — *uṇṭāgu* — "to come about; to arise".

> ಮಳೆಯಿಂದ ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ತೊಂದರೆ ಉಂಟಾಯಿತು.
>
> *maḷeyinda rasteyalli tondare uṇṭāyitu.*
>
> The rain caused trouble on the road.

Past (it …): ಉಂಟಾಯಿತು, *uṇṭāyitu*.

ಉಂಟು, there is, + ಆಗು. ಉಂಟುಮಾಡು, to cause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಂಟಾಗು** (*uṇṭāgu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *uṇṭu*, "there is" (DEDR 697), and *āgu*, "to become" (DEDR 333).
