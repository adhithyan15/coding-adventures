Persian, chapter 483: Honesty and Lies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صادق (sâdeq) — honest, sincere.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a grudge, then the Persian for ashamed.

You'll want to know: صادق.
صادق (sâdeq) — "honest, sincere".
دوست من همیشه با من صادق است. dust-e man hamishe bâ man sâdeq ast. My friend is always honest with me.
bâ kasi sâdeq budan — to be honest with someone.

The word, taken apart.
sâdeq was borrowed from Arabic ṣādiq, 'true, sincere; reliable' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
صداقت (sedâqat) — honesty, sincerity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for ashamed, then the Persian for honest.

You'll want to know: صداقت.
صداقت (sedâqat) — "honesty, sincerity".
صداقت برای من از پول مهمتر است. sedâqat barâ-ye man az pul mohemtar ast. Honesty matters more to me than money.
A noun; it shares its Arabic root with sâdeq 'honest'.

The word, taken apart.
sedâqat is a loan, usually traced to Arabic ṣadāqa, a word from the same root as ṣādiq, 'true, sincere'. In Arabic ṣadāqa means 'friendship'; Persian took it as 'honesty'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: رک.
رک (rok) — "frank, blunt".
او خیلی رک است و نظرش را میگوید. u kheyli rok ast va nazarash râ miguyad. She's very frank and says what she thinks.
Common in speech; also rok-o-râst 'straight out'.

The word, taken apart.
rok is an everyday Persian word for frank, but no source used here gives its history, and its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دروغ گفتن (dorugh goftan) — to lie, to tell a lie.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for honesty, then the Persian for frank.

You'll want to know: دروغ گفتن.
دروغ گفتن (dorugh goftan) — "to lie, to tell a lie".
بچه ها گاهی به پدر و مادرشان دروغ میگویند. bachchehâ gâhi be pedar-o mâdarashân dorugh miguyand. Children sometimes lie to their parents.
dorugh 'a lie' + goftan 'to say'; present stem gu-. The person lied to takes be. The opposite is râst goftan.

The word, taken apart.
dorugh is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Old Persian drauga, 'lie', and on to a Proto-Indo-European root for deceiving, and lists German Betrug, 'deceit', as a cousin (EtymDB). goftan, 'to say', is inherited too.

Lesson 5 of 5.
دروغگو (dorughgu) — a liar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for frank, then the Persian for to lie.

You'll want to know: دروغگو.
دروغگو (dorughgu) — "a liar".
هیچکس حرف یک دروغگو را باور نمیکند. hichkas harf-e yek dorughgu râ bâvar nemikonad. Nobody believes a liar.
dorugh 'lie' + gu 'saying' (stem of goftan). Plural dorughguhâ.

The word, taken apart.
dorughgu is built on dorugh, 'lie', which EtymDB traces to Old Persian drauga (EtymDB), and gu, the present stem of goftan, 'to say': a lie-teller.
