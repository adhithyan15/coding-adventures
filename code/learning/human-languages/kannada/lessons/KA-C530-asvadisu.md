## You'll want to know: ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C530-BIDUVU530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸು** — *āsvādisu* — "to savour; to relish".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಪ್ರತಿ ಹಾಡನ್ನು ಕಣ್ಣು ಮುಚ್ಚಿ ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *ajja prati hāḍannu kaṇṇu mucci āsvādisuttāre.*
>
> Grandfather savours every song with his eyes closed.

Past (I …): ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸಿದೆ, *āsvādiside*.

Of food, music or a view.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಸ್ವಾದಿಸು** (*āsvādisu*) is built on Sanskrit *āsvāda*, "eating with relish, tasting, enjoying" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
