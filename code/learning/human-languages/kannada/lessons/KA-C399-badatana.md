## You'll want to know: ಬಡತನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C399-BADATANA399-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಡತನ** — *baḍatana* — "poverty".

> ಬಡತನದಿಂದ ಅವನು ಶಾಲೆ ಬಿಟ್ಟನು.
>
> *baḍatanadinda avanu śāle biṭṭanu.*
>
> He left school because of poverty.

ಬಡ (poor) + -ತನ (-ness).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಡತನ** (*baḍatana*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5222 lists Kannada *baḍa*, "poor", and *baḍatana*, "poverty", with the ending -*tana*, "-ness".
