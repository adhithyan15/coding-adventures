## You'll want to know: らいげつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C141-RAIGETSU]; assesses=[] -->

**らいげつ** — *raigetsu* — "next month". The **らい** at its start is the
same as in **らいしゅう**: "the one coming".

> **らいげつ うみに いきます。** — *raigetsu umi ni ikimasu* — "I will go
> to the sea next month."
