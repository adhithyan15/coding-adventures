## You'll want to know: die Behauptung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C274-GERUECHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Behauptung** — "the claim, the assertion".

> Diese Behauptung ist falsch.
>
> *This claim is false.*

Plural: *die Behauptungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Behauptung* is built on *behaupten* "to claim", which rests on *Haupt* "head", an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB).
