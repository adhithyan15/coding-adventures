# മുന്നിൽ പിന്നിൽ (munnil, pinnil) — in front of, behind

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C84-PLACE-01, ML-LEX-C84-PLACE-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *on top of* and *below*. You have the up-and-down pair; these two
are the other axis.

## You'll want to know: മുന്നിൽ, പിന്നിൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C84-PLACE-03, ML-LEX-C84-PLACE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**മുന്നിൽ** — *munnil* — in front of.

**പിന്നിൽ** — *pinnil* — behind.

Both go where the other two went, after a noun carrying the owner-ending, and
the set is now closed:

| | |
|---|---|
| **മുകളിൽ** | on top of |
| **താഴെ** | below |
| **മുന്നിൽ** | in front of |
| **പിന്നിൽ** | behind |

**കസേരയുടെ മുന്നിൽ** — in front of the chair. **കസേരയുടെ പിന്നിൽ** — behind the
chair.

The two new ones are a near-pair on the page as well as in meaning: *munnil* and
*pinnil* differ in their **first** sound and agree in everything after it. Say
them one after the other until the opening is what you hear, because that
opening is the only thing carrying the difference.
