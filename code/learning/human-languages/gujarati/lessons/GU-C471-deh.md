## You'll want to know: દેહ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C471-VAN471-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દેહ** — *deh* — "the body (in careful speech); build".

> કસરતને લીધે અરુણનો દેહ મજબૂત છે.
>
> *kasratne līdhe Aruṇno deh majbūt che.*
>
> Thanks to exercise, Arun has a strong build.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દેહ** (*deh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *deha*, "the body", as Monier-Williams records it, from a root meaning "to mould, to fashion"; everyday Gujarati says **શરીર** (*śarīr*).
