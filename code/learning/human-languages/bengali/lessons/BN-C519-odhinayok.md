## You'll want to know: অধিনায়ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C519-KHELA519-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অধিনায়ক** — *ôdhināyôk* — "a team captain".

> আমার বন্ধু দলের অধিনায়ক।
>
> *Āmār bôndhu dôler ôdhināyôk.*
>
> My friend is the captain of the team.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অধিনায়ক** (*ôdhināyôk*) joins Sanskrit *adhi*, "over, above", to *nāyaka*, "a guide, a leader" (Monier-Williams): the leader set over the rest.
