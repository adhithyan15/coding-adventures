Sanskrit, chapter 109: Pomegranate, Grapes, Jackfruit, Lemon, Cake.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
दाडिमम् (dāḍimam) — a pomegranate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a pumpkin, then the Sanskrit for a coconut.

You'll want to know: दाडिमम्.
दाडिमम् — dāḍimam — "a pomegranate".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dāḍimam]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dāḍimam, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dāḍimam]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for a pumpkin, then the Sanskrit for a coconut, then say dāḍimam again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does दाडिमम् (dāḍimam) mean? ("A pomegranate".) Say it once more.
