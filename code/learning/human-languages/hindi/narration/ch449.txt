Hindi, chapter 449: Night in the Fields.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सियार (siyār) — a jackal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to peck, then the Hindi for to prick.

You'll want to know: सियार.
सियार — siyār — "a jackal".
रात को खेतों में सियार घूमते हैं। rāt ko khetoṁ meṁ siyār ghūmte haiṁ. Jackals roam the fields at night.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
सियार (siyār) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit śṛgāla, "a jackal" (CDIAL 12578): the g between vowels has dropped and the vowels have run together.

The word, taken apart.
झींगुर (jhī̃gur) goes back to jhiṅgura, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5387) and glosses as a cockroach; it lists Hindi jhĩgur, "a cricket".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a firefly, then the Hindi for a cricket.

You'll want to know: चरना.
चरना — carnā — "to graze".
मैदान में गायें चर रही थीं। maidān meṁ gāyẽ car rahī thīṁ. Cows were grazing in the field.
Past (it …): वह भैंस दिन भर चरी, vah bhaĩs din bhar carī.

The word, taken apart.
चरना (carnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit carati, "moves" and also "grazes" (CDIAL 4686), which lists Hindi carnā, "to move about, graze".

Lesson 5 of 5.
चराना (carānā) — to take to graze; to graze (cattle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a cricket, then the Hindi for to graze.

You'll want to know: चराना.
चराना — carānā — "to take to graze; to graze (cattle)".
एक लड़का नदी के किनारे बकरियाँ चरा रहा था। ek laṛkā nadī ke kināre bakriyā̃ carā rahā thā. A boy was grazing goats on the river bank.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने भेड़ें चराईं, usne bheṛẽ carāīṁ.

The word, taken apart.
चराना (carānā) is the causative partner of चरना (carnā), from Sanskrit carati, "moves, grazes" (CDIAL 4686), which lists Hindi carānā, "to graze" a herd.
