## You'll want to know: مهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C336-HADIYYA336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مهر** — *mahr* — "a dowry, a bride gift".

> دفع العريس مهرا للعروس.
>
> *dafaʿa al-ʿarīs mahran li-l-ʿarūs.*
>
> The groom paid a bride gift to the bride.

The groom gives it to the bride; it is hers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مهر** (*mahr*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-h-r* (Lane), where *mahr* is the gift a man gives his bride; *muhr*, a foal, is written the same.
