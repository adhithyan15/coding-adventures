Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 190: Clothes and Jewels.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
धोती (dhotī) — a dhoti.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a threshold, then the Marwadi for an open-sided room.

You'll want to know: धोती.
धोती — dhotī — "a dhoti".
दादा धोती पैरै है। dādā dhotī pairai hai. Grandfather wears a dhoti.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
धोती (dhotī) comes from a reconstructed dhōtta, "cloth"; CDIAL lists Hindi, Gujarati and Sindhi dhotī (CDIAL 6881). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 5 of 7.
गहणो (gahṇo) — jewellery; a piece of jewellery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an outfit, then the Marwadi for a handkerchief.

You'll want to know: गहणो.
गहणो — gahṇo — "jewellery; a piece of jewellery".
म्हारो गहणो जूनो है। mhāro gahṇo jūno hai. My jewellery is old.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गहणो (gahṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit grahaṇa, "seizing", also "putting on (clothes)"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari gahaṇo, "a jewel" (CDIAL 4364).
