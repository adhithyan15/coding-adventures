## You'll want to know: indiscutível
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C341-PROVA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**indiscutível** — "indisputable, beyond question".

> O talento dela é indiscutível.
>
> *Her talent is beyond question.*

The opposite of *discutível*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indiscutível* is built from the prefix *in-* "not" and *discutível* "debatable", on the verb *discutir*, which comes from Latin *discutere* "to strike asunder" (Lewis & Short).
