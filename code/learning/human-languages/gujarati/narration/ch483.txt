Gujarati, chapter 483: Where It Hurts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વેદના (vednā) — agony; deep pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for flat on one's back, then the Gujarati for to bang.

You'll want to know: વેદના.
વેદના — vednā — "agony; deep pain".
દર્દીની વેદના જોઈને નર્સ ઉદાસ થઈ. dardīnī vednā joīne nars udās thaī. Seeing the patient's agony, the nurse grew sad.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વેદના (vednā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vedanā, "pain, torture, agony", as Monier-Williams records it.

You'll want to know: ચસકો.
ચસકો — casko — "a twinge; a sudden sharp pain".
સવારે કમરમાં ચસકો આવ્યો. savāre kamarmā̃ casko āvyo. This morning I felt a twinge in my back.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચસકો (casko) goes back to a reconstructed cassakk-, "throb, twitch, sudden pain"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati casak, "acute pain" (CDIAL 4730).
