## You'll want to know: ताकीद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C542-NIYAM542-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ताकीद** — *tākīd* — "a stern warning; a reprimand".

> उशिरा आल्यामुळे व्यवस्थापकाने त्याला ताकीद दिली.
>
> *uśirā ālyāmuḷe vyavasthāpakāne tyālā tākīd dilī.*
>
> Because he came late, the manager gave him a stern warning.

Feminine.

ताकीद देणे, to warn sternly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताकीद** (*tākīd*) is a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic *taʾkīd*, "emphasis, insistence".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
