Spanish, chapter 503: Ponerse en forma.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
adelgazar — to lose weight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the joke, then the Spanish for to translate.

You'll want to know: adelgazar.
adelgazar — "to lose weight".
Quiere adelgazar antes del verano. He wants to lose weight before the summer.
The opposite is engordar. It takes c before e: adelgacé.
