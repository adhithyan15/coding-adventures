## You'll want to know: گناہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C360-RAHM360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**گناہ** — *gunāh* — "a sin; a wrong".

> جھوٹ بولنا گناہ ہے۔
>
> *jhūṭ bolnā gunāh hai.*
>
> Telling lies is a sin.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گناہ** (*gunāh*) is usually traced to Persian *gunāh*, "a sin, a fault".
