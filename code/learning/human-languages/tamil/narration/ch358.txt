Tamil, chapter 358: Theories and Principles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கோட்பாடு (kōṭpāṭu) — a theory; a doctrine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for likewise, then the Tamil for to differ.

You'll want to know: கோட்பாடு.
கோட்பாடு — kōṭpāṭu — "a theory; a doctrine".
இந்தக் கோட்பாடு எனக்குப் புரியவில்லை. intak kōṭpāṭu eṉakkup puriyavillai. I do not understand this theory.
கோட்பாட்டை, the theory as an object: a noun in -டு doubles it to -ட்டு- before a case ending.

The word, taken apart.
கோட்பாடு (kōṭpāṭu) is native: kōḷ, "taking, opinion, tenet", the noun of koḷ, "to take, to hold" (DEDR 2151), with pāṭu, the noun of paṭu: "what is held to be so".

Lesson 2 of 5.
தத்துவம் (tattuvam) — a philosophy; a principle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to differ, then the Tamil for a theory.

You'll want to know: தத்துவம்.
தத்துவம் — tattuvam — "a philosophy; a principle".
என் அண்ணன் கல்லூரியில் தத்துவம் படிக்கிறான். eṉ aṇṇaṉ kallūriyil tattuvam paṭikkiṟāṉ. My elder brother studies philosophy at college.
வாழ்க்கைத் தத்துவம் (tattuvam), a philosophy of life. Plural தத்துவங்கள், principles.

The word, taken apart.
தத்துவம் (tattuvam) is borrowed from Sanskrit tattva, "the real state of things, truth" (CDIAL 5642). Tamil splits the cluster tv with a vowel: tattuvam.

Lesson 3 of 5.
சித்தாந்தம் (cittāntam) — an ideology; a doctrine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a theory, then the Tamil for a philosophy.

You'll want to know: சித்தாந்தம்.
சித்தாந்தம் — cittāntam — "an ideology; a doctrine".
அவருடைய சித்தாந்தம் எனக்குப் பிடிக்கவில்லை. avaruṭaiya cittāntam eṉakkup piṭikkavillai. I do not like his ideology.
A formal word for a whole system of ideas: அவருடைய சித்தாந்தம் (cittāntam), his ideology.

The word, taken apart.
சித்தாந்தம் (cittāntam) is borrowed from Sanskrit siddhānta, "established end, doctrine" (CDIAL 13404), from siddha, "settled", and anta, "end": a settled conclusion.

Lesson 4 of 5.
கொள்கை (koḷkai) — a policy; a principle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a philosophy, then the Tamil for an ideology.

You'll want to know: கொள்கை.
கொள்கை — koḷkai — "a policy; a principle".
அரசின் புதிய கொள்கையை மக்கள் வரவேற்றார்கள். araciṉ putiya koḷkaiyai makkaḷ varavēṟṟārkaḷ. People welcomed the new government policy.
Also a personal principle: அது என் கொள்கை (koḷkai), that is my principle.

The word, taken apart.
கொள்கை (koḷkai) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2151 lists Tamil koḷ, "to take, receive, regard", and koḷkai, "taking, acceptance", the noun that now means "a principle one holds".

Lesson 5 of 5.
நிறுவு (niṟuvu) — to establish; to found.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an ideology, then the Tamil for a policy.

You'll want to know: நிறுவு.
நிறுவு — niṟuvu — "to establish; to found".
அவர் இந்த ஊரில் ஒரு பள்ளியை நிறுவினார். avar inta ūril oru paḷḷiyai niṟuviṉār. He founded a school in this town.
Past (I …): நிறுவினேன், niṟuviṉēṉ.
What is founded takes -ஐ; a fact can be established too: உண்மையை நிறுவு (niṟuvu).

The word, taken apart.
நிறுவு (niṟuvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3675 lists Tamil niṟuvu, "to set up, establish", in the family of nil, "to stand": to make something stand.
