Lesson 1 of 5.
kaufen — to buy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a pupil, then the German for a pupil.

You'll want to know: kaufen.
kaufen — "to buy".

The word, taken apart.
kaufen from Old High German koufōn from Proto-Germanic kaupōną, from kaupô, "merchant" from Latin caupō, "tradesman, innkeeper" (EtymDB 2.1) — an early loan. English cheap comes from the same Latin word.
