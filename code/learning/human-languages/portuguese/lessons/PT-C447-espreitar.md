## You'll want to know: espreitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C447-PASSEIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**espreitar** — "to peek, to peep".

> As crianças espreitam pela janela.
>
> *The children peek through the window.*

*Espreitar uma oportunidade* — to watch for a chance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*espreitar* is inherited from Old Portuguese *espreitar* (EtymDB); it is usually traced to Latin *explicitāre* 'to unfold, to examine', but that is not settled.
