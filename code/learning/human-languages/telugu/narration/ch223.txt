Telugu, chapter 223: Personal, Private, Useless, Messy, Rude.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వ్యక్తిగత (vyaktigata) — personal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for different, then the Telugu for fair.

You'll want to know: వ్యక్తిగత.
వ్యక్తిగత — vyaktigata — "personal".

The word, taken apart.
వ్యక్తిగత (vyaktigata) is Sanskrit: vyakti, "individual", and gata, "gone", here "relating to" (CDIAL 4008).

Lesson 3 of 5.
పనికిరాని (panikirāni) — useless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for personal, then the Telugu for private.

You'll want to know: పనికిరాని.
పనికిరాని — panikirāni — "useless".

The word, taken apart.
పనికిరాని (panikirāni) is native Dravidian: pani, "work" (DEDR 3884), with -ki, "for", and rāni, "not coming", from rā-, "to come" (DEDR 5270): "not coming to use".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: చిందరవందర.
చిందరవందర — cindaravandara — "messy, in disarray".

The word, taken apart.
చిందరవందర (cindaravandara) is a rhyming pair built on cindara, which DEDR 1547 lists as cindaṟa, "fragment, piece; ruin", with Tamil citar, "to split".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: మొరటు.
మొరటు — moraṭu — "rude, coarse".

The word, taken apart.
మొరటు (moraṭu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4971 lists moraṭu, "rough, rude", with Tamil muraṭu, "roughness, rudeness". DEDR compares Sanskrit murumba, a lexicon word for crushed stone.
