Telugu, chapter 410: Giving and Serving.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సేవ (sēva) — service; selfless work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for solidarity, then the Telugu for to put into practice.

You'll want to know: సేవ.
సేవ — sēva — "service; selfless work".
ఆమె పేదవాళ్ళకు సేవ చేస్తుంది. āme pēdavāḷḷaku sēva cēstundi. She serves the poor.
సేవ (sēva) చేయు, to serve; ప్రజా సేవ (sēva), public service.

The word, taken apart.
సేవ (sēva) is borrowed from Sanskrit sevā, "going to; service, attendance; devotion" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for service, then the Telugu for a donation.

You'll want to know: స్వచ్ఛంద.
స్వచ్ఛంద — svacchanda — "voluntary".
ఆమె ఒక స్వచ్ఛంద సంస్థలో పని చేస్తుంది. āme oka svacchanda saṁsthalō pani cēstundi. She works for a voluntary organisation.
స్వచ్ఛంద (svacchanda) సంస్థ, a voluntary organisation; స్వచ్ఛందంగా, of one’s own will.

The word, taken apart.
స్వచ్ఛంద (svacchanda) is borrowed from Sanskrit svacchanda, "one’s own free will; acting at pleasure, independent" (Monier-Williams).

కార్యకర్త — kāryakarta — "an activist; a volunteer".
కార్యకర్తలు ఇంటింటికీ వెళ్ళి సహాయం చేశారు. kāryakartalu iṇṭiṇṭikī veḷḷi sahāyaṁ cēśāru. The volunteers went from house to house and helped.
Plural: కార్యకర్తలు, kāryakartalu.
A party worker or a volunteer.

The word, taken apart.
కార్యకర్త (kāryakarta) is built from Sanskrit kārya, "to be done; business" (CDIAL 3078), and kartṛ, "a doer, an agent" (Monier-Williams).
