## You'll want to know: رعد و برق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C475-SAEGHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رعد و برق** (*ra'd-o barq*) — "a thunderstorm; thunder and lightning".

> دیشب رعد و برق شدیدی بود.
>
> *Dishab ra'd-o barq-e shadidi bud.*
>
> Last night there was a heavy thunderstorm.

*ra'd* 'thunder' + *o* 'and' + *barq* 'lightning'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ra'd-o barq* joins two Arabic loans with Persian *o* 'and': *ra'd* from Arabic *raʿd* 'thunder' and *barq* from Arabic *barq* 'flashing, lightning' (EtymDB lists both).
