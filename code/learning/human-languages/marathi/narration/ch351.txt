Marathi, chapter 351: Linking Ideas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मात्र (mātra) — however; though.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to blame, then the Marathi for to accuse.

You'll want to know: मात्र.
मात्र — mātra — "however; though".
घर लहान आहे, मात्र खूप स्वच्छ आहे. ghar lahān āhe, mātra khūp svaccha āhe. The house is small; it is very clean, though.
Often after the first word: मी मात्र (mātra) घरीच राहिलो, I, however, stayed home.

The word, taken apart.
मात्र (mātra) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit mātra, "measure", which at the end of a compound meant "nothing but, only" (Monier-Williams); Marathi uses it on its own for "only" and "however".

Lesson 2 of 5.
तसेच (tasec) — likewise; as well as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to accuse, then the Marathi for however.

You'll want to know: तसेच.
तसेच — tasec — "likewise; as well as".
आम्ही पुस्तकं वाचली, तसेच गाणी ऐकली. āmhī pustakaṁ vāclī, tasec gāṇī aiklī. We read books and also listened to songs.
Formal; तसंच in speech. Built from तसे, in that way, and -च.

The word, taken apart.
तसेच (tasec) is built from तसे, "in that way", and the stressing ending -च. तसा is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi tasā, "suchlike", under Sanskrit tādṛśa, "suchlike" (CDIAL 5760).

Lesson 3 of 5.
किंबहुना (kimbahunā) — in fact; rather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for however, then the Marathi for likewise.

You'll want to know: किंबहुना.
किंबहुना — kimbahunā — "in fact; rather".
तो हुशार आहे, किंबहुना वर्गात सर्वात हुशार आहे. to huśār āhe, kimbahunā vargāt sarvāt huśār āhe. He is clever; in fact, the cleverest in the class.
Formal: it makes what was said stronger.

The word, taken apart.
किंबहुना (kimbahunā) is a tatsama phrase, taken whole from Sanskrit kiṁ, "what", and bahunā, "with more" (Monier-Williams): what need is there to say more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
तथापि (tathāpi) — nevertheless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for likewise, then the Marathi for in fact.

You'll want to know: तथापि.
तथापि — tathāpi — "nevertheless".
पाऊस पडत होता, तथापि कार्यक्रम वेळेवर सुरू झाला. pāūs paḍat hotā, tathāpi kāryakram veḷevar surū jhālā. It was raining; nevertheless, the programme began on time.
Formal and written; in speech पण or तरीही.

The word, taken apart.
तथापि (tathāpi) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit tathāpi, "even so, nevertheless" (CDIAL 5647), from tathā, "so", and api, "even".

Lesson 5 of 5.
उदाहरणार्थ (udāharaṇārtha) — for example.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for in fact, then the Marathi for nevertheless.

You'll want to know: उदाहरणार्थ.
उदाहरणार्थ — udāharaṇārtha — "for example".
मला फळं आवडतात, उदाहरणार्थ आंबा आणि केळं. malā phaḷaṁ āvaḍtāt, udāharaṇārtha āmbā āṇi keḷaṁ. I like fruit, for example mangoes and bananas.
उदाहरण + अर्थ: for the sake of an example.

The word, taken apart.
उदाहरणार्थ (udāharaṇārtha) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit udāharaṇa, "example", and artha, "aim, sake" (Monier-Williams).
