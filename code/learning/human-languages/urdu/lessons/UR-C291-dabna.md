## You'll want to know: دبنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C291-ZALZALA291-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دبنا** — *dabnā* — "to be pressed down; to be trapped under".

> کچھ لوگ چھت کے نیچے دب گئے۔
>
> *kuchh log chhat ke nīche dab gae.*
>
> Some people were trapped under the roof.

Past (he …): وہ دب گیا, *voh dab gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دبنا** (*dabnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *dabnā*, "to be covered", under *\*dabb*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6173). It is being pressed under a weight.
