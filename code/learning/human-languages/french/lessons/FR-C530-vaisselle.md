## You'll want to know: la vaisselle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C530-VAISSELLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la vaisselle** — "dishes, crockery".

> Ce soir, c'est mon frère qui fait la vaisselle.
>
> *Tonight it is my brother who does the dishes.*

*Faire la vaisselle* — to do the washing-up. In Switzerland also *relaver*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la vaisselle* is inherited, through Old French *vaissele*, from Vulgar Latin *vascella* (EtymDB), from Latin *vāsculum* "a small vessel", built on *vās* "a vessel" (Lewis & Short).
