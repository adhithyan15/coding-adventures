Malayalam, chapter 229: To set, To shine, To wilt, To doubt, To agree.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
അസ്തമിക്കുക (astamikkuka) — to set (of the sun).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to enjoy, then the Malayalam for to hate.

You'll want to know: അസ്തമിക്കുക.
അസ്തമിക്കുക — astamikkuka — "to set (of the sun)".

The word, taken apart.
അസ്തമിക്കുക (astamikkuka) is built on Sanskrit astam, "home", as in astam ēti, "goes home, sets", said of the sun (CDIAL 973).
