Arabic, chapter 356: Viewers and listeners.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
متابع (mutābiʿ) — a follower; an observer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an editorial, then the Arabic for to deny officially.

You'll want to know: متابع.
متابع — mutābiʿ — "a follower; an observer".
للمغنية مليون متابع. li-l-mughanniya milyūn mutābiʿ. The singer has a million followers.
Also someone who follows the news closely.

The word, taken apart.
متابع (mutābiʿ) is built on the native Arabic root t-b-ʿ (Lane), "to follow". Mutābiʿ is the participle of the taught tābaʿa, "one who keeps following".

Lesson 5 of 6.
غطى (ghaṭṭā) — to cover; to report on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a listener, then the Arabic for a photographer.

You'll want to know: غطى.
غطى — ghaṭṭā — "to cover; to report on".
غطى المراسل المباراة من الملعب. ghaṭṭā al-murāsil al-mubārāh min al-malʿab. The correspondent covered the match from the stadium.
Present: يغطي, yughaṭṭī.
Of a blanket too: غطت الأم الطفل.

The word, taken apart.
غطى (ghaṭṭā) is built on the native Arabic root gh-ṭ-w (Lane), the root of the taught ghiṭāʾ, a cover. It is Form II, "to put a cover over"; the press sense matches English cover and is usually taken as modelled on it.
