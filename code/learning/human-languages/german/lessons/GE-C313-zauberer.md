## You'll want to know: der Zauberer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C313-MAERCHEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zauberer** — "the wizard, the magician".

> Der Zauberer hatte einen langen weißen Bart.
>
> *The wizard had a long white beard.*

Plural: *die Zauberer*. At a party, a magician.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zauberer* is built on *Zauber* "magic", Old High German *zoubar*, from Proto-Germanic *taubrą* "magic" (EtymDB).
