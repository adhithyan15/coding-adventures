# కథకుడు (kathakuḍu) — a storyteller; a narrator

## You'll want to know: కథకుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C372-KATHAKUDU372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కథకుడు** — *kathakuḍu* — "a storyteller; a narrator".

> కథకుడు పిల్లలకు ఒక వింత కథ చెప్పాడు.
>
> *kathakuḍu pillalaku oka vinta katha ceppāḍu.*
>
> The storyteller told the children a strange story.

కథ, a story, with -కుడు: one who tells it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కథకుడు** (*kathakuḍu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kathaka*, "a narrator, one who recites a story" (Monier-Williams), built on *kath*, "to tell".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
