## You'll want to know: लपेटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C432-MAAL432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लपेटना** — *lapeṭnā* — "to wrap".

> दुकानदार ने किताब काग़ज़ में लपेटी।
>
> *dukāndār ne kitāb kāġaz meṁ lapeṭī.*
>
> The shopkeeper wrapped the book in paper.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने तोहफ़ा काग़ज़ में लपेटा, *usne tohfā kāġaz meṁ lapeṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लपेटना** (*lapeṭnā*) goes back to *\*lappēṭṭ*, "wrap", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10942), which lists Hindi *lapeṭnā*, "to wrap, fold, wind".
