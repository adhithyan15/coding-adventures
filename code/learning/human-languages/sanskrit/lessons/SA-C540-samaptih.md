## You'll want to know: समाप्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C540-ASANNA540-03]; assesses=[] -->

**समाप्तिः** — *samāptiḥ* — "completion".

> यदि कार्यस्य समाप्तिः भवति, तर्हि वयं विश्रामं करिष्यामः।
>
> *yadi kāryasya samāptiḥ bhavati, tarhi vayaṃ viśrāmaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ.*
>
> When the work is finished, we will rest.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समाप्तिः** (*samāptiḥ*) is built on *sam*, "wholly", and the root √āp, "to reach", which also gives *samāpayati*, "finishes"; MW gives *samāpti* as accomplishment, completion, conclusion (MW).
