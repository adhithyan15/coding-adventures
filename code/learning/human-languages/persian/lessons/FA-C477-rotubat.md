## You'll want to know: رطوبت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C477-SHARJI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رطوبت** (*rotubat*) — "humidity, damp".

> رطوبت هوا امروز خیلی بالاست.
>
> *Rotubat-e havâ emruz kheyli bâlâst.*
>
> The humidity is very high today.

*Martub* — humid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rotubat* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *ruṭūba* 'moisture, damp'. EtymDB does not list it.
