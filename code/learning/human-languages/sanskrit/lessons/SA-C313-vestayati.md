## You'll want to know: वेष्टयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C313-UPADRAVA313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वेष्टयति** — *veṣṭayati* — "surrounds".

> आरक्षकाः विपणिं वेष्टयन्ति।
>
> *ārakṣakāḥ vipaṇiṃ veṣṭayanti.*
>
> The police surround the market.

Past (he …): सः विपणिं वेष्टितवान्, *saḥ vipaṇiṃ veṣṭitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेष्टयति** (*veṣṭayati*) comes from the root √veṣṭ, "to wind or twist round"; MW gives its causative *veṣṭayati* as to wrap up, enclose, surround (MW).
