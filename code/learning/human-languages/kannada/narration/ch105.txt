Kannada, chapter 105: Wishes and Warmth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು (śubhāśayagaḷu) — best wishes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a driver, then the Kannada for a shop.

You'll want to know: ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು.
ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು (śubhāśayagaḷu) — "best wishes", ಶುಭ (śubha), "good", with ಆಶಯ (āśaya), "wish", in the plural.

What you've built.
Good wishes, many of them.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: śubhāśayagaḷu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: śubhāśayagaḷu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say śubhāśayagaḷu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for a driver, then the Kannada for a shop, then say śubhāśayagaḷu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು (śubhāśayagaḷu) mean? ("Best wishes".) Say it once more.

ಹಬ್ಬದ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು (habbada śubhāśayagaḷu) — "festival greetings".
