## You'll want to know: ಕೈಚೀಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C469-VARANTI469-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈಚೀಲ** — *kaicīla* — "a handbag".

> ಅಮ್ಮ ತಮ್ಮ ಕೈಚೀಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಪರ್ಸ್ ಇಟ್ಟರು.
>
> *amma tamma kaicīladalli pars iṭṭaru.*
>
> Mother put her purse in her handbag.

ಕೈ, hand, plus ಚೀಲ, a bag.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈಚೀಲ** (*kaicīla*) is a native Kannada compound of *kai*, "hand", and ಚೀಲ, "a bag, a sack" (DEDR 2632).
