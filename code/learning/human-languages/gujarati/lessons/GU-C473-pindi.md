## You'll want to know: પિંડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C473-JANGH473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પિંડી** — *pinḍī* — "the calf of the leg".

> લાંબું ચાલ્યા પછી મારી પિંડી દુખે છે.
>
> *lā̃bũ cālyā pachī mārī pinḍī dukhe che.*
>
> After a long walk my calf aches.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પિંડી** (*pinḍī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *piṇḍa*, "lump", which also meant "calf of the leg"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8168).
