Telugu, chapter 153: Nobody, Never, Nowhere, Anyone, Anything.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎవరూ (evarū) — nobody (with a negative verb).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for shade, then the Telugu for a stream.

You'll want to know: ఎవరూ.
ఎవరూ — evarū — "nobody (with a negative verb)". Telugu negates a past verb with లేదు (lēdu) joined onto it: ఎవరూ రాలేదు — evarū rālēdu — "Nobody came." To say something is not a thing, it uses కాదు (kādu): ఇది నా ఇల్లు కాదు — idi nā illu kādu — "This is not my house."

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఎవరైనా (evarainā) — anyone, someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for never, then the Telugu for nowhere.

You'll want to know: ఎవరైనా.
ఎవరైనా — evarainā — "anyone, someone". A yes-or-no question ends in -ఆ (-ā): ఉన్నారు becomes ఉన్నారా. అక్కడ ఎవరైనా ఉన్నారా? — akkaḍa evarainā unnārā? — "Is anyone there?"
