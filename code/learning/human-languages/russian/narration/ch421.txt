Russian, chapter 421: Take-off and landing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
взлетать (vzletát) — to take off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a customs declaration, then the Russian for to declare.

You'll want to know: взлетать.
взлетать (vzletát) — "to take off".
Самолёт медленно взлетает над морем. Samolyót médlenno vzletáyet nad mórem. The plane slowly takes off over the sea.
Imperfective; perfective взлететь (взлечу, взлетишь). Вз- means up.

The word, taken apart.
взлетать (vzletát) is built from вз- 'up' and летать 'to fly'. EtymDB lists the noun взлёт (vzlyot) from Proto-Slavic vŭzletŭ.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Во время взлёта нельзя вставать. Vo vrémya vzlyóta nelzyá vstavát. You may not stand up during take-off.
Masculine. Взлётная полоса — the runway.

The word, taken apart.
взлёт (vzlyot) is inherited from Proto-Slavic vŭzletŭ (EtymDB), built from vŭz- 'up' and the root of летать 'to fly'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
приземляться (prizemlyátsya) — to land.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to take off, then the Russian for take-off.

You'll want to know: приземляться.
приземляться (prizemlyátsya) — "to land".
Самолёт приземляется поздно ночью. Samolyót prizemlyáyetsya pózdno nóchyu. The plane lands late at night.
Imperfective; perfective приземлиться. Built on земля — the ground.

The word, taken apart.
приземляться (prizemlyátsya) is built from при- 'to', земля 'earth, the ground' and -ся. Земля is inherited from Proto-Slavic zemľa 'earth, land' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
иллюминатор (illyuminátor) — a porthole, a plane window.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for take-off, then the Russian for to land.

You'll want to know: иллюминатор.
иллюминатор (illyuminátor) — "a porthole, a plane window".
Ребёнок всю дорогу смотрел в иллюминатор. Rebyónok vsyu dorógu smotrél v illyuminátor. The child looked out of the window the whole way.
Masculine. The window of a ship or a plane; a house window is окно.

The word, taken apart.
иллюминатор (illyuminátor) is usually traced to Latin illūmināre 'to light up' (EtymDB): a round window that lets the light in. EtymDB does not list the Russian word, so its route is hedged.

Пассажиры спускаются по трапу. Passazhíry spuskáyutsya po trápu. The passengers come down the steps.
Masculine. По трапу — up or down the steps.

The word, taken apart.
трап was borrowed from Dutch trap (EtymDB), the everyday Dutch word for stairs.
