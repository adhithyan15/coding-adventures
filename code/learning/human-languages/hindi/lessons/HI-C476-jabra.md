## You'll want to know: जबड़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C476-CHEHRA476-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जबड़ा** — *jabṛā* — "a jaw".

> गिरने से अरुण के जबड़े में चोट लगी।
>
> *girne se Aruṇ ke jabṛe meṁ coṭ lagī.*
>
> Arun hurt his jaw in a fall.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जबड़ा** (*jabṛā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *jābṛā*, "lower jaw-bone, jaw", under Sanskrit *jámbha*, "tooth, jaw" (CDIAL 5137); *jabṛā* is the common spoken form.
