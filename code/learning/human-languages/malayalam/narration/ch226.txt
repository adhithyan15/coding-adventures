Malayalam, chapter 226: To discuss, To explain, To prepare, To finish, To check.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ചർച്ച ചെയ്യുക (carcca ceyyuka) — to discuss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to return, then the Malayalam for to take.

You'll want to know: ചർച്ച ചെയ്യുക.
ചർച്ച ചെയ്യുക — carcca ceyyuka — "to discuss".

The word, taken apart.
ചർച്ച ചെയ്യുക (carcca ceyyuka) joins Sanskrit carcā, "repetition, going over" (CDIAL 4695), to native ceyyuka, "to do" (DEDR 1957).
