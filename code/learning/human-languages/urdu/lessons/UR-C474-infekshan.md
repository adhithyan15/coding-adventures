## You'll want to know: انفیکشن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C474-SPREAD474-03]; assesses=[] -->

**انفیکشن** — *infekshan* — "an infection".

> زخم میں انفیکشن ہو گیا۔
>
> *zakhm meṅ infekshan ho gayā.*
>
> The wound became infected.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انفیکشن** (*infekshan*) is borrowed from English *infection*.
