Portuguese, chapter 39: Child, Neighbour, Guest, Boy, Girl.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a criança — a child.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a grandson, then the Portuguese for a granddaughter.

You'll want to know: a criança.
a criança — "a child".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does a criança mean? ("A child".) Say it once more.
