## You'll want to know: ઉજાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C303-VAVAZODU303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉજાડવું** — *ujāḍvũ* — "to lay waste".

> વાવાઝોડાએ આખું ગામ ઉજાડ્યું.
>
> *vāvājhoḍāe ākhũ gām ujāḍyũ.*
>
> The cyclone laid waste to the whole village.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ખેતર ઉજાડ્યું, *teṇe khetar ujāḍyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉજાડવું** (*ujāḍvũ*) comes from *\*ujjaṭati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to lay waste" (CDIAL 1661). A storm, a war or a flood **ઉજાડે છે**: it leaves a place empty.
