Spanish, chapter 690: Un día de playa.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bañarse — to have a swim; to have a bath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the marsh, then the Spanish for the cliff.

You'll want to know: bañarse.
bañarse — "to have a swim; to have a bath".
Nos bañamos en el mar por la mañana. We swim in the sea in the morning.
At the beach, bañarse is to go in the water, not to swim lengths.

The word, taken apart.
bañarse is built on baño "bath", which is inherited from Latin balneum "a bath".

Lesson 3 of 5.
la sombrilla — the sunshade, the beach umbrella.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to have a swim, then the Spanish for to get a tan.

You'll want to know: la sombrilla.
la sombrilla — "the sunshade, the beach umbrella".
Ponemos la sombrilla cerca del agua. We put the sunshade up near the water.
El paraguas is for rain; la sombrilla is for sun.

The word, taken apart.
sombrilla is a little sombra "shade". The origin of sombra is not settled; it is often linked to Latin umbra "shade".
