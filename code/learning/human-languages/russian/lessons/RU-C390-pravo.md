## You'll want to know: право
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C390-PORYADOK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**право** (*právo*) — "a right; law (as a subject)".

> Каждый имеет право на отдых.
>
> *Kázhdyy iméyet právo na ótdykh.*
>
> Everyone has the right to rest.

Neuter. *Иметь право на* + accusative, or + infinitive. It is also law as a subject: *изучать право*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**право** is built on *правый* "right, correct", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*pravŭ* "right, correct" (EtymDB): a right is what is due by what is correct.
