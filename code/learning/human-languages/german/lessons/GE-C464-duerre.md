## You'll want to know: die Dürre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C464-HITZEWELLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Dürre** — "the drought".

> Nach der langen Dürre ist das Gras braun.
>
> *After the long drought the grass is brown.*

Plural: *die Dürren*. *Dürr* — very dry; thin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Dürre* is built on *dürr* 'dry, withered', an inherited Germanic adjective related to *Durst* 'thirst'. EtymDB does not list the noun.
