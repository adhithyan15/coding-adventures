# કાઢવું (kāḍhvũ) — to take out

## You'll want to know: કાઢવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C202-THEFT202-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કાઢવું** — *kāḍhvũ* — "to take out".

> **તેણે થેલીમાંથી પૈસા કાઢ્યા.** — *teṇe thelīmā̃thī paisā kāḍhyā* — "He took the money out of the bag."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાઢવું** (*kāḍhvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to pull out", under *\*kaḍḍhati*, "pulls, draws", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 2660).
