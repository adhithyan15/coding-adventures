## You'll want to know: قابلمه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C358-QABLAME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قابلمه** (*qâblame*) — "a cooking pot, a saucepan".

> آب را در قابلمه ریخت.
>
> *Âb râ dar qâblame rikht.*
>
> She poured the water into the pot.

Plural *qâblame-hâ*. The lid is *dar-e qâblame*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *qâblame* "a cooking pot" is not settled, and EtymDB lists no entry.
