## You'll want to know: आपटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C403-KHADDA403-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आपटणे** — *āpaṭṇe* — "to bang against".

> खड्ड्यामुळे माझं डोकं खिडकीला आपटलं.
>
> *khaḍḍyāmuḷe mājhaṁ ḍokaṁ khiḍkīlā āpaṭlaṁ.*
>
> Because of the pothole my head banged against the window.

Past (he …): तो आपटला, *to āpaṭlā*.

What gets hit takes -ला or -वर: दाराला आपटणे, to bang against the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपटणे** (*āpaṭṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *apaṭṇẽ*, "to strike against", under Sanskrit *āpāṭyate* (CDIAL 1203).
