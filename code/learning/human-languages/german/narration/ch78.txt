Lesson 1 of 5.
der Schrank — a cupboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a room, then the German for a bed.

You'll want to know: der Schrank.
der Schrank — "a cupboard".

The word, taken apart.
Schrank from Middle High German schranc, "a closed-off space" — inherited, and related to the word for "a barrier". Its older history is uncertain, and EtymDB 2.1 has no source for it.

The word, taken apart.
Boden from Old High German bodam, "floor, bottom" from Proto-Germanic butmaz — inherited, the same word as English bottom. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Treppe — the stairs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a cupboard, then the German for the floor; the ground.

You'll want to know: die Treppe.
die Treppe — "the stairs".

The word, taken apart.
Treppe came into standard German from Low German: Middle Low German treppe meant "step, stair" (EtymDB 2.1). It is related to an old verb for "to tread".
