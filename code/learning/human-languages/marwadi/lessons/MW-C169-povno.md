## You'll want to know: पोवणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C169-ROTI169-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पोवणो** — *povṇo* — "to knead (dough); to make rotis by hand".

> मां दिनूगै रोटी पोवै है।
>
> *mā̃ dinūgai roṭī povai hai.*
>
> Mother makes the rotis by hand early in the morning.

Past (I …): म्हैं रोटी पोई, *mhaĩ roṭī poī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोवणो** (*povṇo*) is inherited, by CDIAL's account, from Sanskrit *pratudati*, "cuts through, pierces"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *povaï* twice, as "kneads (bread)" and as "threads (beads)" (CDIAL 8627).
