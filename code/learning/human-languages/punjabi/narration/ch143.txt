Punjabi, chapter 143: Dry, Dirty, Sour, Bitter, Sharp.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਸੁੱਕਾ (sukkā) — dry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for narrow, then the Punjabi for wet.
[pause 1 second]
Read on without stopping: ਮਨਨ ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਹੈ। ਉਹ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹੈ।.

You'll want to know: ਸੁੱਕਾ.
ਸੁੱਕਾ — sukkā — "dry".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sukkā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sukkā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sukkā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for narrow, then the Punjabi for wet, then say sukkā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਸੁੱਕਾ (sukkā) mean? ("Dry".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: a ship, a traveller, an office, a factory, a library]

A heel? (ਅੱਡੀ, aḍḍī.) An ankle? (ਗਿੱਟਾ, giṭṭā.) A ship? (ਜਹਾਜ਼, jahāz.) A traveller? (ਮੁਸਾਫ਼ਰ, musāfar.) An office? (ਦਫ਼ਤਰ, daftar.) A factory? (ਕਾਰਖ਼ਾਨਾ, kārkhānā.) A library? (ਲਾਇਬ੍ਰੇਰੀ, lāibrerī.) A town square? (ਚੌਂਕ, cauṅk.) A garden? (ਬਾਗ਼, bāg.) A pencil? (ਪੈਨਸਿਲ, painsil.) A class? (ਜਮਾਤ, jamāt.) A teacher? (ਅਧਿਆਪਕ, adhiāpak.)
