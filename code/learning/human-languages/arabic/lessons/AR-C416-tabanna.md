## You'll want to know: تبنى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C416-BADAL416-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تبنى** — *tabannā* — "to adopt".

> تبنت الأسرة طفلا صغيرا.
>
> *tabannat al-usra ṭiflan ṣaghīran.*
>
> The family adopted a small child.

Present: يتبنى, *yatabannā*.

Also to adopt an idea: تبنى الفكرة.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تبنى** (*tabannā*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-n-y*, "to build" (Lane). It is Form V, to take someone as one's son, *ibn*, a word Lane files under this root: to adopt.
