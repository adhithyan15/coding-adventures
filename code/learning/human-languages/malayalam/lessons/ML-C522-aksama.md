## You'll want to know: അക്ഷമ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C522-JIJNASA522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**അക്ഷമ** — *akṣama* — "impatience".

> ബസ് വൈകിയപ്പോൾ യാത്രക്കാരുടെ അക്ഷമ കൂടി.
>
> *basŭ vaikiyappōḷ yātrakkāruṭe akṣama kūṭi.*
>
> As the bus got later, the passengers' impatience grew.

അ- not + ക്ഷമ patience, the word inside ക്ഷമിക്കണം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അക്ഷമ** (*akṣama*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *akṣamā*, "impatience" (Monier-Williams), *a-*, "not", and *kṣamā*, "patience".
