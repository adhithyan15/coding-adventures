## You'll want to know: der Krieg
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C318-KRIEG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Krieg** — "the war".

> Der Krieg dauerte sechs Jahre.
>
> *The war lasted six years.*

Plural: *die Kriege*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Krieg* is inherited from Middle High German *kriec*, Old High German *krieg*, from Proto-Germanic *krīgaz* (EtymDB); it is usually said to have first meant "effort, struggle".
