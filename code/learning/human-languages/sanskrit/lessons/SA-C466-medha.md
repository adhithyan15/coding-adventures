## You'll want to know: मेधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C466-MEDHA466-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मेधा** — *medhā* — "mental vigour".

> बालस्य मेधा तीक्ष्णा अस्ति।
>
> *bālasya medhā tīkṣṇā asti.*
>
> The boy has a sharp mind.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेधा** (*medhā*) MW gives "mental vigour or power, intelligence, prudence, wisdom"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
