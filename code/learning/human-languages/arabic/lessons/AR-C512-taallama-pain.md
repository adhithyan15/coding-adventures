# تألم (taʾallama) — to be in pain

## You'll want to know: تألم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C512-SHIFA512-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تألم** — *taʾallama* — "to be in pain".

> تألم الرجل كثيرا من ظهره.
>
> *taʾallama ar-rajul kathīran min ẓahrihi.*
>
> The man suffered a lot from his back.

Present: يتألم, *yataʾallam*.

It takes من for the place: تألم من ظهره.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تألم** (*taʾallama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-l-m*, "pain" (Lane). Alam, pain, which you know, is from the same root; Form V is to feel it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
