## You'll want to know: جوش آوردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C427-TAMIRGAH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جوش آوردن** (*jush âvardan*) — "to overheat (of an engine); to boil over; to lose your temper".

> در گرمای تابستان ماشین ما جوش آورد.
>
> *Dar garmâ-ye tâbestân mâshin-e mâ jush âvard.*
>
> In the summer heat our car overheated.

*jush* 'boiling' + *âvardan* 'to bring'. Said of people too: *jush âvard* — he lost his temper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jush âvardan* is built from *jush* 'boiling, simmering', a native word that EtymDB lists, and *âvardan* 'to bring', usually traced to Middle Persian *āwurdan*.
