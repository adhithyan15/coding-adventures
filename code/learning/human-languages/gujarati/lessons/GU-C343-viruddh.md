# વિરુદ્ધ (viruddh) — against

## You'll want to know: વિરુદ્ધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C343-TEKO343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વિરુદ્ધ** — *viruddh* — "against".

> ઘણા માણસો આ યોજનાની વિરુદ્ધ છે.
>
> *ghaṇā māṇso ā yojnānī viruddh che.*
>
> Many people are against this plan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિરુદ્ધ** (*viruddh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *viruddha*, "opposed, hindered" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
