# ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ — the garden is not open

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-STATUS-KHULLHA-OPEN-01, PA-STATUS-BAND-CLOSED-01, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *the garden is open in the morning*, then *the garden is
closed*. Then *I don't drink tea*.

## Grammar Lens: when ਹੈ is the verb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-COPULA-212]; assesses=[PA-STATUS-KHULLHA-OPEN-01, PA-STATUS-BAND-CLOSED-01, PA-LEX-C138-THINGS138-02, PA-NEG-CLAUSE-01] -->

In **ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ** the verb is **ਹੈ** itself. So **ਨਹੀਂ** goes directly before
**ਹੈ**:

> **ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।** — *bāgh khullhā nahīṁ hai.* — The garden is not open.

> **ਬਾਗ਼ ਬੰਦ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।** — *bāgh band nahīṁ hai.* — The garden is not closed.

> **ਉਹ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।** — *uh ghar vichch nahīṁ hai.* — He is not at home.
