## You'll want to know: die Galerie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C519-GALERIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Galerie** — "gallery".

> In der Galerie hängen Bilder von jungen Künstlern.
>
> *Pictures by young artists hang in the gallery.*

Plural: *die Galerien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Galerie* came into German from Italian *galleria*, through French *galerie*; the older origin of the Italian word is not settled, and EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
