## Chapter 65 closes the pre-A1 writing runway

Three short lessons now join two fully known sentences into Sanskrit's first
produced Devanagari text: **नमस्ते। अहं संस्कृतं वदामि।** The learner copies
the visible text, reproduces it after a delay, and finally writes it from
meaning alone with no Devanagari model or romanized cue. Every word, sign,
conjunct, anusvāra, vocalic-ṛ mark, and danda was taught earlier, so the bridge
adds no hidden vocabulary or form load. Each lesson remains at three minutes or
less, and only the complete Devanagari text earns credit.
