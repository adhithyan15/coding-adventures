## You'll want to know: ਖਿਝਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C327-UPSET327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਿਝਣਾ** — *khijhṇā* — "to get annoyed".

> ਰੌਲੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਾਦਾ ਜੀ ਖਿਝਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *raule ton dādā jī khijhde han.*
>
> Grandfather gets annoyed at noise.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਭੀੜ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਖਿਝਿਆ, *maiṁ bhīṛ vich bahut khijhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਿਝਣਾ** (*khijhṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kṣīyate*, "is worn away, is injured"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khijhṇā*, "to be vexed", under it (CDIAL 3695).
