# আন্দোলন (āndolôn) — a movement; a campaign

## You'll want to know: আন্দোলন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C318-BIPLOB318-02]; assesses=[] -->

**আন্দোলন** — *āndolôn* — "a movement; a campaign".

> শহরে ছাত্রদের একটা বড় আন্দোলন শুরু হল।
>
> *shôhôre chhātrôder ekṭā bôṛo āndolôn shuru hôlô.*
>
> A big student movement began in the city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আন্দোলন** (*āndolôn*) is Sanskrit *āndolana*, 'swinging, trembling' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a political movement', a people set swaying, is the modern one.
