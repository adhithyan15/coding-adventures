## You'll want to know: das Ladegerät
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C392-GERAET-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Ladegerät** — "the charger".

> Ich habe mein Ladegerät im Hotel vergessen.
>
> *I left my charger at the hotel.*

Plural: *die Ladegeräte*. The cable alone is *das Ladekabel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ladegerät* joins *laden* "to load", Old High German *hladan* (EtymDB), to *Gerät* "device", Middle High German *geræte* "equipment" (EtymDB).
