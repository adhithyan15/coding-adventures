German, chapter 410: Ein kleiner Unfall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Auffahrunfall — the rear-end collision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to close, then the German for the motorway service area.

You'll want to know: der Auffahrunfall.
der Auffahrunfall — "the rear-end collision".
Im Stau gibt es einen kleinen Auffahrunfall. There is a small rear-end collision in the traffic jam.
Plural: die Auffahrunfälle.

The word, taken apart.
Auffahrunfall is built from auffahren 'to drive into the car in front' and Unfall 'accident', inherited from Middle High German unval 'mishap' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
auffahren — to drive into the back (of another car); to drive up.

You'll want to know: auffahren.
auffahren — "to drive into the back (of another car); to drive up".
Der Fahrer hat nicht gebremst und ist aufgefahren. The driver did not brake and drove into the car in front.
Separable; Perfekt with sein: er ist auf das Auto aufgefahren.

The word, taken apart.
auffahren is built from auf 'onto, up' and the inherited verb fahren 'to travel, to drive', Proto-Germanic faraną (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
schleudern — to skid; to fling; to spin (washing).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the rear-end collision, then the German for to drive into the back.

You'll want to know: schleudern.
schleudern — "to skid; to fling; to spin (washing)".
Bei Regen schleudert das Auto in der Kurve. In the rain the car skids on the bend.
Perfekt with sein for a car: das Auto ist geschleudert. A washing machine also schleudert: it spins.

The word, taken apart.
schleudern is built on Schleuder 'sling', first for throwing a stone. EtymDB lists the verb without a source, and the origin of Schleuder is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Fußgänger — the pedestrian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to drive into the back, then the German for to skid.

You'll want to know: der Fußgänger.
der Fußgänger — "the pedestrian".
Der Fußgänger geht bei Rot über die Straße. The pedestrian crosses the road on a red light.
Same form in the plural. A woman: die Fußgängerin.

The word, taken apart.
Fußgänger is inherited from Middle High German vuozgenger (EtymDB), built from Fuß 'foot' and Gänger 'goer', from gehen. Dutch voetganger is a cousin.

Lesson 5 of 5.

der Radfahrer — "the cyclist".
In der Stadt gibt es viele Radfahrer. There are lots of cyclists in the town.
Same form in the plural. A woman: die Radfahrerin. Rad fahren — to cycle.

The word, taken apart.
Radfahrer is built from Rad 'wheel', short for Fahrrad 'bicycle', and Fahrer 'rider, driver', from the inherited verb fahren (EtymDB).
