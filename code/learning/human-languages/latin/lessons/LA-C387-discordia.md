## You'll want to know: discordia, discordiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C387-SEDITIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**discordia, discordiae** — "disagreement, discord".

> Discordia cīvium urbem perdidit.
>
> *The discord of the citizens ruined the city.*

*discordia, discordiae* f., *dis-* apart + *cor*: hearts apart, the opposite of *concordia*. English *discord*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discordia* is built on *discors* "at odds" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *dis-* "apart" and *cor, cordis* "heart". English *discord* comes from it.
