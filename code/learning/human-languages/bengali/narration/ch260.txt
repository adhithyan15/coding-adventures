Bengali, chapter 260: Longing, Prize, Punishment, Competition, Victory.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সাধ (sādh) — a longing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an application, then the Bengali for an identity card.

You'll want to know: সাধ.
সাধ — sādh — "a longing".

The word, taken apart.
সাধ (sādh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śraddhā, "trust; longing" (CDIAL 12678). CDIAL lists Bengali sādh, "wish, fancy".
