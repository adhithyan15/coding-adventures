Persian, chapter 409: Government and elections.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حکومت (hokumat) — a government, rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for corruption, then the Persian for a charge.

You'll want to know: حکومت.
حکومت (hokumat) — "a government, rule".
در آن زمان حکومت در دست یک پادشاه بود. dar ân zamân hokumat dar dast-e yek pâdeshâh bud. At that time power was in the hands of a king.
Hokumat kardan — to rule. Dowlat is the government of the day.

The word, taken apart.
حکومت (hokumat) was borrowed from Arabic ḥukūma "government" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as ḥukm "ruling".

Lesson 3 of 5.
انتخابات (entekhâbât) — an election, elections.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a government, then the Persian for politics.

You'll want to know: انتخابات.
انتخابات (entekhâbât) — "an election, elections".
انتخابات سال آینده برگزار میشود. entekhâbât sâl-e âyande bargozâr mishavad. The election will be held next year.
The Arabic-style plural of entekhâb "choice", the usual word for an election.

The word, taken apart.
انتخابات (entekhâbât) is the Arabic-style plural of entekhâb "choice", from Arabic intikhāb "choosing": an election is many choices.

Lesson 4 of 5.
رای دادن (ra'y dâdan) — to vote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for politics, then the Persian for an election.

You'll want to know: رای دادن.
رای دادن (ra'y dâdan) — "to vote".
ما فردا رای میدهیم. mâ fardâ ra'y midahim. We vote tomorrow.
Ra'y "opinion; vote" + dâdan (stem deh-). Be kasi ra'y dâdan — to vote for someone. Many writers put a hamza on the alef of ra'y.

The word, taken apart.
رای is usually traced to Arabic ra'y "opinion, view"; with dâdan "to give" (EtymDB traces it to PIE deh₃-), you give your opinion at the ballot box.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مجلس (majles) — parliament; a gathering.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an election, then the Persian for to vote.

You'll want to know: مجلس.
مجلس (majles) — "parliament; a gathering".
نماینده ما در مجلس سخنرانی کرد. namâyande-ye mâ dar majles sokhanrâni kard. Our representative gave a speech in parliament.
Iran's parliament is the majles. Also a formal gathering.

The word, taken apart.
مجلس (majles) was borrowed from Arabic majlis "place of sitting, session" (EtymDB): a parliament is where its members sit.
