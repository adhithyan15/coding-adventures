## You'll want to know: злиться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C472-ANGER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**злиться** (*zlítsya*) — "to be angry".

> Папа злится, когда мы опаздываем.
>
> *Pápa zlítsya, kogdá my opázdyvayem.*
>
> Dad gets angry when we're late.

Imperfective; perfective разозлиться. *Злиться на кого* — to be angry with someone. A little more colloquial than *сердиться*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*злиться* is built on *злой* 'angry, wicked', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *zъlъ* 'bad, evil' (EtymDB).
