## You'll want to know: कुंज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C447-JHADI447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंज** — *kuñj* — "a bower, a grove".

> बगीचे के कोने में एक छोटा कुंज है।
>
> *bagīce ke kone meṁ ek choṭā kuñj hai.*
>
> There is a little bower in the corner of the garden.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंज** (*kuñj*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kuñja*, "a place overrun with plants or creepers, a bower" (Monier-Williams).
