Punjabi, chapter 287: When the Rain Comes.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਬੂੰਦ (būnd) — a drop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a squall, then the Punjabi for to flash.

You'll want to know: ਬੂੰਦ.
ਬੂੰਦ — būnd — "a drop".
ਛੱਤ ਤੋਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਦੀ ਇੱਕ ਬੂੰਦ ਡਿੱਗੀ। chhatt ton pāṇī dī ikk būnd ḍiggī. A drop of water fell from the roof.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਬੂੰਦ (būnd) is inherited from Sanskrit bindu, "drop, spot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bund, "drop", under it (CDIAL 9240).

You'll want to know: ਭਿੱਜਣਾ.
ਭਿੱਜਣਾ — bhijjṇā — "to get wet".
ਜੇ ਮੀਂਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ ਤਾਂ ਅਸੀਂ ਭਿੱਜਾਂਗੇ। je mīṁh āvegā tāṁ asīṁ bhijjāṅge. If the rain comes, we will get wet.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਭਿੱਜਿਆ, maiṁ mīṁh vich bhijjiā.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ਟਪਕਣਾ (ṭapakṇā) — to drip; to leak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to get wet, then the Punjabi for a flood.

You'll want to know: ਟਪਕਣਾ.
ਟਪਕਣਾ — ṭapakṇā — "to drip; to leak".
ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਪੁਰਾਣੀ ਛੱਤ ਟਪਕਦੀ ਹੈ। mīṁh vich purāṇī chhatt ṭapakdī hai. The old roof drips in the rain.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਛੱਤ ਟਪਕੀ, oh chhatt ṭapkī.

The word, taken apart.
ਟਪਕਣਾ (ṭapakṇā) goes back to a reconstructed ṭapp, "drip, drop", an imitative root; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists Hindi ṭapaknā, "to drip" (CDIAL 5444).
