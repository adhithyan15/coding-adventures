## You'll want to know: સરોવર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C441-LAKE441-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સરોવર** — *sarovar* — "a large lake".

> આ સરોવરમાં ઘણી માછલીઓ છે.
>
> *ā sarovarmā̃ ghaṇī māchlīo che.*
>
> There are many fish in this lake.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સરોવર** (*sarovar*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sarovara*, "a lake, a large pond" (Monier-Williams); a તળાવ is usually smaller.
