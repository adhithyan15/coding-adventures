French, chapter 291: Les gendarmes.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
le gendarme — the gendarme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the offender, then the French for crime.

You'll want to know: le gendarme.
le gendarme — "the gendarme".
Les gendarmes ont arrêté la voiture sur la route. The gendarmes stopped the car on the road.
In France, les gendarmes are soldiers who do police work, mostly outside big towns; les policiers work in the cities.

The word, taken apart.
gendarme comes from gens d'armes "men of arms" (EtymDB lists the word without a parent): gens goes back to Latin gēns "a people, a clan" and armes to Latin arma "weapons" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: patrouiller.
patrouiller — "to patrol".
Des policiers patrouillent dans le parc la nuit. Police officers patrol the park at night.
Patrouiller dans un quartier — to patrol a neighbourhood.

The word, taken apart.
patrouiller comes from Old French patrouiller "to paddle about, to tramp in mud" (EtymDB), built on patte "paw", whose origin is not settled.
