## You'll want to know: hilaritās, hilaritātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C489-GAUD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**hilaritās, hilaritātis** — "cheerfulness, merriment".

> Hilaritās avī omnēs hospitēs laetōs facit.
>
> *Grandfather's cheerfulness makes all the guests happy.*

Feminine, third declension. English *hilarity* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**hilaritās** is built on *hilaris* "cheerful", an early borrowing from Greek *hilaros* "cheerful" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *hilarity* comes from it.
