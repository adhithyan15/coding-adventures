## You'll want to know: увольнять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C362-POVYSHENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**увольнять** (*uvolnyát*) — "to dismiss, to fire".

> Компания увольняет сто человек.
>
> *Kompániya uvolnyáyet sto chelovék.*
>
> The company is laying off a hundred people.

Imperfective; perfective *уволить*. *Увольняться* — to resign, to quit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**увольнять** is formed from *уволить* with the suffix *-ять* (EtymDB). *Уволить* is built on *воля* "will, freedom", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*voľa* (EtymDB): to dismiss someone was first to set them free from service.
