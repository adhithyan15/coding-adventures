## You'll want to know: ಶೀತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C82-UNWELL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶೀತ** (*śīta*) — "a cold", the kind with a runny nose. The word also means
"cold, chill".

**ನನಗೆ ಶೀತ ಆಗಿದೆ** (*nanage śīta āgide*) — "I have a cold".

## What you've built
