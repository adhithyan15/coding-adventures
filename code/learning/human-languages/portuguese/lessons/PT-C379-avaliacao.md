## You'll want to know: a avaliação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C379-CHUMBAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a avaliação** — "the assessment, the evaluation".

> A avaliação final é em junho.
>
> *The final assessment is in June.*

*Avaliação contínua* — continuous assessment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avaliação* is built on *avaliar* "to assess", on *valia* "worth", from *valer*, inherited from Latin *valēre* "to be strong, to be worth" (Lewis & Short).
