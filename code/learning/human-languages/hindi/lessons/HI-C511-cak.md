## You'll want to know: चाक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C511-KUMHAR511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चाक** — *cāk* — "a potter's wheel".

> कुम्हार चाक पर मिट्टी के दीये बनाता है।
>
> *Kumhār cāk par miṭṭī ke dīye banātā hai.*
>
> The potter makes clay lamps on his wheel.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चाक** (*cāk*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *cakra*, "a wheel" (CDIAL 4538). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "any kind of wheel"; at the potter's it is his wheel.
