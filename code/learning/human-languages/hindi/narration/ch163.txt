जीतना (jītnā) — to win.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to cry, then the Hindi for to forget.

You'll want to know: जीतना.
जीतना — jītnā — "to win".

The word, taken apart.
जीतना (jītnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jita, "won, conquered" (CDIAL 5224).

The word, taken apart.
बेचना (becnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vetyayati, "makes a price", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12100). CDIAL builds it on vetana, "wages, price", and calls other proposals less probable.
