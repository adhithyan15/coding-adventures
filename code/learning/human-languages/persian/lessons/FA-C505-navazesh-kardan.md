## You'll want to know: نوازش کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C505-SUPPORT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نوازش کردن** (*navâzesh kardan*) — "to caress, to stroke".

> او گربه را آرام نوازش کرد.
>
> *u gorbe râ ârâm navâzesh kard.*
>
> She stroked the cat gently.

*navâzesh* 'a caress', from the stem *navâz-* of *navâkhtan*, + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navâzesh* is built on *navâz-*, the present stem of the native verb *navâkhtan*, 'to caress; to play music', with the noun ending *-esh*. EtymDB has no entry for it.
