## You'll want to know: हमी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C540-VIMA540-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हमी** — *hamī* — "a guarantee".

> या पंख्यावर दोन वर्षांची हमी आहे.
>
> *yā paṅkhyāvar don varṣāñcī hamī āhe.*
>
> This fan has a two-year guarantee.

Feminine.

हमी देणे, to guarantee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हमी** (*hamī*) is the word for a guarantee or pledge; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
