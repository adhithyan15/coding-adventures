## You'll want to know: खेद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C457-KHED457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खेद** — *khed* — "regret".

> त्याला आपल्या चुकीचा खेद वाटला.
>
> *tyālā āplyā cukīcā khed vāṭlā.*
>
> He regretted his mistake.

Masculine.

Formal: खेद वाटणे, to regret, with the dative and the genitive of the thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खेद** (*khed*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kheda*, "lassitude, pain" (CDIAL 3919); in Marathi it is regret.
