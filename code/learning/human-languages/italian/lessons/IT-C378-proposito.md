## You'll want to know: il proposito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C378-SCOPO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il proposito** — "the resolution, the intention".

> Il mio proposito per l'anno nuovo è fare più sport.
>
> *My New Year's resolution is to do more sport.*

*A proposito!* — by the way! *Di proposito* — on purpose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*proposito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōpositum* "a plan, a purpose" (Lewis & Short), from *prōpōnere* "to set forth", built on *pōnere* "to put".
