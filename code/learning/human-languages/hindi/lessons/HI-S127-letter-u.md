# उ — the same vowel, standing on its own feet

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-120, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-126] -->

[PAUSE 1s] You already have the little hook that adds a *u* underneath a
consonant. Before the new one: which mark is it, and where does it sit?

## Script you'll notice: उ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-127]; assesses=[] -->

**उ** — *u*.

This is the **independent vowel**. The hook you already know rides under a
consonant and cannot stand alone. When a word **begins** with *u*, there is no
consonant to hang the hook on, so the vowel gets a full letter of its own.

What it is made of:

- a joined upper bowl and lower loop
- the top shirorekhā

## Script: two ways to write one sound
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-127, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-120] -->

| where the *u* falls | how it is written |
|---|---|
| under a consonant | the hook you already have |
| at the start of a word | **उ** |

Both say *u*. Which one a word uses is settled by **what comes before it**, and
nothing else. Every vowel in this script works this way, so learning the pair
once tells you what to expect from all of them.

## Writing: उ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-127] -->

- **1.** start at the headline junction, curve down and left around the upper bowl, then sweep back through the waist and around the lower loop without lifting
- **2.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 1.** The pen comes up once and no more.

> Verified two-stroke teaching form; everyday handwriting may divide the bowl
> and loop differently.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari उ stroke order.svg’, panels 1–2, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.
