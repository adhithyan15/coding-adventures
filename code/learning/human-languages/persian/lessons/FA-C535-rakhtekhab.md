## You'll want to know: رختخواب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C535-COSY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رختخواب** (*rakhtekhâb*) — "bedding; a bed (on the floor)".

> مادر رختخوابها را پهن کرد.
>
> *mâdar rakhtekhâbhâ râ pahn kard.*
>
> Mother laid out the bedding.

*rakht* 'clothes, things' + *khâb* 'sleep'. *rakhtekhâb pahn kardan* — to make up a bed on the floor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rakhtekhâb* is built on two native Persian words, as EtymDB records: *rakht*, 'clothes, gear', and *khâb*, 'sleep' (EtymDB).
