## You'll want to know: बेशुद्ध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C296-DHADAK296-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बेशुद्ध** — *beśuddha* — "unconscious".

> अपघातानंतर तो काही मिनिटं बेशुद्ध होता.
>
> *apghātānantar to kāhī miniṭaṁ beśuddha hotā.*
>
> After the accident he was unconscious for a few minutes.

बेशुद्ध होणे, to faint; शुद्धीवर येणे, to come round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेशुद्ध** (*beśuddha*) is built from the Persian prefix *be-*, 'without', and Sanskrit *śuddha*, 'clear, pure' (Monier-Williams), here in the sense of a clear mind.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
