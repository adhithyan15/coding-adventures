## You'll want to know: आंदोलन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C325-ANDOLAN325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आंदोलन** — *āndolan* — "a movement (of people)".

> आज़ादी का आंदोलन पूरे देश में फैल गया।
>
> *āzādī kā āndolan pūre deś meṁ phail gayā.*
>
> The freedom movement spread across the whole country.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आंदोलन** (*āndolan*) is Sanskrit *āndolana*, "a swinging, a rocking" (Monier-Williams). Modern Hindi uses it for a political movement; the dictionary has only the older sense.
