## You'll want to know: ഉപജീവനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C546-VYAVASAYAM546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉപജീവനം** — *upajīvanaṁ* — "a livelihood".

> മീൻപിടിത്തമാണ് അവരുടെ ഉപജീവനം.
>
> *mīnpiṭittamāṇŭ avaruṭe upajīvanaṁ.*
>
> Fishing is their livelihood.

ഉപജീവനമാർഗ്ഗം, a means of living.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉപജീവനം** (*upajīvanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *upajīvana*, "livelihood, subsistence" (Monier-Williams), from *upa-jīv*, "to live on".

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
