## You'll want to know: ਬਾਜ਼ੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C352-BOARD352-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਾਜ਼ੀ** — *bāzī* — "a round (of a game); a game".

> ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਪਹਿਲੀ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ ਜਿੱਤੀ।
>
> *Aman ne pahilī bāzī jittī.*
>
> Aman won the first round.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਾਜ਼ੀ** (*bāzī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *bāzī*, "play".
