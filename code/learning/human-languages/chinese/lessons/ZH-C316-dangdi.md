## You'll want to know: 当地
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C316-PLACE316-03]; assesses=[] -->

**当地** — *dāngdì* — "of that place; in the place in question".

> **我到了当地才找到房子** *wǒ dào le dāngdì cái zhǎodào fángzi* — I only found somewhere to live once I got there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**当地** (*dāngdì*) is built from 当 'at' and 地 'a place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tang* and *dijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to face' and 'to meet': the very place.
