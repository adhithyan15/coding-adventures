# অনুভূতি (ônubhūti) — a feeling; a sensation

## You'll want to know: অনুভূতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C438-MON438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুভূতি** — *ônubhūti* — "a feeling; a sensation".

> এই গানটা শুনে আমার একটা নতুন অনুভূতি হল।
>
> *ei gānṭā shune āmār ekṭā nôtun ônubhūti hôlô.*
>
> Listening to this song, I had a new feeling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুভূতি** (*ônubhūti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *anubhūti*, "perception" (Monier-Williams); in Bengali it names a feeling or a sensation.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
