## You'll want to know: клеить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C532-DIY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**клеить** (*kléit*) — "to glue; to hang (wallpaper)".

> В выходные мы клеили обои.
>
> *V vykhodnýye my kléili obói.*
>
> At the weekend we were hanging wallpaper.

Imperfective; present клею, клеишь. Perfective склеить or поклеить.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*клеить* is built on *клей* 'glue'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
