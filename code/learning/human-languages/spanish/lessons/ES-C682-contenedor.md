## You'll want to know: el contenedor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C682-CALLE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el contenedor** — "the large bin; the container".

> El vidrio va al contenedor verde.
>
> *Glass goes in the green bin.*

*El contenedor de reciclaje* — the recycling bin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contenedor* is built on *contener* "to contain", which comes from Latin *continēre* "to hold together, to contain" (EtymDB), built on *tenēre* "to hold".
