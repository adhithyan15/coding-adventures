Arabic, chapter 352: Fines and quarrels.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مخالفة (mukhālafa) — an offence; a traffic ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to forge, then the Arabic for to conceal.

You'll want to know: مخالفة.
مخالفة — mukhālafa — "an offence; a traffic ticket".
حصل أبي على مخالفة أمس. ḥaṣala abī ʿalā mukhālafa ams. My father got a traffic ticket yesterday.

The word, taken apart.
مخالفة (mukhālafa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-l-f (Lane), the root of the taught khalfa, behind. Mukhālafa is the verbal noun of Form III, "going against" a rule.

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to forge, then the Arabic for to conceal, then say mukhālafa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does مخالفة (mukhālafa) mean? ("An offence; a traffic ticket".) Say it once more.

كانت الغرامة خمسين دينارا. kānat al-gharāma khamsīn dīnāran. The fine was fifty dinars.

غرامة (gharāma) is built on the native Arabic root gh-r-m (Lane), "to pay a debt or a fine"; gharām, passion, is the same root, a love that will not let go.

Lesson 3 of 5.
شجار (shijār) — a brawl, a quarrel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an offence, then the Arabic for a fine.

You'll want to know: شجار.
شجار — shijār — "a brawl, a quarrel".
وقع شجار بين رجلين في المقهى. waqaʿa shijār bayna rajulayn fī al-maqhā. A fight broke out between two men in the café.
The taught تشاجر, to quarrel, is the same root.

The word, taken apart.
شجار (shijār) is built on the native Arabic root sh-j-r (Lane), the root of shajara, a tree, where branches tangle; tashājara is "to get tangled with one another".
