Marathi, chapter 423: Shopping and Bargains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खरेदी करणे (kharedī karṇe) — to go shopping; to purchase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for bustling, then the Marathi for bustle.

You'll want to know: खरेदी करणे.
खरेदी करणे — kharedī karṇe — "to go shopping; to purchase".
सणासाठी आम्ही खूप खरेदी केली. saṇāsāṭhī āmhī khūp kharedī kelī. We did a lot of shopping for the festival.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने खरेदी केली, tyāne kharedī kelī.
खरेदी is feminine, so the past is खरेदी केली.

The word, taken apart.
खरेदी करणे (kharedī karṇe) joins खरेदी, "buying", a loan usually traced to Persian xarīdī, to the light verb करणे, "to do".

Lesson 2 of 5.
सौदा (saudā) — a deal; a bargain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for bustle, then the Marathi for to go shopping.

You'll want to know: सौदा.
सौदा — saudā — "a deal; a bargain".
घराचा सौदा शेवटी पक्का झाला. gharācā saudā śevṭī pakkā jhālā. In the end the deal for the house was settled.
Masculine.
सौदा (saudā) पक्का होणे, a deal is closed; सौदा (saudā) करणे, to strike a deal.

The word, taken apart.
सौदा (saudā) is a loan usually traced to Persian saudā, "trade, a deal".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: किंमत.
किंमत — kimmat — "a price; a value".
या साडीची किंमत किती आहे? yā sāḍīcī kimmat kitī āhe? What is the price of this sari?
Feminine.
Also worth: त्याला किंमत (kimmat) नाही, it has no value.

The word, taken apart.
किंमत (kimmat) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic qīmat, "value, price".

Lesson 4 of 5.
भाव करणे (bhāv karṇe) — to bargain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a deal, then the Marathi for a price.

You'll want to know: भाव करणे.
भाव करणे — bhāv karṇe — "to bargain".
आजीने भाजीवाल्याशी भाव केला. ājīne bhājīvālyāśī bhāv kelā. Grandmother bargained with the vegetable seller.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भाव केला, tyāne bhāv kelā.
The seller takes -शी: दुकानदाराशी भाव करणे (bhāv karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
भाव करणे (bhāv karṇe) joins भाव, "the going rate", inherited from Sanskrit bhāva, where CDIAL lists Marathi bhāv, "market rate" (CDIAL 9475), to करणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
विकत घेणे (vikat gheṇe) — to buy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a price, then the Marathi for to bargain.

You'll want to know: विकत घेणे.
विकत घेणे — vikat gheṇe — "to buy".
मी बाजारातून नवीन छत्री विकत घेतली. mī bājārātūn navīn chatrī vikat ghetlī. I bought a new umbrella at the market.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने विकत घेतलं, tyāne vikat ghetlaṁ.
The verb agrees with what is bought: छत्री विकत घेतली, पुस्तक विकत घेतलं.

The word, taken apart.
विकत घेणे (vikat gheṇe) is built from vikat, "by purchase", from the family of Sanskrit vikraya, "sale" (CDIAL 11638), and घेणे, "to take".
