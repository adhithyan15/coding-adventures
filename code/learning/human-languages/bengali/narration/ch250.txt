Bengali, chapter 250: Crocodile, Giraffe, Zebra, Spider, Kite.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
কুমির (kumir) — a crocodile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for mint, then the Bengali for a cashew.

You'll want to know: কুমির.
কুমির — kumir — "a crocodile".
