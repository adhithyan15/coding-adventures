## You'll want to know: اٹل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C538-TAZABZUB538-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اٹل** — *aṭal* — "unshakable; immovable".

> اگر میرا ارادہ اٹل ہے تو کوئی نہیں روک سکتا۔
>
> *agar merā irādā aṭal hai to koī nahīṅ rok saktā.*
>
> If my mind is made up, nobody can stop me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اٹل** (*aṭal*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aṭala*, "not shaky, firm" (Monier-Williams).
