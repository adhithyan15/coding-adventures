## You'll want to know: কুস্তি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C522-BYAYAM522-05]; assesses=[] -->

**কুস্তি** — *kusti* — "wrestling".

> গ্রামের মেলায় কুস্তির প্রতিযোগিতা হল।
>
> *Grāmer melāy kustir prôtijogitā hôlô.*
>
> There was a wrestling competition at the village fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কুস্তি** (*kusti*) is usually traced to Persian *kushtī*, "wrestling".
