## You'll want to know: 事后
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C295-AGAIN295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**事后** — *shìhòu* — "after the event; in hindsight".

> **事后我们都笑了** *shìhòu wǒmen dōu xiào le* — Afterwards we all laughed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**事后** (*shìhòu*) is built from 事 'a matter' and 后 'after', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzriH* and *huwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'first and after'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
