Lesson 1 of 7.
なかよし (nakayoshi) — close friends; being close.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to tease, then the Japanese for to imitate.

You'll want to know: なかよし.
なかよし — nakayoshi — "close friends; being close".
いもうとと となりの こどもは なかよしです。 — imōto to tonari no kodomo wa nakayoshi desu — "My younger sister and the child next door are close friends."

The word, taken apart.
なかよし (nakayoshi) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: naka, "relationship" and yoshi, "good" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
