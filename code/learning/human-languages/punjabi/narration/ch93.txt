5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੁੜਤਾ (kuṛtā) — a kurta, a long shirt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a market, then the Punjabi for clothes.

You'll want to know: ਕੁੜਤਾ.
ਕੁੜਤਾ — kuṛtā — "a kurta, a long shirt".

The word, taken apart.
ਕੁੜਤਾ (kuṛtā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kurta, "a long shirt".

The word, taken apart.
ਮੁੰਦਰੀ (mundrī) is inherited. CDIAL lists the matching Lahnda mundrī, "ring", and Punjabi mundī, "ring", under Sanskrit mudrā, "seal, signet-ring", a word it takes, probably, from Iranian (CDIAL 10203).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਘੜੀ (ghaṛī) — a watch, a clock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a pocket, then the Punjabi for a ring.

You'll want to know: ਘੜੀ.
ਘੜੀ — ghaṛī — "a watch, a clock".

The word, taken apart.
ਘੜੀ (ghaṛī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ghaṭī, "pot, water-clock" (CDIAL 4406). The water-clock gave its name to the clock and the watch.
