## You'll want to know: खंभा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C423-GARD423-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खंभा** — *khambhā* — "a pole; a pillar".

> बिजली का खंभा सड़क पर गिर गया।
>
> *bijlī kā khambhā saṛak par gir gayā.*
>
> The electricity pole fell onto the road.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खंभा** (*khambhā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *skambha*, "a prop, a pillar" (CDIAL 13639), which lists Hindi *khambh*, "a pillar, a pole"; sk- became kh-.
