The tranche is chained, and chapters 97 and 106 each close with two reviews.
Where a word is built from pieces the reader already knows, its lesson says so:

- ಹೊಟ್ಟೆನೋವು is built like ತಲೆನೋವು.
- ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ comes from ನಿಲ್ಲು.
- ಹಬ್ಬ is the second half of ಹುಟ್ಟುಹಬ್ಬ.
- ದಯೆ is the *daya* of ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು.

No new word uses ಛ, ಔ or ಙ, which the track has not taught yet.
