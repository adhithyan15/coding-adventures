Mandarin Chinese, chapter 171: Switching Things On.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
打开 (dǎkāi) — to open; to switch on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a fork, then the Mandarin for a microwave oven.

You'll want to know: 打开.
打开 — dǎkāi — "to open; to switch on".
你去打开书房的门 nǐ qù dǎkāi shūfáng de mén — Go and open the study door.

The word, taken apart.
打开 (dǎkāi) is built from 打 'to strike' and 开 'to open', both inherited from Middle Chinese, taengX and khoj (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), though dǎ is not the sound taengX would regularly give.

Lesson 2 of 5.
kōngtiáo — air conditioner; air conditioning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a microwave oven, then the Mandarin for to switch on.

You'll want to know: kōngtiáo.
kōngtiáo — "air conditioner; air conditioning".
jīntiān tài rè le wǒmen kāi kōngtiáo — It's far too hot today: let's put the air conditioner on.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kōngtiáo is built from kōng 'empty', here the air, and tiáo 'to adjust': a short name for the machine that adjusts the air. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese khuwng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'empty'; the second is listed there as dew, glossed 'to harmonize'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
yáokòngqì — remote control.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to switch on, then the Mandarin for an air conditioner.

You'll want to know: yáokòngqì.
yáokòngqì — "remote control".
yáokòngqì zài nǎr — Where's the remote?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yáokòngqì is built from yáo 'far', kòng 'to control' and qì 'device'. The last is inherited from Middle Chinese khijH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a vessel'; the first two are listed there as yew, glossed 'far', and khuwngH, glossed 'to pull': a device that pulls from far away.
