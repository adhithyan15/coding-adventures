## You'll want to know: ਲੁਕਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C346-SORRY346-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੁਕਾਉਣਾ** — *lukāuṇā* — "to hide something; to keep something secret".

> ਮੈਂ ਕੁਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਲੁਕਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ kujh nahīṁ lukāundā hāṁ.*
>
> I am not hiding anything.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਚਾਬੀ ਲੁਕਾਈ, *maiṁ chābī lukāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੁਕਾਉਣਾ** (*lukāuṇā*) is the causative partner of *lukṇā*, "to hide oneself", which goes back to Sanskrit *lupyate*, "is lost, is removed"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *lukṇā* under it (CDIAL 11083).
