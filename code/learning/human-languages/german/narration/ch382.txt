German, chapter 382: Gegen die Regeln.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Verbot — the ban.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to keep to, then the German for obliged.

You'll want to know: das Verbot.
das Verbot — "the ban".
In vielen Parks gibt es ein Verbot für Hunde. In many parks there is a ban on dogs.
Plural: die Verbote. From verbieten — to forbid.

The word, taken apart.
Verbot is the noun of verbieten "to forbid", built with ver- on bieten "to offer, to announce"; EtymDB lists English bid as a cousin of bieten.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
zulässig — permitted, allowed.

You'll want to know: zulässig.
zulässig — "permitted, allowed".
Auf dieser Straße sind nur dreißig Kilometer pro Stunde zulässig. On this road only thirty kilometres an hour are permitted.
Formal. The opposite is unzulässig.

The word, taken apart.
zulässig is built with -ig on zulassen "to allow", from lassen "to let", Old High German lāzan (EtymDB): what may be let through.

Lesson 4 of 5.
verstoßen — to break (a rule), to violate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for permitted, then the German for punishable.

You'll want to know: verstoßen.
verstoßen — "to break (a rule), to violate".
Der Spieler hat gegen die Regeln verstoßen. The player broke the rules.
Gegen etwas verstoßen — to break a rule. Strong: er verstößt, er hat verstoßen.

The word, taken apart.
verstoßen is built with ver- on stoßen "to push, to thrust", inherited from Old High German stōzan and Proto-Germanic stautaną (EtymDB): you push against a rule.
