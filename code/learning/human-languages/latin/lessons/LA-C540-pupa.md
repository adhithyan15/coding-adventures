## You'll want to know: pūpa, pūpae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C540-PUPA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**pūpa, pūpae** — "a doll; a little girl".

> Fīliola cum pūpā suā dormit.
>
> *The little daughter sleeps with her doll.*

Feminine, first declension. Before her wedding a Roman girl gave her dolls to the household gods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pūpa** is the feminine of *pūpus* "boy, child" (Lewis & Short). English *puppet* comes from it, and *pupil* from the diminutive *pūpillus*.
