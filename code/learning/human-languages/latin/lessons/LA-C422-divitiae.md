## You'll want to know: dīvitiae, dīvitiārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C422-DIVITIAE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**dīvitiae, dīvitiārum** — "riches, wealth".

> Dīvitiae virum nōn semper fēlīcem faciunt.
>
> *Riches do not always make a man happy.*

*dīvitiae, dīvitiārum* f. pl., built on *dīves*, rich.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dīvitiae* is built on *dīves, dīvitis* "rich" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
