Telugu, chapter 418: Possible or Not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సాధ్యం (sādhyaṁ) — possible; feasible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for except for, then the Telugu for to apply.

You'll want to know: సాధ్యం.
సాధ్యం — sādhyaṁ — "possible; feasible".
ఒక్క రోజులో ఆ పని సాధ్యం కాదు. okka rōjulō ā pani sādhyaṁ kādu. That job is not possible in a single day.
సాధ్యం (sādhyaṁ) కాదు, it is not possible; సాధ్యమైనంత, as much as possible.

The word, taken apart.
సాధ్యం (sādhyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit sādhya, "to be mastered, won or managed" (Monier-Williams), from sādh, "to accomplish".

Lesson 2 of 5.
అసాధ్యం (asādhyaṁ) — impossible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to apply, then the Telugu for possible.

You'll want to know: అసాధ్యం.
అసాధ్యం — asādhyaṁ — "impossible".
కష్టపడితే ఏదీ అసాధ్యం కాదు. kaṣṭapaḍitē ēdī asādhyaṁ kādu. If you work hard, nothing is impossible.
అ-, not, + సాధ్యం (sādhyaṁ), possible.

The word, taken apart.
అసాధ్యం (asādhyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit asādhya, "not to be effected or accomplished" (Monier-Williams): a-, "not", with sādhya.

Lesson 3 of 5.
సంభావ్యత (sambhāvyata) — probability; likelihood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for possible, then the Telugu for impossible.

You'll want to know: సంభావ్యత.
సంభావ్యత — sambhāvyata — "probability; likelihood".
రేపు వర్షం పడే సంభావ్యత ఎక్కువ. rēpu varṣaṁ paḍē sambhāvyata ekkuva. The likelihood of rain tomorrow is high.
A careful, technical word; in talk, అవకాశం, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
సంభావ్యత (sambhāvyata) is built on Sanskrit sambhāvya, "to be supposed or expected; possible, probable" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త.

Lesson 4 of 5.
అనివార్యం (anivāryaṁ) — unavoidable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for impossible, then the Telugu for probability.

You'll want to know: అనివార్యం.
అనివార్యం — anivāryaṁ — "unavoidable".
ఆ మార్పు అనివార్యం. ā mārpu anivāryaṁ. That change is unavoidable.
అనివార్యంగా, unavoidably.

The word, taken apart.
అనివార్యం (anivāryaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit anivārya, "not to be warded off, unavoidable, irresistible" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
వీలైతే (vīlaitē) — if possible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for probability, then the Telugu for unavoidable.

You'll want to know: వీలైతే.
వీలైతే — vīlaitē — "if possible".
వీలైతే నేను రేపు మీ ఇంటికి వస్తాను. vīlaitē nēnu rēpu mī iṇṭiki vastānu. If possible, I will come to your house tomorrow.
వీలు, a chance, + అయితే, if it is. వీలు కాదు, it is not convenient.

The word, taken apart.
వీలైతే (vīlaitē) is built from vīlu, "convenience, a chance", whose origin is uncertain, and aitē, "if it is", a form of avu, "to be" (DEDR 333).
