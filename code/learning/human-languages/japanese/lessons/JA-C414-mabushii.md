## You'll want to know: まぶしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C414-SHITSUDO414-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まぶしい** — *mabushii* — "dazzling; too bright".

> **ゆきが ひかって、まぶしいです。** — *yuki ga hikatte, mabushii desu* — "The snow is shining and it is dazzling."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まぶしい** (*mabushii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
