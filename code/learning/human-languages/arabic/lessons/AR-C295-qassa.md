# قص (qaṣṣa) — to tell a story; to cut

## You'll want to know: قص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C295-RIWAYA295-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قص** — *qaṣṣa* — "to tell a story; to cut".

> قصت المعلمة على التلاميذ قصة قصيرة.
>
> *qaṣṣat al-muʿallima ʿalā at-talāmīdh qiṣṣa qaṣīra.*
>
> The teacher told the pupils a short story.

Present: يقص, *yaquṣṣ*.

It takes على for the listener: قص على الأطفال. With scissors it means to cut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قص** (*qaṣṣa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ṣ-ṣ* (Lane), "to follow a track step by step" and "to clip". To tell a story is to follow events in order; *qiṣṣa*, a story, is its noun.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
