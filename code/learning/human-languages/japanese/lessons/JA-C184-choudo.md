## You'll want to know: ちょうど
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C184-GOODS184-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうど** — *chōdo* — "exactly; precisely, right".

> **ちょうど せんえんです。** — *chōdo sen'en desu* — "It is exactly a thousand yen."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうど** (*chōdo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō* (Middle Chinese *teng*) and *do*, "degree" (*duH*); JMdict marks it as usually written in kana (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
