## You'll want to know: a pechincha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C453-CAIXA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a pechincha** — "the bargain, the snip".

> Este casaco foi uma pechincha.
>
> *This coat was a bargain.*

*Que pechincha!* — what a bargain!
