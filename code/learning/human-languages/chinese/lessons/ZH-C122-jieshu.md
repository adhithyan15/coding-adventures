## You'll want to know: jiéshù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C122-HOURS122-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiéshù* — "to come to an end; to finish".

> *kè jǐ diǎn jiéshù* — What time does the lesson finish?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiéshù* is built from jié 'to tie' and shù 'to bind', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ket* and *syowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
