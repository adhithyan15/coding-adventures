## You'll want to know: হাওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C393-PANGCHAR393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**হাওয়া** — *hāoyā* — "air; a breeze".

> পেট্রোল পাম্পে আমরা চাকায় হাওয়া ভরলাম।
>
> *peṭrol pāmpe āmrā chākāy hāoyā bhôrlām.*
>
> At the petrol station we put air in the tyres.

Also the breeze you feel: বাতাস is the wind; হাওয়া is the air moving, or the air in a tyre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হাওয়া** (*hāoyā*) is usually traced to Arabic *hawāʾ*, "air", which came into Bengali through Persian.
