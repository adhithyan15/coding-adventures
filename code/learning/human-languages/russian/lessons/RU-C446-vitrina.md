## You'll want to know: витрина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C446-VITRINA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**витрина** (*vitrína*) — "a shop window".

> В витрине стоят новые туфли.
>
> *V vitríne stoyát nóvyye túfli.*
>
> There are new shoes in the shop window.

Feminine. Also a glass display case in a museum.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*витрина* is usually traced to French *vitrine* 'a shop window, a display case', which EtymDB builds on *vitre* 'a pane of glass'.
