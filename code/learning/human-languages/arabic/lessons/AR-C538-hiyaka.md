## You'll want to know: حياكة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C538-IBRA538-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حياكة** — *ḥiyāka* — "knitting, weaving".

> تحب خالتي الحياكة في الشتاء.
>
> *tuḥibbu khālatī al-ḥiyāka fī ash-shitāʾ.*
>
> My aunt loves knitting in the winter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حياكة** (*ḥiyāka*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-k*, "to weave" (Lane). Lane gives ḥiyāka as one of the nouns of weaving.
