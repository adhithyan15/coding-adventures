# あし — foot or leg

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-A-01, JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01, JA-LEX-KUCHI] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Write **あ** and **し**. Retrieve **くち** — mouth.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ASHI]; assesses=[] -->

> **あし** — *ashi* — foot; leg

Context chooses the narrower English translation. The Japanese word stays the
same.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ASHI, JA-SCRIPT-A-01, JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01] -->

1. Hear *ashi*; touch a foot or leg.
2. Read **あ | し**.
3. Hide it and write both known signs.
