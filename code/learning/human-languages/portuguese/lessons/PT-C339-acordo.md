## You'll want to know: o acordo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C339-ACORDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o acordo** — "the agreement, the deal".

> Depois de duas horas, chegámos a um acordo.
>
> *After two hours, we reached an agreement.*

*Estar de acordo* — to agree; *de acordo!* — agreed, OK.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acordo* is built on the verb *acordar* in its old sense "to agree", usually traced to Vulgar Latin *accordāre*, a formation on Latin *cor, cordis* "heart" (Lewis & Short): hearts that beat together.
