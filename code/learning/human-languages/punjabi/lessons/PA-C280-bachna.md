## You'll want to know: ਬਚਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C280-CHANGE280-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਚਣਾ** — *bachṇā* — "to be left over; to remain".

> ਸੌਦੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਕੁਝ ਪੈਸੇ ਬਚਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *saude ton bāad mere kol kujh paise bachde han.*
>
> After the shopping I have some money left.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਰੋਟੀ ਬਚੀ, *oh roṭī bachī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਚਣਾ** (*bachṇā*) goes back to Sanskrit *vañcati*, "moves; escapes from"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bacṇā*, "to escape", under it, as a word taken from Hindi *bacnā* (CDIAL 11208).
