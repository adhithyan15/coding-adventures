## You'll want to know: invejar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C468-INVEJA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**invejar** — "to envy".

> Invejo a calma da minha irmã.
>
> *I envy my sister's calm.*

A little more formal than *ter inveja de*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invejar* is built on *inveja* "envy", which is inherited from Latin *invidia* (Lewis & Short).
