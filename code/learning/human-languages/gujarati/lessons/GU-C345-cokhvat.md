# ચોખવટ (cokhvaṭ) — a clear understanding (on a point)

## You'll want to know: ચોખવટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C345-KHULASO345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોખવટ** — *cokhvaṭ* — "a clear understanding (on a point)".

> પૈસાની ચોખવટ પહેલેથી કરવી જોઈએ.
>
> *paisānī cokhvaṭ pahelethī karvī joīe.*
>
> Money matters should be made clear beforehand.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોખવટ** (*cokhvaṭ*) is built on *cokh*, "clean, clear", the base of **ચોખ્ખું** (*cokhkhũ*), which goes back to Sanskrit *cokṣa*, "pure, clean" (CDIAL 4918): a matter made clean and clear.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
