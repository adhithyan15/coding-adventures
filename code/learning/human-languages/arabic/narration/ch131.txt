Arabic, chapter 131: Grass, Seed, Root, Branch, Island.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عشب (ʿushb) — grass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for soup, then the Arabic for a cake.
[pause 1 second]
Say "he loved." (Aḥabba — and ḥubb, "love," from the same root.)

You'll want to know: عشب.
عشب — ʿushb — "grass".
