## You'll want to know: венчание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C306-POMOLVKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**венчание** (*venchániye*) — "a church wedding".

> Венчание было в старой церкви.
>
> *Venchániye býlo v stároy tsérkvi.*
>
> The church wedding was in an old church.

Neuter. In the Orthodox rite the couple are crowned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*венчание* is built on *венчать* "to crown, to wed", from *венец* "a crown, a wreath" (EtymDB lists *венец* with no parent): crowns are held over the couple.
