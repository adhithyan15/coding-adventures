## You'll want to know: बडिशम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C418-JALA418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बडिशम्** — *baḍiśam* — "a fishhook".

> बालः बडिशेन मत्स्यं गृह्णाति।
>
> *bālaḥ baḍiśena matsyaṃ gṛhṇāti.*
>
> The boy catches a fish with a hook.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बडिशम्** (*baḍiśam*) is the word MW gives for a hook, a fishhook, also written *vaḍiśa*; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
