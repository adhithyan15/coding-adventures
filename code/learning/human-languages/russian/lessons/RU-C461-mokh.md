## You'll want to know: мох
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C461-POLYANA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**мох** (*mokh*) — "moss".

> Камни у ручья покрыты мхом.
>
> *Kámni u ruchyá pokrýty mkhom.*
>
> The stones by the stream are covered in moss.

Masculine; the о drops out: *мха*, *во мху*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мох* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *mŭxŭ* 'moss' (EtymDB), which EtymDB takes back to a PIE root *mews-*.
