## You'll want to know: شكك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C366-IDDIA366-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شكك** — *shakkaka* — "to cast doubt".

> شكك الصحفي في الأرقام الرسمية.
>
> *shakkaka aṣ-ṣaḥafī fī al-arqām ar-rasmiyya.*
>
> The journalist cast doubt on the official figures.

Present: يشكك, *yushakkik*.

It takes في: شكك في ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شكك** (*shakkaka*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-k-k*, "to doubt" (Lane). It is Form II of *shakka*, "to doubt": to make others doubt.
