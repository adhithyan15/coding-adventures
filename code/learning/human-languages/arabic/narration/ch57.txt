Arabic, chapter 57: Teacher, To drink, To hear, To sit, To sleep.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
معلم (muʿallim) — a teacher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a woman, then the Arabic for a guest.

You'll want to know: معلم.
معلم — muʿallim — "a teacher".

The word, taken apart.
معلم (muʿallim) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-l-m, "to know" (Lane). Muʿallim is the Form II participle, "one who makes others know", a teacher.

The word, taken apart.
شرب (shariba) is built on the native Arabic root sh-r-b, "to drink" (Lane). English syrup and sherbet both go back to words on this root, through Latin and through Turkish.

نام (nāma) is built on the native Arabic root n-w-m, "to sleep". Lane's lexicon has no article on this root. Nāma is the plain verb, "he slept".
