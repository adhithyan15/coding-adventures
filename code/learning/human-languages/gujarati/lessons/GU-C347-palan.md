## You'll want to know: પાલન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C347-KAYDO347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પાલન** — *pālan* — "observance (keeping a rule)".

> દરેકે નિયમોનું પાલન કરવું જોઈએ.
>
> *dareke niyamonũ pālan karvũ joīe.*
>
> Everyone should follow the rules.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાલન** (*pālan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pālana*, "guarding, keeping" (Monier-Williams).
