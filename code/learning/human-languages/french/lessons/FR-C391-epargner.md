## You'll want to know: épargner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C391-EPARGNE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**épargner** — "to save up (money); to spare".

> Elle épargne un peu chaque mois.
>
> *She saves a little every month.*

Also to spare someone: *épargner quelqu'un*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*épargner* is inherited from Old French *espargnier* (EtymDB), from a Frankish *sparanjan* "to spare".
