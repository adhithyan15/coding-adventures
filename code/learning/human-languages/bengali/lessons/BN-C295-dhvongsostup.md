## You'll want to know: ধ্বংসস্তূপ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C295-KHOTI295-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ধ্বংসস্তূপ** — *dhvôngsôstūp* — "rubble; a heap of ruins".

> স্বেচ্ছাসেবকরা ধ্বংসস্তূপ সরিয়ে রাস্তা খুলল।
>
> *svechchhāsebôkrā dhvôngsôstūp sôriye rāstā khullô.*
>
> The volunteers cleared the rubble and opened the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধ্বংসস্তূপ** (*dhvôngsôstūp*) joins **ধ্বংস**, ruin, to Sanskrit *stūpa*, 'a heap, a mound' (Monier-Williams), the word behind the Buddhist stupa: a heap of what was destroyed.
