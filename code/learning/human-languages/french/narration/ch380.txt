French, chapter 380: Les règles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la norme — the norm, the standard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the doctorate, then the French for the grant.

You'll want to know: la norme.
la norme — "the norm, the standard".
Ce casque est aux normes. This helmet meets the standards.
Être aux normes — to meet the standards.

The word, taken apart.
norme is a learned borrowing from Latin nōrma "a carpenter's square, a rule, a pattern" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
le règlement — the regulations, the rules; the payment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the grant, then the French for the norm.

You'll want to know: le règlement.
le règlement — "the regulations, the rules; the payment".
Le règlement de l'école interdit les téléphones en classe. The school rules forbid phones in class.
It also means payment: règlement par carte.

The word, taken apart.
règlement is built from régler "to regulate, to settle" and -ment. The verb rests on règle "rule", from Latin rēgula "a ruler, a rule", built on regere "to guide" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
obligatoire — compulsory, mandatory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the norm, then the French for the regulations.

You'll want to know: obligatoire.
obligatoire — "compulsory, mandatory".
Le port du casque est obligatoire sur le chantier. Wearing a helmet is compulsory on the building site.
One form for both genders.

The word, taken apart.
obligatoire is a learned borrowing from Late Latin obligātōrius "binding" (EtymDB), from obligāre "to bind", built on ligāre "to tie" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
facultatif — optional.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the regulations, then the French for compulsory.

You'll want to know: facultatif.
facultatif — "optional".
Le cours de musique est facultatif. The music class is optional.
Feminine facultative.

The word, taken apart.
facultatif is built on faculté "power, option", from Latin facultās "ability, power" (Lewis & Short), from facilis "easy, doable": you may do it if you choose.
