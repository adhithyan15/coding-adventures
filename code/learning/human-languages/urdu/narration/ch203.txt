Lesson 1 of 5.
ماموں (māmūṅ) — a maternal uncle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a taxi, then the Urdu for a rickshaw.

You'll want to know: ماموں.
ماموں — māmūṅ — "a maternal uncle".

The word, taken apart.
ماموں (māmūṅ) is a form of māmā, "mother's brother", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit māma, "uncle", and calls a nursery word, with similar words in Dravidian (CDIAL 10055).

خالہ — khālā — "a maternal aunt".

The word, taken apart.
خالہ (khālā) is a loan from Arabic khāla, "a mother's sister", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is kh-w-l (Lane).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a maternal uncle, then the Urdu for a maternal aunt.

You'll want to know: پھوپھی.
پھوپھی — phuphī — "a paternal aunt".

The word, taken apart.
پھوپھی (phuphī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from phupphī, "father's sister", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9089). It is one of the forms CDIAL reconstructs as phupphu-, phupphī-.
