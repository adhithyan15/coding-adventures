## You'll want to know: die Schublade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C533-GEMUETLICH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Schublade** — "drawer".

> Die Schere ist in der obersten Schublade.
>
> *The scissors are in the top drawer.*

Plural: *die Schubladen*. In Austria also *die Lade*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schublade* is built on *schieben* "to push", from Proto-Germanic *skeubaną*, and *Lade* "box, drawer", inherited from Middle High German *lade* (EtymDB).
