## You'll want to know: पुरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C324-PURA324-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरा** — *purā* — "long ago; formerly".

> पुरा अत्र एकः राजा वसति स्म।
>
> *purā atra ekaḥ rājā vasati sma.*
>
> Long ago a king lived here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरा** (*purā*) MW gives *purā* as before, formerly, of old, and compares *puras* and *pūrva*, but gives no root, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
