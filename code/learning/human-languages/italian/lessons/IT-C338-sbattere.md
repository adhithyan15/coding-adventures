## You'll want to know: sbattere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C338-FRUSTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sbattere** — "to beat (eggs); to bang, to slam".

> Sbatti le uova con lo zucchero.
>
> *Beat the eggs with the sugar.*

Also *sbattere la porta* — to slam the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sbattere* is *s-* with *battere* "to beat", from Latin *battuere* (Lewis & Short).
