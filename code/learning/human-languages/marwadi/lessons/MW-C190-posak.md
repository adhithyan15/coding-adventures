## You'll want to know: पोसाक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C190-DHOTI190-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पोसाक** — *posāk* — "an outfit; dress".

> म्हारी पोसाक नुवी है।
>
> *mhārī posāk nuvī hai.*
>
> My outfit is new.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोसाक** (*posāk*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *pošāk*, "clothing, dress".
