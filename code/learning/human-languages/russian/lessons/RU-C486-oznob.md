## You'll want to know: озноб
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C486-FEVER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**озноб** (*oznób*) — "chills, shivering".

> Вечером у меня начался озноб.
>
> *Vécherom u menyá nachalsyá oznób.*
>
> In the evening I started shivering.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*озноб* shares its root with *знобить* 'to have the shivers'; EtymDB has no entry, and the origin is not settled.
