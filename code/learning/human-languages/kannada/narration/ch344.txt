Lesson 1 of 5.
ಆಯುಧ (āyudha) — a weapon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an episode, then the Kannada for a background.

You'll want to know: ಆಯುಧ.
ಆಯುಧ — āyudha — "a weapon".
ಸೈನಿಕರು ತಮ್ಮ ಆಯುಧಗಳನ್ನು ಕೆಳಗೆ ಇಟ್ಟರು. sainikaru tamma āyudhagaḷannu keḷage iṭṭaru. The soldiers laid down their weapons.
Plural: ಆಯುಧಗಳು, āyudhagaḷu.
Also a tool for work: ರೈತನ ಆಯುಧ (āyudha).

The word, taken apart.
ಆಯುಧ (āyudha) is borrowed from Sanskrit āyudha, "a weapon" (CDIAL 1291), from the root yudh, "to fight", as in ಯುದ್ಧ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ರಣರಂಗ (raṇaraṅga) — a battlefield.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a weapon, then the Kannada for a gun.

You'll want to know: ರಣರಂಗ.
ರಣರಂಗ — raṇaraṅga — "a battlefield".
ಯುದ್ಧದ ನಂತರ ರಣರಂಗ ಮೌನವಾಗಿತ್ತು. yuddhada nantara raṇaraṅga maunavāgittu. After the war the battlefield was silent.
ರಣ, battle, and ರಂಗ, a stage: the stage of battle.

The word, taken apart.
ರಣರಂಗ (raṇaraṅga) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of raṇa, "battle, war", and raṅga, "a stage, an arena" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಾದಾಡು (kādāḍu) — to do battle; to clash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a battlefield, then the Kannada for a chariot.

You'll want to know: ಕಾದಾಡು.
ಕಾದಾಡು — kādāḍu — "to do battle; to clash".
ಎರಡು ಸೈನ್ಯಗಳು ಇಡೀ ದಿನ ಕಾದಾಡಿದವು. eraḍu sainyagaḷu iḍī dina kādāḍidavu. The two armies did battle all day.
Past (I …): ಕಾದಾಡಿದೆ, kādāḍide.
Who one fights with takes -ಜೊತೆ or -ಒಡನೆ. Animals ಕಾದಾಡು (kādāḍu) too.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾದಾಡು (kādāḍu) is a native Dravidian compound: kādu, "to war, to fight, to contend" (DEDR 1447), and āḍu, "to move, to act" (DEDR 347).
