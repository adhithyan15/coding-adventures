# fàngjià — to have time off; to go on holiday

## You'll want to know: fàngjià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C137-HOLIDAY137-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngjià* — "to have time off; to go on holiday".

> *míngtiān wǒmen fàngjià* — We're off tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngjià* is built from *fàng* 'to let go' and *jià* 'leave', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjangH* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to borrow' and 'leave from duty'.
