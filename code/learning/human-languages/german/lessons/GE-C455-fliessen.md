## You'll want to know: fließen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C455-WASSERFALL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**fließen** — "to flow".

> Der Fluss fließt langsam durch das Tal.
>
> *The river flows slowly through the valley.*

Strong; Perfekt with *sein*: *fließt*, *floss*, *ist geflossen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fließen* is inherited from Old High German *fliozan* and Proto-Germanic *fleutaną* (EtymDB). English *fleet* in its old sense 'to float' is a cousin.
