# ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ (patrikōdyama) — journalism

## You'll want to know: ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C370-PATRIKODYAMA370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ** — *patrikōdyama* — "journalism".

> ಅವಳು ಕಾಲೇಜಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ ಕಲಿತಳು.
>
> *avaḷu kālējinalli patrikōdyama kalitaḷu.*
>
> She studied journalism at college.

ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ, a newspaper, and ಉದ್ಯಮ, an enterprise: the newspaper trade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ** (*patrikōdyama*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of *patrikā*, "a leaf, a paper", from *pattra* (CDIAL 7733), and *udyama*, "undertaking, striving" (CDIAL 2048).
