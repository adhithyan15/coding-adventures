## You'll want to know: क्रूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C466-DAYA466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रूर** — *krūr* — "cruel".

> कुत्ते को मारना एक क्रूर काम है।
>
> *kutte ko mārnā ek krūr kām hai.*
>
> Beating a dog is a cruel thing to do.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रूर** (*krūr*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *krūra*, first "wounded, sore" and then "cruel, harsh" (Monier-Williams).
