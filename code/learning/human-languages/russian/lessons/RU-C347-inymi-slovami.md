## You'll want to know: иными словами
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C347-ODNAKO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**иными словами** (*inými slovámi*) — "in other words".

> Он не ответил, иными словами, он против.
>
> *On ne otvétil, inými slovámi, on prótiv.*
>
> He did not answer; in other words, he is against it.

The instrumental plural of *иной* "other" and *слово* "word". *Другими словами* means the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**иными словами** is the instrumental plural of *иной* "other", the adjective behind *иначе*, and *слово* "word", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*slovo* (EtymDB).
