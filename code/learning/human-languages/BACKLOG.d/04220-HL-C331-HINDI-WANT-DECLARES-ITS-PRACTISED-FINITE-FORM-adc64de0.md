## HL-C331 — Hindi want declares its practised finite form

After the ability repair, **चाहता** is the highest-ranked independent modern
Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 70 already contrasts
**चाहना** with **सकना**, models **चाहता** (*chāhtā*) repeatedly in complete
sentences, and asks learners to produce the form in guided practice and an
assessed activity.

The old metadata declared only infinitive **चाहना**, leaving the explicitly
taught masculine-singular form invisible to reports and downstream cards.

Declare **चाहना / चाहता** as the lesson headword, print the Devanagari
sentences in guided practice, and regenerate the derived Hindi artifacts. This
is a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new vocabulary claim.
