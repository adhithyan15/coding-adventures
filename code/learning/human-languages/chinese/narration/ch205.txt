Lesson 1 of 8.
离 (lí) — from; away from (in distance).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for video, then the Mandarin for to type on a keyboard.

You'll want to know: 离.
离 — lí — "from; away from (in distance)".
wǒ jiā lí xuéxiào hěn jìn — My home is close to the school.
The new character, in pieces: 离 (lí) — ten strokes in three pieces: the dot and the bar that cap 文; two short strokes crossing inside a box open at the top; then a frame that hooks, holding a turning stroke and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
离 (lí) is inherited from Middle Chinese lje (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says that being apart while near is lí, and being apart while far is another word; in another tone it is glossed 'to leave'. Its use for distance from a place is not given there.

Script — a cap, a box and a hooked frame.
离 (lí).
Three pieces, top to bottom.
The top, two strokes: the dot and the bar that cap 文.
The middle, four strokes. A short stroke falling to the left and a dot falling to the right, crossing like a small x. Then a box open at the top round them: a vertical down the left that turns along the foot, and a short vertical on the right.
The bottom, four strokes. A vertical down the left. A bar that turns down the right side and hooks to the left at its foot. Inside, a stroke that falls to the left and turns right, then a dot.
2 + 4 + 4 = ten.

Lesson 3 of 8.
公里 (gōnglǐ) — kilometre.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to type on a keyboard, then the Mandarin for away from.

You'll want to know: 公里.
公里 — gōnglǐ — "kilometre".
书店在东边离这儿两公里 shūdiàn zài dōngbian lí zhèr liǎng gōnglǐ — The bookshop is to the east, two kilometres from here.
The new character, in pieces: 公 (gōng) — four strokes: 八 on top, then a stroke that falls to the left and turns right, and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
公里 (gōnglǐ) is built from 公 (gōng) 'common, public' and 里 lǐ. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese kuwng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'common' and 'without bias'; the dictionary glosses 里 as a neighbourhood of twenty-five households. Its use as a measure of distance, and the pair's use for the kilometre, are not given there, so they are not settled.

Script — 八 over a turn and a dot.
公 (gōng).
Two pieces, top before bottom.
The top piece, 八, two strokes: a stroke falling to the left, then a longer one falling to the right, spread wide apart.
The bottom piece, two strokes. A stroke that falls to the left and turns to run right along the foot, then a dot at its right end. The same pair sits in the top left corner of 能.
2 + 2 = four.

Lesson 5 of 8.
zuǒyòu — about; or so (after a number).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for away from, then the Mandarin for kilometre.

You'll want to know: zuǒyòu.
zuǒyòu — "about; or so (after a number)".
zǒulù èrshí fēnzhōng zuǒyòu — It's about twenty minutes on foot.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zuǒyòu is built from zuǒ 'left' and yòu 'right', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsaX and hjuwX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a little to either side of the number.

chàbuduō — "about; almost".
cóng zhèr dào chēzhàn chàbuduō liǎng gōnglǐ — From here to the station is almost two kilometres.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
不 loses its tone in the middle of this word: chàbuduō.

The word, taken apart.
chàbuduō is built from chà 'short of', 不 'not' and duō 'much', all inherited from Middle Chinese, tsrhae, pjut and ta (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): not much short.
