## You'll want to know: فرعی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C447-NABSH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**فرعی** (*far'i*) — "side, minor (of a road); secondary".

> از خیابان اصلی وارد یک خیابان فرعی شدیم.
>
> *Az khiyâbân-e asli vâred-e yek khiyâbân-e far'i shodim.*
>
> From the main road we turned into a side street.

The opposite of *asli*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*far'i* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *farʿī* 'of a branch', from *farʿ* 'branch'. EtymDB does not list it.
