## You'll want to know: заголовок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C279-IZVESTIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**заголовок** (*zagolóvok*) — "a headline, a title".

> Заголовок в газете был очень большой.
>
> *Zagolóvok v gazéte byl óchen bolshóy.*
>
> The headline in the newspaper was very big.

Genitive *заголовка*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*заголовок* is built on *за-* and *голова* "head", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *golva* (EtymDB): what stands at the head.
