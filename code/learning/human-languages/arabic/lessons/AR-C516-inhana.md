## You'll want to know: انحنى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C516-HARAKA516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**انحنى** — *inḥanā* — "to bend down".

> انحنى الرجل وأخذ المفتاح من الأرض.
>
> *inḥanā ar-rajul wa-akhadha al-miftāḥ min al-arḍ.*
>
> The man bent down and took the key from the floor.

Present: ينحني, *yanḥanī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انحنى** (*inḥanā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-n-w*, "to bend, to curve" (Lane). Form VII inḥanā is to bend oneself.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
