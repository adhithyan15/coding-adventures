## You'll want to know: વડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C443-TREE443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વડ** — *vaḍ* — "a banyan tree".

> ગામના ચોકમાં જૂનો વડ છે.
>
> *gāmnā cokmā̃ jūno vaḍ che.*
>
> There is an old banyan in the village square.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વડ** (*vaḍ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *vaṭa*, "the banyan"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 11211).
