## You'll want to know: 警报
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C311-POLICE311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**警报** — *jǐngbào* — "alarm; a warning siren".

> **有警报大家快走** *yǒu jǐngbào, dàjiā kuài zǒu* — There's an alarm, everyone out, quick!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**警报** (*jǐngbào*) is built from 警 'to warn' and 报 'to report', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjaengX* and *pawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a warning sent out.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
