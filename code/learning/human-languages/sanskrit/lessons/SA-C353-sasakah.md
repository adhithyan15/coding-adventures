## You'll want to know: शासकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C353-SHASANA353-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शासकः** — *śāsakaḥ* — "a ruler; a governor".

> शासकः देशस्य सुखं चिन्तयेत्।
>
> *śāsakaḥ deśasya sukhaṃ cintayet.*
>
> A ruler should think of the country's well-being.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शासकः** (*śāsakaḥ*) comes from the root √śās, "to rule", with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *śāsaka* "a chastiser, a teacher, a governor, a ruler" (MW).
