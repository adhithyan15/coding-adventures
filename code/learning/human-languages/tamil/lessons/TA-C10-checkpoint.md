# Chapter 10 checkpoint — the week, three letters and a sign

sign this chapter put on the page.

## Guided Practice — the week
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-VAARA-KIZHAMAI-01, TA-PRAGMATICS-VAARA-KIZHAMAI-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the seven planet-words, Monday to Sunday, each followed by *kizhamai*]

(*Thiṅgaḷ, cevvāy, pudhaṉ, viyāzhaṉ, veḷḷi, saṉi, ñāyiṟu*.)

- What does *kizhamai* mean, and whose word is it? ("Day": Tamil's own, not the
  Sanskrit *vāram*.)
- Which four planet-words are Tamil's own, and which three came from Sanskrit?
  (Moon, Mars, Venus and Sun are native: *thiṅgaḷ, cevvāy, veḷḷi, ñāyiṟu*.
  Mercury, Jupiter and Saturn are borrowed: *pudhaṉ, viyāzhaṉ, saṉi*.)

## Script — ந, ன, ற and ◌ா
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-VOWEL-SIGNS-NANDRI-01, TA-SCRIPT-VOWEL-SIGNS-NANDRI-02, TA-SCRIPT-DRIZZLE-05, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-114] -->

- Which n is **ந**, and which is **ன**? (**ந** is the dental *n*: top bar, two
  verticals, the curl that sweeps left. **ன** is the alveolar *ṉ*: **ண** minus
  one arch.)
- Which letter hangs below the baseline, and whose consonants does it complete?
  (**ற**, the hard *ṟ*; with it you have every consonant of **நன்றி**.)
- In **நான்**, which mark turns the *a* of **ந** into *ā*, and where does it
  sit? (**◌ா**, written after the consonant.)

[YOU READ: **நான்** aloud, and point to the ◌ா in it]
