# ಇದ್ದಿದ್ದರೆ … ಹೋಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ (iddiddare … hōguttidde) — what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C317-REAL-CONDITION-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-ONGOING-PAST-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-HABITUAL-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home." (*nāḷe maḷe bandare nānu maneyallē iruttēne*.) Then "I was reading". (*ōduttidde*.)

[PAUSE 1s] The first is a real "if". Today's "if" is one that did not happen, and the second form comes back to say what would have followed.

## You'll want to know: an if that did not happen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C318-UNREAL-IF-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C02-NANNA-01, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-05, KA-LEX-C294-VASTU294-04, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C317-ARE-CONDITIONAL-01] -->

> **ನನ್ನ ಹತ್ತಿರ ದುಡ್ಡು ಇದ್ದಿದ್ದರೆ ನಾನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ.**
>
> *nanna hattira duḍḍu iddiddare nānu hōguttidde.* — "If I had had money, I would have gone."

**ನನ್ನ ಹತ್ತಿರ**, "near me", is how Kannada says what you have on you: **ನನ್ನ ಹತ್ತಿರ ದುಡ್ಡು ಇದೆ**, "I have money (on me)".

For an "if" that did not happen, Kannada puts **ಇದ್ದರೆ** after the **-ಇ** or **-ಉ** form: **ಬಂದು** + **ಇದ್ದರೆ** → **ಬಂದಿದ್ದರೆ** (*bandiddare*, "if (you) had come"); **ಮಾಡಿ** + **ಇದ್ದರೆ** → **ಮಾಡಿದ್ದರೆ** (*māḍiddare*, "if (you) had done"); **ಇದ್ದು** + **ಇದ್ದರೆ** → **ಇದ್ದಿದ್ದರೆ** (*iddiddare*, "if (there) had been").

## You'll want to know: would have
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C318-WOULD-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C315-KHUSI-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-FEELING-DATIVE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-ONGOING-PAST-01] -->

The result takes **-ಉತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ**, the form of "I was doing" and "I used to do", here "I would have done": **ಹೋಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ**, "I would have gone". For "it", **-ಉತ್ತಿತ್ತು**:

> **ನೀನು ಬಂದಿದ್ದರೆ ನನಗೆ ಖುಷಿ ಆಗುತ್ತಿತ್ತು.**
>
> *nīnu bandiddare nanage khuṣi āguttittu.* — "If you had come, I would have been glad."

The if-clause tells you which meaning you have. With **-ಇದ್ದರೆ** in front of it, **ಹೋಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ** is "would have gone", not "was going".

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C318-UNREAL-IF-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C318-WOULD-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "If (you) had come"? (**ಬಂದಿದ್ದರೆ**.) "If there had been"? (**ಇದ್ದಿದ್ದರೆ**.) "I would have gone"? (**ಹೋಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ**.) "It would have been"? (**-ಉತ್ತಿತ್ತು**: **ಆಗುತ್ತಿತ್ತು**.) What tells you it means "would"? (**The -ಇದ್ದರೆ clause in front of it.**)
