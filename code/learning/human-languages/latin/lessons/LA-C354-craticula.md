## You'll want to know: crāticula, crāticulae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C354-ASSUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**crāticula, crāticulae** — "a gridiron, a grill".

> Piscēs in crāticulā super carbōnēs coquēbantur.
>
> *The fish were cooking on a grill over the coals.*

Feminine, a small grid of iron. Martial makes a small present of one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**crāticula** is a diminutive of *crātis*, "wickerwork, a hurdle" (Lewis & Short): a little grid.
