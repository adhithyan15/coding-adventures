## You'll want to know: مکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C457-TRUST457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مکار** — *makkār* — "crafty; deceitful".

> لومڑی بڑی مکار ہوتی ہے۔
>
> *lomṛī baṛī makkār hotī hai.*
>
> The fox is a crafty animal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مکار** (*makkār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *makkār*, "full of tricks".
