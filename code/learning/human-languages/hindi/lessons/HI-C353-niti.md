## You'll want to know: नीति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C353-SHASAN353-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नीति** — *nīti* — "a policy".

> सरकार की नई नीति किसानों के लिए अच्छी है।
>
> *sarkār kī naī nīti kisānoṁ ke liye acchī hai.*
>
> The government's new policy is good for farmers.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नीति** (*nīti*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nīti*, "conduct, policy" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
