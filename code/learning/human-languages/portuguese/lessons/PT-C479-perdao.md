## You'll want to know: o perdão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C479-PERDAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o perdão** — "forgiveness, pardon".

> O rapaz pediu perdão à mãe.
>
> *The boy asked his mother's forgiveness.*

*Pedir perdão* — to ask for forgiveness. *Perdão?* — sorry, could you repeat that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perdão* comes from *perdoar* "to forgive" (EtymDB), from Late Latin *perdōnāre*, built on *dōnāre* "to give".
