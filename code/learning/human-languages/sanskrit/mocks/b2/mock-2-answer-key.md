## Writing

Task 1 must address Sumedha, acknowledge one access benefit, explain one staff
or access concern, propose a workable safeguard, and request two genuinely
different kinds of decision evidence in 100–130 independently produced
Sanskrit words. Task 2 must sustain a developed 220–280-word civic article
with a position, examples from two domains, a distinction between testimony
and verified fact, a counterargument and response, an ethical limit,
measurable criteria, and a conclusion. Apply the shared rubric's external
sandhi, word-final visarga or anusvara, continuous śirorekhā, and Devanagari
criteria. Copied prompt language is not independent production.

## Speaking

Use the complete presentation, collaborative brief, and all five defended-
discussion prompts. Enforce the 210–240-second presentation and 240–300-second
collaboration bands, with keyword notes only after ten minutes of preparation.
Score the candidate's own task development, organization, interaction and
repair, Sanskrit vocabulary and grammar, and pronunciation and fluency in the
declared documented pronunciation tradition. Record the complete paper for
independent scoring; a partner's contribution cannot compensate for the
candidate's performance.
