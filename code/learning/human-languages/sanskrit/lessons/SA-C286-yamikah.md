## You'll want to know: यामिकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C286-CAURYA286-02]; assesses=[] -->

**यामिकः** — *yāmikaḥ* — "a night watchman".

> ग्रामः रात्रौ यामिकाधीनः।
>
> *grāmaḥ rātrau yāmikādhīnaḥ.*
>
> At night the village depends on its watchman.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यामिकः** (*yāmikaḥ*) is built on *yāma*, "a watch of the night" (MW: a turn of three hours), with the ending *-ika*; MW gives *yāmika* as a night-watcher or watchman (MW).
