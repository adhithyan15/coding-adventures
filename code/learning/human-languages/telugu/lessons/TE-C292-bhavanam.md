## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**భవనం** (*bhavanaṁ*) is Sanskrit *bhavana*, "place of abode", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending *-ṁ* (CDIAL 9417).
