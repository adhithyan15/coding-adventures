## You'll want to know: تأثر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C415-INJAZ415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تأثر** — *taʾaththara* — "to be affected".

> تأثر الأطفال بالفيلم.
>
> *taʾaththara al-aṭfāl bi-l-film.*
>
> The children were moved by the film.

Present: يتأثر, *yataʾaththar*.

It takes بـ: تأثر بـ ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تأثر** (*taʾaththara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-th-r*, "a trace, a track" (Lane). It is Form V, to take a trace from a thing: to be affected, to be moved.
