Tamil, chapter 194: To pour, To chop, To peel, To deep-fry, To knead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ஊற்று (ūṟṟu) — to pour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a coach, a trainer, then the Tamil for a carpenter.

You'll want to know: ஊற்று.
ஊற்று — ūṟṟu — "to pour".

The word, taken apart.
ஊற்று (ūṟṟu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 761 lists Kannada ūṟu, "to spring, ooze". It is the causative of ūṟu, "to flow, to spring": to pour is "to make flow".

One more word for this chapter.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
