## You'll want to know: ਔਖਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C227-DIN227-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਔਖਾ** — *aukhā* — "hard to do; difficult".

> ਸਰਦੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਨਹਾਉਣਾ ਔਖਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *sardī vich savere nahāuṇā aukhā hai.*
>
> Bathing early on a winter morning is hard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਔਖਾ** (*aukhā*) is inherited: CDIAL sets it under Sanskrit *apakṣa*, "without wings, without followers", and lists Punjabi *aukhā*, "difficult, distressed" (CDIAL 414).
