## You'll want to know: گانٹھ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C499-CHARKHA499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گانٹھ** — *gāṅṭh* — "a knot".

> اس رسی میں ایک گانٹھ ہے۔
>
> *is rassī meṅ ek gāṅṭh hai.*
>
> There is a knot in this rope.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گانٹھ** (*gāṅṭh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *granthi*, "a knot" (CDIAL 4354).
