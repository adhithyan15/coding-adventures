## You'll want to know: nóngcūn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C225-FARM225-01]; assesses=[] -->

*nóngcūn* — "the countryside; a farming village".

> *wǒ lǎojiā zài nóngcūn* — My home town is out in the countryside.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nóngcūn* is built from *nóng* 'farming' and *cūn* 'village', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nowng* and *tshwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'farming the fields' and the second 'a farmstead'.
