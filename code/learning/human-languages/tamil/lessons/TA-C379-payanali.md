## You'll want to know: பயனாளி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C379-TANNARVALAR379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**பயனாளி** — *payaṉāḷi* — "a beneficiary".

> இந்தத் திட்டத்தின் பயனாளிகள் யார்?
>
> *intat tiṭṭattiṉ payaṉāḷikaḷ yār?*
>
> Who are the beneficiaries of this scheme?

Plural: பயனாளிகள், *payaṉāḷikaḷ*.

பயன், benefit + -ஆளி, the one who holds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பயனாளி** (*payaṉāḷi*) is built from *payaṉ*, "use, benefit", and native *āḷi*, "one who holds" (DEDR 399), as in முதலாளி.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
