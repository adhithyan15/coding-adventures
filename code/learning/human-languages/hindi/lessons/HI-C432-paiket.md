## You'll want to know: पैकेट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C432-MAAL432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पैकेट** — *paikeṭ* — "a packet".

> मुझे चाय का एक पैकेट दीजिए।
>
> *mujhe cāy kā ek paikeṭ dījie.*
>
> Give me a packet of tea, please.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पैकेट** (*paikeṭ*) is borrowed from English *packet*.
