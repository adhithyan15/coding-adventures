## You'll want to know: 手心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C475-BODY475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**手心** — *shǒuxīn* — "the palm of the hand".

> **我手心很热是不是体温高了** *wǒ shǒuxīn hěn rè shì bu shì tǐwēn gāo le* — My palms are hot. Is my temperature up?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**手心** (*shǒuxīn*) is built from 手 'hand' and 心 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuwX* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the heart, or middle, of the hand.
