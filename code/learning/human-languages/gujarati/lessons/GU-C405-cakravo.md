## You'll want to know: ચકરાવો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C405-FLIGHT405-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચકરાવો** — *cakrāvo* — "a circle in the air; a detour".

> વિમાને શહેર ઉપર બે ચકરાવા લીધા અને પછી નીચે ઊતર્યું.
>
> *vimāne śaher upar be cakrāvā līdhā ane pachī nīce ūtaryũ.*
>
> The plane circled twice over the city and then came down.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચકરાવો** (*cakrāvo*) seems to be built on Sanskrit *cakra*, "a wheel" (Monier-Williams): a round that a plane, a bird or a traveller makes before coming down or going on.
