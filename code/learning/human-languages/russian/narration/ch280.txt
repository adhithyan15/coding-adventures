Russian, chapter 280: Reporting and denying.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сообщать (soobshchát) — to report, to inform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an article, then the Russian for a sensation.

You'll want to know: сообщать.
сообщать (soobshchát) — "to report, to inform".
Радио сообщает о сильном ветре. Rádio soobshcháyet o sílnom vétre. The radio is reporting a strong wind.
Imperfective; perfective сообщить (soobshchít). The person is in the dative, the news in the accusative or with о: сообщить маме новость.

The word, taken apart.
сообщать (soobshchát) is built on со- and общий "common", so "to make shared"; общий is itself a Church Slavonic form (EtymDB: from Church Slavonic obĭščĭ, Proto-Slavic obĭťĭ "common"), and EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.

Lesson 2 of 5.
отрицать (otritsát) — to deny.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sensation, then the Russian for to report.

You'll want to know: отрицать.
отрицать (otritsát) — "to deny".
Он отрицает, что был там. On otritsáyet, shto byl tam. He denies that he was there.
Imperfective, with no everyday perfective. It takes the accusative: отрицать всё (otritsát vsyo) — to deny everything.

The word, taken apart.
отрицать (otritsát) is usually described as a Church Slavonic verb, traced to от- "away, against" and the root of рещи "to say", so "to speak against"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
публиковать (publikovát) — to publish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to report, then the Russian for to deny.

You'll want to know: публиковать.
публиковать (publikovát) — "to publish".
Газета публикует эти письма. Gazéta publikúyet éti písma. The newspaper publishes these letters.
Imperfective; the perfective is опубликовать (opublikovát). Present публикую, публикуешь.

The word, taken apart.
публиковать (publikovát) goes back to Latin pūblicāre "to make public", from pūblicus "of the people" (Lewis & Short), with the Russian verb ending -овать; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.

Lesson 4 of 5.
подтверждать (podtverzhdát) — to confirm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to deny, then the Russian for to publish.

You'll want to know: подтверждать.
подтверждать (podtverzhdát) — "to confirm".
Врач подтвердил, что всё в порядке. Vrach podtverdíl, shto vsyo v poryádke. The doctor confirmed that everything was all right.
Imperfective; perfective подтвердить (podtverdít), я подтвержу.

The word, taken apart.
подтверждать (podtverzhdát) is built on под- and the root of твёрдый "firm", Proto-Slavic tvĭrdŭ, in its Church Slavonic shape -твержд-: to make firm. EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
опровергать (oprovergát) — to refute, to deny officially.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to publish, then the Russian for to confirm.

You'll want to know: опровергать.
опровергать (oprovergát) — "to refute, to deny officially".
Компания опровергает эту информацию. Kompániya oprovergáyet étu informátsiyu. The company refutes this information.
Imperfective; perfective опровергнуть (oprovérgnut). Stronger than отрицать (otritsát): to show something is false.

The word, taken apart.
опровергать (oprovergát) is usually described as a Church Slavonic verb, built on о-, про- and вергнуть "to throw", so "to throw down"; EtymDB lists no parent.
