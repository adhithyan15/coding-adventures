## You'll want to know: పోలిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C389-VISLESANA389-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పోలిక** — *pōlika* — "a likeness; a resemblance; a comparison".

> అన్నకు తమ్ముడికి చాలా పోలిక ఉంది.
>
> *annaku tammuḍiki cālā pōlika undi.*
>
> The two brothers look very much alike.

Plural: పోలికలు, *pōlikalu*.

From the same root as పోల్చు, to compare, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పోలిక** (*pōlika*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4597 lists Telugu *pōlika*, "resemblance", beside *pōl(u)cu*, "to compare", and Tamil *pōl*, "to resemble".
