Persian, chapter 119: Beans, Chickpeas, Coin, Bracelet, Tie.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لوبیا (lubiyâ) — beans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a plum, then the Persian for a squash.

You'll want to know: لوبیا.
لوبیا — lubiyâ — "beans".

The word, taken apart.
lubiyâ goes back to Greek lobia, "pods, beans" (EtymDB); Arabic lūbiyā is the same word.

nokhod is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian naxōd, "chickpea" (EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
dastband is dast, "hand, wrist" (EtymDB), with band, "band, tie" (EtymDB): a band for the wrist. Both are native Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
کراوات (kerâvât) — a tie.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a coin, then the Persian for a bracelet.

You'll want to know: کراوات.
کراوات — kerâvât — "a tie".

The word, taken apart.
kerâvât is borrowed from French cravate, "cravat, tie" (EtymDB). The French named the neckcloth after the Croatian soldiers who wore it (EtymDB).
