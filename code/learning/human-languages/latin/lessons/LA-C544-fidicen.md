## You'll want to know: fidicen, fidicinis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C544-CITH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fidicen, fidicinis** — "a lyre-player".

> Fidicen ad convīvium venit.
>
> *A lyre-player comes to the dinner party.*

Masculine, third declension: *fidēs* 'lyre strings' + *canō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fidicen** is built on *fidēs* "the strings of a lyre" and *canō* "to sing, play" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
