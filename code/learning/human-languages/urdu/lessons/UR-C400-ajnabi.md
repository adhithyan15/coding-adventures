## You'll want to know: اجنبی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C400-PARDES400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اجنبی** — *ajnabī* — "a stranger".

> پردیس میں سب لوگ اجنبی تھے۔
>
> *pardes meṅ sab log ajnabī the.*
>
> Abroad, everyone was a stranger.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجنبی** (*ajnabī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ajnabī*, "foreign, a stranger".
