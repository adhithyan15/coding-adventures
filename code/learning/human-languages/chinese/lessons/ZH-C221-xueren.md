## You'll want to know: 雪人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C221-SNOW221-01]; assesses=[] -->

**雪人** — *xuěrén* — "snowman".

> **门口有一个雪人** *ménkǒu yǒu yí ge xuěrén* — There's a snowman by the door.

The new character, in pieces: 雪 — eleven strokes in two pieces: 雨 pressed flat on top, eight strokes, and under it three bars joined on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**雪人** (*xuěrén*) is built from 雪 'snow' and 人 'person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjwet* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'frozen rain'.
