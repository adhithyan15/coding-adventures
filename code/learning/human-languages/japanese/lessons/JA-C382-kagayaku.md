## You'll want to know: かがやく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C382-KAIGAN382-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かがやく** — *kagayaku* — "to sparkle; to shine brightly".

> **うみが あさひで かがやいています。** — *umi ga asahi de kagayaite imasu* — "The sea is sparkling in the morning sun."

Polite **かがやきます**, *kagayakimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **うみが かがやきました。**, *umi ga kagayakimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かがやく** (*kagayaku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
