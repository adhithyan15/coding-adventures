## You'll want to know: نسخة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C350-NASHIR350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نسخة** — *nuskha* — "a printed copy".

> اشتريت نسخة من الصحيفة.
>
> *ishtaraytu nuskha min aṣ-ṣaḥīfa.*
>
> I bought a copy of the newspaper.

Plural: نسخ, *nusakh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نسخة** (*nuskha*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-s-kh* (Lane), "to copy out a writing", the root of the taught *nasakha*. *Nuskha* is one copy.
