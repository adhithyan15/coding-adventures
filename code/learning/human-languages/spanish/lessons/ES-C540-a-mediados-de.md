## You'll want to know: a mediados de
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C540-TEMPORADA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a mediados de** — "in the middle of, around the middle of".

> La boda es a mediados de mayo.
>
> *The wedding is in the middle of May.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a mediados de* is built on *mediado*, from *mediar* 'to reach the middle', which comes from Latin *medius* 'middle' (Lewis & Short).
