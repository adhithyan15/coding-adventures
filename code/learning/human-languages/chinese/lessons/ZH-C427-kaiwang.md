## You'll want to know: 开往
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C427-COMEGO427-05]; assesses=[] -->

**开往** — *kāiwǎng* — "to be bound for a place".

> **开往机场的车在酒店门口** *kāiwǎng jīchǎng de chē zài jiǔdiàn ménkǒu* — The bus to the airport is at the hotel entrance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开往** (*kāiwǎng*) is built from 开 'to drive off' and 往 'to go towards', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *hjwangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to go'.
