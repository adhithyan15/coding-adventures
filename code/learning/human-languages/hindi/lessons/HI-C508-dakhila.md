# दाख़िला (dākhilā) — admission (to a school or course)

## You'll want to know: दाख़िला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C508-NAUKRI508-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दाख़िला** — *dākhilā* — "admission (to a school or course)".

> मेरे भाई को बड़े स्कूल में दाख़िला मिला।
>
> *Mere bhāī ko baṛe skūl meṁ dākhilā milā.*
>
> My brother got a place at the big school.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाख़िला** (*dākhilā*) is usually traced to Arabic *dākhila*, "an entry", through Persian; Hindi uses it for being let into a school or a course.
