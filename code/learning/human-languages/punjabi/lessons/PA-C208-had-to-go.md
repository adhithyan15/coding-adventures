## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-PIA-208, PA-LEX-MAINU, PA-LEX-JANA, PA-GRAM-NU-DATIVE, PA-GRAMMAR-NA-INFINITIVE] -->

> **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਜਾਣਾ ਪਿਆ** — *mainu jaana piaa* — I had to go

For this one frame, begin with familiar **ਮੈਨੂੰ**, keep familiar infinitive
**ਜਾਣਾ** in the middle, and place new whole form **ਪਿਆ** last. Nothing else in
the necessity or agreement system is added.
