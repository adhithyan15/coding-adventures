## You'll want to know: फळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C188-PHUL188-02]; assesses=[] -->

**फळ** — *phaḷ* — "a fruit".

> ये फळ मीठो है।
>
> *ye phaḷ mīṭho hai.*
>
> This fruit is sweet.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फळ** (*phaḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *phala*, "fruit"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *phaḷ* and Hindi *phal* (CDIAL 9051). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
