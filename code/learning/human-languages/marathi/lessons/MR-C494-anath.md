## You'll want to know: अनाथ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C494-NATE494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनाथ** — *anāth* — "an orphan".

> त्या संस्थेत अनाथ मुलांची काळजी घेतली जाते.
>
> *tyā saṁsthet anāth mulāñcī kāḷjī ghetlī jāte.*
>
> Orphaned children are cared for at that institution.

Masculine.

Also an adjective: अनाथ मुलगी. अनाथालय, an orphanage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनाथ** (*anāth*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *anātha*, "having no master or protector" (Monier-Williams).
