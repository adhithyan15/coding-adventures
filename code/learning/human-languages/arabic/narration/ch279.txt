Arabic, chapter 279: Rectangle, Coat hanger, Sponge, Tape, Glue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مستطيل (mustaṭīl) — a rectangle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a ladle, then the Arabic for a barrel.

You'll want to know: مستطيل.
مستطيل — mustaṭīl — "a rectangle".

The word, taken apart.
مستطيل (mustaṭīl) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-w-l, "to be long" (Lane). Mustaṭīl is the Form X participle, "lengthened".
