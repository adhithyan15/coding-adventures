## You'll want to know: le récit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C322-RECIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le récit** — "the account, the story".

> Son récit de voyage était très drôle.
>
> *His account of the trip was very funny.*

*Un récit de voyage* — a travel story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*récit* is built on *réciter* "to recite" (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *recitāre* "to read aloud".
