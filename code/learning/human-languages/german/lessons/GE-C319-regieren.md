## You'll want to know: regieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C319-KAISER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**regieren** — "to govern".

> Die Partei regierte zwölf Jahre lang.
>
> *The party governed for twelve years.*

*Die Regierung*, already known, is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regieren* comes from Middle High German *regieren* (EtymDB), a borrowing usually traced through Old French to Latin *regere* "to guide, to rule" (Lewis & Short).
