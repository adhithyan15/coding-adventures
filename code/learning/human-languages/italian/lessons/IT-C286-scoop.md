## You'll want to know: lo scoop
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C286-EDIZIONE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lo scoop** — "the scoop, the exclusive story".

> Quel giornalista ha fatto uno scoop.
>
> *That journalist got a scoop.*

Borrowed from English; plural unchanged: *gli scoop*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scoop* was borrowed from English *scoop* (EtymDB), a shovel; in the press, a story scooped up before anyone else.
