## You'll want to know: گواہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C276-CHORI276-04]; assesses=[] -->

**گواہ** — *gavāh* — "a witness".

> سب کچھ گواہ پر منحصر ہے۔
>
> *sab kuchh gavāh par munhasir hai.*
>
> Everything depends on the witness.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *gavāh*, "one who bears witness". A **گواہ** saw what happened and says so.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
