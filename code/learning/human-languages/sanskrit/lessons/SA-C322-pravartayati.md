## You'll want to know: प्रवर्तयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C322-PURVAJA322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवर्तयति** — *pravartayati* — "starts; introduces".

> राजा नवम् उत्सवं प्रवर्तयति।
>
> *rājā navam utsavaṃ pravartayati.*
>
> The king starts a new festival.

Past (he …): सः उत्सवं प्रवर्तितवान्, *saḥ utsavaṃ pravartitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवर्तयति** (*pravartayati*) is built from *pra* and the causative of the root √vṛt, "to turn, roll"; MW gives *pravartita* as set in motion, set up, established, introduced (MW).
