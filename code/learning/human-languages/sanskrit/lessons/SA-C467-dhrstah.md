# धृष्टः (dhṛṣṭaḥ) — impudent

## You'll want to know: धृष्टः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C467-SISTA467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धृष्टः** — *dhṛṣṭaḥ* — "impudent".

> धृष्टः बालः वृद्धम् उपहसति।
>
> *dhṛṣṭaḥ bālaḥ vṛddham upahasati.*
>
> The impudent boy laughs at the old man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धृष्टः** (*dhṛṣṭaḥ*) comes from the root √dhṛṣ, "to dare"; MW gives "bold, daring, confident, audacious, impudent" (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
