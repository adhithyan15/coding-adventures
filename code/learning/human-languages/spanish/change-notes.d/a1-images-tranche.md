### Spanish A1 images tranche — three exam-derived words (chapter 405)

Chapter 405 adds **la fotografía**, **la foto** and **permitir**. The final
tranche-B audit found that the two image items need all three forms: mock 1 uses
the clipped noun in an ID requirement, while mock 2 uses the full noun and the
permission verb in a public sign. Their supporting **hacer**, **la sala** and
**el pasaporte** atoms were already taught and are retrieved rather than
duplicated.

Two review lessons keep the contexts separate before joining them: **una foto y
el pasaporte** for the form, then **no está permitido hacer fotografías en la
sala** for the sign.
