## You'll want to know: línyǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C514-WEATHER514-02]; assesses=[] -->

*línyǔ* — "to get caught in the rain".

> *zuótiān línyǔ yǐhòu wǒ gǎnjué hěn lěng* — After I got caught in the rain yesterday, I felt cold.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*línyǔ* is built from *lín* 'to pour on' and 雨 'rain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lim* and *hjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as pouring water over something: to be poured on by the rain.
