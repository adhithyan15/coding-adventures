## You'll want to know: विद्वान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C364-VISHESHAGYA364-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विद्वान** — *vidvān* — "a scholar, a learned person".

> मेरे दादा इतिहास के बड़े विद्वान थे।
>
> *mere dādā itihās ke baṛe vidvān the.*
>
> My grandfather was a great scholar of history.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विद्वान** (*vidvān*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vidvas*, "knowing, wise" (Monier-Williams).
