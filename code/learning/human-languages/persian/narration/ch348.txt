Persian, chapter 348: Fairies, magic and treasure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پری (pari) — a fairy, a peri.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a dragon, then the Persian for a demon.

You'll want to know: پری.
پری (pari) — "a fairy, a peri".
در قصه یک پری زیبا به پسر کمک کرد. Dar qesse yek pari-ye zibâ be pesar komak kard. In the tale a beautiful fairy helped the boy.
Also a girl's name. Pari-ru "fairy-faced", very beautiful.

The word, taken apart.
pari is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian parīg, a female spirit that was once feared as a witch and later became the lovely fairy of poetry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سرنوشت (sarnevesht) — fate, destiny.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a witch, then the Persian for a treasure.

You'll want to know: سرنوشت.
سرنوشت (sarnevesht) — "fate, destiny".
سرنوشت (sarnevesht) آنها را دوباره به هم رساند. Sarnevesht ânhâ râ dobâre be ham resând. Fate brought them together again.
Literally "what is written on the head": sar "head" + nevesht "writing".

The word, taken apart.
sarnevesht joins sar "head" and nevesht, the past stem of neveshtan "to write"; EtymDB glosses it "destiny, fate". Both parts are native Persian.
