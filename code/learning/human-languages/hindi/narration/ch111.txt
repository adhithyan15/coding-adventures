Hindi, chapter 111: How Do You Say It?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हिंदी में (hindī meṁ) — in Hindi.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for please wait, then the Hindi for meaning.

You'll want to know: हिंदी में.
हिंदी में (hindī meṁ) — "in Hindi".
It is में, "in", the word you met on रहना, after the name of the language. अंग्रेज़ी में — "in English".

What you've built.
A way to name the language you want an answer in.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: hindī meṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hindī meṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say hindī meṁ, then aṅgrezī meṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for please wait, then the Hindi for meaning, then say hindī meṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does हिंदी में (hindī meṁ) mean? ("In Hindi".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
कैसे कहते हैं? (kaise kahte haiṁ?) — how do you say it?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for meaning, then the Hindi for in Hindi.

You'll want to know: कैसे कहते हैं?
कैसे कहते हैं? (kaise kahte haiṁ?) — "how does one say it?".
हिंदी में (hindī meṁ) 'book' कैसे कहते हैं? (kaise kahte haiṁ?) — "how do you say 'book' in Hindi?". कहना is "to say".

What you've built.
The one question that lets you learn from anyone.

[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for meaning, then the Hindi for in Hindi, then say kaise kahte haiṁ? again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for in Hindi, then the Hindi for how do you say it.

You'll want to know: दिखाइए.
दिखाइए (dikhāie) — "please show me".
It is built on देखना, "to see": to show is to make someone see.

What you've built.
A request for when words run out.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सुनाई नहीं दिया (sunāī nahīṁ diyā) — I could not hear it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for how do you say it, then the Hindi for please show me.

You'll want to know: सुनाई नहीं दिया.
सुनाई नहीं दिया (sunāī nahīṁ diyā) — "I could not hear it", literally "hearing was not given".
It is built on सुनना, "to hear". Pair it with a fix: ज़ोर से बोलिए.

What you've built.
An honest reason to ask again.

Lesson 5 of 5.
लिख दीजिए (likh dījie) — please write it down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for please show me, then the Hindi for I could not hear it.

You'll want to know: लिख दीजिए.
लिख दीजिए (likh dījie) — "please write it down (for me)".
लिखना is "to write"; दीजिए adds "for me", as in एक चाय दीजिए.

What you've built.
In Hindi, how do you say it, show me, I could not hear, write it down.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does लिख दीजिए (likh dījie) mean? ("Please write it down".) Say it once more.
