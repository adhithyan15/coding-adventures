# জমে যাওয়া (jôme jāoyā) — to freeze; to set hard

## You'll want to know: জমে যাওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C426-THANDA426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**জমে যাওয়া** — *jôme jāoyā* — "to freeze; to set hard".

> পাহাড়ে রাতে কলের জল জমে গেল।
>
> *pāhāṛe rāte kôler jôl jôme gelô.*
>
> In the hills the water in the tap froze overnight.

Past (he/she …): সেই জল জমে গেল, *sei jôl jôme gelô*.

Also of a crowd or a party: আড্ডা জমে গেল, the chat got going.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জমে যাওয়া** (*jôme jāoyā*) joins the -এ form of জমা, which CDIAL lists as Bengali *jamā*, "to collect, to coagulate, to freeze", under Sanskrit *yamyátē* (CDIAL 10428), to যাওয়া, to go: to go hard.
