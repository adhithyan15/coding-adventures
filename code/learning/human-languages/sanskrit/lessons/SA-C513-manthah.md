## You'll want to know: मन्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C513-MATHNATI513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मन्थः** — *manthaḥ* — "a churning stick".

> सा मन्थेन दधि मथ्नाति।
>
> *sā manthena dadhi mathnāti.*
>
> She churns the curd with a churning stick.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मन्थः** (*manthaḥ*) comes from the root √manth, "to stir, to churn"; MW gives *mantha* as churning and as a churning-stick (MW).
