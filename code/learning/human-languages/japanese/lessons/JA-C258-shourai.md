# しょうらい (shōrai) — the future; one's prospects

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C258-FEELING-01, JA-LEX-C141-RAINEN, JA-LEX-C137-SHINPAI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say "next year" and "worry". (*rainen*, *shinpai*.)

## You'll want to know: しょうらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C258-SHOURAI-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C141-TSUMORI, JA-LEX-C204-TRIP204-01] -->

**しょうらい** — *shōrai* — "the future", the years ahead of you: further off than **らいねん**.

> **しょうらいが しんぱいです。**
>
> *shōrai ga shinpai desu.* — "I worry about the future."

> **しょうらい りょこうに いく つもりです。**
>
> *shōrai ryokō ni iku tsumori desu.* — "In the future I plan to travel."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうらい** (*shōrai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, which KANJIDIC2 glosses "from now on" among other senses, and *rai*, "come" (Middle Chinese *loj*), the *rai* of **らいねん** (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict glosses the word "future; (future) prospects" (JMdict).
