Spanish, chapter 499: Algo de comer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el bocadillo — the sandwich (in a bread roll).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to ride, then the Spanish for to draw.

You'll want to know: el bocadillo.
el bocadillo — "the sandwich (in a bread roll)".
Un bocadillo de queso, por favor. A cheese sandwich, please.
The word used in Spain; in much of Latin America a sandwich is un sándwich.
