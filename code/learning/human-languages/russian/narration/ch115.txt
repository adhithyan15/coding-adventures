Russian, chapter 115: Thunderstorm, Ice, Hill, Rock, Wave.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
гроза (grozá) — a thunderstorm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a holiday, then the Russian for fog.

You'll want to know: гроза.
гроза (grozá) — "a thunderstorm".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: grozá]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: grozá, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say grozá]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for a holiday, then the Russian for fog, then say grozá again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does гроза (grozá) mean? ("A thunderstorm".) Say it once more.
