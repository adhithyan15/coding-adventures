Marathi, chapter 495: Nephews and Grandchildren.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पुतण्या (putaṇyā) — a brother's son.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an orphan, then the Marathi for full-blood.

You'll want to know: पुतण्या.
पुतण्या — putaṇyā — "a brother's son".
माझा पुतण्या पुण्यात शिकतो. mājhā putaṇyā puṇyāt śikto. My nephew studies in Pune.
Masculine.
A brother's son; a brother's daughter is पुतणी.

The word, taken apart.
पुतण्या (putaṇyā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi putṇyā, "brother's son", under the reconstructed pitriyajāni, "wife of one of the father's family" (CDIAL 8189).

Lesson 2 of 5.
भाचा (bhācā) — a sister's son.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for full-blood, then the Marathi for a brother's son.

You'll want to know: भाचा.
भाचा — bhācā — "a sister's son".
सुट्टीत माझा भाचा आमच्याकडे राहायला आला. suṭṭīt mājhā bhācā āmcyākaḍe rāhāylā ālā. My nephew came to stay with us in the holidays.
Masculine.
A sister's son; भाची, a sister's daughter. To the child, the mother's brother is मामा.

The word, taken apart.
भाचा (bhācā) is filed by CDIAL under bhrātrya, "pertaining to a brother", together with Prakrit bhacca, "sister's son" (CDIAL 9673); how a brother's word came to name a sister's son is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
नातू (nātū) — a grandson.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a brother's son, then the Marathi for a sister's son.

You'll want to know: नातू.
नातू — nātū — "a grandson".
आजोबा नातवाबरोबर बागेत फिरतात. ājobā nātvābarobar bāget phirtāt. Grandfather walks in the garden with his grandson.
Masculine.
Oblique नातव-: नातवाला. नात, a granddaughter.

The word, taken apart.
नातू (nātū) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit naptṛ, "grandson"; CDIAL lists Marathi nātū (CDIAL 6955b).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पणजोबा (paṇjobā) — a great-grandfather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sister's son, then the Marathi for a grandson.

You'll want to know: पणजोबा.
पणजोबा — paṇjobā — "a great-grandfather".
माझे पणजोबा शंभर वर्षं जगले. mājhe paṇjobā śambhar varṣaṁ jagle. My great-grandfather lived a hundred years.
Masculine.
Built like आजोबा; पणजी, a great-grandmother.

The word, taken apart.
पणजोबा (paṇjobā) is a Marathi kin word with the prefix पण-, "great-", the one CDIAL shows in Marathi paṇtū, "great-grandson", under praṇaptṛ (CDIAL 8523); the rest of the word is usually traced to the family of आजोबा.

Lesson 5 of 5.
आत्या (ātyā) — a father's sister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a grandson, then the Marathi for a great-grandfather.

You'll want to know: आत्या.
आत्या — ātyā — "a father's sister".
माझी आत्या गावात राहते. mājhī ātyā gāvāt rāhte. My father's sister lives in the village.
Feminine.
A father's sister; a mother's sister is मावशी.

The word, taken apart.
आत्या (ātyā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi āt, ātū and āte, "father's sister", under Sanskrit attā, "mother, elder sister" (CDIAL 222), a word usually traced to Dravidian, as CDIAL does: Tamil attai, "aunt".
