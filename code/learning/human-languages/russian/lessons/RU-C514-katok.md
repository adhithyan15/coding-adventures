## You'll want to know: каток
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C514-ICE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**каток** (*katók*) — "a skating rink".

> Зимой мы часто ходим на каток.
>
> *Zimóy my chásto khódim na katók.*
>
> In winter we often go to the skating rink.

Masculine; genitive катка.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*каток* is built on the root of *катать* 'to roll' and *кататься* 'to ride, to skate'; EtymDB has no entry.
