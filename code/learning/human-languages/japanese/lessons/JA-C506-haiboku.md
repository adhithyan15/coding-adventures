## You'll want to know: はいぼく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C506-RACE506-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はいぼく** — *haiboku* — "a defeat".

> **はいぼくは くやしいです。** — *haiboku wa kuyashii desu* — "Defeat is bitter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はいぼく** (*haiboku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hai*, "failure" (Middle Chinese *baejH*) and *boku*, "north" (*pok*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
