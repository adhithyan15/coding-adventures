## You'll want to know: la charcuterie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C444-COMMERCES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la charcuterie** — "the pork butcher, the delicatessen; cold meats".

> À la charcuterie, nous achetons du jambon et du pâté.
>
> *At the pork butcher's we buy ham and pâté.*

*De la charcuterie* also means the cold meats themselves.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*charcuterie* is built on *charcutier*, from *chair cuite* 'cooked meat'; *chair* goes back to Latin *carō* 'flesh' and *cuite* to *coquere* 'to cook' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists it without a source.
