## You'll want to know: глотать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C490-RECOVERY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**глотать** (*glotát*) — "to swallow".

> Мне больно глотать.
>
> *Mne bólno glotát.*
>
> It hurts to swallow.

Imperfective; perfective проглотить (проглочу).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *глотать*, glossed 'to swallow', without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *glъtati*.
