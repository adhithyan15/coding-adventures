## You'll want to know: انخفض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C480-NASHRA480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**انخفض** — *inkhafaḍa* — "to drop; to fall (of a level)".

> انخفضت الحرارة في الليل.
>
> *inkhafaḍat al-ḥarāra fī al-layl.*
>
> The temperature dropped at night.

Present: ينخفض, *yankhafiḍ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انخفض** (*inkhafaḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-f-ḍ*, "to be low; to lower" (Lane). It is Form VII.
