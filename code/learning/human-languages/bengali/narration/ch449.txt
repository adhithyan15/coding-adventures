Bengali, chapter 449: Affection.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
স্নেহ (sneh) — affection; tender love for someone younger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for anxious with longing, then the Bengali for to cry out in despair.

You'll want to know: স্নেহ.
স্নেহ — sneh — "affection; tender love for someone younger".
দাদু নাতিকে স্নেহ করেন। dādu nātike sneh kôren. Grandfather is very fond of his grandson.
স্নেহ (sneh) runs downward, from an elder to someone younger; what runs back up is সম্মান (taught), respect.

The word, taken apart.
স্নেহ (sneh) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sneha, which Monier-Williams gives first as "oiliness, smoothness" and then as "tenderness, fondness"; Bengali keeps only the tenderness.

Lesson 2 of 5.
মায়া (māyā) — tender attachment; fond pity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to cry out in despair, then the Bengali for affection.

You'll want to know: মায়া.
মায়া — māyā — "tender attachment; fond pity".
পুরনো বাড়িটা ছাড়তে আমার মায়া লাগল। purôno bāṛiṭā chhāṛte āmār māyā lāglô. Leaving the old house, I felt a pang of attachment.
মায়া (māyā) is the soft pull you feel toward a person, a pet or a place; the older sense, illusion, belongs to philosophy.

The word, taken apart.
মায়া (māyā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit māyā, "supernatural power, illusion" (CDIAL 10060), where CDIAL, like Monier-Williams, also records a sense of compassion, from the lexicographers; the everyday Bengali sense of tender attachment probably grew from that one.

Lesson 3 of 5.
আদর (ādôr) — affectionate care; cuddling and petting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for affection, then the Bengali for tender attachment.

You'll want to know: আদর.
আদর — ādôr — "affectionate care; cuddling and petting".
বাবা মেয়েকে আদর করলেন। bābā meyeke ādôr kôrlen. Father gave his daughter a cuddle.
আদর (ādôr) করা, to pet, to cuddle; আদরের, beloved, the pet of the family.

The word, taken apart.
আদর (ādôr) is a tatsama, Sanskrit ādara, "respect, regard, care" (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it for warm, petting affection, a sense the dictionary does not give.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ভক্তি করা (bhôkti kôrā) — to be devoted to; to revere (an elder).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for cuddling and petting, then the Bengali for romantic love.

You'll want to know: ভক্তি করা.
ভক্তি করা — bhôkti kôrā — "to be devoted to; to revere (an elder)".
আমার ভাই বাবাকে খুব ভক্তি করে। āmār bhāi bābāke khub bhôkti kôre. My brother is deeply devoted to our father.
Past (he/she …): তিনি তাঁর বাবাকে ভক্তি করতেন, tini tā̃r bābāke bhôkti kôrten.
স্নেহ (sneh) goes down, to someone younger; ভক্তি goes up, to a parent, an elder or a god.

The word, taken apart.
ভক্তি করা (bhôkti kôrā) puts ভক্তি, the tatsama Sanskrit bhakti, devotion and fond attachment (Monier-Williams), before করা, to do. ভক্ত, a devotee, is the related Sanskrit bhakta.
