## Guided Practice — from memory
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-ANCHOR-THAMAN, AR-LEX-ANCHOR-MUDARRIS, AR-LEX-ANCHOR-AHLAN, AR-CONCEPT-C02-ISM-02, AR-CONCEPT-C02-TASHARRAFNA-02, AR-CONCEPT-C02-II-MY-02, AR-CONCEPT-C02-MAA-ISMUKA-02, AR-CONCEPT-C02-ISMII-02, AR-CONCEPT-C02-ANTA-ANTI-02, AR-CONCEPT-C02-ANTA-ANTI-03, AR-CONCEPT-C02-MAA-02, AR-CONCEPT-C02-PRACTICE-02] -->

- "A price," "a teacher," "welcome"? (*Thaman*; *mudarris*; *ahlan*.)
- *Ism*'s cousin, and *tasharrafnā* literally? (Hebrew *shem*, not *name*; "we have been honoured," *sh–r–f*.)
- How are "my" and "your" built, and what does *ismī Arun* leave out? (Glued on: *-ī*; *-ka* / *-ki*. "Is": the zero copula.)
- What splits *anta* from *anti*, where French splits "you" by register? Before a noun, *mā* or *māḏā*? (Gender; *mā*.)

## Script — the bowl, the dial and the marks
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-C02-ISM-01, AR-CONCEPT-C02-II-MY-01, AR-CONCEPT-C02-MAA-01, AR-CONCEPT-C02-ANTA-ANTI-01, AR-CONCEPT-C02-TASHARRAFNA-01, AR-CONCEPT-W04-DOTS-FAMILY-01, AR-CONCEPT-W04-DOTS-FAMILY-02, AR-CONCEPT-W04-DOTS-FAMILY-03, AR-CONCEPT-W05-YA-01, AR-CONCEPT-W06-HARAKAT-01, AR-CONCEPT-W06-HARAKAT-02, AR-CONCEPT-W06-SUKUN-01, AR-CONCEPT-W06-SHADDA-02, AR-CONCEPT-W06-FATHATAN-03, AR-CONCEPT-W06-HAMZA-02, AR-LEX-ANCHOR-THAMAN, AR-LEX-ANCHOR-MUDARRIS, AR-LEX-ANCHOR-AHLAN] -->

- What separates **ب ن ت ث ي**? (The dots: one below; one, two, three above; two below.)
- Which letters build **ما**, which letter of *tasharrafnā* is new, and what builds **أنت**? (**م ا**; *fāʾ*; **أ**, hamza on an *alif* seat, then **ن** and **ت**.)
- What do **َ ِ ُ** and **ْ ّ ً** tell you, and which makes **أنتِ**? (*a*, *i*, *u*; no vowel, double, *-an*; a *kasra* under the final **ت**.)
- Which marks does *mudarris* carry, and which two does *ahlan* add? (*fatḥa*, *kasra*, *ḍamma*, *shadda*; *sukūn*, *fatḥatan*.)

[YOU READ: aloud, right to left, with meanings — **ما**, **اسمي**, **أنتَ**, **أنتِ**, **ثمن**]
