## You'll want to know: zweifeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C337-IRRTUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**zweifeln** — "to have doubts".

> Ich zweifle daran, dass der Bus pünktlich kommt.
>
> *I have doubts that the bus will come on time.*

*An etwas zweifeln* — to have doubts about something. *Ich zweifle* drops the *e* of *zweifele*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zweifeln* is inherited from Middle High German *zwīvelen* (EtymDB), the verb of *Zweifel* "doubt", which is built on the word for "two".
