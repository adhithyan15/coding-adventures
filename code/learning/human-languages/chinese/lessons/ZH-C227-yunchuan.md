## You'll want to know: yùnchuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C227-SEA227-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yùnchuán* — "to get seasick".

> *wǒ yùnchuán le hěn nánshòu* — I got seasick and felt awful.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùnchuán* is built from *yùn* 'dizzy' and *chuán* 'boat'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *zywen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first, *hjunH* there, as a halo round the sun or moon, so how it came to mean dizziness is not settled.
