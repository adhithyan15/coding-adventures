Lesson 2 of 5.
apagar — to switch off, to put out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a bill, then the Portuguese for to switch on.

You'll want to know: apagar.
apagar — "to switch off, to put out".

The word, taken apart.
apagar is probably built from a- and pagar in its old sense "to quiet, to pacify" ( from Latin pacare): to quiet a flame. That is the usual account, not a certain one.
