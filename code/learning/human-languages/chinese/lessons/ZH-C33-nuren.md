## You'll want to know: 女人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C33-FAMILY33-03]; assesses=[] -->

**女人** — *nǚrén* — "woman".

> **我家有四个女人** *wǒ jiā yǒu sì ge nǚrén* — There are four women in my family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**女人** (*nǚrén*) is built from 女 'female' and 人 'person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nrjoX* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
