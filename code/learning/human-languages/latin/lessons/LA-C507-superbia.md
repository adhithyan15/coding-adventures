## You'll want to know: superbia, superbiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C507-SUPB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**superbia, superbiae** — "pride, haughtiness".

> Superbia rēgis cīvēs vexat.
>
> *The king's arrogance troubles the citizens.*

Feminine, first declension, from *superbus*. Usually a fault in Latin, not a virtue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**superbia** is built on *superbus* "proud", which Lewis & Short and EtymDB take from *super* "above": one who holds himself above others.
