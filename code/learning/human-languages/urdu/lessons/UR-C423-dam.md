## You'll want to know: دام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C423-SAUDA423-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دام** — *dām* — "the asking price; a price".

> دکاندار نے کم دام مانگے۔
>
> *dukāndār ne kam dām māṅge.*
>
> The shopkeeper asked a low price.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دام** (*dām*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *dām*, "a copper coin, money, a price", under Sanskrit *dramma*, "a coin", which was itself taken from Greek *drachmē* (CDIAL 6622).
