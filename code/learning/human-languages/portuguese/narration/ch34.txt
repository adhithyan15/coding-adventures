Portuguese, chapter 34: Pain, Fever, Cough, Medicine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a dor — pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the throat, then the Portuguese for the stomach.

You'll want to know: a dor.
a dor — "pain". dor de cabeça: a headache.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: a dor]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does a dor mean? ("Pain".) Say it once more.
