Punjabi, chapter 100: Rainy season, Spring, Autumn, Every day, Date.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਬਰਸਾਤ (barsāt) — the rainy season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for summer, then the Punjabi for winter.

You'll want to know: ਬਰਸਾਤ.
ਬਰਸਾਤ — barsāt — "the rainy season".

The word, taken apart.
ਬਰਸਾਤ (barsāt) matches Hindi barsāt, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit varṣārātri, "the rainy season", or else as a blend with varṣartu, "rainy season" (CDIAL 11398). It gives no Punjabi form.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਬਸੰਤ (basant) — spring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for winter, then the Punjabi for the rainy season.

You'll want to know: ਬਸੰਤ.
ਬਸੰਤ — basant — "spring".

The word, taken apart.
ਬਸੰਤ (basant) is a semi-tatsama: Sanskrit vasanta, "spring season", borrowed after the old sound changes had run their course and then lightly reshaped (CDIAL 11439). Punjabi has b- for the v-; CDIAL lists no Punjabi form.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਪਤਝੜ (patjhaṛ) — autumn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for the rainy season, then the Punjabi for spring.

You'll want to know: ਪਤਝੜ.
ਪਤਝੜ — patjhaṛ — "autumn".

The word, taken apart.
ਪਤਝੜ (patjhaṛ) is built from two inherited words: pat, "leaf", as in Punjabi patt, from Sanskrit pattra (CDIAL 7733), and jhaṛ, from jhaṛṇā, "to fall off", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed jhaṭati, "falls" (CDIAL 5328). Autumn is "leaf-fall".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਤਾਰੀਖ਼ (tārīkh) — a date.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for autumn, then the Punjabi for every day.

You'll want to know: ਤਾਰੀਖ਼.
ਤਾਰੀਖ਼ — tārīkh — "a date".

The word, taken apart.
ਤਾਰੀਖ਼ (tārīkh) is a loan from Arabic tārīkh, "a dating, a date", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ʾ-r-kh (Lane).
