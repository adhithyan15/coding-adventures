## You'll want to know: आवर्तते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C522-PADANYASA522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आवर्तते** — *āvartate* — "spins round; turns back".

> नर्तकः शीघ्रम् आवर्तते।
>
> *nartakaḥ śīghram āvartate.*
>
> The dancer turns round quickly.

Past (he …): सः शीघ्रम् आवृत्तवान्, *saḥ śīghram āvṛttavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आवर्तते** (*āvartate*) is built on *ā* and the root √vṛt, "to turn", which also gives *vartate*; MW gives *āvṛt* as to turn or draw round, to turn back (MW).
