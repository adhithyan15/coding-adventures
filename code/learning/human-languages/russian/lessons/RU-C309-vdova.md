## You'll want to know: вдова
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C309-POKHORONY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**вдова** (*vdová*) — "a widow".

> Вдова жила одна в большом доме.
>
> *Vdová zhilá odná v bolshóm dóme.*
>
> The widow lived alone in a big house.

A widower is *вдовец*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вдова* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *vĭdova*, from PIE *h₁widʰéwh₂* "widow" (EtymDB); English *widow* and Latin *vidua* are cousins.
