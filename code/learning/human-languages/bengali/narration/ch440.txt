Bengali, chapter 440: Surprise and Wonder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বিস্ময় (bismôy) — wonder; amazement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to enjoy, then the Bengali for to get carried away.

You'll want to know: বিস্ময়.
বিস্ময় — bismôy — "wonder; amazement".
শিশুটি বিস্ময়ে চাঁদটা দেখছিল। shishuṭi bismôye chā̃dṭā dekhchhilô. The child was gazing at the moon in wonder.

The word, taken apart.
বিস্ময় (bismôy) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vismaya, "wonder, surprise, amazement" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: কৌতূহল.
কৌতূহল — koutūhôl — "curiosity".
নতুন বাক্সটা দেখে ছেলেটার কৌতূহল হল। nôtun bāksôṭā dekhe chheleṭār koutūhôl hôlô. Seeing the new box, the boy grew curious.
কৌতূহলী, curious.

The word, taken apart.
কৌতূহল (koutūhôl) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kautūhala, "curiosity, eager interest" (Monier-Williams); it shows two of the new vowel signs, ৌ and ূ, in one word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for wonder, then the Bengali for curiosity.

You'll want to know: মুগ্ধ.
মুগ্ধ — mugdhô — "enchanted; spellbound".
মীরার গান শুনে আমি মুগ্ধ হলাম। mīrār gān shune āmi mugdhô hôlām. Hearing Mira sing, I was spellbound.

The word, taken apart.
মুগ্ধ (mugdhô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit mugdha, "bewildered" and also "lovely, charming" (Monier-Williams); Bengali keeps the sense of being held by something beautiful.

Lesson 4 of 5.
অবাক হওয়া (ôbāk hôoyā) — to be surprised; to be amazed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for curiosity, then the Bengali for enchanted.

You'll want to know: অবাক হওয়া.
অবাক হওয়া — ôbāk hôoyā — "to be surprised; to be amazed".
বাবাকে হঠাৎ দেখে আমি অবাক হলাম। bābāke hôṭhāt dekhe āmi ôbāk hôlām. I was surprised to see my father all of a sudden.
Past (he/she …): তিনি খবরটা শুনে অবাক হলেন, tini khôbôrṭā shune ôbāk hôlen.

The word, taken apart.
অবাক হওয়া (ôbāk hôoyā) is ôbāk, "speechless, dumbfounded", which CDIAL lists for Bengali (CDIAL 882a), with hôoyā, "to be, to become" (CDIAL 9416): to be struck dumb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
চমকানো (chômkāno) — to be startled; to give a start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for enchanted, then the Bengali for to be surprised.

You'll want to know: চমকানো.
চমকানো — chômkāno — "to be startled; to give a start".
দরজায় আওয়াজ শুনে মীরা চমকে গেল। dôrjāy āoyāj shune mīrā chômke gelô. Mira gave a start at a noise at the door.
Past (he/she …): তিনি চমকালেন, tini chômkālen.
Most often in its -এ form: চমকে যাওয়া or চমকে ওঠা, to give a start.

The word, taken apart.
চমকানো (chômkāno) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as both glittering and being startled, under a reconstructed camakka, "a sudden movement" (CDIAL 4676).
