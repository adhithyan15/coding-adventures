## You'll want to know: soportar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C719-CONFIANZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**soportar** — "to bear, to put up with".

> No soporto el ruido.
>
> *I cannot stand the noise.*

*No soporto a mi vecino* — I cannot stand my neighbour. A false friend: to support is *apoyar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soportar* comes from Latin *supportāre* "to carry, to convey" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *sub* "under" and *portāre* "to carry".
