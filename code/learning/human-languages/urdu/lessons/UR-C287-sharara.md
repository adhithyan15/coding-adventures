## You'll want to know: شرارہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C287-AAG287-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شرارہ** — *sharārā* — "a spark".

> چولہے سے ایک شرارہ نکلا۔
>
> *chūlhe se ek sharārā niklā.*
>
> A spark flew out of the stove.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *sharāra*, "a spark". One **شرارہ** is enough to start a fire.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
