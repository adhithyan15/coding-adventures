# Sanskrit pre-A1 mock 1 — learner paper

Give this paper to the learner. Keep
[`mock-1-examiner-script.md`](mock-1-examiner-script.md) separate. Total
administration is 32 written minutes plus 8 speaking minutes. Pre-A1 Sanskrit
remains unjoined at word boundaries; correct external sandhi is accepted but
never required.

Choose the English meaning or situation.

dictionary, translator, or coaching is allowed.
