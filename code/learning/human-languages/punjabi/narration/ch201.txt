Lesson 2 of 5.
One school noun for a class or grade — school class or grade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the familiar first and last words of the identity frame.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਕਲਾਸ (kalaas) — kalās — school class; grade.
Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole school noun rather than inventing smaller Punjabi pieces. This lesson owns the word alone; the complete identity frame comes only after a separate retrieval.

The word, taken apart — a documented source.
Punjabi University's Punjabipedia explicitly records English Class as the source of modern written ਕਲਾਸ (kalaas). It also lists several meanings; this lesson keeps only the bounded school class-or-grade meaning. The Punjabi school noun comes from English Class. Source: Punjabi University Punjabipedia.
The Punjab School Education Board's current circular page uses the word in school scheduling prose. That current school use supports the narrow meaning here. The deeper history and other meanings stay outside this lesson. Source: Punjab School Education Board.

Lesson 4 of 5.
Put the new school noun into one identity frame — this is a school class.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਇਹ, then the new school noun, then ਹੈ.

Grammar Lens.
ਇਹ ਕਲਾਸ ਹੈ (eh kalaas hai) — eh kalās hai — this is a class.
Keep the new whole noun between familiar ਇਹ and ਹੈ. Familiar ਹੈ stays last in the already-owned identity frame. This chapter teaches only this one school label; it does not add a form rule or a wider construction.
