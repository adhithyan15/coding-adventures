## You'll want to know: पोतेरे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C524-SAPHAI524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पोतेरे** — *potere* — "a floor cloth".

> न्हाणीघराबाहेर एक जुनं पोतेरं पडलं होतं.
>
> *nhāṇīgharābāher ek junaṁ poteraṁ paḍlaṁ hotaṁ.*
>
> An old floor cloth lay outside the bathroom.

Neuter.

The dictionary writes पोतेरे; a sentence says पोतेरं, like जुनं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोतेरे** (*potere*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *potrẽ*, "a rag for smearing", under Sanskrit *pōta*, "cloth" (CDIAL 8400).
