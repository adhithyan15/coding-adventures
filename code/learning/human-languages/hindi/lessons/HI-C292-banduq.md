## You'll want to know: बंदूक़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C292-DAKAITI292-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बंदूक़** — *bandūq* — "a gun".

> एक डाकू के हाथ में बंदूक़ थी।
>
> *ek ḍākū ke hāth meṁ bandūq thī.*
>
> One of the bandits had a gun in his hand.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बंदूक़** (*bandūq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bunduq*, a hazelnut and then a small round pellet; the step from pellet to gun is said to have come through the pellet-bow. Hindi also writes it without the dot.
