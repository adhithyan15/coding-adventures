## You'll want to know: അഴിമതി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C430-AZHIMATI430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അഴിമതി** — *aḻimati* — "corruption".

> ആ ഓഫീസിൽ വലിയ അഴിമതി നടന്നു.
>
> *ā ōphīsil valiya aḻimati naṭannu.*
>
> There was large-scale corruption in that office.

Corruption is നടക്കുക, carried on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അഴിമതി** (*aḻimati*) is a Malayalam word whose origin is not settled; DEDR has no entry for it.
