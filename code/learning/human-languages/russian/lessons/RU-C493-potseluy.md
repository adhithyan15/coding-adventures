## You'll want to know: поцелуй
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C493-SWEETHEARTS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**поцелуй** (*potselúy*) — "a kiss".

> Она на всю жизнь запомнила первый поцелуй.
>
> *Oná na vsyu zhizn zapómnila pérvyy potselúy.*
>
> She remembered her first kiss for the rest of her life.

Masculine; genitive поцелуя. The verb is *целовать* — to kiss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поцелуй* is built on *целовать* 'to kiss', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic *cělovati* 'to greet; to kiss', derived from *cělъ* 'whole', as if a kiss once wished someone good health; EtymDB has no entry for *поцелуй*.
