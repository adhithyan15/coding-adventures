Russian, chapter 328: Slicing and grating.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ломтик (lómtik) — a slice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a treasure, then the Russian for a buried treasure.

You'll want to know: ломтик.
ломтик (lómtik) — "a slice".
Она положила на тарелку два ломтика сыра. Oná polozhíla na tarélku dva lómtika sýra. She put two slices of cheese on the plate.
Diminutive of ломоть "a hunk, a slice".

The word, taken apart.
ломтик (lómtik) is a diminutive of ломоть "a hunk of bread", built on the root of ломать "to break": a piece broken off. EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 2 of 5.
кусочек (kusóchek) — a little piece, a bit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a buried treasure, then the Russian for a slice.

You'll want to know: кусочек.
кусочек (kusóchek) — "a little piece, a bit".
Он съел кусочек торта. On syel kusóchek tórta. He ate a piece of cake.
Diminutive of кусок "a piece"; genitive кусочка.

The word, taken apart.
кусочек (kusóchek) is a diminutive of кусок "a piece", built on кус "a mouthful", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic kǫsŭ "piece", from kǫsati "to bite" (EtymDB), the root of кусать.

Lesson 3 of 5.
нарезать (narézat) — to slice, to cut up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a slice, then the Russian for a little piece.

You'll want to know: нарезать.
нарезать (narézat) — "to slice, to cut up".
Мама нарезала хлеб. Máma narézala khleb. Mum sliced the bread.
Perfective, stressed on -ре-: нарежу. The imperfective is нарезать (narézat) (narezát), stressed on the ending.

The word, taken apart.
нарезать (narézat) is built on на- and резать "to cut", inherited from Proto-Slavic rězati (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
натирать (natirát) — to grate; to rub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a little piece, then the Russian for to slice.

You'll want to know: натирать.
натирать (natirát) — "to grate; to rub".
Я натёр сыр для пиццы. Ya natyór syr dlya pítstsy. I grated cheese for the pizza.
Imperfective; the perfective is натереть (naterét), натру, past натёр (natyór).

The word, taken apart.
натирать (natirát) is built on на- and тереть "to rub", inherited from Proto-Slavic terti, from PIE terh₁- "to rub" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Тёрка лежит рядом с раковиной. Tyórka lezhít ryádom s rákovinoy. The grater is next to the sink.

The word, taken apart.
тёрка (tyórka) is built on тереть "to rub", inherited from Proto-Slavic terti, PIE terh₁- (EtymDB), with the ending -ка for a tool.
