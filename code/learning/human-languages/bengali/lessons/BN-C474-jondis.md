## You'll want to know: জন্ডিস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C474-HANPANI474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**জন্ডিস** — *jônḍis* — "jaundice".

> জন্ডিস হলে চোখ হলুদ হয়ে যায়।
>
> *jônḍis hôle chokh hôlud hôye jāy.*
>
> With jaundice, the eyes turn yellow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জন্ডিস** (*jônḍis*) is borrowed from English *jaundice*.
