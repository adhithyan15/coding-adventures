French, chapter 149: To discover, To seem, To serve, To suffer, To dream.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
découvrir — to discover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to fold, then the French for to own.

You'll want to know: découvrir.
découvrir — "to discover".

The word, taken apart.
découvrir from Old French descovrir, "to uncover", from des- and the verb for "to cover", from Latin cooperire. (EtymDB 2.1).
