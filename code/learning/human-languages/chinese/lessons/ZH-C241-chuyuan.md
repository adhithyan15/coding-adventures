## You'll want to know: chūyuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C241-WARD241-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chūyuàn* — "to leave hospital".

> *tā xià xīngqī kěyǐ chūyuàn* — He can leave hospital next week.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chūyuàn* is built from 出 'to go out' and *yuàn* 'a walled courtyard', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *hjwenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
