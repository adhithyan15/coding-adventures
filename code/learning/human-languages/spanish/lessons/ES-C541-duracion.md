## You'll want to know: la duración
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C541-DURAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la duración** — "the length, the duration".

> La duración del viaje es de dos horas.
>
> *The journey takes two hours.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duración* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *dūrātiō* (EtymDB), built on *dūrāre* 'to last', from *dūrus* 'hard'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
