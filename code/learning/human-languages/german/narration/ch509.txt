German, chapter 509: Pokal und Medaille.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Wettkampf — competition, contest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for opponent, then the German for tournament.

You'll want to know: der Wettkampf.
der Wettkampf — "competition, contest".
Beim Wettkampf war sie sehr nervös. She was very nervous at the competition.
Plural: die Wettkämpfe. For sport; a general contest is der Wettbewerb.

The word, taken apart.
Wettkampf is built on Wette "bet", inherited from Old High German wetti, and Kampf "fight", which came through Old High German kampf from Latin campus "field" (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: der Pokal.
der Pokal — "trophy, cup".
Der Kapitän hob den Pokal in die Luft. The captain lifted the trophy into the air.
Plural: die Pokale.

The word, taken apart.
Pokal was borrowed from Italian boccale "jug" (EtymDB), from Late Latin baucalis, from Greek: a drinking cup before it was a prize.

Plural: die Medaillen. Said roughly me-DAL-ye.

The word, taken apart.
Medaille came from French médaille, from Italian medaglia (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin metallum "metal".

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Siegerehrung — award ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

die Siegerehrung — "award ceremony".
Nach dem Rennen gab es eine Siegerehrung auf dem Marktplatz. After the race there was an award ceremony in the market square.
Plural: die Siegerehrungen. Die Ehrung — honouring.

The word, taken apart.
Siegerehrung is built on Sieger "winner", from Sieg, Proto-Germanic segaz (EtymDB), and Ehrung "honouring", from ehren "to honour".
