## You'll want to know: a inundação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C303-CHEIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a inundação** — "the flood".

> A inundação cobriu as ruas do centro.
>
> *The flood covered the streets of the centre.*

When a river overflows people also say *a cheia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inundação* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inundātiō* "a flooding" (EtymDB), built on *unda* "a wave".
