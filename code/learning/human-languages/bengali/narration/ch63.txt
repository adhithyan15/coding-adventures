Bengali, chapter 63: Knife, Bottle, Door, Book, Exercise book.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ছুরি (chhuri) — a knife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a plate, then the Bengali for a spoon.

You'll want to know: ছুরি.
ছুরি — chhuri — "a knife".

The word, taken apart.
ছুরি (chhuri) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kṣurī, "knife, dagger" (CDIAL 3727).

Lesson 4 of 5.
বই (bôi) — a book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bottle, then the Bengali for a door.

You'll want to know: বই.
বই — bôi — "a book".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists Bengali baï, "account-book, ledger", under Sanskrit vahikā, known only in rājavahikā, "king's diary" (CDIAL 11460). In Bengali the word has widened to mean any book.
