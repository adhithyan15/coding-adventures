## You'll want to know: جريح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C301-TARIQ301-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جريح** — *jarīḥ* — "a wounded person; wounded".

> نقلت سيارة الإسعاف الجريح إلى المستشفى.
>
> *naqalat sayyārat al-isʿāf al-jarīḥ ilā al-mustashfā.*
>
> The ambulance took the wounded man to hospital.

Plural: جرحى, *jarḥā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جريح** (*jarīḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-ḥ* (Lane), "to wound", the root of the taught *jurḥ*, a wound. The *faʿīl* pattern gives the one who bears it.
