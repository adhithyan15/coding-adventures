## You'll want to know: करमणूक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C528-CHHAND528-04]; assesses=[] -->

**करमणूक** — *karamṇūk* — "entertainment".

> जत्रेत मुलांच्या करमणुकीसाठी खूप खेळ होते.
>
> *jatret mulāñcyā karamṇukīsāṭhī khūp kheḷ hote.*
>
> At the fair there were lots of games to entertain the children.

Feminine.

The oblique is करमणुकी-: करमणुकीसाठी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**करमणूक** (*karamṇūk*) is built on करमणे, "to be diverted, to pass the time"; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
