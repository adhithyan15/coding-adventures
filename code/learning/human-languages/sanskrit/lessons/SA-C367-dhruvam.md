## You'll want to know: ध्रुवम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C367-SAMBHAVA367-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ध्रुवम्** — *dhruvam* — "for certain; surely".

> यदि भवान् आगच्छति, तर्हि सः ध्रुवं प्रसन्नः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhavān āgacchati, tarhi saḥ dhruvaṃ prasannaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you come, he will certainly be pleased.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ध्रुवम्** (*dhruvam*) is built on *dhruva*, "fixed, firm"; MW gives *dhruvam* "firmly, constantly, certainly, surely" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
