Italian, chapter 379: A scuola.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il liceo — the upper secondary school (academic).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the resolution, then the Italian for the goal.

You'll want to know: il liceo.
il liceo — "the upper secondary school (academic)".
Mia figlia va al liceo scientifico. My daughter goes to a science high school.
From about fourteen to nineteen. Il liceo classico teaches Latin and Greek.

The word, taken apart.
liceo is a learned borrowing from Latin lycēum, from Greek lýkeion, the name of a gymnasium with gardens where a great teacher of ancient Greece taught (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
frequentare — to attend; to go around with (people).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Italian for the goal, then the Italian for the upper secondary school.

You'll want to know: frequentare.
frequentare — "to attend; to go around with (people)".
Se vuoi passare l'esame, devi frequentare le lezioni. If you want to pass the exam, you have to attend the lessons.
Also of people: frequentare brutte compagnie — to keep bad company.

The word, taken apart.
frequentare is a learned borrowing from Latin frequentāre "to visit often, to crowd" (Lewis & Short), from frequēns "crowded, frequent".

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'assenza — the absence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the upper secondary school, then the Italian for to attend.

You'll want to know: l'assenza.
l'assenza — "the absence".
Se fai troppe assenze, non ti ammettono all'esame. If you miss too many days, they will not let you sit the exam.
Fare un'assenza — to miss a day of school.

The word, taken apart.
assenza is a learned borrowing from Latin absentia "absence" (EtymDB), from absēns "absent", built on abesse "to be away", from ab and esse "to be".

Lesson 5 of 5.
il preside — the head teacher, the principal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the absence, then the Italian for the school report.

You'll want to know: il preside.
il preside — "the head teacher, the principal".
Il preside ha chiamato i miei genitori. The head teacher called my parents.
A woman is la preside. The official title today is dirigente scolastico.

The word, taken apart.
preside is a learned borrowing from Latin praeses, praesidis "a guardian, a governor" (Lewis & Short), from praesidēre "to sit in front", built on sedēre "to sit".
