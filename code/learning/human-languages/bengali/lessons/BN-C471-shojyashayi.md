## You'll want to know: শয্যাশায়ী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C471-OSUKH471-05]; assesses=[] -->

**শয্যাশায়ী** — *shôjyāshāyī* — "bedridden; confined to bed (by illness)".

> অসুখের পরে দাদু এক মাস শয্যাশায়ী ছিলেন।
>
> *ôsukher pôre dādu ek mās shôjyāshāyī chhilen.*
>
> After his illness my grandfather was confined to bed for a month.

Another sign of being unwell: মাথা ঘোরা, to feel dizzy (the head goes round): আমার মাথা ঘুরছে.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শয্যাশায়ী** (*shôjyāshāyī*) puts Sanskrit *śayyā*, "a bed", with *śāyin*, "lying, resting" (Monier-Williams): lying in bed, and so laid up by illness.
