**pünktlich** — "punctual".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pünktlich** is the word for "point", from Latin *pūnctum*, "a prick, a point" (EtymDB 2.1), + *-lich*: on the dot.
