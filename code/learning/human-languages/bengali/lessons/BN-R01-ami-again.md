# আমি, and the sentence that can drop it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C03-AMI-01] -->

[PAUSE 5s] Say *I*.

## Guided Practice — the distant band
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C03-AMI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMARNAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-ALAAP-01] -->

- [YOU SAY: *I* — *āmi*]
- [YOU SAY: "my name is —"]
- [YOU SAY: "pleased to meet you" — *ālāp kore bhālo lāglo*]
- [YOU SAY: "I am well" **without** the pronoun (*bhālo āchhi*)]

That last one is the test. The pronoun is carried by the **ি** on the end of the
verb, so a speaker who drops **আমি** loses nothing at all — and a learner who
cannot drop it is still translating rather than speaking.

Say the polite formula once more, whole, at speed. It is the longest thing this
book asks anyone to say from memory.

## Writing — from sound
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-AMI-READ-01] -->

Cover every model. Write *I* from the sound alone.

- [YOU WRITE: আমি]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C03-AMI-01, BN-SCRIPT-AMI-READ-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which piece of the verb carries "I"? (**The ি ending**.) So is
**আমি** required? (**No** — it is emphasis.)
