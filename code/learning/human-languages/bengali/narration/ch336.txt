Bengali, chapter 336: What Matters Most.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গুরুত্ব (gurutvô) — importance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an example, then the Bengali for a topic of talk.

You'll want to know: গুরুত্ব.
গুরুত্ব — gurutvô — "importance".
এই সিদ্ধান্তের গুরুত্ব অনেক। ei siddhānter gurutvô ônek. This decision matters a great deal.

The word, taken apart.
গুরুত্ব (gurutvô) is Sanskrit gurutva, weight (Monier-Williams), from guru 'heavy'. Bengali uses the weight for importance, as English speaks of a weighty matter.

Lesson 3 of 5.
মূল (mūl) — main; chief; the root (of something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for importance, then the Bengali for special.

You'll want to know: মূল.
মূল — mūl — "main; chief; the root (of something)".
তোমার বক্তব্যের মূল কথাটা কী? tomār bôktôbyer mūl kôthāṭā kī? What is the main point of what you are saying?

The word, taken apart.
মূল (mūl) is Sanskrit mūla, a root, and so the basis or origin of a thing (Monier-Williams). Before a noun it means the main one, the one the rest grows from: মূল কথা, the main point.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ব্যাখ্যা করা (bækhyā kôrā) — to interpret; to explain in detail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for special, then the Bengali for main.

You'll want to know: ব্যাখ্যা করা.
ব্যাখ্যা করা — bækhyā kôrā — "to interpret; to explain in detail".
তুমি এই কবিতাটা কীভাবে ব্যাখ্যা করবে? tumi ei kôbitāṭā kībhābe bækhyā kôrbe? How will you interpret this poem?
Past (he/she …): তিনি আইনটা ব্যাখ্যা করলেন, tini āinṭā bækhyā kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
ব্যাখ্যা করা (bækhyā kôrā) is Sanskrit vyākhyā, explanation or commentary (Monier-Williams), with করা. It goes further than বোঝানো: it lays out what a text or a law means.
