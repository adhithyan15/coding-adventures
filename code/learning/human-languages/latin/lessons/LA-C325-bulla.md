## You'll want to know: bulla, bullae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C325-BULLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**bulla, bullae** — "an amulet (worn by a child); a bubble".

> Puer bullam auream in collō gerēbat.
>
> *The boy wore a golden amulet round his neck.*

Feminine. First a bubble, then the round locket a freeborn boy wore until he came of age. English *bullet* and the papal *bull* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**bulla** first meant "a bubble", anything swelling into a round shape (Lewis & Short); where the word comes from is not settled. English *bull*, a papal letter with a round seal, comes from it, and *bullet* through French.
