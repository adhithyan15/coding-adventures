# পূর্ব (pūrbô) — east

## You'll want to know: পূর্ব
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C401-DIK401-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পূর্ব** — *pūrbô* — "east".

> ভোরে পূর্ব আকাশ লাল হল।
>
> *bhore pūrbô ākāsh lāl hôlô.*
>
> At dawn the eastern sky turned red.

Also former, earlier, in careful speech: পূর্বে, before.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পূর্ব** (*pūrbô*) is Sanskrit *pūrva*, "being before, in front", and so "eastern" and "earlier" (Monier-Williams): the east was the side you faced.
