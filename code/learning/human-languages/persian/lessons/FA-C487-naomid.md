## You'll want to know: ناامید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C487-HOPE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ناامید** (*nâomid*) — "disappointed; hopeless".

> او از نتیجه امتحان ناامید شد.
>
> *u az natije-ye emtehân nâomid shod.*
>
> He was disappointed with the exam result.

*nâ-* 'not' + *omid* 'hope'. The noun is *nâomidi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nâomid* is built on native Persian pieces: the prefix *nâ-*, 'not', and *omid*, 'hope', which is usually traced to Middle Persian *ummēd*.
