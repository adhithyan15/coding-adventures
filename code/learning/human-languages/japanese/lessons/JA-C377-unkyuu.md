## You'll want to know: うんきゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C377-CHIEN377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うんきゅう** — *unkyū* — "a suspension of service".

> **ゆきで しんかんせんが うんきゅうに なりました。** — *yuki de shinkansen ga unkyū ni narimashita* — "Because of the snow, the bullet train stopped running."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うんきゅう** (*unkyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *un*, "transport" (Middle Chinese *hjunH*) and *kyū*, "rest" (*xjuw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
