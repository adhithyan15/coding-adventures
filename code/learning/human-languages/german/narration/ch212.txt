Lesson 1 of 5.
bewölkt — cloudy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for excellent, then the German for working.

You'll want to know: bewölkt.
bewölkt — "cloudy".

The word, taken apart.
bewölkt is be- + Wolke, "cloud", as a participle: covered with cloud. Wolke from Proto-Germanic wulkaną. (EtymDB 2.1).
