## You'll want to know: 高中
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C248-TERM248-04]; assesses=[] -->

**高中** — *gāozhōng* — "senior high school".

> **我们学校有高中** *wǒmen xuéxiào yǒu gāozhōng* — Our school has a senior high section.

The new character, in pieces: 高 — ten strokes: a dot and a long bar, then 口; under them a frame open at the foot, with a small 口 inside it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**高中** (*gāozhōng*) is built from 高 'high' and 中 'middle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaw* and *trjuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'up' and 'lofty': the higher middle school, a short form of a longer name.
