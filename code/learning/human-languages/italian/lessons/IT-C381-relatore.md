## You'll want to know: il relatore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C381-DOCENTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il relatore** — "the thesis supervisor; the speaker (at a conference)".

> Il mio relatore legge la tesi pagina per pagina.
>
> *My supervisor reads the thesis page by page.*

At a conference, *il relatore* gives a talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*relatore* is a learned borrowing from Latin *relātor* "one who reports, a narrator" (Lewis & Short), from *relātus*, the past participle of *referre* "to bring back, to report".
