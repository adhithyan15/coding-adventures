Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 268: Telling and Explaining.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कैवणो (kaivṇo) — to say.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for waiting, then the Marwadi for to hold on.

You'll want to know: कैवणो.
कैवणो — kaivṇo — "to say".
वो साची बात कैवै है। vo sāchī bāt kaivai hai. He tells the truth.
Past (I …): म्हैं एक सबद कोनी कैयो, mhaĩ ek sabad konī kaiyo.

The word, taken apart.
कैवणो (kaivṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit kathayati, "converses with, describes"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari forms kahaï and kai under it (CDIAL 2703).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सुणावणो (suṇāvṇo) — to recite; to tell (a story).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to hold on, then the Marwadi for to say.

You'll want to know: सुणावणो.
सुणावणो — suṇāvṇo — "to recite; to tell (a story)".
दादी गीत सुणावै है। dādī gīt suṇāvai hai. Grandmother recites a song.
Past (I …): म्हैं गीत सुणायो, mhaĩ gīt suṇāyo.

The word, taken apart.
सुणावणो (suṇāvṇo) is built on सुणणो, to hear, as its causative: "to make someone hear". CDIAL lists Marwari suṇṇo, "hears", under Sanskrit śṛṇoti (CDIAL 12598).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सलाह (salāh) — counsel; advice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to say, then the Marwadi for to recite.

You'll want to know: सलाह.
सलाह — salāh — "counsel; advice".
दादी चोखी सलाह देवै है। dādī chokhī salāh devai hai. Grandmother gives good advice.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सलाह (salāh) is usually traced, through Persian salāh, to Arabic ṣalāḥ, "counsel". It is feminine: चोखी सलाह, good advice.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मतलब (matlab) — a meaning; what someone means.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to recite, then the Marwadi for counsel.

You'll want to know: मतलब.
मतलब — matlab — "a meaning; what someone means".
थारो मतलब कांई है? thāro matlab kāĩ hai? What do you mean?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मतलब (matlab) is usually traced, through Persian matlab, to Arabic maṭlab, "something sought, an aim"; the sense "what someone means" is the one the word has in everyday use. It is masculine: थारो मतलब, what you mean.
