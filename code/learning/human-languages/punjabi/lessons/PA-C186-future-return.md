# Start with two verbs you know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HONA, PA-LEX-AUNA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the Punjabi infinitive for “to be,” then the infinitive for “to
come.”

## Grammar Lens: one future contrast at a time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HONA, PA-LEX-AUNA] -->

Keep the two familiar meanings steady. The next steps will make a masculine
future form and then a feminine future form from the first verb. Only after
that contrast is secure will the same two endings move to the second verb.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HONA, PA-LEX-AUNA] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *honaa*; YOU SAY: **ਹੋਣਾ**]
- [YOU HEAR: *aaunaa*; YOU SAY: **ਆਉਣਾ**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HONA, PA-LEX-AUNA] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say “to be; to come” once more in Punjabi.

This voice-first review introduces no new written target.
