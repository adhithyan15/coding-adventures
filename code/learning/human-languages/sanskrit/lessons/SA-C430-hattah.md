# हट्टः (haṭṭaḥ) — a bazaar; a market fair

## You'll want to know: हट्टः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C430-HATTA430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हट्टः** — *haṭṭaḥ* — "a bazaar; a market fair".

> सोमवासरे ग्रामे हट्टः भवति।
>
> *somavāsare grāme haṭṭaḥ bhavati.*
>
> On Monday there is a bazaar in the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हट्टः** (*haṭṭaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a market, a fair; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it lives on as Hindi *hāṭ*, "a market" (MW; CDIAL 13944).
