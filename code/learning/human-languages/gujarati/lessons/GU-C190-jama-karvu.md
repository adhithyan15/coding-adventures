# જમા કરવું (jamā karvũ) — to deposit

## You'll want to know: જમા કરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C190-BANK190-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જમા કરવું** — *jamā karvũ* — "to deposit".

> **હું બેંકમાં પૈસા જમા કરું છું.** — *hũ bẽkmā̃ paisā jamā karũ chũ* — "I deposit money in the bank."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જમા કરવું** (*jamā karvũ*) is built from *jamā*, "deposited, credited", a loan from Arabic *jamʿ*, "gathering, sum", that reached Gujarati through Persian, and the light verb *karvũ*, "to do". Its Arabic root is *j-m-ʿ*, "to gather" (Lane).
