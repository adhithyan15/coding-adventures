## You'll want to know: protestar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C364-PROTESTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**protestar** — "to protest".

> Os moradores protestaram contra o barulho.
>
> *The residents protested against the noise.*

*Protestar contra* — to protest against.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protestar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōtestārī* "to declare in public, to bear witness" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *prō* "in public" and *testārī* "to bear witness", on *testis* "witness".
