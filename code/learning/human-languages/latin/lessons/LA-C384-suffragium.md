## You'll want to know: suffrāgium, suffrāgiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C384-SUFFRAGIUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**suffrāgium, suffrāgiī** — "a vote; the right to vote".

> Cīvēs suffrāgia ferunt.
>
> *The citizens cast their votes.*

*suffrāgium, suffrāgiī* n. *Suffrāgium ferre*, to vote. English *suffrage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *suffrāgium* is not settled: Lewis & Short link it, with a "perhaps", to *suffrāgō* "the hock of a leg", and EtymDB has no parent for it. English *suffrage* comes from it.
