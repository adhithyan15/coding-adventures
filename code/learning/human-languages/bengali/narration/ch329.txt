Lesson 1 of 7.
আরম্ভ (ārômbhô) — a start; a commencement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a summary, then the Bengali for to compose.

You'll want to know: আরম্ভ.
আরম্ভ — ārômbhô — "a start; a commencement".
উপন্যাসটা একটা ঝড়ের রাতে আরম্ভ হয়। upônyāsṭā ekṭā jhôṛer rāte ārômbhô hôy. The novel begins on a stormy night.

The word, taken apart.
আরম্ভ (ārômbhô) is Sanskrit ārambha, 'undertaking, beginning' (Monier-Williams). It is more formal than শুরু.

চিরকাল — chirôkāl — "forever; for all time".
এই গল্পটা আমি চিরকাল মনে রাখব। ei gôlpoṭā āmi chirôkāl mône rākhbô. I will remember this story forever.

চিরকাল (chirôkāl) joins Sanskrit cira, 'long, lasting a long time' (Monier-Williams), to kāla, time: for a long, lasting time.

Lesson 4 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a close, then the Bengali for forever.

You'll want to know: অবিস্মরণীয়.
অবিস্মরণীয় — ôbismôrônīyô — "unforgettable".
সেই রাতের গল্পগুলো ছিল অবিস্মরণীয়। sei rāter gôlpogulo chhilô ôbismôrônīyô. The stories of that night were unforgettable.

The word, taken apart.
অবিস্মরণীয় (ôbismôrônīyô) puts Sanskrit a-, 'not', before vismaraṇīya, 'to be forgotten' (Monier-Williams): not to be forgotten.

You'll want to know: উৎসর্গ করা.
উৎসর্গ করা — utsôrgô kôrā — "to dedicate".
লেখক বইটা তাঁর দাদুকে উৎসর্গ করলেন। lekhôk bôiṭā tā̃r dāduke utsôrgô kôrlen. The writer dedicated the book to his grandfather.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বইটা বাবাকে উৎসর্গ করলেন, tini bôiṭā bābāke utsôrgô kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
উৎসর্গ করা (utsôrgô kôrā) puts Sanskrit utsarga, 'a pouring forth, a letting go' (Monier-Williams), before করা; to dedicate a book is to give it away to someone.
