## Grammar Lens — the seam, from the other side
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-YOTTSU, JA-LEX-YON, JA-GRAMMAR-KUN-IN-THE-COUNT] -->

Chapter fourteen taught **よん** and said something that was left hanging: *that
split is coming back; it is the same seam the counters run along.* Here it is.

**よん** is a native word standing inside the borrowed count. **よっつ** is that
same native word in the count it belongs to. They begin with the same beat, and
that is not an accident — it is one word appearing twice:

| number | borrowed | native |
|---|---|---|
| one | いち | ひと- |
| two | に | ふた- |
| three | さん | み- |
| **four** | *shi* | **よ-** |

Every other line of the borrowed column is genuinely borrowed. At four, the
native word came across into the borrowed count and stayed — which is why the
number you actually say for four is **よん** and not *shi*. That is what chapter
fourteen was pointing at, and you can now see both halves of it at once.
