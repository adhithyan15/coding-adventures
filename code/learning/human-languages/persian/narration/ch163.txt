Persian, chapter 163: Colourful, Thin, Smooth, Broken, Different.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رنگارنگ (rangârang) — colourful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for brave, then the Persian for talkative.

You'll want to know: رنگارنگ.
رنگارنگ — rangârang — "colourful".

The word, taken apart.
rangârang is rang, "colour", doubled with a linking â: colour upon colour. rang is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian rang, "colour, dye".

Lesson 3 of 5.
صاف (sâf) — smooth; straight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for colourful, then the Persian for thin.

You'll want to know: صاف.
صاف — sâf — "smooth; straight".

The word, taken apart.
sâf is borrowed from Arabic ṣāfī, "pure, clear". Its Arabic root is ṣ-f-w (Lane). Persian uses it for smooth and straight.
