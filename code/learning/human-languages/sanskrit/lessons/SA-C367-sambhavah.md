## You'll want to know: सम्भवः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C367-SAMBHAVA367-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भवः** — *sambhavaḥ* — "a possibility".

> यदि धनम् अस्ति, तर्हि यात्रायाः सम्भवः अस्ति।
>
> *yadi dhanam asti, tarhi yātrāyāḥ sambhavaḥ asti.*
>
> If there is money, a journey is possible.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भवः** (*sambhavaḥ*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √bhū, "to be"; MW gives *sam-bhava* "capacity, ability, possibility" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
