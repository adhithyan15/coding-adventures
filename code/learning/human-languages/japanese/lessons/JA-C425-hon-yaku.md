## You'll want to know: ほんやく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C425-TSUUYAKU425-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ほんやく** — *hon'yaku* — "translation (of writing)".

> **この てがみの ほんやくを おねがいします。** — *kono tegami no hon'yaku o onegai shimasu* — "Please translate this letter for me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほんやく** (*hon'yaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hon*, "turn over" (Middle Chinese *phjon*) and *yaku*, "translate" (*yek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
