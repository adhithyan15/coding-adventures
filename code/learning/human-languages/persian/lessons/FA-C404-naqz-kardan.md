## You'll want to know: نقض کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C404-MOQARRARAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نقض کردن** (*naqz kardan*) — "to break, to violate".

> آن شرکت قانون را نقض کرد.
>
> *ân sherkat qânun râ naqz kard.*
>
> That company broke the law.

For laws, rules and agreements; formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقض** is usually traced to Arabic *naqḍ* "undoing, breaking"; Persian says the Arabic *ḍ* as *z*.
