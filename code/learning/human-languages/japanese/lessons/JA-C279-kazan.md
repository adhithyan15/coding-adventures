## You'll want to know: かざん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C279-KAZAN279-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かざん** — *kazan* — "a volcano".

> **日本には かざんが おおいです。** — *nihon ni wa kazan ga ōi desu* — "Japan has many volcanoes."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かざん** (*kazan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ka*, "fire" (Middle Chinese *xwaX*) and *zan*, "mountain" (*srean*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The second is voiced from *san* to *zan*.
