## You'll want to know: ಅಡಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C345-ADAGU345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಡಗು** — *aḍagu* — "to hide oneself".

> ಕಳ್ಳ ಬಾವಿಯ ಹಿಂದೆ ಅಡಗಿದನು.
>
> *kaḷḷa bāviya hinde aḍagidanu.*
>
> The thief hid behind the well.

Past (I …): ಅಡಗಿದೆ, *aḍagide*.

To hide oneself is ಅಡಗು; to hide something is ಅಡಗಿಸು or ಬಚ್ಚಿಡು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಡಗು** (*aḍagu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 63 lists Kannada *aḍagu* beside Tamil *aṭaṅku*, "to be compressed, to cease, to disappear".
