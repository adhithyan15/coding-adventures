## You'll want to know: बलात्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C290-DASYU290-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बलात्** — *balāt* — "by force".

> दस्युः बलात् धनं गृह्णाति।
>
> *dasyuḥ balāt dhanaṃ gṛhṇāti.*
>
> The robber takes the money by force.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बलात्** (*balāt*) is built on *bala*, "strength, force": MW records its ablative *balāt* as meaning forcibly, against one's will (MW).
