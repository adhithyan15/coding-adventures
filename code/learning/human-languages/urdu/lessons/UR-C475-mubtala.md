## You'll want to know: مبتلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C475-LONG475-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مبتلا** — *mubtalā* — "afflicted (with)".

> وہ دو سال سے دمے میں مبتلا ہے۔
>
> *voh do sāl se dame meṅ mubtalā hai.*
>
> He has suffered from asthma for two years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مبتلا** (*mubtalā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mubtalā*, "tried, afflicted".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
