## You'll want to know: die Markthalle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C441-FASSADE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Markthalle** — "the covered market, the market hall".

> In der Markthalle kauft sie Käse und Obst.
>
> *She buys cheese and fruit in the covered market.*

Plural: *die Markthallen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Markthalle* is built from *Markt* 'market', which goes back through Old High German *marchāt* to Latin *mercātus* (EtymDB), and *Halle* 'hall', an inherited Germanic word.
