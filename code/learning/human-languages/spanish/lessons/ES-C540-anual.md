## You'll want to know: anual
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C540-TEMPORADA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**anual** — "yearly, annual".

> La fiesta anual del pueblo es en agosto.
>
> *The town's annual festival is in August.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anual* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *annuālis* 'yearly' (EtymDB), built on *annus* 'year'.
