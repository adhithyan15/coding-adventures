## You'll want to know: قطعی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C392-EHTEMAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**قطعی** (*qat'i*) — "definite, final".

> تاریخ سفر هنوز قطعی نیست.
>
> *târikh-e safar hanuz qat'i nist.*
>
> The date of the trip is not definite yet.

From *qat'* "cutting" + *-i*: a cut-and-dried decision.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قطعی** is usually traced to Arabic *qaṭ'ī* "decisive", built on *qaṭ'* "cutting": a definite answer cuts off doubt.
