## You'll want to know: ماضی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C144-MAZI144-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ماضی** — *māzī* — "the past". Grammar books use it for the past tense too. It came into Urdu from Arabic.
