Malayalam, chapter 230: To suggest, To criticise, To imagine, To solve, To be bored.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
നിർദ്ദേശിക്കുക (nirddēśikkuka) — to suggest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to doubt, then the Malayalam for to agree.

You'll want to know: നിർദ്ദേശിക്കുക.
നിർദ്ദേശിക്കുക — nirddēśikkuka — "to suggest".

The word, taken apart.
നിർദ്ദേശിക്കുക (nirddēśikkuka) is built on Sanskrit nirdēśa, "pointing out, instruction" (CDIAL 7347), with the ending that makes a Malayalam verb.
