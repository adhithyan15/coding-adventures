Lesson 1 of 5.
પરોણાગત (paroṇāgat) — hospitality (to a guest).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a rest on the way, then the Gujarati for to walk up and down.

You'll want to know: પરોણાગત.
પરોણાગત — paroṇāgat — "hospitality (to a guest)".
આ હોટેલની પરોણાગત ઘણી સારી છે. ā hoṭelnī paroṇāgat ghaṇī sārī che. The hospitality at this hotel is very good.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પરોણાગત (paroṇāgat) is built on પરોણો, a guest, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit prāhuṇa, "a guest" (CDIAL 8973): the care a house gives to the guest who stays in it.

નાના ઓરડામાં અમને ઘણી અગવડ પડી. nānā orḍāmā̃ amne ghaṇī agvaḍ paḍī. We were very uncomfortable in the small room.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અગવડ (agvaḍ) is the opposite of સગવડ (sagvaḍ), a convenience, and the two are usually heard together. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

You'll want to know: આવકારવું.
આવકારવું — āvkārvũ — "to welcome (a guest)".
મેનેજર દરેક મહેમાનને હસીને આવકારે છે. menejar darek mahemānne hasīne āvkāre che. The manager welcomes every guest with a smile.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મહેમાનને આવકાર્યો, teṇe mahemānne āvkāryo.

The word, taken apart.
આવકારવું (āvkārvũ) is built on આવકાર, "a welcome", which seems to go with આવવું, to come. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
