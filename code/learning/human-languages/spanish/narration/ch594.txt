Spanish, chapter 594: En equipo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el liderazgo — the leadership.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to do for a living, then the Spanish for efficient.

You'll want to know: el liderazgo.
el liderazgo — "the leadership".
Necesitamos un buen liderazgo. We need good leadership.
Built on el líder — the leader.

The word, taken apart.
liderazgo is built on líder, which was borrowed from English leader (EtymDB), with the ending -azgo.

Lesson 4 of 5.
dirigir — to run, to manage; to direct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to motivate, then the Spanish for the collaboration.

You'll want to know: dirigir.
dirigir — "to run, to manage; to direct".
Ella dirige el proyecto. She runs the project.
Spelling: dirijo. Dirigir una película — to direct a film.

The word, taken apart.
dirigir is a learned borrowing from Latin dirigere 'to set straight, to direct', built on regere 'to guide, to rule' (EtymDB).
