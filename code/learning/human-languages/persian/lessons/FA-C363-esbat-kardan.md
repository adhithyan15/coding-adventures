## You'll want to know: اثبات کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C363-EDDEA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اثبات کردن** (*esbât kardan*) — "to prove".

> او نمیتواند حرفش را اثبات کند.
>
> *u nemitavânad harfash râ esbât konad.*
>
> He cannot prove what he says.

*Esbât* "proof" + *kardan*. Formal; everyday speech prefers *sâbet kardan*. In Persian, ث sounds like s.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اثبات** comes from Arabic *ithbāt* "proving, establishing" (EtymDB); Persian says the Arabic *th* as *s*. With *kardan* "to do" it becomes a verb.
