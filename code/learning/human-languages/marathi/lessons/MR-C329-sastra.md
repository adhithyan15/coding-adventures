## You'll want to know: शस्त्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C329-SHASTRA329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शस्त्र** — *śastra* — "a weapon".

> शत्रूने सगळी शस्त्रं खाली ठेवली.
>
> *śatrūne sagḷī śastraṁ khālī ṭhevlī.*
>
> The enemy laid down all their weapons.

Neuter.

Plural शस्त्रं; शस्त्र खाली ठेवणे, to lay down arms.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शस्त्र** (*śastra*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śastra*, 'a knife, a sword, a weapon' (Monier-Williams).
