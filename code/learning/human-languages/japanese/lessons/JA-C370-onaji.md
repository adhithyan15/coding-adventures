## You'll want to know: おなじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C370-MATOMARU370-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おなじ** — *onaji* — "the same".

> **みんなが おなじ いけんに なったら、きめます。** — *minna ga onaji iken ni nattara, kimemasu* — "Once everyone has the same opinion, we will decide."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おなじ** (*onaji*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
