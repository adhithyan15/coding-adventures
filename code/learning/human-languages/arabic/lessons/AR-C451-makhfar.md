## You'll want to know: مخفر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C451-MAKHFAR451-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مخفر** — *makhfar* — "a police station".

> ذهب الرجل إلى المخفر بعد الحادث.
>
> *dhahaba ar-rajul ilā al-makhfar baʿda al-ḥādith.*
>
> The man went to the police station after the accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخفر** (*makhfar*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-f-r*, "to guard, to protect" (Lane). The pattern *mafʿal* makes it a guard post.
