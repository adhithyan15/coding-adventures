## You'll want to know: تاج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C350-PADESHAH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تاج** (*tâj*) — "a crown".

> پادشاه یک تاج طلا بر سر داشت.
>
> *Pâdeshâh yek tâj-e talâ bar sar dâsht.*
>
> The king wore a gold crown on his head.

*Tâj-o takht* "crown and throne", royal power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tâj* is an Iranian word: EtymDB traces it to Parthian *tāg*, "crown", and lists Arabic *tāj* beside it.
