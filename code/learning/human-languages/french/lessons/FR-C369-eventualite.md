## You'll want to know: l'éventualité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C369-EVENTUEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'éventualité** — "the possibility, the eventuality".

> Il faut prévoir toutes les éventualités.
>
> *We have to plan for every possibility.*

*Dans l'éventualité où* takes the conditional. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*éventualité* is built from *éventuel* and *-ité*. *éventuel* rests on Latin *ēventus* "an outcome", from *ēvenīre* "to happen" (Lewis & Short).
