## You'll want to know: 交通
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C409-TRAFFIC409-02]; assesses=[] -->

**交通** — *jiāotōng* — "traffic; transport".

> *zhèr de jiāotōng hěn fāngbiàn* — It's easy to get around here.

The new character, in pieces: 通 — ten strokes in two pieces: the right piece first, seven strokes, a short bar that turns down to the left with a dot under it, then a frame with two short bars and a vertical through them; the walking piece of 这, three strokes, sits under it and is written last. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**交通** (*jiāotōng*) is built from 交 'to cross' and 通 'to go through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaew* and *thuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to reach through'.
