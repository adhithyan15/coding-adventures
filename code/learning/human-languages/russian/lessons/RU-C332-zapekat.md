## You'll want to know: запекать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C332-ZAPEKAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**запекать** (*zapekát*) — "to bake, to roast".

> Мама запекает рыбу в духовке.
>
> *Máma zapekáyet rýbu v dukhóvke.*
>
> Mum is baking fish in the oven.

Imperfective; perfective *запечь* (*zapéch*), *запеку*, *запечёшь* (*zapechyósh*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*запекать* is built on *за-* and *печь* "to bake", an inherited Slavic verb (Proto-Slavic *pekti*); EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
