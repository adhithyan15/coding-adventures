## You'll want to know: चोरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C288-CHORI288-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चोरी** — *corī* — "a theft".

> चोरी के बाद क्या होगा, यह पुलिस पर निर्भर करता है।
>
> *corī ke bād kyā hogā, yah pulis par nirbhar kartā hai.*
>
> What happens after the theft depends on the police.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from Sanskrit *caurikā*, "theft" (CDIAL 4937). It sits beside **चोर**, a thief: **चोर** is the person, **चोरी** is what the person does, and **चोरी होना** is for a theft to take place.
