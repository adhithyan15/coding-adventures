## You'll want to know: प्रगल्भः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C463-PARAKRAMA463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रगल्भः** — *pragalbhaḥ* — "bold".

> प्रगल्भः बालः सभायां वदति।
>
> *pragalbhaḥ bālaḥ sabhāyāṃ vadati.*
>
> The bold boy speaks up in the assembly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रगल्भः** (*pragalbhaḥ*) is built from *pra* and the root √galbh, "to be bold"; MW gives "bold, confident, resolute, brave" (MW).
