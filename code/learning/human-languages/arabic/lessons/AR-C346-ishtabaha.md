# اشتبه (ishtabaha) — to suspect

## You'll want to know: اشتبه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C346-JASUS346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتبه** — *ishtabaha* — "to suspect".

> اشتبهت الشرطة في رجل طويل.
>
> *ishtabahat ash-shurṭa fī rajul ṭawīl.*
>
> The police suspected a tall man.

Present: يشتبه, *yashtabih*.

It takes في or بـ. A suspect is مشتبه به.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتبه** (*ishtabaha*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-b-h* (Lane), "to resemble", the root of the taught *mushābih*, similar. Form VIII is "to be doubtful", when things look alike: to suspect.
