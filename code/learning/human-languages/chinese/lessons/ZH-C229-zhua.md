## You'll want to know: zhuā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C229-PET229-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuā* — "to scratch; to grab".

> *bié pà wǒ de māo bù zhuā rén* — Don't worry, my cat doesn't scratch.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuā* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsraew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to scratch and pinch'; the vowel of zhuā is not the regular outcome of that form.
