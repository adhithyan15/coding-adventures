Russian, chapter 387: Knowledge and skills.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
знание (znániye) — knowledge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a concept, then the Russian for a term.

You'll want to know: знание.
знание (znániye) — "knowledge".
Для этой работы нужны знания языков. Dlya étoy rabóty nuzhný znániya yazykóv. This job needs a knowledge of languages.
Neuter; usually plural, знания: хорошие знания.

The word, taken apart.
знание (znániye) is built on знать "to know", inherited through Old East Slavic from Proto-Slavic znati (EtymDB).

Умение слушать — редкий дар. Uméniye slúshat — rédkiy dar. The ability to listen is a rare gift.
Neuter. From уметь, to know how.

умение (uméniye) is built on уметь "to know how", which is usually linked with ум "mind", inherited from Proto-Slavic umŭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
усваивать (usváivat) — to take in, to master.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for knowledge, then the Russian for an ability.

You'll want to know: усваивать.
усваивать (usváivat) — "to take in, to master".
Дети быстро усваивают новые слова. Déti býstro usváivayut nóvyye slová. Children take in new words quickly.
Imperfective; perfective усвоить. Built on свой, one’s own: to make something your own.

The word, taken apart.
усваивать (usváivat) is the imperfective of усвоить, built on свой "one’s own": to master a thing is to make it your own.

Lesson 4 of 5.
отчислять (otchislyát) — to expel (from a university); to deduct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an ability, then the Russian for to take in.

You'll want to know: отчислять.
отчислять (otchislyát) — "to expel (from a university); to deduct".
Студента отчислили за прогулы. Studénta otchíslili za progúly. The student was expelled for missing classes.
Imperfective; perfective отчислить. It also means to deduct or transfer money: отчислять (otchislyát) налоги.

The word, taken apart.
отчислять (otchislyát) is built from от- and число "number", inherited from Proto-Slavic čislo (EtymDB): to take a name off the count.

Lesson 5 of 5.
образованный (obrazóvannyy) — educated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to take in, then the Russian for to expel.

You'll want to know: образованный.
образованный (obrazóvannyy) — "educated".
Он очень образованный человек. On óchen obrazóvannyy chelovék. He is a very educated man.
Short form образован. From образование, education.

The word, taken apart.
образованный (obrazóvannyy) is the participle of образовать "to form", from образ "a shape, an image", Proto-Slavic obrazŭ (EtymDB): a person whose mind has been given shape.
