Portuguese, chapter 401: Na autoestrada.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a faixa — the lane (of a road); the strip, the band.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a jungle, then the Portuguese for a slipper.

You'll want to know: a faixa.
a faixa — "the lane (of a road); the strip, the band".
Na autoestrada, os carros lentos vão na faixa da direita. On the motorway, slow cars keep to the right-hand lane.
A faixa de rodagem — the carriageway. Uma faixa is also a strip of cloth or a banner.

The word, taken apart.
faixa is inherited from Latin fascia 'band, strip of cloth' (EtymDB): a lane is a long strip of road.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.
o separador — the central reservation; the divider, the tab.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the lane, then the Portuguese for the hard shoulder.

You'll want to know: o separador.
o separador — "the central reservation; the divider, the tab".
Há flores no separador da autoestrada. There are flowers on the central reservation of the motorway.
O separador central is the strip between the two directions. In a browser or a folder, um separador is a tab or a divider.

The word, taken apart.
separador is built on separar 'to separate', a learned borrowing from Latin sēparāre 'to put apart' (EtymDB).

o nó — "the junction (of motorways); the knot".
Saímos da autoestrada no próximo nó. We leave the motorway at the next junction.
Also a knot in a rope or a tie: dar um nó.

The word, taken apart.
nó is inherited from Latin nōdus 'knot' (EtymDB): a junction is where roads are tied together.
