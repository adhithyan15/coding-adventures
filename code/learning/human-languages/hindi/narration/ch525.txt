Hindi, chapter 525: Seeds and Soil.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फटकना (phaṭaknā) — to winnow (grain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to set (curd), then the Hindi for to rinse out.

You'll want to know: फटकना.
फटकना — phaṭaknā — "to winnow (grain)".
किसान आँगन में अनाज फटक रहा है। Kisān āṁgan meṁ anāj phaṭak rahā hai. The farmer is winnowing grain in the courtyard.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चावल फटके, usne cāval phaṭke.

The word, taken apart.
फटकना (phaṭaknā) is a tadbhava, inherited from phaṭ, "sudden movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9038). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to winnow".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: रोपना.
रोपना — ropnā — "to plant (a sapling)".
हमने बगीचे में आम का एक पेड़ रोपा। Hamne bagīce meṁ ām kā ek peṛ ropā. We planted a mango tree in the garden.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने फूल रोपे, usne phūl rope.

The word, taken apart.
रोपना (ropnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit rōpayati, "plants", which CDIAL files with rupati, "pierces, plants" (CDIAL 10783). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to place on, plant, sow".
