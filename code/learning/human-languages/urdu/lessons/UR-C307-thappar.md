## You'll want to know: تھپڑ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C307-JHAGRA307-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تھپڑ** — *thappaṛ* — "a slap".

> اسے ایک تھپڑ لگا۔
>
> *use ek thappaṛ lagā.*
>
> He got a slap.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تھپڑ** (*thappaṛ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *thappaṛ*, "a slap", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6091).
