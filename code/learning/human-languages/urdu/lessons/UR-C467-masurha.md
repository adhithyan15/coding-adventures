## You'll want to know: مسوڑھا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C467-MOUTH467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مسوڑھا** — *masūṛhā* — "a gum (of the teeth)".

> دانت کے درد سے مسوڑھا سوج گیا۔
>
> *dānt ke dard se masūṛhā sūj gayā.*
>
> The gum swelled up with the toothache.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسوڑھا** (*masūṛhā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *masūṛā* under *māṃsapuṭaka*, "the gums", a form it reconstructs from the words for "flesh" and "fold" (CDIAL 9984).
