## You'll want to know: うやまう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C479-BOND479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うやまう** — *uyamau* — "to revere; to honour".

> **みんな あの せんせいを うやまっています。** — *minna ano sensei o uyamatte imasu* — "Everyone respects that teacher."

Polite **うやまいます**, *uyamaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **がくせいが せんせいを うやまいました。**, *gakusei ga sensei o uyamaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うやまう** (*uyamau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
