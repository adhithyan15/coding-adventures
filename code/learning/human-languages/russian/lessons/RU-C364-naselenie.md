## You'll want to know: население
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C364-OBSHCHESTVO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**население** (*naseléniye*) — "the population".

> Население города — больше миллиона человек.
>
> *Naseléniye góroda — bólshe millióna chelovék.*
>
> The population of the town is over a million people.

Neuter. *Население страны* — a country’s population.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**население** is built on *населить* "to people, to settle", from *на-* and the root of *селить* "to settle" and *село* "a village": the people settled in a place.
