## You'll want to know: la batalla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C567-GUERRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la batalla** — "the battle".

> La batalla duró tres días.
>
> *The battle lasted three days.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*batalla* comes from Late Latin *battuālia* 'fighting and fencing drills' (EtymDB), from *battuere* 'to beat', probably through Occitan.
