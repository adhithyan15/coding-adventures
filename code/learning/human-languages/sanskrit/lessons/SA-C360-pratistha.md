## You'll want to know: प्रतिष्ठा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C360-YASHAS360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिष्ठा** — *pratiṣṭhā* — "standing; reputation".

> वैद्यस्य ग्रामे प्रतिष्ठा अस्ति।
>
> *vaidyasya grāme pratiṣṭhā asti.*
>
> The doctor has standing in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिष्ठा** (*pratiṣṭhā*) is built on *prati* and the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives *prati-ṣṭhā* "steadfastness" and "high rank or position, fame" (MW).
