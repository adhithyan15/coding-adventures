Russian, chapter 308: School and university.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
выпускник (vypuskník) — a school-leaver, a graduate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to quit, then the Russian for an inheritance.

You'll want to know: выпускник.
выпускник (vypuskník) — "a school-leaver, a graduate".
Выпускники подарили учителю цветы. Vypusknikí podaríli uchítelyu tsvetý. The school-leavers gave the teacher flowers.
A woman is выпускница.

The word, taken apart.
выпускник (vypuskník) is built on выпуск "a graduating class", from выпускать "to let out", whose root is Proto-Slavic pustiti "to let go" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
поступать (postupát) — to enter, to get into (a university); to act.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an inheritance, then the Russian for a school-leaver.

You'll want to know: поступать.
поступать (postupát) — "to enter, to get into (a university); to act".
Брат поступил в университет. Brat postupíl v universitét. My brother got into university.
Imperfective; perfective поступить (postupít). Поступить в + accusative. Also "to act": он поступил честно.

The word, taken apart.
поступать (postupát) is built on по- and ступать "to step", the root of ступень "a step", Proto-Slavic stǫpenĭ (EtymDB): to step in.

Lesson 3 of 5.
оканчивать (okánchivat) — to finish, to graduate from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a school-leaver, then the Russian for to enter.

You'll want to know: оканчивать.
оканчивать (okánchivat) — "to finish, to graduate from".
Он окончил школу в прошлом году. On okónchil shkólu v próshlom godú. He finished school last year.
Imperfective; perfective окончить (okónchit). It takes the accusative: окончить университет.

The word, taken apart.
оканчивать (okánchivat) is built on о- and конец "end", inherited through Old East Slavic konĭcĭ from Proto-Slavic konĭcĭ "end" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
диплом (diplóm) — a degree, a diploma.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to enter, then the Russian for to finish.

You'll want to know: диплом.
диплом (diplóm) — "a degree, a diploma".
Она получила диплом врача. Oná poluchíla diplóm vrachá. She got her medical degree.
Masculine. Дипломная работа — a final-year thesis.

The word, taken apart.
диплом (diplóm) is usually traced through Latin diplōma to Greek díplōma "a paper folded double"; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
аттестат (attestát) — a school-leaving certificate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to finish, then the Russian for a degree.

You'll want to know: аттестат.
аттестат (attestát) — "a school-leaving certificate".
В июне мы получили аттестаты. V iyúne my poluchíli attestáty. In June we got our school-leaving certificates.
Аттестат зрелости — the full name, "certificate of maturity".

The word, taken apart.
аттестат (attestát) goes back to Latin attestātus "confirmed" (EtymDB), from attestārī "to bear witness", built on testis "a witness" (Lewis & Short).
