Kannada, chapter 506: Sleepy Nights.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹೊದೆ (hode) — to cover oneself; to wrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to strive, then the Kannada for to beg.

You'll want to know: ಹೊದೆ.
ಹೊದೆ — hode — "to cover oneself; to wrap".
ಚಳಿಗೆ ಅಜ್ಜ ಕಂಬಳಿ ಹೊದ್ದು ಮಲಗಿದರು. caḷige ajja kambaḷi hoddu malagidaru. In the cold, grandfather slept wrapped in a blanket.
Past (I …): ಹೊದ್ದೆ, hodde.
The participle is ಹೊದ್ದು: ಕಂಬಳಿ ಹೊದ್ದು, wrapped in a blanket. ಹೊದ್ದುಕೊ, to wrap yourself up. Keep it apart from ಹೋದೆ, I went, with a long ō.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೊದೆ (hode) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4509 lists Kannada pode, "to put over, put on, wrap round", and podisu, "to cover". Old p- became h-.

ಕನವರಿಸು — kanavarisu — "to talk in sleep".
ನಿನ್ನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ತಮ್ಮ ನಿದ್ದೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕನವರಿಸಿದನು. ninne rātri tamma niddeyalli kanavarisidanu. Last night my younger brother talked in his sleep.
Past (I …): ಕನವರಿಸಿದೆ, kanavariside.
Built on ಕನಸು, a dream: talking from inside a dream.

The word, taken apart.
ಕನವರಿಸು (kanavarisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1407 lists Kannada kana, kanasu, "a dream", and kanavarisu, a verb of dreaming.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮೈಮುರಿ (maimuri) — to stretch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to talk in sleep, then the Kannada for to roll over.

You'll want to know: ಮೈಮುರಿ.
ಮೈಮುರಿ — maimuri — "to stretch".
ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಎದ್ದ ಬೆಕ್ಕು ಮೈಮುರಿಯಿತು. beḷagge edda bekku maimuriyitu. The cat got up in the morning and stretched.
Past (I …): ಮೈಮುರಿದೆ, maimuride.
ಮೈ, the body + ಮುರಿ, to bend: the long stretch after sleep.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೈಮುರಿ (maimuri) is a native Dravidian compound: mai, "the body" (DEDR 5073), and muri, "to bend; to stretch oneself with windings of the limbs" (DEDR 4977).

ಮಗುವಿನ ರೆಪ್ಪೆಗಳು ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಮುಚ್ಚಿದವು. maguvina reppegaḷu nidhānavāgi muccidavu. The baby's eyelids slowly closed.
