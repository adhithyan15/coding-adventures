## You'll want to know: பந்தயம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C533-OTTAM533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**பந்தயம்** — *pantayam* — "a race; a bet".

> நூறு மீட்டர் பந்தயத்தில் அவன் முதலில் வந்தான்.
>
> *nūṟu mīṭṭar pantayattil avaṉ mutalil vantāṉ.*
>
> He came first in the hundred-metre race.

Both "a race" and "a bet": பந்தயம் கட்டு, to place a bet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

DEDR 3921 gives **பந்தயம்** (*pantayam*), "a contest for a prize, a wager", with cognates across the south, and reports a proposal to derive the group from Sanskrit *paṇita*, "a wager"; the origin is not settled.
