## You'll want to know: ఇంధనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C444-INDHANAM444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇంధనం** — *indhanaṁ* — "fuel".

> దారి మధ్యలో కారులో ఇంధనం అయిపోయింది.
>
> *dāri madhyalō kārulō indhanaṁ ayipōyindi.*
>
> Halfway there the car ran out of fuel.

A word for every kind of fuel: firewood, petrol, gas. అయిపోవు, to run out, goes with it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇంధనం** (*indhanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *indhana*, "lighting, kindling, fuel", from the root *indh*, "to kindle" (CDIAL 1584).
