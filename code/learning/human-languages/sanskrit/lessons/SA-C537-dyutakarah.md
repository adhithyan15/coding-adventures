## You'll want to know: द्यूतकारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C537-PASAKA537-05]; assesses=[] -->

**द्यूतकारः** — *dyūtakāraḥ* — "a gambler".

> यदि द्यूतकारः सदा जयति, तर्हि सः वञ्चकः भवेत्।
>
> *yadi dyūtakāraḥ sadā jayati, tarhi saḥ vañcakaḥ bhavet.*
>
> If a gambler wins every time, he may be a cheat.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**द्यूतकारः** (*dyūtakāraḥ*) is built from *dyūta*, "gambling, dice", and *kāra*, "doing", from the root √kṛ; MW gives *dyūtakāra* as the same as *dyūtakara*, a gambler (MW).
