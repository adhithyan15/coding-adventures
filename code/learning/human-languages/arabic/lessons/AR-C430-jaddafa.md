## You'll want to know: جدف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C430-MARFA430-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جدف** — *jaddafa* — "to row".

> جدف أخي نحو الجزيرة.
>
> *jaddafa akhī naḥwa al-jazīra.*
>
> My brother rowed towards the island.

Present: يجدف, *yujaddif*.

An oar is *mijdāf*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جدف** (*jaddafa*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *j-d-f*, "to row". It is Form II.
