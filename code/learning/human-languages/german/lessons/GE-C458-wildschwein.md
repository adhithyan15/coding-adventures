## You'll want to know: das Wildschwein
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C458-WILDTIERE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Wildschwein** — "the wild boar".

> Nachts kommen die Wildschweine bis an das Dorf.
>
> *At night the wild boar come right up to the village.*

Plural: *die Wildschweine*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wildschwein* is built from *wild* 'wild' and *Schwein* 'pig', both inherited: *Schwein* goes back to Proto-Germanic *swīną* and is a cousin of English *swine* (EtymDB).
