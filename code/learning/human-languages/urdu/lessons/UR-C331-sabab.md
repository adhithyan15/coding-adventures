## You'll want to know: سبب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C331-SABAB331-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سبب** — *sabab* — "a cause".

> اس حادثے کا سبب کیا تھا؟
>
> *is hādse kā sabab kyā thā?*
>
> What was the cause of this accident?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سبب** (*sabab*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *sabab*, "a cause, a means".
