## You'll want to know: わな
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C304-ONGAESHI304-03]; assesses=[] -->

**わな** — *wana* — "a trap; a snare".

> **おじいさんは わなの とりを たすけました。** — *ojiisan wa wana no tori o tasukemashita* — "The old man rescued the bird from the trap."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わな** (*wana*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
