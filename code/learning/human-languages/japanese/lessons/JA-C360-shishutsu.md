## You'll want to know: ししゅつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C360-SHUUNYUU360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ししゅつ** — *shishutsu* — "spending; expenditure".

> **ししゅつが ふえたら、せつやくした ほうが いいです。** — *shishutsu ga fuetara, setsuyaku shita hō ga ii desu* — "If spending goes up, we had better economize."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ししゅつ** (*shishutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "support" (Middle Chinese *tsye*) and *shutsu*, "exit" (*tsyhwit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
