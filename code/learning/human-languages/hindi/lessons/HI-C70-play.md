# खेलना (khelnā) — to play

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C70-LEISURE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *khel*, then write **खेल**: ख, then े above it, then ल.

## You'll want to know: खेलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C70-LEISURE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खेलना** (*khelnā*) — "to play".

The same खेल you learnt in the last lesson, with **-ना** on the end. That is the
whole of it: Hindi makes a doing word out of a naming word by adding those two
letters, and you have now watched it happen once.

Look at what that gives you. **जाना** was built the same way, and so were
**बोलना**, **लिखना**, **सुनना**. Four you already own and one you have just
made — the ending is a tool, not a list to memorise.

**मैं खेलता हूँ** is "I play", in the frame you have used since **मैं हिंदी
बोलता हूँ**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A game, and the doing word that comes out of it.
