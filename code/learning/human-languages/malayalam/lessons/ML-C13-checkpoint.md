# Chapter 13 checkpoint — head, hand, ത and ട

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Nothing new here. The two body words out loud first, then the two
letters this chapter put on the page.

## Guided Practice — head and hand
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-CONCEPT-C13-SHAREERA-BHAAGANGAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "head", then "hand", in Malayalam]

(*Tala*; *kai*.)

- How close are they to Tamil's words? (Very close: Tamil *talai* and *kai*.
  Both pairs are native Dravidian, unlike the family words, where Malayalam
  went its own way.)

## Script — ത and ട
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-08, ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-117] -->

- **തല** is *tala*, "head". Which letter opens it, and what does it carry?
  (**ത**, *ta*: a consonant with its *a* built in.)
- **രണ്ട്** is *raṇṭŭ*, "two". Which letter in it carries *ṭa*? (**ട**.)

[YOU READ: **തല** aloud, and point to the ത in it]

[YOU READ: **രണ്ട്** aloud, and point to the ട in it]

## Writing — from sound
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-08, ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-117] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

[YOU COVER: the Script block above, so no model stays in view]

[YOU WRITE: the letter for *ta*, then the letter for *ṭa*]

[YOU CHECK: against the key — **ത**, **ട**]

[YOU WRITE: one repaired shape, then stop]
