## You'll want to know: cirandar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C447-PASSEIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**cirandar** — "to potter about, to go here and there".

> Passei a manhã a cirandar pelas lojas.
>
> *I spent the morning pottering round the shops.*

Built on *a ciranda*, a sieve and also a round dance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cirandar* is built on *ciranda* 'sieve; a round dance' (EtymDB lists the word); the origin of *ciranda* is not settled.
