## You'll want to know: ವಯಸ್ಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C529-JIVANA529-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಯಸ್ಕ** — *vayaska* — "an adult".

> ಹದಿನೆಂಟು ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ವಯಸ್ಕರು ಆಗುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *hadineṇṭu varṣada nantara makkaḷu vayaskaru āguttāre.*
>
> After eighteen, children become adults.

Plural: ವಯಸ್ಕರು, *vayaskaru*.

Built on ವಯಸ್ಸು, age.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಯಸ್ಕ** (*vayaska*) is built on Sanskrit *vayas*, "age, vigour of life", which Monier-Williams also lists in compounds with -*vayaska* (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
