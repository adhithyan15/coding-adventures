## You'll want to know: visitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C678-VISITA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**visitar** — "to visit".

> Queremos visitar el museo mañana.
>
> *We want to visit the museum tomorrow.*

For people, use *a*: *visitar a mis abuelos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*visitar* comes from Latin *vīsitāre* "to see, to go to see" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built on *vīsere* "to go and see", from *vidēre* "to see".
