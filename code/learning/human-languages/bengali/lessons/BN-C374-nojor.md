## You'll want to know: নজর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C374-PROTIRODH374-05]; assesses=[] -->

**নজর** — *nôjôr* — "watch; a close eye (on something)".

> বাজারে গেলে ব্যাগের ওপর নজর রাখো।
>
> *bājāre gele byāger opôr nôjôr rākho.*
>
> If you go to the market, keep an eye on your bag.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নজর** (*nôjôr*) is usually traced to Arabic *naẓar*, a look, through Persian. নজর রাখা is to keep watch on something.
