# व — the loop you have drawn before

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-145] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: **श** — how many runs did its left side take?

[PAUSE 2s] This one is easier, and its left side is a shape already in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: व
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-146]; assesses=[] -->

**व** — *va*, somewhere between an English *v* and *w*.

What it is made of:

- a counterclockwise left loop
- a top-to-bottom right stem
- the top shirorekhā

Look back at **ग** and you have seen this before: the same counterclockwise loop
and the same right stem. What differs is where the loop sits and how it meets
the stem.

## Script: the last of the four
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-146, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-145] -->

| letter | sound |
|---|---|
| य | *ya* |
| र | *ra* |
| ल | *la* |
| **व** | ***va*** |

These four sit between the consonants and the vowels — you shape them like
consonants and they glide like vowels. Three were already yours. **This is the
fourth**, and the set is complete.

Words you have said since the beginning:

- **धन्यवाद** — thank you
- **अलविदा** — goodbye
- **स्वागत** — welcome
- **सोमवार** — Monday

## Writing: व
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-146] -->

- **1.** start at the upper-right of the body, travel left around the top, and continue counterclockwise around the loop to the right-side junction
- **2.** lift and draw the right stem top-to-bottom
- **3.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 2.** The same count as **श**, over much less distance.

> Verified three-stroke teaching form; everyday handwriting may join or simplify
> the body differently.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: JackPotte,
> ‘Devanagari v व.gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 29 March 2009.
