French, chapter 388: La peine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la peine — the sentence, the penalty; sorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for guilty, then the French for innocent.

You'll want to know: la peine.
la peine — "the sentence, the penalty; sorrow".
Il a eu une peine de deux ans de prison. He got a two-year prison sentence.
Avoir de la peine — to be sad. Ce n'est pas la peine — it is not worth it.

The word, taken apart.
peine is inherited through Old French from Latin poena "a penalty, punishment" (Lewis & Short), itself from Greek poinḗ "a fine".

What you've built.

You'll want to know: condamner.
condamner — "to sentence, to condemn".
Le tribunal l'a condamné à une amende. The court sentenced him to a fine.
The m is silent: condaner.

The word, taken apart.
condamner is inherited through Old French (EtymDB) from Latin condemnāre "to sentence, to condemn" (Lewis & Short), built on damnāre "to condemn", from damnum "loss, harm".

Lesson 4 of 5.
le détenu — the prisoner, the inmate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to sentence, then the French for the conviction.

You'll want to know: le détenu.
le détenu — "the prisoner, the inmate".
Les détenus peuvent recevoir des visites le samedi. The inmates can have visitors on Saturdays.
Feminine détenue. From détenir, to hold.

The word, taken apart.
détenu is the past participle of détenir "to hold, to detain", from Latin dētinēre "to hold back" (Lewis & Short), built on tenēre "to hold".

Lesson 5 of 5.
punir — to punish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the conviction, then the French for the prisoner.

You'll want to know: punir.
punir — "to punish".
Ses parents l'ont puni parce qu'il a cassé la fenêtre. His parents punished him because he broke the window.
A -ir verb like finir: ils punissent.

The word, taken apart.
punir comes through Old French punir (EtymDB) from Latin pūnīre "to inflict a penalty on" (Lewis & Short), from poena "penalty".
