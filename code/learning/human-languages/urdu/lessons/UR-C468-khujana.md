## You'll want to know: کھجانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C468-SMELL468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کھجانا** — *khujānā* — "to scratch an itch".

> مچھر کے کاٹنے پر وہ ہاتھ کھجا رہا تھا۔
>
> *machchhar ke kāṭne par voh hāth khujā rahā thā.*
>
> He was scratching his hand where the mosquito had bitten.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سر کھجایا, *us ne sar khujāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھجانا** (*khujānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khujānā*, "to itch", under Sanskrit *kharju*, "itching, scratching" (CDIAL 3827); **کھجلی**, "an itch", is from the same word.
