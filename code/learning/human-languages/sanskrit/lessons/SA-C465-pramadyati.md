## You'll want to know: प्रमाद्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C465-UDYAMIN465-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमाद्यति** — *pramādyati* — "is careless".

> चालकः कदापि न प्रमाद्येत्।
>
> *cālakaḥ kadāpi na pramādyet.*
>
> A driver should never be careless.

Past (he …): सः प्रमत्तवान्, *saḥ pramattavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमाद्यति** (*pramādyati*) is built from *pra* and the root √mad, "to be drunk, to rejoice"; MW gives "to be careless or negligent", and its ppp *pramatta* is "careless, heedless" (MW).
