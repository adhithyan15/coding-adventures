## You'll want to know: たがやす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C489-FARM489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**たがやす** — *tagayasu* — "to plough; to till".

> **のうかは はるに たんぼを たがやします。** — *nōka wa haru ni tanbo o tagayashimasu* — "Farmers plough the rice fields in spring."

Polite **たがやします**, *tagayashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **のうかが たんぼを たがやしました。**, *nōka ga tanbo o tagayashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たがやす** (*tagayasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
