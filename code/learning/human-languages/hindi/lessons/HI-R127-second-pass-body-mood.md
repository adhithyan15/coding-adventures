# What the body needs, and how you feel — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C124-BODY-01, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You have walked all afternoon. Say you are hungry, and thirsty.

## Grammar Lens: things that come to you
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C124-BODY-03, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-04, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-05] -->

| phrase | literally |
|---|---|
| **नींद आ रही है** | sleep is coming |
| **पसीना आ रहा है** | sweat is coming |
| **मज़ा आया** | fun came |

Sleep, sweat and fun all *come* to you, on **आना**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C124-BODY-01, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-02, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-03, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-04, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-05, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-01, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-02, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-03, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-04, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-05, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-04, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: say you are sleepy; say you have a headache]
- [YOU SAY: say you are sweating a lot]
- [YOU SAY: ask a nervous friend to stay calm; say you feel restless]
- [YOU SAY: say you don't feel like it today; then that you enjoyed it]
- [YOU SAY: a friend offers a plan: "is that so?" then "that'll do"]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C124-BODY-01, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-02, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-03, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-04, HI-LEX-C124-BODY-05, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-01, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-02, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-03, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-04, HI-LEX-C125-CALM-05, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-04, HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-05] -->

(**पसीना**.) A headache? (**सिरदर्द**.) Calm? (**शांत**.) Nervousness? (**घबराहट**.)
Restless? (**बेचैन**.) Not in the mood? (**मन नहीं है**.) Fun? (**मज़ा**.)
