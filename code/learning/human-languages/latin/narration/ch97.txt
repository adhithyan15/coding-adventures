Latin, chapter 97: Easy, Hard, Dear, Brave, Wise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
facilis, facile — easy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for bright, then the Latin for dark.

You'll want to know: facilis, facile.
facilis, facile — "easy". English facile.

What you've built.
One more word for easy, hard, dear, brave, wise.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: facilis]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: facilis, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say facilis]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Latin for bright, then the Latin for dark, then say facilis again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does facilis, facile mean? ("Easy".) Say it once more.
