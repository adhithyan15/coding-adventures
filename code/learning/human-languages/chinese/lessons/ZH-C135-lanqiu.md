## You'll want to know: lánqiú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C135-SPORT135-02]; assesses=[] -->

*lánqiú* — "basketball".

> *wǒ gēge hěn gāo tā xǐhuan lánqiú* — My brother is tall, and he loves basketball.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lánqiú* is built from *lán* 'basket' and *qiú* 'ball'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *lam* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a basket', a different word from the *lán* of blue; the second is the *qiú* of football.
