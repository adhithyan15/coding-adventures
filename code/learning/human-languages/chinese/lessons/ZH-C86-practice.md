## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-WHERE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C86-NAR-01, ZH-LEX-C86-SHANGBIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C86-XIABIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C86-XISHOUJIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C85-NAR-01, ZH-LEX-C85-WAIBIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C85-ZAI-01] -->

> **A:** 我的手机呢
>
> *wǒ de shǒujī ne* — Where's my phone?
>
> **B:** *zài zhuōzi shàngbian* — On the table.
>
> **A:** *bú zài zhuōzi shàngbian* — It isn't on the table.
>
> **B:** *zài yǐzi xiàbian* — Under the chair, then.
>
> **A:** 对 谢谢
>
> *duì, xièxie* — Yes. Thanks.
>
> **A:** *qǐngwèn, xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr* — Excuse me, where's the toilet?
>
> **B:** 在那儿
>
> *zài nàr* — Over there.
>
> **A:** 你在哪儿
>
> *nǐ zài nǎr* — And where will you be?
>
> **B:** *wǒ zài wàibian* — Outside.

Every answer starts at 在, or drops even that. The thing being looked for was named once, and the rest of the exchange leaves it out.

## What you've built — one question, any answer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-WHERE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-NA-WHERE-01, ZH-LEX-C86-NAR-01, ZH-LEX-C86-SHANGBIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C86-XIABIAN-01] -->

One question, **你在哪儿** *nǐ zài nǎr*, and the answer slots into the same place:

- **我在那儿** *wǒ zài nàr* — over there.
- *zài zhuōzi shàngbian* — on the table.
- *zài yǐzi xiàbian* — under the chair.

**Listen.** *Nǐ de shǒujī zài nǎr?*

**Speak.** Answer it three ways, then ask it back.

[YOU WRITE: 哪 — 口 first, then 那]
