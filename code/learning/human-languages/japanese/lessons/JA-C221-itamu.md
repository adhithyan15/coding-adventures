## You'll want to know: いたむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C221-FEVER221-03]; assesses=[] -->

**いたむ** — *itamu* — "to ache; to hurt".

> **はが いたみますか。** — *ha ga itamimasu ka* — "Does your tooth ache?"

Polite **いたみます**, *itamimasu*; a godan verb. The noun **いたみ**, *itami*, means "pain".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いたむ** (*itamu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
