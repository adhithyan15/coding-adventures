## You'll want to know: قلم مو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C525-PAINTING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قلم مو** (*qalam mu*) — "a paintbrush".

> او با یک قلم موی نازک کار میکند.
>
> *u bâ yek qalam mu-ye nâzok kâr mikonad.*
>
> He works with a thin paintbrush.

*qalam* 'pen' + *mu* 'hair': a hair pen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qalam mu* is built in Persian on *qalam*, 'pen', from Arabic *qalam* (EtymDB), and *mu*, 'hair', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
