## You'll want to know: ಸಮೀಕ್ಷೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C407-SAMSHODHANE407-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮೀಕ್ಷೆ** — *samīkṣe* — "a survey".

> ಸಮೀಕ್ಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನೂರು ಕುಟುಂಬಗಳು ಭಾಗವಹಿಸಿದವು.
>
> *samīkṣeyalli nūru kuṭumbagaḷu bhāgavahisidavu.*
>
> A hundred families took part in the survey.

ಸಮೀಕ್ಷೆ ನಡೆಸು, to conduct a survey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮೀಕ್ಷೆ** (*samīkṣe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samīkṣā*, "thorough or close inspection" (Monier-Williams).
