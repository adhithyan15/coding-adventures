## You'll want to know: fákuǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C312-PRISON312-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fákuǎn* — "to impose a fine; a penalty fine".

> *kāichē tài kuài yào fákuǎn* — Driving too fast gets you a fine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fákuǎn* is built from *fá* 'to punish' and *kuǎn* 'a sum', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjot* and *khwanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a punishment' and the second 'sincere'; its sense 'a sum of money' is not given there.
