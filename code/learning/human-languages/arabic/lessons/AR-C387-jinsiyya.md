## You'll want to know: جنسية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C387-UMMA387-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جنسية** — *jinsiyya* — "nationality".

> يحمل صديقي جنسيتين.
>
> *yaḥmilu ṣadīqī jinsiyyatayn.*
>
> My friend holds two nationalities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جنسية** (*jinsiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-n-s*, "a kind, a sort" (Lane). *Jinsiyya* is a modern abstract noun from *jins*, kind: the kind of citizen one is, nationality.
