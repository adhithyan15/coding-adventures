## You'll want to know: fàngxué
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C249-STUDY249-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngxué* — "to finish school for the day; (of a school) to let out".

> *wǒmen xiàwǔ sì diǎn fàngxué* — We finish school at four in the afternoon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngxué* is built from *fàng* 'to let go' and 学 'to study', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjangH* and *haewk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to drive out' and 'to send away'. Letting pupils go home from school is not given there.
