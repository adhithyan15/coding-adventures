## You'll want to know: ਮੈਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C227-DIN227-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੈਲ** — *mail* — "dirt; grime".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਪੈਰਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ ਮੈਲ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere pairāṁ utte mail hai.*
>
> There is dirt on my feet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੈਲ** (*mail*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*malin*, "dirty"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *mail*, "dirt" (CDIAL 9904).
