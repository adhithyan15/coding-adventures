## You'll want to know: のはら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C410-JIMOTO410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のはら** — *nohara* — "an open field; a meadow".

> **こどもが のはらを はしっています。** — *kodomo ga nohara o hashitte imasu* — "The children are running across the meadow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のはら** (*nohara*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *no*, "field" and *hara*, "plain"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
