## You'll want to know: il ladro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C391-ACCUSA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il ladro** — "the thief".

> Il ladro è entrato dalla finestra.
>
> *The thief came in through the window.*

A woman is *la ladra*. *Al ladro!* — stop thief!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ladro* is inherited from Latin *latrō, latrōnis* "a hired soldier; a robber, a bandit" (Lewis & Short).
