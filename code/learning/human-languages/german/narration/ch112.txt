Lesson 1 of 5.
der Teppich — a rug, a carpet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a lift, then the German for to fly.
[pause 3 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — read: der Flughafen, das Hotel, das Kino aloud as one line, without stopping between the words]

You'll want to know: der Teppich.
der Teppich — "a rug, a carpet".

The word, taken apart.
Teppich from Old High German teppih, from Latin tapētum, "carpet, tapestry" (EtymDB 2.1 has the Latin word), itself from Greek — borrowed. English tapestry shares the word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Fernseher — a television.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to fly, then the German for a rug.

You'll want to know: der Fernseher.
der Fernseher — "a television".

The word, taken apart.
Fernseher is the word for "far" + sehen, "to see", + -er: "the far-seer". It translates television piece by piece, Greek tēle, "far", + Latin vīsiō, "seeing". sehen from Proto-Germanic sehwaną. (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: der Kühlschrank.
der Kühlschrank — "a fridge".

The word, taken apart.
Kühlschrank is the word for "cool" + Schrank, "cupboard": a cooling cupboard. The "cool" word, Old High German kuoli, is listed in EtymDB 2.1 beside English cool.

The word, taken apart.
Herd from Old High German herd from Proto-Germanic herþaz, "hearth" — inherited, the same word as English hearth. (EtymDB 2.1) A cooker took over the hearth's job.
