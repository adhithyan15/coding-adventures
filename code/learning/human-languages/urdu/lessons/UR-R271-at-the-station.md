# Review — at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "how do you get to the station?" (*isṭeshan kaise jāte haiṅ?*) Then "turn left". (*bāeṅ muṛiye*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-02, UR-LEX-C271-CHHUTNA-01, UR-LEX-C271-HADSA-01, UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-02, UR-LEX-C111-THINGS111-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: A ticket to Lahore, please. How much is it?]
- [YOU SAY: When is the next train? It will leave at four.]
- [YOU SAY: I missed my train.]
- [YOU SAY: My bag got lost, and I told the police.]
- [YOU SAY: There was an accident on the road.]
- [YOU WRITE: **حادثہ** once, and point at ث inside it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-01, UR-LEX-C271-CHHUTNA-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "A ticket, please"? (**ایک ٹکٹ دیجیے**.) Now the other way round: in **میری گاڑی چھوٹ گئی**, which word sets **گئی**? (**گاڑی**.) "My book got lost"? (**میری کتاب کھو گئی**.) Which of these takes **نے**: **کھو گیا** or **بتایا**? (**بتایا**.)
