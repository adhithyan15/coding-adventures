Portuguese, chapter 276: Quinzenas e trimestres.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
a quinzena — the fortnight, two weeks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the twilight, then the Portuguese for the eve.

You'll want to know: a quinzena.
a quinzena — "the fortnight, two weeks".
Passámos uma quinzena na praia. We spent a fortnight at the beach.
Na primeira quinzena de agosto — in the first half of August.

The word, taken apart.
quinzena is built on quinze "fifteen", from Latin quīndecim (Lewis & Short): a stretch of fifteen days.

mensal is a learned word built on Latin mēnsis "a month" (Lewis & Short), with the adjective ending -al.

Lesson 5 of 6.
posterior — subsequent, later; back, rear.

You'll want to know: posterior.
posterior — "subsequent, later; back, rear".
O médico marcou uma consulta posterior. The doctor booked a later appointment.
Also ‘at the back’: a parte posterior do carro.

The word, taken apart.
posterior is a learned borrowing from Latin posterior "coming after, later" (EtymDB), the comparative of posterus, from post "after".
