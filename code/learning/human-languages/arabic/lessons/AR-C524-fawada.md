## You'll want to know: فاوض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C524-TAAWUN524-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فاوض** — *fāwaḍa* — "to hold talks (with); to negotiate".

> فاوض العمال المدير على الأجر.
>
> *fāwaḍa al-ʿummāl al-mudīr ʿalā al-ajr.*
>
> The workers negotiated their pay with the manager.

Present: يفاوض, *yufāwiḍ*.

It takes على for the matter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فاوض** (*fāwaḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-w-ḍ*, "to entrust, to share in" (Lane). Form III fāwaḍa is to talk a matter through with someone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
