## You'll want to know: наследство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C307-RAZVOD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**наследство** (*naslédstvo*) — "an inheritance".

> Он получил в наследство старый дом.
>
> *On poluchíl v naslédstvo stáryy dom.*
>
> He inherited an old house.

Neuter. *Получить в наследство* — to inherit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наследство* is built on *наследовать* "to inherit", from *на-* and *след* "a track" (EtymDB): what comes after, in the footsteps of the dead.
