## You'll want to know: کھمبا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C410-IMARAT410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کھمبا** — *khambā* — "a pole; a pillar".

> بجلی کے کھمبے کے پاس بائیں مڑیے۔
>
> *bijlī ke khambe ke pās bāeṅ muṛiye.*
>
> Turn left by the electricity pole.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھمبا** (*khambā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khambh*, "a pillar, a pole", under Sanskrit *skambha*, "a prop" (CDIAL 13639).
