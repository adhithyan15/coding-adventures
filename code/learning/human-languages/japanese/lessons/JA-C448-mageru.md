## You'll want to know: まげる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C448-STIFF448-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まげる** — *mageru* — "to bend".

> **ひざを ゆっくり まげて ください。** — *hiza o yukkuri magete kudasai* — "Please bend your knees slowly."

Polite **まげます**, *magemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **かんじゃが ゆびを まげました。**, *kanja ga yubi o magemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まげる** (*mageru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: the transitive partner of *magaru*, "to turn; to bend", and its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
