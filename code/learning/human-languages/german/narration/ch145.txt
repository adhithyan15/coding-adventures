Lesson 1 of 5.
benutzen — to use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to go out, then the German for to look.

You'll want to know: benutzen.
benutzen — "to use".

The word, taken apart.
benutzen is be- + the verb "to use, to profit from", Old High German nuzzōn from Proto-Germanic nutōną (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: einkaufen.
einkaufen — "to go shopping".

The word, taken apart.
einkaufen is ein-, "in", + kaufen, "to buy": to buy in supplies. kaufen goes back to Latin caupō, "tradesman". (EtymDB 2.1).

empfehlen from Middle High German enpfelhen, "to entrust to someone" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited, built from ent- and an old verb for "to hand over". To recommend is to entrust.
