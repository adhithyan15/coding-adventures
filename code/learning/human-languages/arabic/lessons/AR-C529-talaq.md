# طلاق (ṭalāq) — a marriage break-up; a divorce

## You'll want to know: طلاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C529-AHL529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طلاق** — *ṭalāq* — "a marriage break-up; a divorce".

> بعد الطلاق سكن صديقي وحده.
>
> *baʿda aṭ-ṭalāq sakana ṣadīqī waḥdahu.*
>
> After the divorce my friend lived alone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طلاق** (*ṭalāq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-l-q*, "to be free, to be let go" (Lane). Ṭalāq is the letting go of a marriage.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
