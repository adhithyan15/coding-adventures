## You'll want to know: صندوق پست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C456-PEYK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صندوق پست** (*sanduq-e post*) — "a postbox (US: a mailbox)".

> نامه را در صندوق پست انداختم.
>
> *Nâme râ dar sanduq-e post andâkhtam.*
>
> I posted the letter in the postbox.

*sanduq* 'box' + *post* 'post'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sanduq-e post* is built from *sanduq* 'chest, box', borrowed from Arabic *ṣundūq* (EtymDB), and *post*, a loan usually traced to French *poste*.
