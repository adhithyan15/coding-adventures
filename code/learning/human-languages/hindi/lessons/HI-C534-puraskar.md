## You'll want to know: पुरस्कार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C534-MAICH534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरस्कार** — *puraskār* — "a prize, an award".

> सबसे अच्छे खिलाड़ी को पुरस्कार मिलेगा।
>
> *Sabse acche khilāṛī ko puraskār milegā.*
>
> The best player will get a prize.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरस्कार** (*puraskār*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *puraskāra*, "placing in front, honouring" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for a prize or an award.
