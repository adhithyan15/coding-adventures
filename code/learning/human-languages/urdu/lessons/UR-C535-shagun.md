## You'll want to know: شگون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C535-SHAGUN535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شگون** — *shagun* — "an omen".

> اگر صبح بارش ہوئی تو یہ اچھا شگون ہو گا۔
>
> *agar subah bārish huī to yih achchhā shagun ho gā.*
>
> If it rains in the morning, that will be a good omen.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شگون** (*shagun*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śakuna*, "a bird; an omen", under which CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *saun*, "a good omen" (CDIAL 12245); the fuller form *shagun* is not among its Hindi rows.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
