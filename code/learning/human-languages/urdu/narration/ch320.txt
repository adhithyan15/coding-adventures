Urdu, chapter 320: Fireworks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آتش بازی (ātish bāzī) — fireworks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to fly a kite, then the Urdu for to be cut.

You'll want to know: آتش بازی.
آتش بازی — ātish bāzī — "fireworks".
رات کو آسمان پر آتش بازی ہوئی۔ rāt ko āsmān par ātish bāzī huī. There were fireworks in the sky at night.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
آتش بازی (ātish bāzī) is usually traced to Persian ātish-bāzī, built from ātish, "fire", and bāzī, "play". It is feminine.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be cut, then the Urdu for fireworks.

You'll want to know: پٹاخا.
پٹاخا — paṭākhā — "a firecracker".
بچوں نے گلی میں پٹاخے چلائے۔ bachchoṅ ne galī meṅ paṭākhe chalāe. The children set off firecrackers in the lane.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
پٹاخا (paṭākhā) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled; it is probably imitative of the crack it makes.

Lesson 3 of 5.
چھڑکنا (chhiṛaknā) — to sprinkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fireworks, then the Urdu for a firecracker.

You'll want to know: چھڑکنا.
چھڑکنا — chhiṛaknā — "to sprinkle".
اس نے پھولوں پر پانی چھڑکا۔ us ne phūloṅ par pānī chhiṛkā. He sprinkled water on the flowers.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نمک چھڑکا, us ne namak chhiṛkā.

The word, taken apart.
چھڑکنا (chhiṛaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin chiṛaknā under chiṭ, splash and scatter, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5035).

Lesson 5 of 5.
جھومنا (jhūmnā) — to sway (with joy or music).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to sprinkle, then the Urdu for to drench.

You'll want to know: جھومنا.
جھومنا — jhūmnā — "to sway (with joy or music)".
گانا سن کر سب جھوم اٹھے۔ gānā sun kar sab jhūm uṭhe. Hearing the song, everyone began to sway.
Past (he …): وہ جھوم اٹھا, voh jhūm uṭhā.

The word, taken apart.
جھومنا (jhūmnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jhūmnā, "to shake", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3726); in Urdu it is swaying with music or joy.
