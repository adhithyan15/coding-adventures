## You'll want to know: నరం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C515-SARIRAM515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నరం** — *naraṁ* — "a nerve; a vein".

> నర్సు నా చేతి నరంలో సూది వేసింది.
>
> *narsu nā cēti naraṁlō sūdi vēsindi.*
>
> The nurse put the needle into a vein in my arm.

Plural: నరాలు, *narālu*.

Plural: నరాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నరం** (*naraṁ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2903 lists Telugu *naramu*, "vein, artery, nerve, tendon", with Tamil *narampu*.
