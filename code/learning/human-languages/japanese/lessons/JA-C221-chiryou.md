## You'll want to know: ちりょう

**ちりょう** — *chiryō* — "treatment; medical care".

> **ちりょうは まだ おわりません。** — *chiryō wa mada owarimasen* — "The treatment is not over yet."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちりょう** (*chiryō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chi*, "to govern, cure" (Middle Chinese *dri*) and *ryō*, "to heal" (*ljewH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
