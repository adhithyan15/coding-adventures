## You'll want to know: かじ

**かじ** — *kaji* — "housework; chores".

> **こどもも かじを てつだいます。** — *kodomo mo kaji o tetsudaimasu* — "The children help with the housework too."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かじ** (*kaji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ka*, "house, home" (Middle Chinese *kae*) and *ji*, "matter, affair" (*dzriH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
