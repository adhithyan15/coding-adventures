## You'll want to know: ਸੁੱਜਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C322-HURT322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁੱਜਣਾ** — *sujjṇā* — "to swell up".

> ਸੱਟ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਮੇਰਾ ਗਿੱਟਾ ਸੁੱਜਿਆ।
>
> *saṭṭ ton bāad merā giṭṭā sujjiā.*
>
> After the injury my ankle swelled up.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਗੋਡਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਸੁੱਜਿਆ, *oh goḍā bahut sujjiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁੱਜਣਾ** (*sujjṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śūyate*, "is swollen"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sujjṇā*, "to swell", under it (CDIAL 12568).
