## You'll want to know: die Schwimmweste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C421-FAEHRE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Schwimmweste** — "the life jacket".

> Unter jedem Sitz liegt eine Schwimmweste.
>
> *There is a life jacket under every seat.*

Plural: *die Schwimmwesten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schwimmweste* is built from *schwimmen* 'to swim, to float', inherited from Proto-Germanic *swimmaną*, and *Weste* 'waistcoat', which came through French and Italian *veste* from Latin *vestis* 'clothing' (EtymDB).
