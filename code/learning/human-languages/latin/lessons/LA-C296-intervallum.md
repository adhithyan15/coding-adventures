## You'll want to know: intervallum, intervallī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C296-INITIUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**intervallum, intervallī** — "a gap, an interval".

> Post breve intervallum nūntius alter vēnit.
>
> *After a short interval a second messenger came.*

Neuter. First the space between two stakes of a rampart, then any gap in space or time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**intervallum** joins *inter*, "between", and *vallum*, "rampart, palisade" (EtymDB): the space between the stakes. English *interval* comes from it through French.
