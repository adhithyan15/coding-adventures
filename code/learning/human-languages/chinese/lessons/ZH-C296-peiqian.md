## You'll want to know: péiqián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C296-CRASH296-05]; assesses=[] -->

*péiqián* — "to pay for damage".

> *tā zhuàng le wǒ de chē, děi péiqián* — He hit my car, so he has to pay for it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*péiqián* is built from *péi* 'to make good a loss' and 钱 'money'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzjen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the Guangyun has no entry for the first, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
