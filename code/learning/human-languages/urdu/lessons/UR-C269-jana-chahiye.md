# جانا چاہیے (jānā chāhiye) — should, and have to

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C104-CAN104-03, UR-LEX-PANI, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I need water". (*mujhe pānī chāhiye*.) **چاہیے** (*chāhiye*) after a noun is "is needed". After a verb it is advice.

## You'll want to know: should
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SHOULD-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-AAP-TUM-TU, UR-LEX-C182-ACT182-02, UR-LEX-C46-FAMILY46-03] -->

> **جانا چاہیے۔**
>
> *jānā chāhiye.* — "One should go."

> **آپ کو جانا چاہیے۔**
>
> *āp ko jānā chāhiye.* — "You should go."

The one who should takes **کو** (*ko*), and the verb stays in its **-نا** form:

> **آپ کو آرام کرنا چاہیے۔**
>
> *āp ko ārām karnā chāhiye.* — "You should rest."

> **مجھے پانی پینا چاہیے۔**
>
> *mujhe pānī pīnā chāhiye.* — "I should drink water."

> **آپ کو نہیں جانا چاہیے۔**
>
> *āp ko nahīṅ jānā chāhiye.* — "You shouldn't go."

## You'll want to know: have to
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SHOULD-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C184-ACT184-01, UR-LEX-C94-TIME94-01, UR-LEX-KAAM] -->

You know **پڑنا** (*paṛnā*) from **بیمار پڑنا** (*bīmār paṛnā*), "to fall ill". After a verb's **-نا** form it is "to have to", and it carries the time:

> **مجھے جانا پڑے گا۔**
>
> *mujhe jānā paṛe gā.* — "I'll have to go."

> **مجھے روز کام کرنا پڑتا ہے۔**
>
> *mujhe roz kām karnā paṛtā hai.* — "I have to work every day."

> **کل مجھے جانا پڑا۔**
>
> *kal mujhe jānā paṛā.* — "Yesterday I had to go."

**چاہیے** is advice; **پڑنا** is no choice.
