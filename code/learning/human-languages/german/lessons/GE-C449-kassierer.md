## You'll want to know: der Kassierer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C449-KASSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kassierer** — "the cashier".

> Der Kassierer gibt mir die Quittung.
>
> *The cashier gives me the receipt.*

Same form in the plural. A woman: *die Kassiererin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kassierer* is built on *kassieren* 'to take money in', from *Kasse* 'till, cash box', which came from Italian *cassa*, from Latin *capsa* 'box' (EtymDB).
