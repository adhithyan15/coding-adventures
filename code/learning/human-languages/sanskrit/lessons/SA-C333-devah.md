## You'll want to know: देवः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C333-SHAPA333-02]; assesses=[] -->

**देवः** — *devaḥ* — "a god".

> देवः ऋषेः तपः पश्यति।
>
> *devaḥ ṛṣeḥ tapaḥ paśyati.*
>
> The god sees the seer's penance.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**देवः** (*devaḥ*) comes from the root √div, "to shine"; MW gives *deva* as heavenly, divine and, as a noun, a deity, a god (MW).
