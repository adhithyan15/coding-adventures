## You'll want to know: штопать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C448-KHIMCHISTKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**штопать** (*shtópat*) — "to darn".

> Бабушка штопает носки.
>
> *Bábushka shtópayet noskí.*
>
> Grandma darns socks.

Imperfective; perfective *заштопать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*штопать* was borrowed from Dutch *stoppen* 'to darn' (EtymDB).
