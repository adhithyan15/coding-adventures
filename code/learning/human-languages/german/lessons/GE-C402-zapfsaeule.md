## You'll want to know: die Zapfsäule
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C402-TANKEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Zapfsäule** — "the petrol pump (at a filling station)".

> Wir halten an der Zapfsäule neben dem kleinen Laden.
>
> *We stop at the pump next to the little shop.*

Plural: *die Zapfsäulen*. *Zapfen* means to draw off a liquid, like beer from a barrel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zapfsäule* is built from *zapfen* 'to draw off a liquid', from *Zapfen* 'plug, bung', and *Säule* 'pillar', an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB lists *Säule*).
