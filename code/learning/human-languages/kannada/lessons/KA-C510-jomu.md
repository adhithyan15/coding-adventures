# ಜೋಮು (jōmu) — numbness; pins and needles

## You'll want to know: ಜೋಮು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C510-NOVU510-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೋಮು** — *jōmu* — "numbness; pins and needles".

> ತುಂಬಾ ಹೊತ್ತು ಕುಳಿತಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ನನ್ನ ಕಾಲಿಗೆ ಜೋಮು ಹಿಡಿಯಿತು.
>
> *tumbā hottu kuḷitiddarinda nanna kālige jōmu hiḍiyitu.*
>
> From sitting too long, my leg went numb.

ಜೋಮು ಹಿಡಿ, to go numb: the numbness "grips" the limb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೋಮು** (*jōmu*) is native Dravidian and imitative: DEDR 2578 lists Kannada *jum*, *jummu* and also *jommu*, *jōmu* for the tingling numbness of a limb kept long in one position.
