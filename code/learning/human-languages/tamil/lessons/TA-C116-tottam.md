## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தோட்டம்** (*tōṭṭam*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3549). The entry lists *tōṭṭam*, "garden, orchard", under the native verb "to dig": a garden is dug ground.
