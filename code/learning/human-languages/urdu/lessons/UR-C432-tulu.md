## You'll want to know: طلوع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C432-TULU432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**طلوع** — *tulū* — "the rising (of the sun)".

> سورج چھ بجے طلوع ہوتا ہے۔
>
> *sūraj chhe baje tulū hotā hai.*
>
> The sun rises at six.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طلوع** (*tulū*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṭulūʿ*, "rising"; with **ہونا** it means the sun comes up.
