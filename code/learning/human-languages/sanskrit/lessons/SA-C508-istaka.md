## You'll want to know: इष्टका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C508-NIRMANA508-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इष्टका** — *iṣṭakā* — "a brick".

> कर्मकरः इष्टकाः आनयति।
>
> *karmakaraḥ iṣṭakāḥ ānayati.*
>
> The worker brings the bricks.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इष्टका** (*iṣṭakā*) is the word MW records for a brick, first a brick of the sacrificial altar; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
