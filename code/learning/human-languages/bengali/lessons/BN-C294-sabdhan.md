## You'll want to know: সাবধান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C294-SOTORKO294-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সাবধান** — *sābdhān* — "careful; watch out".

> সাবধান, সিঁড়িটা ভাঙা!
>
> *sābdhān, sĩṛiṭā bhāngā!*
>
> Careful, the stairs are broken!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাবধান** (*sābdhān*) is Sanskrit *sāvadhāna*, 'attentive, careful' (Monier-Williams): *sa-*, 'with', and *avadhāna*, 'attention'. Said alone it is a warning: সাবধান!
