# … ধরে / থেকে … পর্যন্ত (dhôre / theke … pôrjôntô) — how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C273-CHEYE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-NOW-01, BN-LEX-C52-VERBS52-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "A river is bigger than a pond". (*nôdī pukurer cheye bôṛo*.) Then **ধরা**, "to hold".

## You'll want to know: for a stretch of time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C273-DHORE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C52-VERBS52-01, BN-LEX-C79-TIME79-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-NOW-01, BN-LEX-C51-VERBS51-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-AMIBANGLABOLI-01] -->

> **আমি দুই বছর ধরে বাংলা শিখছি।**
>
> *āmi dui bôchhôr dhôre bānglā shikhchhi.* — "I have been learning Bengali for two years."

**ধরে** is **ধরা** in the *-e* form: "holding" a stretch of time. With the going-on present, it says what started then and is still going on: English says "have been", Bengali says "am". In the past it is a stretch that is over: **আমরা তিন দিন ধরে হাঁটলাম** (*āmrā tin din dhôre hā̃ṭlām*), "we walked for three days".

## You'll want to know: from … until
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C273-THEKE-PORJONTO-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C82-PLACE82-02, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-03, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-01, BN-LEX-C49-VERBS49-05, BN-CONCEPT-C05-THAKA-01] -->

**থেকে**, "from", which you have met, is **থাকা** in the *-e* form; **পর্যন্ত** (*pôrjôntô*) is "until, as far as". They frame a stretch with two ends:

> **দোকান সকাল থেকে রাত পর্যন্ত খোলা থাকে।**
>
> *dokān sôkāl theke rāt pôrjôntô kholā thāke.* — "The shop is open from morning until night."

**পর্যন্ত** measures places too: **বাড়ি থেকে স্টেশন পর্যন্ত** (*bāṛi theke sṭeshôn pôrjôntô*), "from the house as far as the station".
