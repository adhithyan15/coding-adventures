## You'll want to know: شاهکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C527-TALENT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شاهکار** (*shâhkâr*) — "a masterpiece".

> این نقاشی شاهکار اوست.
>
> *in naqqâshi shâhkâr-e ust.*
>
> This painting is his masterpiece.

*shâh* 'king' + *kâr* 'work': the king of works.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shâhkâr* is built on two inherited words: *shâh*, 'king', and *kâr*, 'work', both of which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
