## You'll want to know: なにか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C522-SOME522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**なにか** — *nanika* — "something; anything".

> **もし なにか あったら、でんわして ください。** — *moshi nanika attara, denwa shite kudasai* — "If anything happens, please call me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なにか** (*nanika*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *nani*, "what", and the particle *ka* (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
