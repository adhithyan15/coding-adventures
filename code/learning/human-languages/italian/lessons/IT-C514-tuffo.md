## You'll want to know: il tuffo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C514-ACQUA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il tuffo** — "dive, plunge".

> Ho fatto un tuffo dal molo.
>
> *I dived in from the pier.*

*Fare un tuffo* — to go for a dip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *tuffo* is not settled; it is often taken to imitate the sound of a body hitting water, or traced to a Germanic word for dipping.
