## You'll want to know: दाता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C308-SAMUHA308-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दाता** — *dātā* — "a giver; a donor".

> दाता शिबिराय धनं ददाति।
>
> *dātā śibirāya dhanaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The donor gives money to the camp.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाता** (*dātā*) comes from the root √dā, "to give", with the ending *-tṛ* of the one who does it; MW gives *dātṛ* as a giver, donor (MW).
