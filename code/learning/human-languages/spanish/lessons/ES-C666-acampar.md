## You'll want to know: acampar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C666-CAMPING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**acampar** — "to camp".

> Está prohibido acampar en la playa.
>
> *Camping on the beach is forbidden.*

*La acampada libre* — wild camping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acampar* was borrowed from Italian *accampare* (EtymDB), built on *campo* "field", from Latin *campus*.
