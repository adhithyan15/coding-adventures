## You'll want to know: دفن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C314-JANAZA314-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دفن** — *dafana* — "to bury".

> دفن الصياد الكنز تحت شجرة.
>
> *dafana aṣ-ṣayyād al-kanz taḥta shajara.*
>
> The fisherman buried the treasure under a tree.

Present: يدفن, *yadfin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دفن** (*dafana*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-f-n* (Lane), "to bury, to hide a thing in the ground".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
