## You'll want to know: गुरु
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C287-GURU287-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गुरु** — *guru* — "a guru; a master teacher".

> गुरु संगीत सिखावै है।
>
> *guru saṅgīt sikhāvai hai.*
>
> The guru teaches music.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुरु** (*guru*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *guru*, "heavy", and so "venerable"; Monier-Williams also gives it as a teacher (Monier-Williams).
