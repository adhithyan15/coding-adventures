# ઠેલવું (ṭhelvũ) — to push back (a date)

## You'll want to know: ઠેલવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C530-TAKID530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠેલવું** — *ṭhelvũ* — "to push back (a date)".

> જો વરસાદ પડશે તો અમે પ્રવાસ આવતા અઠવાડિયા પર ઠેલીશું.
>
> *jo varsād paḍśe to ame pravās āvtā aṭhvāḍiyā par ṭhelīśũ.*
>
> If it rains, we will push the trip back to next week.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પ્રવાસ ઠેલ્યો, *teṇe pravās ṭhelyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠેલવું** (*ṭhelvũ*) comes from *\*ṭhēḍḍ*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "to push" (CDIAL 5512); a plan or a date is pushed back.
