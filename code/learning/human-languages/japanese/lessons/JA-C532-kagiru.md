## You'll want to know: かぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C532-LIMIT532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かぎる** — *kagiru* — "to limit; to restrict".

> **もし じかんを いちじかんに かぎったら、みんな いそぐでしょう。** — *moshi jikan o ichijikan ni kagittara, minna isogu deshō* — "If we limit the time to one hour, everyone will hurry."

Polite **かぎります**, *kagirimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **みせが きゃくを じゅうにんに かぎりました。**, *mise ga kyaku o jūnin ni kagirimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かぎる** (*kagiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
