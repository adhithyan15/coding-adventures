## You'll want to know: کھوجنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C349-TAHQIQ349-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کھوجنا** — *khojnā* — "to search out; to investigate".

> وہ اس بیماری کی وجہ کھوج رہے تھے۔
>
> *voh is bīmārī kī vajah khoj rahe the.*
>
> They were searching out the cause of this illness.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے راز کھوجا, *us ne rāz khojā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھوجنا** (*khojnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khojnā*, "to seek, to enquire", under *\*khōjja*, "a footprint", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3929). To follow a trail of footprints.
