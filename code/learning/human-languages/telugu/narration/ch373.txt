Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ఆత్మకథ.
ఆత్మకథ — ātmakatha — "an autobiography".
ఆ నాయకుడు తన ఆత్మకథ రాశాడు. ā nāyakuḍu tana ātmakatha rāśāḍu. That leader wrote his autobiography.
ఆత్మ, self, and కథ: one's own story.

The word, taken apart.
ఆత్మకథ (ātmakatha) is built from Sanskrit ātman, "breath; self" (CDIAL 1135), and kathā, "a story".

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a biography, then the Telugu for a proverb.

You'll want to know: బుర్రకథ.
బుర్రకథ — burrakatha — "a burrakatha ballad".
జాతరలో మేము బుర్రకథ విన్నాము. jātaralō mēmu burrakatha vinnāmu. We heard a burrakatha at the fair.
A Telugu storytelling show: a lead singer and two companions sing and talk through a tale.

The word, taken apart.
బుర్రకథ (burrakatha) is a Telugu compound: native burra, "an empty gourd shell; the head" (DEDR 4332), and katha, "a story", from Sanskrit.

Lesson 5 of 6.
శ్రీకారం చుట్టు (śrīkāraṁ cuṭṭu) — to make a start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a proverb, then the Telugu for a burrakatha ballad.

You'll want to know: శ్రీకారం చుట్టు.
శ్రీకారం చుట్టు — śrīkāraṁ cuṭṭu — "to make a start".
ఆ రచయిత కొత్త నవలకు శ్రీకారం చుట్టాడు. ā racayita kotta navalaku śrīkāraṁ cuṭṭāḍu. The writer made a start on a new novel.
Past (I …): శ్రీకారం చుట్టాను, śrīkāraṁ cuṭṭānu.
శ్రీకారం is the auspicious letter శ్రీ written at the top of a new page; to wind it, చుట్టు, is to begin.

The word, taken apart.
శ్రీకారం చుట్టు (śrīkāraṁ cuṭṭu) is a Telugu idiom: śrīkāraṁ, from Sanskrit śrīkāra, "the word Śrī written at the top of a book or letter" (Monier-Williams), and native cuṭṭu, "to wind, roll" (DEDR 2715).
