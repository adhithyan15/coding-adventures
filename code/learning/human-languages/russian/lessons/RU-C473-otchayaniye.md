## You'll want to know: отчаяние
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C473-LETDOWN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**отчаяние** (*otcháyaniye*) — "despair".

> В отчаянии она позвонила сестре.
>
> *V otcháyanii oná pozvoníla sestré.*
>
> In despair she phoned her sister.

Neuter. The verbs are *отчаиваться / отчаяться* — to despair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отчаяние* is built on *отчаяться* 'to give up hope', from *от-* and *чаять* 'to hope', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *čajati* 'to expect, to wait' (EtymDB).
