Lesson 3 of 8.
ਕਰਦੇ (karde) — more than one, or respectful agreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Read yesterday’s one-man form: ਕਰਦਾ (kardaa).

Grammar Lens: change only the ending.
ਕਰਦੇ — karde — do; work, with plural or respectful agreement.
The stem stays kar-. The visible ending changes from ਦਾ to ਦੇ. In a complete sentence: ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ (karde) ਹਨ — “They work,” or respectful “He works.”
