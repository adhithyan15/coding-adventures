## You'll want to know: 洗
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C99-WASH99-01]; assesses=[] -->

**洗** — *xǐ* — "to wash".

> **我去洗手** *wǒ qù xǐ shǒu* — I'm going to wash my hands.

The new character, in pieces: 洗 — the water piece of 汉 on the left; on the right a six-stroke piece read xiān, the first syllable of xiānsheng. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**洗** (*xǐ*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *sejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to wash and bathe'.

One more word for everyday actions.
