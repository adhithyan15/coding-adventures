# जोखीम (jokhīm) — a risk

## You'll want to know: जोखीम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C540-VIMA540-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जोखीम** — *jokhīm* — "a risk".

> कोणताही व्यवसाय सुरू करताना थोडी जोखीम असतेच.
>
> *koṇtāhī vyavsāy surū kartānā thoḍī jokhīm astec.*
>
> Starting any business always carries some risk.

Feminine.

जोखीम घेणे, to take a risk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जोखीम** (*jokhīm*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *jokhīm*, "risk, venture", under Sanskrit *yōgakṣēma*, "the safe keeping of one's property" (CDIAL 10527).
