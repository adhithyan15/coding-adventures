## You'll want to know: xiàngcè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C344-MEMORY344-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàngcè* — "photo album".

> *wǒ zài xiàngcè lǐ kàndào le xiǎoshíhou de zhàopiàn* — In the album I came across photos of myself as a child.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàngcè* is built from *xiàng* 'a likeness' and *cè* 'a volume', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjangH* and *tsrheak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to look at' and the second 'bound bamboo slips'. The sense 'a photograph' for the first is modern.
