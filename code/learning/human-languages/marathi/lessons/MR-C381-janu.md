## You'll want to know: जणू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C381-JOPARYANT381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जणू** — *jaṇū* — "as if".

> तो असा वागला, जणू काही घडलंच नाही.
>
> *to asā vāglā, jaṇū kāhī ghaḍlañc nāhī.*
>
> He behaved as if nothing had happened.

जणू काही, as though; often after असा: असा … जणू.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जणू** (*jaṇū*) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
