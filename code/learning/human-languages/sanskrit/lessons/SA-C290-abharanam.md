## You'll want to know: आभरणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C290-DASYU290-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आभरणम्** — *ābharaṇam* — "a piece of jewellery".

> दस्युः आभरणं हरति।
>
> *dasyuḥ ābharaṇaṃ harati.*
>
> The robber takes the jewellery.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आभरणम्** (*ābharaṇam*) is built from *ā* and the root √bhṛ, "to bear, carry"; MW gives *ābharaṇa* as decorating and as an ornament such as jewels (MW).
