Spanish, chapter 750: Bajo el mismo techo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el vínculo — the bond, the link.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the great-grandfather, then the Spanish for the orphan.

You'll want to know: el vínculo.
el vínculo — "the bond, the link".
Tengo un vínculo especial con mi abuela. I have a special bond with my grandmother.

The word, taken apart.
vínculo is a learned borrowing from Latin vinculum "bond, link" (EtymDB), built on vincīre "to bind".

Lesson 2 of 5.
mimar — to spoil, to pamper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the orphan, then the Spanish for the bond.

You'll want to know: mimar.
mimar — "to spoil, to pamper".
Los abuelos miman mucho a los nietos. Grandparents really spoil their grandchildren.
Mimado — spoilt. Los mimos — cuddles, pampering.

The word, taken apart.
mimar is built on mimo "a cuddle, pampering", which is usually traced to Latin mīmus "mime, clown", from Greek mîmos: the playful faces made to a child.
