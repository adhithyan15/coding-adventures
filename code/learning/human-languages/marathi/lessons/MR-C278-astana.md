# असताना (astānā) — while it was going on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, MR-JOIN-JEVHA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was reading". (*mī vācat hoto*.) Then the two-part "when … then" frame. (*jevhā … tevhā*.)

## You'll want to know: असताना, 'while'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ASTANA-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-ITHE, MR-LEX-GHAR, MR-LEX-LAHAAN] -->

> **मी वाचत असताना मीरा आली.**
>
> *mī vācat astānā mīrā ālī.* — "While I was reading, Meera came."

**असताना** is **असणे**, "to be", with **-ताना**, "while being". Put it after the **-त** shape, and the scene needs no second clause: **मी वाचत असताना**. It follows a place or a state too: **मी घरी असताना** (*mī gharī astānā*), "while I was at home"; **लहान असताना** (*lahān astānā*), "when (I was) small".

It does not change for person or gender. The event after it does: **मीरा आली**.

## You'll want to know: जेव्हा … तेव्हा with the past
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C278-WHEN-PAST-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C74-HEALTH74-03, MR-LEX-CHAHA, MR-LEX-KARNE] -->

> **मी घरी आलो, तेव्हा आई चहा करत होती.**
>
> *mī gharī ālo, tevhā āī cahā karat hotī.* — "When I came home, Mother was making tea."

**जेव्हा** can stay or go: with the past, the first clause alone sets the time, and **तेव्हा** picks it up.
