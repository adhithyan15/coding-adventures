## You'll want to know: булочная
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C452-BULOCHNAYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**булочная** (*búlochnaya*) — "a bakery (shop)".

> Утром я покупаю хлеб в булочной.
>
> *Útrom ya pokupáyu khleb v búlochnoy.*
>
> In the morning I buy bread at the bakery.

An adjective used as a noun. Traditionally *чн* is pronounced *шн* here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*булочная* is built on *булочка*, *булка* 'a roll' with an adjective ending: the shop for rolls. EtymDB lists *булка* without a deeper source.
