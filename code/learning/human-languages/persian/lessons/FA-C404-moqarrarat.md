## You'll want to know: مقررات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C404-MOQARRARAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مقررات** (*moqarrarât*) — "regulations, rules".

> مقررات این ساختمان خیلی سخت است.
>
> *moqarrarât-e in sâkhtemân kheyli sakht ast.*
>
> The rules of this building are very strict.

A plural noun with the Arabic ending *-ât*; one rule is *qâ'ede* or *qânun*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقررات** is usually traced to Arabic *muqarrarāt* "things decided, laid down", from the same Arabic family as *qarār* "a settled decision".
