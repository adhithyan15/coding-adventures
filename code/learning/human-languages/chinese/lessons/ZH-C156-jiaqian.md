## You'll want to know: jiàqián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C156-JIAQIAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàqián* — "price".

> *jiàqián tài guì le* — The price is too high.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàqián* is built from jià 'price' and *qián* 'money', the word you know. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaeH* and *dzjen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'price; value' and 'coin'. The jià of *fàngjià*, a holiday, sounds the same and is a different word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-01, ZH-LEX-C93-TAIHAOLE-01] -->

*jiàqián* names the price as a fact, the figure on the list; *duōshao qián* asks it aloud.

太 … 了 is still the complaint: *jiàqián tài guì le*.
