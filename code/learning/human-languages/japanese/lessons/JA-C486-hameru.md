## You'll want to know: はめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C486-PARTS486-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はめる** — *hameru* — "to fit in; to put on (a ring)".

> **ゆびわを ゆびに はめます。** — *yubiwa o yubi ni hamemasu* — "I put the ring on my finger."

Polite **はめます**, *hamemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あねが ゆびわを はめました。**, *ane ga yubiwa o hamemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はめる** (*hameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
