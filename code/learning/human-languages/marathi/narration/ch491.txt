Marathi, chapter 491: Sleep and Naps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निद्रानाश (nidrānāś) — insomnia.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to apply heat, then the Marathi for to rub.

You'll want to know: निद्रानाश.
निद्रानाश — nidrānāś — "insomnia".
काळजीमुळे त्याला निद्रानाश झाला. kāḷjīmuḷe tyālā nidrānāś jhālā. Worry gave him insomnia.
Masculine.
निद्रा, sleep, + नाश, ruin.

The word, taken apart.
निद्रानाश (nidrānāś) is a learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, nidrā, "sleep" (CDIAL 7200), and nāśa, "loss, destruction" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
घोरणे (ghorṇe) — to snore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rub, then the Marathi for insomnia.

You'll want to know: घोरणे.
घोरणे — ghorṇe — "to snore".
आजोबा रात्री मोठ्याने घोरतात. ājobā rātrī moṭhyāne ghortāt. Grandfather snores loudly at night.
Past (he …): तो घोरला, to ghorlā.
घोरण्याचा आवाज, the sound of snoring.

The word, taken apart.
घोरणे (ghorṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi ghorṇẽ, "to snore", under Sanskrit ghurati, "cries out frighteningly" (CDIAL 4487).

Lesson 3 of 5.
जांभई देणे (jāmbhaī deṇe) — to yawn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for insomnia, then the Marathi for to snore.

You'll want to know: जांभई देणे.
जांभई देणे — jāmbhaī deṇe — "to yawn".
कंटाळवाण्या भाषणात सगळे जांभई देत होते. kaṇṭāḷvāṇyā bhāṣṇāt sagḷe jāmbhaī det hote. Everyone was yawning through the boring speech.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने जांभई दिली, tyāne jāmbhaī dilī.
जांभई, a yawn; जांभई येणे, to feel a yawn coming, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
जांभई देणे (jāmbhaī deṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on जांभई, "a yawn", which is inherited: CDIAL lists Old Marathi jāmbhaī, "yawning", under jṛmbhāyita (CDIAL 5266); देणे, "to give", is the light verb.

Lesson 4 of 5.
डुलकी घेणे (ḍulkī gheṇe) — to take a nap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to snore, then the Marathi for to yawn.

You'll want to know: डुलकी घेणे.
डुलकी घेणे — ḍulkī gheṇe — "to take a nap".
जेवणानंतर आजोबा थोडी डुलकी घेतात. jevṇānantar ājobā thoḍī ḍulkī ghetāt. After lunch Grandfather takes a short nap.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने डुलकी घेतली, tyāne ḍulkī ghetlī.
डुलकी, a short doze; डुलकी लागणे, to nod off.

The word, taken apart.
डुलकी घेणे (ḍulkī gheṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on डुलकी, "a doze", whose origin is not settled, with घेणे, "to take".

Lesson 5 of 5.
निजणे (nijṇe) — to lie down; to fall asleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to yawn, then the Marathi for to take a nap.

You'll want to know: निजणे.
निजणे — nijṇe — "to lie down; to fall asleep".
बाळ आईच्या कुशीत निजलं. bāḷ āīcyā kuśīt nijlaṁ. The baby fell asleep in its mother's arms.
Past (he …): तो निजला, to nijlā.
A softer, older word beside झोपणे.

The word, taken apart.
निजणे (nijṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit nidrāyati, "falls asleep"; CDIAL lists Marathi nijṇẽ (CDIAL 7201).
