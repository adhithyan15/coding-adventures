## You'll want to know: भुयार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C336-LENI336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भुयार** — *bhuyār* — "an underground passage".

> किल्ल्यातून नदीपर्यंत एक गुप्त भुयार होतं.
>
> *killyātūn nadīparyant ek gupta bhuyār hotaṁ.*
>
> There was a secret passage from the fort to the river.

Neuter.

भुयारी मार्ग, a subway.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भुयार** (*bhuyār*) is the Marathi word for a passage under the ground; its origin is not settled.
