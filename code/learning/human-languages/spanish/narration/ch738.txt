Spanish, chapter 738: En la consulta del médico.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el diagnóstico — the diagnosis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the hip, then the Spanish for the rib.

You'll want to know: el diagnóstico.
el diagnóstico — "the diagnosis".
El médico ya tiene el diagnóstico. The doctor has the diagnosis now.

The word, taken apart.
diagnóstico is a learned word built on Greek diagnōstikós "able to distinguish", from diagignṓskein "to tell apart", built on gignṓskein "to know".
