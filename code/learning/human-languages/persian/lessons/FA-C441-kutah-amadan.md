## You'll want to know: کوتاه آمدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C441-DARKHAST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کوتاه آمدن** (*kutâh âmadan*) — "to back down, to give in".

> مسافر عصبانی بود ولی آخر کوتاه آمد.
>
> *Mosâfer 'asabâni bud vali âkhar kutâh âmad.*
>
> The passenger was angry but in the end he backed down.

Literally 'to come short'. Present: *kutâh miâyad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kutâh âmadan* is built from native *kutâh* 'short', usually traced to Middle Persian *kōtāh*, and *âmadan* 'to come', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root *gʷem-*.
