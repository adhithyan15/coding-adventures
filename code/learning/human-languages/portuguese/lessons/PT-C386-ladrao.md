## You'll want to know: o ladrão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C386-ACUSACAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o ladrão** — "the thief".

> O ladrão fugiu pela janela.
>
> *The thief escaped through the window.*

Plural *os ladrões*; feminine *a ladra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ladrão* is inherited from Latin *latrō, latrōnis* "a hired servant, a mercenary", later "a robber" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).
