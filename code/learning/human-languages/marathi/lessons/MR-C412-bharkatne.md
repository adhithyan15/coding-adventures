## You'll want to know: भरकटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C412-DISHA412-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भरकटणे** — *bharkaṭṇe* — "to stray; to drift".

> धुक्यात आम्ही वाटेवरून भरकटलो.
>
> *dhukyāt āmhī vāṭevarūn bharkaṭlo.*
>
> In the fog we strayed from the path.

Past (he …): तो भरकटला, *to bharkaṭlā*.

For people who lose their way, and for a mind that wanders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भरकटणे** (*bharkaṭṇe*) is a Marathi verb for drifting off the right path; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for getting around.
