## You'll want to know: вязать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C500-KNITTING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**вязать** (*vyazát*) — "to knit; to tie".

> Бабушка вяжет мне тёплый шарф.
>
> *Bábushka vyázhet mne tyóplyy sharf.*
>
> Grandma is knitting me a warm scarf.

Imperfective; present вяжу, вяжешь (з becomes ж). Perfective связать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вязать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *vęzati* 'to tie, to bind' (EtymDB).
