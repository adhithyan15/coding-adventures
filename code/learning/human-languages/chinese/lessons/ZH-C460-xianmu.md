## You'll want to know: xiànmù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C460-LOW460-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànmù* — "to envy".

> *nǐ qù guo hěn duō guójiā wǒ hěn xiànmù nǐ* — You've been to so many countries; I really envy you.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànmù* is built from *xiàn* 'to covet' and *mù* 'to long for', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zjenH* and *muH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first under three readings and glosses this one 'to covet'; it glosses the second as thinking of someone with longing.
