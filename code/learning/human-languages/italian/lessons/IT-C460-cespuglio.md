## You'll want to know: il cespuglio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C460-BOSCO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il cespuglio** — "the bush, the shrub".

> Il gatto si nasconde dietro un cespuglio.
>
> *The cat hides behind a bush.*

*Un cespuglio di rose* — a rose bush.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cespuglio* is traced in EtymDB to Latin *caespuculum*, a little form of *caespes* 'turf, sod'; it is inherited.
