## You'll want to know: कौतुकम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C326-DIPAVALI326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कौतुकम्** — *kautukam* — "festive excitement; eagerness".

> उत्सवे बालस्य कौतुकं वर्धते।
>
> *utsave bālasya kautukaṃ vardhate.*
>
> At the festival the child's excitement grows.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कौतुकम्** (*kautukam*) is built on *kutūhala*, "curiosity" (MW abbreviates it), and MW gives *kautuka* as curiosity, eagerness, and festivity, a show (MW).
