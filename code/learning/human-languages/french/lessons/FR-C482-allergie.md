## You'll want to know: l'allergie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C482-ALLERGIE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'allergie** — "allergy".

> Mon fils a une allergie aux chats.
>
> *My son has an allergy to cats.*

Feminine. *Être allergique à* — to be allergic to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'allergie* was borrowed from German *Allergie*, a modern learned word built from Greek *állos* "other" and *érgon* "work, action".
