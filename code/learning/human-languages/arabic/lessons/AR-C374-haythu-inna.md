# حيث إن (ḥaythu inna) — given that

## You'll want to know: حيث إن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C374-RABT374-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حيث إن** — *ḥaythu inna* — "given that".

> حيث إن الطريق طويل، سنخرج مبكرا.
>
> *ḥaythu inna aṭ-ṭarīq ṭawīl, sa-nakhruju mubakkiran.*
>
> Given that the road is long, we will leave early.

Formal; in speech بما أن or لأن.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حيث إن** (*ḥaythu inna*) joins two native Arabic particles: *ḥaythu*, "where", and *inna*, "indeed, truly". The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.
