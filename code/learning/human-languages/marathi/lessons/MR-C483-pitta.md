## You'll want to know: पित्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C483-PACAN483-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पित्त** — *pitta* — "acidity; bile".

> उपाशी राहिल्यामुळे त्याला पित्त झालं.
>
> *upāśī rāhilyāmuḷe tyālā pitta jhālaṁ.*
>
> He got acidity from going without food.

Neuter.

पित्त होणे, to get acidity, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पित्त** (*pitta*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pitta*, "bile" (CDIAL 8181).
