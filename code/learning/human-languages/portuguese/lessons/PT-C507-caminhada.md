## You'll want to know: a caminhada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C507-RODAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a caminhada** — "(long) walk, hike".

> Fizemos uma caminhada de dez quilómetros.
>
> *We went on a ten-kilometre walk.*

*Fazer uma caminhada* — to go for a walk or a hike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caminhada* is built on *caminho* "path", inherited from Late Latin *camminus* "a road", which is usually traced to a Celtic word.
