Gujarati, chapter 56: Sing, Dance, Laugh, Listen, Learn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગાવું (gāvũ) — to sing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to bring, then the Gujarati for to drink.

You'll want to know: ગાવું.
ગાવું (gāvũ) — "to sing".

The word, taken apart.
ગાવું (gāvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from gāyati, "sings" (CDIAL 4135).

What you've built.
One more word for sing, dance, laugh, listen, learn.
