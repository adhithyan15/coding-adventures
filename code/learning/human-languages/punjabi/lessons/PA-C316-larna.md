## You'll want to know: ਲੜਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C316-QUARREL316-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੜਨਾ** — *laṛnā* — "to fight".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਗਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੋ ਮੁੰਡੇ ਲੜੇ।
>
> *kall galī vich do muṇḍe laṛe.*
>
> Yesterday two boys fought in the lane.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਦੋਸਤ ਨਾਲ ਲੜਿਆ, *maiṁ āpṇe dost nāl laṛiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੜਨਾ** (*laṛnā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*laḍ*, "fight"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *laṛṇā*, "to fight", under it (CDIAL 10920).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
