# Name the action or describe a regular meeting

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-MILNA-188, PA-FORM-MILDE-188] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say the naming form, then the regular-group form.

## Grammar Lens: two jobs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-MILNA-188, PA-FORM-MILDE-188, PA-PRON-ASI-01, PA-GRAMMAR-MAIN-HAAN-03] -->

- **ਅਸੀਂ ਮਿਲਦੇ ਹਾਂ** — says “we meet” as a regular pattern.
