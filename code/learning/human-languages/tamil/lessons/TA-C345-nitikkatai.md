## You'll want to know: நீதிக்கதை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C345-KATHAICOLLI345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**நீதிக்கதை** — *nītikkatai* — "a moral tale; a fable".

> ஒவ்வொரு நீதிக்கதையும் ஒரு பாடம் சொல்கிறது.
>
> *ovvoru nītikkataiyum oru pāṭam colkiṟatu.*
>
> Every moral tale teaches a lesson.

A story for children that ends with a lesson.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நீதிக்கதை** (*nītikkatai*) is built from நீதி, "right conduct", borrowed from Sanskrit *nīti* (CDIAL 7546), with கதை, "story", from Sanskrit *kathā* (CDIAL 2704).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
