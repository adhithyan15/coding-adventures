## You'll want to know: la locanda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C429-DORMIRE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la locanda** — "the inn".

> La locanda è sopra un vecchio ristorante.
>
> *The inn is above an old restaurant.*

A small, old-fashioned place to eat and sleep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*locanda* comes from Latin *locanda* '(a house) to be let' (EtymDB), a form of *locāre* 'to place, to let', built on *locus* 'place' (Lewis & Short).
