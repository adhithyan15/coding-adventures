## You'll want to know: मृत्युः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C491-JARA491-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मृत्युः** — *mṛtyuḥ* — "death".

> पितामहस्य मृत्युः ह्यः अभवत्।
>
> *pitāmahasya mṛtyuḥ hyaḥ abhavat.*
>
> Grandfather died yesterday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मृत्युः** (*mṛtyuḥ*) comes from the root √mṛ, "to die", the root of **म्रियते**; MW gives "death, dying" (MW).
