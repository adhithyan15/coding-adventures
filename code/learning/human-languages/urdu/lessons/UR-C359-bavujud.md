# باوجود (bāvujūd) — despite (کے باوجود)

## You'll want to know: باوجود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C359-BAVUJUD359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**باوجود** — *bāvujūd* — "despite (کے باوجود)".

> بارش کے باوجود وہ دفتر گیا۔
>
> *bārish ke bāvujūd voh daftar gayā.*
>
> He went to the office despite the rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**باوجود** (*bāvujūd*) is usually traced to Persian *bāvujūd*, "with the existence of": *bā*, "with", and *vujūd*, "existence", a word Persian took from Arabic.
