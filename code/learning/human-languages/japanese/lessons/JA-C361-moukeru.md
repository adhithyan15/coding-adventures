## You'll want to know: もうける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C361-MOUKERU361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**もうける** — *mōkeru* — "to make a profit".

> **よく うれたら、みせは もうけるでしょう。** — *yoku uretara, mise wa mōkeru deshō* — "If it sells well, the shop will make a profit."

Polite **もうけます**, *mōkemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **みせが もうけました。**, *mise ga mōkemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もうける** (*mōkeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
