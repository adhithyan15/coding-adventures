## You'll want to know: پیادہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C534-BISAT534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پیادہ** — *piyādā* — "a pawn (in chess)".

> اگر پیادہ آخر تک پہنچا تو وزیر بن جائے گا۔
>
> *agar piyādā ākhir tak pahuṅchā to vazīr ban jāe gā.*
>
> If the pawn reaches the end, it will become a queen.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیادہ** (*piyādā*) is usually traced to Persian *piyāda*, "on foot; a foot soldier".
