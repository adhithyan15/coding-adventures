Uncover **અત્યારે** once. The hard part is in the middle: **ત** carries the **્** stroke that deletes its own vowel and glues it to **ય**, which is exactly the join you first met in *namaste*. Around it stand **અ**, the **ા** stroke, **ર**, and the **ે** sign. Name the six pieces aloud in order, then
read the whole word once.
