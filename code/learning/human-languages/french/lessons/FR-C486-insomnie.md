## You'll want to know: l'insomnie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C486-NUITS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'insomnie** — "insomnia, sleeplessness".

> Quand je suis stressé, je souffre d'insomnie.
>
> *When I am stressed, I suffer from insomnia.*

Feminine. Often plural: *avoir des insomnies*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'insomnie* is a learned borrowing from Latin *insomnia* "sleeplessness" (EtymDB), built on *somnus* "sleep" (Lewis & Short).
