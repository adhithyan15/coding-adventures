## Added — A2 vocabulary: chapters 225-364; Punjabi attains A2

- Five tranches of 28 chapters with five `word` lessons each (700 words), and a
  review every few chapters (25). Every chapter opens by ear.

  | tranche | chapters | nodes |
  |---|---|---|
  | a | 225-252 | SAY-WHAT-I-DO, NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS, NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS |
  | b | 253-280 | READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS, ASK-LOCATION, SAY-WHAT-I-WANT |
  | c | 281-308 | TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE, DESCRIBE-QUALITIES, NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS |
  | d | 309-336 | TALK-ABOUT-PAST, CHECK-WELLBEING, EXCHANGE-NAMES |
  | e | 337-364 | NEGATE-AND-ASK, SAY-WHAT-I-LIKE, SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO |

- Each tranche was drafted, then reviewed independently. The reviews:
  - checked gender and agreement;
  - replaced words that duplicated taught ones, for example ਪੰਧ (beside ਵਾਟ)
    became ਸਾਥੀ, ਸੰਭਾਲਣਾ (a doublet of ਸਾਂਭਣਾ) became ਉਸਾਰਨਾ, and ਗੁਆਚਣਾ became
    ਭਾਲਣਾ;
  - made examples natural;
  - checked every etymology against its CDIAL row.
- Origins and slugs:
  - A row CDIAL marks as borrowed from Hindi is worded as a word taken from
    Hindi, and a form found only in Lahnda is named as Lahnda.
  - A sense CDIAL does not give is said so.
  - Distinct etyma keep distinct slugs, for example `indo-aryan-ghunta` for
    ਘੁੰਡੀ, which no longer borrows a Kannada slug.
- The past forms keep the core's ergative frame: ਮੈਂ / ਅਸੀਂ / ਤੁਸੀਂ without ਨੇ,
  or a name or ਉਸ with ਨੇ. Requests use the -ੋ form on consonant stems only,
  so no lesson needs the untaught letter ਓ.
- The level gate: attained A1 -> A2, with 1,331 headwords and 297 verbs at or
  below A2. THIN is 0, forward references stay at 2, and letter anchoring is
  unchanged.
- The book preamble gains the HL-C88 quote fix: quote blocks are set ragged
  right, as its tcolorboxes already are.
