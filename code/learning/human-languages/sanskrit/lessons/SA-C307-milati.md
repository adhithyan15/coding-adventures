## You'll want to know: मिलति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C307-VIYOGA307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मिलति** — *milati* — "meets; finds again".

> माता शिबिरे बालेन सह मिलति।
>
> *mātā śibire bālena saha milati.*
>
> The mother meets her child in the camp.

Past (she …): सा बालेन सह मिलितवती, *sā bālena saha militavatī*.

Future: मिलिष्यति, *miliṣyati*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिलति** (*milati*) comes from the root √mil, "to meet, come together" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
