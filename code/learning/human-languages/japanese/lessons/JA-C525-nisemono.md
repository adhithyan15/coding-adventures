## You'll want to know: にせもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C525-REAL525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**にせもの** — *nisemono* — "a fake; a forgery".

> **もし にせものが みつかったら、けいさつに しらせて ください。** — *moshi nisemono ga mitsukattara, keisatsu ni shirasete kudasai* — "If you find a fake, please inform the police."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にせもの** (*nisemono*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *nise*, "false" and *mono*, "thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
