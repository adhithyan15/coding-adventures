## You'll want to know: jízhěn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C482-TREAT482-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jízhěn* — "emergency department; urgent treatment".

> *wǒ xiǎng dāng jízhěn yīshēng* — I want to be an emergency doctor.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jízhěn* is built from *jí* 'urgent' and *zhěn* 'to examine', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kip* and *tsyinX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'urgent' and the second as feeling the pulse.
