Lesson 1 of 5.
કઢી (kaḍhī) — kadhi.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for peanuts, then the Gujarati for dhokla.

You'll want to know: કઢી.
કઢી (kaḍhī) — "kadhi".

The word, taken apart.
કઢી (kaḍhī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kvathita, "boiled" (CDIAL 3637).

Lesson 4 of 5.
હલવો (halvo) — halwa.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for fritters, then the Gujarati for chutney.

You'll want to know: હલવો.
હલવો (halvo) — "halwa".

The word, taken apart.
હલવો (halvo) is a loan from Arabic ḥalwā, "a sweetmeat", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḥ-l-w (Lane).
