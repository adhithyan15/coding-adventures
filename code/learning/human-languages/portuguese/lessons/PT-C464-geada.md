## You'll want to know: a geada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C464-GEADA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a geada** — "the frost".

> De manhã, a relva estava branca de geada.
>
> *In the morning, the grass was white with frost.*

*Cair geada* — to frost over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*geada* is inherited from Latin *gelāta* 'frozen' (EtymDB), from *gelāre* 'to freeze', on *gelū* 'frost'.
