## You'll want to know: とおる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C194-WAY194-04]; assesses=[] -->

**とおる** — *tōru* — "to pass through; to go along, to pass by".

> **まいにち この みちを とおります。** — *mainichi kono michi o tōrimasu* — "I go along this road every day."

Polite **とおります**, *tōrimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とおる** (*tōru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
