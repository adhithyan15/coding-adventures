## You'll want to know: द्वेषः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C454-IRSYA454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**द्वेषः** — *dveṣaḥ* — "hatred".

> द्वेषः मित्रम् अपि शत्रुं करोति।
>
> *dveṣaḥ mitram api śatruṃ karoti.*
>
> Hatred turns even a friend into an enemy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**द्वेषः** (*dveṣaḥ*) comes from the root √dviṣ, "to hate, to dislike"; MW gives "hatred, dislike, repugnance, enmity" (MW).
