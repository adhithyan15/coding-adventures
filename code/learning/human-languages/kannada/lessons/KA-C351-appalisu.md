# ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸು (appaḷisu) — to crash against; to strike

## You'll want to know: ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C351-KARYA351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸು** — *appaḷisu* — "to crash against; to strike".

> ದೊಡ್ಡ ಅಲೆಗಳು ದಡಕ್ಕೆ ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸಿದವು.
>
> *doḍḍa alegaḷu daḍakke appaḷisidavu.*
>
> Huge waves crashed against the shore.

Past (it …): ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸಿತು, *appaḷisitu*.

What is hit takes -ಗೆ. Storms, waves and news ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸು** (*appaḷisu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 157 lists Kannada *appaḷisu*, "to strike against, to slap".
