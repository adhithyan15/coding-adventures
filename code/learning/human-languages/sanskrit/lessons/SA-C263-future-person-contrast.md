# Who will go?

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Listen for the ending before you translate.

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: “he will go” — *saḥ gamiṣyati*]
- [YOU SAY: “I will go” — *ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: teacher points away — *gamiṣyati*]
- [YOU SAY: teacher points to you as speaker — *gamiṣyāmi*]
