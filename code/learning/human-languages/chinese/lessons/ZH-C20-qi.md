# 七 — seven

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-NUM-QI, ZH-LEX-NUM-LIU] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write 七, then say six.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-NUM-QI]; assesses=[ZH-TONE-LEXICAL, ZH-LEX-NUM-YI] -->

**七** *qī* means **seven**.

**qī** is **first tone**, the high level one — the same shape as *yī*, one. Say
them in a row and hold the pitch flat across both: **yī, qī**.

The **q** is the piece to work on, because English has no letter for it. Put the
tongue where it goes for **y** in *yes*, then release the air the way you do for
**ch**. It is not the *k* of *queen* and it is not an English *ch*; it is the
tongue of one and the release of the other.
