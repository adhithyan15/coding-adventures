Lesson 1 of 6.
प्रजा (prajā) — subjects; the people of a king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a princess, then the Marathi for a minister.

You'll want to know: प्रजा.
प्रजा — prajā — "subjects; the people of a king".
तो राजा प्रजेची नेहमी काळजी घ्यायचा. to rājā prajecī nehmī kāḷjī ghyāycā. That king always took care of his subjects.
Feminine.
Singular even for a whole people: प्रजा (prajā) सुखी होती.

The word, taken apart.
प्रजा (prajā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prajā, 'offspring; people, subjects' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
वसवणे (vasavṇe) — to found a settlement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a banner, then the Marathi for a chieftain.

You'll want to know: वसवणे.
वसवणे — vasavṇe — "to found a settlement".
नदीकाठी राजाने एक नवं शहर वसवलं. nadīkāṭhī rājāne ek navaṁ śahar vasavlaṁ. The king founded a new city on the riverbank.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वसवलं, tyāne vasavlaṁ.
Transitive: शहर वसवणे (vasavṇe), to found a town; the place itself वसतो, settles.

The word, taken apart.
वसवणे (vasavṇe) is a Marathi causative, 'to make settle', built from the inherited stem vas- with -av-: CDIAL lists Marathi vasṇẽ, 'settles', under Sanskrit vasati, 'stays, dwells' (CDIAL 11435).
