## You'll want to know: kǎoshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C79-SCHOOL79-05]; assesses=[] -->

*kǎoshì* — "to take an exam".

> *míngtiān kǎoshì jīntiān wǒ xuéxí* — The exam is tomorrow, so today I'm studying.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kǎoshì* is built from kǎo and shì, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khawX* and *syiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as 'to check' and the second as 'to use'; the sense 'exam' is not given there.
