## You'll want to know: आदर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C50-COURTESY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आदर** (*ādar*) — "respect".

Sanskrit *ādara*, 'regard, attentiveness', from *ā-dṛ-*, 'to heed'. Hindi carries this word inside its own machinery: the आप form is called *ādar-sūcak*, the respect-marking form.

So this is not only a word for a feeling somebody has. It is the name of a choice the language makes you make in nearly every sentence you speak, whether you have thought about it or not.
