Arabic, chapter 104: South, East, West, Place, Address.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جنوب (janūb) — south.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a corner, then the Arabic for north.

You'll want to know: جنوب.
جنوب — janūb — "south".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: janūb]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: janūb, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say janūb]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a corner, then the Arabic for north, then say janūb again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does جنوب (janūb) mean? ("South".) Say it once more.
