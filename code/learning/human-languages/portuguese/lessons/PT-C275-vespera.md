## You'll want to know: a véspera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C275-ENTARDECER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a véspera** — "the eve, the day before".

> Na véspera do exame, não dormi nada.
>
> *The day before the exam I did not sleep at all.*

*Na véspera da partida* — the day before leaving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*véspera* is inherited from Latin *vespera* "evening" (EtymDB): the evening before a feast became the day before it.
