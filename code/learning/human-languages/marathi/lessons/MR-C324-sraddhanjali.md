## You'll want to know: श्रद्धांजली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C324-SHRADDHA324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रद्धांजली** — *śraddhāñjalī* — "a tribute".

> सभेत सगळ्यांनी त्यांना श्रद्धांजली वाहिली.
>
> *sabhet sagaḷyānnī tyānnā śraddhāñjalī vāhilī.*
>
> At the meeting everyone paid tribute to him.

Feminine.

श्रद्धांजली वाहणे, to pay tribute.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रद्धांजली** (*śraddhāñjalī*) is built from Sanskrit *śraddhā*, 'faith, reverence', and *añjali*, 'hands held together in offering' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
