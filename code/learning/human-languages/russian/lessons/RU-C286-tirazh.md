## You'll want to know: тираж
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C286-OSVESHCHAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**тираж** (*tirázh*) — "a print run, a circulation; a lottery draw".

> У этой газеты большой тираж.
>
> *U étoy gazéty bolshóy tirázh.*
>
> This newspaper has a large circulation.

Masculine; the stress moves to the ending: *тиража*, *тиражи*. *Выиграть в тираже* — to win in a lottery draw.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тираж* was borrowed from French *tirage* "a printing; a drawing of lots" (EtymDB). The French noun is usually traced to *tirer* "to draw, to pull", as copies are drawn off a press and numbers out of a drum; EtymDB lists no parent for *tirage* itself.
