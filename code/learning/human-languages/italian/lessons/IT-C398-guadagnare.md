## You'll want to know: guadagnare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C398-GUADAGNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**guadagnare** — "to earn; to gain".

> Se lavori il sabato, guadagni di più.
>
> *If you work on Saturdays, you earn more.*

*Guadagnarsi da vivere* — to earn a living. *Guadagnare tempo* — to gain time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guadagnare* comes from a Germanic verb: EtymDB traces it through Vulgar Latin *wadanio* "to pursue, to win; to graze" to Proto-Germanic *waiþanjaną* (EtymDB). Italian turned the Germanic *w-* into *gu-*, as in *guerra*.
