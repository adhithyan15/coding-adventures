## You'll want to know: すいどうすい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C491-STOVE491-04]; assesses=[] -->

**すいどうすい** — *suidōsui* — "tap water".

> **この まちの すいどうすいは おいしいです。** — *kono machi no suidōsui wa oishii desu* — "The tap water in this town tastes good."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいどうすい** (*suidōsui*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sui*, "water" (Middle Chinese *sywijX*), *dō*, "road" (*dawX*) and *sui*, "water", again: the water of the *suidō*, the water main (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
