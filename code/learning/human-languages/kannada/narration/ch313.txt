Kannada, chapter 313: Asking the Way.
3 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು? (hēge hōgabēku?) — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "where?" (elli) and "how?" (hēge). Then "I must go early tomorrow." (nānu nāḷe bēga hōgabēku.)

You'll want to know: ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು? and ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?
ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ, ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣಕ್ಕೆ ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು? kṣamisi, bas nildāṇakke hēge hōgabēku? — "Excuse me, how do I get to the bus station?"
Word for word it is "how must (one) go to the bus station", with the -ಬೇಕು of "must". The place takes the "to" ending: ನಿಲ್ದಾಣಕ್ಕೆ, ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಗೆ. ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ, "sorry", opens the question politely, the way English says "excuse me".
ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? āspatre ellide? — "Where is the hospital?"
ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ is ಎಲ್ಲಿ, "where", run together with ಇದೆ, "is".

You'll want to know: how far.
ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ? illinda eṣṭu dūra? — "How far is it from here?"
ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ is ಇಲ್ಲಿ, "here", with -ಇಂದ, "from". ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ is "how much distance". The answers:
ಹತ್ತಿರ ಇದೆ. hattira ide. — "It's near."
ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ದೂರ ಇದೆ. svalpa dūra ide. — "It's a little far."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Excuse me, how do I get to the bus station?" — kṣamisi, bas nildāṇakke hēge hōgabēku?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "How far is it from here?" — illinda eṣṭu dūra?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It's near" — hattira ide]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"How do I get to the station?" (ನಿಲ್ದಾಣಕ್ಕೆ ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು? (hēge hōgabēku?)) "Where is it?" (ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?) "From here"? (ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ.) "How far?" (ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ?)


Lesson 2 of 3.
ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ (nēravāgi hōgi) — giving the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "left", "right" and "straight". (eḍa, bala, nēra.) Then "How far is it from here?" (illinda eṣṭu dūra?)

You'll want to know: straight on, left, right.
ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ. ಮೊದಲ ತಿರುವಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗಿ. nēravāgi hōgi. modala tiruvinalli eḍakke hōgi. — "Go straight on. At the first turn, go left."
ಹೋಗಿ here is a polite "go": the same -ಇ form as "having gone", said to a listener, as in ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ, "please phone". ನೇರವಾಗಿ is ನೇರ with -ವಾಗಿ, the piece you met in ಸ್ವಚ್ಛವಾಗಿದೆ: "straight on". ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ is ಎಡ with -ಕ್ಕೆ, "to": "to the left"; and ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ (balakke), "to the right".
ಮೊದಲ (modala, "first") is the ಮೊದಲು of a story, standing in front of a noun. ತಿರುವಿನಲ್ಲಿ (tiruvinalli, "at the turn") is ತಿರುವು with -ಇನ- and -ಅಲ್ಲಿ, "at".

You'll want to know: what it is next to.
ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ ಇದೆ, ಅಂಗಡಿಯ ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ. āspatre balakke ide, aṅgaḍiya pakkadalli. — "The hospital is on the right, next to the shop."
ಅಂಗಡಿಯ (aṅgaḍiya, "of the shop"); ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ (pakkadalli, "at the side, next to"), from ಪಕ್ಕ. Most directions are these few pieces in a row: straight on, a turn, left or right, and one thing it is next to.
ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ಇದೆ, ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ. bas nildāṇa eḍakke ide, hōṭel pakkadalli. — "The bus station is on the left, next to the hotel."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Go straight on. At the first turn, go left." — nēravāgi hōgi. modala tiruvinalli eḍakke hōgi.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Go to the right" — balakke hōgi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "The hospital is on the right, next to the shop" — āspatre balakke ide, aṅgaḍiya pakkadalli]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the way from your door to the nearest shop, in three steps]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Straight on"? (ನೇರವಾಗಿ.) "To the left"? (ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ.) "To the right"? (ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ.) "At the first turn"? (ಮೊದಲ ತಿರುವಿನಲ್ಲಿ.) "Next to the shop"? (ಅಂಗಡಿಯ ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — asking the way.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Warm-up.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Read ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ aloud, and name its small letter. (svaccha; ಛ.) Then "You should see a doctor." (nīvu vaidyarannu nōḍabēku.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Excuse me, where is the station? How far is it from here?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It's near. Go straight on, then go right at the first turn.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: The hotel is on the left, next to the hospital.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: You must not go that way, because there is no path.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask the way to a shop, then give the answer yourself]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"How do I get to …?" (… ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು? (hēge hōgabēku?)) "How far?" (ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ?) "Go straight on"? (ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ (nēravāgi hōgi).) "Next to"? (ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ.)
