# પ — the consonant pa

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LA-01] -->

Write *la* once from memory. Check its rounded body; the next form begins with a
high hook instead.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-PA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **પ** visible. It is *pa*. Begin at the high left hook, curl over it,
descend the left stem, and sweep around the broad lower body into the right
shoulder. Lift once; descend the tall right spine into its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **પ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice high left hook, broad
lower body, separate right spine; one pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **પ** once. Compare only the high hook and lower body; repair one
landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W05-pa-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-PA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write pa from memory.","answer":"પ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"પ — the high hook and separate right spine are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at પ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *pa* once. Uncover **પ**, compare, and stop after one
repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-PA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name the first landmark and the pen-lift count.
