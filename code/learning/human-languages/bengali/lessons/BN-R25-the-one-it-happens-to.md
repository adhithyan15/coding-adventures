and one question decides which of them a noun may take. Run all of it.

- [YOU SAY: the question that chooses between them — is it **জীব** or **জিনিস**?]

share the slot at once? (**One**.) What does Bengali say instead of *I like
tea*? (**Tea feels good to me**.) And what is the difference between the two
words spelled **কে**? (**Where they sit** — one is stuck on, one stands alone.)
