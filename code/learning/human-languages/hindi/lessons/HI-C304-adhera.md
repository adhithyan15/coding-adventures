## You'll want to know: अँधेरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C304-TOOFAN304-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अँधेरा** — *ãdherā* — "darkness".

> दोपहर में ही अँधेरा हो गया।
>
> *dopahar meṁ hī ãdherā ho gayā.*
>
> It grew dark in the middle of the afternoon.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अँधेरा** (*ãdherā*) goes back to Sanskrit *andhakāra*, "darkness" (CDIAL 386), built on *andha*, blind. **अँधेरा होना** is for it to grow dark; its opposite is **उजाला**.
