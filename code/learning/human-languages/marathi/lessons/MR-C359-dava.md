## You'll want to know: दावा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C359-KHATLA359-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दावा** — *dāvā* — "a claim; a lawsuit".

> भावाने घरावर दावा केला.
>
> *bhāvāne gharāvar dāvā kelā.*
>
> The brother laid claim to the house.

Masculine. Plural: दावे, *dāve*.

X वर दावा करणे, to lay claim to X; दावा दाखल करणे, to file a suit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दावा** (*dāvā*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic *daʿwā*, "a claim, a lawsuit".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
