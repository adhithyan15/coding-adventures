## You'll want to know: पोशाक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C435-REZGARI435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पोशाक** — *pośāk* — "a dress, an outfit".

> मेले में बच्चे नई पोशाक पहनकर आए।
>
> *mele meṁ bacce naī pośāk pahankar āe.*
>
> The children came to the fair in new clothes.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोशाक** (*pośāk*) is usually traced to Persian *pošāk*, "clothing, dress"; it is feminine.
