## You'll want to know: kòngwèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C407-PULLIN407-03]; assesses=[] -->

*kòngwèi* — "empty seat".

> *wǒ zài chē lǐ zhǎo bu dào kòngwèi* — I can't find an empty seat on the bus.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kòngwèi* is built from *kòng* 'vacant' and *wèi* 'a place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khuwngH* and *hwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'empty, lacking' and the second as a place in a row.
