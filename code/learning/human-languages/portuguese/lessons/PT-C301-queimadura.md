## You'll want to know: a queimadura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C301-INCENDIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a queimadura** — "the burn".

> Ele tem uma queimadura na mão.
>
> *He has a burn on his hand.*

Built on *queimar*. *Queimadura solar* — sunburn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*queimadura* is built on *queimar* "to burn", which is usually traced to Latin *cremāre* "to burn" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-dura*.
