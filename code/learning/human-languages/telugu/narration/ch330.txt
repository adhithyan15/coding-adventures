Telugu, chapter 330: The Reporter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
విలేకరి (vilēkari) — a reporter; a correspondent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to make off with, then the Telugu for to sense.

You'll want to know: విలేకరి.
విలేకరి — vilēkari — "a reporter; a correspondent".
విలేకరి సాక్షితో మాట్లాడాడు. vilēkari sākṣitō māṭlāḍāḍu. The reporter spoke to the witness.
Plural: విలేకరులు, vilēkarulu.
A paper's reporter in a town is its విలేకరి (vilēkari) there.

The word, taken apart.
విలేకరి (vilēkari) is the Telugu word for a newspaper reporter; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
పత్రిక (patrika) — a periodical; a magazine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to sense, then the Telugu for a reporter.

You'll want to know: పత్రిక.
పత్రిక — patrika — "a periodical; a magazine".
ఆ పత్రిక ప్రతి వారం వస్తుంది. ā patrika prati vāraṁ vastundi. That magazine comes out every week.
Plural: పత్రికలు, patrikalu.
It is the second half of వార్తాపత్రిక, a newspaper: a news sheet.

The word, taken apart.
పత్రిక (patrika) is borrowed from Sanskrit pattrikā, "a leaf for writing upon; a letter, a document" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
కథనం (kathanaṁ) — a news story; an account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a periodical, then the Telugu for a headline.

You'll want to know: కథనం.
కథనం — kathanaṁ — "a news story; an account".
విలేకరి ఆ సంఘటన మీద కథనం రాశాడు. vilēkari ā saṅghaṭana mīda kathanaṁ rāśāḍu. The reporter wrote a story on that incident.
A cousin of కథ, a story: the news calls each report a కథనం (kathanaṁ).

The word, taken apart.
కథనం (kathanaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit kathana, "narration, telling" (CDIAL 2702), from the root kath, "to tell", which also gives కథ, a story.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ప్రచురించు (pracuriñcu) — to publish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a headline, then the Telugu for a news story.

You'll want to know: ప్రచురించు.
ప్రచురించు — pracuriñcu — "to publish".
పత్రిక ఆ కథనం ప్రచురించింది. patrika ā kathanaṁ pracuriñcindi. The magazine published that story.
Past (I …): ప్రచురించాను, pracuriñcānu.
A -ఇంచు verb: Telugu makes verbs of Sanskrit words with -ఇంచు, as in ముద్రించు, to print.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రచురించు (pracuriñcu) is built on Sanskrit pracura, "abundant, plentiful" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu: to publish is to spread a text wide.
