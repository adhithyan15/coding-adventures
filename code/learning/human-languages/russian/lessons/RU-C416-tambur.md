## You'll want to know: тамбур
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C416-PROVODNIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**тамбур** (*támbur*) — "a vestibule (between train carriages)".

> Пассажир разговаривал по телефону в тамбуре.
>
> *Passazhír razgovárival po telefónu v támbure.*
>
> The passenger was talking on the phone in the vestibule.

Masculine. Also the small space inside the door of a building.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тамбур* is usually traced to French *tambour* 'a drum', which EtymDB traces to Arabic *ṭunbūr*; the sense of a small closed space came later. EtymDB does not list the Russian word.
