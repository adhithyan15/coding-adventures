## You'll want to know: જોડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C393-ENGINE393-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જોડવું** — *joḍvũ* — "to couple (a carriage or an engine); to attach".

> સ્ટેશન પર તેમણે ટ્રેનમાં નવું એન્જિન જોડ્યું.
>
> *sṭeśan par temṇe ṭrenmā̃ navũ enjin joḍyũ.*
>
> At the station they coupled a new engine to the train.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે એન્જિન જોડ્યું, *teṇe enjin joḍyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જોડવું** (*joḍvũ*) goes back to *\*yuṭati*, "is joined", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to join, to fasten" (CDIAL 10496). જોડી, a pair, and જોડાવું, to join, are its relatives.
