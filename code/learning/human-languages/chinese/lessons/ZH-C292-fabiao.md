## You'll want to know: fābiǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C292-RADIO292-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fābiǎo* — "to publish an article; to make public".

> *tā fābiǎo le yì piān xiǎoshuō* — He published a short story.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fābiǎo* is built from 发 'to send out' and *biǎo* 'the outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjot* and *pjewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to make clear' and 'a letter to the throne': to send out where all can see.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
