# مالش کرنا (mālish karnā) — to massage

## You'll want to know: مالش کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C481-BETTER481-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مالش کرنا** — *mālish karnā* — "to massage".

> امی نے دادی کی کمر کی مالش کی۔
>
> *ammī ne dādī kī kamar kī mālish kī.*
>
> Mum massaged my grandmother's back.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سر کی مالش کی, *us ne sar kī mālish kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مالش کرنا** (*mālish karnā*) is built from **مالش**, usually traced to Persian *mālish*, "rubbing", and **کرنا**, "to do".
