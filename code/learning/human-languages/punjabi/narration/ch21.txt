Punjabi, chapter 21: The Fourth Form Line: Work.
8 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
Select one known work value — select between two separately practised work values.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — attach one cue to each value until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Read the closed practice bank once:
A — ਖੇਤੀ B — ਨੌਕਰੀ.

Script — attach one cue to each value.
Point to A, then ਖੇਤੀ. Point to B, then ਨੌਕਰੀ. Cover the bank and say which value B requests. Selection is separate from spelling.

Guided Practice.
Reveal, point, cover, and select once more. Do not write the form line yet.

Wrap-up recall.
These cues belong only to this invented two-item bank.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
In this closed practice bank, which Gurmukhi value is paired with B?


Lesson 2 of 8.
Fill one supported work field — fill one work line with a visible two-value bank.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Keep this invented bank visible:
A — ਖੇਤੀ B — ਨੌਕਰੀ.

Writing — model visible.
Complete ਕੰਮ: __________ (work field after a short delay) for A. Read the label, select the A value, and copy it. This is supported entry, not independent writing evidence.

Guided Practice.
Compare selection, spelling, spacing, field-value agreement, and placement in separate passes.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one supported line.
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
With the two-value bank visible, complete the work field for A.


Lesson 3 of 8.
Check only the work-value spelling — check one work value one script piece at a time.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read ਖੇਤੀ and ਨੌਕਰੀ once. Touch each script piece without writing a form line.

Writing — one spelling pass.
Cover both models and write ਨੌਕਰੀ once. Reveal it and compare ਨੌ, then ਕ, then ਰੀ. Ignore the field label and spacing today.

Guided Practice.
If a piece differs, rewrite only that piece before copying the whole value once more.

Wrap-up recall.
Selection and spacing are not scored in this pass.
[question — say your answer, then pause 22 seconds]
Write the job value and check it in the three chunks ਨੌ, ਕ, ਰੀ.


Lesson 4 of 8.
Give the work value its own space — separate the work label and value clearly.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read ਕੰਮ: ਖੇਤੀ (work field spacing). Point once to the label and once to the value.

Writing — one spacing pass.
Copy ਕੰਮ:, leave a clear space, then copy ਖੇਤੀ. Judge only the boundary after the colon.

Guided Practice.
Cover the model and repeat once. If the boundary is unclear, repair only the space.

Wrap-up recall.
Selection, spelling, and agreement are not scored in this spacing pass.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
Copy the work field with a clear space after the colon.


Lesson 5 of 8.
Keep the field and value together — keep a work value on the work line.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read ਕੰਮ, then ਖੇਤੀ and ਨੌਕਰੀ. Both values answer the work label in this closed bank.

Writing — one agreement pass.
Choose the line whose value agrees with the work label: ਕੰਮ: ਨੌਕਰੀ (work field repair) (work field and value agreement) or ਕੰਮ: ਸ਼ਹਿਰ. Copy only the agreeing line. This checks field-value meaning, not grammatical gender.

Guided Practice.
Point to the label, say “work,” then point to the chosen value and say its meaning. Ignore handwriting neatness.

Wrap-up recall.
This pass separates field-value meaning from spelling and spacing.
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Which complete line keeps a known work value on the work field?


Lesson 6 of 8.
Hide, wait, then fill the work field — fill one work line after hiding the known bank.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read A — ਖੇਤੀ, B — ਨੌਕਰੀ once. Hide the bank and count slowly to ten.

Writing — short delayed entry.
With the bank hidden, complete ਕੰਮ: __________ (work field after a short delay) for B. Finish the attempt before revealing the bank.

Guided Practice.
Reveal and compare selection, spelling, spacing, agreement, then placement. Do not repair yet.

Wrap-up recall.
One delayed line is enough.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
After hiding the bank for ten seconds, complete the work field for B.


Lesson 7 of 8.
Repair one work-field difference — repair one dimension of a completed work field.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Compare ਕੰਮ:ਨੌਕਰੀ with ਕੰਮ: ਨੌਕਰੀ (work field repair) (work field and value agreement). The selection, letters, and field-value meaning match; one boundary differs.

Writing — one repair pass.
Rewrite the first line with a clear space after the colon. Leave every letter unchanged.

Guided Practice.
Name the repaired dimension: selection, spelling, spacing, agreement, or placement. Stop after one repair.

Wrap-up recall.
The next lesson asks for a fresh line rather than a repair.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
Repair only the spacing in ਕੰਮ:ਨੌਕਰੀ.


Lesson 8 of 8.
One no-model work field — choose and write one known work value from a nonverbal cue with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Close the previous lesson. There is no value bank, support-language label, or romanized answer below.

Writing — independent controlled choice.
Complete the requested field:
A — ਕੰਮ: __________ (work field after a short delay).
Choose the known value attached to A and write it from memory. Do not reopen an earlier lesson until the attempt is complete.

Guided Practice — separate repair passes.
After writing, compare selection, spelling, spacing, agreement, and placement. Repair only the first differing dimension.

Wrap-up recall.
This closes only the work-field ladder. The remaining form fields are separate backlog work.
[question — say your answer, then pause 35 seconds]
With no bank, romanization, or copyable answer, complete the work field for A.
