## You'll want to know: تیمارداری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C480-CARE480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تیمارداری** — *tīmārdārī* — "nursing the sick".

> بہن نے رات بھر ماں کی تیمارداری کی۔
>
> *bahan ne rāt bhar māṅ kī tīmārdārī kī.*
>
> My sister nursed our mother all night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تیمارداری** (*tīmārdārī*) is usually traced to Persian *tīmārdārī*, "tending the sick", built on *tīmār*, "care".
