## You'll want to know: ಓಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C457-VIHARA457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಓಣಿ** — *ōṇi* — "a lane; an alley".

> ಹಳೆಯ ಊರಿನ ಓಣಿಗಳು ತುಂಬಾ ಕಿರಿದು.
>
> *haḷeya ūrina ōṇigaḷu tumbā kiridu.*
>
> The lanes of the old town are very narrow.

Plural: ಓಣಿಗಳು, *ōṇigaḷu*.

A narrow way between houses; ಬೀದಿ is a proper street.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಓಣಿ** (*ōṇi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1046 lists Kannada *ōṇi*, "a lane, an alley, a street".
