## You'll want to know: ನಾಯಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C539-SPARDHE539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಯಕ** — *nāyaka* — "a captain; a leader".

> ಅವನು ನಮ್ಮ ಕ್ರಿಕೆಟ್ ತಂಡದ ನಾಯಕ.
>
> *avanu namma krikeṭ taṇḍada nāyaka.*
>
> He is the captain of our cricket team.

Also the hero of a film; ನಾಯಕಿ, a heroine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಯಕ** (*nāyaka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nāyaka*, "a guide, leader, chief" (Monier-Williams), from *nī*, "to lead".
