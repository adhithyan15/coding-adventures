## You'll want to know: याउलट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C348-SAMBANDH348-05]; assesses=[] -->

**याउलट** — *yāulaṭ* — "on the contrary; by contrast".

> भाऊ शांत आहे, याउलट बहीण खूप बोलकी आहे.
>
> *bhāū śānt āhe, yāulaṭ bahīṇ khūp bolkī āhe.*
>
> The brother is quiet; by contrast, the sister is very talkative.

या + उलट, this + the reverse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**याउलट** (*yāulaṭ*) is built from या, "this" (oblique), and उलट, "the reverse": the reverse of this. उलट goes with the verb उलटणे; CDIAL lists Marathi *ulaṭṇẽ*, "to be upset, to turn over", under *\*ullaṭati*, "turns over" (CDIAL 2368).
