## You'll want to know: tamponare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C413-INGORGO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**tamponare** — "to rear-end, to bump into (the car in front)".

> Un furgone ci ha tamponato al semaforo.
>
> *A van ran into the back of us at the lights.*

Also to stop a leak or a wound: *tamponare una ferita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tamponare* is built on *tampone* 'plug, pad', which was borrowed from French *tampon*.
