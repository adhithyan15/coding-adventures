## You'll want to know: جمہوریت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C342-VOT342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جمہوریت** — *jamhūriyat* — "democracy".

> جمہوریت میں ہر شہری کی رائے ضروری ہے۔
>
> *jamhūriyat meṅ har shahrī kī rāe zarūrī hai.*
>
> In a democracy every citizen's opinion matters.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جمہوریت** (*jamhūriyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jumhūriyya*, "a republic", built on *jumhūr*, "the people at large".
