## You'll want to know: веко
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C482-HEAD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**веко** (*véko*) — "an eyelid".

> У меня с утра дёргается веко.
>
> *U menyá s utrá dyórgayetsya véko.*
>
> My eyelid has been twitching since the morning.

Neuter; plural веки.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*веко* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *věko* 'eyelid' (EtymDB).
