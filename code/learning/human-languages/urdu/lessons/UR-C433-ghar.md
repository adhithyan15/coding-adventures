## You'll want to know: غار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C433-TILA433-03]; assesses=[] -->

**غار** — *ghār* — "a cave".

> پہاڑ میں ایک پرانا غار ہے۔
>
> *pahāṛ meṅ ek purānā ghār hai.*
>
> There is an old cave in the mountain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غار** (*ghār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghār*, "a cave, a grotto".
