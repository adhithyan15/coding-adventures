## You'll want to know: تطوع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C519-WAZIFA519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تطوع** — *taṭawwaʿa* — "to volunteer".

> تطوعت أختي في المستشفى في الصيف.
>
> *taṭawwaʿat ukhtī fī al-mustashfā fī aṣ-ṣayf.*
>
> My sister volunteered at the hospital in the summer.

Present: يتطوع, *yataṭawwaʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تطوع** (*taṭawwaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-w-ʿ*, "to comply, to be willing" (Lane). Lane gives Form V taṭawwaʿa as to do a good thing that is not required.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
