## You'll want to know: यदि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C379-YADI379-01]; assesses=[] -->

**यदि** — *yadi* — "if (formal)".

> यदि आप सहमत हैं तो कृपया यहाँ हस्ताक्षर कीजिए।
>
> *yadi āp sahmat haiṁ to kṛpayā yahā̃ hastākṣar kījiye.*
>
> If you agree, please sign here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यदि** (*yadi*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *yadi*, "if" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
