## You'll want to know: はやまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C512-CHANGE512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**はやまる** — *hayamaru* — "to be brought forward".

> **もし しあいが はやまったら、しらせて ください。** — *moshi shiai ga hayamattara, shirasete kudasai* — "If the match is brought forward, please let me know."

Polite **はやまります**, *hayamarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かいぎが はやまりました。**, *kaigi ga hayamarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はやまる** (*hayamaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
