# ശ (śa) — the letter that opens ശരി

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-147, ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-148] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ഠ and ഫ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ശരി** (*śari*), "okay".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ശ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-SHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ശ** — *śa*, the sound *sh*.

It opens **ശരി** (*śari*), "okay", one of the first words in this book.

## Writing: ശ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-SHA-01] -->

Put your pen on ശ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
