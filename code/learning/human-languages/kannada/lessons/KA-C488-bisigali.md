# ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿ (bisigāḷi) — a heatwave; a hot wind

## You'll want to know: ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C488-BISIGALI488-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿ** — *bisigāḷi* — "a heatwave; a hot wind".

> ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿಯ ಕಾರಣ ಮಧ್ಯಾಹ್ನ ಹೊರಗೆ ಹೋಗಬಾರದು.
>
> *bisigāḷiya kāraṇa madhyāhna horage hōgabāradu.*
>
> Because of the heatwave nobody should go out at midday.

ಬಿಸಿ, hot, plus ಗಾಳಿ, wind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಿಸಿಗಾಳಿ** (*bisigāḷi*) is a native Kannada compound of *bisi*, "heat, hot" (DEDR 5517), and ಗಾಳಿ, "wind" (DEDR 1499).
