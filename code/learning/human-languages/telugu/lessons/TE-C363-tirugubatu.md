# తిరుగుబాటు (tirugubāṭu) — a rebellion; a revolt

## You'll want to know: తిరుగుబాటు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C363-TIRUGUBATU363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**తిరుగుబాటు** — *tirugubāṭu* — "a rebellion; a revolt".

> రైతులు రాజు మీద తిరుగుబాటు చేశారు.
>
> *raitulu rāju mīda tirugubāṭu cēśāru.*
>
> The farmers rebelled against the king.

తిరుగుబాటు చేయు, to rebel; from తిరుగు, to turn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తిరుగుబాటు** (*tirugubāṭu*) is native Dravidian, built on *tirugu*, "to turn, turn back" (DEDR 3246): a turning against.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
