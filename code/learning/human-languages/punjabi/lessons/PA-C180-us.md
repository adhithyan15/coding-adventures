# ਉਸ — pointing to that place

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PLACE-LOK-PEOPLE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *people* once. (**ਲੋਕ**.)

## Grammar Lens: one pointing word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-PLACE-US-THAT-01]; assesses=[PA-PLACE-LOK-PEOPLE-01, PA-LEX-C138-THINGS138-02, PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01] -->

> **ਉਸ ਬਾਗ਼ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੋਕ ਹਨ।** — *us bāġ vic lok han* — people are in that garden.

Here **ਉਸ** points to *that* garden. Treat the whole line as one place model; it does not teach every form of *that*.
