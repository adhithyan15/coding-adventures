## You'll want to know: clāmitō, clāmitāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C462-NUNDINOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**clāmitō, clāmitāre** — "to keep shouting, to cry out again and again".

> Mercātōrēs in forō clāmitant.
>
> *The merchants in the forum keep shouting.*

*clāmitō, clāmitāre, clāmitāvī, clāmitātum*, a frequentative of *clāmō*: the *-itō* means 'again and again'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*clāmitō* is built on *clāmō* 'to shout' with the frequentative ending *-itō* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
