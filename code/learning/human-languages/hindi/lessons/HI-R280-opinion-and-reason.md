# Review — what I think, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C280-LAGNA-01, HI-LEX-C280-AISA-01, HI-SCRIPT-C280-AI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'm hungry" and "I think so". (*mujhe bhūkh lagī hai*, *mujhe aisā lagtā hai*.) Then write **ऐ** from memory: the strokes of **ए**, the arc, the head-line.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C280-LAGTA-KI-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-RAY-MEIN-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-VAJAH-SE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-REASON-IS-01, HI-LEX-C280-LAGNA-01, HI-LEX-C280-AISA-01, HI-SCRIPT-C280-AI-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I think it will rain tomorrow.]
- [YOU SAY: What do you think? In my opinion this book is good.]
- [YOU SAY: Because of the rain the train came late.]
- [YOU SAY: I'm thirsty. I like this tea.]
- [YOU WRITE: **ऐसा**, then **मुझे ऐसा लगता है।**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C280-LAGTA-KI-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-RAY-MEIN-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-VAJAH-SE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-REASON-IS-01, HI-LEX-C280-LAGNA-01, HI-LEX-C280-AISA-01, HI-SCRIPT-C280-AI-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I think that …"? (**मुझे लगता है कि …**) "In my opinion"? (**मेरी राय में**.) "Because of"? (**की वजह से**, or **के कारण**.) "The reason is that …"? (**वजह यह है कि …**) How is **ऐ** unlike **ए**? (One arc above the head-line.)
