## You'll want to know: ползать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C495-EXPECTING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ползать** (*pólzat*) — "to crawl".

> Наш сын уже ползает по всей квартире.
>
> *Nash syn uzhé pólzayet po vsey kvartíre.*
>
> Our son already crawls all over the flat.

Imperfective, in many directions; the one-way verb is *ползти*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ползать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *pьlzati* (EtymDB).
