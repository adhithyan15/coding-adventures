## You'll want to know: जंतू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C484-ROG484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जंतू** — *jantū* — "a germ".

> साबणाने हात धुतले की जंतू जातात.
>
> *sābṇāne hāt dhutle kī jantū jātāt.*
>
> Washing your hands with soap gets rid of germs.

Masculine.

Masculine; plural जंतू. जंतुनाशक, a disinfectant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जंतू** (*jantū*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *jantu*, "creature; any animal of the lowest order" (CDIAL 5110).
