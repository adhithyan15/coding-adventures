## You'll want to know: o biberão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C494-BEBE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o biberão** — "baby's bottle".

> A avó aqueceu o leite do biberão.
>
> *Grandma warmed the milk in the baby's bottle.*

In Brazil: *a mamadeira*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biberão* was borrowed from French *biberon* (EtymDB), built on Latin *bibere* "to drink".
