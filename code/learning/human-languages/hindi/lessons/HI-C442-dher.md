## You'll want to know: ढेर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ढेर** — *ḍher* — "a heap, a pile".

> गली के कोने पर कूड़े का ढेर है।
>
> *galī ke kone par kūṛe kā ḍher hai.*
>
> There is a heap of rubbish at the corner of the lane.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढेर** (*ḍher*) goes back to *\*ḍhēra*, "a lump, a heap", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5599), which lists Hindi *ḍher*, "heap".
