## You'll want to know: ടയർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C470-INDHANAM470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ടയർ** — *ṭayar* — "a tyre".

> കാറിന്റെ ഒരു ടയർ പഴയതാണ്.
>
> *kāṟinṟe oru ṭayar paḻayatāṇŭ.*
>
> One of the car's tyres is old.

Plural: ടയറുകൾ, *ṭayaṟukaḷ*.

Plural: ടയറുകൾ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ടയർ** (*ṭayar*) was borrowed from English *tyre*, the rubber ring around a wheel.
