## You'll want to know: el espejo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C774-DESVAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el espejo** — "the mirror".

> En el pasillo había un espejo enorme.
>
> *In the hall there was a huge mirror.*

*Mirarse en el espejo* — to see yourself in the mirror.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*espejo* is inherited from Latin *speculum* 'a mirror' (Lewis & Short), built on *specere* 'to look' (EtymDB).
