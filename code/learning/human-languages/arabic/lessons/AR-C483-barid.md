## You'll want to know: بارد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C483-BARD483-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بارد** — *bārid* — "chilly; cold".

> كان الماء باردا جدا.
>
> *kāna al-māʾ bāridan jiddan.*
>
> The water was very cold.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بارد** (*bārid*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-r-d*, "to be cold" (Lane). It is the active participle.
