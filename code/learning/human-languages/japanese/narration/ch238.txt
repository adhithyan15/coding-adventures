Japanese, chapter 238: Comics, Novels and TV.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
まんが (manga) — comics; a comic book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to gather, then the Japanese for a doll.

You'll want to know: まんが.
まんが — manga — "comics; a comic book".
きのう おとうとは まんがを よみました。 — kinō otōto wa manga o yomimashita — "Yesterday my younger brother read comics."

The word, taken apart.
まんが (manga) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are man, "unrestrained" (Middle Chinese manH) and ga, "picture" (hweaH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ばんぐみ (bangumi) — a programme; a TV or radio programme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a novel, then the Japanese for reading.

You'll want to know: ばんぐみ.
ばんぐみ — bangumi — "a programme; a TV or radio programme".
きのう おもしろい ばんぐみを みました。 — kinō omoshiroi bangumi o mimashita — "Yesterday I watched an interesting programme."

The word, taken apart.
ばんぐみ (bangumi) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese ban, "turn, number" (Middle Chinese phjon), borrowed from Chinese, joined to native kumi, "set, group", from kumu, "to put together" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
つまらない (tsumaranai) — boring; dull.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for reading, then the Japanese for a programme.

You'll want to know: つまらない.
つまらない — tsumaranai — "boring; dull".
きのう つまらない えいがを みました。 — kinō tsumaranai eiga o mimashita — "Yesterday I watched a boring film."

The word, taken apart.
つまらない (tsumaranai) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from tsumaru, "to be packed", with the negative ending -nai, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
