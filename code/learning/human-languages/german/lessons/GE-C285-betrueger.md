## You'll want to know: der Betrüger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C285-BETRUG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Betrüger** — "the con man, the fraudster".

> Der Betrüger rief am Telefon an und wollte Geld.
>
> *The con man phoned and wanted money.*

Plural: *die Betrüger*. Feminine *die Betrügerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Betrüger* is built on *betrügen* "to cheat", from Proto-Germanic *bidreuganą* "to deceive" (EtymDB), with the ending *-er* for a person.
