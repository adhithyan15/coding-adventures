## You'll want to know: 这么
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C280-ZHEME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**这么** — *zhème* — "so; this much; like this".

> **今天这么冷** *jīntiān zhème lěng* — It's so cold today!

> **今天天气这么好** *jīntiān tiānqì zhème hǎo* — The weather's so good today!

Both characters are ones you write.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**这么** (*zhème*) is built from 这 'this' and the ending 么 of 什么. No dictionary we use records an older form of 这 in this sense, or of the ending, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C153-NAME-01, ZH-LEX-C51-FEEL51-03, ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-01] -->

那么 points at something over there, as in 他没有你那么高. 这么 points at what is here and now: 今天这么冷.

Both go before an adjective: 这么冷, 这么好.
