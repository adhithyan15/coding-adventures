## You'll want to know: قطيع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C478-QATI478-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قطيع** — *qaṭīʿ* — "a herd; a flock".

> رأينا قطيعا من الغزلان.
>
> *raʾaynā qaṭīʿan min al-ghizlān.*
>
> We saw a herd of gazelles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قطيع** (*qaṭīʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ṭ-ʿ*, "to cut" (Lane). A herd is a portion cut off from the rest.
