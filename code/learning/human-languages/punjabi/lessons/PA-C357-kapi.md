## You'll want to know: ਕਾਪੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C357-SCHOOL357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਪੀ** — *kāpī* — "a notebook; an exercise book".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਬਸਤੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਤਿੰਨ ਕਾਪੀਆਂ ਹਨ।
>
> *mere baste vich tinn kāpīāṁ han.*
>
> There are three exercise books in my school bag.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਪੀ** (*kāpī*) is borrowed from English *copy*.
