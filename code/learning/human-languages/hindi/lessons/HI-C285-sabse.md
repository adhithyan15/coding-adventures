## You'll want to know: सबसे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C285-SABSE-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C245-THG245-02, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-03, HI-LEX-C82-BODY-01, HI-LEX-FAMILY, HI-LEX-C41-ADJ-03, HI-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-01] -->

**सबसे** — *sabse* — "most, of all".

> **मेरे परिवार में मीरा सबसे लंबी है।**
>
> *mere parivār meṁ Mīrā sabse lambī hai.* — "In my family Mira is the tallest."

It is **सब**, "all", with the **से** of **बच्चे से बड़ा**: "taller than all". The adjective after it agrees as always: **सबसे लंबी** for Mira, **सबसे लंबा** for Arun. The group you choose from takes **में**.

- **यह सबसे अच्छा है।** (*yah sabse acchā hai.*) — This is the best.
- **सबसे पहले** (*sabse pahle*) — first of all

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सबसे** (*sabse*) is two words written as one: **सब**, which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *sarva*, "all, whole" (CDIAL 13276), and **से**.
