## You'll want to know: কঠোর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C460-NISHTHUR460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কঠোর** — *kôṭhor* — "stern; harsh".

> নতুন বিচারক কঠোর শাস্তি দিলেন।
>
> *nôtun bichārôk kôṭhor shāsti dilen.*
>
> The new judge handed down a harsh punishment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কঠোর** (*kôṭhor*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kaṭhora*, "hard, solid, stiff" (Monier-Williams); it shows the new letter ঠ.
