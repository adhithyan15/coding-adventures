## You'll want to know: مرکز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C450-MARKAZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مرکز** (*markaz*) — "a centre".

> هتل ما در مرکز شهر است.
>
> *Hotel-e mâ dar markaz-e shahr ast.*
>
> Our hotel is in the town centre.

Arabic plural *marâkez*. *Markaz-e kharid* — a shopping centre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*markaz* was borrowed from Arabic *markaz* 'centre', which EtymDB lists.
