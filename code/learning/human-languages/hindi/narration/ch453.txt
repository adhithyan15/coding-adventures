Hindi, chapter 453: Joy and Delight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आनंद (ānand) — joy; bliss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to feel, then the Hindi for to well up.

You'll want to know: आनंद.
आनंद — ānand — "joy; bliss".
बच्चों के साथ खेलकर दादा को बहुत आनंद आया। baccoṁ ke sāth khelkar dādā ko bahut ānand āyā. Grandfather had a wonderful time playing with the children.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
आनंद (ānand) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit ānanda, "happiness, joy" (Monier-Williams). ānand ānā, "joy comes", is how Hindi says someone enjoyed a thing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: प्रसन्न.
प्रसन्न — prasann — "pleased; cheerful".
अरुण का काम देखकर उसके पिता प्रसन्न हुए। Aruṇ kā kām dekhkar uske pitā prasann hue. Arun's father was pleased when he saw his work.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसन्न (prasann) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit prasanna, "clear, bright" and also "soothed, pleased" (Monier-Williams). It is a more formal word than khuś.
