# ಮುಂಗಾರು (muṅgāru) — the monsoon; the early rains

## You'll want to know: ಮುಂಗಾರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C485-MUNGARU485-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಂಗಾರು** — *muṅgāru* — "the monsoon; the early rains".

> ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಮುಂಗಾರು ಒಂದು ವಾರ ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದಿತು.
>
> *ī varṣa muṅgāru ondu vāra taḍavāgi banditu.*
>
> This year the monsoon came a week late.

The June rains that end the summer heat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಂಗಾರು** (*muṅgāru*) is a native Kannada compound of *mun*, "front, first" (DEDR 5020), and *kār*, "blackness, the rainy season" (DEDR 1278).
