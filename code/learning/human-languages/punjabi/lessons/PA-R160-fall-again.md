# An old word for falling

This is one familiar action, not a new form to memorise. First try to
remember it from the English meaning. The model comes after the pause.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C148-ACT148-02] -->

Hear **to fall**. [PAUSE 4s] Say the Punjabi word before the model:
**ਡਿੱਗਣਾ** (*ḍiggṇā*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C148-ACT148-02] -->

Look at **ਡਿੱਗਣਾ** and say its familiar meaning. [PAUSE 4s]
The meaning is **to fall**. Now hear **to fall** again, look away, and
say **ਡਿੱਗਣਾ** before the model. No sentence is needed yet.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C148-ACT148-02] -->

Hear **to fall** once more. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਡਿੱਗਣਾ**.
If it did not come back, listen to the model and retry once. This
untimed word return is not part of a scored A1 mock.
