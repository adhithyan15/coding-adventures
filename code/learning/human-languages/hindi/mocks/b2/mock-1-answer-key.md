## Writing

Task 1 must identify the omitted access issue, explain the affected group, cite
a meeting observation, request a correction, and propose a more inclusive next
consultation. Task 2 must defend a clear position with at least two service
examples, explain the attraction of a single measure, consider the burden or
confusion risk of multiple measures, and propose proportionate measurement that
protects privacy. Apply the rubric rather than matching fixed wording.
Scorer-only reference openings:

- **आदरणीय समन्वयक, प्रकाशित सारांश में सुगम उतरने की दूरी संबंधी हमारी चर्चा…**
- **एक सुविधाजनक संख्या निर्णय को सरल बना सकती है, पर सेवा के उद्देश्य को…**

Do not show these stems to the learner.

## Speaking

The presentation must sustain four minutes and develop purpose, stakeholders,
evidence, an unintended effect, an evidence limitation, and a recommendation.
Collaborative problem-solving must compare at least three options against the
given constraints and reach an explicit main use, secondary use, and review
plan. The defended discussion requires supported positions, responses,
clarification, and a credible condition for changing one's mind. Score the
learner's own development, interaction, negotiation, Hindi control,
organisation, register, and intelligibility; no fixed position or wording is
required.
