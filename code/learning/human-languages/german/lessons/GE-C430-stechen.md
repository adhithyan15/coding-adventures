## You'll want to know: stechen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C430-MUECKE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**stechen** — "to bite, to sting (of insects); to prick".

> Eine Mücke hat mich am Arm gestochen.
>
> *A mosquito bit me on the arm.*

Strong verb: *sticht*, *stach*, *hat gestochen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stechen* is inherited through Old High German *stehhan* from Proto-Germanic *stikaną* 'to pierce' (EtymDB). English *stick* is a cousin.
