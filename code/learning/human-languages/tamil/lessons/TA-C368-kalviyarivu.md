# கல்வியறிவு (kalviyaṟivu) — literacy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C367-NANAM367-04, TA-LEX-C367-NANAM367-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for a genius, then the Tamil for to teach formally.

## You'll want to know: கல்வியறிவு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C368-KALVIYARIVU368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கல்வியறிவு** — *kalviyaṟivu* — "literacy".

> இந்த மாவட்டத்தில் கல்வியறிவு அதிகரித்திருக்கிறது.
>
> *inta māvaṭṭattil kalviyaṟivu atikarittirukkiṟatu.*
>
> Literacy has risen in this district.

கல்வி, education + அறிவு, knowledge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கல்வியறிவு** (*kalviyaṟivu*) is native: *kalvi*, "learning, education", from *kal*, "to learn" (DEDR 1297), and *aṟivu*, "knowledge" (DEDR 314).
