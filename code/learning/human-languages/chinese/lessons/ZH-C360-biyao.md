## You'll want to know: 必要
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C360-NEED360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**必要** — *bìyào* — "necessary".

> **我觉得没有必要问这个问题** *wǒ juéde méiyǒu bìyào wèn zhège wèntí* — I don't think there's any need to ask that.

The new character, in pieces: 必 — five strokes: a dot on the left, the long curve along the foot that hooks up, as in 心, a dot on top, a long left-falling stroke down through the curve, and a dot on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**必要** (*bìyào*) is built from *bì* and 要 'to need', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjit* and *'jiewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'certain' and 'so', and the second 'to bind'.
