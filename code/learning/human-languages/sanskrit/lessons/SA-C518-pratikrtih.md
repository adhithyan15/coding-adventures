## You'll want to know: प्रतिकृतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C518-TULIKA518-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिकृतिः** — *pratikṛtiḥ* — "a portrait; a likeness".

> चित्रकारः मम मातुः प्रतिकृतिम् आलिखति।
>
> *citrakāraḥ mama mātuḥ pratikṛtim ālikhati.*
>
> The painter paints my mother's portrait.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिकृतिः** (*pratikṛtiḥ*) is built on *prati*, "in return, against", and the root √kṛ, "to make": a thing made to answer another; MW gives *pratikṛti* as an image, a likeness, a model (MW).
