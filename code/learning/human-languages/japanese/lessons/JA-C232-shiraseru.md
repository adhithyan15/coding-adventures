## You'll want to know: しらせる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C232-TALK232-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しらせる** — *shiraseru* — "to inform; to let know".

> **せんせいは がくせいに しけんの じかんを しらせます。** — *sensei wa gakusei ni shiken no jikan o shirasemasu* — "The teacher tells the students the time of the exam."

Polite **しらせます**, *shirasemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しらせる** (*shiraseru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built on *shiru*, "to know", and means "to make someone know" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
