## You'll want to know: छुरिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C512-CHURIKA512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**छुरिका** — *churikā* — "a knife".

> माता छुरिकया शाकं छिनत्ति।
>
> *mātā churikayā śākaṃ chinatti.*
>
> Mother cuts the vegetables with a knife.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छुरिका** (*churikā*) comes from *kṣura*, "a razor", as MW derives it; MW records *churikā* as a knife (MW).
