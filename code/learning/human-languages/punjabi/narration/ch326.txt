Punjabi, chapter 326: Fear and Anger.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਡਰਨਾ (ḍarnā) — to be afraid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to chew, then the Punjabi for an ache.

You'll want to know: ਡਰਨਾ.
ਡਰਨਾ — ḍarnā — "to be afraid".
ਬੱਚਾ ਹਨੇਰੇ ਤੋਂ ਡਰਦਾ ਹੈ। bachchā hanere ton ḍardā hai. The child is afraid of the dark.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਡਰਿਆ, maiṁ rāt nū̃ bahut ḍariā.

The word, taken apart.
ਡਰਨਾ (ḍarnā) is inherited from Sanskrit darati, "is frightened"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍarnā, "to fear", under it (CDIAL 6190).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਗੁੱਸਾ (gussā) — anger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an ache, then the Punjabi for to be afraid.

You'll want to know: ਗੁੱਸਾ.
ਗੁੱਸਾ — gussā — "anger".
ਝਗੜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਗੁੱਸਾ ਸੀ। jhagṛe ton bāad Aman nū̃ bahut gussā sī. After the quarrel Aman was very angry.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੁੱਸਾ (gussā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic ghuṣṣa, "a choking; grief".
