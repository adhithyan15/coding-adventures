## You'll want to know: shùnlì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C393-BESURE393-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shùnlì* — "smooth; without a hitch".

> *rúguǒ shùnlì dehuà wǒmen wǔ diǎn jiù dào* — If all goes well, we'll be there by five.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shùnlì* is built from *shùn* 'to go along with' and *lì* 'favourable', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zywinH* and *lijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to follow' and the second 'auspicious'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
