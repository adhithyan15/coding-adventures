## You'll want to know: 机长
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C413-FLY413-03]; assesses=[] -->

**机长** — *jīzhǎng* — "plane's captain; pilot in command".

> **机长说飞机马上要到了** *jīzhǎng shuō fēijī mǎshàng yào dào le* — The captain says we'll be there soon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**机长** (*jīzhǎng*) is built from 机 'a plane' and 长 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kj+j* and *trjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
