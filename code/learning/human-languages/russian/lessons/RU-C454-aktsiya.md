## You'll want to know: акция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C454-RASPRODAZHA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**акция** (*áktsiya*) — "a special offer, a promotion; a share (in a company)".

> По акции второй кофе — бесплатно.
>
> *Po áktsii vtoróy kófe — besplátno.*
>
> On special offer, the second coffee is free.

Feminine. *По акции* — on special offer. Also a share on the stock market, or a protest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*акция* is usually traced, probably through German or French, to Latin *āctiō* 'a doing, an action' (EtymDB lists the Latin word), from *agere* 'to drive, to do'.
