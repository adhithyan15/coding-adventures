## You'll want to know: сочинять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C319-LEGENDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сочинять** (*sochinyát*) — "to make up, to compose".

> Мама сочиняла сказки для нас.
>
> *Máma sochinyála skázki dlya nas.*
>
> Mum made up fairy tales for us.

Imperfective; perfective *сочинить* (*sochinít*). Also of music or verse; in speech also "to tell tales": *не сочиняй!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сочинять* is built on *со-* "together" and *чин* "order, rank", inherited from Proto-Slavic *činŭ* (EtymDB): to put together in order.
