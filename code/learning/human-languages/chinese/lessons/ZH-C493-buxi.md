## You'll want to know: bǔxí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C493-TUTOR493-01]; assesses=[] -->

*bǔxí* — "to take extra lessons".

> *wǒ bǔxí le yì nián shùxué* — I had extra maths lessons for a year.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǔxí* is built from *bǔ* 'to patch' and *xí* 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *puX* and *zip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to mend clothes' and the second 'to learn'.
