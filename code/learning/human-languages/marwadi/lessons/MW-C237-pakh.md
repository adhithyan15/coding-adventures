## You'll want to know: पांख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C237-FIELD237-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पांख** — *pā̃kh* — "a wing; a feather".

> चिड़ी पांख खोलै है।
>
> *chiṛī pā̃kh kholai hai.*
>
> The sparrow spreads its wings.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पांख** (*pā̃kh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pakṣa*, "a wing"; CDIAL lists an Old Marwari word for a wing or a feather under it (CDIAL 7627).
