## You'll want to know: xīnshǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C277-LIKES277-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnshǎng* — "to admire; to appreciate".

> *wǒ hěn xīnshǎng tā* — I really admire him.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnshǎng* is built from *xīn* 'glad' and *shǎng* 'to reward', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xj+n* and *syangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'glad' and the second 'to bestow'.
