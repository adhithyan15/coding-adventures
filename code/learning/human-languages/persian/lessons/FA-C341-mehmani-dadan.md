## You'll want to know: مهمانی دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C341-MEHMANI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مهمانی دادن** (*mehmâni dâdan*) — "to throw a party, to give a party".

> برای تولد خواهرم مهمانی دادیم.
>
> *Barâ-ye tavallod-e khâharam mehmâni dâdim.*
>
> We threw a party for my sister's birthday.

Present *mehmâni midahad*. *Mehmâni raftan* is "to go to a party".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mehmâni* "a party" is built on *mehmân* "guest", a native Persian word, and *dâdan* "to give" is inherited Persian: to give a party.
