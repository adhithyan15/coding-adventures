Arabic, chapter 501: Wise, skilled and sure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حكيم (ḥakīm) — wise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for cowardly, then the Arabic for bold.

You'll want to know: حكيم.
حكيم — ḥakīm — "wise".
جدتي امرأة حكيمة. jaddatī imraʾa ḥakīma. My grandmother is a wise woman.
In some spoken dialects حكيم (ḥakīm) is also a doctor (colloquial).

The word, taken apart.
حكيم (ḥakīm) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-k-m, "to judge, to restrain" (Lane). The noun ḥikma is wisdom.

You'll want to know: ماهر.

صنع النجار الماهر طاولة جميلة. ṣanaʿa an-najjār al-māhir ṭāwila jamīla. The skilful carpenter made a beautiful table.
The noun مهارة, a skill, shares the root.

The word, taken apart.
ماهر (māhir) is built on the native Arabic root m-h-r, "to be skilled" (Lane). Māhir is the active participle.

ابنتي طالبة مبدعة في الرسم. ibnatī ṭāliba mubdiʿa fī ar-rasm. My daughter is a creative student in drawing.

مبدع (mubdiʿ) is built on the native Arabic root b-d-ʿ, "to originate, to make a thing new" (Lane). Mubdiʿ is the participle of Form IV: one who makes a new thing.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ثقة (thiqa) — confidence; trust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for creative, then the Arabic for sensible.

You'll want to know: ثقة.
ثقة — thiqa — "confidence; trust".
تتكلم أختي الإنجليزية بثقة. tatakallamu ukhtī al-injilīziyya bi-thiqa. My sister speaks English with confidence.
ثقة (thiqa) بالنفس is self-confidence.

The word, taken apart.
ثقة (thiqa) is built on the native Arabic root w-th-q, "to trust, to rely on" (Lane). The verb wathiqa, to trust, which you know, is from the same root; thiqa drops the first letter w.
