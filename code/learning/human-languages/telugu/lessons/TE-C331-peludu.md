# పేలుడు (pēluḍu) — an explosion; a blast

## You'll want to know: పేలుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C331-MANTA331-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పేలుడు** — *pēluḍu* — "an explosion; a blast".

> పేలుడు శబ్దం చాలా దూరం వినిపించింది.
>
> *pēluḍu śabdaṁ cālā dūraṁ vinipiñcindi.*
>
> The sound of the blast was heard far away.

The noun of పేలు, to burst: a balloon or a cracker పేలుతుంది.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పేలుడు** (*pēluḍu*) is native Dravidian, the noun of *pēlu*, "to explode, burst, crack", which DEDR 4194 lists with Tamil *piḷ*, "to burst open".
