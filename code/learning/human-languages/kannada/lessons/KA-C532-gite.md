## You'll want to know: ಗೀತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C532-GITA532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೀತೆ** — *gīte* — "a song".

> ಶಾಲೆಯ ಸಮಾರಂಭದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಒಂದು ಗೀತೆ ಹಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *śāleya samārambhadalli makkaḷu ondu gīte hāḍidaru.*
>
> The children sang a song at the school function.

Formal; in talk people say ಹಾಡು. ನಾಡಗೀತೆ, the state song.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೀತೆ** (*gīte*) goes back to Sanskrit *gīta*, "singing" (CDIAL 4167), from *gai*, "to sing".
