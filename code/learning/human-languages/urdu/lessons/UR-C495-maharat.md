## You'll want to know: مہارت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C495-AJRAT495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مہارت** — *mahārat* — "expertise; great skill".

> اس کاریگر کے ہاتھوں میں بڑی مہارت ہے۔
>
> *is kārīgar ke hāthoṅ meṅ baṛī mahārat hai.*
>
> This craftsman has great skill in his hands.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مہارت** (*mahārat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mahāra*, "skill, cleverness".
