## You'll want to know: अनुकरोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C522-PADANYASA522-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुकरोति** — *anukaroti* — "imitates".

> शिष्यः गुरोः पदन्यासम् अनुकरोति।
>
> *śiṣyaḥ guroḥ padanyāsam anukaroti.*
>
> The pupil imitates the teacher's steps.

Past (he …): सः गुरुम् अनुकृतवान्, *saḥ gurum anukṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुकरोति** (*anukaroti*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √kṛ, "to do", which also gives *karoti*: to do after another; MW gives *anukṛ* as to imitate, to copy (MW).
