## You'll want to know: xīnxiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C177-FULL177-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnxiān* — "fresh".

> *zhèr de yú gèng xīnxiān* — The fish here is fresher.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnxiān* is built from *xīn* 'new' and *xiān* 'bright and clean', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sin* and *sjen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
