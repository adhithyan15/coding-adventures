## You'll want to know: le réservoir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C403-POMPE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le réservoir** — "the tank (for fuel); the reservoir".

> Le réservoir est presque vide.
>
> *The tank is almost empty.*

*Faire le plein* — to fill the tank.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réservoir* is built on *réserver* 'to keep aside', a learned borrowing from Latin *reservāre*, from *servāre* 'to keep safe' (Lewis & Short).
