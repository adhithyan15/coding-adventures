## You'll want to know: ರಹಸ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C348-NIDHI348-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಹಸ್ಯ** — *rahasya* — "a secret; a mystery".

> ನಿಧಿಯ ರಹಸ್ಯ ಒಂದು ಹಳೆಯ ನಕ್ಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *nidhiya rahasya ondu haḷeya nakṣeyalli ittu.*
>
> The treasure's secret was in an old map.

Plural: ರಹಸ್ಯಗಳು, *rahasyagaḷu*.

As an adjective: ರಹಸ್ಯ ದಾರಿ, a secret path. ರಹಸ್ಯವಾಗಿ, secretly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಹಸ್ಯ** (*rahasya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rahasya*, "private, secret" (CDIAL 10669), from *rahas*, "a lonely spot".
