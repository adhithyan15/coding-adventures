## You'll want to know: aboutir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C275-DELAI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aboutir** — "to succeed, to come to a result; to lead to".

> Le projet a enfin abouti.
>
> *The project finally succeeded.*

*Aboutir à* — to lead to: *la rue aboutit à la place*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aboutir* is built on *à* and *bout* "end": to reach the end. *Bout* comes from *bouter* "to push", which is usually traced to a Frankish verb; the origin is not settled (EtymDB lists no parent).
