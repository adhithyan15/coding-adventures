## You'll want to know: さんみゃく

**さんみゃく** — *sanmyaku* — "a mountain range".

> **ひこうきから さんみゃくが みえました。** — *hikōki kara sanmyaku ga miemashita* — "We could see the mountain range from the plane."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さんみゃく** (*sanmyaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *san*, "mountain" (Middle Chinese *srean*) and *myaku*, "vein" (*meak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
