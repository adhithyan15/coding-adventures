## You'll want to know: la carrozza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C419-CARROZZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la carrozza** — "the coach (of a train); the horse-drawn carriage".

> Abbiamo i posti nella carrozza sette.
>
> *We have seats in coach seven.*

On a ticket *carrozza* is the coach number; *la carrozza* is also a horse-drawn carriage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carrozza* is built on *carro* 'cart' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *carrus*, a word of Gaulish origin.
