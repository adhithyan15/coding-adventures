## You'll want to know: jiějué
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C217-FINE217-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiějué* — "to solve; to settle".

> *zhège wèntí wǒmen néng jiějué* — We can sort this problem out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiějué* is built from *jiě* 'to untie' and *jué* 'to break through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keaX* and *kwet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to explain' and 'to loosen', and the second 'to break'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
