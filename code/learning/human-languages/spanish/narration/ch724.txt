Spanish, chapter 724: Sinceros y cotillas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la sinceridad — sincerity, frankness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for stingy, then the Spanish for cruel.

You'll want to know: la sinceridad.
la sinceridad — "sincerity, frankness".
Te lo digo con toda sinceridad. I am telling you in all honesty.
Con sinceridad — honestly, frankly.

The word, taken apart.
sinceridad is usually described as a learned borrowing from Latin sinceritās "purity, soundness", built on sincērus "genuine", the source of sincero (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
cotillear — to gossip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for cruel, then the Spanish for sincerity.

You'll want to know: cotillear.
cotillear — "to gossip".
Mis vecinas cotillean en la escalera. My neighbours gossip on the stairs.
Spain; a gossip is un cotilla. In Latin America people say chismear.

The word, taken apart.
cotillear is built on cotilla "a gossip" with the ending -ear. For cotilla in this sense the origin is not settled; an older cotilla was a corset.
