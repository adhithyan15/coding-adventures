## You'll want to know: der Blumenladen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C444-BAUMARKT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Blumenladen** — "the flower shop, the florist's".

> Für den Geburtstag kauft er Rosen im Blumenladen.
>
> *He buys roses at the flower shop for the birthday.*

Plural: *die Blumenläden*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Blumenladen* is built from *Blume* 'flower', inherited from Old High German *bluomo* (EtymDB), and *Laden* 'shop', first 'plank, shutter', from Middle High German *laden* (EtymDB).
