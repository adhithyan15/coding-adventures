## You'll want to know: しけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C226-EXAM226-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しけん** — *shiken* — "an exam; a test".

> **がっこうは まいしゅう しけんが あります。** — *gakkō wa maishū shiken ga arimasu* — "The school has an exam every week."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しけん** (*shiken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "try" (Middle Chinese *syiH*) and *ken*, "verify" (*ngjemH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
