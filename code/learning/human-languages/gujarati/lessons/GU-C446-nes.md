## You'll want to know: નેસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C446-HERD446-04]; assesses=[] -->

**નેસ** — *nes* — "a nes (a herders' hamlet in the forest)".

> જંગલમાં ભરવાડોનો નાનો નેસ છે.
>
> *jangalmā̃ bharvāḍono nāno nes che.*
>
> There is a small herders' hamlet in the forest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નેસ** (*nes*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *nivēśa*, "a dwelling place, a camp"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a shepherd's hut" (CDIAL 7424).
