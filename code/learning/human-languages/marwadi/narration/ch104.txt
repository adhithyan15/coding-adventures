Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 104: To buy, To uncover, To call, To ask for, To look.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खरीदणो (kharīdṇo) — to buy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to break, then the Marwadi for to join.

You'll want to know: खरीदणो.
खरीदणो — kharīdṇo — "to buy".

The word, taken apart.
खरीदणो (kharīdṇo) is a loan, usually traced to Persian xarīd, "bought; to buy". Persian xarīdan means "to buy"; Marwadi added its own verb ending.

Lesson 2 of 5.
उघाड़णो (ughāṛṇo) — to uncover, to open up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to join, then the Marwadi for to buy.

You'll want to know: उघाड़णो.
उघाड़णो — ughāṛṇo — "to uncover, to open up".

The word, taken apart.
उघाड़णो (ughāṛṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from udghāṭayati, "opens". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 1968); the match is through Hindi ughāṛnā and Gujarati ughāṛvũ. It is built on ud, "up", and the root ghaṭ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बुलावणो (bulāvṇo) — to call.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to buy, then the Marwadi for to uncover.

You'll want to know: बुलावणो.
बुलावणो — bulāvṇo — "to call".

The word, taken apart.
बुलावणो (bulāvṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from boll, "speak", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari bolaï under it (CDIAL 9321). It is the causing form of bolṇo, "to speak": "make speak", so "call".

You'll want to know: जोवणो.
जोवणो — jovṇo — "to look, to look for".

The word, taken apart.
जोवणो (jovṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dyotate, "shines; makes clear". CDIAL lists Old Marwari jovaï under it (CDIAL 6612). The Old Marwari row means "watches"; CDIAL sets the verb beside Sanskrit jyotate, "shines".
