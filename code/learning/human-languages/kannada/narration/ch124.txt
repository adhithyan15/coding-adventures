Kannada, chapter 124: Potato, Aubergine, Ginger, Garlic, Honey.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಆಲೂಗಡ್ಡೆ (ālūgaḍḍe) — a potato.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a coconut, then the Kannada for an onion.

You'll want to know: ಆಲೂಗಡ್ಡೆ.
ಆಲೂಗಡ್ಡೆ — ālūgaḍḍe — "a potato".

The word, taken apart.
ಆಲೂಗಡ್ಡೆ (ālūgaḍḍe) is ālū, "potato", from Hindustani ālū, and gaḍḍe, "bulbous root", native Dravidian (DEDR 1171).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಬದನೆಕಾಯಿ (badanekāyi) — an aubergine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an onion, then the Kannada for a potato.

You'll want to know: ಬದನೆಕಾಯಿ.
ಬದನೆಕಾಯಿ — badanekāyi — "an aubergine".

The word, taken apart.
ಬದನೆಕಾಯಿ (badanekāyi) is native Dravidian: badane, "brinjal", (DEDR 5301, with Tamil vaḻutalai), and kāyi, "fruit" (DEDR 1459). The old v- has become b-. DEDR 5301 compares Sanskrit bhaṇṭākī.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಬೆಳ್ಳುಳ್ಳಿ (beḷḷuḷḷi) — garlic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an aubergine, then the Kannada for ginger.

You'll want to know: ಬೆಳ್ಳುಳ್ಳಿ.
ಬೆಳ್ಳುಳ್ಳಿ — beḷḷuḷḷi — "garlic".

The word, taken apart.
ಬೆಳ್ಳುಳ್ಳಿ (beḷḷuḷḷi) is native Dravidian, "white onion": beḷ, "white" (DEDR 5496), and uḷḷi, "onion" (DEDR 705). Tamil veḷḷ-uḷḷi is built the same way. DEDR 705 compares Sanskrit ūlī.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಜೇನು (jēnu) — honey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಜೇನು.
ಜೇನು — jēnu — "honey".

The word, taken apart.
ಜೇನು (jēnu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil tēṉ (DEDR 3268). DEDR lists Kannada jēnu and tēnu, "honey". DEDR 3268 compares, with a query, Prakrit tiṇisa.
