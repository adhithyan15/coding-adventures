## You'll want to know: કંપની
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C513-KARKHANU513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કંપની** — *kampnī* — "a company (a business)".

> મારા ભાઈએ એક નાની કંપની શરૂ કરી.
>
> *mārā bhāīe ek nānī kampnī śarū karī.*
>
> My brother started a small company.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કંપની** (*kampnī*) is borrowed from English *company*.
