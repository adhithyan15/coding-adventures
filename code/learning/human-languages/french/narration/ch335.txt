French, chapter 335: Le débat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le débat — the debate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to state firmly, then the French for convincing.

You'll want to know: le débat.
le débat — "the debate".
Ce soir, il y a un débat à la télévision sur l'école. Tonight there is a debate on television about schools.
Un débat animé — a lively debate.

The word, taken apart.
débat is formed from the verb débattre, built on battre "to beat", which is inherited from Latin battuere "to beat, to strike" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). Ideas are knocked back and forth.

Lesson 2 of 5.
débattre — to debate, to discuss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for convincing, then the French for the debate.

You'll want to know: débattre.
débattre — "to debate, to discuss".
Nous avons débattu de ce problème pendant deux heures. We debated this problem for two hours.
Débattre de quelque chose. Conjugated like battre: je débats, nous débattons.

The word, taken apart.
débattre is inherited from Old French debatre "to fight, to contend" (EtymDB), built from dé- and battre, which comes from Latin battuere "to beat" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la thèse — the thesis, the position argued for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the debate, then the French for to debate.

You'll want to know: la thèse.
la thèse — "the thesis, the position argued for".
Sa thèse est simple: nous travaillons trop. His thesis is simple: we work too much.
At university, une thèse is a doctoral thesis.

The word, taken apart.
thèse is a learned borrowing from Latin thesis "a proposition" (Lewis & Short), which came from Greek thésis "a setting down, a statement" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
l'argumentation — the line of argument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to debate, then the French for the thesis.

You'll want to know: l'argumentation.
l'argumentation — "the line of argument".
Ton argumentation est claire, mais je ne suis pas d'accord. Your line of argument is clear, but I do not agree.
The whole chain of reasons; one single reason is un argument. Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
argumentation is a learned borrowing from Latin argūmentātiō "the bringing of proof" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from argūmentum "proof", built on arguere "to make clear".

Lesson 5 of 5.
persuader — to persuade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the thesis, then the French for the line of argument.

You'll want to know: persuader.
persuader — "to persuade".
J'ai essayé de persuader mon père d'acheter une nouvelle voiture. I tried to persuade my father to buy a new car.
Persuader quelqu'un de faire quelque chose. Close to convaincre.

The word, taken apart.
persuader comes from Latin persuādēre "to bring over by talking, to convince" (Lewis & Short), through Middle French (EtymDB). It is built from per- and suādēre "to advise".
