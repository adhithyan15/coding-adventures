## You'll want to know: o beco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C437-RUAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o beco** — "the alley, the dead end".

> O carro entrou num beco sem saída.
>
> *The car went into a dead end.*

*Um beco sem saída* — a dead end; also a hopeless situation.
