## You'll want to know: a ameaça
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C290-ARMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a ameaça** — "the threat".

> Ela recebeu uma ameaça por telefone.
>
> *She received a threat by phone.*

*Sob ameaça* — under threat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ameaça* comes from Vulgar Latin *minācia* "a threat" (EtymDB), from Latin *mināx* "threatening".
