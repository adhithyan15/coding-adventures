Mandarin Chinese, chapter 432: Busy and Quiet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
少 (shǎo) — few; not much.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for many, then the Mandarin for to pose for a group photo.

You'll want to know: 少.
少 — shǎo — "few; not much".
今年这家酒店的客人很少 jīnnián zhè jiā jiǔdiàn de kèrén hěn shǎo — This hotel has had very few guests this year.

The word, taken apart.
少 (shǎo) is inherited from Middle Chinese syewX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'not many'. The dictionary lists a second reading, syewH, glossed 'young': the shào of 少年.

Lesson 2 of 5.
dànjì — off season; quiet season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to pose for a group photo, then the Mandarin for few.

You'll want to know: dànjì.
dànjì — "off season; quiet season".
dànjì de shíhou yóukè hěn shǎo — In the off season there are very few visitors.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dànjì is built from dàn 'thin, slack' and jì 'a season', both inherited from Middle Chinese, damX and kjwijH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a thin taste and the second as the youngest of brothers; the sense 'a season' is not given there.

Lesson 3 of 5.
节假日 (jiéjiàrì) — public holiday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for few, then the Mandarin for the off season.

You'll want to know: 节假日.
节假日 — jiéjiàrì — "public holiday".
jiéjiàrì de shíhou hěn duō rén zuò huǒchē qù hǎibiān — On public holidays lots of people take the train to the seaside.

The word, taken apart.
节假日 (jiéjiàrì) is built from 节 'a festival', 假 'leave' and 日 'a day', all inherited from Middle Chinese, tset, kaeH and nyit (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the joint of a bamboo stem and the second 'to borrow' and 'leave'; the sense 'a festival' for the first is not given there.

Lesson 4 of 5.
全票 (quánpiào) — full-price ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the off season, then the Mandarin for a public holiday.

You'll want to know: 全票.
全票 — quánpiào — "full-price ticket".
wǒmen mǎi le liǎng zhāng quánpiào — We bought two full-price tickets.

The word, taken apart.
全票 (quánpiào) is built from 全 'whole' and 票 'a ticket'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese dzjwen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'complete'; no dictionary we use records an older form of 票, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
guānbì — to shut down; to be shut to the public.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: guānbì.
guānbì — "to shut down; to be shut to the public".
dōngtiān zhège jǐngqū guānbì — This scenic area is closed in winter.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
guānbì is built from 关 'to bar' and bì 'to shut', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwaen and pejH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the bar across a door and the second 'to shut a door'.
