## You'll want to know: el incidente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C574-ENTERARSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el incidente** — "the incident".

> El vuelo llegó sin incidentes.
>
> *The flight arrived without incident.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incidente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *incidēns* 'falling upon', from *incidere* 'to fall into, to happen' (Lewis & Short), built on *in* and *cadere* 'to fall'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
