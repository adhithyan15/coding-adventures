Sanskrit, chapter 434: Loans and Deposits.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उत्तमर्णः (uttamarṇaḥ) — a creditor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a share, then the Sanskrit for a guarantor.

You'll want to know: उत्तमर्णः.
उत्तमर्णः — uttamarṇaḥ — "a creditor".
उत्तमर्णः ऋणं ददाति। uttamarṇaḥ ṛṇaṃ dadāti. The creditor gives a loan.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उत्तमर्णः (uttamarṇaḥ) is built from uttama, "highest, upper", and ṛṇa, "a debt"; MW gives the compound as a creditor (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कुसीदम् (kusīdam) — money lent at interest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a creditor, then the Sanskrit for a debtor.

You'll want to know: कुसीदम्.
कुसीदम् — kusīdam — "money lent at interest".
कुसीदेन धनिकस्य धनं वर्धते। kusīdena dhanikasya dhanaṃ vardhate. The rich man's wealth grows by lending at interest.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
कुसीदम् (kusīdam) is the word MW gives for a loan to be repaid with interest and for lending money upon interest; MW suggests ku and the root √sad with a query, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
निक्षेपः (nikṣepaḥ) — a deposit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a debtor, then the Sanskrit for money lent at interest.

You'll want to know: निक्षेपः.
निक्षेपः — nikṣepaḥ — "a deposit".
सः वित्तकोषे निक्षेपं स्थापयति। saḥ vittakoṣe nikṣepaṃ sthāpayati. He places a deposit in the bank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
निक्षेपः (nikṣepaḥ) is built from ni, "down", and the root √kṣip, "to throw", which also gives nikṣipati, "puts in, deposits"; MW gives nikṣepa as putting down and as a deposit, a pledge (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
निर्यातयति (niryātayati) — pays back; returns.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for money lent at interest, then the Sanskrit for a deposit.

You'll want to know: निर्यातयति.
निर्यातयति — niryātayati — "pays back; returns".
अधमर्णः उत्तमर्णाय धनं निर्यातयति। adhamarṇaḥ uttamarṇāya dhanaṃ niryātayati. The debtor pays the money back to the creditor.
Past (he …): सः धनं निर्यातितवान्, saḥ dhanaṃ niryātitavān.

The word, taken apart.
निर्यातयति (niryātayati) is built from nir, "out", and the causative of the root √yat, which also gives yatate, "tries"; MW gives its participle niryātita as restored, returned, requited (MW).
