Telugu, chapter 298: Blunt, Crooked, Flat, Hollow, Dim.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
మొద్దు (moddu) — blunt; dull-witted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for naughty, then the Telugu for honesty.

You'll want to know: మొద్దు.
మొద్దు — moddu — "blunt; dull-witted".

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does మొద్దు (moddu) mean? ("Blunt; dull-witted".) Say it once more.
