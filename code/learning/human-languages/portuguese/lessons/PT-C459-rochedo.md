## You'll want to know: o rochedo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C459-ROCHEDOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o rochedo** — "the big rock, the crag".

> Sentámo-nos num rochedo a ver o mar.
>
> *We sat on a big rock watching the sea.*

Built on *a rocha*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rochedo* is built on *rocha* 'rock' with *-edo*, a group of things. *Rocha* came from French *roche* (EtymDB), whose earlier origin is not settled.
