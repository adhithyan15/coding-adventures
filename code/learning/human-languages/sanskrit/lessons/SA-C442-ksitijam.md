## You'll want to know: क्षितिजम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C442-UDAYA442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षितिजम्** — *kṣitijam* — "the horizon".

> प्रातः क्षितिजम् अरुणं भवति।
>
> *prātaḥ kṣitijam aruṇaṃ bhavati.*
>
> In the morning the horizon turns red.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षितिजम्** (*kṣitijam*) is built from *kṣiti*, "the earth", and *ja*, "born": MW gives *kṣitija* as earth-born and, as a neuter, the horizon (MW).
