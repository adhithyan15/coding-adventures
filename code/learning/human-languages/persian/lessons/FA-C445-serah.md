## You'll want to know: سه راه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C445-TAGHATO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سه راه** (*serâh*) — "a T-junction, a three-way junction".

> به سه راه رسیدیم و نمیدانستیم کدام طرف برویم.
>
> *Be serâh residim va nemidânestim kodâm taraf beravim.*
>
> We reached the junction and didn't know which way to go.

*se* 'three' + *râh* 'road'. A crossroads is *chahârrâh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*serâh* is a native compound of *se* 'three', usually traced to PIE *tréyes*, and *râh* 'road', from Middle Persian *rāh* (EtymDB).
