## You'll want to know: అతిశయోక్తి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C336-PUKARU336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అతిశయోక్తి** — *atiśayōkti* — "an exaggeration".

> అతని మాటల్లో చాలా అతిశయోక్తి ఉంది.
>
> *atani māṭallō cālā atiśayōkti undi.*
>
> There is a lot of exaggeration in his words.

అతిశయ, excess, and ఉక్తి, a saying.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అతిశయోక్తి** (*atiśayōkti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *atiśayokti*, "hyperbolical language" (Monier-Williams), from *atiśaya*, "excess", and *ukti*, "a saying".
