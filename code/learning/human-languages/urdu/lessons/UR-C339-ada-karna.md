## You'll want to know: ادا کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C339-IKHTIYAR339-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ادا کرنا** — *adā karnā* — "to pay (a debt); to fulfil (a duty)".

> ہر شہری کو اپنا فرض ادا کرنا چاہیے۔
>
> *har shahrī ko apnā farz adā karnā chāhiye.*
>
> Every citizen should do his duty.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے قرض ادا کیا, *us ne qarz adā kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ادا کرنا** (*adā karnā*) is built from *adā*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *adāʾ*, "payment, performance", and **کرنا**, "to do".
