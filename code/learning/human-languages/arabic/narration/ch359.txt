Arabic, chapter 359: Points of view.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انطباع (inṭibāʿ) — an impression.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to prove, then the Arabic for to convince.

You'll want to know: انطباع.
انطباع — inṭibāʿ — "an impression".
ترك الفندق انطباعا جيدا عندي. taraka al-funduq inṭibāʿan jayyidan ʿindī. The hotel left a good impression on me.

The word, taken apart.
انطباع (inṭibāʿ) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-b-ʿ, "to stamp, to imprint" (Lane). Inṭibāʿ is the Form VII verbal noun, being stamped: the mark a thing leaves on the mind, an impression.

You'll want to know: منظور.
منظور — manẓūr — "a perspective".
من منظور الطبيب، النوم مهم جدا. min manẓūr aṭ-ṭabīb, an-nawm muhimm jiddan. From the doctor's perspective, sleep is very important.

منظور (manẓūr) is built on the native Arabic root n-ẓ-r, "to look, to regard" (Lane). Manẓūr is the passive participle, what is looked at; in modern use it is the angle you look from.

Lesson 3 of 5.
افترض (iftaraḍa) — to assume.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an impression, then the Arabic for a perspective.

You'll want to know: افترض.
افترض — iftaraḍa — "to assume".
افترضنا أن القطار سيصل مبكرا. iftaraḍnā anna al-qiṭār sa-yaṣilu mubakkiran. We assumed that the train would arrive early.
Present: يفترض, yaftariḍ.
Followed by أن: افترض (iftaraḍa) أن …, assumed that ….

The word, taken apart.
افترض (iftaraḍa) is built on the native Arabic root f-r-ḍ, "to make a notch; to make binding" (Lane). It is Form VIII: to take a thing as laid down, to assume it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
اقترح (iqtaraḥa) — to suggest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a perspective, then the Arabic for to assume.

You'll want to know: اقترح.
اقترح — iqtaraḥa — "to suggest".
اقترح المعلم رحلة إلى المتحف. iqtaraḥa al-muʿallim riḥla ilā al-matḥaf. The teacher suggested a trip to the museum.
Present: يقترح, yaqtariḥ.
The noun اقتراح, iqtirāḥ, a suggestion, comes from it.

The word, taken apart.
اقترح (iqtaraḥa) is built on the native Arabic root q-r-ḥ, "to wound" (Lane). It is Form VIII. Its modern sense, to put an idea forward, is usually traced to an older one, to bring out something new.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: مقنع.
مقنع — muqniʿ — "convincing".
كان كلام المدير مقنعا. kāna kalām al-mudīr muqniʿan. The manager's words were convincing.
After كان the adjective takes -an: كان مقنعا.

The word, taken apart.
مقنع (muqniʿ) is built on the native Arabic root q-n-ʿ, "to be content, satisfied" (Lane). Muqniʿ is the Form IV active participle: that which convinces.
