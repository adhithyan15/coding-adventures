Russian, chapter 371: Let us suppose.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
гипотеза (gipóteza) — a hypothesis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for otherwise, then the Russian for even if.

You'll want to know: гипотеза.
гипотеза (gipóteza) — "a hypothesis".
Учёные проверили эту гипотезу. Uchyónyye provérili étu gipótezu. The scientists tested this hypothesis.
Feminine. Выдвинуть гипотезу — to put forward a hypothesis.

The word, taken apart.
гипотеза (gipóteza) comes from Latin hypothesis, from Greek hypóthesis "the basis of an argument, a supposition" (EtymDB), built from hypó "under" and thésis "a placing".

Lesson 2 of 5.
предполагать (predpolagát) — to suppose, to assume.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for even if, then the Russian for a hypothesis.

You'll want to know: предполагать.
предполагать (predpolagát) — "to suppose, to assume".
Я предполагаю, что он уже дома. Ya predpolagáyu, chto on uzhé dóma. I assume that he is already at home.
Imperfective; perfective предположить. Предполагается, что… — it is expected that…

The word, taken apart.
предполагать (predpolagát) is built from пред- "before" and полагать "to lay down, to think", on the root of ложить "to lay": to lay a thought down in advance.

The мы form of предположить, used to set up an idea, as in предположим (predpolózhim), что…

The word, taken apart.
предположим (predpolózhim) is a form of предположить "to suppose", built from пред- "before" and положить "to lay down", on the root of ложить.

Lesson 4 of 5.
допустим (dopústim) — let us say, say.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to suppose, then the Russian for let us suppose.

You'll want to know: допустим.
допустим (dopústim) — "let us say, say".
Допустим, ты прав. Что тогда? Dopústim, ty prav. Chto togdá? Let us say you are right. What then?
A form of допустить, to allow: let us allow that… It also gives an example: допустим (dopústim), в пятницу.

The word, taken apart.
допустим (dopústim) is a form of допустить "to allow, to admit", built from до- and пустить "to let go", which is usually linked with пустой "empty".
