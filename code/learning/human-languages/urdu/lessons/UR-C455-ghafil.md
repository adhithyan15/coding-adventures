## You'll want to know: غافل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C455-FLAW455-05]; assesses=[] -->

**غافل** — *ghāfil* — "heedless; careless".

> وہ اپنے کام سے غافل ہے۔
>
> *voh apne kām se ghāfil hai.*
>
> He pays no heed to his work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غافل** (*ghāfil*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghāfil*, "heedless, unmindful".
