## You'll want to know: apparō, apparāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C394-APPARO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**apparō, apparāre** — "to prepare, to get ready".

> Convīvium magnum apparāvit.
>
> *He prepared a great banquet.*

*apparō, apparāre, apparāvī, apparātum*, *ad* + *parō*. *Apparātus*, equipment, splendour; English *apparatus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apparō* is built from *ad* "towards" and *parō* "to make ready" (Lewis & Short). English *apparatus* comes from its noun.
