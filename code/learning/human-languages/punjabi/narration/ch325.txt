Punjabi, chapter 325: Getting Better.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸੁਧਰਨਾ (sudharnā) — to improve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for sweat, then the Punjabi for a dizzy spell.

You'll want to know: ਸੁਧਰਨਾ.
ਸੁਧਰਨਾ — sudharnā — "to improve".
ਦਵਾਈ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਦਾਦੀ ਦੀ ਸਿਹਤ ਸੁਧਰੀ। davāī ton bāad dādī dī sihat sudhrī. After the medicine my grandmother's health got better.
Past (he …): ਉਹ ਇੱਕ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੁਧਰਿਆ, oh ikk hafte vich sudhriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੁਧਰਨਾ (sudharnā) is inherited from a reconstructed śuddhakāra, "making clean"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sudharnā, "to repair", under it (CDIAL 12521).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਵਿਗੜਨਾ (vigaṛnā) — to get worse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a dizzy spell, then the Punjabi for to improve.

You'll want to know: ਵਿਗੜਨਾ.
ਵਿਗੜਨਾ — vigaṛnā — "to get worse".
ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬੱਚੇ ਦਾ ਬੁਖ਼ਾਰ ਵਿਗੜਿਆ। rāt nū̃ bachche dā bukhār vigṛiā. At night the child's fever got worse.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਮੌਸਮ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਵਿਗੜਿਆ, oh mausam shām nū̃ vigṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਿਗੜਨਾ (vigaṛnā) is inherited from Sanskrit vighaṭate, "flies apart, is marred"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vigaṛṇā, "to be spoilt", under it (CDIAL 11673).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਪਰਹੇਜ਼ (parhez) — keeping off certain food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to improve, then the Punjabi for to get worse.

You'll want to know: ਪਰਹੇਜ਼.
ਪਰਹੇਜ਼ — parhez — "keeping off certain food".
ਡਾਕਟਰ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ ਕਿ ਮਿੱਠੇ ਤੋਂ ਪਰਹੇਜ਼ ਕਰੋ। ḍākṭar kahindā hai ki miṭṭhe ton parhez karo. The doctor says to keep off sweet things.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਰਹੇਜ਼ (parhez) is a loan, usually traced to Persian parhez, "abstinence".

ਚਬਾਉਣਾ — chabāuṇā — "to chew".
ਬੱਚਾ ਰੋਟੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਚਬਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। bachchā roṭī haulī chabāundā hai. The child chews his bread slowly.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਚਬਾਈ, maiṁ roṭī haulī chabāī.

The word, taken apart.
ਚਬਾਉਣਾ (chabāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit carvati, "chews"; CDIAL lists Punjabi cabāuṇā under it (CDIAL 4711).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਪੀੜ (pīṛ) — an ache.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for keeping off certain food, then the Punjabi for to chew.

You'll want to know: ਪੀੜ.
ਪੀੜ — pīṛ — "an ache".
ਦਵਾਈ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਪੀੜ ਘੱਟ ਹੈ। davāī ton bāad pīṛ ghaṭṭ hai. After the medicine the ache is less.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੀੜ (pīṛ) is inherited from Sanskrit pīḍā, "pain, damage"; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists Lahnda pīṛ, "pain" (CDIAL 8227).
