## You'll want to know: la seggiovia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C515-NEVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la seggiovia** — "chairlift".

> Saliamo in seggiovia fino in cima.
>
> *We go up to the top by chairlift.*

Built from *seggio* "seat" and *via* "way".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seggiovia* is a modern word built from *seggio* "seat", from Latin *sedēre* "to sit" (Lewis & Short), and *via* "way".
