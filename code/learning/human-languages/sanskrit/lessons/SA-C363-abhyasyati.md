## You'll want to know: अभ्यस्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C363-ADHYAYANA363-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अभ्यस्यति** — *abhyasyati* — "practises (a skill)".

> मीरा प्रतिदिनं वीणाम् अभ्यस्यति।
>
> *mīrā pratidinaṃ vīṇām abhyasyati.*
>
> Mira practises the veena every day.

Past (she …): सा वीणाम् अभ्यस्तवती, *sā vīṇām abhyastavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभ्यस्यति** (*abhyasyati*) is built on *abhi*, "towards", and the root √as, "to throw"; MW gives *abhy-√as* "to practise, to exercise, to study" (MW).
