## You'll want to know: आतडे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C478-HAD478-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आतडे** — *ātḍe* — "the gut; an intestine".

> पोटात आतडी असतात.
>
> *poṭāt ātḍī astāt.*
>
> The intestines are in the belly.

Neuter. Plural: आतडी, *ātḍī*.

Neuter; the plural आतडी is the intestines.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आतडे** (*ātḍe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *āntra*, "entrail"; CDIAL lists Marathi *ā̃tḍẽ*, "entrail" (CDIAL 1182).
