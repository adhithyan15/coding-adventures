## You'll want to know: درج کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C278-THANA278-03]; assesses=[] -->

**درج کرنا** — *darj karnā* — "to register (in a record)".

> داروغے نے رپورٹ درج کی۔
>
> *dāroghe ne riporṭ darj kī.*
>
> The inspector registered the report.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے رپورٹ درج کی, *us ne riporṭ darj kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**درج کرنا** (*darj karnā*) is built from *darj*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *darj*, "a roll, a written record", and **کرنا**: to put a thing on the record. The thing written agrees: **رپورٹ درج کی**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
