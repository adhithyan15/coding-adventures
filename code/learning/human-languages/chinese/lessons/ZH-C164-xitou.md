## You'll want to know: xǐtóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C164-READY164-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐtóu* — "to wash one's hair".

> *wǒ měitiān zǎoshang xǐtóu* — I wash my hair every morning.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐtóu* is built from 洗 'to wash' and *tóu* 'head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sejX* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
