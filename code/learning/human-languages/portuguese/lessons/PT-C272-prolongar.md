## You'll want to know: prolongar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C272-DURAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**prolongar** — "to prolong, to extend".

> Decidimos prolongar as férias mais uma semana.
>
> *We decided to extend the holiday by another week.*

For time and for length: *prolongar uma estrada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prolongar* was borrowed from Latin *prōlongāre* "to lengthen" (EtymDB), built on *longus* "long".
