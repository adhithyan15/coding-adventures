## You'll want to know: le pari
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C500-CARTES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le pari** — "bet".

> J'ai perdu mon pari avec mon frère.
>
> *I lost my bet with my brother.*

*Faire un pari* — to make a bet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le pari* is built on *parier* "to bet" (EtymDB), which comes from Late Latin *pariāre* "to make equal", from *pār* (Lewis & Short).
