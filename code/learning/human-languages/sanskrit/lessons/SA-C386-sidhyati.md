## You'll want to know: सिध्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C386-SIDDHI386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सिध्यति** — *sidhyati* — "succeeds; comes off".

> यदि योजना सिध्यति, तर्हि जनाः प्रसन्नाः भविष्यन्ति।
>
> *yadi yojanā sidhyati, tarhi janāḥ prasannāḥ bhaviṣyanti.*
>
> If the plan comes off, people will be pleased.

Past (it …): कार्यं सिद्धवत्, *kāryaṃ siddhavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिध्यति** (*sidhyati*) comes from the root √sidh, "to be accomplished, to succeed"; MW gives *siddha* "accomplished, fulfilled, gained" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
