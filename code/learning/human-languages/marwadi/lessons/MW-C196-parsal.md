## You'll want to know: पारसल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C196-POST196-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पारसल** — *pārsal* — "a parcel".

> पारसल घणो भारी है।
>
> *pārsal ghaṇo bhārī hai.*
>
> The parcel is very heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पारसल** (*pārsal*) is borrowed from English *parcel*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
