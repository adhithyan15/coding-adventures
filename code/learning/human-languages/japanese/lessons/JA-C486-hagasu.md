## You'll want to know: はがす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C486-PARTS486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**はがす** — *hagasu* — "to peel off; to tear off".

> **ふうとうの きってを はがします。** — *fūtō no kitte o hagashimasu* — "I peel the stamp off the envelope."

Polite **はがします**, *hagashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが きってを はがしました。**, *kodomo ga kitte o hagashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はがす** (*hagasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
