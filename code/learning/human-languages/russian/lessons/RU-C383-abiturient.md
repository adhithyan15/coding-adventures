## You'll want to know: абитуриент
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C383-POSTUPLENIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**абитуриент** (*abituriyént*) — "a university applicant".

> Абитуриенты сдают экзамены летом.
>
> *Abituriyénty sdayút ekzámeny létom.*
>
> University applicants sit their exams in the summer.

Masculine. Someone applying to a university; in German the same word means a school leaver.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**абитуриент** was borrowed from German *Abiturient* (EtymDB), from Latin *abiturīre* "to wish to leave", built on *abīre* "to go away", from *īre* "to go": a pupil about to leave school.
