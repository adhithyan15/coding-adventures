## You'll want to know: लिखवाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C290-THANA290-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लिखवाना** — *likhvānā* — "to have written down".

> अरुण ने पुलिस से अपनी शिकायत लिखवाई।
>
> *Aruṇ ne pulis se apnī śikāyat likhvāī.*
>
> Arun had the police write down his complaint.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने रिपोर्ट लिखवाई, *usne riporṭ likhvāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लिखवाना** (*likhvānā*) is the second causative of **लिखना**, to write, which goes back to Sanskrit *likhati*, "scratches, writes" (CDIAL 11048). The ending *-vā-* hands the work to someone else: **शिकायत लिखवाई**, got the complaint written down.
