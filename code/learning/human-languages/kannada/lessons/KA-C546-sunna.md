## You'll want to know: ಸುಣ್ಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C546-NEMMADI546-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಣ್ಣ** — *suṇṇa* — "lime; whitewash".

> ಹಬ್ಬಕ್ಕೆ ಮುಂಚೆ ನಾವು ಗೋಡೆಗಳಿಗೆ ಸುಣ್ಣ ಬಳಿದೆವು.
>
> *habbakke muñce nāvu gōḍegaḷige suṇṇa baḷidevu.*
>
> Before the festival we whitewashed the walls.

ಸುಣ್ಣ ಬಳಿ, to whitewash. ಸೀಮೆಸುಣ್ಣ, chalk, holds the same word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಣ್ಣ** (*suṇṇa*) goes back to Sanskrit *cūrṇa*, "something ground, a powder" (CDIAL 4889), through a Prakrit form.
