Gujarati, chapter 206: Arriving at last.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
પહોંચવું (pahõcvũ) — to arrive, to reach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to halt, then the Gujarati for to get off.

You'll want to know: પહોંચવું.
પહોંચવું — pahõcvũ — "to arrive, to reach".
અમે રાત્રે શહેરમાં પહોંચ્યા. — ame rātre śahermā̃ pahõcyā — "We reached the city at night."

The word, taken apart.
પહોંચવું (pahõcvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited through Prakrit and reshaped by sound change. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit prábhūta, "much, great", by way of Prakrit pahuccaï, "is powerful, reaches" (CDIAL 8716).

હું લાંબી મુસાફરીથી થાક્યો. — hũ lā̃bī musāpharīthī thākyo — "I got tired from the long journey."

The word, taken apart.
થાકવું (thākvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from sthakk, "stop, halt", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to become weary" (CDIAL 13737).

You'll want to know: પ્રવાસી.
પ્રવાસી — pravāsī — "tourist, traveller".
ગામમાં ઘણા પ્રવાસી આવ્યા. — gāmmā̃ ghaṇā pravāsī āvyā — "Many tourists came to the village."
Built on પ્રવાસ, pravās, 'trip'.

The word, taken apart.
પ્રવાસી (pravāsī) is Sanskrit pravāsin, "one who is away from home", built on pravāsa, "a journey", and taken over as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 4 of 6.
મુકામ (mukām) — stopping place, destination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get tired, then the Gujarati for tourist.

You'll want to know: મુકામ.
મુકામ — mukām — "stopping place, destination".
અમે સાંજે મુકામ પર પહોંચ્યા. — ame sā̃je mukām par pahõcyā — "We reached our stopping place in the evening."

The word, taken apart.
મુકામ (mukām) is a loan from Arabic muqām, maqām, "a standing place, a place of stay", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-w-m, "to stand" (Lane).
