## You'll want to know: besteigen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C454-WANDERUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**besteigen** — "to climb (a mountain); to board".

> Im Sommer besteigen sie einen hohen Berg.
>
> *In summer they climb a high mountain.*

Inseparable, with an object: *sie hat den Gipfel bestiegen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*besteigen* is built from *be-* and *steigen* 'to climb', inherited from Old High German *stīgan* (EtymDB).
