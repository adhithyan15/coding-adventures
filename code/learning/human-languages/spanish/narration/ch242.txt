Spanish, chapter 242: Comisteis, Tuvisteis --- The Last Gap Closes.
1 lesson. All 1 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 1.
comisteis, tuvisteis — the last gap closes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Hablasteis. Now the other two families, and then the hard stems.

Grammar Lens: -isteis, for everything else.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
comer, comisteis.
vivir, vivisteis.
[pause 2 seconds]
One ending for both, exactly as everywhere else in the preterite. The -er and -ir families stopped being two families the first time you met this tense.

Grammar Lens: and the strong stems take it too.
[pause 2 seconds]
The stems that gave you trouble — tuv-, hic-, estuv-, dij- — take the same -isteis, with no surprises left in them:
tuvisteis, hicisteis, estuvisteis, dijisteis.
[pause 2 seconds]
And ser/ir, which share one past: fuisteis.

Why it's said this way.
[pause 2 seconds]
That was the last hole. The preterite is finished — every family, every person, regular and strong alike, with nothing outstanding.
[pause 2 seconds]
It has taken a long stretch of this book to build, and it is the largest single structure in it. The review that named this debt out loud can now be read without a caveat.
