# ढळणो (ḍhaḷṇo) — to go down (of the sun); to slope

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C229-LIGHT229-05, MW-LEX-C230-SUN230-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to shine, then the Marwadi for to be scorching.

## You'll want to know: ढळणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C230-SUN230-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ढळणो** — *ḍhaḷṇo* — "to go down (of the sun); to slope".

> सूरज आथण ढळै है।
>
> *sūraj āthaṇ ḍhaḷai hai.*
>
> The sun goes down in the evening.

Past (it …): वो सूरज ढळ्यो, *vo sūraj ḍhaḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढळणो** (*ḍhaḷṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhalati*, "bends over, falls"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *ḍhalaï*, "falls, declines" (CDIAL 5581).
