## You'll want to know: سینکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C481-BETTER481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سینکنا** — *seṅknā* — "to warm a sore place; to toast".

> دادی گرم پانی سے گھٹنا سینکتی ہیں۔
>
> *dādī garm pānī se ghuṭnā seṅktī haiṅ.*
>
> My grandmother warms her knee with hot water.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کمر سینکی, *us ne kamar seṅkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سینکنا** (*seṅknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sẽknā*, "to warm, to foment, to toast", under *sēkk*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13581).
