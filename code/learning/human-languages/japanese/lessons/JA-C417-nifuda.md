## You'll want to know: にふだ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C417-TENIMOTSU417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**にふだ** — *nifuda* — "a luggage tag".

> **にふだに なまえを かいて ください。** — *nifuda ni namae o kaite kudasai* — "Please write your name on the luggage tag."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にふだ** (*nifuda*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ni*, "load" and *fuda*, "tag"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
