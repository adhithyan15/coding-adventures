Punjabi, chapter 280: Small Change.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਭਾਨ (bhān) — small change; coins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to exchange, then the Punjabi for a complaint.

You'll want to know: ਭਾਨ.
ਭਾਨ — bhān — "small change; coins".
ਕੀ ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ ਭਾਨ ਹੈ? kī tuhāḍe kol bhān hai? Do you have small change?
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਭਾਨ (bhān) is inherited from Sanskrit bhājana, "dividing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bhāṇ, "small change for a rupee", under it (CDIAL 9437).

Lesson 2 of 7.
ਭਨਾਉਣਾ (bhanāuṇā) — to break a note into change.

You'll want to know: ਭਨਾਉਣਾ.
ਭਨਾਉਣਾ — bhanāuṇā — "to break a note into change".
ਮੈਂ ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੌ ਦਾ ਨੋਟ ਭਨਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ baiṅk vich sau dā noṭ bhanāundā hāṁ. I break a hundred note into change at the bank.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਨੋਟ ਭਨਾਇਆ, maiṁ noṭ bhanāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਭਨਾਉਣਾ (bhanāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit bhajyate, "is broken"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bhanāuṇā, "to change (money)", under it (CDIAL 9361).

Lesson 3 of 7.
ਬਚਣਾ (bachṇā) — to be left over; to remain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for small change, then the Punjabi for to break a note into change.

You'll want to know: ਬਚਣਾ.
ਬਚਣਾ — bachṇā — "to be left over; to remain".
ਸੌਦੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਕੁਝ ਪੈਸੇ ਬਚਦੇ ਹਨ। saude ton bāad mere kol kujh paise bachde han. After the shopping I have some money left.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਰੋਟੀ ਬਚੀ, oh roṭī bachī.

The word, taken apart.
ਬਚਣਾ (bachṇā) goes back to Sanskrit vañcati, "moves; escapes from"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bacṇā, "to escape", under it, as a word taken from Hindi bacnā (CDIAL 11208).

Lesson 4 of 7.
ਪੁੱਗਣਾ (puggṇā) — to be affordable; to be worth it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to break a note into change, then the Punjabi for to be left over.

You'll want to know: ਪੁੱਗਣਾ.
ਪੁੱਗਣਾ — puggṇā — "to be affordable; to be worth it".
ਮਹਿੰਗਾ ਕਿਰਾਇਆ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੁੱਗਦਾ ਹੈ। mahiṅgā kirāiā mainū̃ nahīṁ puggdā hai. I cannot afford an expensive fare.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਸੌਦਾ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੁੱਗਿਆ, oh saudā mainū̃ puggiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੁੱਗਣਾ (puggṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit pūryate, "is filled"; CDIAL lists Punjabi puggṇā, "to be completed, reach", under it (CDIAL 8342). The sense "to be affordable" is not given there.

Lesson 5 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to be left over, then the Punjabi for to be affordable.

You'll want to know: ਬਾਕੀ.
ਬਾਕੀ — bākī — "the rest; remaining".
ਬਾਕੀ ਪੈਸੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਜੇਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਨ। bākī paise merī jeb vich han. The rest of the money is in my pocket.

The word, taken apart.
ਬਾਕੀ (bākī) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic bāqī, "remaining".
