## You'll want to know: چیچک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C474-SPREAD474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**چیچک** — *chechak* — "smallpox".

> ٹیکے کی وجہ سے اب چیچک نہیں ہوتی۔
>
> *ṭīke kī vajah se ab chechak nahīṅ hotī.*
>
> Because of vaccination there is no smallpox now.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چیچک** (*chechak*) is usually traced to Persian *chēchak*, "smallpox".
