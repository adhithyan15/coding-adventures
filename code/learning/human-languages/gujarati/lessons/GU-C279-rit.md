# રીત (rīt) — a way; a manner

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C278-SO-RESULT-01, GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "it was raining, so we stayed at home". (*varsād paḍto hato, eṭle ame ghare rahyā*.) Then "by bus". (**બસમાં**.)

## You'll want to know: રીત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C279-RIT-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-AAVVU, GU-PRON-TE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C01-SAARUN-01] -->

**રીત** — *rīt* — "a way; a manner": the way a thing is done. It is feminine: **તેની રીત**, "his way".

> **તેની રીત સારી છે.**
>
> *tenī rīt sārī che.* — "His way of doing it is good."

**કેવી રીતે** (*kevī rīte*), "in what way", is how Gujarati says "how": **કેવી** asks "what kind of", agreeing with **રીત**, and **રીતે** takes the **-ે** of **સવારે**.

> **તે કેવી રીતે આવી?**
>
> *te kevī rīte āvī?* — "How did she come?"

The answer can be **બસમાં**: by bus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રીત** (*rīt*) goes back to Sanskrit *rīti*, "going, a course", and later "a way of doing things" (Monier-Williams). CDIAL's entry for *rīti* (CDIAL 10751) lists an older Gujarati *rī*, "method, manner"; **રીત** keeps the Sanskrit *-t*, so it is probably the learned form.
