## You'll want to know: आश्रयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C306-SHIBIRA306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्रयः** — *āśrayaḥ* — "shelter; refuge".

> वृद्धः शिबिरे आश्रयं याचते।
>
> *vṛddhaḥ śibire āśrayaṃ yācate.*
>
> The old man asks for shelter in the camp.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्रयः** (*āśrayaḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √śri, "to lean on, resort to"; MW gives *āśraya* as that on which anything rests or depends, a refuge (MW).
