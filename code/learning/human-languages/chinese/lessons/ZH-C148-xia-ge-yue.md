## You'll want to know: 下个月
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C148-XIAGEYUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**下个月** — *xià ge yuè* — "next month".

> **下个月我们去** *xià ge yuè wǒmen qù* — We're going next month.

The next lesson puts the last character in your hand. It is the *yuè* you already say for a month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**下个月** (*xià ge yuè*) is built from 下 'below; after', 个, the measure word, and 月 'month'. All three are inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX*, *kaH* and *ngjwot* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The Guangyun glosses *haeX* with, among other things, 'after' and 'the bottom', and quotes an old saying about the moon under *ngjwot*; 'next' in a row of months is assumed from 'after', not quoted.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C45-TODAY45-05, ZH-LEX-C147-HOUTIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C47-DATE47-03] -->

下 here is the month after this one. Like 明天 and 后天, 下个月 goes early in the sentence: before the verb, or before the person.

明年 says next year with 明; the month and the week take 下.
