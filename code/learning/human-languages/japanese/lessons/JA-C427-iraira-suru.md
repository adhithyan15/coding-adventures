## You'll want to know: いらいらする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C427-NERVES427-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いらいらする** — *iraira suru* — "to get irritated".

> **でんしゃが おくれて、いらいらしました。** — *densha ga okurete, iraira shimashita* — "The train was late, and I got irritated."

Polite **いらいらします**, *irairashimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ちちが いらいらしました。**, *chichi ga iraira shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いらいらする** (*iraira suru*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, *iraira*, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, made a verb with *suru*, "to do"; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
