## You'll want to know: चराना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C449-SIYAR449-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चराना** — *carānā* — "to take to graze; to graze (cattle)".

> एक लड़का नदी के किनारे बकरियाँ चरा रहा था।
>
> *ek laṛkā nadī ke kināre bakriyā̃ carā rahā thā.*
>
> A boy was grazing goats on the river bank.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने भेड़ें चराईं, *usne bheṛẽ carāīṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चराना** (*carānā*) is the causative partner of चरना, from Sanskrit *carati*, "moves, grazes" (CDIAL 4686), which lists Hindi *carānā*, "to graze" a herd.
