## Script — grass, a roof, a small tree
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-CHA-TEA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 茶

**Three pieces, top to bottom.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The grass piece, three strokes,** as in 菜: a horizontal, then two short verticals through it.

**Then 人, two strokes,** spread wide like a roof.

**At the foot, four strokes:** a horizontal, a vertical with a hook, then a short stroke falling to the left and a dot falling to the right, one on each side.

3 + 2 + 4 = nine.
