## You'll want to know: सौजन्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C461-SATPURUSA461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सौजन्यम्** — *saujanyam* — "goodness; kindness".

> तस्य सौजन्येन सर्वे प्रसन्नाः भवन्ति।
>
> *tasya saujanyena sarve prasannāḥ bhavanti.*
>
> Everyone is pleased by his kindness.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौजन्यम्** (*saujanyam*) is built on *su-jana*, "a good person" (*su*, "good", and **जनः**), with the lengthening of its first vowel; MW gives "goodness, kindness, benevolence" (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
