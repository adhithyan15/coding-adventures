## You'll want to know: la révision
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C377-REVISION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la révision** — "the revision; the service (of a car)".

> Pendant les révisions, je ne sors pas.
>
> *During revision, I do not go out.*

In the plural for exam revision: *les révisions*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*révision* is a learned borrowing from Latin *revīsiō* "a seeing again" (EtymDB), from *revidēre* "to go to see again", built on *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
