Arabic, chapter 378: Elections.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انتخاب (intikhāb) — an election.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a parliament, then the Arabic for a constitution.

You'll want to know: انتخاب.
انتخاب — intikhāb — "an election".
شارك الناس في الانتخاب بأعداد كبيرة. shāraka an-nās fī al-intikhāb bi-aʿdād kabīra. People took part in the election in large numbers.
More often plural: الانتخابات, al-intikhābāt, the elections.

The word, taken apart.
انتخاب (intikhāb) is built on the native Arabic root n-kh-b, "to choose, to select" (Lane). Intikhāb is the Form VIII verbal noun, a choosing out: an election.

Lesson 3 of 5.
صوت (ṣawwata) — to vote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an election, then the Arabic for to elect.

You'll want to know: صوت.
صوت — ṣawwata — "to vote".
صوتت أمي في الصباح الباكر. ṣawwatat ummī fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ al-bākir. My mother voted early in the morning.
Present: يصوت, yuṣawwit.
صوت (ṣawwata) لـ, voted for. The noun صوت (ṣawwata), ṣawt, voice, also means a vote.

The word, taken apart.
صوت (ṣawwata) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-w-t, "to make a sound" (Lane). It is Form II, to raise one's voice: to cast a vote.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مرشح (murashshaḥ) — a candidate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to vote, then the Arabic for a voter.

You'll want to know: مرشح.
مرشح — murashshaḥ — "a candidate".
تكلم المرشح عن خطته. takallama al-murashshaḥ ʿan khiṭṭatih. The candidate talked about his plan.

The word, taken apart.
مرشح (murashshaḥ) is built on the native Arabic root r-sh-ḥ, "to sweat, to ooze" (Lane). Lane gives Form II for a mother training her young; murashshaḥ is one made ready for a post: a candidate.
