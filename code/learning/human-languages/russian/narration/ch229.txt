The word, taken apart.
секретарь (sekretár) — known from 1614; it came through German Sekretär or Polish sekretarz from Latin sēcrētārius, "a keeper of secrets" (Vasmer).

Lesson 4 of 5.
программист (programmíst) — a programmer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an accountant, then the Russian for a manager.

You'll want to know: программист.
программист (programmíst) — "a programmer".

The word, taken apart.
программист (programmíst) is программа, "a program", with -ист, "one who works at". программа goes back to Greek prógramma, "a public notice", from prográphein, "to write before".

Lesson 5 of 5.
преподаватель (prepodavátel) — a lecturer, a teacher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a manager, then the Russian for a programmer.

You'll want to know: преподаватель.
преподаватель (prepodavátel) — "a lecturer, a teacher".

The word, taken apart.
преподаватель (prepodavátel) is преподавать, "to teach", with -тель, "one who does it". The verb is пре- and по- with давать, "to give". Vasmer takes давать from дать, "to give", kin to Lithuanian dovanà, "a gift".
