## You'll want to know: упоминать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C283-UTVERZHDAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**упоминать** (*upominát*) — "to mention".

> В письме она упомянула твоё имя.
>
> *V pismé oná upomyanúla tvoyó ímya.*
>
> In the letter she mentioned your name.

Imperfective; perfective *упомянуть* (*upomyanút*). It takes the accusative or *о* + prepositional.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*упоминать* is built on *у-* and the Slavic root of *помнить* "to remember" and *память* "memory": to bring to mind. EtymDB lists no parent.
