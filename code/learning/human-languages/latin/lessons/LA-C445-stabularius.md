## You'll want to know: stabulārius, stabulāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C445-DEVERTICULUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**stabulārius, stabulāriī** — "a stable-keeper, an innkeeper (of a stable inn)".

> Stabulārius equōs viātōrum cūrat.
>
> *The stable-keeper looks after the travellers' horses.*

Second declension, masculine: *stabulārius, stabulāriī m.* The keeper of a *stabulum*, the cheapest kind of roadside lodging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stabulārius* is built on *stabulum* 'stable, lodging' with the suffix *-ārius* 'one who works with' (Lewis & Short).
