## You'll want to know: o orvalho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C464-GEADA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o orvalho** — "the dew".

> O orvalho molha os pés de manhã cedo.
>
> *The dew wets your feet early in the morning.*

Drops on the grass at dawn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orvalho* is usually traced to Latin *rōs, rōris* 'dew' (Lewis & Short), but the details are not settled.
