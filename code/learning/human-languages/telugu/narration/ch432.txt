Telugu, chapter 432: Ideals and Standing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నీతి (nīti) — ethics; a moral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for effective, then the Telugu for to test out.

You'll want to know: నీతి.
నీతి — nīti — "ethics; a moral".
ఆ కథలో మంచి నీతి ఉంది. ā kathalō mañci nīti undi. That story has a good moral.
నీతి (nīti) కథ, a fable with a moral; నీతిగా, honestly.

The word, taken apart.
నీతి (nīti) is borrowed from Sanskrit nīti, "conduct" (CDIAL 7546), and especially "right or wise conduct" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Telugu for to test out, then the Telugu for ethics.

You'll want to know: ఆదర్శం.
ఆదర్శం — ādarśaṁ — "an ideal; a model".
మా అమ్మే నాకు ఆదర్శం. mā ammē nāku ādarśaṁ. My mother is my role model.
Before a noun, ఆదర్శ: ఆదర్శ గ్రామం, a model village.

The word, taken apart.
ఆదర్శం (ādarśaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit ādarśa, "a mirror" (CDIAL 1143); Telugu uses it for a model to follow.

ఆ పని వల్ల కుటుంబ ప్రతిష్ఠ పెరిగింది. ā pani valla kuṭumba pratiṣṭha perigindi. That deed raised the family’s standing.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an ideal, then the Telugu for prestige.

You'll want to know: విశ్వసనీయత.
విశ్వసనీయత — viśvasanīyata — "credibility; reliability".
ఆ పత్రిక విశ్వసనీయత తగ్గింది. ā patrika viśvasanīyata taggindi. That paper’s credibility has fallen.
విశ్వసనీయమైన, reliable, trustworthy; of the same family as విశ్వసించు.

The word, taken apart.
విశ్వసనీయత (viśvasanīyata) is built on Sanskrit viśvasanīya, "to be trusted or relied on, reliable, credible" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త.

Lesson 5 of 5.
అవగాహన (avagāhana) — awareness; understanding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for prestige, then the Telugu for credibility.

You'll want to know: అవగాహన.
అవగాహన — avagāhana — "awareness; understanding".
జనంలో ఆరోగ్యం మీద అవగాహన పెరిగింది. janaṁlō ārōgyaṁ mīda avagāhana perigindi. People’s awareness of health has grown.
అవగాహన (avagāhana) కల్పించు, to raise awareness.

The word, taken apart.
అవగాహన (avagāhana) is borrowed from Sanskrit avagāhana, "immersion, bathing" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for being steeped in a subject, understanding.
