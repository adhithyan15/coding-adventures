## You'll want to know: gurges, gurgitis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C483-STAGNUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**gurges, gurgitis** — "a whirlpool, an abyss of water; a flood".

> Nauta in gurgitem cadit, sed amīcī eum servant.
>
> *A sailor falls into a whirlpool, but his friends save him.*

Third declension, masculine: *gurges, gurgitis m.* Also a glutton, one who swallows everything. English *regurgitate* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gurges* is inherited and Lewis & Short link it with *gula* 'throat'; EtymDB lists Greek *gorgyra* 'underground drain' as a cousin. English *regurgitate* comes from a word built on it.
