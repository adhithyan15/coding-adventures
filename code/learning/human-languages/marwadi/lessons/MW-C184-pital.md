## You'll want to know: पीतळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C184-OKHLI184-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पीतळ** — *pītaḷ* — "brass".

> पीतळ पीळो है।
>
> *pītaḷ pīḷo hai.*
>
> Brass is yellow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पीतळ** (*pītaḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pittala*, "brass"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *pitaḷ* and Hindi *pītal* (CDIAL 8184). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
