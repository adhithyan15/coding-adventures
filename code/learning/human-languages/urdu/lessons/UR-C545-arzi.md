## You'll want to know: عارضی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C545-MUSTAQIL545-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عارضی** — *ārzī* — "temporary".

> اگر یہ نوکری عارضی ہے تو میں نہیں لوں گا۔
>
> *agar yih naukrī ārzī hai to maiṅ nahīṅ lūṅ gā.*
>
> If this job is temporary, I won't take it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عارضی** (*ārzī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿāriḍī*, "passing, accidental".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
