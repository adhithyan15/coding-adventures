Portuguese, chapter 429: No quarto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o berço — the cot, the cradle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to put up, then the Portuguese for the doorman.

You'll want to know: o berço.
o berço — "the cot, the cradle".
Pedimos um berço para o bebé. We asked for a cot for the baby.
Nascer em berço de ouro — to be born with a silver spoon in your mouth.

The word, taken apart.
berço comes from Vulgar Latin berciolum 'cradle' (EtymDB), probably from a Gaulish word; the details are not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
o secador — the hair dryer; the dryer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the cot, then the Portuguese for the pillowcase.

You'll want to know: o secador.
o secador — "the hair dryer; the dryer".
O secador da casa de banho não funciona. The hair dryer in the bathroom does not work.
O secador de cabelo in full; a máquina de secar dries clothes.

The word, taken apart.
secador is built on secar 'to dry', which is inherited from Latin siccāre, from siccus 'dry' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
o roupão — the bathrobe, the dressing gown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the hair dryer, then the Portuguese for the coat hanger.

You'll want to know: o roupão.
o roupão — "the bathrobe, the dressing gown".
No hotel há um roupão branco para cada hóspede. At the hotel there is a white bathrobe for each guest.
Built on a roupa with -ão.

The word, taken apart.
roupão is built on roupa 'clothes' with the big-size ending -ão; roupa comes from Gothic raupa (EtymDB).
