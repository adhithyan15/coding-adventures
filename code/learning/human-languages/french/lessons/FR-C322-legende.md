## You'll want to know: la légende
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C322-RECIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la légende** — "the legend; the caption".

> Dans la légende, un dragon vivait dans ce lac.
>
> *In the legend, a dragon lived in this lake.*

Under a photo or a map, *la légende* is the caption or the key.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*légende* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *legenda* "things to be read", from *legere* "to read" (Lewis & Short).
