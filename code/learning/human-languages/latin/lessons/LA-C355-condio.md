## You'll want to know: condiō, condīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C355-CONDIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**condiō, condīre** — "to season, to spice; to pickle".

> Coquus carnem pipere et sale condīvit.
>
> *The cook seasoned the meat with pepper and salt.*

*condiō, condīre, condīvī, condītum*. First to preserve fruit in vinegar or salt, then to flavour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**condiō** is taken by Lewis & Short as a side form of *condō*, "to put away, to store": first to put fruit away in pickle, then to season.
