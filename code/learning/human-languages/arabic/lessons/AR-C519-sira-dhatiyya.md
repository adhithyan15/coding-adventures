## You'll want to know: سيرة ذاتية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C519-WAZIFA519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سيرة ذاتية** — *sīra dhātiyya* — "a CV; a life story".

> أرسلت سيرتي الذاتية إلى الشركة.
>
> *arsaltu sīratī adh-dhātiyya ilā ash-sharika.*
>
> I sent my CV to the company.

سيرة alone is a life story, a biography.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سيرة ذاتية** (*sīra dhātiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-y-r*, "to go, to travel" (Lane): a *sīra* is a way of going, the path of a life. *Dhātiyya* is "of the self", from *dhāt*, self.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
