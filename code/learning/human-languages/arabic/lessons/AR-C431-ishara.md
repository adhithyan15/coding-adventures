## You'll want to know: إشارة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C431-MURUR431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**إشارة** — *ishāra* — "a signal; traffic lights".

> وقفت السيارة عند الإشارة الحمراء.
>
> *waqafat as-sayyāra ʿinda al-ishāra al-ḥamrāʾ.*
>
> The car stopped at the red light.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إشارة** (*ishāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-w-r*, "to point, to make a sign" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form IV *ashāra*.
