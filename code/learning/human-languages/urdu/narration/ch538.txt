Urdu, chapter 538: In Two Minds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تذبذب (tazabzub) — indecision; wavering.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a touchstone, then the Urdu for to select.

You'll want to know: تذبذب.
تذبذب — tazabzub — "indecision; wavering".
اگر آپ تذبذب میں ہیں تو کل بتائیے۔ agar āp tazabzub meṅ haiṅ to kal batāiye. If you can't decide, let me know tomorrow.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تذبذب (tazabzub) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tadhabdhub, "dangling to and fro".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ڈگمگانا (ḍagmagānā) — to totter; to wobble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for hesitant, then the Urdu for a hunch.

You'll want to know: ڈگمگانا.
ڈگمگانا — ḍagmagānā — "to totter; to wobble".
اگر سیڑھی ڈگمگائی تو میں گر جاؤں گا۔ agar sīṛhī ḍagmagāī to maiṅ gir jāūṅ gā. If the ladder wobbles, I will fall.
Past (he …): وہ بوڑھا آدمی ڈگمگایا, voh būṛhā ādmī ḍagmagāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ڈگمگانا (ḍagmagānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins ḍagmagḍagānā, "to tremble", and ḍigmig, "trembling", under ḍag, "tremble", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5522).
