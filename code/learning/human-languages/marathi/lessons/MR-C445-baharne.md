## You'll want to know: बहरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C445-ANKUR445-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बहरणे** — *baharṇe* — "to blossom".

> आंब्याचं झाड फुलांनी बहरलं.
>
> *āmbyācaṁ jhāḍ phulānnī baharlaṁ.*
>
> The mango tree blossomed all over.

Past (he …): तो बहरला, *to baharlā*.

A whole tree in flower बहरतो; the noun is बहर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहरणे** (*baharṇe*) is built on **बहर**, "full bloom", a loan usually traced to Persian *bahār*, "spring".
