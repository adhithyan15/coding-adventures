Telugu, chapter 241: To swallow, To bite, To breathe in, To sneeze, To vomit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
మింగు (miṅgu) — to swallow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to push, then the Telugu for to throw.

You'll want to know: మింగు.
మింగు — miṅgu — "to swallow".

The word, taken apart.
మింగు (miṅgu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil miḻuṅku and Kannada miṅgu (DEDR 4866). DEDR lists Telugu miṅgu, "to swallow".
