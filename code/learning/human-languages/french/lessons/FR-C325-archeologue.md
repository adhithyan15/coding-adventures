## You'll want to know: l'archéologue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C325-PASSE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'archéologue** — "the archaeologist".

> Les archéologues ont trouvé des pièces très anciennes.
>
> *The archaeologists found some very old coins.*

The science is *l'archéologie*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*archéologue* is a learned word built from Greek *arkhaîos* "ancient" and *-logos* "one who studies" (EtymDB lists no parent).
