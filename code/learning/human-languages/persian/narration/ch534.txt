Persian, chapter 534: Furnishing a Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مبلمان (moblemân) — furniture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be broadcast, then the Persian for to stare.

You'll want to know: مبلمان.
مبلمان (moblemân) — "furniture".
آنها برای خانه جدید مبلمان خریدند. ânhâ barâ-ye khâne-ye jadid moblemân kharidand. They bought furniture for their new house.
A collective noun; mobl is one sofa or armchair.

The word, taken apart.
moblemân was borrowed from French meublement, 'furnishing' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
آباژور (âbâzhur) — a lamp (with a shade); a lampshade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to stare, then the Persian for furniture.

You'll want to know: آباژور.
آباژور (âbâzhur) — "a lamp (with a shade); a lampshade".
کنار تخت یک آباژور کوچک است. kenâr-e takht yek âbâzhur-e kuchek ast. There's a small lamp beside the bed.
A table or floor lamp with a shade.

The word, taken apart.
âbâzhur was borrowed from French abat-jour, which EtymDB builds from abattre, 'to throw down', and jour, 'day, daylight', itself from Latin diurnum (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
قالیچه (qâliche) — a rug, a small carpet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for furniture, then the Persian for a lamp.

You'll want to know: قالیچه.
قالیچه (qâliche) — "a rug, a small carpet".
جلوی در یک قالیچه قرمز است. jelo-ye dar yek qâliche-ye qermez ast. There's a red rug in front of the door.
qâli 'carpet' + the diminutive -che.

The word, taken apart.
qâliche is built on qâli, 'carpet', with the native diminutive -che. EtymDB lists qâli without a source, and its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کوسن (kusan) — a cushion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a lamp, then the Persian for a rug.

You'll want to know: کوسن.
کوسن (kusan) — "a cushion".
او کوسنها را روی مبل چید. u kusanhâ râ ru-ye mobl chid. She arranged the cushions on the sofa.
Plural kusanhâ.

The word, taken apart.
kusan is a modern loan, usually traced to French coussin, 'cushion'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
