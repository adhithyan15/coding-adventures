# জনগণ (jônôgôn) — the people; the public

## You'll want to know: জনগণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C344-RASHTRO344-05]; assesses=[] -->

**জনগণ** — *jônôgôn* — "the people; the public".

> সরকার জনগণের জন্য কাজ করে।
>
> *sôrkār jônôgôner jônyô kāj kôre.*
>
> The government works for the people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জনগণ** (*jônôgôn*) is Sanskrit *jana*, people, with *gaṇa*, a multitude (Monier-Williams): the whole body of the people, the public a government serves.
