## You'll want to know: 难题
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C495-MARKS495-05]; assesses=[] -->

**难题** — *nántí* — "hard problem; difficult question".

> **这个难题我想了一个小时** *zhège nántí wǒ xiǎng le yí ge xiǎoshí* — I spent an hour thinking about this hard question.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**难题** (*nántí*) is built from 难 'hard' and 题 'a heading', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nan* and *dej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'hard to do' and the second 'a written heading'.
