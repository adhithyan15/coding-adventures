## You'll want to know: ふやす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C338-MUDA338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふやす** — *fuyasu* — "to increase something; to add to".

> **けんこうの ために、やさいを ふやした ほうが いいです。** — *kenkō no tame ni, yasai o fuyashita hō ga ii desu* — "For your health, you should eat more vegetables."

Polite **ふやします**, *fuyashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが やさいを ふやしました。**, *chichi ga yasai o fuyashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふやす** (*fuyasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
