## You'll want to know: shàng'àn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C445-SEA445-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shàng'àn* — "to go ashore".

> *xiàwǔ sì diǎn wǒmen shàng'àn zuò chē huí jiǔdiàn* — At four in the afternoon we go ashore and take the bus back to the hotel.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shàng'àn* is built from 上 'to go up' and *àn* 'a bank', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangX* and *nganH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to climb' and the second as the high edge of the water.
