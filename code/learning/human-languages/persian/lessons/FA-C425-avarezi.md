## You'll want to know: عوارضی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C425-OTOBAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عوارضی** (*'avârezi*) — "a toll booth, a toll gate".

> در عوارضی ماشینها کمی صبر میکنند.
>
> *Dar 'avârezi mâshin-hâ kami sabr mikonand.*
>
> At the toll gate the cars wait a little.

Built on *'avârez* 'tolls, charges', an Arabic broken plural, + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'avârezi* is built from *'avârez* 'tolls, charges', a loan usually traced to the Arabic plural *ʿawāriḍ* 'things that come up, extra charges', and the Persian ending *-i*. EtymDB does not list it.
