## You'll want to know: علامت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C363-ANDESHA363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**علامت** — *alāmat* — "a symptom; a sign (of something)".

> اگر بخار ہے تو یہ بیماری کی علامت ہے۔
>
> *agar bukhār hai to yih bīmārī kī alāmat hai.*
>
> If there is a fever, it is a sign of illness.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**علامت** (*alāmat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿalāma*, "a mark, a sign".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
