## You'll want to know: अवधी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C555-GHAI555-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधी** — *avadhī* — "a span of time; time left".

> परीक्षेला आता फक्त एक आठवडा अवधी आहे.
>
> *parīkṣelā ātā phakta ek āṭhavḍā avadhī āhe.*
>
> There is only a week left before the exam.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधी** (*avadhī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *avadhi*, "a limit, a term" (Monier-Williams).
