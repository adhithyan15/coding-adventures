# به گفته (be gofte-ye) — according to

## You'll want to know: به گفته
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C311-MANBA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**به گفته** (*be gofte-ye*) — "according to".

> به گفته پلیس، کسی آسیب ندید.
>
> *Be gofte-ye polis, kasi âsib nadid.*
>
> According to the police, nobody was hurt.

It takes the ezafe: *be gofte-ye u* "according to him". *gofte* "what was said" is the participle of *goftan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*be gofte-ye* is built from *be* "to" and *gofte* "what was said", the participle of *goftan* "to say". All of it is native Persian.
