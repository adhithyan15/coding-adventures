Marathi, chapter 273: Memory, Temperament, Behaviour, Respect, Pity.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आठवण (āṭhvaṇ) — a memory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for responsibility, then the Marathi for an opportunity.

You'll want to know: आठवण.
आठवण — āṭhvaṇ — "a memory".

The word, taken apart.
आठवण (āṭhvaṇ) is built on āṭhavṇẽ, "to come to mind", which comes from Sanskrit āsthāpayati, "causes to stay, fixes into" (CDIAL 1516).
