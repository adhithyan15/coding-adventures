## You'll want to know: शेगडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C526-OTA526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शेगडी** — *śegḍī* — "a stove".

> आईने शेगडीवर दूध ठेवलं.
>
> *āīne śegḍīvar dūdh ṭhevlaṁ.*
>
> Mother put the milk on the stove.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शेगडी** (*śegḍī*) is the word for a cooking stove; its origin is not settled.
