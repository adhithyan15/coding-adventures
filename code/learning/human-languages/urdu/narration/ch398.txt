Urdu, chapter 398: Flights.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پرواز (parvāz) — a flight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to turn (something), then the Urdu for to show (someone) around.

You'll want to know: پرواز.
پرواز — parvāz — "a flight".
ہماری پرواز رات دس بجے ہے۔ hamārī parvāz rāt das baje hai. Our flight is at ten at night.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
پرواز (parvāz) is usually traced to Persian parvāz, "flying, a flight".

Lesson 2 of 5.
طیارہ (tayyārā) — an aircraft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to show (someone) around, then the Urdu for a flight.

You'll want to know: طیارہ.
طیارہ — tayyārā — "an aircraft".
طیارہ بادلوں کے اوپر اڑ رہا تھا۔ tayyārā bādaloṅ ke ūpar uṛ rahā thā. The aircraft was flying above the clouds.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
طیارہ (tayyārā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṭayyāra, "an aeroplane", from a word for flying; the news prefers it to ہوائی جہاز.

Lesson 3 of 5.
نشست (nashist) — a seat (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a flight, then the Urdu for an aircraft.

You'll want to know: نشست.
نشست — nashist — "a seat (formal)".
میری نشست کھڑکی کے پاس ہے۔ merī nashist khiṛkī ke pās hai. My seat is by the window.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
نشست (nashist) is usually traced to Persian nishast, "a sitting, a seat", from the verb nishastan, "to sit"; tickets and announcements use it where talk says سیٹ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کسنا (kasnā) — to tighten; to fasten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an aircraft, then the Urdu for a seat (formal).

You'll want to know: کسنا.
کسنا — kasnā — "to tighten; to fasten".
پرواز سے پہلے بیلٹ کس لیجیے۔ parvāz se pehle belṭ kas lījiye. Please fasten your seat belt before the flight.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے رسی کسی, us ne rassī kasī.

The word, taken apart.
کسنا (kasnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin kasnā, "to tighten", under Sanskrit kárṣati, "draws, pulls" (CDIAL 2908).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ملتوی (multavī) — postponed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a seat (formal), then the Urdu for to tighten.

You'll want to know: ملتوی.
ملتوی — multavī — "postponed".
طوفان کی وجہ سے پرواز ملتوی ہو گئی۔ tūfān kī vajah se parvāz multavī ho gaī. The flight was put off because of the storm.

The word, taken apart.
ملتوی (multavī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic multawī, "turned aside, bent"; the sense "put off till later" probably grew in Persian and Urdu.
