## You'll want to know: غرامة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C352-MUKHALAFA352-02]; assesses=[] -->

**غرامة** — *gharāma* — "a fine, a penalty fee".

> كانت الغرامة خمسين دينارا.
>
> *kānat al-gharāma khamsīn dīnāran.*
>
> The fine was fifty dinars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غرامة** (*gharāma*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-r-m* (Lane), "to pay a debt or a fine"; *gharām*, passion, is the same root, a love that will not let go.
