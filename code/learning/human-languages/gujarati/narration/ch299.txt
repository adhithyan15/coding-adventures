Gujarati, chapter 299: Shouts in the Street.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચીસ (cīs) — a scream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for consolation, then the Gujarati for to bear.

You'll want to know: ચીસ.
ચીસ — cīs — "a scream".
અચાનક મીરાના મોઢામાંથી ચીસ નીકળી ગઈ. acānak Mīrānā moḍhāmā̃thī cīs nīkḷī gaī. Suddenly a scream broke from Mira.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચીસ (cīs) comes from cīssa, "a sharp pain", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists Gujarati cīs, "a scream" (CDIAL 4847): the cry that pain draws out. A scream નીકળે છે, comes out of you: ચીસ નીકળી ગઈ.

A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બૂમ (būm) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. A shout is given with મારવું: બૂમ મારી, called out loudly.

Lesson 3 of 5.
રોકવું (rokvũ) — to bring to a stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a scream, then the Gujarati for a shout.

You'll want to know: રોકવું.
રોકવું — rokvũ — "to bring to a stop".
સિપાઈએ ગાડી રોકી. sipāīe gāḍī rokī. The constable stopped the car.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગાડી રોકી, teṇe gāḍī rokī.

The word, taken apart.
રોકવું (rokvũ) comes from rōkk, "stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 10827). It is the doing partner of રોકાવું, to stay on: સિપાઈએ ગાડી રોકી.

You'll want to know: અટકાવવું.
અટકાવવું — aṭkāvvũ — "to hold back; to prevent".
મીરાએ હાથ પકડીને બાળકને અટકાવ્યું. Mīrāe hāth pakḍīne bāḷakne aṭkāvyũ. Mira took the child's hand and held him back.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને અટકાવ્યું, teṇe bāḷakne aṭkāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
અટકાવવું (aṭkāvvũ) is the causative of અટકવું, to stop, from aṭṭakk, "obstruct, stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 182): to make someone stop before something happens.

Lesson 5 of 5.
સલામત (salāmat) — safe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to bring to a stop, then the Gujarati for to hold back.

You'll want to know: સલામત.
સલામત — salāmat — "safe".
છેવટે બધા સલામત હતા. chevṭe badhā salāmat hatā. In the end everyone was safe.

The word, taken apart.
સલામત (salāmat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic salāma, "safety, soundness". It describes people and things out of danger: બધા સલામત છે, everyone is safe.
