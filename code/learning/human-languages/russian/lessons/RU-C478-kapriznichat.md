## You'll want to know: капризничать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C478-STUBBORN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**капризничать** (*kapríznichat*) — "to play up (of a child)".

> Вечером дочь капризничает, потому что устала.
>
> *Vécherom doch kapríznichayet, potomú chto ustála.*
>
> In the evening my daughter plays up because she's tired.

Imperfective, colloquial; present капризничаю.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*капризничать* is built on *каприз* 'whim', which was borrowed from French *caprice*, from Italian *capriccio* (EtymDB).
