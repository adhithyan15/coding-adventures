## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-OU-03]; assesses=[FR-GRAMMAR-INTONATION-01, FR-GRAMMAR-ESTCEQUE-02] -->

> **où** — where

> **Où est la gare ?** — Where is the station?
> **Tu vas où ?** — Where are you going?

Both are correct. The question word can open the sentence or sit at the end where
the answer would go, and the second is what you will hear far more in speech.

**Now the accent, which is not decoration.** French has two words spelled *o-u*:

- **ou** — *or*
- **où** — *where*

The grave accent is the **only** difference in writing, and in speech there is no
difference at all: both are *oo*. This is the one place in French where an accent
does nothing to the sound and everything to the meaning, which is why it is never
optional.

You now hold the first question word, and with it a question you can actually use
on the first day in a French city.
