## You'll want to know: l'inflazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C395-RISPARMIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'inflazione** — "the inflation".

> Con l'inflazione alta, i soldi valgono meno.
>
> *With high inflation, money is worth less.*

Literally a swelling: prices blow up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inflazione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnflātiō* "a blowing into, a swelling" (Lewis & Short), from *īnflāre* "to blow into", built on *flāre* "to blow"; the economic sense probably came through English.
