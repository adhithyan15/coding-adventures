## You'll want to know: yuǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C62-FAST62-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yuǎn* — "far".

> *Běijīng hěn yuǎn* — Beijing is a long way away.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuǎn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *hjwonX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'distant'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
