## You'll want to know: پاداش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C378-DASTMOZD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پاداش** (*pâdâsh*) — "a bonus, a reward".

> آخر سال همه کارمندها پاداش گرفتند.
>
> *âkhar-e sâl hame-ye kârmandhâ pâdâsh gereftand.*
>
> At the end of the year all the employees got a bonus.

Also a reward in a story; its opposite is *mojâzât*, a punishment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پاداش** is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian *pādāšn* "reward, retribution", what is given back for a deed.
