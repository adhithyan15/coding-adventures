## You'll want to know: تفاوض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C386-TAAWUN386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تفاوض** — *tafāwaḍa* — "to negotiate".

> تفاوض العمال مع المدير.
>
> *tafāwaḍa al-ʿummāl maʿa al-mudīr.*
>
> The workers negotiated with the manager.

Present: يتفاوض, *yatafāwaḍ*.

The noun مفاوضات, *mufāwaḍāt*, negotiations.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفاوض** (*tafāwaḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-w-ḍ*, "to entrust; to share" (Lane). It is Form VI, to consult with one another: to negotiate.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
