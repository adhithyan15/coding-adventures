## You'll want to know: بامداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C305-SEPIDEDAM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بامداد** (*bâmdâd*) — "early morning, the small hours".

> زلزله ساعت چهار بامداد اتفاق افتاد.
>
> *Zelzele sâ'at-e chahâr-e bâmdâd ettefâq oftâd.*
>
> The earthquake happened at four in the morning.

The news gives night-time hours with *bâmdâd*: *sâ'at-e se-ye bâmdâd* "3 a.m.".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâmdâd* is inherited from Middle Persian *bāmdād*, "dawn, morning" (EtymDB).
