## You'll want to know: schwanger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C296-GEBURT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**schwanger** — "pregnant".

> Meine Schwester war im Sommer schwanger.
>
> *My sister was pregnant in the summer.*

*Schwanger werden* — to get pregnant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schwanger* is inherited from Middle High German *swanger*, Old High German *swangar* (EtymDB); its origin beyond that is not settled.
