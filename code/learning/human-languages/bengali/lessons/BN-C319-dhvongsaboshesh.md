# ধ্বংসাবশেষ (dhvôngsābôshesh) — ruins; remains

## You'll want to know: ধ্বংসাবশেষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C319-PURATON319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ধ্বংসাবশেষ** — *dhvôngsābôshesh* — "ruins; remains".

> নদীর পাশে একটা পুরনো শহরের ধ্বংসাবশেষ আছে।
>
> *nôdīr pāshe ekṭā purôno shôhôrer dhvôngsābôshesh āchhe.*
>
> Beside the river are the ruins of an old city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধ্বংসাবশেষ** (*dhvôngsābôshesh*) joins **ধ্বংস**, ruin, to Sanskrit *avaśeṣa*, 'what is left' (Monier-Williams), the word in **অবশেষে**: what is left of a ruin.
