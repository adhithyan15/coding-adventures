## You'll want to know: ਗਾਲ੍ਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C316-QUARREL316-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਲ੍ਹ** — *gālh* — "a swear word".

> ਉਸ ਨੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਗਾਲ੍ਹ ਕੱਢੀ।
>
> *us ne mainū̃ gālh kaḍḍhī.*
>
> He swore at me.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਲ੍ਹ** (*gālh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gāli*, "abusive speech"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gāl* and *gālī*, "abuse", under it (CDIAL 4145).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
