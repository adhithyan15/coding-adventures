# বেঞ্চ (bench) — a bench

## You'll want to know: বেঞ্চ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C385-GHONTA385-05]; assesses=[] -->

**বেঞ্চ** — *bench* — "a bench".

> প্ল্যাটফর্মের বেঞ্চে বসে আমরা দুই ঘণ্টা অপেক্ষা করলাম।
>
> *plæṭphôrmer benche bôse āmrā dui ghônṭā ôpekkhā kôrlām.*
>
> We sat on a bench on the platform and waited for two hours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেঞ্চ** (*bench*) is borrowed from English *bench*; ঞ্চ is the n-ch joined letter.
