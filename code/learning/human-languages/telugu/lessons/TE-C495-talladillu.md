# తల్లడిల్లు (tallaḍillu) — to be distraught

## You'll want to know: తల్లడిల్లు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C495-BHAYAM495-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తల్లడిల్లు** — *tallaḍillu* — "to be distraught".

> బాబు కనిపించలేదు, అమ్మ తల్లడిల్లింది.
>
> *bābu kanipiñcalēdu, amma tallaḍillindi.*
>
> The little boy could not be found; his mother was distraught.

Past (I …): తల్లడిల్లాను, *tallaḍillānu*.

A strong verb for being torn with worry or grief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తల్లడిల్లు** (*tallaḍillu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3104 lists it, with *tallaḍamu*, "turmoil, anxiety", beside Kannada *tallaṇa*, "agitation, alarm, grief".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
