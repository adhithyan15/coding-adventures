## You'll want to know: ઓછાબોલું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C463-VACAL463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓછાબોલું** — *ochābolũ* — "quiet; of few words".

> અરુણ ઓછાબોલો છે, પણ ઘણું સમજે છે.
>
> *Aruṇ ochābolo che, paṇ ghaṇũ samje che.*
>
> Arun is a man of few words, but he understands a lot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓછાબોલું** (*ochābolũ*) is built from **ઓછું** (*ochũ*), "less", and the stem of **બોલવું** (*bolvũ*), "to speak": someone who speaks little. CDIAL lists Gujarati *ochũ* under *\*ōccha-* (CDIAL 2540) and *bolvũ* under *\*bōll-* (CDIAL 9321).
