## Five letters now come after the word that holds them (HL-C443)

Gentle writing teaches a letter from a word the reader already knows. Nine
Hindi letter lessons came *before* any word containing their letter. Five of
them now sit directly after that word:

| letter | lesson | was | now after |
|---|---|---|---|
| ृ | HI-S144 | ch7, before कृपया | कृपया (ch8) |
| ऋ | HI-S125 | ch13, before ऋतु | ऋतु (ch14) |
| झ | HI-S133 | ch31 | साँझ (ch32) |
| औ | HI-S135 | ch34 | औरत (ch39) |
| ण | HI-S147 | ch36 | प्रणाम (ch50) |

Each warm-up now names the word the letter comes from. Each anchor word no
longer lists its letter lesson as a prerequisite and no longer assesses the
letter's atom. कृपया's warm-up now recalls धन्यवाद instead, and its note on
कृ says the next lesson writes that mark. ऋतु keeps its link to हाथ, which
used to run through the letter lesson. The meeting lesson (HI-C51-sweet) now
asks the reader to find ण in प्रणाम, so that letter still gets its second
revisit.

Script closure is unchanged at 20. The anchor headwords come with their
romanization, so they are exposure, not load-bearing. The Hindi script-order
test now pins the new order.

Four cold letters remain, and each needs more than a move: घ and ढ depend on
each other in the script ladder, and ऊ and ओ have no word before chapter 96.
