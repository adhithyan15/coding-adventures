## You'll want to know: বিছানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C509-GALICHA509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**বিছানো** — *bichhāno* — "to spread out (a mat or a sheet)".

> আমরা মেঝেতে মাদুর বিছিয়ে খেলাম।
>
> *Āmrā mejhete mādur bichhiye khelām.*
>
> We spread a mat on the floor and ate.

Past (he/she …): তিনি বিছানায় চাদর বিছালেন, *tini bichhānāy chādôr bichhālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিছানো** (*bichhāno*) is a tadbhava from a reconstructed *vicchādayati*; CDIAL lists Bengali *bichāna*, "to spread, to strew" (CDIAL 11692). বিছানা, a bed, comes from the same family.
