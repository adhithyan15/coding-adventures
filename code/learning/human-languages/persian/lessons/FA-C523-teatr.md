## You'll want to know: تئاتر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C523-THEATRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تئاتر** (*te'âtr*) — "a theatre; a play".

> ما دیشب به تئاتر رفتیم.
>
> *mâ dishab be te'âtr raftim.*
>
> We went to the theatre last night.

Both the place and the play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*te'âtr* was borrowed from French *théâtre*, which EtymDB traces through Latin *theatrum* to Greek *theatron*, 'a place for viewing' (EtymDB); Lewis & Short gloss *theatrum* 'a theatre' (Lewis & Short).
