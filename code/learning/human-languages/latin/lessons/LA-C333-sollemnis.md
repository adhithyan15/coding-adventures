## You'll want to know: sollemnis, sollemne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C333-FESTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sollemnis, sollemne** — "yearly, solemn; customary".

> Sacrificium sollemne quotannīs fīēbat.
>
> *A solemn sacrifice was held every year.*

Two endings. A rite that comes round every year, and so solemn and customary. English *solemn*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sollemnis** is explained by Lewis & Short as *sollus*, "whole", and *annus*, "year": what happens every year; EtymDB derives it from *sollus*. English *solemn* comes from it through French.
