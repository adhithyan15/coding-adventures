## You'll want to know: చిత్రలేఖనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C542-SILPAM542-01]; assesses=[] -->

**చిత్రలేఖనం** — *citralēkhanaṁ* — "the art of painting".

> నా చెల్లెలు చిత్రలేఖనం నేర్చుకుంటోంది.
>
> *nā cellelu citralēkhanaṁ nērcukuṇṭōndi.*
>
> My younger sister is learning to paint.

చిత్రం, picture, + లేఖనం, drawing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చిత్రలేఖనం** (*citralēkhanaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *citra*, "bright, variegated" (CDIAL 4803), and *lēkhana*, "scraping", the noun of the root of writing (CDIAL 11103).
