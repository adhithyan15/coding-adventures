## You'll want to know: yuánzé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C371-THOUGH371-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yuánzé* — "principle".

> *wǒ juéde měi ge rén dōu yīnggāi yǒu yuánzé* — I think everyone should have principles.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuánzé* is built from *yuán* and *zé*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjwon* and *tsok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a broad plain' and the second 'a rule'. How the first came to mean a source is not settled.
