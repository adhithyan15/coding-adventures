## You'll want to know: hǎotīng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C128-MUSIC128-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎotīng* — "pleasant to hear".

> *Zhōngguó yīnyuè hěn hǎotīng* — Chinese music is lovely to listen to.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎotīng* is built from 好 'good' and *tīng* 'to listen', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xawX* and *theng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), the same shape as 好看 and 好吃.
