Marathi, chapter 396: Trains and Rails.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आगगाडी (āggāḍī) — a train; a steam train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for dance, then the Marathi for a stage.

You'll want to know: आगगाडी.
आगगाडी — āggāḍī — "a train; a steam train".
आगगाडी पुलावरून हळू जाते. āggāḍī pulāvarūn haḷū jāte. The train goes slowly over the bridge.
Feminine. Plural: आगगाड्या, āggāḍyā.
The plural is आगगाड्या; in everyday talk गाडी alone often means the train.

The word, taken apart.
आगगाडी (āggāḍī) is a Marathi compound from the age of steam: आग, "fire", inherited from Sanskrit agni (CDIAL 55), and गाडी, "a cart", inherited from gāḍḍa (CDIAL 4116).

Lesson 2 of 5.
रूळ (rūḷ) — a rail; a railway track.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a stage, then the Marathi for a train.

You'll want to know: रूळ.
रूळ — rūḷ — "a rail; a railway track".
आगगाडी रुळावरून घसरली. āggāḍī ruḷāvarūn ghasarlī. The train came off the rails.
Masculine.
Before an ending the long ऊ shortens: रूळ (rūḷ), but रुळावर, on the rails.

The word, taken apart.
रूळ (rūḷ) names one steel rail of a track, and in the plural the line itself; where the word comes from is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
लोहमार्ग (lohmārga) — a railway line.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a train, then the Marathi for a rail.

You'll want to know: लोहमार्ग.
लोहमार्ग — lohmārga — "a railway line".
हा लोहमार्ग डोंगरातून जातो. hā lohmārga ḍoṅgarātūn jāto. This railway line runs through the hills.
Masculine.
A formal word, common on signs and in the news; people say रेल्वे or गाडीचा रस्ता.

The word, taken apart.
लोहमार्ग (lohmārga) is built from two tatsama words, Sanskrit loha, "iron", and mārga, "road, way" (Monier-Williams): the iron road.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मालगाडी (mālgāḍī) — a goods train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rail, then the Marathi for a railway line.

You'll want to know: मालगाडी.
मालगाडी — mālgāḍī — "a goods train".
रात्री एक लांब मालगाडी गेली. rātrī ek lāmb mālgāḍī gelī. A long goods train went past at night.
Feminine.
It carries freight, not people; a passenger train is a प्रवासी गाडी.

The word, taken apart.
मालगाडी (mālgāḍī) is a Marathi compound of माल, "goods", a loan usually traced to Arabic māl, "property, goods", and गाडी, "a cart, a train" (CDIAL 4116).

Lesson 5 of 5.
खडखडणे (khaḍkhaḍṇe) — to rattle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a railway line, then the Marathi for a goods train.

You'll want to know: खडखडणे.
खडखडणे — khaḍkhaḍṇe — "to rattle".
जुना डबा रुळावर खडखडत होता. junā ḍabā ruḷāvar khaḍkhaḍat hotā. The old coach was rattling on the rails.
Past (he …): तो खडखडला, to khaḍkhaḍlā.
An echo word made into a verb: खड खड is the noise itself.

The word, taken apart.
खडखडणे (khaḍkhaḍṇe) is inherited and imitates its sound: CDIAL lists Marathi khaḍkhaḍṇẽ, "to rattle", under Sanskrit khaṭakhaṭāyate, "crackles" (CDIAL 3771).
