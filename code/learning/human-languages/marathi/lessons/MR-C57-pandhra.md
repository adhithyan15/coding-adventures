## You'll want to know: पंधरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-PANDHRA]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **पंधरा** — *pandhrā* — **15**

Sanskrit *pañcadaśa*, *five-ten*, written with a nasal this book has not
taught. The **पं-** is the **पाच** of
chapter thirteen with its vowel squeezed out, and the **-रा** is the ten. You
have now seen the same construction five times.
