## You'll want to know: ठगना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C293-THAGI293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ठगना** — *ṭhagnā* — "to cheat; to swindle".

> अगर कोई तुम्हें ठग रहा है, तो पुलिस को बताओ।
>
> *agar koī tumhẽ ṭhag rahā hai, to pulis ko batāo.*
>
> If someone is cheating you, tell the police.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे ठगा, *usne mujhe ṭhagā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठगना** (*ṭhagnā*) is inherited from *\*ṭhagg*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5489), which also lists the noun *ṭhag*, a swindler. English *thug* was taken from that noun.
