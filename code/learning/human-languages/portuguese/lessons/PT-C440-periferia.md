## You'll want to know: a periferia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C440-MORADA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a periferia** — "the outskirts, the edge".

> Muitas famílias vivem na periferia da cidade.
>
> *Many families live on the outskirts of the city.*

Also the edge of a circle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*periferia* is a learned borrowing, through Late Latin, from Greek *periphéreia* 'circumference', from *perí* 'around' and *phérein* 'to carry' (Lewis & Short).
