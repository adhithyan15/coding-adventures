## You'll want to know: yǐnliào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C102-DRINK102-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yǐnliào* — "drink; soft drink".

> *nǐ xiǎng hē shénme yǐnliào* — What would you like to drink?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǐnliào* is built from yǐn 'to drink' and liào, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'imX* and *lew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'to measure' and lists it with a departing tone too, *lewH*, the one liào continues; how the pair came to mean a drink is not settled.
