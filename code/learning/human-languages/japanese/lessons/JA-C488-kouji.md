## You'll want to know: こうじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C488-BUILD488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**こうじ** — *kōji* — "construction work".

> **みちで こうじを しています。** — *michi de kōji o shiteimasu* — "There is construction work on the road."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうじ** (*kōji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "construction" (Middle Chinese *kuwng*) and *ji*, "matter" (*dzriH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
