## You'll want to know: mūla, mūlae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C433-IUMENTUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mūla, mūlae** — "a she-mule".

> Mūla parva est sed fortis.
>
> *The she-mule is small but strong.*

First declension, feminine: *mūla, mūlae f.*, the female of *mūlus*. Romans said a mule never foals, so *cum mūla pepererit* meant 'never'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mūla* is the feminine of *mūlus* 'mule' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB traces *mūlus* to PIE *mukslós*.
