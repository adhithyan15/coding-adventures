## You'll want to know: اضافه کاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C378-DASTMOZD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اضافه کاری** (*ezâfe kâri*) — "overtime".

> این ماه خیلی اضافه کاری کردم.
>
> *in mâh kheyli ezâfe kâri kardam.*
>
> This month I did a lot of overtime.

*Ezâfe* "extra" + *kâri* "working". *Ezâfe kâri kardan* — to work overtime.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اضافه کاری** joins *ezâfe*, from Arabic *iḍāfa* "addition, adjunct" (EtymDB), to *kâri*, from the inherited Persian *kâr* "work" (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian): added work.
