## You'll want to know: reler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C523-LIVROS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**reler** — "to reread".

> Gosto de reler os livros da minha infância.
>
> *I like rereading the books of my childhood.*

Conjugated like *ler*: *releio*, *relês*, *relê*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reler* is built from *re-* "again" and *ler* "to read", which is inherited from Latin *legere* "to gather, to read" (Lewis & Short).
