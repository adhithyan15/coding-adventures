## You'll want to know: gratuito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C633-RIQUEZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**gratuito** — "free of charge".

> La entrada es gratuita.
>
> *Entry is free.*

More formal than *gratis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gratuito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *gratuitus* 'freely given', built on *gratia* 'favour, kindness' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
