## You'll want to know: こぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C381-FUNATABI381-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こぐ** — *kogu* — "to row; to pedal".

> **ちちと ふねを こぎました。** — *chichi to fune o kogimashita* — "I rowed a boat with my father."

Polite **こぎます**, *kogimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが ふねを こぎました。**, *chichi ga fune o kogimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こぐ** (*kogu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
