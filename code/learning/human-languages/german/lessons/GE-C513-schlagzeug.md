## You'll want to know: das Schlagzeug
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C513-INSTRUMENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Schlagzeug** — "drums, drum kit".

> Der Nachbar übt jeden Abend Schlagzeug.
>
> *The neighbour practises the drums every evening.*

Plural: *die Schlagzeuge*. One drum is *die Trommel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schlagzeug* is built on *Schlag* "blow", from Proto-Germanic *slagiz*, and *Zeug* "gear, stuff", from Middle High German *ziuc* (EtymDB): the kit you strike.
