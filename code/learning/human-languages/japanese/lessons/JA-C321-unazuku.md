## You'll want to know: うなずく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C321-ZETTAI321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うなずく** — *unazuku* — "to nod".

> **わかったので、こどもは うなずきました。** — *wakatta node, kodomo wa unazukimashita* — "The child understood, so he nodded."

Polite **うなずきます**, *unazukimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが うなずきました。**, *kodomo ga unazukimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うなずく** (*unazuku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
