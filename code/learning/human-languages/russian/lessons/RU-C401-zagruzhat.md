## You'll want to know: загружать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C401-PRILOZHENIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**загружать** (*zagruzhát*) — "to load, to upload; to keep busy".

> Страница долго загружается.
>
> *Stranítsa dólgo zagruzháyetsya.*
>
> The page takes a long time to load.

Imperfective; perfective *загрузить*. Of work: *меня загрузили работой* — they have loaded me with work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**загружать** is built from *за-* and *груз* "a load", the root of *грузовик* "a lorry": to put a load in.
