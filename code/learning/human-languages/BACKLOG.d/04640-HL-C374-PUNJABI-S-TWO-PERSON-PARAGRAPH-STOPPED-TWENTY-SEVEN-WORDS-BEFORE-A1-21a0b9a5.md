## HL-C374 — Punjabi's two-person paragraph stopped twenty-seven words before A1

After Hindi reached all three A1 reading parts, Punjabi became the smallest
measured gap. Its 43-word passage was already a comparison of Aman and Manan;
the first published A1 part begins at 70 words.

Six short lines now make that comparison do more work. The passage moves from
Aman's Punjabi and opinion to Manan's matching forms, then back to the narrator
before asking where Manan is. Every added token already appears elsewhere in
the same passage, so the expansion changes connected-reading stamina without
quietly charging the learner for new vocabulary.

The resulting 72-word passage moves Punjabi from **0/3 to 1/3** published A1
reading parts while preserving **3/3** at pre-A1. The remaining ranked reading
gaps are now Punjabi (+18 words for its final two parts), Marwari (+47 for one
part), and Spanish (+89 for its first long part).
