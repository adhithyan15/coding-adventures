## You'll want to know: गप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C473-BATUNI473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गप** — *gap* — "idle chat; gossip".

> शाम को दादा दोस्तों के साथ गप मारते हैं।
>
> *śām ko dādā dostoṁ ke sāth gap mārte haiṁ.*
>
> In the evening my grandfather chats with his friends.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गप** (*gap*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *gap*, "tittle-tattle", under *\*gappa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4022). *gap mārnā* is to chat idly.
