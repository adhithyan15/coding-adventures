## You'll want to know: contradizer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C338-DESACORDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**contradizer** — "to contradict".

> O meu irmão contradiz tudo o que eu digo.
>
> *My brother contradicts everything I say.*

Conjugated like *dizer*: *contradigo, contradizes, contradiz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contradizer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *contrādīcere* "to speak against" (EtymDB), built from *contrā* "against" and *dīcere* "to say"; Portuguese reshaped it on *dizer*.
