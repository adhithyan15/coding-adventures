Spanish, chapter 603: Derechos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el privilegio — the privilege.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the voter, then the Spanish for the democracy.

You'll want to know: el privilegio.
el privilegio — "the privilege".
Estudiar en esta universidad es un privilegio. Studying at this university is a privilege.
Privilegiado — privileged.

The word, taken apart.
privilegio is a learned borrowing from Latin privilegium 'a law made for one person' (EtymDB), from privus 'one's own' and lex 'law'.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: la desigualdad.
la desigualdad — "the inequality".
La desigualdad crece en las ciudades. Inequality is growing in the cities.
The opposite of la igualdad.

The word, taken apart.
desigualdad is built on igualdad with the prefix des-; igualdad is inherited from Latin aequalitas, from aequus 'equal' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
injusto — unfair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the inequality, then the Spanish for the justice.

You'll want to know: injusto.
injusto — "unfair".
Es injusto pagar más. It is unfair to pay more.
Es injusto que + subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
injusto is a learned borrowing from Latin iniustus 'unfair', built from in- 'not' and iustus 'right' (Lewis & Short).
