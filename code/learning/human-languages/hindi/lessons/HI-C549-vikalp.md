## You'll want to know: विकल्प
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C549-VIKALP549-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विकल्प** — *vikalp* — "an option; an alternative".

> हमारे पास दो विकल्प हैं: बस या ट्रेन।
>
> *Hamāre pās do vikalp haiṁ: bas yā ṭren.*
>
> We have two options: the bus or the train.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विकल्प** (*vikalp*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *vikalpa*, "alternation, option" (Monier-Williams).
