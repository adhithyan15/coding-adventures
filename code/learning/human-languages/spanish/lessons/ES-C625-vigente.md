## You'll want to know: vigente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C625-LEGAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**vigente** — "in force, current".

> Esta ley ya no está vigente.
>
> *This law is no longer in force.*

For laws, rules and prices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vigente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vigens* 'thriving, strong' (EtymDB), from *vigere* 'to be lively'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
