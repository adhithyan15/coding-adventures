# നിധി (nidhi) — a treasure

## You'll want to know: നിധി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C403-PRETHAM403-03]; assesses=[] -->

**നിധി** — *nidhi* — "a treasure".

> കർഷകൻ വയലിൽ ഒരു നിധി കണ്ടെത്തി.
>
> *karṣakan vayalil oru nidhi kaṇṭetti.*
>
> The farmer found a treasure in the field.

Also a fund of money set aside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നിധി** (*nidhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nidhi*, "a setting down; a hoard" (CDIAL 7207): what was put down and kept.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
