Arabic, chapter 437: Finding the way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بوصلة (būṣala) — a compass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a porter, then the Arabic for to travel about.

You'll want to know: بوصلة.
بوصلة — būṣala — "a compass".
حمل أخي بوصلة في رحلته إلى الصحراء. ḥamala akhī būṣala fī riḥlatihi ilā aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ. My brother took a compass on his trip to the desert.

The word, taken apart.
بوصلة (būṣala) was borrowed from Italian bussola (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
مرشد (murshid) — a tour guide; a guide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a compass, then the Arabic for speed.

You'll want to know: مرشد.
مرشد — murshid — "a tour guide; a guide".
شرح المرشد تاريخ المدينة. sharaḥa al-murshid tārīkh al-madīna. The guide explained the city's history.

The word, taken apart.
مرشد (murshid) is built on the native Arabic root r-sh-d, "to follow the right way" (Lane). It is the Form IV participle, "one who leads others the right way".

Lesson 5 of 5.
خط سير (khaṭṭ sayr) — an itinerary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a tour guide, then the Arabic for to guide.

You'll want to know: خط سير.
خط سير — khaṭṭ sayr — "an itinerary".
كتب المرشد خط سير الرحلة. kataba al-murshid khaṭṭ sayr ar-riḥla. The guide wrote out the itinerary of the trip.

The word, taken apart.
خط سير (khaṭṭ sayr) is built from two native Arabic words: khaṭṭ, "a line", on the root kh-ṭ-ṭ (Lane), and sayr, "going", on the root s-y-r (Lane): the line a journey follows.
