## You'll want to know: le filleul
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C490-PARRAIN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le filleul** — "godson, godchild".

> Elle emmène son filleul au zoo.
>
> *She takes her godson to the zoo.*

A goddaughter is *la filleule*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le filleul* is inherited, through Old French *fillol*, from Latin *fīliolus* "little son" (EtymDB), from *fīlius* "son" (Lewis & Short).
