## You'll want to know: il settimanale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C282-CRONACA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il settimanale** — "the weekly (magazine or paper)".

> Mia madre legge un settimanale di cucina.
>
> *My mother reads a weekly cookery magazine.*

Built on *settimana*, ‘week’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*settimanale* is built on *settimana* "week", from Late Latin *septimāna*, built on *septem* "seven" (Lewis & Short).
