## You'll want to know: ஆசிரியர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C46-ROLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆசிரியர்** (*āsiriyar*) — "a teacher".

Sanskrit *ācārya*, one who teaches by how they behave, arriving in Tamil with *-ar* on the end — the respectful ending already met on *ivar* and *naṇbar*. So the word is polite before it is anything else: there is no way to say it that does not show regard.

Tamil keeps the older and plainer ஆசான் (*āsāṉ*) beside it, and that one is what a student of an art calls a master.
