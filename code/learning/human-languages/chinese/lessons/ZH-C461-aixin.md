## You'll want to know: 爱心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C461-KIND461-02]; assesses=[] -->

**爱心** — *àixīn* — "compassion; love and care for others".

> *nàge lùrén hěn yǒu àixīn wǒ hěn gǎnjī tā* — That passer-by was so kind, and I'm very grateful to him.

The new character, in pieces: 爱 — ten strokes in three pieces: at the top a short stroke falling to the left, two dots and a stroke falling to the left, like a hand reaching down; a cover of two strokes, a short stroke down and a bar that turns down with a hook; and under it a bar, a long stroke falling to the left, and 又 *yòu*. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**爱心** (*àixīn*) is built from *ài* 'to love' and 心 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'ojH* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to cherish'.
