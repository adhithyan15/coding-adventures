Marathi, chapter 444: Puddles and Seepage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
डबके (ḍabke) — a puddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a large lake, then the Marathi for to gurgle.

You'll want to know: डबके.
डबके — ḍabke — "a puddle".
रस्त्यावर पावसाचं डबकं साचलं. rastyāvar pāvsācaṁ ḍabkaṁ sāclaṁ. A puddle of rain collected on the road.
Neuter. Plural: डबकी, ḍabkī.
Written डबके (ḍabke), said and often written डबकं; the plural is डबकी.

The word, taken apart.
डबके (ḍabke) is a Marathi word for a small pool of standing water; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
गाळ (gāḷ) — silt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to gurgle, then the Marathi for a puddle.

You'll want to know: गाळ.
गाळ — gāḷ — "silt".
पुरानंतर शेतात गाळ साचला. purānantar śetāt gāḷ sāclā. After the flood silt settled in the fields.
Masculine.
गाळ (gāḷ) काढणे, to clear the silt from a tank or a well.

The word, taken apart.
गाळ (gāḷ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi gāḷ, "dregs, mud", under gāḍa (CDIAL 4114).

Lesson 4 of 5.
झिरपणे (jhirapṇe) — to seep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for silt, then the Marathi for muddy.

You'll want to know: झिरपणे.
झिरपणे — jhirapṇe — "to seep".
पावसाचं पाणी छपरातून झिरपलं. pāvsācaṁ pāṇī chaprātūn jhiraplaṁ. The rainwater seeped through the roof.
Past (he …): तो झिरपला, to jhiraplā.
Slower than पाझरणे: drop by drop.

The word, taken apart.
झिरपणे (jhirapṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi jhirapṇẽ, "to trickle", under jhirati (CDIAL 5390).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ओसंडणे (osaṇḍṇe) — to overflow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for muddy, then the Marathi for to seep.

You'll want to know: ओसंडणे.
ओसंडणे — osaṇḍṇe — "to overflow".
धरण भरून ओसंडलं. dharaṇ bharūn osaṇḍlaṁ. The dam filled and overflowed.
Past (he …): तो ओसंडला, to osaṇḍlā.
Often with भरून: भरून ओसंडणे (osaṇḍṇe), to be full to overflowing.

The word, taken apart.
ओसंडणे (osaṇḍṇe) is a Marathi verb for water spilling over the top; its origin is not settled.
