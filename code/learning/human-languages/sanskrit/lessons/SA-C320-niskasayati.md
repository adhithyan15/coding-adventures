## You'll want to know: निष्कासयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C320-VIPLAVA320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कासयति** — *niṣkāsayati* — "drives out; expels".

> प्रजाः क्रूरं सेनापतिं निष्कासयन्ति।
>
> *prajāḥ krūraṃ senāpatiṃ niṣkāsayanti.*
>
> The people drive out the cruel general.

Past (he …): सः शत्रुं नगरात् निष्कासितवान्, *saḥ śatruṃ nagarāt niṣkāsitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कासयति** (*niṣkāsayati*) is built from *niṣ*, "out", and the causative of the root √kas, "to go"; MW gives *niṣ-kāsayati* as to drive or turn out, expel (MW).
