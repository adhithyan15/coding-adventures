## You'll want to know: राय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C275-SABAS275-05]; assesses=[] -->

**राय** — *rāy* — "an opinion".

> थारी राय कांई है?
>
> *thārī rāy kāĩ hai?*
>
> What's your opinion?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राय** (*rāy*) is usually traced, through Persian *rāy*, to Arabic *raʾy*, "opinion, view". It is feminine: **थारी राय**, your opinion.
