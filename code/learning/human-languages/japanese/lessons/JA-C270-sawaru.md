## You'll want to know: さわる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C270-HANNIN270-05]; assesses=[] -->

**さわる** — *sawaru* — "to touch".

> **なにも さわらないで ください。** — *nanimo sawaranaide kudasai* — "Please do not touch anything."

Polite **さわります**, *sawarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おとこが かばんに さわりました。**, *otoko ga kaban ni sawarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さわる** (*sawaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
