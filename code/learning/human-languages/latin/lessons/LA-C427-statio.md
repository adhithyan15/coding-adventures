## You'll want to know: statiō, statiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C427-MANSIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**statiō, statiōnis** — "a post, a station; a stopping place; an anchorage".

> Ad statiōnem mīlitēs viātōrēs exspectant.
>
> *At the post soldiers wait for the travellers.*

Third declension, feminine: *statiō, statiōnis f.* A guard post on a road, or a place where ships lie at anchor. English *station* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*statiō* is built on *stō* 'to stand', from PIE *steh₂-* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): first a standing still, then a place where people stand or stop. English *station* comes from it.
