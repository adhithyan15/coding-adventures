## You'll want to know: うんどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うんどう** — *undō* — "exercise; physical training".

> **きのう ちちと うんどうを しました。** — *kinō chichi to undō o shimashita* — "Yesterday I exercised with my father."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うんどう** (*undō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *un*, "carry, transport" (Middle Chinese *hjunH*) and *dō*, "move" (*duwngX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
