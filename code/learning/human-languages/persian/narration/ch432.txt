Persian, chapter 432: At the airport.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گیت (git) — a gate (at an airport).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an airline, then the Persian for a boarding pass.

You'll want to know: گیت.
گیت (git) — "a gate (at an airport)".
مسافران جلوی گیت شماره پنج صف کشیدند. Mosâferân jolow-ye git-e shomâre-ye panj saf keshidand. The passengers queued in front of gate number five.
Also the security gate you walk through.

The word, taken apart.
git was borrowed from English and is usually traced to English gate. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
گذرنامه (gozarnâme) — a passport (formal word).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a boarding pass, then the Persian for a gate.

You'll want to know: گذرنامه.
گذرنامه (gozarnâme) — "a passport (formal word)".
مامور فرودگاه گذرنامه من را نگاه کرد. Ma'mur-e forudgâh gozarnâme-ye man râ negâh kard. The airport officer looked at my passport.
The formal word; in speech pâsport is more common. gozar 'passing' + nâme 'letter'.

The word, taken apart.
gozarnâme is a native compound, a letter for passing: gozar is the present stem of gozashtan 'to pass', which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian verb 'to pass, to cross', and nâme 'letter' is usually traced to Middle Persian nāmag.

Lesson 3 of 5.
اضافه بار (ezâfe-bâr) — excess baggage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a gate, then the Persian for a passport.

You'll want to know: اضافه بار.
اضافه بار (ezâfe-bâr) — "excess baggage".
چمدان من سنگین بود و اضافه بار دادم. Chamedân-e man sangin bud va ezâfe-bâr dâdam. My suitcase was heavy and I paid for excess baggage.
ezâfe 'extra' + bâr 'load, luggage'. Ezâfe-bâr dâdan — to pay for excess baggage.

The word, taken apart.
ezâfe-bâr is built from ezâfe 'extra', borrowed from Arabic iḍāfa 'adding, an adjunct' (EtymDB), and native bâr 'load, burden', which EtymDB lists.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بازرسی کردن (bâzrasi kardan) — to search, to inspect (bags or people).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a passport, then the Persian for excess baggage.

You'll want to know: بازرسی کردن.
بازرسی کردن (bâzrasi kardan) — "to search, to inspect (bags or people)".
در فرودگاه کیف مرا بازرسی کردند. Dar forudgâh kif-e marâ bâzrasi kardand. At the airport they searched my bag.
bâzrasi 'inspection' + kardan. An inspector is a bâzras.

The word, taken apart.
bâzrasi kardan is built from native bâzrasi 'inspection', from bâz 'again, back' and ras, the stem of residan 'to reach', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
مهماندار (mehmândâr) — a flight attendant; a host.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for excess baggage, then the Persian for to search.

You'll want to know: مهماندار.
مهماندار (mehmândâr) — "a flight attendant; a host".
مهماندار (mehmândâr) به ما آب و چای داد. Mehmândâr be mâ âb va chây dâd. The flight attendant gave us water and tea.
mehmân 'guest' + dâr 'keeper'. On a plane it is the cabin crew.

The word, taken apart.
mehmândâr is a native compound of mehmân 'guest' and dâr 'holder, keeper', from the stem of dâshtan 'to have, to hold'. EtymDB lists the compound without older forms.
