## You'll want to know: flexibel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C374-ENTSCHLUSS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**flexibel** — "flexible".

> Bei der Arbeitszeit bin ich ganz flexibel.
>
> *I am very flexible about working hours.*

With an ending the *e* drops out: *flexible Arbeitszeiten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flexibel* is a learned borrowing from Latin *flexibilis* "that may be bent, pliant" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *flectere* "to bend".
