## You'll want to know: صقيع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C483-BARD483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صقيع** — *ṣaqīʿ* — "frost".

> كان الصقيع على السيارة في الصباح.
>
> *kāna aṣ-ṣaqīʿ ʿalā as-sayyāra fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> There was frost on the car in the morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صقيع** (*ṣaqīʿ*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ṣ-q-ʿ*, "hoarfrost".
