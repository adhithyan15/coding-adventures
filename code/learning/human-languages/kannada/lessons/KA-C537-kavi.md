## You'll want to know: ಕವಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C537-KAVYA537-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕವಿ** — *kavi* — "a poet".

> ಆ ಕವಿ ಮಳೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಸುಂದರ ಹಾಡು ಬರೆದರು.
>
> *ā kavi maḷeya bagge sundara hāḍu baredaru.*
>
> That poet wrote a beautiful song about the rain.

Plural: ಕವಿಗಳು, *kavigaḷu*.

A woman poet is ಕವಯಿತ್ರಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕವಿ** (*kavi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kavi*, "wise; a wise man, a poet" (CDIAL 2964).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
