## You'll want to know: তিল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C469-GHAM469-03]; assesses=[] -->

**তিল** — *til* — "a mole (on the skin)".

> মীরার গালে একটা ছোট তিল আছে।
>
> *mīrār gāle ekṭā chhoṭo til āchhe.*
>
> Mira has a small mole on her cheek.

তিল is also the sesame seed; CDIAL thinks the two may be one word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তিল** (*til*) is Sanskrit *tila*, "a mole on the skin" (CDIAL 5828), which CDIAL thinks may be the same word as *tila*, the sesame seed: a seed-sized dark spot.
