## Script — four strokes in one piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-CHANG-LONG-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 长

**One piece, four strokes.**

**A short left-falling stroke** at the top right, falling toward the left.

**A long horizontal** across the middle.

**A vertical** from the top left, down through the bar, that turns and flicks up to the right at the foot.

**A long stroke falling to the right**, from the bar down to the bottom right.
