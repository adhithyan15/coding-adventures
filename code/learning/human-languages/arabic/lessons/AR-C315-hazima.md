## You'll want to know: هزيمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C315-NASR315-02]; assesses=[] -->

**هزيمة** — *hazīma* — "a defeat".

> كانت الهزيمة صعبة على الفريق.
>
> *kānat al-hazīma ṣaʿba ʿalā al-farīq.*
>
> The defeat was hard on the team.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هزيمة** (*hazīma*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-z-m* (Lane), "to rout, to put to flight". *Hazīma* is the rout of the side that loses.
