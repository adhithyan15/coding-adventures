## You'll want to know: गुज़ारना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C416-KHEMA416-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गुज़ारना** — *guzārnā* — "to spend (time); to get through".

> हमने जंगल में दो रातें गुज़ारीं।
>
> *hamne jaṅgal meṁ do rāteṁ guzārīṁ.*
>
> We spent two nights in the forest.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने वहाँ एक साल गुज़ारा, *usne vahā̃ ek sāl guzārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुज़ारना** (*guzārnā*) is the causative partner of गुज़रना, "to pass by", usually traced to Persian *guzār*, "passing": time passes, and you make it pass.
