You'll want to know: 还.
还 — hái — "still; yet".
他还在家 tā hái zài jiā — He's still at home.
我还没吃饭 wǒ hái méi chī fàn — I haven't eaten yet.
The next lesson puts the character in your hand. It is the hái of hái xíng.

The word, taken apart.
还 (hái) is the hái of hái xíng, inherited from Middle Chinese hwaen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to return'. The regular sound change gives huán, the huán 'to give back' you know, not hái; how hái split off from it, and how it came to mean 'still', are not settled.

What you've built.
还 (hái) goes before the verb: still. 还没 before a verb is not yet: 我还没吃饭.
Set it against yǐjīng, already: wǒ yǐjīng chī le, I've eaten already; 我还没吃饭, not yet.

Script — 不, then the walking piece last.
还 (hái).
Two pieces you own, in the order of 这 and 过.
不, four strokes, on the upper right: the bar, the left-falling stroke, the vertical, the dot.
辶, three strokes, last: the dot, the stroke that turns down, turns again and falls to the left, then the long base under 不.
4 + 3 = seven.

Lesson 3 of 6.
还是 (háishi) — or, in a question.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: read the character you have written, then a sentence with 要.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 还没, aloud — hái méi, not yet]
[your turn — recall: say 我要水 — wǒ yào shuǐ, I want some water — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 还是.
还是 — háishi — "or", in a question.
你要水还是要饭 nǐ yào shuǐ háishi yào fàn — Do you want water or food?
你去还是他去 nǐ qù háishi tā qù — Are you going, or is he?

The word, taken apart.
还是 (háishi) is built from 还 (hái) 'still', the character you have written, and 是 'to be'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, hwaen and dzyeX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); 'or' is the pair's own sense, which neither gloss gives.

What you've built.
还是 (háishi) goes between the two choices, and the answer picks one: 我要水.
Each choice can keep its own verb: 要水还是要饭. The question needs no 吗: 还是 (háishi) already makes it one.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: huòzhě.
huòzhě — "or", in a statement.
míngtiān huòzhě hòutiān dōu kěyǐ — Tomorrow or the day after: either is fine.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
huòzhě is built from huò and zhě. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, hwok and tsyaeX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'not fixed; in doubt' and 'a particle'. 'Or' for the pair is assumed here, not quoted.

What you've built.
还是 asks; huòzhě states. 你去还是他去 nǐ qù háishi tā qù wants an answer; nǐ qù huòzhě tā qù dōu kěyǐ says either will do.
都 after the two choices says both are fine.

You'll want to know: bié.
bié — "don't".
bié qù — Don't go.
bié shuō le — Don't say any more.
Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
bié is inherited from Middle Chinese bjet (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'different; to part'. It is the bié of biérén and biéde. How it came to mean 'don't' is not settled.

What you've built.
bié and 不要 both stop someone: bié qù and 不要去 say the same thing, and bié is shorter.
With 了 at the end it means stop now, enough: bié shuō le.

Lesson 6 of 6.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 还没 — hái méi, not yet — R2]

The exchange.
A: 你要水还是要饭 nǐ yào shuǐ háishi yào fàn — Do you want water or food? B: 我要水 wǒ yào shuǐ — Water, please. A: 你吃饭了吗 nǐ chī fàn le ma — Have you eaten? B: 还没 hái méi — Not yet. A: 我们去吃饭还是在家吃 wǒmen qù chī fàn háishi zài jiā chī — Shall we go out to eat, or eat at home? B: 在家吃 妈妈还在做饭 zài jiā chī, māma hái zài zuò fàn — At home. Mum's still cooking. A: 我们明天去还是后天去 wǒmen míngtiān qù háishi hòutiān qù — Are we going tomorrow or the day after? B: míngtiān huòzhě hòutiān dōu kěyǐ — Tomorrow or the day after: either is fine. A: hǎo, bié wàng le — Fine. Don't forget.
还是 (háishi) asks, and the answer picks one; huòzhě refuses to pick. 还没 is a whole answer: not yet. bié wàng le: don't forget.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Has B eaten yet?

What you've built — or, still, not yet, don't.
Four small words that steer a conversation:
你要水还是要饭 nǐ yào shuǐ háishi yào fàn — water or food?
míngtiān huòzhě hòutiān dōu kěyǐ — tomorrow or the day after, either is fine.
我还没吃饭 wǒ hái méi chī fàn — I haven't eaten yet.
bié qù — don't go.
Listen. Nǐ hē chá háishi hē shuǐ?
Speak. Offer a friend two choices, then tell them not to forget.
