# Retrieve the new agreement form

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-CHAHUNDE-189] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Hear “want, plural or respectful” and prepare the single form.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-CHAHUNDE-189, PA-LEX-C60-VERBS60-03, PA-FORM-KARDE-183] -->

Keep **ਚਾਹੁੰਦੇ** distinct from the infinitive **ਚਾਹੁਣਾ**. Its agreement job
matches the familiar plural or respectful pattern in **ਕਰਦੇ**.
