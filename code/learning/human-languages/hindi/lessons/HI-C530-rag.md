## You'll want to know: राग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C530-SANGEET530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**राग** — *rāg* — "a raga; a melodic mode".

> सुबह के लिए अलग राग होते हैं।
>
> *Subah ke liye alag rāg hote haiṁ.*
>
> There are separate ragas for the morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राग** (*rāg*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *rāga*, "colouring; a musical mode" (Monier-Williams).
