## You'll want to know: بازرسی کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C432-GIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بازرسی کردن** (*bâzrasi kardan*) — "to search, to inspect (bags or people)".

> در فرودگاه کیف مرا بازرسی کردند.
>
> *Dar forudgâh kif-e marâ bâzrasi kardand.*
>
> At the airport they searched my bag.

*bâzrasi* 'inspection' + *kardan*. An inspector is a *bâzras*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâzrasi kardan* is built from native *bâzrasi* 'inspection', from *bâz* 'again, back' and *ras*, the stem of *residan* 'to reach', and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
