## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-GU-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KU-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01] -->

> **く** *ku* + **゛** → **ぐ** *gu*

Write **く** in full: one stroke, down to the left and back down to the right.
Then add the two short strokes of the voicing mark at the upper right,
beside the point, the left one first.

The *k* row voices to *g*, as **か** did to **が** in **ありがとう** and **こ** to
**ご** in **ございます**.

With **いり** in front and **ち** after it, you can now write the word from the
last lesson: **いりぐち**, *iriguchi*, an entrance.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-GU-01, JA-SCRIPT-KU-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-IRIGUCHI] -->

1. Write **く**, then **ぐ**, and say *ku*, *gu*.
2. Write **か が**, **こ ご** and **く ぐ** in pairs, and say each one.
3. Write **いりぐち** from memory and say *iriguchi*.
