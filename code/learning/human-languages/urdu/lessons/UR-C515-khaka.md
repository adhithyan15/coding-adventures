## You'll want to know: خاکہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C515-TASVIR515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خاکہ** — *khākā* — "a sketch; an outline".

> مصور نے پہلے پنسل سے خاکہ بنایا۔
>
> *musavvir ne pehle pensil se khākā banāyā.*
>
> The painter first made a sketch in pencil.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خاکہ** (*khākā*) is usually traced to Persian *khāka*, "a sketch, an outline".
