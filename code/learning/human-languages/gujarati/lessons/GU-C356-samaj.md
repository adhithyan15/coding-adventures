## You'll want to know: સમાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C356-BANDHARAN356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સમાજ** — *samāj* — "society".

> સારો સમાજ ગરીબ માણસોની કાળજી લે છે.
>
> *sāro samāj garīb māṇsonī kāḷjī le che.*
>
> A good society takes care of its poor.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સમાજ** (*samāj*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *samāja*, "a meeting, an assembly" (Monier-Williams).
