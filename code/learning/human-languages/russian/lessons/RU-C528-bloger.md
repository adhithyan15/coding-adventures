## You'll want to know: блогер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C528-GAMING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**блогер** (*blóger*) — "a blogger".

> Этот блогер рассказывает о путешествиях.
>
> *Étot blóger rasskázyvayet o puteshéstviyakh.*
>
> This blogger talks about travel.

Masculine; also of a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*блогер* is usually traced to English *blogger*; EtymDB has no entry.
