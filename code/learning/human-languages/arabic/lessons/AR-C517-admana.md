## You'll want to know: أدمن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C517-ADAT517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أدمن** — *admana* — "to become addicted".

> أدمن الشاب ألعاب الفيديو.
>
> *admana ash-shābb alʿāb al-fīdiyū.*
>
> The young man became addicted to video games.

Present: يدمن, *yudmin*.

It takes a direct object, or على.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أدمن** (*admana*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-m-n*, "to keep to a thing" (Lane). Form IV admana is to keep at a thing without stopping.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
