- [YOU SAY: "I speak Punjabi" (m./f.) — *maiṁ panjābī boldā/boldī hāṁ*]
- [YOU SAY: "I live in Amritsar" — *maiṁ Amritsar rahindā/rahindī hāṁ*]
- [YOU SAY: "I work" — *maiṁ kamm kardā/kardī hāṁ*]

## The word, taken apart — roots you now carry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-ETYMON-KR-DO-05, PA-ETYMON-PANJ-AAB-05, PA-LEX-PANJABI-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *bolṇā* "to speak" (native Indo-Aryan)
- *rahiṇā* "to live" ← *rah-* "to remain"
- *karnā* "to do" ← *√kṛ* — the root of *namaskār* and *karma*
- *panjābī* ← *panj* "five" (cousin of English *five*) + *āb* "river"

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-I-SPEAK-PUNJABI-05, PA-LEX-RAHNA-05, PA-LEX-KAMM-05, PA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-HABITUAL-GENDER-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In one breath, tell me your language, your city, and your work in
Punjabi (as a woman). (*Maiṁ panjābī boldī hāṁ. Maiṁ … rahindī hāṁ. Maiṁ kamm
kardī hāṁ.*)

[PAUSE 2s] Independent Gurmukhi reading and writing are **not scored here**.
Romanization is evidence for spoken recall only; a typed romanization never
counts as Gurmukhi writing.
