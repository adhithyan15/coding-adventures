## You'll want to know: आश्रम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C334-KATHA334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्रम** — *āśram* — "a hermitage; an ashram".

> ऋषि का आश्रम नदी के किनारे था।
>
> *ṛṣi kā āśram nadī ke kināre thā.*
>
> The sage's hermitage was on the riverbank.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्रम** (*āśram*) is Sanskrit *āśrama*, "a hermitage" (Monier-Williams): the forest home of a sage and his pupils.
