## You'll want to know: warnen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C292-RETTUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**warnen** — "to warn".

> Die Nachbarin warnte uns vor dem Hund.
>
> *The neighbour warned us about the dog.*

*Vor etwas warnen* — to warn about something. *Die Warnung* — the warning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*warnen* is inherited from Middle High German *warnen* "to watch over, to protect" (EtymDB), a cousin of English *warn*.
