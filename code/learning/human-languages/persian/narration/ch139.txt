Persian, chapter 139: A Timetable, a Sign, the Rent, an Advert, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
جدول زمانی (jadval-e zamâni) — a timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to decide, then the Persian for to hope.

You'll want to know: جدول زمانی.
جدول زمانی — jadval-e zamâni — "a timetable".
[once you have stopped driving — read: the board]
The board says اتوبوس: هشت صبح — otobus: hasht-e sobh — "Bus: 8 a.m." The bus leaves at eight, so be there before eight.

Still? (هنوز, hanuz.) A word that opens a yes-or-no question? (آیا, âyâ.) At all? (اصلا, aslan.) Surely... not, in a question? (مگر, magar.) When? (چه وقت, che vaqt.) Recently? (به تازگی, be-tâzegi.) Already? (قبلا, qablan.) Last year? (پارسال, pârsâl.) Once? (یک بار, yek bâr.) To spend? (سپری کردن, separi kardan.)
