## You'll want to know: સ્વભાવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C270-NATURE270-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વભાવ** — *svabhāv* — "temperament, nature".

> **તેનો સ્વભાવ શાંત નથી.** — *teno svabhāv śānt nathī* — "His temperament is not calm."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વભાવ** (*svabhāv*) is Sanskrit *svabhāva*, "own being, nature", built from *sva*, "own", and *bhāva*, "being", and taken over as a tatsama; EtymDB lists it as borrowed from Sanskrit (EtymDB).
