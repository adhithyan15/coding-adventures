## You'll want to know: روتختی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C439-MALAFE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**روتختی** (*rutakhti*) — "a bedspread, a bedcover".

> روتختی اتاق ما آبی بود.
>
> *Rutakhti-ye otâq-e mâ âbi bud.*
>
> The bedspread in our room was blue.

*ru* 'on' + *takht* 'bed' + *-i*: what goes on the bed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rutakhti* is a native compound of *ru* 'face, on top of', *takht* 'bed, throne' and the ending *-i*. EtymDB does not list the compound.
