## You'll want to know: টয়লেট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C405-BATHRUM405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**টয়লেট** — *ṭôyleṭ* — "a toilet".

> স্টেশনের টয়লেট নোংরা ছিল।
>
> *sṭeshôner ṭôyleṭ nongrā chhilô.*
>
> The toilets at the station were dirty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টয়লেট** (*ṭôyleṭ*) is borrowed from English *toilet*.
