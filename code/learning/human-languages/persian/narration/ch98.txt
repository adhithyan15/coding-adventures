Persian, chapter 98: Sandals, Stew, Soup, Salad, Kebab.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
صندل (sandal) — sandals.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a necklace, then the Persian for boots.

You'll want to know: صندل.
صندل — sandal — "sandals".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sandal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sandal, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sandal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for a necklace, then the Persian for boots, then say sandal again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does صندل (sandal) mean? ("Sandals".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Can, want and why — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a salad and a kebab.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: to be able to, a skill, ability, a right, a chance]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a tool, an owner, power, a wish, hope]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a plan, a need, a decision, a goal, a request]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: why?, because, because, a reason, a cause]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: therefore, a pillow, a blanket, a curtain, a nail]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To be able to? (توانستن, tavânestan.) A skill? (مهارت, mahârat.) Ability? (توانایی, tavânâyi.) A right? (حق, hagh.) A chance? (فرصت, forsat.) A tool? (ابزار, abzâr.) An owner? (صاحب, sâheb.) Power? (قدرت, ghodrat.) A wish? (آرزو, ârezu.) Hope? (امید, omid.) A plan? (برنامه, barnâme.) A need? (نیاز, niyâz.) A decision? (تصمیم, tasmim.) A goal? (هدف, hadaf.) A request? (خواهش, khâhesh.) Asking why? (چرا, cherâ.) Because? (زیرا, zirâ.) Because? (چون, chon.) A reason? (دلیل, dalil.) A cause? (سبب, sabab.) Therefore? (بنابراین, banâbarin.) A pillow? (بالش, bâlesh.) A blanket? (پتو, patu.) A curtain? (پرده, parde.) A nail? (میخ, mikh.)
