## You'll want to know: ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C379-OMMATA379-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸು** — *sammatisu* — "to consent".

> ಅಪ್ಪ ನನ್ನ ಪ್ರವಾಸಕ್ಕೆ ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *appa nanna pravāsakke sammatisidaru.*
>
> Father agreed to my trip.

Past (I …): ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸಿದೆ, *sammatiside*.

What you consent to takes -ಗೆ. ಸಮ್ಮತಿ, consent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸು** (*sammatisu*) is built on Sanskrit *sammati*, "agreement, approval" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
