## You'll want to know: いけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-IKE]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-I-01] -->

**いけ** — *ike* — "a pond".

Two beats: *i–ke*. The first sign is the **い** you already write. The second one
is new, and the next lesson writes it.
