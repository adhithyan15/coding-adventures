# സാരി (sāri) — a sari

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C100-MUNDU-01, ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *a mundu*. Then say *old*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C100-SARI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**സാരി** — *sāri* — **a sari.**

> **ഇത് പഴയ സാരിയാണ്.** — *itŭ paḻaya sāriyāṇŭ* — "This is an old sari."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C100-SARI-01, ML-LEX-C100-MUNDU-01, ML-LEX-AANU-01] -->

**Two garments, two different landings.** **മുണ്ട്** ends in the half-u and
takes **ആണ്** straight on. **സാരി** ends in **ഇ**, and you know what **ഇ** does:

| word | joined to **ആണ്** |
|---|---|
| **മുണ്ട്** | **മുണ്ടാണ്** (*muṇṭāṇŭ*) |
| **സാരി** | **സാരിയാണ്** (*sāriyāṇŭ*) |

The **യ** in **സാരിയാണ്** is the one **കളി** and **ഭംഗി** take, for the reason
you were given then: **ഇ** calls for it.

**പഴയ** behaves as **പുതിയ** did — in front, unchanged.
