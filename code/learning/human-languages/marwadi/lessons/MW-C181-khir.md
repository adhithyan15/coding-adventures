## You'll want to know: खीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C181-BILOVO181-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खीर** — *khīr* — "rice pudding".

> आज मां खीर बणावै है।
>
> *āj mā̃ khīr baṇāvai hai.*
>
> Mother makes rice pudding today.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खीर** (*khīr*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kṣīrī*, "a dish made with milk"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *khīr*, rice boiled in milk (CDIAL 3705). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
