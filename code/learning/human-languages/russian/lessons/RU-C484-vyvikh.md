## You'll want to know: вывих
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C484-PLASTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**вывих** (*vývikh*) — "a dislocation".

> Врач сказал, что у меня вывих плеча.
>
> *Vrach skazál, chto u menyá vývikh plechá.*
>
> The doctor said I had a dislocated shoulder.

Masculine. The verb is *вывихнуть* — to dislocate, to twist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вывих* is built on *вы-* 'out' and the stem of *вывихнуть* 'to dislocate'; EtymDB has no entry, and the origin of the stem is not settled.
