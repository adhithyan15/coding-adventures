## You'll want to know: l'afa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C466-AFA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'afa** — "the sultry heat, the mugginess".

> Ad agosto in città c'è un'afa terribile.
>
> *In August the city is terribly muggy.*

Heat with no wind and damp air.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *afa* is not settled; it may imitate the sound of gasping for breath.
