Portuguese, chapter 499: Gratos e fiéis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
grato — grateful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for confidant, then the Portuguese for loyalty.

You'll want to know: grato.
grato — "grateful".
Estou muito grato pela vossa ajuda. I am very grateful for your help.
More formal than obrigado: estar grato por.

The word, taken apart.
grato is a learned borrowing from Latin grātus "pleasing, thankful" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ingrato — ungrateful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for grateful, then the Portuguese for gratitude.

You'll want to know: ingrato.
ingrato — "ungrateful".
O filho ingrato nunca telefona à mãe. The ungrateful son never phones his mother.
Of a job: thankless, um trabalho ingrato.

The word, taken apart.
ingrato comes from Latin ingrātus "unpleasant, ungrateful" (EtymDB), the opposite of grātus.

fiel — "faithful, loyal".
O cão é um amigo fiel. A dog is a faithful friend.
Plural fiéis.

The word, taken apart.
fiel is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Latin fidēlis "faithful" (EtymDB), built on fidēs "faith".
