# بالإضافة إلى (bi-l-iḍāfa ilā) — in addition to

## You'll want to know: بالإضافة إلى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C416-BADAL416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بالإضافة إلى** — *bi-l-iḍāfa ilā* — "in addition to".

> اشتريت خبزا بالإضافة إلى الحليب.
>
> *ishtaraytu khubzan bi-l-iḍāfa ilā al-ḥalīb.*
>
> I bought bread in addition to milk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بالإضافة إلى** (*bi-l-iḍāfa ilā*) joins *bi-*, "with", *al-iḍāfa*, "the adding", built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-y-f*, "to be a guest; to join to" (Lane), and *ilā*, "to": in addition to. أضاف, to add, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
