## You'll want to know: lùfèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C429-HOME429-05]; assesses=[] -->

*lùfèi* — "travel costs; the cost of getting there".

> *zuò huǒchē qù Běijīng lùfèi shì duōshao* — How much does it cost to get to Beijing by train?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lùfèi* is built from 路 'a road' and *fèi* 'a cost', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *luH* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
