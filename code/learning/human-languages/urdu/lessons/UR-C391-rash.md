## You'll want to know: رش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C391-QATAR391-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رش** — *rash* — "a rush (of people); a crowd".

> صبح کے وقت اسٹیشن پر رش ہوتا ہے۔
>
> *subah ke vaqt isṭeshan par rash hotā hai.*
>
> The station is very crowded in the mornings.

Masculine.

Everyday spoken Urdu; news reports and notices write ہجوم (hujūm), "a crowd".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رش** (*rash*) is borrowed from English *rush*; in Urdu it names the crowd itself.
