## You'll want to know: らいねん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C141-RAINEN]; assesses=[] -->

**らいねん** — *rainen* — "next year": **きょねん**, **ことし**,
**らいねん**, last year, this year, next year.

> **らいねん ともだちと やまに いきます。** — *rainen tomodachi to yama ni
> ikimasu* — "Next year I will go to the mountains with a friend." The
> particle **と** means "with".
