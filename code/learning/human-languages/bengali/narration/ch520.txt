Bengali, chapter 520: Medals and Records.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পদক (pôdôk) — a medal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a referee, then the Bengali for to be beaten.

You'll want to know: পদক.
পদক — pôdôk — "a medal".
সাঁতারে মেয়েটা একটা পদক জিতল। Sā̃tāre meyeṭā ekṭā pôdôk jitlô. The girl won a medal in swimming.

The word, taken apart.
পদক (pôdôk) is a Sanskrit word built on pada; Monier-Williams gives padaka as a kind of ornament, and the sense "a medal" is the modern language's own.

প্রতিযোগিতায় দশজন প্রতিযোগী ছিল। Prôtijogitāy dôshjôn prôtijogī chhilô. There were ten contestants in the competition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a sporting record, then the Bengali for a contestant.

You'll want to know: জেতানো.
জেতানো — jetāno — "to lead to victory; to make win".
অধিনায়ক একা দলকে জেতাল। Ôdhināyôk ekā dôlke jetālô. The captain won the match for the team on her own.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দলকে জেতালেন, tini dôlke jetālen.

The word, taken apart.
জেতানো (jetāno) is built on জেতা, "to win", as its causative partner: to make someone win.
