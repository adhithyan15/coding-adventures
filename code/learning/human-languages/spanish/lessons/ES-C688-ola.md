## You'll want to know: la ola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C688-MAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la ola** — "the wave".

> Hoy hay olas muy grandes.
>
> *The waves are very big today.*

*Una ola de calor* — a heat wave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *ola* is not settled; several sources have been proposed, none of them sure.
