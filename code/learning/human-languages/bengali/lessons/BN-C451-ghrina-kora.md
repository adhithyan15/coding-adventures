## You'll want to know: ঘৃণা করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C451-GHRINA451-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘৃণা করা** — *ghrinā kôrā* — "to hate; to despise".

> মীরা মিথ্যেকে ঘৃণা করে।
>
> *mīrā mittheke ghrinā kôre.*
>
> Mira hates lies.

Past (he/she …): তিনি যুদ্ধকে ঘৃণা করলেন, *tini juddhôke ghrinā kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘৃণা করা** (*ghrinā kôrā*) is Sanskrit *ghṛṇā*, which Monier-Williams gives for warm feeling and pity and also for contempt and disgust; Bengali keeps the loathing, with *kôrā*, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
