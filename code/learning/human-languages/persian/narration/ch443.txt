Persian, chapter 443: Service and tips.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
خدمات (khadamât) — services.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to take back, then the Persian for to give back.

You'll want to know: خدمات.
خدمات (khadamât) — "services".
خدمات (khadamât) این هتل خیلی خوب است. Khadamât-e in hotel kheyli khub ast. This hotel's services are very good.
An Arabic plural of khedmat 'service'.

The word, taken apart.
khadamât is the Arabic plural of khedmat 'service', which EtymDB takes from Arabic ḫidma 'service'. EtymDB does not list the plural.

Lesson 2 of 6.
کیفیت (keyfiyat) — quality.

You'll want to know: کیفیت.
کیفیت (keyfiyat) — "quality".
کیفیت (keyfiyat) غذای هتل پایین بود. Keyfiyat-e ghazâ-ye hotel pâyin bud. The quality of the hotel's food was poor.
Bâ keyfiyat — of good quality.

The word, taken apart.
keyfiyat was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic kayfiyya 'the how of a thing, its manner', built on kayfa 'how', which EtymDB lists.

ما از اتاق و صبحانه راضی بودیم. Mâ az otâq va sobhâne râzi budim. We were pleased with the room and the breakfast.
The cause takes az. Note the letter ض, said z.

Lesson 4 of 6.
انعام (an'âm) — a tip, a gratuity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for quality, then the Persian for satisfied.

You'll want to know: انعام.
انعام (an'âm) — "a tip, a gratuity".
به گارسون انعام دادیم. Be gârson an'âm dâdim. We gave the waiter a tip.
An'âm dâdan — to tip.

The word, taken apart.
an'âm was borrowed from Arabic inʿām 'that which is bestowed or granted' (EtymDB); in Persian it means a reward, and today a tip.

Lesson 5 of 6.
تشکر کردن (tashakkor kardan) — to thank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for satisfied, then the Persian for a tip.

You'll want to know: تشکر کردن.
تشکر کردن (tashakkor kardan) — "to thank".
از راننده تاکسی تشکر کردیم و پیاده شدیم. Az rânande-ye tâksi tashakkor kardim va piyâde shodim. We thanked the taxi driver and got out.
The person thanked takes az. Everyday thanks is mamnun or merci.

The word, taken apart.
tashakkor kardan is built from tashakkor 'thanks', borrowed from Arabic tašakkur (EtymDB), and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

What you've built.
