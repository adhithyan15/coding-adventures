## You'll want to know: चेतना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C295-CETANA295-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चेतना** — *cetanā* — "consciousness".

> आहतस्य चेतना शनैः आगच्छति।
>
> *āhatasya cetanā śanaiḥ āgacchati.*
>
> The injured man slowly comes round.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चेतना** (*cetanā*) comes from the root √cit, "to perceive, attend to"; MW gives *cetanā* as consciousness and understanding (MW).
