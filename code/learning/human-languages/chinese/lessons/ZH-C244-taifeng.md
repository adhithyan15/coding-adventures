## You'll want to know: táifēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C244-STORM244-01]; assesses=[] -->

*táifēng* — "typhoon".

> *hòutiān huì yǒu táifēng* — There'll be a typhoon the day after tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*táifēng* is built from *tái* and 风 'wind'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *pjuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the first in this sense, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
