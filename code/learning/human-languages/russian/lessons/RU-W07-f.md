# ф — a circle on a stick

## Warm-up

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SCRIPT-F-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ф

A **new shape**: a vertical stroke with a closed loop threaded onto it, the loop
sitting on both sides of the line. Draw the vertical first, then the loop across
it.

It says **f**, exactly as in English *foot*. No trap at all.

This is Greek **phi**, and that ancestry shows in where you find it. Native
Slavic words almost never contain ф — nearly every Russian word with this letter
came in from Greek or later from Western Europe. When you meet it, the word is
very often one you can half-guess, because it was borrowed from a language you
know.
