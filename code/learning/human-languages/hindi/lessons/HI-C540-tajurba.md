## You'll want to know: तजुर्बा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C540-KOSHISH540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तजुर्बा** — *tajurbā* — "experience (gained over time)".

> अरुण को इस काम का दस साल का तजुर्बा है।
>
> *Aruṇ ko is kām kā das sāl kā tajurbā hai.*
>
> Arun has ten years' experience of this work.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तजुर्बा** (*tajurbā*) is usually traced to Arabic *tajriba*, "a trial, a test", through Persian; Hindi uses it for what years of doing something teach you.
