## You'll want to know: وبخ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C534-NASIHA534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**وبخ** — *wabbakha* — "to scold".

> وبخ المدرب اللاعبين بعد الخسارة.
>
> *wabbakha al-mudarrib al-lāʿibīn baʿda al-khasāra.*
>
> The coach scolded the players after the defeat.

Present: يوبخ, *yuwabbikh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وبخ** (*wabbakha*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-b-kh*, "to blame, to rebuke" (Lane). Form II wabbakha is to rebuke sharply.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
