## You'll want to know: suspīrō, suspīrāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C491-LACR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**suspīrō, suspīrāre** — "to sigh".

> Iuvenis suspīrat et dē puellā cōgitat.
>
> *The young man sighs and thinks about the girl.*

*suspīrō, suspīrāre, suspīrāvī, suspīrātum*: *sub* 'up from below' + *spīrō* 'to breathe'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**suspīrō** is built on *sub-* "up from below" and *spīrō* "to breathe" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB): a sigh is a breath drawn up from deep down. English *inspire* and *respire* share *spīrō*.
