## You'll want to know: le délit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C386-ACCUSER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le délit** — "the misdemeanour, the offence".

> Voler dans un magasin est un délit.
>
> *Shoplifting is an offence.*

Less serious than *un crime*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*délit* comes through Old French *delit* (EtymDB) from Latin *dēlictum* "a fault, an offence", from *dēlinquere* "to fail, to do wrong" (Lewis & Short).
