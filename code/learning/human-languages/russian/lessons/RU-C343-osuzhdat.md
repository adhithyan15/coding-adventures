## You'll want to know: осуждать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C343-KRITIKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**осуждать** (*osuzhdát*) — "to condemn, to disapprove of".

> Я не осуждаю его: он очень устал.
>
> *Ya ne osuzhdáyu yegó: on óchen ustál.*
>
> I do not judge him harshly: he is very tired.

Imperfective; perfective *осудить*. In court it also means to convict.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**осуждать** is built from *о-* and *судить* "to judge", on *суд*, inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*sǫdŭ* (EtymDB). The *жд* is the Church Slavonic softening of *д*.
