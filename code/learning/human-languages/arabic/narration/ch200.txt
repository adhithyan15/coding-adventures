Arabic, chapter 200: Cold, Cough, Allergy, Wound, Health.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
زكام (zukām) — a cold (illness).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to whisper, then the Arabic for to harvest.

You'll want to know: زكام.
زكام — zukām — "a cold (illness)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: zukām]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: zukām, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say zukām]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to whisper, then the Arabic for to harvest, then say zukām again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does زكام (zukām) mean? ("A cold (illness)".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To ride? (ركب, rakiba.) To pay; to push? (دفع, dafaʿa.) To save? (وفر, waffara.) To receive? (استلم, istalama.) To cost? (كلف, kallafa.) To take off? (خلع, khalaʿa.) To try? (جرب, jarraba.) To measure? (قاس, qāsa.) To bargain? (ساوم, sāwama.) To go shopping? (تسوق, tasawwaqa.) To swallow? (ابتلع, ibtalaʿa.) To chew? (مضغ, maḍagha.) To touch? (لمس, lamasa.) To smell? (شم, shamma.) To hold? (أمسك, amsaka.) To resign? (استقال, istaqāla.) To manage? (أدار, adāra.) To meet? (قابل, qābala.) To get lost? (ضل, ḍalla.) To show the way? (دل, dalla.)

To notice? (لاحظ, lāḥaẓa.) To pay attention? (انتبه, intabaha.) To consider? (اعتبر, iʿtabara.) To allow? (سمح, samaḥa.) To prevent? (منع, manaʿa.) To happen? (حدث, ḥadatha.) To fall? (سقط, saqaṭa.) To dig? (حفر, ḥafara.) To shake? (هز, hazza.) To bite? (عض, ʿaḍḍa.) To blow? (نفخ, nafakha.) To whisper? (همس, hamasa.) To harvest? (حصد, ḥaṣada.) A cold? (زكام, zukām.) A cough? (سعال, suʿāl.)
