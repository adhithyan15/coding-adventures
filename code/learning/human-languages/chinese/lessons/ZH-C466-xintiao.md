## You'll want to know: xīntiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C466-CALM466-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xīntiào* — "heartbeat".

> *wǒ gǎnjué xīntiào hěn kuài* — I can feel my heart racing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīntiào* is built from 心 'heart' and *tiào* 'to jump', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sim* and *dew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to leap'. The regular change from *dew* gives a rising tone; how *tiào* came to its falling tone is not settled.
