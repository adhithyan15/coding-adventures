Spanish, chapter 505: Estudiar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la educación — education; manners.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the factory, then the Spanish for to damage.

You'll want to know: la educación.
la educación — "education; manners".
La educación es gratuita. Education is free.
Tener buena educación — to have good manners.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: la educación]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: la educación, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with la educación]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the factory, then the Spanish for to damage, then say la educación again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does la educación mean? ("Education; manners".) Say it once more.
