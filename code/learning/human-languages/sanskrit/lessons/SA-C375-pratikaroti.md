## You'll want to know: प्रतिकरोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C375-VIPATTI375-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिकरोति** — *pratikaroti* — "remedies; counteracts".

> यदि रोगः आगच्छति, तर्हि वैद्यः तं प्रतिकरोति।
>
> *yadi rogaḥ āgacchati, tarhi vaidyaḥ taṃ pratikaroti.*
>
> If an illness comes, the doctor remedies it.

Past (he …): सः रोगं प्रतिकृतवान्, *saḥ rogaṃ pratikṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिकरोति** (*pratikaroti*) is built on *prati*, "against", and the root √kṛ, "to do"; MW gives *prati-√kṛ* "to counteract, to resist" and "to repay, to requite" (MW).

## What you've built
