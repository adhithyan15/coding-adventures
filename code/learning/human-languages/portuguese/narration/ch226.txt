Lesson 1 of 5.
a raquete — a racket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for energy, then the Portuguese for electricity.

You'll want to know: a raquete.
a raquete — "a racket".

The word, taken apart.
raquete from French raquette (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. The French word may come from Arabic rāḥa, "palm of the hand".
