## You'll want to know: солить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C333-SOLIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**солить** (*solít*) — "to salt".

> Ты уже солил суп?
>
> *Ty uzhé solíl sup?*
>
> Have you salted the soup yet?

Imperfective; perfective *посолить* (*posolít*). Also to pickle in salt: *солить огурцы*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*солить* is built on *соль* "salt", an inherited Slavic word from the PIE root of Latin *sal* and English *salt*; EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.
