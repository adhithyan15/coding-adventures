## You'll want to know: सन्दिग्धः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C312-NIRAKARANA312-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्दिग्धः** — *sandigdhaḥ* — "doubtful; questionable".

> प्रवक्तुः उत्तरं सन्दिग्धम् अस्ति।
>
> *pravaktuḥ uttaraṃ sandigdham asti.*
>
> The spokesman's answer is doubtful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्दिग्धः** (*sandigdhaḥ*) is built from *sam* and the root √dih, like *sandeha*; MW gives *saṃdigdha* as smeared over, confused, and questionable (MW).
