## You'll want to know: o apresentador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C278-NOTICIARIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o apresentador** — "the presenter, the host".

> O apresentador do programa é muito simpático.
>
> *The programme’s host is very friendly.*

A woman is *a apresentadora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apresentador* is built on *apresentar* "to present", from Late Latin *praesentāre* "to show, to hold out", from *praesēns* "present" (Lewis & Short).
