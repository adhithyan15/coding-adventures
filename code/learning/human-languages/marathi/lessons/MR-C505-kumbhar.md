## You'll want to know: कुंभार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C505-KARAGIR505-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंभार** — *kumbhār* — "a potter".

> कुंभार मातीची भांडी बनवतो.
>
> *kumbhār mātīcī bhāṇḍī banavto.*
>
> The potter makes clay pots.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंभार** (*kumbhār*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *kumbhakāra*, "potter" (CDIAL 3310), from *kumbha*, "a pot", and *kāra*, "maker".
