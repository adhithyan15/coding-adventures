## You'll want to know: सर्वसामान्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C354-NAGARIK354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सर्वसामान्य** — *sarvasāmānya* — "common; ordinary".

> सर्वसामान्य माणसाला हा कायदा समजत नाही.
>
> *sarvasāmānya māṇsālā hā kāydā samjat nāhī.*
>
> The ordinary person does not understand this law.

सर्व + सामान्य, all + common. Invariable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सर्वसामान्य** (*sarvasāmānya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sarvasāmānya*, "common to all" (Monier-Williams).
