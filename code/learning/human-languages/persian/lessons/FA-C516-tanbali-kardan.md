## You'll want to know: تنبلی کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C516-FITNESS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تنبلی کردن** (*tanbali kardan*) — "to be lazy, to slack".

> امروز تنبلی کردم و ورزش نکردم.
>
> *emruz tanbali kardam va varzesh nakardam.*
>
> Today I was lazy and didn't exercise.

*tanbal* 'lazy' + *-i* + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tanbali* is built on *tanbal*, 'lazy', with the noun ending *-i*. EtymDB lists *tanbal* without a source, and its origin is not settled.
