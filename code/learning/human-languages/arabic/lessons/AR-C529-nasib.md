## You'll want to know: نسيب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C529-AHL529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نسيب** — *nasīb* — "an in-law".

> جاء نسيبي لزيارتنا في العيد.
>
> *jāʾa nasībī li-ziyāratinā fī al-ʿīd.*
>
> My in-law came to visit us at the feast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نسيب** (*nasīb*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-s-b*, "lineage, relationship" (Lane). Nasab, lineage, is from the same root: a nasīb is kin by marriage.
