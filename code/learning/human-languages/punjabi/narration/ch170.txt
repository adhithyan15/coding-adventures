Lesson 1 of 1.
The old two-card selector — choose date card A or B from an already-taught mark.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Guided Practice and Wrap-up Recall.

Stop before looking at or marking the printed cards. This is a short return to the familiar fictional bank, not a real personal date or a scored A1 mock.

Warm-up.
Look at the two old cards: A ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫ and B ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫. The mark ਕ asks for A; ਖ asks for B. These marks are selectors, not month names.

Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Keep both cards and the selector map visible. Point to ਖ, then to B. Write the letter B beside ਖ. Do the same for ਕ and A. Compare each link with the printed map before trying the pair once more with the map covered. If one link differs, uncover the map and repair that link. No new date is needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Cover the map for a few seconds. Point to the card requested by ਕ, then the card requested by ਖ. Uncover the map and check A, then B. The bank remains available for a gentle repair; this is not independent scored writing.
