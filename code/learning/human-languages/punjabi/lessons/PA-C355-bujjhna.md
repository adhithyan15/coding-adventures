## You'll want to know: ਬੁੱਝਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C355-RIDDLE355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੁੱਝਣਾ** — *bujjhṇā* — "to solve a riddle; to guess".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਹਰ ਪਹੇਲੀ ਬੁੱਝਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī bhaiṇ har pahelī bujjhdī hai.*
>
> My sister solves every riddle.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਹੇਲੀ ਬੁੱਝੀ, *maiṁ pahelī bujjhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੁੱਝਣਾ** (*bujjhṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *budhyate*, "is awakened, understands"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bujjhṇā*, "to guess, solve", under it (CDIAL 9279).
