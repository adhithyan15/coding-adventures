## You'll want to know: 经过
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C306-WEDDING306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**经过** — *jīngguò* — "the sequence of events; what happened".

> **他说了那天的经过** *tā shuō le nà tiān de jīngguò* — He told us what happened that day.

The new character, in pieces: 经 — eight strokes in three pieces: the silk piece of 给 on the left, three strokes; on the right a fork of two strokes, a bar that turns into a sweep to the lower left and a short stroke falling to the right, over 工, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**经过** (*jīngguò*) is built from 经 'to pass through' and 过 'to go past', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keng* and *kwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the warp of a cloth' and 'a path' and the second 'to cross' and 'to go beyond'.
