# ಕನವರಿಸು (kanavarisu) — to talk in sleep

## You'll want to know: ಕನವರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C506-NIDDE506-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕನವರಿಸು** — *kanavarisu* — "to talk in sleep".

> ನಿನ್ನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ತಮ್ಮ ನಿದ್ದೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕನವರಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *ninne rātri tamma niddeyalli kanavarisidanu.*
>
> Last night my younger brother talked in his sleep.

Past (I …): ಕನವರಿಸಿದೆ, *kanavariside*.

Built on ಕನಸು, a dream: talking from inside a dream.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕನವರಿಸು** (*kanavarisu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1407 lists Kannada *kana*, *kanasu*, "a dream", and *kanavarisu*, a verb of dreaming.
