## You'll want to know: জ্বলা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C279-SHIKHA279-01]; assesses=[] -->

**জ্বলা** — *jvôlā* — "to burn; to be alight".

> যদি রাতে বাড়িতে আলো জ্বলে, তাহলে বুঝব যে কেউ আছে।
>
> *jôdi rāte bāṛite ālo jvôle, tāhôle bujhbô je keu āchhe.*
>
> If there's a light on in the house at night, I'll know someone is there.

Past (he/she …): সেই বাতি জ্বলল, *sei bāti jvôllô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জ্বলা** (*jvôlā*) comes down from Sanskrit *jvalati*, 'burns brightly' (CDIAL 5306, which lists Bengali *jalā*, 'to burn'). It is the partner of **জ্বালানো**: you light a lamp, and it burns.
