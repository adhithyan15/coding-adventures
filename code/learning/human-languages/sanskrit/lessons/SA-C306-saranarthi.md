## You'll want to know: शरणार्थी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C306-SHIBIRA306-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शरणार्थी** — *śaraṇārthī* — "a refugee".

> शरणार्थी शिबिरे भोजनं प्राप्नोति।
>
> *śaraṇārthī śibire bhojanaṃ prāpnoti.*
>
> The refugee gets food in the camp.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शरणार्थी** (*śaraṇārthī*) is built from *śaraṇa*, "refuge", and *arthin*, "seeking"; MW gives *śaraṇārthin* as seeking refuge, and a refugee is its modern use as a noun (MW).
