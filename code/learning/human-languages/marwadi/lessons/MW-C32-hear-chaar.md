# Hear *chār* — four

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-BAATI-01, MW-LEX-BACHCHA-01, MW-LEX-TEEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 18s] Say the hard wheat roll, say child, then count to three.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-CHAAR-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-TEEN-01, MW-LEX-DO-TWO-01, MW-LEX-EK-01] -->

> *chār* — **four**

Put a hand in front of your mouth and say it. There is no puff of air on the
opening consonant. That plain *ch* is the one **बच्चा** doubles.

Hold on to that, because the next chapter opens six with the same consonant
carrying a puff. In Marwari, four and six begin a breath apart.

Count: *ek, do, tīn, chār*.
