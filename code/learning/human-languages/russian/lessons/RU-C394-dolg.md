## You'll want to know: долг
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C394-DOLG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**долг** (*dolg*) — "a debt; a duty".

> У него большие долги.
>
> *U negó bolshíye dolgí.*
>
> He has big debts.

Masculine; plural *долги*. *Брать в долг* — to borrow. *Долг* is also a duty: *мой долг — помочь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**долг** is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*dŭlgŭ* (EtymDB) "a debt, an obligation".
