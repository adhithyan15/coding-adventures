Japanese, chapter 388: What the Journey Costs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
りょひ (ryohi) — travel expenses.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a plane ticket, then the Japanese for a business trip.

You'll want to know: りょひ.
りょひ — ryohi — "travel expenses".
りょこうの りょひは ちちが はらいました。 — ryokō no ryohi wa chichi ga haraimashita — "My father paid the travel expenses for the trip."

The word, taken apart.
りょひ (ryohi) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ryo, "trip" (Middle Chinese ljoX) and hi, "expense" (phj+jH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
