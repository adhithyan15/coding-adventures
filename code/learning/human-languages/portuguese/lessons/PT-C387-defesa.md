## You'll want to know: a defesa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C387-TRIBUNAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a defesa** — "the defence".

> A defesa pediu mais tempo.
>
> *The defence asked for more time.*

Also in sport, the defence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*defesa* is inherited from Late Latin *dēfēnsa* "defence" (EtymDB), from *dēfendere* "to ward off, to defend" (Lewis & Short).
