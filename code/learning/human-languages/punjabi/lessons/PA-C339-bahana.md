## You'll want to know: ਬਹਾਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C339-REFUSE339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਹਾਨਾ** — *bahānā* — "an excuse".

> ਬਹਾਨੇ ਨਾ ਕਰੋ।
>
> *bahāne nā karo.*
>
> Don't make excuses.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਹਾਨਾ** (*bahānā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *bahāna*, "pretext".
