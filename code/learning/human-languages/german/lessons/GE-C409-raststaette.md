## You'll want to know: die Raststätte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C409-UMLEITUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Raststätte** — "the motorway service area".

> An der Raststätte trinken wir einen Kaffee.
>
> *At the service area we have a coffee.*

Plural: *die Raststätten*. A smaller one without a restaurant is *der Rastplatz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Raststätte* is built from *Rast* 'rest, a break on a journey' and *Stätte* 'place', inherited from Middle High German *stat* (EtymDB).
