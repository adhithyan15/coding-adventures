# ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ (kāltuḷita) — a stampede

## You'll want to know: ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C339-GALABHE339-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ** — *kāltuḷita* — "a stampede".

> ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ ಆಗಿ ಹಲವರಿಗೆ ಗಾಯವಾಯಿತು.
>
> *jātreyalli kāltuḷita āgi halavarige gāyavāyitu.*
>
> There was a stampede at the fair and many were hurt.

ಕಾಲು, foot, and ತುಳಿತ, trampling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾಲ್ತುಳಿತ** (*kāltuḷita*) is a native Dravidian compound: *kāl*, "foot, leg" (DEDR 1479), and a noun from *tuḷi*, "to tread, to trample" (DEDR 3522).
