## You'll want to know: दासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C320-VIPLAVA320-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दासः** — *dāsaḥ* — "a slave; a servant".

> दासः क्षेत्रे कार्यं करोति।
>
> *dāsaḥ kṣetre kāryaṃ karoti.*
>
> The slave works in the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दासः** (*dāsaḥ*) MW's oldest senses are a fiend and the name of certain foes in the Veda, and it also gives *dāsa* as a slave or servant; MW gives no root in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
