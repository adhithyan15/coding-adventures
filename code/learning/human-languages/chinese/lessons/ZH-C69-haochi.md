## You'll want to know: 好吃
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C69-MEALS69-05]; assesses=[] -->

**好吃** — *hǎochī* — "tasty; good to eat".

> **我妈妈做饭很好吃** *wǒ māma zuòfàn hěn hǎochī* — My mum's cooking is delicious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**好吃** (*hǎochī*) is built from 好 'good' and 吃 'to eat': good to eat. 好 is inherited from Middle Chinese *xawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); 吃 is probably the *khek* the dictionary writes with another character and glosses 'to eat', though chī is not the sound *khek* would regularly give.
