Urdu, chapter 386: Engines and Carriages.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انجن (injan) — an engine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fruit seller, then the Urdu for a greengrocer.

You'll want to know: انجن.
انجن — injan — "an engine".
ریل گاڑی کا انجن کالا اور بڑا ہے۔ rel gāṛī kā injan kālā aur baṛā hai. The train's engine is big and black.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
انجن (injan) is borrowed from English engine; at a station it means the locomotive at the front of the train.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ڈبہ (ḍabbā) — a compartment (of a train); a box.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a greengrocer, then the Urdu for an engine.

You'll want to know: ڈبہ.
ڈبہ — ḍabbā — "a compartment (of a train); a box".
ہمارا ڈبہ انجن کے پیچھے ہے۔ hamārā ḍabbā injan ke pīchhe hai. Our carriage is right behind the engine.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ڈبہ (ḍabbā) comes from the word CDIAL files as ḍabba, "a box", of non-Aryan, perhaps Dravidian, origin (CDIAL 5528); Urdu also calls a railway carriage a box.

یہ لائن لاہور تک جاتی ہے۔ yih lāin lāhaur tak jātī hai. This line runs as far as Lahore.
Feminine.

لائن (lāin) is borrowed from English line; Urdu uses it for a railway line and for a telephone line.

Lesson 4 of 5.
سیٹی (sīṭī) — a whistle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a compartment (of a train), then the Urdu for a railway line.

You'll want to know: سیٹی.
سیٹی — sīṭī — "a whistle".
میں نے صبح انجن کی سیٹی سنی۔ maiṅ ne subah injan kī sīṭī sunī. In the morning I heard the engine's whistle.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
سیٹی (sīṭī) is inherited from sīṭṭa, "a whistle", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as an imitation of the sound (CDIAL 13427).

Lesson 5 of 5.
بجنا (bajnā) — to sound; to ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a railway line, then the Urdu for a whistle.

You'll want to know: بجنا.
بجنا — bajnā — "to sound; to ring".
اسٹیشن پر سیٹی بجی اور گاڑی چلی۔ isṭeshan par sīṭī bajī aur gāṛī chalī. The whistle sounded at the station and the train moved off.
Past (it …): وہ سیٹی بجی, voh sīṭī bajī.

The word, taken apart.
بجنا (bajnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bājnā, "to sound", under Sanskrit vādyate, "is made to sound" (CDIAL 11513). بجانا, "to play an instrument", is the form for making a thing sound, and بجے, "o'clock", is the hour that has struck.
