## You'll want to know: ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C512-ROGA512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ** — *hṛdayāghāta* — "a heart attack".

> ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ ಆದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಪ್ಪ ಸಿಗರೇಟು ಬಿಟ್ಟರು.
>
> *hṛdayāghāta āda mēle appa sigarēṭu biṭṭaru.*
>
> After his heart attack, father gave up cigarettes.

ಹೃದಯ, the heart + ಆಘಾತ, a blow: the word for a shock, now a blow to the heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ** (*hṛdayāghāta*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *hṛdaya*, "the heart", and *āghāta*, "a blow" (Monier-Williams).
