## You'll want to know: कटवाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C484-SANVARNA484-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कटवाना** — *kaṭvānā* — "to have (something) cut".

> शादी से पहले अरुण ने नाई से बाल कटवाए।
>
> *śādī se pahle Aruṇ ne nāī se bāl kaṭvāe.*
>
> Before the wedding Arun had his hair cut at the barber's.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बाल कटवाए, *usne bāl kaṭvāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कटवाना** (*kaṭvānā*) is the causative of *kāṭnā*, "to cut", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *kártati*, "cuts" (CDIAL 2854): with *-vā-*, someone else does the cutting for you. The person who does it takes *se*: *nāī se*.
