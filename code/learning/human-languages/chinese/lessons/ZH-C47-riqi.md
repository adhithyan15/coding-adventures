## You'll want to know: 日期
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C47-DATE47-05]; assesses=[] -->

**日期** — *rìqī* — "date".

> **请看日期** *qǐng kàn rìqī* — Please check the date.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**日期** (*rìqī*) is built from 日 'day' and 期 'appointed time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyit* and *gi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
