## You'll want to know: qualificarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C512-TORNEO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**qualificarsi** — "to qualify".

> Se vinciamo domani, ci qualifichiamo per la finale.
>
> *If we win tomorrow, we qualify for the final.*

*Qualificarsi per* — to qualify for.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qualificarsi* comes from Medieval Latin *quālificāre* "to give a quality", built on Latin *quālis* "of what kind" and *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short).
