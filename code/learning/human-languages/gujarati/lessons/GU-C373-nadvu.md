## You'll want to know: નડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C373-UPAY373-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નડવું** — *naḍvũ* — "to get in the way".

> જો વરસાદ નડશે તો મેચ મુલતવી રહેશે.
>
> *jo varsād naḍśe to mec mulatvī raheśe.*
>
> If the rain gets in the way, the match will be put off.

Past (it …): તે ભીડ નડી, *te bhīḍ naḍī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નડવું** (*naḍvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to obstruct", under a reconstructed *naṭati*, "trembles, totters" (CDIAL 6934).
