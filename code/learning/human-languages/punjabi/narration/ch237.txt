Punjabi, chapter 237: Milk on the Fire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉਬਾਲਣਾ (ubālṇā) — to boil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a small water pot, then the Punjabi for a deep bowl.

You'll want to know: ਉਬਾਲਣਾ.
ਉਬਾਲਣਾ — ubālṇā — "to boil".
ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਉਬਾਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ savere dudh ubāldī hāṁ. I boil the milk in the morning.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਉਬਾਲਿਆ, maiṁ dudh ubāliā.

The word, taken apart.
ਉਬਾਲਣਾ (ubālṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed ubbāl, "rise, swell, boil"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ubālaṇā, "to boil" (CDIAL 2339).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਔਟਣਾ (auṭṇā) — to boil down (milk).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a deep bowl, then the Punjabi for to boil.

You'll want to know: ਔਟਣਾ.
ਔਟਣਾ — auṭṇā — "to boil down (milk)".
ਦਾਦੀ ਦੁੱਧ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੇਰ ਔਟਦੀ ਹੈ। dādī dudh bahut der auṭdī hai. Grandmother boils the milk down for a long time.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਔਟਿਆ, maiṁ dudh auṭiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਔਟਣਾ (auṭṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit āvartayati, "causes to turn round" (the entry also gives āvartana, "stirring"); CDIAL lists Punjabi auṭṇā, "to boil", and Hindi auṭnā, "to boil, to evaporate over a fire" (CDIAL 1420).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਦਹੀਂ (dahīṁ) — yoghurt; curd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to boil, then the Punjabi for to boil down.

You'll want to know: ਦਹੀਂ.
ਦਹੀਂ — dahīṁ — "yoghurt; curd".
ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਾਲ ਦਹੀਂ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ roṭī nāl dahīṁ khāndā hāṁ. I eat yoghurt with my roti.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਦਹੀਂ (dahīṁ) is inherited from Sanskrit dadhi, "thick sour milk"; CDIAL lists Punjabi dahī̃, "thick sour milk" (CDIAL 6146).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਚਾਟੀ (chāṭī) — a churning pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to boil down, then the Punjabi for yoghurt.

You'll want to know: ਚਾਟੀ.
ਚਾਟੀ — chāṭī — "a churning pot".
ਚਾਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਹੀਂ ਹੈ। chāṭī vich dahīṁ hai. There is curd in the churning pot.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਚਾਟੀ (chāṭī) is inherited from a reconstructed cāṭṭa, "pot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi cāṭṭī, "large earthen vessel" (CDIAL 4738). CDIAL takes cāṭṭa itself from Dravidian (Tamil caṭṭi).
