## You'll want to know: ಆಸನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C435-BERTH435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಸನ** — *āsana* — "a formal seat; a posture".

> ಬಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಹಿರಿಯರಿಗೆ ಬೇರೆ ಆಸನಗಳು ಇವೆ.
>
> *bassinalli hiriyarige bēre āsanagaḷu ive.*
>
> On the bus there are separate seats for elders.

Plural: ಆಸನಗಳು, *āsanagaḷu*.

The formal word of signs and tickets, where everyday speech says ಸೀಟು; in yoga an ಆಸನ is a posture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಸನ** (*āsana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āsana*, "sitting, a seat" (CDIAL 1484), from the root *ās*, "to sit".
