## You'll want to know: visto que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C349-VISTOQUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**visto que** — "since, seeing that".

> Visto que está a chover, ficamos em casa.
>
> *Since it is raining, we are staying at home.*

Takes the indicative. A little formal; in speech *como* at the start of a sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*visto que* is built from *visto* "seen", the participle of *ver*, inherited from Latin *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short), and *que*, from Latin *quid* (EtymDB): "it being seen that".
