## You'll want to know: ઝોળી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C433-OUTFIT433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝોળી** — *jhoḷī* — "a shoulder bag (of cloth)".

> દાદાએ ઝોળીમાં ફળ ભર્યાં.
>
> *dādāe jhoḷīmā̃ phaḷ bharyā̃.*
>
> Grandfather filled his shoulder bag with fruit.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝોળી** (*jhoḷī*) goes back to *\*jhōla*, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a bag" (CDIAL 5415).
