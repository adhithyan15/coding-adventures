## You'll want to know: fiel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C499-GRATIDAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fiel** — "faithful, loyal".

> O cão é um amigo fiel.
>
> *A dog is a faithful friend.*

Plural *fiéis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fiel* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Latin *fidēlis* "faithful" (EtymDB), built on *fidēs* "faith".
