## You'll want to know: సంతాపం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C344-SANTAPAM344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సంతాపం** — *santāpaṁ* — "condolence; sympathy".

> అందరూ ఆ కుటుంబానికి సంతాపం తెలిపారు.
>
> *andarū ā kuṭumbāniki santāpaṁ telipāru.*
>
> Everyone offered condolences to that family.

సంతాపం తెలుపు, to express condolence; the one who receives it takes -కి. It is offered to a family in దుఃఖం, grief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంతాపం** (*santāpaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁtāpa*, "burning heat", and then "affliction, remorse" (CDIAL 12885).
