## You'll want to know: ફૂટબોલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C549-GOAL549-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂટબોલ** — *phūṭbol* — "football".

> જો શનિવારે મેદાન ખાલી હશે તો અમે ફૂટબોલ રમીશું.
>
> *jo śanivāre medān khālī haśe to ame phūṭbol ramīśũ.*
>
> If the ground is free on Saturday, we'll play football.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂટબોલ** (*phūṭbol*) is borrowed from English *football*.

## What you've built
