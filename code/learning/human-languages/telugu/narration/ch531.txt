Telugu, chapter 531: Babies and Heirs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
బిడ్డ (biḍḍa) — an infant; one's child.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a kinsman, then the Telugu for a granny.

You'll want to know: బిడ్డ.
బిడ్డ — biḍḍa — "an infant; one's child".
ఆ తల్లి బిడ్డను ఎత్తుకుంది. ā talli biḍḍanu ettukundi. That mother picked up her baby.
Also one's own child at any age: నా బిడ్డ (biḍḍa).

The word, taken apart.
బిడ్డ (biḍḍa) is a Telugu word for a baby or one's own child; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
శిశువు (śiśuvu) — a newborn; an infant child.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a granny, then the Telugu for an infant.

You'll want to know: శిశువు.
శిశువు — śiśuvu — "a newborn; an infant child".
ఆసుపత్రిలో శిశువు ఆరోగ్యంగా ఉంది. āsupatrilō śiśuvu ārōgyaṅgā undi. The newborn in the hospital is healthy.
Formal and medical; పాప or బాబు in speech.

The word, taken apart.
శిశువు (śiśuvu) is borrowed from Sanskrit śiśu, "the young of an animal, a child" (CDIAL 12476).

Lesson 3 of 5.
సంతానం (santānaṁ) — offspring; children.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an infant, then the Telugu for a newborn.

You'll want to know: సంతానం.
సంతానం — santānaṁ — "offspring; children".
ఆ దంపతులకు ముగ్గురు సంతానం. ā dampatulaku mugguru santānaṁ. That couple has three children.
All of one's children taken together.

The word, taken apart.
సంతానం (santānaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṁtāna, "continued succession, an unbroken line" (Monier-Williams): the line a family continues in.

Lesson 4 of 5.
వారసుడు (vārasuḍu) — an heir; a successor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a newborn, then the Telugu for offspring.

You'll want to know: వారసుడు.
వారసుడు — vārasuḍu — "an heir; a successor".
ఆ ఆస్తికి అతనే వారసుడు. ā āstiki atanē vārasuḍu. He alone is the heir to that property.
Feminine వారసురాలు; వారసత్వం, inheritance.

The word, taken apart.
వారసుడు (vārasuḍu) is usually traced to Arabic through Hindustani wāris, "an heir", with the Telugu masculine ending -డు.

Lesson 5 of 5.
జోలపాట (jōlapāṭa) — a lullaby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for offspring, then the Telugu for an heir.

You'll want to know: జోలపాట.
జోలపాట — jōlapāṭa — "a lullaby".
అమ్మ జోలపాట పాడగానే పాప నిద్రపోయింది. amma jōlapāṭa pāḍagānē pāpa nidrapōyindi. As soon as mum sang a lullaby, the baby fell asleep.
జోల, rocking, + పాట, song.

The word, taken apart.
జోలపాట (jōlapāṭa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2889 lists Telugu jōla, "lullaby, cradle song", beside Kannada jōl, "to swing"; పాట is "song" (DEDR 4065).
