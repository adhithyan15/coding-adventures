## You'll want to know: o fuso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C421-AEROPORTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o fuso** — "the time zone (o fuso horário); the spindle".

> Mudamos de fuso horário e perdemos uma hora.
>
> *We change time zones and lose an hour.*

*O fuso horário* — the time zone. A spindle for spinning wool is also *um fuso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fuso* is inherited from Latin *fūsus* 'spindle' (EtymDB): the time zones on a globe have the shape of a spindle.
