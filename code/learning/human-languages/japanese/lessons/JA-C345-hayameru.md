## You'll want to know: はやめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C345-HENKOU345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**はやめる** — *hayameru* — "to bring forward; to make earlier".

> **ゆきが ふったら、しゅっぱつを はやめます。** — *yuki ga futtara, shuppatsu o hayamemasu* — "If it snows, we will set off earlier."

Polite **はやめます**, *hayamemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが しゅっぱつを はやめました。**, *chichi ga shuppatsu o hayamemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はやめる** (*hayameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
