# പരിഗണന (parigaṇana) — consideration

## You'll want to know: പരിഗണന
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C556-VANCANA556-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിഗണന** — *parigaṇana* — "consideration".

> പ്രായമായവർക്ക് ബസിൽ പ്രത്യേക പരിഗണന കിട്ടണം.
>
> *prāyamāyavarkkŭ basil pratyēka parigaṇana kiṭṭaṇaṁ.*
>
> Elderly people should get special consideration on the bus.

പരിഗണിക്കുക is to consider.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിഗണന** (*parigaṇana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *parigaṇana*, a reckoning or counting over (Monier-Williams): to consider someone is to count them in.
