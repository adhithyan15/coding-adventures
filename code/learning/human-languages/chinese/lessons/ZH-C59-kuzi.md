## You'll want to know: kùzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C59-WEAR59-04]; assesses=[] -->

*kùzi* — "trousers".

> *zhè shì wǒ gēge de kùzi* — These are my brother's trousers.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kùzi* is built from kù and the noun ending zi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khuH* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first under a variant form of the character and explains it, through an older book, as a garment for the shins.
