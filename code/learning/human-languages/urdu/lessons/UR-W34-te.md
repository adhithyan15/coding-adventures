# ت (te) — the letter that opens تم

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-VAO-01, UR-SCRIPT-C34-BE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write و and ب once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **تم** (*tum*), "you (familiar)".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C34-TE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ت** — *te*, the sound *t*, with the tongue against the teeth.

It opens **تم** (*tum*), the familiar "you". The same boat again, now with **two
dots above**.

## Writing: ت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C34-TE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: sweep the independent be-series bowl from right to left
- **2.** after one lift, place the left dot above the main line
- **3.** after another lift, place the right dot beside it — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, Te, mīm, jīm, che, and more diacritics, independent ت handwriting animation and Te instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-VAO-01, UR-SCRIPT-C34-BE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C34-TE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **تم** and point at ت inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ت three times, saying *t* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ب, then ت, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **تم** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-VAO-01, UR-SCRIPT-C34-BE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C34-TE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ت? (**te**, *t*.) Which word did it
come from? (**تم**, *tum*, "you (familiar)".)
