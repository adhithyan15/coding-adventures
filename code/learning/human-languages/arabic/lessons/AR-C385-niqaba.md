## You'll want to know: نقابة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C385-MUNAZZAMA385-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نقابة** — *niqāba* — "a trade union".

> دافعت النقابة عن العمال.
>
> *dāfaʿat an-niqāba ʿan al-ʿummāl.*
>
> The union defended the workers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقابة** (*niqāba*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-b*, "to bore through; a chief" (Lane). A *naqīb* is the chief of a group; *niqāba* is his office, and now a trade union.
