## You'll want to know: उखड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C450-JHADNA450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उखड़ना** — *ukhaṛnā* — "to be uprooted; to come loose".

> तूफ़ान में कई पेड़ उखड़ गए।
>
> *tūfān meṁ kaī peṛ ukhaṛ gae.*
>
> Several trees were uprooted in the storm.

Past (it …): वह खंभा उखड़ गया, *vah khambhā ukhaṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उखड़ना** (*ukhaṛnā*) goes back to *\*utskarati*, "digs out", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1895), which lists Hindi *ukhaṛnā*, "to be rooted up".
