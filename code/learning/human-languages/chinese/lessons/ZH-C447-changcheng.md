## You'll want to know: 长城
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C447-WALK447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**长城** — *Chángchéng* — "the Great Wall".

> **我们坐车去长城** *wǒmen zuò chē qù Chángchéng* — We're going to the great wall by bus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**长城** (*Chángchéng*) is built from 长 'long' and 城 'a wall round a city', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjang* and *dzyeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'long' and 'far'.
