## You'll want to know: घाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C241-FALL241-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घाव** — *ghāv* — "a wound".

> घाव छोटो है, पण दरद घणो है।
>
> *ghāv chhoṭo hai, paṇ darad ghaṇo hai.*
>
> The wound is small, but it hurts a lot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घाव** (*ghāv*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ghāta*, "a blow, a bruise". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *ghāu*, "wound" (CDIAL 4460).
