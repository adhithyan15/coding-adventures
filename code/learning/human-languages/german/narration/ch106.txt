5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Supermarkt — a supermarket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a theatre, then the German for a town hall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say der Morgen, then the one article German uses for every plural: die.
