## You'll want to know: बहनोई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C260-BAHU260-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बहनोई** — *bahnoī* — "a sister's husband".

> म्हारो बहनोई मास्टर है।
>
> *mhāro bahnoī māsṭar hai.*
>
> My sister's husband is a teacher.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहनोई** (*bahnoī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhaginīpati*, "a sister's husband"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bahaṇeu* under it (CDIAL 9350).
