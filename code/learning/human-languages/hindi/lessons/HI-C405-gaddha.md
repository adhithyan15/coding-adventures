## You'll want to know: गड्ढा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C405-GATI405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गड्ढा** — *gaḍḍhā* — "a pothole; a pit".

> इस सड़क में बहुत गड्ढे हैं।
>
> *is saṛak meṁ bahut gaḍḍhe haiṁ.*
>
> This road has a lot of potholes.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गड्ढा** (*gaḍḍhā*) belongs with *\*gaḍḍa*, "hole, pit", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3981); CDIAL gives Hindi *gāṛā*, "hole", there, but not this form with ḍḍh, so the link is probable rather than shown.
