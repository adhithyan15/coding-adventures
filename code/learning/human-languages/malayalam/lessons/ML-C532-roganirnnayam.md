## You'll want to know: രോഗനിർണ്ണയം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C532-ROGAM532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**രോഗനിർണ്ണയം** — *rōganirṇṇayaṁ* — "a diagnosis".

> പരിശോധനയ്ക്ക് ശേഷം വൈദ്യൻ രോഗനിർണ്ണയം നടത്തി.
>
> *pariśōdhanaykkŭ śēṣaṁ vaidyan rōganirṇṇayaṁ naṭatti.*
>
> After the tests the doctor made a diagnosis.

രോഗം an illness + നിർണ്ണയം a settling, a decision.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**രോഗനിർണ്ണയം** (*rōganirṇṇayaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *roga*, "disease", and *nirṇaya*, "taking off, settling, a decision" (Monier-Williams).
