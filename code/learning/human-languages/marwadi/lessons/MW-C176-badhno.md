## You'll want to know: बांधणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C176-GANTH176-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बांधणो** — *bā̃dhṇo* — "to tie".

> हूं गाय बांधूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ gāy bā̃dhū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I tie up the cow.

Past (I …): म्हैं गाय बांधी, *mhaĩ gāy bā̃dhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बांधणो** (*bā̃dhṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bandhati*, "binds"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb under it (CDIAL 9139).
