Urdu, chapter 511: Laying the Cloth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ترکیب (tarkīb) — a recipe; a method.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (something) ground, then the Urdu for to squeeze out.

You'll want to know: ترکیب.
ترکیب — tarkīb — "a recipe; a method".
یہ نانی کی ترکیب ہے۔ yih nānī kī tarkīb hai. This is grandmother's recipe.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ترکیب (tarkīb) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tarkīb, "putting together".

Lesson 3 of 5.
پکوانا (pakvānā) — to have (food) cooked.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a recipe, then the Urdu for a cloth spread for a meal.

You'll want to know: پکوانا.
پکوانا — pakvānā — "to have (food) cooked".
ابو نے مہمانوں کے لیے بریانی پکوائی۔ abbū ne mehmānoṅ ke liye biryānī pakvāī. Dad had biryani cooked for the guests.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے حلوہ پکوایا, us ne halvā pakvāyā.

The word, taken apart.
پکوانا (pakvānā) is the second causative of paknā, "to be cooked", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit pakva, "cooked, ripe" (CDIAL 7621): to have someone cook.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a cloth spread for a meal, then the Urdu for to have (food) cooked.

You'll want to know: پھونکنا.
پھونکنا — phūṅknā — "to blow (on)".
بچہ گرم دودھ پھونک کر پیتا ہے۔ bachchā garm dūdh phūṅk kar pītā hai. The child blows on hot milk before drinking it.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چولہے کی آگ پھونکی, us ne chūlhe kī āg phūṅkī.

The word, taken apart.
پھونکنا (phūṅknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phū̃knā, "to blow", under phūtka, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9102).

Lesson 5 of 5.
چکھانا (chakhānā) — to give (someone) a taste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (food) cooked, then the Urdu for to blow (on).

You'll want to know: چکھانا.
چکھانا — chakhānā — "to give (someone) a taste".
امی نے مہمان کو حلوہ چکھایا۔ ammī ne mehmān ko halvā chakhāyā. Mum gave the guest a taste of the halwa.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مہمانوں کو حلوہ چکھایا, us ne mehmānoṅ ko halvā chakhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
چکھانا (chakhānā) is the causative of chakhnā, "to taste"; CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin cākhnā, "to taste", under cakṣati, "tastes", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4557).
