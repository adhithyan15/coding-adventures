# খুঁতখুঁতে (khũtkhũte) — fussy; hard to please

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C464-GOMBHIR464-01, BN-LEX-C464-GOMBHIR464-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for grave, then the Bengali for unpretentious.

## You'll want to know: খুঁতখুঁতে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C464-GOMBHIR464-03]; assesses=[] -->

**খুঁতখুঁতে** — *khũtkhũte* — "fussy; hard to please".

> খাবার নিয়ে অরুণ খুঁতখুঁতে।
>
> *khābār niye ôrun khũtkhũte.*
>
> Arun is fussy about food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খুঁতখুঁতে** (*khũtkhũte*) doubles *khũt*, "a flaw": someone who finds a flaw in everything. The origin of *khũt* is not settled.
