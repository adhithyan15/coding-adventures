## You'll want to know: انطباع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C359-NAZAR359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انطباع** — *inṭibāʿ* — "an impression".

> ترك الفندق انطباعا جيدا عندي.
>
> *taraka al-funduq inṭibāʿan jayyidan ʿindī.*
>
> The hotel left a good impression on me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انطباع** (*inṭibāʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-b-ʿ*, "to stamp, to imprint" (Lane). *Inṭibāʿ* is the Form VII verbal noun, being stamped: the mark a thing leaves on the mind, an impression.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
