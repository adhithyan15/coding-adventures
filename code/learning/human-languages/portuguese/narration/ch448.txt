Portuguese, chapter 448: Jornais e tabaco.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
a tabacaria — the tobacconist, the newsagent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to potter about, then the Portuguese for the viewpoint.

You'll want to know: a tabacaria.
a tabacaria — "the tobacconist, the newsagent".
Compro o jornal na tabacaria da esquina. I buy the paper at the newsagent on the corner.
It sells newspapers, magazines and lottery tickets too.

The word, taken apart.
tabacaria is built on tabaco with -aria, a shop (EtymDB); tabaco was borrowed from Spanish tabaco.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 6.

You'll want to know: o fósforo.
o fósforo — "the match; phosphorus".
Acendi a vela com um fósforo. I lit the candle with a match.
Uma caixa de fósforos — a box of matches.

The word, taken apart.
fósforo is a learned borrowing, through Latin, from Greek phōsphóros 'light-bearing', from phôs 'light' and phérein 'to carry' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the lighter, then the Portuguese for the match.

You'll want to know: a raspadinha.
a raspadinha — "the scratch card".
A minha tia compra uma raspadinha todas as semanas. My aunt buys a scratch card every week.
Built on raspar, to scratch off.

The word, taken apart.
raspadinha is a small form built on raspar 'to scrape', which is inherited from Vulgar Latin raspāre (EtymDB).

folhear — "to leaf through, to flick through".
Folheei uma revista enquanto esperava. I leafed through a magazine while I waited.
Built on a folha, the page.

folhear is built on folha 'leaf, page', which is inherited from Latin folia, the plural of folium 'leaf' (EtymDB).
