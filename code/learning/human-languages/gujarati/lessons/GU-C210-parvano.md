## You'll want to know: પરવાનો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C210-PAPERS210-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પરવાનો** — *parvāno* — "permit, licence".

> **ડ્રાઈવર પાસે પરવાનો ન હતો.** — *ḍraivar pāse parvāno na hato* — "The driver had no licence."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરવાનો** (*parvāno*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *parvāna*, "warrant, licence". CDIAL has no entry for it.
