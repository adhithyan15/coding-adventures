## You'll want to know: はげしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C278-INAZUMA278-03]; assesses=[] -->

**はげしい** — *hageshii* — "violent; fierce".

> **きのう 日本中で はげしい あめが ふりました。** — *kinō nihonjū de hageshii ame ga furimashita* — "Yesterday fierce rain fell all over Japan."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はげしい** (*hageshii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
