Latin, chapter 64: Neck, Shoulder, Knee, Heart.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
collum, collī — the neck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for the tongue; a language, then the Latin for the nose.

You'll want to know: collum, collī.
collum, collī — "the neck". A collar goes round it.

What you've built.
One more word for neck, shoulder, knee, heart.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: collum]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: collum, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say collum]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Latin for the tongue; a language, then the Latin for the nose, then say collum again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does collum, collī mean? ("The neck".) Say it once more.
