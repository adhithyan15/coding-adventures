## You'll want to know: दोहराना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C391-PRAYAS391-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दोहराना** — *dohrānā* — "to repeat, to go over again".

> अगर तुम यह पाठ दो बार दोहराओगे तो याद हो जाएगा।
>
> *agar tum yah pāṭh do bār dohrāoge to yād ho jāegā.*
>
> If you go over this lesson twice, you'll remember it.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सवाल दोहराया, *usne savāl dohrāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दोहराना** (*dohrānā*) is a verb made on दोहरा, "double", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed *\*dudhāra* (CDIAL 6407): to do a thing twice over.
