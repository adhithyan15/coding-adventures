## You'll want to know: મહોલ્લો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C419-STREET419-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મહોલ્લો** — *mahollo* — "a quarter of a town; a locality".

> આ મહોલ્લામાં એક મોટું મંદિર છે.
>
> *ā mahollāmā̃ ek moṭũ mandir che.*
>
> There is a big temple in this quarter.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મહોલ્લો** (*mahollo*) is usually traced to Arabic *maḥalla*, "a place where people settle; a quarter of a town", which came through Persian.
