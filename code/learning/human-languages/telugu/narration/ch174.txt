Telugu, chapter 174: On the other side, That way, This way, Cave, Valley.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
అవతల (avatala) — on the other side, beyond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for beside, then the Telugu for a post office.

You'll want to know: అవతల.
అవతల — avatala — "on the other side, beyond".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: avatala]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: avatala, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say avatala]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for beside, then the Telugu for a post office, then say avatala again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does అవతల (avatala) mean? ("On the other side, beyond".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.

Diarrhoea? (విరేచనాలు, virēcanālu.) A tablet? (మాత్ర, mātra.) Sweat? (చెమట, cemaṭa.) A swelling? (వాపు, vāpu.) Jealousy? (అసూయ, asūya.) Pride? (గర్వం, garvaṁ.) Enthusiasm? (ఉత్సాహం, utsāhaṁ.) Peace? (శాంతి, śānti.) Patience? (సహనం, sahanaṁ.) A sore? (పుండు, puṇḍu.) A blister? (బొబ్బ, bobba.) A rash? (దద్దుర్లు, daddurlu.) A scar? (మచ్చ, macca.) A sprain? (బెణుకు, beṇuku.)
