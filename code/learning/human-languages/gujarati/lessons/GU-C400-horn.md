## You'll want to know: હોર્ન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C400-SCOOTER400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**હોર્ન** — *horn* — "a vehicle horn".

> ડ્રાઈવરે ગલીમાં હોર્ન વગાડ્યું.
>
> *ḍrāīvre galīmā̃ horn vagāḍyũ.*
>
> The driver sounded the horn in the lane.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હોર્ન** (*horn*) is borrowed from English *horn*, the horn of a car or a scooter.
