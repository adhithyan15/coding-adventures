## You'll want to know: گردباد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C475-SAEGHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گردباد** (*gerdbâd*) — "a whirlwind, a tornado".

> در بیابان یک گردباد کوچک دیدیم.
>
> *Dar biyâbân yek gerdbâd-e kuchek didim.*
>
> We saw a small whirlwind in the desert.

*gerd* 'round' + *bâd* 'wind'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gerdbâd* is a native compound of *gerd* 'round', which EtymDB links with PIE *wert-* 'to turn', and *bâd* 'wind'.
