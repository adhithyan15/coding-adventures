# Review — describing an experience

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-MEILLEUR-02, FR-GRAM-DEPUIS-PRESENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the coffee is better than the tea". (*Le café est meilleur
que le thé.*) Then "I have been living here for three years". (*J'habite ici
depuis trois ans.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-PLUS-QUE-01, FR-GRAM-PENDANT-DUREE-02, FR-GRAM-SENTIMENT-ETAIS-01, FR-GRAM-ESPERER-INFINITIF-01] -->

Two chapters, and an experience can be told whole: how it felt, what you
hope next, how it compares, and how long it lasted. *Plus … que*, *moins …
que*, *aussi … que*, with *meilleur* for the one adjective that refuses.
*Depuis* with the present for what is still going on, *pendant* with the
passé composé for what is over, and *il y a* for "ago".

[PAUSE 2s] Here is a life in four lines:

> *J'ai habité à Paris pendant deux ans. C'était une ville plus chère que
> Lyon, mais j'étais content. J'habite à Lyon depuis janvier. J'espère
> rester ici.*

*Rester*, "to stay", is one of the standing verb's children, Latin
*re-stāre*: to stay back.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-PLUS-QUE-01, FR-GRAM-DEPUIS-PRESENT-01, FR-GRAM-PENDANT-DUREE-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In *j'habite à Lyon depuis janvier*, why the present? (The living
is **still going on**.) Say "less expensive than the car". (***Moins cher que
la voiture.***) And which word says "ago"? (***Il y a.***)
