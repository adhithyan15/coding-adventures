## You'll want to know: testificar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C523-PRESO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**testificar** — "to testify".

> La vecina testificó en el juicio.
>
> *The neighbour testified at the trial.*

It takes *qu* before *e*: *testifiqué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testificar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *testificārī* 'to bear witness' (EtymDB), built on *testis* 'a witness' and *facere* 'to make'.

## What you've built
