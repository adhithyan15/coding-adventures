# Ask the reader, then share one small answer

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-HOW-ARE-YOU-03] -->

The opening already names Manan. The old respectful question asks how the
reader is. The old **ਮੈਂ** sentence gives Aman's own wellbeing.

## Reading — two taught jobs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-HOW-ARE-YOU-03, PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

> ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਹੋ?
>
> ਮੈਂ ਠੀਕ-ਠਾਕ ਹਾਂ।

The first line faces Manan; the second speaks for Aman. The question mark
belongs to the first line, the Gurmukhi sentence mark to the second.

## Writing — supported wellbeing block
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-HOW-ARE-YOU-03, PA-GRAMMAR-TUSI-HO-03] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy the two lines once. Keep **ਹੋ** with **ਤੁਸੀਂ** and **ਹਾਂ** with
**ਮੈਂ**. No new reply or verb form is needed.
