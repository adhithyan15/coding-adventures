# એ — independent e

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-GA-01, GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01] -->

Read **ગ** once, then write independent **અ** once from memory. Say “standing
alone,” then put both away. The next independent vowel has its own whole-letter
form.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-E-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **એ** visible. Circle the left bowl and continue through the lower body
and small right arch. Lift once for the full-height right stem and foot. Lift
again and sweep the high arc from left to right.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-E-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **એ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice joined body, separate
stem, high arc; two pen lifts.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-E-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **એ** once. Compare only the separate stem and high arc; repair
one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-E-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W06-independent-e-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-E-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write independent e from memory.","answer":"એ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"એ — the body, stem, and high arc are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at એ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write independent *e* once. Uncover **એ**, compare, and stop
after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-E-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say why this is a whole vowel letter rather than a mark attached to
a consonant.
