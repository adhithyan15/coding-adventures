## You'll want to know: ਕਵਿਤਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C349-SONG349-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਵਿਤਾ** — *kavitā* — "a poem; poetry".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਹਰ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਕਵਿਤਾ ਲਿਖਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī bhaiṇ har hafte ikk kavitā likhdī hai.*
>
> My sister writes a poem every week.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਵਿਤਾ** (*kavitā*) is taken over from Sanskrit *kavitā*, "a poem", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
