## You'll want to know: लाडका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C460-MAYA460-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लाडका** — *lāḍkā* — "beloved; pet".

> ही मांजर आमची लाडकी आहे.
>
> *hī māñjar āmcī lāḍkī āhe.*
>
> This cat is our darling.

Agrees: लाडका मुलगा, लाडकी मुलगी, लाडकं बाळ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाडका** (*lāḍkā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *lāḍkā*, "darling", under the reconstructed *\*laḍikka*, "child" (CDIAL 10924).
