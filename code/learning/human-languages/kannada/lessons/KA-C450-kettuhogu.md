## You'll want to know: ಕೆಟ್ಟುಹೋಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C450-GARAGE450-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೆಟ್ಟುಹೋಗು** — *keṭṭuhōgu* — "to break down; to go bad".

> ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿಯ ಮಧ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನಮ್ಮ ಬಸ್ ಕೆಟ್ಟುಹೋಯಿತು.
>
> *heddāriya madhyadalli namma bas keṭṭuhōyitu.*
>
> Our bus broke down in the middle of the highway.

Past (it …): ಕೆಟ್ಟುಹೋಯಿತು, *keṭṭuhōyitu*.

Of food it means to spoil: ಹಾಲು ಕೆಟ್ಟುಹೋಯಿತು, the milk went off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೆಟ್ಟುಹೋಗು** (*keṭṭuhōgu*) is native Dravidian: *keṭṭu*, the past stem of *keḍu*, "to be ruined, to spoil" (DEDR 1942), with ಹೋಗು, to go.
