Gujarati, chapter 460: Innocent or Sly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ભોળું (bhoḷũ) — innocent; trusting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for good-hearted, then the Gujarati for to soften with pity.

You'll want to know: ભોળું.
ભોળું — bhoḷũ — "innocent; trusting".
નાનું બાળક ભોળું હોય છે. nānũ bāḷak bhoḷũ hoy che. A small child is trusting.

The word, taken apart.
ભોળું (bhoḷũ) goes back to a reconstructed bhulla-, "defective"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "foolish, credulous" (CDIAL 9539), and today it is mostly the innocence.

Lesson 2 of 5.
લુચ્ચું (luccũ) — sly; crafty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to soften with pity, then the Gujarati for trusting.

You'll want to know: લુચ્ચું.
લુચ્ચું — luccũ — "sly; crafty".
તે લુચ્ચો વેપારી બધાને છેતરે છે. te lucco vepārī badhāne chetre che. That sly trader cheats everyone.

The word, taken apart.
લુચ્ચું (luccũ) goes back to a reconstructed lucca-, "defective"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with the gloss "lewd" (CDIAL 11073). Gujarati today uses it for someone sly, and no dictionary we use says how the two senses are linked.

Lesson 4 of 5.
કપટ (kapaṭ) — deceit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for sly, then the Gujarati for shrewd.

You'll want to know: કપટ.
કપટ — kapaṭ — "deceit".
તેના વચનમાં કપટ હતું. tenā vacanmā̃ kapaṭ hatũ. There was deceit in his promise.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
કપટ (kapaṭ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kapaṭa, "fraud, deceit", as Monier-Williams records it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ફોસલાવવું (phoslāvvũ) — to coax; to cajole.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for shrewd, then the Gujarati for deceit.

You'll want to know: ફોસલાવવું.
ફોસલાવવું — phoslāvvũ — "to coax; to cajole".
માતા બાળકને મીઠાઈથી ફોસલાવે છે. mātā bāḷakne mīṭhāīthī phoslāve che. The mother coaxes the child with sweets.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને ફોસલાવ્યું, teṇe bāḷakne phoslāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ફોસલાવવું (phoslāvvũ) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to cajole", under Sanskrit spṛśati, "touches, strokes" (CDIAL 13815).
