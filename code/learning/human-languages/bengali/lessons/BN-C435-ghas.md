## You'll want to know: ঘাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C435-GHAS435-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘাস** — *ghās* — "grass".

> সকালে মাঠের ঘাস শিশিরে ভেজা।
>
> *sôkāle māṭher ghās shishire bhejā.*
>
> In the morning the grass on the field is wet with dew.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘাস** (*ghās*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ghās*, "grass", under Sanskrit *ghāsá*, "fodder, grass" (CDIAL 4471).
