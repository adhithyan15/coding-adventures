## You'll want to know: la navette
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C414-COUCHETTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la navette** — "the shuttle (bus or train)".

> Une navette relie le centre-ville à l'aéroport.
>
> *A shuttle links the town centre with the airport.*

*Faire la navette* — to go back and forth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navette* is a small *nef* 'ship', from Latin *nāvis* 'ship' (Lewis & Short): first the boat-shaped shuttle of a loom that runs back and forth.
