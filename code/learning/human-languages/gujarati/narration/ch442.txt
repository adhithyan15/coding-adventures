Gujarati, chapter 442: Waves and Nets.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સમુદ્ર (samudra) — the ocean; the sea (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a riverbank, then the Gujarati for to sink in.

You'll want to know: સમુદ્ર.
સમુદ્ર — samudra — "the ocean; the sea (formal)".
સમુદ્રનું પાણી ખારું છે. samudranũ pāṇī khārũ che. The water of the ocean is salty.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સમુદ્ર (samudra) is a tatsama, Sanskrit samudra, "a gathering together of waters", the sea (Monier-Williams); speech says દરિયો.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ડોલવું (ḍolvũ) — to rock gently; to sway (on water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a river boat, then the Gujarati for a fishing net.

You'll want to know: ડોલવું.
ડોલવું — ḍolvũ — "to rock gently; to sway (on water)".
લહેરોમાં હોડી ડોલે છે. laheromā̃ hoḍī ḍole che. The boat rocks on the waves.
Past (it …): તે હોડી ડોલી, te hoḍī ḍolī.

The word, taken apart.
ડોલવું (ḍolvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit dōlāyatē, "swings to and fro"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to dangle, to oscillate" (CDIAL 6585).
