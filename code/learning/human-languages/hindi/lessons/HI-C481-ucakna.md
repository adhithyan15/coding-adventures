# उचकना (ucaknā) — to stand on tiptoe; to spring up

## You'll want to know: उचकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C481-LETNA481-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उचकना** — *ucaknā* — "to stand on tiptoe; to spring up".

> ऊपर की अलमारी तक पहुँचने के लिए मीरा उचकी।
>
> *ūpar kī almārī tak pahũcne ke liye Mīrā ucakī.*
>
> Mira stood on tiptoe to reach the top cupboard.

Past (he …): वह उचका, *vah ucakā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उचकना** (*ucaknā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *ucaknā*, "to leap up", under Sanskrit *ucca*, "high" (CDIAL 1634): to push oneself higher.
