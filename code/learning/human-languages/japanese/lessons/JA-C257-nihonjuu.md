# 日本中 (nihonjū) — all over Japan

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C257-NORIOKURERU-01, JA-LEX-C256-NIHONJIN-01, JA-LEX-C253-NENJUU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say "a Japanese person" and "all year round". (*nihonjin*, *nenjū*.)

## You'll want to know: 日本中
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C257-NIHONJUU-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C25-FAMILY25-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-JIN-01] -->

**日本中** — *nihonjū* — "all over Japan", the whole country through. The **じゅう** at its end is the one in **年じゅう**.

> **日本中に ともだちが います。**
>
> *nihonjū ni tomodachi ga imasu.* — "I have friends all over Japan."

You write **日本**. The third sign is new, read **じゅう** (*jū*) here; the next lesson writes it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**日本中** (*nihonjū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ni*, "sun" (Middle Chinese *nyit*), *hon*, "origin" (*pwonX*) and *jū*, "in, inside, middle" (*trjuwng*), voiced from *chū* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict glosses it "throughout Japan" (JMdict).
