## You'll want to know: evacuare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C304-TERREMOTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**evacuare** — "to evacuate".

> Hanno evacuato tutto il paese.
>
> *They evacuated the whole village.*

People or buildings: *evacuare la scuola*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*evacuare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēvacuāre* "to empty out" (EtymDB), built on *vacuus* "empty".
