## You'll want to know: ēducō, ēducāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C324-PUER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ēducō, ēducāre** — "to bring up, to rear".

> Avia eum ēducāvit, quod parentēs aberant.
>
> *His grandmother brought him up, because his parents were away.*

*ēducō, ēducāre, ēducāvī, ēducātum*, first conjugation, short *u*. Do not confuse *ēdūcō, ēdūcere*, 'to lead out'. English *educate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ēducō** is built on *ēdūcō*, "to lead out, to draw out" (Lewis & Short), and so on *dūcō*, "to lead". English *educate* comes from it.
