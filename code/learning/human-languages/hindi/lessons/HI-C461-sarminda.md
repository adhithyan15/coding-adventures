## You'll want to know: शर्मिंदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C461-SANKOCH461-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शर्मिंदा** — *śarmindā* — "ashamed; embarrassed".

> अपनी ग़लती पर अरुण शर्मिंदा था।
>
> *apnī ġaltī par Aruṇ śarmindā thā.*
>
> Arun was ashamed of his mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शर्मिंदा** (*śarmindā*) is usually traced to Persian *sharminda*, "ashamed", the adjective beside the noun that gave Hindi *śarm*.
