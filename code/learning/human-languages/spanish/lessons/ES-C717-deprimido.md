## You'll want to know: deprimido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C717-SUFRIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**deprimido** — "depressed, low".

> Desde que perdió el trabajo está deprimido.
>
> *Since he lost his job he has been depressed.*

Used with *estar*. For the illness, say *tener depresión*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*deprimido* is the participle of *deprimir*, which comes from Latin *dēprimere* "to press down" (EtymDB), a learned word built on *premere* "to press".
