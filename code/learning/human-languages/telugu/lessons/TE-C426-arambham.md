## You'll want to know: ఆరంభం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C426-ARAMBHAM426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరంభం** — *ārambhaṁ* — "a beginning; a start".

> ఆరంభంలో పని కష్టంగా ఉంది.
>
> *ārambhaṁlō pani kaṣṭaṅgā undi.*
>
> At the start the work is hard.

Formal beside మొదలు; ఆరంభించు, to begin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరంభం** (*ārambhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ārambha*, "beginning" (CDIAL 1307).
