## You'll want to know: ਫ਼ਿਲਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C350-SHOW350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਿਲਮ** — *filam* — "a film".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੁਰਾਣੀਆਂ ਫ਼ਿਲਮਾਂ ਪਸੰਦ ਹਨ।
>
> *mainū̃ purāṇīāṁ filmāṁ pasand han.*
>
> I like old films.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਿਲਮ** (*filam*) is borrowed from English *film*.
