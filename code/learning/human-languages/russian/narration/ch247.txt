Lesson 1 of 5.
радио (rádio) — a radio.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a saucer, then the Russian for a mug.

You'll want to know: радио.
радио (rádio) — "a radio".

The word, taken apart.
радио (rádio) is an international word from Latin radius, "a ray, a spoke": signals sent in rays.

Lesson 2 of 5.
наушники (naúshniki) — headphones.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a mug, then the Russian for a radio.

You'll want to know: наушники.
наушники (naúshniki) — "headphones".

The word, taken apart.
наушники (naúshniki) is на, "on", with уши, "ears", and -ники: things on the ears. ухо is a cousin of Latin auris, "ear" (Vasmer).

You'll want to know: паспорт.
паспорт (pásport) — "a passport".

The word, taken apart.
паспорт (pásport) — пашпорт in 1633, паспорт (pásport) by 1710; it came through German Passport (Vasmer), from French passeport, "pass (through) the port".

The word, taken apart.
документ (dokumént) — from Peter the Great's time; it came through German Dokument or Polish dokument from Latin documentum, "a proof, a lesson", from docēre, "to teach, to show" (Vasmer).
