# वहाँ (vahā̃) — there — where I am not

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-02, HI-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You can already name things. This is how you POINT at them.

## You'll want to know: वहाँ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वहाँ** — *vahā̃* — there — where I am not.

Say it, and picture the thing it lands on as you say it. That is the whole word:
it does not mean anything on its own, it means whatever the speaker is pointing at.

Its partner is **यहाँ** *yahā̃*, which you met in the last lesson. The two of them
differ by one sound at the front — **v-** for far — and that is not a
coincidence. It is the whole system, and the last lesson of this chapter says so.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वहाँ** (*vahāṁ*) pairs the stem of **वह**, "that", with the same place ending *-hāṁ* that **यहाँ**, "here", has. CDIAL has no entry for it, so its older history is left open here.
