Kannada, chapter 325: A Wedding Is Planned.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಿವಾಹ (vivāha) — a marriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to extinguish, then the Kannada for to rescue.

You'll want to know: ವಿವಾಹ.
ವಿವಾಹ — vivāha — "a marriage".
ಎಲ್ಲವೂ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ನಡೆದರೆ ಮುಂದಿನ ತಿಂಗಳು ಅವರ ವಿವಾಹ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತದೆ. ellavū sariyāgi naḍedare mundina tiṅgaḷu avara vivāha naḍeyuttade. If all goes well, their marriage takes place next month.
ವಿವಾಹ (vivāha) is the formal word, on cards and in the news; at home people say ಮದುವೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿವಾಹ (vivāha) is borrowed from Sanskrit vivāha, "marriage", literally "leading away" of the bride (CDIAL 11920).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ (āratakṣate) — a wedding reception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to rescue, then the Kannada for a marriage.

You'll want to know: ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ.
ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ — āratakṣate — "a wedding reception".
ಮದುವೆಯ ಹಿಂದಿನ ಸಂಜೆ ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ ಇತ್ತು. maduveya hindina sañje āratakṣate ittu. The wedding reception was on the evening before the wedding.
The evening when guests meet the couple and bless them with rice grains.

The word, taken apart.
ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ (āratakṣate) is usually traced to ಆರತಿ, the waving of a lamp, and ಅಕ್ಷತೆ, grains of unbroken rice, from Sanskrit akṣata, "unbroken" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ (varadakṣiṇe) — a dowry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a marriage, then the Kannada for a wedding reception.

You'll want to know: ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ.
ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ — varadakṣiṇe — "a dowry".
ಆ ಕುಟುಂಬ ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ ಕೇಳಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ಮದುವೆ ನಿಂತುಹೋಯಿತು. ā kuṭumba varadakṣiṇe kēḷiddarinda maduve nintuhōyitu. The wedding was called off because that family asked for a dowry.
The money or gifts demanded from the bride's family; the practice is against the law.

The word, taken apart.
ವರದಕ್ಷಿಣೆ (varadakṣiṇe) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of vara, "a suitor, a bridegroom" (CDIAL 11309), and dakṣiṇā, "a fee, a gift" (CDIAL 6120).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಹರಸು (harasu) — to bless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a wedding reception, then the Kannada for a dowry.

You'll want to know: ಹರಸು.
ಹರಸು — harasu — "to bless".
ಹಿರಿಯರು ಹೊಸ ಜೋಡಿಯನ್ನು ಹರಸಿದರು. hiriyaru hosa jōḍiyannu harasidaru. The elders blessed the newly married couple.
Past (I …): ಹರಸಿದೆ, haraside.
The person blessed takes -ಅನ್ನು. A blessing or a vow is a ಹರಕೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಹರಸು (harasu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3951 lists Kannada parasu, "to utter a benediction, to bless"; old p- became h-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ದಿಬ್ಬಣ (dibbaṇa) — a wedding party.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a dowry, then the Kannada for to bless.

You'll want to know: ದಿಬ್ಬಣ.
ದಿಬ್ಬಣ — dibbaṇa — "a wedding party".
ಗಂಡಿನ ಕಡೆಯ ದಿಬ್ಬಣ ಸಂಜೆ ಊರಿಗೆ ಬಂತು. gaṇḍina kaḍeya dibbaṇa sañje ūrige bantu. The groom's wedding party reached the village in the evening.
The procession of the groom's family and friends to the wedding.

The word, taken apart.
ದಿಬ್ಬಣ (dibbaṇa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3230 records Kannada dibbaṇa, "a nuptial procession, the friends and relatives of the bridegroom".
