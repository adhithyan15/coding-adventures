Russian, chapter 148: Healthy, Weak, Strong, Pale, Tired.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
здоровый (zdoróvyy) — healthy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a runny nose, then the Russian for a pill.

You'll want to know: здоровый.
здоровый (zdoróvyy) — "healthy".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: zdoróvyy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: zdoróvyy, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say zdoróvyy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for a runny nose, then the Russian for a pill, then say zdoróvyy again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does здоровый (zdoróvyy) mean? ("Healthy".) Say it once more.
