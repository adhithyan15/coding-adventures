## You'll want to know: آلودگی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C388-ALUDEGI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آلودگی** (*âludegi*) — "pollution".

> آلودگی هوا در شهرهای بزرگ زیاد است.
>
> *âludegi-ye havâ dar shahrhâ-ye bozorg ziyâd ast.*
>
> Air pollution is high in big cities.

From *âlude* "dirty, polluted", the participle of *âludan* "to stain", + *-gi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آلودگی** is native Persian, built on *âlude* "stained, polluted", the participle of *âludan* "to stain", with the noun suffix *-gi*.
