## You'll want to know: 市区
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C448-MARKET448-03]; assesses=[] -->

**市区** — *shìqū* — "the built-up part of a city; downtown".

> *wǒmen zhù zài shìqū de yì jiā jiǔdiàn* — We're staying at a hotel in town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**市区** (*shìqū*) is built from 市 'a market town' and 区 'an area', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyiX* and *khju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
