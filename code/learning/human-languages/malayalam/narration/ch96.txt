Malayalam, chapter 96: Easy and Difficult.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
എളുപ്പം (eḷuppaṁ) — easy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say this. Then say the little word that finishes a sentence by saying something is so.

You'll want to know.
എളുപ്പം — eḷuppaṁ — easy.
ഇത് എളുപ്പമാണ്. — itŭ eḷuppamāṇŭ — "This is easy."

The word, taken apart.
The word ends in ം, and you have been told once already that a word ending that way says in advance how it will behave. Here is another thing it says.
Put ആണ് after it and the two do not stay apart. The ം turns into മ and the pair runs together:
എളുപ്പം (eḷuppaṁ) + ആണ് becomes എളുപ്പമാണ്.
You have been doing this since you first answered that you were well. Look at what you already say:
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
സുഖമാണ്, (I am) well.
ഇഷ്ടമാണ്, (I) like it.
മോശമാണ്, (it) is bad.
Every one of those is a word ending in ം with ആണ് welded on. Nobody pointed it out at the time because there was nothing you needed to do about it. Now there is: you can do it yourself, to a word you met a minute ago.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: eḷuppaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: eḷuppamāṇŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: itŭ eḷuppamāṇŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — look: at സുഖമാണ് and എളുപ്പമാണ്, and find the part that is the same]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What is easy? (എളുപ്പം (eḷuppaṁ).) What happens to its ം when ആണ് arrives? (It becomes മ and the two run together.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
പ്രയാസം (prayāsaṁ) — difficult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say this is easy. All of it, in one breath.

You'll want to know.
പ്രയാസം — prayāsaṁ — difficult.
ഇത് പ്രയാസമാണ്. — itŭ prayāsamāṇŭ — "This is difficult."
It ends in ം as well, so it behaves the way the last one did, and you did not have to be told a second time.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
ഇത് എളുപ്പമാണ്, this is easy.
ഇത് പ്രയാസമാണ്, this is difficult.
One word apart, and the sentence is the opposite. Everything around it holds still.

What you've built.
This is a pair you can say about anything you can point at — a word, a letter, a road, a lesson. It is also the first thing most learners want to say about the language itself, and you can now say it in the language, which is a better place to complain from.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: prayāsaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: itŭ prayāsamāṇŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the two sentences one after the other, and hear where they part]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Easy and difficult? (എളുപ്പം (eḷuppaṁ), പ്രയാസം (prayāsaṁ).) Did the second one need a new rule? (No — it ends the same way, so it behaves the same way.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
(easy, difficult) (eḷuppavuṁ prayāsavuṁ enna ōrmma) — from cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Close the lessons before this one. Say this is easy, then say the opposite.

Grammar Lens: the welding was already yours.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
ഇത് എളുപ്പമാണ്, this is easy.
ഇത് പ്രയാസമാണ്, this is difficult.
Two new words, and no new grammar at all. Both end in ം, and a word ending in ം welds onto ആണ് by turning that ം into മ.
You were doing it long before this chapter:
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
സുഖമാണ്, well.
ഇഷ്ടമാണ്, liked.
മോശമാണ്, bad.
None of those was ever presented as a rule, because until now you had no word of your own to put through it. The pattern sat in your mouth for most of the book, doing its work silently.

What you've built.
A judgement you can make about anything you can point at.
It is worth noticing what the chapter did not need. No new arrangement, no new ending, no exception. Two words, dropped into a frame that was already running. That is what most of this stage looks like now: the language was built first and the vocabulary arrives into it.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: eḷuppaṁ becomes eḷuppamāṇŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: prayāsaṁ becomes prayāsamāṇŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: itŭ eḷuppamāṇŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: itŭ prayāsamāṇŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: one older word that welds the same way]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Easy and difficult? (എളുപ്പം (eḷuppaṁ), പ്രയാസം (prayāsaṁ).) What does the ം become when ആണ് follows? (മ.) Was that rule new in this chapter? (No — you had been using it since near the beginning.)
