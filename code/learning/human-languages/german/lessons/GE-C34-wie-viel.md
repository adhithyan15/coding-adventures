# wie viel, wie viele — how much and how many

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WIE-02, GE-LEX-ZWANZIG-01, GE-GRAMMAR-ZIFFER-PLUS-ZEHN-01, GE-LEX-ZWOELF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You can count to twenty and you cannot ask anybody for a number. Say
**zwölf** and **zwanzig**, then take the question that was missing.

## You'll want to know: wie viel · wie viele
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-WIE-VIEL-01, GE-LEX-WIE-VIELE-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WIE-02, GE-LEX-MILCH-02, GE-LEX-UHR-01] -->

> **wie viel** — "how much", for something you would not count.
> **wie viele** — "how many", for things you would.

> **Wie viel Milch?** — how much milk. · **Wie viele Hunde?** — how many dogs.

English draws the identical line with two words too, *much* against *many*, so
nothing is new except the German for it. The **-e** on *viele* is the plural
ending you have carried since *die*.

One phrase to bank whole: **Wie viel Uhr ist es?**, literally *how much clock is
it*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WIE-VIEL-01, GE-LEX-WIE-VIELE-01] -->

**viel** is Proto-Indo-European ***\*pelh₁-***, "to fill". Grimm's law turned
that **p** into **f**, as it did in *Fuß* against Latin *pes*, and the word
reached English as **full**. The same root sits in Greek *poly-* and Latin
*plenus*. German asks *how full* where English asks *how much*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WIE-VIEL-01, GE-LEX-WIE-VIELE-01, GE-LEX-MILCH-02, GE-SOUND-MILCH-03, GE-LEX-KAFFEE-02, GE-LEX-WASSER-01, GE-LEX-HUND-02, GE-LEX-KATZE-04, GE-LEX-UHR-01, GE-GRAMMAR-ES-IST-UHR-01, GE-LEX-FUSS-02, GE-ETYMON-FUSS-04, GE-LEX-WELCHER-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Decide countable or not before you speak.

- [YOU SAY: not countable — "Wie viel Milch, Wasser, Kaffee?"]
- [YOU SAY: countable — "Wie viele Hunde? Wie viele Katzen?"]
- [YOU SAY: the clock phrase, then the answer — "Wie viel Uhr ist es?" "Es ist
  ein Uhr."]
- [YOU SAY: the *p* that became *f*, twice — "Fuß und viel"]
- [YOU SAY: the two questions side by side — "Welcher Kaffee?" and "Wie viel
  Kaffee?"]

[REPEAT x2] "Wie viel Milch? Wie viele Hunde?"

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WIE-VIEL-01, GE-LEX-WIE-VIELE-01, GE-LEX-UHR-01, GE-ETYMON-FUSS-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which one goes with milk, and why? (***Wie viel*** — milk is not
counted.) Which goes with dogs? (***Wie viele***.) What English word is *viel*?
(***Full***, by the same *p → f* step that gave *Fuß*.) Ask the time. (**Wie viel
Uhr ist es?**)
