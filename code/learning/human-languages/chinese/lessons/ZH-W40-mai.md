## Script — a lid, two dots, and a cut-down 大
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-MAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 买

**Two pieces, top before bottom.**

**The top piece, three strokes.** A horizontal that hooks down at its right end, like a lid. Then two dots under it, one above the other.

**The bottom piece, three strokes.** A long horizontal, a left-falling stroke through it, and a dot on the right. It is 大 with its last stroke cut down to a dot.

3 + 3 = six.
