6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
fritar — to fry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the onion and olive oil base, then the Portuguese for low heat.

You'll want to know: fritar.
fritar — "to fry".
Ela fritou dois ovos para o pequeno-almoço. She fried two eggs for breakfast.
Batatas fritas — chips, crisps.

The word, taken apart.
fritar is built on frito "fried" (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin frīctus, from frīgere "to roast, to fry".

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 6.
grelhar — to grill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to cook in water, then the Portuguese for to roast.

You'll want to know: grelhar.
grelhar — "to grill".
No verão, grelhamos peixe no quintal. In summer we grill fish in the back yard.
Peixe grelhado — grilled fish.

The word, taken apart.
grelhar is built on grelha "a grill", borrowed from Old French greille (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin crāticula "a small gridiron", from crātis "a wickerwork" (Lewis & Short).
