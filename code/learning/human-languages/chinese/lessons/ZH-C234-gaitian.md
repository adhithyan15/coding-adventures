## You'll want to know: gǎitiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C234-RAIN234-03]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎitiān* — "some other day; another time".

> *jīntiān xiàyǔ wǒmen gǎitiān qù* — It's raining today; let's go another day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎitiān* is built from *gǎi* 'to change' and 天 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kojX* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to alter'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
