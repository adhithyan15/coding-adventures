Telugu, chapter 400: Elections.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎన్నిక (ennika) — an election.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for injustice, then the Telugu for to honour.

You'll want to know: ఎన్నిక.
ఎన్నిక — ennika — "an election".
వచ్చే నెలలో మా రాష్ట్రంలో ఎన్నికలు జరుగుతాయి. vaccē nelalō mā rāṣṭraṁlō ennikalu jarugutāyi. Elections will be held in our state next month.
Plural: ఎన్నికలు, ennikalu.
Mostly plural: ఎన్నికలు, the elections. It is built on ఎన్ను, to count, to reckon.

The word, taken apart.
ఎన్నిక (ennika) is native Dravidian: DEDR 793 lists Telugu ennika, "counting, number, esteem", beside ennu, "to count, to reckon, to think"; Telugu now uses it for an election, a choosing by count.

Lesson 2 of 5.
అభ్యర్థి (abhyarthi) — a candidate; an applicant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to honour, then the Telugu for an election.

You'll want to know: అభ్యర్థి.
అభ్యర్థి — abhyarthi — "a candidate; an applicant".
ఆ ఎన్నికలో ఐదుగురు అభ్యర్థులు ఉన్నారు. ā ennikalō aiduguru abhyarthulu unnāru. There are five candidates in that election.
Plural: అభ్యర్థులు, abhyarthulu.
Also someone who applies for a job or a course.

The word, taken apart.
అభ్యర్థి (abhyarthi) is borrowed from Sanskrit abhyarthin, "asking, requesting" (Monier-Williams): a candidate is one who asks for a post.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఎన్నుకో (ennukō) — to elect; to vote in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an election, then the Telugu for a candidate.

You'll want to know: ఎన్నుకో.
ఎన్నుకో — ennukō — "to elect; to vote in".
విద్యార్థులు ఆమెను తరగతి నాయకురాలిగా ఎన్నుకున్నారు. vidyārthulu āmenu taragati nāyakurāligā ennukunnāru. The students elected her class leader.
Past (I …): ఎన్నుకున్నాను, ennukunnānu.
Close to ఎంచుకో, to choose, which you know; ఎన్నుకో (ennukō) is choosing by a vote.

The word, taken apart.
ఎన్నుకో (ennukō) is native Dravidian: ennu, "to count, to reckon, to judge" (DEDR 793), with -కో, from konu, "to take" (DEDR 2151): to pick out for oneself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ప్రచారం (pracāraṁ) — a campaign; publicity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a candidate, then the Telugu for to elect.

You'll want to know: ప్రచారం.
ప్రచారం — pracāraṁ — "a campaign; publicity".
ఎన్నికల ప్రచారం నిన్నటితో ముగిసింది. ennikala pracāraṁ ninnaṭitō mugisindi. The election campaign ended yesterday.
ప్రచారం (pracāraṁ) చేయు, to campaign, to spread the word.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రచారం (pracāraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit pracāra, "roaming; coming forth, manifestation" (Monier-Williams); in Telugu it is the spreading of a word or a cause.
