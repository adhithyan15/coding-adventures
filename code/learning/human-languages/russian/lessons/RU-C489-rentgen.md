## You'll want to know: рентген
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C489-CHECKUP-04]; assesses=[] -->

**рентген** (*rentgén*) — "an X-ray".

> Мне сделали рентген руки.
>
> *Mne sdélali rentgén rukí.*
>
> They X-rayed my arm.

Masculine. *Сделать рентген* — to have an X-ray.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рентген* was borrowed from German *Röntgen* 'X-ray' (EtymDB), a word made from the surname of the physicist who found the rays.
