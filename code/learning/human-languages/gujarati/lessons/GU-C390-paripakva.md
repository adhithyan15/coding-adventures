## You'll want to know: પરિપક્વ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C390-PAK390-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પરિપક્વ** — *paripakva* — "mature".

> જો તે થોડો વધારે પરિપક્વ હોત તો આવી ભૂલ ન કરત.
>
> *jo te thoḍo vadhāre paripakva hot to āvī bhūl na karat.*
>
> If he were a little more mature, he would not make such a mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરિપક્વ** (*paripakva*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *paripakva*, "completely cooked, fully ripe" (Monier-Williams).
