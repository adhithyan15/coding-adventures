## You'll want to know: आदेशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C353-SHASANA353-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आदेशः** — *ādeśaḥ* — "an official order; a command".

> मन्त्री आदेशं पालयति।
>
> *mantrī ādeśaṃ pālayati.*
>
> The minister carries out the order.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आदेशः** (*ādeśaḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √diś, "to point out"; MW gives *ā-deśa* "rule, command, order" (MW).

## What you've built
