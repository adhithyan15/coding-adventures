## You'll want to know: a seca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C462-CALOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a seca** — "the drought".

> Este ano houve uma seca no sul.
>
> *This year there was a drought in the south.*

Informal in Portugal: *Que seca!* — How boring!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seca* is built on *seco* 'dry', which is inherited from Latin *siccus* (Lewis & Short).
