## You'll want to know: verlieben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C492-VERLIEBT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**verlieben** — "to fall in love (sich verlieben)".

> Im Sommer hat sie sich in ihren Tanzlehrer verliebt.
>
> *In the summer she fell in love with her dance teacher.*

Reflexive: *sich verlieben in* + accusative. *Verliebt sein* — to be in love.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verlieben* is built on *ver-* and *lieben* "to love" (EtymDB lists both), a Germanic verb related to *lieb* "dear", from Proto-Germanic *leubaz*.
