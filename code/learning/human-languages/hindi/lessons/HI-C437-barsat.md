## You'll want to know: बरसात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C437-MANSUN437-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बरसात** — *barsāt* — "the rainy season".

> बरसात में गाँव बहुत हरा हो जाता है।
>
> *barsāt meṁ gā̃v bahut harā ho jātā hai.*
>
> In the rainy season the village turns very green.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बरसात** (*barsāt*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *varṣārātri*, "the rainy season" (CDIAL 11398), literally the rain nights; CDIAL lists Hindi *barsāt*.
