## You'll want to know: परखना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C339-RAJKUMAR339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परखना** — *parakhnā* — "to test; to put to the test".

> राजकुमार ने भेस बदलकर लोगों को परखा।
>
> *rājkumār ne bhes badalkar logoṁ ko parkhā.*
>
> The prince went in disguise and put the people to the test.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सोना परखा, *usne sonā parkhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

goes back to Sanskrit *parīkṣate*, "examines" (CDIAL 7904), the family of **परीक्षा**, an examination. You **परखना** gold, a coin or a friend.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
