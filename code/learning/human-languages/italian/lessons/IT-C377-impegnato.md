## You'll want to know: impegnato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C377-IMPEGNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**impegnato** — "busy, tied up; committed".

> Domani sono impegnato tutto il giorno.
>
> *Tomorrow I am busy all day.*

Also socially engaged: *un cantante impegnato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impegnato* is the past participle of *impegnare* "to pledge, to engage", built from *in-* and *pegno* "a pledge", inherited from Latin *pignus* "a pledge" (Lewis & Short).
