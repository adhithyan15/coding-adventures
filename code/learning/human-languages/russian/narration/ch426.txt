Russian, chapter 426: In a boat.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
катер (káter) — a motor boat, a launch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to make seasick or carsick, then the Russian for an anchor.

You'll want to know: катер.
катер (káter) — "a motor boat, a launch".
На катере мы быстро добрались до острова. Na kátere my býstro dobrális do óstrova. We quickly got to the island by motor boat.
Masculine; plural катера.

The word, taken apart.
катер (káter) was borrowed from English cutter (EtymDB), a small fast boat, built on cut.

Lesson 2 of 6.
шлюпка (shlyúpka) — a ship's boat, a lifeboat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an anchor, then the Russian for a motor boat.

You'll want to know: шлюпка.
шлюпка (shlyúpka) — "a ship's boat, a lifeboat".
На корабле есть шлюпки для всех пассажиров. Na korablé yest shlyúpki dlya vsekh passazhírov. There are lifeboats on the ship for all the passengers.
Feminine. Спасательная шлюпка (shlyúpka) — a lifeboat.

The word, taken apart.
шлюпка (shlyúpka) is a diminutive of шлюп 'a sloop' (EtymDB lists шлюп), which is usually traced to Dutch sloep, a word EtymDB links with a Middle Dutch verb for gliding.

Lesson 5 of 6.
грести (grestí) — to row; to rake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a waistcoat, then the Russian for an oar.

You'll want to know: грести.
грести (grestí) — "to row; to rake".
Отец гребёт, а мы сидим и отдыхаем. Otéts grebyót, a my sidím i otdykháyem. Dad rows, and we sit and rest.
Imperfective. Present гребу, гребёшь; past грёб, гребла.

The word, taken apart.
грести (grestí) is inherited from Proto-Slavic greti 'to dig, to scrape, to rake' (EtymDB): an oar pulls through water as a rake pulls through soil.
