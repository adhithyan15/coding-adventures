## You'll want to know: egoísta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C723-GENEROSO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**egoísta** — "selfish; the selfish person".

> No seas egoísta y comparte.
>
> *Do not be selfish, share.*

Same form for men and women.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*egoísta* is built on Latin *ego* "I" with the ending *-ista*; the word probably came in through French *égoïste*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
