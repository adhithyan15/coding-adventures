Lesson 5 of 5.
gli stivali — boots.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for gloves, then the Italian for a scarf.

You'll want to know: gli stivali.
gli stivali — "boots".

The word, taken apart.
stivali is the plural of stivale from Old French estival — borrowed; behind it is Medieval Latin aestivale, a summer boot, from aestivus, "of summer". (EtymDB 2.1).
