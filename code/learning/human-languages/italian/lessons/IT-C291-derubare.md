## You'll want to know: derubare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C291-SCIPPO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**derubare** — "to rob someone, to steal from".

> In vacanza ci hanno derubato.
>
> *We were robbed on holiday.*

*Derubare* takes the person, *rubare* the thing: *mi hanno derubato*, *mi hanno rubato il portafoglio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*derubare* is built on *rubare* "to steal"; EtymDB links it with French *dérober* "to steal", from Old French *desrober*. *Rubare* goes back, through Medieval Latin, to a Frankish word for plunder (EtymDB).
