## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C304-WELL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**enfermo** — "ill."

Latin **infirmus** is *in-* ("not") plus *firmus* ("firm, steady"). Illness named
as **the loss of steadiness** — not pain, not fever, just the going of the thing
that held you up.

English borrowed it twice and kept the shape both times: *infirm*, and the
*infirmary* where the not-firm are put.

*Enfermo* is the strong one in this chapter. You already have *la fiebre* and *el
dolor*; this is the word for the state they belong to. And *la enfermera* — the
nurse — is built straight off it: the one who attends the not-firm.
