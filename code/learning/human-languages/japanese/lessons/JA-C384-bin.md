## You'll want to know: びん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C384-HIKOUJOU384-02]; assesses=[] -->

**びん** — *bin* — "a flight; a scheduled service".

> **つぎの びんは なんじに でますか。** — *tsugi no bin wa nanji ni demasu ka* — "What time does the next flight leave?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**びん** (*bin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese: *bin*, "convenience" (Middle Chinese *bjienH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
