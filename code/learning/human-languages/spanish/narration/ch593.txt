Spanish, chapter 593: Mis tareas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la tarea — the task; homework.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the head office, then the Spanish for the branch.

You'll want to know: la tarea.
la tarea — "the task; homework".
Mi tarea es hablar con los clientes. My task is to talk to the customers.
In Latin America la tarea is also school homework; Spain says los deberes.

The word, taken apart.
tarea was borrowed from Andalusian Arabic ṭaríḥa, a set amount of work (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
desempeñar — to carry out, to perform; to hold (a post).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the branch, then the Spanish for the task.

You'll want to know: desempeñar.
desempeñar — "to carry out, to perform; to hold (a post)".
Desempeña un papel importante en la empresa. She plays an important role in the company.
Desempeñar un cargo — to hold a post.

The word, taken apart.
desempeñar is built on empeñar 'to pawn' with des-. Empeñar goes with empeño 'a pledge', which EtymDB traces to Latin in pignus 'in pledge'. To redeem a pledge became to carry out a duty.

Lesson 3 of 5.
el rendimiento — the performance, the output; the yield.

You'll want to know: el rendimiento.
el rendimiento — "the performance, the output; the yield".
Su rendimiento en el trabajo es excelente. Her performance at work is excellent.
El rendimiento escolar — performance at school.

The word, taken apart.
rendimiento is built on rendir with -miento (EtymDB); rendir is inherited from spoken Latin rendere, Latin reddere 'to give back'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
dedicarse — to do for a living; to devote oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to carry out, then the Spanish for the performance.

You'll want to know: dedicarse.
dedicarse — "to do for a living; to devote oneself".
¿A qué te dedicas? What do you do for a living?
¿A qué te dedicas? is the usual way to ask about someone's job. Spelling: me dediqué.

The word, taken apart.
dedicarse is a learned borrowing from Latin dedicare 'to devote, to dedicate', built on dicare 'to proclaim', a relative of dicere 'to say' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
eficiente — efficient.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: eficiente.
eficiente — "efficient".
Es una trabajadora muy eficiente. She is a very efficient worker.
The same for both genders.

The word, taken apart.
eficiente is a learned borrowing from Latin efficiens 'bringing about' (EtymDB), from efficere, built from ex and facere 'to make'.
