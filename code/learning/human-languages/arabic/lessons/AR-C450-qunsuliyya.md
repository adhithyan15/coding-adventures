## You'll want to know: قنصلية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C450-BALADIYYA450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قنصلية** — *qunṣuliyya* — "a consulate".

> القنصلية مغلقة يوم الجمعة.
>
> *al-qunṣuliyya mughlaqa yawm al-jumʿa.*
>
> The consulate is closed on Friday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قنصلية** (*qunṣuliyya*) is usually traced to the European word *consul*, from Latin, with the Arabic ending *-iyya*.
