# ચાંલ્લો (cā̃llo) — bindi, forehead mark

## You'll want to know: ચાંલ્લો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C156-ADORN156-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાંલ્લો** — *cā̃llo* — "bindi, forehead mark".

> **તે કપાળે ચાંલ્લો કરે છે.** — *te kapāḷe cā̃llo kare che* — "She puts a bindi on her forehead."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ચાંલ્લો** (*cā̃llo*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
