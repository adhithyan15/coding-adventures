## You'll want to know: દાઝવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C300-AAG300-05]; assesses=[] -->

**દાઝવું** — *dājhvũ* — "to be scalded; to get a burn".

> ગરમ તેલથી મીરાનો હાથ દાઝી ગયો.
>
> *garam telthī Mīrāno hāth dājhī gayo.*
>
> Mira burned her hand on hot oil.

Past (he …): તે છોકરો દાઝી ગયો, *te chokro dājhī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાઝવું** (*dājhvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dahyátē*, "is burnt", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be burnt, be scalded" (CDIAL 6248). It is skin that **દાઝે છે**; wood and paper **બળે છે**.
