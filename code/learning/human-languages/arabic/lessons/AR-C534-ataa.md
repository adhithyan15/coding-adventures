## You'll want to know: أطاع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C534-NASIHA534-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أطاع** — *aṭāʿa* — "to obey".

> أطاع الولد أباه وذهب إلى النوم.
>
> *aṭāʿa al-walad abāhu wa-dhahaba ilā an-nawm.*
>
> The boy obeyed his father and went to bed.

Present: يطيع, *yuṭīʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أطاع** (*aṭāʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-w-ʿ*, "to comply, to be willing" (Lane). Istaṭāʿa, to be able, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
