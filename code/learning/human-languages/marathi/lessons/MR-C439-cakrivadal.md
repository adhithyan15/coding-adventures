## You'll want to know: चक्रीवादळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C439-VAVTAL439-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्रीवादळ** — *cakrīvādaḷ* — "a cyclone".

> चक्रीवादळामुळे गावात खूप पडझड झाली.
>
> *cakrīvādaḷāmuḷe gāvāt khūp paḍjhaḍ jhālī.*
>
> The cyclone brought down a lot in the village.

Neuter.

चक्री, whirling, and वादळ, a storm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्रीवादळ** (*cakrīvādaḷ*) is built from *cakrī*, "whirling", on Sanskrit *cakra*, "a wheel" (CDIAL 4538), and **वादळ**, "a storm".
