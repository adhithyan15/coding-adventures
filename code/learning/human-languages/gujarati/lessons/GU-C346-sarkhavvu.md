## You'll want to know: સરખાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C346-DAKHLO346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સરખાવવું** — *sarkhāvvũ* — "to compare".

> બે દુકાનોના ભાવ સરખાવીને પછી ખરીદો.
>
> *be dukānonā bhāv sarkhāvīne pachī kharīdo.*
>
> Compare the prices at two shops and then buy.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બંને ભાવ સરખાવ્યા, *teṇe banne bhāv sarkhāvyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સરખાવવું** (*sarkhāvvũ*) is built on *sarkhũ*, "alike", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *sadṛkṣa*, "like" (CDIAL 13119), with the causative ending *-āvvũ*: to set things side by side.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
