## Script — a framed top over 二 and three strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-PIAO-TICKET-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 票

**Two pieces, top before bottom.**

**The top piece, six strokes.** A bar across the top. Then a frame under it: a short vertical on the left, a stroke along the top that turns down the right side, two short verticals down through the top of the frame, and a bar that closes it.

**The bottom piece, five strokes.** 二: a short bar, then a longer one. A vertical down the middle that hooks to the left at its foot. A dot falling to the left, then a dot falling to the right.

6 + 5 = eleven.
