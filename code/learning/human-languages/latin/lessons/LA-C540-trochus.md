## You'll want to know: trochus, trochī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C540-PUPA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**trochus, trochī** — "a hoop".

> Puer trochum per viam agit.
>
> *The boy drives his hoop along the street.*

Masculine, second declension. A metal hoop with little rings, driven with a stick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**trochus** was borrowed from Greek *trochos* "wheel, hoop" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
