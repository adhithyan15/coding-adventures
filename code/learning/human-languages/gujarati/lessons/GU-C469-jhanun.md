## You'll want to know: ઝનૂન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C469-TARANGI469-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝનૂન** — *jhanūn* — "obsession; frenzy".

> મીરાને ક્રિકેટનું ઝનૂન છે.
>
> *Mīrāne krikeṭnũ jhanūn che.*
>
> Mira is obsessed with cricket.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝનૂન** (*jhanūn*) is usually traced to Arabic *junūn*, "madness", through Persian.
