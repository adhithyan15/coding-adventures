## You'll want to know: گرم کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C538-APPLIANCES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**گرم کردن** (*garm kardan*) — "to heat, to warm up".

> او شیر بچه را گرم کرد.
>
> *u shir-e bachche râ garm kard.*
>
> She warmed the baby's milk.

*garm* 'warm' + *kardan*. Players also *garm mikonand* 'warm up' before a match.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garm kardan* is built on two inherited words: *garm*, 'warm', which EtymDB traces to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning 'to heat, to warm' (EtymDB), and *kardan*, 'to do'.
