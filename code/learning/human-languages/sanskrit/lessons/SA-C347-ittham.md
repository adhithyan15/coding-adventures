## You'll want to know: इत्थम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C347-UDAHARANA347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**इत्थम्** — *ittham* — "in this way; thus".

> भवान् इत्थं करोतु।
>
> *bhavān itthaṃ karotu.*
>
> Please do it this way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इत्थम्** (*ittham*) is built on the pronoun stem *id*, "this"; MW gives *ittham* "thus, in this manner" (MW).
