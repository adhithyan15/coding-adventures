## You'll want to know: jiǎnyā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C485-MIND485-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎnyā* — "to relieve stress".

> *tīng yīnyuè kěyǐ jiǎnyā* — Listening to music helps relieve stress.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎnyā* is built from *jiǎn* 'to lessen' and *yā* 'to press', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keamX* and *'aep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first under two readings and glosses *keamX* 'to reduce'; it glosses the second 'to press down'.
