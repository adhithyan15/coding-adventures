# വിചാരണ (vicāraṇa) — a trial

## You'll want to know: വിചാരണ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C368-KODATHI368-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വിചാരണ** — *vicāraṇa* — "a trial".

> കേസിന്റെ വിചാരണ അടുത്ത മാസം തുടങ്ങും.
>
> *kēsinṟe vicāraṇa aṭutta māsaṁ tuṭaṅṅuṁ.*
>
> The trial of the case will begin next month.

A court holds it: വിചാരണ നടത്തുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിചാരണ** (*vicāraṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vicāraṇa*, "consideration, reflexion, discussion" (Monier-Williams): a trial is the court's weighing of a case.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
