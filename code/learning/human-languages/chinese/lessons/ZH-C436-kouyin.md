## You'll want to know: 口音
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C436-SOUTH436-05]; assesses=[] -->

**口音** — *kǒuyīn* — "accent".

> **他说话有南方口音** *tā shuōhuà yǒu nánfāng kǒuyīn* — He speaks with a southern accent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**口音** (*kǒuyīn*) is built from 口 'the mouth' and 音 'a sound', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khuwX* and *'im* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a sound'.
