## You'll want to know: cético
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C340-DUVIDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cético** — "sceptical".

> Estou cético: essa promessa parece boa demais.
>
> *I am sceptical: that promise seems too good.*

Many speakers in Portugal pronounce the *p* and write *céptico*; the agreement accepts both spellings. Feminine *cética*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cético* is a learned word that goes back to Greek *skeptikós* "inquiring, reflective", built on *sképtesthai* "to consider, to examine"; Latin had *Sceptici* for the philosophers of that school (Lewis & Short).
