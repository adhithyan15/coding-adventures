# HL75 — Punjabi pre-A1 full mock 2

## Status

Proposed for issue #17719 under the Punjabi bridge #13384 and the
Indian-language C2 programme #13295.

## Problem

Punjabi now has one structurally executable project-defined pre-A1 mock, but
the assessment contract requires two independent timed forms. One form cannot
show that the task envelope has a usable alternate administration.

## Required artifact set

Make `pre-a1-mock-2` the second structurally complete Punjabi mock by adding a
distinct learner paper, confidential examiner and recording script,
form-specific answer key, and version-1 structure manifest. Make the existing
level-owned rubric request-neutral so it scores either water or bread without
changing the published part weights. Cover all fourteen reading items,
fourteen listening items, eight writing items, and six speaking prompts in the
published 10/10/12/8-minute envelope.

Form 2 must use a different objective-item sequence, different confidential
writing targets, different personal details, and a different short-request
cue from form 1. All tested Punjabi remains inside the taught corpus. The
learner paper must contain no romanization, transcript, confidential writing
target, or answer model. Four separately collected skill packets preserve the
boundary between earlier receptive material and later productive work.

## Contract and scoring

Add `paper` and `manifest` references only to `pre-a1-mock-2`. The shared
rubric continues to score both forms as four independent 100-point papers with
the exact 40/30/30, 20/40/40, and 30/40/30 part weights. A learner meets this
form's scoring threshold only by reaching 60/100 on every paper; this is not a
claim that the learner passes a rung.

## Evidence boundary

This tranche proves artifact presence, form distinction, and structural
fidelity only. It does not prove pedagogical quality, a clean administration,
standard setting, qualified Punjabi review, book-only human validation,
learner attainment, exam readiness, accreditation, or equivalence to an
external syllabus.

## Ownership and verification

A new focused Punjabi corpus owner pins only form 2's files, manifest envelope,
item counts, taught-source boundary, all-listening-stimuli confidentiality,
confidential writing targets, duration bands, request-neutral shared scoring,
form distinction, and evidence boundary. Generic manifest, artifact, and
completion-plan gates own moving aggregate counts. Do not rewrite the form-1
owner. Limit the shared-rubric edit to making the short-request criterion
pictured-item-neutral; do not change its part weights or evidence boundary.
