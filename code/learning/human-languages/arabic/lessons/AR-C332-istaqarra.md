## You'll want to know: استقر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C332-HIJRA332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**استقر** — *istaqarra* — "to settle down".

> استقرت الأسرة في مدينة على البحر.
>
> *istaqarrat al-usra fī madīna ʿalā al-baḥr.*
>
> The family settled in a city by the sea.

Present: يستقر, *yastaqirr*.

Of a situation: استقر الوضع, things calmed down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استقر** (*istaqarra*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-r* (Lane), "to stay in a place, to be at rest". It is Form X, "to come to rest".
