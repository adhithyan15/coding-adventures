# Tea or milk: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-HABITUAL-215, PA-GRAMMAR-ASK-AND-ANSWER-215] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask *do you drink tea?* and answer it.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-HABITUAL-215, PA-GRAMMAR-ASK-AND-ANSWER-215, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *do you go home every day?*] Say **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਘਰ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹੋ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *no, I don't go home every day*] Answer as yourself.
- [YOU READ: **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ?**] Give its meaning: does she eat roti?
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ?**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-HABITUAL-215, PA-GRAMMAR-ASK-AND-ANSWER-215, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Hold the whole four-line exchange once more, both parts, without
the model.
