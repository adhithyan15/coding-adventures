Gujarati, chapter 341: Making a Point.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
દલીલ (dalīl) — a line of argument; a reason put forward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an excuse, then the Gujarati for to accomplish.

You'll want to know: દલીલ.
દલીલ — dalīl — "a line of argument; a reason put forward".
મીરાની દલીલ મજબૂત હતી. Mīrānī dalīl majbūt hatī. Mira's argument was a strong one.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દલીલ (dalīl) is usually traced to Arabic dalīl, "a guide; a proof", through Persian. Gujarati uses it for a reason put forward in an argument.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ચર્ચા (carcā) — a discussion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to accomplish, then the Gujarati for a line of argument.

You'll want to know: ચર્ચા.
ચર્ચા — carcā — "a discussion".
બેઠકમાં નવા નિયમ પર લાંબી ચર્ચા થઈ. beṭhakmā̃ navā niyam par lā̃bī carcā thaī. There was a long discussion about the new rule at the meeting.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચર્ચા (carcā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit carcā, "repetition, going over a thing again" (Monier-Williams); "a discussion" is the sense Gujarati uses.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a discussion, then the Gujarati for a point at issue.

You'll want to know: સંદર્ભ.
સંદર્ભ — sandarbh — "a context".
આ શબ્દનો અર્થ સંદર્ભ પર આધાર રાખે છે. ā śabdano arth sandarbh par ādhār rākhe che. The meaning of this word depends on the context.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સંદર્ભ (sandarbh) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit saṃdarbha, "a stringing together" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati uses it for the setting that gives words their sense.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ઉઠાવવું.
ઉઠાવવું — uṭhāvvũ — "to raise a point; to raise a question".
મીરાએ બેઠકમાં પગારનો મુદ્દો ઉઠાવ્યો. Mīrāe beṭhakmā̃ pagārno muddo uṭhāvyo. At the meeting Mira raised the question of pay.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે એક પ્રશ્ન ઉઠાવ્યો, teṇe ek praśna uṭhāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઉઠાવવું (uṭhāvvũ) is inherited from a form CDIAL reconstructs as ut-sthāpayati, "raises"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 1903).
