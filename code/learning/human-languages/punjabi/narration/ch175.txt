Warm-up.
Use only these old fictional cards, never your own details. ਕ chooses A: age ੧੫, phone ੦੧੨ ੨੫੧, date ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫. ਖ chooses B: age ੨੫, phone ੦੨੫ ੧੨੫, date ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫. Keep the bank visible. Check the card first, then the three labels and values one line at a time. This gentle return is not a scored A1 mock.

Guided Practice.
The card is ਖ. In this printed form, the age and date already match B; the phone's last two digits do not. Point to the single different line. Copy only its correction, ਫ਼ੋਨ: ੦੨੫ ੧੨੫, beside the form. Do not erase or rewrite the two correct lines.
ਉਮਰ: ੨੫ ਫ਼ੋਨ: ੦੨੫ ੧੫੨ ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫.

Wrap-up Recall.
Cover the bank and the guided correction. The card is now ਕ. Compare the printed three-field form below with the values you remember. Find the one different line and write its repaired version on a fresh line without looking back. Leave the two matching lines alone.
ਉਮਰ: ੧੫ ਫ਼ੋਨ: ੦੧੨ ੨੫੧ ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ੧੫/੦੧੨੦੨੫.
Now uncover the bank and check your repair: ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫. The only change was the missing slash; every digit, age, and phone stays the same. This is a brief independent correction within a supported practice session, not an independent timed task, paper scoring, or human A1 validation.
