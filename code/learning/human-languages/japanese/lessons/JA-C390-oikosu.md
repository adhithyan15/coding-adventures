## You'll want to know: おいこす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C390-KOUSOKU390-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おいこす** — *oikosu* — "to overtake; to pass".

> **こうそくどうろで くるまを おいこしました。** — *kōsokudōro de kuruma o oikoshimashita* — "I overtook a car on the expressway."

Polite **おいこします**, *oikoshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **くるまが じてんしゃを おいこしました。**, *kuruma ga jitensha o oikoshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おいこす** (*oikosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *oi*, "chasing" (from *ou*) and *kosu*, "to cross over"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
