## You'll want to know: la basura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C485-LIMPIEZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la basura** — "the rubbish, the trash".

> ¿Quién saca la basura?
>
> *Who takes out the rubbish?*

*Sacar la basura* — to take out the rubbish.
