Marathi, chapter 268: Tool, Tabla, Flute, Sitar, Ball.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
अवजार (avjār) — a tool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for mud, then the Marathi for dust.

You'll want to know: अवजार.
अवजार — avjār — "a tool".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: avjār]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: avjār, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say avjār]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for mud, then the Marathi for dust, then say avjār again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does अवजार (avjār) mean? ("A tool".) Say it once more.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Generous? (उदार, udār.) Talkative? (बोलका / बोलकी, bolkā / bolkī.) Worried? (चिंताग्रस्त, cintāgrasta.) Unmarried? (अविवाहित, avivāhit.) Married? (विवाहित, vivāhit.) Ugly? (कुरूप, kurūp.) Tired? (थकलेला, thaklelā.) Hungry? (भुकेला, bhukelā.) Thirsty? (तहानलेला, tahānlelā.) Short-tempered? (रागीट, rāgīṭ.) Temperature? (तापमान, tāpmān.) A fly? (माशी, māśī.) Society? (समाज, samāj.) The government? (सरकार, sarkār.) A law? (कायदा, kāydā.) A singer? (गायक / गायिका, gāyak / gāyikā.) A programme? (कार्यक्रम, kāryakram.) An actor? (अभिनेता, abhinetā.) An actress? (अभिनेत्री, abhinetrī.) A poem? (कविता, kavitā.)
