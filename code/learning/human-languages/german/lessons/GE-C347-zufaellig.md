## You'll want to know: zufällig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C347-ZUFALL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**zufällig** — "by chance, accidental".

> Ich habe meine alte Lehrerin zufällig im Supermarkt getroffen.
>
> *I met my old teacher by chance in the supermarket.*

An adverb and an adjective: *ein zufälliges Treffen* — a chance meeting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zufällig* is built with *-ig* on *Zufall* "chance", from *zu* and *Fall* "fall", Old High German *val* (EtymDB).
