## You'll want to know: لہر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C293-SAILAB293-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لہر** — *lahar* — "a wave".

> ایک اونچی لہر آئی۔
>
> *ek ū̃chī lahar āī.*
>
> A high wave came.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لہر** (*lahar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *laharī*, "a wave" (CDIAL 10999). It is feminine: **اونچی لہر**.
