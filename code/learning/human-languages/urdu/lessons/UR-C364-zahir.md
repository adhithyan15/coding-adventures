## You'll want to know: ظاہر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C364-ZAHIR364-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ظاہر** — *zāhir* — "evident; plain".

> اگر وہ نہیں آیا تو ظاہر ہے وہ ناراض ہے۔
>
> *agar voh nahīṅ āyā to zāhir hai voh nārāz hai.*
>
> If he has not come, he is clearly cross.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ظاہر** (*zāhir*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ẓāhir*, "outward, apparent".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
