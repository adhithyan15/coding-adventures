## You'll want to know: comemorar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C318-FESTEJAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**comemorar** — "to commemorate, to mark (a date)".

> A cidade comemora o fim da guerra todos os anos.
>
> *The city marks the end of the war every year.*

In Brazil it is also the everyday word for ‘to celebrate’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comemorar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commemorāre* "to call to mind" (EtymDB), built on *memor* "mindful".
