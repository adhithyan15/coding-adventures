5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le déjeuner — lunch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for honey, then the French for oil.

You'll want to know: le déjeuner.
le déjeuner — "lunch".

The word, taken apart.
déjeuner from Vulgar Latin disieiunare, "to break the fast" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1 records the Latin verb.) The French word for "dinner" comes from the same verb by another path.

Lesson 3 of 5.
le petit-déjeuner — breakfast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for lunch, then the French for dinner.

You'll want to know: le petit-déjeuner.
le petit-déjeuner — "breakfast". Literally "little lunch".

The word, taken apart.
petit-déjeuner — built from the French word for "little" and déjeuner: the small first breaking of the fast, as English breakfast is.
