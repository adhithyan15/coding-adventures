## You'll want to know: хакер

**хакер** (*kháker*) — "a hacker".

> Хакер украл данные клиентов.
>
> *Kháker ukrál dánnyye kliyéntov.*
>
> A hacker stole the customers’ data.

Masculine. From English *hacker*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**хакер** was borrowed from English *hacker* (EtymDB), keeping the English stress on the first syllable.
