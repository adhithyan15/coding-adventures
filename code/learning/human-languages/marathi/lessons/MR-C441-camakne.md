## You'll want to know: चमकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C441-KSHITIJ441-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चमकणे** — *camakṇe* — "to shine; to flash".

> रात्री आकाशात तारे चमकतात.
>
> *rātrī ākāśāt tāre camaktāt.*
>
> At night the stars shine in the sky.

Past (he …): तो चमकला, *to camaklā*.

A short bright shine: वीज चमकली, lightning flashed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चमकणे** (*camakṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ċamakṇẽ*, "to glitter", under *\*camakka*, "sudden movement" (CDIAL 4676).
