## You'll want to know: sextārius, sextāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C465-MODIUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sextārius, sextāriī** — "a pint (a sixth of a larger measure)".

> Caupō sextārium vīnī prō duōbus assibus vendit.
>
> *The innkeeper sells a pint of wine for two asses.*

Second declension, masculine: *sextārius, sextāriī m.* A sixth of a *congius*, a little over half a litre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sextārius* is built on *sextus* 'sixth' with the suffix *-ārius* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): a sixth of a larger measure.
