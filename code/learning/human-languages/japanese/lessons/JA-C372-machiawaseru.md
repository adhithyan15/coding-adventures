## You'll want to know: まちあわせる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C372-EKIMAE372-03]; assesses=[] -->

**まちあわせる** — *machiawaseru* — "to meet up (at an agreed place)".

> **ともだちと えきまえで まちあわせました。** — *tomodachi to ekimae de machiawasemashita* — "I met up with my friend in front of the station."

Polite **まちあわせます**, *machiawasemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちと ははが えきで まちあわせました。**, *chichi to haha ga eki de machiawasemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まちあわせる** (*machiawaseru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *machi*, "waiting" (from *matsu*) and *awaseru*, "to bring together"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
