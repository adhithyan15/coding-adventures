French, chapter 405: La dépanneuse arrive.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dépanner — to get going again (after a breakdown); to help out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the jack, then the French for to pump up.

You'll want to know: dépanner.
dépanner — "to get going again (after a breakdown); to help out".
Un voisin nous a dépannés avec ses câbles. A neighbour got us going again with his cables.
Also: tu peux me dépanner de dix euros? — can you lend me ten euros?

The word, taken apart.
dépanner is built from dé- 'un-' and panne 'breakdown' (EtymDB). Panne is usually traced to a sailing phrase, mettre en panne, for a ship held still with its sails turned against the wind.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: le dépanneur.
le dépanneur — "the breakdown mechanic".
Le dépanneur a changé la batterie au bord de la route. The breakdown mechanic changed the battery by the side of the road.
In Québec, un dépanneur is also a corner shop that stays open late.

The word, taken apart.
dépanneur is built on dépanner with the ending -eur: the person who gets you out of a panne. Dépanner is built from dé- and panne (EtymDB).

remorquer — "to tow".

Une remorque — a trailer.

The word, taken apart.
remorquer was borrowed from Italian rimorchiare 'to tow' (EtymDB), which is usually traced through Late Latin to a Greek word for towing with a rope.

Lesson 5 of 5.
le garagiste — the garage owner, the car mechanic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the breakdown mechanic, then the French for to tow.

You'll want to know: le garagiste.
le garagiste — "the garage owner, the car mechanic".
Le garagiste a trouvé le problème tout de suite. The mechanic found the problem straight away.
Le garagiste runs the garage; le mécanicien does the repairs.

The word, taken apart.
garagiste is built on garage with -iste. Garage comes from garer 'to put in a safe place', which is usually traced to a Germanic verb meaning 'to watch over'.
