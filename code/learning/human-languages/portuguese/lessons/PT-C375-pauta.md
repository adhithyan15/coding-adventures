## You'll want to know: a pauta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C375-ESCOLA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a pauta** — "the list of marks; the musical stave".

> As notas já estão na pauta.
>
> *The marks are already on the list.*

A European school word: the posted list of final marks. Also a stave in music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pauta* goes back to Latin *pacta* "agreements" (EtymDB), the plural of *pactum* "an agreement" (Lewis & Short): first a written list of terms, then any ruled list.
