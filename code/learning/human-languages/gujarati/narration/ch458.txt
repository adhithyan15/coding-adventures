Gujarati, chapter 458: Missing Someone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વિરહ (virah) — the pain of separation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for envy, then the Gujarati for dislike.

You'll want to know: વિરહ.
વિરહ — virah — "the pain of separation".
પતિ દૂર ગયો છે, તેથી પત્નીને વિરહનું દુઃખ છે. pati dūr gayo che, tethī patnīne virahnũ duḥkh che. Her husband has gone far away, so the wife suffers the pain of separation.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વિરહ (virah) is a tatsama, Sanskrit viraha, "abandonment, parting, separation, especially of lovers", as Monier-Williams records it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for solitude, then the Gujarati for deserted.

You'll want to know: તરસવું.
તરસવું — tarasvũ — "to long for; to crave".
દાદી દીકરાને જોવા તરસે છે. dādī dīkrāne jovā tarse che. Grandmother is longing to see her son.
Past (he …): તે ઘરે જવા તરસતો હતો, te ghare javā tarasto hato.

The word, taken apart.
તરસવું (tarasvũ) goes back to a reconstructed tṛṣati, "is thirsty"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "is thirsty" (CDIAL 5935), and Gujarati also uses it for longing, a thirst of the heart.
