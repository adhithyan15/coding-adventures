## You'll want to know: el ejercicio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C742-DIETA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el ejercicio** — "exercise".

> Hago ejercicio tres veces por semana.
>
> *I exercise three times a week.*

*Hacer ejercicio* — to exercise. Also a school exercise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ejercicio* comes from Latin *exercitium* "exercise" (EtymDB), from *exercēre* "to keep busy, to train".
