## You'll want to know: はぐれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C424-OTOSHIMONO424-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はぐれる** — *hagureru* — "to get separated (from one's group)".

> **まつりで かぞくと はぐれました。** — *matsuri de kazoku to haguremashita* — "I got separated from my family at the festival."

Polite **はぐれます**, *haguremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが ははと はぐれました。**, *kodomo ga haha to haguremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はぐれる** (*hagureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese (JMdict).
