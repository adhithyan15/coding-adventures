## You'll want to know: خیال کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C391-FARZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خیال کردن** (*khiyâl kardan*) — "to imagine; to think wrongly".

> خیال کردم او رفته است، اما خانه بود.
>
> *khiyâl kardam u rafte ast, ammâ khâne bud.*
>
> I thought he had gone, but he was at home.

Often a belief that turns out wrong. *Khiyâl* "imagination" + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خیال** was borrowed from Arabic *khayāl* "imagination" (EtymDB); with the inherited verb *kardan* "to do", it means to imagine, often wrongly.
