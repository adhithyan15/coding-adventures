## You'll want to know: りし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C359-SHAKKIN359-02]; assesses=[] -->

**りし** — *rishi* — "interest on money; interest on a loan".

> **おかねを かりたら、りしを はらいます。** — *okane o karitara, rishi o haraimasu* — "If you borrow money, you pay interest."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りし** (*rishi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ri*, "advantage" (Middle Chinese *lijH*) and *shi*, "child" (*tsiX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
