Urdu, chapter 456: Kind and Cruel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ظالم (zālim) — cruel; oppressive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for slothful, then the Urdu for heedless.

You'll want to know: ظالم.
ظالم — zālim — "cruel; oppressive".
پرانا بادشاہ بڑا ظالم تھا۔ purānā bādshāh baṛā zālim thā. The old king was very cruel.

The word, taken apart.
ظالم (zālim) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ẓālim, "a wrongdoer".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: zālim]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: zālim, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: purānā bādshāh baṛā zālim thā. — The old king was very cruel.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for slothful, then the Urdu for heedless, then say zālim again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ظالم (zālim) mean? ("Cruel; oppressive".) Say it once more.
