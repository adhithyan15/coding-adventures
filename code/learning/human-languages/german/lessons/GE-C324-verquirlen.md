## You'll want to know: verquirlen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C324-RUEHREN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**verquirlen** — "to whisk, to beat (eggs)".

> Er verquirlte die Eier mit etwas Milch.
>
> *He whisked the eggs with a little milk.*

Built on *der Quirl*, a kitchen whisk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verquirlen* is built on *Quirl* "a stirring stick, a whisk", usually traced to Old High German *dweran* "to turn"; EtymDB lists no parent for *Quirl*.
