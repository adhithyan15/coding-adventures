Tamil, chapter 107: See You Later.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
விடைபெறுகிறேன் (viṭaipeṟukiṟēṉ) — I take my leave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for let's go, then the Tamil for a holiday.

You'll want to know: விடைபெறுகிறேன்.
விடைபெறுகிறேன் (viṭaipeṟukiṟēṉ) — "I take my leave", literally "I receive leave".
விடை is "leave, permission". The formal goodbye.

The word, taken apart.
விடைபெறுகிறேன் (viṭaipeṟukiṟēṉ) is native: viṭai, "leave, permission to go" (DEDR 5393), and peṟu, "to get" (DEDR 4422): "I get my leave".

What you've built.
A formal way out of a room.

[your turn — say: viṭaipeṟukiṟēṉ, once more]

[your turn — say: say viṭaipeṟukiṟēṉ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for let's go, then the Tamil for a holiday, then say viṭaipeṟukiṟēṉ again]

Lesson 2 of 5.
கிளம்பலாமா? (kiḷampalāmā?) — shall we leave?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a holiday, then the Tamil for I take my leave.

You'll want to know: கிளம்பலாமா?
கிளம்பலாமா? (kiḷampalāmā?) — "shall we leave?".
கிளம்பு is "to set out"; -லாமா asks "shall we?".

The word, taken apart.
கிளம்பலாமா? (kiḷampalāmā?) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1583 lists Malayalam kiḷampuka, "to rise". The entry lists kiḷampu, "to rise, to start up": to leave is to get up and go.

What you've built.
A gentle nudge to go.

Lesson 3 of 5.
அப்புறம் பார்க்கலாம் (appuṟam pārkkalām) — see you later.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for I take my leave, then the Tamil for shall we leave.

You'll want to know: அப்புறம் பார்க்கலாம்.
அப்புறம் பார்க்கலாம் (appuṟam pārkkalām) — "see you later".
அப்புறம் is "afterwards"; பார்க்கலாம் is "we can see (each other)". To one friend: உன்னை அப்புறம் பார்க்கிறேன் — "I'll see you later", with உன்னை, "you" as the one it is done to.

The word, taken apart.
அப்புறம் பார்க்கலாம் (appuṟam pārkkalām) is native: appuṟam, "after that", from the far base a- (DEDR 1) and puṟam, "side" (DEDR 4333), and pārkkalām, "let us see", from pār (DEDR 4091).

What you've built.
A casual goodbye.

Lesson 4 of 5.
இனிய பயணம் (iṉiya payaṇam) — have a good journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for shall we leave, then the Tamil for see you later.

You'll want to know: இனிய பயணம்.
இனிய பயணம் (iṉiya payaṇam) — "have a good journey", literally "a sweet journey".
இனிய is "sweet, pleasant".

The word, taken apart.
இனிய பயணம் (iṉiya payaṇam) is native iṉiya, "sweet, pleasant" (DEDR 530), and payaṇam, "journey", from Sanskrit prayāṇa (CDIAL 8732).

What you've built.
A wish for someone setting off.

Lesson 5 of 5.

மீண்டும் வாருங்கள் (mīṇṭum vāruṅkaḷ) — "please come again".

மீண்டும் வாருங்கள் (mīṇṭum vāruṅkaḷ) is native: mīṇṭum, "again", from mīḷ, "to return" (DEDR 4883), and vā, "come" (DEDR 5270), with the polite ending.

What you've built.
Take leave, shall we go, see you, a good journey, come again.
