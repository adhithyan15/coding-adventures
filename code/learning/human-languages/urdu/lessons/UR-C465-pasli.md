## You'll want to know: پسلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C465-BONE465-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پسلی** — *paslī* — "a rib".

> گرنے سے اس کی پسلی میں درد ہے۔
>
> *girne se us kī paslī meṅ dard hai.*
>
> Her rib hurts since she fell.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پسلی** (*paslī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *párśu*, "rib" (CDIAL 7948).
