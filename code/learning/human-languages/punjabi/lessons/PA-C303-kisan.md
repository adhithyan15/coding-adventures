## You'll want to know: ਕਿਸਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C303-WATER303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿਸਾਨ** — *kisān* — "a farmer".

> ਕਿਸਾਨ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਖੇਤ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *kisān savere khet jāndā hai.*
>
> The farmer goes to the field in the morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿਸਾਨ** (*kisān*) goes back to Sanskrit *kṛṣāṇa*, "ploughing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kisāṇ*, "farmer", under it and marks it as borrowed rather than inherited, as its *-s-* shows (CDIAL 3447).
