Lesson 2 of 5.
அகராதி (akarāti) — a dictionary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for bread, then the Tamil for a pencil.

You'll want to know: அகராதி.
அகராதி — akarāti — "a dictionary".

The word, taken apart.
அகராதி (akarāti) is "from a onward": akaram, the letter a, and āti, "beginning", from Sanskrit ādi (CDIAL 1152). A dictionary runs from the first letter.

Lesson 4 of 5.
கடிதம் (kaṭitam) — a letter (to post).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a dictionary, then the Tamil for a fee.

You'll want to know: கடிதம்.
கடிதம் — kaṭitam — "a letter (to post)".

The word, taken apart.
கடிதம் (kaṭitam) is filed in DEDR's appendix of Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 23): it first meant a cloth covered with paste for writing on, then paper and a letter. The entry compares Sanskrit kaḍitra, "a skin for writing on".
