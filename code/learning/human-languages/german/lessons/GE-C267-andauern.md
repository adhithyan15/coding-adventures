## You'll want to know: andauern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C267-DAUERN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**andauern** — "to go on, to persist".

> Der Regen dauerte die ganze Nacht an.
>
> *The rain went on all night.*

Separable: *es dauert an*. *Andauernd* means 'constantly'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*andauern* is built on *dauern* "to last", from Middle High German *dūren*, which was borrowed from Latin *dūrāre* "to last" (EtymDB), with *an* "on".
