## You'll want to know: ગોદામ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C431-STOCK431-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોદામ** — *godām* — "a warehouse; a godown".

> વેપારીના ગોદામમાં ઘઉં ભરેલા છે.
>
> *vepārīnā godāmmā̃ ghaũ bharelā che.*
>
> The merchant's warehouse is full of wheat.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોદામ** (*godām*) is usually traced to Malay *gudang*, "a storehouse", which came round the coasts through Portuguese; English *godown* is the same word.
