Arabic, chapter 534: Obeying and forgiving.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أطاع (aṭāʿa) — to obey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to stand by, then the Arabic for a loved one.

You'll want to know: أطاع.
أطاع — aṭāʿa — "to obey".
أطاع الولد أباه وذهب إلى النوم. aṭāʿa al-walad abāhu wa-dhahaba ilā an-nawm. The boy obeyed his father and went to bed.
Present: يطيع, yuṭīʿ.

The word, taken apart.
أطاع (aṭāʿa) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-w-ʿ, "to comply, to be willing" (Lane). Istaṭāʿa, to be able, which you know, is from the same root.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

عصى — ʿaṣā — "to disobey".
عصت البنت أمها وخرجت في المطر. ʿaṣat al-bint ummahā wa-kharajat fī al-maṭar. The girl disobeyed her mother and went out in the rain.
Present: يعصي, yaʿṣī.

The word, taken apart.
عصى (ʿaṣā) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-ṣ-y, "to resist, to rebel" (Lane). Its last root letter y shows as alif in the past and as ī in the present.

وبخ المدرب اللاعبين بعد الخسارة. wabbakha al-mudarrib al-lāʿibīn baʿda al-khasāra. The coach scolded the players after the defeat.

The word, taken apart.
وبخ (wabbakha) is built on the native Arabic root w-b-kh, "to blame, to rebuke" (Lane). Form II wabbakha is to rebuke sharply.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to disobey, then the Arabic for to scold.

You'll want to know: احترام.
احترام — iḥtirām — "esteem, respect".
يتكلم الطلاب مع المعلم باحترام. yatakallamu aṭ-ṭullāb maʿa al-muʿallim bi-iḥtirām. The students speak to the teacher with respect.
The verb احترم, to respect, goes with it.

The word, taken apart.
احترام (iḥtirām) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-r-m, "to be forbidden, to be sacred" (Lane). Iḥtirām is the verbal noun of Form VIII: treating someone as not to be touched.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عفا (ʿafā) — to pardon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to scold, then the Arabic for esteem.

You'll want to know: عفا.
عفا — ʿafā — "to pardon".
عفا المعلم عن الطالب. ʿafā al-muʿallim ʿan aṭ-ṭālib. The teacher pardoned the student.
Present: يعفو, yaʿfū.
It takes عن.

The word, taken apart.
عفا (ʿafā) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-f-w, "to efface; to pardon; to give health" (Lane). ʿAfwan, which you know, is from the same root.
