# Review — at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-HOW-FAR-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-GIVE-WAY-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-LANDMARK-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask the way to the station and how far it is, then give it: straight, then right, opposite the temple. (*sṭeśanlā kasaṁ jāycaṁ? ithūn kitī lāmb āhe?* *saraḷ jā, mag ujvīkaḍe vaḷā. sṭeśan mandirācyā samor āhe.*) Then say **ऋतू** and **ज्ञान**, and name the letters **ऋ** and **ञ**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C283-TIKIT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-BUY-TICKET-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-WHEN-LEAVES-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-LOST-REPORT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-MISSED-01, MR-LEX-C236-VASTU236-01, MR-LEX-C281-JNAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: One ticket to Pune, please. A return ticket.]
- [YOU SAY: What time does the train leave? Which platform?]
- [YOU SAY: My bag is lost. I complained to the police.]
- [YOU SAY: I was late, so I missed my train.]
- [YOU SAY: what went wrong on a trip of yours, in two sentences]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C283-TIKIT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-BUY-TICKET-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-WHEN-LEAVES-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-LOST-REPORT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-MISSED-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] A ticket? (**तिकीट**.) "When does it leave?" (**किती वाजता निघते?**) "Lost"? (**हरवली**.) "Missed"? (**चुकली**.) Which letters hide in **ज्ञान** and start **ऋतू**? (**ञ**, **ऋ**.)
