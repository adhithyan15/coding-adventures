## You'll want to know: ಸುಂಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C439-GADI439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಂಕ** — *suṅka* — "a customs duty; a toll".

> ಚಿನ್ನದ ಆಭರಣಗಳಿಗೆ ಸುಂಕ ಕಟ್ಟಬೇಕು.
>
> *cinnada ābharaṇagaḷige suṅka kaṭṭabēku.*
>
> Duty has to be paid on gold jewellery.

ಸುಂಕ ಕಟ್ಟು, to pay duty. ಶುಲ್ಕ, a fee, is the learned form of the same Sanskrit word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಂಕ** (*suṅka*) comes from Sanskrit *śulka*, "price, toll", through a Prakrit form *suṅka* (CDIAL 12543). ಶುಲ್ಕ, a fee, is the same word borrowed again in its learned shape.
