## You'll want to know: قارب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C429-QARIB429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قارب** — *qārib* — "a boat".

> ركبنا قاربا صغيرا إلى الجزيرة.
>
> *rakibnā qāriban ṣaghīran ilā al-jazīra.*
>
> We took a small boat to the island.

Plural: قوارب, *qawārib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قارب** (*qārib*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-b*, "to be near" (Lane). Lane lists *qārib* as a separate sense of the root: a ship's small boat, a skiff the seamen use for their errands.
