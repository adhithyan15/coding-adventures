Lesson 1 of 5.
образование (obrazovániye) — education.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a crib sheet, then the Russian for to get distracted.

You'll want to know: образование.
образование (obrazovániye) — "education".
У неё хорошее образование. U neyó khorósheye obrazovániye. She has a good education.
Neuter. Высшее образование (obrazovániye) — higher education. It also means formation: образование (obrazovániye) льда.

The word, taken apart.
образование (obrazovániye) is built on образовать "to form, to shape", from образ "a shape, an image", inherited from Proto-Slavic obrazŭ (EtymDB). Education gives a mind its shape.

Lesson 2 of 5.
диссертация (dissertátsiya) — a dissertation, a thesis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get distracted, then the Russian for education.

You'll want to know: диссертация.
диссертация (dissertátsiya) — "a dissertation, a thesis".
Он пишет диссертацию о русской истории. On píshet dissertátsiyu o rússkoy istórii. He is writing a dissertation on Russian history.
Feminine. Защитить диссертацию — to defend a thesis.

The word, taken apart.
диссертация (dissertátsiya) is usually traced to Latin dissertātiō "a discourse" (Lewis & Short), from dissertāre "to discuss", the frequentative of disserere "to set out in order", built on serere "to join, to string together".

Lesson 3 of 5.
кафедра (káfedra) — a university department; a lectern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for education, then the Russian for a dissertation.

You'll want to know: кафедра.
кафедра (káfedra) — "a university department; a lectern".
Он работает на кафедре физики. On rabótayet na káfedre fíziki. He works in the physics department.
Feminine. A department within a faculty: на кафедре. It is also the lecturer’s desk.

The word, taken apart.
кафедра (káfedra) is usually traced, through Latin cathedra, to Greek kathédra "a seat, a chair", built from katá "down" and hédra "a seat": the teacher’s chair, then the department round it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
бакалавр (bakalávr) — a bachelor (degree holder).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dissertation, then the Russian for a university department.

You'll want to know: бакалавр.
бакалавр (bakalávr) — "a bachelor (degree holder)".
После четырёх лет учёбы он стал бакалавром. Pósle chetyryókh let uchyóby on stal bakalávrom. After four years of study he became a bachelor.
Masculine. Степень бакалавра — a bachelor’s degree.

The word, taken apart.
бакалавр (bakalávr) is usually traced to Medieval Latin baccalaureus, a reshaping of baccalārius "a young man, a junior"; the further origin is not settled.
