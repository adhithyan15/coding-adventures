## You'll want to know: मार्ग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C417-DISHA417-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मार्ग** — *mārg* — "a route; a way (formal)".

> यह बस किस मार्ग से जाती है?
>
> *yah bas kis mārg se jātī hai?*
>
> Which route does this bus take?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मार्ग** (*mārg*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mārga*, first the track of a wild animal and then any road or way (Monier-Williams); street signs and bus boards use it, and it is the second half of राजमार्ग.
