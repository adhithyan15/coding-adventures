## You'll want to know: fāshēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C167-PAST167-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fāshēng* — "to happen; to take place".

> *qiántiān wǎnshang fāshēng le shénme* — What happened the night before last?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fāshēng* is built from two parts inherited from Middle Chinese (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): *pjot*, glossed 'to rise' and 'to start', the word in *chūfā*, and *srjaeng*, 生 'to be born': to start up and come into being.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
