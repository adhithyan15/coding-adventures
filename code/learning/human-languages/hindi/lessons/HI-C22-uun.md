## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊन** (*ūn*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *ūrṇā*, "wool" (CDIAL 2424).
