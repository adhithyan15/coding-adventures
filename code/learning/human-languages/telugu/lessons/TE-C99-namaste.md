# నమస్తే (namastē) — namaste, hello

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C98-REPLY-04, TE-LEX-C98-REPLY-05] -->

## You'll want to know: నమస్తే
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C99-WELCOME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**నమస్తే** (*namastē*) — "namaste", said with the palms together.

It sits beside **నమస్కారం**, your first word. Both bow: **నమస్తే** is "a bow to you", heard all
across India; **నమస్కారం** is the fuller Telugu form.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A second hello, shared with most of India.
