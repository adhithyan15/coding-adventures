## You'll want to know: انبعاث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C463-MUNAKH463-04]; assesses=[] -->

**انبعاث** — *inbiʿāth* — "an emission; a release".

> كان الانبعاث من المصنع كبيرا.
>
> *kāna al-inbiʿāth min al-maṣnaʿ kabīran.*
>
> The emission from the factory was large.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انبعاث** (*inbiʿāth*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-ʿ-th*, "to send forth, to rouse" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form VII, "being sent out".
