# লক্ষ করা (lôkkhô kôrā) — to observe; to watch closely

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for strange, then the Bengali for a shadow.

## You'll want to know: লক্ষ করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C286-DEKHA286-05]; assesses=[] -->

**লক্ষ করা** — *lôkkhô kôrā* — "to observe; to watch closely".

> পুলিশ অনেক দিন ধরে বাড়িটা লক্ষ করছিল।
>
> *pulish ônek din dhôre bāṛiṭā lôkkhô kôrchhilô.*
>
> The police had been watching the house for many days.

Past (he/she …): তিনি লোকটাকে লক্ষ করলেন, *tini lokṭāke lôkkhô kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লক্ষ করা** (*lôkkhô kôrā*) puts Sanskrit *lakṣa*, 'a mark, a target' (Monier-Williams), before করা: to make something your mark, to keep your eyes on it.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
