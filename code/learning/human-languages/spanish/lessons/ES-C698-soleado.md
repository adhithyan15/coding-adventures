## You'll want to know: soleado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C698-CIELO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**soleado** — "sunny".

> Fue un día soleado y tranquilo.
>
> *It was a sunny, quiet day.*

*Hace sol* is the everyday way to say it is sunny.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soleado* is built on *sol* "sun", which is inherited from Latin *sōl* (EtymDB).
