## You'll want to know: la liquidación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C631-REBAJAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la liquidación** — "the clearance sale; the settlement".

> La tienda cierra y hay liquidación.
>
> *The shop is closing and there is a clearance sale.*

Also the final pay you receive when you leave a job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liquidación* is built on *liquidar*, which comes from *líquido*, a learned borrowing from Latin *liquidus* 'fluid, clear' (EtymDB).
