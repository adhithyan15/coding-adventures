Arabic, chapter 342: Heritage and inventions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تراث (turāth) — heritage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a treaty, then the Arabic for to betray.

You'll want to know: تراث.
تراث — turāth — "heritage".
تحافظ القرية على تراثها القديم. tuḥāfiẓu al-qarya ʿalā turāthihā al-qadīm. The village takes care of its old heritage.

The word, taken apart.
تراث (turāth) is built on the native Arabic root w-r-th (Lane), "to inherit". Lane explains turāth as wurāth with t in place of the w: what a people inherits.

You'll want to know: مخطوطة.
مخطوطة — makhṭūṭa — "a manuscript".
وجد الباحث مخطوطة قديمة في المكتبة. wajada al-bāḥith makhṭūṭa qadīma fī al-maktaba. The researcher found an old manuscript in the library.

The word, taken apart.
مخطوطة (makhṭūṭa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-ṭ-ṭ (Lane), "to draw a line; to write", the root of khaṭṭ, handwriting. Makhṭūṭa is "a thing written by hand".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a manuscript, then the Arabic for an inscription.

You'll want to know: لقب.
لقب — laqab — "a title; a nickname".
كان لقب جدي في القرية الحكيم. kāna laqab jaddī fī al-qarya al-ḥakīm. My grandfather’s nickname in the village was the Wise One.
Plural: ألقاب, alqāb.
In sport: لقب (laqab) البطولة, the title.

The word, taken apart.
لقب (laqab) is built on the native Arabic root l-q-b (Lane), where laqab is a name given to a person besides his own, for praise or in jest.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اخترع (ikhtaraʿa) — to invent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an inscription, then the Arabic for a title.

You'll want to know: اخترع.
اخترع — ikhtaraʿa — "to invent".
اخترع العالم آلة جديدة. ikhtaraʿa al-ʿālim āla jadīda. The scientist invented a new machine.
Present: يخترع, yakhtariʿ.
An invention is اختراع.

The word, taken apart.
اخترع (ikhtaraʿa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-r-ʿ (Lane), "to split, to cleave". It is Form VIII, "to bring a thing out new, without a model".
