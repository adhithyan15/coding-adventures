# ਵਿਛਾਉਣਾ (vichhāuṇā) — to spread out (a rug or bedding)

## You'll want to know: ਵਿਛਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C233-DIN233-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਛਾਉਣਾ** — *vichhāuṇā* — "to spread out (a rug or bedding)".

> ਮੈਂ ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ ਉੱਤੇ ਦਰੀ ਵਿਛਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ farsh utte darī vichhāundī hāṁ.*
>
> I spread the rug on the floor.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਚਾਦਰ ਵਿਛਾਈ, *maiṁ chādar vichhāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਛਾਉਣਾ** (*vichhāuṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*vicchādayati*, "spread out"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vichāuṇā*, "to spread out (bedding &c.)" (CDIAL 11692).
