## You'll want to know: पश्चात्तापः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C551-PARITYAJATI551-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पश्चात्तापः** — *paścāttāpaḥ* — "remorse".

> यदि त्वं सत्यं वदसि, तर्हि पश्चात्तापः न भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ satyaṃ vadasi, tarhi paścāttāpaḥ na bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you tell the truth, there will be no remorse.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पश्चात्तापः** (*paścāttāpaḥ*) is built from *paścāt*, "after", and *tāpa*, "heat, pain", from the root √tap; MW gives *paścāttāpa* as after-pain, sorrow, regret, repentance (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
