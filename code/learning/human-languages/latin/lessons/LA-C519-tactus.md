## You'll want to know: tāctus, tāctūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C519-SENS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**tāctus, tāctūs** — "touch".

> Caecus tāctū litterās cognōscit.
>
> *The blind man recognises the letters by touch.*

Masculine, fourth declension, from *tangō* — to touch. English *tact*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**tāctus** is the action noun of *tangō* "to touch" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *tact* comes from it, and *contact* from the compound *contingō*.
