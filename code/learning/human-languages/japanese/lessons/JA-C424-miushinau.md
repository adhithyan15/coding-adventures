## You'll want to know: みうしなう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C424-OTOSHIMONO424-03]; assesses=[] -->

**みうしなう** — *miushinau* — "to lose sight of".

> **えきで ともだちを みうしないました。** — *eki de tomodachi o miushinaimashita* — "I lost sight of my friend at the station."

Polite **みうしないます**, *miushinaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが こどもを みうしないました。**, *haha ga kodomo o miushinaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みうしなう** (*miushinau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *mi*, "seeing" (from *miru*) and *ushinau*, "to lose"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
