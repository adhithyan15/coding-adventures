## You'll want to know: મુકાબલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C548-TEAM548-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મુકાબલો** — *mukāblo* — "a match-up".

> જો કાલે વરસાદ પડશે તો મુકાબલો રવિવારે થશે.
>
> *jo kāle varsād paḍśe to mukāblo ravivāre thaśe.*
>
> If it rains tomorrow, the contest will be held on Sunday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુકાબલો** (*mukāblo*) is usually traced to Arabic *muqābala*, "facing one another, an encounter", through Persian.
