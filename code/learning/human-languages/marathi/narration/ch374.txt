Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रक्रिया (prakriyā) — a process.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an analysis, then the Marathi for a method.

You'll want to know: प्रक्रिया.
प्रक्रिया — prakriyā — "a process".
प्रवेशाची प्रक्रिया एका आठवड्यात पूर्ण होईल. praveśācī prakriyā ekā āṭhvaḍyāt pūrṇa hoīl. The admission process will be complete in a week.
Feminine.
Feminine: X ची प्रक्रिया (prakriyā).

The word, taken apart.
प्रक्रिया (prakriyā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prakriyā, "producing; procedure" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सूत्र (sūtra) — a formula.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a process, then the Marathi for a phase.

You'll want to know: सूत्र.
सूत्र — sūtra — "a formula".
गणिताचं हे सूत्र लक्षात ठेवायला सोपं आहे. gaṇitācaṁ he sūtra lakṣāt ṭhevāylā sopaṁ āhe. This maths formula is easy to remember.
Neuter.
Neuter; also a thread, and a short rule in old texts.

The word, taken apart.
सूत्र (sūtra) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sūtra, "thread, cord" (CDIAL 13561); a short rule is a thread to hold on to. The same entry lists the inherited Marathi sūt, "thread".
