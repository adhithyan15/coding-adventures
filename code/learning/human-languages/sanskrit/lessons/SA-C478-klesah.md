## You'll want to know: क्लेशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C478-VEDANA478-04]; assesses=[] -->

**क्लेशः** — *kleśaḥ* — "suffering".

> रोगः बहु क्लेशं ददाति।
>
> *rogaḥ bahu kleśaṃ dadāti.*
>
> Illness causes much suffering.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्लेशः** (*kleśaḥ*) comes from the root √kliś, "to torment, to suffer"; MW gives "pain, affliction, distress, pain from disease" (MW).
