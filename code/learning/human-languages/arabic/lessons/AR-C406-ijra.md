## You'll want to know: إجراء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C406-TATALLUB406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**إجراء** — *ijrāʾ* — "a procedure".

> الإجراء بسيط ويأخذ دقيقة.
>
> *al-ijrāʾ basīṭ wa-yaʾkhudhu daqīqa.*
>
> The procedure is simple and takes a minute.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إجراء** (*ijrāʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-y*, "to run, to flow" (Lane). *Ijrāʾ* is the Form IV verbal noun, making a thing run: carrying out, a procedure.
