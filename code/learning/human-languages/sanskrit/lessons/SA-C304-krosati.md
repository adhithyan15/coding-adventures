## You'll want to know: क्रोशति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C304-RASHI304-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रोशति** — *krośati* — "shouts; cries out".

> सः नरः उच्चैः क्रोशति।
>
> *saḥ naraḥ uccaiḥ krośati.*
>
> The man shouts loudly.

Past (he …): सः उच्चैः क्रुष्टवान्, *saḥ uccaiḥ kruṣṭavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रोशति** (*krośati*) comes from the root √kruś, "to cry out, shout"; MW compares Greek *krazō* (MW).
