## You'll want to know: qìchē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C406-BUSY406-04]; assesses=[] -->

*qìchē* — "motor vehicle; car".

> *wǒmen zuò qìchē qù jīchǎng* — We're going to the airport by car.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qìchē* is built from *qì* 'steam' and 车 'vehicle'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.
