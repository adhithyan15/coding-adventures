## You'll want to know: ноздря
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C482-HEAD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ноздря** (*nozdryá*) — "a nostril".

> У меня заложена правая ноздря.
>
> *U menyá zalózhena právaya nozdryá.*
>
> My right nostril is blocked.

Feminine; plural ноздри.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ноздря* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *nozdra*, which EtymDB traces to PIE *néh₂s 'nose', the root of *нос*.
