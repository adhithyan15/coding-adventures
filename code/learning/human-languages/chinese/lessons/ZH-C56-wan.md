## You'll want to know: wán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C56-PLAY56-01]; assesses=[] -->

*wán* — "to play; to have fun".

> *háizi xǐhuan wán* — Children love to play.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wán* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ngwanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to toy with'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
