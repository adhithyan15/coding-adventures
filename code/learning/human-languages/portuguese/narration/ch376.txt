Portuguese, chapter 376: Na universidade.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a faculdade — the faculty (of a university); the ability.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the list of marks, then the Portuguese for the school subject.

You'll want to know: a faculdade.
a faculdade — "the faculty (of a university); the ability".
Ele estuda na faculdade de medicina. He studies at the faculty of medicine.
In Portugal people often say a faculdade for university.

The word, taken apart.
faculdade is a learned borrowing from Latin facultās "capability, power, means" (Lewis & Short), built on facul, facilis "easy, doable".

Lesson 2 of 5.
o caloiro — the first-year student, the fresher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the school subject, then the Portuguese for the faculty.

You'll want to know: o caloiro.
o caloiro — "the first-year student, the fresher".
Os caloiros chegam em setembro. The first-year students arrive in September.
European Portuguese; in Brazil o calouro. Feminine a caloira.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of caloiro is not settled; it is often linked with Greek kalógēros "good old man, monk", through an old word for a novice monk. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
a inscrição — the enrolment, the registration; the inscription.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the faculty, then the Portuguese for the first-year student.

You'll want to know: a inscrição.
a inscrição — "the enrolment, the registration; the inscription".
A inscrição no curso custa cinquenta euros. Signing up for the classes costs fifty euros.
Fazer a inscrição — to enrol, to sign up.

The word, taken apart.
inscrição is a learned borrowing from Latin īnscrīptiō "a writing upon, an inscription, a title" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from īnscrībere, on scrībere "to write": your name written on the list.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the first-year student, then the Portuguese for the enrolment.

You'll want to know: o semestre.
o semestre — "the semester, the half-year".
Este semestre tenho cinco cadeiras. This semester I have five course units.
In Portuguese universities a course unit is uma cadeira.

The word, taken apart.
semestre comes from Latin sēmestris "of six months, half-yearly" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on sex "six" and mēnsis "month".

Lesson 5 of 5.
a propina — the tuition fee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the enrolment, then the Portuguese for the semester.

You'll want to know: a propina.
a propina — "the tuition fee".
As propinas da faculdade subiram. The university fees have gone up.
European Portuguese for university fees; in Brazil a propina is a bribe, and the fee is a mensalidade.

The word, taken apart.
propina comes from Late Latin propīna (EtymDB), built on Latin propīnāre "to drink to someone's health, to hand over" (Lewis & Short): first a tip, then a fee.
