## You'll want to know: a costureira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C451-ARRANJOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a costureira** — "the seamstress, the dressmaker".

> A costureira arranjou o meu vestido.
>
> *The seamstress altered my dress.*

Masculine: *o costureiro*, often a fashion designer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*costureira* is built on *costura* 'sewing' with *-eira*, the person who does it; *costura* goes back to Vulgar Latin *consūtūra*, from Latin *consuere* 'to sew together', on *suere* 'to sew' (Lewis & Short).
