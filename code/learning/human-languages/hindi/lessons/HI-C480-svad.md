## You'll want to know: स्वाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C480-SPARSH480-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वाद** — *svād* — "a taste; a flavour".

> माँ के हाथ के खाने का स्वाद सबसे अच्छा है।
>
> *māṁ ke hāth ke khāne kā svād sabse acchā hai.*
>
> Food cooked by my mother tastes best of all.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वाद** (*svād*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *svāda*, "taste, flavour, savour" (Monier-Williams).
