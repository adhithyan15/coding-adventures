Marathi, chapter 552: When Plans Fail.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फिसकटणे (phiskaṭṇe) — to fall apart; to fall through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for reserved, then the Marathi for unforeseen.

You'll want to know: फिसकटणे.
फिसकटणे — phiskaṭṇe — "to fall apart; to fall through".
पावसामुळे आमचा सहलीचा बेत फिसकटला. pāvsāmuḷe āmcā sahlīcā bet phiskaṭlā. Because of the rain our plan for a trip fell through.
Past (he …): तो सौदा फिसकटला, to saudā phiskaṭlā.

The word, taken apart.
फिसकटणे (phiskaṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi phiskaṭṇẽ, "to fall away", under phiss, "slip" (CDIAL 9080).

पैसे नसल्यामुळे नवीन दुकानाची योजना बारगळली. paise naslyāmuḷe navīn dukānācī yojnā bārgaḷlī. For lack of money the plan for a new shop fizzled out.
Past (he …): तो प्रयत्न बारगळला, to prayatna bārgaḷlā.

The word, taken apart.
बारगळणे (bārgaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for a plan that comes to nothing; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: ढासळणे.
ढासळणे — ḍhāsaḷṇe — "to crumble; to cave in".
जोरदार पावसात जुन्या किल्ल्याची भिंत ढासळली. jordār pāvsāt junyā killyācī bhint ḍhāsaḷlī. In the heavy rain the old fort's wall crumbled.
Past (he …): तो किल्ला ढासळला, to killā ḍhāsaḷlā.

The word, taken apart.

You'll want to know: कोलमडणे.
कोलमडणे — kolmaḍṇe — "to break down; to collapse".
वडील आजारी पडले आणि घराचं सगळं गणित कोलमडलं. vaḍīl ājārī paḍle āṇi gharācaṁ sagḷaṁ gaṇit kolmaḍlaṁ. Father fell ill and the family's whole budget collapsed.
Past (he …): तो कोलमडला, to kolmaḍlā.

The word, taken apart.
कोलमडणे (kolmaḍṇe) is a Marathi verb for a person or a plan that gives way; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सुटणे (suṭṇe) — to come loose; to be let off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to crumble, then the Marathi for to break down.

You'll want to know: सुटणे.
सुटणे — suṭṇe — "to come loose; to be let off".
गाठ नीट बांधली नव्हती, म्हणून दोरी सुटली. gāṭh nīṭ bāndhlī navhatī, mhaṇūn dorī suṭlī. The knot was not tied well, so the rope came loose.
Past (he …): तो सुटला, to suṭlā.
Also शाळा सुटली, school let out, and प्रश्न सुटला, the problem was solved.

The word, taken apart.
सुटणे (suṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi suṭā, "loosened", under kṣuṭyatē, "is released" (CDIAL 3707).
