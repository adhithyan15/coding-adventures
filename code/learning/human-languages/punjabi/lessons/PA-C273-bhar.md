## You'll want to know: ਭਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C273-WEIGH273-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਾਰ** — *bhār* — "weight; a load".

> ਆਲੂਆਂ ਦਾ ਭਾਰ ਪੰਜ ਕਿੱਲੋ ਹੈ।
>
> *ālūāṁ dā bhār panj killo hai.*
>
> The potatoes weigh five kilos.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਾਰ** (*bhār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhāra*, "burden, weight"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bhār*, "load", under it (CDIAL 9459).
