Marathi, chapter 476: The Face Up Close.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बुबुळ (bubuḷ) — the eyeball; the iris.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for unconcerned, then the Marathi for to be scared of.

You'll want to know: बुबुळ.
बुबुळ — bubuḷ — "the eyeball; the iris".
डोळ्याच्या बुबुळावर एक लहान डाग आहे. ḍoḷyācyā bubuḷāvar ek lahān ḍāg āhe. There is a small spot on the eyeball.
Neuter.
Neuter: डोळ्याचं बुबुळ (bubuḷ).

The word, taken apart.
बुबुळ (bubuḷ) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
नाकपुडी (nākpuḍī) — a nostril.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be scared of, then the Marathi for the eyeball.

You'll want to know: नाकपुडी.
नाकपुडी — nākpuḍī — "a nostril".
सर्दीमुळे एक नाकपुडी बंद झाली. sardīmuḷe ek nākpuḍī band jhālī. With the cold, one nostril got blocked.
Feminine. Plural: नाकपुड्या, nākpuḍyā.
नाक + पुडी, a little packet.

The word, taken apart.
नाकपुडी (nākpuḍī) is a Marathi compound built from नाक, "nose", which CDIAL lists under nakka (CDIAL 6909), and पुडी, "a little packet".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a nostril, then the Marathi for a gum.

You'll want to know: दाढी.
दाढी — dāḍhī — "a beard".
आजोबांची पांढरी दाढी लांब आहे. ājobāñcī pāṇḍhrī dāḍhī lāmb āhe. Grandfather's white beard is long.
Feminine.
दाढी (dāḍhī) करणे, to shave; दाढी (dāḍhī) वाढवणे, to grow a beard.

The word, taken apart.
दाढी (dāḍhī) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi dāḍhī, "fang, tusk, beard", under dāṁṣṭra (CDIAL 6250).
