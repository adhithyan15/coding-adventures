## You'll want to know: prōmunturium, prōmunturiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C441-PORTUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prōmunturium, prōmunturiī** — "a headland, a promontory".

> Nāvis prope prōmunturium altum nāvigat.
>
> *The ship sails near a high headland.*

Second declension, neuter: *prōmunturium, prōmunturiī n.*, also spelled *prōmontōrium*. English *promontory* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prōmunturium* is usually traced to *prō* 'forward' and *mineō* 'to jut out' (Lewis & Short): land that juts forward into the sea. English *promontory* comes from it.
