## You'll want to know: তদন্ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C283-OPORADH283-04]; assesses=[] -->

**তদন্ত** — *tôdôntô* — "an investigation; an inquiry".

> পুলিশ ঘটনার তদন্ত শুরু করল।
>
> *pulish ghôṭônār tôdôntô shuru kôrlô.*
>
> The police began an investigation into the incident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তদন্ত** (*tôdôntô*) is usually explained as Sanskrit *tad*, 'that', and *anta*, 'end': going to the end of a matter. Monier-Williams lists *tadanta*, 'coming to an end by that'; the sense 'an inquiry' is the modern one.
