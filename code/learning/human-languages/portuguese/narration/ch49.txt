Portuguese, chapter 49: Bed, Key, Wall, Roof, Garden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a cama — a bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a table, then the Portuguese for a chair.

You'll want to know: a cama.
a cama — "a bed".

The word, taken apart.
cama from Late Latin cama, "a low bed" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. Where the Latin word came from is unknown.
