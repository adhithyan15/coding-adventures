## You'll want to know: أفرغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C426-JAMARIK426-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أفرغ** — *afragha* — "to empty out; to unpack".

> أفرغ المسافر حقيبته أمام الموظف.
>
> *afragha al-musāfir ḥaqībatahu amāma al-muwaẓẓaf.*
>
> The traveller emptied out his bag in front of the officer.

Present: يفرغ, *yufrigh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أفرغ** (*afragha*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-gh*, "to be empty, to be free" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to make empty", beside *fārigh*, "empty".
