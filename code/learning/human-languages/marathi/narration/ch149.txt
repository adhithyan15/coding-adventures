Marathi, chapter 149: Salty, Sour, Bitter, Spicy, Fresh.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
खारट (khāraṭ) — salty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for lukewarm, then the Marathi for dry.

You'll want to know: खारट.
खारट — khāraṭ — "salty".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: khāraṭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: khāraṭ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say khāraṭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for lukewarm, then the Marathi for dry, then say khāraṭ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does खारट (khāraṭ) mean? ("Salty".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
School, clothes and food — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for spicy and fresh.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a shore, an island, lightning; electricity, a storm, a crow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sparrow, a parrot, a pigeon, an owl, a frog]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a tortoise, a crocodile, a deer, a rabbit, a wolf]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a fox, a donkey, an ox, a mosquito, the forehead]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A shore? (किनारा, kinārā.) An island? (बेट, beṭ.) Lightning; electricity? (वीज, vīj.) A storm? (वादळ, vādaḷ.) A crow? (कावळा, kāvḷā.) A sparrow? (चिमणी, cimṇī.) A parrot? (पोपट, popaṭ.) A pigeon? (कबूतर, kabūtar.) An owl? (घुबड, ghubaḍ.) A frog? (बेडूक, beḍūk.) A tortoise? (कासव, kāsav.) A crocodile? (मगर, magar.) A deer? (हरीण, harīṇ.) A rabbit? (ससा, sasā.) A wolf? (लांडगा, lāṇḍgā.) A fox? (कोल्हा, kolhā.) A donkey? (गाढव, gāḍhav.) An ox? (बैल, bail.) A mosquito? (डास, ḍās.) The forehead? (कपाळ, kapāḷ.)


Lesson 7 of 7.
Food and nature — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a cheek and the tongue.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a cheek, the tongue, the chin, an elbow, a wrist]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a thumb, a fingernail, the back, the waist, the chest]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the skin, a bone, wide, narrow, thin]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: heavy, soft, hard, lukewarm, dry]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: salty, sour, bitter, spicy, fresh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A cheek? (गाल, gāl.) The tongue? (जीभ, jībh.) The chin? (हनुवटी, hanuvaṭī.) An elbow? (कोपर, kopar.) A wrist? (मनगट, managaṭ.) A thumb? (अंगठा, aṅgṭhā.) A fingernail? (नख, nakh.) The back? (पाठ, pāṭh.) The waist? (कंबर, kambar.) The chest? (छाती, chātī.) The skin? (त्वचा, tvacā.) A bone? (हाड, hāḍ.) Wide? (रुंद, ruṇd.) Narrow? (अरुंद, aruṇd.) Thin? (पातळ, pātaḷ.) Heavy? (जड, jaḍ.) Soft? (मऊ, maū.) Hard? (कडक, kaḍak.) Lukewarm? (कोमट, komaṭ.) Dry? (सुका, sukā.) Salty? (खारट, khāraṭ.) Sour? (आंबट, āmbaṭ.) Bitter? (कडू, kaḍū.) Spicy? (तिखट, tikhaṭ.) Fresh? (ताजा, tājā.)
