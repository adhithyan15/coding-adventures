## You'll want to know: अनुभव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C392-TAIYARI392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुभव** — *anubhav* — "experience".

> अगर तुम्हें अनुभव होगा तो यह नौकरी जल्दी मिलेगी।
>
> *agar tumhẽ anubhav hogā to yah naukrī jaldī milegī.*
>
> If you have experience, you'll get this job quickly.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुभव** (*anubhav*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *anubhava*, "perception, experience" (Monier-Williams).
