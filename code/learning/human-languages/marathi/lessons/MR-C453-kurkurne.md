## You'll want to know: कुरकुरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C453-TAPAT453-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कुरकुरणे** — *kurkurṇe* — "to grumble".

> जेवण थंड होतं म्हणून आजोबा कुरकुरले.
>
> *jevaṇ thaṇḍ hotaṁ mhaṇūn ājobā kurkurle.*
>
> Grandfather grumbled because the food was cold.

Past (he …): तो कुरकुरला, *to kurkurlā*.

कुरकुर, grumbling: कुरकुर करणे, to keep grumbling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुरकुरणे** (*kurkurṇe*) is a Marathi verb built by doubling a syllable, कुर-कुर; its origin is not settled.
