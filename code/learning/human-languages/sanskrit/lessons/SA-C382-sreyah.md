## You'll want to know: श्रेयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C382-HITA382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रेयः** — *śreyaḥ* — "the better course; welfare".

> यदि भवान् रुग्णः, तर्हि विश्रामः श्रेयः।
>
> *yadi bhavān rugṇaḥ, tarhi viśrāmaḥ śreyaḥ.*
>
> If you are ill, rest is the better course.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रेयः** (*śreyaḥ*) MW gives *śreyas* "better" and, as a neuter noun, "the better state, the better fortune"; MW gives no derivation here, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
