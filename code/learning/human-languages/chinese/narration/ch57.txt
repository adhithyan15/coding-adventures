Mandarin Chinese, chapter 57: At Home.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
fángzi — house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to run, then the Mandarin for to laugh.

You'll want to know: fángzi.
fángzi — "house".
zhè shì wǒ de fángzi — This is my house.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
fángzi is built from fáng 'room' and the noun ending zi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, bjang and tsiX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
One more word about people.

Lesson 2 of 7.
fángjiān — room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: fángjiān.
fángjiān — "room".
wǒ de fángjiān hěn xiǎo — My room is small.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
fángjiān is built from fáng 'room' and jiān 'space between', both inherited from Middle Chinese, bjang and kean (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the second under an older form of the character, glossed 'a gap'.

Lesson 3 of 7.
zhuōzi — table.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for house, then the Mandarin for room.

You'll want to know: zhuōzi.
zhuōzi — "table".
wǒ de zhuōzi hěn dà — My desk is big.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhuōzi is built from zhuō and the noun ending zi. The Guangyun lists the first character as traewk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), an old way of writing a word for 'high'; the table sense is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.

You'll want to know: yǐzi.
yǐzi — "chair".

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yǐzi is built from yǐ and the noun ending zi. The Guangyun records the first character as 'jeX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), and in a second reading as the name of a tree; the chair sense is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 7.
这儿 (zhèr) — here.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for table, then the Mandarin for chair.

You'll want to know: 这儿.
这儿 — zhèr — "here".
你想来这儿吗 nǐ xiǎng lái zhèr ma — Would you like to come here?

The word, taken apart.
这儿 (zhèr) is built from 这 'this' and 儿, the ending that runs into the syllable before it as an r.

What you've built.
