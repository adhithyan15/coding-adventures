# دھننا (dhunnā) — to card (cotton)

## You'll want to know: دھننا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C499-CHARKHA499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دھننا** — *dhunnā* — "to card (cotton)".

> دادی نے رضائی کے لیے کپاس دھنی۔
>
> *dādī ne razāī ke liye kapās dhunī.*
>
> Grandmother carded cotton for the quilt.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کپاس دھنی, *us ne kapās dhunī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دھننا** (*dhunnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *dhunnā*, "to beat, to scutch cotton", under Sanskrit *dhūnōti*, "shakes" (CDIAL 6846).
