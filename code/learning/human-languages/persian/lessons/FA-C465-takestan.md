## You'll want to know: تاکستان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C465-MAZRAE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تاکستان** (*tâkestân*) — "a vineyard".

> در پاییز در تاکستان انگور میچینند.
>
> *Dar pâyiz dar tâkestân angur michinand.*
>
> In autumn they pick grapes in the vineyard.

*tâk* 'vine' + *-estân* 'place of'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tâkestân* is a native compound of *tâk* 'vine' and the ending *-estân* 'place of', as in *bimârestân* 'hospital'. EtymDB does not list it.
