## You'll want to know: متردد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C497-TAWATTUR497-04]; assesses=[] -->

**متردد** — *mutaraddid* — "hesitant".

> كان الزبون مترددا بين القميصين.
>
> *kāna az-zabūn mutaraddidan bayna al-qamīṣayn.*
>
> The customer was hesitating between the two shirts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متردد** (*mutaraddid*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-d-d*, "to send back, to return" (Lane). Mutaraddid is the participle of Form V: one who goes back and forth.
