## You'll want to know: ربيب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C532-HAYAT532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ربيب** — *rabīb* — "a stepson".

> يحب الرجل ربيبه مثل ابنه.
>
> *yuḥibbu ar-rajul rabībahu mithla ibnihi.*
>
> The man loves his stepson like his own son.

ربيبة is a stepdaughter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ربيب** (*rabīb*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-b-b*, "a lord, an owner; to rear" (Lane). Lane gives rabīb as a wife's son, reared by her husband.
