## You'll want to know: ಕರಪತ್ರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C335-PATRIKE335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕರಪತ್ರ** — *karapatra* — "a leaflet; a pamphlet".

> ಹುಡುಗರು ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದಲ್ಲಿ ಕರಪತ್ರ ಹಂಚಿದರು.
>
> *huḍugaru bas nildāṇadalli karapatra hañcidaru.*
>
> The boys handed out leaflets at the bus stand.

Plural: ಕರಪತ್ರಗಳು, *karapatragaḷu*.

ಕರ, hand, and ಪತ್ರ, a leaf: a sheet put into people's hands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕರಪತ್ರ** (*karapatra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of *kara*, "the hand" (Monier-Williams), and *pattra*, "a leaf, the leaf of a book" (CDIAL 7733).
