## You'll want to know: niúnǎi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C39-DRINK39-04]; assesses=[] -->

*niúnǎi* — "milk".

> *háizi xǐhuan hē niúnǎi* — Children like milk.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niúnǎi* is built from niú 'cattle' and nǎi 'milk'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *ngjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a large beast'. The second is probably the word the Guangyun records as *nreaX*, glossed 'milk', under another way of writing the character.
