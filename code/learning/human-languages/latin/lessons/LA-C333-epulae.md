## You'll want to know: epulae, epulārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C333-FESTUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**epulae, epulārum** — "a feast, a banquet".

> Post sacrificium epulae magnae parātae sunt.
>
> *After the sacrifice a great feast was prepared.*

Feminine, plural; the singular is the neuter *epulum*, a public banquet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**epulae** is the plural that goes with *epulum*, "a banquet"; Lewis & Short call its origin doubtful and suggest, with hesitation, a link with *edō*, "to eat".
