## You'll want to know: दिदृक्षा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C549-LALASA549-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दिदृक्षा** — *didṛkṣā* — "a wish to see".

> यदि नगरस्य दिदृक्षा अस्ति, तर्हि श्वः तत्र गच्छामः।
>
> *yadi nagarasya didṛkṣā asti, tarhi śvaḥ tatra gacchāmaḥ.*
>
> If you want to see the city, we will go there tomorrow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिदृक्षा** (*didṛkṣā*) comes from the desiderative of the root √dṛś, "to see": MW gives *didṛkṣā* as the desire of seeing (MW).
