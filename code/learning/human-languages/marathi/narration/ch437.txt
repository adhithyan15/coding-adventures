Marathi, chapter 437: A Biting Chill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गारवा (gārvā) — coolness; a chill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for humid, then the Marathi for to blaze.

You'll want to know: गारवा.
गारवा — gārvā — "coolness; a chill".
संध्याकाळी हवेत गारवा आला. sandhyākāḷī havet gārvā ālā. In the evening a chill came into the air.
Masculine.
A pleasant coolness; गारवा (gārvā) येणे.

The word, taken apart.
गारवा (gārvā) is built on gār, "cool, cold", with a noun ending; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
हिमवर्षाव (himvarṣāv) — a snowfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to blaze, then the Marathi for coolness.

You'll want to know: हिमवर्षाव.
हिमवर्षाव — himvarṣāv — "a snowfall".
डोंगरावर रात्रभर हिमवर्षाव झाला. ḍoṅgarāvar rātrabhar himvarṣāv jhālā. It snowed on the mountain all night.
Masculine.
हिम, snow, and वर्षाव, a shower.

The word, taken apart.
हिमवर्षाव (himvarṣāv) is built from Sanskrit hima, "snow" (Monier-Williams), and वर्षाव, "a shower", from the family of varṣati, "rains" (CDIAL 11394).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for coolness, then the Marathi for a snowfall.

You'll want to know: झोंबणे.
झोंबणे — jhombṇe — "to sting; to nip".
पहाटेचा थंड वारा अंगाला झोंबला. pahāṭecā thaṇḍ vārā aṅgālā jhomblā. The cold dawn wind stung my body.
Past (he …): तो झोंबला, to jhomblā.
What is stung takes -ला: अंगाला, कानाला झोंबणे (jhombṇe).

The word, taken apart.
झोंबणे (jhombṇe) is a Marathi verb for cold, smoke or spice that stings; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a snowfall, then the Marathi for to sting.

You'll want to know: गोठणे.
गोठणे — goṭhṇe — "to freeze".
थंडीत तळ्याचं पाणी गोठलं. thaṇḍīt taḷyācaṁ pāṇī goṭhlaṁ. In the cold the water of the pond froze.
Past (he …): तो गोठला, to goṭhlā.
Water and blood गोठतात; not the same word as गोठा, a cowshed.

The word, taken apart.
गोठणे (goṭhṇe) is a Marathi verb for water turning to ice; its origin is not settled.

उन्हात बर्फ लवकर वितळतो. unhāt barpha lavkar vitaḷto. Ice melts quickly in the sun.
Past (he …): तो वितळला, to vitaḷlā.
Ice, butter and wax वितळतात.

The word, taken apart.
वितळणे (vitaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for something solid turning liquid; its origin is not settled.
