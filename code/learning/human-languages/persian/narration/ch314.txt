Persian, chapter 314: Events and taking part.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رویداد (ruydâd) — an event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a summary, then the Persian for to summarise.

You'll want to know: رویداد.
رویداد (ruydâd) — "an event".
جشنواره فیلم مهمترین رویداد فرهنگی شهر است. Jashnvâre-ye film mohemtarin ruydâd-e farhangi-ye shahr ast. The film festival is the most important cultural event in the city.
A modern word, built on ruy dâdan "to occur". Plural ruydâd-hâ.

The word, taken apart.
ruydâd is derived from ruy dâdan "to occur" (EtymDB): ruy "face" with dâd, the past stem of dâdan "to give". Both parts are native Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.

واقعه (vâqe'e) — "an incident, an occurrence".
این واقعه در یک شب سرد اتفاق افتاد. In vâqe'e dar yek shab-e sard ettefâq oftâd. This incident happened on a cold night.
Formal; broken plural vaqâye'. Often for serious or historic events.

The word, taken apart.
vâqe'e is borrowed from Arabic wāqi'a, "event, incident" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
برگزار شدن (bargozâr shodan) — to be held.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an event, then the Persian for an incident.

You'll want to know: برگزار شدن.
برگزار شدن (bargozâr shodan) — "to be held".
جلسه در سالن بزرگ مدرسه برگزار شد. Jalase dar sâlon-e bozorg-e madrese bargozâr shod. The meeting was held in the big hall of the school.
For organised events. Present bargozâr mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
bargozâr "carried out" joins bar "up, on" and the native stem gozâr- "to set out"; shodan "to become" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.

برگزار کردن (bargozâr kardan) — "to hold, to organise (an event)".
دانشگاه هر سال یک جشنواره برگزار میکند. Dâneshgâh har sâl yek jashnvâre bargozâr mikonad. The university holds a festival every year.
Present bargozâr mikonad. The event takes râ when it is definite.

The word, taken apart.
bargozâr kardan joins the native bargozâr "carried out" (bar + gozâr-) with kardan "to do", inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
شرکت کردن (sherkat kardan) — to take part, to participate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be held, then the Persian for to hold.

You'll want to know: شرکت کردن.
شرکت کردن (sherkat kardan) — "to take part, to participate".
صد نفر در مسابقه شرکت کردند. Sad nafar dar mosâbeqe sherkat kardand. A hundred people took part in the competition.
With dar: dar jalase sherkat kard. The noun sherkat alone is "a company".

The word, taken apart.
sherkat is borrowed from Arabic shirka (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "partnership, sharing". kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.
