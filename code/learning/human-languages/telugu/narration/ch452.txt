Telugu, chapter 452: Ships and Harbours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
రేవు (rēvu) — a harbour; a landing place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for going on foot, then the Telugu for a wayfarer.

You'll want to know: రేవు.
రేవు — rēvu — "a harbour; a landing place".
సాయంత్రం పడవలు రేవుకు తిరిగి వచ్చాయి. sāyantraṁ paḍavalu rēvuku tirigi vaccāyi. In the evening the boats came back to the harbour.
A harbour for ships, and also the steps down to a river where people bathe and boats land.

The word, taken apart.
రేవు (rēvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 516 lists Telugu rēvu, "a landing place, a port, a ford", in the family of Tamil iṟaṅku, "to descend, to disembark".

Lesson 2 of 5.
నౌక (nauka) — a ship; a vessel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a wayfarer, then the Telugu for a harbour.

You'll want to know: నౌక.
నౌక — nauka — "a ship; a vessel".
పెద్ద నౌక సముద్రంలో దూరంగా కనిపించింది. pedda nauka samudraṁlō dūraṅgā kanipiñcindi. A big ship appeared far out at sea.
Plural: నౌకలు, naukalu.
A formal word. Plural నౌకలు.

The word, taken apart.
నౌక (nauka) is borrowed from Sanskrit naukā, "a boat, a ship" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
నావికుడు (nāvikuḍu) — a sailor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a harbour, then the Telugu for a ship.

You'll want to know: నావికుడు.
నావికుడు — nāvikuḍu — "a sailor".
నా మామయ్య నావికుడిగా పనిచేశాడు. nā māmayya nāvikuḍigā panicēśāḍu. My uncle worked as a sailor.
నావ, a boat, + -ఇకుడు, one who works with it.

The word, taken apart.
నావికుడు (nāvikuḍu) is borrowed from Sanskrit nāvika, "a sailor, a helmsman" (CDIAL 7082), from nāvā, "a boat".

Lesson 4 of 5.
లంగరు (laṅgaru) — an anchor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a ship, then the Telugu for a sailor.

You'll want to know: లంగరు.
లంగరు — laṅgaru — "an anchor".
నావికులు రేవులో లంగరు వేశారు. nāvikulu rēvulō laṅgaru vēśāru. The sailors dropped anchor in the harbour.
లంగరు (laṅgaru) వేయు, to drop anchor; లంగరు (laṅgaru) ఎత్తు, to weigh anchor.

The word, taken apart.
లంగరు (laṅgaru) is usually traced to Persian langar, "an anchor", which reached the coasts of India with sea trade.

Lesson 5 of 5.
దీపస్తంభం (dīpastambhaṁ) — a lighthouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sailor, then the Telugu for an anchor.

You'll want to know: దీపస్తంభం.
దీపస్తంభం — dīpastambhaṁ — "a lighthouse".
రాత్రి దీపస్తంభం వెలుగు నౌకలకు దారి చూపిస్తుంది. rātri dīpastambhaṁ velugu naukalaku dāri cūpistundi. At night the light of the lighthouse shows the ships the way.
దీపం, a lamp, + స్తంభం, a pillar: a pillar with a lamp.

The word, taken apart.
దీపస్తంభం (dīpastambhaṁ) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: dīpa, "a lamp" (CDIAL 6348), and stambha, "a pillar, a post" (CDIAL 13682).
