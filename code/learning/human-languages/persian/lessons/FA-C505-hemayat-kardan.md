# حمایت کردن (hemâyat kardan) — to support, to back

## You'll want to know: حمایت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C505-SUPPORT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حمایت کردن** (*hemâyat kardan*) — "to support, to back".

> خانواده ام همیشه از من حمایت میکنند.
>
> *khânevâde-am hamishe az man hemâyat mikonand.*
>
> My family always supports me.

*az kasi hemâyat kardan*; *hemâyat* 'protection, support' + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hemâyat* comes from Arabic *ḥimāya*, 'protection' (EtymDB). *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
