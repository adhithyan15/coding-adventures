Punjabi, chapter 359: Studying Hard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਬਕ (sabak) — a lesson to learn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for history, then the Punjabi for to teach a subject.

You'll want to know: ਸਬਕ.
ਸਬਕ — sabak — "a lesson to learn".
ਅੱਜ ਦਾ ਸਬਕ ਛੋਟਾ ਹੈ। ajj dā sabak chhoṭā hai. Today's lesson is short.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਬਕ (sabak) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic sabaq, "lesson".

ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਅਭਿਆਸ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ har roz abhiās kardā hāṁ. I practise every day.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਧਿਆਨ (dhiān) — attention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for practice, then the Punjabi for hard work.

You'll want to know: ਧਿਆਨ.
ਧਿਆਨ — dhiān — "attention".
ਜਮਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਧਿਆਨ ਨਾਲ ਸੁਣੋ। jamāt vich dhiān nāl suṇo. Listen carefully in class.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਧਿਆਨ (dhiān) is taken over from Sanskrit dhyāna, "meditation, thought, reflection", a tatsama (Monier-Williams); the everyday sense "attention" is not among them.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਸੁਧਾਰਨਾ (sudhārnā) — to correct; to put right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for hard work, then the Punjabi for attention.

You'll want to know: ਸੁਧਾਰਨਾ.
ਸੁਧਾਰਨਾ — sudhārnā — "to correct; to put right".
ਅਧਿਆਪਕ ਮੇਰੇ ਗ਼ਲਤ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਸੁਧਾਰਦਾ ਹੈ। adhiāpak mere ghalat shabad sudhārdā hai. The teacher corrects my wrong words.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਗ਼ਲਤ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਸੁਧਾਰੇ, maiṁ ghalat shabad sudhāre.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੁਧਾਰਨਾ (sudhārnā) is inherited from a reconstructed śuddhakāra, "making clean"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sudharnā, "to repair", under it (CDIAL 12521). CDIAL does not give the sense "to correct".
