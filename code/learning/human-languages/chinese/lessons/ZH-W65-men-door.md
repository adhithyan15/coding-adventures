## Script — a dot, a post, a frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-MEN-DOOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 门

**One piece, three strokes**, the same three as on the right of 们.

**A dot** at the top left.

**A vertical** down the left side, the door post.

**A horizontal that turns down the right side** and hooks to the left at the foot.
