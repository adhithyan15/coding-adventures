## You'll want to know: أرضع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C530-ATFAL530-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أرضع** — *arḍaʿa* — "to breastfeed".

> أرضعت الأم طفلها في الصباح.
>
> *arḍaʿat al-umm ṭiflahā fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> The mother fed her baby in the morning.

Present: يرضع, *yurḍiʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أرضع** (*arḍaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ḍ-ʿ*, "to suck the breast" (Lane). Form IV arḍaʿa is to give the breast.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
