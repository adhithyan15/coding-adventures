## You'll want to know: ворчать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C478-STUBBORN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ворчать** (*vorchát*) — "to grumble".

> Дед ворчит, что молодёжь сидит в телефонах.
>
> *Ded vorchít, chto molodyózh sidít v telefónakh.*
>
> Grandad grumbles that young people are glued to their phones.

Imperfective; present ворчу, ворчишь. Perfective проворчать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *ворчать*, glossed 'grumble', without a source; it is usually taken as imitative, like *урчать* 'to rumble'.
