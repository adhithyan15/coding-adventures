## You'll want to know: der Zwerg
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C315-RITTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zwerg** — "the dwarf".

> Sieben Zwerge wohnten hinter den Bergen.
>
> *Seven dwarfs lived beyond the mountains.*

Plural: *die Zwerge*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zwerg* is inherited from Old High German *twerc*, from Proto-Germanic *dwergaz* (EtymDB), the same source as English *dwarf*.
