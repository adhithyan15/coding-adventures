Hindi, chapter 502: Grandchildren and Nephews.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पोता (potā) — a grandson (a son's son).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to keep up, then the Hindi for a reconciliation.

You'll want to know: पोता.
पोता — potā — "a grandson (a son's son)".
दादा अपने पोते के साथ खेल रहे हैं। dādā apne pote ke sāth khel rahe haiṁ. Grandfather is playing with his grandson.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पोता (potā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi potā, "son's son", under Sanskrit paútra, "grandson" (CDIAL 8416). The granddaughter is potī.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: झुलाना.
झुलाना — jhulānā — "to rock; to swing (someone)".
दादी पोते को झूले में झुला रही हैं। dādī pote ko jhūle meṁ jhulā rahī haiṁ. Grandmother is rocking her grandson in the swing.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को झुलाया, usne bacce ko jhulāyā.

The word, taken apart.
झुलाना (jhulānā) is the causative partner of jhūlnā, "to swing", which CDIAL lists under jhulyati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5406); jhūlā, "a swing", belongs to the same family.

Lesson 5 of 5.
इकलौता (iklautā) — only (child).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a nephew (a sister's son), then the Hindi for to rock.

You'll want to know: इकलौता.
इकलौता — iklautā — "only (child)".
अरुण अपने माँ बाप का इकलौता बेटा है। Aruṇ apne māṁ bāp kā iklautā beṭā hai. Arun is his parents' only son.

The word, taken apart.
इकलौता (iklautā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi iklautā, "only begotten, only son", under ēkkalaputra, a form it reconstructs from ēkkala, "alone", and putra, "son" (CDIAL 2507). akelā, "alone", comes from the same ēkkala.
