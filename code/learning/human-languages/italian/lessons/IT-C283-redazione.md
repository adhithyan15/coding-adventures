## You'll want to know: la redazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C283-REDAZIONE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la redazione** — "the newsroom, the editorial staff".

> In redazione lavorano venti persone.
>
> *Twenty people work in the newsroom.*

Also the writing of a text: *la redazione di un documento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*redazione* is usually traced to French *rédaction*, built on Latin *redāctus*, the past participle of *redigere* "to bring back, to put in order" (Lewis & Short).
