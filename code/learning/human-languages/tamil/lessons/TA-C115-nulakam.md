## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நூலகம்** (*nūlakam*) is native: *nūl*, "thread, treatise, book" (DEDR 3726), and *akam*, "house, inside" (DEDR 7): "the house of books".
