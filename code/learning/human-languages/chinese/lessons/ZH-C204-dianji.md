## You'll want to know: diǎnjī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C204-ONLINE204-03]; assesses=[] -->

*diǎnjī* — "to click (on a screen)".

> *qǐng diǎnjī zhèr* — Please click here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diǎnjī* is built from 点 'a dot, to touch' and *jī* 'to strike', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *temX* and *kek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to hit'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
