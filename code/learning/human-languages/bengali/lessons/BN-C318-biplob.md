## You'll want to know: বিপ্লব
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C318-BIPLOB318-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিপ্লব** — *biplôb* — "a revolution".

> বিপ্লবের পরে দেশে নতুন সরকার এল।
>
> *biplôber pôre deshe nôtun sôrkār elô.*
>
> After the revolution a new government came to power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিপ্লব** (*biplôb*) is Sanskrit *viplava*, 'tumult, a revolt; ruin' (Monier-Williams), from *vi-√plu*, 'to float apart'.
