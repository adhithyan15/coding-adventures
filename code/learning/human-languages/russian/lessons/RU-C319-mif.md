## You'll want to know: миф
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C319-LEGENDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**миф** (*mif*) — "a myth".

> Это только миф.
>
> *Éto tólko mif.*
>
> That is only a myth.

Masculine. *Мифы Древней Греции* — the myths of ancient Greece.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*миф* goes back to Greek *mûthos* "a word, a tale" (EtymDB).
