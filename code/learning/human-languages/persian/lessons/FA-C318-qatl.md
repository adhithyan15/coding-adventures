## You'll want to know: قتل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C318-JENAYAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قتل** (*qatl*) — "a murder, a killing".

> قتل در یک خانه قدیمی اتفاق افتاد.
>
> *Qatl dar yek khâne-ye qadimi ettefâq oftâd.*
>
> The murder happened in an old house.

Formal and legal. *Be qatl residan* "to be murdered".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qatl* is borrowed from Arabic *qatl*, "killing" (EtymDB).
