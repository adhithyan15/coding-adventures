Lesson 1 of 5.
పూజారి (pūjāri) — a priest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a subject, then the Telugu for mathematics.

You'll want to know: పూజారి.
పూజారి — pūjāri — "a priest".

The word, taken apart.
పూజారి (pūjāri) is Sanskrit pūjā, "honour, worship" (CDIAL 8317), with the ending -āri: "one who does worship".
