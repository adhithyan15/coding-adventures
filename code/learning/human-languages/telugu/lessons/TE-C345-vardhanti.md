## You'll want to know: వర్ధంతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C345-SMRTI345-04]; assesses=[] -->

**వర్ధంతి** — *vardhanti* — "a death anniversary".

> నిన్న తాతగారి మొదటి వర్ధంతి జరిగింది.
>
> *ninna tātagāri modaṭi vardhanti jarigindi.*
>
> Yesterday was grandfather's first death anniversary.

Families remember the dead on this day each year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వర్ధంతి** (*vardhanti*) comes from Sanskrit, but its exact source is not settled.
