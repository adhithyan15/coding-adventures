## You'll want to know: রসিক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C463-DUSHTU463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**রসিক** — *rôsik* — "witty; with a sense of humour".

> আমার রসিক মামার গল্প শুনে আমরা হাসলাম।
>
> *āmār rôsik māmār gôlpo shune āmrā hāslām.*
>
> We laughed at my witty uncle's stories.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রসিক** (*rôsik*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *rasika*, "tasteful, having a fine sense of taste" (Monier-Williams); রসিকতা (taught), a joke, is its noun.

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
