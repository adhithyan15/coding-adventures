## You'll want to know: نالی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C416-KURA416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نالی** — *nālī* — "a drain".

> بارش کے بعد نالی بھر گئی۔
>
> *bārish ke bād nālī bhar gaī.*
>
> After the rain the drain filled up.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نالی** (*nālī*) is built on *nāl*, "a hollow stalk, a tube", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *nāḍī*, "a tube" (CDIAL 7047), with the ending *-ī*.
