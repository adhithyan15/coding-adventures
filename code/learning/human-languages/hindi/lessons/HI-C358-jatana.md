## You'll want to know: जताना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C358-SEVA358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जताना** — *jatānā* — "to show, to make known (a feeling)".

> मीरा ने अपना दुख नहीं जताया।
>
> *Mīrā ne apnā dukh nahīṃ jatāyā.*
>
> Mira didn't show her sorrow.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना प्यार जताया, *usne apnā pyār jatāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जताना** (*jatānā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *jñapta*, "instructed" (CDIAL 5273). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to make known".
