# బడ్జెట్టు (baḍjeṭṭu) — a budget

## You'll want to know: బడ్జెట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C405-ARTHIKA405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**బడ్జెట్టు** — *baḍjeṭṭu* — "a budget".

> మా ఇంటి బడ్జెట్టు చాలా చిన్నది.
>
> *mā iṇṭi baḍjeṭṭu cālā cinnadi.*
>
> Our household budget is very small.

Like బస్సు, the English loan ends in -ఉ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బడ్జెట్టు** (*baḍjeṭṭu*) is a loan from English *budget*, with the final -ఉ that many English loans take.
