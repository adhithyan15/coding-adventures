# تثاءب (tathāʾaba) — to yawn

## You'll want to know: تثاءب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C514-TAAB514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تثاءب** — *tathāʾaba* — "to yawn".

> تثاءب الطالب في المحاضرة.
>
> *tathāʾaba aṭ-ṭālib fī al-muḥāḍara.*
>
> The student yawned in the lecture.

Present: يتثاءب, *yatathāʾab*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تثاءب** (*tathāʾaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-ʾ-b*, "to yawn" (Lane). Form VI tathāʾaba is the usual verb.
