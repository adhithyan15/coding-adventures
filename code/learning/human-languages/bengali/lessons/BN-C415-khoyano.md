## You'll want to know: খোয়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C415-SUD415-05]; assesses=[] -->

**খোয়ানো** — *khoyāno* — "to lose (money or property); to forfeit".

> ব্যবসায় অরুণ তার সব সঞ্চয় খোয়াল।
>
> *bæbsāy ôrun tār sôb sôñchôy khoyālô.*
>
> Arun lost all his savings in the business.

Past (he/she …): তিনি সব টাকা খোয়ালেন, *tini sôb ṭākā khoyālen*.

হারানো is to lose anything, a key or the way; খোয়ানো is to lose what you had of value, often by your own doing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খোয়ানো** (*khoyāno*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *khoyā*, "thrown away, lost", under Sanskrit *kṣapita*, "cast away, destroyed" (CDIAL 3653).
