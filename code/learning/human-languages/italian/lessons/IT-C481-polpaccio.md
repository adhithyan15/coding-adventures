## You'll want to know: il polpaccio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C481-GAMBA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il polpaccio** — "calf (of the leg)".

> Dopo la gita in montagna ho i polpacci duri.
>
> *After the trip to the mountains my calves are stiff.*

Built on *polpa* "flesh, pulp".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*polpaccio* is built on *polpa* "flesh, pulp", which is inherited from Latin *pulpa* "the fleshy part" (Lewis & Short).
