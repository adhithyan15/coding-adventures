# ص (ṣād) — the letter that opens صباح

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-W11-HA-AND-TA-MARBUTA-01, AR-SCRIPT-C46-SHIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ه and ش once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **صباح الخير** (*ṣabāḥ al-khayr*), "good morning".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C46-SAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ص** — *ṣād*, a heavy *s*.

It opens **صباح الخير** (*ṣabāḥ al-khayr*), "good morning". Say *ṣabāḥ* and feel
the tongue press low and the back of the mouth widen: that heaviness is what the
dot under the romanization (*ṣ*) marks. The letter is a closed **oval** with a
bowl trailing after it.

## Writing: ص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C46-SAD-01] -->

- **1.** start here: close the oval clockwise from its lower-left junction
- **2.** turn left and rise into the short shoulder without lifting
- **3.** lift, restart at the baseline junction, and sweep through the trailing bowl — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Introduction to Arabic: Egyptian Arabic for first-year students, Alphabet: س ش ص ض, independent Saad at 00:01.1–00:03.3 (University of Oregon Libraries Open Textbook, 2023).
