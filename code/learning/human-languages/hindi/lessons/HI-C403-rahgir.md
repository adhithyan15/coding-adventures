## You'll want to know: राहगीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C403-STAIND403-02]; assesses=[] -->

**राहगीर** — *rāhgīr* — "a passer-by".

> एक राहगीर ने हमें रास्ता बताया।
>
> *ek rāhgīr ne hamẽ rāstā batāyā.*
>
> A passer-by showed us the way.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राहगीर** (*rāhgīr*) is usually traced to Persian *rāhgīr*, "one who takes the road", from *rāh*, "road": anyone walking past whom you might ask the way.
