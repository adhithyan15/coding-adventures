# ದಿಬ್ಬಣ (dibbaṇa) — a wedding party

## You'll want to know: ದಿಬ್ಬಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C325-VIVAHA325-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಿಬ್ಬಣ** — *dibbaṇa* — "a wedding party".

> ಗಂಡಿನ ಕಡೆಯ ದಿಬ್ಬಣ ಸಂಜೆ ಊರಿಗೆ ಬಂತು.
>
> *gaṇḍina kaḍeya dibbaṇa sañje ūrige bantu.*
>
> The groom's wedding party reached the village in the evening.

The procession of the groom's family and friends to the wedding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಿಬ್ಬಣ** (*dibbaṇa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3230 records Kannada *dibbaṇa*, "a nuptial procession, the friends and relatives of the bridegroom".
