## You'll want to know: mulgeō, mulgēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C478-OPILIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mulgeō, mulgēre** — "to milk".

> Ancilla mane caprās mulget.
>
> *In the morning the maid milks the goats.*

*mulgeō, mulgēre, mulsī, mulsum* or *mulctum*. English *milk* is a Germanic cousin, and *emulsion* comes from *ēmulgēre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mulgeō* is inherited from PIE *h₂melǵ-* 'to milk' (EtymDB); German *melken* and English *milk* are Germanic cousins (Lewis & Short). English *emulsion* comes from the compound *ēmulgēre*.
