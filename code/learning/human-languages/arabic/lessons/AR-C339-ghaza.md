# غزا (ghazā) — to invade, to raid

## You'll want to know: غزا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C339-GHAZW339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غزا** — *ghazā* — "to invade, to raid".

> غزا الجيش البلاد المجاورة.
>
> *ghazā al-jaysh al-bilād al-mujāwira.*
>
> The army invaded the neighbouring lands.

Present: يغزو, *yaghzū*.

Of space: غزو الفضاء, the conquest of space.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غزا** (*ghazā*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-z-w* (Lane), "to aim at, to seek", and "to go out to fight"; *ghazw* is a raid.
