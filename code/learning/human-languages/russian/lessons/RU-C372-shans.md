## You'll want to know: шанс
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C372-VEROYATNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**шанс** (*shans*) — "a chance".

> У тебя есть шанс получить эту работу.
>
> *U tebyá yest shans poluchít étu rabótu.*
>
> You have a chance of getting this job.

Masculine. *Шансы* — the odds: *у него мало шансов*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**шанс** was borrowed from French *chance* "luck, chance", from Vulgar Latin *cadentia* "a falling", from Latin *cadere* "to fall, to happen" (EtymDB): how the dice fall.
