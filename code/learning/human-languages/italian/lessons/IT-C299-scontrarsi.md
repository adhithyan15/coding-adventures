## You'll want to know: scontrarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C299-SCONTRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**scontrarsi** — "to collide".

> Due auto si sono scontrate all'incrocio.
>
> *Two cars collided at the crossroads.*

With *essere*: *si sono scontrate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scontrarsi* is the verb behind *scontro*, built on *incontrare*, from Latin *in contrā* "against" (EtymDB).
