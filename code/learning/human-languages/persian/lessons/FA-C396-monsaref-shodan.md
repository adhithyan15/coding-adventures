# منصرف شدن (monsaref shodan) — to change one's mind, to give up an idea

## You'll want to know: منصرف شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C396-MONSAREF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**منصرف شدن** (*monsaref shodan*) — "to change one's mind, to give up an idea".

> او از سفر منصرف شد.
>
> *u az safar monsaref shod.*
>
> He decided not to go on the trip.

It takes *az*: *az chizi monsaref shodan*. Stem *shav-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منصرف** is usually traced to Arabic *munṣarif* "turning away"; with the Persian verb *shodan* "to become", you turn away from a plan.
