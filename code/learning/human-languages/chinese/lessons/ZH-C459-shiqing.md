## You'll want to know: 事情
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C459-MIND459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**事情** — *shìqing* — "matter; affair".

> **今天我有很多事情要做** *jīntiān wǒ yǒu hěn duō shìqing yào zuò* — I've got a lot of things to do today.

The new character, in pieces: 情 — eleven strokes in three pieces: on the left the heart piece, a dot, a second dot and a long vertical; at the top right a bar, a second bar, a vertical through them and a long bar; and under them 月 *yuè*, four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**事情** (*shìqing*) is built from 事 'an affair' and 情 'feeling', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzriH* and *dzjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to employ' and 'to set up', and explains the second, after an older book, as what a person desires; in this word the second is said light, and why it joins the first is not settled.
