# ゆっくり — slowly, with a held beat

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-SUKOSHI, JA-SCRIPT-KU-01, JA-SCRIPT-RI-01, JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Read **すこし**, then write known **く**, **り**, and **し**.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-YUKKURI]; assesses=[] -->

> **ゆっくり** — *yu-k-ku-ri* — slowly

Do not squeeze the middle. The small **っ** holds one beat of closure before
*ku*. The spelling is exposure today; this lesson earns the sound and practical
job without pretending the two new shapes are already readable.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-YUKKURI] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *yukkuri* → choose **slowly**]
- [YOU TAP: *yu | hold | ku | ri*]
- [YOU SAY: *yukkuri* slowly, without adding a vowel in the held beat]
