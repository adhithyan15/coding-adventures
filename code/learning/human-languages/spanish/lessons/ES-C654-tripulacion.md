## You'll want to know: la tripulación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C654-AVION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la tripulación** — "the crew".

> La tripulación es muy amable.
>
> *The crew are very kind.*

One member is *un tripulante*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tripulación* is built on *tripular* "to crew a ship", which is usually traced to Latin *interpolāre* "to refurbish, to patch up" (EtymDB).
