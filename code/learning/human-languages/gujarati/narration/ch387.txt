Gujarati, chapter 387: Better and Worse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બહેતર (bahetar) — better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for subject to, then the Gujarati for to give in.

You'll want to know: બહેતર.
બહેતર — bahetar — "better".
જો તમે વહેલા સૂશો તો તમારી તબિયત બહેતર થશે. jo tame vahelā sūśo to tamārī tabiyat bahetar thaśe. If you go to bed early, your health will improve.

The word, taken apart.
બહેતર (bahetar) is usually traced to Persian behtar, "better".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ઉત્તમ.
ઉત્તમ — uttam — "excellent".
જો તમે તાજું શાક વાપરશો તો દાળ ઉત્તમ બનશે. jo tame tājũ śāk vāparśo to dāḷ uttam banśe. If you use fresh vegetables, the dal will turn out excellent.

The word, taken apart.
ઉત્તમ (uttam) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit uttama, "uppermost, highest, best" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ચડિયાતું (caḍiyātũ) — superior.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for better, then the Gujarati for excellent.

You'll want to know: ચડિયાતું.
ચડિયાતું — caḍiyātũ — "superior".
જો તમે રોજ કસરત કરશો તો તમે બીજા ખેલાડીઓ કરતાં ચડિયાતા થશો. jo tame roj kasrat karśo to tame bījā khelāḍīo kartā̃ caḍiyātā thaśo. If you exercise every day, you will be better than the other players.

The word, taken apart.
ચડિયાતું (caḍiyātũ) is built on caḍvũ, "to climb, to rise", the same verb as ચઢવું (caḍhvũ), from a reconstructed caḍhati, "rises" (CDIAL 4578): one who has climbed higher.

Lesson 4 of 5.
સરસ (saras) — lovely; fine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for excellent, then the Gujarati for superior.

You'll want to know: સરસ.
સરસ — saras — "lovely; fine".
જો કાલે તડકો હશે તો ઉજાણીનો સરસ દિવસ હશે. jo kāle taḍko haśe to ujāṇīno saras divas haśe. If it is sunny tomorrow, it will be a lovely day for a picnic.

The word, taken apart.
સરસ (saras) is inherited from Sanskrit sárasa, "juicy; moist, beautiful"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "juicy, excellent" (CDIAL 13255).

Lesson 5 of 5.
કથળવું (kathaḷvũ) — to deteriorate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for superior, then the Gujarati for lovely.

You'll want to know: કથળવું.
કથળવું — kathaḷvũ — "to deteriorate".
જો ખોટ વધશે તો ધંધો કથળશે. jo khoṭ vadhśe to dhandho kathaḷśe. If the losses grow, the business will go downhill.
Past (it …): તે ધંધો કથળ્યો, te dhandho kathaḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
કથળવું (kathaḷvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be put out of order, to be spoilt", under a reconstructed kusthala, "a bad place" (CDIAL 3383).
