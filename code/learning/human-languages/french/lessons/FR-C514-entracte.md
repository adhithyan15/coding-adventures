## You'll want to know: l'entracte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C514-THEATRE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'entracte** — "interval, intermission".

> Pendant l'entracte, nous avons bu un café.
>
> *During the interval we had a coffee.*

Masculine. Literally between the acts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'entracte* is built from *entre* "between", from Latin *inter*, and *acte* "act", a learned borrowing from Latin *āctus*, from *agere* "to do, to drive" (Lewis & Short).
