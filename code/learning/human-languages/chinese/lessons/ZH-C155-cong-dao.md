## Grammar Lens: 从 … 到 …, a start and an end
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-CONG-DAO-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C155-CONG-01, ZH-SCRIPT-DAO-ARRIVE-01, ZH-LEX-C48-CLOCK48-01, ZH-LEX-C46-WEEK46-02, ZH-LEX-C79-SCHOOL79-01] -->

> **从 marks where something starts, 到 where it ends. A time or a place goes after each.**

> **从八点到十点** *cóng bā diǎn dào shí diǎn* — from eight to ten o'clock
> **从这儿到大学** *cóng zhèr dào dàxué* — from here to the university

On a sign, 到 often stands alone between two days or two times, with 从 left out:

> **星期一到星期五** *xīngqīyī dào xīngqīwǔ* — Monday to Friday

In a sentence the whole phrase goes before the verb:

> **我从八点到十点上课** *wǒ cóng bā diǎn dào shí diǎn shàngkè* — I have class from eight to ten.
