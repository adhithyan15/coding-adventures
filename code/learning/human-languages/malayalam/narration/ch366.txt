Malayalam, chapter 366: Clues and Footprints.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സൂചന (sūcana) — a clue; a hint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to arrest, then the Malayalam for to seize.

You'll want to know: സൂചന.
സൂചന — sūcana — "a clue; a hint".
പോലീസിന് കള്ളനെക്കുറിച്ച് ഒരു സൂചന കിട്ടി. pōlīsinŭ kaḷḷanekkuṟiccŭ oru sūcana kiṭṭi. The police got a clue about the thief.
Also a hint or a sign: മഴയുടെ സൂചന (sūcana), a sign of rain.

The word, taken apart.
സൂചന (sūcana) is borrowed from Sanskrit sūcanā, "pointing out, indication, communication" (Monier-Williams).

കള്ളൻ ഭിത്തിയിൽ ഒരു അടയാളം ഇട്ടു. kaḷḷan bhittiyil oru aṭayāḷaṁ iṭṭu. The thief left a mark on the wall.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 3 of 5.
കാൽപ്പാട് (kālppāṭŭ) — a footprint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a clue, then the Malayalam for a mark.

You'll want to know: കാൽപ്പാട്.
കാൽപ്പാട് — kālppāṭŭ — "a footprint".
മണലിൽ കള്ളന്റെ കാൽപ്പാടുകൾ ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു. maṇalil kaḷḷanṟe kālppāṭukaḷ uṇṭāyirunnu. The thief's footprints were in the sand.
Plural: കാൽപ്പാടുകൾ, kālppāṭukaḷ.
കാൽ, foot, + പാട്, a mark, a trace.

The word, taken apart.
കാൽപ്പാട് (kālppāṭŭ) is a native Dravidian compound of കാൽ (kāl), "leg, foot" (DEDR 1479), and പാട്, "a mark, a trace": the mark a foot leaves.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ദുരൂഹമായ (durūhamāya) — mysterious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a footprint, then the Malayalam for a fingerprint.

You'll want to know: ദുരൂഹമായ.
ദുരൂഹമായ — durūhamāya — "mysterious".
ആ കത്ത് വളരെ ദുരൂഹമായിരുന്നു. ā kattŭ vaḷare durūhamāyirunnu. That letter was very mysterious.
The noun is ദുരൂഹത, a mystery.

The word, taken apart.
ദുരൂഹമായ (durūhamāya) is built from Sanskrit durūha, "hard to infer or understand" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam adjective ending -ആയ: a mysterious thing is one nobody can work out.
