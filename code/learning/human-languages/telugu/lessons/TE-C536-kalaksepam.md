## You'll want to know: కాలక్షేపం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C536-VIRAMAM536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కాలక్షేపం** — *kālakṣēpaṁ* — "a pastime".

> తాతయ్యకు పుస్తకాలు చదవడమే కాలక్షేపం.
>
> *tātayyaku pustakālu cadavaḍamē kālakṣēpaṁ.*
>
> Reading books is grandpa's pastime.

Literally throwing time: passing the time pleasantly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కాలక్షేపం** (*kālakṣēpaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *kāla*, "time" (CDIAL 3084), and *kṣēpa*, "sending, throwing" (CDIAL 3738): sending the time on its way.
