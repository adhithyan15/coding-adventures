# সত্ত্বেও (sôttveo) — in spite of; despite

## You'll want to know: সত্ত্বেও
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C369-KINA369-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সত্ত্বেও** — *sôttveo* — "in spite of; despite".

> শীত সত্ত্বেও খেলা হবে, কিন্তু ঝড় হলে বন্ধ হবে।
>
> *shīt sôttveo khælā hôbe, kintu jhôṛ hôle bôndhô hôbe.*
>
> The match will go ahead despite the cold, but if there is a storm it will be called off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সত্ত্বেও** (*sôttveo*) is Sanskrit *sattva*, being or existence (Monier-Williams), with the Bengali endings -এ and -ও: 'even with its being so'. The sense 'in spite of' is not one the dictionary gives; the word follows its noun: শীত সত্ত্বেও.
