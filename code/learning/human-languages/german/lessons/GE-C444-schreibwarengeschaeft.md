## You'll want to know: das Schreibwarengeschäft
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C444-BAUMARKT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**das Schreibwarengeschäft** — "the stationer, the stationery shop".

> Im Schreibwarengeschäft kauft das Kind neue Hefte.
>
> *At the stationer the child buys new exercise books.*

Plural: *die Schreibwarengeschäfte*. *Die Schreibwaren* — paper, pens and so on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schreibwarengeschäft* is built from *schreiben* 'to write', an early borrowing from Latin *scrībere* (Lewis & Short), *Ware* 'goods', from Middle Low German *ware* (EtymDB), and *Geschäft* 'shop'.
