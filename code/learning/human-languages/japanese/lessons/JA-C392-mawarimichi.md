## You'll want to know: まわりみち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C392-YORIMICHI392-02]; assesses=[] -->

**まわりみち** — *mawarimichi* — "a detour; a roundabout way".

> **じゅうたいで まわりみちを しました。** — *jūtai de mawarimichi o shimashita* — "Because of the traffic jam we took a detour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まわりみち** (*mawarimichi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *mawari*, "going round" (from *mawaru*) and *michi*, "road"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
