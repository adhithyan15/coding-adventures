Lesson 1 of 5.
der Nachteil — a disadvantage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for caution, then the German for an advantage.

You'll want to know: der Nachteil.
der Nachteil — "a disadvantage".

The word, taken apart.
Nachteil is the word for "after" + the word for "part", Old High German teil from Proto-Germanic dailą (EtymDB 2.1): the share that comes last.
