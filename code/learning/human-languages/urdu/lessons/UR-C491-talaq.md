## You'll want to know: طلاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C491-MENDING491-04]; assesses=[] -->

**طلاق** — *talāq* — "a divorce".

> ان کی طلاق دو سال پہلے ہوئی۔
>
> *un kī talāq do sāl pehle huī.*
>
> They were divorced two years ago.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طلاق** (*talāq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṭalāq*, "release, divorce".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
