# બાદબાકી (bādbākī) — subtraction

## You'll want to know: બાદબાકી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C426-LAKH426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બાદબાકી** — *bādbākī* — "subtraction".

> બાળકો શાળામાં બાદબાકી શીખે છે.
>
> *bāḷko śāḷāmā̃ bādbākī śīkhe che.*
>
> Children learn subtraction at school.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બાદબાકી** (*bādbākī*) is built from બાદ, "after, taken away", usually traced to Arabic *baʿd*, "after", and બાકી, "what remains", usually traced to Arabic *bāqī*: you take away and look at what is left.
