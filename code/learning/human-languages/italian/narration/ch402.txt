Italian, chapter 402: In rete.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la rete — the network; the internet; the net.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to paste, then the Italian for the backup.

You'll want to know: la rete.
la rete — "the network; the internet; the net".
Ho trovato la ricetta in rete. I found the recipe online.
In rete — online. In football, la rete is the goal net, and a goal.

The word, taken apart.
rete is inherited from Latin rēte "a net" (Lewis & Short): a fishing net, then any network of threads, roads or computers.

What you've built.

The word, taken apart.
connessione is a learned borrowing from Latin cōnexiō, cōnexiōnis "a binding together" (EtymDB), from cōnectere "to tie together", built on nectere "to tie".

connettersi — "to connect, to go online".
Se ti connetti al wifi dell'albergo, non paghi. If you connect to the hotel wifi, you do not pay.
Connettersi a — to connect to. Past participle connesso.

The word, taken apart.
connettersi comes from Latin cōnectere "to tie together" (Lewis & Short), built from con- "together" and nectere "to tie".

Lesson 5 of 6.
il link — the link.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to connect, then the Italian for to browse.

You'll want to know: il link.
il link — "the link".
Ti mando il link: clicca e guarda. I will send you the link: click and have a look.
Masculine, invariable: i link. Also il collegamento.

The word, taken apart.
link was borrowed from English link "a ring of a chain" (EtymDB), a word usually traced to Old Norse.
