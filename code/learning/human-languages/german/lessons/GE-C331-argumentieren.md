## You'll want to know: argumentieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C331-UEBERZEUGEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**argumentieren** — "to argue (a case), to give arguments".

> Sie argumentiert gut, aber ich bleibe bei meiner Meinung.
>
> *She argues well, but I am sticking to my opinion.*

It means to reason, not to quarrel; a quarrel is *der Streit*. Perfekt: *hat argumentiert*, with no *ge-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*argumentieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *argūmentārī* "to bring forward proof, to prove" (Lewis & Short), from *argūmentum* "proof", with the German verb ending *-ieren*.
