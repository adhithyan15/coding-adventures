Tamil, chapter 139: Cumin, Leafy greens, Jackfruit, Guava, Lemon.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
சீரகம் (cīrakam) — cumin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for pepper, then the Tamil for mustard seed.

You'll want to know: சீரகம்.
சீரகம் — cīrakam — "cumin".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does சீரகம் (cīrakam) mean? ("Cumin".) Say it once more.

A curtain? (திரைச்சீலை, tiraiccīlai.) A mattress? (மெத்தை, mettai.) A television? (தொலைக்காட்சி, tolaikkāṭci.) A mobile phone? (கைப்பேசி, kaippēci.) A computer? (கணினி, kaṇiṉi.)
