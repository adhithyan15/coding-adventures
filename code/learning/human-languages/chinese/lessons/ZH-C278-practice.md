## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-SEQUENCE-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-SEQUENCE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-YIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C278-JIEZHE-01, ZH-LEX-C278-ZUIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-NE-01, ZH-LEX-C69-MEALS69-02, ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-05, ZH-LEX-C255-PARTTIME255-01] -->

> **A:** 你早上做什么
>
> *nǐ zǎoshang zuò shénme* — What do you do in the morning?
>
> **B:** 我先洗手 然后吃早饭
>
> *wǒ xiān xǐ shǒu, ránhòu chī zǎofàn* — I wash my hands first, then have breakfast.
>
> **A:** 吃了早饭以后呢
>
> *chī le zǎofàn yǐhòu ne* — And after breakfast?
>
> **B:** *jiēzhe qù xuéxiào* — Straight off to school.
>
> **A:** 下了课以后呢
>
> *xià le kè yǐhòu ne* — And after class?
>
> **B:** 先去打工 最后回家
>
> *xiān qù dǎgōng, zuìhòu huí jiā* — First my part-time job, and last home.

The order of the clauses is the order of the day. B never says 我 after the first line: the person is clear, so Mandarin leaves it out.

## What you've built — telling it in order
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-SEQUENCE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-ZUI-01, ZH-SCRIPT-RAN-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-SEQUENCE-01, ZH-LEX-C278-ZUIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C278-SHOUXIAN-01] -->

Five ways to keep a story in order, all at the front of a clause or right before the verb:

- **先** *xiān* — first, before the verb.
- *shǒuxiān* — first of all, at the very front.
- **然后** *ránhòu* and *jiēzhe* — then, and straight after.
- **最后** *zuìhòu* — last.
- **吃了饭以后** *chī le fàn yǐhòu* — after doing something.

**Listen.** *Nǐ zuótiān xiān zuò le shénme?*

**Speak.** Tell your morning in four steps: 先, 然后, *jiēzhe*, 最后.

[YOU WRITE: 最后 — 日 first, then the six strokes, then the piece from the left of 对]
