# ফাঁকি দেওয়া (phā̃ki deoyā) — to shirk; to dodge (work)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C462-OLOS462-02, BN-LEX-C462-OLOS462-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for industrious, then the Bengali for in disorder.

## You'll want to know: ফাঁকি দেওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C462-OLOS462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ফাঁকি দেওয়া** — *phā̃ki deoyā* — "to shirk; to dodge (work)".

> অলস কর্মচারীটা কাজে ফাঁকি দিত।
>
> *ôlôs kôrmôchārīṭā kāje phā̃ki ditô.*
>
> The lazy employee used to shirk his work.

Past (he/she …): তিনি কাজে ফাঁকি দিলেন, *tini kāje phā̃ki dilen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফাঁকি দেওয়া** (*phā̃ki deoyā*) is *phā̃ki*, "a trick, a dodge", which CDIAL lists for Bengali (CDIAL 9035), with *deoyā*, "to give" (CDIAL 6141).
