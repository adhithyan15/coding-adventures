## You'll want to know: दिखवाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C488-JANCH488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दिखवाना** — *dikhvānā* — "to have (something) looked at".

> अरुण ने अपनी रिपोर्ट डाक्टर को दिखवाई।
>
> *Aruṇ ne apnī riporṭ ḍākṭar ko dikhvāī.*
>
> Arun had the doctor look at his report.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने आँख दिखवाई, *usne ā̃kh dikhvāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिखवाना** (*dikhvānā*) is the second causative of *dekhnā*, "to see", which CDIAL lists under *\*dṛkṣati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6507): *dikhānā* is "to show", and *dikhvānā* is "to have something seen to", usually by a doctor.
