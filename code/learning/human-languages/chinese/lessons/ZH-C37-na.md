## You'll want to know: 那
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C37-THIS37-03]; assesses=[] -->

**那** — *nà* — "that".

> **那是我的家** *nà shì wǒ de jiā* — That's my home.

The new character, in pieces: 那 — a four-stroke piece on the left, and on the right a two-stroke piece shaped like an ear. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**那** (*nà*) is an old character: the Guangyun records it as *na* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'how, what'. The sense 'that' is not given there, so how it came to mean that is not settled.
