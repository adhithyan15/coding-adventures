## You'll want to know: ओस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C222-COLD222-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ओस** — *os* — "dew".

> दिनूगै खेत मांय ओस है।
>
> *dinūgai khet mā̃y os hai.*
>
> Early in the morning there's dew on the field.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओस** (*os*) is inherited from Sanskrit *avaśyā*, "hoar-frost"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *os*, "dew". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 855).
