6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
اسٹیج (isṭej) — a stage (in a theatre).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to engrave, then the Urdu for an idol.

You'll want to know: اسٹیج.
اسٹیج — isṭej — "a stage (in a theatre)".
اداکار اسٹیج پر آیا۔ adākār isṭej par āyā. The actor came onto the stage.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اسٹیج (isṭej) is borrowed from English stage.

Lesson 3 of 6.
شائقین (shāiqīn) — fans; keen spectators.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a stage (in a theatre), then the Urdu for a clap.

You'll want to know: شائقین.
شائقین — shāiqīn — "fans; keen spectators".
شائقین اداکاروں کو دیکھ کر ہنسے۔ shāiqīn adākāroṅ ko dekh kar hãse. The audience laughed as they watched the actors.
Masculine, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
شائقین (shāiqīn) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic shāʾiqīn, the plural of shāʾiq, "eager, longing": the people keen on a game or a show.

Lesson 5 of 6.
لبھانا (lubhānā) — to charm; to entice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fans of a game or show, then the Urdu for a dialogue.

You'll want to know: لبھانا.
لبھانا — lubhānā — "to charm; to entice".
اس کے گیت نے سب کو لبھایا۔ us ke gīt ne sab ko lubhāyā. His song charmed everyone.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سب کو لبھایا, us ne sab ko lubhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
لبھانا (lubhānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lubhānā, "to excite desire", under Sanskrit lubhyati, "desires" (CDIAL 11086).
