## You'll want to know: مصطلح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C396-MUSTALAH396-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مصطلح** — *muṣṭalaḥ* — "a term".

> هذا مصطلح علمي جديد.
>
> *hādhā muṣṭalaḥ ʿilmī jadīd.*
>
> This is a new scientific term.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصطلح** (*muṣṭalaḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-l-ḥ*, "to be good, right" (Lane). *Muṣṭalaḥ* is the Form VIII passive participle, what people have agreed on: a term.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
