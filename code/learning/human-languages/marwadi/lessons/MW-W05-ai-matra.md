# ै — turn *ha* into *hai*

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-HAI-01, MW-SCRIPT-HA-01, MW-COURTESY-AABHAAR-FORMAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Say **आभार** for formal thanks. Say the closing word *hai*,
then write known **ह** once.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-AI-MATRA-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HA-01] -->

> ह + ै → है

Attached **ै** replaces the built-in *a*. Read the whole word **है** as
*hai*. Do not confuse **ै** with the taller **ो** in **रो**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AI-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **है** visible and copy it once. Point to **ह**, then **ै**, and read *hai*.
