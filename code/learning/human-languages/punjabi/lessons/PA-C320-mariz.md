## You'll want to know: ਮਰੀਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C320-CLINIC320-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਰੀਜ਼** — *marīz* — "a patient".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਹਸਪਤਾਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਰੀਜ਼ ਸਨ।
>
> *kall haspatāl vich bahut marīz san.*
>
> There were a lot of patients in the hospital yesterday.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਰੀਜ਼** (*marīz*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *marīḍ*, "sick".
