## You'll want to know: শতাংশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C356-SHOTANGSHO356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**শতাংশ** — *shôtāngshô* — "a percentage; per cent".

> ছাত্রদের পঞ্চাশ শতাংশ পাস করল।
>
> *chhātrôder pônchāsh shôtāngshô pās kôrlô.*
>
> Fifty per cent of the students passed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শতাংশ** (*shôtāngshô*) is Sanskrit *śata*, a hundred, with *aṃśa*, a share (Monier-Williams): a share out of a hundred.
