## You'll want to know: ریه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C495-ORGANS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ریه** (*riye*) — "a lung".

> سیگار به ریه آسیب میزند.
>
> *sigâr be riye âsib mizanad.*
>
> Smoking harms the lungs.

Plural *riyehâ*. In everyday speech also *shosh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*riye* was borrowed from Arabic *riʾa*, 'lung' (EtymDB).
