## You'll want to know: 开会
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C157-KAIHUI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**开会** — *kāihuì* — "to have a meeting".

> **明天下午开会** *míngtiān xiàwǔ kāihuì* — There's a meeting tomorrow afternoon.

> **我们在开会** *wǒmen zài kāihuì* — We're in a meeting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开会** (*kāihuì*) is built from 开 'to open' and 会 'a gathering'. 会 is the character of 会 'can' and 会 'will', inherited from Middle Chinese *hwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to come together': here it keeps that sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C155-KAIMEN-01, ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-02, ZH-GRAMMAR-ZAI-PROGRESSIVE-01] -->

开 starts many things you know: 开门, 开车, and now 开会.

在 before it says the meeting is going on: 我们在开会.
