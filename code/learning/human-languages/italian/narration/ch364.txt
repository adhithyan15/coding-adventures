Italian, chapter 364: In piazza.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la protesta — the protest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the freedom, then the Italian for the equality.

You'll want to know: la protesta.
la protesta — "the protest".
La protesta degli studenti è durata tre giorni. The students' protest lasted three days.
Per protesta — as a protest.

The word, taken apart.
protesta is built on protestare, a learned borrowing from Latin prōtestārī "to declare in public" (Lewis & Short), built on testis "a witness".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la manifestazione.
la manifestazione — "the demonstration, the march; the event".
Sabato c'è una manifestazione per la pace. On Saturday there is a march for peace.
It can also be any public event: una manifestazione sportiva.

The word, taken apart.
manifestazione is a learned borrowing from Late Latin manifestātiō "a making plain" (Lewis & Short), from manifestāre, built on manifestus "evident, plain" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
lo striscione — the banner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the demonstration, then the Italian for to demonstrate.

You'll want to know: lo striscione.
lo striscione — "the banner".
Sullo striscione c'era scritto: più scuole, meno traffico. The banner said: more schools, less traffic.
Big and long, held by two people or hung from a balcony; also at the stadium.

The word, taken apart.
striscione is a big striscia "strip, stripe", with the ending -one "big". EtymDB links striscia to Latin stria "a furrow, a groove" (EtymDB), but other dictionaries treat its origin as not settled.
