Hindi, chapter 326: Ruins of the Past.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खंडहर (khaṇḍhar) — ruins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a sacrifice, then the Hindi for to entrust.

You'll want to know: खंडहर.
खंडहर — khaṇḍhar — "ruins".
खंडहर के लिए सीधे जाइए और मंदिर के बाद दाएँ मुड़िए। khaṇḍhar ke liye sīdhe jāie aur mandir ke bād dāẽ muṛiye. For the ruins, go straight on and turn right after the temple.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
खंडहर (khaṇḍhar) is probably khaṇḍ, broken, from Sanskrit khaṇḍa, "a piece, a fragment" (Monier-Williams), with घर, a house, worn down: a house in pieces.

Lesson 2 of 5.
खोदना (khodnā) — to dig.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to entrust, then the Hindi for ruins.

You'll want to know: खोदना.
खोदना — khodnā — "to dig".
मज़दूरों ने खंडहर के पास मिट्टी खोदी। mazdūroṁ ne khaṇḍhar ke pās miṭṭī khodī. The labourers dug up the earth near the ruins.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कुआँ खोदा, usne kuā̃ khodā.

The word, taken apart.
खोदना (khodnā) is inherited from khōdd, a form CDIAL reconstructs for digging (CDIAL 3934). What is dug agrees: मिट्टी खोदी, कुआँ खोदा.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for ruins, then the Hindi for to dig.

You'll want to know: मूर्ति.
मूर्ति — mūrti — "a statue; an image".
मज़दूरों को मिट्टी में एक पत्थर की मूर्ति मिली। mazdūroṁ ko miṭṭī meṁ ek patthar kī mūrti milī. The labourers found a stone statue in the earth.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
मूर्ति (mūrti) is Sanskrit mūrti, "a solid body, a form" (Monier-Williams): a figure of stone, metal or clay, and in a temple the image of a god.

Lesson 4 of 5.
शिलालेख (śilālekh) — an inscription (on stone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to dig, then the Hindi for a statue.

You'll want to know: शिलालेख.
शिलालेख — śilālekh — "an inscription (on stone)".
उस शिलालेख पर एक राजा का नाम है। us śilālekh par ek rājā kā nām hai. That inscription bears a king's name.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
शिलालेख (śilālekh) joins Sanskrit śilā, "a stone", and lekha, "a writing" (Monier-Williams): words cut into rock, one of the oldest sources of history.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अवशेष (avśeṣ) — remains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a statue, then the Hindi for an inscription (on stone).

You'll want to know: अवशेष.
अवशेष — avśeṣ — "remains".
खंडहर में एक पुराने मंदिर के अवशेष भी हैं। khaṇḍhar meṁ ek purāne mandir ke avśeṣ bhī haiṁ. Among the ruins are the remains of an old temple too.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अवशेष (avśeṣ) is Sanskrit avaśeṣa, "a remainder, what is left" (Monier-Williams). It is mostly used in the plural sense: what is left of a building or a body.
