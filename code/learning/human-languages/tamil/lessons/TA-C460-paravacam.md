# பரவசம் (paravacam) — ecstasy; rapture

## You'll want to know: பரவசம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C460-VIYAPPU460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**பரவசம்** — *paravacam* — "ecstasy; rapture".

> இசையைக் கேட்டு அவர் பரவசம் அடைந்தார்.
>
> *icaiyaik kēṭṭu avar paravacam aṭaintār.*
>
> Listening to the music, he was enraptured.

பரவசம் அடை, to be carried away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பரவசம்** (*paravacam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paravaśa*, "under another's power" (Monier-Williams): in Tamil, carried away by feeling.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
