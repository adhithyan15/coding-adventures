Marathi, chapter 145: Cheek, Tongue, Chin, Elbow, Wrist.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गाल (gāl) — a cheek.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a mosquito, then the Marathi for the forehead.

You'll want to know: गाल.
गाल — gāl — "a cheek".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gāl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gāl, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say gāl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a mosquito, then the Marathi for the forehead, then say gāl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does गाल (gāl) mean? ("A cheek".) Say it once more.
