# HL89 — Punjabi A2 plural/polite want prerequisite

Issue: #17992

The Punjabi A2 mock uses `ਚਾਹੁੰਦੇ` five times across the learner paper and
confidential examiner master. The infinitive `ਚਾਹੁਣਾ` already has a direct
owner, and the auxiliary frames `ਹਨ` and `ਹੋ` are familiar, but the shared
plural/respectful form `ਚਾਹੁੰਦੇ` has no instructional owner.

Chapter 189 closes that narrow dependency gap without editing either mock. The
ramp retrieves familiar `ਚਾਹੁਣਾ` and the already-taught plural/respectful
habitual pattern, directly teaches `ਚਾਹੁੰਦੇ`, and gives the form an immediate
retrieval step. It then builds the third-person plural frame `ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਨਾ
ਚਾਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ`, retrieves it, builds the second-person plural/polite question
`ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੀ ਕਰਨਾ ਚਾਹੁੰਦੇ ਹੋ`, contrasts the two frames, and finishes with a
four-skill payoff.

`ਚਾਹੁੰਦੇ` has one direct owner and at least two later retrieval lessons. All
supporting Punjabi must occur before the chapter, and the target must not leak
into authored or generated material before its owner. The broader perfective
family and unrelated high-frequency mock words remain out of scope. This is a
prerequisite tranche, not mock-readiness or attainment evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
