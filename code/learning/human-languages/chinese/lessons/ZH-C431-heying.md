## You'll want to know: héyǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C431-OUTING431-05]; assesses=[] -->

*héyǐng* — "to have a group photo taken; to pose for a group photo".

> *wǒmen zài jiǔdiàn ménkǒu héyǐng* — We're having a group photo taken outside the hotel.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*héyǐng* is built from *hé* 'together' and *yǐng* 'a likeness', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hop* and *'jaengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to join' and the second as a shape and its shadow; the sense 'a photograph' is modern.
