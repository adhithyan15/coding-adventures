## You'll want to know: bàomíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C268-EXAM268-01]; assesses=[] -->

*bàomíng* — "to sign up; to enter (one's name)".

> *nǐ bàomíng le ma* — Have you signed up?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàomíng* is built from *bào* 'to report' and 名 'a name', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawH* and *mjieng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to report'.
