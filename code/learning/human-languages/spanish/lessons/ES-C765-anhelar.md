## You'll want to know: anhelar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C765-ILUSION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**anhelar** — "to yearn for, to long for".

> Anhela una vida tranquila.
>
> *She longs for a quiet life.*

Literary; in conversation people say *desear mucho*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anhelar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *anhēlāre* "to pant, to gasp" (EtymDB); to long for something is to be out of breath for it.
