Telugu, chapter 456: Strolling Around.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
షికారు (ṣikāru) — a jaunt; an outing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for entry, then the Telugu for a pleasure trip.

You'll want to know: షికారు.
షికారు — ṣikāru — "a jaunt; an outing".
సాయంత్రం మేము సముద్రం దగ్గర షికారుకు వెళ్ళాం. sāyantraṁ mēmu samudraṁ daggara ṣikāruku veḷḷāṁ. In the evening we went for a stroll by the sea.
షికారుకు వెళ్ళు, to go for an outing. An outing with no aim but pleasure.

The word, taken apart.
షికారు (ṣikāru) is a loan from Persian shikār, "hunting, the chase", which reached Telugu through Hindustani; a hunting trip became any trip for pleasure.

Lesson 2 of 5.
విహరించు (vihariñcu) — to stroll; to roam for pleasure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a pleasure trip, then the Telugu for a jaunt.

You'll want to know: విహరించు.
విహరించు — vihariñcu — "to stroll; to roam for pleasure".
పర్యాటకులు తోటలో హాయిగా విహరించారు. paryāṭakulu tōṭalō hāyigā vihariñcāru. The tourists strolled happily in the garden.
Past (I …): విహరించాను, vihariñcānu.
A rather literary verb; విహారయాత్ర, an excursion, holds the same root.

The word, taken apart.
విహరించు (vihariñcu) is built from Sanskrit vihāra, "roaming, pleasure" (CDIAL 12033), from vi-hṛ, "to roam about", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a jaunt, then the Telugu for to stroll.

You'll want to know: దృశ్యం.
దృశ్యం — dṛśyaṁ — "a view; a scene".
కొండ మీద నుంచి దృశ్యం చాలా అందంగా ఉంది. koṇḍa mīda nuñci dṛśyaṁ cālā andaṅgā undi. The view from the top of the hill is very beautiful.
A view of nature, and also a scene in a film or a play.

The word, taken apart.
దృశ్యం (dṛśyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit dṛśya, "visible, what is to be seen" (Monier-Williams), from dṛś, "to see".

Lesson 4 of 5.
సంధ్య (sandhya) — dusk; twilight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to stroll, then the Telugu for a view.

You'll want to know: సంధ్య.
సంధ్య — sandhya — "dusk; twilight".
సంధ్య వేళ ఆకాశం ఎర్రగా ఉంది. sandhya vēḷa ākāśaṁ erragā undi. At dusk the sky is red.
సంధ్య (sandhya) వేళ, at dusk. The word also names the meeting of day and night at dawn.

The word, taken apart.
సంధ్య (sandhya) is borrowed from Sanskrit sandhyā, "a joining, the twilight" where day and night meet (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
మజిలీ (majilī) — a stopover; a halt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a view, then the Telugu for dusk.

You'll want to know: మజిలీ.
మజిలీ — majilī — "a stopover; a halt".
రెండు రోజుల ప్రయాణంలో మొదటి మజిలీ ఒక చిన్న పట్టణం. reṇḍu rōjula prayāṇaṁlō modaṭi majilī oka cinna paṭṭaṇaṁ. On the two-day journey the first stopover was a small town.
A stop on a long journey. మజిలీ (majilī) కథలు are stories told at each halt of a journey.

The word, taken apart.
మజిలీ (majilī) is usually traced to Arabic manzil, "a place where travellers halt", which came into Telugu through Persian and Hindustani.
