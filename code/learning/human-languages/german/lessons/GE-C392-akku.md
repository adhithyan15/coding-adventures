## You'll want to know: der Akku
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C392-GERAET-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Akku** — "the rechargeable battery".

> Mein Akku ist fast leer.
>
> *My battery is almost flat.*

Plural: *die Akkus*. Short for *der Akkumulator*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Akku* is short for *Akkumulator*, a learned formation on Latin *accumulāre* "to heap up, to accumulate" (Lewis & Short), built on *cumulus* "a heap": it stores up electricity.
