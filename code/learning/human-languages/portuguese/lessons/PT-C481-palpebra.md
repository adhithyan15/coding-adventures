## You'll want to know: a pálpebra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C481-ROSTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a pálpebra** — "eyelid".

> Com sono, as pálpebras ficam pesadas.
>
> *When you are sleepy, your eyelids get heavy.*

A learned word; in everyday speech people talk about *os olhos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pálpebra* is a learned borrowing from Latin *palpebra* "eyelid" (EtymDB), related to *palpāre* "to stroke gently".
