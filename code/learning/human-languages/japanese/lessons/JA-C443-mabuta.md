## You'll want to know: まぶた
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C443-WRIST443-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まぶた** — *mabuta* — "an eyelid".

> **ねむいので、まぶたが おもいです。** — *nemui node, mabuta ga omoi desu* — "I am sleepy, so my eyelids are heavy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まぶた** (*mabuta*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
