# হইচই (hôichôi) — an uproar; a commotion

## You'll want to know: হইচই
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C298-UTTEJONA298-03]; assesses=[] -->

**হইচই** — *hôichôi* — "an uproar; a commotion".

> খবরটা শুনে পাড়ায় হইচই পড়ে গেল।
>
> *khôbôrṭā shune pāṛāy hôichôi pôṛe gelô.*
>
> When the news came, the neighbourhood was in an uproar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হইচই** (*hôichôi*) is an expressive word, like English *hubbub*: its sound is the noise. হইচই পড়ে যাওয়া, an uproar breaks out. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
