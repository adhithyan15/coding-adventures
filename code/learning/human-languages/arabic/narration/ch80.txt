Arabic, chapter 80: Tiger, Elephant, Monkey, Snake, Bear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نمر (namir) — a tiger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a mouse, then the Arabic for a lion.

You'll want to know: نمر.
نمر — namir — "a tiger".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: namir]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: namir, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say namir]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a mouse, then the Arabic for a lion, then say namir again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does نمر (namir) mean? ("A tiger".) Say it once more.
