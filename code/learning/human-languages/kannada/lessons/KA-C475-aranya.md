## You'll want to know: ಅರಣ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C475-ARANYA475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅರಣ್ಯ** — *araṇya* — "woodland; a forest".

> ಅರಣ್ಯ ಇಲಾಖೆ ಹೊಸ ಮರಗಳನ್ನು ನೆಟ್ಟಿತು.
>
> *araṇya ilākhe hosa maragaḷannu neṭṭitu.*
>
> The forest department planted new trees.

A formal word; ಕಾಡು is the everyday forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅರಣ್ಯ** (*araṇya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *araṇya*, "foreign land, wilderness, a forest" (CDIAL 600).
