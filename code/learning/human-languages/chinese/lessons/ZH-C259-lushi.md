## You'll want to know: lǜshī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C259-JOB259-03]; assesses=[] -->

*lǜshī* — "lawyer".

> *wǒ jiějie shì lǜshī* — My sister is a lawyer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǜshī* is built from *lǜ* 'the law' and *shī* 'a master', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lwit* and *srij* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the law' and the second 'a model'.
