## You'll want to know: बटण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C177-PHAD177-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बटण** — *baṭaṇ* — "a button".

> म्हारी कमीज माथै नुवो बटण है।
>
> *mhārī kamīj māthai nuvo baṭaṇ hai.*
>
> There's a new button on my shirt.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बटण** (*baṭaṇ*) is borrowed from English *button*.
