## You'll want to know: মূল্যবোধ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C350-ADORSHO350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**মূল্যবোধ** — *mūlyôbodh* — "moral values".

> পরিবার থেকেই আমরা মূল্যবোধ শিখি।
>
> *pôribār thekei āmrā mūlyôbodh shikhi.*
>
> We learn our values from our families.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মূল্যবোধ** (*mūlyôbodh*) is a modern Bengali compound of Sanskrit *mūlya*, value or price, and *bodha*, understanding (Monier-Williams): a sense of what things are worth.
