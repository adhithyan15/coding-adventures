Gujarati, chapter 319: The Old Fort.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કિલ્લો (killo) — a fort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a royal court, then the Gujarati for a throne.

You'll want to know: કિલ્લો.
કિલ્લો — killo — "a fort".
ટેકરી પર એક જૂનો કિલ્લો છે. ṭekrī par ek jūno killo che. There is an old fort on the hill.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કિલ્લો (killo) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qalʿa, "a fortress". It is masculine: જૂનો કિલ્લો, an old fort.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ખંડેર (khanḍer) — ruins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a throne, then the Gujarati for a fort.

You'll want to know: ખંડેર.
ખંડેર — khanḍer — "ruins".
આજે ત્યાં ફક્ત ખંડેર છે. āje tyā̃ phakt khanḍer che. Today only ruins remain there.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ખંડેર (khanḍer) probably goes back to khaṇḍitaghara, "a broken house", a form CDIAL reconstructs, where it lists Gujarati khãḍiyer, "ruins" (CDIAL 3798). It is what is left when walls and roofs have fallen.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a fort, then the Gujarati for ruins.

You'll want to know: સ્મારક.
સ્મારક — smārak — "a memorial".
ગામના લોકોએ ત્યાં એક સ્મારક બનાવ્યું. gāmnā lokoe tyā̃ ek smārak banāvyũ. The villagers built a memorial there.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સ્મારક (smārak) is a tatsama, Sanskrit smāraka, "recalling, reminding of" (Monier-Williams): a thing put up so that people remember.

Lesson 4 of 5.
મૂર્તિ (mūrti) — a statue; an idol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for ruins, then the Gujarati for a memorial.

You'll want to know: મૂર્તિ.
મૂર્તિ — mūrti — "a statue; an idol".
કિલ્લામાં પથ્થરની એક સુંદર મૂર્તિ છે. killāmā̃ paththarnī ek sundar mūrti che. There is a beautiful stone statue in the fort.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મૂર્તિ (mūrti) is a tatsama, Sanskrit mūrti, "a solid body, a form, an image" (Monier-Williams). It is a statue of a person, and in a temple the image of a god.

Lesson 5 of 5.
વસવું (vasvũ) — to settle (in a place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a memorial, then the Gujarati for a statue.

You'll want to know: વસવું.
વસવું — vasvũ — "to settle (in a place)".
ઘણાં વરસ પહેલાં લોકો આ નદી કિનારે વસ્યા. ghaṇā̃ varas pahelā̃ loko ā nadī kināre vasyā. Many years ago people settled on the bank of this river.
Past (he …): તે અહીં વસ્યો, te ahī̃ vasyo.

The word, taken apart.
વસવું (vasvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit vásati, "dwells", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "settles" (CDIAL 11435). People વસે છે where they make their homes; રહેવું is everyday living.
