## You'll want to know: 古代
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C323-ERA323-04]; assesses=[] -->

**古代** — *gǔdài* — "ancient times".

> **我喜欢看古代的小说** *wǒ xǐhuan kàn gǔdài de xiǎoshuō* — I like reading novels from ancient times.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**古代** (*gǔdài*) is built from 古 'ancient' and 代 'an age', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *dojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'former' and the second 'to take turns'.
