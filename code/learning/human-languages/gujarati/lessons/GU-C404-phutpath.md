## You'll want to know: ફૂટપાથ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C404-PATH404-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂટપાથ** — *phūṭpāth* — "a pavement; a footpath (beside a road)".

> બાળકોએ ફૂટપાથ પર ચાલવું જોઈએ.
>
> *bāḷkoe phūṭpāth par cālvũ joīe.*
>
> Children should walk on the pavement.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂટપાથ** (*phūṭpāth*) is borrowed from English *footpath*, the raised walk beside a city road.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
