Lesson 1 of 6.
খসড়া (khôsṛā) — a draft (a first version).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to stall, then the Bengali for to settle on.

You'll want to know: খসড়া.
খসড়া — khôsṛā — "a draft (a first version)".
আমি পরিকল্পনার একটা খসড়া বানালাম। Āmi pôrikôlpônār ekṭā khôsṛā bānālām. I made a draft of the plan.

The word, taken apart.
খসড়া (khôsṛā) is usually traced to an Arabic word khasra, a rough record, which came through Persian.

আমি ফাইলগুলো তারিখের ক্রমে সাজালাম। Āmi phāilgulo tārikher krôme sājālām. I arranged the files in order of date.

ক্রম (krôm) is a tatsama, Sanskrit krama, "a step", and from that "regular order, succession" (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a list, then the Bengali for a sequence.

You'll want to know: রূপরেখা.
রূপরেখা — rūprekhā — "an outline".
ম্যানেজার নতুন কাজের একটা রূপরেখা দিলেন। Mænejār nôtun kājer ekṭā rūprekhā dilen. The manager gave an outline of the new work.

The word, taken apart.
রূপরেখা (rūprekhā) joins Sanskrit rūpa, "form, shape", and rekhā, "a line, a stroke" (Monier-Williams): the lines of a shape before it is filled in.

Lesson 5 of 6.
বাদ দেওয়া (bād deoyā) — to leave out; to drop (from a list).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a sequence, then the Bengali for an outline.

You'll want to know: বাদ দেওয়া.
বাদ দেওয়া — bād deoyā — "to leave out; to drop (from a list)".
এই নামটা বাদ দিলে তালিকাটা ছোট হবে। Ei nāmṭā bād dile tālikāṭā chhoṭo hôbe. If you drop this name, the list will be shorter.
Past (he/she …): তিনি পুরনো খরচটা বাদ দিলেন, tini purôno khôrôchṭā bād dilen.

The word, taken apart.
বাদ দেওয়া (bād deoyā) joins বাদ, "omission, leaving aside", usually traced to Arabic baʿd, "after", through Persian, to দেওয়া, "to give"; the sense "leaving out" is not the Arabic one.
