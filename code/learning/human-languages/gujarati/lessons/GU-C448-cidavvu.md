## You'll want to know: ચીડવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C448-HASYA448-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચીડવવું** — *cīḍavvũ* — "to tease".

> મોટો ભાઈ નાની બહેનને રોજ ચીડવે છે.
>
> *moṭo bhāī nānī bahenne roj cīḍve che.*
>
> The big brother teases his little sister every day.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને ચીડવ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne cīḍavyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચીડવવું** (*cīḍavvũ*) is the causative partner of **ચિડાવું** (*ciḍāvũ*), "to get irritated": to tease is to make someone cross. Both go back to a reconstructed *\*ciḍ-*, "be angry" (CDIAL 4794).
