## You'll want to know: സാക്ഷി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C349-VARTTA349-01]; assesses=[] -->

**സാക്ഷി** — *sākṣi* — "a witness".

> അപകടം കണ്ട സാക്ഷി പോലീസിനോട് സംസാരിച്ചു.
>
> *apakaṭaṁ kaṇṭa sākṣi pōlīsinōṭŭ saṁsāriccu.*
>
> The witness who saw the accident spoke to the police.

Plural: സാക്ഷികൾ. To give evidence is സാക്ഷി പറയുക, literally to say as a witness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സാക്ഷി** (*sākṣi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sākṣin*, "a witness" (CDIAL 13321). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word apart as *sa-* "with" and *akṣi* "eye": the witness is the one who was there with his own eyes.
