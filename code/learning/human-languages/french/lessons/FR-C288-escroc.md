## You'll want to know: l'escroc
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C288-ARNAQUE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'escroc** — "the swindler, the con artist".

> L'escroc se faisait passer pour un policier.
>
> *The con artist was passing himself off as a police officer.*

The final *c* is silent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escroc* was borrowed from Italian *scrocco* "sponging, living at others' cost" (EtymDB).
