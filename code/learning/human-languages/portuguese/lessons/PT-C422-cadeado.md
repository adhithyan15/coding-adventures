## You'll want to know: o cadeado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C422-BAGAGENS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o cadeado** — "the padlock".

> Fechei a mochila com um cadeado.
>
> *I locked my backpack with a padlock.*

*O cadeado da bicicleta* — the bike lock.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cadeado* is inherited from Late Latin *catēnātum* 'chained' (EtymDB), built on *catēna* 'chain'.
