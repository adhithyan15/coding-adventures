Malayalam, chapter 355: Journalists and Crowds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പത്രപ്രവർത്തകൻ (patrapravarttakan) — a journalist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to applaud, then the Malayalam for to mention.

You'll want to know: പത്രപ്രവർത്തകൻ.
പത്രപ്രവർത്തകൻ — patrapravarttakan — "a journalist".
എന്റെ അമ്മാവൻ ഒരു പത്രപ്രവർത്തകൻ ആണ്. enṟe ammāvan oru patrapravarttakan āṇŭ. My uncle is a journalist.
A woman journalist is പത്രപ്രവർത്തക. The trade is പത്രപ്രവർത്തനം, journalism.

The word, taken apart.
പത്രപ്രവർത്തകൻ (patrapravarttakan) is built from പത്രം (patraṁ), "a newspaper", and പ്രവർത്തകൻ, "a worker", from Sanskrit pravartaka, "setting in motion, promoting" (Monier-Williams), the same word as in സഹപ്രവർത്തകൻ, "a colleague".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ആരോപണം (ārōpaṇaṁ) — an allegation; a charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to mention, then the Malayalam for a journalist.

You'll want to know: ആരോപണം.
ആരോപണം — ārōpaṇaṁ — "an allegation; a charge".
അവന് എതിരെ ഒരു ആരോപണം ഉണ്ട്. avanŭ etire oru ārōpaṇaṁ uṇṭŭ. There is an allegation against him.
Against takes എതിരെ: അവന് എതിരെ, against him.

The word, taken apart.
ആരോപണം (ārōpaṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit āropaṇa, "causing to mount, raising up" (Monier-Williams). An allegation lays a charge upon someone.

Lesson 3 of 5.
വെളിപ്പെടുത്തുക (veḷippeṭuttuka) — to reveal; to disclose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a journalist, then the Malayalam for an allegation.

You'll want to know: വെളിപ്പെടുത്തുക.
വെളിപ്പെടുത്തുക — veḷippeṭuttuka — "to reveal; to disclose".
ലേഖകൻ സംഭവത്തിന്റെ സത്യാവസ്ഥ വെളിപ്പെടുത്തി. lēkhakan sambhavattinṟe satyāvastha veḷippeṭutti. The correspondent revealed the real facts of the incident.
Past: വെളിപ്പെടുത്തി, veḷippeṭutti.
Built on വെളി, the open, the outside: to bring into the open.

The word, taken apart.
വെളിപ്പെടുത്തുക (veḷippeṭuttuka) is native Dravidian: വെളി (veḷi), "an open field; the outside" (DEDR 5498), with -പ്പെടുത്തുക, "to cause to be". To reveal is to put a thing out in the open.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ഒത്തുകൂടുക (ottukūṭuka) — to gather; to assemble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an allegation, then the Malayalam for to reveal.

You'll want to know: ഒത്തുകൂടുക.
ഒത്തുകൂടുക — ottukūṭuka — "to gather; to assemble".
വാർത്ത കേട്ട് ആളുകൾ കവലയിൽ ഒത്തുകൂടി. vārtta kēṭṭŭ āḷukaḷ kavalayil ottukūṭi. Hearing the news, people gathered at the junction.
Past: ഒത്തുകൂടി, ottukūṭi.
The subject is plural: people gather. ഒത്ത് means together.

The word, taken apart.
ഒത്തുകൂടുക (ottukūṭuka) is native Dravidian: കൂടുക (kūṭuka), "to come together, to meet" (DEDR 1882), with ഒത്ത്, "together", in front.

Lesson 5 of 5.
കൂവുക (kūvuka) — to boo; to hoot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to reveal, then the Malayalam for to gather.

You'll want to know: കൂവുക.
കൂവുക — kūvuka — "to boo; to hoot".
കളി തോറ്റപ്പോൾ ആളുകൾ കൂവി. kaḷi tōṟṟappōḷ āḷukaḷ kūvi. When the game was lost, people booed.
Past: കൂവി, kūvi.
The same verb is the crowing of a cock at dawn: കോഴി കൂവി. At a speech it is a jeer.

The word, taken apart.
കൂവുക (kūvuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1868 groups it with Tamil kū, a cry, a call. Malayalam uses it for a cock crowing and for a crowd jeering.
