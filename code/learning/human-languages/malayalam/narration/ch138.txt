Malayalam, chapter 138: Animals of Kerala.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പക്ഷി (pakṣi) — a bird.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an aeroplane, then the Malayalam for an auto.

You'll want to know: പക്ഷി.
പക്ഷി (pakṣi) — "a bird". A കാക്ക is one.

What you've built.
The general word for anything with wings.

Guided Practice.
