## You'll want to know: കോപം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C517-VIKARAM517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**കോപം** — *kōpaṁ* — "wrath; anger".

> അച്ഛന്റെ കോപം പെട്ടെന്ന് മാറി.
>
> *acchanṟe kōpaṁ peṭṭennŭ māṟi.*
>
> Father's anger passed quickly.

More formal than ദേഷ്യം, and common in stories and old songs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കോപം** (*kōpaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kopa*, "fury, anger", from the root *kup*, "to be agitated, to be angry" (Monier-Williams).
