# بسبب (bi-sabab) — because of

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-YAJIBU-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-SUBJUNCTIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I must go now". (*Yajibu an adhhaba al-ān*.) Then ask
"why?". (*Limādhā?*)

[PAUSE 1s] The answer can be a whole sentence, or a single thing. Arabic
uses a different word for each.

## Grammar Lens: a thing, or a sentence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-BI-SABAB-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C174-SABAB174-01, AR-LEX-C255-ACT255-01, AR-LEX-C91-TOWN91-01, AR-LEX-C155-QUAL155-05] -->

**بسبب** — *bi-sabab* — "because of". It is *bi-*, "by", with *sabab*,
"a reason", and a noun follows it:

> **تأخرت بسبب المطر.** — *taʾakhkhartu bi-sabab al-maṭar* — I was late
> because of the rain.

Where the reason is a whole sentence, with its own verb, it takes *li-anna*:

> **تأخرت لأن الطريق كان مزدحما.** — *taʾakhkhartu li-anna aṭ-ṭarīqa kāna
> muzdaḥiman* — I was late because the road was busy.

So *bi-sabab al-maṭar*, a thing; *li-anna aṭ-ṭarīqa kāna muzdaḥiman*, a
sentence.

## Grammar Lens: the reason is that
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-AS-SABAB-HUWA-01]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C40-ANNA-01, AR-LEX-C92-TOWN92-01, AR-LEX-C174-SABAB174-02] -->

To give the reason as an answer of its own, say **السبب هو أن** — *as-sababu
huwa anna* — "the reason is that". *Huwa*, "he, it", stands where English has "is":

> **السبب هو أن القطار تأخر.** — *as-sababu huwa anna al-qiṭāra
> taʾakhkhara* — The reason is that the train was late.

*Natīja*, "a result", is its partner: *as-sabab* looks back, *an-natīja*
looks forward.
