## You'll want to know: こうくうけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C387-DEMUKAERU387-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こうくうけん** — *kōkūken* — "a plane ticket".

> **なつやすみの こうくうけんは たかかったです。** — *natsuyasumi no kōkūken wa takakatta desu* — "The plane tickets for the summer holidays were expensive."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうくうけん** (*kōkūken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "navigate" (Middle Chinese *hang*), *kū*, "sky" (*khuwng*) and *ken*, "ticket" (*khjwonH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
