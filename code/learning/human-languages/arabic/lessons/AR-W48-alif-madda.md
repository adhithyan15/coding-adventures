# آ (alif madda) — the alif that opens آسف

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-QAF-01, AR-SCRIPT-C48-DAD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ق and ض once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **آسف** (*āsif*), "sorry".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: آ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-ALIF-MADDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آ** — *alif* with a **madda**, a long *ā* at the start of a word.

It opens **آسف** (*āsif*), "sorry". The wavy stroke on top, the *madda*, stretches
the vowel: where a plain **ا** at the start of a word needs a hamza to say *a*,
**آ** says a long *ā*.

## Writing: آ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-ALIF-MADDA-01] -->

Put your pen on آ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this character or which way
> to travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
