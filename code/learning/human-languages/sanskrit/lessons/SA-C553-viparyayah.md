## You'll want to know: विपर्ययः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C553-SKHALANA553-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विपर्ययः** — *viparyayaḥ* — "a reversal; a mix-up".

> यदि दिशायाः विपर्ययः न अभविष्यत्, तर्हि अहं समये आगमिष्यम्।
>
> *yadi diśāyāḥ viparyayaḥ na abhaviṣyat, tarhi ahaṃ samaye āgamiṣyam.*
>
> If I had not mixed up the direction, I would have come on time.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विपर्ययः** (*viparyayaḥ*) is built on *vi*, *pari* and the root √i, "to go": a going round the wrong way; MW gives *viparyaya* as reversed, inverted, contrary (MW).
