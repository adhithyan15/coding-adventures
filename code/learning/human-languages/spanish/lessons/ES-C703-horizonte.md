## You'll want to know: el horizonte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C703-ANOCHECER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el horizonte** — "the horizon".

> El sol se pone en el horizonte.
>
> *The sun sets on the horizon.*

*Nuevos horizontes* — new horizons.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*horizonte* is a learned borrowing from Latin *horizōn*, from Greek *horízōn* "bounding (circle)" (EtymDB), from *hóros* "boundary".
