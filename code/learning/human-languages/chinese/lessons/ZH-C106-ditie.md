## You'll want to know: dìtiě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C106-STOP106-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dìtiě* — "underground; metro".

> *wǒmen zuò dìtiě qù* — We'll take the metro.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìtiě* is built from dì 'ground' and tiě 'iron', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *thet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as black metal. The railway under the ground is a modern sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
