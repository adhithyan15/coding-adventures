## You'll want to know: قدیم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C304-DARANZAMAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قدیم** (*qadim*) — "the old days, olden times".

> در قدیم مردم با اسب سفر میکردند.
>
> *Dar qadim mardom bâ asb safar mikardand.*
>
> In the old days people travelled on horseback.

*Qadim-hâ* "long ago" in speech. The adjective for old things is *qadimi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qadim* is borrowed from Arabic *qadīm*, "old, ancient" (EtymDB).
