# ನೆರೆಹೊರೆ (nerehore) — a neighbourhood

## You'll want to know: ನೆರೆಹೊರೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C546-NEMMADI546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೆರೆಹೊರೆ** — *nerehore* — "a neighbourhood".

> ನಮ್ಮ ನೆರೆಹೊರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಸ್ನೇಹದಿಂದ ಇದ್ದಾರೆ.
>
> *namma nerehoreyalli ellarū snēhadinda iddāre.*
>
> Everyone in our neighbourhood is friendly.

ನೆರೆಯವರು are the neighbours themselves.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೆರೆಹೊರೆ** (*nerehore*) is a native Kannada pair-word built on *nere*, "adjoining, proximity, neighbourhood" (DEDR 3770); the second half echoes it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
