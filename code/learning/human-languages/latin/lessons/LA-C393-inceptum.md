## You'll want to know: inceptum, inceptī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C393-STATUO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**inceptum, inceptī** — "an undertaking, a venture".

> Inceptum tuum laudō.
>
> *I praise your undertaking.*

*inceptum, inceptī* n., the participle of *incipiō*, to begin: a thing begun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inceptum* is the participle of *incipiō* "to begin" (Lewis & Short), from *in* and *capiō* "to take": a thing taken in hand. English *inception* comes from its family.
