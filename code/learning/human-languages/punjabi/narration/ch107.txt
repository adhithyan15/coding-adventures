Punjabi, chapter 107: Something, Oneself, One's own, Both, Several.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਕੁਝ (kujh) — something, some.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for such, then the Punjabi for every.

You'll want to know: ਕੁਝ.
ਕੁਝ — kujh — "something, some".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kujh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kujh, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kujh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for such, then the Punjabi for every, then say kujh again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਕੁਝ (kujh) mean? ("Something, some".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Seasons and months — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for both and several.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: midnight, daybreak, the day after tomorrow, summer, winter]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the rainy season, spring, autumn, every day, a date]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: right now, January, February, March, April]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: May, June, July, August, September]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Midnight? (ਅੱਧੀ ਰਾਤ, adhī rāt.) Daybreak? (ਤੜਕਾ, taṛkā.) The day after tomorrow? (ਪਰਸੋਂ, parsõ.) Summer? (ਗਰਮੀ, garmī.) Winter? (ਸਰਦੀ, sardī.) The rainy season? (ਬਰਸਾਤ, barsāt.) Spring? (ਬਸੰਤ, basant.) Autumn? (ਪਤਝੜ, patjhaṛ.) Every day? (ਰੋਜ਼, roz.) A date? (ਤਾਰੀਖ਼, tārīkh.) Right now? (ਹੁਣੇ, huṇe.) January? (ਜਨਵਰੀ, janvarī.) February? (ਫ਼ਰਵਰੀ, farvarī.) March? (ਮਾਰਚ, mārc.) April? (ਅਪ੍ਰੈਲ, aprail.) May? (ਮਈ, maī.) June? (ਜੂਨ, jūn.) July? (ਜੁਲਾਈ, julāī.) August? (ਅਗਸਤ, agast.) September? (ਸਤੰਬਰ, satambar.)
