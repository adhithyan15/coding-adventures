French, chapter 510: Glace et neige.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le patin — skate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for breaststroke, then the French for to row.

You'll want to know: le patin.
le patin — "skate".
J'ai reçu des patins pour mon anniversaire. I got skates for my birthday.
Le patin à glace — ice skating; le patin à roulettes — roller skating.

The word, taken apart.
le patin is built on patte "paw, foot" with -in (EtymDB); the origin of patte is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la luge — sledge, sled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for skate, then the French for to skate.

You'll want to know: la luge.
la luge — "sledge, sled".
Les enfants font de la luge sur la colline. The children are sledging on the hill.
Faire de la luge — to go sledging. In Québec la traîne sauvage.

The word, taken apart.
la luge came into French from the dialects of the Alps. It is usually traced to a Gaulish word; the origin is not fully settled.

la patinoire is built on patiner "to skate" with -oire, the ending for a place.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la piste — slope, run; track.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for sledge, then the French for ice rink.

You'll want to know: la piste.
la piste — "slope, run; track".
Cette piste noire est trop difficile pour moi. This black run is too difficult for me.
Also a running track, a dance floor, or a clue: une bonne piste.

The word, taken apart.
la piste was borrowed from Italian pista "a beaten track" (EtymDB), built on pistare "to pound", which goes back to Latin pinsere "to pound" (Lewis & Short).
