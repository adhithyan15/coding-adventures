## You'll want to know: عزى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C319-AZA319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عزى** — *ʿazzā* — "to console, to offer condolences".

> عزى الجيران الأسرة بعد الجنازة.
>
> *ʿazzā al-jīrān al-usra baʿda al-janāza.*
>
> The neighbours offered the family their condolences after the funeral.

Present: يعزي, *yuʿazzī*.

عزى someone في the person who died: عزاه في أبيه.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عزى** (*ʿazzā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-z-y* (Lane), patience under loss. It is Form II, "to bid someone be patient": to console.
