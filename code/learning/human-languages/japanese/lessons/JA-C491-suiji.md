## You'll want to know: すいじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C491-STOVE491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**すいじ** — *suiji* — "kitchen work; cooking".

> **ちちは にちようびに すいじを します。** — *chichi wa nichiyōbi ni suiji o shimasu* — "My father does the cooking on Sunday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいじ** (*suiji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sui*, "cook" (Middle Chinese *tsyhwe*) and *ji*, "matter" (*dzriH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
