## You'll want to know: अवनतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C547-UTTHANA547-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अवनतिः** — *avanatiḥ* — "a decline".

> यदि व्यापारस्य अवनतिः भवति, तर्हि वणिक् दुःखी भवति।
>
> *yadi vyāpārasya avanatiḥ bhavati, tarhi vaṇik duḥkhī bhavati.*
>
> If trade declines, the merchant is unhappy.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवनतिः** (*avanatiḥ*) is built on *ava*, "down", and the root √nam, "to bow", which also gives *namati*; MW gives *avanati* as bowing down, stooping; "a decline", of trade or fortune, is the modern sense (MW).
