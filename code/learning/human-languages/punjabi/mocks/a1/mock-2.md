# Punjabi A1 mock 2 — learner paper

This is a project-defined practice mock. It does not establish readiness,
attainment, accreditation, or equivalence to an external examination.

Give this paper to the learner. Keep the examiner script, answer key, and
rubric separate. Total administration is 58 written minutes plus 10 speaking
minutes, with five-minute speaking preparation before the speaking paper.
Written Punjabi answers must use Gurmukhi. No dictionary, translator,
romanization, transcript, spell-checker, or answer model is allowed.

For secure administration, print the four skill sections as separate packets
and give the learner only the current packet. Collect the Reading packet before
giving Listening; collect Listening before Writing; collect Writing before
Speaking. Never return an earlier packet during the sitting.

## Reading — 20 minutes

Read the Punjabi sources and choose A, B, or C.

## Writing — 20 minutes

Keep one timer running through both tasks. Write only in Gurmukhi. Do not use
romanization, a dictionary, a translator, a spell-checker, or any visible
Punjabi answer model.

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
### Part 1 — practical form, 7 minutes

1. Complete all seven fields with your own or fictional details. The unscored
   support-language example “phone: 025 125” shows only the response type. Use
   one line per field, write clearly, and leave no requested field blank.

   - **ਨਾਂ:** ____________________
   - **ਭਾਸ਼ਾ:** ____________________
   - **ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼:** ____________________
   - **ਕੰਮ:** ____________________
   - **ਉਮਰ:** ____________________
   - **ਫ਼ੋਨ:** ____________________
   - **ਤਾਰੀਖ਼:** ____________________
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->
### Part 2 — named-reader message, 13 minutes

2. Your named reader is **ਅਮਨ**. Write **30–40 Punjabi words** to greet Aman and
   arrange a meeting tomorrow. Give your name, residence, one language, and one
   preference; suggest meeting at the market, then close politely. No response
   wording is supplied.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->

## Speaking — 10 minutes

You have five minutes to prepare parts 2 and 3. Notes may contain keywords,
not a written answer script. The examiner conducts the prompts in Punjabi and
records the complete paper.

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
### Part 1 — personal interview

Answer the five questions over 60–120 seconds in total.

1. State your name.
2. Say whether you live in a village or city.
3. Say one language you speak.
4. Say one activity you like.
5. Say where one familiar object is.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
### Part 2 — prepared description

6. Speak for one minute about a familiar person. Say who the person is, where
   that person lives, one language they speak, and one thing they like.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
### Part 3 — transactional role-play

7. Continue the exchange for 60–120 seconds. At a small shop, greet the server,
   ask for a book or pen, ask the price, and close politely.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
