# कसणो (kasṇo) — to tighten; to harness

## You'll want to know: कसणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C233-GEAR233-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कसणो** — *kasṇo* — "to tighten; to harness".

> हूं ऊंट माथै पलाण कसूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ ū̃ṭ māthai palāṇ kasū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I tighten the saddle on the camel.

Past (I …): म्हैं पलाण कस्यो, *mhaĩ palāṇ kasyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसणो** (*kasṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karṣati*, "draws, pulls"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *kasaï*, "harnesses, binds", and Hindi *kasnā*, "to tighten" (CDIAL 2908).
