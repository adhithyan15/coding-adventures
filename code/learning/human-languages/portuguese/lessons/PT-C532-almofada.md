## You'll want to know: a almofada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C532-ACOLHEDORA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a almofada** — "cushion; pillow".

> O sofá tem muitas almofadas.
>
> *The sofa has lots of cushions.*

In Portugal also the pillow on a bed; in Brazil that is *o travesseiro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*almofada* comes, through Andalusian Arabic, from Arabic *mikhadda* "a cushion" (EtymDB).
