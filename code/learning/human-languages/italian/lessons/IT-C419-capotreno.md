## You'll want to know: il capotreno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C419-CARROZZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il capotreno** — "the guard, the train manager".

> Il capotreno annuncia la prossima fermata.
>
> *The guard announces the next stop.*

*Capo* + *treno*: the head of the train crew.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*capotreno* is built from *capo* 'head', inherited from Latin *caput*, and *treno* 'train', which was borrowed from French *train* (EtymDB).
