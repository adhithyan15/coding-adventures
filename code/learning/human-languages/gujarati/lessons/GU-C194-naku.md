## You'll want to know: નાકું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C194-CORNER194-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નાકું** — *nākũ* — "street corner, end of a road".

> **દુકાન ગલીના નાકે છે.** — *dukān galīnā nāke che* — "The shop is at the end of the lane."

A neuter noun; **નાકે**, *nāke*, 'at the corner'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નાકું** (*nākũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "eye of a needle, end of a road, passage", under *\*nākka*, "like a nose, projecting", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 7037).
