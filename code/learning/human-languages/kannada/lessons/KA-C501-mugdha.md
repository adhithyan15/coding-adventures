## You'll want to know: ಮುಗ್ಧ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C501-SAUMYA501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಗ್ಧ** — *mugdha* — "innocent; naive".

> ಮಗುವಿನ ಮುಗ್ಧ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಗೆ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ನಕ್ಕರು.
>
> *maguvina mugdha praśnege ellarū nakkaru.*
>
> Everyone laughed at the child's innocent question.

ಮುಗ್ಧತೆ, innocence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಗ್ಧ** (*mugdha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mugdha*, "gone astray, bewildered, foolish" (CDIAL 10175). The sense softened in Kannada to "artless, innocent".
