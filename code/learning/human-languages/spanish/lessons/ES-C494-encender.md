## You'll want to know: encender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C494-MANANA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**encender** — "to switch on; to light".

> Enciende la luz, por favor.
>
> *Switch on the light, please.*

The e breaks into ie under stress: *enciendo*. The opposite is *apagar*.
