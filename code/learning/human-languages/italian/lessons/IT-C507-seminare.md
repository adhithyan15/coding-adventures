## You'll want to know: seminare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C507-ORTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**seminare** — "to sow".

> In primavera semino i pomodori.
>
> *In spring I sow tomatoes.*

Built on *il seme* "seed".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seminare* is inherited from Latin *sēmināre* "to sow" (EtymDB), from *sēmen* "a seed".
