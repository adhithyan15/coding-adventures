## You'll want to know: مقر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C524-TAAWUN524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مقر** — *maqarr* — "headquarters, a base".

> مقر الشركة في وسط المدينة.
>
> *maqarr ash-sharika fī wasaṭ al-madīna.*
>
> The company's headquarters is in the city centre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقر** (*maqarr*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-r*, "to settle, to stay firm" (Lane). A maqarr is a place where a thing settles.
