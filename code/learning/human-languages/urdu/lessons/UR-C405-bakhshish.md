## You'll want to know: بخشش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C405-RAJISTAR405-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بخشش** — *bakhshish* — "a tip (for service); a gift".

> سارا نے بیرے کو بخشش دی۔
>
> *sārā ne bere ko bakhshish dī.*
>
> Sara gave the waiter a tip.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بخشش** (*bakhshish*) is usually traced to Persian *bakhshish*, "a gift, a giving".
