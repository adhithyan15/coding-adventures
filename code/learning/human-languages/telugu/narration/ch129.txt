Telugu, chapter 129: Window curtain, Clay pot, Water pot, Cupboard, Table.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
కిటికీతెర (kiṭikītera) — a window curtain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the skin, then the Telugu for blood.

You'll want to know: కిటికీతెర.
కిటికీతెర — kiṭikītera — "a window curtain".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: kiṭikītera]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kiṭikītera, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kiṭikītera]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for the skin, then the Telugu for blood, then say kiṭikītera again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does కిటికీతెర (kiṭikītera) mean? ("A window curtain".) Say it once more.

A tiger? (పులి, puli.) A lion? (సింహం, siṁhaṁ.) A bear? (ఎలుగుబంటి, elugubaṇṭi.) A monkey? (కోతి, kōti.) A rabbit? (కుందేలు, kundēlu.) A deer? (జింక, jiṅka.) A fox? (నక్క, nakka.) A snake? (పాము, pāmu.) A tortoise? (తాబేలు, tābēlu.) A parrot? (చిలక, cilaka.)
