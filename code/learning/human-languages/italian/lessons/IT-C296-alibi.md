## You'll want to know: l'alibi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C296-MOVENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'alibi** — "the alibi".

> Lui ha un alibi: era al cinema.
>
> *He has an alibi: he was at the cinema.*

Plural unchanged: *gli alibi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alibi* was taken from Latin *alibī* "elsewhere" (EtymDB), built on *alius* "other" and *ibī* "there".
