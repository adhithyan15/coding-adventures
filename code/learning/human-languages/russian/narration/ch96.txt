Russian, chapter 96: Towel, Soap, Toothbrush, Comb, Rubbish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
полотенце (poloténtse) — a towel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pillow, then the Russian for a blanket.

You'll want to know: полотенце.
полотенце (poloténtse) — "a towel".

The word, taken apart.
полотенце (poloténtse) is полотно, "linen cloth", with the diminutive -енце. полотно from Proto-Slavic poltĭno, which Vasmer relates to Sanskrit paṭa-, "cloth".

Lesson 3 of 5.
зубная щётка (zubnáya shchyótka) — a toothbrush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a towel, then the Russian for soap.

You'll want to know: зубная щётка.
зубная щётка (zubnáya shchyótka) — "a toothbrush".

The word, taken apart.
зубная щётка (zubnáya shchyótka) is "tooth brush": зубная, from зуб, "tooth", with щётка, "brush", from щеть, "bristle", the root of щетина, "stubble". Vasmer finds the proposed origins of that root doubtful.

Lesson 4 of 5.
расчёска (raschyóska) — a comb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for soap, then the Russian for a toothbrush.

You'll want to know: расчёска.
расчёска (raschyóska) — "a comb".

The word, taken apart.
расчёска (raschyóska) is рас-, "apart", with чесать, "to comb", and -ка. чесать from Proto-Slavic česati, a cousin of Lithuanian kasýti, "to scratch" (Vasmer).
