## You'll want to know: ગેરસમજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C351-HAKIKAT351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગેરસમજ** — *gersamaj* — "a misunderstanding".

> આ નાની ગેરસમજને કારણે બે મિત્રો લડ્યા.
>
> *ā nānī gersamajne kāraṇe be mitro laḍyā.*
>
> The two friends fought because of this small misunderstanding.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગેરસમજ** (*gersamaj*) is built from *ger-*, "mis-, not", usually traced to Persian *ghair*, "other", and *samaj*, "understanding", the noun of **સમજવું** (*samajvũ*), from Sanskrit *saṃbudhyatē* (CDIAL 12959).
