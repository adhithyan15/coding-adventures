## You'll want to know: ઈજા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C227-INJURY227-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઈજા** — *ījā* — "injury".

> **શું તમને ઈજા થઈ?** — *śũ tamne ījā thaī?* — "Did you get hurt?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઈજા** (*ījā*) is a loan from Arabic *īdhā'*, "harm, hurt", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *'-dh-y* (Lane).
