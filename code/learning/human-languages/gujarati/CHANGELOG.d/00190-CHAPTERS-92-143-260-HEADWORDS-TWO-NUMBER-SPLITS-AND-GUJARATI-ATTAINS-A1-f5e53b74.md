## Chapters 92-143: 260 headwords, two number splits, and Gujarati attains A1

Gujarati had five kinds of A1 gap:

- four A1 spine nodes had no lesson: time of day, definite reference, everyday
  things, and saying what I have and can do;
- it taught 357 headwords against 600, and 25 verbs against 40;
- two chapter-6 number lessons introduced four atoms each;
- fourteen atoms were revisited fewer than twice.

**The words.** Fifty-two chapters of five, closed by twelve review lessons:

- **time:** **મધરાત**, **પરોઢ**, **પરમદિવસ**, the seasons (**ઉનાળો**,
  **શિયાળો**, **ચોમાસું**), and the months in both calendars, from
  **જાન્યુઆરી** to **કારતક** and **આસો** (TIME-OF-DAY)
- **this and that:** **પેલું**, **આવું**, **એવું**, **દરેક**, **કોઈ**,
  **બંને** and more (DEFINITE-REFERENCE)
- **have and can:** **શકવું**, **પાસે**, **તાકાત**, **હક**, **તક**,
  **મિલકત** and more (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO)
- **things:** 125 of them, covering the house, clothes, food, nature,
  animals, the body, the town, school and work (NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS)
- **qualities:** thirty-five describing words, from **નીચું** to
  **જરૂરી** (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)
- **verbs:** thirty, from **કૂદવું** to **બદલવું** (NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS),
  with five more among the have-and-can words

**Checks.** Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword,
romanization and lesson text. Every word uses only the 45 signs the book
already teaches, all of which `data/scripts/gujarati.json` covers. That rules
out common words with ડ, ઝ or ષ, so no candidate uses them. **આ** and **એ**
already existed and **મળવું** appears in earlier lesson text, so those three
were left out. Thirty-three more were set aside to keep the tranche at 260.

**The splits.** Both over-budget lessons now end where their first idea ends:

- **one to five** keeps the five numbers and the headless-script clue. The two
  surprises, **બે** with *b* and **ત્રણ** with *r*, move to *numbers 1-5,
  more*.
- **number histories** keeps *be* and its Sanskrit source. The *r* that
  *traṇ* regained moves to *number histories, more*.

Each continuation has its own practice and wrap-up.

**The revisits.** Eighteen thin atoms each get warm-up retrievals in the new
chapters, including:

- the chapter-5 sentences
- *paṇ* as "also"
- the *ke* frames
- *je … te*
- the two words for *we*
- the reading skills

An atom with no revisit gets two lines, in two lessons.

      level gate           pre-A1 -> A1
