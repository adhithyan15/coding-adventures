# બ — the consonant ba

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHA-01] -->

Write *cha* once from memory. Check its small middle loop, then put that model
away.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-BA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **બ** visible. It is *ba*. Circle clockwise around the rounded left body,
wind through the compact inner turn, and exit across the right shoulder. Lift
once; descend the tall right spine and turn through its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **બ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice rounded outer body,
compact inner turn, separate right spine; one pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **બ** once. Compare only the outer body and inner turn; repair one
landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W05-ba-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-BA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write ba from memory.","answer":"બ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"બ — the rounded body and compact inner turn are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at બ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *ba* once. Uncover **બ**, compare, and stop after one
repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name the inner landmark and the pen-lift count.
