## You'll want to know: la pensión
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C632-PAGO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la pensión** — "the pension; the guesthouse".

> Mi abuela cobra una pensión.
>
> *My grandmother gets a pension.*

Also a cheap guesthouse: *una pensión en el centro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pensión* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pensio* 'a payment', built on *pendere* 'to weigh out, to pay' (EtymDB).
