## You'll want to know: ساهم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C371-WASILA371-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ساهم** — *sāhama* — "to contribute".

> ساهم الجيران في تنظيف الشارع.
>
> *sāhama al-jīrān fī tanẓīf ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> The neighbours contributed to cleaning the street.

Present: يساهم, *yusāhim*.

It takes في: ساهم في ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ساهم** (*sāhama*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-h-m*, "an arrow; a share" (Lane). It is Form III, to take a share with others: to contribute.
