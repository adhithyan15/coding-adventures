## You'll want to know: गोणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C413-UNNAYA413-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गोणी** — *goṇī* — "a sack".

> भारवाहः गोणीं पृष्ठे वहति।
>
> *bhāravāhaḥ goṇīṃ pṛṣṭhe vahati.*
>
> The porter carries the sack on his back.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोणी** (*goṇī*) is the word MW gives for a sack, in Pāṇini's examples and later texts; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
