## You'll want to know: पाकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C511-KASTHA511-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पाकः** — *pākaḥ* — "cooking".

> मम मातुः पाकः उत्तमः अस्ति।
>
> *mama mātuḥ pākaḥ uttamaḥ asti.*
>
> My mother's cooking is excellent.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाकः** (*pākaḥ*) comes from the root √pac, "to cook", which also gives *pacati*; MW gives *pāka* as cooking, baking, roasting, boiling (MW).
