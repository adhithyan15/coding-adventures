## You'll want to know: नस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C478-PHEPHDA478-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नस** — *nas* — "a vein; a nerve".

> बूढ़े हाथों पर नसें साफ़ दिखती हैं।
>
> *būṛhe hāthoṁ par naseṁ sāf dikhtī haiṁ.*
>
> The veins show clearly on old hands.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नस** (*nas*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *nas*, "muscle, tendon, vein, nerve", under Sanskrit *snasā*, "sinew" (CDIAL 13784). Everyday Hindi uses one word for every cord under the skin.
