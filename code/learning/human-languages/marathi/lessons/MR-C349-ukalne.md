## You'll want to know: उकलणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C349-UPAY349-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उकलणे** — *ukalṇe* — "to unravel; to solve (a puzzle)".

> मुलाने ते कोडं दहा मिनिटांत उकललं.
>
> *mulāne te koḍaṁ dahā miniṭānt ukallaṁ.*
>
> The boy solved the puzzle in ten minutes.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कोडं उकललं, *tyāne koḍaṁ ukallaṁ*.

Also intransitive: गाठ उकलली, the knot came undone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उकलणे** (*ukalṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ukalṇẽ*, "to be loosened, be disentangled", under *\*utkalati*, "goes out", beside Sanskrit *utkalayati*, "loosens" (CDIAL 1716).
