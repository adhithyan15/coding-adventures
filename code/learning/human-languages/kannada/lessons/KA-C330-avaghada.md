## You'll want to know: ಅವಘಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C330-DIKKI330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅವಘಡ** — *avaghaḍa* — "a mishap".

> ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಣ್ಣ ಅವಘಡ ಆಯಿತು, ಯಾರಿಗೂ ಏನೂ ಆಗಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *kārkhāneyalli saṇṇa avaghaḍa āyitu, yārigū ēnū āgalilla.*
>
> There was a small mishap at the factory; nobody was hurt.

A news word for an accident with fire, gas or machines.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅವಘಡ** (*avaghaḍa*) is a Kannada word of the newspapers whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
