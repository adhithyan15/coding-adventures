Malayalam, chapter 470: Fuel and Tyres.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പെട്രോൾ (peṭrōḷ) — petrol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to make way, then the Malayalam for to cross over.

You'll want to know: പെട്രോൾ.
പെട്രോൾ — peṭrōḷ — "petrol".
പെട്രോളിന്റെ വില വീണ്ടും കൂടി. peṭrōḷinṟe vila vīṇṭuṁ kūṭi. The price of petrol went up again.
A petrol station is a പെട്രോൾ (peṭrōḷ) പമ്പ് in everyday Malayalam.

The word, taken apart.
പെട്രോൾ (peṭrōḷ) was borrowed from English petrol, the fuel of cars and motorbikes.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ഇന്ധനം (indhanaṁ) — fuel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to cross over, then the Malayalam for petrol.

You'll want to know: ഇന്ധനം.
ഇന്ധനം — indhanaṁ — "fuel".
വിമാനത്തിന് ധാരാളം ഇന്ധനം വേണം. vimānattinŭ dhārāḷaṁ indhanaṁ vēṇaṁ. A plane needs a lot of fuel.
The general word: petrol, diesel, gas and firewood are all ഇന്ധനം (indhanaṁ).

The word, taken apart.
ഇന്ധനം (indhanaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit indhana, "kindling, lighting", and so what is burnt (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
പഞ്ചറാകുക (pañcaṟākuka) — to get a puncture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a tyre, then the Malayalam for a brake.

You'll want to know: പഞ്ചറാകുക.
പഞ്ചറാകുക — pañcaṟākuka — "to get a puncture".
വഴിയിൽ വച്ച് സൈക്കിളിന്റെ ടയർ പഞ്ചറായി. vaḻiyil vaccŭ saikkiḷinṟe ṭayar pañcaṟāyi. On the way the bicycle's tyre got a puncture.
Past: പഞ്ചറായി, pañcaṟāyi.
English puncture plus ആകുക, to become. The noun alone is പഞ്ചർ.

The word, taken apart.
പഞ്ചറാകുക (pañcaṟākuka) is a Malayalam verb built from പഞ്ചർ, which was borrowed from English puncture, and ആകുക, to become.
