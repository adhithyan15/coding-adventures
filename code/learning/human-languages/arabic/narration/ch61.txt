Arabic, chapter 61: To learn, To forget, To want, To send, To cut.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعلم (taʿallama) — to learn (he learned).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to cry, then the Arabic for to dance.

You'll want to know: تعلم.
تعلم — taʿallama — "to learn (he learned)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: taʿallama]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: taʿallama, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say taʿallama]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to cry, then the Arabic for to dance, then say taʿallama again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does تعلم (taʿallama) mean? ("To learn (he learned)".) Say it once more.
