Hindi, chapter 505: Mourning.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
शोक (śok) — mourning; grief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to slip from memory, then the Hindi for an inheritance.

You'll want to know: शोक.
शोक — śok — "mourning; grief".
दादा की मौत पर पूरे गाँव ने शोक मनाया। dādā kī maut par pūre gā̃v ne śok manāyā. The whole village mourned my grandfather's death.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
शोक (śok) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit śoka, "sorrow, anguish, grief" (Monier-Williams). śok manānā is to observe mourning.

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 5 of 7.
दफ़नाना (dafnānā) — to bury (the dead).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a funeral pyre, then the Hindi for a widow.

You'll want to know: दफ़नाना.
दफ़नाना — dafnānā — "to bury (the dead)".
चिड़िया मर गई, तो बच्चों ने उसे बाग़ में दफ़नाया। ciṛiyā mar gaī, to baccoṁ ne use bāġ meṁ dafnāyā. The bird died, so the children buried it in the garden.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चिड़िया को दफ़नाया, usne ciṛiyā ko dafnāyā.

The word, taken apart.
दफ़नाना (dafnānā) is dafn, "burial", made into a verb with -ānā; dafn is usually traced to Arabic dafn, "burial", which reached Hindi through Persian.
