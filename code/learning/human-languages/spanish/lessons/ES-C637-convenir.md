## You'll want to know: convenir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C637-FAVOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**convenir** — "to suit, to be advisable; to agree".

> Te conviene descansar.
>
> *You would do well to rest.*

Like *venir*: *conviene, convino*. *Me conviene* — it suits me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convenir* is inherited from Latin *convenire* 'to come together, to agree, to suit', built on *venire* 'to come' (EtymDB).
