## You'll want to know: さかえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C313-KIROKU313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**さかえる** — *sakaeru* — "to prosper; to flourish".

> **むかし この まちは さかえていました。** — *mukashi kono machi wa sakaete imashita* — "Long ago this town was prosperous."

Polite **さかえます**, *sakaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **まちが さかえました。**, *machi ga sakaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さかえる** (*sakaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
