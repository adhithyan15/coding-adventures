[PAUSE 3s] To destroy? (**नष्ट करणे**, *naṣṭa karṇe*.) To exaggerate? (**अतिशयोक्ती करणे**, *atiśayoktī karṇe*.) To celebrate? (**साजरा करणे**, *sājrā karṇe*.) To float? (**तरंगणे**, *taraṅgṇe*.) To chase? (**पाठलाग करणे**, *pāṭhlāg karṇe*.) To praise? (**कौतुक करणे**, *kautuk karṇe*.) To go forward? (**पुढे जाणे**, *puḍhe jāṇe*.) To go back? (**मागे जाणे**, *māge jāṇe*.) To defend? (**बचाव करणे**, *bacāv karṇe*.) To disappear? (**नाहीसे होणे**, *nāhīse hoṇe*.) A tablet? (**गोळी**, *goḷī*.) An ointment? (**मलम**, *malam*.) A bandage; a strip? (**पट्टी**, *paṭṭī*.) An ambulance? (**रुग्णवाहिका**, *rugṇavāhikā*.) A diet? (**आहार**, *āhār*.) The beginning? (**सुरुवात**, *suruvāt*.) A deadline? (**मुदत**, *mudat*.) Daily? (**दररोज**, *darroj*.) Meanwhile? (**दरम्यान**, *darmyān*.) For good? (**कायम**, *kāyam*.) Together? (**एकत्र**, *ekatra*.) Perhaps? (**कदाचित**, *kadācit*.) Besides? (**शिवाय**, *śivāy*.) Of course? (**अर्थात**, *arthāt*.) That is to say? (**म्हणजे**, *mhaṇje*.)
