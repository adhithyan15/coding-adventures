## You'll want to know: событие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C285-SOBYTIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**событие** (*sobýtiye*) — "an event".

> Это было важное событие для всей семьи.
>
> *Éto býlo vázhnoye sobýtiye dlya vsey semí.*
>
> It was an important event for the whole family.

Neuter. *Место событий* — the scene.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*событие* is built on *со-* "together" and *бытие* "being", from *быть* "to be", inherited from Proto-Slavic *byti*, from PIE *bʰuH-* (EtymDB): what came to be.
