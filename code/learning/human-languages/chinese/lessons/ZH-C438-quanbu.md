## You'll want to know: 全部
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C438-NORTH438-05]; assesses=[] -->

**全部** — *quánbù* — "all of it; the whole".

> *wǒmen de xíngli quánbù fàng zài chē lǐ* — All our luggage is in the car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**全部** (*quánbù*) is built from 全 'whole' and 部 'a part', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjwen* and *buX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'complete'.
