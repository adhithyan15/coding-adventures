Telugu, chapter 404: Rich and Poor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పేదరికం (pēdarikaṁ) — poverty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a scheme, then the Telugu for welfare.

You'll want to know: పేదరికం.
పేదరికం — pēdarikaṁ — "poverty".
పేదరికం వల్ల చాలా మంది పిల్లలు చదువుకోలేరు. pēdarikaṁ valla cālā mandi pillalu caduvukōlēru. Because of poverty many children cannot go to school.
పేద, poor, which you know, + -రికం.

The word, taken apart.
పేదరికం (pēdarikaṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4437 lists Telugu pēda, "poor", and pēdaṟikamu, "poverty".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ధనిక (dhanika) — rich; wealthy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for welfare, then the Telugu for poverty.

You'll want to know: ధనిక.
ధనిక — dhanika — "rich; wealthy".
ధనిక దేశాలు పేద దేశాలకు సహాయం చేయాలి. dhanika dēśālu pēda dēśālaku sahāyaṁ cēyāli. Rich countries should help poor countries.
Before a noun: ధనిక (dhanika) కుటుంబం, a rich family.

The word, taken apart.
ధనిక (dhanika) is borrowed from Sanskrit dhanika, "wealthy, opulent" (Monier-Williams), from dhana, "wealth".

Lesson 3 of 5.
సంపద (sampada) — wealth; riches.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for poverty, then the Telugu for rich.

You'll want to know: సంపద.
సంపద — sampada — "wealth; riches".
ఆ కుటుంబం దగ్గర చాలా సంపద ఉంది. ā kuṭumbaṁ daggara cālā sampada undi. That family has a great deal of wealth.
Also a country’s riches: సహజ సంపద (sampada), natural resources.

The word, taken apart.
సంపద (sampada) is borrowed from Sanskrit sampad, "success" (CDIAL 12932), which Monier-Williams also glosses "accomplishment, perfection"; Telugu uses it for wealth.

Lesson 4 of 5.
స్వతంత్ర (svatantra) — independent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for rich, then the Telugu for wealth.

You'll want to know: స్వతంత్ర.
స్వతంత్ర — svatantra — "independent".
ఉద్యోగం వచ్చాక ఆమె స్వతంత్ర జీవితం గడుపుతోంది. udyōgaṁ vaccāka āme svatantra jīvitaṁ gaḍuputōndi. Since getting a job she has been living an independent life.
Before a noun: స్వతంత్ర (svatantra) జీవితం, an independent life. స్వతంత్రంగా, independently.

The word, taken apart.
స్వతంత్ర (svatantra) is borrowed from Sanskrit svatantra, "self-dependent, independent, free" (Monier-Williams), from sva, "own", and tantra, "a system, a rule".

Lesson 5 of 5.
నిరుద్యోగం (nirudyōgaṁ) — unemployment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for wealth, then the Telugu for independent.

You'll want to know: నిరుద్యోగం.
నిరుద్యోగం — nirudyōgaṁ — "unemployment".
మా ప్రాంతంలో నిరుద్యోగం పెరిగింది. mā prāntaṁlō nirudyōgaṁ perigindi. Unemployment has risen in our area.
నిర్-, without, + ఉద్యోగం, a job, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
నిరుద్యోగం (nirudyōgaṁ) is built from Sanskrit nir-, "without", and udyoga, "exertion, undertaking" (Monier-Williams), the word Telugu has as ఉద్యోగం, a job.
