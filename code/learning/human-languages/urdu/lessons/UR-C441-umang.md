## You'll want to know: امنگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C441-JOY441-05]; assesses=[] -->

**امنگ** — *umang* — "a surge of eagerness; high spirits".

> نئے سال کی صبح میرے دل میں نئی امنگ تھی۔
>
> *naye sāl kī subah mere dil meṅ naī umang thī.*
>
> On the first morning of the year my heart was full of fresh eagerness.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امنگ** (*umang*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *umaṅg*, "elation", under Sanskrit *únmajjati*, "emerges, comes up" (CDIAL 2110).
