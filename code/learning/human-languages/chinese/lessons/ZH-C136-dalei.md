## You'll want to know: dǎléi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C136-WEATHER136-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎléi* — "to thunder".

> *dǎléi le wǒmen bù chūmén le* — It's thundering, so we're staying in.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎléi* is built from 打 'to strike' and *léi* 'thunder', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *taengX* and *lwoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), though dǎ is not the sound *taengX* would regularly give. The dictionary explains the second, after an older book, as yin and yang pressing on each other.
