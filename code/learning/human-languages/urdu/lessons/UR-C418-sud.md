## You'll want to know: سود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C418-HISAB418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سود** — *sūd* — "interest (on money)".

> بینک قرض پر سود لیتا ہے۔
>
> *baink qarz par sūd letā hai.*
>
> The bank charges interest on a loan.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سود** (*sūd*) is usually traced to Persian *sūd*, "profit, gain"; in Urdu it is the interest on a loan or on savings.
