## You'll want to know: હાલત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C296-AMBULANS296-05]; assesses=[] -->

**હાલત** — *hālat* — "a condition (of health or repair)".

> હવે દર્દીની હાલત સારી છે.
>
> *have dardīnī hālat sārī che.*
>
> The patient is in a good condition now.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હાલત** (*hālat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥāla*, "a state, a condition". It is feminine: **તેની હાલત ગંભીર છે**, his condition is serious.
