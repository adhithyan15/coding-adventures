## You'll want to know: تاکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C388-SIGNAL388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تاکنا** — *tāknā* — "to gaze (at); to look out (at)".

> سارا کھڑکی سے کھیتوں کو تاکتی ہے۔
>
> *sārā khiṛkī se khetoṅ ko tāktī hai.*
>
> Sara gazes out of the window at the fields.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کھڑکی سے باہر تاکا, *us ne khiṛkī se bāhar tākā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تاکنا** (*tāknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *tāknā*, "to look for", under Sanskrit *tarkayati*, "guesses, thinks" (CDIAL 5716); the sense "to gaze at" probably grew from looking out for something.
