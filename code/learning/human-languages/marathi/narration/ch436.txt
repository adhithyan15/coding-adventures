Marathi, chapter 436: Heat and Humidity.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वातावरण (vātāvaraṇ) — the atmosphere; the weather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to dry up, then the Marathi for to go bad.

You'll want to know: वातावरण.
वातावरण — vātāvaraṇ — "the atmosphere; the weather".
आज वातावरण ढगाळ आहे. āj vātāvaraṇ ḍhagāḷ āhe. Today the weather is cloudy.
Neuter.
Also the mood of a place: घरात आनंदी वातावरण (vātāvaraṇ) होतं.

The word, taken apart.
वातावरण (vātāvaraṇ) is a modern word built from Sanskrit vāta, "wind, air", and āvaraṇa, "a covering" (Monier-Williams): the cover of air.

Lesson 2 of 5.
उष्णता (uṣṇatā) — great heat; heat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to go bad, then the Marathi for the atmosphere.

You'll want to know: उष्णता.
उष्णता — uṣṇatā — "great heat; heat".
दुपारी उष्णता खूप वाढते. dupārī uṣṇatā khūp vāḍhte. In the afternoon the heat rises a lot.
Feminine.
A formal word, also heat in science.

The word, taken apart.
उष्णता (uṣṇatā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit uṣṇatā, "heat", from uṣṇa, "hot" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
उकाडा (ukāḍā) — sultry heat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the atmosphere, then the Marathi for great heat.

You'll want to know: उकाडा.
उकाडा — ukāḍā — "sultry heat".
पावसाआधी खूप उकाडा होतो. pāvsāādhī khūp ukāḍā hoto. Before the rain it gets very sultry.
Masculine.
Heavy, sticky heat; उकाडा (ukāḍā) होणे.

The word, taken apart.
उकाडा (ukāḍā) is inherited, from the family of Sanskrit utkvathati, "boils out", where CDIAL lists Marathi ukaḍṇẽ, "to be hot (of weather)" (CDIAL 1741).

Lesson 4 of 5.
दमट (damaṭ) — humid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for great heat, then the Marathi for sultry heat.

You'll want to know: दमट.
दमट — damaṭ — "humid".
समुद्राजवळची हवा दमट असते. samudrājavaḷcī havā damaṭ aste. The air near the sea is humid.
It does not change: दमट (damaṭ) हवा, दमट (damaṭ) कपडे.

The word, taken apart.
दमट (damaṭ) is a Marathi adjective for damp, sticky air; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
तळपणे (taḷapṇe) — to blaze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for sultry heat, then the Marathi for humid.

You'll want to know: तळपणे.
तळपणे — taḷapṇe — "to blaze".
दुपारी सूर्य तळपत होता. dupārī sūrya taḷpat hotā. At noon the sun was blazing.
Past (he …): तो तळपला, to taḷaplā.
For the sun and for a bright sword.

The word, taken apart.
तळपणे (taḷapṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi taḷapṇẽ, "to be brandished", under taḍapphaḍ, "agitate" (CDIAL 5634).
