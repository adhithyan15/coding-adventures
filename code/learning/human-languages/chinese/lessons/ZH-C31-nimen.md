## You'll want to know: 你们
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C31-PEOPLE31-05]; assesses=[] -->

**你们** — *nǐmen* — "you all; you (more than one)".

> *nǐmen zěnmeyàng* — How are you all doing?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**你们** (*nǐmen*) is built from 你 'you' and the plural ending men. No dictionary we use records an older form of either, so its history is not settled.
