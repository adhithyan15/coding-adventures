# Return to a familiar question

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-YOUR-NAME-02, PA-LEX-KI-02] -->

[YOU HEAR: *tuhāḍā nāṁ kī hai?*] Say **ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਂ ਕੀ ਹੈ?**, "what is your name?"

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KI-02, PA-LEX-YATRI-202, PA-GRAMMAR-TUSI-HO-03] -->

In that question **ਕੀ** means "what", and it sits where the answer will go. You
also know **ਯਾਤਰੀ**, "a traveller", and **ਤੁਸੀਂ … ਹੋ**, "you are".

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-YOUR-NAME-02, PA-LEX-YATRI-202, PA-GRAMMAR-TUSI-HO-03] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *what is your name?*] Say **ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਂ ਕੀ ਹੈ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *a traveller*] Say **ਯਾਤਰੀ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *how are you?*] Say **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਹੋ?**

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KI-02, PA-LEX-YATRI-202] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *what*, then *a traveller*.
