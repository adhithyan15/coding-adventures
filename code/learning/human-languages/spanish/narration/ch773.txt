Lesson 1 of 5.
la cobertura — the signal, the coverage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to slip, then the Spanish for the compass.

You'll want to know: la cobertura.
la cobertura — "the signal, the coverage".
En el pueblo no hay cobertura. There is no signal in the village.
For an insurance policy it is the cover: la cobertura del seguro.

The word, taken apart.
cobertura comes from Late Latin coopertūra 'a covering' (EtymDB), from coopertus, the participle of cooperīre 'to cover over', the verb behind cubrir.

Te mando el enlace del billete. I'll send you the link to the ticket.
At a station or airport it is a connection: perder el enlace.

The word, taken apart.
enlace is built on enlazar 'to tie together' (EtymDB), which comes from Latin inlaqueāre, built on laqueus 'a noose' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
recargar — to top up; to recharge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the app, then the Spanish for the link.

You'll want to know: recargar.
recargar — "to top up; to recharge".
Tengo que recargar el móvil antes del viaje. I have to top up my phone before the trip.
It takes gu before e: recargué. It also means to recharge a battery.

The word, taken apart.
recargar is built on cargar 'to load, to charge' with re- 'again'. Cargar comes from Late Latin carricāre 'to load a cart', from carrus 'a cart' (Lewis & Short).
