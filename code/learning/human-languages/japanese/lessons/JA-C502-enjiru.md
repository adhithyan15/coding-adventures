## You'll want to know: えんじる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C502-STAGE502-05]; assesses=[] -->

**えんじる** — *enjiru* — "to play (a role); to perform".

> **かれは えいがで いしゃを えんじます。** — *kare wa eiga de isha o enjimasu* — "He plays a doctor in the film."

Polite **えんじます**, *enjimasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **やくしゃが いしゃを えんじました。**, *yakusha ga isha o enjimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんじる** (*enjiru*) mixes the two layers, native and Sino-Japanese: Sino-Japanese *en*, "performance" (Middle Chinese *yenX*), borrowed from Chinese and the native ending *jiru* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
