## You'll want to know: げか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C450-CLINIC450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**げか** — *geka* — "the surgery department; surgery".

> **けがを したので、げかに いきました。** — *kega o shita node, geka ni ikimashita* — "I hurt myself, so I went to the surgery department."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**げか** (*geka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ge*, "outside" (Middle Chinese *ngwajH*) and *ka*, "department" (*khwa*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
