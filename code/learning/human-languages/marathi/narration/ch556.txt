Marathi, chapter 556: Favourable or Not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अनुकूल (anukūl) — favourable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rush, then the Marathi for a scramble.

You'll want to know: अनुकूल.
अनुकूल — anukūl — "favourable".
हवामान अनुकूल असेल तर आम्ही उद्या निघू. havāmān anukūl asel tar āmhī udyā nighū. If the weather is favourable we will set out tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
अनुकूल (anukūl) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit anukūla, "following the bank or slope" (Monier-Williams), so going with the stream.

Lesson 3 of 5.
घोळ (ghoḷ) — a muddle; a mess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for favourable, then the Marathi for unfavourable.

You'll want to know: घोळ.
घोळ — ghoḷ — "a muddle; a mess".
तिकिटांच्या आरक्षणात मोठा घोळ झाला. tikiṭāñcyā ārakṣaṇāt moṭhā ghoḷ jhālā. There was a big muddle in the ticket booking.
Masculine.
घोळ (ghoḷ) होणे, things get into a muddle.

The word, taken apart.
घोळ (ghoḷ) is built on घोळणे, "to stir about"; CDIAL lists Marathi ghoḷṇẽ under Sanskrit ghōlayati, "mixes, stirs together" (CDIAL 4526).

Lesson 4 of 5.
बिकट (bikaṭ) — dire; critical.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for unfavourable, then the Marathi for a muddle.

You'll want to know: बिकट.
बिकट — bikaṭ — "dire; critical".
पुरामुळे गावातली परिस्थिती बिकट झाली. purāmuḷe gāvātlī paristhitī bikaṭ jhālī. Because of the flood, things in the village became dire.
Mostly of a situation or a road: बिकट (bikaṭ) परिस्थिती, बिकट (bikaṭ) वाट.

The word, taken apart.
बिकट (bikaṭ) is usually traced to Sanskrit vikaṭa, "dreadful, monstrous, of unusual size" (Monier-Williams), with the v- said as b-; CDIAL lists no Marathi बिकट, so the path is not settled.
