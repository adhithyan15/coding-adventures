## You'll want to know: l'atout
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C352-FREIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'atout** — "the asset, the strong point; the trump card".

> Parler trois langues est un vrai atout.
>
> *Speaking three languages is a real asset.*

In card games, *l'atout* is the trump suit. Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atout* is built from *à* and *tout* "all": a card that beats all the others. *tout* is inherited from Latin *tōtus* "whole" (Lewis & Short).
