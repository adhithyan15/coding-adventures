## You'll want to know: فروش رفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C462-HARAJ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فروش رفتن** (*forush raftan*) — "to sell (well), to be sold".

> همه بلیتها در یک روز فروش رفت.
>
> *Hame-ye bilit-hâ dar yek ruz forush raft.*
>
> All the tickets sold in one day.

*forush* 'sale' + *raftan* 'to go'. *Khub forush miravad* — it sells well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*forush raftan* is built from *forush* 'sale', the present stem of *forukhtan* 'to sell', usually traced to Middle Persian *frōxtan*, and the native verb *raftan* 'to go'.
