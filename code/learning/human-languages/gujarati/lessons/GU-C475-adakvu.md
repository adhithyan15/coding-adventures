## You'll want to know: અડકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C475-SENSES475-05]; assesses=[] -->

**અડકવું** — *aḍakvũ* — "to touch".

> ગરમ તપેલીને અડકશો નહીં.
>
> *garam tapelīne aḍakśo nahī̃.*
>
> Don't touch the hot pot.

Past (he …): તે ગરમ તપેલીને અડક્યો, *te garam tapelīne aḍakyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અડકવું** (*aḍakvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*aḍ-*, "obstruct, stop", which CDIAL connects with Dravidian; CDIAL lists the Gujarati *aṛakvũ*, "to touch" (CDIAL 187).
