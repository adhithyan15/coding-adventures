## You'll want to know: entfernen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C446-REINIGUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**entfernen** — "to remove".

> Die Reinigung entfernt den Fleck vom Kleid.
>
> *The dry cleaner removes the stain from the dress.*

Formal. *Entfernt* also means distant: *zwei Kilometer entfernt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entfernen* is inherited from Middle High German *entverren* (EtymDB), built on *fern* 'far': to put something far away.
