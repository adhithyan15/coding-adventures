## You'll want to know: दायः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C550-AMSA550-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दायः** — *dāyaḥ* — "an inheritance".

> यदि पितुः दायः अस्ति, तर्हि भ्रातरः तं वण्टयन्ति।
>
> *yadi pituḥ dāyaḥ asti, tarhi bhrātaraḥ taṃ vaṇṭayanti.*
>
> If there is an inheritance from their father, the brothers share it.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दायः** (*dāyaḥ*) comes from the root √dā, "to give", which also gives *dadāti*; MW gives *dāya* as a share, a portion, an inheritance (MW).
