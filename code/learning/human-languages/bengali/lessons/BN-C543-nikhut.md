## You'll want to know: নিখুঁত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C543-SIDDHI543-03]; assesses=[] -->

**নিখুঁত** — *nikhũt* — "flawless; perfect".

> দর্জি একটা নিখুঁত জামা বানাল।
>
> *Dôrji ekṭā nikhũt jāmā bānālô.*
>
> The tailor made a perfect shirt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নিখুঁত** (*nikhũt*) joins the prefix নি-, "without", to খুঁত, "a flaw"; where খুঁত comes from is not settled.
