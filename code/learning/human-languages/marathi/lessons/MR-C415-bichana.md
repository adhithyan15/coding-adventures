## You'll want to know: बिछाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C415-BICHANA415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बिछाना** — *bichānā* — "bedding; a bed".

> आईने पाहुण्यांसाठी बिछाना तयार केला.
>
> *āīne pāhuṇyāṁsāṭhī bichānā tayār kelā.*
>
> Mother got the bedding ready for the guests.

Masculine.

The mattress, sheet and pillow together, laid out for sleep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिछाना** (*bichānā*) is a loan, usually traced to Hindustani: CDIAL lists Hindi *bichānā*, "to spread", under *\*vicchādayati*, "spread out" (CDIAL 11692).
