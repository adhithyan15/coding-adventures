## You'll want to know: ਝੱਗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C227-DIN227-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝੱਗ** — *jhagg* — "lather; foam".

> ਸਾਬਣ ਦੀ ਝੱਗ ਚਿੱਟੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *sābaṇ dī jhagg chiṭṭī hai.*
>
> The soap lather is white.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝੱਗ** (*jhagg*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhaggā*, "froth, scum"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jhagg*, "foam, froth, scum" (CDIAL 5322).
