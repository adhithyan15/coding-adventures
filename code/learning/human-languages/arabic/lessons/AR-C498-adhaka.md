# أضحك (aḍḥaka) — to make laugh

## You'll want to know: أضحك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C498-ATHAR498-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أضحك** — *aḍḥaka* — "to make laugh".

> أضحكتنا قصة جدي.
>
> *aḍḥakatnā qiṣṣat jaddī.*
>
> My grandfather's story made us laugh.

Present: يضحك, *yuḍḥik*.

Muḍḥik, funny, is its participle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أضحك** (*aḍḥaka*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-ḥ-k*, "to laugh" (Lane). It is Form IV of ḍaḥika, to laugh: to make someone laugh.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
