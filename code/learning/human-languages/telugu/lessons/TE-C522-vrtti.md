## You'll want to know: వృత్తి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C522-VRTTI522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వృత్తి** — *vṛtti* — "a profession; a calling".

> మా నాన్నగారి వృత్తి వైద్యం.
>
> *mā nānnagāri vṛtti vaidyaṁ.*
>
> My father's profession is medicine.

Your calling: వృత్తిరీత్యా, by profession.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వృత్తి** (*vṛtti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vṛtti*, "mode of life, course of action" (Monier-Williams): the way you make your living.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
