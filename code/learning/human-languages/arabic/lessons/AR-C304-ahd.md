## You'll want to know: عهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C304-ZAMAN304-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عهد** — *ʿahd* — "a reign, an era; a pledge".

> بني الجسر في عهد الملك الأول.
>
> *buniya al-jisr fī ʿahd al-malik al-awwal.*
>
> The bridge was built in the reign of the first king.

Plural: عهود, *ʿuhūd*.

في عهد: in the days of, under. Also a promise: وفى بالعهد, he kept the pledge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عهد** (*ʿahd*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-h-d* (Lane), "to enjoin, to bind by a compact". A ruler's pledge marks his time, so *ʿahd* is also a reign.
