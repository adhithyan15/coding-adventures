## You'll want to know: l'égalité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C362-CITOYEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'égalité** — "the equality".

> Ils demandent l'égalité entre les hommes et les femmes.
>
> *They are asking for equality between men and women.*

In sport, *à égalité* — level, a draw. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*égalité* is built from *égal* "equal" and *-ité* (EtymDB). *égal* comes from Latin *aequālis* "equal", from *aequus* "level, even" (Lewis & Short).
