## You'll want to know: der Tümpel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C455-WASSERFALL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Tümpel** — "the small pond, the pool".

> Im Tümpel hinter dem Haus leben Frösche.
>
> *Frogs live in the little pond behind the house.*

Same form in the plural. Smaller than a *Teich*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tümpel* is inherited through Middle High German *tümpfel* from Old High German *tumphilo* 'pool' (EtymDB). EtymDB lists English *dimple* as a cousin.
