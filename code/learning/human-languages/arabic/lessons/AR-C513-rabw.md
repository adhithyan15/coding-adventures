## You'll want to know: ربو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C513-MUZMIN513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ربو** — *rabw* — "asthma".

> يحمل أخي دواء الربو في حقيبته.
>
> *yaḥmilu akhī dawāʾ ar-rabw fī ḥaqībatihi.*
>
> My brother carries his asthma medicine in his bag.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ربو** (*rabw*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-b-w*, "to grow; to be out of breath" (Lane). Lane gives rabw as being out of breath, panting after running.
