## You'll want to know: أطل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C443-MULAA443-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أطل** — *aṭalla* — "to overlook".

> تطل غرفتنا على البحر.
>
> *tuṭillu ghurfatunā ʿalā al-baḥr.*
>
> Our room overlooks the sea.

Present: يطل, *yuṭill*.

It takes *ʿalā*: *aṭalla ʿalā*, "to look out over".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أطل** (*aṭalla*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ṭ-l-l*, "to look down from above". It is Form IV of a doubled root.
