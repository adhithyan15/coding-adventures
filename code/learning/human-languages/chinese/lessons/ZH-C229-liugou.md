## You'll want to know: liùgǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C229-PET229-03]; assesses=[] -->

*liùgǒu* — "to walk the dog".

> *wǒ měitiān wǎnshang liùgǒu* — I walk the dog every evening.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liùgǒu* is built from *liù* 'to stroll' and *gǒu* 'dog'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *kuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'dog'; the dictionary glosses the first, *ljuw* there, 'to linger', and the falling tone of liù is not the regular outcome of that form.
