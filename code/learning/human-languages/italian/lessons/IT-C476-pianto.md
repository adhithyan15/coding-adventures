## You'll want to know: il pianto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C476-DELUSIONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il pianto** — "crying, tears".

> Dalla stanza si sentiva il pianto di un bambino.
>
> *From the room you could hear a child crying.*

The noun for *piangere*; *scoppiare in un pianto* — to burst into tears.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pianto* is inherited from Latin *planctus* "a beating of the breast in grief, a lament" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *plangere*.
