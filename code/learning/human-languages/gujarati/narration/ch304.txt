Gujarati, chapter 304: The Flood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
તણાવું (taṇāvũ) — to be swept away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a hut, then the Gujarati for to lay waste.

You'll want to know: તણાવું.
તણાવું — taṇāvũ — "to be swept away".
પૂરમાં અમારી ગાય તણાઈ ગઈ. pūrmā̃ amārī gāy taṇāī gaī. Our cow was swept away in the flood.
Past (it …): તે ઝૂંપડું તણાઈ ગયું, te jhū̃pḍũ taṇāī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
તણાવું (taṇāvũ) goes back to Sanskrit tanṓti, "stretches", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be dragged" (CDIAL 5659): a flood drags things along with it. તણાઈ ગયું, it was swept away.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ડુબાડવું (ḍubāḍvũ) — to submerge; to flood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to lay waste, then the Gujarati for to be swept away.

You'll want to know: ડુબાડવું.
ડુબાડવું — ḍubāḍvũ — "to submerge; to flood".
નદીએ આખું ગામ ડુબાડી દીધું. nadīe ākhũ gām ḍubāḍī dīdhũ. The river flooded the whole village.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે હોડી ડુબાડી, teṇe hoḍī ḍubāḍī.

The word, taken apart.
ડુબાડવું (ḍubāḍvũ) is the causative of ડૂબવું, to sink, from ḍubb, "sink", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5561): to make something go under. The long ū of ડૂબવું shortens in the longer verb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
નુકસાન (nuksān) — damage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be swept away, then the Gujarati for to submerge.

You'll want to know: નુકસાન.
નુકસાન — nuksān — "damage".
પૂરથી ગામને ખૂબ નુકસાન થયું. pūrthī gāmne khūb nuksān thayũ. The flood did the village a great deal of damage.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
નુકસાન (nuksān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic nuqṣān, "a decrease, a loss". Harm is done with થવું: નુકસાન થયું, there was damage.

Lesson 4 of 5.
આફત (āphat) — a calamity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to submerge, then the Gujarati for damage.

You'll want to know: આફત.
આફત — āphat — "a calamity".
ગામના લોકો કહે છે કે આ સૌથી મોટી આફત હતી. gāmnā loko kahe che ke ā sauthī moṭī āphat hatī. The villagers say that this was the worst calamity they have known.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આફત (āphat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic āfa, "a blight, a calamity". It is feminine: મોટી આફત, a great calamity; આફત આવી, disaster struck.

Lesson 5 of 5.
સંકટ (sankaṭ) — a crisis; danger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for damage, then the Gujarati for a calamity.

You'll want to know: સંકટ.
સંકટ — sankaṭ — "a crisis; danger".
પૂર પછી ગામમાં પાણીનું સંકટ હતું. pūr pachī gāmmā̃ pāṇīnũ sankaṭ hatũ. After the flood the village faced a water crisis.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સંકટ (sankaṭ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit saṁkaṭá, "narrow", and as a noun "a difficulty, a danger" (CDIAL 12817). It is neuter: મોટું સંકટ, a grave crisis.
