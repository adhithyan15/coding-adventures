# ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ (mādhyama) — the media; a medium

## You'll want to know: ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C329-MADHYAMA329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ** — *mādhyama* — "the media; a medium".

> ಮಾಧ್ಯಮಗಳು ಆ ಘಟನೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ದಿನವಿಡೀ ಮಾತಾಡಿದವು.
>
> *mādhyamagaḷu ā ghaṭaneya bagge dinaviḍī mātāḍidavu.*
>
> The media talked about that incident all day.

Also a medium of teaching: ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ ಶಾಲೆ, a Kannada-medium school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾಧ್ಯಮ** (*mādhyama*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mādhyama*, "relating to the middle, central" (Monier-Williams), from *madhya*, "middle" (CDIAL 9804); the sense "a medium, the media" is modern.
