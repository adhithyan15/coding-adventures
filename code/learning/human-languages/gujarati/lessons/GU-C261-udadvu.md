# ઉડાડવું (uḍāḍvũ) — to fly a kite, to make fly

## You'll want to know: ઉડાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C261-KITES261-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉડાડવું** — *uḍāḍvũ* — "to fly a kite, to make fly".

> **અમે બપોરે પતંગ ઉડાડ્યા.** — *ame bapore patang uḍāḍyā* — "We flew kites in the afternoon."

The causative of **ઊડવું**, *ūḍvũ*, to fly up: to make something fly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉડાડવું** (*uḍāḍvũ*), to make something fly, is a causative of the inherited *ūḍvũ*, "to fly up". CDIAL lists that verb, as *uḍvũ*, under Sanskrit *uḍḍīyatē*, "flies up", and *uḍāḍvũ* under its causative *uḍḍāpayati* (CDIAL 1697).
