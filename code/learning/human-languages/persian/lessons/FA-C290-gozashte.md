# گذشته (gozashte) — last year, last month, and the past itself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01, FA-LEX-C137-GOZASHTE137-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I went", then "we went". (*Raftam*, *raftim*.) Then the one
word for "last year". (*Pârsâl*.)

[PAUSE 1s] *Pârsâl* works only for the year. For last month and last week,
Persian has one word that fits them all.

## You'll want to know: گذشته
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C290-GOZASHTE-01]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-EZAFE-OWNER, FA-LEX-C64-ADJ64-05, FA-LEX-MAH, FA-LEX-C64-ADJ64-04] -->

**گذشته** — *gozashte* — "last", of a stretch of time, and "the past".

It follows its noun with the ezafe, the same *-e* as in *esm-e man*:

> **سال گذشته به تهران رفتم.** — *sâl-e gozashte be Tehrân raftam* — Last
> year I went to Tehran.

So *mâh-e gozashte* is "last month", and *hafte-ye gozashte* is "last week":
after a final vowel the ezafe is said *-ye*, and in plain print it is often
not written at all, **هفته گذشته**.

Standing alone it is a noun: **در گذشته**, *dar gozashte*, "in the past".

Say it in three beats, *go-zash-te*. Its second letter is one you have not
written yet. The next lesson takes it out and puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-RAFTAN] -->

*gozashte* is a past stem with *-e* on the end, the way Persian makes a
participle: **گذشت**, *gozasht*, from *gozashtan*, "to pass", and then *-e*:
"passed", the time that has gone by. *Rafte*, "gone", is built the same way
from *raft*.

EtymDB takes *gozashtan* back to a Middle Persian verb, written *wtštn'* in
Pahlavi letters, "to pass, to cross". It records no older parent, so no
English cousin is claimed here.
