Mandarin Chinese, chapter 499: The Team.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
qiúduì — a sports team (in a ball game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to shoot at goal, then the Mandarin for to score a goal.

You'll want to know: qiúduì.
qiúduì — "a sports team (in a ball game)".
zhè shì wǒmen xuéxiào zuì hǎo de qiúduì — This is our school's best team.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qiúduì is built from 球 'a ball' and duì 'a troop', both inherited from Middle Chinese, gjuw and dwojH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a band of people'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
xùnliàn — to do training (for a sport); to drill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a player in a ball game, then the Mandarin for a team captain.

You'll want to know: xùnliàn.
xùnliàn — "to do training (for a sport); to drill".
wǒmen měi tiān xùnliàn liǎng ge xiǎoshí — We train for two hours a day.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xùnliàn is built from xùn 'to instruct' and liàn 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, xjunH and lenH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to admonish' and the second 'white silk'; the sense 'to practise' is not given there.

Lesson 5 of 5.
传球 (chuánqiú) — to pass the ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a team captain, then the Mandarin for to do training (for a sport).

You'll want to know: 传球.
传球 — chuánqiú — "to pass the ball".
他传球很快 tā chuánqiú hěn kuài — He passes the ball quickly.

The word, taken apart.
传球 (chuánqiú) is built from 传 'to hand on' and 球 'a ball', both inherited from Middle Chinese, drjwen and gjuw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn'; the sense 'to pass on' it gives only under the other reading, drjwenH.
