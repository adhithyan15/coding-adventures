## You'll want to know: constipado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C733-GRIPE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**constipado** — "having a cold, bunged up".

> Estoy constipado y no puedo respirar bien.
>
> *I have a cold and cannot breathe properly.*

A false friend, mainly in Spain: *estar constipado* is to have a cold. Constipated is *estreñido*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*constipado* comes from Latin *cōnstīpātus* "crowded or pressed closely together" (EtymDB), from *stīpāre* "to pack": a head packed tight.
