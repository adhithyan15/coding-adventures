Japanese, chapter 327: Good Manners.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
れいぎ (reigi) — courtesy; manners.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a violation, then the Japanese for a punishment.

You'll want to know: れいぎ.
れいぎ — reigi — "courtesy; manners".
こどもに れいぎを おしえた ほうが いいです。 — kodomo ni reigi o oshieta hō ga ii desu — "You should teach children good manners."

The word, taken apart.
れいぎ (reigi) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are rei, "bow" (Middle Chinese lejX) and gi, "ceremony" (ngje) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

You'll want to know: おじぎ.
おじぎ — ojigi — "a bow (as a greeting)".

The word, taken apart.
