Gujarati, chapter 443: Trees.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઝાડ (jhāḍ) — a tree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a fishing net, then the Gujarati for to rock gently.

You'll want to know: ઝાડ.
ઝાડ — jhāḍ — "a tree".
અમારા આંગણામાં લીમડાનું મોટું ઝાડ છે. amārā ā̃gṇāmā̃ līmḍānũ moṭũ jhāḍ che. There is a big neem tree in our courtyard.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઝાડ (jhāḍ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit jhāṭa, "a thicket, an arbour"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a tree" (CDIAL 5362).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ફૂટવું (phūṭvũ) — to sprout; to burst out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a peepal, then the Gujarati for a mango tree.

You'll want to know: ફૂટવું.
ફૂટવું — phūṭvũ — "to sprout; to burst out".
વસંતમાં ઝાડ પર નવાં પાન ફૂટે છે. vasantmā̃ jhāḍ par navā̃ pān phūṭe che. In spring new leaves sprout on the trees.
Past (it …): તે પાન ફૂટ્યું, te pān phūṭyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ફૂટવું (phūṭvũ) goes back to sphuṭyati, "splits open", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to split, to burst" (CDIAL 13845): a bud that bursts into leaf.
