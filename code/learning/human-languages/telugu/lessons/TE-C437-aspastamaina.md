## You'll want to know: అస్పష్టమైన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C437-SANKLISTAM437-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అస్పష్టమైన** — *aspaṣṭamaina* — "vague; unclear".

> అతను ఒక అస్పష్టమైన సమాధానం ఇచ్చాడు.
>
> *atanu oka aspaṣṭamaina samādhānaṁ iccāḍu.*
>
> He gave a vague answer.

అ-, not, + స్పష్టమైన, clear, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అస్పష్టమైన** (*aspaṣṭamaina*) is built on Sanskrit *aspaṣṭa*, "indistinct" (Monier-Williams), with Telugu -మైన.
