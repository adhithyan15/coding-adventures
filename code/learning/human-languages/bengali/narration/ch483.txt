Bengali, chapter 483: In-Laws.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
জামাই (jāmāi) — a son-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a brother's daughter, then the Bengali for a child's father-in-law.

You'll want to know: জামাই.
জামাই — jāmāi — "a son-in-law".
জামাই এলে শাশুড়ি মাছ রান্না করেন। jāmāi ele shāshuṛi māchh rānnā kôren. When the son-in-law visits, his mother-in-law cooks fish.

The word, taken apart.
জামাই (jāmāi) comes from Sanskrit jāmātṛ, "a daughter's husband" (CDIAL 5198), which CDIAL lists in this Bengali form.

The word, taken apart.
ভাশুর (bhāshur) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under Sanskrit bhrātṛśvaśura, "brother-father-in-law" (CDIAL 9669): an elder brother of the husband, treated with a father-in-law's respect.
