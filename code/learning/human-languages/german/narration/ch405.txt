German, chapter 405: Kratzer und Dellen.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
der Kofferraum — the boot (of a car; US: trunk).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the jack, then the German for the warning triangle.

You'll want to know: der Kofferraum.
der Kofferraum — "the boot (of a car; US: trunk)".
Die Koffer passen nicht alle in den Kofferraum. The suitcases do not all fit in the boot.
Plural: die Kofferräume.

The word, taken apart.
Kofferraum is built from Koffer 'suitcase', borrowed from French coffre 'chest' (EtymDB), and Raum 'space', inherited from Old High German rūm (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

die Motorhaube — "the bonnet (of a car; US: hood)".
Der Mechaniker öffnet die Motorhaube und schaut lange hinein. The mechanic opens the bonnet and looks inside for a long time.

The word, taken apart.
Motorhaube is built from Motor 'engine', from Latin mōtor 'mover' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), and Haube 'cap, hood', an inherited Germanic word for a head covering.

Lesson 5 of 6.
verbeulen — to dent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the scratch, then the German for the dent.

You'll want to know: verbeulen.
verbeulen — "to dent".
Ein Ball hat die Tür von unserem Auto verbeult. A ball dented the door of our car.
Often as an adjective: ein verbeultes Auto — a dented car.

The word, taken apart.
verbeulen is built from ver- and Beule 'bump, dent', which is inherited through Middle High German biule from Old High German būlla (EtymDB).
