## You'll want to know: مقدار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C376-MIQDAR376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مقدار** — *miqdār* — "an amount; a quantity".

> اگر دوا کی مقدار زیادہ ہوئی تو نقصان ہو گا۔
>
> *agar davā kī miqdār zyādā huī to nuqsān ho gā.*
>
> If the dose is too large, it will do harm.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقدار** (*miqdār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *miqdār*, "a measure, an amount".
