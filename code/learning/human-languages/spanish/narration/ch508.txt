Spanish, chapter 508: Cómo es.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
increíble — incredible, unbelievable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the inhabitant, then the Spanish for the debate.

You'll want to know: increíble.
increíble — "incredible, unbelievable".
¡Es increíble! It is incredible!

The word, taken apart.
increíble from Latin incredibilis, "not to be believed" (EtymDB 2.1): in-, "not", and credibilis, from credere, "to believe" — the verb worn down in creer, "to believe".

You'll want to know: conocido.
conocido — "well known; an acquaintance".
Es un cantante muy conocido. He is a very well-known singer.
As a noun, un conocido is someone you know, not quite a friend.

The word, taken apart.
conocido is the participle of conocer, "to know", from Latin cognoscere, "to get to know" — inherited: the g before n fell away.

Lesson 4 of 5.
actual — current, present-day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for independent, then the Spanish for well known.

You'll want to know: actual.
actual — "current, present-day".
El precio actual es de diez euros. The current price is ten euros.
A false friend: actual in English is real.

The word, taken apart.
actual from Late Latin actualis, "active, practical" (EtymDB 2.1), from actus, "a doing" — a learned word. It means current, not English actual.
