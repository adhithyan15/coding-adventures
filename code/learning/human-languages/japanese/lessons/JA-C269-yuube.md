## You'll want to know: ゆうべ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C269-DOROBOU269-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうべ** — *yūbe* — "last night".

> **ゆうべ けいさつから でんわが ありました。** — *yūbe keisatsu kara denwa ga arimashita* — "Last night the police phoned us."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうべ** (*yūbe*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
