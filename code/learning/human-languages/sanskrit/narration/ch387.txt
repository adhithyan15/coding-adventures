Sanskrit, chapter 387: Getting Better.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उन्नतिः (unnatiḥ) — advancement; a rise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a rise in fortune, then the Sanskrit for prosperity.

You'll want to know: उन्नतिः.
उन्नतिः — unnatiḥ — "advancement; a rise".
यदि शिक्षा उत्तमा, तर्हि समाजस्य उन्नतिः भविष्यति। yadi śikṣā uttamā, tarhi samājasya unnatiḥ bhaviṣyati. If education is good, society will advance.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उन्नतिः (unnatiḥ) is built on ut, "up", and the root √nam, "to bend"; MW gives un-nati "rising, ascending" and "elevation, height", and "advancement" is how modern Sanskrit uses it (MW).

You'll want to know: दुर्गतिः.
दुर्गतिः — durgatiḥ — "hard times; misfortune".
यदि कार्यं न अस्ति, तर्हि ग्रामस्य दुर्गतिः भवति। yadi kāryaṃ na asti, tarhi grāmasya durgatiḥ bhavati. If there is no work, the village falls on hard times.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दुर्गतिः (durgatiḥ) is built from dur-, "bad, hard", and gati, "a going, a way", from the root √gam, "to go"; MW gives dur-gati "misfortune, distress, poverty" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: संस्करोति.
संस्करोति — saṃskaroti — "refines; improves".
यदि लेखकः लेखं संस्करोति, तर्हि सः उत्तमः भविष्यति। yadi lekhakaḥ lekhaṃ saṃskaroti, tarhi saḥ uttamaḥ bhaviṣyati. If the writer polishes his article, it will be excellent.
Past (he …): सः लेखं संस्कृतवान्, saḥ lekhaṃ saṃskṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
संस्करोति (saṃskaroti) is built on sam, "together", and the root √kṛ, "to make", with an inserted s; MW gives saṃ-s-√kṛ "to put together, to prepare, to refine" and saṃ-skṛta "perfected" (MW).
