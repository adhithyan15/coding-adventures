## You'll want to know: disdire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C431-CAPARRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**disdire** — "to cancel (a booking)".

> Abbiamo dovuto disdire la prenotazione.
>
> *We had to cancel the booking.*

Conjugated like *dire*: *ho disdetto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disdire* is built from *dis-* 'un-' and *dire* 'to say', which is inherited from Latin *dīcere* (Lewis & Short): to unsay what was agreed.
