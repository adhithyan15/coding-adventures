## You'll want to know: faciēs, faciēī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C515-VULT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**faciēs, faciēī** — "appearance, shape; face".

> Puer faciem in aquā lavat.
>
> *The boy washes his face in the water.*

Feminine, fifth declension. The shape and look of a face, or of anything; *vultus* is the face as it shows feeling. English *face*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**faciēs** is usually traced to the root of *faciō* "to make" (EtymDB): the make of a thing, its shape; Lewis & Short links it with *fax* "torch" instead. English *face* and *surface* come from it.
