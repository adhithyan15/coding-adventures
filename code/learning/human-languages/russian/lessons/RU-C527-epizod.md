## You'll want to know: эпизод
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C527-SERIES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**эпизод** (*epizód*) — "an episode".

> Я посмотрел три эпизода подряд.
>
> *Ya posmotrél tri epizóda podryád.*
>
> I watched three episodes in a row.

Masculine. Also a short scene or incident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *эпизод* without a source; it is usually traced through French *épisode* to Greek *epeisódion* 'an added scene'.
