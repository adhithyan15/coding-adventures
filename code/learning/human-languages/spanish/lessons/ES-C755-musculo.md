## You'll want to know: el músculo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C755-ENTRENAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el músculo** — "the muscle".

> Me duelen los músculos después del gimnasio.
>
> *My muscles ache after the gym.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*músculo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mūsculus* "little mouse, muscle" (EtymDB), from *mūs* "mouse"; a moving muscle looked like a mouse under the skin.
