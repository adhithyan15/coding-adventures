## You'll want to know: ഉലയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C476-KATATTU476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉലയുക** — *ulayuka* — "to toss about; to sway".

> ശക്തമായ കാറ്റിൽ ചെറിയ വള്ളം ഉലഞ്ഞു.
>
> *śaktamāya kāṟṟil ceṟiya vaḷḷaṁ ulaññu.*
>
> In the strong wind the little boat tossed about.

Past: ഉലഞ്ഞു, *ulaññu*.

The past is ഉലഞ്ഞു: verbs in -അയുക make the past in -അഞ്ഞു.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉലയുക** (*ulayuka*) is native Dravidian, "to be agitated, to shake" (DEDR 1003).
