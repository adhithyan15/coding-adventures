## You'll want to know: गर्भवती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C497-JANM497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गर्भवती** — *garbhvatī* — "pregnant".

> मेरी भाभी गर्भवती हैं, इसलिए भारी सामान नहीं उठातीं।
>
> *merī bhābhī garbhvatī haiṁ, isliye bhārī sāmān nahīṃ uṭhātīṃ.*
>
> My sister-in-law is pregnant, so she doesn't lift heavy things.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गर्भवती** (*garbhvatī*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *garbhavatī*, "pregnant", built on *garbha*, "womb", and *-vatī*, "having" (Monier-Williams).
