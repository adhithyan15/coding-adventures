## You'll want to know: гипотеза
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C371-GIPOTEZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**гипотеза** (*gipóteza*) — "a hypothesis".

> Учёные проверили эту гипотезу.
>
> *Uchyónyye provérili étu gipótezu.*
>
> The scientists tested this hypothesis.

Feminine. *Выдвинуть гипотезу* — to put forward a hypothesis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**гипотеза** comes from Latin *hypothesis*, from Greek *hypóthesis* "the basis of an argument, a supposition" (EtymDB), built from *hypó* "under" and *thésis* "a placing".
