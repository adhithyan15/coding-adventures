## You'll want to know: نقل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C299-ATHNA299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نقل** — *naqala* — "to pass on, to report; to carry".

> نقلت الصحيفة كلام المدير.
>
> *naqalat aṣ-ṣaḥīfa kalām al-mudīr.*
>
> The newspaper reported what the manager said.

Present: ينقل, *yanqul*.

It also moves people and goods: نقل الأثاث, to move the furniture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقل** (*naqala*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-l* (Lane), "to move a thing from one place to another". Words moved from one mouth to another are reported.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
