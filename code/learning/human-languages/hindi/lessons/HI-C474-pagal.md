## You'll want to know: पागल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C474-AQL474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पागल** — *pāgal* — "mad; crazy".

> क्या तुम पागल हो गए हो?
>
> *kyā tum pāgal ho gae ho?*
>
> Have you gone mad?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पागल** (*pāgal*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *pāgal*, "mad", under *\*paggala*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 7643).
