## You'll want to know: तारुण्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C498-VAY498-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तारुण्य** — *tāruṇya* — "youth".

> तारुण्यात त्याने खूप प्रवास केला.
>
> *tāruṇyāt tyāne khūp pravās kelā.*
>
> He travelled a lot in his youth.

Neuter.

From तरुण, young.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तारुण्य** (*tāruṇya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tāruṇya*, "youth, youthfulness" (Monier-Williams).
