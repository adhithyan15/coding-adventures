## You'll want to know: きかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C349-YOSOU349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きかい** — *kikai* — "an opportunity; a chance".

> **きかいが あったら、日本に いきたいです。** — *kikai ga attara, nihon ni ikitai desu* — "If I get the chance, I want to go to Japan."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きかい** (*kikai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "opportunity" (Middle Chinese *kj+j*) and *kai*, "meeting" (*hwajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
