## You'll want to know: поручать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C359-OBYAZANNOSTI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**поручать** (*poruchát*) — "to entrust, to put in charge of".

> Ему часто поручают самые трудные задачи.
>
> *Yemú chásto porucháyut sámyye trúdnyye zadáchi.*
>
> He is often given the hardest tasks.

Imperfective; perfective *поручить* (*поручу, поручишь*). *Поручать кому что*, or + infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**поручать** "to commission, to entrust" is built on *ручать* "to vouch, to guarantee", from *рука* "hand" (EtymDB). To entrust a task is to put it into someone’s hands.
