## You'll want to know: فاصلہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C397-FASLA397-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فاصلہ** — *fāslā* — "a distance".

> اسٹیشن سے ہوٹل کا فاصلہ کم ہے۔
>
> *isṭeshan se hoṭal kā fāslā kam hai.*
>
> It isn't far from the station to the hotel.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فاصلہ** (*fāslā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fāṣila*, "an interval, a space between".
