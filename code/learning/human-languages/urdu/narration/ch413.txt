Urdu, chapter 413: Houses Large and Small.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کوٹھی (koṭhī) — a large house; a bungalow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the countryside, then the Urdu for next door.

You'll want to know: کوٹھی.
کوٹھی — koṭhī — "a large house; a bungalow".
امیر لوگ بڑی کوٹھیوں میں رہتے ہیں۔ amīr log baṛī koṭhiyoṅ meṅ rahte haiṅ. Rich people live in big houses.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
کوٹھی (koṭhī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin koṭhī, "a granary, a large house", under Sanskrit kóṣṭha, "a storeroom" (CDIAL 3546).

Lesson 5 of 5.
بسانا (basānā) — to found (a town); to settle (people).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a compound, then the Urdu for a thatched roof.

You'll want to know: بسانا.
بسانا — basānā — "to found (a town); to settle (people)".
کسانوں نے دریا کے پاس ایک بستی بسائی۔ kisānoṅ ne daryā ke pās ek bastī basāī. Farmers founded a settlement near the river.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نیا شہر بسایا, us ne nayā shahr basāyā.

The word, taken apart.
بسانا (basānā) is built on basnā, "to dwell", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit vásati, "dwells" (CDIAL 11435): it is the form for making people settle; بستی, "a settlement", belongs to the same family.
