## You'll want to know: ਨਕਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C257-BANK257-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਕਦ** — *nakad* — "in cash".

> ਮੈਂ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਨਕਦ ਪੈਸੇ ਦਿੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ dukān utte nakad paise dindā hāṁ.*
>
> I pay in cash at the shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਕਦ** (*nakad*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *naqd*, "ready money".
