## You'll want to know: brigar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C497-SEPARACAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**brigar** — "to quarrel, to fight".

> Os irmãos brigam por tudo.
>
> *The brothers fight over everything.*

Very common in Brazil; in Portugal *zangar-se* and *discutir* are frequent too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB traces *brigar* to Gothic *brikan* "to break"; other accounts suggest other sources, so the origin is not fully settled.
