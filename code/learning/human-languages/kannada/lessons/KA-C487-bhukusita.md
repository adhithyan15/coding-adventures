## You'll want to know: ಭೂಕುಸಿತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C487-CANDAMARUTA487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭೂಕುಸಿತ** — *bhūkusita* — "a landslide".

> ಘಾಟಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಭೂಕುಸಿತದಿಂದ ರಸ್ತೆ ಮುಚ್ಚಿತು.
>
> *ghāṭiyalli bhūkusitadinda raste muccitu.*
>
> The ghat road was closed by a landslide.

ಭೂ, earth, plus ಕುಸಿತ, a sinking or collapse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭೂಕುಸಿತ** (*bhūkusita*) joins Sanskrit *bhū*, "the earth, the ground" (CDIAL 9546), and native *kusita*, "a collapse", from *kusi*, "to sink, to give way" (DEDR 1636).
