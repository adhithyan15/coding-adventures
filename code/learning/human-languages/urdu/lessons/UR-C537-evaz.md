## You'll want to know: عوض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C537-MUTABADIL537-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عوض** — *evaz* — "something in exchange (for)".

> اگر قلم چاہیے تو اس کے عوض اپنی کتاب دیجیے۔
>
> *agar qalam chāhiye to us ke evaz apnī kitāb dījiye.*
>
> If you want the pen, give me your book in exchange.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عوض** (*evaz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿiwaḍ*, "a substitute, an exchange".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
