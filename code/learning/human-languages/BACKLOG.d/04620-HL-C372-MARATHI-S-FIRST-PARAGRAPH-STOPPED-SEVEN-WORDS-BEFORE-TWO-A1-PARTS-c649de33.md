## HL-C372 — Marathi's first paragraph stopped seven words before two A1 parts

After the Latin and Italian reading PRs merged, `report:reading-reach` left four
measurable A1 tracks short of their next rung. Marathi was the smallest gap:
its 83-word passage already cleared the 70-word part, while both remaining parts
begin at 90. Hindi needs twelve words to make the same jump; Punjabi needs 27 to
reach its first A1 part; Marwari needs 47.

The eight-word line **माझी बहीण पण हुशार आहे आणि खूप वाचते** is not padding.
It restores a family member already taught in chapter 18, makes the three-gender
possessive agreement from chapter 9 carry meaning on the page, and uses only
forms already present before the reading chapter.

The resulting 91-word passage moves Marathi from **1/3 to 3/3** published A1
reading parts while preserving **3/3** at pre-A1. The remaining ranked reading
gaps are now Hindi (+12 words for two parts), Punjabi (+27 for one), and Marwari
(+47 for one), before the much larger Spanish jump from 61 to 150.
