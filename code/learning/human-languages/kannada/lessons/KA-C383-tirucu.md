# ತಿರುಚು (tirucu) — to twist; to distort

## You'll want to know: ತಿರುಚು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C383-SANDEHA383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಿರುಚು** — *tirucu* — "to twist; to distort".

> ಆ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ ಅವರ ಮಾತನ್ನು ತಿರುಚಿತು.
>
> *ā patrike avara mātannu tirucitu.*
>
> That newspaper twisted their words.

Past (I …): ತಿರುಚಿದೆ, *tirucide*.

Of facts, words and limbs: ಕೈ ತಿರುಚು, to twist an arm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಿರುಚು** (*tirucu*) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled: DEDR does not list it.
