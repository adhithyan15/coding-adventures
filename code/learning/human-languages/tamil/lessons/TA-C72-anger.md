## You'll want to know: கோபம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C72-FEEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கோபம்** (*kōpam*) — "anger".

Anger — the ordinary everyday word for it, not a literary one.

You can read all of it already, and one part of it you only just learned: **கோ** is க with the
◌ோ sign, the sign whose free-standing letter ஓ you met last chapter.

It ends in **ம்**, like வணக்கம், தூக்கம் and பணம் before it. That ending is now four words
deep, and it is a good bet for a Tamil noun.
