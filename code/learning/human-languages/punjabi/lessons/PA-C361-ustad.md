## You'll want to know: ਉਸਤਾਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C361-COLLEGE361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਸਤਾਦ** — *ustād* — "a master of an art; a teacher of a craft".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਉਸਤਾਦ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤਬਲਾ ਸਿਖਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā ustād mainū̃ tablā sikhāundā hai.*
>
> My master teaches me the tabla.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਸਤਾਦ** (*ustād*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *ustād*, "master".
