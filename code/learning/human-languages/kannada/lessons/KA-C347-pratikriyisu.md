## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C347-GOSHTHI347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸು** — *pratikriyisu* — "to give a reaction; to respond".

> ಆ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಗೆ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿ ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *ā praśnege adhikāri pratikriyisalilla.*
>
> The officer did not respond to that question.

Past (I …): ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸಿದೆ, *pratikriyiside*.

What one responds to takes -ಗೆ. The noun is ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯೆ, a response.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸು** (*pratikriyisu*) is built on Sanskrit *pratikriyā*, "requital, counteraction" (Monier-Williams), from *prati* "back, in return" and *kriyā* "an act", with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
