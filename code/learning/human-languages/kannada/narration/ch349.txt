Kannada, chapter 349: Swords, Arrows and Armour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಖಡ್ಗ (khaḍga) — a sword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a secret, then the Kannada for a sorcerer.

You'll want to know: ಖಡ್ಗ.
ಖಡ್ಗ — khaḍga — "a sword".
ಆ ಯೋಧ ಖಡ್ಗ ಹಿಡಿದು ಮುಂದೆ ನಡೆದನು. ā yōdha khaḍga hiḍidu munde naḍedanu. That warrior walked ahead, sword in hand.
Plural: ಖಡ್ಗಗಳು, khaḍgagaḷu.
The everyday word is ಕತ್ತಿ; ಖಡ್ಗ (khaḍga) belongs to stories and history.

The word, taken apart.
ಖಡ್ಗ (khaḍga) is borrowed from Sanskrit khaḍga, "a sword, a scimitar" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an arrow, then the Kannada for armour.

You'll want to know: ಸಾರಥಿ.
ಸಾರಥಿ — sārathi — "a charioteer".
ಸಾರಥಿ ರಥವನ್ನು ವೇಗವಾಗಿ ಓಡಿಸಿದನು. sārathi rathavannu vēgavāgi ōḍisidanu. The charioteer drove the chariot fast.
Plural: ಸಾರಥಿಗಳು, sārathigaḷu.
Today also a leader who steers a group: ತಂಡದ ಸಾರಥಿ (sārathi).

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾರಥಿ (sārathi) is borrowed from Sanskrit sārathi, "a charioteer, the driver of a car" (Monier-Williams), from sa-ratha, "with the chariot".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮಡಿ (maḍi) — to fall in battle; to die.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for armour, then the Kannada for a charioteer.

You'll want to know: ಮಡಿ.
ಮಡಿ — maḍi — "to fall in battle; to die".
ಆ ಯುದ್ಧದಲ್ಲಿ ಸಾವಿರಾರು ಸೈನಿಕರು ಮಡಿದರು. ā yuddhadalli sāvirāru sainikaru maḍidaru. Thousands of soldiers fell in that war.
Past (I …): ಮಡಿದೆ, maḍide.
A respectful verb for dying, used of soldiers and heroes. A different ಮಡಿ (maḍi) means ritual cleanness.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಡಿ (maḍi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4653 lists Kannada maḍi, "to die", and maḍuhu, "to kill".
