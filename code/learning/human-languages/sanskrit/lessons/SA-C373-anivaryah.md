## You'll want to know: अनिवार्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C373-ANUMATI373-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अनिवार्यः** — *anivāryaḥ* — "compulsory; unavoidable".

> यदि परीक्षा अनिवार्या अस्ति, तर्हि अहं पठिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi parīkṣā anivāryā asti, tarhi ahaṃ paṭhiṣyāmi.*
>
> If the exam is compulsory, I will study.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनिवार्यः** (*anivāryaḥ*) is built from *a-*, "not", and *nivārya*, "to be warded off"; MW gives *a-nivārya* "not to be warded off, unavoidable", and "compulsory" is its modern sense (MW).
