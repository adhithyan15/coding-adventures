# सख्खा (sakkhā) — full-blood (of a sibling)

## You'll want to know: सख्खा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C494-NATE494-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सख्खा** — *sakkhā* — "full-blood (of a sibling)".

> अरुण माझा सख्खा भाऊ आहे.
>
> *aruṇ mājhā sakkhā bhāū āhe.*
>
> Arun is my own brother.

Agrees: सख्खा भाऊ, सख्खी बहीण. Also close: सख्खे मित्र.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सख्खा** (*sakkhā*) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
