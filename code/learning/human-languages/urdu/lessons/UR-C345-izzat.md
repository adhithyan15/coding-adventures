## You'll want to know: عزت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C345-QADR345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عزت** — *izzat* — "honour; respect".

> محلے میں اس کی بڑی عزت ہے۔
>
> *mahalle meṅ us kī baṛī izzat hai.*
>
> He is greatly respected in the neighbourhood.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عزت** (*izzat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿizza*, "might, honour".
