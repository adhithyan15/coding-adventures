## You'll want to know: فارغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C527-MASRUF527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فارغ** — *fārigh* — "free (not busy)".

> اگر میں فارغ ہوتا تو آپ کے ساتھ جاتا۔
>
> *agar maiṅ fārigh hotā to āp ke sāth jātā.*
>
> If I were free, I would go with you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فارغ** (*fārigh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fārigh*, "empty, at leisure".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
