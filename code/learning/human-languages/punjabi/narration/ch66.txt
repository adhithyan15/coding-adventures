Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪਨੀਰ (panīr) — cheese.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for salt, then the Punjabi for butter.

You'll want to know: ਪਨੀਰ.
ਪਨੀਰ — panīr — "cheese".

The word, taken apart.
ਪਨੀਰ (panīr) is a loan, usually traced to Persian panīr, "cheese".

ਸ਼ਹਿਦ — shahid — "honey".

The word, taken apart.
ਸ਼ਹਿਦ (shahid) is a loan from Arabic shahd, "honey", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is sh-h-d (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਨਿੰਬੂ (nimbū) — a lemon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for grapes, then the Punjabi for an orange.

You'll want to know: ਨਿੰਬੂ.
ਨਿੰਬੂ — nimbū — "a lemon".

The word, taken apart.
ਨਿੰਬੂ (nimbū) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nimbū, "the lime" (CDIAL 7247). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word itself from Austroasiatic.
