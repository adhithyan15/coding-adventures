## You'll want to know: ओढ़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C494-DEKHBHAL494-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढ़ाना** — *oṛhānā* — "to cover someone (with a blanket)".

> माँ ने बच्चे को कंबल ओढ़ाया।
>
> *māṁ ne bacce ko kambal oṛhāyā.*
>
> Mother tucked a blanket round the child.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कंबल ओढ़ाया, *usne kambal oṛhāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढ़ाना** (*oṛhānā*) is the causative partner of *oṛhnā*, "to cover oneself", which CDIAL lists under *\*ōḍḍh*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2547): to wrap someone else up.
