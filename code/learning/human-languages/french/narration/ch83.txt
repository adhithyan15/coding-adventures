French, chapter 83: Sun, Star, Cloud, Rain, Snow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le soleil — the sun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a beach, then the French for an island.

You'll want to know: le soleil.
le soleil — "the sun".

The word, taken apart.
soleil from Vulgar Latin soliculus, "a little sun", from Latin sol, "sun" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
