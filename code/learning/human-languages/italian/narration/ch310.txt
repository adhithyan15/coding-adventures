Italian, chapter 310: Cambiare città.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
convivere — to live together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the generation, then the Italian for to hand down.

You'll want to know: convivere.
convivere — "to live together".
Convivono da tre anni. They have been living together for three years.
Like vivere: hanno convissuto.

The word, taken apart.
convivere is inherited from Latin convīvere "to live together" (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: integrarsi.
integrarsi — "to fit in, to integrate".
Si è integrato bene nel nuovo paese. He has fitted in well in his new country.
With essere: si è integrato.

The word, taken apart.
integrarsi is built on integrare "to make whole, to complete", a learned borrowing from Latin integrāre, from integer "whole, untouched" (Lewis & Short).

ambientarsi — "to settle in, to adapt (to new surroundings)".
I bambini si sono ambientati subito nella nuova scuola. The children settled in at the new school straight away.
Built on ambiente, ‘surroundings’.

ambientarsi is built on ambiente "surroundings", from Latin ambiēns "going round", from ambīre (Lewis & Short).
