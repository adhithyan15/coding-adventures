## You'll want to know: छंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C528-CHHAND528-01]; assesses=[] -->

**छंद** — *chand* — "a hobby".

> माझ्या बहिणीला नाणी गोळा करण्याचा छंद आहे.
>
> *mājhyā bahiṇīlā nāṇī goḷā karṇyācā chand āhe.*
>
> My sister's hobby is collecting coins.

Masculine.

The person takes -ला: तिला छंद आहे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छंद** (*chand*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *chanda*, "pleasure, wish" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
