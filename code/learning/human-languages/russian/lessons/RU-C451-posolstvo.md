## You'll want to know: посольство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C451-POSOLSTVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**посольство** (*posólstvo*) — "an embassy".

> Мы пошли в посольство за визой.
>
> *My poshlí v posólstvo za vízoy.*
>
> We went to the embassy for a visa.

Neuter. Built on *посол* — an ambassador.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*посольство* is built on *посол* 'an envoy, an ambassador' with *-ство*. *Посол* goes with *посылать* 'to send': one who is sent. EtymDB lists *посольство* without a deeper source.
