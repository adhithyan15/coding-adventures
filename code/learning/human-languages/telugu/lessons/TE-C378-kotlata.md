## You'll want to know: కొట్లాట
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C378-KOTLATA378-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కొట్లాట** — *koṭlāṭa* — "a brawl; a scuffle".

> బజారులో ఇద్దరి మధ్య కొట్లాట జరిగింది.
>
> *bajārulō iddari madhya koṭlāṭa jarigindi.*
>
> There was a brawl between two men in the market.

Plural: కొట్లాటలు, *koṭlāṭalu*.

From కొట్టు, to hit: a fight with blows.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కొట్లాట** (*koṭlāṭa*) is native Dravidian, built on *koṭṭu*, "to beat, strike" (DEDR 2063).

## What you've built
