## You'll want to know: きんようび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C164-WEEK164-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きんようび** — *kin'yōbi* — "Friday".

> **きんようびに うちに かえります。** — *kin'yōbi ni uchi ni kaerimasu* — "I will go home on Friday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きんようび** (*kin'yōbi*) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese *kin*, "gold, metal" (Middle Chinese *kim*), and *yō*, "planet, weekday" (*yewH*), borrowed from Chinese, with native *hi*, "day", voiced to *bi* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
