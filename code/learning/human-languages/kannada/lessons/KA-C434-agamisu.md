# ಆಗಮಿಸು (āgamisu) — to arrive

## You'll want to know: ಆಗಮಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C434-VILAMBA434-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಗಮಿಸು** — *āgamisu* — "to arrive".

> ವಿಮಾನ ಸಮಯಕ್ಕೆ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ಆಗಮಿಸಿತು.
>
> *vimāna samayakke sariyāgi āgamisitu.*
>
> The plane arrived right on time.

Past (I …): ಆಗಮಿಸಿದೆ, *āgamiside*.

A formal verb of notices and announcements; in speech people say ಬಂದಿತು, it came.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಗಮಿಸು** (*āgamisu*) is built from Sanskrit *āgama*, "coming, arrival" (Monier-Williams), from *ā-* "towards" and *gam* "to go", with the Kannada ending -*isu* that makes a verb of a Sanskrit noun.
