## You'll want to know: ಗುಲಾಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C352-DANGE352-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುಲಾಮ** — *gulāma* — "a slave".

> ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆ ಗುಲಾಮ ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಸ್ವತಂತ್ರನಾದನು.
>
> *katheyalli ā gulāma konege svatantranādanu.*
>
> In the story the slave became free at last.

Plural: ಗುಲಾಮರು, *gulāmaru*.

ಗುಲಾಮಗಿರಿ is slavery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುಲಾಮ** (*gulāma*) is a loan from Arabic *ġulām*, "a boy, a servant, a slave", which came through Persian and Hindustani.
