Lesson 4 of 5.
జిగురు (jiguru) — glue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for pliers, then the Telugu for a ladder.

You'll want to know: జిగురు.
జిగురు — jiguru — "glue".

The word, taken apart.
జిగురు (jiguru) is native Dravidian. DEDR 2488 lists jiguru, "gum, birdlime", with Kannada jigi, "gum, stickiness". DEDR compares Sanskrit cikka, "bird-lime" (CDIAL 4780), which CDIAL reports as taken from Dravidian, or else from Munda.

Lesson 5 of 5.
కొలబద్ద (kolabadda) — a ruler, a measuring stick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: కొలబద్ద.
కొలబద్ద — kolabadda — "a ruler, a measuring stick".

The word, taken apart.
కొలబద్ద (kolabadda) is native Dravidian: kola, "measurement" (DEDR 1827), and badda, "a thin strip, piece" (DEDR 3962): "measuring strip".
