# ತಲೆಮಾರು (talemāru) — a family generation

## You'll want to know: ತಲೆಮಾರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C341-VAMSHA341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲೆಮಾರು** — *talemāru* — "a family generation".

> ಈ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾಲ್ಕು ತಲೆಮಾರಿನವರು ಒಟ್ಟಿಗೆ ಇದ್ದರು.
>
> *ī maneyalli nālku talemārinavaru oṭṭige iddaru.*
>
> Four generations lived together in this house.

Plural: ತಲೆಮಾರುಗಳು, *talemārugaḷu*.

Grandparents, parents and children are three ತಲೆಮಾರು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲೆಮಾರು** (*talemāru*) is built on native *tale*, "head, top" (DEDR 3103); the origin of the second part, -ಮಾರು, is not settled.
