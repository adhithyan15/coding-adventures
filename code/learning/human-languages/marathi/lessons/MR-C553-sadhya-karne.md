# साध्य करणे (sādhya karṇe) — to achieve

## You'll want to know: साध्य करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C553-DHYEY553-01]; assesses=[] -->

**साध्य करणे** — *sādhya karṇe* — "to achieve".

> खूप कष्ट करून तिने तिचं ध्येय साध्य केलं.
>
> *khūp kaṣṭa karūn tine ticaṁ dhyey sādhya kelaṁ.*
>
> With much hard work she achieved her goal.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ध्येय साध्य केलं, *tyāne dhyey sādhya kelaṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साध्य करणे** (*sādhya karṇe*) is the tatsama साध्य, from Sanskrit *sādhya*, "to be accomplished" (Monier-Williams), with करणे.
