## You'll want to know: تندباد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C475-SAEGHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تندباد** (*tondbâd*) — "a gale, a strong gust of wind".

> تندباد چند درخت را انداخت.
>
> *Tondbâd chand derakht râ andâkht.*
>
> The gale blew down several trees.

*tond* 'fast, strong' + *bâd* 'wind'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tondbâd* is a native compound of *tond* 'fast, violent', usually traced to Middle Persian *tund*, and *bâd* 'wind', usually traced to Middle Persian *wād*.
