# નાગરિક (nāgrik) — a citizen

## You'll want to know: નાગરિક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C353-MATDAN353-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નાગરિક** — *nāgrik* — "a citizen".

> દરેક નાગરિકને મત આપવાનો હક છે.
>
> *darek nāgrikne mat āpvāno hak che.*
>
> Every citizen has the right to vote.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નાગરિક** (*nāgrik*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nāgarika*, "living in a town, civic" (Monier-Williams).
