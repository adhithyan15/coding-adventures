## You'll want to know: a editora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C525-BIBLIOTECA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a editora** — "publisher, publishing house".

> A editora vai publicar o livro em maio.
>
> *The publisher is going to bring out the book in May.*

Built on *editar* "to publish".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*editora* is built on *editar* "to publish", which came through French *éditer* (EtymDB) from Latin *ēdere* "to give out, to put forth" (Lewis & Short).
