## You'll want to know: bifurquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C400-PERIPH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bifurquer** — "to turn off, to fork".

> Au village, la route bifurque vers la montagne.
>
> *At the village the road forks towards the mountains.*

*La bifurcation* — the fork in the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bifurquer* is a learned formation on Latin *bifurcus* 'with two prongs', from *bi-* 'two' and *furca* 'fork' (Lewis & Short).
