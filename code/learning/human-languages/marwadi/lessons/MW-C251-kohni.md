## You'll want to know: कोहणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C251-KANDHO251-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कोहणी** — *kohṇī* — "an elbow".

> म्हारी कोहणी माथै घाव है।
>
> *mhārī kohṇī māthai ghāv hai.*
>
> There is a wound on my elbow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोहणी** (*kohṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kaphoṇi*, "elbow". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *kohnī* (CDIAL 2757).
