# అపాయం (apāyaṁ) — a danger; a hazard

## You'll want to know: అపాయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C421-APAYAM421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అపాయం** — *apāyaṁ* — "a danger; a hazard".

> వరదల సమయంలో నదిలోకి వెళ్ళడం అపాయం.
>
> *varadala samayaṁlō nadilōki veḷḷaḍaṁ apāyaṁ.*
>
> Going into the river during floods is dangerous.

ప్రమాదం, which you know, is an accident or a danger; అపాయం is the danger itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అపాయం** (*apāyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *apāya*, "departure, death, misfortune" (CDIAL 481).
