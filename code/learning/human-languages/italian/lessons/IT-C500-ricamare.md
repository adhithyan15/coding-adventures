## You'll want to know: ricamare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C500-CUCITO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ricamare** — "to embroider".

> Ha ricamato il suo nome sul fazzoletto.
>
> *She embroidered her name on the handkerchief.*

The noun is *il ricamo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ricamare* comes from Arabic *raqama* "to mark, to embroider" (EtymDB lists *raqm* "a mark"), probably through Medieval Latin.
