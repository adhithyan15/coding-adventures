## You'll want to know: der Kofferraum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C405-DELLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kofferraum** — "the boot (of a car; US: trunk)".

> Die Koffer passen nicht alle in den Kofferraum.
>
> *The suitcases do not all fit in the boot.*

Plural: *die Kofferräume*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kofferraum* is built from *Koffer* 'suitcase', borrowed from French *coffre* 'chest' (EtymDB), and *Raum* 'space', inherited from Old High German *rūm* (EtymDB).
