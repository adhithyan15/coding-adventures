Punjabi, chapter 302: Sowing Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕਿਆਰੀ (kiārī) — a garden bed; a bed in a field.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to plough, then the Punjabi for to yoke.

You'll want to know: ਕਿਆਰੀ.
ਕਿਆਰੀ — kiārī — "a garden bed; a bed in a field".
ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਕਿਆਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਾਗ ਬੀਜਦੇ ਹਨ। mātā jī kiārī vich sāg bījde han. Mother sows greens in the garden bed.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਿਆਰੀ (kiārī) is inherited from Sanskrit kēdāra, "field"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kiārī, "bed in a field or garden", under it (CDIAL 3463).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਖਿਲਾਰਨਾ (khilārnā) — to scatter; to spread about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a garden bed, then the Punjabi for a manure heap.

You'll want to know: ਖਿਲਾਰਨਾ.
ਖਿਲਾਰਨਾ — khilārnā — "to scatter; to spread about".
ਮੈਂ ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਬੀਜ ਖਿਲਾਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ khet vich bīj khilārdā hāṁ. I scatter seed in the field.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੀਜ ਖਿਲਾਰੇ, maiṁ bīj khilāre.

The word, taken apart.
ਖਿਲਾਰਨਾ (khilārnā) is inherited from a reconstructed khiḍ, "be open, expand"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khillarṇā, "to be scattered", and its causative khilāraṇā under it (CDIAL 3882).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ਉੱਗਣਾ.
ਉੱਗਣਾ — uggṇā — "to sprout; to come up".
ਮੀਂਹ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਬੀਜ ਉੱਗਣਗੇ। mīṁh ton bāad bīj uggaṇge. After the rain the seeds will sprout.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਘਾਹ ਉੱਗਿਆ, oh ghāh uggiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਉੱਗਣਾ (uggṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed udgāti, "rises, comes forth"; CDIAL lists Punjabi uggṇā, "to spring up, grow", under it (CDIAL 1954).
