# பெரியப்பா (periyappā) — the father's elder brother

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C101-GRAND-04, TA-LEX-C101-GRAND-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for an aunt, then the Tamil for the father's younger brother.

## You'll want to know: பெரியப்பா
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C102-INLAWS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**பெரியப்பா** (*periyappā*) — your father's elder brother, the *big father*.

**பெரிய** is "big". Beside **சித்தப்பா**, the little father.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Big father and little father.
