Lesson 1 of 1.
hablábamos — the imperfect gets its plural.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Hablaba — I used to speak, or I was speaking. The tense for what was going on.
[pause 1 second]
Until now you have had only its singular.

Grammar Lens: the three that were missing.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say them: nosotros hablábamos. Vosotros hablabais. Ellos hablaban.
[pause 2 seconds]
The -aba- runs all the way through. The endings on top of it are the ordinary plural ones you have used since the present tense: -mos, -is, -n.

Why it's said this way.
Now look at the accent on hablábamos, because it is the only one in the set and it is doing real work.
[pause 2 seconds]
The word ends in -s, so Spanish's default would put the stress on the next-to-last syllable: habla-BA-mos. But the stress actually falls a syllable earlier, on the -BA-... which is not where the default lands, so the accent has to say so.
[pause 2 seconds]
Without it you would read hablabamos with the wrong beat. The mark is not decoration; it is the difference between the word being a past tense and being nothing at all.
[pause 2 seconds]
None of the other five needs one, because in each of those the default already lands where the stress is.
