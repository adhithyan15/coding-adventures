## You'll want to know: লাভ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C331-UDDESHYO331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**লাভ** — *lābh* — "a gain; a profit".

> এই ব্যবসায় কি কোনো লাভ আছে?
>
> *ei bæbsāy ki kono lābh āchhe?*
>
> Is there any profit in this business?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লাভ** (*lābh*) is Sanskrit *lābha*, getting or gaining (Monier-Williams). It covers money made in trade and any advantage gained.
