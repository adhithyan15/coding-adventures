## You'll want to know: ねんれい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C419-KOKUSEKI419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ねんれい** — *nenrei* — "age (a formal word)".

> **ここに ねんれいを かいて ください。** — *koko ni nenrei o kaite kudasai* — "Please write your age here."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねんれい** (*nenrei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nen*, "year" (Middle Chinese *nen*) and *rei*, "age" (*leng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
