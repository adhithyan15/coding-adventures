## You'll want to know: صاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C310-NAJDA310-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صاح** — *ṣāḥa* — "to cry out, to shout".

> صاح الولد عندما رأى الكلب.
>
> *ṣāḥa al-walad ʿindamā raʾā al-kalb.*
>
> The boy shouted when he saw the dog.

Present: يصيح, *yaṣīḥ*.

A cockerel صاح too: it crowed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صاح** (*ṣāḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-y-ḥ* (Lane), "to cry out, to raise the voice"; *ṣayḥa* is a shout.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
