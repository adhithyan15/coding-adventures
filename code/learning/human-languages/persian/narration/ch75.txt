Persian, chapter 75: Jacket, Belt, Twelve, Fourteen, Sixteen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کت (kot) — a jacket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a hat, then the Persian for a shoe.

You'll want to know: کت.
کت — kot — "a jacket".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kot]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kot, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kot]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for a hat, then the Persian for a shoe, then say kot again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does کت (kot) mean? ("A jacket".) Say it once more.
