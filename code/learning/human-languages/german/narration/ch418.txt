German, chapter 418: An Bord.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Flugbegleiter — the flight attendant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to declare, then the German for the passport.

You'll want to know: der Flugbegleiter.
der Flugbegleiter — "the flight attendant".
Der Flugbegleiter bringt uns Wasser und Kaffee. The flight attendant brings us water and coffee.
Same form in the plural. A woman: die Flugbegleiterin.

The word, taken apart.
Flugbegleiter is built from Flug 'flight', inherited from Proto-Germanic flugiz (EtymDB), and Begleiter 'companion', from begleiten 'to accompany', which is built on leiten 'to lead'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Fensterplatz is built from Fenster 'window', an early borrowing from Latin fenestra (Lewis & Short), and Platz 'place', which came through Old French place from Latin platea 'broad street' (EtymDB).

der Gangplatz — "the aisle seat".
Auf langen Flügen nimmt er lieber einen Gangplatz. On long flights he prefers an aisle seat.
Plural: die Gangplätze. Der Gang — the aisle, the corridor.

The word, taken apart.
Gangplatz is built from Gang 'passage, aisle', from gehen 'to go', inherited from Old High German gang (EtymDB), and Platz 'place', from Latin platea.

Lesson 4 of 5.
abfliegen — to take off, to fly out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the window seat, then the German for the aisle seat.

You'll want to know: abfliegen.
abfliegen — "to take off, to fly out".
Das Flugzeug fliegt pünktlich um sieben ab. The plane takes off on time at seven.
Separable; Perfekt with sein: wir sind abgeflogen. Der Abflug — the departure.

The word, taken apart.
abfliegen is built from ab 'off, away' and fliegen 'to fly', inherited from Proto-Germanic fleuganą (EtymDB). English fly is a cousin.
