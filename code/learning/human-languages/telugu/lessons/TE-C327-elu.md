# ఏలు (ēlu) — to reign over

## You'll want to know: ఏలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C327-RAJYAM327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఏలు** — *ēlu* — "to reign over".

> ఆ రాజు చాలా సంవత్సరాలు రాజ్యం ఏలాడు.
>
> *ā rāju cālā saṁvatsarālu rājyaṁ ēlāḍu.*
>
> That king ruled the kingdom for many years.

Past (I …): ఏలాను, *ēlānu*.

A native verb of the old stories: రాజ్యం ఏలు, to rule a kingdom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఏలు** (*ēlu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5157 lists Telugu *ēlu*, "to rule, govern", with Tamil *āḷ*, "to rule, reign over".
