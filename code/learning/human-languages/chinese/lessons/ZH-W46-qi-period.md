## Script — eight strokes, then the moon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QI-PERIOD-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 期

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, eight strokes.** A horizontal at the top. Two verticals down through it. Two short horizontals between them. A long horizontal across the foot. Then a left-falling stroke and a dot under it.

**The moon, four strokes.** A left-falling stroke down the left. A horizontal that turns down the right and hooks. Two short horizontals inside. It is the lower part of 有.

8 + 4 = twelve.
