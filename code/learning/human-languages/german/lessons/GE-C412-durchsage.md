## You'll want to know: die Durchsage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C412-BAHNSTEIG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Durchsage** — "the announcement (over a loudspeaker)".

> Laut der Durchsage hat der Zug zehn Minuten Verspätung.
>
> *According to the announcement the train is ten minutes late.*

Plural: *die Durchsagen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Durchsage* is built from *durchsagen* 'to pass a message on', from *durch* 'through' and *sagen* 'to say', inherited from Proto-Germanic *sagjaną* (EtymDB).
