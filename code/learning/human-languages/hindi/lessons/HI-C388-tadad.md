## You'll want to know: तादाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C388-PARYAPT388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तादाद** — *tādād* — "a number, a quantity (of people or things)".

> अगर मेहमानों की तादाद बढ़ेगी तो खाना कम पड़ेगा।
>
> *agar mehmānoṁ kī tādād baṛhegī to khānā kam paṛegā.*
>
> If the number of guests goes up, the food will run short.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तादाद** (*tādād*) is usually traced to Arabic *taʿdād*, "counting, a number", which reached Hindi through Persian.
