## You'll want to know: cūnae, cūnārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C324-PUER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cūnae, cūnārum** — "a cradle".

> Īnfāns in cūnīs dormiēbat.
>
> *The baby was sleeping in the cradle.*

Feminine, plural. In Plautus a grown man is asked if he still sleeps *in cūnīs*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cūnae** is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*koinā*, "cradle" (EtymDB), from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to lie down".
