## You'll want to know: بیتنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C361-IMKAN361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بیتنا** — *bītnā* — "to go by (of time); to pass".

> اگر وقت بیت گیا تو کچھ نہیں ہو سکے گا۔
>
> *agar vaqt bīt gayā to kuchh nahīṅ ho sake gā.*
>
> If the time slips by, nothing can be done.

Past (it …): وہ سال جلدی بیت گیا, *voh sāl jaldī bīt gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بیتنا** (*bītnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bītnā*, "to pass (of time)", under Sanskrit *vṛttá*, "turned, passed" (CDIAL 12069).
