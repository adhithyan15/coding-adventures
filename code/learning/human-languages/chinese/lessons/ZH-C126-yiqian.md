## You'll want to know: 以前
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C126-OFTEN126-03]; assesses=[] -->

**以前** — *yǐqián* — "before; in the past".

> **以前我不会说汉语** *yǐqián wǒ bú huì shuō hànyǔ* — I couldn't speak Chinese before.

The new character, in pieces: 以 — four strokes: a vertical that flicks up at its foot, a dot beside it, then a long stroke falling to the left and a dot at the lower right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**以前** (*yǐqián*) is built from 以, glossed 'to use' and 'with', and 前 'before', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yiX* and *dzen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
