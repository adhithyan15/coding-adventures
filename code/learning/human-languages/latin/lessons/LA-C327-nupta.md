## You'll want to know: nūpta, nūptae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C327-SPONSA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**nūpta, nūptae** — "a bride, a married woman".

> Nūpta ad domum marītī ducta est.
>
> *The bride was led to her husband's house.*

Feminine, the participle of *nūbō* (taught), said of the woman who marries. *Nova nūpta*: the new bride.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**nūpta** is the feminine participle of *nūbō*, "to marry" (EtymDB), which is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to wed". English *nuptial* comes from the same family.
