# Review — a journey that goes wrong, and is put right

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-ALLER-RETOUR-01, FR-LEX-RATE-LE-TRAIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a return ticket". (*Un aller-retour.*) Then "I've missed the
train". (*J'ai raté le train.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-BILLET-POUR-02, FR-GRAM-SIGNALER-PROBLEME-02, FR-GRAM-POUR-ALLER-A-02, FR-GRAM-QUAND-COUPE-01] -->

Two chapters, and a whole journey you can handle alone. Find the way:
*excusez-moi, pour aller à… ?* Buy the ticket: *un aller-retour pour…*. And
when it goes wrong, say so in three parts: a courtesy, what happened, what
you need.

[PAUSE 2s] Here is the whole of it, told afterwards as a story:

> *J'avais un aller-retour pour Paris. Quand je suis arrivé sur le quai, le
> train est parti. Donc j'ai demandé : « Excusez-moi, le prochain train part à
> quelle heure ? »*

The scene in the imparfait, the events in the passé composé, and the
question quoted whole. *Est parti* is *partir* in the passé composé, with
*être*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-BILLET-POUR-02, FR-GRAM-SIGNALER-PROBLEME-02, FR-GRAM-DEUX-FLECHES-01, FR-LEX-DEMANDER-04, FR-GRAM-TOURNEZ-02, FR-LEX-TOUT-DROIT-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: at the window — *Je voudrais un aller-retour pour Paris, s'il vous plaît.*]
- [YOU SAY: on the platform — *Excusez-moi, j'ai un problème. J'ai raté le train.*]
- [YOU SAY: the story, afterwards — *Quand je suis arrivé sur le quai, le train est parti.*]
- [YOU SAY: directions to the platform — *Allez tout droit, puis tournez à gauche.*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SIGNALER-PROBLEME-02, FR-LEX-ALLER-RETOUR-01, FR-GRAM-DECOR-EVENEMENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What are the three parts of a complaint? (A **courtesy**, **what
went wrong**, **what you need**.) Which ticket is one-way? (***Un aller
simple.***) In the story, why is *j'avais* in the imparfait? (The ticket in
your pocket is **scene**.)
