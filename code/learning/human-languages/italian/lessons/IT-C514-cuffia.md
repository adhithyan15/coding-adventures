## You'll want to know: la cuffia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C514-ACQUA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la cuffia** — "swimming cap; headphones".

> In piscina la cuffia è obbligatoria.
>
> *In the pool a swimming cap is compulsory.*

Also headphones: *le cuffie*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cuffia* comes from Late Latin *cofia* "a cap, a bonnet" (EtymDB), probably of Germanic origin.
