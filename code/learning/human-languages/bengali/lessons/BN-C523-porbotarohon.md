## You'll want to know: পর্বতারোহণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C523-BAIRE523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পর্বতারোহণ** — *pôrbôtārohôn* — "mountain climbing".

> পর্বতারোহণ একটা বিপজ্জনক খেলা।
>
> *Pôrbôtārohôn ekṭā bipôjjônôk khælā.*
>
> Mountain climbing is a dangerous sport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পর্বতারোহণ** (*pôrbôtārohôn*) joins Sanskrit *parvata*, "a mountain", and *ārohaṇa*, "ascending" (Monier-Williams).
