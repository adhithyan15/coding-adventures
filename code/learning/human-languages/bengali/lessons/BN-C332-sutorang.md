## You'll want to know: সুতরাং
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C332-SUTORANG332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সুতরাং** — *sutôrāng* — "therefore; and so".

> বাস নেই, সুতরাং আমরা হেঁটে যাব।
>
> *bās nei, sutôrāng āmrā hẽṭe jābô.*
>
> There is no bus, so we will walk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সুতরাং** (*sutôrāng*) is Sanskrit *sutarām*, 'all the more' (Monier-Williams), with its final m heard as ng. The sense 'therefore' is not one the dictionary gives; in Bengali the word draws the conclusion from what was said before.
