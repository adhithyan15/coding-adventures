## You'll want to know: a narina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C481-ROSTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a narina** — "nostril".

> O ar entra pelas narinas.
>
> *Air goes in through the nostrils.*

Usually plural: *as narinas*. The whole nose is *o nariz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narina* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *narīna* (EtymDB), built on Latin *nāris* "a nostril" (Lewis & Short).
