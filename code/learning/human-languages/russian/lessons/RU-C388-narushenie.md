## You'll want to know: нарушение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C388-ZAKON-05]; assesses=[] -->

**нарушение** (*narushéniye*) — "a violation, a breach".

> Это серьёзное нарушение правил.
>
> *Éto seryóznoye narushéniye právil.*
>
> This is a serious breach of the rules.

Neuter. *Нарушение правил* — a breach of the rules.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**нарушение** is built on *нарушить* "to violate", from *на-* and *рушить* "to pull down", with the noun suffix *-ение*.
