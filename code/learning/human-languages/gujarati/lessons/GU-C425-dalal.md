## You'll want to know: દલાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C425-LOAN425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દલાલ** — *dalāl* — "a broker; a middleman".

> દલાલે અમને સસ્તો ફ્લેટ બતાવ્યો.
>
> *dalāle amne sasto phleṭ batāvyo.*
>
> The broker showed us a cheap flat.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દલાલ** (*dalāl*) is usually traced to Arabic *dallāl*, "one who shows the way; a broker", which came through Persian.
