## You'll want to know: la spada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C327-BATTAGLIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la spada** — "the sword".

> Il cavaliere ha alzato la spada.
>
> *The knight raised his sword.*

*Il pesce spada* — swordfish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spada* is inherited from Latin *spatha* "a broad sword" (EtymDB), from Greek *spáthē* "a blade".
