## You'll want to know: संवेदनशील
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C468-SAUMYA468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संवेदनशील** — *saṁvedanśīl* — "sensitive".

> संवेदनशील माणूस लवकर दुखावतो.
>
> *saṁvedanśīl māṇūs lavkar dukhāvto.*
>
> A sensitive person is quickly hurt.

संवेदना, feeling, + शील, of the nature of. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संवेदनशील** (*saṁvedanśīl*) is a learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, *saṃvedana*, "perceiving, feeling", and *śīla*, "habit, nature" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
