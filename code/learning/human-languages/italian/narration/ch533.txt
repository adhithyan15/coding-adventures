Italian, chapter 533: A tavola.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
apparecchiare — to lay (a table).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for detergent, then the Italian for vacuum cleaner.

You'll want to know: apparecchiare.
apparecchiare — "to lay (a table)".
Apparecchio la tavola per sei persone. I lay the dinner table for six people.
Apparecchiare la tavola.

The word, taken apart.
apparecchiare is usually traced to Vulgar Latin appariculāre "to prepare", from Latin apparāre, built on parāre "to make ready" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to lay, then the Italian for to clear.

You'll want to know: la tovaglia.
la tovaglia — "tablecloth".
Per la festa mettiamo la tovaglia bianca. For the party we put on the white tablecloth.
Una tovaglia a quadri — a checked tablecloth.

The word, taken apart.
tovaglia comes from a Germanic word, Frankish thwahillja "a cloth" (EtymDB, under English towel), the same source as English towel.
