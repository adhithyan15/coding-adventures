## You'll want to know: अध्ययनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C363-ADHYAYANA363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अध्ययनम्** — *adhyayanam* — "studying; study".

> मम अध्ययनं प्रातः भवति।
>
> *mama adhyayanaṃ prātaḥ bhavati.*
>
> I do my studying in the morning.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अध्ययनम्** (*adhyayanam*) is built on *adhi*, "over, upon", and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives *adhy-ayana* "reading, studying" (MW).
