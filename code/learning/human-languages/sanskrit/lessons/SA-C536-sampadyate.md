## You'll want to know: सम्पद्यते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C536-ADRSTA536-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्पद्यते** — *sampadyate* — "turns out well".

> यदि सर्वं सम्यक् सम्पद्यते, तर्हि वयम् उत्सवं करिष्यामः।
>
> *yadi sarvaṃ samyak sampadyate, tarhi vayam utsavaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ.*
>
> If everything turns out well, we will celebrate.

Past (it …): कार्यं सम्पन्नवत्, *kāryaṃ sampannavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्पद्यते** (*sampadyate*) is built on *sam*, "together, well", and the root √pad, "to fall, to go"; MW gives *sampad* as to fall out well, to turn out well, to succeed; its past participle is *sampanna* (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
