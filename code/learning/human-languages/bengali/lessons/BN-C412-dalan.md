## You'll want to know: দালান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C412-PANCHIL412-03]; assesses=[] -->

**দালান** — *dālān* — "a brick building; a hall".

> রাস্তার পাশে একটা পুরনো দালান আছে।
>
> *rāstār pāshe ekṭā purôno dālān āchhe.*
>
> There is an old brick building by the road.

In a village, the one house built of brick rather than mud and thatch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দালান** (*dālān*) is usually traced to Persian *dālān*, "a hall, a covered porch".
