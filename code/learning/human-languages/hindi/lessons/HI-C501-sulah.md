## You'll want to know: सुलह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C501-NATA501-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सुलह** — *sulah* — "a reconciliation; making peace".

> बड़ों ने दोनों परिवारों में सुलह कराई।
>
> *baṛoṁ ne donõ parivāroṁ meṁ sulah karāī.*
>
> The elders brought the two families back together.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुलह** (*sulah*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṣulḥ*, "peace, reconciliation", which reached Hindi through Persian.
