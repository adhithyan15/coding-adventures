Arabic, chapter 512: Getting well again.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شفاء (shifāʾ) — recovery, a cure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a splint, then the Arabic for to bind up.

You'll want to know: شفاء.
شفاء — shifāʾ — "recovery, a cure".
نتمنى لك الشفاء العاجل. natamannā laka ash-shifāʾ al-ʿājil. We wish you a quick recovery.
نتمنى لك الشفاء is what you say to someone who is ill.

The word, taken apart.
شفاء (shifāʾ) is built on the native Arabic root sh-f-y, "to heal, to cure" (Lane). Shifāʾ is the healing itself.

The word, taken apart.
نقاهة (naqāha) is usually traced to the native Arabic root n-q-h, "to recover from an illness": the weak days between sickness and full health.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تعافى (taʿāfā) — to recover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for recovery, then the Arabic for convalescence.

You'll want to know: تعافى.
تعافى — taʿāfā — "to recover".
تعافى اللاعب وعاد إلى الفريق. taʿāfā al-lāʿib wa-ʿāda ilā al-farīq. The player recovered and went back to the team.
Present: يتعافى, yataʿāfā.

The word, taken apart.
تعافى (taʿāfā) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-f-w, "to efface; to pardon; to give health" (Lane). ʿAfwan, which you know, is from the same root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تألم (taʾallama) — to be in pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to recover, then the Arabic for good health.

You'll want to know: تألم.
تألم — taʾallama — "to be in pain".
تألم الرجل كثيرا من ظهره. taʾallama ar-rajul kathīran min ẓahrihi. The man suffered a lot from his back.
Present: يتألم, yataʾallam.
It takes من for the place: تألم (taʾallama) من ظهره.

The word, taken apart.
تألم (taʾallama) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-l-m, "pain" (Lane). Alam, pain, which you know, is from the same root; Form V is to feel it.
