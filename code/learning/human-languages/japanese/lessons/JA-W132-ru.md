## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-RU-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-RO-01] -->

> **る** — *ru*

One stroke, and the pen never lifts:

1. a short **bar** at the top, left to right
2. turn sharply and run the long **diagonal** down to the lower left
3. swing back up to the right and round the big **bowl**
4. at the bottom, curl up into a small **loop** and finish where it meets the base

Up to the bowl, this is **ろ**. Only the ending differs:

| you write | it ends with |
|---|---|
| **ろ**, *ro* | a short tail curving left |
| **る**, *ru* | a small closed loop |

With **く** and **ま** around it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**くるま**, *kuruma*, a car.
