## You'll want to know: вор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C288-KRAZHA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**вор** (*vor*) — "a thief".

> Вор взял сумку и убежал.
>
> *Vor vzyal súmku i ubezhál.*
>
> The thief took the bag and ran off.

Genitive *вора*, plural *воры*, *воров*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вор* is an old Russian word whose earlier sense was "a rogue, a rebel"; its origin is not settled, and EtymDB lists no parent.
