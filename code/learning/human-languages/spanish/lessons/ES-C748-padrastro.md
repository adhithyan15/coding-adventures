## You'll want to know: el padrastro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C748-NIETOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el padrastro** — "the stepfather; the hangnail".

> Mi padrastro me enseñó a nadar.
>
> *My stepfather taught me to swim.*

Also a hangnail beside a fingernail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*padrastro* is usually traced to a spoken Latin *patraster*, built on *pater* "father" with the belittling ending *-aster*.
