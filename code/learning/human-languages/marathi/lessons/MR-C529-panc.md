## You'll want to know: पंच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C529-SAMNA529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पंच** — *pañc* — "an umpire; a referee".

> पंचांनी पावसामुळे खेळ थांबवला.
>
> *pañcānnī pāvsāmuḷe kheḷ thāmbavlā.*
>
> The umpires stopped play because of the rain.

Masculine.

A match has two, so often plural: पंचांनी, the umpires.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पंच** (*pañc*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *pañca*, "five" (CDIAL 7655): a council of five once settled disputes, and the word came to mean a judge of fair play.
