# ਤੁਹਾਡੀ — respectful your, with a feminine noun

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-YOUR-NAME-02, PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Retrieve **ਤੁਹਾਡਾ** from the respectful name question. Then say
**ਯਾਤਰਾ**.

## Grammar Lens: keep respect, change agreement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-TUHADI-185]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-YOUR-NAME-02] -->

> **ਤੁਹਾਡੀ** — *tuhaadii* — your, respectful, with a feminine noun

The respectful owner does not change. Only the ending becomes *-ii*. Read
**ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ**: “your journey.”
