## You'll want to know: attendibile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C345-AFFERMARE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**attendibile** — "credible, trustworthy (of news or a source)".

> Questa notizia non è attendibile.
>
> *This news is not reliable.*

For information and sources: *una notizia attendibile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*attendibile* is built on *attendere* in its old sense "to pay heed to", from Latin *attendere* "to stretch towards, to give heed" (Lewis & Short), built on *tendere* "to stretch": worth heeding.
