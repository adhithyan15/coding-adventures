## You'll want to know: झोळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C225-PACK225-02]; assesses=[] -->

**झोळी** — *jhoḷī* — "a cloth bag (carried on the shoulder)".

> हूं काल झोळी मांय रोटी राखसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl jhoḷī mā̃y roṭī rākhsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll put flatbread in my bag.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झोळी** (*jhoḷī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhōla*, "a bag"; CDIAL lists Hindi *jholī* and Gujarati *jhoḷī*, "a bag". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5415).
