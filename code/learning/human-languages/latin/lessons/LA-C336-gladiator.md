## You'll want to know: gladiātor, gladiātōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C336-GLADIATOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**gladiātor, gladiātōris** — "a gladiator".

> Gladiātōrēs in harēnā pugnābant.
>
> *Gladiators were fighting in the arena.*

Masculine, 3rd declension, from *gladius*, 'sword' (taught). Most were slaves or prisoners; a few were volunteers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**gladiātor** is built on *gladius*, "sword" (EtymDB): the swordsman. English *gladiator* is the same word.
