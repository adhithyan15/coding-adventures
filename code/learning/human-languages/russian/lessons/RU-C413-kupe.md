## You'll want to know: купе
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C413-PROYEZD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**купе** (*kupé*) — "a compartment (on a train)".

> В купе четыре полки.
>
> *V kupé chetýre pólki.*
>
> There are four berths in a compartment.

Neuter and indeclinable: *в купе*, *из купе*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*купе* is usually traced to French *coupé* 'cut off', from *couper* 'to cut': a carriage cut into small rooms. EtymDB does not list the Russian word, so the line is hedged.
