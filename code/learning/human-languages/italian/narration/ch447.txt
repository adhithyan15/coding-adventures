Italian, chapter 447: Uffici pubblici.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la questura — the police headquarters.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the cloister, then the Italian for to wander.

You'll want to know: la questura.
la questura — "the police headquarters".
Per il passaporto devi andare in questura. For a passport you need to go to the police headquarters.
Run by il questore; you go there for a lost passport too.

The word, taken apart.
questura is a learned borrowing from Latin quaestūra 'the office of a quaestor', from quaerere 'to seek, to inquire' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

La prefettura è nel palazzo in piazza. The prefecture is in the building on the square.
The office of il prefetto, the state's representative in a province.

The word, taken apart.
prefettura is a learned borrowing from Latin praefectūra 'the office of a prefect', from praefectus 'one put in charge', built on facere 'to make' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la caserma — the barracks; the police station (of the carabinieri).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the police headquarters, then the Italian for the prefecture.

You'll want to know: la caserma.
la caserma — "the barracks; the police station (of the carabinieri)".
La caserma dei carabinieri è in fondo alla strada. The carabinieri station is at the end of the street.
La caserma dei vigili del fuoco — the fire station.

The word, taken apart.
caserma is usually traced through French caserne and Provençal to Latin quaterna 'a set of four': first a small hut for four soldiers. It came into Italian as a borrowing.

Ho segnalato il furto alla polizia. I reported the theft to the police.
La segnalazione — a report, a notice.

segnalare is built on segnale 'signal', a learned borrowing from Late Latin signāle, from signum 'sign' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
la carta d'identità — the identity card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the barracks, then the Italian for to report.

You'll want to know: la carta d'identità.
la carta d'identità — "the identity card".
In albergo chiedono la carta d'identità. At the hotel they ask for your identity card.
You get it from il comune, the town hall.

The word, taken apart.
carta d'identità is built from carta 'paper, card', inherited from Latin charta, and identità, a learned borrowing from Late Latin identitās, from idem 'the same' (Lewis & Short).
