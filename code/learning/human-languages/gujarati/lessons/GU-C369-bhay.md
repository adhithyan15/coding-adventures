## You'll want to know: ભય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C369-BHAY369-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ભય** — *bhay* — "dread".

> જો નદીમાં પૂર આવશે તો ગામને ભય છે.
>
> *jo nadīmā̃ pūr āvśe to gāmne bhay che.*
>
> If the river floods, the village is in danger.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભય** (*bhay*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *bhaya*, "fear, alarm, dread" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
