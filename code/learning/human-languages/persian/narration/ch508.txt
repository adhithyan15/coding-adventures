Lesson 1 of 5.
خوشنویسی (khoshnevisi) — calligraphy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to cook, then the Persian for a collection.

You'll want to know: خوشنویسی.
خوشنویسی (khoshnevisi) — "calligraphy".
او سالها خوشنویسی یاد گرفت. u sâlhâ khoshnevisi yâd gereft. He studied calligraphy for years.
khosh 'fine' + nevis 'writing' (stem of neveshtan) + -i. A calligrapher is khoshnevis.

The word, taken apart.
khoshnevisi is built on native Persian parts: khosh, 'fine', nevis, the present stem of neveshtan, which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and -i: fine writing.

Lesson 3 of 5.
قلاب بافی (qollâb bâfi) — crochet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for calligraphy, then the Persian for embroidery.

You'll want to know: قلاب بافی.
قلاب بافی (qollâb bâfi) — "crochet".
خواهرم برای مادرم یک شال قلاب بافی کرد. khâharam barâ-ye mâdaram yek shâl qollâb bâfi kard. My sister crocheted a scarf for my mother.
qollâb 'hook' + bâf 'weaving' (stem of bâftan) + -i: hook-knitting.

The word, taken apart.
qollâb bâfi is built in Persian on qollâb, 'hook', a loan usually traced to Arabic, and bâf, the stem of native bâftan, 'to knit, to weave'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

کاردستی (kârdasti) — "a handicraft, craft work".
بچه ها در مدرسه کاردستی درست کردند. bachchehâ dar madrese kârdasti dorost kardand. The children made crafts at school.
kâr 'work' + dast 'hand' + -i: handwork.

The word, taken apart.
kârdasti is built on native Persian kâr, 'work', and dast, 'hand', both of which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), with the ending -i.
