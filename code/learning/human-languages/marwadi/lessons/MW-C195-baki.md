## You'll want to know: बाकी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C195-BANK195-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बाकी** — *bākī* — "remaining; still owed".

> दस रुपिया बाकी है।
>
> *das rupiyā bākī hai.*
>
> Ten rupees are still owed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाकी** (*bākī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bāqī*, "remaining".
