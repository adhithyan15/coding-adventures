Arabic, chapter 109: Wealth, Experience, Habit, Skill, Art.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ثروة (tharwa) — wealth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a right, then the Arabic for an owner.

You'll want to know: ثروة.
ثروة — tharwa — "wealth".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tharwa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tharwa, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say tharwa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a right, then the Arabic for an owner, then say tharwa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ثروة (tharwa) mean? ("Wealth".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.

[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a lift, a corridor, a public square, an airport, a port]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a neighbourhood, an alley, a building, a bathroom, a balcony]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a roof, stairs, a ceiling, behind, between]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: beside, left, the middle, a corner, north]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: south, east, west, a place, an address]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A lift? (مصعد, miṣʿad.) A corridor? (ممر, mamarr.) A public square? (ميدان, maydān.) An airport? (مطار, maṭār.) A port? (ميناء, mīnāʾ.) A neighbourhood? (حي, ḥayy.) An alley? (زقاق, zuqāq.) A building? (مبنى, mabnā.) A bathroom? (حمام, ḥammām.) A balcony? (شرفة, shurfa.) A roof? (سطح, saṭḥ.) Stairs? (درج, daraj.) A ceiling? (سقف, saqf.) Behind? (خلف, khalfa.) Between? (بين, bayna.) Beside? (بجانب, bi-jānib.) Left? (يسار, yasār.) The middle? (وسط, wasaṭ.) A corner? (زاوية, zāwiya.) North? (شمال, shamāl.) South? (جنوب, janūb.) East? (شرق, sharq.) West? (غرب, gharb.) A place? (مكان, makān.) An address? (عنوان, ʿunwān.)

Success? (نجاح, najāḥ.) Mastery? (إتقان, itqān.) Ability? (قدرة, qudra.) Competence? (كفاءة, kafāʾa.) A certificate? (شهادة, shahāda.) Ownership? (ملكية, milkiyya.) To be able to? (استطاع, istaṭāʿa.) To be able to? (قدر, qadara.) It is possible? (يمكن, yumkin.) With? (لدى, ladā.) Strength? (قوة, quwwa.) A right? (حق, ḥaqq.) An owner? (مالك, mālik.) Wealth? (ثروة, tharwa.) Experience? (خبرة, khibra.) A habit? (عادة, ʿāda.) A skill? (مهارة, mahāra.) An art? (فن, fann.)
