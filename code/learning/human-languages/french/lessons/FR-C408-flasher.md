## You'll want to know: flasher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C408-RADAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**flasher** — "to flash (of a speed camera), to catch speeding".

> Je me suis fait flasher à quatre-vingt-dix.
>
> *I got caught by a speed camera at ninety.*

Informal; mostly *se faire flasher*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flasher* is built on *flash*, which was borrowed from English *flash* (EtymDB): the camera flashes when you go too fast.
