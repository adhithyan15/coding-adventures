## You'll want to know: બ્રેક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C295-VALANK295-02]; assesses=[] -->

**બ્રેક** — *brek* — "a brake".

> ડ્રાઈવરે છેલ્લી પળે બ્રેક મારી.
>
> *ḍrāīvre chellī paḷe brek mārī.*
>
> The driver braked at the last moment.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બ્રેક** (*brek*) is borrowed from English *brake*, taken in as a feminine noun. A driver **બ્રેક મારે છે**, slams on the brakes.
