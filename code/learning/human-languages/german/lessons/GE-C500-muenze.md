## You'll want to know: die Münze
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C500-SAMMELN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Münze** — "coin".

> Er sammelt Münzen aus aller Welt.
>
> *He collects coins from all over the world.*

Plural: *die Münzen*. Also a mint, where coins are made.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Münze* is inherited from Old High German *muniz*, from Proto-Germanic *munitą*, an early borrowing from Latin *monēta* "place for making coins, coined money" (EtymDB).
