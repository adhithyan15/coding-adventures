## You'll want to know: flāgitium, flāgitiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C414-INIURIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**flāgitium, flāgitiī** — "a disgraceful act, a scandal".

> Hoc flāgitium tacēre nōn possum.
>
> *I cannot keep quiet about this scandal.*

*flāgitium, flāgitiī* n., a shameful deed, and the person who does it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flāgitium* is built on *flāgitō* "to demand loudly" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): first a furious demand, then the shameful act that people cried out against.
