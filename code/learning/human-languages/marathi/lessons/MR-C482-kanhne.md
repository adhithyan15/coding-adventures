## You'll want to know: कण्हणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C482-DUKH482-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कण्हणे** — *kaṇhṇe* — "to groan".

> तापामुळे तो रात्रभर कण्हत होता.
>
> *tāpāmuḷe to rātrabhar kaṇhat hotā.*
>
> He groaned all night with fever.

Past (he …): तो कण्हला, *to kaṇhlā*.

A low sound of pain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कण्हणे** (*kaṇhṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *kanhṇẽ*, "to groan", under Sanskrit *kvaṇati*, "sounds" (CDIAL 3632).
