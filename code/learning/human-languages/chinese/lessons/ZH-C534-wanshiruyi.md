## You'll want to know: 万事如意
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C534-RATHER534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**万事如意** — *wànshì rúyì* — "may all go as you wish".

> **希望你万事如意** *xīwàng nǐ wànshì rúyì* — I hope everything goes well for you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**万事如意** (*wànshì rúyì*) is built from 万 'ten thousand', 事 'a matter', 如 'like' and 意 'what one means', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjonH*, *dzriH*, *nyo* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): every matter as you would have it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
