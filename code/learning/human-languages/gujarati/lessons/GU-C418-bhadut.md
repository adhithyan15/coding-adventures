## You'll want to know: ભાડૂત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C418-SOCIETY418-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાડૂત** — *bhāḍūt* — "a tenant".

> ભાડૂત મહિનાના અંતે ભાડું ચૂકવે છે.
>
> *bhāḍūt mahinānā ante bhāḍũ cūkve che.*
>
> The tenant pays the rent at the end of the month.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાડૂત** (*bhāḍūt*) is built on ભાડું, rent, which CDIAL lists under *\*bhārta*, "pertaining to servants" (CDIAL 9468): the one who pays the rent.
