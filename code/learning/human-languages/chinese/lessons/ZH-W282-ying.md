## Script — the roof of 店, two dots, a falling stroke and a base
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YING-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 应

**Two pieces, and you write the first already.**

**The roof of 店, three strokes**: the dot on top, the bar, and a long left-falling stroke down the left side.

**Under it, four strokes**: a dot on the left, a dot in the middle, a left-falling stroke on the right, and last a long horizontal along the foot.

3 + 4 = seven.
