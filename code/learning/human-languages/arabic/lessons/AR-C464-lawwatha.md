## You'll want to know: لوث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C464-TALAWWUTH464-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لوث** — *lawwatha* — "to pollute".

> لوث المصنع ماء البحيرة.
>
> *lawwatha al-maṣnaʿ māʾ al-buḥayra.*
>
> The factory polluted the water of the lake.

Present: يلوث, *yulawwith*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لوث** (*lawwatha*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-w-th*, "to soil, to stain" (Lane). It is Form II.
