Kannada, chapter 366: Disgrace and Dignity.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಖುಲಾಸೆ (khulāse) — an acquittal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to shove, then the Kannada for a patrol.

You'll want to know: ಖುಲಾಸೆ.
ಖುಲಾಸೆ — khulāse — "an acquittal".
ಕೊನೆಗೆ ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯದಿಂದ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಖುಲಾಸೆ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. konege nyāyālayadinda avanige khulāse sikkitu. In the end he was acquitted by the court.
ಖುಲಾಸೆ (khulāse) ಆಗು, to be acquitted: ಆರೋಪಿ ಖುಲಾಸೆ (khulāse) ಆದನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಖುಲಾಸೆ (khulāse) came into Kannada through Hindustani and is usually traced to Arabic; the exact Arabic source word is not settled.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a patrol, then the Kannada for an acquittal.

You'll want to know: ಕಳಂಕ.
ಕಳಂಕ — kaḷaṅka — "a stain; a disgrace".
ಆ ಮೋಸ ಅವರ ಹೆಸರಿಗೆ ಕಳಂಕ ತಂದಿತು. ā mōsa avara hesarige kaḷaṅka tanditu. That fraud brought a stain on his name.
A stain on a name or a family: ಕಳಂಕ (kaḷaṅka) ತರು.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಳಂಕ (kaḷaṅka) is borrowed from Sanskrit kalaṅka, "a stain, a spot" (CDIAL 2911), and also "defamation" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಘನತೆ (ghanate) — dignity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an acquittal, then the Kannada for a stain.

You'll want to know: ಘನತೆ.
ಘನತೆ — ghanate — "dignity".
ಅಜ್ಜ ಕಷ್ಟದಲ್ಲೂ ಘನತೆಯಿಂದ ಬದುಕಿದರು. ajja kaṣṭadallū ghanateyinda badukidaru. Grandfather lived with dignity even in hardship.
ಘನ, weighty, with -ತೆ, -ness.

The word, taken apart.
ಘನತೆ (ghanate) is built on Sanskrit ghana, "compact, firm, dense" (CDIAL 4424), with the Kannada abstract ending -te: weight of character.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಆಣೆ (āṇe) — an oath; a vow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a stain, then the Kannada for dignity.

You'll want to know: ಆಣೆ.
ಆಣೆ — āṇe — "an oath; a vow".
ನಾನು ಸತ್ಯ ಹೇಳುತ್ತೇನೆ ಅಂತ ಅವನು ಆಣೆ ಮಾಡಿದನು. nānu satya hēḷuttēne anta avanu āṇe māḍidanu. He swore an oath that he would tell the truth.
ಆಣೆ (āṇe) ಮಾಡು or ಆಣೆ (āṇe) ಇಡು, to swear. ಆಣೆ (āṇe) is also an old coin, a sixteenth of a rupee.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಣೆ (āṇe) is usually traced to Sanskrit ājñā, "a command" (CDIAL 1095), through a Prakrit form āṇā.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಗೋಳಾಡು (gōḷāḍu) — to wail; to lament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for dignity, then the Kannada for an oath.

You'll want to know: ಗೋಳಾಡು.
ಗೋಳಾಡು — gōḷāḍu — "to wail; to lament".
ಮನೆ ಕಳೆದುಕೊಂಡ ಹೆಂಗಸು ಗೋಳಾಡಿದಳು. mane kaḷedukoṇḍa heṅgasu gōḷāḍidaḷu. The woman who had lost her house wailed.
Past (I …): ಗೋಳಾಡಿದೆ, gōḷāḍide.
ಗೋಳು, wailing, and ಆಡು: to keep on wailing. ಗೋಳು ಹೊಯ್ದುಕೊ is the same.

The word, taken apart.
ಗೋಳಾಡು (gōḷāḍu) is a native Dravidian compound: gōḷ, "the sound of howling, lamenting, wailing" (DEDR 2252), and āḍu, "to move, to act" (DEDR 347).
