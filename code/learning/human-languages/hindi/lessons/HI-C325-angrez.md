## You'll want to know: अंग्रेज़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C325-ANDOLAN325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अंग्रेज़** — *aṅgrez* — "an English person".

> अंग्रेज़ दो सौ साल तक इस देश में रहे।
>
> *aṅgrez do sau sāl tak is deś meṁ rahe.*
>
> The English stayed in this country for two hundred years.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंग्रेज़** (*aṅgrez*) is usually traced to Portuguese *inglês*, "English", through Persian *angrez*. **अंग्रेज़ी**, the English language, is built on it.
