## You'll want to know: ڈسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C327-KHAZANA327-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈسنا** — *ḍasnā* — "to bite (of a snake)".

> ناگ نے چور کو ڈسا۔
>
> *nāg ne chor ko ḍasā.*
>
> The cobra bit the thief.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اسے ڈسا, *us ne use ḍasā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈسنا** (*ḍasnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍasnā*, "bites, stings", under Sanskrit *dáśati* (CDIAL 6230).
