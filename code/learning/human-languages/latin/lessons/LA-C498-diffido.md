## You'll want to know: diffīdō, diffīdere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C498-SPES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**diffīdō, diffīdere** — "to distrust; to lose hope".

> Mercātor nautīs diffīdit.
>
> *The merchant distrusts the sailors.*

Semi-deponent: *diffīdō, diffīdere, diffīsus sum*, with the dative. The opposite of *cōnfīdō*. English *diffident*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**diffīdō** is built on *dis-* "apart, not" and *fīdō* "to trust" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *diffident* comes from it.
