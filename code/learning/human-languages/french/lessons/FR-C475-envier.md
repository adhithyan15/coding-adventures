## You'll want to know: envier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C475-RANCUNE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**envier** — "to envy".

> J'envie mon frère : il voyage beaucoup.
>
> *I envy my brother: he travels a lot.*

*Envier quelque chose à quelqu'un* — to envy someone something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*envier* is built on *envie* (EtymDB), inherited from Latin *invidia* "envy", from *invidēre* "to look askance at", built on *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
