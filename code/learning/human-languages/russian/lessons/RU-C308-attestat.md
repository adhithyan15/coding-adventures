## You'll want to know: аттестат
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C308-VYPUSKNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**аттестат** (*attestát*) — "a school-leaving certificate".

> В июне мы получили аттестаты.
>
> *V iyúne my poluchíli attestáty.*
>
> In June we got our school-leaving certificates.

*Аттестат зрелости* — the full name, "certificate of maturity".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*аттестат* goes back to Latin *attestātus* "confirmed" (EtymDB), from *attestārī* "to bear witness", built on *testis* "a witness" (Lewis & Short).
