## You'll want to know: qiántái
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C206-FLOOR206-05]; assesses=[] -->

*qiántái* — "front desk; reception".

> *yàoshi zài qiántái* — The key is at reception.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiántái* is built from 前 'front' and *tái* 'a counter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzen* and *doj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a raised platform.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
