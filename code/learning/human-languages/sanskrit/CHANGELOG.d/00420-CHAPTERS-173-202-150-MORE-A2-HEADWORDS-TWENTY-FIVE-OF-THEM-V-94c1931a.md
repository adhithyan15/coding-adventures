## Chapters 173-202: 150 more A2 headwords, twenty-five of them verbs

The second of four Sanskrit A2 vocabulary tranches. Thirty chapters of five
words each, in four runs of at most nine chapters, and each run closes with two
reviews.

- **Chapters 173-177: twenty-five verbs**, including **क्षिपति** (throws),
  **लभते** (gets), **चोरयति** (steals), **पालयति** (keeps), **वहति** (carries),
  **सहते** (endures), **आमन्त्रयति** (invites), **शोधयति** (cleans),
  **वितरति** (distributes) and **विश्वसिति** (trusts).
- **Chapters 178-180: people**: the in-laws (**श्वशुरः**, **श्वश्रूः**,
  **जामाता**, **स्नुषा**), the law (**न्यायाधीशः**, **अधिवक्ता**), and
  **सैनिकः**, **लेखकः**, **पण्डितः**.
- **Chapters 181-182: well-being**: **दन्तपीडा**, **प्रतिश्यायः**, **नाडी**,
  **शोकः**, **सन्तोषः**, **कृतज्ञता**.
- **Chapter 183: time** (**युगम्**, **होरा**, **चिरम्**, **चिरात्**).
- **Chapter 184: place and loudness** (**दूरतः**, **निकटे**, **अन्तरा**,
  **उच्चैः**, **नीचैः**).
- **Chapter 185: how much, what kind, and certainty** (**कियत्**, **कीदृशः**,
  **नूनम्**, **अवश्यम्**).
- **Chapters 186-188: fifteen qualities**, such as **कोमलः**, **विशालः**,
  **दयालुः**, **उदारः**, **कृपणः**, **व्यस्तः** and **उज्ज्वलः**.
- **Chapters 189-202: seventy nouns**:
  - the kitchen (**उलूखलम्**, **मुसलम्**, **चालनी**);
  - grain and fruit (**यवः**, **आमलकम्**);
  - dress (**हारः**, **परिधानम्**);
  - buying and selling (**क्रयः**, **विक्रयः**);
  - occasions (**निमन्त्रणम्**, **सभा**);
  - the country (**राजधानी**, **जनपदः**, **सीमा**);
  - nature (**गुहा**, **इन्द्रधनुः**, **ज्योत्स्ना**);
  - animals (**हरिणः**, **गण्डकः**, **नीडम्**);
  - language and study (**वाक्यम्**, **भाषा**, **व्याकरणम्**, **गणितम्**);
  - plans and problems (**योजना**, **समस्या**, **समाधानम्**);
  - directions (**दिशा**, **पूर्वा**, **पश्चिमा**, **उत्तरा**).
- **Book:** the book passes page 999 (1275 pages). The contents page-number
  box widens from 1.55em to 2.4em, with the right margin widened to match. It
  is the same fix the Tamil and German preambles carry, and it removes 111
  overfull boxes, one per four-digit contents entry. Chapter 171's title
  becomes "Message, Facility, Arrangement, Programme, Competition", so its
  contents line no longer wraps under the wider margin. **सुविधा** means both.
- **Pins:** the Sanskrit lesson-content budget goes from 944 to 1102.

  -> 0.
