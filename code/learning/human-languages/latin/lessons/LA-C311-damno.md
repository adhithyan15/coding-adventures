## You'll want to know: damnō, damnāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C311-DAMNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**damnō, damnāre** — "to condemn, to find guilty".

> Iūdicēs eum fūrtī damnāvērunt.
>
> *The judges found him guilty of theft.*

*damnō, damnāre, damnāvī, damnātum*, with the genitive of the charge. From *damnum*, 'loss' (taught). English *damn*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**damnō** is built on *damnum*, "loss, damage" (EtymDB): to inflict a loss, a penalty. English *damn* and *condemn* come from its family.
