## You'll want to know: квитанция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C436-VOZVRAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**квитанция** (*kvitántsiya*) — "a receipt (for a payment or a service)".

> Кассир дал мне квитанцию.
>
> *Kassír dal mne kvitántsiyu.*
>
> The cashier gave me a receipt.

Feminine. A *квитанция* is a paper slip for a payment or a service; a till receipt is a *чек*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*квитанция* was borrowed from Dutch *kwitantie* (EtymDB), from Middle Dutch *quitantie*.
