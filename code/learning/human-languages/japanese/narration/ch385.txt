Japanese, chapter 385: Going Abroad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
かいがい (kaigai) — overseas; abroad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for landing (of a plane), then the Japanese for to set off on a journey.

You'll want to know: かいがい.
かいがい — kaigai — "overseas; abroad".
らいねん かいがいに いきたいです。 — rainen kaigai ni ikitai desu — "I want to go abroad next year."

The word, taken apart.
かいがい (kaigai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kai, "sea" (Middle Chinese xojX) and gai, "outside" (ngwajH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
めざす (mezasu) — to aim for; to make for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for international, then the Japanese for a time difference.

You'll want to know: めざす.
めざす — mezasu — "to aim for; to make for".
ふねは みなみの しまを めざしています。 — fune wa minami no shima o mezashite imasu — "The ship is making for the southern islands."
Polite めざします, mezashimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): ふねが しまを めざしました。, fune ga shima o mezashimashita.

The word, taken apart.
めざす (mezasu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from me, "eye" and zasu, "pointing" (from sasu); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
