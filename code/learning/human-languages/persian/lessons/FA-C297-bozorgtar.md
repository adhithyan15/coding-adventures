# بزرگتر از (bozorgtar az) — bigger than, and the biggest

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C58-ANIMALS58-02, FA-LEX-C58-ANIMALS58-03, FA-LEX-C164-SEFAT164-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "big", "small" and "better". (*Bozorg*, *kuchek*, *behtar*.)

[PAUSE 1s] *Behtar* already holds the pattern. This lesson opens it up for
every adjective you know.

## Grammar Lens: -tar, and az for "than"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-TAR-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C45-HOME45-02, FA-LEX-C66-TIME66-03, FA-LEX-C70-TOWN70-05, FA-LEX-C70-TOWN70-04, FA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-MI-01] -->

Add **-tar** to the adjective, and put **az**, "from", in front of the thing you
compare against:

> **این اتاق از آن اتاق بزرگتر است.** — *in otâq az ân otâq bozorgtar ast* —
> This room is bigger than that room.

> **قطار از اتوبوس زودتر میرسد.** — *qatâr az otobus zudtar miresad* — The
> train arrives earlier than the bus.

So *kuchektar*, smaller; *zudtar*, earlier; *dirtar*, later. The *az* phrase
usually sits before the *-tar* word; *bozorgtar az ân otâq* is heard as well.
*Behtar* is the same pattern on *beh*, "good", a word that lives today mostly
inside *behtar* and *behtarin*.

## Grammar Lens: -tarin, in front of the noun
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-SUPERLATIVE-TARIN-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C296-GHAZA-01, FA-LEX-C73-TOWN73-02, FA-LEX-C72-TOWN72-05] -->

For "the most", add **-tarin** and move the adjective in front of its noun,
with no ezafe between them:

> **بهترین غذا** — *behtarin ghazâ* — the best food

> **این بهترین رستوران شهر است.** — *in behtarin resturân-e shahr ast* — This
> is the best restaurant in town.

An ordinary adjective follows its noun with the ezafe, *ghazâ-ye khub*. The
*-tarin* one jumps ahead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB 2.1 traces the suffix *-tar* back to an Indo-European suffix that set
one of two things against the other. The same suffix is usually seen at
the end of English **other**, one of two, though EtymDB does not draw that
link itself. *-tarin* is *-tar* with a further *-in*.
