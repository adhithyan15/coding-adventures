Sanskrit, chapter 72: Cook, Wash, Bring, Give.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पचति (pacati) — cooks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for he stands up, then the Sanskrit for it falls.

You'll want to know: पचति.
पचति (pacati) — "(he, she) cooks". माता ओदनं पचति — "mother cooks rice".

What you've built.
What happens to the rice.
