## You'll want to know: શાતા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C489-KUSHAL489-02]; assesses=[] -->

**શાતા** — *śātā* — "ease; relief from pain".

> મીરાએ ફોન કરીને દાદીની શાતા પૂછી.
>
> *Mīrāe phon karīne dādīnī śātā pūchī.*
>
> Mira phoned and asked how her grandmother was feeling.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શાતા** (*śātā*) probably goes back to Sanskrit *śāta*, "joy, pleasure, happiness", which Monier-Williams records from the old word lists; Gujarati uses it for ease after pain, and **શાતા પૂછવી** is to ask after someone who is ill.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
