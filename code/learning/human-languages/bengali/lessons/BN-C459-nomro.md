## You'll want to know: নম্র
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C459-BINOYI459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**নম্র** — *nômrô* — "gentle; soft-spoken".

> নার্সটি রোগীদের সঙ্গে নম্র।
>
> *nārsṭi rogīder sôngge nômrô.*
>
> The nurse is gentle with the patients.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নম্র** (*nômrô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *namra*, "bowing, bent" and so "humble" (Monier-Williams).
