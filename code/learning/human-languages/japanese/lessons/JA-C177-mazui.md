## You'll want to know: まずい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C177-TASTE177-03]; assesses=[] -->

**まずい** — *mazui* — "bad-tasting; unpleasant".

> **この コーヒーは まずいです。** — *kono kōhī wa mazui desu* — "This coffee tastes bad."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まずい** (*mazui*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed; JMdict marks it as usually written in kana, and its kanji spelling was chosen for its meaning (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
