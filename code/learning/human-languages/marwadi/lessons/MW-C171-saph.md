## You'll want to know: साफ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C171-BASAN171-04]; assesses=[] -->

**साफ** — *sāph* — "clean".

> म्हारी थाळी साफ है।
>
> *mhārī thāḷī sāph hai.*
>
> My plate is clean.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साफ** (*sāph*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *sāf*, "clean", which Persian took from Arabic *ṣāfī*, "pure, clear". It does not change: **साफ कपड़ा**, **साफ थाळी**.
