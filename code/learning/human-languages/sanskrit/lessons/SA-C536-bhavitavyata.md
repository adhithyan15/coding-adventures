## You'll want to know: भवितव्यता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C536-ADRSTA536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भवितव्यता** — *bhavitavyatā* — "what must be; destiny".

> यदि भवितव्यता एवम् अस्ति, तर्हि किं करोमि?
>
> *yadi bhavitavyatā evam asti, tarhi kiṃ karomi?*
>
> If that is what must be, what can I do?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भवितव्यता** (*bhavitavyatā*) is built on *bhavitavya*, "what is to be", from the root √bhū, "to be", with the abstract ending *-tā*; MW gives *bhavitavyatā* as inevitable consequence, necessity, destiny (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
