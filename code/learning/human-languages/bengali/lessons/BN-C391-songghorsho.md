# সংঘর্ষ (sôngghôrshô) — a collision; a clash

## You'll want to know: সংঘর্ষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C391-SONGHORSHO391-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সংঘর্ষ** — *sôngghôrshô* — "a collision; a clash".

> মোড়ে একটা বাস আর একটা ট্রাকের সংঘর্ষ হল।
>
> *moṛe ekṭā bās ār ekṭā ṭrāker sôngghôrshô hôlô.*
>
> A bus and a lorry collided at the crossroads.

A word of the news: the paper reports a সংঘর্ষ where a witness says the two vehicles hit each other.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংঘর্ষ** (*sôngghôrshô*) is Sanskrit *saṃgharṣa*, "rubbing together, friction, rivalry" (Monier-Williams); the sense of two vehicles meeting is the modern use.
