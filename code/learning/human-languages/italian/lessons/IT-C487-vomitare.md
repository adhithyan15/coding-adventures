## You'll want to know: vomitare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C487-STOMACO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**vomitare** — "to vomit, to be sick".

> Ho mangiato troppo e ho vomitato.
>
> *I ate too much and was sick.*

More politely: *rimettere*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vomitare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vomitāre* "to vomit often" (EtymDB), from *vomere* "to vomit" (Lewis & Short).
