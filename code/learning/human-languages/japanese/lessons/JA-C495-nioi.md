## You'll want to know: におい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C495-SMELL495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**におい** — *nioi* — "a smell".

> **だいどころから いい においが します。** — *daidokoro kara ii nioi ga shimasu* — "A good smell is coming from the kitchen."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**におい** (*nioi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
