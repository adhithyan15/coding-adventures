## You'll want to know: పెంపకం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C532-PENPAKAM532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పెంపకం** — *pempakaṁ* — "upbringing".

> పిల్లల మంచి నడవడికి తల్లిదండ్రుల పెంపకమే కారణం.
>
> *pillala mañci naḍavaḍiki tallidaṇḍrula pempakamē kāraṇaṁ.*
>
> Good parenting is the reason for the children's good behaviour.

The noun of పెంచు, to raise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పెంపకం** (*pempakaṁ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4436 lists Telugu *pempakamu*, "rearing", beside *pen(u)cu*, "to nourish, to foster, to rear".
