# ふるえる (furueru) — to shiver; to tremble

## You'll want to know: ふるえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C216-UNWELL216-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ふるえる** — *furueru* — "to shiver; to tremble".

> **ねつで からだが ふるえますか。** — *netsu de karada ga furuemasu ka* — "Are you shivering with fever?"

Polite **ふるえます**, *furuemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふるえる** (*furueru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
