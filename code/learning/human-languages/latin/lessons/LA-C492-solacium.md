## You'll want to know: sōlācium, sōlāciī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C492-SOLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sōlācium, sōlāciī** — "solace, relief".

> Librī mihi in senectūte sōlācium sunt.
>
> *Books are a solace to me in old age.*

Neuter, second declension. English *solace* comes from it through Old French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sōlācium** is built on *sōlor* "to comfort, soothe" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *solace* comes from it through Old French.
