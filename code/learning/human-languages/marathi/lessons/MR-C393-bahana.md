## You'll want to know: बहाणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C393-SABAB393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बहाणा** — *bahāṇā* — "a pretext".

> पोटदुखीचा बहाणा करून तो शाळेत गेला नाही.
>
> *poṭdukhīcā bahāṇā karūn to śāḷet gelā nāhī.*
>
> Pretending to have a stomach ache, he did not go to school.

Masculine.

X चा बहाणा करणे, to pretend X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहाणा** (*bahāṇā*) is a loan from Persian *bahāna*, "pretext, excuse".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
