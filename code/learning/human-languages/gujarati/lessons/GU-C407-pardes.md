## You'll want to know: પરદેશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C407-ABROAD407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પરદેશ** — *pardeś* — "abroad; a foreign country".

> મારો ભાઈ ત્રણ વરસથી પરદેશમાં રહે છે.
>
> *māro bhāī traṇ varasthī pardeśmā̃ rahe che.*
>
> My brother has lived abroad for three years.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરદેશ** (*pardeś*) comes from Sanskrit *paradeśa*, "another or a foreign country" (Monier-Williams): any land that is not your own.
