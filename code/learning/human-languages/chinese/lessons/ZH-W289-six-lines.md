# Six lines to a friend, a story with advice at the end

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C289-BURAN-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-RUGUO-JIUHUI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A trip of yours went wrong: a train missed, a bag lost, rain where there should have been sun. A friend is about to make the same trip.

[PAUSE 1s] This lesson tells them what happened, and what to do.

- [YOU RECALL: say 不然 — *bùrán*, otherwise — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say 如果 … 就会 … — *rúguǒ … jiù huì …* — **R2**]

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-SEQUENCE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YINWEI-SUOYI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-RUGUO-JIU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Six connected lines are not six sentences in a row: each leans on the one before, and the reader does not have to ask what happened next.

[PAUSE 2s] You own what does the leaning. **Order words**: 先, 然后, 突然, 最后. **The time at the front**: 那天, 第二天, 的时候. **The subject said once**: 我 in the first line, then the order word or the time. **因为 … 所以 …** gives the reason; **如果 … 就 …** or **应该** turns the story into advice.

[PAUSE 2s] Your reader is the friend. Your purpose is to save them the same trouble.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-SEQUENCE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-SHIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C279-TURAN-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YINWEI-SUOYI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YINGGAI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-RUGUO-JIU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-RUGUO-JIUHUI-01, ZH-LEX-C289-BURAN-01, ZH-LEX-C278-ZUIHOU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-COMPLETION-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-GUO-EXPERIENCE-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **six lines** to your friend. Here is a frame for each; the words are yours, and there is no model to copy.

- Line 1: when and where, and what you did first, with 先 and 了.
- Line 2: what came next, with 然后.
- Line 3: what went wrong, with 突然, or with 的时候 and 正在.
- Line 4: how it ended, with 最后, or what you had never done before, with 过.
- Line 5: the reason it went wrong, with 因为 … 所以 ….
- Line 6: your advice, with 如果 … 就 … or 应该.

[PAUSE 2s] A word whose characters you have not written goes in pinyin, inside the line, with its tone marks. Every character comes from the 227 you have learned, each in the order you learned it, each in the same size of square.

[PAUSE 2s] No Chinese commas or full stops: leave a space between clauses, as this book prints them.

[PAUSE 3s] Write the six lines. Then read them once as your friend would, top to bottom: would they know what to do?
