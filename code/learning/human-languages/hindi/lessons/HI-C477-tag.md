## You'll want to know: टाँग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C477-HATHELI477-04]; assesses=[] -->

**टाँग** — *ṭā̃g* — "a leg".

> सीढ़ी से गिरकर अरुण की टाँग टूट गई।
>
> *sīṛhī se girkar Aruṇ kī ṭā̃g ṭūṭ gaī.*
>
> Arun broke his leg falling down the stairs.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टाँग** (*ṭā̃g*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *ṭā̃g*, "leg", under Sanskrit *ṭaṅka* (CDIAL 5428). *pair* is the foot; *ṭā̃g* is the whole leg.
