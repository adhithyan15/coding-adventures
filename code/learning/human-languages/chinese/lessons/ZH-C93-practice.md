## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-TOO-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C93-TAIHAOLE-01, ZH-LEX-C93-YOUDIANR-01, ZH-LEX-C93-ZANG-01, ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-04, ZH-LEX-HAOKAN-01] -->

> **A:** *zhège zěnmeyàng* — How's this one?
>
> **B:** 太大了
>
> *tài dà le* — Too big.
>
> **A:** 那个呢
>
> *nàge ne* — And that one?
>
> **B:** 有点儿小
>
> *yǒudiǎnr xiǎo* — A bit small.
>
> **A:** 这个很好看
>
> *zhège hěn hǎokàn* — This one's nice.
>
> **B:** *yǒudiǎnr zāng* — It's a bit dirty.
>
> **A:** 我们去吃饭
>
> *wǒmen qù chī fàn* — Let's go and eat, then.
>
> **B:** 太好了
>
> *tài hǎo le* — Great!

Three strengths of complaint, 有点儿, 很 and 太 … 了, and one delight to finish: 太好了.

## What you've built — three strengths
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-TOO-01, ZH-SCRIPT-TAI-01, ZH-LEX-C93-TAIHAOLE-01, ZH-LEX-C93-YOUDIANR-01, ZH-LEX-C93-GANJING-01, ZH-LEX-C93-ZANG-01] -->

- **很大** *hěn dà* — big; a plain statement.
- **有点儿大** *yǒudiǎnr dà* — a bit big, and I'd rather it wasn't.
- **太大了** *tài dà le* — too big.
- **太好了** *tài hǎo le* — great!

**Listen.** *Fángjiān zěnmeyàng?*

**Speak.** Answer it three ways: *gānjìng*, a bit dirty, too dirty.
