## You'll want to know: बारसे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C318-BARSE318-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बारसे** — *bārse* — "a naming ceremony".

> बाळाचं बारसं बाराव्या दिवशी झालं.
>
> *bāḷācaṁ bārsaṁ bārāvyā divśī jhālaṁ.*
>
> The baby's naming ceremony was on the twelfth day.

Neuter.

Neuter, written बारसं in a sentence like मोठं; held on the twelfth day after a birth, from बारा, twelve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बारसे** (*bārse*) is built on *bārā*, 'twelve', the day the rite falls on; CDIAL lists Marathi *bārāvā*, 'twelfth', under Sanskrit *dvādaśama* (CDIAL 6660).
