## You'll want to know: धोती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C190-DHOTI190-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धोती** — *dhotī* — "a dhoti".

> दादा धोती पैरै है।
>
> *dādā dhotī pairai hai.*
>
> Grandfather wears a dhoti.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धोती** (*dhotī*) comes from a reconstructed *\*dhōtta*, "cloth"; CDIAL lists Hindi, Gujarati and Sindhi *dhotī* (CDIAL 6881). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
