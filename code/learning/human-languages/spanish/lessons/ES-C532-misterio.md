## You'll want to know: el misterio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C532-SECRETO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el misterio** — "the mystery".

> Lo que pasó aquella noche sigue siendo un misterio.
>
> *What happened that night is still a mystery.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*misterio* comes from Latin *mystērium*, a learned word taken from Greek *mystḗrion* 'a secret rite' (EtymDB).
