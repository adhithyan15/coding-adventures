## You'll want to know: ざんぎょうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C451-COMMUTE451-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ざんぎょうする** — *zangyō suru* — "to work overtime".

> **こんばんも ざんぎょうします。** — *konban mo zangyō shimasu* — "I am working overtime tonight too."

Polite **ざんぎょうします**, *zangyōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ちちが ざんぎょうしました。**, *chichi ga zangyō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ざんぎょうする** (*zangyō suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *zangyō* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zan*, "leftover" (Middle Chinese *dzan*) and *gyō*, "business" (*ngjaep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
