## You'll want to know: el empresario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C592-EMPRESA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el empresario** — "the businessman, the employer".

> Su padre es empresario.
>
> *His father is a businessman.*

A woman is *la empresaria*. *Los empresarios* can mean employers as a group.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*empresario* is built on *empresa*, which was borrowed from Italian *impresa* 'undertaking', built on Latin *prehendere* 'to take hold of' (EtymDB).
