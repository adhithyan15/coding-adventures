Kannada, chapter 438: Weighing the Bags.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತೂಗು (tūgu) — to weigh; to swing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to scan, then the Kannada for a pilot.

You'll want to know: ತೂಗು.
ತೂಗು — tūgu — "to weigh; to swing".
ಸಿಬ್ಬಂದಿ ನನ್ನ ಸೂಟ್ಕೇಸನ್ನು ತೂಗಿದರು. sibbandi nanna sūṭkēsannu tūgidaru. The staff weighed my suitcase.
Past (I …): ತೂಗಿದೆ, tūgide.
The noun beside it is ತೂಕ, weight; ತೊಟ್ಟಿಲು ತೂಗು (tūgu) is to rock a cradle.

The word, taken apart.
ತೂಗು (tūgu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3376 lists Kannada tūgu, tūṅku, "to weigh, to swing, to rock a cradle".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮಿತಿ (miti) — a limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a pilot, then the Kannada for to weigh.

You'll want to know: ಮಿತಿ.
ಮಿತಿ — miti — "a limit".
ಸಾಮಾನಿನ ತೂಕಕ್ಕೆ ಒಂದು ಮಿತಿ ಇದೆ. sāmānina tūkakke ondu miti ide. There is a limit on the weight of the luggage.
ವೇಗದ ಮಿತಿ (miti) is a speed limit.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಿತಿ (miti) is borrowed from Sanskrit miti, "measuring, measure, weight" (Monier-Williams), from the root mā, "to measure".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಮೀರು.
ಮೀರು — mīru — "to exceed; to go beyond".
ನನ್ನ ಚೀಲದ ತೂಕ ಮಿತಿಯನ್ನು ಮೀರಿತು. nanna cīlada tūka mitiyannu mīritu. The weight of my bag went over the limit.
Past (I …): ಮೀರಿದೆ, mīride.
What is exceeded takes -ಅನ್ನು: ಮಿತಿಯನ್ನು ಮೀರು (mīru).

The word, taken apart.
ಮೀರು (mīru) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4884 lists Kannada mīṟu, "to go beyond, to exceed, to transgress".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ (vimānayāna) — air travel; aviation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a limit, then the Kannada for to exceed.

You'll want to know: ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ.
ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ — vimānayāna — "air travel; aviation".
ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ ಈಗ ಮೊದಲಿಗಿಂತ ಅಗ್ಗವಾಗಿದೆ. vimānayāna īga modaliginta aggavāgide. Air travel is cheaper now than before.
ವಿಮಾನ, an aeroplane, plus ಯಾನ, going.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ (vimānayāna) is built from two Sanskrit words: vimāna, "a flying car of the gods" (CDIAL 11823), now an aeroplane, and yāna, "going, a vehicle" (CDIAL 10459).

ಹೆಲಿಕಾಪ್ಟರ್ ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಮೇಲೆ ಇಳಿಯಿತು. helikāpṭar beṭṭada mēle iḷiyitu. The helicopter landed on the hill.
ಇಳಿ, to get down, also means to land.
