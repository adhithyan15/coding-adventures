## You'll want to know: l'aveu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C295-AVEUX-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'aveu** — "the confession, the avowal".

> Ses aveux ont surpris tout le monde.
>
> *His confession surprised everyone.*

Often plural: *passer aux aveux* — to confess.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aveu* is built on *avouer* "to confess", which is inherited from Latin *advocāre* "to call to" (Lewis & Short).
