## You'll want to know: picar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C695-FAUNA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**picar** — "to bite, to sting (insects); to be spicy; to nibble".

> Me picó un mosquito en el brazo.
>
> *A mosquito bit me on the arm.*

*Esta salsa pica* — this sauce is hot. *Picar algo* — to have a snack.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*picar* comes from Vulgar Latin *piccāre* "to prick, to strike" (EtymDB); where that verb came from is not settled.
