## You'll want to know: embater
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C409-FILAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**embater** — "to crash into, to hit".

> A mota embateu num carro parado.
>
> *The motorbike crashed into a parked car.*

*Embater em* — to crash into.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*embater* is built from *em-* and *bater* 'to hit', which is inherited from Late Latin *battere*, Latin *battuere* 'to beat' (Lewis & Short).
