Persian, chapter 358: On the stove.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قابلمه (qâblame) — a cooking pot, a saucepan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for yeast, then the Persian for a clay oven.

You'll want to know: قابلمه.
قابلمه (qâblame) — "a cooking pot, a saucepan".
آب را در قابلمه ریخت. Âb râ dar qâblame rikht. She poured the water into the pot.
Plural qâblame-hâ. The lid is dar-e qâblame.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of qâblame "a cooking pot" is not settled, and EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
جوش آمدن (jush âmadan) — to come to the boil.

Before the new one: say the Persian for a clay oven, then the Persian for a cooking pot.

You'll want to know: جوش آمدن.
جوش آمدن (jush âmadan) — "to come to the boil".
وقتی آب جوش آمد، برنج را در آن ریخت. Vaqti âb jush âmad, berenj râ dar ân rikht. When the water came to the boil, she put the rice in.
In speech also "to lose one's temper". Present jush miâyad.

The word, taken apart.
jush "boiling, a simmer" is a Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; âmadan "to come" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cooking pot, then the Persian for to come to the boil.

You'll want to know: قل زدن.
قل زدن (qol zadan) — "to bubble, to simmer".
خورش آرام قل میزد. Khoresh ârâm qol mizad. The stew was simmering gently.
Qol imitates the sound of bubbling. Present qol mizanad.

The word, taken apart.
qol is a sound-word that imitates bubbling; zadan "to strike" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
جا افتادن (jâ oftâdan) — to be well cooked (of a stew); to settle in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to come to the boil, then the Persian for to bubble.

You'll want to know: جا افتادن.
جا افتادن (jâ oftâdan) — "to be well cooked (of a stew); to settle in".
خورش بعد از دو ساعت جا افتاد. Khoresh ba'd az do sâ'at jâ oftâd. After two hours the stew was nicely cooked.
Of a stew, the flavours have come together. Of a bone, "to go back into place"; of an idea, "to be accepted".

The word, taken apart.
jâ "place" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and oftâdan "to fall" is native Persian: to fall into place.

Lesson 5 of 5.
کفگیر (kafgir) — a slotted spoon, a skimmer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to bubble, then the Persian for to be well cooked.

You'll want to know: کفگیر.
کفگیر (kafgir) — "a slotted spoon, a skimmer".
برنج را با کفگیر در بشقاب کشید. Berenj râ bâ kafgir dar boshqâb keshid. She served the rice onto the plate with a skimmer.
kaf "foam" + gir "taker". Kafgir be tah-e dig khord, "the skimmer hit the bottom of the pot": nothing is left.

The word, taken apart.
kafgir joins kaf "foam" and gir-, the present stem of gereftan "to take": the foam-taker. EtymDB lists the word without a parent.
