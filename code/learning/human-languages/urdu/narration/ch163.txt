Urdu, chapter 163: Pointless, Forced, Free, Pure, Embarrassed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فضول (fuzūl) — pointless, wasteful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for Indian, then the Urdu for grey.

You'll want to know: فضول.
فضول — fuzūl — "pointless, wasteful".

The word, taken apart.
فضول (fuzūl) is a loan from Arabic fuḍūl, "excesses, what is left over", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is f-ḍ-l (Lane). It is the plural of faḍl, "excess".
