## You'll want to know: dichiarare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C427-DOGANA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dichiarare** — "to declare".

> Ha qualcosa da dichiarare?
>
> *Do you have anything to declare?*

*Niente da dichiarare* — nothing to declare.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dichiarare* is inherited from Latin *dēclārāre* 'to make clear' (EtymDB), built on *clārus* 'clear': Latin *cl-* became *chi-*, as in *chiaro*.
