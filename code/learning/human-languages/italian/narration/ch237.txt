Italian, chapter 237: To warn, To burn, To throw away, To calculate, To hit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
avvisare — to warn, to let know.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to be bored, then the Italian for to have fun.

You'll want to know: avvisare.
avvisare — "to warn, to let know".

The word, taken apart.
avvisare is the verb of avviso, "a notice", which from Old French avis. (EtymDB 2.1).
