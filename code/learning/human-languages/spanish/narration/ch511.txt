Spanish, chapter 511: Hacer y parecer.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
parecer — to seem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to hate, then the Spanish for to surprise.

You'll want to know: parecer.
parecer — "to seem".
Parece fácil, pero no lo es. It seems easy, but it is not.
¿Qué te parece? — what do you think? It takes a z in parezco.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: parecer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: parecer, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with parecer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for to hate, then the Spanish for to surprise, then say parecer again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does parecer mean? ("To seem".) Say it once more.
