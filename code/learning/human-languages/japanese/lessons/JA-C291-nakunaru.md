## You'll want to know: なくなる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C291-SOUSHIKI291-01]; assesses=[] -->

**なくなる** — *nakunaru* — "to pass away".

> **きょねん、そぼが なくなりました。** — *kyonen, sobo ga nakunarimashita* — "My grandmother passed away last year."

Polite **なくなります**, *nakunarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **そふが なくなりました。**, *sofu ga nakunarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なくなる** (*nakunaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
