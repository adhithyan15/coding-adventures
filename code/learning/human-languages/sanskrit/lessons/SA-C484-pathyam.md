## You'll want to know: पथ्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C484-AYURVEDA484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पथ्यम्** — *pathyam* — "a wholesome diet".

> आतुरः पथ्यं खादेत्।
>
> *āturaḥ pathyaṃ khādet.*
>
> A patient should eat wholesome food.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पथ्यम्** (*pathyam*) is built on *patha*, "a way": MW gives "belonging to the way", then "suitable, wholesome (esp. of diet)" (MW).
