## You'll want to know: 必须
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C362-MUST362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**必须** — *bìxū* — "must; it is required that".

> **上课的时候必须关手机** *shàngkè de shíhou bìxū guān shǒujī* — You have to switch your phone off in class.

The new character, in pieces: 须 — nine strokes in two pieces: on the left three left-falling strokes, one under another; on the right the page piece of 题, six strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**必须** (*bìxū*) is built from 必 'surely' and *xū*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjit* and *sju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'certain' and the second 'what the mind wants' and, quoting an older dictionary, 'the hair of the face'.
