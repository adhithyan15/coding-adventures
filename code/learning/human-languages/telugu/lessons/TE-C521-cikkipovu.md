# చిక్కిపోవు (cikkipōvu) — to grow thin

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-01, TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for frailty, then the Telugu for to grow weak.

## You'll want to know: చిక్కిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**చిక్కిపోవు** — *cikkipōvu* — "to grow thin".

> జబ్బు తరువాత అతను బాగా చిక్కిపోయాడు.
>
> *jabbu taruvāta atanu bāgā cikkipōyāḍu.*
>
> After his illness he grew very thin.

Past (I …): చిక్కిపోయాను, *cikkipōyānu*.

చిక్కు, to grow lean, + పోవు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చిక్కిపోవు** (*cikkipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2503 lists Telugu *cikku*, "to become lean or thin", with Tamil *ciṅku*, "to wane"; the verb ends in *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
