# guójí — citizenship; the country one belongs to

## You'll want to know: guójí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C455-FORMS455-02]; assesses=[] -->

*guójí* — "citizenship; the country one belongs to".

> *tā zhù zài Běijīng dànshì tā de guójí shì Yīngguó* — He lives in Beijing, but he's a UK citizen.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guójí* is built from 国 'a country' and *jí* 'a register', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwok* and *dzjek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a written roll.
