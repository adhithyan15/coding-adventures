## You'll want to know: छीनना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C289-BHAGA289-02]; assesses=[] -->

**छीनना** — *chīnnā* — "to snatch".

> उसने मीरा के हाथ से मोबाइल छीन लिया।
>
> *usne Mīrā ke hāth se mobāil chīn liyā.*
>
> He snatched the mobile phone out of Mira's hand.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने थैला छीना, *usne thailā chīnā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छीनना** (*chīnnā*) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit *chinna*, "cut off" (CDIAL 5047): what is snatched is torn away from its owner. It often takes **लेना**: **छीन लिया**, snatched it away.
