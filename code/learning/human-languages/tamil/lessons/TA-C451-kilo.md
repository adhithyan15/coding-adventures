# கிலோ (kilō) — a kilo

## You'll want to know: கிலோ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C451-VALAKAM451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**கிலோ** — *kilō* — "a kilo".

> ஒரு கிலோ தக்காளி எவ்வளவு?
>
> *oru kilō takkāḷi evvaḷavu?*
>
> How much is a kilo of tomatoes?

Numbers come before it: இரண்டு கிலோ அரிசி.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கிலோ** (*kilō*) is a loan from English *kilo*.
