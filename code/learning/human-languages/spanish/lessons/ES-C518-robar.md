## You'll want to know: robar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C518-ROBO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**robar** — "to steal, to rob".

> Me robaron la cartera en el metro.
>
> *Someone stole my wallet on the metro.*

*Robar algo a alguien* — to steal something from someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*robar* comes from a Germanic verb *\*raubōn* 'to rob, to plunder', through Medieval Latin *raubāre* (EtymDB). English *rob* goes back to the same Germanic verb.
