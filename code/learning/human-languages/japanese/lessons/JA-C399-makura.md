## You'll want to know: まくら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C399-MAKURA399-01]; assesses=[] -->

**まくら** — *makura* — "a pillow".

> **この まくらは かたいです。** — *kono makura wa katai desu* — "This pillow is hard."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まくら** (*makura*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
