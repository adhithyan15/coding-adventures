## You'll want to know: こうてんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C526-PROGRESS526-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こうてんする** — *kōten suru* — "to turn for the better".

> **もし てんきが こうてんしたら、しあいを します。** — *moshi tenki ga kōtenshitara, shiai o shimasu* — "If the weather improves, we will hold the match."

Polite **こうてんします**, *kōtenshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **びょうきが こうてんしました。**, *byōki ga kōtenshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうてんする** (*kōten suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *kōten* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "pleasing" (Middle Chinese *xawX*) and *ten*, "revolve" (*trjwenX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
