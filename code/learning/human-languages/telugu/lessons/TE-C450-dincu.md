## You'll want to know: దించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C450-PARKINGU450-04]; assesses=[] -->

**దించు** — *diñcu* — "to unload; to lower".

> కూలీలు లారీ నుంచి బస్తాలు దించారు.
>
> *kūlīlu lārī nuñci bastālu diñcāru.*
>
> The labourers unloaded the sacks from the lorry.

Past (I …): దించాను, *diñcānu*.

The causative of దిగు, to get down: to bring something down. బరువు దించు, to put down a load.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దించు** (*diñcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 502 lists Telugu *dincu* beside *digu*, "to descend, to get off".
