Hindi, chapter 446: Nature.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रकृति (prakṛti) — nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the high tide, then the Hindi for a shell.

You'll want to know: प्रकृति.
प्रकृति — prakṛti — "nature".
पहाड़ों में प्रकृति बहुत सुंदर है। pahāṛoṁ meṁ prakṛti bahut sundar hai. Nature is very beautiful in the mountains.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रकृति (prakṛti) is a tatsama, Sanskrit prakṛti, "the original or natural form of a thing" (Monier-Williams); it also means a person's nature, their temperament.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पर्यावरण (paryāvaraṇ) — the environment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a shell, then the Hindi for nature.

You'll want to know: पर्यावरण.
पर्यावरण — paryāvaraṇ — "the environment".
पेड़ काटना पर्यावरण के लिए बुरा है। peṛ kāṭnā paryāvaraṇ ke liye burā hai. Cutting down trees is bad for the environment.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पर्यावरण (paryāvaraṇ) is a modern coinage from Sanskrit pari, "around", and āvaraṇa, "covering" (Monier-Williams): what covers us all round.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पौधा (paudhā) — a plant; a sapling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for nature, then the Hindi for the environment.

You'll want to know: पौधा.
पौधा — paudhā — "a plant; a sapling".
मेरे कमरे में एक छोटा पौधा है। mere kamre meṁ ek choṭā paudhā hai. There is a small plant in my room.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पौधा (paudhā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit pravṛddha, "grown up, great" (CDIAL 8807), which lists Hindi paudhā, "young plants for transplanting"; Hindi widened it to any plant.

Lesson 4 of 5.
खिलना (khilnā) — to bloom; to open (of a flower).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the environment, then the Hindi for a plant.

You'll want to know: खिलना.
खिलना — khilnā — "to bloom; to open (of a flower)".
वसंत में बगीचे में फूल खिलते हैं। vasant meṁ bagīce meṁ phūl khilte haiṁ. In spring the flowers bloom in the garden.
Past (it …): वह फूल सुबह खिला, vah phūl subah khilā.

The word, taken apart.
खिलना (khilnā) goes back to khiḍ, khil, "be open, expand", forms CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3882), which lists Hindi khilnā, "to open, bloom". A face खिलता too, when it lights up.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कुम्हलाना (kumhlānā) — to wilt; to fade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a plant, then the Hindi for to bloom.

You'll want to know: कुम्हलाना.
कुम्हलाना — kumhlānā — "to wilt; to fade".
पानी के बिना पौधे कुम्हला गए। pānī ke binā paudhe kumhlā gae. Without water the plants wilted.
Past (it …): वह फूल धूप में कुम्हलाया, vah phūl dhūp meṁ kumhlāyā.

The word, taken apart.
कुम्हलाना (kumhlānā) goes back to kōmh, "wither", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3524), which lists Hindi kumhlānā, "to fade, wither".
