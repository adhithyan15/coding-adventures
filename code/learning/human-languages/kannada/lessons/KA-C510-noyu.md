# ನೋಯು (nōyu) — to ache; to be hurt

## You'll want to know: ನೋಯು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C510-NOVU510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೋಯು** — *nōyu* — "to ache; to be hurt".

> ತುಂಬಾ ನಡೆದಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ನನ್ನ ಕಾಲು ನೋಯುತ್ತಿದೆ.
>
> *tumbā naḍediddarinda nanna kālu nōyuttide.*
>
> My leg aches because I walked a lot.

Past (I …): ನೊಂದೆ, *nonde*.

The noun is ನೋವು, pain. Feelings ache too: ಅವನ ಮಾತಿನಿಂದ ನಾನು ನೊಂದೆ, his words hurt me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೋಯು** (*nōyu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3793 lists Kannada *nōyu*, "to feel pain, be grieved", beside Tamil *nō*. The noun ನೋವು, pain, is built on it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
