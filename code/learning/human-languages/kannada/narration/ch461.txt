Lesson 1 of 6.
ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹ (atithigṛha) — a guest house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a receptionist, then the Kannada for a counter.

You'll want to know: ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹ.
ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹ — atithigṛha — "a guest house".
ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೊಠಡಿಗಳು ಅಗ್ಗ. sarkāri atithigṛhadalli koṭhaḍigaḷu agga. Rooms in the government guest house are cheap.
ಅತಿಥಿ, a guest, plus ಗೃಹ, a house.

The word, taken apart.
ಅತಿಥಿಗೃಹ (atithigṛha) is built from two Sanskrit words: atithi, "a guest", and gṛha, "a house" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಠೇವಣಿ (ṭhēvaṇi) — a security deposit; a deposit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a guest house, then the Kannada for a hostel.

You'll want to know: ಠೇವಣಿ.
ಠೇವಣಿ — ṭhēvaṇi — "a security deposit; a deposit".
ಮನೆಯ ಬಾಡಿಗೆಗೆ ಅವರು ಹತ್ತು ತಿಂಗಳ ಠೇವಣಿ ಕೇಳಿದರು. maneya bāḍigege avaru hattu tiṅgaḷa ṭhēvaṇi kēḷidaru. For renting the house they asked for ten months as a deposit.
ಠೇವಣಿ (ṭhēvaṇi) ಇಡು, to make a deposit; ಮುಂಗಡ is an advance.

The word, taken apart.
ಠೇವಣಿ (ṭhēvaṇi) is usually traced to Marathi ṭhevṇī, "a thing placed, a deposit", from a verb meaning "to put, to keep"; the path into Kannada is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಮರುಪಾವತಿ (marupāvati) — a refund; a repayment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a security deposit, then the Kannada for a locker.

You'll want to know: ಮರುಪಾವತಿ.
ಮರುಪಾವತಿ — marupāvati — "a refund; a repayment".
ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ರದ್ದು ಮಾಡಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ನನಗೆ ಮರುಪಾವತಿ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. ṭikeṭ raddu māḍiddarinda nanage marupāvati sikkitu. Because I cancelled the ticket I got a refund.
ಮರು-, back or again, plus ಪಾವತಿ, a payment, as in ಪಾವತಿಸು, to pay.

The word, taken apart.
ಮರುಪಾವತಿ (marupāvati) joins the native prefix maṟu, "again, back" (DEDR 4766), and pāvati, "a payment", whose own source is not settled.
