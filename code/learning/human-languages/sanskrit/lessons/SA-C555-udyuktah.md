## You'll want to know: उद्युक्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C555-PRERANA555-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्युक्तः** — *udyuktaḥ* — "all set; prepared".

> यदि सर्वे उद्युक्ताः सन्ति, तर्हि वयम् अधुना गच्छामः।
>
> *yadi sarve udyuktāḥ santi, tarhi vayam adhunā gacchāmaḥ.*
>
> If everyone is ready, we leave now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्युक्तः** (*udyuktaḥ*) is built on *ud*, "up", and the root √yuj, "to yoke", as its past participle: yoked up; MW gives *udyukta* as prepared or ready for, zealously active (MW).
