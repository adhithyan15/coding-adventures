## You'll want to know: la ceremonia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C548-BANQUETE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la ceremonia** — "the ceremony".

> La ceremonia fue corta y bonita.
>
> *The ceremony was short and lovely.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ceremonia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *caerimōnia* 'a sacred rite' (EtymDB).
