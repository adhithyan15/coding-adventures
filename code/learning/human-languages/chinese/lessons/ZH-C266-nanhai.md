## You'll want to know: 男孩
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C266-AGE266-03]; assesses=[] -->

**男孩** — *nánhái* — "boy".

> **那个男孩很高** *nàge nánhái hěn gāo* — That boy is tall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**男孩** (*nánhái*) is built from 男 'male' and 孩 'a child', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nom* and *hoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for what I have and can do.
