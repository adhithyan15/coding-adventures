Italian, chapter 317: Cin cin.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
lo spumante — the sparkling wine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the bow, then the Italian for the balloon.

You'll want to know: lo spumante.
lo spumante — "the sparkling wine".
A mezzanotte apriamo lo spumante. At midnight we open the sparkling wine.
Built on spuma, ‘foam’.

The word, taken apart.
spumante is the present participle of spumare "to foam", from Latin spūma "foam" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: stappare.
stappare — "to uncork, to open (a bottle)".
Chi stappa la bottiglia? Who is opening the bottle?
The opposite of tappare, to stop up.

The word, taken apart.
stappare is s- "un-" with tappo "cork", from Frankish tappo (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
il cavatappi — the corkscrew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the cork, then the Italian for to uncork.

You'll want to know: il cavatappi.
il cavatappi — "the corkscrew".
Non trovo il cavatappi! I cannot find the corkscrew!
Cava (pulls out) + tappi (corks). Plural unchanged.

The word, taken apart.
cavatappi joins cava, from cavare "to pull out", Latin cavāre (Lewis & Short), and tappi "corks".
