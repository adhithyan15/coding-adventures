## You'll want to know: लोकसंख्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C354-NAGARIK354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लोकसंख्या** — *loksaṅkhyā* — "population".

> पुण्याची लोकसंख्या दरवर्षी वाढत आहे.
>
> *puṇyācī loksaṅkhyā darvarṣī vāḍhat āhe.*
>
> Pune's population is growing every year.

Feminine.

लोक + संख्या, people + number.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोकसंख्या** (*loksaṅkhyā*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *loka*, "world, people", and *saṁkhyā*, "number" (Monier-Williams).
