Bengali, chapter 357: Reporting Findings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
প্রবন্ধ (prôbôndhô) — an essay; an article.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a yardstick, then the Bengali for to assess.

You'll want to know: প্রবন্ধ.
প্রবন্ধ — prôbôndhô — "an essay; an article".
মীরা পত্রিকায় একটা প্রবন্ধ লিখল। mīrā pôtrikāy ekṭā prôbôndhô likhlô. Mira wrote an article in a magazine.

The word, taken apart.
প্রবন্ধ (prôbôndhô) is Sanskrit prabandha, a binding together (Monier-Williams), and so a connected piece of writing. Bengali uses it for an essay or an article.

Lesson 2 of 5.
বিষয় (bishôy) — a subject; a topic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to assess, then the Bengali for an essay.

You'll want to know: বিষয়.
বিষয় — bishôy — "a subject; a topic".
তোমার প্রবন্ধের বিষয় কী? tomār prôbôndher bishôy kī? What is your essay about?

The word, taken apart.
বিষয় (bishôy) is Sanskrit viṣaya, a sphere or an object of attention (Monier-Williams). In Bengali it is the subject of a talk, a book or a school course.
