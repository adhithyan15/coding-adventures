## You'll want to know: झगड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C346-BAHAS346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झगड़ना** — *jhagaṛnā* — "to quarrel".

> दोनों भाई खिलौने के लिए झगड़ते हैं।
>
> *donõ bhāī khilaune ke liye jhagaṛte haiṁ.*
>
> The two brothers fight over the toy.

Past (he …): वह अपने भाई से झगड़ा, *vah apne bhāī se jhagṛā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झगड़ना** (*jhagaṛnā*) is inherited from *\*jhagaḍ*, "quarrel", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5321).
