## You'll want to know: ہانڈی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C509-CHHACHH509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ہانڈی** — *hāṅḍī* — "an earthen cooking pot".

> دادی مٹی کی ہانڈی میں دال پکاتی تھیں۔
>
> *dādī miṭṭī kī hāṅḍī meṅ dāl pakātī thīṅ.*
>
> Grandmother used to cook lentils in a clay pot.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہانڈی** (*hāṅḍī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *hā̃ḍī*, "an earthen cooking pot", under *\*hāṇḍa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 14050).
