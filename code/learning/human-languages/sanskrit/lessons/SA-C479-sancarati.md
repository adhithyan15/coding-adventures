## You'll want to know: सञ्चरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C479-VYADHI479-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सञ्चरति** — *sañcarati* — "goes about; spreads".

> ग्रीष्मे ज्वरः ग्रामे सञ्चरति।
>
> *grīṣme jvaraḥ grāme sañcarati.*
>
> In summer fever goes about the village.

Past (it …): ज्वरः ग्रामे सञ्चरितवान्, *jvaraḥ grāme sañcaritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सञ्चरति** (*sañcarati*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √car, "to move", the root of **चरति**; MW gives "to go or wander about" (MW).
