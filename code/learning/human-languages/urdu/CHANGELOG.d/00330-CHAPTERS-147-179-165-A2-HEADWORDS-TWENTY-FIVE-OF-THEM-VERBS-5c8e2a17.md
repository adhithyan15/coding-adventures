## Chapters 147-179: 165 A2 headwords, twenty-five of them verbs

The first of four Urdu A2 vocabulary tranches. It has thirty-three chapters of
five words each, in four runs of at most nine chapters. Each run closes with
two reviews.

- **Verbs (25):** school (*پڑھانا*, *یاد کرنا*), feelings (*چیخنا*,
  *مسکرانا*, *گھبرانا*), money (*چننا*, *بدلنا*, *ادھار لینا*, *قرض دینا*,
  *بچانا*), getting about (*پار کرنا*, *گھومنا*, *سیر کرنا*, *کودنا*,
  *پھسلنا*), paperwork (*چھاپنا*, *دستخط کرنا*, *فون کرنا*) and a cold
  (*چھینکنا*, *کھانسنا*, *سانس لینا*).
- **Nouns and qualities (140):** illness (*بیماری*, *زکام*, *سر درد*), the
  clinic (*نرس*, *مریض*, *ایمبولینس*), January to March, festivals (*تہوار*,
  *نیا سال*), the town (*محلہ*, *فٹ پاتھ*, *چوک*, *بندرگاہ*), linking words
  (*اس لیے*, *چونکہ*, *اگر*, *ورنہ*), character (*مہربان*, *سمجھدار*,
  *محنتی*), texture and shape, belonging (*مقامی*, *پاکستانی*), the home,
  gadgets, jewellery, sweets, birds, music and fairs, family (*والدین*,
  *چچا*, *چچی*), language (*جملہ*, *حرف*, *مطلب*), peace and war, the
  garden, grains, the trades and the court (*وزیر*, *بادشاہ*).
- **Screening:** every headword is written with letters the course has
  taught, so script closure gains no violation. A candidate that an earlier
  lesson already uses untaught was dropped. Glosses that would collide with a
  taught word were sharpened: *زکام* is "a head cold" beside *سردی*, *چوک* "a
  town square" beside *چوراہا*, *پڑھانا* "to tutor" beside *سکھانا*.
- **Language review:** every spelling and romanization was checked. In this
  tranche the review corrected *machherā* and respelled the eyebrow as
  *بھوں*.
