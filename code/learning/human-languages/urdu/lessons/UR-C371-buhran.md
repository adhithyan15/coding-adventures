## You'll want to know: بحران
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C371-BUHRAN371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بحران** — *buhrān* — "a crisis".

> اگر بارش نہیں ہوئی تو پانی کا بحران ہو گا۔
>
> *agar bārish nahīṅ huī to pānī kā buhrān ho gā.*
>
> If it does not rain, there will be a water crisis.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بحران** (*buhrān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *buḥrān*, "the crisis of a fever", the turning point of an illness.
