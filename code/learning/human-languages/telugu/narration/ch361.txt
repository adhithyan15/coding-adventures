Telugu, chapter 361: On the Battlefield.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
యుద్ధభూమి (yuddhabhūmi) — a battlefield.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a bow, then the Telugu for a gun.

You'll want to know: యుద్ధభూమి.
యుద్ధభూమి — yuddhabhūmi — "a battlefield".
యుద్ధభూమిలో రెండు సైన్యాలు ఎదురయ్యాయి. yuddhabhūmilō reṇḍu sainyālu edurayyāyi. Two armies met on the battlefield.
యుద్ధం, war, and భూమి, ground.

The word, taken apart.
యుద్ధభూమి (yuddhabhūmi) is borrowed from Sanskrit: yuddha, "battle" (CDIAL 10499), and bhūmi, "earth, ground" (CDIAL 9557).

Lesson 2 of 5.
దళం (daḷaṁ) — a troop; a squad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a gun, then the Telugu for a battlefield.

You'll want to know: దళం.
దళం — daḷaṁ — "a troop; a squad".
ఒక చిన్న దళం కోటను కాపాడింది. oka cinna daḷaṁ kōṭanu kāpāḍindi. A small troop defended the fort.
Plural: దళాలు, daḷālu.
Also a petal or a leaf: a part of a whole.

The word, taken apart.
దళం (daḷaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit dala, "a party, a band" (CDIAL 6215); CDIAL notes that this sense may come from Dravidian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ముట్టడించు (muṭṭaḍiñcu) — to besiege; to lay siege.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a battlefield, then the Telugu for a troop.

You'll want to know: ముట్టడించు.
ముట్టడించు — muṭṭaḍiñcu — "to besiege; to lay siege".
శత్రువులు కోటను నెల రోజులు ముట్టడించారు. śatruvulu kōṭanu nela rōjulu muṭṭaḍiñcāru. The enemies besieged the fort for a month.
Past (I …): ముట్టడించాను, muṭṭaḍiñcānu.
The noun is ముట్టడి, a siege.

The word, taken apart.
ముట్టడించు (muṭṭaḍiñcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5018 lists Telugu muṭṭaḍincu, "to besiege", and muṭṭaḍi, "a siege", with Tamil muṟṟu, "to surround, besiege".

Lesson 4 of 5.
జయించు (jayiñcu) — to conquer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a troop, then the Telugu for to besiege.

You'll want to know: జయించు.
జయించు — jayiñcu — "to conquer".
ఆ రాజు చాలా రాజ్యాలను జయించాడు. ā rāju cālā rājyālanu jayiñcāḍu. That king conquered many kingdoms.
Past (I …): జయించాను, jayiñcānu.
Also to overcome: భయాన్ని జయించు (jayiñcu), to conquer fear.

The word, taken apart.
జయించు (jayiñcu) is built on Sanskrit jaya, "victory" (CDIAL 5141), with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
పరాజయం (parājayaṁ) — a defeat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to besiege, then the Telugu for to conquer.

You'll want to know: పరాజయం.
పరాజయం — parājayaṁ — "a defeat".
ఆ యుద్ధంలో రాజుకు పరాజయం ఎదురైంది. ā yuddhaṁlō rājuku parājayaṁ eduraindi. The king met with defeat in that war.
The opposite of విజయం, a victory.

The word, taken apart.
పరాజయం (parājayaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit parājaya, "conquest, defeat" (CDIAL 7799p).
