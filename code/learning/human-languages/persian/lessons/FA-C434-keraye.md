## You'll want to know: کرایه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C434-PELLE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کرایه** (*kerâye*) — "a fare; a rent, a hire charge".

> کرایه تاکسی را پرداختیم.
>
> *Kerâye-ye tâksi râ pardâkhtim.*
>
> We paid the taxi fare.

*Kerâye kardan* — to hire (a car, a hall).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kerâye* was borrowed from Arabic *kirāʾ* 'hire, wage' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
