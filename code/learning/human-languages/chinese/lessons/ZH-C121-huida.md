## You'll want to know: huídá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C121-LEARN121-03]; assesses=[] -->

*huídá* — "to answer".

> *qǐng nǐ huídá* — Please answer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huídá* is built from huí 'to turn back' and dá 'to respond', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwoj* and *top* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second 'to match'.
