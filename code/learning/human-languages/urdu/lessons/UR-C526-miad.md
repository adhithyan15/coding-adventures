## You'll want to know: میعاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C526-MIAD526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**میعاد** — *mīād* — "a time limit; a term".

> اگر میعاد ختم ہوئی تو ٹکٹ بیکار ہو گا۔
>
> *agar mīād khatm huī to ṭikaṭ bekār ho gā.*
>
> If the time limit runs out, the ticket will be useless.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**میعاد** (*mīād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mīʿād*, "an appointed time".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
