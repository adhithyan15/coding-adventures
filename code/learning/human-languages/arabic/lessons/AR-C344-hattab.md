## You'll want to know: حطاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C344-HIKAYA344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حطاب** — *ḥaṭṭāb* — "a woodcutter".

> كان في الغابة حطاب فقير.
>
> *kāna fī al-ghāba ḥaṭṭāb faqīr.*
>
> There was a poor woodcutter in the forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حطاب** (*ḥaṭṭāb*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ṭ-b* (Lane), where *ḥaṭab* is firewood. The *faʿʿāl* pattern names the trade: "the firewood man".
