## You'll want to know: befristet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C350-ARBEITGEBER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**befristet** — "fixed-term, temporary".

> Mein Vertrag ist auf ein Jahr befristet.
>
> *My contract is fixed-term, for one year.*

The participle of *befristen* — to set a time limit. The opposite is *unbefristet* — permanent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*befristet* is the participle of *befristen*, built on *Frist* "time limit", inherited from Middle High German *vrist*, Old High German *frist* and Proto-Germanic *fristiz* (EtymDB).
