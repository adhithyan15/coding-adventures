Italian, chapter 52: Fruit, Apple, Banana, Orange, Grapes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la frutta — fruit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for salt, then the Italian for chicken.

You'll want to know: la frutta.
la frutta — "fruit".

The word, taken apart.
frutta from Late Latin fructa, from Latin fructus, "produce, enjoyment" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
