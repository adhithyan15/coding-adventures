Punjabi, chapter 312: Visitors at the Door.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ (mulākāt) — a meeting with someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to snatch, then the Punjabi for damage.

You'll want to know: ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ.
ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ — mulākāt — "a meeting with someone".
ਪਿਛਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ ਹੋਈ। pichhle hafte merī Aman nāl mulākāt hoī. I met Aman last week.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ (mulākāt) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic mulāqāt, "a meeting".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਸੱਦਣਾ (saddṇā) — to invite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for damage, then the Punjabi for a meeting with someone.

You'll want to know: ਸੱਦਣਾ.
ਸੱਦਣਾ — saddṇā — "to invite".
ਪਿਛਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਰੋਟੀ ਲਈ ਸੱਦਿਆ। pichhle hafte asīṁ Aman nū̃ roṭī laī saddiā. Last week we invited Aman for a meal.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਦੋਸਤ ਨੂੰ ਘਰ ਸੱਦਿਆ, maiṁ āpṇe dost nū̃ ghar saddiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੱਦਣਾ (saddṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit śabdayati, "calls"; CDIAL lists Punjabi saddṇā, "called", under it (CDIAL 12300).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਖੜਕਾਉਣਾ (khaṛkāuṇā) — to knock on a door.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a meeting with someone, then the Punjabi for to invite.

You'll want to know: ਖੜਕਾਉਣਾ.
ਖੜਕਾਉਣਾ — khaṛkāuṇā — "to knock on a door".
ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਦਾ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ਾ ਖੜਕਾਇਆ। shām nū̃ maiṁ Aman dā darvāzā khaṛkāiā. In the evening I knocked on Aman's door.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਖਿੜਕੀ ਖੜਕਾਈ, maiṁ khiṛkī khaṛkāī.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੜਕਾਉਣਾ (khaṛkāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit khaṭakhaṭāyate, "crackles", an imitative word; CDIAL lists Punjabi khaṛkāuṇā, "to make rattle, thump", under it (CDIAL 3771).
