## You'll want to know: रेंगाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C411-SAFAR411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रेंगाळणे** — *reṅgāḷṇe* — "to linger".

> मुलं बागेत उशिरापर्यंत रेंगाळली.
>
> *mulaṁ bāget uśirāparyant reṅgāḷlī.*
>
> The children lingered in the park until late.

Past (he …): तो रेंगाळला, *to reṅgāḷlā*.

It hints that you should already be gone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेंगाळणे** (*reṅgāḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb for staying on longer than you should; its origin is not settled.
