## You'll want to know: la perte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C394-FAILLITE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la perte** — "the loss".

> Ce voyage est une perte de temps.
>
> *This trip is a waste of time.*

*Une perte de temps* — a waste of time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perte* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *perdita* "a thing lost" (EtymDB), from *perdere* "to lose" (Lewis & Short).
