## You'll want to know: રિયાઝ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C551-TALIM551-03]; assesses=[] -->

**રિયાઝ** — *riyājh* — "music practice".

> જો મીરા રોજ રિયાઝ કરશે તો તેનો સૂર વધારે મીઠો થશે.
>
> *jo Mīrā roj riyājh karśe to teno sūr vadhāre mīṭho thaśe.*
>
> If Mira practises every day, her singing will get sweeter.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રિયાઝ** (*riyājh*) is usually traced to Arabic *riyāḍa*, "exercise, training", through Persian *riyāz*; a musician does it: **રિયાઝ કરવો**.

## What you've built
