## You'll want to know: রোবট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C504-JONTRO504-01]; assesses=[] -->

**রোবট** — *robôṭ* — "a robot".

> এই কারখানায় রোবটরা গাড়ি বানায়।
>
> *Ei kārkhānāy robôṭrā gāṛi bānāy.*
>
> In this factory robots build cars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রোবট** (*robôṭ*) is borrowed from English *robot*.
