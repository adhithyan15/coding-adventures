Telugu, chapter 501: Making Others Feel.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
సంతోషపెట్టు (santōṣapeṭṭu) — to make happy; to please.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to thrill, then the Telugu for cheerfulness.

You'll want to know: సంతోషపెట్టు.
సంతోషపెట్టు — santōṣapeṭṭu — "to make happy; to please".
పుట్టినరోజున అన్నయ్య అమ్మను సంతోషపెట్టాడు. puṭṭinarōjuna annayya ammanu santōṣapeṭṭāḍu. On her birthday my brother made mum happy.
Past (I …): సంతోషపెట్టాను, santōṣapeṭṭānu.
సంతోషం, happiness, + పెట్టు, to put: to make someone happy.

The word, taken apart.
సంతోషపెట్టు (santōṣapeṭṭu) joins సంతోషం, from Sanskrit saṁtōṣa, "satisfaction" (CDIAL 12895), to the native verb peṭṭu, "to put".

Lesson 2 of 6.
ఆశ్చర్యపరచు (āścaryaparacu) — to surprise; to astonish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for cheerfulness, then the Telugu for to make happy.

You'll want to know: ఆశ్చర్యపరచు.
ఆశ్చర్యపరచు — āścaryaparacu — "to surprise; to astonish".
ఆమె పాట అందరినీ ఆశ్చర్యపరిచింది. āme pāṭa andarinī āścaryaparicindi. Her song astonished everyone.
Past (I …): ఆశ్చర్యపరిచాను, āścaryaparicānu.
-పరచు makes a causative from a noun: ఆశ్చర్యం, surprise; ఆశ్చర్యపరచు (āścaryaparacu), to surprise someone.

The word, taken apart.
ఆశ్చర్యపరచు (āścaryaparacu) is built from ఆశ్చర్యం, from Sanskrit āścarya, "wonderful" (CDIAL 1464) and paracu, which DEDR 3853 lists as "to cause to feel", the causative partner of paḍu.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to make happy, then the Telugu for to surprise.

You'll want to know: సత్కరించు.
సత్కరించు — satkariñcu — "to honour formally; to felicitate".
బడిలో మంచి మార్కులు వచ్చిన విద్యార్థులను సత్కరించారు. baḍilō mañci mārkulu vaccina vidyārthulanu satkariñcāru. The school honoured the students who got good marks.
Past (I …): సత్కరించాను, satkariñcānu.
Honouring someone in public, often with a shawl and a garland. The noun is సత్కారం.

The word, taken apart.
సత్కరించు (satkariñcu) is built on Sanskrit satkāra, "kind treatment, honour, hospitality" (Monier-Williams), from sat, "good", and kāra, "doing", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 6.
మెప్పించు (meppiñcu) — to impress; to win approval.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to surprise, then the Telugu for to honour formally.

You'll want to know: మెప్పించు.
మెప్పించు — meppiñcu — "to impress; to win approval".
ఆ పిల్లవాడు తన పాటతో అందరినీ మెప్పించాడు. ā pillavāḍu tana pāṭatō andarinī meppiñcāḍu. That boy impressed everyone with his song.
Past (I …): మెప్పించాను, meppiñcānu.
To do something so well that others praise you; మెప్పు is the praise you earn.

The word, taken apart.
మెప్పించు (meppiñcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4722 lists Telugu meppincu, "to please, to satisfy, to win the approval or admiration of", beside meccu, "to praise", and meppu, "praise".
