## You'll want to know: juéwàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C533-WISH533-04]; assesses=[] -->

*juéwàng* — "to give up all hope; to despair".

> *jíshǐ shībài le yě búyào juéwàng* — Even if you fail, don't give up hope.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juéwàng* is built from *jué* 'to cut off' and 望 'a hope', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjwet* and *mjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to break off'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
