## You'll want to know: il passeggino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C496-NEONATO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il passeggino** — "pushchair, stroller".

> Porto la bambina al parco con il passeggino.
>
> *I take the little girl to the park in the pushchair.*

Built on *passeggiare* "to stroll". A pram for a newborn is *la carrozzina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*passeggino* is built on *passeggiare* "to go for a walk", from *passo* "step", inherited from Latin *passus* "a step" (Lewis & Short).
