## You'll want to know: छाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C490-CHHALA490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**छाला** — *chālā* — "a blister from rubbing".

> नए जूतों से मेरे पैर में छाला पड़ गया।
>
> *nae jūtoṁ se mere pair meṁ chālā paṛ gayā.*
>
> My new shoes gave me a blister on my foot.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छाला** (*chālā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *chālā*, with the sense "skin, hide", under Sanskrit *challi*, "bark" (CDIAL 5005), beside *chālī*, "a small blister". The "blister" sense of *chālā* itself, skin lifted from the flesh, is not given there.
