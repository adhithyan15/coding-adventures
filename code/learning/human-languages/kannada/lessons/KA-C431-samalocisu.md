## You'll want to know: ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C431-TIRMANISU431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸು** — *samālōcisu* — "to confer; to consult".

> ನಿರ್ಧಾರ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳುವ ಮೊದಲು ನಾನು ಅಪ್ಪನ ಜೊತೆ ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *nirdhāra tegedukoḷḷuva modalu nānu appana jote samālōciside.*
>
> Before deciding I conferred with Father.

Past (I …): ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸಿದೆ, *samālōciside*.

With whom: -ಜೊತೆ. ಸಮಾಲೋಚನೆ, a consultation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸು** (*samālōcisu*) is built on Sanskrit *sam-*, "together", and *ālocana*, "seeing, considering" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
