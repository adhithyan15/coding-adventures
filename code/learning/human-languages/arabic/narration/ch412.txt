Arabic, chapter 412: Going up and down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ازداد (izdāda) — to become greater; to increase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to struggle against, then the Arabic for to approve formally.

You'll want to know: ازداد.
ازداد — izdāda — "to become greater; to increase".
ازداد عدد السكان. izdāda ʿadad as-sukkān. The number of inhabitants grew.
Present: يزداد, yazdād.

The word, taken apart.
ازداد (izdāda) is built on the native Arabic root z-y-d, "to increase, to grow more" (Lane). It is Form VIII of zāda, to increase: to become greater.

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نسبة (nisba) — a proportion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to double, then the Arabic for to reduce.

You'll want to know: نسبة.
نسبة — nisba — "a proportion".
نسبة (nisba) النجاح عالية هذه السنة. nisbat an-najāḥ ʿāliya hādhihi as-sana. The proportion of passes is high this year.
بالنسبة إلى, bi-n-nisba ilā, regarding, as for.

The word, taken apart.
نسبة (nisba) is built on the native Arabic root n-s-b, "to trace lineage, to relate" (Lane). A nisba is a relation, and so a ratio: a proportion, a percentage.
