## You'll want to know: しょうひん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C421-NEGIRU421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうひん** — *shōhin* — "merchandise; a product".

> **この みせの しょうひんは たかいです。** — *kono mise no shōhin wa takai desu* — "The goods in this shop are expensive."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうひん** (*shōhin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "selling" (Middle Chinese *syang*) and *hin*, "goods" (*phimX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
