Marathi, chapter 406: Walking in Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पदपथ (padpath) — a pavement; a footpath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for border customs duty, then the Marathi for a visa.

You'll want to know: पदपथ.
पदपथ — padpath — "a pavement; a footpath".
या पदपथावर भाजीवाले बसतात. yā padpathāvar bhājīvāle bastāt. Vegetable sellers sit on this pavement.
Masculine.
A formal word for the raised walk beside a town road.

The word, taken apart.
पदपथ (padpath) is built from Sanskrit pada, "foot, step", and patha, "path" (Monier-Williams): a path for feet.

Lesson 4 of 5.
उड्डाणपूल (uḍḍāṇpūl) — a flyover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pedestrian, then the Marathi for a signboard.

You'll want to know: उड्डाणपूल.
उड्डाणपूल — uḍḍāṇpūl — "a flyover".
उड्डाणपुलामुळे चौकातली गर्दी कमी झाली. uḍḍāṇpulāmuḷe caukātlī gardī kamī jhālī. Thanks to the flyover the crowding at the square went down.
Masculine.
Before an ending the long ऊ of पूल shortens: उड्डाणपुलावर.

The word, taken apart.
उड्डाणपूल (uḍḍāṇpūl) is a Marathi compound of उड्डाण, "a flight, a leap up", built on Sanskrit uḍḍayana, "flying up" (Monier-Williams), and पूल, "a bridge": a bridge that leaps over a road.

Lesson 5 of 5.
न्याहाळणे (nyāhāḷṇe) — to gaze at.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a signboard, then the Marathi for a flyover.

You'll want to know: न्याहाळणे.
न्याहाळणे — nyāhāḷṇe — "to gaze at".
मुलं दुकानातली खेळणी न्याहाळत होती. mulaṁ dukānātlī kheḷṇī nyāhāḷat hotī. The children were gazing at the toys in the shop.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने न्याहाळलं, tyāne nyāhāḷlaṁ.
A slow, careful look; the thing looked at is the object.

The word, taken apart.
न्याहाळणे (nyāhāḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for looking at something slowly and closely; its origin is not settled.
