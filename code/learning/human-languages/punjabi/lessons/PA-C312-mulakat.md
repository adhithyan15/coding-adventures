## You'll want to know: ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C312-VISIT312-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ** — *mulākāt* — "a meeting with someone".

> ਪਿਛਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ ਹੋਈ।
>
> *pichhle hafte merī Aman nāl mulākāt hoī.*
>
> I met Aman last week.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ** (*mulākāt*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *mulāqāt*, "a meeting".
