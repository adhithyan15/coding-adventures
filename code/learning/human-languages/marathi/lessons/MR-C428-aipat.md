## You'll want to know: ऐपत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C428-SHILLAK428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ऐपत** — *aipat* — "what one can afford; means".

> घर घेण्याइतकी माझी ऐपत नाही.
>
> *ghar gheṇyāitkī mājhī aipat nāhī.*
>
> I cannot afford to buy a house.

Feminine.

Usually with the owner in -ची: माझी ऐपत, my means.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऐपत** (*aipat*) is a Marathi word for how much money one has to spend; its origin is not settled.
