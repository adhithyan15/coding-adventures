## You'll want to know: 午饭
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C69-MEALS69-03]; assesses=[] -->

**午饭** — *wǔfàn* — "lunch".

> **你吃午饭了吗** *nǐ chī wǔfàn le ma* — Have you had lunch?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**午饭** (*wǔfàn*) is built from 午 'noon' and 饭 'meal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nguX* and *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
