## You'll want to know: 温和
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C469-GENTLE469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**温和** — *wēnhé* — "gentle in manner; soft-spoken".

> **他的个性比我温和** *tā de gèxìng bǐ wǒ wēnhé* — He has a gentler nature than I do.

The new character, in pieces: 温 — twelve strokes in three pieces: on the left the water piece, a dot, a second dot and a rising stroke; at the top right 日 *rì*, four strokes; at the foot a dish of five strokes, a vertical, a stroke that runs right and turns down, two short verticals and a bar. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**温和** (*wēnhé*) is built from 温 'warm' and 和 'harmony', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'won* and *hwa* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'mild', 'good', 'soft' and 'warm' and the second 'in tune'.
