## You'll want to know: 果树
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C516-TREES516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**果树** — *guǒshù* — "fruit tree".

> **我外公家有一些果树** *wǒ wàigōng jiā yǒu yìxiē guǒshù* — There are some fruit trees at my grandad's.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**果树** (*guǒshù*) is built from 果 'fruit' and 树 'a tree', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaX* and *dzyuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'bold' and 'firm', and also 'the fruit of a tree'.
