# ли — the question you are not asking

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-LI-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Two letters, and it means *whether*. Say it, then say the longer word
it was hiding inside for six chapters before it stood alone.

## You'll want to know: two ways to make a sentence longer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-LI-01, RU-GRAMMAR-KOTORYY-CLAUSE-01, RU-LEX-KOTORYY-01] -->

Both were taught in one chapter and neither has been asked for since. They are
the two ways a short Russian sentence grows:

| | |
|---|---|
| **который** | hangs a whole clause on a **noun** |
| **ли** | hangs a **doubt** on a verb — *whether*, a question not asked aloud |

The difference is what each attaches to. **который** reaches back to a thing and
says more about it. **ли** reaches forward into a question and reports it
instead of posing it.

Four chapters of numbers have passed since either appeared. Saying them now,
with nothing on the page, is the first real evidence they were learned rather
than met.
