## You'll want to know: dǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C176-KNOCK176-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎo* — "to topple over; to fall down (of a thing)".

> *tā de zìxíngchē dǎo le* — His bike fell over.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to fall forward'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
