## You'll want to know: त्रस्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C463-PARAKRAMA463-04]; assesses=[] -->

**त्रस्यति** — *trasyati* — "trembles with fear".

> मूषकः मार्जारात् त्रस्यति।
>
> *mūṣakaḥ mārjārāt trasyati.*
>
> The mouse trembles in fear of the cat.

Past (it …): मूषकः मार्जारात् त्रस्तवान्, *mūṣakaḥ mārjārāt trastavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्रस्यति** (*trasyati*) comes from the root √tras, "to tremble, to be afraid of", which takes the ablative of what is feared (MW).
