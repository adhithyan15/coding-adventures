## You'll want to know: a mágoa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C479-PERDAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a mágoa** — "hurt, sorrow".

> Ainda guarda mágoa da discussão com o irmão.
>
> *She still feels hurt about the argument with her brother.*

*Guardar mágoa* — to keep feeling hurt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mágoa* is usually traced to Latin *macula* "a spot, a stain" (Lewis & Short), the same source as *magoar*.
