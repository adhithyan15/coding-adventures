Marathi, chapter 484: Germs and Infections.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रोग (rog) — a disease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to vomit, then the Marathi for to be digested.

You'll want to know: रोग.
रोग — rog — "a disease".
स्वच्छ पाण्यामुळे बरेच रोग टळतात. svaccha pāṇyāmuḷe barec rog ṭaḷtāt. Clean water keeps many diseases away.
Masculine.
Masculine; plural रोग (rog).

The word, taken apart.
रोग (rog) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit roga, "disease" (CDIAL 10829).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an infection, then the Marathi for a germ.

You'll want to know: विषाणू.
विषाणू — viṣāṇū — "a virus".
सर्दी एका विषाणूमुळे होते. sardī ekā viṣāṇūmuḷe hote. A cold is caused by a virus.
Masculine.
विष, poison, + अणू, a tiny particle.

The word, taken apart.
विषाणू (viṣāṇū) is a modern learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, viṣa, "poison", and aṇu, "minute, atomic" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
साथ (sāth) — an epidemic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a germ, then the Marathi for a virus.

You'll want to know: साथ.
साथ — sāth — "an epidemic".
पावसाळ्यात गावात तापाची साथ आली. pāvsāḷyāt gāvāt tāpācī sāth ālī. In the monsoon a fever epidemic came to the village.
Feminine.
साथीचा रोग (rog), an infectious disease. A second साथ (sāth) means company: साथ (sāth) देणे, to stand by someone.

The word, taken apart.
साथ (sāth) is inherited in the sense "company": CDIAL lists Marathi sāth, "companionship", under Sanskrit sārtha, "caravan, company" (CDIAL 13364). Whether the epidemic sense is the same word is not settled.
