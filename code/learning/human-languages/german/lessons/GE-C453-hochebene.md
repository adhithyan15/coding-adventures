## You'll want to know: die Hochebene
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C453-GIPFEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Hochebene** — "the plateau".

> Auf der Hochebene ist es kühl und windig.
>
> *On the plateau it is cool and windy.*

Plural: *die Hochebenen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hochebene* is built from *hoch* 'high', inherited from Proto-Germanic *hauhaz* (EtymDB), and *Ebene* 'plain', from Old High German *ebanī* 'flatness' (EtymDB).
