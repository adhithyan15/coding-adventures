# શણગારવું (śaṇgārvũ) — to adorn

## You'll want to know: શણગારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C329-RANGOLI329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**શણગારવું** — *śaṇgārvũ* — "to adorn".

> લોકોએ આખું ગામ શણગાર્યું.
>
> *lokoe ākhũ gām śaṇgāryũ.*
>
> People decorated the whole village.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘર શણગાર્યું, *teṇe ghar śaṇgāryũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

comes from *\*śṛṅgārayati*, "adorns", a form CDIAL reconstructs on *śṛṅgāra*, finery (CDIAL 12593). **સજાવવું** is to decorate; **શણગારવું** is to dress up richly, a bride or a whole town.
