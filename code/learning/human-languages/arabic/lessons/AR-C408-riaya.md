## You'll want to know: رعاية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C408-MASLAHA408-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رعاية** — *riʿāya* — "care; welfare".

> يحتاج الجد إلى رعاية خاصة.
>
> *yaḥtāju al-jadd ilā riʿāya khāṣṣa.*
>
> Grandfather needs special care.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رعاية** (*riʿāya*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ʿ-y*, "to pasture, to tend" (Lane). *Riʿāya* is tending a flock: care, and in public life, welfare.
