## You'll want to know: el margen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C638-LIMITE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el margen** — "the margin; the room".

> No hay margen para errores.
>
> *There is no room for mistakes.*

*Al margen* — on the sidelines.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*margen* is inherited from Latin *margo, marginis* 'edge, border', through Old Spanish *margen* (EtymDB).
