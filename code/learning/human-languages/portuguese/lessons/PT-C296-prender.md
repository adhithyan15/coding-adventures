## You'll want to know: prender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C296-ALGEMAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**prender** — "to catch, to arrest; to fasten".

> A polícia prendeu o assaltante.
>
> *The police caught the robber.*

Also ‘to tie up, to fasten’: *prender o cabelo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prender* is inherited from Latin *prehendere* "to grasp, to seize" (EtymDB).
