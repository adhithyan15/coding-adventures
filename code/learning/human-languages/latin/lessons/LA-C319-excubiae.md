## You'll want to know: excubiae, excubiārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C319-SIPHO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**excubiae, excubiārum** — "the watch, guard duty".

> Vigilēs per tōtam noctem excubiās agēbant.
>
> *The watchmen kept watch all night long.*

Feminine, plural only: lying out of doors on guard. *Excubiās agere*: to keep watch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**excubiae** is built on *ex*, "out", and *cubō*, "to lie down" (Lewis & Short): a lying out on guard.
