## You'll want to know: l'abonné
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C278-PRESSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'abonné** — "the subscriber".

> Les abonnés reçoivent le journal à la maison.
>
> *Subscribers get the paper at home.*

*Être abonné à un journal* — to take a paper. Feminine: *l'abonnée*. Online: *les abonnés* are followers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abonné* is the past participle of *abonner* "to subscribe" (EtymDB), built on Old French *bonne*, *borne* "a boundary", which is usually traced to a Gaulish word: a price fixed within limits.
