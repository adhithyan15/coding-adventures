Bengali, chapter 459: Modest and Rude.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বিনয়ী (binôyī) — modest; humble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for cunning, then the Bengali for to cheat.

You'll want to know: বিনয়ী.
বিনয়ী — binôyī — "modest; humble".
গায়িকা খুব বিখ্যাত, কিন্তু তিনি বিনয়ী। gāyikā khub bikhyātô, kintu tini binôyī. The singer is very famous, but she is modest.

The word, taken apart.
বিনয়ী (binôyī) is binôy, Sanskrit vinaya, "training, discipline, modesty" (Monier-Williams), with the ending -ī, "having".

You'll want to know: বেয়াদব.
বেয়াদব — beyādôb — "rude; ill-mannered (towards elders)".
বাবা বেয়াদব ছেলেটাকে বকলেন। bābā beyādôb chheleṭāke bôklen. Father scolded the ill-mannered boy.

The word, taken apart.
বেয়াদব (beyādôb) is usually traced to Persian be-adab, "without manners", where adab is Arabic for good manners.

Lesson 5 of 5.
তোষামোদ করা (toshāmod kôrā) — to flatter; to fawn on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for insolent, then the Bengali for ill-mannered.

You'll want to know: তোষামোদ করা.
তোষামোদ করা — toshāmod kôrā — "to flatter; to fawn on".
চাকরির জন্য লোকটা ম্যানেজারকে তোষামোদ করত। chākrir jônyô lokṭā mænejārke toshāmod kôrtô. The man used to flatter the manager to keep his job.
Past (he/she …): তিনি মন্ত্রীকে তোষামোদ করলেন, tini môntrīke toshāmod kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
তোষামোদ করা (toshāmod kôrā) is toshāmod, "flattery", usually traced to Persian khushāmad, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
