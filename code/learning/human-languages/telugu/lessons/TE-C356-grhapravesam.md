# గృహప్రవేశం (gṛhapravēśaṁ) — a housewarming

## You'll want to know: గృహప్రవేశం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C356-UTSAVAM356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గృహప్రవేశం** — *gṛhapravēśaṁ* — "a housewarming".

> మా కొత్త ఇంటి గృహప్రవేశం ఆదివారం జరిగింది.
>
> *mā kotta iṇṭi gṛhapravēśaṁ ādivāraṁ jarigindi.*
>
> The housewarming of our new house was on Sunday.

గృహం, a house, and a Sanskrit word for entering: the first entry into a new home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గృహప్రవేశం** (*gṛhapravēśaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *gṛha*, "a house", and *praveśa*, "an entering" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
