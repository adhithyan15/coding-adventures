## You'll want to know: たべる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-TABERU]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-TA-01, JA-SCRIPT-RU-01] -->

**たべる** — *taberu* — "to eat". This is the dictionary form, the one a word
list gives. Said politely, it is *tabemasu*.

Three beats: *ta–be–ru*. **た** and **る** you already write. The middle sign
is **へ** with the voicing mark, so *he* becomes *be*. The next lesson writes it.
