## You'll want to know: o suspiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C477-CHORO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o suspiro** — "sigh; meringue".

> Ela deu um suspiro e fechou o livro.
>
> *She gave a sigh and closed the book.*

The noun of *suspirar*. In a Portuguese pastry shop, *um suspiro* is a meringue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suspiro* comes from *suspirar* "to sigh", inherited from Latin *suspīrāre* (Lewis & Short), from *spīrāre* "to breathe".
