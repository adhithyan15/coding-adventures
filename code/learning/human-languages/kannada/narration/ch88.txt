Kannada, chapter 88: Welcome and Congratulations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಭಿನಂದನೆಗಳು (abhinandanegaḷu) — congratulations.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for is that so, then the Kannada for the same.

You'll want to know: ಅಭಿನಂದನೆಗಳು.
ಅಭಿನಂದನೆಗಳು (abhinandanegaḷu) — "congratulations". The ending ‑ಗಳು (-gaḷu) is the plural: many good wishes.

What you've built.
Good news deserves a word of its own.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: abhinandanegaḷu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: abhinandanegaḷu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say abhinandanegaḷu!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for is that so, then the Kannada for the same, then say abhinandanegaḷu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಅಭಿನಂದನೆಗಳು (abhinandanegaḷu) mean? ("Congratulations".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಒಳಗೆ ಬನ್ನಿ (oḷage banni) — please come in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an invitation, then the Kannada for a gift.

You'll want to know: ಒಳಗೆ ಬನ್ನಿ.
ಒಳಗೆ ಬನ್ನಿ (oḷage banni) — "please come in". ಒಳಗೆ is "inside", and ಬನ್ನಿ is the polite form of ಬಾ, "come".

The first thing said at the door.
