## You'll want to know: বৃথা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C383-SARTHOK383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বৃথা** — *brithā* — "in vain; wasted".

> সবাই না এলে আমাদের পরিকল্পনা বৃথা হবে।
>
> *sôbāi nā ele āmāder pôrikôlpônā brithā hôbe.*
>
> If everyone doesn't come, our plan will be wasted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বৃথা** (*brithā*) is Sanskrit *vṛthā*, in vain (Monier-Williams): the opposite of সার্থক.
