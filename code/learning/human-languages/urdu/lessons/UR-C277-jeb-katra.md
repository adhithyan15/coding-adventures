## You'll want to know: جیب کترا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C277-FARAR277-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جیب کترا** — *jeb katrā* — "a pickpocket".

> بس میں ایک جیب کترا تھا۔
>
> *bas meṅ ek jeb katrā thā.*
>
> There was a pickpocket on the bus.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جیب کترا** (*jeb katrā*) is built from **جیب**, a pocket, usually traced to Persian *jēb*, and *katrā*, one who snips, from *katarnā*, "to clip", which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit *kartarī*, "scissors" (CDIAL 2858): the one who cuts into pockets.
