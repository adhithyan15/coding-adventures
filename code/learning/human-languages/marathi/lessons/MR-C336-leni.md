## You'll want to know: लेणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C336-LENI336-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लेणी** — *leṇī* — "rock-cut caves".

> डोंगरात कोरलेली प्राचीन लेणी आम्ही पाहिली.
>
> *ḍoṅgrāt korlelī prācīn leṇī āmhī pāhilī.*
>
> We saw the ancient caves carved into the hill.

Neuter.

A neuter plural, the usual form: ही लेणी, these caves; one cave is लेणं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लेणी** (*leṇī*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *layana*, 'a place of rest, a cell', which Pali has as *lēṇa*, 'a rock cell used by ascetics', and Prakrit as *lēṇa*, 'a cave' (CDIAL 10963); CDIAL gives no Marathi form, so the path is not settled.
