## You'll want to know: کھنگالنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C506-SAFAI506-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کھنگالنا** — *khaṅgālnā* — "to rinse out".

> اس نے نل کے نیچے گلاس کھنگالے۔
>
> *us ne nal ke nīche glās khaṅgāle.*
>
> She rinsed the glasses under the tap.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بالٹی کھنگالی, *us ne balṭī khaṅgālī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھنگالنا** (*khaṅgālnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khãgālnā*, "to rinse", under *\*khakkhālayati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3762).
