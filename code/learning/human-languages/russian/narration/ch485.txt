Russian, chapter 485: Feeling Sick and Faint.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
тошнить (toshnít) — to feel sick (impersonal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a strain, then the Russian for to limp.

You'll want to know: тошнить.
тошнить (toshnít) — "to feel sick (impersonal)".
В автобусе меня часто тошнит. V avtóbuse menyá chásto toshnít. I often feel sick on the bus.
Impersonal: меня тошнит — I feel sick. Imperfective; perfective стошнить — to be sick.

The word, taken apart.
тошнить (toshnít) is built on тошно 'sickening, miserable'; EtymDB has no entry, and the root is usually linked to the one in тоска 'longing'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
головокружение (golovokruzhéniye) — dizziness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to feel sick, then the Russian for nausea.

You'll want to know: головокружение.
головокружение (golovokruzhéniye) — "dizziness".
Летом от жары у меня было головокружение. Létom ot zharý u menyá býlo golovokruzhéniye. In the summer the heat made me dizzy.
Neuter. У меня кружится голова — I feel dizzy.

The word, taken apart.
головокружение (golovokruzhéniye) is a compound of голова 'head', inherited from Proto-Slavic golva (EtymDB), and кружение 'spinning', built on круг 'circle'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
обморок (óbmorok) — a faint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for nausea, then the Russian for dizziness.

You'll want to know: обморок.
обморок (óbmorok) — "a faint".
В душном зале одна женщина упала в обморок. V dúshnom zále odná zhénshchina upála v óbmorok. In the stuffy hall a woman fainted.
Masculine. Упасть в обморок (óbmorok) — to faint.

The word, taken apart.
обморок (óbmorok) is built on об- and морок, which EtymDB glosses 'darkness, cloud, fog': fainting is a darkening of the eyes.

Lesson 5 of 5.
знобить (znobít) — to have the shivers (impersonal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for dizziness, then the Russian for a faint.

You'll want to know: знобить.
знобить (znobít) — "to have the shivers (impersonal)".
У меня температура, и меня знобит. U menyá temperatúra, i menyá znobít. I've got a temperature and I've got the shivers.
Impersonal: меня знобит. Imperfective only.

The word, taken apart.
знобить (znobít) shares its root with озноб 'a chill'; EtymDB has no entry for either, and the origin is not settled.
