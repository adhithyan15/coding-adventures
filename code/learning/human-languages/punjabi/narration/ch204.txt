Lesson 2 of 5.
One whole form for children before a postposition — children before a postposition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the familiar singular noun for a child, then stop.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਬੱਚਿਆਂ (bachchiaan) — bachchiā̃ — children, before a postposition.
Hear and say the whole form before reading it. Keep ਬੱਚਿਆਂ (bachchiaan) whole. Use this form for “children” before the familiar ਨਾਲ. The next lesson retrieves the new form alone before that old postposition returns.

The word, taken apart — a small, well-marked trail.
Punjabi University Patiala's noun grammar explains the form pattern used here. Its lexical statistics identifies this exact word as the plural-oblique form of the familiar child noun, and University Lesson 12 independently presents the same pattern. Sources: Punjabi University noun grammar, Punjabi University lexical statistics, and Punjabi University Lesson 12.
An earlier lesson records that the origin of ਬੱਚਾ (bachchaa) remains unsettled. This lesson makes no new historical claim and keeps the new form whole.

For this one frame, place the whole children form before ਨਾਲ. The noun form comes first; the familiar postposition follows it. Nothing else is added.
