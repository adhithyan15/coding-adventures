## You'll want to know: la niñez
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C545-NINEZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la niñez** — "childhood".

> Tuve una niñez muy feliz.
>
> *I had a very happy childhood.*

*La infancia* means the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niñez* is built on *niño* 'child' with the ending *-ez*. *Niño* is usually traced to a baby-talk word, *ninno*, not to any Latin noun.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
