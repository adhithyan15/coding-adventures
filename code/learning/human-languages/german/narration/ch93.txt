German, chapter 93: Right, Wrong, Important, Simple, Brown.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
richtig — right, correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for thin, then the German for quiet.

You'll want to know: richtig.
richtig — "right, correct".

The word, taken apart.
richtig from Old High German rihtīg, built from reht, "right", + -ig; reht from Proto-Germanic rehtaz, the same word as English right. (EtymDB 2.1).

Before the new one: say the German for right, then the German for wrong.

You'll want to know: wichtig.
wichtig — "important".

The word, taken apart.
wichtig was borrowed from Middle Low German wichtich, "weighty", built on the word for "weight"; EtymDB 2.1 lists that word beside English weight. What matters carries weight.
