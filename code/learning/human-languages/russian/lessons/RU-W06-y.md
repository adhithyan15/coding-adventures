## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SCRIPT-Y-01]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-U-01, RU-SCRIPT-Z-01, RU-SCRIPT-K-01, RU-SCRIPT-L-01, RU-SCRIPT-M-01] -->

> ы

Now a letter with no English equivalent at all — not a shape problem, a **sound**
problem.

**ы** is a vowel made with the tongue pulled **back and high**, roughly where you
hold it for *oo*, but with the lips **unrounded** and spread as for *ee*. English
has no such vowel and no letter for it. Books usually transliterate it *y*, which
helps nobody.

The reliable way in: say *ee*. Hold it. Now, without moving your lips, pull your
tongue back towards your throat. What comes out is close.

The shape is two pieces — a hard sign body and a vertical — and it is **never
capitalised at the start of a word**, because no Russian word begins with it.
