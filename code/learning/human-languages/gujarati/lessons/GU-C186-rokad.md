## You'll want to know: રોકડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C186-TILL186-02]; assesses=[] -->

**રોકડ** — *rokaḍ* — "cash".

> **મારી પાસે રોકડ નથી.** — *mārī pāse rokaḍ nathī* — "I have no cash on me."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રોકડ** (*rokaḍ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "cash", under *\*rōkka*, "ready money", a reconstructed form, extended with the suffix *-ḍ-* (CDIAL 10828).
