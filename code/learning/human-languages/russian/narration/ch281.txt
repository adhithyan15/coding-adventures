Lesson 1 of 5.
корреспондент (korrespondént) — a correspondent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to confirm, then the Russian for to refute.

You'll want to know: корреспондент.
корреспондент (korrespondént) — "a correspondent".
Корреспондент поехал на север. Korrespondént poyékhal na séver. The correspondent went north.
Also журналист. Feminine forms are colloquial, and корреспондент (korrespondént) serves for a woman too.

The word, taken apart.
корреспондент (korrespondént) is usually traced through German Korrespondent or French correspondant to Medieval Latin correspondēre "to answer together"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 2 of 5.
репортаж (reportázh) — a report (on TV or radio), a news story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to refute, then the Russian for a correspondent.

You'll want to know: репортаж.
репортаж (reportázh) — "a report (on TV or radio), a news story".
Вечером мы смотрели репортаж о футболе. Vécherom my smotréli reportázh o futbóle. In the evening we watched a report about football.
Masculine. Вести репортаж (reportázh) — to report live.

The word, taken apart.
репортаж (reportázh) was borrowed from French reportage (EtymDB), built on reporter "to bring back, to report" and the ending -age; the verb goes back to Latin reportāre, from portāre "to carry".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ведущий (vedúshchiy) — a presenter, a host.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a correspondent, then the Russian for a report.

You'll want to know: ведущий.
ведущий (vedúshchiy) — "a presenter, a host".
Ведущий программы очень весёлый. Vedúshchiy prográmmy óchen vesyólyy. The presenter of the programme is very cheerful.
A participle of вести "to lead" used as a noun; a woman is ведущая. It declines like an adjective.

The word, taken apart.
ведущий (vedúshchiy) is the present participle of вести "to lead" (EtymDB), an inherited Slavic verb from the same root as водить: the one who leads the show.

Lesson 4 of 5.
эфир (efír) — the air (broadcasting).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a report, then the Russian for a presenter.

You'll want to know: эфир.
эфир (efír) — "the air (broadcasting)".
Программа вышла в эфир в семь часов. Prográmma výshla v efír v sem chasóv. The programme went on air at seven.
В прямом эфире — live. In chemistry it also means ether.

The word, taken apart.
эфир (efír) was borrowed from Greek aithḗr "the upper air" (EtymDB), built on aíthō "to burn, to shine".

Lesson 5 of 5.
телеканал (telekanál) — a TV channel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a presenter, then the Russian for the air.

You'll want to know: телеканал.
телеканал (telekanál) — "a TV channel".
Этот телеканал показывает много фильмов. Étot telekanál pokázyvayet mnógo fílmov. This TV channel shows a lot of films.
Masculine. Short for телевизионный канал.

The word, taken apart.
телеканал (telekanál) joins теле-, from Greek tēle "far off", and канал "a channel", which comes from Latin canālis "a pipe, a channel" (EtymDB).
