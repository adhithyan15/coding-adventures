## You'll want to know: 时代
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C324-RECALL324-04]; assesses=[] -->

**时代** — *shídài* — "era; age".

> **现在是手机的时代** *xiànzài shì shǒujī de shídài* — This is the age of the mobile phone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**时代** (*shídài*) is built from 时 'a time' and 代 'an age', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyi* and *dojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with another word for a time and the second 'to take turns'.
