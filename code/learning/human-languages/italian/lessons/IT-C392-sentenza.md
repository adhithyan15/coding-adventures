## You'll want to know: la sentenza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C392-DIFESA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la sentenza** — "the verdict, the ruling".

> La sentenza arriva la settimana prossima.
>
> *The verdict comes next week.*

*Sputare sentenze* — to lay down the law, to pontificate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sentenza* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sententia* "an opinion, a judgement, a maxim" (Lewis & Short), from *sentīre* "to feel, to think".
