## You'll want to know: دوراهی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C445-TAGHATO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دوراهی** (*dorâhi*) — "a fork (in the road); a dilemma".

> در دوراهی راه سمت چپ را گرفتیم.
>
> *Dar dorâhi râh-e samt-e chap râ gereftim.*
>
> At the fork we took the road on the left.

Also figurative: *sar-e dorâhi budan* — to be at a crossroads in life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dorâhi* is a native compound of *do* 'two', which EtymDB traces to PIE *dwóh₁*, *râh* 'road', from Middle Persian *rāh* (EtymDB), and the ending *-i*.
