## You'll want to know: el chaleco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C655-VUELO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el chaleco** — "the waistcoat, the vest".

> El chaleco salvavidas está debajo del asiento.
>
> *The life jacket is under the seat.*

*El chaleco salvavidas* — the life jacket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chaleco* is usually traced to Turkish *yelek* "waistcoat", a word that reached Spanish through Arabic.
