## You'll want to know: આંટો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C408-ROUNDS408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**આંટો** — *ā̃ṭo* — "a turn; a short walk".

> સાંજે અમે બગીચામાં આંટો માર્યો.
>
> *sā̃je ame bagīcāmā̃ ā̃ṭo māryo.*
>
> In the evening we took a turn in the garden.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આંટો** (*ā̃ṭo*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *āvarta*, "a whirlpool, a turning"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a turn, a twist" (CDIAL 1418). આંટો મારવો is to go for a short walk.
