## You'll want to know: कारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C341-PRAYOJANA341-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कारयति** — *kārayati* — "has something done".

> राजा मन्दिरं कारयति।
>
> *rājā mandiraṃ kārayati.*
>
> The king has a temple built.

Past (he …): सः गृहं कारितवान्, *saḥ gṛhaṃ kāritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कारयति** (*kārayati*) comes from the root √kṛ, "to do, to make", in its causative form; MW gives *kārita* "caused to be made or done" (MW).
