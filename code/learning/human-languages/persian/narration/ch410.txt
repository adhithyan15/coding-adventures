Persian, chapter 410: Parties and new laws.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حزب (hezb) — a political party.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to vote, then the Persian for parliament.

You'll want to know: حزب.
حزب (hezb) — "a political party".
او عضو یک حزب کوچک است. u 'ozv-e yek hezb-e kuchek ast. She is a member of a small party.
Plural ahzâb, an Arabic broken plural.

The word, taken apart.
حزب (hezb) was borrowed from Arabic ḥizb "a party, a group" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
لایحه (lâyehe) — a draft law, a bill (in parliament).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for parliament, then the Persian for a political party.

You'll want to know: لایحه.
لایحه (lâyehe) — "a draft law, a bill (in parliament)".
دولت یک لایحه تازه به مجلس فرستاد. dowlat yek lâyehe-ye tâze be majles ferestâd. The government sent a new bill to parliament.
A government bill; once passed it becomes a qânun.

The word, taken apart.
لایحه (lâyehe) is usually traced to Arabic lā'iḥa "a written statement, a memorandum".

Lesson 3 of 5.
تصویب کردن (tasvib kardan) — to pass (a law), to approve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a political party, then the Persian for a draft law.

You'll want to know: تصویب کردن.
تصویب کردن (tasvib kardan) — "to pass (a law), to approve".
مجلس این لایحه را تصویب کرد. majles in lâyehe râ tasvib kard. Parliament passed this bill.
Tasvib "approval" + kardan. Tasvib shodan — to be passed.

The word, taken apart.
تصویب is usually traced to Arabic taṣwīb "approving as right"; with kardan "to do" it means to pass a law.

Lesson 4 of 5.
سند (sanad) — a document; a deed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a draft law, then the Persian for to pass.

You'll want to know: سند.
سند (sanad) — "a document; a deed".
سند خانه به نام مادرم است. sanad-e khâne be nâm-e mâdaram ast. The deed of the house is in my mother's name.
Plural asnâd, an Arabic broken plural.

The word, taken apart.
سند was borrowed from Arabic sanad (EtymDB), a word usually glossed as a support, what a claim leans on; in Persian it is a document or a deed.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تابعیت (tâbe'iyat) — citizenship, nationality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pass, then the Persian for a document.

You'll want to know: تابعیت.
تابعیت (tâbe'iyat) — "citizenship, nationality".
او تابعیت دو کشور را دارد. u tâbe'iyat-e do keshvar râ dârad. He has the citizenship of two countries.
Tâbe'iyyat gereftan — to become a citizen.

The word, taken apart.
تابعیت (tâbe'iyat) is usually traced to Arabic tābi'iyya, built on tābi' "following, subject to": a citizen belongs to a state.
