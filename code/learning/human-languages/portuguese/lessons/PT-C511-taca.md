## You'll want to know: a taça
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C511-TORNEIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a taça** — "cup, trophy; bowl".

> A equipa levantou a taça no fim do jogo.
>
> *The team lifted the cup at the end of the match.*

Also a wine glass or a bowl: *uma taça de fruta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*taça* came through Spanish *taza* from Arabic *ṭāsa* "a bowl" (EtymDB), which goes back to Persian.
