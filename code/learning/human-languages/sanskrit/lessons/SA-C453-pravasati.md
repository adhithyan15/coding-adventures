## You'll want to know: प्रवसति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C453-VIRAHA453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवसति** — *pravasati* — "lives away from home".

> मम भ्राता दूरे नगरे प्रवसति।
>
> *mama bhrātā dūre nagare pravasati.*
>
> My brother lives away from home in a distant city.

Past (he …): सः संवत्सरं प्रोषितवान्, *saḥ saṃvatsaraṃ proṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवसति** (*pravasati*) is built from *pra*, "forth", and the root √vas, "to dwell", the root of **वसति**; MW gives "to go or sojourn abroad" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
