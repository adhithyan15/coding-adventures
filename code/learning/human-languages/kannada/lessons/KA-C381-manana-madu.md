# ಮನನ ಮಾಡು (manana māḍu) — to reflect on

## You'll want to know: ಮನನ ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C381-PARIGANISU381-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮನನ ಮಾಡು** — *manana māḍu* — "to reflect on".

> ಗುರುಗಳ ಮಾತನ್ನು ನಾನು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮನನ ಮಾಡಿದೆ.
>
> *gurugaḷa mātannu nānu maneyalli manana māḍide.*
>
> At home I reflected on my teacher's words.

Past (I …): ಮನನ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *manana māḍide*.

What you reflect on takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮನನ ಮಾಡು** (*manana māḍu*) is built from Sanskrit *manana*, "thinking, reflection" (Monier-Williams), from *man*, "to think", with *māḍu*, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
