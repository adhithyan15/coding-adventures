## You'll want to know: la jardinería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C754-PESCA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la jardinería** — "gardening".

> Mi abuelo dedica la mañana a la jardinería.
>
> *My grandfather spends the morning gardening.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jardinería* is built on *jardín*, which is usually traced to French *jardin*, from Frankish *gardo* "enclosure".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
