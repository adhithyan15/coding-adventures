## You'll want to know: आसीस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C266-HAJAR266-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आसीस** — *āsīs* — "a blessing".

> दादी आसीस देवै है।
>
> *dādī āsīs devai hai.*
>
> Grandmother gives her blessing.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आसीस** (*āsīs*) goes back to Sanskrit *āśis*, "a wish, a prayer; a blessing" (CDIAL 1457; Monier-Williams). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, and no other modern form either: the Marwadi word keeps the Sanskrit shape, with स for श. It is feminine, and an elder gives it: **आसीस देवै है**.
