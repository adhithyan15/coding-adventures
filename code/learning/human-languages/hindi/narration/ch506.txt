Hindi, chapter 506: The Craftsman's Bench.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कारीगर (kārīgar) — a craftsman; an artisan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for turning over (in bed), then the Hindi for a stretch (of the body).

You'll want to know: कारीगर.
कारीगर — kārīgar — "a craftsman; an artisan".
यह कुर्सी गाँव के एक कारीगर ने बनाई। Yah kursī gā̃v ke ek kārīgar ne banāī. A craftsman from the village made this chair.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
कारीगर (kārīgar) is usually traced to Persian kārīgar, "a craftsman".

Lesson 2 of 5.
औज़ार (auzār) — a tool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a stretch (of the body), then the Hindi for a craftsman.

You'll want to know: औज़ार.
औज़ार — auzār — "a tool".
बढ़ई अपने औज़ार लकड़ी के एक डिब्बे में रखता है। Baṛhaī apne auzār lakṛī ke ek ḍibbe meṁ rakhtā hai. The carpenter keeps his tools in a wooden box.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
औज़ार (auzār) is usually traced to Arabic awzār, a plural meaning "loads" or "gear", which reached Hindi through Persian; the sense "tools" is the one Persian and Hindi use.

Lesson 4 of 5.
गढ़ना (gaṛhnā) — to shape; to forge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a tool, then the Hindi for a skill.

You'll want to know: गढ़ना.
गढ़ना — gaṛhnā — "to shape; to forge".
लोहार गरम लोहे से एक कुदाल गढ़ रहा है। Lohār garam lohe se ek kudāl gaṛh rahā hai. The blacksmith is shaping a hoe from hot iron.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने लोहे से एक बर्तन गढ़ा, usne lohe se ek bartan gaṛhā.

The word, taken apart.
गढ़ना (gaṛhnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from gaṭhati, "makes, forms", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3966). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to form by hammering".
