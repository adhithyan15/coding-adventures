Urdu, chapter 360: Mercy and Loyalty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رحم (rahm) — mercy; pity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for however, then the Urdu for to give in.

You'll want to know: رحم.
رحم — rahm — "mercy; pity".
جج نے ملزم پر رحم کیا۔ jaj ne mulzim par rahm kiyā. The judge showed the accused mercy.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
رحم (rahm) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic raḥm, "tenderness, compassion".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for loyal, then the Urdu for a sin.

You'll want to know: تھامنا.
تھامنا — thāmnā — "to hold on to; to hold up".
مشکل وقت میں اس نے میرا ہاتھ تھاما۔ mushkil vaqt meṅ us ne merā hāth thāmā. In a hard time she held my hand.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے رسی تھامی, us ne rassī thāmī.

The word, taken apart.
تھامنا (thāmnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin thāmnā, "to prop, to stop", under Sanskrit stámbhate (CDIAL 13683).
