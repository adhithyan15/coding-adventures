## You'll want to know: हळवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C468-SAUMYA468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हळवा** — *haḷvā* — "tender-hearted".

> माझी बहीण खूप हळवी आहे, लगेच रडते.
>
> *mājhī bahīṇ khūp haḷvī āhe, lagec raḍte.*
>
> My sister is very tender-hearted; she cries at once.

Agrees: हळवा, हळवी, हळवं. हळवं मन, a sensitive heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हळवा** (*haḷvā*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *laghu*, "light, quick": CDIAL lists Marathi *haḷū*, "light", and *haḷvā*, "ripening quickly", under it (CDIAL 10896). The sense "tender-hearted" is not given there, so the link is not certain.
