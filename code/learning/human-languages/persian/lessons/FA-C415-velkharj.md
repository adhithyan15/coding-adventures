## You'll want to know: ولخرج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C415-MAKHAREJ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ولخرج** (*velkharj*) — "a spendthrift; extravagant".

> برادرم خیلی ولخرج است.
>
> *barâdaram kheyli velkharj ast.*
>
> My brother spends money too freely.

*Vel* "loose" + *kharj* "spending". Colloquial. *Velkharji kardan* — to splash out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ولخرج** joins the Persian word *vel* "loose, let go" to *kharj* "spending", usually traced to Arabic *kharj* "outlay": someone whose money runs loose.
