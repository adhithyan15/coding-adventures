## You'll want to know: обязанность
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C359-OBYAZANNOSTI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**обязанность** (*obyázannost*) — "a duty, a responsibility".

> В мои обязанности входит работа с клиентами.
>
> *V moí obyázannosti vkhódit rabóta s kliyéntami.*
>
> My duties include working with clients.

Feminine. *Входить в обязанности* — to be part of one’s duties.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**обязанность** is built on *обязать* "to oblige", made of *о-* and *вязать* "to tie, to bind", from Proto-Slavic *\*vęzati*: a duty is what binds you.
