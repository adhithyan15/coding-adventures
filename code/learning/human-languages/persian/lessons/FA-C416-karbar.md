## You'll want to know: کاربر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C416-KARBAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کاربر** (*kârbar*) — "a user".

> این برنامه میلیونها کاربر دارد.
>
> *in barnâme milyunhâ kârbar dârad.*
>
> This app has millions of users.

*Kâr* "work" + *bar* "carrying". *Nâm-e kârbari* — a username.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کاربر** is a modern native coinage built from *kâr* "work" + *bar* "carrying", from *bar-*, the present stem of *bordan* "to carry". A user is one who puts a tool to work.
