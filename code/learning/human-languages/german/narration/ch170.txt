Lesson 2 of 5.
meistens — mostly, usually.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for angry, then the German for rarely.

You'll want to know: meistens.
meistens — "mostly, usually".

The word, taken apart.
meistens is the word for "most", Old High German meist from Proto-Germanic maistaz, + the adverb ending -ens (EtymDB 2.1). It is the same word as English most.

Lesson 3 of 5.
gleich — right away, in a moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for rarely, then the German for mostly.

You'll want to know: gleich.
gleich — "right away, in a moment".

The word, taken apart.
gleich from Old High German gilīh, "alike" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited: gi-, "together", + an old word for "body, shape", the same build as English alike. "Right away" means "at the same moment".
