## You'll want to know: ਚਿਪਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C296-SLIP296-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਿਪਕਣਾ** — *chipakṇā* — "to stick; to cling".

> ਚਿੱਕੜ ਜੁੱਤੀ ਨਾਲ ਚਿਪਕਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *chikkaṛ juttī nāl chipakdā hai.*
>
> Mud sticks to the shoe.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੱਤਾ ਚਿਪਕਿਆ, *oh pattā chipkiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਿਪਕਣਾ** (*chipakṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*cippa*, "gummy matter"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cipakṇā*, "to stick", under it (CDIAL 4819).
