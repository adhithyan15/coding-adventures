## You'll want to know: झक्की
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C468-DHAIRYA468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झक्की** — *jhakkī* — "cranky; set in odd ways".

> मेरे नाना थोड़े झक्की हैं, हर काम अपने तरीक़े से करते हैं।
>
> *mere nānā thoṛe jhakkī haiṁ, har kām apne tarīqe se karte haiṁ.*
>
> My grandfather is a bit cranky; he does everything his own way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झक्की** (*jhakkī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *jhakkī*, "stubborn", under *\*jhakk*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5319). It is the stubbornness of odd, fixed habits.
