Sanskrit, chapter 421: Hamlets and Suburbs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पल्ली (pallī) — a hamlet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for comes upon, then the Sanskrit for refreshes.

You'll want to know: पल्ली.
पल्ली — pallī — "a hamlet".
पर्वतस्य अधः एका पल्ली अस्ति। parvatasya adhaḥ ekā pallī asti. Below the mountain there is a hamlet.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पल्ली (pallī) is the word MW gives for a small village; CDIAL, after T. Burrow, traces it to Dravidian, and DEDR 4018 sets Tamil paḷḷi and Kannada haḷḷi, "a hamlet", in one family, so it is usually taken as a loan from Dravidian (MW; CDIAL 7972; DEDR 4018).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उपनगरम् (upanagaram) — a suburb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for refreshes, then the Sanskrit for a hamlet.

You'll want to know: उपनगरम्.
उपनगरम् — upanagaram — "a suburb".
मम मित्रम् उपनगरे वसति। mama mitram upanagare vasati. My friend lives in a suburb.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
उपनगरम् (upanagaram) is built from upa, "near", and nagara, "a city"; MW gives the compound as a suburb (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

सायं चत्वरे बालाः क्रीडन्ति। sāyaṃ catvare bālāḥ krīḍanti. In the evening children play in the square.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
चत्वरम् (catvaram) is the word MW gives for a square place, a place where many ways meet; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
हर्म्यम् (harmyam) — a mansion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a suburb, then the Sanskrit for a town square.

You'll want to know: हर्म्यम्.
हर्म्यम् — harmyam — "a mansion".
नगरे धनिकस्य विशालं हर्म्यम् अस्ति। nagare dhanikasya viśālaṃ harmyam asti. In the city there is a rich man's huge mansion.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
हर्म्यम् (harmyam) is the word MW gives for a large house, a palace, a mansion; MW doubts the old derivation from √hṛ and connects it rather with gharma, "heat", so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सम्भाषते (sambhāṣate) — converses; chats.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a town square, then the Sanskrit for a mansion.

You'll want to know: सम्भाषते.
सम्भाषते — sambhāṣate — "converses; chats".
यात्री चत्वरे जनैः सह सम्भाषते। yātrī catvare janaiḥ saha sambhāṣate. The traveller chats with people in the square.
Past (he …): सः जनैः सह सम्भाषितवान्, saḥ janaiḥ saha sambhāṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
सम्भाषते (sambhāṣate) is built from sam, "together", and the root √bhāṣ, "to speak", which also gives bhāṣate, "speaks"; MW gives sam-bhāṣ as to speak together, to converse (MW).
