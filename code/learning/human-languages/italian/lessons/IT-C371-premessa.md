## You'll want to know: la premessa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C371-RILEVANTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la premessa** — "the premise; the preface, the introduction".

> Faccio una premessa: non sono un esperto.
>
> *Let me say first: I am not an expert.*

*Fare una premessa* — to say something first. In a book it is the preface.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*premessa* is the past participle of *premettere* "to put first", from Latin *praemittere* "to send ahead" (Lewis & Short), built from *prae* "before" and *mittere* "to send".
