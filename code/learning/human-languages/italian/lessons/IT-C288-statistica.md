## You'll want to know: la statistica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C288-ANNUNCIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la statistica** — "the statistic; statistics".

> Secondo le statistiche, il turismo è cresciuto.
>
> *According to the statistics, tourism has grown.*

*Secondo le statistiche* — according to the figures.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*statistica* is usually traced to Modern Latin *statisticus* "of the state", built on Latin *status* "condition, state" (Lewis & Short): first, figures about a state.
