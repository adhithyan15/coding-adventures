Persian, chapter 128: Golden, Round, Kind, Simple, Calm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طلایی (talâyi) — golden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for purple, then the Persian for grey.

You'll want to know: طلایی.
طلایی — talâyi — "golden".

The word, taken apart.
talâyi is talâ, "gold", with the colour ending -i. talâ is borrowed from Arabic ṭilāʾ, "coating, gilding" (root ṭ-l-y, Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
گرد (gerd) — round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for grey, then the Persian for golden.

You'll want to know: گرد.
گرد — gerd — "round".

The word, taken apart.
gerd is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian gird, "round". EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root wert-, "to turn", the root of Latin vertere. (It also lists gard, "dust", a different word.)

Lesson 3 of 5.
مهربان (mehrabân) — kind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for golden, then the Persian for round.

You'll want to know: مهربان.
مهربان — mehrabân — "kind".

The word, taken apart.
mehrabân is mehr, "love, kindness", with -bân, "keeper": one who keeps kindness. EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian mihrbān. mehr is also the name of the old god Mithra.
