## You'll want to know: el lienzo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C763-ARTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el lienzo** — "the canvas".

> Compré un lienzo para pintar.
>
> *I bought a canvas to paint on.*

Also an old word for linen cloth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lienzo* is inherited from Latin *linteum* "linen cloth" (EtymDB), through Old Spanish *lienço*.
