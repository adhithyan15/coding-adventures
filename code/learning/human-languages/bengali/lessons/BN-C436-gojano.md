## You'll want to know: গজানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C436-PHOTA436-04]; assesses=[] -->

**গজানো** — *gôjāno* — "to sprout; to grow (of leaves or hair)".

> বর্ষায় উঠোনে ঘাস গজাল।
>
> *bôrshāy uṭhone ghās gôjālô.*
>
> In the monsoon grass sprang up in the courtyard.

Past (he/she …): সেই ঘাস গজাল, *sei ghās gôjālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গজানো** (*gôjāno*) is the everyday verb for leaves, grass or hair coming up; its origin is not settled.
