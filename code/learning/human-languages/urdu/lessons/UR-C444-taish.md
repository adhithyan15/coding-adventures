## You'll want to know: طیش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C444-ANGER444-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طیش** — *taish* — "a fit of rage".

> طیش میں آ کر اس نے گلاس توڑ دیا۔
>
> *taish meṅ ā kar us ne glās toṛ diyā.*
>
> In a fit of rage he broke the glass.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طیش** (*taish*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṭaysh*, "rashness"; the sense "rage" is probably a later one.
