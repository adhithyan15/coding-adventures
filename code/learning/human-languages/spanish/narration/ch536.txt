Spanish, chapter 536: Mientras tanto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
mientras tanto — meanwhile, in the meantime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for finally, then the Spanish for later.

You'll want to know: mientras tanto.
mientras tanto — "meanwhile, in the meantime".
Mientras tanto, su hermano llamó a la ambulancia. Meanwhile, his brother called the ambulance.

The word, taken apart.
mientras tanto is built on mientras 'while', which is usually traced to Latin dum interim 'while meanwhile', and tanto, which comes from Latin tantum 'so much' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
en cuanto — as soon as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for at the same time, then the Spanish for at once.

You'll want to know: en cuanto.
en cuanto — "as soon as".
En cuanto llegó, se fue a dormir. As soon as he arrived, he went to bed.
About the future it takes the subjunctive: En cuanto llegues, llámame.

The word, taken apart.
en cuanto is built on cuanto, which is inherited from Latin quantus 'how great, how much' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
tan pronto como — the moment that, as soon as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for at once, then the Spanish for as soon as.

You'll want to know: tan pronto como.
tan pronto como — "the moment that, as soon as".
Tan pronto como sonó la alarma, salimos. As soon as the alarm went off, we went out.
The same as en cuanto, a little more formal.

The word, taken apart.
tan pronto como is built on tan, from Latin tam 'so', and pronto, which comes from Latin promptus 'brought forth, ready' (EtymDB).
