## You'll want to know: આંચકી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C479-MANDU479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**આંચકી** — *ā̃ckī* — "a fit; a convulsion".

> તાવમાં બાળકને આંચકી આવી.
>
> *tāvmā̃ bāḷakne ā̃ckī āvī.*
>
> The child had a convulsion during the fever.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આંચકી** (*ā̃ckī*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*acca-*, "sudden shock"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati *ācko*, "shock, push" (CDIAL 141), and this word is built on it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
