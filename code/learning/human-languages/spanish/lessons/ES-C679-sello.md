## You'll want to know: el sello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C679-SERVICIOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el sello** — "the stamp; the seal".

> Necesito un sello para esta carta.
>
> *I need a stamp for this letter.*

In Latin America *la estampilla*, and in Mexico *el timbre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sello* is inherited from Latin *sigillum* "a little image, a seal", a little *signum* "sign".
