## You'll want to know: ਸੱਜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C263-TURN263-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਜਾ** — *sajjā* — "right-hand; on the right".

> ਬੈਂਕ ਸੜਕ ਦੇ ਸੱਜੇ ਪਾਸੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *baiṅk saṛak de sajje pāse hai.*
>
> The bank is on the right-hand side of the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਜਾ** (*sajjā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sajya*, "strung (of a bow)"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sajjā*, "righthand", under it (CDIAL 13095).
