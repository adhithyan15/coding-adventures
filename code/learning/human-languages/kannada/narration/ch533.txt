Kannada, chapter 533: Handwork.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಸೂತಿ (kasūti) — embroidery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lullaby, then the Kannada for to reminisce.

You'll want to know: ಕಸೂತಿ.
ಕಸೂತಿ — kasūti — "embroidery".
ಅಮ್ಮ ಕರವಸ್ತ್ರದ ಮೇಲೆ ಕಸೂತಿ ಮಾಡಿದರು. amma karavastrada mēle kasūti māḍidaru. Mother embroidered a handkerchief.
ಕಸೂತಿ (kasūti) ಮಾಡು, to embroider.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಸೂತಿ (kasūti) is the Kannada word for embroidery; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕರಕುಶಲ (karakuśala) — handicraft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to reminisce, then the Kannada for embroidery.

You'll want to know: ಕರಕುಶಲ.
ಕರಕುಶಲ — karakuśala — "handicraft".
ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕರಕುಶಲ ವಸ್ತುಗಳನ್ನು ಮಾರುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು. jātreyalli karakuśala vastugaḷannu māruttiddaru. Handicrafts were being sold at the fair.
ಕರ, hand + ಕುಶಲ, skilful.

The word, taken apart.
ಕರಕುಶಲ (karakuśala) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit kara, "the hand", and kuśala, "skilful, clever" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹೆಣೆ (heṇe) — to knit; to plait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for embroidery, then the Kannada for handicraft.

You'll want to know: ಹೆಣೆ.
ಹೆಣೆ — heṇe — "to knit; to plait".
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಚಳಿಗಾಲಕ್ಕೆ ಉಣ್ಣೆಯ ಸ್ವೆಟರ್ ಹೆಣೆದರು. ajji caḷigālakke uṇṇeya sveṭar heṇedaru. Grandmother knitted a woollen sweater for the winter.
Past (I …): ಹೆಣೆದೆ, heṇede.
Hair too: ಜಡೆ ಹೆಣೆ (heṇe), to plait a braid.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೆಣೆ (heṇe) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4160 lists Kannada peṇe, "to tie together, intertwine, twist, plait, braid". Old p- became h-.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪೋಣಿಸು (pōṇisu) — to string beads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for handicraft, then the Kannada for to knit.

You'll want to know: ಪೋಣಿಸು.
ಪೋಣಿಸು — pōṇisu — "to string beads".
ತಂಗಿ ಮಲ್ಲಿಗೆ ಹೂವುಗಳನ್ನು ದಾರದಲ್ಲಿ ಪೋಣಿಸಿದಳು. taṅgi mallige hūvugaḷannu dāradalli pōṇisidaḷu. My younger sister strung jasmine flowers on a thread.
Past (I …): ಪೋಣಿಸಿದೆ, pōṇiside.
Beads, pearls or flowers on a thread.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೋಣಿಸು (pōṇisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4584 lists Kannada pōṇisu, pavaṇisu, "to couple, to string together, to thread", beside Telugu pōhaṇincu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಿಂಗರಿಸು (siṅgarisu) — to adorn; to dress up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to knit, then the Kannada for to string beads.

You'll want to know: ಸಿಂಗರಿಸು.
ಸಿಂಗರಿಸು — siṅgarisu — "to adorn; to dress up".
ಹಬ್ಬಕ್ಕೆ ಮನೆಯನ್ನು ಹೂವುಗಳಿಂದ ಸಿಂಗರಿಸಿದರು. habbakke maneyannu hūvugaḷinda siṅgarisidaru. They decorated the house with flowers for the festival.
Past (I …): ಸಿಂಗರಿಸಿದೆ, siṅgariside.
Close to ಅಲಂಕರಿಸು; ಸಿಂಗಾರ is finery.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಿಂಗರಿಸು (siṅgarisu) is built on siṅgāra, "finery", a Kannada form of Sanskrit śṛṅgāra, which Monier-Williams glosses as love and also "elegant dress, finery" (Monier-Williams).
