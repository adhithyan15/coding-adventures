5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
erwarten — to expect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to feed, then the German for to reach.

You'll want to know: erwarten.
erwarten — "to expect".

The word, taken apart.
erwarten is er- + warten, "to wait", which first meant "to watch" from Proto-Germanic wardāną (EtymDB 2.1): to watch out for something.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: fernsehen.
fernsehen — "to watch television".

The word, taken apart.
fernsehen is the word for "far" + sehen, "to see" (EtymDB 2.1): a piece-by-piece copy of television, Greek tēle, "far", + Latin vīsiō, "seeing".

The word, taken apart.
flüstern is a sound-word, shaped to imitate whispering, as English whisper is. EtymDB 2.1 lists Dutch fluisteren beside it.
