## You'll want to know: सभा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C370-SABHA370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सभा** — *sabhā* — "an assembly, a public meeting".

> शाम को गाँव में एक सभा होगी।
>
> *śām ko gā̃v meṁ ek sabhā hogī.*
>
> There will be a public meeting in the village this evening.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सभा** (*sabhā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sabhā*, "an assembly" (Monier-Williams).
