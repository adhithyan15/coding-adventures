Sanskrit, chapter 306: The Relief Camp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शिबिरम् (śibiram) — a camp; a tent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for brings down, then the Sanskrit for saves.

You'll want to know: शिबिरम्.
शिबिरम् — śibiram — "a camp; a tent".
आर्ताः जनाः शिबिरे वसन्ति। ārtāḥ janāḥ śibire vasanti. People in distress live in the camp.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
शिबिरम् (śibiram) MW gives śibira as a royal camp, a tent, any tent, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
आश्रयः (āśrayaḥ) — shelter; refuge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for saves, then the Sanskrit for a camp.

You'll want to know: आश्रयः.
आश्रयः — āśrayaḥ — "shelter; refuge".
वृद्धः शिबिरे आश्रयं याचते। vṛddhaḥ śibire āśrayaṃ yācate. The old man asks for shelter in the camp.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आश्रयः (āśrayaḥ) is built from ā and the root √śri, "to lean on, resort to"; MW gives āśraya as that on which anything rests or depends, a refuge (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
दानम् (dānam) — a donation; giving.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: दानम्.
दानम् — dānam — "a donation; giving".
जनाः शिबिराय अन्नस्य दानं प्रयच्छन्ति। janāḥ śibirāya annasya dānaṃ prayacchanti. People hand over food to the camp as a donation.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
दानम् (dānam) comes from the root √dā, "to give", as in dadāti; MW gives dāna as the act of giving and a gift (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
शरणार्थी (śaraṇārthī) — a refugee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for shelter, then the Sanskrit for a donation.

You'll want to know: शरणार्थी.
शरणार्थी — śaraṇārthī — "a refugee".
शरणार्थी शिबिरे भोजनं प्राप्नोति। śaraṇārthī śibire bhojanaṃ prāpnoti. The refugee gets food in the camp.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
शरणार्थी (śaraṇārthī) is built from śaraṇa, "refuge", and arthin, "seeking"; MW gives śaraṇārthin as seeking refuge, and a refugee is its modern use as a noun (MW).

You'll want to know: सान्त्वयति.
सान्त्वयति — sāntvayati — "comforts; consoles".
माता बालिकां सान्त्वयति। mātā bālikāṃ sāntvayati. The mother comforts the girl.
Past (she …): सा बालिकां सान्त्वितवती, sā bālikāṃ sāntvitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
सान्त्वयति (sāntvayati) is built on sāntva, "kind words, consolation": MW treats sāntvayati as rather a verb made from that noun (MW).
