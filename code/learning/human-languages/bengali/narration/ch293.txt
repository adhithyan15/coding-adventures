Bengali, chapter 293: When the Earth Shook.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ভূমিকম্প (bhūmikômpô) — an earthquake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to crack, then the Bengali for to be scorched.

You'll want to know: ভূমিকম্প.
ভূমিকম্প — bhūmikômpô — "an earthquake".
ভূমিকম্পে শহরের অনেক পুরনো বাড়ি ভেঙে পড়ল। bhūmikômpe shôhôrer ônek purôno bāṛi bhenge pôṛlô. Many old buildings in the city collapsed in the earthquake.

The word, taken apart.
ভূমিকম্প (bhūmikômpô) is Sanskrit bhūmikampa, 'an earthquake' (Monier-Williams): bhūmi, 'the earth', and kampa, 'trembling'. In the example, ভেঙে carries ঙ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ধসে পড়া (dhôse pôṛā) — to cave in; to collapse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an earthquake, then the Bengali for a tremor.

You'll want to know: ধসে পড়া.
ধসে পড়া — dhôse pôṛā — "to cave in; to collapse".
ভূমিকম্পে পাহাড়ের রাস্তা ধসে পড়ল। bhūmikômpe pāhāṛer rāstā dhôse pôṛlô. The mountain road caved in during the earthquake.
Past (he/she …): সেই দেয়াল ধসে পড়ল, sei deyāl dhôse pôṛlô.

The word, taken apart.
ধসে পড়া (dhôse pôṛā) is the -e form of ধসা, to fall in — Sanskrit dhvaṁsati, 'falls to pieces' (CDIAL 6896, which lists Bengali dhasā) — before পড়া, to fall.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a tremor, then the Bengali for to collapse.

You'll want to know: ধ্বংস.
ধ্বংস — dhvôngsô — "destruction; ruin".
ভূমিকম্পে পুরো শহরটা ধ্বংস হল। bhūmikômpe puro shôhôrṭā dhvôngsô hôlô. The whole city was destroyed in the earthquake.

The word, taken apart.
ধ্বংস (dhvôngsô) is Sanskrit dhvaṃsa, 'a falling down, ruin' (Monier-Williams), the learned noun beside ধসে পড়া (dhôse pôṛā), which wore the same root down to ধস. ধ্বংস হওয়া, to be destroyed.

Lesson 5 of 5.
চাপা পড়া (chāpā pôṛā) — to be trapped underneath; to be buried under.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to collapse, then the Bengali for destruction.

You'll want to know: চাপা পড়া.
চাপা পড়া — chāpā pôṛā — "to be trapped underneath; to be buried under".
ভাঙা বাড়ির নিচে কিছু মানুষ চাপা পড়ল। bhāngā bāṛir niche kichhu mānush chāpā pôṛlô. Some people were trapped under the collapsed house.
Past (he/she …): সেই লোক দেয়ালের নিচে চাপা পড়ল, sei lok deyāler niche chāpā pôṛlô.

The word, taken apart.
চাপা পড়া (chāpā pôṛā) puts চাপা, 'pressed down' — CDIAL 4674, a Middle Indo-Aryan capp-, which lists Bengali cāpā, 'to press' — before পড়া: to fall under a weight.
