# بے جا (be-jā) — unwarranted; out of place

## You'll want to know: بے جا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C354-MAQUL354-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بے جا** — *be-jā* — "unwarranted; out of place".

> آپ کی شکایت بے جا ہے۔
>
> *āp kī shikāyat be-jā hai.*
>
> Your complaint is unwarranted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بے جا** (*be-jā*) is usually traced to Persian *bē-jā*, "out of place": *bē*, "without", and *jā*, "a place".
