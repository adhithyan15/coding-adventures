## You'll want to know: regnare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C325-REGNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**regnare** — "to reign".

> Ha regnato per quarant'anni.
>
> *She reigned for forty years.*

Also figurative: *regna il silenzio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regnare* is inherited from Latin *rēgnāre* "to reign" (EtymDB).
