## You'll want to know: el codo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C737-CUERPO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el codo** — "the elbow".

> Me di un golpe en el codo.
>
> *I banged my elbow.*

*Codo con codo* — side by side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*codo* is inherited from Latin *cubitus* "elbow" (EtymDB), through Old Spanish *cobdo*.

## What you've built
