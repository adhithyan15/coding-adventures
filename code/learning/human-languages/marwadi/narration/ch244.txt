Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 244: A Quarrel Next Door.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
झगड़ो (jhagṛo) — a quarrel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to go missing, then the Marwadi for to cheat.

You'll want to know: झगड़ो.
झगड़ो — jhagṛo — "a quarrel".
झगड़ो मत करो। jhagṛo mat karo. Don't quarrel.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
झगड़ो (jhagṛo) is inherited from a reconstructed jhagaḍ, "quarrel"; CDIAL lists Marwari jhagṛo under it (CDIAL 5321).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happened.

You'll want to know: लड़णो.
लड़णो — laṛṇo — "to fight; to quarrel".
छोरो अर छोरी काल लड़्या। chhoro ar chhorī kāl laṛyā. The boy and the girl had a fight yesterday.
Past (he …): वो पाड़ोसी लड़्यो, vo pāṛosī laṛyo.

The word, taken apart.
लड़णो (laṛṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed laḍ, "fight". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi laṛnā, "to fight" (CDIAL 10920).
