## You'll want to know: inonder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C465-TEMPETE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**inonder** — "to flood".

> La rivière a inondé les champs.
>
> *The river flooded the fields.*

Figurative: *inonder de messages* — to flood with messages.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inonder* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inundāre* 'to overflow' (EtymDB), from *unda* 'wave'.
