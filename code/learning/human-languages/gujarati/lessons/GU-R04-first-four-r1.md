# ળ — થ — અ — િ: four forms return

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01, GU-SCRIPT-THA-01, GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01, GU-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->

Keep the four cards visible. Point as you say retroflex *lla*, aspirated *tha*,
independent short *a*, and attached short *i*. For **િ**, also say: seen before,
heard after.

Read the row once from left to right. Give **િ** its role as an attached sign,
not as an independent letter. Record a reading score out of four.

each column, with one focused repair allowed.
