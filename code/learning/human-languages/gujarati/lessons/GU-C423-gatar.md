## You'll want to know: ગટર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C423-TOWN423-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગટર** — *gaṭar* — "a drain; a sewer".

> વરસાદમાં ગટર છલકાઈ ગઈ.
>
> *varsādmā̃ gaṭar chalkāī gaī.*
>
> The drain overflowed in the rain.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગટર** (*gaṭar*) is borrowed from English *gutter*; in Gujarati a ગટર is any drain or sewer.
