## You'll want to know: invius, invia, invium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C452-ABERRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**invius, invia, invium** — "without roads, pathless, impassable".

> Silva magna et invia est; equī trānsīre nōn possunt.
>
> *The wood is big and pathless; the horses cannot get through.*

An adjective of three endings, from *in-* 'not' and *via* 'road'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invius* is built from *in-* 'not' and *via* 'road' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): without a road, so hard to pass.
