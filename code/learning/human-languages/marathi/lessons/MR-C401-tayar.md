## You'll want to know: टायर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C401-INDHAN401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**टायर** — *ṭāyar* — "a tyre".

> दुचाकीचा मागचा टायर जुना झाला आहे.
>
> *ducākīcā māgcā ṭāyar junā jhālā āhe.*
>
> The back tyre of the two-wheeler has worn out.

Masculine.

The plural is the same word: चार टायर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टायर** (*ṭāyar*) is a loan from English *tyre*.
