# тебе стоит, лучше, ты должен — advice at three strengths

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C204-ACT204-04, RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-04, RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-KAZHETSYA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to cost" and "to rest". (*Stóit. Otdykhát.*) Then "it
seems to me". (*Mne kázhetsya.*) *Мне* was "to me"; the same shape for
"you" is **тебе**, *tebé*.

## Grammar Lens: тебе стоит, it is worth your while
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-STOIT-INFINITIVE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C204-ACT204-04, RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-04, RU-GRAMMAR-INFINITIVE-AFTER-VERB-01] -->

> **Тебе стоит отдохнуть.** — *tebé stóit otdokhnút* — You should rest.

**Стоит**, "it costs", here means "it is worth it": rest would pay you
back. The infinitive follows, and the person advised takes the "to you"
form: **тебе**, or polite **вам**. *Отдохнуть* is the perfective partner
of *отдыхать*: one rest, taken.

## Grammar Lens: лучше, it is better
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-LUCHSHE-INFINITIVE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-BRAT, RU-GRAMMAR-SUPPLETIVE-ASPECT-PAIR, RU-LEX-C80-PLACES-05, RU-LEX-C83-TIME83-04, RU-LEX-C91-LOC91-02, RU-LEX-C72-WHERE1-03, RU-LEX-C75-NUM1-01] -->

> **— Уже поздно, а метро далеко. — Тебе лучше взять такси.** — *uzhé
> pózdno, a metró dalekó. tebé lúchshe vzyat taksí* — It's late already
> and the metro is far. You'd better take a taxi.

**Лучше** — *lúchshe* — "better": one choice against another. **Взять**
is the perfective partner of *брать*, the pair with two different roots.

## Grammar Lens: ты должен, you must
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-DOLZHEN-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C232-ACT232-03, RU-LEX-MAMA, RU-LEX-C231-RABOTA231-02] -->

> **Ты должен позвонить маме.** — *ty dólzhen pozvonít máme* — You must
> ring Mum.

**Должен** is a duty, and it agrees like the past: **должен** to a man,
**должна** (*dolzhná*) to a woman, **должны** (*dolzhný*) to more than
one or to *вы*. It can sound strong between friends; **стоит** and
**лучше** are gentler.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C231-RABOTA231-02] -->

**должен** comes from the Russian noun for "a debt, a duty", which EtymDB
2.1 traces to Proto-Slavic *\*dŭlgŭ*. A duty is a debt: *Я должен тебе сто рублей*
still means "I owe you a hundred roubles".

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-STOIT-INFINITIVE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-LUCHSHE-INFINITIVE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-DOLZHEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] The gentlest advice: "it's worth your while"? (***Тебе стоит***
…) "You'd better"? (***Тебе лучше*** …) The strongest, to a woman? (***Ты
должна*** …) And what form of the verb follows all three? (**The
infinitive.**)
