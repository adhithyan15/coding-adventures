Spanish, chapter 617: Elegir.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la decisión — the decision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the setback, then the Spanish for the alternative.

You'll want to know: la decisión.
la decisión — "the decision".
Es una decisión difícil. It is a difficult decision.
Tomar una decisión — to make a decision.

The word, taken apart.
decisión is a learned borrowing from Latin decisio 'a settlement', built on decidere 'to cut off, to settle', from caedere 'to cut' (EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
dilema comes from Latin dilemma, taken from Greek dilēmma 'a double premise', from dis 'twice' and lēmma 'premise' (EtymDB).
