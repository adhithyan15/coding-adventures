## You'll want to know: سہرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C487-WEDDING487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سہرا** — *sehrā* — "a bridegroom's veil of flowers".

> دولہا کے سر پر پھولوں کا سہرا تھا۔
>
> *dūlhā ke sar par phūloṅ kā sehrā thā.*
>
> The bridegroom wore a veil of flowers on his head.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سہرا** (*sehrā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sehrā*, "a garland worn at a wedding", under Sanskrit *śēkhara*, "the top of the head, a chaplet" (CDIAL 12604).
