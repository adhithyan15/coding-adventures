## You'll want to know: favōnius, favōniī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C487-AQUILO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**favōnius, favōniī** — "the west wind (of spring)".

> Vēre favōnius venit, et flōrēs aperiuntur.
>
> *In spring the west wind comes, and the flowers open.*

Second declension, masculine: *favōnius, favōniī m.* The mild wind that brings spring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*favōnius* is built on *faveō* 'to be kind, to favour' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the kindly wind of spring. The Alpine wind name *Föhn* is usually traced to it.
