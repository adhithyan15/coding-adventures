## You'll want to know: পথ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C387-POTH387-01]; assesses=[] -->

**পথ** — *pôth* — "a way; a path".

> গ্রামের পথে আমরা একটা পুকুর দেখলাম।
>
> *grāmer pôthe āmrā ekṭā pukur dekhlām.*
>
> On the village path we saw a pond.

পথে, on the way: পথে দেখা হল, we met on the way. রাস্তা is the road itself; পথ is also the way you take.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পথ** (*pôth*) is Sanskrit *patha*, "a way, a path, a road" (Monier-Williams), the stem Sanskrit uses at the end of compounds, taken over whole.
