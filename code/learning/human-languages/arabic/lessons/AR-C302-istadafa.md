## You'll want to know: استضاف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C302-HIWAR302-04]; assesses=[] -->

**استضاف** — *istaḍāfa* — "to host, to have as a guest".

> استضافت المدرسة كاتبا معروفا.
>
> *istaḍāfat al-madrasa kātiban maʿrūfan.*
>
> The school hosted a well-known writer.

Present: يستضيف, *yastaḍīf*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استضاف** (*istaḍāfa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-y-f* (Lane), the root of the taught *ḍayf*, a guest. It is Form X, first "to ask someone for protection or refuge"; today a programme or a city that hosts.
