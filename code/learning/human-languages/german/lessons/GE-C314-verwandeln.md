## You'll want to know: verwandeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C314-ZAUBERN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**verwandeln** — "to turn into, to transform".

> Die Hexe verwandelte ihn in einen Frosch.
>
> *The witch turned him into a frog.*

*In etwas verwandeln*; reflexive, *sich verwandeln* — to change into.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verwandeln* is inherited from Middle High German *verwandelen* (EtymDB), built on *wandeln* "to change", a relative of *wenden* "to turn".
