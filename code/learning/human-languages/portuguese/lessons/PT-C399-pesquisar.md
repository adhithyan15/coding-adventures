## You'll want to know: pesquisar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C399-REDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pesquisar** — "to search, to look up; to research".

> Pesquisei o horário do comboio no telemóvel.
>
> *I looked up the train times on my phone.*

*Fazer uma pesquisa* — to do a search, to do research.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pesquisar* is built on *pesquisa* "search", which EtymDB traces through Spanish *pesquisa* to Latin *perquīrere* "to make diligent search for" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *quaerere* "to seek".
