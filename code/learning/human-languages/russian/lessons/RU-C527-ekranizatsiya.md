## You'll want to know: экранизация
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C527-SERIES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**экранизация** (*ekranizátsiya*) — "a screen adaptation".

> Экранизация оказалась лучше книги.
>
> *Ekranizátsiya okazálas lúchshe knígi.*
>
> The film version turned out better than the book.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*экранизация* is built on *экран* 'a screen', which was borrowed from French *écran* (EtymDB); EtymDB has no entry for *экранизация*.
