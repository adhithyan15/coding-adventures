Urdu, chapter 337: Denial and Confession.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تصدیق کرنا (tasdīq karnā) — to confirm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a document, then the Urdu for genuine.

You'll want to know: تصدیق کرنا.
تصدیق کرنا — tasdīq karnā — "to confirm".
پولیس نے اس خبر کی تصدیق کی۔ polīs ne is khabar kī tasdīq kī. The police confirmed the news.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے خبر کی تصدیق کی, us ne khabar kī tasdīq kī.

The word, taken apart.
تصدیق کرنا (tasdīq karnā) is built from tasdīq, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taṣdīq, "declaring something true", and کرنا, "to do".

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Lesson 3 of 5.
جھٹلانا (jhuṭlānā) — to deny; to give the lie to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to confirm, then the Urdu for to acknowledge.

You'll want to know: جھٹلانا.
جھٹلانا — jhuṭlānā — "to deny; to give the lie to".
وہ ہر سچی بات کو جھٹلاتا ہے۔ voh har sachchī bāt ko jhuṭlātā hai. He denies everything that is true.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے خبر کو جھٹلایا, us ne khabar ko jhuṭlāyā.

The word, taken apart.
جھٹلانا (jhuṭlānā) is built on جھوٹ, "a lie", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jhūṭhā, "lying, false", under jhūṭṭha, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5407). To call a thing a lie.

Lesson 5 of 5.
غلط فہمی (ghalat-fahmī) — a misunderstanding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to give the lie to, then the Urdu for to own up to.

You'll want to know: غلط فہمی.
غلط فہمی — ghalat-fahmī — "a misunderstanding".
یہ ایک غلط فہمی تھی، کوئی جھوٹ نہیں۔ yih ek ghalat-fahmī thī, koī jhūṭ nahīṅ. It was a misunderstanding, not a lie.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
غلط فہمی (ghalat-fahmī) is usually traced to Persian ghalat-fahmī, "a mistaken understanding": غلط, "wrong", with fahmī, "understanding".
