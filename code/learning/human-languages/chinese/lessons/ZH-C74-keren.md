## You'll want to know: 客人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C74-VISIT74-02]; assesses=[] -->

**客人** — *kèrén* — "guest".

> **客人来了快开门** *kèrén lái le kuài kāi mén* — The guests are here: quick, open the door!

The new character, in pieces: 客 — the roof of 家, a walking foot under it, and 口 at the bottom. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**客人** (*kèrén*) is built from 客 'guest' and 人 'person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaek* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
