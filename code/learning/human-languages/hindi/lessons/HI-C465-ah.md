## You'll want to know: आह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C465-VILAP465-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आह** — *āh* — "a sigh".

> थककर दादी ने एक लंबी आह भरी।
>
> *thakkar dādī ne ek lambī āh bharī.*
>
> Worn out, my grandmother heaved a long sigh.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आह** (*āh*) is the sound of a sigh made into a noun; Monier-Williams gives Sanskrit *āha* as an exclamation (Monier-Williams). *āh bharnā*, "to fill a sigh", is to heave one.
