# ق (qāf) — the letter inside اللقاء

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-ALIF-MAQSURA-01, AR-SCRIPT-C47-FA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ى and ف once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **إلى اللقاء** (*ilā l-liqāʾ*), "see you".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-QAF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ق** — *qāf*, a *k* made far back in the throat.

It is inside **اللقاء** (*al-liqāʾ*), "the meeting", in **إلى اللقاء**. Its head is
the same as **ف**, which you wrote in the last lesson, but it carries **two dots**
and, standing alone, its bowl dips deeper, below the line.

## Writing: ق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-QAF-01] -->

- **1.** start here: loop counterclockwise around the small closed head
- **2.** continue down and left through the deep bowl without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then place the upper-right dot
- **4.** lift again, then place the upper-left dot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Introduction to Arabic: Egyptian Arabic for first-year students, Alphabet ف ق, independent Qaf at 00:01.5–00:03.5 (University of Oregon Libraries Open Textbook, 2023; accessed 2026-08-23).
