# Review — advice, and what follows

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-EXPLANATION-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "in my opinion this is beautiful". (*mama matena etat sundaram*.) Then "because of the rain". (*varṣākāraṇāt*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SO-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SO-02, SA-LEX-C279-UPADESAH-01, SA-LEX-C26-ROLE-05, SA-LEX-C243-QUAL243-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: You are ill. You should go to the doctor.]
- [YOU SAY: You should read every day, to a friend.]
- [YOU SAY: It is raining, so we will stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: She is tired, therefore she is not coming.]
- [YOU SAY: I have a piece of advice: he should tell the truth.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SO-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SO-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **गच्छेत्** or **गच्छति**, for advice? (**गच्छेत्**.) **पठेः**: to whom? (A friend, **त्वम्**.) Now the other way round: "so, therefore"? (**अतः**, **तस्मात्**.) "I must go"? (**मया गन्तव्यम्**.)
