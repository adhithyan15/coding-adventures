## You'll want to know: ātriēnsis, ātriēnsis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C531-IANI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ātriēnsis, ātriēnsis** — "a steward, house-manager".

> Ātriēnsis servīs labōrem dat.
>
> *The steward gives the slaves their work.*

Masculine, third declension, from *ātrium*: the slave in charge of the hall and the house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ātriēnsis** is built on *ātrium* "hall" with *-ēnsis* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
