Gujarati, chapter 424: At the Bank Counter.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
કેશિયર (keśiyar) — a cashier.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a stink, then the Gujarati for to set up a home.

You'll want to know: કેશિયર.
કેશિયર — keśiyar — "a cashier".
બેંકના કેશિયરે મને નવી નોટો આપી. beṅknā keśiyre mane navī noṭo āpī. The bank cashier gave me new notes.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કેશિયર (keśiyar) is borrowed from English cashier.

What you've built.

Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a cashier, then the Gujarati for a passbook.

કાપલી — kāplī — "a slip of paper".
પૈસા જમા કરવા માટે આ કાપલી ભરો. paisā jamā karvā māṭe ā kāplī bharo. Fill in this slip to deposit money.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કાપલી (kāplī) is built on કાપવું, to cut, a tadbhava of Sanskrit kalpayati, "trims, cuts" (CDIAL 2944): a strip of paper cut off.

ટોકન — ṭokan — "a token (a numbered slip for your turn)".
બેંકમાં મને પાંચ નંબરનું ટોકન મળ્યું. beṅkmā̃ mane pā̃c nambarnũ ṭokan maḷyũ. At the bank I got token number five.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટોકન (ṭokan) is borrowed from English token: at a bank or a clinic, the numbered slip that keeps your place in the queue.
