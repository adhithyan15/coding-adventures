## You'll want to know: la fábula
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C563-RELATO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la fábula** — "the fable".

> En la fábula, la tortuga le gana a la liebre.
>
> *In the fable, the tortoise beats the hare.*

*La moraleja* is the moral at the end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fábula* is a learned borrowing from Latin *fābula* 'a story, a tale' (EtymDB), built on *fārī* 'to speak' (Lewis & Short). The inherited form of the same word is *habla*.
