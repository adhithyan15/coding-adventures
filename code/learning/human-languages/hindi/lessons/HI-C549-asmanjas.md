## You'll want to know: असमंजस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C549-VIKALP549-03]; assesses=[] -->

**असमंजस** — *asmañjas* — "indecision; uncertainty".

> नौकरी बदलने को लेकर वह असमंजस में है।
>
> *Naukrī badalne ko lekar vah asmañjas meṁ hai.*
>
> He cannot decide whether to change jobs.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**असमंजस** (*asmañjas*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *asamañjasa*, "unfit, unbecoming" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for not knowing which way to turn.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
