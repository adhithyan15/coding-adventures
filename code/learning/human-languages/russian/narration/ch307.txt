Russian, chapter 307: Divorce and moving on.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
развод (razvód) — a divorce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a church wedding, then the Russian for to part.

You'll want to know: развод.
развод (razvód) — "a divorce".
После развода он уехал в другой город. Pósle razvóda on uyékhal v drugóy górod. After the divorce he moved to another town.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
развод (razvód) is built on разводить "to lead apart", from раз- and водить "to lead", inherited from Proto-Slavic voditi (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
разводиться (razvodítsya) — to get divorced.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to part, then the Russian for a divorce.

You'll want to know: разводиться.
разводиться (razvodítsya) — "to get divorced".
Его родители развелись давно. Yevó rodíteli razvelís davnó. His parents divorced long ago.
Imperfective; the perfective is развестись (razvestís), past развёлся (razvyólsya), развелась. It takes с + instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
разводиться (razvodítsya) is built on раз- "apart" and водить "to lead", inherited from Proto-Slavic voditi (EtymDB), with the reflexive -ся: to be led apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
увольняться (uvolnyátsya) — to quit (a job), to resign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get divorced, then the Russian for a move.

You'll want to know: увольняться.
увольняться (uvolnyátsya) — "to quit (a job), to resign".
Она уволилась и открыла своё кафе. Oná uvólilas i otkrýla svoyó kafé. She quit her job and opened her own café.
Imperfective; perfective уволиться (uvólitsya). It takes с + genitive: уволиться с работы.

The word, taken apart.
увольняться (uvolnyátsya) is built on у- and воля "will, freedom", inherited from Proto-Slavic voľa, from PIE welh₁- "to choose, to wish" (EtymDB): to set oneself free.

Warm-up.

наследство (naslédstvo) — "an inheritance".

Neuter. Получить в наследство (naslédstvo) — to inherit.

The word, taken apart.
наследство (naslédstvo) is built on наследовать "to inherit", from на- and след "a track" (EtymDB): what comes after, in the footsteps of the dead.
