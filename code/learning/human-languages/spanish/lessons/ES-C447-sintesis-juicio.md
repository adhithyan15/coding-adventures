# (un mensaje desde urgencias) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-03, ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *urgencias* and *torpe*. One of them is where the writer is, and
the other is how she explains getting there.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-01, ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-03, ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-04] -->

Read both, then answer.

**1 — de Lucía**

> Estoy en **urgencias**, pero no es grave. Me caí bajando del autobús, soy una
> **torpe**. Me han hecho un **análisis** y una radiografía y estoy esperando.

**2 — la respuesta**

> ¿Quieres que me acerque? Me **comentó** tu hermana que hoy librabas, así que
> no me pilla lejos. Dime algo cuando tengas los resultados.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: where Lucía is, and why]
- [YOU SAY: what has been done to her so far]
- [YOU SAY: what the second person offers, and how she knew]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-02, ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-03, ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-01] -->

Two things in that exchange do more work than they look like.

**Pero no es grave**, in the first line, before any of the detail. A Spanish
message from a hospital almost always leads with the reassurance, because the
word *urgencias* lands hard and the writer is defusing it immediately. Putting
that clause last would read as burying it.

And **me comentó tu hermana**: not *me dijo*, not *me contó*. *Comentar* is the
mildest of the three — it says the sister mentioned it in passing, with no
weight on it, which is exactly the right temperature for explaining how you
happen to know somebody's day off. Choosing *me dijo* would sound like she was
told on purpose.

That is the difference between having the words and using them. Both writers
pick the softest option available, twice each, and the message reads as calm
because of it.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-01, ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-02, ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-03, ES-LEX-C447-JUICIO-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four sentences from a
waiting room: say where you are, say it is not serious, say what test they have
done, and say somebody mentioned they might come.

Then check yourself: did you put the reassurance **before** the detail?
