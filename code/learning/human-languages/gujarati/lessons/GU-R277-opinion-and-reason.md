# Review — opinions and reasons

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C276-INE-FORM-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C276-FIRST-THEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I went home and ate". (*hũ ghare jaīne jamyo*.) Then "first we drank tea, then we went to the market". (*pahelā̃ ame cā pīdhī, pachī ame bajār gayā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C277-MAT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C277-LAGE-CHE-KE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C277-OPINION-ASK-01, GU-LEX-C277-KARAN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C277-KARAN-KE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C277-NE-LIDHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C274-NEGATIVE-SHORT-VERBS-01, GU-LEX-VARSAAD-01, GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: What do you think? What is your opinion?]
- [YOU SAY: I think it will rain. In my opinion the train will come late.]
- [YOU SAY: I came late, because the train was late.]
- [YOU SAY: Because of the rain, we stayed at home. Because of the rain, I won't go.]
- [YOU SAY: What is the reason for it? I have two reasons.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C277-MAT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C277-LAGE-CHE-KE-01, GU-LEX-C277-KARAN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C277-KARAN-KE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C277-NE-LIDHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "In my opinion"? (**મારા મતે**.) "I don't think so"? (**મને નથી લાગતું**.) Which takes a clause, **કારણ કે** or **-ને લીધે**? (**કારણ કે**.)
