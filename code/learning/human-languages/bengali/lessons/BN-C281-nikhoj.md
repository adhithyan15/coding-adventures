# নিখোঁজ (nikhõj) — missing

## You'll want to know: নিখোঁজ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C281-BHAGYO281-02]; assesses=[] -->

**নিখোঁজ** — *nikhõj* — "missing".

> ঝড়ের পরে অনেক জেলে নিখোঁজ ছিল।
>
> *jhôṛer pôre ônek jele nikhõj chhilô.*
>
> After the storm many fishermen were missing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নিখোঁজ** (*nikhõj*) puts the prefix *ni-*, 'without', before খোঁজ, a trace or news of someone, the noun behind **খোঁজা**, to look for (CDIAL 3929 lists Bengali *khõj*, 'trace'): without a trace, missing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
