## You'll want to know: ટપવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C404-PATH404-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ટપવું** — *ṭapvũ* — "to jump over; to leap across".

> છોકરો ખાડો ટપી ગયો.
>
> *chokro khāḍo ṭapī gayo.*
>
> The boy jumped over the pothole.

Past (he …): તે ખાડો ટપી ગયો, *te khāḍo ṭapī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટપવું** (*ṭapvũ*) goes back to *\*tarpati*, "jumps", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to leap, to jump over" (CDIAL 5727).
