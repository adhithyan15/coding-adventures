## You'll want to know: うつわ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C483-CLAY483-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うつわ** — *utsuwa* — "a bowl; a vessel".

> **りょうりを しろい うつわに もります。** — *ryōri o shiroi utsuwa ni morimasu* — "I serve the food in a white bowl."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うつわ** (*utsuwa*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
