## You'll want to know: la consigne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C414-COUCHETTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la consigne** — "the left-luggage office; the instruction".

> Nous avons laissé nos sacs à la consigne.
>
> *We left our bags at the left-luggage office.*

*Les consignes de sécurité* — the safety instructions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consigne* is built on *consigner* 'to put in safe keeping', a learned borrowing from Latin *consignāre* 'to seal', from *signum* 'mark' (Lewis & Short).
