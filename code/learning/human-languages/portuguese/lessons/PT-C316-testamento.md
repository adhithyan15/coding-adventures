## You'll want to know: o testamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C316-HERANCA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o testamento** — "the will, the testament".

> O avô deixou um testamento.
>
> *Grandfather left a will.*

*Fazer testamento* — to make a will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testamento* comes from Latin *testāmentum* "a will" (EtymDB), from *testārī* "to bear witness", built on *testis* "a witness".
