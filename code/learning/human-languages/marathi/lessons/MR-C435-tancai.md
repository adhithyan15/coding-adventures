## You'll want to know: टंचाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C435-TANCHAI435-01]; assesses=[] -->

**टंचाई** — *ṭañcāī* — "a shortage".

> उन्हाळ्यात गावात पाण्याची टंचाई असते.
>
> *unhāḷyāt gāvāt pāṇyācī ṭañcāī aste.*
>
> In summer the village is short of water.

Feminine.

The thing in short supply takes -ची: पाण्याची टंचाई.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टंचाई** (*ṭañcāī*) is a Marathi word for a shortage of water, food or money; its origin is not settled.
