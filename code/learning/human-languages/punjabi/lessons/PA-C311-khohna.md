# ਖੋਹਣਾ (khohṇā) — to snatch

## You'll want to know: ਖੋਹਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C311-LOST311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੋਹਣਾ** — *khohṇā* — "to snatch".

> ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚੇ ਦੇ ਹੱਥ ਤੋਂ ਚਾਕੂ ਖੋਹਿਆ।
>
> *maiṁ bachche de hatth ton chākū khohiā.*
>
> I snatched the knife out of the child's hand.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕੁੱਤੇ ਤੋਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖੋਹੀ, *maiṁ kutte ton roṭī khohī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੋਹਣਾ** (*khohṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*skoṣati*, "plucks out, pokes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khohṇā*, "to take away by force", under it (CDIAL 13661).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
