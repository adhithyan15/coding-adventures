## You'll want to know: निदर्शन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C362-ANDOLAN362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निदर्शन** — *nidarśan* — "a demonstration (protest)".

> कामगारांनी कार्यालयासमोर निदर्शनं केली.
>
> *kāmgārānnī kāryālyāsamor nidarśanaṁ kelī.*
>
> The workers demonstrated in front of the office.

Neuter. Plural: निदर्शनं, *nidarśanaṁ*.

Mostly plural: निदर्शनं करणे, to demonstrate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निदर्शन** (*nidarśan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nidarśana*, "pointing to, showing" (Monier-Williams).
