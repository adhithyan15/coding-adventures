## You'll want to know: સદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C317-ITIHAS317-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સદી** — *sadī* — "a century".

> આ મંદિર ત્રણ સદી જૂનું છે.
>
> *ā mandir traṇ sadī jūnũ che.*
>
> This temple is three centuries old.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સદી** (*sadī*) is usually traced to Persian *sadī*, "a century", built on the word for a hundred. It is feminine: **આ સદીમાં**, in this century.
