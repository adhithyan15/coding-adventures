## You'll want to know: सम्भाषते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C421-PALLI421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भाषते** — *sambhāṣate* — "converses; chats".

> यात्री चत्वरे जनैः सह सम्भाषते।
>
> *yātrī catvare janaiḥ saha sambhāṣate.*
>
> The traveller chats with people in the square.

Past (he …): सः जनैः सह सम्भाषितवान्, *saḥ janaiḥ saha sambhāṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भाषते** (*sambhāṣate*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √bhāṣ, "to speak", which also gives *bhāṣate*, "speaks"; MW gives *sam-bhāṣ* as to speak together, to converse (MW).
