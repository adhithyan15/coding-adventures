## You'll want to know: il carabiniere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C294-PATTUGLIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il carabiniere** — "the carabiniere, the military police officer".

> Ho chiesto aiuto a un carabiniere.
>
> *I asked a carabiniere for help.*

One of the two main police forces; the other is *la polizia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carabiniere* is usually traced to French *carabinier*, a soldier armed with a *carabine*, a short gun. EtymDB derives *carabine* from *carabin* "a dragoon", whose own origin is not settled.
