Lesson 1 of 5.
विपत्तिः (vipattiḥ) — adversity; a calamity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a mishap, then the Sanskrit for ceases.

You'll want to know: विपत्तिः.
विपत्तिः — vipattiḥ — "adversity; a calamity".
यदि विपत्तिः आगच्छति, तर्हि मित्राणि साहाय्यं करिष्यन्ति। yadi vipattiḥ āgacchati, tarhi mitrāṇi sāhāyyaṃ kariṣyanti. If adversity comes, friends will help.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विपत्तिः (vipattiḥ) is built on vi, "apart", and the root √pad, "to go, to fall"; MW gives vi-patti "going wrongly, adversity, misfortune, failure, disaster" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for ceases, then the Sanskrit for adversity.

You'll want to know: आपद्.
आपद् — āpad — "a calamity".
यदि आपत् आगच्छति, तर्हि धैर्यं धारयतु। yadi āpat āgacchati, tarhi dhairyaṃ dhārayatu. If a calamity comes, keep your courage.
A feminine noun.
The stem ends in -d; said on its own, as here, the word ends in -t: आपत्, āpat.

The word, taken apart.
आपद् (āpad) is built on ā and the root √pad, "to fall"; MW gives āpad "misfortune, calamity, distress" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनावृष्टिः (anāvṛṣṭiḥ) — a drought.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a calamity, then the Sanskrit for destruction.

You'll want to know: अनावृष्टिः.
अनावृष्टिः — anāvṛṣṭiḥ — "a drought".
यदि अनावृष्टिः भवति, तर्हि कृषकः दुःखी भविष्यति। yadi anāvṛṣṭiḥ bhavati, tarhi kṛṣakaḥ duḥkhī bhaviṣyati. If there is a drought, the farmer will suffer.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अनावृष्टिः (anāvṛṣṭiḥ) is built from an-, "not", and ā-vṛṣṭi, "rain", from the root √vṛṣ, "to rain"; MW gives an-āvṛṣṭi "want of rain, drought" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रतिकरोति (pratikaroti) — remedies; counteracts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for destruction, then the Sanskrit for a drought.

You'll want to know: प्रतिकरोति.
प्रतिकरोति — pratikaroti — "remedies; counteracts".
यदि रोगः आगच्छति, तर्हि वैद्यः तं प्रतिकरोति। yadi rogaḥ āgacchati, tarhi vaidyaḥ taṃ pratikaroti. If an illness comes, the doctor remedies it.
Past (he …): सः रोगं प्रतिकृतवान्, saḥ rogaṃ pratikṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिकरोति (pratikaroti) is built on prati, "against", and the root √kṛ, "to do"; MW gives prati-√kṛ "to counteract, to resist" and "to repay, to requite" (MW).
