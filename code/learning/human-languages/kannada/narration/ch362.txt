Kannada, chapter 362: Builders of Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಾಗರಿಕತೆ (nāgarikate) — a civilization.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a bastion, then the Kannada for to bury.

You'll want to know: ನಾಗರಿಕತೆ.
ನಾಗರಿಕತೆ — nāgarikate — "a civilization".
ನದಿಯ ದಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಾಚೀನ ನಾಗರಿಕತೆ ಬೆಳೆಯಿತು. nadiya daḍadalli ondu prācīna nāgarikate beḷeyitu. An ancient civilization grew up on the river bank.
Plural: ನಾಗರಿಕತೆಗಳು, nāgarikategaḷu.
-ತೆ makes an abstract noun of an adjective: town-bred ways become civilization.

The word, taken apart.
ನಾಗರಿಕತೆ (nāgarikate) is built on Sanskrit nāgarika, "born or living in a town, civic, polite" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada abstract ending -te.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು.
ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು — sthāpisu — "to found; to establish".
ಆ ರಾಜ ಈ ನಗರವನ್ನು ಐನೂರು ವರ್ಷದ ಹಿಂದೆ ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸಿದನು. ā rāja ī nagaravannu ainūru varṣada hinde sthāpisidanu. That king founded this city five hundred years ago.
Past (I …): ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸಿದೆ, sthāpiside.
Cities, schools and companies are founded: ಶಾಲೆಯನ್ನು ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು (sthāpisu).

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು (sthāpisu) is built on Sanskrit sthāpana, "causing to stand, fixing" (CDIAL 13755), from sthāpayati, "makes stand", with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ನಿರ್ಮಿಸು (nirmisu) — to construct; to build.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a civilization, then the Kannada for to found.

You'll want to know: ನಿರ್ಮಿಸು.
ನಿರ್ಮಿಸು — nirmisu — "to construct; to build".
ಕೆಲಸಗಾರರು ನದಿಗೆ ಹೊಸ ಸೇತುವೆ ನಿರ್ಮಿಸಿದರು. kelasagāraru nadige hosa sētuve nirmisidaru. The workers built a new bridge over the river.
Past (I …): ನಿರ್ಮಿಸಿದೆ, nirmiside.
A formal partner of ಕಟ್ಟು. Films are "made" with it too: ಸಿನಿಮಾ ನಿರ್ಮಿಸು (nirmisu).

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರ್ಮಿಸು (nirmisu) is built on Sanskrit nirmāṇa, "measuring, making, creating" (CDIAL 7368), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕೆಡವು (keḍavu) — to demolish; to knock down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to found, then the Kannada for to construct.

You'll want to know: ಕೆಡವು.
ಕೆಡವು — keḍavu — "to demolish; to knock down".
ಅವರು ಹಳೆಯ ಕಟ್ಟಡವನ್ನು ಕೆಡವಿದರು. avaru haḷeya kaṭṭaḍavannu keḍavidaru. They demolished the old building.
Past (I …): ಕೆಡವಿದೆ, keḍavide.
What is knocked down takes -ಅನ್ನು. A wrestler can ಕೆಡವು (keḍavu) his rival too.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೆಡವು (keḍavu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1524 lists Kannada keḍavu, "to throw down, to fell", under words for lying down.
