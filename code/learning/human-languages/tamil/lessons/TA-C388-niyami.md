## You'll want to know: நியமி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C388-OPPUTAL388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**நியமி** — *niyami* — "to appoint".

> நிறுவனம் ஒரு புதிய மேலாளரை நியமித்தது.
>
> *niṟuvaṉam oru putiya mēlāḷarai niyamittatu.*
>
> The company appointed a new manager.

Past (I …): நியமித்தேன், *niyamittēṉ*.

Who is appointed takes -ஐ; as what: மேலாளராக நியமி.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நியமி** (*niyami*) is a verb built on Sanskrit *niyama*, "restraint; fixed law" (CDIAL 7250), with the loan-verb ending -இ: to fix someone in a post.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
