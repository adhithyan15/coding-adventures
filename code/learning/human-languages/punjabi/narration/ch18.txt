Lesson 3 of 7.
ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ (rihāish) — assemble the residence label.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one label until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Touch the pieces in order: ਰਿ, ਹਾ, ਇ, ਸ਼.

Script — one label.
Join ਰਿ + ਹਾ + ਇ + ਸ਼ = ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ (rihāish). On a form, ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ (rihāish) labels the residence line. Copy the label once and leave its answer blank.

Guided Practice.
Point to the label and say what information belongs there. Do not add a value yet.

Wrap-up recall.
The familiar spoken word ਰਹਿਣਾ can remind you of living somewhere; only the form label is scored here.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
A form shows ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ (rihāish). What information belongs on that line?
