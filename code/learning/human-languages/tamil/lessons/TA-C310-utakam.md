## You'll want to know: ஊடகம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C310-OODAKAM310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊடகம்** — *ūṭakam* — "the media; a medium".

> ஊடகங்கள் அந்தச் சம்பவத்தைப் பெரிதாகப் பேசின.
>
> *ūṭakaṅkaḷ antac campavattaip peritākap pēciṉa.*
>
> The media made a lot of that incident.

Plural: ஊடகங்கள், *ūṭakaṅkaḷ*.

Usually plural: ஊடகங்கள், the media.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊடகம்** (*ūṭakam*) is built on native ஊடு, "the middle, what comes between" (DEDR 737): the media stand between events and their readers.
