Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रत्यागच्छति (pratyāgacchati) — comes back, returns.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for shows, then the Sanskrit for worships.

You'll want to know: प्रत्यागच्छति.
प्रत्यागच्छति — pratyāgacchati — "comes back, returns".

The word, taken apart.
pratyāgacchati is built from prati "back, against", ā "toward" and the root √gam "go", whose present stem is gaccha- (MW): "comes back, returns". It is āgacchati "comes" with prati in front, and MW sets √gam beside English come.

शेते — śete — "lies down".

The word, taken apart.
śete comes from the root √śī "lie, rest, sleep" (MW; CDIAL 12605), already in the Rigveda, with the vowel strengthened to e throughout the present. śayana "bed, lying down" comes from the same root (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
आस्ते (āste) — sits, stays.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for comes back, then the Sanskrit for lies down.

You'll want to know: आस्ते.
आस्ते — āste — "sits, stays".

The word, taken apart.
āste comes from the root √ās "sit, stay, remain" (CDIAL 1480), inherited from Indo-European (EtymDB): MW compares Greek hēmai "sit". It lives on as Marathi asṇe "to wait, to exist" (CDIAL 1480), and āsana "seat, posture" comes from the same root.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बिभेति (bibheti) — is afraid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for lies down, then the Sanskrit for sits.

You'll want to know: बिभेति.
बिभेति — bibheti — "is afraid".

The word, taken apart.
bibheti comes from the root √bhī "fear", with the doubled syllable bi- of class-3 verbs (MW; CDIAL 9241). MW compares German beben "tremble", and bhaya "fear", which you met in bhayaṅkara, comes from the same root.

You'll want to know: ध्यायति.
ध्यायति — dhyāyati — "meditates, thinks of".

The word, taken apart.
dhyāyati comes from the root √dhyai "think of, contemplate, meditate" (MW). dhyāna "meditation" comes from the same root (MW), and Pali kept it as jhāna "contemplation" (CDIAL 6889).
