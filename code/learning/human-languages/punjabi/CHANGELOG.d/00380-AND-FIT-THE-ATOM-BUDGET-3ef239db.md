## ਹੋਣਾ and ਖਾਣਾ fit the atom budget

Two chapter-7 verb lessons each introduced four atoms against a budget of
three. Each now ends where its third atom ends, and a continuation straight
after it carries the rest:

| lesson | keeps | continuation carries |
|---|---|---|
| **ਹੋਣਾ** *hoṇā* | the word, the **-ṇā** infinitive, and *bhū-* → *be* | `PA-C07-hona-two-roots`: *hoṇā* and *hai* are two old verbs braided into one, as English *be* and *is* are |
| **ਖਾਣਾ** *khāṇā* | the word and *khādati* | `PA-C07-khana-tone`: the tone only Punjabi kept, and the *kòṛā* / *koṛā* pair |

Each kept lesson's practice and recall now ask only about what it still
teaches. The tone and two-roots questions move with their sections.

The **ਹੋਣਾ** continuation pushed the panj convergence's post-nasal voicing
and its *panjāh / pacās* evidence one lesson past R1. Both are retrieved in the
continuation's warm-up. The moves also close three older windows: R2 and R3
for the panj comparison, and R3 for the top-line clue.
