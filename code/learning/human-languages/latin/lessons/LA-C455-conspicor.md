## You'll want to know: cōnspicor, cōnspicārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C455-CONSPICOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnspicor, cōnspicārī** — "to catch sight of, to see".

> Tandem viātōrēs mūrōs urbis cōnspicantur.
>
> *At last the travellers catch sight of the city walls.*

Deponent: *cōnspicor, cōnspicārī, cōnspicātus sum*, with an accusative. A related active verb is *cōnspiciō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōnspicor* is built from *con-* and the root of *speciō* 'to look' (Lewis & Short), the root seen in *spectō* 'to watch'. EtymDB lists it but gives no parent.
