## You'll want to know: दाद देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C539-DAD539-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दाद देणे** — *dād deṇe* — "to applaud; to show appreciation".

> गायिकेच्या गाण्याला लोकांनी मनापासून दाद दिली.
>
> *gāyikecyā gāṇyālā lokānnī manāpāsūn dād dilī.*
>
> People warmly applauded the singer's song.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दाद दिली, *tyāne dād dilī*.

What is praised takes -ला: गाण्याला दाद देणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाद देणे** (*dād deṇe*) is दाद, a loan from Persian *dād*, "justice, redress", with देणे: to give a performer their due.
