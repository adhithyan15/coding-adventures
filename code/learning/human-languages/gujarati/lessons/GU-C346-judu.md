## You'll want to know: જુદું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C346-DAKHLO346-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જુદું** — *judũ* — "different".

> મારો મત તમારા મત કરતાં જુદો છે.
>
> *māro mat tamārā mat kartā̃ judo che.*
>
> My view is different from yours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જુદું** (*judũ*) is usually traced to Persian *judā*, "separate, apart", with the Gujarati adjective ending *-ũ*.
