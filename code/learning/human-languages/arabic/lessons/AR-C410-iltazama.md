## You'll want to know: التزم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C410-IBAHA410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**التزم** — *iltazama* — "to comply".

> التزم الجميع بالقواعد.
>
> *iltazama al-jamīʿ bi-l-qawāʿid.*
>
> Everyone complied with the rules.

Present: يلتزم, *yaltazim*.

It takes بـ: التزم بـ ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**التزم** (*iltazama*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-z-m*, "to cling, to stick to" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to bind oneself to a thing: to comply, to commit.
