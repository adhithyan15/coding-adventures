## You'll want to know: گالری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C525-PAINTING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گالری** (*gâleri*) — "a gallery".

> نقاشی های او در یک گالری است.
>
> *naqqâshihâ-ye u dar yek gâleri ast.*
>
> Her paintings are in a gallery.

*gâleri-ye naqqâshi* — an art gallery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gâleri* was borrowed from French *galerie* (EtymDB).
