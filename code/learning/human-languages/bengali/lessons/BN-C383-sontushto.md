# সন্তুষ্ট (sôntushṭô) — satisfied; content

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C383-SARTHOK383-01, BN-LEX-C383-SARTHOK383-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for worthwhile, then the Bengali for in vain.

## You'll want to know: সন্তুষ্ট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C383-SARTHOK383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সন্তুষ্ট** — *sôntushṭô* — "satisfied; content".

> খদ্দের সন্তুষ্ট হলে আবার আসবে।
>
> *khôdder sôntushṭô hôle ābār āsbe.*
>
> If customers are satisfied, they'll come back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সন্তুষ্ট** (*sôntushṭô*) is Sanskrit *saṃtuṣṭa*, quite satisfied (Monier-Williams). What satisfies you takes -এ: কাজে সন্তুষ্ট.
