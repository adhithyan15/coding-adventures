## You'll want to know: empacotar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C422-BAGAGENS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**empacotar** — "to pack up, to wrap into parcels".

> Empacotamos os livros em caixas de papel.
>
> *We pack up the books in cardboard boxes.*

For a suitcase people usually say *fazer a mala*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*empacotar* is built from *em-* and *pacote* 'packet', a small form of *paca* 'bundle' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
