## You'll want to know: причал
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C424-PAROM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**причал** (*prichál*) — "a mooring, a berth".

> Лодки стоят у причала.
>
> *Lódki stoyát u prichála.*
>
> The boats are at the mooring.

Masculine. Built on *причалить* — to moor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*причал* is built on *причалить* 'to moor', from *при-* and *чалить* 'to tie up (a boat)'. EtymDB lists *чалить* without a source, so its origin is left open.
