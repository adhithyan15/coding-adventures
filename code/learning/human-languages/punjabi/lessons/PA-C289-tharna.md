# ਠਰਨਾ (ṭharnā) — to be chilled; to go numb with cold

## You'll want to know: ਠਰਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C289-COLD289-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠਰਨਾ** — *ṭharnā* — "to be chilled; to go numb with cold".

> ਪੋਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੱਥ ਠਰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *poh vich hatth ṭharde han.*
>
> In Poh your hands go numb with cold.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਠਰਿਆ, *maiṁ bahut ṭhariā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠਰਨਾ** (*ṭharnā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*stharati*, "is firm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭharnā*, "to be chilled or numb", under it (CDIAL 13742).
