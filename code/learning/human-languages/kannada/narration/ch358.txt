Kannada, chapter 358: The Case Goes to Court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಿಚಾರಣೆ (vicāraṇe) — a hearing; an inquiry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to reveal, then the Kannada for a plot.

You'll want to know: ವಿಚಾರಣೆ.
ವಿಚಾರಣೆ — vicāraṇe — "a hearing; an inquiry".
ಪ್ರಕರಣದ ವಿಚಾರಣೆ ಮುಂದಿನ ವಾರ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತದೆ. prakaraṇada vicāraṇe mundina vāra naḍeyuttade. The hearing of the case takes place next week.
In court, a hearing; at an office, an inquiry.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಚಾರಣೆ (vicāraṇe) is borrowed from Sanskrit vicāraṇā, "deliberation, investigation" (Monier-Williams), from vicāra, "consideration".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ (mokaddame) — a lawsuit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a plot, then the Kannada for a hearing.

You'll want to know: ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ.
ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ — mokaddame — "a lawsuit".
ನೆರೆಯವರ ನಡುವೆ ಜಮೀನಿನ ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ ಹತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ನಡೆಯಿತು. nereyavara naḍuve jamīnina mokaddame hattu varṣa naḍeyitu. A land lawsuit between the neighbours went on for ten years.
Plural: ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆಗಳು, mokaddamegaḷu.
A lawsuit is brought against someone: ಅವನ ಮೇಲೆ ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ (mokaddame).

The word, taken apart.
ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ (mokaddame) is a loan from Arabic muqaddama, "a beginning, a case at law", which came through Persian and Hindustani.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪ್ರಕರಣ (prakaraṇa) — a case; an affair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a hearing, then the Kannada for a lawsuit.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಕರಣ.
ಪ್ರಕರಣ — prakaraṇa — "a case; an affair".
ಆ ದರೋಡೆಯ ಪ್ರಕರಣ ಇನ್ನೂ ಮುಗಿದಿಲ್ಲ. ā darōḍeya prakaraṇa innū mugidilla. That robbery case is not over yet.
Plural: ಪ್ರಕರಣಗಳು, prakaraṇagaḷu.
A matter for the police or a court. A book chapter is also a ಪ್ರಕರಣ (prakaraṇa).

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಕರಣ (prakaraṇa) is borrowed from Sanskrit prakaraṇa, "treatment, a treatise, a subject" (CDIAL 8432).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಜಾಮೀನು (jāmīnu) — bail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lawsuit, then the Kannada for a case.

You'll want to know: ಜಾಮೀನು.
ಜಾಮೀನು — jāmīnu — "bail".
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ ಆರೋಪಿಗೆ ಜಾಮೀನು ಕೊಟ್ಟಿತು. nyāyālaya ārōpige jāmīnu koṭṭitu. The court granted bail to the accused.
Bail is "given": ಜಾಮೀನು (jāmīnu) ಕೊಡು. One out on bail is ಜಾಮೀನಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಹೊರಗೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಾಮೀನು (jāmīnu) is a loan from Persian zāmin, "a surety", a word of Arabic origin, which came through Hindustani.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತಪ್ಪೊಪ್ಪಿಕೊ (tappoppiko) — to confess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a case, then the Kannada for bail.

You'll want to know: ತಪ್ಪೊಪ್ಪಿಕೊ.
ತಪ್ಪೊಪ್ಪಿಕೊ — tappoppiko — "to confess".
ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಕಳ್ಳ ತಪ್ಪೊಪ್ಪಿಕೊಂಡನು. konege kaḷḷa tappoppikoṇḍanu. In the end the thief confessed.
Past (I …): ತಪ್ಪೊಪ್ಪಿಕೊಂಡೆ, tappoppikoṇḍe.
ತಪ್ಪು, a wrong, ಒಪ್ಪು, to accept, and -ಕೊ: to accept one's wrong.

The word, taken apart.
ತಪ್ಪೊಪ್ಪಿಕೊ (tappoppiko) is a native Dravidian compound: tappu, "a slip, an error" (DEDR 3071), oppu, "to agree, to assent" (DEDR 924), and -ko, from koḷ, "to take" (DEDR 2151).
