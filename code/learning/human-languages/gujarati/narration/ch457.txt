Gujarati, chapter 457: Envy and Hatred.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નફરત (naphrat) — hatred.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for disregard, then the Gujarati for a knock.

You'll want to know: નફરત.
નફરત — naphrat — "hatred".
મને ખોટા વચનથી નફરત છે. mane khoṭā vacanthī naphrat che. I hate empty promises.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નફરત (naphrat) is usually traced to Arabic nafra, "aversion, running away", which reached Gujarati through Persian nafrat.

બધા સ્વાર્થી માણસને ધિક્કારે છે. badhā svārthī māṇasne dhikkāre che. Everyone despises a selfish person.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સ્વાર્થી માણસને ધિક્કાર્યો, teṇe svārthī māṇasne dhikkāryo.

ધિક્કારવું (dhikkārvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit dhikkāra, "reproach", built on dhik, "fie!"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 6808).

Lesson 4 of 5.
અદેખાઈ (adekhāī) — envy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to despise, then the Gujarati for contempt.

You'll want to know: અદેખાઈ.
અદેખાઈ — adekhāī — "envy".
મિત્રની નવી ગાડી જોઈને તેને અદેખાઈ થઈ. mitranī navī gāḍī joīne tene adekhāī thaī. Seeing his friend's new car, he felt envious.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અદેખાઈ (adekhāī) is built from the negative a-, "not", the stem of dekhvũ, "to see", which CDIAL traces to dṛkṣati, "sees" (CDIAL 6507), and the noun ending -āī: probably the feeling of not bearing to see another's good.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for contempt, then the Gujarati for envy.

You'll want to know: અણગમો.
અણગમો — aṇgamo — "dislike".
મીરાને ભીડ માટે અણગમો છે. Mīrāne bhīḍ māṭe aṇgamo che. Mira dislikes crowds.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અણગમો (aṇgamo) is built from the negative aṇ-, "not", and the stem of ગમવું (gamvũ), "to like", with the noun ending -o: a not-liking. CDIAL lists the verb, "to approve", under Sanskrit gamyatē, "one goes, it is understood" (CDIAL 4033).
