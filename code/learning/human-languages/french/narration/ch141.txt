French, chapter 141: The Future, This Evening, a Plan, To Foresee, To Organise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'avenir — the future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for formerly, then the French for to happen.

You'll want to know: l'avenir.
l'avenir — "the future". French builds the future on the infinitive: parler becomes je parlerai — "I will speak" — then tu parleras, il parlera.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ce soir — this evening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to happen, then the French for the future.

You'll want to know: ce soir.
ce soir — "this evening". The time word can stand first: Ce soir, je travaillerai. — "This evening I will work." Dans a length of time gives "in": dans deux jours — "in two days".

prévoir — to foresee, to plan for.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the French for this evening, then the French for a plan.

You'll want to know: prévoir.
prévoir — "to foresee, to plan for". voir with pré-, "before": seeing a thing before it comes. The future of être is irregular: je serai, tu seras, il sera.
