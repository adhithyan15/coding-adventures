## You'll want to know: تغلب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C345-MUWAJAHA345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تغلب** — *taghallaba* — "to overcome".

> تغلبت البنت على خوفها من الماء.
>
> *taghallabat al-bint ʿalā khawfihā min al-māʾ.*
>
> The girl overcame her fear of water.

Present: يتغلب, *yataghallab*.

It takes على: تغلب على.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تغلب** (*taghallaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-l-b* (Lane), "to overpower", the root of the taught *ghāliban*, mostly. It is Form V, "to get the upper hand".
