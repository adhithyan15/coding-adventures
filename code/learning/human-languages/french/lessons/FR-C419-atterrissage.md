## You'll want to know: l'atterrissage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C419-DECOLLAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'atterrissage** — "the landing (of a plane)".

> L'atterrissage a été un peu brusque.
>
> *The landing was a bit rough.*

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atterrissage* is built on *atterrir* 'to land' with *-age*; *atterrir* comes from *terre* 'land', from Latin *terra* (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list the noun.
