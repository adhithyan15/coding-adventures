## You'll want to know: عواقب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C370-PAYAMAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عواقب** (*'avâqeb*) — "repercussions, bad consequences".

> او به عواقب کارش فکر نکرد.
>
> *u be 'avâqeb-e kârash fekr nakard.*
>
> He did not think about the consequences of his action.

An Arabic broken plural of *'âqebat* "end, outcome". In nearly every case the consequences are bad ones.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عواقب** is usually traced to Arabic *'awāqib*, a broken plural of *'āqiba* "end, outcome": what follows at the end.
