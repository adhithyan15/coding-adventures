## You'll want to know: डाकू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C292-DAKAITI292-01]; assesses=[] -->

**डाकू** — *ḍākū* — "a bandit".

> रात को दरवाज़ा बंद रखो, नहीं तो डाकू आ जाएँगे।
>
> *rāt ko darvāzā band rakho, nahīṃ to ḍākū ā jāeṅge.*
>
> Keep the door shut at night, or the bandits will get in.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

comes from *\*ḍākka*, "robbery", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5543). A **डाकू** belongs to a gang that robs on the roads and in villages, not a single **चोर** who steals in secret.
