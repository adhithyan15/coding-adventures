## You'll want to know: consolar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C716-LASTIMA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**consolar** — "to console, to comfort".

> Consolé a mi amiga después del examen.
>
> *I comforted my friend after the exam.*

Stem change: *consuelo*, *consuelas*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consolar* is inherited from Latin *cōnsōlārī* "to console, to offer comfort" (EtymDB).
