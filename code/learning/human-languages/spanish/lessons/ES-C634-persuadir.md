## You'll want to know: persuadir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C634-PACTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**persuadir** — "to persuade".

> Me persuadió para firmar.
>
> *She persuaded me to sign.*

*Persuadir a alguien para* or *de* + infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*persuadir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *persuadere* 'to convince', built from *per* and *suadere* 'to advise' (EtymDB).
