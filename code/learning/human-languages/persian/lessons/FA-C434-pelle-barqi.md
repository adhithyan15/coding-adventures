## You'll want to know: پله برقی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C434-PELLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پله برقی** (*pelle-barqi*) — "an escalator".

> در ایستگاه مترو پله برقی خراب بود.
>
> *Dar istgâh-e metro pelle-barqi kharâb bud.*
>
> The escalator at the metro station was out of order.

Literally 'electric stairs': *pelle* 'step' + *barqi* 'electric'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pelle-barqi* is built from *pelle* 'step, stair', whose origin is not settled, and *barqi* 'electric', from *barq* 'electricity', borrowed from Arabic *barq* 'lightning, flashing' (EtymDB).
