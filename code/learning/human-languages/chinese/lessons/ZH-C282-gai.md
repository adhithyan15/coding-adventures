## You'll want to know: 该
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C282-GAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**该** — *gāi* — "it's time to; should (by now)".

> **我该回家了** *wǒ gāi huí jiā le* — It's time I went home.

> **该你了** *gāi nǐ le* — Your turn.

The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**该** (*gāi*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *koj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'complete' and 'all'. 'Should' and 'it's time to' are not given there, so how the word came by them is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-CHANGED-STATE-01, ZH-LEX-C55-LIKE55-01] -->

With 了 at the end, 该 says the moment has come: 我该回家了, 该你了.

It sits before the verb, after the person, where 要 and 想 sit.
