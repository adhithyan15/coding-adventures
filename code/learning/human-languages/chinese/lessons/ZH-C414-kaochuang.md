## You'll want to know: kàochuāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C414-CABIN414-02]; assesses=[] -->

*kàochuāng* — "next to the window".

> *wǒ xiǎng yào yí ge kàochuāng de zuòwèi* — I'd like a window seat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kàochuāng* is built from *kào* 'to be next to' and *chuāng* 'a window', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khawH* and *tsrhaewng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second, after an older dictionary, as an opening through a wall.
