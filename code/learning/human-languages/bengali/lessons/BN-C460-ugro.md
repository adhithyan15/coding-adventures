## You'll want to know: উগ্র
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C460-NISHTHUR460-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উগ্র** — *ugrô* — "aggressive; fierce".

> উগ্র লোকটা রাস্তায় ঝগড়া শুরু করল।
>
> *ugrô lokṭā rāstāy jhôgṛā shuru kôrlô.*
>
> The aggressive man started a quarrel in the street.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উগ্র** (*ugrô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ugra*, "powerful, violent, fierce" (Monier-Williams).
