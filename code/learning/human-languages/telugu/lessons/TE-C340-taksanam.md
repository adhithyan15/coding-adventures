# తక్షణం (takṣaṇaṁ) — instantly; at once

## You'll want to know: తక్షణం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C340-ANUKOKUNDA340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**తక్షణం** — *takṣaṇaṁ* — "instantly; at once".

> సాక్షి తక్షణం పోలీసులకు ఫోను చేశాడు.
>
> *sākṣi takṣaṇaṁ pōlīsulaku phōnu cēśāḍu.*
>
> The witness phoned the police at once.

Literally that very moment: తత్ and క్షణం. తక్షణమే is stronger still. Where ఈలోగా, meanwhile, leaves a little time, తక్షణం leaves none.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తక్షణం** (*takṣaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tatkṣaṇam*, "at that very moment", from *tad*, "that", and *kṣaṇa*, "a moment" (CDIAL 5640).
