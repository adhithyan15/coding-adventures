# Hear *nǚ'ér* — daughter

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-NU-01, ZH-LEX-ERZI-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **female**, then **son**.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-NUER-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *nǚ'ér* — **daughter**

Say two clear syllables: low *nǚ*, rising *ér*. The apostrophe only keeps the
vowel boundary visible in pinyin. It is not a pause and not part of the Chinese
writing.
