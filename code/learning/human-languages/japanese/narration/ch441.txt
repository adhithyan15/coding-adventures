Japanese, chapter 441: Proud, Cheeky or Lazy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
じまんする (jiman suru) — to boast; to be proud of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for neatly, then the Japanese for suitable.

You'll want to know: じまんする.
じまんする — jiman suru — "to boast; to be proud of".
ちちは むすこを じまんしています。 — chichi wa musuko o jiman shite imasu — "My father is proud of his son."
Polite じまんします, jimanshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): かれは くるまを じまんしました。, kare wa kuruma o jiman shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
じまんする (jiman suru) is Sino-Japanese (kango) jiman with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ji, "oneself" (Middle Chinese dzijH) and man, "ridicule" (maenH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
なまいき (namaiki) — cheeky; impertinent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for suitable, then the Japanese for to boast.

You'll want to know: なまいき.
なまいき — namaiki — "cheeky; impertinent".
おとうとは すこし なまいきです。 — otōto wa sukoshi namaiki desu — "My younger brother is a little cheeky."
A na-adjective: な before a noun.

The word, taken apart.
なまいき (namaiki) is a mixed word: native nama, "raw", joined to Sino-Japanese iki, "spirit", from i, "mind" (Middle Chinese 'iH), and ki, "spirit" (khj+jH), borrowed from Chinese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

まいにち べんきょうして、えらいですね。 — mainichi benkyō shite, erai desu ne — "You study every day: well done."
An i-adjective.

The word, taken apart.
えらい (erai) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

なまけもの — namakemono — "a lazy person".

The word, taken apart.
なまけもの (namakemono) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: namake, "idling (namakeru)" and mono, "person, thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
