## You'll want to know: सांधणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C280-MAIDAN280-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सांधणो** — *sā̃dhṇo* — "to take aim".

> छोरो निसाणो सांधै है।
>
> *chhoro nisāṇo sā̃dhai hai.*
>
> The boy takes aim at the target.

Past (I …): म्हैं निसाणो सांध्यो, *mhaĩ nisāṇo sā̃dhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सांधणो** (*sā̃dhṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *samdadhāti*, "places on, combines"; CDIAL lists an Old Marwari form of this verb glossed "aims", and Gujarati *sā̃dhvũ*, "to unite, attach" (CDIAL 12898).
