## You'll want to know: ਜੁਲਾਹਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C334-WOOL334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੁਲਾਹਾ** — *julāhā* — "a weaver".

> ਜੁਲਾਹਾ ਸਾਰਾ ਦਿਨ ਖੇਸ ਬੁਣਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *julāhā sārā din khes buṇdā hai.*
>
> The weaver weaves thick cotton sheets all day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੁਲਾਹਾ** (*julāhā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *jūlāh*, "a weaver".
