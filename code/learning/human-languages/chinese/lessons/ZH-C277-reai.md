## You'll want to know: rè'ài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C277-LIKES277-05]; assesses=[] -->

*rè'ài* — "to love deeply".

> *tā rè'ài yīnyuè* — He loves music.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rè'ài* is built from 热 'hot' and *ài* 'to love', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyet* and *'ojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to cherish'.
