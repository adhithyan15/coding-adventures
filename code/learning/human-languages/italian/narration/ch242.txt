Lesson 1 of 5.
rallentare — to slow down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to destroy, then the Italian for to exaggerate.

You'll want to know: rallentare.
rallentare — "to slow down".

The word, taken apart.
rallentare — built from lento, "slow", with ra-: to make slow. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for it; the parts are plain to see.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: riscuotere.
riscuotere — "to collect (money)".

The word, taken apart.
riscuotere is ri- plus scuotere, "to shake": to shake out (money owed). scuotere from Vulgar Latin excotere, from Latin excutere, "to shake off". (EtymDB 2.1).
