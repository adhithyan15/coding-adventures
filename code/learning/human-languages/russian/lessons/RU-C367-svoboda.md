## You'll want to know: свобода
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C367-RAVENSTVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**свобода** (*svobóda*) — "freedom".

> Для меня свобода важнее денег.
>
> *Dlya menyá svobóda vazhnéye déneg.*
>
> For me, freedom matters more than money.

Feminine. *Свобода слова* — freedom of speech. *На свободе* — at large.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**свобода** is inherited through Old East Slavic *svoboda* from Proto-Slavic *\*svoboda* "freedom" (EtymDB), usually traced to the root of *свой* "one’s own".
