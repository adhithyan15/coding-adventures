## You'll want to know: वय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C498-VAY498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वय** — *vay* — "age".

> तुझं वय किती आहे?
>
> *tujhaṁ vay kitī āhe?*
>
> How old are you?

Neuter.

Neuter: माझं वय दहा वर्षं आहे. वयस्कर, elderly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वय** (*vay*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vayas*, "energy, prime of life, age" (CDIAL 11305), without its final *-s*.
