## You'll want to know: фальшивить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C516-MELODY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**фальшивить** (*falshívit*) — "to sing out of tune".

> Он громко поёт, но немного фальшивит.
>
> *On grómko poyót, no nemnógo falshívit.*
>
> He sings loudly but a little out of tune.

Imperfective; present фальшивлю, фальшивишь (в becomes вл).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*фальшивить* is derived from *фальшь* 'falseness' (EtymDB), which was borrowed from German *falsch* 'false'.
