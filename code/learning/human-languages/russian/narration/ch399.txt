Russian, chapter 399: Online.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сеть (set) — a network; the internet; a net.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to insure, then the Russian for insurance.

You'll want to know: сеть.
сеть (set) — "a network; the internet; a net".
Я нашёл эту информацию в сети. Ya nashyól étu informátsiyu v setí. I found this information online.
Feminine. В сети — online. Социальная сеть (set) — a social network. First of all a fishing net.

The word, taken apart.
сеть (set) is inherited from Proto-Slavic sětĭ "a net, a snare" (EtymDB). The internet is a net in the old fisherman’s sense.

Masculine. На сайте — on the website.

сайт (sayt) was borrowed from English site (EtymDB), which is usually traced, through French, to Latin situs "a place, a position" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ссылка (ssýlka) — a link; a reference; exile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a network, then the Russian for a website.

You'll want to know: ссылка.
ссылка (ssýlka) — "a link; a reference; exile".
Я отправил тебе ссылку. Ya otprávil tebé ssýlku. I have sent you the link.
Feminine. Ссылка на + accusative — a link to, a reference to. Historically, exile: отправить в ссылку.

The word, taken apart.
ссылка (ssýlka) is built on ссылать "to send away" and ссылаться "to refer", from слать "to send", the root of посылка. A link sends you on to another page.

Lesson 5 of 5.
поисковик (poiskovík) — a search engine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a link, then the Russian for a web browser.

You'll want to know: поисковик.
поисковик (poiskovík) — "a search engine".
Я ищу ответ в поисковике. Ya ishchú otvét v poiskovíke. I am looking for the answer in a search engine.
Masculine, colloquial. Built on поиск, a search.

The word, taken apart.
поисковик (poiskovík) is built on поисковый "search", from поиск "a search", from искать "to look for", inherited from Proto-Slavic jĭskati "to look for" (EtymDB).
