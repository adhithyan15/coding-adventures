Russian, chapter 81: The Letter Yo.
3 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ё (yo) — the yo inside ребёнок.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Script you'll notice: ё and Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write я and ю once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say ребёнок (rebyónok), "a child". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ё.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
ё — yo.
It is inside ребёнок (rebyónok), "a child", and in мёд, чёрный and тёплый. It is е with two dots above it, and the syllable that carries it takes the stress. Many printed texts leave the dots off; this book keeps them, so you can see how the word is said.

Writing: ё.
Follow the numbered strip: start where movement 1 begins, and let each panel show you the next movement before your pen makes it. Then write ё yourself — slowly, and larger than it is printed.
The strip shows where to start this letter and which way to travel, in one attested order, and names its source beneath it. Teachers vary, so treat it as a sound way in, not the only one.

Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — look: at ребёнок and point at ё (yo) inside it]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: ё three times, saying yo as you finish each one]
[once you have stopped driving — write: ю, then ё (yo), from memory]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ребёнок aloud once more]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — ё? (yo, yo.) Which word did it come from? (ребёнок, rebyónok, "a child".)


Lesson 2 of 3.
(one letter) (odna bukva) — From the letter to the word — a first pass.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say ребёнок (rebyónok), then write the letter it gave you.

Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
[a table of 1 row, read aloud]
letter: ё. name: yo. the word it came from: ребёнок rebyónok.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[once you have stopped driving — write: each letter in the table from memory, then check it]
[your turn — say: the word each one came from]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: each word in the table aloud, and point at its letter]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word did each letter come from? (ё (yo) from ребёнок.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
(one letter) (odna bukva) — From the word back to the letter — a second pass.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Write ё (yo) without looking, then say the word it came from.

Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
[a table of 1 row, read aloud]
letter: ё. name: yo. the word it came from: ребёнок rebyónok.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: each word in the table, then write the letter it gave you without looking]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — write: each letter once more, slowly, larger than it is printed]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say each word in the table once more, and name the letter you took from it.
