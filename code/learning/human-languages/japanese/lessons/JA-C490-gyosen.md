## You'll want to know: ぎょせん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C490-SEA490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょせん** — *gyosen* — "a fishing boat".

> **みなとに ぎょせんが ならんでいます。** — *minato ni gyosen ga narandeimasu* — "Fishing boats are lined up in the harbour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょせん** (*gyosen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gyo*, "fishing" and *sen*, "ship" (Middle Chinese *zywen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
