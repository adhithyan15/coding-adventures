## You'll want to know: huāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C301-ESCAPE301-02]; assesses=[] -->

*huāng* — "flustered; in a panic".

> *bié huāng, wǒmen xiān bàojǐng* — Don't panic, let's call the police first.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huāng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *xwangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists it in a two-syllable word meaning 'dazed'. The level tone of huāng is irregular for *xwangX*.
