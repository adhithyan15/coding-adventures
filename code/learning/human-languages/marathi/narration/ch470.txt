Marathi, chapter 470: Trust and Trickery.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लबाड (labāḍ) — sly; dishonest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for fierce, then the Marathi for deceitful.

You'll want to know: लबाड.
लबाड — labāḍ — "sly; dishonest".
तो लबाड दुकानदार कमी वजन देतो. to labāḍ dukāndār kamī vajan deto. That sly shopkeeper gives short weight.
Unchanging; also a noun, a cheat. लबाडी, trickery.

The word, taken apart.
लबाड (labāḍ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi labāḍ, "false, untrue", under the reconstructed lappha, "defective" (CDIAL 10943).

ढोंगी — ḍhoṅgī — "hypocritical".
बाहेर गोड बोलणारा तो माणूस ढोंगी आहे. bāher goḍ bolṇārā to māṇūs ḍhoṅgī āhe. That man who talks sweetly in public is a hypocrite.

The word, taken apart.
ढोंगी (ḍhoṅgī) is a Marathi adjective built on ढोंग, "a sham", whose origin is not settled.

Unchanging; निरागसपणा, innocence.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ठकवणे (ṭhakavṇe) — to dupe; to swindle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for naive, then the Marathi for innocent.

You'll want to know: ठकवणे.
ठकवणे — ṭhakavṇe — "to dupe; to swindle".
एका माणसाने आजींना ठकवलं आणि पैसे नेले. ekā māṇsāne ājīnnā ṭhakavlaṁ āṇi paise nele. A man swindled Grandmother and took her money.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मला ठकवलं, tyāne malā ṭhakavlaṁ.
The one cheated takes -ला; ठक, a swindler.

The word, taken apart.
ठकवणे (ṭhakavṇe) is built on the verb that CDIAL lists as Marathi ṭhakṇẽ, "to be deceived", under the reconstructed ṭhagg (CDIAL 5489), with the causative ending -avṇe: to make someone be deceived.
