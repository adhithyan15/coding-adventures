# ですから / それで (desu kara / sore de) — so, and so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C255-DAKARA-01, JA-LEX-C112-THINGS112-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "so I won't go", to a friend. (*dakara, ikimasen*.) Then say "a holiday". (*yasumi*.)

## You'll want to know: ですから
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C255-SO-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C191-SHUT191-01, JA-LEX-C57-PLACE57-05, JA-LEX-C54-TIME54-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C253-ONGOING-01] -->

To a teacher, in a shop or at work, **だから** becomes **ですから** (*desu kara*), with **です** in place of **だ**:

> **あしたは やすみです。**
>
> *ashita wa yasumi desu.* — "Tomorrow is a holiday."

> **ですから、みせは しまっています。**
>
> *desu kara, mise wa shimatte imasu.* — "So the shop is closed."

## You'll want to know: それで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C255-SO-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C152-READY152-05, JA-LEX-C134-DENSHA, JA-LEX-C199-DEPART199-04] -->

**それで** (*sore de*) is "and so": it tells what came of something that happened. It looks back at an event, where **だから** and **ですから** also give advice and decisions.

> **でんしゃが とまりました。**
>
> *densha ga tomarimashita.* — "The train stopped."

> **それで、おくれました。**
>
> *sore de, okuremashita.* — "And so I was late."
