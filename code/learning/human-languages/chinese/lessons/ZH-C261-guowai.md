## You'll want to know: 国外
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C261-EVER261-04]; assesses=[] -->

**国外** — *guówài* — "abroad; overseas".

> **你去过国外吗** *nǐ qù guo guówài ma* — Have you ever been abroad?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**国外** (*guówài*) is built from 国 'a country' and 外 'outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwok* and *ngwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'outside' and 'far'.
