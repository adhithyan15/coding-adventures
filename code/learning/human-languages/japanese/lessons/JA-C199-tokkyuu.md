## You'll want to know: とっきゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C199-DEPART199-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とっきゅう** — *tokkyū* — "a limited express".

> **とっきゅうの しゅっぱつは ごごです。** — *tokkyū no shuppatsu wa gogo desu* — "The limited express leaves in the afternoon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とっきゅう** (*tokkyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *toku*, "special" (Middle Chinese *dok*) and *kyū*, "urgent" (*kip*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The word is a shortening (JMdict), and the two join as *tok-kyū*.
