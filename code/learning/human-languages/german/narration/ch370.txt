German, chapter 370: Ob es klappt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
klappen — to work out (informal); to fold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the guarantee, then the German for to guarantee.

You'll want to know: klappen.
klappen — "to work out (informal); to fold".
Mit der neuen Wohnung hat es endlich geklappt. It finally worked out with the new flat.
Informal and very common: Das klappt nicht — that does not work. Literally to fold or flap: den Sitz nach vorne klappen.

The word, taken apart.
klappen imitates a sharp sound; EtymDB lists English clap and Dutch klappen as cousins. It is usually said to have come into the standard language from Low German. The sense "to work out" is usually explained as things snapping neatly into place.

Lesson 2 of 5.
misslingen — to go wrong, to fail (of things).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to guarantee, then the German for to work out.

You'll want to know: misslingen.
misslingen — "to go wrong, to fail (of things)".
Der erste Kuchen ist mir misslungen. My first cake went wrong.
Perfekt with sein: es ist misslungen. The thing is the subject and the person goes in the dative: Es misslingt mir.

The word, taken apart.
misslingen is built with miss- "wrongly" on the old verb behind gelingen "to succeed", Old High German gilingan, Proto-Germanic lingwaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
eintreten — to happen, to occur; to enter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to work out, then the German for to go wrong.

You'll want to know: eintreten.
eintreten — "to happen, to occur; to enter".
Der schlimmste Fall ist zum Glück nicht eingetreten. Luckily the worst case did not happen.
Separable, Perfekt with sein: es tritt ein, es ist eingetreten. Also to enter: in einen Verein eintreten — to join a club.

The word, taken apart.
eintreten is built from ein "in" and treten "to step", inherited from Old High German tretan and Proto-Germanic tredaną (EtymDB): an event steps in.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: zutreffen.
zutreffen — "to be true, to apply".
Das trifft nicht auf alle Schüler zu. That does not apply to all pupils.
Separable and strong: das trifft zu, das hat zugetroffen. Zutreffend — correct.

The word, taken apart.
zutreffen is built from zu and treffen "to hit", inherited from Old High German treffan and Proto-Germanic drepaną (EtymDB): a statement that hits the mark.

Lesson 5 of 5.

herausstellen — "to turn out (reflexive, sich herausstellen)".
Später hat sich herausgestellt, dass er recht hatte. Later it turned out that he was right.
Reflexive and separable: es stellt sich heraus, dass …, es hat sich herausgestellt.

The word, taken apart.
herausstellen is built from heraus "out" and stellen "to put", Old High German stellen, Proto-Germanic stallijaną (EtymDB): the truth is put out where all can see it.
