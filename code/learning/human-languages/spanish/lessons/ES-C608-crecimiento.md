## You'll want to know: el crecimiento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C608-AUMENTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el crecimiento** — "the growth".

> El crecimiento de la ciudad es rápido.
>
> *The growth of the city is fast.*

Built on *crecer* — to grow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crecimiento* is built on *crecer*, inherited from Latin *crescere* 'to grow' (Lewis & Short).
