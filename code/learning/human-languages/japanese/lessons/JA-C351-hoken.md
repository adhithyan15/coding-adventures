## You'll want to know: ほけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C351-MANICHI351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ほけん** — *hoken* — "insurance".

> **ほけんが あったら、あんしんでしょう。** — *hoken ga attara, anshin deshō* — "If you have insurance, you can feel easy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほけん** (*hoken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ho*, "preserve" (Middle Chinese *pawX*) and *ken*, "steep place" (*xjemX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
