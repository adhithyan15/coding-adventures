## You'll want to know: обследование
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C489-CHECKUP-02]; assesses=[] -->

**обследование** (*obslédovaniye*) — "a medical examination, a check-up".

> Каждый год я прохожу обследование.
>
> *Kázhdyy god ya prokhozhú obslédovaniye.*
>
> Every year I have a check-up.

Neuter. *Проходить обследование* — to have a check-up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обследование* is built on *обследовать* 'to examine' with *-ание*; the root is that of *след* 'track, trail', which EtymDB lists without a source, and EtymDB has no entry for the noun.
