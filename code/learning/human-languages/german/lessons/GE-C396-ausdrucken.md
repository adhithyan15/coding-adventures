## You'll want to know: ausdrucken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C396-COMPUTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ausdrucken** — "to print out".

> Ich drucke die Fahrkarte zu Hause aus.
>
> *I print out the ticket at home.*

Separable: *ich drucke aus*, *ich habe ausgedruckt*. Careful: *ausdrücken*, with an umlaut, means to express.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ausdrucken* is built from *aus* "out" and *drucken* "to print", from Proto-Germanic *þrukjaną* "to press" (EtymDB): you press the text out onto paper.
