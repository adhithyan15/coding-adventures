## You'll want to know: a tripulação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C424-VOO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a tripulação** — "the crew".

> A tripulação deseja-nos uma boa viagem.
>
> *The crew wishes us a good trip.*

The verb *tripular* means to crew a ship or plane.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tripulação* is built on *tripular* 'to crew'. The origin of *tripular* is not settled; one view links it with Latin *interpolāre* 'to refurbish', and EtymDB has no entry for it.
