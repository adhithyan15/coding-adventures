## You'll want to know: ಒರಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C543-GRHA543-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒರಗು** — *oragu* — "to lean back; to recline".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಗೋಡೆಗೆ ಒರಗಿ ಕುಳಿತರು.
>
> *ajja gōḍege oragi kuḷitaru.*
>
> Grandfather sat leaning against the wall.

Past (I …): ಒರಗಿದೆ, *oragide*.

Against what: -ಗೆ. ಒರಗು ಕುರ್ಚಿ, an easy chair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒರಗು** (*oragu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 707 lists Kannada *oṟagu*, "to incline, recline, lean upon, lie down", and the noun *oṟagu*, "a cushion to lean the back against".
