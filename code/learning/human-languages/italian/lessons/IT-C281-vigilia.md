## You'll want to know: la vigilia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C281-VIGILIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la vigilia** — "the eve, the day before".

> La vigilia dell'esame non ho dormito.
>
> *On the eve of the exam I did not sleep.*

Above all the eve of a holiday or of a big day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vigilia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vigilia* "a staying awake, a night watch" (Lewis & Short), from *vigil* "awake": the night people stayed up before a feast.
