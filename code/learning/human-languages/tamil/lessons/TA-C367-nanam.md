## You'll want to know: ஞானம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C367-NANAM367-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ஞானம்** — *ñāṉam* — "wisdom".

> அனுபவத்தால் ஞானம் வருகிறது.
>
> *aṉupavattāl ñāṉam varukiṟatu.*
>
> Wisdom comes with experience.

A deeper word than அறிவு, knowledge; ஞானி, a sage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஞானம்** (*ñāṉam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jñāna*, "knowledge" (CDIAL 5281). Tamil writes the cluster jñ with one letter, ஞ, as Pali does with *ñāṇa*.
