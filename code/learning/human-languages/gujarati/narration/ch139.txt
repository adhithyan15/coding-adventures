Gujarati, chapter 139: To send, To forget, To look for, To use, To make.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મોકલવું (mokalvũ) — to send.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to open, then the Gujarati for to call.

You'll want to know: મોકલવું.
મોકલવું (mokalvũ) — "to send".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of મોકલવું (mokalvũ) is not settled; CDIAL has no entry for the verb. It may belong with mokḷũ, "loose, free", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed mukna, "loosed" (CDIAL 10157). Sindhi mokilaṇu, "to send", stands in the same group there.

The word, taken apart.
ભૂલવું (bhūlvũ) comes from bhull, "err, forget", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9538). CDIAL calls one proposed older source doubtful.

શોધવું (śodhvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, with the senses "to purify, search", under Sanskrit śoddhum, "to purify" (CDIAL 12626).

The word, taken apart.
વાપરવું (vāparvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vyāparati, "is engaged in", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12203).

Lesson 5 of 5.
બનાવવું (banāvvũ) — to make.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: બનાવવું.
બનાવવું (banāvvũ) — "to make".

The word, taken apart.
બનાવવું (banāvvũ) is the causing form of banvũ, "to be made", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit vanati, "desires, gains, makes ready" (CDIAL 11260). CDIAL marks the Gujarati verb as taken from Hindi.
