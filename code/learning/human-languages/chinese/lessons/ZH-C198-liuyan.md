## You'll want to know: liúyán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C198-PHONE198-03]; assesses=[] -->

*liúyán* — "to leave a message".

> *tā bú zài nǐ kěyǐ liúyán* — He isn't in, but you can leave a message.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liúyán* is built from *liú* 'to leave behind' and *yán* 'words', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljuw* and *ngjon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stay' and 'to stop', and the second 'speech'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
