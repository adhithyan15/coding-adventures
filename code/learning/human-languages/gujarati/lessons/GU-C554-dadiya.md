# દાંડિયા (dā̃ḍiyā) — dandiya sticks

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C553-NAGARU553-05, GU-LEX-C554-RAAS554-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be itching to dance, then the Gujarati for raas.

## You'll want to know: દાંડિયા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C554-RAAS554-02]; assesses=[] -->

**દાંડિયા** — *dā̃ḍiyā* — "dandiya sticks".

> જો તારી પાસે દાંડિયા નહીં હોય તો હું તને મારા આપીશ.
>
> *jo tārī pāse dā̃ḍiyā nahī̃ hoy to hũ tane mārā āpīś.*
>
> If you don't have dandiya sticks, I'll give you mine.

A masculine noun, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાંડિયા** (*dā̃ḍiyā*) is built on *dā̃ḍo*, "a pole, a stick", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *daṇḍá*, "a stick" (CDIAL 6128); dancers strike them together in the raas.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
