## You'll want to know: ہڑبڑانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C289-BUJHI289-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ہڑبڑانا** — *haṛbaṛānā* — "to rush in a panic; to fumble".

> سائرن سن کر سب ہڑبڑا گئے۔
>
> *sāiran sun kar sab haṛbaṛā gae.*
>
> When they heard the siren, everyone rushed about in a panic.

Past (he …): وہ ہڑبڑا گیا, *voh haṛbaṛā gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہڑبڑانا** (*haṛbaṛānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *haṛbaṛānā*, "to hurry", beside *haṛbaṛī*, "confusion", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13949). The doubled sound is the scramble itself.
