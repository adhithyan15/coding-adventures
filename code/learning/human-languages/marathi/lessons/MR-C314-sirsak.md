## You'll want to know: शीर्षक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C314-VACHAK314-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शीर्षक** — *śīrṣak* — "a title; a heading".

> या कवितेचं शीर्षक फार सुंदर आहे.
>
> *yā kavitecaṁ śīrṣak phār sundar āhe.*
>
> The title of this poem is very beautiful.

Neuter.

शीर्षकाखाली, under the heading.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शीर्षक** (*śīrṣak*) is built on Sanskrit *śīrṣa*, 'the head' (Monier-Williams): what stands at the head of a page.
