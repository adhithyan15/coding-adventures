Arabic, chapter 388: Corruption and reform.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فساد (fasād) — corruption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a majority, then the Arabic for a minority.

You'll want to know: فساد.
فساد — fasād — "corruption".
تحدث الناس كثيرا عن الفساد. taḥaddatha an-nās kathīran ʿan al-fasād. People talked a lot about corruption.

The word, taken apart.
فساد (fasād) is built on the native Arabic root f-s-d, "to go bad, to be corrupt" (Lane). Fasād is going bad: decay, and so corruption.

Lesson 2 of 5.
رشوة (rashwa) — a bribe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a minority, then the Arabic for corruption.

You'll want to know: رشوة.
رشوة — rashwa — "a bribe".
رفض الموظف الرشوة. rafaḍa al-muwaẓẓaf ar-rashwa. The employee refused the bribe.

The word, taken apart.
رشوة (rashwa) is built on the native Arabic root r-sh-w, "a well rope" (Lane). It is usually linked with rishāʾ, a well rope: a bribe is the rope let down to draw up what one wants.

You'll want to know: إصلاح.
إصلاح — iṣlāḥ — "reform".
تحتاج الشركة إلى إصلاح كبير. taḥtāju ash-sharika ilā iṣlāḥ kabīr. The company needs major reform.

The word, taken apart.
إصلاح (iṣlāḥ) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-l-ḥ, "to be good, right" (Lane). Iṣlāḥ is the Form IV verbal noun of aṣlaḥa, to put right: repair, reform.

Lesson 4 of 5.
انتهك (intahaka) — to violate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bribe, then the Arabic for reform.

You'll want to know: انتهك.
انتهك — intahaka — "to violate".
انتهك الجيران حقنا في النوم. intahaka al-jīrān ḥaqqanā fī an-nawm. The neighbours violated our right to sleep.
Present: ينتهك, yantahik.

The word, taken apart.
انتهك (intahaka) is built on the native Arabic root n-h-k, "to wear out, to exhaust" (Lane). It is Form VIII; Lane gives it for violating what is held sacred: to violate.

Lesson 5 of 5.
استغل (istaghalla) — to exploit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for reform, then the Arabic for to violate.

You'll want to know: استغل.
استغل — istaghalla — "to exploit".
استغل صاحب المصنع العمال. istaghalla ṣāḥib al-maṣnaʿ al-ʿummāl. The factory owner exploited the workers.
Present: يستغل, yastaghill.
Also, neutrally, to make use of: استغل (istaghalla) الوقت, made good use of the time.

The word, taken apart.
استغل (istaghalla) is built on the native Arabic root gh-l-l, "to yield produce" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek the yield of a thing: to make use of it, or to exploit it.
