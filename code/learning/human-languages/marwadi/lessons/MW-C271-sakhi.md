## You'll want to know: साखी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C271-SAHI271-04]; assesses=[] -->

**साखी** — *sākhī* — "a witness".

> राम म्हारो साखी है।
>
> *rām mhāro sākhī hai.*
>
> Ram is my witness.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साखी** (*sākhī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sākṣin*, "a witness"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *sākhī*, "witness" (CDIAL 13321).
