## You'll want to know: веселиться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C317-IMENINNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**веселиться** (*veselítsya*) — "to have fun, to enjoy oneself".

> Дети веселились весь вечер.
>
> *Déti veselílis ves vécher.*
>
> The children had fun all evening.

Imperfective; perfective *повеселиться* (*poveselítsya*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*веселиться* is built on *весёлый* "merry", inherited from Proto-Slavic *veselŭ* (EtymDB), with the reflexive *-ся*.
