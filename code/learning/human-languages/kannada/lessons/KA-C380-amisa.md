## You'll want to know: ಆಮಿಷ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C380-MANAVOLISU380-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಮಿಷ** — *āmiṣa* — "an inducement; a lure".

> ಹಣದ ಆಮಿಷಕ್ಕೆ ಅವನು ಒಪ್ಪಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *haṇada āmiṣakke avanu oppalilla.*
>
> He did not give in to the lure of money.

ಆಮಿಷ ಒಡ್ಡು, to dangle an inducement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಮಿಷ** (*āmiṣa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āmiṣa*, "flesh, prey", and so "a bait" (CDIAL 1256).
