## You'll want to know: نادان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C462-SENSE462-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نادان** — *nādān* — "naive".

> وہ ابھی نادان ہے، دنیا کو نہیں جانتا۔
>
> *voh abhī nādān hai, dunyā ko nahīṅ jāntā.*
>
> He is still naive and does not know the world.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نادان** (*nādān*) is usually traced to Persian *nādān*, "unknowing": *nā-*, "not", and *dān*, "knowing".
