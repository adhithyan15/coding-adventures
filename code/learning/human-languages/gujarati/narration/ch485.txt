Gujarati, chapter 485: A Stay in Hospital.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઓપરેશન (opreśan) — an operation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cure, then the Gujarati for an injection.

You'll want to know: ઓપરેશન.
ઓપરેશન — opreśan — "an operation".
દાદાનું ઓપરેશન સારી રીતે થયું. dādānũ opreśan sārī rīte thayũ. Grandfather's operation went well.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઓપરેશન (opreśan) is borrowed from English operation.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ટપકાવવું (ṭapkāvvũ) — to put in drops.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an operation, then the Gujarati for admitted.

You'll want to know: ટપકાવવું.
ટપકાવવું — ṭapkāvvũ — "to put in drops".
નર્સ દિવસમાં ત્રણ વખત આંખમાં દવા ટપકાવે છે. nars divasmā̃ traṇ vakhat ā̃khmā̃ davā ṭapkāve che. Three times a day the nurse puts drops in my eye.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આંખમાં દવા ટપકાવી, teṇe ā̃khmā̃ davā ṭapkāvī.

The word, taken apart.
ટપકાવવું (ṭapkāvvũ) is the causative of ટપકવું (ṭapakvũ), "to drip", built on a reconstructed ṭapp-, "drip, drop", an imitative word (CDIAL 5444).

માત્રા — mātrā — "a dose".
દવાની માત્રા દાક્તરને પૂછીને લો. davānī mātrā dāktarne pūchīne lo. Ask the doctor about the dose before you take the medicine.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
માત્રા (mātrā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit mātrā, "measure, quantity, size", as Monier-Williams records it.
