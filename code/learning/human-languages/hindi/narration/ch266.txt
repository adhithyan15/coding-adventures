Lesson 1 of 5.
चिटकनी (ciṭkanī) — a latch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a cotton rug, then the Hindi for a carpet.

You'll want to know: चिटकनी.
चिटकनी — ciṭkanī — "a latch".

The word, taken apart.
चिटकनी (ciṭkanī) is inherited: CDIAL lists it, as chiṭkanī, "bolt", under a reconstructed chaṭa, "stick, cane", in the variant chiṭṭa (CDIAL 4966). A bolt is a small stick across the door.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कुदाल (kudāl) — a pickaxe, a hoe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a latch, then the Hindi for a saw.

You'll want to know: कुदाल.
कुदाल — kudāl — "a pickaxe, a hoe".

The word, taken apart.
कुदाल (kudāl) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kuddāla, "a kind of spade or mattock" (CDIAL 3286). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word, probably, from Dravidian.
