## You'll want to know: つきあたり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C195-CORNER195-02]; assesses=[] -->

**つきあたり** — *tsukiatari* — "end of the street; dead end".

> **この みちの つきあたりに ゆうびんきょくが あります。** — *kono michi no tsukiatari ni yūbinkyoku ga arimasu* — "The post office is at the end of this road."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つきあたり** (*tsukiatari*) is native Japanese, built from *tsuki*, "thrusting", and *atari*, "hitting", from *ataru*, "to hit": the place where a street runs into something (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
