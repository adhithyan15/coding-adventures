## You'll want to know: ریگمال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C496-VARKSHAP496-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ریگمال** — *regmāl* — "sandpaper".

> بڑھئی نے ریگمال سے میز چکنی کی۔
>
> *baṛhaī ne regmāl se mez chiknī kī.*
>
> The carpenter smoothed the table with sandpaper.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ریگمال** (*regmāl*) is usually traced to Persian *rēgmāl*, built from *rēg*, "sand", and *māl-*, "to rub".
