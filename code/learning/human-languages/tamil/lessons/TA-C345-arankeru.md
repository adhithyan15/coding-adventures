## You'll want to know: அரங்கேறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C345-KATHAICOLLI345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அரங்கேறு** — *araṅkēṟu* — "to be staged".

> அவளுடைய நடனம் நேற்று அரங்கேறியது.
>
> *avaḷuṭaiya naṭaṉam nēṟṟu araṅkēṟiyatu.*
>
> Her dance was staged for the first time yesterday.

Past (it …): அரங்கேறியது, *araṅkēṟiyatu*.

Also of a dancer's first public show.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அரங்கேறு** (*araṅkēṟu*) joins அரங்கு, "a stage, a hall", which DEDR lists among its loans (DEDR App. 8), with native ஏறு, "to climb, go up" (DEDR 916).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
