# (reading) — the first passage

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SKILL-READ-WORDS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Six characters, then six words. Now ten lines.

[PAUSE 1s] Thirty-four characters, and not one of them is new.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-ZHONGGUOREN-01, ZH-LEX-SUI-01] -->

> 我是中国人
> 我有一个名字
> 我看书
> 我喜欢看书
> 我十八岁
> 你呢
> 对不对
> 你好
> 谢谢
> 再见

[PAUSE 3s] Read it through once without stopping. Do not translate as you go.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and this time notice that the passage turns at line six. The
first five are about the writer. The last five are addressed to you.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-NE-01, ZH-LEX-DUI-01, ZH-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **你呢** is two characters and does the work of a whole English
sentence: *and what about you?* It only means that because of the five lines
before it, which is the first time this book has asked a line to depend on
what came earlier.

[PAUSE 2s] **我十八岁** has no verb in it at all. Mandarin states an age by
putting the number and 岁 straight after the person, and a reader who waits for
a verb waits forever.

[PAUSE 2s] This is the first whole passage the book has asked you to hold at
once, and it works because every character was paid for.
