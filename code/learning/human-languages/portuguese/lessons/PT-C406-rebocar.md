## You'll want to know: rebocar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C406-AVARIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rebocar** — "to tow".

> Um camião rebocou o carro até à cidade.
>
> *A truck towed the car to the town.*

*Rebocar* also means to plaster a wall, a different word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rebocar* 'to tow' is inherited from Vulgar Latin *remulcāre*, built on Latin *remulcum* 'towrope' (EtymDB).
