## You'll want to know: ぐあい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C216-UNWELL216-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぐあい** — *guai* — "condition; how one feels".

> **ぐあいは いいですか。** — *guai wa ii desu ka* — "Are you feeling well?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぐあい** (*guai*) is built from Sino-Japanese *gu*, "tool, means" (Middle Chinese *gjuH*), borrowed from Chinese, and native *ai*, "fitting", from *au*, "to meet, fit" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Baxter's transcription).
