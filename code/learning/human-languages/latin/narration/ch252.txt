Lesson 1 of 5.
ampulla, ampullae — a small flask, a phial.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for a chain, then the Latin for a shield.

You'll want to know: ampulla, ampullae.
ampulla, ampullae — "a small flask, a phial".

The word, taken apart.
ampulla is usually taken as a diminutive of amphora, "a two-handled jar" (EtymDB 2.1): a little jar. English ampoule comes from it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
furnus, furnī — an oven.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for a small flask, then the Latin for a frying pan.

You'll want to know: furnus, furnī.
furnus, furnī — "an oven".

The word, taken apart.
furnus is inherited, usually traced to Proto-Indo-European gwhrnos, "warm, an oven", from gwher-, "warm, hot" (EtymDB 2.1); English warm is a cousin. fornāx, "a furnace", is built on it.
