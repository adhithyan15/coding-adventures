## You'll want to know: سپرده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C414-ARZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سپرده** (*seporde*) — "a deposit, savings".

> او پولش را در بانک سپرده گذاشت.
>
> *u pulash râ dar bânk seporde gozâsht.*
>
> He put his money in a savings deposit at the bank.

The past participle of *sepordan* "to entrust".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سپرده** is native Persian, the past participle of *sepordan* "to entrust, to deposit", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian.
