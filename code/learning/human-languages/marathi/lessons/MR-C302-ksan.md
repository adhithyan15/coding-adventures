## You'll want to know: क्षण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C302-KSHAN302-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षण** — *kṣaṇ* — "a moment; an instant".

> एका क्षणात सगळं बदललं.
>
> *ekā kṣaṇāt sagḷaṁ badallaṁ.*
>
> Everything changed in a moment.

Masculine.

क्षणभर, for a moment; त्या क्षणी, at that moment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षण** (*kṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kṣaṇa*, 'an instant, a moment' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
