Arabic, chapter 207: Only, Very, Even, If, As.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فقط (faqaṭ) — only.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for joy, then the Arabic for fear.

You'll want to know: فقط.
فقط — faqaṭ — "only".

What you've built.
