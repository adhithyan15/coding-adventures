## You'll want to know: chīsù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C451-SPECIAL451-01]; assesses=[] -->

*chīsù* — "to eat no meat; to be vegetarian".

> *wǒ chīsù bù chī ròu* — I'm vegetarian: I don't eat meat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chīsù* is built from 吃 'to eat' and *sù* 'plain'. For the first the dictionary has *khek*, glossed 'to eat', under another way of writing it (there 吃 itself is *kj+t*, 'to stammer'), though *khek* would regularly give qì; the second is inherited from Middle Chinese *suH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as plain white silk, and the sense 'meatless' is not given there.
