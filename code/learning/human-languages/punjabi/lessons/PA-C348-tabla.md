## You'll want to know: ਤਬਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C348-MUSIC348-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਬਲਾ** — *tablā* — "a tabla; a pair of hand drums".

> ਅਮਨ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਤਬਲਾ ਵਜਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *Aman har roz tablā vajāundā hai.*
>
> Aman plays the tabla every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਬਲਾ** (*tablā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ṭabl*, "a drum".
