## You'll want to know: законодательство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C391-ZAKONODATELSTVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**законодательство** (*zakonodátelstvo*) — "legislation, the laws (of a country)".

> В законодательстве есть новые правила.
>
> *V zakonodátelstve yest nóvyye právila.*
>
> There are new rules in the legislation.

Neuter. All the laws of a country taken together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**законодательство** is built from *закон* "a law" and *-датель*, from *дать* "to give", with the suffix *-ство*: the giving of laws.
