## You'll want to know: ميدالية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C547-BUTULA547-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ميدالية** — *mīdāliya* — "a medal".

> فاز أخي بميدالية ذهبية.
>
> *fāza akhī bi-mīdāliya dhahabiyya.*
>
> My brother won a gold medal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ميدالية** (*mīdāliya*) is a modern loan, usually traced to Italian *medaglia* or French *médaille*, which go back to Latin *metallum*, "metal".
