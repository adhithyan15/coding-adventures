Sanskrit, chapter 378: A Little.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लेशः (leśaḥ) — a tiny portion; a little bit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for completely, then the Sanskrit for exceeds.

You'll want to know: लेशः.
लेशः — leśaḥ — "a tiny portion; a little bit".
यदि दोषस्य लेशः अपि अस्ति, तर्हि पुनः चिन्तयतु। yadi doṣasya leśaḥ api asti, tarhi punaḥ cintayatu. If there is even a little of a mistake in it, think again.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
लेशः (leśaḥ) MW gives leśa "a small part or portion, a particle, a little bit" and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
मनाक् (manāk) — a little; in a small degree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for exceeds, then the Sanskrit for a tiny portion.

You'll want to know: मनाक्.
मनाक् — manāk — "a little; in a small degree".
यदि सः मनाक् अपि यतते, तर्हि सफलतां प्राप्नोति। yadi saḥ manāk api yatate, tarhi saphalatāṃ prāpnoti. If he tries even a little, he succeeds.

The word, taken apart.
मनाक् (manāk) MW says manāk probably comes from manā and the root √añc ("perceivably"), and gives "a little, slightly, in a small degree" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: अर्धम्.
अर्धम् — ardham — "a half".

A neuter noun.

अर्धम् (ardham) MW gives ardha "half, halved" and, as a noun, "the half"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
न्यूनः (nyūnaḥ) — less; falling short.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for in a small degree, then the Sanskrit for a half.

You'll want to know: न्यूनः.
न्यूनः — nyūnaḥ — "less; falling short".
यदि अन्नं न्यूनम्, तर्हि अहं न खादिष्यामि। yadi annaṃ nyūnam, tarhi ahaṃ na khādiṣyāmi. If there is too little food, I will not eat.

The word, taken apart.
न्यूनः (nyūnaḥ) is built from ni and ūna, "wanting, deficient"; MW gives ny-ūna "less, diminished, defective, deficient" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ह्रसति (hrasati) — dwindles; grows less.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a half, then the Sanskrit for falling short.

You'll want to know: ह्रसति.
ह्रसति — hrasati — "dwindles; grows less".
यदि वर्षा न भवति, तर्हि नद्याः जलं ह्रसति। yadi varṣā na bhavati, tarhi nadyāḥ jalaṃ hrasati. If it does not rain, the river's water dwindles.
Past (it …): जलं ह्रसितवत्, jalaṃ hrasitavat.

The word, taken apart.
ह्रसति (hrasati) comes from the root √hras, "to become short or small, to be diminished"; MW gives hrasita "shortened, curtailed, diminished" (MW).
