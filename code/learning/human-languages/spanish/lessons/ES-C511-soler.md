## You'll want to know: soler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C511-HACER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**soler** — "to usually do".

> Suelo cenar a las nueve.
>
> *I usually have dinner at nine.*

Always followed by an infinitive. The o breaks into ue: *suelo*.
