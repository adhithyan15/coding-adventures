## You'll want to know: 怎么回事
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C540-NOWONDER540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**怎么回事** — *zěnme huí shì* — "what is going on here; what's the matter".

> **这是怎么回事** *zhè shì zěnme huí shì* — What's going on here?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**怎么回事** (*zěnme huí shì*) is built from 怎么 'how', 回 'a time' and 事 'a matter'. 回 and 事 are inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwoj* and *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of 怎, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
