## You'll want to know: すっきり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C426-FEEL426-04]; assesses=[] -->

**すっきり** — *sukkiri* — "refreshed; clear and tidy".

> **よく ねたので、きぶんが すっきりしました。** — *yoku neta node, kibun ga sukkiri shimashita* — "I slept well, so my head feels clear."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すっきり** (*sukkiri*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
