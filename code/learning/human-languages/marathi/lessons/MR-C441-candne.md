## You'll want to know: चांदणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C441-KSHITIJ441-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चांदणे** — *cāndṇe* — "moonlight".

> अंगणात चांदणं पडलं होतं.
>
> *aṅgaṇāt cāndṇaṁ paḍlaṁ hotaṁ.*
>
> Moonlight lay in the courtyard.

Neuter.

Written चांदणे, said and often written चांदणं, like other neuters in -णे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चांदणे** (*cāndṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ċā̃dṇẽ*, "moonlight", under *\*cāndraṇa*, "moonlight", a derivative of Sanskrit *candra*, "the moon" (CDIAL 4745).
