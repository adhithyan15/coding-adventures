## You'll want to know: láibují
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-04]; assesses=[] -->

*láibují* — "to run out of time".

> *xiànzài qù láibují le* — It's too late to go now.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Its middle syllable loses its tone: láibují, with bu light between two full syllables.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*láibují* is built from 来 'to come', 不 'not' and jí 'to reach', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *loj*, *pjut* and *gip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): coming, but not reaching in time.
