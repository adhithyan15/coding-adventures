## You'll want to know: હમદર્દી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C489-KUSHAL489-05]; assesses=[] -->

**હમદર્દી** — *hamdardī* — "sympathy".

> પડોશીઓએ અમારા દુઃખમાં હમદર્દી બતાવી.
>
> *paḍośīoe amārā duḥkhmā̃ hamdardī batāvī.*
>
> The neighbours showed sympathy in our sorrow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હમદર્દી** (*hamdardī*) is usually traced to Persian *hamdardī*, "sharing pain", built from *ham*, "together", and *dard*, "pain".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
