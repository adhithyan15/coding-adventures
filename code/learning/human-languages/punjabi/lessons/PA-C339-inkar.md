## You'll want to know: ਇਨਕਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C339-REFUSE339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਨਕਾਰ** — *inkār* — "a refusal; saying no".

> ਮੈਂ ਇਨਕਾਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ inkār nahīṁ kardā hāṁ.*
>
> I am not refusing.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਨਕਾਰ** (*inkār*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *inkār*, "denial".
