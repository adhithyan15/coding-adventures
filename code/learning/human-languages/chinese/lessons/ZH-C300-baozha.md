## You'll want to know: bàozhà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C300-DEATH300-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bàozhà* — "to explode; to blow up".

> *zuótiān wǎnshang fāshēng le bàozhà* — There was an explosion last night.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàozhà* is built from *bào* 'to burst' and *zhà* 'to blow apart'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *paewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'fire cracking'; the Guangyun has no entry for the second, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
