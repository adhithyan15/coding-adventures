## You'll want to know: सही
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C112-RIGHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सही** (*sahī*) — "right, correct".

**सही जवाब** — "the right answer". Said alone, **सही!** agrees: "right!".
