## You'll want to know: અજમાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C253-HOBBY253-05]; assesses=[] -->

**અજમાવવું** — *ajmāvvũ* — "to try out, to test".

> **મેં નવો શોખ અજમાવ્યો.** — *mẽ navo śokh ajmāvyo* — "I tried out a new hobby."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અજમાવવું** (*ajmāvvũ*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *āzmā-*, the stem of *āzmūdan*, "to test, to try", made into a Gujarati verb with *-āv-*. CDIAL has no entry for it.
