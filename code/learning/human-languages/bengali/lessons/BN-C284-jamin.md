## You'll want to know: জামিন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C284-THANA284-05]; assesses=[] -->

**জামিন** — *jāmin* — "bail".

> দুই দিন পরে আদালত তাকে জামিন দিল।
>
> *dui din pôre ādālôt tāke jāmin dilô.*
>
> Two days later the court granted him bail.

জামিন দেওয়া, to grant bail; জামিন পাওয়া, to get bail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জামিন** (*jāmin*) is usually traced to Persian *zāmin*, 'a surety', a word Persian took from Arabic; it is the guarantee that lets a prisoner go home until the trial.
