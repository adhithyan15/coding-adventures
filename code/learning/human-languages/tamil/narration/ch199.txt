Tamil, chapter 199: To believe, To agree, To refuse, To discuss, To compare.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
நம்பு (nampu) — to believe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to quarrel, then the Tamil for to tease.

You'll want to know: நம்பு.
நம்பு — nampu — "to believe".

The word, taken apart.
நம்பு (nampu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3600 lists Malayalam nampuka, Kannada nambu and Telugu nammu.
