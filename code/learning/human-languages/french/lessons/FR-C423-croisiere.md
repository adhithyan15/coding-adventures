## You'll want to know: la croisière
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C423-CROISIERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la croisière** — "the cruise".

> Mes grands-parents ont fait une croisière sur le fleuve.
>
> *My grandparents went on a river cruise.*

*Une vitesse de croisière* — cruising speed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*croisière* is built on *croiser* 'to cross, to sail to and fro', from *croix* 'cross', which is inherited from Latin *crux* (Lewis & Short).
