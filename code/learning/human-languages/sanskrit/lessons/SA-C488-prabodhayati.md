## You'll want to know: प्रबोधयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C488-PRATIBODHA488-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबोधयति** — *prabodhayati* — "wakes someone up".

> माता प्रातः पुत्रं प्रबोधयति।
>
> *mātā prātaḥ putraṃ prabodhayati.*
>
> Mother wakes her son in the morning.

Past (she …): सा पुत्रं प्रबोधितवती, *sā putraṃ prabodhitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबोधयति** (*prabodhayati*) is built on *pra* and the causative of the root √budh, "to wake, to know", the root of **बुध्यते**: to make someone wake; MW gives "to wake up, awaken (trans.)" (MW).
