## You'll want to know: ਉਡਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C353-PLAY353-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਡਾਉਣਾ** — *uḍāuṇā* — "to make something fly; to fly a kite".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਾਦਾ ਛੱਤ ਉੱਤੇ ਕਬੂਤਰ ਉਡਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā dādā chhatt utte kabūtar uḍāundā hai.*
>
> My grandfather flies pigeons from the roof.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਬੂਤਰ ਉਡਾਏ, *maiṁ kabūtar uḍāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਡਾਉਣਾ** (*uḍāuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *uḍḍīyate*, "flies up"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *uḍāuṇā*, "to cause to fly", under it (CDIAL 1697).
