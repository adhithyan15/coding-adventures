## You'll want to know: داستان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C324-DASTAN324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**داستان** — *dāstān* — "an epic tale; a long story".

> دادا نے ایک لمبی داستان سنائی۔
>
> *dādā ne ek lambī dāstān sunāī.*
>
> Grandfather told a long tale.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *dāstān*, "a tale". Urdu's old **داستان** went on for nights, with kings, giants and fairies.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
