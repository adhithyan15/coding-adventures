## You'll want to know: ফুরানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C393-PANGCHAR393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ফুরানো** — *phurāno* — "to run out; to be used up".

> পথে গাড়ির পেট্রোল ফুরিয়ে গেল।
>
> *pôthe gāṛir peṭrol phuriye gelô.*
>
> The car ran out of petrol on the way.

Past (he/she …): সেই পেট্রোল ফুরিয়ে গেল, *sei peṭrol phuriye gelô*.

The thing that runs out is the subject: টাকা ফুরিয়ে গেল, the money ran out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফুরানো** (*phurāno*) is an everyday Bengali verb whose origin is not settled; CDIAL gives no Bengali form that matches it.
