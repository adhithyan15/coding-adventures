## You'll want to know: बाळंतपण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C499-BAL499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बाळंतपण** — *bāḷantpaṇ* — "childbirth; confinement".

> मुलीचं बाळंतपण आईच्या घरी झालं.
>
> *mulīcaṁ bāḷantpaṇ āīcyā gharī jhālaṁ.*
>
> The daughter gave birth at her mother's house.

Neuter.

बाळंत, a woman who has recently given birth, + -पण.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाळंतपण** (*bāḷantpaṇ*) is a Marathi derivation: बाळंत, a woman who has recently given birth, with *-paṇ*; the origin of बाळंत is not settled.
