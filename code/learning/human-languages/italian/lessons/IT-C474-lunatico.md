## You'll want to know: lunatico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C474-UMORE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lunatico** — "moody".

> Mio fratello è lunatico: un giorno ride, un giorno no.
>
> *My brother is moody: one day he laughs, the next he does not.*

Not like English *lunatic*: it means moody, full of ups and downs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lunatico* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *lūnāticus* "moonstruck" (EtymDB), built on *lūna* "the moon": moods were thought to follow its phases.
