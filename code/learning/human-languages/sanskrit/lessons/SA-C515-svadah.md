## You'll want to know: स्वादः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C515-SVADA515-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वादः** — *svādaḥ* — "a flavour".

> एतस्य सूपस्य स्वादः उत्तमः अस्ति।
>
> *etasya sūpasya svādaḥ uttamaḥ asti.*
>
> This dal has a fine flavour.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वादः** (*svādaḥ*) comes from the root √svad, "to be sweet, to relish"; MW gives *svāda* as taste, flavour, savour (MW).
