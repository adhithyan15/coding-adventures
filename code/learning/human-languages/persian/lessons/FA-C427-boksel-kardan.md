## You'll want to know: بکسل کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C427-TAMIRGAH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بکسل کردن** (*boksel kardan*) — "to tow".

> برادرم ماشین خراب مرا با طناب بکسل کرد.
>
> *Barâdaram mâshin-e kharâb-e marâ bâ tanâb boksel kard.*
>
> My brother towed my broken-down car with a rope.

*boksel* (a tow rope) + *kardan*. Present stem *kon-*: *boksel mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*boksel kardan* is built from *boksel* 'a tow, a tow rope', which EtymDB takes from Russian *buksír* 'towing', and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
