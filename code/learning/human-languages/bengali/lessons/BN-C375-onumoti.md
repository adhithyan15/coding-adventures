## You'll want to know: অনুমতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C375-ONUMOTI375-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুমতি** — *ônumôti* — "permission".

> অনুমতি না পেলে আমরা ছবি তুলব না।
>
> *ônumôti nā pele āmrā chhôbi tulbô nā.*
>
> We won't take photos unless we get permission.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুমতি** (*ônumôti*) is Sanskrit *anumati*, assent or permission (Monier-Williams). It is asked for with চাওয়া and given with দেওয়া.
