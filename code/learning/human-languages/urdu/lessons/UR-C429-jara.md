## You'll want to know: جاڑا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C429-THAND429-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جاڑا** — *jāṛā* — "winter; the cold season".

> جاڑے میں پہاڑوں پر برف گرتی ہے۔
>
> *jāṛe meṅ pahāṛoṅ par barf girtī hai.*
>
> In the cold season snow falls on the mountains.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جاڑا** (*jāṛā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jāṛā*, "coldness, winter", under Sanskrit *jāḍya*, "chilliness" (CDIAL 5180).
