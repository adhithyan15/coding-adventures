Punjabi, chapter 177: A Good Boy and a Very Good Girl.
5 lessons. You can do the first 4 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
Two familiar nouns, now with their gender — boy is masculine; girl is feminine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
From the last chapter, say ਕਿਤਾਬ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ once. Now say the two familiar people words: ਮੁੰਡਾ (muṇḍā, boy), then ਕੁੜੀ (kuṛī, girl). Nothing about their meaning or spelling is new.

Grammar Lens: masculine and feminine nouns.
Punjabi nouns have grammatical gender. For this first bounded pair:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
noun: ਮੁੰਡਾ — muṇḍā. meaning: boy. gender: masculine.
noun: ਕੁੜੀ — kuṛī. meaning: girl. gender: feminine.
The endings help with this pair, but do not turn them into a universal guessing rule. Punjabi has masculine and feminine nouns whose endings do not advertise their gender. Learn the gender with the noun.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — hear: ਮੁੰਡਾ; YOU SAY: masculine]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — hear: ਕੁੜੀ; YOU SAY: feminine]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਮੁੰਡਾ, ਕੁੜੀ; YOU NAME: masculine, feminine]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which familiar noun is masculine? (ਮੁੰਡਾ.) Which is feminine? (ਕੁੜੀ.) Keep the claim to this pair for now.
Source: University of Waterloo, PUN 1100 Course Package (2018), pp. 54–55, Nouns and Gender.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda) — put the quality before the noun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
First retrieve the last chapter's location frame once: ਕਿਤਾਬ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ. Then retrieve ਚੰਗਾ (changā, good) and ਮੁੰਡਾ (muṇḍā, boy). Name ਮੁੰਡਾ as masculine. Keep the last two words separate for one breath.

Grammar Lens: one attributive position.
Join them in this order:
ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda) — changā muṇḍā — good boy.
In this noun phrase, ਚੰਗਾ describes ਮੁੰਡਾ and sits immediately before it: adjective first, noun second. This lesson owns that attributive pattern. It does not claim that an adjective can never appear after a noun in another kind of Punjabi sentence.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — build: ਚੰਗਾ + ਮੁੰਡਾ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — point: adjective ਚੰਗਾ first; noun ਮੁੰਡਾ second]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say good boy. (ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda).) Which word comes immediately before the noun? (ਚੰਗਾ, the adjective.)
Source: Columbia University Punjabi Language Resource Center, Simple Sentence Structure.


Lesson 3 of 5.
ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda), ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ — let the adjective agree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda). Then retrieve the familiar feminine noun ਕੁੜੀ. Keep the adjective before the noun in both phrases.

Grammar Lens: change the adjective ending.
ਚੰਗਾ is a variable adjective. In this singular direct contrast, its ending agrees with the noun's gender:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
noun gender: masculine. adjective + noun: ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda) — changā muṇḍā. meaning: good boy.
noun gender: feminine. adjective + noun: ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ — changī kuṛī. meaning: good girl.
The noun changes from ਮੁੰਡਾ to ਕੁੜੀ; the adjective changes from ਚੰਗਾ to ਚੰਗੀ. This is one bounded singular direct contrast, not the whole Punjabi adjective system. Plural and oblique forms wait.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — hear: ਮੁੰਡਾ; YOU BUILD: ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — hear: ਕੁੜੀ; YOU BUILD: ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — switch: ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda) becomes ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the masculine phrase, then the feminine phrase. Which piece changes to agree? (ਚੰਗਾ / ਚੰਗੀ, the adjective.)
Sources: University of Waterloo, PUN 1100 Course Package (2018), pp. 71–74, Adjectives; Columbia University Punjabi Language Resource Center, Simple Sentence Structure.


Lesson 4 of 5.
ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) — turn good into very good.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda), then ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ. Hear the one ending that changes and the adjective-before-noun order that stays.

You'll want to know: ਬਹੁਤ.
ਬਹੁਤ — bahut — very.
Put ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) before the adjective. It increases the degree; it does not take the adjective's agreement ending:
ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda) — bahut changā muṇḍā — very good boy ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ — bahut changī kuṛī — very good girl.
The order is degree word + agreeing adjective + noun. Only ਚੰਗਾ / ਚੰਗੀ changes with this noun pair; ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) stays ਬਹੁਤ (bahut).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda) becomes ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ (changa munda)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ becomes ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — check: ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) first; agreeing adjective second; noun last]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Make ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ stronger. (ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ.) Which word adds very? (ਬਹੁਤ (bahut).) Which word still agrees? (ਚੰਗੀ.)
Source: University of Waterloo, PUN 1100 Course Package (2018), pp. 72–74, Adjective position and degree examples.


Lesson 5 of 5.
One phrase before the verb — this is a very good girl - retrieve a multiword noun phrase before hai.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Build ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ one piece at a time. Bring back ਇਹ for this and ਹੈ for is. Keep ਹੈ waiting for the end.

Grammar Lens: the words work as one noun phrase.
ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ (eh bahut changi kuri hai)। eh bahut changī kuṛī hai. This is a very good girl.
Before ਹੈ, the words ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ travel together as a multiword noun phrase: degree word + agreeing adjective + noun. The noun is feminine, so the variable adjective is ਚੰਗੀ. The verb remains last.
This proves only this one bounded singular direct pattern. It does not add noun plural, genitive, an article, or a rule for every Punjabi adjective.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — hear: eh bahut changī kuṛī hai; YOU POINT: ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ (eh bahut changi kuri hai)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ (eh bahut changi kuri hai)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ (eh bahut changi kuri hai)]
[once you have stopped driving — write: ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ (eh bahut changi kuri hai) after covering the model]
[once you have stopped driving — check: ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) + ਚੰਗੀ + ਕੁੜੀ all come before ਹੈ]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Rebuild the sentence from this, very, good, girl, is. Name the multiword noun phrase before the verb. (ਬਹੁਤ (bahut) ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ.)
