## You'll want to know: تبلیغات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C383-TOWLID-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تبلیغات** (*tablighât*) — "advertising, advertisements".

> در تلویزیون تبلیغات زیادی هست.
>
> *dar televiziyon tablighât-e ziyâdi hast.*
>
> There is a lot of advertising on television.

The Arabic-style plural of *tabligh* "an advertisement". It can also mean propaganda.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تبلیغات** is usually traced to Arabic *tablīgh* "conveying, delivering a message", with the Arabic plural ending *-ât*.
