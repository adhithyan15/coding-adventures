## You'll want to know: पीढो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C186-DHAKKAN186-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पीढो** — *pīḍho* — "a low stool".

> रसोई मांय एक पीढो है।
>
> *rasoī mā̃y ek pīḍho hai.*
>
> There's a low stool in the kitchen.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पीढो** (*pīḍho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pīṭha*, "a stool, a bench"; CDIAL lists Hindi *pīṛhā*, "a large square stool" (CDIAL 8222). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
