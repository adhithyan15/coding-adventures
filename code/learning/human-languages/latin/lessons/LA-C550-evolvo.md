## You'll want to know: ēvolvō, ēvolvere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C550-LIBR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ēvolvō, ēvolvere** — "to unroll; to read (a book)".

> Discipulus librum ēvolvit et legit.
>
> *The pupil unrolls the book and reads.*

*ēvolvō, ēvolvere, ēvolvī, ēvolūtum*. Books were rolls, so to read was to unroll. English *evolve*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ēvolvō** is built on *ē-* "out" and *volvō* "to roll" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to unroll a book. English *evolve* comes from it.
