## You'll want to know: आश्चर्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C454-ACHAMBHA454-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्चर्य** — *āścarya* — "surprise; wonder".

> मुझे अरुण की बात पर बहुत आश्चर्य हुआ।
>
> *mujhe Aruṇ kī bāt par bahut āścarya huā.*
>
> I was very surprised at what Arun said.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्चर्य** (*āścarya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āścarya*, "appearing rarely, wonderful", and as a noun "a wonder" (Monier-Williams).
