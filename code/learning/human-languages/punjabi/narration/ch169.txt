Punjabi, chapter 169: Return to Two Fictional Dates.
2 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 2.
The old fictional date A — the already-taught date A, recalled after a long gap.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Stop before looking at or writing on this date card. This is a short return to a familiar fictional example, not a real personal date or a scored A1 mock.

Warm-up.
Look at the closed bank's A card: ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫ (fictional January date A). The three pieces are day ੧੫, January month position ੦੧, and year ੨੦੨੫. The slash marks keep this form's day/month/year order visible.

Guided Practice.
Keep A visible. Copy only its day and first slash, then its month and second slash, then its year. Check one box at a time against ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫ (fictional January date A). No new date or birthday is requested.

Wrap-up Recall.
Cover A for a moment. Point to its January box, then try the familiar A line once on paper. Uncover the card and repair only the first mismatched box. This is gentle retrieval, not independent scored writing.


Lesson 2 of 2.
The old fictional date B — the already-taught date B, recalled after a long gap.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Stop before reading or writing this date card. B is the second example in the same closed fictional bank. This is not a real personal form or a scored A1 writing task.

Warm-up.
Look at ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫ (fictional February date B). B has day ੨੫ and February month position ੦੨; the familiar year ੨੦੨੫ stays the same as A. The two slashes still divide day, month, and year.

Guided Practice.
With B visible, copy ੨੫, then ੦੨, then ੨੦੨੫, placing one slash between each pair of boxes. Compare your line with ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫ (fictional February date B). If a box differs, repair just that box before moving on.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hide B for a few seconds. From the old cue “fictional date B,” try its day and month boxes once, then uncover the card and check. The visible model remains available for repair; no independent mock score is assigned.
