## You'll want to know: fàngsōng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C184-HOLIDAY184-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngsōng* — "to relax; to loosen".

> *wǒ xiǎng fàngsōng bù xiǎng chūqù* — I want to relax; I don't feel like going out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngsōng* is built from *fàng* 'to let go' and *sōng* 'loose', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjangH* and *sowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as hair hanging loose.
