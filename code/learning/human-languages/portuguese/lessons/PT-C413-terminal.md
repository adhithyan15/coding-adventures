## You'll want to know: o terminal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C413-PARAGEM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o terminal** — "the terminus, the terminal".

> O autocarro acaba a viagem no terminal.
>
> *The bus ends its journey at the terminus.*

Also an airport terminal: *o terminal 2*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terminal* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *terminālis* 'of a boundary', from *terminus* 'boundary, end' (EtymDB).
