## You'll want to know: पृथक्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C544-PARASPARA544-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पृथक्** — *pṛthak* — "separately".

> यदि पुस्तकानि पृथक् सन्ति, तर्हि तानि पेटिकायां स्थापयतु।
>
> *yadi pustakāni pṛthak santi, tarhi tāni peṭikāyāṃ sthāpayatu.*
>
> If the books are lying about separately, put them in the box.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पृथक्** (*pṛthak*) is built on the root √pṛth (or *prath*), "to spread", and *añc*, "to turn", as MW gives it: spread apart; MW gives *pṛthak* as widely apart, separately, singly (MW).
