## You'll want to know: 学期
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C248-TERM248-01]; assesses=[] -->

**学期** — *xuéqī* — "school term; semester".

> **这个学期很长** *zhège xuéqī hěn cháng* — This term is a long one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**学期** (*xuéqī*) is built from 学 'to study' and 期 'a set time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haewk* and *gi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to meet' and 'a limit'. Middle Chinese *gi* would regularly give a rising tone, qí; the level tone of qī is irregular.
