## The B1 core and vocabulary, chapters 264–547 *(authored)*

Chapters 264–275 complete the past and the future (the perfective, تھا, the
agent نے with the verb following its object, گا / گی / گے for every person)
and realize the five B1 spine nodes, with seven copy-only letters (ؤ ع غ ذ ث ڈ
ص). Chapters 276–547 add the 1,360-word B1 vocabulary in five tranches, and
Urdu attains B1 on the level gate. Still ahead: ژ, the standalone ء, the vowel
marks, ۂ and ۓ, the subjunctive and the passive, and the four B2 spine nodes.
