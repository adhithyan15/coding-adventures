## You'll want to know: o inspetor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C294-INVESTIGAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o inspetor** — "the inspector".

> O inspetor fez muitas perguntas.
>
> *The inspector asked a lot of questions.*

A woman is *a inspetora*. Before the 1990 spelling reform, *inspector*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inspetor* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnspector* "one who looks into", from *īnspicere* "to look into" (Lewis & Short).
