# ઊછળવું (ūchaḷvũ) — to bounce, to leap up

## You'll want to know: ઊછળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C258-CRICKET258-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊછળવું** — *ūchaḷvũ* — "to bounce, to leap up".

> **દડો દીવાલથી ઊછળ્યો.** — *daḍo dīvālthī ūchaḷyo* — "The ball bounced off the wall."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊછળવું** (*ūchaḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*ut-śalati*, "springs up", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit *ucchalati*; CDIAL writes the Gujarati verb *uchaḷvũ*, "to leap" (CDIAL 1843).
