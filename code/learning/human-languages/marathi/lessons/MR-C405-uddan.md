## You'll want to know: उड्डाण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C405-VIMAN405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उड्डाण** — *uḍḍāṇ* — "a flight; a take-off".

> धुक्यामुळे सकाळचं उड्डाण उशिरा झालं.
>
> *dhukyāmuḷe sakāḷcaṁ uḍḍāṇ uśirā jhālaṁ.*
>
> Because of the fog the morning flight was late.

Neuter.

उड्डाण करणे, to take off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उड्डाण** (*uḍḍāṇ*) is built on Sanskrit *uḍḍayana*, "flying up, soaring" (Monier-Williams), reshaped in Marathi.
