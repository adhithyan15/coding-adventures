## You'll want to know: lǐfà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C212-SERVICE212-05]; assesses=[] -->

*lǐfà* — "to get a haircut".

> *wǒ yùyuēle míngtiān qù lǐfà* — I've booked to get my hair cut tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǐfà* is built from *lǐ* 'to put in order' and *fà* 'the hair of the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *liX* and *pjot* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to work jade'.
