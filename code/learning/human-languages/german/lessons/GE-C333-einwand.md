## You'll want to know: der Einwand
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C333-WIDERSPRUCH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Einwand** — "the objection".

> Mein Vater hatte einen Einwand: Die Reise war ihm zu teuer.
>
> *My father had an objection: the trip was too expensive for him.*

Plural: *die Einwände*. *Einen Einwand gegen etwas haben* — to have an objection to something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Einwand* is formed from *einwenden* "to object" (EtymDB), built on *wenden* "to turn", inherited from Old High German *wenten* and Proto-Germanic *wandijaną* (EtymDB): something you turn against an idea.
