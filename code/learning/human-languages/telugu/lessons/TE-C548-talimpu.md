# తాలింపు (tālimpu) — tempering; seasoning

## You'll want to know: తాలింపు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C548-VANTA548-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తాలింపు** — *tālimpu* — "tempering; seasoning".

> ఆవాలు, కరివేపాకుతో పప్పుకు తాలింపు పెట్టారు.
>
> *āvālu, karivēpākutō pappuku tālimpu peṭṭāru.*
>
> They tempered the dal with mustard seeds and curry leaves.

Spices fried in oil and poured over a dish: తాలింపు వేయు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తాలింపు** (*tālimpu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3186 lists Telugu *tālimpu*, "seasoning", and *tālincu*, "to season", with Tamil *tāḷi*, "to season with spices fried in ghee".
