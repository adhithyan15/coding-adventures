## You'll want to know: il drago
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C326-MAGIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il drago** — "the dragon".

> Il drago dorme nella grotta.
>
> *The dragon sleeps in the cave.*

Plural *i draghi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*drago* is inherited from Latin *dracō* "a serpent, a dragon" (EtymDB), from Greek *drákōn*.
