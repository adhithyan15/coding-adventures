Russian, chapter 518: Rehearsals and Tours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
исполнять (ispolnyát) — to perform; to carry out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to conduct, then the Russian for a composer.

You'll want to know: исполнять.
исполнять (ispolnyát) — "to perform; to carry out".
Певица исполняет старые народные песни. Pevítsa ispolnyáyet stáryye naródnyye pésni. The singer performs old folk songs.
Imperfective; perfective исполнить. Also исполнять (ispolnyát) мечту — to make a dream come true.

The word, taken apart.
исполнять (ispolnyát) is built on ис- and полный 'full', so to perform is to fulfil; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
репетиция (repetítsiya) — a rehearsal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to perform, then the Russian for a performer.

You'll want to know: репетиция.
репетиция (repetítsiya) — "a rehearsal".
Репетиция начинается в семь часов. Repetítsiya nachináyetsya v sem chasóv. The rehearsal starts at seven.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
репетиция (repetítsiya) was borrowed from Polish repetycja (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin repetītiō 'a repetition' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a performer, then the Russian for a rehearsal.

You'll want to know: репетировать.
репетировать (repetírovat) — "to rehearse".
Актёры репетируют новый спектакль. Aktyóry repetíruyut nóvyy spektákl. The actors are rehearsing a new play.
Imperfective; present репетирую. Perfective отрепетировать.

репетировать (repetírovat) is built on the stem of репетиция (repetítsiya) 'a rehearsal', borrowed from Polish repetycja (EtymDB); EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
гастроли (gastróli) — a tour (by performers).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a rehearsal, then the Russian for to rehearse.

You'll want to know: гастроли.
гастроли (gastróli) — "a tour (by performers)".
Летом театр едет на гастроли. Létom teátr yédet na gastróli. In the summer the theatre company goes on tour.
Plural. Ехать на гастроли (gastróli) — to go on tour.

The word, taken apart.
гастроли (gastróli) is the plural of гастроль, which was borrowed from German Gastrolle 'a guest role' (EtymDB).
