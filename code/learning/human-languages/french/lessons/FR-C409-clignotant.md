## You'll want to know: le clignotant
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C409-VIRAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le clignotant** — "the indicator, the turn signal".

> Il tourne sans mettre son clignotant.
>
> *He turns without using his indicator.*

Built on *clignoter* 'to blink on and off'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*clignotant* is built on *clignoter* 'to flash on and off', from *cligner* 'to blink'. The origin of *cligner* is not settled, and EtymDB does not list the word.
