# म्हारो, म्हारी — my, agreeing with what is mine

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-PHRASE-MY-NAME-IS-01, MW-SCRIPT-MHARO-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-THAT-01, MW-LEX-C49-FAMILY49-03, MW-LEX-C49-FAMILY49-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *my name is Ram*, then *this is my house*. Then say *a boy* and *a
girl*: **छोरो**, **छोरी**.

## Grammar Lens: the thing decides
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSS-AGREE-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-MHARO-01, MW-SCRIPT-GHAR-01, MW-LEX-C70-NATURE70-04] -->

**म्हारो** ends like **छोरो**, and it changes the way **छोरो** turns into
**छोरी**: it follows the thing that is yours.

> **यो म्हारो घर है।**
>
> *yo mhāro ghar hai.* — This is my house.

> **म्हारी गाय अठै है।**
>
> *mhārī gāy aṭhai hai.* — My cow is here.

**घर** is masculine, so **म्हारो**; **गाय** is feminine, so **म्हारी**. A woman
says **म्हारो घर** too: the ending follows the house, not the owner.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSS-AGREE-01, MW-SCRIPT-THARO-01, MW-SCRIPT-ROTI-01, MW-LEX-C155-FOOD155-01, MW-SCRIPT-BHAI-01, MW-LEX-C74-ADJ74-01] -->

**थारो**, *your*, does the same: **थारो दूध**, **थारी रोटी**.

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: **म्हारी रोटी गरम है।** — *mhārī roṭī garam hai.* — My roti is hot.]
- [YOU SAY: **थारो दूध अठै है।** — *thāro dūdh aṭhai hai.* — Your milk is here.]
- [YOU SAY: **म्हारो भाई बठै है।** — *mhāro bhāī baṭhai hai.* — My brother is there.]
