## You'll want to know: la crique
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C458-LITTORAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la crique** — "the cove, the creek".

> Nous avons trouvé une petite crique sans personne.
>
> *We found a little cove with nobody in it.*

A small, sheltered beach between rocks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crique* was borrowed from Old Norse *kriki* 'bend, nook' (EtymDB).
