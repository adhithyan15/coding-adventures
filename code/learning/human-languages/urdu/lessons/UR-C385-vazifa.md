## You'll want to know: وظیفہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C385-DAKHILA385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**وظیفہ** — *vazīfā* — "a scholarship; a stipend".

> اگر مجھے وظیفہ ملا تو میں یونیورسٹی جاؤں گا۔
>
> *agar mujhe vazīfā milā to maiṅ yūnīvarsiṭī jāūṅ gā.*
>
> If I get a scholarship, I will go to university.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وظیفہ** (*vazīfā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *waẓīfa*, "a duty; an allowance".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
