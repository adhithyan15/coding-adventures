## You'll want to know: a popa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C419-NAVIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a popa** — "the stern (of a ship)".

> As crianças ficaram na popa a ver as ondas.
>
> *The children stayed at the stern watching the waves.*

*De vento em popa* — going very well, with the wind behind you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*popa* is inherited from Latin *puppis* 'stern of a ship' (Lewis & Short).
