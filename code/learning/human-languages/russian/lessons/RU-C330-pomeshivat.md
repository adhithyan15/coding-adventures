## You'll want to know: помешивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C330-SMESHIVAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**помешивать** (*poméshivat*) — "to stir (now and then)".

> Суп нужно помешивать.
>
> *Sup núzhno poméshivat.*
>
> The soup needs stirring.

Imperfective only: to stir from time to time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*помешивать* is built on *по-* and *мешать* "to stir", a cousin of *месить* "to knead", which EtymDB traces to PIE *meyḱ-* "to mix".
