Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 184: Mortar and Metal.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ओखळी (okhḷī) — a mortar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for kohl, then the Marwadi for gram flour.

You'll want to know: ओखळी.
ओखळी — okhḷī — "a mortar".
ओखळी मांय मिरच है। okhḷī mā̃y mirach hai. There are chillies in the mortar.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ओखळी (okhḷī) is inherited from Sanskrit ulūkhala, "a wooden mortar"; CDIAL lists Hindi okhlī, "a small wooden mortar" (CDIAL 2360). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry. Its pestle is the मूसळ you know.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: चौको.
चौको — chauko — "the cooking place".
चौको साफ है। chauko sāph hai. The cooking place is clean.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चौको (chauko) is inherited from Sanskrit catuṣka, "a set of four", also "a square courtyard"; CDIAL lists Hindi caukā, a place for cooking and eating (CDIAL 4629). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
