# ஒருவழியாக (oruvaḻiyāka) — at long last

## You'll want to know: ஒருவழியாக
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C313-NERAM313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒருவழியாக** — *oruvaḻiyāka* — "at long last".

> ஒருவழியாக, பேருந்து வந்தது.
>
> *oruvaḻiyāka, pēruntu vantatu.*
>
> At long last, the bus came.

Said with relief after a wait or a struggle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒருவழியாக** (*oruvaḻiyāka*) is built from ஒரு, "one", and native வழி, "way, means" (DEDR 5297), with the adverb ending -ஆக: somehow, one way or another.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
