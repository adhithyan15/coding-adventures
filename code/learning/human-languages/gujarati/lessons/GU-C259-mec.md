## You'll want to know: મેચ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C259-MATCH259-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મેચ** — *mec* — "match".

> **ગઈકાલે અમારી શાળાની મેચ હતી.** — *gaīkāle amārī śāḷānī mec hatī* — "Yesterday our school had a match."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મેચ** (*mec*) is a loan from English *match*, a game between two sides.
