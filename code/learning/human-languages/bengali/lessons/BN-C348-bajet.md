## You'll want to know: বাজেট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C348-RAJOSHO348-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বাজেট** — *bājeṭ* — "a budget".

> এই বছরের বাজেটে স্বাস্থ্যের জন্য কত টাকা আছে?
>
> *ei bôchhôrer bājeṭe svāsthyer jônyô kôtô ṭākā āchhe?*
>
> How much money is there for health in this year's budget?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাজেট** (*bājeṭ*) is borrowed from English *budget*, with the English vowel heard as ā.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
