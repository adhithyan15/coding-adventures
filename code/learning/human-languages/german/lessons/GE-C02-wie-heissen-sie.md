# wie heißen Sie? — "what's your name?"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Assemble it: "how" + "are you called" = the polite question that
opens the introduction.

## You'll want to know: wie heißen Sie?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-WIE-HEISSEN-SIE-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WIE-02, GE-LEX-DU-SIE-02] -->

- **wie** (how) + **heißen Sie** ("are you [formal] called").

> **wie heißen Sie?** = literally "**how are you called?**" = the polite
> **"what's your name?"**

## Grammar Lens: verb in second place
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-GRAMMAR-VERB-SECOND-02]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-VERB-ENDINGS-01, GE-LEX-ICH-HEISSE-01] -->

German likes the verb in the **second** slot of a sentence:

> *Wie* (1) *heißen* (2) *Sie*?

The **informal** version swaps in *du* and its ending:

> *Wie **heißt du**?*

So the one verb *heißen* runs the whole exchange: *ich heiße…* to give your
name, *wie heißen Sie?* to ask for someone else's. (Reply to *wie heißen Sie?*
with *Ich heiße…*.)
