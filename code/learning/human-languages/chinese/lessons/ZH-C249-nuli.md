## You'll want to know: nǔlì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C249-STUDY249-05]; assesses=[] -->

*nǔlì* — "hard-working; trying hard".

> *tā xuéxí hěn nǔlì* — She works hard at her studies.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nǔlì* is built from *nǔ* and *lì* 'strength', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nuX* and *lik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with this very word and the second 'sinew'.

## What you've built

One more word for saying what I do.
