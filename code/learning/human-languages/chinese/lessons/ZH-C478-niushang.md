## You'll want to know: niǔshāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C478-HURT478-04]; assesses=[] -->

*niǔshāng* — "to sprain".

> *wǒ niǔshāng guo jiǎo* — I've sprained my ankle before.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niǔshāng* is built from *niǔ* 'to twist' and 伤 'to hurt', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nrjuwX* and *syang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first under two readings and glosses *nrjuwX* as a hand turning.
