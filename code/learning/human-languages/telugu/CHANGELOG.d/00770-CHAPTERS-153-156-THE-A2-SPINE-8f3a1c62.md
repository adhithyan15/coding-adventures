## Chapters 153-156: negation and questions, the past, the future and practical texts

Telugu had realized one of its five A2 spine nodes. Four chapters realize the
other four, so the A2 gate reports no missing spine node; vocabulary and verb
vocabulary remain.

- Chapter 153 (`SPINE-NEGATE-AND-ASK`): ఎవరూ, ఎప్పుడూ, ఎక్కడా, ఎవరైనా,
  ఏమైనా. The notes show the past negative with లేదు (రాలేదు), కాదు for "is
  not" (ఇది నా ఇల్లు కాదు), and the yes-or-no question in -ఆ (ఉన్నారా).
- Chapter 154 (`SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-PAST`): ఇటీవల, ఇప్పటికే, గత, ఒకసారి, గడుపు.
  The notes show the past for "I" in -ఆను (చదివాను).
- Chapter 155 (`SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE`): వచ్చే, ఇకపై, భవిష్యత్తు, ఆశించు,
  ఎదురుచూడు. The notes show the future for "I" in -తాను (వెళ్తాను).
- Chapter 156 (`SPINE-READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS`): కాలపట్టిక, సూచన, అద్దె,
  ప్రకటన, రసీదు, each with a notice to read and act on.
- The last words of chapters 153 and 156 read ఛాయ and ఝరి again, so the
  letter lessons for ఛ and ఝ are revisited in the R2 and R3 windows. Without
  that, the longer track left both under-reinforced and the A1 claim failed.
- Chapter 103's గ్రంథాలయం is romanized *granthālayaṁ*, not *grandhālayaṁ*.
- Every headword and example avoids the letters the course has not yet taught
  (ఈ ఊ ఓ ఔ). Extensions carry stage A2. Chapter 156 closes with two reviews. A
  native-level reviewer checked the notes, and its fixes are applied.
