# No model — a whole message to a named reader

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C97-MESSAGE-01, HI-CONCEPT-C97-ADDRESS-01] -->

Close the preceding lesson. Wait ten seconds. Name the three parts of a message
to yourself without looking at them.

## Writing: produce the message from the cue alone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C97-MESSAGE-01, HI-LEX-C97-PRIYA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C97-ADDRESS-01, HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-01, HI-CONCEPT-C04-KALMILTEHAIN-01, HI-SCRIPT-DANDA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

There is no Devanagari model and no romanized answer below. Keep only this cue:

> **Write to Arun. Greet him, then say you will meet tomorrow. Put your own
> practice name at the foot.**

Write all three parts from memory. Then compare in four separate passes:

1. three parts present, in order;
2. the comma after the opening and one **।** after each sentence;
3. spelling and vowel marks;
4. the name at the foot.

Choose the first differing pass, repair only that dimension, and stop.
