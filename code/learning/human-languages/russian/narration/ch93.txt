Russian, chapter 93: The Letters Shcha and E.
4 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 4.
э (e) — the e that opens этот.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: э and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach them.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write я and ё once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say этот (étot), "this". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: э.
э — e.
It opens этот (étot), "this", and эта. It looks like a с turned to face the other way, with a short bar across the middle. It is the plain e sound, with no y in front of it, which is why it is mostly found at the start of a word.

Writing: э.
Follow the numbered strip: start where movement 1 begins, and let each panel show you the next movement before your pen makes it. Then write э yourself — slowly, and larger than it is printed.
The strip shows where to start this letter and which way to travel, in one attested order, and names its source beneath it. Teachers vary, so treat it as a sound way in, not the only one.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — look: at этот and point at э inside it]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: э three times, saying e as you finish each one]
[once you have stopped driving — write: ё, then э, from memory]
[once you have stopped driving — read: этот aloud once more]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — э? (e, e.) Which word did it come from? (этот, étot, "this".)


Lesson 2 of 4.
щ (shcha) — the shch inside площадь.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: щ and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach them.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write ё and э once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say площадь (plóshchad), "a square". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: щ.
щ (shcha) — shch.
It is inside площадь (plóshchad), "a square". It is ш with a small tail at the bottom right, and it sounds like a long, soft sh.

Writing: щ.
Follow the numbered strip: start where movement 1 begins, and let each panel show you the next movement before your pen makes it. Then write щ (shcha) yourself — slowly, and larger than it is printed.
The strip shows where to start this letter and which way to travel, in one attested order, and names its source beneath it. Teachers vary, so treat it as a sound way in, not the only one.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — look: at площадь and point at щ (shcha) inside it]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: щ (shcha) three times, saying shch as you finish each one]
[once you have stopped driving — write: э, then щ (shcha), from memory]
[once you have stopped driving — read: площадь aloud once more]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 3 of 4.
(two letters) (dve bukvy) — From the letter to the word — a first pass.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say этот (étot), then write the letter it gave you.

Guided Practice.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
letter: э. name: e. the word it came from: этот étot.
letter: щ. name: shcha. the word it came from: площадь plóshchad.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[once you have stopped driving — write: each letter in the table from memory, then check it]
[your turn — say: the word each one came from]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: each word in the table aloud, and point at its letter]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word did each letter come from? (э from этот; щ (shcha) from площадь.)
