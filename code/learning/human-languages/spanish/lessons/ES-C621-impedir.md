## You'll want to know: impedir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C621-IMPIDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**impedir** — "to stop, to prevent".

> La lluvia nos impidió salir.
>
> *The rain stopped us going out.*

Like *pedir*: *impido, impidió*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impedir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *impedire* 'to hinder, to entangle the feet', built on *pes, pedis* 'foot' (EtymDB).
