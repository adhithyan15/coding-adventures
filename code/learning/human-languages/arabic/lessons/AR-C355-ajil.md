## You'll want to know: عاجل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C355-AJIL355-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عاجل** — *ʿājil* — "urgent; breaking news".

> قطع التلفاز البرنامج لخبر عاجل.
>
> *qaṭaʿa at-tilfāz al-barnāmaj li-khabar ʿājil.*
>
> The television interrupted the programme for breaking news.

خبر عاجل: breaking news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عاجل** (*ʿājil*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-j-l* (Lane), "to hasten", the root of the taught *mustaʿjil*, in a hurry. *ʿĀjil* is "what comes quickly".
