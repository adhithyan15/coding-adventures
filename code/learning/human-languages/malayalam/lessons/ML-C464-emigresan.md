# എമിഗ്രേഷൻ (emigrēṣan) — passport control

## You'll want to know: എമിഗ്രേഷൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C464-ATIRTTI464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**എമിഗ്രേഷൻ** — *emigrēṣan* — "passport control".

> എമിഗ്രേഷനിൽ അവർ എന്റെ പാസ്പോർട്ടും വിസയും പരിശോധിച്ചു.
>
> *emigrēṣanil avar enṟe pāspōrṭṭuṁ visayuṁ pariśōdhiccu.*
>
> At immigration they checked my passport and visa.

At an airport, Malayalam speakers usually call the passport desk എമിഗ്രേഷൻ, the English name of the check on people leaving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**എമിഗ്രേഷൻ** (*emigrēṣan*) was borrowed from English *emigration*, the passport check for people leaving a country.
