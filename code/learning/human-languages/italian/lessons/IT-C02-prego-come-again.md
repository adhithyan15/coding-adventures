# Prego? — "sorry, come again?"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-PRAGMATIC-PREGO-HERE-YOU-ARE-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You know **prego** after thanks, at a doorway, and with an offered
object. The last common use changes the voice, not the spelling.

## You'll want to know — One more job
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-PRAGMATIC-PREGO-COME-AGAIN-06]; assesses=[IT-SOUND-PREGO-02] -->

If you did not hear, lift your voice into a question:

> **Prego?** — "Sorry? Come again?"

The rising question sound separates this repair from a flat **prego** while
handing something over. Listen to the situation and the voice together.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-PRAGMATIC-PREGO-GO-AHEAD-04, IT-PRAGMATIC-PREGO-HERE-YOU-ARE-05, IT-PRAGMATIC-PREGO-COME-AGAIN-06] -->

- [YOU SAY: flat "Prego" — here you are]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-PRAGMATIC-PREGO-COME-AGAIN-06] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You did not catch a sentence. What do you ask? (**Prego?**) What
marks the listening-repair use? (**Question intonation and the situation.**)
