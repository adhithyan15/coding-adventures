## You'll want to know: погибать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C324-POBEDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**погибать** (*pogibát*) — "to die (in war or an accident), to perish".

> В той войне погибло много людей.
>
> *V toy voyné pogíblo mnógo lyudéy.*
>
> Many people died in that war.

Imperfective; perfective *погибнуть* (*pogíbnut*), past *погиб*. For a violent death, not an illness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*погибать* is built on *по-* and *гибнуть* "to perish", inherited from Proto-Slavic *gybnǫti* (EtymDB), the root of *гибель* "doom".
