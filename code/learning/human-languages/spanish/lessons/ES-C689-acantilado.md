## You'll want to know: el acantilado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C689-AGUA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el acantilado** — "the cliff".

> El faro está sobre un acantilado.
>
> *The lighthouse stands on a cliff.*

A cliff by the sea; inland, *la pared de roca*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acantilado* is built on *acantilar* (EtymDB), from *cantil* "a shelf of rock", which is built on *canto* "edge"; *canto* is usually traced to Latin *canthus* "an iron tyre".
