## You'll want to know: ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C347-GOSHTHI347-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ** — *suddigōṣṭhi* — "a press conference".

> ಪೊಲೀಸ್ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿ ಸಂಜೆ ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ ಕರೆದರು.
>
> *polīs adhikāri sañje suddigōṣṭhi karedaru.*
>
> The police officer called a press conference in the evening.

A press conference is "called": ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ ಕರೆ, or "held": ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ ನಡೆಸು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ** (*suddigōṣṭhi*) is a Kannada compound of ಸುದ್ದಿ, "news", which comes from Sanskrit *śuddhi* (CDIAL 12523), and ಗೋಷ್ಠಿ, borrowed from Sanskrit *goṣṭhī*, "an assembly, a meeting, a conversation" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
