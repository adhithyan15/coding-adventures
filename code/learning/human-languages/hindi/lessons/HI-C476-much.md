## You'll want to know: मूँछ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C476-CHEHRA476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मूँछ** — *mū̃ch* — "a moustache".

> नाना की मूँछें बहुत लंबी हैं।
>
> *nānā kī mū̃cheṁ bahut lambī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandfather has a very long moustache.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूँछ** (*mū̃ch*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *mūch*, "moustache", under *\*mucchā*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10180). Hindi often uses the plural, *mū̃cheṁ*, for one moustache.
