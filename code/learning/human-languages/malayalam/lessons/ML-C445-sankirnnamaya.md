## You'll want to know: സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C445-GAURAVAM445-05]; assesses=[] -->

**സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ** — *saṅkīrṇṇamāya* — "complex; complicated".

> ഇത് വളരെ സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ ഒരു പ്രശ്നമാണ്.
>
> *itŭ vaḷare saṅkīrṇṇamāya oru praśnamāṇŭ.*
>
> This is a very complex problem.

The opposite of ലളിതമായ, simple.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ** (*saṅkīrṇṇamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *saṁkīrṇa*, "poured together, mixed, confused" (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ആയ: a complex thing has many parts mixed.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
