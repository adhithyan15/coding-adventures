## You'll want to know: ಮಹತ್ವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C376-PURAVE376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಹತ್ವ** — *mahatva* — "importance".

> ಈ ಸಭೆಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಮಹತ್ವ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ī sabhege tumbā mahatva ide.*
>
> This meeting is very important.

Before a noun it takes -ದ: ಮಹತ್ವದ ವಿಷಯ, an important matter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಹತ್ವ** (*mahatva*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mahattva*, "greatness" (CDIAL 9933), from *mahat*, "great", with -*tva*, "-ness".
