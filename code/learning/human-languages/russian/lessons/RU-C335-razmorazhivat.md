## You'll want to know: размораживать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C335-OSTYVAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**размораживать** (*razmorázhivat*) — "to defrost, to thaw".

> Я разморозил курицу.
>
> *Ya razmorózil kúritsu.*
>
> I defrosted the chicken.

Imperfective; perfective *разморозить* (*razmorózit*), *разморожу*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*размораживать* is built on *раз-* "un-" and *мороз* "frost", inherited through Old East Slavic *morozŭ* (EtymDB).
