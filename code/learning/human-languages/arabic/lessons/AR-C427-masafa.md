## You'll want to know: مسافة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C427-TAKHTIT427-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مسافة** — *masāfa* — "a distance".

> المسافة من القرية إلى المدينة قصيرة.
>
> *al-masāfa min al-qarya ilā al-madīna qaṣīra.*
>
> The distance from the village to the city is short.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسافة** (*masāfa*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-w-f*, "to smell" (Lane). Lane records the old account: a desert guide smelled the ground to know the way, so the stretch he sniffed out was a *masāfa*.
