## You'll want to know: der Besuch

**der Besuch** — "a visit".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**Besuch** is the noun of *besuchen*, "to visit", which is *be-* + *suchen*, "to seek" (EtymDB 2.1): a seeking out.
