## You'll want to know: अधिकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C351-ADHIKAR351-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिकार** — *adhikār* — "a right".

> हर बच्चे को पढ़ने का अधिकार है।
>
> *har bacce ko paṛhne kā adhikār hai.*
>
> Every child has the right to study.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिकार** (*adhikār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *adhikāra*, "authority, jurisdiction" (Monier-Williams). Hindi has narrowed it to a right that someone holds.
