## You'll want to know: ひと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C309-OKURU309-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ひと** — *hito* — "a person; people".

> **となりの ひとが やさいを くれました。** — *tonari no hito ga yasai o kuremashita* — "The person next door gave us some vegetables."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひと** (*hito*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the *-bito* of *koibito*, "a sweetheart".
