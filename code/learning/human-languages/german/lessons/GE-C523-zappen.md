## You'll want to know: zappen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C523-FERNSEHEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**zappen** — "to flick through channels".

> Am Abend zappt er stundenlang durch die Programme.
>
> *In the evening he flicks through the channels for hours.*

Informal. Regular: *hat gezappt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zappen* was borrowed from English *to zap*; EtymDB has no entry for the German verb.
