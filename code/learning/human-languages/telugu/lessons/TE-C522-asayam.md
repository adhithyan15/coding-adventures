## You'll want to know: ఆశయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C522-VRTTI522-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆశయం** — *āśayaṁ* — "an ambition; an ideal".

> డాక్టరు కావడమే నా ఆశయం.
>
> *ḍākṭaru kāvaḍamē nā āśayaṁ.*
>
> Becoming a doctor is my ambition.

A life goal, nobler than a passing wish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆశయం** (*āśayaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āśaya* (CDIAL 1453), which first meant a resting place; in Telugu it names the aim a person lives for.

## What you've built
