## You'll want to know: परी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C338-RAKSHAS338-02]; assesses=[] -->

**परी** — *parī* — "a fairy".

> परीने मुलीला सोन्याचे बूट दिले.
>
> *parīne mulīlā sonyāce būṭ dile.*
>
> The fairy gave the girl golden shoes.

Feminine.

परीकथा, a fairy tale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परी** (*parī*) is a loan from Persian *parī*, 'a fairy, a winged being'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
