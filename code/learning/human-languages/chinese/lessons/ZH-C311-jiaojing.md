## You'll want to know: jiāojǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C311-POLICE311-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāojǐng* — "traffic police officer".

> *jiāojǐng shuō wǒ kāichē tài kuài* — The traffic officer said I was driving too fast.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāojǐng* is built from *jiāo* 'to cross' and 警 'to warn', a short form of a longer name for the police of the roads; both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaew* and *kjaengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to join'.
