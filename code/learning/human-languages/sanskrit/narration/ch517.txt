Lesson 1 of 6.
चतुरंगम् (caturaṅgam) — chess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a collection, then the Sanskrit for a liking.

You'll want to know: चतुरंगम्.
चतुरंगम् — caturaṅgam — "chess".
अरुणः मीरा च चतुरंगं क्रीडतः। aruṇaḥ mīrā ca caturaṅgaṃ krīḍataḥ. Arun and Mira are playing chess.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
चतुरंगम् (caturaṅgam) is built from catur, "four", and aṅga, "a limb": the four arms of an old army; MW gives caturaṅga also as a kind of chess played by four (MW).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for chess, then the Sanskrit for a riddle.

You'll want to know: क्रीडकः.
क्रीडकः — krīḍakaḥ — "a player".
सः उत्तमः क्रीडकः अस्ति। saḥ uttamaḥ krīḍakaḥ asti. He is a good player.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
क्रीडकः (krīḍakaḥ) comes from the root √krīḍ, "to play", which also gives krīḍati, with the agent ending -aka; MW gives krīḍaka as one who sports, a player (MW).

बालिका पुत्रिकया क्रीडति। bālikā putrikayā krīḍati. The girl plays with a doll.

The word, taken apart.
पुत्रिका (putrikā) is built on putra, "a son, a child", with the ending -ikā; MW gives putrikā as a daughter, and as a puppet, a doll (MW).
