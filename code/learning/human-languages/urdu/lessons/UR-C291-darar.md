## You'll want to know: دراڑ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C291-ZALZALA291-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دراڑ** — *darāṛ* — "a crack".

> دیوار میں دراڑ آ گئی۔
>
> *dīvār meṅ darāṛ ā gaī.*
>
> A crack appeared in the wall.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دراڑ** (*darāṛ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *darār*, "a crack, a rent", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6193); Urdu writes it with **ڑ**. A crack is said to come: **دراڑ آ گئی**.
