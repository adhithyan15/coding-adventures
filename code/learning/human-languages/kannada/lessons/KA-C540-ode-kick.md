## You'll want to know: ಒದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C540-KUSTI540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒದೆ** — *ode* — "to kick".

> ಹುಡುಗ ಚೆಂಡನ್ನು ಜೋರಾಗಿ ಒದ್ದನು.
>
> *huḍuga ceṇḍannu jōrāgi oddanu.*
>
> The boy kicked the ball hard.

Past (I …): ಒದ್ದೆ, *odde*.

The past ಒದ್ದೆ, I kicked, is spelled like ಒದ್ದೆ, wet; the sentence tells them apart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒದೆ** (*ode*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 616 lists Kannada *ode*, *odi*, "to kick, spur", with *odd-* in the past.
