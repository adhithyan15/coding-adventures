## You'll want to know: राक्षस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C338-RAKSHAS338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**राक्षस** — *rākṣas* — "an ogre; a demon".

> गोष्टीतल्या राक्षसाला सात डोकी होती.
>
> *goṣṭītalyā rākṣasālā sāt ḍokī hotī.*
>
> The ogre in the story had seven heads.

Masculine.

Plural राक्षस.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राक्षस** (*rākṣas*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *rākṣasa*, 'a demon, an evil being' (Monier-Williams).
