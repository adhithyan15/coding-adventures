## You'll want to know: شلوغ کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C536-CHORES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شلوغ کردن** (*sholugh kardan*) — "to make a mess; to make a racket".

> بچه ها خانه را شلوغ کردند.
>
> *bachchehâ khâne râ sholugh kardand.*
>
> The children made a mess of the house.

*sholugh* 'crowded, busy, messy' + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sholugh* was borrowed from Azerbaijani, a Turkic language (EtymDB). *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
