## You'll want to know: 同情
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C460-LOW460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**同情** — *tóngqíng* — "to sympathize with".

> *nàge háizi gēn māma zǒusàn le wǒ hěn tóngqíng tā* — That boy got separated from his mother, and I really feel for him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**同情** (*tóngqíng*) is built from 同 'the same' and 情 'feeling', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duwng* and *dzjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'equal' and 'together'.
