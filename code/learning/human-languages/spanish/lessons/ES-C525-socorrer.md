## You'll want to know: socorrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C525-HERIDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**socorrer** — "to help, to come to the aid of".

> Unos vecinos socorrieron a los heridos.
>
> *Some neighbours came to the aid of the injured.*

*¡Socorro!* — help! *Socorrer a alguien* — to help someone in danger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*socorrer* is inherited from Latin *succurrere* 'to run up, to run to help' (EtymDB), built on *sub* and *currere* 'to run' (Lewis & Short).
