# ممتن (mumtann) — grateful

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Arabic for to take pride, then the Arabic for gratitude.

## You'll want to know: ممتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C491-FAKHR491-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ممتن** — *mumtann* — "grateful".

> أنا ممتن لك على مساعدتك.
>
> *anā mumtann laka ʿalā musāʿadatik.*
>
> I am grateful to you for your help.

ممتن لـ someone على something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ممتن** (*mumtann*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-n-n*, "to grant a favour" (Lane). Mumtann is the participle that goes with imtinān, gratitude.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
