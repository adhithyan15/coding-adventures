## You'll want to know: माफ करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C503-MAPH503-05]; assesses=[] -->

**माफ करणे** — *māph karṇe* — "to forgive".

> मी चूक केली, तरी आईने मला माफ केलं.
>
> *mī cūk kelī, tarī āīne malā māph kelaṁ.*
>
> I made a mistake, but Mother forgave me.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मला माफ केलं, *tyāne malā māph kelaṁ*.

माफी मागणे, to apologise; the one forgiven takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माफ करणे** (*māph karṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on माफ, "excused", a loan from Persian *muaf*, usually traced to Arabic, with करणे.
