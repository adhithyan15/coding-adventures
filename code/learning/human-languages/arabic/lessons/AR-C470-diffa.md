## You'll want to know: ضفة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C470-YANBU470-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ضفة** — *ḍiffa* — "a riverbank".

> جلسنا على ضفة النهر.
>
> *jalasnā ʿalā ḍiffat an-nahr.*
>
> We sat on the riverbank.

Plural: ضفاف, *ḍifāf*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضفة** (*ḍiffa*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ḍ-f-f*, "the side of a river".
