# ঠাকুমা (ṭhākumā) — a paternal grandmother

## You'll want to know: ঠাকুমা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C481-MA481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠাকুমা** — *ṭhākumā* — "a paternal grandmother".

> ঠাকুমা আমাদের রাজার গল্প বলতেন।
>
> *ṭhākumā āmāder rājār gôlpo bôlten.*
>
> Grandmother used to tell us stories about kings.

দিদিমা (taught) is the mother's mother; ঠাকুমা is the father's.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠাকুমা** (*ṭhākumā*) puts *ṭhākur*, a title of respect, from Sanskrit *ṭhakkura*, "a deity; a title" (CDIAL 5488), with মা, mother.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
