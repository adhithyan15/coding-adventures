Kannada, chapter 347: The Press Conference.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ (suddigōṣṭhi) — a press conference.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a deluge, then the Kannada for an injured person.

You'll want to know: ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ.
ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ — suddigōṣṭhi — "a press conference".
ಪೊಲೀಸ್ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿ ಸಂಜೆ ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ ಕರೆದರು. polīs adhikāri sañje suddigōṣṭhi karedaru. The police officer called a press conference in the evening.
A press conference is "called": ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ (suddigōṣṭhi) ಕರೆ, or "held": ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ (suddigōṣṭhi) ನಡೆಸು.

The word, taken apart.
ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿ (suddigōṣṭhi) is a Kannada compound of ಸುದ್ದಿ, "news", which comes from Sanskrit śuddhi (CDIAL 12523), and ಗೋಷ್ಠಿ, borrowed from Sanskrit goṣṭhī, "an assembly, a meeting, a conversation" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ಪ್ರಶ್ನಿಸು (praśnisu) — to question.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an injured person, then the Kannada for a press conference.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಶ್ನಿಸು.
ಪ್ರಶ್ನಿಸು — praśnisu — "to question".
ವರದಿಗಾರರು ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಯನ್ನು ಅಪಘಾತದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಿಸಿದರು. varadigāraru adhikāriyannu apaghātada bagge praśnisidaru. The reporters questioned the officer about the accident.
Past (I …): ಪ್ರಶ್ನಿಸಿದೆ, praśniside.
The person questioned takes -ಅನ್ನು. It can also mean to challenge: ನಿರ್ಧಾರವನ್ನು ಪ್ರಶ್ನಿಸು (praśnisu).

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಶ್ನಿಸು (praśnisu) is built on Sanskrit praśna, "a question" (CDIAL 8818), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸು (pratikriyisu) — to give a reaction; to respond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a press conference, then the Kannada for to question.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸು.
ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸು — pratikriyisu — "to give a reaction; to respond".
ಆ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಗೆ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿ ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ. ā praśnege adhikāri pratikriyisalilla. The officer did not respond to that question.
Past (I …): ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸಿದೆ, pratikriyiside.
What one responds to takes -ಗೆ. The noun is ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯೆ, a response.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರತಿಕ್ರಿಯಿಸು (pratikriyisu) is built on Sanskrit pratikriyā, "requital, counteraction" (Monier-Williams), from prati "back, in return" and kriyā "an act", with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to question, then the Kannada for to give a reaction.

You'll want to know: ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸು.
ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸು — ullēkhisu — "to mention; to refer to".
ಅವರು ಮಾತಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಿನ್ನೆಯ ಘಟನೆಯನ್ನು ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸಿದರು. avaru mātinalli ninneya ghaṭaneyannu ullēkhisidaru. In the talk he mentioned yesterday's incident.
Past (I …): ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸಿದೆ, ullēkhiside.
What is mentioned takes -ಅನ್ನು. A formal word; in talk people say ಹೇಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸು (ullēkhisu) is built on Sanskrit ullekha, "causing to come forth clearly" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಸಾರಾಂಶ (sārāṃśa) — a summary; the gist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to give a reaction, then the Kannada for to mention.

You'll want to know: ಸಾರಾಂಶ.
ಸಾರಾಂಶ — sārāṃśa — "a summary; the gist".
ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿಯ ಸಾರಾಂಶ ರಾತ್ರಿಯ ವಾರ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂತು. suddigōṣṭhiya sārāṃśa rātriya vārteyalli bantu. A summary of the press conference came on the night news.
ಸಾರ is the essence: the essential part of something said or written.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾರಾಂಶ (sārāṃśa) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of sāra, "the core, the essence", and aṃśa, "a part, a share" (Monier-Williams).
