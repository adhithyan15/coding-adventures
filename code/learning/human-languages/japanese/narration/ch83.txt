Japanese, chapter 83: This, That, Which, This kind of, That kind of.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
この (kono) — this (next to me).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the opposite side, then the Japanese for upstream.

You'll want to know: この.
この — kono — "this (next to me)".

The word, taken apart.
この (kono) is native: ko-, the root that points near the speaker, with no, "of" (JMdict). It is inherited rather than borrowed, and is usually written in kana.

Lesson 4 of 5.
こんな (konna) — this kind of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for that, then the Japanese for which.

You'll want to know: こんな.
こんな — konna — "this kind of".

The word, taken apart.
こんな (konna) is native, built on ko-, the root that points near the speaker; it is usually explained as a shortening of kono yō na, "of this kind". It is inherited rather than borrowed and has no kanji (JMdict).
