## You'll want to know: やくす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C228-LEARN228-02]; assesses=[] -->

**やくす** — *yakusu* — "to translate".

> **ちちは にほんごの てがみを やくします。** — *chichi wa nihongo no tegami o yakushimasu* — "My father translates letters written in Japanese."

Polite **やくします**, *yakushimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やくす** (*yakusu*) is a mixed word, built on Sino-Japanese *yaku*, "translate" (Middle Chinese *yek*), borrowed from Chinese, with a native verb ending *-su* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
