## You'll want to know: たつまき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C278-INAZUMA278-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たつまき** — *tatsumaki* — "a tornado; a whirlwind".

> **たつまきで やねが とびました。** — *tatsumaki de yane ga tobimashita* — "The tornado blew the roof off."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たつまき** (*tatsumaki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tatsu*, "dragon", and *maki*, "winding" (from *maku*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
