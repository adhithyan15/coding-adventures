Russian, chapter 517: Choir and Orchestra.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
хор (khor) — a choir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to hum, then the Russian for to sing out of tune.

You'll want to know: хор.
хор (khor) — "a choir".
В школе я пел в хоре. V shkóle ya pel v khóre. At school I sang in the choir.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
хор (khor) comes from Greek khorós 'a company of dancers or singers' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
оркестр (orkéstr) — an orchestra.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to sing out of tune, then the Russian for a choir.

You'll want to know: оркестр.
оркестр (orkéstr) — "an orchestra".
Оркестр играл два часа без перерыва. Orkéstr igrál dva chasá bez pererýva. The orchestra played for two hours without a break.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
оркестр (orkéstr) comes from Greek orkhḗstra, the dancing floor of a theatre, built on orkhéomai 'to dance' (EtymDB).

Дирижёр поднял палочку, и в зале стало тихо. Dirizhyór pódnyal pálochku, i v zále stálo tíkho. The conductor raised his baton and the hall went quiet.
Masculine.

Lesson 4 of 5.
дирижировать (dirizhírovat) — to conduct (music).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an orchestra, then the Russian for a conductor.

You'll want to know: дирижировать.
дирижировать (dirizhírovat) — "to conduct (music)".
Сегодня оркестром дирижирует молодая женщина. Segódnya orkéstrom dirizhíruyet molodáya zhénshchina. Today a young woman is conducting the orchestra.
Imperfective; the present is дирижирую. Takes the instrumental: дирижировать (dirizhírovat) оркестром.

The word, taken apart.
дирижировать (dirizhírovat) is usually traced to French diriger 'to direct', with -ировать; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
композитор (kompozítor) — a composer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a conductor, then the Russian for to conduct.

You'll want to know: композитор.
композитор (kompozítor) — "a composer".
Этот композитор пишет музыку для кино. Étot kompozítor píshet múzyku dlya kinó. This composer writes film music.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
композитор (kompozítor) was borrowed from Latin compositor 'an arranger' (EtymDB), from compōnere 'to put together' (Lewis & Short).
