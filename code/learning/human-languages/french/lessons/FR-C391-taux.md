## You'll want to know: le taux
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C391-EPARGNE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le taux** — "the rate".

> Le taux d'intérêt est de trois pour cent.
>
> *The interest rate is three per cent.*

The same form in the plural. *Le taux de chômage* — the unemployment rate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*taux* comes from Old French *tauxer* "to fix a price" (EtymDB), from Latin *taxāre* "to appraise, to value" (Lewis & Short).
