Hindi, chapter 468: Patience and Temper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धैर्य (dhairya) — forbearance; patience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a betrayal of trust, then the Hindi for crooked.

You'll want to know: धैर्य.
धैर्य — dhairya — "forbearance; patience".
बच्चों को पढ़ाने के लिए बहुत धैर्य चाहिए। baccoṁ ko paṛhāne ke liye bahut dhairya cāhiye. Teaching children takes a great deal of patience.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
धैर्य (dhairya) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit dhairya, "firmness, constancy, calmness, patience" (Monier-Williams). It is the formal sister of dhīraj.

Lesson 2 of 5.
अधीर (adhīr) — impatient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: अधीर.
अधीर — adhīr — "impatient".
ट्रेन देर से आई, इसलिए सब यात्री अधीर थे। ṭren der se āī, isliye sab yātrī adhīr the. The train came late, so all the passengers were impatient.

The word, taken apart.
अधीर (adhīr) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit adhīra, "not steady, excitable", built on a-, "not", and dhīra, "steady" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for someone who cannot wait.

Lesson 3 of 5.
झक्की (jhakkī) — cranky; set in odd ways.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for forbearance, then the Hindi for impatient.

You'll want to know: झक्की.
झक्की — jhakkī — "cranky; set in odd ways".
मेरे नाना थोड़े झक्की हैं, हर काम अपने तरीक़े से करते हैं। mere nānā thoṛe jhakkī haiṁ, har kām apne tarīqe se karte haiṁ. My grandfather is a bit cranky; he does everything his own way.

The word, taken apart.
झक्की (jhakkī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi jhakkī, "stubborn", under jhakk, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5319). It is the stubbornness of odd, fixed habits.

You'll want to know: झिड़कना.
झिड़कना — jhiṛaknā — "to snap at; to rebuke".
देर से आने पर मालिक ने नौकर को झिड़का। der se āne par mālik ne naukar ko jhiṛkā. The master snapped at the servant for coming late.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नौकर को झिड़का, usne naukar ko jhiṛkā.

The word, taken apart.
झिड़कना (jhiṛaknā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi jhiṛaknā, "to shake, scold", under jhaṭ, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5327). It is a short, sharp telling-off, sharper than ḍā̃ṭnā.

Lesson 5 of 5.
झल्लाना (jhallānā) — to snap irritably.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for cranky, then the Hindi for to snap at.

You'll want to know: झल्लाना.
झल्लाना — jhallānā — "to snap irritably".
बार बार वही सवाल सुनकर पिता झल्ला गए। bār bār vahī savāl sunkar pitā jhallā gae. Hearing the same question again and again, my father snapped.
Past (he …): वह झल्ला गया, vah jhallā gayā.

The word, taken apart.
झल्लाना (jhallānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi jhallānā, "to burn", beside jhallā, "hot", under jhal, "flash", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5352). The sense "to flare up in irritation" is a turn CDIAL does not give, though the heat behind it is plain.
