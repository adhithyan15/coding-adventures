## You'll want to know: la corteccia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C461-ALBERI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la corteccia** — "the bark (of a tree)".

> La corteccia del pino è ruvida.
>
> *Pine bark is rough.*

Also the cortex of the brain: *la corteccia cerebrale*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*corteccia* is inherited from Latin *corticea* (EtymDB), from *cortex, corticis* 'bark'.
