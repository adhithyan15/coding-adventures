Marathi, chapter 367: Customs and Ways of Thinking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रथा (prathā) — a practice; a custom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for renunciation, then the Marathi for welfare.

You'll want to know: प्रथा.
प्रथा — prathā — "a practice; a custom".
सणाला नवे कपडे घालण्याची प्रथा आहे. saṇālā nave kapḍe ghālaṇyācī prathā āhe. There is a custom of wearing new clothes at festivals.
Feminine.
X ची प्रथा (prathā) आहे, it is the custom to X.

The word, taken apart.
प्रथा (prathā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prathā, "spreading; fame" (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for a practice that has spread.

काही जुन्या रूढी आता बदलत आहेत. kāhī junyā rūḍhī ātā badlat āhet. Some old conventions are now changing.

रूढी (rūḍhī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit rūḍhi, "rise, increase" (CDIAL 10802); the same entry lists an inherited Marathi rūḍh, "prevalence".

You'll want to know: विचारसरणी.
विचारसरणी — vicārsarṇī — "an ideology; a way of thinking".
दोघांची विचारसरणी अगदी वेगळी आहे. doghāñcī vicārsarṇī agdī vegḷī āhe. The two have very different ways of thinking.
Feminine.
विचार + सरणी, thought + path.

The word, taken apart.
विचारसरणी (vicārsarṇī) is a learned compound built from विचार, "thought", and Sanskrit saraṇi, "path, way" (Monier-Williams).
