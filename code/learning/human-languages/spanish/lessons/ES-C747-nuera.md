## You'll want to know: la nuera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C747-POLITICA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la nuera** — "the daughter-in-law".

> Se lleva muy bien con su nuera.
>
> *She gets on very well with her daughter-in-law.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nuera* is inherited from Latin *nurus* "daughter-in-law", through spoken *nora* and Old Spanish *nuera* (EtymDB).
