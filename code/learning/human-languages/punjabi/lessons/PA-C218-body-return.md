# Return to getting up and sitting

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-01, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-04, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217] -->

[YOU HEAR: *uṭṭhṇā; baiṭhṇā*] Say **ਉੱਠਣਾ · ਬੈਠਣਾ**. Then *Aman came yesterday*.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-MILIA-188, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217] -->

You already say **ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਮਿਲਿਆ**, "I met Aman". Listen to its end: *-iā*,
the same sound that ends **ਆਇਆ**. The next lesson gives that ending to getting
up and sitting down.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-01, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-04, PA-FORM-MILIA-188] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *to get up*] Say **ਉੱਠਣਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *to sit*] Say **ਬੈਠਣਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *I met Aman*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਮਿਲਿਆ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-01, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *please get up*, then *please sit*.
