# (reading) — six words, counted in shapes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-VEEDU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Every word below you have written. Not one is new.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that nobody says them first.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-SKILL-READ-WORDS]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C44-ASK-05, TA-LEX-PAAL-01] -->

> வீடு
> புத்தகம்
> தண்ணீர்
> பால்
> அம்மா
> அப்பா

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and this time count the shapes in each word rather than the
sounds.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-THANNEER-ARISI-01, TA-ETYMON-KUDUMBAM-01, TA-SKILL-READ-WORDS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] They do not match. Tamil joins a consonant and its vowel into one
shape, so **வீடு** is four sounds and two shapes.

[PAUSE 2s] That is the thing a reader has to stop fighting. You learned these
words as sounds and wrote them as shapes, and reading asks you to take the
shape whole rather than to unpack it back into sounds.

[PAUSE 2s] **அம்மா** and **அப்பா** are the clearest pair: same length, same
rhythm, one shape different in the middle.
