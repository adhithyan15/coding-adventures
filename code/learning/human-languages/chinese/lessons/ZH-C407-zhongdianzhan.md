## You'll want to know: zhōngdiǎnzhàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C407-PULLIN407-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōngdiǎnzhàn* — "terminus; the end of the line".

> *wǒmen zài zhōngdiǎnzhàn xià chē* — We get off at the last stop.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōngdiǎnzhàn* is built from *zhōng* 'the end', 点 'a point' and 站 'a station', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyuwng*, *temX* and *treamH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the utmost, the end'.
