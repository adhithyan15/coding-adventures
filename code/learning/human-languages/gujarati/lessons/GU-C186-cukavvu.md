## You'll want to know: ચૂકવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C186-TILL186-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂકવવું** — *cūkavvũ* — "to pay, to settle".

> **હું દુકાનદારને પૈસા ચૂકવું છું.** — *hũ dukāndārne paisā cūkavũ chũ* — "I pay the shopkeeper the money."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂકવવું** (*cūkavvũ*) is a tadbhava, the causative of a verb built on *\*cukk*, "fall short of, stop", a reconstructed form. CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "to discharge, settle" (CDIAL 4848).
