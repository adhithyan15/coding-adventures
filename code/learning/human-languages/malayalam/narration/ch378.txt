Malayalam, chapter 378: Shelter and Flight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
അഭയം (abhayaṁ) — shelter; refuge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to be cut off, then the Malayalam for to go missing.

You'll want to know: അഭയം.
അഭയം — abhayaṁ — "shelter; refuge".
വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കത്തിൽ ആളുകൾ പള്ളിക്കൂടത്തിൽ അഭയം തേടി. veḷḷappokkattil āḷukaḷ paḷḷikkūṭattil abhayaṁ tēṭi. In the flood people took shelter in the school.
Literally freedom from fear: a safe place in a disaster.

The word, taken apart.
അഭയം (abhayaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit abhaya, "fearless", and as a noun "safety" (CDIAL 507): a-, "without", and bhaya, "fear".

Lesson 2 of 5.
അഭയാർത്ഥി (abhayārtthi) — a refugee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to go missing, then the Malayalam for shelter.

You'll want to know: അഭയാർത്ഥി.
അഭയാർത്ഥി — abhayārtthi — "a refugee".
യുദ്ധം കാരണം ഒരുപാട് അഭയാർത്ഥികൾ അതിർത്തി കടന്നു. yuddhaṁ kāraṇaṁ orupāṭŭ abhayārtthikaḷ atirtti kaṭannu. Because of the war many refugees crossed the border.
Plural: അഭയാർത്ഥികൾ, abhayārtthikaḷ.
അഭയം (abhayaṁ), refuge, + അർത്ഥി, one who seeks, as in വിദ്യാർത്ഥി.

The word, taken apart.
അഭയാർത്ഥി (abhayārtthi) is built from Sanskrit abhaya, "safety" (CDIAL 507), and arthin, "desiring, one who seeks" (CDIAL 640), the same ending as in വിദ്യാർത്ഥി, "a student".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for shelter, then the Malayalam for a refugee.

You'll want to know: ജീവഹാനി.
ജീവഹാനി — jīvahāni — "loss of life".
ഭാഗ്യത്തിന് അപകടത്തിൽ ജീവഹാനി ഉണ്ടായില്ല. bhāgyattinŭ apakaṭattil jīvahāni uṇṭāyilla. Luckily there was no loss of life in the accident.
A news word; in speech people say ആരും മരിച്ചില്ല, nobody died.

The word, taken apart.
ജീവഹാനി (jīvahāni) is built from Sanskrit jīva, "life" (CDIAL 5239), and hāni, "abandonment, loss" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
പലായനം ചെയ്യുക (palāyanaṁ ceyyuka) — to flee; to take flight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a refugee, then the Malayalam for loss of life.

You'll want to know: പലായനം ചെയ്യുക.
പലായനം ചെയ്യുക — palāyanaṁ ceyyuka — "to flee; to take flight".
യുദ്ധം കാരണം ആയിരങ്ങൾ നാട്ടിൽ നിന്ന് പലായനം ചെയ്തു. yuddhaṁ kāraṇaṁ āyiraṅṅaḷ nāṭṭil ninnŭ palāyanaṁ ceytu. Because of the war thousands fled their homeland.
Past: പലായനം ചെയ്തു, palāyanaṁ ceytu.
The noun പലായനം is a flight, an exodus. From where takes -ഇൽ നിന്ന്.

The word, taken apart.
പലായനം ചെയ്യുക (palāyanaṁ ceyyuka) joins പലായനം, "flight, running away", borrowed from Sanskrit palāyana (Monier-Williams), and ചെയ്യുക, "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
തേടുക (tēṭuka) — to seek; to look for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for loss of life, then the Malayalam for to flee.

You'll want to know: തേടുക.
തേടുക — tēṭuka — "to seek; to look for".
ജോലി തേടി അവൻ നഗരത്തിൽ പോയി. jōli tēṭi avan nagarattil pōyi. He went to the city in search of work.
Past: തേടി, tēṭi.
തേടി often means in search of. A more literary word than തിരയുക.

The word, taken apart.
തേടുക (tēṭuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3456 lists Malayalam tēṭuka, "to seek, to acquire", beside Tamil tēṭu, "to seek, to search for".
