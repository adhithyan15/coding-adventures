## You'll want to know: усваивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C387-ZNANIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**усваивать** (*usváivat*) — "to take in, to master".

> Дети быстро усваивают новые слова.
>
> *Déti býstro usváivayut nóvyye slová.*
>
> Children take in new words quickly.

Imperfective; perfective *усвоить*. Built on *свой*, one’s own: to make something your own.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**усваивать** is the imperfective of *усвоить*, built on *свой* "one’s own": to master a thing is to make it your own.
