## You'll want to know: आधार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C343-KARAN343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आधार** — *ādhār* — "a basis, grounds".

> तुम्हारी बात का आधार क्या है?
>
> *tumhārī bāt kā ādhār kyā hai?*
>
> What are your grounds for saying that?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आधार** (*ādhār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ādhāra*, "a support, a prop" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
