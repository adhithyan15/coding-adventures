## You'll want to know: ഗുണപാഠം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C402-PURANAM402-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗുണപാഠം** — *guṇapāṭhaṁ* — "a moral".

> ആ കഥയുടെ ഗുണപാഠം എന്താണ്?
>
> *ā kathayuṭe guṇapāṭhaṁ entāṇŭ?*
>
> What is the moral of that story?

ഗുണം, a virtue, + പാഠം, a lesson: the good lesson a story teaches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗുണപാഠം** (*guṇapāṭhaṁ*) is built from two Sanskrit words, *guṇa*, "a quality, a good quality" (CDIAL 4190), and *pāṭha*, "study, a text" (CDIAL 8035), the source of പാഠം (*pāṭhaṁ*), "a lesson".
