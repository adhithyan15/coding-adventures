Gujarati, chapter 164: The spice tin.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ડબ્બો (ḍabbo) — tin, box.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to serve, then the Gujarati for to share out.

You'll want to know: ડબ્બો.
ડબ્બો — ḍabbo — "tin, box".
મસાલાનો ડબ્બો રસોડામાં છે. — masālāno ḍabbo rasoḍāmā̃ che — "The spice tin is in the kitchen."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ડબ્બો (ḍabbo) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ખાંડ (khā̃ḍ) — sugar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to share out, then the Gujarati for tin.

You'll want to know: ખાંડ.
ખાંડ — khā̃ḍ — "sugar".
ડબ્બામાં ખાંડ નથી. — ḍabbāmā̃ khā̃ḍ nathī — "There is no sugar in the tin."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ખાંડ (khā̃ḍ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit khaṇḍa, khaṇḍu, "sugar; partially dried molasses", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "coarse sugar" (CDIAL 3800).

Lesson 4 of 6.
રાઈ (rāī) — mustard seed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for sugar, then the Gujarati for black pepper.

You'll want to know: રાઈ.
રાઈ — rāī — "mustard seed".
રાઈ નાની અને કાળી છે. — rāī nānī ane kāḷī che — "Mustard seed is small and black."

The word, taken apart.
રાઈ (rāī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from rājikā, "mustard", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "mustard" (CDIAL 10688).
