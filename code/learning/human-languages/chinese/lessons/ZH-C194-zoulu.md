## You'll want to know: zǒulù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C194-ROAD194-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒulù* — "to walk; to go on foot".

> *wǒ měitiān zǒulù qù shàngbān* — I walk to work every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒulù* is built from *zǒu* and 路 'road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsuwX* and *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to hurry'; in Mandarin it has come to mean walking.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
