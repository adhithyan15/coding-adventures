Spanish, chapter 519: La policía actúa.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
detener — to detain, to arrest; to stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to hold up, then the Spanish for to flee.

You'll want to know: detener.
detener — "to detain, to arrest; to stop".
La policía detuvo al ladrón en la estación. The police arrested the thief at the station.
It changes like tener: detuve, detuvo. Detenerse is to stop: El coche se detuvo.

The word, taken apart.
detener is inherited from Latin dētinēre 'to hold back' (EtymDB), built on dē- and tenēre 'to hold'. English detain is the same Latin verb through French.

Lesson 4 of 6.
la patrulla — the patrol; the patrol car.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to arrest, then the Spanish for the arrest.

You'll want to know: la patrulla.
la patrulla — "the patrol; the patrol car".
Una patrulla llegó en cinco minutos. A patrol car arrived in five minutes.
Estar de patrulla — to be on patrol. Patrullar is to patrol.

The word, taken apart.
patrulla is built on patrullar 'to patrol', which was borrowed from French patrouiller (EtymDB). In Old French that verb meant 'to paddle, to paw about', and EtymDB builds it on patte 'a paw'. The patrol tramped round on foot.
