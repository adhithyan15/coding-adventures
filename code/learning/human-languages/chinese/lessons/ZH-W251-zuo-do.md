# 作 — the person piece, then a post with two short bars

## Script — the person piece, then a post with two short bars
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZUO-DO-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 作

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, 亻, two strokes,** as in 你 and 做: a stroke falling to the left, then a vertical down to the foot.

**The right piece, five strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left at the top. A bar to the right from its middle. A long vertical down from the bar to the foot. Then two short bars against the right side of the vertical, the upper one first.

2 + 5 = seven.
