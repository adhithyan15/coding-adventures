## You'll want to know: أضعف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C418-TAQWIYA418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أضعف** — *aḍʿafa* — "to weaken".

> أضعفت الحمى جسمه.
>
> *aḍʿafat al-ḥummā jismah.*
>
> The fever weakened his body.

Present: يضعف, *yuḍʿif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أضعف** (*aḍʿafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-ʿ-f*, "to be weak; to double" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make weak: to weaken. ضعيف, weak, is from the same root.
