## You'll want to know: સત્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C313-AFVA313-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સત્ય** — *satya* — "the truth".

> પોલીસે લોકોને સત્ય જણાવ્યું.
>
> *polīse lokone satya jaṇāvyũ.*
>
> The police told people the truth.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સત્ય** (*satya*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *satya*, "true, real" (Monier-Williams). The noun is neuter; **સાચું** is the everyday adjective, true.
