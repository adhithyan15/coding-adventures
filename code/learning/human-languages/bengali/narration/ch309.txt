Bengali, chapter 309: Kites and the New Year.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘুড়ি (ghuṛi) — a paper kite; a kite (toy).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a festive procession, then the Bengali for to embrace.

You'll want to know: ঘুড়ি.
ঘুড়ি — ghuṛi — "a paper kite; a kite (toy)".
ছুটির দিনে ছেলেরা ছাদে ঘুড়ি নিয়ে খেলছিল। chhuṭir dine chhelerā chhāde ghuṛi niye khelchhilô. On the holiday the boys were playing with kites on the roof.

The word, taken apart.
ঘুড়ি (ghuṛi) is the paper kite; its origin is not settled. A kite the bird is চিল.

বিকেলে বাবা আর আমি ছাদে ঘুড়ি ওড়ালাম। bikele bābā ār āmi chhāde ghuṛi oṛālām. In the late afternoon my father and I flew kites on the roof.
Past (he/she …): সেই ছেলে ঘুড়ি ওড়াল, sei chhele ghuṛi oṛālô.

ওড়ানো (oṛāno) is ওড়া, to fly — CDIAL 1697, Sanskrit uḍḍīyatē, 'flies up' — with the -ānō ending: to make something fly.

Lesson 3 of 5.
নববর্ষ (nôbôbôrshô) — the new year.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a paper kite, then the Bengali for to fly a kite.

You'll want to know: নববর্ষ.
নববর্ষ — nôbôbôrshô — "the new year".
নববর্ষের দিন সবাই নতুন জামা পরে বেড়াতে যায়। nôbôbôrsher din sôbāi nôtun jāmā pôre beṛāte jāy. On the first day of the new year everyone puts on new clothes and goes out visiting.

The word, taken apart.
নববর্ষ (nôbôbôrshô) joins Sanskrit nava, 'new', to varṣa, 'rain; a year' (Monier-Williams), the year counted in rainy seasons.

Lesson 4 of 5.
হালখাতা (hālkhātā) — a new account book opened at the New Year.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to fly a kite, then the Bengali for the new year.

You'll want to know: হালখাতা.
হালখাতা — hālkhātā — "a new account book opened at the New Year".
নববর্ষের দিন দোকানদাররা হালখাতা খোলেন। nôbôbôrsher din dokāndārrā hālkhātā kholen. On the first day of the new year shopkeepers open a new account book.

The word, taken apart.
হালখাতা (hālkhātā) joins হাল, 'the current state' — usually traced to Arabic ḥāl — to খাতা, an account book: the fresh ledger a shop opens on the first day of the year, with sweets for its customers.

Lesson 5 of 5.
প্রথা (prôthā) — a custom; a tradition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the new year, then the Bengali for a new account book.

You'll want to know: প্রথা.
প্রথা — prôthā — "a custom; a tradition".
নতুন বছরে হালখাতা খোলা একটা পুরনো প্রথা। nôtun bôchhôre hālkhātā kholā ekṭā purôno prôthā. Opening a new ledger for the new year is an old custom.

The word, taken apart.
প্রথা (prôthā) is Sanskrit prathā, 'a spreading out; fame' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a custom', a practice that has spread, is the later one.
