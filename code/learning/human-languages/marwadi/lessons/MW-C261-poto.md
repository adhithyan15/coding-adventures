## You'll want to know: पोतो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C261-MASI261-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पोतो** — *poto* — "a son's son; a grandson".

> म्हारो पोतो घणो छोटो है।
>
> *mhāro poto ghaṇo chhoṭo hai.*
>
> My grandson is very small.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोतो** (*poto*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pautra*, "a son's son". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *potā* (CDIAL 8416).
