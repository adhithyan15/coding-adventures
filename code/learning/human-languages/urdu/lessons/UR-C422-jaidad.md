## You'll want to know: جائیداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C422-DALAL422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جائیداد** — *jāidād* — "property; an estate".

> ان کی جائیداد میں ایک کوٹھی ہے۔
>
> *un kī jāidād meṅ ek koṭhī hai.*
>
> Their property includes a large house.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جائیداد** (*jāidād*) is usually traced to Persian *jāydād*, "property, an estate".
