## You'll want to know: पादुका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C337-VANAVASA337-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पादुका** — *pādukā* — "a sandal".

> भ्राता तस्य पादुके सिंहासने स्थापयति।
>
> *bhrātā tasya pāduke siṃhāsane sthāpayati.*
>
> His brother places his sandals on the throne.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पादुका** (*pādukā*) is usually traced to *pāda*, "a foot"; MW gives *pādukā* as a shoe or slipper, and the footprint of a holy person (MW).
