## You'll want to know: संग्रहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C516-AVAKASA516-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संग्रहः** — *saṅgrahaḥ* — "a collection".

> एषः मम भ्रातुः संग्रहः अस्ति।
>
> *eṣaḥ mama bhrātuḥ saṅgrahaḥ asti.*
>
> This is my brother's collection.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संग्रहः** (*saṅgrahaḥ*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √grah, "to seize, to take"; MW gives *saṃgraha* as collecting, gathering, an accumulation (MW).
