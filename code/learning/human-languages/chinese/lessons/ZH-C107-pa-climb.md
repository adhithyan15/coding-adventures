## You'll want to know: pá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C107-MOVE107-02]; assesses=[] -->

*pá* — "to climb; to crawl".

> *wǒmen míngtiān qù pá shān* — We're going up into the hills tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pá* is inherited from Middle Chinese *bae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to scratch'; how it came to mean to climb is not settled.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
