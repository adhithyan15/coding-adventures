## You'll want to know: طور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C418-TAQWIYA418-03]; assesses=[] -->

**طور** — *ṭawwara* — "to develop".

> طور المهندس برنامجا جديدا.
>
> *ṭawwara al-muhandis barnāmajan jadīdan.*
>
> The engineer developed a new program.

Present: يطور, *yuṭawwir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طور** (*ṭawwara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-w-r*, "a time, a stage" (Lane). It is Form II, to move a thing on from stage to stage: to develop.
