## You'll want to know: admettre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C337-COMPROMIS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**admettre** — "to admit".

> Je dois admettre que ton idée était meilleure.
>
> *I have to admit that your idea was better.*

Conjugated like *mettre*; past participle *admis*. It also means to let in: *être admis à l'université*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*admettre* comes through Old French *admettre* from Latin *admittere* "to let in, to allow" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built from *ad* "to" and *mittere* "to send": you let a fact in.
