## You'll want to know: বিশেষজ্ঞ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C501-BISHESHOGGO501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিশেষজ্ঞ** — *bisheshôgyô* — "an expert; a specialist".

> আমাদের পাড়ার ডাক্তার শিশুদের বিশেষজ্ঞ।
>
> *Āmāder pāṛār ḍāktār shishuder bisheshôgyô.*
>
> The doctor in our neighbourhood is a specialist in children.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিশেষজ্ঞ** (*bisheshôgyô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *viśeṣajña*, "one who knows the distinctions" (Monier-Williams).
