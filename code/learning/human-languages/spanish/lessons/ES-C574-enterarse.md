## You'll want to know: enterarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C574-ENTERARSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**enterarse** — "to hear about, to find out".

> Me enteré de la noticia por la radio.
>
> *I heard the news on the radio.*

*Enterarse de algo* — to find out about something. *¿Te has enterado?* — have you heard?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enterarse* is built on *entero* 'whole', inherited from Latin *integer* 'untouched, whole' (Lewis & Short). To find out is to get the whole story.
