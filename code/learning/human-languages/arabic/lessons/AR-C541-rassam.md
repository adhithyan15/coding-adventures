## You'll want to know: رسام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C541-FANN541-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رسام** — *rassām* — "a painter (artist)".

> رسم الرسام البحر عند الغروب.
>
> *rasama ar-rassām al-baḥr ʿinda al-ghurūb.*
>
> The painter painted the sea at sunset.

The pattern faʿʿāl names a trade or a habit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رسام** (*rassām*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-s-m*, "to mark, to trace" (Lane). Rasama, to draw, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
