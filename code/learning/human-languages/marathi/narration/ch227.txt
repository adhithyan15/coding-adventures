Marathi, chapter 227: To destroy, To exaggerate, To celebrate, To float, To chase.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नष्ट करणे (naṣṭa karṇe) — to destroy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to take a risk, then the Marathi for to solve.

You'll want to know: नष्ट करणे.
नष्ट करणे — naṣṭa karṇe — "to destroy".

The word, taken apart.
नष्ट is Sanskrit naṣṭa, "lost, destroyed", a tatsama. करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पाठलाग करणे (pāṭhlāg karṇe) — to chase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to celebrate, then the Marathi for to float.

You'll want to know: पाठलाग करणे.
पाठलाग करणे — pāṭhlāg karṇe — "to chase".

The word, taken apart.
पाठलाग is "back-touching": pāṭh, "back", from Sanskrit pṛṣṭi, "back" (CDIAL 8370), and lāg, as in lāgṇẽ, "to touch", from Sanskrit lagyati, "adheres to" (CDIAL 10895). करणे is "to do".
