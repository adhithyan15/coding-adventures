## You'll want to know: нуждаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C368-POMOSHCH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**нуждаться** (*nuzhdátsya*) — "to need, to be in need".

> Эта семья нуждается в помощи.
>
> *Éta semyá nuzhdáyetsya v pómoshchi.*
>
> This family is in need of help.

Imperfective only. *Нуждаться в* + prepositional. More formal than *нужен*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**нуждаться** is built on *нужда* "need, want", the noun behind *нужный* "necessary", inherited from Proto-Slavic.
