## You'll want to know: விரிவாக
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C312-VIRIVAKA312-01]; assesses=[] -->

**விரிவாக** — *virivāka* — "in detail".

> சாட்சி நடந்ததை விரிவாக விளக்கினார்.
>
> *cāṭci naṭantatai virivāka viḷakkiṉār.*
>
> The witness explained what happened in detail.

-ஆக makes the adverb; the opposite is சுருக்கமாக, briefly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**விரிவாக** (*virivāka*) is built on native விரிவு, "spread, expansion", from விரி, "to expand, spread out" (DEDR 5411), with the adverb ending -ஆக.

One more word for telling what happened.
