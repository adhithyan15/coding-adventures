## You'll want to know: ਖਾਤਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C257-BANK257-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਾਤਾ** — *khātā* — "a bank account; an account book".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਖਾਤਾ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦੇ ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā khātā shahir de baiṅk vich hai.*
>
> My account is at the bank in town.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਾਤਾ** (*khātā*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *kṣatra*, "might, rule"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khāttā*, "account book", there, as a word taken from Hindi *khātā* (CDIAL 3648). How the sense moved is not settled.
