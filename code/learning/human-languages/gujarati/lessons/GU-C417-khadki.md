## You'll want to know: ખડકી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C417-POL417-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખડકી** — *khaḍkī* — "a small gated lane (inside a pol)".

> અમારી ખડકીમાં પાંચ ઘર છે.
>
> *amārī khaḍkīmā̃ pā̃c ghar che.*
>
> There are five houses in our little lane.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખડકી** (*khaḍkī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *khaṭakkikā*, "a side door"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a courtyard, a lane" (CDIAL 3770). The name of the small gate seems to have passed to the lane behind it.
