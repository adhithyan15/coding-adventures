## You'll want to know: ज़रिया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C540-KOSHISH540-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़रिया** — *zariyā* — "a means, a way (of doing something)".

> मोबाइल बात करने का सबसे आसान ज़रिया है।
>
> *Mobāil bāt karne kā sabse āsān zariyā hai.*
>
> A mobile is the easiest way to talk.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़रिया** (*zariyā*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḏarīʿa*, "a means", through Persian.
