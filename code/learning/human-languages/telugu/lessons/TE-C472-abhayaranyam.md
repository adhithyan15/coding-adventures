# అభయారణ్యం (abhayāraṇyaṁ) — a wildlife sanctuary

## You'll want to know: అభయారణ్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C472-VANYAPRANI472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అభయారణ్యం** — *abhayāraṇyaṁ* — "a wildlife sanctuary".

> ఆ అభయారణ్యంలో పులులు ఉన్నాయి.
>
> *ā abhayāraṇyaṁlō pululu unnāyi.*
>
> There are tigers in that sanctuary.

అభయ, freedom from fear, + అరణ్యం, a forest: a forest where animals need not fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అభయారణ్యం** (*abhayāraṇyaṁ*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *abhaya*, "fearlessness, safety", and *araṇya*, "a wild place, a forest" (CDIAL 600).
