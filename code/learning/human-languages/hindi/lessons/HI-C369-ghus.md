## You'll want to know: घूस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C369-SANSTHA369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घूस** — *ghūs* — "a bribe (everyday)".

> अधिकारी ने घूस ली।
>
> *adhikārī ne ghūs lī.*
>
> The official took a bribe.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घूस** (*ghūs*) is inherited from *\*ghūssā*, "a present", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4499). CDIAL glosses the Hindi word "bribe"; the step from a present to a bribe is not spelled out there.
