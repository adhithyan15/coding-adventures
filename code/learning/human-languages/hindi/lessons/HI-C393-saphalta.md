## You'll want to know: सफलता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C393-SAFALTA393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सफलता** — *saphaltā* — "success".

> अगर तुम हार नहीं मानोगे तो सफलता ज़रूर मिलेगी।
>
> *agar tum hār nahīṃ mānoge to saphaltā zarūr milegī.*
>
> If you don't give up, success will surely come.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सफलता** (*saphaltā*) is Sanskrit *saphala*, "bearing fruit; having good results" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending *-tā*.
