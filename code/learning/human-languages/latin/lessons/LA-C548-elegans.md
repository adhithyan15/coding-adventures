## You'll want to know: ēlegāns, ēlegantis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C548-VENU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ēlegāns, ēlegantis** — "elegant, refined, tasteful".

> Domus eius parva, sed ēlegāns est.
>
> *His house is small but elegant.*

One ending. First choosy, then of good taste. English *elegant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ēlegāns** is probably an old form of *ēligēns*, the participle of *ēligō* "to pick out" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from *legō* "to choose". English *elegant* comes from it.
