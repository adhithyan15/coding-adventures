## You'll want to know: ઝાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C519-SAGDI519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાળ** — *jhāḷ* — "a tongue of flame".

> ચૂલાની ઝાળ લાલ અને પીળી છે.
>
> *cūlānī jhāḷ lāl ane pīḷī che.*
>
> The flame of the stove is red and yellow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાળ** (*jhāḷ*) comes from *\*jhāla*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "flame, heat" (CDIAL 5379).
