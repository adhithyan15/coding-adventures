## You'll want to know: ضلع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C358-JALSA358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ضلع** — *zilā* — "a district".

> ہمارا ضلع دریا کے پاس ہے۔
>
> *hamārā zilā daryā ke pās hai.*
>
> Our district lies by the river.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضلع** (*zilā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḍilʿ*, "a side, a part": one part of a province.
