## You'll want to know: દરબાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C318-RAJA318-04]; assesses=[] -->

**દરબાર** — *darbār* — "a royal court".

> રાજાના દરબારમાં એક વેપારી આવ્યો.
>
> *rājānā darbārmā̃ ek vepārī āvyo.*
>
> A merchant came to the king's court.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દરબાર** (*darbār*) is usually traced to Persian *darbār*, "a court, a royal audience": the hall where a king hears his people. **દરબારમાં**, at court.
