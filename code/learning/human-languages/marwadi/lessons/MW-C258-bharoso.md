## You'll want to know: भरोसो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C258-CHINTA258-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भरोसो** — *bharoso* — "trust; confidence".

> भाई माथै म्हारो पूरो भरोसो है।
>
> *bhāī māthai mhāro pūro bharoso hai.*
>
> I trust my brother completely.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भरोसो** (*bharoso*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*bharavaśya*, "dependence on support"; CDIAL lists Marwari *bharoso*, "confidence", under it (CDIAL 9398).
