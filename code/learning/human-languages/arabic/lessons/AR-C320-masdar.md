## You'll want to know: مصدر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C320-ILAM320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مصدر** — *maṣdar* — "a source".

> قال مصدر في الشرطة إن اللص هرب.
>
> *qāla maṣdar fī ash-shurṭa inna al-liṣṣ haraba.*
>
> A police source said the thief had escaped.

Plural: مصادر, *maṣādir*.

In grammar the مصدر is the verbal noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصدر** (*maṣdar*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-d-r* (Lane), "to go back from the watering place". *Maṣdar* is the place a thing comes out of: a source.
