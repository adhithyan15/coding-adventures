Telugu, chapter 113: Stove, Candle, Matchbox, Doll, Ball.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పొయ్యి (poyyi) — a stove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a drinking glass, then the Telugu for a spoon.

You'll want to know: పొయ్యి.
పొయ్యి — poyyi — "a stove".

The word, taken apart.
పొయ్యి (poyyi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil pori and Kannada puri (DEDR 4537). DEDR lists Telugu poyi, poyyi, "oven, stove, hearth", under the verb for "to fry, parch".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Telugu for a spoon, then the Telugu for a stove.
[pause 2 seconds]
Greet the afternoon and the night: శుభ మధ్యాహ్నం, శుభ రాత్రి.

You'll want to know: కొవ్వొత్తి.
కొవ్వొత్తి — kovvotti — "a candle".

The word, taken apart.
కొవ్వొత్తి (kovvotti) is native kovvu, "fat", one word with Tamil koḻu (DEDR 2146), and vatti, "wick", from Sanskrit varti, "wick" (CDIAL 11359): a fat-wick.
