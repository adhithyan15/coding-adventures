## You'll want to know: কৌশল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C324-KOUSHOL324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কৌশল** — *koushôl* — "a trick; a clever plan".

> শিয়াল একটা কৌশল করে কুমিরকে বোকা বানাল।
>
> *shiyāl ekṭā koushôl kôre kumirke bokā bānālô.*
>
> The jackal played a trick and made a fool of the crocodile.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কৌশল** (*koushôl*) is Sanskrit *kauśala*, 'welfare; skilfulness, cleverness' (Monier-Williams); in Bengali it is the clever plan itself. Its ৌ is read *ou*.
