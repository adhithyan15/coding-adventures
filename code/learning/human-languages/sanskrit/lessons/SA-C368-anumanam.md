## You'll want to know: अनुमानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C368-SHANKA368-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमानम्** — *anumānam* — "an inference; a guess".

> यदि मम अनुमानं सत्यम्, तर्हि सः श्वः गमिष्यति।
>
> *yadi mama anumānaṃ satyam, tarhi saḥ śvaḥ gamiṣyati.*
>
> If my guess is right, he will go tomorrow.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमानम्** (*anumānam*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √mā, "to measure"; MW gives *anu-māna* "inference, consideration" and "guess, conjecture" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
