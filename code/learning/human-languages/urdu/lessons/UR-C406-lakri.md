## You'll want to know: لکڑی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C406-BASERA406-01]; assesses=[] -->

**لکڑی** — *lakṛī* — "wood; firewood".

> ہم جنگل سے لکڑی لائے۔
>
> *ham jangal se lakṛī lāe.*
>
> We brought wood back from the forest.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لکڑی** (*lakṛī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lakṛī*, "a stick, firewood", under Sanskrit *lakuṭa*, "a club" (CDIAL 10875).
