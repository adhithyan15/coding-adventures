## You'll want to know: संरक्षण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C549-SANRAKSHAN549-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संरक्षण** — *saṁrakṣaṇ* — "protection".

> किल्ल्याच्या संरक्षणासाठी उंच भिंती होत्या.
>
> *killyācyā saṁrakṣaṇāsāṭhī uñc bhintī hotyā.*
>
> There were high walls to protect the fort.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संरक्षण** (*saṁrakṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṁrakṣaṇa*, "guarding, protection" (Monier-Williams).
