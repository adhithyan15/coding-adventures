# বিচ্ছেদ (bichchhed) — a separation; a break-up

## You'll want to know: বিচ্ছেদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C490-BICHCHHED490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিচ্ছেদ** — *bichchhed* — "a separation; a break-up".

> বিয়ের দশ বছর পরে মেসো আর মাসির বিচ্ছেদ হল।
>
> *biyer dôsh bôchhôr pôre meso ār māsir bichchhed hôlô.*
>
> Ten years after their wedding, my aunt and her husband separated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিচ্ছেদ** (*bichchhed*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *viccheda*, "cutting asunder, separation" (Monier-Williams).
