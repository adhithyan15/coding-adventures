## You'll want to know: پوشش خبری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C312-MONTASHER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پوشش خبری** (*pushesh-e khabari*) — "news coverage".

> این جشن پوشش خبری زیادی داشت.
>
> *In jashn pushesh-e khabari-ye ziyâdi dâsht.*
>
> This celebration got a lot of news coverage.

*pushesh* "covering", from *pushidan* "to cover, to wear", + *khabari* "of the news".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pushesh* "covering" is native Persian, from *pushidan* "to cover, to wear". *khabari* adds the Persian *-i* to *khabar* "news", borrowed from Arabic *khabar* (EtymDB).
