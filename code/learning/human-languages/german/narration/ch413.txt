6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
der Schlafwagen — the sleeping car.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the conductor, then the German for to check.

You'll want to know: der Schlafwagen.
der Schlafwagen — "the sleeping car".
Im Schlafwagen schläft sie bis zum Morgen. In the sleeping car she sleeps until morning.
Same form in the plural. A cheaper car with couchettes is der Liegewagen.

The word, taken apart.
Schlafwagen is built from schlafen 'to sleep' and Wagen 'carriage', both inherited: Wagen goes back to Proto-Germanic wagnaz (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 6.

You'll want to know: der Speisewagen.
der Speisewagen — "the dining car, the restaurant car".
Im Speisewagen essen wir eine warme Suppe. In the dining car we eat a hot soup.
Same form in the plural. Also das Bordrestaurant.

The word, taken apart.
Speisewagen is built from Speise 'food, dish', which goes back to Middle High German spīse (EtymDB) and is usually traced to Latin expēnsa 'outlay, expense' (Lewis & Short), and Wagen 'carriage'.

Lesson 3 of 6.
die Endstation — the end of the line, the terminus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the sleeping car, then the German for the dining car.

You'll want to know: die Endstation.
die Endstation — "the end of the line, the terminus".
An der Endstation steigen alle aus. At the end of the line everyone gets off.
Plural: die Endstationen. Also figurative: Endstation — this is as far as it goes.

The word, taken apart.
Endstation is built from End(e) 'end', inherited from Proto-Germanic andiją, and Station, from Latin statiō 'a standing, a post' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 6.
reservieren — to book, to reserve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the dining car, then the German for the end of the line.

You'll want to know: reservieren.
reservieren — "to book, to reserve".
Für die Nacht reserviert er ein Bett im Schlafwagen. For the night he books a bed in the sleeping car.
Past participle without ge-: ich habe reserviert. Die Reservierung — the reservation.

The word, taken apart.
reservieren is a learned borrowing from Latin reservāre 'to keep back, to save up' (Lewis & Short), probably through French réserver.

Lesson 5 of 6.
weiterfahren — to travel on, to drive on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the end of the line, then the German for to book.

You'll want to know: weiterfahren.
weiterfahren — "to travel on, to drive on".
Nach einer kurzen Pause fahren wir weiter. After a short break we drive on.
Separable; Perfekt with sein: wir sind weitergefahren.

The word, taken apart.
weiterfahren is built from weiter 'further, on' and the inherited verb fahren, Proto-Germanic faraną (EtymDB).
