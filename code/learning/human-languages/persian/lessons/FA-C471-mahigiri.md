## You'll want to know: ماهیگیری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C471-SHEKAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ماهیگیری** (*mâhigiri*) — "fishing".

> پدرم جمعه ها به ماهیگیری میرود.
>
> *Pedaram jom'e-hâ be mâhigiri miravad.*
>
> My father goes fishing on Fridays.

*mâhi* 'fish' + *gir* 'catching' + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mâhigiri* is a native compound of *mâhi* 'fish', from Middle Persian *māhīg* (EtymDB), and *gir*, the present stem of *gereftan* 'to take', with the ending *-i*.
