# বিরহ (birôhô) — the pain of separation; longing for someone far away

## You'll want to know: বিরহ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C448-BIROHO448-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিরহ** — *birôhô* — "the pain of separation; longing for someone far away".

> এই গানটা বিরহের গান।
>
> *ei gānṭā birôher gān.*
>
> This is a song of longing for someone far away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিরহ** (*birôhô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *viraha*, "parting, separation", especially of lovers (Monier-Williams); a great deal of Bengali song is built on it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
