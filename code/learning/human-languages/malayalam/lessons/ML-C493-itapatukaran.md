## You'll want to know: ഇടപാടുകാരൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C493-VANGAL493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇടപാടുകാരൻ** — *iṭapāṭukāran* — "a customer; a client".

> ആ കടയിൽ എപ്പോഴും ധാരാളം ഇടപാടുകാർ ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *ā kaṭayil eppōḻuṁ dhārāḷaṁ iṭapāṭukār uṇṭŭ.*
>
> That shop has plenty of customers all the time.

Plural: ഇടപാടുകാർ, *iṭapāṭukār*.

ഇടപാട് is a dealing or a transaction; -കാരൻ is the one who has it. Plural: ഇടപാടുകാർ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇടപാടുകാരൻ** (*iṭapāṭukāran*) is built from ഇടപാട്, a dealing or transaction, with -കാരൻ, the one who does it; the origin of ഇടപാട് is not settled.
