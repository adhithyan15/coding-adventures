## You'll want to know: پنجہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C466-LIMB466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پنجہ** — *panjā* — "a paw; an outspread hand".

> بلی نے پنجے سے چوہا پکڑا۔
>
> *billī ne panje se chūhā pakṛā.*
>
> The cat caught the mouse with its paw.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پنجہ** (*panjā*) is usually traced to Persian *panja*, "the hand with its five fingers", built on *panj*, "five".
