## You'll want to know: كذلك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C375-TADADD375-04]; assesses=[] -->

**كذلك** — *kadhālika* — "likewise".

> أختي تحب الموسيقى، وأمي كذلك.
>
> *ukhtī tuḥibbu al-mūsīqā, wa-ummī kadhālika.*
>
> My sister loves music, and so does my mother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كذلك** (*kadhālika*) is built from the native Arabic *ka-*, "like", and *dhālika*, "that": like that, likewise. The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.

## What you've built
