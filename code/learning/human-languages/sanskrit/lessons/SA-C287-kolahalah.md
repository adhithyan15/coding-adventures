## You'll want to know: कोलाहलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C287-ANUDHAVANA287-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कोलाहलः** — *kolāhalaḥ* — "an uproar".

> यदि कोलाहलः अभविष्यत्, तर्हि यामिकः आगमिष्यत्।
>
> *yadi kolāhalaḥ abhaviṣyat, tarhi yāmikaḥ āgamiṣyat.*
>
> If there had been an uproar, then the watchman would have come.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोलाहलः** (*kolāhalaḥ*) comes from the sound itself: MW marks *kolāhala* as onomatopoeic, a loud and confused noise of people or animals (MW).
