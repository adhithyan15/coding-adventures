Urdu, chapter 127: Camel, Fox, Mosquito, Fly, Rabbit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اونٹ (ūṅṭ) — a camel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an ant, then the Urdu for a donkey.

You'll want to know: اونٹ.
اونٹ — ūṅṭ — "a camel".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.
