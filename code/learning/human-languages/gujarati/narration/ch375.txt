Gujarati, chapter 375: What Must Come First.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
આવશ્યક (āvaśyak) — essential.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to keep in check, then the Gujarati for to avert.

You'll want to know: આવશ્યક.
આવશ્યક — āvaśyak — "essential".
જો તમે બેંકમાં ખાતું ખોલશો તો ઓળખપત્ર આવશ્યક છે. jo tame beṅkmā̃ khātũ kholśo to oḷakhpatra āvaśyak che. If you open a bank account, an identity card is essential.

The word, taken apart.
આવશ્યક (āvaśyak) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit āvaśyaka, "necessary, inevitable" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
અનિવાર્ય (anivārya) — unavoidable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to avert, then the Gujarati for essential.

You'll want to know: અનિવાર્ય.
અનિવાર્ય — anivārya — "unavoidable".
જો પેટ્રોલનો ભાવ વધશે તો બસનું ભાડું વધવું અનિવાર્ય છે. jo peṭrolno bhāv vadhśe to basnũ bhāḍũ vadhvũ anivārya che. If the price of petrol goes up, a rise in bus fares is unavoidable.

The word, taken apart.
અનિવાર્ય (anivārya) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit anivārya, "not to be warded off, unavoidable" (Monier-Williams). It is built on the same root as નિવારવું (nivārvũ), "to avert", with a-, "not".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ફરજિયાત (pharjiyāt) — compulsory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for essential, then the Gujarati for unavoidable.

You'll want to know: ફરજિયાત.
ફરજિયાત — pharjiyāt — "compulsory".
જો તમે આ શાળામાં ભણશો તો ગણવેશ ફરજિયાત છે. jo tame ā śāḷāmā̃ bhaṇśo to gaṇveś pharjiyāt che. If you study at this school, the uniform is compulsory.

The word, taken apart.
ફરજિયાત (pharjiyāt) is built on ફરજ (pharaj), "a duty", usually traced to Arabic farḍ, "obligation", with the ending -iyāt: what duty requires.

Lesson 4 of 5.
પતવું (patvũ) — to get done.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for unavoidable, then the Gujarati for compulsory.

You'll want to know: પતવું.
પતવું — patvũ — "to get done".
જો કામ વહેલું પતશે તો આપણે ફિલ્મ જોવા જઈશું. jo kām vahelũ patśe to āpaṇe philm jovā jaīśũ. If the work gets done early, we will go and see a film.
Past (it …): તે કામ પત્યું, te kām patyũ.

The word, taken apart.
પતવું (patvũ) is the intransitive partner of પતાવવું (patāvvũ), "to finish"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be settled", under Sanskrit prāpta, "obtained" (CDIAL 8945).

Lesson 5 of 5.
પૂરું પાડવું (pūrũ pāḍvũ) — to provide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for compulsory, then the Gujarati for to get done.

You'll want to know: પૂરું પાડવું.
પૂરું પાડવું — pūrũ pāḍvũ — "to provide".
જો સરકાર પાણી પૂરું પાડશે તો ખેતરો લીલાં થશે. jo sarkār pāṇī pūrũ pāḍśe to khetro līlā̃ thaśe. If the government supplies water, the fields will turn green.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધું સાધન પૂરું પાડ્યું, teṇe badhũ sādhan pūrũ pāḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
પૂરું પાડવું (pūrũ pāḍvũ) is built from pūrũ, "full", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit pūra, "filling" (CDIAL 8330), and pāḍvũ, "to make fall", the causative of પડવું (paḍvũ), from Sanskrit patati (CDIAL 7722): to make a supply full.
