## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಳಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C346-SPHOTA346-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಳಯ** — *praḷaya* — "a deluge; a catastrophe".

> ಆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಪ್ರಳಯ ಬಂದ ಹಾಗೆ ಅನಿಸಿತು.
>
> *ā rātri praḷaya banda hāge anisitu.*
>
> That night it felt as if the end of the world had come.

In the old stories, the flood that ends the world; in talk, any huge disaster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಳಯ** (*praḷaya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pralaya*, "dissolution, destruction of the world" (CDIAL 8755).
