## You'll want to know: ふりむく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C393-MEJIRUSHI393-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ふりむく** — *furimuku* — "to turn round; to look back".

> **うしろから こえが したので、ふりむきました。** — *ushiro kara koe ga shita node, furimukimashita* — "I heard a voice behind me, so I turned round."

Polite **ふりむきます**, *furimukimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ともだちが ふりむきました。**, *tomodachi ga furimukimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふりむく** (*furimuku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
