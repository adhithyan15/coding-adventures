## You'll want to know: il pizzico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C331-RICETTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il pizzico** — "the pinch".

> Aggiungi un pizzico di sale.
>
> *Add a pinch of salt.*

Built on *pizzicare*, ‘to pinch’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pizzico* is built on *pizzicare* "to pinch" (EtymDB), a verb usually traced to the sound and gesture of pinching.
