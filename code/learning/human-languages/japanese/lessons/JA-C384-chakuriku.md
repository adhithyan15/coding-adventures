## You'll want to know: ちゃくりく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C384-HIKOUJOU384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゃくりく** — *chakuriku* — "landing (of a plane)".

> **ひこうきが くうこうに ちゃくりくしました。** — *hikōki ga kūkō ni chakuriku shimashita* — "The plane landed at the airport."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゃくりく** (*chakuriku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chaku*, "arrive" (Middle Chinese *drjak*) and *riku*, "land" (*ljuwk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
