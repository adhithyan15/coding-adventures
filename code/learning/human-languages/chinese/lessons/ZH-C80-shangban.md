## You'll want to know: shàngbān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shàngbān* — "to go to work".

> *wǒ jiǔ diǎn shàngbān* — I start work at nine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shàngbān* is built from 上 'to go up to' and bān, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangX* and *paen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary explains the second, through an older book, as dividing up jade tallies; a shift of work is a likely step, but it is not settled.
