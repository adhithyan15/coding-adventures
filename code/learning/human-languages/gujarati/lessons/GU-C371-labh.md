## You'll want to know: લાભ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C371-FAYDO371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લાભ** — *lābh* — "an advantage".

> જો તમે આ યોજનામાં જોડાશો તો તમને ઘણો લાભ થશે.
>
> *jo tame ā yojnāmā̃ joḍāśo to tamne ghaṇo lābh thaśe.*
>
> If you join this scheme, you will gain a lot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાભ** (*lābh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *lābha*, "finding, gain" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
