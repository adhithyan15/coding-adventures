Persian, chapter 332: Birth and upbringing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
باردار (bârdâr) — pregnant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to evacuate, then the Persian for to take shelter.

You'll want to know: باردار.
باردار (bârdâr) — "pregnant".
خواهرم پنج ماه است که باردار است. Khâharam panj mâh ast ke bârdâr ast. My sister has been pregnant for five months.
bâr "load" + dâr "having". Hâmele is a synonym of Arabic origin.

The word, taken apart.
bârdâr joins bâr "load, burden" and dâr-, the present stem of dâshtan "to have, to hold": carrying a load. Both parts are native Persian; EtymDB lists no entry.

زایمان (zâymân) او در بیمارستان خوب پیش رفت. Zâymân-e u dar bimârestân khub pish raft. Her delivery at the hospital went well.
From zâ-, the present stem of zâyidan "to give birth".

The word, taken apart.
zâymân is built on zâ-, the present stem of the native verb zâyidan "to give birth", with the noun suffix -mân. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
به دنیا آمدن (be donyâ âmadan) — to be born.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for pregnant, then the Persian for childbirth.

You'll want to know: به دنیا آمدن.
به دنیا آمدن (be donyâ âmadan) — "to be born".
پسرم در یک شب برفی به دنیا آمد. Pesaram dar yek shab-e barfi be donyâ âmad. My son was born on a snowy night.
Literally "to come into the world". Be donyâ âvardan is "to give birth to".

The word, taken apart.
donyâ "world" is borrowed from Arabic dunyā (EtymDB). âmadan "to come" is inherited Persian; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root gʷem- "to step".

Lesson 5 of 5.
تربیت کردن (tarbiyat kardan) — to bring up, to raise (a child).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be born, then the Persian for a newborn baby.

You'll want to know: تربیت کردن.
تربیت کردن (tarbiyat kardan) — "to bring up, to raise (a child)".
مادربزرگم شش بچه را تربیت کرد. Mâdarbozorgam shesh bachche râ tarbiyat kard. My grandmother brought up six children.
It stresses manners and education. Present tarbiyat mikonad. Tarbiyat-e badani is "physical education".

The word, taken apart.
tarbiyat is usually traced to Arabic tarbiya, "upbringing, education"; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.
