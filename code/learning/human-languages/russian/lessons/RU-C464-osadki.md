## You'll want to know: осадки
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C464-PROGNOZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**осадки** (*osádki*) — "precipitation (rain or snow)".

> Сегодня без осадков.
>
> *Sevódnya bez osádkov.*
>
> Today there will be no rain or snow.

Plural; a weather word. In the singular, *осадок* is a sediment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*осадки* is the plural of *осадок* 'a sediment, what settles', from *о-* and *садить* 'to set down'. EtymDB lists *осадки* without a deeper source.
