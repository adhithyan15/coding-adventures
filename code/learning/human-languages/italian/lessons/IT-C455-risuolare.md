## You'll want to know: risuolare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C455-ARTIGIANI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**risuolare** — "to resole".

> Faccio risuolare gli stivali.
>
> *I am having my boots resoled.*

*La suola* — the sole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*risuolare* is built from *ri-* 'again' and *suola* 'sole', which is inherited from Latin *solea* 'sandal, sole', built on *solum* 'ground' (Lewis & Short).
