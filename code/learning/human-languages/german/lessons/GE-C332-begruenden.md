## You'll want to know: begründen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C332-BEGRUENDEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**begründen** — "to give reasons for, to justify; to found".

> Der Lehrer hat seine Entscheidung gut begründet.
>
> *The teacher gave good reasons for his decision.*

*Etwas begründen* — to back it up with reasons. It also means to found: *eine Schule begründen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*begründen* is inherited from Middle High German *begründen* "to found, to make solid" (EtymDB), built on *Grund* "ground, reason", from Proto-Germanic *grunduz* "ground": you put ground under your words.
