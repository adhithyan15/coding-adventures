## You'll want to know: le revenu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C390-BUDGET-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le revenu** — "the income".

> Leur revenu a baissé cette année.
>
> *Their income went down this year.*

Often plural: *les revenus*. From *revenir*: what comes back to you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revenu* is the past participle of *revenir* "to come back" used as a noun: what comes back to you. *revenir* comes from Latin *revenīre*, built on *venīre* "to come" (Lewis & Short).
