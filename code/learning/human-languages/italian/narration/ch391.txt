Italian, chapter 391: Le accuse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
accusare — to accuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for unlawful, then the Italian for unauthorised.

You'll want to know: accusare.
accusare — "to accuse".
Mi accusano di aver preso i soldi, ma non è vero. They accuse me of taking the money, but it is not true.
Accusare qualcuno di qualcosa. Accusare un dolore — to feel a pain.

The word, taken apart.
accusare is inherited from Latin accūsāre "to call to account, to accuse" (EtymDB), built from ad "to" and causa "a case, a lawsuit".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la querela — the criminal complaint, the lawsuit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the accusation, then the Italian for the thief.

You'll want to know: la querela.
la querela — "the criminal complaint, the lawsuit".
Se continua a insultarmi, sporgo querela. If he keeps insulting me, I will take legal action.
Legal Italian: sporgere querela — to lodge a complaint. Stress: querèla.

The word, taken apart.
querela is a learned borrowing from Latin querēla "a complaint, a lament" (Lewis & Short), from querī "to complain".
