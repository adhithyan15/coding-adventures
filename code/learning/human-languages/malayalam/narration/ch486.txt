Malayalam, chapter 486: Embassies and Offices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
എംബസി (embasi) — an embassy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a waiting room, then the Malayalam for a roadside food stall.

You'll want to know: എംബസി.
എംബസി — embasi — "an embassy".
വിസയ്ക്കായി അവൾ എംബസിയിൽ പോയി. visaykkāyi avaḷ embasiyil pōyi. She went to the embassy for a visa.
Each country has one embassy in the capital, and a consulate in some other cities.

The word, taken apart.
എംബസി (embasi) was borrowed from English embassy, the office of a foreign government in the capital.

Lesson 2 of 5.
വകുപ്പ് (vakuppŭ) — a department.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a roadside food stall, then the Malayalam for an embassy.

You'll want to know: വകുപ്പ്.
വകുപ്പ് — vakuppŭ — "a department".
റെയിൽവേ ഒരു വലിയ സർക്കാർ വകുപ്പാണ്. ṟeyilvē oru valiya sarkkār vakuppāṇŭ. The railway is a big government department.
It also means a section of a law: നിയമത്തിന്റെ ഒരു വകുപ്പ് (vakuppŭ).

The word, taken apart.
വകുപ്പ് (vakuppŭ) is native Dravidian, from the root വക, to divide; DEDR gives the Malayalam noun as a section or kind (DEDR 5202).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ശാഖ (śākha) — a branch office; a branch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an embassy, then the Malayalam for a department.

You'll want to know: ശാഖ.
ശാഖ — śākha — "a branch office; a branch".
ആ ബാങ്കിന് ഞങ്ങളുടെ ഗ്രാമത്തിൽ ഒരു ശാഖ ഉണ്ട്. ā bāṅkinŭ ñaṅṅaḷuṭe grāmattil oru śākha uṇṭŭ. That bank has a branch in our village.
In formal Malayalam it is also the branch of a tree; in speech that is a കൊമ്പ്.

The word, taken apart.
ശാഖ (śākha) is borrowed from Sanskrit śākhā, "a branch" of a tree, and so a part of a larger body (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
പ്രവൃത്തിസമയം (pravṛttisamayaṁ) — office hours; working hours.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a department, then the Malayalam for a branch office.

You'll want to know: പ്രവൃത്തിസമയം.
പ്രവൃത്തിസമയം — pravṛttisamayaṁ — "office hours; working hours".
ബാങ്കിന്റെ പ്രവൃത്തിസമയം രാവിലെ പത്ത് മുതൽ നാല് വരെയാണ്. bāṅkinṟe pravṛttisamayaṁ rāvile pattŭ mutal nālŭ vareyāṇŭ. The bank's working hours are from ten in the morning to four.
പ്രവൃത്തി, work, plus സമയം, time. You find it on the board at every office door.

The word, taken apart.
പ്രവൃത്തിസമയം (pravṛttisamayaṁ) is a Malayalam compound built from two words borrowed from Sanskrit, pravṛtti, "moving onwards, activity", and samaya, "time" (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

പ്രവൃത്തിദിനം — pravṛttidinaṁ — "a working day".
ശനിയാഴ്ച ഇവിടെ പ്രവൃത്തിദിനമല്ല. śaniyāḻca iviṭe pravṛttidinamalla. Saturday is not a working day here.
Its opposite is an അവധിദിനം, a holiday.

The word, taken apart.
പ്രവൃത്തിദിനം (pravṛttidinaṁ) is a Malayalam compound built from two words borrowed from Sanskrit, pravṛtti, "activity", and dina, "a day" (Monier-Williams).
