Gujarati, chapter 377: As Things Stand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
દશા (daśā) — a plight; a state of affairs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for slackness, then the Gujarati for to get better.

You'll want to know: દશા.
દશા — daśā — "a plight; a state of affairs".
જો આ વરસે પણ વરસાદ ઓછો પડશે તો ખેતરોની દશા ખરાબ થશે. jo ā varse paṇ varsād ocho paḍśe to khetronī daśā kharāb thaśe. If the rain falls short again this year, the fields will be in a bad way.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દશા (daśā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit daśā, "a state, a condition of life" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for steady, then the Gujarati for normal.

You'll want to know: બદલાવું.
બદલાવું — badlāvũ — "to become different".
જો હવામાન બદલાશે તો અમે ઘરે રહીશું. jo havāmān badlāśe to ame ghare rahīśũ. If the weather changes, we will stay at home.
Past (it …): તે શહેર ઘણું બદલાયું, te śaher ghaṇũ badlāyũ.

The word, taken apart.
બદલાવું (badlāvũ) is the -āvũ partner of બદલવું (badalvũ), "to change", which is usually traced to Arabic badal, "a substitute, an exchange", through Persian.
