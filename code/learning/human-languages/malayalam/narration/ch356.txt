Lesson 1 of 6.
സംഭാഷണം (sambhāṣaṇaṁ) — a conversation; dialogue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to gather, then the Malayalam for to boo.

You'll want to know: സംഭാഷണം.
സംഭാഷണം — sambhāṣaṇaṁ — "a conversation; dialogue".
ആ സിനിമയിലെ സംഭാഷണം വളരെ രസകരമായിരുന്നു. ā sinimayile sambhāṣaṇaṁ vaḷare rasakaramāyirunnu. The dialogue in that film was very interesting.
The written word for a conversation, and the dialogue of a film or a play.

The word, taken apart.
സംഭാഷണം (sambhāṣaṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit sambhāṣaṇa, "conversation, discourse with someone" (Monier-Williams), built on bhāṣ, "to speak", the root of ഭാഷ (bhāṣa), "language".

വിവരണം (vivaraṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit vivaraṇa, "uncovering, laying open; explanation" (Monier-Williams). It is the noun beside വിവരിക്കുക (vivarikkuka), "to describe".

Lesson 4 of 6.
കേട്ടറിയുക (kēṭṭaṟiyuka) — to learn by hearsay; to hear of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a description, then the Malayalam for footage.

You'll want to know: കേട്ടറിയുക.
കേട്ടറിയുക — kēṭṭaṟiyuka — "to learn by hearsay; to hear of".
ആ സംഭവം ഞാൻ കേട്ടറിഞ്ഞു. ā sambhavaṁ ñān kēṭṭaṟiññu. I heard about that incident from others.
Past: കേട്ടറിഞ്ഞു, kēṭṭaṟiññu.
Joined from കേട്ട്, having heard, and അറിയുക, to know: to know something only because you heard it.

The word, taken apart.
കേട്ടറിയുക (kēṭṭaṟiyuka) is a native Dravidian compound of കേട്ട് (kēṭṭŭ), "having heard", from കേൾക്കുക (DEDR 2017), and അറിയുക (aṟiyuka), "to know" (DEDR 314). What you know this way, you did not see yourself.

Lesson 5 of 6.
കെട്ടിച്ചമയ്ക്കുക (keṭṭiccamaykkuka) — to fabricate; to make up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for footage, then the Malayalam for to learn by hearsay.

You'll want to know: കെട്ടിച്ചമയ്ക്കുക.
കെട്ടിച്ചമയ്ക്കുക — keṭṭiccamaykkuka — "to fabricate; to make up".
അവൻ ആ കഥ മുഴുവൻ കെട്ടിച്ചമച്ചു. avan ā katha muḻuvan keṭṭiccamaccu. He made up the whole story.
Past: കെട്ടിച്ചമച്ചു, keṭṭiccamaccu.
Said of false stories and false evidence: കെട്ടിച്ചമച്ച കഥ, a made-up story.

The word, taken apart.
കെട്ടിച്ചമയ്ക്കുക (keṭṭiccamaykkuka) is a native Dravidian compound of കെട്ടുക (keṭṭuka), "to tie, to build" (DEDR 1147), and ചമയ്ക്കുക (camaykkuka), "to make, to put together" (DEDR 2342): a made-up story is tied and built out of nothing.
