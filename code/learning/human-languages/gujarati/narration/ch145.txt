Gujarati, chapter 145: Saying not.
8 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
તે ગુજરાતી બોલે છે — the present, for someone else.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to change, the last verb of the A1 words. Then the first sentence you ever built about yourself.
[your turn — recall: badalvũ, then hũ gujarātī bolũ chũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
That sentence has three working pieces: the stem બોલ- (speak), the ending -ું that says I, and છું, the copula that agrees with I. Change the person and two of the three pieces move.

Grammar Lens: one ending for he, she and તું.
તે ગુજરાતી બોલે છે. — te gujarātī bole che. — He speaks Gujarati. She speaks Gujarati.
The stem stays બોલ-. The person ending becomes -ે, and the copula becomes છે, the commonest word in this book. The familiar you takes the very same pair:
તું ગુજરાતી બોલે છે. — tũ gujarātī bole che. — You speak Gujarati.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
who: હું. verb: બોલું. copula: છું.
who: તું, તે. verb: બોલે. copula: છે.
તું and તે share one verb form, so only the pronoun in front tells them apart. જવું bends its stem a little and keeps the idea: તે જાય છે, she goes.

[your turn — say: hũ gujarātī bolũ chũ, then te gujarātī bole che]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: તે વાંચે છે — te vā̃ce che, she reads]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: તે અહીં રહે છે — te ahī̃ rahe che, he lives here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the last two again to a friend, with તું in front]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which two pieces change when I speak becomes she speaks? (The ending -ું becomes -ે, and છું becomes છે.) Which pronoun shares her verb form? (તું, the familiar you.)


Lesson 2 of 8.
તેઓ (teo) — they.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say she speaks Gujarati. Then the pronoun on its own, તે, the word for anyone who is not here.

You'll want to know: તેઓ.
તેઓ (teo) — "they".
It is તે with -ઓ (o) on the end. તે names one person who is not here; તેઓ (teo) names more than one.
તેઓ ગુજરાતી બોલે છે. — teo gujarātī bole che. — They speak Gujarati.
The verb does not move: બોલે છે serves તેઓ (teo) exactly as it serves તે. In the present, he, she, familiar you and they all meet on -ે છે.
તેઓ (teo) does a second job. Said of one person, it is a respectful he or she: a teacher, a grandparent, a guest. So તેઓ (teo) અહીં છે is they are here, or he is here, said with respect.

The word, taken apart.
તેઓ (teo) is તે plus -ઓ (o), the ending Gujarati nouns take for "more than one". તે continues the old Sanskrit demonstrative stem ta-, whose plural te already meant "they" (CDIAL 5612).

What you've built.
One more pronoun, and the present now has four people on one ending.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: teo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: તેઓ અહીં રહે છે — teo ahī̃ rahe che, they live here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: te, then teo: one person, then more than one]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does તેઓ (teo) mean? (They, or a respectful he or she.) Which letter at its end have you read but never written? (ઓ (o), the next lesson.)


Lesson 3 of 8.
ઓ — the standing vowel o.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Script — meet one form, Writing — recall after hiding and Wrap-up Recall.

Warm-up.
[your turn — recall: say they — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the standing vowel આ, and the sign ો that gives a consonant its o]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
તેઓ (teo) ends on a vowel that stands alone. After a consonant, o is a sign: તો, જો. At the start of a syllable there is no consonant to carry it, so o needs a letter of its own.

You'll want to know: આ with an arc above.
ઓ (o) is built on આ. Keep the whole of આ, the body and its stems on the right, and set one high arc over the top.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
letter: આ. sound: ā. what it is made of: the body and its stems.
letter: ઓ. sound: o. what it is made of: આ, then a high arc.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — meet one form.
ઓ — o.
Keep ઓ (o) visible. Write the joined body of આ first: the open curve on the left, the broad lower body, the small shoulder and arch. Lift, and bring the first stem down the right side to its foot. Lift again and bring the trailing stem down beside it. Lift a last time and sweep the high arc from left to right over the top. Four movements, three lifts.
The arc sits high and clear of the stems. Without it the letter reads આ.

Writing — notice and trace.
Finger-trace ઓ (o) once, then air-trace it once. Notice the body of આ, the two stems, and the arc that rides above them.

Writing — copy with the model.
Copy visible ઓ (o) once. Compare only two things: are both stems there, and does the arc clear their tops? Repair one landmark if needed.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Writing — recall after hiding.
Hide the model and write the standing vowel o once. Uncover ઓ, compare, and stop after one repair.
[once you have stopped driving — question: Hide the model and write the standing vowel o from memory.]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read આ ઓ (o) તેઓ (teo) aloud. What turns આ into ઓ (o)? (The high arc.)

Lesson 4 of 8.
અમે ગુજરાતી બોલીએ છીએ — the present for we, and for you.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say they speak Gujarati. Then the two words for we: અમે, which leaves the listener out, and આપણે, which takes the listener in.
[your turn — recall: teo gujarātī bole che; ame, āpṇe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — write: ઓ (o) once from memory]

Grammar Lens: we.
અમે ગુજરાતી બોલીએ છીએ. — ame gujarātī bolīe chīe. — We speak Gujarati.
The ending is -ીએ, and the copula has a form you have not met before: છીએ (chīe). Both finish on the same -ીએ, so the sentence chimes. આપણે takes the same pair: આપણે ગુજરાતી બોલીએ છીએ, we (you and I) speak Gujarati.

You'll want to know: respectful you.
તમે કેમ છો? has carried છો since the second day. The verb beside તમે takes the matching -ો:
તમે ગુજરાતી બોલો છો. — tame gujarātī bolo cho. — You speak Gujarati.
The present endings for one person:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
who: હું. verb: બોલું. copula: છું.
who: તું, તે. verb: બોલે. copula: છે.
And for more than one, or for respect:
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
who: અમે, આપણે. verb: બોલીએ. copula: છીએ.
who: તમે. verb: બોલો. copula: છો.
who: તેઓ (teo). verb: બોલે. copula: છે.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: તમે ક્યાં રહો છો? — tame kyā̃ raho cho?, where do you live?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 5 of 8.
હું નથી બોલતો — saying not about what people do.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say we speak Gujarati and you speak Gujarati. Then the repair sentence from long ago: સમજતો નથી, I do not understand.
That sentence already holds the whole negative. Here is how it is built.

Grammar Lens: નથી takes the copula's place.
હું ગુજરાતી નથી બોલતો. — hũ gujarātī nathī bolto. — I don't speak Gujarati.
Two changes, and only two:
છું goes, and નથી comes in. નથી is is not worn into one word, and it is the same for every person: હું નથી, તે નથી, અમે નથી.
The verb drops its person ending and takes -તો, the shape સમજતો has had all along.
-તો is the man's form. A woman says હું ગુજરાતી નથી બોલતી, with -તી. A group, or a respectful you, take -તા: અમે નથી બોલતા, તમે નથી બોલતા.
નથી may also stand after the verb, as it does in સમજતો નથી. Both orders are everyday Gujarati: હું ગુજરાતી બોલતો નથી says the same thing.

Grammar Lens: when there is no other verb.
With the copula alone, નથી replaces છે and nothing else moves: તે અહીં નથી, she is not here. That was the first job નથી ever did for you.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: hũ cā nathī pīto, or pītī, I don't drink tea]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: તે ગુજરાતી નથી વાંચતી — te gujarātī nathī vā̃ctī, she doesn't read Gujarati]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: અમે અહીં નથી રહેતા — ame ahī̃ nathī rahetā, we don't live here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say that you don't drink tea, then that she isn't here.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word replaces the copula? (નથી, the same for every person.) What ending does the verb take? (-તો, -તી or -તા: a man, a woman, or more than one.)


Lesson 6 of 8.
ન and નહીં — the two smaller nots.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I don't drink tea. Then the reply you have given since the first chapters to thanks or to an apology: વાંધો નહીં, no problem. And the word for worry: ચિંતા.

Grammar Lens: ન in front of a request.
નથી denies a statement. A request is not a statement, so it takes the bare ન (na) in front of the verb:
ચિંતા ન કરો. — cintā na karo. — Don't worry.
કરો is the polite do! that માફ કરો has carried since the courtesy chapter. Put ન in front of it and the request becomes a request not to.

You'll want to know: નહીં, for one word.
નહીં (nahī̃) cancels a word or a phrase rather than a whole sentence:
ચા નહીં, પાણી. — cā nahī̃, pāṇī. — Not tea: water. હમણાં નહીં. — hamṇā̃ nahī̃. — Not now.
વાંધો નહીં is built this way: objection — none.
Three negatives, three jobs:
નથી — a statement: it is not so;
ન — a request: don't;
નહીં — one word: not this.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: cintā na karo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ચા નહીં, દૂધ — cā nahī̃, dūdh, not tea: milk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hamṇā̃ nahī̃]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — choose: નથી, ન or નહીં for she isn't here (નથી: a statement)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which negative goes in front of કરો? (ન.) Which one cancels a single word? (નહીં.)


Lesson 7 of 8.
કોઈ નથી — nobody, nothing, never.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say don't worry, then not now. And two words from the chapters on this and that: કોઈ, someone, and કંઈક, something.

Grammar Lens: કોઈ with નથી.
અહીં કોઈ નથી. — ahī̃ koī nathī. — There is nobody here.
Gujarati needs no separate word for nobody. It says someone is-not: કોઈ, then નથી.

You'll want to know: કંઈ and ક્યારેય.
કંઈક is કંઈ with an extra -ક. Before a negative, Gujarati uses the shorter કંઈ (kaī̃):
હું કંઈ નથી ખાતો. — hũ kaī̃ nathī khāto. — I'm not eating anything.
ક્યારેય (kyārey) is ક્યારે, when, with -ય, ever, on the end. With નથી it means never:
તે ક્યારેય ચા નથી પીતી. — te kyārey cā nathī pītī. — She never drinks tea.
The shape is the same three times: an any word, then નથી.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ahī̃ koī nathī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hũ kaī̃ nathī khāto, or khātī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: હું ક્યારેય દૂધ નથી પીતો — hũ kyārey dūdh nathī pīto, I never drink milk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does કોઈ નથી say, word for word? (Someone is-not: nobody.) What does -ય add to ક્યારે? (Ever; with નથી, never.) What does કંઈક become before a negative? (કંઈ.)
