## You'll want to know: giustificare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C353-REAZIONE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**giustificare** — "to justify".

> Niente può giustificare un comportamento così.
>
> *Nothing can justify behaviour like that.*

*Giustificarsi* — to justify yourself. At school, *giustificare un ritardo* — to give a reason for being late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*giustificare* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *iūstificāre* "to deal justly with, to justify" (Lewis & Short), built from *iūstus* "right, fair" and *facere* "to make".
