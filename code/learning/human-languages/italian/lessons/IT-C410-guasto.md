## You'll want to know: il guasto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C410-GUASTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il guasto** — "the breakdown, the fault".

> C'è un guasto al motore.
>
> *There is a fault in the engine.*

As an adjective, *guasto* means out of order: *il telefono è guasto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guasto* is built on *guastare* 'to spoil', which is inherited from Latin *vāstāre* 'to lay waste' (EtymDB).
