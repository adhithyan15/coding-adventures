# ছটফট করা (chhôṭphôṭ kôrā) — to fidget; to toss and turn

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C442-UDBEG442-03, BN-LEX-C442-UDBEG442-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for restless, then the Bengali for free of worry.

## You'll want to know: ছটফট করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C442-UDBEG442-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ছটফট করা** — *chhôṭphôṭ kôrā* — "to fidget; to toss and turn".

> জ্বরে ছেলেটা সারারাত ছটফট করল।
>
> *jbôre chheleṭā sārārāt chhôṭphôṭ kôrlô.*
>
> The boy tossed and turned all night with fever.

Past (he/she …): তিনি ব্যথায় ছটফট করলেন, *tini bæthāy chhôṭphôṭ kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছটফট করা** (*chhôṭphôṭ kôrā*) is *chhôṭphôṭ*, an expressive word that paints quick, restless movement, with *kôrā*, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
