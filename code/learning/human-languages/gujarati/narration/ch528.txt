Lesson 1 of 5.
યાદી (yādī) — a list.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to throw down, then the Gujarati for to tire out (an opponent).

You'll want to know: યાદી.
યાદી — yādī — "a list".
જો તમે યાદી બનાવશો તો તમે કશું નહીં ભૂલો. jo tame yādī banāvśo to tame kaśũ nahī̃ bhūlo. If you make a list, you won't forget anything.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
યાદી (yādī) is usually traced to Persian yād, "memory", with the ending -ī: something written down to remember by.

Lesson 3 of 5.
વ્યવસ્થા (vyavasthā) — an arrangement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a list, then the Gujarati for a budget.

You'll want to know: વ્યવસ્થા.
વ્યવસ્થા — vyavasthā — "an arrangement".
જો મહેમાનો વધારે હશે તો આપણે જમવાની વ્યવસ્થા બદલવી પડશે. jo mahemāno vadhāre haśe to āpaṇe jamvānī vyavasthā badalvī paḍśe. If there are more guests, we will have to change the dinner arrangements.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વ્યવસ્થા (vyavasthā) is a tatsama, from Sanskrit vy-ava-sthā, "to stand apart, to be settled" (Monier-Williams): things set each in its place.

Lesson 5 of 5.
યોજવું (yojvũ) — to organise (an event).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an arrangement, then the Gujarati for a diary.

You'll want to know: યોજવું.
યોજવું — yojvũ — "to organise (an event)".
જો વરસાદ નહીં પડે તો અમે બગીચામાં મેળો યોજીશું. jo varsād nahī̃ paḍe to ame bagīcāmā̃ meḷo yojīśũ. If it doesn't rain, we will hold a fair in the garden.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મેળો યોજ્યો, teṇe meḷo yojyo.

The word, taken apart.
યોજવું (yojvũ) is a learned verb built on Sanskrit yojana, "joining, arranging" (Monier-Williams), the word behind યોજના, a plan.
