Bengali, chapter 521: Bat and Ball.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ব্যাট (bæṭ) — a cricket bat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a contestant, then the Bengali for to lead to victory.

You'll want to know: ব্যাট.
ব্যাট — bæṭ — "a cricket bat".
আমার নতুন ব্যাটটা ভারী। Āmār nôtun bæṭṭā bhārī. My new bat is heavy.

The word, taken apart.
ব্যাট (bæṭ) is borrowed from English bat.

Lesson 5 of 5.
পেটানো (peṭāno) — to beat; to hit hard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a run in cricket, then the Bengali for a catch in cricket.

You'll want to know: পেটানো.
পেটানো — peṭāno — "to beat; to hit hard".
অধিনায়ক বলটা মাঠের বাইরে পেটাল। Ôdhināyôk bôlṭā māṭher bāire peṭālô. The captain hit the ball right out of the ground.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বলটা পেটালেন, tini bôlṭā peṭālen.

The word, taken apart.
পেটানো (peṭāno) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali piṭā, "to beat", under Sanskrit piṭṭayati, "stamps into a solid mass" (CDIAL 8165).
