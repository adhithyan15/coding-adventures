## You'll want to know: चटई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C523-GALICHA523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चटई** — *caṭaī* — "a mat".

> मुलं चटईवर बसून जेवली.
>
> *mulaṁ caṭaīvar basūn jevlī.*
>
> The children sat on the mat and ate.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चटई** (*caṭaī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *caṭāī*, "matting", under *\*caṭṭa*, "bamboo matting" (CDIAL 4574).
