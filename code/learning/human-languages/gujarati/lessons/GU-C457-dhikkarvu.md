# ધિક્કારવું (dhikkārvũ) — to despise

## You'll want to know: ધિક્કારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C457-NAFRAT457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ધિક્કારવું** — *dhikkārvũ* — "to despise".

> બધા સ્વાર્થી માણસને ધિક્કારે છે.
>
> *badhā svārthī māṇasne dhikkāre che.*
>
> Everyone despises a selfish person.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સ્વાર્થી માણસને ધિક્કાર્યો, *teṇe svārthī māṇasne dhikkāryo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધિક્કારવું** (*dhikkārvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhikkāra*, "reproach", built on *dhik*, "fie!"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 6808).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
