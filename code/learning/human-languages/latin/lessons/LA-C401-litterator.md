## You'll want to know: litterātor, litterātōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C401-PRAECEPTOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**litterātor, litterātōris** — "an elementary teacher of letters".

> Litterātor puerōs legere et scrībere docet.
>
> *The elementary teacher teaches the boys to read and write.*

*litterātor, litterātōris* m., built on *littera*, a letter. He was also called *lūdī magister*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*litterātor* is built on *littera* "a letter" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tor* of the one who does it.
