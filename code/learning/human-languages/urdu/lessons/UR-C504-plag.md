## You'll want to know: پلگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C504-SVICH504-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پلگ** — *plag* — "a plug (on a cable)".

> اس چارجر کا پلگ کہاں ہے؟
>
> *is chārjar kā plag kahāṅ hai?*
>
> Where is the plug for this charger?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پلگ** (*plag*) is borrowed from English *plug*.
