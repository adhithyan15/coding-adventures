Russian, chapter 449: A haircut.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
парикмахерская (parikmákherskaya) — a hairdresser's, a barber's.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to sew up, then the Russian for to sew on.

You'll want to know: парикмахерская.
парикмахерская (parikmákherskaya) — "a hairdresser's, a barber's".
Парикмахерская работает до восьми. Parikmákherskaya rabótayet do vosmí. The hairdresser's is open until eight.
An adjective used as a noun.

The word, taken apart.
парикмахерская (parikmákherskaya) is built on парикмахер 'a hairdresser' with an adjective ending; парикмахер was borrowed from German Perückenmacher 'a wig-maker' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.

стрижка (strízhka) — "a haircut".
Тебе идёт новая стрижка. Tebé idyót nóvaya strízhka. The new haircut suits you.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
стрижка (strízhka) is built on стричь (strich) 'to cut hair, to shear' with -ка. Стричь is inherited from Proto-Slavic striťi (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
стричь (strich) — to cut (hair), to trim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a hairdresser's, then the Russian for a haircut.

You'll want to know: стричь.
стричь (strich) — "to cut (hair), to trim".
Мастер стрижёт меня уже пять лет. Máster strizhyót menyá uzhé pyat let. The hairdresser has been cutting my hair for five years now.
Imperfective; the perfective is постричь. Present стригу, стрижёшь, стригут; past стриг. Стричься — to have one's hair cut.

The word, taken apart.
стричь (strich) is inherited from Proto-Slavic striťi (EtymDB), which EtymDB takes back to a PIE root streyg-.

бриться (brítsya) — "to shave".
Отец бреется каждое утро. Otéts bréyetsya kázhdoye útro. Father shaves every morning.
Imperfective; perfective побриться. Present бреюсь, бреешься.

The word, taken apart.
бриться (brítsya) is брить 'to shave' with -ся 'oneself'. Брить is inherited from Proto-Slavic briti (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
чёлка (chyólka) — a fringe (of hair), bangs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to cut, then the Russian for to shave.

You'll want to know: чёлка.
чёлка (chyólka) — "a fringe (of hair), bangs".
У девочки длинная чёлка. U dévochki dlínnaya chyólka. The girl has a long fringe.
Feminine. In American English, bangs.

The word, taken apart.
чёлка (chyólka) is usually derived from чело 'a brow', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic čelo 'a forehead' (EtymDB): the hair that falls on the brow. EtymDB does not list чёлка (chyólka) itself.
