Punjabi, chapter 78: Bird, Mouse, Lion, Elephant, Monkey.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪੰਛੀ (panchhī) — a bird.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a goat, then the Punjabi for a sheep.

You'll want to know: ਪੰਛੀ.
ਪੰਛੀ — panchhī — "a bird".

The word, taken apart.
ਪੰਛੀ (panchhī) goes back to Sanskrit pakṣin, "winged; bird". CDIAL lists the Punjabi form paṅkhī under it (CDIAL 7636); panchhī is the same word with chh for the kṣ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਚੂਹਾ (chūhā) — a mouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a sheep, then the Punjabi for a bird.

You'll want to know: ਚੂਹਾ.
ਚੂਹਾ — chūhā — "a mouse".

The word, taken apart.
ਚੂਹਾ (chūhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cūha, "rat, mouse", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4899).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਹਾਥੀ (hāthī) — an elephant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a mouse, then the Punjabi for a lion.

You'll want to know: ਹਾਥੀ.
ਹਾਥੀ — hāthī — "an elephant".

The word, taken apart.
ਹਾਥੀ (hāthī) matches Hindi hāthī, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit hastin, "having hands; elephant" (CDIAL 14039). CDIAL wonders whether hastin remade an older, perhaps non-Aryan word for the animal, linked by folk etymology with kara, "hand".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਬਾਂਦਰ (bāndar) — a monkey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਬਾਂਦਰ.
ਬਾਂਦਰ — bāndar — "a monkey".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists ਬਾਂਦਰ (bāndar), from the Punjabi of Ludhiana, with Hindi bāṁdar, under Sanskrit vānara, "monkey", a word it derives from vana, "forest" (CDIAL 11515).
