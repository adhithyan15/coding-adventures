# થયું — *it happened*

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-GAYO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-AAVYO-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-ANAND-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I went*, then *she came*. And the greeting from the second day:
**તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો**, *pleased to meet you*.

## Grammar Lens: the past that was there from the start
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-THAYO-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-ANAND-01, GU-LEX-C50-FEEL-04, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-DATIVE-SG-01] -->

**થયો** is the past of **થવું**, *to happen, to become*. In **તમને મળીને આનંદ
થયો** the joy is the subject (*to you, meeting, joy happened*), and **આનંદ** is
masculine, so the verb says **થયો**.

Change the subject and the ending follows it:

- **આનંદ થયો** — joy happened: masculine;
- **મને ચિંતા થઈ** — worry happened to me, *I got worried*: feminine;
- **કામ થયું** — the work got done: neuter;
- **તેઓ ખુશ થયા** — they became happy: more than one.

**થયો** goes back to Sanskrit *sthita*, "standing", which CDIAL lists as a past
of *to be* (CDIAL 13768).
