Lesson 1 of 5.
o dispositivo — the device.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the password, then the Portuguese for the profile.

You'll want to know: o dispositivo.
o dispositivo — "the device".
Posso usar a aplicação em três dispositivos. I can use the app on three devices.
Dispositivo móvel — mobile device.

The word, taken apart.
dispositivo is a learned formation on Latin dispositus "arranged", the participle of dispōnere "to set in order", on pōnere "to put" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
instalar — to install.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the device, then the Portuguese for the laptop.

You'll want to know: instalar.
instalar — "to install".
Instalei um jogo novo no portátil. I installed a new game on the laptop.
Also to settle in: instalar-se numa casa nova.

The word, taken apart.
instalar was borrowed from French installer (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Medieval Latin installāre "to put into a stall (a seat in church)", built on a Germanic word for "standing place" (EtymDB lists its cousins).

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: atualizar.
atualizar — "to update".
O telemóvel está a atualizar. The phone is updating.
AO90 atualizar; older actualizar. A atualização — the update.

The word, taken apart.
atualizar is built on atual "current", from Late Latin āctuālis "active, practical", on agere "to do" (Lewis & Short).
