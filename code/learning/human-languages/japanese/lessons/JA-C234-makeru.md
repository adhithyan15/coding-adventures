## You'll want to know: まける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C234-MATCH234-03]; assesses=[] -->

**まける** — *makeru* — "to be beaten; to lose (a game)".

> **せんしゅうの しあいで まけました。** — *senshū no shiai de makemashita* — "We lost last week's match."

Polite **まけます**, *makemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まける** (*makeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
