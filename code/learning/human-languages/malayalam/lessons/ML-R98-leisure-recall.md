## Grammar Lens: the vowel at the end chooses
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C98-GAME-01, ML-LEX-C98-CINEMA-01, ML-LEX-C60-ANIMAL-01, ML-LEX-C97-BEAUTY-01, ML-LEX-AANU-01] -->

**ആണ്** opens with a vowel. A word that also ends in one cannot sit straight
against it, so a letter comes in between. **Which letter is not free** — the
word's own last vowel settles it:

| ends in | word | joined to **ആണ്** |
|---|---|---|
| **ഇ** | **ഭംഗി** | **ഭംഗിയാണ്** |
| **ഇ** | **കളി** | **കളിയാണ്** |
| **അ** | **സിനിമ** | **സിനിമയാണ്** |
| **ഉ** | **പശു** | **പശുവാണ്** |

**Three rows out of four take യ, and that is exactly why the fourth is worth
holding on to.** If you carry away *vowels take യ*, the cow will catch you out.
What you want is *the last vowel decides* — and **ഇ** and **അ** happen to decide
the same way, while **ഉ** does not.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C98-GAME-01, ML-LEX-C98-PLAY-01, ML-CONCEPT-C33-VAAYIKKUKA-01, ML-LEX-NJAAN-01] -->

**കളി** is the game. **കളിക്കുക** is doing it — the noun still visible inside
the verb, wearing an ending you had long before this chapter:

> **ഞാൻ വായിക്കുന്നു.** — I read.
>
> **ഞാൻ കളിക്കുന്നു.** — I play.

Two lines built the same way, and only one of them was ever taught to you as a
pattern. The other you assembled.
