## You'll want to know: संभाव्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C382-SHAKYA382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संभाव्य** — *sambhāvya* — "likely; potential".

> संभाव्य धोका लक्षात घेऊन शाळा बंद ठेवल्या.
>
> *sambhāvya dhokā lakṣāt gheūn śāḷā band ṭhevalyā.*
>
> Keeping the possible danger in mind, the schools were kept closed.

Invariable; संभाव्यता, probability.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संभाव्य** (*sambhāvya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sambhāvya*, "possible, probable" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
