## You'll want to know: yǒuhǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C258-KIND258-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒuhǎo* — "friendly".

> *zhèr de rén hěn yǒuhǎo* — People here are very friendly.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: yǒu is said with a rising tone before hǎo, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒuhǎo* is built from *yǒu* 'a friend' and 好 'good', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuwX* and *xawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a friend'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
