# કંડક્ટર (kanḍakṭar) — a bus conductor

## You'll want to know: કંડક્ટર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C402-CART402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કંડક્ટર** — *kanḍakṭar* — "a bus conductor".

> બસના કંડક્ટરે મને ટિકિટ આપી.
>
> *basnā kanḍakṭare mane ṭikiṭ āpī.*
>
> The bus conductor gave me a ticket.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કંડક્ટર** (*kanḍakṭar*) is borrowed from English *conductor*, the one who sells tickets on a bus.
