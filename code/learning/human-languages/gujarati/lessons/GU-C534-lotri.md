## You'll want to know: લોટરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C534-JUGAR534-04]; assesses=[] -->

**લોટરી** — *loṭrī* — "a lottery".

> જો મને લોટરી લાગશે તો હું મારી માતા માટે નવું ઘર ખરીદીશ.
>
> *jo mane loṭrī lāgśe to hũ mārī mātā māṭe navũ ghar kharīdīś.*
>
> If I win the lottery, I'll buy a new house for my mother.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લોટરી** (*loṭrī*) is borrowed from English *lottery*. A lottery "strikes" the winner: **મને લોટરી લાગી**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
