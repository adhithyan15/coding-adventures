## You'll want to know: भिजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C438-VALIV438-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भिजणे** — *bhijṇe* — "to get wet".

> छत्री नव्हती म्हणून मी भिजलो.
>
> *chatrī navhatī mhaṇūn mī bhijlo.*
>
> I had no umbrella, so I got wet.

Past (he …): तो भिजला, *to bhijlā*.

A woman says मी भिजले; ओलाचिंब भिजणे, to get soaked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भिजणे** (*bhijṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *bhij̈ṇẽ*, "to be wet", under *\*bhiyajyate*, "is smeared, gets wet" (CDIAL 9502).
