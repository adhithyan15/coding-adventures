German, chapter 481: Allergien und Ausschlag.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
die Allergie — allergy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for dizzy, then the German for circulation.

You'll want to know: die Allergie.
die Allergie — "allergy".
Meine Tochter hat eine Allergie gegen Nüsse. My daughter has an allergy to nuts.
Plural: die Allergien. Allergie gegen + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
Allergie is a modern learned word built from Greek állos "other" and érgon "work": the body reacts in another way. EtymDB lists the German word without a source.

Lesson 3 of 6.
der Ausschlag — rash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for allergy, then the German for allergic.

You'll want to know: der Ausschlag.
der Ausschlag — "rash".
Nach der neuen Creme bekam sie einen Ausschlag. After the new cream she got a rash.
Plural: die Ausschläge. Den Ausschlag geben — to tip the balance.

The word, taken apart.
Ausschlag is built on aus "out" and Schlag "blow", inherited from Old High German slag, from Proto-Germanic slagiz (EtymDB): something that breaks out on the skin.

Lesson 5 of 6.
eincremen — to put cream on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for rash, then the German for to scratch.

You'll want to know: eincremen.
eincremen — "to put cream on".
Bevor wir an den Strand gehen, cremen wir die Kinder gut ein. Before the beach we put plenty of cream on the children.
Separable: ich creme ein. Also reflexive: sich eincremen.

The word, taken apart.
eincremen is built on ein "in" and Creme, which was borrowed from French crème "cream" (EtymDB); EtymDB has no entry for the verb.
