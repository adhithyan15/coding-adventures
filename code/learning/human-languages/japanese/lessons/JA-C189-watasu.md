## You'll want to know: わたす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C189-PARCEL189-04]; assesses=[] -->

**わたす** — *watasu* — "to hand over; to pass, to give".

> **てんいんに おかねを わたします。** — *ten'in ni okane o watashimasu* — "I hand the money to the shop assistant."

Polite **わたします**, *watashimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わたす** (*watasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
