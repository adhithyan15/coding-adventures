## You'll want to know: पितरौ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C501-SAMBANDHA501-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पितरौ** — *pitarau* — "parents".

> अहं प्रतिदिनं पितरौ नमामि।
>
> *ahaṃ pratidinaṃ pitarau namāmi.*
>
> Every day I bow to my parents.

A masculine noun, used in the dual.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पितरौ** (*pitarau*) is built on *pitṛ*, "father" (**पिता**): MW gives its dual *pitarau*, "father and mother, parents" (MW).
