## You'll want to know: el huérfano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C749-EDADES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el huérfano** — "the orphan".

> Se quedó huérfano a los diez años.
>
> *He was orphaned at ten.*

*Quedarse huérfano* — to be orphaned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huérfano* is usually traced to Late Latin *orphanus*, borrowed from Greek *orphanós* "bereft".
