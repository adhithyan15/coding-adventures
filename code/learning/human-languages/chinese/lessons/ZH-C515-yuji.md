## You'll want to know: 雨季
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C515-SEASONS515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**雨季** — *yǔjì* — "rainy season".

> **我最不喜欢雨季** *wǒ zuì bù xǐhuan yǔjì* — I like the rainy season least of all.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**雨季** (*yǔjì*) is built from 雨 'rain' and 季 'a season', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuX* and *kjwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as the youngest of brothers, so its sense 'season' is not given there.
