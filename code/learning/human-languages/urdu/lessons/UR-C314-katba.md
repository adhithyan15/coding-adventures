## You'll want to know: کتبہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C314-KHANDAR314-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کتبہ** — *katbā* — "an inscription".

> پتھر پر ایک کتبہ تھا۔
>
> *patthar par ek katbā thā.*
>
> There was an inscription on the stone.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کتبہ** (*katbā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *katba*, "a piece of writing". It is writing cut into stone or metal.
