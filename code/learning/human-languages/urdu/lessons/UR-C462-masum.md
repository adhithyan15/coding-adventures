## You'll want to know: معصوم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C462-SENSE462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**معصوم** — *māsūm* — "innocent".

> بچے کا چہرہ بڑا معصوم ہے۔
>
> *bachche kā chehrā baṛā māsūm hai.*
>
> The child has a very innocent face.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معصوم** (*māsūm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maʿṣūm*, "kept safe from wrongdoing".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
