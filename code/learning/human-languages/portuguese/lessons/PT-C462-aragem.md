## You'll want to know: a aragem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C462-CALOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a aragem** — "the light breeze".

> À tarde corre uma aragem fresca.
>
> *In the afternoon there is a cool breeze.*

Softer than *o vento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aragem* is built on *ar* 'air' with *-agem* (EtymDB); *ar* is inherited from Latin *āēr*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
