## You'll want to know: सूत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C282-CHARKHO282-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सूत** — *sūt* — "yarn".

> यो सूत लाल है।
>
> *yo sūt lāl hai.*
>
> This yarn is red.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूत** (*sūt*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sūtra*, "thread, cord"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *sūt*, "thread" (CDIAL 13561).
