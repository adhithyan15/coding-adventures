Marathi, chapter 302: In a Split Second.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दचकणे (dacakṇe) — to be startled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to peep, then the Marathi for to observe closely.

You'll want to know: दचकणे.
दचकणे — dacakṇe — "to be startled".
दार अचानक वाजलं आणि मी दचकलो. dār acānak vājlaṁ āṇi mī dacaklo. The door suddenly banged and I started.
Past (he …): तो दचकला, to dacaklā.
Intransitive: आवाजाने दचकणे (dacakṇe), to start at a sound; a woman says मी दचकले. The letter ऋ opens ऋतू, a season.

The word, taken apart.
दचकणे (dacakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi dacakṇe, beside dackā, 'fear', under dacc, 'fear' (CDIAL 6126).

थबकणे — thabakṇe — "to stop short".
रस्त्यात साप दिसल्यावर ती थबकली. rastyāt sāp disalyāvar tī thabaklī. When she saw a snake on the road, she stopped short.
Past (he …): तो थबकला, to thabaklā.
Intransitive: थबकून उभं राहणे, to stand stock-still.

The word, taken apart.
थबकणे (thabakṇe) is a Marathi verb of stopping short; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
क्षण (kṣaṇ) — a moment; an instant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be startled, then the Marathi for to stop short.

You'll want to know: क्षण.
क्षण — kṣaṇ — "a moment; an instant".
एका क्षणात सगळं बदललं. ekā kṣaṇāt sagḷaṁ badallaṁ. Everything changed in a moment.
Masculine.
क्षणभर, for a moment; त्या क्षणी, at that moment.

The word, taken apart.
क्षण (kṣaṇ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kṣaṇa, 'an instant, a moment' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
तेवढ्यात (tevaḍhyāt) — at that very moment; meanwhile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to stop short, then the Marathi for a moment.

You'll want to know: तेवढ्यात.
तेवढ्यात — tevaḍhyāt — "at that very moment; meanwhile".
मी निघणार होतो, तेवढ्यात पाऊस सुरू झाला. mī nighṇār hoto, tevaḍhyāt pāūs surū jhālā. I was about to leave when, at that very moment, it started to rain.
Built on तेवढा, that much, with -त: in that much time.

The word, taken apart.
तेवढ्यात (tevaḍhyāt) is built from tevaḍhā, 'that much', with the locative ending -t: within that much time. The origin of tevaḍhā is not settled.
