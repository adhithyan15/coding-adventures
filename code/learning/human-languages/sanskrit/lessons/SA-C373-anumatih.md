## You'll want to know: अनुमतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C373-ANUMATI373-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमतिः** — *anumatiḥ* — "permission".

> यदि पिता अनुमतिं ददाति, तर्हि अहं गमिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi pitā anumatiṃ dadāti, tarhi ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi.*
>
> If father gives permission, I will go.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमतिः** (*anumatiḥ*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √man, "to think"; MW gives *anu-mati* "assent, permission, approbation" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
