## You'll want to know: सेवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C358-SEVA358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सेवा** — *sevā* — "service (to others)".

> वह बीमारों की सेवा करती है।
>
> *vah bīmāroṁ kī sevā kartī hai.*
>
> She looks after the sick.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेवा** (*sevā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sevā*, "service, attendance" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
