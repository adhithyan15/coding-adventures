## You'll want to know: اصلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C447-NABSH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اصلی** (*asli*) — "main, principal".

> این خیابان اصلی شهر است.
>
> *In khiyâbân-e asli-ye shahr ast.*
>
> This is the main street of the town.

*Khiyâbân-e asli* — a main road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*asli* was borrowed from Arabic *aṣlī* (EtymDB), an adjective built on *aṣl* 'origin, root'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
