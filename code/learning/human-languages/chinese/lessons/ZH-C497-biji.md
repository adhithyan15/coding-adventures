## You'll want to know: bǐjì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C497-SELFSTUDY497-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bǐjì* — "notes (taken in class)".

> *nǐ de bǐjì xiě de hěn hǎo* — Your notes are really well written.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǐjì* is built from *bǐ* 'a brush' and 记 'to record', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pit* and *kiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to record'.
