## You'll want to know: cōnclūsiō, cōnclūsiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C364-CONCLUDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnclūsiō, cōnclūsiōnis** — "a conclusion; the close of a speech".

> Cōnclūsiō tua falsa est.
>
> *Your conclusion is false.*

*cōnclūsiō, cōnclūsiōnis* f. The last step of an argument, and the last part of a speech. English *conclusion*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōnclūsiō* is the noun built on *cōnclūdō* "to shut up, to infer" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *conclusion* comes from it.
