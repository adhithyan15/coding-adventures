## You'll want to know: पेशी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C479-NADI479-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पेशी** — *peśī* — "a cell (of the body)".

> प्रत्येक जीव पेशींनी बनलेला असतो.
>
> *pratyek jīv peśīnnī banlelā asto.*
>
> Every living thing is made of cells.

Feminine.

Feminine; plural पेशी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेशी** (*peśī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *peśī*, "piece of flesh, muscle" (CDIAL 8384); modern Marathi gave it the sense "cell".
