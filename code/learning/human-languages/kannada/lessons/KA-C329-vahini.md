# ವಾಹಿನಿ (vāhini) — a TV channel

## You'll want to know: ವಾಹಿನಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C329-MADHYAMA329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಾಹಿನಿ** — *vāhini* — "a TV channel".

> ಈ ವಾಹಿನಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ದಿನವಿಡೀ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *ī vāhiniyalli dinaviḍī suddi baruttade.*
>
> This channel has news all day.

Plural: ವಾಹಿನಿಗಳು, *vāhinigaḷu*.

ಸುದ್ದಿ ವಾಹಿನಿ, a news channel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಾಹಿನಿ** (*vāhini*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vāhinī*, "an army", and in later use "a river", that which flows and carries (Monier-Williams); the sense "a channel" is modern.
