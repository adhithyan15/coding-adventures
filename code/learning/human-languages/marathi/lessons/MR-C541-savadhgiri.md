## You'll want to know: सावधगिरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C541-SAVADH541-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सावधगिरी** — *sāvadhgirī* — "caution".

> पावसात गाडी चालवताना सावधगिरी हवी.
>
> *pāvsāt gāḍī cālavtānā sāvadhgirī havī.*
>
> You need caution when driving in the rain.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सावधगिरी** (*sāvadhgirī*) is built on सावध, "careful", from Sanskrit *sāvadhāna*, "attentive" (Monier-Williams), with the Persian ending *-girī*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
