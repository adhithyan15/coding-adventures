## You'll want to know: ટીમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C548-TEAM548-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ટીમ** — *ṭīm* — "a team".

> જો અમારી ટીમ આજે જીતશે તો અમે મીઠાઈ વહેંચીશું.
>
> *jo amārī ṭīm āje jītśe to ame mīṭhāī vahẽcīśũ.*
>
> If our team wins today, we'll hand out sweets.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટીમ** (*ṭīm*) is borrowed from English *team*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
