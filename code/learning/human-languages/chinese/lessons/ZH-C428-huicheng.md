## You'll want to know: huíchéng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C428-MOVEON428-03]; assesses=[] -->

*huíchéng* — "return journey; the journey back".

> *huíchéng wǒmen zuò gāotiě* — On the way back we're taking the fast train.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huíchéng* is built from 回 'to go back' and *chéng* 'a stage of a journey', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwoj* and *drjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a set period' and 'a rule'; the sense 'a stretch of road' is not given there.
