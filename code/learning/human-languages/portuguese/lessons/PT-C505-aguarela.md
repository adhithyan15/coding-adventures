## You'll want to know: a aguarela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C505-PINTURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a aguarela** — "watercolour".

> A minha tia pinta paisagens a aguarela.
>
> *My aunt paints landscapes in watercolour.*

In Brazil: *aquarela*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aguarela* is usually traced to Italian *acquerello*, built on *acqua* "water", from Latin *aqua* (Lewis & Short).
