## You'll want to know: গড়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C390-SOROK390-05]; assesses=[] -->

**গড়ানো** — *gôṛāno* — "to roll; to roll down".

> পাথরটা পাহাড় থেকে গড়িয়ে পড়ল।
>
> *pāthôrṭā pāhāṛ theke gôṛiye pôṛlô.*
>
> The stone rolled down the hill.

Past (he/she …): সেই পাথর গড়াল, *sei pāthôr gôṛālô*.

Not গড়া, to build: a different verb that sounds close.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গড়ানো** (*gôṛāno*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *gaṛāna*, "to make slide or roll", under Sanskrit *gáḍati*, "drops, runs as a liquid" (CDIAL 3973).
