# Tomorrow, I will go

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall the one word for “tomorrow.” (**श्वः**, *śvaḥ*.)

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-02, SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02] -->

Put the known time word first, then choose the speaker form.

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: “Tomorrow, I will go” — *śvaḥ gamiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: “Tomorrow, he will go” — *śvaḥ saḥ gamiṣyati*]
- [YOU SAY: change “he will go” to “I will go” — *gamiṣyāmi*]

## Wrap-up Recall

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You have made a decision for tomorrow. Say only: “Tomorrow, I will
go.” (**श्वः गमिष्यामि।**)
