## You'll want to know: le peignoir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C427-CHAMBRE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le peignoir** — "the bathrobe, the dressing gown".

> Après la douche, il met le peignoir de l'hôtel.
>
> *After the shower he puts on the hotel bathrobe.*

*Un peignoir de bain* — a bathrobe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*peignoir* is built on *peigner* 'to comb', from Latin *pectināre*, from *pecten* 'comb' (Lewis & Short): first the gown worn while your hair was combed.
