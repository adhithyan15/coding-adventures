## You'll want to know: பல்லாங்குழி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C529-VILAIYATTU529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**பல்லாங்குழி** — *pallāṅkuḻi* — "a seed-and-pit board game".

> பாட்டியும் பேத்தியும் பல்லாங்குழி விளையாடுகிறார்கள்.
>
> *pāṭṭiyum pēttiyum pallāṅkuḻi viḷaiyāṭukiṟārkaḷ.*
>
> Grandmother and granddaughter are playing the pit game.

Two players move seeds or shells through rows of small pits in a wooden board.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பல்லாங்குழி** (*pallāṅkuḻi*) ends in the native குழி (*kuḻi*), "a pit", for the hollows of the board; the origin of the first part is not settled.
