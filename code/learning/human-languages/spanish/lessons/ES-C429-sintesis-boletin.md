# Synthesis — reading a report card

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C429-EDU-02, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the two words for a mark. One of them is about to appear as a
column heading.

## Reading: el boletín de calificaciones
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C429-EDU-01, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-02, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-03, ES-LEX-C358-REF-14] -->

> **IES Miguel Hernández**
> **Boletín de calificaciones**

| clase | calificación |
|---|---|
| Matemáticas | 7 |
| Historia | 4 |
| Inglés | 9 |

Three rows, and everything in them is now readable. **IES** is the school.
**Calificación** heads the column because this is a printed document. The numbers
are out of ten.

## How to answer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C429-EDU-04, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-05, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-02] -->

Two of the three rows passed and one did not. Say which verb goes with each:

> *He aprobado Matemáticas e Inglés.*
> *He suspendido Historia.*

[PAUSE 4s]

The history row is the one that hangs. The student will carry it into the next
term as **una asignatura pendiente**, and the family will ask about it in
exactly those words.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C429-EDU-02, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-04, ES-LEX-C429-EDU-05] -->

> — *¿Qué notas has sacado?*
> — *He aprobado casi todo. Historia no.*

The question uses **notas** because it is spoken. The answer names the one that
did not pass, which is what the question is really asking about.
