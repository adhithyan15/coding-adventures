## You'll want to know: रंगना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C527-KALA527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रंगना** — *raṅgnā* — "to colour; to paint; to dye".

> बच्चों ने दीवार हरे रंग से रंगी।
>
> *Baccoṁ ne dīvār hare raṅg se raṅgī.*
>
> The children painted the wall green.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कुर्सी रंगी, *usne kursī raṅgī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रंगना** (*raṅgnā*) is the verb of *raṅg*, "colour", probably from the same source as Hindi *rãgānā*, "to be coloured", which CDIAL files under *\*raṅgayati*, "dyes", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 10570).
