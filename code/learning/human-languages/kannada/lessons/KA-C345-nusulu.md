# ನುಸುಳು (nusuḷu) — to sneak in; to slip through

## You'll want to know: ನುಸುಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C345-ADAGU345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ನುಸುಳು** — *nusuḷu* — "to sneak in; to slip through".

> ಗೂಢಚಾರ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಅರಮನೆಯೊಳಗೆ ನುಸುಳಿದನು.
>
> *gūḍhacāra rātri aramaneyoḷage nusuḷidanu.*
>
> The spy slipped into the palace at night.

Past (I …): ನುಸುಳಿದೆ, *nusuḷide*.

Also used of crossing a border secretly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನುಸುಳು** (*nusuḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3714 lists Kannada *nusuḷ* beside Tamil words meaning "to creep through a narrow passage, to penetrate".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
