Bengali, chapter 541: Waiting Your Turn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
প্রতীক্ষা (prôtīkkhā) — waiting; expectation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for armour, then the Bengali for to finish off.

You'll want to know: প্রতীক্ষা.
প্রতীক্ষা — prôtīkkhā — "waiting; expectation".
এক বছরের প্রতীক্ষার পরে ছেলেটা বাড়ি ফিরল। Ek bôchhôrer prôtīkkhār pôre chheleṭā bāṛi phirlô. After a year of waiting the boy came home.

The word, taken apart.
প্রতীক্ষা (prôtīkkhā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit pratīkṣā, "expectation" (Monier-Williams): the longer, hopeful kind of waiting.

Lesson 5 of 5.
পর্যায় (pôrjāy) — a phase; a stage (of a process).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for reserved, then the Bengali for a queue token.

You'll want to know: পর্যায়.
পর্যায় — pôrjāy — "a phase; a stage (of a process)".
প্রথম পর্যায়ের কাজ শেষ হলে আমরা ছুটি নেব। Prothom pôrjāyer kāj shesh hôle āmrā chhuṭi nebô. When the first stage of the work is done, we'll take a holiday.

The word, taken apart.
পর্যায় (pôrjāy) is a tatsama, Sanskrit paryāya, which Monier-Williams gives as "a turning round" and as "succession, a turn"; the sense "a stage, a phase" is the modern language's own.
