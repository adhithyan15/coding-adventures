## You'll want to know: qióng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C375-HELP375-01]; assesses=[] -->

*qióng* — "poor (having little money)".

> *yǐqián zhèr de rén dōu hěn qióng* — People here used to be poor.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qióng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *gjuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to come to the very end'. How running out came to mean being poor is not given there.
