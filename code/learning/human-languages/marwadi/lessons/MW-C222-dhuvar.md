## You'll want to know: धूंवर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C222-COLD222-05]; assesses=[] -->

**धूंवर** — *dhū̃var* — "fog; mist".

> आज दिनूगै धूंवर है।
>
> *āj dinūgai dhū̃var hai.*
>
> There's fog this morning.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूंवर** (*dhū̃var*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhūmarī*, "mist, fog", which CDIAL sets beside a reconstructed *\*dhūmara*, "smoky", from *dhūma*, "smoke" (CDIAL 6852); CDIAL lists Gujarati *dhũvar*, "fog". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6853).
