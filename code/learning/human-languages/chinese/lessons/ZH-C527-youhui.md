## You'll want to know: yōuhuì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C527-ORDER527-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yōuhuì* — "a favourable deal (for customers)".

> *huìyuán yǒu yōuhuì* — Members get a special deal.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yōuhuì* is built from *yōu* and *huì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'juw* and *hwejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'plentiful' and the second 'kindness'.
