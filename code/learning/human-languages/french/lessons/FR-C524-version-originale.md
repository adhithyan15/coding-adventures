## You'll want to know: la version originale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C524-CINEMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la version originale** — "original-language version".

> Je préfère voir les films en version originale.
>
> *I prefer to watch films in the original language.*

Often *la VO*; *la VOST* has subtitles; *la VF* is the French dub.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la version originale* joins *version*, from Medieval Latin *versiō* "a turning", from *vertere* "to turn", and *originale*, from Latin *orīgō* "beginning", from *orīrī* "to rise" (Lewis & Short).
