## You'll want to know: réveillonner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C319-REVEILLON-02]; assesses=[] -->

**réveillonner** — "to have the festive late-night dinner".

> Nous avons réveillonné jusqu'à trois heures du matin.
>
> *We had our festive dinner until three in the morning.*

On 24 December or 31 December.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réveillonner* is built on *réveillon*, from *réveiller* "to wake up", which goes back to Latin *vigilāre* "to keep watch" (Lewis & Short).
