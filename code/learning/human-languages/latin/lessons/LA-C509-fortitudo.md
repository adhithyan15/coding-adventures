## You'll want to know: fortitūdō, fortitūdinis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C509-FORT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**fortitūdō, fortitūdinis** — "courage, bravery".

> Mīlitēs magnā fortitūdine pugnant.
>
> *The soldiers fight with great courage.*

Feminine, third declension, from *fortis*. One of the four great virtues of ancient ethics. English *fortitude*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fortitūdō** is built on *fortis* "strong, brave" with *-tūdō* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *fortitude* comes from it.
