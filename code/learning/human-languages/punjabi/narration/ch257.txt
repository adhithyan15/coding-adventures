Punjabi, chapter 257: At the Bank Counter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਖਾਤਾ (khātā) — a bank account; an account book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to send off, then the Punjabi for to tear.

You'll want to know: ਖਾਤਾ.
ਖਾਤਾ — khātā — "a bank account; an account book".
ਮੇਰਾ ਖਾਤਾ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦੇ ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ। merā khātā shahir de baiṅk vich hai. My account is at the bank in town.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਖਾਤਾ (khātā) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit kṣatra, "might, rule"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khāttā, "account book", there, as a word taken from Hindi khātā (CDIAL 3648). How the sense moved is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਰਕਮ (rakam) — a sum of money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to tear, then the Punjabi for a bank account.

You'll want to know: ਰਕਮ.
ਰਕਮ — rakam — "a sum of money".
ਇਹ ਰਕਮ ਬਹੁਤ ਵੱਡੀ ਹੈ। eh rakam bahut vaḍḍī hai. This sum is very large.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਰਕਮ (rakam) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic raqam, "a figure, a written sum".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਕਢਵਾਉਣਾ (kaḍhvāuṇā) — to withdraw; to have something taken out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a sum of money, then the Punjabi for in cash.

You'll want to know: ਕਢਵਾਉਣਾ.
ਕਢਵਾਉਣਾ — kaḍhvāuṇā — "to withdraw; to have something taken out".
ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਬੈਂਕ ਤੋਂ ਪੈਸੇ ਕਢਵਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ har mahīne baiṅk ton paise kaḍhvāundā hāṁ. I withdraw money from the bank every month.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੈਂਕ ਤੋਂ ਪੈਸੇ ਕਢਵਾਏ, maiṁ baiṅk ton paise kaḍhvāe.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਢਵਾਉਣਾ (kaḍhvāuṇā) is the causative of kaḍḍhṇā, "to pull out", inherited from a reconstructed kaḍḍhati, "pulls, draws"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kaḍḍhṇā under it (CDIAL 2660).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਵਧਣਾ (vadhṇā) — to go up; to grow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for in cash, then the Punjabi for to withdraw.

You'll want to know: ਵਧਣਾ.
ਵਧਣਾ — vadhṇā — "to go up; to grow".
ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਪੈਸੇ ਹੌਲੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਵਧਦੇ ਹਨ। baiṅk vich paise haulī haulī vadhde han. Money slowly grows in the bank.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਰੁੱਖ ਬਹੁਤ ਵਧਿਆ, oh rukkh bahut vadhiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਧਣਾ (vadhṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit vardhate, "grows, increases"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vadhṇā, "to increase, grow, advance", under it (CDIAL 11376).
