# ६ ७ ८ — the last two traps

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-PRICE-ANSWER-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FOUR-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 18s] Write the too-expensive answer, then write the digits for four and
five.

## Script — the last three of the traps
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SIX-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SEVEN-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-EIGHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **६** — six  **७** — seven  **८** — eight

**६** is close enough to its Latin cousin that a reader guesses it correctly.
The other two are not.

**७** carries a hook that pulls the eye towards a nine. **८** is closed and
round and looks more like a letter than a figure. Both stand next to **६** here
on purpose: a trap is easiest to learn beside something safe.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SIX-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SEVEN-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-EIGHT-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHHA-SIX-01, MW-SCRIPT-SAAT-01, MW-SCRIPT-AATH-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **६**, **७**, **८** three times each, saying *chha*, *sāt*, *āṭh* as you
go. Then cover the models and write **७** and **८** from the words alone.
