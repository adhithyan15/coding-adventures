## You'll want to know: じき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C346-KONDO346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**じき** — *jiki* — "a time; a season; a period".

> **あつい じきに なったら、みずを のんで ください。** — *atsui jiki ni nattara, mizu o nonde kudasai* — "When the hot season comes, please drink water."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じき** (*jiki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "time" (Middle Chinese *dzyi*) and *ki*, "period" (*gi*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
