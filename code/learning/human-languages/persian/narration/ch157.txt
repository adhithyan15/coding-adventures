Persian, chapter 157: Dead end, Dormitory, Prison, Port, Chimney.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
بنبست (bonbast) — a dead end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an underground water channel, then the Persian for a small shop.

You'll want to know: بنبست.
بنبست — bonbast — "a dead end".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bonbast]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bonbast, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bonbast]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for an underground water channel, then the Persian for a small shop, then say bonbast again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does بنبست (bonbast) mean? ("A dead end".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Words from chapters 149-152 — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a port and a chimney.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: to waste, to economise, to divide, to compare, to expect]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the body, a heel, the throat, a wound, a pill]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a blister, a pimple, an ointment, a drop, a capsule]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: this year, daily, monthly, last, after]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To waste? (هدر دادن, hadar dâdan.) To economise? (صرفه جویی کردن, sarfejuyi kardan.) To divide? (تقسیم کردن, taqsim kardan.) To compare? (مقایسه کردن, moqâyese kardan.) To expect? (انتظار داشتن, entezâr dâshtan.) The body? (بدن, badan.) A heel? (پاشنه, pâshne.) The throat? (گلو, galu.) A wound? (زخم, zakhm.) A pill? (قرص, qors.) A blister? (تاول, tâval.) A pimple? (جوش, jush.) An ointment? (پماد, pomâd.) A drop? (قطره, qatre.) A capsule? (کپسول, kapsul.) This year? (امسال, emsâl.) Daily? (روزانه, ruzâne.) Monthly? (ماهانه, mâhâne.) Last? (آخر, âkhar.) After? (پس از, pas az.)

A butcher's shop? (قصابی, qassâbi.) A café? (کافه, kâfe.) A doctor's surgery? (مطب, matab.) A secondary school? (دبیرستان, dabirestân.) A police station? (کلانتری, kalântari.) The town hall? (شهرداری, shahrdâri.) An embassy? (سفارت, sefârat.) Customs? (گمرک, gomrok.) A well? (چاه, châh.) A hut? (کلبه, kolbe.) A mill? (آسیاب, âsiyâb.) An underground water channel? (قنات, qanât.) A small shop? (دکان, dokkân.) A dead end? (بنبست, bonbast.) A dormitory? (خوابگاه, khâbgâh.) A prison? (زندان, zendân.) A port? (بندر, bandar.) A chimney? (دودکش, dudkesh.)
