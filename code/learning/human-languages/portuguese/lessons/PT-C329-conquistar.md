## You'll want to know: conquistar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C329-BATALHA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**conquistar** — "to conquer; to win (over)".

> O rei conquistou a cidade.
>
> *The king conquered the city.*

Also to win someone over: *conquistou o público*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conquistar* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *conquaesītāre*, from Latin *conquīrere* "to seek out", built on *quaerere* "to seek" (Lewis & Short).
