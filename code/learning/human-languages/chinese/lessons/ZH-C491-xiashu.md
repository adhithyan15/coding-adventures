## You'll want to know: xiàshǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C491-BOSS491-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàshǔ* — "subordinate; someone under one at work".

> *tā de xiàshǔ dōu hěn yǒu jīngshen* — The people under him are all full of energy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàshǔ* is built from 下 'below' and *shǔ* 'to belong to', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *dzyowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'low' and the second 'attached to' and 'a kind'.
