## You'll want to know: حجة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C358-HUJJA358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حجة** — *ḥujja* — "an argument".

> قدم الطالب حجة قوية في النقاش.
>
> *qaddama aṭ-ṭālib ḥujja qawiyya fī an-niqāsh.*
>
> The student put forward a strong argument in the discussion.

Plural: حجج, *ḥujaj*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حجة** (*ḥujja*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-j-j*, "to make for; to overcome in argument" (Lane). A *ḥujja* is what wins a dispute: an argument, or a proof.
