# Review — two places, and how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-TAR-01, FA-GRAMMAR-PISH-AGO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "smaller than that room". (*Az ân otâq kuchektar.*) Then
"three years ago". (*Se sâl pish.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-TAR-01, FA-GRAMMAR-SUPERLATIVE-TARIN-01, FA-LEX-C297-MESL-01, FA-GRAMMAR-DURATION-AST-KE-01, FA-GRAMMAR-PISH-AGO-01, FA-LEX-C72-TOWN72-05] -->

Two chapters, and an experience can now be measured. *-tar az* sets one
thing against another, *-tarin* crowns one, and *mesl* says two are alike.
*Pish* fixes a moment in the past; *... ast ke* with the present measures a
stretch that is still running.

[PAUSE 2s] Someone who moved, describing it:

> *do sâl pish be in shahr âmadam. do sâl ast ke injâ zendegi mikonam. in
> shahr mesl-e Tehrân ast, vali kuchektar ast. ghazâ-ye injâ behtar ast!
> avval nârâhat budam, vali ba'd khoshhâl shodam.*

The last line goes back to chapter two hundred and ninety-six: a mood that
lasted, *budam*, and a change that arrived, *shodam*.

## Wrap-up Recall
