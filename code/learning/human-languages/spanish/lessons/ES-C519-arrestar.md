## You'll want to know: arrestar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C519-POLICIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**arrestar** — "to arrest".

> Arrestaron a tres personas.
>
> *They arrested three people.*

*Detener* is the more common word in the news; *arrestar* is also used.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arrestar* comes from spoken Latin *\*arrestāre* 'to make stop', built on *ad* and *restāre* 'to stay back, to remain' (EtymDB).
