## You'll want to know: નોટિસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C151-READ151-01]; assesses=[GU-Q-KYAAN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-OBJECT-AGREE-01, GU-LEX-VANCHVU] -->

**નોટિસ** (*noṭis*) — "a posted notice, a notice".

A notice is a sheet put up for everyone: on a shop door, at a station, at
work. Gujarati took the English word whole.

> **નોટિસ ક્યાં છે?** — *noṭis kyā̃ che?* — Where is the notice?
> **મેં નોટિસ વાંચી.** — *mẽ noṭis vā̃cī.* — I read the notice.

**વાંચી** ends in **-ી** because **નોટિસ** is feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નોટિસ** (*noṭis*) is a loan from English *notice*. English took it from
French, and French from Latin *notitia*, "a being known", built on *nōscere*, "to
come to know".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading the notices on a street.
