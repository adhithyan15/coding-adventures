## You'll want to know: ವಿಚಾರಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C358-VICHARANE358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಚಾರಣೆ** — *vicāraṇe* — "a hearing; an inquiry".

> ಪ್ರಕರಣದ ವಿಚಾರಣೆ ಮುಂದಿನ ವಾರ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *prakaraṇada vicāraṇe mundina vāra naḍeyuttade.*
>
> The hearing of the case takes place next week.

In court, a hearing; at an office, an inquiry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಚಾರಣೆ** (*vicāraṇe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vicāraṇā*, "deliberation, investigation" (Monier-Williams), from *vicāra*, "consideration".
