## You'll want to know: gēshǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C257-SHOW257-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gēshǒu* — "singer".

> *tā shì wǒ zuì xǐhuan de gēshǒu* — He's my favourite singer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gēshǒu* is built from *gē* 'a song' and 手 'a hand', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ka* and *syuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes an older book for the first: the voice of people is called song. A hand here is someone skilled at a thing; that sense is not given there.
