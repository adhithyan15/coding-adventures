## You'll want to know: हवालदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C290-POLICE290-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हवालदार** — *havāldār* — "a constable".

> आम्हाला किती वेळ थांबावं लागेल, हे हवालदारावर अवलंबून आहे.
>
> *āmhālā kitī veḷ thāmbāvaṁ lāgel, he havāldārāvar avlambūn āhe.*
>
> How long we will have to wait depends on the constable.

Masculine.

हवालदाराला, to the constable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हवालदार** (*havāldār*) is a loan from Persian *ḥavāladār*, 'one entrusted with a charge', usually traced to Arabic *ḥawāla*, 'a charge, a trust'.
