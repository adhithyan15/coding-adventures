## You'll want to know: ஒழி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C395-NATATTU395-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒழி** — *oḻi* — "to abolish; to root out".

> எல்லோரும் சேர்ந்தால் ஊழலை ஒழிக்கலாம்.
>
> *ellōrum cērntāl ūḻalai oḻikkalām.*
>
> If everyone joins together, we can root out corruption.

Past (I …): ஒழித்தேன், *oḻittēṉ*.

Transitive with -ஐ and the past ஒழித்தேன்; the intransitive ஒழி, to cease, has ஒழிந்தது.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒழி** (*oḻi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1009 lists Tamil *oḻi*, "to cease, be finished" with the past in -ந்த-, and "to finish, bring to an end" with the past in -த்த-.
