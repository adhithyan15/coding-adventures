Punjabi, chapter 259: When the Phone Rings.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਨੰਬਰ (nambar) — a number; a phone number.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a banknote, then the Punjabi for to earn.

You'll want to know: ਨੰਬਰ.
ਨੰਬਰ — nambar — "a number; a phone number".
ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਨੰਬਰ ਕੀ ਹੈ? tuhāḍā fon nambar kī hai? What is your phone number?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਨੰਬਰ (nambar) is borrowed from English number.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nambar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nambar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tuhāḍā fon nambar kī hai? — What is your phone number?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for a banknote, then the Punjabi for to earn, then say nambar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਨੰਬਰ (nambar) mean? ("A number; a phone number".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 6.
ਘੰਟੀ (ghaṇṭī) — a bell; a ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to earn, then the Punjabi for a number.

You'll want to know: ਘੰਟੀ.
ਘੰਟੀ — ghaṇṭī — "a bell; a ring".
ਫ਼ੋਨ ਦੀ ਘੰਟੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਉੱਚੀ ਹੈ। fon dī ghaṇṭī bahut uchchī hai. The phone's ring is very loud.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਘੰਟੀ (ghaṇṭī) is inherited from Sanskrit ghaṇṭikā, "a small bell", beside ghaṇṭā, "bell"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ghaṇḍā, "a bell used to attract deer", under it (CDIAL 4421).

Lesson 3 of 6.
ਵੱਜਣਾ (vajjṇā) — to ring; to sound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a number, then the Punjabi for a bell.

You'll want to know: ਵੱਜਣਾ.
ਵੱਜਣਾ — vajjṇā — "to ring; to sound".
ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਨਹੀਂ ਵੱਜਦਾ ਹੈ। rāt nū̃ fon nahīṁ vajjdā hai. The phone does not ring at night.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਘੰਟੀ ਵੱਜੀ, oh ghaṇṭī vajjī.

The word, taken apart.
ਵੱਜਣਾ (vajjṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit vādyate, "is made to sound"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vajjṇā, "to sound (of bell, clock)", under it (CDIAL 11513).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ਮਿਲਾਉਣਾ (milāuṇā) — to dial a number; to put through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to ring, then the Punjabi for a voice.

You'll want to know: ਮਿਲਾਉਣਾ.
ਮਿਲਾਉਣਾ — milāuṇā — "to dial a number; to put through".
ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਅਮਨ ਦਾ ਨੰਬਰ ਮਿਲਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ shām nū̃ Aman dā nambar milāundā hāṁ. In the evening I dial Aman's number.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੈਂਕ ਦਾ ਨੰਬਰ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ, maiṁ baiṅk dā nambar milāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਿਲਾਉਣਾ (milāuṇā) is the causative partner of milṇā, "to meet"; CDIAL lists Punjabi milṇā, "to meet, be obtained", under Sanskrit milati, "meets" (CDIAL 10133).
