Russian, chapter 278: The first time and for ever.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
впервые (vpervýye) — for the first time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for annual, then the Russian for further.

You'll want to know: впервые.
впервые (vpervýye) — "for the first time".
Я впервые увидел море в девять лет. Ya vpervýye uvídel móre v dévyat let. I saw the sea for the first time at nine.
Also в первый раз.

The word, taken apart.
впервые (vpervýye) is built on в and первый "first", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic pĭrvŭ "first" (EtymDB).

Она зашла ненадолго и скоро ушла. Oná zashlá nenadólgo i skóro ushlá. She dropped in for a short while and soon left.

Lesson 4 of 6.
вспоминать (vspominát) — to recall, to remember.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for for ever, then the Russian for for a short while.

You'll want to know: вспоминать.
вспоминать (vspominát) — "to recall, to remember".
Бабушка часто вспоминает свою деревню. Bábushka chásto vspomináyet svoyú derévnyu. Grandma often recalls her village.
Imperfective; perfective вспомнить (vspómnit). It takes the accusative or о + prepositional. Помнить is to keep in memory; вспоминать (vspominát) is to bring something back to mind.

The word, taken apart.
вспоминать (vspominát) is built on вс- "up" and помнить "to remember", from the Slavic root of память "memory"; EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.

You'll want to know: запоминать.
запоминать (zapominát) — "to memorize, to commit to memory".
Я легко запоминаю имена. Ya legkó zapomináyu imená. I remember names easily.
Imperfective; perfective запомнить (zapómnit): я запомнил дорогу — I memorized the way.

The word, taken apart.
запоминать (zapominát) is built on за- and помнить "to remember", from the Slavic root of память "memory": to fix in memory. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
