## You'll want to know: सचिवः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C357-SAMITI357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सचिवः** — *sacivaḥ* — "a secretary; a counsellor".

> सचिवः सभायाः कार्यं करोति।
>
> *sacivaḥ sabhāyāḥ kāryaṃ karoti.*
>
> The secretary does the assembly's work.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सचिवः** (*sacivaḥ*) MW gives *saciva* "an associate, a companion" and "a king's counsellor, a minister", and "a secretary" is its modern sense; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
