## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అరుదైన** (*arudaina*) is native Dravidian: *arudu*, "rare", one word with Tamil *aru*, "rare" (DEDR 221), with *aina*, "which is" (DEDR 333).
