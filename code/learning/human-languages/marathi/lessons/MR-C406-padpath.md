## You'll want to know: पदपथ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C406-PADPATH406-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पदपथ** — *padpath* — "a pavement; a footpath".

> या पदपथावर भाजीवाले बसतात.
>
> *yā padpathāvar bhājīvāle bastāt.*
>
> Vegetable sellers sit on this pavement.

Masculine.

A formal word for the raised walk beside a town road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पदपथ** (*padpath*) is built from Sanskrit *pada*, "foot, step", and *patha*, "path" (Monier-Williams): a path for feet.
