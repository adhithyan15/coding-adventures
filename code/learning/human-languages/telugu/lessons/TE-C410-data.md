## You'll want to know: దాత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C410-SEVA410-05]; assesses=[] -->

**దాత** — *dāta* — "a donor".

> ఒక దాత పాఠశాలకు పుస్తకాలు ఇచ్చాడు.
>
> *oka dāta pāṭhaśālaku pustakālu iccāḍu.*
>
> A donor gave books to the school.

Plural: దాతలు, *dātalu*.

రక్తదాత, a blood donor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దాత** (*dāta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dātṛ*, "a giver" (Monier-Williams), from *dā*, "to give".
