Lesson 1 of 5.
ಡಬ್ಬಿ (ḍabbi) — a tin, a container.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an app, then the Kannada for a video.

You'll want to know: ಡಬ್ಬಿ.
ಡಬ್ಬಿ — ḍabbi — "a tin, a container".

The word, taken apart.
ಡಬ್ಬಿ (ḍabbi) is probably the northern word for "box", Hindi ḍabbī, ḍabbā, which CDIAL 5528 traces to a reconstructed ḍabba, "box", and calls non-Aryan in origin, citing a view that takes it from Dravidian. CDIAL does not list the Kannada word.

You'll want to know: ಪೊಟ್ಟಣ.

The word, taken apart.
Where ಪೊಟ್ಟಣ (poṭṭaṇa) comes from is uncertain. It looks like Sanskrit pōṭṭala, "a bundle" (CDIAL 8396), whose northern forms include Hindi poṭlī, but no source records the Kannada word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಉಕ್ಕು (ukku) — steel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a jar, then the Kannada for a packet.

You'll want to know: ಉಕ್ಕು.
ಉಕ್ಕು — ukku — "steel".

The word, taken apart.
ಉಕ್ಕು (ukku) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil urukku and Telugu ukku (DEDR 661). DEDR lists urku, ukku, "steel", under the root for "to melt": steel is "what is melted".
