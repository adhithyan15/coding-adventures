## You'll want to know: la versión
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C516-VERSION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la versión** — "the version, the account".

> Cada persona tiene su versión de lo que pasó.
>
> *Each person has their own version of what happened.*

*En versión original* on a cinema listing means not dubbed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*versión* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *versiō* 'a turning, a translation' (EtymDB), built on *vertere* 'to turn'. A version is a story turned one particular way.
