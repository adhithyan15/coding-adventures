## You'll want to know: umschlagen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C460-WETTERBERICHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**umschlagen** — "to change suddenly (of the weather); to turn over".

> In den Bergen schlägt das Wetter schnell um.
>
> *In the mountains the weather changes quickly.*

Separable; Perfekt with *sein* for the weather: *das Wetter ist umgeschlagen*. *Der Umschlag* — the envelope.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*umschlagen* is built from *um* 'over, round' and *schlagen* 'to strike, to turn', inherited from Proto-Germanic *slahaną* (EtymDB).
