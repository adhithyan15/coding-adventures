# ਸਾਢੇ (sāḍhe) — plus a half; half past

## You'll want to know: ਸਾਢੇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C260-TIME260-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਢੇ** — *sāḍhe* — "plus a half; half past".

> ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਦਾ ਕੰਮ ਸਾਢੇ ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ ਸ਼ੁਰੂ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *daftar dā kamm sāḍhe nauṁ vaje shurū hundā hai.*
>
> Work at the office starts at half past nine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਢੇ** (*sāḍhe*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sārdha*, "plus a half"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sāḍhe*, "plus a half", under it (CDIAL 13369).
