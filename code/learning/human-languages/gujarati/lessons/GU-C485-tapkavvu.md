## You'll want to know: ટપકાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C485-DAKHAL485-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ટપકાવવું** — *ṭapkāvvũ* — "to put in drops".

> નર્સ દિવસમાં ત્રણ વખત આંખમાં દવા ટપકાવે છે.
>
> *nars divasmā̃ traṇ vakhat ā̃khmā̃ davā ṭapkāve che.*
>
> Three times a day the nurse puts drops in my eye.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આંખમાં દવા ટપકાવી, *teṇe ā̃khmā̃ davā ṭapkāvī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટપકાવવું** (*ṭapkāvvũ*) is the causative of **ટપકવું** (*ṭapakvũ*), "to drip", built on a reconstructed *\*ṭapp-*, "drip, drop", an imitative word (CDIAL 5444).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
