## You'll want to know: बुबुळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C476-CEHRA476-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बुबुळ** — *bubuḷ* — "the eyeball; the iris".

> डोळ्याच्या बुबुळावर एक लहान डाग आहे.
>
> *ḍoḷyācyā bubuḷāvar ek lahān ḍāg āhe.*
>
> There is a small spot on the eyeball.

Neuter.

Neuter: डोळ्याचं बुबुळ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुबुळ** (*bubuḷ*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
