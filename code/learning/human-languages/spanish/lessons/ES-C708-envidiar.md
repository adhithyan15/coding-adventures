## You'll want to know: envidiar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C708-CELOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**envidiar** — "to envy".

> No envidio a los famosos.
>
> *I do not envy famous people.*

*Envidiar algo a alguien* — to envy someone something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*envidiar* is built on *envidia*, which was borrowed from Latin *invidia* "envy", from *vidēre* "to see" (EtymDB).
