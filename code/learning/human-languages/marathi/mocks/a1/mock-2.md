# Marathi A1 mock 2 — learner paper

Give this paper to the learner. Keep
[`mock-2-examiner-script.md`](mock-2-examiner-script.md) separate. Administration
is 58 written minutes plus 10 speaking minutes after 5 minutes of preparation.
Support-language directions may explain the action but may not translate
answer-bearing Marathi.

## Writing — 20 minutes

Use one continuous clock: 7 minutes for task 1 and 13 minutes for task 2. Move
on when the first 7 minutes end and stop at 20 minutes.

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
1. Complete all six fields independently in Devanagari. Use your own details or
   consistent invented details. Do not copy a model: **नाव**, **शहर**, **भाषा**,
   **आवडते पेय**, **आवडती कृती**, **मित्राचे नाव**. Keep every field legible,
   use one answer per field, and complete it within seven minutes.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->
2. Write 30–40 Marathi words to a new neighbour named **नील**. Greet him,
   introduce yourself, state your city and one language, say one routine or
   preference, invite him to a familiar place at a stated time, and close
   politely. Make the reader and purpose clear; do not copy a learned model.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->

## Speaking — 10 minutes

The examiner uses the separate script. No written answer script, romanization,
dictionary, translator, or coaching is allowed.

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
1. Answer five familiar questions about your identity, home, language, study
   routine, and one preference.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
2. Prepare for five minutes using keywords only, then describe a familiar
   person or place for exactly one minute.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
3. Role-play a bookshop visit: greet the seller, request one book, ask its
   price, respond to one clarification, and close politely.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
