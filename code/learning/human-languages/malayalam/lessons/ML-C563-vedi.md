## You'll want to know: വേദി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C563-RANGAM563-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വേദി** — *vēdi* — "a stage; a venue".

> ഗായകൻ വേദിയിൽ കയറിയപ്പോൾ എല്ലാവരും എഴുന്നേറ്റു.
>
> *gāyakan vēdiyil kayaṟiyappōḷ ellāvaruṁ eḻunnēṟṟu.*
>
> When the singer came on stage, everyone stood up.

Any stage or meeting place: സമ്മേളന വേദി, a conference venue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വേദി** (*vēdi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vedi*, the raised ground prepared for a rite (Monier-Williams); a raised place for a rite became a raised place for a show.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
