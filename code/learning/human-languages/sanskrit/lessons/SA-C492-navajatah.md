## You'll want to know: नवजातः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C492-JANMA492-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नवजातः** — *navajātaḥ* — "newborn".

> नवजातः शिशुः बहु स्वपिति।
>
> *navajātaḥ śiśuḥ bahu svapiti.*
>
> A newborn baby sleeps a lot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नवजातः** (*navajātaḥ*) is built from **नव**, "new", and *jāta*, "born", from the root √jan; MW gives "recently born, new" (MW).
