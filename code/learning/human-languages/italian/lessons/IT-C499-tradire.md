## You'll want to know: tradire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C499-LITIGI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**tradire** — "to betray; to cheat on".

> Si sentiva tradita dalla sua migliore amica.
>
> *She felt betrayed by her best friend.*

*Tradire il partner* — to be unfaithful. An *-isc-* verb: *tradisco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tradire* is inherited from Latin *trādere* "to hand over, to betray" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
