## You'll want to know: dìtú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C211-LOCAL211-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dìtú* — "map".

> *nǐ yǒu Běijīng de dìtú ma* — Have you got a map of Beijing?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìtú* is built from 地 'ground' and *tú* 'a plan, a chart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *du* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to plan'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
