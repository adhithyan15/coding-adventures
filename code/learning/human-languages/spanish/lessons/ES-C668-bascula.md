## You'll want to know: la báscula
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C668-EQUIPAJE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la báscula** — "the scales (for weighing)".

> Pon la maleta en la báscula.
>
> *Put the suitcase on the scales.*

In the bathroom it weighs you; at the airport it weighs your bag.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*báscula* comes from French *bascule* "seesaw" (EtymDB): old scales tipped like a seesaw.
