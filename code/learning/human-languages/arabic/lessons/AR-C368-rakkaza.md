## You'll want to know: ركز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C368-AHAMMIYYA368-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ركز** — *rakkaza* — "to focus".

> ركز الطالب على دروسه.
>
> *rakkaza aṭ-ṭālib ʿalā durūsih.*
>
> The student focused on his lessons.

Present: يركز, *yurakkiz*.

It takes على: ركز على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ركز** (*rakkaza*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-k-z*, "to fix firmly in the ground" (Lane). It is Form II, to plant firmly: to fix the mind on one thing.
