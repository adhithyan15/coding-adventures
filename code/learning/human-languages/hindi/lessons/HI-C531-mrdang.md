## You'll want to know: मृदंग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C531-DHOLAK531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मृदंग** — *mṛdaṅg* — "a mridang (a barrel drum)".

> मंदिर में एक बूढ़ा आदमी मृदंग बजा रहा था।
>
> *Mandir meṁ ek būṛhā ādmī mṛdaṅg bajā rahā thā.*
>
> In the temple an old man was playing the mridang.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मृदंग** (*mṛdaṅg*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *mṛdaṃga*, "a kind of drum" (Monier-Williams).
