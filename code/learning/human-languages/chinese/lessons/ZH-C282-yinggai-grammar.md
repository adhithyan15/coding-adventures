## Grammar Lens: 应该 and 不应该, advice and what is right
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-YINGGAI-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C282-YINGGAI-01, ZH-LEX-C282-GAI-01, ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-04, ZH-LEX-C214-HELP214-05, ZH-LEX-C280-ZHEME-01, ZH-LEX-C49-DAY49-03] -->

> **应该 before a verb gives advice, or says what is right; 不应该 says what is not.**

> **你应该去看医生** *nǐ yīnggāi qù kàn yīshēng* — You should see a doctor.
> **你不应该这么晚回家** *nǐ bù yīnggāi zhème wǎn huí jiā* — You shouldn't come home so late.

不 goes in front of 应该, and stays *bù* before the level yīng. 晚 here is late, the 晚 of 晚上.

To ask for advice: *wǒ yīnggāi zěnme bàn?*, what should I do?

该 says the time has come; 应该 says what is right, whatever the time.
