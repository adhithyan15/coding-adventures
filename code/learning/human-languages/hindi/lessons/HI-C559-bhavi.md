## You'll want to know: भावी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C559-KALPANA559-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भावी** — *bhāvī* — "future; to come".

> भावी डाक्टर मेहनत से पढ़ रहे हैं।
>
> *Bhāvī ḍākṭar mehnat se paṛh rahe haiṁ.*
>
> The doctors of the future are studying hard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भावी** (*bhāvī*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *bhāvin*, "becoming; future" (Monier-Williams).
