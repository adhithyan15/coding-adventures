## You'll want to know: immerhin
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C367-EVENTUELL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**immerhin** — "after all, still (on the bright side)".

> Wir haben verloren. Immerhin haben wir ein Tor geschossen.
>
> *We lost. Still, we scored a goal.*

It names the good side of a bad result. The verb comes right after it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*immerhin* joins *immer* "always", from Middle High German *immer* (EtymDB), to *hin* "onward, away": whatever happens, this much stands.
