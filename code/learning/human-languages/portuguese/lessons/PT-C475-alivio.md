## You'll want to know: o alívio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C475-NERVOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o alívio** — "relief".

> Que alívio! O exame correu bem.
>
> *What a relief! The exam went well.*

*Sentir alívio* — to feel relieved.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alívio* comes from *aliviar* "to relieve" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *alleviāre* "to lighten", built on *levis* "light".
