## You'll want to know: ਗੁੱਸਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C326-FEAR326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੁੱਸਾ** — *gussā* — "anger".

> ਝਗੜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਗੁੱਸਾ ਸੀ।
>
> *jhagṛe ton bāad Aman nū̃ bahut gussā sī.*
>
> After the quarrel Aman was very angry.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੁੱਸਾ** (*gussā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ghuṣṣa*, "a choking; grief".
