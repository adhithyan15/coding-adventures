# ശ്മശാനം (śmaśānaṁ) — a cremation ground

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C390-YATRAYAYAPPU390-05, ML-LEX-C391-SAVAM391-01] -->

## You'll want to know: ശ്മശാനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C391-SAVAM391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ശ്മശാനം** — *śmaśānaṁ* — "a cremation ground".

> ഗ്രാമത്തിന്റെ അറ്റത്ത് ഒരു ശ്മശാനം ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *grāmattinṟe aṟṟattŭ oru śmaśānaṁ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> There is a cremation ground at the edge of the village.

Also any burial ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശ്മശാനം** (*śmaśānaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śmaśāna*, "a place where the dead are burnt" (CDIAL 12658).

One more word for telling what happened.
