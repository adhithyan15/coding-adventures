## You'll want to know: प्रेरयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C341-PRAYOJANA341-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेरयति** — *prerayati* — "urges on; inspires".

> आचार्यः शिष्यान् प्रेरयति।
>
> *ācāryaḥ śiṣyān prerayati.*
>
> The preceptor inspires his pupils.

Past (he …): सः मां प्रेरितवान्, *saḥ māṃ preritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेरयति** (*prerayati*) is built on *pra*, "forth", and the root √īr, "to set in motion", in its causative form; MW gives *prerita* "urged, impelled, sent" (MW).
