## You'll want to know: ਹੱਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C308-FEED308-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੱਕਣਾ** — *hakkṇā* — "to drive cattle; to herd".

> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਮੱਝਾਂ ਘਰ ਹੱਕਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *shām nū̃ maiṁ majjhāṁ ghar hakkāṅgā.*
>
> In the evening I will drive the buffaloes home.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੱਝਾਂ ਹੱਕੀਆਂ, *maiṁ majjhāṁ hakkīāṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੱਕਣਾ** (*hakkṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *hakkayati*, "calls to"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *hakkṇā*, "to drive away", under it (CDIAL 13939).
