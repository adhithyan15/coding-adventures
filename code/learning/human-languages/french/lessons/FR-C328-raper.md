## You'll want to know: râper
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C328-COUPER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**râper** — "to grate".

> Il râpe du fromage sur les pâtes.
>
> *He grates cheese over the pasta.*

*Le fromage râpé* — grated cheese; the tool is *la râpe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*râper* comes from a Germanic verb meaning "to scrape together" (EtymDB lists Old High German *raspōn* beside it).
