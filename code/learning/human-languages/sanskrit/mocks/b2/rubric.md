# Sanskrit B2 mock rubric

Score either B2 mock against
[`task-shapes/b2.json`](../../task-shapes/b2.json). Reading, listening, writing,
and speaking are independent 100-point papers. A learner passes only by earning
at least **60/100 on every paper** in one administration; a 240/400 total
cannot compensate for a failed skill, and every section must be attempted.

## Reading and listening

Each of the four reading parts and each of the four listening parts is worth 25
points. Divide each part evenly among its eight reading or seven listening
items and retain one decimal place. Score the keyed purpose, detail,
relationship, inference, organization, implication, tone, or stance. B2
sources use external sandhi; fluent decoding remains part of the construct.

Play every listening part once after one unscored orienting preview, at 150–170
words per minute. Each form uses multiple speakers and documents two distinct
regional Sanskrit pronunciation traditions in the confidential examiner
script. A documented pronunciation tradition is evidence, not theatrical
imitation or romanized dialect spelling. No transcript, romanization,
dictionary, translator, or learner-controlled replay is allowed.

## Writing

The 100–130 word interaction text is worth 35 points: task fulfilment and
relevant content 10, audience and register 7, organization and cohesion 6,
Sanskrit vocabulary and grammar 8, external sandhi 2, and orthographic control
2. The 220–280 word extended text is worth 65 points: task fulfilment and
development 20, organization and cohesion 14, genre and register control 10,
Sanskrit vocabulary and grammar 13, external sandhi 4, and orthographic control
4. A response outside its band loses only content that cannot be evidenced;
there is no automatic length penalty.

Orthographic control includes recognizable ordered letter bodies, vowel signs
and conjuncts, continuous śirorekhā where the word requires it, word-final
visarga or anusvara, and clear word boundaries. Romanized, traced, translated,
copied, non-Devanagari, or answer-model-dependent work earns no independent
writing credit. Vedic accent and morphology remain outside this assessment.

## Speaking

The 210–240 second prepared presentation is worth 35 points, collaborative
problem-solving 30, and the 240–300 second defended discussion 35. Within each
part award 30% for task fulfilment and relevant development, 25% for
organization or interaction and repair, 30% for Sanskrit vocabulary and
grammar, and 15% for pronunciation and fluency. Permit paper keyword notes
made during ten minutes of preparation, never a written answer script. Accept
a consistent documented pronunciation tradition. Score paired candidates only
on their own contributions; a partner's performance cannot compensate.

## Evidence boundary

These source papers, confidential masters, keys, and rubric are not qualified
Sanskrit recordings, independent human review, standard setting, or book-only
validation. They make no readiness or validated-pass claim. Those artifacts
remain separate prerequisites. `assessment.json` is active as a source-artifact
contract; its presence does not satisfy them.
