Persian, chapter 401: Studying for exams.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مرور کردن (morur kardan) — to revise, to go over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a scholarship, then the Persian for a course credit.

You'll want to know: مرور کردن.
مرور کردن (morur kardan) — "to revise, to go over".
قبل از امتحان همه درسها را مرور کردم. qabl az emtehân hame-ye darshâ râ morur kardam. Before the exam I went over all the lessons.
Morur "passing over" + kardan (stem kon-).

The word, taken apart.
مرور is usually traced to Arabic murūr "passing": to revise is to pass over the lessons again. Kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تمرکز کردن (tamarkoz kardan) — to concentrate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a course credit, then the Persian for to revise.

You'll want to know: تمرکز کردن.
تمرکز کردن (tamarkoz kardan) — "to concentrate".
وقتی تلویزیون روشن است نمیتوانم تمرکز کنم. vaqti televiziyon roshan ast nemitavânam tamarkoz konam. When the television is on I cannot concentrate.
Tamarkoz "concentration" + kardan. It takes ruye: ruye chizi tamarkoz kardan.

The word, taken apart.
تمرکز is usually traced to Arabic tamarkuz, a verbal noun built on markaz "centre": to concentrate is to gather at the centre.

Lesson 4 of 5.
جزوه (jozve) — lecture notes, a handout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to concentrate, then the Persian for to cheat.

You'll want to know: جزوه.
جزوه (jozve) — "lecture notes, a handout".
جزوه استاد را از دوستم گرفتم. jozve-ye ostâd râ az dustam gereftam. I got the professor's lecture notes from my friend.
Plural jozvehâ. Students lend and copy each other's jozve before exams.

The word, taken apart.
جزوه (jozve) is usually traced to Arabic juz' "a part, a section": a booklet that is one part of a course.
