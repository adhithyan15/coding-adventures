## You'll want to know: par cœur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C377-REVISION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**par cœur** — "by heart".

> Il apprend le poème par cœur.
>
> *He is learning the poem by heart.*

*Connaître par cœur* — to know by heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cœur* is inherited from Latin *cor* "heart" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), once thought to be the seat of memory.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
