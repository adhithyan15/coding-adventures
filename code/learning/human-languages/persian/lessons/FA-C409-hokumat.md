## You'll want to know: حکومت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C409-HOKUMAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حکومت** (*hokumat*) — "a government, rule".

> در آن زمان حکومت در دست یک پادشاه بود.
>
> *dar ân zamân hokumat dar dast-e yek pâdeshâh bud.*
>
> At that time power was in the hands of a king.

*Hokumat kardan* — to rule. *Dowlat* is the government of the day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حکومت** was borrowed from Arabic *ḥukūma* "government" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as *ḥukm* "ruling".
