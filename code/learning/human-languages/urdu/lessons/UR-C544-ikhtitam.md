## You'll want to know: اختتام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C544-AGHAZ544-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اختتام** — *ikhtitām* — "the close (of an event)".

> اگر اختتام پر وقت ہوا تو ہم سوال پوچھیں گے۔
>
> *agar ikhtitām par vaqt huā to ham savāl pūchheṅ ge.*
>
> If there's time at the close, we'll ask questions.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اختتام** (*ikhtitām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ikhtitām*, "sealing, concluding".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
