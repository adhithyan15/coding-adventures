## You'll want to know: тесто
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C331-TESTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**тесто** (*tésto*) — "dough".

> Тесто должно постоять час.
>
> *Tésto dolzhnó postoyát chas.*
>
> The dough has to rest for an hour.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тесто* is inherited through Old East Slavic *těsto* from Proto-Slavic *těsto* "dough" (EtymDB).
