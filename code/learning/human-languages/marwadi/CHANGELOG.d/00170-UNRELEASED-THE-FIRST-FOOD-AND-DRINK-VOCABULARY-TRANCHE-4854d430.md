## Unreleased — the first food and drink vocabulary tranche

- Added twenty-five <=5-minute sessions across Chapters 23--25 for **दाल, घी,
  सबजी, रोटी, बाटी, चूरमा, चाय**, bringing the track to 197 lessons and 53
  distinct taught headwords against the 300-word pre-A1 target.
- Secured every meaning by ear before spelling. Chapter 23 adds no Devanagari
  sign at all; Chapter 24 isolates only **ट** and Chapter 25 only **य**, each
  one lesson before the word that needs it.
- Contrasted each new sign against the neighbour it is confusable with -- **ट**
  against the **ठ** of **ठेला**, **य** against the **भ** of **भाई** -- so the
  hand learns the difference rather than the shape alone.
- Closed three-word, five-word, and seven-word food maps with separately scored
  listening, speaking, reading, and independent writing.
- Recorded that the source prints **सबजी** without a vowel-killer while other
  Rajasthani writing uses a clustered spelling, instead of presenting one form
  as the only one anybody uses.
- Kept offering, accepting, refusing, asking for more, and every meal-time
  exchange as explicit later work; seven food nouns are a vocabulary map, not a
  transaction.
- Registered `retroflex-tt` and `semivowel-y` in the track's own sound-tag
  owner, the two sounds Chapters 24--25 introduce.
- Grounded all seven forms in Marwari Pathshala Lesson 6, which also
  corroborates the **पाणी** spelling taught in Chapter 4.
- Added exact R1--R4 recalls as they became due, including the long-distance

  brought into range. The strict ramp remains all-zero at 197 lessons: no
  script-closure violation, no glyph shown before it is taught, no headword
  without romanization, and no missed reinforcement window.
