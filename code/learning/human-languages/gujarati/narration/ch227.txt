Gujarati, chapter 227: A fall in the street.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઈજા (ījā) — injury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to swallow, then the Gujarati for lung.

You'll want to know: ઈજા.
ઈજા — ījā — "injury".
શું તમને ઈજા થઈ? — śũ tamne ījā thaī? — "Did you get hurt?"

The word, taken apart.
ઈજા (ījā) is a loan from Arabic īdhā', "harm, hurt", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is '-dh-y (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઘા (ghā) — wound, cut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for lung, then the Gujarati for injury.

You'll want to know: ઘા.
ઘા — ghā — "wound, cut".
આ ઘા મોટો નથી. — ā ghā moṭo nathī — "This wound is not big."

The word, taken apart.
ઘા (ghā) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit ghāta, "killing; blow, bruise", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "stroke, cut" (CDIAL 4460).

આ હાડકું નબળું નથી. — ā hāḍkũ nabaḷũ nathī — "This bone is not weak."

The word, taken apart.
હાડકું (hāḍkũ) is built on hāḍ, "bone", with the suffix -kũ. CDIAL lists hāḍ under the lexical Sanskrit haḍḍa, "bone" (CDIAL 13952).

Lesson 4 of 5.
મચકોડાવું (mackoḍāvũ) — to sprain, to get sprained.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wound, then the Gujarati for bone.

You'll want to know: મચકોડાવું.
મચકોડાવું — mackoḍāvũ — "to sprain, to get sprained".
શું તમારો પગ મચકોડાયો? — śũ tamāro pag mackoḍāyo? — "Did you sprain your foot?"
Word for word, did your foot get sprained.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of મચકોડાવું (mackoḍāvũ) is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form like it. It is the -ā- form, of a thing that happens to you, beside mackoḍvũ, "to twist".

Lesson 5 of 5.
લંગડાવું (langḍāvũ) — to limp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: લંગડાવું.
લંગડાવું — langḍāvũ — "to limp".
તે હવે નથી લંગડાતો. — te have nathī langḍāto — "He no longer limps."

લંગડાવું (langḍāvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists Gujarati lãgṛũ, "lame", and lãgṛāvũ under Sanskrit laṅga, "lame" (CDIAL 10877).
