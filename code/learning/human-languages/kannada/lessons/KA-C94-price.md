## You'll want to know: ಬೆಲೆ

**ಬೆಲೆ** (*bele*) — "price".

**ಇದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು?** (*idara bele eṣṭu?*) — "what is the price of this?", with
**ಎಷ್ಟು**, "how much".
