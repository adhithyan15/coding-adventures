## You'll want to know: উপচে পড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C506-TANK506-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উপচে পড়া** — *upôche pôṛā* — "to overflow; to brim over".

> কলটা বন্ধ না করলে বালতি থেকে জল উপচে পড়বে।
>
> *Kôlṭā bôndhô nā kôrle bālti theke jôl upôche pôṛbe.*
>
> If you don't turn off the tap, the water will overflow the bucket.

Past (he/she …): সেই দুধ উপচে পড়ল, *sei dudh upôche pôṛlô*.

Said of milk boiling over too, as in the past form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপচে পড়া** (*upôche pôṛā*) joins উপচে, the -এ form of a verb that CDIAL lists as Bengali *upacāna*, "to boil over, to overflow", under a reconstructed *utpacyatē*, "boils over" (CDIAL 1808), to পড়া, "to fall": the water falls over the rim as it spills.
