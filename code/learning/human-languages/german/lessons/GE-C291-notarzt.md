## You'll want to know: der Notarzt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C291-NOTRUF-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Notarzt** — "the emergency doctor".

> Der Notarzt kam nach zehn Minuten.
>
> *The emergency doctor came after ten minutes.*

Plural: *die Notärzte*. Feminine *die Notärztin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Notarzt* joins *Not* "need" and *Arzt* "doctor", from Old High German *arzat*, which came from Latin *archiāter* "physician" (EtymDB).
