Gujarati, chapter 544: Same or Different.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ફેર (pher) — a difference.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for capable, then the Gujarati for special.

You'll want to know: ફેર.
ફેર — pher — "a difference".
જો તમે બંને ગીતો સાંભળશો તો તમે ફેર ઓળખી શકશો. jo tame banne gīto sā̃bhaḷśo to tame pher oḷkhī śakśo. If you listen to both songs, you'll be able to tell the difference.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફેર (pher) is filed by CDIAL under phirati, "turns", with a Gujarati pher, "a turn" (CDIAL 9078); how the word came to mean a difference is not explained there.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for capable, then the Gujarati for special, then say pher again]

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for special, then the Gujarati for a difference.

You'll want to know: તફાવત.
તફાવત — taphāvat — "a distinction".
જો બંનેના ભાવમાં મોટો તફાવત હશે તો હું સસ્તું ખરીદીશ. jo bannenā bhāvmā̃ moṭo taphāvat haśe to hũ sastũ kharīdīś. If there's a big difference between the two prices, I'll buy the cheaper one.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
તફાવત (taphāvat) is usually traced to Arabic tafāwut, "a difference, a disparity", through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a difference, then the Gujarati for a distinction.

You'll want to know: અલગ.
અલગ — alag — "separate".
જો બાળકો અલગ ઓરડામાં સૂઈ જશે તો ઘર શાંત રહેશે. jo bāḷko alag orḍāmā̃ sūī jaśe to ghar śānt raheśe. If the children sleep in a separate room, the house will stay quiet.

The word, taken apart.
અલગ (alag) is usually linked to Sanskrit alagna, "unconnected" (CDIAL 700), under which CDIAL gives a Gujarati aḷgũ, "not touching"; this form is not listed there.

You'll want to know: નકલ.

જો આ ચિત્ર નકલ હશે તો તેની કિંમત ઓછી હશે. jo ā citra nakal haśe to tenī kimmat ochī haśe. If this painting is a copy, it will be worth less.

Lesson 5 of 5.
બરાબર (barābar) — evenly matched.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for separate, then the Gujarati for a copy.

You'll want to know: બરાબર.
બરાબર — barābar — "evenly matched".
જો બંને ભાગ બરાબર હશે તો કોઈ ફરિયાદ નહીં કરે. jo banne bhāg barābar haśe to koī phariyād nahī̃ kare. If both parts are equal, nobody will complain.

The word, taken apart.
બરાબર (barābar) is usually traced to Persian barābar, "equal, level". It is also an everyday "right, exactly": બરાબર છે.
