# త్వరగా (tvaragā) — quickly, soon

## You'll want to know: త్వరగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**త్వరగా** (*tvaragā*) — "quickly", and at a parting, "soon".

**త్వరగా రండి** — "come back soon". The respectful **raṇḍi** you already have.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

An invitation to return, built from pieces you own.
