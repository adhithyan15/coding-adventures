## You'll want to know: دیہات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C412-QASBA412-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دیہات** — *dehāt* — "the countryside; villages".

> دیہات میں لوگ کھیتوں میں کام کرتے ہیں۔
>
> *dehāt meṅ log khetoṅ meṅ kām karte haiṅ.*
>
> In the countryside people work in the fields.

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دیہات** (*dehāt*) is built on Persian *deh*, "a village", with the Arabic plural ending *-āt*: it is usually traced to Persian *dehāt*, "villages".
