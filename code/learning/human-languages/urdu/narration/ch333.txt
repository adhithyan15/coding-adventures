Urdu, chapter 333: What It Is For.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مقصد (maqsad) — a purpose; an aim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to spoil, then the Urdu for to spread.

You'll want to know: مقصد.
مقصد — maqsad — "a purpose; an aim".
اس میٹنگ کا مقصد کیا ہے؟ is mīṭing kā maqsad kyā hai? What is the purpose of this meeting?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مقصد (maqsad) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maqṣad, "a place aimed at": in Urdu, the end a thing is done for.

Lesson 2 of 5.
خاطر (khātir) — for the sake of (کی خاطر).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to spread, then the Urdu for a purpose.

You'll want to know: خاطر.
خاطر — khātir — "for the sake of (کی خاطر)".
اس نے اپنے بچوں کی خاطر نوکری چھوڑی۔ us ne apne bachchoṅ kī khātir naukrī chhoṛī. She gave up her job for her children's sake.

The word, taken apart.
خاطر (khātir) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khāṭir, "the mind, regard"; after کی it means "out of regard for", "for the sake of".

Lesson 5 of 5.
استعمال کرنا (istemāl karnā) — to use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a motive, then the Urdu for a benefit.

You'll want to know: استعمال کرنا.
استعمال کرنا — istemāl karnā — "to use".
میں یہ قلم روز استعمال کرتا ہوں۔ maiṅ yih qalam roz istemāl kartā hūṅ. I use this pen every day.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے قلم استعمال کیا, us ne qalam istemāl kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
استعمال کرنا (istemāl karnā) is built from istemāl, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic istiʿmāl, "putting to work", and کرنا, "to do".
