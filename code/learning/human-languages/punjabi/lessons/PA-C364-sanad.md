## You'll want to know: ਸਨਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C364-JOB364-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਨਦ** — *sanad* — "a certificate".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਦਰਜ਼ੀ ਦੇ ਕੰਮ ਦੀ ਸਨਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere kol darzī de kamm dī sanad hai.*
>
> I have a certificate in tailoring.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਨਦ** (*sanad*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *sanad*, "support".
