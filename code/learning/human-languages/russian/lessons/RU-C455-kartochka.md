## You'll want to know: карточка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C455-OPLATA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**карточка** (*kártochka*) — "a bank card; a small card".

> Можно заплатить карточкой?
>
> *Mózhno zaplatít kártochkoy?*
>
> Can I pay by card?

Feminine; a diminutive of *карта*. In speech *карточка* is a bank card.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*карточка* is a diminutive of *карта* 'a card', which EtymDB traces through Polish *karta* and German *Karte*; the source is Latin *charta* 'paper' (Lewis & Short).
