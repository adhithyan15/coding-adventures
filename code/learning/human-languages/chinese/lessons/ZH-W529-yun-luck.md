## Script — 云 first, then the walking piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YUN-LUCK-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 运

**Two pieces, and the walking piece comes last**, though it runs up the left and along the foot. Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The inside piece, 云, four strokes**, as you wrote it: a short bar, a longer bar, a stroke that falls to the left and turns right along the bottom, then a dot.

**The walking piece, three strokes**, as in 这: a dot, a stroke that zigzags down, then a long sweep along the foot to the right.

4 + 3 = seven.
