## You'll want to know: पदन्यासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C522-PADANYASA522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पदन्यासः** — *padanyāsaḥ* — "a dance step".

> नर्तकस्य पदन्यासः सुन्दरः अस्ति।
>
> *nartakasya padanyāsaḥ sundaraḥ asti.*
>
> The dancer's footwork is beautiful.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पदन्यासः** (*padanyāsaḥ*) is built from *pada*, "a foot, a step", and *nyāsa*, "a putting down"; MW gives *padanyāsa* as putting down the feet, a step (MW).
