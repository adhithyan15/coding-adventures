## You'll want to know: yīyuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C44-PLACES44-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yīyuàn* — "hospital".

> *wǒ māma qù yīyuàn le* — My mum has gone to the hospital.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yīyuàn* is built from yī 'healing' and yuàn 'courtyard', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'i* and *hjwenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
