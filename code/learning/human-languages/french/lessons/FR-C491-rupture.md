## You'll want to know: la rupture
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C491-RUPTURE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la rupture** — "break-up; breaking".

> Après la rupture, elle est partie vivre à la campagne.
>
> *After the break-up, she went to live in the country.*

*Être en rupture de stock* — to be out of stock.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la rupture* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *ruptūra* "a break", from *rumpere* "to break" (Lewis & Short).
