## You'll want to know: संगणक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C516-PHON516-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संगणक** — *saṅgaṇak* — "a computer".

> माझ्या भावाने नवीन संगणक घेतला.
>
> *mājhyā bhāvāne navīn saṅgaṇak ghetlā.*
>
> My brother bought a new computer.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संगणक** (*saṅgaṇak*) is a modern Marathi coinage built from Sanskrit *saṁ-*, "together", and *gaṇaka*, "one who counts" (Monier-Williams): the machine that reckons.
