## You'll want to know: بلدية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C450-BALADIYYA450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بلدية** — *baladiyya* — "a town hall; a municipality".

> ذهبت إلى البلدية لدفع الرسوم.
>
> *dhahabtu ilā al-baladiyya li-dafʿ ar-rusūm.*
>
> I went to the town hall to pay the fees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بلدية** (*baladiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-l-d*, "a town, a country" (Lane). It is a noun in *-iyya* made from *balad*: the body that runs a town.
