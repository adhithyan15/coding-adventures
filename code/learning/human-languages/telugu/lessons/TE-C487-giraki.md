## You'll want to know: గిరాకీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C487-KHARCU487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గిరాకీ** — *girākī* — "demand".

> వేసవిలో మామిడి పండ్లకు గిరాకీ ఎక్కువ.
>
> *vēsavilō māmiḍi paṇḍlaku girākī ekkuva.*
>
> In summer there is high demand for mangoes.

Demand for goods in a market; also customers for a shop: గిరాకీ లేదు, there are no buyers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గిరాకీ** (*girākī*) is a market word for demand whose origin is not settled.
