## You'll want to know: चौधरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C262-JANO262-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चौधरी** — *chaudhrī* — "a village headman".

> चौधरी घणो भलो आदमी है।
>
> *chaudhrī ghaṇo bhalo ādmī hai.*
>
> The headman is a very kind man.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चौधरी** (*chaudhrī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *caturdhara*, a family name. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *caudharī*, "the headman of a village" (CDIAL 4612).
