# لعل (laʿalla) — it may be that; perhaps

## You'll want to know: لعل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C421-FURSA421-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لعل** — *laʿalla* — "it may be that; perhaps".

> لعل الطقس يتحسن غدا.
>
> *laʿalla aṭ-ṭaqs yataḥassanu ghadan.*
>
> Perhaps the weather will improve tomorrow.

Like إن, it is followed by a noun or a pronoun: لعله, perhaps he.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لعل** (*laʿalla*) is an old native Arabic particle of hope or expectation, perhaps. Grammarians often trace it to *ʿalla* with *la-*; its origin is not settled.
