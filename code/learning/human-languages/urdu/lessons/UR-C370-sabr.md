## You'll want to know: صبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C370-JALDBAZI370-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صبر** — *sabr* — "patience".

> اگر آپ صبر کریں گے تو سب ٹھیک ہو گا۔
>
> *agar āp sabr kareṅ ge to sab ṭhīk ho gā.*
>
> If you are patient, all will be well.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صبر** (*sabr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣabr*, "patience, endurance".
