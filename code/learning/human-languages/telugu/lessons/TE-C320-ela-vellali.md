# ఎలా వెళ్ళాలి? (elā veḷḷāli?) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-06, TE-LEX-C03-ELAA-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-OBLIGATIVE-01, TE-LEX-C320-SUTIGA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "where?" (*ekkaḍa*), "how?" (*elā*) and "I must go" (*nēnu veḷḷāli*). Then "go
straight on". (*sūṭigā veḷḷaṇḍi*.)

## You'll want to know: asking
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C320-ASK-WAY-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-03, TE-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-06, TE-LEX-C03-ELAA-01, TE-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-03, TE-LEX-C100-LOC100-05, TE-LEX-C77-FROM-01] -->

> **క్షమించండి, ఆసుపత్రి ఎక్కడ ఉంది?**
>
> *kṣamiñcaṇḍi, āsupatri ekkaḍa undi?* — "Excuse me, where is the hospital?"

> **ఆసుపత్రికి ఎలా వెళ్ళాలి?**
>
> *āsupatriki elā veḷḷāli?* — "How do I get to the hospital?"

The second is "how must (I) go to …", with the **-ఆలి** you learned for reasons.

> **ఇక్కడి నుంచి దూరమా?**
>
> *ikkaḍi nuñci dūramā?* — "Is it far from here?"

**-ఆ** at the end makes a yes-or-no question: **దూరం**, "far" → **దూరమా?**, "far?". **ఇక్కడి** is the
form **ఇక్కడ** takes before **నుంచి**.

## You'll want to know: following the answer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C320-GIVE-WAY-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C320-SUTIGA-01, TE-LEX-C81-ORDINAL-02, TE-LEX-C104-LOC104-05, TE-LEX-C100-LOC100-02, TE-LEX-C100-LOC100-01, TE-LEX-C100-LOC100-04] -->

> **సూటిగా వెళ్ళండి. రెండవ మలుపులో ఎడమ వైపు తిరగండి.**
>
> *sūṭigā veḷḷaṇḍi. reṇḍava malupulō eḍama vaipu tiragaṇḍi.* — "Go straight on. At the second turning, turn left."

> **ఆసుపత్రి కుడి వైపు ఉంది. దగ్గరే.**
>
> *āsupatri kuḍi vaipu undi. daggarē.* — "The hospital is on the right. It is close."

**వైపు** (*vaipu*, "side"): **ఎడమ వైపు**, "on the left, to the left"; **కుడి వైపు**, "on the right".
**తిరగండి** (*tiragaṇḍi*, "turn") is the polite command of a verb for turning, with the **-అండి** of **క్షమించండి**.
**దగ్గరే** (*daggarē*, "close by") is **దగ్గర** with an **-ఏ** that leans on it.
