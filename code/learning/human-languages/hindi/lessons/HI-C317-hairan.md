## You'll want to know: हैरान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C317-RAHASYA317-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हैरान** — *hairān* — "astonished".

> मुखबिर का नाम सुनकर दरोगा हैरान रह गया।
>
> *mukhbir kā nām sunkar darogā hairān rah gayā.*
>
> The inspector was astonished to hear the informer's name.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हैरान** (*hairān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥayrān*, "bewildered, at a loss". **हैरान रह जाना** is to be left speechless with surprise.
