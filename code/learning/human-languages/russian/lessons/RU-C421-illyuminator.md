## You'll want to know: иллюминатор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C421-VZLYOT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**иллюминатор** (*illyuminátor*) — "a porthole, a plane window".

> Ребёнок всю дорогу смотрел в иллюминатор.
>
> *Rebyónok vsyu dorógu smotrél v illyuminátor.*
>
> The child looked out of the window the whole way.

Masculine. The window of a ship or a plane; a house window is *окно*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*иллюминатор* is usually traced to Latin *illūmināre* 'to light up' (EtymDB): a round window that lets the light in. EtymDB does not list the Russian word, so its route is hedged.
