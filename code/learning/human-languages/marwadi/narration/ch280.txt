Lesson 1 of 5.
टीम (ṭīm) — a team.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a contest, then the Marwadi for wrestling.

You'll want to know: टीम.
टीम — ṭīm — "a team".
म्हारी टीम जीतै है। mhārī ṭīm jītai hai. My team wins.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
टीम (ṭīm) is borrowed from English team. It is feminine: म्हारी टीम.

Lesson 2 of 5.
गेंद (gẽd) — a ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for wrestling, then the Marwadi for a team.

You'll want to know: गेंद.
गेंद — gẽd — "a ball".
म्हारी गेंद लाल है। mhārī gẽd lāl hai. My ball is red.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गेंद (gẽd) is inherited from Sanskrit genduka, "a ball for play"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi gẽd, "ball" (CDIAL 4248).

Lesson 3 of 5.
झेलणो (jhelṇo) — to catch a ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a team, then the Marwadi for a ball.

You'll want to know: झेलणो.
झेलणो — jhelṇo — "to catch a ball".
भाई गेंद झेलै है। bhāī gẽd jhelai hai. My brother catches the ball.
Past (I …): म्हैं गेंद झेली, mhaĩ gẽd jhelī.

The word, taken apart.
झेलणो (jhelṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed jhēlati, "undergoes"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari jhelaï, "to undergo, suffer", Hindi jhelnā, "to take", and Marathi jhelṇẽ, "to catch" (CDIAL 5413). CDIAL gives the catching sense only for Marathi; the Marwadi sense here is the everyday one.

Lesson 4 of 5.
निसाणो (nisāṇo) — a target; an aim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a ball, then the Marwadi for to catch a ball.

You'll want to know: निसाणो.
निसाणो — nisāṇo — "a target; an aim".
निसाणो दूर है। nisāṇo dūr hai. The target is far away.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
निसाणो (nisāṇo) is usually traced to Persian nishāna, "a mark, a target", built on nishān, "a sign"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सांधणो (sā̃dhṇo) — to take aim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to catch a ball, then the Marwadi for a target.

You'll want to know: सांधणो.
सांधणो — sā̃dhṇo — "to take aim".
छोरो निसाणो सांधै है। chhoro nisāṇo sā̃dhai hai. The boy takes aim at the target.
Past (I …): म्हैं निसाणो सांध्यो, mhaĩ nisāṇo sā̃dhyo.

The word, taken apart.
सांधणो (sā̃dhṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit samdadhāti, "places on, combines"; CDIAL lists an Old Marwari form of this verb glossed "aims", and Gujarati sā̃dhvũ, "to unite, attach" (CDIAL 12898).
