## You'll want to know: हिंमत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C464-HIMMAT464-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हिंमत** — *himmat* — "nerve; courage".

> रात्री एकट्याने जायची त्याची हिंमत झाली नाही.
>
> *rātrī ekṭyāne jāycī tyācī himmat jhālī nāhī.*
>
> He did not have the nerve to go alone at night.

Feminine.

हिंमत होणे, to dare, with the genitive: माझी हिंमत झाली. हिंमत हरणे, to lose heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिंमत** (*himmat*) is a loan from Persian *himmat*, usually traced to Arabic *himma*, "resolve".
