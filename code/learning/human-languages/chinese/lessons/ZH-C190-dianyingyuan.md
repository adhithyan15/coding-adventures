# diànyǐngyuàn — cinema

## You'll want to know: diànyǐngyuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C190-TOWN190-04]; assesses=[] -->

*diànyǐngyuàn* — "cinema".

> *diànyǐngyuàn zài shāngchǎng lǐ* — The cinema is inside the shopping centre.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diànyǐngyuàn* is built from *diànyǐng* 'film' and *yuàn* 'a walled courtyard', the *yuàn* of *yīyuàn*; all three parts are inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH*, *'jaengX* and *hjwenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
