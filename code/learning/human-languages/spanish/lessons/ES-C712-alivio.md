## You'll want to know: el alivio

**el alivio** — "relief".

> ¡Qué alivio! Ya llegaste.
>
> *What a relief! You are here.*

*¡Qué alivio!* — What a relief!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alivio* is built on *aliviar*, which comes from Latin *allevāre* or *alleviāre* "to lighten" (EtymDB), from *levis* "light".

## What you've built
