## You'll want to know: 手工
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C274-FREE274-04]; assesses=[] -->

**手工** — *shǒugōng* — "handicraft; things made by hand".

> **我们先做手工** *wǒmen xiān zuò shǒugōng* — Let's do the crafts first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**手工** (*shǒugōng*) is built from 手 'the hand' and 工 'work', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuwX* and *kuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
