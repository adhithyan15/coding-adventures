## You'll want to know: бюджет
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C395-SBEREZHENIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**бюджет** (*byudzhét*) — "a budget".

> У нас маленький семейный бюджет.
>
> *U nas málenkiy seméynyy byudzhét.*
>
> We have a small family budget.

Masculine. *Государственный бюджет* — the state budget.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**бюджет** is usually traced to English *budget*, from Old French *bougette* "a little bag", from Latin *bulga* "a leather bag" (Lewis & Short): first the bag the money was kept in.
