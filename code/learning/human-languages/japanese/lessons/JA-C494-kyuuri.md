## You'll want to know: きゅうり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C494-VEG494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうり** — *kyūri* — "a cucumber".

> **なつは きゅうりが やすいです。** — *natsu wa kyūri ga yasui desu* — "Cucumbers are cheap in summer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうり** (*kyūri*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
