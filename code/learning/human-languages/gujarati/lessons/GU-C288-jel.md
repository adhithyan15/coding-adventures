## You'll want to know: જેલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C288-KABUL288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જેલ** — *jel* — "a jail".

> ચોર હવે જેલમાં છે.
>
> *cor have jelmā̃ che.*
>
> The thief is in jail now.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જેલ** (*jel*) is borrowed from English *jail*. It is feminine: **જેલમાં**, in jail; **જેલમાંથી**, out of jail.
