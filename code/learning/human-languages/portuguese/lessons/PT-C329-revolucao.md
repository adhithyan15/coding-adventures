## You'll want to know: a revolução
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C329-BATALHA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a revolução** — "the revolution".

> A revolução aconteceu sem violência.
>
> *The revolution happened without violence.*

Also a turn of a wheel or a planet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revolução* was borrowed from Late Latin *revolūtiō* "a turning back" (EtymDB), from *revolvere* "to roll back", built on *volvere* "to roll".
