## You'll want to know: شرح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C350-AUSAT350-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شرح** — *sharah* — "a rate".

> بینک کی شرح بڑھ گئی۔
>
> *baink kī sharah baṛh gaī.*
>
> The bank's rate has gone up.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شرح** (*sharah*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *sharḥ*, "an explanation, a laying out"; Urdu also uses it for a rate (of interest, of growth), a sense our sources do not trace.
