## You'll want to know: ਮੁੜਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C263-TURN263-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁੜਨਾ** — *muṛnā* — "to turn; to turn off".

> ਬੱਸ ਚੌਂਕ ਤੋਂ ਮੁੜਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *bass chauṅk ton muṛdī hai.*
>
> The bus turns at the square.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੋੜ ਤੋਂ ਮੁੜਿਆ, *maiṁ moṛ ton muṛiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁੜਨਾ** (*muṛnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *muṭati*, "twists"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *muṛṇā*, "to be twisted", under it (CDIAL 10186).
