Sanskrit, chapter 193: Purchase, Sale, Object, Item, Tool.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
क्रयः (krayaḥ) — a purchase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for clothing, then the Sanskrit for a garment.

You'll want to know: क्रयः.
क्रयः — krayaḥ — "a purchase".

The word, taken apart.
kraya is built on the root √krī, "to buy", whose present is krīṇāti (MW; CDIAL 3581). MW compares Greek priamai, "I buy". The verb lives on as Bengali kinā, "to buy" (CDIAL 3594), though most modern languages replaced it with other verbs.

Lesson 3 of 5.
वस्तु (vastu) — an object, a thing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a purchase, then the Sanskrit for a sale.

You'll want to know: वस्तु.
वस्तु — vastu — "an object, a thing".

The word, taken apart.
vastu is built on the root √vas, "to dwell, abide" (MW), the root of vasāmi, "I live", one of your first verbs. It first meant "the seat or place of something", then "a really existing thing" (CDIAL 11449).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पदार्थः (padārthaḥ) — an item, a thing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a sale, then the Sanskrit for an object.

You'll want to know: पदार्थः.
पदार्थः — padārthaḥ — "an item, a thing".

The word, taken apart.
padārtha is built from pada, "word", and artha, "meaning" (MW). It first meant "the meaning of a word", then the thing the word stands for, and so "an item, a thing".

Lesson 5 of 5.
उपकरणम् (upakaraṇam) — a tool, an instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an object, then the Sanskrit for an item.

You'll want to know: उपकरणम्.
उपकरणम् — upakaraṇam — "a tool, an instrument".

The word, taken apart.
upakaraṇa is built on upa-, "towards", and the root √kṛ, "to do" (MW). It means both "doing someone a service" and "an implement", the thing that does the work for you (CDIAL 2138).
