## You'll want to know: じもと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C410-JIMOTO410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**じもと** — *jimoto* — "one's home area; the local area".

> **じもとの まつりは なつです。** — *jimoto no matsuri wa natsu desu* — "The festival back home is in summer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じもと** (*jimoto*) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the Sino-Japanese *ji*, "ground" (Middle Chinese *dijH*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, and the native Japanese (wago) *moto*, "origin", inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
