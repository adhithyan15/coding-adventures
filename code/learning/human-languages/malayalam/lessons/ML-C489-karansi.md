## You'll want to know: കറൻസി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C489-PANAM489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കറൻസി** — *kaṟansi* — "currency".

> വിമാനത്താവളത്തിൽ വിദേശ കറൻസി കിട്ടും.
>
> *vimānattāvaḷattil vidēśa kaṟansi kiṭṭuṁ.*
>
> You can get foreign currency at the airport.

വിദേശ കറൻസി, foreign currency; Indian money is the രൂപ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കറൻസി** (*kaṟansi*) was borrowed from English *currency*, the money of a country.
