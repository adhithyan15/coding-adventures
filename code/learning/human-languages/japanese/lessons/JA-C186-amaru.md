## You'll want to know: あまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C186-SAVE186-01]; assesses=[] -->

**あまる** — *amaru* — "to be in surplus; to be left over".

> **おかねが すこし あまります。** — *okane ga sukoshi amarimasu* — "A little money is left over."

Polite **あまります**, *amarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あまる** (*amaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
