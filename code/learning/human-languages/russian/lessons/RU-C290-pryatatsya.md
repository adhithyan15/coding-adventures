## You'll want to know: прятаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C290-MOSHENNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**прятаться** (*pryátatsya*) — "to hide (oneself)".

> Кошка прячется под кроватью.
>
> *Kóshka pryáchetsya pod krovátyu.*
>
> The cat is hiding under the bed.

Imperfective; perfective *спрятаться* (*spryátatsya*). Present *прячусь*, *прячешься*. Without *-ся*, *прятать* hides something else.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прятаться* is *прятать* "to hide" with the reflexive *-ся* (EtymDB): to hide oneself. The origin of *прятать* is not settled.
