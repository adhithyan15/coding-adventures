Hindi, chapter 296: A Crash on the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रफ़्तार (raftār) — speed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a fine, then the Hindi for handcuffs.

You'll want to know: रफ़्तार.
रफ़्तार — raftār — "speed".
ट्रक की रफ़्तार बस से ज़्यादा थी। ṭrak kī raftār bas se zyādā thī. The lorry was going faster than the bus.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
रफ़्तार (raftār) is usually traced to Persian raftār, "a going, a gait", from the Persian verb to go. It is feminine: तेज़ रफ़्तार, high speed.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Hindi for handcuffs, then the Hindi for speed.

You'll want to know: टकराना.
टकराना — ṭakrānā — "to collide; to crash into".
ट्रक एक बस से टकराया। ṭrak ek bas se ṭakrāyā. The lorry crashed into a bus.
Past (it …): वह ट्रक पेड़ से टकराया, vah ṭrak peṛ se ṭakrāyā.

The word, taken apart.
टकराना (ṭakrānā) is the verb made on ṭakkar, a blow or a crash, which CDIAL 5424 lists for Hindi under ṭakkarā, "a blow on the head". What you hit takes से: बस से टकराया.

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सवारी (savārī) is usually traced to Persian savārī, "riding", from savār, a rider. It is feminine whatever the passenger: चालीस सवारियाँ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पहिया (pahiyā) — a wheel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to collide, then the Hindi for a passenger.

You'll want to know: पहिया.
पहिया — pahiyā — "a wheel".
स्कूटर का पिछला पहिया टूट गया। skūṭar kā pichlā pahiyā ṭūṭ gayā. The scooter's back wheel broke.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पहिया (pahiyā) goes back to Sanskrit pradhi, "the rim of a wheel" (CDIAL 8672), where CDIAL records Hindi pahiyā. Its plural is पहिए.

Past (it …): वह गाड़ी पलट गई, vah gāṛī palaṭ gaī.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited from pallaṭṭ, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 7968). It is a whole turning over: a bus पलट गई, and a page or a decision can पलटना (palaṭnā) too.
