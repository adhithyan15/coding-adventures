## You'll want to know: શાણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C464-SANU464-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શાણું** — *śāṇũ* — "wise; discreet".

> શાણો માણસ ગુસ્સામાં કંઈ બોલતો નથી.
>
> *śāṇo māṇas gussāmā̃ kãī bolto nathī.*
>
> A wise man says nothing in anger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શાણું** (*śāṇũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*sajāna-*, "clever"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "wise, discreet, courteous" (CDIAL 13088).
