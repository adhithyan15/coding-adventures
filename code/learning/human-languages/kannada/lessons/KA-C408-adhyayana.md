## You'll want to know: ಅಧ್ಯಯನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C408-PATHYAKRAMA408-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಧ್ಯಯನ** — *adhyayana* — "a study; studying".

> ಅವನು ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ನೀರಿನ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಅಧ್ಯಯನ ಮಾಡಿದನು.
>
> *avanu haḷḷiya nīrina bagge adhyayana māḍidanu.*
>
> He made a study of the village's water.

ಅಧ್ಯಯನ ಮಾಡು, to study (a subject in depth).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಧ್ಯಯನ** (*adhyayana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *adhyayana*, "reading, studying" (Monier-Williams).
