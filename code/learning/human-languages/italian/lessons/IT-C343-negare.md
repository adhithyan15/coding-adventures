## You'll want to know: negare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C343-DUBBIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**negare** — "to deny".

> Il ragazzo nega di aver rotto il vetro.
>
> *The boy denies having broken the glass.*

*Negare di aver fatto qualcosa* — to deny having done something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*negare* is inherited from Latin *negāre* "to say no, to deny, to refuse" (Lewis & Short), a verb built on the same *ne-* "not" as *nōn*.
