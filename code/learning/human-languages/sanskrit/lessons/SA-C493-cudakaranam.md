## You'll want to know: चूडाकरणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C493-SAMSKARA493-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चूडाकरणम्** — *cūḍākaraṇam* — "the first haircut".

> चूडाकरणे नापितः शिशोः केशान् छिनत्ति।
>
> *cūḍākaraṇe nāpitaḥ śiśoḥ keśān chinatti.*
>
> At the first haircut the barber cuts the baby's hair.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूडाकरणम्** (*cūḍākaraṇam*) is built from *cūḍā*, "a tuft of hair", and *karaṇa*, "making"; MW gives "forming the crest, the ceremony of tonsure" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
