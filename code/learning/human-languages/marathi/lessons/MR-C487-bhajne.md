## You'll want to know: भाजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C487-CATKA487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भाजणे** — *bhājṇe* — "to roast; to get burnt".

> स्वयंपाक करताना ती भाजली.
>
> *svayampāk kartānā tī bhājlī.*
>
> She got burnt while cooking.

Past (he …): तो भाजला, *to bhājlā*.

Of people, to get burnt; of food, to roast: शेंगदाणे भाजणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाजणे** (*bhājṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *bhājṇẽ*, "fries, parches", under Sanskrit *bhṛjjati* (CDIAL 9583).
