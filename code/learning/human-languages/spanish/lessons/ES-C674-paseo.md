## You'll want to know: el paseo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C674-PASEO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el paseo** — "the walk, the stroll; the promenade".

> Damos un paseo después de cenar.
>
> *We go for a walk after dinner.*

*Dar un paseo* — to go for a walk. *El paseo marítimo* — the seafront.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*paseo* is built on *pasear* "to stroll" (EtymDB), from *paso* "step", Latin *passus*.
