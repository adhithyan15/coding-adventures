## You'll want to know: die Wanderung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C454-WANDERUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Wanderung** — "the hike, the walk".

> Die Wanderung zum See dauert drei Stunden.
>
> *The hike to the lake takes three hours.*

Plural: *die Wanderungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wanderung* is built on *wandern* 'to hike, to wander' (EtymDB), inherited from Middle High German *wandern* and Old High German *wantarōn*.
