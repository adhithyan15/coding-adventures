## You'll want to know: mientras tanto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C536-MIENTRAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**mientras tanto** — "meanwhile, in the meantime".

> Mientras tanto, su hermano llamó a la ambulancia.
>
> *Meanwhile, his brother called the ambulance.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mientras tanto* is built on *mientras* 'while', which is usually traced to Latin *dum interim* 'while meanwhile', and *tanto*, which comes from Latin *tantum* 'so much' (Lewis & Short).
