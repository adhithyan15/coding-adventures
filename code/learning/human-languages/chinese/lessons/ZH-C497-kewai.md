## You'll want to know: 课外
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C497-SELFSTUDY497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**课外** — *kèwài* — "outside class; extracurricular".

> **我还没有做课外作业** *wǒ hái méiyǒu zuò kèwài zuòyè* — I haven't done my homework yet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**课外** (*kèwài*) is built from 课 'a lesson' and 外 'outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khwaH* and *ngwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a tax' and 'to test' and the second 'the outside'.
