Kannada, chapter 332: After the Disaster.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಿಪತ್ತು (vipattu) — a disaster; a calamity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for ancient, then the Kannada for an era.

You'll want to know: ವಿಪತ್ತು.
ವಿಪತ್ತು — vipattu — "a disaster; a calamity".
ಪ್ರವಾಹ ಆ ಜಿಲ್ಲೆಗೆ ದೊಡ್ಡ ವಿಪತ್ತು ತಂದಿತು. pravāha ā jillege doḍḍa vipattu tanditu. The flood brought a great disaster to that district.
Plural: ವಿಪತ್ತುಗಳು, vipattugaḷu.
ಪ್ರಕೃತಿ ವಿಕೋಪ is the news phrase for a natural disaster.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಪತ್ತು (vipattu) is borrowed from Sanskrit vipatti, "misfortune, adversity, disaster" (CDIAL 11788).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸಿಲುಕು (siluku) — to be caught; to be trapped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an era, then the Kannada for a disaster.

You'll want to know: ಸಿಲುಕು.
ಸಿಲುಕು — siluku — "to be caught; to be trapped".
ಪ್ರವಾಹದಲ್ಲಿ ನೂರು ಜನ ಸಿಲುಕಿದರು. pravāhadalli nūru jana silukidaru. A hundred people were trapped in the flood.
Past (I …): ಸಿಲುಕಿದೆ, silukide.
Where one is trapped takes -ಅಲ್ಲಿ: ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಿಲುಕು (siluku).

The word, taken apart.
ಸಿಲುಕು (siluku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2498 lists Kannada siluku beside Tamil cikku, "to be entangled, caught, ensnared".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬದುಕುಳಿ (badukuḷi) — to survive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a disaster, then the Kannada for to be caught.

You'll want to know: ಬದುಕುಳಿ.
ಬದುಕುಳಿ — badukuḷi — "to survive".
ಆ ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಬ್ಬನೇ ಬದುಕುಳಿದನು. ā apaghātadalli obbanē badukuḷidanu. Only one man survived that accident.
Past (I …): ಬದುಕುಳಿದೆ, badukuḷide.
To be left alive after danger; ಉಳಿ is to remain.

The word, taken apart.
ಬದುಕುಳಿ (badukuḷi) is a native Dravidian compound: baduku, "to live" (DEDR 5372), and uḷi, "to remain" (DEDR 1009).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತ (santrasta) — a victim; one affected.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to be caught, then the Kannada for to survive.

You'll want to know: ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತ.
ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತ — santrasta — "a victim; one affected".
ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತರಿಗೆ ಊಟ ಮತ್ತು ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಕೊಟ್ಟರು. santrastarige ūṭa mattu baṭṭe koṭṭaru. They gave food and clothes to the victims.
Plural: ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತರು, santrastaru.
A news word for people hit by a flood, a fire or a quake: ಪ್ರವಾಹ ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತರು.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತ (santrasta) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃtrasta, "trembling with fear, alarmed" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ (munsūcane) — a forewarning; a forecast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to survive, then the Kannada for a victim.

You'll want to know: ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ.
ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ — munsūcane — "a forewarning; a forecast".
ಹವಾಮಾನ ಇಲಾಖೆ ಮೂರು ದಿನದ ಮುಂಚೆ ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ ಕೊಟ್ಟಿತ್ತು. havāmāna ilākhe mūru dinada muñce munsūcane koṭṭittu. The weather department had given a warning three days earlier.
ಮುನ್, before, and ಸೂಚನೆ, a notice.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ (munsūcane) is a Kannada compound of native mun, "before, in front" (DEDR 5020), and ಸೂಚನೆ, from Sanskrit sūcanā, "an indication, a communication" (Monier-Williams).
