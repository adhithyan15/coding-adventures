## You'll want to know: посыпать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C333-SOLIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**посыпать** (*posýpat*) — "to sprinkle".

> Она посыпала салат укропом.
>
> *Oná posýpala salát ukrópom.*
>
> She sprinkled the salad with dill.

Perfective, stressed on *-сы-*: *посыплю*; the imperfective is *посыпать* (*posypát*). The topping is in the instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*посыпать* is built on *по-* and *сыпать* "to pour grains or powder", an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists no parent.
