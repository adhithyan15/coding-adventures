## You'll want to know: मसक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C225-PACK225-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मसक** — *masak* — "a water-skin".

> हूं मसक मांय पाणी भरसूं।
>
> *hū̃ masak mā̃y pāṇī bharsū̃.*
>
> I'll fill the water-skin.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मसक** (*masak*) is usually traced to Persian *mašk*, "a leather bag for water"; Marwadi breaks up its final *-šk* with a vowel.
