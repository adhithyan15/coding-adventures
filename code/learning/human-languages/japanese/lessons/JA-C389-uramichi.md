## You'll want to know: うらみち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C389-OODOORI389-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うらみち** — *uramichi* — "a back street; a byway".

> **うらみちを とおって、えきに いきました。** — *uramichi o tōtte, eki ni ikimashita* — "I went to the station by the back streets."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うらみち** (*uramichi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ura*, "back" and *michi*, "road"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
