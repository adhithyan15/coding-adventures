## You'll want to know: おおや
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C358-YACHIN358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おおや** — *ōya* — "a landlord; a landlady".

> **へやが こわれたら、おおやに れんらくして ください。** — *heya ga kowaretara, ōya ni renraku shite kudasai* — "If something in the flat breaks, contact the landlord."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おおや** (*ōya*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *ō*, "big" and *ya*, "house", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
