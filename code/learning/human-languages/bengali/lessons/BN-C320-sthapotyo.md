## You'll want to know: স্থাপত্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C320-SMRITI320-05]; assesses=[] -->

**স্থাপত্য** — *sthāpôtyô* — "architecture".

> দুর্গের স্থাপত্য দেখতে অনেক পর্যটক আসেন।
>
> *durger sthāpôtyô dekhte ônek pôrjôṭôk āsen.*
>
> Many tourists come to see the architecture of the fort.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্থাপত্য** (*sthāpôtyô*) is Sanskrit *sthāpatya*, which Monier-Williams gives as 'architecture, building' and derives from *sthapati*, a master builder: the master builder's art.
