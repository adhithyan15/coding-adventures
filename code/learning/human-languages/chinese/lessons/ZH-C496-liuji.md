## You'll want to know: liújí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C496-LEVELS496-05]; assesses=[] -->

*liújí* — "to repeat a school year".

> *tā de chéngjì bù hǎo liújí le* — His marks were poor, so he had to repeat the year.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liújí* is built from *liú* 'to stay' and 级 'a grade', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljuw* and *kip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stay' and 'to stop'.
