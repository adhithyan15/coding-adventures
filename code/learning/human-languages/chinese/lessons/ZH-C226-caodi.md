## You'll want to know: 草地
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C226-LAKE226-01]; assesses=[] -->

**草地** — *cǎodì* — "lawn; grassy ground".

> *gōngyuán lǐ yǒu yí kuài dà cǎodì* — There's a big lawn in the park.

The new character, in pieces: 草 — nine strokes: the grass piece of 菜 on top, then 早 under it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**草地** (*cǎodì*) is built from 草 'grass' and 地 'ground', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshawX* and *dijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'all the plants'.
