## Script you'll notice: ஓ — the letter behind a sign you already read
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-126]; assesses=[] -->

**ஓ** — *ō*.

You have been reading its **sign**, ◌ோ, for a long time: it is the hook on போ (*pō*), "go",
and it is in கோலம் too. This is the other half of the same vowel — the **independent** shape,
used when a word *begins* with the sound.

That is the Tamil pattern you already know from உ, met once more. Every vowel has two bodies:
a free-standing letter for the front of a word, and a sign for inside one.

The next lesson opens with this letter, which is why it arrives here and not later.
