Kannada, chapter 327: All of a Sudden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಷ್ಟರಲ್ಲಿ (aṣṭaralli) — meanwhile; by then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to kidnap, then the Kannada for a hostage.

You'll want to know: ಅಷ್ಟರಲ್ಲಿ.
ಅಷ್ಟರಲ್ಲಿ — aṣṭaralli — "meanwhile; by then".
ನಾನು ಬಾಗಿಲು ತೆರೆದೆ. ಅಷ್ಟರಲ್ಲಿ ಕಳ್ಳ ಓಡಿಹೋಗಿದ್ದನು. nānu bāgilu terede. aṣṭaralli kaḷḷa ōḍihōgiddanu. I opened the door. By then the thief had run away.
ಅಷ್ಟು, that much, with -ಅಲ್ಲಿ, in: "within that much time".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಷ್ಟರಲ್ಲಿ (aṣṭaralli) is native Dravidian: aṣṭu, "that much", is built on the pointing base a-, "that, yonder" (DEDR 1), with the ending -alli, "in".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ (haṭhāttāgi) — all of a sudden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a hostage, then the Kannada for meanwhile.

You'll want to know: ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ.
ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ — haṭhāttāgi — "all of a sudden".
ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ ದೀಪ ಆರಿಹೋಯಿತು. haṭhāttāgi dīpa ārihōyitu. All of a sudden the light went out.
A more bookish partner of ಇದ್ದಕ್ಕಿದ್ದಂತೆ. ಹಠಾತ್ ಸಾವು is a sudden death.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ (haṭhāttāgi) is built on Sanskrit haṭhāt, "by force", the ablative of haṭha, "violence, force" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada adverb ending -āgi.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕೂಡಲೇ (kūḍalē) — at once; immediately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for meanwhile, then the Kannada for all of a sudden.

You'll want to know: ಕೂಡಲೇ.
ಕೂಡಲೇ — kūḍalē — "at once; immediately".
ಸುದ್ದಿ ಕೇಳಿದ ಕೂಡಲೇ ಅಜ್ಜ ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಗೆ ಹೊರಟರು. suddi kēḷida kūḍalē ajja āspatrege horaṭaru. As soon as he heard the news, Grandfather set off for the hospital.
After a past form it means "as soon as": ಕೇಳಿದ ಕೂಡಲೇ (kūḍalē), as soon as (he) heard.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೂಡಲೇ (kūḍalē) is built on the native Dravidian verb kūḍu, "to join, to meet" (DEDR 1882), with the emphatic ending -ē; how it came to mean "at once" is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಏಕಾಏಕಿ (ēkāēki) — abruptly; all at once.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for all of a sudden, then the Kannada for at once.

You'll want to know: ಏಕಾಏಕಿ.
ಏಕಾಏಕಿ — ēkāēki — "abruptly; all at once".
ಏಕಾಏಕಿ ಬಸ್ ನಿಂತಿತು. ēkāēki bas nintitu. All at once the bus stopped.
Of something sudden and without warning: ಏಕಾಏಕಿ (ēkāēki) ನಿರ್ಧಾರ, an abrupt decision.

The word, taken apart.
ಏಕಾಏಕಿ (ēkāēki) is usually traced to Hindustani ekāek, "suddenly, all at once"; its earlier history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು (beccibīḷu) — to be startled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for at once, then the Kannada for abruptly.

You'll want to know: ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು.
ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು — beccibīḷu — "to be startled".
ಗುಡುಗು ಕೇಳಿ ನಾನು ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬಿದ್ದೆ. guḍugu kēḷi nānu beccibidde. I was startled when I heard the thunder.
Past (I …): ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬಿದ್ದೆ, beccibidde.
ಬೆಚ್ಚಿ, startled, and ಬೀಳು, to fall: to jump with fright. The past is ಬಿದ್ದೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು (beccibīḷu) is a native Dravidian compound: beccu, "to be startled, alarmed" (DEDR 5489), and bīḷ, "to fall" (DEDR 5430).
