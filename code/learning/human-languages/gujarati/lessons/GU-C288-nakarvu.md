# નકારવું (nakārvũ) — to deny

## You'll want to know: નકારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C288-KABUL288-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નકારવું** — *nakārvũ* — "to deny".

> પહેલાં તેણે બધું નકાર્યું.
>
> *pahelā̃ teṇe badhũ nakāryũ.*
>
> At first he denied everything.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગુનો નકાર્યો, *teṇe guno nakāryo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નકારવું** (*nakārvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *nakāra*, "the word *na*, no" (CDIAL 6907): to deny is to say no to something. You deny a thing: **ગુનો નકાર્યો**.
