## You'll want to know: avalar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C635-PROMESA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**avalar** — "to vouch for, to guarantee; to back".

> Mis padres me avalan para el piso.
>
> *My parents are my guarantors for the flat.*

*El aval* — the guarantee; *el avalista* — the guarantor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avalar* is built on *aval* 'guarantee', which was borrowed from French *aval* (EtymDB); where the French word comes from is not settled.
