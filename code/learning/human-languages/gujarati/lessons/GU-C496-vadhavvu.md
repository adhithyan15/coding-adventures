## You'll want to know: વધાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C496-DHAVVU496-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વધાવવું** — *vadhāvvũ* — "to welcome with rites; to greet with joy".

> દાદીએ બાળકને ગીત ગાઈને વધાવ્યું.
>
> *dādīe bāḷakne gīt gāīne vadhāvyũ.*
>
> Grandmother welcomed the baby with a song.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને વધાવ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne vadhāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વધાવવું** (*vadhāvvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vardhāpayati*, "increases, gladdens, congratulates"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to welcome ceremonially" (CDIAL 11383).
