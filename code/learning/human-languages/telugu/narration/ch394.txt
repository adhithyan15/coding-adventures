Telugu, chapter 394: Explaining and Justifying.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వివరణ (vivaraṇa) — an explanation; a clarification.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for harmful, then the Telugu for to be useful.

You'll want to know: వివరణ.
వివరణ — vivaraṇa — "an explanation; a clarification".
అతని వివరణ నాకు అర్థం కాలేదు. atani vivaraṇa nāku arthaṁ kālēdu. I did not understand his explanation.
The noun of వివరించు, to explain, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
వివరణ (vivaraṇa) is borrowed from Sanskrit vivaraṇa, "an uncovering, an opening", and so "explanation, exposition" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
సమర్థన (samarthana) — a justification; a defence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an explanation, then the Telugu for clarity.

You'll want to know: సమర్థన.
సమర్థన — samarthana — "a justification; a defence".
తన తప్పుకు అతని దగ్గర ఏ సమర్థన లేదు. tana tappuku atani daggara ē samarthana lēdu. He has no justification for his mistake.
The reasons that back an act or a view.

The word, taken apart.
సమర్థన (samarthana) is borrowed from Sanskrit samarthana, "reflection, deliberation" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for the case made for an act or a view.

Lesson 4 of 5.
సమర్థించు (samarthiñcu) — to justify; to defend (a view).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for clarity, then the Telugu for a justification.

You'll want to know: సమర్థించు.
సమర్థించు — samarthiñcu — "to justify; to defend (a view)".
అతను తన పనిని గట్టిగా సమర్థించాడు. atanu tana panini gaṭṭigā samarthiñcāḍu. He firmly defended his work.
Past (I …): సమర్థించాను, samarthiñcānu.
Also to back someone else: ఆ ప్రతిపాదనను సమర్థించు (samarthiñcu), to support that proposal.

The word, taken apart.
సమర్థించు (samarthiñcu) is built on Sanskrit samarthana (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; the noun is సమర్థన.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఖచ్చితమైన (khaccitamaina) — exact; precise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a justification, then the Telugu for to justify.

You'll want to know: ఖచ్చితమైన.
ఖచ్చితమైన — khaccitamaina — "exact; precise".
అతను ఖచ్చితమైన లెక్క చెప్పాడు. atanu khaccitamaina lekka ceppāḍu. He gave the exact figure.
ఖచ్చితంగా, exactly, for certain.

The word, taken apart.
ఖచ్చితమైన (khaccitamaina) has an origin that is not settled: it may go back to Sanskrit khacita, "set, inlaid" (Monier-Williams), but the step to "exact" is unclear. The ending -మైన makes an adjective.
