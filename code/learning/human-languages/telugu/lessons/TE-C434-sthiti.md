## You'll want to know: స్థితి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C434-VYAVASTHA434-03]; assesses=[] -->

**స్థితి** — *sthiti* — "a state of affairs; a state".

> రోగి స్థితి ఇప్పుడు బాగుంది.
>
> *rōgi sthiti ippuḍu bāgundi.*
>
> The patient is in a good state now.

పరిస్థితి, a situation, which you know, is the same word with పరి-.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**స్థితి** (*sthiti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sthiti*, "standing upright, continued existence" (CDIAL 13769), from *sthā*, "to stand".
