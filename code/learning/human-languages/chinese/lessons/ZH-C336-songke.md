## You'll want to know: 送客
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C336-SEEOFF336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**送客** — *sòngkè* — "to see a guest out".

> **客人走的时候他去门口送客** *kèrén zǒu de shíhou tā qù ménkǒu sòngkè* — When the guests left, he saw them to the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**送客** (*sòngkè*) is built from 送 'to send' and 客 'a guest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *suwngH* and *khaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a guest'.
