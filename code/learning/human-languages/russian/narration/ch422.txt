Russian, chapter 422: At the airport.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
вылет (výlet) — a departure (of a flight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a porthole, then the Russian for the steps.

You'll want to know: вылет.
вылет (výlet) — "a departure (of a flight)".
Вылет задержали на час. Výlet zaderzháli na chas. The departure was delayed by an hour.
Masculine; the stress is on вы- (vý-). Время вылета — departure time.

The word, taken apart.
вылет (výlet) is built from вы- 'out' and лёт 'flight', the root of летать 'to fly'. EtymDB lists взлёт from the same root, Proto-Slavic vŭzletŭ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
вылетать (vyletát) — to fly out, to depart by plane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the steps, then the Russian for a departure.

You'll want to know: вылетать.
вылетать (vyletát) — "to fly out, to depart by plane".
Мы вылетаем рано утром. My vyletáyem ráno útrom. We fly out early in the morning.
Imperfective; perfective вылететь (вылечу, вылетишь). Взлетать is the take-off itself; вылетать (vyletát) is leaving by air.

The word, taken apart.
вылетать (vyletát) is built from вы- 'out' and летать 'to fly'. EtymDB lists взлёт from the same root, Proto-Slavic vŭzletŭ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
терминал (terminál) — a terminal (at an airport); a card machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to fly out, then the Russian for an arrival.

You'll want to know: терминал.
терминал (terminál) — "a terminal (at an airport); a card machine".
Между терминалами ходит автобус. Mézhdu terminálami khódit avtóbus. A bus runs between the terminals.
Masculine. Also a card reader in a shop: оплатить через терминал (terminál).

The word, taken apart.
терминал (terminál) is usually traced to English terminal, which EtymDB derives through French from Late Latin terminālis, on terminus 'a boundary, an end'. The Russian word is not in EtymDB.
