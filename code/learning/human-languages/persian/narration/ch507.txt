Persian, chapter 507: Hobbies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عکاس ('akkâs) — a photographer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to visit each other, then the Persian for reputation.

You'll want to know: عکاس.
عکاس ('akkâs) — "a photographer".
یک عکاس از مسابقه عکس گرفت. yek 'akkâs az mosâbeqe 'aks gereft. A photographer took photos of the match.
Built on 'aks 'photo'. 'aks gereftan — to take a photo.

The word, taken apart.
'akkâs is built on 'aks, 'picture', a loan usually traced to Arabic ʿaks, 'reflection', in the Arabic pattern for a person who does something.

Lesson 2 of 5.
عکاسی ('akkâsi) — photography.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for reputation, then the Persian for a photographer.

You'll want to know: عکاسی.
عکاسی ('akkâsi) — "photography".
سرگرمی پدرم عکاسی است. sargarmi-ye pedaram 'akkâsi ast. My father's hobby is photography.
'akkâs 'photographer' + -i. 'akkâsi kardan — to take photographs.

The word, taken apart.
'akkâsi is built on 'akkâs, 'photographer', with the Persian noun ending -i (EtymDB); 'akkâs itself is built on 'aks, a loan from Arabic.

باغبانی (bâghbâni) — "gardening".
مادربزرگ در بهار باغبانی میکند. mâdarbozorg dar bahâr bâghbâni mikonad. Grandmother gardens in spring.
bâgh 'garden' + -bân 'keeper' + -i. A gardener is bâghbân.

The word, taken apart.
bâghbâni is built on native Persian parts: bâgh, 'garden', usually traced to Middle Persian bāg, the ending -bân, 'keeper', and -i.

آشپزی کردن (âshpazi kardan) — to cook, to do the cooking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for photography, then the Persian for gardening.

You'll want to know: آشپزی کردن.
آشپزی کردن (âshpazi kardan) — "to cook, to do the cooking".
پدرم آخر هفته ها آشپزی میکند. pedaram âkhar-e haftehâ âshpazi mikonad. My father cooks at weekends.
âshpaz 'cook' + -i + kardan. pokhtan is to cook a particular dish.

The word, taken apart.
âshpazi is built on native Persian parts: âsh, 'soup', paz, the present stem of pokhtan, 'to cook', and -i: soup-cooking. kardan is inherited too.
