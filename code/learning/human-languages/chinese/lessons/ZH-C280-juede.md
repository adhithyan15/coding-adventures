## Grammar Lens: 我觉得 and a whole sentence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-JUEDE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C144-MATTER144-05, ZH-LEX-C256-THINK256-01, ZH-LEX-ZENMEYANG-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-COMPLEMENT-01, ZH-LEX-C51-FEEL51-03] -->

> **我觉得 and then a whole sentence gives your opinion: the sentence after it is what you think.**

> **我觉得他说得对** *wǒ juéde tā shuō de duì* — I think he's right.
> **我觉得今天太冷了** *wǒ juéde jīntiān tài lěng le* — I find it too cold today.

Both characters of *juéde*, 觉 and 得, are ones you write, so 我觉得 prints in characters now.

To ask, put 你觉得 in front: *nǐ juéde zěnmeyàng?*, what do you think?

*rènwéi* says the same more formally, a view you hold and would defend; 我觉得 is the everyday one.
