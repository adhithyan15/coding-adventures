Warm-up.

Say तीन, चार and पाँच, then the path behind the first: trīṇi, then tiṇṇi, then तीन.

You'll want to know: डिब्बा.
डिब्बा — ḍibbā — "a box".

The word, taken apart.
डिब्बा (ḍibbā) belongs with Hindi ḍabbā and ḍibbī, "box", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed ḍabba, "box", and its variant ḍibba (CDIAL 5528). CDIAL takes the group to be non-Aryan, perhaps Dravidian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
थैला (thailā) — a bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a broom, then the Hindi for a box.

You'll want to know: थैला.
थैला — thailā — "a bag".

The word, taken apart.
थैला (thailā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sthavi, "sack, bag" (CDIAL 13746). The -l- is an added suffix; the Sanskrit word is known only from the old word-lists.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मोमबत्ती (mombattī) — a candle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a box, then the Hindi for a bag.

You'll want to know: मोमबत्ती.
मोमबत्ती — mombattī — "a candle".

The word, taken apart.
मोमबत्ती (mombattī) is "wax-wick". mom, "wax", is usually traced to Persian mom, and battī, "wick", comes from Sanskrit varti, "wick" (CDIAL 11359).
