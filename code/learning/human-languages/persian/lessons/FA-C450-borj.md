## You'll want to know: برج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C450-MARKAZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**برج** (*borj*) — "a tower; a tower block".

> از بالای برج تمام شهر را دیدیم.
>
> *Az bâlâ-ye borj tamâm-e shahr râ didim.*
>
> From the top of the tower we saw the whole town.

Arabic plural *boruj*. Also a sign of the zodiac.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*borj* was borrowed from Arabic *burj* 'tower', which EtymDB traces through Syriac to Greek *pýrgos* 'tower'.
