## You'll want to know: てつどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C200-CHANGE200-03]; assesses=[] -->

**てつどう** — *tetsudō* — "a railway".

> **この てつどうは みなとまで いきます。** — *kono tetsudō wa minato made ikimasu* — "This railway goes as far as the harbour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てつどう** (*tetsudō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tetsu*, "iron" (Middle Chinese *thet*) and *dō*, "way" (*dawX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
