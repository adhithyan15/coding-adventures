## You'll want to know: जीर्णोद्धारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C509-JARJARA509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जीर्णोद्धारः** — *jīrṇoddhāraḥ* — "a repair".

> एतस्य गृहस्य जीर्णोद्धारः आवश्यकः अस्ति।
>
> *etasya gṛhasya jīrṇoddhāraḥ āvaśyakaḥ asti.*
>
> This house needs repairs.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जीर्णोद्धारः** (*jīrṇoddhāraḥ*) is built from *jīrṇa*, "worn out", and *uddhāra*, "a raising up"; MW gives *jīrṇoddhāra* as the repairing of what has fallen into ruin in a building (MW).

## What you've built
