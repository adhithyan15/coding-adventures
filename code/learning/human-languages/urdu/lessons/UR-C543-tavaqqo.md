## You'll want to know: توقع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C543-ATKAL543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**توقع** — *tavaqqo* — "what one expects (to happen)".

> اگر نتیجہ توقع سے بہتر ہوا تو جشن منائیں گے۔
>
> *agar natījā tavaqqo se behtar huā to jashn manāeṅ ge.*
>
> If the result is better than expected, we'll celebrate.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**توقع** (*tavaqqo*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tawaqquʿ*, "expecting, waiting for".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
