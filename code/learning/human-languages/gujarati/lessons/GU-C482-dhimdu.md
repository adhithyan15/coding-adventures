## You'll want to know: ઢીમડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C482-THOKAR482-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢીમડું** — *ḍhīmḍũ* — "a bump (from a knock)".

> મારા માથા પર મોટું ઢીમડું થયું.
>
> *mārā māthā par moṭũ ḍhīmḍũ thayũ.*
>
> A big bump came up on my head.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢીમડું** (*ḍhīmḍũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ḍhīmma-*, "lump"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "big swelling or bump" (CDIAL 5591).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
