# भ्रातृजः (bhrātṛjaḥ) — a brother's son

## You'll want to know: भ्रातृजः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C499-SVASR499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रातृजः** — *bhrātṛjaḥ* — "a brother's son".

> मम भ्रातृजः विद्यालये पठति।
>
> *mama bhrātṛjaḥ vidyālaye paṭhati.*
>
> My brother's son studies at school.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रातृजः** (*bhrātṛjaḥ*) is built from *bhrātṛ*, "brother" (**भ्राता**), and *ja*, "born", from the root √jan; MW gives "a brother's son" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
