## You'll want to know: साबित करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C348-PRAMAN348-02]; assesses=[] -->

**साबित करना** — *sābit karnā* — "to prove".

> मीरा ने अपनी बात साबित की।
>
> *Mīrā ne apnī bāt sābit kī.*
>
> Mira proved her point.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपनी बात साबित की, *usne apnī bāt sābit kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साबित करना** (*sābit karnā*) joins साबित to करना, "to do". साबित is usually traced to Arabic *thābit*, "firm, established", which reached Hindi through Persian.
