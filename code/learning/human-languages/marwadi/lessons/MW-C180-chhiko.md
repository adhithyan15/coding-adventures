## You'll want to know: छींको
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C180-DHARO180-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छींको** — *chhī̃ko* — "a hanging net for pots".

> छींको नुवो है।
>
> *chhī̃ko nuvo hai.*
>
> The hanging net is new.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छींको** (*chhī̃ko*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śikya*, "a rope sling for carrying things"; CDIAL lists Hindi *chī̃kā* and Sindhi *chiko*, a net or shelf that hangs (CDIAL 12427). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
