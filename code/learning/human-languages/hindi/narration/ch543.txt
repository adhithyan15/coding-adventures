Hindi, chapter 543: Keeping One's Word.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वचन (vacan) — a word given; a pledge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an offer, then the Hindi for a mediator.

You'll want to know: वचन.
वचन — vacan — "a word given; a pledge".
राजा ने रानी को एक वचन दिया। Rājā ne rānī ko ek vacan diyā. The king gave the queen his word.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
वचन (vacan) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit vacana, "speaking, an utterance, a statement" (Monier-Williams); Hindi uses it for the word one gives.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 4 of 5.
शपथ (śapath) — an oath (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an oath, then the Hindi for willing.

You'll want to know: शपथ.
शपथ — śapath — "an oath (formal)".
नए मंत्री ने शपथ ली। Nae mantrī ne śapath lī. The new minister took the oath.
Feminine.
A formal word, used for an official oath; everyday speech says क़सम (qasam).

The word, taken apart.
शपथ (śapath) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit śapatha, "a curse; an oath" (Monier-Williams).
