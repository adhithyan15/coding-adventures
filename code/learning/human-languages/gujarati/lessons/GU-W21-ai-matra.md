# ૈ — one sign for ai

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-AI-MATRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

The attached sign **ૈ** carries the *ai* sound. It sits above a consonant; by
itself here it is only a writing shape, not a word.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-AI-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Watch the model: make the lower high arc from left to right, lift, then add the
second higher arc from left to right. Trace **ૈ** twice and copy it once.
