Gujarati, chapter 184: What it costs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ભાવ (bhāv) — rate, going price.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to weigh, then the Gujarati for kilo.

You'll want to know: ભાવ.
ભાવ — bhāv — "rate, going price".
કેરીનો ભાવ શું છે? — kerīno bhāv śũ che? — "What is the rate for mangoes?"
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભાવ (bhāv) comes from Sanskrit bhāvá, "being, state", built on bhū, "to be". CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with the sense "market rate" (CDIAL 9475).

Lesson 2 of 5.
લાગવું (lāgvũ) — to cost; to seem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for kilo, then the Gujarati for rate.

You'll want to know: લાગવું.
લાગવું — lāgvũ — "to cost; to seem".
આ થેલીના કેટલા રૂપિયા લાગે છે? — ā thelīnā keṭlā rūpiyā lāge che? — "How many rupees does this bag cost?"
It also means 'to seem': સારું લાગે છે, 'it seems nice'.

The word, taken apart.
લાગવું (lāgvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit lagyati, "adheres to", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to come into contact with" (CDIAL 10895).

Lesson 3 of 5.
વજન (vajan) — weight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for rate, then the Gujarati for to cost.

You'll want to know: વજન.
વજન — vajan — "weight".
આ થેલીનું વજન પાંચ કિલો છે. — ā thelīnũ vajan pā̃c kilo che — "This bag weighs five kilos."

The word, taken apart.
વજન (vajan) is a loan from Arabic wazn, "weight, weighing", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is w-z-n, "to weigh" (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
માપવું (māpvũ) — to measure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cost, then the Gujarati for weight.

You'll want to know: માપવું.
માપવું — māpvũ — "to measure".
દરજી ખમીસ માપે છે. — darjī khamīs māpe che — "The tailor measures the shirt."

The word, taken apart.
માપવું (māpvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from māpyatē, "is measured", a reconstructed passive of Sanskrit māpayati, "measures", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to measure" (CDIAL 10054).

Lesson 5 of 5.
માગવું (māgvũ) — to ask for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for weight, then the Gujarati for to measure.

You'll want to know: માગવું.
માગવું — māgvũ — "to ask for".
ગ્રાહક દુકાનદાર પાસે થેલી માગે છે. — grāhak dukāndār pāse thelī māge che — "The customer asks the shopkeeper for a bag."
CDIAL also lists the spelling માંગવું, mā̃gvũ.

The word, taken apart.
માગવું (māgvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mā́rgati, "seeks, requests" (CDIAL 10074).
