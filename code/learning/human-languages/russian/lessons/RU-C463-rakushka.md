## You'll want to know: ракушка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C463-PRILIV-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ракушка** (*rakúshka*) — "a seashell".

> Дети собирают ракушки на пляже.
>
> *Déti sobiráyut rakúshki na plyázhe.*
>
> The children are collecting seashells on the beach.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ракушка* is built on *раковина* 'a shell' (EtymDB), a word that Old East Slavic used for mother of pearl.
