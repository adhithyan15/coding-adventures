Hindi, chapter 316: Trouble in the Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उपद्रव (upadrav) — a disturbance; rioting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for information, then the Hindi for to put off.

You'll want to know: उपद्रव.
उपद्रव — upadrav — "a disturbance; rioting".
अफ़वाह के बाद शहर में उपद्रव हुआ। afvāh ke bād śahar meṁ upadrav huā. After the rumour there was rioting in the city.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
उपद्रव (upadrav) is Sanskrit upadrava, "what attacks or happens suddenly" (Monier-Williams). In the news it is unrest in the streets.

फ़साद (fasād) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic fasād, "disorder, a spoiling". फ़साद करना is to stir up trouble.

You'll want to know: फूटना.
फूटना — phūṭnā — "to burst; to shatter".
पत्थर लगने से बस का शीशा फूट गया। patthar lagne se bas kā śīśā phūṭ gayā. A stone hit the bus and its glass shattered.
Past (it …): वह बोतल फूट गई, vah botal phūṭ gaī.

The word, taken apart.
फूटना (phūṭnā) is inherited from sphuṭyati, "bursts open", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 13845). Glass, a pot or a bubble फूटता है; a quarrel can फूट पड़ना, break out.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कर्फ़्यू (karfyū) — a curfew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to burst, then the Hindi for a staff.

You'll want to know: कर्फ़्यू.
कर्फ़्यू — karfyū — "a curfew".
मेरी राय में कर्फ़्यू ज़रूरी था। merī rāy meṁ karfyū zarūrī thā. In my opinion the curfew was necessary.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
कर्फ़्यू (karfyū) is borrowed from English curfew, taken into Hindi as a masculine noun. A curfew लगता है, is imposed, and हटता है, is lifted.
