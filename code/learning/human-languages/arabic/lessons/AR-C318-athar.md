## You'll want to know: أثر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C318-HADARA318-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أثر** — *athar* — "a trace, a mark; an effect".

> رأينا أثر قدم في الرمل.
>
> *raʾaynā athar qadam fī ar-raml.*
>
> We saw a footprint in the sand.

Plural: آثار, *āthār*.

The plural الآثار is antiquities: عالم آثار, an archaeologist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أثر** (*athar*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-th-r* (Lane), where *athar* is a mark or track left behind; *athara* is also "to hand down a report".
