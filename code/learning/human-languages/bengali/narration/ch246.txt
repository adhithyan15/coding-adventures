Lesson 1 of 5.
বিদেশি (bideshi) — foreign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bakery, then the Bengali for a library.

You'll want to know: বিদেশি.
বিদেশি — bideshi — "foreign".

The word, taken apart.
বিদেশি (bideshi) is Sanskrit videśa, "a foreign land", taken over whole, + the ending -i, "of".
