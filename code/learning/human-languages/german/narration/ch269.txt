German, chapter 269: Jahrzehnte.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
das Jahrzehnt — the decade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the length, then the German for the deadline.

You'll want to know: das Jahrzehnt.
das Jahrzehnt — "the decade".
Das Dorf hat sich in einem Jahrzehnt sehr verändert. The village changed a lot in a decade.
Plural: die Jahrzehnte. Ten years, as a Jahrhundert is a hundred.

The word, taken apart.
Jahrzehnt joins Jahr "year" and -zehnt, built on zehn "ten"; EtymDB lists Dutch jaartien "decade" as its cousin. Both parts are inherited Germanic words.

Lesson 3 of 6.
die Epoche — the era, the epoch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the decade, then the German for the millennium.

You'll want to know: die Epoche.
die Epoche — "the era, the epoch".
Das war eine schwierige Epoche für das Land. That was a difficult era for the country.
Plural: die Epochen. Three syllables: E-po-che.

The word, taken apart.
Epoche was borrowed, through Late Latin epocha, from Greek epokhē "a holding back, a fixed point in time"; EtymDB lists no parent, so this follows the usual account.

You'll want to know: jahrelang.
jahrelang — "for years".
Er arbeitete jahrelang in einer Fabrik. He worked in a factory for years.
Built the same way: tagelang, wochenlang, monatelang — for days, weeks, months.

The word, taken apart.
jahrelang joins Jahre "years" and lang "long", both inherited Germanic words (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 6.
stundenlang — for hours.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the era, then the German for for years.

You'll want to know: stundenlang.
stundenlang — "for hours".
Wir haben stundenlang auf den Bus gewartet. We waited for the bus for hours.
An adverb: it answers wie lange?

The word, taken apart.
stundenlang joins Stunden "hours" and lang "long". Stunde is inherited and has the old English cousin stound "a while" (EtymDB).
