Arabic, chapter 368: What matters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أهمية (ahammiyya) — importance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for awareness, then the Arabic for knowledge.

You'll want to know: أهمية.
أهمية — ahammiyya — "importance".
يعرف الجميع أهمية (ahammiyya) النوم. yaʿrifu al-jamīʿ ahammiyyat an-nawm. Everyone knows the importance of sleep.

The word, taken apart.
أهمية (ahammiyya) is built on the native Arabic root h-m-m, "to concern, to trouble" (Lane). Ahammiyya is a modern abstract noun from ahamm, more important: importance.

قيمة (qīma) is built on the native Arabic root q-w-m, "to stand, to rise" (Lane). A qīma is what a thing stands at: its worth, its value.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ركز (rakkaza) — to focus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for importance, then the Arabic for a value.

You'll want to know: ركز.
ركز — rakkaza — "to focus".
ركز الطالب على دروسه. rakkaza aṭ-ṭālib ʿalā durūsih. The student focused on his lessons.
Present: يركز, yurakkiz.
It takes على: ركز (rakkaza) على ….

The word, taken apart.
ركز (rakkaza) is built on the native Arabic root r-k-z, "to fix firmly in the ground" (Lane). It is Form II, to plant firmly: to fix the mind on one thing.

Lesson 4 of 5.
أهمل (ahmala) — to neglect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a value, then the Arabic for to focus.

You'll want to know: أهمل.
أهمل — ahmala — "to neglect".
أهمل الرجل حديقته فماتت الزهور. ahmala ar-rajul ḥadīqatahu fa-mātat az-zuhūr. The man neglected his garden, so the flowers died.
Present: يهمل, yuhmil.

The word, taken apart.
أهمل (ahmala) is built on the native Arabic root h-m-l, whose Form I Lane gives for water overflowing (Lane). Form IV is to leave cattle to graze with no herdsman, and so to leave a thing to itself: to neglect.

اهتم — ihtamma — "to care about".

Present: يهتم, yahtamm.

The word, taken apart.
اهتم (ihtamma) is built on the native Arabic root h-m-m, "to concern, to trouble" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to be concerned for: to care about.
