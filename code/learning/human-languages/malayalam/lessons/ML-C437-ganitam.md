## You'll want to know: ഗണിതം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C437-GANITAM437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗണിതം** — *gaṇitaṁ* — "formal mathematics; maths".

> എനിക്ക് ഗണിതം ഒട്ടും ഇഷ്ടമല്ല.
>
> *enikkŭ gaṇitaṁ oṭṭuṁ iṣṭamalla.*
>
> I don't like mathematics at all.

At school people also say കണക്ക്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗണിതം** (*gaṇitaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gaṇita*, "counted, reckoned" (CDIAL 3996): mathematics is reckoning.
