## You'll want to know: zǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒu* — "to walk; to leave".

> *wǒ zǒu le* — I'm off.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to hurry'. The sense 'to walk' is not given there, so when it arrived is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
