## You'll want to know: ਸਿਆਹੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C357-SCHOOL357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਆਹੀ** — *siāhī* — "ink".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਕਲਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਨੀਲੀ ਸਿਆਹੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī kalam vich nīlī siāhī hai.*
>
> There is blue ink in my pen.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਆਹੀ** (*siāhī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *siyāhī*, "blackness".
