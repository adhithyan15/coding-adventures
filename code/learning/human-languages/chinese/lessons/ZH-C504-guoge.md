## You'll want to know: 国歌
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C504-SONGS504-03]; assesses=[] -->

**国歌** — *guógē* — "national anthem".

> *tīng guógē de shíhou wǒ gǎnjué hěn jīdòng* — When I hear the national anthem, I feel very moved.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**国歌** (*guógē*) is built from 国 'a country' and 歌 'a song', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwok* and *ka* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
