Russian, chapter 361: Profit and loss.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
предприятие (predpriyátiye) — an enterprise, a business; a plant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a shift, then the Russian for to report.

You'll want to know: предприятие.
предприятие (predpriyátiye) — "an enterprise, a business; a plant".
Отец работает на большом предприятии. Otéts rabótayet na bolshóm predpriyátii. My father works at a large plant.
Neuter. На предприятии — at a plant. Малое предприятие (predpriyátiye) — a small business.

The word, taken apart.
предприятие (predpriyátiye) is built on предпринять "to undertake", from пред- "before" and the root of принять "to take", from the old verb "to take" seen in снимать: an undertaking.

The word, taken apart.
прибыль (príbyl) is built on прибыть "to arrive, to increase", from при- and быть "to be", from Proto-Slavic byti: what comes to be in addition.

доход (dokhód) is built from до- "up to" and ход "a going", from ходить "to go", Proto-Slavic xoditi: what comes up to you.
