## You'll want to know: ਮਲਾਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C318-MISHAP318-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਲਾਹ** — *malāh* — "a boatman".

> ਮਲਾਹ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਤੱਕ ਤਰਿਆ।
>
> *malāh kināre tak tariā.*
>
> The boatman swam to the shore.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਲਾਹ** (*malāh*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *mallāḥ*, "a sailor".
