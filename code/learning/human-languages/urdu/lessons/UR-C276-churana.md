## You'll want to know: چرانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C276-CHORI276-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چرانا** — *churānā* — "to steal".

> چور نے میرا بٹوا چرایا۔
>
> *chor ne merā baṭuā churāyā.*
>
> The thief stole my wallet.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کتاب چرائی, *us ne kitāb churāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چرانا** (*churānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *cornā* under Sanskrit *cōráyati*, "steals" (CDIAL 4933). With **نے** the verb follows the thing stolen: **بٹوا چرایا**, **کتاب چرائی**.
