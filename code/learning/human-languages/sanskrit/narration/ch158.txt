7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
लेखनी (lekhanī) — a pen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for tired, then the Sanskrit for hungry.

लेखनी — lekhanī — "a pen".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

(दिनांकः, dināṅkaḥ.) A century? (शताब्दी, śatābdī.) From here? (इतः, itaḥ.) From there? (ततः, tataḥ.) On both sides? (उभयतः, ubhayataḥ.) All around? (परितः, paritaḥ.) On all sides? (समन्ततः, samantataḥ.)
