## You'll want to know: चतुरंगम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C517-CATURANGA517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चतुरंगम्** — *caturaṅgam* — "chess".

> अरुणः मीरा च चतुरंगं क्रीडतः।
>
> *aruṇaḥ mīrā ca caturaṅgaṃ krīḍataḥ.*
>
> Arun and Mira are playing chess.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चतुरंगम्** (*caturaṅgam*) is built from *catur*, "four", and *aṅga*, "a limb": the four arms of an old army; MW gives *caturaṅga* also as a kind of chess played by four (MW).
