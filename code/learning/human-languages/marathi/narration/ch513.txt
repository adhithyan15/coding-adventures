Marathi, chapter 513: Workshops and Orders.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कार्यशाळा (kāryaśāḷā) — a workshop; a training session.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a supply, then the Marathi for to sell well.

You'll want to know: कार्यशाळा.
कार्यशाळा — kāryaśāḷā — "a workshop; a training session".
शिक्षकांसाठी तीन दिवसांची कार्यशाळा झाली. śikṣakāṁsāṭhī tīn divsāñcī kāryaśāḷā jhālī. There was a three-day workshop for teachers.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कार्यशाळा (kāryaśāḷā) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit kārya, "work", and śālā, "hall" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सादर करणे (sādar karṇe) — to present; to submit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a command, then the Marathi for a department.

You'll want to know: सादर करणे.
सादर करणे — sādar karṇe — "to present; to submit".
मीराने बैठकीत तिचा अहवाल सादर केला. mīrāne baiṭhkīt ticā ahvāl sādar kelā. Meera presented her report at the meeting.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अहवाल सादर केला, tyāne ahvāl sādar kelā.
A report submitted or a song performed: गाणं सादर करणे (sādar karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
सादर करणे (sādar karṇe) is सादर, a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic ṣādir, "issued, going forth", with करणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सूत्रसंचालन करणे (sūtrasañcālan karṇe) — to host a programme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a department, then the Marathi for to present.

You'll want to know: सूत्रसंचालन करणे.
सूत्रसंचालन करणे — sūtrasañcālan karṇe — "to host a programme".
शाळेच्या कार्यक्रमाचं सूत्रसंचालन अरुणने केलं. śāḷecyā kāryakramācaṁ sūtrasañcālan aruṇne kelaṁ. Arun hosted the school programme.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सूत्रसंचालन केलं, tyāne sūtrasañcālan kelaṁ.
The event takes -चं: कार्यक्रमाचं सूत्रसंचालन करणे (sūtrasañcālan karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
सूत्रसंचालन करणे (sūtrasañcālan karṇe) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit sūtra, "thread" (Monier-Williams), and संचालन, "running": the host holds the thread of the programme.
