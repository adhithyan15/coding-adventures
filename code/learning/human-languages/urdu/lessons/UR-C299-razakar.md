## You'll want to know: رضاکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C299-IMDAD299-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رضاکار** — *razākār* — "a volunteer".

> سو رضاکار گاؤں پہنچے۔
>
> *sau razākār gāoṅ pahuṅche.*
>
> A hundred volunteers reached the village.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رضاکار** (*razākār*) is usually traced to Persian *razākār*, built from Arabic *riḍā*, "consent", and Persian *-kār*, "one who does": one who works by choice.
