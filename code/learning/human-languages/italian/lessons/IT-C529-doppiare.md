## You'll want to know: doppiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C529-CINEMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**doppiare** — "to dub".

> Quasi tutti i film stranieri sono doppiati.
>
> *Almost all foreign films are dubbed.*

Also to lap another runner, or to sail round a cape.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*doppiare* is inherited from Late Latin *dūplāre* "to double" (EtymDB), from *duplus* "double".
