## You'll want to know: ঘেরা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C316-GHERA316-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘেরা** — *gherā* — "to surround; to besiege".

> শত্রুর সৈন্যরা দুর্গটা ঘিরে ফেলল।
>
> *shôtrur soinyôrā durgôṭā ghire phellô.*
>
> The enemy soldiers surrounded the fort.

Past (he/she …): সেই সেনাবাহিনী শহরটা ঘিরল, *sei senābāhinī shôhôrṭā ghirlô*.

The stem vowel rises in the past: ঘিরল, surrounded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘেরা** (*gherā*) is filed in CDIAL 4474 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *ghir-*, 'go round', and *ghēr-*, 'surround', which list Bengali *gherā*, 'to surround'.
