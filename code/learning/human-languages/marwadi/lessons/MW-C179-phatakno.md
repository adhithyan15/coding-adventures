## You'll want to know: फटकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C179-PISO179-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फटकणो** — *phaṭakṇo* — "to winnow".

> मां बाजरो फटकै है।
>
> *mā̃ bājro phaṭkai hai.*
>
> Mother winnows the millet.

Past (I …): म्हैं बाजरो फटक्यो, *mhaĩ bājro phaṭakyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फटकणो** (*phaṭakṇo*) comes from a reconstructed *\*phaṭ*, "a sudden movement", a word that imitates a sharp sound; CDIAL lists Hindi *phaṭaknā*, "to winnow" (CDIAL 9038). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
