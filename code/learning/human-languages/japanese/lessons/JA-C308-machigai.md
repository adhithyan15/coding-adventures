## You'll want to know: まちがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C308-MACHIGAI308-01]; assesses=[] -->

**まちがい** — *machigai* — "a mistake; an error".

> **まちがいの でんわが ありました。** — *machigai no denwa ga arimashita* — "Someone rang the wrong number."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まちがい** (*machigai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: the noun of *machigau*, "to be mistaken", the intransitive partner of *machigaeru* (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
