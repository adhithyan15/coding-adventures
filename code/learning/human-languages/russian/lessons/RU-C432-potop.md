## You'll want to know: потоп
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C432-PROTEKAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**потоп** (*potóp*) — "a flood (in a flat); a deluge".

> Соседи сверху устроили нам потоп.
>
> *Sosédi svérkhu ustróili nam potóp.*
>
> The upstairs neighbours flooded our flat.

Masculine; colloquial for a flooded flat. *Всемирный потоп* — the Flood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*потоп* is built on *потопить* 'to flood, to sink', from *топить* 'to drown', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic *topiti*.
