## You'll want to know: صورت حال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C365-HALAT365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صورت حال** — *sūrat-e-hāl* — "a situation".

> اگر صورت حال بدلی تو ہم بھی بدلیں گے۔
>
> *agar sūrat-e-hāl badlī to ham bhī badleṅ ge.*
>
> If the situation changes, we will change too.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صورت حال** (*sūrat-e-hāl*) is built from two loans, each usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic: Arabic *ṣūra*, "a form", and Arabic *ḥāl*, "a state". The shape things are in.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
