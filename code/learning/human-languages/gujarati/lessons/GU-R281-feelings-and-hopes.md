# Review — feelings and hopes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C280-LOST-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C278-VU-PADE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "my bag is lost". (*mārī thelī khovāī gaī*.) Then "I'll have to go". (*māre javũ paḍśe*.)

- [YOU WRITE: **ઃ** in **દુઃખ**, then **ઇ** in **ઇચ્છા**, from memory]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C281-DUKH-01, GU-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C281-FEEL-NOUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C281-FEEL-ADJ-01, GU-LEX-C281-ICCHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-C281-I-01, GU-LEX-C281-SAPNU-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C281-VU-CHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C281-WISH-VANI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I'm afraid. I got angry. I felt sad.]
- [YOU SAY: Today I'm happy. She was sad. I'm tired.]
- [YOU SAY: I want to become a doctor. I don't want to go to the city.]
- [YOU SAY: My wish is to live in the village.]
- [YOU SAY: I had a dream last night. My dream is that one day I'll become a doctor.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C281-FEEL-NOUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C281-FEEL-ADJ-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C281-VU-CHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C281-WISH-VANI-01, GU-LEX-C281-DUKH-01, GU-LEX-C281-ICCHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I'm afraid": **હું** or **મને**? (**મને બીક લાગે છે**.) "I'm happy"? (**હું ખુશ છું**.) "I want to drink tea"? (**મારે ચા પીવી છે**.) Which two dots sit in **દુઃખ**? (**ઃ**, the visarga.)
