## You'll want to know: sinon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C368-CONDITION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sinon** — "otherwise, or else".

> Dépêche-toi, sinon nous allons manquer le train.
>
> *Hurry up, or else we will miss the train.*

It can also mean if not, apart from: *personne, sinon lui*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sinon* is built from *si* "if" and *non* "not", which are inherited from Latin *sī* "if" and *nōn* "not" (Lewis & Short): if not.
