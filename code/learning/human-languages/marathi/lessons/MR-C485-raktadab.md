## You'll want to know: रक्तदाब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C485-MADHUMEH485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रक्तदाब** — *raktadāb* — "blood pressure".

> दवाखान्यात त्यांनी माझा रक्तदाब तपासला.
>
> *davākhānyāt tyānnī mājhā raktadāb tapāslā.*
>
> At the clinic they checked my blood pressure.

Masculine.

रक्त + दाब, pressure, from दाबणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रक्तदाब** (*raktadāb*) is a modern Marathi compound of रक्त, "blood", a tatsama of Sanskrit *rakta* (CDIAL 10539), and दाब, "pressure", the noun of दाबणे.
