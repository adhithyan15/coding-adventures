Persian, chapter 390: Provided that.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
به شرط اینکه (be shart-e inke) — provided that, on condition that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for violence, then the Persian for addiction.

You'll want to know: به شرط اینکه.
به شرط اینکه (be shart-e inke) — "provided that, on condition that".
میآیم، به شرط اینکه زود برگردیم. miâyam, be shart-e inke zud bargardim. I will come, provided that we come back early.
It takes the subjunctive: be shart-e inke + bargardim. With a noun: be shart-e ….

The word, taken apart.
به شرط اینکه (be shart-e inke) is literally "on the condition that": shart was borrowed from Arabic sharṭ "condition" (EtymDB), and be "to" and inke "that" (in "this" + ke "that") are Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
در صورتی که (dar surati ke) — in the event that, if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for addiction, then the Persian for provided that.

You'll want to know: در صورتی که.
در صورتی که (dar surati ke) — "in the event that, if".
در صورتی که باران بیاید، در خانه میمانیم. dar surati ke bârân biyâyad, dar khâne mimânim. If it rains, we will stay at home.
It takes the subjunctive, like agar, and sounds more formal. With the indicative it can also mean "whereas".

The word, taken apart.
در صورتی که (dar surati ke) is literally "in a form that": surat comes from Arabic ṣūra "form, image" (EtymDB), here a state of affairs, and dar "in" and ke "that" are Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
در صورت (dar surat-e) — in case of, in the event of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for provided that, then the Persian for in the event that.

You'll want to know: در صورت.
در صورت (dar surat-e) — "in case of, in the event of".
در صورت نیاز، دکتر به خانه میآید. dar surat-e niyâz, doktor be khâne miâyad. If needed, the doctor comes to the house.
It takes a noun with ezafe: dar surat-e niyâz "if needed", dar surat-e bârân "if it rains".

The word, taken apart.
در صورت (dar surat-e) joins the Persian preposition dar "in" to surat, from Arabic ṣūra "form, image" (EtymDB): in the shape of, in the case of.

Lesson 4 of 5.
مگر اینکه (magar inke) — unless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for in the event that, then the Persian for in case of.

You'll want to know: مگر اینکه.
مگر اینکه (magar inke) — "unless".
فردا میرویم، مگر اینکه برف بیاید. fardâ miravim, magar inke barf biyâyad. We will go tomorrow, unless it snows.
It takes the subjunctive: magar inke + biyâyad.

The word, taken apart.
مگر اینکه (magar inke) is native Persian, built from magar, which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian phrase glossed "perhaps; lest", + inke "that", from in "this" and ke "that".

Lesson 5 of 5.
در غیر این صورت (dar gheyr-e in surat) — otherwise, if not.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for in case of, then the Persian for unless.

You'll want to know: در غیر این صورت.
در غیر این صورت (dar gheyr-e in surat) — "otherwise, if not".
او باید پول را تا فردا بدهد، در غیر این صورت جریمه میشود. u bâyad pul râ tâ fardâ bedahad, dar gheyr-e in surat jarime mishavad. He must pay by tomorrow; otherwise he will be fined.
A formal vagarna; a whole clause follows.

The word, taken apart.
در غیر این صورت (dar gheyr-e in surat) is literally "in other than this form": gheyr comes from Arabic ghayr "other than" and surat from Arabic ṣūra "form" (both in EtymDB), held together by the Persian words dar "in" and in "this".
