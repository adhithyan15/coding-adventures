## You'll want to know: थप्पड़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C319-MARPEET319-04]; assesses=[] -->

**थप्पड़** — *thappaṛ* — "a slap".

> मीरा ने जेबकतरे को थप्पड़ मारा।
>
> *Mīrā ne jebkatre ko thappaṛ mārā.*
>
> Mira slapped the pickpocket.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थप्पड़** (*thappaṛ*) is inherited from *\*thapp*, a flat blow, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6091). Like **घूँसा**, it is struck: **थप्पड़ मारा**.
