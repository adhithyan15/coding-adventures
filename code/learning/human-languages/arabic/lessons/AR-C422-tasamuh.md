## You'll want to know: تسامح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C422-MAWDUI422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تسامح** — *tasāmuḥ* — "tolerance".

> التسامح مهم بين الجيران.
>
> *at-tasāmuḥ muhimm bayna al-jīrān.*
>
> Tolerance is important between neighbours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تسامح** (*tasāmuḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-m-ḥ*, "to be generous, to grant" (Lane). *Tasāmuḥ* is the Form VI verbal noun, being generous with one another: tolerance. سامح, to forgive, is from the same root.
