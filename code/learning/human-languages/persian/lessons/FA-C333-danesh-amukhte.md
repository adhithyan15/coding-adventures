## You'll want to know: دانش آموخته
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C333-NOWJAVANI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دانش آموخته** (*dânesh âmukhte*) — "a graduate".

> او دانش آموخته رشته پزشکی است.
>
> *U dânesh âmukhte-ye reshte-ye pezeshki ast.*
>
> She is a graduate in medicine.

Formal; literally "one who has learned knowledge". The everyday word is *fâregh-ottahsil*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dânesh âmukhte* is built from Persian *dânesh* "knowledge" and *âmukhte* "learned", the participle of *âmukhtan* "to learn". Both are native words.
