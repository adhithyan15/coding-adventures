Gujarati, chapter 328: Evening Prayers at the Temple.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પૂજા (pūjā) — worship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a custom, then the Gujarati for a holy day.

You'll want to know: પૂજા.
પૂજા — pūjā — "worship".
સવારે દાદી મંદિરમાં પૂજા કરે છે. savāre dādī mandirmā̃ pūjā kare che. In the mornings my grandmother worships at the temple.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પૂજા (pūjā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit pūjā, "honour, worship" (CDIAL 8317). It is done with કરવું: પૂજા કરે છે.

Lesson 2 of 5.
આરતી (ārtī) — an aarti (worship with lamps).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a holy day, then the Gujarati for worship.

You'll want to know: આરતી.
આરતી — ārtī — "an aarti (worship with lamps)".
સાંજે મંદિરમાં આરતી થઈ. sā̃je mandirmā̃ ārtī thaī. In the evening the aarti was held at the temple.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આરતી (ārtī) goes back to Sanskrit ārātrika, the waving of lamps before an image, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "the ceremony" (CDIAL 1315). It is feminine: આરતી થઈ, the aarti took place.

Lesson 3 of 5.
પ્રસાદ (prasād) — a blessed offering (of food).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for worship, then the Gujarati for an aarti.

You'll want to know: પ્રસાદ.
પ્રસાદ — prasād — "a blessed offering (of food)".
આરતી પછી દાદીએ બધાને પ્રસાદ આપ્યો. ārtī pachī dādīe badhāne prasād āpyo. After the aarti my grandmother gave everyone some of the blessed food.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પ્રસાદ (prasād) is a tatsama, Sanskrit prasāda, "clearness, kindness, favour" (Monier-Williams): the sweet or the fruit offered to a god and then shared out as the god's favour.

Lesson 4 of 5.
પ્રગટાવવું (pragṭāvvũ) — to light (a lamp).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an aarti, then the Gujarati for a blessed offering.

You'll want to know: પ્રગટાવવું.
પ્રગટાવવું — pragṭāvvũ — "to light (a lamp)".
મીરાએ સાંજે દીવો પ્રગટાવ્યો. Mīrāe sā̃je dīvo pragṭāvyo. Mira lit the lamp in the evening.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દીવો પ્રગટાવ્યો, teṇe dīvo pragṭāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
પ્રગટાવવું (pragṭāvvũ) is built on Sanskrit prakaṭa, "manifest, visible" (CDIAL 8428): to make a flame appear. A lamp or a candle પ્રગટાવે છે; સળગાવવું is for fires.
