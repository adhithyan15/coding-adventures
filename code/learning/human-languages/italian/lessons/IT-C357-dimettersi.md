## You'll want to know: dimettersi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C357-DIMISSIONI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dimettersi** — "to resign".

> Il direttore si è dimesso ieri.
>
> *The director resigned yesterday.*

Reflexive, with *essere*; past participle *dimesso*. From a hospital, *essere dimesso* is to be discharged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dimettersi* comes from Latin *dīmittere* "to send away, to let go, to give up" (Lewis & Short), built from *dis-* "apart" and *mittere* "to send".
