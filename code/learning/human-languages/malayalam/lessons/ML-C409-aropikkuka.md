# ആരോപിക്കുക (ārōpikkuka) — to allege; to accuse

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to deny, then the Malayalam for to claim.

## You'll want to know: ആരോപിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C409-KURRAM409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ആരോപിക്കുക** — *ārōpikkuka* — "to allege; to accuse".

> അയൽക്കാരൻ പണം എടുത്തു എന്ന് അവൾ ആരോപിച്ചു.
>
> *ayalkkāran paṇaṁ eṭuttu ennŭ avaḷ ārōpiccu.*
>
> She alleged that the neighbour had taken the money.

Past: ആരോപിച്ചു, *ārōpiccu*.

The noun is ആരോപണം, an allegation. Against someone: -ന് എതിരെ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആരോപിക്കുക** (*ārōpikkuka*) is built from Sanskrit *āropa*, "laying on (as a burden), charging with" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക: to allege is to lay a charge on someone.

## What you've built
