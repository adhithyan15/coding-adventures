Persian, chapter 387: Migration and minorities.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مهاجرت (mohâjerat) — migration, emigration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for poverty, then the Persian for wealth.

You'll want to know: مهاجرت.
مهاجرت (mohâjerat) — "migration, emigration".
مهاجرت به خارج برای او آسان نبود. mohâjerat be khârej barâye u âsân nabud. Moving abroad was not easy for her.
Mohâjerat kardan — to emigrate.

The word, taken apart.
مهاجرت (mohâjerat) is usually traced to Arabic muhājara "emigration", from the same Arabic family as hijra "departure", which EtymDB lists.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for migration, then the Persian for a migrant.

You'll want to know: اقلیت.
اقلیت (aqalliyat) — "a minority".
آنها در این کشور یک اقلیت کوچک هستند. ânhâ dar in keshvar yek aqalliyat-e kuchek hastand. They are a small minority in this country.
Plural aqalliyathâ. The opposite is aksariyat.

The word, taken apart.
اقلیت (aqalliyat) is usually traced to Arabic aqalliyya "minority", built on aqall "fewer, least".

اکثریت مردم با این طرح موافق هستند. aksariyat-e mardom bâ in tarh movâfeq hastand. The majority of people agree with this plan.

The word, taken apart.
اکثریت (aksariyat) is usually traced to Arabic akthariyya "majority", built on akthar "more, most"; Persian says the Arabic th as s.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تبعیض (tab'iz) — discrimination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a minority, then the Persian for a majority.

You'll want to know: تبعیض.
تبعیض (tab'iz) — "discrimination".
تبعیض بین دختر و پسر درست نیست. tab'iz beyn-e dokhtar o pesar dorost nist. Discrimination between girls and boys is wrong.
Formal. Tab'iz-e nezhâdi is racial discrimination.

The word, taken apart.
تبعیض (tab'iz) is usually traced to Arabic tab'īḍ "dividing into parts": discrimination splits people into groups and treats them apart.
