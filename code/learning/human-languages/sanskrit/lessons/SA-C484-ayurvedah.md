## You'll want to know: आयुर्वेदः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C484-AYURVEDA484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आयुर्वेदः** — *āyurvedaḥ* — "the science of health; Ayurveda".

> आयुर्वेदः आरोग्यस्य ज्ञानम् अस्ति।
>
> *āyurvedaḥ ārogyasya jñānam asti.*
>
> Ayurveda is the knowledge of health.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आयुर्वेदः** (*āyurvedaḥ*) is built from *āyus*, "life", and *veda*, "knowledge", from the root √vid; MW gives "the science of health or medicine" (MW).
