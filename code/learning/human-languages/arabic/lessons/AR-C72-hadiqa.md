## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حديقة** (*ḥadīqa*) is an old native noun, filed under the root *ḥ-d-q* (Lane). Lane's *ḥadīqa* is a garden surrounded by a wall.
