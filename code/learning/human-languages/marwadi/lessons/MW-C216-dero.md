## You'll want to know: डेरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C216-OUT216-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डेरो** — *ḍero* — "a camp; a stopping place for the night".

> आज रात म्हारो डेरो डूंगर कनै है।
>
> *āj rāt mhāro ḍero ḍū̃gar kanai hai.*
>
> Tonight my camp is near the hill.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डेरो** (*ḍero*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍēra*, "a resting-place"; CDIAL lists Marwari *ḍero*, "a tent, a shelter" (CDIAL 5564).
