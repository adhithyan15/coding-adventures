Lesson 1 of 5.
il soldato — a soldier.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a coast, then the Italian for a valley.

You'll want to know: il soldato.
il soldato — "a soldier".

The word, taken apart.
soldato is "one paid": built from soldo, "pay", which from Late Latin soldum, from Latin solidus, a gold coin. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.
la bandiera — a flag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a valley, then the Italian for a soldier.

You'll want to know: la bandiera.
la bandiera — "a flag".

The word, taken apart.
bandiera probably came through Old Provençal bandiera — borrowed; it is built on banda, "an ensign", from Gothic bandwa, "a sign". EtymDB 2.1 builds it on banda and lists Spanish bandera as its cognate.
