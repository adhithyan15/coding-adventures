Hindi, chapter 339: The Prince in Disguise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
राजकुमार (rājkumār) — a prince.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a witch, then the Hindi for a miracle.

You'll want to know: राजकुमार.
राजकुमार — rājkumār — "a prince".
राजकुमार घोड़े पर जंगल में गया। rājkumār ghoṛe par jaṅgal meṁ gayā. The prince rode into the forest.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
राजकुमार (rājkumār) is Sanskrit rājakumāra (Monier-Williams), राजा, a king, and kumāra, a boy: a king's son.

Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
भेस (bhes) goes back to Sanskrit veṣa, "dress, appearance" (CDIAL 12129), where CDIAL lists Hindi bhes. भेस बदलना is to go in disguise.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को लुभाया, usne bacce ko lubhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
लुभाना (lubhānā) goes back to Sanskrit lubhyati, "is eager, longs for" (CDIAL 11086): to make someone want something so much that they follow.
