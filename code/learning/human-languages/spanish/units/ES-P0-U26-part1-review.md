# Unit 26: Part I Review — Foundations, Cumulative

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Four chapters, one recombination. This closes Part I.

## New Material

No new vocabulary — the whole point is reuse. One connected mini-monologue,
touching every major Part I concept:

> "**Hola, me llamo [name]. Soy de [place]. Hoy es [day].**" *(Chapter 1:
> greetings, ser)*
> "**Son las tres. No entiendo — ¿puede repetir?**" *(Chapter 2: time,
> survival phrases)*
> "**Mi familia es grande. Mi madre es alta; mi padre es pequeño.**"
> *(Chapter 3: gender, family, adjective agreement)*
> "**Tengo una casa. No hay problema — o, bueno, hay un problema pequeño.**"
> *(Chapter 4: tener, hay, and Chapter 3's *el problema* exception, all in
> one line)*

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the full monologue above, filling in your own name/place/day]
- [YOU SAY: one *ser* sentence and one *estar* sentence, back to back, and
  say out loud *why* you picked each verb]
- [YOU SAY: "no tengo nada" — the double negative, one more time]

[REPEAT x2] The full monologue, start to finish, without pausing between
lines.

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Across all of Part I, which single concept do you feel least
solid on? Whatever the answer, that's what the bonus queue on your next
few drives should lean toward — the schedule generates reviews from what's
due, but you know your own weak spot better than a fixed formula does.
