## You'll want to know: adoptar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C748-NIETOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**adoptar** — "to adopt".

> Adoptaron a una niña.
>
> *They adopted a little girl.*

Also for pets and ideas: *adoptar un perro*, *adoptar una medida*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adoptar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adoptāre* "to choose for oneself" (EtymDB), built on *optāre* "to choose".
