## You'll want to know: ورشکسته
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C412-SARMAYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ورشکسته** (*varshekaste*) — "bankrupt".

> آن شرکت سال گذشته ورشکسته شد.
>
> *ân sherkat sâl-e gozashte varshekaste shod.*
>
> That company went bankrupt last year.

*Var* + *shekaste* "broken". *Varshekast shodan* — to go bankrupt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ورشکسته** is native Persian, built on *shekaste* "broken", the participle of *shekastan* "to break", which EtymDB traces to PIE *skeyd-* "to break". A bankrupt business is broken.
