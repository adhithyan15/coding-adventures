## You'll want to know: 改名
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C392-NEWPLAN392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**改名** — *gǎimíng* — "to change the name (of)".

> **那家饭店改名了** *nà jiā fàndiàn gǎimíng le* — That restaurant has changed its name.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**改名** (*gǎimíng*) is built from 改 'to alter' and 名 'a name', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kojX* and *mjieng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
