## You'll want to know: промежуток
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C276-DESYATILETIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**промежуток** (*promezhútok*) — "an interval, a gap".

> В промежутке между уроками мы пили чай.
>
> *V promezhútke mézhdu urókami my píli chay.*
>
> In the gap between lessons we drank tea.

Genitive *промежутка*. *Промежуток времени* — a stretch of time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*промежуток* is built on *промеж* "between", a cousin of *между*; both go back to the Slavic root of *межа* "a boundary strip", Proto-Slavic *medja* (EtymDB). EtymDB lists no parent for *промежуток*.
