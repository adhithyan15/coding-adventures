## You'll want to know: manūmittō, manūmittere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C389-SERVITUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**manūmittō, manūmittere** — "to set free (a slave)".

> Dominus servum fidēlem manūmīsit.
>
> *The master freed his faithful slave.*

*manūmittō, manūmittere, manūmīsī, manūmissum*: *manū* from the hand + *mittō*, to let go. The freed slave became a *lībertus*. English *manumit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manūmittō* is built from *manū* "from the hand", the ablative of *manus*, and *mittō* "to let go" (Lewis & Short): to release from one's power. English *manumit* comes from it.
