## You'll want to know: le chef d'orchestre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C513-ORCHESTRE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le chef d'orchestre** — "conductor (of an orchestra)".

> Le chef d'orchestre lève sa baguette.
>
> *The conductor raises his baton.*

A woman is often *la cheffe d'orchestre* or *la chef d'orchestre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le chef d'orchestre* is "the head of the orchestra": *chef* is inherited from Latin *caput* "head" (Lewis & Short).
