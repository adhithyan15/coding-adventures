## You'll want to know: عربة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C428-SIKKA428-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عربة** — *ʿaraba* — "a carriage; a cart".

> جلسنا في العربة الأخيرة من القطار.
>
> *jalasnā fī al-ʿaraba al-akhīra min al-qiṭār.*
>
> We sat in the last carriage of the train.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عربة** (*ʿaraba*) names a cart and a railway carriage; the origin is not settled, and some trace it to Turkish *araba*.
