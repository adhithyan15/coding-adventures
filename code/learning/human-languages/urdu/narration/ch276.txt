Urdu, chapter 276: A Theft in the Lane.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چور (chor) — a thief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fruit seller, then the Urdu for a greengrocer.

You'll want to know: چور.
چور — chor — "a thief".
چور کھڑکی سے آیا۔ chor khiṛkī se āyā. The thief came in through the window.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
چور (chor) is inherited from Sanskrit cōrá, "thief" (CDIAL 4931). It names a man or a woman: the verb shows which, چور آیا or چور آئی.

کل رات گلی میں چوری ہوئی۔ kal rāt galī meṅ chorī huī. There was a theft in the lane last night.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited from Sanskrit caurikā, "theft" (CDIAL 4937). It sits beside چور (chor): the چور (chor) is the person, the چوری (chorī) is the deed, and چوری (chorī) ہوئی says that one took place.

Lesson 3 of 5.
چرانا (churānā) — to steal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a thief, then the Urdu for a theft.

You'll want to know: چرانا.
چرانا — churānā — "to steal".
چور نے میرا بٹوا چرایا۔ chor ne merā baṭuā churāyā. The thief stole my wallet.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کتاب چرائی, us ne kitāb churāī.

The word, taken apart.
چرانا (churānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin cornā under Sanskrit cōráyati, "steals" (CDIAL 4933). With نے the verb follows the thing stolen: بٹوا چرایا, کتاب چرائی.

Lesson 5 of 5.
واردات (vārdāt) — a crime (as an incident); a raid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to steal, then the Urdu for a witness.

You'll want to know: واردات.
واردات — vārdāt — "a crime (as an incident); a raid".
یہ اس گلی میں دوسری واردات ہے۔ yih is galī meṅ dūsrī vārdāt hai. This is the second crime in this lane.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
واردات (vārdāt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic wāridāt, "things that arrive, happenings"; in Urdu the word has narrowed, it seems, to an incident of crime: چوری (chorī) کی واردات, a theft as the police report it.
