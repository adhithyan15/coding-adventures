# ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸು (hastāntarisu) — to hand over

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a refugee, then the Kannada for condolence.

## You'll want to know: ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C375-SHIBIRA375-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸು** — *hastāntarisu* — "to hand over".

> ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವಕರು ಊಟದ ಪೊಟ್ಟಣಗಳನ್ನು ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳಿಗೆ ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *svayaṃsēvakaru ūṭada poṭṭaṇagaḷannu adhikārigaḷige hastāntarisidaru.*
>
> The volunteers handed over the food parcels to the officials.

Past (I …): ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸಿದೆ, *hastāntariside*.

What is handed over takes -ಅನ್ನು; who receives it takes -ಗೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸು** (*hastāntarisu*) is built from Sanskrit *hasta*, "the hand" (CDIAL 14024), and *antara*, "the inside, another" (CDIAL 357): into another's hand, with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
