# దొంగతనం (doṅgatanaṁ) — a theft; a burglary

## You'll want to know: దొంగతనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C329-DONGATANAM329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**దొంగతనం** — *doṅgatanaṁ* — "a theft; a burglary".

> నిన్న రాత్రి మా వీధిలో దొంగతనం జరిగింది.
>
> *ninna rātri mā vīdhilō doṅgatanaṁ jarigindi.*
>
> Last night there was a theft in our street.

దొంగ, a thief, with -తనం, the ending that makes a noun of a state or a deed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దొంగతనం** (*doṅgatanaṁ*) is native Dravidian: *doṅga*, "a thief" (DEDR 2982), with the Telugu noun ending *-tanaṁ*.
