Tamil, chapter 227: To correct, To enquire, To register, To book in advance, To apply.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
திருத்து (tiruttu) — to correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to swallow, then the Tamil for to chew.

You'll want to know: திருத்து.
திருத்து — tiruttu — "to correct".

The word, taken apart.
திருத்து (tiruttu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3251 lists Malayalam tiruttuka, Kannada tiddu and Telugu diddu. It is the causative of tiruntu, "to be correct".

You'll want to know: பதிவு செய்.

The word, taken apart.
பதிவு செய் (pativu cey) is native: pativu, "impression, record", from pati, "to be imprinted" (DEDR 3911), and sey, "to do" (DEDR 1957): "make a record".
