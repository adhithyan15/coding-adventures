## You'll want to know: 一起
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C84-TOGETHER84-01]; assesses=[] -->

**一起** — *yìqǐ* — "together".

> **三年前我们一起上学** *sān nián qián wǒmen yìqǐ shàngxué* — Three years ago we were at school together.

yī changes its tone here, falling to yì before the dipping qǐ: yìqǐ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**一起** (*yìqǐ*) is built from 一 'one' and 起 'to rise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit* and *khiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). Rising as one is a likely picture, but how the pair came to mean together is not settled.
