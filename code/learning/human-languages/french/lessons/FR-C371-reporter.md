## You'll want to know: reporter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C371-ANNULER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**reporter** — "to postpone, to put off".

> Nous avons reporté la réunion à jeudi.
>
> *We postponed the meeting to Thursday.*

*Reporter à plus tard* — to put off till later. The noun *un reporter* is a news reporter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reporter* is built from *re-* "back" and *porter* "to carry", after Latin *reportāre* "to carry back" (Lewis & Short), on *portāre* "to carry": you carry the date further on.
