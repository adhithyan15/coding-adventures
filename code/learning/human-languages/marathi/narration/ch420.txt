Marathi, chapter 420: Embassies and Zoos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दूतावास (dūtāvās) — an embassy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a narrow lane, then the Marathi for a tenement.

You'll want to know: दूतावास.
दूतावास — dūtāvās — "an embassy".
मीरा व्हिसासाठी दूतावासात गेली. mīrā vhisāsāṭhī dūtāvāsāt gelī. Meera went to the embassy for her visa.
Masculine.
दूत is an envoy and आवास a dwelling: the envoy’s house.

The word, taken apart.
दूतावास (dūtāvās) is a modern word built from Sanskrit dūta, "an envoy", and āvāsa, "a dwelling" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्राणिसंग्रहालय (prāṇisaṅgrahālay) — a zoo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an embassy, then the Marathi for a planetarium.

You'll want to know: प्राणिसंग्रहालय.
प्राणिसंग्रहालय — prāṇisaṅgrahālay — "a zoo".
प्राणिसंग्रहालयात आम्ही वाघ पाहिला. prāṇisaṅgrahālayāt āmhī vāgh pāhilā. We saw a tiger at the zoo.
Neuter.
प्राणी, an animal, shortens to प्राणि- at the front of a compound.

The word, taken apart.
प्राणिसंग्रहालय (prāṇisaṅgrahālay) is a modern word built from Sanskrit prāṇin, "a living being", saṁgraha, "a collection", and ālaya, "a house" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
रहिवासी (rahivāsī) — a resident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a planetarium, then the Marathi for a zoo.

You'll want to know: रहिवासी.
रहिवासी — rahivāsī — "a resident".
या इमारतीतले रहिवासी शांत आहेत. yā imāratītle rahivāsī śānt āhet. The residents of this building are quiet.
Masculine.
The plural is the same: एक रहिवासी (rahivāsī), सगळे रहिवासी (rahivāsī).

The word, taken apart.
रहिवासी (rahivāsī) is a Marathi word for someone who lives in a place; it ends like प्रवासी, but how it was formed is not settled.

Masculine.
शाळेचा परिसर (parisar), the school grounds and what is around them.

The word, taken apart.
परिसर (parisar) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit parisara, "neighbourhood, environs" (Monier-Williams).
