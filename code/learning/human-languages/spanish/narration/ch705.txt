Spanish, chapter 705: Orgullo y vergüenza.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el orgullo — pride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to be moved, then the Spanish for to cry.

You'll want to know: el orgullo.
el orgullo — "pride".
Mis hijos son mi orgullo. My children are my pride.
It can be good (pride in your work) or bad (too proud to say sorry).

The word, taken apart.
orgullo was borrowed from Catalan orgull, which comes from Frankish urgōli "pride", a Germanic word (EtymDB). French orgueil is the same word.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: orgulloso.
orgulloso — "proud".
Estoy orgulloso de ti. I am proud of you.
Estar orgulloso de — to be proud of. Feminine orgullosa.

The word, taken apart.
orgulloso is built on orgullo "pride" with the ending -oso, which makes adjectives; orgullo was borrowed from Catalan orgull, from Frankish urgōli (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la vergüenza — shame, embarrassment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for pride, then the Spanish for proud.

You'll want to know: la vergüenza.
la vergüenza — "shame, embarrassment".
¡Qué vergüenza! Me caí en la calle. How embarrassing! I fell over in the street.
Me da vergüenza — I am embarrassed (to). The two dots on ü make the u sound.

The word, taken apart.
vergüenza is inherited from Latin verēcundia "modesty, shame", built on verērī "to respect, to fear" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
avergonzado — ashamed, embarrassed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for proud, then the Spanish for shame.

You'll want to know: avergonzado.
avergonzado — "ashamed, embarrassed".
Está avergonzado por su error. He is ashamed of his mistake.
Used with estar. In Latin America apenado is also common for embarrassed.

The word, taken apart.
avergonzado is built on the verb avergonzar, made from vergüenza, which is inherited from Latin verēcundia "shame" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
avergonzarse — to be ashamed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for shame, then the Spanish for ashamed.

You'll want to know: avergonzarse.
avergonzarse — "to be ashamed".
No te avergüences de llorar. Do not be ashamed of crying.
Avergonzarse de algo. Stressed forms take üe: me avergüenzo.

The word, taken apart.
avergonzarse is built on vergüenza "shame" with the prefix a-; vergüenza is inherited from Latin verēcundia (EtymDB).
