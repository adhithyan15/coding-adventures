## You'll want to know: شغف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C540-NADI540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شغف** — *shaghaf* — "passion".

> يتكلم المعلم عن الأدب بشغف.
>
> *yatakallamu al-muʿallim ʿan al-adab bi-shaghaf.*
>
> The teacher talks about literature with passion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شغف** (*shaghaf*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-gh-f*, "the heart's covering; to strike the heart" (Lane). Lane gives shaghaf as love that reaches the shaghāf, the covering of the heart.
