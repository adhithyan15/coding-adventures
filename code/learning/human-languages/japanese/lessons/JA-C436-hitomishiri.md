## You'll want to know: ひとみしり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C436-BRIGHT436-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ひとみしり** — *hitomishiri* — "shy with strangers".

> **むすめは すこし ひとみしりです。** — *musume wa sukoshi hitomishiri desu* — "My daughter is a little shy with strangers."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひとみしり** (*hitomishiri*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *hito*, "person", *mi*, "seeing (miru)", and *shiri*, "knowing (shiru)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
