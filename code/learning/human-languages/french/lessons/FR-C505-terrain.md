## You'll want to know: le terrain
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C505-TERRAIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le terrain** — "pitch, ground; plot of land".

> Les joueurs entrent sur le terrain.
>
> *The players come onto the pitch.*

*Un terrain de foot* — a football pitch. Also a plot: *acheter un terrain*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le terrain* is inherited from Latin *terrēnum* "land, ground" (EtymDB), from *terra* "earth" (Lewis & Short).
