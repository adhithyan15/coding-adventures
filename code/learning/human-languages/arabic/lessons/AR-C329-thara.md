# ثار (thāra) — to revolt; to flare up

## You'll want to know: ثار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C329-THAWRA329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ثار** — *thāra* — "to revolt; to flare up".

> ثار الفلاحون على الحاكم الظالم.
>
> *thāra al-fallāḥūn ʿalā al-ḥākim aẓ-ẓālim.*
>
> The farmers rose up against the unjust ruler.

Present: يثور, *yathūr*.

ثار غضبه: his anger flared up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ثار** (*thāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-w-r* (Lane), "to rise up", said of dust and of anger, and then of a people.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
