# きっぷを にまい ください (kippu o nimai kudasai) — at the ticket window

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C256-MAI-01, JA-LEX-C205-TICKET205-01, JA-LEX-C205-TICKET205-04, JA-LEX-C205-TICKET205-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "two tickets, please". (*kippu o nimai kudasai*.) Then "a ticket window", "one way" and "a round trip". (*madoguchi*, *katamichi*, *ōfuku*.)

## You'll want to know: 〜までの きっぷ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C256-TICKET-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C203-FLY203-02, JA-LEX-C134-DENSHA] -->

> **くうこうまでの きっぷを ください。**
>
> *kūkō made no kippu o kudasai.* — "A ticket to the airport, please."

**まで** says how far; **の** joins it to the ticket: "a ticket as far as the airport". The number and **まい** go before **ください**:

> **くうこうまでの きっぷを にまい ください。**
>
> *kūkō made no kippu o nimai kudasai.* — "Two tickets to the airport, please."

> **かたみちですか。**
>
> *katamichi desu ka.* — "One way?"

> **いいえ、おうふくを にまい ください。**
>
> *iie, ōfuku o nimai kudasai.* — "No, two returns, please."

## You'll want to know: なんじに でますか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C256-TICKET-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C171-ORDER171-03, JA-LEX-C200-CHANGE200-02, JA-LEX-C200-CHANGE200-04] -->

**なんじ** (*nanji*), "what time", is **なん**, "what", with **じ** (*ji*), "o'clock". **でます** (*demasu*) is what a train does when it leaves:

> **つぎの でんしゃは なんじに でますか。**
>
> *tsugi no densha wa nanji ni demasu ka.* — "What time does the next train leave?"

> **くじに でます。**
>
> *kuji ni demasu.* — "It leaves at nine."

> **のりばは どこですか。**
>
> *noriba wa doko desu ka.* — "Where does it leave from?"
