## You'll want to know: procus, procī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C535-AMAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**procus, procī** — "a suitor".

> Multī procī fīliam rēgis petunt.
>
> *Many suitors seek the king's daughter.*

Masculine, second declension; usually plural. A word of epic and comedy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**procus** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to ask" (EtymDB), the same root as *precor* "to pray": a suitor is one who asks.
