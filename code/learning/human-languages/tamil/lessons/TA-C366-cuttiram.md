## You'll want to know: சூத்திரம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C366-TURAI366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**சூத்திரம்** — *cūttiram* — "a formula".

> ஆசிரியர் கரும்பலகையில் ஒரு சூத்திரத்தை எழுதினார்.
>
> *āciriyar karumpalakaiyil oru cūttirattai eḻutiṉār.*
>
> The teacher wrote a formula on the blackboard.

Sanskrit sūtra is a thread: a short rule that strings ideas together. வெற்றியின் சூத்திரம், the formula for success.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சூத்திரம்** (*cūttiram*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sūtra*, "thread, cord" (CDIAL 13561), which also named a short rule in grammar and science. Tamil writes the s as ச.
