## You'll want to know: غذا دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C539-GARDEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**غذا دادن** (*ghazâ dâdan*) — "to feed".

> دخترم هر صبح به ماهیها غذا میدهد.
>
> *dokhtaram har sobh be mâhihâ ghazâ midahad.*
>
> My daughter feeds the fish every morning.

*be kasi ghazâ dâdan*; *ghazâ* 'food' + *dâdan* 'to give'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ghazâ* comes from Arabic *ġiḏāʾ*, 'nourishment, food' (EtymDB). *dâdan*, 'to give', is inherited Persian.
