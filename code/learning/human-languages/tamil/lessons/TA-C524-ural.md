## You'll want to know: உரல்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C524-SAMAIYAL524-02]; assesses=[] -->

**உரல்** — *ural* — "a mortar (for pounding)".

> முன்பு அரிசியை உரலில் இடித்தார்கள்.
>
> *muṉpu ariciyai uralil iṭittārkaḷ.*
>
> In the past they pounded rice in a mortar.

Grain is pounded in an உரல் with a heavy pole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உரல்** (*ural*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 651 gives உரல் (*ural*), "a mortar", with cognates across the south.
