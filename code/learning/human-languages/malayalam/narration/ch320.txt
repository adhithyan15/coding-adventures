Malayalam, chapter 320: Famous, Ordinary, Dear, Terrible, Awesome.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പ്രശസ്തമായ (praśastamāya) — famous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for arrogance, then the Malayalam for strict.

You'll want to know: പ്രശസ്തമായ.
പ്രശസ്തമായ — praśastamāya — "famous".

The word, taken apart.
പ്രശസ്തമായ (praśastamāya) joins Sanskrit praśasta, "praised, celebrated", to native āya, "having become" (DEDR 333). DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

ഭയങ്കരമായ (bhayaṅkaramāya) is Sanskrit bhayaṅkara, "causing fear": bhaya, "fear", and kara, "making", with native āya, "having become" (DEDR 333). DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

Lesson 5 of 5.
അടിപൊളി (aṭipoḷi) — awesome, great (slang).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for dear, then the Malayalam for terrible.

You'll want to know: അടിപൊളി.
അടിപൊളി — aṭipoḷi — "awesome, great (slang)".

The word, taken apart.
അടിപൊളി (aṭipoḷi) is slang built from native parts: aṭi, "a blow" (DEDR 77), and poḷi, from the verb "to break open" (DEDR 4560), much like English smashing.
