Persian, chapter 240: Button, Sleeve, Umbrella, Chick, Herbs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دکمه (dokme) — a button.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a basket, then the Persian for rubbish.
