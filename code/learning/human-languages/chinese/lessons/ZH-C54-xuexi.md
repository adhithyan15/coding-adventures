## You'll want to know: xuéxí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C54-LEARN54-01]; assesses=[] -->

*xuéxí* — "to study; to learn".

> *wǒ xuéxí hànyǔ* — I'm learning Chinese.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuéxí* is built from 学 'to learn' and xí 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haewk* and *zip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses *zip* with the first.
