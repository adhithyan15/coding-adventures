# ఉక్కపోత (ukkapōta) — muggy heat

## You'll want to know: ఉక్కపోత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C476-USHNOGRATA476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉక్కపోత** — *ukkapōta* — "muggy heat".

> వర్షం ముందు ఉక్కపోత భరించలేకపోయాం.
>
> *varṣaṁ mundu ukkapōta bhariñcalēkapōyāṁ.*
>
> Before the rain we could not bear the muggy heat.

ఉక్క, closeness of the air, + పోత. The heat that makes you sweat without any breeze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉక్కపోత** (*ukkapōta*) is a Telugu compound: ఉక్క, "sultriness, closeness of the weather" (DEDR 568), native Dravidian, and -పోత, whose origin is not settled.
