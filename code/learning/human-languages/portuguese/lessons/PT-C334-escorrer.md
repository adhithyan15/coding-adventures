## You'll want to know: escorrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C334-GRATINAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**escorrer** — "to drain".

> Ela escorreu a massa e juntou o molho.
>
> *She drained the pasta and added the sauce.*

*Pôr a escorrer* — to leave to drain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escorrer* comes from Latin *excurrere* "to run out" (EtymDB), from *ex* "out" and *currere* "to run".
