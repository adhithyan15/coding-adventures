## You'll want to know: yáotóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C476-NECK476-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yáotóu* — "to shake one's head".

> *wǒ wèn tā qù bu qù tā yáotóu* — I asked him whether he was going, and he shook his head.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yáotóu* is built from *yáo* 'to shake' and 头 'head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yew* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move'.
