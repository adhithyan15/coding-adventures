# આશા રાખવી (āśā rākhvī) — to hope

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to invite, then the Gujarati for to pester.

## You'll want to know: આશા રાખવી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C272-PROMISE272-05]; assesses=[] -->

**આશા રાખવી** — *āśā rākhvī* — "to hope".

> **હું આશા રાખું છું કે તમે આવશો.** — *hũ āśā rākhũ chũ ke tame āvśo* — "I hope that you will come."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આશા રાખવી** (*āśā rākhvī*), to keep a hope, is built from *āśā*, Sanskrit *āśā́*, "hope", taken over as a tatsama (EtymDB; CDIAL 7278 cites it), and the taught verb *rākhvũ*, "to keep".
