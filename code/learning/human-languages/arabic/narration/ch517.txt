Arabic, chapter 517: Habits good and bad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دخن (dakhkhana) — to smoke tobacco.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to have a cough, then the Arabic for a sprain.

You'll want to know: دخن.
دخن — dakhkhana — "to smoke tobacco".
لا يدخن أبي في البيت. lā yudakhkhinu abī fī al-bayt. My father does not smoke at home.
Present: يدخن, yudakhkhin.
The noun تدخين is smoking.

The word, taken apart.
دخن (dakhkhana) is built on the native Arabic root d-kh-n, "smoke" (Lane). Midkhana, a chimney, which you know, is from the same root.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

اشتهى — ishtahā — "to crave".
اشتهت أختي الشوكولاتة في الليل. ishtahat ukhtī ash-shūkūlāta fī al-layl. My sister craved chocolate in the night.
Present: يشتهي, yashtahī.

The word, taken apart.
اشتهى (ishtahā) is built on the native Arabic root sh-h-w, "to desire, to long for" (Lane). Form VIII ishtahā is to long for a thing, often food.

أدمن الشاب ألعاب الفيديو. admana ash-shābb alʿāb al-fīdiyū. The young man became addicted to video games.
Present: يدمن, yudmin.

The word, taken apart.
أدمن (admana) is built on the native Arabic root d-m-n, "to keep to a thing" (Lane). Form IV admana is to keep at a thing without stopping.

Lesson 5 of 5.
شبع (shabiʿa) — to be full (from eating).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

شبع — shabiʿa — "to be full (from eating)".
أكلت كثيرا وشبعت. akaltu kathīran wa-shabiʿtu. I ate a lot and was full.
Present: يشبع, yashbaʿ.

The word, taken apart.
شبع (shabiʿa) is built on the native Arabic root sh-b-ʿ, "to be satisfied with food" (Lane). Shabiʿa is the opposite of jāʿa, to get hungry.
