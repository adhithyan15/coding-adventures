## You'll want to know: उत्सुक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C464-UBNA464-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सुक** — *utsuk* — "eager; keen".

> बच्चे नया खिलौना देखने के लिए उत्सुक थे।
>
> *bacce nayā khilaunā dekhne ke liye utsuk the.*
>
> The children were eager to see the new toy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सुक** (*utsuk*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *utsuka*, "restless, uneasy" and so "eager" (Monier-Williams).
