## You'll want to know: इम्तिहान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C286-IMTIHAN286-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इम्तिहान** — *imtihān* — "an exam".

> काल इम्तिहान है।
>
> *kāl imtihān hai.*
>
> There's an exam tomorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इम्तिहान** (*imtihān*) is usually traced, through Persian *imtihān*, to Arabic *imtiḥān*, "a test, a trial".
