# ഋ (ṛ) — the letter that opens ഋതു

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-128, ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-109] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **ൃ** and **ര** once each, and name them. (The *ṛ* sign, as in
**കൃഷി**; and *ra*.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ഋതു** (*ṛtu*), "a season". This lesson takes its first letter out and puts it
in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ഋ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C346-VOCALIC-R-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C346-RTU-01] -->

**ഋ** — *ṛ*, the Sanskrit vowel *r*, at the start of a word. Malayalam says it *ru*, with a short
*u* after the *r*: **ഋതു** sounds close to *rutu*.

After a consonant it is the sign **ൃ**, as in **കൃഷി** and **സുഹൃത്ത്**. At the start of a word it
stands as a letter of its own, **ഋ**. Only a few learned words begin with it.

Its shape is two upper loops joined by a sagging waist, with an oval below.

## Writing: ഋ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C346-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

**ഋ** is two parts: on top, two loops joined by a sagging waist, and below them, an oval. Write
the top first, then the oval under it.

Write **ഋ** yourself, slowly, and larger than it is printed. Copy the printed shape.
