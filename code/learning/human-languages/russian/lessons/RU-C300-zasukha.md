## You'll want to know: засуха
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C300-NAVODNENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**засуха** (*zásukha*) — "a drought".

> Летом была сильная засуха.
>
> *Létom bylá sílnaya zásukha.*
>
> In the summer there was a severe drought.

Feminine; stressed on *за-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*засуха* is built on *за-* and *сухой* "dry", inherited from Proto-Slavic *suxŭ*, from PIE *h₂sews-* "dry" (EtymDB).
