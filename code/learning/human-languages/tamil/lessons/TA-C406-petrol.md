# பெட்ரோல் (peṭrōl) — petrol

## You'll want to know: பெட்ரோல்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C406-PETROL406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**பெட்ரோல்** — *peṭrōl* — "petrol".

> பெட்ரோல் விலை மீண்டும் ஏறியது.
>
> *peṭrōl vilai mīṇṭum ēṟiyatu.*
>
> The price of petrol went up again.

Prices climb in Tamil: விலை ஏறியது, the price went up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பெட்ரோல்** (*peṭrōl*) is a loan from English *petrol*.
