## You'll want to know: ফর্দ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C422-PORAKH422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ফর্দ** — *phôrdô* — "a shopping list; a list".

> বাজারে যাওয়ার আগে মীরা একটা ফর্দ লিখল।
>
> *bājāre jāoyār āge mīrā ekṭā phôrdô likhlô.*
>
> Before going to the market Mira wrote a list.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফর্দ** (*phôrdô*) is usually traced to Persian *fard*, "a single sheet, a list", from Arabic.
