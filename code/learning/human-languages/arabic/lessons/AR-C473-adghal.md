## You'll want to know: أدغال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C473-WAHA473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أدغال** — *adghāl* — "a jungle; thick undergrowth".

> تعيش النمور في الأدغال.
>
> *taʿīshu an-numūr fī al-adghāl.*
>
> Tigers live in the jungle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أدغال** (*adghāl*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *d-gh-l*, "a thicket". It is a plural in form.
