## You'll want to know: لخص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C394-ITQAN394-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لخص** — *lakhkhaṣa* — "to summarize".

> لخص المعلم القصة في جملتين.
>
> *lakhkhaṣa al-muʿallim al-qiṣṣa fī jumlatayn.*
>
> The teacher summed up the story in two sentences.

Present: يلخص, *yulakhkhiṣ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لخص** (*lakhkhaṣa*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-kh-ṣ*, "to make clear, to explain" (Lane). It is Form II; from making a thing clear it came to mean drawing out its gist: to summarize.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
