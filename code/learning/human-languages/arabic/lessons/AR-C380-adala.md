## You'll want to know: عدالة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C380-ADALA380-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عدالة** — *ʿadāla* — "justice".

> يطالب الناس بالعدالة.
>
> *yuṭālibu an-nās bi-l-ʿadāla.*
>
> People are demanding justice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عدالة** (*ʿadāla*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-d-l*, "to be equal, straight" (Lane). *ʿAdāla* is a verbal noun, being fair and upright: justice.
