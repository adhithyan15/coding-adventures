## You'll want to know: obstetrīx, obstetrīcis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C323-PARTUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**obstetrīx, obstetrīcis** — "a midwife".

> Obstetrīx māne ad domum vēnit.
>
> *The midwife came to the house in the morning.*

Feminine, 3rd declension: she stands by the mother. English *obstetrics*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**obstetrīx** is built on *obstō*, "to stand before" (EtymDB), with the feminine ending *-trīx*: she who stands by. English *obstetrics* comes from it.
