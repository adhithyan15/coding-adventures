## You'll want to know: أغضب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C498-ATHAR498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أغضب** — *aghḍaba* — "to make angry".

> أغضب التأخير المدير.
>
> *aghḍaba at-taʾkhīr al-mudīr.*
>
> The delay made the manager angry.

Present: يغضب, *yughḍib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أغضب** (*aghḍaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-ḍ-b*, "anger, wrath" (Lane). It is Form IV: to cause anger.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
