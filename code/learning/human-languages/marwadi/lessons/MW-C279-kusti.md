## You'll want to know: कुस्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C279-GOTHIYO279-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कुस्ती** — *kustī* — "wrestling".

> भाई कुस्ती सीखै है।
>
> *bhāī kustī sīkhai hai.*
>
> My brother is learning wrestling.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुस्ती** (*kustī*) is usually traced to Persian *kushtī*, "wrestling".
