## You'll want to know: preriscaldare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C336-FORNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**preriscaldare** — "to preheat".

> Preriscalda il forno a 180 gradi.
>
> *Preheat the oven to 180 degrees.*

*Il forno preriscaldato* — the preheated oven.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*preriscaldare* joins *pre-* "before", *ri-* and *scaldare* "to warm", from Late Latin *excaldāre*, built on *calidus* "hot" (Lewis & Short).
