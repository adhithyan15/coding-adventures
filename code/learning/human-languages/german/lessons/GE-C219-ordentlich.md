## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ordentlich** ← Middle High German *ordenlich* (EtymDB 2.1), from the word for "order, rank", which came from Latin *ōrdō*, + *-lich*: in good order.
