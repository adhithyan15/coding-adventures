## You'll want to know: ڈگمگانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C538-TAZABZUB538-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈگمگانا** — *ḍagmagānā* — "to totter; to wobble".

> اگر سیڑھی ڈگمگائی تو میں گر جاؤں گا۔
>
> *agar sīṛhī ḍagmagāī to maiṅ gir jāūṅ gā.*
>
> If the ladder wobbles, I will fall.

Past (he …): وہ بوڑھا آدمی ڈگمگایا, *voh būṛhā ādmī ḍagmagāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈگمگانا** (*ḍagmagānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *ḍagmagḍagānā*, "to tremble", and *ḍigmig*, "trembling", under *\*ḍag*, "tremble", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5522).
