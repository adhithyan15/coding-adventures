Telugu, chapter 462: At the Office Counter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కౌంటరు (kauṇṭaru) — a counter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to shift elsewhere, then the Telugu for to deliver.

You'll want to know: కౌంటరు.
కౌంటరు — kauṇṭaru — "a counter".
టిక్కెట్టు కౌంటరు దగ్గర పెద్ద వరుస ఉంది. ṭikkeṭṭu kauṇṭaru daggara pedda varusa undi. There is a long queue at the ticket counter.
Any service counter: in a bank, a station, a shop.

The word, taken apart.
కౌంటరు (kauṇṭaru) is a loan from English counter, with the Telugu final -ఉ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
శాఖ (śākha) — a branch office; a branch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to deliver, then the Telugu for a counter.

You'll want to know: శాఖ.
శాఖ — śākha — "a branch office; a branch".
ఆ బ్యాంకుకు మా పట్టణంలో కొత్త శాఖ వచ్చింది. ā byāṅkuku mā paṭṭaṇaṁlō kotta śākha vaccindi. That bank has opened a new branch in our town.
A branch of a tree, of a bank, of a government: వాతావరణ శాఖ (śākha), the weather department.

The word, taken apart.
శాఖ (śākha) is borrowed from Sanskrit śākhā, "a branch" of a tree (CDIAL 12376); Telugu uses it for the branches of offices too.

Lesson 3 of 5.
పూరించు (pūriñcu) — to fill in; to complete.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a counter, then the Telugu for a branch office.

You'll want to know: పూరించు.
పూరించు — pūriñcu — "to fill in; to complete".
నేను దరఖాస్తు పత్రం పూరించి కౌంటరులో ఇచ్చాను. nēnu darakhāstu patraṁ pūriñci kauṇṭarulō iccānu. I filled in the application form and handed it in at the counter.
Past (I …): పూరించాను, pūriñcānu.
Said of a form, a blank or a gap: ఖాళీలను పూరించు (pūriñcu), to fill the blanks.

The word, taken apart.
పూరించు (pūriñcu) is built from Sanskrit pūr-, "to fill" (CDIAL 8335, pūrayati, "fills"), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 5.
జారీ చేయు (jārī cēyu) — to issue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a branch office, then the Telugu for to fill in.

You'll want to know: జారీ చేయు.
జారీ చేయు — jārī cēyu — "to issue".
ప్రభుత్వం కొత్త పాస్పోర్టు జారీ చేసింది. prabhutvaṁ kotta pāspōrṭu jārī cēsindi. The government issued a new passport.
Past (I …): జారీ చేశాను, jārī cēśānu.
Said of an office giving out a document, an order or a warning.

The word, taken apart.
జారీ చేయు (jārī cēyu) is a Telugu phrase: జారీ, a loan from Arabic jārī, "running, current", which came through Persian and Hindustani, and the native చేయు, "to do" (DEDR 1957). To make a paper current is to issue it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
మంజూరు చేయు (mañjūru cēyu) — to sanction; to grant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to fill in, then the Telugu for to issue.

You'll want to know: మంజూరు చేయు.
మంజూరు చేయు — mañjūru cēyu — "to sanction; to grant".
బ్యాంకు రైతులకు అప్పు మంజూరు చేసింది. byāṅku raitulaku appu mañjūru cēsindi. The bank granted a loan to the farmers.
Past (I …): మంజూరు చేశాను, mañjūru cēśānu.
An official word: a loan, a leave or a visa is మంజూరు, granted.

The word, taken apart.
మంజూరు చేయు (mañjūru cēyu) is a Telugu phrase: మంజూరు, usually traced to Arabic manẓūr, "approved, accepted", through Persian and Hindustani, and the native చేయు, "to do" (DEDR 1957).
