## You'll want to know: la barella
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C301-SOCCORSO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la barella** — "the stretcher".

> Lo hanno messo sulla barella.
>
> *They put him on the stretcher.*

*In barella* — on a stretcher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *barella* is not settled; it is often linked with *bara* "a bier", which EtymDB traces to a Longobard word.
