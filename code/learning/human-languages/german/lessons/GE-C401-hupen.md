## You'll want to know: hupen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C401-GURT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**hupen** — "to hoot, to sound the horn".

> Der Taxifahrer hupt zweimal vor unserem Haus.
>
> *The taxi driver hoots twice in front of our house.*

Regular: *er hupt*, *er hat gehupt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hupen* is built on *Hupe* 'horn', which is usually explained as a sound word. EtymDB lists neither, so the origin stays hedged.
