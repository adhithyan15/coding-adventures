## You'll want to know: ਟਿੱਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C293-LAND293-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟਿੱਲਾ** — *ṭillā* — "a hillock; a mound".

> ਪਿੰਡ ਦੇ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਇੱਕ ਉੱਚਾ ਟਿੱਲਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *piṇḍ de pichchhe ikk uchchā ṭillā hai.*
>
> There is a high mound behind the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟਿੱਲਾ** (*ṭillā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭalla*, "heap"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭillā*, "hillock, mound", under it (CDIAL 5451).
