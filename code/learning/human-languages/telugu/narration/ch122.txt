Lesson 1 of 5.
జలపాతం (jalapātaṁ) — a waterfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a wave, then the Telugu for a hill.

You'll want to know: జలపాతం.
జలపాతం — jalapātaṁ — "a waterfall".

The word, taken apart.
జలపాతం (jalapātaṁ) is Sanskrit jala, "water" (CDIAL 5155), and pāta, "fall" (CDIAL 8052): "a water-fall".

Lesson 4 of 5.
గుర్రం (gurraṁ) — a horse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an island, then the Telugu for a cow.

You'll want to know: గుర్రం.
గుర్రం — gurraṁ — "a horse".

The word, taken apart.
DEDR 1711 lists guṟṟamu, "horse", beside Tamil kutirai, and raises the possibility that it came from Indo-Aryan, Sanskrit ghoṭaka, "horse". CDIAL 4516 in turn calls ghoṭa non-Aryan, probably Dravidian, so the source of గుర్రం (gurraṁ) is not settled.
