## You'll want to know: staubsaugen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C526-PUTZEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**staubsaugen** — "to vacuum".

> Jeden Freitag staubsaugt mein Mann die ganze Wohnung.
>
> *Every Friday my husband vacuums the whole flat.*

Both forms are correct: *er staubsaugt* and *er saugt Staub*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*staubsaugen* is built on *Staub* "dust" and *saugen* "to suck", inherited from Old High German *sūgan*, from Proto-Germanic *sūganą* (EtymDB).
