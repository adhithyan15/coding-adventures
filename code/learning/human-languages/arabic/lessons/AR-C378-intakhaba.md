## You'll want to know: انتخب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C378-INTIKHAB378-02]; assesses=[] -->

**انتخب** — *intakhaba* — "to elect".

> انتخب السكان امرأة شابة.
>
> *intakhaba as-sukkān imraʾa shābba.*
>
> The residents elected a young woman.

Present: ينتخب, *yantakhib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتخب** (*intakhaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-kh-b*, "to choose, to select" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to choose out for oneself: to elect.
