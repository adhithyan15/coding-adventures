## You'll want to know: けんがく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C403-BUNKA403-03]; assesses=[] -->

**けんがく** — *kengaku* — "a study visit; a tour of a place".

> **こうじょうを けんがくしました。** — *kōjō o kengaku shimashita* — "We went on a tour of the factory."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けんがく** (*kengaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ken*, "see" (Middle Chinese *kenH*) and *gaku*, "study" (*haewk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
