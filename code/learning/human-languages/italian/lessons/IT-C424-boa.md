## You'll want to know: la boa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C424-APPRODO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la boa** — "the buoy".

> Non nuotare oltre le boe.
>
> *Do not swim beyond the buoys.*

On beaches the buoys mark the safe swimming area.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*boa* is usually traced to a Germanic word for a sign or marker, through Old French, but the origin is not settled.
