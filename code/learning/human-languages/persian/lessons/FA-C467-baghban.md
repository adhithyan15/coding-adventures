## You'll want to know: باغبان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C467-BAGH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**باغبان** (*bâghbân*) — "a gardener".

> باغبان درختهای باغ را آب داد.
>
> *Bâghbân derakht-hâ-ye bâgh râ âb dâd.*
>
> The gardener watered the trees in the garden.

*bâgh* + *-bân* 'keeper', as in *negahbân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâghbân* is a native compound of *bâgh* 'garden', from Middle Persian *bāg* (EtymDB), and *-bân* 'keeper', as in *negahbân* 'guard'.
