## You'll want to know: a polémica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C347-POLEMICA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a polémica** — "the controversy, the public row".

> O novo horário criou uma grande polémica na escola.
>
> *The new timetable caused a big controversy at the school.*

European *polémica*; Brazil *polêmica*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*polémica* is usually traced to French *polémique* "polemic", which EtymDB derives from Greek *polemikós* "of war", built on *pólemos* "war" (EtymDB).
