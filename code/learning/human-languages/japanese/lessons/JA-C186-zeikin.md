## You'll want to know: ぜいきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C186-SAVE186-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜいきん** — *zeikin* — "tax".

> **ぜいきんを はらいます。** — *zeikin o haraimasu* — "I pay tax."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜいきん** (*zeikin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zei*, "tax, duty" (Middle Chinese *sywejH*) and *kin*, "gold, money" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
