## You'll want to know: la terapia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C743-MENTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la terapia** — "therapy".

> Voy a terapia una vez por semana.
>
> *I go to therapy once a week.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terapia* comes from Greek *therapeía* "service, medical treatment" (EtymDB), from *therapeúein* "to serve, to treat".
