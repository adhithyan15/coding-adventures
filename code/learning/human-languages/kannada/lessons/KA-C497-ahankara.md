## You'll want to know: ಅಹಂಕಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C497-ABHIMANA497-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಹಂಕಾರ** — *ahaṅkāra* — "arrogance; conceit".

> ಹಣ ಬಂದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಅಹಂಕಾರ ಬಂದಿತು.
>
> *haṇa banda mēle avanige ahaṅkāra banditu.*
>
> Once the money came, he grew arrogant.

From ಅಹಂ, I, and ಕಾರ, making: making everything about "I".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಹಂಕಾರ** (*ahaṅkāra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ahaṃkāra*, "the making of self, egotism, pride" (Monier-Williams).
