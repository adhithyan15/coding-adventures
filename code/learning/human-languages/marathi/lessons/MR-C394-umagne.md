## You'll want to know: उमगणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C394-BHAN394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उमगणे** — *umagṇe* — "to dawn on; to become clear".

> खूप वेळाने मला त्याच्या बोलण्याचा अर्थ उमगला.
>
> *khūp veḷāne malā tyācyā bolaṇyācā artha umaglā.*
>
> Much later the meaning of his words dawned on me.

Past (to him …): त्याला उमगलं, *tyālā umaglaṁ*.

Dative: मला उमगलं, it dawned on me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उमगणे** (*umagṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *umagṇẽ*, "to search out; to become known", under *\*unmārgati*, "searches out" (CDIAL 2126).
