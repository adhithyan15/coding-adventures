## You'll want to know: लेख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C364-VISHESHAGYA364-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लेख** — *lekh* — "an article (in a paper)".

> मैंने अख़बार में उसका लेख पढ़ा।
>
> *maiṁne akhbār meṁ uskā lekh paṛhā.*
>
> I read his article in the newspaper.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लेख** (*lekh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *lekha*, "a writing, a document" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
