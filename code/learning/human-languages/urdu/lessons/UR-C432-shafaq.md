## You'll want to know: شفق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C432-TULU432-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شفق** — *shafaq* — "the glow of sunset; twilight".

> غروب کے بعد آسمان پر شفق تھی۔
>
> *ghurūb ke bād āsmān par shafaq thī.*
>
> After sunset the sky glowed red.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شفق** (*shafaq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shafaq*, "the red of twilight".
