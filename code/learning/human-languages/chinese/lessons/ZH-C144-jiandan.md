## You'll want to know: jiǎndān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C144-MATTER144-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎndān* — "simple".

> *zhège hěn jiǎndān* — This one's simple.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎndān* is built from *jiǎn* 'brief' and *dān* 'single', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keanX* and *tan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a slip of bamboo for writing on, and 'brief'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
