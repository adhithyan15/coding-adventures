German, chapter 392: Geräte laden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Gerät — the device, the appliance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to acquire, then the German for to insure.

You'll want to know: das Gerät.
das Gerät — "the device, the appliance".
In der Küche stehen viele elektrische Geräte. There are many electrical appliances in the kitchen.
Plural: die Geräte. In a gym, die Geräte are the machines.

The word, taken apart.
Gerät is inherited from Middle High German geræte "consultation; equipment" and Old High German girāti "advice" (EtymDB), of the family of Rat "advice": what you need to provide for a task.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Akku — the rechargeable battery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to insure, then the German for the device.

You'll want to know: der Akku.
der Akku — "the rechargeable battery".
Mein Akku ist fast leer. My battery is almost flat.
Plural: die Akkus. Short for der Akkumulator.

The word, taken apart.
Akku is short for Akkumulator, a learned formation on Latin accumulāre "to heap up, to accumulate" (Lewis & Short), built on cumulus "a heap": it stores up electricity.

Lesson 3 of 5.
aufladen — to charge (a battery); to load.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the device, then the German for the rechargeable battery.

You'll want to know: aufladen.
aufladen — "to charge (a battery); to load".
Ich lade mein Handy jede Nacht auf. I charge my mobile every night.
Separable and strong: er lädt auf, er hat aufgeladen. Also to load: das Gepäck aufladen.

The word, taken apart.
aufladen is built from auf "up, on" and laden "to load", inherited from Old High German hladan and Proto-Germanic hlaþaną (EtymDB): you load the battery up.

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Ladegerät — the charger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the rechargeable battery, then the German for to charge.

You'll want to know: das Ladegerät.
das Ladegerät — "the charger".
Ich habe mein Ladegerät im Hotel vergessen. I left my charger at the hotel.
Plural: die Ladegeräte. The cable alone is das Ladekabel.

The word, taken apart.
Ladegerät joins laden "to load", Old High German hladan (EtymDB), to Gerät "device", Middle High German geræte "equipment" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Stecker — the plug.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to charge, then the German for the charger.

You'll want to know: der Stecker.
der Stecker — "the plug".
Im Ausland passt mein Stecker oft nicht. Abroad my plug often does not fit.
Plural: die Stecker. Den Stecker ziehen — to pull the plug.

The word, taken apart.
Stecker is formed with -er on stecken "to put in, to stick", inherited from Old High German steckōn (EtymDB): the thing you stick in.
