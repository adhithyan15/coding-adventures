# hónglǜdēng — traffic lights

## You'll want to know: hónglǜdēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C194-ROAD194-05]; assesses=[] -->

*hónglǜdēng* — "traffic lights".

> *lùkǒu yǒu hónglǜdēng hé bānmǎxiàn* — There are traffic lights and a zebra crossing at the junction.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hónglǜdēng* is built from *hóng* 'red', *lǜ* 'green' and *dēng* 'a lamp', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwng*, *ljowk* and *tong* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
