## You'll want to know: ભેદભાવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C349-SAMAN349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ભેદભાવ** — *bhedbhāv* — "discrimination".

> શાળામાં છોકરા અને છોકરી વચ્ચે ભેદભાવ ન હોવો જોઈએ.
>
> *śāḷāmā̃ chokrā ane chokrī vacce bhedbhāv na hovo joīe.*
>
> There should be no discrimination between boys and girls at school.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભેદભાવ** (*bhedbhāv*) is built from Sanskrit *bheda*, "a difference", and *bhāva*, "a state, a feeling" (Monier-Williams): treating people as different.
