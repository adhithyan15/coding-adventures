Hindi, chapter 470: Courage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
साहस (sāhas) — daring; boldness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for greedy, then the Hindi for a beggar.

You'll want to know: साहस.
साहस — sāhas — "daring; boldness".
रात में अकेले जंगल में जाने के लिए साहस चाहिए। rāt meṁ akele jaṅgal meṁ jāne ke liye sāhas cāhiye. It takes daring to go into the forest alone at night.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
साहस (sāhas) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sāhasa, "boldness, daring", also "rashness" (Monier-Williams).

निर्भीक पत्रकार ने सच लिखा। nirbhīk patrakār ne sac likhā. The fearless journalist wrote the truth.

The word, taken apart.
निर्भीक (nirbhīk) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit nirbhīka, "fearless", built on niḥ, "without", and bhī, "fear" (Monier-Williams), the root that also gives bhay.

Lesson 5 of 5.
जोखिम (jokhim) — a risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for bold, then the Hindi for fearless.

You'll want to know: जोखिम.
जोखिम — jokhim — "a risk".
पहाड़ पर अकेले चढ़ने में बहुत जोखिम है। pahāṛ par akele caṛhne meṁ bahut jokhim hai. Climbing the mountain alone is very risky.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
जोखिम (jokhim) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi forms of this word, with the senses "charge for securing property from accidents, risk, danger, loss", under Sanskrit yōgakṣēmá, "preservation of one's property; an insurance charge" (CDIAL 10527): the price of safety became the danger itself.
