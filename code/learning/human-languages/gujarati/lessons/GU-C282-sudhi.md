# સુધી (sudhī) — until; as far as

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C282-KARTAN-01, GU-LEX-SAANJ-01, GU-LEX-NADI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "the city is bigger than the village". (*śaher gām kartā̃ moṭũ che*.) Then "evening" and "a river". (**સાંજ**, **નદી**.)

## You'll want to know: સુધી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C282-SUDHI-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01, GU-LEX-C54-MOVE-02] -->

**સુધી** — *sudhī* — "until; as far as". It comes after its word, as **કરતાં** does: an end in time, or in space.

> **હું સાંજ સુધી ઘરે છું.**
>
> *hũ sānj sudhī ghare chũ.* — "I'm at home until evening."

> **અમે નદી સુધી ચાલ્યા.**
>
> *ame nadī sudhī cālyā.* — "We walked as far as the river."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સુધી** (*sudhī*) is a small word with no older form in any dictionary we use, so its history is not settled.
