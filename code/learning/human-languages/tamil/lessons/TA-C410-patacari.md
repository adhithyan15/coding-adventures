# பாதசாரி (pātacāri) — a pedestrian

## You'll want to know: பாதசாரி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C410-THERU410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**பாதசாரி** — *pātacāri* — "a pedestrian".

> பாதசாரிகள் நடைபாதையில் நடக்க வேண்டும்.
>
> *pātacārikaḷ naṭaipātaiyil naṭakka vēṇṭum.*
>
> Pedestrians should walk on the footpath.

The plural is பாதசாரிகள்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பாதசாரி** (*pātacāri*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pādacārin*, "going on foot, a pedestrian" (Monier-Williams), from *pāda*, "foot".
