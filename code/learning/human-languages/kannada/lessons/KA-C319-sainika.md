# ಸೈನಿಕ (sainika) — a soldier

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C307-KELASA307-05, KA-LEX-C319-YUDDHA319-01] -->

## You'll want to know: ಸೈನಿಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C319-YUDDHA319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೈನಿಕ** — *sainika* — "a soldier".

> ಸೈನಿಕರು ಯುದ್ಧದಿಂದ ಮನೆಗೆ ಹಿಂತಿರುಗಿದರು.
>
> *sainikaru yuddhadinda manege hintirugidaru.*
>
> The soldiers came back home from the war.

Plural: ಸೈನಿಕರು, *sainikaru*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೈನಿಕ** (*sainika*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sainika*, "belonging to an army", and as a noun "a soldier" (Monier-Williams). It is built on *senā*, "an army", with the ending -*ika*, "one who belongs to".
