## You'll want to know: lóutī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C112-EXIT112-05]; assesses=[] -->

*lóutī* — "stairs; staircase".

> *lóutī zài ménkǒu pángbiān* — The stairs are next to the doorway.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lóutī* is built from lóu and tī, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *luw* and *thej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a house built on a house' and 'wooden steps'.
