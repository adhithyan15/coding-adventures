## You'll want to know: lógico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C337-ARGUMENTAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lógico** — "logical".

> A tua resposta é lógica: se chove, ficamos em casa.
>
> *Your answer is logical: if it rains, we stay at home.*

*É lógico!* — of course! Feminine *lógica*; *a lógica* is also the noun logic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lógico* is a learned borrowing from Latin *logicus* (EtymDB), from Greek *logikós* "of speech, of reasoning", built on *lógos* "word, reason".
