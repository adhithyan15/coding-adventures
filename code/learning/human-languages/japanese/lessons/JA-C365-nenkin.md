## You'll want to know: ねんきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C365-KEIZAI365-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ねんきん** — *nenkin* — "a pension".

> **しごとを やめたら、ねんきんで くらします。** — *shigoto o yametara, nenkin de kurashimasu* — "When I stop working, I will live on my pension."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねんきん** (*nenkin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nen*, "year" (Middle Chinese *nen*) and *kin*, "gold" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
