## You'll want to know: ಏಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C478-HADDU478-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏಡಿ** — *ēḍi* — "a crab".

> ಕಡಲತೀರದ ಮರಳಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಏಡಿಗಳು ಓಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು.
>
> *kaḍalatīrada maraḷinalli ēḍigaḷu ōḍuttiddavu.*
>
> Crabs were running about on the sand of the beach.

Plural: ಏಡಿಗಳು, *ēḍigaḷu*.

The plural takes -ಗಳು: ಏಡಿಗಳು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏಡಿ** (*ēḍi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2901 lists Kannada *ēḍi*, beside *esaḍi* and *naḷḷi*, with Tamil *ñaṇṭu*, "a crab".
