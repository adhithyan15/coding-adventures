## You'll want to know: সম্রাট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C313-RAJYO313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্রাট** — *sômrāṭ* — "an emperor".

> সম্রাট দুর্গ থেকে পুরো রাজ্য দেখতেন।
>
> *sômrāṭ durgô theke puro rājyô dekhten.*
>
> The emperor used to look out over the whole kingdom from his fort.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্রাট** (*sômrāṭ*) is Sanskrit *samrāj*, 'a universal ruler, a paramount sovereign' (Monier-Williams), in its nominative *samrāṭ*; an emperor rules over kings.
