Italian, chapter 77: Green, Grey, Clean, Dirty, Pleased.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
verde — green.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for poor, then the Italian for yellow.

You'll want to know: verde.
verde — "green".

The word, taken apart.
verde from Latin viridis, "green", spoken as virdis — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
