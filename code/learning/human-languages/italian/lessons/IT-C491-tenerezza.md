## You'll want to know: la tenerezza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C491-INNAMORATI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la tenerezza** — "tenderness".

> Mi guarda con tenerezza.
>
> *She looks at me tenderly.*

Built on *tenero* "tender, soft".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tenerezza* is built on *tenero* "tender", inherited from Latin *tener* "soft, delicate" (Lewis & Short), with *-ezza* for a quality.
