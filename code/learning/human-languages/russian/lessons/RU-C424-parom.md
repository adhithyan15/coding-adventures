## You'll want to know: паром
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C424-PAROM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**паром** (*paróm*) — "a ferry".

> На остров ходит паром.
>
> *Na óstrov khódit paróm.*
>
> A ferry goes to the island.

Masculine. *На пароме* — by ferry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*паром* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *pormŭ* 'a ferry' (EtymDB).
