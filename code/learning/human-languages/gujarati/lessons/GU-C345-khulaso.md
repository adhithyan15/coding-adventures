## You'll want to know: ખુલાસો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C345-KHULASO345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ખુલાસો** — *khulāso* — "an explanation (of one's conduct)".

> તેણે મોડા આવવાનો ખુલાસો કર્યો.
>
> *teṇe moḍā āvvāno khulāso karyo.*
>
> He explained why he was late.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખુલાસો** (*khulāso*) is usually traced to Arabic *khulāṣa*, "an extract, the gist", through Persian; Gujarati uses it for clearing up a matter.
