## You'll want to know: さびる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C486-PARTS486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さびる** — *sabiru* — "to rust".

> **じてんしゃが あめで さびました。** — *jitensha ga ame de sabimashita* — "My bicycle rusted in the rain."

Polite **さびます**, *sabimasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **じてんしゃが さびました。**, *jitensha ga sabimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さびる** (*sabiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
