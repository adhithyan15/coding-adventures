## You'll want to know: সৈন্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C314-SHOTRU314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সৈন্য** — *soinyô* — "a soldier".

> রাজার সৈন্যরা দুর্গের দরজায় দাঁড়াল।
>
> *rājār soinyôrā durger dôrjāy dā̃ṛālô.*
>
> The king's soldiers took up their places at the gate of the fort.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সৈন্য** (*soinyô*) is Sanskrit *sainya*, 'belonging to an army', and as a noun 'a soldier' (Monier-Williams); its ৈ is read *oi*. An army is a **সেনাবাহিনী**.
