## You'll want to know: la sequenza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C276-VICENDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la sequenza** — "the sequence, the series".

> Ricordo bene la sequenza dei fatti.
>
> *I remember the sequence of events well.*

Also a film word: *la prima sequenza del film*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sequenza* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *sequentia* "a following" (EtymDB), from *sequī* "to follow".
