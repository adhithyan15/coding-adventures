## You'll want to know: بیعانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C464-MAGHAZE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بیعانه** (*bey'âne*) — "a down payment (to hold a purchase); a deposit".

> برای فرش بیعانه دادیم و فردا بقیه پول را میدهیم.
>
> *Barâ-ye farsh bey'âne dâdim va fardâ baqiye-ye pul râ midahim.*
>
> We paid a deposit on the carpet and we'll pay the rest tomorrow.

Paid when you agree to buy: a house, a car, a carpet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bey'âne* is usually traced to Arabic *bayʿ* 'sale', with the Persian ending *-âne*. EtymDB does not list it.
