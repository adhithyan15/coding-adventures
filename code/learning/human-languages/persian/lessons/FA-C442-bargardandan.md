## You'll want to know: برگرداندن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C442-JOBRAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**برگرداندن** (*bargardândan*) — "to give back, to return (something)".

> فروشنده پول ما را برگرداند.
>
> *Forushande pul-e mâ râ bargardând.*
>
> The shopkeeper gave our money back.

The causative of *bargashtan* 'to go back'. Present stem *bargardân-*: *barmigardânam*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bargardândan* is built from *bar* 'up, back' and *gardândan* 'to turn something', the causative built on *gard-*, the present stem of *gashtan* 'to turn', usually traced to Middle Persian *gaštan*.
