Italian, chapter 507: Il giardinaggio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il giardinaggio — gardening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for roll of film, then the Italian for to develop.

You'll want to know: il giardinaggio.
il giardinaggio — "gardening".
Mia madre fa giardinaggio tutti i fine settimana. My mother does gardening every weekend.
Fare giardinaggio — to garden.

The word, taken apart.
giardinaggio comes from French jardinage, built on jardin "garden", from Medieval Latin jardīnus (EtymDB), which is usually traced to a Frankish word for an enclosure.

Lesson 3 of 5.
annaffiare — to water (plants).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for gardening, then the Italian for to sow.

You'll want to know: annaffiare.
annaffiare — "to water (plants)".
Chi annaffia le piante quando siamo in vacanza? Who waters the plants when we are on holiday?
Also spelt innaffiare. The watering can is l'annaffiatoio.

The word, taken apart.
annaffiare is inherited from Vulgar Latin inafflāre "to blow onto, to sprinkle" (EtymDB), from Latin afflāre, built on flāre "to blow".
