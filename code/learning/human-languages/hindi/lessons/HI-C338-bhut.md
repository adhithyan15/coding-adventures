## You'll want to know: भूत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C338-PARI338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भूत** — *bhūt* — "a ghost".

> लोग कहते थे कि उस पुराने महल में भूत रहता है।
>
> *log kahte the ki us purāne mahal meṁ bhūt rahtā hai.*
>
> People used to say that a ghost lived in that old palace.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भूत** (*bhūt*) is Sanskrit *bhūta*, "what has been", and so a being and a spirit (Monier-Williams). The same word means the past in grammar; in stories it is a ghost.
