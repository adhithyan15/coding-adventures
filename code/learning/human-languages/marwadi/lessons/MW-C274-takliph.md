## You'll want to know: तकलीफ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C274-SAHARO274-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तकलीफ** — *taklīph* — "trouble".

> कोई तकलीफ है?
>
> *koī taklīph hai?*
>
> Is anything the matter?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तकलीफ** (*taklīph*) is usually traced, through Persian *taklīf*, to Arabic *taklīf*, "a burden laid on someone".
