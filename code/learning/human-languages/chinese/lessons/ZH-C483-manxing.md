## You'll want to know: mànxìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C483-BETTER483-05]; assesses=[] -->

*mànxìng* — "chronic (of an illness)".

> *tā yǒu mànxìngbìng yào chángcháng chī yào* — He has a chronic illness and has to take medicine regularly.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mànxìng* is built from *màn* 'slow' and 性 'nature', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *maenH* and *sjengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'idle' and the second 'nature and conduct'.
