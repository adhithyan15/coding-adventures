## You'll want to know: в любом случае
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C379-ZAPASNOY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**в любом случае** (*v lyubóm slúchaye*) — "in any case, anyway".

> В любом случае я тебе позвоню.
>
> *V lyubóm slúchaye ya tebé pozvonyú.*
>
> In any case I will call you.

*Любой* means any. It closes an argument: whatever happens.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**в любом случае** is "in any case": *любой* "any", on the root of *любить* "to love", and *случай* "a case", from Proto-Slavic *\*sŭlučajĭ* (EtymDB).
