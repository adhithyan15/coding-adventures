## You'll want to know: रजई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C523-GALICHA523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रजई** — *rajaī* — "a quilt".

> थंडीत आजी जाड रजई घेते.
>
> *thaṇḍīt ājī jāḍ rajaī ghete.*
>
> In the cold, grandmother covers herself with a thick quilt.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रजई** (*rajaī*) is usually traced to Persian *razāī*, "a quilt".
