## You'll want to know: le jour férié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C316-CELEBRER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le jour férié** — "the public holiday, the bank holiday".

> Le premier mai est un jour férié.
>
> *The first of May is a public holiday.*

*Faire le pont* — to take the day off between a holiday and the weekend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*férié* is a learned borrowing from Latin *fēriātus* "on holiday", from *fēriae* "days of rest, holidays" (Lewis & Short); *jour* comes from Latin *diurnum*.
