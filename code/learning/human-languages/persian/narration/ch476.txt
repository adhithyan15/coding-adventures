Persian, chapter 476: Snow and ice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کولاک (kulâk) — a snowstorm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a gale, then the Persian for a whirlwind.

You'll want to know: کولاک.
کولاک (kulâk) — "a snowstorm".
کولاک (kulâk) جاده کوهستانی را بست. Kulâk jâdde-ye kuhestâni râ bast. The snowstorm closed the mountain road.
Snow with strong wind.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of kulâk is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بوران (burân) — a blizzard (with driving snow).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a whirlwind, then the Persian for a snowstorm.

You'll want to know: بوران.
بوران (burân) — "a blizzard (with driving snow)".
در بوران راننده ها جاده را نمیدیدند. Dar burân rânande-hâ jâdde râ nemididand. In the blizzard the drivers couldn't make out the road.
Close in sense to kulâk.

The word, taken apart.
burân is usually traced to Turkic buran 'a snowstorm'. EtymDB lists an Arabic būrān without a gloss, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
یخبندان (yakhbandân) — a frost, freezing weather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a snowstorm, then the Persian for a blizzard.

You'll want to know: یخبندان.
یخبندان (yakhbandân) — "a frost, freezing weather".
در یخبندان لوله های آب ترکید. Dar yakhbandân lule-hâ-ye âb tarkid. In the frost the water pipes burst.
yakh 'ice' + band 'binding' + -ân.

The word, taken apart.
yakhbandân is a native compound of yakh 'ice', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Iranian aixa-, band 'binding', from the root of bastan, and the ending -ân.

Lesson 4 of 5.
یخ زدن (yakh zadan) — to freeze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a blizzard, then the Persian for a frost.

You'll want to know: یخ زدن.
یخ زدن (yakh zadan) — "to freeze".
آب حوض شب یخ زد. Âb-e howz shab yakh zad. The water in the pool froze overnight.
yakh 'ice' + zadan. Of people: yakh zadam — I'm frozen.

The word, taken apart.
yakh zadan is built from native yakh 'ice', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Iranian aixa-, and the native verb zadan 'to strike'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
آب شدن (âb shodan) — to melt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a frost, then the Persian for to freeze.

You'll want to know: آب شدن.
آب شدن (âb shodan) — "to melt".
در آفتاب برفها آب شد. Dar âftâb barf-hâ âb shod. The snow melted in the sun.
Literally 'to become water'. Also to lose weight.

The word, taken apart.
âb shodan is built from âb 'water', inherited from Middle Persian āb (EtymDB), and the native verb shodan 'to become'.
