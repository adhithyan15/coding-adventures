## You'll want to know: das Praktikum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C377-HOCHSCHULE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Praktikum** — "the internship, the work placement".

> Im Sommer mache ich ein Praktikum bei einer Zeitung.
>
> *In the summer I am doing an internship at a newspaper.*

Plural: *die Praktika*. *Der Praktikant, die Praktikantin* — the intern.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Praktikum* is a learned formation on Medieval Latin *practicus* "active, practical", from Greek *praktikós* "fit for action", from *prássein* "to do". EtymDB has no entry for the German word.
