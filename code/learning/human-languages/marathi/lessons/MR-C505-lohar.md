## You'll want to know: लोहार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C505-KARAGIR505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लोहार** — *lohār* — "a blacksmith".

> लोहाराने शेतासाठी नवीन अवजार बनवलं.
>
> *lohārāne śetāsāṭhī navīn avjār banavlaṁ.*
>
> The blacksmith made a new tool for the field.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोहार** (*lohār*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *lōhakāra*, "iron-worker" (CDIAL 11159), from *lōha*, "iron", and *kāra*, "maker".
