## You'll want to know: क्रमशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C306-KRAMASH306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रमशः** — *kramaśaḥ* — "step by step; gradually".

> नदीचं पाणी क्रमशः कमी झालं.
>
> *nadīcaṁ pāṇī kramaśaḥ kamī jhālaṁ.*
>
> The water in the river went down step by step.

Formal; at the end of a serial story it means to be continued.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रमशः** (*kramaśaḥ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kramaśas*, 'gradually, by degrees' (Monier-Williams).
