## You'll want to know: खातम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C422-PRAKARA422-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खातम्** — *khātam* — "a ditch".

> कृषकः क्षेत्रस्य समीपे खातं खनति।
>
> *kṛṣakaḥ kṣetrasya samīpe khātaṃ khanati.*
>
> The farmer digs a ditch near the field.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खातम्** (*khātam*) comes from the root √khan, "to dig": it is the participle, "dug", and MW gives *khāta* as dug, excavated and, as a noun, a ditch (MW).
