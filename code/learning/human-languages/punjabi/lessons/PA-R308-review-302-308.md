[PAUSE 3s] A garden bed? (**ਕਿਆਰੀ**, *kiārī*.) A manure heap? (**ਰੂੜੀ**, *rūṛī*.) To scatter? (**ਖਿਲਾਰਨਾ**, *khilārnā*.) To sprout? (**ਉੱਗਣਾ**, *uggṇā*.) Mustard? (**ਸਰ੍ਹੋਂ**, *sarhoṁ*.) A canal? (**ਨਹਿਰ**, *nahir*.) A well? (**ਖੂਹ**, *khūh*.) To irrigate? (**ਸਿੰਜਣਾ**, *sinjṇā*.) A farmer? (**ਕਿਸਾਨ**, *kisān*.) A Persian wheel? (**ਹਲਟ**, *halaṭ*.) A weeding hoe? (**ਖੁਰਪਾ**, *khurpā*.) To hoe? (**ਗੋਡਣਾ**, *goḍṇā*.) To ripen? (**ਪੱਕਣਾ**, *pakkṇā*.) An ear of grain? (**ਸਿੱਟਾ**, *siṭṭā*.) A grain? (**ਦਾਣਾ**, *dāṇā*.) Reaping? (**ਵਾਢੀ**, *vāḍhī*.) To reap? (**ਲੁਣਨਾ**, *luṇnā*.) A sickle? (**ਦਾਤੀ**, *dātī*.) To thresh? (**ਗਾਹੁਣਾ**, *gāhuṇā*.) A heap? (**ਢੇਰ**, *ḍher*.) A winnowing basket? (**ਛੱਜ**, *chhajj*.) To winnow? (**ਛੱਟਣਾ**, *chhaṭṭṇā*.) Chopped straw? (**ਤੂੜੀ**, *tūṛī*.) Rice straw? (**ਪਰਾਲੀ**, *parālī*.) A sack? (**ਬੋਰੀ**, *borī*.) A buffalo? (**ਮੱਝ**, *majjh*.) A buffalo calf? (**ਕੱਟਾ**, *kaṭṭā*.) To milk an animal? (**ਚੋਣਾ**, *choṇā*.) A teat? (**ਥਣ**, *thaṇ*.) To graze? (**ਚਰਨਾ**, *charnā*.) Fodder? (**ਚਾਰਾ**, *chārā*.) Cow dung? (**ਗੋਹਾ**, *gohā*.) A calf? (**ਵੱਛਾ**, *vachchhā*.) A horn? (**ਸਿੰਗ**, *siṅg*.) To drive cattle? (**ਹੱਕਣਾ**, *hakkṇā*.)
