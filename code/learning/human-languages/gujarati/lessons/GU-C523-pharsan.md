## You'll want to know: ફરસાણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C523-FARSAN523-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ફરસાણ** — *pharsāṇ* — "savoury snacks".

> દિવાળીમાં અમે ઘરે ઘણું ફરસાણ બનાવીએ છીએ.
>
> *divāḷīmā̃ ame ghare ghaṇũ pharsāṇ banāvīe chīe.*
>
> At Diwali we make lots of savoury snacks at home.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફરસાણ** (*pharsāṇ*) is the savoury side of a Gujarati meal or a festival tray. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
