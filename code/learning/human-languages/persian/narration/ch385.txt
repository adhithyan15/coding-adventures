Persian, chapter 385: Society and generations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جامعه (jâme'e) — a society, a community.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to succeed, then the Persian for to be defeated.

You'll want to know: جامعه.
جامعه (jâme'e) — "a society, a community".
جوانها آینده جامعه هستند. javânhâ âyande-ye jâme'e hastand. Young people are the future of society.
Plural javâme', an Arabic broken plural. Jâme'e-shenâsi — sociology.

The word, taken apart.
جامعه (jâme'e) comes from Arabic jāmi'a (EtymDB), a word usually explained as what gathers people together; in Persian it is a society.

Lesson 2 of 5.
اجتماعی (ejtemâ'i) — social.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be defeated, then the Persian for a society.

You'll want to know: اجتماعی.
اجتماعی (ejtemâ'i) — "social".
او آدم خیلی اجتماعی است. u âdam-e kheyli ejtemâ'i ast. He is a very sociable person.
From ejtemâ' "gathering, society" + -i. Masâ'el-e ejtemâ'i — social issues.

The word, taken apart.
اجتماعی (ejtemâ'i) was borrowed from Arabic ijtimā'ī "social" (EtymDB), the adjective of ijtimā' "gathering".

Lesson 4 of 5.
سالمند (sâlmand) — elderly; an elderly person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for social, then the Persian for a generation.

You'll want to know: سالمند.
سالمند (sâlmand) — "elderly; an elderly person".
پرستار هر روز به سالمندها کمک میکند. parastâr har ruz be sâlmandhâ komak mikonad. The nurse helps the elderly every day.
Sâl "year" + -mand "having". More respectful than pir "old".

The word, taken apart.
سالمند (sâlmand) is native Persian, built from sâl "year" + the suffix -mand "having". An elderly person is rich in years.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مذهب (mazhab) — a religion, a faith.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a generation, then the Persian for elderly.

You'll want to know: مذهب.
مذهب (mazhab) — "a religion, a faith".
در این شهر مردم مذهبهای مختلفی دارند. dar in shahr mardom mazhabhâ-ye mokhtalefi dârand. In this city people follow different religions.
Plural mazâheb, an Arabic broken plural. Also a branch within a religion.

The word, taken apart.
مذهب (mazhab) is usually traced to Arabic madhhab "a way to go, a school of thought"; Persian says the Arabic dh as z.
