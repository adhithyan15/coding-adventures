## You'll want to know: addebitare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C459-BANCA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**addebitare** — "to charge (to an account), to debit".

> La banca mi ha addebitato due euro.
>
> *The bank charged me two euros.*

*L'addebito* — the debit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*addebitare* is built from *a-* and *debito* 'debt', a learned borrowing from Latin *dēbitum* 'what is owed', from *dēbēre* 'to owe' (Lewis & Short).
