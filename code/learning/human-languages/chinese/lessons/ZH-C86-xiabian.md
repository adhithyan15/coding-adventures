## You'll want to know: xiàbian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C86-XIABIAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàbian* — "under; below".

> *māo zài yǐzi xiàbian* — The cat is under the chair.

Its second character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàbian* is built from xià 'below', the 下 you write, and bian 'side'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), *haeX* glossed 'to go down' and 'the bottom'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C86-SHANGBIAN-01] -->

*shàngbian* and *xiàbian*, on top and under: the pair you write as 上 and 下, with the same ending as *lǐbian* and *wàibian*.
