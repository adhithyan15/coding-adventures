## You'll want to know: segnare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C511-RISULTATI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**segnare** — "to score; to mark, to note down".

> Ha segnato due gol nel primo tempo.
>
> *He scored two goals in the first half.*

Also to note down: *segnare un numero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*segnare* is inherited from Latin *signāre* "to mark, to seal" (EtymDB), from *signum* "a mark".
