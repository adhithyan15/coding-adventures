## You'll want to know: कसम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C271-SAHI271-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कसम** — *kasam* — "an oath".

> हूं कसम खाऊं हूं, यो साचो है।
>
> *hū̃ kasam khāū̃ hū̃, yo sācho hai.*
>
> I swear it: this is true.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसम** (*kasam*) is usually traced, through Persian *qasam*, to Arabic *qasam*, "an oath". You "eat" an oath: **कसम खाऊं हूं**, I swear.
