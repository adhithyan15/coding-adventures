You'll want to know: la chitarra.
la chitarra — "a guitar".

The word, taken apart.
chitarra from Arabic qitara, "a guitar" — borrowed; the Arabic word came from Greek kithara, "a lyre". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for energy, then the Italian for a guitar.

You'll want to know: il pianoforte.
il pianoforte — "a piano".

The word, taken apart.
pianoforte is piano, "soft", plus forte, "loud": the first keyboard that could play both. forte from Latin fortis, "strong". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
il violino — a violin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a guitar, then the Italian for a piano.

You'll want to know: il violino.
il violino — "a violin".

The word, taken apart.
violino — built from viola, the stringed instrument, with -ino, "little". EtymDB 2.1 derives that viola — a different word from the colour — from Old Provençal viola, from Medieval Latin vitula, "a stringed instrument".
