## You'll want to know: velejar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C506-AGUA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**velejar** — "to sail".

> Aos fins de semana, velejamos na baía.
>
> *At weekends, we sail in the bay.*

Built on *a vela* — a sail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*velejar* is built on *vela* "sail", which is inherited from Latin *vēla*, plural of *vēlum* "a sail, a cloth" (Lewis & Short).
