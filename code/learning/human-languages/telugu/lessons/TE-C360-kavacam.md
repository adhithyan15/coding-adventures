# కవచం (kavacaṁ) — armour; a shield

## You'll want to know: కవచం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C360-AYUDHAM360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కవచం** — *kavacaṁ* — "armour; a shield".

> సైనికులు ఇనుప కవచాలు ధరించారు.
>
> *sainikulu inupa kavacālu dhariñcāru.*
>
> The soldiers wore iron armour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కవచం** (*kavacaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kavaca*, "a coat of mail" (CDIAL 2957); CDIAL notes a view that the Sanskrit word itself came from Dravidian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
