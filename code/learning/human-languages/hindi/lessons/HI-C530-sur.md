## You'll want to know: सुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C530-SANGEET530-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सुर** — *sur* — "a musical note; a pitch".

> गायक का सुर बहुत मीठा है।
>
> *Gāyak kā sur bahut mīṭhā hai.*
>
> The singer's voice is very sweet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुर** (*sur*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *sur*, "sound, words, tune", under Sanskrit *sura*, "sound", a variant of *svara*, "a musical note" (CDIAL 13498).
