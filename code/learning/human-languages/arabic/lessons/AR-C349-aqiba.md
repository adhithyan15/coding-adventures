## You'll want to know: عقب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C349-MARRA349-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عقب** — *ʿaqiba* — "right after, following".

> نام الطفل عقب العشاء مباشرة.
>
> *nāma aṭ-ṭifl ʿaqiba al-ʿashāʾ mubāsharatan.*
>
> The child fell asleep straight after dinner.

A written word; بعد is the everyday one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقب** (*ʿaqiba*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-b* (Lane), the root of *ʿaqib*, the heel. What comes *ʿaqiba* a thing comes at its heels.
