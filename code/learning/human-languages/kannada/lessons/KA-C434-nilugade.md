# ನಿಲುಗಡೆ (nilugaḍe) — a halt; a stop

## You'll want to know: ನಿಲುಗಡೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C434-VILAMBA434-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿಲುಗಡೆ** — *nilugaḍe* — "a halt; a stop".

> ಈ ರೈಲಿಗೆ ಆ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಲುಗಡೆ ಇಲ್ಲ.
>
> *ī railige ā ūrinalli nilugaḍe illa.*
>
> This train has no stop in that town.

Built on ನಿಲ್ಲು, to stop: a bus stop or a parking place can also be a ನಿಲುಗಡೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿಲುಗಡೆ** (*nilugaḍe*) is a native Kannada noun built on *nil*, *nilu*, "to stand, to stop" (DEDR 3675). Where the ending -*gaḍe* comes from is not settled.
