## Script — the top of lǎo, then two strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-KAO-TEST-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 考

**The top of 老, four strokes**, as in 老 and on the left of 教: a short bar, a vertical through it, a long bar, and a long stroke falling to the left.

**Then two strokes under it**: a short bar, and a stroke that starts under the bar's left end, drops a little, runs right, then turns down and hooks to the left.

4 + 2 = six.
