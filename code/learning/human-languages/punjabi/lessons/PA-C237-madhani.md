## You'll want to know: ਮਧਾਣੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C237-KAM237-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਧਾਣੀ** — *madhāṇī* — "a churning stick".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਮਧਾਣੀ ਨਾਲ ਲੱਸੀ ਬਣਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī madhāṇī nāl lassī baṇāundī hai.*
>
> Grandmother makes lassi with a churning stick.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਧਾਣੀ** (*madhāṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *manthāna*, "churning-stick"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *madhāṇī*, "churning-stick" (CDIAL 9842).
