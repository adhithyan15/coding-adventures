Gujarati, chapter 542: Meeting Halfway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સમાધાન (samādhān) — a compromise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a loophole, then the Gujarati for a proposal.

You'll want to know: સમાધાન.
સમાધાન — samādhān — "a compromise".
જો તમે બંને બેસીને વાત કરશો તો સમાધાન થશે. jo tame banne besīne vāt karśo to samādhān thaśe. If you two sit down and talk, you'll reach a compromise.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સમાધાન (samādhān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit samādhāna, "putting together" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is the settling of a quarrel.

What you've built.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a proposal, then the Gujarati for a compromise.

You'll want to know: વાટાઘાટ.
વાટાઘાટ — vāṭāghāṭ — "negotiations".
જો વાટાઘાટ સારી ચાલશે તો કાલે કારખાનું ફરી ખૂલશે. jo vāṭāghāṭ sārī cālśe to kāle kārkhānũ pharī khūlśe. If the talks go well, the factory will open again tomorrow.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વાટાઘાટ (vāṭāghāṭ) is a jingling pair of words for talk back and forth between two sides. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

જો કોઈ મધ્યસ્થી મદદ કરશે તો બંને ભાઈ ફરી મળશે. jo koī madhyasthī madad karśe to banne bhāī pharī maḷśe. If a mediator helps, the two brothers will meet again.

મધ્યસ્થી (madhyasthī) is built on the tatsama Sanskrit madhyastha, "standing in the middle" (Monier-Williams): the one who stands between two sides.
