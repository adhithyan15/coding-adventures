## You'll want to know: der Angeber
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C475-ANGEBER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Angeber** — "show-off".

> Der neue Kollege ist ein echter Angeber.
>
> *The new colleague is a real show-off.*

Plural: *die Angeber*. A woman is *die Angeberin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Angeber* is built on *angeben* "to show off" with *-er*; *geben* is inherited from Old High German *geban*, from Proto-Germanic *gebaną* (EtymDB).
