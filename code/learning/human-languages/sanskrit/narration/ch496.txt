Sanskrit, chapter 496: A Wedding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मंगलसूत्रम् (maṅgalasūtram) — the marriage thread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for holds back, then the Sanskrit for a celibate student.

You'll want to know: मंगलसूत्रम्.
मंगलसूत्रम् — maṅgalasūtram — "the marriage thread".
विवाहे वरः मंगलसूत्रं बध्नाति। vivāhe varaḥ maṅgalasūtraṃ badhnāti. At the wedding the groom ties the marriage thread.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
मंगलसूत्रम् (maṅgalasūtram) is built from maṅgala, "good luck, a blessing", and सूत्रम्, "a thread": MW gives "lucky thread", the marriage thread tied by the bridegroom round the bride's neck (MW).

[pause 2 seconds]

वधूः रक्तां शाटिकां धारयति। vadhūḥ raktāṃ śāṭikāṃ dhārayati. The bride wears a red sari.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वधूः (vadhūḥ) is traced to √vadh, a form of the root √vah, "to carry", by MW: the one led home; MW gives "a bride, a newly married woman" (MW).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the marriage thread, then the Sanskrit for a bride.

You'll want to know: परिणयति.
परिणयति — pariṇayati — "marries".
युवकः युवतिं परिणयति। yuvakaḥ yuvatiṃ pariṇayati. The young man marries the young woman.
Past (he …): सः युवतिं परिणीतवान्, saḥ yuvatiṃ pariṇītavān.

The word, taken apart.
परिणयति (pariṇayati) is built from pari, "around", and the root √nī, "to lead": to lead the bride round the fire; MW's ppp pariṇīta is "married" (MW).

वधूः मण्डपं प्रविशति। vadhūḥ maṇḍapaṃ praviśati. The bride enters the pavilion.

The word, taken apart.
मण्डपः (maṇḍapaḥ) MW divides it maṇḍa-pa but gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it gives "an open hall or temporary shed erected on festive occasions, a pavilion" (MW).
