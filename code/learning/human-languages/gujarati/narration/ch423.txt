Gujarati, chapter 423: Towns and Drains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નગર (nagar) — a town; a city (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a sweet-maker, then the Gujarati for to sell well.

You'll want to know: નગર.
નગર — nagar — "a town; a city (formal)".
આ નગર નદીના કિનારે છે. ā nagar nadīnā kināre che. This town is on the bank of the river.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
નગર (nagar) is a tatsama, Sanskrit nagara, "a town, a city" (Monier-Williams); it ends many place names.

Lesson 2 of 5.
જિલ્લો (jillo) — a district (of a state).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sell well, then the Gujarati for a town.

You'll want to know: જિલ્લો.
જિલ્લો — jillo — "a district (of a state)".
અમારા જિલ્લામાં ઘણાં ગામ છે. amārā jillāmā̃ ghaṇā̃ gām che. There are many villages in our district.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
જિલ્લો (jillo) is usually traced to Arabic ḍilʿ, "a side, a rib", which came through Persian as a word for a division of land.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a town, then the Gujarati for a district.

You'll want to know: ગટર.
ગટર — gaṭar — "a drain; a sewer".
વરસાદમાં ગટર છલકાઈ ગઈ. varsādmā̃ gaṭar chalkāī gaī. The drain overflowed in the rain.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગટર (gaṭar) is borrowed from English gutter; in Gujarati a ગટર is any drain or sewer.

Lesson 5 of 5.
વસાવવું (vasāvvũ) — to set up a home; to found (a town).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a sewer, then the Gujarati for a stink.

You'll want to know: વસાવવું.
વસાવવું — vasāvvũ — "to set up a home; to found (a town)".
દાદાએ ગામમાં નવું ઘર વસાવ્યું. dādāe gāmmā̃ navũ ghar vasāvyũ. Grandfather set up a new home in the village.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નવું ગામ વસાવ્યું, teṇe navũ gām vasāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
વસાવવું (vasāvvũ) is the causative partner of વસવું, to settle, a tadbhava of Sanskrit vasati, "dwells" (CDIAL 11435): to make people or a home settle in a place.
