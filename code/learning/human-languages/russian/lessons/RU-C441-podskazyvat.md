## You'll want to know: подсказывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C441-PROKHOZHIY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**подсказывать** (*podskázyvat*) — "to tell (someone the way); to prompt".

> Прохожий подсказывает нам дорогу к вокзалу.
>
> *Prokhózhiy podskázyvayet nam dorógu k vokzálu.*
>
> A passer-by tells us the way to the station.

Imperfective; perfective *подсказать* (*подскажу, подскажешь*). Takes the dative: *подскажите, пожалуйста* — could you tell me, please.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подсказывать* is built from *под-* 'under, quietly' and *-сказывать*, from *сказать* 'to say': to say something quietly to help. EtymDB lists *сказать* without a deeper source.
