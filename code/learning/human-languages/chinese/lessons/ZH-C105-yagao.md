## You'll want to know: yágāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C105-BATH105-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yágāo* — "toothpaste".

> *méiyǒu yágāo le* — We're out of toothpaste.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yágāo* is built from yá 'tooth' and gāo 'paste', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngae* and *kaw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as fat or grease.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
