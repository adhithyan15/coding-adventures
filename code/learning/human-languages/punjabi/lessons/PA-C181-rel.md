# ਰੇਲ — rail or train

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C90-TOWN90-04, PA-LEX-C90-TOWN90-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ**, then the older train word **ਗੱਡੀ**.

## Grammar Lens: one transport word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-TRAVEL-RAIL-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C90-TOWN90-04, PA-LEX-C90-TOWN90-05] -->

> **ਰੇਲ** — *rel* — rail; train

Keep this as one practical transport word. The older **ਗੱਡੀ** remains useful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੇਲ** (*rel*) is a loan from English *rail*.
