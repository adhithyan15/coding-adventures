Marathi, chapter 473: Mischief and Fun.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
खोडकर (khoḍkar) — naughty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for hasty, then the Marathi for impatient.

You'll want to know: खोडकर.
खोडकर — khoḍkar — "naughty".
खोडकर मुलगा वर्गात सारखा बोलत असतो. khoḍkar mulgā vargāt sārkhā bolat asto. The naughty boy keeps talking in class.
Built on खोड, a bad habit or prank (not the tree trunk): खोडी काढणे, to play a prank. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
खोडकर (khoḍkar) is a Marathi adjective, खोड, "mischief", with the ending -kar; the origin of खोड is not settled.

विनोदी — vinodī — "funny; witty".
आमच्या मावशी खूप विनोदी आहेत. āmcyā māvśī khūp vinodī āhet. Our aunt is very funny.
From विनोद, a joke. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
विनोदी (vinodī) is a Marathi adjective built on विनोद, "a joke", a tatsama from Sanskrit vinoda, "driving away; diversion" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
थट्टा करणे (thaṭṭā karṇe) — to tease; to joke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for funny, then the Marathi for amusement.

You'll want to know: थट्टा करणे.
थट्टा करणे — thaṭṭā karṇe — "to tease; to joke".
मित्रांनी माझ्या टोपीची थट्टा केली. mitrānnī mājhyā ṭopīcī thaṭṭā kelī. My friends teased me about my cap.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने थट्टा केली, tyāne thaṭṭā kelī.
The target takes the genitive: भावाची थट्टा करणे (thaṭṭā karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
थट्टा करणे (thaṭṭā karṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on थट्टा, "a joke", which CDIAL lists under the reconstructed ṭhaṭṭha, "joke" (CDIAL 5492), with करणे.
