Malayalam, chapter 205: Simple, Useful, Excellent, Strange, Modern.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ലളിതമായ (laḷitamāya) — simple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for rough, then the Malayalam for round.

You'll want to know: ലളിതമായ.
ലളിതമായ — laḷitamāya — "simple".

The word, taken apart.
ലളിതമായ (laḷitamāya) joins Sanskrit lalita, "graceful, easy", to native āya, "having become" (DEDR 333). DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

Lesson 3 of 5.
മികച്ച (mikacca) — excellent, best.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for simple, then the Malayalam for useful.

You'll want to know: മികച്ച.
മികച്ച — mikacca — "excellent, best".

The word, taken apart.
മികച്ച (mikacca) is native Dravidian, a past form of the verb "to increase, be foremost"; DEDR 4838 lists the root with Tamil miku, Kannada mikku and Telugu migulu.
