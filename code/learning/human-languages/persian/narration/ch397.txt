Persian, chapter 397: Being prepared.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
احتیاط (ehtiyât) — caution, care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to coordinate, then the Persian for to carry out.

You'll want to know: احتیاط.
احتیاط (ehtiyât) — "caution, care".
او با احتیاط از پله ها پایین رفت. u bâ ehtiyât az pelle hâ pâyin raft. He went down the stairs with care.
Bâ ehtiyât — carefully. Ehtiyât kardan — to be careful.

The word, taken apart.
احتیاط (ehtiyât) is usually traced to Arabic iḥtiyāṭ "caution, precaution".

Lesson 2 of 5.
محض احتیاط (mahz-e ehtiyât) — as a precaution, in case.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to carry out, then the Persian for caution.

You'll want to know: محض احتیاط.
محض احتیاط (mahz-e ehtiyât) — "as a precaution, in case".
محض احتیاط یک چتر برداشتم. mahz-e ehtiyât yek chatr bardâshtam. I took an umbrella in case.
Mahz "pure" + ehtiyât "caution": purely for caution.

The word, taken apart.
محض احتیاط (mahz-e ehtiyât) joins two words usually traced to Arabic, maḥḍ "pure, mere" and iḥtiyāṭ "caution", with a Persian ezafe: purely for caution's sake.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پیش آمدن (pish âmadan) — to come up, to crop up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for as a precaution, then the Persian for readiness.

You'll want to know: پیش آمدن.
پیش آمدن (pish âmadan) — "to come up, to crop up".
یک مشکل کوچک پیش آمد. yek moshkel-e kuchek pish âmad. A small problem came up.
Pish "forward" + âmadan "to come" (present stem â-).

The word, taken apart.
پیش آمدن (pish âmadan) is native Persian, built from pish "forward, before" + âmadan "to come", which EtymDB traces to PIE gʷem- "to step". A problem steps forward.

گزینه (gozine) — "an option, a choice".
ما دو گزینه داریم. mâ do gozine dârim. We have two options.
From gozin-, the present stem of gozidan "to choose".

The word, taken apart.
گزینه (gozine) is a modern native word, built on gozin-, the present stem of gozidan "to choose": what you can choose.
