## You'll want to know: दीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C496-SASAR496-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दीर** — *dīr* — "a husband's brother".

> लग्नानंतर तिचा दीर तिच्याशी नीट वागतो.
>
> *lagnānantar ticā dīr ticyāśī nīṭ vāgto.*
>
> After the wedding her husband's brother treats her well.

Masculine.

A woman's husband's brother, usually the younger one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीर** (*dīr*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *devṛ*, "husband's brother"; CDIAL lists Marathi *dīr* (CDIAL 6546).
