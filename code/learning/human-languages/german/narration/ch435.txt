German, chapter 435: Abkürzung oder Umweg.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Abkürzung — the shortcut; the abbreviation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the turning, then the German for to branch off.

You'll want to know: die Abkürzung.
die Abkürzung — "the shortcut; the abbreviation".
Durch den Park gibt es eine Abkürzung zum Bahnhof. There is a shortcut to the station through the park.
Plural: die Abkürzungen. Usw. is the Abkürzung for und so weiter.

The word, taken apart.
Abkürzung is built on abkürzen, from kurz 'short', which goes back to Latin curtus 'shortened' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

abkürzen — "to take a shortcut; to shorten".
Wir kürzen über den Parkplatz ab. We take a shortcut across the car park.

abkürzen is built from ab and kürzen 'to shorten', from kurz 'short', which goes back to Latin curtus 'shortened' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Umweg — the detour, the long way round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the shortcut, then the German for to take a shortcut.

You'll want to know: der Umweg.
der Umweg — "the detour, the long way round".
Wegen der Baustelle machen wir einen Umweg. Because of the road works we take a detour.
Plural: die Umwege. Auf Umwegen — in a roundabout way.

The word, taken apart.
Umweg is built from um 'round, around' and Weg 'way', inherited from Proto-Germanic wegaz (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
umkehren — to turn back, to turn round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to take a shortcut, then the German for the detour.

You'll want to know: umkehren.
umkehren — "to turn back, to turn round".
Die Straße ist gesperrt, deshalb kehren wir um. The road is closed, so we turn back.
Separable; Perfekt with sein: wir sind umgekehrt.

The word, taken apart.
umkehren is inherited from Middle High German ummekēren (EtymDB), built from um 'round' and kehren 'to turn'; Old English had a matching verb, ymbċierran.

Lesson 5 of 5.
verfahren — to get lost driving (mostly reflexive: sich verfahren); to proceed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the detour, then the German for to turn back.

You'll want to know: verfahren.
verfahren — "to get lost driving (mostly reflexive: sich verfahren); to proceed".
Ohne Navi hat sich mein Vater verfahren. Without the satnav my father got lost.
Reflexive for getting lost: ich habe mich verfahren. Das Verfahren — the procedure.

The word, taken apart.
verfahren is inherited from Middle High German vervarn and Old High German firfaran (EtymDB), built from ver- and fahren 'to travel'.
