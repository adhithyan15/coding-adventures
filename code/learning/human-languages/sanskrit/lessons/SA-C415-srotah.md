## You'll want to know: स्रोतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C415-SROTAS415-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्रोतः** — *srotaḥ* — "a flowing current; a stream".

> वर्षायां नद्याः स्रोतः वर्धते।
>
> *varṣāyāṃ nadyāḥ srotaḥ vardhate.*
>
> In the rains the river's current grows.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्रोतः** (*srotaḥ*) comes from the root √sru, "to flow"; MW gives *srotas* as the current of a river, a stream (MW).
