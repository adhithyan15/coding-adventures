## You'll want to know: جنایت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C318-JENAYAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جنایت** (*jenâyat*) — "a serious crime, a felony".

> این جنایت همه شهر را شوکه کرد.
>
> *In jenâyat hame-ye shahr râ shoke kard.*
>
> This crime shocked the whole city.

For violent crimes. *jenâyatkâr* "a criminal".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jenâyat* is borrowed from Arabic *jināya*, "crime" (EtymDB).
