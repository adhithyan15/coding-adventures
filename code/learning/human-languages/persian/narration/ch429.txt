Persian, chapter 429: The railway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
راه آهن (râh-âhan) — a railway (US: a railroad).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pickup, then the Persian for to run over.

You'll want to know: راه آهن.
راه آهن (râh-âhan) — "a railway (US: a railroad)".
این شهر ایستگاه راه آهن ندارد. In shahr istgâh-e râh-âhan nadârad. This town has no railway station.
Literally 'iron road'. Istgâh-e râh-âhan — the railway station.

The word, taken apart.
râh-âhan is a native compound, an iron road: râh 'road' goes back to Middle Persian rāh (EtymDB), and âhan 'iron' is a native word that EtymDB lists.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سکو (sakku) — a platform (at a station); a raised step.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to run over, then the Persian for a railway.

You'll want to know: سکو.
سکو (sakku) — "a platform (at a station); a raised step".
قطار از سکوی دو حرکت میکند. Qatâr az sakku-ye do harekat mikonad. The train leaves from platform two.
Also a stone step or ledge in front of a house.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of sakku is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it. It first meant a raised stone seat or ledge, and later a station platform.

Lesson 3 of 5.
واگن (vâgon) — a carriage (of a train), a railway car.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a railway, then the Persian for a platform.

You'll want to know: واگن.
واگن (vâgon) — "a carriage (of a train), a railway car".
واگن (vâgon) ما آخر قطار بود. Vâgon-e mâ âkhar-e qatâr bud. Our carriage was at the end of the train.
Vâgon-e ghazâkhori — the dining car.

The word, taken apart.
vâgon was borrowed from a European language: it is usually traced to French wagon, which itself came from English wagon. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کوپه (kupe) — a compartment (in a train).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a platform, then the Persian for a carriage.

You'll want to know: کوپه.
کوپه (kupe) — "a compartment (in a train)".
در کوپه ما چهار تخت بود. Dar kupe-ye mâ chahâr takht bud. There were four berths in our compartment.
Night trains in Iran have four- or six-berth kupe.

The word, taken apart.
kupe was borrowed from French and is usually traced to French coupé, first a closed carriage. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

قطار از ریل خارج شد. Qatâr az reyl khârej shod. The train came off the rails.
