## You'll want to know: વીનવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C361-TIKA361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વીનવવું** — *vīnavvũ* — "to entreat".

> મીરા દાક્તરને વહેલા આવવા વીનવે છે.
>
> *Mīrā dāktarne vahelā āvvā vīnve che.*
>
> Mira is pleading with the doctor to come early.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘણું વીનવ્યું, *teṇe ghaṇũ vīnavyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વીનવવું** (*vīnavvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *víjñapayati*, "makes known, begs"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to entreat" (CDIAL 11705).
