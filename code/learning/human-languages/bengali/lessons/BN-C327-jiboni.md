## You'll want to know: জীবনী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C327-UPONNAS327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**জীবনী** — *jībônī* — "a biography".

> তিনি এক বিখ্যাত বিজ্ঞানীর জীবনী লিখলেন।
>
> *tini ek bikhyātô bigyānīr jībônī likhlen.*
>
> He wrote a biography of a famous scientist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জীবনী** (*jībônī*) is **জীবন**, life — Sanskrit *jīvana* (Monier-Williams) — with the ending -ī: the written story of a life.
