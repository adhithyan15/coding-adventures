## You'll want to know: تخیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C522-VERSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تخیل** (*takhayyol*) — "imagination".

> بچه ها تخیل قوی دارند.
>
> *bachchehâ takhayyol-e qavi dârand.*
>
> Children have strong imaginations.

The adjective is *takhayyoli* 'imaginary'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*takhayyol* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *taḫayyul*, 'imagining'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
