## You'll want to know: स्थलांतर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C447-BAGLA447-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थलांतर** — *sthalāntar* — "migration".

> हिवाळ्यात अनेक पक्षी इथे स्थलांतर करतात.
>
> *hivāḷyāt anek pakṣī ithe sthalāntar kartāt.*
>
> In winter many birds migrate here.

Neuter.

स्थलांतर करणे, to migrate; also people moving for work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थलांतर** (*sthalāntar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sthalāntara*, "another place" (Monier-Williams): going over to another place.
