## You'll want to know: der Schneepflug
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C442-GLATTEIS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schneepflug** — "the snowplough (US: snowplow)".

> In der Nacht fährt der Schneepflug durch unsere Straße.
>
> *In the night the snowplough goes along our street.*

Plural: *die Schneepflüge*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schneepflug* is built from *Schnee* 'snow' and *Pflug* 'plough', both inherited: *Pflug* comes from Middle High German *pfluoc*, a cousin of English *plough* (EtymDB).
