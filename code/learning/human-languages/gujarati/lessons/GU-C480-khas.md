## You'll want to know: ખસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C480-KHAS480-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ખસ** — *khas* — "an itchy rash; scabies".

> ગંદા પાણીથી બાળકને ખસ થઈ.
>
> *gandā pāṇīthī bāḷakne khas thaī.*
>
> The child got an itchy rash from dirty water.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખસ** (*khas*) goes back to Sanskrit *khasa*, "itch, scab", which CDIAL thinks probably came from Dravidian; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 3854).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
