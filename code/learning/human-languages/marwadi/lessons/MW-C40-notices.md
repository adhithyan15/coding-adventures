# (reading) — six lines off a counter

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-KONI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson gave you single words. These give each word
something to stand on.

[PAUSE 1s] Still nothing new — the market lines you already say, on the page.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SKILL-READ-NOTICE]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-MAHANGO-01, MW-SCRIPT-DAS-01] -->

> ये कितणे कू है?
> ये घणो महंगो है।
> दस करो।
> कोनी।
> पैसा ले लो।
> पाछे मिलसू।

[PAUSE 3s] Read down once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and this time hear whose line each one is. Three belong to
you and three to the person behind the counter.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-KONI-01, MW-SCRIPT-PAISA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **कोनी।** One word, and it is a whole turn in the conversation. A
notice does not have to be long to be complete.

[PAUSE 2s] Read in order, these six are not six notices at all — they are one
bargain, from the first question to the walk-away. You have practised every one
of them out loud; this is the first time you have seen them written down in the
order they actually happen.

[PAUSE 2s] That order is the thing to carry away. A market exchange has a
shape, and knowing the shape is most of reading it.
