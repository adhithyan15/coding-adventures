## You'll want to know: بحث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C366-BAHS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بحث** (*bahs*) — "a discussion, a debate".

> بعد از جلسه یک بحث طولانی داشتیم.
>
> *ba'd az jalase yek bahs-e tulâni dâshtim.*
>
> After the meeting we had a long discussion.

Plural *bahshâ*, or formal *mabâhes*. In Persian, ث sounds like s.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بحث** was borrowed from Arabic *baḥth* "search, examination, discussion" (EtymDB): a debate is a search for the answer.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
