## You'll want to know: حاملہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C483-BABY483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حاملہ** — *hāmilā* — "pregnant".

> میری بہن حاملہ ہے۔
>
> *merī bahan hāmilā hai.*
>
> My sister is pregnant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حاملہ** (*hāmilā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥāmila*, "carrying", and so "pregnant".
