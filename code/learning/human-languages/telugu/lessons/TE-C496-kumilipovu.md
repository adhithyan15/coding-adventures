# కుమిలిపోవు (kumilipōvu) — to grieve inwardly

## You'll want to know: కుమిలిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C496-BADHA496-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కుమిలిపోవు** — *kumilipōvu* — "to grieve inwardly".

> ఆమె ఎవరికీ చెప్పకుండా కుమిలిపోయింది.
>
> *āme evarikī ceppakuṇḍā kumilipōyindi.*
>
> She grieved inside without telling anyone.

Past (I …): కుమిలిపోయాను, *kumilipōyānu*.

Grief that smoulders without being spoken.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కుమిలిపోవు** (*kumilipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1752 lists Telugu *kumulu*, "to smoulder without flame, to be consumed inwardly, to grieve", and the verb ends in *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
