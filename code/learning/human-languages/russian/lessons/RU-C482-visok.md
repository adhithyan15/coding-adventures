## You'll want to know: висок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C482-HEAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**висок** (*visók*) — "a temple (of the head)".

> У него седые волосы на висках.
>
> *U negó sedýye vólosy na viskákh.*
>
> He has grey hair at the temples.

Masculine; genitive виска, plural виски (stress on the end). Not to be confused with *виски* — whisky.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *висок* without a source; it is usually linked to *висеть* 'to hang', after the hair that hangs at the temples, but the link is not settled.
