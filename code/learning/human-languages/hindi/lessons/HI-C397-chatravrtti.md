## You'll want to know: छात्रवृत्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C397-VISHAY397-03]; assesses=[] -->

**छात्रवृत्ति** — *chātravṛtti* — "a scholarship (a grant for study)".

> अगर मुझे छात्रवृत्ति मिलेगी तो मैं शहर में पढ़ूँगा।
>
> *agar mujhe chātravṛtti milegī to maiṁ śahar meṁ paṛhūṅgā.*
>
> If I get a scholarship, I'll study in the city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छात्रवृत्ति** (*chātravṛtti*) joins two Sanskrit words, *chāttra*, "a pupil" (Hindi छात्र), and *vṛtti*, "conduct; a livelihood" (Monier-Williams): a pupil's keep. The compound is a modern one.
