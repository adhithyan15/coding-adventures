Gujarati, chapter 270: Kind, honest and funny.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સ્વભાવ (svabhāv) — temperament, nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to remember fondly, then the Gujarati for respect.

You'll want to know: સ્વભાવ.
સ્વભાવ — svabhāv — "temperament, nature".
તેનો સ્વભાવ શાંત નથી. — teno svabhāv śānt nathī — "His temperament is not calm."

The word, taken apart.
સ્વભાવ (svabhāv) is Sanskrit svabhāva, "own being, nature", built from sva, "own", and bhāva, "being", and taken over as a tatsama; EtymDB lists it as borrowed from Sanskrit (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
રમૂજી (ramūjī) — funny, witty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for kind, then the Gujarati for honest.

You'll want to know: રમૂજી.
રમૂજી — ramūjī — "funny, witty".
મારા કાકા રમૂજી છે, પણ મારા પિતા નથી. — mārā kākā ramūjī che, paṇ mārā pitā nathī — "My uncle is funny, but my father is not."

The word, taken apart.
રમૂજી (ramūjī) is built on ramūj, "jest", with -ī. Ramūj is usually traced to Arabic rumūz, plural of ramz, "a sign, a hint", through Persian; its Arabic root is r-m-z (Lane).
