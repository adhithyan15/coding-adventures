# ಚಟ (caṭa) — an addiction; a bad habit

## You'll want to know: ಚಟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C505-SHRADDHE505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಟ** — *caṭa* — "an addiction; a bad habit".

> ಅವನಿಗೆ ಸಿಗರೇಟಿನ ಚಟ ಇದೆ.
>
> *avanige sigarēṭina caṭa ide.*
>
> He has a smoking habit.

ಚಟ ಬಿಡು, to kick a habit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಟ** (*caṭa*) is an everyday Kannada word for a habit that has a grip on you; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
