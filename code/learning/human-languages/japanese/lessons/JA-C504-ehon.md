## You'll want to know: えほん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C504-ART504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**えほん** — *ehon* — "a picture book".

> **ははは こどもに えほんを よみます。** — *haha wa kodomo ni ehon o yomimasu* — "My mother reads picture books to the children."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えほん** (*ehon*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *e*, "picture" and *hon*, "book" (Middle Chinese *pwonX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
