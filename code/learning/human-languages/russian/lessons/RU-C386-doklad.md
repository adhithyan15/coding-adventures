## You'll want to know: доклад
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C386-DOKLAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**доклад** (*doklád*) — "a talk, a paper (given aloud)".

> На конференции я делаю доклад.
>
> *Na konferéntsii ya délayu doklád.*
>
> I am giving a talk at the conference.

Masculine. A prepared talk, or a report to a superior: *доклад начальнику*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**доклад** is built on *докладывать* "to report", from *до-* and *класть* "to lay", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*klasti*: to lay a matter before someone.
