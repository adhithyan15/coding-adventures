## You'll want to know: píngzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C186-THINGS186-04]; assesses=[] -->

*píngzi* — "bottle".

> *zhège píngzi lǐ yǒu shuǐ* — There's water in this bottle.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*píngzi* is built from *píng* and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *beng* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a vessel for drawing water. In the word the second loses its tone.
