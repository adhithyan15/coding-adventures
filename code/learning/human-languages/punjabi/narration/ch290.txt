Punjabi, chapter 290: The Week's Weather.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਅਸਮਾਨ (asmān) — the sky.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to be chilled, then the Punjabi for the cold season.

You'll want to know: ਅਸਮਾਨ.
ਅਸਮਾਨ — asmān — "the sky".
ਅੱਜ ਅਸਮਾਨ ਨੀਲਾ ਹੈ। ajj asmān nīlā hai. Today the sky is blue.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਅਸਮਾਨ (asmān) is a loan, usually traced to Persian āsmān, "sky".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਗੜੇ (gaṛe) — hail; hailstones.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for the cold season, then the Punjabi for the sky.

You'll want to know: ਗੜੇ.
ਗੜੇ — gaṛe — "hail; hailstones".
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਗੜੇ ਪੈਣਗੇ। kall gaṛe paiṇge. There will be hail tomorrow.
A masculine noun, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੜੇ (gaṛe) is inherited from a reconstructed gaḍa, "dropping"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gaṛā, "hail", under it (CDIAL 3969).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਵਗਣਾ (vagṇā) — to blow; to flow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to clear up, then the Punjabi for to go down.

You'll want to know: ਵਗਣਾ.
ਵਗਣਾ — vagṇā — "to blow; to flow".
ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਤੇਜ਼ ਹਵਾ ਵਗੇਗੀ। rāt nū̃ tez havā vagegī. A strong wind will blow at night.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਹਵਾ ਵਗੀ, oh havā vagī.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਗਣਾ (vagṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit vrajati, "goes, wanders"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vagṇā, "to move, flow, blow", under it (CDIAL 12225).
