Lesson 2 of 5.
सोनेरी (sonerī) — golden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for orange, then the Marathi for grey.

You'll want to know: सोनेरी.
सोनेरी — sonerī — "golden".

The word, taken apart.
सोनेरी (sonerī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from suvarṇadhara, "containing gold", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13522). It is built on suvarṇa, "gold".

Lesson 4 of 5.
उतरणे (utarṇe) — to get down, to get off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for golden, then the Marathi for to climb.

You'll want to know: उतरणे.
उतरणे — utarṇe — "to get down, to get off".

The word, taken apart.
उतरणे (utarṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from uttarati, "passes over; alights, descends" (CDIAL 1770). CDIAL traces the sense from "crosses over" to "lands" to "alights" and so "descends".
