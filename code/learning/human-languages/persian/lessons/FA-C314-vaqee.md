## You'll want to know: واقعه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C314-RUYDAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**واقعه** (*vâqe'e*) — "an incident, an occurrence".

> این واقعه در یک شب سرد اتفاق افتاد.
>
> *In vâqe'e dar yek shab-e sard ettefâq oftâd.*
>
> This incident happened on a cold night.

Formal; broken plural *vaqâye'*. Often for serious or historic events.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vâqe'e* is borrowed from Arabic *wāqi'a*, "event, incident" (EtymDB).
