## You'll want to know: संयमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C489-VYASANA489-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संयमः** — *saṃyamaḥ* — "self-control".

> संयमेन व्यसनं त्यजतु।
>
> *saṃyamena vyasanaṃ tyajatu.*
>
> Give up the vice through self-control.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संयमः** (*saṃyamaḥ*) is built from *sam* and the root √yam, "to hold"; MW gives "restraint, control of the senses, self-control" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
