Persian, chapter 414: At the bank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ارز (arz) — foreign currency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a subsidy, then the Persian for a rate.

You'll want to know: ارز.
ارز (arz) — "foreign currency".
نرخ ارز امروز بالا رفت. nerkh-e arz emruz bâlâ raft. The exchange rate went up today.
Different from arzesh "value". Arz-e khâreji — foreign currency.

The word, taken apart.
ارز (arz) "currency" is listed in EtymDB without a parent, and its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
اعتبار (e'tebâr) — credit; validity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a rate, then the Persian for foreign currency.

You'll want to know: اعتبار.
اعتبار (e'tebâr) — "credit; validity".
اعتبار این کارت تا آخر ماه است. e'tebâr-e in kârt tâ âkhar-e mâh ast. This card is valid until the end of the month.
Also trust or standing: e'tebâr dâshtan. Kârt-e e'tebâri — a credit card.

The word, taken apart.
اعتبار (e'tebâr) comes from Arabic i'tibār "regard, consideration" (EtymDB): credit is the regard others have for you.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سپرده (seporde) — a deposit, savings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for foreign currency, then the Persian for credit.

You'll want to know: سپرده.
سپرده (seporde) — "a deposit, savings".
او پولش را در بانک سپرده گذاشت. u pulash râ dar bânk seporde gozâsht. He put his money in a savings deposit at the bank.
The past participle of sepordan "to entrust".

The word, taken apart.
سپرده (seporde) is native Persian, the past participle of sepordan "to entrust, to deposit", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
برداشت کردن (bardâsht kardan) — to withdraw (money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for credit, then the Persian for a deposit.

You'll want to know: برداشت کردن.
برداشت کردن (bardâsht kardan) — "to withdraw (money)".
از خودپرداز صد هزار تومان برداشت کردم. az khodpardâz sad hezâr tumân bardâsht kardam. I withdrew a hundred thousand tomans from the cash machine.
Az hesâb bardâsht kardan — to withdraw from an account. Also to harvest.

The word, taken apart.
برداشت کردن (bardâsht kardan) is native Persian, built from bardâsht "taking up", from bar "up" and dâsht, the past stem of dâshtan "to hold", with kardan "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
انتقال دادن (enteqâl dâdan) — to transfer, to move.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a deposit, then the Persian for to withdraw.

You'll want to know: انتقال دادن.
انتقال دادن (enteqâl dâdan) — "to transfer, to move".
پول را به حساب برادرم انتقال دادم. pul râ be hesâb-e barâdaram enteqâl dâdam. I transferred the money to my brother's account.
Enteqâl "transfer" + dâdan. Also to move a patient or an office.

The word, taken apart.
انتقال comes from Arabic intiqāl "moving, passing" (EtymDB); with dâdan "to give" it means to move something on.
