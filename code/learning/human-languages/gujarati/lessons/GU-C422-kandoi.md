## You'll want to know: કંદોઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C422-STALL422-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કંદોઈ** — *kandoī* — "a sweet-maker; a sweet seller".

> દિવાળીમાં કંદોઈની દુકાને ભીડ છે.
>
> *divāḷīmā̃ kandoīnī dukāne bhīḍ che.*
>
> At Diwali there is a crowd at the sweet-maker's shop.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કંદોઈ** (*kandoī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kāndavika*, "a baker", built on *kandu*, an iron pan; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a confectioner" (CDIAL 3034).
