## You'll want to know: થડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C444-BUD444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**થડ** — *thaḍ* — "a tree trunk".

> વડનું થડ ઘણું મોટું છે.
>
> *vaḍnũ thaḍ ghaṇũ moṭũ che.*
>
> The banyan's trunk is very big.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થડ** (*thaḍ*) goes back to *\*thuḍa*, "a trunk, a branch", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 6098).
