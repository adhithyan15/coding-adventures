## You'll want to know: nocēns, nocentis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C311-DAMNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**nocēns, nocentis** — "guilty; harmful".

> Nocēns poenam dare dēbet.
>
> *The guilty man must pay the penalty.*

One ending, genitive *nocentis*: the present participle of a verb meaning 'to harm'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**nocēns** is the present participle of *noceō*, "to harm" (Lewis & Short): doing harm, and so guilty.
