## You'll want to know: शीर्षक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C341-PANNA341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शीर्षक** — *śīrṣak* — "a title; a heading".

> मेरी राय में उपन्यास का शीर्षक बहुत छोटा है।
>
> *merī rāy meṁ upanyās kā śīrṣak bahut choṭā hai.*
>
> In my opinion the novel's title is too short.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शीर्षक** (*śīrṣak*) is Sanskrit *śīrṣaka* (Monier-Williams), from *śīrṣa*, the head: what stands at the head of a book or a page.
