## You'll want to know: ملتوی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C398-PARVAZ398-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ملتوی** — *multavī* — "postponed".

> طوفان کی وجہ سے پرواز ملتوی ہو گئی۔
>
> *tūfān kī vajah se parvāz multavī ho gaī.*
>
> The flight was put off because of the storm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملتوی** (*multavī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *multawī*, "turned aside, bent"; the sense "put off till later" probably grew in Persian and Urdu.
