## You'll want to know: しはつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C375-SHUUDEN375-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しはつ** — *shihatsu* — "the first train of the day".

> **しはつは ごじに でます。** — *shihatsu wa goji ni demasu* — "The first train leaves at five."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しはつ** (*shihatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "begin" (Middle Chinese *syiX*) and *hatsu*, "departure" (*pjot*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
