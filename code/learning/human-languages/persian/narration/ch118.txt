Persian, chapter 118: Road, Car park, Raisins, Plum, Squash.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
جاده (jâdde) — a road, a highway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a ship, then the Persian for an aeroplane.

You'll want to know: جاده.
جاده — jâdde — "a road, a highway".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: jâdde]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jâdde, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say jâdde]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for a ship, then the Persian for an aeroplane, then say jâdde again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does جاده (jâdde) mean? ("A road, a highway".) Say it once more.

A ladder? (نردبان, nardebân.) A chest? (صندوق, sanduq.) A suitcase? (چمدان, chamedân.) A backpack? (کوله پشتی, kuleposhti.) A battery? (باتری, bâtri.) A card? (کارت, kârt.) A ticket? (بلیت, bilit.) A stamp? (تمبر, tamr.) An envelope? (پاکت, pâkat.) A pencil sharpener? (مدادتراش, medâdtarâsh.)
