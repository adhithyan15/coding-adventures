Malayalam, chapter 483: Service and Meals.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സേവനം (sēvanaṁ) — a service.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to refund, then the Malayalam for an arrangement.

You'll want to know: സേവനം.
സേവനം — sēvanaṁ — "a service".
ആ ഹോട്ടലിലെ സേവനം വളരെ നല്ലതായിരുന്നു. ā hōṭṭalile sēvanaṁ vaḷare nallatāyirunnu. The service at that hotel was very good.
A bus service, a bank service and public service are all സേവനം (sēvanaṁ).

The word, taken apart.
സേവനം (sēvanaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit sevana, "serving, attending on", from the root sev, to serve (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
കൊതുകുവല (kotukuvala) — a mosquito net.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an arrangement, then the Malayalam for a service.

You'll want to know: കൊതുകുവല.
കൊതുകുവല — kotukuvala — "a mosquito net".
രാത്രി കുട്ടികൾ കൊതുകുവലയ്ക്കുള്ളിൽ ഉറങ്ങി. rātri kuṭṭikaḷ kotukuvalaykkuḷḷil uṟaṅṅi. At night the children slept inside a mosquito net.
കൊതുക്, a mosquito, plus വല, a net. A fishing net is also a വല.

The word, taken apart.
കൊതുകുവല (kotukuvala) is a native Dravidian compound of കൊതുക്, a mosquito (DEDR 2086), and വല, a net (DEDR 5288).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ശുചിത്വം (śucitvaṁ) — hygiene; cleanliness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a service, then the Malayalam for a mosquito net.

You'll want to know: ശുചിത്വം.
ശുചിത്വം — śucitvaṁ — "hygiene; cleanliness".
അടുക്കളയിൽ ശുചിത്വം വളരെ പ്രധാനമാണ്. aṭukkaḷayil śucitvaṁ vaḷare pradhānamāṇŭ. Hygiene is very important in a kitchen.
A more formal word than വൃത്തി, cleanliness; it is common on notices.

The word, taken apart.
ശുചിത്വം (śucitvaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit śucitva, "clearness, purity" (Monier-Williams), from śuci, clean, with the abstract ending -tva.

Lesson 4 of 5.
മെനു (menu) — a menu.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a mosquito net, then the Malayalam for hygiene.

You'll want to know: മെനു.
മെനു — menu — "a menu".
ഹോട്ടലിലെ മെനുവിൽ മീൻകറി ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു. hōṭṭalile menuvil mīnkaṟi uṇṭāyirunnu. There was fish curry on the hotel menu.
A loan ending in -ഉ adds -വിൽ for in: മെനുവിൽ.

The word, taken apart.
മെനു (menu) was borrowed from English menu, the list of dishes in a restaurant.

വെയിറ്റർ — veyiṟṟar — "a waiter".

In a small eating place people call out ചേട്ടാ to him instead.

The word, taken apart.
വെയിറ്റർ (veyiṟṟar) was borrowed from English waiter, a person who serves food at tables.
