## You'll want to know: વગર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C546-VAGAR546-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વગર** — *vagar* — "without".

> જો તમે છત્રી વગર જશો તો તમે પલળી જશો.
>
> *jo tame chatrī vagar jaśo to tame palḷī jaśo.*
>
> If you go without an umbrella, you'll get soaked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વગર** (*vagar*) is usually traced to Persian *baghair*, "without". It follows its noun: **છત્રી વગર**.
