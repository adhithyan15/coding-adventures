# Review — a story in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-WHEN-PAST-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-HABITUAL-PAST-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "when I came home, Mother was making tea" and "I used to go". (*mī gharī ālo, tevhā āī cahā karat hotī*, *mī jāyco*.) Then say **ऋतू** and name its first letter: **ऋ**, said *ru*.

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: First I went to the station. Then I set out for the village.]
- [YOU SAY: After that I went home and slept.]
- [YOU SAY: He brought tea. After school I came home.]
- [YOU SAY: I like the monsoon season very much, with **ऋतू**]
- [YOU SAY: Arun's monsoon day, in four sentences, with **आधी**, **मग**, **त्यानंतर** and **शेवटी**]
