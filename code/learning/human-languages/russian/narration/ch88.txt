Russian, chapter 88: Post office, Cinema, Supermarket, Swimming pool, Stadium.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
почта (póchta) — a post office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a stop, then the Russian for a square.

You'll want to know: почта.
почта (póchta) — "a post office".

The word, taken apart.
почта (póchta) — recorded from 1669; it came through Polish poczta from Italian posta, from Latin posita, "placed", for the horses placed at relay stations (Vasmer). German Post is the same word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
кино (kinó) — a cinema.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a square, then the Russian for a post office.

You'll want to know: кино.
кино (kinó) — "a cinema".

The word, taken apart.
кино (kinó) is cut short from кинематограф, "cinematograph", from Greek kínēma, "motion", and gráphein, "to write"; German Kino was cut the same way (Trubachev, in Vasmer).
