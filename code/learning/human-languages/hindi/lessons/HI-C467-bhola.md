## You'll want to know: भोला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C467-KAPAT467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भोला** — *bholā* — "guileless; trusting".

> मेरा भाई बहुत भोला है, हर आदमी की बात मान लेता है।
>
> *merā bhāī bahut bholā hai, har ādmī kī bāt mān letā hai.*
>
> My brother is very trusting; he believes whatever anyone tells him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भोला** (*bholā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *bholā*, "foolish, guileless", under *\*bhulla*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9539). In Hindi it is mostly kind: simple, trusting, without tricks.
