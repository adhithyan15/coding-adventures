## You'll want to know: બરાબર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C544-TAPHAVAT544-05]; assesses=[] -->

**બરાબર** — *barābar* — "evenly matched".

> જો બંને ભાગ બરાબર હશે તો કોઈ ફરિયાદ નહીં કરે.
>
> *jo banne bhāg barābar haśe to koī phariyād nahī̃ kare.*
>
> If both parts are equal, nobody will complain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બરાબર** (*barābar*) is usually traced to Persian *barābar*, "equal, level". It is also an everyday "right, exactly": **બરાબર છે**.
