## You'll want to know: ഉത്ഭവം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C395-PURVIKAN395-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉത്ഭവം** — *utbhavaṁ* — "an origin; a source".

> ആ പുഴയുടെ ഉത്ഭവം ഒരു മലയിൽ ആണ്.
>
> *ā puḻayuṭe utbhavaṁ oru malayil āṇŭ.*
>
> The source of that river is on a hill.

Also the origin of a word or a custom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉത്ഭവം** (*utbhavaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *udbhava*, "origin, birth" (CDIAL 2039), from *ud-bhū*, "to come up, to arise".
