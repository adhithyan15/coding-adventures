Russian, chapter 454: Sales and bargains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
распродажа (rasprodázha) — a sale (clearance).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to pinch, then the Russian for to exchange.

You'll want to know: распродажа.
распродажа (rasprodázha) — "a sale (clearance)".
В январе во всех магазинах распродажа. V yanvaré vo vsekh magazínakh rasprodázha. In January there is a sale in all the shops.
Feminine. На распродаже — in the sale.

The word, taken apart.
распродажа (rasprodázha) is built from рас- 'off, all over' and продажа 'a sale', from продать 'to sell', on дать 'to give'. EtymDB lists продажа without a deeper source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
акция (áktsiya) — a special offer, a promotion; a share (in a company).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sale, then the Russian for a markdown.

You'll want to know: акция.
акция (áktsiya) — "a special offer, a promotion; a share (in a company)".
По акции второй кофе — бесплатно. Po áktsii vtoróy kófe — besplátno. On special offer, the second coffee is free.
Feminine. По акции — on special offer. Also a share on the stock market, or a protest.

The word, taken apart.
акция (áktsiya) is usually traced, probably through German or French, to Latin āctiō 'a doing, an action' (EtymDB lists the Latin word), from agere 'to drive, to do'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
торговаться (torgovátsya) — to haggle, to bargain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a markdown, then the Russian for a special offer.

You'll want to know: торговаться.
торговаться (torgovátsya) — "to haggle, to bargain".
На рынке принято торговаться. Na rýnke prínyato torgovátsya. At the market it is usual to haggle.
Imperfective; the perfective is сторговаться. Present торгуюсь, торгуешься.

The word, taken apart.
торговаться (torgovátsya) is built on торг 'trade, a market', which is inherited through Old East Slavic tŭrgŭ from Proto-Slavic tŭrgŭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
переплачивать (perepláchivat) — to overpay, to pay too much.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a special offer, then the Russian for to haggle.

You'll want to know: переплачивать.
переплачивать (perepláchivat) — "to overpay, to pay too much".
В туристских местах мы всегда переплачиваем. V turístskikh mestákh my vsegdá perepláchivayem. In touristy places we always pay too much.
Imperfective; perfective переплатить (переплачу, переплатишь). Takes за + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
переплачивать (perepláchivat) is built from пере- 'over, too much' and -плачивать, from платить 'to pay', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic platiti.
