## You'll want to know: 话题
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C354-TOPIC354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**话题** — *huàtí* — "topic of conversation".

> **我觉得这个话题很有意思** *wǒ juéde zhège huàtí hěn yǒu yìsi* — I think this is an interesting topic.

The new character, in pieces: 题 — fifteen strokes in two pieces: on the left 是 *shì*, nine strokes, its last stroke sweeping out long under the right side; on the right the page piece, six strokes: a bar, a short left-falling stroke, a narrow box, then a left-falling stroke and a dot under it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**话题** (*huàtí*) is built from 话 'talk' and 题 'a heading', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwaejH* and *dej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'speech' and the second 'the title of a book' and, quoting an older dictionary, 'the forehead'.
