Arabic, chapter 472: Rocks and peaks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صخرة (ṣakhra) — a rock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a current, then the Arabic for depth.

You'll want to know: صخرة.
صخرة — ṣakhra — "a rock".
جلسنا على صخرة كبيرة. jalasnā ʿalā ṣakhra kabīra. We sat on a big rock.
Plural: صخور, ṣukhūr.

The word, taken apart.
صخرة (ṣakhra) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-kh-r, "great hard rocks" (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
هضبة (haḍba) — a plateau; a hill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for depth, then the Arabic for a rock.

You'll want to know: هضبة.
هضبة — haḍba — "a plateau; a hill".
تقع القرية على هضبة واسعة. taqaʿu al-qarya ʿalā haḍba wāsiʿa. The village lies on a wide plateau.

The word, taken apart.
هضبة (haḍba) is built on the native Arabic root h-ḍ-b: Lane gives haḍba as a hill, or a mountain spreading over the surface of the ground, beside the root's sense of steady rain (Lane). Modern Arabic uses it for a plateau.

قمة — qimma — "a summit; a peak".
وصلنا إلى قمة (qimma) الجبل بعد الظهر. waṣalnā ilā qimmat al-jabal baʿda aẓ-ẓuhr. We reached the summit of the mountain in the afternoon.
Plural: قمم, qimam.
A meeting of heads of state is also a qimma.

The word, taken apart.
قمة (qimma) is built on the native Arabic root q-m-m, "the top of a thing" (Lane).
