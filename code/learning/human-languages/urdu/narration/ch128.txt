Urdu, chapter 128: Deer, Pigeon, Parrot, Bear, Waist.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ہرن (hiran) — a deer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fly, then the Urdu for a rabbit.

You'll want to know: ہرن.
ہرن — hiran — "a deer".

The word, taken apart.
ہرن (hiran) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from hariṇa, "deer" (CDIAL 13982). It is built on hari, "yellow".

بھالو (bhālū) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhallūka, "bear" (CDIAL 9415). CDIAL gives an Indo-European source as possible and mentions a proposed Austroasiatic one.
