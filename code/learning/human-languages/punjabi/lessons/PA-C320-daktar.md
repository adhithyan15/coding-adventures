# ਡਾਕਟਰ (ḍākṭar) — a doctor

## You'll want to know: ਡਾਕਟਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C320-CLINIC320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕਟਰ** — *ḍākṭar* — "a doctor".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੈਂ ਡਾਕਟਰ ਕੋਲ ਗਿਆ।
>
> *kall maiṁ ḍākṭar kol giā.*
>
> Yesterday I went to the doctor.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕਟਰ** (*ḍākṭar*) is borrowed from English *doctor*.
