German, chapter 355: Ein Projekt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Projekt — the project.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to present, then the German for the presentation.

You'll want to know: das Projekt.
das Projekt — "the project".
Unser Projekt muss bis Ende Mai fertig sein. Our project has to be finished by the end of May.
Plural: die Projekte. The stress is on the second syllable: pro-JEKT.

The word, taken apart.
Projekt is a learned borrowing from Latin prōiectum (EtymDB), from prōicere "to throw forth" (Lewis & Short), built on iacere "to throw": a plan thrown forward.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Auftrag — the order, the task, the commission.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the presentation, then the German for the project.

You'll want to know: der Auftrag.
der Auftrag — "the order, the task, the commission".
Die Firma hat einen großen Auftrag aus dem Ausland bekommen. The company has received a large order from abroad.
Plural: die Aufträge. Im Auftrag von — on behalf of.

The word, taken apart.
Auftrag is the noun of auftragen "to charge someone with a task", built on tragen "to carry", from Proto-Germanic draganą (EtymDB): a task laid on someone to carry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
zusammenarbeiten — to work together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the project, then the German for the order.

You'll want to know: zusammenarbeiten.
zusammenarbeiten — "to work together".
Wir arbeiten seit drei Jahren eng zusammen. We have been working closely together for three years.
Separable: wir arbeiten zusammen, wir haben zusammengearbeitet.

The word, taken apart.
zusammenarbeiten is built from zusammen "together" and arbeiten "to work", from Arbeit, Proto-Germanic arbaidiz "labour" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Zusammenarbeit — the cooperation, the teamwork.

You'll want to know: die Zusammenarbeit.
die Zusammenarbeit — "the cooperation, the teamwork".
Danke für die gute Zusammenarbeit! Thank you for the good cooperation!
No plural in everyday use. In Zusammenarbeit mit — in cooperation with.

The word, taken apart.
Zusammenarbeit joins zusammen "together" to Arbeit "work", Old High German arbeit, Proto-Germanic arbaidiz (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
organisieren — to organize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to work together, then the German for the cooperation.

You'll want to know: organisieren.
organisieren — "to organize".
Meine Kollegin organisiert das Fest für die ganze Firma. My colleague is organizing the party for the whole firm.
Perfekt: hat organisiert, with no ge-. Sich organisieren — to get organized.

The word, taken apart.
organisieren was borrowed from French organiser (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin organizāre, built on Latin organum "instrument, tool", from Greek órganon.
