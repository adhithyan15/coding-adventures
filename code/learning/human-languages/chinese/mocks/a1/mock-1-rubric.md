# Chinese A1 project mock — form 1 scoring rubric

This original rubric applies only to form 1, not to an official HSK exam.
Assess Standard Mandarin and simplified characters at a gentle A1 floor.
Administer reading for 17 minutes, listening for 15 minutes, writing for 15
minutes, and individual speaking for 8 minutes after 2 separate preparation
minutes. Two plays per listening item.
No dictionary, pinyin aid, translator, answer bank, or copyable model.
Score the four skills separately out of 100; **60/100 in each skill** is needed.
Do not round up a deficient skill from an overall average.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-form-fields -->
Items 1–10: 5 points for each keyed answer, 0 otherwise. No partial credit.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-form-fields -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->
Items 11–20: 5 points for each keyed answer, 0 otherwise. The source message,
not the English distractors, is the assessed reading stimulus.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->

## Listening — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-familiar-cues -->
Items 21–30: 5 points for each keyed answer, 0 otherwise. Confirm that each
recorded cue lasts 2–10 seconds and is played twice at a consistent pace.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-familiar-cues -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->
Items 31–40: 5 points for each keyed answer, 0 otherwise. Confirm each
recorded exchange lasts 4–18 seconds and is played twice.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->

## Writing — 100 points

Writing must be independent production. Never present the examiner's examples
as a model. Accept comprehensible A1 variants, personal names in their usual
script, and minor character slips that do not change meaning. Do not silently
turn copied English labels or pinyin-only answers into Chinese production.

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
Item 41, five fields × 8 = 40 points. For each field, award 2 each for
(1) relevant content, (2) comprehensible and appropriately organised response,
(3) A1 vocabulary/grammar/register, and (4) readable characters and punctuation
or an ordinary personal name. A blank or English-only field earns 0 for that
field. A truthful non-student answer is as valid as a student one.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-short-message -->
Item 42, 60 points, four equally weighted dimensions of 15 each: (1) task
fulfilment and the four requested moves, (2) comprehensible, organised
2–4-clause connection, (3) A1 vocabulary/grammar/register, and (4) legible
characters and punctuation. Do not require one wording. A fragment copied
from item 41 without an independently formed message cannot earn connection
or task-move points it does not evidence.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-short-message -->

## Speaking — 100 points

Record independently; one slow repeat of a prompt is allowed without a
penalty. Accent variation is acceptable if meaning remains clear. Score what
the learner produces, not an examiner-supplied phrase or a rehearsed script.

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->
Items 43–47, 8 points each = 40. For each, award 2 each for (1) on-task
content, (2) comprehensible interaction and reciprocal move where asked,
(3) A1 vocabulary/grammar/register, and (4) tones, pronunciation and fluency.
Responses target 2–15 seconds. A silent response is 0.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->
Item 48, 60 points, four equally weighted dimensions of 15 each: (1) task
content—doorway question, response, and distinct exit follow-up; (2)
comprehensible connected turn-taking; (3) A1 vocabulary/grammar/register;
and (4) tones, pronunciation and fluency. Target 20–45 seconds. A monologue
with no exchange cannot earn turn-taking points. Do not award examiner-provided
language to the learner.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->

Two trained raters independently score writing and speaking and retain item
marks and reasons. If a skill total differs by more than 2 points, or one
rater would pass and the other fail, a third rater adjudicates before reporting.
Qualified independent Chinese-language review is required before any
mock-ready claim. Even both mocks passed do not establish learner-proven
readiness without the preregistered book-only pilot required by the assessment
specification. This rubric cannot establish official HSK equivalence.
