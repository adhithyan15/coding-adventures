# मीरा अरुण से लंबी है (Mīrā Aruṇ se lambī hai) — comparing two, and choosing the most

## You'll want to know: more and less
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C285-MORE-LESS-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C92-DEG-03, HI-LEX-C64-MEASURE-05, HI-LEX-C64-MEASURE-04, HI-LEX-C72-COST-04, HI-LEX-C82-BODY-01, HI-CONCEPT-C36-KAMRA-01, HI-LEX-C136-THINGS136-02, HI-LEX-C72-COST-01, HI-LEX-C84-POSS-05] -->

> **मीरा अरुण से लंबी है।**
>
> *Mīrā Aruṇ se lambī hai.* — "Mira is taller than Arun."

You know the frame: what is measured against takes **से**, and the adjective agrees with the one described, **लंबी** for Mira. Two words sharpen it:

- **से ज़्यादा** (*se zyādā*), "more than": **यह कमरा उस कमरे से ज़्यादा बड़ा है।** (*yah kamrā us kamre se zyādā baṛā hai.*) — This room is bigger than that one.
- **से कम** (*se kam*), "less than": **यह साड़ी उससे कम महँगी है।** (*yah sāṛī usse kam mahãgī hai.*) — This sari is less expensive than that one.

They work with nouns too: **मेरे पास तुमसे ज़्यादा पैसे हैं** (*mere pās tumse zyādā paise haiṁ*), "I have more money than you".

## You'll want to know: the most, in a group
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C285-SUPERLATIVE-GROUP-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C285-SABSE-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-RAHNA-01, HI-LEX-C45-ASK-01, HI-LEX-C139-THINGS139-03, HI-CONCEPT-C35-PASAND-01, HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-03, HI-LEX-C56-ROAD-03] -->

> **मुझे फलों में आम सबसे ज़्यादा पसंद है।**
>
> *mujhe phaloṁ meṁ ām sabse zyādā pasand hai.* — "Of all fruits I like mangoes the most."

**सबसे** picks one from a group, and the group takes **में**: **फलों में**, **इस शहर में**. With a liking, "the most" is **सबसे ज़्यादा**. A place can be the group too, with **का**:

- **यह शहर का सबसे बड़ा बाज़ार है।** (*yah śahar kā sabse baṛā bāzār hai.*) — This is the biggest market in the city.
