## You'll want to know: цитировать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C284-INTERVYU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**цитировать** (*tsitírovat*) — "to quote".

> Учитель любил цитировать стихи.
>
> *Uchítel lyubíl tsitírovat stikhí.*
>
> The teacher liked to quote poems.

Imperfective; perfective *процитировать* (*protsitírovat*). *Цитата* — a quotation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*цитировать* was borrowed from German *zitieren* with the Russian ending *-овать* (EtymDB); the German verb comes from Latin *citāre* "to set in motion, to summon" (Lewis & Short).
