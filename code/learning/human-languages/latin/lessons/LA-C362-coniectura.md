## You'll want to know: coniectūra, coniectūrae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C362-REFELLO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**coniectūra, coniectūrae** — "a guess, a conjecture; an inference".

> Hoc nōn sciō, sed coniectūrā tantum dīcō.
>
> *I do not know this; I say it only by guesswork.*

*coniectūra, coniectūrae* f. Built on *coniciō*, to throw together: pieces thrown together to make a guess. English *conjecture*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coniectūra* is built on *coniciō* "to throw together, to infer" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *con-* and *iaciō* "to throw". English *conjecture* comes from it.
