## You'll want to know: طابور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C450-BALADIYYA450-04]; assesses=[] -->

**طابور** — *ṭābūr* — "a line of people; a queue".

> وقفنا في طابور طويل أمام القنصلية.
>
> *waqafnā fī ṭābūr ṭawīl amāma al-qunṣuliyya.*
>
> We stood in a long queue in front of the consulate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طابور** (*ṭābūr*) was borrowed from Ottoman Turkish (EtymDB), where *tabur* names a battalion, a line of soldiers.
