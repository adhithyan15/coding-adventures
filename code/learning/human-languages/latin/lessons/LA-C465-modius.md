## You'll want to know: modius, modiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C465-MODIUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**modius, modiī** — "a peck (a measure of grain)".

> Agricola trēs modiōs frūmentī vendit.
>
> *The farmer sells three pecks of grain.*

Second declension, masculine: *modius, modiī m.*, genitive plural often *modium*. About nine litres, the standard grain measure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modius* is built on *modus* 'measure' with the suffix *-ius* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
