## You'll want to know: l'istante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C277-DURATA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'istante** — "the instant".

> In un istante la stanza era vuota.
>
> *In an instant the room was empty.*

*All'istante* means ‘at once, immediately’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*istante* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnstāns* "pressing, present", from *īnstāre* "to stand upon, to press" (EtymDB): the moment that presses on you now.
