## You'll want to know: ದರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C435-BERTH435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ದರ** — *dara* — "a fare; a rate".

> ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಬಸ್ ದರ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಯಿತು.
>
> *ī varṣa bas dara heccāyitu.*
>
> This year the bus fare went up.

ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ದರ is a fare; ಬಡ್ಡಿ ದರ is a rate of interest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದರ** (*dara*) is usually traced to Hindustani *dar*, "rate, price"; its earlier source is not settled.
