Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 163: Plans and Perhaps.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परसूं हूं बाजार जासूं — a plan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I will go to the market tomorrow. Then the day after tomorrow: परसूं.

Grammar Lens: the time first.
A plan opens on its time, then says what you will do:
परसूं हूं बाजार जासूं। parsū̃ hū̃ bājār jāsū̃. — The day after tomorrow I'll go to the market.
आज आथण हूं घरै आसूं। āj āthaṇ hū̃ gharai āsū̃. — This evening I'll come home.

Guided Practice.
Two plans join with अर:
काल हूं दिनूगै उठसूं अर बाजार जासूं। kāl hū̃ dinūgai uṭhsū̃ ar bājār jāsū̃. — Tomorrow I'll get up early and go to the market.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: परसूं हूं बाजार जासूं। — parsū̃ hū̃ bājār jāsū̃. — The day after tomorrow I'll go to the market.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: आज आथण हूं घरै आसूं। — āj āthaṇ hū̃ gharai āsū̃. — This evening I'll come home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Where does the time go in a plan? (First.) What does the verb take? (-सूं.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Say your plan: the day after tomorrow you'll go to the market.


Lesson 2 of 5.
सायद (sāyad) — perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say now, today and every day. (अबार, आज, रोज.)

You'll want to know: सायद.
सायद — sāyad — "perhaps".
सायद हूं काल आसूं। sāyad hū̃ kāl āsū̃. — Perhaps I'll come tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
सायद (sāyad) is a loan, usually traced to Persian shāyad, "it may be". Marwari says s where the Persian has sh, as it does in हमेसा.

What you've built.
सायद opens the sentence and leaves the rest as it was: सायद परसूं हूं बाजार जासूं। (sāyad parsū̃ hū̃ bājār jāsū̃.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sāyad]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: सायद हूं काल कोनी आसूं। — sāyad hū̃ kāl konī āsū̃. — Perhaps I won't come tomorrow.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say now, then sāyad again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सायद (sāyad) mean? ("Perhaps".) Say it once more.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Marwadi for 'perhaps'.


Lesson 3 of 5.
फेर (pher) — again.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say perhaps I'll come tomorrow. Then later, पछै.

You'll want to know: फेर.
फेर — pher — "again".
हूं काल फेर आसूं। hū̃ kāl pher āsū̃. — I'll come again tomorrow.
It opens on a letter you have not written yet: ph, a प with a breath after it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
फेर (pher) is built on the old verb for turning. CDIAL gives Hindi and Gujarati pher, "again", under a reconstructed phirati, "turns", and lists Old Marwari phiraï, "turns", in the same entry (CDIAL 9078).

What you've built.
फेर (pher) sits right before the verb: फेर (pher) आसूं, फेर (pher) जासूं.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: pher]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: राम फेर आयो। — rām pher āyo. — Ram came again.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say later, then pher again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does फेर (pher) mean? ("Again".) Say it once more.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Marwadi for 'again'.


Lesson 4 of 5.
फ (pha) — the letter that opens फेर (pher).

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script you'll notice: फ, Guided Practice and Wrap-up Recall.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write प once, and name it.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say फेर (pher), "again". It opens on a प with a breath after it: ph. This lesson puts that letter in your hand.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script you'll notice: फ.
फ — pha, the breathed p.
It opens फेर (pher). It stands beside प as ध stands beside द. Its left part is a stem and a lower bowl joined to a central stem, much like प; then comes a separate arch on the right, and the headline.

Writing: फ.
1. descend the left stem, curve right around the lower bowl, rise along the central side to the headline, then retrace down the central stem without lifting.
2. lift at the central junction and sweep clockwise over and down through the open right arch.
3. lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right.
Pen lifts: 2.
verified three-stroke teaching form; another learner source stages the components differently.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: JackPotte, ‘Devanagari pʰ फ (pha).gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 29 March 2009.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — look: at फेर (pher) and point at फ (pha) at its start]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: फ (pha) three times, saying ph as you finish each one]
[once you have stopped driving — write: प, then फ (pha), from memory]
[once you have stopped driving — read: फेर (pher) aloud once more]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — फ? (pha, the breathed p.) Which word did it come from? (फेर, pher, "again".)
[once you have stopped driving — question: Write the letter that opens pher (again).]


Lesson 5 of 5.
Plans and perhaps — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the day after tomorrow I'll go to the market.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tomorrow I'll get up early and go to the market]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: perhaps I'll come tomorrow; perhaps I won't come tomorrow]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Perhaps? (सायद (sāyad).) Again? (फेर (pher).) I'll come? (आसूं.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Say: perhaps I'll come again tomorrow.
