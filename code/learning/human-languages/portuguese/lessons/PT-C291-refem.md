## You'll want to know: o refém
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C291-RAPTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o refém** — "the hostage".

> O refém voltou para casa ao fim de dois dias.
>
> *The hostage went back home after two days.*

The same form for a woman: *a refém*. Plural *reféns*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*refém* comes from Arabic *rahīn* "a pledge, a hostage" (EtymDB).
