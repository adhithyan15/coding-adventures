**die Burg** — "a castle".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**Burg** ← Old High German *burg* ← Proto-Germanic *\*burgz*, "stronghold" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) English *borough* and the *-bury* of place names are the same word.
