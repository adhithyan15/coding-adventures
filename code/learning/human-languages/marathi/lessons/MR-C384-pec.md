## You'll want to know: पेच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C384-PARISTHITI384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पेच** — *pec* — "a tight spot; a dilemma".

> कोणता पर्याय निवडावा, हा मोठा पेच होता.
>
> *koṇtā paryāy nivḍāvā, hā moṭhā pec hotā.*
>
> Which option to choose was a real dilemma.

Masculine.

पेचात पडणे, to land in a fix; also a screw thread, a twist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेच** (*pec*) is a loan from Persian *pech*, "a twist, a coil": a problem that turns back on itself.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
