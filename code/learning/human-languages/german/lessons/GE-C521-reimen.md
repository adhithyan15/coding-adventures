## You'll want to know: reimen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C521-GEDICHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**reimen** — "to rhyme".

> Herz reimt sich auf Schmerz.
>
> *Herz rhymes with Schmerz.*

Mostly reflexive: *sich reimen auf* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reimen* is inherited from Middle High German *rīmen* "to compose, to put into verse, to rhyme" (EtymDB).
