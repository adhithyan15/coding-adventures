## You'll want to know: रेल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C218-RAIL218-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रेल** — *rel* — "a train".

> हूं काल रेल मांय बैठसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl rel mā̃y baiṭhsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll take the train.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेल** (*rel*) is borrowed from English *rail*; Marwadi uses it for the train itself.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what comes next.
