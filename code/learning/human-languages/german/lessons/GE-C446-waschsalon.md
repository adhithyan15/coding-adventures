## You'll want to know: der Waschsalon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C446-REINIGUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Waschsalon** — "the launderette (US: laundromat)".

> Im Waschsalon liest sie ein Buch, während die Maschine läuft.
>
> *In the launderette she reads a book while the machine runs.*

Plural: *die Waschsalons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Waschsalon* is built from *waschen* 'to wash', inherited from Proto-Germanic *waskaną*, and *Salon* 'room', borrowed from French *salon*, which came from Italian *salone* 'large hall' (EtymDB).
