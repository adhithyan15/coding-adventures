Marathi, chapter 555: Time Pressure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अवधी (avadhī) — a span of time; time left.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for mostly, then the Marathi for roughly.

You'll want to know: अवधी.
अवधी — avadhī — "a span of time; time left".
परीक्षेला आता फक्त एक आठवडा अवधी आहे. parīkṣelā ātā phakta ek āṭhavḍā avadhī āhe. There is only a week left before the exam.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अवधी (avadhī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit avadhi, "a limit, a term" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
दिरंगाई (diraṅgāī) — a needless delay; dilatoriness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for roughly, then the Marathi for a span of time.

You'll want to know: दिरंगाई.
दिरंगाई — diraṅgāī — "a needless delay; dilatoriness".
कामात दिरंगाई केली तर दंड भरावा लागतो. kāmāt diraṅgāī kelī tar daṇḍ bharāvā lāgto. If you put off the work, you have to pay a fine.
Feminine.
दिरंगाई (diraṅgāī) करणे, to drag one's feet; दिरंगाई (diraṅgāī) होणे, a delay happens.

The word, taken apart.
दिरंगाई (diraṅgāī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian dirang, "delay, tarrying", with the Marathi noun ending -आई; the path into Marathi is not recorded in our sources.

Lesson 3 of 5.
तातडी (tātḍī) — urgency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a span of time, then the Marathi for a needless delay.

You'll want to know: तातडी.
तातडी — tātḍī — "urgency".
कामाची तातडी होती, म्हणून तो रविवारीही कार्यालयात गेला. kāmācī tātḍī hotī, mhaṇūn to ravivārīhī kāryālayāt gelā. The work was urgent, so he went to the office even on Sunday.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तातडी (tātḍī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi tā̃tḍī, "urgency, flurry", under Sanskrit tānti, "suffocation" (CDIAL 5764).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Marathi for a needless delay, then the Marathi for urgency.

You'll want to know: धांदल.
धांदल — dhāndal — "a rush; a flurry".
सकाळी शाळेच्या वेळी घरात खूप धांदल असते. sakāḷī śāḷecyā veḷī gharāt khūp dhāndal aste. In the morning at school time the house is in a great rush.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
धांदल (dhāndal) is a Marathi word for a busy rush; its origin is not settled.
