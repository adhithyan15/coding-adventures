## You'll want to know: کینسر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C475-LONG475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کینسر** — *kainsar* — "cancer".

> کینسر ایک خطرناک بیماری ہے۔
>
> *kainsar ek khatarnāk bīmārī hai.*
>
> Cancer is a dangerous illness.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کینسر** (*kainsar*) is borrowed from English *cancer*.
