## You'll want to know: a charcutaria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C450-MERCEARIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a charcutaria** — "the deli (cured meats and cheese)".

> Na charcutaria compro presunto e chouriço.
>
> *At the deli I buy cured ham and chouriço.*

Also the deli counter in a supermarket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*charcutaria* was borrowed from French *charcuterie*, from *chair cuite* 'cooked flesh'; *chair* is inherited from Latin *carō* 'flesh' (Lewis & Short).
