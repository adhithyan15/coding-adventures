Lesson 1 of 5.
банк (bank) — a bank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: банк.
банк (bank) — "a bank".

The word, taken apart.
банк — recorded from 1707; it came through German Bank from Italian banco, "a money-changer's table", then "a bank" (Vasmer).

Lesson 2 of 5.
вокзал (vokzál) — a railway station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a school, then the Russian for a bank.

You'll want to know: вокзал.
вокзал (vokzál) — "a railway station". From Vauxhall, in London.

The word, taken apart.
вокзал (vokzál) — first фоксал, in 1777, a pleasure hall; Vasmer takes it from English Vauxhall, a London pleasure garden. The station at Pavlovsk had such a hall with concerts, and the name passed to stations. Trubachev's note gives another account of the English name.

музей (muzéy) — "a museum".

The word, taken apart.
музей (muzéy) came from Polish muzeum or German Museum, from Latin mūsēum, Greek mouseîon, "a place of the Muses, a school with a library" (Vasmer).
