## You'll want to know: കാലഹരണപ്പെടുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C488-REKHA488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**കാലഹരണപ്പെടുക** — *kālaharaṇappeṭuka* — "to expire; to become outdated".

> എന്റെ ലൈസൻസ് കഴിഞ്ഞ മാസം കാലഹരണപ്പെട്ടു.
>
> *enṟe laisansŭ kaḻiñña māsaṁ kālaharaṇappeṭṭu.*
>
> My licence expired last month.

Past: കാലഹരണപ്പെട്ടു, *kālaharaṇappeṭṭu*.

A formal verb for papers and medicines; in speech people say the date has passed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കാലഹരണപ്പെടുക** (*kālaharaṇappeṭuka*) is a Malayalam verb built from Sanskrit *kāla*, "time", and *haraṇa*, "taking away" (Monier-Williams), with -പ്പെടുക, the ending of verbs that something undergoes.
