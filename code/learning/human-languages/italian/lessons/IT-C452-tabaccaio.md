## You'll want to know: il tabaccaio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C452-EDICOLA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il tabaccaio** — "the tobacconist".

> Il tabaccaio mi dà il resto.
>
> *The tobacconist gives me my change.*

The person; the shop is *la tabaccheria*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tabaccaio* is built on *tabacco* 'tobacco', which was borrowed from Spanish *tabaco*, a word of Caribbean origin.
