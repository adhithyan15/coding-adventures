## You'll want to know: pellis, pellis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C517-VENA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pellis, pellis** — "a hide, skin".

> Pāstor pellem ovis vendit.
>
> *The shepherd sells the sheep's hide.*

Feminine, third declension. Mostly an animal's hide; *cutis* is skin as part of the body.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pellis** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to cover, skin" (EtymDB), and Old English *fell* "hide" is a cousin. English *pellicle*, a thin skin, comes from its diminutive.
