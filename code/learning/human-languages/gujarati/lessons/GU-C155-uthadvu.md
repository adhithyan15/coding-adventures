## You'll want to know: ઉઠાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C155-READY155-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉઠાડવું** — *uṭhāḍvũ* — "to wake someone, to get someone up".

> **માતા મને સવારે ઉઠાડે છે.** — *mātā mane savāre uṭhāḍe che* — "Mother wakes me in the morning."

Built on **ઉઠવું**, *uṭhvũ*, to get up: this verb is to get someone else up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉઠાડવું** (*uṭhāḍvũ*) is built on the inherited *uṭhvũ*, "to get up", with the causative *-āḍ-*. CDIAL traces *uṭhvũ* to *\*utsthāti*, "stands up", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 1900); it lists no Gujarati form of the causative itself.
