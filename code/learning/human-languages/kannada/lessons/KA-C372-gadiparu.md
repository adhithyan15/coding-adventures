# ಗಡೀಪಾರು (gaḍīpāru) — banishment; deportation

## You'll want to know: ಗಡೀಪಾರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C372-VANAVASA372-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಡೀಪಾರು** — *gaḍīpāru* — "banishment; deportation".

> ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ ಆ ರೌಡಿಗೆ ಗಡೀಪಾರು ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ ವಿಧಿಸಿತು.
>
> *nyāyālaya ā rauḍige gaḍīpāru śikṣe vidhisitu.*
>
> The court sentenced that thug to banishment.

ಗಡೀಪಾರು ಮಾಡು, to banish someone from a district or a country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಡೀಪಾರು** (*gaḍīpāru*) is usually traced to ಗಡಿ, "a border, a limit" (DEDR 1109), and ಪಾರು, "across, beyond", from Sanskrit *pāra*, "the further bank" (CDIAL 8100): sent beyond the border.
