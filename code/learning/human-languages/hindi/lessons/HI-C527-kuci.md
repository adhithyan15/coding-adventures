## You'll want to know: कूची
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C527-KALA527-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कूची** — *kūcī* — "a paintbrush".

> चित्रकार ने कूची से आसमान नीला किया।
>
> *Citrakār ne kūcī se āsmān nīlā kiyā.*
>
> The painter made the sky blue with a brush.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कूची** (*kūcī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *kūrca*, "a bunch, a brush" (CDIAL 3408).
