## You'll want to know: গুপ্তধন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C326-ASHCHORJO326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**গুপ্তধন** — *guptôdhôn* — "a hidden treasure".

> গল্পে নায়ক একটা পুরনো কুয়োর নিচে গুপ্তধন পেল।
>
> *gôlpe nāyôk ekṭā purôno kuyor niche guptôdhôn pelô.*
>
> In the story the hero found hidden treasure at the bottom of an old well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গুপ্তধন** (*guptôdhôn*) joins Sanskrit *gupta*, 'guarded; hidden' (Monier-Williams), to *dhana*, 'wealth': hidden wealth.
