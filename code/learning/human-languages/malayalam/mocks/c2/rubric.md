# Malayalam C2 mock rubric

Each skill is a separate 100-point paper. Every paper must independently reach 60/100,
and the overall score must reach 240/400. A stronger paper cannot compensate for a
failed paper. Every section must be attempted. This is a project-defined assessment,
not an external qualification.

## Reading and listening

Each paper contains four equally weighted parts. Convert raw item totals to 100 points:
reading has 40 one-point items (raw score multiplied by 2.5), and listening has 32
one-point items (raw score multiplied by 3.125). Award credit only for the keyed choice.
At C2, distractors may be plausible at proposition level; the key depends on voice,
qualification, pragmatic force, intertextual relation, framing, or rhetorical effect.

## Writing

The 220–280 word three-source synthesis is worth 40 points: accurate multi-source
synthesis 10, selection and audience reframing 8, organisation and cohesion 6,
genre/register control 6, Malayalam vocabulary and grammar 7, and orthographic
control 3. The 420–520
word contrasting extended response is worth 60 points: conceptual precision and
task fulfilment 18, architecture and cohesion 10, genre/register mastery 10,
rhetorical flexibility 7, Malayalam vocabulary and grammar 10, and orthographic
control 5. A C2 answer distinguishes claims from warrants, preserves material
uncertainty while reframing it, integrates a serious counter-position, and can shift
register without flattening meaning. Either traditional or reformed Malayalam orthography
earns full credit when used consistently. Romanized, translated,
non-Malayalam-script, or visibly copied answers earn zero independent-writing credit.

## Speaking

The synthesis/mediation presentation is worth 35 points, multi-party negotiation 30,
and defence/reformulation 35. Within each part reward source mediation, conceptual
precision, interaction and repair, audience/register mastery, lexical and grammatical
range, pronunciation, and fluency. A high C2 performance tracks layered positions,
names the cost of its own proposal, responds to irony or implicit resistance, and
performs a genuine second-audience reformulation rather than merely simplifying words.
No written answer script is allowed; fifteen-minute preparation notes may contain
keywords and relationship diagrams, not sentences.

## Readiness boundary

The mock is executable but not self-validating. A readiness claim additionally
requires clean timed administration and qualified human review of the paper,
recordings, productive responses, regional-voice documentation, and scoring decisions.
