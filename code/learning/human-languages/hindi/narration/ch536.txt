Hindi, chapter 536: Making a Plan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
योजना (yojnā) — a plan; a scheme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a medal, then the Hindi for to make run.

You'll want to know: योजना.
योजना — yojnā — "a plan; a scheme".
हमने गर्मी की छुट्टी के लिए एक योजना बनाई। Hamne garmī kī chuṭṭī ke liye ek yojnā banāī. We made a plan for the summer holiday.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
योजना (yojnā) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit yojanā, "use, arrangement, preparation" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for a plan.

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.
रूपरेखा (rūprekhā) — an outline; a rough plan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a decision (formal), then the Hindi for an arrangement, a system.

You'll want to know: रूपरेखा.
रूपरेखा — rūprekhā — "an outline; a rough plan".
अरुण ने योजना की एक रूपरेखा बनाई। Aruṇ ne yojnā kī ek rūprekhā banāī. Arun drew up an outline of the plan.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
रूपरेखा (rūprekhā) is a modern compound of two Sanskrit words, rūpa, "outward appearance, form", and rekhā, "a line" (Monier-Williams): the lines that give a plan its shape.
