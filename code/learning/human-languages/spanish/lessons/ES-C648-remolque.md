## You'll want to know: el remolque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C648-APARCAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el remolque** — "the trailer; towing".

> Llevamos el barco en un remolque.
>
> *We are taking the boat on a trailer.*

For a boat, a horse or a small caravan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remolque* is built on *remolcar* "to tow", from a Vulgar Latin verb (EtymDB) built on Latin *remulcum* "a tow-rope" (Lewis & Short).
