Arabic, chapter 538: Needle and thread.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تطريز (taṭrīz) — embroidery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for pottery, then the Arabic for a crossword.

You'll want to know: تطريز.
تطريز — taṭrīz — "embroidery".
تعلمت جدتي التطريز في طفولتها. taʿallamat jaddatī at-taṭrīz fī ṭufūlatihā. My grandmother learned embroidery in her childhood.

The word, taken apart.
تطريز (taṭrīz) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-r-z, "to embroider, to figure a cloth" (Lane). Taṭrīz is the verbal noun of Form II.

تحب خالتي الحياكة في الشتاء. tuḥibbu khālatī al-ḥiyāka fī ash-shitāʾ. My aunt loves knitting in the winter.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خاط (khāṭa) — to sew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for knitting, then the Arabic for to knit.

You'll want to know: خاط.
خاط — khāṭa — "to sew".
خاط الخياط زرا على قميصي. khāṭa al-khayyāṭ zirran ʿalā qamīṣī. The tailor sewed a button on my shirt.
Present: يخيط, yakhīṭ.

The word, taken apart.
خاط (khāṭa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-y-ṭ, "to sew" (Lane). Khayṭ, a thread, and khayyāṭ, a tailor, which you know, share the root.
