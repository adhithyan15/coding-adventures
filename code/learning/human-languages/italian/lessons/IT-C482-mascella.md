## You'll want to know: la mascella
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C482-VISO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la mascella** — "jaw".

> Quando è nervoso stringe la mascella.
>
> *When he is nervous he clenches his jaw.*

The lower jaw is also *la mandibola*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mascella* is inherited from Latin *maxilla* "jaw" (EtymDB), a little form of *māla* "cheekbone" (Lewis & Short).
