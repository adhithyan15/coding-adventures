## You'll want to know: مودب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C489-MANNERS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مودب** (*mo'addab*) — "polite, well-mannered".

> بچه های آنها خیلی مودب هستند.
>
> *bachchehâ-ye ânhâ kheyli mo'addab hastand.*
>
> Their children are very polite.

Built on *adab* 'good manners'. The opposite is *biadab*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mo'addab* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *muʾaddab*, 'well-bred', a word built on *adab*, 'manners'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
