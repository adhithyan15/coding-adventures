# ભાડું (bhāḍũ) — rent

## You'll want to know: ભાડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C152-READ152-03]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C148-YUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-OBJECT-AGREE-01, GU-SCRIPT-C147-DDA-01] -->

**ભાડું** (*bhāḍũ*) — "rent, a fare".

It is the money paid for using something that is not yours: a room, a house,
a seat on a bus.

> **ભાડું કેટલું છે?** — *bhāḍũ keṭlũ che?* — How much is the rent? How much is the fare?
> **મેં ભાડું આપ્યું.** — *mẽ bhāḍũ āpyũ.* — I paid the rent.

**ભાડું** is neuter, so **આપ્યું**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાડું** (*bhāḍũ*) is inherited. CDIAL lists the Gujarati word under a
reconstructed *bhārta*, "pertaining to servants", which Middle Indo-Aryan
continued as *bhāṭa*, "wages" (CDIAL 9468). Pay for a servant's work became pay
for a room.
