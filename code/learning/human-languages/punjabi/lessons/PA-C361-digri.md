## You'll want to know: ਡਿਗਰੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C361-COLLEGE361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਿਗਰੀ** — *ḍigrī* — "a degree".

> ਅਮਨ ਕੋਲ ਵਿਗਿਆਨ ਦੀ ਡਿਗਰੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *Aman kol vigiān dī ḍigrī hai.*
>
> Aman has a degree in science.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਿਗਰੀ** (*ḍigrī*) is borrowed from English *degree*.
