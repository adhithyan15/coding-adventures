## You'll want to know: el mito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C564-HEROE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el mito** — "the myth".

> Es un mito que los toros odian el rojo.
>
> *It is a myth that bulls hate red.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mito* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *mythos*, taken from Greek *mŷthos* 'a word, a story' (EtymDB).
