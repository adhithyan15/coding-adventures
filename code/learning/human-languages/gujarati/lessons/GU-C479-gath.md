## You'll want to know: ગાંઠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C479-MANDU479-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાંઠ** — *gā̃ṭh* — "a lump; a knot".

> દાક્તરે ગળામાં નાની ગાંઠ જોઈ.
>
> *dāktare gaḷāmā̃ nānī gā̃ṭh joī.*
>
> The doctor found a small lump in the throat.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાંઠ** (*gā̃ṭh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *granthi*, "knot, bunch, protuberance"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "knot" (CDIAL 4354).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
