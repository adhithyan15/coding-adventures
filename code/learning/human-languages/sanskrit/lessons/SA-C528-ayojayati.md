## You'll want to know: आयोजयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C528-AYOJAYATI528-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आयोजयति** — *āyojayati* — "organises; arranges".

> यदि भवान् इच्छति, तर्हि अहं सभाम् आयोजयामि।
>
> *yadi bhavān icchati, tarhi ahaṃ sabhām āyojayāmi.*
>
> If you wish, I will organise a meeting.

Past (he …): सः उत्सवम् आयोजितवान्, *saḥ utsavam āyojitavān*.

Future: आयोजयिष्यति, *āyojayiṣyati*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आयोजयति** (*āyojayati*) is built on *ā* and the causative of the root √yuj, "to join", which also gives *yojayati*; MW gives *āyojayati* as to join together, to form; "to organise" is its modern use (MW).
