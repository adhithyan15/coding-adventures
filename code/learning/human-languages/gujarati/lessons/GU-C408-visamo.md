## You'll want to know: વિસામો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C408-ROUNDS408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વિસામો** — *visāmo* — "a rest on the way; a resting place".

> ચઢાણ પછી અમે મંદિર પાસે વિસામો લીધો.
>
> *caḍhāṇ pachī ame mandir pāse visāmo līdho.*
>
> After the climb we stopped for a rest by the temple.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિસામો** (*visāmo*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *viśrāma*, "rest", and so "a resting place"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 11956).
