Bengali, chapter 525: Making Music.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সুর (sur) — a tune; a melody.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to carve, then the Bengali for to dye.

You'll want to know: সুর.
সুর — sur — "a tune; a melody".
এই গানের সুর সুন্দর। Ei gāner sur sundôr. This song has a lovely tune.

The word, taken apart.
সুর (sur) goes back to Sanskrit svara, "sound, a note" (Monier-Williams), worn down in everyday speech.

তবলা গানের তাল রাখে। Tôblā gāner tāl rākhe. The tabla keeps the rhythm of the song.

তাল (tāl) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali tāl, "clapping", under Sanskrit tāḍa, where tāla means a clap and musical time (CDIAL 5748).

Lesson 4 of 5.
গমগম করা (gômgôm kôrā) — to resound; to boom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for rhythm, then the Bengali for musical notation.

You'll want to know: গমগম করা.
গমগম করা — gômgôm kôrā — "to resound; to boom".
গানের সময় পুরো ঘরটা গমগম করছিল। Gāner sômôy puro ghôrṭā gômgôm kôrchhilô. During the song the whole room was resounding.
Past (he/she …): সেই ঘর গানে গমগম করল, sei ghôr gāne gômgôm kôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
গমগম করা (gômgôm kôrā) joins গমগম, an imitative word for a deep, filling sound, to করা, "to do". No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
শিস দেওয়া (shis deoyā) — to whistle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for musical notation, then the Bengali for to resound.

You'll want to know: শিস দেওয়া.
শিস দেওয়া — shis deoyā — "to whistle".
ছেলেটা শিস দিয়ে একটা গান গাইল। Chheleṭā shis diye ekṭā gān gāilô. The boy whistled a song.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শিস দিলেন, tini shis dilen.

The word, taken apart.
শিস দেওয়া (shis deoyā) joins শিস, probably an imitative word for a whistle, to দেওয়া, "to give". No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
