## You'll want to know: inicialmente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C269-PRIMEIRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**inicialmente** — "initially, at the start".

> Inicialmente, ninguém acreditou nele.
>
> *Initially, nobody believed him.*

Tells what was true at the start, before things changed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inicialmente* is built on *inicial*, a learned borrowing from Latin *initiālis* "of the beginning", from *initium* "a beginning" (Lewis & Short).
