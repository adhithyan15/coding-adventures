You'll want to know: lo scontrino.
lo scontrino — "a till receipt".

The word, taken apart.
scontrino — built from scontrare, "to check against", with -ino, "little": a slip to check a sale against. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la moneta — a coin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a till receipt, then the Italian for change.

You'll want to know: la moneta.
la moneta — "a coin".

The word, taken apart.
moneta from Latin moneta, "a mint, coin" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) Rome's mint stood by the temple of Juno Moneta, and the coins took her name.

The word, taken apart.
banconota is banco, "a bank", plus nota, "a note", after English banknote. nota from Latin nota, "a mark". (EtymDB 2.1).
