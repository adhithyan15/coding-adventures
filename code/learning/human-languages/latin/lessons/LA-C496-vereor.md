## You'll want to know: vereor, verērī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C496-PAV-02]; assesses=[] -->

**vereor, verērī** — "to respect; to fear".

> Discipulī magistrum verentur.
>
> *The pupils respect their teacher.*

Deponent: *vereor, verērī, veritus sum*. Fear mixed with respect. *Vereor nē…* — I am afraid that…

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vereor** is inherited through Proto-Italic from a Proto-Indo-European root *wer-* (EtymDB), and Lewis & Short compares Greek *horao* "to see" and English *ward*. English *revere* comes from *revereor*.
