## You'll want to know: rogātiō, rogātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C410-ROGATIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rogātiō, rogātiōnis** — "a bill (put to the people); a request".

> Tribūnus rogātiōnem ad populum tulit.
>
> *The tribune put a bill to the people.*

*rogātiō, rogātiōnis* f., built on *rogō*, to ask: the magistrate asked the people whether they wished it. If they voted yes, the bill became a *lēx*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rogātiō* is the noun built on *rogō* "to ask" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the question put to the people. English *rogation* comes from it.
