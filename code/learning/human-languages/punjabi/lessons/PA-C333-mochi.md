## You'll want to know: ਮੋਚੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C333-BARBER333-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋਚੀ** — *mochī* — "a cobbler".

> ਮੋਚੀ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੁੱਤੀਆਂ ਸਿਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *mochī bāzār vich juttīāṁ siundā hai.*
>
> The cobbler stitches shoes in the market.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋਚੀ** (*mochī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mocika*, "a tanner and cobbler"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *mocī*, "cobbler", under it (CDIAL 10349). CDIAL traces the word for a shoe behind it to Iranian.
