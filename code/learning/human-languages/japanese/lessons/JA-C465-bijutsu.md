## You'll want to know: びじゅつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C465-PAINT465-05]; assesses=[] -->

**びじゅつ** — *bijutsu* — "fine art".

> **あねは だいがくで びじゅつを べんきょうしています。** — *ane wa daigaku de bijutsu o benkyō shite imasu* — "My elder sister studies fine art at university."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**びじゅつ** (*bijutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bi*, "beauty" (Middle Chinese *mijX*) and *jutsu*, "art" (*zywit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
