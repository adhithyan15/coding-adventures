## Script — 日, with 木 running through it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-GUO-FRUIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 果

**Two pieces, and you write both already, one woven through the other.**

**日, four strokes**, on top: the left side, the top that turns down the right side, the middle bar, the bottom bar. It is closed before anything else.

**木, four strokes**, as in 本: the long horizontal under 日; then the vertical, which starts at the top of 日 and runs down through it to the foot; then the left-falling stroke and the right-falling stroke.

4 + 4 = eight.
