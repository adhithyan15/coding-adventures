Telugu, chapter 357: Generations.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
వంశం (vaṁśaṁ) — a lineage; a dynasty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a farewell, then the Telugu for adoption.

You'll want to know: వంశం.
వంశం — vaṁśaṁ — "a lineage; a dynasty".
ఆ రాజు గొప్ప వంశంలో పుట్టాడు. ā rāju goppa vaṁśaṁlō puṭṭāḍu. That king was born into a great dynasty.
Plural: వంశాలు, vaṁśālu.

The word, taken apart.
వంశం (vaṁśaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit vaṁśa, "a bamboo", and also "a family, a lineage" (CDIAL 11175).

మా పూర్వీకులు రైతులు. mā pūrvīkulu raitulu. Our ancestors were farmers.

Lesson 5 of 6.
బతుకు (batuku) — to survive; to live.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for ancestors, then the Telugu for an orphan.

You'll want to know: బతుకు.
బతుకు — batuku — "to survive; to live".
ఆ ప్రమాదంలో అతను బతికాడు. ā pramādaṁlō atanu batikāḍu. He survived that accident.
Past (I …): బతికాను, batikānu.
Also a noun: బతుకు (batuku), a life, a living.

The word, taken apart.
బతుకు (batuku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5372 lists Telugu batuku, braduku, "to live, subsist, survive".
