## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಕರಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C358-VICHARANE358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಕರಣ** — *prakaraṇa* — "a case; an affair".

> ಆ ದರೋಡೆಯ ಪ್ರಕರಣ ಇನ್ನೂ ಮುಗಿದಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *ā darōḍeya prakaraṇa innū mugidilla.*
>
> That robbery case is not over yet.

Plural: ಪ್ರಕರಣಗಳು, *prakaraṇagaḷu*.

A matter for the police or a court. A book chapter is also a ಪ್ರಕರಣ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಕರಣ** (*prakaraṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prakaraṇa*, "treatment, a treatise, a subject" (CDIAL 8432).

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
