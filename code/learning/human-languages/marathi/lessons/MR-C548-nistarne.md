## You'll want to know: निस्तरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C548-ADTHALA548-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निस्तरणे** — *nistarṇe* — "to sort out; to set right".

> भावाने केलेली चूक मला निस्तरावी लागली.
>
> *bhāvāne kelelī cūk malā nistarāvī lāglī.*
>
> I had to sort out the mistake my brother made.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चूक निस्तरली, *tyāne cūk nistarlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निस्तरणे** (*nistarṇe*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *nistaraṇa*, "getting across, rescue" (Monier-Williams).
