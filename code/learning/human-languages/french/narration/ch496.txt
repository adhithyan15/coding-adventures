French, chapter 496: Fil et aiguille.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
coudre — to sew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to betray, then the French for betrayal.

You'll want to know: coudre.
coudre — "to sew".
Ma grand-mère m'a appris à coudre. My grandmother taught me to sew.
Irregular: je couds, nous cousons; past j'ai cousu. Coudre un bouton — to sew on a button.

The word, taken apart.
coudre is inherited from Vulgar Latin cōsere (EtymDB), a form of Latin cōnsuere "to sew together", built on suere "to sew" (Lewis & Short).

Le tricot — knitting; also a knitted jumper.

The word, taken apart.
tricoter goes back to Old French tricoter (EtymDB), which once meant "to beat with sticks". Its origin is not settled; a Germanic source has been proposed.
