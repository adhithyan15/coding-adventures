# el carné — the card with your name on it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-DOCUMENTO, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el documento** and **apuntarse**. You sign up, and then they
hand you something.

## You'll want to know: el carné
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-03]; assesses=[ES-LEX-DOCUMENTO] -->

**el carné** — the card: an official one, with your name on it.

*El carné de identidad* — the ID card. *El carné de conducir* — the driving
licence. *El carné de la biblioteca* — the library card. *El carné joven* —
the youth discount card.

Plural **los carnés**. You will also see the French spelling **carnet**, which
is common on forms and older signs.

## Grammar Lens: carné is not tarjeta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-03, ES-LEX-DOCUMENTO] -->

Two Spanish words land on English "card", and they divide the work cleanly:

| the card | the word |
|---|---|
| proves who you are or what you may do | **el carné** |
| pays, greets, or carries an address | **la tarjeta** |

*La tarjeta de crédito*, *la tarjeta de cumpleaños*, *la tarjeta de visita* —
none of those could be a *carné*. And a driving licence could never be a
*tarjeta*, however much plastic it is made of.

The test is whether somebody official issued it about **you**. If yes, it is a
*carné*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-03, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-02] -->

French **carnet**, a small notebook — from Old French *quernet*, and behind
that Latin **quaternum**, a set of four.

Four what? Four sheets of paper folded together, which is how a small notebook
was made. The same *quaternum* gave Spanish **el cuaderno**, and the two words
are the same object seen at different sizes: a *cuaderno* stayed a notebook,
while the French *carnet* shrank to the single card you carry.

Latin **quattuor**, four, is behind both, and behind *cuatro*, *cuarto* and
*cuarenta*. A driving licence has four sheets of paper folded inside its
history.
