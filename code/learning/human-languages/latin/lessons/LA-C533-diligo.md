## You'll want to know: dīligō, dīligere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C533-FAMI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dīligō, dīligere** — "to hold dear, esteem".

> Parentēs līberōs suōs dīligunt.
>
> *Parents hold their children dear.*

*dīligō, dīligere, dīlēxī, dīlēctum*. Love by choice and esteem, calmer than *amō*. *Dīligēns*, already known, comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dīligō** is built on *dis-* "apart" and *legō* "to choose" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB): to single out. English *diligent* and *predilection* come from the family.
