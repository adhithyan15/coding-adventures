## You'll want to know: ఉబ్బసం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C513-VYADHI513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉబ్బసం** — *ubbasaṁ* — "asthma".

> చలికాలంలో అమ్మకు ఉబ్బసం ఎక్కువవుతుంది.
>
> *calikālaṁlō ammaku ubbasaṁ ekkuvavutundi.*
>
> In winter mum's asthma gets worse.

Also called ఆస్తమా, from English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉబ్బసం** (*ubbasaṁ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 632 lists Telugu *ubbasamu*, "asthma", and marks Tamil *uppacam* as borrowed from Telugu.
