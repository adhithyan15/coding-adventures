# きざむ (kizamu) — to chop; to mince, to carve

## You'll want to know: きざむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C174-KITCH174-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きざむ** — *kizamu* — "to chop; to mince, to carve".

> **ほうちょうで だいこんを きざみます。** — *hōchō de daikon o kizamimasu* — "I chop a daikon with a kitchen knife."

Polite **きざみます**, *kizamimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きざむ** (*kizamu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
