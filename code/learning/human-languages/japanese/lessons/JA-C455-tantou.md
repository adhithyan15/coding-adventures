## You'll want to know: たんとう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C455-TEAM455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たんとう** — *tantō* — "being in charge".

> **かれは かいぎの たんとうです。** — *kare wa kaigi no tantō desu* — "He is in charge of the meeting."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たんとう** (*tantō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tan*, "shouldering" (Middle Chinese *tam*) and *tō*, "appropriate" (*tangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
