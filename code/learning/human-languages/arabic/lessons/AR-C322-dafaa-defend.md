## You'll want to know: دافع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C322-QALA322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دافع** — *dāfaʿa* — "to defend".

> دافع الجنود عن المدينة.
>
> *dāfaʿa al-junūd ʿan al-madīna.*
>
> The soldiers defended the city.

Present: يدافع, *yudāfiʿ*.

It takes عن: دافع عن صديقه.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دافع** (*dāfaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-f-ʿ* (Lane), "to push", the root of the taught *dafaʿa*. It is Form III, "to push back against someone": to defend.
