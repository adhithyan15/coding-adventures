# کمرے (kamre) — the shape a noun takes before a postposition

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KAMRA, UR-GRAMMAR-AAP-KA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **this room**. Then say **your name**, and notice the little word
standing between **آپ** and **نام**.

## You'll want to know: the noun changes first
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KAMRA, UR-GRAMMAR-KE-LIYE] -->

> **کمرہ** — *kamrā* — a room, standing on its own.

> **کمرے کے لیے** — *kamre ke liye* — **for the room.**

Look at what happened to the noun. It was **کمرہ** and it is now **کمرے**, and
nothing about the meaning changed. The final *-ā* became **-ے** because a
postposition was coming.

This is the single most important rule in Urdu grammar that a learner can be
taught, and this book has used it since chapter three without stating it. **آپ
کا نام** and **کے لیے** are both postpositions, and both have been quietly
demanding this change all along.

## Grammar Lens: two shapes, and only two
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE, UR-LEX-KAMRA, UR-GRAMMAR-ADJECTIVE-AGREEMENT, UR-LEX-KALA] -->

Sanskrit inflected a noun eight ways. Urdu kept **one** distinction:

| shape | when | example |
|---|---|---|
| **direct** | the noun is a subject, or alone | **کمرہ** |
| **oblique** | a postposition follows it | **کمرے** |

And the adjective goes with it. **کالا کمرہ** becomes **کالے کمرے**: the
adjective is agreeing with the noun's new shape, not merely with its gender.

Nouns not ending in *-ā* do not visibly change — **ماں** is **ماں** either way.
A reader can go a long way without noticing the shape-change, and then be unable
to build a phrase.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE, UR-LEX-KAMRA, UR-LEX-KALA, UR-LEX-PURANA, UR-GRAMMAR-KE-LIYE, UR-LEX-MAAN] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the pair — *kamrā*, then *kamre ke liye*]
- [YOU SAY: with the adjective following it over — *kālā kamrā*, then *kāle kamre ke liye*]
- [YOU SAY: *purāne kamre ke liye*]
- [YOU SAY: the one that does not move — *māṅ*, and *māṅ ke liye*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE, UR-LEX-KAMRA, UR-GRAMMAR-ADJECTIVE-AGREEMENT] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What makes a noun change shape? (**A postposition coming after it**.)
What does **کمرہ** become? (**کمرے**.) Does the adjective follow it over?
(**Yes** — **کالے کمرے**.) And which nouns do not visibly change? (**Those not
ending in *-ā***.)
