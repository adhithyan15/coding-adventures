Punjabi, chapter 328: Caring for the Sick.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਹੌਸਲਾ (hauslā) — courage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for sorrow, then the Punjabi for a teardrop.

You'll want to know: ਹੌਸਲਾ.
ਹੌਸਲਾ — hauslā — "courage".
ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਮਰੀਜ਼ ਨੂੰ ਹੌਸਲਾ ਦਿੰਦੇ ਹਨ। mātā jī marīz nū̃ hauslā dinde han. Mother keeps the patient's spirits up.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਹੌਸਲਾ (hauslā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic ḥawṣala, "capacity".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਦਬਾਉਣਾ (dabāuṇā) — to press gently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for upset, then the Punjabi for reassurance.

You'll want to know: ਦਬਾਉਣਾ.
ਦਬਾਉਣਾ — dabāuṇā — "to press gently".
ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਦਾਦੀ ਦੇ ਪੈਰ ਦਬਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। rāt nū̃ maiṁ dādī de pair dabāundā hāṁ. At night I press my grandmother's feet to ease them.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦਾਦਾ ਜੀ ਦਾ ਸਿਰ ਦਬਾਇਆ, maiṁ dādā jī dā sir dabāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਦਬਾਉਣਾ (dabāuṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed dabb, "press"; CDIAL lists Punjabi dabāuṇā, "to press down", under it (CDIAL 6173).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਸਹਿਣਾ (sahiṇā) — to put up with.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for reassurance, then the Punjabi for to press gently.

You'll want to know: ਸਹਿਣਾ.
ਸਹਿਣਾ — sahiṇā — "to put up with".
ਦਾਦੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਦਰਦ ਸਹਿੰਦੀ ਹੈ ਪਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਰੋਂਦੀ ਹੈ। dādī bahut darad sahindī hai par nahīṁ rondī hai. Grandmother puts up with a lot of pain, but she does not cry.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਦਰਦ ਸਹਿਆ, maiṁ bahut darad sahiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਹਿਣਾ (sahiṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit sahate, "conquers; bears, suffers"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sahiṇā, "to bear, put up with", under it (CDIAL 13304).
