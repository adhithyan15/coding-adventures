## You'll want to know: утешение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C480-LAUGHTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**утешение** (*uteshéniye*) — "consolation, comfort".

> Твои слова — для меня большое утешение.
>
> *Tvoí slová — dlya menyá bolshóye uteshéniye.*
>
> Your words are a great comfort to me.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*утешение* is built on *утешить* 'to comfort' with *-ение*; the root is that of *тешить* 'to please', usually traced to Proto-Slavic *těšiti*.
