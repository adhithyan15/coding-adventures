## You'll want to know: پروژه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C382-PROZHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پروژه** (*prozhe*) — "a project".

> این پروژه شش ماه طول میکشد.
>
> *in prozhe shesh mâh tul mikeshad.*
>
> This project takes six months.

A loan from French. Plural *prozhehâ*. ژ sounds like the s in English pleasure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پروژه** was borrowed from French *projet* "project" (EtymDB), from Latin *prōiectus* "thrown forward", built on *iacere* "to throw" (Lewis & Short): a plan thrown ahead of you.

## What you've built

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
