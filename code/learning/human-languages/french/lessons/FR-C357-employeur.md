## You'll want to know: l'employeur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C357-STAGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'employeur** — "the employer".

> Mon employeur me paie à la fin du mois.
>
> *My employer pays me at the end of the month.*

The feminine *employeuse* is rare.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*employeur* is built from *employer* and *-eur*. *employer* is inherited from Latin *implicāre* "to enfold, to involve" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
