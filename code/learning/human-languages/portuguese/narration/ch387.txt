Portuguese, chapter 387: No tribunal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a queixa — the complaint (to the police); the grievance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for lawful, then the Portuguese for unlawful.

You'll want to know: a queixa.
a queixa — "the complaint (to the police); the grievance".
Ela apresentou queixa na polícia. She reported it to the police.
Apresentar queixa — to report something to the police, to file a complaint.

The word, taken apart.
queixa is built on queixar "to complain", usually traced to Vulgar Latin quassiāre, from Latin quassāre "to shake", on quatere (Lewis & Short); Spanish queja is a cousin (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
o arguido — the accused, the formal suspect; the defendant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for unlawful, then the Portuguese for the complaint.

You'll want to know: o arguido.
o arguido — "the accused, the formal suspect; the defendant".
O arguido não disse nada ao juiz. The accused said nothing to the judge.
A European legal term for someone formally named as a suspect in a criminal case, through to the trial; in Brazil o réu or o acusado. Feminine a arguida.

The word, taken apart.
arguido is the participle of arguir "to accuse, to argue" (EtymDB), from Latin arguere "to make clear, to prove, to accuse" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
a testemunha — the witness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the complaint, then the Portuguese for the accused.

You'll want to know: a testemunha.
a testemunha — "the witness".
A testemunha viu o acidente da janela. The witness saw the accident from the window.
Feminine in form even for a man: ele é a única testemunha.

The word, taken apart.
testemunha is usually traced to Latin testimōnium "witness, evidence, testimony", built on testis "witness" (Lewis & Short); the evidence came to name the person who gives it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a sentença — the court sentence, the ruling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the witness, then the Portuguese for the defence.

You'll want to know: a sentença.
a sentença — "the court sentence, the ruling".
O juiz leu a sentença. The judge read out the sentence.
Mostly a legal word; a grammatical sentence is uma frase.

The word, taken apart.
sentença is inherited from Latin sententia "way of thinking, opinion, judgement" (EtymDB), built on sentīre "to feel, to perceive" (Lewis & Short).
