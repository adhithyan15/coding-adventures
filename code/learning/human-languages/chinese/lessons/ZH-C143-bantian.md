## You'll want to know: 半天
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C143-CLOCK143-05]; assesses=[] -->

**半天** — *bàntiān* — "a long while; half a day".

> *wǒ děng le bàntiān* — I waited for ages.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**半天** (*bàntiān*) is built from 半 'half' and 天 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *panH* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): half a day, and in everyday talk, often with a sigh, a long while.
