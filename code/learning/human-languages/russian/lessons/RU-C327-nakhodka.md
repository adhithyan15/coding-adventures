## You'll want to know: находка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C327-KLAD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**находка** (*nakhódka*) — "a find, a discovery".

> Эта находка удивила учёных.
>
> *Éta nakhódka udivíla uchyónykh.*
>
> This find amazed the scientists.

Feminine; genitive plural *находок*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*находка* (EtymDB: "a find, a discovery") is built on *находить* "to find", from *на-* and *ходить* "to go", Proto-Slavic *xoditi* (EtymDB): what you come upon.
