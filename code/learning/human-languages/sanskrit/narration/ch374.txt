Sanskrit, chapter 374: Taking Care.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अवहितः (avahitaḥ) — attentive; careful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an exception, then the Sanskrit for compulsory.

You'll want to know: अवहितः.
अवहितः — avahitaḥ — "attentive; careful".
यदि भवान् अवहितः, तर्हि दोषः न भविष्यति। yadi bhavān avahitaḥ, tarhi doṣaḥ na bhaviṣyati. If you are attentive, there will be no mistake.

The word, taken apart.
अवहितः (avahitaḥ) is built on ava, "down", and hita, "put", the participle of √dhā; MW gives ava-hita "attentive" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for carelessness, then the Sanskrit for avoids.

You'll want to know: अनर्थः.
अनर्थः — anarthaḥ — "a mishap; a bad turn".
यदि चालकः श्रान्तः, तर्हि अनर्थः भविष्यति। yadi cālakaḥ śrāntaḥ, tarhi anarthaḥ bhaviṣyati. If the driver is tired, there will be a mishap.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अनर्थः (anarthaḥ) is built from an-, "not", and artha, "aim, use"; MW gives an-artha "a worthless object" and "a disappointing occurrence, a reverse, an evil" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: विरमति.
विरमति — viramati — "ceases; stops".
यदि वर्षा विरमति, तर्हि अहं बहिः गमिष्यामि। yadi varṣā viramati, tarhi ahaṃ bahiḥ gamiṣyāmi. If the rain stops, I will go out.
Past (it …): वर्षा विरतवती, varṣā viratavatī.

The word, taken apart.
विरमति (viramati) is built on vi and the root √ram, "to rest"; MW gives vi-√ram "to stop, to pause, to cease, to come to an end" (MW).
