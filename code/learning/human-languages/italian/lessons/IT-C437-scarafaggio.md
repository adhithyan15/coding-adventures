## You'll want to know: lo scarafaggio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C437-RUMORE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lo scarafaggio** — "the cockroach".

> In cucina c'era uno scarafaggio!
>
> *There was a cockroach in the kitchen!*

A good reason to ask for another room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scarafaggio* is usually traced to Latin *scarabaeus* 'beetle' (Lewis & Short), from Greek *kárabos*; it is inherited, with a changed ending.
