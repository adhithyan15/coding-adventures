## You'll want to know: हींग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C185-MASALO185-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हींग** — *hī̃g* — "asafoetida".

> दाल मांय हींग घालो।
>
> *dāl mā̃y hī̃g ghālo.*
>
> Put asafoetida in the dal.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हींग** (*hī̃g*) is inherited from Sanskrit *hiṅgu*, the asafoetida plant and the gum from its root; CDIAL lists Hindi *hī̃g* (CDIAL 14079). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
