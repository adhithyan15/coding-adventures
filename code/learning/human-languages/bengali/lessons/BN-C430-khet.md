## You'll want to know: খেত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C430-KHET430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**খেত** — *khet* — "a field (of crops)".

> গ্রামের চারপাশে ধানের খেত।
>
> *grāmer chārpāshe dhāner khet.*
>
> All round the village are rice fields.

মাঠ is open ground; খেত is ground that grows something: ধানের খেত, a rice field.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খেত** (*khet*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *khet*, "a field", under Sanskrit *kṣḗtra*, "land" (CDIAL 3735).
