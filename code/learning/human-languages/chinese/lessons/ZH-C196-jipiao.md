## You'll want to know: 机票
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C196-TICKET196-02]; assesses=[] -->

**机票** — *jīpiào* — "plane ticket".

> **我买了十号的机票** *wǒ mǎile shí hào de jīpiào* — I've bought a plane ticket for the tenth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**机票** (*jīpiào*) is built from 机, short for *fēijī* 'aeroplane', and 票 'a ticket'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *kj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of 票, so its history is not settled.
