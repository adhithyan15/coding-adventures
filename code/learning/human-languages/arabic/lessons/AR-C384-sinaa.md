## You'll want to know: صناعة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C384-TANMIYA384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صناعة** — *ṣināʿa* — "industry".

> صناعة السيارات كبيرة في هذا البلد.
>
> *ṣināʿat as-sayyārāt kabīra fī hādhā al-balad.*
>
> The car industry is big in this country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صناعة** (*ṣināʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-n-ʿ*, "to make, to manufacture" (Lane). *Ṣināʿa* is a craft, the work of making: industry.
