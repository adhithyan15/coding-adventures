## You'll want to know: угадывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C380-BUDUSHCHEYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**угадывать** (*ugádyvat*) — "to guess (right)".

> Он всегда угадывает, что я хочу.
>
> *On vsegdá ugádyvayet, chto ya khochú.*
>
> He always guesses what I want.

Imperfective; perfective *угадать*. It means guessing right; to guess at something is *догадываться*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**угадывать** is built from *у-* and *гадать* "to guess, to tell fortunes", an inherited Slavic verb: to hit on the answer.
