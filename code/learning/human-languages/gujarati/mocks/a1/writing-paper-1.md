# Gujarati A1 writing paper 1 — learner sheet

This is one **writing-only** paper for the project-defined Gujarati A1
assessment, not a complete four-skill mock or a CEFR certificate. Administer
it outside the lesson sequence. A lesson remains at most five minutes; this
paper uses one continuous **20-minute** clock. Give the learner only this sheet,
not the examiner rubric, another skill sheet, or
an answer model. Written support-language directions are allowed at A1; the
responses must be independently written in Gujarati script. Do not use
romanization, a dictionary, translator, spell-checker, or generative assistant.
Safe invented personal details are welcome.

Start the clock now. Spend **7 minutes** on Task 1, then move immediately to
Task 2 for the remaining **13 minutes**. Do not stop or restart the clock. Stop
writing when the 20 minutes end; review only after the sheet is collected.

## Task 1 — practical form (7 minutes; 40 points)

A local library is preparing a reading card for you. Fill in **seven** short
answers in Gujarati script. Give a chosen name and safe invented contact
details if you prefer. Write your city or village, a language you speak, a
book topic you like, your preferred visit day, and your preferred visit time.
The labels identify the fields; they do not give you sentences to copy.
