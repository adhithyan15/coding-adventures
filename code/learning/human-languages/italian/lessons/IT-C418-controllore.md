## You'll want to know: il controllore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C418-BIGLIETTERIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il controllore** — "the ticket inspector".

> Il controllore passa tra i sedili.
>
> *The inspector comes along the seats.*

*Il controllore di volo* is an air traffic controller.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*controllore* is built on *controllo* 'check', which was borrowed from French *contrôle*, from *contre-rôle*: a second list kept to check the first.
