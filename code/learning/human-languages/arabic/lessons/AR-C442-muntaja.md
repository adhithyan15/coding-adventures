## You'll want to know: منتجع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C442-MUNTAJA442-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منتجع** — *muntajaʿ* — "a resort".

> قضينا العطلة في منتجع على البحر.
>
> *qaḍaynā al-ʿuṭla fī muntajaʿ ʿalā al-baḥr.*
>
> We spent the holiday at a resort by the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منتجع** (*muntajaʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-j-ʿ*, "to go in search of pasture" (Lane). It is the place-noun of Form VIII: once a place people went to for fresh pasture.
