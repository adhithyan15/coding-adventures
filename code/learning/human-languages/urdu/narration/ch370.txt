Urdu, chapter 370: Haste and Care.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
جلد بازی (jald-bāzī) — haste; rushing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to pester, then the Urdu for to throw away.

You'll want to know: جلد بازی.
جلد بازی — jald-bāzī — "haste; rushing".
اگر تم نے جلد بازی کی تو حادثہ ہو گا۔ agar tum ne jald-bāzī kī to hādsā ho gā. If you rush, there will be an accident.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
جلد بازی (jald-bāzī) is usually traced to Persian jaldbāzī, "haste": jald, "quick", and bāzī, "play". Playing at speed.

Lesson 2 of 6.
دھیان (dhyān) — attention; care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to throw away, then the Urdu for haste.

You'll want to know: دھیان.
دھیان — dhyān — "attention; care".
دھیان سے چلیے، ورنہ آپ گر جائیں گے۔ dhyān se chaliye, varnā āp gir jāeṅ ge. Walk carefully, or you will fall.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
دھیان (dhyān) is borrowed from Sanskrit dhyāna, "meditation, thought" (CDIAL 6889); in Urdu, the mind kept on what you are doing.

Lesson 4 of 6.
پھوڑنا (phoṛnā) — to smash; to break (something brittle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for attention, then the Urdu for patience.

You'll want to know: پھوڑنا.
پھوڑنا — phoṛnā — "to smash; to break (something brittle)".
اگر تم نے جلدی کی تو برتن پھوڑ دو گے۔ agar tum ne jaldī kī to bartan phoṛ do ge. If you hurry, you will smash the dishes.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے برتن پھوڑا, us ne bartan phoṛā.

The word, taken apart.
پھوڑنا (phoṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phoṛnā, "to break", under Sanskrit sphōṭayati, "bursts" (CDIAL 13857).
