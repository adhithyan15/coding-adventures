## You'll want to know: die Schulden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C388-KREDIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Schulden** — "the debts".

> Nach dem Autokauf hatte er hohe Schulden.
>
> *After buying the car he had large debts.*

A plural noun: *Schulden haben* — to be in debt. The singular *die Schuld* means guilt or blame.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schulden* is the plural of *Schuld* "debt, guilt", inherited from Old High German *sculd* and Proto-Germanic *skuldiz* (EtymDB): what you owe.
