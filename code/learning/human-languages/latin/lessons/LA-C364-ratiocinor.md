## You'll want to know: ratiōcinor, ratiōcinārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C364-CONCLUDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ratiōcinor, ratiōcinārī** — "to reason, to calculate".

> Bene ratiōcināris, sed falsum est initium.
>
> *You reason well, but the starting point is false.*

Deponent: *ratiōcinor, ratiōcinārī, ratiōcinātus sum*. Built on *ratiō*, reckoning: to work a thing out step by step. English *ratiocination*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ratiōcinor* is built on *ratiō* "a reckoning, reason" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to keep accounts, then to reason. English *ratiocination* comes from it.
