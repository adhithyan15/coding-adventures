# তৃপ্তি (tripti) — satisfaction; contentment

## You'll want to know: তৃপ্তি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C439-SUKH439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**তৃপ্তি** — *tripti* — "satisfaction; contentment".

> দিদিমার রান্না খেয়ে আমরা তৃপ্তি পেলাম।
>
> *didimār rānnā kheye āmrā tripti pelām.*
>
> We ate my grandmother's cooking with real satisfaction.

তৃপ্তি পাওয়া, to find satisfaction; তৃপ্তি করে খাওয়া, to eat with relish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তৃপ্তি** (*tripti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *tṛpti*, "satisfaction, contentment" (Monier-Williams): the feeling of having had enough.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
