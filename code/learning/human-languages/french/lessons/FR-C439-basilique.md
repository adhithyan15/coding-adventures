## You'll want to know: la basilique
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C439-CLOCHERS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la basilique** — "the basilica".

> On monte à la basilique par un long escalier.
>
> *You go up to the basilica by a long flight of steps.*

A large and important church.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*basilique* is a learned borrowing from Latin *basilica*, from Greek *basilikḗ (stoá)* 'royal hall' (EtymDB).
