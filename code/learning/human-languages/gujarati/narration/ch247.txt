Lesson 1 of 5.
સાહેબ (sāheb) — boss, sir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for studies, then the Gujarati for to work hard.

You'll want to know: સાહેબ.
સાહેબ — sāheb — "boss, sir".
અમારા સાહેબ સવારે વહેલા આવે છે. — amārā sāheb savāre vahelā āve che — "Our boss comes early in the morning."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સાહેબ (sāheb) is a loan from Arabic ṣāḥib, "companion, owner", which reached Gujarati through Persian as a respectful word for a master. Its Arabic root is ṣ-ḥ-b (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
કર્મચારી (karmacārī) — employee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to work hard, then the Gujarati for boss.

You'll want to know: કર્મચારી.
કર્મચારી — karmacārī — "employee".
આ કારખાનામાં સો કર્મચારી કામ કરે છે. — ā kārkhānāmā̃ so karmacārī kām kare che — "A hundred employees work in this factory."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કર્મચારી (karmacārī) is a learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, a tatsama: karman, "act, work" (CDIAL 2892), and cārin, "moving about" (CDIAL 4765).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ધંધો (dhandho) — business, trade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for boss, then the Gujarati for employee.

You'll want to know: ધંધો.
ધંધો — dhandho — "business, trade".
મારા કાકા ચાનો ધંધો કરે છે. — mārā kākā cāno dhandho kare che — "My uncle runs a tea business."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ધંધો (dhandho) is not settled. CDIAL lists a Gujarati dhandho only in the sense "fuss", under Sanskrit dhandha, "awkwardness" (CDIAL 6727), and EtymDB links the word to a Prakrit dhaṁdha.

Lesson 4 of 5.
કમાવું (kamāvũ) — to earn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for employee, then the Gujarati for business.

You'll want to know: કમાવું.
કમાવું — kamāvũ — "to earn".
હવે તે સારું કમાય છે. — have te sārũ kamāy che — "Now he earns well."

The word, taken apart.
કમાવું (kamāvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from karmāpayati, "works, earns", a reconstructed form built on Sanskrit karman, "work"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to earn" (CDIAL 2897).

Lesson 5 of 5.
મહેનતુ (mahenatu) — hardworking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for business, then the Gujarati for to earn.

You'll want to know: મહેનતુ.
મહેનતુ — mahenatu — "hardworking".
આ કર્મચારી મહેનતુ છે. — ā karmacārī mahenatu che — "This employee is hardworking."

The word, taken apart.
મહેનતુ (mahenatu) is built on mahenat, "hard work", with the adjective suffix -u. Mahenat is a loan from Arabic miḥnat, "trial, affliction", through Persian (Lane).
