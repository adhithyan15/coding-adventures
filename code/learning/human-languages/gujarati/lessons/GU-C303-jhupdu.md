# ઝૂંપડું (jhū̃pḍũ) — a hut

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to strike, then the Gujarati for a thatched roof.

## You'll want to know: ઝૂંપડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C303-VAVAZODU303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂંપડું** — *jhū̃pḍũ* — "a hut".

> ગરીબ લોકોનાં ઝૂંપડાં તૂટી ગયાં.
>
> *garīb lokonā̃ jhū̃pḍā̃ tūṭī gayā̃.*
>
> The poor people's huts were wrecked.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

comes from *\*jhuppa*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "a hut" (CDIAL 5403). It is neuter: **ઝૂંપડું**, plural **ઝૂંપડાં**.
