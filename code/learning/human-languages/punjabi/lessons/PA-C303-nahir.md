## You'll want to know: ਨਹਿਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C303-WATER303-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਹਿਰ** — *nahir* — "a canal".

> ਸਾਡੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਨਹਿਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍe piṇḍ de neṛe nahir hai.*
>
> There is a canal near our village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਹਿਰ** (*nahir*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *nahr*, "river"; its sense here, "canal", is a shift from that.
