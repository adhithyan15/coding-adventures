## You'll want to know: rimorchiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C410-GUASTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rimorchiare** — "to tow".

> Il carro attrezzi ci rimorchia fino al paese.
>
> *The tow truck tows us to the village.*

*Il rimorchio* is the towing, or the vehicle being towed; in slang, *rimorchiare* is to chat someone up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rimorchiare* is built on *rimorchio* 'towing' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *remulcum* 'a towing rope' (Lewis & Short), itself from Greek.
