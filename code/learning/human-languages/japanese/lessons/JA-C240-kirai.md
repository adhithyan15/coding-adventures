## You'll want to know: きらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C240-LIKES240-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きらい** — *kirai* — "disliked; hated".

> **おとうとは やさいが きらいでした。** — *otōto wa yasai ga kirai deshita* — "My younger brother disliked vegetables."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きらい** (*kirai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it comes from *kirau*, "to dislike", and it is usually written in kana (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
