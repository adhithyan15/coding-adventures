## You'll want to know: مرشد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C437-BUSALA437-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مرشد** — *murshid* — "a tour guide; a guide".

> شرح المرشد تاريخ المدينة.
>
> *sharaḥa al-murshid tārīkh al-madīna.*
>
> The guide explained the city's history.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرشد** (*murshid*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-sh-d*, "to follow the right way" (Lane). It is the Form IV participle, "one who leads others the right way".
