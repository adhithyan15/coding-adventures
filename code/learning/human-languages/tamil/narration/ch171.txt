Tamil, chapter 171: Often, Sometimes, Always, Never, Delay.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அடிக்கடி (aṭikkaṭi) — often.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for diarrhoea, then the Tamil for the brain.

You'll want to know: அடிக்கடி.
அடிக்கடி — aṭikkaṭi — "often".

The word, taken apart.
அடிக்கடி (aṭikkaṭi) is native Dravidian. DEDR 84 lists Malayalam aṭikkaṭi, "repeatedly". It sits beside aṭi, "to be on the increase, to abound".

Lesson 5 of 5.
தாமதம் (tāmatam) — a delay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for always, then the Tamil for never.

You'll want to know: தாமதம்.
தாமதம் — tāmatam — "a delay".

The word, taken apart.
தாமதம் (tāmatam) is usually traced to Sanskrit tāmasa, "dark, dull" (CDIAL 5775), from tamas, "darkness, inertia": delay as sluggishness.
