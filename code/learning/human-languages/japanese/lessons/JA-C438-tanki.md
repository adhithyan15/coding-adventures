## You'll want to know: たんき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C438-STUBBORN438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**たんき** — *tanki* — "short-tempered".

> **あには すこし たんきです。** — *ani wa sukoshi tanki desu* — "My elder brother is a little short-tempered."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たんき** (*tanki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tan*, "short" (Middle Chinese *twanX*) and *ki*, "spirit" (*khj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
