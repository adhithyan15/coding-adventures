# HL100 — Punjabi A2 `ਸਬਜ਼ੀ` prerequisite

Issue: #18204

After PR #18189 merged, an aggregate-only audit of the preserved confidential
Punjabi A2 mock drafts against merged main
`1cefff5febbd5542841adf95ee301b6d3fff2442` finds **ਸਬਜ਼ੀ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs three times in the confidential learner
material and zero times in the confidential examiner material. Neither
confidential body belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 200 closes that single-word gap through one deliberately narrow
identity frame: **ਇਹ ਸਬਜ਼ੀ ਹੈ** — “this is a vegetable / cooked vegetable
dish.” The voice-first opener retrieves only familiar **ਇਹ** and **ਹੈ**. One
lesson directly owns exact **ਸਬਜ਼ੀ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound,
meaning, and script, a bounded frame lesson places it between the two familiar
words, and a four-skill payoff retrieves the complete label again. The target
has one direct owner and three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored,
generated-book, or narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-LEX-SABZI-200`. The lesson keeps the Punjabi form whole and
owns only the everyday noun sense “vegetable” or “cooked vegetable dish.” It
does not teach gender, plural or oblique forms, derive the word from smaller
Punjabi parts, teach compounds or agricultural classification, introduce
synonyms, or claim that every English use of “vegetable,” “greens,” and “dish”
is interchangeable.

Punjabi University's Punjabipedia documents **ਸਬਜ਼ੀਆਂ** as the present-day
Punjabi category for vegetables:
<https://punjabipedia.org/topic.aspx?txt=%E0%A8%B8%E0%A8%AC%E0%A9%9B%E0%A9%80%E0%A8%86%E0%A8%82>.
A Government of Punjab canteen rate list uses singular **ਸਬਜ਼ੀ** in ordinary
food-service prose:
<https://punjab.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Hospitality-Food-and-Beverages-Rate-List-M.L.A-Hostel-Canteen-Punjab-Chd.pdf>.

The exact origin of modern written **ਸਬਜ਼ੀ** remains unknown in the sources
used here. The lesson therefore makes no etymological claim and does not turn
spelling resemblance into a productive derivation.

This is the twenty-third prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
