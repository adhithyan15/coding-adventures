Lesson 1 of 6.
ঢাক (ḍhāk) — a dhak; a big festival drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a gift, then the Bengali for to entertain guests.

You'll want to know: ঢাক.
ঢাক — ḍhāk — "a dhak; a big festival drum".
পুজোর সকালে একটা লোক ঢাক বাজাচ্ছিল। pujor sôkāle ekṭā lok ḍhāk bājāchchhilô. On the morning of the puja a man was playing the dhak.

The word, taken apart.
ঢাক (ḍhāk) is usually linked with Sanskrit ḍhakkā, 'a large drum' (Monier-Williams). Its ঢ is the breathy partner of ড.

কাঁসর — kā̃sôr — "a bell-metal gong".
ঢাকের সঙ্গে একটা ছেলে কাঁসর বাজাল। ḍhāker sôngge ekṭā chhele kā̃sôr bājālô. A boy played the gong along with the dhak.

কাঁসর (kā̃sôr) is built on কাঁসা, bell-metal — Sanskrit kāṁsya, 'made of bell-metal' (CDIAL 2987, which lists Bengali kā̃sā) — and is a flat gong of it, struck beside the drums.

You'll want to know: ধুনুচি.
ধুনুচি — dhunuchi — "an incense burner".
পুজোর সন্ধ্যায় পুজারি ধুনুচি হাতে নিয়ে নাচলেন। pujor sôndhyāy pujāri dhunuchi hāte niye nāchlen. On the evening of the puja the priest danced with an incense burner in his hand.

The word, taken apart.
ধুনুচি (dhunuchi) is built on ধুনো, the resin burned as incense — Sanskrit dhūṇa, 'gum, resin' (CDIAL 6838, which lists Bengali dhunā) — and is the clay cup it smoulders in.

Lesson 4 of 6.
আরতি (ārôti) — an evening worship with lamps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bell-metal gong, then the Bengali for an incense burner.

You'll want to know: আরতি.
আরতি — ārôti — "an evening worship with lamps".
সন্ধ্যায় মন্দিরে আরতি হয়। sôndhyāy mondire ārôti hôy. There is an evening worship with lamps at the temple.

The word, taken apart.
আরতি (ārôti) comes down from Sanskrit ārātrika, the light waved at night before an image, and the rite itself (Monier-Williams; CDIAL 1315, which lists Bengali ārati, the ceremony).
