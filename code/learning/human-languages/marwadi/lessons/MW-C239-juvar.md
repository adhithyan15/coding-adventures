## You'll want to know: जुवार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C239-CROP239-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जुवार** — *juvār* — "sorghum; the great millet".

> खेत मांय जुवार हरी है।
>
> *khet mā̃y juvār harī hai.*
>
> The sorghum in the field is green.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जुवार** (*juvār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *yavākāra*, "shaped like barley"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *juvāri*, "millet" (CDIAL 10437). In Marwadi it names sorghum, the tall millet, beside **बाजरो**.
