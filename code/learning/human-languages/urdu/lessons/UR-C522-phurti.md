## You'll want to know: پھرتی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C522-PHURTI522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پھرتی** — *phurtī* — "agility; quickness".

> اس کھلاڑی میں بڑی پھرتی ہے۔
>
> *is khilāṛī meṅ baṛī phurtī hai.*
>
> This player is very quick on his feet.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھرتی** (*phurtī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *phurtī*, "activity", under Sanskrit *sphurati*, "darts, springs" (CDIAL 13849).
