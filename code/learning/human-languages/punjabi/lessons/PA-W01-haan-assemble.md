# ਹਾਂ — three known pieces become one known answer

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HAAN-NAHIN-01, PA-SCRIPT-HA-01, PA-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, PA-SCRIPT-BINDI-01] -->

Point to the three pieces you met separately: **ਹ**, **ਾ**, **ਂ**. Say their jobs:
*h*, long *ā*, nasal ending.

## Script — assemble with no new sign
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-HAAN-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HAAN-NAHIN-01, PA-SCRIPT-HA-01, PA-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, PA-SCRIPT-BINDI-01] -->

Slide the known pieces together from left to right:

> **ਹ** + **ਾ** + **ਂ** → **ਹਾਂ**

Read *hā̃*, “yes.” Point once to each piece while you say its part, then read the
whole answer once at natural speed. Your pencil stays down today; assembly and
copying are separate steps.
