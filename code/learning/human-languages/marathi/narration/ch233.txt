Lesson 1 of 5.
गडद (gaḍad) — dark (of a colour).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for smooth, then the Marathi for rough.

You'll want to know: गडद.
गडद — gaḍad — "dark (of a colour)".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of गडद (gaḍad) is not settled. Neither CDIAL nor DEDR, searched by form and by meaning, has an entry for it.

The word, taken apart.
फिकट (phikaṭ) goes with Marathi phikā, "pale, tasteless", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed phikka-, "defective" (CDIAL 9037).

Lesson 4 of 5.
वक्तशीर (vaktaśīr) — punctual.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for pale, then the Marathi for distant.

You'll want to know: वक्तशीर.
वक्तशीर — vaktaśīr — "punctual".

The word, taken apart.
वक्तशीर (vaktaśīr) is built on vakta, "time", usually traced through Persian to Arabic waqt, "time". The source of the ending is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सहनशील (sahanśīl) — patient, tolerant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for distant, then the Marathi for punctual.

You'll want to know: सहनशील.
सहनशील — sahanśīl — "patient, tolerant".

The word, taken apart.
सहनशील (sahanśīl) is a compound of Sanskrit parts: sahana, "endurance, patience" (CDIAL 13305), and śīla, "habit, nature" (CDIAL 12501).
