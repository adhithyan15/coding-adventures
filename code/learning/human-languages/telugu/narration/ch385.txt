Telugu, chapter 385: Agreeing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అంగీకరించు (aṅgīkariñcu) — to accept; to agree to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to recommend, then the Telugu for a point.

You'll want to know: అంగీకరించు.
అంగీకరించు — aṅgīkariñcu — "to accept; to agree to".
కంపెనీ మా షరతులు అంగీకరించింది. kampenī mā ṣaratulu aṅgīkariñcindi. The company accepted our conditions.
Past (I …): అంగీకరించాను, aṅgīkariñcānu.
Formal beside the everyday ఒప్పుకో.

The word, taken apart.
అంగీకరించు (aṅgīkariñcu) is built on Sanskrit aṅgīkāra, "agreement, a promise" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 2 of 5.
అంగీకారం (aṅgīkāraṁ) — consent; acceptance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a point, then the Telugu for to accept.

You'll want to know: అంగీకారం.
అంగీకారం — aṅgīkāraṁ — "consent; acceptance".
తల్లిదండ్రుల అంగీకారంతో వాళ్ళ పెళ్ళి జరిగింది. tallidaṇḍrula aṅgīkārantō vāḷḷa peḷḷi jarigindi. Their wedding took place with their parents’ consent.
The noun of అంగీకరించు (aṅgīkariñcu): అంగీకారం (aṅgīkāraṁ) తెలుపు, to give one’s consent.

The word, taken apart.
అంగీకారం (aṅgīkāraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit aṅgīkāra, "agreement, a promise" (Monier-Williams); its verb is అంగీకరించు (aṅgīkariñcu).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఏకీభవించు (ēkībhaviñcu) — to agree with.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to accept, then the Telugu for consent.

You'll want to know: ఏకీభవించు.
ఏకీభవించు — ēkībhaviñcu — "to agree with".
నేను మీ అభిప్రాయంతో ఏకీభవిస్తాను. nēnu mī abhiprāyantō ēkībhavistānu. I agree with your opinion.
Past (I …): ఏకీభవించాను, ēkībhaviñcānu.
The person or view you agree with takes -తో: మీతో ఏకీభవిస్తాను.

The word, taken apart.
ఏకీభవించు (ēkībhaviñcu) is built on Sanskrit ekībhāva, "the becoming one, coalition" (Monier-Williams), from eka, "one", and bhāva, "becoming", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 5.
సమ్మతించు (sammatiñcu) — to consent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for consent, then the Telugu for to agree with.

You'll want to know: సమ్మతించు.
సమ్మతించు — sammatiñcu — "to consent".
మా నాన్న ఆ ప్రయాణానికి సమ్మతించాడు. mā nānna ā prayāṇāniki sammatiñcāḍu. My father consented to that trip.
Past (I …): సమ్మతించాను, sammatiñcānu.
What you consent to takes -కి: దానికి సమ్మతించు (sammatiñcu). The noun is సమ్మతి, consent.

The word, taken apart.
సమ్మతించు (sammatiñcu) is built on Sanskrit sammati, "sameness of opinion, agreement, approval" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 5 of 5.
మద్దతు (maddatu) — backing; support.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to agree with, then the Telugu for to consent.

You'll want to know: మద్దతు.
మద్దతు — maddatu — "backing; support".
ఆ ఆలోచనకు చాలా మంది మద్దతు ఇచ్చారు. ā ālōcanaku cālā mandi maddatu iccāru. Many people backed that idea.
మద్దతు (maddatu) ఇచ్చు, to give support; the person or idea backed takes -కి.

The word, taken apart.
మద్దతు (maddatu) is usually traced to Hindustani madad, "help", a word of Arabic origin; the Telugu form and its sense of backing a cause are its own.
