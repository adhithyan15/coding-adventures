## You'll want to know: たび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C151-KIMONO151-03]; assesses=[] -->

**たび** — *tabi* — "split-toe socks".

> **まつりで しろい たびを はきます。** — *matsuri de shiroi tabi o hakimasu* — "I wear white split-toe socks at the festival."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たび** (*tabi*) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its two characters, "foot" and "bag", are read together as one word rather than one by one (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
