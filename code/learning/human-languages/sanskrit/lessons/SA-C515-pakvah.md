## You'll want to know: पक्वः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C515-SVADA515-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्वः** — *pakvaḥ* — "ripe; cooked".

> एतत् आम्रं पक्वं न अस्ति।
>
> *etat āmraṃ pakvaṃ na asti.*
>
> This mango is not ripe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्वः** (*pakvaḥ*) comes from the root √pac, "to cook", and MW treats it as that root's past participle: cooked, baked, and so ripe (MW).
