## You'll want to know: ज्वरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C66-UNWELL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वरः** (*jvaraḥ*) — "a fever". **मम ज्वरः अस्ति** (*mama jvaraḥ asti*) — "I have a
fever", literally "of me a fever is", with **मम** and **अस्ति**, which you know.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

An illness, said with of-me and is.
