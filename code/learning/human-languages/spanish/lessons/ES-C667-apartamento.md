## You'll want to know: el apartamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C667-APARTAMENTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el apartamento** — "the flat, the apartment".

> Alquilamos un apartamento cerca de la playa.
>
> *We rented a flat near the beach.*

Spain also says *el piso*; much of Latin America says *el departamento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apartamento* is usually traced to Italian *appartamento*, perhaps through French *appartement*; the Italian is built on *appartare* "to set apart", from Latin *ad* and *pars* "part" (EtymDB).
