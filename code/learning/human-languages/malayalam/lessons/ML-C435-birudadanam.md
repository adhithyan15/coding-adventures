# ബിരുദദാനം (birudadānaṁ) — a graduation ceremony

## You'll want to know: ബിരുദദാനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C435-BIRUDAM435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ബിരുദദാനം** — *birudadānaṁ* — "a graduation ceremony".

> ബിരുദദാനത്തിന് അച്ഛനും അമ്മയും വന്നു.
>
> *birudadānattinŭ acchanuṁ ammayuṁ vannu.*
>
> Father and mother came to the graduation ceremony.

ദാനം, a giving: the giving of degrees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ബിരുദദാനം** (*birudadānaṁ*) is a Malayalam compound built from ബിരുദം, "a degree", from Sanskrit *biruda*, "a panegyric" (Monier-Williams, under *viruda*), and *dāna*, "giving, a gift" (CDIAL 6265): the giving of degrees.
