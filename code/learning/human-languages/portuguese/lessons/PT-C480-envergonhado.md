## You'll want to know: envergonhado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C480-REMORSO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**envergonhado** — "ashamed, embarrassed; shy".

> Fiquei envergonhado quando me enganei no nome dela.
>
> *I was embarrassed when I got her name wrong.*

Also shy: *uma criança envergonhada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*envergonhado* goes back to Old Portuguese *envergonnado* (EtymDB), the participle of *envergonhar*, built on *vergonha* "shame", from Latin *verēcundia*.
