## You'll want to know: zhídá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C405-GATES405-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhídá* — "to go direct; to go without changing".

> *zuò gāotiě kěyǐ zhídá Běijīng* — The high-speed train takes you straight to Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhídá* is built from *zhí* 'straight' and *dá* 'to get through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drik* and *dat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'upright' and the second 'to reach through'.
