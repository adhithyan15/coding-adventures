## You'll want to know: el embarque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C653-EMBARQUE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el embarque** — "the boarding".

> El embarque empieza a las diez.
>
> *Boarding starts at ten.*

*La puerta de embarque* — the gate; *la tarjeta de embarque* — the boarding pass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*embarque* is built on *embarcar* "to board", which comes from *barca* "boat", Late Latin *barca* (EtymDB).
