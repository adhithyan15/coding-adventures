## You'll want to know: پیپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C478-CUT478-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پیپ** — *pīp* — "pus".

> زخم میں پیپ ہے۔
>
> *zakhm meṅ pīp hai.*
>
> There is pus in the wound.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیپ** (*pīp*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pīp*, "pus", under Sanskrit *pīvan*, "well-nourished, swelling" (CDIAL 8242).
