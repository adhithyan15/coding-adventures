## You'll want to know: तैयारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C392-TAIYARI392-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तैयारी** — *taiyārī* — "preparation".

> अगर तैयारी अच्छी होगी तो परीक्षा से डर नहीं लगेगा।
>
> *agar taiyārī acchī hogī to parīkṣā se ḍar nahīṃ lagegā.*
>
> If your preparation is good, you won't be afraid of the exam.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तैयारी** (*taiyārī*) is usually traced to Arabic *tayyār*, "ready", which reached Hindi through Persian. The Hindi noun adds the ending *-ī*.
