## You'll want to know: げんかん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C155-MOVE155-05]; assesses=[] -->

**げんかん** — *genkan* — "the front entrance; the entry hall".

> **げんかんで くつを ぬぎます。** — *genkan de kutsu o nugimasu* — "I take off my shoes in the entry hall."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**げんかん** (*genkan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gen*, "dark, deep" (Middle Chinese *hwen*) and *kan*, "barrier, gateway" (*kwaen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
