## You'll want to know: లోపం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C428-LOPAM428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**లోపం** — *lōpaṁ* — "a flaw; a shortcoming".

> ఆ ప్రణాళికలో ఒక పెద్ద లోపం ఉంది.
>
> *ā praṇāḷikalō oka pedda lōpaṁ undi.*
>
> There is a big flaw in that plan.

Plural: లోపాలు, *lōpālu*.

Plural లోపాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లోపం** (*lōpaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *lopa*, "breaking, injury; neglect, violation" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for a fault or a lack.
