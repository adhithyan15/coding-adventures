## You'll want to know: खंडहर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C326-KHANDAHAR326-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खंडहर** — *khaṇḍhar* — "ruins".

> खंडहर के लिए सीधे जाइए और मंदिर के बाद दाएँ मुड़िए।
>
> *khaṇḍhar ke liye sīdhe jāie aur mandir ke bād dāẽ muṛiye.*
>
> For the ruins, go straight on and turn right after the temple.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खंडहर** (*khaṇḍhar*) is probably *khaṇḍ*, broken, from Sanskrit *khaṇḍa*, "a piece, a fragment" (Monier-Williams), with **घर**, a house, worn down: a house in pieces.
