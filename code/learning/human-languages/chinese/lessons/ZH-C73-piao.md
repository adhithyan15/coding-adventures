## You'll want to know: piào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-05]; assesses=[] -->

*piào* — "ticket".

> *wǒ mǎi le piào* — I've bought the tickets.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piào* is written with a character the Guangyun records, under an older form, as *phjiew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'flying fire'. That is probably a different word: the ticket sense is not given there, and *phjiew* would regularly give piāo, not piào. How the character came to write the word for ticket is not settled.
