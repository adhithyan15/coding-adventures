## You'll want to know: மலை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C53-LAND-02]; assesses=[] -->

**மலை** (*malai*) — "a hill".

Proto-Dravidian, and it covers a hill and a mountain both — Tamil does not split them the way English does. Telugu keeps a small hill of its own, గుట్ట (*guṭṭa*).

You have been reading this word all along without being told. The name *Malayāḷam* begins with it: the language of the hill country, named for its hills.
