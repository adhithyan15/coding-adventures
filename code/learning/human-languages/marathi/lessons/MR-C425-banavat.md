## You'll want to know: बनावट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C425-TARAJU425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बनावट** — *banāvaṭ* — "fake; counterfeit".

> पोलिसांनी बनावट नोटा पकडल्या.
>
> *polisānnī banāvaṭ noṭā pakḍalyā.*
>
> The police seized fake banknotes.

It does not change: बनावट नोट, बनावट सही.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बनावट** (*banāvaṭ*) is a loan, usually traced to Hindustani *banāvaṭ*, "make, something put together".
