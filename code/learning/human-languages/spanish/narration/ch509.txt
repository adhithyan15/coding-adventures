Spanish, chapter 509: Planes y deseos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
planear — to plan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for current, then the Spanish for acceptable.

You'll want to know: planear.
planear — "to plan".
Planeamos un viaje a México. We are planning a trip to Mexico.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: planear]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: planear, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with planear]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for current, then the Spanish for acceptable, then say planear again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does planear mean? ("To plan".) Say it once more.
