## You'll want to know: вздрагивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C476-FRIGHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**вздрагивать** (*vzdrágivat*) — "to flinch".

> Собака вздрагивает от каждого звука.
>
> *Sobáka vzdrágivayet ot kázhdogo zvúka.*
>
> The dog flinches at every sound.

Imperfective; perfective вздрогнуть (once). Related to *дрожать* — to tremble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вздрагивать* is built on *вз-* and the root of *дрогнуть* 'to quiver' and *дрожать* 'to tremble'; EtymDB lists these words without a source.
