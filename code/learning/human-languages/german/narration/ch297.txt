German, chapter 297: Aufwachsen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
aufwachsen — to grow up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to baptise, then the German for the christening.

You'll want to know: aufwachsen.
aufwachsen — "to grow up".
Ich bin auf dem Land aufgewachsen. I grew up in the country.
Separable, strong, with sein: wuchs auf, ist aufgewachsen.

The word, taken apart.
aufwachsen joins auf "up" and wachsen "to grow", from Proto-Germanic wahsijaną (EtymDB), a cousin of English wax.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Einschulung — starting school, the first day at school.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the christening, then the German for to grow up.

You'll want to know: die Einschulung.
die Einschulung — "starting school, the first day at school".
Zur Einschulung bekam sie eine große Schultüte. For her first day at school she got a big school cone.
A family occasion: the child gets a Schultüte, a big paper cone full of sweets.

The word, taken apart.
Einschulung is built on Schule "school", from Old High German scuola, which came from Latin schola (EtymDB): being taken into school.

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Abitur — the school-leaving exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to grow up, then the German for starting school.

You'll want to know: das Abitur.
das Abitur — "the school-leaving exam".
Nach dem Abitur machte er ein Jahr Pause. After his school-leaving exam he took a year off.
Short: das Abi. In Austria and Switzerland: die Matura.

The word, taken apart.
Abitur is a learned formation from Latin abitūrus "about to go away", from abīre "to go away" (Lewis & Short): the exam of those about to leave school.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Abschluss — the degree, the qualification; the end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: der Abschluss.
der Abschluss — "the degree, the qualification; the end".
Sie machte ihren Abschluss an der Universität. She got her degree at the university.
Plural: die Abschlüsse. Built on abschließen.

The word, taken apart.
Abschluss is built on Schluss "end", from schließen "to close", Old High German sliozan (EtymDB): the closing of a course.

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Umzug — the move (to a new home); the parade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the school-leaving exam, then the German for the degree.

You'll want to know: der Umzug.
der Umzug — "the move (to a new home); the parade".
Der Umzug in die neue Wohnung dauerte zwei Tage. The move to the new flat took two days.
Plural: die Umzüge. Built on umziehen. At a festival, a parade.

The word, taken apart.
Umzug is built on Zug "a pull, a move", from ziehen "to pull", Proto-Germanic teuhaną (EtymDB), with um "around".
