## You'll want to know: خوشبین
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C487-HOPE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خوشبین** (*khoshbin*) — "optimistic".

> پدرم همیشه به آینده خوشبین است.
>
> *pedaram hamishe be âyande khoshbin ast.*
>
> My father is always optimistic about the future.

*khosh* 'good' + *bin* 'seeing' (stem of *didan*). What you are hopeful about takes *be*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khoshbin* is built on native Persian parts: *khosh*, 'good, pleasant', and *bin*, the present stem of *didan*, 'to see': someone who sees things well.
