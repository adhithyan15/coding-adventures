## You'll want to know: frēnō, frēnāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C431-HABENA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**frēnō, frēnāre** — "to bridle; to hold back".

> Puer equum frēnat, deinde ascendit.
>
> *The boy bridles the horse, then gets on.*

*frēnō, frēnāre, frēnāvī, frēnātum*. Also figurative: *īram frēnāre*, to hold back anger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frēnō* is built on *frēnum* 'bridle' (Lewis & Short), a regular first-conjugation verb made from the noun.
