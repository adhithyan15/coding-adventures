## You'll want to know: le reportage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C281-REPORTAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le reportage** — "the report, the feature (on TV, radio or in a paper)".

> J'ai vu un reportage sur la vie à la montagne.
>
> *I watched a feature about life in the mountains.*

*Faire un reportage* — to report on something, on location.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reportage* is built with the suffix *-age* on the verb meaning "to carry back, to report" (EtymDB), which comes from Latin *reportāre* "to carry back", built on *portāre* "to carry" (Lewis & Short).
