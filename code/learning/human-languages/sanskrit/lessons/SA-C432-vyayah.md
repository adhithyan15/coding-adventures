## You'll want to know: व्ययः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C432-VYAYA432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्ययः** — *vyayaḥ* — "an expense".

> तीर्थयात्रायां महान् व्ययः भवति।
>
> *tīrthayātrāyāṃ mahān vyayaḥ bhavati.*
>
> A pilgrimage brings a big expense.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्ययः** (*vyayaḥ*) is built from *vi*, "away", and the root √i, "to go": a going away of money; MW gives *vyaya* as spending, expense (MW).
