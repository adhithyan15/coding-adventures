## You'll want to know: வெயில்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C91-WEATHER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**வெயில்** (*veyil*) — "sunshine", the heat of the sun.

**வெயில் அதிகம்** — "the sun is strong", literally "the sunshine is much".
