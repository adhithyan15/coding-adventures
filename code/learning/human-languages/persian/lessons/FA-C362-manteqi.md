## You'll want to know: منطقی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C362-ESTEDLAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منطقی** (*manteqi*) — "logical, reasonable".

> این یک تصمیم منطقی است.
>
> *in yek tasmim-e manteqi ast.*
>
> This is a logical decision.

*Manteq* + the adjective suffix *-i*. The opposite is *gheyr-e manteqi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منطقی** comes from Arabic *manṭiqī* "logical" (EtymDB), the adjective of *manṭiq* "logic"; Persian hears it as *manteq* + *-i*.
