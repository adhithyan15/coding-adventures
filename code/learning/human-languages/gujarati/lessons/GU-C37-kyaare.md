## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-Q-KYAARE-01]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-JYAARE-01] -->

Put them side by side. This is the neatest pattern in the chapter:

> **ક્યારે** — *when?* · **જ્યારે** — *when …*

**One letter apart.** The **ક-** asks; the **જ-** answers. You learned the answer
two chapters ago without knowing it was half of a pair, and the question costs
you almost nothing now.

That pairing runs through the whole language — **ક-** for asking, **જ-** for the
clause that answers, **ત-** for the thing pointed at. **જે … તે …** was the same
system seen from the other side.
