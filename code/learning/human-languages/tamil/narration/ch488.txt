Tamil, chapter 488: Weight and Shape.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பருமன் (parumaṉ) — fatness; bulk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to digest, then the Tamil for to burp.

You'll want to know: பருமன்.
பருமன் — parumaṉ — "fatness; bulk".
உடல் பருமன் பல நோய்களுக்குக் காரணம். uṭal parumaṉ pala nōykaḷukkuk kāraṇam. Obesity is the cause of many illnesses.
உடல் பருமன் (parumaṉ), obesity.

The word, taken apart.
பருமன் (parumaṉ) is native Dravidian, built on பரு paru, "to become large, bulky" (DEDR 3972).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
