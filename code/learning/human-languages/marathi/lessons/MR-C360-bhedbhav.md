## You'll want to know: भेदभाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C360-SAMANTA360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भेदभाव** — *bhedbhāv* — "discrimination".

> आमचे शिक्षक कोणताही भेदभाव करत नाहीत.
>
> *āmce śikṣak koṇtāhī bhedbhāv karat nāhīt.*
>
> Our teachers show no discrimination at all.

Masculine.

भेदभाव करणे, to discriminate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भेदभाव** (*bhedbhāv*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *bheda*, "splitting, difference", and *bhāva*, "state, attitude" (Monier-Williams).
