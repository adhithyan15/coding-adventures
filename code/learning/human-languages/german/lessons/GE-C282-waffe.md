## You'll want to know: die Waffe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C282-UEBERFALL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Waffe** — "the weapon".

> Die Polizei fand keine Waffe.
>
> *The police found no weapon.*

Plural: *die Waffen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Waffe* is inherited from Old High German *wāfan* "weapon", from Proto-Germanic *wēpną* (EtymDB), the same source as English *weapon*.
