German, chapter 340: Kritik üben.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
beurteilen — to judge, to assess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to bring out, then the German for to set out.

You'll want to know: beurteilen.
beurteilen — "to judge, to assess".
Das kann ich nicht beurteilen, ich kenne das Buch nicht. I cannot judge that; I do not know the book.
Inseparable: er beurteilt, er hat beurteilt.

The word, taken apart.
beurteilen is built on Urteil "judgement", inherited from Old High German urteil and Proto-Germanic uzdailiją "a dealing out" (EtymDB), on the word for "part": a judge deals out to each his part.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.
kritisieren — to criticize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to assess, then the German for the assessment.

You'll want to know: kritisieren.
kritisieren — "to criticize".
Die Zeitung kritisiert den Bürgermeister wegen der hohen Preise. The newspaper criticizes the mayor because of the high prices.
Jemanden für etwas kritisieren. Perfekt: hat kritisiert, with no ge-.

The word, taken apart.
kritisieren goes with Kritik, borrowed from French critique, which goes back through Latin criticus to Greek kritikós "able to judge" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Kritik — the criticism; the review.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the assessment, then the German for to criticize.

You'll want to know: die Kritik.
die Kritik — "the criticism; the review".
Der neue Film hat gute Kritiken bekommen. The new film got good reviews.
Plural: die Kritiken. Kritik üben an + dative — to criticize.

The word, taken apart.
Kritik was borrowed from French critique (EtymDB), which goes back through Latin criticus to Greek kritikós "able to judge", from krínein "to judge".
