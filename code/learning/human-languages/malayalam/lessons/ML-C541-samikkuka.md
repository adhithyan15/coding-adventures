# ശമിക്കുക (śamikkuka) — to subside; to ease

## You'll want to know: ശമിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C541-DAHANAM541-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ശമിക്കുക** — *śamikkuka* — "to subside; to ease".

> മരുന്ന് കഴിച്ചപ്പോൾ വേദന ശമിച്ചു.
>
> *marunnŭ kaḻiccappōḷ vēdana śamiccu.*
>
> The pain eased when I took the medicine.

Past: ശമിച്ചു, *śamiccu*.

Formal; also said of rain, anger and fire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശമിക്കുക** (*śamikkuka*) is built from Sanskrit *śama*, "tranquillity, calm" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
