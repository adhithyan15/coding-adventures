## Chapter 154 joins three taught Punjabi A1 form fields

Seven sessions of at most three minutes bring the previously taught fictional
age, phone, and date fields together. Learners first rehearse label order and
one shared nonverbal selector, then move from visible-bank copying to a short
delay, separate repair passes, and a no-model three-line checkpoint. No new
personal details are requested, and this untimed slice does not claim the
complete six-field form or A1 exam readiness.
