## You'll want to know: रहस्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C340-RAHASYA340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रहस्य** — *rahasya* — "a mystery; a secret".

> त्या जुन्या वाड्याचं रहस्य कोणालाच माहीत नाही.
>
> *tyā junyā vāḍyācaṁ rahasya koṇālāc māhīt nāhī.*
>
> Nobody knows the secret of that old mansion.

Neuter.

रहस्यकथा, a mystery story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रहस्य** (*rahasya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *rahasya*, 'secret, private; a mystery' (Monier-Williams).
