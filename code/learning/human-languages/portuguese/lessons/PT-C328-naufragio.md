## You'll want to know: o naufrágio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C328-NAVEGAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o naufrágio** — "the shipwreck".

> Só dois homens sobreviveram ao naufrágio.
>
> *Only two men survived the shipwreck.*

*Naufragar* — to be shipwrecked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naufrágio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *naufragium* "a shipwreck" (EtymDB), built on *nāvis* "a ship" and *frangere* "to break".
