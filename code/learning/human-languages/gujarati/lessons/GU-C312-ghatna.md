# ઘટના (ghaṭnā) — an incident; an event

## You'll want to know: ઘટના
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C312-PATRAKAR312-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘટના** — *ghaṭnā* — "an incident; an event".

> પત્રકારે આખી ઘટના લખી.
>
> *patrakāre ākhī ghaṭnā lakhī.*
>
> The journalist wrote down the whole incident.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘટના** (*ghaṭnā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ghaṭanā*, "taking effect, a coming together" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is something that happens, the word of reports: **આખી ઘટના**, the whole incident.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
