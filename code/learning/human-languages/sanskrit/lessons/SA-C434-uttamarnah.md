## You'll want to know: उत्तमर्णः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C434-RNA434-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तमर्णः** — *uttamarṇaḥ* — "a creditor".

> उत्तमर्णः ऋणं ददाति।
>
> *uttamarṇaḥ ṛṇaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The creditor gives a loan.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तमर्णः** (*uttamarṇaḥ*) is built from *uttama*, "highest, upper", and *ṛṇa*, "a debt"; MW gives the compound as a creditor (MW).
