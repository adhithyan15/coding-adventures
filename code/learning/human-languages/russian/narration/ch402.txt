Russian, chapter 402: Staying connected.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
подключать (podklyuchát) — to connect, to plug in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to delete, then the Russian for to load.

You'll want to know: подключать.
подключать (podklyuchát) — "to connect, to plug in".
Мы подключаем интернет в новой квартире. My podklyucháyem internét v nóvoy kvartíre. We are getting the internet connected in the new flat.
Imperfective; perfective подключить. Подключаться к + dative — to connect to.

The word, taken apart.
подключать (podklyuchát) is built from под- and ключ "a key", inherited from Proto-Slavic ključĭ: to lock one thing into another.

заряжать (zaryazhát) — "to charge (a battery)".
Я заряжаю телефон каждую ночь. Ya zaryazháyu telefón kázhduyu noch. I charge my phone every night.
Imperfective; perfective зарядить. Зарядка is a charger, and also morning exercises.

The word, taken apart.
заряжать (zaryazhát) is built from за- and the root of ряд "a row, an order", as in снаряжать "to equip": first to load a gun, now to charge a battery.

Lesson 3 of 7.
переписываться (perepísyvatsya) — to correspond, to message each other.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to connect, then the Russian for to charge.

You'll want to know: переписываться.
переписываться (perepísyvatsya) — "to correspond, to message each other".
Мы переписываемся с другом уже пять лет. My perepísyvayemsya s drúgom uzhé pyat let. My friend and I have been corresponding for five years.
Imperfective; perfective переписаться. Переписываться с + instrumental. Without -ся, переписывать means to copy out.

The word, taken apart.
переписываться (perepísyvatsya) is built from пере- "across, back and forth" and писать "to write", inherited from Proto-Slavic pisati "to write; to paint", from PIE peyḱ- "to paint, to colour" (EtymDB): letters going back and forth.

Before the new one: say the Russian for to correspond, then the Russian for to forward.

You'll want to know: устройство.
устройство (ustróystvo) — "a device; the structure, the set-up".
Это устройство работает без интернета. Éto ustróystvo rabótayet bez internéta. This device works without the internet.
Neuter. Мобильное устройство (ustróystvo) — a mobile device. Also the way a thing is arranged: устройство (ustróystvo) общества.

The word, taken apart.
устройство (ustróystvo) is built on устроить "to arrange, to set up", from строить "to build", on the old Slavic root stroji "order, arrangement".
