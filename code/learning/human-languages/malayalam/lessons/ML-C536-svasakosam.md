# ശ്വാസകോശം (śvāsakōśaṁ) — the lungs; a lung

## You'll want to know: ശ്വാസകോശം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C536-RAKTAM536-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ശ്വാസകോശം** — *śvāsakōśaṁ* — "the lungs; a lung".

> പുകവലി ശ്വാസകോശത്തിന് ദോഷമാണ്.
>
> *pukavali śvāsakōśattinŭ dōṣamāṇŭ.*
>
> Smoking is bad for the lungs.

ശ്വാസം breath + കോശം a vessel, a store.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശ്വാസകോശം** (*śvāsakōśaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *śvāsa*, "breath" (CDIAL 12769), and *kośa*, "a cask, a vessel" (Monier-Williams): the store of breath.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
