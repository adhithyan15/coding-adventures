## You'll want to know: ಪದವಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C406-VISHVAVIDYALAYA406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪದವಿ** — *padavi* — "a degree; a rank".

> ಅವನು ಕಳೆದ ವರ್ಷ ಪದವಿ ಮುಗಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *avanu kaḷeda varṣa padavi mugisidanu.*
>
> He finished his degree last year.

Also a post or rank: ಉನ್ನತ ಪದವಿ, a high post.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪದವಿ** (*padavi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *padavī*, "a path; a station, a position" (Monier-Williams).
