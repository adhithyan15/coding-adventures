## You'll want to know: विमृशति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C541-DVIDHA541-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विमृशति** — *vimṛśati* — "weighs up".

> यदि त्वं विमृशसि, तर्हि उचितः मार्गः स्पष्टः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ vimṛśasi, tarhi ucitaḥ mārgaḥ spaṣṭaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you think it over, the right way will become clear.

Past (he …): सः चिरं विमृष्टवान्, *saḥ ciraṃ vimṛṣṭavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विमृशति** (*vimṛśati*) is built on *vi*, "apart", and the root √mṛś, "to touch": to touch with the mind; MW gives *vimṛś* as to consider, to reflect on, to deliberate; its past participle is *vimṛṣṭa* (MW).
