## You'll want to know: きろく

**きろく** — *kiroku* — "a record; an account".

> **むかしの きろくが のこっています。** — *mukashi no kiroku ga nokotte imasu* — "Accounts from long ago survive."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きろく** (*kiroku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "account" (Middle Chinese *kiH*) and *roku*, "record" (*ljowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
