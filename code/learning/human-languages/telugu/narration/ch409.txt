Telugu, chapter 409: Living Together.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
హింస (hiṁsa) — violence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a member, then the Telugu for discrimination.

You'll want to know: హింస.
హింస — hiṁsa — "violence".
హింస ఏ సమస్యకూ పరిష్కారం కాదు. hiṁsa ē samasyakū pariṣkāraṁ kādu. Violence is no solution to any problem.
అహింస, non-violence.

The word, taken apart.
హింస (hiṁsa) is borrowed from Sanskrit hiṁsā, "injury, harm, hurt" (Monier-Williams).

రైతులకు విద్యార్థులు సంఘీభావం తెలిపారు. raitulaku vidyārthulu saṅghībhāvaṁ telipāru. The students expressed solidarity with the farmers.

అతను చెప్పిన మాటను తానే ఆచరించాడు. atanu ceppina māṭanu tānē ācariñcāḍu. He practised what he preached.
Past (I …): ఆచరించాను, ācariñcānu.
For customs, rules and advice: ఆచారం, a custom, which you know, is the same family.

The word, taken apart.
ఆచరించు (ācariñcu) is built on Sanskrit ācaraṇa, "undertaking, practising, performing; conduct" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
