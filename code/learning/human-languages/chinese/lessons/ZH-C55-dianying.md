## You'll want to know: diànyǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C55-LIKE55-03]; assesses=[] -->

*diànyǐng* — "film; movie".

> *wǒ xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng* — I like going to the cinema.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diànyǐng* is built from diàn 'lightning', now 'electricity', and yǐng 'shadow', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *'jaengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): electric shadows.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
