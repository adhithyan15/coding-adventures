## You'll want to know: সিংহাসন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C313-RAJYO313-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সিংহাসন** — *singhāsôn* — "a throne".

> নতুন রাজা সিংহাসনে বসলেন।
>
> *nôtun rājā singhāsône bôslen.*
>
> The new king took the throne.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সিংহাসন** (*singhāsôn*) is Sanskrit *siṃhāsana*, 'a lion's seat, a king's seat' (Monier-Williams): **সিংহ**, a lion, and **আসন**, a seat.
