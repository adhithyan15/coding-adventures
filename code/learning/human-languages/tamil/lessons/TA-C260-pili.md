**பிழி** — *piḻi* — "to squeeze, to wring out".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பிழி** (*piḻi*) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4183 lists Malayalam *piḻiyuka*, "to wring out" and Kannada *piḻi*.
