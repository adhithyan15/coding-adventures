## You'll want to know: شرارة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C354-HUTAM354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شرارة** — *sharāra* — "a spark".

> بدأ الحريق من شرارة صغيرة.
>
> *badaʾa al-ḥarīq min sharāra ṣaghīra.*
>
> The fire started from a small spark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شرارة** (*sharāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-r* (Lane), where Lane gives *sharar* as sparks flying from a fire, beside *sharr*, evil.
