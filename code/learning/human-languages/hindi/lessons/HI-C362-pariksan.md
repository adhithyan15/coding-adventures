## You'll want to know: परीक्षण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C362-NISHKARSH362-05]; assesses=[] -->

**परीक्षण** — *parīkṣaṇ* — "a test, a trial (in research)".

> नई दवा का परीक्षण चल रहा है।
>
> *naī davā kā parīkṣaṇ cal rahā hai.*
>
> The new medicine is being tested.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परीक्षण** (*parīkṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *parīkṣaṇa*, "trying, testing" (Monier-Williams).
