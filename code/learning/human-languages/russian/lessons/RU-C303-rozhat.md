## You'll want to know: рожать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C303-ROZHDENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**рожать** (*rozhát*) — "to give birth".

> Она родила дочку в марте.
>
> *Oná rodilá dóchku v márte.*
>
> She gave birth to a daughter in March.

Imperfective; perfective *родить* (*rodít*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рожать* is inherited through Old East Slavic *ražati* from Proto-Slavic *radjati* (EtymDB), the imperfective partner of *roditi* "to give birth".
