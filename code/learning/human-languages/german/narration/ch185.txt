Lesson 1 of 5.
retten — to rescue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to test, then the German for to clean.

You'll want to know: retten.
retten — "to rescue".

The word, taken apart.
retten from Middle High German retten from Old High German retten — inherited; EtymDB 2.1 lists it beside English rid and Dutch redden. To rescue is to rid someone of danger.

You'll want to know: schlagen.
schlagen — "to hit, to beat".

The word, taken apart.
schlagen goes back to Old High German slahan from Proto-Germanic slahaną, "to strike, to fight" — inherited. EtymDB 2.1 has the Proto-Germanic verb; English slay and slaughter share it.
