## You'll want to know: ਸੱਸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C329-INLAW329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਸ** — *sass* — "a mother-in-law".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਸੱਸ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗਾ ਖਾਣਾ ਪਕਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī sass bahut changā khāṇā pakāundī hai.*
>
> My mother-in-law cooks very good food.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਸ** (*sass*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śvaśrū*, "a husband's or wife's mother"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sass*, "husband's or wife's mother", under it (CDIAL 12759).
