Punjabi, chapter 304: Weeding and Ripening.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਖੁਰਪਾ (khurpā) — a weeding hoe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a farmer, then the Punjabi for a Persian wheel.

You'll want to know: ਖੁਰਪਾ.
ਖੁਰਪਾ — khurpā — "a weeding hoe".
ਮੈਂ ਖੁਰਪੇ ਨਾਲ ਘਾਹ ਕੱਟਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ khurpe nāl ghāh kaṭṭdā hāṁ. I cut the grass with a weeding hoe.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੁਰਪਾ (khurpā) is inherited from Sanskrit kṣurapra, "a sort of hoe"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khurpā, "grubber for grass", under it (CDIAL 3730).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਗੋਡਣਾ (goḍṇā) — to hoe; to weed with a hoe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a Persian wheel, then the Punjabi for a weeding hoe.

You'll want to know: ਗੋਡਣਾ.
ਗੋਡਣਾ — goḍṇā — "to hoe; to weed with a hoe".
ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਮੱਕੀ ਗੋਡਾਂਗੇ। asīṁ agle hafte makkī goḍāṅge. We will hoe the maize next week.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਪਾਹ ਗੋਡੀ, maiṁ kapāh goḍī.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੋਡਣਾ (goḍṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed khōdd, "dig", in its form gōḍḍ; CDIAL lists Punjabi goḍṇā and goḍḍī, "hoeings", under it, and Lahnda goḍaṇ, "to hoe" (CDIAL 3934).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਪੱਕਣਾ (pakkṇā) — to ripen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a weeding hoe, then the Punjabi for to hoe.

You'll want to know: ਪੱਕਣਾ.
ਪੱਕਣਾ — pakkṇā — "to ripen".
ਵਿਸਾਖ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਣਕ ਪੱਕੇਗੀ। visākh vich kaṇak pakkegī. The wheat will ripen in Visakh.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਕੇਲਾ ਪੱਕਿਆ, oh kelā pakkiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੱਕਣਾ (pakkṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit pakva, "cooked, ripe"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pakkṇā, "to ripen", under it (CDIAL 7621).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਦਾਣਾ (dāṇā) — a grain; a seed of grain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to ripen, then the Punjabi for an ear of grain.

You'll want to know: ਦਾਣਾ.
ਦਾਣਾ — dāṇā — "a grain; a seed of grain".
ਹਰ ਸਿੱਟੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਦਾਣੇ ਹਨ। har siṭṭe vich bahut dāṇe han. There are many grains in every ear.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਦਾਣਾ (dāṇā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian dāna, "grain, seed".
