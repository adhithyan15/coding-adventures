## You'll want to know: साथ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C484-ROG484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**साथ** — *sāth* — "an epidemic".

> पावसाळ्यात गावात तापाची साथ आली.
>
> *pāvsāḷyāt gāvāt tāpācī sāth ālī.*
>
> In the monsoon a fever epidemic came to the village.

Feminine.

साथीचा रोग, an infectious disease. A second साथ means company: साथ देणे, to stand by someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साथ** (*sāth*) is inherited in the sense "company": CDIAL lists Marathi *sāth*, "companionship", under Sanskrit *sārtha*, "caravan, company" (CDIAL 13364). Whether the epidemic sense is the same word is not settled.
