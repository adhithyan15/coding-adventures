Hindi, chapter 125: Calm and Restless.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शांत (śānt) — calm, peaceful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for sweat, then the Hindi for a headache.

You'll want to know: शांत.
शांत (śānt) — "calm, peaceful", of a person or a place.
शांत (śānt) रहिए — "please stay calm".

What you've built.
Calm.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: śānt]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: śānt, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say śānt rahie]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for sweat, then the Hindi for a headache, then say śānt again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does शांत (śānt) mean? ("Calm, peaceful".) Say it once more.

घबराहट (ghabrāhaṭ) — "nervousness", a flutter of panic.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बेचैन (becain) — restless, uneasy.

You'll want to know: बेचैन.
बेचैन (becain) — "restless, uneasy".
बे- is "without", and चैन is "peace": restless is without peace.

What you've built.
A word that shows its parts.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मन नहीं है (man nahīṁ hai) — I don't feel like it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for nervousness, then the Hindi for restless.

You'll want to know: मन नहीं है.
मन नहीं है (man nahīṁ hai) — "I don't feel like it", literally "there is no mind for it".
मन is "mind, heart". आज खाने का मन नहीं है (man nahīṁ hai) — "I don't feel like eating today".

What you've built.
A gentle refusal.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does मन नहीं है (man nahīṁ hai) mean? ("I don't feel like it".) Say it once more.


Lesson 5 of 5.
मज़ा (mazā) — fun, enjoyment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for restless, then the Hindi for I don't feel like it.

You'll want to know: मज़ा.
मज़ा (mazā) — "fun, enjoyment".
मज़ा (mazā) आया — "I enjoyed it", literally "fun came". It comes to you, like laughter.

What you've built.
Calm, nervous, restless, not in the mood, and fun.
