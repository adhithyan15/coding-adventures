## You'll want to know: કૃપણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C461-KRUPAN461-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કૃપણ** — *kṛpaṇ* — "miserly".

> મારા કાકા ઘણા કૃપણ છે.
>
> *mārā kākā ghaṇā kṛpaṇ che.*
>
> My uncle is very miserly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કૃપણ** (*kṛpaṇ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kṛpaṇa*, as Monier-Williams records it; its older senses are "pitiable, miserable", and the sense "miserly" grew from them.
