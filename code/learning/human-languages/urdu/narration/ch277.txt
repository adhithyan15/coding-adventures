Urdu, chapter 277: The Thief Got Away.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جیب کترا (jeb katrā) — a pickpocket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a witness, then the Urdu for a crime.

You'll want to know: جیب کترا.
جیب کترا — jeb katrā — "a pickpocket".
بس میں ایک جیب کترا تھا۔ bas meṅ ek jeb katrā thā. There was a pickpocket on the bus.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
جیب کترا (jeb katrā) is built from جیب, a pocket, usually traced to Persian jēb, and katrā, one who snips, from katarnā, "to clip", which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit kartarī, "scissors" (CDIAL 2858): the one who cuts into pockets.

Lesson 2 of 5.
چھیننا (chhīnnā) — to snatch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a crime, then the Urdu for a pickpocket.

You'll want to know: چھیننا.
چھیننا — chhīnnā — "to snatch".
اس نے میرا موبائل چھینا۔ us ne merā mobāil chhīnā. He snatched my mobile.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے تھیلا چھینا, us ne thailā chhīnā.

The word, taken apart.
چھیننا (chhīnnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin chīnnā, "to snatch, to pluck", under Sanskrit chinná, "cut off" (CDIAL 5047). It is taking by force, in one quick pull.

Lesson 3 of 5.
جھپٹنا (jhapaṭnā) — to swoop; to pounce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a pickpocket, then the Urdu for to snatch.

You'll want to know: جھپٹنا.
جھپٹنا — jhapaṭnā — "to swoop; to pounce".
چور تھیلے پر جھپٹا۔ chor thaile par jhapaṭā. The thief swooped on the bag.
Past (he …): وہ تھیلے پر جھپٹا, voh thaile par jhapaṭā.

The word, taken apart.
جھپٹنا (jhapaṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jhapaṭnā, "to run quickly", under jhapp, a jerk, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5336); in Urdu it is the sudden rush of a hawk or a thief: تھیلے پر جھپٹا.

Lesson 4 of 5.
فرار (farār) — on the run; escaped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to snatch, then the Urdu for to swoop.

You'll want to know: فرار.
فرار — farār — "on the run; escaped".
چور ابھی تک فرار ہے۔ chor abhī tak farār hai. The thief is still on the run.

The word, taken apart.
فرار (farār) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic firār, "flight, running away". فرار ہے is still at large; فرار ہو گیا has got away.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مجرم (mujrim) — a criminal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to swoop, then the Urdu for on the run.

You'll want to know: مجرم.
مجرم — mujrim — "a criminal".
اگر پولیس ہوتی تو مجرم نہ بھاگتا۔ agar polīs hotī to mujrim na bhāgtā. If the police had been there, the criminal would not have run off.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
مجرم (mujrim) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mujrim, "one who has done wrong". It is the word of the courts and the papers for anyone who broke the law.
