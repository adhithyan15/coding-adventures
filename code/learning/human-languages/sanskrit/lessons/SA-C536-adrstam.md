## You'll want to know: अदृष्टम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C536-ADRSTA536-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अदृष्टम्** — *adṛṣṭam* — "the unseen; fate".

> यदि अदृष्टं साहाय्यं करोति, तर्हि सः जयति।
>
> *yadi adṛṣṭaṃ sāhāyyaṃ karoti, tarhi saḥ jayati.*
>
> If fate helps him, he wins.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अदृष्टम्** (*adṛṣṭam*) is built on *a*, "not", and *dṛṣṭa*, "seen", from the root √dṛś, "to see": the unseen; MW gives *adṛṣṭa* as what is beyond observation, the merit or demerit of one's deeds, fate (MW).
