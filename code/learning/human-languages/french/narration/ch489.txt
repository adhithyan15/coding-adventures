French, chapter 489: Fiançailles et noces.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
les fiançailles — engagement (to marry).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to whisper, then the French for to entrust.

You'll want to know: les fiançailles.
les fiançailles — "engagement (to marry)".
Ils ont fêté leurs fiançailles avec toute la famille. They celebrated their engagement with the whole family.
Always plural and feminine: des fiançailles.

The word, taken apart.
les fiançailles is built on fiancer "to promise in marriage", from old fiance "trust, promise", which goes back to Vulgar Latin fīdāre, from Latin fīdere "to trust" (Lewis & Short).

The word, taken apart.
l'alliance is built on allier "to unite", inherited from Latin alligāre "to bind to", built on ligāre "to tie" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 6.
les noces — wedding (celebration).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for engagement, then the French for wedding ring.

You'll want to know: les noces.
les noces — "wedding (celebration)".
Mes grands-parents ont fêté leurs noces d'or. My grandparents celebrated their golden wedding anniversary.
Usually plural. Le voyage de noces — the honeymoon trip.

The word, taken apart.
les noces is inherited from Vulgar Latin noptiae, a form of Latin nūptiae "a wedding", from nūbere "to marry" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 6.
la lune de miel — honeymoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for wedding ring, then the French for wedding.

You'll want to know: la lune de miel.
la lune de miel — "honeymoon".
Pour leur lune de miel, ils sont partis au bord de la mer. For their honeymoon they went to the seaside.
Built on the same picture as English honeymoon.

The word, taken apart.
la lune de miel translates English honeymoon word for word: lune is inherited from Latin lūna "moon" and miel from Latin mel "honey" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 6.

You'll want to know: la cérémonie.
la cérémonie — "ceremony".
La cérémonie a eu lieu dans un petit jardin. The ceremony took place in a small garden.
Sans cérémonie — informally.

The word, taken apart.
la cérémonie is a learned borrowing from Latin caerimōnia "a sacred rite, reverence" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
