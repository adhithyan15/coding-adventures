## You'll want to know: заказчик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C363-PEREGOVORY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**заказчик** (*zakázchik*) — "a client (who orders work)".

> Заказчик недоволен нашей работой.
>
> *Zakázchik nedovólen náshey rabótoy.*
>
> The client is unhappy with our work.

Masculine. The one who orders a job or a product, from *заказывать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**заказчик** "buyer, client" (EtymDB) is built on *заказывать* "to order", whose root is *казать* "to show, to say", Proto-Slavic *\*kazati* (EtymDB).
