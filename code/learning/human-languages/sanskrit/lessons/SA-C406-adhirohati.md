## You'll want to know: अधिरोहति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C406-ADHIROHA406-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिरोहति** — *adhirohati* — "gets on; mounts".

> यात्री लोकयानम् अधिरोहति।
>
> *yātrī lokayānam adhirohati.*
>
> The passenger gets on the bus.

Past (he …): सः लोकयानम् अधिरूढवान्, *saḥ lokayānam adhirūḍhavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिरोहति** (*adhirohati*) is built from *adhi*, "over, onto", and the root √ruh, "to rise", which also gives *rohati*, "grows"; MW gives *adhi-ruh* as to rise above, ascend or mount, and getting on a bus or a train is that sense in modern use (MW).
