## You'll want to know: মুকুট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C313-RAJYO313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**মুকুট** — *mukuṭ* — "a crown".

> রানির মুকুট ঝলমল করছিল।
>
> *rānir mukuṭ jhôlmôl kôrchhilô.*
>
> The queen's crown was glittering.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মুকুট** (*mukuṭ*) is Sanskrit *mukuṭa*, 'a tiara, a diadem, a crown' (Monier-Williams).
