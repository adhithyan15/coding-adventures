## You'll want to know: गोलंदाज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C531-KHELADU531-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गोलंदाज** — *golandāj* — "a bowler".

> गोलंदाजाने खूप वेगाने चेंडू टाकला.
>
> *golandājāne khūp vegāne ceṇḍū ṭāklā.*
>
> The bowler bowled the ball very fast.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोलंदाज** (*golandāj*) is a loan from Persian *golandāz*, "gunner", from *gola*, "a ball", and *andāz*, "thrower".
