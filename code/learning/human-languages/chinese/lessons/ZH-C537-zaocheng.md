## You'll want to know: zàochéng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C537-TURNS537-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zàochéng* — "to cause damage or trouble".

> *rúguǒ nǐ bù shuō jiù huì zàochéng wùhuì* — If you don't say anything, it will cause a misunderstanding.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zàochéng* is built from *zào* 'to make' and 成 'to complete', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzawX* and *dzyeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to make'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
