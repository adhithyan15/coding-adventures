Gujarati, chapter 152: Reading a note about a repair.
6 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ચિઠ્ઠી (ciṭṭhī) — a note.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say a posted notice, then the shop is open only in the morning.

You'll want to know: ચિઠ્ઠી.
ચિઠ્ઠી (ciṭṭhī) — "a note, a short written message".
A notice is for everyone; a ચિઠ્ઠી (ciṭṭhī) is for one person: a line left under a door, a message from a neighbour.
મેં તેને ચિઠ્ઠી લખી. — mẽ tene ciṭṭhī lakhī. — I wrote her a note. ચિઠ્ઠી ક્યાં છે? — ciṭṭhī kyā̃ che? — Where is the note?
લખી ends in -ી because ચિઠ્ઠી (ciṭṭhī) is feminine. The ઠ્ઠ in the middle is ઠ twice, joined by a virama: a doubled, breathy, tongue-curled ṭṭh.

The word, taken apart.
ચિઠ્ઠી (ciṭṭhī) is inherited. CDIAL reconstructs an Indo-Aryan ciṣṭa, "message", and lists Gujarati ciṭṭhī, "message", under it, beside Hindi ciṭṭhī, "letter" (CDIAL 4832).

What you've built.
One more word for the notes people leave for each other.

[your turn — say: ciṭṭhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mẽ tene ciṭṭhī lakhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ciṭṭhī kyā̃ che?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 2 of 6.
મરામત (marāmat) — a repair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say I wrote her a note, then ask where is the note?

You'll want to know: મરામત.
મરામત (marāmat) — "a repair, repair work".
It is the job of putting something right: a window, a door, a road.
આવતીકાલે મરામત થશે. — āvtīkāle marāmat thaśe. — The repair will be done tomorrow. તે મરામત કરશે. — te marāmat karśe. — He will do the repair.
થશે and કરશે split the work the way English be done and do do: થશે when the repair happens, કરશે when someone does it.

The word, taken apart.
મરામત (marāmat) came into Gujarati through Persian from Arabic maramma, "a repairing". Its root is r-m-m, "to repair, to put into a sound state", said in Lane of a worn rope, a house or a wall (Lane).

What you've built.
One more word for the notes people leave for each other.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: āvtīkāle marāmat thaśe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 3 of 6.
ભાડું (bhāḍũ) — rent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the repair will be done tomorrow, then he will do the repair.

You'll want to know: ભાડું.
ભાડું (bhāḍũ) — "rent, a fare".
It is the money paid for using something that is not yours: a room, a house, a seat on a bus.
ભાડું કેટલું છે? — bhāḍũ keṭlũ che? — How much is the rent? How much is the fare? મેં ભાડું આપ્યું. — mẽ bhāḍũ āpyũ. — I paid the rent.
ભાડું (bhāḍũ) is neuter, so આપ્યું.

The word, taken apart.
ભાડું (bhāḍũ) is inherited. CDIAL lists the Gujarati word under a reconstructed bhārta, "pertaining to servants", which Middle Indo-Aryan continued as bhāṭa, "wages" (CDIAL 9468). Pay for a servant's work became pay for a room.

What you've built.
One more word for the notes people leave for each other.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
