## You'll want to know: ম্যাচ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C519-KHELA519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ম্যাচ** — *mæch* — "a match (a game)".

> রবিবার আমাদের ফুটবল ম্যাচ।
>
> *Rôbibār āmāder phuṭbôl mæch.*
>
> We have a football match on Sunday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ম্যাচ** (*mæch*) is borrowed from English *match*.
