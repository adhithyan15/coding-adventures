Lesson 1 of 5.
l'impôt — a tax.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the truth, then the French for a lie.

You'll want to know: l'impôt.
l'impôt — "a tax".

The word, taken apart.
impôt from Middle French impost from Medieval Latin impositum, "a thing laid on", from imponere (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing; the circumflex marks the s French later stopped writing.
