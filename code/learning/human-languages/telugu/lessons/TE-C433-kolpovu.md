## You'll want to know: కోల్పోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C433-VYUHAM433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**కోల్పోవు** — *kōlpōvu* — "to be deprived of; to lose".

> వరదల్లో వాళ్ళు తమ ఇల్లు కోల్పోయారు.
>
> *varadallō vāḷḷu tama illu kōlpōyāru.*
>
> They lost their home in the floods.

Past (I …): కోల్పోయాను, *kōlpōyānu*.

For losing what you had: a chance, a right, a home, a person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కోల్పోవు** (*kōlpōvu*) is native Telugu, ending in *pōvu*, "to go, to be lost" (DEDR 4572); the origin of the first part is uncertain.
