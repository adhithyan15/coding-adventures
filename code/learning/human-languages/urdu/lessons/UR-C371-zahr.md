## You'll want to know: زہر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C371-BUHRAN371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**زہر** — *zahr* — "poison".

> اگر کتے نے زہر کھایا تو وہ مر جائے گا۔
>
> *agar kutte ne zahr khāyā to voh mar jāe gā.*
>
> If the dog eats poison, it will die.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زہر** (*zahr*) is usually traced to Persian *zahr*, "poison".
