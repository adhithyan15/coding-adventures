## You'll want to know: उधळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C428-SHILLAK428-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उधळणे** — *udhaḷṇe* — "to squander".

> त्याने सगळा पगार एका आठवड्यात उधळला.
>
> *tyāne sagḷā pagār ekā āṭhvaḍyāt udhaḷlā.*
>
> He squandered his whole salary in one week.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने उधळलं, *tyāne udhaḷlaṁ*.

Also to scatter: फुलं उधळणे, to shower flowers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उधळणे** (*udhaḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *udhaḷṇẽ*, "to scatter; to spend lavishly", under Sanskrit *uddhūlayati*, "sprinkles with powder" (CDIAL 2027).
