## You'll want to know: कावीळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C485-MADHUMEH485-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कावीळ** — *kāvīḷ* — "jaundice".

> काविळीत डोळे पिवळे दिसतात.
>
> *kāviḷīt ḍoḷe pivḷe distāt.*
>
> In jaundice the eyes look yellow.

Feminine.

Feminine; oblique काविळी-: काविळीत, in jaundice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कावीळ** (*kāvīḷ*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *kāmalā*; CDIAL lists Marathi *kāvīḷ*, "jaundice" (CDIAL 3044).
