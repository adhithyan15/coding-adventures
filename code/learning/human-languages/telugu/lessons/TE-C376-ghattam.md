# ఘట్టం (ghaṭṭaṁ) — an episode; a phase

## You'll want to know: ఘట్టం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C376-SPANDANA376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఘట్టం** — *ghaṭṭaṁ* — "an episode; a phase".

> కథలో ఆ ఘట్టం నాకు బాగా గుర్తుంది.
>
> *kathalō ā ghaṭṭaṁ nāku bāgā gurtundi.*
>
> I remember that episode of the story well.

Plural: ఘట్టాలు, *ghaṭṭālu*.

Also a landing place; in a story, a stage of it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఘట్టం** (*ghaṭṭaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ghaṭṭa*, "a landing place, a quay" (CDIAL 4414); CDIAL gives its basic sense as a passage.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
