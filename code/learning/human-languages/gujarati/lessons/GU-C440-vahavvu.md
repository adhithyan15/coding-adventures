# વહાવવું (vahāvvũ) — to let flow (water); to channel

## You'll want to know: વહાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C440-STREAM440-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વહાવવું** — *vahāvvũ* — "to let flow (water); to channel".

> અમે નહેરનું પાણી ખેતરમાં વહાવ્યું.
>
> *ame nahernũ pāṇī khetarmā̃ vahāvyũ.*
>
> We let the canal water flow into the field.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પાણી વહાવ્યું, *teṇe pāṇī vahāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વહાવવું** (*vahāvvũ*) is the causative partner of વહેવું, "to flow", a tadbhava of Sanskrit *vahati*, "carries" (CDIAL 11453): to make water flow.
