## You'll want to know: عزم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C378-HADAF378-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عزم** — *azm* — "a resolve; determination".

> اگر عزم پختہ ہے تو کوئی کام مشکل نہیں۔
>
> *agar azm pukhtā hai to koī kām mushkil nahīṅ.*
>
> If your resolve is firm, no task is hard.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عزم** (*azm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿazm*, "resolve, determination".
