## Added — Marathi B1 vocabulary, 1,350 words in chapters 288-557; Marathi attains B1

- Added chapters 288-557: 1,350 B1 headwords (408 verbs), one word per
  lesson, with eight reviews in each of five tranches (40 in all; 1,390
  lessons). Each lesson gives the word, its romanization (the track's ISO
  15919 letters, with the inherent a as the word is said), an example with
  romanization and translation, a note (the case a verb takes, the gender
  where it trips learners, a light-verb pair such as चोरी होणे / चोरी करणे)
  and an origin citing CDIAL (inherited words), Monier-Williams (tatsamas
  CDIAL lacks), a named donor for a loan (Persian, Arabic, English,
  Portuguese), or hedged. Tranches sit on the B1 nodes: narrating (288-341),
  reasons and conditions (342-395), travel (396-449), experience (450-503),
  and work, the home, free time, then plans and risks (504-557).
- Verbs are given as the -णे infinitive the A2 chapters use, with the 3rd
  person past: तो बुडाला "he sank" for an intransitive verb, त्याने लुटलं "he
  looted" with the ergative -ने for a transitive one, and the dative for a
  dative-subject verb.
- The first words of chapters 288-295 practise the core's last grammar points
  (… -वर अवलंबून आहे, the past + असतो, जर … तर and -ल्यास), two words each;
  the first words of chapters 302 and 319 bring back ऋ through ऋतू, and of
  305 and 322 ञ through ज्ञान. No atom of the track is revisited fewer than
  twice.
- With the B1 core, Marathi teaches 2,572 headwords at or below B1 and
  attains B1 on the level gate. Forward references stay at the pinned 4, none
  in chapters 276 or later; root-slug splits add none;
  data/scripts/devanagari.json and data/ductus are unchanged.
- Pins: lessons 1519 → 2909 (no idiom, sense or culture claim is added) and
  the per-chapter sizes for 288-557.
