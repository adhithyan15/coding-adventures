## You'll want to know: धारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C443-NAHAR443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धारा** — *dhārā* — "a current; a stream".

> नदी की धारा बहुत तेज़ थी।
>
> *nadī kī dhārā bahut tez thī.*
>
> The river's current was very strong.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धारा** (*dhārā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dhārā*, "a stream or current of water" (Monier-Williams). In law and in the news it is also a section of an act.
