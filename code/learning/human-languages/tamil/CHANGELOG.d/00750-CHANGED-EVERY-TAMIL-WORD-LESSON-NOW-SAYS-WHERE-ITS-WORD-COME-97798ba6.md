## Changed — every Tamil word lesson now says where its word comes from

All 1,200 Tamil word lessons that lacked an etymology now carry one (HL24 §9). Before this change, 1,185 had no `etymology_hook` and 1,144 had no visible etymology. Now no lesson lacks a hook, and four lack a visible block (see below).

- **Shape.** Each lesson has a `## The word, taken apart` block (or `## The phrase, taken apart`) with `<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->`, placed after the block that introduces the word, and a matching `etymology_hook` of 120 characters or fewer. TA-C05-practice, a recap of four verbs, has a `## The words, taken apart` block. 56 lessons in chapters 1–66 that already stated an origin in their body gained only the hook. Sixteen lessons whose hook was not etymology (for example a usage note) got a new hook, and twelve of them also got a block.
- **Sources.** Native words cite the *Dravidian Etymological Dictionary* (Burrow & Emeneau, DEDR) by entry number, for example "(DEDR 4411)". The Indo-Aryan loans that DEDR lists in its appendix are cited as "DEDR App. N". Sanskrit loans cite Turner's *Comparative Dictionary of the Indo-Aryan Languages* (CDIAL) by entry number. Both were read locally from the moli-mandala/data (Jambu) files, and every number was checked against those files. No source text is copied: each statement is our own sentence. A cognate is named only when the cited DEDR entry lists it. Kannada, Telugu and Malayalam forms are given in transliteration.
- **Tallies (1,200 lessons).** 876 native Dravidian; 158 Sanskrit or Indo-Aryan loans; 43 other loans, each naming its donor (English; Portuguese, such as *mēcai* from *mesa* and *cāvi* from *chave*; Persian and Arabic through Hindustani, such as *nakal* from Arabic *naql*); 65 built from parts the learner already knows; 58 of uncertain origin. The uncertain ones give the usual account with a hedge, or say that DEDR has no entry. Examples: *cantai*, *tiṭṭam*, *karumpu*, and *sari*, which is usually traced to Sanskrit *sadṛśa* through Prakrit (CDIAL 13120). The added text cites DEDR 2,430 times (639 distinct entries) and CDIAL 352 times (157 distinct entries).
- **Rejected rows.** A same-spelling DEDR entry was never accepted on spelling alone. Rejected examples: DEDR 697 *uḷ* "to be" for *uḷ* "inside" (DEDR 698 is the right entry); DEDR 213 *ari* "to sift" for *arippu* "itch" (DEDR 214); DEDR 3544 *tōṭu* "shell" for *tōṭu* "earring" (DEDR 3545); DEDR 1373 *kaḷai* "to weed" for *kaḷai* "to be weary"; DEDR 3569 *nakal* "laughing" for *nakal* "copy". Cognates that a DEDR entry does not list were removed.
- **Corrections to existing text.** TA-C01-sari no longer calls *sari* "native Tamil"; its origin is uncertain. TA-C03-paravayillai now says *paravāy* is Persian *parvā*, "care", which reached Tamil through Hindustani. It also says that DEDR 2559 files Telugu *lēdu* with the other negatives, instead of saying Telugu "breaks away". TA-C24-night-register-darkness no longer says *iruḷ* and *iravu* come from different roots: DEDR 2552 files them in one entry. The wrap-up answer was changed to match.
- **Four lessons with no block.** TA-C06-dative-subject, TA-C37-ivar, TA-C37-ivar-en-nanbar and TA-C39-evvalavu already estimate at 294–299 seconds. Any visible block would push them past the 300-second limit, so only their hooks state the etymology.
- **Roots.** 1,148 lessons gained `roots:` slugs: 872 distinct slugs, of which 91 already existed in some track and 781 are new. `check:root-slug-splits` reports no new split.
- **Durations.** 19 lessons had `duration.max_seconds` raised to their computed length. All are under 300 s: TA-C01-answering 120→282, TA-C01-sari 120→203, TA-C108-howareyou 200→204, TA-C16-thamizh-maadangal 267→288, TA-C20-pathinondru-irupathu 248→276, TA-C29-kaalai-vanakkam 240→247, TA-C30-maalai-vanakkam 265→288, TA-C32-po 240→262, TA-C32-vaa 240→248, TA-C37-avar 240→273, TA-C39-vendum 285→296, TA-C75-permission 235→255, TA-C76-choice 230→281, TA-C78-why 215→230, TA-C80-price 230→276, TA-C82-first 230→237, TA-C86-ai 240→288, TA-C86-rational 240→280, TA-C93-howsay 200→206.
