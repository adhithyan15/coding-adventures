Sanskrit, chapter 492: A New Baby.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गर्भिणी (garbhiṇī) — a pregnant woman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for funeral rites, then the Sanskrit for a rite for the ancestors.

You'll want to know: गर्भिणी.
गर्भिणी — garbhiṇī — "a pregnant woman".
गर्भिणी पथ्यं भोजनं खादति। garbhiṇī pathyaṃ bhojanaṃ khādati. The pregnant woman eats wholesome food.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गर्भिणी (garbhiṇī) is built on garbha, "the womb"; MW's garbhin is "pregnant", and this is its feminine (MW).

प्रसूते — prasūte — "gives birth".
धेनुः वत्सं प्रसूते। dhenuḥ vatsaṃ prasūte. The cow gives birth to a calf.
Past (she …): सा पुत्रं प्रसूतवती, sā putraṃ prasūtavatī.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसूते (prasūte) is built from pra and the root √sū, "to beget, to bear"; MW gives "to procreate, bring forth" (MW).

मम भ्रातुः जन्म रात्रौ अभवत्। mama bhrātuḥ janma rātrau abhavat. My brother was born at night.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
जन्म (janma) comes from the root √jan, "to be born", the root of जायते; MW gives "birth, production, origin" (MW).
