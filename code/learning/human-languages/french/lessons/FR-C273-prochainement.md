## You'll want to know: prochainement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C273-VEILLE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prochainement** — "soon, shortly".

> Le magasin va ouvrir prochainement.
>
> *The shop is going to open soon.*

A word of announcements and posters: *prochainement au cinéma*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prochainement* is built on *prochain* "next", inherited from Vulgar Latin *propeānus*, built on Latin *prope* "near" (Lewis & Short).
