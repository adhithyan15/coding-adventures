## You'll want to know: 信心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C319-CALL319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**信心** — *xìnxīn* — "confidence; faith".

> **我对你有信心** *wǒ duì nǐ yǒu xìnxīn* — I have faith in you.

The new character, in pieces: 信 — nine strokes: the person piece 亻 on the left; on the right a dot, three bars and 口. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**信心** (*xìnxīn*) is built from 信 'to trust' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sinH* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'loyal and true' and 'sincere'.
