## You'll want to know: chēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C526-WEIGHT526-01]; assesses=[] -->

*chēng* — "to weigh on a scale".

> *qǐng nǐ bāng wǒ chēng zhèxiē píngguǒ* — Could you weigh these apples for me?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chēng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyhing* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to know light from heavy'; the dictionary also lists *tsyhingH*, glossed 'fitting' and, in a common spelling, the balance that weighs things.
