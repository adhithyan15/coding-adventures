## You'll want to know: बाँह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C477-HATHELI477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बाँह** — *bā̃h* — "an arm".

> माँ ने बच्चे को बाँहों में उठाया।
>
> *māṁ ne bacce ko bā̃hoṁ meṁ uṭhāyā.*
>
> The mother lifted the child in her arms.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाँह** (*bā̃h*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *bā̃h*, "upper arm", under Sanskrit *bāhú*, "arm" (CDIAL 9229).
