## You'll want to know: desfiar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C330-DESCASCAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**desfiar** — "to shred (into strands); to fray".

> O meu pai desfia o bacalhau à mão.
>
> *My father shreds the salt cod by hand.*

Also a fabric that frays: *a camisola desfiou*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desfiar* is built from *des-* and *fio* "thread", which is inherited from Latin *fīlum* "a thread" (Lewis & Short): to pull apart thread by thread.
