## You'll want to know: el fracaso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C573-LOGRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el fracaso** — "the failure".

> La película fue un fracaso.
>
> *The film was a failure.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fracaso* comes from Italian *fracasso* 'a crash, a smash' (EtymDB), from *fracassare* 'to smash to pieces'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
