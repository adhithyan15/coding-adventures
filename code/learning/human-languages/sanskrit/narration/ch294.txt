Sanskrit, chapter 294: A Crash on the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संघट्टः (saṅghaṭṭaḥ) — a collision; a crash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a prison, then the Sanskrit for a chain.

You'll want to know: संघट्टः.
संघट्टः — saṅghaṭṭaḥ — "a collision; a crash".
मार्गे एकः संघट्टः घटितः। mārge ekaḥ saṅghaṭṭaḥ ghaṭitaḥ. A collision happened on the road.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
संघट्टः (saṅghaṭṭaḥ) is built from sam, "together", and the root √ghaṭṭ, "to rub, touch, shake"; MW gives saṃghaṭṭa as a rubbing or clashing together, a collision (MW).

आहतः बालः मूर्छति। āhataḥ bālaḥ mūrchati. The injured boy faints.
Past (he …): सः मार्गे मूर्छितवान्, saḥ mārge mūrchitavān.

The word, taken apart.
मूर्छति (mūrchati) comes from the root √mūrch, which MW gives as to thicken or grow rigid and also as to faint or swoon; MW spells it with one c, mūrchati (MW).
