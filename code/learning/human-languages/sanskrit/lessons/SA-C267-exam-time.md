## Grammar Lens: one timetable turn

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C267-SCHOOL-PLAN-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-05, SA-LEX-C18-ASK-01, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-02, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-04] -->

Put four known pieces together:

> **परीक्षा कदा भविष्यति?**
>
> *Parīkṣā kadā bhaviṣyati?*
>
> When will the exam be?

Answer from the smallest possible timetable:

> **श्वः परीक्षा भविष्यति।**
>
> *Śvaḥ parīkṣā bhaviṣyati.*
>
> The exam will be tomorrow.

This is one useful school-time pattern. It does not teach a new future
paradigm or a complete timetable system.
