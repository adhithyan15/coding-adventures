6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
उदाहरणम् (udāharaṇam) — an example.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a doubt, then the Sanskrit for in fact.

You'll want to know: उदाहरणम्.
उदाहरणम् — udāharaṇam — "an example".
कृपया एकम् उदाहरणं ददातु। kṛpayā ekam udāharaṇaṃ dadātu. Please give one example.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
उदाहरणम् (udāharaṇam) is built on ud, ā and the root √hṛ, "to take, to carry"; MW gives ud-āharaṇa "declaration" and "an example, an illustration" (MW).

You'll want to know: दृष्टान्तः.
दृष्टान्तः — dṛṣṭāntaḥ — "an illustration (in an argument)".
गुरुः दृष्टान्तेन विचारं स्पष्टं करोति। guruḥ dṛṣṭāntena vicāraṃ spaṣṭaṃ karoti. The teacher makes the idea clear with an illustration.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दृष्टान्तः (dṛṣṭāntaḥ) is built from dṛṣṭa, "seen", and anta, "an end", which MW explains as the end or aim of what is seen; MW gives the senses "example, standard" and "exemplification" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an illustration (in an argument), then the Sanskrit for in this way.

You'll want to know: बोधयति.
बोधयति — bodhayati — "makes (someone) understand".
अध्यापकः उदाहरणेन छात्रान् बोधयति। adhyāpakaḥ udāharaṇena chātrān bodhayati. The teacher makes the students understand with an example.
Past (he …): सः छात्रान् बोधितवान्, saḥ chātrān bodhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
बोधयति (bodhayati) comes from the root √budh, "to wake, to understand", in its causative form; MW gives bodhita "made known, apprised, explained" (MW).
