## You'll want to know: পরী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C322-RAKKHOSH322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পরী** — *pôrī* — "a fairy".

> গল্পে একটা পরী শিশুটিকে বাঁচাল।
>
> *gôlpe ekṭā pôrī shishuṭike bā̃chālô.*
>
> In the story a fairy saved the child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পরী** (*pôrī*) is usually traced to Persian *parī*, 'a fairy', the winged spirit of Persian stories.
