## You'll want to know: சரிதான்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C96-NOTATALL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**சரிதான்** (*caritāṉ*) — "that's right", "quite so".

**சரி** with **-தான்**, "that and no other".
