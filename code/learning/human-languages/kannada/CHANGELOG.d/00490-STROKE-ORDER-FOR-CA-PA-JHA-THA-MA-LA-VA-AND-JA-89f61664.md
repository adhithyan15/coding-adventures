## Stroke order for ca, pa, jha, tha, ma, la, va and ja

Eight more consonants now have a cited stroke order: ಚ (KA-S122, chapter 13),
ಪ (KA-S124, chapter 15), ಝ (KA-S128, chapter 20), ಥ (KA-S129, chapter 23), ಮ
(KA-S134, chapter 67), ಲ (KA-S137, chapter 69), ವ (KA-S139, chapter 71) and ಜ
(KA-S150, chapter 72). Their writing blocks stop saying "copy what you see"
and list the movements, the pen lifts and the source, in the same form as the
earlier consonants, and all eight letter lessons now print a stroke-order
filmstrip.

- **Source.** Gopala Krishna A's `Kannada-alphabet-<slug>.gif` animations on
  Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0, 25 May 2016), the series the vowels and the
  first twelve consonants cite.
- **Lifts.** ಲ has no top bar and is one unbroken run. ವ and ಜ lift once: ವ
  for the hooked bar, ಜ for the arc across the top. ಪ and ಮ lift twice: ಪ
  sets its dot before the hooked bar, ಮ adds its right bowl after it. ಚ and
  ಥ lift three times: ಚ draws ಬ's body and then ಕ's lower bar, link and
  hooked bar; ಥ draws ದ's body, the hooked bar, the tail and last the dot. ಝ
  lifts four times: the bowl, the hooked bar, each of its two arms and the
  tail.
- **ಥ is filed as "thha".** That series spells dental ತ "tha", so its
  aspirated partner takes a second *h*. The KA-S129 writing block says so next
  to the citation.
