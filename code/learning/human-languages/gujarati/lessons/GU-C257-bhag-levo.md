# ભાગ લેવો (bhāg levo) — to take part

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C257-TEAM257-02, GU-LEX-C257-TEAM257-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for player, then the Gujarati for competition.

## You'll want to know: ભાગ લેવો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C257-TEAM257-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાગ લેવો** — *bhāg levo* — "to take part".

> **મેં હરીફાઈમાં ભાગ લીધો.** — *mẽ harīphāīmā̃ bhāg līdho* — "I took part in the competition."

Past **મેં ભાગ લીધો**, *mẽ bhāg līdho*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાગ લેવો** (*bhāg levo*), to take a part, is built from *bhāg*, Sanskrit *bhāga*, "portion, fraction" (CDIAL 9430; EtymDB), and the verb *levũ*, "to take".
