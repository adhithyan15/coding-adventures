## You'll want to know: स्वयंसेवक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C311-MADAD311-02]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयंसेवक** — *svayaṁsevak* — "a volunteer".

> मुझे उम्मीद है कि कल और स्वयंसेवक आएँगे।
>
> *mujhe ummīd hai ki kal aur svayaṁsevak āeṅge.*
>
> I hope more volunteers will come tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयंसेवक** (*svayaṁsevak*) is a modern compound of Sanskrit *svayam*, "oneself, of one's own accord", and *sevaka*, "a servant" (Monier-Williams): someone who serves because they chose to.
