## You'll want to know: ఉప్పెన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C473-DVIPAM473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉప్పెన** — *uppena* — "a tidal surge".

> తుపాను వల్ల తీరంలో ఉప్పెన వచ్చింది.
>
> *tupānu valla tīraṁlō uppena vaccindi.*
>
> Because of the storm a tidal surge hit the coast.

The sea rising over the land in a storm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉప్పెన** (*uppena*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 666 lists Telugu *uppena*, "a swell of the sea, an inundation", beside *ubbu*, "to swell, to overflow". The sea swells over the land.
