## You'll want to know: कमण्डलुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C394-TIRTHA394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कमण्डलुः** — *kamaṇḍaluḥ* — "an ascetic's water-pot".

> यात्रिकः कमण्डलौ जलं वहति।
>
> *yātrikaḥ kamaṇḍalau jalaṃ vahati.*
>
> The pilgrim carries water in his water-pot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कमण्डलुः** (*kamaṇḍaluḥ*) is the word MW gives for the water-pot that ascetics and pilgrims carry; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
