Italian, chapter 458: Alla posta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la raccomandata — the registered letter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the clearance sale, then the Italian for to haggle.

You'll want to know: la raccomandata.
la raccomandata — "the registered letter".
Ho spedito una raccomandata con ricevuta di ritorno. I sent a registered letter with a return receipt.
Raccomandare — to recommend, and to register a letter.

The word, taken apart.
raccomandata is built on raccomandare 'to entrust' (EtymDB), from ri- and comandare, which goes back to Latin commendāre 'to entrust', built on mandāre; the words are inherited.

What you've built.

You'll want to know: la spedizione.
la spedizione — "the shipping, the dispatch; the expedition".
La spedizione costa cinque euro. Shipping costs five euros.
Also an expedition to a mountain or to the poles.

The word, taken apart.
spedizione is a learned borrowing from Latin expedītiō 'a setting out' (EtymDB), from expedīre 'to free the feet, to get ready', built on pēs 'foot'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
affrancare — to put postage on; to set free.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the shipping, then the Italian for the sender.

You'll want to know: affrancare.
affrancare — "to put postage on; to set free".
Devo affrancare questa lettera per l'estero. I need to put postage on this letter going abroad.
Its first meaning is to set free: franco once meant free.

The word, taken apart.
affrancare is built from a- and franco 'free', which was borrowed from Frankish, the Germanic language of the Franks: a free man was a Frank.

Lesson 5 of 5.
recapitare — to deliver (mail).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the sender, then the Italian for to put postage on.

You'll want to know: recapitare.
recapitare — "to deliver (mail)".
Il postino ha recapitato il pacco al vicino. The postman delivered the parcel to the neighbour.
Il recapito — the address, the contact details.

The word, taken apart.
recapitare is built on recapito 'address, delivery'; the deeper origin is not settled.
