## You'll want to know: けっか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C342-GENIN342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**けっか** — *kekka* — "a result; an outcome".

> **がんばったので、いい けっかに なりました。** — *ganbatta node, ii kekka ni narimashita* — "We worked hard, so it turned out well."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けっか** (*kekka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ketsu*, "tie" (Middle Chinese *ket*) and *ka*, "fruit" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as *kek-ka*.
