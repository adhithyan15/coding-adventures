## You'll want to know: ਪਰਾਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C306-WINNOW306-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਾਲੀ** — *parālī* — "rice straw".

> ਸਿਆਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਪਰਾਲੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਸੌਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *siāl vich asīṁ parālī utte saunde hāṁ.*
>
> In winter we sleep on rice straw.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਾਲੀ** (*parālī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *palāla*, "stalk, straw"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *parālī*, "straw of rice or millet", under it (CDIAL 7958).
