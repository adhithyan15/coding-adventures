## You'll want to know: ঠোঙা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C423-THONGA423-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠোঙা** — *ṭhongā* — "a paper bag; a paper cone".

> মুদি একটা ঠোঙায় মুড়ি দিলেন।
>
> *mudi ekṭā ṭhongāy muṛi dilen.*
>
> The grocer gave me puffed rice in a paper bag.

Folded and pasted from old newspaper, the ঠোঙা holds roasted nuts, puffed rice and sweets.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠোঙা** (*ṭhongā*) is the paper bag of every Bengal shop; its origin is not settled.
