## You'll want to know: la mélancolie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C472-NOSTALGIE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la mélancolie** — "melancholy, gloom".

> L'automne me donne toujours un peu de mélancolie.
>
> *Autumn always makes me a little melancholy.*

The adjective is *mélancolique*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la mélancolie* comes through Latin *melancholia* from Greek *melankholía* (EtymDB), built on *mélas* "black" and *kholḗ* "bile": old medicine blamed sadness on black bile.
