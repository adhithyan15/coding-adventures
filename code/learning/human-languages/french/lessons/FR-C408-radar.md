## You'll want to know: le radar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C408-RADAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le radar** — "the speed camera; the radar".

> Il y a un radar à l'entrée du village.
>
> *There is a speed camera at the edge of the village.*

*Un radar fixe* stays in one place; *un radar mobile* moves.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*radar* was borrowed from English *radar*, made from the first letters of *radio detection and ranging*. EtymDB lists the word without a source line.
