## You'll want to know: il sindacato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C359-SCIOPERO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il sindacato** — "the trade union".

> Il sindacato chiede stipendi più alti.
>
> *The union is asking for higher wages.*

A union member is *un iscritto al sindacato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sindacato* is built on *sindaco*, a learned borrowing from Late Latin *syndicus* "a representative of a body" (Lewis & Short), from Greek *sýndikos* "one who helps in court", built on *díkē* "justice".
