## You'll want to know: नमूनो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C210-CHOOSE210-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नमूनो** — *namūno* — "a sample".

> ये नमूनो आछो है।
>
> *ye namūno āchho hai.*
>
> This sample is good.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नमूनो** (*namūno*) is usually traced to Persian *namūna*, "a sample, a model".
