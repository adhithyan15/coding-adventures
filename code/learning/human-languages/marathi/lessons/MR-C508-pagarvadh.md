## You'll want to know: पगारवाढ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C508-PAGAR508-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पगारवाढ** — *pagārvāḍh* — "a pay rise".

> या वर्षी सगळ्यांना चांगली पगारवाढ मिळाली.
>
> *yā varṣī sagḷyānnā cāṅglī pagārvāḍh miḷālī.*
>
> Everyone got a good pay rise this year.

Feminine.

पगार is masculine, but पगारवाढ follows वाढ and is feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पगारवाढ** (*pagārvāḍh*) is a Marathi compound of पगार, "salary", a loan from Portuguese *pagar*, "to pay", and वाढ, "growth", from वाढणे.
