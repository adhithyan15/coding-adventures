## You'll want to know: sēditiōsus, sēditiōsa, sēditiōsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C387-SEDITIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sēditiōsus, sēditiōsa, sēditiōsum** — "seditious, rebellious".

> Sēditiōsī cīvēs forum occupāvērunt.
>
> *Rebellious citizens took over the Forum.*

*sēditiōsus, sēditiōsa, sēditiōsum*, built on *sēditiō*. English *seditious*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sēditiōsus* is built on *sēditiō* "a rising" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-ōsus* "full of". English *seditious* comes from it.

## What you've built
