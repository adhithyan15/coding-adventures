## You'll want to know: मिठी मारणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C501-MITHI501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मिठी मारणे** — *miṭhī mārṇe* — "to hug".

> मित्राला पाहून त्याने मिठी मारली.
>
> *mitrālā pāhūn tyāne miṭhī mārlī.*
>
> When he saw his friend he gave him a hug.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मिठी मारली, *tyāne miṭhī mārlī*.

मिठी, a hug; the one hugged takes -ला: आईला मिठी मारणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिठी मारणे** (*miṭhī mārṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on मिठी, "a hug", whose origin is not settled, with मारणे as the light verb.
