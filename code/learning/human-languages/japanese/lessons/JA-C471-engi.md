## You'll want to know: えんぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C471-STAGE471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**えんぎ** — *engi* — "acting; a performance".

> **あの はいゆうの えんぎは すごいです。** — *ano haiyū no engi wa sugoi desu* — "That actor's acting is amazing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんぎ** (*engi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *en*, "performance" (Middle Chinese *yenX*) and *gi*, "skill" (*gjeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
