## You'll want to know: कातना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C512-KARGHA512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कातना** — *kātnā* — "to spin (thread)".

> वह चरखे पर कपास से धागा कात रही है।
>
> *Vah carkhe par kapās se dhāgā kāt rahī hai.*
>
> She is spinning thread from cotton on the wheel.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने धागा काता, *usne dhāgā kātā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कातना** (*kātnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*kartati*, "spins", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2855).
