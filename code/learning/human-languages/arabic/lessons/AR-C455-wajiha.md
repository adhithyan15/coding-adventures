## You'll want to know: واجهة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C455-WAJIHA455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**واجهة** — *wājiha* — "a shop window; a front".

> رأيت فستانا جميلا في واجهة المتجر.
>
> *raʾaytu fustānan jamīlan fī wājihat al-matjar.*
>
> I saw a beautiful dress in the shop window.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**واجهة** (*wājiha*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-j-h*, "the face; to face" (Lane). It is the part of a building that faces the street.
