## You'll want to know: die Kathedrale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C438-ALTSTADT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Kathedrale** — "the cathedral".

> Die Kathedrale ist über siebenhundert Jahre alt.
>
> *The cathedral is over seven hundred years old.*

Plural: *die Kathedralen*. Many German towns say *der Dom* or *das Münster*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kathedrale* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *(ecclēsia) cathedrālis* 'church with a bishop's chair', from Latin *cathedra* 'chair', from Greek *kathédra* (Lewis & Short).
