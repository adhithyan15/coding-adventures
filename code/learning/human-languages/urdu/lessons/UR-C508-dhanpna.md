## You'll want to know: ڈھانپنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C508-DHAKKAN508-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھانپنا** — *ḍhāṅpnā* — "to cover".

> اس نے ڈھکن سے دیگچی ڈھانپی۔
>
> *us ne ḍhakkan se degchī ḍhāṅpī.*
>
> She covered the pot with a lid.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کھانا ڈھانپا, *us ne khānā ḍhāṅpā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھانپنا** (*ḍhāṅpnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍhā̃pnā*, "to cover", under *\*ḍhapp*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5579).
