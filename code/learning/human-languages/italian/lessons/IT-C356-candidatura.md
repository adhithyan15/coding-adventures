## You'll want to know: la candidatura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C356-CANDIDATURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la candidatura** — "the application (for a job); the candidacy".

> La candidatura va inviata entro venerdì.
>
> *The application must be sent by Friday.*

*Presentare la propria candidatura* — to apply. *Va inviata* means must be sent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*candidatura* is built on *candidato*, a learned borrowing from Latin *candidātus* "dressed in white", from *candidus* "shining white" (Lewis & Short).
