# Choosing your "you" — putting it together

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-TU, ES-LEX-USTED, ES-LEX-QUIEN, ES-ETYMON-TU-THOU, ES-REGISTER-TU-USTED, ES-GRAMMAR-USTED-THIRD-PERSON, ES-LEX-VOS-01, ES-CULTURE-VOSEO-02, ES-ETYMON-VOS-03, ES-ETYMON-VUESTRA-MERCED] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Three words for “you,” plus **quién** for “who.” Nothing new in
this lesson — only what you already have.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-TU, ES-LEX-USTED, ES-LEX-QUIEN, ES-ETYMON-TU-THOU, ES-REGISTER-TU-USTED, ES-GRAMMAR-USTED-THIRD-PERSON, ES-LEX-VOS-01, ES-CULTURE-VOSEO-02, ES-ETYMON-VOS-03, ES-ETYMON-VUESTRA-MERCED] -->

> To a friend: **tú**.
> To a stranger, an elder, an official: **usted**.
> Heard across the Southern Cone and Central America: **vos** — understand it, keep saying *tú*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-TU, ES-LEX-USTED, ES-LEX-QUIEN, ES-ETYMON-TU-THOU, ES-REGISTER-TU-USTED, ES-GRAMMAR-USTED-THIRD-PERSON, ES-LEX-VOS-01, ES-CULTURE-VOSEO-02, ES-ETYMON-VOS-03, ES-ETYMON-VUESTRA-MERCED] -->
