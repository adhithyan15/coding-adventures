## You'll want to know: supposer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C367-HYPOTHESE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**supposer** — "to suppose, to assume".

> Je suppose que tu es fatigué après ce voyage.
>
> *I suppose you are tired after that trip.*

*Supposer que* + indicative. *À supposer que* takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*supposer* comes through Middle French (EtymDB) from Latin *suppōnere* "to put under, to put in place of" (Lewis & Short), which took the sense "to assume" in Medieval Latin; it is built on *pōnere* "to put".
