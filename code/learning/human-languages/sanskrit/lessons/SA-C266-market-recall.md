# Bring the market words back together

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C248-THG248-04, SA-GRAMMAR-C261-DUAL-OBJECTS-03, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “a stake,” then choose the correct two-person object form, then
say the request form “please give.” (*Paṇaḥ; the taught dual object form;
dadātu.*)

- [YOU RECALL: a shop; a market — **आपणः; विपणिः** — *āpaṇaḥ; vipaṇiḥ*]
