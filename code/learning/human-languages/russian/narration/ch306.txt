Russian, chapter 306: Love and engagement.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
помолвка (pomólvka) — an engagement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a graduation party, then the Russian for a generation.

You'll want to know: помолвка.
помолвка (pomólvka) — "an engagement".
После помолвки они поехали к родителям. Pósle pomólvki oní poyékhali k rodítelyam. After the engagement they went to visit the parents.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
помолвка (pomólvka) is built on помолвить "to promise in marriage", from молвить "to say", inherited from Proto-Slavic mŭlviti (EtymDB): a spoken promise.

Lesson 2 of 5.
влюбляться (vlyublyátsya) — to fall in love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a generation, then the Russian for an engagement.

You'll want to know: влюбляться.
влюбляться (vlyublyátsya) — "to fall in love".
Он влюбился в неё с первого взгляда. On vlyubílsya v neyó s pérvovo vzglyáda. He fell in love with her at first sight.
Imperfective; perfective влюбиться (vlyubítsya), влюблюсь. It takes в + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
влюбляться (vlyublyátsya) is built on в- "into" and любить "to love", inherited from Proto-Slavic ľubiti, from PIE lewbʰ- (EtymDB); English love is a cousin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ухаживать (ukházhivat) — to court; to look after.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an engagement, then the Russian for to fall in love.

You'll want to know: ухаживать.
ухаживать (ukházhivat) — "to court; to look after".
Он красиво ухаживал за ней. On krasívo ukházhival za ney. He courted her beautifully.
Imperfective; it takes за + instrumental. Also to look after someone: ухаживать (ukházhivat) за бабушкой.

The word, taken apart.
ухаживать (ukházhivat) is built on у- and хаживать, an old repeated form of ходить "to go", inherited from Proto-Slavic xoditi (EtymDB): to keep going round someone.

венчание (venchániye) — "a church wedding".
Венчание было в старой церкви. Venchániye býlo v stároy tsérkvi. The church wedding was in an old church.
Neuter. In the Orthodox rite the couple are crowned.

The word, taken apart.
венчание (venchániye) is built on венчать "to crown, to wed", from венец "a crown, a wreath" (EtymDB lists венец with no parent): crowns are held over the couple.

Lesson 5 of 5.
расставаться (rasstavátsya) — to part, to break up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to court, then the Russian for a church wedding.

You'll want to know: расставаться.
расставаться (rasstavátsya) — "to part, to break up".
Они расстались друзьями. Oní rasstális druzyámi. They parted as friends.
Imperfective; perfective расстаться (rasstátsya), расстанусь. Present расстаюсь, расстаёшься (rasstayóshsya). It takes с + instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
расставаться (rasstavátsya) is built on рас- "apart" and стать "to stand", inherited from Proto-Slavic stati, from PIE steh₂- "to stand" (EtymDB): to stand apart.
