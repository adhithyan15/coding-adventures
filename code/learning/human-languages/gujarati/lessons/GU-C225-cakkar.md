## You'll want to know: ચક્કર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C225-UNWELL225-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચક્કર** — *cakkar* — "dizziness, a turn".

> **શું તમને ચક્કર આવે છે?** — *śũ tamne cakkar āve che?* — "Do you feel dizzy?"

A neuter noun. Word for word, does a turn come to you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચક્કર** (*cakkar*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *cakra*, "wheel", so dizziness is the head going round. CDIAL lists only the inherited Gujarati *cāk*, "wheel", under *cakra*, not *cakkar* itself (CDIAL 4538).
