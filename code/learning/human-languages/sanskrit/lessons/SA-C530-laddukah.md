## You'll want to know: लड्डुकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C530-BHOJYA530-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लड्डुकः** — *laḍḍukaḥ* — "a laddu".

> यदि उत्सवः भवति, तर्हि माता लड्डुकान् पचति।
>
> *yadi utsavaḥ bhavati, tarhi mātā laḍḍukān pacati.*
>
> If there is a festival, my mother makes laddus.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लड्डुकः** (*laḍḍukaḥ*) is the word MW gives, beside *laḍḍu*, for a sweet made of ground pulse or flour with sugar and spice, fried in ghee; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
