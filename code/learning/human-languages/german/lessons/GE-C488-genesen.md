## You'll want to know: genesen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C488-GENESEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**genesen** — "to recover, to get well".

> Nach zwei Wochen war der Patient wieder genesen.
>
> *After two weeks the patient had recovered.*

Formal. Perfekt with *sein*: *Er ist genesen.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*genesen* is inherited from Old High German *ginesan*, from Proto-Germanic *ganesaną*, built on *nesaną*, which goes back to a root meaning "to return, to heal" (EtymDB).
