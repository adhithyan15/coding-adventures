## You'll want to know: ostacolare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C354-OSTACOLO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ostacolare** — "to hinder, to obstruct".

> Il traffico ostacola il lavoro delle ambulanze.
>
> *The traffic hinders the work of the ambulances.*

Stress on the second syllable: *ostàcolo*, *ostàcola*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ostacolare* is built on *ostacolo* "obstacle", a learned borrowing from Latin *obstāculum*, from *obstāre* "to stand in the way" (Lewis & Short), built on *stāre* "to stand".
