## You'll want to know: やっと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C266-OBORE266-04]; assesses=[] -->

**やっと** — *yatto* — "at last; finally".

> **こどもは やっと たすかりました。** — *kodomo wa yatto tasukarimashita* — "In the end the child was saved."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やっと** (*yatto*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
