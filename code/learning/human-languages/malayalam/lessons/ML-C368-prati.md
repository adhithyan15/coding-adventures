## You'll want to know: പ്രതി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C368-KODATHI368-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രതി** — *prati* — "the accused; a defendant".

> പ്രതി കോടതിയിൽ ഒന്നും പറഞ്ഞില്ല.
>
> *prati kōṭatiyil onnuṁ paṟaññilla.*
>
> The accused said nothing in court.

Also a copy of a book or a paper: രണ്ട് പ്രതി, two copies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രതി** (*prati*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *prati*, "towards, against, in opposition to" (CDIAL 8540): the accused is the one the case stands against.
