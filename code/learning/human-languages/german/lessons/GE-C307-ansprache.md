## You'll want to know: die Ansprache
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C307-GASTGEBER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ansprache** — "the speech, the address".

> Der Vater der Braut hielt eine kurze Ansprache.
>
> *The bride's father gave a short speech.*

Plural: *die Ansprachen*. *Eine Ansprache halten*. More personal than *eine Rede*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ansprache* is built on *Sprache* "speech", from Old High German *sprāhha*, Proto-Germanic *sprēkijō* (EtymDB), with *an*: words spoken to someone.
