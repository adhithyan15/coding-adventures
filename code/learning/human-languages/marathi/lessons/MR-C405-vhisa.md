## You'll want to know: व्हिसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C405-VIMAN405-05]; assesses=[] -->

**व्हिसा** — *vhisā* — "a visa".

> मीराला व्हिसा मिळाला.
>
> *mīrālā vhisā miḷālā.*
>
> Meera got her visa.

Masculine.

व्हिसा मिळणे takes the dative: तिला व्हिसा मिळाला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्हिसा** (*vhisā*) is a loan from English *visa*.
