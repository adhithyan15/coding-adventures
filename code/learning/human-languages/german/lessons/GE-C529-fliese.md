## You'll want to know: die Fliese
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C529-RENOVIEREN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Fliese** — "tile".

> Im Bad sind die Fliesen weiß.
>
> *In the bathroom the tiles are white.*

Plural: *die Fliesen*. Also *die Kachel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fliese* was borrowed from Middle Low German *vlīse* (EtymDB), a word of the north.
