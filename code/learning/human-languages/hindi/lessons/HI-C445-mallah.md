## You'll want to know: मल्लाह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C445-KISHTI445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मल्लाह** — *mallāh* — "a boatman".

> मल्लाह ने हमें नदी के पार पहुँचाया।
>
> *mallāh ne hamẽ nadī ke pār pahũcāyā.*
>
> The boatman took us across the river.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मल्लाह** (*mallāh*) is usually traced to Arabic *mallāḥ*, "a sailor", through Persian; on Indian rivers it is the man who rows the ferry.
