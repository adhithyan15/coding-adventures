# کتنا (kitnā) — how much, how many

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C146-PARH146-03, UR-LEX-C78-TIME78-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SHOULD-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "a fare" and "far". (*kirāyā*, *dūr*.) A traveller asks about both.

## You'll want to know: کتنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C270-KITNA-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C146-PARH146-03, UR-LEX-C78-TIME78-02, UR-LEX-C202-CHIZ202-03, UR-LEX-C80-TOWN80-01] -->

**کتنا** — *kitnā* — "how much; how many".

> **کرایہ کتنا ہے؟**
>
> *kirāyā kitnā hai?* — "How much is the fare?"

It follows its noun like **اچھا** (*achchhā*): **کتنا** for **کرایہ**, **کتنے** (*kitne*) for a plural.

> **کتنے مسافر تھے؟**
>
> *kitne musāfir the?* — "How many passengers were there?"

**کتنی دور** (*kitnī dūr*), "how far", goes as one pair:

> **اسٹیشن کتنی دور ہے؟**
>
> *isṭeshan kitnī dūr hai?* — "How far is the station?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کتنا** (*kitnā*) is inherited. CDIAL lists its Hindi twin, with the sense "how great?", under a reconstructed *kiyatta* (CDIAL 3167).
