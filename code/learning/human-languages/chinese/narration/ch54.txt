Mandarin Chinese, chapter 54: Learning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
xuéxí — to study; to learn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to understand, then the Mandarin for slow.

You'll want to know: xuéxí.
xuéxí — "to study; to learn".
wǒ xuéxí hànyǔ — I'm learning Chinese.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xuéxí is built from 学 'to learn' and xí 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, haewk and zip (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses zip with the first.

What you've built.
One more word for asking politely.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: xuéxí]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: xuéxí, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wǒ xuéxí hànyǔ — I'm learning Chinese.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for to understand, then the Mandarin for slow, then say xuéxí again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does xuéxí mean? ("To study; to learn".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

jīntiān yǒu hànyǔ kè — There's a Chinese class today.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kè is inherited from Middle Chinese khwaH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a tax' and 'a test'. The sense 'lesson' is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.
