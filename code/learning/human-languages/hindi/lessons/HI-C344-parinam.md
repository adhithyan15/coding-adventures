## You'll want to know: परिणाम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C344-PARINAM344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परिणाम** — *pariṇām* — "a consequence, an outcome".

> हर काम का कोई परिणाम होता है।
>
> *har kām kā koī pariṇām hotā hai.*
>
> Every action has some consequence.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिणाम** (*pariṇām*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pariṇāma*, "a change; a result" (Monier-Williams).
