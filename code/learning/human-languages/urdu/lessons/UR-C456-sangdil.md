## You'll want to know: سنگدل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C456-KIND456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سنگدل** — *sangdil* — "hard-hearted".

> سنگدل آدمی لوگوں کا دکھ نہیں سمجھتا۔
>
> *sangdil ādmī logoṅ kā dukh nahīṅ samajhtā.*
>
> A hard-hearted man does not understand people's sorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سنگدل** (*sangdil*) is usually traced to Persian *sangdil*, "stone-hearted": *sang*, "stone", and **دل**, "heart".
