## You'll want to know: eitel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C475-ANGEBER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**eitel** — "vain".

> Er ist so eitel, dass er ständig in den Spiegel schaut.
>
> *He is so vain that he keeps looking in the mirror.*

Old meaning: pure, mere — *eitel Sonnenschein*, nothing but sunshine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eitel* is inherited from Middle High German *ītel* "empty, vain" and Old High German *ītal*, from Proto-Germanic *īdalaz* "empty" (EtymDB); English *idle* is a cousin.
