## You'll want to know: холст
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C521-PAINTING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**холст** (*kholst*) — "canvas".

> Картина написана маслом на холсте.
>
> *Kartína napísana máslom na kholsté.*
>
> The picture is painted in oils on canvas.

Masculine; genitive холста.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *холст* without a source, and the origin is not settled.
