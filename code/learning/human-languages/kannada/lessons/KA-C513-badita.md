## You'll want to know: ಬಡಿತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C513-CHETARIKE513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಡಿತ** — *baḍita* — "a heartbeat; a throb".

> ವೈದ್ಯರು ಮಗುವಿನ ಹೃದಯದ ಬಡಿತ ಕೇಳಿದರು.
>
> *vaidyaru maguvina hṛdayada baḍita kēḷidaru.*
>
> The doctor listened to the baby's heartbeat.

ಎದೆ ಬಡಿತ, the beating of the heart; fear makes it ಜೋರು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಡಿತ** (*baḍita*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5224 lists Kannada *baḍi*, "to beat, strike", with nouns for beating; -*ta* makes the noun.
