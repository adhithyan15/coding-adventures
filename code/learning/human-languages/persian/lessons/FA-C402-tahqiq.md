## You'll want to know: تحقیق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C402-TAHQIQ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تحقیق** (*tahqiq*) — "research, an investigation".

> او درباره زبانهای قدیمی تحقیق میکند.
>
> *u darbâre-ye zabânhâ-ye qadimi tahqiq mikonad.*
>
> She does research on ancient languages.

*Tahqiq kardan* — to research. Plural *tahqiqât*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحقیق** comes from Arabic *taḥqīq* (EtymDB), a verbal noun from the same Arabic family as *ḥaqīqa* "truth": research finds out what is true.
