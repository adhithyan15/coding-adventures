## You'll want to know: kàndài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C349-VIEW349-01]; assesses=[] -->

*kàndài* — "to regard (in a certain way)".

> *nǐ zěnme kàndài zhège wèntí* — How do you view this question?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kàndài* is built from 看 and *dài*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khan* and *dojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to view' and the second 'to wait'.
