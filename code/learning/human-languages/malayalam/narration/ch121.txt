Malayalam, chapter 121: Out and About.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വിളിക്കൂ (viḷikkū) — please call.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for please help, then the Malayalam for please stop.

You'll want to know: വിളിക്കൂ.
വിളിക്കൂ (viḷikkū) — "please call (someone)", or "please phone".

What you've built.
The request when help is needed.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: viḷikkū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: viḷikkū, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say viḷikkū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for please help, then the Malayalam for please stop, then say viḷikkū again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does വിളിക്കൂ (viḷikkū) mean? ("Please call".) Say it once more.
