## You'll want to know: der Pannendienst
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C295-PANNE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Pannendienst** — "the breakdown service".

> Der Pannendienst kam nach einer Stunde.
>
> *The breakdown service came after an hour.*

Plural: *die Pannendienste*. *Der Dienst* — service.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Pannendienst* joins *Panne*, borrowed from French *panne* "breakdown" (EtymDB), and the inherited *Dienst* "service".
