## You'll want to know: چوری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C276-CHORI276-02]; assesses=[] -->

**چوری** — *chorī* — "a theft".

> کل رات گلی میں چوری ہوئی۔
>
> *kal rāt galī meṅ chorī huī.*
>
> There was a theft in the lane last night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from Sanskrit *caurikā*, "theft" (CDIAL 4937). It sits beside **چور**: the **چور** is the person, the **چوری** is the deed, and **چوری ہوئی** says that one took place.
