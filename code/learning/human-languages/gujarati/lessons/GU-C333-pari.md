## You'll want to know: પરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C333-PARI333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પરી** — *parī* — "a fairy".

> વાર્તામાં એક સુંદર પરી હતી.
>
> *vārtāmā̃ ek sundar parī hatī.*
>
> There was a beautiful fairy in the story.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરી** (*parī*) is usually traced to Persian *parī*, "a fairy", the winged spirit of Persian tales. It is feminine: **સુંદર પરી**.
