Telugu, chapter 331: Fire!.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
మంట (maṇṭa) — a flame; a blaze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a news story, then the Telugu for to publish.

You'll want to know: మంట.
మంట — maṇṭa — "a flame; a blaze".
వంటగదిలో మంటలు లేచాయి. vaṇṭagadilō maṇṭalu lēcāyi. Flames rose in the kitchen.
Plural: మంటలు, maṇṭalu.
The plural మంటలు is the everyday word for a fire that is spreading.

The word, taken apart.
మంట (maṇṭa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4680 lists Telugu maṇṭa, "flame, blaze", beside maṇḍu, "to burn, blaze", and Tamil maṇṭu, "to blaze up".

పేలుడు శబ్దం చాలా దూరం వినిపించింది. pēluḍu śabdaṁ cālā dūraṁ vinipiñcindi. The sound of the blast was heard far away.
The noun of పేలు, to burst: a balloon or a cracker పేలుతుంది.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ప్రాణం (prāṇaṁ) — a life; the breath of life.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a flame, then the Telugu for an explosion.

You'll want to know: ప్రాణం.
ప్రాణం — prāṇaṁ — "a life; the breath of life".
ఆ మంటల్లో చాలా ప్రాణాలు పోయాయి. ā maṇṭallō cālā prāṇālu pōyāyi. Many lives were lost in that fire.
Plural: ప్రాణాలు, prāṇālu.
ప్రాణం (prāṇaṁ) పోయింది, the life went: a plain way to say that someone died.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రాణం (prāṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit prāṇa, "breath, the breath of life" (CDIAL 8928).

Lesson 4 of 5.
రక్షించు (rakṣiñcu) — to rescue; to save.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an explosion, then the Telugu for a life.

You'll want to know: రక్షించు.
రక్షించు — rakṣiñcu — "to rescue; to save".
పోలీసులు పిల్లలను మంటల నుంచి రక్షించారు. pōlīsulu pillalanu maṇṭala nuñci rakṣiñcāru. The police rescued the children from the flames.
Past (I …): రక్షించాను, rakṣiñcānu.
Who is saved takes -ను; what from takes నుంచి.

The word, taken apart.
రక్షించు (rakṣiñcu) is built on the Sanskrit root rakṣ, "to guard, protect" (CDIAL 10547), with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
పరుగులు తీయు (parugulu tīyu) — to run for it; to rush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a life, then the Telugu for to rescue.

You'll want to know: పరుగులు తీయు.
పరుగులు తీయు — parugulu tīyu — "to run for it; to rush".
మంటలు చూసి జనం పరుగులు తీశారు. maṇṭalu cūsi janaṁ parugulu tīśāru. Seeing the flames, people ran for it.
Past (I …): పరుగులు తీశాను, parugulu tīśānu.
Literally to draw runs, with తీయు, to pull: running in a hurry or in fear.

The word, taken apart.
పరుగులు తీయు (parugulu tīyu) is a native Telugu phrase: parugu, "a run" (DEDR 3963, with Tamil pari, "to run"), and tīyu, "to draw, pull out".
