Russian, chapter 465: A downpour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ливень (líven) — a downpour, a heavy shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for overcast, then the Russian for to clear up.

You'll want to know: ливень.
ливень (líven) — "a downpour, a heavy shower".
После обеда начался сильный ливень. Pósle obéda nachalsyá sílnyy líven. After lunch a heavy downpour began.
Masculine; the е drops out: ливня.

The word, taken apart.
ливень (líven) is built on лить (lit) 'to pour' with -ень: rain that pours. EtymDB takes лить (lit) from Proto-Slavic liti 'to pour'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
моросить (morosít) — to drizzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to clear up, then the Russian for a downpour.

You'll want to know: моросить.
моросить (morosít) — "to drizzle".
С утра моросит мелкий дождь. S utrá morosít mélkiy dozhd. A fine rain has been drizzling since morning.
Imperfective; mostly impersonal: моросит — it is drizzling.

The word, taken apart.
моросить (morosít) is built on морось 'drizzle, fine rain'. EtymDB lists neither word, so its deeper origin is left open.

Lesson 3 of 5.
промокать (promokát) — to get soaked, to get wet through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a downpour, then the Russian for to drizzle.

You'll want to know: промокать.
промокать (promokát) — "to get soaked, to get wet through".
Без зонта мы всегда промокаем. Bez zónta my vsegdá promokáyem. Without an umbrella we always get soaked.
Imperfective; perfective промокнуть (past промок). Промокнуть до нитки — to get soaked to the skin.

The word, taken apart.
промокать (promokát) is built from про- 'through' and -мокать, on the root of мокрый 'wet', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic mokrŭ (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: лить.
лить (lit) — "to pour".
Дождь льёт с утра. Dozhd lyot s utrá. It has been pouring since the morning.
Imperfective. Present лью, льёшь, льют; past лил, лила. Льёт как из ведра — it is pouring with rain.
