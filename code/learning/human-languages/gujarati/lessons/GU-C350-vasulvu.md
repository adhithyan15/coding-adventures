## You'll want to know: વસૂલવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C350-KARAR350-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વસૂલવું** — *vasūlvũ* — "to recover a debt; to collect money owed".

> બેંક બાકી રકમ વ્યાજ સાથે વસૂલશે.
>
> *beṅk bākī rakam vyāj sāthe vasūlśe.*
>
> The bank will recover the outstanding amount with interest.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધી રકમ વસૂલી, *teṇe badhī rakam vasūlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વસૂલવું** (*vasūlvũ*) is built on *vasūl*, "recovered, received", usually traced to Arabic *wuṣūl*, "arrival, receipt", through Persian.
