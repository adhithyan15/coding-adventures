## You'll want to know: ఆరాధించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C504-ABHIMANAM504-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరాధించు** — *ārādhiñcu* — "to adore; to worship".

> ఆ అమ్మాయి తన అక్కను ఆరాధిస్తుంది.
>
> *ā ammāyi tana akkanu ārādhistundi.*
>
> That girl adores her elder sister.

Past (I …): ఆరాధించాను, *ārādhiñcānu*.

The noun is ఆరాధన, adoration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరాధించు** (*ārādhiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ārādhana*, "worship" (CDIAL 1316), the noun of *ārādhayati*, "to win over", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
