## You'll want to know: la bosse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C479-BLESSURES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la bosse** — "bump, lump".

> Après sa chute, l'enfant a une bosse sur le front.
>
> *After his fall, the child has a bump on his forehead.*

Also a camel's hump: *la bosse du chameau*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la bosse* goes back to Old French *boce* (EtymDB). Its earlier origin is not settled; a Vulgar Latin *bottia* "swelling" is sometimes proposed.
