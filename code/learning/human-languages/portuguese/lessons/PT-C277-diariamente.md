## You'll want to know: diariamente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C277-FREQUENCIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**diariamente** — "daily, every day".

> O jornal chega diariamente às oito horas.
>
> *The newspaper arrives every day at eight.*

*Todos os dias* is the everyday way to say it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diariamente* is built on *diário* "daily", from Latin *diārium* "a daily allowance", from *diēs* "day" (Lewis & Short).
