## You'll want to know: l'infrazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C386-VIOLAZIONE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'infrazione** — "the offence, the infringement (of a rule)".

> Passare con il rosso è un'infrazione grave.
>
> *Going through a red light is a serious offence.*

A breach of a rule rather than a crime: traffic and sport rules.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*infrazione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnfrāctiō* "a breaking" (Lewis & Short), from *īnfringere* "to break in", built on *frangere* "to break".
