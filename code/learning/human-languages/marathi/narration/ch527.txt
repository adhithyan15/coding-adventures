Lesson 1 of 6.
चिरणे (cirṇe) — to chop; to slice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to knead, then the Marathi for to grate.

You'll want to know: चिरणे.
चिरणे — cirṇe — "to chop; to slice".
भाजीसाठी मी दोन कांदे चिरले. bhājīsāṭhī mī don kānde cirle. I chopped two onions for the vegetable dish.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कांदा चिरला, tyāne kāndā cirlā.

The word, taken apart.
चिरणे (cirṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi cirṇẽ, "to split", under cīrayati, "splits" (CDIAL 4844).

Lesson 2 of 6.
उकळणे (ukaḷṇe) — to boil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to grate, then the Marathi for to chop.

You'll want to know: उकळणे.
उकळणे — ukaḷṇe — "to boil".
चहासाठी आईने पाणी उकळलं. cahāsāṭhī āīne pāṇī ukaḷlaṁ. Mother boiled water for tea.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाणी उकळलं, tyāne pāṇī ukaḷlaṁ.
Both ways: पाणी उकळतं, the water boils; मी पाणी उकळतो, I boil the water.

The word, taken apart.
उकळणे (ukaḷṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ukaḷṇẽ, "to boil", under utkalati, "goes out or up" (CDIAL 1716).

आमटी उकळताना मी ती चमच्याने ढवळली. āmṭī ukaḷtānā mī tī camcyāne ḍhavaḷlī. While the dal was boiling I stirred it with a spoon.

ढवळणे (ḍhavaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for stirring; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
करपणे (karapṇe) — to get burnt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to fry, then the Marathi for to stir.

You'll want to know: करपणे.
करपणे — karapṇe — "to get burnt".
माझं लक्ष नव्हतं, म्हणून भात करपला. mājhaṁ lakṣa navhataṁ, mhaṇūn bhāt karaplā. I was not paying attention, so the rice got burnt.
Past (he …): तो भात थोडा करपला, to bhāt thoḍā karaplā.
करपणे (karapṇe) is for food that burns in the pan.

The word, taken apart.
करपणे (karapṇe) is a Marathi verb for food that scorches in the pan; its origin is not settled.
