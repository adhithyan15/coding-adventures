Arabic, chapter 363: Theories and concepts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نظرية (naẓariyya) — a theory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to deduce, then the Arabic for a contradiction.

You'll want to know: نظرية.
نظرية — naẓariyya — "a theory".
درسنا نظرية جديدة في الصف. darasnā naẓariyya jadīda fī aṣ-ṣaff. We studied a new theory in class.
نظريا, naẓariyyan, in theory.

The word, taken apart.
نظرية (naẓariyya) is built on the native Arabic root n-ẓ-r, "to look, to regard" (Lane). Naẓariyya is a modern abstract noun from naẓar, looking, consideration: a way of seeing a thing, a theory.

Lesson 3 of 5.
مفهوم (mafhūm) — a concept.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a theory, then the Arabic for a hypothesis.

You'll want to know: مفهوم.
مفهوم — mafhūm — "a concept".
مفهوم الصداقة مختلف عند كل شخص. mafhūm aṣ-ṣadāqa mukhtalif ʿinda kulli shakhṣ. The concept of friendship differs from person to person.
Also an adjective, understood, clear: هذا مفهوم (mafhūm).

The word, taken apart.
مفهوم (mafhūm) is built on the native Arabic root f-h-m, "to understand" (Lane). Mafhūm is the passive participle of fahima, "to understand": what is understood, a concept.

Lesson 5 of 5.
فسر (fassara) — to interpret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a concept, then the Arabic for a phenomenon.

You'll want to know: فسر.
فسر — fassara — "to interpret".
فسر المعلم القصيدة للطلاب. fassara al-muʿallim al-qaṣīda li-ṭ-ṭullāb. The teacher interpreted the poem for the students.
Present: يفسر, yufassir.
The noun تفسير, tafsīr, an interpretation.

The word, taken apart.
فسر (fassara) is built on the native Arabic root f-s-r, "to uncover, to make plain" (Lane). It is Form II, to make plain: to explain what a thing means.
