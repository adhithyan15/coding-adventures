## You'll want to know: विश्राम्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C382-HITA382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्राम्यति** — *viśrāmyati* — "rests".

> यदि भवान् श्रान्तः, तर्हि क्षणं विश्राम्यतु।
>
> *yadi bhavān śrāntaḥ, tarhi kṣaṇaṃ viśrāmyatu.*
>
> If you are tired, rest for a moment.

Past (he …): सः वृक्षस्य छायायां विश्रान्तवान्, *saḥ vṛkṣasya chāyāyāṃ viśrāntavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्राम्यति** (*viśrāmyati*) is built on *vi* and the root √śram, "to be weary"; MW gives *vi-√śram* "to rest, to repose" and *vi-śrānta* "rested" (MW).
