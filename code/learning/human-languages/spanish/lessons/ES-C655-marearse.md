## You'll want to know: marearse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C655-VUELO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**marearse** — "to feel sick (in a vehicle), to get dizzy".

> Me mareo si leo en el coche.
>
> *I feel sick if I read in the car.*

*Estar mareado* — to feel sick or dizzy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marearse* is built on *mar* "sea", inherited from Latin *mare* (EtymDB); it first meant to be seasick.
