## You'll want to know: कायर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C457-BHAY457-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कायर** — *kāyar* — "cowardly; a coward".

> मेरा कुत्ता बहुत कायर है, चूहे से भी डरता है।
>
> *merā kuttā bahut kāyar hai, cūhe se bhī ḍartā hai.*
>
> My dog is a real coward; it's even afraid of a mouse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कायर** (*kāyar*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *kāyar*, "coward", under Sanskrit *kātara*, "cowardly, timid" (CDIAL 3027); the *t* between vowels wore away to *y*.
