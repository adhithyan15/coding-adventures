## You'll want to know: 酒店
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C425-HOTEL425-02]; assesses=[] -->

**酒店** — *jiǔdiàn* — "big hotel".

> **这家酒店很大** *zhè jiā jiǔdiàn hěn dà* — This hotel is very big.

The new character, in pieces: 酒 — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left the water piece of 洗, three strokes; on the right a bar on top, a frame, a short stroke falling to the left and a vertical bending right inside it, a short bar, and a bar closing the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**酒店** (*jiǔdiàn*) is built from 酒 'wine' and 店 'a shop, an inn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsjuwX* and *temH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as wine and sweet wine; how the pair came to mean a big hotel is not settled.
