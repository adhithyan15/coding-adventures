## You'll want to know: تکذیب کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C310-TAKZIB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تکذیب کردن** (*takzib kardan*) — "to refute, to deny (a report)".

> وزیر این خبر را تکذیب کرد.
>
> *Vazir in khabar râ takzib kard.*
>
> The minister denied this report.

For denying a report or a rumour in public. Present *takzib mikonad*. The opposite is *ta'yid kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*takzib* is usually traced to Arabic *takdhīb*, "calling something a lie"; EtymDB lists no entry. *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
