## You'll want to know: ਦੁੱਖ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C327-UPSET327-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੁੱਖ** — *dukkh* — "sorrow".

> ਉਸ ਖ਼ਬਰ ਤੋਂ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੁੱਖ ਹੋਇਆ।
>
> *us khabar ton mainū̃ bahut dukkh hoiā.*
>
> That news made me very sad.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੁੱਖ** (*dukkh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *duḥkha*, "difficulty, pain"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dukkh*, "sorrow, pain", under it (CDIAL 6375).
