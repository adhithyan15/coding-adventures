Arabic, chapter 91: Road, Bank, Station, Car, Bus.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طريق (ṭarīq) — a road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a shop, then the Arabic for a street.

You'll want to know: طريق.
طريق — ṭarīq — "a road".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ṭarīq]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ṭarīq, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ṭarīq]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a shop, then the Arabic for a street, then say ṭarīq again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does طريق (ṭarīq) mean? ("A road".) Say it once more.
