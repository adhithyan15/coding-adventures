## You'll want to know: измена
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C494-MARRIAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**измена** (*izména*) — "infidelity; betrayal".

> Она не смогла простить мужу измену.
>
> *Oná ne smoglá prostít múzhu izménu.*
>
> She couldn't forgive her husband for being unfaithful.

Feminine. Also treason: *измена родине*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*измена* is built on *из-* and the root of *менять* 'to change'; EtymDB lists *измена* without a source.
