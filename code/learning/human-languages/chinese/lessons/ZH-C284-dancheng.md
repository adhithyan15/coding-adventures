## You'll want to know: dānchéng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C284-DANCHENG-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dānchéng* — "one way", of a ticket or a trip.

> *yì zhāng dānchéng piào* — a one-way ticket

> *dānchéng háishi wǎngfǎn* — One way or return?

*wǎngfǎn*, there and back, is the other choice. Its characters come later; for now *dānchéng* is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dānchéng* is built from dān 'single' and chéng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tan* and *drjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'single, not double' and the second 'a term; a limit'; 'one way' for the pair, a single stage of a journey, is the word's own sense.
