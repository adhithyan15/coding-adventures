Persian, chapter 183: To marry, To celebrate, To congratulate, To meet, To get to know.

Lesson 1 of 5.
ازدواج کردن (ezdevâj kardan) — to marry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to translate, then the Persian for to talk.

You'll want to know: ازدواج کردن.
ازدواج کردن — ezdevâj kardan — "to marry".

The word, taken apart.
ezdevâj kardan pairs ezdevâj, borrowed from Arabic izdiwāj, "pairing, marriage" (root z-w-j, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
جشن گرفتن (jashn gereftan) — to celebrate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to talk, then the Persian for to marry.

You'll want to know: جشن گرفتن.
جشن گرفتن — jashn gereftan — "to celebrate".

The word, taken apart.
jashn gereftan is "to take a festival": jashn is usually traced to Middle Persian jašn, "festival", from the old word for "worship" that Avestan wrote yasna. Both parts are native. gereftan, "to take", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root gʰrebh₂-, "to seize".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: تبریک گفتن.
تبریک گفتن — tabrik goftan — "to congratulate".

The word, taken apart.
tabrik goftan is "to say a blessing": tabrik is borrowed from Arabic tabrīk, "blessing, congratulation" (EtymDB; root b-r-k, Lane), and goftan, "to say", is native (Middle Persian guftan, EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
âshnâ shodan pairs âshnâ, "acquainted", with shodan, "to become". âshnâ is native, usually traced to Middle Persian āšnāg, "known". shodan, "to become", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian šudan, "to go".
