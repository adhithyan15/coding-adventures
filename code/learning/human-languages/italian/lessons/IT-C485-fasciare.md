## You'll want to know: fasciare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C485-STORTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**fasciare** — "to bandage, to wrap".

> Il medico mi ha fasciato la mano.
>
> *The doctor bandaged my hand.*

The bandage is *la fascia* or *la benda*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fasciare* is built on *fascia* "band", inherited from Latin *fascia* "a band, a bandage" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
