## You'll want to know: жалоба
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C434-ZHALOBA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**жалоба** (*zháloba*) — "a complaint".

> Туристы подали жалобу на шум.
>
> *Turísty podáli zhálobu na shum.*
>
> The tourists made a complaint about the noise.

Feminine. Takes *на* + accusative: *жалоба на соседей*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*жалоба* goes with *жалеть* 'to pity' and *жаль* 'a pity': to complain is to call for pity. EtymDB lists *жалоба* with the sense 'complaint' but without a source.
