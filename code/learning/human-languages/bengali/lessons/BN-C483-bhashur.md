## You'll want to know: ভাশুর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C483-JAMAI483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাশুর** — *bhāshur* — "a husband's elder brother".

> বিয়ের পরে মীরা ভাশুরকে প্রথম দেখল।
>
> *biyer pôre mīrā bhāshurke prothom dekhlô.*
>
> After the wedding Mira met her husband's elder brother for the first time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাশুর** (*bhāshur*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under Sanskrit *bhrātṛśvaśura*, "brother-father-in-law" (CDIAL 9669): an elder brother of the husband, treated with a father-in-law's respect.
