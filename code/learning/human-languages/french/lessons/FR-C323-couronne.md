## You'll want to know: la couronne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C323-REGNE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la couronne** — "the crown; the wreath".

> Le roi portait une couronne d'or.
>
> *The king wore a gold crown.*

Also a ring of flowers or of bread: *une couronne de fleurs*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*couronne* is inherited from Latin *corōna* "a wreath, a crown" (EtymDB), itself a borrowing from Greek.
