# हुलकावणी देणे (hulkāvṇī deṇe) — to elude; to give the slip

## You'll want to know: हुलकावणी देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C553-DHYEY553-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हुलकावणी देणे** — *hulkāvṇī deṇe* — "to elude; to give the slip".

> यशाने त्याला दोनदा हुलकावणी दिली.
>
> *yaśāne tyālā dondā hulkāvṇī dilī.*
>
> Success eluded him twice.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने हुलकावणी दिली, *tyāne hulkāvṇī dilī*.

The one dodged takes -ला: त्याला हुलकावणी दिली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हुलकावणी देणे** (*hulkāvṇī deṇe*) is हुलकावणी, "a dodge, a feint", with देणे; the origin of हुलकावणी is not settled.
