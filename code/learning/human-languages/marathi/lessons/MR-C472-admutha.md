## You'll want to know: आडमुठा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C472-HATTI472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आडमुठा** — *āḍmuṭhā* — "obstinate; pig-headed".

> तो इतका आडमुठा आहे की कोणाचंच ऐकत नाही.
>
> *to itkā āḍmuṭhā āhe kī koṇācañc aikat nāhī.*
>
> He is so pig-headed that he listens to no one.

Agrees; harsher than हट्टी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आडमुठा** (*āḍmuṭhā*) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
