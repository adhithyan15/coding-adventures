## You'll want to know: el pretexto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C586-JUSTIF-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el pretexto** — "the pretext".

> Usó la lluvia como pretexto.
>
> *He used the rain as a pretext.*

*Con el pretexto de* — on the pretext of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pretexto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praetextus* 'a cover, a show', built on *praetexere* 'to weave in front', from *texere* 'to weave' (Lewis & Short).
