Arabic, chapter 141: Tongue, Belly, Leg, Skin, Bone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لسان (lisān) — a tongue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a heel, then the Arabic for a lip.
[pause 1 second]
Say Sunday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday. (Al-aḥad, al-khamīs, al-jumʿa, as-sabt.)

You'll want to know: لسان.
لسان — lisān — "a tongue".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: lisān]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: lisān, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say lisān]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a heel, then the Arabic for a lip, then say lisān again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does لسان (lisān) mean? ("A tongue".) Say it once more.
