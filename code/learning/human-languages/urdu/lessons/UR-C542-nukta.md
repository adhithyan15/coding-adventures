## You'll want to know: نکتہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C542-KHAIR542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نکتہ** — *nuktā* — "a point (in an argument)".

> اگر یہی نکتہ ہے تو بات ختم ہوئی۔
>
> *agar yihī nuktā hai to bāt khatm huī.*
>
> If that is the point, then there is nothing more to say.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نکتہ** (*nuktā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nukta*, "a dot; a subtle point".
