## You'll want to know: હરખ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C447-HARAKH447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**હરખ** — *harakh* — "delight; joy at good news".

> દીકરાનું લગ્ન નક્કી થયું, તેથી દાદીને ઘણો હરખ થયો.
>
> *dīkrānũ lagna nakkī thayũ, tethī dādīne ghaṇo harakh thayo.*
>
> My grandmother was filled with delight when her son's wedding was fixed.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હરખ** (*harakh*) probably goes back to Sanskrit *harṣa*, "joy, delight", which Monier-Williams records; it is the everyday spoken shape of that learned word.
