## You'll want to know: નિબંધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C244-SUBJECT244-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નિબંધ** — *nibandh* — "essay".

> **તે ગુજરાતીમાં નિબંધ લખે છે.** — *te gujarātīmā̃ nibandh lakhe che* — "She writes an essay in Gujarati."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિબંધ** (*nibandh*) is Sanskrit *nibandha*, "fastening" and later "literary composition", taken over whole as a tatsama (CDIAL 7222).
