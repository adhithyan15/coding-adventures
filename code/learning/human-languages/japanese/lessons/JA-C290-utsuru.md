## You'll want to know: うつる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C290-UTSURU290-01]; assesses=[] -->

**うつる** — *utsuru* — "to move (house); to move over".

> **かぞくで いなかに うつりました。** — *kazoku de inaka ni utsurimashita* — "Our family moved to the country."

Polite **うつります**, *utsurimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが となりの まちに うつりました。**, *ani ga tonari no machi ni utsurimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うつる** (*utsuru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
