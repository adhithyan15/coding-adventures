Sanskrit, chapter 88: Girls, Boys and Friends.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बालिका (bālikā) — a girl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a hundred, then the Sanskrit for a thousand.

You'll want to know: बालिका.
बालिका (bālikā) — "a girl". बालः is "a boy"; -इका makes the girl.

What you've built.
The boy word, made feminine.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bālikā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bālikā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bālikā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for a hundred, then the Sanskrit for a thousand, then say bālikā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does बालिका (bālikā) mean? ("A girl".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.

सखी (sakhī) — "a friend", said of a woman or a girl; मित्रम् is any friend.
