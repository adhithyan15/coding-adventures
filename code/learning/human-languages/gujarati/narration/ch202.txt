Gujarati, chapter 202: A theft in the bazaar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચોરવું (corvũ) — to steal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for barber, then the Gujarati for carpenter.

You'll want to know: ચોરવું.
ચોરવું — corvũ — "to steal".
કોઈએ મારું પાકીટ ચોર્યું. — koīe mārũ pākīṭ coryũ — "Someone stole my wallet."

The word, taken apart.
ચોરવું (corvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit cōráyati, "steals", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to steal" (CDIAL 4933).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for police station, then the Gujarati for to rob.

You'll want to know: જડવું.
જડવું — jaḍvũ — "to be found, to turn up".
મને પાકીટ જડ્યું. — mane pākīṭ jaḍyũ — "I found my wallet."
The finder takes -ને: મને જડ્યું.

The word, taken apart.
જડવું (jaḍvũ) is listed by CDIAL as jaṛvũ, "to join, meet with, set jewels", under jaḍati, "joins, sets", a word that CDIAL takes from Dravidian, citing Bloch (CDIAL 5091). Of its senses, "meet with" is the nearest to "to be found".
