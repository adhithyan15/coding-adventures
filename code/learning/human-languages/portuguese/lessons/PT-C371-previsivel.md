## You'll want to know: previsível
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C371-IMPREVISTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**previsível** — "predictable".

> O final do filme era muito previsível.
>
> *The end of the film was very predictable.*

Built on *prever* + *-ível*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*previsível* is built on *previsto*, the participle of *prever* "to foresee", with *-ível*; the verb rests on Latin *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
