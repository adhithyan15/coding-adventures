Malayalam, chapter 174: Stop, Wait, Search, Find, Forget.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
നിർത്തുക (nirttuka) — to stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to teach, then the Malayalam for to begin.

You'll want to know: നിർത്തുക.
നിർത്തുക — nirttuka — "to stop".

The word, taken apart.
നിർത്തുക (nirttuka) is native Dravidian, the causative of the verb "to stand": "make stand"; DEDR 3675 lists the root with Tamil nil and Kannada nil.

Lesson 2 of 5.
കാത്തിരിക്കുക (kāttirikkuka) — to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to begin, then the Malayalam for to stop.
[pause 1 second]
Say "a story." (Katha.)

You'll want to know: കാത്തിരിക്കുക.
കാത്തിരിക്കുക — kāttirikkuka — "to wait".

The word, taken apart.
കാത്തിരിക്കുക (kāttirikkuka) is native Dravidian, "sit watching": the verb "to guard, watch, wait" (DEDR 1416, with Tamil kā) joined to the verb "to sit, to be" (DEDR 480, with Tamil iru).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: കണ്ടെത്തുക.
കണ്ടെത്തുക — kaṇṭettuka — "to find".

The word, taken apart.
കണ്ടെത്തുക (kaṇṭettuka) is native Dravidian, "reach having seen": the past of the seeing verb (DEDR 1443, with Tamil kāṇ) and the verb "to reach, arrive" (DEDR 809, with Tamil eytu).
