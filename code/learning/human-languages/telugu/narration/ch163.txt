Telugu, chapter 163: To suspect, To realize, To pay attention to, To compete, To kick.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అనుమానించు (anumāniñcu) — to suspect, to doubt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to praise, then the Telugu for to describe.

You'll want to know: అనుమానించు.
అనుమానించు — anumāniñcu — "to suspect, to doubt".

The word, taken apart.
అనుమానించు (anumāniñcu) is Sanskrit anumāna, "inference, thought" (CDIAL 328), made into a verb with the Telugu ending -iñcu. Telugu gives it the sense "suspicion".

The word, taken apart.
గ్రహించు (grahiñcu) is the Sanskrit root grah, "to seize, grasp", made into a verb with the Telugu ending -iñcu. CDIAL 4364 lists its noun grahaṇa, "seizing, holding".

Lesson 3 of 5.
పట్టించుకో (paṭṭiñcukō) — to pay attention to, to care about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to suspect, then the Telugu for to realize.

You'll want to know: పట్టించుకో.
పట్టించుకో — paṭṭiñcukō — "to pay attention to, to care about".

The word, taken apart.
పట్టించుకో (paṭṭiñcukō) is native Dravidian, built on paṭṭu, "to hold, catch", one word with Kannada paṭṭu, "to seize" (DEDR 4034), which also lists paṭṭimpu, "attention, not overlooking". The ending -kō means "for oneself" (DEDR 2151).
