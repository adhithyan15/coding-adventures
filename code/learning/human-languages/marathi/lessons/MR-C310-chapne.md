## You'll want to know: छापणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C310-VRUTTAPATRA310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**छापणे** — *chāpṇe* — "to print".

> त्या छापखान्याने हजार पुस्तकं छापली.
>
> *tyā chāpkhānyāne hajār pustakaṁ chāplī.*
>
> That press printed a thousand books.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने छापलं, *tyāne chāplaṁ*.

What is printed agrees: पुस्तक छापलं, बातमी छापली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छापणे** (*chāpṇe*) is the Marathi verb for printing and stamping; its origin is not settled.

One more word for telling what happened.
