## You'll want to know: धुंडाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C291-SUTKA291-05]; assesses=[] -->

**धुंडाळणे** — *dhuṇḍāḷṇe* — "to comb through; to search high and low".

> आम्ही हरवलेल्या मुलासाठी सगळं गाव धुंडाळलं.
>
> *āmhī haravlelyā mulāsāṭhī sagḷaṁ gāv dhuṇḍāḷlaṁ.*
>
> We searched the whole village for the lost child.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने धुंडाळलं, *tyāne dhuṇḍāḷlaṁ*.

The place searched is the object: घर धुंडाळणे, to search the whole house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धुंडाळणे** (*dhuṇḍāḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *dhuṇḍāḷṇe*, 'to search', under *\*dhūṇḍh*, 'seek' (CDIAL 6839).
