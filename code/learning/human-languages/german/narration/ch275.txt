German, chapter 275: Angeblich.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
angeblich — supposedly, allegedly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the claim, then the German for to confirm.

You'll want to know: angeblich.
angeblich — "supposedly, allegedly".
Angeblich war der Dieb ein junger Mann. Supposedly the thief was a young man.
The speaker does not promise that it is true.

The word, taken apart.
angeblich is built on angeben "to state", from geben "to give", Old High German geban (EtymDB): as it is stated.

Lesson 2 of 5.
offenbar — apparently, evidently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to confirm, then the German for supposedly.

You'll want to know: offenbar.
offenbar — "apparently, evidently".
Er war offenbar sehr müde. He was evidently very tired.
It sounds sure, because there is a sign: Offenbar hat es geregnet, die Straße ist nass.

The word, taken apart.
offenbar joins offen "open", an inherited Germanic word, and the ending -bar: plain to see. EtymDB lists no parent for the adverb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
zufolge — according to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for supposedly, then the German for apparently.

You'll want to know: zufolge.
zufolge — "according to".
Dem Bericht zufolge gab es keine Verletzten. According to the report there were no injured people.
It follows a dative noun: dem Bericht zufolge. Formal and common in the news.

The word, taken apart.
zufolge joins zu and Folge "what follows", from Middle High German volge (EtymDB): following the report.

Lesson 4 of 5.
vermuten — to suspect, to presume.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for apparently, then the German for according to.

You'll want to know: vermuten.
vermuten — "to suspect, to presume".
Ich vermutete, dass er krank war. I suspected that he was ill.
With dass and the verb at the end: Ich vermute, dass er recht hat.

The word, taken apart.
vermuten is built on Mut, once "mind, mood", from Proto-Germanic mōdaz (EtymDB), with the prefix ver-: to have something in mind.

vermutlich is built on vermuten "to suspect", which rests on Mut "mind", from Proto-Germanic mōdaz (EtymDB).
