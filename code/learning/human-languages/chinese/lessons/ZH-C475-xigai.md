## You'll want to know: xīgài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C475-BODY475-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xīgài* — "knee".

> *wǒ zǒu le yì tiān lù xīgài hěn téng* — I walked all day, and my knees really hurt.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīgài* is built from *xī* 'the knee' and *gài* 'a cover', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sit* and *kajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as the joint of the leg and glosses the second 'to cover'.
