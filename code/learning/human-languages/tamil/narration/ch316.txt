Tamil, chapter 316: That Very Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பின்னர் (piṉṉar) — later on; afterwards.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for at present, then the Tamil for once in a while.

You'll want to know: பின்னர்.
பின்னர் — piṉṉar — "later on; afterwards".
முதலில் நாங்கள் சாப்பிட்டோம், பின்னர் கடைக்குப் போனோம். mutalil nāṅkaḷ cāppiṭṭōm, piṉṉar kaṭaikkup pōṉōm. First we ate, and later on we went to the shop.
A more formal word for பிறகு.

The word, taken apart.
பின்னர் (piṉṉar) is native Dravidian: it is built on பின், "back, what comes after" (DEDR 4205).

Lesson 2 of 5.
அன்றைய (aṉṟaiya) — of that day; that day's.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for once in a while, then the Tamil for later on.

You'll want to know: அன்றைய.
அன்றைய — aṉṟaiya — "of that day; that day's".
அன்றைய நாளிதழில் அந்தச் செய்தி வந்தது. aṉṟaiya nāḷitaḻil antac ceyti vantatu. That news appeared in that day's newspaper.
Like இன்றைய, today's, and நேற்றைய, yesterday's; only before a noun.

The word, taken apart.
அன்றைய (aṉṟaiya) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1 lists அன்று and அன்றை, "that day", built on the far-pointing அ-.

Before the new one: say the Tamil for later on, then the Tamil for of that day.

You'll want to know: முந்தைய.
முந்தைய — muntaiya — "previous; earlier".
முந்தைய நாள் இரவு நல்ல மழை பெய்தது. muntaiya nāḷ iravu nalla maḻai peytatu. The night before, it rained heavily.

The word, taken apart.
முந்தைய (muntaiya) is native Dravidian, from முந்து, "to come in front, be prior in time" (DEDR 5020).

Lesson 4 of 5.
அதன்பின் (ataṉpiṉ) — after that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for of that day, then the Tamil for previous.

You'll want to know: அதன்பின்.
அதன்பின் — ataṉpiṉ — "after that".
அதன்பின் அவனை யாரும் பார்க்கவில்லை. ataṉpiṉ avaṉai yārum pārkkavillai. After that, nobody saw him.
Like அதன் பிறகு; written as one word or two.

The word, taken apart.
அதன்பின் (ataṉpiṉ) is built from அதன், "of that", and native பின், "back, what comes after" (DEDR 4205).

Lesson 5 of 5.
அதே சமயம் (atē camayam) — at the same time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for previous, then the Tamil for after that.

You'll want to know: அதே சமயம்.
அதே சமயம் — atē camayam — "at the same time".
அதே சமயம், வெளியே ஒரு சத்தம் கேட்டது. atē camayam, veḷiyē oru cattam kēṭṭatu. At the same time, a noise was heard outside.
அதே, that same + சமயம், time.

The word, taken apart.
அதே சமயம் (atē camayam) joins அதே, "that same", with சமயம், "time, occasion", borrowed from Sanskrit samaya, "meeting, time" (CDIAL 13185).
