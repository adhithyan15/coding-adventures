Urdu, chapter 434: Waves and Marshes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
موج (mauj) — a wave; a billow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a straw, then the Urdu for to be uprooted.

You'll want to know: موج.
موج — mauj — "a wave; a billow".
سمندر میں اونچی موجیں ہیں۔ samandar meṅ ū̃chī maujeṅ haiṅ. There are high waves on the sea.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
موج (mauj) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mawj, "waves, surging".

Lesson 2 of 5.
دلدل (daldal) — a marsh; a swamp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be uprooted, then the Urdu for a billow.

You'll want to know: دلدل.
دلدل — daldal — "a marsh; a swamp".
دریا کے پاس ایک دلدل ہے۔ daryā ke pās ek daldal hai. There is a marsh near the river.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
دلدل (daldal) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi daldal, "a marsh", under dal, "quiver", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6212): a marsh is ground that shakes underfoot.

Lesson 4 of 5.
اچھالنا (uchhālnā) — to toss (up); to throw up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a marsh, then the Urdu for a landing place (on a river).

You'll want to know: اچھالنا.
اچھالنا — uchhālnā — "to toss (up); to throw up".
موجوں نے کشتی کو اچھالا۔ maujoṅ ne kashtī ko uchhālā. The waves tossed the boat about.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گیند اچھالی, us ne gend uchhālī.

The word, taken apart.
اچھالنا (uchhālnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin uchālnā, "to throw up", under ut-śālayati, "causes to leap up", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1848); اچھلنا, "to bounce", is its partner.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ڈبونا (ḍubonā) — to dip; to sink (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a landing place (on a river), then the Urdu for to toss (up).

You'll want to know: ڈبونا.
ڈبونا — ḍubonā — "to dip; to sink (something)".
سارا نے دریا میں ہاتھ ڈبوئے۔ sārā ne daryā meṅ hāth ḍuboe. Sara dipped her hands in the river.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کشتی ڈبوئی, us ne kashtī ḍuboī.

The word, taken apart.
ڈبونا (ḍubonā) is built on ḍūbnā, "to sink", which comes from ḍubb, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5561): this is the form for putting something under water.
