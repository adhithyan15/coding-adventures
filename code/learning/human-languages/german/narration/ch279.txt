German, chapter 279: Was geschah.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Ereignis — the event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to sign, then the German for the conference.

You'll want to know: das Ereignis.
das Ereignis — "the event".
Die Hochzeit war das große Ereignis des Jahres. The wedding was the big event of the year.
Plural: die Ereignisse.

The word, taken apart.
Ereignis is usually traced to Middle High German eröugen "to bring before the eyes", built on Auge "eye"; the spelling with -ei- came later. EtymDB lists no parent.

Der Unfall ereignete sich am frühen Morgen. The accident occurred early in the morning.

ereignen is usually traced to Middle High German eröugen "to show, to bring before the eyes", built on Auge "eye"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
stattfinden — to take place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the event, then the German for to occur.

You'll want to know: stattfinden.
stattfinden — "to take place".
Das Konzert fand im Park statt. The concert took place in the park.
Separable: es findet statt, es hat stattgefunden. For planned events.

The word, taken apart.
stattfinden joins the old noun Statt "place" and finden "to find", a cousin of English find (EtymDB): to find its place.

Lesson 4 of 5.
vorkommen — to happen, to occur now and then; to seem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to occur, then the German for to take place.

You'll want to know: vorkommen.
vorkommen — "to happen, to occur now and then; to seem".
So etwas kommt manchmal vor. Something like that happens sometimes.
Separable, Perfekt with sein: Das ist schon oft vorgekommen. Das kommt mir bekannt vor — that seems familiar to me.

The word, taken apart.
vorkommen joins vor "forward" and kommen "to come", from Old High German kweman, Proto-Germanic kwemaną (EtymDB): to come forward.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: der Vorfall.
der Vorfall — "the incident".
Nach dem Vorfall rief er die Polizei. After the incident he called the police.
Plural: die Vorfälle.

The word, taken apart.
Vorfall joins vor and Fall "a fall, a case", from Old High German val (EtymDB): something that falls out before you.
