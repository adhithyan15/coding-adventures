## You'll want to know: ہیلمٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C395-TAYAR395-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ہیلمٹ** — *helmeṭ* — "a helmet".

> موٹر سائیکل پر ہیلمٹ پہننا چاہیے۔
>
> *moṭar sāikil par helmeṭ pahannā chāhiye.*
>
> You should wear a helmet on a motorbike.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہیلمٹ** (*helmeṭ*) is borrowed from English *helmet*.
