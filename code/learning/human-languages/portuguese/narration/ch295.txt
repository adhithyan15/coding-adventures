Portuguese, chapter 295: Suspeitos e detidos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o suspeito — the suspect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the detective, then the Portuguese for the clue.

You'll want to know: o suspeito.
o suspeito — "the suspect".
O suspeito tem trinta anos. The suspect is thirty years old.
Also an adjective: um carro suspeito.

The word, taken apart.
suspeito comes from Latin suspectus "suspected", from suspicere "to look up at, to suspect", built on specere "to look" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
deter — to arrest, to detain; to stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the suspect, then the Portuguese for the suspicion.

You'll want to know: deter.
deter — "to arrest, to detain; to stop".
A polícia deteve dois homens. The police arrested two men.
Conjugated like ter: detenho, deteve.

The word, taken apart.
deter is inherited from Latin dētinēre "to hold back, to detain" (EtymDB), built on tenēre "to hold".

Lesson 4 of 5.

a detenção — "the arrest, the detention".
A detenção aconteceu de manhã cedo. The arrest happened early in the morning.
On the news: foi feita uma detenção.

The word, taken apart.
detenção was borrowed from Latin dētentiō "a holding back" (EtymDB), from dētinēre.

Lesson 5 of 5.
o detido — the person arrested, the detainee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to arrest, then the Portuguese for the arrest.

You'll want to know: o detido.
o detido — "the person arrested, the detainee".
O detido vai ser ouvido amanhã. The man arrested will be questioned tomorrow.
The past participle of deter used as a noun.

The word, taken apart.
detido is the past participle of deter "to arrest" used as a noun; the verb comes from Latin dētinēre "to hold back" (EtymDB).
