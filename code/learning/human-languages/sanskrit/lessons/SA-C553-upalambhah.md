## You'll want to know: उपालम्भः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C553-SKHALANA553-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उपालम्भः** — *upālambhaḥ* — "a reproach".

> यदि अहं सत्यम् अवदिष्यम्, तर्हि उपालम्भः न अभविष्यत्।
>
> *yadi ahaṃ satyam avadiṣyam, tarhi upālambhaḥ na abhaviṣyat.*
>
> If I had told the truth, there would have been no reproach.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपालम्भः** (*upālambhaḥ*) is built on *upa*, *ā* and the root √labh, "to take hold of", which also gives *labhate*; MW lists *upālambha* under *upālabh*, "to censure, to reproach", and gives it as reproach, censure, finding fault (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
