Arabic, chapter 438: Sightseeing.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
سياحة (siyāḥa) — tourism.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to guide, then the Arabic for an itinerary.

You'll want to know: سياحة.
سياحة — siyāḥa — "tourism".
تعمل أختي في السياحة. taʿmalu ukhtī fī as-siyāḥa. My sister works in tourism.

The word, taken apart.
سياحة (siyāḥa) is built on the native Arabic root s-y-ḥ, "to flow; to roam the land" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of sāḥa, "to travel about".

Lesson 5 of 6.
رحالة (raḥḥāla) — a great traveller; an explorer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an outing, then the Arabic for to wander around.

You'll want to know: رحالة.
رحالة — raḥḥāla — "a great traveller; an explorer".
كتب الرحالة عن رحلته في الصحراء. kataba ar-raḥḥāla ʿan riḥlatihi fī aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ. The explorer wrote about his journey in the desert.
The ending -a here marks a man who does a thing a great deal, not a woman.

The word, taken apart.
رحالة (raḥḥāla) is built on the native Arabic root r-ḥ-l, "to set out on a journey" (Lane). It shares its root with riḥla, "a journey".
