## You'll want to know: प्रजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C327-PRAJA327-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रजा** — *prajā* — "subjects; the people of a king".

> तो राजा प्रजेची नेहमी काळजी घ्यायचा.
>
> *to rājā prajecī nehmī kāḷjī ghyāycā.*
>
> That king always took care of his subjects.

Feminine.

Singular even for a whole people: प्रजा सुखी होती.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रजा** (*prajā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prajā*, 'offspring; people, subjects' (Monier-Williams).
