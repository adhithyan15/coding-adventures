## You'll want to know: راهبندان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C426-RAHBANDAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**راهبندان** (*râhbandân*) — "a traffic jam, a hold-up".

> به خاطر راهبندان دیر به خانه رسیدیم.
>
> *Be khâter-e râhbandân dir be khâne residim.*
>
> Because of the traffic jam we got home late.

Literally 'road-blocking': *râh* 'road' + *band* 'tie, block' + *-ân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*râhbandân* is a native compound: *râh* 'road' goes back to Middle Persian *rāh* (EtymDB), and *band* 'a tie, a block' to the root of *bastan* 'to bind', which EtymDB traces to PIE *bʰendʰ-*.
