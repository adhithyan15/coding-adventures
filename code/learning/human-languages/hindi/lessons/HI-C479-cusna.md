## You'll want to know: चूसना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C479-MUNH479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चूसना** — *cūsnā* — "to suck".

> बच्चा अपना अंगूठा चूसता है।
>
> *baccā apnā aṅgūṭhā cūstā hai.*
>
> The baby sucks its thumb.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने आम चूसा, *usne ām cūsā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूसना** (*cūsnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *cūsnā*, "to suck", under Sanskrit *cūṣati*, "sucks" (CDIAL 4898). A ripe mango is often sucked rather than cut.
