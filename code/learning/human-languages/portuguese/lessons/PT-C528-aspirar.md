## You'll want to know: aspirar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C528-LIMPEZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**aspirar** — "to vacuum, to hoover; to breathe in".

> Ao sábado aspiro a casa toda.
>
> *On Saturdays I vacuum the whole house.*

Also to aspire: *aspirar a uma vida melhor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aspirar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aspīrāre* "to breathe on, to aspire" (EtymDB), built on *spīrāre* "to breathe".
