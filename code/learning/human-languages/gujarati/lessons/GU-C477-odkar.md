## You'll want to know: ઓડકાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C477-ODKAR477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓડકાર** — *oḍkār* — "a belch".

> જમ્યા પછી દાદાએ મોટો ઓડકાર ખાધો.
>
> *jamyā pachī dādāe moṭo oḍkār khādho.*
>
> After the meal my grandfather gave a loud belch.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓડકાર** (*oḍkār*) probably goes back to Sanskrit *udgāra*, "the act of discharging, spitting out", as Monier-Williams records it.
