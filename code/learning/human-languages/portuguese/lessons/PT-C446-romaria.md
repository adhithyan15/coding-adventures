## You'll want to know: a romaria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C446-ARRAIAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a romaria** — "the pilgrimage festival, the pilgrimage".

> A aldeia inteira vai à romaria.
>
> *The whole village goes to the pilgrimage festival.*

A trip to a chapel, then food, music and dancing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*romaria* is built on *romeiro* 'pilgrim' with *-ia* (EtymDB lists *romaria* without giving a source).
