## You'll want to know: неисправный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C434-ZHALOBA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**неисправный** (*neisprávnyy*) — "out of order, faulty".

> В номере был неисправный холодильник.
>
> *V nómere byl neisprávnyy kholodílnik.*
>
> There was a fridge in the room that was out of order.

The opposite is *исправный* — in working order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*неисправный* is built from *не-* 'not' and *исправный* 'in good order', from *исправить* 'to put right', on *править* 'to set right'. EtymDB lists *неисправный* without a deeper source.
