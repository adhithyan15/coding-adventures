Portuguese, chapter 101: Necklace, Photograph, Candle, Calendar, Message.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o colar — a necklace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a wallet, then the Portuguese for a ring.

You'll want to know: o colar.
o colar — "a necklace".

The word, taken apart.
colar from Latin collare, "neckband" (Lewis & Short), from collum, "neck" — inherited.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a necklace, then the Portuguese for a photograph.

You'll want to know: a vela.
a vela — "a candle".

The word, taken apart.
vela comes from the verb "to keep watch", which from Latin vigilare, "to stay awake" (Lewis & Short): a candle kept the night watch.

The word, taken apart.
mensagem from Old French message, "message" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. Behind it is Late Latin missaticum, from mittere, "to send"; the -n- crept in later.
