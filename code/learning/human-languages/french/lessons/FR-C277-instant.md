## You'll want to know: l'instant
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C277-ETAPE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'instant** — "the instant, the moment".

> Pendant un instant, personne n'a parlé.
>
> *For a moment, nobody spoke.*

*Pour l'instant* — for now; *à l'instant* — a moment ago.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*instant* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnstāns* "present, pressing" (EtymDB), from *īnstāre* "to stand near, to press on" (Lewis & Short).
