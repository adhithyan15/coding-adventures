## You'll want to know: zǒngjié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C371-THOUGH371-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒngjié* — "to sum up".

> *lǎoshī zuìhòu zǒngjié le jīntiān de kè* — At the end the teacher summed up the lesson.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒngjié* is built from *zǒng* and *jié*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsuwngX* and *ket* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to bind together' and the second 'to tie'.
