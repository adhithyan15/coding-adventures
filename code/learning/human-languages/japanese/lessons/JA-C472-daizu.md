## You'll want to know: だいず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C472-INGR472-05]; assesses=[] -->

**だいず** — *daizu* — "soybeans".

> **なっとうは だいずの たべものです。** — *nattō wa daizu no tabemono desu* — "Natto is a food made from soybeans."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だいず** (*daizu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dai*, "large" (Middle Chinese *dajH*) and *zu*, "beans" (*duwH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
