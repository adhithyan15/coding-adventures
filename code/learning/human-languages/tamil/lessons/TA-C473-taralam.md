# தாராளம் (tārāḷam) — generosity; plenty

## You'll want to know: தாராளம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C473-AZHAKU473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**தாராளம்** — *tārāḷam* — "generosity; plenty".

> அவர் ஏழைகளுக்குத் தாராளமாக உதவினார்.
>
> *avar ēḻaikaḷukkut tārāḷamāka utaviṉār.*
>
> He helped the poor generously.

தாராளமாக, generously; also plenty. There is plenty of room: தாராளமாக இடம் இருக்கிறது.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **தாராளம்** (*tārāḷam*) is not settled; none of our sources lists it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
