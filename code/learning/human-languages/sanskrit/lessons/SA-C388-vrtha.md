## You'll want to know: वृथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C388-VYAVAHARA388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वृथा** — *vṛthā* — "in vain".

> यदि भवान् न शृणोति, तर्हि मम उपदेशः वृथा।
>
> *yadi bhavān na śṛṇoti, tarhi mama upadeśaḥ vṛthā.*
>
> If you do not listen, my advice is in vain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वृथा** (*vṛthā*) MW gives *vṛthā* "at will" and "in vain, uselessly, fruitlessly"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
