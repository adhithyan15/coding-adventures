Mandarin Chinese, chapter 424: On the Road.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
suìdào — tunnel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to head down south, then the Mandarin for north and south.

You'll want to know: suìdào.
suìdào — "tunnel".
qiánbian yǒu yì tiáo suìdào — There's a tunnel up ahead.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
suìdào is built from suì 'a passage' and dào 'a way', both inherited from Middle Chinese, zwijH and dawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the passage into a tomb and the second 'a road'.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 2 of 6.
diàotóu — to turn round (in a car); to make a U-turn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for north and south, then the Mandarin for a tunnel through a hill.

You'll want to know: diàotóu.
diàotóu — "to turn round (in a car); to make a U-turn".
zhèr bù néng diàotóu wǎng qián kāi — You can't turn round here: drive on.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
diàotóu is built from diào 'to turn' and 头 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dewH and duw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to shake'; the sense 'to turn' is not given there.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to make a U-turn, then the Mandarin for a road sign.

You'll want to know: 时速.
时速 — shísù — "speed per hour".
我们的时速是一百公里 wǒmen de shísù shì yìbǎi gōnglǐ — We're doing a hundred kilometres an hour.

The word, taken apart.
时速 (shísù) is built from 时 'an hour' and 速 'speed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyi and suwk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
