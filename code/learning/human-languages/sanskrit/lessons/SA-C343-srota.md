## You'll want to know: श्रोता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C343-VADA343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रोता** — *śrotā* — "a listener".

> श्रोता शान्तः तिष्ठेत्।
>
> *śrotā śāntaḥ tiṣṭhet.*
>
> A listener should stay quiet.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रोता** (*śrotā*) is built on the root √śru, "to hear", with the agent ending *-tṛ*; MW gives *śrotṛ* "one who hears, a hearer" (MW).
