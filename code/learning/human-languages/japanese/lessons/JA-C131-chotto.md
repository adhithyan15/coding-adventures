## You'll want to know: ちょっと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-CHOTTO]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01, JA-SCRIPT-TO-01] -->

**ちょっと** — *chotto* — "a little", and, said on its own with a small pause,
"one moment".

Three beats: *cho*, a held silence, *to*. The silence is the small **っ** you
already write. The first beat is **ち** with a small sign after it that you do not
write yet; it joins forward, the way **ゃ** does, and the next lesson
writes it.
