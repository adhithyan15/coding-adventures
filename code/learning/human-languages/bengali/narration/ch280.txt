Bengali, chapter 280: The Rescue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পৌঁছানো (poũchhāno) — to arrive; to reach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to burn down, then the Bengali for an explosion.

You'll want to know: পৌঁছানো.
পৌঁছানো — poũchhāno — "to arrive; to reach".
আমরা কত তাড়াতাড়ি পৌঁছাব, সেটা তোমার ওপর নির্ভর করে। āmrā kôtô tāṛātāṛi poũchhābô, seṭā tomār opôr nirbhôr kôre. How fast we get there is up to you.
Past (he/she …): সেই দমকল পৌঁছাল, sei dômkôl poũchhālô.

The word, taken apart.
পৌঁছানো (poũchhāno) is filed in CDIAL 8716 under Sanskrit prabhūta, 'much, great', which lists Bengali paũchā, 'to arrive'; how the sense moved is not set out there. Past: পৌঁছাল, arrived.

Lesson 2 of 5.
উদ্ধার করা (uddhār kôrā) — to rescue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an explosion, then the Bengali for to arrive.

You'll want to know: উদ্ধার করা.
উদ্ধার করা — uddhār kôrā — "to rescue".
দমকলের লোকেরা আগুন থেকে দুই শিশুকে উদ্ধার করল। dômkôler lokerā āgun theke dui shishuke uddhār kôrlô. The firefighters rescued two children from the fire.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শিশুটিকে উদ্ধার করলেন, tini shishuṭike uddhār kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
উদ্ধার করা (uddhār kôrā) puts Sanskrit uddhāra, 'raising, lifting up' (Monier-Williams), before করা: to rescue is to lift someone out of danger.

Lesson 3 of 5.
মই (môi) — a ladder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to arrive, then the Bengali for to rescue.

You'll want to know: মই.
মই — môi — "a ladder".
তারা জানালায় একটা মই লাগাল। tārā jānālāy ekṭā môi lāgālô. They put a ladder up against the window.

The word, taken apart.
মই (môi) comes down from Sanskrit matya, a kind of harrow (CDIAL 9755); CDIAL lists Bengali moi as both a harrow and a ladder.

Lesson 4 of 5.
সাইরেন (sāiren) — a siren.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to rescue, then the Bengali for a ladder.

You'll want to know: সাইরেন.
সাইরেন — sāiren — "a siren".
সাইরেন শুনে সবাই রাস্তার পাশে দাঁড়াল। sāiren shune sôbāi rāstār pāshe dā̃ṛālô. When they heard the siren, everyone stood at the side of the road.

The word, taken apart.
সাইরেন (sāiren) is borrowed from English siren, the warning wail of a fire engine or an ambulance; English took the name from the Sirens of Greek legend.

Lesson 5 of 5.
আশ্রয় (āshrôy) — shelter; refuge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a ladder, then the Bengali for a siren.

You'll want to know: আশ্রয়.
আশ্রয় — āshrôy — "shelter; refuge".
আগুনের পরে অনেক পরিবার স্কুলে আশ্রয় নিল। āguner pôre ônek pôribār skule āshrôy nilô. After the fire, many families took shelter in the school.

The word, taken apart.
আশ্রয় (āshrôy) is Sanskrit āśraya, that on which something leans or rests (Monier-Williams). আশ্রয় নেওয়া, to take shelter.
