6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
el experimento — the experiment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to make an effort, then the Spanish for to contribute.

You'll want to know: el experimento.
el experimento — "the experiment".
Hicimos un experimento en clase. We did an experiment in class.
Hacer un experimento — to do an experiment.

The word, taken apart.
experimento is a learned borrowing from Latin experimentum 'a test, a trial', built on experiri 'to try out' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
