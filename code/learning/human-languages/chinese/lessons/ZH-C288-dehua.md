## You'll want to know: 的话
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C288-DEHUA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**的话** — *dehuà* — "if", closing a condition from behind.

> **下雨的话 我们就不去了** *xiàyǔ dehuà, wǒmen jiù bú qù le* — If it rains, we won't go.

> **要是你有时间的话 就来我家** *yàoshi nǐ yǒu shíjiān dehuà, jiù lái wǒ jiā* — If you've got time, come round.

Both characters are ones you write.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**的话** (*dehuà*) is built from 的 and 话 'words'. 话 is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'speech'; the particle 的 is the one whose history is not settled. 'If' for the pair is the modern word's own sense, and its first syllable is light.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C288-YAOSHI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YAOSHI-JIU-01, ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-01] -->

要是 opens the condition from the front; 的话 closes it from behind. Use either, or both: 要是你有时间的话.
