## You'll want to know: servitūs, servitūtis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C389-SERVITUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**servitūs, servitūtis** — "slavery, servitude".

> Lībertās servitūte melior est.
>
> *Freedom is better than slavery.*

*servitūs, servitūtis* f., built on *servus*, a slave. English *servitude*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*servitūs* is built on *servus* "a slave" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tūs* that makes a state.
