## You'll want to know: orientale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C443-CARDINALI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**orientale** — "eastern".

> La costa orientale ha il mare più calmo.
>
> *The eastern coast has the calmer sea.*

*L'arte orientale* — Eastern art.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orientale* is a learned borrowing from Latin *orientālis* 'of the east' (EtymDB), from *oriēns* 'rising (sun)'.
