## You'll want to know: சபி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C344-MAYAM344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**சபி** — *capi* — "to curse".

> கோபமான முனிவர் மன்னரைச் சபித்தார்.
>
> *kōpamāṉa muṉivar maṉṉaraic capittār.*
>
> The angry sage cursed the king.

Past (I …): சபித்தேன், *capittēṉ*.

The noun is சாபம், a curse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சபி** (*capi*) is a Tamil verb built on Sanskrit *śapati*, "curses, swears" (CDIAL 12289).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
