Spanish, chapter 571: Un gran descubrimiento.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el inventor — the inventor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the scandal, then the Spanish for the controversy.

You'll want to know: el inventor.
el inventor — "the inventor".
El inventor del teléfono fue muy famoso. The inventor of the telephone was very famous.
A woman is la inventora.

The word, taken apart.
inventor is a learned borrowing from Latin inventor 'one who finds out, a deviser' (EtymDB), from invenīre 'to come upon' (Lewis & Short).

Fue un descubrimiento muy importante para la medicina. It was a very important discovery for medicine.

The word, taken apart.
descubrimiento is built on descubrir with the ending -miento. Descubrir comes from cubrir, inherited from Latin cooperīre 'to cover over' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: el desarrollo.
el desarrollo — "the development".
El desarrollo de la ciudad fue muy rápido. The city's development was very fast.

The word, taken apart.
desarrollo is built on desarrollar 'to unroll, to develop', from des- and arrollar 'to roll up', built on rollo 'a roll', which is usually traced to Latin rotulus 'a little wheel'.
