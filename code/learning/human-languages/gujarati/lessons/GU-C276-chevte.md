# છેવટે (chevṭe) — finally; in the end

## You'll want to know: છેવટે
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C276-CHEVTE-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-02, GU-LEX-C173-WEEK173-05, GU-LEX-GAAM-01, GU-LEX-C206-ARRIVE206-01] -->

**છેવટે** — *chevṭe* — "finally; in the end".

> **છેવટે ટ્રેન આવી.**
>
> *chevṭe ṭren āvī.* — "In the end the train came."

**છેવટે** comes after waiting, or after a long run of things: it closes them off.

> **અમે આખો દિવસ ચાલ્યા, અને છેવટે ગામ પહોંચ્યા.**
>
> *ame ākho divas cālyā, ane chevṭe gām pahõcyā.* — "We walked all day, and finally reached the village."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છેવટે** (*chevṭe*) is built from **છેવટ**, "the end", and the **-ે** of **સવારે** and **ઘરે**: "at the end". CDIAL lists Gujarati *chevaṭ*, "end", under Sanskrit *cheda*, "a cut, a break" (CDIAL 5064), the entry that also holds the *che-* of **છેલ્લું**, "last".
