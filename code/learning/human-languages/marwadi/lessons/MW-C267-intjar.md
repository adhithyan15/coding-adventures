## You'll want to know: इंतजार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C267-RAJA267-04]; assesses=[] -->

**इंतजार** — *intjār* — "waiting".

> हूं थारो इंतजार करूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ thāro intjār karū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I'm waiting for you.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इंतजार** (*intjār*) is usually traced, through Persian *intizār*, to Arabic *intiẓār*, "waiting". The one waited for comes first: **थारो इंतजार**, waiting for you.
