## You'll want to know: ਪਹੁੰਚਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C283-ROAD283-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਹੁੰਚਣਾ** — *pahunchṇā* — "to arrive; to reach".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਪਿੰਡ ਪਹੁੰਚਾਂਗੇ।
>
> *asīṁ shām nū̃ piṇḍ pahunchāṅge.*
>
> We will reach the village in the evening.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਪਹੁੰਚਿਆ, *maiṁ ghar pahunchiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਹੁੰਚਣਾ** (*pahunchṇā*) goes back to Sanskrit *prabhūta*, "much, great"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pahũcṇā*, "to arrive", under it (CDIAL 8716).
