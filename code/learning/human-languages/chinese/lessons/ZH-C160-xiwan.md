## You'll want to know: xǐwǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C160-CHORE160-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐwǎn* — "to do the washing-up; to wash the dishes".

> *chī fàn yǐhòu wǒ xǐwǎn* — After we eat, I'll do the dishes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones: xǐwǎn is said xíwǎn, the first rising.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐwǎn* is built from 洗 'to wash' and *wǎn* 'bowl', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sejX* and *'wanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which writes the second another way and glosses it 'a vessel'.
