## You'll want to know: el tranvía
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C650-LINEA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el tranvía** — "the tram, the streetcar".

> El tranvía pasa cada diez minutos.
>
> *The tram comes every ten minutes.*

It ends in -a but is masculine: *el tranvía*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tranvía* was borrowed from English *tramway*, with the second half remade as Spanish *vía* "track, way", from Latin *via*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
