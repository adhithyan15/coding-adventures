## You'll want to know: क्रम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C551-YADI551-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रम** — *kram* — "a sequence; an order".

> शिक्षकांनी मुलांची नावं वर्णमालेच्या क्रमाने वाचली.
>
> *śikṣakānnī mulāñcī nāvaṁ varṇamālecyā kramāne vāclī.*
>
> The teacher read out the children's names in alphabetical order.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रम** (*kram*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *krama*, "a step, a sequence" (Monier-Williams).
