## You'll want to know: посадочный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C419-REGISTRATSIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**посадочный** (*posádochnyy*) — "boarding (adjective)".

> Без посадочного талона в самолёт не пустят.
>
> *Bez posádochnogo talóna v samolyót ne pústyat.*
>
> They will not let you onto the plane without a boarding pass.

*Посадочный талон* — a boarding pass. *Посадка* is boarding, and also landing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*посадочный* is built on *посадка* 'boarding; landing', from *по-* and *садить* 'to seat'. EtymDB lists *садить* without a deeper source.
