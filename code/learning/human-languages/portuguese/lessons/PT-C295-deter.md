## You'll want to know: deter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C295-SUSPEITO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**deter** — "to arrest, to detain; to stop".

> A polícia deteve dois homens.
>
> *The police arrested two men.*

Conjugated like *ter*: *detenho, deteve*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*deter* is inherited from Latin *dētinēre* "to hold back, to detain" (EtymDB), built on *tenēre* "to hold".
