## You'll want to know: esculpir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C522-GALERIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**esculpir** — "to sculpt, to carve".

> O artista esculpiu a figura em pedra.
>
> *The artist carved the figure in stone.*

Sculpture is *a escultura*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esculpir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sculpere* "to carve, to chisel" (Lewis & Short).
