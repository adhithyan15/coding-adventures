## You'll want to know: টাক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C470-TAK470-01]; assesses=[] -->

**টাক** — *ṭāk* — "a bald patch; baldness".

> চল্লিশ বছরে বাবার মাথায় টাক পড়ল।
>
> *chôllish bôchhôre bābār māthāy ṭāk pôṛlô.*
>
> At forty, my father started going bald.

টাক পড়া, to go bald.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টাক** (*ṭāk*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a bald patch, under a reconstructed *ṭakka*, "bald" (CDIAL 5422); it is not the টাকা (taught) of money, which is another entry.
