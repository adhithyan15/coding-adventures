Sanskrit, chapter 411: Streets and Blockages.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रथ्या (rathyā) — a carriage road; a street.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for split open, then the Sanskrit for scrapes.

You'll want to know: रथ्या.
रथ्या — rathyā — "a carriage road; a street".
रथ्यायां वाहनानि चलन्ति। rathyāyāṃ vāhanāni calanti. Vehicles move along the carriage road.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
रथ्या (rathyā) is built on ratha, "a chariot", with the ending -ya; MW gives rathyā as a carriage road, a highway, a street (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for scrapes, then the Sanskrit for a carriage road.

You'll want to know: अपयाति.
अपयाति — apayāti — "moves off; goes away".
गजः मार्गात् अपयाति। gajaḥ mārgāt apayāti. The elephant moves off the road.
Past (it …): गजः मार्गात् अपयातवान्, gajaḥ mārgāt apayātavān.

The word, taken apart.
अपयाति (apayāti) is built from apa, "away", and the root √yā, "to go"; MW gives apa-yā as to go away or depart, with the participle apayāta (MW).

You'll want to know: संकेतः.
संकेतः — saṅketaḥ — "a signal; a sign".
आरक्षकः हस्तेन संकेतं ददाति। ārakṣakaḥ hastena saṅketaṃ dadāti. The policeman gives a signal with his hand.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
संकेतः (saṅketaḥ) is built from sam, "together", and the root √cit, "to perceive"; MW gives saṃketa as an agreement or an appointment, and a sign or signal agreed on is that sense (MW).

मार्गे महान् रोधः अस्ति। mārge mahān rodhaḥ asti. There is a big blockage on the road.

Lesson 5 of 5.
वर्जयति (varjayati) — avoids; keeps away from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a signal, then the Sanskrit for a blockage.

You'll want to know: वर्जयति.
वर्जयति — varjayati — "avoids; keeps away from".
चालकः तं मार्गं वर्जयति। cālakaḥ taṃ mārgaṃ varjayati. The driver keeps away from that road.
Past (he …): सः तं मार्गं वर्जितवान्, saḥ taṃ mārgaṃ varjitavān.

The word, taken apart.
वर्जयति (varjayati) is built on the root √vṛj, "to twist, to turn aside", in its causative; MW gives its participle varjita as excluded, abandoned, avoided (MW).
