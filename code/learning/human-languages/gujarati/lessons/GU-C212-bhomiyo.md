## You'll want to know: ભોમિયો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C212-REST212-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ભોમિયો** — *bhomiyo* — "guide, one who knows the way".

> **ભોમિયાએ અમને રસ્તો બતાવ્યો.** — *bhomiyāe amne rasto batāvyo* — "The guide showed us the way."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભોમિયો** (*bhomiyo*) is built on *bhom*, "land", with the agent ending *-iyo*: the one who knows the land. CDIAL lists Gujarati *bhom*, "a piece of land, field", under Sanskrit *bhaumika*, "being in the earth", a derivative of *bhū́mi*, "earth" (CDIAL 9642).
