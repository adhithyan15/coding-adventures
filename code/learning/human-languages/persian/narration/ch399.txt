Persian, chapter 399: Entrance exams.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
کنکور (konkur) — the university entrance exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a priority, then the Persian for to weigh up.

You'll want to know: کنکور.
کنکور (konkur) — "the university entrance exam".
برادرم برای کنکور درس میخواند. barâdaram barâye konkur dars mikhânad. My brother is studying for the entrance exam.
The national exam for university places in Iran.

The word, taken apart.
کنکور (konkur) was borrowed from French concours "competition" (EtymDB), from Latin concursus "a running together", built on currere "to run" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: آزمون.
آزمون (âzmun) — "a test, an examination".
آزمون زبان را دیروز دادم. âzmun-e zabân râ diruz dâdam. I took the language test yesterday.
Formal; in speech emtehân. Âzmun dâdan — to sit a test.

The word, taken apart.
آزمون (âzmun) is native Persian, built on âzmudan "to test, to try": a test is a trying.

قبولی (qabuli) — "a pass (in an exam)".
همه برای قبولی او خوشحال شدند. hame barâye qabuli-ye u khoshhâl shodand. Everyone was happy that she passed.
From qabul "accepted" + -i. Qabul shodan — to pass.

The word, taken apart.
قبولی (qabuli) is built on qabul "accepted", usually traced to Arabic qabūl "acceptance", with the Persian noun suffix -i.

Lesson 4 of 6.
مردود شدن (mardud shodan) — to fail (an exam).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a test, then the Persian for a pass.

You'll want to know: مردود شدن.
مردود شدن (mardud shodan) — "to fail (an exam)".
او در امتحان ریاضی مردود شد. u dar emtehân-e riyâzi mardud shod. He failed the maths exam.
Formal; in speech rad shodan or oftâdan. Stem shav-.

The word, taken apart.
مردود is usually traced to Arabic mardūd "rejected, sent back", from the same Arabic family as radd, the source of Persian rad; shodan "to become" is inherited.

Lesson 5 of 6.
معدل (mo'addel) — a grade average.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pass, then the Persian for to fail.

You'll want to know: معدل.
معدل (mo'addel) — "a grade average".
معدل او امسال بیست بود. mo'addel-e u emsâl bist bud. Her grade average this year was twenty.
Iranian marks go up to twenty.

The word, taken apart.
معدل (mo'addel) is usually traced to Arabic mu'addal "evened out, balanced", from the same Arabic family as 'adl "justice": an average evens out the marks.
