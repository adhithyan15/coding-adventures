## You'll want to know: autorizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C624-PERMISO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**autorizar** — "to authorise, to allow".

> El director autorizó la salida al museo.
>
> *The head teacher authorised the trip to the museum.*

Spelling: *autoricé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*autorizar* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *auctorizare*, built on *auctor* 'originator, guarantor', from *augere* 'to increase' (EtymDB).
