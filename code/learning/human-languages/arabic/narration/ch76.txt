Arabic, chapter 76: Forest, River, Stone, Sand, Field.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غابة (ghāba) — a forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a flower, then the Arabic for a mountain.
