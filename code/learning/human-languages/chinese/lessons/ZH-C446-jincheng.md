## You'll want to know: 进城
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C446-OLDTOWN446-05]; assesses=[] -->

**进城** — *jìnchéng* — "to go to town; to move to the city".

> **村子里很多人进城打工** *cūnzi lǐ hěn duō rén jìnchéng dǎgōng* — A lot of people from the village move to the city for work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**进城** (*jìnchéng*) is built from 进 'to enter' and 城 'a walled town', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsinH* and *dzyeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go forward'.
