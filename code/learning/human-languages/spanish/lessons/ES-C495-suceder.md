## You'll want to know: suceder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C495-NOTICIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**suceder** — "to happen, to take place".

> El accidente sucedió en la autopista.
>
> *The accident happened on the motorway.*

News reports use it alongside *ocurrir*.
