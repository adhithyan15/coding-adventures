Gujarati, chapter 268: Quarrels and advice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
લડવું (laḍvũ) — to quarrel, to fight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get irritated, then the Gujarati for to sulk.

You'll want to know: લડવું.
લડવું — laḍvũ — "to quarrel, to fight".
અમે ક્યારેય નથી લડતા. — ame kyārey nathī laḍtā — "We never quarrel."

The word, taken apart.
લડવું (laḍvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from laḍ, "fight", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to fight" (CDIAL 10920).

Lesson 2 of 5.

મારી માતા મને નથી વઢતી. — mārī mātā mane nathī vaḍhtī — "My mother does not scold me."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of વઢવું (vaḍhvũ) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati verb of this form with the meaning "to scold", and our other sources do not trace it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
મનાવવું (manāvvũ) — to persuade, to coax.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to scold, then the Gujarati for to deceive.

You'll want to know: મનાવવું.
મનાવવું — manāvvũ — "to persuade, to coax".
શું તમે તેને મનાવશો? — śũ tame tene manāvśo? — "Will you persuade him?"
The causative of માનવું, mānvũ, "to believe": to bring someone round.

The word, taken apart.
મનાવવું (manāvvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to persuade", beside mānvũ under Sanskrit mányatē, "thinks, honours; agrees" (CDIAL 9857).

Lesson 5 of 5.
સલાહ (salāh) — advice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to deceive, then the Gujarati for to persuade.

You'll want to know: સલાહ.
સલાહ — salāh — "advice".
તે કોઈની સલાહ નથી માનતો. — te koīnī salāh nathī mānto — "He does not take anyone's advice."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સલાહ (salāh) is a loan usually traced to Arabic ṣalāḥ, "soundness, goodness", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṣ-l-ḥ, "to be good, to be put right" (Lane).
