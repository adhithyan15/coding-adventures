## Script — 八 over a turn and a dot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-GONG-PUBLIC-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 公

**Two pieces, top before bottom.**

**The top piece, 八, two strokes:** a stroke falling to the left, then a longer one falling to the right, spread wide apart.

**The bottom piece, two strokes.** A stroke that falls to the left and turns to run right along the foot, then a dot at its right end. The same pair sits in the top left corner of 能.

2 + 2 = four.
