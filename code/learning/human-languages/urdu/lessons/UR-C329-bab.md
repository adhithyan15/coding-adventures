## You'll want to know: باب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C329-AFSANA329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**باب** — *bāb* — "a chapter of a book".

> پہلا باب سب سے اچھا ہے۔
>
> *pehlā bāb sab se achchhā hai.*
>
> The first chapter is the best.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**باب** (*bāb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bāb*, "a door", and so a way into one part of a book.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
