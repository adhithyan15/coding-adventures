## You'll want to know: la fotocopia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C657-PAPELES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la fotocopia** — "the photocopy".

> Trae una fotocopia del pasaporte.
>
> *Bring a photocopy of your passport.*

The verb is *fotocopiar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fotocopia* is built from Greek *phōs, phōtós* "light" and *copia* "copy", which comes from Latin *cōpia* "plenty".
