## You'll want to know: fēn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C48-CLOCK48-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fēn* — "minute; to divide".

> *bā diǎn shí fēn* — Ten past eight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *pjun* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to divide'. Its use for minutes is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
