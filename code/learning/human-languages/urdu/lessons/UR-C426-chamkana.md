## You'll want to know: چمکانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C426-KHALIS426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**چمکانا** — *chamkānā* — "to polish; to make (something) shine".

> اس نے کپڑے سے انگوٹھی چمکائی۔
>
> *us ne kapṛe se angūṭhī chamkāī.*
>
> She polished the ring with a cloth.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے جوتے چمکائے, *us ne jūte chamkāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چمکانا** (*chamkānā*) is built on *chamaknā*, "to shine", which CDIAL lists under *camakka*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4676): this is the form for making something shine.
