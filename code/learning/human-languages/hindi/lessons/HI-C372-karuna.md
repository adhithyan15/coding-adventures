## You'll want to know: करुणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C372-DAYA372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**करुणा** — *karuṇā* — "compassion".

> उसकी आँखों में करुणा थी।
>
> *uskī āṅkhoṁ meṁ karuṇā thī.*
>
> There was compassion in his eyes.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**करुणा** (*karuṇā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *karuṇā*, "pity, compassion" (Monier-Williams).
