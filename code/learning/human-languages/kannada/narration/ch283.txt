Kannada, chapter 283: Pulse, Cramp, Fracture, Operation, Pregnant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಾಡಿ (nāḍi) — the pulse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a nurse, then the Kannada for surprise.

You'll want to know: ನಾಡಿ.
ನಾಡಿ — nāḍi — "the pulse".

The word, taken apart.
ನಾಡಿ (nāḍi) is Sanskrit nāḍī, "tubular organ, tube", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the final -ī shortened (CDIAL 7047). The pulse is felt in a vessel.
