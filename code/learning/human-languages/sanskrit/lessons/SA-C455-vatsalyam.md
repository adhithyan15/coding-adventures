## You'll want to know: वात्सल्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C455-ANURAGA455-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वात्सल्यम्** — *vātsalyam* — "tenderness for a child".

> मातुः वात्सल्यं शिशुं रक्षति।
>
> *mātuḥ vātsalyaṃ śiśuṃ rakṣati.*
>
> A mother's tenderness protects her baby.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वात्सल्यम्** (*vātsalyam*) is built on *vatsala*, "fond, tender", from *vatsa*, "a calf" (**वत्सः**); MW gives "affection or tenderness, esp. towards offspring" (MW).
