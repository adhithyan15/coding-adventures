## You'll want to know: a canoa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C418-BARCO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a canoa** — "the canoe".

> Descemos o rio de canoa.
>
> *We went down the river by canoe.*

A light, narrow boat that you paddle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*canoa* was borrowed through Spanish from Taíno *kanoa* 'dugout canoe' (EtymDB), a word of the Caribbean islands.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
