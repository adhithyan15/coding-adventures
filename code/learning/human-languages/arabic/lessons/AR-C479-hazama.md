## You'll want to know: حزم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C479-TAZALLUJ479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حزم** — *ḥazama* — "to pack (luggage)".

> حزمت أمتعتي قبل الرحلة بيوم.
>
> *ḥazamtu amtiʿatī qabla ar-riḥla bi-yawm.*
>
> I packed my luggage a day before the trip.

Present: يحزم, *yaḥzim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حزم** (*ḥazama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-z-m*, "to bind, to tie up a bundle" (Lane). It shares its root with *ḥizām*, "a belt".
