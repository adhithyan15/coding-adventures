# উদাসীন (udāsīn) — indifferent; careless (of something)

## You'll want to know: উদাসীন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C464-GOMBHIR464-04]; assesses=[] -->

**উদাসীন** — *udāsīn* — "indifferent; careless (of something)".

> স্বাস্থ্য নিয়ে অরুণ উদাসীন।
>
> *svāsthyô niye ôrun udāsīn.*
>
> Arun does not care about his health.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উদাসীন** (*udāsīn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *udāsīna*, "sitting apart, indifferent" (Monier-Williams).
