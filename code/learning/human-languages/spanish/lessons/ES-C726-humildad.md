## You'll want to know: la humildad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C726-HUMILDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la humildad** — "humility".

> Habla de sus éxitos con humildad.
>
> *She speaks about her successes with humility.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*humildad* is usually traced to Latin *humilitās* "lowness", built on *humilis*, from *humus* "earth".
