# jìniàn — to commemorate; to honour the memory of

## You'll want to know: jìniàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C324-RECALL324-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jìniàn* — "to commemorate; to honour the memory of".

> *měinián zhè yì tiān wǒmen dōu jìniàn tā* — Every year on this day we remember him.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìniàn* is built from *jì* and *niàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kiX* and *nemH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, 'to note' and 'a span of twelve years', and the second 'to think of'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
