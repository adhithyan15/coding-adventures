## You'll want to know: chatouiller
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C474-GAIETE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**chatouiller** — "to tickle".

> Le grand-père chatouille le bébé, qui rit.
>
> *The grandfather tickles the baby, who laughs.*

*Être chatouilleux* — to be ticklish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *chatouiller* is not settled. It is probably an expressive word; Germanic words for tickling, like Old English *citelian*, look similar (EtymDB).
