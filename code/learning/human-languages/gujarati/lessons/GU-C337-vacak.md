## You'll want to know: વાચક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C337-PANU337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વાચક** — *vācak* — "a reader".

> આ પુસ્તક વાચકોને ખૂબ ગમ્યું.
>
> *ā pustak vāckone khūb gamyũ.*
>
> Readers loved this book.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાચક** (*vācak*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vācaka*, "speaking, telling" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is the one who reads, close to **વાંચવું**, to read.
