## You'll want to know: испуг
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C476-FRIGHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**испуг** (*ispúg*) — "a fright".

> От испуга кошка прыгнула на шкаф.
>
> *Ot ispúga kóshka prýgnula na shkaf.*
>
> The cat jumped onto the wardrobe in fright.

Masculine. The adjective is *испуганный* — frightened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*испуг* is built on *ис-* and the root of *пугать* 'to frighten'; EtymDB lists *пугать* without a source, so its further origin is not settled.
