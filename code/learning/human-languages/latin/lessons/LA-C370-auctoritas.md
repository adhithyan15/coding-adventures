## You'll want to know: auctōritās, auctōritātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C370-AUCTORITAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**auctōritās, auctōritātis** — "authority, influence; backing".

> Senex magnam auctōritātem in oppidō habet.
>
> *The old man has great influence in the town.*

*auctōritās, auctōritātis* f. A man's weight in public life, earned by age and deeds. English *authority*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*auctōritās* is built on *auctor* "a backer, an originator" (EtymDB), which comes from *augeō* "to increase" (Lewis & Short). English *authority* comes from it through French.
