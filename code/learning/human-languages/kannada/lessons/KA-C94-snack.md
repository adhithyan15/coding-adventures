# ತಿಂಡಿ (tiṇḍi) — a snack, a light meal

## You'll want to know: ತಿಂಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C94-TABLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಿಂಡಿ** (*tiṇḍi*) — "a snack, a light meal", also the word for breakfast. It
comes from **ತಿನ್ನು** (*tinnu*), "to eat".

**ತಿಂಡಿ ಆಯ್ತಾ?** (*tiṇḍi āytā?*) — "have you had breakfast?"

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Something to eat, from the verb to eat.
