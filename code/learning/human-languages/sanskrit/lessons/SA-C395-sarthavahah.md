## You'll want to know: सार्थवाहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C395-SARTHA395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सार्थवाहः** — *sārthavāhaḥ* — "a caravan leader".

> सार्थवाहः मार्गं सम्यक् जानाति।
>
> *sārthavāhaḥ mārgaṃ samyak jānāti.*
>
> The caravan leader knows the road well.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सार्थवाहः** (*sārthavāhaḥ*) is built from *sārtha*, "a caravan", and *vāha*, "bearing, leading", from the root √vah, "to carry"; MW gives the compound as the leader of a caravan, a merchant (MW).
