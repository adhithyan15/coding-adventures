## You'll want to know: संकेत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C375-ASHA375-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संकेत** — *saṅket* — "a sign, an indication".

> अगर बच्चा खाना नहीं खाता तो यह बीमारी का संकेत हो सकता है।
>
> *agar baccā khānā nahīṃ khātā to yah bīmārī kā saṅket ho saktā hai.*
>
> If a child won't eat, it can be a sign of illness.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकेत** (*saṅket*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃketa*, "an agreement; a sign" (Monier-Williams).
