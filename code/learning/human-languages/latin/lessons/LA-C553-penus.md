## You'll want to know: penus, penūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C553-LIMN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**penus, penūs** — "provisions, the store of food".

> Vīlica penum in cellā servat.
>
> *The housekeeper keeps the provisions in the storeroom.*

Masculine or feminine, fourth declension; also *penum*, neuter. The stored food of a household.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**penus** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European word for "food" (EtymDB).
