## Grammar Lens: 要是 … 就 …, if and then
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-YAOSHI-JIU-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C288-JIU-01, ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-03, ZH-LEX-C50-SLEEP50-05, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-CHANGED-STATE-01, ZH-LEX-C45-TODAY45-05, ZH-TONE-SANDHI-BU] -->

> **要是 opens what might happen; 就 opens what follows if it does.**

> **要是明天下雨 我就不去了** *yàoshi míngtiān xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù bú qù le* — If it rains tomorrow, I won't go.
> **要是你病了 就回家** *yàoshi nǐ bìng le, jiù huí jiā* — If you're ill, go home.

就 goes after the subject of the second clause, right before its verb: 我就不去了. With no subject there, 就 opens the clause: 就回家.

了 at the end of 我就不去了 says the plan has changed.
