Urdu, chapter 248: False, Alive, Dead, Healthy, Present.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جھوٹا (jhūṭā) — false, lying.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for useful, then the Urdu for dangerous.

You'll want to know: جھوٹا.
جھوٹا — jhūṭā — "false, lying".

The word, taken apart.
جھوٹا (jhūṭā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jhūṭṭha, "false", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5407).

تندرست (tandurust) is a loan, usually traced to Persian tandurust, "healthy, sound of body". It is built from tan, "body", and durust, "sound".

Lesson 5 of 5.
موجود (maujūd) — present, available.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for dead, then the Urdu for healthy.

You'll want to know: موجود.
موجود — maujūd — "present, available".

The word, taken apart.
موجود (maujūd) is a loan from Arabic mawjūd, "found, existing", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is w-j-d (Lane).
