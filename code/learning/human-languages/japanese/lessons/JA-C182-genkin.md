## You'll want to know: げんきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C182-PAY182-03]; assesses=[] -->

**げんきん** — *genkin* — "cash".

> **げんきんで はらいます。** — *genkin de haraimasu* — "I pay in cash."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**げんきん** (*genkin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gen*, "present, actual" (Middle Chinese *henH*) and *kin*, "gold, money" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
