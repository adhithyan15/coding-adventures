## You'll want to know: مهاجر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C387-MOHAJERAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مهاجر** (*mohâjer*) — "a migrant, an immigrant".

> خیلی از مهاجرها در این محله زندگی میکنند.
>
> *kheyli az mohâjerhâ dar in mahalle zendegi mikonand.*
>
> Many migrants live in this neighbourhood.

Plural *mohâjerân* or *mohâjerhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مهاجر** comes from Arabic *muhājir* "emigrant" (EtymDB): one who has left home.
