## You'll want to know: らいきゃく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C347-HOUMON347-05]; assesses=[] -->

**らいきゃく** — *raikyaku* — "a visitor; a caller".

> **らいきゃくが あったら、でんわして ください。** — *raikyaku ga attara, denwa shite kudasai* — "If a visitor comes, please phone me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**らいきゃく** (*raikyaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *rai*, "come" (Middle Chinese *loj*) and *kyaku*, "visitor" (*khaek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
