# Does the pair include the speaker?

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PRON-C261-AVAM-01, SA-PRON-C261-YUVAM-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Give “us two”, then “you two”. (*Āvām; yuvām.*)

## Grammar Lens: choose the pair

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C261-DUAL-OBJECTS-03]; assesses=[] -->

Both forms point to exactly two people. Choose by whether the speaker belongs
to the pair:

- speaker plus one other: **आवाम्** (*āvām*), “us two”;
- two familiar addressees: **युवाम्** (*yuvām*), “you two”.

That is the dual contrast. Count two first; then ask whether the speaker is
inside the pair.

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-PASHYATI-SEE, SA-PRON-C260-MAM-01, SA-PRON-C260-TVAM-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C260-SINGULAR-OBJECTS-03, SA-PRON-C261-AVAM-01, SA-PRON-C261-YUVAM-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C261-DUAL-OBJECTS-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: “he sees us two” — *saḥ āvām paśyati*]
- [YOU SAY: “he sees you two” — *saḥ yuvām paśyati*]
- [YOU CONTRAST: “me” then “us two” — *mām; āvām*]
- [YOU CONTRAST: “you one” then “you two” — *tvām; yuvām*]
- [YOU SAY: pair includes the speaker — *āvām*; pair is addressed — *yuvām*]
- [YOU CONTRAST: *āvām; yuvām; āvām; yuvām*]

## Wrap-up Recall

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PRON-C261-AVAM-01, SA-PRON-C261-YUVAM-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C261-DUAL-OBJECTS-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say both dual sentences, then say the deciding question. (*Saḥ āvām
paśyati; saḥ yuvām paśyati; does the pair include the speaker?*)
