# اجتهد (ijtahada) — to work hard, to strive

## You'll want to know: اجتهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C524-TAAWUN524-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اجتهد** — *ijtahada* — "to work hard, to strive".

> اجتهد الطالب ونجح في الامتحان.
>
> *ijtahada aṭ-ṭālib wa-najaḥa fī al-imtiḥān.*
>
> The student worked hard and passed the exam.

Present: يجتهد, *yajtahid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجتهد** (*ijtahada*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-h-d*, "to strive, to exert oneself" (Lane). Mujtahid, hard-working, which you know, is its participle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
