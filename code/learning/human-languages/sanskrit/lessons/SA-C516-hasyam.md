## You'll want to know: हास्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C516-AVAKASA516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हास्यम्** — *hāsyam* — "fun; laughter".

> बालस्य हास्यं मह्यं रोचते।
>
> *bālasya hāsyaṃ mahyaṃ rocate.*
>
> I love the child's laughter.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हास्यम्** (*hāsyam*) comes from the root √has, "to laugh", which also gives *hasati*; MW gives *hāsya* as laughter, mirth, and jest, fun, amusement (MW).
