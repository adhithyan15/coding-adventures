German, chapter 352: Ohne Arbeit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
entlassen — to lay off, to dismiss; to discharge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the hour of overtime, then the German for the end of the working day.

You'll want to know: entlassen.
entlassen — "to lay off, to dismiss; to discharge".
Die Fabrik hat fünfzig Mitarbeiter entlassen. The factory laid off fifty employees.
Inseparable and strong: sie entlässt, sie hat entlassen. From hospital: Er wird morgen entlassen — he is being discharged tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
entlassen is built with ent- "away" on lassen "to let, to let go", inherited from Old High German lāzan and Proto-Germanic lētaną (EtymDB): you let someone go.

Lesson 2 of 5.
arbeitslos — unemployed, out of work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the end of the working day, then the German for to lay off.

You'll want to know: arbeitslos.
arbeitslos — "unemployed, out of work".
Nach der Kündigung war er drei Monate arbeitslos. After he was given notice, he was out of work for three months.
Der Arbeitslose, die Arbeitslose — the unemployed person, declined like an adjective.

The word, taken apart.
arbeitslos joins Arbeit "work", Proto-Germanic arbaidiz (EtymDB), to -los "without", from Old High German lōs "loose, free, lacking" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Arbeitslosigkeit — unemployment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to lay off, then the German for unemployed.

You'll want to know: die Arbeitslosigkeit.
die Arbeitslosigkeit — "unemployment".
In dieser Region ist die Arbeitslosigkeit sehr hoch. Unemployment is very high in this region.
No plural in everyday use. Die Arbeitslosenquote — the unemployment rate.

The word, taken apart.
Arbeitslosigkeit is formed from arbeitslos with -igkeit; the adjective joins Arbeit "work" to -los "without", from Old High German lōs (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Probezeit — the trial period, the probation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for unemployed, then the German for unemployment.

You'll want to know: die Probezeit.
die Probezeit — "the trial period, the probation".
In der Probezeit kann man schneller kündigen. During the trial period you can give notice more quickly.
Plural: die Probezeiten. Usually the first six months of a new job.

The word, taken apart.
Probezeit joins Probe "trial, test", borrowed from Late Latin proba "proof", from probāre "to test, to approve" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), to Zeit "time".

Lesson 5 of 5.
einarbeiten — to train in (a new colleague); to settle in (sich einarbeiten).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for unemployment, then the German for the trial period.

You'll want to know: einarbeiten.
einarbeiten — "to train in (a new colleague); to settle in (sich einarbeiten)".
Eine Kollegin arbeitet mich in den ersten Wochen ein. A colleague is training me in during the first weeks.
Separable: sie arbeitet mich ein, sie hat mich eingearbeitet. Sich einarbeiten — to get to know a new job.

The word, taken apart.
einarbeiten is built from ein "in" and arbeiten "to work", from Arbeit, Old High German arbeit and Proto-Germanic arbaidiz "labour, hardship" (EtymDB): you work someone into a job.
