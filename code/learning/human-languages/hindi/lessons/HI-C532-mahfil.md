## You'll want to know: महफ़िल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C532-GEET532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**महफ़िल** — *mahfil* — "a gathering (for music or poetry)".

> शाम को घर पर संगीत की महफ़िल थी।
>
> *Śām ko ghar par saṅgīt kī mahfil thī.*
>
> In the evening there was a musical gathering at home.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**महफ़िल** (*mahfil*) is usually traced to Arabic *maḥfil*, "a gathering", through Persian.
