## You'll want to know: 社区
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C377-AREA377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**社区** — *shèqū* — "community; neighbourhood".

> **我们社区里有一家书店** *wǒmen shèqū lǐ yǒu yì jiā shūdiàn* — There's a bookshop in our neighbourhood.

The new character, in pieces: 区 — four strokes: a bar across the top, two strokes crossing inside, and a frame down the left and along the foot, open on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**社区** (*shèqū*) is built from 社 'the altar of the soil' and 区 'an area', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyaeX* and *khju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary's entry for the second names a marsh and quotes a note on bent shoots; the sense 'an area' is not given there.
