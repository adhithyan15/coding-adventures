## You'll want to know: व्यवस्था
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C536-YOJNA536-03]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवस्था** — *vyavasthā* — "an arrangement, a system".

> शादी में खाने की व्यवस्था बहुत अच्छी थी।
>
> *Śādī meṁ khāne kī vyavasthā bahut acchī thī.*
>
> The food arrangements at the wedding were very good.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवस्था** (*vyavasthā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *vyavasthā*, "settlement, a fixed rule" (Monier-Williams).
