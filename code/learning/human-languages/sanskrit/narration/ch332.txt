Lesson 1 of 6.
आख्यानम् (ākhyānam) — a legend; a tale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for chants softly, then the Sanskrit for holy.

You'll want to know: आख्यानम्.
आख्यानम् — ākhyānam — "a legend; a tale".
पितामही प्राचीनम् आख्यानं कथयति। pitāmahī prācīnam ākhyānaṃ kathayati. Grandmother tells an old legend.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
आख्यानम् (ākhyānam) is built from ā and the root √khyā, "to tell, make known"; MW gives ākhyāna as telling, communication, and a tale, story, legend (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for holy, then the Sanskrit for a legend.

You'll want to know: ऋषिः.
ऋषिः — ṛṣiḥ — "a seer; a sage".
पुरा वने एकः ऋषिः वसति स्म। purā vane ekaḥ ṛṣiḥ vasati sma. Long ago a seer lived in the forest.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ऋषिः (ṛṣiḥ) MW reports several derivations, among them an old root meaning to see, and gives ṛṣi as a singer of sacred hymns, an inspired poet or seer, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 3 of 6.
मुनिः (muniḥ) — a hermit; a silent sage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a legend, then the Sanskrit for a seer.

You'll want to know: मुनिः.
मुनिः — muniḥ — "a hermit; a silent sage".
मुनिः मौनं धारयति। muniḥ maunaṃ dhārayati. The hermit keeps silent.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मुनिः (muniḥ) MW reports a grammarians' derivation from the root √man, "to think", which it does not settle, so its origin is uncertain; MW gives muni as an inspired person, a sage, ascetic or hermit, especially one under a vow of silence (MW).

तपः — tapaḥ — "penance; austerity".
ऋषिः वने कठिनं तपः करोति। ṛṣiḥ vane kaṭhinaṃ tapaḥ karoti. The seer does hard penance in the forest.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
तपः (tapaḥ) comes from the root √tap, "to be hot, burn", as in tapati; MW gives tapas as warmth, heat, and religious austerity, penance (MW).
