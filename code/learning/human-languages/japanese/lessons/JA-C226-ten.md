## You'll want to know: てん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C226-EXAM226-05]; assesses=[] -->

**てん** — *ten* — "a mark; a score; a point".

> **あにの しけんの てんは いいです。** — *ani no shiken no ten wa ii desu* — "My elder brother's exam mark is good."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てん** (*ten*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese: *ten*, "dot, mark" (Middle Chinese *temX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
