## You'll want to know: ಹಸಿವು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C63-HEALTH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಸಿವು** (*hasivu*) — "hunger".

ಹಸಿವು is the naming-word made out of the everyday word for going hungry, and it takes a shape you have had since the knowing-sentence: ನನಗೆ ಹಸಿವು. The ನನಗೆ in front, the thing standing plain, and nothing joining them.

That is the whole design. Hunger happens to you in Kannada rather than being something you have, and the sentence is built to say so.

It begins like ಹಸು and has nothing to do with cows. Two words can share an opening and share no history at all.
