## You'll want to know: आडम्बरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C458-DARPA458-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आडम्बरः** — *āḍambaraḥ* — "bombast".

> तस्य भाषणे केवलम् आडम्बरः अस्ति।
>
> *tasya bhāṣaṇe kevalam āḍambaraḥ asti.*
>
> His speech is mere bombast.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आडम्बरः** (*āḍambaraḥ*) MW's first sense is "a kind of drum", then "a great noise" and "noisy behaviour, bombast"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
