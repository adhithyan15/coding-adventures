Bengali, chapter 313: Kingdoms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
রাজ্য (rājyô) — a kingdom; a state.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for ululation, then the Bengali for the groom's wedding party.

You'll want to know: রাজ্য.
রাজ্য — rājyô — "a kingdom; a state".
অনেক দিন আগে এখানে একটা ছোট রাজ্য ছিল। ônek din āge ekhāne ekṭā chhoṭo rājyô chhilô. Long ago there was a small kingdom here.

The word, taken apart.
রাজ্য (rājyô) is Sanskrit rājya, 'kingship, sovereignty; a kingdom' (Monier-Williams), the abstract of rājan, a king — রাজা. Today it is also a state of India.

Lesson 5 of 5.
শাসন করা (shāsôn kôrā) — to rule; to govern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a throne, then the Bengali for a crown.

You'll want to know: শাসন করা.
শাসন করা — shāsôn kôrā — "to rule; to govern".
সেই রাজা চল্লিশ বছর রাজ্য শাসন করলেন। sei rājā chôllish bôchhôr rājyô shāsôn kôrlen. That king ruled the kingdom for forty years.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দেশ শাসন করলেন, tini desh shāsôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
শাসন করা (shāsôn kôrā) puts Sanskrit śāsana, 'chastising; teaching; an order' (Monier-Williams), before করা. It shares its root with শাসানো, to threaten, and শাস্তি, a punishment.
