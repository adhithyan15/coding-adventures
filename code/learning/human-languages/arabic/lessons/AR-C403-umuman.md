## You'll want to know: عموما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C403-IRTIYAB403-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عموما** — *ʿumūman* — "in general".

> الطقس عموما جميل هنا.
>
> *aṭ-ṭaqs ʿumūman jamīl hunā.*
>
> The weather here is generally pleasant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عموما** (*ʿumūman*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-m-m*, "to be general, to include all" (Lane). *ʿUmūman* is the verbal noun *ʿumūm*, generality, with the adverb ending *-an*: in general.
