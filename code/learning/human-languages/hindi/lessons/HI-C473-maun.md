## You'll want to know: मौन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C473-BATUNI473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मौन** — *maun* — "silence".

> स्कूल में बच्चों ने दो मिनट का मौन रखा।
>
> *skūl meṁ baccoṁ ne do minaṭ kā maun rakhā.*
>
> At school the children kept two minutes' silence.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मौन** (*maun*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *mauna*, "silence, taciturnity", from *muni*, "a silent sage" (Monier-Williams). It is the formal, chosen silence; *cup* is the everyday kind.
