## You'll want to know: kontrollieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C412-BAHNSTEIG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**kontrollieren** — "to check, to inspect; to control".

> Im Zug kontrolliert der Schaffner die Fahrkarten.
>
> *On the train the conductor checks the tickets.*

Past participle without *ge-*: *er hat die Pässe kontrolliert*. *Die Kontrolle* — the check.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kontrollieren* was borrowed from French *contrôler* (EtymDB), built on Old French *contrerole* 'counter-roll', a second list kept to check the first.
