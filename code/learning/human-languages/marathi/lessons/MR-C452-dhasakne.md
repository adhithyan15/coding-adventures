## You'll want to know: धसकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C452-DHAKKA452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धसकणे** — *dhasakṇe* — "to take fright".

> दार अचानक वाजलं आणि आजी धसकली.
>
> *dār acānak vājlaṁ āṇi ājī dhasaklī.*
>
> The door banged suddenly and Grandmother took fright.

Past (he …): तो धसकला, *to dhasaklā*.

धसका, a sudden fright; धसका घेणे, to take something badly to heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धसकणे** (*dhasakṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
