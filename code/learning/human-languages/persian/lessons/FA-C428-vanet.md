## You'll want to know: وانت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C428-KAMIYUN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وانت** (*vânet*) — "a pickup (truck), a small van".

> میوه فروش میوه را با وانت آورد.
>
> *Mive-forush mive râ bâ vânet âvard.*
>
> The fruit seller brought the fruit in a pickup.

Vans like this carry goods, furniture and fruit all over Iranian towns.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vânet* is a loan word whose exact source is not settled: it is usually linked with English *van*, a small goods vehicle. EtymDB does not list it.
