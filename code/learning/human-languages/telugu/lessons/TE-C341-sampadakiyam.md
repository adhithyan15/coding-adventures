## You'll want to know: సంపాదకీయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C341-SAMPADAKUDU341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సంపాదకీయం** — *sampādakīyaṁ* — "an editorial".

> పత్రిక సంపాదకీయంలో ఆ సంఘటన గురించి రాసింది.
>
> *patrika sampādakīyaṁlō ā saṅghaṭana guriñci rāsindi.*
>
> The paper wrote about the incident in its editorial.

The editor's own piece of writing: built on సంపాదకుడు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంపాదకీయం** (*sampādakīyaṁ*) is a modern word built on Sanskrit *sampādaka*, "producing, bringing about" (Monier-Williams), with the Sanskrit ending *-īya*, "belonging to".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
