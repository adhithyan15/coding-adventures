## You'll want to know: wéi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C63-PHONE63-03]; assesses=[] -->

*wéi* — "hello on the phone".

> *wéi nǐ tīng wǒ shuō* — Hello? Listen to me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

On the phone it is said with a rising tone, as a question: wéi?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wéi* is the call you answer the phone with. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
