## You'll want to know: gubernō, gubernāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C385-REGO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**gubernō, gubernāre** — "to steer; to govern".

> Nauta nāvem gubernat.
>
> *The sailor steers the ship.*

*gubernō, gubernāre, gubernāvī, gubernātum*. *Gubernātor*, a helmsman, is already known. English *govern* comes from it through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gubernō* was borrowed from Greek *kybernaō* "to steer a ship" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *govern* comes from it through French.
