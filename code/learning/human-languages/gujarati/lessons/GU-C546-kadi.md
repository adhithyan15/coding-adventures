## You'll want to know: કદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C546-VAGAR546-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કદી** — *kadī* — "ever".

> જો તમે કદી અમારા ગામ આવશો તો તમારે અમારા ઘરે જ રહેવું પડશે.
>
> *jo tame kadī amārā gām āvśo to tamāre amārā ghare ja rahevũ paḍśe.*
>
> If you ever come to our village, you'll have to stay at our house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કદી** (*kadī*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *kadā*, "when?" (Monier-Williams). With a negative it is "never": **કદી નહીં**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
