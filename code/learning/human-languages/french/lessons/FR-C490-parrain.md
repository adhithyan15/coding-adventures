## You'll want to know: le parrain
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C490-PARRAIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le parrain** — "godfather; sponsor".

> Mon parrain m'offre toujours un livre pour mon anniversaire.
>
> *My godfather always gives me a book for my birthday.*

Also someone who sponsors a newcomer: *le parrain d'un projet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le parrain* is inherited, through Old French *parain*, from Vulgar Latin *patrīnus* (EtymDB), built on *pater* "father" (Lewis & Short).
