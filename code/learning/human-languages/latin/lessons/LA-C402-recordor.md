## You'll want to know: recordor, recordārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C402-EDISCO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**recordor, recordārī** — "to call to mind, to remember".

> Diem illum saepe recordor.
>
> *I often recall that day.*

Deponent: *recordor, recordārī, recordātus sum*, built on *cor*, heart: to bring back to the heart. English *record* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recordor* is built from *re-* "back" and *cor, cordis* "heart" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), the seat of memory for the Romans. English *record* comes from it through French.
