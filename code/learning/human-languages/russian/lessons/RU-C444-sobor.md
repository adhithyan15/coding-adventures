## You'll want to know: собор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C444-RATUSHA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**собор** (*sobór*) — "a cathedral; a council".

> Рядом с ратушей — большой собор.
>
> *Ryádom s rátushey — bolshóy sobór.*
>
> Next to the town hall there is a large cathedral.

Masculine. A big main church; also an assembly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*собор* came from Church Slavonic *sŭborŭ* 'a meeting, a council' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *sŭborŭ* 'a gathering', a noun usually linked with *собирать* 'to gather': first the gathering of the faithful, then their great church.
