Russian, chapter 141: To meet, To get acquainted, To invite, To thank, To apologise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
встречать (vstrechát) — to meet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to do the laundry, then the Russian for to iron; to stroke.

You'll want to know: встречать.
встречать (vstrechát) — "to meet".

The word, taken apart.
встречать (vstrechát) is the ongoing partner of встретить, "to meet", Proto-Slavic sŭrěsti. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian surėsti, "to seize, to catch", and finds a link with randù, "I find", less convincing.

Lesson 2 of 5.
знакомиться (znakómitsya) — to get acquainted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to iron; to stroke, then the Russian for to meet.

You'll want to know: знакомиться.
знакомиться (znakómitsya) — "to get acquainted".

The word, taken apart.
знакомиться (znakómitsya) is знакомый, "acquainted", made into a verb with -иться. знакомый is built on знать, "to know", a cousin of English know.

Lesson 3 of 5.
приглашать (priglashát) — to invite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to meet, then the Russian for to get acquainted.

You'll want to know: приглашать.
приглашать (priglashát) — "to invite".

The word, taken apart.
приглашать (priglashát) is при-, "to, toward", with -глашать, from глас, the Church Slavonic shape of голос, "voice": to call someone over. Vasmer relates голос to Old Norse kalla, "to call".

Lesson 4 of 5.
благодарить (blagodarít) — to thank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get acquainted, then the Russian for to invite.

You'll want to know: благодарить.
благодарить (blagodarít) — "to thank".

The word, taken apart.
благодарить (blagodarít) is благо, "good", with дарить, "to give": "to give good" in return. благо is Church Slavonic, says Vasmer, for native болого; благо- often translates Greek eu-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
извиняться (izvinyátsya) — to apologise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to invite, then the Russian for to thank.

You'll want to know: извиняться.
извиняться (izvinyátsya) — "to apologise".

The word, taken apart.
извиняться (izvinyátsya) is из-, "out of", with вина, "guilt", and -ся, "oneself": to get out of the blame. Vasmer relates вина to Lithuanian vaina, "a fault", and Latvian vaĩna, "guilt".
