## You'll want to know: जपमाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C400-PARIKRAMA400-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जपमाला** — *japamālā* — "a string of prayer beads".

> वृद्धः हस्ते जपमालां धारयति।
>
> *vṛddhaḥ haste japamālāṃ dhārayati.*
>
> The old man holds prayer beads in his hand.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जपमाला** (*japamālā*) is built from *japa*, "the quiet repeating of prayers", from the root √jap, "to mutter", and *mālā*, "a garland"; MW gives the compound as a rosary for counting prayers (MW).
