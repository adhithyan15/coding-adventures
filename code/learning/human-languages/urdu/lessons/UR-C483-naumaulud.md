## You'll want to know: نومولود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C483-BABY483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نومولود** — *naumaulūd* — "a newborn".

> نومولود بچہ دن بھر سوتا ہے۔
>
> *naumaulūd bachchā din bhar sotā hai.*
>
> A newborn baby sleeps all day long.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نومولود** (*naumaulūd*) is built from Persian *nau*, "new", and Arabic *maulūd*, "born", the way it is usually traced.
