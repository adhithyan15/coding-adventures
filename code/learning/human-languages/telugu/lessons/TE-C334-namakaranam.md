# నామకరణం (nāmakaraṇaṁ) — a naming ceremony

## You'll want to know: నామకరణం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C334-JANANAM334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నామకరణం** — *nāmakaraṇaṁ* — "a naming ceremony".

> పాపకు ఆదివారం నామకరణం చేశారు.
>
> *pāpaku ādivāraṁ nāmakaraṇaṁ cēśāru.*
>
> They held the baby's naming ceremony on Sunday.

నామం, a name, and కరణం, a making: నామకరణం చేయు, to name a child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నామకరణం** (*nāmakaraṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nāmakaraṇa*, "the ceremony of naming after birth" (CDIAL 7066), from *nāman*, "a name", and *karaṇa*, "a making".
