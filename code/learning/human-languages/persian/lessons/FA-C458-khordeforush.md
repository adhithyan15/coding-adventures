## You'll want to know: خرده فروش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C458-DASTFORUSH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خرده فروش** (*khordeforush*) — "a retailer".

> خرده فروش برنج را کیلویی میفروشد.
>
> *Khordeforush berenj râ kiluyi miforushad.*
>
> The retailer sells rice by the kilo.

*khorde* 'small bits' + *forush* 'seller'; the opposite of *'omdeforush*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khordeforush* is a native compound of *khorde* 'small bits', which EtymDB lists, and *forush*, the present stem of *forukhtan* 'to sell'.
