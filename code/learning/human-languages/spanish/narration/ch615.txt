Lesson 1 of 6.
programar — to schedule, to plan; to program.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the guarantee, then the Spanish for the protection.

You'll want to know: programar.
programar — "to schedule, to plan; to program".
Programamos la reunión para el martes. We scheduled the meeting for Tuesday.
Also to write a computer program.

The word, taken apart.
programar is built on programa, from Late Latin programma, taken from Greek programma 'a public notice', from graphein 'to write' (EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
planificar is built on plan with the ending -ificar; plan rests on plano, a learned borrowing from Latin planus 'flat' (Lewis & Short).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: el propósito.
el propósito — "the purpose, the aim; the resolution".
Mi propósito es aprender inglés. My aim is to learn English.
A propósito — on purpose; by the way. Los propósitos de Año Nuevo — New Year resolutions.

The word, taken apart.
propósito is a learned borrowing from Latin propositum 'that which is proposed, an aim', built on proponere 'to put forward' (EtymDB).
