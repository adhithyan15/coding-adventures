## You'll want to know: die Demokratie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C358-GESELLSCHAFT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Demokratie** — "democracy".

> In einer Demokratie wählen die Bürger das Parlament.
>
> *In a democracy the citizens elect parliament.*

Plural: *die Demokratien*. The stress is on the last syllable: *de-mo-kra-TIE*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Demokratie* was borrowed from French *démocratie* (EtymDB), from Latin *dēmocratia* and Greek *dēmokratía*, built from *dêmos* "the common people" and *krátos* "strength, power".
