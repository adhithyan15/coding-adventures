Gujarati, chapter 551: Training for the Big Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
તાલીમ (tālīm) — training.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pierce (a target), then the Gujarati for a runner.

You'll want to know: તાલીમ.
તાલીમ — tālīm — "training".
જો તમે સારી તાલીમ લેશો તો તમે ઝડપી દોડી શકશો. jo tame sārī tālīm leśo to tame jhaḍpī doḍī śakśo. If you get good training, you'll be able to run fast.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
તાલીમ (tālīm) is usually traced to Arabic taʿlīm, "teaching, instruction", through Persian.

જો મીરા રોજ રિયાઝ કરશે તો તેનો સૂર વધારે મીઠો થશે. jo Mīrā roj riyājh karśe to teno sūr vadhāre mīṭho thaśe. If Mira practises every day, her singing will get sweeter.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: દોડાવવું.
દોડાવવું — doḍāvvũ — "to make (someone) run".
જો કોચ અમને રોજ ન દોડાવત તો અમે આટલા ઝડપી ન હોત. jo koc amne roj na doḍāvat to ame āṭlā jhaḍpī na hot. If the coach hadn't made us run every day, we wouldn't be this fast.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે છોકરાઓને દોડાવ્યા, teṇe chokrāone doḍāvyā.

The word, taken apart.
દોડાવવું (doḍāvvũ) is built from દોડવું, "to run", with the causative -āv-. CDIAL files દોડવું under Sanskrit drávati, "runs" (CDIAL 6624).
