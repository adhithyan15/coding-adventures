# सहसा (sahsā) — usually

## You'll want to know: सहसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C389-PRAMANE389-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सहसा** — *sahsā* — "usually".

> मी सहसा सकाळी लवकर उठतो.
>
> *mī sahsā sakāḷī lavkar uṭhto.*
>
> I usually get up early in the morning.

With a negative, not readily: तो सहसा रागावत नाही.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहसा** (*sahsā*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *sahasā*, "forcibly, suddenly" (Monier-Williams); in Marathi it means as a rule.
