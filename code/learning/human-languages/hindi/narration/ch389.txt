Hindi, chapter 389: Better and Worse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बेहतर (behtar) — better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for minor, then the Hindi for close to.

You'll want to know: बेहतर.
बेहतर — behtar — "better".
अगर तुम आराम करोगे तो कल तबीयत बेहतर होगी। agar tum ārām karoge to kal tabīyat behtar hogī. If you rest, you'll feel better tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
बेहतर (behtar) is usually traced to Persian behtar, "better".

Lesson 5 of 5.
निखरना (nikharnā) — to brighten, to come out at its best.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for first-rate, then the Hindi for normal.

You'll want to know: निखरना.
निखरना — nikharnā — "to brighten, to come out at its best".
अगर तुम रोज़ अभ्यास करोगे तो तुम्हारा गाना निखर जाएगा। agar tum roz abhyās karoge to tumhārā gānā nikhar jāegā. If you practise every day, your singing will come into its own.
Past (it …): वह रंग धूप में निखर गया, vah raṅg dhūp meṁ nikhar gayā.

The word, taken apart.
निखरना (nikharnā) is inherited from niḥkṣarati, "flows out", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7095). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to become clear".
