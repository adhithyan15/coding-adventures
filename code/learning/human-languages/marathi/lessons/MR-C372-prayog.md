## You'll want to know: प्रयोग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C372-SANSHODHAN372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोग** — *prayog* — "an experiment".

> विद्यार्थ्यांनी वर्गात एक सोपा प्रयोग केला.
>
> *vidyārthyānnī vargāt ek sopā prayog kelā.*
>
> The students did a simple experiment in class.

Masculine.

प्रयोग करणे, to experiment; also a stage performance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोग** (*prayog*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prayoga*, "joining; application, use" (Monier-Williams).
