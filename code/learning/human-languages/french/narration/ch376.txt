French, chapter 376: À l'université.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la faculté — the faculty, the university; the ability.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the school report, then the French for the head teacher.

You'll want to know: la faculté.
la faculté — "the faculty, the university; the ability".
Elle étudie à la faculté de médecine. She studies at the faculty of medicine.
In speech, la fac: aller à la fac. It can also mean an ability.

The word, taken apart.
faculté is a learned borrowing from Latin facultās "ability, power" (Lewis & Short), from facilis "easy, doable".

Lesson 2 of 5.
inscrire — to enrol, to register (mostly reflexive, s'inscrire); to write down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the head teacher, then the French for the faculty.

You'll want to know: inscrire.
inscrire — "to enrol, to register (mostly reflexive, s'inscrire); to write down".
Je me suis inscrit à un cours d'anglais. I enrolled in an English course.
S'inscrire à — to sign up for. Conjugated like écrire: je m'inscris.

The word, taken apart.
inscrire is a learned borrowing from Latin īnscrībere "to write on, to inscribe" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), reshaped on écrire; it is built on scrībere "to write".

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'amphithéâtre — the lecture hall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the registration, then the French for the semester.

You'll want to know: l'amphithéâtre.
l'amphithéâtre — "the lecture hall".
Le cours a lieu dans le grand amphithéâtre. The lecture takes place in the big lecture hall.
Shortened to l'amphi. Also an ancient arena. Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
amphithéâtre comes from Latin amphitheātrum (EtymDB), from Greek amphithéatron, built from amphí "around" and théatron "a place for seeing".
