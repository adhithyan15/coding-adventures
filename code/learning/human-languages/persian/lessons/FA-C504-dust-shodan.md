## You'll want to know: دوست شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C504-FRIENDS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دوست شدن** (*dust shodan*) — "to make friends".

> او در مدرسه جدید زود با همه دوست شد.
>
> *u dar madrese-ye jadid zud bâ hame dust shod.*
>
> At the new school he quickly made friends with everyone.

*bâ kasi dust shodan*; *dust* 'friend' + *shodan* 'to become'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dust shodan* is built on two inherited words: *dust*, 'friend', usually traced to Old Persian *dauštā*, 'beloved', and *shodan*, 'to become'.
