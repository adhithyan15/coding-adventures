## You'll want to know: ಸಂದೇಹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C383-SANDEHA383-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂದೇಹ** — *sandēha* — "a misgiving; a doubt".

> ಅವನ ಕಥೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ನನಗೆ ಸಂದೇಹ ಇದೆ.
>
> *avana katheya bagge nanage sandēha ide.*
>
> I have doubts about his story.

Who doubts takes -ಗೆ: ನನಗೆ ಸಂದೇಹ ಇದೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂದೇಹ** (*sandēha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃdeha*, "doubt, uncertainty" (Monier-Williams).
