Urdu, chapter 68: Goat, Sheep, Bird, Mouse, Lion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بکری (bakrī) — a goat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a horse, then the Urdu for a cow.

You'll want to know: بکری.
بکری — bakrī — "a goat".

The word, taken apart.
بکری (bakrī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from barkara, "kid, goat" (CDIAL 9153).
