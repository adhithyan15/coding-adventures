Bengali, chapter 403: Checking In.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘর (ghôr) — a room (in a house); a home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for at a loss, then the Bengali for to go round in circles.

You'll want to know: ঘর.
ঘর — ghôr — "a room (in a house); a home".
হোটেলে আমাদের ঘরটা ছোট, কিন্তু পরিচ্ছন্ন। hoṭele āmāder ghôrṭā chhoṭo, kintu pôrichchhônnô. Our room at the hotel is small but clean.
ঘর (ghôr) is any room, and home as well: ঘরে ফেরা, to come home. রুম is the hotel word.

The word, taken apart.
ঘর (ghôr) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali ghar, "a house", under ghara, "a house" (CDIAL 4428), and notes that the usual link with Sanskrit gṛha is hard to make work by sound.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 5 of 5.
বন্দোবস্ত করা (bôndobôstô kôrā) — to arrange; to make arrangements.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a lift, then the Bengali for a reception desk.

You'll want to know: বন্দোবস্ত করা.
বন্দোবস্ত করা — bôndobôstô kôrā — "to arrange; to make arrangements".
মামা আমাদের থাকার বন্দোবস্ত করলেন। māmā āmāder thākār bôndobôstô kôrlen. My uncle arranged somewhere for us to stay.
Past (he/she …): তিনি গাড়ির বন্দোবস্ত করলেন, tini gāṛir bôndobôstô kôrlen.
The noun alone, বন্দোবস্ত, is an arrangement; ব্যবস্থা says much the same in a more careful way.

The word, taken apart.
বন্দোবস্ত করা (bôndobôstô kôrā) joins বন্দোবস্ত, usually traced to Persian band-o-bast, "tying and binding", a settlement of affairs, to করা, to do.
