Gujarati, chapter 477: After a Big Meal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઓડકાર (oḍkār) — a belch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to lie down to sleep, then the Gujarati for sleeplessness.

You'll want to know: ઓડકાર.
ઓડકાર — oḍkār — "a belch".
જમ્યા પછી દાદાએ મોટો ઓડકાર ખાધો. jamyā pachī dādāe moṭo oḍkār khādho. After the meal my grandfather gave a loud belch.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઓડકાર (oḍkār) probably goes back to Sanskrit udgāra, "the act of discharging, spitting out", as Monier-Williams records it.

વધારે ભોજન પછી પેટમાં આફરો ચઢ્યો. vadhāre bhojan pachī peṭmā̃ āphro caḍhyo. After the big meal my stomach felt bloated.

આફરો (āphro) goes back to a reconstructed āsphara-, "swelling"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "wind in belly" (CDIAL 1526).

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દૂધ ઓક્યું, teṇe dūdh okyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઓકવું (okvũ) goes back to a reconstructed ōkk-, "vomit", an imitative word; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 2538).
