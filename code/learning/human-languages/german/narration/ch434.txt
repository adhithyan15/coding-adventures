German, chapter 434: Den Schildern nach.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Wegweiser — the signpost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to find one's way around, then the German for to get lost.

You'll want to know: der Wegweiser.
der Wegweiser — "the signpost".
Auf dem Wegweiser steht: Bahnhof zwei Kilometer. The signpost says: station two kilometres.
Same form in the plural. Also a guidebook or a guide.

The word, taken apart.
Wegweiser is built from Weg 'way', inherited from Proto-Germanic wegaz (EtymDB), and Weiser 'one who shows', from weisen 'to show, to direct' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
das Schild — the sign (on a road or a door).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to get lost, then the German for the signpost.

You'll want to know: das Schild.
das Schild — "the sign (on a road or a door)".
Auf dem Schild steht, dass man hier nicht parken darf. The sign says that you cannot park here.
Plural: die Schilder. Der Schild (masculine) is a shield.

The word, taken apart.
Schild is an inherited Germanic word, first 'shield', and English shield is a cousin (EtymDB). The neuter das Schild 'sign' is usually explained as growing from shields that carried a mark.

Plural: die Pfeile.

Pfeil is an early borrowing from Latin pīlum 'javelin' (Lewis & Short), already in Old High German as pfīl (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Abzweigung — the turning, the fork (in a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the sign, then the German for the arrow.

You'll want to know: die Abzweigung.
die Abzweigung — "the turning, the fork (in a road)".
Nach der Brücke kommt eine Abzweigung nach rechts. After the bridge there is a turning to the right.
Plural: die Abzweigungen.

The word, taken apart.
Abzweigung is built on abzweigen 'to branch off', from Zweig 'twig, branch', inherited from Old High German zwīg and a cousin of English twig (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
abzweigen — to branch off, to turn off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the arrow, then the German for the turning.

You'll want to know: abzweigen.
abzweigen — "to branch off, to turn off".
Hinter dem Dorf zweigt ein kleiner Weg zum See ab. Beyond the village a small path branches off to the lake.
Separable; Perfekt with sein when someone turns off: wir sind nach links abgezweigt.

The word, taken apart.
abzweigen is built from ab 'off' and Zweig 'branch', inherited from Old High German zwīg (EtymDB).
