ഭൂമി (bhūmi) — the earth, land.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a direction, then the Malayalam for north.

You'll want to know: ഭൂമി.
ഭൂമി — bhūmi — "the earth, land".

The word, taken apart.
ഭൂമി (bhūmi) from Sanskrit bhūmi, "earth, ground", taken over whole as a tatsama (CDIAL 9557).
