## You'll want to know: مجسمه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C450-MARKAZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مجسمه** (*mojassame*) — "a statue".

> وسط میدان یک مجسمه بزرگ است.
>
> *Vasat-e meydân yek mojassame-ye bozorg ast.*
>
> There is a big statue in the middle of the square.

Built on *jesm* 'body': a thing given a body.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mojassame* was borrowed from Arabic *mujassama* (EtymDB glosses it 'maquette'), a word built on *jism* 'body'.
