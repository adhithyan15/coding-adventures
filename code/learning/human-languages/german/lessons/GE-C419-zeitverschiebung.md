## You'll want to know: die Zeitverschiebung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C419-ZWISCHENLANDUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Zeitverschiebung** — "the time difference; jet lag".

> Wegen der Zeitverschiebung ist er um vier Uhr morgens wach.
>
> *Because of the time difference he is awake at four in the morning.*

Plural: *die Zeitverschiebungen*. People also say *der Jetlag*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zeitverschiebung* is built from *Zeit* 'time', inherited from Proto-Germanic *tīdiz* (EtymDB), and *Verschiebung* 'shift', from *schieben* 'to push', inherited from Old High German *skioban* (EtymDB).
