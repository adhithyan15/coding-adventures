## You'll want to know: एकाग्रता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C375-BUDDHI375-03]; assesses=[] -->

**एकाग्रता** — *ekāgratā* — "concentration".

> अभ्यास करताना एकाग्रता हवी.
>
> *abhyās kartānā ekāgratā havī.*
>
> You need concentration while studying.

Feminine.

एकाग्र, intent on one point, + -ता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एकाग्रता** (*ekāgratā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ekāgratā*, "intentness on one object" (Monier-Williams).
