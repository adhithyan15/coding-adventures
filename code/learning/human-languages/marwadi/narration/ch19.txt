Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 19: Cheap and Goods: No New Signs.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
Hear sastā — cheap.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the three-word shopping payoff and say price.
[once you have stopped driving — write: ु, दुकान, वस्तु, भाव, and भाई]
Then say heat.

You'll want to know.
sastā — cheap; inexpensive.
Say the two beats once from a low-price cue. This is the source's dictionary form; full bargaining phrases remain later work.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What does sastā say about a price?


Lesson 2 of 7.
सस्ता (sastā) — cheap on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 25 seconds]
Say cheap and price.
[once you have stopped driving — write: भाव, वस्तु, बरसात, and पति]
Say sister, then recall ् and त.

You'll want to know.
सस् + ता becomes सस्ता — sastā — cheap.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, and wait five seconds.
[once you have stopped driving — write: सस्ता (sastā) — check the middle consonant join and final long ा]

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From sastā, write cheap.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Payoff — five shopping words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the earlier shopping and weather payoffs and say money.
[once you have stopped driving — write: द and ड]

Guided Practice.
Identify five heard words.
Produce five words from meaning cues.
Match five printed cards to meanings.
[once you have stopped driving — write: all five heard words without a model]
Pass each skill separately; a strong word cannot compensate for a missed one.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 42 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write shop, item, price, cheap, and goods.
