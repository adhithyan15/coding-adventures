Bengali, chapter 495: Pay Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বেতন (betôn) — pay; wages.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a job interview, then the Bengali for to join a job.

You'll want to know: বেতন.
বেতন — betôn — "pay; wages".
মাসের শেষে আমরা বেতন পাই। Māser sheshe āmrā betôn pāi. We get our pay at the end of the month.

The word, taken apart.
বেতন (betôn) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vetana, "wages, hire" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
পোষানো (poshāno) — to be worth it; to pay enough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for earnings, then the Bengali for a pension.

You'll want to know: পোষানো.
পোষানো — poshāno — "to be worth it; to pay enough".
এই মাইনেতে আমার পোষায় না। Ei māinete āmār poshāy nā. This salary isn't worth my while.
Past (he/she …): সেই মাইনেতে তার পোষাল না, sei māinete tār poshālô nā.
The person it pays takes the genitive: আমার পোষায় না, it isn't worth it for me.

The word, taken apart.
পোষানো (poshāno) is a tadbhava from Sanskrit poṣayati, "nourishes, keeps" (CDIAL 8410); the sense "to be worth it" is Bengali's own.
