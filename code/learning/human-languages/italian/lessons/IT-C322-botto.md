## You'll want to know: il botto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C322-FUOCHI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il botto** — "the firework bang; the crash".

> A fine anno si sentono botti dappertutto.
>
> *At New Year you hear bangs everywhere.*

*Di botto* — suddenly. *I botti* are firecrackers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*botto* is usually explained as a sound word that imitates a bang (EtymDB has no entry).
