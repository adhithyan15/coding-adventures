## You'll want to know: ऋषिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C332-AKHYANA332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ऋषिः** — *ṛṣiḥ* — "a seer; a sage".

> पुरा वने एकः ऋषिः वसति स्म।
>
> *purā vane ekaḥ ṛṣiḥ vasati sma.*
>
> Long ago a seer lived in the forest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऋषिः** (*ṛṣiḥ*) MW reports several derivations, among them an old root meaning to see, and gives *ṛṣi* as a singer of sacred hymns, an inspired poet or seer, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
