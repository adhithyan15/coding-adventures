## You'll want to know: রূপরেখা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C531-KHOSRA531-04]; assesses=[] -->

**রূপরেখা** — *rūprekhā* — "an outline".

> ম্যানেজার নতুন কাজের একটা রূপরেখা দিলেন।
>
> *Mænejār nôtun kājer ekṭā rūprekhā dilen.*
>
> The manager gave an outline of the new work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রূপরেখা** (*rūprekhā*) joins Sanskrit *rūpa*, "form, shape", and *rekhā*, "a line, a stroke" (Monier-Williams): the lines of a shape before it is filled in.
