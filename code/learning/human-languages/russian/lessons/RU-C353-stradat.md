## You'll want to know: страдать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C353-USHCHERB-04]; assesses=[] -->

**страдать** (*stradát*) — "to suffer".

> Он страдает от головной боли.
>
> *On stradáyet ot golovnóy bóli.*
>
> He suffers from headaches.

Imperfective. *Страдать от* + genitive (from a cause), *страдать* + instrumental (from an illness).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**страдать** belongs with *страда* "hard farm work, harvest time" (EtymDB lists both, and glosses the verb "to suffer, to drudge"). Toil and suffering sit side by side in the one word.
