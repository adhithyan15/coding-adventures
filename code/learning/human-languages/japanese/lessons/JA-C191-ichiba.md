## You'll want to know: いちば
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C191-SHUT191-02]; assesses=[] -->

**いちば** — *ichiba* — "market; marketplace".

> **いちばで さかなを かいます。** — *ichiba de sakana o kaimasu* — "I buy fish at the market."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いちば** (*ichiba*) is native Japanese, built from *ichi*, "market", and *ba*, "place", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
