## You'll want to know: उधेड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C383-SANKAT383-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उधेड़ना** — *udheṛnā* — "to unpick, to unravel".

> अगर तुम यह धागा खींचोगे तो पूरा स्वेटर उधेड़ दोगे।
>
> *agar tum yah dhāgā khīñcoge to pūrā sveṭar udheṛ doge.*
>
> If you pull this thread, you'll unravel the whole jumper.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पुराना स्वेटर उधेड़ा, *usne purānā sveṭar udheṛā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उधेड़ना** (*udheṛnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *uddharati*, "takes out" (CDIAL 2009).
