Lesson 2 of 5.
One noun for information or a notice — information or notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the familiar first and last words of the identity frame.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਸੂਚਨਾ (suchnaa) — sūchnā — information; notice.
Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole noun rather than inventing smaller Punjabi pieces. This lesson owns the word alone; the complete identity frame comes only after a separate retrieval.

The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out.
Punjabi University's Punjabipedia records this exact noun with information, news, and notice meanings. It supports the bounded present-day information and notice senses taught here. Source: Punjabi University Punjabipedia.
The exact history of modern written ਸੂਚਨਾ (suchnaa) remains unresolved in the sources used here, so this lesson makes no etymological claim. Keep the present-day Punjabi noun whole rather than turning spelling resemblance into a derivation. Lesson 4 of 5.
Put the new noun into one identity frame — this is information or a notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਇਹ, then the new noun, then ਹੈ.

Grammar Lens.
ਇਹ ਸੂਚਨਾ ਹੈ (eh suchnaa hai) — eh sūchnā hai — this is information / a notice.
Keep the new whole noun between familiar ਇਹ and ਹੈ. Familiar ਹੈ stays last in the already-owned identity frame. This chapter teaches only this one label; it does not add an inflection rule or a wider construction.
