## You'll want to know: interrogō, interrogāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C412-COGNITIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**interrogō, interrogāre** — "to question, to examine".

> Iūdex testem interrogāvit.
>
> *The judge questioned the witness.*

*interrogō, interrogāre, interrogāvī, interrogātum*, *inter* + *rogō*. English *interrogate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*interrogō* is built from *inter* "between" and *rogō* "to ask" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *interrogate* comes from it.
