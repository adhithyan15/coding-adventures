Tamil, chapter 163: To prepare, To serve, To share, To bargain, To check.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தயார் செய் (tayār cey) — to prepare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to move, then the Tamil for to lift.

You'll want to know: தயார் செய்.
தயார் செய் — tayār cey — "to prepare".

The word, taken apart.
தயார் செய் (tayār cey) is tayār, "ready", a loan from Arabic tayyār through Persian and Hindustani, with native sey, "to do" (DEDR 1957): "make ready".

The word, taken apart.
பரிமாறு (parimāṟu) ends in native māṟu, "to change, to exchange" (DEDR 4834). The first part, pari, is not settled, so the origin of the whole word is uncertain.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to prepare, then the Tamil for to serve.

You'll want to know: பகிர்.
பகிர் — pakir — "to share".

The word, taken apart.
பகிர் (pakir) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3808 lists Malayalam pakuka, "to divide". It belongs with paku, "to be divided, to share out".

Lesson 4 of 5.
பேரம் பேசு (pēram pēcu) — to bargain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: பேரம் பேசு.
பேரம் பேசு — pēram pēcu — "to bargain".

The word, taken apart.
பேரம் பேசு (pēram pēcu) is pēram, "bargain", with native pēsu, "to speak" (DEDR 4430): "to talk a bargain". The origin of pēram is uncertain; DEDR has no entry for it.

The word, taken apart.
சரிபார் (caripār) is sari, "correct", and native pār, "to look" (DEDR 4091): "look to see it is right". The origin of sari is uncertain, as its own lesson said.
