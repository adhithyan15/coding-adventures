## You'll want to know: गाळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C444-DABKE444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गाळ** — *gāḷ* — "silt".

> पुरानंतर शेतात गाळ साचला.
>
> *purānantar śetāt gāḷ sāclā.*
>
> After the flood silt settled in the fields.

Masculine.

गाळ काढणे, to clear the silt from a tank or a well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गाळ** (*gāḷ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *gāḷ*, "dregs, mud", under *\*gāḍa* (CDIAL 4114).
