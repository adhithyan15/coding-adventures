## Chapters 154-157: negation and questions, the past, the future and practical texts

Kannada had realized one of its five A2 spine nodes. Four chapters realize the
other four, so the A2 gate reports no missing spine node; vocabulary and verb
vocabulary remain.

- Chapter 154 (`SPINE-NEGATE-AND-ASK`): ಯಾರೂ, ಎಂದಿಗೂ, ಎಲ್ಲಿಯೂ, ಯಾರಾದರೂ,
  ಏನಾದರೂ. The notes show ಇಲ್ಲ joined onto a verb (ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ), ಅಲ್ಲ for "is
  not" (ಇದು ನನ್ನ ಮನೆ ಅಲ್ಲ), and the yes-or-no question in -ಆ (ಇದ್ದಾರಾ).
- Chapter 155 (`SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-PAST`): ಇತ್ತೀಚೆಗೆ, ಈಗಾಗಲೇ, ಕಳೆದ, ಒಮ್ಮೆ,
  ಕಳೆ. The notes show the past for "I" in -ಎ (ಓದಿದೆ, ಹೋದೆ).
- Chapter 156 (`SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE`): ಮುಂದಿನ, ಇನ್ನು ಮುಂದೆ, ಭವಿಷ್ಯ,
  ನಿರ್ಧರಿಸು, ನಿರೀಕ್ಷಿಸು. The notes show the future for "I" in -ಉತ್ತೇನೆ
  (ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ).
- Chapter 157 (`SPINE-READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS`): ವೇಳಾಪಟ್ಟಿ, ಸೂಚನೆ, ಬಾಡಿಗೆ,
  ಜಾಹೀರಾತು, ರಸೀದಿ, each with a notice to read and act on.
- Extensions carry stage A2. Chapter 157 closes with two reviews. A
  native-level reviewer checked the notes; its fixes (how ಇಲ್ಲ joins the verb,
  how -ಆ replaces the last vowel) are applied.
