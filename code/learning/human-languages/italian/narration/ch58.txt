Italian, chapter 58: Bedroom, Floor, Stairs, Mirror, Cup.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la camera — a bedroom, a room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a lamp, then the Italian for a bathroom.

You'll want to know: la camera.
la camera — "a bedroom, a room".

The word, taken apart.
camera from Latin camera, "a vaulted room" — inherited; Latin took it from Greek kamara, "vault". (EtymDB 2.1).
