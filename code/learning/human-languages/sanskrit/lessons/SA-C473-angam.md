## You'll want to know: अंगम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C473-ANGA473-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगम्** — *aṅgam* — "a limb".

> व्यायामेन अंगानि दृढानि भवन्ति।
>
> *vyāyāmena aṅgāni dṛḍhāni bhavanti.*
>
> Exercise makes the limbs strong.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगम्** (*aṅgam*) is traced to a root √am in the Uṇādi list that MW follows; MW gives "a limb of the body, a member" (MW).
