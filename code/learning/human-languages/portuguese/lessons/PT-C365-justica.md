## You'll want to know: a justiça
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C365-JUSTICA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a justiça** — "the justice; the courts".

> As pessoas querem justiça.
>
> *People want justice.*

*Fazer justiça* — to do justice. *A justiça* also means the courts as a system.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*justiça* comes from Latin *iūstitia* "justice, equity" (Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese *justiça* (EtymDB); it is built on *iūstus* "fair, righteous".
