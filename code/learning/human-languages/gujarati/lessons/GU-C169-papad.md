## You'll want to know: પાપડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C169-DISH169-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પાપડ** — *pāpaḍ* — "papad, poppadom".

> **પાપડ પાતળો છે.** — *pāpaḍ pātḷo che* — "The papad is thin."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાપડ** (*pāpaḍ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *parpaṭa*, "a thin cake of rice or pease-meal", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati *pāpaḍ*, "a thin cake" (CDIAL 7934).
