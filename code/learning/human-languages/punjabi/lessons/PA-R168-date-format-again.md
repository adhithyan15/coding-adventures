# The old printed date order

The fictional form still prints **day / month / year**. Another form may
choose another order; follow what this one prints. No complete personal
date or scored A1 response is requested here.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->

Look at the printed order from left to right: **day**, **month**, **year**. The familiar model
**__/__/____** has two places, two places, then four places. Slashes separate
the boxes; they are not digits.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy only the empty frame **__/__/____**. Label its boxes in English:
day, month, year. Keep the two slash marks visible; do not fill a real date.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-FORMAT-01] -->

Cover the model. Draw the empty frame and point to the **middle month** box.
Uncover and check its order and two separators. This is a no-stakes retrieval,
not independent scored writing.
