## You'll want to know: der Scheinwerfer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C400-SICHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Scheinwerfer** — "the headlight; the spotlight".

> Am Abend schaltet sie die Scheinwerfer ein.
>
> *In the evening she switches the headlights on.*

Same form in the plural: *die Scheinwerfer*. On a stage, the spotlight is also *der Scheinwerfer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Scheinwerfer* is built from *Schein* 'light, glow' and *Werfer* 'thrower', from *werfen* 'to throw': a thrower of light. Both are inherited: *Schein* goes back to Proto-Germanic *skīnaną* 'to shine' and *werfen* to *werpaną* 'to throw' (EtymDB).
