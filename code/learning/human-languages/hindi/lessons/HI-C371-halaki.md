## You'll want to know: हालाँकि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C371-HALANKI371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हालाँकि** — *hālā̃ki* — "although".

> हालाँकि बारिश हो रही थी, फिर भी हम बाज़ार गए।
>
> *hālā̃ki bāriś ho rahī thī, phir bhī ham bāzār gae.*
>
> Although it was raining, we still went to the market.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हालाँकि** (*hālā̃ki*) is usually traced to Persian *hālānki*, "although".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
