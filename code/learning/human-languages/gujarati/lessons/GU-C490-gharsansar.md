## You'll want to know: ઘરસંસાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C490-SASRA490-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘરસંસાર** — *gharsansār* — "married life; running a household".

> લગ્ન પછી મીરાનો ઘરસંસાર સારો ચાલે છે.
>
> *lagna pachī Mīrāno gharsansār sāro cāle che.*
>
> Mira's married life is going well.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘરસંસાર** (*gharsansār*) is built from **ઘર** (*ghar*), "home", and *sansār*, Sanskrit *saṃsāra*, "worldly existence" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
