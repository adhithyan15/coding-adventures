Hindi, chapter 334: A Tale of Long Ago.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कथा (kathā) — a tale; a sacred story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be scattered, then the Hindi for the floating of lamps.

You'll want to know: कथा.
कथा — kathā — "a tale; a sacred story".
दादी हर रात एक नई कथा सुनाती थीं। dādī har rāt ek naī kathā sunātī thīṁ. Grandmother used to tell a new tale every night.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कथा (kathā) is Sanskrit kathā, "a story, a conversation" (Monier-Williams). Beside कहानी, it is the old or sacred tale.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: पहेली.
पहेली — pahelī — "a riddle".
ऋषि ने राजा से एक पहेली पूछी। ṛṣi ne rājā se ek pahelī pūchī. The sage asked the king a riddle.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
पहेली (pahelī) goes back to Sanskrit prahelikā, "a riddle" (CDIAL 8911). You पहेली पूछना, ask one, and पहेली बूझना, solve it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कहावत (kahāvat) — a saying; a proverb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a hermitage, then the Hindi for a riddle.

You'll want to know: कहावत.
कहावत — kahāvat — "a saying; a proverb".
दादी हर कहानी के बाद एक कहावत कहती थीं। dādī har kahānī ke bād ek kahāvat kahtī thīṁ. After every story my grandmother would say a proverb.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कहावत (kahāvat) is inherited from kathāvārttā, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 2706), joining words for telling and for talk: a saying everyone repeats.
