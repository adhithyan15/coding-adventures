## You'll want to know: अवतारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C305-UDDHARANA305-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अवतारयति** — *avatārayati* — "brings down; lowers".

> सैनिकाः आहतं पर्वतात् अवतारयन्ति।
>
> *sainikāḥ āhataṃ parvatāt avatārayanti.*
>
> Soldiers bring the injured man down from the hill.

Past (he …): सः बालं वृक्षात् अवतारितवान्, *saḥ bālaṃ vṛkṣāt avatāritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवतारयति** (*avatārayati*) is built from *ava*, "down", and the causative of the root √tṝ, "to cross"; MW gives *ava-tārita* as caused to descend, fetched down (MW).
