# घड़णो (ghaṛṇo) — to shape; to forge (metal or clay)

## You'll want to know: घड़णो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C264-LUHAR264-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घड़णो** — *ghaṛṇo* — "to shape; to forge (metal or clay)".

> सुनार अंगूठी घड़ै है।
>
> *sunār aṅgūṭhī ghaṛai hai.*
>
> The goldsmith shapes a ring.

Past (I …): म्हैं कड़ो घड़्यो, *mhaĩ kaṛo ghaṛyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घड़णो** (*ghaṛṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ghaṭate*, "is busy with"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ghaṛī*, "formed", under it (CDIAL 4407).
