## You'll want to know: píngyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C437-LAND437-02]; assesses=[] -->

*píngyuán* — "plain; flat open land".

> *běifāng de píngyuán hěn dà* — The plains in the north are huge.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*píngyuán* is built from *píng* 'level' and *yuán* 'broad flat land', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjaeng* and *ngjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'right' and 'at peace' and the second, after an older dictionary, as broad flat land; the sense 'level' for the first is not given there.
