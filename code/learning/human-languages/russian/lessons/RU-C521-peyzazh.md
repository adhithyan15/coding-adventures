## You'll want to know: пейзаж
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C521-PAINTING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**пейзаж** (*peyzázh*) — "a landscape".

> На стене висит пейзаж с лесом и рекой.
>
> *Na stené visít peyzázh s lésom i rekóy.*
>
> A landscape with a forest and a river hangs on the wall.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пейзаж* was borrowed from French *paysage* 'countryside, landscape' (EtymDB).
