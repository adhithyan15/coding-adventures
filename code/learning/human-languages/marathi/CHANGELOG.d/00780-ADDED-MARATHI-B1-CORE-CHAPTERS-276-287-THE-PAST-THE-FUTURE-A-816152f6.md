## Added — Marathi B1 core, chapters 276–287, the past, the future and all five B1 spine nodes

- Added twelve core chapters, 48 lessons (sequence 16000–16470). Chapters
  276–277 bridge the two A2 grammar nodes the track had left empty: the past
  (मी गेलो, the ergative त्याने काम केलं with the verb agreeing with the
  object, and होता / नव्हता) and the future (मी जाईन, the plan -णार आहे and
  the everyday "won't" -णार नाही). Marathi's spine mirrors 0220 and 0230 no
  longer omit VERB-PAST and VERB-FUTURE.
- Chapters 278–287 realize the five B1 nodes, two chapters each: narrating
  (-त होतो, असताना, जेव्हा … तेव्हा with the past, the habitual -ायचो, आधी /
  मग / त्यानंतर / शेवटी, -नंतर, the -ऊन form beside -ल्यावर), giving reasons
  (मला वाटतं की, माझ्या मते, म्हणून, त्यामुळे, -ायला पाहिजे / हवं, -ऊ नये,
  -मुळे, कारण with a whole past sentence), handling travel (… ला कसं जायचं?,
  इथून किती लांब आहे?, the polite वळा, landmarks with -च्या, buying a ticket,
  when a train leaves, a lost bag and a missed train), describing experience
  (feelings in the dative with आला / वाटलं / झालं, -ायचं आहे and व्हायचं आहे,
  -पेक्षा, जास्त, सर्वात, -पासून for a span up to now, -पासून … -पर्यंत, -भर)
  and expressing conditions (जर … तर with the past form looking ahead, जर …
  असेल, the formal -ल्यास, नाहीतर, … -वर अवलंबून आहे, and the counterfactual
  past + असतो / असता). Spine mirrors 0250–0290 no longer omit any concept.
- Six new headwords: ऋतू, ज्ञान, तिकीट, राग, भीती and ध्येय, each with a cited
  origin (Monier-Williams, CDIAL, or a named English loan).
- Two letter lessons, each anchored by the word just before it: ऋ
  (MR-W279-letter-vocalic-r, after ऋतू) and ञ (MR-W281-letter-nya, after
  ज्ञान). Each quotes its inventory row's numbered stroke order, pen lifts and
  source, which script-ductus already draws; no row changes. Both letters are
  revisited by their chapter review, every later core review and the six-line
  writing task.
- An eighty-six-word reading in chapter 279 (a monsoon day in the village)
  and a six-line connected writing task, MR-W287-six-lines, in chapter 287.
  Each chapter ends on its review, which is the chapter's payoff.
- The book's preamble sets quote blocks ragged right (HL-C88, extended to
  quotes), as the Malayalam and Telugu preambles do, so the B1 example
  sentences cannot stretch into underfull or overfull lines; \hbadness,
  \hfuzz and the warning baseline are unchanged.
- Pins: Marathi leaves the A2 reach list and joins the over-A2 list;
  letter-anchoring unreadInventory 2 → 0; filmstrip targets 53 → 55; lessons
  1471 → 1519 and the per-chapter sizes for 276–287.
