## You'll want to know: ڈھول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C519-DHOL519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھول** — *ḍhol* — "a drum".

> شادی میں لڑکے ڈھول بجا رہے تھے۔
>
> *shādī meṅ laṛke ḍhol bajā rahe the.*
>
> At the wedding the boys were playing the drum.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھول** (*ḍhol*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ḍhōla*, "a large drum" (CDIAL 5608).
