## You'll want to know: права
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C408-SKOROST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**права** (*pravá*) — "a driving licence; rights".

> Инспектор проверил права и паспорт.
>
> *Inspéktor provéril pravá i pásport.*
>
> The inspector checked the driving licence and the passport.

The plural of *право* (*právo*) — a right; the stress moves: *права* (*pravá*). In full, *водительские права*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*права* is the plural of *право* 'a right', built on *правый* 'right, straight'. EtymDB lists *право* beside its Ukrainian cousin *právo*: a licence is the right to drive.
