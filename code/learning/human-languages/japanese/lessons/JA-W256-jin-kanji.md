# 人 — a person, two strokes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C256-NIHONJIN-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-DASU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *nihonjin*, a Japanese person. You write **日** and **本**. Then call up **出**: the long stem first.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-JIN-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-DASU-01] -->

> **人** — *jin*

Its parts:

- a stroke falling to the left
- a stroke falling to the right, leaning on it

Two strokes, one lift:

1. draw the long falling stroke from the top, down to the left
2. lift, start partway down the first stroke and draw the second down to the right

The second stroke starts on the first, not at the top. Here it is read **じん** (*jin*), as in **日本人**; this lesson asks only for that reading.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-JIN-01, JA-LEX-C256-NIHONJIN-01] -->

1. Find **人** in **日本人** and say *nihonjin*.
2. Copy **人** three times, saying *jin* at each one.
3. Find it once more, in **ともだちは 日本人です** (*tomodachi wa nihonjin desu*), and say the line.
