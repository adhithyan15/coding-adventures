Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 236: Farm Animals.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गधो (gadho) — a donkey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to rear (animals), then the Marwadi for chaff.

You'll want to know: गधो.
गधो — gadho — "a donkey".
गधो भारी बोझ उठावै है। gadho bhārī bojh uṭhāvai hai. The donkey carries a heavy load.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गधो (gadho) is inherited from Sanskrit gardabha, "an ass"; CDIAL lists Hindi gadahā and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4054).

कूकड़ो तड़कै बोलै है। kūkṛo taṛkai bolai hai. The rooster crows at daybreak.
