## You'll want to know: le compromis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C337-COMPROMIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le compromis** — "the compromise".

> Finalement, nous avons trouvé un compromis : un film, puis une pizza.
>
> *In the end we found a compromise: a film, then a pizza.*

*Trouver un compromis* — to reach a compromise. The same form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*compromis* comes from Latin *compromissum* "a mutual promise to abide by an arbiter's award" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built on *prōmittere* "to promise".
