## You'll want to know: સ્ફૂર્તિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C486-AAROGYA486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્ફૂર્તિ** — *sphūrti* — "vigour; get-up-and-go".

> સવારની કસરત પછી મને સ્ફૂર્તિ લાગે છે.
>
> *savārnī kasrat pachī mane sphūrti lāge che.*
>
> After my morning exercise I feel full of energy.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્ફૂર્તિ** (*sphūrti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sphūrti*, "throbbing, quivering" and "breaking forth", as Monier-Williams records it.
