## You'll want to know: કડાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C169-DISH169-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કડાઈ** — *kaḍāī* — "wok, deep pan".

> **કડાઈમાં તેલ ગરમ છે.** — *kaḍāīmā̃ tel garam che* — "The oil in the wok is hot."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કડાઈ** (*kaḍāī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *kaṭāha*, "boiler, saucepan with handles", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "frying-pan" (CDIAL 2638).
