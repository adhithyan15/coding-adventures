## You'll want to know: vāticinor, vāticinārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C348-ORACULUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vāticinor, vāticinārī** — "to prophesy, to foretell".

> Vātēs bellum longum vāticinātus est.
>
> *The seer foretold a long war.*

*vāticinor, vāticinārī, vāticinātus sum*, a deponent, from *vātēs* + *canō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vāticinor** is built on *vātēs*, "seer" (Lewis & Short), with a second part from *canō*, "to sing": to sing as a seer.
