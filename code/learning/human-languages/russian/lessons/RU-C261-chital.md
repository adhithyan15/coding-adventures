# читал, читала, читали — the past, and who did it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-CHITAT, RU-LEX-C137-PAS137-01, RU-LEX-C69-TIME2-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "recently" and "yesterday". (*Nedávno. Vcherá.*) The first
came with one past sentence: *Nedávno ya chitál knígu.* This lesson takes
that *-л* apart.

## Grammar Lens: -ть becomes -л
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-L-GENDER-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-CHITAT, RU-LEX-C38-VERB1-01, RU-LEX-MAMA, RU-LEX-PAPA] -->

Drop the infinitive's **-ть** and put **-л** in its place: *читать*,
**читал**; *работать*, **работал**. The past has no endings for *I* or
*you*. It changes for **who** did it: **-л** for a man, **-ла** for a
woman, **-ло** for a neuter noun.

> **Вчера я читал.** — *vcherá ya chitál* — Yesterday I read; a man says it.
> **Вчера я читала.** — *vcherá ya chitála* — the same, from a woman.
> **Мама работала, а папа читал.** — *máma rabótala, a pápa chitál* — Mum
> was working and Dad was reading.

*Папа* ends in *-а* and still takes **-л**: a father is a man.

## Grammar Lens: -ли for more than one
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-LI-PLURAL-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C134-ACT134-02, RU-LEX-C80-PLACES-04, RU-GRAMMAR-NATURAL-GENDER-OVERRIDE] -->

For more than one, gender drops away: **-ли**. **Мы гуляли в парке.** —
*my gulyáli v párke* — We went for a walk in the park. The polite **вы**
takes **-ли** even for one person: **Вы читали?** — *vy chitáli?*

## Grammar Lens: был, была, было, были
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-BYL-PAST-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-BYT, RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-03] -->

*Быть* is silent in the present, but its past follows the same pattern:
**был** (*byl*), **была** (*bylá*), **было** (*býlo*), **были** (*býli*).
The stress moves to the end for a woman.

> **Я была в музее.** — *ya bylá v muzéye* — I was at the museum.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-BYT] -->

By the usual historical account, the *-л* form was once a describing word,
"having read", said beside a present form of **быть**: Old Russian
*čitalŭ jesmĭ*. The helping verb wore away and the describing word stayed,
so the past still agrees like an adjective. **Быть** goes back to
Proto-Indo-European *\*bʰuH-*, "to become" (EtymDB 2.1); English *be*
comes from the same root.
