## You'll want to know: расспрашивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C441-PROKHOZHIY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**расспрашивать** (*rasspráshivat*) — "to ask around, to question".

> Туристы расспрашивают местных о дороге.
>
> *Turísty rasspráshivayut méstnykh o doróge.*
>
> The tourists ask the locals about the way.

Imperfective; perfective *расспросить*. Takes the accusative of the person and *о* + prepositional.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*расспрашивать* is built from *рас-* 'all round' and *спрашивать* 'to ask', on the root of *просить* 'to ask', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic *prositi* and the PIE root *preḱ-*.
