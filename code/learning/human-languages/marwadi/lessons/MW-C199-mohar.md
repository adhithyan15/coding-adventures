## You'll want to know: मोहर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C199-SEAL199-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहर** — *mohar* — "an official stamp; a seal".

> अरजी माथै मोहर कोनी।
>
> *arjī māthai mohar konī.*
>
> There is no stamp on the application.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहर** (*mohar*) is usually traced to Persian *muhr*, "seal, stamp".
