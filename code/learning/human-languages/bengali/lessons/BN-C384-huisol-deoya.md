## You'll want to know: হুইসল দেওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C384-REL384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**হুইসল দেওয়া** — *huisôl deoyā* — "to blow a whistle (of a train or a guard)".

> ট্রেনটা হুইসল দিয়ে স্টেশন ছাড়ল।
>
> *ṭrenṭā huisôl diye sṭeshôn chhāṛlô.*
>
> The train blew its whistle and left the station.

Past (he/she …): সেই ট্রেন হুইসল দিল, *sei ṭren huisôl dilô*.

Used of the engine's whistle and of the guard's whistle on the platform.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হুইসল দেওয়া** (*huisôl deoyā*) joins *whistle*, borrowed from English, to দেওয়া, to give: you give a whistle in Bengali as you give a push.
