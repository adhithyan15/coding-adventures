## You'll want to know: استدل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C366-IDDIA366-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استدل** — *istadalla* — "to infer".

> استدل الطبيب على الزكام من السعال.
>
> *istadalla aṭ-ṭabīb ʿalā az-zukām min as-suʿāl.*
>
> The doctor inferred a cold from the cough.

Present: يستدل, *yastadill*.

It takes على: استدل على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استدل** (*istadalla*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-l-l*, "to guide, to show the way" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek guidance: to infer one thing from another.
