Portuguese, chapter 78: Hat, Coat, Skirt, Socks, Belt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o chapéu — a hat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for trousers, then the Portuguese for shoes.

You'll want to know: o chapéu.
o chapéu — "a hat".
