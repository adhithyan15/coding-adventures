## You'll want to know: از طرف دیگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C367-AMMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**از طرف دیگر** (*az taraf-e digar*) — "on the other hand".

> این خانه بزرگ است. از طرف دیگر، خیلی گران است.
>
> *in khâne bozorg ast. az taraf-e digar, kheyli gerân ast.*
>
> This house is big. On the other hand, it is very expensive.

Often paired with *az yek taraf* "on the one hand". A whole clause follows it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**از طرف دیگر** is literally "from the other side": *az* "from" and *digar* "other" are inherited Persian (EtymDB traces *digar* to PIE *dwóh₁* "two"), and *taraf* was borrowed from Arabic *ṭaraf* "side" (EtymDB).
