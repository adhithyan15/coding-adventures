## You'll want to know: こっせつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C449-BURN449-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こっせつ** — *kossetsu* — "a fracture; a broken bone".

> **ころんで、うでを こっせつしました。** — *koronde, ude o kossetsu shimashita* — "I fell over and broke my arm."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こっせつ** (*kossetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ko*, "bone" (Middle Chinese *kwot*) and *setsu*, "break" (*tsyet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
