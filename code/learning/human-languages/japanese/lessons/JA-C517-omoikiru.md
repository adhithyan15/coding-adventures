## You'll want to know: おもいきる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C517-DECIDE517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいきる** — *omoikiru* — "to give up all thought of".

> **もし ねだんが あがったら、くるまは おもいきります。** — *moshi nedan ga agattara, kuruma wa omoikirimasu* — "If the price goes up, I will give up on the car."

Polite **おもいきります**, *omoikirimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが くるまを おもいきりました。**, *chichi ga kuruma o omoikirimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいきる** (*omoikiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *omou*, "to think", and *kiru*, "to cut"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
