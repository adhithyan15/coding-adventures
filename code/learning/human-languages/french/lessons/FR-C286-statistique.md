## You'll want to know: la statistique
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C286-CHIFFRES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la statistique** — "the statistic; statistics".

> Les statistiques montrent une baisse des accidents.
>
> *The statistics show a fall in accidents.*

Plural in the sense of figures; *la statistique* is also the field of study.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*statistique* was borrowed from German *Statistik* (EtymDB), built on Latin *status* "state", from *stāre* "to stand": first the science of the state.
