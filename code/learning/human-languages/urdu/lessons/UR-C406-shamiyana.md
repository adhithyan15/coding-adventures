## You'll want to know: شامیانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C406-BASERA406-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شامیانہ** — *shāmiyānā* — "a canopy; a large awning".

> میدان میں ایک بڑا شامیانہ تھا۔
>
> *maidān meṅ ek baṛā shāmiyānā thā.*
>
> There was a big canopy on the field.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شامیانہ** (*shāmiyānā*) is usually traced to Persian *shāmiyāna*, "a canopy"; weddings and camps put one up against the sun and the rain.
