# ف (fāʾ) — the letter inside عفوا

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-ALIF-HAMZA-BELOW-01, AR-SCRIPT-C47-ALIF-MAQSURA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write إ and ى once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **عفوا** (*ʿafwan*), "you're welcome".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-FA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ف** — *fāʾ*, the sound *f*.

It is the second letter of **عفوا** (*ʿafwan*), "you're welcome". The letter is a
small closed **head** on a broad bowl, with **one dot above**.

## Writing: ف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-FA-01] -->

- **1.** start here: loop counterclockwise around the small closed head
- **2.** continue left through the broad bowl without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then place the upper dot last — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Introduction to Arabic: Egyptian Arabic for first-year students, Alphabet ف ق, independent Faa at 00:01.7–00:03.3 (University of Oregon Libraries Open Textbook, 2023; accessed 2026-08-23).
