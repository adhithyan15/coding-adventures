## You'll want to know: il pareggio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C511-RISULTATI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il pareggio** — "draw, tie".

> La partita è finita con un pareggio.
>
> *The match ended in a draw.*

Built on *pari* "equal".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pareggio* comes from *pareggiare* "to make even" (EtymDB), built on *pari* "equal", from Latin *pār, paris* (Lewis & Short).
