## You'll want to know: सीळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C228-HEAT228-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सीळो** — *sīḷo* — "cool; pleasantly cold".

> आथण हवा सीळी है।
>
> *āthaṇ havā sīḷī hai.*
>
> In the evening the breeze is cool.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सीळो** (*sīḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śītala*, "cold"; CDIAL lists Hindi *sīlā*, "cool, moist", and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 12487).
