Urdu, chapter 69: Elephant, Monkey, Snake, Bear, Big.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ہاتھی (hāthī) — an elephant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a mouse, then the Urdu for a lion.

You'll want to know: ہاتھی.
ہاتھی — hāthī — "an elephant".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: hāthī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hāthī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say hāthī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for a mouse, then the Urdu for a lion, then say hāthī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ہاتھی (hāthī) mean? ("An elephant".) Say it once more.
