# ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು (sthāpisu) — to found; to establish

## You'll want to know: ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C362-NIRMANA362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು** — *sthāpisu* — "to found; to establish".

> ಆ ರಾಜ ಈ ನಗರವನ್ನು ಐನೂರು ವರ್ಷದ ಹಿಂದೆ ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *ā rāja ī nagaravannu ainūru varṣada hinde sthāpisidanu.*
>
> That king founded this city five hundred years ago.

Past (I …): ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸಿದೆ, *sthāpiside*.

Cities, schools and companies are founded: ಶಾಲೆಯನ್ನು ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಥಾಪಿಸು** (*sthāpisu*) is built on Sanskrit *sthāpana*, "causing to stand, fixing" (CDIAL 13755), from *sthāpayati*, "makes stand", with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
