Persian, chapter 452: Offices and queues.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نوبت (nowbat) — a turn (in a queue); an appointment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a passer-by, then the Persian for someone from the same town.

You'll want to know: نوبت.
نوبت (nowbat) — "a turn (in a queue); an appointment".
در بانک باید منتظر نوبت بمانیم. Dar bânk bâyad montazer-e nowbat bemânim. At the bank we have to wait our turn.
Nowbat-e shomâst — it's your turn.

The word, taken apart.
nowbat was borrowed from Arabic nawba 'a turn, a change of the guard' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
نوبت گرفتن (nowbat gereftan) — to get an appointment; to take a queue number.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for someone from the same town, then the Persian for a turn.

You'll want to know: نوبت گرفتن.
نوبت گرفتن (nowbat gereftan) — "to get an appointment; to take a queue number".
برای دکتر از هفته قبل نوبت گرفتم. Barâ-ye doktor az hafte-ye qabl nowbat gereftam. I made the doctor's appointment a week ago.
nowbat + gereftan 'to take'. Present stem gir-: nowbat migiram.

The word, taken apart.
nowbat gereftan is built from nowbat 'a turn', borrowed from Arabic nawba (EtymDB), and the native verb gereftan 'to take', usually traced to Old Persian grab-.

Lesson 3 of 5.
معطل شدن (mo'attal shodan) — to be kept waiting, to be held up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a turn, then the Persian for to get an appointment.

You'll want to know: معطل شدن.
معطل شدن (mo'attal shodan) — "to be kept waiting, to be held up".
در اداره دو ساعت معطل شدیم. Dar edâre do sâ'at mo'attal shodim. We were kept waiting for two hours at the office.
mo'attal 'idle, waiting' + shodan. Kasi râ mo'attal kardan — to keep someone waiting.

The word, taken apart.
mo'attal shodan is built from mo'attal 'held up, idle', borrowed from Arabic muʿaṭṭal, which EtymDB lists, and the native verb shodan 'to become'. It shares its root with ta'til 'closure'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
فیش (fish) — a payment slip, a bank receipt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get an appointment, then the Persian for to be kept waiting.

You'll want to know: فیش.
فیش (fish) — "a payment slip, a bank receipt".
فیش (fish) بانکی را به منشی دادم. Fish-e bânki râ be monshi dâdam. I gave the bank slip to the secretary.
Offices often ask for a fish to show you have paid.

The word, taken apart.
fish was borrowed from French and is usually traced to French fiche 'a slip of paper, an index card'. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مالیات (mâliyât) — tax.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be kept waiting, then the Persian for a payment slip.

You'll want to know: مالیات.
مالیات (mâliyât) — "tax".
هر سال مالیات خانه را میپردازیم. Har sâl mâliyât-e khâne râ mipardâzim. Every year we pay the tax on the house.
An Arabic-shaped plural built on mâl 'property'.

The word, taken apart.
mâliyât is an Arabic-shaped plural, usually traced to Arabic māliyya, from māl 'property, wealth'. EtymDB does not list it.
