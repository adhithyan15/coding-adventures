## You'll want to know: कागद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C215-WRAP215-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कागद** — *kāgad* — "paper; a letter".

> कागद मेज माथै है।
>
> *kāgad mej māthai hai.*
>
> The paper is on the table.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कागद** (*kāgad*) is usually traced to Persian *kāghaz*, "paper".
