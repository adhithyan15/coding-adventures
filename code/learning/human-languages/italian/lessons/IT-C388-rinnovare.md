## You'll want to know: rinnovare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C388-VALIDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rinnovare** — "to renew".

> Se non rinnovi la carta, non puoi prendere libri in biblioteca.
>
> *If you do not renew the card, you cannot borrow books from the library.*

*Rinnovare la casa* — to do up the house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rinnovare* comes from Latin *renovāre* "to renew, to make new again" (Lewis & Short), built from *re-* and *novus* "new".
