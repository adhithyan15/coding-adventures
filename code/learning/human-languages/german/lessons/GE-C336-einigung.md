## You'll want to know: die Einigung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C336-EINIGUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Einigung** — "the agreement (that is reached)".

> Nach drei Stunden kamen sie endlich zu einer Einigung.
>
> *After three hours they finally reached an agreement.*

Plural: *die Einigungen*. It belongs with *sich einigen* — to come to an agreement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Einigung* is the noun of *sich einigen*, from *einig* "at one, united", built on *ein* "one", inherited from Proto-Germanic *ainaz* (EtymDB): two sides become one.
