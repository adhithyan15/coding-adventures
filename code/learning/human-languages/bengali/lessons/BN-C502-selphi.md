## You'll want to know: সেলফি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C502-KOMPIUTAR502-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সেলফি** — *selphi* — "a selfie".

> মেয়েটা ফোনে একটা সেলফি তুলল।
>
> *Meyeṭā phone ekṭā selphi tullô.*
>
> The girl took a selfie with her phone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সেলফি** (*selphi*) is borrowed from English *selfie*.
