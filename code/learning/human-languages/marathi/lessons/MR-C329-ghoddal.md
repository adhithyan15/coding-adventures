## You'll want to know: घोडदळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C329-SHASTRA329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**घोडदळ** — *ghoḍdaḷ* — "cavalry".

> घोडदळ वेगाने पुढे गेलं.
>
> *ghoḍdaḷ vegāne puḍhe gelaṁ.*
>
> The cavalry went forward at speed.

Neuter.

घोडा, a horse, + दळ, a troop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोडदळ** (*ghoḍdaḷ*) is built from *ghoḍā*, 'a horse', and *daḷ*, 'a troop', from Sanskrit *dala*, 'a part, a troop' (Monier-Williams).
