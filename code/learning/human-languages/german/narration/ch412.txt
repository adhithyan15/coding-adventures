German, chapter 412: Auf dem Bahnsteig.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Bahnsteig — the platform (at a station).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to stamp, then the German for to get a ticket.

You'll want to know: der Bahnsteig.
der Bahnsteig — "the platform (at a station)".
Unser Zug fährt heute von einem anderen Bahnsteig. Our train leaves from a different platform today.
Plural: die Bahnsteige. The track is das Gleis. In Switzerland: der Perron.

The word, taken apart.
Bahnsteig is built from Bahn 'track, railway' and Steig 'steep path', from steigen 'to climb', inherited from Old High German stīgan (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Schaffner — the conductor, the ticket inspector (on a train).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the announcement, then the German for the compartment.

You'll want to know: der Schaffner.
der Schaffner — "the conductor, the ticket inspector (on a train)".
Der Schaffner kommt und will unsere Fahrkarten sehen. The conductor comes and wants to see our tickets.
Same form in the plural. A woman: die Schaffnerin. Today the official word is der Zugbegleiter.

The word, taken apart.
Schaffner is built on schaffen in the old sense 'to arrange, to manage', inherited from Middle High German schaffen (EtymDB); the word is usually said to have first named a steward who ran a household.

Lesson 5 of 5.
kontrollieren — to check, to inspect; to control.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the compartment, then the German for the conductor.

You'll want to know: kontrollieren.
kontrollieren — "to check, to inspect; to control".
Im Zug kontrolliert der Schaffner die Fahrkarten. On the train the conductor checks the tickets.
Past participle without ge-: er hat die Pässe kontrolliert. Die Kontrolle — the check.

The word, taken apart.
kontrollieren was borrowed from French contrôler (EtymDB), built on Old French contrerole 'counter-roll', a second list kept to check the first.
