## You'll want to know: oppugnō, oppugnāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C341-OBSIDIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**oppugnō, oppugnāre** — "to attack, to assault".

> Mīlitēs mūrōs oppidī oppugnābant.
>
> *The soldiers were attacking the walls of the town.*

*oppugnō, oppugnāre, oppugnāvī, oppugnātum*: *ob* + *pugnō* (taught). To attack, not yet to win.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**oppugnō** is built on *pugnō*, "to fight", with *ob-*, "against" (EtymDB). English *oppugn* comes from it.
