## You'll want to know: क्षमा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C352-AHIMSA352-05]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षमा** — *kṣamā* — "forbearance; patience".

> क्रोधस्य औषधं क्षमा अस्ति।
>
> *krodhasya auṣadhaṃ kṣamā asti.*
>
> The medicine for anger is forbearance.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षमा** (*kṣamā*) comes from the root √kṣam, "to be patient, to endure"; MW gives *kṣamā* "patience, forbearance, indulgence" (MW).
