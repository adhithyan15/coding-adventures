## You'll want to know: もる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C174-KITCH174-05]; assesses=[] -->

**もる** — *moru* — "to dish up; to serve (food), to heap".

> **おさらに ごはんを もります。** — *osara ni gohan o morimasu* — "I dish up rice onto a plate."

Polite **もります**, *morimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もる** (*moru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
