## You'll want to know: actualizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C776-CLAVE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**actualizar** — "to update".

> Si no funciona, actualiza la aplicación.
>
> *If it doesn't work, update the app.*

It takes *c* before *e*: *actualicé*. *La actualización* is the update.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*actualizar* is built on *actual* 'current' with the ending *-izar*. *Actual* comes from Latin *āctuālis* 'active, practical' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *agere* 'to do'. In Spanish *actual* means current, not real.
