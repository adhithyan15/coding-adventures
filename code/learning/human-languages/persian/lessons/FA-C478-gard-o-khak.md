## You'll want to know: گرد و خاک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C478-RIZGARD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گرد و خاک** (*gard-o khâk*) — "dust".

> باد گرد و خاک را به خانه آورد.
>
> *Bâd gard-o khâk râ be khâne âvard.*
>
> The wind blew dust into the house.

*gard* 'dust' + *o* 'and' + *khâk* 'earth'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gard-o khâk* is a native pair: *gard* 'dust', which EtymDB lists, and *khâk* 'earth', inherited from Middle Persian *xāk* (EtymDB).
