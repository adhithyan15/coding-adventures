# 字 — the familiar shape stands alone

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-ZI-02] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Say **written character** in Mandarin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-ZI-02, ZH-SCRIPT-ZI-CHAR] -->

> **字** — *zì* — **a written Chinese character**

Hide the pinyin. Read 字 from its familiar roof-over-child shape, then say its
falling tone. You are attaching a standalone word to writing you already know.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZI-CHAR] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **字** for three seconds. Cover it, wait five seconds, then write it once.
Check only two things: roof before child, and six strokes.
