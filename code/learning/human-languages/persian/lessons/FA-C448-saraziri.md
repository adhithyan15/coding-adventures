## You'll want to know: سرازیری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C448-MIYANBOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سرازیری** (*sarâziri*) — "a downhill slope, a descent".

> در سرازیری ماشین تند میرود.
>
> *Dar sarâziri mâshin tond miravad.*
>
> Going downhill the car goes fast.

The opposite of *sarbâlâyi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarâziri* is usually analysed as native *sar* 'head' and a form of *zir* 'under, down', with the ending *-i*: head-downwards. EtymDB does not list the compound.
