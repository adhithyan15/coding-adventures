## You'll want to know: かんがえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C227-THINK227-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かんがえる** — *kangaeru* — "to think over; to consider".

> **ちちは いつも ゆっくり かんがえます。** — *chichi wa itsumo yukkuri kangaemasu* — "My father always thinks things over slowly."

Polite **かんがえます**, *kangaemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんがえる** (*kangaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
