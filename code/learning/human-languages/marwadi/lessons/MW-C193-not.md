## You'll want to know: नोट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C193-MONEY193-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नोट** — *noṭ* — "a banknote".

> म्हारो नोट नुवो है।
>
> *mhāro noṭ nuvo hai.*
>
> My banknote is new.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नोट** (*noṭ*) is borrowed from English *note*, as in a bank note.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
