## You'll want to know: غم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C443-SAD443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غم** — *gham* — "grief".

> اس کے دل میں بڑا غم ہے۔
>
> *us ke dil meṅ baṛā gham hai.*
>
> There is great grief in his heart.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غم** (*gham*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghamm*, "grief, distress".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
