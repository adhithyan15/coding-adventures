# কলকল করা (kôlkôl kôrā) — to babble; to murmur (of running water)

## You'll want to know: কলকল করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C433-JHIL433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**কলকল করা** — *kôlkôl kôrā* — "to babble; to murmur (of running water)".

> ঝরনার জল কলকল করছিল।
>
> *jhôrnār jôl kôlkôl kôrchhilô.*
>
> The water of the waterfall was babbling.

Past (he/she …): সেই জল কলকল করল, *sei jôl kôlkôl kôrlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কলকল করা** (*kôlkôl kôrā*) joins কলকল, an imitative word for the sound of running water, to করা, to do. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
