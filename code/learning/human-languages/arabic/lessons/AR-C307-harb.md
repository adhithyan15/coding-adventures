## You'll want to know: حرب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C307-JAYSH307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حرب** — *ḥarb* — "a war".

> كان جدي صغيرا في زمن الحرب.
>
> *kāna jaddī ṣaghīran fī zaman al-ḥarb.*
>
> My grandfather was a small boy in wartime.

Plural: حروب, *ḥurūb*.

حرب is feminine: حرب طويلة, a long war.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حرب** (*ḥarb*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-r-b* (Lane), where *ḥaraba* is "to strip a man of his goods". War, which strips people of their goods, is usually explained from it.
