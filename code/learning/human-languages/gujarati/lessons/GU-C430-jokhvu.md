## You'll want to know: જોખવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C430-SCALES430-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જોખવું** — *jokhvũ* — "to weigh out (goods)".

> દુકાનદારે બે કિલો ચોખા જોખ્યા.
>
> *dukāndāre be kilo cokhā jokhyā.*
>
> The shopkeeper weighed out two kilos of rice.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ખાંડ જોખી, *teṇe khā̃ḍ jokhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જોખવું** (*jokhvũ*) goes back to *\*yōkṣati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to examine, to weigh" (CDIAL 10525).
