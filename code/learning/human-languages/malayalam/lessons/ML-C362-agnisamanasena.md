# അഗ്നിശമനസേന (agniśamanasēna) — the fire brigade

## You'll want to know: അഗ്നിശമനസേന
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C362-TEE362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**അഗ്നിശമനസേന** — *agniśamanasēna* — "the fire brigade".

> അഗ്നിശമനസേന അര മണിക്കൂറിനുള്ളിൽ എത്തി.
>
> *agniśamanasēna ara maṇikkūṟinuḷḷil etti.*
>
> The fire brigade arrived within half an hour.

Three Sanskrit words in a row: fire, quenching, force.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അഗ്നിശമനസേന** (*agniśamanasēna*) is built from three Sanskrit words: *agni*, "fire" (CDIAL 55), *śamana*, "calming, extinguishing", and *senā*, "an army" (Monier-Williams). It is the force that puts fires out.
