Italian, chapter 319: Il carnevale.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il carnevale — the carnival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the refreshments, then the Italian for the sugared almond.

You'll want to know: il carnevale.
il carnevale — "the carnival".
Il carnevale finisce il martedì grasso. Carnival ends on Shrove Tuesday.
A carnevale ogni scherzo vale — at carnival any prank goes.

The word, taken apart.
carnevale is inherited from Medieval Latin carnelevāle (EtymDB), from carō "meat" and levāre "to take away": the farewell to meat before Lent.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Italian for the carnival, then the Italian for the mask.

You'll want to know: i coriandoli.
i coriandoli — "the confetti".
La strada è piena di coriandoli. The street is full of confetti.
Singular il coriandolo, which is also the coriander plant.

The word, taken apart.
coriandoli are named after coriandolo "coriander", from Latin coriandrum (Lewis & Short): sugared seeds were once thrown at carnival.
