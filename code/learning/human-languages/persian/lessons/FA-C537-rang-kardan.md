## You'll want to know: رنگ کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C537-CHANGES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رنگ کردن** (*rang kardan*) — "to decorate with paint (a wall or room)".

> ما دیوار اتاق را آبی رنگ کردیم.
>
> *mâ divâr-e otâq râ âbi rang kardim.*
>
> We painted the wall of the room blue.

*rang* 'colour, paint' + *kardan*. For pictures, *naqqâshi kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rang kardan* is built on two inherited words: *rang*, 'colour', which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian word for 'colour, dye' (EtymDB), and *kardan*, 'to do'.
