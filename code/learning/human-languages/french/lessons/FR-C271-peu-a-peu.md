## You'll want to know: peu à peu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C271-PEU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**peu à peu** — "little by little".

> Peu à peu, la ville s'est réveillée.
>
> *Little by little, the town woke up.*

The same as *petit à petit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*peu* is inherited from Latin *paucum* "a little", the neuter of *paucus* "few" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
