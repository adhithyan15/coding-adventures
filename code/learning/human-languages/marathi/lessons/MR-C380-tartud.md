## You'll want to know: तरतूद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C380-ANIVARYA380-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तरतूद** — *tartūd* — "a provision (in a law or budget)".

> अर्थसंकल्पात शिक्षणासाठी मोठी तरतूद आहे.
>
> *arthasaṅkalpāt śikṣaṇāsāṭhī moṭhī tartūd āhe.*
>
> The budget has a large provision for education.

Feminine.

X ची तरतूद करणे, to make provision for X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तरतूद** (*tartūd*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
