# のりおくれる (noriokureru) — to miss (a train or bus)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C257-PROBLEM-01, JA-LEX-C198-RIDE198-01, JA-LEX-C152-READY152-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say "to ride" and "to be late". (*noru*, *okureru*.)

## You'll want to know: のりおくれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C257-NORIOKURERU-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C134-DENSHA, JA-LEX-C75-TIME75-05, JA-GRAMMAR-C255-SO-02] -->

**のりおくれる** — *noriokureru* — "to miss" a train or a bus: to be too late to get on. Polite **のりおくれます**, *noriokuremasu*; an ichidan verb, like **おくれる**. Past (polite): **でんしゃに のりおくれました**, *densha ni noriokuremashita*.

> **でんしゃに のりおくれました。**
>
> *densha ni noriokuremashita.* — "I missed the train."

> **あさねぼうを しました。**
>
> *asanebō o shimashita.* — "I overslept."

> **それで、でんしゃに のりおくれました。**
>
> *sore de, densha ni noriokuremashita.* — "And so I missed the train."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のりおくれる** (*noriokureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: the stem of **のる** (*noru*), "to ride", and **おくれる** (*okureru*), "to be late". JMdict glosses it "to miss (a train, bus, etc.)" (JMdict).
