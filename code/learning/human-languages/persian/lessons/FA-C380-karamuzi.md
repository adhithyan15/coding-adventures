## You'll want to know: کارآموزی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C380-REZUME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کارآموزی** (*kârâmuzi*) — "an internship, training".

> او تابستان دوره کارآموزی را در یک بیمارستان گذراند.
>
> *u tâbestân dowre-ye kârâmuzi râ dar yek bimârestân gozarând.*
>
> In the summer he did his internship in a hospital.

*Kârâmuz* + the noun suffix *-i*. *Dowre-ye kârâmuzi* — a training period.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کارآموزی** is native Persian, built from *kârâmuz* "trainee", from *kâr* "work" and *âmuz-* "learn", with the noun suffix *-i*.
