## You'll want to know: пешка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C504-BOARDGAMES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**пешка** (*péshka*) — "a pawn".

> Пешка — самая слабая фигура в шахматах.
>
> *Péshka — sámaya slábaya figúra v shákhmatakh.*
>
> The pawn is the weakest piece in chess.

Feminine. Figuratively, a person of no importance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пешка* is built on *пеший* 'on foot', so a pawn is a foot soldier; EtymDB lists *пешка* without a source.
