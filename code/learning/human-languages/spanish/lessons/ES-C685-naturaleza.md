## You'll want to know: la naturaleza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C685-PAISAJE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la naturaleza** — "nature".

> Me gusta pasear por la naturaleza.
>
> *I like walking in nature.*

*Por naturaleza* — by nature.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naturaleza* is built on *natural* with *-eza* (EtymDB); *natural* comes from Latin *nātūrālis*, built on *nātus* "born".
