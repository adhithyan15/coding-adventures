Punjabi, chapter 278: Packets and Accounts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਹਿਸਾਬ (hisāb) — a reckoning; an account of money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a thing, then the Punjabi for goods.

You'll want to know: ਹਿਸਾਬ.
ਹਿਸਾਬ — hisāb — "a reckoning; an account of money".
ਦੁਕਾਨ ਦਾ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਸਹੀ ਹੈ। dukān dā hisāb sahī hai. The shop's accounts are correct.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਹਿਸਾਬ (hisāb) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic hisāb, "counting, reckoning".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਲੱਦਣਾ (laddṇā) — to load.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for goods, then the Punjabi for a reckoning.

You'll want to know: ਲੱਦਣਾ.
ਲੱਦਣਾ — laddṇā — "to load".
ਮੁੰਡਾ ਸਾਮਾਨ ਬੱਸ ਉੱਤੇ ਲੱਦਦਾ ਹੈ। muṇḍā sāmān bass utte ladddā hai. The boy loads the luggage onto the bus.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਾਮਾਨ ਲੱਦਿਆ, maiṁ sāmān laddiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੱਦਣਾ (laddṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit lardayati, "loads"; CDIAL lists Punjabi laddṇā, "to load", under it (CDIAL 10966).

ਵਿਕਣਾ — vikṇā — "to be sold; to sell".
ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਸਤੇ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਵਿਕਦੇ ਹਨ। bāzār vich saste kappṛe vikde han. Cheap clothes sell in the market.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਘਰ ਵਿਕਿਆ, oh purāṇā ghar vikiā.

ਵਿਕਣਾ (vikṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit vikrīyate, "is sold"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vikṇā, "to be sold", under it (CDIAL 11642).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਜੋੜਨਾ (joṛnā) — to add up; to join.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to be sold, then the Punjabi for a packet.

You'll want to know: ਜੋੜਨਾ.
ਜੋੜਨਾ — joṛnā — "to add up; to join".
ਮੈਂ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਉੱਤੇ ਸਭ ਪੈਸੇ ਜੋੜਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ kāghaz utte sabh paise joṛdā hāṁ. I add up all the money on paper.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਜੋੜਿਆ, maiṁ hisāb joṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਜੋੜਨਾ (joṛnā) is inherited from a reconstructed yuṭati, "is joined"; CDIAL lists Punjabi joṛṇā, "to join", under it (CDIAL 10496).
