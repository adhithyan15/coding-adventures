## You'll want to know: 他们
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C31-PEOPLE31-03]; assesses=[] -->

**他们** — *tāmen* — "they; them".

> **他们是中国人** *tāmen shì Zhōngguó rén* — They're Chinese.

The new character, in pieces: 们 — the person piece on the left again, and on the right a three-stroke piece read mén, the word for door, which gives the sound. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**他们** (*tāmen*) is built from 他 'he' and the plural ending men, which goes on words for people. No dictionary we use records an older form of the ending, so its history is not settled.
