# ఉద్దేశించు (uddēśiñcu) — to intend; to mean for

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C422-HAMI422-01, TE-LEX-C422-HAMI422-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a guarantee, then the Telugu for to fulfil.

## You'll want to know: ఉద్దేశించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C422-HAMI422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉద్దేశించు** — *uddēśiñcu* — "to intend; to mean for".

> నేను మిమ్మల్ని బాధపెట్టాలని ఉద్దేశించలేదు.
>
> *nēnu mimmalni bādhapeṭṭālani uddēśiñcalēdu.*
>
> I did not mean to hurt you.

Past (I …): ఉద్దేశించాను, *uddēśiñcānu*.

The verb of ఉద్దేశం, an intention, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉద్దేశించు** (*uddēśiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *uddeśa*, "the act of pointing to; direction" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; ఉద్దేశం is the same word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
