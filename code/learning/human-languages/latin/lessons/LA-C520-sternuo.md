## You'll want to know: sternuō, sternuere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C520-SPIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sternuō, sternuere** — "to sneeze".

> Puer propter pulverem sternuit.
>
> *The boy sneezes because of the dust.*

*sternuō, sternuere, sternuī*. To Roman eyes a sneeze could be an omen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sternuō** is a native Latin verb; Lewis & Short compares Greek *ptarnusthai* "to sneeze", but EtymDB gives no parent, so the origin is not settled.
