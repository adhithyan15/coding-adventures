# ਖੜਕਾਉਣਾ (khaṛkāuṇā) — to knock on a door

## You'll want to know: ਖੜਕਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C312-VISIT312-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੜਕਾਉਣਾ** — *khaṛkāuṇā* — "to knock on a door".

> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਦਾ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ਾ ਖੜਕਾਇਆ।
>
> *shām nū̃ maiṁ Aman dā darvāzā khaṛkāiā.*
>
> In the evening I knocked on Aman's door.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਖਿੜਕੀ ਖੜਕਾਈ, *maiṁ khiṛkī khaṛkāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੜਕਾਉਣਾ** (*khaṛkāuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *khaṭakhaṭāyate*, "crackles", an imitative word; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khaṛkāuṇā*, "to make rattle, thump", under it (CDIAL 3771).
