## You'll want to know: осматривать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C442-EKSKURSIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**осматривать** (*osmátrivat*) — "to look round (a place), to go sightseeing; to examine".

> Весь день туристы осматривали старый город.
>
> *Ves den turísty osmátrivali stáryy górod.*
>
> All day the tourists looked round the old town.

Imperfective; perfective *осмотреть* (*осмотрю, осмотришь*). Also of a doctor: to examine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*осматривать* is built from *о-* 'round' and *-сматривать*, from *смотреть* 'to look', which EtymDB relates to Proto-Slavic *motriti* 'to watch'.
