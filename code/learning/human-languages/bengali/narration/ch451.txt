Bengali, chapter 451: Disgust and Envy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘিনঘিনে (ghinghine) — disgusting; revolting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to taunt, then the Bengali for to belittle.

You'll want to know: ঘিনঘিনে.
ঘিনঘিনে — ghinghine — "disgusting; revolting".
আমার কাছে মাকড়সা ঘিনঘিনে লাগে। āmār kāchhe mākôṛsā ghinghine lāge. I find spiders revolting.

The word, taken apart.
ঘিনঘিনে (ghinghine) doubles ghin, "loathing", which CDIAL lists for Bengali under Sanskrit ghṛṇā (CDIAL 4500), and adds the adjective ending -e.

Lesson 2 of 5.
বিতৃষ্ণা (bitrishnā) — aversion; distaste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to belittle, then the Bengali for revolting.

You'll want to know: বিতৃষ্ণা.
বিতৃষ্ণা — bitrishnā — "aversion; distaste".
রোজ একই খাবার খেয়ে অরুণের বিতৃষ্ণা এল। roj eki khābār kheye ôruner bitrishnā elô. Eating the same food every day, Arun grew sick of it.

The word, taken apart.
বিতৃষ্ণা (bitrishnā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vitṛṣṇā, which Monier-Williams gives as freedom from desire, vi- "without" on tṛṣṇā "thirst"; Bengali has pushed it on to distaste, a sense the dictionary does not give.

Lesson 3 of 5.
পরশ্রীকাতর (pôrôshrīkātôr) — envious of others' good fortune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for revolting, then the Bengali for aversion.

You'll want to know: পরশ্রীকাতর.
পরশ্রীকাতর — pôrôshrīkātôr — "envious of others' good fortune".
প্রতিবেশীর নতুন গাড়ি দেখে লোকটা পরশ্রীকাতর হল। prôtibeshīr nôtun gāṛi dekhe lokṭā pôrôshrīkātôr hôlô. The man grew envious when he saw his neighbour's new car.

The word, taken apart.
পরশ্রীকাতর (pôrôshrīkātôr) puts together Sanskrit para, "another", śrī, "good fortune", and kātara, "despairing, agitated" (Monier-Williams): pained by someone else's good luck.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ঘৃণা করা (ghrinā kôrā) — to hate; to despise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for aversion, then the Bengali for envious of others' good fortune.

You'll want to know: ঘৃণা করা.
ঘৃণা করা — ghrinā kôrā — "to hate; to despise".
মীরা মিথ্যেকে ঘৃণা করে। mīrā mittheke ghrinā kôre. Mira hates lies.
Past (he/she …): তিনি যুদ্ধকে ঘৃণা করলেন, tini juddhôke ghrinā kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
ঘৃণা করা (ghrinā kôrā) is Sanskrit ghṛṇā, which Monier-Williams gives for warm feeling and pity and also for contempt and disgust; Bengali keeps the loathing, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

সবজি দেখে ছেলেটা নাক সিঁটকাল। sôbji dekhe chheleṭā nāk sĩṭkālô. The boy turned up his nose at the vegetables.

The word, taken apart.
নাক সিঁটকানো (nāk sĩṭkāno) is নাক (taught), the nose (CDIAL 6909), with sĩṭkāno, "to wrinkle up", a verb whose origin is not settled.
