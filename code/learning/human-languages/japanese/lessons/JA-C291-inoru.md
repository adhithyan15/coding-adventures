## You'll want to know: いのる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C291-SOUSHIKI291-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いのる** — *inoru* — "to pray".

> **じんじゃで かぞくの けんこうを いのりました。** — *jinja de kazoku no kenkō o inorimashita* — "At the shrine I prayed for my family's health."

Polite **いのります**, *inorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが いのりました。**, *haha ga inorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いのる** (*inoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
