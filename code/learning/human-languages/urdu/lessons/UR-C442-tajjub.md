## You'll want to know: تعجب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C442-SURPRISE442-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تعجب** — *tājjub* — "astonishment".

> اس خبر پر سب کو تعجب ہوا۔
>
> *is khabar par sab ko tājjub huā.*
>
> Everyone was astonished at this news.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعجب** (*tājjub*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taʿajjub*, "wondering at something".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
