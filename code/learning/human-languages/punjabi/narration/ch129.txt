Lesson 1 of 5.
ਹਨੇਰਾ (hanerā) — darkness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for the earth, then the Punjabi for moonlight.

You'll want to know: ਹਨੇਰਾ.
ਹਨੇਰਾ — hanerā — "darkness".

The word, taken apart.
ਹਨੇਰਾ (hanerā) is inherited. CDIAL lists the Punjabi forms anherā and nherā, "dark; darkness", under andhīkāra, a form beside Sanskrit andhakāra, "darkness" (CDIAL 386).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਚਾਨਣ (cānaṇ) — light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for moonlight, then the Punjabi for darkness.

You'll want to know: ਚਾਨਣ.
ਚਾਨਣ — cānaṇ — "light".

The word, taken apart.
ਚਾਨਣ (cānaṇ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cāndraṇa, "moonlight", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4745). CDIAL lists Punjabi cānaṇ, "light".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਸਤਰੰਗੀ ਪੀਂਘ (satrangī pīṅgh) — a rainbow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for light, then the Punjabi for shade.

You'll want to know: ਸਤਰੰਗੀ ਪੀਂਘ.
ਸਤਰੰਗੀ ਪੀਂਘ — satrangī pīṅgh — "a rainbow".

The word, taken apart.
ਸਤਰੰਗੀ ਪੀਂਘ (satrangī pīṅgh) is "the seven-coloured swing". Sat, "seven", comes from Sanskrit sapta (CDIAL 13139); raṅgī is built on raṅg, "colour"; and pīṁgh, "swing", comes from Sanskrit preṅkha, "swing" (CDIAL 8996).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਖ਼ਰਗੋਸ਼.
ਖ਼ਰਗੋਸ਼ — khargosh — "a rabbit".

The word, taken apart.
ਖ਼ਰਗੋਸ਼ (khargosh) is a loan, usually traced to Persian khargōsh, "hare". It is built from khar, "donkey", and gōsh, "ear".
