## You'll want to know: रुकावट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C541-RUKAVAT541-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रुकावट** — *rukāvaṭ* — "an obstacle, a hold-up".

> सड़क पर एक पेड़ गिरा था, इसलिए रास्ते में रुकावट थी।
>
> *Saṛak par ek peṛ girā thā, isliye rāste meṁ rukāvaṭ thī.*
>
> A tree had fallen on the road, so the way was blocked.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुकावट** (*rukāvaṭ*) is the noun of *ruknā*, "to stop", which CDIAL lists beside *roknā* under a reconstructed *\*rokk* (CDIAL 10827), with the ending -āvaṭ that makes nouns of verbs.
