## You'll want to know: पवित्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C331-NADITIRA331-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पवित्रः** — *pavitraḥ* — "holy; pure".

> सा नदी पवित्रा अस्ति।
>
> *sā nadī pavitrā asti.*
>
> That river is holy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पवित्रः** (*pavitraḥ*) MW gives *pavitra* first as a means of purification, a filter, and as an adjective purifying, pure, holy, sacred, without a root in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
