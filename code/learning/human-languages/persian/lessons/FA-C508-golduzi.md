## You'll want to know: گلدوزی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C508-CRAFTS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گلدوزی** (*golduzi*) — "embroidery".

> مادربزرگ روی دستمال گلدوزی میکند.
>
> *mâdarbozorg ru-ye dastmâl golduzi mikonad.*
>
> Grandmother embroiders handkerchiefs.

*gol* 'flower' + *duz* 'sewing' (stem of *dukhtan*) + *-i*: flower-sewing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*golduzi* is built on native Persian parts: *gol*, 'flower', *duz*, the present stem of *dukhtan*, 'to sew', and *-i*. EtymDB has no entry for the compound.
