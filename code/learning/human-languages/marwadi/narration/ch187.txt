Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 187: Rooms and Corners.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कोठो (koṭho) — a storeroom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a plank seat, then the Marwadi for a bedstead.

You'll want to know: कोठो.
कोठो — koṭho — "a storeroom".
म्हारो कोठो छोटो है। mhāro koṭho chhoṭo hai. My storeroom is small.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कोठो (koṭho) is inherited from Sanskrit koṣṭha, "a granary, a storeroom"; CDIAL lists Marwari koṭho, "a room" (CDIAL 3546).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

रसोई मांय एक छोटी बारी है। rasoī mā̃y ek chhoṭī bārī hai. There's a small window in the kitchen.

खूणो (khūṇo) comes from a reconstructed khuṇṭa, "a corner"; CDIAL lists Gujarati khūṇo and Hindi khū̃ṭ (CDIAL 3898). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
