## You'll want to know: ちゅうい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C142-CHUUI]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうい** — *chūi* — "caution", "take care". It heads warning signs and
notices. The small **ゅ** joins **ち** into one beat, *chu*, and **う**
holds it long: *chū*, then *i*.
On a real sign this word is usually printed in kanji, which this book has
not reached yet; the reading is the same.

> **くるまに ちゅうい** — *kuruma ni chūi* — "Watch out for cars."

Read a notice like this and act on it: stop, look, and cross with care.
