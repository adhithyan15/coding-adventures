## You'll want to know: বেতন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C495-BETON495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বেতন** — *betôn* — "pay; wages".

> মাসের শেষে আমরা বেতন পাই।
>
> *Māser sheshe āmrā betôn pāi.*
>
> We get our pay at the end of the month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেতন** (*betôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vetana*, "wages, hire" (Monier-Williams).
