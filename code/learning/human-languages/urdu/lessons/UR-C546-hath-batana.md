# ہاتھ بٹانا (hāth baṭānā) — to lend a hand

## You'll want to know: ہاتھ بٹانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C546-MASHAQQAT546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہاتھ بٹانا** — *hāth baṭānā* — "to lend a hand".

> اگر سب نے ہاتھ بٹایا تو کام ختم ہو گا۔
>
> *agar sab ne hāth baṭāyā to kām khatm ho gā.*
>
> If everyone lends a hand, the work will get finished.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے امی کا ہاتھ بٹایا, *us ne ammī kā hāth baṭāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہاتھ بٹانا** (*hāth baṭānā*) is built from **ہاتھ** (*hāth*), "a hand", and *baṭānā*, "to share in", which sits beside the Hindi cousin *bā̃ṭnā*, "shares", that CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *váṇṭati* (CDIAL 11238): to take a share of someone's work.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
