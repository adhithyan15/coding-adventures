Sanskrit, chapter 406: Getting On and Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अधिरोहति (adhirohati) — gets on; mounts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for last, then the Sanskrit for arrives.

You'll want to know: अधिरोहति.
अधिरोहति — adhirohati — "gets on; mounts".
यात्री लोकयानम् अधिरोहति। yātrī lokayānam adhirohati. The passenger gets on the bus.
Past (he …): सः लोकयानम् अधिरूढवान्, saḥ lokayānam adhirūḍhavān.

The word, taken apart.
अधिरोहति (adhirohati) is built from adhi, "over, onto", and the root √ruh, "to rise", which also gives rohati, "grows"; MW gives adhi-ruh as to rise above, ascend or mount, and getting on a bus or a train is that sense in modern use (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: अवतरति.
अवतरति — avatarati — "gets off; alights".
यात्री स्थानके अवतरति। yātrī sthānake avatarati. The passenger gets off at the station.
Past (he …): सः स्थानके अवतीर्णवान्, saḥ sthānake avatīrṇavān.

The word, taken apart.
अवतरति (avatarati) is built from ava, "down", and the root √tṝ, "to cross"; MW gives ava-tṝ as to descend or alight, with the participle avatīrṇa, "alighted" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सम्मर्दः (sammardaḥ) — a crush of people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gets on, then the Sanskrit for gets off.

You'll want to know: सम्मर्दः.
सम्मर्दः — sammardaḥ — "a crush of people".
प्रातः लोकयाने महान् सम्मर्दः भवति। prātaḥ lokayāne mahān sammardaḥ bhavati. In the morning there is a great crush in the bus.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सम्मर्दः (sammardaḥ) is built from sam, "together", and the root √mṛd, "to press, to crush"; MW gives sam-marda as pressing together and trampling, and jana-sammarda as a throng of people; the crush in a bus is that sense in modern use (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पंक्तिः (paṅktiḥ) — a queue; a line of people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gets off, then the Sanskrit for a crush of people.

You'll want to know: पंक्तिः.
पंक्तिः — paṅktiḥ — "a queue; a line of people".
जनाः यानपत्राय पंक्तौ तिष्ठन्ति। janāḥ yānapatrāya paṅktau tiṣṭhanti. People stand in a queue for tickets.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पंक्तिः (paṅktiḥ) is built on pañcan, "five": MW gives paṅkti first as a set of five, then as a row, a line, a series; a queue is the row in modern use (MW).

लोकयाने अवकाशः न अस्ति। lokayāne avakāśaḥ na asti. There is no room in the bus.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अवकाशः (avakāśaḥ) is built from ava and the root √kāś, "to appear, to shine"; MW gives avakāśa as place, space, room, and also an occasion or opportunity (MW).
