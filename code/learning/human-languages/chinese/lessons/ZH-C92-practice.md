## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-ORDER-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C92-YE-01, ZH-LEX-C92-CAIDAN-01, ZH-LEX-C92-FUWUYUAN-01, ZH-LEX-C92-BIEDE-01, ZH-LEX-C89-GEI-01, ZH-SCRIPT-YAO-01, ZH-LEX-C70-FOOD70-04] -->

> **A:** *fúwùyuán, qǐng gěi wǒmen càidān* — Excuse me, could we have the menu?
>
> **B:** 好 你们喝什么
>
> *hǎo, nǐmen hē shénme* — Of course. What will you drink?
>
> **A:** 我要水
>
> *wǒ yào shuǐ* — Water for me.
>
> **C:** 我也要
>
> *wǒ yě yào* — Me too.
>
> **B:** *nǐmen yào biéde ma* — Anything else?
>
> **A:** *wǒ yào jiǎozi* — Dumplings for me.
>
> **C:** *wǒ yě yào jiǎozi* — Dumplings for me too.
>
> **B:** 好
>
> *hǎo* — Right you are.

Ordering is *yào* and the thing; agreeing is 也 in front of it. Nobody says *I* would like: 我要 does the job.
