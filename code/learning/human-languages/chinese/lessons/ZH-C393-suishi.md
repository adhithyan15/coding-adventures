## You'll want to know: suíshí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C393-BESURE393-04]; assesses=[] -->

*suíshí* — "at any time; whenever needed".

> *yàoshi yǒushì nǐ suíshí lái zhǎo wǒ* — If anything comes up, come and find me any time.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suíshí* is built from *suí* 'to follow' and 时 'the time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zjwe* and *dzyi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go along with': whatever the time.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
