## You'll want to know: ٹھکرانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C358-JALSA358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھکرانا** — *ṭhukrānā* — "to spurn; to turn down flatly".

> جلسے نے اس کی تجویز ٹھکرا دی۔
>
> *jalse ne us kī tajvīz ṭhukrā dī.*
>
> The meeting flatly turned down his proposal.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مشورہ ٹھکرایا, *us ne mashvarā ṭhukrāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھکرانا** (*ṭhukrānā*) is built on *ṭhokar*, "a kick, a blow", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭhokar* under *\*ṭhōkk*, "knock", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5513). To push a thing away with the foot.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
