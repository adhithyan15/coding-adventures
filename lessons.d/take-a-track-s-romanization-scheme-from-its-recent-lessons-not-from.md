# Take a track's romanization scheme from its recent lessons, not from its earliest ones

The Persian B1 briefs told five drafters to romanize ق as *gh*, because
the track's first chapters do (*ghahve*, *otâgh*). From chapter 100 on,
though, the track writes *q*: 63 ق words against 28, and chapters 150
onward are all *q*. One drafter flagged the mismatch after finishing a
300-word tranche. Every drafter then had to convert word by word from the
Persian spelling, because a blind replacement of "gh" would also have
changed the غ words, and the slugs of about 60 lessons changed.

What to do differently: before writing a brief for new lessons, measure
the convention on the track's latest chapters. For example, count how
headwords containing a given letter are romanized, bucketed by chapter.
Where the track changed its mind partway, follow the later choice, and
put the rule in both the drafting brief and the review brief.
