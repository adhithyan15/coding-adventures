Sanskrit, chapter 295: Help for the Injured.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चेतना (cetanā) — consciousness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for injured, then the Sanskrit for faints.

You'll want to know: चेतना.
चेतना — cetanā — "consciousness".
आहतस्य चेतना शनैः आगच्छति। āhatasya cetanā śanaiḥ āgacchati. The injured man slowly comes round.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चेतना (cetanā) comes from the root √cit, "to perceive, attend to"; MW gives cetanā as consciousness and understanding (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उत्थापयति (utthāpayati) — lifts up; raises.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for faints, then the Sanskrit for consciousness.

You'll want to know: उत्थापयति.
उत्थापयति — utthāpayati — "lifts up; raises".
जनाः आहतम् उत्थापयन्ति। janāḥ āhatam utthāpayanti. People lift up the injured man.
Past (he …): सः आहतम् उत्थापितवान्, saḥ āhatam utthāpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उत्थापयति (utthāpayati) is built from ud, "up", and the causative of the root √sthā, "to stand": to make stand up, to raise (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: शिबिका.
शिबिका — śibikā — "a litter; a stretcher".
ते आहतं शिबिकया नयन्ति। te āhataṃ śibikayā nayanti. They carry the injured man on a litter.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
शिबिका (śibikā) MW gives śibikā as a palanquin, a litter or a bier, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain; a stretcher is its modern use (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अवलम्बते (avalambate) — leans on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a litter, then the Sanskrit for an emergency.

You'll want to know: अवलम्बते.
अवलम्बते — avalambate — "leans on".
आहतः मित्रस्य स्कन्धम् अवलम्बते। āhataḥ mitrasya skandham avalambate. The injured man leans on his friend's shoulder.
Past (he …): सः मित्रम् अवलम्बितवान्, saḥ mitram avalambitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अवलम्बते (avalambate) is built from ava, "down", and the root √lamb, "to hang"; MW gives it as to hang down and to cling to or rest upon as a support (MW).
