## You'll want to know: दाटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C404-VALSA404-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दाटणे** — *dāṭṇe* — "to crowd thickly".

> संध्याकाळी रस्त्यावर गर्दी दाटली.
>
> *sandhyākāḷī rastyāvar gardī dāṭlī.*
>
> In the evening the crowd thickened on the road.

Past (he …): तो दाटला, *to dāṭlā*.

Clouds, crowds and feelings all दाटतात: ढग दाटले, the clouds gathered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाटणे** (*dāṭṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *dāṭṇẽ*, "to be pressed in, become thick", under *\*draṭ* (CDIAL 6618).
