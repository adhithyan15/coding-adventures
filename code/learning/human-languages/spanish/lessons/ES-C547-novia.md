**la novia** — "the girlfriend; the bride".

> La novia llevaba un vestido azul.
>
> *The bride wore a blue dress.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*novia* is the feminine of *novio*, inherited from spoken Latin *\*novius*, *\*novia* 'newly married', built on *novus* 'new' (EtymDB).
