# ಗರಿಷ್ಠ (gariṣṭha) — maximum

## You'll want to know: ಗರಿಷ್ಠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C414-GARISHTHA414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗರಿಷ್ಠ** — *gariṣṭha* — "maximum".

> ಈ ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಗರಿಷ್ಠ ವೇಗ ನಲವತ್ತು.
>
> *ī rasteyalli gariṣṭha vēga nalavattu.*
>
> The maximum speed on this road is forty.

ಗರಿಷ್ಠ ಅಂಕ, the top mark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗರಿಷ್ಠ** (*gariṣṭha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gariṣṭha*, "heaviest", the superlative of *guru*, "heavy" (Monier-Williams).
