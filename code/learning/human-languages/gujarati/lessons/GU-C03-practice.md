# Chapter 3 — The how-are-you exchange

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. The whole responding cycle, from atoms you now own.

## The exchange

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: "How are you?" (the everyday hello) — *tame kem chho?*]
- [YOU SAY: "I'm well, thank you." — *hũ majāmā chhũ, ābhār.*]
- [YOU SAY: "No problem / you're welcome." — *vāndho nahĩ.*]
- [YOU SAY: the familiar question — *tũ kem chhe?*]

## The atoms

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: the k- question-word for "how" (*kem*)]
- [YOU SAY: "I" and its copula (*hũ*, *chhũ*)]
- [YOU SAY: which of your words is Persian, not Sanskrit (*majā*)]

## Roots you now carry

[PAUSE 2s]
- *kem* ← the interrogative *k-* (PIE *\*kʷo-*), cousin of English *how/what*
- *hũ* ← Sanskrit *aham* → Latin *ego*, English **I**
- *majā* ← **Persian** *maza*, "taste/relish" — the trade-language layer
- *nahĩ* ← PIE *\*ne*, English *no* (in *vāndho nahĩ*)

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Someone greets you with *tame kem chho?* — reply that you're well and
thank them. (*Hũ majāmā chhũ, ābhār* — and to their thanks, *vāndho nahĩ*.)
