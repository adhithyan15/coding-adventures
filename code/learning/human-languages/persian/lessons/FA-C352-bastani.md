## You'll want to know: باستانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C352-BASTAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**باستانی** (*bâstâni*) — "ancient".

> در موزه سکه های باستانی دیدیم.
>
> *Dar muze sekke-hâ-ye bâstâni didim.*
>
> We saw ancient coins in the museum.

*Varzesh-e bâstâni* is the traditional strength training of the *zurkhâne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâstâni* is built from Persian *bâstân* "antiquity", a native word, with the adjective suffix *-i*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
