Arabic, chapter 424: Takeoff and landing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أقلع (aqlaʿa) — to lift off; to take off (of a plane).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for boarding, then the Arabic for a runway.

You'll want to know: أقلع.
أقلع — aqlaʿa — "to lift off; to take off (of a plane)".
أقلعت الرحلة متأخرة بسبب الضباب. aqlaʿat ar-riḥla mutaʾakhkhira bi-sabab aḍ-ḍabāb. The flight took off late because of the fog.
Present: يقلع, yuqliʿ.
With ʿan it means to give up a habit.

The word, taken apart.
أقلع (aqlaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root q-l-ʿ, "to pull up, to uproot" (Lane). Aqlaʿa is Form IV; the same root gives qilʿ, usually glossed as the sail of a ship.

Lesson 2 of 5.
هبط (habaṭa) — to land; to go down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a runway, then the Arabic for to lift off.

You'll want to know: هبط.
هبط — habaṭa — "to land; to go down".
هبطت الرحلة في مطار صغير. habaṭat ar-riḥla fī maṭār ṣaghīr. The flight landed at a small airport.
Present: يهبط, yahbiṭ.
Lane gives the present both as yahbiṭu and yahbuṭu; hubūṭ is a landing.

The word, taken apart.
هبط (habaṭa) is built on the native Arabic root h-b-ṭ, "to descend, to go down" (Lane). It is the plain verb.

إقلاع (iqlāʿ) is built on the native Arabic root q-l-ʿ, "to pull up, to uproot" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form IV aqlaʿa, "to take off".

Lesson 4 of 5.
حزام أمان (ḥizām amān) — a seat belt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to land, then the Arabic for takeoff.

You'll want to know: حزام أمان.
حزام أمان — ḥizām amān — "a seat belt".
ربط الطفل حزام الأمان. rabaṭa aṭ-ṭifl ḥizām al-amān. The child fastened the seat belt.

The word, taken apart.
حزام أمان (ḥizām amān) is built from two native Arabic words: ḥizām, "a belt", on the root ḥ-z-m, "to bind, to tie up" (Lane), and amān, "safety", on the root ʾ-m-n, "to be safe" (Lane): a belt of safety.

Lesson 5 of 5.
طاقم (ṭāqam) — a crew; a set.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for takeoff, then the Arabic for a seat belt.

You'll want to know: طاقم.
طاقم — ṭāqam — "a crew; a set".
كان طاقم الرحلة لطيفا جدا. kāna ṭāqam ar-riḥla laṭīfan jiddan. The flight crew was very kind.
It also names a matching set: ṭāqam shāy, a tea set.

The word, taken apart.
طاقم (ṭāqam) was borrowed from Ottoman Turkish (EtymDB), where takım means a set or a team.
