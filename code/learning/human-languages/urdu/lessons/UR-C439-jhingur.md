## You'll want to know: جھینگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C439-GIDAR439-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جھینگر** — *jhīṅgur* — "a cricket (insect)".

> رات کو جھینگر شور کرتے ہیں۔
>
> *rāt ko jhīṅgur shor karte haiṅ.*
>
> At night the crickets chirp.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھینگر** (*jhīṅgur*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhĩgur*, "a cricket", under *jhiṅgura*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5387).
