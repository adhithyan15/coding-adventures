# ਮਾਂਜਣਾ (mānjṇā) — to scrub (pots)

## You'll want to know: ਮਾਂਜਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C236-KAM236-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਂਜਣਾ** — *mānjṇā* — "to scrub (pots)".

> ਮੈਂ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਮਾਂਜਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ rāt nū̃ bhāṇḍe mānjdī hāṁ.*
>
> At night I scrub the pots.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਮਾਂਜੇ, *maiṁ bhāṇḍe mānje*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਂਜਣਾ** (*mānjṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mārjati*, "wipes off, rubs clean"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *mā̃jṇā*, "to rub" (CDIAL 10080). The pot-scouring sense is CDIAL's Lahnda gloss: *mā̃jhaṇ*, "to clean out a vessel with sand or ashes".
