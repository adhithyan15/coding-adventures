## You'll want to know: ઉપયોગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C371-FAYDO371-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપયોગ** — *upyog* — "use; usefulness".

> જો તમે આ પુસ્તકનો ઉપયોગ કરશો તો પાઠ સહેલો થશે.
>
> *jo tame ā pustakno upyog karśo to pāṭh sahelo thaśe.*
>
> If you use this book, the lesson will be easy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપયોગ** (*upyog*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *upayoga*, "employment, use, application" (Monier-Williams).
