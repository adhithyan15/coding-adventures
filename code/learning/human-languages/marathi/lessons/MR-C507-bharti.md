## You'll want to know: भरती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C507-ROJGAR507-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भरती** — *bhartī* — "recruitment; enrolment".

> पोलिसांची भरती पुढच्या महिन्यात आहे.
>
> *polisāñcī bhartī puḍhcyā mahinyāt āhe.*
>
> The police recruitment is next month.

Feminine.

भरती होणे, to be recruited; भरती करणे, to recruit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भरती** (*bhartī*) is built on भरणे, "to fill": a recruitment fills the empty places. The verb is inherited from Sanskrit *bharati*, "bears, fills" (CDIAL 9397).
