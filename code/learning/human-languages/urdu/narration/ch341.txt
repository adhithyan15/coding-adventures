Urdu, chapter 341: Who Governs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حکمران (hukmrān) — a ruler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an arbiter, then the Urdu for to testify.

You'll want to know: حکمران.
حکمران — hukmrān — "a ruler".
اس ملک کا حکمران بادشاہ تھا۔ is mulk kā hukmrān bādshāh thā. The ruler of this country was a king.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
حکمران (hukmrān) is usually traced to Persian hukmrān: Arabic ḥukm, "an order", with Persian -rān, "driving". One who runs the orders.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تجویز (tajvīz) — a proposal; a suggestion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a ruler, then the Urdu for a policy.

You'll want to know: تجویز.
تجویز — tajvīz — "a proposal; a suggestion".
وزیر نے ایک نئی تجویز دی۔ vazīr ne ek naī tajvīz dī. The minister put forward a new proposal.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تجویز (tajvīz) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tajwīz, "allowing, approving"; Urdu uses it for a proposal put forward for others to approve, a sense our sources do not trace.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ٹھہرانا (ṭhahrānā) — to hold to be; to declare (guilty).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a proposal, then the Urdu for the power to rule.

You'll want to know: ٹھہرانا.
ٹھہرانا — ṭhahrānā — "to hold to be; to declare (guilty)".
لوگوں نے حکومت کو ذمہ دار ٹھہرایا۔ logoṅ ne hukūmat ko zimme-dār ṭhahrāyā. People held the government responsible.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مجھے ذمہ دار ٹھہرایا, us ne mujhe zimme-dār ṭhahrāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ٹھہرانا (ṭhahrānā) is the causative of ٹھہرنا, "to stop, to settle", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭhaharnā under stabhira, "fixed, firm" (CDIAL 13680). To make a verdict settle on someone.
