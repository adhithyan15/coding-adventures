## You'll want to know: سپاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C351-SEPAH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سپاه** (*sepâh*) — "an army".

> سپاه شب به شهر رسید.
>
> *Sepâh shab be shahr resid.*
>
> The army reached the city at night.

Literary and military; *lashkar* is a synonym.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sepâh* is a native Persian word that EtymDB glosses "army" without a parent; it is usually traced to Middle Persian *spāh*.
