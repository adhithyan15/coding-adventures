## You'll want to know: повышение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C362-POVYSHENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**повышение** (*povyshéniye*) — "a promotion; a rise".

> Он получил повышение на работе.
>
> *On poluchíl povyshéniye na rabóte.*
>
> He got a promotion at work.

Neuter. *Повышение зарплаты* — a pay rise. From *повышать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**повышение** is built on *повысить* "to raise", from *выше* "higher", the comparative of *высокий*, inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*vysokŭ* "high, tall" (EtymDB).
