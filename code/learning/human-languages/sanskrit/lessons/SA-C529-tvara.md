## You'll want to know: त्वरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C529-PARIVARTANA529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**त्वरा** — *tvarā* — "haste".

> यदि त्वरा न अस्ति, तर्हि मन्दं गच्छतु।
>
> *yadi tvarā na asti, tarhi mandaṃ gacchatu.*
>
> If there is no hurry, go slowly.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्वरा** (*tvarā*) comes from the root √tvar, "to hurry"; MW gives *tvarā* as haste, speed (MW).
