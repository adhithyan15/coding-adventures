## You'll want to know: はりきる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C428-JOY428-05]; assesses=[] -->

**はりきる** — *harikiru* — "to be full of energy; to be eager".

> **おとうとは はりきって れんしゅうしています。** — *otōto wa harikitte renshū shite imasu* — "My younger brother is practising with great energy."

Polite **はりきります**, *harikirimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せんしゅが はりきりました。**, *senshu ga harikirimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はりきる** (*harikiru*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *hari*, "stretching (haru)" and *kiru*, "to cut" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
