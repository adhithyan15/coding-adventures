Gujarati, chapter 188: Genuine goods and fakes.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
મફત (maphat) — free of charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for per cent, then the Gujarati for discount.

You'll want to know: મફત.
મફત — maphat — "free of charge".
અહીં પાણી મફત છે. — ahī̃ pāṇī maphat che — "Water is free here."

The word, taken apart.
મફત (maphat) is a loan, usually traced to Persian muft, "free, gratis". CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 6.
નકલી (naklī) — fake, counterfeit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for discount, then the Gujarati for free of charge.

You'll want to know: નકલી.
નકલી — naklī — "fake, counterfeit".
આ નોટ નકલી છે. — ā noṭ naklī che — "This note is fake."

The word, taken apart.
નકલી (naklī) is built on nakal, "a copy", a loan from Arabic naql, "transfer, copying", which reached Gujarati through Persian; the ending -ī makes the adjective. Its Arabic root is n-q-l (Lane).
