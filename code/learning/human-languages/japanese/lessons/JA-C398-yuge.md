## You'll want to know: ゆげ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C398-ONSEN398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆげ** — *yuge* — "rising steam; steam from hot water".

> **おんせんの ゆげが みえます。** — *onsen no yuge ga miemasu* — "You can see the steam from the hot spring."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆげ** (*yuge*) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the native Japanese (wago) *yu*, "hot water", inherited rather than borrowed, and the Sino-Japanese *ke*, "air" (Middle Chinese *khj+jH*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, joined as *yuge* (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
