## You'll want to know: かんしん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C433-LONG433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かんしん** — *kanshin* — "admiration; being impressed".

> **かれの しごとに かんしんしました。** — *kare no shigoto ni kanshin shimashita* — "I was impressed by his work."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんしん** (*kanshin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "emotion" (Middle Chinese *komX*) and *shin*, "heart" (*sim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
