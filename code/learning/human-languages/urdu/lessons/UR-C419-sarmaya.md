## You'll want to know: سرمایہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C419-MUNAFA419-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سرمایہ** — *sarmāyā* — "capital (money); funds".

> دکان کے لیے سرمایہ چاہیے۔
>
> *dukān ke liye sarmāyā chāhiye.*
>
> You need capital to open a shop.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرمایہ** (*sarmāyā*) is built from Persian *sar*, "a head", and *māya*, "substance": it is usually traced to Persian *sarmāya*, "capital".
