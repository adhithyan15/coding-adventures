## You'll want to know: der Sieg
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C319-KAISER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Sieg** — "the victory".

> Nach dem Sieg feierte das ganze Land.
>
> *After the victory the whole country celebrated.*

Plural: *die Siege*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sieg* is inherited from Old High German *sigu*, from Proto-Germanic *segaz* "victory" (EtymDB).
