## You'll want to know: ડેમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C441-LAKE441-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ડેમ** — *ḍem* — "a dam".

> નદી પર નવો ડેમ બન્યો છે.
>
> *nadī par navo ḍem banyo che.*
>
> A new dam has been built on the river.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડેમ** (*ḍem*) is borrowed from English *dam*.
