Tamil, chapter 154: To wear, To call, To grow, To count, To shout.

Lesson 4 of 5.
எண்ணு (eṇṇu) — to count.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to call, then the Tamil for to grow.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count with care: oṉṟu, mūṉṟu, with the hard ṉṟ; onpatu, one short of pattu; then பதினொன்று to இருபது, two tens.

You'll want to know: எண்ணு.
