Tamil, chapter 169: Ankle, Waist, Throat, Heart, Blood.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
கணுக்கால் (kaṇukkāl) — an ankle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to slip, then the Tamil for to yawn.

You'll want to know: கணுக்கால்.
கணுக்கால் — kaṇukkāl — "an ankle".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kaṇukkāl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kaṇukkāl, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kaṇukkāl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for to slip, then the Tamil for to yawn, then say kaṇukkāl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does கணுக்கால் (kaṇukkāl) mean? ("An ankle".) Say it once more.

To move? (நகர், nakar.) To lift? (தூக்கு, tūkku.) To prepare? (தயார் செய், tayār cey.) To serve? (பரிமாறு, parimāṟu.) To share? (பகிர், pakir.) To bargain? (பேரம் பேசு, pēram pēcu.) To check? (சரிபார், caripār.) To describe? (விவரி, vivari.) To hand over? (ஒப்படை, oppaṭai.) To practise? (பயிற்சி செய், payiṟci cey.) To pass? (தேர்ச்சி பெறு, tērcci peṟu.) To pay attention? (கவனி, kavaṉi.)
