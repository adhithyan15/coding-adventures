## You'll want to know: पहेली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C334-KATHA334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पहेली** — *pahelī* — "a riddle".

> ऋषि ने राजा से एक पहेली पूछी।
>
> *ṛṣi ne rājā se ek pahelī pūchī.*
>
> The sage asked the king a riddle.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पहेली** (*pahelī*) goes back to Sanskrit *prahelikā*, "a riddle" (CDIAL 8911). You **पहेली पूछना**, ask one, and **पहेली बूझना**, solve it.
