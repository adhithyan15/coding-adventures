# عیدی (īdī) — money given at Eid

## You'll want to know: عیدی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C318-EIDMORNING318-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عیدی** — *īdī* — "money given at Eid".

> بچوں کو دادا سے عیدی ملی۔
>
> *bachchoṅ ko dādā se īdī milī.*
>
> The children got Eid money from their grandfather.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عیدی** (*īdī*) is built on **عید**, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿīd*, with the ending *-ī*: the money elders give children on the day.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
