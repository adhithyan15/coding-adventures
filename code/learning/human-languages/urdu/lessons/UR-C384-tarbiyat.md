## You'll want to know: تربیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C384-TARBIYAT384-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تربیت** — *tarbiyat* — "upbringing; training".

> اگر تربیت اچھی ہوئی تو بچے اچھے بنیں گے۔
>
> *agar tarbiyat achchhī huī to bachche achchhe baneṅ ge.*
>
> If they are brought up well, children will turn out well.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تربیت** (*tarbiyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tarbiya*, "bringing up, rearing".
