## You'll want to know: jìnshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C471-EYES471-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jìnshì* — "short-sighted".

> *wǒ gǎnjué yǎnjing bú tài hǎo kěnéng jìnshì le* — My eyes don't feel right; I might be getting short-sighted.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìnshì* is built from *jìn* 'near' and *shì* 'to look', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gj+nX* and *dzyijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'close by' and the second 'to view'.
