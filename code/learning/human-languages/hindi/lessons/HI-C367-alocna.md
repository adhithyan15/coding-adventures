# आलोचना (ālocnā) — criticism

## You'll want to know: आलोचना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C367-TARIF367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आलोचना** — *ālocnā* — "criticism".

> सरकार की नई नीति की बहुत आलोचना हुई।
>
> *sarkār kī naī nīti kī bahut ālocnā huī.*
>
> The government's new policy was much criticised.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आलोचना** (*ālocnā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ālocana*, "considering, reflecting" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for judging something's faults.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
