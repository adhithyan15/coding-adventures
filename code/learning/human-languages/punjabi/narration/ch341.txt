Punjabi, chapter 341: I'm Not So Sure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸ਼ੱਕ (shakk) — doubt; suspicion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to admit, then the Punjabi for a compromise.

You'll want to know: ਸ਼ੱਕ.
ਸ਼ੱਕ — shakk — "doubt; suspicion".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਗੱਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ੱਕ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ। mainū̃ tuhāḍī gall utte koī shakk nahīṁ hai. I have no doubt about what you say.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸ਼ੱਕ (shakk) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic shakk, "doubt".

What you've built.
One more word for asking and answering.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: shakk, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mainū̃ tuhāḍī gall utte koī shakk nahīṁ hai. — I have no doubt about what you say.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for to admit, then the Punjabi for a compromise, then say shakk again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਸ਼ੱਕ (shakk) mean? ("Doubt; suspicion".) Say it once more.

ਭਰੋਸਾ — bharosā — "trust; reliance".
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਮੇਰੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਭਰੋਸਾ ਕਰਦੇ ਹੋ? kī tusī̃ mere utte bharosā karde ho? Do you trust me?

The word, taken apart.
ਭਰੋਸਾ (bharosā) is inherited from a reconstructed bharavaśya, "dependence on support"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bharossā, "confidence", under it (CDIAL 9398).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਜਚਣਾ (jachṇā) — to seem right; to convince.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for trust, then the Punjabi for a guess.

You'll want to know: ਜਚਣਾ.
ਜਚਣਾ — jachṇā — "to seem right; to convince".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਉਸ ਦੀ ਗੱਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਚਦੀ ਹੈ। mainū̃ us dī gall nahīṁ jachdī hai. What he says doesn't convince me.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਜਵਾਬ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਜਚਿਆ, oh javāb mainū̃ jachiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਜਚਣਾ (jachṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit yācyate, "is asked"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jacāuṇā, "to cause to be examined", under it and notes jacṇā, "to be tested", formed from it (CDIAL 10449). CDIAL does not give the everyday sense "to seem right".
