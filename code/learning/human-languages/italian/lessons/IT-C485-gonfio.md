## You'll want to know: gonfio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C485-STORTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**gonfio** — "swollen".

> Ho la caviglia gonfia.
>
> *My ankle is swollen.*

The feminine is *gonfia*. The verb is *gonfiarsi* — to swell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gonfio* is built on *gonfiare* "to blow up, to swell", inherited from Latin *cōnflāre* "to blow together" (Lewis & Short), from *flāre* "to blow".
