## You'll want to know: じんこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C333-WAKAMONO333-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じんこう** — *jinkō* — "population".

> **まちの じんこうが すくなく なったので、みせが しまりました。** — *machi no jinkō ga sukunaku natta node, mise ga shimarimashita* — "The town's population fell, so the shops closed."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じんこう** (*jinkō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jin*, "person" (Middle Chinese *nyin*) and *kō*, "mouth" (*khuwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
