German, chapter 530: Morgens im Bad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
kämmen — to comb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for tile, then the German for attic.

You'll want to know: kämmen.
kämmen — "to comb".
Sie kämmt ihrer kleinen Tochter die Haare. She combs her little daughter's hair.
Sich kämmen — to comb your hair. The comb is der Kamm.

The word, taken apart.
kämmen is inherited from Middle High German kemben (EtymDB), built on the word that is today Kamm "comb", from Old High German chamb and Proto-Germanic kambaz.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
gurgeln — to gargle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for attic, then the German for to comb.

You'll want to know: gurgeln.
gurgeln — "to gargle".
Bei Halsschmerzen gurgelt sie mit Salzwasser. When she has a sore throat, she gargles with salt water.
Regular: hat gegurgelt. Also of water running away: Das Wasser gurgelt im Abfluss.

The word, taken apart.
gurgeln is built on Gurgel "throat", which comes from Latin gurguliō "throat, windpipe" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
schminken — to put on make-up (sich schminken).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to comb, then the German for to gargle.

You'll want to know: schminken.
schminken — "to put on make-up (sich schminken)".
Vor der Party schminkt sie sich im Bad. Before the party she does her make-up in the bathroom.
Mostly reflexive: sich schminken. Die Schminke — make-up.

The word, taken apart.
schminken is built on Schminke "make-up", which EtymDB links to Old High German smeichen "to smooth"; the details are not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Bademantel — bathrobe, dressing gown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to gargle, then the German for to put on make-up.

You'll want to know: der Bademantel.
der Bademantel — "bathrobe, dressing gown".
Am Sonntag frühstückt er im Bademantel. On Sundays he has breakfast in his bathrobe.
Plural: die Bademäntel.

The word, taken apart.
Bademantel is built on Bad "bath", from Proto-Germanic baþą, and Mantel "coat" (EtymDB lists both German words).

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Schlafanzug — pyjamas.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to put on make-up, then the German for bathrobe.

You'll want to know: der Schlafanzug.
der Schlafanzug — "pyjamas".
Die Kinder haben schon ihre Schlafanzüge an. The children already have their pyjamas on.
Plural: die Schlafanzüge. One pair is ein Schlafanzug.

The word, taken apart.
Schlafanzug is built on schlafen "to sleep" and Anzug "suit" (EtymDB lists it), from anziehen "to put on", built on ziehen "to pull".
