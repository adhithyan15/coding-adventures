## You'll want to know: a nuca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C482-CORPO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a nuca** — "nape (of the neck)".

> O sol queimou-me a nuca.
>
> *The sun burned the back of my neck.*

The back of the neck; the front is *a garganta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nuca* comes from Medieval Latin *nucha*, which is usually traced to an Arabic word for the spinal marrow.
