## You'll want to know: flammeum, flammeī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C328-FLAMMEUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**flammeum, flammeī** — "a bridal veil".

> Spōnsa flammeum gerēbat.
>
> *The bride wore a flame-coloured veil.*

Neuter, from *flammeus*, 'flame-coloured': the bride's orange-red veil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**flammeum** is the neuter of *flammeus*, "flame-coloured", with *vēlum*, "veil", understood (Lewis & Short); *flammeus* is built on *flamma*, "flame".
