Urdu, chapter 358: The Public Meeting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جلسہ (jalsā) — a public meeting; a rally.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for string-pulling, then the Urdu for transparent.

You'll want to know: جلسہ.
جلسہ — jalsā — "a public meeting; a rally".
کل شام میدان میں جلسہ ہو گا۔ kal shām maidān meṅ jalsā ho gā. There will be a rally on the ground tomorrow evening.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
جلسہ (jalsā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic jalsa, "a sitting, a session".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ٹھکرانا (ṭhukrānā) — to spurn; to turn down flatly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a district, then the Urdu for a majority.

You'll want to know: ٹھکرانا.
ٹھکرانا — ṭhukrānā — "to spurn; to turn down flatly".
جلسے نے اس کی تجویز ٹھکرا دی۔ jalse ne us kī tajvīz ṭhukrā dī. The meeting flatly turned down his proposal.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مشورہ ٹھکرایا, us ne mashvarā ṭhukrāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ٹھکرانا (ṭhukrānā) is built on ṭhokar, "a kick, a blow", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭhokar under ṭhōkk, "knock", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5513). To push a thing away with the foot.
