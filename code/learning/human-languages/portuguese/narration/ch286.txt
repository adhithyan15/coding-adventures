Portuguese, chapter 286: Delitos e vítimas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o delito — the offence, the misdeed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the TV viewer, then the Portuguese for the reader.

You'll want to know: o delito.
o delito — "the offence, the misdeed".
Conduzir sem carta é um delito. Driving without a licence is an offence.
Em flagrante delito — caught in the act.

The word, taken apart.
delito was borrowed from Latin dēlictum "a fault, a misdeed" (EtymDB), from dēlinquere "to fall short, to do wrong".

O vandalismo na estação custou muito dinheiro. The vandalism at the station cost a lot of money.
Atos de vandalismo — acts of vandalism.

The word, taken apart.
vandalismo is built on vândalo with -ismo, an ending that comes from Greek -ismós through Latin (EtymDB has vândalo).

Lesson 5 of 5.
o homicídio — the homicide, the killing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for vandalism, then the Portuguese for the victim.

You'll want to know: o homicídio.
o homicídio — "the homicide, the killing".
A polícia acha que foi um homicídio. The police think it was a homicide.
A news and court word; in speech, o assassinato.

The word, taken apart.
homicídio is a learned borrowing from Latin homicīdium "a killing of a person", from homō "a human being" and caedere "to cut down, to kill" (Lewis & Short).
