# దోమతెర (dōmatera) — a mosquito net

## You'll want to know: దోమతెర
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C458-GADI458-03]; assesses=[] -->

**దోమతెర** — *dōmatera* — "a mosquito net".

> దోమతెర లేకుండా రాత్రి నిద్ర పట్టలేదు.
>
> *dōmatera lēkuṇḍā rātri nidra paṭṭalēdu.*
>
> Without a mosquito net I could not sleep at night.

దోమ, a mosquito, + తెర, a screen, a curtain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దోమతెర** (*dōmatera*) is native Dravidian: దోమ, "a mosquito" (DEDR 2991), and తెర, "a screen, a curtain" (DEDR 3244).
