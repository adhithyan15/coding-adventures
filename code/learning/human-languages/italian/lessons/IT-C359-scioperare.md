## You'll want to know: scioperare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C359-SCIOPERO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**scioperare** — "to go on strike, to strike".

> Gli insegnanti scioperano contro la nuova legge.
>
> *The teachers are striking against the new law.*

Stress: *sciòpero*, *sciòperano*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scioperare* is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin *exoperāre* "to be out of work", built from *ex-* "out of" and *operāre* "to work", on *opus, operis* "work" (Lewis & Short).
