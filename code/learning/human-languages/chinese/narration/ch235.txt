Mandarin Chinese, chapter 235: For a Reason.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
为了 (wèile) — for; in order to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a raincoat, then the Mandarin for to shelter from the rain.

You'll want to know: 为了.
为了 — wèile — "for; in order to".
为了身体我们都不吃点心了 wèile shēntǐ wǒmen dōu bù chī diǎnxin le — For our health, we've all given up snacks.

The word, taken apart.
为了 (wèile) is built from 为 'for' and 了, both inherited from Middle Chinese, hjweH and lewX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to help'; in the word the second loses its tone.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: yóuyú.
yóuyú — "owing to; because of".
yóuyú tiānqì bù hǎo wǒmen méi qù hǎibiān — Because of the bad weather, we didn't go to the seaside.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yóuyú is built from yóu 'from' and yú 'at', both inherited from Middle Chinese, yuw and 'jo (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to follow' and 'by way of', and the second 'to dwell' and lists it as a particle.

Lesson 4 of 6.
zhǐhǎo — to have no choice but to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for owing to, then the Mandarin for grounds for doing something.

You'll want to know: zhǐhǎo.
zhǐhǎo — "to have no choice but to".
méiyǒu chē le wǒmen zhǐhǎo zǒulù — There was no transport left, so we had to walk.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
Tone: zhǐ and hǎo are both third tones, so the first rises and it sounds like zhíhǎo.

The word, taken apart.
zhǐhǎo is built from zhǐ 'only' and 好 'good'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese xawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the first, tsyeX there, as a particle.

Lesson 5 of 6.
guàibude — no wonder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for grounds for doing something, then the Mandarin for to have no choice but.

You'll want to know: guàibude.
guàibude — "no wonder".
tā gǎnmào le guàibude tā méi lái — He's got a cold; no wonder he didn't come.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
Tone: 不 is light here, bu, with no tone of its own.

The word, taken apart.
guàibude is built from guài 'to find strange', 不 'not' and 得 'can', all inherited from Middle Chinese, kweajH, pjut and tok (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'strange'; in the word the last two lose their tones.
