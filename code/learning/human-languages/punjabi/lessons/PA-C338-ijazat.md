## You'll want to know: ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C338-PERM338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ** — *ijāzat* — "permission".

> ਅਧਿਆਪਕ ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ ਨਹੀਂ ਦਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *adhiāpak ijāzat nahīṁ dindā hai.*
>
> The teacher does not give permission.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ** (*ijāzat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ijāza*, "permission".
