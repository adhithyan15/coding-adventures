# తేలిపోవు (tēlipōvu) — to float away

## You'll want to know: తేలిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C453-TEPPA453-04]; assesses=[] -->

**తేలిపోవు** — *tēlipōvu* — "to float away".

> గాలికి నా టోపీ నీటిలో తేలిపోయింది.
>
> *gāliki nā ṭōpī nīṭilō tēlipōyindi.*
>
> In the wind my cap floated away on the water.

Past (it …): తేలిపోయింది, *tēlipōyindi*.

తేలు, to float, + పోవు, to go: carried off on the surface. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: తేలిపోయింది, it floated away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తేలిపోవు** (*tēlipōvu*) is native Dravidian: తేలు, "to float" (DEDR 3464), with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
