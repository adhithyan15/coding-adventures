## You'll want to know: ذاكر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C392-MUHADIR392-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ذاكر** — *dhākara* — "to study for".

> ذاكر الطالب دروسه حتى منتصف الليل.
>
> *dhākara aṭ-ṭālib durūsahu ḥattā muntaṣaf al-layl.*
>
> The student studied his lessons until midnight.

Present: يذاكر, *yudhākir*.

Common in spoken Arabic; formal writing often says درس.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذاكر** (*dhākara*) is built on the native Arabic root *dh-k-r*, usually traced to the sense "to remember, to mention". It is Form III, to go over a thing with oneself again: to study for a test.
