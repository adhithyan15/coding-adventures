## You'll want to know: verschlüsseln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C395-PASSWORT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**verschlüsseln** — "to encrypt, to encode".

> Die Nachrichten werden automatisch verschlüsselt.
>
> *The messages are encrypted automatically.*

Inseparable: *es verschlüsselt, es hat verschlüsselt*. Built on *der Schlüssel* — the key.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verschlüsseln* is built with *ver-* on *Schlüssel* "key", inherited from Old High German *sluzzil* and Proto-Germanic *slutilaz* (EtymDB), of the family of *schließen* "to lock".
