## You'll want to know: juntarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C560-ENCUENTRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**juntarse** — "to get together; to join".

> Los amigos se juntan los viernes.
>
> *The friends get together on Fridays.*

Common in Latin America for meeting friends. *Juntarse con alguien* — to hang out with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juntarse* is built on *junto* 'together', which comes from Latin *iūnctus* 'joined', the participle of *iungere* 'to join, to yoke' (Lewis & Short).
