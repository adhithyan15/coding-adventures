## You'll want to know: سسکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C453-LAUGH453-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سسکنا** — *sisaknā* — "to sob".

> لڑکی کونے میں بیٹھ کر سسک رہی تھی۔
>
> *laṛkī kone meṅ baiṭh kar sisak rahī thī.*
>
> The girl was sitting in the corner, sobbing.

Past (she …): وہ دیر تک سسکی, *voh der tak siskī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سسکنا** (*sisaknā*) is probably inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi *siskārī*, "sobbing", under *śuṣati*, "hisses, pants", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12545).
