## You'll want to know: ہیرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C327-KHAZANA327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ہیرا** — *hīrā* — "a diamond".

> خزانے میں سو ہیرے تھے۔
>
> *khazāne meṅ sau hīre the.*
>
> There were a hundred diamonds in the treasure.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہیرا** (*hīrā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *hīra*, "a diamond" (CDIAL 14130), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *hīrā*.
