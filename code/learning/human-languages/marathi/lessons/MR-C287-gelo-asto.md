# मी गेलो असतो (mī gelo asto) — what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C287-DEPENDS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C286-JAR-TAR-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "it depends on the weather". (*te havāmānāvar avlambūn āhe*.) Then "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home". (*jar udyā pāūs paḍlā, tar mī gharī rāhīn*.)

[PAUSE 1s] That looks ahead at what may still happen. This lesson looks back at what did not.

## You'll want to know: -लो असतो, 'would have'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C287-WOULD-01]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, MR-LEX-JANE, MR-LEX-YENE] -->

> **मी गेलो असतो.**
>
> *mī gelo asto.* — "I would have gone."

The past, **गेलो**, then **असतो** (*asto*), which takes the same endings: **तो गेला असता** (*to gelā astā*), **ती गेली असती** (*tī gelī astī*), **आम्ही आलो असतो** (*āmhī ālo asto*). A woman says **मी गेले असते** (*mī gele aste*).

## You'll want to know: the if that did not happen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C287-UNREAL-IF-01]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C286-JAR-TAR-01, MR-LEX-C111-TIME111-05, MR-LEX-C99-NATURE99-01] -->

> **जर मला वेळ असता, तर मी आलो असतो.**
>
> *jar malā veḷ astā, tar mī ālo asto.* — "If I had had time, I would have come."

In the condition, **असता** (*astā*) alone says "had been"; with a verb, the past comes first: **जर पाऊस पडला नसता** (*jar pāūs paḍlā nastā*), "if it had not rained", with **नसता** (*nastā*, "had not been"). Both halves wear **असता**, and that is how a listener knows it did not happen: **जर तू आला असतास, तर आम्ही बोललो असतो** (*jar tū ālā astās, tar āmhī bollo asto*), "if you had come, we would have talked".
