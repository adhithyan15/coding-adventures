Hindi, chapter 480: The Senses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्वाद (svād) — a taste; a flavour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to chew, then the Hindi for to spit.

You'll want to know: स्वाद.
स्वाद — svād — "a taste; a flavour".
माँ के हाथ के खाने का स्वाद सबसे अच्छा है। māṁ ke hāth ke khāne kā svād sabse acchā hai. Food cooked by my mother tastes best of all.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
स्वाद (svād) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit svāda, "taste, flavour, savour" (Monier-Williams).

माँ के स्पर्श से बच्चा सो गया। māṁ ke sparś se baccā so gayā. At its mother's touch the baby fell asleep.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
स्पर्श (sparś) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sparśa, "touch, the sense of touch" (Monier-Williams). It is the noun; the everyday verb is chūnā.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: गंध.
गंध — gandh — "a smell; an odour".
रसोई से मसालों की गंध आ रही है। rasoī se masāloṁ kī gandh ā rahī hai. The smell of spices is coming from the kitchen.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
गंध (gandh) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi gandh, "smell", under Sanskrit gandhá, "smell" (CDIAL 4014). It can be pleasant or not; khuśbū is only a good smell.

Lesson 4 of 5.
महक (mahak) — an aroma; a scent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a touch, then the Hindi for a smell.

You'll want to know: महक.
महक — mahak — "an aroma; a scent".
बारिश के बाद मिट्टी की महक आती है। bāriś ke bād miṭṭī kī mahak ātī hai. After the rain there is a scent of earth.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
महक (mahak) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi mahak, "fragrance", under Sanskrit magha, which it glosses as a fragrance and the name of a flower (CDIAL 9703). It is a strong, spreading smell, usually a pleasant one.

Lesson 5 of 5.
चाटना (cāṭnā) — to lick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a smell, then the Hindi for an aroma.

You'll want to know: चाटना.
चाटना — cāṭnā — "to lick".
बच्चे ने उँगली से शहद चाटा। bacce ne uṅglī se śahad cāṭā. The child licked honey off his finger.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने शहद चाटा, usne śahad cāṭā.

The word, taken apart.
चाटना (cāṭnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi cāṭnā, "to lick, taste, eat", under caṭṭ, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4573).
