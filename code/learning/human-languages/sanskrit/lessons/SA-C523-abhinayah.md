## You'll want to know: अभिनयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C523-NATA523-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनयः** — *abhinayaḥ* — "acting".

> तस्य अभिनयः मह्यं रोचते।
>
> *tasya abhinayaḥ mahyaṃ rocate.*
>
> I like his acting.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनयः** (*abhinayaḥ*) is built on *abhi* and the root √nī, "to lead"; MW gives *abhinaya* as acting, dramatic action, the showing of a feeling by look and gesture (MW).
