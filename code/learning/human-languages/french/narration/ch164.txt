Lesson 1 of 5.
le passeport — a passport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a lock, then the French for a doorbell.

You'll want to know: le passeport.
le passeport — "a passport".

The word, taken apart.
passeport — the verb for "to pass" and Old French port, "a harbour", from Latin portus: leave to pass through a port. (EtymDB 2.1).
