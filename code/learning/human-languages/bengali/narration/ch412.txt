Bengali, chapter 412: Walls and Gates.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পাঁচিল (pā̃chil) — a boundary wall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a postman, then the Bengali for to send.

You'll want to know: পাঁচিল.
পাঁচিল — pā̃chil — "a boundary wall".
বাগানের চারপাশে উঁচু পাঁচিল। bāgāner chārpāshe ũchu pā̃chil. There is a high wall all round the garden.
The outer wall round a yard or garden; দেয়াল is any wall, inside or out.

The word, taken apart.
পাঁচিল (pā̃chil) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali pā̃cil, "a rampart", under Sanskrit prācīra, "an enclosure, a wall" (CDIAL 8921); প্রাচীর, the same word taken whole, is the grander city wall.

রাস্তার পাশে একটা পুরনো দালান আছে। rāstār pāshe ekṭā purôno dālān āchhe. There is an old brick building by the road.
In a village, the one house built of brick rather than mud and thatch.

দালান (dālān) is usually traced to Persian dālān, "a hall, a covered porch".

Past (he/she …): তিনি পাঁচিল টপকালেন, tini pā̃chil ṭôpkālen.

টপকানো (ṭôpkāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali ṭapkāna, "to leap over", under the reconstructed tarpati, "jumps" (CDIAL 5727).
