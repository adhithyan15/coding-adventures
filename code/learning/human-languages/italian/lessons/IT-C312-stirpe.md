## You'll want to know: la stirpe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C312-ANTENATI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la stirpe** — "the lineage, the stock".

> Viene da una stirpe di pescatori.
>
> *He comes from a line of fishermen.*

Literary.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stirpe* is a learned borrowing from Latin *stirps* "a stem, a stock, a family" (Lewis & Short).
