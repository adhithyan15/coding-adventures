## You'll want to know: мёрзнуть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C467-MOROZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**мёрзнуть** (*myórznut*) — "to feel the cold, to freeze".

> Без шапки уши быстро мёрзнут.
>
> *Bez shápki úshi býstro myórznut.*
>
> Without a hat your ears soon get cold.

Imperfective; perfective *замёрзнуть*. Past *мёрз, мёрзла*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мёрзнуть* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *mĭrznǫti* (EtymDB), on the same root as *мороз* 'frost'.
