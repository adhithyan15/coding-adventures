## You'll want to know: સોની
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C510-SONI510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સોની** — *sonī* — "a goldsmith".

> અમારી ગલીમાં એક સોનીની દુકાન છે.
>
> *amārī galīmā̃ ek sonīnī dukān che.*
>
> There is a goldsmith's shop in our lane.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સોની** (*sonī*) is built on the Gujarati word for gold, which goes back to Sanskrit *suvárṇa*, "gold" (CDIAL 13519). CDIAL also lists the older *sonār*, "a goldsmith", from Sanskrit *suvarṇakāra* (CDIAL 13520).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
