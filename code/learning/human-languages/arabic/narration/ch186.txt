Lesson 1 of 5.
هواية (hiwāya) — a hobby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bull, then the Arabic for an insect.

You'll want to know: هواية.
هواية — hiwāya — "a hobby".

The word, taken apart.
هواية (hiwāya) is built on the native Arabic root h-w-y, "to love, to desire" (Lane). Hiwāya is a modern word built on hawā, "liking".

Lesson 2 of 5.
شطرنج (shiṭranj) — chess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an insect, then the Arabic for a hobby.

You'll want to know: شطرنج.
شطرنج — shiṭranj — "chess".

The word, taken apart.
شطرنج (shiṭranj) is a loan: Lane's sources call it a Persian word, arabicized, and quote a story that it means ṣad rang, "a hundred stratagems". That is a folk etymology: Persian chatrang goes back to Sanskrit caturaṅga, "four-limbed", the four arms of an army.

Lesson 4 of 5.
لوحة (lawḥa) — a painting; a board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for chess, then the Arabic for a novel.

You'll want to know: لوحة.
لوحة — lawḥa — "a painting; a board".

The word, taken apart.
لوحة (lawḥa), "a board, a plank", is an old native noun, filed under the root l-w-ḥ (Lane). Lawḥa is a board; the painting is a later sense.
