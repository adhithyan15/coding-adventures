# ಕಾದಾಡು (kādāḍu) — to do battle; to clash

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C344-AYUDHA344-03, KA-LEX-C344-AYUDHA344-04] -->

## You'll want to know: ಕಾದಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C344-AYUDHA344-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾದಾಡು** — *kādāḍu* — "to do battle; to clash".

> ಎರಡು ಸೈನ್ಯಗಳು ಇಡೀ ದಿನ ಕಾದಾಡಿದವು.
>
> *eraḍu sainyagaḷu iḍī dina kādāḍidavu.*
>
> The two armies did battle all day.

Past (I …): ಕಾದಾಡಿದೆ, *kādāḍide*.

Who one fights with takes -ಜೊತೆ or -ಒಡನೆ. Animals ಕಾದಾಡು too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾದಾಡು** (*kādāḍu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *kādu*, "to war, to fight, to contend" (DEDR 1447), and *āḍu*, "to move, to act" (DEDR 347).
