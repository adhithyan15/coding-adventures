## You'll want to know: युक्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C540-KOSHISH540-02]; assesses=[] -->

**युक्ति** — *yukti* — "a clever way; a device".

> अरुण ने एक युक्ति से भारी मेज़ अकेले उठाई।
>
> *Aruṇ ne ek yukti se bhārī mez akele uṭhāī.*
>
> Arun found a clever way to lift the heavy table on his own.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**युक्ति** (*yukti*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *yukti*, "union; a contrivance, a means" (Monier-Williams).
