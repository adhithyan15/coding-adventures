## You'll want to know: сирена
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C299-SIRENA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**сирена** (*siréna*) — "a siren".

> Вдалеке выла сирена.
>
> *Vdaleké výla siréna.*
>
> A siren was wailing in the distance.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сирена* is usually traced through French *sirène* to Greek *Seirḗn*, a sea creature of legend whose song drew sailors; EtymDB lists no parent.
