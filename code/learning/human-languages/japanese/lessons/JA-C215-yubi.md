## You'll want to know: ゆび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C215-BODY215-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆび** — *yubi* — "a finger; a toe".

> **ゆびが うごきません。** — *yubi ga ugokimasen* — "My finger does not move."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆび** (*yubi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
