## You'll want to know: لثه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C496-NERVES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لثه** (*lase*) — "the gum (in the mouth)".

> دندانپزشک لثه او را معاینه کرد.
>
> *dandânpezeshk lase-ye u râ mo'âyene kard.*
>
> The dentist examined her gums.

Plural *lasehâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lase* is a loan, usually traced to the Arabic word for the gum of the mouth. EtymDB has no entry for it.
