# സഹോദരൻ (sahōdaran) — a brother

## You'll want to know: സഹോദരൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C553-SAHODARAN553-05]; assesses=[] -->

**സഹോദരൻ** — *sahōdaran* — "a brother".

> എന്റെ സഹോദരൻ വിദേശത്ത് ജോലി ചെയ്യുന്നു.
>
> *enṟe sahōdaran vidēśattŭ jōli ceyyunnu.*
>
> My brother works abroad.

Formal or written; at home one says ചേട്ടൻ or അനിയൻ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സഹോദരൻ** (*sahōdaran*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sahodara*, "born of the same womb" (Monier-Williams), with the masculine ending -ൻ.
