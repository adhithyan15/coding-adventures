# ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ (akkasāliga) — a goldsmith

## You'll want to know: ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C522-KASUBU522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ** — *akkasāliga* — "a goldsmith".

> ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ ಮದುವೆಗೆ ಚಿನ್ನದ ಸರ ಮಾಡಿದನು.
>
> *akkasāliga maduvege cinnada sara māḍidanu.*
>
> The goldsmith made a gold necklace for the wedding.

Plural: ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗರು, *akkasāligaru*.

The ending -ಇಗ marks the worker, as in many trade names.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ** (*akkasāliga*) is the Kannada name of the goldsmith; its origin is not settled.
