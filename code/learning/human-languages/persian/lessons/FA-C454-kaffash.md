## You'll want to know: کفاش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C454-KAFFASH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کفاش** (*kaffâsh*) — "a shoemaker, a cobbler".

> کفاش کفش پاره مرا دوخت.
>
> *Kaffâsh kafsh-e pâre-ye marâ dukht.*
>
> The cobbler stitched my torn shoe.

Built on *kafsh* 'shoe' in the Arabic pattern of trade names, like *najjâr* 'carpenter'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kaffâsh* is built on native *kafsh* 'shoe', from Middle Persian *kafš* (EtymDB), shaped in the Arabic pattern of trade names such as *najjâr* 'carpenter'.
