## You'll want to know: குயில்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C438-PARAVAI438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**குயில்** — *kuyil* — "a koel; an Indian cuckoo".

> காலையில் குயில் இனிமையாகப் பாடியது.
>
> *kālaiyil kuyil iṉimaiyākap pāṭiyatu.*
>
> In the morning the koel sang sweetly.

Its call is heard most in the hot months.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**குயில்** (*kuyil*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1764), "the koel, the Indian cuckoo".
