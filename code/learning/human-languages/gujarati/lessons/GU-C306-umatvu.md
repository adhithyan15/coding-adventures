## You'll want to know: ઊમટવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C306-RAHAT306-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊમટવું** — *ūmaṭvũ* — "to pour in; to throng (of people)".

> આખા શહેરમાંથી લોકો મદદ લઈને ઊમટ્યા.
>
> *ākhā śahermā̃thī loko madad laīne ūmaṭyā.*
>
> People poured in from all over the city, bringing help.

Past (it …): તે ટોળું ઊમટ્યું, *te ṭoḷũ ūmaṭyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊમટવું** (*ūmaṭvũ*) probably goes back to Sanskrit *únmṛṣṭa*, "rubbed off", and so "made clear": CDIAL lists Gujarati *umaṭvũ*, "to appear, come to light", there (CDIAL 2133). The sense of crowds that **ઊમટે છે**, pour out all at once, is the modern language's.
