## You'll want to know: поисковик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C399-SET-05]; assesses=[] -->

**поисковик** (*poiskovík*) — "a search engine".

> Я ищу ответ в поисковике.
>
> *Ya ishchú otvét v poiskovíke.*
>
> I am looking for the answer in a search engine.

Masculine, colloquial. Built on *поиск*, a search.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**поисковик** is built on *поисковый* "search", from *поиск* "a search", from *искать* "to look for", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*jĭskati* "to look for" (EtymDB).
