# ચમત્કાર (camatkār) — a miracle

## You'll want to know: ચમત્કાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C333-PARI333-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચમત્કાર** — *camatkār* — "a miracle".

> બધાએ કહ્યું કે આ ચમત્કાર છે.
>
> *badhāe kahyũ ke ā camatkār che.*
>
> Everyone said it was a miracle.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચમત્કાર** (*camatkār*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *camatkāra*, "astonishment, something wonderful" (Monier-Williams): what makes people cry out in wonder.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
