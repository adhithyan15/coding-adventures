Tamil, chapter 229: To praise, To scold, To dream, To separate, To throw.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பாராட்டு (pārāṭṭu) — to praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to hurry, then the Tamil for to love.

You'll want to know: பாராட்டு.
பாராட்டு — pārāṭṭu — "to praise".

The word, taken apart.
பாராட்டு (pārāṭṭu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4092 lists Malayalam pārāṭṭuka, "to extol".
