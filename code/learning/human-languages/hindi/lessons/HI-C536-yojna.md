## You'll want to know: योजना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C536-YOJNA536-01]; assesses=[] -->

**योजना** — *yojnā* — "a plan; a scheme".

> हमने गर्मी की छुट्टी के लिए एक योजना बनाई।
>
> *Hamne garmī kī chuṭṭī ke liye ek yojnā banāī.*
>
> We made a plan for the summer holiday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**योजना** (*yojnā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *yojanā*, "use, arrangement, preparation" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for a plan.
