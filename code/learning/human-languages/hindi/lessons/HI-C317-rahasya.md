## You'll want to know: रहस्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C317-RAHASYA317-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रहस्य** — *rahasya* — "a mystery; a secret".

> चोरी का रहस्य पाँच साल तक नहीं खुला।
>
> *corī kā rahasya pā̃c sāl tak nahīṃ khulā.*
>
> The mystery of the theft stayed unsolved for five years.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रहस्य** (*rahasya*) is Sanskrit *rahasya*, "secret, private" (Monier-Williams), from *rahas*, solitude. A mystery **खुलता है**, opens, when it is solved.
