## You'll want to know: उड़ान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C409-UDAN409-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उड़ान** — *uṛān* — "a flight".

> मेरी उड़ान रात दस बजे है।
>
> *merī uṛān rāt das baje hai.*
>
> My flight is at ten at night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उड़ान** (*uṛān*) is the noun of उड़ना, "to fly", which goes back to Sanskrit *uḍḍīyate*, "flies up" (CDIAL 1697); CDIAL does not list the noun itself.
