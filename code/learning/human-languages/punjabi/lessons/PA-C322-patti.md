## You'll want to know: ਪੱਟੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C322-HURT322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੱਟੀ** — *paṭṭī* — "a bandage".

> ਮੈਂ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਉੱਤੇ ਸਾਫ਼ ਪੱਟੀ ਬੰਨ੍ਹੀ।
>
> *maiṁ zakham utte sāf paṭṭī bannhī.*
>
> I tied a clean bandage over the wound.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੱਟੀ** (*paṭṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *paṭṭa*, "cloth; a bandage"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *paṭṭī*, "coarse woollen cloth, bandage", under it (CDIAL 7700).
