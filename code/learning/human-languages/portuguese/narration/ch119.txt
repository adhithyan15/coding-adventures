Portuguese, chapter 119: Squirrel, Owl, Whale, Dolphin, Elbow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o esquilo — a squirrel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a frog, then the Portuguese for a tortoise.

You'll want to know: o esquilo.
o esquilo — "a squirrel".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: o esquilo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: o esquilo, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say o esquilo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a frog, then the Portuguese for a tortoise, then say o esquilo again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o esquilo mean? ("A squirrel".) Say it once more.
