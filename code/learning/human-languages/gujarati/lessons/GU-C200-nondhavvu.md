# નોંધાવવું (nõdhāvvũ) — to register, to book

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C200-POST200-03, GU-LEX-C200-POST200-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for parcel, then the Gujarati for to put in.

## You'll want to know: નોંધાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C200-POST200-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નોંધાવવું** — *nõdhāvvũ* — "to register, to book".

> **મેં પત્ર નોંધાવ્યો.** — *mẽ patra nõdhāvyo* — "I had the letter registered."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નોંધાવવું** (*nõdhāvvũ*) is the causative of *nõdhvũ*, "to note down, enter in a book", which CDIAL lists under *\*nibandhati*, beside Sanskrit *níbadhnāti*, "binds on; writes down" (CDIAL 7223).
