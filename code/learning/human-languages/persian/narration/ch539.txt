Lesson 1 of 7.
آبیاری کردن (âbyâri kardan) — to water (plants).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to heat, then the Persian for to get cold.

You'll want to know: آبیاری کردن.
آبیاری کردن (âbyâri kardan) — "to water (plants)".
پدرم عصرها گلها را آبیاری میکند. pedaram 'asrhâ golhâ râ âbyâri mikonad. My father waters the flowers in the afternoons.
âbyâri 'irrigation' + kardan. In speech also âb dâdan.

The word, taken apart.
âbyâri is built on native âb, 'water', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB); the rest is usually explained as yâri, 'help': helping with water. kardan is inherited.

Lesson 2 of 7.
چمن زدن (chaman zadan) — to mow the lawn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get cold, then the Persian for to water.

You'll want to know: چمن زدن.
چمن زدن (chaman zadan) — "to mow the lawn".
همسایه ما هر هفته چمن میزند. hamsâye-ye mâ har hafte chaman mizanad. Our neighbour mows the lawn every week.
chaman 'lawn, grass' + zadan 'to strike'.

The word, taken apart.
chaman is the Persian word for a lawn; EtymDB lists it beside chamidan, 'to stroll' (EtymDB), but its origin is not settled. zadan, 'to strike', is inherited.

حیوان خانگی (heyvân-e khânegi) — "a pet".
ما یک گربه به عنوان حیوان خانگی داریم. mâ yek gorbe be 'onvân-e heyvân-e khânegi dârim. We have a cat as a pet.
heyvân 'animal' + khânegi 'of the home'.

The word, taken apart.
heyvân-e khânegi is built in Persian on heyvân, from Arabic ḥayawān, 'animal' (EtymDB), and khânegi, 'of the home', from native khâne, 'house'.

دخترم هر صبح به ماهیها غذا میدهد. dokhtaram har sobh be mâhihâ ghazâ midahad. My daughter feeds the fish every morning.
be kasi ghazâ dâdan; ghazâ 'food' + dâdan 'to give'.

The word, taken apart.
ghazâ comes from Arabic ġiḏāʾ, 'nourishment, food' (EtymDB). dâdan, 'to give', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 7.
دورهمی (dowrehami) — a get-together (of family or friends).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pet, then the Persian for to feed.

You'll want to know: دورهمی.
دورهمی (dowrehami) — "a get-together (of family or friends)".
آخر هفته ها در خانه مادربزرگ دورهمی داریم. âkhar-e haftehâ dar khâne-ye mâdarbozorg dowrehami dârim. At weekends we have a get-together at grandmother's house.
Colloquial: dowr-e ham 'around each other' + -i.

The word, taken apart.
dowrehami is built in Persian on dowr, 'circle, round', a loan usually traced to Arabic dawr, and native ham, 'together', with -i: sitting round together. EtymDB has no entry for it.
