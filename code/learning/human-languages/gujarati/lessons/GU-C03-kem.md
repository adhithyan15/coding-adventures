# કેમ (kem) — "how," a proper k- question

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-SHUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Where *shũ* ("what") hid its k-, this one wears it plainly — and it
opens the most Gujarati of greetings.

## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-CONCEPT-C03-KEM-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-SHUN-01] -->

**ક** (*ka*) + **ે** (the *e*-sign) + **મ** (*ma*) → **કેમ** (*kem*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-SHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C03-KEM-01] -->

**કેમ** (*kem*, "how"; also "why") is from the interrogative stem **ક-** (*ka-*),
PIE **\*kʷo-** — the same root behind English **wh-** and Latin *qu-*. It heads
the plain *k-* family Gujarati mostly kept: *kem* (how), *kōṇ* (who), *kyā̃*
(where), *kyāre* (when). And **કેમ છો?** (*kem chho?*, "how are you?") is, for
most Gujaratis, the real everyday hello — warmer than *namaste*.
