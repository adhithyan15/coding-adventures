# റെയിൽപ്പാളം (ṟeyilppāḷaṁ) — a railway track

## You'll want to know: റെയിൽപ്പാളം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C474-TIVANDI474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**റെയിൽപ്പാളം** — *ṟeyilppāḷaṁ* — "a railway track".

> റെയിൽപ്പാളം മുറിച്ചുകടക്കുന്നത് അപകടകരമാണ്.
>
> *ṟeyilppāḷaṁ muṟiccukaṭakkunnatŭ apakaṭakaramāṇŭ.*
>
> Crossing the railway track is dangerous.

In speech പാളം alone often means the whole railway track.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**റെയിൽപ്പാളം** (*ṟeyilppāḷaṁ*) is a Malayalam compound built from റെയിൽ, which was borrowed from English *rail*, and the native word പാളം, a cast metal bar (DEDR 4114).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
