# ず — す, voiced to zu

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-MIZU, JA-LEX-C134-BENKYOU, JA-LEX-C127-QUAL127-01] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Three recalls, then the sign.

- [YOU RECALL: say *water* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *study* — **R3**, twenty lessons back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *many*, *a lot* — **R4**, eighty lessons back]

## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-ZU-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SU-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01] -->

> **す** *su* + **゛** → **ず** *zu*

Write **す** in full: the horizontal, then the vertical with its loop. Then
add the two short strokes of the voicing mark at the upper right, the left
one first, clear of the horizontal.

As with **さ** and **ざ**, and **そ** and **ぞ**, the *s* sign voices to *z*:
*su* becomes *zu*.

With **み** in front of it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**みず**, *mizu*, water.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-ZU-01, JA-SCRIPT-SU-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-MIZU] -->

1. Write **す**, then **ず**, and say *su*, *zu*.
2. Write **さ ざ**, **し じ** and **す ず** in pairs, and say each one.
3. Write **みず** from memory and say *mizu*.
