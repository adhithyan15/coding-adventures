French, chapter 322: Le récit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le récit — the account, the story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the dragon, then the French for the fable.

You'll want to know: le récit.
le récit — "the account, the story".
Son récit de voyage était très drôle. His account of the trip was very funny.
Un récit de voyage — a travel story.

The word, taken apart.
récit is built on réciter "to recite" (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin recitāre "to read aloud".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la légende — the legend; the caption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the account, then the French for to narrate.

You'll want to know: la légende.
la légende — "the legend; the caption".
Dans la légende, un dragon vivait dans ce lac. In the legend, a dragon lived in this lake.
Under a photo or a map, la légende is the caption or the key.

The word, taken apart.
légende is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin legenda "things to be read", from legere "to read" (Lewis & Short).
