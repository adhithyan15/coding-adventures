## You'll want to know: نخرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C452-PRIDE452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نخرا** — *nakhrā* — "fussiness; airs and graces".

> یہ بچہ کھانے میں بڑے نخرے کرتا ہے۔
>
> *yih bachchā khāne meṅ baṛe nakhre kartā hai.*
>
> This child is very fussy about food.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نخرا** (*nakhrā*) is usually traced to Persian *nakhra*, "coquetry, airs".
