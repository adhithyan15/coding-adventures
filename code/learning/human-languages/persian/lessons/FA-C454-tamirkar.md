## You'll want to know: تعمیرکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C454-KAFFASH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تعمیرکار** (*ta'mirkâr*) — "a repairman, a repairer".

> تعمیرکار یخچال ما را درست کرد.
>
> *Ta'mirkâr yakhchâl-e mâ râ dorost kard.*
>
> The repairman fixed our fridge.

*ta'mir* 'repair' + *kâr* 'worker'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'mirkâr* is built from *ta'mir* 'repair', a loan usually traced to Arabic *taʿmīr*, and native *kâr* 'work, worker'.
