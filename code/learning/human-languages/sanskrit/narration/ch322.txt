Sanskrit, chapter 322: Ancestors.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पूर्वजः (pūrvajaḥ) — an ancestor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for anoints, then the Sanskrit for pronounces.

You'll want to know: पूर्वजः.
पूर्वजः — pūrvajaḥ — "an ancestor".
मम पूर्वजाः तस्मिन् ग्रामे वसन्ति स्म। mama pūrvajāḥ tasmin grāme vasanti sma. My ancestors used to live in that village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पूर्वजः (pūrvajaḥ) is built from pūrva, "earlier, former", and ja, "born", from the root √jan; MW gives pūrvaja as born before, and an ancestor or forefather (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
वंशः (vaṃśaḥ) — a family line; a dynasty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for pronounces, then the Sanskrit for an ancestor.

You'll want to know: वंशः.
वंशः — vaṃśaḥ — "a family line; a dynasty".
सः राजा प्राचीनस्य वंशस्य अस्ति। saḥ rājā prācīnasya vaṃśasya asti. That king comes from an ancient line.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वंशः (vaṃśaḥ) is built on the image of a bamboo cane: MW explains the sense of a lineage, race or dynasty from the line of joints in a bamboo (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कुलम् (kulam) — a clan; a family.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an ancestor, then the Sanskrit for a family line.

You'll want to know: कुलम्.
कुलम् — kulam — "a clan; a family".
तस्य कुलं प्रसिद्धम् अस्ति। tasya kulaṃ prasiddham asti. His clan is famous.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
कुलम् (kulam) MW gives kula as a herd, troop or multitude, and also a race, family, community or noble house, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a family line, then the Sanskrit for a clan.

You'll want to know: संस्थापयति.
संस्थापयति — saṃsthāpayati — "founds; establishes".
राजा नवं नगरं संस्थापयति। rājā navaṃ nagaraṃ saṃsthāpayati. The king founds a new city.
Past (he …): सः नगरं संस्थापितवान्, saḥ nagaraṃ saṃsthāpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
संस्थापयति (saṃsthāpayati) is built from sam and the causative of the root √sthā, "to stand", as in sthāpayati; MW gives saṃ-sthāpayati as to make stand firm, raise up, restore (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

प्रवर्तयति — pravartayati — "starts; introduces".
राजा नवम् उत्सवं प्रवर्तयति। rājā navam utsavaṃ pravartayati. The king starts a new festival.
Past (he …): सः उत्सवं प्रवर्तितवान्, saḥ utsavaṃ pravartitavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रवर्तयति (pravartayati) is built from pra and the causative of the root √vṛt, "to turn, roll"; MW gives pravartita as set in motion, set up, established, introduced (MW).
