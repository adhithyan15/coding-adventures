## You'll want to know: ていど
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C367-HAKKIRI367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ていど** — *teido* — "a degree; an extent".

> **くすりは ねつの ていどに よります。** — *kusuri wa netsu no teido ni yorimasu* — "The medicine depends on how high the fever is."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ていど** (*teido*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tei*, "extent" (Middle Chinese *drjeng*) and *do*, "degrees" (*duH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
