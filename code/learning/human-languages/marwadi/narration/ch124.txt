Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 124: Comb, Soap, Towel, Needle, Thread.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कांगसी (kā̃gsī) — a comb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a curtain, then the Marwadi for a mirror.

You'll want to know: कांगसी.
कांगसी — kā̃gsī — "a comb".

The word, taken apart.
कांगसी (kā̃gsī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kaṅkata, "comb". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2598); the match is through Gujarati kā̃gsī. CDIAL notes that the Gujarati form swapped its sounds, and calls the further origin of the Sanskrit word doubtful.

Lesson 2 of 5.
साबण (sābaṇ) — soap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a mirror, then the Marwadi for a comb.

You'll want to know: साबण.
साबण — sābaṇ — "soap".

The word, taken apart.
साबण (sābaṇ) is a loan, usually traced to Arabic ṣābūn, "soap", which reached Marwadi through Persian; its Arabic root is ṣ-b-n (Lane). Portuguese sabão is another proposed source.
