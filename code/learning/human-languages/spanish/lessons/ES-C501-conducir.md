## You'll want to know: conducir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C501-CAMINO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**conducir** — "to drive".

> Conduzco al trabajo.
>
> *I drive to work.*

It takes *zc* in the first person present: *conduzco*. In much of Latin America people say *manejar*.
