# అభ్యసించు (abhyasiñcu) — to study closely; to practise

## You'll want to know: అభ్యసించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C545-SADHANA545-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అభ్యసించు** — *abhyasiñcu* — "to study closely; to practise".

> అతను రోజూ రెండు గంటలు సంగీతం అభ్యసిస్తాడు.
>
> *atanu rōjū reṇḍu gaṇṭalu saṅgītaṁ abhyasistāḍu.*
>
> He practises music for two hours every day.

Past (I …): అభ్యసించాను, *abhyasiñcānu*.

Formal; అభ్యాసం is practice or exercise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అభ్యసించు** (*abhyasiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *abhyasana*, "practice, exercise" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
