Portuguese, chapter 278: O noticiário da noite.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o noticiário — the news bulletin, the news programme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for daily, then the Portuguese for beforehand.

You'll want to know: o noticiário.
o noticiário — "the news bulletin, the news programme".
Ouço o noticiário na rádio todas as manhãs. I listen to the news on the radio every morning.
On radio or TV; a single piece of news is uma notícia.

The word, taken apart.
noticiário is built on notícia "news", from Latin nōtitia "knowledge, a notice", from nōscere "to get to know" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
em direto — live (on air).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the TV news, then the Portuguese for the presenter.

You'll want to know: em direto.
em direto — "live (on air)".
O jogo passa em direto na televisão. The match is on live on television.
In Brazil: ao vivo. Before the 1990 spelling reform, em directo.

The word, taken apart.
direto is a learned borrowing from Latin dīrēctus "straight, direct", the past participle of dīrigere "to set straight", built on regere "to guide" (Lewis & Short); the inherited form of the same word is direito.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a transmissão — the broadcast, the transmission.

You'll want to know: a transmissão.
a transmissão — "the broadcast, the transmission".
A transmissão do concerto começou tarde. The broadcast of the concert started late.
Also passing on an illness: a transmissão do vírus.

The word, taken apart.
transmissão was borrowed from Latin trānsmissiō "a sending across" (EtymDB), from trānsmittere, built on mittere "to send".
