## You'll want to know: वहम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C556-SANDEH556-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वहम** — *vaham* — "a groundless fear; a fancy".

> यह तुम्हारा वहम है, कोई चोर नहीं है।
>
> *Yah tumhārā vaham hai, koī cor nahīṃ hai.*
>
> That is only in your head; there is no thief.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वहम** (*vaham*) is usually traced to Arabic *wahm*, "a fancy, a supposition", through Persian.
