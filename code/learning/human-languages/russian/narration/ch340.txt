Russian, chapter 340: Agreeing and objecting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
соглашаться (soglashátsya) — to agree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for confidence, then the Russian for to doubt.

You'll want to know: соглашаться.
соглашаться (soglashátsya) — "to agree".
Я не всегда соглашаюсь с отцом. Ya ne vsegdá soglasháyus s ottsóm. I do not always agree with my father.
Imperfective; perfective согласиться. Соглашаться с + instrumental (with a person or a view), на + accusative (to accept an offer).

The word, taken apart.
соглашаться (soglashátsya) is built on глас "voice", the Church Slavonic form of native голос; both come from Proto-Slavic golsŭ "voice" (EtymDB). The -ла- is the Church Slavonic shape where Russian has -оло-: to agree is to speak with one voice.

Lesson 2 of 5.
согласие (soglásiye) — agreement, consent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to doubt, then the Russian for to agree.

You'll want to know: согласие.
согласие (soglásiye) — "agreement, consent".
Родители дали согласие на свадьбу. Rodíteli dáli soglásiye na svádbu. The parents gave their consent to the wedding.
Neuter. Согласие на + accusative — consent to. В согласии с — in agreement with.

The word, taken apart.
согласие (soglásiye) is built from со- "together" and глас "voice", which Russian took from Church Slavonic; the native form of the same Proto-Slavic word, golsŭ, is голос (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
возражать (vozrazhát) — to object.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for agreement, then the Russian for disagreement.

You'll want to know: возражать.
возражать (vozrazhát) — "to object".
Если никто не возражает, мы начнём. Yésli niktó ne vozrazháyet, my nachnyóm. If nobody objects, we will begin.
Imperfective; the perfective is возразить (возражу, возразишь). Возражать против + genitive. Я не возражаю — I do not mind.

The word, taken apart.
возражать (vozrazhát) is built from воз- "back, against" and the root of разить "to strike" (EtymDB lists razítĭ "to strike"). To object is to strike back with words.

Lesson 5 of 5.
возражение (vozrazhéniye) — an objection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for disagreement, then the Russian for to object.

You'll want to know: возражение.
возражение (vozrazhéniye) — "an objection".
Возражения есть? Нет? Тогда решили. Vozrazhéniya yest? Net? Togdá reshíli. Any objections? No? Then it is decided.
Neuter. Без возражений — without objection.

The word, taken apart.
возражение (vozrazhéniye) is built on возразить "to object" with the noun suffix -ение; the root is that of разить "to strike" (EtymDB).
