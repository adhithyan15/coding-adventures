## You'll want to know: はんけつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C273-SAIBAN273-03]; assesses=[] -->

**はんけつ** — *hanketsu* — "a verdict; a judgement".

> **はんけつは らいしゅうです。** — *hanketsu wa raishū desu* — "The verdict is next week."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はんけつ** (*hanketsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *han*, "judgement" (Middle Chinese *phanH*) and *ketsu*, "decide" (*kwet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
