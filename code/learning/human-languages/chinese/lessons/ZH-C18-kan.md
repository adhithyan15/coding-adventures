# 看 — the familiar sound meets the shape

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-KAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Say **look at or watch** in Mandarin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-KAN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01] -->

> **看** — *kàn* — **look at; watch**

Hide the pinyin. Read 看 from the hand-over-eye shape, say the falling tone,
then give the meaning. One form now connects listening, speaking, reading, and
writing.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Hear *kàn*. Wait three seconds, then write **看** once without a model.
