## You'll want to know: хлопушка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C314-SALYUT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**хлопушка** (*khlopúshka*) — "a party popper, a cracker".

> Хлопушка громко хлопнула.
>
> *Khlopúshka grómko khlópnula.*
>
> The party popper went off with a loud bang.

Feminine; genitive plural *хлопушек*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хлопушка* is built on *хлопать* "to bang, to clap", a word that imitates the sound; EtymDB lists no parent.
