Russian, chapter 488: A Sleepless Night.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
бессонница (bessónnitsa) — insomnia.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a vaccination, then the Russian for to get infected.

You'll want to know: бессонница.
бессонница (bessónnitsa) — "insomnia".
Из-за стресса у меня бессонница. Iz-za stréssa u menyá bessónnitsa. I've got insomnia because of stress.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
бессонница (bessónnitsa) is built on бессонный 'sleepless', from без 'without' and сон 'sleep', with -ница; EtymDB lists бессонный without a source and has no entry for бессонница (bessónnitsa).

Lesson 2 of 5.
храпеть (khrapét) — to snore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get infected, then the Russian for insomnia.

You'll want to know: храпеть.
храпеть (khrapét) — "to snore".
Мой муж так храпит, что я не могу заснуть. Moy muzh tak khrapít, chto ya ne mogú zasnút. My husband snores so loudly that I can't get to sleep.
Imperfective; present храплю, храпишь (п becomes пл). The noun is храп — snoring.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists храпеть (khrapét), glossed 'to snore', without a source; it is usually taken as imitative.

Lesson 4 of 5.
кошмар (koshmár) — a nightmare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to snore, then the Russian for to yawn.

You'll want to know: кошмар.
кошмар (koshmár) — "a nightmare".
Ночью мне приснился кошмар. Nóchyu mne prisnílsya koshmár. I had a nightmare in the night.
Masculine. Colloquially also an exclamation: Какой кошмар (koshmár)! — How awful!

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists кошмар (koshmár) without a source; it is usually traced to French cauchemar 'nightmare'.

Lesson 5 of 5.

дремать (dremát) — "to doze".
Дедушка дремлет в кресле. Dédushka drémlet v krésle. Grandad is dozing in the armchair.
Imperfective; present дремлю, дремлешь (м becomes мл). Perfective задремать — to doze off.

The word, taken apart.
