Lesson 1 of 5.
மூங்கில் (mūṅkil) — bamboo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a butterfly, then the Tamil for a bee.

You'll want to know: மூங்கில்.
மூங்கில் — mūṅkil — "bamboo".

The word, taken apart.
மூங்கில் (mūṅkil) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5028 lists Malayalam mūṅkil.
