## You'll want to know: સીમા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C406-BORDER406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સીમા** — *sīmā* — "a border; a boundary".

> આ ગામ સીમાની નજીક છે.
>
> *ā gām sīmānī najīk che.*
>
> This village is near the border.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સીમા** (*sīmā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sīmā*, "a boundary, a border" (Monier-Williams): the line where one land ends and the next begins.
