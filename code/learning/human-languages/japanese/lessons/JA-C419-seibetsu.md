## You'll want to know: せいべつ

**せいべつ** — *seibetsu* — "sex (on a form)".

> **せいべつの らんも あります。** — *seibetsu no ran mo arimasu* — "There is a box for sex as well."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいべつ** (*seibetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "sex" (Middle Chinese *sjengH*) and *betsu*, "separate" (*bjet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
