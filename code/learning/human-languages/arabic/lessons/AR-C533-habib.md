## You'll want to know: حبيب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C533-RIAYA533-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حبيب** — *ḥabīb* — "a loved one, a darling".

> سافر حبيبها وانتظرته سنة.
>
> *sāfara ḥabībuhā wa-intaẓarathu sana.*
>
> Her sweetheart went away and she waited a year for him.

Plural: أحباب, *aḥbāb*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حبيب** (*ḥabīb*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-b-b*, "to love" (Lane). Ḥubb, love, and aḥabba, to love, which you know, share the root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
