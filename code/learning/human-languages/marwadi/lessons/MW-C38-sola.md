## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-SOLA-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-SA-01, MW-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-LLA-01] -->

> **सो** + **ळा** → **सोळा** — *soḷā* — sixteen

**सो** is already a word in this book: a hundred. Sixteen is that syllable with
**ळा** after it — which is why the retroflex letter had to come first.

Write **सो** and **सोळा** one above the other. Two signs apart, a hundred and
sixteen.
