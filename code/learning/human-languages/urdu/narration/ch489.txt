Urdu, chapter 489: More of the Family.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دیور (devar) — a husband's younger brother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a son-in-law, then the Urdu for to marry off.

You'll want to know: دیور.
دیور — devar — "a husband's younger brother".
میرا دیور کالج میں پڑھتا ہے۔ merā devar kālij meṅ paṛhtā hai. My husband's younger brother is at college.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
دیور (devar) is inherited from Sanskrit dēvara, "husband's younger brother" (CDIAL 6546).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

میرے جیٹھ ڈاکٹر ہیں۔ mere jeṭh ḍākṭar haiṅ. My husband's elder brother is a doctor.

The word, taken apart.
جیٹھ (jeṭh) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jeṭh, "the eldest", under Sanskrit jyḗṣṭha, "first, eldest" (CDIAL 5286).
