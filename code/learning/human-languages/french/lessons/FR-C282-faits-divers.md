## You'll want to know: les faits divers
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C282-TITRES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**les faits divers** — "short news items, minor news stories".

> Il aime lire les faits divers.
>
> *He likes reading the short news items.*

Small stories: thefts, odd events, accidents. Singular: *un fait divers*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fait* is inherited from Latin *factum* "a deed" (Lewis & Short), and *divers* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dīversus* "turned different ways" (EtymDB).
