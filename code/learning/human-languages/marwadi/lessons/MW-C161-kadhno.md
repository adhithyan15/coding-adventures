# काढणो (kāḍhṇo) — to take out

## You'll want to know: काढणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C161-VERB161-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C161-MHAI-AGENT-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C161-OBJ-AGREE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01] -->

**काढणो** — *kāḍhṇo* — "to take out; to draw (water)".

> **म्हैं पाणी काढ्यो।**
>
> *mhaĩ pāṇī kāḍhyo.* — I drew water.

> **वो रोज पाणी काढै है।**
>
> *vo roj pāṇī kāḍhai hai.* — He draws water every day.

It holds a letter you have not written yet: *ḍh*, a **ड** with a breath after it.
The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काढणो** (*kāḍhṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed verb, *kaḍḍhati*, "pulls,
draws". CDIAL lists Old Marwari *kāḍhaï*, "to pull out" (CDIAL 2660).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C161-VERB161-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01] -->

Its stem **काढ** ends in a consonant: **काढूं हूं**, **काढै है**, and in the past,
with **म्हैं**, **काढ्यो**.
