# പ്രതിഷേധം (pratiṣēdhaṁ) — a protest

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C424-ADHIKARAM424-04, ML-LEX-C424-ADHIKARAM424-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for authority, then the Malayalam for a leader.

## You'll want to know: പ്രതിഷേധം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C425-SAMARAM425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രതിഷേധം** — *pratiṣēdhaṁ* — "a protest".

> വില കൂടിയതിനെതിരെ നഗരത്തിൽ പ്രതിഷേധം നടന്നു.
>
> *vila kūṭiyatinetire nagarattil pratiṣēdhaṁ naṭannu.*
>
> There was a protest in the city against the price rise.

Against something: -ന് എതിരെ പ്രതിഷേധം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രതിഷേധം** (*pratiṣēdhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pratiṣedha*, "keeping back, warding off, prohibition" (Monier-Williams). A protest pushes back against a decision.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
