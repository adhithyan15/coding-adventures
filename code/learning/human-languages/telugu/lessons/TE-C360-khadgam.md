## You'll want to know: ఖడ్గం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C360-AYUDHAM360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖడ్గం** — *khaḍgaṁ* — "a sword".

> సైనికుడు ఖడ్గం పట్టుకుని నిలబడ్డాడు.
>
> *sainikuḍu khaḍgaṁ paṭṭukuni nilabaḍḍāḍu.*
>
> The soldier stood holding a sword.

A word of stories; at home a knife is కత్తి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖడ్గం** (*khaḍgaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *khaḍga*, "a sword" (CDIAL 3787).
