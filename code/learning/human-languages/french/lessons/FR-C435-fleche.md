## You'll want to know: la flèche
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C435-PANNEAUX-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la flèche** — "the arrow".

> Il suit les flèches jusqu'au parking.
>
> *He follows the arrows to the car park.*

*Partir en flèche* — to shoot off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flèche* comes from Old French *fleche*, which goes back to a Frankish word for 'arrow' (EtymDB).
