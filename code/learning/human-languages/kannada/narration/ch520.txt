Kannada, chapter 520: Teamwork.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತೊಡಗು (toḍagu) — to get involved; to set to work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to assign, then the Kannada for to save up.

You'll want to know: ತೊಡಗು.
ತೊಡಗು — toḍagu — "to get involved; to set to work".
ಊರಿನ ಯುವಕರು ಕೆರೆಯ ಕೆಲಸದಲ್ಲಿ ತೊಡಗಿದರು. ūrina yuvakaru kereya kelasadalli toḍagidaru. The young men of the town set to work on the tank.
Past (I …): ತೊಡಗಿದೆ, toḍagide.
In what: -ಅಲ್ಲಿ. ತೊಡಗಿಸಿಕೊ, to get yourself involved.

The word, taken apart.
ತೊಡಗು (toḍagu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3481 lists Kannada toḍagu, "to engage (as in work), begin, undertake".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವಹಿಸು (vahisu) — to take charge of; to take on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to save up, then the Kannada for to get involved.

You'll want to know: ವಹಿಸು.
ವಹಿಸು — vahisu — "to take charge of; to take on".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ಊಟದ ಜವಾಬ್ದಾರಿಯನ್ನು ಮಾವ ವಹಿಸಿದರು. habbada ūṭada javābdāriyannu māva vahisidaru. Uncle took charge of the festival meal.
Past (I …): ವಹಿಸಿದೆ, vahiside.
Often with ಜವಾಬ್ದಾರಿ or ಕಾಳಜಿ: ಕಾಳಜಿ ವಹಿಸು (vahisu), to take care.

The word, taken apart.
ವಹಿಸು (vahisu) is built on the Sanskrit root vah, "to carry, to bear" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu: to shoulder a task.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪಳಗು (paḷagu) — to get practised; to become skilled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to get involved, then the Kannada for to take charge of.

You'll want to know: ಪಳಗು.
ಪಳಗು — paḷagu — "to get practised; to become skilled".
ಎರಡು ವರ್ಷದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನು ಈ ಕೆಲಸದಲ್ಲಿ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ಪಳಗಿದನು. eraḍu varṣadalli avanu ī kelasadalli cennāgi paḷagidanu. In two years he became well practised in this work.
Past (I …): ಪಳಗಿದೆ, paḷagide.
In what: -ಅಲ್ಲಿ. ಪಳಗಿಸು, to tame or train an animal.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಳಗು (paḷagu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4000 lists older Kannada paḻagu, "to become used to, to be trained", and paḻaka, "use, practice, habit", beside Tamil paḻaku.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆ (mēlvicāraṇe) — supervision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to take charge of, then the Kannada for to get practised.

You'll want to know: ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆ.
ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆ — mēlvicāraṇe — "supervision".
ಎಂಜಿನಿಯರ್ ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆಲಸ ನಡೆಯಿತು. eñjiniyar mēlvicāraṇeyalli kelasa naḍeyitu. The work went on under the engineer's supervision.
ಮೇಲ್, over + ವಿಚಾರಣೆ, looking into: overseeing.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆ (mēlvicāraṇe) joins native mēl, "above, over" (DEDR 5086), and Sanskrit vicāraṇa, "consideration, examination" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಮಯಪಾಲನೆ (samayapālane) — punctuality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to get practised, then the Kannada for supervision.

You'll want to know: ಸಮಯಪಾಲನೆ.
ಸಮಯಪಾಲನೆ — samayapālane — "punctuality".
ಸಮಯಪಾಲನೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ನೌಕರನ ಗುಣ. samayapālane oḷḷeya naukarana guṇa. Punctuality is the mark of a good employee.
ಸಮಯ, time + ಪಾಲನೆ, keeping.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮಯಪಾಲನೆ (samayapālane) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit samaya, "a meeting, an agreed time" (Monier-Williams), and pālana, "protecting, keeping" (CDIAL 8128).
