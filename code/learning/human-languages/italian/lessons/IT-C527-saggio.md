## You'll want to know: il saggio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C527-GENERI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il saggio** — "essay; non-fiction book".

> Ha scritto un saggio sulla storia della musica.
>
> *She wrote an essay on the history of music.*

As an adjective, *saggio* means wise; that word has a different origin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saggio* "essay" comes from Late Latin *exagium* "a weighing, a test" (EtymDB): an essay tries out an idea.
