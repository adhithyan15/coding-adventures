## You'll want to know: छांटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C167-KAM167-03]; assesses=[] -->

**छांटणो** — *chhā̃ṭṇo* — "to sprinkle".

> दिनूगै मां पाणी छांटै है।
>
> *dinūgai mā̃ pāṇī chhā̃ṭai hai.*
>
> Early in the morning my mother sprinkles water.

Past (I …): म्हैं पाणी छांट्यो, *mhaĩ pāṇī chhā̃ṭyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छांटणो** (*chhā̃ṭṇo*) comes from a reconstructed *\*chaṇṭ*, "scatter"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *chā̃ṭvũ*, "to sprinkle" (CDIAL 4970). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
