5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મૂલ્ય (mūlya) — a moral value; worth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for valid, then the Gujarati for to preserve.

You'll want to know: મૂલ્ય.
મૂલ્ય — mūlya — "a moral value; worth".
અમારા કુટુંબમાં સાચું બોલવું એ મોટું મૂલ્ય છે. amārā kuṭumbmā̃ sācũ bolvũ e moṭũ mūlya che. In our family, telling the truth matters a great deal.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
મૂલ્ય (mūlya) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit mūlya, "price, worth" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati also uses it for what people hold dear.

Lesson 5 of 5.
અપનાવવું (apnāvvũ) — to adopt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a policy, then the Gujarati for a social practice.

You'll want to know: અપનાવવું.
અપનાવવું — apnāvvũ — "to adopt".
આપણે સારી ટેવો અપનાવવી જોઈએ. āpaṇe sārī ṭevo apnāvvī joīe. We should take up good habits.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નવી રીત અપનાવી, teṇe navī rīt apnāvī.

The word, taken apart.
અપનાવવું (apnāvvũ) is probably borrowed from Hindi apnānā, "to make one's own", built on Hindi apnā, "own", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed ātmanaka, "own", under Sanskrit ātmán, "self" (CDIAL 1135). Gujarati આપણું (āpṇũ) comes from the same source. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of the verb.
