Lesson 1 of 5.
il conto corrente — a bank account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a lie, then the Italian for a secret.

You'll want to know: il conto corrente.
il conto corrente — "a bank account".

The phrase, taken apart.
conto corrente is "a running account": conto from Vulgar Latin contus, "a reckoning", and corrente, "running" from Latin currens. (EtymDB 2.1).
