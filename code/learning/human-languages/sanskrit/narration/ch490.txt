Sanskrit, chapter 490: Age and Appearance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गौरः (gauraḥ) — pale; whitish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gives up entirely, then the Sanskrit for liquor.

You'll want to know: गौरः.
गौरः — gauraḥ — "pale; whitish".
शिशुः गौरः सुन्दरः च अस्ति। śiśuḥ gauraḥ sundaraḥ ca asti. The baby is fair and lovely.

The word, taken apart.
गौरः (gauraḥ) MW gives "white, yellowish, reddish, pale red"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
रूपम् (rūpam) — appearance; beauty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for grey-haired, then the Sanskrit for a wrinkle.

You'll want to know: रूपम्.
रूपम् — rūpam — "appearance; beauty".
मीरायाः रूपं मातुः इव अस्ति। mīrāyāḥ rūpaṃ mātuḥ iva asti. Mira looks like her mother.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
रूपम् (rūpam) MW suggests, with a "perhaps", a link with varpa, varpas, "form"; it gives "form, shape, figure" and "loveliness, beauty", and its origin is not settled (MW).
