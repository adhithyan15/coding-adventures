## You'll want to know: しょうにん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C487-SHOP487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうにん** — *shōnin* — "a merchant; a trader".

> **しょうにんが いちばで やさいを うります。** — *shōnin ga ichiba de yasai o urimasu* — "Traders sell vegetables at the market."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうにん** (*shōnin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "merchant" (Middle Chinese *syang*) and *nin*, "person" (*nyin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
