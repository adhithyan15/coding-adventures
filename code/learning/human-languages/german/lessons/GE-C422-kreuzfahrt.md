## You'll want to know: die Kreuzfahrt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C422-KREUZFAHRT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Kreuzfahrt** — "the cruise".

> Meine Großeltern machen im Sommer eine Kreuzfahrt.
>
> *My grandparents are going on a cruise in the summer.*

Plural: *die Kreuzfahrten*. *Das Kreuzfahrtschiff* — the cruise ship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kreuzfahrt* is built from *kreuzen* 'to cruise, to sail back and forth', from *Kreuz* 'cross', which goes back to Latin *crux* (Lewis & Short), and *Fahrt* 'voyage', from *fahren* (EtymDB).
