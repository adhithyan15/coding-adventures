## You'll want to know: sǎomǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C202-TILL202-01]; assesses=[] -->

*sǎomǎ* — "to scan a code (to pay)".

> *kěyǐ sǎomǎ ma* — Can I pay by scanning the code?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: sǎo is said with a rising tone before mǎ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sǎomǎ* is built from 扫 'to sweep' and *mǎ* 'a code', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sawX* and *maeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The pair is recent, and how sweeping came to mean scanning is not settled.
