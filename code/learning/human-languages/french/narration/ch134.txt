French, chapter 134: Grass, Ear, Lip, Number (on a door, Address.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'herbe — grass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a storm, then the French for fog.

You'll want to know: l'herbe.
l'herbe — "grass".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: l'herbe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does l'herbe mean? ("Grass".) Say it once more.
