## You'll want to know: సంపాదకుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C341-SAMPADAKUDU341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సంపాదకుడు** — *sampādakuḍu* — "an editor".

> సంపాదకుడు ఆ శీర్షిక మార్చాడు.
>
> *sampādakuḍu ā śīrṣika mārcāḍu.*
>
> The editor changed that headline.

Plural: సంపాదకులు, *sampādakulu*.

The person who puts a paper together each day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంపాదకుడు** (*sampādakuḍu*) is built on Sanskrit *sampādaka*, "producing, bringing about" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu ending *-uḍu*: the one who brings the paper about.
