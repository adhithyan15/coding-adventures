## Fixed — writing lessons that print a filmstrip now point the learner at it

22 writing lessons print a stroke-order filmstrip in the book, but
their prose still said the book gave no stroke order ("this book does not yet
tell you where to start", "copy what you see"). That was true when the lessons
were written and stopped being true when the cited stroke data arrived.

- **Rewritten:** the Writing block now says to follow the numbered strip —
  start where movement 1 begins and let each panel show the next movement —
  and that the strip is one attested order with its source printed beneath it,
  not the only one. Where the heading said "copy what you see", it now says
  "follow the numbered strip".
- **Lessons:** TA-S101-letter-pa, TA-S102-vowel-sign-oo, TA-S112-letter-e,
  TA-S114-vowel-sign-aa, TA-S115-letter-llla, TA-S116-letter-ca,
  TA-S117-letter-ra, TA-S118-letter-tta, TA-S122-letter-ta,
  TA-S123-vowel-sign-ee, TA-S124-letter-ya, TA-S125-letter-u,
  TA-S128-vowel-sign-ai, TA-S132-vowel-sign-ii, TA-S133-vowel-sign-e,
  TA-W07-write-sari, TA-W08-read-en, TA-W08-short-o-observe, TA-W09-read-peyar,
  TA-W11-read-niingal, TA-W16-read-tamizh, TA-W18-read-uur.
- **Word lessons:** TA-W07, TA-W08-read-en, TA-W09, TA-W11, TA-W16 and TA-W18
  told the reader not to draw their letters because none had a sourced stroke
  order or an entry in the script data. Every letter in those words now has
  both, and the book prints a strip for each word, so the notes now point at
  the strip. TA-W09's note that ெ had no entry of its own is replaced by its
  cited placement (written before the consonant); TA-W11 and TA-W16 now say
  which sound descriptions come from the data, and TA-W18 that its source
  builds ஊ from உ and ள. TA-W08's
  short-*o* observe lesson says the strip gives the sign's written order.
- Lessons that print no filmstrip keep their "no source yet" wording.
- Narration and modality were regenerated. Every edited lesson stays under
  the 300-second duration budget, and no declared duration changed.
