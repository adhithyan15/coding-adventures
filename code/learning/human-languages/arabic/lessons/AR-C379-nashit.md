## You'll want to know: ناشط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C379-HIZB379-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ناشط** — *nāshiṭ* — "an activist".

> تعمل الناشطة مع الأطفال الفقراء.
>
> *taʿmalu an-nāshiṭa maʿa al-aṭfāl al-fuqarāʾ.*
>
> The activist works with poor children.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ناشط** (*nāshiṭ*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-sh-ṭ*, "to be brisk, lively" (Lane). *Nāshiṭ* is the active participle: one who is active, an activist. نشيط, active, is from the same root.
