Arabic, chapter 386: Working together.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعاون (taʿāwana) — to cooperate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a council, then the Arabic for a member.

You'll want to know: تعاون.
تعاون — taʿāwana — "to cooperate".
تعاون الجيران لتنظيف الحديقة. taʿāwana al-jīrān li-tanẓīf al-ḥadīqa. The neighbours cooperated to clean the park.
Present: يتعاون, yataʿāwan.

The word, taken apart.
تعاون (taʿāwana) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-w-n, "to help, to aid" (Lane). It is Form VI, to help one another: to cooperate.

Lesson 3 of 5.
صادق (ṣādaqa) — to ratify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to cooperate, then the Arabic for to negotiate.

You'll want to know: صادق.
صادق — ṣādaqa — "to ratify".
صادق البرلمان على الاتفاق. ṣādaqa al-barlamān ʿalā al-ittifāq. Parliament ratified the agreement.
Present: يصادق, yuṣādiq.
It takes على: صادق (ṣādaqa) على …. A friend, صديق, is from the same root.

The word, taken apart.
صادق (ṣādaqa) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-d-q, "to be truthful" (Lane). It is Form III; ṣādaqa ʿalā is to confirm a thing as true: to ratify.
