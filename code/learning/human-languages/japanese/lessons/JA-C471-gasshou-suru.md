## You'll want to know: がっしょうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C471-STAGE471-04]; assesses=[] -->

**がっしょうする** — *gasshō suru* — "to sing in a choir".

> **がっこうで みんなで がっしょうしました。** — *gakkō de minna de gasshō shimashita* — "We all sang together as a choir at school."

Polite **がっしょうします**, *gasshōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **こどもが がっしょうしました。**, *kodomo ga gasshō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がっしょうする** (*gasshō suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *gasshō* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ga*, "join" (Middle Chinese *hop*) and *shō*, "chant" (*tsyhangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
