## You'll want to know: احتج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C382-IHTIJAJ382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**احتج** — *iḥtajja* — "to protest".

> احتج السكان على إغلاق الحديقة.
>
> *iḥtajja as-sukkān ʿalā ighlāq al-ḥadīqa.*
>
> The residents protested against closing the park.

Present: يحتج, *yaḥtajj*.

It takes على: احتج على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتج** (*iḥtajja*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-j-j*, "to make for; to overcome in argument" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to bring an argument against: to protest.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
