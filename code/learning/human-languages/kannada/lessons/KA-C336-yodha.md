## You'll want to know: ಯೋಧ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C336-SAINYA336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಯೋಧ** — *yōdha* — "a warrior".

> ಆ ಯೋಧ ಯುದ್ಧದಲ್ಲಿ ಊರನ್ನು ಕಾಪಾಡಿದನು.
>
> *ā yōdha yuddhadalli ūrannu kāpāḍidanu.*
>
> That warrior protected the town in the war.

Plural: ಯೋಧರು, *yōdharu*.

Today it is also a respectful word for a soldier: ನಮ್ಮ ಯೋಧರು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಯೋಧ** (*yōdha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *yodha*, "a fighter, a warrior, a soldier" (Monier-Williams), from the root *yudh*, "to fight", as in ಯುದ್ಧ.
