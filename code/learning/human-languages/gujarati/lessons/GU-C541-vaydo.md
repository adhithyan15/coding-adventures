## You'll want to know: વાયદો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C541-SHAPATH541-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વાયદો** — *vāydo* — "an undertaking (to do something by a time)".

> જો તમે વાયદો કરશો તો તમારે તે પાળવો પડશે.
>
> *jo tame vāydo karśo to tamāre te pāḷvo paḍśe.*
>
> If you make a promise, you will have to keep it.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Arabic *waʿda*, "a promise", through Persian. **વચન** is one's word; **વાયદો** is often a promise with a date in it.
