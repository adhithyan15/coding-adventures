(तत्क्षणम्, tatkṣaṇam.) Formerly? (प्राक्, prāk.) Afterwards? (अनन्तरम्, anantaram.) As long as? (यावत्, yāvat.) Exactly? (एव, eva.) Indeed? (खलु, khalu.) Like? (इव, iva.) Without? (विना, vinā.) Towards? (प्रति, prati.) Moving? (चलः, calaḥ.) Slow? (मन्दः, mandaḥ.) Sharp? (तीक्ष्णः, tīkṣṇaḥ.) Blunt? (कुण्ठः, kuṇṭhaḥ.) Valuable? (मूल्यवान्, mūlyavān.)
