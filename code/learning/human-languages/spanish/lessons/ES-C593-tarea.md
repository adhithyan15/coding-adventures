## You'll want to know: la tarea
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C593-TAREA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la tarea** — "the task; homework".

> Mi tarea es hablar con los clientes.
>
> *My task is to talk to the customers.*

In Latin America *la tarea* is also school homework; Spain says *los deberes*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tarea* was borrowed from Andalusian Arabic *ṭaríḥa*, a set amount of work (EtymDB).
