## You'll want to know: مطابق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C351-MUTALAA351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مطابق** — *mutābiq* — "according to (کے مطابق)".

> آپ کے مشورے کے مطابق میں نے دوا لی۔
>
> *āp ke mashvare ke mutābiq maiṅ ne davā lī.*
>
> I took the medicine as you advised.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مطابق** (*mutābiq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muṭābiq*, "matching, in keeping with".
