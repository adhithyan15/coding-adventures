## You'll want to know: しゅうきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C408-SENKOU408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうきょう** — *shūkyō* — "religion".

> **せんせいと しゅうきょうの ことを はなしました。** — *sensei to shūkyō no koto o hanashimashita* — "I talked with my teacher about religion."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうきょう** (*shūkyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shū*, "religion" (Middle Chinese *tsowng*) and *kyō*, "teach" (*kaewH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
