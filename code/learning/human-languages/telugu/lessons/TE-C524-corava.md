## You'll want to know: చొరవ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C524-PRATIBHA524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**చొరవ** — *corava* — "initiative; drive".

> ఆమె చొరవ తీసుకుని సమస్యను పరిష్కరించింది.
>
> *āme corava tīsukuni samasyanu pariṣkariñcindi.*
>
> She took the initiative and solved the problem.

చొరవ తీసుకో, to take the initiative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చొరవ** (*corava*) is a Telugu word for the drive to step forward and act; its origin is not settled.
