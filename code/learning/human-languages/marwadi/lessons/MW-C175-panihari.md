## You'll want to know: पणिहारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C175-PANI175-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पणिहारी** — *paṇihārī* — "a water-carrier; a woman who fetches water".

> पणिहारी बावड़ी सूं पाणी लावै है।
>
> *paṇihārī bāvṛī sū̃ pāṇī lāvai hai.*
>
> The water-carrier brings water from the stepwell.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पणिहारी** (*paṇihārī*) is built on a reconstructed *\*pānīyahāra*, "a water-carrier", from the word behind **पाणी** and a word for carrying; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *pāṇiyārī* (CDIAL 8088).

One more word for saying what you do.
