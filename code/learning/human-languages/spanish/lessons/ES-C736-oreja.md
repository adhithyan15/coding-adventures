## You'll want to know: la oreja
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C736-CARA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la oreja** — "the ear (outer)".

> Lleva un pendiente en la oreja.
>
> *He wears an earring in his ear.*

*La oreja* is the outer ear; *el oído* is hearing and the inner ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*oreja* is inherited from Latin *auricula* "little ear" through spoken *oricla* (EtymDB), a small form of *auris* "ear".
