## You'll want to know: sangloter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C471-DECEPTION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sangloter** — "to sob".

> L'enfant sanglotait dans les bras de sa mère.
>
> *The child was sobbing in his mother's arms.*

*Un sanglot* — a sob.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sangloter* is inherited from Latin *singultāre* "to hiccup, to sob" (EtymDB), from *singultus* "a hiccup, a sob" (Lewis & Short).
