## You'll want to know: બંધન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C540-BALJABRI540-02]; assesses=[] -->

**બંધન** — *bandhan* — "a tie (that binds)".

> જો નોકરીનું બંધન ન હોત તો હું આખું વરસ પ્રવાસ કરત.
>
> *jo nokrīnũ bandhan na hot to hũ ākhũ varas pravās karat.*
>
> If my job didn't tie me down, I would travel all year.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બંધન** (*bandhan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bandhana*, "binding, a bond" (Monier-Williams), from the root of **બાંધવું**.
