Arabic, chapter 105: Entrance, Exit, Straight ahead, Around, At.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مدخل (madkhal) — an entrance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a place, then the Arabic for an address.

You'll want to know: مدخل.
مدخل — madkhal — "an entrance".

The word, taken apart.
مدخل (madkhal) is built on the native Arabic root d-kh-l, "to enter" (Lane). Madkhal is the ma- place noun, "a place of entering".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: مستقيم.
مستقيم — mustaqīm — "straight ahead".

The word, taken apart.
مستقيم (mustaqīm) is built on the native Arabic root q-w-m, "to stand up, to be upright" (Lane). Mustaqīm is the Form X participle, "standing upright", hence "straight".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for straight ahead, then the Arabic for around.

You'll want to know: عند.
عند — ʿinda — "at, by".

The word, taken apart.
عند (ʿinda), "at, near, by", is an old preposition, filed under the root ʿ-n-d (Lane), beside ʿand, "vicinity".
