## You'll want to know: 父母
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C509-PARENTS509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**父母** — *fùmǔ* — "parents; one's mother and father".

> *wǒ gēn fùmǔ zhù le èrshí nián* — I lived with my parents for twenty years.

The new character, in pieces: 母 — five strokes: a frame of two, one down the left that turns right along the bottom and one along the top that turns down the right side and hooks; a dot inside the top half; a long bar across; a dot inside the bottom half. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**父母** (*fùmǔ*) is built from 父 'a father' and 母 'a mother', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjuX* and *muwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a mother' and, after an older book, reads its two dots as a picture of a nursing mother's breasts.
