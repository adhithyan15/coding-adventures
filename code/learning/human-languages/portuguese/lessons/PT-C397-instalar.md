## You'll want to know: instalar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C397-INSTALAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**instalar** — "to install".

> Instalei um jogo novo no portátil.
>
> *I installed a new game on the laptop.*

Also to settle in: *instalar-se numa casa nova*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*instalar* was borrowed from French *installer* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Medieval Latin *installāre* "to put into a stall (a seat in church)", built on a Germanic word for "standing place" (EtymDB lists its cousins).
