## You'll want to know: चरित्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C475-CHARITRA475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चरित्र** — *caritra* — "character; moral nature".

> मीरा के दादा का चरित्र बहुत साफ़ था।
>
> *Mīrā ke dādā kā caritra bahut sāf thā.*
>
> Mira's grandfather was a man of spotless character.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरित्र** (*caritra*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *caritra*, "acting, behaving, behaviour, deeds" (Monier-Williams), from *car*, "to move, to act".
