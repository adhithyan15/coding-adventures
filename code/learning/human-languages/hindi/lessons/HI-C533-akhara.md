## You'll want to know: अखाड़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C533-KUSHTI533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अखाड़ा** — *akhāṛā* — "a wrestling ground".

> अखाड़े की मिट्टी नरम है।
>
> *Akhāṛe kī miṭṭī naram hai.*
>
> The earth of the wrestling ground is soft.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अखाड़ा** (*akhāṛā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *akṣavāṭa*, "a wrestling ground" (CDIAL 39).
