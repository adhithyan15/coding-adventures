## You'll want to know: le dimissioni
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C357-DIMISSIONI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le dimissioni** — "the resignation".

> Ho dato le dimissioni perché lo stipendio era troppo basso.
>
> *I handed in my resignation because the pay was too low.*

Always plural: *dare le dimissioni* — to resign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dimissioni* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dīmissiō* "a sending away, a discharge" (Lewis & Short), from *dīmittere*, built on *mittere* "to send".
