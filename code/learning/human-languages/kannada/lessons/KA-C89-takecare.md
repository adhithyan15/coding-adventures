# ಹುಷಾರು (huṣāru) — take care, careful

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C88-WELCOME-04, KA-LEX-C88-WELCOME-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for come in, then the Kannada for welcome.

## You'll want to know: ಹುಷಾರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C89-CARE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುಷಾರು** (*huṣāru*) — "careful, take care". Said at parting it means "look after
yourself"; said on its own it can also mean "well again".
