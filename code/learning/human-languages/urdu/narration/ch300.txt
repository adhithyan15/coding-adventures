Urdu, chapter 300: The Morning News.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ریڈیو (reḍiyo) — a radio.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for morale, then the Urdu for a donation.

You'll want to know: ریڈیو.
ریڈیو — reḍiyo — "a radio".
ریڈیو پر گاؤں کی خبر آئی۔ reḍiyo par gāoṅ kī khabar āī. The news about the village came on the radio.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ریڈیو (reḍiyo) is borrowed from English radio. News comes ریڈیو پر, on the radio.

Lesson 2 of 5.
نشر کرنا (nashr karnā) — to broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a donation, then the Urdu for a radio.

You'll want to know: نشر کرنا.
نشر کرنا — nashr karnā — "to broadcast".
ریڈیو نے خبر نشر کی۔ reḍiyo ne khabar nashr kī. The radio broadcast the news.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اعلان نشر کیا, us ne elān nashr kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
نشر کرنا (nashr karnā) is built from nashr, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic nashr, "a spreading out", and کرنا: to spread news over the air. What is broadcast agrees: خبر نشر کی.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سرخی (surkhī) — a headline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a radio, then the Urdu for to broadcast.

You'll want to know: سرخی.
سرخی — surkhī — "a headline".
اخبار میں بڑی سرخی تھی۔ akhbār meṅ baṛī surkhī thī. There was a big headline in the paper.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
سرخی (surkhī) is usually traced to Persian surkhī, "redness", from surkh, "red"; headlines are thought to take the name from the red ink they were once printed in.

Lesson 4 of 5.
اطلاع (ittilā) — an official notice; word sent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to broadcast, then the Urdu for a headline.

You'll want to know: اطلاع.
اطلاع — ittilā — "an official notice; word sent".
پولیس کو اطلاع ملی۔ polīs ko ittilā milī. The police were informed.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
اطلاع (ittilā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic iṭṭilāʿ, "a coming to know". اطلاع ملی says word reached someone; اطلاع دینا is to inform.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سنسنی (sansanī) — a sensation; excitement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a headline, then the Urdu for an official notice.

You'll want to know: سنسنی.
سنسنی — sansanī — "a sensation; excitement".
اس خبر سے شہر میں سنسنی تھی۔ is khabar se shahr meṅ sansanī thī. The news caused a sensation in the city.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
سنسنی (sansanī) belongs with sansanānā, "to whizz, to be excited", which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit svaná, "a noise" (CDIAL 13901): the hum that runs through a crowd.
