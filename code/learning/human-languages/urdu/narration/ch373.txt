Urdu, chapter 373: If Things Go Wrong.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بکھرنا (bikharnā) — to scatter; to fall apart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to crack, then the Urdu for medium.

You'll want to know: بکھرنا.
بکھرنا — bikharnā — "to scatter; to fall apart".
اگر تھیلا گرا تو سب چیزیں بکھر جائیں گی۔ agar thailā girā to sab chīzeṅ bikhar jāeṅ gī. If the bag falls, everything will scatter.
Past (it …): وہ ہار بکھر گیا, voh hār bikhar gayā.

The word, taken apart.
بکھرنا (bikharnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bikharnā, "to be scattered", under viṣkirati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11985).

What you've built.
