## You'll want to know: स्टैंड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C403-STAIND403-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्टैंड** — *sṭaiṇḍ* — "a stand (for buses, taxis or rickshaws)".

> बस स्टैंड यहाँ से पास है।
>
> *bas sṭaiṇḍ yahā̃ se pās hai.*
>
> The bus stand is close to here.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्टैंड** (*sṭaiṇḍ*) is borrowed from English *stand*, the place where buses, taxis or rickshaws wait for passengers: बस स्टैंड, रिक्शा स्टैंड.
