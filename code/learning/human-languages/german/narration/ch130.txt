German, chapter 130: Pink, Orange, Pretty, Round, Wide.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
rosa — pink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a concert, then the German for a celebration.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count eins, then say ein, the same word with its counting -s dropped. Then say sieben, with the s that buzzes.

You'll want to know: rosa.
rosa — "pink".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
