## You'll want to know: こうつうひ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C388-RYOHI388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こうつうひ** — *kōtsūhi* — "transport costs".

> **かいしゃが こうつうひを はらいます。** — *kaisha ga kōtsūhi o haraimasu* — "The company pays the transport costs."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうつうひ** (*kōtsūhi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "coming & going" (Middle Chinese *kaew*), *tsū*, "traffic" (*thuwng*) and *hi*, "expense" (*phj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
