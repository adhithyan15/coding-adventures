## HL-C359 — Hindi first declares its taught agreement forms

Chapter 82 teaches **पहला** together with feminine **पहली** and adverbial
**पहले**, explains why their endings differ, and assesses both contrasts at
wrap-up. The declaration exposes only **पहला**, leaving the two explicitly
taught forms looking untaught to the corpus report.

Declare **पहली** and **पहले** beside **पहला**. This makes the lesson's agreement
and “before” teaching visible to reports and downstream study artifacts without
claiming any form the lesson does not explain.
