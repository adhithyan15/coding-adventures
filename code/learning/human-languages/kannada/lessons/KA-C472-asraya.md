# ಆಶ್ರಯ (āśraya) — shelter; refuge

## You'll want to know: ಆಶ್ರಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C472-HALU472-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಶ್ರಯ** — *āśraya* — "shelter; refuge".

> ಮಳೆ ಬಂದಾಗ ನಾವು ಒಂದು ಅಂಗಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆಶ್ರಯ ಪಡೆದೆವು.
>
> *maḷe bandāga nāvu ondu aṅgaḍiyalli āśraya paḍedevu.*
>
> When it rained we took shelter in a shop.

ಆಶ್ರಯ ಪಡೆ, to take shelter; ಆಶ್ರಯ ಕೊಡು, to give shelter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಶ್ರಯ** (*āśraya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āśraya*, "a resting place, a refuge, a dwelling" (CDIAL 1466).
