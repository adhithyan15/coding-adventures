## You'll want to know: احتمال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C392-EHTEMAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمال** (*ehtemâl*) — "a probability, a chance".

> احتمال باران امروز زیاد است.
>
> *ehtemâl-e bârân emruz ziyâd ast.*
>
> There is a good chance of rain today.

*Ehtemâl dârad ke* + subjunctive — it is likely that. The adverb is *ehtemâlan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمال** is usually traced to Arabic *iḥtimāl* "likelihood, what can be borne", on the root *ḥ-m-l* "to carry", the same source as *ehtemâlan* "probably".
