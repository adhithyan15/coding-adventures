## You'll want to know: अक्षः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C409-CAKRA409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अक्षः** — *akṣaḥ* — "an axle".

> शकटस्य अक्षः दृढः अस्ति।
>
> *śakaṭasya akṣaḥ dṛḍhaḥ asti.*
>
> The cart's axle is strong.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अक्षः** (*akṣaḥ*) is the word MW gives for an axle; MW gives no derivation within Sanskrit, so its origin is uncertain; MW compares Latin *axis* and Greek *axōn* (MW).
