## You'll want to know: ताडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C383-BHAKIT383-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ताडणे** — *tāḍṇe* — "to size up; to guess rightly".

> तो खोटं बोलत आहे हे आईने लगेच ताडलं.
>
> *to khoṭaṁ bolat āhe he āīne lagec tāḍlaṁ.*
>
> Mother saw at once that he was lying.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ताडलं, *tyāne tāḍlaṁ*.

Transitive: what is guessed is the object; हे ताडलं, guessed this.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताडणे** (*tāḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *tāḍṇẽ*, "to investigate, discover, infer", under *\*taḍati*, "stretches" (CDIAL 5633).
