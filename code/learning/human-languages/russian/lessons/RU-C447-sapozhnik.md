## You'll want to know: сапожник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C447-MASTERSKAYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**сапожник** (*sapózhnik*) — "a shoemaker, a cobbler".

> Сапожник починил мои старые сапоги.
>
> *Sapózhnik pochiníl moí stáryye sapogí.*
>
> The cobbler mended my old boots.

Masculine. Built on *сапог* — a boot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сапожник* is built on *сапог* 'a boot' with *-ник*. *Сапог* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *sapogŭ* (EtymDB).
