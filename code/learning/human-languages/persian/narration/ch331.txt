Persian, chapter 331: Disaster.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ویران شدن (virân shodan) — to be destroyed, to be ruined.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a warning, then the Persian for to warn.

You'll want to know: ویران شدن.
ویران شدن (virân shodan) — "to be destroyed, to be ruined".
در زلزله صدها خانه ویران شد. Dar zelzele sad-hâ khâne virân shod. Hundreds of houses were destroyed in the earthquake.
Present virân mishavad. Virân "ruined" is also an adjective.

The word, taken apart.
virân "desolate, ruined" is a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; shodan "to become" is inherited Persian.

سقف مدرسه قدیمی فرو ریخت. Saqf-e madrese-ye qadimi foru rikht. The roof of the old school collapsed.
foru "down" + rikhtan "to pour". Present stem riz-: foru mirizad.

The word, taken apart.
foru "down" is native Persian, and rikhtan "to pour" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB): to pour down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to collapse, then the Persian for rubble.

You'll want to know: تخلیه کردن.
تخلیه کردن (takhliye kardan) — "to evacuate, to empty".
پلیس ساختمان را تخلیه کرد. Polis sâkhtemân râ takhliye kard. The police evacuated the building.
Also "to empty, to unload". Present takhliye mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
takhliye is borrowed from Arabic takhliya (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "an emptying, a clearing". kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for rubble, then the Persian for to evacuate.

You'll want to know: پناه بردن.
پناه بردن (panâh bordan) — "to take shelter, to take refuge".
مردم از باران به زیر پل پناه بردند. Mardom az bârân be zir-e pol panâh bordand. People took shelter from the rain under the bridge.
panâh "refuge" + bordan "to carry". Present stem bar-: panâh mibarad.

The word, taken apart.
panâh "refuge" is inherited from Middle Persian panāh, "protector, refuge" (EtymDB); bordan "to carry" is inherited Persian too.
