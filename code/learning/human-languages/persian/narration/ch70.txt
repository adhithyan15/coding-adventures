Persian, chapter 70: Bank, Station, Car, Bus, Train.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بانک (bânk) — a bank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for left, then the Persian for a street.

You'll want to know: بانک.
بانک — bânk — "a bank".

The word, taken apart.
bânk is borrowed from French banque, "bank" (EtymDB). The French word goes back to Italian banco, "bench" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ایستگاه (istgâh) — a station, a stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a street, then the Persian for a bank.

You'll want to know: ایستگاه.
ایستگاه — istgâh — "a station, a stop".

The word, taken apart.
istgâh is built from ist-, "stand" (the stem of istâdan, "to stand"), and gâh, "place": "standing place". Both parts are native Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
قطار (ghatâr) — a train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a car, then the Persian for a bus.

You'll want to know: قطار.
قطار — ghatâr — "a train".

The word, taken apart.
ghatâr is borrowed from Arabic qiṭār, "line of camels". Its Arabic root is q-ṭ-r (Lane). Lane describes camels tied one behind another; Persian moved the word to the train.
