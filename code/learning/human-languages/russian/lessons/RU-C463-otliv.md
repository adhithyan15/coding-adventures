## You'll want to know: отлив
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C463-PRILIV-02]; assesses=[] -->

**отлив** (*otlív*) — "a low tide, an ebb tide".

> Во время отлива можно дойти до острова пешком.
>
> *Vo vrémya otlíva mózhno doytí do óstrova peshkóm.*
>
> At low tide you can walk to the island.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отлив* is built from *от-* 'away' and *-лив* 'a pouring', from *лить* 'to pour', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic *liti*.
