# నెరవేర్చు (neravērcu) — to fulfil; to carry out

## You'll want to know: నెరవేర్చు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C422-HAMI422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నెరవేర్చు** — *neravērcu* — "to fulfil; to carry out".

> అతను తన వాగ్దానాన్ని నెరవేర్చాడు.
>
> *atanu tana vāgdānānni neravērcāḍu.*
>
> He kept his promise.

Past (I …): నెరవేర్చాను, *neravērcānu*.

For promises, wishes and duties: కోరిక నెరవేర్చు, to fulfil a wish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నెరవేర్చు** (*neravērcu*) is a Telugu verb whose origin is uncertain; DEDR has no article for it. Its intransitive partner is నెరవేరు, to come true.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
