## You'll want to know: गळू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C487-CATKA487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गळू** — *gaḷū* — "an abscess; a boil".

> पाठीवरचं गळू आता फुटलं.
>
> *pāṭhīvarcaṁ gaḷū ātā phuṭlaṁ.*
>
> The boil on the back has burst now.

Neuter.

Neuter; गळू होणे, to get a boil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गळू** (*gaḷū*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *gaḷū̃*, "boil", under Sanskrit *gaḍu* (CDIAL 3977).
