## You'll want to know: alquilar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C667-APARTAMENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**alquilar** — "to rent, to hire; to rent out".

> Alquilamos un coche para tres días.
>
> *We are hiring a car for three days.*

*Se alquila* on a sign — for rent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alquilar* is built on *alquiler* "rent" (EtymDB), which comes from Andalusian Arabic *al-kirá* "the hire, the rent", with the Arabic article fused on.
