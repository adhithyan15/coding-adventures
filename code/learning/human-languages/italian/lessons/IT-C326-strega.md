## You'll want to know: la strega
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C326-MAGIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la strega** — "the witch".

> La strega vive nel bosco.
>
> *The witch lives in the woods.*

Plural *le streghe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*strega* is inherited from Latin *strīga* "an evil spirit" (EtymDB), built on *strīx* "a screech owl".
