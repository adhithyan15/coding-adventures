## You'll want to know: くらべる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C227-THINK227-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くらべる** — *kuraberu* — "to compare".

> **がくせいは ふたつの こたえを くらべます。** — *gakusei wa futatsu no kotae o kurabemasu* — "The students compare two answers."

Polite **くらべます**, *kurabemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くらべる** (*kuraberu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
