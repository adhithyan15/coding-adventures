## You'll want to know: andererseits
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C336-EINIGUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**andererseits** — "on the other hand".

> Ich möchte gern ein Auto. Andererseits fahre ich gern Fahrrad.
>
> *I would like a car. On the other hand, I like cycling.*

The partner of *einerseits*, but it can also stand alone. The verb comes right after it: *andererseits fahre ich …*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*andererseits* joins *ander* "other" to *Seite* "side", from Old High German *sīta* and Proto-Germanic *sīdōn* (EtymDB), with the adverb ending *-s*.
