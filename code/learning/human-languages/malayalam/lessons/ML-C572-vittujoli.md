## You'll want to know: വീട്ടുജോലി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C572-VITTUJOLI572-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വീട്ടുജോലി** — *vīṭṭujōli* — "housework; chores".

> ഞായറാഴ്ച എല്ലാവരും വീട്ടുജോലിയിൽ സഹായിച്ചു.
>
> *ñāyaṟāḻca ellāvaruṁ vīṭṭujōliyil sahāyiccu.*
>
> On Sunday everyone helped with the housework.

വീട് house + ജോലി work; വീട് becomes വീട്ടു- in a compound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വീട്ടുജോലി** (*vīṭṭujōli*) is a Malayalam compound built from വീട്, "a house", which is native (DEDR 5393), and ജോലി, "work".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
