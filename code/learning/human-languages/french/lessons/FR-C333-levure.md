## You'll want to know: la levure
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C333-PETRIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la levure** — "the yeast; baking powder".

> Le boulanger met de la levure dans la pâte.
>
> *The baker puts yeast in the dough.*

*La levure chimique* — baking powder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*levure* is built on *lever* "to raise", which is inherited from Latin *levāre* "to lift" (EtymDB).
