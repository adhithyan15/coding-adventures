Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 267: May I Go?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
इजाजत (ijājat) — permission.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a petition, then the Marwadi for a blessing.

You'll want to know: इजाजत.
इजाजत — ijājat — "permission".
म्हैं इजाजत ली अर घरै गयो। mhaĩ ijājat lī ar gharai gayo. I got permission and went home.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
इजाजत (ijājat) is usually traced, through Persian ijāzat, to Arabic ijāza, "permission". It is feminine, so म्हैं इजाजत ली, I got permission.

हूं रजा मांगूं हूं। hū̃ rajā māṅgū̃ hū̃. I ask leave to go.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
रजा (rajā) is usually traced to Persian riżā, "consent"; asking for it is asking leave to go.

Lesson 4 of 5.
इंतजार (intjār) — waiting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for leave to go, then the Marwadi for an order.

You'll want to know: इंतजार.
इंतजार — intjār — "waiting".
हूं थारो इंतजार करूं हूं। hū̃ thāro intjār karū̃ hū̃. I'm waiting for you.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
इंतजार (intjār) is usually traced, through Persian intizār, to Arabic intiẓār, "waiting". The one waited for comes first: थारो इंतजार, waiting for you.

Lesson 5 of 5.
थमणो (thamṇo) — to stop; to hold on; to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an order, then the Marwadi for waiting.

You'll want to know: थमणो.
थमणो — thamṇo — "to stop; to hold on; to wait".
थोड़ु थमो सा। thoṛu thamo sā. Hold on a moment, please.
Past (he …): वो थम्यो, vo thamyo.

The word, taken apart.
थमणो (thamṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit stambhate, "supports, arrests"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Sindhi thamaṇu, "to stop, subside", Nepali thāmnu, "to hold, stop, wait", and Hindi thāmnā, "to prop, stop" (CDIAL 13683).
