Bengali, chapter 158: Graveyard, Palace, Fort, Minaret, Ceiling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
কবরস্থান (kôbôrsthān) — a graveyard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a canal, then the Bengali for a zoo.

You'll want to know: কবরস্থান.
কবরস্থান — kôbôrsthān — "a graveyard".

The word, taken apart.
কবরস্থান (kôbôrsthān) is kôbôr, "grave", from Arabic qabr, "grave", which reached Bengali through Persian (Lane), + sthān, "place", Sanskrit sthāna taken over whole. Persian has the matching qabristān.

Lesson 4 of 5.
মিনার (minār) — a minaret, a tower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a palace, then the Bengali for a fort.

You'll want to know: মিনার.
মিনার — minār — "a minaret, a tower".

The word, taken apart.
মিনার (minār) is a loan from Arabic manār, "lighthouse, minaret", which reached Bengali through Persian. Its Arabic root is n-w-r (Lane). The word belongs with Arabic nūr, "light".
