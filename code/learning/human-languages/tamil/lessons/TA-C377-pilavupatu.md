# பிளவுபடு (piḷavupaṭu) — to be divided; to split

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C377-VANMURAI377-03, TA-LEX-C377-VANMURAI377-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for unity, then the Tamil for diversity.

## You'll want to know: பிளவுபடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C377-VANMURAI377-05]; assesses=[] -->

**பிளவுபடு** — *piḷavupaṭu* — "to be divided; to split".

> அந்த முடிவால் எங்கள் குழு இரண்டாகப் பிளவுபட்டது.
>
> *anta muṭivāl eṅkaḷ kuḻu iraṇṭākap piḷavupaṭṭatu.*
>
> Because of that decision, our group split in two.

Past (it …): பிளவுபட்டது, *piḷavupaṭṭatu*.

பிளவு, a split + படு, to undergo.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பிளவுபடு** (*piḷavupaṭu*) is native: DEDR 4194 lists Tamil *piḷa*, "to be split", and *piḷavu*, "cleft, division, disunion", here with *paṭu*, "to undergo".
