# రవాణా (ravāṇā) — transport

## You'll want to know: రవాణా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C454-PAILATU454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**రవాణా** — *ravāṇā* — "transport".

> నగరంలో ప్రజా రవాణా బాగుంది.
>
> *nagaraṁlō prajā ravāṇā bāgundi.*
>
> Public transport in the city is good.

ప్రజా రవాణా, public transport; సరుకు రవాణా, the carrying of goods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రవాణా** (*ravāṇā*) is usually traced to Persian *ravāna*, "setting out, dispatched", which came into Telugu through Hindustani; sending goods on their way became transport.
