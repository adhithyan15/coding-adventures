## You'll want to know: ہاضمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C472-STOMACH472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہاضمہ** — *hāzmā* — "digestion".

> دہی ہاضمے کے لیے اچھا ہے۔
>
> *dahī hāzme ke liye achchhā hai.*
>
> Yoghurt is good for the digestion.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہاضمہ** (*hāzmā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *hāḍima*, "digesting", of the same family as **ہضم**.
