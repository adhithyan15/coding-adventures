## You'll want to know: দোলনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C485-BACHCHA485-05]; assesses=[] -->

**দোলনা** — *dolnā* — "a cradle; a swing".

> বাচ্চাটা দোলনায় শুয়ে আছে।
>
> *bāchchāṭā dolnāy shuye āchhe.*
>
> The baby is lying in the cradle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দোলনা** (*dolnā*) is built on the verb *dolā*, "to swing", which CDIAL lists for Bengali under Sanskrit *dōlāyate*, "swings" (CDIAL 6585).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
