# Give me one familiar item

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C247-THG247-05, SA-PRON-C260-TVAM-02, SA-PATTERN-C264-NEAR-TERM-PLAN-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “drum,” then “you” as one object, then make one plan for
tomorrow. (*Paṭahaḥ; tvām; śvaḥ* plus a taught first-person future form.)

## Grammar Lens: receiver, item, request

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02]; assesses=[SA-PRON-DATIVE-ME-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01] -->

Build the request in three small pieces:

> **मह्यम्** + familiar item + **ददातु**
>
> to me + item + please give

So **मह्यम् पानीयं ददातु** (*mahyaṃ pānīyaṃ dadātu*) asks, “Please give me
water.” The item changes; the frame stays still.

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PRON-DATIVE-ME-01, SA-LEX-ANNAM-FOOD, SA-LEX-PANIYAM-WATER, SA-LEX-KSHIRAM-MILK, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: “Give me food” — *mahyaṃ annaṃ dadātu*]
- [YOU SAY: “Give me water” — *mahyaṃ pānīyaṃ dadātu*]
- [YOU SAY: “Give me milk” — *mahyaṃ kṣīraṃ dadātu*]
