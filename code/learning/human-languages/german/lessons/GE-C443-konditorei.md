## You'll want to know: die Konditorei
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C443-FEINKOST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Konditorei** — "the cake shop, the patisserie".

> In der Konditorei gibt es den besten Kuchen der Stadt.
>
> *The cake shop has the best cake in town.*

Plural: *die Konditoreien*. *Der Konditor* — the pastry cook.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Konditorei* is built on *Konditor* 'confectioner' with the ending *-ei* (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *condītor* 'a seasoner', from *condīre* 'to season, to preserve' (Lewis & Short).
