# ಹಾಸ್ಟೆಲ್ (hāsṭel) — a hostel

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C460-VASATI460-05, KA-LEX-C461-ATITHI461-01] -->

## You'll want to know: ಹಾಸ್ಟೆಲ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C461-ATITHI461-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾಸ್ಟೆಲ್** — *hāsṭel* — "a hostel".

> ನಗರದಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ತಂಗಿ ಹಾಸ್ಟೆಲ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಇರುತ್ತಾಳೆ.
>
> *nagaradalli nanna taṅgi hāsṭelnalli iruttāḷe.*
>
> In the city my younger sister lives in a hostel.

For students, workers and budget travellers alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾಸ್ಟೆಲ್** (*hāsṭel*) is a loan from English *hostel*.
