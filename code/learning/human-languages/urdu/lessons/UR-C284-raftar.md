## You'll want to know: رفتار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C284-TAKKAR284-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رفتار** — *raftār* — "speed".

> میں کم رفتار سے جاؤں گا۔
>
> *maiṅ kam raftār se jāūṅ gā.*
>
> I will go at a low speed.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رفتار** (*raftār*) is usually traced to Persian *raftār*, "a going, a gait", from the Persian verb to go. It is feminine: **تیز رفتار**, high speed.
