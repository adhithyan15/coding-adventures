## You'll want to know: mātrōna, mātrōnae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C328-FLAMMEUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**mātrōna, mātrōnae** — "a married woman, a lady of the house".

> Mātrōna servōs et domum cūrābat.
>
> *The lady of the house looked after the slaves and the home.*

Feminine. The *stola* (taught) was the dress of a *mātrōna*. English *matron*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mātrōna** is built on *māter*, "mother" (EtymDB): a married woman of standing. English *matron* comes from it through French.
