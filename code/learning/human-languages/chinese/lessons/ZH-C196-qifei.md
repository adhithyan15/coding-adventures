## You'll want to know: qǐfēi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C196-TICKET196-04]; assesses=[] -->

*qǐfēi* — "to take off (of a plane)".

> *fēijī shí diǎn qǐfēi* — The plane takes off at ten.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qǐfēi* is built from 起 'to rise' and *fēi* 'to fly', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khiX* and *pj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
