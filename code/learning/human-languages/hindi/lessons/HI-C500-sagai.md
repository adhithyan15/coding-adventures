## You'll want to know: सगाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C500-BARAT500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सगाई** — *sagāī* — "an engagement (to marry)".

> पिछले साल मीरा की सगाई हुई।
>
> *pichle sāl Mīrā kī sagāī huī.*
>
> Mira got engaged last year.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सगाई** (*sagāī*) is probably the noun of *sagā*, "of full blood relationship", which CDIAL lists under *\*svagya-*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13896b): the promise that makes two families kin. CDIAL does not give the "engagement" sense itself.
