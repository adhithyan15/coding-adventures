## You'll want to know: ಸವೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C480-BAYALU480-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸವೆ** — *save* — "to wear away; to erode".

> ತುಂಬಾ ನಡೆದಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ನನ್ನ ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ ಸವೆಯಿತು.
>
> *tumbā naḍediddarinda nanna cappali saveyitu.*
>
> From so much walking my sandals wore out.

Past (it …): ಸವೆಯಿತು, *saveyitu*.

ಸವೆತ, wear or erosion: ಮಣ್ಣಿನ ಸವೆತ, soil erosion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸವೆ** (*save*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2343 lists Kannada *same*, *save*, "to be consumed, to wear away, to be abraded".
