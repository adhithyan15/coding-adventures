# बारिश हो रही थी, इसलिए … (bāriś ho rahī thī, isliye …) — the cause, then so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C247-THG247-03, HI-LEX-C281-ATAH-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-VAJAH-SE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "because of the rain". (**बारिश की वजह से**.) Then the two words for "therefore", the everyday one and the formal one. (**इसलिए**, **अतः**.)

## You'll want to know: cause, इसलिए, result
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C281-ISLIYE-RESULT-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C247-THG247-03, HI-JOIN-KYONKI-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

> **बारिश हो रही थी, इसलिए मैं नहीं गया।**
>
> *bāriś ho rahī thī, isliye maiṁ nahīṁ gayā.* — "It was raining, so I didn't go."

**इसलिए** stands between two whole sentences: the cause first, then the result. **क्योंकि** turns the order round.

| order | sentence |
|---|---|
| cause, **इसलिए**, result | **बारिश हो रही है, इसलिए मैं घर पर हूँ।** (*bāriś ho rahī hai, isliye maiṁ ghar par hūṁ.*) |
| result, **क्योंकि**, cause | **मैं घर पर हूँ, क्योंकि बारिश हो रही है।** (*maiṁ ghar par hūṁ, kyoṁki bāriś ho rahī hai.*) |

In formal writing, **अतः** takes the place of **इसलिए**.

## You'll want to know: तो, so then
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C281-TO-SO-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C121-CARE-05, HI-CONCEPT-C03-THIK-01, HI-LEX-C133-THINGS133-02, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-03, HI-CONCEPT-C04-KALMILTEHAIN-01] -->

> **बारिश हो रही है? तो छाता लीजिए।**
>
> *bāriś ho rahī hai? to chātā lījie.* — "It's raining? Then take an umbrella."

**तो** (*to*), "so, then", takes up what was said and draws the next step from it. It opens a reply:

- **तो चलिए।** (*to calie.*) — So let's go.
- **ठीक है, तो कल मिलते हैं।** (*ṭhīk hai, to kal milte haiṁ.*) — All right, so see you tomorrow.

**तो** has a bigger job still, after **अगर**, "if"; it comes when the book reaches conditions.
