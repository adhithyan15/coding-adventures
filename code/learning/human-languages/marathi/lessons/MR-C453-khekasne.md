## You'll want to know: खेकसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C453-TAPAT453-04]; assesses=[] -->

**खेकसणे** — *khekasṇe* — "to snap at".

> थकलेला दुकानदार ग्राहकावर खेकसला.
>
> *thaklelā dukāndār grāhkāvar khekaslā.*
>
> The tired shopkeeper snapped at a customer.

Past (he …): तो खेकसला, *to khekaslā*.

The person snapped at takes -वर: तो माझ्यावर खेकसला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खेकसणे** (*khekasṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
