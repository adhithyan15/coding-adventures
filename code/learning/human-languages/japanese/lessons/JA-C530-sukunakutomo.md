# すくなくとも (sukunakutomo) — at the very least; no fewer than

## You'll want to know: すくなくとも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C530-ATLEAST530-02]; assesses=[] -->

**すくなくとも** — *sukunakutomo* — "at the very least; no fewer than".

> **もし くるまで いったら、すくなくとも いちじかん かかるでしょう。** — *moshi kuruma de ittara, sukunakutomo ichijikan kakaru deshō* — "If you go by car, it will take at least an hour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すくなくとも** (*sukunakutomo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built on *sukunai*, "few", with the ending *tomo*, "even"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
