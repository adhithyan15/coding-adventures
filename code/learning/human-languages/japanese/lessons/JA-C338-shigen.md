## You'll want to know: しげん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C338-MUDA338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しげん** — *shigen* — "resources".

> **しげんが すくないので、せつやくした ほうが いいです。** — *shigen ga sukunai node, setsuyaku shita hō ga ii desu* — "Resources are scarce, so we should use them carefully."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しげん** (*shigen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "resources" (Middle Chinese *tsij*) and *gen*, "source" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
