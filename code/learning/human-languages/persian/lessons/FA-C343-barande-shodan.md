## You'll want to know: برنده شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C343-BARANDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**برنده شدن** (*barande shodan*) — "to win".

> تیم ما در مسابقه برنده شد.
>
> *Tim-e mâ dar mosâbeqe barande shod.*
>
> Our team won the match.

*barande* "a winner", from *bordan* "to carry off, to win". Present *barande mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barande* "a winner" is built on *bar-*, the present stem of the native verb *bordan* "to carry off, to win", with *-ande* "one who"; *shodan* "to become" is inherited too.
