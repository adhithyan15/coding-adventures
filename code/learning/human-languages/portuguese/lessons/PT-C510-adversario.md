## You'll want to know: o adversário
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C510-RESULTADO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o adversário** — "opponent".

> O nosso adversário de domingo é muito forte.
>
> *Our opponent on Sunday is very strong.*

Also an adjective: *a equipa adversária*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adversário* comes from Latin *adversārius* "an opponent" (EtymDB), built on *adversus* "turned towards, against".
