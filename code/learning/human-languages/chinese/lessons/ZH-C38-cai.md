## You'll want to know: cài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C38-EAT38-05]; assesses=[] -->

*cài* — "dish of food; vegetable".

> *nǐ xǐhuan chī shénme cài* — What sort of food do you like?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cài* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tshojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as any plant that can be eaten.
