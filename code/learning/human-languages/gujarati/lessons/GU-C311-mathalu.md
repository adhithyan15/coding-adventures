## You'll want to know: મથાળું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C311-SAMACHAR311-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મથાળું** — *mathāḷũ* — "a headline".

> છાપાનું મથાળું હતું: પોલીસે ઠગને ઝડપ્યો.
>
> *chāpānũ mathāḷũ hatũ: polīse ṭhagne jhaḍapyo.*
>
> The headline read: the police nab a swindler.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મથાળું** (*mathāḷũ*) comes from *\*mastatalaka*, a form CDIAL reconstructs on Sanskrit *mastaka*, the head, and CDIAL lists Gujarati *mathāḷũ*, "top, heading" (CDIAL 9927). In a newspaper it is the line at the head of a story.
