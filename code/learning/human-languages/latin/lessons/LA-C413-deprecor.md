## You'll want to know: dēprecor, dēprecārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C413-CONDEMNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dēprecor, dēprecārī** — "to beg to be spared, to plead against".

> Reus poenam dēprecātus est.
>
> *The accused begged to be spared the punishment.*

Deponent: *dēprecor, dēprecārī, dēprecātus sum*, *dē* + *precor*, to pray: to pray a thing away. English *deprecate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēprecor* is built from *dē* "away" and *precor* "to pray" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to pray a thing away. English *deprecate* comes from it.
