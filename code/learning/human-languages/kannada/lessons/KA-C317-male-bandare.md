# ಮಳೆ ಬಂದರೆ (maḷe bandare) — if it rains

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C317-ARE-CONDITIONAL-01, KA-LEX-C176-KARANA176-02, KA-LEX-C252-KARANA252-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "If you come, let's eat together." (*nīnu bandare nāvu oṭṭige ūṭa māḍōṇa*.) Then "otherwise". (*illadiddare*.)

## You'll want to know: if this, then that
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C317-REAL-CONDITION-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C04-NAALE-SIGONA-01, KA-LEX-C103-HOME-01, KA-LEX-C53-SKY-02, KA-LEX-C110-LOC110-02, KA-GRAMMAR-C32-TINNU-02] -->

> **ನಾಳೆ ಮಳೆ ಬಂದರೆ ನಾನು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ ಇರುತ್ತೇನೆ.**
>
> *nāḷe maḷe bandare nānu maneyallē iruttēne.* — "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home."

The if-clause comes first and the result after it, in the **-ಉತ್ತೇನೆ** that says "will" as well as "do". For something that holds every time, the same shape:

> **ಬಿಸಿಲು ಇದ್ದರೆ ನಾವು ಕಡಲತೀರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇವೆ.**
>
> *bisilu iddare nāvu kaḍalatīrakke hōguttēve.* — "When it is sunny, we go to the beach."

**ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇವೆ** (*hōguttēve*, "we go"), the "we" of **ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ**.

## You'll want to know: if not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C317-NEGATIVE-CONDITION-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C64-JOIN-04, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-03, KA-LEX-C32-HOOGU-01, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01, KA-LEX-C176-KARANA176-02] -->

> **ನೀನು ಬೇಗ ಬರದಿದ್ದರೆ ಬಸ್ ಹೋಗುತ್ತದೆ.**
>
> *nīnu bēga baradiddare bas hōguttade.* — "If you don't come quickly, the bus will go."

**ಬರದಿದ್ದರೆ** is **ಬರದೆ** (*barade*, "without coming") with **ಇದ್ದರೆ**: "if (you) are without coming". It is built the way **ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ** is, **ಇಲ್ಲದೆ** + **ಇದ್ದರೆ**. More of them: **ಮಾಡದಿದ್ದರೆ**, "if (you) don't do it"; **ಹೋಗದಿದ್ದರೆ**, "if (you) don't go".
