Urdu, chapter 452: Pride and Showing Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غرور (ghurūr) — arrogance; conceit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for contrite, then the Urdu for embarrassment.

You'll want to know: غرور.
غرور — ghurūr — "arrogance; conceit".
اسے اپنی طاقت پر غرور ہے۔ use apnī tāqat par ghurūr hai. He is conceited about his strength.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
غرور (ghurūr) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ghurūr, "self-delusion, vanity".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Past (he …): وہ اترایا, voh itrāyā.

The word, taken apart.
اترانا (itrānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin itrānā, "to strut", under Sanskrit itvará, "walking" (CDIAL 1566).
