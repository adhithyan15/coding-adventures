## You'll want to know: jìxù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C244-STORM244-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jìxù* — "to carry on; to continue".

> *yǔ tíng le wǒmen jìxù zǒu* — The rain's stopped; let's carry on.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìxù* is built from *jì* 'to continue' and *xù* 'to join on'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *zjowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to continue' and 'to join'; the dictionary glosses the first, *kejH* there, 'to carry on'.

## What you've built
