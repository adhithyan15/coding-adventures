# देवरः (devaraḥ) — a husband's brother

## You'll want to know: देवरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C500-DEVARA500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**देवरः** — *devaraḥ* — "a husband's brother".

> मम देवरः चिकित्सकः अस्ति।
>
> *mama devaraḥ cikitsakaḥ asti.*
>
> My husband's brother is a doctor.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**देवरः** (*devaraḥ*) is built on *devṛ*, "a husband's brother (esp. his younger brother)"; MW compares Greek *daēr* and Latin *levir* (MW).

## What you've built
