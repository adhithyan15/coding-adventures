# मान्य करणे (mānya karṇe) — to acknowledge; to agree to

## You'll want to know: मान्य करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C350-SVIKAR350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मान्य करणे** — *mānya karṇe* — "to acknowledge; to agree to".

> शेवटी त्याने आपली चूक मान्य केली.
>
> *śevṭī tyāne āplī cūk mānya kelī.*
>
> In the end he acknowledged his mistake.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मान्य केलं, *tyāne mānya kelaṁ*.

मान्य असणे, to be acceptable: मला हे मान्य आहे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मान्य करणे** (*mānya karṇe*) joins the tatsama मान्य, Sanskrit *mānya*, "to be respected or honoured" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to make".
