## You'll want to know: гирлянда
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C313-YOLKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**гирлянда** (*girlyánda*) — "fairy lights; a garland".

> На окне горела гирлянда.
>
> *Na okné goréla girlyánda.*
>
> Fairy lights were glowing in the window.

Feminine. A string of lights or of paper flowers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гирлянда* is usually traced through German or French to Italian *ghirlanda* "a garland"; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.
