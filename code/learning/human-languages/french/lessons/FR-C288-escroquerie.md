## You'll want to know: l'escroquerie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C288-ARNAQUE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'escroquerie** — "the fraud, the swindle".

> Il a été arrêté pour escroquerie.
>
> *He was arrested for fraud.*

The formal and legal word; *l'arnaque* is the everyday one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escroquerie* is built on *escroquer* "to swindle" (EtymDB), which was borrowed from Italian *scroccare*.
