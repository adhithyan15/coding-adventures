Lesson 1 of 5.
difficile — difficult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for fast, then the French for easy.

You'll want to know: difficile.
difficile — "difficult".

The word, taken apart.
difficile from Latin difficilis, "hard to do": facile with dis-, "not", in front — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).

haut from Latin altus, "high", blended with Frankish hauh, "high", which gave it its h (EtymDB 2.1 gives the Frankish side) — inherited.
