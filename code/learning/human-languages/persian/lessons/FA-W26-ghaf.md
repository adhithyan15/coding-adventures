# ق (ghâf) — the letter inside خوشوقتم

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-ZA-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ظ and ف once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **خوشوقتم** (*khoshvaghtam*), "pleased to meet you".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-GHAF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ق** — *ghâf*, a *gh* made far back in the throat.

It is inside **خوشوقتم** (*khoshvaghtam*), "pleased to meet you". Its head is the
head of **ف**, from the last lesson, but it carries **two dots**, and standing
alone its bowl dips below the line.

## Writing: ق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-GHAF-01] -->

- **1.** start here: loop counterclockwise around the small closed head
- **2.** continue down and left through the deep bowl without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then place the upper-right dot
- **4.** lift again, then place the upper-left dot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, ق demonstration at 02:14–02:18 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-ZA-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-GHAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **خوشوقتم** and point at ق inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ق three times, saying *gh* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ف, then ق, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **خوشوقتم** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-ZA-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-GHAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ق? (**ghâf**, *gh*.) Which word did it
come from? (**خوشوقتم**, *khoshvaghtam*, "pleased to meet you".)
