## You'll want to know: ਚਿਮਟਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C239-KAM239-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਿਮਟਾ** — *chimṭā* — "tongs".

> ਮੈਂ ਚਿਮਟੇ ਨਾਲ ਰੋਟੀ ਚੁੱਕਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ chimṭe nāl roṭī chukkdī hāṁ.*
>
> I lift the roti with tongs.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਿਮਟਾ** (*chimṭā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*cimb*, "pinch"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cimṭā*, "tongs" (CDIAL 4822).
