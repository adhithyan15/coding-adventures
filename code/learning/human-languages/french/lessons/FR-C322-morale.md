## You'll want to know: la morale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C322-RECIT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la morale** — "the moral (of a story); morals".

> La morale de cette histoire est simple.
>
> *The moral of this story is simple.*

*Faire la morale à quelqu'un* — to lecture someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*morale* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mōrālis* "of manners", built on *mōs* "custom, habit" (Lewis & Short).
