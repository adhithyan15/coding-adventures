Russian, chapter 276: Spans of time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
десятилетие (desyatilétiye) — a decade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to happen again, then the Russian for to coincide.

You'll want to know: десятилетие.
десятилетие (desyatilétiye) — "a decade".
За последнее десятилетие город сильно изменился. Za poslédneye desyatilétiye górod sílno izmenílsya. Over the last decade the town has changed a lot.
Neuter. Ten years: десять лет.

The word, taken apart.
десятилетие (desyatilétiye) joins десять "ten" and лето "summer", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic lěto "year" (EtymDB): ten years.

Lesson 2 of 5.
период (períod) — a period, a time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to coincide, then the Russian for a decade.

You'll want to know: период.
период (períod) — "a period, a time".
Это был трудный период в его жизни. Éto byl trúdnyy períod v yevó zhízni. It was a hard period in his life.
Masculine. В период (períod) + genitive — during: в период (períod) дождей.

The word, taken apart.
период (períod) is a borrowing usually traced through French or German to Greek períodos "a going round", from perí "around" and hodós "way"; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
этап (etáp) — a stage, a phase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a decade, then the Russian for a period.

You'll want to know: этап.
этап (etáp) — "a stage, a phase".
Первый этап работы закончен. Pérvyy etáp rabóty zakónchen. The first stage of the work is finished.
Masculine. На этом этапе — at this stage.

The word, taken apart.
этап (etáp) was borrowed from French étape "a stage, a stopping place" (EtymDB), from Old French estaple "a warehouse, a market", itself from Middle Dutch stapel "a pillar, a market" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
промежуток (promezhútok) — an interval, a gap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a period, then the Russian for a stage.

You'll want to know: промежуток.
промежуток (promezhútok) — "an interval, a gap".
В промежутке между уроками мы пили чай. V promezhútke mézhdu urókami my píli chay. In the gap between lessons we drank tea.
Genitive промежутка. Промежуток времени — a stretch of time.

The word, taken apart.
промежуток (promezhútok) is built on промеж "between", a cousin of между; both go back to the Slavic root of межа "a boundary strip", Proto-Slavic medja (EtymDB). EtymDB lists no parent for промежуток (promezhútok).

Lesson 5 of 5.
срок (srok) — a deadline, a time limit; a term.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a stage, then the Russian for an interval.

You'll want to know: срок.
срок (srok) — "a deadline, a time limit; a term".
Срок сдачи проекта — пятница. Srok sdáchi proyékta — pyátnitsa. The deadline for the project is Friday.
В срок (srok) — on time; срок (srok) годности — shelf life.

The word, taken apart.
срок (srok) is usually traced to с- and the root of речь "speech", Proto-Slavic rekti "to say" (EtymDB lists речь), so "what was agreed"; EtymDB lists no parent, and the origin is not fully settled.
