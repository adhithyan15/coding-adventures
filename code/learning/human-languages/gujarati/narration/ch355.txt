Gujarati, chapter 355: Taxes and Public Money.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વેરો (vero) — a tax.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for permission, then the Gujarati for to appoint.

You'll want to know: વેરો.
વેરો — vero — "a tax".
દરેક વરસે અમારે ઘરનો વેરો ભરવો પડે છે. darek varse amāre gharno vero bharvo paḍe che. Every year we have to pay the house tax.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વેરો (vero) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

મંદિર માટે દરેક કુટુંબે ફાળો આપ્યો. mandir māṭe darek kuṭumbe phāḷo āpyo. Every family contributed to the temple.

The word, taken apart.
ફાળો (phāḷo) CDIAL lists Gujarati phāḷo, "a share", under a reconstructed phāla, "a piece split off" (CDIAL 9073): one's share of a common cost.

આર્થિક (ārthik) — economic; financial.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a tax, then the Gujarati for a contribution.

You'll want to know: આર્થિક.
આર્થિક — ārthik — "economic; financial".
નોકરી ગયા પછી અરુણની આર્થિક પરિસ્થિતિ નબળી થઈ. nokrī gayā pachī Aruṇnī ārthik paristhiti nabḷī thaī. After he lost his job, Arun's finances grew weak.

The word, taken apart.
આર્થિક (ārthik) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit ārthika, built on artha, "aim, wealth" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઉઘરાવવું (ughrāvvũ) — to collect money (from people).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for economic, then the Gujarati for a grant.

You'll want to know: ઉઘરાવવું.
ઉઘરાવવું — ughrāvvũ — "to collect money (from people)".
ગામના જુવાનો ઉજાણી માટે ફાળો ઉઘરાવે છે. gāmnā juvāno ujāṇī māṭe phāḷo ughrāve che. The young people of the village are collecting money for a picnic.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધો ફાળો ઉઘરાવ્યો, teṇe badho phāḷo ughrāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઉઘરાવવું (ughrāvvũ) is inherited from udgharati, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to collect money promised" (CDIAL 1971).
