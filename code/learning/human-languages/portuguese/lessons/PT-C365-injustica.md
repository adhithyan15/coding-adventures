## You'll want to know: a injustiça
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C365-JUSTICA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a injustiça** — "the injustice".

> Ela luta contra a injustiça.
>
> *She fights against injustice.*

*Que injustiça!* — how unfair!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*injustiça* is a learned borrowing from Latin *iniūstitia* "injustice" (EtymDB), from *iniūstus* "unjust", built on *iūstus* "fair, righteous" (Lewis & Short).
