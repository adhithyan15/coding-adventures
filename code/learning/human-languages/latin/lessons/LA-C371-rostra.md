## You'll want to know: rōstra, rōstrōrum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C371-ROSTRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rōstra, rōstrōrum** — "the speakers' platform (in the Forum)".

> Senex in rōstra ascendit.
>
> *The old man went up onto the speakers' platform.*

*rōstra, rōstrōrum* n. pl. The platform in the Forum was decked with the bronze beaks (*rōstra*) of captured warships. English *rostrum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rōstra* is the plural of *rōstrum* "a beak, a ship's ram", which is built on *rōdō* "to gnaw" (Lewis & Short). English *rostrum* comes from it.
