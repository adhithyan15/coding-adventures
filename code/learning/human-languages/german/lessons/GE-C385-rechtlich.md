## You'll want to know: rechtlich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C385-AUSNAHME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rechtlich** — "legally, in law".

> Rechtlich ist das kein Problem.
>
> *Legally, that is no problem.*

*Rechtlich* is about the law in general; *gesetzlich* about what a statute says.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rechtlich* is built with *-lich* on *Recht* "law, right", inherited from Old High German *reht* and Proto-Germanic *rehtaz* "right, straight" (EtymDB), a cousin of English *right*.
