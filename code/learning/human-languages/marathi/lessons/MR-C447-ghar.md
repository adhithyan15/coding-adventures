## You'll want to know: घार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C447-BAGLA447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घार** — *ghār* — "a black kite".

> आकाशात एक घार उडत होती.
>
> *ākāśāt ek ghār uḍat hotī.*
>
> A kite was flying in the sky.

Feminine.

A feminine noun: घार उडत होती.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घार** (*ghār*) is the Marathi word for the black kite, the brown bird of prey seen over every town; its origin is not settled.
