## You'll want to know: かわる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C167-DONE167-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かわる** — *kawaru* — "to become different; to change".

> **らいげつ しごとの じかんが かわります。** — *raigetsu shigoto no jikan ga kawarimasu* — "Next month my working hours will change."

Polite **かわります**, *kawarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かわる** (*kawaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
