# acompañar — going as the one who shares bread

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-01, ES-ETYMON-COMPANION-03, ES-LEX-C446-MOVER-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el compañero** and recall what it is made of. Say **mover**.
Turn the companion into something you do.

## You'll want to know: acompañar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C446-MOVER-03]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-01] -->

**acompañar** — to go with somebody, to accompany.

*Te acompaño* — I will come with you, and also *I am here with you* at a
funeral. *¿Me acompañas al médico?* — will you come with me to the doctor?
*Acompañado de* — accompanied by, on a form or a menu.

It is the ordinary way to offer to come along, and it is warmer than the English
*accompany*, which sounds formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C446-MOVER-03, ES-ETYMON-COMPANION-03] -->

**a-** plus **compañ-** plus **-ar**, and the middle is the word you already
took apart.

**el compañero** is *com-* together plus *pānis*, bread — **the one you share
bread with**. So *acompañar* is **to go along as that person**, and a Spanish
speaker offering *te acompaño* is, at root, offering to share a meal's worth of
company.

| word | the bread in it |
|---|---|
| el compañero | the one you share it with |
| acompañar | to be that person for somebody |
| el pan | the bread itself |

That is the third verb in these chapters built the same way — **a-** plus a word
you own plus **-ar**, meaning *to make it that*: *acercar* from *cerca*,
*acompañar* from *compañero*.

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C446-MOVER-03] -->

The warmth is not decoration; it decides where the word is used.

At a Spanish funeral the standard thing to say is **te acompaño en el
sentimiento** — *I accompany you in the feeling*. English has no comfortable
equivalent and reaches for *I'm sorry for your loss*, which places the speaker
outside the grief. The Spanish places them alongside it.

That is worth knowing before you need it, because it is a fixed phrase and
improvising in its place is conspicuous.
