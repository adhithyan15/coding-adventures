## You'll want to know: der Dirigent
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C515-CHOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Dirigent** — "conductor (of music)".

> Am Ende verbeugte sich der Dirigent.
>
> *At the end the conductor took a bow.*

Weak noun: *den Dirigenten*. Plural: *die Dirigenten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Dirigent* comes from Latin *dīrigēns* "directing, steering" (EtymDB), the present participle of *dīrigere* "to direct".
