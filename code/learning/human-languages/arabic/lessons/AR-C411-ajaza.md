## You'll want to know: أجاز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C411-IJAZA411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أجاز** — *ajāza* — "to authorize".

> أجاز الأستاذ استعمال القاموس.
>
> *ajāza al-ustādh istiʿmāl al-qāmūs.*
>
> The professor authorized the use of the dictionary.

Present: يجيز, *yujīz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أجاز** (*ajāza*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-w-z*, "to pass, to go beyond" (Lane). It is Form IV, to let a thing pass: to allow, to authorize.
