**ಕೊಡಲಿ** — *koḍali* — "an axe".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಡಲಿ** (*koḍali*) is a loan from Indo-Aryan. DEDR lists Kannada *koḍali*, "axe", with Tamil *kōṭāli*, in its appendix of such loans (DEDR App. 32).
