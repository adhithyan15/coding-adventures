## You'll want to know: zhuàngguān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C435-SUMMIT435-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuàngguān* — "magnificent; spectacular".

> *shāndǐng de rìchū hěn zhuàngguān* — The sunrise from the summit is spectacular.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuàngguān* is built from *zhuàng* 'grand' and *guān* 'a sight', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrjangH* and *kwan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'big' and the second 'to view'.
