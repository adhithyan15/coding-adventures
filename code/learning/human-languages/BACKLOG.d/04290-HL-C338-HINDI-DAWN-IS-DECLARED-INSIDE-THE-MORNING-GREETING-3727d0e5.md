## HL-C338 — Hindi dawn is declared inside the morning greeting

After the taking-form repair, **प्रभात** is the highest-ranked independent
modern Hindi word in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 31 already
defines it as "dawn, daybreak," derives **सुप्रभात** from **सु + प्रभात**,
contrasts it with everyday **सुबह**, and requires learners to say it inside the
formal greeting. The lesson headword declares only the compound, however, so
reports and downstream cards cannot see the standalone noun learners meet.

Declare **प्रभात** beside **सुप्रभात**, practise it independently before
rebuilding the compound, and recall its meaning. This turns the existing
etymological explanation into reusable vocabulary without adding a new concept
or increasing the gentle-ramp debt.
