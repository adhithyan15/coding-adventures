## You'll want to know: دفنانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C493-MOURNING493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دفنانا** — *dafnānā* — "to bury the dead".

> انہوں نے دادا کو قبرستان میں دفنایا۔
>
> *unhoṅ ne dādā ko qabristān meṅ dafnāyā.*
>
> They buried my grandfather in the graveyard.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پرندے کو دفنایا, *us ne parinde ko dafnāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دفنانا** (*dafnānā*) is built on *dafn*, "burial", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dafn*, made into an Urdu verb with *-ānā*.
