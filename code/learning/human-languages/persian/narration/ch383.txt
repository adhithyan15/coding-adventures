Persian, chapter 383: Business and competition.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
تولید کردن (towlid kardan) — to produce, to manufacture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an organisation, then the Persian for professional.

You'll want to know: تولید کردن.
تولید کردن (towlid kardan) — "to produce, to manufacture".
این کارخانه کفش تولید میکند. in kârkhâne kafsh towlid mikonad. This factory produces shoes.
Towlid "production" + kardan (stem kon-).

The word, taken apart.
تولید is usually traced to Arabic tawlīd "generating, bringing forth", from the same Arabic family as tavallod "birth"; kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 6.
محصول (mahsul) — a product; a crop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for professional, then the Persian for to produce.

You'll want to know: محصول.
محصول (mahsul) — "a product; a crop".
محصولات این شرکت خیلی معروف است. mahsulât-e in sherkat kheyli ma'ruf ast. This company's products are very well known.
Plural mahsulât. Also a harvest from the land.

The word, taken apart.
محصول (mahsul) is usually traced to Arabic maḥṣūl "yield, what is obtained": a product or a crop is what work brings in.

You'll want to know: رقابت.
رقابت (reqâbat) — "competition, rivalry".
رقابت بین دو تیم خیلی سخت بود. reqâbat beyn-e do tim kheyli sakht bud. The competition between the two teams was very tough.
Reqâbat kardan — to compete.

The word, taken apart.
رقابت (reqâbat) is usually traced to Arabic raqāba, from the same Arabic family as raqīb "rival" (EtymDB), the source of Persian raqib.

Lesson 4 of 6.
رقیب (raqib) — a rival, a competitor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a product, then the Persian for competition.

You'll want to know: رقیب.
رقیب (raqib) — "a rival, a competitor".
رقیب ما قیمتها را پایین آورد. raqib-e mâ qeymathâ râ pâyin âvard. Our rival brought prices down.
Plural raqibân. In sport too: raqib-e mâ, our opponent.

The word, taken apart.
رقیب (raqib) comes from Arabic raqīb (EtymDB), a word usually explained as one who watches: a rival keeps an eye on you.

Lesson 5 of 6.
تبلیغات (tablighât) — advertising, advertisements.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for competition, then the Persian for a rival.

You'll want to know: تبلیغات.
تبلیغات (tablighât) — "advertising, advertisements".
در تلویزیون تبلیغات زیادی هست. dar televiziyon tablighât-e ziyâdi hast. There is a lot of advertising on television.
The Arabic-style plural of tabligh "an advertisement". It can also mean propaganda.

The word, taken apart.
تبلیغات (tablighât) is usually traced to Arabic tablīgh "conveying, delivering a message", with the Arabic plural ending -ât.
