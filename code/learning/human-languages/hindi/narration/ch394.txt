Hindi, chapter 394: Prizes and Rewards.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
इनाम (inām) — a prize, a reward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a promotion, then the Hindi for a matter of honour.

You'll want to know: इनाम.
इनाम — inām — "a prize, a reward".
अगर तुम दौड़ जीतोगे तो तुम्हें इनाम मिलेगा। agar tum dauṛ jītoge to tumhẽ inām milegā. If you win the race, you'll get a prize.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
इनाम (inām) is usually traced to Arabic inʿām, "a favour, a gift", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
प्रतियोगिता (pratiyogitā) — a competition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a matter of honour, then the Hindi for a prize.

You'll want to know: प्रतियोगिता.
प्रतियोगिता — pratiyogitā — "a competition".
अगर कल प्रतियोगिता होगी तो पूरा स्कूल आएगा। agar kal pratiyogitā hogī to pūrā skūl āegā. If the competition is tomorrow, the whole school will come.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतियोगिता (pratiyogitā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit pratiyogitā, "correlation" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams does not give the sense of a contest, which is usually taken as a modern Hindi one.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सँवारना (sãvārnā) — to put in order, to make (something) look its best.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a post, then the Hindi for capable.

You'll want to know: सँवारना.
सँवारना — sãvārnā — "to put in order, to make (something) look its best".
अगर तुम अपना कमरा सँवारोगे तो मेहमान ख़ुश होंगे। agar tum apnā kamrā sãvāroge to mehmān khuś hoṅge. If you tidy up your room, the guests will be pleased.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना घर सँवारा, usne apnā ghar sãvārā.

The word, taken apart.
सँवारना (sãvārnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit saṃvārayati, "keeps off; puts in order" (CDIAL 13021).
