Marathi, chapter 355: Election Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मतदान (matdān) — voting; a poll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for population, then the Marathi for rural.

You'll want to know: मतदान.
मतदान — matdān — "voting; a poll".
रविवारी सकाळपासून मतदान सुरू झालं. ravivārī sakāḷpāsūn matdān surū jhālaṁ. Voting began on Sunday morning.
Neuter.
मतदान (matdān) करणे, to vote; मतदान (matdān) केंद्र, a polling station.

The word, taken apart.
मतदान (matdān) is a learned Marathi compound built from Sanskrit mata, "opinion", and dāna, "giving" (Monier-Williams): the giving of one's opinion.

You'll want to know: प्रतिनिधी.
प्रतिनिधी — pratinidhī — "a representative; a delegate".
आमच्या भागाचा प्रतिनिधी लोकांचे प्रश्न मांडतो. āmacyā bhāgācā pratinidhī lokāñce praśna māṇḍto. Our area's representative puts forward people's problems.
Masculine.
Used for a woman too: ती आमची प्रतिनिधी (pratinidhī) आहे; लोकप्रतिनिधी, an elected representative.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिनिधी (pratinidhī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pratinidhi, "a substitute, a representative" (Monier-Williams). Marathi lengthens the final vowel.

Lesson 5 of 5.
निवडून येणे (nivḍūn yeṇe) — to be elected.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a candidate, then the Marathi for canvassing.

You'll want to know: निवडून येणे.
निवडून येणे — nivḍūn yeṇe — "to be elected".
आमच्या गावचा तरुण निवडून आला. āmacyā gāvcā taruṇ nivḍūn ālā. A young man from our village was elected.
Past (he …): तो निवडून आला, to nivḍūn ālā.
Intransitive: the one elected is the subject; निवडून, chosen, + येणे, to come.

The word, taken apart.
निवडून येणे (nivḍūn yeṇe) joins निवडून, the -ऊन form of निवडणे, "to choose", an inherited verb, to येणे, "to come"; CDIAL lists Marathi nivaḍ, "choice, picked", under nirvarayati (CDIAL 7393).
