## You'll want to know: shèmén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C498-PITCH498-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shèmén* — "to shoot at goal".

> *tā shèmén de shíhou qiúmén qiánbian méiyǒu rén* — When he shot, there was nobody in front of the goal.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shèmén* is built from *shè* 'to shoot' and 门 'a gate', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zyaeH* and *mwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with the arts of archery.
