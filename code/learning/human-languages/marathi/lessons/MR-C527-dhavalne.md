# ढवळणे (ḍhavaḷṇe) — to stir

## You'll want to know: ढवळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C527-SHIJAVNE527-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ढवळणे** — *ḍhavaḷṇe* — "to stir".

> आमटी उकळताना मी ती चमच्याने ढवळली.
>
> *āmṭī ukaḷtānā mī tī camcyāne ḍhavaḷlī.*
>
> While the dal was boiling I stirred it with a spoon.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आमटी ढवळली, *tyāne āmṭī ḍhavaḷlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढवळणे** (*ḍhavaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb for stirring; its origin is not settled.
