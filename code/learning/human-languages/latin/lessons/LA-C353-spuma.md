## You'll want to know: spūma, spūmae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C353-FERVEO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**spūma, spūmae** — "foam, froth, scum".

> Spūma alba in aquā ferventī natābat.
>
> *White foam was floating on the boiling water.*

Feminine. English *spume*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**spūma** is linked by Lewis & Short with *spuō*, "to spit". English *spume* comes from it.
