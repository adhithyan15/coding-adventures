## You'll want to know: indicō, indicāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C451-PERCONTOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**indicō, indicāre** — "to make known, to indicate, to show".

> Mīliārium indicat urbem decem mīlia passuum abesse.
>
> *The milestone shows that the city is ten miles away.*

*indicō, indicāre, indicāvī, indicātum*, first conjugation. Not the same as *indīcō, indīcere* 'to proclaim'. English *indicate* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indicō* is built from *in-* and the root of *dīcō* 'to say, to show' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short); *index* 'a pointer' belongs to the same family. English *indicate* comes from it.
