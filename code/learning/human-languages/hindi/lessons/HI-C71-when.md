# कब (kab) — when

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: कब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कब** (*kab*) — "when".

Sanskrit कदा (*kadā*), "when". Look at the first letter and you have the whole
point of this lesson: every Hindi asking word starts with **क**.

You have been collecting them without being told they were a set: *kyā*,
*kaun*, *kahā̃*, *kaise* — what, who, where, how — and now **कब**, when. Five of
them, every one written with a क at the front, all doing the same job.

That letter is the signal. Hear a **क** at the front of a short word at the head
of somebody's sentence and you are being asked something, before you have worked
out what.

कब sits where the answer would sit, not at the front of the sentence the way an
English question word does: **गाड़ी कब है?**
