Punjabi, chapter 249: Fire and Smoke.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਬਾਲਣਾ (bālṇā) — to light a fire; to light a lamp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a chain, then the Punjabi for a wall peg.

You'll want to know: ਬਾਲਣਾ.
ਬਾਲਣਾ — bālṇā — "to light a fire; to light a lamp".
ਦਾਦੀ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਬਾਲਦੀ ਹੈ। dādī savere chullhā bāldī hai. Grandmother lights the stove in the morning.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਾਲਿਆ, maiṁ dīvā bāliā.

The word, taken apart.
ਬਾਲਣਾ (bālṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed dvālayati, "kindles"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bālaṇā, "to kindle" (CDIAL 6671).

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਫੂਕਿਆ, maiṁ chullhā phūkiā.

ਫੂਕਣਾ (phūkṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed phūtka, "blowing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi phūkṇā, "to blow" (CDIAL 9102).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਧੂੰਆਂ (dhūṁāṁ) — smoke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to light a fire, then the Punjabi for to blow on a fire.

You'll want to know: ਧੂੰਆਂ.
ਧੂੰਆਂ — dhūṁāṁ — "smoke".
ਗਿੱਲੀ ਲੱਕੜ ਨਾਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਧੂੰਆਂ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ। gillī lakkaṛ nāl bahut dhūṁāṁ hundā hai. Wet wood makes a lot of smoke.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਧੂੰਆਂ (dhūṁāṁ) is inherited from Sanskrit dhūma, "smoke, vapour"; CDIAL lists Punjabi dhūā̃, "smoke" (CDIAL 6849).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਕੋਲਾ (kolā) — charcoal; coal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to blow on a fire, then the Punjabi for smoke.

You'll want to know: ਕੋਲਾ.
ਕੋਲਾ — kolā — "charcoal; coal".
ਕੋਲਾ ਕਾਲਾ ਅਤੇ ਹੌਲਾ ਹੈ। kolā kālā ate haulā hai. Charcoal is black and light.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੋਲਾ (kolā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Punjabi kolā, "dead coal, charcoal", under a late Sanskrit kōkila, "lighted coal"; the entry records the view that this Sanskrit word was taken from Middle Indo-Aryan and, before that, perhaps from a Munda language (CDIAL 3484).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for smoke, then the Punjabi for charcoal.

You'll want to know: ਅੰਗਿਆਰ.
ਅੰਗਿਆਰ — aṅgiār — "a glowing coal; an ember".
ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਲਾਲ ਅੰਗਿਆਰ ਹਨ। chullhe vich lāl aṅgiār han. There are red embers in the stove.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਅੰਗਿਆਰ (aṅgiār) is inherited from Sanskrit aṅgāra, "glowing charcoal"; CDIAL lists Punjabi aṅgyār, "charcoal" (CDIAL 125).
