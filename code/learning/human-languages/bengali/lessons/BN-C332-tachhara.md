# তাছাড়া (tāchhāṛā) — besides; apart from that

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C331-UDDESHYO331-05, BN-LEX-C332-SUTORANG332-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for useful, then the Bengali for therefore.

## You'll want to know: তাছাড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C332-SUTORANG332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**তাছাড়া** — *tāchhāṛā* — "besides; apart from that".

> আজ আমি ক্লান্ত, তাছাড়া আমার অনেক কাজ আছে।
>
> *āj āmi klāntô, tāchhāṛā āmār ônek kāj āchhe.*
>
> I'm tired today, and besides, I have a lot of work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তাছাড়া** (*tāchhāṛā*) is তা 'that' with ছাড়া 'apart from' (CDIAL 5612 and 4998 list Bengali tā and chāṛā): 'apart from that'. It adds a second reason to the first.

## What you've built
