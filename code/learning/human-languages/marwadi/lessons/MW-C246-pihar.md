## You'll want to know: पीहर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C246-PIHAR246-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पीहर** — *pīhar* — "a woman's parents' home".

> मां काल पीहर गई।
>
> *mā̃ kāl pīhar gaī.*
>
> Mother went to her parents' home yesterday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पीहर** (*pīhar*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*pitṛghara*, "father's house"; CDIAL lists Marwari *pīhar* under it (CDIAL 8180).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
