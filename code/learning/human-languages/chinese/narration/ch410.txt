Mandarin Chinese, chapter 410: Long Trips.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
chángtú — long-distance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a ticket seller, then the Mandarin for to take a taxi.

You'll want to know: chángtú.
chángtú — "long-distance".
zuò chángtú chē qù hěn lèi — Going by long-distance coach is tiring.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
chángtú is built from 长 'long' and tú 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, drjang and du (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a road'.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for long-distance, then the Mandarin for the fare for a ride.

You'll want to know: 通过.
通过 — tōngguò — "to pass through".
检票以后我们从这儿通过 jiǎnpiào yǐhòu wǒmen cóng zhèr tōngguò — After the ticket check we go through here.

The word, taken apart.
通过 (tōngguò) is built from 通 'to go through' and 过 'to cross', both inherited from Middle Chinese, thuwng and kwaH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to go beyond'.
