# -ആൻ — one ending swapped for another

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-CONCEPT-C33-VAAYIKKUKA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the verb for *to read*. Then say two more verbs from that
chapter, and listen to how all of them end.

[PAUSE 2s] That shared ending is about to be replaced.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C74-AAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **-ആൻ** — *-āṉ* — **the "to do it" form**

Every verb you have met ends in **-ഉക** when it is sitting in a dictionary.
Take that ending off and put **-ആൻ** on instead:

| the dictionary form | the -ആൻ form |
|---|---|
| **വായിക്കുക** *vāyikkuka* | **വായിക്കാൻ** *vāyikkāṉ* |
| **പോകുക** *pōkuka* | **പോകാൻ** *pōkāṉ* |
| **കാണുക** *kāṇuka* | **കാണാൻ** *kāṇāṉ* |

**Nothing else in the word changes.** The piece in front of the ending — the
stem — stays exactly as it was, so a verb you can say you can already swap.

This is the ending Malayalam builds two separate things on, and the next two
lessons are those two things. Getting the swap into your hand now makes both of
them free.
