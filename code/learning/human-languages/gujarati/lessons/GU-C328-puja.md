## You'll want to know: પૂજા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C328-PUJA328-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂજા** — *pūjā* — "worship".

> સવારે દાદી મંદિરમાં પૂજા કરે છે.
>
> *savāre dādī mandirmā̃ pūjā kare che.*
>
> In the mornings my grandmother worships at the temple.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂજા** (*pūjā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pūjā*, "honour, worship" (CDIAL 8317). It is done with **કરવું**: **પૂજા કરે છે**.
