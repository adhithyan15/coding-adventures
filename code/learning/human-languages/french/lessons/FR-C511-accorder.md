## You'll want to know: accorder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C511-INSTRUMENTS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**accorder** — "to tune (an instrument); to grant".

> Avant le concert, le guitariste accorde sa guitare.
>
> *Before the concert the guitarist tunes his guitar.*

Also to grant: *accorder une pause*. *S'accorder* — to agree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accorder* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *accordāre* "to bring into agreement" (EtymDB), built on Latin *cor* "heart" (Lewis & Short): to make hearts agree.
