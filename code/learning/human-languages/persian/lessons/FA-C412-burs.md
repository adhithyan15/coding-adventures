## You'll want to know: بورس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C412-SARMAYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بورس** (*burs*) — "the stock exchange".

> قیمت سهام در بورس پایین آمد.
>
> *qeymat-e sahâm dar burs pâyin âmad.*
>
> Share prices fell on the stock exchange.

A French loan. It also turns up in *bursiye* "scholarship".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بورس** is usually traced to French *bourse* "stock exchange; purse", from Late Latin *bursa* "purse".
