# ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ (vimānayāna) — air travel; aviation

## You'll want to know: ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C438-TUKA438-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ** — *vimānayāna* — "air travel; aviation".

> ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ ಈಗ ಮೊದಲಿಗಿಂತ ಅಗ್ಗವಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *vimānayāna īga modaliginta aggavāgide.*
>
> Air travel is cheaper now than before.

ವಿಮಾನ, an aeroplane, plus ಯಾನ, going.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಮಾನಯಾನ** (*vimānayāna*) is built from two Sanskrit words: *vimāna*, "a flying car of the gods" (CDIAL 11823), now an aeroplane, and *yāna*, "going, a vehicle" (CDIAL 10459).

One more word for getting around.
