Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ (patrikōdyama) — journalism.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a jail, then the Kannada for to appear.

You'll want to know: ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ.
ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ — patrikōdyama — "journalism".
ಅವಳು ಕಾಲೇಜಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ ಕಲಿತಳು. avaḷu kālējinalli patrikōdyama kalitaḷu. She studied journalism at college.
ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ, a newspaper, and ಉದ್ಯಮ, an enterprise: the newspaper trade.

The word, taken apart.
ಪತ್ರಿಕೋದ್ಯಮ (patrikōdyama) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of patrikā, "a leaf, a paper", from pattra (CDIAL 7733), and udyama, "undertaking, striving" (CDIAL 2048).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸಾಕ್ಷ್ಯಚಿತ್ರ (sākṣyacitra) — a documentary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಸಾಕ್ಷ್ಯಚಿತ್ರ.
ಸಾಕ್ಷ್ಯಚಿತ್ರ — sākṣyacitra — "a documentary".
ಆ ಸಾಕ್ಷ್ಯಚಿತ್ರ ಕಾಡಿನ ಹುಲಿಗಳ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಇದೆ. ā sākṣyacitra kāḍina huligaḷa bagge ide. That documentary is about the tigers of the forest.
Plural: ಸಾಕ್ಷ್ಯಚಿತ್ರಗಳು, sākṣyacitragaḷu.
ಸಾಕ್ಷ್ಯ, evidence, and ಚಿತ್ರ, a picture: a film of what is real.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಕ್ಷ್ಯಚಿತ್ರ (sākṣyacitra) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of sākṣya, "evidence" (CDIAL 13322), and citra, "bright, a picture" (CDIAL 4803).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಓದುಗ (ōduga) — a reader.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for journalism, then the Kannada for a documentary.

You'll want to know: ಓದುಗ.
ಓದುಗ — ōduga — "a reader".
ಈ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಗೆ ಲಕ್ಷ ಓದುಗರು ಇದ್ದಾರೆ. ī patrikege lakṣa ōdugaru iddāre. This newspaper has a hundred thousand readers.
Plural: ಓದುಗರು, ōdugaru.
-ಗ makes "one who does": ಓದು, to read, gives ಓದುಗ (ōduga), a reader.

The word, taken apart.
ಓದುಗ (ōduga) is a native Kannada noun: ōdu, "to read, to recite" (DEDR 1052), with the ending -ga, "one who does".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a documentary, then the Kannada for a reader.

You'll want to know: ಕೇಳುಗ.
ಕೇಳುಗ — kēḷuga — "a listener".
ರೇಡಿಯೋ ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ರಮಕ್ಕೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಕೇಳುಗರು ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿದರು. rēḍiyō kāryakramakke tumbā kēḷugaru phōn māḍidaru. Many listeners phoned the radio programme.
Plural: ಕೇಳುಗರು, kēḷugaru.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೇಳುಗ (kēḷuga) is a native Kannada noun: kēḷu, "to hear, to listen" (DEDR 2017), with the ending -ga, "one who does".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ (kukhyāta) — notorious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a reader, then the Kannada for a listener.

You'll want to know: ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ.
ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ — kukhyāta — "notorious".
ಆ ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ ಕಳ್ಳನನ್ನು ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಹಿಡಿದರು. ā kukhyāta kaḷḷanannu konege polīsaru hiḍidaru. The police finally caught that notorious thief.
ಕು-, bad, and ಖ್ಯಾತ, known: well known for bad deeds. ಪ್ರಖ್ಯಾತ is famous.

The word, taken apart.
ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ (kukhyāta) is borrowed from Sanskrit: ku-, "bad", with khyāta, "named, well known, notorious" (Monier-Williams).
