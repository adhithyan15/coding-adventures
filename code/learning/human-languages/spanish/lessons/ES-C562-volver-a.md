# volver a — to do again

## You'll want to know: volver a
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C562-VOLVER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**volver a** — "to do again".

> Volvió a llamar a las diez.
>
> *She called again at ten.*

*Volver a* + infinitive: *No volveré a hacerlo* — I won't do it again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*volver a* is built on *volver* 'to return', which is inherited from Latin *volvere* 'to roll, to turn' (Lewis & Short). To do something again is to turn back to it.
