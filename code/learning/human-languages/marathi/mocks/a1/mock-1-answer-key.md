## Writing

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
Score six independently completed fields. Accept the learner's own consistent
details or internally consistent invented details. The labels require the
requested type of content; a city in **भाषा** or a copied model does not fulfil
the field. Accept recognizable Devanagari and documented regional Marathi
forms. Apply the 40-point form rubric.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->
The response must contain 30–40 Marathi words, address **रीमा**, introduce the
writer, name a city and language, state one preferred activity, propose a place
and time, and close appropriately. Accept natural masculine, feminine, or
other first-person agreement when it is internally consistent. Apply the
60-point message rubric; do not award independent-production credit for a
copied model.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->

## Speaking

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
Score five relevant personal responses across the combined 60–120 second
window. Accept natural regional vocabulary and internally consistent gender
agreement. A response may be short, but it must answer the question asked.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
The learner must sustain a one-minute description of a daily routine, organize
at least three relevant details, and speak from keywords rather than a written
answer script. Apply the 30-point speaking criteria.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
The learner must greet, request a drink, ask the price, answer the clarification,
and close politely within 60–120 seconds. Accept taught requests such as
**चहा, कृपया**, **पाणी द्या, कृपया**, and consistent regional alternatives.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
