## You'll want to know: হৃৎপিণ্ড
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C468-HRITPINDO468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**হৃৎপিণ্ড** — *hritpinḍô* — "the heart (the organ)".

> ব্যায়াম করলে হৃৎপিণ্ড ভালো থাকে।
>
> *bæyām kôrle hritpinḍô bhālo thāke.*
>
> Exercise keeps the heart healthy.

হৃদয় (taught) is the heart of feeling; হৃৎপিণ্ড is the pump the doctor listens to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হৃৎপিণ্ড** (*hritpinḍô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *hṛtpiṇḍa*, "heart-mass", the heart (Monier-Williams).
