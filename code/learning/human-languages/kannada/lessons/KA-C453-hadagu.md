## You'll want to know: ಹಡಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C453-HADAGU453-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಡಗು** — *haḍagu* — "a ship".

> ಬಂದರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ದೊಡ್ಡ ಹಡಗು ನಿಂತಿದೆ.
>
> *bandarinalli ondu doḍḍa haḍagu nintide.*
>
> A big ship is standing in the harbour.

Plural: ಹಡಗುಗಳು, *haḍagugaḷu*.

A ದೋಣಿ is a boat; a ಹಡಗು crosses the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಡಗು** (*haḍagu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3838 lists Kannada *paḍagu*, *haḍagu*, the old *p-* turned into *h-*.
