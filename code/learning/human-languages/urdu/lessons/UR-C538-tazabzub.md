## You'll want to know: تذبذب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C538-TAZABZUB538-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تذبذب** — *tazabzub* — "indecision; wavering".

> اگر آپ تذبذب میں ہیں تو کل بتائیے۔
>
> *agar āp tazabzub meṅ haiṅ to kal batāiye.*
>
> If you can't decide, let me know tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تذبذب** (*tazabzub*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tadhabdhub*, "dangling to and fro".
