## You'll want to know: a tela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C505-PINTURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a tela** — "canvas".

> O pintor comprou uma tela grande.
>
> *The painter bought a large canvas.*

In Brazil it also means a screen; in Portugal a screen is *o ecrã*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tela* is inherited from Latin *tēla* "a web, cloth on a loom" (EtymDB), from *texere* "to weave".
