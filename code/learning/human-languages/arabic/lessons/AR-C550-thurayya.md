## You'll want to know: ثريا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C550-SIYANA550-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ثريا** — *thurayyā* — "a chandelier".

> في غرفة الجلوس ثريا كبيرة.
>
> *fī ghurfat al-julūs thurayyā kabīra.*
>
> There is a big chandelier in the sitting room.

الثريا is also the Pleiades, a cluster of stars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ثريا** (*thurayyā*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-r-w*, "to be many, to abound" (Lane). Lane gives al-thurayyā as the Pleiades, a cluster of many stars, and a cluster of lamps.

## What you've built
