## You'll want to know: ঠোকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C507-PHATOL507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠোকা** — *ṭhokā* — "to hammer in; to knock".

> মিস্ত্রি দেওয়ালে একটা পেরেক ঠুকল।
>
> *Mistri deoyāle ekṭā perek ṭhuklô.*
>
> The mechanic hammered a nail into the wall.

Past (he/she …): তিনি দরজায় ঠুকলেন, *tini dôrjāy ṭhuklen*.

The stem vowel rises in the past: ঠুকল, he hammered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠোকা** (*ṭhokā*) comes from a reconstructed root *ṭhōkk*, "knock"; CDIAL lists Bengali *ṭhokā*, "to strike" (CDIAL 5513).
