## You'll want to know: o cume
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C458-SERRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o cume** — "the summit, the top".

> Chegámos ao cume ao meio-dia.
>
> *We reached the summit at midday.*

Also the peak of a career.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cume* goes back to Latin *culmen* 'top, roof' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the Portuguese word without a source.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
