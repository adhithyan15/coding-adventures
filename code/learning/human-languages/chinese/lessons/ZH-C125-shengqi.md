## You'll want to know: 生气
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C125-FEEL125-01]; assesses=[] -->

**生气** — *shēngqì* — "to get angry".

> **因为我没有来他生气了** *yīnwèi wǒ méiyǒu lái tā shēngqì le* — He got angry because I didn't come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**生气** (*shēngqì*) is built from 生 'to grow' and 气 'breath', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjaeng* and *khj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The sense angry is not given there, so how the pair came to it is not settled.
