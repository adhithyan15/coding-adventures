## You'll want to know: ипотека
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C398-ARENDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ипотека** (*ipotéka*) — "a mortgage".

> Они купили квартиру в ипотеку.
>
> *Oní kupíli kvartíru v ipotéku.*
>
> They bought a flat with a mortgage.

Feminine. *Взять ипотеку* — to take out a mortgage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ипотека** is usually traced to Greek *hypothḗkē* "a pledge, a deposit", from *hypotithénai* "to put under, to pledge": the flat is put down as a pledge.
