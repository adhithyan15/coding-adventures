## You'll want to know: کثیف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C440-SARSEDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کثیف** (*kasif*) — "dirty".

> حوله های حمام کثیف بود.
>
> *Hole-hâ-ye hammâm kasif bud.*
>
> The bathroom towels were dirty.

The opposite of *tamiz*. Note the letter ث, said *s*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kasif* was borrowed from Arabic *kaṯīf* (EtymDB). In Arabic the word usually means 'thick, dense'; in Persian the sense moved to 'dirty'.
