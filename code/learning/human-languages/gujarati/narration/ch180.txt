Gujarati, chapter 180: Visits and invitations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મુલાકાત (mulākāt) — visit, meeting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for beforehand, then the Gujarati for certain.

You'll want to know: મુલાકાત.
મુલાકાત — mulākāt — "visit, meeting".
આવતીકાલે અમારી મુલાકાત થશે. — āvtīkāle amārī mulākāt thaśe — "We will meet tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
મુલાકાત (mulākāt) is a loan from Arabic mulāqāt, "meeting", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is l-q-y, "to meet" (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
રાહ જોવી (rāh jovī) — to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for visit, then the Gujarati for invitation.

You'll want to know: રાહ જોવી.
રાહ જોવી — rāh jovī — "to wait".
હું સ્ટેશને તમારી રાહ જોઈશ. — hũ sṭeśane tamārī rāh joīś — "I will wait for you at the station."
Word for word, to watch the road: રાહ, rāh, and જોવું, jovũ.

The word, taken apart.
રાહ જોવી (rāh jovī) is built from rāh, "road, way", usually traced to Persian rāh, "road", and the inherited verb jovũ, "to see".

Lesson 4 of 5.
રોકાવું (rokāvũ) — to stay on, to stop over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for invitation, then the Gujarati for to wait.

You'll want to know: રોકાવું.
રોકાવું — rokāvũ — "to stay on, to stop over".
મહેમાન બે દિવસ રોકાશે. — mahemān be divas rokāśe — "The guests will stay two days."

The word, taken apart.
રોકાવું (rokāvũ) is built on the inherited rokvũ, "to stop, to hold back", with the intransitive ending -āvũ. CDIAL traces rokvũ to rōkk, "stop", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 10827).

Lesson 5 of 5.
વિતાવવું (vitāvvũ) — to pass (time), to spend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to wait, then the Gujarati for to stay on.

You'll want to know: વિતાવવું.
વિતાવવું — vitāvvũ — "to pass (time), to spend".
અમે રજા ગામમાં વિતાવીશું. — ame rajā gāmmā̃ vitāvīśũ — "We will spend the holiday in the village."
The causative of વીતવું, to pass.

The word, taken apart.
વિતાવવું (vitāvvũ) is the causative of the inherited vītvũ, "to pass, of time", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit vṛtta, "passed, elapsed (of time)" (CDIAL 12069).
