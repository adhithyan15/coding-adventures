German, chapter 521: Gedichte und Reime.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
das Gedicht — poem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for non-fiction book, then the German for villain.

You'll want to know: das Gedicht.
das Gedicht — "poem".
Zum Muttertag hat das Kind ein Gedicht gelernt. The child learnt a poem for Mother's Day.
Plural: die Gedichte.

The word, taken apart.
Gedicht is inherited from Middle High German getiht, built on tihten "to compose" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin dictāre "to say often, to dictate" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: der Dichter.
der Dichter — "poet".
Der Dichter las im Café aus seinem neuen Buch vor. The poet read from his new book in the café.
Plural: die Dichter. A woman is die Dichterin.

The word, taken apart.
Dichter is built on dichten "to write poetry" with -er; EtymDB lists dichten without a source, and it is usually traced to Latin dictāre "to say often, to dictate" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 6.
reimen — to rhyme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for poet, then the German for rhyme.

You'll want to know: reimen.
reimen — "to rhyme".
Herz reimt sich auf Schmerz. Herz rhymes with Schmerz.
Mostly reflexive: sich reimen auf + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
reimen is inherited from Middle High German rīmen "to compose, to put into verse, to rhyme" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 6.
der Schriftsteller — writer, author.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for rhyme, then the German for to rhyme.

You'll want to know: der Schriftsteller.
der Schriftsteller — "writer, author".
Mein Onkel ist Schriftsteller und schreibt Krimis. My uncle is a writer and writes crime novels.
Plural: die Schriftsteller. A woman is die Schriftstellerin.

The word, taken apart.
Schriftsteller is built on Schrift "writing", which came through Old High German scrift from Latin scrīptum (EtymDB), and Steller, from stellen "to set": one who sets down writing.
