# بخیل (bakhīl) — miserly

## You'll want to know: بخیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C459-GIVE459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بخیل** — *bakhīl* — "miserly".

> وہ بخیل آدمی پیسہ خرچ نہیں کرتا۔
>
> *voh bakhīl ādmī paisā kharch nahīṅ kartā.*
>
> That miserly man does not spend money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بخیل** (*bakhīl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bakhīl*, "miserly".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
