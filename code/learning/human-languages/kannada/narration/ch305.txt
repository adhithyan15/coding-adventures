The word, taken apart.
ಅರ್ಚಕ (arcaka) is Sanskrit arcaka, "worshipper", from the verb for "to praise, worship".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಲಾವಿದ (kalāvida) is Sanskrit kalā, "any practical art" (CDIAL 2927), with -vid, "knowing": "one who knows an art". CDIAL 2927 takes the Sanskrit kalā itself from Dravidian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನಟ (naṭa) — an actor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an artist, then the Kannada for a singer.

You'll want to know: ನಟ.
ನಟ — naṭa — "an actor".

The word, taken apart.
ನಟ (naṭa) is Sanskrit naṭa, "dancer, actor", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 6933). CDIAL 6933 thinks the Sanskrit word itself probably came from a Middle Indo-Aryan form. DEDR 2936 has a Kannada naṭike, "the cracking of joints", but that is a different word.
