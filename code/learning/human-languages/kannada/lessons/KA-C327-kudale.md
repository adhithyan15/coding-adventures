# ಕೂಡಲೇ (kūḍalē) — at once; immediately

## You'll want to know: ಕೂಡಲೇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C327-THATKSHANA327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೂಡಲೇ** — *kūḍalē* — "at once; immediately".

> ಸುದ್ದಿ ಕೇಳಿದ ಕೂಡಲೇ ಅಜ್ಜ ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಗೆ ಹೊರಟರು.
>
> *suddi kēḷida kūḍalē ajja āspatrege horaṭaru.*
>
> As soon as he heard the news, Grandfather set off for the hospital.

After a past form it means "as soon as": ಕೇಳಿದ ಕೂಡಲೇ, as soon as (he) heard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೂಡಲೇ** (*kūḍalē*) is built on the native Dravidian verb *kūḍu*, "to join, to meet" (DEDR 1882), with the emphatic ending -*ē*; how it came to mean "at once" is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
