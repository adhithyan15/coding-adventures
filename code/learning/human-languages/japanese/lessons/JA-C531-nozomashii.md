## You'll want to know: のぞましい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C531-PURPOSE531-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のぞましい** — *nozomashii* — "desirable; best".

> **のぞましい ほうほうは ばあいに よります。** — *nozomashii hōhō wa baai ni yorimasu* — "The best method depends on the case."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のぞましい** (*nozomashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built on the same stem as *nozomu*, "to wish for"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
