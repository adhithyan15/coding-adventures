## You'll want to know: ふらふら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C447-TIRED447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふらふら** — *furafura* — "unsteadily; dizzily".

> **ねつで ふらふらします。** — *netsu de furafura shimasu* — "The fever makes me unsteady on my feet."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふらふら** (*furafura*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
