## You'll want to know: دود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C328-ATASHSUZI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دود** (*dud*) — "smoke".

> دود سیاه از پنجره بیرون میآمد.
>
> *Dud-e siyâh az panjere birun miâmad.*
>
> Black smoke was coming out of the window.

*Dud kardan* "to smoke; to give off smoke".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dud* is a native Persian word; EtymDB glosses it "smoke; sorrow" and lists no parent.
