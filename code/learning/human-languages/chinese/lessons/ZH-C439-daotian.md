## You'll want to know: dàotián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C439-COUNTRY439-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dàotián* — "rice paddy; paddy field".

> *wǒ yéye nǎinai zhù zài dàotián pángbiān* — My grandparents live next to the rice paddies.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàotián* is built from *dào* 'rice in the field' and *tián* 'a field', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dawX* and *den* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as rice and the second, after an older book, as land that has been ploughed.
