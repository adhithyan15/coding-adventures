## You'll want to know: नापास होणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C371-PARIKSHA371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नापास होणे** — *nāpās hoṇe* — "to fail (an exam)".

> तो गणितात नापास झाला.
>
> *to gaṇitāt nāpās jhālā.*
>
> He failed in maths.

Past (he …): तो नापास झाला, *to nāpās jhālā*.

The school subject takes -त: गणितात नापास, failed in maths.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नापास होणे** (*nāpās hoṇe*) joins नापास, a hybrid of the Persian prefix *nā-*, "not", and a loan from English *pass*, to होणे, "to become".
