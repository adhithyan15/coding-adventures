## You'll want to know: رقم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C417-RAQAM417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رقم** — *raqam* — "a sum of money; an amount".

> اکاؤنٹ میں رقم کم ہے۔
>
> *akāunṭ meṅ raqam kam hai.*
>
> There isn't much money in the account.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رقم** (*raqam*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *raqam*, "a figure, a written mark"; in Urdu it is a sum of money.
