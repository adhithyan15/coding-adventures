## You'll want to know: сквозняк
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C431-SKVOZNYAK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**сквозняк** (*skvoznyák*) — "a draught (of air)".

> Из окна дует, в комнате сквозняк.
>
> *Iz okná dúyet, v kómnate skvoznyák.*
>
> Air is coming in through the window; there is a draught in the room.

Masculine. Built on *сквозь* — through.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сквозняк* is built on *сквозь* 'through' with *-няк*: air that blows right through a room. EtymDB lists the word without a deeper source.
