Portuguese, chapter 350: De onde vem.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
a origem — the origin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for consequently, then the Portuguese for due to.

You'll want to know: a origem.
a origem — "the origin".
Ninguém sabe a origem desta tradição. Nobody knows the origin of this tradition.
Ter origem em — to originate in. De origem portuguesa — of Portuguese origin.

The word, taken apart.
origem is a learned borrowing from Latin orīgō, orīginis "beginning, source, descent" (EtymDB), built on orīrī "to rise, to arise" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 6.
resultar — to result; to work out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the origin, then the Portuguese for to derive.

You'll want to know: resultar.
resultar — "to result; to work out".
O acidente resultou de um erro do motorista. The accident resulted from the driver's mistake.
Resultar de — to result from; resultar em — to lead to. Also to work: o plano resultou.

The word, taken apart.
resultar comes from Medieval Latin resultāre (EtymDB), Latin resultāre "to spring or leap back, to rebound" (Lewis & Short), built on saltāre "to jump".

Lesson 5 of 6.
graças a — thanks to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to result, then the Portuguese for to give rise to.

You'll want to know: graças a.
graças a — "thanks to".
Graças a ti, cheguei a horas. Thanks to you, I arrived on time.
For good results; for bad ones Portuguese says por causa de.

The word, taken apart.
graças a is built on graça "grace, favour", inherited from Latin grātia "favour, gratitude, thanks" (Lewis & Short).
