## You'll want to know: a fronha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C429-QUARTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a fronha** — "the pillowcase".

> A fronha estava suja e pedi outra.
>
> *The pillowcase was dirty and I asked for another.*

Sheets are *os lençóis*.
