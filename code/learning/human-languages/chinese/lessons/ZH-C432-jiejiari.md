## You'll want to know: 节假日
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C432-QUIET432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**节假日** — *jiéjiàrì* — "public holiday".

> *jiéjiàrì de shíhou hěn duō rén zuò huǒchē qù hǎibiān* — On public holidays lots of people take the train to the seaside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**节假日** (*jiéjiàrì*) is built from 节 'a festival', 假 'leave' and 日 'a day', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tset*, *kaeH* and *nyit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the joint of a bamboo stem and the second 'to borrow' and 'leave'; the sense 'a festival' for the first is not given there.
