## You'll want to know: નીમવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C354-ADHIKARI354-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નીમવું** — *nīmvũ* — "to appoint".

> સરકાર નવા અધિકારીને નીમશે.
>
> *sarkār navā adhikārīne nīmśe.*
>
> The government will appoint a new official.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નવો ચોકીદાર નીમ્યો, *teṇe navo cokīdār nīmyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નીમવું** (*nīmvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *niyamayati*, "restrains"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to fix, to appoint" (CDIAL 7251). **નિયમ** (*niyam*), "a rule", is its learned cousin.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
