Kannada, chapter 240: To bark, To shout, To sink, To argue, To imagine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬೊಗಳು (bogaḷu) — to bark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to pay a visit, then the Kannada for to hug.

You'll want to know: ಬೊಗಳು.
ಬೊಗಳು — bogaḷu — "to bark".

The word, taken apart.
ಬೊಗಳು (bogaḷu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 5204). DEDR lists bogaḷu, "to bark".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮುಳುಗು (muḷugu) — to sink; to set (of the sun).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ಮುಳುಗು.
ಮುಳುಗು — muḷugu — "to sink; to set (of the sun)".

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಳುಗು (muḷugu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil muḻuku and Malayalam muḻukuka (DEDR 4993). DEDR gives the older Kannada muḻugu, "to sink, dive".

The word, taken apart.
ವಾದಿಸು (vādisu) is Sanskrit vāda, "speech, dispute" (CDIAL 11507), made into a Kannada verb with -isu.
