## You'll want to know: o transbordo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C416-ATRASOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o transbordo** — "the change (of trains or buses), the transfer".

> Esta viagem tem um transbordo a meio do caminho.
>
> *This trip has a change of trains halfway.*

*Fazer transbordo* — to change trains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*transbordo* is built from *trans-* 'across' and *bordo* 'ship's side': first moving cargo from one ship to another. *Bordo* goes back to Old Portuguese *bordo* (EtymDB) and is usually traced to a Germanic word for 'plank, side'.
