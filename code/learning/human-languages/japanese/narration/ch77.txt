Lesson 1 of 5.
ふつか (futsuka) — the second of the month.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for half a day, then the Japanese for the first of the month.

You'll want to know: ふつか.
ふつか — futsuka — "the second of the month".

The word, taken apart.
ふつか (futsuka) is native: futsu, a form of futa, "two", with ka, "day", an old native word that KANJIDIC2 lists as a reading of the character for day. It is inherited rather than borrowed.

Lesson 4 of 5.
いつか (itsuka) — the fifth of the month; some day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the third of the month, then the Japanese for the fourth of the month.

You'll want to know: いつか.
いつか — itsuka — "the fifth of the month; some day".

The word, taken apart.
いつか (itsuka) is two native phrases that sound alike. "The fifth" is itsu, "five", with ka, "day" (KANJIDIC2); "some day" is itsu, "when", with ka, "or so". Both are inherited rather than borrowed.
