## You'll want to know: le dragon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C321-CONTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le dragon** — "the dragon".

> Le dragon dormait sur une montagne d'or.
>
> *The dragon was sleeping on a mountain of gold.*

In old armies, *un dragon* was also a horse soldier.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dragon* is inherited from Latin *dracō*, *dracōnem* (EtymDB), a borrowing from Greek *drákōn* "serpent".
