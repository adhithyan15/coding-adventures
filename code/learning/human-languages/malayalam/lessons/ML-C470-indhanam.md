## You'll want to know: ഇന്ധനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C470-INDHANAM470-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇന്ധനം** — *indhanaṁ* — "fuel".

> വിമാനത്തിന് ധാരാളം ഇന്ധനം വേണം.
>
> *vimānattinŭ dhārāḷaṁ indhanaṁ vēṇaṁ.*
>
> A plane needs a lot of fuel.

The general word: petrol, diesel, gas and firewood are all ഇന്ധനം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇന്ധനം** (*indhanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *indhana*, "kindling, lighting", and so what is burnt (Monier-Williams).
