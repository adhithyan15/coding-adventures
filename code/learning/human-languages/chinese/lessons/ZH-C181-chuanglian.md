## You'll want to know: chuānglián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C181-BED181-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chuānglián* — "curtain".

> *wòshì de chuānglián hěn hǎokàn* — The bedroom curtains are lovely.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chuānglián* is built from *chuāng* 'window' and *lián* 'a hanging screen', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrhaewng* and *ljem* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a screen of thin strips.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
