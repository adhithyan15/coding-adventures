## You'll want to know: सौम्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C388-VYAVAHARA388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सौम्यः** — *saumyaḥ* — "gentle; mild-mannered".

> यदि नायकः सौम्यः, तर्हि जनाः तम् अनुगच्छन्ति।
>
> *yadi nāyakaḥ saumyaḥ, tarhi janāḥ tam anugacchanti.*
>
> If a leader is gentle, people follow him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौम्यः** (*saumyaḥ*) is built on *soma*, the Soma drink and the moon, with the ending *-ya*; MW gives *saumya* "relating to Soma", then "resembling the moon", and so "placid, gentle, mild" (MW).
