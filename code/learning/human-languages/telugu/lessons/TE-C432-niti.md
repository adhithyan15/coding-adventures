## You'll want to know: నీతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C432-ADARSAM432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**నీతి** — *nīti* — "ethics; a moral".

> ఆ కథలో మంచి నీతి ఉంది.
>
> *ā kathalō mañci nīti undi.*
>
> That story has a good moral.

నీతి కథ, a fable with a moral; నీతిగా, honestly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నీతి** (*nīti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nīti*, "conduct" (CDIAL 7546), and especially "right or wise conduct" (Monier-Williams).
