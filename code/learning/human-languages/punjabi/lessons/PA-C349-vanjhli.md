## You'll want to know: ਵੰਝਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C349-SONG349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੰਝਲੀ** — *vanjhlī* — "a flute; a simple pipe".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵੰਝਲੀ ਦੀ ਧੁਨ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ vanjhlī dī dhun pasand hai.*
>
> I like the tune of the flute.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੰਝਲੀ** (*vanjhlī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vaṁśī*, "flute, pipe"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vañjhlī*, "pipe, flute", under it (CDIAL 11180).
