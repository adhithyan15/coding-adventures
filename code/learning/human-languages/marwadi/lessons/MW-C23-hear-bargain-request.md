# Hear *thoṛu sasto karo* — make it a little cheaper

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-VASTU-01, MW-SCRIPT-VASTU-01, MW-LEX-PRICE-ANSWER-01, MW-SCRIPT-SASTA-01] -->

[PAUSE 24s] Say *this is very expensive*, then say item and cheap and write
both.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-BARGAIN-REQUEST-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-THODU-01, MW-LEX-KARO-01, MW-LEX-SASTA-01] -->

> *thoṛu sasto karo* — **make it a little cheaper**

Three words, all yours: *thoṛu* shrinks the amount, *sasto* is the *sastā* you
learned in Chapter 19, and *karo* asks.

The source prints *sasto* here, with *-o* rather than the *-ā* the earlier
lesson taught. Both forms are used; the ending shifts with what the word is
describing. Learn the printed line as it stands, and treat *sastā* and *sasto*
as the same word wearing two endings rather than two different words.

Say the whole exchange in order: ask the price, say it is too much, then this.
