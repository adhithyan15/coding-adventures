## You'll want to know: अनुसार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C363-ADHYAYAN363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुसार** — *anusār* — "according to (के अनुसार)".

> नए अध्ययन के अनुसार चाय सेहत के लिए अच्छी है।
>
> *nae adhyayan ke anusār cāy sehat ke liye acchī hai.*
>
> According to a new study, tea is good for your health.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुसार** (*anusār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *anusāra*, "following, conformity" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
