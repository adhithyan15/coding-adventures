## You'll want to know: crever
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C404-CREVAISON-02]; assesses=[] -->

**crever** — "to get a flat tyre; to burst".

> J'ai crevé à dix kilomètres de la maison.
>
> *I got a flat tyre ten kilometres from home.*

Informal: *je suis crevé* — I am worn out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crever* is inherited through Old French *crever* from Latin *crepāre* 'to crack, to burst' (EtymDB).
