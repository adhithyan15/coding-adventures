## You'll want to know: へや
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-HEYA]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-YA-01, JA-SCRIPT-HO-01] -->

**へや** — *heya* — "a room".

Two beats: *he–ya*. The second sign is the **や** you already write. The first
one is new: a single low peak, like a roof. When you wrote **ほ**, the row
*ha, hi, fu, he, ho* had one sign missing. This is it, and the next lesson
writes it.
