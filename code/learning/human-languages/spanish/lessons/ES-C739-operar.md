## You'll want to know: operar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C739-CURAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**operar** — "to operate (on)".

> Me operan de la rodilla en mayo.
>
> *I am having a knee operation in May.*

*Operarse de* — to have an operation on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*operar* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *operāre* "to work" (EtymDB), built on *opus* "work".
