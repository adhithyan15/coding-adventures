## You'll want to know: రిక్షా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C451-RIKSHA451-01]; assesses=[] -->

**రిక్షా** — *rikṣā* — "a rickshaw".

> ఆ రోజుల్లో మా తాత రిక్షా తొక్కేవాడు.
>
> *ā rōjullō mā tāta rikṣā tokkēvāḍu.*
>
> In those days my grandfather used to pedal a rickshaw.

A cycle rickshaw is pedalled: రిక్షా తొక్కు. The motor ఆటో took its place in most towns.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రిక్షా** (*rikṣā*) is a loan from English *rickshaw*, which English shortened from a Japanese word for a man-pulled cart.
