## You'll want to know: أوضح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C320-ILAM320-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أوضح** — *awḍaḥa* — "to clarify, to make clear".

> أوضح المعلم الدرس بمثال.
>
> *awḍaḥa al-muʿallim ad-dars bi-mithāl.*
>
> The teacher made the lesson clear with an example.

Present: يوضح, *yūḍiḥ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أوضح** (*awḍaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ḍ-ḥ* (Lane), "to be plain, clear", the root of *wāḍiḥ*, clear. It is Form IV, "to make clear".
