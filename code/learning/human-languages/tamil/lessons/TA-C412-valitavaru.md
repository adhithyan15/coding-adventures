# வழிதவறு (vaḻitavaṟu) — to lose one's way

## You'll want to know: வழிதவறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C412-VAZHI412-02]; assesses=[] -->

**வழிதவறு** — *vaḻitavaṟu* — "to lose one's way".

> காட்டில் நாங்கள் வழிதவறினோம்.
>
> *kāṭṭil nāṅkaḷ vaḻitavaṟiṉōm.*
>
> We lost our way in the forest.

Past (I …): வழிதவறினேன், *vaḻitavaṟiṉēṉ*.

வழி, the way, + தவறு, to slip or miss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வழிதவறு** (*vaḻitavaṟu*) is built from வழி, "a way" (DEDR 5297), and the verb தவறு, "to slip, to miss" (as in தவறவிடு, to miss).
