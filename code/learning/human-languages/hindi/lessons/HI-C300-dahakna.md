## You'll want to know: दहकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C300-DAHAK300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दहकना** — *dahaknā* — "to glow red-hot".

> आग के बाद भी अंगारे दहक रहे थे।
>
> *āg ke bād bhī aṅgāre dahak rahe the.*
>
> Even after the fire the embers were still glowing.

Past (it …): वह कोयला दहका, *vah koylā dahkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दहकना** (*dahaknā*) goes back to Sanskrit *dahati*, "burns" (CDIAL 6245). It is the glow of coals with no flame: embers **दहकते हैं**.
