Telugu, chapter 85: Cold, Hot, and How You Feel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
చలి (cali) — cold, the cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for thirst, then the Telugu for rest.

You'll want to know: చలి.
చలి (cali) — "cold, the cold".
ఇవాళ చలిగా ఉంది — "it is cold today". నాకు చలిగా ఉంది — "I feel cold".
The same frame again: with నాకు it is your body, without it it is the day.

What you've built.
The first word that works for both the weather and the body.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: cali]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cali, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ivāḷa caligā undi, then nāku caligā undi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for thirst, then the Telugu for rest, then say cali again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does చలి (cali) mean? ("Cold, the cold".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
వేడి (vēḍi) — heat; hot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for rest, then the Telugu for the cold.

You'll want to know: వేడి.
వేడి (vēḍi) — "heat", and before a noun, "hot".
ఇవాళ చాలా వేడిగా ఉంది — "it is very hot today". వేడి (vēḍi) నీళ్ళు — "hot water".
It is the pair of చలి (cali), and it slots into the same frame.

What you've built.
Cold and hot, as a pair, in one frame.

భయం (bhayaṁ) — "fear".
నాకు భయంగా ఉంది — "I am afraid". The frame that carried tiredness and cold now carries a feeling, with nothing else changed.

What you've built.
The first feeling, in a frame you can already build.

Lesson 4 of 5.
కోపం (kōpaṁ) — anger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for heat, then the Telugu for fear.

You'll want to know: కోపం.
కోపం (kōpaṁ) — "anger".
నాకు కోపం (kōpaṁ) వచ్చింది — "I got angry", literally "to me anger came". Anger arrives the way a fever did, on the come-verb, and the person it happens to sits in the dative.

What you've built.
A feeling that comes to you, like a fever.

Lesson 5 of 5.
బాధ (bādha) — sorrow, distress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for fear, then the Telugu for anger.

You'll want to know: బాధ.
బాధ (bādha) — "sorrow, distress".
నాకు బాధగా ఉంది — "I feel sad". It also covers the ache behind worry, so it answers మీరు ఎలా ఉన్నారు? honestly on a bad day.

What you've built.
An honest answer to how are you, when the answer is not బాగా.
