## You'll want to know: 人生
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C316-PLACE316-04]; assesses=[] -->

**人生** — *rénshēng* — "a person's life; life from birth to death".

> *rénshēng hěn cháng, nǐ kěyǐ chóngxīn kāishǐ* — Life is long; you can start over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**人生** (*rénshēng*) is built from 人 'a person' and 生 'to live', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyin* and *srjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to grow'.
