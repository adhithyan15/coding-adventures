## You'll want to know: ملحن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C543-MUSIQA543-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ملحن** — *mulaḥḥin* — "a composer".

> كتب الملحن موسيقى الفيلم.
>
> *kataba al-mulaḥḥin mūsīqā al-fīlm.*
>
> The composer wrote the music for the film.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملحن** (*mulaḥḥin*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-ḥ-n*, "to speak with a tone; to make a mistake in speech" (Lane). Mulaḥḥin is the participle of Form II, to set to a tune.
