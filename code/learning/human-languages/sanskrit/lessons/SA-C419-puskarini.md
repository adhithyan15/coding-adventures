## You'll want to know: पुष्करिणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C419-VAPI419-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पुष्करिणी** — *puṣkariṇī* — "a lotus pool".

> पुष्करिण्यां पद्मानि सन्ति।
>
> *puṣkariṇyāṃ padmāni santi.*
>
> There are lotuses in the lotus pool.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुष्करिणी** (*puṣkariṇī*) is built on *puṣkara*, "a blue lotus", with the ending *-iṇī*; MW gives *puṣkariṇī* as a lotus pool, any pool or pond (MW).
