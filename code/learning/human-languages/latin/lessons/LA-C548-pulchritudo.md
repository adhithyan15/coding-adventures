## You'll want to know: pulchritūdō, pulchritūdinis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C548-VENU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**pulchritūdō, pulchritūdinis** — "beauty".

> Pulchritūdō hortī hospitēs dēlectat.
>
> *The beauty of the garden delights the guests.*

Feminine, third declension, from *pulcher*. English *pulchritude*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pulchritūdō** is built on *pulcher* "beautiful" with *-tūdō* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *pulchritude* comes from it.
