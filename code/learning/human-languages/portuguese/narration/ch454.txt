Portuguese, chapter 454: Nos correios.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o remetente — the sender.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to haggle, then the Portuguese for to wrap.

You'll want to know: o remetente.
o remetente — "the sender".
O remetente escreve a morada nas costas do envelope. The sender writes his address on the back of the envelope.
On envelopes: Rem. — sender.

The word, taken apart.
remetente is built on remeter 'to send', which goes back to Latin remittere 'to send back', on mittere 'to send' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists Spanish remitente as a cousin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
o destinatário — the addressee, the recipient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to wrap, then the Portuguese for the sender.

You'll want to know: o destinatário.
o destinatário — "the addressee, the recipient".
A carta voltou porque o destinatário mudou de casa. The letter came back because the addressee had moved.
O destino is where it goes; o destinatário is who gets it.

The word, taken apart.
destinatário is built on destinar 'to intend, to send to', a learned borrowing from Latin dēstināre 'to make fast, to intend' (Lewis & Short).

A encomenda custa dez euros mais os portes. The parcel costs ten euros plus postage.

The word, taken apart.
porte is built on portar 'to carry', from Latin portāre 'to carry' (Lewis & Short): the price of carrying something.

Lesson 4 of 5.
expedir — to dispatch, to send off.

Warm-up.

expedir — "to dispatch, to send off".
A loja expediu a encomenda ontem. The shop dispatched the parcel yesterday.

The word, taken apart.
expedir is a learned borrowing from Latin expedīre 'to free the feet, to get ready' (Lewis & Short), on pēs 'foot'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
registar — to register; to record.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the postage, then the Portuguese for to dispatch.

You'll want to know: registar.
registar — "to register; to record".
Registei a carta para ter um recibo. I registered the letter to get a receipt.
Uma carta registada — a registered letter. In Brazil: registrar.

The word, taken apart.
registar is built on registo 'register', from Late Latin regesta 'things recorded', on regerere 'to carry back, to record' (Lewis & Short).
