## You'll want to know: предотвращать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C373-OSTOROZHNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**предотвращать** (*predotvrashchát*) — "to prevent".

> Эти правила предотвращают аварии.
>
> *Éti právila predotvrashcháyut avárii.*
>
> These rules prevent accidents.

Imperfective; the perfective is *предотвратить*, with *предотвращу, предотвратишь*. Formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**предотвращать** is built from *пред-* "before", *от-* "away" and *вращать* "to turn", the Church Slavonic twin of native *ворочать*: to turn a danger away in advance. The *-ра-* and the *щ* are both Church Slavonic shapes.
