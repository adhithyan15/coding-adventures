Marathi, chapter 414: Guests and Hosts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अतिथी (atithī) — an honoured guest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an overnight stay, then the Marathi for an eating house.

You'll want to know: अतिथी.
अतिथी — atithī — "an honoured guest".
समारंभाला मोठे अतिथी आले होते. samārambhālā moṭhe atithī āle hote. Distinguished guests had come to the ceremony.
Masculine.
More formal than पाहुणा; the plural is the same: एक अतिथी (atithī), अनेक अतिथी (atithī).

The word, taken apart.
अतिथी (atithī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit atithi, "a guest" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
आदरातिथ्य (ādarātithya) — hospitality.

You'll want to know: आदरातिथ्य.
आदरातिथ्य — ādarātithya — "hospitality".
त्यांच्या आदरातिथ्याने आम्ही खूश झालो. tyāñcyā ādarātithyāne āmhī khūś jhālo. We were delighted by their hospitality.
Neuter.
आदर, respect, joined to आतिथ्य, guest care.

The word, taken apart.
आदरातिथ्य (ādarātithya) is built from आदर, "respect", and Sanskrit ātithya, "proper for a guest, hospitality" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
स्वागतकक्ष (svāgatkakṣa) — a reception desk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an honoured guest, then the Marathi for hospitality.

You'll want to know: स्वागतकक्ष.
स्वागतकक्ष — svāgatkakṣa — "a reception desk".
स्वागतकक्षात आम्हाला खोलीची किल्ली मिळाली. svāgatkakṣāt āmhālā kholīcī killī miḷālī. We got our room key at the reception desk.
Masculine.
The front desk of a hotel or an office; कक्ष is a room.

The word, taken apart.
स्वागतकक्ष (svāgatkakṣa) is a modern word built from Sanskrit svāgata, "welcome", and kakṣa, "an inner room" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
विचारपूस करणे (vicārpūs karṇe) — to ask after.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for hospitality, then the Marathi for a reception desk.

You'll want to know: विचारपूस करणे.
विचारपूस करणे — vicārpūs karṇe — "to ask after".
आजीने फोनवर सगळ्यांची विचारपूस केली. ājīne phonvar sagaḷyāñcī vicārpūs kelī. Grandmother asked after everyone on the phone.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने विचारपूस केली, tyāne vicārpūs kelī.
The person asked about takes -ची: माझी विचारपूस केली, asked after me.

The word, taken apart.
विचारपूस करणे (vicārpūs karṇe) is built from विचार, "a thought, a question", and pūs, "asking", from the family of Sanskrit pṛcchati, "asks", where CDIAL lists Marathi pusṇẽ, "to ask" (CDIAL 8352); करणे makes it a verb.

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
फिरकणे (phirakṇe) — to drop by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a reception desk, then the Marathi for to ask after.

You'll want to know: फिरकणे.
फिरकणे — phirakṇe — "to drop by".
सुट्टीत तो आमच्याकडे फिरकलाच नाही. suṭṭīt to āmcyākaḍe phiraklāc nāhī. In the holidays he did not even drop by our place.
Past (he …): तो फिरकला, to phiraklā.
Mostly with a negative: इकडे फिरकला नाही, he never came this way.

The word, taken apart.
फिरकणे (phirakṇe) is a Marathi verb that looks built on phir-, "to turn" (CDIAL 9078); how it came to mean dropping in is not settled.
