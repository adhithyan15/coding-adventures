## You'll want to know: 游记
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C434-GROUP434-05]; assesses=[] -->

**游记** — *yóujì* — "travel notes; account of a journey".

> *huílai yǐhòu tā xiě le yì běn yóujì* — After she came back, she wrote a book about the trip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**游记** (*yóujì*) is built from 游 'to roam' and 记 'to record', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yuw* and *kiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to record'.
