## You'll want to know: تفوق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C393-TAQYIM393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تفوق** — *tafawwaqa* — "to excel".

> تفوق ابن جارنا في الرياضيات.
>
> *tafawwaqa ibn jārinā fī ar-riyāḍiyyāt.*
>
> Our neighbour's son excelled in mathematics.

Present: يتفوق, *yatafawwaq*.

It takes في for the field, and على for the people one beats.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفوق** (*tafawwaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-w-q*, "to be above" (Lane). It is Form V, to rise above others: to excel. فوق, above, is from the same root.
