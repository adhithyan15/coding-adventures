Mandarin Chinese, chapter 497: Learning on Your Own.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
自学 (zìxué) — to teach oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for elementary (in level), then the Mandarin for to repeat a school year.

You'll want to know: 自学.
自学 — zìxué — "to teach oneself".
我自学了两年汉语现在是中级 wǒ zìxué le liǎng nián Hànyǔ xiànzài shì zhōngjí — I taught myself Chinese for two years, and now I'm at intermediate level.

The word, taken apart.
自学 (zìxué) is built from 自 'oneself' and 学 'to learn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzijH and haewk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'from'; the sense 'oneself' is not given there.

课外 — kèwài — "outside class; extracurricular".
我还没有做课外作业 wǒ hái méiyǒu zuò kèwài zuòyè — I haven't done my homework yet.

The word, taken apart.
课外 (kèwài) is built from 课 'a lesson' and 外 'outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khwaH and ngwajH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a tax' and 'to test' and the second 'the outside'.

Lesson 4 of 6.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for outside class, then the Mandarin for notes (taken in class).

You'll want to know: zhīshi.
zhīshi — "knowledge".
zhīshi bǐ qián zhòngyào — Knowledge matters more than money.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhīshi is built from zhī 'to know' and shí 'to recognise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, trje and syik (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to perceive' and the second 'to know'; in the word the second syllable is light.
