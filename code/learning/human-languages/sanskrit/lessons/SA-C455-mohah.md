## You'll want to know: मोहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C455-ANURAGA455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहः** — *mohaḥ* — "infatuation; delusion".

> धनस्य मोहः जनं मूर्खं करोति।
>
> *dhanasya mohaḥ janaṃ mūrkhaṃ karoti.*
>
> Infatuation with money makes a person foolish.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहः** (*mohaḥ*) comes from the root √muh, "to be bewildered"; MW gives "bewilderment, infatuation, delusion" (MW).
