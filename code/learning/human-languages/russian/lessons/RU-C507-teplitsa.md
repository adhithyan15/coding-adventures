## You'll want to know: теплица
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C507-BLOSSOM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**теплица** (*teplítsa*) — "a greenhouse".

> В теплице у нас растут огурцы.
>
> *V teplítse u nas rastút ogurtsý.*
>
> We grow cucumbers in the greenhouse.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*теплица* is built on the root of *тёплый* 'warm' with *-ица*; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
