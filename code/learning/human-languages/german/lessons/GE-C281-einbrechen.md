## You'll want to know: einbrechen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C281-EINBRUCH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**einbrechen** — "to break in, to burgle".

> In der Nacht brachen Diebe in das Geschäft ein.
>
> *At night thieves broke into the shop.*

Separable, Perfekt with *sein*: *Jemand ist eingebrochen*. Also of ice: *Das Eis brach ein*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*einbrechen* joins *ein* "in" and *brechen* "to break", from Old High German *brehhan*, Proto-Germanic *brekaną* (EtymDB).
