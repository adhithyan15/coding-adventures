Malayalam, chapter 516: Blocked Roads.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
കുഴി (kuḻi) — a pothole; a pit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to get tangled, then the Malayalam for to grope for.

You'll want to know: കുഴി.
കുഴി — kuḻi — "a pothole; a pit".
വഴിയിലെ വലിയ കുഴിയിൽ ബൈക്ക് വീണു. vaḻiyile valiya kuḻiyil baikkŭ vīṇu. The motorbike fell into a big pothole in the road.
Plural: കുഴികൾ, kuḻikaḷ.
Any hole in the ground: a pit, a grave, a pothole.

The word, taken apart.
കുഴി (kuḻi) is native Dravidian, "a hollow, a hole, a pit" (DEDR 1818).

Lesson 3 of 7.
അടയുക (aṭayuka) — to get blocked; to be shut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a pothole, then the Malayalam for dust.

You'll want to know: അടയുക.
അടയുക — aṭayuka — "to get blocked; to be shut".
ചവറ് കാരണം തോടുകൾ അടഞ്ഞു. cavaṟŭ kāraṇaṁ tōṭukaḷ aṭaññu. Because of the litter, the streams got blocked.
Past: അടഞ്ഞു, aṭaññu.
The intransitive partner of അടയ്ക്കുക, to close: a road, a drain or a nose gets blocked.

The word, taken apart.
അടയുക (aṭayuka) is native Dravidian, "to be shut, to be blocked up" (DEDR 83).

Lesson 4 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for dust, then the Malayalam for to get blocked.

You'll want to know: തടയുക.
തടയുക — taṭayuka — "to block; to stop".
പോലീസ് പാലത്തിൽ വണ്ടികൾ തടഞ്ഞു. pōlīsŭ pālattil vaṇṭikaḷ taṭaññu. The police stopped the vehicles at the bridge.
Past: തടഞ്ഞു, taṭaññu.
What is stopped takes no ending or -എ: വണ്ടികൾ തടഞ്ഞു.

The word, taken apart.
തടയുക (taṭayuka) is native Dravidian, "to be obstructed; to stop" (DEDR 3031).

Lesson 5 of 7.
തടസ്സപ്പെടുക (taṭassappeṭuka) — to be obstructed; to be held up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to get blocked, then the Malayalam for to block.

You'll want to know: തടസ്സപ്പെടുക.
തടസ്സപ്പെടുക — taṭassappeṭuka — "to be obstructed; to be held up".
മരം വീണ് ഗതാഗതം തടസ്സപ്പെട്ടു. maraṁ vīṇŭ gatāgataṁ taṭassappeṭṭu. A tree fell and the traffic was held up.
Past: തടസ്സപ്പെട്ടു, taṭassappeṭṭu.
തടസ്സം, an obstacle, plus -പ്പെടുക, to undergo.

The word, taken apart.
തടസ്സപ്പെടുക (taṭassappeṭuka) is a native Dravidian verb built on തടസ്സം, an obstruction (DEDR 3031), with -പ്പെടുക.
