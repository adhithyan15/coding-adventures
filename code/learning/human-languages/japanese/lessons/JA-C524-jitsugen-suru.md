## You'll want to know: じつげんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C524-IMAGINE524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**じつげんする** — *jitsugen suru* — "to be realized; to come true".

> **もし ゆめが じつげんしたら、あねは よろこぶでしょう。** — *moshi yume ga jitsugen shitara, ane wa yorokobu deshō* — "If her dream comes true, my elder sister will be overjoyed."

Polite **じつげんします**, *jitsugenshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かれの ゆめが じつげんしました。**, *kare no yume ga jitsugenshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じつげんする** (*jitsugen suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *jitsugen* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jitsu*, "reality" (Middle Chinese *zyit*) and *gen*, "present" (*henH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
