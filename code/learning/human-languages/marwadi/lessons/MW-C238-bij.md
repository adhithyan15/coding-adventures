## You'll want to know: बीज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C238-SOW238-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बीज** — *bīj* — "a seed".

> म्हारो बीज आछो है।
>
> *mhāro bīj āchho hai.*
>
> My seed is good.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बीज** (*bīj*) is a tatsama, taken over from Sanskrit *bīja*, "a seed" (Monier-Williams); the inherited forms CDIAL lists, such as Hindi *biyā*, have lost the *j* (CDIAL 9250).
