## You'll want to know: અવ્યવસ્થા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C391-GADBAD391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અવ્યવસ્થા** — *avyavasthā* — "disorder".

> જો સ્ટેશન પર ભીડ વધશે તો અવ્યવસ્થા ફેલાશે.
>
> *jo sṭeśan par bhīḍ vadhśe to avyavasthā phelāśe.*
>
> If the crowd at the station grows, disorder will spread.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અવ્યવસ્થા** (*avyavasthā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *avyavasthā*, "irregularity, want of order" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for saying what happens if.
