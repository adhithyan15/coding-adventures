## You'll want to know: нападающий
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C509-FOOTBALL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**нападающий** (*napadáyushchiy*) — "a forward, a striker".

> Наш нападающий — самый быстрый в команде.
>
> *Nash napadáyushchiy — sámyy býstryy v kománde.*
>
> Our striker is the fastest in the team.

Declined like an adjective. The present participle of *нападать* — to attack.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*нападающий* is the present participle of *нападать* 'to attack', built on *на-* and *падать* 'to fall'; EtymDB has no entry.
