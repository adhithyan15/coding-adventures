## You'll want to know: प्रमुखः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C357-SAMITI357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमुखः** — *pramukhaḥ* — "foremost; chief".

> सः सभायाः प्रमुखः सदस्यः अस्ति।
>
> *saḥ sabhāyāḥ pramukhaḥ sadasyaḥ asti.*
>
> He is the foremost member of the assembly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमुखः** (*pramukhaḥ*) is built from *pra*, "before", and *mukha*, "the face"; MW gives *pra-mukha* "first, foremost, chief, principal" (MW).
