## You'll want to know: پکنک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C513-FURSAT513-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پکنک** — *piknik* — "a picnic".

> اتوار کو ہم پارک میں پکنک پر جائیں گے۔
>
> *itvār ko ham pārk meṅ piknik par jāeṅ ge.*
>
> On Sunday we will go for a picnic in the park.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پکنک** (*piknik*) is borrowed from English *picnic*.
