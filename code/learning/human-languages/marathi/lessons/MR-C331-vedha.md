## You'll want to know: वेढा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C331-VEDHA331-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वेढा** — *veḍhā* — "a siege".

> शत्रूचा वेढा सहा महिने चालला.
>
> *śatrūcā veḍhā sahā mahine cāllā.*
>
> The enemy's siege lasted six months.

Masculine.

वेढा घालणे, to lay siege; वेढा उठवणे, to lift it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेढा** (*veḍhā*) is inherited, of the family of Sanskrit *veṣṭa*, 'a band, an enclosure': CDIAL lists Marathi *veḍh*, 'a ring, a circumference', under it (CDIAL 12130), and *veḍhṇẽ*, 'to twist, surround', under *veṣṭayati*, 'surrounds' (CDIAL 12132).
