Kannada, chapter 103: Where I Live.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮನೆ (mane) — house, home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for only one, then the Kannada for for no reason.

You'll want to know: ಮನೆ.
ಮನೆ (mane) — "house, home".
ನಮ್ಮ ಮನೆ (namma mane) — "our home".

What you've built.
The place every question about you starts from.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mane]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mane, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say namma mane]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for only one, then the Kannada for for no reason, then say mane again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಮನೆ (mane) mean? ("House, home".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 5.
