## You'll want to know: jíshǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C396-NOMATTER396-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jíshǐ* — "even if".

> *jíshǐ xiàyǔ wǒmen yě yào qù* — Even if it rains, we're still going.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jíshǐ* is built from *jí* 'to go up to' and *shǐ* 'to make (someone do)', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsik* and *sriX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go to' and 'now' and the second 'to employ' and 'to order'. How the pair came to mean 'even if' is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
