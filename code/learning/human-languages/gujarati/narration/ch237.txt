Gujarati, chapter 237: Wings in the air.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કાગડો (kāgḍo) — crow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for beak, then the Gujarati for to peck.

You'll want to know: કાગડો.
કાગડો — kāgḍo — "crow".
ગઈકાલે એક કાગડો ઘરમાં આવ્યો. — gaīkāle ek kāgḍo gharmā̃ āvyo — "Yesterday a crow came into the house."

The word, taken apart.
કાગડો (kāgḍo) is usually traced to Sanskrit kāka, "crow", an imitative word that CDIAL says took various suffixes, with -k- or -g-, in the modern languages. CDIAL lists only Gujarati kāu, "crow", there, so the path to kāgḍo is not spelled out (CDIAL 2993).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઘુવડ (ghuvaḍ) — owl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to peck, then the Gujarati for crow.

You'll want to know: ઘુવડ.
ઘુવડ — ghuvaḍ — "owl".
રાત્રે ઘુવડ બોલ્યું. — rātre ghuvaḍ bolyũ — "The owl called at night."

The word, taken apart.
ઘુવડ (ghuvaḍ) is inherited from Sanskrit ghūka, "owl", which CDIAL takes from Dravidian, as in Tamil kūkai, after T. Burrow (CDIAL 4494).

Lesson 3 of 5.
પાંખ (pā̃kh) — wing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for crow, then the Gujarati for owl.

You'll want to know: પાંખ.
પાંખ — pā̃kh — "wing".
પંખીની પાંખ ભીની હતી. — pankhīnī pā̃kh bhīnī hatī — "The bird's wing was wet."

The word, taken apart.
પાંખ (pā̃kh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pakṣa, "wing, feather; side"; CDIAL queries where its nasal came from (CDIAL 7627).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ફફડવું (phaphaḍvũ) — to flutter, to flap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for owl, then the Gujarati for wing.

You'll want to know: ફફડવું.
ફફડવું — phaphaḍvũ — "to flutter, to flap".
પંખીની પાંખ ફફડી. — pankhīnī pā̃kh phaphaḍī — "The bird's wing fluttered."

The word, taken apart.
ફફડવું (phaphaḍvũ) is imitative of the sound of wings. CDIAL lists the close Gujarati phaṛphaṛvũ, "to flutter", under phaṭ, "sudden movement", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 9038).

Warm-up.

ઊડવું — ūḍvũ — "to fly up, to fly".
કાગડો આકાશમાં ઊડ્યો. — kāgḍo ākāśmā̃ ūḍyo — "The crow flew up into the sky."

The word, taken apart.
ઊડવું (ūḍvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from uḍḍīyate, "flies up" (CDIAL 1697).
