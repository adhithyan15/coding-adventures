হাজার (hājār) — a thousand.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: হাজার.
হাজার — hājār — "a thousand".

The word, taken apart.
হাজার (hājār) is a loan, usually traced to Persian hazār, "thousand".

Lesson 3 of 5.
রং (rông) — a colour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a thousand, then the Bengali for the world.

You'll want to know: রং.
রং — rông — "a colour".

The word, taken apart.
রং (rông) matches both Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour", under which CDIAL lists Bengali rāṅā, "red" (CDIAL 10560), and Persian rang, "colour"; the two may have run together.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ছুটি (chhuṭi) — a holiday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a colour, then the Bengali for a song.

You'll want to know: ছুটি.
ছুটি — chhuṭi — "a holiday".

The word, taken apart.
ছুটি (chhuṭi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kṣuṭyate, "is released", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3707).
