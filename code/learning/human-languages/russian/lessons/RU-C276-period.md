## You'll want to know: период
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C276-DESYATILETIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**период** (*períod*) — "a period, a time".

> Это был трудный период в его жизни.
>
> *Éto byl trúdnyy períod v yevó zhízni.*
>
> It was a hard period in his life.

Masculine. *В период* + genitive — during: *в период дождей*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*период* is a borrowing usually traced through French or German to Greek *períodos* "a going round", from *perí* "around" and *hodós* "way"; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.
