## You'll want to know: zǒngshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C162-OFTEN162-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒngshì* — "every single time; always".

> *tā zǒngshì hěn máng* — He's busy all the time.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒngshì* is built from *zǒng* 'all, gathered together' and 是 'to be', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsuwngX* and *dzyeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); how the pair came to mean every time is not settled.
