## You'll want to know: प्राणिसंग्रहालय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C420-DUTAVAS420-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राणिसंग्रहालय** — *prāṇisaṅgrahālay* — "a zoo".

> प्राणिसंग्रहालयात आम्ही वाघ पाहिला.
>
> *prāṇisaṅgrahālayāt āmhī vāgh pāhilā.*
>
> We saw a tiger at the zoo.

Neuter.

प्राणी, an animal, shortens to प्राणि- at the front of a compound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राणिसंग्रहालय** (*prāṇisaṅgrahālay*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *prāṇin*, "a living being", *saṁgraha*, "a collection", and *ālaya*, "a house" (Monier-Williams).
