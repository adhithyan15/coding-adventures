Portuguese, chapter 130: Sixty, Seventy, Eighty, Ninety, To begin.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
sessenta — sixty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to listen, then the Portuguese for to look.
[pause 2 seconds]
