## You'll want to know: 请教
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C493-TUTOR493-05]; assesses=[] -->

**请教** — *qǐngjiào* — "to seek advice (from someone who knows more)".

> **我常常请教我的上司** *wǒ chángcháng qǐngjiào wǒ de shàngsi* — I often ask my boss for advice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**请教** (*qǐngjiào*) is built from 请 'to ask' and 教 'to instruct', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjengX* and *kaewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to beg' and 'to ask'.
