## You'll want to know: أعفى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C410-IBAHA410-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أعفى** — *aʿfā* — "to exempt".

> أعفى المدير الموظف من العمل يوم العيد.
>
> *aʿfā al-mudīr al-muwaẓẓaf min al-ʿamal yawm al-ʿīd.*
>
> The manager exempted the employee from work on the feast day.

Present: يعفي, *yuʿfī*.

It takes من: أعفاه من ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أعفى** (*aʿfā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-f-w*, "to efface; to pardon" (Lane). It is Form IV, to release someone from a thing: to exempt.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
