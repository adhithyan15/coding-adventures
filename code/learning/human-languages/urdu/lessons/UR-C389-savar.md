## You'll want to know: سوار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C389-UNGHNA389-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سوار** — *savār* — "on board; riding".

> ہم صبح گاڑی میں سوار تھے۔
>
> *ham subah gāṛī meṅ savār the.*
>
> We were on board the train in the morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *savār*, "a rider, mounted"; in Urdu a passenger who has got on a bus or a train is **سوار** too.
