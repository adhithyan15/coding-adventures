## You'll want to know: 小心眼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C471-EYES471-03]; assesses=[] -->

**小心眼** — *xiǎoxīnyǎn* — "petty; narrow-minded".

> **他有点儿小心眼** *tā yǒudiǎnr xiǎoxīnyǎn* — He's a bit petty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**小心眼** (*xiǎoxīnyǎn*) is built from 小 'small', 心 'heart' and 眼 'eye', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX*, *sim* and *ngeanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a heart with a small eye, which sees only small things.
