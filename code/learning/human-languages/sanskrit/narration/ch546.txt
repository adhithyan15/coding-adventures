Lesson 1 of 5.
सन्तुष्टः (santuṣṭaḥ) — satisfied; contented.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for at first, then the Sanskrit for reviews.

You'll want to know: सन्तुष्टः.
सन्तुष्टः — santuṣṭaḥ — "satisfied; contented".
यदि परिणामः शुभः अस्ति, तर्हि पिता सन्तुष्टः भवति। yadi pariṇāmaḥ śubhaḥ asti, tarhi pitā santuṣṭaḥ bhavati. If the result is good, father is satisfied.

The word, taken apart.
सन्तुष्टः (santuṣṭaḥ) is built on sam, "fully", and tuṣṭa, "pleased", the past participle of the root √tuṣ, "to be pleased", which also gives tuṣyati; MW gives saṃtuṣṭa as quite satisfied, contented, well pleased (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
उद्भवति (udbhavati) — arises.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for satisfied, then the Sanskrit for completed.

You'll want to know: उद्भवति.
उद्भवति — udbhavati — "arises".
यदि प्रश्नः उद्भवति, तर्हि अध्यापकं पृच्छतु। yadi praśnaḥ udbhavati, tarhi adhyāpakaṃ pṛcchatu. If a question comes up, ask the teacher.
Past (it …): प्रश्नः उद्भूतवान्, praśnaḥ udbhūtavān.

The word, taken apart.
उद्भवति (udbhavati) is built on ud, "up", and the root √bhū, "to become", which also gives bhavati; MW gives udbhū as to come forth, to arise, to spring from; its past participle is udbhūta (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for completed, then the Sanskrit for arises.

You'll want to know: प्राप्तिः.
प्राप्तिः — prāptiḥ — "an attainment".
यदि लक्ष्यस्य प्राप्तिः भवति, तर्हि वयम् उत्सवं करिष्यामः। yadi lakṣyasya prāptiḥ bhavati, tarhi vayam utsavaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ. If we reach our goal, we will celebrate.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्राप्तिः (prāptiḥ) is built on pra, "forth", and the root √āp, "to reach", which also gives prāpnoti; MW gives prāpti as reaching, obtaining, attainment (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
विपाकः (vipākaḥ) — a consequence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for arises, then the Sanskrit for an attainment.

You'll want to know: विपाकः.
विपाकः — vipākaḥ — "a consequence".
यदि कर्म उत्तमम् अस्ति, तर्हि विपाकः अपि उत्तमः। yadi karma uttamam asti, tarhi vipākaḥ api uttamaḥ. If a deed is good, its outcome is good too.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विपाकः (vipākaḥ) is built on vi and the root √pac, "to cook, to ripen", which also gives pacati; MW gives vipāka as ripening, maturing, the result or consequence of actions (MW).
