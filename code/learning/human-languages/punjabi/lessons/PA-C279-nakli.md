## You'll want to know: ਨਕਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C279-SWAP279-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਕਲੀ** — *naklī* — "fake; imitation".

> ਇਹ ਘੜੀ ਨਕਲੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh ghaṛī naklī hai.*
>
> This watch is fake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਕਲੀ** (*naklī*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *naql*, "a copy", with the Punjabi ending *-ī*.
