## You'll want to know: いくら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C181-SHOP181-03]; assesses=[] -->

**いくら** — *ikura* — "how much".

> **この ほんは いくらですか。** — *kono hon wa ikura desu ka* — "How much is this book?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いくら** (*ikura*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi; JMdict marks it as usually written in kana (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
