## You'll want to know: yāoqiú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C399-ONLYIF399-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yāoqiú* — "to require; to demand (of someone)".

> *lǎoshī yāoqiú wǒmen měitiān shuō hànyǔ* — The teacher asks us to speak Chinese every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yāoqiú* is built from *yāo* 'to press' and *qiú* 'to seek', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jiew* and *gjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to force, in common speech' and the second 'to look for'.
