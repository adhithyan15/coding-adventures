## You'll want to know: પ્રદર્શન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C525-SHILP525-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રદર્શન** — *pradarśan* — "an exhibition".

> શાળામાં ચિત્રોનું પ્રદર્શન છે.
>
> *śāḷāmā̃ citronũ pradarśan che.*
>
> There is an exhibition of paintings at the school.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રદર્શન** (*pradarśan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pradarśana*, "a look, a showing" (Monier-Williams).
