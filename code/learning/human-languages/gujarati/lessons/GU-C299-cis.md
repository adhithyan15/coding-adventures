## You'll want to know: ચીસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C299-BUM299-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચીસ** — *cīs* — "a scream".

> અચાનક મીરાના મોઢામાંથી ચીસ નીકળી ગઈ.
>
> *acānak Mīrānā moḍhāmā̃thī cīs nīkḷī gaī.*
>
> Suddenly a scream broke from Mira.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચીસ** (*cīs*) comes from *\*cīssa*, "a sharp pain", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists Gujarati *cīs*, "a scream" (CDIAL 4847): the cry that pain draws out. A scream **નીકળે છે**, comes out of you: **ચીસ નીકળી ગઈ**.
