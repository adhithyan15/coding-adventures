## You'll want to know: ٹوکن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C421-KAGHAZ421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹوکن** — *ṭokan* — "a token".

> بینک میں پہلے ٹوکن لیجیے۔
>
> *baink meṅ pehle ṭokan lījiye.*
>
> At the bank, please take a token first.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹوکن** (*ṭokan*) is borrowed from English *token*.
