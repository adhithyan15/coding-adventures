Italian, chapter 496: Il primo anno.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il pannolino — nappy, diaper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for stepmother, then the Italian for half-brother.

You'll want to know: il pannolino.
il pannolino — "nappy, diaper".
Devo cambiare il pannolino al bambino. I have to change the baby's nappy.
Built on panno "cloth".

The word, taken apart.
pannolino is a little form built on panno "cloth", inherited from Latin pannus "a cloth, a rag" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Porto la bambina al parco con il passeggino. I take the little girl to the park in the pushchair.
Built on passeggiare "to stroll". A pram for a newborn is la carrozzina.

The word, taken apart.
passeggino is built on passeggiare "to go for a walk", from passo "step", inherited from Latin passus "a step" (Lewis & Short).

Ha allattato il figlio per sei mesi. She breastfed her son for six months.

Lesson 4 of 5.
cullare — to rock (a baby).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for pushchair, then the Italian for to breastfeed.

You'll want to know: cullare.
cullare — "to rock (a baby)".
La mamma culla il bambino finché si addormenta. The mother rocks the baby until he falls asleep.
Built on la culla "cradle".

The word, taken apart.
cullare is built on culla "cradle", inherited from Vulgar Latin cunla (EtymDB), a shortened little form of cūnae "cradle" (Lewis & Short has cūnulae "a little cradle").
