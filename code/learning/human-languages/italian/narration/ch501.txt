Italian, chapter 501: Un giorno di pesca.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pescare — to fish; to draw (a card).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for crochet, then the Italian for to darn.

You'll want to know: pescare.
pescare — "to fish; to draw (a card)".
Ogni domenica mio zio va a pescare al lago. Every Sunday my uncle goes fishing at the lake.
In card games: pescare una carta — to draw a card.

The word, taken apart.
pescare is inherited from Latin piscārī "to fish" (EtymDB), from piscis "a fish".

Lesson 2 of 5.
la canna da pesca — fishing rod.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to darn, then the Italian for to fish.

You'll want to know: la canna da pesca.
la canna da pesca — "fishing rod".
Ho comprato una canna da pesca nuova. I bought a new fishing rod.
La canna alone is a reed or a cane.

The word, taken apart.
canna da pesca is built on canna "reed, cane", inherited from Latin canna (EtymDB), from Greek kánna "a reed", and pesca "fishing", from pescare, which goes back to Latin piscis "a fish".

Lesson 4 of 5.
abboccare — to bite (the bait); to fall for it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for fishing rod, then the Italian for bait.

You'll want to know: abboccare.
abboccare — "to bite (the bait); to fall for it".
Dopo un'ora finalmente un pesce ha abboccato. After an hour a fish finally bit.
Also of people: ha abboccato — he fell for it.

The word, taken apart.
abboccare is built on bocca "mouth", inherited from Latin bucca "cheek, mouth" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.

la borraccia — "water bottle, flask".
Porto sempre la borraccia piena d'acqua. I always carry a full water bottle.
A bottle you can fill again, for walks and sport.

The word, taken apart.
borraccia was borrowed from Spanish borracha "a leather wine bottle" (EtymDB); the deeper origin is not settled.
