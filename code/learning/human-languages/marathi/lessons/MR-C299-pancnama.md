## You'll want to know: पंचनामा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C299-TAPAS299-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पंचनामा** — *pañcnāmā* — "a scene report; an official record of a scene".

> हवालदाराने घटनास्थळी पंचनामा केला.
>
> *havāldārāne ghaṭnāsthaḷī pañcnāmā kelā.*
>
> The constable drew up an official report at the scene.

Masculine.

पंचनामा करणे, to draw one up, signed by witnesses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पंचनामा** (*pañcnāmā*) is built from *pañc*, 'five; the panel of witnesses', from Sanskrit *pañca* (Monier-Williams), and Persian *nāma*, 'a writing, a document'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
