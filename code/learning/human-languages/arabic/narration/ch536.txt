Arabic, chapter 536: Fishing and unwinding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صيد (ṣayd) — hunting; fishing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to fall out, then the Arabic for to abandon.

You'll want to know: صيد.
صيد — ṣayd — "hunting; fishing".
يحب خالي الصيد في البحر. yuḥibbu khālī aṣ-ṣayd fī al-baḥr. My uncle loves fishing in the sea.

The word, taken apart.
صيد (ṣayd) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-y-d, "to hunt, to catch" (Lane). Ṣāda, to hunt, and ṣayyād, a fisherman, which you know, share the root.

The word, taken apart.
صنارة (ṣinnāra), a fish hook and a fishing rod, was borrowed from Syriac ṣenārtā, "a fishing gaff, a hooked tool" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
اصطاد (iṣṭāda) — to go fishing; to hunt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for hunting, then the Arabic for a fishing rod.

You'll want to know: اصطاد.
اصطاد — iṣṭāda — "to go fishing; to hunt".
اصطاد أخي سمكة كبيرة. iṣṭāda akhī samaka kabīra. My brother caught a big fish.
Present: يصطاد, yaṣṭād.

The word, taken apart.
اصطاد (iṣṭāda) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-y-d, "to hunt, to catch" (Lane). Form VIII iṣṭāda, with t turning into ṭ, is to catch for oneself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
استجم (istajamma) — to unwind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a fishing rod, then the Arabic for to go fishing.

You'll want to know: استجم.
استجم — istajamma — "to unwind".
استجم أبي في القرية أسبوعا. istajamma abī fī al-qarya usbūʿan. My father unwound in the village for a week.
Present: يستجم, yastajimm.

The word, taken apart.
استجم (istajamma) is built on the native Arabic root j-m-m, "to rest, to gather again (of water in a well)" (Lane). Form X istajamma is to rest until one's strength comes back.

Lesson 5 of 5.
استمتع (istamtaʿa) — to enjoy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to go fishing, then the Arabic for to unwind.

You'll want to know: استمتع.
استمتع — istamtaʿa — "to enjoy".
استمتعنا بالنزهة كثيرا. istamtaʿnā bi-n-nuzha kathīran. We enjoyed the outing very much.
Present: يستمتع, yastamtiʿ.
It takes بـ for what you enjoy.

The word, taken apart.
استمتع (istamtaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root m-t-ʿ, "to enjoy, to profit by" (Lane). Mumtiʿ, enjoyable, which you know, is from the same root.
