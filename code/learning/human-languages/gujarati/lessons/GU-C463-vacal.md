## You'll want to know: વાચાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C463-VACAL463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વાચાળ** — *vācāḷ* — "talkative".

> વાચાળ મામી આખો દિવસ બોલે છે.
>
> *vācāḷ māmī ākho divas bole che.*
>
> My talkative aunt talks all day long.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાચાળ** (*vācāḷ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vācāla*, "talkative, chattering", as Monier-Williams records it.
