## You'll want to know: عزز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C418-TAQWIYA418-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عزز** — *ʿazzaza* — "to strengthen".

> عززت الرياضة ثقته بنفسه.
>
> *ʿazzazat ar-riyāḍa thiqatahu bi-nafsih.*
>
> Sport strengthened his self-confidence.

Present: يعزز, *yuʿazziz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عزز** (*ʿazzaza*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-z-z*, "to be strong, mighty" (Lane). It is Form II, to make strong: to strengthen, to reinforce.
