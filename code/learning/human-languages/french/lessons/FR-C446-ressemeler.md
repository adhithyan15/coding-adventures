## You'll want to know: ressemeler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C446-CORDONNIER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ressemeler** — "to resole".

> Il fait ressemeler ses chaussures chaque hiver.
>
> *He has his shoes resoled every winter.*

*La semelle* — the sole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ressemeler* is built from *re-* 'again' and *semelle* 'sole' (EtymDB lists the verb). The origin of *semelle* is not settled.
